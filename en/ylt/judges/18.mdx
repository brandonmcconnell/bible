<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 17:6; Judges 19:1; Judges 1:34"> In those days there is no king in Israel, and in those days the tribe of the Danite is seeking for itself an inheritance to inhabit, for &#91;that&#93; hath not fallen to it unto that day in the midst of the tribes of Israel by inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 13:25; Joshua 2:1; Judges 18:11"> And the sons of Dan send, out of their family, five men of them, men, sons of valour, from Zorah, and from Eshtaol, to traverse the land, and to search it, and they say unto them, 'Go, search the land;' and they come into the hill-country of Ephraim, unto the house of Micah, and lodge there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 12:6; Genesis 27:22; Matthew 26:73"> They &#91;are&#93; with the household of Micah, and they have discerned the voice of the young man, the Levite, and turn aside there, and say to him, 'Who hath brought thee hither? and what art thou doing in this &#91;place?&#93; and what to thee here?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 17:10; Ezekiel 13:19; Proverbs 28:21"> And he saith unto them, 'Thus and thus hath Micah done to me; and he hireth me, and I am to him for a priest.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 8:10; Isaiah 30:1; 1 Kings 22:5"> And they say to him, 'Ask, we pray thee, at God, and we know whether our way is prosperous on which we are going.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 22:6; 1 Kings 22:15; Psalms 33:18"> And the priest saith to them, 'Go in peace; over-against Jehovah &#91;is&#93; your way in which ye go.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 19:47; Judges 18:27–28; 1 Samuel 3:13"> And the five men go, and come in to Laish, and see the people which &#91;is&#93; in its midst, dwelling confidently, according to the custom of Zidonians, quiet and confident; and there is none putting to shame in the land in &#91;any&#93; thing, possessing restraint, and they &#91;are&#93; far off from the Zidonians, and have no word with &#91;any&#93; man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 18:11; Judges 18:2; Judges 13:2"> And they come in unto their brethren, at Zorah and Eshtaol, and their brethren say to them, 'What — ye?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 2:23–24; Numbers 13:30; Joshua 18:3"> And they say, 'Rise, and we go up against them, for we have seen the land, and lo, very good; and ye are keeping silent! be not slothful to go — to enter to possess the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 18:27; Judges 18:7; Deuteronomy 2:29"> When ye go, ye come in unto a people confident, and the land &#91;is&#93; large on both hands, for God hath given it into your hand, a place where there is no lack of anything which &#91;is&#93; in the land.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 18:16"> And there journey thence, of the family of the Danite, from Zorah, and from Eshtaol, six hundred men girded with weapons of war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 13:25; Joshua 15:60; 2 Chronicles 1:4"> And they go up and encamp in Kirjath-Jearim, in Judah, therefore they have called that place, 'Camp of Dan,' till this day; lo, behind Kirjath-Jearim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 24:30; Judges 19:1; Judges 18:2–3"> And they pass over thence &#91;to&#93; the hill-country of Ephraim, and come in unto the house of Micah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 17:5; Judges 18:3–4; 1 Samuel 14:28"> And the five men, those going to traverse the land of Laish, answer and say unto their brethren, 'Have ye known that there is in these houses an ephod, and teraphim, and graven image, and molten image? and now, know what ye do.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 43:27; 1 Samuel 17:22; John 14:27"> And they turn aside thither, and come in unto the house of the young man the Levite, the house of Micah, and ask of him of welfare, —</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 18:11"> (and the six hundred men girded with their weapons of war, who &#91;are&#93; of the sons of Dan, are standing at the opening of the gate), —</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 18:14; Judges 17:4–5; Judges 18:2"> yea, the five men, those going to traverse the land, go up — they have come in thither — they have taken the graven image, and the ephod, and the teraphim, and the molten image — and the priest is standing at the opening of the gate, and the six hundred men who are girded with weapons of war —</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> yea, these have entered the house of Micah, and take the graven image, the ephod, and the teraphim, and the molten image; and the priest saith unto them, 'What are ye doing?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 29:9; Job 21:5; Judges 17:10"> and they say to him, 'Keep silent, lay thy hand on thy mouth, and go with us, and be to us for a father and for a priest: is it better thy being a priest to the house of one man, or thy being priest to a tribe and to a family in Israel?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 56:11; Ezekiel 13:19; Proverbs 30:15"> And the heart of the priest is glad, and he taketh the ephod, and the teraphim, and the graven image, and goeth into the midst of the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and they turn and go, and put the infants, and the cattle, and the baggage, before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> They have been far off from the house of Micah — and the men who &#91;are&#93; in the houses which &#91;are&#93; near the house of Micah have been called together, and overtake the sons of Dan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 114:5; 2 Samuel 14:5; 1 Samuel 11:5"> and call unto the sons of Dan, and they turn round their faces, and say to Micah, 'What — to thee that thou hast been called together?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:18–19; Isaiah 44:18–20; Psalms 115:8"> And he saith, 'My gods which I made ye have taken, and the priest, and ye go; and what to me more? and what &#91;is&#93; this ye say unto me, What — to thee!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 27:2; Job 3:5; 1 Samuel 30:6"> And the sons of Dan say unto him, 'Let not thy voice be heard with us, lest men bitter in soul fall upon you, and thou hast gathered thy life, and the life of thy household;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and the sons of Dan go on their way, and Micah seeth that they are stronger than he, and turneth, and goeth back unto his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 19:47; Judges 18:7; Judges 18:10"> And they have taken that which Micah had made, and the priest whom he had, and come in against Laish, against a people quiet and confident, and smite them by the mouth of the sword, and the city have burnt with fire,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:6; Judges 18:7; Numbers 13:21"> and there is no deliverer, for it &#91;is&#93; far off from Zidon, and they have no word with &#91;any&#93; man, and it &#91;is&#93; in the valley which &#91;is&#93; by Beth-Rehob; and they build the city, and dwell in it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 19:47; Genesis 14:14; 1 Kings 15:20"> and call the name of the city Dan, by the name of Dan their father, who was born to Israel; and yet Laish &#91;is&#93; the name of the city at the first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:10–11; Judges 13:1; Judges 17:5"> And the sons of Dan raise up for themselves the graven image, and Jonathan son of Gershom, son of Manasseh, he and his sons have been priests to the tribe of the Danite, till the day of the removal of &#91;the people&#93; of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; 1 Samuel 1:3; Judges 19:18"> And they appoint for them the graven image of Micah, which he had made, all the days of the house of God being in Shiloh.</span>
