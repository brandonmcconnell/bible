<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 15:1; Revelation 19:1–3; Psalms 18:1"> And Deborah singeth — also Barak son of Abinoam — on that day, saying: —</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 5:9; 2 Chronicles 17:16; Psalms 110:3"> 'For freeing freemen in Israel, For a people willingly offering themselves Bless ye Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 138:4–5; Psalms 119:46; Psalms 49:1–2"> Hear, ye kings; give ear, ye princes, I, to Jehovah, I — I do sing, I sing praise to Jehovah, God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:2; Psalms 68:7–8; Psalms 77:17"> Jehovah, in Thy going forth out of Seir, In Thy stepping out of the field of Edom, Earth trembled, also the heavens dropped, Also thick clouds dropped water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 19:18; Psalms 97:5; Isaiah 64:1–3"> Hills flowed from the face of Jehovah, This one — Sinai — From the face of Jehovah, God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 33:8; Judges 3:31; Lamentations 1:4"> In the days of Shamgar son of Anath — In the days of Jael — The ways have ceased, And those going in the paths go &#91;in&#93; crooked ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; 2 Samuel 20:19; Esther 9:19"> Villages ceased in Israel — they ceased, Till that I arose — Deborah, That I arose, a mother in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:16–17; Judges 2:12; Judges 2:17"> He chooseth new gods, Then war &#91;is&#93; at the gates! A shield is not seen — and a spear Among forty thousand in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 5:2; 2 Corinthians 8:3–4; 2 Corinthians 9:5"> My heart &#91;is&#93; to the lawgivers of Israel, Who are offering themselves willingly among the people, Bless ye Jehovah!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 12:14; Judges 10:4; Psalms 105:2"> Riders on white asses — Sitters on a long robe — And walkers by the way — meditate!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Micah 6:5; Judges 5:8; 1 Samuel 12:7"> By the voice of shouters Between the places of drawing water, There they give out righteous acts of Jehovah, Righteous acts of His villages in Israel, Then ruled in the gates have the people of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 4:8; Psalms 68:18; Psalms 57:8"> Awake, awake, Deborah; Awake, awake, utter a song; Rise, Barak, and take captive thy captivity, Son of Abinoam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 2:26–27; Romans 8:37; Psalms 75:7"> Then him who is left of the honourable ones He caused to rule the people of Jehovah, He caused me to rule among the mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 12:15; Numbers 32:39–40; Judges 3:27"> Out of Ephraim their root &#91;is&#93; against Amalek. After thee, Benjamin, among thy peoples. Out of Machir came down lawgivers, And out of Zebulun those drawing with the reed of a writer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 4:14; Proverbs 22:13; Acts 20:13"> And princes in Issachar &#91;are&#93; with Deborah, Yea, Issachar &#91;is&#93; right with Barak, Into the valley he was sent on his feet. In the divisions of Reuben, Great &#91;are&#93; the decrees of heart!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 32:24; Numbers 32:1–5; Judges 5:15"> Why hast thou abode between the boundaries, To hear lowings of herds? For the divisions of Reuben, Great &#91;are&#93; the searchings of heart!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 13:25; Joshua 19:24–31; Joshua 13:31"> Gilead beyond the Jordan did tabernacle, And Dan — why doth he sojourn &#91;in&#93; ships? Asher hath abode at the haven of the seas, And by his creeks doth tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 4:10; Judges 4:6; Esther 4:16"> Zebulun &#91;is&#93; a people who exposed its soul to death, Naphtali also — on high places of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Judges 5:30; Judges 1:27; 1 Kings 4:12"> Kings came — they fought; Then fought kings of Canaan, In Taanach, by the waters of Megiddo; Gain of money they took not!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 10:11; 1 Samuel 7:10; Psalms 77:17–18"> From the heavens they fought: The stars from their highways fought with Sisera.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 4:7; Micah 7:10; Psalms 44:5"> The brook Kishon swept them away, The brook most ancient — the brook Kishon. Thou dost tread down strength, O my soul!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 20:7; Psalms 147:10–11; Jeremiah 47:4"> Then broken were the horse-heels, By pransings — pransings of its mighty ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:10; Judges 21:9–10; 1 Corinthians 16:22"> Curse Meroz — said a messenger of Jehovah, Cursing, curse ye its inhabitants, For they came not to the help of Jehovah, To the help of Jehovah among the mighty!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 1:42; Luke 1:28; Judges 4:17"> Blessed above women is Jael, Wife of Heber the Kenite, Above women in the tent she is blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Judges 4:19–21"> Water he asked — milk she gave; In a lordly dish she brought near butter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:49–51; Judges 4:21; 2 Samuel 20:22"> Her hand to the pin she sendeth forth, And her right hand to the labourers' hammer, And she hammered Sisera — she smote his head, Yea, she smote, and it passed through his temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 52:7; James 2:13; Matthew 7:2"> Between her feet he bowed — He fell, he lay down; Between her feet he bowed, he fell; Where he bowed, there he fell — destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 4:15; James 5:7; Song of Solomon 8:14"> Through the window she hath looked out — Yea, she crieth out — the mother of Sisera, Through the lattice: Wherefore is his chariot delaying to come? Wherefore tarried have the steps of his chariot?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> The wise ones, her princesses, answer her, Yea, she returneth her sayings to herself:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 15:9; 2 Samuel 13:18; Genesis 37:3"> Do they not find? — they apportion spoil, A female — two females — for every head, Spoil of finger-work for Sisera, Spoil of embroidered finger-work, Finger-work — a pair of embroidered things, For the necks of the spoil!</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 37:6; 2 Samuel 23:4; Psalms 19:4–5"> So do all Thine enemies perish, O Jehovah, And those loving Him &#91;are&#93; As the going out of the sun in its might!' and the land resteth forty years.</span>
