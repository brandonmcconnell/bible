<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:41; Judges 12:1–6; Job 5:2"> And the men of Ephraim say unto him, 'What &#91;is&#93; this thing thou hast done to us — not to call for us when thou didst go to fight with Midian?' and they strive with him severely;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:4–7; Philippians 2:2–3; James 3:13–18"> and he saith unto them, 'What have I done now like you? are not the gleanings of Ephraim better than the harvest of Abi-Ezer?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; Judges 7:24–25; John 4:37"> Into your hand hath God given the heads of Midian, Oreb and Zeeb; and what have I been able to do like you?' Then their temper desisted from off him in his speaking this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; 2 Corinthians 4:16; 1 Samuel 14:28–29"> And Gideon cometh in unto the Jordan, passing over, he and the three hundred men who &#91;are&#93; with him — wearied, and pursuing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 33:17; Psalms 60:6; 1 Samuel 25:18"> and he saith to the men of Succoth, 'Give, I pray you, cakes of bread to the people who &#91;are&#93; at my feet, for they &#91;are&#93; wearied, and I am pursuing after Zebah and Zalmunna kings of Midian.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 20:11; Philippians 2:21; Proverbs 18:23"> And the heads of Succoth say, 'Is the hand of Zebah and Zalmunna now in thy hand, that we give to thy host bread?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 7:15"> And Gideon saith, 'Therefore — in Jehovah's giving Zebah and Zalmunna into my hand — I have threshed your flesh with the thorns of the wilderness, and with the threshing instruments.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 32:30–31; 1 Kings 12:25"> And he goeth up thence &#91;to&#93; Penuel, and speaketh unto them thus; and the men of Penuel answer him as the men of Succoth answered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 8:17; 1 Kings 22:27–28"> And he speaketh also to the men of Penuel, saying, 'In my turning back in peace, I break down this tower.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 7:12; Judges 20:25; Judges 20:2"> And Zebah and Zalmunna &#91;are&#93; in Karkor, and their camps with them, about fifteen thousand, all who are left of all the camp of the sons of the east; and those falling &#91;are&#93; a hundred and twenty thousand men, drawing sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 32:35; Numbers 32:42; Judges 18:27"> And Gideon goeth up the way of those who tabernacle in tents, on the east of Nobah and Jogbehah, and smiteth the camp, and the camp was confident;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 83:11; Amos 2:14; Job 12:16–21"> and Zebab and Zalmunna flee, and he pursueth after them, and captureth the two kings of Midian, Zebah and Zalmunna, and all the camp he hath caused to tremble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And Gideon son of Joash turneth back from the battle, at the going up of the sun,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 1:24–25; 1 Samuel 30:11–15"> and captureth a young man of the men of Succoth, and asketh him, and he describeth unto him the heads of Succoth, and its elders — seventy and seven men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 8:6–7"> And he cometh in unto the men of Succoth, and saith, 'Lo Zebah and Zalmunna, with whom ye reproached me, saying, Is the hand of Zebah and Zalmunna now in thy hand that we give to thy men who &#91;are&#93; wearied bread?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 8:7; Ezra 2:6; Proverbs 10:13"> And he taketh the elders of the city, and the thorns of the wilderness, and the threshing instruments, and teacheth by them the men of Succoth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 8:9; 1 Kings 12:25"> and the tower of Penuel he hath broken down, and slayeth the men of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 4:6; Jude 1:16; Psalms 89:12"> And he saith unto Zebah and unto Zalmunna, 'How — the men whom ye slew in Tabor?' and they say, 'As thou — so they, one — as the form of the king's sons.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And he saith, 'My brethren — sons of my mother — they; Jehovah liveth, if ye had kept them alive — I had not slain you.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 149:9; 1 Samuel 15:33; Joshua 10:24"> And he saith to Jether his first-born, 'Rise, slay them;' and the young man hath not drawn his sword, for he hath been afraid, for he &#91;is&#93; yet a youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 83:11; Judges 8:26; Isaiah 3:18"> And Zebah saith — also Zalmunna — 'Rise thou, and fall upon us; for as the man — his might;' and Gideon riseth, and slayeth Zebah and Zalmunna, and taketh their round ornaments which &#91;are&#93; on the necks of their camels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 6:15; Judges 9:8–15; 1 Samuel 12:12"> And the men of Israel say unto Gideon, 'Rule over us, both thou, and thy son, and thy son's son, for thou hast saved us from the hand of Midian.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 33:22; 1 Samuel 12:12; 1 Samuel 10:19"> And Gideon saith unto them, 'I do not rule over you, nor doth my son rule over you; Jehovah doth rule over you.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 25:13; Genesis 37:25; Genesis 37:28"> And Gideon saith unto them, 'Let me ask of you a petition, and give ye to me each the ring of his prey, for they have rings of gold, for they &#91;are&#93; Ishmaelites.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And they say, 'We certainly give;' and they spread out the garment, and cast thither each the ring of his prey;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 18:16; John 19:2; Revelation 18:12"> and the weight of the rings of gold which he asked is a thousand and seven hundred &#91;shekels&#93; of gold, apart from the round ornaments, and the drops, and the purple garments, which &#91;are&#93; on the kings of Midian, and apart from the chains which &#91;are&#93; on the necks of their camels,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 17:5; Judges 18:14; Psalms 106:39"> and Gideon maketh it into an ephod, and setteth it up in his city, in Ophrah, and all Israel go a-whoring after it there, and it is to Gideon and to his house for a snare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 5:31; Judges 3:11; Judges 3:30"> And Midian is humbled before the sons of Israel, and have not added to lift up their head; and the land resteth forty years in the days of Gideon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 6:32; Judges 7:1; 1 Samuel 12:11"> And Jerubbaal son of Joash goeth and dwelleth in his own house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 9:5; Judges 9:2; 1 Kings 11:3"> and to Gideon there have been seventy sons, coming out of his loin, for he had many wives;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 9:18; Genesis 22:24; Genesis 16:15"> and his concubine, who &#91;is&#93; in Shechem, hath born to him — even she — a son, and he appointeth his name Abimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 25:8; Judges 6:24; Job 5:26"> And Gideon son of Joash dieth, in a good old age, and is buried in the burying-place of Joash his father, in Ophrah of the Abi-Ezrite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Judges 9:46; Judges 2:19; Judges 9:4"> And it cometh to pass, when Gideon &#91;is&#93; dead, that the sons of Israel turn back and go a-whoring after the Baalim, and set over them Baal-Berith for a god;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 78:42; Psalms 106:21; Psalms 78:11"> and the sons of Israel have not remembered Jehovah their God, who is delivering them out of the hand of all their enemies round about,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:14–15; Judges 9:5; Judges 9:16–19"> neither have they done kindness with the house of Jerubbaal — Gideon — according to all the good which he did with Israel.</span>
