<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 18:2; Joshua 17:14–18; Judges 10:1"> And there is a man of the hill-country of Ephraim, and his name &#91;is&#93; Micah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:25; Ruth 3:10; 1 Samuel 23:21"> and he saith to his mother, 'The eleven hundred silverlings which have been taken of thine, and &#91;of which&#93; thou hast sworn, and also spoken in mine ears; lo, the silver &#91;is&#93; with me, I have taken it;' and his mother saith, 'Blessed &#91;is&#93; my son of Jehovah.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 20:4; Exodus 20:23; Leviticus 19:4"> And he giveth back the eleven hundred silverlings to his mother, and his mother saith, 'I had certainly sanctified the silver to Jehovah, from my hand, for my son, to make a graven image, and a molten image; and now, I give it back to thee.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 46:6–7; Jeremiah 10:9–10"> And he giveth back the money to his mother, and his mother taketh two hundred silverlings, and giveth them to a refiner, and he maketh them a graven image, and a molten image, and it is in the house of Micah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 18:14; Judges 8:27; Genesis 31:19"> As to the man Micah, he hath a house of gods, and he maketh an ephod, and teraphim, and consecrateth the hand of one of his sons, and he is to him for a priest;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:8; Proverbs 14:12; Judges 21:25"> in those days there is no king in Israel, each that which is right in his own eyes doth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Matthew 2:1; Ruth 1:1–2"> And there is a young man of Beth-Lehem-Judah, of the family of Judah, and he &#91;is&#93; a Levite, and he &#91;is&#93; a sojourner there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:10–11; Judges 17:11; Joshua 24:33"> And the man goeth out of the city, out of Beth-Lehem-Judah, to sojourn where he doth find, and cometh to the hill-country of Ephraim, unto the house of Micah, to work his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Micah saith to him, 'Whence comest thou?' and he saith unto him, 'A Levite &#91;am&#93; I, of Beth-Lehem-Judah, and I am going to sojourn where I do find.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:19; Judges 18:19–20; 1 Timothy 6:10"> And Micah saith to him, 'Dwell with me, and be to me for a father and for a priest, and I give to thee ten silverlings for the days, and a suit of garments, and thy sustenance;' and the Levite goeth &#91;in&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the Levite is willing to dwell with the man, and the young man is to him as one of his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 18:30; Numbers 16:8–10; Numbers 16:5"> And Micah consecrateth the hand of the Levite, and the young man is to him for a priest, and he is in the house of Micah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 10:2–3; Proverbs 14:12; John 16:2"> and Micah saith, 'Now I have known that Jehovah doth good to me, for the Levite hath been to me for a priest.'</span>
