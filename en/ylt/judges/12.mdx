<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 8:1; James 4:1–2; James 3:16"> And the men of Ephraim are called together, and pass over northward, and say to Jephthah, 'Wherefore has thou passed over to fight against the Bene-Ammon, and on us hast not called to go with thee? thy house we burn over thee with fire.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 11:12–33"> And Jephthah saith unto them, 'A man of great strife I have been (I and my people) with the Bene-Ammon, and I call you, and ye have not saved me out of their hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:5; 1 Samuel 28:21; Job 13:14"> and I see that thou art not a saviour, and I put my life in my hand, and pass over unto the Bene-Ammon, and Jehovah giveth them into my hand — and why have ye come up unto me this day to fight against me?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:10; Deuteronomy 3:12–17; Proverbs 15:1"> And Jephthah gathered all the men of Gilead, and fighteth with Ephraim, and the men of Gilead smite Ephraim, because they said, 'Fugitives of Ephraim &#91;are&#93; ye Gileadites, in the midst of Ephraim — in the midst of Manasseh.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 3:28; Joshua 22:11; Joshua 2:7"> And Gilead captureth the passages of the Jordan to Ephraim, and it hath been, when &#91;any of&#93; the fugitives of Ephraim say, 'Let me pass over,' and the men of Gilead say to him, 'An Ephramite thou?' and he saith, 'No;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 69:2; Psalms 69:15; Mark 14:70"> that they say to him, 'Say, I pray thee, Shibboleth;' and he saith, 'Sibboleth,' and is not prepared to speak right — and they seize him, and slaughter him at the passages of the Jordan, and there fall at that time, of Ephraim, forty and two chiefs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And Jephthah judged Israel six years, and Jephthah the Gileadite dieth, and is buried in &#91;one of&#93; the cities of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Micah 5:2; 1 Samuel 16:1; Matthew 2:1"> And after him Ibzan of Beth-Lehem judgeth Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 10:4; Judges 12:14"> and he hath thirty sons and thirty daughters, he hath sent without and thirty daughters hath brought in to his sons from without; and he judgeth Israel seven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Ibzan dieth, and is buried in Beth-Lehem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And after him Elon the Zebulunite judgeth Israel, and he judgeth Israel ten years,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 8:13; 1 Chronicles 6:69; Joshua 19:42"> and Elon the Zebulunite dieth, and is buried in Aijalon, in the land of Zebulun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And after him, Abdon son of Hillel, the Pirathonite, judgeth Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 5:10; Judges 10:4"> and he hath forty sons, and thirty grandsons, riding on seventy ass-colts, and he judgeth Israel eight years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 5:14; Judges 3:13; Judges 3:27"> And Abdon son of Hillel, the Pirathonite, dieth, and is buried in Pirathon, in the land of Ephraim, in the hill-country of the Amalekite.</span>
