<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 11:32; Judges 6:12; 2 Kings 5:1"> And Jephthah the Gileadite hath been a mighty man of valour, and he &#91;is&#93; son of a woman, a harlot; and Gilead begetteth Jephthah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 12:10; Proverbs 2:16; Deuteronomy 23:2"> and the wife of Gilead beareth to him sons, and the wife's sons grow up and cast out Jephthah, and say to him, 'Thou dost not inherit in the house of our father; for son of another woman &#91;art&#93; thou.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 9:4; 1 Samuel 22:2; 2 Samuel 10:8"> And Jephthah fleeth from the face of his brethren, and dwelleth in the land of Tob; and vain men gather themselves together unto Jephthah, and they go out with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 10:9"> And it cometh to pass, after a time, that the Bene-Ammon fight with Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; Acts 7:35–39; 1 Corinthians 1:27–29"> and it cometh to pass, when the Bene-Ammon have fought with Israel, that the elders of Gilead go to take Jephthah from the land of Tob;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> and they say unto Jephthah, 'Come, and thou hast been to us for captain, and we fight against the Bene-Ammon.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 3:9; Genesis 45:4–5; Isaiah 60:14"> And Jephthah saith to the elders of Gilead, 'Have not ye hated me? and ye cast me out from the house of my father, and wherefore have ye come unto me now when ye are in distress?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 10:18; 1 Kings 13:6; Exodus 10:17"> and the elders of Gilead say unto Jephthah, 'Therefore, now, we have turned back unto thee; and thou hast gone with us, and fought against the Bene-Ammon, and thou hast been to us for head — to all the inhabitants of Gilead.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 32:20–29"> And Jephthah saith unto the elders of Gilead, 'If ye are taking me back to fight against the Bene-Ammon, and Jehovah hath given them before me — I, am I to you for a head?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:5; Genesis 31:50; Jeremiah 29:23"> And the elders of Gilead say unto Jephthah, 'Jehovah is hearkening between us — if according to thy word we do not so.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 10:17; 1 Samuel 10:17; Judges 20:1"> And Jephthah goeth with the elders of Gilead, and the people set him over them for head and for captain, and Jephthah speaketh all his words before Jehovah in Mizpeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:26; Matthew 18:15–16; Proverbs 25:8–9"> And Jephthah sendeth messengers unto the king of the Bene-Ammon, saying, 'What — to me and to thee, that thou hast come in unto me, to fight in my land.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 32:22; Numbers 21:24–26; Deuteronomy 2:37"> And the king of the Bene-Ammon saith unto the messengers of Jephthah, 'Because Israel took my land in his coming up out of Egypt, from Arnon, and unto the Jabbok, and unto the Jordan; and now, restore them in peace.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 3:11; Romans 12:18; Hebrews 12:14"> And Jephthah addeth yet and sendeth messengers unto the king of the Bene-Ammon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:19; Deuteronomy 2:9; Numbers 21:27–30"> and saith to him, 'Thus said Jephthah, Israel took not the land of Moab, and the land of the Bene-Ammon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 14:25; Deuteronomy 1:40; Numbers 20:1"> for in their coming up out of Egypt, Israel goeth in the wilderness unto the Red Sea, and cometh in to Kadesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 20:14–21; Deuteronomy 2:4–9; Numbers 20:1"> and Israel sendeth messengers unto the king of Edom, saying, Let me pass over, I pray thee, through thy land, and the king of Edom hearkened not; and also unto the king of Moab hath &#91;Israel&#93; sent, and he hath not been willing; and Israel abideth in Kadesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:1–8; Numbers 22:36; Numbers 21:4–13"> and he goeth through the wilderness, and compasseth the land of Edom and the land of Moab, and cometh in at the rising of the sun of the land of Moab, and they encamp beyond Arnon, and have not come into the border of Moab, for Arnon &#91;is&#93; the border of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 21:21–35; Deuteronomy 2:26–36; Deuteronomy 3:1–17"> 'And Israel sendeth messengers unto Sihon, king of the Amorite, king of Heshbon, and Israel saith to him, Let us pass over, we pray thee, through thy land, unto my place,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 13:15–32; Numbers 21:23–25; Psalms 136:17–21"> and Sihon hath not trusted Israel to pass over through his border, and Sihon gathereth all his people, and they encamp in Jahaz, and fight with Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and Jehovah, God of Israel, giveth Sihon and all his people into the hand of Israel, and they smite them, and Israel possesseth all the land of the Amorite, the inhabitant of that land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:36"> and they possess all the border of the Amorite from Arnon, and unto the Jabbok, and from the wilderness, and unto the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> 'And now, Jehovah, God of Israel, hath dispossessed the Amorite from the presence of His people Israel, and thou wouldest possess it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 21:29; Psalms 78:55; Micah 4:5"> That which Chemosh thy god causeth thee to possess — dost thou not possess it? and all that which Jehovah our God hath dispossessed from our presence, — it we do possess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Micah 6:5; Joshua 24:9–10; Deuteronomy 23:3–4"> 'And now, &#91;art&#93; thou at all better than Balak son of Zippor, king of Moab? did he at all strive with Israel? did he at all fight against them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:36; Joshua 12:5; Judges 10:8"> In Israel's dwelling in Heshbon and in its towns, and in Aroer and in its towns, and in all the cities which &#91;are&#93; by the sides of Arnon three hundred years — and wherefore have ye not delivered them in that time?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:15; Genesis 16:5; 1 Samuel 24:12"> And I — I have not sinned against thee, and thou art doing with me evil — to fight against me. Jehovah, the Judge, doth judge to-day between the sons of Israel and the sons of Ammon.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Kings 14:11; Proverbs 16:18"> And the king of the Bene-Ammon hath not hearkened unto the words of Jephthah which he sent unto him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 11:25; Judges 3:10; Judges 13:25"> and the Spirit of Jehovah is on Jephthah, and he passeth over Gilead and Manasseh, and passeth over Mizpeh of Gilead, and from Mizpeh of Gilead he hath passed over to the Bene-Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 28:20; 1 Samuel 1:11; Ecclesiastes 5:1–2"> And Jephthah voweth a vow to Jehovah, and saith, 'If Thou dost at all give the Bene-Ammon into my hand —</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 66:13–14; Leviticus 27:28–29; 1 Samuel 1:28"> then it hath been, that which at all cometh out from the doors of my house to meet me in my turning back in peace from the Bene-Ammon — it hath been to Jehovah, or I have offered up for it — a burnt-offering.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Judges 1:4; Judges 2:18; Judges 3:10"> And Jephthah passeth over unto the Bene-Ammon to fight against them, and Jehovah giveth them into his hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:17; Deuteronomy 2:36"> and he smiteth them from Aroer, and unto thy going in to Minnith, twenty cities, and unto the meadow of the vineyards — a very great smiting; and the Bene-Ammon are humbled at the presence of the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:4; Exodus 15:20; Judges 10:17"> And Jephthah cometh into Mizpeh, unto his house, and lo, his daughter is coming out to meet him with timbrels, and with choruses, and save her alone, he hath none, son or daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 14:7–9; Genesis 37:29–30; Genesis 42:36–38"> And it cometh to pass, when he seeth her, that he rendeth his garments, and saith, 'Alas, my daughter, thou hast caused me greatly to bend, and thou hast been among those troubling me; and I — I have opened my mouth unto Jehovah, and I am not able to turn back.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:19; Philippians 2:30; Romans 16:4"> And she saith unto him, 'My father — thou hast opened thy mouth unto Jehovah, do to me as it hath gone out from thy mouth, after that Jehovah hath done for thee vengeance on thine enemies, on the Bene-Ammon.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 1:25; 1 Samuel 1:6"> And she saith unto her father, 'Let this thing be done to me; desist from me two months, and I go on, and have gone down on the hills, and I weep for my virginity — I and my friends.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And he saith, 'Go;' and he sendeth her away two months, and she goeth, she and her friends, and she weepeth for her virginity on the hills;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 66:3; Judges 11:31; Leviticus 27:28–29"> and it cometh to pass at the end of two months that she turneth back unto her father, and he doth to her his vow which he hath vowed, and she knew not a man; and it is a statute in Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Kings 9:25; Judges 5:11"> from time to time the daughters of Israel go to talk to the daughter of Jephthah the Gileadite, four days in a year.</span>
