<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 5:1; Hosea 7:1; Zephaniah 3:3"> And as bands do wait for a man, A company of priests do murder — the way to Shechem, For wickedness they have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 5:3; Jeremiah 23:14; Jeremiah 5:30–31"> In the house of Israel I have seen a horrible thing, There &#91;is&#93; the whoredom of Ephraim — defiled is Israel.</span>
