<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:22; Micah 6:2; Isaiah 3:13–14"> 'Hear a word of Jehovah, sons of Israel, For a strife &#91;is&#93; to Jehovah with inhabitants of the land, For there is no truth, nor kindness, Nor knowledge of God, in the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 6:9; Jeremiah 6:7; Hosea 7:1"> Swearing, and lying, and murdering, And stealing, and committing adultery — have increased, And blood against blood hath touched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:3; Isaiah 24:4–12; Nahum 1:4"> Therefore mourn doth the land, And weak is every dweller in it, With the beast of the field, And with the fowl of the heavens, And the fishes of the sea — they are removed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:12; Hosea 4:17; Amos 5:13"> Only, let no one strive, nor reprove a man, And thy people &#91;are&#93; as those striving with a priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:8; Hosea 2:2; Galatians 4:26"> And thou hast stumbled in the day, And stumbled hath also a prophet with thee in the night, And I have cut off thy mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 5:13; Proverbs 19:2; Hosea 6:6"> Cut off have been My people for lack of knowledge, Because thou knowledge hast rejected, I reject thee from being priest to Me, And thou forgettest the law of thy God, I forget thy sons, I also!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 13:6; Philippians 3:19; 1 Samuel 2:30"> According to their abundance so they sinned against Me, Their honour into shame I change.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Isaiah 56:11; Titus 1:11"> The sin of My people they do eat, And unto their iniquity lift up their soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 24:2; Jeremiah 5:31; Matthew 15:14"> And it hath been, like people, like priest, And I have charged on it its ways, And its habitual doings I return to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 6:14; Leviticus 26:26; Haggai 1:6"> And they have eaten, and are not satisfied, They have gone a-whoring, and increase not, For they have left off taking heed to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Luke 21:34; Isaiah 28:7"> Whoredom, and wine, and new wine, take the heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:27; Hosea 5:4; Habakkuk 2:19"> My people at its staff asketh and its rod declareth to it, For a spirit of whoredoms hath caused to err, And they go a-whoring from under their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:29; Jeremiah 3:6; Ezekiel 6:13"> On tops of the mountains they do sacrifice, And on the hills they make perfume, Under oak, and poplar, and terebinth, For good &#91;is&#93; its shade.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hosea 4:1; John 8:43; Ephesians 4:18"> Therefore commit whoredom do your daughters, And your spouses commit adultery, I do not see after your daughters when they commit whoredom, And after your spouses when they commit adultery, For they with the harlots are separated, And with the whores they do sacrifice, A people that doth not understand kicketh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 12:11; Hosea 9:15; Amos 4:4"> Though a harlot thou &#91;art&#93;, O Israel, Let not Judah become guilty, And come not ye in to Gilgal, nor go up to Beth-Aven, Nor swear ye, Jehovah liveth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:24; Jeremiah 8:5; Jeremiah 3:11"> For as a refractory heifer hath Israel turned aside, Now doth Jehovah feed them as a lamb in a large place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 81:12; Revelation 22:11; Hosea 13:2"> Joined to idols &#91;is&#93; Ephraim, let him alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Amos 5:12; 2 Kings 17:7–17"> Sour &#91;is&#93; their drink, They have gone diligently a-whoring, Her protectors have loved shame thoroughly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 1:29; Jeremiah 4:11–12; Hosea 13:15"> Distressed her hath wind with its wings, And they are ashamed of their sacrifices!</span>
