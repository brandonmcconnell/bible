<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 9:8; Micah 7:2; Micah 3:9"> 'Hear this, O priests, and attend, O house of Israel, And, O house of the king, give ear, For the judgment &#91;is&#93; for you, For, a snare ye have been on Mizpah, And a net spread out on Tabor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 9:15; Isaiah 29:15; Hosea 6:9"> And to slaughter sinners have gone deep, And I &#91;am&#93; a fetter to them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 3:2; Hebrews 4:13; Hosea 13:1"> I have known Ephraim, And Israel hath not been hid from me, For now thou hast gone a-whoring, Ephraim, Defiled is Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 4:12; 1 John 2:3–4; Hosea 4:1"> They give not up their habitual doings, To turn back unto their God, For a spirit of whoredoms &#91;is&#93; in their midst, And Jehovah they have not known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 7:10; Amos 5:2; Ezekiel 23:31–35"> And humbled hath been the excellency of Israel to his face, And Israel and Ephraim stumble by their iniquity, Stumbled also hath Judah with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Micah 6:6–7; John 7:34; Proverbs 1:28"> With their flock and with their herd, They go to seek Jehovah, and do not find, He hath withdrawn from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 6:7; Isaiah 48:8; Jeremiah 3:20"> Against Jehovah they dealt treacherously, For strange sons they have begotten, Now consume them doth a month &#91;with&#93; their portions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hosea 4:15; Hosea 9:9; 1 Samuel 15:34"> Blow ye a cornet in Gibeah, a trumpet in Ramah, Shout, O Beth-Aven, after thee, O Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 1:6; Isaiah 46:10; Hosea 9:11–17"> Ephraim is for a desolation in a day of reproof, Among the tribes of Israel I have made known a sure thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:14; Deuteronomy 27:17; Psalms 93:3–4"> Princes of Judah have been as those removing a border, On them I do pour out as water My wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:33; Micah 6:16; 1 Kings 12:26–33"> Oppressed is Ephraim, broken in judgment, When he pleased he went after the command.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 51:8; Job 13:28; Isaiah 50:9"> And I &#91;am&#93; as a moth to Ephraim, And as a rotten thing to the house of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 7:11; Hosea 12:1; Hosea 10:6"> And see doth Ephraim his sickness, and Judah his wound, And Ephraim goeth unto Asshur, And sendeth unto a warlike king, And he is not able to give healing to you, Nor doth he remove from you a scar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 7:2; Micah 5:8; Hosea 13:7–8"> For I &#91;am&#93; as a lion to Ephraim, And as a young lion to the house of Judah, I — I tear and go, I bear away, and there is no deliverer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 26:16; Jeremiah 29:12–14; Deuteronomy 4:29–31"> I go — I turn back unto My place, Till that they are desolate, and have sought My face. In their distress they do seek Me speedily!'</span>
