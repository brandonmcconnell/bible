<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 8:11; Leviticus 26:1; Philippians 2:21"> 'An empty vine &#91;is&#93; Israel, Fruit he maketh like to himself, According to the abundance of his fruit, He hath multiplied for the altars, According to the goodness of his land, They have made goodly standing-pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 5:13; 1 Kings 18:21; Zephaniah 1:5"> Their heart hath been divided, now they are guilty, He doth break down their altars, He doth destroy their standing-pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 10:15; Hosea 10:7; Hosea 13:11"> For now they say: We have no king, Because we have not feared Jehovah, And the king — what doth he for us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 5:7; Amos 6:12; Hosea 4:2"> They have spoken words, To swear falsehood in making a covenant, And flourished as a poisonous herb hath judgment, on the furrows of a field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 9:11; Hosea 5:8; 2 Kings 23:5"> For the calves of Beth-Aven fear do inhabitants of Samaria, Surely mourned on account of it hath its people, And its priests on account of it leap about, Because of its honour, for it hath removed from it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 5:13; Isaiah 30:3; Daniel 11:8"> Also it to Asshur is carried, a present to a warlike king, Shame doth Ephraim receive, And ashamed is Israel of its own counsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 10:3; Jude 1:13; 2 Kings 1:3"> Cut off is Samaria! Its king &#91;is&#93; as a chip on the face of the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 23:30; Revelation 6:16; Isaiah 2:19"> And destroyed have been high places of Aven, the sin of Israel. Thorn and bramble go up on their altars, And they have said to hills, Cover us, And to heights, Fall upon us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 9:9; Judges 20:5; Judges 19:22–30"> From the days of Gibeah thou hast sinned, O Israel, There they have stood, Not overtake them in Gibeah doth battle, Because of sons of perverseness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:13; Hosea 4:9; Jeremiah 16:16"> When I desire, then I do bind them, And gathered against them have peoples, When they bind themselves to their two iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 4:16; Jeremiah 50:11; Deuteronomy 25:4"> And Ephraim &#91;is&#93; a trained heifer — loving to thresh, And I — I have passed over on the goodness of its neck, I cause &#91;one&#93; to ride Ephraim, Plough doth Judah, harrow for him doth Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:3–4; James 3:18; Isaiah 45:8"> Sow for yourselves in righteousness, Reap according to loving-kindness, Till for yourselves tillage of knowledge, To seek Jehovah, Till he come and shew righteousness to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 4:8; Galatians 6:7–8; Proverbs 1:31"> Ye have ploughed wickedness, Perversity ye have reaped, Ye have eaten the fruit of lying, For thou hast trusted in thy way, In the abundance of thy might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hosea 13:16; 2 Kings 18:9–10; Amos 3:8"> And rise doth a tumult among thy people, And all thy fortresses are spoiled, As the spoiling of Shalman of Beth-Arbel, In a day of battle, Mother against sons dashed in pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 10:5; Hosea 10:7; Romans 7:13"> Thus hath Beth-El done to you, Because of the evil of your wickedness, In the dawn cut off utterly is a king of Israel!</span>
