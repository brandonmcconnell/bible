<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 10:5; Hosea 4:12; Jeremiah 44:17"> 'Rejoice not, O Israel, be not joyful like the peoples, For thou hast gone a-whoring from thy God, Thou hast loved a gift near all floors of corn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 2:9; Micah 6:13–16; Joel 1:3–7"> Floor and wine-press do not delight them, And new wine doth fail in her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:13; Hosea 8:13; Leviticus 25:23"> They do not abide in the land of Jehovah, And turned back hath Ephraim &#91;to&#93; Egypt, And in Asshur an unclean thing they eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 8:13; Deuteronomy 26:14; Jeremiah 6:20"> They pour not out wine to Jehovah, Nor are they sweet to Him, Their sacrifices &#91;are&#93; as bread of mourners to them, All eating it are unclean: For their bread &#91;is&#93; for themselves, It doth not come into the house of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 10:3; Jeremiah 5:31; Hosea 2:11"> What do ye at the day appointed? And at the day of Jehovah's festival?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 10:8; Isaiah 5:6; Isaiah 7:23"> For, lo, they have gone because of destruction, Egypt gathereth them, Moph burieth them, The desirable things of their silver, Nettles possess them — a thorn &#91;is&#93; in their tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 26:24–25; Micah 7:4; 2 Kings 9:11"> Come in have the days of inspection, Come in have the days of recompence, Israel doth know! a fool &#91;is&#93; the prophet, Mad &#91;is&#93; the man of the Spirit, Because of the abundance of thine iniquity, And great &#91;is&#93; the hatred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hosea 5:1; Ezekiel 3:17; Isaiah 62:6"> Ephraim is looking &#91;away&#93; from My God, The prophet! a snare of a fowler &#91;is&#93; over all his ways, Hatred &#91;is&#93; in the house of his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 10:9; Judges 19:16–21; Hosea 8:13"> They have gone deep — have done corruptly, As &#91;in&#93; the days of Gibeah, He doth remember their iniquity, He doth inspect their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:13; Hosea 4:14; Numbers 25:1–18"> As grapes in a wilderness I found Israel, As the first-fruit in a fig-tree, at its beginning, I have seen your fathers, They — they have gone in &#91;to&#93; Baal-Peor, And are separated to a shameful thing, And are become abominable like their love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:18; Hosea 9:14; Hosea 4:7"> Ephraim &#91;is&#93; as a fowl, Fly away doth their honour, without birth, And without womb, and without conception.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 7:13; Deuteronomy 32:25; Deuteronomy 31:17"> For though they nourish their sons, I have made them childless — without man, Surely also, woe to them, when I turn aside from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:1–21; Ezekiel 27:3; Hosea 13:8"> Ephraim! when I have looked to the rock, Is planted in comeliness, And Ephraim &#91;is&#93; to bring out unto a slayer his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 23:29; 1 Corinthians 7:26; Mark 13:17"> Give to them, Jehovah — what dost Thou give? Give to them miscarrying womb, and dry breasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 4:15; Isaiah 1:23; Amos 5:5"> All their evil &#91;is&#93; in Gilgal, Surely there I have hated them, Because of the evil of their doings, Out of My house I do drive them, I add not to love them, all their heads &#91;are&#93; apostates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 9:11–13; Ezekiel 24:21; Isaiah 5:24"> Ephraim hath been smitten, Their root hath dried up, fruit they yield not, Yea, though they bring forth, I have put to death the desired of their womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hosea 7:13; Deuteronomy 28:64–65; Acts 3:23"> Reject them doth my God, Because they have not hearkened to Him, And they are wanderers among nations!</span>
