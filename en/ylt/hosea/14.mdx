<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 34:32; Isaiah 55:13; James 1:17"> O Ephraim, what to Me any more with idols? I — I afflicted, and I cause him to sing: 'I &#91;am&#93; as a green fir-tree,' From Me is thy fruit found.</span>
