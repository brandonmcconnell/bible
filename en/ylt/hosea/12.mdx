<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 11:12; Hosea 8:7; Ezekiel 17:10"> Ephraim is enjoying wind, And is pursuing an east wind, All the day lying and spoiling he multiplieth, And a covenant with Asshur they make, And oil to Egypt is carried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 6:2; Isaiah 10:6; Hosea 4:1"> And a controversy hath Jehovah with Judah, To lay a charge on Jacob according to his ways, According to his doings He returneth to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 25:26; Genesis 32:24–28; Romans 9:11"> In the womb he took his brother by the heel, And by his strength he was a prince with God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 35:15; Malachi 3:1; Genesis 35:9–10"> Yea, he is a prince unto the Messenger, And he overcometh &#91;by&#93; weeping, And he maketh supplication to Him, At Bethel He doth find him, And there He doth speak with us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 3:15; Isaiah 42:8; Psalms 135:13"> Even Jehovah, God of the Hosts, Jehovah &#91;is&#93; His memorial.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Micah 6:8; Hosea 14:1; Micah 7:7"> And thou, through thy God, dost turn, Kindness and judgment keep thou, And wait on thy God continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Micah 6:10–11; Amos 8:5–6; Proverbs 11:1"> Canaan! in his hand &#91;are&#93; balances of deceit! To oppress he hath loved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 3:17; Psalms 62:10; Psalms 52:7"> And Ephraim saith: 'Surely I have become rich, I have found wealth for myself, All my labours — they find not against me iniquity that &#91;is&#93; sin.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 23:40–43; Nehemiah 8:15–17; Hosea 13:4"> And I — Jehovah thy God from the land of Egypt, Again do I turn thee back into tents, As in the days of the appointed time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 17:13; Ezekiel 20:49; Jeremiah 19:1"> And I have spoken unto the prophets, And I have multiplied vision, And by the hand of the prophets I use similes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 4:15; Hosea 6:8; Hosea 8:11"> Surely Gilead &#91;is&#93; iniquity, Only, vanity they have been, In Gilead bullocks they have sacrificed, Also their altars &#91;are&#93; as heaps, on the furrows of a field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 31:41; Genesis 27:43; Genesis 28:1–22"> And Jacob doth flee to the country of Aram, And Israel doth serve for a wife, Yea, for a wife he hath kept watch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 12:50–51; Acts 3:22–23; Psalms 77:20"> And by a prophet hath Jehovah brought up Israel out of Egypt, And by a prophet it hath been watched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:13; Daniel 11:18; 2 Kings 17:7–18"> Ephraim hath provoked most bitterly, And his blood on himself he leaveth, And his reproach turn back to him doth his Lord!</span>
