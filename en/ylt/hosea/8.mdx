<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 6:7; Deuteronomy 28:49; Hosea 5:8"> 'Unto thy mouth — a trumpet, As an eagle against the house of Jehovah, Because they transgressed My covenant, And against My law they have rebelled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:16; Matthew 7:21; Luke 13:25"> To Me they cry, 'My God, we — Israel — have known Thee.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 3:66; Deuteronomy 28:25; 1 Timothy 5:12"> Cast off good hath Israel, an enemy pursueth him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 15:10–30; Hosea 13:9–10; Hosea 2:8"> They have made kings, and not by Me, They have made princes, and I have not known, Their silver and their gold they have made to them idols, So that they are cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 10:5; Jeremiah 13:27; Proverbs 1:22"> Cast off hath thy calf, O Samaria, Burned hath Mine anger against them, Till when are they not capable of purity?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 10:2; Isaiah 44:9–20; Psalms 135:15–18"> For even it &#91;is&#93; of Israel; an artificer made it, And it &#91;is&#93; not God, For the calf of Samaria is fragments!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 22:8; Job 4:8; Galatians 6:7"> For wind they sow, and a hurricane they reap, Stalk it hath none — a shoot not yielding grain, If so be it yield — strangers do swallow it up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:28; Jeremiah 51:34; Romans 9:22"> Israel hath been swallowed up, Now they have been among nations, As a vessel in which is no delight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:24; Hosea 7:11; Ezekiel 16:33–34"> For they — they have gone up &#91;to&#93; Asshur, A wild ass alone by himself &#91;is&#93; Ephraim, They have hired lovers!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:37; Ezekiel 23:22–26; Isaiah 10:8"> Also though they hire among nations, Now I gather them, and they are pained a little, From the burden of a king of princes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 12:11; Hosea 10:1–2; Hosea 10:8"> Because Ephraim did multiply altars to sin, They have been to him altars to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:6–8; Nehemiah 9:26; Proverbs 22:20"> I write for him numerous things of My law, As a strange thing they have been reckoned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 9:9; Hosea 9:6; Amos 8:7"> The sacrifices of Mine offerings! They sacrifice flesh, and they eat, Jehovah hath not accepted them, Now doth He remember their iniquity, And inspect their sin, They — &#91;to&#93; Egypt they turn back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:18; Jeremiah 17:27; Amos 2:5"> And forget doth Israel his Maker, and buildeth temples, And Judah hath multiplied cities of defence, And I have sent a fire into his cities, And it hath consumed their palaces!</span>
