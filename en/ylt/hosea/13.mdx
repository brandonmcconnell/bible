<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 11:2; Joshua 3:7; Numbers 13:16"> When Ephraim speaketh tremblingly, He hath been lifted up in Israel, When he becometh guilty in Baal he dieth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 44:17–20; 1 Kings 19:18; Isaiah 46:6"> And now do they add to sin, And make to them a molten image of their silver, By their own understanding — idols, A work of artisans — all of it, Of them they say, who &#91;are&#93; sacrificers among men, 'The calves let them kiss.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 6:4; Psalms 1:4; Psalms 68:2"> Therefore they are as a cloud of the morning, And as dew, rising early, going away, As chaff tossed about out of a floor, And as smoke out of a window.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 45:21–22; Hosea 12:9; Isaiah 43:10–13"> And I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah thy God from the land of Egypt, And a God besides Me thou dost not know, And a Saviour — there is none save Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:15; Deuteronomy 2:7; Deuteronomy 32:10"> I — I have known thee in a wilderness, In a land of droughts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13–15; Deuteronomy 8:12–14; Nehemiah 9:35"> According to their feedings they are satiated, They have been satiated, And their heart is lifted up, Therefore they have forgotten Me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 5:14; Jeremiah 5:6; Lamentations 3:10"> And I am to them as a lion, As a leopard by the way I look out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:8; Isaiah 5:29; Psalms 50:22"> I do meet them as a bereaved bear, And I rend the enclosure of their heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:17; Jeremiah 2:19; Deuteronomy 33:26"> And I consume them there as a lioness, A beast of the field doth rend them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 8:4; 1 Samuel 8:5–6; Hosea 10:3"> Thou hast destroyed thyself, O Israel, But in Me &#91;is&#93; thy help, Where &#91;is&#93; thy king now — And he doth save thee in all thy cities? And thy judges of whom thou didst say, 'Give to me a king and heads?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:19; 1 Samuel 15:22–23; 1 Kings 14:7–16"> I give to thee a king in Mine anger, And I take away in My wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:34–35; Job 14:17; Romans 2:5"> Bound up &#91;is&#93; the iniquity of Ephraim, Hidden &#91;is&#93; his sin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 37:3; Isaiah 13:8; Psalms 48:6"> Pangs of a travailing woman come to him, He &#91;is&#93; a son not wise, For he remaineth not the time for the breaking forth of sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 25:8; Revelation 21:4; Isaiah 26:19"> From the hand of Sheol I do ransom them, From death I redeem them, Where &#91;is&#93; thy plague, O death? Where thy destruction, O Sheol? Repentance is hid from Mine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:12; Ezekiel 17:10; Genesis 41:52"> Though he among brethren produceth fruit, Come in doth an east wind, a wind of Jehovah, From a wilderness it is coming up, And it drieth up his fountain, And become dry doth his spring, It — it spoileth a treasure — every desirable vessel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 8:12; 2 Kings 15:16; Isaiah 13:16"> Become desolate doth Samaria, Because she hath rebelled against her God, By sword they do fall, Their sucklings are dashed in pieces, And its pregnant ones are ripped up!</span>
