<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 37:28; Psalms 105:17; Genesis 37:36"> And Joseph hath been brought down to Egypt, and Potiphar, a eunuch of Pharaoh, head of the executioners, an Egyptian man, buyeth him out of the hands of the Ishmaelites who have brought him thither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:14; Psalms 91:15; Jeremiah 15:20"> And Jehovah is with Joseph, and he is a prosperous man, and he is in the house of his lord the Egyptian,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 1:3; Genesis 30:27; Matthew 5:16"> and his lord seeth that Jehovah is with him, and all that he is doing Jehovah is causing to prosper in his hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 39:21–22; Proverbs 27:18; Genesis 19:19"> and Joseph findeth grace in his eyes and serveth him, and he appointeth him over his house, and all that he hath he hath given into his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 30:27; 2 Samuel 6:11–12; Acts 27:24"> And it cometh to pass from the time that he hath appointed him over his house, and over all that he hath, that Jehovah blesseth the house of the Egyptian for Joseph's sake, and the blessing of Jehovah is on all that he hath, in the house, and in the field;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:12; Luke 19:17; Proverbs 31:11"> and he leaveth all that he hath in the hand of Joseph, and he hath not known anything that he hath, except the bread which he is eating. And Joseph is of a fair form, and of a fair appearance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:11; Ezekiel 16:34; 2 Peter 2:14"> And it cometh to pass after these things, that his lord's wife lifteth up her eyes unto Joseph, and saith, 'Lie with me;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 23:26–28; Proverbs 22:14; Proverbs 9:13–18"> and he refuseth, and saith unto his lord's wife, 'Lo, my lord hath not known what &#91;is&#93; with me in the house, and all that he hath he hath given into my hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:13; Genesis 20:6; Genesis 42:18"> none is greater in this house than I, and he hath not withheld from me anything, except thee, because thou &#91;art&#93; his wife; and how shall I do this great evil? — then have I sinned against God.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Genesis 39:8; 1 Thessalonians 5:22"> And it cometh to pass at her speaking unto Joseph day &#91;by&#93; day, that he hath not hearkened unto her, to lie near her, to be with her;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 5:12; Ephesians 5:3; Malachi 3:5"> and it cometh to pass about this day, that he goeth into the house to do his work, and there is none of the men of the house there in the house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:22; Proverbs 7:13–27; Ecclesiastes 7:26"> and she catcheth him by his garment, saying, 'Lie with me;' and he leaveth his garment in her hand, and fleeth, and goeth without.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And it cometh to pass when she seeth that he hath left his garment in her hand, and fleeth without,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 10:21; 1 Peter 2:20; Psalms 35:11"> that she calleth for the men of her house, and speaketh to them, saying, 'See, he hath brought in to us a man, a Hebrew, to play with us; he hath come in unto me, to lie with me, and I call with a loud voice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and it cometh to pass, when he heareth that I have lifted up my voice and call, that he leaveth his garment near me, and fleeth, and goeth without.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 37:32; Jeremiah 4:22; Jeremiah 9:3–5"> And she placeth his garment near her, until the coming in of his lord unto his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 23:1; 1 Kings 18:17; Psalms 37:14"> And she speaketh unto him according to these words, saying, 'The Hebrew servant whom thou hast brought unto us, hath come in unto me to play with me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and it cometh to pass, when I lift my voice and call, that he leaveth his garment near me, and fleeth without.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 4:5–6; Proverbs 18:17; Proverbs 6:34–35"> And it cometh to pass when his lord heareth the words of his wife, which she hath spoken unto him, saying, 'According to these things hath thy servant done to me,' that his anger burneth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 40:15; 2 Timothy 2:9; Psalms 105:18–19"> and Joseph's lord taketh him, and putteth him unto the round-house, a place where the king's prisoners &#91;are&#93; bound; and he is there in the round-house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 3:21; Isaiah 41:10; 1 Peter 3:17"> And Jehovah is with Joseph, and stretcheth out kindness unto him, and putteth his grace in the eyes of the chief of the round-house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 40:3–4; Genesis 39:4; 1 Samuel 2:30"> and the chief of the round-house giveth into the hand of Joseph all the prisoners who &#91;are&#93; in the round-house, and of all that they are doing there, he hath been doer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Genesis 39:2–3; Psalms 1:3"> the chief of the round-house seeth not anything under his hand, because Jehovah &#91;is&#93; with him, and that which he is doing Jehovah is causing to prosper.</span>
