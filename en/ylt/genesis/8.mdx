<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 7:11; Matthew 8:26–27; Proverbs 8:28"> and closed are the fountains of the deep and the net-work of the heavens, and restrained is the shower from the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 7:24; Genesis 7:11"> And turn back do the waters from off the earth, going on and returning; and the waters are lacking at the end of a hundred and fifty days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 19:37; Jeremiah 51:27; Isaiah 37:38"> And the ark resteth, in the seventh month, in the seventeenth day of the month, on mountains of Ararat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 7:11"> and the waters have been going and becoming lacking till the tenth month; in the tenth &#91;month&#93;, on the first of the month, appeared the heads of the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 6:16; Daniel 6:10"> And it cometh to pass, at the end of forty days, that Noah openeth the window of the ark which he made,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 147:9; 1 Kings 17:6; 1 Kings 17:4"> and he sendeth forth the raven, and it goeth out, going out and turning back till the drying of the waters from off the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:14; Song of Solomon 1:15; Matthew 10:16"> And he sendeth forth the dove from him to see whether the waters have been lightened from off the face of the ground,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 11:28; Psalms 116:7; Deuteronomy 28:65"> and the dove hath not found rest for the sole of her foot, and she turneth back unto him, unto the ark, for waters &#91;are&#93; on the face of all the earth, and he putteth out his hand, and taketh her, and bringeth her in unto him, unto the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; Genesis 7:10; Genesis 8:12"> And he stayeth yet other seven days, and addeth to send forth the dove from the ark;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:15; Zechariah 4:12–14; Romans 10:15"> and the dove cometh in unto him at even-time, and lo, an olive leaf torn off in her mouth; and Noah knoweth that the waters have been lightened from off the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 25:9; Isaiah 30:18; Genesis 8:10"> And he stayeth yet other seven days, and sendeth forth the dove, and it added not to turn back unto him any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 7:11"> And it cometh to pass in the six hundredth and first year, in the first &#91;month&#93;, in the first of the month, the waters have been dried from off the earth; and Noah turneth aside the covering of the ark, and looketh, and lo, the face of the ground hath been dried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 7:13–14; Genesis 7:11"> And in the second month, in the seven and twentieth day of the month, the earth hath become dry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And God speaketh unto Noah, saying, 'Go out from the ark, thou, and thy wife, and thy sons, and thy sons' wives with thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 7:13; Psalms 121:8; Psalms 91:11"> every living thing that &#91;is&#93; with thee, of all flesh, among fowl, and among cattle, and among every creeping thing which is creeping on the earth, bring out with thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 1:22; Genesis 9:1; Jeremiah 31:27–28"> and they have teemed in the earth, and been fruitful, and have multiplied on the earth.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 121:8"> And Noah goeth out, and his sons, and his wife, and his sons' wives with him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> every beast, every creeping thing, and every fowl; every creeping thing on the earth, after their families, have gone out from the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15–16; Romans 12:1; Genesis 22:9"> And Noah buildeth an altar to Jehovah, and taketh of every clean beast, and of every clean fowl, and causeth burnt-offerings to ascend on the altar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:15; Ephesians 5:2; Isaiah 54:9–10"> and Jehovah smelleth the sweet fragrance, and Jehovah saith unto His heart, 'I continue not to disesteem any more the ground because of man, though the imagination of the heart of man &#91;is&#93; evil from his youth; and I continue not to smite any more all living, as I have done;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 5:7; Psalms 74:16–17; Jeremiah 5:24"> during all days of the earth, seed-time and harvest, and cold and heat, and summer and winter, and day and night, do not cease.'</span>
