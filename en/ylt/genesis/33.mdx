<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 32:6–7; Genesis 27:41–42; Genesis 32:16"> And Jacob lifteth up his eyes, and looketh, and lo, Esau is coming, and with him four hundred men; and he divideth the children unto Leah, and unto Rachel, and unto the two maid-servants;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 29:30; Genesis 30:22–24; Genesis 37:3"> and he setteth the maid-servants and their children first, and Leah and her children behind, and Rachel and Joseph last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 42:6; Luke 14:11; Genesis 18:2"> And he himself passed over before them, and boweth himself to the earth seven times, until his drawing nigh unto his brother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 45:14–15; Psalms 34:4; Luke 15:20"> and Esau runneth to meet him, and embraceth him, and falleth on his neck, and kisseth him, and they weep;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 127:3; Isaiah 8:18; Genesis 48:9"> and he lifteth up his eyes, and seeth the women and the children, and saith, 'What &#91;are&#93; these to thee?' And he saith, 'The children with whom God hath favoured thy servant.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And the maid-servants draw nigh, they and their children, and bow themselves;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and Leah also draweth nigh, and her children, and they bow themselves; and afterwards Joseph hath drawn nigh with Rachel, and they bow themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 32:5; Genesis 39:5; Genesis 32:13–20"> And he saith, 'What to thee &#91;is&#93; all this camp which I have met?' and he saith, 'To find grace in the eyes of my lord.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 27:39; Philemon 1:7; Genesis 27:41"> And Esau saith, 'I have abundance, my brother, let it be to thyself that which thou hast.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 33:26; Revelation 22:4; Genesis 32:30"> And Jacob saith, 'Nay, I pray thee, if, I pray thee, I have found grace in thine eyes, then thou hast received my present from my hand, because that I have seen thy face, as the seeing of the face of God, and thou art pleased with me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:27; Philippians 4:18; Genesis 30:43"> receive, I pray thee, my blessing, which is brought to thee, because God hath favoured me, and because I have all &#91;things&#93;;' and he presseth on him, and he receiveth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and saith, 'Let us journey and go on, and I go on before thee.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; Ezekiel 34:15–16; Ezekiel 34:23–25"> And he saith unto him, 'My lord knoweth that the children &#91;are&#93; tender, and the suckling flock and the herd &#91;are&#93; with me; when they have beaten them one day, then hath all the flock died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 32:3; Isaiah 40:11; Romans 15:1"> Let my lord, I pray thee, pass over before his servant, and I — I lead on gently, according to the foot of the work which &#91;is&#93; before me, and to the foot of the children, until that I come unto my lord, to Seir.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ruth 2:13; Genesis 47:25; Genesis 34:11"> And Esau saith, 'Let me, I pray thee, place with thee some of the people who &#91;are&#93; with me;' and he said, 'Why &#91;is&#93; this? I find grace in the eyes of my lord.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And turn back on that day doth Esau on his way to Seir;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 60:6; Joshua 13:27; Judges 8:5"> and Jacob hath journeyed to Succoth, and buildeth to himself a house, and for his cattle hath made booths, therefore hath he called the name of the place Succoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 9:1; Acts 7:16; Joshua 24:1"> And Jacob cometh in to Shalem, a city of Shechem, which &#91;is&#93; in the land of Canaan, in his coming from Padan-Aram, and encampeth before the city,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 7:16; John 4:5; Joshua 24:32"> and he buyeth the portion of the field where he hath stretched out his tent, from the hand of the sons of Hamor, father of Shechem, for a hundred kesitah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 35:7; Genesis 21:33; Genesis 32:28"> and he setteth up there an altar, and proclaimeth at it God — the God of Israel.</span>
