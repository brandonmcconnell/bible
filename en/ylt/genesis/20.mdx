<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 26:1; Genesis 26:6; Genesis 18:1"> And Abraham journeyeth from thence toward the land of the south, and dwelleth between Kadesh and Shur, and sojourneth in Gerar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 12:15; Genesis 26:7; Genesis 12:11–13"> and Abraham saith concerning Sarah his wife, 'She is my sister;' and Abimelech king of Gerar sendeth and taketh Sarah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 20:7; Matthew 27:19; Psalms 105:14"> And God cometh in unto Abimelech in a dream of the night, and saith to him, 'Lo, thou &#91;art&#93; a dead man, because of the woman whom thou hast taken — and she married to a husband.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 18:23–25; 1 Chronicles 21:17; 2 Samuel 4:11"> And Abimelech hath not drawn near unto her, and he saith, 'Lord, also a righteous nation dost thou slay?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 7:8; 1 Kings 9:4; Daniel 6:22"> hath not he himself said to me, She &#91;is&#93; my sister! and she, even she herself, said, He &#91;is&#93; my brother; in the integrity of my heart, and in the innocency of my hands, I have done this.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:34; Proverbs 21:1; 1 Samuel 25:26"> And God saith unto him in the dream, 'Yea, I — I have known that in the integrity of thy heart thou hast done this, and I withhold thee, even I, from sinning against Me, therefore I have not suffered thee to come against her;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 42:8; 1 Samuel 7:5; 2 Samuel 24:17"> and now send back the man's wife, for he &#91;is&#93; inspired, and he doth pray for thee, and live thou; and if thou do not send back, know that dying thou dost die, thou, and all that thou hast.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And Abimelech riseth early in the morning, and calleth for all his servants, and speaketh all these words in their ears; and the men fear exceedingly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 12:18; Genesis 26:10; Genesis 39:9"> and Abimelech calleth for Abraham, and saith to him, 'What hast thou done to us? and what have I sinned against thee, that thou hast brought upon me, and upon my kingdom, a great sin? works which are not done thou hast done with me.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Abimelech also saith unto Abraham, 'What hast thou seen that thou hast done this thing?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 26:7; Genesis 12:12; Job 28:28"> And Abraham saith, 'Because I said, 'Surely the fear of God is not in this place, and they have slain me for the sake of my wife;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 11:29; Genesis 12:13; 1 Thessalonians 5:22"> and also, truly she is my sister, daughter of my father, only not daughter of my mother, and she becometh my wife;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 12:1; Genesis 12:9; Acts 7:3–5"> and it cometh to pass, when God hath caused me to wander from my father's house, that I say to her, This &#91;is&#93; thy kindness which thou dost with me: at every place whither we come, say of me, He &#91;is&#93; my brother.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 12:16; Genesis 20:2; Genesis 20:7"> And Abimelech taketh sheep and oxen, and servants and handmaids, and giveth to Abraham, and sendeth back to him Sarah his wife;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 34:10; Genesis 13:9; Genesis 47:6"> and Abimelech saith, 'Lo, my land &#91;is&#93; before thee, where it is good in thine eyes, dwell;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 27:5; Genesis 20:5; Genesis 24:65"> and to Sarah he hath said, 'Lo, I have given a thousand silverlings to thy brother; lo, it is to thee a covering of eyes, to all who are with thee;' and by all this she is reasoned with.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 5:16; Matthew 21:22; Job 42:9–10"> And Abraham prayeth unto God, and God healeth Abimelech and his wife, and his handmaids, and they bear:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 12:17; Genesis 30:2; Genesis 16:2"> for Jehovah restraining had restrained every womb of the house of Abimelech, because of Sarah, Abraham's wife.</span>
