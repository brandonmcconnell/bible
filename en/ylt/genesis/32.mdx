<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 91:11; Psalms 34:7; Hebrews 1:4"> And Jacob hath gone on his way, and messengers of God come upon him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:8; Joshua 5:14; Joshua 21:38"> and Jacob saith, when he hath seen them, 'This &#91;is&#93; the camp of God;' and he calleth the name of that place 'Two Camps.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 25:30; Joshua 24:4; Deuteronomy 2:5"> And Jacob sendeth messengers before him unto Esau his brother, towards the land of Seir, the field of Edom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 3:6; Luke 14:11; Genesis 33:8"> and commandeth them, saying, 'Thus do ye say to my lord, to Esau: Thus said thy servant Jacob, With Laban I have sojourned, and I tarry until now;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 33:8; Genesis 33:15; Genesis 33:11"> and I have ox, and ass, flock, and man-servant, and maid-servant, and I send to declare to my lord, to find grace in his eyes.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 33:1; Genesis 32:8; Genesis 32:11"> And the messengers turn back unto Jacob, saying, 'We came in unto thy brother, unto Esau, and he also is coming to meet thee, and four hundred men with him;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 35:3; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10; Exodus 14:10"> and Jacob feareth exceedingly, and is distressed, and he divideth the people who &#91;are&#93; with him, and the flock, and the herd, and the camels, into two camps,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 10:16; Genesis 33:1–3"> and saith, 'If Esau come in unto the one camp, and have smitten it — then the camp which is left hath been for an escape.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 31:42; Psalms 34:4–6; Genesis 31:3"> And Jacob saith, 'God of my father Abraham, and God of my father Isaac, Jehovah who saith unto me, Turn back to thy land, and to thy kindred, and I do good with thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 18:35; Genesis 24:27; 2 Samuel 7:18"> I have been unworthy of all the kind acts, and of all the truth which Thou hast done with thy servant — for, with my staff I passed over this Jordan, and now I have become two camps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 59:1–2; Proverbs 18:19; Matthew 6:13"> 'Deliver me, I pray Thee, from the hand of my brother, from the hand of Esau: for I am fearing him, less he come and have smitten me — mother beside sons;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 28:13–15; Hebrews 6:17; Titus 1:2"> and Thou — Thou hast said, I certainly do good with thee, and have set thy seed as the sand of the sea, which is not numbered because of the multitude.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 18:16; Genesis 43:11; Proverbs 19:6"> And he lodgeth there during that night, and taketh from that which is coming into his hand, a present for Esau his brother:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 1:3; Deuteronomy 8:18; Job 42:12"> she-goats two hundred, and he-goats twenty, ewes two hundred, and rams twenty,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> suckling camels and their young ones thirty, cows forty, and bullocks ten, she-asses twenty, and foals ten;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 33:8–9; Genesis 32:20; Proverbs 2:11"> and he giveth into the hand of his servants, every drove by itself, and saith unto his servants, 'Pass over before me, and a space ye do put between drove and drove.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 33:3"> And he commandeth the first, saying, 'When Esau my brother meeteth thee, and hath asked thee, saying, Whose &#91;art&#93; thou? and whither goest thou? and whose &#91;are&#93; these before thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 32:4–5"> then thou hast said, Thy servant Jacob's: it &#91;is&#93; a present sent to my lord, to Esau; and lo, he also &#91;is&#93; behind us.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And he commandeth also the second, also the third, also all who are going after the droves, saying, 'According to this manner do ye speak unto Esau in your finding him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 42:8–9; Proverbs 21:14; Genesis 43:11"> and ye have said also, Lo, thy servant Jacob &#91;is&#93; behind us;' for he said, 'I pacify his face with the present which is going before me, and afterwards I see his face; it may be he lifteth up my face;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and the present passeth over before his face, and he hath lodged during that night in the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:16; Deuteronomy 2:37; 1 Timothy 5:8"> And he riseth in that night, and taketh his two wives, and his two maid-servants, and his eleven children, and passeth over the passage of Jabbok;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and he taketh them, and causeth them to pass over the brook, and he causeth that which he hath to pass over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hosea 12:3–5; Ephesians 6:12; Luke 13:24"> And Jacob is left alone, and one wrestleth with him till the ascending of the dawn;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:7–9; Hosea 12:3–4; Matthew 26:41"> and he seeth that he is not able for him, and he cometh against the hollow of his thigh, and the hollow of Jacob's thigh is disjointed in his wrestling with him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hosea 12:4; Psalms 115:12–13; 2 Corinthians 12:8–9"> and he saith, 'Send me away, for the dawn hath ascended:' and he saith, 'I send thee not away, except thou hast blessed me.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And he saith unto him, 'What &#91;is&#93; thy name?' and he saith, 'Jacob.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 2:17; Genesis 35:10; Isaiah 62:2–4"> And he saith, 'Thy name is no more called Jacob, but Israel; for thou hast been a prince with God and with men, and dost prevail.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6; Judges 13:16–18; Job 11:7"> And Jacob asketh, and saith, 'Declare, I pray thee, thy name;' and he saith, 'Why &#91;is&#93; this, thou askest for My name?' and He blesseth him there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 24:10–11; Numbers 12:8; Isaiah 6:5"> And Jacob calleth the name of the place Peniel: for 'I have seen God face unto face, and my life is delivered;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:7; Genesis 19:15; Judges 8:8"> and the sun riseth on him when he hath passed over Penuel, and he is halting on his thigh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:5"> therefore the sons of Israel do not eat the sinew which shrank, which &#91;is&#93; on the hollow of the thigh, unto this day, because He came against the hollow of Jacob's thigh, against the sinew which shrank.</span>
