<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:32–33; Genesis 23:1–2; Genesis 28:1"> And Abraham addeth and taketh a wife, and her name &#91;is&#93; Keturah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:32–33; Numbers 25:17–18; Job 2:11"> and she beareth to him Zimran, and Jokshan, and Medan, and Midian, and Ishbak, and Shuah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:13; Jeremiah 49:8; Ezekiel 27:20"> And Jokshan hath begotten Sheba and Dedan; and the sons of Dedan were Asshurim, and Letushim, and Leummim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 60:6"> and the sons of Midian &#91;are&#93; Ephah, and Epher, and Hanoch, and Abidah, and Eldaah: all these &#91;are&#93; sons of Keturah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 24:36; Hebrews 1:2; Galatians 3:29"> And Abraham giveth all that he hath to Isaac;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 21:14; Judges 6:3; Acts 14:17"> and to the sons of the concubines whom Abraham hath, Abraham hath given gifts, and sendeth them away from Isaac his son (in his being yet alive) eastward, unto the east country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 12:4"> And these &#91;are&#93; the days of the years of the life of Abraham, which he lived, a hundred and seventy and five years;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 35:28–29; Genesis 15:15; 1 Chronicles 29:28"> and Abraham expireth, and dieth in a good old age, aged and satisfied, and is gathered unto his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 50:13; Genesis 35:29; Genesis 49:29–30"> And Isaac and Ishmael his sons bury him at the cave of Machpelah, at the field of Ephron, son of Zoar the Hittite, which &#91;is&#93; before Mamre —</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 23:16; Genesis 49:31"> the field which Abraham bought from the sons of Heth — there hath Abraham been buried, and Sarah his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Genesis 16:14; Genesis 24:62"> And it cometh to pass after the death of Abraham, that God blesseth Isaac his son; and Isaac dwelleth by the Well of the Living One, my Beholder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 83:6; Genesis 17:20; Genesis 21:13"> And these &#91;are&#93; births of Ishmael, Abraham's son, whom Hagar the Egyptian, Sarah's handmaid, hath borne to Abraham;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:29–31; Isaiah 60:7; Song of Solomon 1:5"> and these &#91;are&#93; the names of the sons of Ishmael, by their names, according to their births: first-born of Ishmael, Nebajoth; and Kedar, and Adbeel, and Mibsam,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 21:11; Isaiah 21:16"> and Mishma, and Dumah, and Massa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:19; Job 2:11"> Hadar, and Tema, Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 17:20; Genesis 17:23"> these are sons of Ishmael, and these their names, by their villages, and by their towers; twelve princes according to their peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 15:15; Genesis 25:7–8"> And these &#91;are&#93; the years of the life of Ishmael, a hundred and thirty and seven years; and he expireth, and dieth, and is gathered unto his people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 16:12; Genesis 20:1; 2 Kings 23:29"> and they tabernacle from Havilah unto Shur, which &#91;is&#93; before Egypt, in &#91;thy&#93; going towards Asshur; in the presence of all his brethren hath he fallen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 1:2; Luke 3:34; Acts 7:8"> And these &#91;are&#93; births of Isaac, Abraham's son: Abraham hath begotten Isaac;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 24:29; Genesis 24:67; Genesis 22:23"> and Isaac is a son of forty years in his taking Rebekah, daughter of Bethuel the Aramaean, from Padan-Aram, sister of Laban the Aramaean, to him for a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:13; Isaiah 58:9; Ezra 8:23"> And Isaac maketh entreaty to Jehovah before his wife, for she &#91;is&#93; barren: and Jehovah is entreated of him, and Rebekah his wife conceiveth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:9; 1 Samuel 28:6; 1 Samuel 30:8"> and the children struggle together within her, and she saith, 'If &#91;it is&#93; right — why &#91;am&#93; I thus?' and she goeth to seek Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 9:10–13; Genesis 27:40; Genesis 27:29"> And Jehovah saith to her, 'Two nations &#91;are&#93; in thy womb, and two peoples from thy bowels are parted; and the &#91;one&#93; people than the &#91;other&#93; people is stronger; and the elder doth serve the younger.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And her days to bear are fulfilled, and lo, twins &#91;are&#93; in her womb;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 27:11; Genesis 27:16; Genesis 27:23"> and the first cometh out all red as a hairy robe, and they call his name Esau;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 27:36; Hosea 12:3; Genesis 38:28–30"> and afterwards hath his brother come out, and his hand is taking hold on Esau's heel, and one calleth his name Jacob; and Isaac &#91;is&#93; a son of sixty years in her bearing them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 11:9; Genesis 21:20; Genesis 10:9"> And the youths grew, and Esau is a man acquainted &#91;with&#93; hunting, a man of the field; and Jacob &#91;is&#93; a plain man, inhabiting tents;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 27:19; Genesis 27:31; Genesis 27:4"> and Isaac loveth Esau, for &#91;his&#93; hunting &#91;is&#93; in his mouth; and Rebekah is loving Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 13:25; Isaiah 40:30–31; 1 Samuel 14:28"> And Jacob boileth pottage, and Esau cometh in from the field, and he &#91;is&#93; weary;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 36:1; Exodus 15:15; Genesis 36:9"> and Esau saith unto Jacob, 'Let me eat, I pray thee, some of this red red thing, for I &#91;am&#93; weary;' therefore hath &#91;one&#93; called his name Edom &#91;Red&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> and Jacob saith, 'Sell to-day thy birthright to me.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Malachi 3:14; Job 21:15; Job 22:17"> And Esau saith, 'Lo, I am going to die, and what is this to me — birthright?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 12:16; Genesis 27:36; Hebrews 6:16"> and Jacob saith, 'Swear to me to-day:' and he sweareth to him, and selleth his birthright to Jacob;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Philippians 3:18–19; Hebrews 12:16–17; 1 Corinthians 15:32"> and Jacob hath given to Esau bread and pottage of lentiles, and he eateth, and drinketh, and riseth, and goeth; and Esau despiseth the birthright.</span>
