<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 2:6; Zephaniah 3:9; Isaiah 19:18"> And the whole earth is of one pronunciation, and of the same words,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 10:10; Daniel 1:2; Genesis 14:1"> and it cometh to pass, in their journeying from the east, that they find a valley in the land of Shinar, and dwell there;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 14:10; Exodus 1:14; Exodus 2:3"> and they say each one to his neighbour, 'Give help, let us make bricks, and burn &#91;them&#93; thoroughly:' and the brick is to them for stone, and the bitumen hath been to them for mortar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; John 5:44; Luke 1:51"> And they say, 'Give help, let us build for ourselves a city and tower, and its head in the heavens, and make for ourselves a name, lest we be scattered over the face of all the earth.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 18:21; Psalms 33:13–14; John 3:13"> And Jehovah cometh down to see the city and the tower which the sons of men have builded;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 2:1–4; Genesis 3:22; Genesis 11:1"> and Jehovah saith, 'Lo, the people &#91;is&#93; one, and one pronunciation &#91;is&#93; to them all, and this it hath dreamed of doing; and now, nothing is restrained from them of that which they have purposed to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 33:10; Psalms 55:9; Job 5:12–13"> Give help, let us go down, and mingle there their pronunciation, so that a man doth not understand the pronunciation of his companion.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 1:51; Deuteronomy 32:8; Genesis 10:25"> And Jehovah doth scatter them from thence over the face of all the earth, and they cease to build the city;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 10:10; Genesis 10:5; Genesis 10:20"> therefore hath &#91;one&#93; called its name Babel, for there hath Jehovah mingled the pronunciation of all the earth, and from thence hath Jehovah scattered them over the face of all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:17–27; Genesis 10:21–22; Luke 3:34–36"> These &#91;are&#93; births of Shem: Shem &#91;is&#93; a son of an hundred years, and begetteth Arphaxad two years after the deluge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 9:7; Psalms 144:12; Genesis 1:28"> And Shem liveth after his begetting Arphaxad five hundred years, and begetteth sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 3:36"> And Arphaxad hath lived five and thirty years, and begetteth Salah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And Arphaxad liveth after his begetting Salah four hundred and three years, and begetteth sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And Salah hath lived thirty years, and begetteth Eber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Salah liveth after his begetting Eber four hundred and three years, and begetteth sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 10:21; 1 Chronicles 1:19; Numbers 24:24"> And Eber liveth four and thirty years, and begetteth Peleg.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Eber liveth after his begetting Peleg four hundred and thirty years, and begetteth sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 3:35"> And Peleg liveth thirty years, and begetteth Reu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And Peleg liveth after his begetting Reu two hundred and nine years, and begetteth sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 3:35"> And Reu liveth two and thirty years, and begetteth Serug.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And Reu liveth after his begetting Serug two hundred and seven years, and begetteth sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 24:2"> And Serug liveth thirty years, and begetteth Nahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And Serug liveth after his begetting Nahor two hundred years, and begetteth sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 3:34; Joshua 24:2"> And Nahor liveth nine and twenty years, and begetteth Terah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And Nahor liveth after his begetting Terah an hundred and nineteen years, and begetteth sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Joshua 24:2; 1 Chronicles 1:26–27; Genesis 22:20–24"> And Terah liveth seventy years, and begetteth Abram, Nahor, and Haran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 12:4; Genesis 14:12; Genesis 11:31"> And these &#91;are&#93; births of Terah: Terah hath begotten Abram, Nahor, and Haran; and Haran hath begotten Lot;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 15:7; Nehemiah 9:7; Acts 7:2–4"> and Haran dieth in the presence of Terah his father, in the land of his birth, in Ur of the Chaldees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 17:15; Genesis 22:20; Genesis 24:15"> And Abram and Nahor take to themselves wives; the name of Abram's wife &#91;is&#93; Sarai, and the name of Nahor's wife &#91;is&#93; Milcah, daughter of Haran, father of Milcah, and father of Iscah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 29:31; Genesis 21:1–2; Luke 1:7"> And Sarai is barren — she hath no child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:7; Acts 7:2–4; Genesis 24:10"> And Terah taketh Abram his son, and Lot, son of Haran, his son's son, and Sarai his daughter-in-law, wife of Abram his son, and they go out with them from Ur of the Chaldees, to go towards the land of Canaan; and they come unto Charan, and dwell there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And the days of Terah are two hundred and five years, and Terah dieth in Charan.</span>
</div>
