<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 12:10; Genesis 25:11; Genesis 21:22–32"> And there is a famine in the land, besides the first famine which was in the days of Abraham, and Isaac goeth unto Abimelech king of the Philistines, to Gerar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 12:7; Psalms 37:3; Genesis 17:1"> And Jehovah appeareth unto him, and saith, 'Go not down towards Egypt, tabernacle in the land concerning which I speak unto thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 28:15; Psalms 105:9; Genesis 17:8"> sojourn in this land, and I am with thee, and bless thee, for to thee and to thy seed I give all these lands, and I have established the oath which I have sworn to Abraham thy father;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 3:25; Genesis 15:5; Genesis 22:17–18"> and I have multiplied thy seed as stars of the heavens, and I have given to thy seed all these lands; and blessed themselves in thy seed have all nations of the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 7:24; Genesis 22:18; Genesis 22:16"> because that Abraham hath hearkened to My voice, and keepeth My charge, My commands, My statutes, and My laws.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 20:1"> And Isaac dwelleth in Gerar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 29:25; Genesis 12:13; Genesis 20:2"> and men of the place ask him of his wife, and he saith, 'She &#91;is&#93; my sister:' for he hath been afraid to say, 'My wife — lest the men of the place kill me for Rebekah, for she &#91;is&#93; of good appearance.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 62:5; Proverbs 5:18–19; Proverbs 7:6"> And it cometh to pass, when the days have been prolonged to him there, that Abimelech king of the Philistines looketh through the window, and seeth, and lo, Isaac is playing with Rebekah his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Abimelech calleth for Isaac, and saith, 'Lo, she &#91;is&#93; surely thy wife; and how hast thou said, She &#91;is&#93; my sister?' and Isaac saith unto him, 'Because I said, Lest I die for her.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 20:9–10; Genesis 12:18–19"> And Abimelech saith, 'What &#91;is&#93; this thou hast done to us? as a little thing one of the people had lain with thy wife, and thou hadst brought upon us guilt;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 105:15; Genesis 20:6; Proverbs 6:29"> and Abimelech commandeth all the people, saying, 'He who cometh against this man or against his wife, dying doth die.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 4:8; Genesis 24:1; Genesis 26:3"> And Isaac soweth in that land, and findeth in that year a hundredfold, and Jehovah blesseth him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 112:3; Proverbs 10:22; Genesis 24:35"> and the man is great, and goeth on, going on and becoming great, till that he hath been very great,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 37:11; Genesis 13:2; Proverbs 27:4"> and he hath possession of a flock, and possession of a herd, and an abundant service; and the Philistines envy him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 21:30; Genesis 21:25"> and all the wells which his father's servants digged in the days of Abraham his father, the Philistines have stopped them, and fill them with dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 1:9"> And Abimelech saith unto Isaac, 'Go from us; for thou hast become much mightier than we;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> and Isaac goeth from thence, and encampeth in the valley of Gerar, and dwelleth there;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 13:2; Hosea 2:17; Numbers 32:38"> and Isaac turneth back, and diggeth the wells of water which they digged in the days of Abraham his father, which the Philistines do stop after the death of Abraham, and he calleth to them names according to the names which his father called them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 4:10–11; John 7:38; Song of Solomon 4:15"> And Isaac's servants dig in the valley, and find there a well of living water,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 21:25"> and shepherds of Gerar strive with shepherds of Isaac, saying, 'The water &#91;is&#93; ours;' and he calleth the name of the well 'Strife,' because they have striven habitually with him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezra 4:6"> and they dig another well, and they strive also for it, and he calleth its name 'Hatred.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 17:6; Exodus 1:7; Psalms 118:5"> And he removeth from thence, and diggeth another well, and they have not striven for it, and he calleth its name Enlargements, and saith, 'For — now hath Jehovah given enlargement to us, and we have been fruitful in the land.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 20:1; Genesis 21:31; Genesis 46:1"> And he goeth up from thence &#91;to&#93; Beer-Sheba,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 15:1; Exodus 3:6; Isaiah 43:1–2"> and Jehovah appeareth unto him during that night, and saith, 'I &#91;am&#93; the God of Abraham thy father, fear not, for I &#91;am&#93; with thee, and have blessed thee, and have multiplied thy seed, because of Abraham My servant;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 13:18; Psalms 116:17; Genesis 12:7–8"> and he buildeth there an altar, and preacheth in the name of Jehovah, and stretcheth out there his tent, and there Isaac's servants dig a well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 21:22–32; Genesis 20:3"> And Abimelech hath gone unto him from Gerar, and Ahuzzath his friend, and Phichol head of his host;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 26:16; Judges 11:7; Acts 7:14"> and Isaac saith unto them, 'Wherefore have ye come unto me, and ye have hated me, and ye send me away from you?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 21:22–23; Isaiah 45:14; Isaiah 61:9"> And they say, 'We have certainly seen that Jehovah hath been with thee, and we say, 'Let there be, we pray thee, an oath between us, between us and thee, and let us make a covenant with thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 24:31; Genesis 21:22; Genesis 22:17"> do not evil with us, as we have not touched thee, and as we have only done good with thee, and send thee away in peace; thou &#91;art&#93; now blessed of Jehovah.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 19:3; Romans 12:18; Hebrews 12:14"> And he maketh for them a banquet, and they eat and drink,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:16–17; Hebrews 6:16; Genesis 25:33"> and rise early in the morning, and swear one to another, and Isaac sendeth them away, and they go from him in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 2:4–5"> And it cometh to pass during that day that Isaac's servants come and declare to him concerning the circumstances of the well which they have digged, and say to him, 'We have found water;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 21:31; Genesis 26:28"> and he calleth it Shebah, &#91;oath,&#93; therefore the name of the city &#91;is&#93; Beer-Sheba, &#91;well of the oath,&#93; unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 36:2; Genesis 36:13; Exodus 34:16"> And Esau is a son of forty years, and he taketh a wife, Judith, daughter of Beeri the Hittite, and Bashemath, daughter of Elon the Hittite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 27:46–2; Genesis 28:8; Genesis 6:2"> and they are a bitterness of spirit to Isaac and to Rebekah.</span>
</div>
