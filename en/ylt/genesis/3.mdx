<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:3; 2 Corinthians 11:14; Revelation 12:9"> And the serpent hath been subtile above every beast of the field which Jehovah God hath made, and he saith unto the woman, 'Is it true that God hath said, Ye do not eat of every tree of the garden?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 58:4"> And the woman saith unto the serpent, 'Of the fruit of the trees of the garden we do eat,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 2:16–17; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Exodus 19:12–13"> and of the fruit of the tree which &#91;is&#93; in the midst of the garden God hath said, Ye do not eat of it, nor touch it, lest ye die.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 8:44; 2 Corinthians 11:3; 1 Timothy 2:14"> And the serpent saith unto the woman, 'Dying, ye do not die,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:2; 2 Thessalonians 2:4; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15"> for God doth know that in the day of your eating of it — your eyes have been opened, and ye have been as God, knowing good and evil.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 John 2:16; James 1:14–15; 1 Timothy 2:14"> And the woman seeth that the tree &#91;is&#93; good for food, and that it &#91;is&#93; pleasant to the eyes, and the tree is desirable to make &#91;one&#93; wise, and she taketh of its fruit and eateth, and giveth also to her husband with her, and he doth eat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 59:6; Genesis 2:25; Genesis 3:5"> and the eyes of them both are opened, and they know that they &#91;are&#93; naked, and they sew fig-leaves, and make to themselves girdles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:24; Proverbs 15:3; Hebrews 4:13"> And they hear the sound of Jehovah God walking up and down in the garden at the breeze of the day, and the man and his wife hide themselves from the face of Jehovah God in the midst of the trees of the garden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 4:9; Genesis 16:8; Genesis 18:20–21"> And Jehovah God calleth unto the man, and saith to him, 'Where &#91;art&#93; thou?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 2:25; Psalms 119:120; Isaiah 47:3"> and he saith, 'Thy sound I have heard in the garden, and I am afraid, for I am naked, and I hide myself.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 3:20; Genesis 4:10; Psalms 50:21"> And He saith, 'Who hath declared to thee that thou &#91;art&#93; naked? of the tree of which I have commanded thee not to eat, hast thou eaten?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; James 1:13–15; Job 31:33"> and the man saith, 'The woman whom Thou didst place with me — she hath given to me of the tree — and I do eat.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:3; 1 Timothy 2:14; Genesis 4:10–12"> And Jehovah God saith to the woman, 'What &#91;is&#93; this thou hast done?' and the woman saith, 'The serpent hath caused me to forget — and I do eat.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Micah 7:17; Isaiah 65:25; Psalms 72:9"> And Jehovah God saith unto the serpent, 'Because thou hast done this, cursed &#91;art&#93; thou above all the cattle, and above every beast of the field: on thy belly dost thou go, and dust thou dost eat, all days of thy life;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 16:20; 1 John 3:8; Revelation 12:17"> and enmity I put between thee and the woman, and between thy seed and her seed; he doth bruise thee — the head, and thou dost bruise him — the heel.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Titus 2:5; Colossians 3:18; 1 Corinthians 11:3"> Unto the woman He said, 'Multiplying I multiply thy sorrow and thy conception, in sorrow dost thou bear children, and toward thy husband &#91;is&#93; thy desire, and he doth rule over thee.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 8:20–22; Genesis 5:29; Isaiah 24:5–6"> And to the man He said, 'Because thou hast hearkened to the voice of thy wife, and dost eat of the tree concerning which I have charged thee, saying, Thou dost not eat of it, cursed &#91;is&#93; the ground on thine account; in sorrow thou dost eat of it all days of thy life,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 13:7; Job 5:5; Isaiah 7:23"> and thorn and bramble it doth bring forth to thee, and thou hast eaten the herb of the field;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:7; Job 34:15; Psalms 104:29"> by the sweat of thy face thou dost eat bread till thy return unto the ground, for out of it hast thou been taken, for dust thou &#91;art&#93;, and unto dust thou turnest back.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 2:23; Matthew 1:23; Genesis 5:29"> And the man calleth his wife's name Eve: for she hath been mother of all living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; 2 Corinthians 5:21; 2 Corinthians 5:2–3"> And Jehovah God doth make to the man and to his wife coats of skin, and doth clothe them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 2:7; Revelation 22:14; Proverbs 3:18"> And Jehovah God saith, 'Lo, the man was as one of Us, as to the knowledge of good and evil; and now, lest he send forth his hand, and have taken also of the tree of life, and eaten, and lived to the age,' —</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 4:2; Genesis 3:19; Genesis 4:12"> Jehovah God sendeth him forth from the garden of Eden to serve the ground from which he hath been taken;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 1:7; Psalms 104:4; Exodus 25:18–22"> yea, he casteth out the man, and causeth to dwell at the east of the garden of Eden the cherubs and the flame of the sword which is turning itself round to guard the way of the tree of life.</span>
