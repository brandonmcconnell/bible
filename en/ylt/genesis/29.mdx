<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 6:3; Judges 6:33; Hosea 12:12"> And Jacob lifteth up his feet, and goeth towards the land of the sons of the east;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 4:14; Isaiah 49:10; John 4:6"> and he looketh, and lo, a well in the field, and lo, there three droves of a flock crouching by it, for from that well they water the droves, and the great stone &#91;is&#93; on the mouth of the well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> (When thither have all the droves been gathered, and they have rolled the stone from off the mouth of the well, and have watered the flock, then they have turned back the stone on the mouth of the well to its place.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 28:10; Genesis 27:43; Acts 7:2"> And Jacob saith to them, 'My brethren, from whence &#91;are&#93; ye?' and they say, 'We &#91;are&#93; from Haran.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 24:24; Genesis 24:29; Genesis 31:53"> And he saith to them, 'Have ye known Laban, son of Nahor?' and they say, 'We have known.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:9; Genesis 37:14; Exodus 18:7"> And he saith to them, 'Hath he peace?' and they say, 'Peace; and lo, Rachel his daughter is coming with the flock.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 5:16; Galatians 6:9–10"> And he saith, 'Lo, the day &#91;is&#93; still great, &#91;it is&#93; not time for the cattle to be gathered; water ye the flock, and go, delight yourselves.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 16:3; Luke 24:2; Genesis 34:14"> And they say, 'We are not able, till that all the droves be gathered together, and they have rolled away the stone from the mouth of the well, and we have watered the flock.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 2:21; Song of Solomon 1:7–8; Exodus 2:15–16"> He is yet speaking with them, and Rachel hath come with the flock which her father hath, for she &#91;is&#93; shepherdess;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 2:17"> and it cometh to pass when Jacob hath seen Rachel, daughter of Laban his mother's brother, and the flock of Laban his mother's brother, that Jacob cometh nigh and rolleth the stone from off the mouth of the well, and watereth the flock of Laban his mother's brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 33:4; Genesis 45:14–15; Genesis 45:2"> And Jacob kisseth Rachel, and lifteth up his voice, and weepeth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 24:28; Genesis 13:8; Genesis 14:14–16"> and Jacob declareth to Rachel that he &#91;is&#93; her father's brother, and that he &#91;is&#93; Rebekah's son, and she runneth and declareth to her father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 24:29; Acts 20:37; Romans 16:16"> And it cometh to pass, when Laban heareth the report of Jacob his sister's son, that he runneth to meet him, and embraceth him, and kisseth him, and bringeth him in unto his house; and he recounteth to Laban all these things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:12–13; Genesis 2:23; Judges 9:2"> and Laban saith to him, 'Only my bone and my flesh &#91;art&#93; thou;' and he dwelleth with him a month of days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 30:28; Genesis 31:7"> And Laban saith to Jacob, 'Is it because thou &#91;art&#93; my brother that thou hast served me for nought? declare to me what &#91;is&#93; thy hire.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ruth 4:11; Genesis 49:31; Genesis 31:4"> And Laban hath two daughters, the name of the elder &#91;is&#93; Leah, and the name of the younger Rachel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 12:11; Genesis 46:19–22; Genesis 24:16"> and the eyes of Leah &#91;are&#93; tender, and Rachel hath been fair of form and fair of appearance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hosea 12:12; Exodus 22:16–17; Genesis 34:12"> And Jacob loveth Rachel, and saith, 'I serve thee seven years for Rachel thy younger daughter:'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 6:11; Isaiah 6:5; Psalms 12:2"> and Laban saith, 'It is better for me to give her to thee than to give her to another man; dwell with me;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:7; Ephesians 5:2; Hosea 12:12"> and Jacob serveth for Rachel seven years; and they are in his eyes as some days, because of his loving her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 15:1; Genesis 29:20; Genesis 4:1"> And Jacob saith unto Laban, 'Give up my wife, for my days have been fulfilled, and I go in unto her;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 19:9; Ruth 4:10–13; Judges 14:10–18"> and Laban gathereth all the men of the place, and maketh a banquet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Micah 7:5; Genesis 38:14–15; Genesis 24:65"> And it cometh to pass in the evening, that he taketh Leah, his daughter, and bringeth her in unto him, and he goeth in unto her;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 16:1; Genesis 30:9–12; Genesis 46:18"> and Laban giveth to her Zilpah, his maid-servant, to Leah his daughter, a maid-servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Proverbs 11:31; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> And it cometh to pass in the morning, that lo, it &#91;is&#93; Leah; and he saith unto Laban, 'What &#91;is&#93; this thou hast done to me? for Rachel have I not served with thee? and why hast thou deceived me?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And Laban saith, 'It is not done so in our place, to give the younger before the first-born;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 18:18; Judges 14:12; Genesis 2:2–3"> fulfil the week of this one, and we give to thee also this one, for the service which thou dost serve with me yet seven other years.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Jacob doth so, and fulfilleth the week of this one, and he giveth to him Rachel his daughter, to him for a wife;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 35:22; Genesis 29:24; Genesis 30:3–8"> and Laban giveth to Rachel his daughter Bilhah his maid-servant, for a maid-servant to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 31:41; Genesis 29:20; Genesis 31:15"> And he goeth in also unto Rachel, and he also loveth Rachel more than Leah; and he serveth with him yet seven other years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:21; Matthew 10:37; Deuteronomy 21:15"> And Jehovah seeth that Leah &#91;is&#93; the hated one, and He openeth her womb, and Rachel &#91;is&#93; barren;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:7; Exodus 4:31; Exodus 3:7"> and Leah conceiveth, and beareth a son, and calleth his name Reuben, for she said, 'Because Jehovah hath looked on mine affliction; because now doth my husband love me.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 34:25; Genesis 49:5–6; Genesis 34:30"> And she conceiveth again, and beareth a son, and saith, 'Because Jehovah hath heard that I &#91;am&#93; the hated one, He also giveth to me even this &#91;one&#93;;' and she calleth his name Simeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 32:26–29; Genesis 49:5–7; Genesis 46:11"> And she conceiveth again, and beareth a son, and saith, 'Now &#91;is&#93; the time, my husband is joined unto me, because I have born to him three sons,' therefore hath &#91;one&#93; called his name Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 1:2; 1 Chronicles 5:2; Genesis 49:8–12"> And she conceiveth again, and beareth a son, and saith this time, 'I praise Jehovah;' therefore hath she called his name Judah; and she ceaseth from bearing.</span>
