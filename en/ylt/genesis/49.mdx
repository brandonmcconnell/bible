<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 24:14; Jeremiah 23:20; Daniel 10:14"> And Jacob calleth unto his sons and saith, 'Be gathered together, and I declare to you that which doth happen with you in the latter end of the days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 34:11; Proverbs 7:1; Proverbs 23:22"> 'Be assembled, and hear, sons of Jacob, And hearken unto Israel your father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:17; Psalms 78:51; Psalms 105:36"> Reuben! my first-born thou, My power, and beginning of my strength, The abundance of exaltation, And the abundance of strength;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 35:22; 1 Chronicles 5:1; Deuteronomy 27:20"> Unstable as water, thou art not abundant; For thou hast gone up thy father's bed; Then thou hast polluted: My couch he went up!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 34:25–31; Genesis 46:10–11; Genesis 29:33–34"> Simeon and Levi &#91;are&#93; brethren! Instruments of violence — their espousals!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; Psalms 26:9; Psalms 16:9"> Into their secret, come not, O my soul! Unto their assembly be not united, O mine honour; For in their anger they slew a man, And in their self-will eradicated a prince.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 19:1–9; 1 Chronicles 4:24–31; 1 Chronicles 6:65"> Cursed &#91;is&#93; their anger, for &#91;it is&#93; fierce, And their wrath, for &#91;it is&#93; sharp; I divide them in Jacob, And I scatter them in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 7:14; Genesis 27:29; 1 Chronicles 5:2"> Judah! thou — thy brethren praise thee! Thy hand &#91;is&#93; on the neck of thine enemies, Sons of thy father bow themselves to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 24:9; Numbers 23:24; Revelation 5:5"> A lion's whelp &#91;is&#93; Judah, For prey, my son, thou hast gone up; He hath bent, he hath crouched as a lion, And as a lioness; who causeth him to arise?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 1:32–33; Numbers 24:17; Isaiah 9:6"> The sceptre turneth not aside from Judah, And a lawgiver from between his feet, Till his Seed come; And his &#91;is&#93; the obedience of peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 63:1–3; Joel 3:18; Revelation 7:14"> Binding to the vine his ass, And to the choice vine the colt of his ass, He hath washed in wine his clothing, And in the blood of grapes his covering;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 23:29"> Red &#91;are&#93; eyes with wine, And white &#91;are&#93; teeth with milk!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:18–19; Genesis 30:20; Joshua 19:10–16"> Zebulun at a haven of the seas doth dwell, And he &#91;is&#93; for a haven of ships; And his side &#91;is&#93; unto Zidon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:32; Genesis 30:18; Deuteronomy 33:18"> Issacher &#91;is&#93; a strong ass, Crouching between the two folds;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 81:6; Matthew 23:4; Joshua 14:15"> And he seeth rest that &#91;it is&#93; good, And the land that &#91;it is&#93; pleasant, And he inclineth his shoulder to bear, And is to tribute a servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 30:6; Deuteronomy 33:22; Judges 18:26–27"> Dan doth judge his people, As one of the tribes of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:35; Judges 18:22–31; Judges 14:1–15"> Dan is a serpent by the way, An adder by the path, Which is biting the horse's heels, And its rider falleth backward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 119:174; Psalms 119:166; Micah 7:7"> For Thy salvation I have waited, Jehovah!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 30:11; 1 Chronicles 5:11–22; Deuteronomy 33:20–21"> Gad! a troop assaulteth him, But he assaulteth last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:24–25; Genesis 30:13; Genesis 46:17"> Out of Asher his bread &#91;is&#93; fat; And he giveth dainties of a king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:23; Genesis 30:8; Joshua 19:32–39"> Naphtali &#91;is&#93; a hind sent away, Who is giving beauteous young ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 41:52; Psalms 128:3; Psalms 1:1–3"> Joseph &#91;is&#93; a fruitful son; A fruitful son by a fountain, Daughters step over the wall;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 37:24; Psalms 64:3; Psalms 118:13"> And embitter him — yea, they have striven, Yea, hate him do archers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 132:2; Isaiah 28:16; Psalms 132:5"> And his bow abideth in strength, And strengthened are the arms of his hands By the hands of the Mighty One of Jacob, Whence is a shepherd, a son of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 28:13; Genesis 35:11; Philippians 4:19"> By the God of thy father who helpeth thee, And the Mighty One who blesseth thee, Blessings of the heavens from above, Blessings of the deep lying under, Blessings of breasts and womb; —</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:15–16; Habakkuk 3:6; Isaiah 66:5"> Thy father's blessings have been mighty Above the blessings of my progenitors, Unto the limit of the heights age-during They are for the head of Joseph, And for the crown of the one Separate &#91;from&#93; his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 20:25; Genesis 35:18; Ezekiel 22:27"> Benjamin! a wolf teareth; In the morning he eateth prey, And at evening he apportioneth spoil.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 7:4; Esther 8:11; 1 Kings 18:31"> All these &#91;are&#93; the twelve tribes of Israel, and this &#91;is&#93; that which their father hath spoken unto them, and he blesseth them; each according to his blessing he hath blessed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 47:30; Genesis 50:13; Genesis 15:15"> And he commandeth them, and saith unto them, 'I am being gathered unto my people; bury me by my fathers, at the cave which &#91;is&#93; in the field of Ephron the Hittite;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 23:20; Genesis 23:16–18; Genesis 23:8–9"> in the cave which &#91;is&#93; in the field of Machpelah, which &#91;is&#93; on the front of Mamre, in the land of Canaan, which Abraham bought with the field from Ephron the Hittite for a possession of a burying-place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 25:9; Genesis 35:29; Genesis 50:13"> (there they buried Abraham and Sarah his wife; there they buried Isaac and Rebekah his wife; and there I buried Leah);</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 23:17–20"> the purchase of the field and of the cave which &#91;is&#93; in it, &#91;is&#93; from Sons of Heth.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 49:29; Genesis 25:8; Genesis 35:29"> And Jacob finisheth commanding his sons, and gathereth up his feet unto the bed, and expireth, and is gathered unto his people.</span>
