<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 11:8; Nehemiah 9:7; Acts 7:2–6"> And Jehovah saith unto Abram, 'Go for thyself, from thy land, and from thy kindred, and from the house of thy father, unto the land which I shew thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:9; Genesis 18:18; Galatians 3:14"> And I make thee become a great nation, and bless thee, and make thy name great; and be thou a blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 3:8; Genesis 22:18; Genesis 27:29"> And I bless those blessing thee, and him who is disesteeming thee I curse, and blessed in thee have been all families of the ground.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 11:8; Genesis 11:31; Genesis 11:27"> And Abram goeth on, as Jehovah hath spoken unto him, and Lot goeth with him, and Abram &#91;is&#93; a son of five and seventy years in his going out from Charan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 14:14; Genesis 11:31; Hebrews 11:8–9"> And Abram taketh Sarai his wife, and Lot his brother's son, and all their substance that they have gained, and the persons that they have obtained in Charan; and they go out to go towards the land of Canaan; and they come in to the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 11:9; Deuteronomy 11:30; Genesis 35:4"> And Abram passeth over into the land, unto the place Shechem, unto the oak of Moreh; and the Canaanite &#91;is&#93; then in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Genesis 18:1; Genesis 13:15"> And Jehovah appeareth unto Abram, and saith, 'To thy seed I give this land;' and he buildeth there an altar to Jehovah, who hath appeared unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:31; Genesis 4:26; Genesis 21:33"> And he removeth from thence towards a mountain at the east of Beth-El, and stretcheth out the tent (Beth-El at the west, and Hai at the east), and he buildeth there an altar to Jehovah, and preacheth in the name of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 13:3; Genesis 24:62; Genesis 13:1"> And Abram journeyeth, going on and journeying towards the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 43:1; Genesis 26:1–3; 1 Kings 17:1–18"> And there is a famine in the land, and Abram goeth down towards Egypt to sojourn there, for the famine &#91;is&#93; grievous in the land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 26:7; Genesis 29:17; Genesis 12:14"> and it cometh to pass as he hath drawn near to enter Egypt, that he saith unto Sarai his wife, 'Lo, I pray thee, I have known that thou &#91;art&#93; a woman of beautiful appearance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 20:11; Genesis 26:7; 1 John 1:8–10"> and it hath come to pass that the Egyptians see thee, and they have said, 'This &#91;is&#93; his wife,' and they have slain me, and thee they keep alive:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 26:7; Genesis 20:12–13; Genesis 20:2"> say, I pray thee, thou &#91;art&#93; my sister, so that it is well with me because of thee, and my soul hath lived for thy sake.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 6:2; Matthew 5:28; Genesis 39:7"> And it cometh to pass, at the entering of Abram into Egypt, that the Egyptians see the woman that she &#91;is&#93; exceeding fair;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 20:2; Genesis 41:1; 2 Kings 18:21"> and princes of Pharaoh see her, and praise her unto Pharaoh, and the woman is taken &#91;to&#93; Pharaoh's house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 20:14; Genesis 13:2; Genesis 24:35"> and to Abram he hath done good because of her, and he hath sheep and oxen, and he-asses, and men-servants, and handmaids, and she-asses, and camels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:21; Genesis 20:18; Psalms 105:14–15"> And Jehovah plagueth Pharaoh and his house — great plagues — for the matter of Sarai, Abram's wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 20:9–10; Genesis 26:9–11; 1 Samuel 14:43"> And Pharaoh calleth for Abram, and saith, 'What &#91;is&#93; this thou hast done to me? why hast thou not declared to me that she &#91;is&#93; thy wife?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Why hast thou said, She &#91;is&#93; my sister, and I take her to myself for a wife? and now, lo, thy wife, take and go.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 105:14–15; Proverbs 21:1; Exodus 18:27"> And Pharaoh chargeth men concerning him, and they send him away, and his wife, an all that he hath.</span>
