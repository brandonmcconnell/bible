<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 1:28"> And it cometh to pass that mankind have begun to multiply on the face of the ground, and daughters have been born to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 82:6–7; 1 John 2:16; 2 Peter 2:14"> and sons of God see the daughters of men that they &#91;are&#93; fair, and they take to themselves women of all whom they have chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:30; Isaiah 63:10; Jeremiah 11:11"> And Jehovah saith, 'My Spirit doth not strive in man — to the age; in their erring they &#91;are&#93; flesh:' and his days have been an hundred and twenty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 13:33; 2 Samuel 21:15–22; 1 Samuel 17:4"> The fallen ones were in the earth in those days, and even afterwards when sons of God come in unto daughters of men, and they have borne to them — they &#91;are&#93; the heroes, who, from of old, &#91;are&#93; the men of name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 15:19; Romans 1:28–31; Mark 7:21–23"> And Jehovah seeth that abundant &#91;is&#93; the wickedness of man in the earth, and every imagination of the thoughts of his heart only evil all the day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 63:10; 1 Samuel 15:11; Ephesians 4:30"> and Jehovah repenteth that He hath made man in the earth, and He grieveth Himself — unto His heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:3; Psalms 37:20; Jeremiah 12:3–4"> And Jehovah saith, 'I wipe away man whom I have prepared from off the face of the ground, from man unto beast, unto creeping thing, and unto fowl of the heavens, for I have repented that I have made them.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 19:19; Hebrews 4:16; Luke 1:30"> And Noah found grace in the eyes of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 1:6; Hebrews 11:7; 2 Peter 2:5"> These &#91;are&#93; births of Noah: Noah &#91;is&#93; a righteous man; perfect he hath been among his generations; with God hath Noah walked habitually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 5:32"> And Noah begetteth three sons, Shem, Ham, and Japheth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:17; Hosea 4:1–2; Habakkuk 2:8"> And the earth is corrupt before God, and the earth is filled &#91;with&#93; violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 53:2–3; Proverbs 15:3; Psalms 14:1–3"> And God seeth the earth, and lo, it hath been corrupted, for all flesh hath corrupted its way on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 4:1–2; 1 Peter 4:7; Ezekiel 7:2–6"> And God said to Noah, 'An end of all flesh hath come before Me, for the earth hath been full of violence from their presence; and lo, I am destroying them with the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 3:20; Luke 17:27; Matthew 24:38"> 'Make for thyself an ark of gopher-wood; rooms dost thou make with the ark, and thou hast covered it within and without with cypress;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:11; Genesis 7:20"> and this &#91;is&#93; that which thou dost with it: three hundred cubits &#91;is&#93; the length of the ark, fifty cubits its breadth, and thirty cubits its height;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 8:6; Genesis 7:16; Ezekiel 41:16"> a window dost thou make for the ark, and unto a cubit thou dost restrain it from above; and the opening of the ark thou dost put in its side, — lower, second, and third &#91;stories&#93; dost thou make it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; 2 Peter 2:5; Genesis 7:21–23"> 'And I, lo, I am bringing in the deluge of waters on the earth to destroy all flesh, in which &#91;is&#93; a living spirit, from under the heavens; all that &#91;is&#93; in the earth doth expire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 11:7; Genesis 7:7; Genesis 17:7"> 'And I have established My covenant with thee, and thou hast come in unto the ark, thou, and thy sons, and thy wife, and thy son's wives with thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 7:15–16; Genesis 7:8–9; Genesis 8:17"> and of all that liveth, of all flesh, two of every &#91;sort&#93; thou dost bring in unto the ark, to keep alive with thee; male and female are they.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 10:11–12; Genesis 2:19; Genesis 7:8–16"> Of the fowl after its kind, and of the cattle after their kind, of every creeping thing of the ground after its kind, two of every &#91;sort&#93; they come in unto thee, to keep alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 1:29–30; Psalms 145:16; Psalms 136:25"> 'And thou, take to thyself of all food that is eaten; and thou hast gathered unto thyself, and it hath been to thee and to them for food.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 40:16; John 15:14; Matthew 7:24–27"> And Noah doth according to all that God hath commanded him; so hath he done.</span>
