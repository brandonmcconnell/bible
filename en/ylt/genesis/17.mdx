<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Ephesians 3:20; Luke 1:6"> And Abram is a son of ninety and nine years, and Jehovah appeareth unto Abram, and saith unto him, 'I &#91;am&#93; God Almighty, walk habitually before Me, and be thou perfect;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 17:4–6; Genesis 12:2; Psalms 105:8–11"> and I give My covenant between Me and thee, and multiply thee very exceedingly.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 13:20; Genesis 17:17; Numbers 16:45"> And Abram falleth upon his face, and God speaketh with him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 35:11; Galatians 3:28–29; Genesis 12:2"> 'I — lo, My covenant &#91;is&#93; with thee, and thou hast become father of a multitude of nations;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 1:21–23; Nehemiah 9:7; Genesis 17:15"> and thy name is no more called Abram, but thy name hath been Abraham, for father of a multitude of nations have I made thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 17:4; Matthew 1:6–17; Genesis 35:11"> and I have made thee exceeding fruitful, and made thee become nations, and kings go out from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 9:7–9; Acts 2:39; Genesis 26:24"> 'And I have established My covenant between Me and thee, and thy seed after thee, to their generations, for a covenant age-during, to become God to thee, and to thy seed after thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 26:12; Psalms 105:11; Exodus 6:7"> and I have given to thee, and to thy seed after thee, the land of thy sojournings, the whole land of Canaan, for a possession age-during, and I have become their God.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 56:4–5; Psalms 25:10; Psalms 103:18"> And God saith unto Abraham, 'And thou dost keep My covenant, thou and thy seed after thee, to their generations;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 2:11–12; Romans 4:9–11; Acts 7:8"> this &#91;is&#93; My covenant which ye keep between Me and you, and thy seed after thee: Every male of you &#91;is&#93; to be circumcised;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 4:11; Acts 7:8; Deuteronomy 10:16"> and ye have circumcised the flesh of your foreskin, and it hath become a token of a covenant between Me and you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 12:3; Luke 2:21; Exodus 12:48–49"> 'And a son of eight days is circumcised by you; every male to your generations, born in the house, or bought with money from any son of a stranger, who is not of thy seed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 12:44; Genesis 37:36; Nehemiah 5:8"> he is certainly circumcised who &#91;is&#93; born in thine house, or bought with thy money; and My covenant hath become in your flesh a covenant age-during;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 4:24–26; Numbers 15:30–31; Exodus 12:19"> and an uncircumcised one, a male, the flesh of whose foreskin is not circumcised, even that person hath been cut off from his people; My covenant he hath broken.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:25; Genesis 32:28; Genesis 17:5"> And God saith unto Abraham, 'Sarai thy wife — thou dost not call her name Sarai, for Sarah &#91;is&#93; her name;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 35:11; Genesis 17:6; Isaiah 49:23"> and I have blessed her, and have also given to thee a son from her; and I have blessed her, and she hath become nations — kings of peoples are from her.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 11:16; Genesis 21:6; Genesis 18:12"> And Abraham falleth upon his face, and laugheth, and saith in his heart, 'To the son of an hundred years is one born? or doth Sarah — daughter of ninety years — bear?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 2:39; Psalms 41:12; Psalms 4:6"> And Abraham saith unto God, 'O that Ishmael may live before Thee;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 1:13–20; Genesis 21:2–3; 2 Kings 4:16–17"> and God saith, 'Sarah thy wife is certainly bearing a son to thee, and thou hast called his name Isaac, and I have established My covenant with him, for a covenant age-during, to his seed after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 21:18; Genesis 21:13; Genesis 25:12–18"> As to Ishmael, I have heard thee; lo, I have blessed him, and made him fruitful, and multiplied him, very exceedingly; twelve princes doth he beget, and I have made him become a great nation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 21:2–3; Genesis 26:2–5; Galatians 3:29"> and My covenant I establish with Isaac, whom Sarah doth bear to thee at this appointed time in the next year;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 18:33; Genesis 35:9–15; Judges 6:21"> and He finisheth speaking with him, and God goeth up from Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 119:60; Genesis 18:19; Genesis 17:10–14"> And Abraham taketh Ishmael his son, and all those born in his house, and all those bought with his money — every male among the men of Abraham's house — and circumciseth the flesh of their foreskin, in this self-same day, as God hath spoken with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 4:11; Genesis 17:17; Romans 4:19–20"> And Abraham &#91;is&#93; a son of ninety and nine years in the flesh of his foreskin being circumcised;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> and Ishmael his son &#91;is&#93; a son of thirteen years in the flesh of his foreskin being circumcised;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 119:60; Genesis 22:3–4; Genesis 12:4"> in this self-same day hath Abraham been circumcised, and Ishmael his son;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 18:19"> and all the men of his house — born in the house, and bought with money from the son of a stranger — have been circumcised with him.</span>
