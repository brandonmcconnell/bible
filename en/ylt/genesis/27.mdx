<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:2; Genesis 48:10; Genesis 25:23–25"> And it cometh to pass that Isaac &#91;is&#93; aged, and his eyes are too dim for seeing, and he calleth Esau his elder son, and saith unto him, 'My son;' and he saith unto him, 'Here &#91;am&#93; I.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 4:14; Ecclesiastes 9:10; Proverbs 27:1"> And he saith, 'Lo, I pray thee, I have become aged, I have not known the day of my death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 25:27–28; Genesis 10:9; 1 Corinthians 6:12"> and now, take up, I pray thee, thy instruments, thy quiver, and thy bow, and go out to the field, and hunt for me provision,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 11:20; Genesis 48:9; Genesis 49:28"> and make for me tasteful things, &#91;such&#93; as I have loved, and bring in to me, and I do eat, so that my soul doth bless thee before I die.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And Rebekah is hearkening while Isaac is speaking unto Esau his son; and Esau goeth to the field to hunt provision — to bring in;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 25:28"> and Rebekah hath spoken unto Jacob her son, saying, 'Lo, I have heard thy father speaking unto Esau thy brother, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 6:26; 1 Samuel 24:19; Deuteronomy 33:1"> Bring for me provision, and make for me tasteful things, and I do eat, and bless thee before Jehovah before my death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1; Genesis 27:13; Acts 5:29"> 'And now, my son, hearken to my voice, to that which I am commanding thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 13:15; 1 Samuel 16:20"> Go, I pray thee, unto the flock, and take for me from thence two good kids of the goats, and I make them tasteful things for thy father, &#91;such&#93; as he hath loved;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> and thou hast taken in to thy father, and he hath eaten, so that his soul doth bless thee before his death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 25:25"> And Jacob saith unto Rebekah his mother, 'Lo, Esau my brother &#91;is&#93; a hairy man, and I a smooth man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:18; Genesis 25:27; Malachi 1:14"> it may be my father doth feel me, and I have been in his eyes as a deceiver, and have brought upon me disesteem, and not a blessing;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; 1 Samuel 25:24; 2 Samuel 14:9"> and his mother saith to him, 'On me thy disesteem, my son; only hearken to my voice, and go, take for me.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 27:9; Proverbs 23:2–3; Genesis 25:28"> And he goeth, and taketh, and bringeth to his mother, and his mother maketh tasteful things, &#91;such&#93; as his father hath loved;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 27:27; Luke 20:46; Luke 15:22"> and Rebekah taketh the desirable garments of Esau her elder son, which &#91;are&#93; with her in the house, and doth put on Jacob her younger son;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> and the skins of the kids of the goats she hath put on his hands, and on the smooth of his neck,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> and she giveth the tasteful things, and the bread which she hath made, into the hand of Jacob her son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And he cometh in unto his father, and saith, 'My father;' and he saith, 'Here &#91;am&#93; I; who &#91;art&#93; thou, my son?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 28:15; Matthew 26:70–74; Zechariah 13:3–4"> And Jacob saith unto his father, 'I &#91;am&#93; Esau thy first-born; I have done as thou hast spoken unto me; rise, I pray thee, sit, and eat of my provision, so that thy soul doth bless me.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:7; Job 13:7; Genesis 24:12"> And Isaac saith unto his son, 'What &#91;is&#93; this thou hast hasted to find, my son?' and he saith, 'That which Jehovah thy God hath caused to come before me.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="James 4:8; Isaiah 57:19; Genesis 27:12"> And Isaac saith unto Jacob, 'Come nigh, I pray thee, and I feel thee, my son, whether thou &#91;art&#93; he, my son Esau, or not.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And Jacob cometh nigh unto Isaac his father, and he feeleth him, and saith, 'The voice &#91;is&#93; the voice of Jacob, and the hands hands of Esau.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 27:16; Hebrews 11:20; Romans 9:11–12"> And he hath not discerned him, for his hands have been hairy, as the hands of Esau his brother, and he blesseth him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Colossians 3:9; Ephesians 4:25; Proverbs 12:22"> and saith, 'Thou art he — my son Esau?' and he saith, 'I &#91;am&#93;.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 27:4"> And he saith, 'Bring nigh to me, and I do eat of my son's provision, so that my soul doth bless thee;' and he bringeth nigh to him, and he eateth; and he bringeth to him wine, and he drinketh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And Isaac his father saith to him, 'Come nigh, I pray thee, and kiss me, my son;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 6:7; Psalms 65:10; Hebrews 11:20"> and he cometh nigh, and kisseth him, and he smelleth the fragrance of his garments, and blesseth him, and saith, 'See, the fragrance of my son &#91;is&#93; as the fragrance of a field which Jehovah hath blessed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:28; Deuteronomy 33:13; Psalms 133:3"> and God doth give to thee of the dew of heaven, and of the fatness of the earth, and abundance of corn and wine;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Numbers 24:9; Isaiah 49:23"> peoples serve thee, and nations bow themselves to thee, be thou mighty over thy brethren, and the sons of thy mother bow themselves to thee; those who curse thee &#91;are&#93; cursed, and those who bless thee &#91;are&#93; blessed.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And it cometh to pass, as Isaac hath finished blessing Jacob, and Jacob is only just going out from the presence of Isaac his father, that Esau his brother hath come in from his hunting;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 27:4"> and he also maketh tasteful things, and bringeth to his father, and saith to his father, 'Let my father arise, and eat of his son's provision, so that thy soul doth bless me.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 27:18"> And Isaac his father saith to him, 'Who &#91;art&#93; thou?' and he saith, 'I &#91;am&#93; thy son, thy first-born, Esau;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 28:3–4; Romans 11:29; Ephesians 1:3"> and Isaac trembleth a very great trembling, and saith, 'Who, now, &#91;is&#93; he who hath provided provision, and bringeth in to me, and I eat of all before thou comest in, and I bless him? — yea, blessed is he.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hebrews 12:17; Proverbs 1:31; 1 Samuel 30:4"> When Esau heareth the words of his father, then he crieth a very great and bitter cry, and saith to his father, 'Bless me, me also, O my father;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:6; Job 13:7; Malachi 2:10"> and he saith, 'Thy brother hath come with subtilty, and taketh thy blessing.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 25:26; John 1:47; Genesis 32:28"> And he saith, 'Is it because &#91;one&#93; called his name Jacob that he doth take me by the heel these two times? my birthright he hath taken; and lo, now, he hath taken my blessing;' he saith also, 'Hast thou not kept back a blessing for me?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:14; Genesis 27:28–29; Genesis 25:23"> And Isaac answereth and saith to Esau, 'Lo, a mighty one have I set him over thee, and all his brethren have I given to him for servants, and &#91;with&#93; corn and wine have I sustained him; and for thee now, what shall I do, my son?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Hebrews 12:17; Genesis 27:34; Isaiah 65:14"> And Esau saith unto his father, 'One blessing hast thou my father? bless me, me also, O my father;' and Esau lifteth up his voice, and weepeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 27:28; Hebrews 11:20; Joshua 24:4"> And Isaac his father answereth and saith unto him, 'Lo, of the fatness of the earth is thy dwelling, and of the dew of the heavens from above;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Kings 8:20–22; Genesis 25:23; 2 Chronicles 25:11–12"> and by thy sword dost thou live, and thy brother dost thou serve; and it hath come to pass when thou rulest, that thou hast broken his yoke from off thy neck.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 John 3:12–15; Genesis 37:4; Genesis 50:3–4"> And Esau hateth Jacob, because of the blessing with which his father blessed him, and Esau saith in his heart, 'The days of mourning &#91;for&#93; my father draw near, and I slay Jacob my brother.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Genesis 37:18–20; 1 Samuel 30:5; Genesis 42:21–22"> And the words of Esau her elder son are declared to Rebekah, and she sendeth and calleth for Jacob her younger son, and saith unto him, 'Lo, Esau thy brother is comforting himself in regard to thee — to slay thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Genesis 27:8; Genesis 11:31; Genesis 27:13"> and now, my son, hearken to my voice, and rise, flee for thyself unto Laban my brother, to Haran,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Genesis 31:38; Genesis 31:41"> and thou hast dwelt with him some days, till thy brother's fury turn back,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Genesis 4:8–16; Genesis 9:5–6"> till thy brother's anger turn back from thee, and he hath forgotten that which thou hast done to him, and I have sent and taken thee from thence; why am I bereaved even of you both the same day?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Genesis 24:3; Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 28:8"> And Rebekah saith unto Isaac, 'I have been disgusted with my life because of the presence of the daughters of Heth; if Jacob take a wife of the daughters of Heth, like these — from the daughters of the land — why do I live?'</span>
