<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 7:12; Joshua 7:10; 2 Kings 8:3–4"> And Jacob seeth that there is corn in Egypt, and Jacob saith to his sons, 'Why do you look at each other?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 43:8; Genesis 45:9; Genesis 43:2"> he saith also, 'Lo, I have heard that there is corn in Egypt, go down thither, and buy for us from thence, and we live and do not die;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 42:5; Genesis 42:13"> and the ten brethren of Joseph go down to buy corn in Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 42:38; Genesis 43:29; Genesis 11:4"> and Benjamin, Joseph's brother, Jacob hath not sent with his brethren, for he said, 'Lest mischief meet him.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 41:57; Acts 7:11; Genesis 12:10"> And the sons of Israel come to buy in the midst of those coming, for the famine hath been in the land of Canaan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 3:9; Genesis 37:7–10; Genesis 41:40–41"> and Joseph is the ruler over the land, he who is selling to all the people of the land, and Joseph's brethren come and bow themselves to him — face to the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 42:30; Genesis 42:14–17; Matthew 15:23–26"> And Joseph seeth his brethren, and discerneth them, and maketh himself strange unto them, and speaketh with them sharp things, and saith unto them, 'From whence have ye come?' and they say, 'From the land of Canaan — to buy food.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 37:2; John 20:14; Luke 24:16"> And Joseph discerneth his brethren, but they have not discerned him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 37:5–9; Exodus 32:35; Joshua 2:1"> and Joseph remembereth the dreams which he dreamed of them, and saith unto them, 'Ye &#91;are&#93; spies; to see the nakedness of the land ye have come.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 37:8; 1 Samuel 26:17; Genesis 27:37"> And they say unto him, 'No, my lord, but thy servants have come to buy food;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 42:19; 2 Corinthians 6:4; John 7:18"> we &#91;are&#93; all of us sons of one man, we &#91;are&#93; right men; thy servants have not been spies;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and he saith unto them, 'No, but the nakedness of the land ye have come to see;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 37:30; Genesis 42:32; Genesis 44:20"> and they say, 'Thy servants &#91;are&#93; twelve brethren; we &#91;are&#93; sons of one man in the land of Canaan, and lo, the young one &#91;is&#93; with our father to-day, and the one is not.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 15:21–28; Job 13:24; Genesis 42:9–11"> And Joseph saith unto them, 'This &#91;is&#93; that which I have spoken unto you, saying, Ye &#91;are&#93; spies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:55; Jeremiah 5:7; Genesis 42:16"> by this ye are proved: Pharaoh liveth! if ye go out from this — except by your young brother coming hither;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 42:11"> send one of you, and let him bring your brother, and ye, remain ye bound, and let your words be proved, whether truth be with you: and if not — Pharaoh liveth! surely ye &#91;are&#93; spies;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 40:4; Hebrews 12:10; Genesis 40:7"> and he removeth them unto charge three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 25:43; Nehemiah 5:15; Luke 18:2"> And Joseph saith unto them on the third day, 'This do and live; God I fear!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 45:23; Isaiah 42:7; Genesis 40:3"> if ye &#91;are&#93; right men, let one of your brethren be bound in the house of your ward, and ye, go, carry in corn &#91;for&#93; the famine of your houses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 44:23; Genesis 43:5; Genesis 42:34"> and your young brother ye bring unto me, and your words are established, and ye die not;' and they do so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hosea 5:15; Mark 9:43–45; Genesis 37:23–28"> And they say one unto another, 'Verily we &#91;are&#93; guilty concerning our brother, because we saw the distress of his soul, in his making supplication unto us, and we did not hearken: therefore hath this distress come upon us.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:22; Psalms 9:12; 1 Kings 2:32"> And Reuben answereth them, saying, 'Spake I not unto you, saying, Sin not against the lad? and ye hearkened not; and his blood also, lo, it is required.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:20; John 16:13–14"> And they have not known that Joseph understandeth, for the interpreter &#91;is&#93; between them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 43:30; Jude 1:22–23; Hebrews 4:15"> and he turneth round from them, and weepeth, and turneth back unto them, and speaketh unto them, and taketh from them Simeon, and bindeth him before their eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Romans 12:17–21; Genesis 45:21"> And Joseph commandeth, and they fill their vessels &#91;with&#93; corn, also to put back the money of each unto his sack, and to give to them provision for the way; and one doth to them so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And they lift up their corn upon their asses, and go from thence,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 4:24; Luke 2:7; Luke 10:34"> and the one openeth his sack to give provender to his ass at a lodging-place, and he seeth his money, and lo, it &#91;is&#93; in the mouth of his bag,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Lamentations 3:37; Genesis 42:36"> and he saith unto his brethren, 'My money hath been put back, and also, lo, in my bag:' and their heart goeth out, and they tremble, one to another saying, 'What &#91;is&#93; this God hath done to us!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And they come in unto Jacob their father, to the land of Canaan, and they declare to him all the things meeting them, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 42:7"> 'The man, the lord of the land, hath spoken with us sharp things, and maketh us as spies of the land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 42:11"> and we say unto him, We &#91;are&#93; right men, we have not been spies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> we &#91;are&#93; twelve brethren, sons of our father, the one is not, and the young one &#91;is&#93; to-day with our father in the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 42:19–20; Genesis 42:15"> 'And the man, the lord of the land, saith unto us, By this I know that ye &#91;are&#93; right men — one of your brethren leave with me, and &#91;for&#93; the famine of your houses take ye and go,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 34:10; Genesis 34:21; 1 Kings 10:15"> and bring your young brother unto me, and I know that ye &#91;are&#93; not spies, but ye &#91;are&#93; right men; your brother I give to you, and ye trade with the land.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 43:21; Genesis 43:12; Genesis 43:15"> And it cometh to pass, they are emptying their sacks, and lo, the bundle of each man's silver &#91;is&#93; in his sack, and they see their bundles of silver, they and their father, and are afraid;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 43:14; Romans 8:31; Isaiah 27:9"> and Jacob their father saith unto them, 'Me ye have bereaved; Joseph is not, and Simeon is not, and Benjamin ye take — against me have been all these.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Genesis 46:9; Micah 6:7; Genesis 43:9"> And Reuben speaketh unto his father, saying, 'My two sons thou dost put to death, if I bring him not in unto thee; give him into my hand, and I — I bring him back unto thee;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 37:35; Genesis 42:4; Genesis 37:33"> and he saith, 'My son doth not go down with you, for his brother &#91;is&#93; dead, and he by himself is left; when mischief hath met him in the way in which ye go, then ye have brought down my grey hairs in sorrow to sheol.'</span>
