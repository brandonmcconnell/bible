<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:1; 2 Kings 4:8; Proverbs 13:20"> And it cometh to pass, at that time, that Judah goeth down from his brethren, and turneth aside unto a man, an Adullamite, whose name &#91;is&#93; Hirah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:3; Genesis 24:3; Genesis 6:2"> and Judah seeth there the daughter of a man, a Canaanite, whose name &#91;is&#93; Shuah, and taketh her, and goeth in unto her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Numbers 26:19"> And she conceiveth, and beareth a son, and he calleth his name Er;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Numbers 26:19"> and she conceiveth again, and beareth a son, and calleth his name Onan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 26:20; Genesis 38:26; Genesis 46:12"> and she addeth again, and beareth a son, and calleth his name Shelah; and he was in Chezib in her bearing him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 24:3; Genesis 21:21; Matthew 1:3"> And Judah taketh a wife for Er, his first-born, and her name &#91;is&#93; Tamar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:3; Genesis 46:12; Numbers 26:19"> and Er, Judah's first-born, is evil in the eyes of Jehovah, and Jehovah doth put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Matthew 22:23–27; Ruth 4:5–11"> And Judah saith to Onan, 'Go in unto the wife of thy brother, and marry her, and raise up seed to thy brother;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Titus 3:3; Deuteronomy 25:6; James 3:16"> and Onan knoweth that the seed is not &#91;reckoned&#93; his; and it hath come to pass, if he hath gone in unto his brother's wife, that he hath destroyed &#91;it&#93; to the earth, so as not to give seed to his brother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Numbers 11:1; 1 Chronicles 21:7"> and that which he hath done is evil in the eyes of Jehovah, and He putteth him also to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 22:13; Ruth 1:11–13"> And Judah saith to Tamar his daughter-in-law, 'Abide a widow at thy father's house, till Shelah my son groweth up;' for he said, 'Lest he die — even he — like his brethren;' and Tamar goeth and dwelleth at her father's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 15:10; 2 Samuel 13:39; Judges 14:1"> And the days are multiplied, and the daughter of Shuah, Judah's wife, dieth; and Judah is comforted, and goeth up unto his sheep-shearers, he and Hirah his friend the Adullamite, to Timnath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And it is declared to Tamar, saying, 'Lo, thy husband's father is going up to Timnath to shear his flock;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 38:26; Genesis 38:11–13; Jeremiah 3:2"> and she turneth aside the garments of her widowhood from off her, and covereth herself with a vail, and wrappeth herself up, and sitteth in the opening of Enayim, which &#91;is&#93; by the way to Timnath, for she hath seen that Shelah hath grown up, and she hath not been given to him for a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Judah seeth her, and reckoneth her for a harlot, for she hath covered her face,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:10; Matthew 26:15; 2 Samuel 13:11"> and he turneth aside unto her by the way, and saith, 'Come, I pray thee, let me come in unto thee,' (for he hath not known that she &#91;is&#93; his daughter-in-law); and she saith, 'What dost thou give to me, that thou mayest come in unto me?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:33; Genesis 38:20; Proverbs 20:16"> and he saith, 'I — I send a kid of the goats from the flock.' And she saith, 'Dost thou give a pledge till thou send &#91;it&#93;?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 38:25–26; Hosea 4:11; Jeremiah 22:24"> and he saith, 'What &#91;is&#93; the pledge that I give to thee?' and she saith, 'Thy seal, and thy ribbon, and thy staff which &#91;is&#93; in thy hand;' and he giveth to her, and goeth in unto her, and she conceiveth to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 38:14; 2 Samuel 14:5; 2 Samuel 14:2"> and she riseth, and goeth, and turneth aside her vail from off her, and putteth on the garments of her widowhood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 20:9; Leviticus 19:17; 2 Samuel 13:3"> And Judah sendeth the kid of the goats by the hand of his friend the Adullamite, to receive the pledge from the hand of the woman, and he hath not found her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And he asketh the men of her place, saying, 'Where &#91;is&#93; the separated one — she in Enayim, by the way?' and they say, 'There hath not been in this &#91;place&#93; a separated one.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And he turneth back unto Judah, and saith, 'I have not found her; and the men of the place also have said, There hath not been in this &#91;place&#93; a separated one,'</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ephesians 5:12; Proverbs 6:33; Revelation 16:15"> and Judah saith, 'Let her take to herself, lest we become despised; lo, I sent this kid, and thou hast not found her.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 21:9; Ezekiel 23:19; Judges 19:2"> And it cometh to pass about three months &#91;after&#93;, that it is declared to Judah, saying, 'Tamar thy daughter-in-law hath committed fornication; and also, lo, she hath conceived by fornication:' and Judah saith, 'Bring her out — and she is burnt.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 37:32; Genesis 38:18; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> She is brought out, and she hath sent unto her husband's father, saying, 'To a man whose these &#91;are&#93;, I &#91;am&#93; pregnant;' and she saith, 'Discern, I pray thee, whose &#91;are&#93; these — the seal, and the ribbons, and the staff.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:17; John 8:9; Titus 2:11–12"> And Judah discerneth and saith, 'She hath been more righteous than I, because that I did not give her to Shelah my son;' and he hath not added to know her again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 25:24"> And it cometh to pass in the time of her bearing, that lo, twins &#91;are&#93; in her womb;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and it cometh to pass in her bearing, that &#91;one&#93; giveth out a hand, and the midwife taketh and bindeth on his hand a scarlet thread, saying, 'This hath come out first.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:4; Matthew 1:3; Genesis 46:12"> And it cometh to pass as he draweth back his hand, that lo, his brother hath come out, and she saith, 'What! thou hast broken forth — on thee &#91;is&#93; the breach;' and he calleth his name Pharez;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:6; 1 Chronicles 2:4; Matthew 1:3"> and afterwards hath his brother come out, on whose hand &#91;is&#93; the scarlet thread, and he calleth his name Zarah.</span>
