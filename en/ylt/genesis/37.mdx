<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Genesis 23:4; Genesis 36:7"> And Jacob dwelleth in the land of his father's sojournings — in the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 35:25–26; Genesis 35:22; 1 Samuel 2:22–24"> These &#91;are&#93; births of Jacob: Joseph, a son of seventeen years, hath been enjoying himself with his brethren among the flock, (and he &#91;is&#93; a youth,) with the sons of Bilhah, and with the sons of Zilpah, his father's wives, and Joseph bringeth in an account of their evil unto their father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 3:35; Genesis 37:32; 2 Samuel 13:18"> And Israel hath loved Joseph more than any of his sons, for he &#91;is&#93; a son of his old age, and hath made for him a long coat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 27:41; 1 John 3:12; 1 John 4:20"> and his brethren see that their father hath loved him more than any of his brethren, and they hate him, and have not been able to speak &#91;to&#93; him peaceably.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 28:12; Genesis 42:9; John 17:14"> And Joseph dreameth a dream, and declareth to his brethren, and they add still more to hate him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 44:18; Judges 9:7"> And he saith unto them, 'Hear ye, I pray you, this dream which I have dreamed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 44:14; Genesis 42:6; Genesis 43:26"> that, lo, we are binding bundles in the midst of the field, and lo, my bundle hath arisen, and hath also stood up, and lo, your bundles are round about, and bow themselves to my bundle.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:27; Acts 7:35; Hebrews 10:29"> And his brethren say to him, 'Dost thou certainly reign over us? dost thou certainly rule over us?' and they add still more to hate him, for his dreams, and for his words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 7:9–14; Genesis 37:7; Daniel 8:10"> And he dreameth yet another dream, and recounteth it to his brethren, and saith, 'Lo, I have dreamed a dream again, and lo, the sun and the moon, and eleven stars, are bowing themselves to me.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 27:29; Philippians 2:10–11; Isaiah 60:14"> And he recounteth unto his father, and unto his brethren; and his father pusheth against him, and saith to him, 'What &#91;is&#93; this dream which thou hast dreamt? do we certainly come — I, and thy mother, and thy brethren — to bow ourselves to thee, to the earth?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 7:9; Luke 2:19; Luke 2:51"> and his brethren are zealous against him, and his father hath watched the matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 37:1; Genesis 33:18; Genesis 34:25–31"> And his brethren go to feed the flock of their father in Shechem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1–3; 1 Samuel 3:8; 1 Samuel 3:16"> and Israel saith unto Joseph, 'Are not thy brethren feeding in Shechem? come, and I send thee unto them;' and he saith to him, 'Here &#91;am&#93; I;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 35:27; Luke 19:42; Psalms 125:5"> and he saith to him, 'Go, I pray thee, see the peace of thy brethren, and the peace of the flock, and bring me back word;' and he sendeth him from the valley of Hebron, and he cometh to Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 4:27; Judges 4:22; Genesis 21:14"> And a man findeth him, and lo, he is wandering in the field, and the man asketh him, saying, 'What seekest thou?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:10; Song of Solomon 1:7"> and he saith, 'My brethren I am seeking, declare to me, I pray thee, where they are feeding?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 6:13"> And the man saith, 'They have journeyed from this, for I have heard some saying, Let us go to Dothan,' and Joseph goeth after his brethren, and findeth them in Dothan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 23:12; Psalms 37:12; Psalms 37:32"> And they see him from afar, even before he draweth near unto them, and they conspire against him to put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 28:12; Genesis 37:11; Genesis 37:5"> And they say one unto another, 'Lo, this man of the dreams cometh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Titus 3:3; Proverbs 27:4; Matthew 2:2–16"> and now, come, and we slay him, and cast him into one of the pits, and have said, An evil beast hath devoured him; and we see what his dreams are.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 42:22; Genesis 35:22; Joshua 10:28"> And Reuben heareth, and delivereth him out of their hand, and saith, 'Let us not smite the life;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 22:12; Deuteronomy 13:9; Genesis 42:22"> and Reuben saith unto them, 'Shed no blood; cast him into this pit which &#91;is&#93; in the wilderness, and put not forth a hand upon him,' — in order to deliver him out of their hand, to bring him back unto his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 37:3; Matthew 27:28; Genesis 42:21"> And it cometh to pass, when Joseph hath come unto his brethren, that they strip Joseph of his coat, the long coat which &#91;is&#93; upon him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:6; Psalms 130:1–2; Zechariah 9:11"> and take him and cast him into the pit, and the pit &#91;is&#93; empty, there is no water in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 37:28; Genesis 43:11; Jeremiah 8:22"> And they sit down to eat bread, and they lift up their eyes, and look, and lo, a company of Ishmaelites coming from Gilead, and their camels bearing spices, and balm, and myrrh, going to take &#91;them&#93; down to Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 37:20; Genesis 4:10; Genesis 25:32"> And Judah saith unto his brethren, 'What gain when we slay our brother, and have concealed his blood?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; Genesis 42:21; 1 Samuel 18:17"> Come, and we sell him to the Ishmaelites, and our hands are not on him, for he &#91;is&#93; our brother — our flesh;' and his brethren hearken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 7:9; Psalms 105:17; Genesis 45:4–5"> And Midianite merchantmen pass by and they draw out and bring up Joseph out of the pit, and sell Joseph to the Ishmaelites for twenty silverlings, and they bring Joseph into Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 1:20; Genesis 37:34; Genesis 44:13"> And Reuben returneth unto the pit, and lo, Joseph is not in the pit, and he rendeth his garments,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 42:13; Jeremiah 31:15; Genesis 42:32"> and he returneth unto his brethren, and saith, 'The lad is not, and I — whither am I going?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 37:23; Proverbs 28:13; Genesis 37:3"> And they take the coat of Joseph, and slaughter a kid of the goats, and dip the coat in the blood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 15:30; Genesis 37:3; Genesis 44:20–23"> and send the long coat, and they bring &#91;it&#93; in unto their father, and say, 'This have we found; discern, we pray thee, whether it &#91;is&#93; thy son's coat or not?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 37:20; Genesis 44:28; John 13:7"> And he discerneth it, and saith, 'My son's coat! an evil beast hath devoured him; torn — torn is Joseph!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 37:29; 2 Samuel 3:31; Psalms 69:11"> And Jacob rendeth his raiment, and putteth sackcloth on his loins, and becometh a mourner for his son many days,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 42:38; Job 2:11; 2 Samuel 12:17"> and all his sons and all his daughters rise to comfort him, and he refuseth to comfort himself, and saith, 'For — I go down mourning unto my son, to Sheol,' and his father weepeth for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 40:4; Genesis 37:28; 2 Kings 25:8"> And the Medanites have sold him unto Egypt, to Potiphar, a eunuch of Pharaoh, head of the executioners.</span>
</div>
