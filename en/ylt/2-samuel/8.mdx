<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 18:1–17; 2 Samuel 7:9; 2 Samuel 21:15–22"> And it cometh to pass afterwards that David smiteth the Philistines, and humbleth them, and David taketh the bridle of the metropolis out of the hand of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 24:17; 1 Samuel 10:27; 2 Samuel 8:6"> And he smiteth Moab, and measureth them with a line, causing them to lie down on the earth, and he measureth two lines to put to death, and the fulness of the line to keep alive, and the Moabites are to David for servants, bearers of a present.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:47; 2 Samuel 10:16; 2 Samuel 10:19"> And David smiteth Hadadezer son of Rehob, king of Zobah, in his going to bring back his power by the River &#91;Euphrates;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 11:6; Joshua 11:9; 1 Chronicles 18:4"> and David captureth from him a thousand and seven hundred horsemen, and twenty thousand footmen, and David destroyeth utterly the whole of the charioteers, only he leaveth of them a hundred charioteers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 11:23–25; Isaiah 7:8; Job 9:13"> And Aram of Damascus cometh to give help to Hadadezer king of Zobah, and David smiteth of Aram twenty and two thousand men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:14; 2 Samuel 7:9; Psalms 18:34–46"> and David putteth garrisons in Aram of Damascus, and Aram is to David for a servant, bearing a present; and Jehovah saveth David whithersoever he hath gone;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:15–16; 1 Kings 14:26–27; 1 Kings 10:16–17"> and David taketh the shields of gold which were on the servants of Hadadezer, and bringeth them to Jerusalem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 18:8; 1 Chronicles 22:16; Ezekiel 47:16"> and from Betah, and from Berothai, cities of Hadadezer, hath king David taken very much brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 8:65; 2 Chronicles 8:4; 1 Chronicles 18:9"> And Toi king of Hamath heareth that David hath smitten all the force of Hadadezer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:10; 1 Chronicles 18:10; Psalms 129:8"> and Toi sendeth Joram his son unto king David to ask of him of welfare, and to bless him, (because that he hath fought against Hadadezer, and smiteth him, for a man of wars &#91;with&#93; Toi had Hadadezer been), and in his hand have been vessels of silver, and vessels of gold, and vessels of brass,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 7:51; 1 Chronicles 29:2; 1 Chronicles 22:14–16"> also them did king David sanctify to Jehovah, with the silver and the gold which he sanctified of all the nations which he subdued:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:14; 2 Samuel 8:2; 1 Chronicles 18:11"> of Aram, and of Moab, and of the Bene-Ammon, and of the Philistines, and of Amalek, and of the spoil of Hadadezer son of Rehob king of Zobah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 14:7; 2 Samuel 7:9; 1 Chronicles 18:12"> And David maketh a name in his turning back from his smiting Aram in the valley of Salt — eighteen thousand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 27:29; Genesis 25:23; 2 Samuel 8:6"> and he putteth in Edom garrisons — in all Edom he hath put garrisons, and all Edom are servants to David; and Jehovah saveth David whithersoever he hath gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Amos 5:15; Psalms 89:14; Psalms 72:2"> And David reigneth over all Israel, and David is doing judgment and righteousness to all his people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 4:3; 1 Chronicles 11:6; 2 Samuel 19:13"> and Joab son of Zeruiah &#91;is&#93; over the host, and Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud &#91;is&#93; remembrancer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:8; 1 Chronicles 18:16; 1 Chronicles 24:3–4"> and Zadok son of Ahitub, and Ahimelech son of Abiathar, &#91;are&#93; priests, and Seraiah &#91;is&#93; scribe,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:14; 2 Samuel 20:7; 2 Samuel 20:23"> and Benaiah son of Jehoiada &#91;is over&#93; both the Cherethite and the Pelethite, and the sons of David have been ministers.</span>
