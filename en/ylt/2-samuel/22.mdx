<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 15:1; Judges 5:1; Isaiah 12:1–6"> And David speaketh to Jehovah the words of this song in the day Jehovah hath delivered him out of the hand of all his enemies, and out of the hand of Saul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 18:2–50; Psalms 31:3; Psalms 71:3"> and he saith: 'Jehovah &#91;is&#93; my rock, And my bulwark, and a deliverer to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Isaiah 12:2; Proverbs 18:10"> My God &#91;is&#93; my rock — I take refuge in Him; My shield, and the horn of my salvation, My high tower, and my refuge! My Saviour, from violence Thou savest me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 18:3; Psalms 48:1; Psalms 96:4"> The Praised One, I call Jehovah: And from mine enemies I am saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jonah 2:3; Psalms 69:14–15; Revelation 17:15"> When the breakers of death compassed me, The streams of the worthless terrify me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 116:3; Proverbs 13:14; Psalms 140:5"> The cords of Sheol have surrounded me, Before me have been the snares of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 18:6; Psalms 116:4; Exodus 3:7"> In mine adversity I call Jehovah, And unto my God I call, And He heareth from His temple my voice, And my cry &#91;is&#93; in His ears,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 26:11; Psalms 97:4; Judges 5:4"> And shake and tremble doth the earth, Foundations of the heavens are troubled, And are shaken, for He hath wrath!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Psalms 18:15; Psalms 18:8"> Gone up hath smoke by His nostrils. And fire from His mouth devoureth, Brands have been kindled by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 8:12; Psalms 97:2; Psalms 144:5"> And He inclineth heaven, and cometh down, And thick darkness &#91;is&#93; under His feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 104:3; Psalms 18:10; 1 Samuel 4:4"> And He rideth on a cherub, and doth fly, And is seen on the wings of the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; 2 Samuel 22:10; Job 36:29"> And He setteth darkness Round about Him — tabernacles, Darkness of waters — thick clouds of the skies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:9"> From the brightness before Him Were brands of fire kindled!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 29:3–9; 1 Samuel 2:10; Job 37:2–5"> Thunder from the heavens doth Jehovah, And the Most High giveth forth His voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:23; Joshua 10:10; Habakkuk 3:11"> And He sendeth forth arrows, And scattereth them; Lightning, and troubleth them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nahum 1:4; Habakkuk 3:8–10; Psalms 114:3–7"> And seen are the streams of the sea, Revealed are foundations of the world, By the rebuke of Jehovah, From the breath of the spirit of His anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Psalms 144:7; Psalms 18:16"> He sendeth from above — He taketh me, He draweth me out of many waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 3:7; 2 Timothy 4:17; Psalms 56:9"> He delivereth me from my strong enemy, From those hating me, For they were stronger than I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 71:20–21; 1 Samuel 23:26–27; Psalms 118:10–13"> They are before me in a day of my calamity, And Jehovah is my support,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 31:8; Psalms 118:5; 2 Samuel 15:26"> And He bringeth me out to a large place, He draweth me out for He delighted in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 24:4; 1 Samuel 26:23; Psalms 7:8"> Jehovah recompenseth me, According to my righteousness, According to the cleanness of my hands, He doth return to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 8:32; Genesis 18:19; Psalms 128:1"> For I have kept the ways of Jehovah, And have not done wickedly against my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 119:30; Psalms 119:102; John 15:14"> For all His judgments &#91;are&#93; before me, As to His statutes, I turn not from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 6:9; Job 1:1; Genesis 17:1"> And I am perfect before Him, And I keep myself from mine iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:21; 2 Corinthians 5:10; Romans 2:7–8"> And Jehovah returneth to me, According to my righteousness, According to my cleanness before His eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; James 2:13"> With the kind Thou shewest Thyself kind, With the perfect man Thou shewest Thyself perfect,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 5:8; Psalms 18:26; Leviticus 26:23–28"> With the pure Thou shewest Thyself pure, And with the perverse Thou shewest Thyself a wrestler.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 72:12–13; Isaiah 5:15; James 4:6–7"> And the poor people Thou dost save, And Thine eyes on the high causest to fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 27:1; John 8:12; Job 29:3"> For Thou &#91;art&#93; my lamp, O Jehovah, And Jehovah doth lighten my darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 18:29; Philippians 4:13; Psalms 118:10–12"> For by Thee I run — a troop, By my God I leap a wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Proverbs 30:5; Psalms 18:30"> God! Perfect &#91;is&#93; His way, The saying of Jehovah is tried, A shield He &#91;is&#93; to all those trusting in Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:2; Deuteronomy 32:31; Jeremiah 10:6–7"> For who is God save Jehovah? And who a Rock save our God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ephesians 6:10; Isaiah 41:10; Philippians 4:13"> God — my bulwark, &#91;my&#93; strength, And He maketh perfect my way;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13; Isaiah 58:14; Habakkuk 3:19"> Making my feet like hinds, And on my high places causeth me to stand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 144:1; Psalms 18:33–34; Ezekiel 39:9–10"> Teaching my hands for battle, And brought down was a bow of brass by mine arms,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ephesians 6:16; Psalms 115:14; Psalms 84:11"> And Thou givest to me the shield of Thy salvation, And Thy lowliness maketh me great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Proverbs 4:12; Psalms 17:5; 1 Samuel 2:9"> Thou enlargest my step under me, And mine ankles have not slidden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Psalms 21:8–9; 2 Samuel 5:18–25; 2 Samuel 10:14"> I pursue mine enemies and destroy them, And I turn not till they are consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Malachi 4:3; Malachi 4:1; Psalms 110:1"> And I consume them, and smite them, And they rise not, and fall under my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Psalms 44:5; Isaiah 60:14; Colossians 1:11"> And Thou girdest me &#91;with&#93; strength for battle, Thou causest my withstanders to bow under me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 23:27; Joshua 10:24; 2 Thessalonians 1:8–9"> And mine enemies — Thou givest to me the neck, Those hating me — and I cut them off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Isaiah 1:15; 1 Samuel 28:6; Micah 3:4"> They look, and there is no saviour; Unto Jehovah, and He hath not answered them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Micah 7:10; Isaiah 10:6; 2 Kings 13:7"> And I beat them as dust of the earth, As mire of the streets I beat them small — I spread them out!</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:1–14; Isaiah 55:5; 2 Samuel 3:1"> And — Thou dost deliver me From the strivings of my people, Thou placest me for a head of nations; A people I have not known do serve me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Psalms 81:15; Psalms 66:3; Isaiah 56:3"> Sons of a stranger feign obedience to me, At the hearing of the ear they hearken to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Micah 7:17; James 1:11; Isaiah 64:6"> Sons of a stranger fade away, And gird themselves by their close places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 89:26; 2 Samuel 22:3; Job 19:25"> Jehovah liveth, and blessed &#91;is&#93; my Rock, And exalted is my God — The Rock of my salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 94:1; Psalms 144:2; 2 Samuel 18:31"> God — who is giving vengeance to me, And bringing down peoples under me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Psalms 140:1; 2 Samuel 7:8–9; Numbers 24:7"> And bringing me forth from mine enemies, Yea, above my withstanders Thou raisest me up. From a man of violence Thou deliverest me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Romans 15:9; Psalms 146:1–2; Psalms 145:1–2"> Therefore I confess Thee, O Jehovah, among nations. And to Thy name I sing praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Psalms 89:29; Psalms 18:50; Psalms 89:20"> Magnifying the salvations of His king, And doing loving-kindness to His anointed, To David, and to his seed — unto the age!'</span>
