<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 6:66–68; Acts 11:23; 2 Samuel 19:15"> And every man of Israel goeth up from after David, after Sheba son of Bichri, and the men of Judah have cleaved to their king, from the Jordan even unto Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:21–22; 2 Samuel 15:16; Genesis 40:3"> And David cometh in unto his house at Jerusalem, and the king taketh the ten women-concubines — whom he had left to keep the house, and putteth them in a house of ward, and sustaineth them, and unto them he hath not gone in, and they are shut up unto the day of their death, in widowhood living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:13; 2 Samuel 17:25; 1 Chronicles 2:17"> And the king saith unto Amasa, 'Call for me the men of Judah &#91;in&#93; three days, and thou, stand here,'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:13; 1 Samuel 13:8"> and Amasa goeth to call Judah, and tarrieth beyond the appointed time that he had appointed him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:17; 2 Samuel 11:11; 1 Kings 1:33"> and David saith unto Abishai, 'Now doth Sheba son of Bichri do evil to us more than Absalom; thou, take the servants of thy lord, and pursue after him, lest he have found for himself fenced cities, and delivered himself &#91;from&#93; our eye.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:18; 1 Kings 1:38; 2 Samuel 20:23"> And the men of Joab go out after him, and the Cherethite, and the Pelethite, and all the mighty men, and they go out from Jerusalem to pursue after Sheba son of Bichri;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:30; 2 Samuel 2:13; 2 Samuel 20:4–5"> they &#91;are&#93; near the great stone that &#91;is&#93; in Gibeon, and Amasa hath gone before them, and Joab &#91;is&#93; girded; his long robe he hath put on him, and upon it a girdle — a sword &#91;is&#93; fastened upon his loins in its sheath; and he hath gone out, and it falleth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 22:47–48; Matthew 26:48–49; Micah 7:2"> And Joab saith to Amasa, 'Art thou &#91;in&#93; peace, my brother?' and the right hand of Joab layeth hold on the beard of Amasa to give a kiss to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:23; 2 Samuel 3:27; Judges 3:21"> and Amasa hath not been watchful of the sword that &#91;is&#93; in the hand of Joab, and he smiteth him with it unto the fifth &#91;rib&#93;, and sheddeth out his bowels to the earth, and he hath not repeated &#91;it&#93; to him, and he dieth; and Joab and Abishai his brother have pursued after Sheba son of Bichri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:13; 2 Samuel 20:21; 2 Kings 9:32"> And a man hath stood by him, of the young men of Joab, and saith, 'He who hath delight in Joab, and he who &#91;is&#93; for David — after Joab!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 24:21–22; Psalms 9:16; 2 Samuel 17:25"> And Amasa is rolling himself in blood, in the midst of the highway, and the man seeth that all the people have stood still, and he bringeth round Amasa out of the highway to the field, and casteth over him a garment, when he hath seen that every one who hath come by him — hath stood still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:12; Isaiah 7:3; 2 Kings 18:17"> When he hath been removed out of the highway, every man hath passed on after Joab, to pursue after Sheba son of Bichri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 15:29; 1 Kings 15:20; 2 Chronicles 16:4"> And he passeth over through all the tribes of Israel to Abel, and to Beth-Maachah, and to all the Berites, and they are assembled, and go in also after him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 19:32; Jeremiah 32:24; 1 Kings 15:20"> and they go in and lay siege against him, in Abel of Beth-Maachah, and cast up a mount against the city, and it standeth in a trench, and all the people who are &#91;are&#93; with Joab are destroying, to cause the wall to fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:2; Ecclesiastes 9:14–18; 1 Samuel 25:32–33"> And a wise woman calleth out of the city, 'Hear, hear; say, I pray you, unto Joab, Come near hither, and I speak unto thee.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:12; 1 Samuel 25:24"> And he cometh near unto her, and the woman saith, 'Art thou Joab?' and he saith, 'I &#91;am&#93;.' And she saith to him, 'Hear the words of thy handmaid;' and he saith, 'I am hearing.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:10–11"> and she speaketh, saying, 'They spake often in former times, saying, Let them diligently ask at Abel, and so they finished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:19; 2 Samuel 21:3; 2 Samuel 17:16"> I &#91;am&#93; of the peaceable — faithful ones of Israel; thou art seeking to destroy a city, and a mother in Israel; why dost thou swallow up the inheritance of Jehovah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:29; Proverbs 28:13; Jeremiah 17:9"> And Joab answereth and saith, 'Far be it — far be it from me; I do not swallow up nor destroy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:9; Judges 2:9; 2 Samuel 17:2–3"> The matter &#91;is&#93; not so; for a man of the hill-country of Ephraim — Sheba son of Bichri his name — hath lifted up his hand against the king, against David; give ye up him by himself, and I go away from the city.' And the woman saith unto Joab, 'Lo, his head is cast unto thee over the wall.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:1; Ecclesiastes 7:19; 2 Samuel 11:6–21"> And the woman cometh unto all the people in her wisdom, and they cut off the head of Sheba son of Bichri, and cast &#91;it&#93; unto Joab, and he bloweth with a trumpet, and they are scattered from the city, each to his tents, and Joab hath turned back to Jerusalem unto the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:16–18; 1 Chronicles 18:15–17; 1 Kings 4:3–6"> And Joab &#91;is&#93; over all the host of Israel, and Benaiah son of Jehoiada &#91;is&#93; over the Cherethite, and over the Pelethite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 4:3; 1 Kings 12:18; 1 Kings 4:6"> and Adoram &#91;is&#93; over the tribute, and Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud &#91;is&#93; the remembrancer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 4:4; 2 Samuel 8:17; 1 Chronicles 18:16"> and Sheva &#91;is&#93; scribe, and Zadok and Abiathar &#91;are&#93; priests,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:38; Exodus 2:14; Genesis 41:45"> and also, Ira the Jairite hath been minister to David.</span>
