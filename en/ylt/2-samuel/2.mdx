<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:31; 1 Samuel 23:4; Genesis 32:2"> And it cometh to pass afterwards, that David asketh at Jehovah, saying, 'Do I go up into one of the cities of Judah?' and Jehovah saith unto him, 'Go up.' And David saith, 'Whither do I go up?' and He saith, 'To Hebron.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:42–43; 1 Samuel 30:5; Luke 22:28–29"> And David goeth up thither, and also his two wives, Ahinoam the Jezreelitess, and Abigail wife of Nabal the Carmelite;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:1; 1 Chronicles 12:1–7; 1 Samuel 27:2–3"> and his men who &#91;are&#93; with him hath David brought up — a man and his household — and they dwell in the cities of Hebron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:5; 1 Samuel 31:11–13; 2 Samuel 5:3"> And the men of Judah come, and anoint there David for king over the house of Judah; and they declare to David, saying, 'The men of Jabesh-Gilead &#91;are&#93; they who buried Saul.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:21; Psalms 115:15; Ruth 2:20"> And David sendeth messengers unto the men of Jabesh-Gilead, and saith unto them, 'Blessed &#91;are&#93; ye of Jehovah, in that ye have done this kindness with your lord, with Saul, that ye bury him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 14:22; Exodus 34:6; Matthew 5:44"> 'And, now, Jehovah doth with you kindness and truth, and also, I do with you this good because ye have done this thing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 6:10; 2 Samuel 10:12; Genesis 15:1"> and now, are your hands strong, and be ye for sons of valour, for your lord Saul. &#91;is&#93; dead, and also — me have the house of Judah anointed for king over them.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:50; Genesis 32:2; 2 Samuel 3:7–8"> And Abner, son of Ner, head of the host which Saul hath, hath taken Ish-Bosheth, son of Saul, and causeth him to pass over to Mahanaim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 19:18; Joshua 13:8–11; Numbers 1:40"> and causeth him to reign over Gilead, and over the Ashurite, and over Jezreel, and over Ephraim, and over Benjamin, and over Israel — all of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> A son of forty years, &#91;is&#93; Ish-Bosheth son of Saul, in his reigning over Israel, and two years he hath reigned, only the house of Judah have been after David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 2:11; 2 Samuel 5:4–5; 1 Chronicles 3:4"> And the number of the days that David hath been king in Hebron, over the house of Judah, is seven years and six months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 18:25; Joshua 10:2; Joshua 9:3"> And Abner son of Ner goeth out, and servants of Ish-Bosheth son of Saul, from Mahanaim to Gibeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:16; 1 Chronicles 2:16; 2 Samuel 20:23"> And Joab son of Zeruiah, and servants of David, have gone out, and they meet by the pool of Gibeon together, and sit down, these by the pool on this &#91;side&#93;, and these by the pool on that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:17; Proverbs 10:23; Proverbs 17:14"> And Abner saith unto Joab, 'Let the youths rise, I pray thee, and they play before us;' and Joab saith, 'Let them rise.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And they rise and pass over, in number twelve of Benjamin, even of Ish-Bosheth son of Saul, and twelve of the servants of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And they lay hold, each on the head of his companion, and his sword &#91;is&#93; in the side of his companion, and they fall together, and &#91;one&#93; calleth that place Helkath-Hazzurim, which &#91;is&#93; in Gibeon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:1"> and the battle is very hard on that day, and Abner is smitten, and the men of Israel, before the servants of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:8; Habakkuk 3:19; Song of Solomon 8:14"> And there are there three sons of Zeruiah, Joab, and Abishai, and Asahel, and Asahel &#91;is&#93; light on his feet, as one of the roes which &#91;are&#93; in the field,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:21; Joshua 1:7; Proverbs 4:27"> And Asahel pursueth after Abner, and hath not turned aside to go to the right or to the left, from after Abner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And Abner looketh behind him, and saith, 'Art thou he — Asahel?' and he saith, 'I &#91;am&#93;.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 14:19"> And Abner saith to him, 'Turn thee aside to thy right hand or to thy left, and seize for thee one of the youths, and take to thee his armour;' and Asahel hath not been willing to turn aside from after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:27; Proverbs 29:1; 2 Kings 14:10–12"> And Abner addeth again, saying unto Asahel, 'Turn thee aside from after me, why do I smite thee to the earth? and how do I lift up my face unto Joab thy brother?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:27; 2 Samuel 4:6; 2 Samuel 5:6"> And he refuseth to turn aside, and Abner smiteth him with the hinder part of the spear unto the fifth &#91;rib&#93;, and the spear cometh out from behind him, and he falleth there, and dieth under it; and it cometh to pass, every one who hath come unto the place where Asahel hath fallen and dieth — they stand still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And Joab and Abishai pursue after Abner, and the sun hath gone in, and they have come in unto the height of Ammah, which &#91;is&#93; on the front of Giah, the way of the wilderness of Gibeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And the sons of Benjamin gather themselves together after Abner, and become one troop, and stand on the top of a certain height,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:14; Jeremiah 46:10; Acts 7:26"> and Abner calleth unto Joab, and saith, 'For ever doth the sword consume? hast thou not known that it is bitterness in the latter end? and till when dost thou not say to the people to turn back from after their brethren?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 17:14; 2 Samuel 2:14; Proverbs 15:1"> And Joab saith, 'God liveth! for unless thou hadst spoken, surely then from the morning had the people gone up each from after his brother.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Joab bloweth with a trumpet, and all the people stand still, and pursue no more after Israel, nor have they added any more to fight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:8; Song of Solomon 2:17"> And Abner and his men have gone through the plain all that night, and pass over the Jordan, and go on &#91;through&#93; all Bithron, and come in to Mahanaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And Joab hath turned back from after Abner, and gathereth all the people, and there are lacking of the servants of David nineteen men, and Asahel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:1; 1 Kings 20:11"> and the servants of David have smitten of Benjamin, even among the men of Abner, three hundred and sixty men — they died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:13–16; 1 Samuel 17:58; 2 Chronicles 21:1"> And they lift up Asahel, and bury him in the burying-place of his father, which &#91;is&#93; in Beth-Lehem, and they go all the night — Joab and his men — and it is light to them in Hebron.</span>
