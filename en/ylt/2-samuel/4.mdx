<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 18:25; Joshua 9:17; 2 Kings 6:23"> And two men, heads of troops, have been &#91;to&#93; the son of Saul, the name of the one &#91;is&#93; Baanah, and the name of the second Rechab, sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, of the sons of Benjamin, for also Beeroth is reckoned to Benjamin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:33; 1 Samuel 31:7"> and the Beerothites flee to Gittaim, and are there sojourners unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:3; 2 Samuel 9:6; 1 Chronicles 9:40"> And to Jonathan son of Saul &#91;is&#93; a son — lame; he was a son of five years at the coming in of the rumour of &#91;the death of&#93; Saul and Jonathan, out of Jezreel, and his nurse lifteth him up, and fleeth, and it cometh to pass in her hasting to flee, that he falleth, and becometh lame, and his name &#91;is&#93; Mephibosheth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 24:33–34; 2 Chronicles 25:27; 1 Kings 16:9"> And the sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, Rechab and Baanah, go, and come in at the heat of the day unto the house of Ish-Bosheth, and he is lying down — the lying down of noon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:23; 2 Samuel 20:10; 2 Samuel 3:27"> and thither they have come, unto the midst of the house, taking wheat, and they smite him unto the fifth &#91;rib&#93;, and Rechab and Baanah his brother have escaped;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 14:11; Mark 6:28–29; 1 Samuel 17:54"> yea, they come in to the house, and he is lying on his bed, in the inner part of his bed-chamber, and they smite him, and put him to death, and turn aside his head, and they take his head, and go the way of the plain all the night,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:29; 2 Samuel 22:48; 1 Samuel 19:2–11"> and bring in the head of Ish-Bosheth unto David in Hebron, and say unto the king, 'Lo, the head of Ish-Bosheth, son of Saul, thine enemy, who sought thy life; and Jehovah doth give to my lord the king vengeance this day, of Saul and of his seed.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 1:29; Genesis 48:16; Psalms 107:2"> And David answereth Rechab and Baanah his brother, sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, and saith to them, 'Jehovah liveth, who hath redeemed my soul out of all adversity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:2–16"> when one is declaring to me, saying, Lo, Saul is dead, and he was as a bearer of tidings in his own eyes, then I take hold on him, and slay him in Ziklag, instead of my giving to him &#91;for&#93; the tidings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 9:12; Numbers 35:31–34; Genesis 9:5–6"> Also — when wicked men have slain the righteous man in his own house, on his bed; and now, do not I require his blood of your hand, and have taken you away from the earth?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:15; 2 Samuel 3:32; Matthew 7:2"> And David commandeth the young men, and they slay them, and cut off their hands and their feet, and hang &#91;them&#93; over the pool in Hebron, and the head of Ish-Bosheth they have taken, and bury &#91;it&#93; in the burying-place of Abner in Hebron.</span>
