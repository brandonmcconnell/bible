<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 8:10; 1 Samuel 8:12; Exodus 17:9"> And David inspecteth the people who &#91;are&#93; with him, and setteth over them heads of thousands and heads of hundreds,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 7:16; 2 Samuel 15:19–22; 2 Samuel 10:7–10"> and David sendeth the third of the people by the hand of Joab, and the third by the hand of Abishai, son of Zeruiah, brother of Joab, and the third by the hand of Ittai the Gittite, and the king saith unto the people, 'I certainly go out — I also — with you.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:17; Exodus 17:10–12; 1 Kings 22:31"> And the people say, 'Thou dost not go out, for if we utterly flee, they do not set &#91;their&#93; heart upon us; and if half of us die, they do not set &#91;their&#93; heart unto us — for now like us &#91;are&#93; ten thousand; and now, better that thou be to us from the city for an helper.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:24; 2 Samuel 18:1; 1 Samuel 29:2"> And the king saith unto them, 'That which is good in your eyes I do;' and the king standeth at the side of the gate, and all the people have gone out by hundreds and by thousands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:18–21; 2 Samuel 16:11; Psalms 103:13"> and the king chargeth Joab, and Abishai, and Ittai, saying, 'Gently — for me, for the youth, for Absalom;' and all the people heard in the king's charging all the heads concerning Absalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 17:18; Joshua 17:15; Judges 12:4–6"> And the people goeth out into the field to meet Israel, and the battle is in a forest of Ephraim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 24:21; 2 Samuel 2:31; 2 Samuel 2:26"> and smitten there are the people of Israel before the servants of David, and the smiting there is great on that day — twenty thousand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 15:10; Joshua 10:11; Psalms 43:1"> and the battle is there scattered over the face of all the land, and the forest multiplieth to devour among the people more than those whom the sword hath devoured in that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:26; Proverbs 30:17; Job 31:3"> And Absalom meeteth before the servants of David, and Absalom is riding on the mule, and the mule cometh in under an entangled bough of the great oak, and his head taketh hold on the oak, and he is placed between the heavens and the earth, and the mule that &#91;is&#93; under him hath passed on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And one man seeth, and declareth to Joab, and saith, 'Lo, I saw Absalom hanged in an oak.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Joab saith to the man who is declaring &#91;it&#93; to him, 'And lo, thou hast seen — and wherefore didst thou not smite him there to the earth — and on me to give to thee ten silverlings and one girdle?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:5"> And the man saith unto Joab, 'Yea, though I am weighing on my hand a thousand silverlings, I do not put forth my hand unto the son of the king; for in our ears hath the king charged thee, and Abishai, and Ittai, saying, Observe ye who &#91;is&#93; against the youth — against Absalom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:19–20; Hebrews 4:13; 2 Samuel 4:10–12"> or I had done against my soul a vain thing, and no matter is hid from the king, and thou — thou dost station thyself over-against.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 45:5; Matthew 12:40; 2 Samuel 14:30"> And Joab saith, 'Not right — I tarry before thee;' and he taketh three darts in his hand, and striketh them into the heart of Absalom, while he &#91;is&#93; alive, in the midst of the oak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And they go round — ten youths bearing weapons of Joab — and smite Absalom, and put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:28; 2 Samuel 20:22; 1 Corinthians 14:8"> And Joab bloweth with a trumpet, and the people turneth back from pursuing after Israel, for Joab hath kept back the people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 7:26; Joshua 8:29; 2 Samuel 19:8"> and they take Absalom and cast him in the forest unto the great pit, and set up over him a very great heap of stones, and all Israel have fled — each to his tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 14:17; 2 Samuel 14:27; 1 Samuel 15:12"> And Absalom hath taken, and setteth up for himself in his life, the standing-pillar that &#91;is&#93; in the king's valley, for he said, 'I have no son to cause my name to be remembered;' and he calleth the standing-pillar by his own name, and it is called 'The monument of Absalom' unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:36; 2 Samuel 18:31; Psalms 9:4"> And Ahimaaz son of Zadok said, 'Let me run, I pray thee, and I bear the king tidings, for Jehovah hath delivered him out of the hand of his enemies;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:5; 2 Samuel 18:33; 2 Samuel 17:16–21"> and Joab saith to him, 'Thou art not a man of tidings this day, but thou hast borne tidings on another day, and this day thou dost not bear tidings, because the king's son &#91;is&#93; dead.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And Joab saith to Cushi, 'Go, declare to the king that which thou hast seen;' and Cushi boweth himself to Joab, and runneth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 1:28; Ephesians 5:4"> And Ahimaaz son of Zadok addeth again, and saith unto Joab, 'And whatever it be, let me run, I pray thee, I also, after the Cushite.' And Joab saith, 'Why &#91;is&#93; this — thou art running, my son, and for thee there are no tidings found?' —</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 20:4"> 'And, whatever it be, &#91;said he,&#93; let me run.' And he saith to him, 'Run;' and Ahimaaz runneth the way of the circuit, and passeth by the Cushite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:8; 2 Samuel 13:34; Ezekiel 33:2–7"> And David is sitting between the two gates, and the watchman goeth unto the roof of the gate, unto the wall, and lifteth up his eyes, and looketh, and lo, a man running by himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And the watchman calleth, and declareth to the king, and the king saith, 'If by himself, tidings &#91;are&#93; in his mouth;' and he cometh, coming on and drawing near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And the watchman seeth another man running, and the watchman calleth unto the gatekeeper, and saith, 'Lo, a man running by himself;' and the king saith, 'Also this one is bearing tidings.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 9:20; 1 Kings 1:42; Romans 10:15"> And the watchman saith, 'I see the running of the first as the running of Ahimaaz son of Zadok.' And the king saith, 'This &#91;is&#93; a good man, and with good tidings he cometh.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:4; Genesis 14:20; Genesis 24:27"> And Ahimaaz calleth and saith unto the king, 'Peace;' and he boweth himself to the king, on his face, to the earth, and saith, 'Blessed &#91;is&#93; Jehovah thy God who hath shut up the men who lifted up their hand against my lord the king.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:22; 2 Samuel 20:9; 2 Samuel 18:19–20"> And the king saith, 'Peace to the youth — to Absalom?' And Ahimaaz saith, 'I saw the great multitude, at the sending away of the servant of the king, even thy servant &#91;by&#93; Joab, and I have not known what &#91;it is&#93;.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And the king saith, 'Turn round, station thyself here;' and he turneth round and standeth still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:19; 2 Samuel 18:28; Luke 18:7–8"> And lo, the Cushite hath come, and the Cushite saith, 'Let tidings be proclaimed, my lord, O king; for Jehovah hath delivered thee to-day out of the hand of all those rising up against thee.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Judges 5:31; 1 Samuel 25:26; Daniel 4:19"> And the king saith unto the Cushite, 'Peace to the youth — to Absalom?' And the Cushite saith, 'Let them be — as the youth — the enemies of my lord the king, and all who have risen up against thee for evil.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:4; 2 Samuel 12:10–23; Proverbs 17:25"> And the king trembleth, and goeth up on the upper chamber of the gate, and weepeth, and thus he hath said in his going, 'My son! Absalom my son; my son Absalom; oh that I had died for thee, Absalom, my son, my son.'</span>
