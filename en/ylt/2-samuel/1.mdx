<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 27:6; 1 Samuel 31:6; 1 Samuel 30:17–26"> And it cometh to pass, after the death of Saul, that David hath returned from smiting the Amalekite, and David dwelleth in Ziklag two days,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:12; 2 Samuel 4:10; Joshua 7:6"> and it cometh to pass, on the third day, that lo, a man hath come in out of the camp from Saul, and his garments &#91;are&#93; rent, and earth on his head; and it cometh to pass, in his coming in unto David, that he falleth to the earth, and doth obeisance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 1:15–19; 2 Kings 5:25"> And David saith to him, 'Whence comest thou?' and he saith unto him, 'Out of the camp of Israel I have escaped.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:16; 1 Samuel 31:1–6; 1 Chronicles 10:1–6"> And David saith unto him, 'What hath been the matter? declare, I pray thee, to me.' And he saith, that 'The people hath fled from the battle, and also a multitude hath fallen of the people, and they die; and also Saul and Jonathan his son have died.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 25:2; Proverbs 14:15"> And David saith unto the youth who is declaring &#91;it&#93; to him, 'How hast thou known that Saul and Jonathan his son &#91;are&#93; dead?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:4; 2 Samuel 1:21; 1 Chronicles 10:4–6"> And the youth who is declaring &#91;it&#93; to him saith, I happened to meet in mount Gilboa, and lo, Saul is leaning on his spear; and lo, the chariots and those possessing horses have followed him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:12; 2 Samuel 9:6; Judges 9:54"> and he turneth behind him, and seeth me, and calleth unto me, and I say, Here &#91;am&#93; I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:17; 1 Samuel 30:13; 1 Samuel 30:1"> And he saith to me, Who &#91;art&#93; thou? and I say unto him, An Amalekite I &#91;am&#93;.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> 'And he saith unto me, Stand, I pray thee, over me, and put me to death, for seized me hath the arrow, for all my soul &#91;is&#93; still in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Judges 9:54; 2 Kings 11:12"> And I stand over him, and put him to death, for I knew that he doth not live after his falling, and I take the crown which &#91;is&#93; on his head, and the bracelet which &#91;is&#93; on his arm, and bring them in unto my lord hither.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:31; Genesis 37:29; 2 Samuel 3:31"> And David taketh hold on his garments, and rendeth them, and also all the men who &#91;are&#93; with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 24:17; Psalms 35:13–14; Matthew 5:44"> and they mourn, and weep, and fast till the evening, for Saul, and for Jonathan his son, and for the people of Jehovah, and for the house of Israel, because they have fallen by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:8"> And David saith unto the youth who is declaring &#91;it&#93; to him, 'Whence &#91;art&#93; thou?' and he saith, 'Son of a sojourner, an Amalekite, I &#91;am&#93;.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:9; 1 Samuel 31:4; 2 Peter 2:10"> And David saith unto him, 'How wast thou not afraid to put forth thy hand to destroy the anointed of Jehovah?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 2:34; Judges 8:20; 2 Samuel 4:10–12"> And David calleth to one of the youths, and saith, 'Draw nigh — fall upon him;' and he smiteth him, and he dieth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; Luke 19:22; Leviticus 20:9"> and David saith unto him, 'Thy blood &#91;is&#93; on thine own head, for thy mouth hath testified against thee, saying, I — I put to death the anointed of Jehovah.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:25; 2 Samuel 1:19; Genesis 50:11"> And David lamenteth with this lamentation over Saul, and over Jonathan his son;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 10:13; Genesis 49:8; Deuteronomy 4:10"> and he saith to teach the sons of Judah 'The Bow;' lo, it is written on the book of the Upright: —</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:27; 2 Samuel 1:25; Deuteronomy 4:7–8"> 'The Roebuck, O Israel, On thy high places &#91;is&#93; wounded; How have the mighty fallen!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Micah 1:10; 1 Samuel 18:6; Judges 11:34"> Declare &#91;it&#93; not in Gath, Proclaim not the tidings in the streets of Ashkelon, Lest they rejoice — The daughters of the Philistines, Lest they exult — The daughters of the Uncircumcised!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:1; Isaiah 21:5; 1 Samuel 10:1"> Mountains of Gilboa! No dew nor rain be on you, And fields of heave-offerings! For there hath become loathsome The shield of the mighty, The shield of Saul — without the anointed with oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:4; Isaiah 34:6–7; 1 Samuel 14:6–14"> From the blood of the wounded, From the fat of the mighty, The bow of Jonathan Hath not turned backward; And the sword of Saul doth not return empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:13; Judges 14:18; Lamentations 4:19"> Saul and Jonathan! They are loved and pleasant in their lives, And in their death they have not been parted. Than eagles they have been lighter, Than lions they have been mightier!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Peter 3:3–5; Jeremiah 2:32; Judges 5:30"> Daughters of Israel! for Saul weep ye, Who is clothing you &#91;in&#93; scarlet with delights. Who is lifting up ornaments of gold on your clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:19; 2 Samuel 1:27; Judges 5:18"> How have the mighty fallen In the midst of the battle! Jonathan! on thy high places wounded!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:17; 1 Samuel 23:16; 1 Samuel 20:41"> I am in distress for thee, my brother Jonathan, Very pleasant wast thou to me; Wonderful was thy love to me, Above the love of women!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:25; 2 Samuel 1:19; Ezekiel 39:9–10"> How have the mighty fallen, Yea, the weapons of war perish!'</span>
