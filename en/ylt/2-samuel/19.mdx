<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:5; 2 Samuel 18:14; Proverbs 17:25"> And it is declared to Joab, 'Lo, the king is weeping and mourning for Absalom;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 19:12; Proverbs 16:15"> and the salvation on that day becometh mourning to all the people, for the people hath heard on that day, saying, 'The king hath been grieved for his son.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 31:27; 2 Samuel 19:32; 2 Samuel 17:24"> And the people stealeth away, on that day, to go in to the city, as the people steal away, who are ashamed, in their fleeing in battle;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:33; 2 Samuel 15:30"> and the king hath covered his face, yea, the king crieth — a loud voice — 'My son Absalom, Absalom, my son, my son.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 18:47–48; Psalms 3:8; Nehemiah 9:27"> And Joab cometh in unto the king to the house, and saith, 'Thou hast put to shame to-day the faces of all thy servants, those delivering thy life to-day, and the life of thy sons, and of thy daughters, and the life of thy wives, and the life of thy concubines,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 23:5; 2 Samuel 3:24–25; Proverbs 19:9–10"> to love thine enemies, and to hate those loving thee, for thou hast declared to-day that thou hast no princes and servants, for I have known to-day that if Absalom &#91;were&#93; alive, and all of us to-day dead, that then it were right in thine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 14:28; Genesis 34:3; Proverbs 19:15"> 'And now, rise, go out and speak unto the heart of thy servants, for by Jehovah I have sworn, that — thou art not going out — there doth not lodge a man with thee to-night; and this &#91;is&#93; worse for thee than all the evil that hath come upon thee from thy youth till now.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:24; 2 Samuel 18:4; 2 Samuel 19:3"> And the king riseth, and sitteth in the gate, and to all the people they have declared, saying, 'Lo, the king is sitting in the gate;' and all the people come in before the king, and Israel hath fled, each to his tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:14; Genesis 3:12–13; 2 Samuel 8:1–14"> And it cometh to pass, all the people are contending through all the tribes of Israel, saying, 'The king delivered us out of the hand of our enemies, yea, he himself delivered us out of the hand of the Philistines, and now he hath fled out of the land because of Absalom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:14; Judges 18:9; Hosea 8:4"> and Absalom whom we anointed over us &#91;is&#93; dead in battle, and now, why are ye silent — to bring back the king?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:29; 1 Kings 2:35; 2 Samuel 15:35–36"> And king David sent unto Zadok and unto Abiathar the priests, saying, 'Speak ye unto the elders of Judah, saying, Why are ye last to bring back the king unto his house? (and the word of all Israel hath come unto the king, unto his house;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:1; Genesis 2:23; Ephesians 5:30"> my brethren ye &#91;are&#93;, my bone and my flesh ye &#91;are&#93;, and why are ye last to bring back the king?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:25; 1 Kings 19:2; Ruth 1:17"> And to Amasa say ye, Art not thou my bone and my flesh? Thus doth God do to me, and thus He doth add, if thou art not head of the host before me all the days instead of Joab.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 20:1; Acts 4:32; Psalms 110:2–3"> And he inclineth the heart of all the men of Judah as one man, and they send unto the king, 'Turn back, thou, and all thy servants.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 5:9; 1 Samuel 11:14–15"> And the king turneth back, and cometh in unto the Jordan, and Judah hath come to Gilgal, to go to meet the king, to bring the king over the Jordan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 2:8; 2 Samuel 16:5–13; 1 Kings 2:36–46"> and Shimei son of Gera, the Benjamite, who &#91;is&#93; from Bahurim, hasteth, and cometh down with the men of Judah, to meet king David,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:1–4; 2 Samuel 9:2; 2 Samuel 9:10"> and a thousand men &#91;are&#93; with him from Benjamin, and Ziba servant of the house of Saul, and his fifteen sons and his twenty servants with him, and they have gone prosperously over the Jordan before the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 81:15; Revelation 3:9; Psalms 66:3"> And passed over hath the ferry-boat to carry over the household of the king, and to do that which &#91;is&#93; good in his eyes, and Shimei son of Gera hath fallen before the king in his passing over into Jordan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:15; 2 Samuel 13:20; 2 Samuel 13:33"> and saith unto the king, 'Let not my lord impute to me iniquity; neither do thou remember that which thy servant did perversely in the day that my lord the king went out from Jerusalem, — for the king to set &#91;it&#93; unto his heart;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 12:25; Psalms 78:34–37; Genesis 48:14"> for thy servant hath known that I have sinned; and lo, I have come to-day, first of all the house of Joseph, to go down to meet my lord the king.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 22:28; 1 Samuel 26:9; 1 Kings 21:10–11"> And Abishai son of Zeruiah answereth and saith, 'For this is not Shimei put to death — because he reviled the anointed of Jehovah?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:13; 2 Samuel 16:10; 2 Samuel 3:39"> And David saith, 'What — to me and to you, O sons of Zeruiah, that ye are to me to-day for an adversary? to-day is any man put to death in Israel? for have I not known that to-day I &#91;am&#93; king over Israel?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 2:46; 1 Kings 2:8–9; 1 Kings 2:37"> And the king saith unto Shimei, 'Thou dost not die;' and the king sweareth to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 41:5; Matthew 6:16; 2 Samuel 15:30"> And Mephibosheth son of Saul hath come down to meet the king — and he prepared not his feet, nor did he prepare his upper lip, yea, his garments he washed not, even from the day of the going away of the king, till the day that he came in peace —</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:17"> and it cometh to pass, when he hath come to Jerusalem to meet the king, that the king saith to him, 'Why didst thou not go with me, Mephibosheth?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:2–3; 2 Samuel 4:4; 2 Samuel 9:3"> And he saith, 'My lord, O king, my servant deceived me, for thy servant said, I saddle for me the ass, and ride on it, and go with the king, for thy servant &#91;is&#93; lame;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:17; 2 Samuel 14:20; 1 Samuel 29:9"> and he uttereth slander against thy servant unto my lord the king, and my lord the king &#91;is&#93; as a messenger of God; and do thou that which is good in thine eyes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:13; 2 Samuel 9:10; 2 Samuel 21:6–9"> for all the house of my father have been nothing except men of death before my lord the king, and thou dost set thy servant among those eating at thy table, and what right have I any more — even to cry any more unto the king?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 18:15; Psalms 101:5; Psalms 82:2"> And the king saith to him, 'Why dost thou speak any more of thy matters? I have said, Thou and Ziba — share ye the field.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Philippians 1:20; Acts 20:24; 2 Samuel 1:26"> And Mephibosheth saith unto the king, 'Yea, the whole let him take, after that my lord the king hath come in peace unto his house.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 2:7; Nehemiah 7:63; Ezra 2:61"> And Barzillai the Gileadite hath gone down from Rogelim, and passeth over the Jordan with the king, to send him away over the Jordan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:2; Job 1:3; Proverbs 16:31"> and Barzillai &#91;is&#93; very aged, a son of eighty years, and he hath sustained the king in his abiding in Mahanaim, for he &#91;is&#93; a very great man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 25:34–40; Luke 22:28–30; 2 Samuel 9:11"> and the king saith unto Barzillai, 'Pass thou over with me, and I have sustained thee with me in Jerusalem.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="James 4:14; 1 Corinthians 7:29; Genesis 47:8–9"> And Barzillai saith unto the king, 'How many &#91;are&#93; the days of the years of my life, that I go up with the king to Jerusalem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:33; Ezra 2:65; Psalms 90:10"> A son of eighty years I &#91;am&#93; to-day; do I know between good and evil? doth thy servant taste that which I am eating, and that which I drink? do I hearken any more to the voice of singers and songstresses? and why is thy servant any more for a burden unto my lord the king?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 6:38"> As a little thing, thy servant doth pass over the Jordan with the king, and why doth the king recompense me this recompense?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Kings 2:7; Jeremiah 41:17; 2 Samuel 19:40"> Let, I pray thee, thy servant turn back again, and I die in mine own city, near the burying-place of my father and of my mother, — and lo, thy servant Chimham, let him pass over with my lord the king, and do thou to him that which &#91;is&#93; good in thine eyes.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And the king saith, 'With me doth Chimham go over, and I do to him that which &#91;is&#93; good in thine eyes, yea, all that thou dost fix on me I do to thee.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 31:55; Ruth 1:14; Genesis 47:7"> And all the people pass over the Jordan, and the king hath passed over, and the king giveth a kiss to Barzillai, and blesseth him, and he turneth back to his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 21:9; Genesis 49:10; 2 Samuel 19:11–15"> And the king passeth over to Gilgal, and Chimham hath passed over with him, and all the people of Judah, and they bring over the king, and also the half of the people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Judges 8:1; Judges 12:1; John 7:5–6"> And, lo, all the men of Israel are coming unto the king, and they say unto the king, 'Wherefore have they stolen thee — our brethren, the men of Judah?' (and they bring the king and his household over the Jordan, and all the men of David with him).</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:12; 2 Samuel 5:1; 1 Chronicles 2:3–17"> And all the men of Judah answer against the men of Israel, 'Because the king &#91;is&#93; near unto us, and why &#91;is&#93; this — ye are displeased about this matter? have we at all eaten of the king's &#91;substance?&#93; a gift hath he lifted up to us?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Galatians 5:20; 2 Samuel 5:1; 1 Kings 11:30–31"> And the men of Israel answer the men of Judah, and say, 'Ten parts we have in the king, and also in David more than you; and wherefore have ye lightly esteemed us, that our word hath not been first to bring back our king?' And the word of the men of Judah is sharper than the word of the men of Israel.</span>
