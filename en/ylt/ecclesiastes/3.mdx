<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:17; Matthew 16:3; Ecclesiastes 8:5–6"> To everything — a season, and a time to every delight under the heavens:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 9:27; Job 14:5; Matthew 15:13"> A time to bring forth, And a time to die. A time to plant, And a time to eradicate the planted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 6:1–2; 1 Samuel 2:6; Jeremiah 45:4"> A time to slay, And a time to heal, A time to break down, And a time to build up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Psalms 30:5; James 4:9"> A time to weep, And a time to laugh. A time to mourn, And a time to skip.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 3:25; Joshua 10:27; Joshua 4:3–9"> A time to cast away stones, And a time to heap up stones. A time to embrace, And a time to be far from embracing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Philippians 3:7–8; Luke 9:24–25; Mark 8:35–37"> A time to seek, And a time to destroy. A time to keep, And a time to cast away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 5:13; Micah 7:5; Lamentations 3:28"> A time to rend, And a time to sew. A time to be silent, And a time to speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 5:25; Ephesians 5:28–29; Luke 14:26"> A time to love, And a time to hate. A time of war, And a time of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:3; Ecclesiastes 5:16; Proverbs 14:23"> What advantage hath the doer in that which he is labouring at?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:13–14; Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> I have seen the travail that God hath given to the sons of man to be humbled by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Romans 1:19–20; Job 11:7"> The whole He hath made beautiful in its season; also, that knowledge He hath put in their heart without which man findeth not out the work that God hath done from the beginning even unto the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 37:3; Ecclesiastes 3:22; 1 Timothy 6:18"> I have known that there is no good for them except to rejoice and to do good during their life,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 128:2; Ecclesiastes 9:7; Ecclesiastes 2:24"> yea, even every man who eateth and hath drunk and seen good by all his labour, it &#91;is&#93; a gift of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 1:17; Psalms 33:11; Romans 11:36"> I have known that all that God doth is to the age, to it nothing is to be added, and from it nothing is to be withdrawn; and God hath wrought that they do fear before Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:9–10; Ecclesiastes 6:10"> What is that which hath been? already it is, and that which &#91;is&#93; to be hath already been, and God requireth that which is pursued.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:1; Ecclesiastes 5:8; Psalms 94:21–22"> And again, I have seen under the sun the place of judgment — there &#91;is&#93; the wicked; and the place of righteousness — there &#91;is&#93; the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Ecclesiastes 12:14; Matthew 16:27"> I said in my heart, 'The righteous and the wicked doth God judge, for a time &#91;is&#93; to every matter and for every work there.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 73:22; Psalms 49:12; Hebrews 9:27"> I said in my heart concerning the matter of the sons of man that God might cleanse them, so as to see that they themselves &#91;are&#93; beasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 49:12; Psalms 49:20; Psalms 89:47–48"> For an event &#91;is to&#93; the sons of man, and an event &#91;is to&#93; the beasts, even one event &#91;is&#93; to them; as the death of this, so &#91;is&#93; the death of that; and one spirit &#91;is&#93; to all, and the advantage of man above the beast is nothing, for the whole &#91;is&#93; vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Job 34:15; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> The whole are going unto one place, the whole have been from the dust, and the whole are turning back unto the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:7; John 14:3; 2 Corinthians 5:1"> Who knoweth the spirit of the sons of man that is going up on high, and the spirit of the beast that is going down below to the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:24; Ecclesiastes 3:11–12; Ecclesiastes 10:14"> And I have seen that there is nothing better than that man rejoice in his works, for it &#91;is&#93; his portion; for who doth bring him in to look on that which is after him?</span>
