<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:3; John 10:27–30; 1 Peter 1:5"> But all this I have laid unto my heart, so as to clear up the whole of this, that the righteous and the wise, and their works, &#91;are&#93; in the hand of God, neither love nor hatred doth man know, the whole &#91;is&#93; before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Malachi 3:18; Ecclesiastes 2:14–16; Malachi 3:5"> The whole &#91;is&#93; as to the whole; one event is to the righteous and to the wicked, to the good, and to the clean, and to the unclean, and to him who is sacrificing, and to him who is not sacrificing; as &#91;is&#93; the good, so &#91;is&#93; the sinner, he who is swearing as he who is fearing an oath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:9; Ecclesiastes 8:11; Ecclesiastes 1:17"> This &#91;is&#93; an evil among all that hath been done under the sun, that one event &#91;is&#93; to all, and also the heart of the sons of man is full of evil, and madness &#91;is&#93; in their heart during their life, and after it — unto the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 14:7–12; Isaiah 38:18; Luke 16:26–29"> But &#91;to&#93; him who is joined unto all the living there is confidence, for to a living dog it &#91;is&#93; better than to the dead lion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 26:14; Ecclesiastes 1:11; Psalms 6:5"> For the living know that they die, and the dead know not anything, and there is no more to them a reward, for their remembrance hath been forgotten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 146:3–4; Ecclesiastes 6:12; Job 3:17–18"> Their love also, their hatred also, their envy also, hath already perished, and they have no more a portion to the age in all that hath been done under the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:12–13; Deuteronomy 12:7; Ecclesiastes 10:19"> Go, eat with joy thy bread, and drink with a glad heart thy wine, for already hath God been pleased with thy works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 19:14; Revelation 3:4–5; Revelation 19:8"> At all times let thy garments be white, and let not perfume be lacking on thy head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 5:18–19; Ecclesiastes 2:10; Malachi 2:15"> See life with the wife whom thou hast loved, all the days of the life of thy vanity, that He hath given to thee under the sun, all the days of thy vanity, for it &#91;is&#93; thy portion in life, even of thy labour that thou art labouring at under the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 3:23; Ephesians 5:16; Romans 12:11"> All that thy hand findeth to do, with thy power do, for there is no work, and device, and knowledge, and wisdom in Sheol whither thou art going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23; Proverbs 21:30–31; Lamentations 3:37–38"> I have turned so as to see under the sun, that not to the swift &#91;is&#93; the race, nor to the mighty the battle, nor even to the wise bread, nor even to the intelligent wealth, nor even to the skilful grace, for time and chance happen with them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Luke 21:34–36; Ecclesiastes 8:5–7"> For even man knoweth not his time; as fish that are taken hold of by an evil net, and as birds that are taken hold of by a snare, like these &#91;are&#93; the sons of man snared at an evil time, when it falleth upon them suddenly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:16; Ecclesiastes 7:15; Ecclesiastes 6:1"> This also I have seen: wisdom under the sun, and it is great to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:15–22; 2 Kings 6:24–20"> A little city, and few men in it, and a great king hath come unto it, and hath surrounded it, and hath built against it great bulwarks;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:13; Genesis 40:23; Esther 6:2–3"> and there hath been found in it a poor wise man, and he hath delivered the city by his wisdom, and men have not remembered that poor man!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:19; Proverbs 24:5; Proverbs 21:22"> And I said, 'Better &#91;is&#93; wisdom than might, and the wisdom of the poor is despised, and his words are not heard.' —</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 3:17–18; Proverbs 28:23; James 1:20"> The words of the wise in quiet are heard, More than the cry of a ruler over fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 7:11–12; Ecclesiastes 9:16; Joshua 7:5"> Better &#91;is&#93; wisdom than weapons of conflict, And one sinner destroyeth much good!</span>
