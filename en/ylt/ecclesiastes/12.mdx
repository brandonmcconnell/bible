<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Lamentations 3:27; Ecclesiastes 11:8; Psalms 71:17–18"> Remember also thy Creators in days of thy youth, While that the evil days come not, Nor the years have arrived, that thou sayest, 'I have no pleasure in them.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 5:30; 1 Samuel 4:18; 1 Samuel 4:15"> While that the sun is not darkened, and the light, And the moon, and the stars, And the thick clouds returned after the rain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 90:9–10; Psalms 102:23; 2 Samuel 21:15–17"> In the day that keepers of the house tremble, And men of strength have bowed themselves, And grinders have ceased, because they have become few. And those looking out at the windows have become dim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:10; 2 Samuel 19:35; Revelation 18:22"> And doors have been shut in the street. When the noise of the grinding is low, And &#91;one&#93; riseth at the voice of the bird, And all daughters of song are bowed down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 17:13; Jeremiah 9:17–20; Job 30:23"> Also of that which is high they are afraid, And of the low places in the way, And the almond-tree is despised, And the grasshopper is become a burden, And want is increased, For man is going unto his home age-during, And the mourners have gone round through the street.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 4:2–3"> While that the silver cord is not removed, And the golden bowl broken, And the pitcher broken by the fountain, And the wheel broken at the well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; Ecclesiastes 3:20–21; Psalms 146:4"> And the dust returneth to the earth as it was, And the spirit returneth to God who gave it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:2; Ecclesiastes 8:8; Ecclesiastes 1:14"> Vanity of vanities, said the preacher, the whole &#91;is&#93; vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 4:32; Proverbs 1:1; 1 Kings 10:8"> And further, because the preacher was wise, he still taught the people knowledge, and gave ear, and sought out — he made right many similes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 1:5; 1 Timothy 1:15; Proverbs 16:21–24"> The preacher sought to find out pleasing words, and, written &#91;by&#93; the upright, words of truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 2:37; Hebrews 4:12; Ezekiel 34:23"> Words of the wise &#91;are&#93; as goads, and as fences planted &#91;by&#93; the masters of collections, they have been given by one shepherd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:18; John 21:25; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> And further, from these, my son, be warned; the making of many books hath no end, and much study &#91;is&#93; a weariness of the flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:12; Micah 6:8; Deuteronomy 6:2"> The end of the whole matter let us hear: — 'Fear God, and keep His commands, for this &#91;is&#93; the whole of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Romans 2:16; Matthew 12:36"> For every work doth God bring into judgment, with every hidden thing, whether good or bad.'</span>
