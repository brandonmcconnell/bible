<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 27:1–2; Luke 23:1–5; Luke 22:66"> And immediately, in the morning, the chief priests having made a consultation, with the elders, and scribes, and the whole sanhedrim, having bound Jesus, did lead away, and delivered &#91;him&#93; to Pilate;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 2:2; Matthew 27:11–14; 1 Timothy 6:13"> and Pilate questioned him, 'Art thou the king of the Jews?' and he answering said to him, 'Thou dost say &#91;it&#93;.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; Mark 15:5; John 18:29–31"> And the chief priests were accusing him of many things, &#91;but he answered nothing.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 19:10; Matthew 27:13; Matthew 26:62"> And Pilate again questioned him, saying, 'Thou dost not answer anything! lo, how many things they do testify against thee!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; Zechariah 3:8; Psalms 71:7"> and Jesus did no more answer anything, so that Pilate wondered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:39–40; Acts 25:9; Acts 24:27"> And at every feast he was releasing to them one prisoner, whomsoever they were asking;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 23:18–19; Matthew 27:16; Luke 23:25"> and there was &#91;one&#93; named Barabbas, bound with those making insurrection with him, who had in the insurrection committed murder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the multitude having cried out, began to ask for themselves as he was always doing to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 19:14–16; John 19:4–5; John 18:39"> and Pilate answered them, saying, 'Will ye &#91;that&#93; I shall release to you the king of the Jews?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 27:4; Titus 3:3; Genesis 4:4–6"> for he knew that because of envy the chief priests had delivered him up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 3:14; John 18:40; Hosea 5:1"> and the chief priests did move the multitude, that he might rather release Barabbas to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5–6; Isaiah 9:6–7; Zechariah 9:9"> And Pilate answering, again said to them, 'What, then, will ye &#91;that&#93; I shall do to him whom ye call king of the Jews?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and they again cried out, 'Crucify him.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 23:41; Luke 23:23–24; Isaiah 53:3"> And Pilate said to them, 'Why — what evil did he?' and they cried out the more vehemently, 'Crucify him;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 19:1; Matthew 27:26; Isaiah 50:6"> and Pilate, wishing to content the multitude, released to them Barabbas, and delivered up Jesus — having scourged &#91;him&#93; — that he might be crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 19:9; Matthew 27:27–31; John 18:28"> And the soldiers led him away into the hall, which is Praetorium, and call together the whole band,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 27:28–30; John 19:2–5; Luke 23:11"> and clothe him with purple, and having plaited a crown of thorns, they put &#91;it&#93; on him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 27:42–43; Mark 15:2; Genesis 37:10"> and began to salute him, 'Hail, King of the Jews.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 14:65; Isaiah 53:3–5; Luke 23:11"> And they were smiting him on the head with a reed, and were spitting on him, and having bent the knee, were bowing to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 19:16; Matthew 27:31"> and when they &#91;had&#93; mocked him, they took the purple from off him, and clothed him in his own garments, and they led him forth, that they may crucify him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 27:32; Luke 23:26; Romans 16:13"> And they impress a certain one passing by — Simon, a Cyrenian, coming from the field, the father of Alexander and Rufus — that he may bear his cross,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 27:33–44; John 19:17–27; Luke 23:27–38"> and they bring him to the place Golgotha, which is, being interpreted, 'Place of a skull;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 27:34; John 19:28–30; Luke 23:36"> and they were giving him to drink wine mingled with myrrh, and he did not receive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 23:34; John 19:23–24; Matthew 27:35–36"> And having crucified him, they were dividing his garments, casting a lot upon them, what each may take;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 19:14; Mark 15:33; Matthew 27:45"> and it was the third hour, and they crucified him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 27:37; Matthew 2:2; John 19:18–22"> and the inscription of his accusation was written above — 'The King of the Jews.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 23:32–33; Matthew 27:38; John 19:18"> And with him they crucify two robbers, one on the right hand, and one on his left,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and the Writing was fulfilled that is saying, 'And with lawless ones he was numbered.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 14:58; Psalms 109:25; Lamentations 1:12"> And those passing by were speaking evil of him, shaking their heads, and saying, 'Ah, the thrower down of the sanctuary, and in three days the builder!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> save thyself, and come down from the cross!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 12:23–24; Matthew 27:41–43; Luke 23:35–37"> And in like manner also the chief priests, mocking with one another, with the scribes, said, 'Others he saved; himself he is not able to save.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 27:44; Luke 23:39–43; Zechariah 9:9"> The Christ! the king of Israel — let him come down now from the cross, that we may see and believe;' and those crucified with him were reproaching him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 27:45; Luke 23:44–45; Amos 8:9–10"> And the sixth hour having come, darkness came over the whole land till the ninth hour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 22:1; Matthew 27:46; Hebrews 5:7"> and at the ninth hour Jesus cried with a great voice, saying, 'Eloi, Eloi, lamma sabachthani?' which is, being interpreted, 'My God, my God, why didst Thou forsake me?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 27:47–49; Mark 9:11–13; Matthew 17:11–13"> And certain of those standing by, having heard, said, 'Lo, Elijah he doth call;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Mark 15:23; John 19:28–30"> and one having run, and having filled a spunge with vinegar, having put &#91;it&#93; also on a reed, was giving him to drink, saying, 'Let alone, let us see if Elijah doth come to take him down.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 19:30; Luke 23:46; Matthew 27:50"> And Jesus having uttered a loud cry, yielded the spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–23; Luke 23:45; Hebrews 9:3–12"> and the veil of the sanctuary was rent in two, from top to bottom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 27:54; Luke 23:47–48; Matthew 27:43"> and the centurion who was standing over-against him, having seen that, having so cried out, he yielded the spirit, said, 'Truly this man was Son of God.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 23:49; Luke 8:2–3; Matthew 27:55–56"> And there were also women afar off beholding, among whom was also Mary the Magdalene, and Mary of James the less, and of Joses, and Salome,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 8:2–3; Matthew 27:55–56"> (who also, when he was in Galilee, were following him, and were ministering to him,) and many other women who came up with him to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 23:50–56; John 19:38–42; John 19:31"> And now evening having come, seeing it was the preparation, that is, the fore-sabbath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 2:38; Luke 2:25; Luke 23:51"> Joseph of Arimathea, an honourable counsellor, who also himself was waiting for the reign of God, came, boldly entered in unto Pilate, and asked the body of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 19:31–37"> And Pilate wondered if he were already dead, and having called near the centurion, did question him if he were long dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 27:58; John 19:38; Mark 15:39"> and having known &#91;it&#93; from the centurion, he granted the body to Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 53:9; Matthew 27:59–60; Isaiah 22:16"> And he, having brought fine linen, and having taken him down, wrapped him in the linen, and laid him in a sepulchre that had been hewn out of a rock, and he rolled a stone unto the door of the sepulchre,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 15:40; Mark 16:1; Luke 23:55–2"> and Mary the Magdalene, and Mary of Joses, were beholding where he is laid.</span>
