<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 12:9–14; Luke 6:6–11; Mark 1:21"> And he entered again into the synagogue, and there was there a man having the hand withered,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 37:32; Luke 14:1; Luke 6:7"> and they were watching him, whether on the sabbaths he will heal him, that they might accuse him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 1:28–30; Isaiah 42:4; Luke 6:8"> And he saith to the man having the hand withered, 'Rise up in the midst.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 6:9; Matthew 12:10–12; Hosea 6:6"> And he saith to them, 'Is it lawful on the sabbaths to do good, or to do evil? life to save, or to kill?' but they were silent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 6:10; Ephesians 4:18; Romans 11:25"> And having looked round upon them with anger, being grieved for the hardness of their heart, he saith to the man, 'Stretch forth thy hand;' and he stretched forth, and his hand was restored whole as the other;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 22:16; Mark 12:13; Matthew 12:14"> and the Pharisees having gone forth, immediately, with the Herodians, were taking counsel against him how they might destroy him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 4:25; Luke 6:17; Matthew 12:15"> And Jesus withdrew with his disciples unto the sea, and a great multitude from Galilee followed him, and from Judea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:15; Isaiah 34:5; Matthew 11:21"> and from Jerusalem, and from Idumea and beyond the Jordan; and they about Tyre and Sidon — a great multitude — having heard how great things he was doing, came unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 5:30; John 6:15"> And he said to his disciples that a little boat may wait on him, because of the multitude, that they may not press upon him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 14:36; Mark 6:56; Matthew 4:23"> for he did heal many, so that they threw themselves on him, in order to touch him — as many as had plagues;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Mark 1:23–24; Luke 8:28"> and the unclean spirits, when they were seeing him, were falling down before him, and were crying, saying — 'Thou art the Son of God;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:16; Mark 1:25; Mark 1:34"> and many times he was charging them that they might not make him manifest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:1; Luke 6:12–16; Matthew 10:1–4"> And he goeth up to the mountain, and doth call near whom he willed, and they went away to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Luke 9:1–6; John 15:16"> and he appointed twelve, that they may be with him, and that he may send them forth to preach,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and to have power to heal the sicknesses, and to cast out the demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 1:42; Acts 1:13; Matthew 16:16–18"> And he put on Simon the name Peter;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Mark 14:33; Jeremiah 23:29"> and James of Zebedee, and John the brother of James, and he put on them names — Boanerges, that is, 'Sons of thunder;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Mark 2:14; Matthew 9:9"> and Andrew, and Philip, and Bartholomew, and Matthew, and Thomas, and James of Alpheus, and Thaddeus, and Simon the Cananite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 6:71; John 13:2; Acts 1:16–25"> and Judas Iscariot, who did also deliver him up; and they come into a house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 6:31; John 4:31–34; Mark 9:28"> And come together again doth a multitude, so that they are not able even to eat bread;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 26:24; John 10:20; Mark 3:31"> and his friends having heard, went forth to lay hold on him, for they said that he was beside himself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 15:1; Matthew 10:25; Matthew 9:34"> and the scribes who &#91;are&#93; from Jerusalem having come down, said — 'He hath Beelzeboul,' and — 'By the ruler of the demons he doth cast out the demons.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 11:17–23; Matthew 12:25–30; Matthew 13:34"> And, having called them near, in similes he said to them, 'How is the Adversary able to cast out the Adversary?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 11:14; Isaiah 19:2–3; 2 Samuel 20:6"> and if a kingdom against itself be divided, that kingdom cannot be made to stand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 3:16; Psalms 133:1; Galatians 5:15"> and if a house against itself be divided, that house cannot be made to stand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and if the Adversary did rise against himself, and hath been divided, he cannot be made to stand, but hath an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 49:24–26; Matthew 12:29; Luke 11:21–23"> 'No one is able the vessels of the strong man — having entered into his house — to spoil, if first he may not bind the strong man, and then his house he will spoil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 12:10; Matthew 12:31–32; 1 John 5:16"> 'Verily I say to you, that all the sins shall be forgiven to the sons of men, and evil speakings with which they might speak evil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 12:31–32; Luke 12:10; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> but whoever may speak evil in regard to the Holy Spirit hath not forgiveness — to the age, but is in danger of age-during judgment;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 10:20; Mark 3:22"> because they said, 'He hath an unclean spirit.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 8:19–21; Matthew 12:46–50"> Then come do his brethren and mother, and standing without, they sent unto him, calling him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> and a multitude was sitting about him, and they said to him, 'Lo, thy mother and thy brethren without do seek thee.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 3:21; Deuteronomy 33:9; Mark 6:3"> And he answered them, saying, 'Who is my mother, or my brethren?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 12:49–50; Luke 11:27–28; Romans 8:29"> And having looked round in a circle to those sitting about him, he saith, 'Lo, my mother and my brethren!</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 2:17; Matthew 7:21; 1 John 3:22–23"> for whoever may do the will of God, he is my brother, and my sister, and mother.'</span>
