<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 22:1–2; Exodus 12:6–20; John 13:1"> And the passover and the unleavened food were after two days, and the chief priests and the scribes were seeking how, by guile, having taken hold of him, they might kill him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 7:40; Proverbs 21:30; John 12:19"> and they said, 'Not in the feast, lest there shall be a tumult of the people.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 7:37–39; John 12:1–8; John 11:2"> And he, being in Bethany, in the house of Simon the leper, at his reclining (at meat), there came a woman having an alabaster box of ointment, of spikenard, very precious, and having broken the alabaster box, did pour on his head;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 12:4–5; Matthew 26:8–9; Malachi 1:12–13"> and there were certain much displeased within themselves, and saying, 'For what hath this waste of the ointment been made?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 13:29; Ephesians 4:28; 1 Corinthians 10:10"> for this could have been sold for more than three hundred denaries, and given to the poor;' and they were murmuring at her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 3:14; Hebrews 10:24; Hebrews 13:21"> And Jesus said, 'Let her alone; why are ye giving her trouble? a good work she wrought on me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:11; Matthew 26:11; James 2:14–16"> for the poor always ye have with you, and whenever ye may will ye are able to do them good, but me ye have not always;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; John 12:7; Mark 15:42–1"> what she could she did, she anticipated to anoint my body for the embalming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 26:12–13; Mark 16:15; Matthew 24:14"> Verily I say to you, wherever this good news may be proclaimed in the whole world, what also this woman did shall be spoken of — for a memorial of her.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 22:3–6; Matthew 26:14–16; John 13:2"> And Judas the Iscariot, one of the twelve, went away unto the chief priests that he might deliver him up to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:10; Hosea 7:3; Jude 1:11"> and having heard, they were glad, and promised to give him money, and he was seeking how, conveniently, he might deliver him up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 12:6; Exodus 12:18; Matthew 26:17–19"> And the first day of the unleavened food, when they were killing the passover, his disciples say to him, 'Where wilt thou, &#91;that,&#93; having gone, we may prepare, that thou mayest eat the passover?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 22:10–13; Matthew 26:18–19; Luke 19:30–33"> And he sendeth forth two of his disciples, and saith to them, 'Go ye away to the city, and there shall meet you a man bearing a pitcher of water, follow him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 3:20; Mark 11:3; John 13:13"> and wherever he may go in, say ye to the master of the house — The Teacher saith, Where is the guest-chamber, where the passover, with my disciples, I may eat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Psalms 110:3; Proverbs 21:1–2"> and he will shew you a large upper room, furnished, prepared — there make ready for us.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 16:4; Luke 22:13; Luke 22:35"> And his disciples went forth, and came to the city, and found as he said to them, and they made ready the passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 22:14; John 13:21–26; Matthew 26:20–24"> And evening having come, he cometh with the twelve,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; John 6:47; John 6:70"> and as they are reclining, and eating, Jesus said, 'Verily I say to you — one of you, who is eating with me — shall deliver me up.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 22:21–23; Matthew 26:22; John 13:22"> And they began to be sorrowful, and to say to him, one by one, 'Is it I?' and another, 'Is it I?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 13:26; Matthew 26:23; John 6:71"> And he answering said to them, 'One of the twelve who is dipping with me in the dish;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 26:24–25; Isaiah 53:1–12; John 19:28"> the Son of Man doth indeed go, as it hath been written concerning him, but woe to that man through whom the Son of Man is delivered up; good were it to him if that man had not been born.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 26:26–29; Mark 14:24; 1 Corinthians 10:16–17"> And as they are eating, Jesus having taken bread, having blessed, brake, and gave to them, and said, 'Take, eat; this is my body.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 14:22; Matthew 26:27; 1 Corinthians 10:16"> And having taken the cup, having given thanks, he gave to them, and they drank of it — all;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 9:11; Exodus 24:8; Revelation 7:9–17"> and he said to them, 'This is my blood of the new covenant, which for many is being poured out;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 26:29; Psalms 104:15; Luke 22:16–18"> verily I say to you, that no more may I drink of the produce of the vine till that day when I may drink it new in the reign of God.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 26:30; Luke 22:39; Acts 16:25"> And having sung an hymn, they went forth to the mount of the Olives,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; John 16:32; Matthew 26:31"> and Jesus saith to them — 'All ye shall be stumbled at me this night, because it hath been written, I will smite the shepherd, and the sheep shall be scattered abroad,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 28:16; Mark 16:7; Matthew 28:7"> but after my having risen I will go before you to Galilee.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 26:33–35; Luke 22:33–34; John 13:36–38"> And Peter said to him, 'And if all shall be stumbled, yet not I;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 13:38; Mark 14:66–72; Genesis 1:5"> And Jesus said to him, 'Verily I say to thee, that to-day, this night, before a cock shall crow twice, thrice thou shalt deny me.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 19:8; John 13:37; Proverbs 16:18"> And he spake the more vehemently, 'If it may be necessary for me to die with thee — I will in no wise deny thee;' and in like manner also said they all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 14:36; Mark 14:39; Psalms 18:5–6"> And they come to a spot, the name of which &#91;is&#93; Gethsemane, and he saith to his disciples, 'Sit ye here till I may pray;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 22:44; Hebrews 5:7; Mark 5:37"> and he taketh Peter, and James, and John with him, and began to be amazed, and to be very heavy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 12:27; Mark 13:35–37; 1 Peter 5:8"> and he saith to them, 'Exceeding sorrowful is my soul — to death; remain here, and watch.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 14:41; Deuteronomy 9:18; Hebrews 5:7"> And having gone forward a little, he fell upon the earth, and was praying, that, if it be possible the hour may pass from him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Galatians 4:6; Romans 8:15–16; John 5:30"> and he said, 'Abba, Father; all things are possible to Thee; make this cup pass from me; but, not what I will, but what Thou.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 26:40; Mark 14:29–31; Hebrews 12:3"> And he cometh, and findeth them sleeping, and saith to Peter, 'Simon, thou dost sleep! thou wast not able to watch one hour!</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 26:41; Matthew 24:42; Luke 21:36"> Watch ye and pray, that ye may not enter into temptation; the spirit indeed is forward, but the flesh weak.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 26:42–44; 2 Corinthians 12:8; Matthew 6:7"> And again having gone away, he prayed, the same word saying;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 9:33–34; Romans 3:19; Genesis 44:16"> and having returned, he found them again sleeping, for their eyes were heavy, and they had not known what they might answer him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 14:35; Mark 9:31; John 17:1"> And he cometh the third time, and saith to them, 'Sleep on henceforth, and rest — it is over; the hour did come; lo, the Son of Man is delivered up to the hands of the sinful;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 26:46; John 18:1–2"> rise, we may go, lo, he who is delivering me up hath come nigh.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 18:3–11; Luke 22:47–53; Matthew 26:47–56"> And immediately — while he is yet speaking — cometh near Judas, one of the twelve, and with him a great multitude, with swords and sticks, from the chief priests, and the scribes, and the elders;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 26:48–50; Proverbs 27:6; Psalms 55:20–21"> and he who is delivering him up had given a token to them, saying, 'Whomsoever I shall kiss, he it is, lay hold on him, and lead him away safely,'</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 23:7–10; Malachi 1:6; Mark 12:14"> and having come, immediately, having gone near him, he saith, 'Rabbi, Rabbi,' and kissed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 18:12; Lamentations 4:20; Acts 2:23"> And they laid on him their hands, and kept hold on him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 18:10–11; Matthew 26:51–54; Luke 22:49–51"> and a certain one of those standing by, having drawn the sword, struck the servant of the chief priest, and took off his ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 22:52–53; 1 Samuel 26:18; 1 Samuel 24:14–15"> And Jesus answering said to them, 'As against a robber ye came out, with swords and sticks, to take me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 18:20; John 8:2; Luke 22:37"> daily I was with you in the temple teaching, and ye did not lay hold on me — but that the Writings may be fulfilled.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Mark 14:27; John 16:32; Psalms 88:18"> And having left him they all fled;</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> and a certain young man was following him, having put a linen cloth about &#91;his&#93; naked body, and the young men lay hold on him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Mark 13:14–16; Genesis 39:12; Job 2:4"> and he, having left the linen cloth, did flee from them naked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Matthew 26:57–68; John 18:12–14; Luke 22:54–62"> And they led away Jesus unto the chief priest, and come together to him do all the chief priests, and the elders, and the scribes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 18:18; Matthew 26:3; John 18:25"> and Peter afar off did follow him, to the inside of the hall of the chief priest, and he was sitting with the officers, and warming himself near the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="1 Peter 3:16–18; Matthew 26:59–60; Matthew 5:22"> And the chief priests and all the sanhedrim were seeking against Jesus testimony — to put him to death, and they were not finding,</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> for many were bearing false testimony against him, and their testimonies were not alike.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Acts 6:13–14; Matthew 26:60–61; John 2:18–21"> And certain having risen up, were bearing false testimony against him, saying —</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="John 2:19; Hebrews 9:11; 2 Corinthians 5:1"> 'We heard him saying — I will throw down this sanctuary made with hands, and by three days, another made without hands I will build;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> and neither so was their testimony alike.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="John 19:9–10; Matthew 26:62–63; Mark 15:3–5"> And the chief priest, having risen up in the midst, questioned Jesus, saying, 'Thou dost not answer anything! what do these testify against thee?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Matthew 26:63–64; Mark 15:2; Isaiah 53:7"> and he was keeping silent, and did not answer anything. Again the chief priest was questioning him, and saith to him, 'Art thou the Christ — the Son of the Blessed?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Daniel 7:13–14; Psalms 110:1; Hebrews 1:3"> and Jesus said, 'I am; and ye shall see the Son of Man sitting on the right hand of the power, and coming with the clouds, of the heaven.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Matthew 26:65; Isaiah 36:22–1; Numbers 14:6"> And the chief priest, having rent his garments, saith, 'What need have we yet of witnesses?</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Leviticus 24:16; John 10:31–33; John 19:7"> Ye heard the evil speaking, what appeareth to you?' and they all condemned him to be worthy of death,</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Matthew 26:67–68; Acts 23:2; Isaiah 50:6"> and certain began to spit on him, and to cover his face, and to buffet him, and to say to him, 'Prophesy;' and the officers were striking him with their palms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Mark 14:54; Matthew 26:69–75; John 18:25–27"> And Peter being in the hall beneath, there doth come one of the maids of the chief priest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Mark 14:54; Mark 10:47; Matthew 2:23"> and having seen Peter warming himself, having looked on him, she said, 'And thou wast with Jesus of Nazareth!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Mark 14:29–31; Matthew 26:71–72; John 13:36–38"> and he denied, saying, 'I have not known &#91;him&#93;, neither do I understand what thou sayest;' and he went forth without to the porch, and a cock crew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="Luke 22:58; Mark 14:38; Galatians 6:1"> And the maid having seen him again, began to say to those standing near — 'This is of them;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="Luke 22:59–60; Matthew 26:73–74; Acts 2:7"> and he was again denying. And after a little again, those standing near said to Peter, 'Truly thou art of them, for thou also art a Galilean, and thy speech is alike;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:9; 1 Corinthians 10:12; 2 Kings 10:32"> and he began to anathematize, and to swear — 'I have not known this man of whom ye speak;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72" data-xref="Mark 14:30; Mark 14:68; Ezekiel 7:16"> and a second time a cock crew, and Peter remembered the saying that Jesus said to him — 'Before a cock crow twice, thou mayest deny me thrice;' and having thought thereon — he was weeping.</span>
