<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 20:1; Luke 24:1; Matthew 28:1"> and early in the morning of the first of the sabbaths, they come unto the sepulchre, at the rising of the sun,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 15:46–47; Matthew 27:60–66"> and they said among themselves, 'Who shall roll away for us the stone out of the door of the sepulchre?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 28:2–4; Luke 24:2; John 20:1"> And having looked, they see that the stone hath been rolled away — for it was very great,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 20:11–12; Luke 24:3–5; John 20:8"> and having entered into the sepulchre, they saw a young man sitting on the right hand, arrayed in a long white robe, and they were amazed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 1:17–18; 1 Corinthians 15:3–7; Luke 24:46"> And he saith to them, 'Be not amazed, ye seek Jesus the Nazarene, the crucified: he did rise — he is not here; lo, the place where they laid him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 14:28; Matthew 26:32; Matthew 28:10"> and go, say to his disciples, and Peter, that he doth go before you to Galilee; there ye shall see him, as he said to you.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 24:22–24; Luke 24:9–11; Mark 16:5–6"> And, having come forth quickly, they fled from the sepulchre, and trembling and amazement had seized them, and to no one said they anything, for they were afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 20:14–19; Luke 8:2; John 20:12"> And he, having risen in the morning of the first of the sabbaths, did appear first to Mary the Magdalene, out of whom he had cast seven demons;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 20:18; Luke 24:17; John 16:6"> she having gone, told those who had been with him, mourning and weeping;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 24:11; Mark 16:13–14; Mark 9:19"> and they, having heard that he is alive, and was seen by her, did not believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 24:13–32; John 21:14; John 21:1"> And after these things, to two of them, as they are going into a field, walking, he was manifested in another form,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 20:25; Luke 24:33–35; John 20:8"> and they having gone, told to the rest; not even them did they believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:5; Luke 24:36–43; Hebrews 3:7–8"> Afterwards, as they are reclining (at meat), he was manifested to the eleven, and did reproach their unbelief and stiffness of heart, because they believed not those having seen him being raised;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 28:19; Mark 13:10; Acts 1:8"> and he said to them, 'Having gone to all the world, proclaim the good news to all the creation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 11:25–26; John 3:36; Romans 10:9–14"> he who hath believed, and hath been baptized, shall be saved; and he who hath not believed, shall be condemned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 5:16; 1 Corinthians 12:28; Acts 8:7"> 'And signs shall accompany those believing these things; in my name demons they shall cast out; with new tongues they shall speak;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 10:19; James 5:14–15; Acts 28:3–6"> serpents they shall take up; and if any deadly thing they may drink, it shall not hurt them; on the ailing they shall lay hands, and they shall be well.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Peter 3:22; Hebrews 1:3; Hebrews 12:2"> The Lord, then, indeed, after speaking to them, was received up to the heaven, and sat on the right hand of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:4–5; Acts 14:3; Romans 15:19"> and they, having gone forth, did preach everywhere, the Lord working with &#91;them&#93;, and confirming the word, through the signs following. Amen.</span>
