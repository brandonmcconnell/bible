<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 3:18–19; 2 Corinthians 5:17; Galatians 3:13"> There is, then, now no condemnation to those in Christ Jesus, who walk not according to the flesh, but according to the Spirit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 6:14; 2 Corinthians 3:17; Romans 6:18"> for the law of the Spirit of the life in Christ Jesus did set me free from the law of the sin and of the death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 13:39; Galatians 4:4–5; Hebrews 7:18–19"> for what the law was not able to do, in that it was weak through the flesh, God, His own Son having sent in the likeness of sinful flesh, and for sin, did condemn the sin in the flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 5:16; Galatians 5:22–25; Colossians 1:22"> that the righteousness of the law may be fulfilled in us, who do not walk according to the flesh, but according to the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; Galatians 5:19–25; John 3:6"> For those who are according to the flesh, the things of the flesh do mind; and those according to the Spirit, the things of the Spirit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 6:8; Romans 8:7; Romans 8:13"> for the mind of the flesh &#91;is&#93; death, and the mind of the Spirit — life and peace;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; 1 John 2:15–16; James 4:4"> because the mind of the flesh &#91;is&#93; enmity to God, for to the law of God it doth not subject itself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 3:5–6; Hebrews 11:5–6; Romans 8:9"> for neither is it able; and those who are in the flesh are not able to please God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 4:6; 1 Corinthians 3:16; 2 Timothy 1:14"> And ye are not in the flesh, but in the Spirit, if indeed the Spirit of God doth dwell in you; and if any one hath not the Spirit of Christ — this one is not His;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 3:17; Galatians 2:20; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> and if Christ &#91;is&#93; in you, the body, indeed, &#91;is&#93; dead because of sin, and the Spirit &#91;is&#93; life because of righteousness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:14; Romans 6:4–5; 2 Corinthians 4:11"> and if the Spirit of Him who did raise up Jesus out of the dead doth dwell in you, He who did raise up the Christ out of the dead shall quicken also your dying bodies, through His Spirit dwelling in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 6:2–15; 1 Corinthians 6:19–20; 1 Peter 4:2–3"> So, then, brethren, we are debtors, not to the flesh, to live according to the flesh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 6:8; 1 Peter 2:11; 1 Corinthians 9:27"> for if according to the flesh ye do live, ye are about to die; and if, by the Spirit, the deeds of the body ye put to death, ye shall live;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 5:18; John 1:12; Galatians 3:26"> for as many as are led by the Spirit of God, these are the sons of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 4:5–7; 2 Timothy 1:7; 1 Corinthians 2:12"> for ye did not receive a spirit of bondage again for fear, but ye did receive a spirit of adoption in which we cry, 'Abba — Father.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 4:13; 2 Corinthians 1:22; 2 Corinthians 5:5"> The Spirit himself doth testify with our spirit, that we are children of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 4:7; Galatians 3:29; Ephesians 3:6"> and if children, also heirs, heirs, indeed, of God, and heirs together of Christ — if, indeed, we suffer together, that we may also be glorified together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:17–18; 1 Peter 4:13; 1 Peter 1:5–7"> For I reckon that the sufferings of the present time &#91;are&#93; not worthy &#91;to be compared&#93; with the glory about to be revealed in us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Malachi 3:17–18; Romans 8:23; 1 John 3:2"> for the earnest looking out of the creation doth expect the revelation of the sons of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 3:17–19; Genesis 5:29; Romans 8:22"> for to vanity was the creation made subject — not of its will, but because of Him who did subject &#91;it&#93; — in hope,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 22:3–5; Acts 3:21; 2 Peter 3:13"> that also the creation itself shall be set free from the servitude of the corruption to the liberty of the glory of the children of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:4; Jeremiah 12:11; John 16:21"> for we have known that all the creation doth groan together, and doth travail in pain together till now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 3:20–21; 2 Corinthians 5:2–5; Hebrews 9:28"> And not only &#91;so&#93;, but also we ourselves, having the first-fruit of the Spirit, we also ourselves in ourselves do groan, adoption expecting — the redemption of our body;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:7; Hebrews 11:1; 2 Corinthians 4:18"> for in hope we were saved, and hope beheld is not hope; for what any one doth behold, why also doth he hope for &#91;it&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 12:12; Hebrews 10:36; Psalms 27:14"> and if what we do not behold we hope for, through continuance we expect &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 10:20; Ephesians 6:18; Galatians 4:6"> And, in like manner also, the Spirit doth help our weaknesses; for, what we may pray for, as it behoveth &#91;us&#93;, we have not known, but the Spirit himself doth make intercession for us with groanings unutterable,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:10; 1 John 5:14–15; Ephesians 2:18"> and He who is searching the hearts hath known what &#91;is&#93; the mind of the Spirit, because according to God he doth intercede for saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; James 1:12; Genesis 50:20"> And we have known that to those loving God all things do work together for good, to those who are called according to purpose;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 1:4–5; Jeremiah 1:5; Ephesians 1:11"> because whom He did foreknow, He also did fore-appoint, conformed to the image of His Son, that he might be first-born among many brethren;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 17:14; Romans 8:28; Ephesians 1:11"> and whom He did fore-appoint, these also He did call; and whom He did call, these also He declared righteous; and whom He declared righteous, these also He did glorify.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 118:6; Jeremiah 1:19; Psalms 56:11"> What, then, shall we say unto these things? if God &#91;is&#93; for us, who &#91;is&#93; against us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 3:16; Romans 8:28; Psalms 84:11"> He who indeed His own Son did not spare, but for us all did deliver him up, how shall He not also with him the all things grant to us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Revelation 12:10–11; Isaiah 50:8–9; Isaiah 54:17"> Who shall lay a charge against the choice ones of God? God &#91;is&#93; He that is declaring righteous,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hebrews 7:25; Romans 8:27; Hebrews 9:24"> who &#91;is&#93; he that is condemning? Christ &#91;is&#93; He that died, yea, rather also, was raised up; who is also on the right hand of God — who also doth intercede for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 10:28; Psalms 103:17; John 16:33"> Who shall separate us from the love of the Christ? tribulation, or distress, or persecution, or famine, or nakedness, or peril, or sword?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 44:22; Isaiah 53:7; 1 Corinthians 15:30"> (according as it hath been written — 'For Thy sake we are put to death all the day long, we were reckoned as sheep of slaughter,')</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:57; 1 John 4:4; 2 Corinthians 2:14"> but in all these we more than conquer, through him who loved us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 10:28; Romans 14:8; 2 Timothy 1:12"> for I am persuaded that neither death, nor life, nor messengers, nor principalities, nor powers, nor things present,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 10:28–30; Romans 8:35; Romans 5:8"> nor things about to be, nor height, nor depth, nor any other created thing, shall be able to separate us from the love of god, that &#91;is&#93; in Christ Jesus our Lord.</span>
