<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 2:25–29; Genesis 25:32; Ecclesiastes 6:11"> What, then, &#91;is&#93; the superiority of the Jew? or what the profit of the circumcision?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 9:4; Acts 7:38; 1 Corinthians 9:17"> much in every way; for first, indeed, that they were intrusted with the oracles of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:13; Hebrews 4:2; Romans 9:6"> for what, if certain were faithless? shall their faithlessness the faithfulness of god make useless?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 51:4; Psalms 116:11; Galatians 2:17"> let it not be! and let God become true, and every man false, according as it hath been written, 'That Thou mayest be declared righteous in Thy words, and mayest overcome in Thy being judged.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 6:19; Galatians 3:15; Romans 9:18–20"> And, if our unrighteousness God's righteousness doth establish, what shall we say? is God unrighteous who is inflicting the wrath? (after the manner of a man I speak)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 18:25; Acts 17:31; Job 8:3"> let it not be! since how shall God judge the world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 3:4; Romans 9:19–20; Acts 13:27–29"> for if the truth of God in my falsehood did more abound to His glory, why yet am I also as a sinner judged?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 6:1; Romans 6:15; Romans 5:20"> and not, as we are evil spoken of, and as certain affirm us to say — 'We may do the evil things, that the good ones may come?' whose judgment is righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:22; Romans 6:15; Isaiah 65:5"> What, then? are we better? not at all! for we did before charge both Jews and Greeks with being all under sin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 53:1–3; Psalms 14:1–3; Romans 3:23"> according as it hath been written — 'There is none righteous, not even one;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 14:2–4; Psalms 53:2; Titus 3:3"> There is none who is understanding, there is none who is seeking after God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 14:3; Ecclesiastes 7:20; Isaiah 64:6"> All did go out of the way, together they became unprofitable, there is none doing good, there is not even one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 5:9; Psalms 140:3; Matthew 23:27–28"> A sepulchre opened &#91;is&#93; their throat; with their tongues they used deceit; poison of asps &#91;is&#93; under their lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Psalms 109:17–18; Psalms 59:12"> Whose mouth is full of cursing and bitterness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7–8; Proverbs 1:16; Proverbs 6:18"> Swift &#91;are&#93; their feet to shed blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Ruin and misery &#91;are&#93; in their ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 59:8; Romans 5:1; Luke 1:79"> And a way of peace they did not know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 36:1; Proverbs 23:17; Proverbs 16:6"> There is no fear of God before their eyes.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Galatians 3:22–23; Romans 2:12–18"> And we have known that as many things as the law saith, to those in the law it doth speak, that every mouth may be stopped, and all the world may come under judgment to God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Galatians 2:16; Acts 13:39; Romans 3:28"> wherefore by works of law shall no flesh be declared righteous before Him, for through law is a knowledge of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 10:43; Jeremiah 33:16; Philippians 3:9"> And now apart from law hath the righteousness of God been manifested, testified to by the law and the prophets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 10:12; Galatians 3:28; Romans 8:1"> and the righteousness of God &#91;is&#93; through the faith of Jesus Christ to all, and upon all those believing, — for there is no difference,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; 1 John 1:8–10; Romans 3:9"> for all did sin, and are come short of the glory of God —</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Peter 1:18–19; Titus 3:5–7; Colossians 1:14"> being declared righteous freely by His grace through the redemption that &#91;is&#93; in Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 3:23–24; 1 Peter 1:18–20; 1 John 4:10"> whom God did set forth a mercy seat, through the faith in his blood, for the shewing forth of His righteousness, because of the passing over of the bygone sins in the forbearance of God —</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Romans 4:5; Romans 3:30"> for the shewing forth of His righteousness in the present time, for His being righteous, and declaring him righteous who &#91;is&#93; of the faith of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 4:2; Romans 2:23; 1 Corinthians 1:29–31"> Where then &#91;is&#93; the boasting? it was excluded; by what law? of works? no, but by a law of faith:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Galatians 2:16; Galatians 3:8; Titus 3:7"> therefore do we reckon a man to be declared righteous by faith, apart from works of law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; Ephesians 3:6; Zechariah 2:11"> The God of Jews only &#91;is He&#93;, and not also of nations?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Galatians 3:8; Galatians 3:20; Philippians 3:3"> yes, also of nations; since one &#91;is&#93; God who shall declare righteous the circumcision by faith, and the uncircumcision through the faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Galatians 2:21; Matthew 5:17; Romans 13:8–10"> Law then do we make useless through the faith? let it not be! yea, we do establish law.</span>
