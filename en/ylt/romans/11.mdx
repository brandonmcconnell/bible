<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:22; 1 Samuel 12:22; Philippians 3:5"> I say, then, Did God cast away His people? let it not be! for I also am an Israelite, of the seed of Abraham, of the tribe of Benjamin:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 94:14; Romans 8:29–30; Exodus 32:1"> God did not cast away His people whom He knew before; have ye not known — in Elijah — what the Writing saith? how he doth plead with God concerning Israel, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 19:10–18; 1 Kings 18:13; Nehemiah 9:26"> 'Lord, Thy prophets they did kill, and Thy altars they dug down, and I was left alone, and they seek my life;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 19:18; Numbers 25:3; Zephaniah 1:4"> but what saith the divine answer to him? 'I left to Myself seven thousand men, who did not bow a knee to Baal.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 9:27; Ephesians 1:5–6; Romans 9:11"> So then also in the present time a remnant according to the choice of grace there hath been;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 2:21; Romans 4:4–5; 2 Timothy 1:9"> and if by grace, no more of works, otherwise the grace becometh no more grace; and if of works, it is no more grace, otherwise the work is no more work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 9:18; Romans 11:5; 2 Corinthians 4:4"> What then? What Israel doth seek after, this it did not obtain, and the chosen did obtain, and the rest were hardened,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:4; Isaiah 29:10; Ezekiel 12:2"> according as it hath been written, 'God gave to them a spirit of deep sleep, eyes not to see, and ears not to hear,' — unto this very day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 69:22–23; Luke 12:20; Psalms 28:4"> and David saith, 'Let their table become for a snare, and for a trap, and for a stumbling-block, and for a recompense to them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 69:23; Deuteronomy 28:64–68; 2 Peter 2:4"> let their eyes be darkened — not to behold, and their back do Thou always bow down.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 13:46–48; Romans 11:31; Romans 10:19"> I say, then, Did they stumble that they might fall? let it not be! but by their fall the salvation &#91;is&#93; to the nations, to arouse them to jealousy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:25; Colossians 1:27; Romans 11:15"> and if the fall of them &#91;is&#93; the riches of a world, and the diminution of them the riches of nations, how much more the fulness of them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 2 Timothy 1:11–12; Ephesians 3:8"> For to you I speak — to the nations — inasmuch as I am indeed an apostle of nations, my ministration I do glorify;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:16; 1 Timothy 4:16; Romans 9:3"> if by any means I shall arouse to jealousy mine own flesh, and shall save some of them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 1:10; Romans 5:10–11; Colossians 1:20–21"> for if the casting away of them &#91;is&#93; a reconciliation of the world, what the reception — if not life out of the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:30; James 1:18; Proverbs 3:9"> and if the first-fruit &#91;is&#93; holy, the lump also; and if the root &#91;is&#93; holy, the branches also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 2:11–13; Ephesians 3:6; Matthew 21:43"> And if certain of the branches were broken off, and thou, being a wild olive tree, wast graffed in among them, and a fellow-partaker of the root and of the fatness of the olive tree didst become —</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:12; Romans 11:20; Ephesians 2:19–20"> do not boast against the branches; and if thou dost boast, thou dost not bear the root, but the root thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 11:17; Romans 11:11–12; Romans 11:23–24"> Thou wilt say, then, 'The branches were broken off, that I might be graffed in;' right!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 12:16; 2 Corinthians 1:24; 1 Corinthians 10:12"> by unbelief they were broken off, and thou hast stood by faith; be not high-minded, but be fearing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4–9; Jeremiah 25:29; Jeremiah 49:12"> for if God the natural branches did not spare — lest perhaps He also shall not spare thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 3:14; Hebrews 3:6; 1 Corinthians 15:2"> Lo, then, goodness and severity of God — upon those indeed who fell, severity; and upon thee, goodness, if thou mayest remain in the goodness, otherwise, thou also shalt be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:16; Zechariah 12:10; Matthew 23:39"> And those also, if they may not remain in unbelief, shall be graffed in, for God is able again to graff them in;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 11:17–18; Romans 11:30"> for if thou, out of the olive tree, wild by nature, wast cut out, and, contrary to nature, wast graffed into a good olive tree, how much rather shall they, who &#91;are&#93; according to nature, be graffed into their own olive tree?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 21:24; 2 Corinthians 3:14–16; Romans 16:25"> For I do not wish you to be ignorant, brethren, of this secret — that ye may not be wise in your own conceits — that hardness in part to Israel hath happened till the fulness of the nations may come in;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 59:20; Jeremiah 31:31–37; Psalms 14:7"> and so all Israel shall be saved, according as it hath been written, 'There shall come forth out of Sion he who is delivering, and he shall turn away impiety from Jacob,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Isaiah 59:21; Ezekiel 36:25–29"> and this to them &#91;is&#93; the covenant from Me, when I may take away their sins.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:15; Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Matthew 21:43"> As regards, indeed, the good tidings, &#91;they are&#93; enemies on your account; and as regards the choice — beloved on account of the fathers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 7:21; Numbers 23:19; Malachi 3:6"> for unrepented of &#91;are&#93; the gifts and the calling of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 3:7; Ephesians 2:1–2; 1 Corinthians 7:25"> for as ye also once did not believe in God, and now did find kindness by the unbelief of these:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 11:15; Romans 10:16; Romans 11:25"> so also these now did not believe, that in your kindness they also may find kindness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Galatians 3:22; Romans 3:9; 1 Timothy 2:4–6"> for God did shut up together the whole to unbelief, that to the whole He might do kindness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 5:9; Job 11:7–9; Psalms 92:5"> O depth of riches, and wisdom and knowledge of God! how unsearchable His judgments, and untraceable His ways!</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 40:13; 1 Corinthians 2:16; Job 15:8"> for who did know the mind of the Lord? or who did become His counsellor?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 41:11; Job 35:7; 1 Corinthians 4:7"> or who did first give to Him, and it shall be given back to him again?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Colossians 1:15–17; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Hebrews 2:10"> because of Him, and through Him, and to Him &#91;are&#93; the all things; to Him &#91;is&#93; the glory — to the ages. Amen.</span>
