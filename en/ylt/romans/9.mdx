<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:7; Romans 1:9; Galatians 1:20"> Truth I say in Christ, I lie not, my conscience bearing testimony with me in the Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 3:18; Psalms 119:136; Jeremiah 13:17"> that I have great grief and unceasing pain in my heart —</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 32:32; Esther 8:6; Romans 11:14"> for I was wishing, I myself, to be anathema from the Christ — for my brethren, my kindred, according to the flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 2:12; Psalms 147:19; Romans 9:6"> who are Israelites, whose &#91;is&#93; the adoption, and the glory, and the covenants, and the lawgiving, and the service, and the promises,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 10:30; John 1:1–3; Colossians 1:16–19"> whose &#91;are&#93; the fathers, and of whom &#91;is&#93; the Christ, according to the flesh, who is over all, God blessed to the ages. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 2:28–29; Galatians 6:16; Romans 11:1–2"> And it is not possible that the word of God hath failed; for not all who &#91;are&#93; of Israel are these Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 21:12; Hebrews 11:18; John 8:37–39"> nor because they are seed of Abraham &#91;are&#93; all children, but — 'in Isaac shall a seed be called to thee;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:13; Romans 4:11–16; Galatians 3:26–29"> that is, the children of the flesh — these &#91;are&#93; not children of God; but the children of the promise are reckoned for seed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 18:10; Genesis 18:14; Genesis 17:21"> for the word of promise &#91;is&#93; this; 'According to this time I will come, and there shall be to Sarah a son.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 25:21–23; Luke 16:26; Romans 5:11"> And not only &#91;so&#93;, but also Rebecca, having conceived by one — Isaac our father —</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Ephesians 1:4–5; 2 Peter 1:10"> (for they being not yet born, neither having done anything good or evil, that the purpose of God, according to choice, might remain; not of works, but of Him who is calling,) it was said to her —</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 25:22–23; 2 Samuel 8:14; 1 Kings 22:47"> 'The greater shall serve the less;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Malachi 1:2–3; Deuteronomy 21:15; Genesis 29:33"> according as it hath been written, 'Jacob I did love, and Esau I did hate.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:7; Psalms 145:17; Deuteronomy 32:4"> What, then, shall we say? unrighteousness &#91;is&#93; with God? let it not be!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 33:19; Romans 9:16; Micah 7:18"> for to Moses He saith, 'I will do kindness to whom I do kindness, and I will have compassion on whom I have compassion;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ephesians 2:8; Matthew 11:25–26; 1 Peter 2:9–10"> so, then — not of him who is willing, nor of him who is running, but of God who is doing kindness:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 9:16; Proverbs 16:4; Exodus 14:17–18"> for the Writing saith to Pharaoh — 'For this very thing I did raise thee up, that I might shew in thee My power, and that My name might be declared in all the land;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 11:20; Exodus 4:21; Romans 9:15–16"> so, then, to whom He willeth, He doth kindness, and to whom He willeth, He doth harden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 4:35; 2 Chronicles 20:6; 1 Corinthians 15:12"> Thou wilt say, then, to me, 'Why yet doth He find fault? for His counsel who hath resisted?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 29:16; Isaiah 45:9–11; Isaiah 64:8"> nay, but, O man, who art thou that art answering again to God? shall the thing formed say to Him who did form &#91;it&#93;, Why me didst thou make thus?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; Proverbs 16:4; 2 Timothy 2:20–21"> hath not the potter authority over the clay, out of the same lump to make the one vessel to honour, and the one to dishonour?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 16:4; Jude 1:4; Romans 9:21"> And if God, willing to shew the wrath and to make known His power, did endure, in much long suffering, vessels of wrath fitted for destruction,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 2:4; 1 Peter 1:2–5; Colossians 1:27"> and that He might make known the riches of His glory on vessels of kindness, that He before prepared for glory, whom also He did call — us —</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; Acts 13:47–48; Ephesians 3:6–8"> not only out of Jews, but also out of nations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hosea 2:23; 1 Peter 2:10; John 16:27"> as also in Hosea He saith, 'I will call what &#91;is&#93; not My people — My people; and her not beloved — Beloved,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hosea 1:9–10; 1 John 3:1–3; Galatians 3:26"> and it shall be — in the place where it was said to them, Ye &#91;are&#93; not My people; there they shall be called sons of the living God.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 10:20–23; Romans 11:4–6; Isaiah 1:9"> And Isaiah doth cry concerning Israel, 'If the number of the sons of Israel may be as the sand of the sea, the remnant shall be saved;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 10:23; Revelation 19:11; Acts 17:31"> for a matter He is finishing, and is cutting short in righteousness, because a matter cut short will the Lord do upon the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 1:9; Isaiah 13:19; Amos 4:11"> and according as Isaiah saith before, 'Except the Lord of Sabaoth did leave to us a seed, as Sodom we had become, and as Gomorrah we had been made like.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Philippians 3:9; Hebrews 11:7; Galatians 2:16"> What, then, shall we say? that nations who are not pursuing righteousness did attain to righteousness, and righteousness that &#91;is&#93; of faith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 11:7; Romans 3:20; James 2:10–11"> and Israel, pursuing a law of righteousness, at a law of righteousness did not arrive;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:23; 1 Peter 2:8; Luke 7:23"> wherefore? because — not by faith, but as by works of law; for they did stumble at the stone of stumbling,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 28:16; Isaiah 8:14–15; Romans 10:11"> according as it hath been written, 'Lo, I place in Sion a stone of stumbling and a rock of offence; and every one who is believing thereon shall not be ashamed.'</span>
