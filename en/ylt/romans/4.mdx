<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 51:2; Romans 4:16; 2 Corinthians 11:22"> What, then, shall we say Abraham our father, to have found, according to flesh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:31; 2 Corinthians 11:30; Philippians 3:9"> for if Abraham by works was declared righteous, he hath to boast — but not before God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 15:6; James 2:23; Galatians 3:6–8"> for what doth the writing say? 'And Abraham did believe God, and it was reckoned to him — to righteousness;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 11:6; Romans 9:32; Romans 11:35"> and to him who is working, the reward is not reckoned of grace, but of debt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 3:9; Romans 3:22; Titus 3:3–7"> and to him who is not working, and is believing upon Him who is declaring righteous the impious, his faith is reckoned — to righteousness:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Philippians 3:9; 1 Corinthians 1:30"> even as David also doth speak of the happiness of the man to whom God doth reckon righteousness apart from works:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 32:1–2; Psalms 130:3–4; Luke 7:47–50"> 'Happy they whose lawless acts were forgiven, and whose sins were covered;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 32:2; 1 Peter 2:24; 2 Corinthians 5:19–20"> happy the man to whom the Lord may not reckon sin.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:14; Colossians 3:11; Galatians 3:26–28"> &#91;Is&#93; this happiness, then, upon the circumcision, or also upon the uncircumcision — for we say that the faith was reckoned to Abraham — to righteousness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 6:15; Galatians 5:6; Genesis 17:10"> how then was it reckoned? he being in circumcision, or in uncircumcision? not in circumcision, but in uncircumcision;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 3:22; Luke 19:9; 2 Corinthians 1:22"> and a sign he did receive of circumcision, a seal of the righteousness of the faith in the uncircumcision, for his being father of all those believing through uncircumcision, for the righteousness also being reckoned to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:8; John 8:39–40; Proverbs 2:20"> and father of circumcision to those not of circumcision only, but who also walk in the steps of the faith, that &#91;is&#93; in the uncircumcision of our father Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 3:29; Genesis 22:17–18; Genesis 28:14"> For not through law &#91;is&#93; the promise to Abraham, or to his seed, of his being heir of the world, but through the righteousness of faith;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 3:18–24; Philippians 3:9; Romans 4:16"> for if they who are of law &#91;are&#93; heirs, the faith hath been made void, and the promise hath been made useless;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 5:13; Galatians 3:10; Romans 3:19–20"> for the law doth work wrath; for where law is not, neither &#91;is&#93; transgression.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 3:24–26; Ephesians 2:8; Galatians 3:22"> Because of this &#91;it is&#93; of faith, that &#91;it may be&#93; according to grace, for the promise being sure to all the seed, not to that which &#91;is&#93; of the law only, but also to that which &#91;is&#93; of the faith of Abraham,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:28; Genesis 17:4–5; John 5:21"> who is father of us all (according as it hath been written — 'A father of many nations I have set thee,') before Him whom he did believe — God, who is quickening the dead, and is calling the things that be not as being.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 8:24; Genesis 15:5–6; Acts 27:25"> Who, against hope in hope did believe, for his becoming father of many nations according to that spoken: 'So shall thy seed be;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 11:11–19; Romans 4:20–21; Genesis 17:17"> and not having been weak in the faith, he did not consider his own body, already become dead, (being about a hundred years old,) and the deadness of Sarah's womb,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 1:45; 1 Corinthians 16:13; Daniel 10:19"> and at the promise of God did not stagger in unbelief, but was strengthened in faith, having given glory to God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:37; Jeremiah 32:27; Genesis 18:14"> and having been fully persuaded that what He hath promised He is able also to do:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 4:6; Romans 4:3"> wherefore also it was reckoned to him to righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:11; Romans 15:4; 1 Corinthians 10:6"> And it was not written on his account alone, that it was reckoned to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 2:24; 1 Peter 1:21; Acts 13:30"> but also on ours, to whom it is about to be reckoned — to us believing on Him who did raise up Jesus our Lord out of the dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 John 4:9–10; Isaiah 53:5–6; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> who was delivered up because of our offences, and was raised up because of our being declared righteous.</span>
