<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 15:1; 1 Corinthians 8:7–13; 1 Corinthians 9:22"> And him who is weak in the faith receive ye — not to determinations of reasonings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:4; Romans 14:22–23; Romans 14:14"> one doth believe that he may eat all things — and he who is weak doth eat herbs;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 14:10; Colossians 2:16–17; Luke 18:9"> let not him who is eating despise him who is not eating: and let not him who is not eating judge him who is eating, for God did receive him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 4:11–12; Romans 9:20; Romans 8:31–39"> Thou — who art thou that art judging another's domestic? to his own master he doth stand or fall; and he shall be made to stand, for God is able to make him stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 2:16–17; Romans 14:23; Galatians 4:9–10"> One doth judge one day above another, and another doth judge every day &#91;alike&#93;; let each in his own mind be fully assured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:30–31; 1 Timothy 4:3–5; Matthew 14:19"> He who is regarding the day, to the Lord he doth regard &#91;it&#93;, and he who is not regarding the day, to the Lord he doth not regard &#91;it&#93;. He who is eating, to the Lord he doth eat, for he doth give thanks to God; and he who is not eating, to the Lord he doth not eat, and doth give thanks to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:2; 2 Corinthians 5:15; 1 Corinthians 6:19–20"> For none of us to himself doth live, and none to himself doth die;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 1:20; 1 Thessalonians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 15:23"> for both, if we may live, to the Lord we live; if also we may die, to the Lord we die; both then if we may live, also if we may die, we are the Lord's;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 1:18; Luke 24:26; Hebrews 12:2"> for because of this Christ both died and rose again, and lived again, that both of dead and of living he may be Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 4:5; Ecclesiastes 12:14"> And thou, why dost thou judge thy brother? or again, thou, why dost thou set at nought thy brother? for we shall all stand at the tribunal of the Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 45:22–25; Philippians 2:10–11; Psalms 72:11"> for it hath been written, 'I live! saith the Lord — to Me bow shall every knee, and every tongue shall confess to God;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 4:5; Matthew 12:36; Matthew 16:27"> so, then, each of us concerning himself shall give reckoning to God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:3; James 4:11; 1 Corinthians 8:9–13"> no longer, therefore, may we judge one another, but this judge ye rather, not to put a stumbling-stone before the brother, or an offence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Romans 14:2; 1 Timothy 4:4"> I have known, and am persuaded, in the Lord Jesus, that nothing &#91;is&#93; unclean of itself, except to him who is reckoning anything to be unclean — to that one &#91;it is&#93; unclean;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:11–12; Philippians 2:2–4; Galatians 5:13"> and if through victuals thy brother is grieved, no more dost thou walk according to love; do not with thy victuals destroy that one for whom Christ died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:22; 1 Corinthians 10:29–30"> Let not, then, your good be evil spoken of,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Romans 15:13; 1 Corinthians 4:20"> for the reign of God is not eating and drinking, but righteousness, and peace, and joy in the Holy Spirit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:21; 2 Corinthians 5:11; 1 Peter 2:20"> for he who in these things is serving the Christ, &#91;is&#93; acceptable to God and approved of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Romans 12:18; Psalms 34:14"> So, then, the things of peace may we pursue, and the things of building up one another;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 14:14–15; Matthew 18:6; Titus 1:15"> for the sake of victuals cast not down the work of God; all things, indeed, &#91;are&#93; pure, but evil &#91;is&#93; to the man who is eating through stumbling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:13; Romans 14:13; Romans 15:1–2"> Right &#91;it is&#93; not to eat flesh, nor to drink wine, nor to &#91;do anything&#93; in which thy brother doth stumble, or is made to fall, or is weak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 John 3:21; Romans 14:5; James 3:13"> Thou hast faith! to thyself have &#91;it&#93; before God; happy is he who is not judging himself in what he doth approve,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 11:6; Titus 1:15; 1 Corinthians 11:29–31"> and he who is making a difference, if he may eat, hath been condemned, because &#91;it is&#93; not of faith; and all that &#91;is&#93; not of faith is sin.</span>
