<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 8:28–39; John 15:9–15; Romans 5:8–10"> He hath brought me in unto a house of wine, And his banner over me &#91;is&#93; love,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Song of Solomon 5:8; Hosea 3:1; 2 Samuel 6:19"> Sustain me with grape-cakes, Support me with citrons, for I &#91;am&#93; sick with love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:17; Isaiah 54:5–10; Jeremiah 32:41"> His left hand &#91;is&#93; under my head, And his right doth embrace me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Song of Solomon 3:5; Song of Solomon 8:4; Song of Solomon 1:5"> I have adjured you, daughters of Jerusalem, By the roes or by the hinds of the field, Stir not up nor wake the love till she please!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:27; Revelation 3:20; John 3:29"> The voice of my beloved! lo, this — he is coming, Leaping on the mountains, skipping on the hills.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Song of Solomon 8:14; Song of Solomon 2:17; John 5:39"> My beloved &#91;is&#93; like to a roe, Or to a young one of the harts. Lo, this — he is standing behind our wall, Looking from the windows, Blooming from the lattice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:13; Song of Solomon 4:7–8; Revelation 22:17"> My beloved hath answered and said to me, 'Rise up, my friend, my fair one, and come away,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1–2; Ecclesiastes 3:11; Isaiah 54:6–8"> For lo, the winter hath passed by, The rain hath passed away — it hath gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 55:12; Colossians 3:16; Psalms 89:15"> The flowers have appeared in the earth, The time of the singing hath come, And the voice of the turtle was heard in our land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:10; Song of Solomon 7:11–13; Isaiah 55:10–11"> The fig-tree hath ripened her green figs, And the sweet-smelling vines have given forth fragrance, Rise, come, my friend, my fair one, yea, come away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezra 9:5–6; Song of Solomon 5:2; Song of Solomon 1:5"> My dove, in clefts of the rock, In a secret place of the ascent, Cause me to see thine appearance, Cause me to hear thy voice, For thy voice &#91;is&#93; sweet, and thy appearance comely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 13:32; 2 Peter 2:1–3; Song of Solomon 7:12"> Seize ye for us foxes, Little foxes — destroyers of vineyards, Even our sweet-smelling vineyards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Song of Solomon 6:3; Song of Solomon 7:10; Psalms 63:1"> My beloved &#91;is&#93; mine, and I &#91;am&#93; his, Who is delighting among the lilies,</span>
