<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 1:1; 1 Timothy 6:3; 2 Timothy 2:25"> Paul, a servant of God, and an apostle of Jesus Christ, according to the faith of the choice ones of God, and an acknowledging of truth that &#91;is&#93; according to piety,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; 1 John 2:25; 2 Timothy 1:9"> upon hope of life age-during, which God, who doth not lie, did promise before times of ages,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:11; Titus 3:4–6; 1 Timothy 1:1"> (and He manifested in proper times His word,) in preaching, which I was entrusted with, according to a charge of God our Saviour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:2; Jude 1:3; 2 Peter 1:1"> to Titus — true child according to a common faith: Grace, kindness, peace, from God the Father, and the Lord Jesus Christ our Saviour!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 14:23; 1 Timothy 1:3; Ecclesiastes 12:9"> For this cause left I thee in Crete, that the things lacking thou mayest arrange, and mayest set down in every city elders, as I did appoint to thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2–7; 1 Timothy 3:12; Titus 1:6–8"> if any one is blameless, of one wife a husband, having children stedfast, not under accusation of riotous living or insubordinate —</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 5:18; 2 Peter 2:10; 1 Peter 4:10"> for it behoveth the overseer to be blameless, as God's steward, not self-pleased, nor irascible, not given to wine, not a striker, not given to filthy lucre;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2; Titus 2:7; 1 Kings 5:7"> but a lover of strangers, a lover of good men, sober-minded, righteous, kind, self-controlled,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:15; 2 Timothy 1:13; 1 Timothy 6:3"> holding — according to the teaching — to the stedfast word, that he may be able also to exhort in the sound teaching, and the gainsayers to convict;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:6; 1 John 4:1; Galatians 2:4"> for there are many both insubordinate, vain-talkers, and mind-deceivers — especially they of the circumcision —</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:6; 1 Timothy 6:5; Romans 3:19"> whose mouth it behoveth to stop, who whole households do overturn, teaching what things it behoveth not, for filthy lucre's sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 16:18; 1 Timothy 4:2; Acts 17:28"> A certain one of them, a prophet of their own, said — 'Cretans! always liars, evil beasts, lazy bellies!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Titus 2:2; 1 Timothy 5:20; Titus 2:15"> this testimony is true; for which cause convict them sharply, that they may be sound in the faith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:4; Colossians 2:22; Galatians 4:9"> not giving heed to Jewish fables and commands of men, turning themselves away from the truth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 14:14; Romans 14:20; 1 Corinthians 10:23"> all things, indeed, &#91;are&#93; pure to the pure, and to the defiled and unstedfast &#91;is&#93; nothing pure, but of them defiled &#91;are&#93; even the mind and the conscience;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 2:4; Isaiah 29:13; Ephesians 5:6"> God they profess to know, and in the works they deny &#91;Him&#93;, being abominable, and disobedient, and unto every good work disapproved.</span>
