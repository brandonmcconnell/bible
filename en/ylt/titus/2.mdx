<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:13; 1 Timothy 6:3; Titus 2:11–14"> And thou — be speaking what doth become the sound teaching;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 5:23; 1 Peter 4:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:8"> aged men to be temperate, grave, sober, sound in the faith, in the love, in the endurance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Titus 2:4; 1 Timothy 3:11; 1 Peter 3:3–5"> aged women, in like manner, in deportment as doth become sacred persons, not false accusers, to much wine not enslaved, of good things teachers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:14; 1 Timothy 5:2; 1 Timothy 5:11"> that they may make the young women sober-minded, to be lovers of &#91;their&#93; husbands, lovers of &#91;their&#93; children,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 31:10–31; Colossians 3:18; 1 Timothy 5:10"> sober, pure, keepers of &#91;their own&#93; houses, good, subject to their own husbands, that the word of God may not be evil spoken of.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:1; 1 Peter 5:5; Joel 2:28"> The younger men, in like manner, be exhorting to be sober-minded;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 5:3; 1 Timothy 4:12; 2 Corinthians 4:2"> concerning all things thyself showing a pattern of good works; in the teaching uncorruptedness, gravity, incorruptibility,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; 1 Peter 2:15; 1 Peter 3:16"> discourse sound, irreprehensible, that he who is of the contrary part may be ashamed, having nothing evil to say concerning you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 2:18–25; 1 Timothy 6:1–2; Colossians 3:22–25"> Servants — to their own masters &#91;are&#93; to be subject, in all things to be well-pleasing, not gainsaying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 5:16; 1 Peter 2:12; 2 John 1:9"> not purloining, but showing all good stedfastness, that the teaching of God our Saviour they may adorn in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 2:8; 1 Timothy 2:4; Isaiah 45:22"> For the saving grace of God was manifested to all men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:27; Matthew 16:24; Romans 6:19"> teaching us, that denying the impiety and the worldly desires, soberly and righteously and piously we may live in the present age,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:8; 1 Corinthians 1:7; Matthew 16:27"> waiting for the blessed hope and manifestation of the glory of our great God and Saviour Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 9:14; Ephesians 2:10; 1 Peter 2:9"> who did give himself for us, that he might ransom us from all lawlessness, and might purify to himself a peculiar people, zealous of good works;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:2; 1 Timothy 4:12–13; Titus 1:13"> these things be speaking, and exhorting, and convicting, with all charge; let no one despise thee!</span>
