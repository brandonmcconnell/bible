<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2–3; Ezra 9:4; Joshua 23:12–13"> and now, let us make a covenant with our God, to cause all the women to go out, and that which is born of them, by the counsel of the Lord, and of those trembling at the command of our God, and according to law it is done;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:10; Ecclesiastes 9:10; Hebrews 12:12–13"> rise, for on thee &#91;is&#93; the matter, and we &#91;are&#93; with thee; be strong, and do.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:25; Nehemiah 5:12; Proverbs 9:9"> And Ezra riseth, and causeth the heads of the priests, the Levites, and all Israel, to swear to do according to this word — and they swear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:18; Nehemiah 3:1; Nehemiah 12:22"> And Ezra riseth from before the house of God, and goeth unto the chamber of Jehohanan son of Eliashib; yea, he goeth there, bread he hath not eaten, and water he hath not drunk, for he is mourning because of the trespass of the removal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezra 1:1; 2 Chronicles 30:5"> And they cause a voice to pass over into Judah and Jerusalem, to all sons of the removal, to be gathered to Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 7:26; John 9:22; Judges 21:5"> and every one who cometh not in by the third day, according to the counsel of the heads and of the elders, all his substance is devoted, and himself separated from the assembly of the removal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:17–18; Jeremiah 10:13; Ezra 7:8–9"> And gathered are all the men of Judah and Benjamin to Jerusalem by the third day, it &#91;is&#93; the ninth month, on the twentieth of the month, and all the people sit in the broad place of the house of God, trembling on account of the matter and of the showers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezra 9:6; Numbers 32:14; Matthew 23:32"> And Ezra the priest riseth, and saith unto them, 'Ye — ye have trespassed, and ye settle strange women, to add to the guilt of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 12:2; Ezra 10:3; Isaiah 1:16–18"> and, now, make confession to Jehovah, God of your fathers, and do His good pleasure, and be separated from the peoples of the land, and from the strange women.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:23; Psalms 78:57; Ezra 10:3–4"> And all the assembly answer and say &#91;with&#93; a great voice, 'Right; according to thy word — on us to do;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra 10:18–44; Matthew 7:13–14"> but the people &#91;are&#93; many, and &#91;it is&#93; the time of showers, and there is no power to stand without, and the work &#91;is&#93; not for one day, nor for two, for we have multiplied to transgress in this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:10; 2 Chronicles 30:8; Numbers 25:4"> 'Let, we pray thee, our heads of all the assembly stand, and all who &#91;are&#93; in our cities, who have settled strange wives, do come in at the times appointed, and with them the elders of city and city, and its judges, till the turning back of the fury of the wrath of our God from us, for this thing.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:16; Nehemiah 10:20; Nehemiah 12:33"> Only Jonathan son of Asahel, and Jahaziah son of Tikvah, stood against this, and Meshullam, and Shabbethai the Levite, helped them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 29:16; Deuteronomy 13:14; John 7:51"> And the sons of the removal do so, and Ezra the priest, &#91;and&#93; men, heads of the fathers, for the house of their fathers, are separated, even all of them by name, and they sit on the first day of the tenth month, to examine the matter;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> and they finish with all the men who have settled strange women unto the first day of the first month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezra 5:2; Ezra 3:2; Zechariah 3:1"> And there are found of the sons of the priests that have settled strange women: of the sons of Jeshua son of Jozadak, and his brethren, Maaseiah, and Eliezer, and Jarib, and Gedaliah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 6:6; 2 Kings 10:15; 2 Chronicles 30:8"> and they give their hand to send out their wives, and, being guilty, a ram of the flock, for their guilt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezra 2:37; 1 Chronicles 24:14; Nehemiah 7:40"> And of the sons of Immer: Hanani and Zebadiah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:8; Ezra 2:39; Nehemiah 7:42"> and of the sons of Harim: Masseiah, and Elijah, and Shemaiah, and Jehiel, and Uzziah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:12; Nehemiah 7:41; Ezra 2:38"> and of the sons of Pashhur: Elioenai, Maaseiah, Ishmael, Nethaneel, Jozabad, and Elasah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:10; Nehemiah 11:16; Ezra 8:33"> And of the Levites: Jozabad, and Shimei, and Kelaiah — he &#91;is&#93; Kelita, — Pethahiah, Judah, and Eliezer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And of the singers: Eliashib. And of the gatekeepers: Shallum, and Telem, and Uri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezra 2:3; Nehemiah 7:8"> And of Israel: of the sons of Parosh: Ramiah, and Jeziah, and Malchijah, and Miamin, and Eleazar, and Malchijah, and Benaiah.</span>
