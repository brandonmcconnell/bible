<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezra 5:17; Proverbs 25:2; Ezra 4:15"> Then Darius the king made a decree, and they sought in the house of the books of the treasuries placed there in Babylon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 17:6"> and there hath been found at Achmetha, in a palace that &#91;is&#93; in the province of Media, a roll, and a record thus written within it &#91;is&#93;:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:22–4; Revelation 21:16; Ezekiel 41:13–15"> 'In the first year of Cyrus the king, Cyrus the king hath made a decree concerning the house of God in Jerusalem: the house let be builded in the place where they are sacrificing sacrifices, and its foundations strongly laid; its height sixty cubits, its breadth sixty cubits;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 6:36; Psalms 68:29; Isaiah 49:23"> three rows of rolled stones, and a row of new wood, and the outlay let be given out of the king's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 5:2; Ezra 5:14; 2 Chronicles 36:6–7"> 'And also, the vessels of the house of God, of gold and silver, that Nebuchadnezzar took forth out of the temple that &#91;is&#93; in Jerusalem, and brought to Babylon, let be given back, and go to the temple that &#91;is&#93; in Jerusalem, &#91;each&#93; to its place, and put &#91;them&#93; down in the house of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra 5:3; Romans 8:31; Proverbs 21:30"> 'Now, Tatnai, governor beyond the river, Shethar-Boznai, and their companions, the Apharsachites, who &#91;are&#93; beyond the river, be ye far from hence;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 5:38–39"> let alone the work of this house of God, let the governor of the Jews, and the elders of the Jews, build this house of God on its place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 6:4; Haggai 2:8; Ezra 7:15–22"> 'And by me is made a decree concerning that which ye do with the elders of these Jews to build this house of God, that of the riches of the king, that &#91;are&#93; of the tribute beyond the river, speedily let the outlay be given to these men, that they cease not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 1:10; Psalms 50:9–13; Mark 9:49"> and what they are needing — both young bullocks, and rams, and lambs for burnt-offerings to the God of heaven, wheat, salt, wine, and oil according to the saying of the priests who &#91;are&#93; in Jerusalem — let be given to them day by day without fail,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:7; Ezra 7:23; 1 Timothy 2:1–2"> that they be bringing near sweet savours to the God of heaven, and praying for the life of the king, and of his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 3:29; Ezra 7:26; Daniel 2:5"> 'And by me is made a decree, that any one who changeth this thing, let wood be pulled down from his house, and being raised up, let him be smitten on it, and his house let be made a dunghill for this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 9:3; Deuteronomy 12:5; Deuteronomy 12:11"> And God, who caused His name to dwell there, doth cast down any king and people that putteth forth his hand to change, to destroy this house of God that &#91;is&#93; in Jerusalem; I Darius have made a decree; speedily let it be done.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra 4:9; Ezra 6:6; Job 5:12–13"> Then Tatnai, governor beyond the river, Shethar-Boznai, and their companions, according to that which Darius the king hath sent, so they have done speedily;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezra 7:1; Ezra 5:13; Ezra 4:24–2"> and the elders of the Jews are building and prospering through the prophecy of Haggai the prophet, and Zechariah son of Iddo, and they have built and finished by the decree of the God of Israel, and by the decree of Cyrus, and Darius, and Artaxerxes king of Persia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esther 3:7; Esther 9:21; Esther 9:1"> And this house hath gone out till the third day of the month Adar, that is &#91;in&#93; the sixth year of the reign of Darius the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:5; 1 Kings 8:63; John 10:22"> And the sons of Israel have made, &#91;and&#93; the priests, and the Levites, and the rest of the sons of the captivity, a dedication of this house of God with joy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezra 8:35; Revelation 7:4–8; 2 Chronicles 29:21–23"> and have brought near for the dedication of this house of God, bullocks a hundred, rams two hundred, lambs four hundred; and young he-goats for a sin-offering for all Israel, twelve, according to the number of the tribes of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 3:6; 1 Chronicles 24:1; 2 Chronicles 35:4–5"> and they have established the priests in their divisions, and the Levites in their courses, over the service of God that &#91;is&#93; in Jerusalem, as it is written in the book of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 12:6–36; 2 Chronicles 30:1–27; Joshua 5:10"> And the sons of the captivity make the passover on the fourteenth of the first month,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:11; Hebrews 7:27; 2 Chronicles 29:34"> for the priests and the Levites have been purified together — all of them &#91;are&#93; pure — and they slaughter the passover for all the sons of the captivity, and for their brethren the priests, and for themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezra 9:11; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Isaiah 52:11"> And the sons of Israel, those returning from the captivity, and every one who is separated from the uncleanness of the nations of the land unto them, to seek to Jehovah, God of Israel, do eat,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezra 1:1; 2 Chronicles 30:21; Proverbs 21:1"> and they make the feast of unleavened things seven days with joy, for Jehovah made them to rejoice, and turned round the heart of the king of Asshur unto them, to strengthen their hands in the work of the house of God, the God of Israel.</span>
