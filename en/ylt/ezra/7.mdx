<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:1; 1 Chronicles 6:4–14; Ezra 7:21"> And after these things, in the reign of Artaxerxes king of Persia, Ezra son of Seraiah, son of Azariah, son of Hilkiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 2:35; 2 Samuel 8:17"> son of Shallum, son of Zadok, son of Ahitub,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> son of Amariah, son of Azariah, son of Meraioth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> son of Zerahiah, son of Uzzi, son of Bukki,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 6:25; 2 Chronicles 26:20; 1 Chronicles 6:4"> son of Abishua, son of Phinehas, son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the head priest;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra 8:22; Ezra 7:28; Ezra 8:31"> Ezra himself hath come up from Babylon, and he &#91;is&#93; a scribe ready in the law of Moses, that Jehovah God of Israel gave, and the king giveth to him — according to the hand of Jehovah his God upon him — all his request.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezra 8:1–20; 1 Chronicles 6:31–48; 1 Chronicles 9:17–27"> And there go up of the sons of Israel, and of the priests, and the Levites, and the singers, and the gatekeepers, and the Nethinim, unto Jerusalem, in the seventh year of Artaxerxes the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And he cometh in to Jerusalem in the fifth month, that &#91;is in&#93; the seventh year of the king,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezra 7:6; Nehemiah 2:8; Nehemiah 2:18"> for on the first of the month he hath founded the ascent from Babylon, and on the first of the fifth month he hath come in unto Jerusalem, according to the good hand of his God upon him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Malachi 2:7; 2 Timothy 4:2; Matthew 7:24"> for Ezra hath prepared his heart to seek the law of Jehovah, and to do, and to teach in Israel statute and judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezra 5:6; Ezra 7:6; Matthew 23:2"> And this &#91;is&#93; a copy of the letter that the king Artaxerxes gave to Ezra the priest, the scribe, a scribe of the words of the commands of Jehovah, and of His statutes on Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 2:37; Ezekiel 26:7; Ezra 4:17"> 'Artaxerxes, king of kings, to Ezra the priest, a perfect scribe of the law of the God of heaven, and at such a time:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra 6:1; Psalms 148:6; 2 Chronicles 30:5"> By me hath been made a decree that every one who is willing, in my kingdom, of the people of Israel and of its priests and Levites, to go to Jerusalem with thee, doth go;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esther 1:14; Ezra 7:25–26; Ezra 7:28"> because that from the king and his seven counsellors thou art sent, to inquire concerning Judah and concerning Jerusalem, with the law of God that &#91;is&#93; in thy hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 135:21; 2 Chronicles 6:2; Ezra 6:12"> and to carry silver and gold that the king and his counsellors willingly offered to the God of Israel, whose tabernacle &#91;is&#93; in Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:6; Ezra 1:4; 1 Chronicles 29:9"> and all the silver and gold that thou findest in all the province of Babylon, with the free-will offerings of the people, and of the priests, offering willingly, for the house of their God that &#91;is&#93; in Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5–11; Numbers 15:4–13; Ezra 6:9–10"> therefore thou dost speedily buy with this money, bullocks, rams, lambs, and their presents, and their libations, and dost bring them near to the altar of the house of your God that &#91;is&#93; in Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 22:7; Ephesians 5:17; 2 Kings 12:15"> and that which to thee and to thy brethren is good to do with the rest of the silver and gold, according to the will of your God ye do.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:17; Ezra 8:33–34; 2 Chronicles 32:19"> 'And the vessels that are given to thee, for the service of the house of thy God, make perfect before the God of Jerusalem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezra 6:4; Ezra 6:8–18"> and the rest of the needful things of the house of thy God, that it falleth to thee to give, thou dost give from the treasure-house of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezra 4:16; Ezra 7:6; Ezra 7:10–13"> 'And by me — I Artaxerxes the king — is made a decree to all treasurers who &#91;are&#93; beyond the river, that all that Ezra the priest, scribe of the law of the God of heaven, doth ask of you, be done speedily:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 16:6–7; Leviticus 2:13; Ezekiel 45:14"> Unto silver a hundred talents, and unto wheat a hundred cors, and unto wine a hundred baths, and unto oil a hundred baths, and salt without reckoning;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 119:4; Ezra 7:18; Ezra 6:10–12"> all that &#91;is&#93; by the decree of the God of heaven, let be done diligently for the house of the God of heaven; for why is there wrath against the kingdom of the king and his sons?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezra 7:7; Ezra 4:13; Ezra 2:36–55"> 'And to you we are making known, that upon any of the priests and Levites, singers, gatekeepers, Nethinim, and servants of the house of God, tribute and custom there is no authority to lift up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezra 7:10; Deuteronomy 16:18; James 1:5"> 'And thou, Ezra, according to the wisdom of thy God, that &#91;is&#93; in thy hand, appoint magistrates and judges who may be judges to all the people who are beyond the river, to all knowing the law of thy God, and he who hath not known ye cause to know;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezra 6:11; Deuteronomy 13:1–18; Leviticus 20:1–27"> and any who doth not do the law of thy God, and the law of the king, speedily is judgment done upon him, whether to death, or to banishment, or to confiscation of riches, and to bonds.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezra 6:22; Revelation 17:17; Isaiah 60:13"> Blessed &#91;is&#93; Jehovah, God of our fathers, who hath given such a thing as this in the heart of the king, to beautify the house of Jehovah that &#91;is&#93; in Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:17–18; Ezra 5:5; Genesis 32:28"> and unto me hath stretched out kindness before the king and his counsellors, and before all the mighty heads of the king: and I have strengthened myself as the hand of Jehovah my God &#91;is&#93; upon me, and I gather out of Israel heads to go up with me.</span>
</div>
