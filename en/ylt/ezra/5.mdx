<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zechariah 4:6–10; Haggai 1:1; Zechariah 1:1–21"> And prophesied have the prophets, (Haggai the prophet, and Zechariah son of Iddo) unto the Jews who &#91;are&#93; in Judah and in Jerusalem, in the name of the God of Israel — unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezra 3:2; Ezra 6:14; Zechariah 3:1–4"> Then have Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, and Jeshua son of Jozadak, risen, and begun to build the house of God, that &#91;is&#93; in Jerusalem, and with them are the prophets of God supporting them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezra 6:6; Ezra 6:13; Ezra 5:9"> At that time come to them hath Tatnai, governor beyond the river, and Shethar-Boznai, and their companions, and thus they are saying to them, 'Who hath made for you a decree this house to build, and this wall to finish?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Then thus we have said to them, 'What &#91;are&#93; the names of the men who are building this building?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 33:18; Ezra 7:6; Ezra 7:28"> And the eye of their God hath been upon the elders of the Jews, and they have not caused them to cease till the matter goeth to Darius, and then they send back a letter concerning this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra 4:9; Ezra 4:11; Ezra 6:6"> The copy of a letter that Tatnai, governor beyond the river, hath sent, and Shethar-Boznai and his companions, the Apharsachites who &#91;are&#93; beyond the river, unto Darius the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezra 4:17; Daniel 6:21; 2 Thessalonians 3:16"> A letter they have sent unto him, and thus is it written in it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:6; Mark 13:1–2; Daniel 3:26"> 'To Darius the king, all peace! be it known to the king that we have gone to the province of Judah, to the great house of God, and it is built &#91;with&#93; rolled stones, and wood is placed in the walls, and this work is done speedily, and prospering in their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezra 5:3–4"> Then we have asked of these elders, thus we have said to them, Who hath made for you a decree this house to build, and this wall to finish?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And also their names we have asked of them, to let thee know, that we might write the names of the men who &#91;are&#93; at their head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 6:16; Jonah 1:9; Acts 27:23"> 'And thus they have returned us word, saying, We &#91;are&#93; servants of the God of heaven and earth, and are building the house that was built many years before this, that a great king of Israel built and finished:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:16–17; Daniel 9:5; 2 Kings 24:2"> but after that our fathers made the God of heaven angry, he gave them into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon the Chaldean, and this house he destroyed, and the people he removed to Babylon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra 6:3–5; Isaiah 44:28–1; Ezra 1:1–8"> but in the first year of Cyrus king of Babylon, Cyrus the king made a decree to build this house of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezra 6:5; Daniel 5:2–3; Ezra 5:16"> and also, the vessels of the house of God, of gold and silver, that Nebuchadnezzar had taken forth out of the temple that &#91;is&#93; in Jerusalem, and brought them to the temple of Babylon, them hath Cyrus the king brought forth out of the temple of Babylon, and they have been given to &#91;one&#93;, Sheshbazzar &#91;is&#93; his name, whom he made governor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezra 6:3; Ezra 1:2; Ezra 3:3"> and said to him, These vessels lift up, go, put them down in the temple that &#91;is&#93; in Jerusalem, and the house of God is builded on its place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezra 3:10; Ezra 6:15; Ezra 3:8"> Then hath this Sheshbazzar come — he hath laid the foundations of the house of God that &#91;is&#93; in Jerusalem, and from thence even till now it hath been building, and is not finished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezra 4:15; Proverbs 25:2; Ezra 6:1–5"> 'And now, if to the king it be good, let search be made in the treasure-house of the king, that &#91;is&#93; there in Babylon, whether it be that of Cyrus the king there was made a decree to build this house of God in Jerusalem, and the will of the king concerning this thing he doth send unto us.'</span>
