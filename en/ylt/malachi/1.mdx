<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:1; Isaiah 13:1; Haggai 1:1"> The burden of a word of Jehovah unto Israel by the hand of Malachi:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 41:8–9; Jeremiah 31:3; Deuteronomy 10:15"> I have loved you, said Jehovah, And ye have said, 'In what hast Thou loved us?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Obadiah 1:10; Ezekiel 35:3–4; Jeremiah 49:16–18"> Is not Esau Jacob's brother? — an affirmation of Jehovah, And I love Jacob, and Esau I have hated, And I make his mountains a desolation, And his inheritance for dragons of a wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:14; Ezekiel 35:9; Ezekiel 11:10"> Because Edom saith, 'We have been made poor, And we turn back and we build the wastes,' Thus said Jehovah of Hosts: They do build, and I do destroy, And &#91;men&#93; have called to them, 'O region of wickedness,' 'O people whom Jehovah defied to the age.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:7; Psalms 58:10–11; Ezekiel 38:16"> And your eyes do see, and ye say, 'Magnified is Jehovah beyond the border of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 6:46; Isaiah 1:2; John 13:13–17"> A son honoureth a father, and a servant his master. And if I &#91;am&#93; a father, where &#91;is&#93; Mine honour? And if I &#91;am&#93; a master, where &#91;is&#93; My fear? Said Jehovah of Hosts to you, O priests, despising My name! And ye have said: 'In what have we despised Thy name?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Malachi 1:12; 1 Corinthians 11:27–32; Malachi 1:8"> Ye are bringing nigh on Mine altar polluted bread, And ye have said: 'In what have we polluted Thee?' In your saying: 'The table of Jehovah — it &#91;is&#93; despicable,'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:21; Leviticus 22:19–25; Hosea 8:13"> And when ye bring nigh the blind for sacrifice, 'There is no evil,' And when ye bring nigh the lame and sick, 'There is no evil;' Bring it near, I pray thee, to thy governor — Doth he accept thee? or doth he lift up thy face? Said Jehovah of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 32:11; Jeremiah 27:18; 1 Peter 1:17"> And now, appease, I pray thee, the face of God, And He doth favour us; From your own hand hath this been, Doth He accept of you appearances? Said Jehovah of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Amos 5:21–24; Jeremiah 6:20"> Who &#91;is&#93; even among you, And he shutteth the two-leaved doors? Yea, ye do not kindle Mine altar for nought, I have no pleasure in you, said Jehovah of Hosts, And a present I do not accept of your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 45:6; Isaiah 54:5; Isaiah 66:19–20"> For, from the rising of the sun to its going in, Great &#91;is&#93; My name among nations, And in every place perfume is brought nigh to My name, and a pure present, For great &#91;is&#93; My name among nations, Said Jehovah of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Malachi 1:6–8; Malachi 2:8; Amos 2:7"> And ye are polluting it in your saying, 'The table of Jehovah — it is polluted, As to its fruit — despicable is its food.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Micah 6:3; Leviticus 22:19–23; Mark 14:37–38"> And ye have said, 'Lo, what a weariness,' And ye have puffed at it, said Jehovah of Hosts, And ye have brought in plunder, And the lame and the sick, And ye have brought in the present! Do I accept it from your hand? said Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; Acts 5:1–10; Ecclesiastes 5:4–5"> And cursed &#91;is&#93; a deceiver, who hath in his drove a male, And is vowing, and is sacrificing a marred thing to the Lord, For a great king &#91;am&#93; I, said Jehovah of Hosts, And My name &#91;is&#93; revered among nations!</span>
