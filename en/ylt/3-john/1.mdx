<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 3:18; Romans 16:23; 1 Corinthians 1:14"> The Elder to Gaius the beloved, whom I love in truth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Peter 1:3–9; 2 Peter 3:18; Colossians 1:4–6"> beloved, concerning all things I desire thee to prosper, and to be in health, even as thy soul doth prosper,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 John 1:4; 2 John 1:2; Psalms 119:11"> for I rejoiced exceedingly, brethren coming and testifying of the truth in thee, even as thou in truth dost walk;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 4:19; 2 Timothy 1:2; Proverbs 23:24"> greater than these things I have no joy, that I may hear of my children in truth walking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 6:10; Luke 12:42; Luke 16:10–12"> Beloved, faithfully dost thou do whatever thou mayest work to the brethren and to the strangers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:12; Titus 3:13; Acts 15:3"> who did testify of thy love before an assembly, whom thou wilt do well, having sent forward worthily of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:5; Revelation 2:3; 2 Kings 5:15–16"> because for &#91;His&#93; name they went forth, nothing receiving from the nations;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:5–9; 1 Corinthians 16:10–11; 2 Corinthians 7:2–3"> we, then, ought to receive such, that fellow-workers we may become to the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Titus 1:7–16; Luke 22:24–27; Matthew 23:4–8"> I did write to the assembly, but he who is loving the first place among them — Diotrephes — doth not receive us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 66:5; 2 Corinthians 13:2; Luke 6:22"> because of this, if I may come, I will cause him to remember his works that he doth, with evil words prating against us; and not content with these, neither doth he himself receive the brethren, and those intending he doth forbid, and out of the assembly he doth cast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 1:16–17; Psalms 37:27; Ephesians 5:1"> Beloved, be not thou following that which is evil, but that which is good; he who is doing good, of God he is, and he who is doing evil hath not seen God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:7; John 21:24; Acts 22:12"> to Demetrius testimony hath been given by all, and by the truth itself, and we also — we do testify, and ye have known that our testimony is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 John 1:12"> Many things I had to write, but I do not wish through ink and pen to write to thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 43:23; Ephesians 6:23; Romans 16:1–16"> and I hope straightway to see thee, and mouth to mouth we shall speak. Peace to thee! salute thee do the friends; be saluting the friends by name.</span>
