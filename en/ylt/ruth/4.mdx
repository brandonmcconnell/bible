<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:7; Ruth 3:12; Deuteronomy 16:18"> And Boaz hath gone up to the gate, and sitteth there, and lo, the redeemer is passing by of whom Boaz had spoken, and he saith, 'Turn aside, sit down here, such a one, such a one;' and he turneth aside and sitteth down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 31:23; 1 Kings 21:8; Acts 6:12"> And he taketh ten men of the elders of the city, and saith, 'Sit down here;' and they sit down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 13:10; Psalms 112:5"> And he saith to the redeemer, 'A portion of the field which &#91;is&#93; to our brother, to Elimelech, hath Naomi sold, who hath come back from the fields of Moab;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 25:25–29; Jeremiah 32:7–12; Romans 12:17"> and I said, I uncover thine ear, saying, Buy before the inhabitants, and before the elders of my people; if thou dost redeem — redeem, and if none doth redeem — declare to me, and I know, for there is none save thee to redeem, and I after thee.' And he saith, I redeem &#91;it&#93;.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 38:8; Deuteronomy 25:5–6; Matthew 22:24"> And Boaz saith, 'In the day of thy buying the field from the hand of Naomi, then from Ruth the Moabitess, wife of the dead, thou hast bought &#91;it&#93;, to raise up the name of the dead over his inheritance.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 25:25; Ruth 3:13"> And the redeemer saith, 'I am not able to redeem &#91;it&#93; for myself, lest I destroy mine inheritance; redeem for thyself — thou — my right of redemption, for I am not able to redeem.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:7–10"> And this &#91;is&#93; formerly in Israel for redemption and for changing, to establish anything: a man hath drawn off his sandal, and given &#91;it&#93; to his neighbour, and this &#91;is&#93; the testimony in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the redeemer saith to Boaz, 'Buy &#91;it&#93; for thyself,' and draweth off his sandal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:10–12; Genesis 23:16–18"> And Boaz saith to the elders, and &#91;to&#93; all the people, 'Witnesses &#91;are&#93; ye to-day that I have bought all that &#91;is&#93; to Elimelech, and all that &#91;is&#93; to Chilion and Mahlon, from the hand of Naomi;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:6; Ephesians 5:25; Genesis 29:27"> and also Ruth the Moabitess, wife of Mahlon, I have bought to myself for a wife, to raise up the name of the dead over his inheritance; and the name of the dead is not cut off from among his brethren, and from the gate of his place; witnesses ye &#91;are&#93; to-day.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 29:32–24; Micah 5:2; Matthew 2:6"> And all the people who &#91;are&#93; in the gate say — also the elders — 'Witnesses! Jehovah make the woman who is coming in unto thy house as Rachel and as Leah, both of whom built the house of Israel; and do thou virtuously in Ephrathah, and proclaim the Name in Beth-Lehem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 38:29; 1 Samuel 2:20; 1 Chronicles 2:4"> and let thy house be as the house of Pharez (whom Tamar bare to Judah), of the seed which Jehovah doth give to thee of this young woman.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 33:5; Genesis 29:31; Ruth 3:11"> And Boaz taketh Ruth, and she becometh his wife, and he goeth in unto her, and Jehovah giveth to her conception, and she beareth a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 1:5–20; Luke 1:58; Genesis 12:2"> And the women say unto Naomi, 'Blessed &#91;is&#93; Jehovah who hath not let a redeemer cease to thee to-day, and his name is proclaimed in Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 55:22; Isaiah 46:4; Genesis 45:11"> and he hath been to thee for a restorer of life, and for a nourisher of thine old age, for thy daughter-in-law who hath loved thee — who is better to thee than seven sons — hath borne him.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Naomi taketh the lad, and layeth him in her bosom, and is to him for a nurse;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 1:58–63"> and the neighbouring women give to him a name, saying, 'There hath been a son born to Naomi,' and they call his name Obed; he &#91;is&#93; father of Jesse, father of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 1:3–6; 1 Chronicles 2:4–8; Luke 3:33"> And these are genealogies of Pharez: Pharez begat Hezron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 3:33; 1 Chronicles 2:9–10; Matthew 1:4"> and Hezron begat Ram, and Ram begat Amminidab,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 1:7; Luke 3:32; Matthew 1:4"> and Amminidab begat Nahshon, and Nahshon begat Salmon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 3:32; Matthew 1:5; 1 Chronicles 2:11–12"> and Salmon begat Boaz, and Boaz begat Obed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:15; Isaiah 11:1; Matthew 1:6"> and Obed begat Jesse, and Jesse begat David.</span>
