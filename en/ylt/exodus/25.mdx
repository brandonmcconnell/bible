<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Nehemiah 11:2; Ezra 2:68"> 'Speak unto the sons of Israel, and they take for Me a heave-offering; from every man whose heart impelleth him ye do take My heave-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:9; Job 28:2"> 'And this &#91;is&#93; the heave-offering which ye take from them; gold, and silver, and brass,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 19:8; Genesis 41:42; Ezekiel 16:10"> and blue, and purple, and scarlet, and linen, and goats' &#91;hair&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 26:37–1; Exodus 26:14–15; Exodus 36:20"> and rams' skins made red, and badgers' skins, and shittim wood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 27:20; Exodus 25:37; Exodus 40:24–25"> oil for the light, spices for the anointing oil, and for the perfume of the spices,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Exodus 28:6–30"> shoham stones, and stones for setting for an ephod, and for a breastplate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Kings 6:13; Revelation 21:3"> 'And they have made for Me a sanctuary, and I have tabernacled in their midst;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Hebrews 8:5; Acts 7:44"> according to all that which I am shewing thee, the pattern of the tabernacle, and the pattern of all its vessels, even so ye do make &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 9:4; Exodus 37:1–3; Deuteronomy 10:1–3"> 'And they have made an ark of shittim wood; two cubits and a half its length, and a cubit and a half its breadth, and a cubit and a half its height;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 25:24; Exodus 30:3; 2 Chronicles 3:4"> and thou hast overlaid it &#91;with&#93; pure gold, within and without thou dost overlay it, and thou hast made on it a ring of gold round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 27:7; Exodus 25:26; Exodus 25:15"> 'And thou hast cast for it four rings of gold, and hast put &#91;them&#93; on its four feet, even two rings on its one side, and two rings on its second side;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:15; Numbers 4:11; Exodus 27:6"> and thou hast made staves of shittim wood, and hast overlaid them &#91;with&#93; gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and hast brought the staves into the rings on the sides of the ark, to bear the ark by them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 8:8; 2 Chronicles 5:9"> in the rings of the ark are the staves, they are not turned aside from it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:26; 1 Kings 8:9; Hebrews 9:4"> and thou hast put unto the ark the testimony which I give unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 9:5; Exodus 37:6; Hebrews 4:16"> 'And thou hast made a mercy-seat of pure gold, two cubits and a half its length, and a cubit and a half its breadth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:20; Hebrews 9:5; 1 Kings 8:6–7"> and thou hast made two cherubs of gold, beaten work dost thou make them, at the two ends of the mercy-seat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and make thou one cherub at the end on this side, and one cherub at the end on that; at the mercy-seat ye do make the cherubs on its two ends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:18; 1 Kings 8:7; Hebrews 9:5"> 'And the cherubs have been spreading out wings on high, covering the mercy-seat over with their wings, and their faces &#91;are&#93; one towards another — towards the mercy-seat are the faces of the cherubs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 26:34; Romans 10:4; Exodus 25:16–17"> 'And thou hast put the mercy-seat on the ark above, and unto the ark thou dost put the testimony which I give unto thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 29:42–43; Numbers 7:89; Isaiah 37:16"> and I have met with thee there, and have spoken with thee from off the mercy-seat (from between the two cherubs, which &#91;are&#93; on the ark of the testimony) all that which I command thee concerning the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 9:2; Exodus 37:10–16; 1 Kings 7:48"> 'And thou hast made a table of shittim wood, two cubits its length, and a cubit its breadth, and a cubit and a half its height,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 25:11; 1 Kings 6:20–22"> and hast overlaid it &#91;with&#93; pure gold, and hast made for it a crown of gold round about,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 37:2; Exodus 30:3"> and hast made for it a border of a handbreadth round about, and hast made a crown of gold to its border round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> 'And thou hast made to it four rings of gold, and hast put the rings on the four corners, which &#91;are&#93; to its four feet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 25:14; Exodus 25:28"> over-against the border are the rings for places for staves to bear the table;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 25:14; Acts 9:15; Numbers 10:17"> and thou hast made the staves of shittim wood, and hast overlaid them with gold, and the table hath been borne with them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 4:7; Exodus 37:16; Revelation 3:20"> and thou hast made its dishes, and its bowls, and its covers, and its cups, with which they pour out; of pure gold thou dost make them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 24:5–9; Matthew 12:4; Exodus 39:36"> and thou hast put on the table bread of the presence before Me continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 7:49; Zechariah 4:2; Exodus 37:17–24"> 'And thou hast made a candlestick of pure gold, of beaten work is the candlestick made; its base, and its branch, its calyxes, its knops, and its flowers are of the same;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> and six branches are coming out of its sides, three branches of the candlestick out of the one side, and three branches of the candlestick out of the second side;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 17:4–8; Jeremiah 1:11–12; Zechariah 4:3"> three calyxes made like almonds in the one branch, a knop and a flower, and three calyxes made like almonds in one branch, a knop and a flower; so for the six branches which are coming out from the candlestick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> 'And in the candlestick &#91;are&#93; four calyxes made like almonds, its knops and its flowers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> and a knop under two branches of the same, and a knop under two branches of the same, and a knop under two branches of the same, &#91;are&#93; to the six branches which are coming out of the candlestick;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 25:18; 1 Kings 10:16–17; 2 Chronicles 9:15"> their knops and their branches are of the same, all of it one beaten work of pure gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 8:2; Revelation 1:20–1; Leviticus 24:2–4"> and thou hast made its seven lamps, and &#91;one&#93; hath caused its lights to go up, and it hath given light over-against its front.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Kings 12:13; Jeremiah 52:18; 2 Kings 25:14"> 'And its snuffers and its snuff dishes &#91;are&#93; of pure gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> of a talent of pure gold he doth make it, with all these vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Hebrews 8:5; Exodus 26:30; Acts 7:44"> And see thou and do &#91;them&#93; by their pattern which thou art shewn in the mount.</span>
