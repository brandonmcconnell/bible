<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 8:3; Exodus 37:25–28; Exodus 40:5"> 'And thou hast made an altar &#91;for&#93; making perfume; &#91;of&#93; shittim wood thou dost make it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 27:2"> a cubit its length, and a cubit its breadth, (it is square), and two cubits its height; its horns &#91;are&#93; of the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 25:11; Exodus 25:24"> 'And thou hast overlaid it with pure gold, its top, and its sides round about, and its horns; and thou hast made to it a crown of gold round about;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 25:27; Exodus 26:29; Exodus 27:7"> and two rings of gold thou dost make to it under its crown; on its two ribs thou dost make &#91;them&#93;, on its two sides, and they have become places for staves, to bear it with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 25:13; Exodus 25:27"> 'And thou hast made the staves of shittim wood, and hast overlaid them with gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 25:21–22; Exodus 30:36; Leviticus 16:13"> and thou hast put it before the vail, which &#91;is&#93; by the ark of the testimony, before the mercy-seat which &#91;is&#93; over the testimony, whither I am met with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:28; 1 Chronicles 23:13; Exodus 27:20–21"> 'And Aaron hath made perfume on it, perfume of spices, morning by morning; in his making the lamps right he doth perfume it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; 1 Thessalonians 5:17; Hebrews 7:25"> and in Aaron's causing the lamps to go up between the evenings, he doth perfume it; a continual perfume before Jehovah to your generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1"> 'Ye do not cause strange perfume to go up upon it, and burnt-offering, and present, and libation ye do not pour out on it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 16:18; Hebrews 9:22–23; Leviticus 16:29–30"> and Aaron hath made atonement on its horns, once in a year, by the blood of the sin-offering of atonements; once in a year doth he make atonement for it, to your generations; it &#91;is&#93; most holy to Jehovah.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 31:50; 1 Chronicles 27:24; Exodus 38:25–26"> 'When thou takest up the sum of the sons of Israel for their numbers, then they have given each an atonement &#91;for&#93; his soul to Jehovah in their being numbered, and there is no plague among them in their being numbered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 27:25; Numbers 3:47; Ezekiel 45:12"> 'This they do give, every one passing over unto those numbered, half a shekel, by the shekel of the sanctuary (the shekel &#91;is&#93; twenty gerahs); half a shekel &#91;is&#93; the heave-offering to Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 1:20; Numbers 1:18; Numbers 1:3"> every one passing over unto those numbered, from a son of twenty years and upwards, doth give the heave-offering of Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 31:50; Leviticus 17:11; Exodus 30:12"> the rich doth not multiply, and the poor doth not diminish from the half-shekel, to give the heave-offering of Jehovah, to make atonement for your souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:32–33; Numbers 16:40; Exodus 38:25–31"> 'And thou hast taken the atonement-money from the sons of Israel, and hast given it for the service of the tent of meeting; and it hath been to the sons of Israel for a memorial before Jehovah, to make atonement for your souls.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 40:7; Exodus 38:8; 1 Kings 7:23"> 'And thou hast made a laver of brass (and its base of brass), for washing; and thou hast put it between the tent of meeting and the altar, and hast put water there;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; Exodus 40:31–32; Psalms 26:6"> and Aaron and his sons have washed at it their hands and their feet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1–3; Hebrews 12:28–29; 1 Chronicles 13:10"> in their going in unto the tent of meeting they wash &#91;with&#93; water, and die not; or in their drawing nigh unto the altar to minister, to perfume a fire-offering to Jehovah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 28:43; Exodus 27:21"> then they have washed their hands and their feet, and they die not, and it hath been to them a statute age-during, to him and to his seed to their generations.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:19; Song of Solomon 4:14; Ezekiel 27:22"> 'And thou, take to thyself principal spices, wild honey five hundred &#91;shekels&#93;; and spice-cinnamon, the half of that, two hundred and fifty; and spice-cane two hundred and fifty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 45:8; Exodus 29:40; Numbers 3:47"> and cassia five hundred, by the shekel of the sanctuary, and olive oil a hin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; 1 Chronicles 9:30; Exodus 40:9"> and thou hast made it a holy anointing oil, a compound mixture, work of a compounder; it is a holy anointing oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 7:1; 1 John 2:20; Isaiah 61:1"> 'And thou hast anointed with it the tent of meeting, and the ark of the testimony,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> and the table and all its vessels, and the candlestick and its vessels, and the altar of perfume,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and the altar of burnt-offering and all its vessels, and the laver and its base;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 23:19; Exodus 29:37; Leviticus 6:18"> and thou hast sanctified them, and they have been most holy; all that is coming against them is holy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 8:12; Exodus 28:3; Leviticus 8:30"> and Aaron and his sons thou dost anoint, and hast sanctified them for being priests to Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; Leviticus 21:10; Psalms 89:20"> 'And unto the sons of Israel thou dost speak, saying, A holy anointing oil is this to Me, to your generations;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 30:25; Exodus 30:37–38; Leviticus 21:10"> on flesh of man it is not poured, and with its proper proportion ye make none like it; it &#91;is&#93; holy; it is holy to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 30:38; Genesis 17:14; Exodus 12:15"> a man who compoundeth &#91;any&#93; like it, or who putteth of it on a stranger — hath even been cut off from his people.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; Exodus 25:6; Exodus 30:23"> And Jehovah saith unto Moses, 'Take to thee spices, stacte, and onycha, and galbanum, spices and pure frankincense; they are part for part;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 2:13; Exodus 30:25; John 12:3"> and thou hast made it a perfume, a compound, work of a compounder, salted, pure, holy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 25:22; Exodus 30:6; Exodus 16:34"> and thou hast beaten &#91;some&#93; of it small, and hast put of it before the testimony, in the tent of meeting, whither I am met with thee; most holy it is to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3; Exodus 29:37; Exodus 30:32–33"> 'As to the perfume which thou makest, with its proper proportion ye do not make to yourselves, holy it is to thee to Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 30:33"> a man who maketh &#91;any&#93; like it — to be refreshed by it — hath even been cut off from his people.'</span>
