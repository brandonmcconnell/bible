<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 36:8–19; Exodus 26:36; Exodus 26:31"> 'And thou dost make the tabernacle: ten curtains of twined linen, and blue, and purple, and scarlet; &#91;with&#93; cherubs, work of a designer, thou dost make them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:2; Numbers 4:25; 1 Chronicles 17:1"> the length of the one curtain &#91;is&#93; eight and twenty by the cubit, and the breadth of the one curtain four by the cubit, one measure &#91;is&#93; to all the curtains;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 36:10; Colossians 2:2; Ephesians 2:21–22"> five of the curtains are joining one unto another, and five curtains are joining one to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 36:11–12; Exodus 36:17; Exodus 26:10–11"> 'And thou hast made loops of blue upon the edge of the one curtain, at the end in the joining; and so thou makest in the edge of the outermost curtain, in the joining of the second.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> fifty loops thou dost make in the one curtain, and fifty loops thou dost make in the edge of the curtain which &#91;is&#93; in the joining of the second, causing the loops to take hold one unto another;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 36:13; Exodus 26:11; Exodus 26:33"> and thou hast made fifty hooks of gold, and hast joined the curtains one to another by the hooks, and the tabernacle hath been one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 26:14; Psalms 45:13; Exodus 26:9"> 'And thou hast made curtains of goats' &#91;hair&#93;, for a tent over the tabernacle; thou dost make eleven curtains:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 26:13; Exodus 26:2"> the length of the one curtain &#91;is&#93; thirty by the cubit, and the breadth of the one curtain four by the cubit; one measure &#91;is&#93; to the eleven curtains;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> and thou hast joined the five curtains apart, and the six curtains apart, and hast doubled the six curtains over-against the front of the tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> 'And thou hast made fifty loops on the edge of the one curtain, the outermost in the joining, and fifty loops on the edge of the curtain which is joining the second;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 26:3; Exodus 26:6"> and thou hast made fifty hooks of brass, and hast brought in the hooks into the loops, and hast joined the tent, and it hath been one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> 'And the superfluity in the curtains of the tent — the half of the curtain which is superfluous — hath spread over the hinder part of the tabernacle;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 26:2; Exodus 26:8"> and the cubit on this side, and the cubit on that, in the superfluity in the length of the curtains of the tent, is spread out over the sides of the tabernacle, on this and on that, to cover it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 36:19; Exodus 25:5; Exodus 35:23"> and thou hast made a covering for the tent, of rams' skins made red, and a covering of badgers' skins above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 36:20–33; Numbers 4:31–32; Exodus 40:17–18"> 'And thou hast made the boards for the tabernacle, of shittim wood, standing up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> ten cubits &#91;is&#93; the length of the board, and a cubit and a half the breadth of the one board;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 36:22; Exodus 26:19; Exodus 36:24"> two handles &#91;are&#93; to the one board, joined one unto another; so thou dost make for all the boards of the tabernacle;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and thou hast made the boards of the tabernacle: twenty boards for the south side southward;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 38:27; Exodus 38:30–31; Exodus 26:37"> and forty sockets of silver thou dost make under the twenty boards, two sockets under the one board for its two handles, and two sockets under the other board for its two handles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> 'And for the second side of the tabernacle, for the north side, &#91;are&#93; twenty boards,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and their forty sockets of silver, two sockets under the one board, and two sockets under another board.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And for the sides of the tabernacle westward, thou dost make six boards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And two boards thou dost make for the corners of the tabernacle in the two sides.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 36:29–30; Psalms 133:1–3; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> And they are pairs beneath, and together they are pairs above its head unto the one ring; so is it for them both, they are for the two corners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And they have been eight boards, and their sockets of silver &#91;are&#93; sixteen sockets, two sockets under the one board, and two sockets under another board.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 15:1; Numbers 4:31; Galatians 6:1–2"> 'And thou hast made bars of shittim wood: five for the boards of the one side of the tabernacle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> and five bars for the boards of the second side of the tabernacle, and five bars for the boards of the side of the tabernacle at the two sides, westward;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and one hath caused the middle bar in the midst of the boards to reach from end unto end;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 25:11–12"> and the boards thou dost overlay &#91;with&#93; gold, and their rings thou dost make of gold places for bars, and hast overlaid their bars with gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Hebrews 8:5; Exodus 25:9"> and thou hast raised up the tabernacle according to its fashion which thou hast been shewn in the mount.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; 2 Chronicles 3:14; Exodus 36:35"> 'And thou hast made a vail of blue, and purple, and scarlet, and twined linen, work of a designer; he maketh it &#91;with&#93; cherubs;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 26:37; Exodus 36:38; Esther 1:6"> and thou hast put it on four pillars of shittim wood, overlaid &#91;with&#93; gold, their pegs &#91;are&#93; of gold, on four sockets of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 9:2–3; Leviticus 16:2; Exodus 40:21"> 'And thou hast put the vail under the hooks, and hast brought in thither within the vail the ark of the testimony; and the vail hath made a separation for you between the holy and the holy of holies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 40:20; Exodus 25:21; Hebrews 9:5"> 'And thou hast put the mercy-seat on the ark of the testimony, in the holy of holies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Exodus 40:24; Exodus 40:22; Hebrews 9:2"> 'And thou hast set the table at the outside of the vail, and the candlestick over-against the table on the side of the tabernacle southward, and the table thou dost put on the north side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 36:37; Numbers 9:15; Exodus 26:31"> 'And thou hast made a covering for the opening of the tent, blue, and purple, and scarlet, and twined linen, work of an embroiderer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 36:38"> and thou hast made for the covering five pillars of shittim &#91;wood&#93;, and hast overlaid them &#91;with&#93; gold, their pegs &#91;are&#93; of gold, and thou hast cast for them five sockets of brass.</span>
