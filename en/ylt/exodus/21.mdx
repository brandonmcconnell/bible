<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:14; Deuteronomy 6:20; Exodus 24:3–4"> 'And these &#91;are&#93; the judgments which thou dost set before them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:12–15; Exodus 12:44; Deuteronomy 15:18"> 'When thou buyest a Hebrew servant — six years he doth serve, and in the seventh he goeth out as a freeman for nought;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:12–14"> if by himself he cometh in, by himself he goeth out; if he &#91;is&#93; owner of a wife, then his wife hath gone out with him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> if his lord give to him a wife, and she hath borne to him sons or daughters — the wife and her children are her lord's, and he goeth out by himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:14–15; Deuteronomy 15:16–17; Isaiah 26:13"> 'And if the servant really say: I have loved my lord, my wife, and my sons — I do not go out free;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:17; Leviticus 25:40; Deuteronomy 19:17–18"> then hath his lord brought him nigh unto God, and hath brought him nigh unto the door, or unto the side-post, and his lord hath bored his ear with an awl, and he hath served him — to the age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:5; Exodus 21:2–3"> 'And when a man selleth his daughter for a handmaid, she doth not go out according to the going out of the men-servants;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 28:8; Deuteronomy 20:7; Malachi 2:11–15"> if evil in the eyes of her lord, so that he hath not betrothed her, then he hath let her be ransomed; to a strange people he hath not power to sell her, in his dealing treacherously with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> 'And if to his son he betroth her, according to the right of daughters he doth to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:1–6"> 'If another &#91;woman&#93; he take for him, her food, her covering, and her habitation, he doth not withdraw;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and if these three he do not to her, then she hath gone out for nought, without money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 9:6; Matthew 26:52; Leviticus 24:17"> 'He who smiteth a man so that he hath died, is certainly put to death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 20:2–9; 1 Samuel 24:18; Deuteronomy 4:41–43"> as to him who hath not laid wait, and God hath brought to his hand, I have even set for thee a place whither he doth flee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 2:28–34; Hebrews 10:26; Numbers 15:30–31"> 'And when a man doth presume against his neighbour to slay him with subtilty, from Mine altar thou dost take him to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:18–21; Proverbs 30:17; 1 Timothy 1:9"> 'And he who smiteth his father or his mother is certainly put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:7; 1 Timothy 1:10; Exodus 22:4"> 'And he who stealeth a man, and hath sold him, and he hath been found in his hand, is certainly put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 20:20; Mark 7:10–11; Deuteronomy 27:16"> 'And he who is reviling his father or his mother is certainly put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 21:20; Deuteronomy 25:11; 2 Samuel 14:6"> 'And when men contend, and a man hath smitten his neighbour with a stone, or with the fist, and he die not, but hath fallen on the bed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:29; Zechariah 8:4"> if he rise, and hath gone up and down without on his staff, then hath the smiter been acquitted; only his cessation he giveth, and he is thoroughly healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 29:19; Deuteronomy 19:21; Numbers 35:19"> 'And when a man smiteth his man-servant or his handmaid, with a rod, and he hath died under his hand — he is certainly avenged;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 25:44–46"> only if he remain a day, or two days, he is not avenged, for he &#91;is&#93; his money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:18–19; Exodus 21:30; Deuteronomy 16:18"> 'And when men strive, and have smitten a pregnant woman, and her children have come out, and there is no mischief, he is certainly fined, as the husband of the woman doth lay upon him, and he hath given through the judges;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:21; Numbers 35:31; Leviticus 24:19"> and if there is mischief, then thou hast given life for life,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:21; Matthew 5:38–40; Matthew 7:2"> eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> burning for burning, wound for wound, stripe for stripe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 21:20; Job 31:13–15; Psalms 72:12–14"> 'And when a man smiteth the eye of his man-servant, or the eye of his handmaid, and hath destroyed it, as a freeman he doth send him away for his eye;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> and if a tooth of his man-servant or a tooth of his handmaid he knock out, as a freeman he doth send him away for his tooth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 21:32; Leviticus 20:15–16; Genesis 9:5–6"> 'And when an ox doth gore man or woman, and they have died, the ox is certainly stoned, and his flesh is not eaten, and the owner of the ox &#91;is&#93; acquitted;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:1–9"> and if the ox is &#91;one&#93; accustomed to gore heretofore, and it hath been testified to its owner, and he doth not watch it, and it hath put to death a man or woman, the ox is stoned, and its owner also is put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 21:22; Exodus 30:12; Numbers 35:31–33"> 'If atonement is laid upon him, then he hath given the ransom of his life, according to all that is laid upon him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> whether it gore a son or gore a daughter, according to this judgment it is done to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 26:15; Zechariah 11:12–13; Matthew 27:3–9"> 'If the ox gore a man-servant or a handmaid, thirty silver shekels he doth give to their lord, and the ox is stoned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 28:10; Jeremiah 18:20; Ecclesiastes 10:8"> 'And when a man doth open a pit, or when a man doth dig a pit, and doth not cover it, and an ox or ass hath fallen thither, —</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 22:6; Exodus 21:29–30; Exodus 22:14"> the owner of the pit doth repay, money he doth give back to its owner, and the dead is his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> 'And when a man's ox doth smite the ox of his neighbour, and it hath died, then they have sold the living ox, and halved its money, and also the dead one they do halve;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> or, it hath been known that the ox is &#91;one&#93; accustomed to gore heretofore, and its owner doth not watch it, he certainly repayeth ox for ox, and the dead is his.</span>
