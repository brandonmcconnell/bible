<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 19:8; Exodus 17:6; Psalms 78:70–72"> And Moses hath been feeding the flock of Jethro his father-in-law, priest of Midian, and he leadeth the flock behind the wilderness, and cometh in unto the mount of God, to Horeb;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 7:30–35; Isaiah 43:2; Mark 12:26"> and there appeareth unto him a messenger of Jehovah in a flame of fire, out of the midst of the bush, and he seeth, and lo, the bush is burning with fire, and the bush is not consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 7:31; Psalms 107:8; Job 37:14"> And Moses saith, 'Let me turn aside, I pray thee, and I see this great appearance; wherefore is the bush not burned?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:16; 1 Samuel 3:6; Acts 9:4"> and Jehovah seeth that he hath turned aside to see, and God calleth unto him out of the midst of the bush, and saith, 'Moses, Moses;' and he saith, 'Here &#91;am&#93; I.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 5:15; Acts 7:33; Exodus 19:12"> And He saith, 'Come not near hither: cast thy shoes from off thy feet, for the place on which thou art standing is holy ground.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 20:37; Matthew 22:32; Exodus 4:5"> He saith also, 'I &#91;am&#93; the God of thy father, God of Abraham, God of Isaac, and God of Jacob;' and Moses hideth his face, for he is afraid to look towards God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 145:19; Psalms 106:44; Exodus 2:23–25"> And Jehovah saith, 'I have certainly seen the affliction of My people who &#91;are&#93; in Egypt, and their cry I have heard, because of its exactors, for I have known its pains;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 50:24; Genesis 15:18–21; Exodus 3:17"> and I go down to deliver it out of the hand of the Egyptians, and to cause it to go up out of the land, unto a land good and broad, unto a land flowing with milk and honey — unto the place of the Canaanite, and the Hittite, and the Amorite, and the Perizzite, and the Hivite, and the Jebusite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 22:22–23; Exodus 2:23; Exodus 1:13–14"> 'And now, lo, the cry of the sons of Israel hath come in unto Me, and I have also seen the oppression with which the Egyptians are oppressing them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 6:4; 1 Samuel 12:6; Psalms 105:26"> and now, come, and I send thee unto Pharaoh, and bring thou out My people, the sons of Israel, out of Egypt.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:18; 2 Corinthians 3:5; Exodus 6:12"> And Moses saith unto God, 'Who &#91;am&#93; I, that I go unto Pharaoh, and that I bring out the sons of Israel from Egypt?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Romans 8:31; Isaiah 41:10"> and He saith, 'Because I am with thee, and this &#91;is&#93; to thee the sign that I have sent thee: in thy bringing out the people from Egypt — ye do serve God on this mount.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 30:4; Isaiah 9:6; Jeremiah 23:6"> And Moses saith unto God, 'Lo, I am coming unto the sons of Israel, and have said to them, The God of your fathers hath sent me unto you, and they have said to me, What &#91;is&#93; His name? what do I say unto them?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 8:58; Hebrews 13:8; Revelation 1:8"> And God saith unto Moses, 'I AM THAT WHICH I AM;' He saith also, 'Thus dost thou say to the sons of Israel, I AM hath sent me unto you.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 17:7–8; Hebrews 13:8; Malachi 3:6"> And God saith again unto Moses, 'Thus dost thou say unto the sons of Israel, Jehovah, God of your fathers, God of Abraham, God of Isaac, and God of Jacob, hath sent me unto you; this &#91;is&#93; My name — to the age, and this My memorial, to generation — generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 8:4; Exodus 4:31; Exodus 4:29"> 'Go, and thou hast gathered the elders of Israel, and hast said unto them: Jehovah, God of your fathers, hath appeareth unto me, God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, saying, I have certainly inspected you, and that which is done to you in Egypt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 15:13–21; Genesis 50:24; Genesis 46:4"> and I say, I bring you up out of the affliction of Egypt, unto the land of the Canaanite, and the Hittite, and the Amorite, and the Perizzite, and the Hivite, and the Jebusite, unto a land flowing &#91;with&#93; milk and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 9:1; Exodus 7:16; Exodus 10:24–26"> 'And they have hearkened to thy voice, and thou hast entered, thou and the elders of Israel, unto the king of Egypt, and ye have said unto him, Jehovah, God of the Hebrews, hath met with us; and now, let us go, we pray thee, a journey of three days into the wilderness, and we sacrifice to Jehovah our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 5:2; Exodus 6:1; Exodus 7:1–14"> 'And I — I have known that the king of Egypt doth not permit you to go, unless by a strong hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 7:36; Nehemiah 9:10; Exodus 9:15"> and I have put forth My hand, and have smitten Egypt with all My wonders, which I do in its midst — and afterwards he doth send you away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 12:36; Psalms 105:37; Exodus 11:3"> 'And I have given the grace of this people in the eyes of the Egyptians, and it hath come to pass, when ye go, ye go not empty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:10; Exodus 11:2; Exodus 12:35–36"> and &#91;every&#93; woman hath asked from her neighbour, and from her who is sojourning in her house, vessels of silver, and vessels of gold, and garments, and ye have put &#91;them&#93; on your sons and on your daughters, and have spoiled the Egyptians.'</span>
