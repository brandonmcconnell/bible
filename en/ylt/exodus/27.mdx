<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 38:1–7; Exodus 20:24–26; Exodus 40:10"> 'And thou hast made the altar of shittim wood, five cubits the length, and five cubits the breadth — the altar is square — and three cubits its height.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 118:27; Exodus 29:12; Leviticus 4:7"> And thou hast made its horns on its four corners, its horns are of the same, and thou hast overlaid it &#91;with&#93; brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 7:45; Numbers 4:14; 1 Chronicles 28:17"> And thou hast made its pots to remove its ashes, and its shovels, and its bowls, and its forks, and its fire-pans, even all its vessels thou dost make of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 35:16; Exodus 25:12; Exodus 38:4–5"> 'And thou hast made for it a grate of net-work of brass, and hast made on the net four rings of brass on its four extremities,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 38:4"> and hast put it under the compass of the altar beneath, and the net hath been unto the middle of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 35:13–15; Numbers 4:44; Exodus 30:4"> 'And thou hast made staves for the altar, staves of shittim wood, and hast overlaid them &#91;with&#93; brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 25:28; Exodus 30:4; Numbers 4:13–14"> And the staves have been brought into the rings, and the staves have been on the two sides of the altar in bearing it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Exodus 25:9; Hebrews 8:5"> Hollow with boards thou dost make it, as it hath been shewed thee in the mount, so do they make &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 38:9–20; Ezekiel 40:32; Ezekiel 42:3"> 'And thou hast made the court of the tabernacle: for the south side southward, hangings for the court of twined linen, a hundred by the cubit &#91;is&#93; the length for the one side,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 26:19–21; Jeremiah 52:21; Exodus 36:38"> and its twenty pillars and their twenty sockets &#91;are&#93; of brass, the pegs of the pillars and their fillets &#91;are&#93; of silver;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and so for the north side in length, hangings of a hundred &#91;cubits&#93; in length, and its twenty pillars and their twenty sockets &#91;are&#93; of brass, the pegs of the pillars and their fillets &#91;are&#93; of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> 'And &#91;for&#93; the breadth of the court at the west side &#91;are&#93; hangings of fifty cubits, their pillars ten, and their sockets ten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And &#91;for&#93; the breadth of the court at the east side, eastward, &#91;are&#93; fifty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:9; Exodus 26:36"> And the hangings at the side &#91;are&#93; fifteen cubits, their pillars three, and their sockets three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And at the second side &#91;are&#93; hangings fifteen &#91;cubits&#93;, their pillars three, and their sockets three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 26:36; Judges 5:30; Exodus 28:39"> 'And for the gate of the court a covering of twenty cubits, blue, and purple, and scarlet, and twined linen, work of an embroiderer; their pillars four, their sockets four.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> All the pillars of the court round about &#91;are&#93; filleted &#91;with&#93; silver, their pegs &#91;are&#93; silver, and their sockets brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> 'The length of the court &#91;is&#93; a hundred by the cubit, and the breadth fifty by fifty, and the height five cubits, of twined linen, and their sockets &#91;are&#93; brass,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 35:18; Isaiah 22:23–25; Numbers 4:32"> even all the vessels of the tabernacle, in all its service, and all its pins, and all the pins of the court, &#91;are&#93; brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 24:1–4; Zechariah 4:11–14; Psalms 23:5"> 'And thou — thou dost command the sons of Israel, and they bring unto thee pure beaten olive oil for the light, to cause the lamp to go up continually;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 19:21; Numbers 18:23; 1 Samuel 3:3"> in the tent of meeting, at the outside of the vail, which &#91;is&#93; over the testimony, doth Aaron — his sons also — arrange it from evening till morning before Jehovah — a statute age-during to their generations, from the sons of Israel.</span>
