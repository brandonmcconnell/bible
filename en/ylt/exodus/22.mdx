<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 19:8; 2 Samuel 12:6; Proverbs 6:31"> 'When a man doth steal an ox or sheep, and hath slaughtered it or sold it, five of the herd he doth repay for the ox, and four of the flock for the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 24:43; Matthew 6:19–20; Numbers 35:27"> 'If in the breaking through, the thief is found, and he hath been smitten, and hath died, there is no blood for him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 21:2; Isaiah 50:1; Matthew 18:25"> if the sun hath risen upon him, blood &#91;is&#93; for him, he doth certainly repay; if he have nothing, then he hath been sold for his theft;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 22:7; Exodus 22:1; Exodus 22:9"> if the theft is certainly found in his hand alive, whether ox, or ass, or sheep — double he repayeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 22:3; Exodus 22:12; Job 20:18"> 'When a man depastureth a field or vineyard, and hath sent out his beast, and it hath pastured in the field of another, &#91;of&#93; the best of his field, and the best of his vineyard, he doth repay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 22:9; Exodus 21:33–34; 2 Samuel 14:30–31"> 'When fire goeth forth, and hath found thorns, and a stack, or the standing corn, or the field, hath been consumed, he who causeth the burning doth certainly repay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 22:4; Proverbs 6:30–31; Jeremiah 2:26"> 'When a man doth give unto his neighbour silver, or vessels to keep, and it hath been stolen out of the man's house; if the thief is found, he repayeth double.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 22:28; Deuteronomy 16:18; Exodus 21:6"> 'If the thief is not found, then the master of the house hath been brought near unto God, whether he hath not put forth his hand against the work of his neighbour;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:1; Exodus 22:4; Numbers 5:6–7"> for every matter of transgression, for ox, for ass, for sheep, for raiment, for any lost thing of which it is said that it is his; unto God cometh the matter of them both; he whom God doth condemn, he repayeth double to his neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 12:48; 2 Timothy 1:12; Luke 16:11"> 'When a man doth give unto his neighbour an ass, or ox, or sheep, or any beast to keep, and it hath died, or hath been hurt, or taken captive, none seeing —</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 6:16; Leviticus 5:1; 1 Kings 2:42–43"> an oath of Jehovah is between them both, that he hath not put forth his hand against the work of his neighbour, and its owner hath accepted, and he doth not repay;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 31:39; Exodus 22:7"> but if it is certainly stolen from him, he doth repay to its owner;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Nahum 2:12; Micah 5:8; Ezekiel 4:14"> if it is certainly torn, he bringeth it in — a witness; the torn thing he doth not repay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:19–20; Exodus 21:34; Deuteronomy 15:2"> 'And when a man doth ask &#91;anything&#93; from his neighbour, and it hath been hurt or hath died — its owner not being with it — he doth certainly repay;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 8:10"> if its owner &#91;is&#93; with it, he doth not repay, — if it &#91;is&#93; a hired thing, it hath come for its hire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:28–29; Genesis 34:2–4"> 'And when a man doth entice a virgin who &#91;is&#93; not betrothed, and hath lain with her, he doth certainly endow her to himself for a wife;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 34:12; 1 Samuel 18:25; Deuteronomy 22:29"> if her father utterly refuse to give her to him, money he doth weigh out according to the dowry of virgins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 20:27; Deuteronomy 18:10–11; 1 Samuel 28:3"> 'A witch thou dost not keep alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 18:23; Deuteronomy 27:21; Leviticus 20:15–16"> 'Whoever lieth with a beast is certainly put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:2–5; Numbers 25:7–8; Deuteronomy 18:20"> 'He who is sacrificing to a god, save to Jehovah alone, is devoted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 23:9; Deuteronomy 10:19; Leviticus 19:33"> 'And a sojourner thou dost not oppress, nor crush him, for sojourners ye have been in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:17; Isaiah 1:17; Isaiah 1:23"> 'Any widow or orphan ye do not afflict;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 18:6; Psalms 10:17–18; Luke 18:7"> if thou dost really afflict him, surely if he at all cry unto Me, I certainly hear his cry;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 109:9; Jeremiah 15:8; Psalms 76:7"> and Mine anger hath burned, and I have slain you by the sword, and your wives have been widows, and your sons orphans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 15:5; Leviticus 25:35–37; Deuteronomy 23:19–20"> 'If thou dost lend My poor people with thee money, thou art not to him as a usurer; thou dost not lay on him usury;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 20:16; Amos 2:8; Deuteronomy 24:6"> if thou dost at all take in pledge the garment of thy neighbour, during the going in of the sun thou dost return it to him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 34:6; 2 Chronicles 30:9; Psalms 136:10–11"> for it alone is his covering, it &#91;is&#93; his garment for his skin; wherein doth he lie down? and it hath come to pass, when he doth cry unto Me, that I have heard, for I &#91;am&#93; gracious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 23:5; Ecclesiastes 10:20; Exodus 22:8–9"> 'God thou dost not revile, and a prince among thy people thou dost not curse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 23:16; Exodus 13:2; James 1:18"> 'Thy fulness and thy liquids thou dost not delay; the first-born of thy sons thou dost give to Me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 22:27; Deuteronomy 15:19; Exodus 13:12"> so thou dost to thine ox, to thy sheep; seven days it is with its dam, on the eighth day thou dost give it to Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:14; Leviticus 19:2; Deuteronomy 14:21"> 'And ye are holy men to Me, and flesh torn in the field ye do not eat, to a dog ye do cast it.</span>
