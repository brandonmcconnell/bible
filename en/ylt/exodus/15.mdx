<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 15:3; Exodus 15:21; Psalms 106:12"> Then singeth Moses and the sons of Israel this song to Jehovah, and they speak, saying: — 'I sing to Jehovah, For triumphing He hath triumphed; The horse and its rider He hath thrown into the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 118:14; Psalms 18:46"> My strength and song is JAH, And He is become my salvation: This &#91;is&#93; my God, and I glorify Him; God of my father, and I exalt Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 24:8; Exodus 14:14; Revelation 19:11–21"> Jehovah &#91;is&#93; a man of battle; Jehovah &#91;is&#93; His name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 14:13–28; Exodus 14:6–7"> Chariots of Pharaoh and his force He hath cast into the sea; And the choice of his captains Have sunk in the Red Sea!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:11; Exodus 15:10; Exodus 14:28"> The depths do cover them; They went down into the depths as a stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 118:15–16; Isaiah 51:9; Revelation 2:27"> Thy right hand, O Jehovah, Is become honourable in power; Thy right hand, O Jehovah, Doth crush an enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 5:24; Isaiah 47:14; Malachi 4:1"> And in the abundance of Thine excellency Thou throwest down Thy withstanders, Thou sendest forth Thy wrath — It consumeth them as stubble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 4:9; Psalms 78:13; Exodus 14:21–22"> And by the spirit of Thine anger Have waters been heaped together; Stood as a heap have flowings; Congealed have been depths In the heart of a sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 5:30; Exodus 14:5; Isaiah 53:12"> The enemy said, I pursue, I overtake; I apportion spoil; Filled is my soul with them; I draw out my sword; My hand destroyeth them: —</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Deuteronomy 11:4; Exodus 15:5"> Thou hast blown with Thy wind The sea hath covered them; They sank as lead in mighty waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 8:23; Psalms 89:5–8; 1 Samuel 2:2"> Who &#91;is&#93; like Thee among the gods, O Jehovah? Who &#91;is&#93; like Thee — honourable in holiness — Fearful in praises — doing wonders?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 15:6"> Thou hast stretched out Thy right hand — Earth swalloweth them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 77:20; 1 Peter 1:5; Psalms 77:14–15"> Thou hast led forth in Thy kindness The people whom Thou hast redeemed. Thou hast led on in Thy strength Unto Thy holy habitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:25; Joshua 2:9–10; Psalms 48:6"> Peoples have heard, they are troubled; Pain hath seized inhabitants of Philistia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 5:1; Joshua 2:11; Joshua 2:9"> Then have chiefs of Edom been troubled: Mighty ones of Moab — Trembling doth seize them! Melted have all inhabitants of Canaan!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:25; Psalms 74:2; Joshua 2:9"> Fall on them doth terror and dread; By the greatness of Thine arm They are still as a stone, Till Thy people pass over, O Jehovah; Till the people pass over Whom Thou hast purchased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 44:2; Psalms 80:8; Psalms 78:54–55"> Thou dost bring them in, And dost plant them In a mountain of Thine inheritance, A fixed place for Thy dwelling Thou hast made, O Jehovah; A sanctuary, O Lord, Thy hands have established;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 10:16; Psalms 29:10; Psalms 146:10"> Jehovah reigneth — to the age, and for ever!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 14:28–29; Exodus 14:22–23; Hebrews 11:29"> For the horse of Pharaoh hath gone in with his chariots and with his horsemen into the sea, and Jehovah turneth back on them the waters of the sea, and the sons of Israel have gone on dry land in the midst of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 150:4; 1 Samuel 18:6; Psalms 68:25"> And Miriam the inspired one, sister of Aaron, taketh the timbrel in her hand, and all the women go out after her, with timbrels and with choruses;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 15:1; Revelation 15:3; Revelation 7:10–12"> and Miriam answereth to them: — 'Sing ye to Jehovah, For Triumphing He hath triumphed; The horse and its rider He hath thrown into the sea!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 16:7; 1 Samuel 15:7; Genesis 25:18"> And Moses causeth Israel to journey from the Red Sea, and they go out unto the wilderness of Shur, and they go three days in the wilderness, and have not found water,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ruth 1:20; Numbers 33:8"> and they come in to Marah, and have not been able to drink the waters of Marah, for they &#91;are&#93; bitter; therefore hath &#91;one&#93; called its name Marah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 16:2; Exodus 14:11; Numbers 20:2–5"> And the people murmur against Moses, saying, 'What do we drink?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:2; Psalms 66:10; Judges 3:1"> and he crieth unto Jehovah, and Jehovah sheweth him a tree, and he casteth unto the waters, and the waters become sweet. There He hath made for them a statute, and an ordinance, and there He hath tried them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 103:3; Deuteronomy 7:15; Exodus 23:25"> and He saith, 'If thou dost really hearken to the voice of Jehovah thy God, and dost that which is right in His eyes, and hast hearkened to His commands, and kept all His statutes: none of the sickness which I laid on the Egyptians do I lay on thee, for I, Jehovah, am healing thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Revelation 22:2; Revelation 7:17; Numbers 33:9"> And they come to Elim, and there &#91;are&#93; twelve fountains of water, and seventy palm trees; and they encamp there by the waters.</span>
