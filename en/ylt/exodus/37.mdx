<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 25:10–20; Exodus 26:33; Numbers 10:33–36"> And Bezaleel maketh the ark of shittim wood, two cubits and a half its length, and a cubit and a half its breadth, and a cubit and a half its height;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 30:3"> and he overlayeth it with pure gold within and without, and maketh for it a wreath of gold round about;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and he casteth for it four rings of gold, on its four feet, even two rings on its one side, and two rings on its second side;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Peter 1:7; Numbers 4:14–15"> and he maketh staves of shittim wood, and overlayeth them with gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; 2 Samuel 6:3–7; Numbers 1:50"> and he bringeth in the staves into the rings, by the sides of the ark, to bear the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 9:5; Titus 2:14; Romans 3:25"> And he maketh a mercy-seat of pure gold, two cubits and a half its length, and a cubit and a half its breadth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:2; Psalms 104:4; Psalms 80:1"> and he maketh two cherubs of gold, of beaten work he hath made them, at the two ends of the mercy-seat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> one cherub at the end on this &#91;side&#93;, and one cherub at the end on that, out of the mercy-seat he hath made the cherubs, at its two ends;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 1:51; Hebrews 1:14; Genesis 28:12"> and the cherubs are spreading out wings on high, covering over the mercy-seat with their wings, and their faces &#91;are&#93; one towards another; towards the mercy-seat have the faces of the cherubs been.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 1:14; Ezekiel 40:39–42; Malachi 1:12"> And he maketh the table of shittim wood; two cubits its length, and a cubit its breadth, and a cubit and a half its height,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and overlayeth it with pure gold, and maketh for it a wreath of gold round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And he maketh for it a border of a handbreadth round about, and maketh a wreath of gold for its border round about;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and he casteth for it four rings of gold, and putteth the rings on the four corners which &#91;are&#93; to its four feet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> over-against the border have the rings been, places for staves to bear the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And he maketh the staves of shittim wood, and overlayeth them with gold, to bear the table;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 25:29; 1 Kings 7:50; 2 Kings 12:13"> and he maketh the vessels which &#91;are&#93; upon the table, its dishes, and its bowls, and its cups, and the cups by which they pour out, of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–39; Hebrews 9:2; 2 Chronicles 13:11"> And he maketh the candlestick of pure gold; of beaten work he hath made the candlestick, its base, and its branch, its calyxes, its knops, and its flowers, have been of the same;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and six branches are coming out of its sides, three branches of the candlestick out of its one side, and three branches of the candlestick out of its second side;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> three calyxes, made like almonds, in the one branch, a knop and a flower; and three calyxes, made like almonds, in another branch, a knop and a flower; so to the six branches which are coming out of the candlestick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:5; Jeremiah 1:11; Exodus 25:33"> And in the candlestick &#91;are&#93; four calyxes, made like almonds, its knops, and its flowers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 25:35"> and a knop under the two branches of the same, and a knop under the two branches of the same, and a knop under the two branches of the same, &#91;are&#93; to the six branches which are coming out of it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:5; Exodus 25:31; Psalms 51:17"> their knops and their branches have been of the same; all of it one beaten work of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 1:12; Exodus 25:37; Revelation 1:20–1"> And he maketh its seven lamps, and its snuffers, and its snuff-dishes, of pure gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> of a talent of pure gold he hath made it, and all its vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 30:1–5; Hebrews 7:25; Revelation 8:3–4"> And he maketh the perfume-altar of shittim wood; a cubit its length, and a cubit its breadth (square), and two cubits its height; its horns have been of the same;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and he overlayeth it with pure gold, its top and its sides round about, and its horns; and he maketh for it a wreath of gold round about;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> and two rings of gold he hath made for it under its wreath, at its two corners, at its two sides, for places for staves to bear it with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And he maketh the staves of shittim wood, and overlayeth them with gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 141:2; Hebrews 7:25; 1 John 2:20"> and he maketh the holy anointing oil, and the pure spice-perfume — work of a compounder.</span>
