<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 28:6–12; Leviticus 8:7; Exodus 25:7"> And he maketh the ephod, of gold, blue, and purple, and scarlet, and twined linen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 36:8; Exodus 26:1"> and they expand the plates of gold, and have cut off wires to work in the midst of the blue, and in the midst of the purple, and in the midst of the scarlet, and in the midst of the linen — work of a designer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> shoulder-pieces they have made for it, joining; at its two ends it is joined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 1:13; Matthew 28:20; Isaiah 11:5"> And the girdle of his ephod which &#91;is&#93; on it is of the same, according to its work, of gold, blue, and purple, and scarlet, and twined linen, as Jehovah hath commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 28:9; Ezekiel 28:13; Job 28:16"> And they prepare the shoham stones, set, embroidered &#91;with&#93; gold, opened with openings of a signet, by the names of the sons of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 4:7; Exodus 28:12; Exodus 28:29"> and he setteth them on the shoulders of the ephod — stones of memorial for the sons of Israel, as Jehovah hath commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Leviticus 8:8–9; Isaiah 59:17"> And he maketh the breastplate, work of a designer, like the work of the ephod, of gold, blue, and purple, and scarlet, and twined linen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> it hath been square; double they have made the breastplate, a span its length, and a span its breadth, doubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 28:21; Exodus 28:16–17; Revelation 21:19–21"> And they fill in it four rows of stones; a row of a sardius, a topaz, and a carbuncle &#91;is&#93; the one row;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 28:18; Ezekiel 28:13"> and the second row an emerald, a sapphire, and a diamond;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and the third row an opal, an agate, and an amethyst;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and the fourth row a beryl, an onyx, and a jasper — set, embroidered &#91;with&#93; gold, in their settings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 21:12"> And the stones, according to the names of the sons of Israel, are twelve, according to their names, openings of a signet, each according to his name, for the twelve tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 10:28; John 17:12; 1 Peter 1:5"> And they make on the breastplate wreathed chains, work of thick bands, of pure gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 25:12"> and they make two embroidered things of gold, and two rings of gold, and put the two rings on the two ends of the breastplate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> and they put the two thick bands of gold on the two rings on the ends of the breastplate;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 28:14; Song of Solomon 1:10"> and the two ends of the two thick bands they have put on the two embroidered things, and they put them on the shoulders of the ephod, over-against its front.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And they make two rings of gold, and set &#91;them&#93; on the two ends of the breastplate, on its border, which &#91;is&#93; on the side of the ephod within;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 26:3"> and they make two rings of gold, and put them on the two shoulders of the ephod below, over-against its front, over-against its joining, above the girdle of the ephod;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 16:24; 1 Corinthians 1:25; 1 Corinthians 1:27"> and they bind the breastplate by its rings unto the rings of the ephod, with a ribbon of blue, to be above the girdle of the ephod, and the breastplate is not loosed from off the ephod, as Jehovah hath commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 28:31–35"> And he maketh the upper robe of the ephod, work of a weaver, completely of blue;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and the opening of the upper robe &#91;is&#93; in its midst, as the opening of a habergeon, a border &#91;is&#93; to its opening round about, it is not rent;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 28:33; Galatians 5:22"> and they make on the hems of the upper robe pomegranates of blue, and purple, and scarlet, twined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 28:33–34; Song of Solomon 4:13; Psalms 89:15"> And they make bells of pure gold, and put the bells in the midst of the pomegranates, on the hems of the upper robe, round about, in the midst of the pomegranates;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 28:34; Song of Solomon 4:13; Song of Solomon 6:7"> a bell and a pomegranate, a bell and a pomegranate, &#91;are&#93; on the hems of the upper robe, round about, to minister in, as Jehovah hath commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:18; 1 Peter 1:13; Isaiah 61:10"> And they make the coats of linen, work of a weaver, for Aaron and for his sons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Ezekiel 44:18; Exodus 28:39"> and the mitre of linen, and the beautiful bonnets of linen, and the linen trousers, of twined linen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 28:39"> and the girdle of twined linen, and blue, and purple, and scarlet, work of an embroiderer, as Jehovah hath commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; Hebrews 7:26; Hebrews 1:3"> And they make the flower of the holy crown of pure gold, and write on it a writing, openings of a signet, 'Holy to Jehovah;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> and they put on it a ribbon of blue, to put &#91;it&#93; on the mitre above, as Jehovah hath commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 39:42–43; Matthew 28:20; Exodus 25:40"> And all the service of the tabernacle of the tent of meeting is completed; and the sons of Israel do according to all that Jehovah hath commanded Moses; so they have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 35:11–19; Exodus 31:7–11; Exodus 36:1–38"> And they bring in the tabernacle unto Moses, the tent, and all its vessels, its hooks, its boards, its bars, and its pillars, and its sockets;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> and the covering of rams' skins, which are made red, and the covering of badgers' skins, and the vail of the covering;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Hebrews 9:5; Exodus 25:17; Hebrews 9:8"> the ark of the testimony and its staves, and the mercy-seat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 25:30; 1 Kings 7:48"> the table, all its vessels, and the bread of the presence;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Philippians 2:15; Exodus 27:21; Matthew 5:14–16"> the pure candlestick, its lamps, the lamps of arrangement, and all its vessels, and the oil for the light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 30:7; Exodus 37:29; Exodus 30:3"> And the golden altar, and the anointing oil, and the spice-perfume, and the covering of the opening of the tent;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 8:64; Exodus 38:30"> the brazen altar and the brazen grate which it hath, its staves, and all its vessels, the laver and its base.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Exodus 27:9–16"> The hangings of the court, its pillars, and its sockets; and the covering for the gate of the court, its cords, and its pins; and all the vessels of the service of the tabernacle, for the tent of meeting;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 28:2; Exodus 31:10; Exodus 39:1"> the coloured clothes to minister in the sanctuary, the holy garments for Aaron the priest, and the garments of his sons, to act as priest in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Exodus 35:10; Exodus 23:21–22; 2 Timothy 2:15"> According to all that Jehovah hath commanded Moses, so have the sons of Israel done all the service;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 6:23–27; Leviticus 9:22–23; 2 Samuel 6:18"> and Moses seeth all the work, and lo, they have done it as Jehovah hath commanded; so they have done. And Moses doth bless them.</span>
