<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 26:30; Exodus 13:4; Numbers 7:1"> 'On the first day of the month, in the first month, thou dost raise up the tabernacle of the tent of meeting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:12; Numbers 4:5; Revelation 11:19"> and hast set there the ark of the testimony, and hast covered over the ark with the vail,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 24:8; Exodus 40:22–25; Exodus 37:10–24"> and hast brought in the table, and set its arrangement in order, and hast brought in the candlestick, and caused its lamps to go up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; Hebrews 9:24; 1 John 2:1–2"> 'And thou hast put the golden altar for perfume before the ark of the testimony, and hast put the covering of the opening to the tabernacle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 40:29; 1 John 4:9–10; Hebrews 13:10"> and hast put the altar of the burnt-offering before the opening of the tabernacle of the tent of meeting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 13:1; 1 John 1:7; Psalms 26:6"> and hast put the laver between the tent of meeting and the altar, and hast put water there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 40:33; Ephesians 4:11–12; Matthew 16:18"> 'And thou hast set the court round about, and hast placed the covering of the gate of the court,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 8:10; Numbers 7:1; Matthew 3:16"> and hast taken the anointing oil, and anointed the tabernacle, and all that &#91;is&#93; in it, and hallowed it, and all its vessels, and it hath been holy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 29:36–37; Luke 1:35; Isaiah 61:1"> and thou hast anointed the altar of the burnt-offering, and all its vessels, and sanctified the altar, and the altar hath been most holy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and thou hast anointed the laver and its base, and sanctified it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 8:1–13; Leviticus 9:1–24; Exodus 29:1–35"> 'And thou hast brought near Aaron and his sons unto the opening of the tent of meeting, and hast bathed them with water;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 28:41; 1 John 2:20; 1 John 2:27"> and thou hast clothed Aaron with the holy garments, and anointed him, and sanctified him, and he hath acted as priest to Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; Romans 8:30; 1 Corinthians 1:30"> 'And his sons thou dost bring near, and hast clothed them with coats,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 25:13; Hebrews 5:1–14; Hebrews 8:1–10"> and anointed them as thou hast anointed their father, and they have acted as priests to Me, and their anointing hath been to be to them for a priesthood age-during, to their generations.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:1; Exodus 40:17–32; Deuteronomy 12:32"> And Moses doth according to all that Jehovah hath commanded him; so he hath done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 7:1; Exodus 40:1–2; Numbers 9:1"> And it cometh to pass, in the first month, in the second year, in the first of the month, the tabernacle hath been raised up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:27–28; 1 Peter 1:5; Leviticus 26:11"> and Moses raiseth up the tabernacle, and setteth its sockets, and placeth its boards, and placeth its bars, and raiseth its pillars,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 26:1–14; Exodus 36:8–19"> and spreadeth the tent over the tabernacle, and putteth the covering of the tent upon it above, as Jehovah hath commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 16:34; Deuteronomy 10:5; Hebrews 10:19–21"> And he taketh and putteth the testimony unto the ark, and setteth the staves on the ark, and putteth the mercy-seat on the ark above;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 26:33; Exodus 40:3; Exodus 35:12"> and bringeth in the ark unto the tabernacle, and placeth the vail of the covering, and covereth over the ark of the testimony, as Jehovah hath commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 26:35; John 6:53–57; Ephesians 3:8"> And he putteth the table in the tent of meeting, on the side of the tabernacle northward, at the outside of the vail,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 25:30; Exodus 40:4; Hebrews 9:2"> and setteth in order upon it the arrangement of bread, before Jehovah, as Jehovah hath commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 26:35; Revelation 2:5; John 1:1"> And he putteth the candlestick in the tent of meeting, over-against the table, on the side of the tabernacle southward,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 40:4; Exodus 25:37; Revelation 4:5"> and causeth the lamps to go up before Jehovah, as Jehovah hath commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 40:5; Hebrews 7:25; John 11:42"> And he setteth the golden altar in the tent of meeting, before the vail,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 30:7"> and maketh perfume on it — spice-perfume — as Jehovah hath commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 40:5; Hebrews 10:19–20; Ephesians 2:18"> And he setteth the covering of the opening to the tabernacle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 40:6; Hebrews 13:5–6; Hebrews 13:10"> and the altar of the burnt-offering he hath set at the opening of the tabernacle of the tent of meeting, and causeth the burnt-offering to go up upon it, and the present, as Jehovah hath commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 40:7; Exodus 38:8; Ezekiel 36:25"> And he putteth the laver between the tent of meeting and the altar, and putteth water there for washing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 30:19–20; Psalms 26:6; John 13:10"> and Moses and Aaron and his sons have washed their hands and their feet at the same;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 73:19; Exodus 30:19–20; Exodus 40:19"> in their going in unto the tent of meeting, and in their drawing near unto the altar, they wash, as Jehovah hath commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 40:8; Exodus 27:9–16; John 17:4"> And he raiseth up the court round about the tabernacle, and about the altar, and placeth the covering of the gate of the court; and Moses completeth the work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Revelation 15:8; Numbers 9:15–23; 2 Chronicles 7:2"> And the cloud covereth the tent of meeting, and the honour of Jehovah hath filled the tabernacle;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 8:11; Leviticus 16:2; 2 Chronicles 7:2"> and Moses hath not been able to go in unto the tent of meeting, for the cloud hath tabernacled on it, and the honour of Jehovah hath filled the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:19; Numbers 9:17; Exodus 13:21–22"> And in the going up of the cloud from off the tabernacle the sons of Israel journey in all their journeys;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 9:19–22; Psalms 31:15"> and if the cloud go not up then they journey not, until the day of its going up:</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Psalms 78:14; Numbers 9:15; Exodus 13:21"> for the cloud of Jehovah &#91;is&#93; on the tabernacle by day, and fire is in it by night, before the eyes of all the house of Israel in all their journeys.</span>
