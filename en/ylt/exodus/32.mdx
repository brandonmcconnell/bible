<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 7:40; Deuteronomy 9:9; Exodus 24:18"> And the people see that Moses is delaying to come down from the mount, and the people assemble against Aaron, and say unto him, 'Rise, make for us gods who go before us, for this Moses — the man who brought us up out of the land of Egypt — we have not known what hath happened to him.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 8:24–27; Exodus 12:35–36; Ezekiel 16:17"> And Aaron saith unto them, 'Break off the rings of gold which &#91;are&#93; in the ears of your wives, your sons, and your daughters, and bring in unto me;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 46:6; Isaiah 40:19–20; Jeremiah 10:9"> and all the people themselves break off the rings of gold which &#91;are&#93; in their ears, and bring in unto Aaron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:18; Deuteronomy 9:16; 1 Kings 12:28"> and he receiveth from their hand, and doth fashion it with a graving tool, and doth make it a molten calf, and they say, 'These thy gods, O Israel, who brought thee up out of the land of Egypt.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 10:20; Leviticus 23:2; Leviticus 23:37"> And Aaron seeth, and buildeth an altar before it, and Aaron calleth, and saith, 'A festival to Jehovah — to-morrow;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:7; Numbers 25:2; Acts 7:41–42"> and they rise early on the morrow, and cause burnt-offerings to ascend, and bring nigh peace-offerings; and the people sit down to eat and to drink, and rise up to play.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 32:11; Hosea 9:9; Judges 2:19"> And Jehovah saith unto Moses, 'Go, descend, for thy people whom thou hast brought up out of the land of Egypt hath done corruptly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 12:28; Exodus 20:23; Exodus 20:3–4"> they have turned aside hastily from the way that I have commanded them; they have made for themselves a molten calf, and bow themselves to it, and sacrifice to it, and say, These thy gods, O Israel, who brought thee up out of the land of Egypt.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Deuteronomy 9:13; Isaiah 48:4"> And Jehovah saith unto Moses, 'I have seen this people, and lo, it &#91;is&#93; a stiff-necked people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:14; Numbers 14:12; Deuteronomy 9:19"> and now, let Me alone, and My anger doth burn against them, and I consume them, and I make thee become a great nation.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:18–20; Deuteronomy 9:26–29; Psalms 106:23"> And Moses appeaseth the face of Jehovah his God, and saith, 'Why, O Jehovah, doth Thine anger burn against Thy people, whom Thou hast brought forth out of the land of Egypt with great power and with a strong hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 14:13–16; Deuteronomy 9:28; Joshua 7:9"> why do the Egyptians speak, saying, For evil He brought them out to slay them among mountains, and to consume them from off the face of the ground? turn back from the heat of Thine anger, and repent of the evil against Thy people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 6:13; Genesis 12:7; Genesis 22:16"> 'Be mindful of Abraham, of Isaac, and of Israel, Thy servants, to whom Thou hast sworn by Thyself, and unto whom Thou speakest: I multiply your seed as stars of the heavens, and all this land, as I have said, I give to your seed, and they have inherited to the age;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jonah 3:10; Psalms 106:45; Jeremiah 26:13"> and Jehovah repenteth of the evil which He hath spoken of doing to His people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:15; Exodus 40:20; Deuteronomy 5:22"> And Moses turneth, and goeth down from the mount, and the two tables of the testimony &#91;are&#93; in his hand, tables written on both their sides, on this and on that &#91;are&#93; they written;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 31:18; Hebrews 8:10; Deuteronomy 10:1"> and the tables are the work of God, and the writing is the writing of God, graven on the tables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 24:13; Exodus 17:9; Joshua 6:20"> And Joshua heareth the voice of the people in their shouting, and saith unto Moses, 'A noise of battle in the camp!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 15:1–18; Daniel 5:4; Daniel 5:23"> and he saith, 'It is not the voice of the crying of might, nor is it the voice of the crying of weakness — a voice of singing I am hearing.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:16–17; Jeremiah 31:32; Lamentations 5:15"> And it cometh to pass, when he hath drawn near unto the camp, that he seeth the calf, and the dancing, and the anger of Moses burneth, and he casteth out of his hands the tables, and breaketh them under the mount;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:21; 2 Kings 23:6; 2 Kings 23:15"> and he taketh the calf which they have made, and burneth &#91;it&#93; with fire, and grindeth until &#91;it is&#93; small, and scattereth on the face of the waters, and causeth the sons of Israel to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 20:9; Joshua 7:19–26; 1 Kings 21:22"> And Moses saith unto Aaron, 'What hath this people done to thee, that thou hast brought in upon it a great sin?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:24; Exodus 15:24; Exodus 16:20"> and Aaron saith, 'Let not the anger of my lord burn; thou — thou hast known the people that it &#91;is&#93; in evil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 32:1–4; Exodus 32:8"> and they say to me, Make for us gods, who go before us, for this Moses — the man who brought us up out of the land of Egypt — we have not known what hath happened to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 32:4; Luke 10:29; Romans 3:10"> and I say to them, Whoso hath gold, let them break &#91;it&#93; off, and they give to me, and I cast it into the fire, and this calf cometh out.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 47:3; Micah 1:11; Revelation 16:15"> And Moses seeth the people that it &#91;is&#93; unbridled, for Aaron hath made it unbridled for contempt among its withstanders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 9:32; Matthew 12:30; 2 Samuel 20:11"> and Moses standeth in the gate of the camp, and saith, 'Who &#91;is&#93; for Jehovah? — unto me!' and all the sons of Levi are gathered unto him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 25:5; Luke 14:26; Deuteronomy 33:8–9"> and he saith to them, 'Thus said Jehovah, God of Israel, Put each his sword by his thigh, pass over and turn back from gate to gate through the camp, and slay each his brother, and each his friend, and each his relation.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:9; Malachi 2:4–6; 1 Corinthians 10:8"> And the sons of Levi do according to the word of Moses, and there fall of the people on that day about three thousand men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 25:11–13; Matthew 10:37; Zechariah 13:3"> and Moses saith, 'Consecrate your hand to-day to Jehovah, for a man &#91;is&#93; against his son, and against his brother, so as to bring on you to-day a blessing.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:20; Numbers 25:13; 2 Kings 17:21"> And it cometh to pass, on the morrow, that Moses saith unto the people, 'Ye — ye have sinned a great sin, and now I go up unto Jehovah, if so be I atone for your sin.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 20:23; Daniel 9:11; Daniel 9:8"> And Moses turneth back unto Jehovah, and saith, 'Oh this people hath sinned a great sin, that they make to themselves a god of gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 69:28; Romans 9:3; Revelation 21:27"> and now, if Thou takest away their sin — and if not — blot me, I pray thee, out of Thy book which Thou hast written.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:20; Ezekiel 18:4; Revelation 20:12"> And Jehovah saith unto Moses, 'Whoso hath sinned against Me — I blot him out of My book;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 23:20; Deuteronomy 32:35; Matthew 23:35"> and now, go, lead the people whithersoever I have spoken to thee of; lo, My messenger goeth before thee, and in the day of my charging — then I have charged upon them their sin.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 7:41; Exodus 32:25; 2 Samuel 12:9–10"> And Jehovah plagueth the people, because they made the calf which Aaron made.</span>
