<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:22; Deuteronomy 5:4; Deuteronomy 4:36"> 'And God speaketh all these words, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:6; Hosea 13:4; Leviticus 26:13"> I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah thy God, who hath brought thee out of the land of Egypt, out of a house of servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:7; Matthew 4:10; Psalms 81:9"> 'Thou hast no other Gods before Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:8; Leviticus 26:1; Deuteronomy 4:15–19"> 'Thou dost not make to thyself a graven image, or any likeness which &#91;is&#93; in the heavens above, or which &#91;is&#93; in the earth beneath, or which &#91;is&#93; in the waters under the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:9; Numbers 14:18; Deuteronomy 4:24"> Thou dost not bow thyself to them, nor serve them: for I, Jehovah thy God, &#91;am&#93; a zealous God, charging iniquity of fathers on sons, on the third &#91;generation&#93;, and on the fourth, of those hating Me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:9; John 14:21; 1 John 5:3"> and doing kindness to thousands, of those loving Me and keeping My commands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:11; Leviticus 19:12; James 5:12"> 'Thou dost not take up the name of Jehovah thy God for a vain thing, for Jehovah acquitteth not him who taketh up His name for a vain thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 26:2; Leviticus 19:3; Exodus 31:13–16"> 'Remember the Sabbath-day to sanctify it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 13:14; Exodus 34:21; Exodus 23:12"> six days thou dost labour, and hast done all thy work,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:14–15; Exodus 34:21; Numbers 15:32–36"> and the seventh day &#91;is&#93; a Sabbath to Jehovah thy God; thou dost not do any work, thou, and thy son, and thy daughter, thy man-servant, and thy handmaid, and thy cattle, and thy sojourner who is within thy gates, —</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 1:1–2; Genesis 2:2–3; Exodus 31:17"> for six days hath Jehovah made the heavens and the earth, the sea, and all that &#91;is&#93; in them, and resteth in the seventh day; therefore hath Jehovah blessed the Sabbath-day, and doth sanctify it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1–3; Colossians 3:20; Deuteronomy 5:16"> 'Honour thy father and thy mother, so that thy days are prolonged on the ground which Jehovah thy God is giving to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Deuteronomy 5:17; Genesis 9:5–6"> 'Thou dost not murder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:4; Deuteronomy 5:18; Romans 7:2–3"> 'Thou dost not commit adultery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Ephesians 4:28; Leviticus 19:11"> 'Thou dost not steal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 19:5; Matthew 19:18; Psalms 15:3"> 'Thou dost not answer against thy neighbour a false testimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Luke 12:15; Hebrews 13:5"> 'Thou dost not desire the house of thy neighbour, thou dost not desire the wife of thy neighbour, or his man-servant, or his handmaid, or his ox, or his ass, or anything which &#91;is&#93; thy neighbour's.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 19:16–18; Jeremiah 23:23; Psalms 139:7–8"> And all the people are seeing the voices, and the flames, and the sound of the trumpet, and the mount smoking; and the people see, and move, and stand afar off,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:16; Acts 7:38; Galatians 3:19"> and say unto Moses, 'Speak thou with us, and we hear, and let not God speak with us, lest we die.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:3; Proverbs 3:7; 1 Samuel 12:20"> And Moses saith unto the people, 'Fear not, for to try you hath God come, and in order that His fear may be before your faces — that ye sin not.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 8:12; Psalms 97:2; Psalms 18:9"> And the people stand afar off, and Moses hath drawn nigh unto the thick darkness where God &#91;is&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:13; Deuteronomy 4:36; Hebrews 12:25–26"> And Jehovah saith unto Moses, 'Thus dost thou say unto the sons of Israel: Ye — ye have seen that from the heavens I have spoken with you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:39; Zephaniah 1:5; 2 Kings 17:33"> ye do not make with Me gods of silver, even gods of gold ye do not make to yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5; 2 Chronicles 6:6; Psalms 132:13–14"> 'An altar of earth thou dost make for Me, and thou hast sacrificed on it thy burnt-offerings and thy peace-offerings, thy flock and thy herd; in every place where I cause My name to be remembered I come in unto thee, and have blessed thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 8:31; Deuteronomy 27:5–6"> 'And if an altar of stones thou dost make to Me, thou dost not build them of hewn work; when thy tool thou hast waved over it, then thou dost pollute it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 1:16; Ecclesiastes 5:1; Leviticus 10:3"> neither dost thou go up by steps on Mine altar, that thy nakedness be not revealed upon it.</span>
