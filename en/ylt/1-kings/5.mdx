<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 14:1; 2 Chronicles 2:3; 1 Kings 5:10"> And Hiram king of Tyre sendeth his servants unto Solomon, for he heard that they had anointed him for king instead of his father, for Hiram was a lover of David all the days;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:3"> and Solomon sendeth unto Hiram, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:3; 1 Chronicles 22:8; Psalms 110:1"> 'Thou hast known David my father, that he hath not been able to build a house to the name of Jehovah his God, because of the wars that have been round about him, till Jehovah's putting them under the soles of his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:9; 1 Kings 4:24; Acts 9:31"> 'And now, Jehovah my God hath given rest to me round about, there is no adversary nor evil occurrence,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:12; 1 Chronicles 22:10; 2 Samuel 7:12–13"> and lo, I am saying to build a house to the name of Jehovah my God, as Jehovah spake unto David my father, saying, Thy son whom I appoint in thy stead on thy throne, he doth build the house for My name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:8; 1 Kings 6:9–10; Romans 12:17"> 'And now, command, and they cut down for me cedars out of Lebanon, and my servants are with thy servants, and the hire of thy servants I give to thee according to all that thou sayest, for thou hast known that there is not among us a man acquainted with cutting wood, like the Sidonians.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 23:24; Isaiah 9:6; 2 Chronicles 2:11–12"> And it cometh to pass at Hiram's hearing the words of Solomon, that he rejoiceth exceedingly, and saith, 'Blessed &#91;is&#93; Jehovah to-day, who hath given to David a wise son over this numerous people.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 6:34; 2 Chronicles 3:5; 2 Samuel 6:5"> And Hiram sendeth unto Solomon, saying, I have heard that which thou hast sent unto me, I do all thy desire concerning cedar-wood, and fir-wood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezra 3:7; Ezekiel 27:17; Acts 12:20"> my servants bring down from Lebanon to the sea, and I make them floats in the sea unto the place that thou sendest unto me, and I have spread them out there; and thou dost take &#91;them&#93; up, and thou dost execute my desire, to give the food of my house.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Hiram is giving to Solomon cedar-trees, and fir-trees, all his desire,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:10; 1 Kings 4:22"> and Solomon hath given to Hiram twenty thousand cors of wheat, food for his house, and twenty cors of beaten oil; thus doth Solomon give to Hiram year by year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 3:12; Amos 1:9; James 1:5"> And Jehovah hath given wisdom to Solomon as He spake to him, and there is peace between Hiram and Solomon, and they make a covenant both of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 9:15; 1 Kings 4:6"> And king Solomon lifteth up a tribute out of all Israel, and the tribute is thirty thousand men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 4:6–19; 1 Chronicles 27:1–15"> and he sendeth them to Lebanon, ten thousand a month, by changes, a month they are in Lebanon, two months in their own house; and Adoniram &#91;is&#93; over the tribute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 9:20–22; 2 Chronicles 2:17–18; 2 Chronicles 8:7–9"> And king Solomon hath seventy thousand bearing burdens, and eighty thousand hewing in the mountain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 9:23; 2 Chronicles 2:2"> apart from the heads of the officers of Solomon, who &#91;are&#93; over the work, three thousand and three hundred, those ruling over the people who are working in the business.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 6:7; 1 Chronicles 22:2; Isaiah 28:16"> And the king commandeth, and they bring great stones, precious stone, to lay the foundation of the house, hewn stones;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 13:5; Ezekiel 27:9; Psalms 83:7"> and the builders of Solomon, and the builders of Hiram, and the Giblites hew, and prepare the wood and the stones to build the house.</span>
