<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 12:42; Luke 11:31; 2 Chronicles 9:1–12"> And the queen of Sheba is hearing of the fame of Solomon concerning the name of Jehovah, and cometh to try him with enigmas,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 25:23; Psalms 4:4; Job 4:2"> and she cometh to Jerusalem, with a very great company, camels bearing spices, and very much gold, and precious stone, and she cometh unto Solomon, and speaketh unto him all that hath been with her heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 1:5–6; Matthew 13:11; 1 Kings 10:1"> And Solomon declareth to her all her matters — there hath not been a thing hid from the king that he hath not declared to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 12:42; 1 Kings 3:28; 2 Chronicles 9:3–4"> And the queen of Sheba seeth all the wisdom of Solomon, and the house that he built,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:16; 1 Kings 4:22–23; 2 Kings 16:18"> and the food of his table, and the sitting of his servants, and the standing of his ministers, and their clothing, and his butlers, and his burnt-offering that he causeth to ascend in the house of Jehovah, and there hath not been in her any more spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:5–6"> And she saith unto the king, 'True hath been the word that I heard in my land, concerning thy matters and thy wisdom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:9; John 20:25–29; 1 John 3:2"> and I gave no credence to the words till that I have come, and my eyes see, and lo, it was not declared to me — the half; thou hast added wisdom and goodness unto the report that I heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 8:34; Proverbs 10:21; Luke 11:31"> O the happiness of thy men, O the happiness of thy servants — these — who are standing before thee continually, who are hearing thy wisdom!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 72:2; 1 Kings 5:7; 2 Samuel 8:15"> Jehovah thy God is blessed who delighted in thee, to put thee on the throne of Israel; in Jehovah's loving Israel to the age He doth set thee for king, to do judgment and righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 10:2; Matthew 2:11; 1 Kings 9:14"> And she giveth to the king a hundred and twenty talents of gold, and spices very many, and precious stone; there came not like that spice any more for abundance that the queen of Sheba gave to king Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 9:27–28; 2 Chronicles 8:18; Psalms 45:9"> And also, the navy of Hiram that bore gold from Ophir, brought in from Ophir almug-trees very many, and precious stone;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:1–31; Psalms 150:3–5; Revelation 14:2–3"> and the king maketh the almug-trees a support for the house of Jehovah, and for the house of the king, and harps and psalteries for singers; there have not come such almug-trees, nor have there been seen &#91;such&#93; unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; Matthew 15:28; Psalms 20:4"> And king Solomon gave to the queen of Sheba all her desire that she asked, apart from that which he gave to her as a memorial of king Solomon, and she turneth and goeth to her land, she and her servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:13–28; 1 Kings 9:28"> And the weight of the gold that hath come to Solomon in one year is six hundred sixty and six talents of gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 72:10; 1 Chronicles 9:24; Isaiah 21:13"> apart from &#91;that of&#93; the tourists, and of the traffic of the merchants, and of all the kings of Arabia, and of the governors of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 14:26–28; 2 Chronicles 12:9–10; 2 Chronicles 9:15–16"> And king Solomon maketh two hundred targets of alloyed gold — six hundred of gold go up on the one target;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 7:2; 1 Kings 14:26"> and three hundred shields of alloyed gold — three pounds of gold go up on the one shield; and the king putteth them &#91;in&#93; the house of the forest of Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 45:8; 1 Kings 10:22; Revelation 20:11"> And the king maketh a great throne of ivory, and overlayeth it with refined gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> six steps hath the throne, and a round top &#91;is&#93; to the throne behind it, and hands &#91;are&#93; on this &#91;side&#93; and on that, unto the place of the sitting, and two lions are standing near the hands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 24:9; Genesis 49:9; Numbers 23:24"> and twelve lions are standing there on the six steps, on this &#91;side&#93; and on that; it hath not been made so for any kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 10:17; 1 Kings 7:2; 2 Chronicles 9:20–22"> And all the drinking vessels of king Solomon &#91;are&#93; of gold, and all the vessels of the house of the forest of Lebanon &#91;are&#93; of refined gold — there are none of silver; it was not reckoned in the days of Solomon for anything,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Kings 22:48; Genesis 10:4; 2 Chronicles 20:36–37"> for a navy of Tarshish hath the king at sea with a navy of Hiram; once in three years cometh the navy of Tarshish, bearing gold, and silver, ivory, and apes, and peacocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 3:12–13; Colossians 2:2–3; 1 Kings 4:29–34"> And king Solomon is greater than any of the kings of the earth for riches and for wisdom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 3:12; James 1:5; 1 Kings 3:28"> and all the earth is seeking the presence of Solomon, to hear his wisdom that God hath put into his heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 42:11; Psalms 72:10; Psalms 72:15"> and they are bringing each his present, vessels of silver, and vessels of gold, and garments, and armour, and spices, horses, and mules, the matter of a year in a year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:25; 1 Kings 4:26; Deuteronomy 17:16"> And Solomon gathereth chariots, and horsemen, and he hath a thousand and four hundred chariots, and twelve thousand horsemen, and he placeth them in the cities of the chariot, and with the king in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:15–17; Deuteronomy 17:17; 2 Chronicles 9:27"> And the king maketh the silver in Jerusalem as stones, and the cedars he hath made as the sycamores that &#91;are&#93; in the low country, for abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:16; 2 Chronicles 9:28; Isaiah 31:1–3"> And the outgoing of the horses that king Solomon hath &#91;is&#93; from Egypt, and from Keveh; merchants of the king take from Keveh at a price;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 1:4; 2 Kings 7:6–7; Malachi 1:1"> and a chariot cometh up and cometh out of Egypt for six hundred silverlings, and a horse for fifty and a hundred, and so for all the kings of the Hittites, and for the kings of Aram; by their hand they bring out.</span>
