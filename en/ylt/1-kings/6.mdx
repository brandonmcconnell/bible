<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:1–2; Acts 7:47; 1 Chronicles 29:19"> And it cometh to pass, in the four hundred and eightieth year of the going out of the sons of Israel from the land of Egypt, in the fourth year — in the month of Zif, it &#91;is&#93; the second month — of the reigning of Solomon over Israel, that he buildeth the house for Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:1–41; Ezekiel 41:1–15; Revelation 21:16–17"> As to the house that king Solomon hath built for Jehovah, sixty cubits &#91;is&#93; its length, and twenty its breadth, and thirty cubits its height.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:11; 2 Chronicles 3:3–4; Ezekiel 41:15"> As to the porch on the front of the temple of the house, twenty cubits &#91;is&#93; its length on the front of the breadth of the house; ten by the cubit &#91;is&#93; its breadth on the front of the house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 41:26; Ezekiel 41:16"> and he maketh for the house windows of narrow lights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 6:16; 1 Kings 6:19–21; 1 Kings 6:31"> And he buildeth against the wall of the house a couch round about, &#91;even&#93; the walls of the house round about, of the temple and of the oracle, and maketh sides round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> The lowest couch, five by the cubit &#91;is&#93; its breadth; and the middle, six by the cubit &#91;is&#93; its breadth; and the third, seven by the cubit &#91;is&#93; its breadth, for withdrawings he hath put to the house round about, without — not to lay hold on the walls of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:5–6; 1 Peter 2:5; Exodus 20:25"> And the house, in its being built, of perfect stone brought &#91;thither&#93; hath been built, and hammer, and the axe — any instrument of iron — was not heard in the house, in its being built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:6–7"> The opening of the middle side &#91;is&#93; at the right shoulder of the house, and with windings they go up on the middle one, and from the middle one unto the third.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 6:14; 1 Kings 6:38"> And he buildeth the house, and completeth it, and covereth the house &#91;with&#93; beams and rows of cedars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And he buildeth the couch against all the house, five cubits &#91;is&#93; its height, and it taketh hold of the house by cedar-wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the word of Jehovah is unto Solomon, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:10; Colossians 1:23; 1 Kings 3:14"> 'This house that thou art building — if thou dost walk in My statutes, and My judgments dost do, yea, hast done all My commands, to walk in them, then I have established My word with thee, which I spake unto David thy father,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:6; Exodus 25:8; Leviticus 26:11"> and have tabernacled in the midst of the sons of Israel, and do not forsake My people Israel.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 6:9; 1 Kings 6:38; Acts 7:47–48"> And Solomon buildeth the house and completeth it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 7:7"> and he buildeth the walls of the house within with beams of cedar, from the floor of the house unto the walls of the ceiling; he hath overlaid with wood the inside, and covereth the floor of the house with ribs of fir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:8; 1 Kings 8:6; Hebrews 9:3"> And he buildeth the twenty cubits on the sides of the house with ribs of cedar, from the floor unto the walls; and he buildeth for it within, for the oracle, for the holy of holies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And forty by the cubit was the house, it &#91;is&#93; the temple before &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 7:24"> And the cedar for the house within &#91;is&#93; carvings of knobs and openings of flowers; the whole &#91;is&#93; cedar, there is not a stone seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 8:6–10; Psalms 28:2; 1 Kings 6:5"> And the oracle in the midst of the house within he hath prepared, to put there the ark of the covenant of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 6:2–3; 1 Kings 6:22; 1 Kings 7:48"> And before the oracle &#91;is&#93; twenty cubits in length, and twenty cubits in breadth, and twenty cubits &#91;is&#93; its height; and he overlayeth it with gold refined, and overlayeth the altar with cedar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:7–9; 2 Chronicles 3:14–16; 1 Kings 6:5"> And Solomon overlayeth the house within with gold refined, and causeth &#91;it&#93; to pass over in chains of gold before the oracle, and overlayeth it with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 30:1; Exodus 30:3; 2 Chronicles 3:7–14"> And the whole of the house he hath overlaid with gold, till the completion of all the house; and the whole of the altar that the oracle hath, he hath overlaid with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 37:7–9; Genesis 3:24; 1 Peter 1:12"> And he maketh within the oracle two cherubs, of the oil-tree, ten cubits &#91;is&#93; their height;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> and five cubits &#91;is&#93; the one wing of the cherub, and five cubits the second wing of the cherub, ten cubits from the ends of its wings even unto the ends of its wings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> and ten by the cubit &#91;is&#93; the second cherub, one measure and one form &#91;are&#93; to the two cherubs,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> the height of the one cherub &#91;is&#93; ten by the cubit, and so &#91;is&#93; the second cherub;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 37:9; Exodus 25:20; 2 Chronicles 5:8"> and he setteth the cherubs in the midst of the inner house, and they spread out the wings of the cherubs, and a wing of the one cometh against the wall, and a wing of the second cherub is coming against the second wall, and their wings &#91;are&#93; unto the midst of the house, coming wing against wing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and he overlayeth the cherubs with gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 6:32; Psalms 92:12–15; 2 Chronicles 4:2–5"> and all the walls of the house round about he hath carved with openings of carvings, cherubs, and palm trees, and openings of flowers, within and without.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 21:18–21; Isaiah 60:17; Isaiah 54:11–12"> And the floor of the house he hath overlaid with gold, within and without;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–20; Ephesians 3:18; John 14:6"> as to the opening of the oracle, he made doors of the oil-tree; the lintel, side-posts, a fifth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 6:18; 1 Kings 6:29"> And the two doors &#91;are&#93; of the oil-tree, and he hath carved upon them carvings of cherubs, and palm-trees, and openings of flowers, and overlaid with gold, and he causeth the gold to go down on the cherubs and on the palm-trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And so he hath made for the opening of the temple, side-posts of the oil-tree, from the fourth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:23–25; 1 Kings 5:8"> And the two doors &#91;are&#93; of fir-tree, the two sides of the one door are revolving, and the two hangings of the second door are revolving.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And he hath carved cherubs, and palms, and openings of flowers, and overlaid with straightened gold the graved work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 7:12; 2 Chronicles 7:7; Exodus 27:9–19"> And he buildeth the inner court, three rows of hewn work, and a row of beams of cedar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Kings 6:1; 2 Chronicles 3:2"> In the fourth year hath the house of Jehovah been founded, in the month Zif,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Ezra 6:14–15; 1 Kings 7:1; Zechariah 4:9"> and in the eleventh year, in the month Bul — &#91;that is&#93; the eighth month — hath the house been finished in all its matters, and in all its ordinances, and he buildeth it seven years.</span>
