<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 4:25; James 5:17; Deuteronomy 28:12"> And the days are many, and the word of Jehovah hath been unto Elijah in the third year, saying, 'Go, appear unto Ahab, and I give rain on the face of the ground;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 6:25; Proverbs 28:1; Leviticus 26:26"> and Elijah goeth to appear unto Ahab. And the famine is severe in Samaria,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:2; 1 Kings 16:9; Matthew 10:28"> and Ahab calleth unto Obadiah, who &#91;is&#93; over the house — and Obadiah hath been fearing Jehovah greatly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 18:13; Matthew 10:40–42; Revelation 17:4–6"> and it cometh to pass, in Jezebel's cutting off the prophets of Jehovah, that Obadiah taketh a hundred prophets, and hideth them, fifty men in a cave, and hath sustained them with bread and water —</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:5–6; Joel 1:18; Joel 2:22"> and Ahab saith unto Obadiah, 'Go through the land, unto all fountains of waters, and unto all the brooks, if so be we find hay, and keep alive horse and mule, and do not cut off any of the cattle.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:3"> And they apportion to themselves the land, to pass over into it; Ahab hath gone in one way by himself, and Obadiah hath gone in another way by himself;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 1:6–8; Genesis 44:20; Isaiah 60:14"> and Obadiah &#91;is&#93; in the way, and lo, Elijah — to meet him; and he discerneth him, and falleth on his face, and saith, 'Art thou he — my lord Elijah?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 13:7; 1 Kings 18:3; 1 Peter 2:17–18"> And he saith to him, 'I &#91;am&#93;; go, say to thy lord, Lo, Elijah.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 5:21; 1 Kings 18:12; 1 Kings 17:18"> And he saith, 'What have I sinned, that thou art giving thy servant into the hand of Ahab — to put me to death?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; 1 Kings 1:29; 1 Kings 18:15"> Jehovah thy God liveth, if there is a nation and kingdom whither my lord hath not sent to seek thee; and they said, He is not, then he caused the kingdom and the nation to swear, that it doth not find thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 18:8; 1 Kings 18:14"> and now, thou art saying, Go, say to thy lord, Lo, Elijah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 2:16; Acts 8:39; Ezekiel 8:3"> and it hath been, I go from thee, and the Spirit of Jehovah doth lift thee up, whither I know not, and I have come to declare to Ahab, and he doth not find thee, and he hath slain me; and thy servant is fearing Jehovah from my youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 18:4; Matthew 25:35; Psalms 18:21–24"> 'Hath it not been declared to my lord that which I have done in Jezebel's slaying the prophets of Jehovah, that I hide of the prophets of Jehovah a hundred men, fifty by fifty in a cave, and sustained them with bread and water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 10:28"> and now thou art saying, Go, say to my lord, Lo, Elijah — and he hath slain me!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; 1 Kings 18:10; Luke 2:13–14"> And Elijah saith, 'Jehovah of Hosts liveth, before whom I have stood, surely to-day I appear unto him.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Obadiah goeth to meet Ahab, and declareth &#91;it&#93; to him, and Ahab goeth to meet Elijah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 7:25; 1 Kings 21:20; Acts 16:20"> and it cometh to pass at Ahab's seeing Elijah, that Ahab saith unto him, 'Art thou he — the troubler of Israel?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; 1 Kings 9:9; Jeremiah 2:19"> And he saith, 'I have not troubled Israel, but thou and the house of thy father, in your forsaking the commands of Jehovah, and thou goest after the Baalim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 19:26; 1 Kings 16:33; 2 Peter 2:1"> and now, send, gather unto me all Israel, unto the mount of Carmel, and the prophets of Baal four hundred and fifty, and the prophets of the shrine, four hundred — eating at the table of Jezebel.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 22:9"> And Ahab sendeth among all the sons of Israel, and gathereth the prophets unto the mount of Carmel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 6:24; Joshua 24:15; 1 Corinthians 10:21–22"> and Elijah cometh nigh unto all the people, and saith, 'Till when are ye leaping on the two branches? — if Jehovah &#91;is&#93; God, go after Him; and if Baal, go after him;' and the people have not answered him a word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Kings 19:10; 1 Kings 19:14; 2 Timothy 4:3–4"> And Elijah saith unto the people, 'I — I have been left a prophet of Jehovah — by myself; and the prophets of Baal &#91;are&#93; four hundred and fifty men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and let them give to us two bullocks, and they choose for themselves the one bullock, and cut it in pieces, and place &#91;it&#93; on the wood, and place no fire; and I — I prepare the other bullock, and have put &#91;it&#93; on the wood, and fire I do not place; —</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 18:38; 1 Chronicles 21:26; 2 Chronicles 7:1"> and ye have called in the name of your god, and I — I call in the name of Jehovah, and it hath been, the god who answereth by fire — He &#91;is&#93; the God.' And all the people answer and say, 'Good &#91;is&#93; the word.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And Elijah saith to the prophets of Baal, 'Choose for you the one bullock, and prepare first, for ye &#91;are&#93; the multitude, and call ye in the name of your god, and place no fire.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:5; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Matthew 6:7"> And they take the bullock that &#91;one&#93; gave to them, and prepare, and call in the name of Baal from the morning even till the noon, saying, 'O Baal, answer us!' and there is no voice, and there is none answering; and they leap on the altar that one had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 78:65–66; Isaiah 41:23; Ecclesiastes 11:9"> And it cometh to pass, at noon, that Elijah playeth on them, and saith, 'Call with a loud voice, for he &#91;is&#93; a god, for he is meditating, or pursuing, or on a journey; it may be he is asleep, an doth awake.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 19:28; Deuteronomy 14:1; Mark 5:5"> And they call with a loud voice, and cut themselves, according to their ordinance, with swords and with spears, till a flowing of blood &#91;is&#93; on them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 18:26; 1 Samuel 18:10; Exodus 29:39"> and it cometh to pass, at the passing by of the noon, that they feign themselves prophets till the going up of the present, and there is no voice, and there is none answering, and there is none attending.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:16; 1 Kings 19:14; 1 Kings 19:10"> And Elijah saith to all the people, 'Come nigh unto me;' and all the people come nigh unto him, and he repaireth the altar of Jehovah that is broken down;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Kings 17:34; Genesis 35:10; Genesis 32:28"> and Elijah taketh twelve stones, according to the number of the tribes of the sons of Jacob, unto whom the word of Jehovah was, saying, 'Israel is thy name;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Colossians 3:17; Exodus 20:24–25; Judges 21:4"> and he buildeth with the stones an altar, in the name of Jehovah, and maketh a trench, as about the space of two measures of seed, round about the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 22:9; Judges 6:20; Leviticus 1:6–8"> And he arrangeth the wood, and cutteth in pieces the bullock, and placeth &#91;it&#93; on the wood, and saith, 'Fill ye four pitchers of water, and pour on the burnt-offering, and on the wood;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:21; 2 Corinthians 4:2"> and he saith, 'Do &#91;it&#93; a second time;' and they do &#91;it&#93; a second time; and he saith, 'Do &#91;it&#93; a third time;' and they do &#91;it&#93; a third time;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 18:32; 1 Kings 18:38"> and the water goeth round about the altar, and also, the trench he hath filled with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 18:29; 2 Kings 19:19; Exodus 3:6"> And it cometh to pass, at the going up of the &#91;evening -&#93;present, that Elijah the prophet cometh nigh and saith, 'Jehovah, God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, to-day let it be known that Thou &#91;art&#93; God in Israel, and I Thy servant, that by Thy word I have done the whole of these things;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Daniel 9:17–19; Luke 1:16–17; Ezekiel 36:25–27"> answer me, O Jehovah, answer me, and this people doth know that Thou &#91;art&#93; Jehovah God; and Thou hast turned their heart backward.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:26; Leviticus 9:24; 2 Chronicles 7:1"> And there falleth a fire of Jehovah, and consumeth the burnt-offering, and the wood, and the stones, and the dust, and the water that &#91;is&#93; in the trench it hath licked up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 18:24; 2 Chronicles 7:3; 1 Kings 18:21"> And all the people see, and fall on their faces, and say, 'Jehovah, He &#91;is&#93; the God, Jehovah, He &#91;is&#93; the God.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:20; Judges 4:7; Deuteronomy 13:5"> And Elijah saith to them, 'Catch ye the prophets of Baal; let not a man escape of them;' and they catch them, and Elijah bringeth them down unto the stream Kishon, and doth slaughter them there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Kings 18:1; Ecclesiastes 9:7; 1 Kings 17:1"> And Elijah saith to Ahab, 'Go up, eat and drink, because of the sound of the noise of the shower.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Daniel 9:7; Joshua 7:6"> And Ahab goeth up to eat, and to drink, and Elijah hath gone up unto the top of Carmel, and he stretcheth himself out on the earth, and he placeth his face between his knees,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:3; Luke 18:7; Luke 18:1"> and saith unto his young man, 'Go up, I pray thee, look attentively the way of the sea;' and he goeth up and looketh attentively, and saith, 'There is nothing;' and he saith, 'Turn back,' seven times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Luke 12:54; Job 8:7; Zechariah 4:10"> And it cometh to pass, at the seventh, that he saith, 'Lo, a little thickness as the palm of a man is coming up out of the sea.' And he saith, 'Go up, say unto Ahab, 'Bind — and go down, and the shower doth not restrain thee.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Kings 18:39–40; Joshua 17:16; 1 Kings 21:1"> And it cometh to pass, in the meantime, that the heavens have become black — thick clouds and wind — and the shower is great; and Ahab rideth, and goeth to Jezreel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:14; Ezekiel 1:3; 2 Kings 9:1"> and the hand of Jehovah hath been on Elijah, and he girdeth up his loins, and runneth before Ahab, till thine entering Jezreel.</span>
