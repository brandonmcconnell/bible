<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 18:40; 1 Kings 21:25; 1 Kings 21:5–7"> And Ahab declareth to Jezebel all that Elijah did, and all how he slew all the prophets by the sword,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ruth 1:17; 2 Kings 6:31; 1 Kings 20:10"> and Jezebel sendeth a messenger unto Elijah, saying, 'Thus doth the gods, and thus do they add, surely about this time to-morrow, I make thy life as the life of one of them.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 21:31; Matthew 26:70–74; Matthew 26:56"> And he feareth, and riseth, and goeth for his life, and cometh in to Beer-Sheba, that &#91;is&#93; Judah's, and leaveth his young man there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jonah 4:3; Numbers 11:15; Jonah 4:8"> and he himself hath gone into the wilderness a day's Journey, and cometh and sitteth under a certain retem-tree, and desireth his soul to die, and saith, 'Enough, now, O Jehovah, take my soul, for I &#91;am&#93; not better than my fathers.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 34:7; Acts 12:7; Hebrews 13:5"> And he lieth down and sleepeth under a certain retem-tree, and lo, a messenger cometh against him, and saith to him, 'Rise, eat;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 17:6; Matthew 4:11; Matthew 6:32"> and he looketh attentively, and lo, at his bolster a cake &#91;baken on&#93; burning stones, and a dish of water, and he eateth, and drinketh, and turneth, and lieth down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 103:13–14; Deuteronomy 33:25"> And the messenger of Jehovah turneth back a second time, and cometh against him, and saith, 'Rise, eat, for the way is too great for thee;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 34:28; Exodus 24:18; Exodus 3:1"> and he riseth, and eateth, and drinketh, and goeth in the power of that food forty days and forty nights, unto the mount of God — Horeb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 19:13; Exodus 33:21–22; Jeremiah 2:18"> And he cometh in there, unto the cave, and lodgeth there, and lo, the word of Jehovah &#91;is&#93; unto him, and saith to him, 'What — to thee, here, Elijah?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 18:4; 1 Kings 18:22; Exodus 34:14"> And he saith, 'I have been very zealous for Jehovah, God of Hosts, for the sons of Israel have forsaken Thy covenant — Thine altars they have thrown down, and Thy prophets they have slain by the sword, and I am left, I, by myself, and they seek my life — to take it.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:4; Psalms 50:3; Job 38:1"> And He saith, 'Go out, and thou hast stood in the mount before Jehovah.' And lo, Jehovah is passing by, and a wind — great and strong — is rending mountains, and shivering rocks before Jehovah: — not in the wind &#91;is&#93; Jehovah; and after the wind a shaking: — not in the shaking &#91;is&#93; Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 4:16; Zechariah 4:6; Deuteronomy 4:33"> and after the shaking a fire: — not in the fire &#91;is&#93; Jehovah; and after the fire a voice still small;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 19:9; Isaiah 6:2; Genesis 16:8"> and it cometh to pass, at Elijah's hearing &#91;it&#93;, that he wrappeth his face in his robe, and goeth out, and standeth at the opening of the cave, and lo, unto him &#91;is&#93; a voice, and it saith, 'What — to thee, here, Elijah?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 19:9–10; Jeremiah 22:9; Isaiah 62:1"> And he saith, 'I have been very zealous for Jehovah, God of Hosts; for the sons of Israel have forsaken Thy covenant, Thine altars they have thrown down, and Thy prophets they have slain by the sword, and I am left, I, by myself, and they seek my life — to take it.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 45:1; Jeremiah 1:10; 2 Kings 8:7–15"> And Jehovah saith unto him, 'Go turn back on thy way to the wilderness of Damascus, and thou hast gone in, and anointed Hazael for king over Aram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 9:1–14; 1 Kings 19:19–21; 2 Kings 2:9"> and Jehu son of Nimshi thou dost anoint for king over Israel, and Elisha son of Shaphat, of Abel-Meholah, thou dost anoint for prophet in thy stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 13:22; 2 Kings 8:12; 2 Kings 13:3"> 'And it hath been, him who is escaped from the sword of Hazael, put to death doth Jehu, and him who is escaped from the sword of Jehu put to death doth Elisha;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 11:4–5; Isaiah 1:9; Isaiah 10:20–22"> and I have left in Israel seven thousand, all the knees that have not bowed to Baal, and every mouth that hath not kissed him.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 2:8; 2 Kings 2:13–14; 1 Samuel 28:14"> And he goeth thence, and findeth Elisha son of Shaphat, and he is plowing; twelve yoke &#91;are&#93; before him, and he &#91;is&#93; with the twelfth; and Elijah passeth over unto him, and casteth his robe upon him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 8:21–22; Luke 9:61–62; Acts 20:37"> and he forsaketh the oxen, and runneth after Elijah, and saith, 'Let me give a kiss, I pray thee, to my father and to my mother, and I go after thee.' And he saith to him, 'Go, turn back, for what have I done to thee?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:22; 2 Timothy 4:11; Philemon 1:13"> And he turneth back from after him, and taketh the yoke of oxen, and sacrificeth it, and with instruments of the oxen he hath boiled their flesh, and giveth to the people, and they eat, and he riseth, and goeth after Elijah, and serveth him.</span>
