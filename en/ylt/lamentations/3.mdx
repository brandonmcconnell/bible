<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 40:4; Luke 15:18–19; Romans 3:19"> He putteth in the dust his mouth, if so be there is hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 16:10; Isaiah 50:6; Matthew 26:67"> He giveth to his smiter the cheek, He is filled with reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 94:14; Psalms 103:8–10; Psalms 77:7"> For the Lord doth not cast off to the age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 78:38; Psalms 30:5; Psalms 103:11"> For though He afflicted, yet He hath pitied, According to the abundance of His kindness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:11; Ezekiel 18:32; Hebrews 12:9–10"> For He hath not afflicted with His heart, Nor doth He grieve the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Zechariah 9:11–12; Isaiah 51:22–23; Psalms 102:20"> To bruise under one's feet any bound ones of earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> To turn aside the judgment of a man, Over-against the face of the Most High,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 59:15; Habakkuk 1:13; Jeremiah 22:3"> To subvert a man in his cause, the Lord hath not approved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Psalms 33:9–11; Isaiah 46:10; Proverbs 16:9"> Who &#91;is&#93; this — he hath said, and it is, &#91;And&#93; the Lord hath not commanded &#91;it&#93;?</span>

<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Isaiah 38:5; Job 34:28; Psalms 6:8–9"> My voice Thou hast heard, Hide not Thine ear at my breathing — at my cry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="James 4:8; Isaiah 41:10; Isaiah 58:9"> Thou hast drawn near in the day I call Thee, Thou hast said, Fear not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Psalms 34:22–1; Jeremiah 51:36; Psalms 103:4"> Thou hast pleaded, O Lord, the pleadings of my soul, Thou hast redeemed my life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Psalms 43:1; Psalms 9:4; Genesis 31:42"> Thou hast seen, O Jehovah, my overthrow, Judge Thou my cause.</span>
