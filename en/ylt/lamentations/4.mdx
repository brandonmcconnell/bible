<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:19–22; Lamentations 2:19; Isaiah 1:21"> How is the gold become dim, Changed the best — the pure gold? Poured out are stones of the sanctuary At the head of all out-places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 30:14; Jeremiah 19:11; Romans 9:21–23"> The precious sons of Zion, Who are comparable with fine gold, How have they been reckoned earthen bottles, Work of the hands of a potter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:10; Luke 23:28–29; Lamentations 2:20"> Even dragons have drawn out the breast, They have suckled their young ones, The daughter of my people is become cruel, Like the ostriches in a wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 22:15; Lamentations 2:11–12; Matthew 7:9–11"> Cleaved hath the tongue of a suckling unto his palate with thirst, Infants asked bread, a dealer out they have none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 6:3–7; Luke 16:19; Luke 15:16"> Those eating of dainties have been desolate in out-places, Those supported on scarlet have embraced dunghills.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 19:25; Luke 12:47; Luke 10:12"> And greater is the iniquity of the daughter of my people, Than the sin of Sodom, That was overturned as &#91;in&#93; a moment, And no hands were stayed on her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 51:7; Song of Solomon 5:10; Psalms 144:12"> Purer were her Nazarites than snow, Whiter than milk, ruddier of body than rubies, Of sapphire their form.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 5:10; Psalms 102:3–5; Job 30:30"> Darker than blackness hath been their visage, They have not been known in out-places, Cleaved hath their skin unto their bone, It hath withered — it hath been as wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:23; Leviticus 26:39; Jeremiah 16:4"> Better have been the pierced of a sword Than the pierced of famine, For these flow away, pierced through, Without the increase of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Lamentations 2:20; Isaiah 49:15; 2 Kings 6:26–29"> The hands of merciful women have boiled their own children, They have been for food to them, In the destruction of the daughter of my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:20; Ezekiel 22:31; Jeremiah 17:27"> Completed hath Jehovah His fury, He hath poured out the fierceness of His anger, And he kindleth a fire in Zion, And it devoureth her foundations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:13; Deuteronomy 29:24–28; Psalms 48:4–6"> Believe not did the kings of earth, And any of the inhabitants of the world, That come would an adversary and enemy Into the gates of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:31; Jeremiah 6:13; Jeremiah 26:8–9"> Because of the sins of her prophets, The iniquities of her priests, Who are shedding in her midst the blood of the righteous,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:34; Isaiah 56:10; Isaiah 1:15"> They have wandered naked in out-places, They have been polluted with blood, Without &#91;any&#93; being able to touch their clothing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 13:45–46"> 'Turn aside — unclean,' they called to them, 'Turn aside, turn aside, touch not,' For they fled — yea, they have wandered, They have said among nations: 'They do not add to sojourn.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Lamentations 5:12; Isaiah 9:14–16; Deuteronomy 28:64–65"> The face of Jehovah hath divided them, He doth not add to behold them, The face of priests they have not lifted up, Elders they have not favoured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:16; Isaiah 20:5; 2 Kings 24:7"> While we exist — consumed are our eyes for our vain help, In our watch-tower we have watched for a nation &#91;that&#93; saveth not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Amos 8:2; Ezekiel 7:2–12; Lamentations 3:52"> They have hunted our steps from going in our broad-places, Near hath been our end, fulfilled our days, For come hath our end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:8; Jeremiah 4:13; Isaiah 5:26–28"> Swifter have been our pursuers, Than the eagles of the heavens, On the mountains they have burned &#91;after&#93; us, In the wilderness they have laid wait for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:21; Ezekiel 12:13; Jeremiah 39:5"> The breath of our nostrils — the anointed of Jehovah, Hath been captured in their pits, of whom we said: 'In his shadow we do live among nations.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Amos 1:11; Revelation 16:15; Job 1:1"> Joy and rejoice, O daughter of Edom, Dwelling in the land of Uz, Even unto thee pass over doth a cup, Thou art drunk, and makest thyself naked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 40:2; Psalms 137:7; Jeremiah 50:20"> Completed &#91;is&#93; thy iniquity, daughter of Zion, He doth not add to remove thee, He hath inspected thy iniquity, O daughter of Edom, He hath removed &#91;thee&#93; because of thy sins!</span>
