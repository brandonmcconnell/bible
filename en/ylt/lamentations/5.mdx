<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 1:7; Zephaniah 1:13; Psalms 79:1–2"> Our inheritance hath been turned to strangers, Our houses to foreigners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:21; Exodus 22:24; Jeremiah 15:8"> Orphans we have been — without a father, our mothers &#91;are&#93; as widows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 3:1; Ezekiel 4:9–17; Deuteronomy 28:48"> Our water for money we have drunk, Our wood for a price doth come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:36–37; Lamentations 1:14; Jeremiah 28:14"> For our neck we have been pursued, We have laboured — there hath been no rest for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 12:1; Hosea 9:3; Hosea 7:11"> &#91;To&#93; Egypt we have given a hand, &#91;To&#93; Asshur, to be satisfied with bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:29; Jeremiah 16:12; Ezekiel 18:2"> Our fathers have sinned — they are not, We their iniquities have borne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:15; Zechariah 11:6; Proverbs 30:22"> Servants have ruled over us, A deliverer there is none from their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:16–17; Jeremiah 40:9–12; Ezekiel 12:18–19"> With our lives we bring in our bread, Because of the sword of the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Lamentations 4:8; Job 30:30; Psalms 119:83"> Our skin as an oven hath been burning, Because of the raging of the famine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 13:16; Zechariah 14:2; Deuteronomy 28:30"> Wives in Zion they have humbled, Virgins — in cities of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Lamentations 4:16; Isaiah 47:6; Lamentations 2:20"> Princes by their hand have been hanged, The faces of elders have not been honoured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 16:21; Exodus 11:5; Matthew 23:4"> Young men to grind they have taken, And youths with wood have stumbled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:34; Isaiah 24:7–11; Ezekiel 26:13"> The aged from the gate have ceased, Young men from their song.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Amos 8:10; Jeremiah 25:10; James 4:9–10"> Ceased hath the joy of our heart, Turned to mourning hath been our dancing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 89:39; Jeremiah 13:18; Isaiah 3:9–11"> Fallen hath the crown &#91;from&#93; our head, Woe &#91;is&#93; now to us, for we have sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 1:5; Job 17:7; Lamentations 2:11"> For this hath our heart been sick, For these have our eyes been dim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Micah 3:12; Jeremiah 9:11; Psalms 74:2–3"> For the mount of Zion — that is desolate, Foxes have gone up on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 102:12; Hebrews 1:8–12; Psalms 45:6"> Thou, O Jehovah, to the age remainest, Thy throne to generation and generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 44:24; Psalms 13:1; Jeremiah 14:19–21"> Why for ever dost Thou forget us? Thou forsakest us for length of days!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 80:3; Psalms 80:7; Psalms 85:4"> Turn us back, O Jehovah, unto Thee, And we turn back, renew our days as of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 60:1–2; Ezekiel 37:11; Psalms 44:9"> For hast Thou utterly rejected us? Thou hast been wroth against us — exceedingly?</span>
