<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 6:16–17; Proverbs 11:21; Proverbs 8:13"> An abomination to Jehovah &#91;is&#93; every proud one of heart, Hand to hand he is not acquitted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 14:16; Job 28:28; 2 Corinthians 7:1"> In kindness and truth pardoned is iniquity, And in the fear of Jehovah Turn thou aside from evil.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 32:1–2; Genesis 44:5; Genesis 44:15"> An oath &#91;is&#93; on the lips of a king, In judgment his mouth trespasseth not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 11:1; Proverbs 20:10; Proverbs 20:23"> A just beam and balances &#91;are&#93; Jehovah's, His work &#91;are&#93; all the stones of the bag.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 25:5; Proverbs 29:14; Luke 12:48"> An abomination to kings &#91;is&#93; doing wickedness, For by righteousness is a throne established.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 14:35; Psalms 101:5–7; Proverbs 22:11"> The delight of kings &#91;are&#93; righteous lips, And whoso is speaking uprightly he loveth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 20:2; Proverbs 19:12; Ecclesiastes 10:4"> The fury of a king &#91;is&#93; messengers of death, And a wise man pacifieth it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 72:6; Job 29:23–24; Hosea 6:3"> In the light of a king's face &#91;is&#93; life, And his good-will &#91;is&#93; as a cloud of the latter rain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 8:10–11; Matthew 16:26; Proverbs 8:19"> To get wisdom — how much better than gold, And to get understanding to be chosen than silver!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 35:8; Proverbs 19:16; Proverbs 10:9"> A highway of the upright &#91;is&#93;, 'Turn from evil,' Whoso is preserving his soul is watching his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 11:2; Isaiah 2:11–12; Proverbs 18:12"> Before destruction &#91;is&#93; pride, And before stumbling — a haughty spirit.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 5:3; Isaiah 57:15; Psalms 138:6"> Better is humility of spirit with the poor, Than to apportion spoil with the proud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:7–8; Psalms 2:12; Isaiah 30:18"> The wise in any matter findeth good, And whoso is trusting in Jehovah, O his happiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 16:23–24; Isaiah 50:4; Proverbs 15:7"> To the wise in heart is called, 'Intelligent,' And sweetness of lips increaseth learning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 13:14; John 6:63; John 5:24"> A fountain of life &#91;is&#93; understanding to its possessors, The instruction of fools is folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 15:28; Matthew 12:34–35; Colossians 3:16"> The heart of the wise causeth his mouth to act wisely, And by his lips he increaseth learning,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 12:18; Proverbs 24:13–14; Proverbs 25:11–12"> Sayings of pleasantness &#91;are&#93; a honeycomb, Sweet to the soul, and healing to the bone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 14:12; Proverbs 12:15; 2 Corinthians 13:5"> There is a way right before a man, And its latter end — ways of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 6:7; Proverbs 14:23; Proverbs 9:12"> A labouring man hath laboured for himself, For his mouth hath caused &#91;him&#93; to bend over it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 3:6; Proverbs 6:14; Isaiah 5:18"> A worthless man is preparing evil, And on his lips — as a burning fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 17:9; Proverbs 18:8; Proverbs 15:18"> A froward man sendeth forth contention, A tale-bearer is separating a familiar friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 2:12–15; Proverbs 12:26; Proverbs 1:10–14"> A violent man enticeth his neighbour, And hath causeth him to go in a way not good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 13:15; Micah 7:3; Matthew 14:7–8"> Consulting his eyes to devise froward things, Moving his lips he hath accomplished evil.</span>

<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="James 1:19; Proverbs 15:18; Proverbs 25:28"> Better &#91;is&#93; the slow to anger than the mighty, And the ruler over his spirit than he who is taking a city.</span>
