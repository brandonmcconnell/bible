<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:7; Psalms 53:5; Isaiah 26:3–4"> The wicked have fled and there is no pursuer. And the righteous as a young lion is confident.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 15:28; 1 Kings 16:8–29; 2 Kings 15:8–31"> By the transgression of a land many &#91;are&#93; its heads. And by an intelligent man, Who knoweth right — it is prolonged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 18:28–30"> A man — poor and oppressing the weak, &#91;Is&#93; a sweeping rain, and there is no bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:32; Ephesians 5:11; Matthew 14:4"> Those forsaking the law praise the wicked, Those keeping the law plead against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 92:6; James 1:5; 1 John 2:27"> Evil men understand not judgment, And those seeking Jehovah understand all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 19:1; Proverbs 28:18; Luke 16:19–23"> Better &#91;is&#93; the poor walking in his integrity, Than the perverse of ways who is rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:3–4; Proverbs 29:3; Proverbs 3:1–35"> Whoso is keeping the law is an intelligent son, And a friend of gluttons, Doth cause his father to blush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 27:16–17; Proverbs 13:22; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> Whoso is multiplying his wealth by biting and usury, For one favouring the poor doth gather it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 66:18; 2 Timothy 4:3–4; Zechariah 7:11–13"> Whoso is turning his ear from hearing the law, Even his prayer &#91;is&#93; an abomination.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 26:27; Psalms 37:25–26; Galatians 2:4"> Whoso is causing the upright to err in an evil way, Into his own pit he doth fall, And the perfect do inherit good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 18:17; Ecclesiastes 9:15–17; Proverbs 26:16"> A rich man is wise in his own eyes, And the intelligent poor searcheth him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 29:2; Proverbs 11:10; Proverbs 28:28"> In the exulting of the righteous the glory &#91;is&#93; abundant, And in the rising of the wicked man is apprehensive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 John 1:8–10; Psalms 32:3–5; Job 31:33"> Whoso is covering his transgressions prospereth not, And he who is confessing and forsaking hath mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 23:17; Psalms 2:11; Isaiah 66:2"> O the happiness of a man fearing continually, And whoso is hardening his heart falleth into evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Matthew 2:16; Proverbs 19:12"> A growling lion, and a ranging bear, &#91;Is&#93; the wicked ruler over a poor people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; Jeremiah 22:15–17; Ecclesiastes 4:1"> A leader lacking understanding multiplieth oppressions, Whoso is hating dishonest gain prolongeth days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 9:6; 1 Kings 21:19; Exodus 21:14"> A man oppressed with the blood of a soul, Unto the pit fleeth, none taketh hold on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 10:9; Proverbs 28:6; Revelation 3:3"> Whoso is walking uprightly is saved, And the perverted of ways falleth at once.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Judges 9:4; Proverbs 27:23–27"> Whoso is tilling his ground is satisfied &#91;with&#93; bread, And whoso is pursuing vanity, Is filled &#91;with&#93; poverty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 16:10–12; Proverbs 13:11; Revelation 2:10"> A stedfast man hath multiplied blessings, And whoso is hasting to be rich is not acquitted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 24:23; Proverbs 18:5; Ezekiel 13:19"> To discern faces is not good, And for a piece of bread doth a man transgress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 23:6; 1 Timothy 6:9; Proverbs 28:20"> Troubled for wealth &#91;is&#93; the man &#91;with&#93; an evil eye, And he knoweth not that want doth meet him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 27:5–6; Matthew 18:15; Psalms 141:5"> Whoso is reproving a man afterwards findeth grace, More than a flatterer with the tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 15:4–6; Proverbs 18:9; Proverbs 19:26"> Whoso is robbing his father, or his mother, And is saying, 'It is not transgression,' A companion he is to a destroyer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 11:25; 1 Timothy 6:6; Proverbs 13:10"> Whoso is proud in soul stirreth up contention, And whoso is trusting on Jehovah is made fat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5; Jeremiah 17:9; Job 28:28"> Whoso is trusting in his heart is a fool, And whoso is walking in wisdom is delivered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Deuteronomy 15:7; Psalms 41:1–3"> Whoso is giving to the poor hath no lack, And whoso is hiding his eyes multiplied curses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 29:2; Job 24:4; Acts 12:23–24"> In the rising of the wicked a man is hidden, And in their destruction the righteous multiply!</span>
