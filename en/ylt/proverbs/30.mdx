<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 31:1; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> Words of a Gatherer, son of an obedient one, the declaration, an affirmation of the man: — I have wearied myself &#91;for&#93; God, I have wearied myself &#91;for&#93; God, and am consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 73:22; 1 Corinthians 3:18; Isaiah 6:5"> For I am more brutish than any one, And have not the understanding of a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 3:7; Ephesians 3:18–19; Romans 11:33"> Nor have I learned wisdom, Yet the knowledge of Holy Ones I know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 3:13; Ephesians 4:8–10; Job 26:8"> Who went up to heaven, and cometh down? Who hath gathered the wind in his fists? Who hath bound waters in a garment? Who established all ends of the earth? What &#91;is&#93; His name? and what His son's name? Surely thou knowest!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 18:30; James 3:17; Psalms 12:6"> Every saying of God &#91;is&#93; tried, A shield He &#91;is&#93; to those trusting in Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; Deuteronomy 4:2; Revelation 22:18–19"> Add not to His words, lest He reason with thee, And thou hast been found false.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 10:42; 2 Kings 2:9; Psalms 27:4"> Two things I have asked from Thee, Withhold not from me before I die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:6–8; Psalms 62:9–10; Psalms 119:37"> Vanity and a lying word put far from me, Poverty or wealth give not to me, Cause me to eat the bread of my portion,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 13:6; Deuteronomy 31:20; Ezekiel 16:49–50"> Lest I become satiated, and have denied, And have said, 'Who &#91;is&#93; Jehovah?' And lest I be poor, and have stolen, And have laid hold of the name of my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 14:4; Ecclesiastes 7:21; Deuteronomy 23:15"> Accuse not a servant unto his lord, Lest he disesteem thee, and thou be found guilty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 20:20; Proverbs 30:17; Exodus 21:17"> A generation &#91;is&#93;, that lightly esteemeth their father, And their mother doth not bless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 18:11; Proverbs 16:2; 2 Timothy 3:5"> A generation — pure in their own eyes, But from their own filth not washed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 6:17; Isaiah 2:11; Psalms 101:5"> A generation — how high are their eyes, Yea, their eyelids are lifted up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Psalms 3:7; Psalms 14:4"> A generation — swords &#91;are&#93; their teeth, And knives — their jaw-teeth, To consume the poor from earth, And the needy from &#91;among&#93; men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 30:21; Proverbs 30:29; Proverbs 6:16"> To the leech &#91;are&#93; two daughters, 'Give, give, Lo, three things are not satisfied, Four have not said 'Sufficiency;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 27:20; Habakkuk 2:5; Genesis 30:1"> Sheol, and a restrained womb, Earth — it &#91;is&#93; not satisfied &#91;with&#93; water, And fire — it hath not said, 'Sufficiency,'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 23:22; Deuteronomy 21:18–21; Proverbs 30:11"> An eye that mocketh at a father, And despiseth to obey a mother, Dig it out do ravens of the valley, And eat it do young eagles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 42:3; Psalms 139:6"> Three things have been too wonderful for me, Yea, four that I have not known:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 40:31; Job 39:27; Exodus 22:16"> The way of the eagle in the heavens, The way of a serpent on a rock, The way of a ship in the heart of the sea, And the way of a man in youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 5:6; Proverbs 7:13–23; Numbers 5:11–30"> So — the way of an adulterous woman, She hath eaten and hath wiped her mouth, And hath said, 'I have not done iniquity.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> For three things hath earth been troubled, And for four — it is not able to bear:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 19:10; Ecclesiastes 10:7; 1 Samuel 25:10–11"> For a servant when he reigneth, And a fool when he is satisfied with bread,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 21:19; Proverbs 27:15; Proverbs 29:21"> For a hated one when she ruleth, And a maid-servant when she succeedeth her mistress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 12:7"> Four &#91;are&#93; little ones of earth, And they are made wiser than the wise:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 6:6–8"> The ants &#91;are&#93; a people not strong, And they prepare in summer their food,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 104:18; Leviticus 11:5"> Conies &#91;are&#93; a people not strong, And they place in a rock their house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joel 2:25; Joel 1:6–7; Joel 2:7–11"> A king there is not to the locust, And it goeth out — each one shouting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> A spider with two hands taketh hold, And is in the palaces of a king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Three there are going well, Yea, four are good in going:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 23:24; Judges 14:18"> An old lion — mighty among beasts, That turneth not back from the face of any,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Daniel 3:15–18; Proverbs 20:2; Proverbs 16:14"> A girt one of the loins, or a he-goat, And a king — no rising up with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 21:5; Job 40:4; Proverbs 26:12"> If thou hast been foolish in lifting up thyself, And if thou hast devised evil — hand to mouth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 17:14; Proverbs 29:22; Proverbs 28:25"> For the churning of milk bringeth out butter, And the wringing of the nose bringeth out blood, And the forcing of anger bringeth out strife!</span>
