<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 1:1; Proverbs 29:15; Proverbs 17:25"> Proverbs of Solomon. A wise son causeth a father to rejoice, And a foolish son &#91;is&#93; an affliction to his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 11:4; Proverbs 12:28; Psalms 49:6–10"> Treasures of wickedness profit not, And righteousness delivereth from death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 37:3; Matthew 6:30–33; Psalms 37:25"> Jehovah causeth not the soul of the righteous to hunger, And the desire of the wicked He thrusteth away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 21:5; Proverbs 20:4; Proverbs 19:15"> Poor &#91;is&#93; he who is working — a slothful hand, And the hand of the diligent maketh rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 6:8; Proverbs 30:25; Proverbs 17:2"> Whoso is gathering in summer &#91;is&#93; a wise son, Whoso is sleeping in harvest &#91;is&#93; a son causing shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:2; Proverbs 28:20; Job 29:13"> Blessings &#91;are&#93; for the head of the righteous, And the mouth of the wicked cover doth violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 112:6; Luke 1:48; Mark 14:9"> The remembrance of the righteous &#91;is&#93; for a blessing, And the name of the wicked doth rot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 7:24–25; Proverbs 12:1; James 3:13"> The wise in heart accepteth commands, And a talkative fool kicketh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 28:18; Matthew 10:26; Psalms 26:11–12"> Whoso is walking in integrity walketh confidently, And whoso is perverting his ways is known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; Proverbs 6:13; 3 John 1:10"> Whoso is winking the eye giveth grief, And a talkative fool kicketh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 13:14; Ephesians 4:29; Proverbs 10:6"> A fountain of life &#91;is&#93; the mouth of the righteous, And the mouth of the wicked cover doth violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 4:8; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7; Proverbs 17:9"> Hatred awaketh contentions, And over all transgressions love covereth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 26:3; Proverbs 6:32; Isaiah 50:4"> In the lips of the intelligent is wisdom found, And a rod &#91;is&#93; for the back of him who is lacking understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 18:7; Proverbs 10:8; Proverbs 13:3"> The wise lay up knowledge, and the mouth of a fool &#91;is&#93; near ruin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 18:11; Proverbs 19:7; Psalms 52:7"> The wealth of the rich &#91;is&#93; his strong city, The ruin of the poor &#91;is&#93; their poverty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 6:23; Isaiah 3:10–11; Hebrews 6:10"> The wage of the righteous &#91;is&#93; for life, The increase of the wicked for sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Peter 1:5–11; Luke 11:28; Proverbs 6:23"> A traveller to life &#91;is&#93; he who is keeping instruction, And whoso is forsaking rebuke is erring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 26:24–26; Psalms 55:21; 2 Samuel 3:27"> Whoso is covering hatred with lying lips, And whoso is bringing out an evil report is a fool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="James 1:19; James 3:2; Proverbs 17:27–28"> In the abundance of words transgression ceaseth not, And whoso is restraining his lips &#91;is&#93; wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:9; Proverbs 12:18; Genesis 6:5"> The tongue of the righteous &#91;is&#93; chosen silver, The heart of the wicked — as a little thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hosea 4:6; Proverbs 17:16; Proverbs 1:29"> The lips of the righteous delight many, And fools for lack of heart die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 107:38; Genesis 26:12; 1 Samuel 2:7–8"> The blessing of Jehovah — it maketh rich, And He addeth no grief with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 15:21; Proverbs 2:14; Proverbs 14:9"> To execute inventions &#91;is&#93; as play to a fool, And wisdom to a man of understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 145:19; Isaiah 66:4; Matthew 5:6"> The feared thing of the wicked it meeteth him, And the desire of the righteous is given.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 15:5; Psalms 58:9; Matthew 7:24–27"> As the passing by of a hurricane, So the wicked is not, And the righteous is a foundation age-during.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 25:13; Proverbs 26:6; Matthew 25:26"> As vinegar to the teeth, And as smoke to the eyes, So &#91;is&#93; the slothful to those sending him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 9:11; Proverbs 3:2; Psalms 91:16"> The fear of Jehovah addeth days, And the years of the wicked are shortened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 8:13; Romans 15:13; Psalms 73:24–26"> The hope of the righteous &#91;is&#93; joyful, And the expectation of the wicked perisheth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 21:15; Romans 2:8–9; Philippians 4:13"> The way of Jehovah &#91;is&#93; strength to the perfect, And ruin to workers of iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 37:22; Psalms 125:1; Psalms 37:9–10"> The righteous to the age is not moved, And the wicked inhabit not the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 37:30; Proverbs 10:13; Proverbs 10:20–21"> The mouth of the righteous uttereth wisdom, And the tongue of frowardness is cut out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 15:28; Proverbs 12:6; Proverbs 11:11"> The lips of the righteous know a pleasing thing, And the mouth of the wicked perverseness!</span>
