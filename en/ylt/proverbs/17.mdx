<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 15:17; Psalms 37:16; Proverbs 21:19"> Better &#91;is&#93; a dry morsel, and rest with it, Than a house full of the sacrifices of strife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 10:5; Ecclesiastes 4:13; Proverbs 14:35"> A wise servant ruleth over a son causing shame, And in the midst of brethren He apportioneth an inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 48:10; Jeremiah 17:10; Psalms 26:2"> A refining pot &#91;is&#93; for silver, and a furnace for gold, And the trier of hearts &#91;is&#93; Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3–4; 1 John 4:5; Proverbs 28:4"> An evil doer is attentive to lips of vanity, Falsehood is giving ear to a mischievous tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 14:31; Job 31:29; 1 John 3:17"> Whoso is mocking at the poor Hath reproached his Maker, Whoso is rejoicing at calamity is not acquitted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 127:3–5; Proverbs 13:22; Proverbs 16:31"> Sons' sons &#91;are&#93; the crown of old men, And the glory of sons &#91;are&#93; their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 29:12; Proverbs 12:19; Psalms 50:16–17"> Not comely for a fool is a lip of excellency, Much less for a noble a lip of falsehood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 23:8; Micah 7:3; 1 Samuel 25:35"> A stone of grace &#91;is&#93; the bribe in the eyes of its possessors, Whithersoever it turneth, it prospereth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 10:12; 1 Peter 4:8; Proverbs 16:28"> Whoso is covering transgression is seeking love, And whoso is repeating a matter Is separating a familiar friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 9:8–9; Proverbs 15:5"> Rebuke cometh down on the intelligent More than a hundred stripes on a fool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 2:46; 2 Samuel 16:5–9; 2 Samuel 18:15"> An evil man seeketh only rebellion, And a fierce messenger is sent against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 13:8; 2 Samuel 17:8; Proverbs 28:15"> The meeting of a bereaved bear by a man, And — not a fool in his folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 109:4–13; 1 Peter 3:9; Romans 12:17"> Whoso is returning evil for good, Evil moveth not from his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 20:3; Proverbs 25:8; 2 Timothy 2:23–24"> The beginning of contention &#91;is&#93; a letting out of waters, And before it is meddled with leave the strife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 23:7; Proverbs 18:5; Isaiah 5:23"> Whoso is justifying the wicked, And condemning the righteous, Even both of these &#91;are&#93; an abomination to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 21:25–26; Proverbs 23:23; Proverbs 18:15"> Why &#91;is&#93; this — a price in the hand of a fool to buy wisdom, And a heart there is none?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 18:24; John 15:13–14; 1 Samuel 20:17"> At all times is the friend loving, And a brother for adversity is born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 11:15; Proverbs 6:1–5; Proverbs 22:26–27"> A man lacking heart is striking hands, A surety he becometh before his friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 16:18; Proverbs 18:12; 1 Samuel 25:36–38"> Whoso is loving transgression is loving debate, Whoso is making high his entrance is seeking destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 3:6–8; Proverbs 10:31; Proverbs 6:12–15"> The perverse of heart findeth not good, And the turned in his tongue falleth into evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 19:13; Proverbs 17:25"> Whoso is begetting a fool hath affliction for it, Yea, the father of a fool rejoiceth not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 15:13; Proverbs 12:25; Proverbs 18:14"> A rejoicing heart doth good to the body, And a smitten spirit drieth the bone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 17:8; Deuteronomy 16:19; Exodus 23:8"> A bribe from the bosom the wicked taketh, To turn aside the paths of judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:14; Ecclesiastes 8:1; Proverbs 14:6"> The face of the intelligent &#91;is&#93; to wisdom, And the eyes of a fool — at the end of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; 2 Samuel 13:1–22; Proverbs 19:13"> A provocation to his father &#91;is&#93; a foolish son, And bitterness to her that bare him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 17:15; Proverbs 18:5; John 18:22"> Also, to fine the righteous is not good, To smite nobles for uprightness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 3:18; James 1:19; Proverbs 10:19"> One acquainted with knowledge is sparing his words, And the cool of temper &#91;is&#93; a man of understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 13:5; Proverbs 15:2; Ecclesiastes 10:14"> Even a fool keeping silence is reckoned wise, He who is shutting his lips intelligent!</span>
