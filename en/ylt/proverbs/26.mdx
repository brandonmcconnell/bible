<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 10:13; Psalms 32:9; 1 Corinthians 4:21"> A whip is for a horse, a bridle for an ass, And a rod for the back of fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 26:5; 1 Peter 3:9; Jude 1:9"> Answer not a fool according to his folly, Lest thou be like to him — even thou.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 12:16; Matthew 16:1–4; Proverbs 28:11"> Answer a fool according to his folly, Lest he be wise in his own eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 10:26; Proverbs 25:13; Proverbs 13:17"> He is cutting off feet, he is drinking injury, Who is sending things by the hand of a fool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 26:9; Psalms 64:8; Proverbs 17:7"> Weak have been the two legs of the lame, And a parable in the mouth of fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 26:1; Proverbs 30:22; Proverbs 19:10"> As one who is binding a stone in a sling, So &#91;is&#93; he who is giving honour to a fool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 23:35"> A thorn hath gone up into the hand of a drunkard, And a parable in the mouth of fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 2:6; Proverbs 11:31"> Great &#91;is&#93; the Former of all, And He is rewarding a fool, And is rewarding transgressors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 2:22; Matthew 12:45; Exodus 8:15"> As a dog hath returned to its vomit, A fool is repeating his folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 29:20; 1 Corinthians 3:18–19; Proverbs 26:5"> Thou hast seen a man wise in his own eyes, More hope of a fool than of him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 22:13; Proverbs 19:15; Proverbs 15:19"> The slothful hath said, 'A lion &#91;is&#93; in the way, A lion &#91;is&#93; in the broad places.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 6:12; Proverbs 12:27; Proverbs 12:24"> The door turneth round on its hinge, And the slothful on his bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 19:24"> The slothful hath hid his hand in a dish, He is weary of bringing it back to his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 26:12; 1 Peter 3:15; Proverbs 12:15"> Wiser &#91;is&#93; the slothful in his own eyes, Than seven &#91;men&#93; returning a reason.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 20:3; 2 Timothy 2:23–24; Luke 12:14"> Laying hold on the ears of a dog, &#91;Is&#93; a passer-by making himself wrath for strife not his own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 25:18; Isaiah 50:11; Proverbs 7:23"> As &#91;one&#93; pretending to be feeble, Who is casting sparks, arrows, and death,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 5:4; Proverbs 10:23; Proverbs 15:21"> So hath a man deceived his neighbour, And hath said, 'Am not I playing?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 22:10; James 3:6; Proverbs 16:28"> Without wood is fire going out, And without a tale-bearer, contention ceaseth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 15:18; Proverbs 29:22; 2 Samuel 20:1"> Coal to burning coals, and wood to fire, And a man of contentions to kindle strife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 18:8; Proverbs 20:19; Ezekiel 22:9"> The words of a tale-bearer &#91;are&#93; as self-inflicted wounds, And they have gone down &#91;to&#93; the inner parts of the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 22:47–48; Luke 11:39; Proverbs 10:18"> Silver of dross spread over potsherd, &#91;Are&#93; burning lips and an evil heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 12:20; Proverbs 10:18; Psalms 41:6"> By his lips doth a hater dissemble, And in his heart he placeth deceit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 6:16–19; Jeremiah 12:6; Psalms 28:3"> When his voice is gracious trust not in him, For seven abominations &#91;are&#93; in his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 55:21–23; 1 Samuel 18:17; 1 Samuel 18:21"> Hatred is covered by deceit, Revealed is its wickedness in an assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 28:10; Psalms 9:15; Psalms 7:15–16"> Whoso is digging a pit falleth into it, And the roller of a stone, to him it turneth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 8:44–49; Luke 20:20–21; John 10:32–33"> A lying tongue hateth its bruised ones, And a flattering mouth worketh an overthrow!</span>
