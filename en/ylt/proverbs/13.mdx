<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 21:23; Proverbs 18:21; Proverbs 18:7"> Whoso is keeping his mouth, is keeping his soul, Whoso is opening wide his lips — ruin to him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 12:24; Proverbs 12:11; 2 Peter 1:5–11"> The soul of the slothful is desiring, and hath not. And the soul of the diligent is made fat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 3:9; Ephesians 4:25; Psalms 119:163"> A false word the righteous hateth, And the wicked causeth abhorrence, and is confounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 11:3; Proverbs 11:5–6; Psalms 140:11"> Righteousness keepeth him who is perfect in the way, And wickedness overthroweth a sin offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 18:11–14; Revelation 3:17; Proverbs 11:24"> There is who is making himself rich, and hath nothing, Who is making himself poor, and wealth &#91;is&#93; abundant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; 1 Peter 1:18–19; Psalms 49:6–10"> The ransom of a man's life &#91;are&#93; his riches, And the poor hath not heard rebuke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 4:18; Proverbs 24:20; Proverbs 20:20"> The light of the righteous rejoiceth, And the lamp of the wicked is extinguished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 19:20; Proverbs 12:15–16; Proverbs 17:14"> A vain man through pride causeth debate, And with the counselled &#91;is&#93; wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 20:21; Proverbs 10:2; James 5:1–5"> Wealth from vanity becometh little, And whoso is gathering by the hand becometh great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 16:22; Proverbs 13:19; Psalms 119:81–83"> Hope prolonged is making the heart sick, And a tree of life &#91;is&#93; the coming desire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 15:31; 2 Chronicles 36:16; 2 John 1:8"> Whoso is despising the Word is destroyed for it, And whoso is fearing the Command is repayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 18:5; Proverbs 10:11; Proverbs 14:27"> The law of the wise &#91;is&#93; a fountain of life, To turn aside from snares of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:19; Proverbs 4:19; Acts 7:10"> Good understanding giveth grace, And the way of the treacherous &#91;is&#93; hard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 15:2; Proverbs 12:22–23; Ephesians 5:17"> Every prudent one dealeth with knowledge, And a fool spreadeth out folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 25:13; 1 Corinthians 4:2; 2 Corinthians 5:20"> A wicked messenger falleth into evil, And a faithful ambassador is healing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 15:5; Proverbs 12:1; Proverbs 15:31–32"> Whoso is refusing instruction — poverty and shame, And whoso is observing reproof is honoured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 7:14–17; Proverbs 13:12; Proverbs 29:27"> A desire accomplished is sweet to the soul, And an abomination to fools &#91;is&#93;: Turn from evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33–34; Proverbs 15:31; Proverbs 9:6"> Whoso is walking with wise men is wise, And a companion of fools suffereth evil.</span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:26; Job 27:16–17; Proverbs 28:8"> A good man causeth sons' sons to inherit, And laid up for the righteous &#91;is&#93; the sinner's wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Proverbs 28:19; Ecclesiastes 5:9"> Abundance of food — the tillage of the poor, And substance is consumed without judgment.</span>
