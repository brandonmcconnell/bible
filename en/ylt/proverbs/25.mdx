<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 1:1; 1 Kings 4:32; Micah 1:1"> Also these are Proverbs of Solomon, that men of Hezekiah king of Judah transcribed: —</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:29; Romans 11:33–34; Job 11:7–8"> The honour of God &#91;is&#93; to hide a thing, And the honour of kings to search out a matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 55:9; Psalms 103:11; 1 Kings 4:29"> The heavens for height, and the earth for depth, And the heart of kings — &#91;are&#93; unsearchable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:20–21; Ezekiel 22:18; Proverbs 17:3"> Take away dross from silver, And a vessel for the refiner goeth forth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 16:12; Proverbs 20:8; Psalms 101:7–8"> Take away the wicked before a king, And established in righteousness is his throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 27:2; Proverbs 25:27; Proverbs 16:19"> Honour not thyself before a king, And in the place of the great stand not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 14:7–11; Luke 18:14; 1 Peter 5:5"> For better &#91;that&#93; he hath said to thee, 'Come thou up hither,' Than &#91;that&#93; he humble thee before a noble, Whom thine eyes have seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 5:25; Proverbs 17:14; Proverbs 18:6"> Go not forth to strive, haste, turn, What dost thou in its latter end, When thy neighbour causeth thee to blush?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 18:5–17; Proverbs 11:13; Proverbs 20:19"> Thy cause plead with thy neighbour, And the secret counsel of another reveal not,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 119:39"> Lest the hearer put thee to shame, And thine evil report turn not back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 15:23; Isaiah 50:4; Ecclesiastes 12:10"> Apples of gold in imagery of silver, &#91;Is&#93; the word spoken at its fit times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 141:5; Proverbs 15:31–32; Proverbs 15:5"> A ring of gold, and an ornament of pure gold, &#91;Is&#93; the wise reprover to an attentive ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 13:17; Proverbs 25:25; Philippians 2:25–30"> As a vessel of snow in a day of harvest, &#91;So is&#93; a faithful ambassador to those sending him, And the soul of his masters he refresheth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 20:6; Luke 14:11; 2 Peter 2:15–19"> Clouds and wind, and rain there is none, &#91;Is&#93; a man boasting himself in a false gift.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; Ecclesiastes 10:4; Proverbs 16:14"> By long-suffering is a ruler persuaded, And a soft tongue breaketh a bone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 25:27; Ephesians 5:18; Proverbs 24:13–14"> Honey thou hast found — eat thy sufficiency, Lest thou be satiated &#91;with&#93; it, and hast vomited it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 19:2–3; Judges 19:18–21; Romans 15:24"> Withdraw thy foot from thy neighbour's house, Lest he be satiated &#91;with&#93; thee, and have hated thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Proverbs 12:18; James 3:6"> A maul, and a sword, and a sharp arrow, &#91;Is&#93; the man testifying against his neighbour a false testimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 36:6; Ezekiel 29:6–7; Job 6:14–20"> A bad tooth, and a tottering foot, &#91;Is&#93; the confidence of the treacherous in a day of adversity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Ecclesiastes 3:4; Isaiah 58:7"> Whoso is taking away a garment in a cold day, &#91;Is as&#93; vinegar on nitre, And a singer of songs on a sad heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 12:20–21; Matthew 5:44; Exodus 23:4–5"> If he who is hating thee doth hunger, cause him to eat bread, And if he thirst, cause him to drink water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:12; Matthew 10:13; 1 Corinthians 15:18"> For coals thou art putting on his head, And Jehovah giveth recompense to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 37:22; Psalms 15:3; Romans 1:30"> A north wind bringeth forth rain, And a secret tongue — indignant faces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 21:19; Proverbs 21:9; Proverbs 27:15–16"> Better to sit on a corner of a roof, Than &#91;with&#93; a woman of contentions, and a house of company.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 15:30; Romans 10:15; Psalms 63:1"> &#91;As&#93; cold waters for a weary soul, So &#91;is&#93; a good report from a far country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:18–19; 1 Samuel 22:14–18; 1 Thessalonians 2:15"> A spring troubled, and a fountain corrupt, &#91;Is&#93; the righteous falling before the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 5:44; Proverbs 27:2; Philippians 2:3"> The eating of much honey is not good, Nor a searching out of one's own honour — honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 16:32; Proverbs 22:24; 2 Chronicles 32:5"> A city broken down without walls, &#91;Is&#93; a man without restraint over his spirit!</span>
