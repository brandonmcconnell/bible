<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 1:20–21; Matthew 3:3; John 7:37"> Doth not wisdom call? And understanding give forth her voice?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 9:14; Proverbs 9:3"> At the head of high places by the way, Between the paths she hath stood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 18:20; Acts 5:20; Job 29:7"> At the side of the gates, at the mouth of the city, The entrance of the openings, she crieth aloud,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 11:15; John 3:16; Psalms 50:1"> 'Unto you, O men, I call, And my voice &#91;is&#93; unto the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 1:22; Acts 26:18; Revelation 3:17–18"> Understand, ye simple ones, prudence, And ye fools, understand the heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 1:26; Matthew 7:28–29; Proverbs 23:16"> Hearken, for noble things I speak, And the opening of my lips &#91;is&#93; uprightness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 37:30; John 8:14; John 17:17"> For truth doth my mouth utter, And an abomination to my lips &#91;is&#93; wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 7:46; Psalms 12:6; Proverbs 8:13"> In righteousness &#91;are&#93; all the sayings of my mouth, Nothing in them is froward and perverse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 14:6; James 1:5; John 7:17"> All of them &#91;are&#93; plain to the intelligent, And upright to those finding knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 2:4–5; Psalms 119:127; Psalms 119:72"> Receive my instruction, and not silver, And knowledge rather than choice gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 3:14–15; Proverbs 4:5–7; Proverbs 16:16"> For better &#91;is&#93; wisdom than rubies, Yea, all delights are not comparable with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 28:26; Exodus 35:30–4; Romans 11:33"> I, wisdom, have dwelt with prudence, And a knowledge of devices I find out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 97:10; Proverbs 16:6; 1 Samuel 2:3"> The fear of Jehovah &#91;is&#93; to hate evil; Pride, and arrogance, and an evil way, And a froward mouth, I have hated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:19; Isaiah 9:6; Isaiah 40:14"> Mine &#91;is&#93; counsel and substance, I &#91;am&#93; understanding, I have might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 13:1; Daniel 2:21; Matthew 28:18"> By me kings reign, and princes decree righteousness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> By me do chiefs rule, and nobles, All judges of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 14:23; John 14:21; Psalms 91:14"> I love those loving me, And those seeking me earnestly do find me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:16; Matthew 6:33; Luke 16:11–12"> Wealth and honour &#91;are&#93; with me, Lasting substance and righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 3:14; Proverbs 10:20; Ecclesiastes 7:12"> Better &#91;is&#93; my fruit than gold, even fine gold, And mine increase than choice silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:32; John 10:27–28; Psalms 32:8"> In a path of righteousness I cause to walk, In midst of paths of judgment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 1:4; Proverbs 24:4; Matthew 25:46"> To cause my lovers to inherit substance, Yea, their treasures I fill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 1:17; John 1:1–2; Proverbs 3:19"> Jehovah possessed me — the beginning of His way, Before His works since then.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 17:24; Psalms 2:6; Genesis 1:26"> From the age I was anointed, from the first, From former states of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 3:20; Genesis 1:2; 1 John 4:9"> In there being no depths, I was brought forth, In there being no fountains heavy &#91;with&#93; waters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 90:2; Psalms 102:25–28; Job 38:4–11"> Before mountains were sunk, Before heights, I was brought forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 1:1–31"> While He had not made the earth, and out-places, And the top of the dusts of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 40:22; Job 26:10; Proverbs 3:19"> In His preparing the heavens I &#91;am&#93; there, In His decreeing a circle on the face of the deep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> In His strengthening clouds above, In His making strong fountains of the deep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 104:9; Jeremiah 5:22; Genesis 1:9–10"> In His setting for the sea its limit, And the waters transgress not His command, In His decreeing the foundations of earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 1:1–3; John 1:18; Isaiah 42:1"> Then I am near Him, a workman, And I am a delight — day by day. Rejoicing before Him at all times,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 16:3; John 13:1; Psalms 40:6–8"> Rejoicing in the habitable part of His earth, And my delights &#91;are&#93; with the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 11:28; Psalms 119:1–2; Psalms 1:1–4"> And now, ye sons, hearken to me, Yea, happy are they who keep my ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 4:1; Psalms 81:11–12; Proverbs 5:1"> Hear instruction, and be wise, and slight not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 11:28; Matthew 7:24; Proverbs 3:13"> O the happiness of the man hearkening to me, To watch at my doors day by day, To watch at the door-posts of my entrance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 3:36; 1 John 5:11–12; Proverbs 12:2"> For whoso is finding me, hath found life, And bringeth out good-will from Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 15:32; Proverbs 1:31; 1 Corinthians 16:22"> And whoso is missing me, is wronging his soul, All hating me have loved death!</span>
