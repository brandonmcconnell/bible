<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; 1 Chronicles 22:9; 1 Chronicles 28:5"> Proverbs of Solomon, son of David, king of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 7:4; Deuteronomy 4:5–6; 2 Timothy 3:15–17"> For knowing wisdom and instruction, For understanding sayings of intelligence,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 2:1–9; Job 22:22; Proverbs 8:10–11"> For receiving the instruction of wisdom, Righteousness, judgment, and uprightness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 8:5; Proverbs 8:17; Psalms 119:9"> For giving to simple ones — prudence, To a youth — knowledge and discretion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 12:1; Proverbs 9:9; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> (The wise doth hear and increaseth learning, And the intelligent doth obtain counsels.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 78:2; Mark 4:11; Psalms 49:4"> For understanding a proverb and its sweetness, Words of the wise and their acute sayings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 28:28; Psalms 111:10–1; Ecclesiastes 12:13"> Fear of Jehovah &#91;is&#93; a beginning of knowledge, Wisdom and instruction fools have despised!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 6:20; Proverbs 30:17; Proverbs 5:1–2"> Hear, my son, the instruction of thy father, And leave not the law of thy mother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 5:29; Proverbs 4:9; Genesis 41:42"> For a graceful wreath &#91;are&#93; they to thy head, And chains to thy neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; Proverbs 16:29; Psalms 1:1"> My son, if sinners entice thee be not willing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:26; Proverbs 1:18; Proverbs 12:6"> If they say, 'Come with us, we lay wait for blood, We watch secretly for the innocent without cause,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 124:3; Psalms 28:1; Romans 3:13"> We swallow them as Sheol — alive, And whole — as those going down &#91;to&#93; the pit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Luke 12:15; Job 24:2–3"> Every precious substance we find, We fill our houses &#91;with&#93; spoil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Thy lot thou dost cast among us, One purse is — to all of us.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:101; Psalms 1:1; Proverbs 4:14–15"> My son! go not in the way with them, Withhold thy foot from their path,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Proverbs 6:18; Proverbs 4:16"> For their feet to evil do run, And they haste to shed blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 35:11; Proverbs 7:23; Jeremiah 8:7"> Surely in vain is the net spread out before the eyes of any bird.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 9:16; Esther 7:10; Proverbs 5:22–23"> And they for their own blood lay wait, They watch secretly for their own lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 15:27; 2 Peter 2:3; James 5:1–4"> So &#91;are&#93; the paths of every gainer of dishonest gain, The life of its owners it taketh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 9:3; Colossians 2:3; Luke 11:49"> Wisdom in an out-place crieth aloud, In broad places she giveth forth her voice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 5:20; Proverbs 9:3; Matthew 13:2"> At the head of the multitudes she calleth, In the openings of the gates, In the city her sayings she saith:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 1:29; Psalms 1:1; Proverbs 5:12"> 'Till when, ye simple, do ye love simplicity? And have scorners their scorning desired? And do fools hate knowledge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 3:19; Ezekiel 33:11; Ezekiel 18:27–30"> Turn back at my reproof, lo, I pour forth to you my spirit, I make known my words with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 65:12; Romans 10:21; Jeremiah 7:13"> Because I have called, and ye refuse, I stretched out my hand, and none is attending,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 81:11; Luke 7:30; Proverbs 1:30"> And ye slight all my counsel, And my reproof ye have not desired.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 2:4; Proverbs 10:24; Psalms 37:13"> I also in your calamity do laugh, I deride when your fear cometh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 3:25–26; Luke 21:34–35; Psalms 58:9"> When your fear cometh as destruction, And your calamity as a hurricane doth come, When on you come adversity and distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Micah 3:4; James 4:3; Jeremiah 11:11"> Then they call me, and I do not answer, They seek me earnestly, and find me not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 1:22; Job 21:14–15; Proverbs 6:23"> Because that they have hated knowledge, And the fear of Jehovah have not chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 81:11; Proverbs 1:25; Luke 14:18–20"> They have not consented to my counsel, They have despised all my reproof,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 14:14; Jeremiah 6:19; Proverbs 22:8"> And they eat of the fruit of their way, And from their own counsels they are filled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 8:36; Jeremiah 2:19; John 3:36"> For the turning of the simple slayeth them, And the security of the foolish destroyeth them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 25:12–13; Psalms 112:7–8; Isaiah 26:3"> And whoso is hearkening to me dwelleth confidently, And &#91;is&#93; quiet from fear of evil!'</span>
