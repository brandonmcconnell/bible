<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:5; Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 29:17"> For whom Jehovah loveth He reproveth, Even as a father the son He is pleased with.</span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 8:11; Job 28:18; Psalms 73:25–26"> Precious she &#91;is&#93; above rubies, And all thy pleasures are not comparable to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 3:2; 2 Corinthians 6:10; Proverbs 22:4"> Length of days &#91;is&#93; in her right hand, In her left &#91;are&#93; wealth and honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 1:79; Isaiah 26:3; Philippians 4:8–9"> Her ways &#91;are&#93; ways of pleasantness, And all her paths &#91;are&#93; peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 11:30; Revelation 2:7; Proverbs 15:4"> A tree of life she &#91;is&#93; to those laying hold on her, And whoso is retaining her &#91;is&#93; happy.</span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 1:8; Deuteronomy 6:6–9; Deuteronomy 32:46–47"> My son! let them not turn from thine eyes, Keep thou wisdom and thoughtfulness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 4:22; Proverbs 1:9; Isaiah 38:16"> And they are life to thy soul, and grace to thy neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 4:12; Proverbs 10:9; Psalms 121:3"> Then thou goest thy way confidently, And thy foot doth not stumble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 3:5; Psalms 4:8; Proverbs 6:22"> If thou liest down, thou art not afraid, Yea, thou hast lain down, And sweet hath been thy sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10–14; Psalms 112:7; 1 Peter 3:14"> Be not afraid of sudden fear, And of the desolation of the wicked when it cometh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 91:3; 1 Samuel 2:9; Proverbs 14:26"> For Jehovah is at thy side, And He hath kept thy foot from capture.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Galatians 6:10; James 2:15–16; Romans 13:7"> Withhold not good from its owners, When thy hand &#91;is&#93; toward God to do &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 19:13; Deuteronomy 24:12–15; 2 Corinthians 8:11"> Say not thou to thy friend, 'Go, and return, and to-morrow I give,' And substance with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 6:14; Proverbs 16:29–30; Psalms 55:20"> Devise not against thy neighbour evil, And he sitting confidently with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:24; Proverbs 29:22; Romans 12:18–21"> Strive not with a man without cause, If he have not done thee evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 24:1; Psalms 37:1; Galatians 5:21"> Be not envious of a man of violence, Nor fix thou on any of his ways.</span>
