<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jude 1:12; Genesis 43:32–34"> When thou sittest to eat with a ruler, Thou considerest diligently that which &#91;is&#93; before thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 3:19; Matthew 18:8–9; 1 Corinthians 9:27"> And thou hast put a knife to thy throat, If thou &#91;art&#93; a man of appetite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 23:6; Psalms 141:4; Ephesians 4:22"> Have no desire to his dainties, seeing it &#91;is&#93; lying food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5; Proverbs 28:20; Matthew 6:19"> Labour not to make wealth, From thine own understanding cease, Dost thou cause thine eyes to fly upon it? Then it is not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 27:24; 1 John 2:16; James 5:1–2"> For wealth maketh to itself wings, As an eagle it flieth to the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 141:4; Proverbs 23:3; Daniel 1:8–10"> Eat not the bread of an evil eye, And have no desire to his dainties,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 12:2; Psalms 55:21; Judges 16:15"> For as he hath thought in his soul, so &#91;is&#93; he, 'Eat and drink,' saith he to thee, And his heart &#91;is&#93; not with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Thy morsel thou hast eaten thou dost vomit up, And hast marred thy words that &#91;are&#93; sweet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 9:7–8; Matthew 7:6; Proverbs 26:4–5"> In the ears of a fool speak not, For he treadeth on the wisdom of thy words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 22:28; Zechariah 7:10; Deuteronomy 27:17"> Remove not a border of olden times, And into fields of the fatherless enter not,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 22:23; Job 19:25; Jeremiah 51:36"> For their Redeemer &#91;is&#93; strong, He doth plead their cause with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 5:1–2; Proverbs 2:2–6; Proverbs 23:19"> Bring in to instruction thy heart, And thine ear to sayings of knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 13:24; Proverbs 19:18; Proverbs 29:15"> Withhold not from a youth chastisement, When thou smitest him with a rod he dieth not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:5; Proverbs 22:15; 1 Corinthians 11:32"> Thou with a rod smitest him, And his soul from Sheol thou deliverest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 23:24–25; 2 John 1:4; Proverbs 29:3"> My son, if thy heart hath been wise, My heart rejoiceth, even mine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 8:6; Ephesians 5:4; James 3:2"> And my reins exult when thy lips speak uprightly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 28:14; 2 Corinthians 7:1; 1 Peter 1:17"> Let not thy heart be envious at sinners, But — in the fear of Jehovah all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 10:35; Jeremiah 29:11; Psalms 9:18"> For, is there a posterity? Then thy hope is not cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 23:26; Proverbs 23:12; Proverbs 4:10–23"> Hear thou, my son, and be wise, And make happy in the way thy heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 21:34; Ephesians 5:18; Isaiah 5:22"> Be not thou among quaffers of wine, Among gluttonous ones of flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 21:17; Proverbs 19:15; Deuteronomy 21:20"> For the quaffer and glutton become poor, And drowsiness clotheth with rags.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 30:17; Proverbs 1:8; John 19:26–27"> Hearken to thy father, who begat thee, And despise not thy mother when she hath become old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 18:15; Matthew 13:44; Psalms 119:72"> Truth buy, and sell not, Wisdom, and instruction, and understanding,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 15:20; Proverbs 23:15–16"> The father of the righteous rejoiceth greatly, The begetter of the wise rejoiceth in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 17:25; Luke 11:27–28; Luke 1:58"> Rejoice doth thy father and thy mother, Yea, she that bare thee is joyful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 119:2; Psalms 119:9–11; Deuteronomy 6:5"> Give, my son, thy heart to me, And let thine eyes watch my ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 22:14"> For a harlot &#91;is&#93; a deep ditch, And a strange woman &#91;is&#93; a strait pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Proverbs 7:12; Proverbs 22:14"> She also, as catching prey, lieth in wait, And the treacherous among men she increaseth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 5:18; Isaiah 5:11; Proverbs 23:21"> Who hath woe? who hath sorrow? Who hath contentions? who hath plaint? Who hath wounds without cause? Who hath redness of eyes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ephesians 5:18; Isaiah 5:11; Proverbs 20:1"> Those tarrying by the wine, Those going in to search out mixed wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 John 2:16; 2 Samuel 11:2; Psalms 119:37"> See not wine when it showeth itself red, When it giveth in the cup its colour, It goeth up and down through the upright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:8; Isaiah 28:7–8; Amos 9:3"> Its latter end — as a serpent it biteth, And as a basilisk it stingeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jude 1:12–13; Psalms 69:12; Daniel 5:4"> Thine eyes see strange women, And thy heart speaketh perverse things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2–7; 1 Kings 20:16–22; 1 Samuel 30:16–17"> And thou hast been as one lying down in the heart of the sea, And as one lying down on the top of a mast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 56:12; Jeremiah 5:3; 2 Peter 2:22"> 'They smote me, I have not been sick, They beat me, I have not known. When I awake — I seek it yet again!'</span>
