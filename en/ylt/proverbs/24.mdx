<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Proverbs 23:17; Proverbs 3:31"> Be not envious of evil men, And desire not to be with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Psalms 7:14; Psalms 28:3"> For destruction doth their heart meditate, And perverseness do their lips speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 14:1; 1 Corinthians 3:9; Proverbs 9:1"> By wisdom is a house builded, And by understanding it establisheth itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 15:6; Proverbs 21:20; Proverbs 20:15"> And by knowledge the inner parts are filled, &#91;With&#93; all precious and pleasant wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:19; Colossians 1:11; Proverbs 21:22"> Mighty &#91;is&#93; the wise in strength, And a man of knowledge is strengthening power,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 11:14; Proverbs 20:18; Proverbs 15:22"> For by plans thou makest for thyself war, And deliverance &#91;is&#93; in a multitude of counsellors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 14:6; Psalms 10:5; 1 Corinthians 2:14"> Wisdom &#91;is&#93; high for a fool, In the gate he openeth not his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 21:11; Ezekiel 38:10–11; Proverbs 14:22"> Whoso is devising to do evil, Him they call a master of wicked thoughts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 15:19; 2 Corinthians 10:5; Isaiah 55:7"> The thought of folly &#91;is&#93; sin, And an abomination to man &#91;is&#93; a scorner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 40:28–31; Ephesians 3:13; Jeremiah 51:46"> Thou hast shewed thyself weak in a day of adversity, Straitened is thy power,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 82:4; Isaiah 58:6–7; 1 John 3:16–17"> If &#91;from&#93; delivering those taken to death, And those slipping to the slaughter — thou keepest back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 34:11; Revelation 2:23; Jeremiah 17:10"> When thou sayest, 'Lo, we knew not this.' Is not the Ponderer of hearts He who understandeth? And the Keeper of thy soul He who knoweth? And He hath rendered to man according to his work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 7:15; Song of Solomon 5:1; Proverbs 25:16"> Eat my son, honey that &#91;is&#93; good, And the honeycomb — sweet to thy palate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 1:25; Jeremiah 15:16; Proverbs 2:10"> So &#91;is&#93; the knowledge of wisdom to thy soul, If thou hast found that there is a posterity And thy hope is not cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 9:24; Isaiah 32:18; 1 Samuel 23:20–23"> Lay not wait, O wicked one, At the habitation of the righteous. Do not spoil his resting-place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 34:19; Psalms 37:24; Micah 7:8–10"> For seven &#91;times&#93; doth the righteous fall and rise, And the wicked stumble in evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Obadiah 1:12; Job 31:29; Proverbs 17:5"> In the falling of thine enemy rejoice not, And in his stumbling let not thy heart be joyful,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 1:15–16; Lamentations 4:21–22"> Lest Jehovah see, and &#91;it be&#93; evil in His eyes, And He hath turned from off him His anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 119:115; Psalms 37:1; Proverbs 23:17"> Fret not thyself at evil doers, Be not envious at the wicked,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 13:9; Job 18:5–6; Psalms 11:6"> For there is not a posterity to the evil, The lamp of the wicked is extinguished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 13:1–7; 1 Peter 2:13–17; Titus 3:1"> Fear Jehovah, my son, and the king, With changers mix not up thyself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 90:11; Hosea 5:11; Numbers 16:31–35"> For suddenly doth their calamity rise, And the ruin of them both — who knoweth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 28:21; Proverbs 18:5; John 7:24"> These also are for the wise: — To discern faces in judgment is not good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 17:15; Isaiah 5:23; Proverbs 11:26"> Whoso is saying to the wicked, 'Thou &#91;art&#93; righteous,' Peoples execrate him — nations abhor him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:20; 1 Samuel 3:13; Proverbs 28:23"> And to those reproving it is pleasant, And on them cometh a good blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 15:23; Genesis 41:38–57; Proverbs 25:11–12"> Lips he kisseth who is returning straightforward words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 14:28–30; 1 Kings 5:17–18; 1 Kings 6:7"> Prepare in an out-place thy work, And make it ready in the field — go afterwards, Then thou hast built thy house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ephesians 4:25; Matthew 26:59–60; Proverbs 25:18"> Be not a witness for nought against thy neighbour, Or thou hast enticed with thy lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 20:22; Romans 12:17–21; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> Say not, 'As he did to me, so I do to him, I render to each according to his work.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Proverbs 6:6–19; Job 4:8"> Near the field of a slothful man I passed by, And near the vineyard of a man lacking heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:18; Isaiah 5:5; Jeremiah 4:3"> And lo, it hath gone up — all of it — thorns! Covered its face have nettles, And its stone wall hath been broken down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:11; Deuteronomy 13:11; 1 Corinthians 10:6"> And I see — I — I do set my heart, I have seen — I have received instruction,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:6–8; Proverbs 6:4–11; Romans 13:11"> A little sleep — a little slumber — A little folding of the hands to lie down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 13:4"> And thy poverty hath come &#91;as&#93; a traveller, And thy want as an armed man!</span>
