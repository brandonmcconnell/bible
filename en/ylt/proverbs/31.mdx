<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 6:20; Proverbs 1:8; 2 Timothy 1:5"> Words of Lemuel a king, a declaration that his mother taught him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 49:15; 1 Samuel 1:11; 1 Samuel 1:28"> 'What, my son? and what, son of my womb? And what, son of my vows?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:17; Nehemiah 13:26; Proverbs 7:26–27"> Give not to women thy strength, And thy ways to wiping away of kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Ecclesiastes 10:17; Isaiah 5:22"> Not for kings, O Lemuel, Not for kings, to drink wine, And for princes a desire of strong drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:5"> Lest he drink, and forget the decree, And change the judgment of any of the sons of affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:23; Psalms 104:15; 1 Samuel 1:10"> Give strong drink to the perishing, And wine to the bitter in soul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 5:18"> He drinketh, and forgetteth his poverty, And his misery he remembereth not again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 82:3–4; Proverbs 24:11–12; Esther 4:13–16"> Open thy mouth for the dumb, For the right of all sons of change.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 1:17; Leviticus 19:15; Amos 5:11–12"> Open thy mouth, judge righteously, Both the cause of the poor and needy!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 19:14; Proverbs 12:4; Proverbs 18:22"> A woman of worth who doth find? Yea, far above rubies &#91;is&#93; her price.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 3:1–7; 2 Kings 4:22–23; 2 Kings 4:9–10"> The heart of her husband hath trusted in her, And spoil he lacketh not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:18–22; 1 Samuel 25:26–27"> She hath done him good, and not evil, All days of her life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Titus 2:5; 1 Timothy 5:10; 2 Thessalonians 3:10–12"> She hath sought wool and flax, And with delight she worketh &#91;with&#93; her hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 31:24; 1 Kings 9:26–28; Ezekiel 27:3–36"> She hath been as ships of the merchant, From afar she bringeth in her bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 12:11; Mark 1:35; Luke 12:42"> Yea, she riseth while yet night, And giveth food to her household, And a portion to her damsels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 13:44; Song of Solomon 8:12; Joshua 15:18"> She hath considered a field, and taketh it, From the fruit of her hands she hath planted a vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 38:3; 1 Peter 1:13; Ephesians 6:10"> She hath girded with might her loins, And doth strengthen her arms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:9; Matthew 25:3–10; Psalms 127:2"> She hath perceived when her merchandise &#91;is&#93; good, Her lamp is not extinguished in the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 35:25–26"> Her hands she hath sent forth on a spindle, And her hands have held a distaff.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 4:28; Hebrews 13:16; Proverbs 22:9"> Her hand she hath spread forth to the poor, Yea, her hands she sent forth to the needy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 45:22; 2 Samuel 1:24; Proverbs 25:20"> She is not afraid of her household from snow, For all her household are clothed &#91;with&#93; scarlet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 19:8; Proverbs 7:16; Genesis 41:42"> Ornamental coverings she hath made for herself, Silk and purple &#91;are&#93; her clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 12:4; Ruth 4:1; Deuteronomy 16:18"> Known in the gates is her husband, In his sitting with elders of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:16; Luke 16:19; Proverbs 31:19"> Linen garments she hath made, and selleth, And a girdle she hath given to the merchant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 40:10; Romans 13:14; Job 29:14"> Strength and honour &#91;are&#93; her clothing, And she rejoiceth at a latter day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Proverbs 16:24; Proverbs 10:31"> Her mouth she hath opened in wisdom, And the law of kindness &#91;is&#93; on her tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:10; Titus 2:4; 2 Thessalonians 3:6"> She &#91;is&#93; watching the ways of her household, And bread of sloth she eateth not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:15–17; 2 Timothy 1:5; 1 Kings 2:19"> Her sons have risen up, and pronounce her happy, Her husband, and he praiseth her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Song of Solomon 6:8–9; Ephesians 5:27"> 'Many &#91;are&#93; the daughters who have done worthily, Thou hast gone up above them all.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 1:7; 1 Peter 3:4–5; Proverbs 11:22"> The grace &#91;is&#93; false, and the beauty &#91;is&#93; vain, A woman fearing Jehovah, she may boast herself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 128:2; Hebrews 6:10; Matthew 7:16"> Give ye to her of the fruit of her hands, And her works do praise her in the gates!</span>
