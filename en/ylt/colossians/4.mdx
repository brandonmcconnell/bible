<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:1–4; Ephesians 6:20; Colossians 4:6"> that I may manifest it, as it behoveth me to speak;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 5:15–17; 1 Thessalonians 4:12; James 3:13"> in wisdom walk ye toward those without, the time forestalling;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; 1 Peter 3:15; Ecclesiastes 10:12"> your word always in grace — with salt being seasoned — to know how it behoveth you to answer each one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 6:21–23; Acts 20:4; 2 Timothy 4:12"> All the things concerning me make known to you shall Tychicus — the beloved brother, and faithful ministrant, and fellow-servant in the Lord —</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 6:22; Colossians 2:2; Philippians 2:28"> whom I did send unto you for this very thing, that he might know the things concerning you, and might comfort your hearts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philemon 1:10–19; Colossians 4:7"> with Onesimus the faithful and beloved brother, who is of you; all things to you shall they make known that &#91;are&#93; here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philemon 1:24; 2 Timothy 4:11; Acts 19:29"> Salute you doth Aristarchus, my fellow-captive, and Marcus, the nephew of Barnabas, (concerning whom ye did receive commands — if he may come unto you receive him,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 11:2; 2 Corinthians 6:1; Acts 10:45"> and Jesus who is called Justus, who are of the circumcision: these only &#91;are&#93; fellow-workers for the reign of God who did become a comfort to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 15:30; Colossians 1:9; Colossians 1:7"> Salute you doth Epaphras, who &#91;is&#93; of you, a servant of Christ, always striving for you in the prayers, that ye may stand perfect and made full in all the will of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 2:1; Romans 10:2; Revelation 1:11"> for I do testify to him, that he hath much zeal for you, and those in Laodicea, and those in Hierapolis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Philemon 1:24; 2 Timothy 4:10–11"> Salute you doth Lukas, the beloved physician, and Demas;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 16:5; Philemon 1:2; 1 Corinthians 16:9"> salute ye those in Laodicea — brethren, and Nymphas, and the assembly in his house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:27; 2 Thessalonians 3:14"> and when the epistle may be read with you, cause that also in the assembly of the Laodiceans it may be read, and the &#91;epistle&#93; from Laodicea that ye also may read;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Philemon 1:2; Acts 1:17; Ezekiel 44:23–24"> and say to Archippus, 'See to the ministration that thou didst receive in the Lord, that thou mayest fulfil it.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 13:3; 2 Timothy 4:22; 1 Timothy 6:21"> The salutation by the hand of me, Paul; remember my bonds; the grace &#91;is&#93; with you. Amen.</span>
