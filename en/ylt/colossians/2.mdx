<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:29; Philippians 1:30; Revelation 3:14–22"> For I wish you to know how great a conflict I have for you and those in Laodicea, and as many as have not seen my face in the flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 1:27; Colossians 4:8; Ephesians 6:22"> that their hearts may be comforted, being united in love, and to all riches of the full assurance of the understanding, to the full knowledge of the secret of the God and Father, and of the Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:24; 1 Corinthians 1:30; Romans 11:33"> in whom are all the treasures of the wisdom and the knowledge hid,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 4:1; Ephesians 4:14; Romans 16:18–19"> and this I say, that no one may beguile you in enticing words,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:3–4; 1 Thessalonians 2:17; 1 Corinthians 16:13"> for if even in the flesh I am absent — yet in the spirit I am with you, joying and beholding your order, and the stedfastness of your faith in regard to Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; 1 John 2:6; 1 Thessalonians 4:1"> as, then, ye did receive Christ Jesus the Lord, in him walk ye,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 3:17; Jeremiah 17:8; Ephesians 2:20–22"> being rooted and built up in him, and confirmed in the faith, as ye were taught — abounding in it in thanksgiving.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:20; Ephesians 5:6; Matthew 7:15"> See that no one shall be carrying you away as spoil through the philosophy and vain deceit, according to the deliverance of men, according to the rudiments of the world, and not according to Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 1:19; John 10:30; John 1:14"> because in him doth tabernacle all the fulness of the Godhead bodily,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 3:19; Colossians 1:16–18; Ephesians 1:20–23"> and ye are in him made full, who is the head of all principality and authority,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 2:29; Philippians 3:3; Romans 6:6"> in whom also ye were circumcised with a circumcision not made with hands, in the putting off of the body of the sins of the flesh in the circumcision of the Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 6:3–5; 1 Peter 3:21; Galatians 3:27"> being buried with him in the baptism, in which also ye rose with &#91;him&#93; through the faith of the working of God, who did raise him out of the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 2:5–6; Romans 8:11; Ephesians 2:1"> And you — being dead in the trespasses and the uncircumcision of your flesh — He made alive together with him, having forgiven you all the trespasses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 44:22; Ephesians 2:14–16; Isaiah 43:25"> having blotted out the handwriting in the ordinances that is against us, that was contrary to us, and he hath taken it out of the way, having nailed it to the cross;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 2:14; Ephesians 4:8; Matthew 12:29"> having stripped the principalities and the authorities, he made a shew of them openly — having triumphed over them in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 14:5–6; Romans 14:10; Galatians 4:10"> Let no one, then, judge you in eating or in drinking, or in respect of a feast, or of a new moon, or of sabbaths,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 10:1; Hebrews 8:5; John 1:17"> which are a shadow of the coming things, and the body &#91;is&#93; of the Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; 1 John 4:1–2; Colossians 2:23"> let no one beguile you of your prize, delighting in humble-mindedness and &#91;in&#93; worship of the messengers, intruding into the things he hath not seen, being vainly puffed up by the mind of his flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 4:15–16; John 15:4–6; 2 Peter 3:18"> and not holding the head, from which all the body — through the joints and bands gathering supply, and being knit together — may increase with the increase of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; Colossians 2:16; Galatians 4:3"> If, then, ye did die with the Christ from the rudiments of the world, why, as living in the world, are ye subject to ordinances?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:3; Genesis 3:3; Isaiah 52:11"> — thou mayest not touch, nor taste, nor handle —</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Titus 1:14; 1 Corinthians 6:13"> which are all for destruction with the using, after the commands and teachings of men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:8; Colossians 2:18; Colossians 2:8"> which are, indeed, having a matter of wisdom in will-worship, and humble-mindedness, and neglecting of body — not in any honour, unto a satisfying of the flesh.</span>
