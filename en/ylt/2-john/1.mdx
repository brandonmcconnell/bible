<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:4; 1 John 3:18; John 8:32"> The Elder to the choice Kyria, and to her children, whom I love in truth, and not I only, but also all those having known the truth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 2:14; 1 John 1:8; 1 John 2:17"> because of the truth that is remaining in us, and with us shall be to the age,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 4:10; Romans 1:7; 1 Timothy 1:14"> there shall be with you grace, kindness, peace, from God the Father, and from the Lord Jesus Christ, the Son of the Father, in truth and love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="3 John 1:3–4; Ephesians 5:8; Ephesians 5:2"> I rejoiced exceedingly that I have found of thy children walking in truth, even as a command we did receive from the Father;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 John 3:11; John 13:34–35; John 15:12"> and now I beseech thee, Kyria, not as writing to thee a new command, but which we had from the beginning, that we may love one another,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 John 5:3; 1 John 2:24; John 15:10"> and this is the love, that we may walk according to His commands; this is the command, even as ye did hear from the beginning, that in it ye may walk,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 2:26; 1 John 4:1–3; 1 John 2:18–22"> because many leading astray did enter into the world, who are not confessing Jesus Christ coming in flesh; this one is he who is leading astray, and the antichrist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:35; Revelation 3:11; 1 Corinthians 3:14"> See to yourselves that ye may not lose the things that we wrought, but a full reward may receive;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 7:16–17; Colossians 3:16; 1 John 2:22–24"> every one who is transgressing, and is not remaining in the teaching of the Christ, hath not God; he who is remaining in the teaching of the Christ, this one hath both the Father and the Son;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Titus 3:10; Romans 16:17–18; 2 Thessalonians 3:6"> if any one doth come unto you, and this teaching doth not bear, receive him not into the house, and say not to him, 'Hail!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:22; Ephesians 5:11; Revelation 18:4"> for he who is saying to him, 'Hail,' hath fellowship with his evil works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="3 John 1:13–14; John 16:12; 1 Corinthians 16:5–7"> Many things having to write to you, I did not intend through paper and ink, but I hope to come unto you, and speak mouth to mouth, that our joy may be full;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 5:13; 2 John 1:1–13"> salute thee do the children of thy choice sister. Amen.</span>
