<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 18:1–3; 1 Chronicles 3:13; 2 Chronicles 26:5"> Hezekiah hath reigned — a son of twenty and five years, and twenty and nine years he hath reigned in Jerusalem, and the name of his mother &#91;is&#93; Abijah daughter of Zechariah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:2; 2 Chronicles 28:1"> and he doth that which is right in the eyes of Jehovah, according to all that David his father did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:24; 2 Chronicles 29:7; Matthew 6:33"> He, in the first year of his reign, in the first month, hath opened the doors of the house of Jehovah, and strengtheneth them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:2; Nehemiah 3:29; 2 Chronicles 32:6"> and bringeth in the priests and the Levites, and gathereth them to the broad place to the east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:6; 1 Corinthians 3:16–17; 2 Chronicles 34:3–8"> And he saith to them, 'Hear me, O Levites, now, sanctify yourselves, and sanctify the house of Jehovah, God of your fathers, and bring out the impurity from the sanctuary,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:16; Jeremiah 2:27; Daniel 9:16"> for our fathers have trespassed, and done that which is evil in the eyes of Jehovah our God, and forsake him, and turn round their faces from the tabernacle of Jehovah, and give the neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:24; Leviticus 24:2–8; 2 Chronicles 29:3"> 'Also — they have shut the doors of the porch, and quench the lamps, and perfume they have not made, and burnt-offering have not caused to ascend in the sanctuary to the God of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:9; Jeremiah 25:18; Jeremiah 29:18"> and the wrath of Jehovah is on Judah and Jerusalem, and He giveth them for a trembling, for an astonishment, and for a hissing, as ye are seeing with your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:17; 2 Chronicles 28:5–8; Lamentations 5:7"> And lo, fallen have our fathers by the sword, and our sons, and our daughters, and our wives &#91;are&#93; in captivity for this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:5; 2 Chronicles 23:16; Ezra 10:3"> 'Now — with my heart — to make a covenant before Jehovah, God of Israel, and the fierceness of His anger doth turn back from us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:8; Galatians 6:7–8; Numbers 8:6–14"> My sons, be not now at rest, for on you hath Jehovah fixed to stand before Him, to serve Him, and to be to Him ministering and making perfume.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 3:19–20; 1 Chronicles 15:5–7; Numbers 4:2–20"> And the Levites rise — Mahath son of Amasai, and Joel son of Azariah, of the sons of the Kohathite; and of the sons of Merari: Kish son of Abdi, and Azariah son of Jehalelel; and of the Gershonite: Joah son of Zimmah, and Eden son of Joah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:39; 1 Chronicles 15:8; 1 Chronicles 15:17"> and of the sons of Elizaphan: Shimri, and Jeiel; and of the sons of Asaph: Zechariah and Mattaniah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:33; 1 Chronicles 25:1; 1 Chronicles 25:6"> and of the sons of Heman: Jehiel and Shimei; and of the sons of Jeduthun: Shemaiah and Uzziel —</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:12; 1 Chronicles 23:28; 2 Chronicles 29:5"> and they gather their brethren, and sanctify themselves, and come in, according to the command of the king in the matters of Jehovah, to cleanse the house of Jehovah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:16; Matthew 21:12–13; Matthew 23:27"> and the priests come in to the inner part of the house of Jehovah to cleanse &#91;it&#93;, and bring out all the uncleanness that they have found in the temple of Jehovah to the court of the house of Jehovah, and the Levites receive &#91;it&#93;, to take &#91;it&#93; out to the brook Kidron without.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 12:2–8; 2 Chronicles 3:4; 1 Kings 6:3"> And they begin on the first of the first month to sanctify, and on the eighth day of the month they have come to the porch of Jehovah, and they sanctify the house of Jehovah in eight days, and on the sixteenth day of the first month they have finished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:1; 2 Chronicles 4:7–8; 2 Chronicles 13:11"> And they come in within unto Hezekiah the king, and say, 'We have cleansed all the house of Jehovah, and the altar of the burnt-offering, and all its vessels, and the table of the arrangement, and all its vessels,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:24"> and all the vessels that king Ahaz cast away in his reign — in his trespass — we have prepared and sanctified, and lo, they &#91;are&#93; before the altar of Jehovah.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 6:12; Jeremiah 25:4; Exodus 24:4"> And Hezekiah the king riseth early, and gathereth the heads of the city, and goeth up to the house of Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 4:3–14; Job 42:8; Ezekiel 45:23"> and they bring in seven bullocks, and seven rams, and seven lambs, and seven young he-goats, for a sin-offering for the kingdom, and for the sanctuary, and for Judah; and he saith to sons of Aaron, the priests, to cause &#91;them&#93; to ascend on the altar of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 4:18; Leviticus 8:19; Leviticus 8:24"> And they slaughter the oxen, and the priests receive the blood, and sprinkle on the altar; and they slaughter the rams, and sprinkle the blood on the altar; and they slaughter the lambs, and sprinkle the blood on the altar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 4:15; Leviticus 4:24; Leviticus 1:4"> and they bring nigh the he-goats of the sin-offering before the king and the assembly, and they lay their hands upon them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 14:20; Ezekiel 45:15; 2 Corinthians 5:18–21"> and the priests slaughter them, and make a sin-offering with their blood on the altar, to make atonement for all Israel, for 'For all Israel,' said the king, '&#91;is&#93; the burnt-offering and the sin-offering.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 23:5; 2 Samuel 24:11"> And he appointeth the Levites in the house of Jehovah with cymbals, with psalteries, and with harps, by the command of David, and of Gad, seer of the king, and of Nathan the prophet, for by the hand of Jehovah &#91;is&#93; the command, by the hand of His prophets;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:5; 1 Chronicles 15:24; Amos 6:5"> and the Levites stand with the instruments of David, and the priests with the trumpets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 23:18; Psalms 137:3–4; 2 Chronicles 20:21"> And Hezekiah saith to cause the burnt-offering to ascend on the altar; and at the time the burnt-offering began — began the song of Jehovah, and the trumpets, even by the hands of the instruments of David king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 5:8–14; Psalms 89:15; Psalms 68:24–26"> And all the assembly are doing obeisance, and the singers singing, and the trumpeters blowing; the whole &#91;is&#93; till the completion of the burnt-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:18; 1 Chronicles 29:20; Philippians 2:10–11"> And at the completion of the offering up bowed have the king and all those found with him, and do obeisance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Philippians 4:4; Psalms 95:1–2; 1 Chronicles 16:7–36"> And Hezekiah the king saith, and the princes, to the Levites to give praise to Jehovah in the words of David, and of Asaph the seer, and they praise — unto joy, and they bow, and do obeisance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:9; Exodus 35:22; Leviticus 7:12"> And Hezekiah answereth and saith, 'Now ye have filled your hand to Jehovah, come nigh, and bring in sacrifices and thank-offerings to the house of Jehovah;' and the assembly bring in sacrifices and thank-offerings, and every willing-hearted one — burnt-offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezra 6:17; 1 Kings 3:4; 1 Chronicles 29:21"> And the number of the burnt-offerings that the assembly have brought in, is seventy oxen, a hundred rams, lambs two hundred; for a burnt-offering to Jehovah &#91;are&#93; all these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And the sanctified things &#91;are&#93; oxen six hundred, and sheep three thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:11; 2 Chronicles 30:3; Psalms 94:15"> Only, the priests have become few, and have not been able to strip the whole of the burnt-offerings, and their brethren the Levites strengthen them till the completion of the work, and till the priests sanctify themselves, for the Levites &#91;are&#93; more upright of heart to sanctify themselves than the priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 15:5–10; Exodus 29:13; 1 Chronicles 16:37–42"> And also, burnt-offerings &#91;are&#93; in abundance, with fat of the peace-offerings, and with oblations for the burnt-offering; and the service of the house of Jehovah is established,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 2:41; 1 Thessalonians 3:8–9; 1 Chronicles 29:9"> and rejoice doth Hezekiah and all the people, because of God's giving preparation to the people, for the thing hath been suddenly.</span>
</div>
