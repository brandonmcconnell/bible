<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 6:1"> and he beginneth to build in the second &#91;day&#93;, in the second month, in the fourth year of his reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 6:2–3; 1 Chronicles 28:11–19"> And &#91;in&#93; these hath Solomon been instructed to build the house of God: The length &#91;in&#93; cubits by the former measure &#91;is&#93; sixty cubits, and the breadth twenty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 10:23; Acts 5:12; Acts 3:11"> As to the porch that &#91;is&#93; on the front, the length &#91;is&#93; by the front of the breadth of the house, twenty cubits, and the height a hundred and twenty, and he overlayeth it within with pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 6:15–17; 1 Kings 6:21–22"> And the large house he hath covered with fir-trees, and he doth cover it with good gold, and causeth to ascend on it palms and chains,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 54:11–12; 1 Chronicles 29:8; Revelation 21:18–21"> and he overlayeth the house with precious stone for beauty, and the gold &#91;is&#93; gold of Parvaim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 6:20–22; Exodus 26:29; Ezekiel 7:20"> and he covereth the house, the beams, the thresholds, and its walls, and its doors, with gold, and hath graved cherubs on the walls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 26:33; 1 Kings 6:19–20; 1 Kings 6:16"> And he maketh the most holy house: its length &#91;is&#93; by the front of the breadth of the house twenty cubits, and its breadth twenty cubits, and he covereth it with good gold, to six hundred talents;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:11"> and the weight of the nails &#91;is&#93; fifty shekels of gold, and the upper chambers he hath covered with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 6:23–28; Exodus 25:18"> And he maketh in the most holy house two cherubs, image work, and he overlayeth them with gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> as to the wings of the cherubs, their length &#91;is&#93; twenty cubits, the wing of the one &#91;is&#93; five cubits, touching the wall of the house, and the other wing &#91;is&#93; five cubits, touching the wing of the other cherub.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And the wing of the other cherub &#91;is&#93; five cubits touching the wall of the house, and the other wing &#91;is&#93; five cubits, adhering to the wing of the other cherub.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 25:20"> The wings of these cherubs are spreading forth twenty cubits, and they are standing on their feet and their faces &#91;are&#93; inward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 9:3; Hebrews 10:20; Matthew 27:51"> And he maketh the vail of blue, and purple, and crimson, and fine linen, and causeth cherubs to go up on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 7:15–24; Jeremiah 52:20–23"> And he maketh at the front of the house two pillars, thirty and five cubits in length, and the ornament that &#91;is&#93; on their heads five cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 7:20; 1 Kings 6:21"> And he maketh chains in the oracle, and putteth on the heads of the pillars, and maketh a hundred pomegranates, and putteth on the chains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 7:21"> And he raiseth up the pillars on the front of the temple, one on the right, and one on the left, and calleth the name of that on the right Jachin, and the name of that on the left Boaz.</span>
