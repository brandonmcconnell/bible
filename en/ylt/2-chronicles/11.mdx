<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 12:21–24; Psalms 33:10; Psalms 33:16"> And Rehoboam cometh in to Jerusalem, and assembleth the house of Judah and Benjamin, a hundred and eighty thousand chosen warriors, to fight with Israel, to bring back the kingdom to Rehoboam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:15; 2 Chronicles 12:5–7; 2 Chronicles 8:14"> And a word of Jehovah is unto Shemaiah, a man of God, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 17:34; Genesis 49:28; Revelation 7:4–8"> 'Speak unto Rehoboam son of Solomon king of Judah, and unto all Israel in Judah and Benjamin, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 50:20; 1 Kings 11:29–38; 2 Chronicles 10:15–16"> Thus said Jehovah, Ye do not go up nor fight with your brethren, turn back each to his house, for from Me hath this thing been;' and they hear the words of Jehovah, and turn back from going against Jeroboam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:2–6; 2 Chronicles 14:6–7; Isaiah 22:8–11"> And Rehoboam dwelleth in Jerusalem, and buildeth cities for a bulwark in Judah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:5; Amos 1:1; 1 Chronicles 4:32"> yea, he buildeth Beth-Lehem and Etam, and Tekoa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 15:35; 1 Samuel 22:1; Joshua 15:58"> and Beth-Zur, and Shocho, and Adullam,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 54:1; Joshua 15:24; 1 Samuel 23:19"> and Gath, and Mareshah, and Ziph,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 15:39; Joshua 15:35; Joshua 10:5"> and Adoraim, and Lachish, and Azekah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 13:22; Joshua 15:33; Joshua 14:14"> and Zorah, and Aijalon, and Hebron, that &#91;are&#93; in Judah and in Benjamin, cities of bulwarks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:19; Isaiah 22:10–11; 2 Chronicles 11:23"> And he strengtheneth the bulwarks, and putteth in them leaders, and treasures of food, and oil, and wine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:22; 2 Chronicles 26:14–15; 2 Chronicles 32:5"> and in every city and city targets and spears, and strengtheneth them very greatly; and he hath Judah and Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the priests, and the Levites, that &#91;are&#93; in all Israel, have stationed themselves by him, out of all their border,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:9; Numbers 35:2–5; 1 Kings 12:28–33"> for the Levites have left their suburbs and their possession, and they come to Judah and to Jerusalem, for Jeroboam and his sons have cast them off from acting as priests to Jehovah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 12:28; 1 Kings 12:31; 1 Kings 13:33"> and he establisheth to him priests for high places, and for goats, and for calves, that he made —</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:9; Acts 11:23; Psalms 84:5–7"> and after them, out of all the tribes of Israel, those giving their heart to seek Jehovah, God of Israel, have come in to Jerusalem to sacrifice to Jehovah, God of their father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:1; 2 Chronicles 1:1–12; 2 Chronicles 7:17–19"> And they strengthen the kingdom of Judah, and strengthen Rehoboam son of Solomon, for three years, because they walked in the way of David and Solomon for three years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:6; 1 Chronicles 27:18; 1 Samuel 17:13"> And Rehoboam taketh to him a wife, Mahalath, child of Jerimoth son of David, &#91;and&#93; Abigail daughter of Eliab, son of Jesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And she beareth to him sons, Jeush, and Shamaria, and Zaham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:2; 2 Chronicles 11:21; 2 Chronicles 12:16"> And after her he hath taken Maachah daughter of Absalom, and she beareth to him Abijah, and Attai, and Ziza, and Shelomith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 8:30; Deuteronomy 17:17; 1 Kings 11:3"> And Rehoboam loveth Maachah daughter of Absalom above all his wives and his concubines — for eighteen wives he hath taken, and sixty concubines — and he begetteth twenty and eight sons, and sixty daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:15–17; 1 Chronicles 29:1; 1 Chronicles 5:1–2"> And Rehoboam appointeth for head Abijah son of Maachah, for leader among his brethren, for to cause him to reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:3; Genesis 25:6; Luke 16:8"> And he hath understanding, and spreadeth out of all his sons to all lands of Judah and Benjamin, to all cities of the bulwarks, and giveth to them provision in abundance; and he asketh a multitude of wives.</span>
