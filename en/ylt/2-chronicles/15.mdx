<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:20; 2 Chronicles 20:14; Numbers 24:2"> And upon Azariah son of Oded hath been the Spirit of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 4:8; 2 Chronicles 24:20; 2 Chronicles 15:4"> and he goeth out before Asa, and saith to him, 'Hear, me, Asa, and all Judah and Benjamin; Jehovah &#91;is&#93; with you — in your being with Him, and if ye seek Him, He is found of you, and if ye forsake Him, He forsaketh you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:9; Leviticus 10:11; 2 Chronicles 17:8–9"> and many days &#91;are&#93; to Israel without a true God, and without a teaching priest, and without law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 55:6; Deuteronomy 4:29–30; 2 Chronicles 15:15"> and it turneth back in its distress unto Jehovah, God of Israel, and they seek Him, and He is found of them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 5:6; Psalms 121:8; 1 Samuel 13:6"> and in those times there is no peace to him who is going out, and to him who is coming in, for many troubles &#91;are&#93; on all the inhabitants of the lands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 21:22–24; Matthew 24:7; 2 Chronicles 33:11"> and they have been beaten down, nation by nation, and city by city, for God hath troubled them with every adversity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; Joshua 1:7; Joshua 1:9"> and ye, be ye strong, and let not your hands be feeble, for there is a reward for your work.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:12; 2 Chronicles 13:19; 2 Chronicles 4:1"> And at Asa's hearing these words, and the prophecy of Oded the prophet, he hath strengthened himself, and doth cause the abominations to pass away out of all the land of Judah and Benjamin, and out of the cities that he hath captured from the hill-country of Ephraim, and reneweth the altar of Jehovah that &#91;is&#93; before the porch of Jehovah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:16; Acts 9:31; Zechariah 8:21–23"> and gathereth all Judah and Benjamin, and the sojourners with them out of Ephraim, and Manasseh, and out of Simeon — for they have fallen unto him from Israel in abundance, in their seeing that Jehovah his God &#91;is&#93; with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esther 8:9"> And they are gathered to Jerusalem in the third month of the fifteenth year of the reign of Asa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:13–15; Numbers 31:28–29; Numbers 31:50"> and sacrifice to Jehovah on that day from the spoil they have brought in — oxen seven hundred, and sheep seven thousand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 24:14; 2 Chronicles 23:16; Deuteronomy 10:12"> and they enter into a covenant to seek Jehovah, God of their fathers, with all their heart, and with all their soul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 22:20; Psalms 115:13; 1 Kings 18:40"> and every one who doth not seek for Jehovah, God of Israel, is put to death, from small unto great, from man unto woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:13; Psalms 81:1–4; Nehemiah 10:29"> And they swear to Jehovah with a loud voice, and with shouting, and with trumpets, and with cornets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:7; 2 Chronicles 15:12; Isaiah 26:8"> and rejoice do all Judah concerning the oath, for with all their heart they have sworn, and with all their good-will they have sought Him, and He is found of them, and Jehovah giveth rest to them round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 15:13–24; 1 Kings 15:10; 2 Kings 23:15"> And also Maachah, mother of Asa the king — he hath removed her from &#91;being&#93; mistress, in that she hath made for a shrine a horrible thing, and Asa cutteth down her horrible thing, and beateth &#91;it&#93; small, and burneth &#91;it&#93; by the brook Kidron:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 14:4; 2 Kings 12:3; 1 Kings 11:4"> yet the high places have not turned aside from Israel; only, the heart of Asa hath been perfect all his days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 7:51; 1 Chronicles 26:20–26; 1 Kings 15:14–15"> And he bringeth in the sanctified things of his father, and his own sanctified things, to the house of God, silver, and gold, and vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 15:31; 1 Kings 15:33; 1 Kings 15:16–17"> And war hath not been till the thirty and fifth year of the reign of Asa.</span>
