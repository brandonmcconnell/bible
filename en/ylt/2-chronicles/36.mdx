<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:11; 2 Chronicles 33:25; 2 Chronicles 26:1"> And the people of the land take Jehoahaz son of Josiah, and cause him to reign instead of his father in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> A son of three and twenty years &#91;is&#93; Jehoahaz in his reigning, and three months he hath reigned in Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 23:33"> and turn him aside doth the king of Egypt in Jerusalem, and fineth the land a hundred talents of silver, and a talent of gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:10–12; 2 Kings 23:34–35; Ezekiel 19:3–4"> and the king of Egypt causeth Eliakim his brother to reign over Judah and Jerusalem, and turneth his name to Jehoiakim; and Jehoahaz his brother hath Necho taken, and bringeth him in to Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 23:36–37; Jeremiah 22:13–19; Jeremiah 26:1"> A son of twenty and five years &#91;is&#93; Jehoiakim in his reigning, and eleven years he hath reigned in Jerusalem, and he doth the evil thing in the eyes of Jehovah his God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:11; 2 Kings 24:5–6; Ezekiel 19:5–9"> against him hath Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon come up, and bindeth him in brazen fetters to take him away to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 24:13; Daniel 5:2–4; Jeremiah 27:16–18"> And of the vessels of the house of Jehovah hath Nebuchadnezzar brought in to Babylon, and putteth them in his temple in Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 24:5–6; Matthew 1:11–12; 1 Chronicles 3:16–17"> And the rest of the matters of Jehoiakim, and his abominations that he hath done, and that which is found against him, lo, they are written on the book of the kings of Israel and Judah, and reign doth Jehoiachin his son in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 24:8–17"> A son of eight years is Jehoiachin in his reigning, and three months and ten days he hath reigned in Jerusalem, and he doth the evil thing in the eyes of Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:1; 2 Chronicles 36:7; 2 Kings 24:10–17"> and at the turn of the year hath king Nebuchadnezzar sent and bringeth him in to Babylon, with the desirable vessels of the house of Jehovah, and causeth Zedekiah his brother to reign over Judah and Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 24:18–20; Jeremiah 52:1–3; Jeremiah 27:1"> A son of twenty and one years &#91;is&#93; Zedekiah in his reigning, and eleven years he hath reigned in Jerusalem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:23; Jeremiah 37:2–21; Jeremiah 38:14–28"> and he doth the evil thing in the eyes of Jehovah his God, he hath not been humbled before Jeremiah the prophet &#91;speaking&#93; from the mouth of Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:2–3; 2 Chronicles 30:8; Ezekiel 17:11–20"> and also, against king Nebuchadnezzar he hath rebelled, who had caused him to swear by God, and he hardeneth his neck, and strengtheneth his heart, against turning back unto Jehovah, God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:8; Ezekiel 22:6; Micah 3:9–11"> Also, all the heads of the priests, and the people, having multiplied to commit a trespass according to all the abominations of the nations, and they defile the house of Jehovah that He hath sanctified in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 35:15; Jeremiah 7:13; Jeremiah 25:3–4"> And Jehovah, God of their fathers, sendeth unto them by the hand of His messengers — rising early and sending — for He hath had pity on His people, and on His habitation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:12–13; 2 Chronicles 30:10; Luke 22:63–64"> and they are mocking at the messengers of God, and despising His words, and acting deceitfully with His prophets, till the going up of the fury of Jehovah against His people — till there is no healing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 74:20; Ezra 9:7; Ezekiel 9:5–7"> And He causeth to go up against them the king of the Chaldeans, and he slayeth their chosen ones by the sword in the house of their sanctuary, and hath had no pity on young man and virgin, old man and very aged — the whole He hath given into his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:7; 2 Chronicles 36:10; Isaiah 39:6"> And all the vessels of the house of God, the great and the small, and the treasures of the house of Jehovah, and the treasures of the king and of his princes — the whole he hath brought in to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 79:1; 1 Kings 9:8; Jeremiah 52:13–15"> And they burn the house of God, and break down the wall of Jerusalem, and all its palaces they have burnt with fire, and all its desirable vessels — to destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:7; Ezra 1:1–11; 2 Chronicles 36:22"> And he removeth those left of the sword unto Babylon, and they are to him and to his sons for servants, till the reigning of the kingdom of Persia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:10; Leviticus 26:34–35; Zechariah 1:12"> to fulfil the word of Jehovah in the mouth of Jeremiah, till the land hath enjoyed its sabbaths; all the days of the desolation it kept sabbath — to the fulness of seventy years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:12; Ezra 1:1–3; Jeremiah 29:10"> And in the first year of Cyrus king of Persia, at the completion of the word of Jehovah in the mouth of Jeremiah, hath Jehovah waked up the spirit of Cyrus king of Persia, and he causeth an intimation to pass over into all his kingdom, and also in writing, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 44:26–28; Ezra 7:13; Romans 8:31"> 'Thus said Cyrus king of Persia, All kingdoms of the earth hath Jehovah, God of the heavens, given to me, and He hath laid a charge on me to build to Him a house in Jerusalem, that &#91;is&#93; in Judah; who is among you of all His people? Jehovah his God &#91;is&#93; with him, and he doth go up.'</span>
