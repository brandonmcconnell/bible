<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:31; 1 Kings 2:10; 1 Kings 14:31"> And Abijah lieth with his fathers, and they bury him in the city of David, and reign doth Asa his son in his stead: in his days was the land quiet ten years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 15:11; 2 Chronicles 31:20; 1 Kings 15:14"> And Asa doth that which is good, and that which is right, in the eyes of Jehovah his God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:5; Exodus 34:13; 2 Kings 23:14"> and turneth aside the altars of the stranger, and the high places, and breaketh the standing-pillars, and cutteth down the shrines,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:19; Psalms 119:10; Psalms 101:2–8"> and saith to Judah to seek Jehovah, God of their fathers, and to do the law and the command;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:4; 2 Chronicles 34:7"> and he turneth aside out of all cities of Judah the high places and the images, and the kingdom is quiet before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:15; 1 Chronicles 22:9; Job 34:29"> And he buildeth cities of bulwarks in Judah, for the land hath quiet, and there is no war with him in these years, because Jehovah hath given rest to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 3:12; 2 Chronicles 14:6; Jeremiah 29:12–14"> And he saith to Judah, 'Let us build these cities, and compass &#91;them&#93; with wall, and towers, two-leaved doors, and bars, while the land &#91;is&#93; before us, because we have sought Jehovah our God, we have sought, and He giveth rest to us round about;' and they build and prosper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:3; 2 Chronicles 17:14–19; 2 Chronicles 11:1"> And there is to Asa a force bearing target and spear, out of Judah three hundred thousand, and out of Benjamin, bearing shield and treading bow, two hundred and eighty thousand: all these &#91;are&#93; mighty of valour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:8; 2 Chronicles 12:2–3; 2 Chronicles 11:8"> And come out unto them doth Zerah the Cushite with a force of a thousand thousand, and chariots three hundred, and he cometh in unto Mareshah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 1:17; Joshua 19:4"> and Asa goeth out before him, and they set battle in array in the valley of Zephathah at Mareshah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:19; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Psalms 22:5"> And Asa calleth unto Jehovah his God, and saith, 'Jehovah! it is nothing with Thee to help, between the mighty and those who have no power; help us, O Jehovah, our God, for on Thee we have leant, and in Thy name we have come against this multitude; O Jehovah, our God thou &#91;art&#93;; let him not prevail with Thee — mortal man!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:15; Psalms 136:17–18; Deuteronomy 28:7"> And Jehovah smiteth the Cushim before Asa, and before Judah, and the Cushim flee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 10:19; Genesis 26:1; Genesis 20:1"> and Asa and the people who &#91;are&#93; with him pursue them even to Gerar, and there fall of the Cushim, for they have no preserving, because they have been broken before Jehovah, and before His camp; and they bear away very much spoil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:10; Genesis 35:5; 2 Chronicles 20:29"> and smite all the cities round about Gerar, for a fear of Jehovah hath been upon them, and they spoil all the cities, for abundant spoil hath been in them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 31:30–47; Numbers 31:9; 1 Samuel 30:20"> and also tents of cattle they have smitten, and they capture sheep in abundance, and camels, and turn back to Jerusalem.</span>
