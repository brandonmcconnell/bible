<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:21; 2 Peter 2:1; 1 Timothy 4:1"> Beloved, every spirit believe not, but prove the spirits, if of God they are, because many false prophets have gone forth to the world;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:14; 1 Corinthians 12:3; 1 Timothy 3:16"> in this know ye the Spirit of God; every spirit that doth confess Jesus Christ in the flesh having come, of God it is,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 2:22; 2 John 1:7; 1 John 2:18"> and every spirit that doth not confess Jesus Christ in the flesh having come, of God it is not; and this is that of the antichrist, which ye heard that it doth come, and now in the world it is already.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 8:31; 1 John 5:4; Romans 8:37"> Ye — of God ye are, little children, and ye have overcome them; because greater is He who &#91;is&#93; in you, than he who is in the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 8:23; 2 Peter 2:2–3; John 17:14"> They — of the world they are; because of this from the world they speak, and the world doth hear them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 14:17; John 10:27; 1 Corinthians 2:12–14"> we — of God we are; he who is knowing God doth hear us; he who is not of God, doth not hear us; from this we know the spirit of the truth, and the spirit of the error.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 4:20–1; 1 John 4:8; 1 Peter 1:22"> Beloved, may we love one another, because the love is of God, and every one who is loving, of God he hath been begotten, and doth know God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 4:16; Psalms 86:15; 2 Corinthians 13:11"> he who is not loving did not know God, because God is love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 3:16; 1 John 5:11; Romans 8:32"> In this was manifested the love of God in us, because His Son — the only begotten — hath God sent to the world, that we may live through him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4–5; Romans 5:8–10; 1 John 4:19"> in this is the love, not that we loved God, but that He did love us, and did send His Son a propitiation for our sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 15:12–13; Colossians 3:13; John 13:34"> Beloved, if thus did God love us, we also ought one another to love;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 1:18; 1 John 2:5; 1 John 4:17–18"> God no one hath ever seen; if we may love one another, God in us doth remain, and His love is having been perfected in us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 2:20–22; 1 John 3:24; 1 Corinthians 3:16–17"> in this we know that in Him we do remain, and He in us, because of His Spirit He hath given us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 John 4:10; John 3:16–17; John 4:42"> And we — we have seen and do testify, that the Father hath sent the Son — Saviour of the world;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 10:9; 1 John 5:5; Matthew 10:32"> whoever may confess that Jesus is the Son of God, God in him doth remain, and he in God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 3:24; Psalms 36:7–9; 1 John 3:1"> and we — we have known and believed the love, that God hath in us; God is love, and he who is remaining in the love, in God he doth remain, and God in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 John 4:12; 1 John 2:28; Romans 8:29"> In this made perfect hath been the love with us, that boldness we may have in the day of the judgment, because even as He is, we — we also are in this world;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7; Romans 8:15; 1 John 4:12"> fear is not in the love, but the perfect love doth cast out the fear, because the fear hath punishment, and he who is fearing hath not been made perfect in the love;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 4:10; John 3:16; John 15:16"> we — we love him, because He — He first loved us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 John 3:17; 1 John 2:9; 1 John 4:12"> if any one may say — 'I love God,' and his brother he may hate, a liar he is; for he who is not loving his brother whom he hath seen, God — whom he hath not seen — how is he able to love?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 22:37–39; Leviticus 19:18; 1 John 3:11"> and this &#91;is&#93; the command we have from Him, that he who is loving God, may also love his brother.</span>
