<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Elihu answereth and saith:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 1:5; 1 Corinthians 14:20; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> Hear, O wise men, my words, And, O knowing ones, give ear to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 12:11; 1 Corinthians 2:15; Job 33:2"> For the ear doth try words, And the palate tasteth to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:21; Judges 19:30; Romans 12:2"> Judgment let us choose for ourselves, Let us know among ourselves what &#91;is&#93; good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 27:2; Job 33:9; Job 16:17"> For Job hath said, 'I have been righteous, And God hath turned aside my right,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 6:4; Job 27:4–6; Job 16:13"> Against my right do I lie? Mortal &#91;is&#93; mine arrow — without transgression.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 15:16; Deuteronomy 29:19; Proverbs 1:22"> Who &#91;is&#93; a man like Job? He drinketh scoffing like water,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 1:1; 1 Corinthians 15:33; Proverbs 13:20"> And he hath travelled for company With workers of iniquity, So as to go with men of wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 21:14–16; Job 35:3; Malachi 3:14"> For he hath said, 'It doth not profit a man, When he delighteth himself with God.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 9:14; Deuteronomy 32:4; Job 8:3"> Therefore, O men of heart, hearken to me; Far be it from God to do wickedness, And &#91;from&#93; the Mighty to do perverseness:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Romans 2:6; Revelation 22:12"> For the work of man he repayeth to him, And according to the path of each He doth cause him to find.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 8:3; Psalms 11:7; Psalms 145:17"> Yea, truly, God doth not do wickedly, And the Mighty doth not pervert judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:11; Isaiah 40:13–14; Romans 11:34–36"> Who hath inspected for Himself the earth? And who hath placed all the habitable world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 104:29; Job 7:17; Job 9:4"> If He doth set on him His heart, His spirit and his breath unto Him He gathereth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Isaiah 57:16"> Expire doth all flesh together, And man to dust returneth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 13:2–6; Job 12:3"> And if &#91;there is&#93; understanding, hear this, Give ear to the voice of my words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 40:8; Genesis 18:25; 2 Samuel 23:3"> Yea, doth one hating justice govern? Or the Most Just dost thou condemn?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 22:28; Proverbs 17:26; Romans 13:7"> Who hath said to a king — 'Worthless,' Unto princes — 'Wicked?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:17; Acts 10:34; James 2:5"> That hath not accepted the person of princes, Nor hath known the rich before the poor, For a work of His hands &#91;are&#93; all of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 73:19; 1 Samuel 25:37–39; 1 Samuel 26:10"> &#91;In&#93; a moment they die, and at midnight Shake do people, and they pass away, And they remove the mighty without hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 15:3; Job 31:4; Jeremiah 16:17"> For His eyes &#91;are&#93; on the ways of each, And all his steps He doth see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Amos 9:2–3; Proverbs 10:29"> There is no darkness nor death-shade, For workers of iniquity to be hidden there;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezra 9:13; Psalms 119:137; Romans 9:20"> For He doth not suffer man any more, To go unto God in judgment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 113:7–8; Psalms 94:5; Daniel 2:21"> He breaketh the mighty — no searching! And He appointeth others in their stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hosea 7:2; Job 34:20; Amos 8:7"> Therefore He knoweth their works, And He hath overturned by night, And they are bruised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 58:10–11; Deuteronomy 21:21; Isaiah 66:24"> As wicked He hath stricken them, In the place of beholders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 28:5; 1 Samuel 15:11; Isaiah 5:12"> Because that against right They have turned aside from after Him, And none of His ways have considered wisely,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="James 5:4; Exodus 3:7; Job 35:9"> To cause to come in unto Him The cry of the poor, And the cry of the afflicted He heareth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 23:13; John 14:27; Psalms 27:9"> And He giveth rest, and who maketh wrong? And hideth the face, and who beholdeth it? And in reference to a nation and to a man, &#91;It is&#93; the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 29:2–12; Job 34:21; 2 Kings 21:9"> From the reigning of a profane man, From the snares of a people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Micah 7:9; Job 33:27; Job 42:6"> For unto God hath any said: 'I have taken away, I do not corruptly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 32:8; Psalms 25:4–5; Psalms 139:23–24"> Besides &#91;that which&#93; I see, shew Thou me, If iniquity I have done — I do not add?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 33:32; Isaiah 45:9; Psalms 135:6"> By thee doth He recompense, That thou hast refused — That thou dost choose, and not I? And what thou hast known, speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:15; Job 34:4; Job 34:16"> Let men of heart say to me, And a wise man is hearkening to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 35:16; Job 38:2; Job 15:2"> Job — not with knowledge doth he speak, And his words &#91;are&#93; not with wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Job 21:7; Job 23:16; Job 12:6"> My Father! let Job be tried — unto victory, Because of answers for men of iniquity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Job 27:23; Isaiah 1:19–20; 1 Samuel 15:23"> For he doth add to his sin, Transgression among us he vomiteth, And multiplieth his sayings to God.</span>
