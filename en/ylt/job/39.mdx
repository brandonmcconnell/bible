<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 29:9; 1 Samuel 24:2; Psalms 104:18"> Hast thou known the time of The bearing of the wild goats of the rock? The bringing forth of hinds thou dost mark!</span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> They bow down, Their young ones they bring forth safely, Their pangs they cast forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Safe are their young ones, They grow up in the field, they have gone out, And have not returned to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 11:12; Job 24:5; Job 6:5"> Who hath sent forth the wild ass free? Yea, the bands of the wild ass who opened?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 107:34; Job 24:5; Deuteronomy 29:23"> Whose house I have made the wilderness, And his dwellings the barren land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 5:18; Job 3:18; Exodus 5:13–16"> He doth laugh at the multitude of a city, The cries of an exactor he heareth not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 104:27–28; Psalms 145:15–16; Job 40:15"> The range of mountains &#91;is&#93; his pasture, And after every green thing he seeketh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 23:22; Deuteronomy 33:17; Psalms 92:10"> Is a Reem willing to serve thee? Doth he lodge by thy crib?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 10:10–11; Psalms 129:3; Job 39:7"> Dost thou bind a Reem in a furrow &#91;with&#93; his thick band? Doth he harrow valleys after thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 1:26; Psalms 144:14; Genesis 42:26"> Dost thou trust in him because great &#91;is&#93; his power? And dost thou leave unto him thy labour?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:15; Proverbs 3:16; Amos 2:13"> Dost thou trust in him That he doth bring back thy seed? And &#91;to&#93; thy threshing-floor doth gather &#91;it&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:7; Zechariah 5:9; 2 Chronicles 9:21"> The wing of the rattling ones exulteth, Whether the pinion of the ostrich or hawk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For she leaveth on the earth her eggs, And on the dust she doth warm them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And she forgetteth that a foot may press it, And a beast of the field tread it down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Lamentations 4:3; Deuteronomy 28:56–57; 1 Kings 3:26–27"> Her young ones it hath hardened without her, In vain &#91;is&#93; her labour without fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 35:11; Job 17:4; Isaiah 57:17"> For God hath caused her to forget wisdom, And He hath not given a portion To her in understanding:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 41:29; Job 39:22; Job 5:22"> At the time on high she lifteth herself up, She laugheth at the horse and at his rider.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 147:10; Psalms 93:1; Psalms 104:1"> Dost thou give to the horse might? Dost thou clothe his neck &#91;with&#93; a mane?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:16; Job 41:20–21; Joel 2:5"> Dost thou cause him to rush as a locust? The majesty of his snorting &#91;is&#93; terrible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:6; Psalms 19:5; 1 Samuel 17:4–10"> They dig in a valley, and he rejoiceth in power, He goeth forth to meet the armour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 41:33; Job 39:16; Job 39:18"> He laugheth at fear, and is not affrighted, And he turneth not back from the face of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 41:26–29"> Against him rattle doth quiver, The flame of a spear, and a halbert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Amos 3:6; Luke 24:41; Job 37:20"> With trembling and rage he swalloweth the ground, And remaineth not stedfast Because of the sound of a trumpet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 70:3; Ezekiel 26:2; Ezekiel 36:2"> Among the trumpets he saith, Aha, And from afar he doth smell battle, Roaring of princes and shouting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:15; Song of Solomon 2:12; Leviticus 16:11"> By thine understanding flieth a hawk? Spreadeth he his wings to the south?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Obadiah 1:4; Jeremiah 49:16; Exodus 19:4"> At thy command goeth an eagle up high? Or lifteth he up his nest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:4"> A rock he doth inhabit, Yea, he lodgeth on the tooth of a rock, and fortress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 9:26"> From thence he hath sought food, To a far off place his eyes look attentively,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 24:28; Luke 17:37; Ezekiel 39:17–19"> And his brood gulp up blood, And where the pierced &#91;are&#93; — there &#91;is&#93; he!</span>
