<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Elihu answereth and saith: —</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 12:36–37; Job 19:6–7; Job 16:17"> This hast thou reckoned for judgment: Thou hast said — 'My righteousness &#91;is&#93; more than God's?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 34:9; Malachi 3:14; Job 9:30–31"> For thou sayest, 'What doth it profit Thee! What do I profit from my sin?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; Job 34:8"> I return thee words, and thy friends with thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 22:12; Isaiah 55:9; Nahum 1:3"> Behold attentively the heavens — and see, And behold the clouds, They have been higher than thou.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:19; Proverbs 8:36; Proverbs 9:12"> If thou hast sinned, what dost thou against Him? And thy transgressions have been multiplied, What dost thou to Him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 22:2–3; Psalms 16:2; Luke 17:10"> If thou hast been righteous, What dost thou give to Him? Or what from thy hand doth He receive?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:30; Job 42:8; Acts 27:24"> For a man like thyself &#91;is&#93; thy wickedness, And for a son of man thy righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 2:23; Job 34:28; Exodus 3:7"> Because of the multitude of oppressions They cause to cry out, They cry because of the arm of the mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 42:8; Acts 16:25; Psalms 149:5"> And none said, 'Where &#91;is&#93; God my maker? Giving songs in the night,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 94:12; Job 36:22; Job 32:8"> Teaching us more than the beasts of the earth, Yea, than the fowl of the heavens He maketh us wiser.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 1:28; John 9:31; Psalms 123:3–4"> There they cry, and He doth not answer, Because of the pride of evil doers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 15:29; Isaiah 1:15; James 4:3"> Surely vanity God doth not hear, And the Mighty doth not behold it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 9:11; Micah 7:7–9; Psalms 37:5–6"> Yea, though thou sayest thou dost not behold Him, Judgment &#91;is&#93; before Him, and stay for Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 89:32; Job 9:14; Job 13:15"> And, now, because there is not, He hath appointed His anger, And He hath not known in great extremity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 38:2; Job 33:8–12; Job 34:35–37"> And Job &#91;with&#93; vanity doth open his mouth, Without knowledge words he multiplieth.</span>
