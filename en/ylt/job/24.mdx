<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 1:7; Ecclesiastes 3:17; 2 Peter 3:7–8"> Wherefore from the Mighty One Times have not been hidden, And those knowing Him have not seen His days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:14; Deuteronomy 27:17; Proverbs 23:10"> The borders they reach, A drove they have taken violently away, Yea, they do evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:6; Deuteronomy 24:10–13; 1 Samuel 12:3"> The ass of the fatherless they lead away, They take in pledge the ox of the widow,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 28:28; Job 24:14; Ezekiel 22:29"> They turn aside the needy from the way, Together have hid the poor of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 2:1; Job 39:5–7; Psalms 104:23"> Lo, wild asses in a wilderness, They have gone out about their work, Seeking early for prey, A mixture for himself — food for young ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Micah 6:15; Deuteronomy 28:51; Deuteronomy 28:33"> In a field his provender they reap, And the vineyard of the wicked they glean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 22:26–27; Job 22:6; Deuteronomy 24:11–13"> The naked they cause to lodge Without clothing. And there is no covering in the cold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 4:5; Song of Solomon 5:2; Hebrews 11:38"> From the inundation of hills they are wet, And without a refuge — have embraced a rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:5; 2 Kings 4:1"> They take violently away From the breast the orphan, And on the poor they lay a pledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 2:7–8; Deuteronomy 24:19; Amos 5:11–12"> Naked, they have gone without clothing, And hungry — have taken away a sheaf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="James 5:4; Jeremiah 22:13; Deuteronomy 25:4"> Between their walls they make oil, Wine-presses they have trodden, and thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:1; Malachi 2:17; Romans 2:4–5"> Because of enmity men do groan, And the soul of pierced ones doth cry, And God doth not give praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 3:19–20; Proverbs 4:19; Jude 1:6"> They have been among rebellious ones of light, They have not discerned His ways, Nor abode in His paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Micah 2:1–2; Psalms 10:8–10; Revelation 3:3"> At the light doth the murderer rise, He doth slay the poor and needy, And in the night he is as a thief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 10:11; Proverbs 7:9–10; Psalms 94:7"> And the eye of an adulterer Hath observed the twilight, Saying, 'No eye doth behold me.' And he putteth the face in secret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 3:20; Ephesians 5:11–13; Matthew 24:43"> He hath dug in the darkness — houses; By day they shut themselves up, They have not known light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 3:5; Jeremiah 2:26; Psalms 73:18–19"> When together, morning &#91;is&#93; to them death shade, When he discerneth the terrors of death shade.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 69:22; Psalms 58:7; Job 9:26"> Light he &#91;is&#93; on the face of the waters, Vilified is their portion in the earth, He turneth not the way of vineyards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 49:14; Luke 16:22; Proverbs 14:32"> Drought — also heat — consume snow-waters, Sheol &#91;those who&#93; have sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; Daniel 4:14; Job 18:16–17"> Forget him doth the womb, Sweeten &#91;on&#93; him doth the worm, No more is he remembered, And broken as a tree is wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:6–7; Job 22:9; Job 29:13"> Treating evil the barren &#91;who&#93; beareth not, And &#91;to&#93; the widow he doth no good,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 6:4–9; Esther 3:8–10; John 19:12–16"> And hath drawn the mighty by his power, He riseth, and none believeth in life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 15:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Revelation 2:23"> He giveth to him confidence, and he is supported, And his eyes &#91;are&#93; on their ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 37:10; Psalms 37:35–36; James 5:1–3"> High they were &#91;for&#93; a little, and they are not, And they have been brought low. As all &#91;others&#93; they are shut up, And as the head of an ear of corn cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 9:24; Job 11:2–3; Job 6:28"> And if not now, who doth prove me a liar, And doth make of nothing my word?</span>
