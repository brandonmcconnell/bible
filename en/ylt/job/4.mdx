<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 8:1; Job 22:1; Job 15:1"> And Eliphaz the Temanite answereth and saith: —</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 32:18–20; Acts 4:20; Jeremiah 6:11"> Hath one tried a word with thee? — Thou art weary! And to keep in words who is able?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3; Hebrews 12:12; Proverbs 10:21"> Lo, thou hast instructed many, And feeble hands thou makest strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 12:18; Psalms 145:14; Hebrews 12:12"> The stumbling one do thy words raise up, And bowing knees thou dost strengthen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 19:21; 2 Corinthians 4:16; Job 2:5"> But now, it cometh in unto thee, And thou art weary; It striketh unto thee, and thou art troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 3:26; Proverbs 14:26; Job 1:1"> Is not thy reverence thy confidence? Thy hope — the perfection of thy ways?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 37:25; 2 Peter 2:9; Job 36:7"> Remember, I pray thee, Who, being innocent, hath perished? And where have the upright been cut off?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 6:7–8; Proverbs 22:8; Hosea 8:7"> As I have seen — ploughers of iniquity, And sowers of misery, reap it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 15:30; Isaiah 11:4; Isaiah 30:33"> From the breath of God they perish, And from the spirit of His anger consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 58:6; Proverbs 30:14; Psalms 3:7"> The roaring of a lion, And the voice of a fierce lion, And teeth of young lions have been broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 34:10; Job 8:3–4; Job 1:19"> An old lion is perishing without prey, And the whelps of the lioness do separate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 26:14; Psalms 62:11; 1 Corinthians 13:12"> And unto me a thing is secretly brought, And receive doth mine ear a little of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 33:14–16; Genesis 2:21; Genesis 31:24"> In thoughts from visions of the night, In the falling of deep sleep on men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Job 33:19; Luke 1:12"> Fear hath met me, and trembling, And the multitude of my bones caused to fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 1:7; Daniel 5:6; Psalms 104:4"> And a spirit before my face doth pass, Stand up doth the hair of my flesh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 19:12"> It standeth, and I discern not its aspect, A similitude &#91;is&#93; over-against mine eyes, Silence! and a voice I hear:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 9:2; Job 25:4; Ecclesiastes 7:20"> 'Is mortal man than God more righteous? Than his Maker is a man cleaner?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 25:5–6; 2 Peter 2:4; Jude 1:6"> Lo, in His servants He putteth no credence, Nor in His messengers setteth praise.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 10:9; Genesis 2:7; Genesis 3:19"> Also — the inhabitants of houses of clay, (Whose foundation &#91;is&#93; in the dust, They bruise them before a moth.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 20:7; Job 14:14; Job 16:22"> From morning to evening are beaten down, Without any regarding, for ever they perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 36:12; Psalms 39:11; Job 8:22"> Hath not their excellency been removed with them? They die, and not in wisdom!</span>
