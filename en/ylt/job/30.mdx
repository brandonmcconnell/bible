<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 12:4; Psalms 35:15–16; Psalms 69:12"> And now, laughed at me, Have the younger in days than I, Whose fathers I have loathed to set With the dogs of my flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Also — the power of their hands, why &#91;is it&#93; to me? On them hath old age perished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 24:5; Hebrews 11:38; Job 24:13–16"> With want and with famine gloomy, Those fleeing to a dry place, Formerly a desolation and waste,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 15:16; 2 Kings 4:38–39; Amos 7:14"> Those cropping mallows near a shrub, And broom-roots &#91;is&#93; their food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 4:25; Daniel 4:32–33; Psalms 109:10"> From the midst they are cast out, (They shout against them as a thief),</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:1–2; Judges 6:2; Isaiah 2:19"> In a frightful place of valleys to dwell, Holes of earth and clefts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 6:5; Job 11:12; Genesis 16:12"> Among shrubs they do groan, Under nettles they are gathered together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 16:22; Job 40:4; Isaiah 32:6"> Sons of folly — even sons without name, They have been smitten from the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Lamentations 3:63; Job 17:6; Lamentations 3:14"> And now, their song I have been, And I am to them for a byword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 26:67; Isaiah 50:6; Numbers 12:14"> They have abominated me, They have kept far from me, And from before me have not spared to spit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 12:21; Ruth 1:21; Job 12:18"> Because His cord He loosed and afflicteth me, And the bridle from before me, They have cast away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 19:12; Isaiah 3:5; Job 19:18"> On the right hand doth a brood arise, My feet they have cast away, And they raise up against me, Their paths of calamity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 3:12; Zechariah 1:15; Psalms 69:26"> They have broken down my path, By my calamity they profit, 'He hath no helper.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:16; Psalms 18:4; Psalms 69:14–15"> As a wide breach they come, Under the desolation have rolled themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 13:3; Job 6:4; Isaiah 44:22"> He hath turned against me terrors, It pursueth as the wind mine abundance, And as a thick cloud, Hath my safety passed away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 42:4; Psalms 22:14; Isaiah 53:12"> And now, in me my soul poureth itself out, Seize me do days of affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 7:4; Job 33:19–21; Isaiah 38:13"> At night my bone hath been pierced in me, And mine eyelids do not lie down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 7:5; Job 19:20; Job 2:7"> By the abundance of power, Is my clothing changed, As the mouth of my coat it doth gird me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 9:31; Job 42:6; Genesis 18:27"> Casting me into mire, And I am become like dust and ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 19:7; Psalms 80:4–5; Psalms 22:2"> I cry unto Thee, And Thou dost not answer me, I have stood, and Thou dost consider me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 10:14–17; Job 16:9–14; Job 19:6–9"> Thou art turned to be fierce to me, With the strength of Thy hand, Thou oppressest me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 27:21; Psalms 1:4; Hosea 4:19"> Thou dost lift me up, On the wind Thou dost cause me to ride, And Thou meltest — Thou levellest me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:5–7; Hebrews 9:27; Ecclesiastes 9:5"> For I have known To death Thou dost bring me back, And &#91;to&#93; the house appointed for all living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Judges 5:31; Matthew 27:39–44; Job 19:7"> Surely not against the heap Doth He send forth the hand, Though in its ruin they have safety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Psalms 35:13–14; Jeremiah 13:17"> Did not I weep for him whose day is hard? Grieved hath my soul for the needy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:15; Job 3:25–26; Jeremiah 14:19"> When good I expected, then cometh evil, And I wait for light, and darkness cometh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Lamentations 2:11; Jeremiah 31:20; Jeremiah 4:19"> My bowels have boiled, and have not ceased, Gone before me have days of affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 42:9; Psalms 38:6; Psalms 43:2"> Mourning I have gone without the sun, I have risen, in an assembly I cry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Micah 1:8; Psalms 102:6; Psalms 44:19"> A brother I have been to dragons, And a companion to daughters of the ostrich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Lamentations 4:8; Psalms 102:3; Psalms 119:83"> My skin hath been black upon me, And my bone hath burned from heat,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Lamentations 5:15; Isaiah 21:4; Isaiah 22:12"> And my harp doth become mourning, And my organ the sound of weeping.</span>
