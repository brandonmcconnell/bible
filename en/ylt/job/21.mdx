<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Job answereth and saith: —</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 2:1; Job 34:2; Job 15:11"> Hear ye diligently my word, And this is your consolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 17:2; Job 16:10; Job 16:20"> Bear with me, and I speak, And after my speaking — ye may deride.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 102:1; Psalms 42:11; Psalms 142:2–3"> I — to man &#91;is&#93; my complaint? and if &#91;so&#93;, wherefore May not my temper become short?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 29:9; Job 40:4; Judges 18:19"> Turn unto me, and be astonished, And put hand to mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:16; Psalms 119:120; Psalms 88:15"> Yea, if I have remembered, then I have been troubled. And my flesh hath taken fright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 73:3–12; Job 12:6; Jeremiah 12:1–3"> Wherefore do the wicked live? They have become old, Yea, they have been mighty in wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 17:6; Job 5:3–4; Job 20:10"> Their seed is established, Before their face with them, And their offspring before their eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 9:34; Psalms 73:5; Psalms 73:19"> Their houses &#91;are&#93; peace without fear, Nor &#91;is&#93; a rod of God upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 23:26; Luke 16:19; Deuteronomy 7:13–14"> His bullock hath eaten corn, and doth not loath. His cow bringeth forth safely, And doth not miscarry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 107:41; Psalms 127:3–5"> They send forth as a flock their sucklings, And their children skip,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 6:4–6; Isaiah 5:12; Genesis 4:21"> They lift &#91;themselves&#93; up at timbrel and harp, And rejoice at the sound of an organ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 36:11; Luke 12:19–20; Psalms 73:4"> They wear out in good their days, And in a moment &#91;to&#93; Sheol go down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:17; Romans 8:7; 2 Thessalonians 2:10–12"> And they say to God, 'Turn aside from us, And the knowledge of Thy ways We have not desired.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 5:2; Job 34:9; Malachi 1:13–14"> What &#91;is&#93; the Mighty One that we serve Him? And what do we profit when we meet with Him?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 1:1; Job 22:18; Genesis 49:6"> Lo, not in their hand &#91;is&#93; their good, (The counsel of the wicked Hath been far from me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 18:5–6; Proverbs 20:20; Proverbs 13:9"> How oft is the lamp of the wicked extinguished, And come on them doth their calamity? Pangs He apportioneth in His anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 1:4; Psalms 83:13; Job 13:25"> They are as straw before wind, And as chaff a hurricane hath stolen away,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; Ezekiel 18:19–20; Ezekiel 18:2"> God layeth up for his sons his sorrow, He giveth recompense unto him — and he knoweth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 14:10; Isaiah 51:17; Psalms 75:8"> His own eyes see his destruction, And of the wrath of the Mighty he drinketh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 14:5; Ecclesiastes 2:18–19; Psalms 55:23"> For what &#91;is&#93; his delight in his house after him, And the number of his months cut off?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 11:34; 1 Corinthians 2:16; Isaiah 40:13–14"> To God doth &#91;one&#93; teach knowledge, And He the high doth judge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 20:22–23; Luke 12:19–21; Psalms 73:4–5"> This &#91;one&#93; dieth in his perfect strength, Wholly at ease and quiet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 3:8; Psalms 17:10; Job 15:27"> His breasts have been full of milk, And marrow his bones doth moisten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:16–17; Job 7:11; Proverbs 14:10"> And this &#91;one&#93; dieth with a bitter soul, And have not eaten with gladness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:2; Isaiah 14:11; Job 20:11"> Together — on the dust they lie down, And the worm doth cover them over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 5:22; Psalms 119:86; Psalms 59:4"> Lo, I have known your thoughts, And the devices against me ye do wrongfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 8:22; Job 20:7; Habakkuk 2:9–11"> For ye say, 'Where &#91;is&#93; the house of the noble? And where the tent — The tabernacles of the wicked?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 129:8"> Have ye not asked those passing by the way? And their signs do ye not know?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 16:4; 2 Peter 2:9–17; 2 Peter 3:7"> That to a day of calamity is the wicked spared. To a day of wrath they are brought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Galatians 2:11; Deuteronomy 7:10; Romans 12:19"> Who doth declare to his face his way? And &#91;for&#93; that which he hath done, Who doth give recompence to him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 16:22; Psalms 49:14; Ezekiel 32:21–32"> And he — to the graves he is brought. And over the heap a watch is kept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 9:27; Job 24:24; Job 30:23"> Sweet to him have been the clods of the valley, And after him every man he draweth, And before him there is no numbering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 16:2; Job 32:3; Job 13:4"> And how do ye comfort me &#91;with&#93; vanity, And in your answers hath been left trespass?</span>
