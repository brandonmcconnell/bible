<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 18:17; 2 Corinthians 5:1; Isaiah 26:14"> Your remembrances &#91;are&#93; similes of ashes, For high places of clay your heights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 7:11; Job 21:3; Job 13:5"> Keep silent from me, and I speak, And pass over me doth what?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 12:3; Psalms 119:109; Ecclesiastes 4:5"> Wherefore do I take my flesh in my teeth? And my soul put in my hand?</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 21:2; Job 33:1; Job 13:6"> Hear ye diligently my word, And my declaration with your ears.</span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 139:12; Job 9:34–35; Revelation 6:15–16"> Only two things, O God, do with me: Then from Thy face I am not hidden.</span>
