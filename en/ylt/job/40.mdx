<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And Job answereth Jehovah, and saith: —</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 30:32; Job 29:9; Job 21:5"> Lo, I have been vile, What do I return to Thee? My hand I have placed on my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 33:14; Job 9:3; Job 9:15"> Once I have spoken, and I answer not, And twice, and I add not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 38:1; 2 Peter 3:10–12; Psalms 50:3–4"> And Jehovah answereth Job out of the whirlwind, and saith: —</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 38:3; Job 42:4; Job 23:3–4"> Gird, I pray thee, as a man, thy loins, I ask thee, and cause thou Me to know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:4; Isaiah 28:18; Job 32:2"> Dost thou also make void My judgment? Dost thou condemn Me, That thou mayest be righteous?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:13; Job 37:4–5; Isaiah 45:9"> And an arm like God hast thou? And with a voice like Him dost thou thunder?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 93:1; Psalms 149:4; Isaiah 59:17"> Put on, I pray thee, excellency and loftiness, Yea, honour and beauty put on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 4:37; Isaiah 2:11–12; Isaiah 2:17"> Scatter abroad the wrath of thine anger, And see every proud one, and make him low.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 63:3; Malachi 4:3; Psalms 60:12"> See every proud one — humble him, And tread down the wicked in their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 2:10; Esther 7:8; Job 14:13"> Hide them in the dust together, Their faces bind in secret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4–9; Romans 5:6; Psalms 44:6"> And even I — I do praise thee, For thy right hand giveth salvation to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 40:20; Psalms 104:14; Genesis 1:24–26"> Lo, I pray thee, Behemoth, that I made with thee: Grass as an ox he eateth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Lo, I pray thee, his power &#91;is&#93; in his loins, And his strength in the muscles of his belly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 41:23"> He doth bend his tail as a cedar, The sinews of his thighs are wrapped together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 48:4; Job 7:12"> His bones &#91;are&#93; tubes of brass, His bones &#91;are&#93; as a bar of iron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 41:33; Job 26:13; Isaiah 27:1"> He &#91;is&#93; a beginning of the ways of God, His Maker bringeth nigh his sword;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 104:26; Psalms 104:14; Job 40:15"> For food do mountains bear for him, And all the beasts of the field play there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 35:7; Isaiah 19:6–7"> Under shades he lieth down, In a secret place of reed and mire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:5; Leviticus 23:40; Isaiah 15:7"> Cover him do shades, &#91;with&#93; their shadow, Cover him do willows of the brook.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 13:10; Isaiah 37:25; Isaiah 28:16"> Lo, a flood oppresseth — he doth not haste, He is confident though Jordan Doth come forth unto his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 41:1–2"> Before his eyes doth &#91;one&#93; take him, With snares doth &#91;one&#93; pierce the nose?</span>
