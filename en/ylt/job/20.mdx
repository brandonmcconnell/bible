<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 42:9; Job 11:1"> And Zophar the Naamathite answereth and saith: —</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 14:29; Psalms 31:22; Job 20:3"> Therefore my thoughts cause me to answer, And because of my sensations in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 33:3; Job 20:2; Psalms 78:2–5"> The chastisement of my shame I hear, And the spirit of mine understanding Doth cause me to answer:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 9:1–3; Job 15:10; Job 32:7"> This hast thou known from antiquity? Since the placing of man on earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 37:35–36; Job 18:5–6; Acts 12:22–23"> That the singing of the wicked &#91;is&#93; short, And the joy of the profane for a moment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 14:13–14; Obadiah 1:3–4; Genesis 11:4"> Though his excellency go up to the heavens, And his head against a cloud he strike —</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 7:10; 1 Kings 14:10; Job 4:20"> As his own dung for ever he doth perish, His beholders say: 'Where &#91;is&#93; he?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 90:5; Psalms 73:20; Isaiah 29:7–8"> As a dream he fleeth, and they find him not, And he is driven away as a vision of the night,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 7:8; Job 7:10; Job 8:18"> The eye hath not seen him, and addeth not. And not again doth his place behold him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 20:18; Luke 19:8; Job 27:16–17"> His sons do the poor oppress, And his hands give back his wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:26; Psalms 25:7; Job 21:26"> His bones have been full of his youth, And with him on the dust it lieth down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 15:16; Psalms 10:7; Ecclesiastes 11:9"> Though he doth sweeten evil in his mouth, Doth hide it under his tongue,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 9:43–49; Romans 8:13; Matthew 5:29–30"> Hath pity on it, and doth not forsake it, And keep it back in the midst of his palate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 3:13; Psalms 51:8–9; Psalms 32:3–4"> His food in his bowels is turned, The bitterness of asps &#91;is&#93; in his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 23:8; Matthew 27:3–4"> Wealth he hath swallowed, and doth vomit it. From his belly God driveth it out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 28:3–6; Romans 3:13; Deuteronomy 32:24"> Gall of asps he sucketh, Slay him doth the tongue of a viper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13–14; Job 29:6; Luke 16:24"> He looketh not on rivulets, Flowing of brooks of honey and butter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 20:10; Job 20:15; Jeremiah 51:44"> He is giving back &#91;what&#93; he laboured for, And doth not consume &#91;it&#93;; As a bulwark &#91;is&#93; his exchange, and he exults not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 35:9; Proverbs 14:31; James 5:4"> For he oppressed — he forsook the poor, A house he hath taken violently away, And he doth not build it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:13–14; Isaiah 57:20–21"> For he hath not known ease in his belly. With his desirable thing he delivereth not himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:11; Job 15:29; Luke 16:24–25"> There is not a remnant to his food, Therefore his good doth not stay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 15:29; Revelation 18:7; 2 Kings 24:2"> In the fulness of his sufficiency he is straitened. Every perverse hand doth meet him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 78:30–31; Numbers 11:33; Psalms 11:6"> It cometh to pass, at the filling of his belly, He sendeth forth against him The fierceness of His anger, Yea, He raineth on him in his eating.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 24:18; Amos 5:19; 2 Samuel 22:35"> He fleeth from an iron weapon, Pass through him doth a bow of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 16:13; Job 18:11; Deuteronomy 32:41"> One hath drawn, And it cometh out from the body, And a glittering weapon from his gall proceedeth. On him &#91;are&#93; terrors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 21:9; Psalms 120:4; Isaiah 30:33"> All darkness is hid for his treasures, Consume him doth a fire not blown, Broken is the remnant in his tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 26:21; Luke 12:2–3; Job 16:18"> Reveal do the heavens his iniquity, And earth is raising itself against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 27:14–19; Revelation 18:17; James 5:1–3"> Remove doth the increase of his house, Poured forth in a day of His anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 27:13; Job 31:2–3; Psalms 11:5–6"> This &#91;is&#93; the portion of a wicked man from God. And an inheritance appointed him by God.</span>
