<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:14–15; Psalms 106:33; Psalms 39:2–3"> After this hath Job opened his mouth, and revileth his day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 18:14"> And Job answereth and saith: —</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:14–18; Jeremiah 15:10; Job 10:18–19"> Let the day perish in which I am born, And the night that hath said: 'A man-child hath been conceived.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 27:20; Exodus 10:22–23; Joel 2:2"> That day — let it be darkness, Let not God require it from above, Nor let light shine upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 10:21–22; Psalms 23:4; Job 28:3"> Let darkness and death-shade redeem it, Let a cloud tabernacle upon it, Let them terrify it as the most bitter of days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> That night — let thick darkness take it, Let it not be united to days of the year, Into the number of months let it not come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 24:8; Jeremiah 7:34; Revelation 18:22–23"> Lo! that night — let it be gloomy, Let no singing come into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 41:1; Job 41:10; Mark 5:38"> Let the cursers of day mark it, Who are ready to wake up Leviathan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 41:18; Jeremiah 13:16; Job 30:26"> Let the stars of its twilight be dark, Let it wait for light, and there is none, And let it not look on the eyelids of the dawn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:17; Ecclesiastes 6:3–5; Genesis 20:18"> Because it hath not shut the doors Of the womb that was mine! And hide misery from mine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 10:18–19; Isaiah 46:3; Psalms 71:6"> Why from the womb do I not die? From the belly I have come forth and gasp!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 66:12; Genesis 30:3; Genesis 50:23"> Wherefore have knees been before me? And what &#91;are&#93; breasts, that I suck?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:10; Job 21:13; Job 14:10–12"> For now, I have lain down, and am quiet, I have slept — then there is rest to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 15:28; Psalms 89:48; Psalms 49:14"> With kings and counsellors of earth, These building wastes for themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 10:27; Job 22:25; Zephaniah 1:18"> Or with princes — they have gold, They are filling their houses &#91;with&#93; silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 58:8; Ecclesiastes 6:3; 1 Corinthians 15:8"> (Or as a hidden abortion I am not, As infants — they have not seen light.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Job 17:16; Hebrews 4:11"> There the wicked have ceased troubling, And there rest do the wearied in power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 39:7; Isaiah 14:3–4; Exodus 5:6–8"> Together prisoners have been at ease, They have not heard the voice of an exactor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:8; Job 30:23; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> Small and great &#91;are&#93; there the same. And a servant &#91;is&#93; free from his lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:18; Job 7:15–16; 1 Samuel 1:10"> Why giveth He to the miserable light, and life to the bitter soul?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 9:6; Proverbs 2:4; 1 Kings 19:4"> Who are waiting for death, and it is not, And they seek it above hid treasures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Who are glad — unto joy, They rejoice when they find a grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 19:8; Lamentations 3:7; Isaiah 40:27"> To a man whose way hath been hidden, And whom God doth shut up?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 102:9; Psalms 42:3–4; Psalms 80:5"> For before my food, my sighing cometh, And poured out as waters &#91;are&#93; my roarings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 1:5; Job 30:15; Job 31:23"> For a fear I feared and it meeteth me, And what I was afraid of doth come to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 7:14; Job 27:9; Psalms 143:11"> I was not safe — nor was I quiet — Nor was I at rest — and trouble cometh!</span>
