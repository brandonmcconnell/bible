<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 27:1; Job 13:12; Numbers 23:7"> And Job addeth to lift up his simile, and saith: —</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 1:10; Job 1:1–5; Jeremiah 31:28"> Who doth make me as &#91;in&#93; months past, As &#91;in&#93; the days of God's preserving me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 18:28; John 8:12; Job 18:6"> In His causing His lamp to shine on my head, By His light I walk &#91;through&#93; darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 25:14; Proverbs 3:32; Psalms 27:5"> As I have been in days of my maturity, And the counsel of God upon my tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 128:3; Psalms 127:3–5; Deuteronomy 33:27–29"> When yet the Mighty One &#91;is&#93; with me. Round about me — my young ones,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 81:16; Job 20:17; Deuteronomy 32:13–14"> When washing my goings with butter, And the firm rock &#91;is&#93; with me rivulets of oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 8:16; Ruth 4:1–2; Deuteronomy 16:18"> When I go out to the gate by the city, In a broad place I prepare my seat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 19:32; Proverbs 16:31; Romans 13:7"> Seen me have youths, and they, been hidden, And the aged have risen — they stood up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 21:5; Job 40:4; Proverbs 10:19"> Princes have kept in words, And a hand they place on their mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 137:6; Ezekiel 3:26"> The voice of leaders hath been hidden, And their tongue to the palate hath cleaved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 4:22; Job 31:20; Proverbs 29:2"> For the ear heard, and declareth me happy, And the eye hath seen, and testifieth &#91;to&#93; me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 72:12; Proverbs 21:13; James 1:27"> For I deliver the afflicted who is crying, And the fatherless who hath no helper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philemon 1:7; Job 22:9; Job 31:19–20"> The blessing of the perishing cometh on me, And the heart of the widow I cause to sing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; Isaiah 59:17; Psalms 132:9"> Righteousness I have put on, and it clotheth me, As a robe and a diadem my justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 10:31; Matthew 11:5; 1 Corinthians 12:12–31"> Eyes I have been to the blind, And feet to the lame &#91;am&#93; I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 29:7; Psalms 68:5; Job 31:18"> A father I &#91;am&#93; to the needy, And the cause I have not known I search out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 3:7; Proverbs 30:14; 1 Samuel 17:35"> And I break the jaw-teeth of the perverse, And from his teeth I cast away prey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 30:6–7; Genesis 32:12; Job 42:16–17"> And I say, 'With my nest I expire, And as the sand I multiply days.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 1:3; Jeremiah 17:8; Hosea 14:5–7"> My root is open unto the waters, And dew doth lodge on my branch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 49:24; Isaiah 40:31; Psalms 18:34"> My honour &#91;is&#93; fresh with me, And my bow in my hand is renewed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 32:11–12; Job 29:9–10"> To me they have hearkened, Yea, they wait, and are silent for my counsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:2; Song of Solomon 4:11; Matthew 22:46"> After my word they change not, And on them doth my speech drop,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hosea 6:3; Psalms 72:6; Zechariah 10:1"> And they wait as &#91;for&#93; rain for me, And their mouth they have opened wide &#91;As&#93; for the latter rain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 89:15; Psalms 4:6; Psalms 126:1"> I laugh unto them — they give no credence, And the light of my face cause not to fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:3–4; 1 Thessalonians 3:2–3; Genesis 14:14–17"> I choose their way, and sit head, And I dwell as a king in a troop, When mourners he doth comfort.</span>
