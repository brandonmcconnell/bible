<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 8:1; Job 25:1"> And Bildad the Shuhite answereth and saith: —</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 13:5–6; Proverbs 18:13; Job 3:17"> When do ye set an end to words? Consider ye, and afterwards do we speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 73:22; Job 17:10; Job 17:4"> Wherefore have we been reckoned as cattle? We have been defiled in your eyes!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 16:9; Job 14:18; Job 13:14"> (He is tearing himself in his anger.) For thy sake is earth forsaken? And removed is a rock from its place?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 13:9; Proverbs 24:20; Proverbs 20:20"> Also, the light of the wicked is extinguished. And there doth not shine a spark of his fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 18:23; Job 21:17; Psalms 18:28"> The light hath been dark in his tent, And his lamp over him is extinguished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 4:12; Psalms 33:10; Psalms 18:36"> Straitened are the steps of his strength, And cast him down doth his own counsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 9:15; Job 22:10; Psalms 35:8"> For he is sent into a net by his own feet, And on a snare he doth walk habitually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14–15; Job 1:15; Job 1:17"> Seize on the heel doth a gin, Prevail over him do the designing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Romans 11:9; Psalms 11:6"> Hidden in the earth is his cord, And his trap on the path.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 15:21; Jeremiah 6:25; Leviticus 26:36"> Round about terrified him have terrors, And they have scattered him — at his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:36; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Psalms 34:10"> Hungry is his sorrow, And calamity is ready at his side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jonah 2:6; Zechariah 14:12; Revelation 6:8"> It consumeth the parts of his skin, Consume his parts doth death's first-born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 10:28; Psalms 55:4; Job 11:20"> Drawn from his tent is his confidence, And it causeth him to step to the king of terrors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 11:6; Deuteronomy 29:23; Isaiah 34:9–10"> It dwelleth in his tent — out of his provender, Scattered over his habitation is sulphur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 9:16; Isaiah 5:24; Job 15:30"> From beneath his roots are dried up, And from above cut off is his crop.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 34:16; Proverbs 10:7; Psalms 109:13"> His memorial hath perished from the land, And he hath no name on the street.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 20:8; Job 10:22; Job 3:20"> They thrust him from light unto darkness, And from the habitable earth cast him out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:30; Isaiah 14:21–22; Job 20:26–28"> He hath no continuator, Nor successor among his people, And none is remaining in his dwellings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 37:13; Jeremiah 50:27; Ezekiel 21:25"> At this day westerns have been astonished And easterns have taken fright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Titus 1:16; Jeremiah 9:3; Jeremiah 10:25"> Only these &#91;are&#93; tabernacles of the perverse, And this the place God hath not known.</span>
