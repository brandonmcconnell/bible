<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Job answereth and saith: —</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 10:1; Psalms 32:4; Job 7:11"> Also — to-day &#91;is&#93; my complaint bitter, My hand hath been heavy because of my sighing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 16:21; Jeremiah 14:7; Job 13:3"> O that I had known — and I find Him, I come in unto His seat,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 13:18; Daniel 9:18–19; Exodus 32:12–13"> I arrange before Him the cause, And my mouth fill &#91;with&#93; arguments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 13:22–23; Job 10:2; 1 Corinthians 4:3–4"> I know the words He doth answer me, And understand what He saith to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 13:21; Job 9:33–34; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> In the abundance of power doth He strive with me? No! surely He putteth &#91;it&#93; in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 13:3; Romans 8:1; Isaiah 1:18"> There the upright doth reason with Him, And I escape for ever from my judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 10:1; Job 9:11; Job 35:14"> Lo, forward I go — and He is not, And backward — and I perceive him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:46; Isaiah 8:17"> &#91;To&#93; the left in His working — and I see not, He is covered &#91;on&#93; the right, and I behold not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 1:12; James 1:2–4; 1 Peter 1:7"> For He hath known the way with me, He hath tried me — as gold I go forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 44:18; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Job 17:9"> On His step hath my foot laid hold, His way I have kept, and turn not aside,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:16; Psalms 119:103; Psalms 119:11"> The command of His lips, and I depart not. Above my allotted portion I have laid up The sayings of His mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 115:3; Proverbs 19:21; Isaiah 46:10"> And He &#91;is&#93; in one &#91;mind&#93;, And who doth turn Him back? And His soul hath desired — and He doth &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:9; Romans 11:33"> For He doth complete my portion, And many such things &#91;are&#93; with Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; Psalms 77:3; Job 31:23"> Therefore, from His presence I am troubled, I consider, and am afraid of Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 22:14; Job 27:2; Psalms 88:16"> And God hath made my heart soft, And the Mighty hath troubled me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 19:8; Isaiah 57:1; 2 Kings 22:20"> For I have not been cut off before darkness, And before me He covered thick darkness.</span>
