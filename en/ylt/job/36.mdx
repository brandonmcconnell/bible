<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Elihu addeth and saith: —</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 21:3; 2 Corinthians 5:20; Job 13:7–8"> Honour me a little, and I shew thee, That yet for God &#91;are&#93; words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 3:17; James 1:17; Deuteronomy 32:4"> I lift up my knowledge from afar, And to my Maker I ascribe righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 8:7–8; Job 37:16; Psalms 49:3"> For, truly, my words &#91;are&#93; not false, The perfect in knowledge &#91;is&#93; with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 22:24; Psalms 138:6; Jeremiah 10:12"> Lo, God &#91;is&#93; mighty, and despiseth not, Mighty &#91;in&#93; power &#91;and&#93; heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 72:12–14; Psalms 140:12; 2 Peter 2:9"> He reviveth not the wicked, And the judgment of the poor appointeth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 34:15; Psalms 33:18; 1 Samuel 2:8"> He withdraweth not from the righteous His eyes, And &#91;from&#93; kings on the throne, And causeth them to sit for ever, and they are high,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 5:22; Psalms 107:10; Lamentations 3:9"> And if prisoners in fetters They are captured with cords of affliction,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 119:71; Job 15:25; 1 Corinthians 11:32"> Then He declareth to them their work, And their transgressions, Because they have become mighty,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 3:8; Job 36:15; Isaiah 55:6–7"> And He uncovereth their ear for instruction, And saith that they turn back from iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:23; Isaiah 1:19; Jeremiah 26:13"> If they do hear and serve, They complete their days in good, And their years in pleasantness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 4:21; Romans 2:8–9; Isaiah 3:11"> And if they do not hearken, By the dart they pass away, And expire without knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 2:5; Job 36:8; Job 27:8–10"> And the profane in heart set the face, They cry not when He hath bound them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:16; Job 15:32; Deuteronomy 23:17"> Their soul dieth in youth, And their life among the defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 36:10; 2 Chronicles 12:8"> He draweth out the afflicted in his affliction, And uncovereth in oppression their ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 23:5; Psalms 118:5; Psalms 18:19"> And also He moved thee from a strait place, &#91;To&#93; a broad place — no straitness under it, And the sitting beyond of thy table Hath been full of fatness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 18:4; Romans 1:32; Job 34:36"> And the judgment of the wicked thou hast fulfilled, Judgment and justice are upheld — because of fury,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 33:24; Ephesians 5:6; Romans 1:18"> Lest He move thee with a stroke, And the abundance of an atonement turn thee not aside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 11:4; Proverbs 11:21; James 5:3"> Doth He value thy riches? He hath gold, and all the forces of power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 19:35; Job 34:20; Exodus 12:29"> Desire not the night, For the going up of peoples in their stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 11:25; Psalms 66:18; 1 Peter 3:17"> Take heed — do not turn unto iniquity, For on this thou hast fixed Rather than &#91;on&#93; affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:7–8; Luke 1:52; Romans 13:1"> Lo, God doth sit on high by His power, Who &#91;is&#93; like Him — a teacher?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:16; Isaiah 40:13–14; Job 8:3"> Who hath appointed unto Him his way? And who said, 'Thou hast done iniquity?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 15:3; Psalms 111:2–4; Psalms 72:18"> Remember that thou magnify His work That men have beheld.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> All men have looked on it, Man looketh attentively from afar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 90:2; Psalms 145:3; Job 11:7–9"> Lo, God &#91;is&#93; high, And we know not the number of His years, Yea, there &#91;is&#93; no searching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 147:8; Psalms 148:8; Psalms 65:9–13"> When He doth diminish droppings of the waters, They refine rain according to its vapour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 3:20; Genesis 7:11–12; Job 37:11–13"> Which clouds do drop, They distil on man abundantly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 37:16; Job 37:2–5; Psalms 18:13"> Yea, doth &#91;any&#93; understand The spreadings out of a cloud? The noises of His tabernacle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 38:8–11; Job 38:34–35; Genesis 1:9"> Lo, He hath spread over it His light, And the roots of the sea He hath covered,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 136:25; Acts 14:17; Job 37:13"> For by them He doth judge peoples, He giveth food in abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 135:7; Psalms 147:8–9; Psalms 148:8"> By two palms He hath covered the light, And layeth a charge over it in meeting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 37:2; Job 36:29; 2 Samuel 22:14"> He sheweth by it &#91;to&#93; his friend substance, Anger against perversity.</span>
