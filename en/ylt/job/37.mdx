<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 38:13; Matthew 24:27; Isaiah 11:12"> Under the whole heavens He directeth it, And its light &#91;is&#93; over the skirts of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 29:3–9; Psalms 68:33; Exodus 15:7–8"> After it roar doth a voice — He thundereth with the voice of His excellency, And He doth not hold them back, When His voice is heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 5:9; Romans 11:33; Isaiah 40:28"> God thundereth with His voice wonderfully, Doing great things and we know not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 38:22; Job 36:27; Ezekiel 13:13"> For to snow He saith, 'Be &#91;on&#93; the earth.' And the small rain and great rain of His power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 111:2; Psalms 109:27; Isaiah 26:11"> Into the hand of every man he sealeth, For the knowledge by all men of His work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 104:22; Job 38:40"> And enter doth the beast into covert, And in its habitations it doth continue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 9:9; Psalms 104:3; Isaiah 21:1"> From the inner chamber cometh a hurricane, And from scatterings winds — cold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 38:29–30; Psalms 147:16–18; Psalms 78:47"> From the breath of God is frost given, And the breadth of waters is straitened,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 36:27–30; Matthew 17:5; Isaiah 18:4"> Yea, by filling He doth press out a cloud, Scatter a cloud doth His light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 148:8; Joel 2:23; James 5:17–18"> And it is turning itself round by His counsels, For their doing all He commandeth them, On the face of the habitable earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 18:45; Job 38:26–27; 1 Samuel 12:18–19"> Whether for a rod, or for His land, Or for kindness — He doth cause it to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 111:2; Psalms 145:10–12; Exodus 14:13"> Hear this, O Job, Stand and consider the wonders of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 36:30–32; Isaiah 40:26; Job 37:11"> Dost thou know when God doth place them, And caused to shine the light of His cloud?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 36:4; Proverbs 3:19–20; Isaiah 40:22"> Dost thou know the balancings of a cloud? The wonders of the Perfect in knowledge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 147:18; Job 6:17; Job 38:31"> How thy garments &#91;are&#93; warm, In the quieting of the earth from the south?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 44:24; Psalms 104:2; Isaiah 40:22"> Thou hast made an expanse with Him For the clouds — strong as a hard mirror!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 38:2; Psalms 73:22; Job 42:3"> Let us know what we say to Him, We set not in array because of darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 11:7–8; Psalms 139:4; Job 6:3"> Is it declared to Him that I speak? If a man hath spoken, surely he is swallowed up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 36:32; Job 26:9; Job 38:25"> And now, they have not seen the light, Bright it &#91;is&#93; in the clouds, And the wind hath passed by and cleanseth them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 104:1; Jude 1:25; Hebrews 12:29"> From the golden north it cometh, Beside God &#91;is&#93; fearful honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:16; Romans 11:33; Psalms 99:4"> The Mighty! we have not found Him out, High in power and judgment, He doth not answer! And abundant in righteousness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:28; 1 Corinthians 1:26; Psalms 130:4"> Therefore do men fear Him, He seeth not any of the wise of heart.</span>
