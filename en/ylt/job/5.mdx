<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 15:15; Job 15:8–10; Job 4:18"> Pray, call, is there any to answer thee? And unto which of the holy ones dost thou turn?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:9; Hosea 7:11; Genesis 30:1"> For provocation slayeth the perverse, And envy putteth to death the simple,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 73:18–20; Psalms 37:35–36; Psalms 92:7"> I — I have seen the perverse taking root, And I mark his habitation straightway,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 127:5; Job 18:16–19; Psalms 119:155"> Far are his sons from safety, And they are bruised in the gate, And there is no deliverer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Lamentations 2:16; Hosea 8:7; Jeremiah 51:44"> Whose harvest the hungry doth eat, And even from the thorns taketh it, And the designing swallowed their wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Lamentations 3:38; 1 Samuel 6:9"> For sorrow cometh not forth from the dust, Nor from the ground springeth up misery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 14:1; Genesis 3:17–19; Ecclesiastes 5:15–17"> For man to misery is born, And the sparks go high to fly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Job 22:21; Psalms 37:5"> Yet I — I inquire for God, And for God I give my word,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 40:5; Job 9:10; Psalms 86:10"> Doing great things, and there is no searching. Wonderful, till there is no numbering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:24; Acts 14:17; Psalms 147:8"> Who is giving rain on the face of the land, And is sending waters on the out-places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 113:7; 1 Samuel 2:7–8; Luke 6:21"> To set the low on a high place, And the mourners have been high &#91;in&#93; safety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 33:10–11; Psalms 21:11; Isaiah 8:10"> Making void thoughts of the subtile, And their hands do not execute wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:19; Psalms 9:15–16; 1 Corinthians 1:19–20"> Capturing the wise in their subtilty, And the counsel of wrestling ones was hastened,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 12:25; Isaiah 59:10; Deuteronomy 28:29"> By day they meet darkness, And as night — they grope at noon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 35:10; Psalms 140:12; Psalms 109:31"> And He saveth the wasted from their mouth, And from a strong hand the needy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 107:42; 1 Samuel 2:8–9; Psalms 63:11"> And there is hope to the poor, And perverseness hath shut her mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 1:12; Psalms 94:12; Proverbs 3:11–12"> Lo, the happiness of mortal man, God doth reprove him: And the chastisement of the Mighty despise not,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hosea 6:1; Deuteronomy 32:39; Isaiah 30:26"> For He doth pain, and He bindeth up, He smiteth, and His hands heal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 34:19; 1 Corinthians 10:13; Proverbs 24:16"> In six distresses He delivereth thee, And in seven evil striketh not on thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 33:19; Hosea 13:14; Psalms 37:19"> In famine He hath redeemed thee from death, And in battle from the hands of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 31:20; Psalms 91:5–7; Jeremiah 18:18"> When the tongue scourgeth thou art hid, And thou art not afraid of destruction, When it cometh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:25; Isaiah 35:9; Psalms 91:13"> At destruction and at hunger thou mockest, And of the beast of the earth, Thou art not afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 26:6; Hosea 2:18; Daniel 6:22"> (For with sons of the field &#91;is&#93; thy covenant, And the beast of the field Hath been at peace with thee.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 121:7–8; Psalms 91:10; Isaiah 4:5–6"> And thou hast known that thy tent &#91;is&#93; peace, And inspected thy habitation, and errest not,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 112:2; Psalms 72:16; Deuteronomy 28:4"> And hast known that numerous &#91;is&#93; Thy seed, And thine offspring as the herb of the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 42:16–17; Proverbs 9:11; Psalms 91:16"> Thou comest in full age unto the grave, As the going up of a stalk in its season.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 111:2; Job 32:11–12; Proverbs 9:12"> Lo, this — we searched it out — it &#91;is&#93; right, hearken; And thou, know for thyself!</span>
