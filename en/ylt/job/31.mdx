<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="James 1:14–15; 1 John 2:16; Matthew 5:28–29"> A covenant I made for mine eyes, And what — do I attend to a virgin?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 20:29; Job 27:13; Hebrews 13:4"> And what &#91;is&#93; the portion of God from above? And the inheritance of the Mighty from the heights?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 21:30; Job 34:22; Matthew 7:13"> Is not calamity to the perverse? And strangeness to workers of iniquity?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 34:21; Proverbs 5:21; 2 Chronicles 16:9"> Doth not He see my ways, And all my steps number?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Psalms 44:20–21; Psalms 12:2"> If I have walked with vanity, And my foot doth hasten to deceit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 7:8–9; Daniel 5:27; Isaiah 26:7"> He doth weigh me in righteous balances, And God doth know my integrity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 15:39; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Matthew 5:29"> If my step doth turn aside from the way, And after mine eyes hath my heart gone, And to my hands cleaved hath blemish,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Micah 6:15; Leviticus 26:16; Deuteronomy 28:38"> Let me sow — and another eat, And my products let be rooted out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 11:4; Ecclesiastes 7:26; Judges 16:5"> If my heart hath been enticed by woman, And by the opening of my neighbour I laid wait,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:10; 2 Samuel 12:11; Isaiah 47:2"> Grind to another let my wife, And over her let others bend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 20:10; Deuteronomy 22:22–24; Job 31:28"> For it &#91;is&#93; a wicked thing, and a judicial iniquity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 6:27; Jeremiah 5:7–9; Job 15:30"> For a fire it &#91;is&#93;, to destruction it consumeth, And among all mine increase doth take root,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:14–15; Exodus 21:26–27; Leviticus 25:46"> If I despise the cause of my man-servant, And of my handmaid, In their contending with me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 2:13; Psalms 9:12; Zechariah 2:13"> Then what do I do when God ariseth? And when He doth inspect, What do I answer Him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 14:31; Malachi 2:10; Job 34:19"> Did not He that made me in the womb make him? Yea, prepare us in the womb doth One.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 38:14; Deuteronomy 15:7–10; Deuteronomy 28:32"> If I withhold from pleasure the poor, And the eyes of the widow do consume,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 29:12–16; John 13:29; Job 22:7"> And I do eat my morsel by myself, And the orphan hath not eat of it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> (But from my youth He grew up with me as &#91;with&#93; a father, And from the belly of my mother I am led.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 22:6; Luke 3:11; Job 24:4"> If I see &#91;any&#93; perishing without clothing, And there is no covering to the needy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:13; Job 29:11"> If his loins have not blessed me, And from the fleece of my sheep He doth not warm himself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 22:9; Job 29:12; Micah 7:3"> If I have waved at the fatherless my hand, When I see in &#91;him&#93; the gate of my court,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 137:6; Job 38:15; Job 31:40"> My shoulder from its blade let fall, And mine arm from the bone be broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 13:11; Psalms 119:120; 2 Corinthians 5:11"> For a dread unto me &#91;is&#93; calamity &#91;from&#93; God, And because of His excellency I am not able.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 49:6–7; Deuteronomy 8:12–14; Proverbs 11:28"> If I have made gold my confidence, And to the pure gold have said, 'My trust,'</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 62:10; Habakkuk 1:16; Isaiah 10:13–14"> If I rejoice because great &#91;is&#93; my wealth, And because abundance hath my hand found,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:16; Deuteronomy 4:19; Deuteronomy 17:3"> If I see the light when it shineth, And the precious moon walking,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:6; Deuteronomy 11:16; Psalms 2:12"> And my heart is enticed in secret, And my hand doth kiss my mouth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:2–7; Job 31:11; Psalms 50:6"> It also &#91;is&#93; a judicial iniquity, For I had lied to God above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 24:17–18; Proverbs 17:5; Psalms 35:13–14"> If I rejoice at the ruin of my hater, And stirred up myself when evil found him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Romans 12:14; Exodus 23:4–5"> Yea, I have not suffered my mouth to sin, To ask with an oath his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 1:18; Proverbs 1:11–12; Micah 3:2–3"> If not — say ye, O men of my tent, 'O that we had of his flesh, we are not satisfied.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 13:2; Genesis 19:2–3; Judges 19:20–21"> In the street doth not lodge a stranger, My doors to the traveller I open.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; Hosea 6:7; Acts 5:8"> If I have covered as Adam my transgressions, To hide in my bosom mine iniquity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 23:2; Proverbs 29:25; Esther 4:11"> Because I fear a great multitude, And the contempt of families doth affright me, Then I am silent, I go not out of the opening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 13:21–22; Job 40:4–5; Job 17:3"> Who giveth to me a hearing? lo, my mark. The Mighty One doth answer me, And a bill hath mine adversary written.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 22:22; Job 29:14; Isaiah 62:3"> If not — on my shoulder I take it up, I bind it a crown on myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Job 13:15; Ephesians 3:12; Hebrews 4:15–16"> The number of my steps I tell Him, As a leader I approach Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:11; James 5:4; Psalms 65:13"> If against me my land doth cry out, And together its furrows weep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 21:19; James 5:4; Isaiah 26:21"> If its strength I consumed without money, And the life of its possessors, I have caused to breathe out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Genesis 3:17–18; Malachi 1:3; Psalms 72:20"> Instead of wheat let a thorn go forth, And instead of barley a useless weed! The words of Job are finished.</span>
