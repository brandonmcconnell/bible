<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 5:7; Ecclesiastes 2:23; Job 25:4"> Man, born of woman! Of few days, and full of trouble!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 40:6–8; 1 Peter 1:24; Job 8:9"> As a flower he hath gone forth, and is cut off, And he fleeth as a shadow and standeth not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 144:3; Psalms 143:2; Psalms 8:4"> Also — on this Thou hast opened Thine eyes, And dost bring me into judgment with Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 3:6; Job 15:14; Ephesians 2:3"> Who giveth a clean thing out of an unclean? not one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 39:4; Acts 17:26; Hebrews 9:27"> If determined are his days, The number of his months &#91;are&#93; with Thee, His limit Thou hast made, And he passeth not over;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 7:19; Psalms 39:13; Job 7:1–2"> Look away from off him that he may cease, Till he enjoy as an hireling his day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 27:6; Isaiah 11:1; Job 14:14"> For there is of a tree hope, if it be cut down, That again it doth change, That its tender branch doth not cease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:36; Isaiah 26:19; John 12:24"> If its root becometh old in the earth, And its stem doth die in the dust,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 11:17–24; Ezekiel 17:3–10; Ezekiel 17:22–24"> From the fragrance of water it doth flourish, And hath made a crop as a plant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 19:26; Job 7:7–10; Job 14:12"> And a man dieth, and becometh weak, And man expireth, and where &#91;is&#93; he?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 19:5; Jeremiah 15:18; Job 6:15–18"> Waters have gone away from a sea, And a river becometh waste and dry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 10:21–22; Job 3:13; Ecclesiastes 3:19–21"> And man hath lain down, and riseth not, Till the wearing out of the heavens they awake not, Nor are roused from their sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 13:32; Isaiah 57:1–2; Isaiah 26:20–21"> O that in Sheol Thou wouldest conceal me, Hide me till the turning of Thine anger, Set for me a limit, and remember me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:51–52; Job 7:1; Acts 26:8"> If a man dieth — doth he revive? All days of my warfare I wait, till my change come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 13:22; Psalms 138:8; Job 10:8"> Thou dost call, and I — I answer Thee; To the work of Thy hands Thou hast desire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 10:6; Job 31:4; Psalms 139:1–4"> But now, my steps Thou numberest, Thou dost not watch over my sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:34; Hosea 13:12; Job 21:19"> Sealed up in a bag &#91;is&#93; my transgression, And Thou sewest up mine iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 6:14; Job 18:4; Jeremiah 4:24"> And yet, a falling mountain wasteth away, And a rock is removed from its place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 7:6; Job 27:8; Job 19:10"> Stones have waters worn away, Their outpourings wash away the dust of earth, And the hope of man Thou hast destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 14:14; Job 2:12; Ecclesiastes 8:8"> Thou prevailest &#91;over&#93; him for ever, and he goeth, He is changing his countenance, And Thou sendest him away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:5; Ecclesiastes 2:18–19; Isaiah 63:16"> Honoured are his sons, and he knoweth not; And they are little, and he attendeth not to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 19:26; Job 19:20; Luke 16:23–24"> Only — his flesh for him is pained, And his soul for him doth mourn.'</span>
