<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 2:11"> And Bildad the Shuhite answereth and saith: —</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 6:26; Job 15:2; 1 Kings 19:11"> Till when dost thou speak these things? And a strong wind — sayings of thy mouth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; 2 Chronicles 19:7; Genesis 18:25"> Doth God pervert judgment? And doth the Mighty One pervert justice?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 1:5; Job 1:18–19; Job 5:4"> If thy sons have sinned before Him, And He doth send them away, By the hand of their transgression,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 4:7–10; Hebrews 3:7–8; Job 11:13"> If thou dost seek early unto God, And unto the Mighty makest supplication,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 51:9; Isaiah 3:10; Proverbs 15:8"> If pure and upright thou &#91;art&#93;, Surely now He waketh for thee, And hath completed The habitation of thy righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 42:12–13; Matthew 13:31–32; Proverbs 19:20"> And thy beginning hath been small, And thy latter end is very great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:7; Deuteronomy 4:32; Job 15:18"> For, ask I pray thee of a former generation, And prepare to a search of their fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:15; Psalms 144:4; Job 14:2"> (For of yesterday we &#91;are&#93;, and we know not, For a shadow &#91;are&#93; our days on earth.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 18:15; Matthew 12:35; Job 12:7–8"> Do they not shew thee — speak to thee, And from their heart bring forth words?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 2:3; Isaiah 19:5–7"> 'Doth a rush wise without mire? A reed increase without water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:6; Psalms 129:6–7; Matthew 13:20"> While it &#91;is&#93; in its budding — uncropped, Even before any herb it withereth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 9:17; Job 11:20; Job 13:16"> So &#91;are&#93; the paths of all forgetting God, And the hope of the profane doth perish,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 59:5–6"> Whose confidence is loathsome, And the house of a spider his trust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 27:18; Luke 6:47–49; Matthew 7:24–27"> He leaneth on his house — and it standeth not: He taketh hold on it — and it abideth not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 80:11; Job 21:7–15; Job 5:3"> Green he &#91;is&#93; before the sun, And over his garden his branch goeth out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 5:24; Mark 11:20; Jude 1:12"> By a heap his roots are wrapped, A house of stones he looketh for.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 7:10; Psalms 37:36; Job 7:8"> If &#91;one&#93; doth destroy him from his place, Then it hath feigned concerning him, I have not seen thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 20:5; Psalms 113:7; 1 Samuel 2:8"> Lo, this &#91;is&#93; the joy of his way, And from the dust others spring up.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 4:7; Psalms 37:37; Psalms 37:24"> Lo, God doth not reject the perfect, Nor taketh hold on the hand of evil doers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 126:2; Genesis 21:6; Psalms 126:6"> While he filleth with laughter thy mouth, And thy lips with shouting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 132:18; Psalms 109:29; Psalms 35:26"> Those hating thee do put on shame, And the tent of the wicked is not!</span>
