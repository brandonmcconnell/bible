<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Eliphaz the Temanite answereth and saith: —</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 17:10; Galatians 6:7–8; Job 35:6–8"> To God is a man profitable, Because a wise man to himself is profitable?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 15:8; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Psalms 147:10–11"> Is it a delight to the Mighty One That thou art righteous? is it gain, That thou makest perfect thy ways?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 14:3; Psalms 143:2; Revelation 3:19"> Because of thy reverence Doth He reason &#91;with&#93; thee? He entereth with thee into judgment:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 40:12; Job 15:5–6; Job 15:31–34"> Is not thy wickedness abundant? And there is no end to thine iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 22:26; Ezekiel 18:12; Ezekiel 18:16"> For thou takest a pledge of thy brother for nought, And the garments of the naked Thou dost strip off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 31:17; Matthew 25:42; Isaiah 58:7"> Thou causest not the weary to drink water, And from the hungry thou withholdest bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 9:15; Job 29:7–17; Job 13:8"> As to the man of arm — he hath the earth, And the accepted of face — he dwelleth in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 24:21; Job 24:3; Ezekiel 22:7"> Widows thou hast sent away empty, And the arms of the fatherless are bruised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 18:8–10; Job 13:21; Job 6:4"> Therefore round about thee &#91;are&#93; snares, And trouble thee doth fear suddenly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 69:1–2; Lamentations 3:54; Jonah 2:3"> Or darkness — thou dost not see, And abundance of waters doth cover thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 11:8; Isaiah 66:1; Psalms 8:3–4"> Is not God high &#91;in&#93; heaven? And see the summit of the stars, That they are high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:12; Psalms 10:11; Ezekiel 9:9"> And thou hast said, 'What — hath God known? Through thickness doth He judge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; Psalms 139:11–12; Psalms 33:14"> Thick clouds &#91;are&#93; a secret place to Him, And He doth not see;' And the circle of the heavens He walketh habitually,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 17:26–27; Genesis 6:5; Genesis 6:11–13"> The path of the age dost thou observe, That men of iniquity have trodden?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 15:32; Genesis 7:17–24; Psalms 55:23"> Who have been cut down unexpectedly, A flood is poured out on their foundation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 21:14–15; Romans 1:28; Psalms 4:6"> Those saying to God, 'Turn aside from us,' And what doth the Mighty One to them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 21:16; Job 12:6; Acts 14:17"> And he hath filled their houses &#91;with&#93; good: (And the counsel of the wicked Hath been far from me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 58:10; Psalms 107:42; Psalms 52:6"> See do the righteous and they rejoice, And the innocent mocketh at them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 2:6–7; Job 1:16; Job 20:26"> 'Surely our substance hath not been cut off, And their excellency hath fire consumed.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:9; Isaiah 27:5; Philippians 4:7"> Acquaint thyself, I pray thee, with Him, And be at peace, Thereby thine increase &#91;is&#93; good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; Job 23:12; Deuteronomy 4:1–2"> Receive, I pray thee, from His mouth a law, And set His sayings in thy heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 11:13–14; Zechariah 1:3; Job 8:5–6"> If thou dost return unto the Mighty Thou art built up, Thou puttest iniquity far from thy tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 13:12; 1 Kings 9:28; Genesis 10:29"> So as to set on the dust a defence, And on a rock of the valleys a covering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 33:6; Psalms 16:5–6; Genesis 15:1"> And the Mighty hath been thy defence, And silver &#91;is&#93; strength to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; Isaiah 58:14; Job 27:10"> For then on the Mighty thou delightest thyself, And dost lift up unto God thy face,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 58:9; Psalms 116:1; 1 John 5:14–15"> Thou dost make supplication unto Him, And He doth hear thee, And thy vows thou completest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; Psalms 90:17; Psalms 20:4"> And thou decreest a saying, And it is established to thee, And on thy ways hath light shone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="James 4:6; Job 5:19–27; Luke 1:52"> For they have made low, And thou sayest, 'Lift up.' And the bowed down of eyes he saveth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 42:7–8; James 5:15–16; Genesis 18:26–32"> He delivereth the not innocent, Yea, he hath been delivered By the cleanness of thy hands.</span>
