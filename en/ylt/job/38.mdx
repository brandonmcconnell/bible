<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 40:6; Job 37:1–2; 2 Kings 2:11"> And Jehovah answereth Job out of the whirlwind, and saith: —</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 42:3; 1 Timothy 1:7; Job 35:16"> Who &#91;is&#93; this — darkening counsel, By words without knowledge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 40:7; Exodus 12:11; Jeremiah 1:17"> Gird, I pray thee, as a man, thy loins, And I ask thee, and cause thou Me to know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 104:5; Hebrews 1:10; Proverbs 30:4"> Where wast thou when I founded earth? Declare, if thou hast known understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 40:12; Zechariah 2:1–2; Job 11:9"> Who placed its measures — if thou knowest? Or who hath stretched out upon it a line?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 26:7; Psalms 104:5; Psalms 93:1"> On what have its sockets been sunk? Or who hath cast its corner-stone?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 5:11; Job 1:6; Ezra 3:11–12"> In the singing together of stars of morning, And all sons of God shout for joy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 1:9; Proverbs 8:29; Jeremiah 5:22"> And He shutteth up with doors the sea, In its coming forth, from the womb it goeth out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 1:2"> In My making a cloud its clothing, And thick darkness its swaddling band,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:22; Psalms 104:9; Psalms 33:7"> And I measure over it My statute, And place bar and doors,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 89:9; Proverbs 8:29; Psalms 65:6–7"> And say, 'Hitherto come thou, and add not, And a command is placed On the pride of thy billows.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 74:16; Psalms 148:3–5; Genesis 1:5"> Hast thou commanded morning since thy days? Causest thou the dawn to know its place?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 104:35; Job 37:3; Exodus 14:27"> To take hold on the skirts of the earth, And the wicked are shaken out of it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 104:6; Psalms 104:2"> It turneth itself as clay of a seal And they station themselves as clothed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 10:15; Psalms 37:17; Job 18:5"> And withheld from the wicked is their light, And the arm lifted up is broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 8:24; Job 26:5–6; Psalms 77:19"> Hast thou come in to springs of the sea? And in searching the deep Hast thou walked up and down?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 9:13; Psalms 107:18; Job 3:5"> Revealed to thee were the gates of death? And the gates of death-shade dost thou see?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 89:11–12; Revelation 20:9; Jeremiah 31:37"> Thou hast understanding, Even unto the broad places of earth! Declare — if thou hast known it all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:8; Genesis 1:14–18; Genesis 1:3–4"> Where &#91;is&#93; this — the way light dwelleth? And darkness, where &#91;is&#93; this — its place?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 10:19; Job 26:10; Genesis 23:17"> That thou dost take it unto its boundary, And that thou dost understand The paths of its house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 15:7; Job 38:12; Job 38:4"> Thou hast known — for then thou art born And the number of thy days &#91;are&#93; many!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 37:6; Psalms 135:7; Psalms 33:7"> Hast thou come in unto the treasure of snow? Yea, the treasures of hail dost thou see?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 10:11; Isaiah 30:30; Exodus 9:18"> That I have kept back for a time of distress, For a day of conflict and battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 38:12–13; Jonah 4:8; Matthew 24:27"> Where &#91;is&#93; this, the way light is apportioned? It scattereth an east wind over the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 28:26; Psalms 29:3–10; Job 37:3–6"> Who hath divided for the flood a conduit? And a way for the lightning of the voices?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:22; Hebrews 6:7–8; Psalms 107:35"> To cause &#91;it&#93; to rain on a land — no man, A wilderness — no man in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 107:35; Psalms 104:14"> To satisfy a desolate and waste place, And to cause to shoot up The produce of the tender grass?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:22; Psalms 147:8; Jeremiah 10:13"> Hath the rain a father? Or who hath begotten the drops of dew?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 147:16–17; Job 37:10; Job 38:8"> From whose belly came forth the ice? And the hoar-frost of the heavens, Who hath begotten it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 37:10"> As a stone waters are hidden, And the face of the deep is captured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Amos 5:8; Job 9:9"> Dost thou bind sweet influences of Kimah? Or the attractions of Kesil dost thou open?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 38:31; Job 9:9; 2 Kings 23:5"> Dost thou bring out Mazzaroth in its season? And Aysh for her sons dost thou comfort?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:35–36; Genesis 8:22; Job 38:12–13"> Hast thou known the statutes of heaven? Or dost thou appoint Its dominion in the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 22:11; Job 36:27–28; Amos 5:8"> Dost thou lift up to the cloud thy voice, And abundance of water doth cover thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 36:32; Job 37:3; Isaiah 65:1"> Dost thou send out lightnings, and they go And say unto thee, 'Behold us?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Job 32:8; Psalms 51:6; Proverbs 2:6"> Who hath put in the inward parts wisdom? Or who hath given To the covered part understanding?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Psalms 147:4; Genesis 15:5; Genesis 8:1"> Who doth number the clouds by wisdom? And the bottles of the heavens, Who doth cause to lie down,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> In the hardening of dust into hardness, And clods cleave together?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 104:21; Job 4:10–11; Psalms 34:10"> Dost thou hunt for a lion prey? And the desire of young lions fulfil?</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Job 37:8; Genesis 49:9; Numbers 24:9"> When they bow down in dens — Abide in a thicket for a covert?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 12:24; Matthew 6:26; Psalms 147:9"> Who doth prepare for a raven his provision, When his young ones cry unto God? They wander without food.</span>
