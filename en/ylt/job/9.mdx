<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Job answereth and saith: —</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 4:17; Romans 3:20; Job 25:4"> Truly I have known that &#91;it is&#93; so, And what — is man righteous with God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 10:2; Job 40:2; Isaiah 57:15–16"> If he delight to strive with Him — He doth not answer him one of a thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 36:5; Romans 11:33; Ephesians 3:20"> Wise in heart and strong in power — Who hath hardened toward Him and is at peace?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; Luke 21:11; Habakkuk 3:10"> Who is removing mountains, And they have not known, Who hath overturned them in His anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Haggai 2:6; Hebrews 12:26; Psalms 75:3"> Who is shaking earth from its place, And its pillars move themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Amos 8:9; Luke 21:25–26"> Who is speaking to the sun, and it riseth not, And the stars He sealeth up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 44:24; Isaiah 40:22; Psalms 104:2–3"> Stretching out the heavens by Himself, And treading on the heights of the sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 5:8; Genesis 1:16; Job 38:31–41"> Making Osh, Kesil, and Kimah, And the inner chambers of the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 5:9; Psalms 72:18; Ephesians 3:20"> Doing great things till there is no searching, And wonderful, till there is no numbering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 23:8–9; Job 35:14; Psalms 77:19"> Lo, He goeth over by me, and I see not, And He passeth on, and I attend not to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 45:9; Job 11:10; Job 23:13"> Lo, He snatches away, who bringeth it back? Who saith unto Him, 'What dost Thou?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 89:10; Job 26:12; Isaiah 30:7"> God doth not turn back His anger, Under Him bowed have proud helpers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 8:27; Job 9:3; Job 4:19"> How much less do I — I answer Him? Choose out my words with Him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 10:15; Job 8:5; Job 10:2"> Whom, though I were righteous, I answer not, For my judgment I make supplication.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 29:24; Exodus 6:9; Psalms 66:18–20"> Though I had called and He answereth me, I do not believe that He giveth ear &#91;to&#93; my voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 16:14; Job 2:3; Job 34:6"> Because with a tempest He bruiseth me, And hath multiplied my wounds for nought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 7:19; Lamentations 3:15; Psalms 88:15–18"> He permitteth me not to refresh my spirit, But filleth me with bitter things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 9:32–33; Job 9:4; 1 Corinthians 1:25"> If of power, lo, the Strong One; And if of judgment — who doth convene me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 15:5–6; Philippians 3:12–15; Psalms 143:2"> If I be righteous, Mine mouth doth declare me wicked, Perfect I am! — it declareth me perverse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 1:1; Psalms 139:23–24; Proverbs 28:26"> Perfect I am! — I know not my soul, I despise my life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:1–3; Luke 13:2–4; Job 10:8"> It is the same thing, therefore I said, 'The perfect and the wicked He is consuming.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 24:12; Job 1:13–19; Job 4:7"> If a scourge doth put to death suddenly, At the trial of the innocent He laugheth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 10:3; Jeremiah 12:1–2; Psalms 73:3–7"> Earth hath been given Into the hand of the wicked one. The face of its judges he covereth, If not — where, who &#91;is&#93; he?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 7:6–7; James 4:14; Psalms 90:9–10"> My days have been swifter than a runner, They have fled, they have not seen good,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:8; Isaiah 18:2; Job 39:27–30"> They have passed on with ships of reed, As an eagle darteth on food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 7:13; Jeremiah 8:18; Job 7:11"> Though I say, 'I forget my talking, I forsake my corner, and I brighten up!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; Psalms 88:15–16; Job 21:6"> I have been afraid of all my griefs, I have known that Thou dost not acquit me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 10:7; Job 21:27; Job 10:14–17"> I — I am become wicked; why &#91;is&#93; this? &#91;In&#93; vain I labour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:22; Psalms 26:6; 1 John 1:8"> If I have washed myself with snow-water, And purified with soap my hands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 64:6; Isaiah 59:6; Job 15:6"> Then in corruption Thou dost dip me, And my garments have abominated me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 9:20; Ecclesiastes 6:10; Psalms 143:2"> But if a man like myself — I answer him, We come together into judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:25; 1 John 2:1–2; Job 9:19"> If there were between us an umpire, He doth place his hand on us both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 39:10; Job 13:11; Job 33:7"> He doth turn aside from off me his rod, And His terror doth not make me afraid,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> I speak, and do not fear Him, But I am not right with myself.</span>
