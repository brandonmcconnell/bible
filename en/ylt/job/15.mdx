<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 42:7; Job 2:11; Job 22:1"> And Eliphaz the Temanite answereth and saith: —</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 6:26; Job 8:2; Hosea 12:1"> Doth a wise man answer &#91;with&#93; vain knowledge? And fill &#91;with&#93; an east wind his belly?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Matthew 12:36–37; Job 13:4–5"> To reason with a word not useful? And speeches — no profit in them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 3:31; Luke 18:1; Amos 6:10"> Yea, thou dost make reverence void, And dost diminish meditation before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:3–5; James 3:5–8; Luke 6:45"> For thy mouth teacheth thine iniquity, And thou chooseth the tongue of the subtile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 19:22; Matthew 12:37; Job 9:20"> Thy mouth declareth thee wicked, and not I, And thy lips testify against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 90:2; Job 12:12; Job 38:4–41"> The first man art thou born? And before the heights wast thou formed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 11:34; Jeremiah 23:18; Job 13:5–6"> Of the secret counsel of God dost thou hear? And withdrawest thou unto thee wisdom?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 13:2; 2 Corinthians 11:5; Job 12:3"> What hast thou known, and we know not? Understandest thou — and it is not with us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 32:6–7; Job 8:8–10; Job 12:12"> Both the gray-headed And the very aged &#91;are&#93; among us — Greater than thy father &#91;in&#93; days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:2; 2 Corinthians 1:3–5; 1 Kings 22:24"> Too few for thee are the comforts of God? And a gentle word &#91;is&#93; with thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Mark 7:21–22"> What — doth thine heart take thee away? And what — are thine eyes high?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 15:25–27; James 3:2–6; Job 9:4"> For thou turnest against God thy spirit? And hast brought out words from thy mouth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; Job 14:4; Proverbs 20:9"> What &#91;is&#93; man that he is pure, And that he is righteous, one born of woman?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 25:5; Job 4:18; Job 5:1"> Lo, in His holy ones He putteth no credence, And the heavens have not been pure in His eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 34:7; Proverbs 19:28; Romans 3:9–19"> Also — surely abominable and filthy Is man drinking as water perverseness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 36:2; Job 34:2; Job 13:5–6"> I shew thee — hearken to me — And this I have seen and declare:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 8:8; Isaiah 38:19; Psalms 71:18"> Which the wise declare — And have not hid — from their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joel 3:17; Genesis 10:32; Genesis 10:25"> To them alone was the land given, And a stranger passed not over into their midst:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 27:13; James 5:1–6; Luke 12:19–21"> 'All days of the wicked he is paining himself, And few years have been laid up for the terrible one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 18:11; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Leviticus 26:36"> A fearful voice &#91;is&#93; in his ears, In peace doth a destroyer come to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 20:24–25; Matthew 27:5; Job 9:16"> He believeth not to return from darkness, And watched &#91;is&#93; he for the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 109:10; Psalms 59:15; Job 18:12"> He is wandering for bread — 'Where &#91;is&#93; it?' He hath known that ready at his hand Is a day of darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 24:34; Proverbs 6:11; Job 6:2–4"> Terrify him do adversity and distress, They prevail over him As a king ready for a boaster.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 5:23; Psalms 52:7; Psalms 73:9"> For he stretched out against God his hand, And against the Mighty he maketh himself mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 18:40; Job 16:12; 2 Chronicles 32:13–17"> He runneth unto Him with a neck, With thick bosses of his shields.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 17:10; Jeremiah 5:28; Psalms 73:7"> For he hath covered his face with his fat, And maketh vigour over &#91;his&#93; confidence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 3:14; Jeremiah 51:37; Micah 7:18"> And he inhabiteth cities cut off, houses not dwelt in, That have been ready to become heaps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 27:16–17; Psalms 49:16–17; James 1:11"> He is not rich, nor doth his wealth rise, Nor doth he stretch out on earth their continuance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 4:9; Job 15:22; Job 18:5–6"> He turneth not aside from darkness, His tender branch doth a flame dry up, And he turneth aside at the breath of His mouth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 59:4; Galatians 6:3; Psalms 62:10"> Let him not put credence in vanity, He hath been deceived, For vanity is his recompence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:17; Psalms 55:23; Hosea 9:16"> Not in his day is it completed, And his bending branch is not green.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:39–40; Isaiah 33:9; Revelation 6:13"> He shaketh off as a vine his unripe fruit, And casteth off as an olive his blossom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 8:13; Job 27:8; Job 8:22"> For the company of the profane &#91;is&#93; gloomy, And fire hath consumed tents of bribery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 7:14; Hosea 10:13; Isaiah 59:4–5"> To conceive misery, and to bear iniquity, Even their heart doth prepare deceit.</span>
