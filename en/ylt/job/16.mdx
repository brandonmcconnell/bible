<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Job answereth and saith: —</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 13:4–5; Psalms 69:26; Job 11:2–3"> I have heard many such things, Miserable comforters &#91;are&#93; ye all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 6:26; Job 15:2; Titus 2:8"> Is there an end to words of wind? Or what doth embolden thee that thou answerest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 109:25; Psalms 22:7; Lamentations 2:15"> I also, like you, might speak, If your soul were in my soul's stead. I might join against you with words, And nod at you with my head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 27:9; Galatians 6:1; Isaiah 35:3–4"> I might harden you with my mouth, And the moving of my lips might be sparing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 10:1; Psalms 88:15–18; Psalms 77:1–9"> If I speak, my pain is not restrained, And I cease — what goeth from me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 7:3; Job 1:15–19; Psalms 6:6–7"> Only, now, it hath wearied me; Thou hast desolated all my company,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 10:17; Job 19:20; Ruth 1:21"> And Thou dost loathe me, For a witness it hath been, And rise up against me doth my failure, In my face it testifieth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 35:16; Job 13:24; Lamentations 2:16"> His anger hath torn, and he hateth me, He hath gnashed at me with his teeth, My adversary sharpeneth his eyes for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 35:15; Psalms 22:13; Isaiah 50:6"> They have gaped on me with their mouth, In reproach they have smitten my cheeks, Together against me they set themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 27:12; Psalms 7:14; Psalms 31:8"> God shutteth me up unto the perverse, And to the hands of the wicked turneth me over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 7:20; Lamentations 3:11–12; Lamentations 3:4"> At ease I have been, and he breaketh me, And he hath laid hold on my neck, And he breaketh me in pieces, And he raiseth me to him for a mark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 20:25; Job 6:4; Lamentations 2:11"> Go round against me do his archers. He splitteth my reins, and spareth not, He poureth out to the earth my gall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 15:8; Lamentations 3:3–5; Joel 2:7"> He breaketh me — breach upon breach, He runneth upon me as a mighty one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 37:34; Psalms 7:5; Psalms 75:5"> Sackcloth I have sewed on my skin, And have rolled in the dust my horn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 14:34; Psalms 102:3–5; Lamentations 1:16"> My face is foul with weeping, And on mine eyelids &#91;is&#93; death-shade.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 15:8; Psalms 66:18–19; Job 27:6–7"> Not for violence in my hands, And my prayer &#91;is&#93; pure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 26:21; Ezekiel 24:7; Genesis 4:11"> O earth, do not thou cover my blood! And let there not be a place for my cry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 1:9; 1 Thessalonians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 11:31"> Also, now, lo, in the heavens &#91;is&#93; my witness, And my testifier in the high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Psalms 142:2; Job 16:4"> My interpreter &#91;is&#93; my friend, Unto God hath mine eye dropped:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 6:10; Isaiah 45:9; Romans 9:20"> And he reasoneth for a man with God, And a son of man for his friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 14:10; Job 14:14; Job 14:5"> When a few years do come, Then a path I return not do I go.</span>
