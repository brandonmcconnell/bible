<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 20:1"> And Zophar the Naamathite answereth and saith: —</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 10:19; James 1:19; Job 18:2"> Is a multitude of words not answered? And is a man of lips justified?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 17:2; Job 24:25; Titus 2:8"> Thy devices make men keep silent, Thou scornest, and none is causing blushing!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 10:7; Job 6:10; Job 35:2"> And thou sayest, 'Pure &#91;is&#93; my discourse, And clean I have been in Thine eyes.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 38:1–2; Job 31:35; Job 33:6–18"> And yet, O that God had spoken! And doth open His lips with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra 9:13; Lamentations 3:22; Daniel 2:28"> And declare to thee secrets of wisdom, For counsel hath foldings. And know thou that God forgetteth for thee, &#91;Some&#93; of thine iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Ecclesiastes 3:11; Psalms 145:3"> By searching dost thou find out God? Unto perfection find out the Mighty One?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 22:12; Psalms 139:6–8; Isaiah 55:9"> Heights of the heavens! — what dost thou? Deeper than Sheol! — what knowest thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 139:9–10; Job 28:24–25; Psalms 65:5–8"> Longer than earth &#91;is&#93; its measure, And broader than the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:12–13; Job 12:14; Revelation 3:7"> If He pass on, and shut up, and assemble, Who then dost reverse it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 10:14; Hebrews 4:13; Revelation 2:23"> For he hath known men of vanity, And He seeth iniquity, And one doth not consider &#91;it&#93;!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 2:20; Ecclesiastes 3:18; James 3:13–17"> And empty man is bold, And the colt of a wild ass man is born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 143:6; Psalms 88:9; 1 Samuel 7:3"> If thou — thou hast prepared thy heart, And hast spread out unto Him thy hands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:23; James 4:8; Job 4:7"> If iniquity &#91;is&#93; in thy hand, put it far off, And let not perverseness dwell in thy tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 22:26; 1 John 2:28; 1 John 3:19–22"> For then thou liftest up thy face from blemish, And thou hast been firm, and fearest not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 65:16; Ecclesiastes 5:20; John 16:21"> For thou dost forget misery, As waters passed away thou rememberest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 58:8–10; Psalms 37:6; Proverbs 4:18"> And above the noon doth age rise, Thou fliest — as the morning thou art.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:24–26; Psalms 43:5; Psalms 3:5"> And thou hast trusted because their is hope, And searched — in confidence thou liest down,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 45:12; Isaiah 60:14; Job 42:8–9"> And thou hast rested, And none is causing trembling, And many have entreated thy face;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:65; Job 31:16; Job 17:5"> And the eyes of the wicked are consumed, And refuge hath perished from them, And their hope &#91;is&#93; a breathing out of soul!</span>
