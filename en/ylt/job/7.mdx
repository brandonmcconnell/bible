<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 39:4; Job 14:5–6; Job 5:7"> Is there not a warfare to man on earth? And as the days of an hireling his days?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 19:13; Deuteronomy 24:15; James 5:4"> As a servant desireth the shadow, And as a hireling expecteth his wage,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 6:6; Ecclesiastes 1:14; Psalms 39:5"> So I have been caused to inherit months of vanity, And nights of misery they numbered to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:67; Job 7:13–14; Job 17:12"> If I lay down then I said, 'When do I rise!' And evening hath been measured, And I have been full of tossings till dawn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 17:14; Isaiah 14:11; Job 2:7–8"> Clothed hath been my flesh &#91;with&#93; worms, And a clod of dust, My skin hath been shrivelled and is loathsome,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 9:25; Job 17:15; Job 17:11"> My days swifter than a weaving machine, And they are consumed without hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 4:14; Psalms 78:39; Jeremiah 15:15"> Remember Thou that my life &#91;is&#93; a breath, Mine eye turneth not back to see good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 20:9; Job 8:18; Psalms 37:36"> The eye of my beholder beholdeth me not. Thine eyes &#91;are&#93; upon me — and I am not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 30:15; Job 10:21; 2 Samuel 14:14"> Consumed hath been a cloud, and it goeth, So he who is going down to Sheol cometh not up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 8:18; Job 20:9; Psalms 103:16"> He turneth not again to his house, Nor doth his place discern him again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 40:9; 1 Samuel 1:10; Job 10:1"> Also I — I withhold not my mouth — I speak in the distress of my spirit, I talk in the bitterness of my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:2–3; Lamentations 3:7; Job 38:6–11"> A sea -&#91;monster&#93; am I, or a dragon, That thou settest over me a guard?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 6:6; Psalms 77:4; Job 9:27–28"> When I said, 'My bed doth comfort me,' He taketh away in my talking my couch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 2:1; Genesis 41:8; Genesis 40:5–7"> And thou hast affrighted me with dreams, And from visions thou terrifiest me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 27:5; 2 Samuel 17:23"> And my soul chooseth strangling, Death rather than my bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 19:4; Job 10:1; Job 9:21"> I have wasted away — not to the age do I live. Cease from me, for my days &#91;are&#93; vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 2:6; Psalms 8:4; Psalms 144:3"> What &#91;is&#93; man that Thou dost magnify him? And that Thou settest unto him Thy heart?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 38:12–13; Jeremiah 9:7; 1 Peter 1:7"> And inspectest him in the mornings, In the evenings dost try him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 9:18; Job 14:6; Psalms 13:1–3"> How long dost Thou not look from me? Thou dost not desist till I swallow my spittle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Lamentations 3:12; Job 7:11–12; Job 3:24"> I have sinned, what do I to Thee, O watcher of man? Why hast Thou set me for a mark to Thee, And I am for a burden to myself — and what?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; Job 10:14; 1 John 1:9"> Thou dost not take away my transgression, And cause to pass away mine iniquity, Because now, for dust I lie down: And Thou hast sought me — and I am not!</span>
