<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> A stream hath broken out from a sojourner, Those forgotten of the foot, They were low, from man they wandered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 28:25–29; Psalms 104:14–15; Genesis 1:11–12"> The earth! from it cometh forth bread, And its under-part is turned like fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 28:16; Exodus 24:10; Isaiah 54:11"> A place of the sapphire &#91;are&#93; its stones, And it hath dust of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 11:6; Job 28:21–23; Job 38:24"> A path — not known it hath a ravenous fowl, Nor scorched it hath an eye of the kite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Nor trodden it have the sons of pride, Not passed over it hath the fierce lion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nahum 1:4–6"> Against the flint he sent forth his hand, He overturned from the root mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:9; Proverbs 14:23; Proverbs 24:4"> Among rocks, brooks he hath cleaved, And every precious thing hath his eye seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:5; Isaiah 45:2–3; Isaiah 44:27"> From overflowing floods he hath bound, And the hidden thing bringeth out &#91;to&#93; light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 2:4–6; Job 28:20; James 1:5"> And the wisdom — whence is it found? And where &#91;is&#93; this, the place of understanding?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 8:11; Proverbs 8:18–19; Proverbs 3:14–15"> Man hath not known its arrangement, Nor is it found in the land of the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 11:33–34"> The deep hath said, 'It &#91;is&#93; not in me,' And the sea hath said, 'It is not with me.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 16:16; Proverbs 8:10–11; Proverbs 8:19"> Gold is not given for it, Nor is silver weighed — its price.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 13:12; Ezekiel 28:13; Psalms 45:9"> It is not valued with pure gold of Ophir, With precious onyx and sapphire,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; Revelation 4:6; Revelation 21:11"> Not equal it do gold and crystal, Nor &#91;is&#93; its exchange a vessel of fine gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:15; Proverbs 31:10; Lamentations 4:7"> Corals and pearl are not remembered, The acquisition of wisdom &#91;is&#93; above rubies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 8:19; Exodus 39:10; Ezekiel 28:13"> Not equal it doth the topaz of Cush, With pure gold it is not valued.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 28:12; James 1:5; James 1:17"> And the wisdom — whence doth it come? And where &#91;is&#93; this, the place of understanding?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 49:3–4; 1 Corinthians 2:7–10; Matthew 11:25"> It hath been hid from the eyes of all living. And from the fowl of the heavens It hath been hidden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 83:10–12; Job 26:6; Job 28:14"> Destruction and death have said: 'With our ears we have heard its fame.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 8:22–31; 1 Corinthians 1:30; Jude 1:25"> God hath understood its way, And He hath known its place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 15:3; Zechariah 4:10; 2 Chronicles 16:9"> For He to the ends of the earth doth look, Under the whole heavens He doth see,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 135:7; Isaiah 40:12; Job 12:15"> To make for the wind a weight, And the waters He meted out in measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 38:25; Job 37:3; Zechariah 10:1"> In His making for the rain a limit, And a way for the brightness of the voices,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 19:1; Proverbs 8:22–29"> Then He hath seen and declareth it, He hath prepared it, and also searched it out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 111:10; Proverbs 1:7; Proverbs 9:10"> And He saith to man: — 'Lo, fear of the Lord, that &#91;is&#93; wisdom, And to turn from evil &#91;is&#93; understanding.'</span>
