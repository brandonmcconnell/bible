<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Job answereth and saith: —</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3–4; Job 4:3–4; Isaiah 41:5–7"> What — thou hast helped the powerless, Saved an arm not strong!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 71:15–18; Job 32:11–13; Job 33:3"> What — thou hast given counsel to the unwise, And wise plans in abundance made known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 22:23–24; Job 32:18; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> With whom hast thou declared words? And whose breath came forth from thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 88:10; Genesis 6:4; Job 41:1–34"> The Rephaim are formed, Beneath the waters, also their inhabitants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 139:8; Proverbs 15:11; Hebrews 4:13"> Naked &#91;is&#93; Sheol over-against Him, And there is no covering to destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 9:8; Psalms 104:2–5; Isaiah 40:22"> Stretching out the north over desolation, Hanging the earth upon nothing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 30:4; Jeremiah 10:13; Isaiah 5:6"> Binding up the waters in His thick clouds, And the cloud is not rent under them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; Job 22:14; Habakkuk 3:3–5"> Taking hold of the face of the throne, Spreading over it His cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 8:29; Job 38:8–11; Jeremiah 5:22"> A limit He hath placed on the waters, Unto the boundary of light with darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:8; Psalms 18:7; Job 15:15"> Pillars of the heavens do tremble, And they wonder because of His rebuke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 51:15; Jeremiah 31:35; Psalms 89:9–10"> By His power He hath quieted the sea, And by His understanding smitten the proud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 27:1; Psalms 104:30; Psalms 74:13–14"> By His Spirit the heavens He beautified, Formed hath His hand the fleeing serpent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:26–29; Psalms 145:3; Romans 11:33"> Lo, these &#91;are&#93; the borders of His way, And how little a matter is heard of Him, And the thunder of His might Who doth understand?</span>
