<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 14:16; Psalms 10:15; Zephaniah 1:12"> That Thou inquirest for mine iniquity, And for my sin seekest?</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 119:73; Isaiah 43:7; Job 10:3"> Thy hands have taken pains about me, And they make me together round about, And Thou swallowest me up!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 2:7; Isaiah 64:8; Genesis 3:19"> Remember, I pray Thee, That as clay Thou hast made me, And unto dust Thou dost bring me back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 139:14–16"> Dost Thou not as milk pour me out? And as cheese curdle me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:4–8; Ephesians 4:16; Job 40:17–18"> Skin and flesh Thou dost put on me, And with bones and sinews dost fence me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 17:28; Job 33:4; Acts 17:25"> Life and kindness Thou hast done with me. And Thy inspection hath preserved my spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 23:13; Deuteronomy 32:39; Isaiah 45:15"> And these Thou hast laid up in Thy heart, I have known that this &#91;is&#93; with Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 7:21; Psalms 130:3; Exodus 34:7"> If I sinned, then Thou hast observed me, And from mine iniquity dost not acquit me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 3:11; Job 9:15; Job 9:20–21"> If I have done wickedly — woe to me, And righteously — I lift not up my head, Full of shame — then see my affliction,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 38:13; Lamentations 3:10; Job 5:9"> And it riseth — as a lion Thou huntest me. And Thou turnest back — Thou shewest Thyself wonderful in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ruth 1:21; Job 16:8; Job 16:11–16"> Thou renewest Thy witnesses against me, And dost multiply Thine anger with me, Changes and warfare &#91;are&#93; with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:14–18; Jeremiah 15:10; Job 11:20"> And why from the womb Hast Thou brought me forth? I expire, and the eye doth not see me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 58:8"> As I had not been, I am, From the belly to the grave I am brought,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 14:1; Psalms 39:13; Psalms 103:15–16"> Are not my days few? Cease then, and put from me, And I brighten up a little,</span>
