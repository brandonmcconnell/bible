<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 8:6; Job 24:25; Job 22:5–30"> and against his three friends hath his anger burned, because that they have not found an answer, and condemn Job.</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 32:2; Exodus 32:19"> And Elihu seeth that there is no answer in the mouth of the three men, and his anger burneth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 15:10; 1 Timothy 5:1; Titus 2:6"> And Elihu son of Barachel the Buzite answereth and saith: — Young I &#91;am&#93; in days, and ye &#91;are&#93; age Therefore I have feared, And am afraid of shewing you my opinion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 16:31; Job 12:12; Hebrews 5:12"> I said: Days do speak, And multitude of years teach wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 38:36; Job 33:4; 2 Timothy 3:16"> Surely a spirit is in man, And the breath of the Mighty One Doth cause them to understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 11:25; Job 12:20; Ecclesiastes 4:13"> The multitude are not wise, Nor do the aged understand judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:25; 1 Corinthians 7:40"> Therefore I have said: Hearken to me, I do shew my opinion — even I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 29:21; Job 5:27; Proverbs 28:11"> Lo, I have waited for your words, I give ear unto your reasons, Till ye search out sayings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 32:3; 1 Timothy 1:7"> And unto you I attend, And lo, there is no reasoner for Job, &#91;Or&#93; answerer of his sayings among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23; 1 Corinthians 1:27–29; 1 Corinthians 3:18"> Lest ye say, We have found wisdom, God doth thrust him away, not man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And he hath not set in array words for me, And with your sayings I do not answer him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 6:24–25; Matthew 22:46; Job 29:22"> (They have broken down, They have not answered again, They removed from themselves words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 1:19; Job 13:5; Amos 5:13"> And I have waited, but they do not speak, For they have stood still, They have not answered any more.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 35:3–4; Job 33:12; Job 32:10"> I answer, even I — my share, I shew my opinion — even I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 39:3; Acts 4:20; 2 Corinthians 5:13–14"> For I have been full of words, Distressed me hath the spirit of my breast,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 9:17"> Lo, my breast &#91;is&#93; as wine not opened, Like new bottles it is broken up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 8:6–7; Job 21:3; Job 13:13"> I speak, and there is refreshment to me, I open my lips and answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 19:15; Job 13:8; Matthew 22:16"> Let me not, I pray you, accept the face of any, Nor unto man give flattering titles,</span>
