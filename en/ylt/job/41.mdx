<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 37:29; Ezekiel 29:4–5; 2 Kings 19:28"> Dost thou put a reed in his nose? And with a thorn pierce his jaw?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 30:10; Proverbs 25:15; Psalms 55:21"> Doth he multiply unto thee supplications? Doth he speak unto thee tender things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 21:6; Deuteronomy 15:17; Psalms 8:5–6"> Doth he make a covenant with thee? Dost thou take him for a servant age-during?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 16:25–30; Job 28:11"> Dost thou play with him as a bird? And dost thou bind him for thy damsels?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 14:11"> (Feast upon him do companions, They divide him among the merchants!)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Dost thou fill with barbed irons his skin? And with fish-spears his head?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 20:11; Luke 14:31–32; 2 Kings 10:4"> Place on him thy hand, Remember the battle — do not add!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 28:19; 1 Samuel 3:11; Luke 21:11"> Lo, the hope of him is found a liar, Also at his appearance is not one cast down?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:4; Psalms 2:11–12; 1 Corinthians 10:22"> None so fierce that he doth awake him, And who &#91;is&#93; he before Me stationeth himself?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 11:35; Psalms 24:1; Job 35:7"> Who hath brought before Me and I repay? Under the whole heavens it &#91;is&#93; mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 1:25"> I do not keep silent concerning his parts, And the matter of might, And the grace of his arrangement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 19:28; Psalms 32:9; James 3:3"> Who hath uncovered the face of his clothing? Within his double bridle who doth enter?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:4; Psalms 57:4; Psalms 58:6"> The doors of his face who hath opened? Round about his teeth &#91;are&#93; terrible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 5:5; Revelation 5:2–3; Jeremiah 9:23"> A pride — strong ones of shields, Shut up — a close seal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> One unto another they draw nigh, And air doth not enter between them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> One unto another they adhere, They stick together and are not separated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 3:9; Revelation 1:14"> His sneezings cause light to shine, And his eyes &#91;are&#93; as the eyelids of the dawn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 18:8"> Out of his mouth do flames go, sparks of fire escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:13–14"> Out of his nostrils goeth forth smoke, As a blown pot and reeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 18:8; Isaiah 30:33; Habakkuk 3:5"> His breath setteth coals on fire, And a flame from his mouth goeth forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 13:14; Job 40:16; 1 Corinthians 15:55–57"> In his neck lodge doth strength, And before him doth grief exult.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> The flakes of his flesh have adhered — Firm upon him — it is not moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:3; Isaiah 48:4; Zechariah 7:12"> His heart &#91;is&#93; firm as a stone, Yea, firm as the lower piece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jonah 1:4–6; Psalms 107:28"> From his rising are the mighty afraid, From breakings they keep themselves free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 39:21–24"> The sword of his overtaker standeth not, Spear — dart — and lance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> He reckoneth iron as straw, brass as rotten wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:10; Job 39:7"> The son of the bow doth not cause him to flee, Turned by him into stubble are stones of the sling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:14"> As stubble have darts been reckoned, And he laugheth at the shaking of a javelin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 41:15"> Under him &#91;are&#93; sharp points of clay, He spreadeth gold on the mire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> He causeth to boil as a pot the deep, The sea he maketh as a pot of ointment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 38:30; Genesis 1:2; Genesis 15:15"> After him he causeth a path to shine, One thinketh the deep to be hoary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 40:19"> There is not on the earth his like, That is made without terror.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:3; Psalms 73:6; Job 28:8"> Every high thing he doth see, He &#91;is&#93; king over all sons of pride.</span>
