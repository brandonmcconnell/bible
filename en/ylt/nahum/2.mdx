<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 3:9–11; 2 Chronicles 25:8; Jeremiah 51:20–23"> Come up hath a scatterer to thy face, Keep the bulwark, watch the way, Strengthen the loins, strengthen power mightily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 80:12–13; Ezekiel 37:23; Zephaniah 3:11"> For turned back hath Jehovah to the excellency of Jacob, As &#91;to&#93; the excellency of Israel, For emptied them out have emptiers, And their branches they have marred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:14–15; Isaiah 63:1–3; Zechariah 6:2"> The shield of his mighty ones is become red, Men of might &#91;are in&#93; scarlet, With fiery torches &#91;is&#93; the chariot in a day of his preparation, And the firs have been caused to tremble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:13; Ezekiel 26:10; Nahum 3:2–3"> In out-places shine do the chariots, They go to and fro in broad places, Their appearances &#91;are&#93; like torches, As lightnings they run.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:12; Jeremiah 51:27–28; Jeremiah 50:29"> He doth remember his honourable ones, They stumble in their goings, They hasten &#91;to&#93; its wall, and prepared is the covering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 45:1–2; 2 Peter 3:10–11"> Gates of the rivers have been opened, And the palace is dissolved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 38:14; Isaiah 59:11; Luke 23:48"> And it is established — she hath removed, She hath been brought up, And her handmaids are leading as the voice of doves, Tabering on their hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:13; Revelation 17:1; Jeremiah 46:5"> And Nineveh &#91;is&#93; as a pool of waters, From of old it &#91;is&#93; — and they are fleeing! 'Stand ye, stand;' and none is turning!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:12; Daniel 11:8; Nahum 2:12–13"> Seize ye silver, seize ye gold, And there is no end to the prepared things, &#91;To&#93; the abundance of all desirable vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joel 2:6; Psalms 22:14; Daniel 5:6"> She is empty, yea, emptiness and waste, And the heart hath melted, And the knees have smitten together, And great pain &#91;is&#93; in all loins, And the faces of all of them have gathered paleness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 5:29; Jeremiah 2:15; Isaiah 31:4"> Where &#91;is&#93; the habitation of lionesses? And a feeding-place it &#91;is&#93; for young lions Where walked hath a lion, an old lion, A lion's whelp, and there is none troubling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:34; Psalms 17:12; Isaiah 10:6–14"> The lion is tearing parts &#91;for&#93; his whelps, And is strangling for his lionesses, And he doth fill &#91;with&#93; prey his holes, And his habitations &#91;with&#93; rapine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:13; Psalms 46:9; Nahum 3:5"> Lo, I &#91;am&#93; against thee, An affirmation of Jehovah of Hosts, And I have burned in smoke its chariot, And thy young lions consume doth a sword, And I have cut off from the land thy prey, And not heard any more is the voice of thy messengers!</span>
