<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:13; Jonah 1:2; Zechariah 9:1"> Burden of Nineveh. The Book of the Vision of Nahum the Elkoshite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 8:2; Exodus 20:5; Psalms 94:1"> A God zealous and avenging &#91;is&#93; Jehovah, An avenger &#91;is&#93; Jehovah, and possessing fury. An avenger &#91;is&#93; Jehovah on His adversaries, And He is watching for His enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 147:5; Exodus 34:6–7; Nehemiah 9:17"> Jehovah &#91;is&#93; slow to anger, and great in power, And Jehovah doth not entirely acquit, In a hurricane and in a tempest &#91;is&#93; His way, And a cloud &#91;is&#93; the dust of His feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 33:9; Psalms 106:9; Psalms 114:5"> He is pushing against a sea, and drieth it up, Yea, all the floods He hath made dry, Languishing &#91;are&#93; Bashan and Carmel, Yea, the flower of Lebanon &#91;is&#93; languishing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 1:4; 2 Samuel 22:8; Jeremiah 4:24"> Mountains have shaken because of Him, And the hills have been melted; And lifted up &#91;is&#93; the earth at His presence, And the world and all dwelling in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:10; Malachi 3:2; Deuteronomy 32:22–23"> Before His indignation who doth stand? And who riseth up in the heat of His anger? His fury hath been poured out like fire, And the rocks have been broken by Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 10:27; 2 Timothy 2:19; Lamentations 3:25"> Good &#91;is&#93; Jehovah for a strong place in a day of distress. And He knoweth those trusting in Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 28:17; Isaiah 8:22; Ezekiel 13:13"> And with a flood passing over, An end He maketh of its place, And His enemies doth darkness pursue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 21:11; Proverbs 21:30; Psalms 33:10"> What do we devise against Jehovah? An end He is making, arise not twice doth distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 7:4; Isaiah 5:24; Isaiah 9:18"> For while princes &#91;are&#93; perplexed, And with their drink are drunken, They have been consumed as stubble fully dried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nahum 1:9; 2 Chronicles 13:7; 2 Kings 18:13–14"> From thee hath come forth a deviser of evil Against Jehovah — a worthless counsellor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 30:19; Joel 2:19; Isaiah 17:14"> Thus said Jehovah: Though complete, and thus many, Yet thus they have been cut off, And he hath passed away. And I afflicted thee, I afflict thee no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 10:27; Isaiah 9:4; Jeremiah 2:20"> And now I break his rod from off thee, And thy bands I do draw away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 109:13; Isaiah 46:1–2; Micah 5:13"> And commanded concerning thee hath Jehovah, 'No more of thy name doth spread abroad, From the house of thy gods I cut off graven and molten image, I appoint thy grave, for thou hast been vile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Romans 10:15; Isaiah 40:9–10"> Lo, on the mountains the feet of one proclaiming tidings, sounding peace! Celebrate, O Judah, thy festivals, complete thy vows, For add no more to pass over into thee doth the worthless, He hath been completely cut off!</span>
