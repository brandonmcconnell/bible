<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 3:12; 1 John 4:12; 1 John 4:7–8"> If, then, any exhortation &#91;is&#93; in Christ, if any comfort of love, if any fellowship of spirit, if any bowels and mercies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 12:16; 1 Peter 3:8–9; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> fulfil ye my joy, that ye may mind the same thing — having the same love — of one soul — minding the one thing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 12:10; Galatians 5:26; Ephesians 5:21"> nothing in rivalry or vain-glory, but in humility of mind one another counting more excellent than yourselves —</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:24; Romans 15:1; James 2:8"> each not to your own look ye, but each also to the things of others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 John 2:6; Matthew 11:29; 1 Peter 2:21"> For, let this mind be in you that &#91;is&#93; also in Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 5:18; 2 Corinthians 4:4; Hebrews 1:3"> who, being in the form of God, thought &#91;it&#93; not robbery to be equal to God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:9; Matthew 20:28; John 1:14"> but did empty himself, the form of a servant having taken, in the likeness of men having been made,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:18; Hebrews 12:2; Romans 5:19"> and in fashion having been found as a man, he humbled himself, having become obedient unto death — death even of a cross,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 19:16; Matthew 28:18; 1 Peter 3:22"> wherefore, also, God did highly exalt him, and gave to him a name that &#91;is&#93; above every name,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 45:23–25; Matthew 28:18; Revelation 5:13–14"> that in the name of Jesus every knee may bow — of heavenlies, and earthlies, and what are under the earth —</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 John 4:15; Romans 14:11; John 13:13"> and every tongue may confess that Jesus Christ &#91;is&#93; Lord, to the glory of God the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Peter 1:5–10; Luke 13:23–24; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> So that, my beloved, as ye always obey, not as in my presence only, but now much more in my absence, with fear and trembling your own salvation work out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 13:21; 2 Corinthians 3:5; 2 Timothy 1:9"> for God it is who is working in you both to will and to work for His good pleasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 4:9; 1 Corinthians 10:10; Philippians 2:3"> All things do without murmurings and reasonings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:14–16; 1 Peter 2:12; Matthew 5:45"> that ye may become blameless and harmless, children of God, unblemished in the midst of a generation crooked and perverse, among whom ye do appear as luminaries in the world,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:26; Galatians 2:2; 1 John 1:1"> the word of life holding forth, for rejoicing to me in regard to a day of Christ, that not in vain did I run, nor in vain did I labour;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:6; Romans 15:16; Hebrews 13:15–16"> but if also I am poured forth upon the sacrifice and service of your faith, I rejoice and joy with you all,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 1:2–4; Philippians 4:4; Philippians 3:1"> because of this do ye also rejoice and joy with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:17; 1 Thessalonians 3:6–8; 1 Thessalonians 3:2"> And I hope, in the Lord Jesus, Timotheus to send quickly to you, that I also may be of good spirit, having known the things concerning you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Philippians 2:2; 1 Corinthians 16:10; Philippians 2:22"> for I have no one like-minded, who sincerely for the things concerning you will care,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:24; Matthew 16:24; Philippians 2:4"> for the whole seek their own things, not the things of the Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:17; 2 Corinthians 2:9; 2 Timothy 1:2"> and the proof of him ye know, that as a child &#91;serveth&#93; a father, with me he did serve in regard to the good news;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:3; Philippians 2:19"> him, indeed, therefore, I hope to send, when I may see through the things concerning me — immediately;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="3 John 1:14; Philemon 1:22; Philippians 1:25–26"> and I trust in the Lord that I myself also shall quickly come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Philippians 4:18; Philemon 1:1–2; Philippians 4:3"> And I thought &#91;it&#93; necessary Epaphroditus — my brother, and fellow-workman, and fellow-soldier, and your apostle and servant to my need — to send unto you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Proverbs 12:25; Ephesians 3:13"> seeing he was longing after you all, and in heaviness, because ye heard that he ailed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 34:19; Philippians 2:30; Psalms 103:3–4"> for he also ailed nigh to death, but God did deal kindly with him, and not with him only, but also with me, that sorrow upon sorrow I might not have.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 16:22; 2 Timothy 1:4; Philippians 2:26–27"> The more eagerly, therefore, I did send him, that having seen him again ye may rejoice, and I may be the less sorrowful;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:12; 1 Corinthians 16:18; 1 Timothy 5:17"> receive him, therefore, in the Lord, with all joy, and hold such in honour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:17; Acts 20:24; Philippians 4:10"> because on account of the work of the Christ he drew near to death, having hazarded the life that he might fill up your deficiency of service unto me.</span>
