<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 17:1; 2 Chronicles 28:4–6; 2 Kings 16:1"> And make a conspiracy doth Hoshea son of Elah against Pekah son of Remaliah, and smiteth him, and putteth him to death, and reigneth in his stead, in the twentieth year of Jotham son of Uzziah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And the rest of the matters of Pekah, and all that he did, lo, they are written on the book of the Chronicles of the kings of Israel.</span>

<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Chronicles 27:1"> A son of twenty and five years was he in his reigning, and sixteen years he hath reigned in Jerusalem, and the name of his mother &#91;is&#93; Jerusha daughter of Zadok,</span>

<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Kings 12:3; 2 Chronicles 23:20; 2 Chronicles 27:3–9"> Only, the high places have not turned aside — yet are the people sacrificing and making perfume in high places; he hath built the high gate of the house of Jehovah.</span>
