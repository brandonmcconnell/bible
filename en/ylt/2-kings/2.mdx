<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 5:24; Hebrews 11:5; 1 Kings 19:16–21"> And it cometh to pass, at Jehovah's taking up Elijah in a whirlwind to the heavens, that Elijah goeth, and Elisha, from Gilgal,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 2:6; Ruth 1:15–18; 2 Kings 4:30"> and Elijah saith unto Elisha, 'Abide, I pray thee, here, for Jehovah hath sent me unto Beth-El;' and Elisha saith, 'Jehovah liveth, and thy soul liveth, if I leave thee;' and they go down to Beth-El.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 4:1; 2 Kings 4:38; 2 Kings 9:1"> And sons of the prophets who &#91;are&#93; in Beth-El come out unto Elisha, and say unto him, 'Hast thou known that to-day Jehovah is taking thy lord from thy head?' and he saith, 'I also have known — keep silent.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 2:2; Joshua 6:26; 1 Kings 16:34"> And Elijah saith to him, 'Elisha, abide, I pray thee, here, for Jehovah hath sent me to Jericho;' and he saith, 'Jehovah liveth, and thy soul liveth, if I leave thee;' and they come in to Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 2:3; Joshua 1:1–2; Acts 1:11"> And sons of the prophets who &#91;are&#93; in Jericho come nigh unto Elisha, and say unto him, 'Hast thou known that to-day Jehovah is taking thy lord from thy head?' and he saith, 'I also have known — keep silent.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 2:2; Joshua 3:15"> And Elijah saith to him, 'Abide, I pray thee, here, for Jehovah hath sent me to the Jordan;' and he saith, 'Jehovah liveth, and thy soul liveth, if I leave thee;' and they go on both of them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 18:13; 2 Kings 2:17; 1 Kings 18:4"> — and fifty men of the sons of the prophets have gone on, and stand over-against afar off — and both of them have stood by the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 19:19; Exodus 14:21–22; Joshua 3:14–17"> And Elijah taketh his robe, and wrappeth &#91;it&#93; together, and smiteth the waters, and they are halved, hither and thither, and they pass over both of them on dry land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 11:17; Deuteronomy 21:17; John 16:7"> And it cometh to pass, at their passing over, that Elijah hath said unto Elisha, 'Ask, what do I do for thee before I am taken from thee?' and Elisha saith, 'Then let there be, I pray thee, a double portion of thy spirit unto me;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 11:22–24; 2 Kings 2:12; John 16:24"> and he saith, 'Thou hast asked a hard thing; if thou dost see me taken from thee, it is to thee so; and if not, it is not.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 6:17; Psalms 104:3–4; Hebrews 1:14"> And it cometh to pass, they are going, going on and speaking, and lo, a chariot of fire, and horses of fire, and they separate between them both, and Elijah goeth up in a whirlwind, to the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 13:14; Isaiah 37:21; Mark 16:19"> And Elisha is seeing, and he is crying, 'My father, my father, the chariot of Israel, and its horsemen;' and he hath not seen him again; and he taketh hold on his garments, and rendeth them into two pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 19:19; 2 Kings 2:8; 1 Kings 9:26"> And he taketh up the robe of Elijah, that fell from off him, and turneth back and standeth on the edge of the Jordan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 14:12; Psalms 42:10; Psalms 115:2"> and he taketh the robe of Elijah that fell from off him, and smiteth the waters, and saith, 'Where &#91;is&#93; Jehovah, God of Elijah — even He?' and he smiteth the waters, and they are halved, hither and thither, and Elisha passeth over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 4:14; Acts 1:8; Joshua 3:7"> And they see him — the sons of the prophets who &#91;are&#93; in Jericho — over-against, and they say, 'Rested hath the spirit of Elijah on Elisha;' and they come to meet him, and bow themselves to him to the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 18:12; Acts 8:39; 2 Corinthians 12:2–3"> and say unto him, 'Lo, we pray thee, there are with thy servants fifty men, sons of valour: let them go, we pray thee, and they seek thy lord, lest the Spirit of Jehovah hath taken him up, and doth cast him on one of the hills, or into one of the valleys;' and he saith, 'Ye do not send.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 8:11; Hebrews 11:5; Romans 10:2"> And they press upon him, till he is ashamed, and he saith, 'Send ye;' and they send fifty men, and they seek three days, and have not found him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and they turn back unto him — and he is abiding in Jericho — and he saith unto them, 'Did I not say unto you, Do not go?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 23:26; Deuteronomy 28:2–4; Deuteronomy 28:11"> And the men of the city say unto Elisha, 'Lo, we pray thee, the site of the city &#91;is&#93; good, as my lord seeth, and the waters &#91;are&#93; bad, and the earth sterile.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 9:45; Zephaniah 2:9; Ezekiel 47:11"> And he saith, 'Bring to me a new dish, and place there salt;' and they bring &#91;it&#93; unto him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 15:25–26; 2 Kings 4:41; Leviticus 2:13"> and he goeth out unto the source of the waters, and casteth there salt, and saith, 'Thus said Jehovah, I have given healing to these waters; there is not thence any more death and sterility.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And the waters are healed unto this day, according to the word of Elisha, that he spake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:16; Job 19:18; Job 30:1"> And he goeth up thence to Beth-El, and he is going up in the way, and little youths have come out from the city, and scoff at him, and say to him, 'Go up, bald-head! go up, bald-head!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 13:9–11; Acts 5:5; Hosea 13:8"> And he looketh behind him, and seeth them, and declareth them vile in the name of Jehovah, and two bears come out of the forest, and rend of them forty and two lads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 4:25; 1 Kings 18:19–20; 1 Kings 18:42"> And he goeth thence unto the hill of Carmel, and thence he hath turned back to Samaria.</span>
