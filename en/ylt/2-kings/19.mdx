<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 21:27; 2 Kings 18:37; 2 Chronicles 32:20–22"> And it cometh to pass, at king Hezekiah's hearing, that he rendeth his garments, and covereth himself with sackcloth, and entereth the house of Jehovah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 1:1; Isaiah 2:1; Matthew 4:14"> and sendeth Eliakim, who &#91;is&#93; over the house, and Shebna the scribe, and the elders of the priests, covering themselves with sackcloth, unto Isaiah the prophet, son of Amoz,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:5–7; Isaiah 66:9; Hosea 13:13"> and they say unto him, 'Thus said Hezekiah — A day of distress, and rebuke, and despising &#91;is&#93; this day; for come have sons unto the birth, and power there is not to bring forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:12; Romans 9:27; Joshua 14:12"> 'It may be Jehovah thy God doth hear all the words of the chief of the butlers with which the king of Asshur his lord hath sent him to reproach the living God, and hath decided concerning the words that Jehovah thy God hath heard, and thou hast lifted up prayer for the remnant that is found.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the servants of king Hezekiah come in unto Isaiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 18:35; 2 Kings 18:17; Isaiah 37:6–38"> and Isaiah saith to them, 'Thus do ye say unto your lord: Thus said Jehovah, Be not afraid because of the words that thou hast heard, with which the servants of the king of Asshur have reviled Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Obadiah 1:1; 2 Kings 7:6; Psalms 18:14–15"> Lo, I am giving in him a spirit, and he hath heard a report, and hath turned back to his land, and I have caused him to fall by the sword in his land.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 18:14; Joshua 10:29; Joshua 12:11"> And the chief of the butlers turneth back and findeth the king of Asshur fighting against Libnah, for he hath heard that he hath journeyed from Lachish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:27; Isaiah 37:9; 2 Kings 18:17"> And he heareth concerning Tirhakah king of Cush, saying, 'Lo, he hath come out to fight with thee;' and he turneth and sendeth messengers unto Hezekiah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 18:5; 2 Kings 18:29–30; 2 Chronicles 32:15–19"> 'Thus do ye speak unto Hezekiah king of Judah, saying, Let not thy God lift thee up in whom thou art trusting, saying, Jerusalem is not given into the hand of the king of Asshur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 10:8–11; 2 Chronicles 32:13–14; 2 Kings 19:17–18"> Lo, thou hast heard that which the kings of Asshur have done to all the lands — to devote them; and thou art delivered!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 17:6; Genesis 11:31; Ezekiel 27:23"> did the gods of the nations deliver them whom my fathers destroyed — Gozan, and Haran, and Rezeph, and the sons of Eden, who &#91;are&#93; in Thelassar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 18:34; 2 Kings 17:24; Isaiah 11:11"> Where &#91;is&#93; the king of Hamath, and the king of Arpad, and the king of the city of Sepharvaim, Hena, and Ivvah?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 37:14; Psalms 91:1–2; Psalms 123:1–4"> And Hezekiah taketh the letters out of the hand of the messengers, and readeth them, and goeth up to the house of Jehovah, and Hezekiah spreadeth it before Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; 2 Kings 5:15; Daniel 4:34–35"> And Hezekiah prayeth before Jehovah, and saith, 'O Jehovah, God of Israel, inhabiting the cherubs, Thou &#91;art&#93; God Himself — Thyself alone — to all the kingdoms of the earth: Thou hast made the heavens and the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:40; Psalms 31:2; 2 Kings 19:4"> Incline, O Jehovah, Thine ear, and hear; open, O Jehovah, Thine eyes, and see; and hear Thou the words of Sennacherib with which he hath sent him to reproach the living God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:26; Job 9:2; 1 Corinthians 14:25"> 'Truly, O Jehovah, kings of Asshur have laid waste the nations, and their land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 17:29; Isaiah 44:9–20; Jeremiah 10:3–9"> and have put their gods into fire, for they &#91;are&#93; no gods, but work of the hands of man, wood and stone, and destroy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 83:18; 1 Kings 8:43; Psalms 67:1–2"> And now, O Jehovah our God, save us, we pray Thee, out of his hand, and know do all kingdoms of the earth that Thou &#91;art&#93; Jehovah God — Thyself alone.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 20:5; 2 Chronicles 32:20–21; Jeremiah 33:3"> And Isaiah son of Amoz sendeth unto Hezekiah, saying, 'Thus said Jehovah, God of Israel, That which thou hast prayed unto Me concerning Sennacherib king of Asshur I have heard:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Lamentations 2:13; Job 16:4; Jeremiah 14:17"> this &#91;is&#93; the word that Jehovah spake concerning him: 'Trampled on thee — laughed at thee, Hath the virgin daughter of Zion Behind thee shaken the head — Hath the daughter of Jerusalem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 5:24; Psalms 71:22; Isaiah 30:11–12"> Whom hast thou reproached and reviled? And against whom lifted up a voice? Yea, thou dost lift up on high thine eyes — Against the Holy One of Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 18:17; Psalms 20:7; Isaiah 10:18"> By the hand of thy messengers Thou hast reproached the Lord, and sayest: In the multitude of my chariots I have come up to a high place of mountains — The sides of Lebanon, And I cut down the height of its cedars, The choice of its firs, And I enter the lodging of its extremity, The forest of its Carmel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 19:6; Exodus 15:9; 2 Samuel 17:13"> I have digged, and drunk strange waters, And I dry up with the sole of my steps All floods of a bulwark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Isaiah 10:5–6; Isaiah 37:26–27"> Hast thou not heard from afar, it I made, From days of old that I formed it? Now I have brought it in, And it becometh a desolation, Ruinous heaps &#91;are&#93; fenced cities,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:30; Psalms 102:11; Jeremiah 51:32"> And their inhabitants &#91;are&#93; feeble-handed, They were broken down, and are dried up, They have been the herb of the field, And the greenness of the tender grass, Grass of the roofs, And blasted corn — before it hath risen up!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 37:28–29; Jeremiah 23:23–24; Psalms 139:1–11"> And thy sitting down, and thy going out, And thy coming in, I have known, And thine anger towards Me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:4; Ezekiel 38:4; 2 Kings 19:33"> Because of thine anger towards Me, And thy noise — it came up into Mine ears, I have put My hook in thy nose, And My bridle in thy lips, And have caused thee to turn back, In the way in which thou camest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 25:20–22; 2 Kings 20:8–9; Exodus 3:12"> And this to thee &#91;is&#93; the sign, Food of the year &#91;is&#93; the spontaneous growth, And in the second year the self-produced, And in the third year sow ye, and reap, And plant vineyards, and eat their fruits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 19:4; Isaiah 27:6; 2 Chronicles 32:22–23"> And it hath continued — The escaped of the house of Judah That hath been left — to take root beneath, And hath made fruit upward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 9:7; Romans 11:5; Romans 9:27"> For from Jerusalem goeth out a remnant, And an escape from mount Zion; The zeal of Jehovah &#91;of Hosts&#93; doth this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–10; 2 Samuel 20:15; Isaiah 37:33–35"> Therefore, thus said Jehovah, Concerning the king of Asshur: He doth not come in unto this city, Nor doth he shoot there an arrow, Nor doth he come before it with shield. Nor doth he pour out against it a mount.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Kings 19:28; 2 Kings 19:36"> In the way that he cometh in — In it he turneth back, And unto this city he doth not come in, The affirmation of Jehovah —</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 11:12–13; 2 Kings 20:6; 1 Kings 15:4"> And I have covered over this city, To save it, for Mine own sake, And for the sake of David My servant.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:21–22; 2 Samuel 24:16; Exodus 12:29–30"> And it cometh to pass, in that night, that a messenger of Jehovah goeth out, and smiteth in the camp of Asshur a hundred eighty and five thousand, and they rise early in the morning, and lo, all of them &#91;are&#93; dead corpses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jonah 1:2; 2 Kings 19:7; 2 Kings 19:33"> And Sennacherib king of Asshur journeyeth, and goeth, and turneth back, and dwelleth in Nineveh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Ezra 4:2; Genesis 8:4; 2 Kings 19:7"> and it cometh to pass, he is bowing himself in the house of Nisroch his god, and Adramelech and Sharezar &#91;his sons&#93; have smitten him with the sword, and they have escaped to the land of Ararat, and Esar-Haddon his son reigneth in his stead.</span>
