<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 1:1; Jeremiah 25:1; Jeremiah 25:9"> In his days hath Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon come up, and Jehoiakim is to him a servant three years; and he turneth and rebelleth against him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 35:11; 2 Kings 23:27; 2 Kings 21:12–14"> and Jehovah sendeth against him the troops of the Chaldeans, and the troops of Aram, and the troops of Moab, and the troops of the sons of Ammon, and He sendeth them against Judah to destroy it, according to the word of Jehovah, that He spake by the hand of His servants the prophets;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 18:25; 2 Kings 21:2–11; 2 Kings 23:26–27"> only, by the command of Jehovah it hath been against Judah to turn &#91;them&#93; aside from His presence, for the sins of Manasseh, according to all that he did,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 21:16; Psalms 106:38; Numbers 35:33"> and also the innocent blood that he hath shed, and he filleth Jerusalem with innocent blood, and Jehovah was not willing to forgive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:8; Jeremiah 26:1–24; Jeremiah 22:13–17"> And the rest of the matters of Jehoiakim, and all that he did, are they not written on the book of the Chronicles of the kings of Judah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:18–19; 2 Chronicles 36:6; Jeremiah 36:30"> And Jehoiakim lieth with his fathers, and Jehoiachin his son reigneth in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:5–7; Jeremiah 46:2; Genesis 15:18"> And the king of Egypt hath not added any more to go out from his own land, for the king of Babylon hath taken, from the brook of Egypt unto the river Phrat, all that had been to the king of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:9; 1 Chronicles 3:16; Jeremiah 37:1"> A son of eighteen years &#91;is&#93; Jehoiachin in his reigning, and three months he hath reigned in Jerusalem, and the name of his mother &#91;is&#93; Nehushta, daughter of Elnathan of Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> and he doth the evil thing in the eyes of Jehovah, according to all that his fathers did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 25:2; Daniel 1:1–2"> At that time come up have servants of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon to Jerusalem, and the city goeth into siege,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon cometh against the city, and his servants are laying siege to it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:1; Jeremiah 52:28; 2 Kings 25:27"> and Jehoiachin king of Judah goeth out unto the king of Babylon, he, and his mother, and his servants, and his chiefs, and his eunuchs, and the king of Babylon taketh him in the eighth year of his reign,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 39:6; 2 Kings 20:17; Jeremiah 20:5"> and bringeth out thence all the treasures of the house of Jehovah, and the treasures of the house of the king, and cutteth in pieces all the vessels of gold that Solomon king of Israel made in the temple of Jehovah, as Jehovah had spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:28; 2 Kings 25:12; 2 Kings 24:16"> And he hath removed all Jerusalem, and all the chiefs, and all the mighty ones of valour — ten thousand &#91;is&#93; the removal — and every artificer and smith, none hath been left save the poor of the people of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:10; Jeremiah 22:24–28; Esther 2:6"> And he removeth Jehoiachin to Babylon, and the mother of the king, and the wives of the king, and his eunuchs, and the mighty ones of the land — he hath caused a removal to go from Jerusalem to Babylon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:28; 2 Kings 24:14; Jeremiah 29:2"> and all the men of valour seven thousand, and the artificers and the smiths a thousand, the whole &#91;are&#93; mighty men, warriors; and the king of Babylon bringeth them in a captivity to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:1; 2 Chronicles 36:4; 2 Kings 23:34"> And the king of Babylon causeth Mattaniah his father's brother to reign in his stead, and turneth his name to Zedekiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 23:31; Jeremiah 52:1–11; Jeremiah 37:1"> A son of twenty and one years &#91;is&#93; Zedekiah in his reigning, and eleven years he hath reigned in Jerusalem, and the name of his mother &#91;is&#93; Hamutal daughter of Jeremiah of Libnah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 23:37; Jeremiah 24:8; Ezekiel 21:25"> and he doth the evil thing in the eyes of Jehovah according to all that Jehoiakim did,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:13; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11; Jeremiah 38:17–21"> for, by the anger of Jehovah it hath been against Jerusalem and against Judah, till he cast them out from His presence, that Zedekiah rebelleth against the king of Babylon.</span>
