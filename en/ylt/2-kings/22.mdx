<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 15:39; 2 Chronicles 34:1–33; Jeremiah 1:2"> A son of eight years &#91;is&#93; Josiah in his reigning, and thirty and one years he hath reigned in Jerusalem, and the name of his mother &#91;is&#93; Jedidah daughter of Adaiah of Boskath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:32; Joshua 1:7; 2 Kings 18:3"> and he doth that which is right in the eyes of Jehovah, and walketh in all the way of David his father, and hath not turned aside — right or left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:3–33"> And it cometh to pass, in the eighteenth year of king Josiah, the king hath sent Shaphan son of Azaliah, son of Meshullam, the scribe, to the house of Jehovah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 12:4; Mark 12:41–42; 2 Chronicles 34:9–18"> 'Go up unto Hilkiah the high priest, and he doth complete the silver that is brought into the house of Jehovah, that the keepers of the threshold have gathered from the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 12:11–14; 2 Kings 12:5; 2 Chronicles 24:12–13"> and they give it into the hand of the doers of the work, the overseers, in the house of Jehovah, and they give it to the doers of the work that &#91;is&#93; in the house of Jehovah, to strengthen the breach of the house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> to artificers, and to builders, and &#91;to repairers of&#93; the wall, and to buy wood and hewn stones to strengthen the house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 12:15; 1 Corinthians 4:2; Nehemiah 7:2"> only, the silver that is given into their hand is not reckoned with them, for in faithfulness they are dealing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:24–26; 2 Chronicles 34:14–28"> And Hilkiah the high priest saith unto Shaphan the scribe, 'A book of the law I have found in the house of Jehovah;' and Hilkiah giveth the book unto Shaphan, and he readeth it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:11; 2 Kings 25:22; 2 Kings 18:18"> And Shaphan the scribe cometh in unto the king, and bringeth the king back word, and saith, 'Thy servants have poured out the silver that hath been found in the house, and give it into the hand of the doers of the work, the inspectors, in the house of Jehovah.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:21; Jeremiah 13:18; Deuteronomy 17:18–20"> And Shaphan the scribe declareth to the king, saying, 'A book hath Hilkiah the priest given to me;' and Shaphan readeth it before the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:19; Joel 2:13; 2 Kings 22:19"> And it cometh to pass, at the king's hearing the words of the book of the law, that he rendeth his garments,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:24; 2 Kings 25:22; 2 Kings 19:2–3"> and the king commandeth Hilkiah the priest, and Ahikam son of Shaphan, and Achbor son of Michaiah, and Shaphan the scribe, and Asahiah servant of the king, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:23–28; Deuteronomy 31:17–18; Jeremiah 37:17"> 'Go, seek Jehovah for me, and for the people, and for all Judah, concerning the words of this book that is found, for great &#91;is&#93; the fury of Jehovah that is kindled against us, because that our fathers have not hearkened unto the words of this book, to do according to all that is written for us.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 4:4; 2 Chronicles 34:22; Micah 6:4"> And Hilkiah the priest goeth, and Ahikam, and Achbor, and Shaphan, and Asahiah, unto Huldah the prophetess, wife of Shallum, son of Tikvah, son of Harhas, keeper of the garments, and she is dwelling in Jerusalem in the second, and they speak unto her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 1:6; 2 Kings 1:16; Jeremiah 23:28"> And she saith unto them, 'Thus said Jehovah, God of Israel, Say to the man who hath sent you unto me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 9:11–14; Joshua 23:15; Deuteronomy 29:27"> Thus said Jehovah, Lo, I am bringing in evil unto this place and on its inhabitants, all the words of the book that the king of Judah hath read,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 21:22; Jeremiah 7:20; Deuteronomy 29:24–28"> because that they have forsaken Me, and make perfume to other gods, so as to provoke Me to anger with every work of their hands, and My wrath hath been kindled against this place, and it is not quenched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:26–28; Malachi 3:16–17; Isaiah 3:10"> 'And unto the king of Judah, who is sending you to seek Jehovah, thus do ye say unto him, Thus said Jehovah, God of Israel, The words that thou hast heard —</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 51:17; 1 Kings 21:29; Isaiah 57:15"> because thy heart &#91;is&#93; tender, and thou art humbled because of Jehovah, in thy hearing that which I have spoken against this place, and against its inhabitants, to be for a desolation, and for a reviling, and dost rend thy garments, and weep before Me — I also have heard — the affirmation of Jehovah —</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 37:37; Isaiah 57:1–2; 1 Chronicles 17:11"> therefore, lo, I am gathering thee unto thy fathers, and thou hast been gathered unto thy grave in peace, and thine eyes do not look on any of the evil that I am bringing in on this place;' and they bring the king back word.</span>
