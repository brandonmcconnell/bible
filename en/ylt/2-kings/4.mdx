<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 18:25; 2 Kings 2:3; Nehemiah 5:2–5"> And a certain woman of the wives of the sons of the prophets hath cried unto Elisha, saying, 'Thy servant, my husband, is dead, and thou hast known that thy servant was fearing Jehovah, and the lender hath come to take my two children to him for servants.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 17:12; James 2:5; 2 Kings 6:26–27"> And Elisha saith unto her, 'What do I do for thee? declare to me, what hast thou in the house?' and she saith, 'Thy maid-servant hath nothing in the house except a pot of oil.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 2:7; Psalms 81:10; John 16:24"> And he saith, 'Go, ask for thee vessels from without, from all thy neighbours — empty vessels — let &#91;them&#93; not be few;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 8:5–9; Matthew 6:6; Ephesians 3:20"> and thou hast entered, and shut the door upon thee, and upon thy sons, and hast poured out into all these vessels, and the full ones thou dost remove.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 5:11; Hebrews 11:7–8; 1 Kings 17:15–16"> And she goeth from him, and shutteth the door upon her, and upon her sons; they are bringing nigh unto her, and she is pouring out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 14:20; 2 Kings 13:19; Luke 6:19"> and it cometh to pass, at the filling of the vessels, that she saith unto her son, 'Bring nigh unto me a vessel more,' and he saith unto her, 'There is not a vessel more;' and the oil stayeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:7–12; 1 Kings 12:22; Romans 12:17"> And she cometh and declareth to the man of God, and he saith, 'Go, sell the oil, and repay thy loan; and thou &#91;and&#93; thy sons do live of the rest.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 19:18; Genesis 19:3; 2 Kings 4:11–12"> And the day cometh that Elisha passeth over unto Shunem, and there &#91;is&#93; a great woman, and she layeth hold on him to eat bread, and it cometh to pass, at the time of his passing over, he turneth aside thither to eat bread,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 17:24; Proverbs 31:10–11; 2 Kings 4:7"> and she saith unto her husband, 'Lo, I pray thee, I have known that a holy man of God he is, passing over by us continually;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:13; Matthew 10:41–42; Matthew 25:40"> let us make, I pray thee, a little upper chamber of the wall, and we set for him there a bed, and a table, and a high seat, and a candlestick; and it hath been, in his coming in unto us, he doth turn aside thither.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the day cometh, that he cometh in thither, and turneth aside unto the upper chamber, and lieth there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 8:4–5; 2 Kings 3:11; 2 Kings 4:29–31"> and he saith unto Gehazi his young man, 'Call for this Shunammite;' and he calleth for her, and she standeth before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:16–18; Philippians 4:18–19; Hebrews 6:10"> And he saith to him, 'Say, I pray thee, unto her, Lo, thou hast troubled thyself concerning us with all this trouble; what — to do for thee? is it to speak for thee unto the king, or unto the head of the host?' and she saith, 'In the midst of my people I am dwelling.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 1:7; Genesis 15:2–3; 1 Samuel 1:8"> And he saith, 'And what — to do for her?' and Gehazi saith, 'Verily she hath no son, and her husband &#91;is&#93; aged.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And he saith, 'Call for her;' and he calleth for her, and she standeth at the opening,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 18:10; Luke 1:13; 2 Kings 4:28"> and he saith, 'At this season, according to the time of life, thou art embracing a son;' and she saith, 'Nay, my lord, O man of God, do not lie unto thy maid-servant.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 113:9; Luke 1:36; Genesis 21:1"> And the woman conceiveth and beareth a son, at this season, according to the time of life, that Elisha spake of unto her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ruth 2:4"> And the lad groweth, and the day cometh that he goeth out unto his father, unto the reapers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 14:1–2; Jeremiah 4:19"> and he saith unto his father, 'My head, my head;' and he saith unto the young man, 'Bear him unto his mother;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 7:12; John 11:3; John 11:14"> and he beareth him, and bringeth him in unto his mother, and he sitteth on her knees till the noon, and dieth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 4:32; 2 Kings 4:10; 2 Kings 4:7"> And she goeth up, and layeth him on the bed of the man of God, and shutteth &#91;the door&#93; upon him, and goeth out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 9:38; 2 Kings 4:26; John 11:3"> and calleth unto her husband, and saith, 'Send, I pray thee, to me, one of the young men, and one of the asses, and I run unto the man of God, and return.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 10:10; Numbers 28:11; 1 Chronicles 23:31"> And he saith, 'Wherefore art thou going unto him to-day? — neither new moon nor sabbath!' and she saith, 'Peace &#91;to thee&#93;!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 4:20; 1 Kings 13:13; 1 Samuel 25:19–20"> And she saddleth the ass, and saith unto her young man, 'Lead, and go, do not restrain riding for me, except I have said &#91;so&#93; to thee.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 2:25; Isaiah 35:2; 1 Kings 18:19"> And she goeth, and cometh in unto the man of God, unto the hill of Carmel, and it cometh to pass, at the man of God's seeing her from over-against, that he saith unto Gehazi his young man, 'Lo, this Shunammite;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 15:36; 2 Kings 4:23; 1 Samuel 17:18"> now, run, I pray thee, to meet her, and say to her, Is there peace to thee? is there peace to thy husband? is there peace to the lad?' and she saith, 'Peace.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:3; Genesis 18:17; Amos 3:7"> And she cometh in unto the man of God, unto the hill, and layeth hold on his feet, and Gehazi cometh nigh to thrust her away, and the man of God saith, 'Let her alone, for her soul &#91;is&#93; bitter to her, and Jehovah hath hidden &#91;it&#93; from me, and hath not declared &#91;it&#93; to me.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 30:1; 2 Kings 4:16"> And she saith, 'Did I ask a son from my lord? did I not say, Do not deceive me?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Kings 2:14; 1 Kings 18:46; Exodus 14:16"> And he saith to Gehazi, 'Gird up thy loins, and take my staff in thy hand, and go; when thou dost meet a man, thou dost not salute him; and when a man doth salute thee, thou dost not answer him; and thou hast laid my staff on the face of the youth.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ruth 1:16–18; 2 Kings 2:2; Exodus 33:12–16"> And the mother of the youth saith, 'Jehovah liveth, and thy soul liveth — if I leave thee;' and he riseth and goeth after her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 11:11; Mark 5:39; Mark 9:19–29"> And Gehazi hath passed on before them, and layeth the staff on the face of the youth, and there is no voice, and there is no attention, and he turneth back to meet him, and declareth to him, saying, 'The youth hath not awaked.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 8:52–53; John 11:17; 1 Kings 17:17"> And Elisha cometh in to the house, and lo, the youth is dead, laid on his bed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 6:6; 2 Kings 4:4; Acts 9:40"> and he goeth in and shutteth the door upon them both, and prayeth unto Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 17:21; Acts 20:10"> And he goeth up, and lieth down on the lad, and putteth his mouth on his mouth, and his eyes on his eyes, and his hands on his hands, and stretcheth himself upon him, and the flesh of the lad becometh warm;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Kings 8:5; 2 Kings 13:21; 1 Kings 17:22"> and he turneth back and walketh in the house, once hither and once thither, and goeth up and stretcheth himself upon him, and the youth sneezeth till seven times, and the youth openeth his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Hebrews 11:35; 1 Kings 17:23; Luke 7:15"> And he calleth unto Gehazi, and saith, 'Call unto this Shunammite;' and he calleth her, and she cometh in unto him, and he saith, 'Lift up thy son.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Hebrews 11:35; 2 Kings 2:15; 1 Kings 17:24"> And she goeth in, and falleth at his feet, and boweth herself to the earth, and lifteth up her son, and goeth out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Kings 8:1; 2 Kings 2:3; 2 Kings 2:1"> And Elisha hath turned back to Gilgal, and the famine &#91;is&#93; in the land, and the sons of the prophets are sitting before him, and he saith to his young man, 'Set on the great pot, and boil pottage for the sons of the prophets.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:21; Isaiah 5:4; Matthew 15:13"> And one goeth out unto the field to gather herbs, and findeth a vine of the field, and gathereth of it gourds of the field — the fulness of his garment — and cometh in and splitteth &#91;them&#93; into the pot of pottage, for they knew &#91;them&#93; not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 16:18; Exodus 15:23; Exodus 10:17"> and they pour out for the men to eat, and it cometh to pass at their eating of the pottage, that they have cried out, and say, 'Death &#91;is&#93; in the pot, O man of God!' and they have not been able to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Kings 2:21; Exodus 15:25; 1 Corinthians 1:25"> And he saith, 'Then bring ye meal;' and he casteth into the pot, and saith, 'Pour out for the people, and they eat;' and there was no evil thing in the pot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:7; John 6:9; Matthew 14:16–21"> And a man hath come from Baal-Shalishah, and bringeth in to the man of God bread of first-fruits, twenty loaves of barley, and full ears of corn in its husk, and he saith, 'Give to the people, and they eat.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 9:13; Matthew 14:20; John 6:9"> And his minister saith, 'What — do I give this before a hundred men?' and he saith, 'Give to the people, and they eat, for thus said Jehovah, Eat and leave;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> and he giveth before them, and they eat and leave, according to the word of Jehovah.</span>
