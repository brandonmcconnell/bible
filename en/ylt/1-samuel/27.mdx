<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:10; 1 Samuel 25:13; 1 Kings 2:39–40"> And David riseth, and passeth over, he and six hundred men who &#91;are&#93; with him, unto Achish son of Maoch king of Gath;</span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:21"> And it is declared to Saul that David hath fled to Gath, and he hath not added any more to seek him.</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:28; Joshua 15:31; 1 Chronicles 12:20"> And Achish giveth to him in that day Ziklag, therefore hath Ziklag been to the kings of Judah till this day.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:3; Joshua 6:21; Deuteronomy 25:17–19"> and David hath smitten the land, and doth not keep alive man and woman, and hath taken sheep, and oxen, and asses, and camels, and garments, and turneth back, and cometh in unto Achish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 1:16; 1 Chronicles 2:9; 1 Chronicles 2:25"> And Achish saith, 'Whither have ye pushed to-day?' and David saith, 'Against the south of Judah, and against the south of the Jerahmeelite, and unto the south of the Kenite.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 29:25; Proverbs 12:19; 1 Samuel 22:22"> Neither man nor woman doth David keep alive, to bring in &#91;word&#93; to Gath, saying, 'Lest they declare &#91;it&#93; against us, saying, Thus hath David done, and thus &#91;is&#93; his custom all the days that he hath dwelt in the fields of the Philistines.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; 1 Samuel 13:4"> And Achish believeth in David, saying, 'He hath made himself utterly abhorred among his people, in Israel, and hath been to me for a servant age-during.'</span>
