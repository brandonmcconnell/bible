<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:18–19; 1 Timothy 5:21; Nehemiah 7:2"> And it cometh to pass, when Samuel &#91;is&#93; aged, that he maketh his sons judges over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:38; Genesis 22:19; 1 Chronicles 6:28"> And the name of his first-born son is Joel, and the name of his second Abiah, judges in Beer-Sheba:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Psalms 15:5; 1 Timothy 6:10"> and his sons have not walked in his ways, and turn aside after the dishonest gain, and take a bribe, and turn aside judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:17; 2 Samuel 5:3; Exodus 3:16"> And all the elders of Israel gather themselves together, and come in unto Samuel to Ramath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:14–15; Hosea 13:10–11; 1 Samuel 8:6–8"> and say unto him, 'Lo, thou hast become aged, and thy sons have not walked in thy ways; now, appoint to us a king, to judge us, like all the nations.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:11; James 1:5; 1 Samuel 12:17"> And the thing is evil in the eyes of Samuel, when they have said, 'Give to us a king to judge us;' and Samuel prayeth unto Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 10:16; 1 Samuel 10:19; Exodus 16:8"> And Jehovah saith unto Samuel, 'Hearken to the voice of the people, to all that they say unto thee, for thee they have not rejected, but Me they have rejected, from reigning over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 106:34–40; Exodus 32:1; Judges 13:1"> According to all the works that they have done from the day of My bringing them up out of Egypt, even unto this day, when they forsake Me, and serve other gods — so they are doing also to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:25; 1 Samuel 8:11–18; Ezekiel 46:18"> And now, hearken to their voice; only, surely thou dost certainly protest to them, and hast declared to them the custom of the king who doth reign over them.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Samuel speaketh all the words of Jehovah unto the people who are asking from him a king,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:52; Deuteronomy 17:14–20; 1 Samuel 10:25"> and saith, 'This is the custom of the king who doth reign over you: Your sons he doth take, and hath appointed for himself among his chariots, and among his horsemen, and they have run before his chariots;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:7; 1 Kings 4:7; 1 Chronicles 27:1–22"> also to appoint for himself heads of thousands, and heads of fifties; also to plow his plowing, and to reap his reaping; and to make instruments of his war, and instruments of his charioteer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> 'And your daughters he doth take for perfumers, and for cooks, and for bakers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:18; 1 Kings 21:7; 1 Kings 21:19"> and your fields, and your vineyards, and your olive-yards — the best — he doth take, and hath given to his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 1:3; Isaiah 39:7; Genesis 37:36"> And your seed and your vineyards he doth tithe, and hath given to his eunuchs, and to his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And your men-servants, and your maid-servants, and your young men — the best, and your asses, he doth take, and hath prepared for his own work;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> your flock he doth tithe, and ye are to him for servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Micah 3:4; Isaiah 1:15; Proverbs 1:25–28"> And ye have cried out in that day because of the king whom ye have chosen for yourselves, and Jehovah doth not answer you in that day.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:16; Jeremiah 7:13; Isaiah 66:4"> And the people refuse to hearken to the voice of Samuel, and say, 'Nay, but a king is over us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:5; Psalms 106:35; Deuteronomy 7:6"> and we have been, even we, like all the nations; and our king hath judged us, and gone out before us, and fought our battles.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 11:11"> And Samuel heareth all the words of the people, and speaketh them in the ears of Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:7; Hosea 13:11"> and Jehovah saith unto Samuel, 'Hearken to their voice, and thou hast caused to reign over them a king.' And Samuel saith unto the men of Israel, 'Go ye each to his city.'</span>
