<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:17; Proverbs 18:24; 1 Samuel 18:3"> And it cometh to pass, when he finisheth to speak unto Saul, that the soul of Jonathan hath been bound to the soul of David, and Jonathan loveth him as his own soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:15; 1 Samuel 16:21–23"> And Saul taketh him on that day, and hath not permitted him to turn back to the house of his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:8–17; 1 Samuel 20:42; 2 Samuel 21:7"> And Jonathan maketh — also David — a covenant, because he loveth him as his own soul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 41:42; Luke 15:22; Esther 6:8–9"> and Jonathan strippeth himself of the upper robe which &#91;is&#93; upon him, and giveth it to David, and his long robe, even unto his sword, and unto his bow, and unto his girdle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:30; 1 Samuel 18:14–15; Colossians 4:5"> And David goeth out whithersoever Saul doth send him; he acted wisely, and Saul setteth him over the men of war, and it is good in the eyes of all the people, and also in the eyes of the servants of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 15:20; Judges 11:34; Psalms 68:25"> And it cometh to pass, in their coming in, in David's returning from smiting the Philistine, that the women come out from all the cities of Israel to sing — also the dancers — to meet Saul the king, with tabrets, with joy, and with three-stringed instruments;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:11; 1 Samuel 29:5; Exodus 15:21"> and the women answer — those playing, and say, 'Saul hath smitten among his thousands, And David among his myriads.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:28; Proverbs 13:10; James 4:5"> And it is displeasing to Saul exceedingly, and this thing is evil in his eyes, and he saith, 'They have given to David myriads, and to me they have given the thousands, and more to him &#91;is&#93; only the kingdom;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 7:22; Genesis 31:2; Matthew 20:15"> and Saul is eyeing David from that day and thenceforth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:9; 2 Thessalonians 2:11; 1 Kings 18:29"> And it cometh to pass, on the morrow, that the spirit of sadness &#91;from&#93; God prospereth over Saul, and he prophesieth in the midst of the house, and David is playing with his hand, as day by day, and the javelin &#91;is&#93; in the hand of Saul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:33; Isaiah 54:17; Luke 4:30"> and Saul casteth the javelin, and saith, 'I smite through David, even through the wall;' and David turneth round out of his presence twice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:15; 1 Samuel 18:29; 1 Samuel 16:13–14"> And Saul is afraid of the presence of David, for Jehovah hath been with him, and from Saul He hath turned aside;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:2; Psalms 121:8; 1 Samuel 18:16–17"> and Saul turneth him aside from him, and appointeth him to himself head of a thousand, and he goeth out an cometh in, before the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 39:23; Matthew 28:20; 1 Samuel 16:18"> And David is in all his ways acting wisely, and Jehovah &#91;is&#93; with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 3:17; Colossians 4:5; James 1:5"> and Saul seeth that he is acting very wisely, and is afraid of him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:5; Numbers 27:17; 1 Kings 3:7"> and all Israel and Judah love David when he is going out and coming in before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:25; 1 Samuel 25:28; 1 Samuel 18:25"> And Saul saith unto David, 'Lo, my elder daughter Merab — her I give to thee for a wife; only, be to me for a son of valour, and fight the battles of Jehovah;' and Saul said, 'Let not my hand be on him, but let the hand of the Philistines be upon him.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:18; 1 Samuel 9:21; 1 Samuel 18:23"> And David saith unto Saul, 'Who &#91;am&#93; I? and what my life — the family of my father in Israel — that I am son-in-law to the king?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Judges 7:22; 2 Samuel 21:8; Judges 14:20"> And it cometh to pass, at the time of the giving of Merab daughter of Saul to David, that she hath been given to Adriel the Meholathite for a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:28"> And Michal daughter of Saul loveth David, and they declare to Saul, and the thing is right in his eyes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:17; Exodus 10:7; Jeremiah 9:8"> and Saul saith, 'I give her to him, and she is to him for a snare, and the hand of the Philistines is on him;' and Saul saith unto David, 'By the second — thou dost become my son-in-law to-day.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 36:1–3; Psalms 55:21; 2 Samuel 13:28–29"> And Saul commandeth his servants, 'Speak unto David gently, saying, Lo, the king hath delighted in thee, and all his servants have loved thee, and now, be son-in-law to the king.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 119:141; Proverbs 14:20; Ecclesiastes 9:15–16"> And the servants of Saul speak in the ears of David these words, and David saith, 'Is it a light thing in your eyes to be son-in-law to the king — and I a poor man, and lightly esteemed?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And the servants of Saul declare to him, saying, 'According to these words hath David spoken.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 34:12; 1 Samuel 18:17; 1 Samuel 14:24"> And Saul saith, 'Thus do ye say to David, There is no delight to the king in dowry, but in a hundred foreskins of the Philistines — to be avenged on the enemies of the king;' and Saul thought to cause David to fall by the hand of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:21"> And his servants declare to David these words, and the thing is right in the eyes of David, to be son-in-law to the king; and the days have not been full,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:14; Judges 14:19; 1 Samuel 18:13"> and David riseth and goeth, he and his men, and smiteth among the Philistines two hundred men, and David bringeth in their foreskins, and they set them before the king, to be son-in-law to the king; and Saul giveth to him Michal his daughter for a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:20; 1 Samuel 26:25; Genesis 37:8–11"> And Saul seeth and knoweth that Jehovah &#91;is&#93; with David, and Michal daughter of Saul hath loved him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:4; John 11:53; James 2:19"> and Saul addeth to be afraid of the presence of David yet; and Saul is an enemy with David all the days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:5; 2 Samuel 11:1; 1 Peter 2:4"> And the princes of the Philistines come out, and it cometh to pass from the time of their coming out, David hath acted more wisely than any of the servants of Saul, and his name is very precious.</span>
