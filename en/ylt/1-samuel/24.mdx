<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:28–29; Ezekiel 22:9; 1 Samuel 23:19"> And it cometh to pass when Saul hath turned back from after the Philistines, that they declare to him, saying, 'Lo, David &#91;is&#93; in the wilderness of En-gedi.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:2; Psalms 104:18; Psalms 37:32"> And Saul taketh three thousand chosen men out of all Israel, and goeth to seek David and his men, on the front of the rocks of the wild goats,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 3:24; Psalms 57:1–11; Psalms 142:1–7"> and he cometh in unto folds of the flock, on the way, and there &#91;is&#93; a cave, and Saul goeth in to cover his feet; and David and his men in the sides of the cave are abiding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:8–11; 1 Samuel 24:10; 1 Samuel 26:23"> And the men of David say unto him, 'Lo, the day of which Jehovah said unto thee, Lo, I am giving thine enemy into thy hand, and thou hast done to him as it is good in thine eyes;' and David riseth and cutteth off the skirt of the upper robe which &#91;is&#93; on Saul — gently.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:10; 2 Kings 22:19; 1 John 3:20–21"> And it cometh to pass afterwards that the heart of David smiteth him, because that he hath cut off the skirt which &#91;is&#93; on Saul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:14; Job 31:29–30; 1 Samuel 26:9–11"> and he saith to his men, 'Far be it from me, by Jehovah; I do not do this thing to my lord — to the anointed of Jehovah — to put forth my hand against him, for the anointed of Jehovah he &#91;is&#93;.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; Psalms 7:4; Romans 12:17–21"> And David subdueth his men by words, and hath not permitted them to rise against Saul; and Saul hath risen from the cave, and goeth on the way;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:23–24; Romans 13:7; Exodus 20:12"> and David riseth afterwards, and goeth out from the cave, and calleth after Saul, saying, 'My lord, O king!' And Saul looketh attentively behind him, and David boweth — face to the earth — and doth obeisance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:21–22; Proverbs 29:12; Proverbs 16:28"> And David saith to Saul, 'Why dost thou hear the words of man, saying, Lo, David is seeking thine evil?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 105:15; 1 Samuel 24:4; 1 Samuel 26:8–9"> Lo, this day have thine eyes seen how that Jehovah hath given thee to-day into my hand in the cave; and &#91;one&#93; said to slay thee, and &#91;mine eye&#93; hath pity on thee, and I say, I do not put forth my hand against my lord, for the anointed of Jehovah he &#91;is&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:20; 1 Samuel 23:14; 1 Samuel 23:23"> 'And, my father, see, yea see the skirt of thine upper robe in my hand; for by cutting off the skirt of thy upper robe, and I have not slain thee, know and see that there is not in my hand evil and transgression, and I have not sinned against thee, and thou art hunting my soul to take it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 43:1; Judges 11:27; Psalms 35:1"> 'Jehovah doth judge between me and thee, and Jehovah hath avenged me of thee, and my hand is not on thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 7:20; Matthew 7:16–18; Matthew 12:33–34"> as saith the simile of the ancients, From the wicked goeth out wickedness, and my hand is not on thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:20; 1 Samuel 17:43; 2 Samuel 9:8"> 'After whom hath the king of Israel come out? after whom art thou pursuing? — after a dead dog! after one flea!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 43:1; Psalms 35:1; Psalms 119:154"> And Jehovah hath been for judge, and hath judged between me and thee, yea, he seeth and pleadeth my cause, and doth deliver me out of thy hand.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:17; Genesis 33:4; Luke 21:15"> And it cometh to pass, when David completeth to speak these words unto Saul, that Saul saith, 'Is this thy voice, my son David?' and Saul lifteth up his voice, and weepeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; 1 Samuel 26:21; Genesis 38:26"> And he saith unto David, 'More righteous thou &#91;art&#93; than I; for thou hast done me good, and I have done thee evil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:23; Psalms 31:8; 1 Samuel 23:12"> and thou hast declared to-day how that thou hast done good with me, how that Jehovah shut me up into thy hand, and thou didst not slay me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 18:20; 1 Samuel 23:21; Proverbs 25:21–22"> and that a man doth find his enemy, and hath sent him away in a good manner; and Jehovah doth repay thee good for that which thou didst to me this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:17; Matthew 2:3–6; 2 Samuel 3:17–18"> 'And, now, lo, I have known that thou dost certainly reign, and the kingdom of Israel hath stood in thy hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 21:23; 2 Samuel 21:6–8; 1 Samuel 20:14–17"> and, now, swear to me by Jehovah — thou dost not cut off my seed after me, nor dost thou destroy my name from the house of my father.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:29; John 2:24; Matthew 10:16–17"> And David sweareth to Saul, and Saul goeth unto his house, and David and his men have gone up unto the fortress.</span>
