<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 15:44; Micah 6:15; Nehemiah 3:17–18"> And they declare to David, saying, 'Lo, the Philistines are fighting against Keilah, and they are spoiling the threshing-floors.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:4; 2 Samuel 5:19; 2 Samuel 5:23"> And David asketh at Jehovah, saying, 'Do I go? — and have I smitten among these Philistines?' And Jehovah saith unto David, 'Go, and thou hast smitten among the Philistines, and saved Keilah.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:5; Psalms 11:1; 1 Samuel 23:15"> And David's men say unto him, 'Lo, we here in Judah are afraid; and how much more when we go to Keilah, unto the ranks of the Philistines?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 7:7; Joshua 8:7; 2 Kings 3:18"> And David addeth again to ask at Jehovah, and Jehovah answereth him, and saith, 'Rise, go down to Keilah, for I am giving the Philistines into thy hand.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And David goeth, and his men, to Keilah, and fighteth with the Philistines, and leadeth away their cattle, and smiteth among them — a great smiting, and David saveth the inhabitants of Keilah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:20; 1 Samuel 14:3; 1 Samuel 14:18"> And it cometh to pass, in the fleeing of Abiathar son of Ahimelech unto David, to Keilah, an ephod came down in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:4–6; Job 20:5; 1 Samuel 26:8–9"> And it is declared to Saul that David hath come in to Keilah, and Saul saith, 'God hath made him known for my hand, for he hath been shut in, to enter into a city of doors and bar.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And Saul summoneth the whole of the people to battle, to go down to Keilah, to lay siege unto David and unto his men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:7; 1 Samuel 23:6; Numbers 27:21"> And David knoweth that against him Saul is devising the evil, and saith unto Abiathar the priest, 'Bring nigh the ephod.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 3:15–16; 1 Samuel 23:8; Genesis 18:24"> And David saith, 'Jehovah, God of Israel, Thy servant hath certainly heard that Saul is seeking to come in unto Keilah, to destroy the city on mine account.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:3; Psalms 50:15; Matthew 7:7–8"> Do the possessors of Keilah shut me up into his hand? doth Saul come down as Thy servant hath heard? Jehovah, God of Israel, declare, I pray Thee, to Thy servant.' And Jehovah saith, 'He doth come down.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:20; Psalms 31:8; Psalms 62:1"> And David saith, 'Do the possessors of Keilah shut me up, and my men, into the hand of Saul?' And Jehovah saith, 'They shut &#91;thee&#93; up.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:2; 1 Samuel 25:13; 1 Samuel 30:9–10"> And David riseth — and his men — about six hundred men, and they go out from Keilah, and go up and down where they go up and down; and to Saul it hath been declared that David hath escaped from Keilah, and he ceaseth to go out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 54:3–4; Psalms 32:7; Joshua 15:24"> And David abideth in the wilderness, in fortresses, and abideth in the hill-country, in the wilderness of Ziph; and Saul seeketh him all the days, and God hath not given him into his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And David seeth that Saul hath come out to seek his life, and David &#91;is&#93; in the wilderness of Ziph, in a forest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3–4; Hebrews 12:12–13; Job 4:3–4"> And Jonathan son of Saul riseth, and goeth unto David to the forest, and strengtheneth his hand in God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:31; 1 Samuel 24:20; Acts 28:16"> and saith unto him, 'Fear not, for the hand of Saul my father doth not find thee, and thou dost reign over Israel, and I am to thee for second, and also so knoweth Saul my father.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:7; 1 Samuel 20:42; 1 Samuel 18:3"> And they make a covenant both of them before Jehovah; and David abideth in the forest, and Jonathan hath gone to his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:1; 1 Samuel 26:3; Psalms 54:1–7"> And the Ziphites go up unto Saul to Gibeah, saying, 'Is not David hiding himself with us in fortresses, in the forest, in the height of Hachilah, which &#91;is&#93; on the south of the desolate place?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:12; Proverbs 29:26; Proverbs 11:23"> And, now, by all the desire of thy soul, O king, to come down, come down, and ours &#91;is&#93; to shut him up into the hand of the king.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:8; Isaiah 66:5; Micah 3:11"> And Saul saith, 'Blessed &#91;are&#93; ye of Jehovah, for ye have pity on me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 5:13"> go, I pray you, prepare yet, and know and see his place where his foot is; who hath seen him there? for &#91;one&#93; hath said unto me, He is very subtile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 3:15–16; Mark 14:1; Numbers 10:36"> And see and know of all the hiding-places where he hideth himself, and ye have turned back unto me prepared, and I have gone with you, and it hath been, if he is in the land, that I have searched him out through all the thousands of Judah.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:2; Joshua 15:55"> And they rise and go to Ziph before Saul, and David and his men &#91;are&#93; in the wilderness of Maon, in the plain, at the south of the desolate place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Judges 15:8; 1 Samuel 23:28"> And Saul and his men go to seek, and they declare to David, and he goeth down the rock, and abideth in the wilderness of Maon; and Saul heareth, and pursueth after David &#91;to&#93; the wilderness of Maon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 17:9; 2 Corinthians 1:8; Psalms 140:1–9"> And Saul goeth on this side of the mountain, and David and his men on that side of the mountain, and David is hastened to go from the face of Saul, and Saul and his men are compassing David and his men, to catch them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 19:9; Psalms 116:3; Deuteronomy 32:36"> And a messenger hath come in unto Saul, saying, 'Haste, and come, for the Philistines have pushed against the land.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Saul turneth back from pursuing after David, and goeth to meet the Philistines, therefore they have called that place 'The Rock of Divisions.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:2; Joshua 15:62; Ezekiel 47:10"> And David goeth up thence, and abideth in fortresses &#91;at&#93; En-gedi.</span>
