<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:7; 1 Samuel 29:11; 1 Samuel 27:8–10"> And it cometh to pass, in the coming in of David and his men to Ziklag, on the third day, that the Amalekites have pushed unto the south, and unto Ziklag, and smite Ziklag, and burn it with fire,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 27:11; Job 38:11; Psalms 76:10"> and they take captive the women who &#91;are&#93; in it; from small unto great they have not put any one to death, and they lead away, and go on their way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 34:19; 1 Peter 1:6–7; Revelation 3:9"> And David cometh in — and his men — unto the city, and lo, burnt with fire, and their wives, and their sons, and their daughters have been taken captive!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:4; Numbers 14:1; Judges 21:2"> And David lifteth up — and the people who &#91;are&#93; with him — their voice and weep, till that they have no power to weep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:42–43; 2 Samuel 2:2; 1 Samuel 1:2"> And the two wives of David have been taken captive, Ahinoam the Jezreelitess, and Abigail wife of Nabal the Carmelite;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 56:3–4; Psalms 56:11; Psalms 62:5"> and David hath great distress, for the people have said to stone him, for the soul of all the people hath been bitter, each for his sons and for his daughters; and David doth strengthen himself in Jehovah his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 2:26; 1 Samuel 22:20–21; 1 Samuel 23:2–9"> And David saith unto Abiathar the priest, son of Ahimelech, 'Bring nigh, I pray thee, to me the ephod;' and Abiathar bringeth nigh the ephod unto David,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:18; Proverbs 3:5–6; 1 Samuel 23:2"> and David asketh at Jehovah, saying, 'I pursue after this troop — do I overtake it?' And He saith to him, 'Pursue, for thou dost certainly overtake, and dost certainly deliver.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 27:2"> And David goeth on, he and six hundred men who &#91;are&#93; with him, and they come in unto the brook of Besor, and those left have stood still,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:9; 1 Samuel 30:21; 1 Samuel 14:31"> and David pursueth, he and four hundred men, (and two hundred men stand still who have been too faint to pass over the brook of Besor),</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 12:20–21; Deuteronomy 23:7; Matthew 25:35"> and they find a man, an Egyptian, in the field, and take him unto David, and give to him bread, and he eateth, and they cause him to drink water,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 15:19; 1 Samuel 14:27; 1 Samuel 30:13"> and give to him a piece of a bunch of dried figs, and two bunches of raisins, and he eateth, and his spirit returneth unto him, for he hath not eaten bread nor drunk water three days and three nights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 2:13; Proverbs 12:10; Job 31:13–15"> And David saith to him, 'Whose &#91;art&#93; thou? and whence &#91;art&#93; thou?' And he saith, 'An Egyptian youth I &#91;am&#93;, servant to a man, an Amalekite, and my lord forsaketh me, for I have been sick three days,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 1:38; 2 Samuel 8:18; 1 Kings 1:44"> we pushed &#91;to&#93; the south of the Cherethite, and against that which &#91;is&#93; to Judah, and against the south of Caleb, and Ziklag we burned with fire.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 2:12; Joshua 9:19–20; Ezekiel 17:16"> And David saith unto him, 'Dost thou bring me down unto this troop?' and he saith, 'Swear to me by God — thou dost not put me to death, nor dost thou shut me up into the hand of my lord — and I bring thee down unto this troop.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 11:10–13; 2 Samuel 13:28; Judges 16:23–30"> And he bringeth him down, and lo, they are spread out over the face of all the earth, eating, and drinking, and feasting, with all the great spoil which they have taken out of the land of the Philistines, and out of the land of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:11; Psalms 18:42; 1 Samuel 15:3"> And David smiteth them from the twilight even unto the evening of the morrow, and there hath not escaped of them a man, except four hundred young men who have ridden on the camels, and are fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 14:16"> And David delivereth all that the Amalekites have taken; also his two wives hath David delivered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 91:9–10; 1 Samuel 30:8; Job 1:10"> And there hath not lacked to them &#91;anything&#93;, from small unto great, and unto sons and daughters, and from the spoil, even unto all that they had taken to themselves, the whole hath David brought back,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:26; Numbers 31:9–12; 2 Chronicles 20:25"> and David taketh the whole of the flock, and of the herd, they have led on before these cattle, and they say, 'This &#91;is&#93; David's spoil.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 13:1; 1 Samuel 30:10; Judges 8:15"> And David cometh in unto the two hundred men who were too faint to go after David, and whom they cause to abide at the brook of Besor, and they go out to meet David, and to meet the people who &#91;are&#93; with him, and David approacheth the people, and asketh of them of welfare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 7:12; Deuteronomy 13:13; 1 Kings 21:10"> And every bad and worthless man, of the men who have gone with David, answereth, yea, they say, 'Because that they have not gone with us we do not give to them of the spoil which we have delivered, except each his wife and his children, and they lead away and go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:18; Psalms 44:2–7; Acts 7:2"> And David saith, 'Ye do not do so, my brethren, with that which Jehovah hath given to us, and He doth preserve us, and doth give the troop which cometh against us into our hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 22:8; Numbers 31:27; Psalms 68:12"> and who doth hearken to you in this thing? for as the portion of him who was brought down into battle, so also &#91;is&#93; the portion of him who is abiding by the vessels — alike they share.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:13"> And it cometh to pass from that day and forward, that he appointeth it for a statute and for an ordinance for Israel unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:27; Genesis 33:11; Isaiah 32:8"> And David cometh in unto Ziklag, and sendeth of the spoil to the elders of Judah, to his friends, (saying, 'Lo, for you a blessing, of the spoil of the enemies of Jehovah),'</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 15:48; Joshua 19:8; Judges 1:22–23"> to those in Beth-El, and to those in South Ramoth, and to those in Jattir,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 13:16; Joshua 15:50; Joshua 21:14"> and to those in Aroer, and to those in Siphmoth, and to those in Eshtemoa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 1:16; 1 Samuel 27:10; 1 Samuel 15:6"> and to those in Rachal, and to those in the cities of the Jerahmeelites, and to those in the cities of the Kenites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 1:17; Joshua 19:4; Joshua 15:42"> and to those in Hormah, and to those in Chor-Ashan, and to those in Athach,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:1; 2 Samuel 15:10; 2 Samuel 4:1"> and to those in Hebron, and to all the places where David had gone up and down, he and his men.</span>
