<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 8:11–12; Psalms 74:9; 1 Samuel 3:21"> And the youth Samuel is serving Jehovah before Eli, and the word of Jehovah hath been precious in those days — there is no vision broken forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:15; Psalms 90:10; Genesis 27:1"> And it cometh to pass, at that time, that Eli is lying down in his place, and his eyes have begun to be dim — he is not able to see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 27:4; Leviticus 24:2–4; Exodus 27:20–21"> And the lamp of God is not yet extinguished, and Samuel is lying down in the temple of Jehovah, where the ark of God &#91;is&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 6:8; 1 Corinthians 12:6–11; Exodus 3:4"> and Jehovah calleth unto Samuel, and he saith, 'Here &#91;am&#93; I.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And he runneth unto Eli, and saith, 'Here &#91;am&#93; I, for thou hast called for me;' and he saith, 'I called not; turn back, lie down;' and he goeth and lieth down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 9:2; 1 Samuel 4:16; 2 Samuel 18:22"> And Jehovah addeth to call again Samuel, and Samuel riseth and goeth unto Eli, and saith, 'Here &#91;am&#93; I, for thou hast called for me;' and he saith, 'I have not called, my son, turn back, lie down.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 19:2; Jeremiah 9:24"> And Samuel hath not yet known Jehovah, and the word of Jehovah is not yet revealed unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 33:14–15; 1 Corinthians 13:11–12"> And Jehovah addeth to call Samuel the third time, and he riseth and goeth unto Eli, and saith, 'Here &#91;am&#93; I, for thou hast called for me;' and Eli understandeth that Jehovah is calling to the youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 20:19; Isaiah 6:8; Psalms 85:8"> And Eli saith to Samuel, 'Go, lie down, and it hath been, if He doth call unto thee, that thou hast said, Speak, Jehovah, for Thy servant is hearing;' and Samuel goeth and lieth down in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:8; 1 Samuel 3:4–6"> And Jehovah cometh, and stationeth Himself, and calleth as time by time, 'Samuel, Samuel;' and Samuel saith, 'Speak, for Thy servant is hearing.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:5; Jeremiah 19:3; 2 Kings 21:12"> And Jehovah saith unto Samuel, 'Lo, I am doing a thing in Israel, at which the two ears of every one hearing it do tingle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:27–36; Zechariah 1:6; Joshua 23:15"> In that day I establish unto Eli all that I have spoken unto his house, beginning and completing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 19:18; Matthew 10:37; 1 Samuel 2:12"> and I have declared to him that I am judging his house — to the age, for the iniquity which he hath known, for his sons are making themselves vile, and he hath not restrained them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 22:14; 1 Samuel 2:25; Jeremiah 15:1"> and therefore I have sworn to the house of Eli: the iniquity of the house of Eli is not atoned for, by sacrifice, and by offering — unto the age.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:6–8; Malachi 1:10; 1 Samuel 1:9"> And Samuel lieth till the morning, and openeth the doors of the house of Jehovah, and Samuel is afraid of declaring the vision unto Eli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Eli calleth Samuel, and saith, 'Samuel, my son;' and he saith, 'Here &#91;am&#93; I.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:35; Ruth 1:17; Psalms 141:5"> And he saith, 'What &#91;is&#93; the word which He hath spoken unto thee? I pray thee, hide it not from me; so doth God do to thee, and so doth He add, if thou hidest from me a word of all the words that He hath spoken unto thee.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 2:10; Isaiah 39:8; Judges 10:15"> And Samuel declareth to him the whole of the words, and hath not hid from him; and he saith, 'It &#91;is&#93; Jehovah; that which is good in His eyes He doth.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 39:2; 1 Samuel 9:6; 1 Kings 8:56"> And Samuel groweth up, and Jehovah hath been with him, and hath not let fall any of his words to the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 20:1; 2 Samuel 3:10; 1 Timothy 1:12"> and all Israel know, from Dan even unto Beer-Sheba, that Samuel is established for a prophet to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:1; Genesis 12:7; 1 Samuel 3:4"> And Jehovah addeth to appear in Shiloh, for Jehovah hath been revealed unto Samuel, in Shiloh, by the word of Jehovah.</span>
