<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:24; 2 Timothy 1:8; Philippians 1:13–16"> For this cause, I Paul, the prisoner of Christ Jesus for you the nations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 1:25–27; 1 Timothy 1:11; 2 Timothy 1:11"> if, indeed, ye did hear of the dispensation of the grace of God that was given to me in regard to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 1:12; Romans 16:25; Ephesians 1:9–11"> that by revelation He made known to me the secret, according as I wrote before in few &#91;words&#93; —</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 4:3; 1 Corinthians 4:1; 2 Corinthians 11:6"> in regard to which ye are able, reading &#91;it&#93;, to understand my knowledge in the secret of the Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 1:10–12; 2 Peter 3:2; Titus 1:1–3"> which in other generations was not made known to the sons of men, as it was now revealed to His holy apostles and prophets in the Spirit —</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 3:26–29; 1 John 1:3; 1 John 2:25"> that the nations be fellow-heirs, and of the same body, and partakers of His promise in the Christ, through the good news,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 1:19; Ephesians 3:20; Ephesians 3:2"> of which I became a ministrant, according to the gift of the grace of God that was given to me, according to the working of His power;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Colossians 1:27; 1 Corinthians 15:9; Ephesians 2:7"> to me — the less than the least of all the saints — was given this grace, among the nations to proclaim good news — the untraceable riches of the Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 1:26; Matthew 13:35; 2 Timothy 1:9"> and to cause all to see what &#91;is&#93; the fellowship of the secret that hath been hid from the ages in God, who the all things did create by Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 1:12; Ephesians 6:12; 1 Corinthians 2:7"> that there might be made known now to the principalities and the authorities in the heavenly &#91;places&#93;, through the assembly, the manifold wisdom of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Ephesians 1:11; Ephesians 1:4"> according to a purpose of the ages, which He made in Christ Jesus our Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 2:18; Romans 5:2; Hebrews 4:14–16"> in whom we have the freedom and the access in confidence through the faith of him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 1:24; 2 Thessalonians 3:13; 2 Corinthians 1:6"> wherefore, I ask &#91;you&#93; not to faint in my tribulations for you, which is your glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 95:6; Ephesians 1:16–19; Philippians 2:10"> For this cause I bow my knees unto the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 1:10; Revelation 5:8–14; Ephesians 1:21"> of whom the whole family in the heavens and on earth is named,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 1:11; Philippians 4:13; Isaiah 40:29–31"> that He may give to you, according to the riches of His glory, with might to be strengthened through His Spirit, in regard to the inner man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Colossians 2:7; 1 John 4:16; John 14:23"> that the Christ may dwell through the faith in your hearts, in love having been rooted and founded,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 103:11–12; Job 11:7–9; Ephesians 1:18–23"> that ye may be in strength to comprehend, with all the saints, what &#91;is&#93; the breadth, and length, and depth, and height,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 1:23; Colossians 2:9–10; Colossians 1:10"> to know also the love of the Christ that is exceeding the knowledge, that ye may be filled — to all the fulness of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:8; Romans 4:21; Jeremiah 32:27"> and to Him who is able above all things to do exceeding abundantly what we ask or think, according to the power that is working in us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jude 1:25; Romans 11:36; 2 Peter 3:18"> to Him &#91;is&#93; the glory in the assembly in Christ Jesus, to all the generations of the age of the ages. Amen.</span>
