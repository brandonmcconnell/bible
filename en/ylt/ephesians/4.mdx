<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; Philippians 1:27; 1 Thessalonians 2:12"> Call upon you, then, do I — the prisoner of the Lord — to walk worthily of the calling with which ye were called,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 3:12–13; 1 Corinthians 13:7; 1 Corinthians 13:4–5"> with all lowliness and meekness, with long-suffering, forbearing one another in love,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:10; Colossians 3:13–15; 1 Corinthians 12:12–13"> being diligent to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of the peace;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 2:18; Romans 12:4–5; 1 Corinthians 12:4–13"> one body and one Spirit, according as also ye were called in one hope of your calling;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:6; 1 Corinthians 12:13; 1 Peter 3:21"> one Lord, one faith, one baptism,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Malachi 2:10; 1 Corinthians 8:6"> one God and Father of all, who &#91;is&#93; over all, and through all, and in you all,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10; Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 12:7–11"> and to each one of you was given the grace, according to the measure of the gift of Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 68:18; Colossians 2:15; 1 Samuel 30:26"> wherefore, he saith, 'Having gone up on high he led captive captivity, and gave gifts to men,' —</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 3:13; Matthew 12:40; Proverbs 30:4"> and that, he went up, what is it except that he also went down first to the lower parts of the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 7:26; Hebrews 4:14; Ephesians 1:20–23"> he who went down is the same also who went up far above all the heavens, that He may fill all things —</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:15; 1 Corinthians 12:28–29; Ephesians 2:20"> and He gave some &#91;as&#93; apostles, and some &#91;as&#93; prophets, and some &#91;as&#93; proclaimers of good news, and some &#91;as&#93; shepherds and teachers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11–14; Ephesians 4:16; 1 Corinthians 12:27"> unto the perfecting of the saints, for a work of ministration, for a building up of the body of the Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 1:28; John 17:21; Ephesians 4:5"> till we may all come to the unity of the faith and of the recognition of the Son of God, to a perfect man, to a measure of stature of the fulness of the Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:9; Romans 16:17–18; 1 Corinthians 14:20"> that we may no more be babes, tossed and borne about by every wind of the teaching, in the sleight of men, in craftiness, unto the artifice of leading astray,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 3:18; Ephesians 4:25; Zechariah 8:16"> and, being true in love, we may increase to Him &#91;in&#93; all things, who is the head — the Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 2:19; John 15:5; Colossians 2:2"> from whom the whole body, being fitly joined together and united, through the supply of every joint, according to the working in the measure of each single part, the increase of the body doth make for the building up of itself in love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 1:21; 1 Peter 4:3–4; 1 Thessalonians 4:1–2"> This, then, I say, and I testify in the Lord; ye are no more to walk, as also the other nations walk, in the vanity of their mind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 13:15; Ephesians 2:12; Romans 1:21–23"> being darkened in the understanding, being alienated from the life of God, because of the ignorance that is in them, because of the hardness of their heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 3:5; Romans 1:24–26; 1 Timothy 4:2"> who, having ceased to feel, themselves did give up to the lasciviousness, for the working of all uncleanness in greediness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Titus 2:11–14; John 6:45; Matthew 11:29"> and ye did not so learn the Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 5:20; Ephesians 1:13; Hebrews 3:7–8"> if so be ye did hear him, and in him were taught, as truth is in Jesus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 6:6; Hebrews 12:1; James 1:21"> ye are to put off concerning the former behaviour the old man, that is corrupt according to the desires of the deceit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 12:2; Romans 8:6; Psalms 51:10"> and to be renewed in the spirit of your mind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Colossians 3:10–14; 2 Corinthians 5:17; Ephesians 2:10"> and to put on the new man, which, according to God, was created in righteousness and kindness of the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Colossians 3:9; Zechariah 8:16; Proverbs 12:22"> Wherefore, putting away the lying, speak truth each with his neighbour, because we are members one of another;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 1:19; Psalms 4:4; Psalms 37:8"> be angry and do not sin; let not the sun go down upon your wrath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 4:7; 1 Peter 5:8; Ephesians 6:16"> neither give place to the devil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:11–12; Galatians 6:10"> whoso is stealing let him no more steal, but rather let him labour, working the thing that is good with the hands, that he may have to impart to him having need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; 1 Thessalonians 5:11; Ecclesiastes 10:12"> Let no corrupt word out of your mouth go forth, but what is good unto the needful building up, that it may give grace to the hearers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:19; Isaiah 63:10; Ephesians 1:13–14"> and make not sorrowful the Holy Spirit of God, in which ye were sealed to a day of redemption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Colossians 3:8; Ecclesiastes 7:9; Titus 3:2–3"> Let all bitterness, and wrath, and anger, and clamour, and evil-speaking, be put away from you, with all malice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Colossians 3:12–13; Matthew 6:14–15; Mark 11:25"> and become one to another kind, tender-hearted, forgiving one another, according as also God in Christ did forgive you.</span>
