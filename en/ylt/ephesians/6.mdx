<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 3:20–25; Proverbs 23:22; Proverbs 6:20"> The children! obey your parents in the Lord, for this is righteous;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Deuteronomy 27:16; Matthew 15:4–6"> honour thy father and mother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:16; Deuteronomy 4:40; Deuteronomy 12:28"> which is the first command with a promise, 'That it may be well with thee, and thou mayest live a long time upon the land.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 3:21; Proverbs 22:6; Proverbs 29:15"> And the fathers! provoke not your children, but nourish them in the instruction and admonition of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 2:18–21; Titus 2:9–10; Colossians 3:17–24"> The servants! obey the masters according to the flesh with fear and trembling, in the simplicity of your heart, as to the Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 3:22–23; 1 Thessalonians 2:4; Galatians 1:10"> not with eye-service as men-pleasers, but as servants of the Christ, doing the will of God out of soul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 3:23; Ephesians 6:5–6; 1 Corinthians 10:31"> with good-will serving, as to the Lord, and not to men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Colossians 3:24; Luke 14:14; Proverbs 11:18"> having known that whatever good thing each one may do, this he shall receive from the Lord, whether servant or freeman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 3:25–1; Acts 10:34; Romans 2:11"> And the masters! the same things do ye unto them, letting threatening alone, having known that also your Master is in the heavens, and acceptance of persons is not with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Isaiah 40:31; Joshua 1:9"> As to the rest, my brethren, be strong in the Lord, and in the power of his might;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4; Romans 13:12; Ephesians 6:13"> put on the whole armour of God, for your being able to stand against the wiles of the devil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Colossians 1:13; 2 Corinthians 4:4; Ephesians 2:2"> because we have not the wrestling with blood and flesh, but with the principalities, with the authorities, with the world-rulers of the darkness of this age, with the spiritual things of the evil in the heavenly places;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4; Luke 21:36; Ephesians 6:11–17"> because of this take ye up the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to resist in the day of the evil, and all things having done — to stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 59:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:8; Isaiah 11:5"> Stand, therefore, having your loins girt about in truth, and having put on the breastplate of the righteousness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Romans 10:15; 2 Corinthians 5:18–21"> and having the feet shod in the preparation of the good-news of the peace;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8–9; 1 John 5:4–5; Proverbs 18:10"> above all, having taken up the shield of the faith, in which ye shall be able all the fiery darts of the evil one to quench,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Isaiah 59:17; Revelation 19:15"> and the helmet of the salvation receive, and the sword of the Spirit, which is the saying of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:17; Colossians 4:2; Philippians 4:6"> through all prayer and supplication praying at all times in the Spirit, and in regard to this same, watching in all perseverance and supplication for all the saints —</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 4:3; Acts 4:29; 2 Thessalonians 3:1"> and in behalf of me, that to me may be given a word in the opening of my mouth, in freedom, to make known the secret of the good news,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:20; Colossians 4:4; 2 Timothy 2:9"> for which I am an ambassador in a chain, that in it I may speak freely — as it behoveth me to speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 20:4; 2 Timothy 4:12; Titus 3:12"> And that ye may know — ye also — the things concerning me — what I do, all things make known to you shall Tychicus, the beloved brother and faithful ministrant in the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Philippians 2:25; Colossians 4:7–8; 2 Thessalonians 2:17"> whom I did send unto you for this very thing, that ye might know the things concerning us, and that he might comfort your hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 6:16; Galatians 5:6; 1 Peter 5:14"> Peace to the brethren, and love, with faith, from God the Father, and the Lord Jesus Christ!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 22:37; 2 Corinthians 8:12; Matthew 6:13"> The grace with all those loving our Lord Jesus Christ — undecayingly! Amen.</span>
