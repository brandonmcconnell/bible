<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 2:13; Ephesians 2:5–6; Colossians 3:1–4"> Also you — being dead in the trespasses and the sins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 6:12; 1 John 5:19; Ephesians 2:3"> in which once ye did walk according to the age of this world, according to the ruler of the authority of the air, of the spirit that is now working in the sons of disobedience,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Titus 3:3; Ephesians 4:22; Galatians 5:16–24"> among whom also we all did walk once in the desires of our flesh, doing the wishes of the flesh and of the thoughts, and were by nature children of wrath — as also the others,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 1:3; Ephesians 1:7; Psalms 86:15"> and God, being rich in kindness, because of His great love with which He loved us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 3:24; Ephesians 2:8; Romans 5:10"> even being dead in the trespasses, did make us to live together with the Christ, (by grace ye are having been saved,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 3:1–3; Ephesians 1:3; Romans 6:4–5"> and did raise &#91;us&#93; up together, and did seat &#91;us&#93; together in the heavenly &#91;places&#93; in Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:16–17; Titus 3:4; Ephesians 2:4"> that He might show, in the ages that are coming, the exceeding riches of His grace in kindness toward us in Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:22–26; Ephesians 2:5; Romans 4:16"> for by grace ye are having been saved, through faith, and this not of you — of God the gift,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Romans 11:6; Titus 3:3–5"> not of works, that no one may boast;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 2:13; Colossians 1:10; Hebrews 13:21"> for of Him we are workmanship, created in Christ Jesus to good works, which God did before prepare, that in them we may walk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Colossians 2:11; Colossians 2:13; Colossians 3:11"> Wherefore, remember, that ye &#91;were&#93; once the nations in the flesh, who are called Uncircumcision by that called Circumcision in the flesh made by hands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 4:8; Colossians 1:21; Jeremiah 31:31–34"> that ye were at that time apart from Christ, having been alienated from the commonwealth of Israel, and strangers to the covenants of the promise, having no hope, and without God, in the world;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 2:39; Colossians 1:20–22; Colossians 1:13–14"> and now, in Christ Jesus, ye being once afar off became nigh in the blood of the Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; Colossians 3:11; Colossians 3:15"> for he is our peace, who did make both one, and the middle wall of the enclosure did break down,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 2:14; Galatians 3:10; Colossians 1:21–22"> the enmity in his flesh, the law of the commands in ordinances having done away, that the two he might create in himself into one new man, making peace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 1:20–22; Romans 5:10; 2 Corinthians 5:18–21"> and might reconcile both in one body to God through the cross, having slain the enmity in it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 57:19–21; Acts 10:36; Romans 5:1"> and having come, he did proclaim good news — peace to you — the far-off and the nigh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:13; Ephesians 3:12; Romans 5:2"> because through him we have the access — we both — in one Spirit unto the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philippians 3:20; 1 John 3:1; Galatians 6:10"> Then, therefore, ye are no more strangers and foreigners, but fellow-citizens of the saints, and of the household of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4–5; Isaiah 28:16; 1 Peter 2:7–8"> being built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Jesus Christ himself being chief corner -&#91;stone&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:16–17; 1 Corinthians 3:9; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> in whom all the building fitly framed together doth increase to an holy sanctuary in the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4–5; 1 Corinthians 3:16; 1 John 4:16"> in whom also ye are builded together, for a habitation of God in the Spirit.</span>
