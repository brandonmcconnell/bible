<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 7:15; Jude 1:4; Deuteronomy 13:1–3"> And there did come also false prophets among the people, as also among you there shall be false teachers, who shall bring in besides destructive sects, and the Master who bought them denying, bringing to themselves quick destruction,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 2:24; Jude 1:10; 1 Peter 2:12"> and many shall follow out their destructive ways, because of whom the way of the truth shall be evil spoken of,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:17; Deuteronomy 32:35; Romans 16:18"> and in covetousness, with moulded words, of you they shall make merchandise, whose judgment of old is not idle, and their destruction doth not slumber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jude 1:6; Matthew 25:41; Romans 8:32"> For if God messengers who sinned did not spare, but with chains of thick gloom, having cast &#91;them&#93; down to Tartarus, did deliver &#91;them&#93; to judgment, having been reserved,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:7; 2 Peter 3:6; Jude 1:14–15"> and the old world did not spare, but the eighth person, Noah, of righteousness a preacher, did keep, a flood on the world of the impious having brought,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jude 1:7; Luke 17:28–30; Deuteronomy 29:23"> and the cities of Sodom and Gomorrah having turned to ashes, with an overthrow did condemn, an example to those about to be impious having set &#91;them&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 19:16; Genesis 19:29; Genesis 19:7–8"> and righteous Lot, worn down by the conduct in lasciviousness of the impious, He did rescue,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 119:158; Ezekiel 9:4; Psalms 119:136"> for in seeing and hearing, the righteous man, dwelling among them, day by day the righteous soul with unlawful works was harassing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 34:15–19; 1 Corinthians 10:13; Revelation 3:10"> The Lord hath known to rescue pious ones out of temptation, and unrighteous ones to a day of judgment, being punished, to keep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jude 1:16; Jude 1:10; Jude 1:18"> and chiefly those going behind the flesh in desire of uncleanness, and lordship despising; presumptuous, self-complacent, dignities they are not afraid to speak evil of,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jude 1:9; 2 Thessalonians 1:7; Psalms 104:4"> whereas messengers, in strength and power being greater, do not bear against them before the Lord an evil speaking judgment;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jude 1:10; Jeremiah 12:3; Galatians 6:8"> and these, as irrational natural beasts, made to be caught and destroyed — in what things they are ignorant of, speaking evil — in their destruction shall be destroyed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 13:13; Romans 2:8–9; Hebrews 2:2–3"> about to receive a reward of unrighteousness, pleasures counting the luxury in the day, spots and blemishes, luxuriating in their deceits, feasting with you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Peter 2:3; 2 Peter 2:18; Ephesians 2:3"> having eyes full of adultery, and unable to cease from sin, enticing unstable souls, having an heart exercised in covetousnesses, children of a curse,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 2:14; Jude 1:11; Deuteronomy 23:4–5"> having forsaken a right way, they did go astray, having followed in the way of Balaam the &#91;son&#93; of Bosor, who a reward of unrighteousness did love,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 22:21–33; Ecclesiastes 7:25; Luke 16:11"> and had a rebuke of his own iniquity — a dumb ass, in man's voice having spoken, did forbid the madness of the prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jude 1:12–13; Ephesians 4:14; Jeremiah 14:3"> These are wells without water, and clouds by a tempest driven, to whom the thick gloom of the darkness to the age hath been kept;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Peter 2:20; Jude 1:15–16; Romans 13:13"> for overswellings of vanity speaking, they do entice in desires of the flesh — lasciviousnesses, those who had truly escaped from those conducting themselves in error,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 8:34; Romans 6:16–22; Galatians 5:13"> liberty to them promising, themselves being servants of the corruption, for by whom any one hath been overcome, to this one also he hath been brought to servitude,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26–27; Hebrews 6:4–8; Philippians 3:19"> for, if having escaped from the pollutions of the world, in the acknowledging of the Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ, and by these again being entangled, they have been overcome, become to them hath the last things worse than the first,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:24; Hebrews 6:4–6; James 4:17"> for it were better to them not to have acknowledged the way of the righteousness, than having acknowledged &#91;it&#93;, to turn back from the holy command delivered to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 26:11"> and happened to them hath that of the true similitude; 'A dog did turn back upon his own vomit,' and, 'A sow having bathed herself — to rolling in mire.'</span>
