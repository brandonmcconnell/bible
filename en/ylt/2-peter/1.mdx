<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 2:13; Romans 3:21–26; Titus 1:4"> Simeon Peter, a servant and an apostle of Jesus Christ, to those who did obtain a like precious faith with us in the righteousness of our God and Saviour Jesus Christ:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Peter 3:18; John 17:3; Philippians 3:8"> Grace to you, and peace be multiplied in the acknowledgement of God and of Jesus our Lord!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; 2 Timothy 1:9; Romans 8:28–30"> As all things to us His divine power (the things pertaining unto life and piety) hath given, through the acknowledgement of him who did call us through glory and worthiness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 4:23–24; Ezekiel 36:25–27; 2 Corinthians 3:18"> through which to us the most great and precious promises have been given, that through these ye may become partakers of a divine nature, having escaped from the corruption in the world in desires.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 4:8; Proverbs 4:23; 2 Peter 3:18"> And this same also — all diligence having brought in besides, superadd in your faith the worthiness, and in the worthiness the knowledge,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Romans 5:3–4; Luke 21:19"> and in the knowledge the temperance, and in the temperance the endurance, and in the endurance the piety,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 13:1; Romans 12:10; 1 Thessalonians 3:12"> and in the piety the brotherly kindness, and in the brotherly kindness the love;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Titus 3:14; John 15:2; Hebrews 6:12"> for these things being to you and abounding, do make &#91;you&#93; neither inert nor unfruitful in regard to the acknowledging of our Lord Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Peter 2:18–20; Ephesians 5:26; Titus 2:14"> for he with whom these things are not present is blind, dim-sighted, having become forgetful of the cleansing of his old sins;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Peter 3:17; Luke 6:47–49; Hebrews 6:11"> wherefore, the rather, brethren, be diligent to make stedfast your calling and choice, for these things doing, ye may never stumble,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; Matthew 25:34; Revelation 3:21"> for so, richly shall be superadded to you the entrance into the age-during reign of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 2:21; Romans 15:14–15; Philippians 3:1"> Wherefore, I will not be careless always to remind you concerning these things, though, having known them, and having been established in the present truth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Peter 3:1; 2 Peter 1:14; Hebrews 13:3"> and I think right, so long as I am in this tabernacle, to stir you up in reminding &#91;you&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:6; John 21:18–19; Deuteronomy 31:14"> having known that soon is the laying aside of my tabernacle, even as also our Lord Jesus Christ did shew to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:19–29; Psalms 71:18; Joshua 24:24–29"> and I will be diligent that also at every time ye have, after my outgoing, power to make to yourselves the remembrance of these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 4:14; John 1:14; 1 Timothy 1:4"> For, skilfully devised fables not having followed out, we did make known to you the power and presence of our Lord Jesus Christ, but eye-witnesses having become of his majesty —</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 9:7; Matthew 17:5; Matthew 3:17"> for having received from God the Father honour and glory, such a voice being borne to him by the excellent glory: 'This is My Son — the beloved, in whom I was well pleased;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 17:6; Joshua 5:15; Exodus 3:5"> and this voice we — we did hear, out of heaven borne, being with him in the holy mount.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 22:16; Isaiah 9:2; Psalms 119:105"> And we have more firm the prophetic word, to which we do well giving heed, as to a lamp shining in a dark place, till day may dawn, and a morning star may arise — in your hearts;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 3:3; Romans 12:6; Romans 6:6"> this first knowing, that no prophecy of the Writing doth come of private exposition,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:15–17; 1 Peter 1:11; 2 Samuel 23:2"> for not by will of man did ever prophecy come, but by the Holy Spirit borne on holy men of God spake.</span>
