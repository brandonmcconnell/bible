<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 4:20–1; 1 Peter 3:8; 1 Peter 1:22"> Let brotherly love remain;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 25:35; Genesis 18:1–3; 1 Peter 4:9"> of the hospitality be not forgetful, for through this unawares certain did entertain messengers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 25:36; Hebrews 10:34; Romans 12:15"> be mindful of those in bonds, as having been bound with them, of those maltreated, as also yourselves being in the body;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 2:24; Proverbs 5:15–23; Galatians 5:19"> honourable &#91;is&#93; the marriage in all, and the bed undefiled, and whoremongers and adulterers God shall judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:8; Joshua 1:5; Deuteronomy 31:6"> Without covetousness the behaviour, being content with the things present, for He hath said, 'No, I will not leave, no, nor forsake thee,'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 118:6–9; Isaiah 41:10; Romans 8:31"> so that we do boldly say, 'The Lord &#91;is&#93; to me a helper, and I will not fear what man shall do to me.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:12–13; Hebrews 13:17; Philippians 3:17"> Be mindful of those leading you, who did speak to you the word of God, whose faith — considering the issue of the behaviour — be imitating,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Malachi 3:6; James 1:17; Revelation 1:8"> Jesus Christ yesterday and to-day the same, and to the ages;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 4:14; 1 Timothy 6:3–5; Ephesians 5:6"> with teachings manifold and strange be not carried about, for &#91;it is&#93; good that by grace the heart be confirmed, not with meats, in which they who were occupied were not profited;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:7–8; 1 Corinthians 10:20; 1 Corinthians 9:13"> we have an altar, of which to eat they have no authority who the tabernacle are serving,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 16:27; Exodus 29:14; Leviticus 9:11"> for of those beasts whose blood is brought for sin into the holy places through the chief priest — of these the bodies are burned without the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 9:12–14; John 19:17–18; Acts 7:58"> Wherefore, also Jesus — that he might sanctify through &#91;his&#93; own blood the people — without the gate did suffer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 11:26; 1 Peter 4:14–16; Acts 5:41"> now, then, may we go forth unto him without the camp, his reproach bearing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Philippians 3:20; Colossians 3:1–3; Hebrews 11:12–16"> for we have not here an abiding city, but the coming one we seek;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 107:21–22; Psalms 50:14; Romans 12:1"> through him, then, we may offer up a sacrifice of praise always to God, that is, the fruit of lips, giving thanks to His name;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:13; Galatians 6:10; Romans 12:13"> and of doing good, and of fellowship, be not forgetful, for with such sacrifices God is well-pleased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:12–13; 1 Timothy 5:17; Hebrews 13:7"> Be obedient to those leading you, and be subject, for these do watch for your souls, as about to give account, that with joy they may do this, and not sighing, for this &#91;is&#93; unprofitable to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:25; 2 Thessalonians 3:1; Colossians 4:3"> Pray for us, for we trust that we have a good conscience, in all things willing to behave well,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philemon 1:22; Romans 1:10–12; Romans 15:31–32"> and more abundantly do I call upon &#91;you&#93; to do this, that more quickly I may be restored to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 10:11; Hebrews 10:22; Psalms 23:1"> And the God of the peace, who did bring up out of the dead the great shepherd of the sheep — in the blood of an age-during covenant — our Lord Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 2:13; 2 Thessalonians 2:17; Philippians 4:13"> make you perfect in every good work to do His will, doing in you that which is well-pleasing before Him, through Jesus Christ, to whom &#91;is&#93; the glory — to the ages of the ages! Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 3:1; 1 Peter 5:12; 2 Corinthians 6:1"> And I entreat you, brethren, suffer the word of the exhortation, for also through few words I have written to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philemon 1:1; 1 Thessalonians 3:2; 2 Timothy 1:8"> Know ye that the brother Timotheus is released, with whom, if he may come more shortly, I will see you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 13:7; Hebrews 13:17; Acts 18:2"> Salute all those leading you, and all the saints; salute you doth those from Italy:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Colossians 4:18; 2 Timothy 4:22; Titus 3:15"> the grace &#91;is&#93; with you all! Amen.</span>
