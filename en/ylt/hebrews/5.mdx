<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 8:3; Hebrews 10:11; Hebrews 7:27"> For every chief priest — out of men taken — in behalf of men is set in things &#91;pertaining&#93; to God, that he may offer both gifts and sacrifices for sins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; Hebrews 2:18; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> able to be gentle to those ignorant and going astray, since himself also is compassed with infirmity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 9:7; Hebrews 9:7; Leviticus 16:6"> and because of this infirmity he ought, as for the people, so also for himself to offer for sins;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 28:1; Numbers 16:40; Numbers 16:5"> and no one to himself doth take the honour, but he who is called by God, as also Aaron:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 2:7; John 8:54; Acts 13:33"> so also the Christ did not glorify himself to become chief priest, but He who spake unto him: 'My Son thou art, I to-day have begotten thee;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 110:4; Hebrews 7:17; Hebrews 7:21"> as also in another &#91;place&#93; He saith, 'Thou &#91;art&#93; a priest — to the age, according to the order of Melchisedek;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 14:32–39; Luke 22:41–44; John 17:1"> who in the days of his flesh both prayers and supplications unto Him who was able to save him from death — with strong crying and tears — having offered up, and having been heard in respect to that which he feared,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 2:8; Hebrews 10:5–9; John 4:34"> through being a Son, did learn by the things which he suffered — the obedience,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 2:10; Hebrews 9:12; Hebrews 9:15"> and having been made perfect, he did become to all those obeying him a cause of salvation age-during,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 5:5–6; Hebrews 6:20"> having been addressed by God a chief priest, according to the order of Melchisedek,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 13:15; Acts 28:27; 2 Peter 3:16"> concerning whom we have much discourse and of hard explanation to say, since ye have become dull of hearing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 6:1; 1 Corinthians 3:1–3; 1 Peter 2:2"> for even owing to be teachers, because of the time, again ye have need that one teach you what &#91;are&#93; the elements of the beginning of the oracles of God, and ye have become having need of milk, and not of strong food,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:20; 1 Peter 2:2; 2 Timothy 3:16"> for every one who is partaking of milk &#91;is&#93; unskilled in the word of righteousness — for he is an infant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14–15; 1 Thessalonians 5:21; Ephesians 1:18"> and of perfect men is the strong food, who because of the use are having the senses exercised, unto the discernment both of good and of evil.</span>
