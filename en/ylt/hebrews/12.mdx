<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:7; 1 Corinthians 9:24–27; Philippians 2:16"> Therefore, we also having so great a cloud of witnesses set around us, every weight having put off, and the closely besetting sin, through endurance may we run the contest that is set before us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 7:7; 1 Peter 3:18; 1 Peter 2:23–24"> looking to the author and perfecter of faith — Jesus, who, over-against the joy set before him — did endure a cross, shame having despised, on the right hand also of the throne of God did sit down;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; 2 Corinthians 4:16; Revelation 2:3"> for consider again him who endured such gainsaying from the sinners to himself, that ye may not be wearied in your souls — being faint.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; Revelation 2:13; Hebrews 10:32–34"> Not yet unto blood did ye resist — with the sin striving;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 3:11–12; Job 5:17–18; Revelation 3:19"> and ye have forgotten the exhortation that doth speak fully with you as with sons, 'My son, be not despising chastening of the Lord, nor be faint, being reproved by Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 3:12; Psalms 119:75"> for whom the Lord doth love He doth chasten, and He scourgeth every son whom He receiveth;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 19:18; Deuteronomy 8:5; Proverbs 13:24"> if chastening ye endure, as to sons God beareth Himself to you, for who is a son whom a father doth not chasten?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 12:6; 1 Peter 5:9–10; Psalms 73:1"> and if ye are apart from chastening, of which all have become partakers, then bastards are ye, and not sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 16:22; Numbers 27:16; Isaiah 42:5"> Then, indeed, fathers of our flesh we have had, chastising &#91;us&#93;, and we were reverencing &#91;them&#93;; shall we not much rather be subject to the Father of the spirits, and live?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Peter 1:4; Leviticus 11:44–45; Titus 2:14"> for they, indeed, for a few days, according to what seemed good to them, were chastening, but He for profit, to be partakers of His separation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 5:3–5; 2 Corinthians 4:17; Hebrews 12:5–6"> and all chastening for the present, indeed, doth not seem to be of joy, but of sorrow, yet afterward the peaceable fruit of righteousness to those exercised through it — it doth yield.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3; Hebrews 12:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:14"> Wherefore, the hanging-down hands and the loosened knees set ye up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 4:26–27; Isaiah 35:3; Galatians 6:1"> and straight paths make for your feet, that that which is lame may not be turned aside, but rather be healed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:1; 1 Peter 1:15–16; 1 Thessalonians 4:7"> peace pursue with all, and the separation, apart from which no one shall see the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 3:12; Deuteronomy 29:18; Galatians 5:4"> looking diligently over lest any one be failing of the grace of God, lest any root of bitterness springing up may give trouble, and through this many may be defiled;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 25:31–34; 1 Corinthians 6:15–20; Revelation 22:15"> lest any one be a fornicator, or a profane person, as Esau, who in exchange for one morsel of food did sell his birthright,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24–31; Matthew 25:11–12; Matthew 7:23"> for ye know that also afterwards, wishing to inherit the blessing, he was disapproved of, for a place of reformation he found not, though with tears having sought it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:11; Exodus 24:17; Exodus 20:18"> For ye came not near to the mount touched and scorched with fire, and to blackness, and darkness, and tempest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:16; 1 Corinthians 15:52; Deuteronomy 4:12"> and a sound of a trumpet, and a voice of sayings, which those having heard did entreat that a word might not be added to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 19:12–13; Exodus 19:16; Galatians 3:10"> for they were not bearing that which is commanded, 'And if a beast may touch the mountain, it shall be stoned, or with an arrow shot through,'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 19:16; Deuteronomy 9:19; Isaiah 6:3–5"> and, (so terrible was the sight,) Moses said, 'I am fearful exceedingly, and trembling.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Galatians 4:26; Philippians 3:20; Revelation 14:1"> But, ye came to Mount Zion, and to a city of the living God, to the heavenly Jerusalem, and to myriads of messengers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="James 1:18; Hebrews 11:40; Luke 10:20"> to the company and assembly of the first-born in heaven enrolled, and to God the judge of all, and to spirits of righteous men made perfect,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 9:15; Hebrews 11:28; Hebrews 11:4"> and to a mediator of a new covenant — Jesus, and to blood of sprinkling, speaking better things than that of Abel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 8:5; Deuteronomy 30:17; Hebrews 2:1–3"> See, may ye not refuse him who is speaking, for if those did not escape who refused him who upon earth was divinely speaking — much less we who do turn away from him who &#91;speaketh&#93; from heaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Haggai 2:6–7; Isaiah 13:13; Exodus 19:18"> whose voice the earth shook then, and now hath he promised, saying, 'Yet once — I shake not only the earth, but also the heaven;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:31; Matthew 24:35; 2 Peter 3:10–11"> and this — 'Yet once' — doth make evident the removal of the things shaken, as of things having been made, that the things not shaken may remain;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 12:1–2; Hebrews 13:15; Daniel 2:44"> wherefore, a kingdom that cannot be shaken receiving, may we have grace, through which we may serve God well-pleasingly, with reverence and religious fear;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:24; Deuteronomy 9:3; Hebrews 10:27"> for also our God &#91;is&#93; a consuming fire.</span>
