<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 5:12–14; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Hebrews 9:14"> Wherefore, having left the word of the beginning of the Christ, unto the perfection we may advance, not again a foundation laying of reformation from dead works, and of faith on God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:13–57; Acts 17:31–32; Romans 6:3–5"> of the teaching of baptisms, of laying on also of hands, of rising again also of the dead, and of judgment age-during,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 18:21; 1 Corinthians 16:7; 1 Corinthians 4:19"> and this we will do, if God may permit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26–29; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Matthew 5:13"> for &#91;it is&#93; impossible for those once enlightened, having tasted also of the heavenly gift, and partakers having became of the Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 2:3; Luke 8:13; Matthew 13:20–21"> and did taste the good saying of God, the powers also of the coming age,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Peter 2:21; Hebrews 10:29; Hebrews 6:4"> and having fallen away, again to renew &#91;them&#93; to reformation, having crucified again to themselves the Son of God, and exposed to public shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 10:12; Isaiah 44:3; Psalms 65:9–13"> For earth, that is drinking in the rain many times coming upon it, and is bringing forth herbs fit for those because of whom also it is dressed, doth partake of blessing from God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 3:17–18; John 15:6; Luke 13:7–9"> and that which is bearing thorns and briers &#91;is&#93; disapproved of, and nigh to cursing, whose end &#91;is&#93; for burning;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 10:39; 2 Corinthians 7:10; Hebrews 6:4–6"> and we are persuaded, concerning you, beloved, the things that are better, and accompanying salvation, though even thus we speak,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 10:42; Hebrews 13:16; Galatians 6:10"> for God is not unrighteous to forget your work, and the labour of the love, that ye shewed to His name, having ministered to the saints and ministering;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 1:5–8; Hebrews 3:6; Romans 5:2–5"> and we desire each one of you the same diligence to shew, unto the full assurance of the hope unto the end,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 13:7; Hebrews 10:36; 1 John 2:25"> that ye may not become slothful, but followers of those who through faith and patient endurance are inheriting the promises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 22:15–18; Luke 1:73; Jeremiah 22:5"> For to Abraham God, having made promise, seeing He was able to swear by no greater, did swear by Himself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Deuteronomy 1:10; Nehemiah 9:23"> saying, 'Blessing indeed I will bless thee, and multiplying I will multiply thee;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 12:2–3; Romans 4:17–25; Exodus 1:7"> and so, having patiently endured, he did obtain the promise;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 22:11; Hebrews 6:13; Genesis 31:53"> for men indeed do swear by the greater, and an end of all controversy to them for confirmation &#91;is&#93; the oath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 6:18; Proverbs 19:21; Isaiah 55:11"> in which God, more abundantly willing to shew to the heirs of the promise the immutability of his counsel, did interpose by an oath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Titus 1:2; 1 Samuel 15:29"> that through two immutable things, in which &#91;it is&#93; impossible for God to lie, a strong comfort we may have who did flee for refuge to lay hold on the hope set before &#91;us&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 43:5; Romans 5:5–10; Hebrews 9:7"> which we have, as an anchor of the soul, both sure and stedfast, and entering into that within the vail,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 4:14; Hebrews 3:1; Hebrews 5:6"> whither a forerunner for us did enter — Jesus, after the order of Melchisedek chief priest having become — to the age.</span>
