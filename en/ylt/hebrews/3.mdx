<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Philippians 3:14; Hebrews 4:14–15"> Wherefore, holy brethren, partakers of a heavenly calling, consider the apostle and chief priest of our profession, Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 12:7; 1 Timothy 3:15; Ephesians 2:22"> being stedfast to Him who did appoint him, as also Moses in all his house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:9; 1 Peter 2:5–7; Zechariah 6:12–13"> for of more glory than Moses hath this one been counted worthy, inasmuch as more honour than the house hath he who doth build it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 3:3; Hebrews 1:2; Esther 2:10"> for every house is builded by some one, and He who the all things did build &#91;is&#93; God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 12:7; Hebrews 3:2; Exodus 14:31"> and Moses indeed &#91;was&#93; stedfast in all his house, as an attendant, for a testimony of those things that were to be spoken,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Corinthians 3:16; Hebrews 10:35"> and Christ, as a Son over his house, whose house are we, if the boldness and the rejoicing of the hope unto the end we hold fast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 95:7–11; Hebrews 3:15; Isaiah 55:3"> Wherefore, (as the Holy Spirit saith, 'To-day, if His voice ye may hear —</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 28:14; Exodus 17:7; Zechariah 7:11–12"> ye may not harden your hearts, as in the provocation, in the day of the temptation in the wilderness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 7:36; Deuteronomy 4:9; Exodus 19:4"> in which tempt Me did your fathers, they did prove Me, and saw My works forty years;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 95:10; 2 Thessalonians 2:10–12; Psalms 67:2"> wherefore I was grieved with that generation, and said, Always do they go astray in heart, and these have not known My ways;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:34–35; Hebrews 4:3; Numbers 32:10–13"> so I sware in My anger, If they shall enter into My rest — !')</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5; Jeremiah 7:24; Jeremiah 17:9"> See, brethren, lest there shall be in any of you an evil heart of unbelief in the falling away from the living God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; Hebrews 10:24–25; 2 Timothy 4:2"> but exhort ye one another every day, while the To-day is called, that none of you may be hardened by the deceitfulness of the sin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 3:6; Hebrews 6:11; Hebrews 12:10"> for partakers we have become of the Christ, if the beginning of the confidence unto the end we may hold fast,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 3:7–8; Psalms 95:7; Hebrews 10:38"> in its being said, 'To-day, if His voice ye may hear, ye may not harden your hearts, as in the provocation,'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 14:2; Numbers 14:30; Numbers 26:65"> for certain having heard did provoke, but not all who did come out of Egypt through Moses;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 14:29; Numbers 26:64–65; Numbers 14:32–33"> but with whom was He grieved forty years? was it not with those who did sin, whose carcasses fell in the wilderness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 14:30; Hebrews 4:6; Deuteronomy 1:34–35"> and to whom did He swear that they shall not enter into His rest, except to those who did not believe? —</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jude 1:5; John 3:36; Mark 16:16"> and we see that they were not able to enter in because of unbelief.</span>
