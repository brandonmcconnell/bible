<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 14:18–20; Psalms 57:2; Hebrews 6:20"> For this Melchisedek, king of Salem, priest of God Most High, who did meet Abraham turning back from the smiting of the kings, and did bless him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 18:21; Psalms 85:10–11; 1 Samuel 8:17"> to whom also a tenth of all did Abraham divide, (first, indeed, being interpreted, 'King of righteousness,' and then also, King of Salem, which is, King of Peace,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 7:17; Hebrews 7:23–28; Exodus 6:20–27"> without father, without mother, without genealogy, having neither beginning of days nor end of life, and being made like to the Son of God, doth remain a priest continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 14:20; Acts 2:29; Acts 7:8–9"> And see how great this one &#91;is&#93;, to whom also a tenth Abraham the patriarch did give out of the best of the spoils,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 27:30–33; 2 Chronicles 31:4–6; Numbers 18:21–32"> and those, indeed, out of the sons of Levi receiving the priesthood, a command have to take tithes from the people according to the law, that is, their brethren, even though they came forth out of the loins of Abraham;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 4:13; Hebrews 7:3–4; Genesis 13:14–17"> and he who was not reckoned by genealogy of them, received tithes from Abraham, and him having the promises he hath blessed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:27; Genesis 48:15–20; 1 Timothy 3:16"> and apart from all controversy, the less by the better is blessed —</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 6:20; Hebrews 5:6; Revelation 1:18"> and here, indeed, men who die do receive tithes, and there &#91;he&#93;, who is testified to that he was living,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 5:12; Genesis 14:20; Hebrews 7:4"> and so to speak, through Abraham even Levi who is receiving tithes, hath paid tithes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 35:11; Hebrews 7:5; Genesis 46:26"> for he was yet in the loins of the father when Melchisedek met him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 8:7; Galatians 2:21; Galatians 4:9"> If indeed, then, perfection were through the Levitical priesthood — for the people under it had received law — what further need, according to the order of Melchisedek, for another priest to arise, and not to be called according to the order of Aaron?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Isaiah 66:21; Ezekiel 16:61"> for the priesthood being changed, of necessity also, of the law a change doth come,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 7:11; Hebrews 7:14; Numbers 17:5"> for he of whom these things are said in another tribe hath had part, of whom no one gave attendance at the altar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Isaiah 11:1; Luke 3:33"> for &#91;it is&#93; evident that out of Judah hath arisen our Lord, in regard to which tribe Moses spake nothing concerning priesthood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 110:4; Hebrews 7:17–21; Hebrews 7:3"> And it is yet more abundantly most evident, if according to the similitude of Melchisedek there doth arise another priest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 1:18; Colossians 2:20; Hebrews 7:28"> who came not according to the law of a fleshly command, but according to the power of an endless life,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 110:4; Hebrews 5:6; Hebrews 7:21"> for He doth testify — 'Thou &#91;art&#93; a priest — to the age, according to the order of Melchisedek;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 8:3; Acts 13:39; Galatians 4:9"> for a disannulling indeed doth come of the command going before because of its weakness, and unprofitableness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 13:39; Hebrews 4:16; Hebrews 9:9"> (for nothing did the law perfect) and the bringing in of a better hope, through which we draw nigh to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 18:7; Hebrews 5:6; Psalms 110:4"> And inasmuch as &#91;it is&#93; not apart from oath, (for those indeed apart from oath are become priests,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 110:4; Hebrews 7:17; Hebrews 6:16–18"> and he with an oath through Him who is saying unto him, 'The Lord sware, and will not repent, Thou &#91;art&#93; a priest — to the age, according to the order of Melchisedek;')</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:25; Genesis 44:32; Hebrews 8:6–12"> by so much of a better covenant hath Jesus become surety,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:10–11; 1 Chronicles 6:3–14; Hebrews 7:8"> and those indeed are many who have become priests, because by death they are hindered from remaining;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 12:34; Hebrews 7:28; Isaiah 9:6–7"> and he, because of his remaining — to the age, hath the priesthood not transient,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 8:34; 1 John 2:1–2; 1 Timothy 2:5"> whence also he is able to save to the very end, those coming through him unto God — ever living to make intercession for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:21; Hebrews 4:14–15; 1 Peter 2:22"> For such a chief priest did become us — kind, harmless, undefiled, separate from the sinners, and become higher than the heavens,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 9:28; Hebrews 9:12; Hebrews 9:14"> who hath no necessity daily, as the chief priests, first for his own sins to offer up sacrifice, then for those of the people; for this he did once, having offered up himself;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 2:10; Hebrews 7:21; Hebrews 1:2"> for the law doth appoint men chief priests, having infirmity, but the word of the oath that &#91;is&#93; after the law &#91;appointeth&#93; the Son — to the age having been perfected.</span>
