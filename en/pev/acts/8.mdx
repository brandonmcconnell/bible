## Philip went to Samaria

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:23; Acts 11:19; Acts 8:1"> The Christians that ran away from Jerusalem went to a lot of different places, and in all those places they told people the good news about Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 6:5; Acts 21:8; Acts 8:35–36"> One of them was Philip. He went to a town in Samaria country and told everyone, “Jesus is the Christ, the special man that God promised to send.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 4:41–42; 2 Chronicles 30:12; Matthew 20:15–16"> God gave Philip a lot of power. A lot of people saw him do powerful things, and they listened to him, and they thought carefully about his message.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:1; Acts 14:8–10; Mark 16:17–18"> You see, a lot of people had bad spirits in them, and Philip used God’s power to force those bad spirits out of them. Those bad spirits screamed as they left those people. And Philip also used God’s power to make a lot of crippled people better, and other people that couldn’t walk because their legs were too weak, Philip made them better too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 13:52; Acts 13:48; Acts 8:39"> So the people in that town were very happy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 13:6; Acts 5:36; John 7:18"> A man that used to do magic lived in that town in Samaria. His name was Simon. He did that magic for a long time before Philip went there. Everyone there was surprised by the things he did. He used to say, “I am a great man.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; 2 Peter 2:2; Revelation 13:3"> All the people talked about him and said, “This man is really great. He has God’s power.” Important people said that, as well as other people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 8:9; Galatians 3:1; Isaiah 44:25"> Simon did that magic for a long time, and the people there were always surprised by the things he did, so they listened to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 1:3; Matthew 28:19"> But then Philip went to that town and told the people the good news about Jesus, and about God’s family, and he told them that God sent Jesus Christ to save people. A lot of men and women listened to Philip and believed in Jesus, and then Philip baptised them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 8:6–7; John 5:20; Acts 19:11"> Simon believed in Jesus too, so Philip baptised him. After that, Simon followed Philip everywhere, and he watched him doing powerful things. Simon was very surprised by those powerful things that Philip did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:13; 1 Thessalonians 3:2; Galatians 2:9"> But at that time, the Holy Spirit didn’t go into any of those Samaria people. Philip only baptised the people to show they belonged to Jesus. Then Jesus’s special workers in Jerusalem heard that the people in Samaria believed God’s word, so they sent Peter and John to Samaria. After they got there, they put their hands on the people that believed in Jesus, and prayed for them. Then the Holy Spirit came into those people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:5; Matthew 10:8; 2 Kings 8:9"> Simon watched Peter and John. He saw them put their hands on people, and then the Holy Spirit went into those people. So Simon said to Peter, “Let me have that power too, so that if I put my hands on people, the Holy Spirit will go into them. If you give me that power, I will pay you money.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 5:17; Matthew 10:8; Acts 2:38"> But Peter said to him, “You think you can buy God’s power with money, but you are wrong. No way. Only God can give people that power. And God can see that you are not thinking the right way, so he will not give you that power, and he will not let you work with us. Look out. God can finish you up, and finish up your money too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:25–26; Daniel 4:27; Hebrews 4:12"> Stop thinking in that really bad way. Turn around and think God’s way. Pray to him and ask him not to punish you for thinking like that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 58:6; Proverbs 5:22; John 8:34"> I can see that you are jealous, and that you want to do bad things, and that those bad things are controlling you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 8:8; Numbers 21:7; Job 42:8"> Then Simon said to Peter, “Pray to God for me so that he will not punish me, and those bad things you said will not happen to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 20:21; Acts 26:22–23; Acts 1:8"> After that, Peter and John talked to the people there, and they told them more of the message about Jesus. Then they left that town to go back to Jerusalem. They went through a lot of little towns along that road in Samaria country, and they told the good news about Jesus to the people in those towns.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Philip talked to a man from Ethiopia

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 5:19; Hebrews 1:14; 2 Kings 1:3"> While Philip was in Samaria, one of God’s angel messengers came to him and said, “Get up, and go south along the desert road, you know, the road that goes from Jerusalem to the town called Gaza.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:10; Psalms 68:31; Isaiah 56:3–8"> So Philip got up, and he started to go along that road, and he saw a man from Ethiopia country sitting in a trailer, and horses were pulling the trailer along that road. He was the sort of man that couldn’t have any kids. He worked for an important woman called Candace. She was the big boss over Ethiopia. That man was one of her important workers. He looked after all her money. But he had a break from that work and went to Jerusalem to show respect to God, and then he started to go back home along that road.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; Isaiah 1:1; Acts 28:25"> Philip saw him sitting in his trailer, and he was reading God’s book. He was reading the part that Isaiah wrote a long time ago. Isaiah was one of God’s men that told people God’s messages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 10:19; Acts 11:12; Acts 20:22–23"> Then the Holy Spirit said to Philip, “Catch up with that trailer and walk along beside it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 24:44–45; John 5:39; Ephesians 5:17"> So Philip ran up close to the trailer, and when he was beside it, he heard that man reading from the words that Isaiah wrote in God’s book. Philip asked him, “Can you understand that book you are reading?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 10:14; Psalms 73:16–17; Isaiah 29:18–19"> The Ethiopian man said, “No, I can’t understand it. I need somebody to help me.” Then he asked Philip to climb up into the trailer and sit beside him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7–8; 1 Peter 2:21–24; Jeremiah 51:40"> He was reading these words in God’s book,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Some people took him away to kill him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">just like men take a sheep away to kill it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he was quiet and didn’t say anything.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You know that a sheep is quiet,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and it doesn’t make a noise while men cut off all its wool,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">well, he was quiet, just like that.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Isaiah 53:8; Zechariah 13:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Men shamed him, and they didn’t give him a proper trial in court.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then they killed him, so now he can’t have any kids.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 15:15; Matthew 13:36; Matthew 2:2–4"> And the Ethiopian man said to Philip, “Who was Isaiah talking about? Was he talking about himself or somebody else?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 24:27; Acts 18:28; Acts 28:23"> Then Philip told him that Isaiah was talking about Jesus. And he kept on talking, and he told that Ethiopian man the good news about Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 10:47; John 3:5; Titus 3:5–6"> They kept going along the road, and they came to some water. The Ethiopian man said, “Look, there is some water. I want you to baptise me now.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 4:1; John 3:22–23"> Then he told the man that was controlling the horses to stop. And the Ethiopian man and Philip went down into the water, and Philip baptised the Ethiopian man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 18:12; 2 Kings 2:16; Ezekiel 8:3"> After they came up out of the water, the Holy Spirit quickly took Philip away. The Ethiopian man never saw him again, but he was really happy, and he kept going on his way home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Acts 21:8; Acts 23:23; Acts 23:33"> But the Holy Spirit put Philip in a town called Azotus. Philip went around telling people the good news about Jesus. He walked north, and he told the good news about Jesus to the people in all the towns along the way, until he got to the town called Caesarea, and he stayed there.</span>
</div>
</div>
