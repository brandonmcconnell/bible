## Jesus told a story about 10 young women at a wedding ceremony

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 12:35–36; Philippians 2:15–16; Matthew 24:42–51"> Jesus said to his followers, “This picture story is about what will happen in God’s family, at the time when I come back from heaven. There were some young women that had a special job at a wedding ceremony. When the man getting married came to the house, they had to meet him, and they had to bring him into the house. Then they were all going to eat a big dinner with that man and his new wife. That wedding was at night time, so the young women had their oil lamps with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:2; 1 Corinthians 10:1–5; Matthew 13:19–23"> There were 10 of these young women. And 5 of them used their brains. They took jars of extra oil with them for their lamps. But the other 5 women were stupid. They didn’t take any extra oil with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:6–8; 2 Peter 3:4–9"> That man getting married didn’t come for a long time, so all the young women got tired and went to sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Matthew 24:31; Matthew 24:44"> In the middle of the night, somebody shouted, ‘He is coming now. Come out to meet him.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 12:35; Revelation 3:19–20; 2 Peter 3:14"> The 10 young women woke up. Then they got up quickly, and they tried to make their lamps burn bright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 13:9; Luke 12:35; Hebrews 4:1"> The 5 stupid women that didn’t have any extra oil said to the other 5 women, ‘Give us some of your oil. Our lamps are going out.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 49:7–9; Revelation 3:17–18; Isaiah 55:6–7"> The other women said, ‘Sorry, we can’t do that. There is not enough oil for all of us. You have to go and buy some oil for yourselves.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 13:25; Matthew 24:44; Luke 12:36–37"> So those 5 stupid women went to buy some oil. While they were away, that man came to the house to get married. The 5 other women were ready, and they went with him into the house, and somebody locked the door. Then they all started to eat the wedding dinner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 7:21–23; Hebrews 12:16–17"> Later, the 5 stupid women came back. They called to that man. They said, ‘Sir, please open the door for us.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:3; 2 Timothy 2:19; John 10:27"> But he said, ‘Who are you? I don’t know you.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 24:42–44; Revelation 16:15; 1 Thessalonians 5:6"> Then Jesus said, “It will be like that for you too. You don’t know when I will come back. You don’t know the day or the time. So you have to keep on waiting for me properly. You have to be ready for me when I come.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## After Jesus comes back, he will judge everybody

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:7–8; Matthew 19:28"> Jesus said, “I am God’s special man, and one day, I will come back from heaven, and all God’s angel messengers will come with me. At that time, I will be really great and powerful. I will be the biggest boss, and I will sit down on the big boss’s seat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Malachi 3:18; Matthew 13:49; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> All the people in the world, from every country, they will all come together in front of me. Then I will split all those people into 2 mobs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 100:3; John 10:26–28; John 21:15–17"> I will put one mob on one side of me, and the other mob on the other side of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Peter 1:19–20; James 2:5; Revelation 21:7"> Then I will say to the people on my right-hand side, ‘Come on, you mob. My father is happy with you, so you can be in his family. A long time ago, at the time when he made the world, he made a good place for you. It is ready for you now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 3:16–19; James 2:15–16; Deuteronomy 15:7–11"> You see, at one time I was hungry, and you gave me some food to eat. I was thirsty, and you gave me some water to drink. I was a stranger with no-where to live, so you took me into your home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="James 1:27; 2 Timothy 1:16–18; James 5:14–15"> In the cold weather time I didn’t have enough clothes to keep me warm, so you gave me some more clothes. I was sick, and you looked after me. I was in prison, and you visited me there.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 6:3; 1 Chronicles 29:14; 1 Peter 5:5–6"> Then these good people will say, ‘Sir, when did we ever see you hungry, and give you food? Or thirsty, and give you water to drink?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> When did we ever see you as a stranger, and take you into our homes? When did we ever see you without enough clothes, and give you some more clothes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> When did we ever see you sick, or in prison, and visit you?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Proverbs 14:31; Matthew 10:42"> I will say to them, ‘Listen. You did all these things to help my followers. They were not important people, but they were mine, so every time you helped them, you helped me at the same time.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Jude 1:6–7; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> Then I will turn to the people on my left-hand side, and I will say to them, ‘Get away from me, you mob that God will punish. Go into that big fire that burns for ever. God made that fire for all the bad spirits and for their boss, the devil. And you have to go there too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="James 2:15–24; John 5:23; Matthew 12:30"> You see, I was hungry, but you didn’t give me anything to eat. I was thirsty, but you didn’t give me a drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> I was a stranger with no-where to live, but you didn’t take me into your home. I didn’t have enough clothes to keep me warm, but you didn’t give me any more clothes. I was sick, and in prison, but you didn’t visit me.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 7:22; Luke 10:29; Matthew 25:24–27"> Then they will say, ‘But, sir, when did we ever see you with those problems and not help you? We never saw you hungry, or thirsty, or a stranger, or without enough clothes, or sick, or in prison.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Proverbs 21:13; Proverbs 17:5; Proverbs 14:31"> And I will say to them, ‘Listen. You didn’t do anything to help my friends. They were not important people, but they were my people. Every time you said no to helping them, at that same time you were saying no to helping me.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; John 3:36; John 5:29"> Then that bad mob will go to the bad place, and God will punish them for ever. But the good mob will go to live in the good place for ever.”</span>
</div>
</div>
