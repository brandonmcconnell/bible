## Jesus told a story about some rubbish plants

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 25:1; Matthew 13:47; Matthew 20:1"> Then Jesus told the people another picture story. He said, “This story shows you something about God’s family. A farmer planted some good food seeds in his garden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; 2 Peter 2:1; Acts 20:30–31"> But there was a bad man that didn’t like that farmer. One night, when everyone was asleep, that bad man went to the farmer’s garden and planted some rubbish plant seeds in it, beside the good seeds. Then that bad man went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 4:26–29"> The good seeds grew into good plants, but at the same time the rubbish plants started to grow there too. Those rubbish plants look like the good plants when they are young, but when they get big we can’t eat their seeds. They are too cheeky.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 16:17; 2 Corinthians 6:1; 2 Corinthians 5:18–20"> So the farmer’s workers went to him and said, ‘Boss, you planted good food seeds in that ground. How come a lot of rubbish plants are growing there too?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:3–7; 1 Thessalonians 5:14; Luke 9:49–54"> The farmer said, ‘I reckon there is a bad man that doesn’t like me, and he planted those rubbish plants.’ The workers said, ‘Do you want us to go and pull up those rubbish plants?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> The farmer said, ‘No, don’t do that. Those plants are all mixed together, so if you pull up the rubbish plants, then you will pull up some of the good food plants with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 3:12; 1 Timothy 5:24; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> No. Just let them all grow up, the good plants and the rubbish plants together. Later, when the right time comes, I will get some men to get the good seed food. But I will talk to them before they do that job. I will tell them to cut down the rubbish plants first, and take them away, and burn them. After that, they will get all the good seed food and bring it into my shed.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus told his followers what the story about the rubbish plants means

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 16:17–20; Matthew 13:11; Matthew 9:28"> Then Jesus went into a house, away from all the people. And his followers went to him and said, “Please tell us what that story means, that picture story about the rubbish plants and the garden.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:5–7; Matthew 13:41; Luke 10:16"> Jesus said to them, “I’m God’s special man from heaven, and I’m like the farmer in that story. I’m like the man that planted good food seeds in a garden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 8:44; Philippians 3:18–19; Acts 13:10"> That garden is like the world. The good food seeds are like the people that belong to God. The rubbish plant seeds are like the people that belong to the devil, the boss over the bad spirits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 13:49; Revelation 14:15–19; Matthew 25:31"> The bad man that planted those rubbish seeds in the garden is like the devil. The time to get all the good seed food is like the time for the end of the world. The workers that cut down the plants are like God’s angel messengers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 13:39"> And in the story the workers cut down the rubbish plants and burned them in the fire. That is a picture of what will happen at the end of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 24:31; Matthew 18:7; 2 Peter 2:1–2"> You see, I’m God’s special man from heaven, and at the end of the world, I will send out God’s angel messengers. They will get the people that belong to the devil. They will get all the people that don’t belong in God’s family. Those people are the ones that break God’s law, and they make other people do bad things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 13:50; Luke 16:23–24; Matthew 8:12"> The angels will chuck all those bad people into a big fire. Those people will cry, and they will be so angry that they will bite their teeth together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Daniel 12:3; Matthew 11:15; Luke 12:32"> But at that time God will show everyone that the good people are really great. It will be like they are shiny, like the sun. And they will be in the family of God, their father.” Then Jesus said, “You’ve got ears, so you have to listen to me properly, and take notice of my words.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus told 2 stories to show us that God’s family is very good

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Proverbs 2:2–5; Colossians 2:3; Philippians 3:7–9"> Jesus told his followers another picture story. He said, “This story shows you more about God’s family. A man buried a lot of gold in the ground, to hide it. Some time later, another man found that gold, but he didn’t own the land, so he couldn’t get it for himself. So he buried it again. Then he went away, and he sold everything that he owned, to get some money. Then he bought that land. After that, he got all that gold for himself, and he was really happy. Well, if you find out how to get into God’s family, it is like you find a lot of gold.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Proverbs 3:13–18; Matthew 16:26; Matthew 13:24"> Jesus talked some more. He said, “This is another picture story that shows you about God’s family. There was a man that used to buy things, and then take them to another place and sell them again at a higher price. That was his job. One day, he was looking for beautiful pearls to buy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ephesians 3:8; Luke 18:28–30; Proverbs 2:4"> He found a really good and beautiful pearl, but its price was very high. So he sold everything that he owned, to get enough money for it. Then he bought that very good pearl. Well, if you find out how to get into God’s family, it is like you find a very good pearl.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus told a story about a fishing net, to show more about God’s family

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 15:6; 2 Timothy 3:2–5; Matthew 22:9–10"> Then Jesus said, “This is another picture story that shows you about God’s family. Some fishermen threw their fishing net into the water and caught a lot of fish in it. There were all sorts of fish in that net.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Matthew 13:30; Matthew 13:40–43; Matthew 3:12"> Then they dragged it up on to the land, and sat down, and sorted out the fish. They put the good fish into baskets to keep them, and they chucked the bad fish away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Revelation 20:12–15; Matthew 24:31; Matthew 13:39"> That’s what it will be like when God will finish up this world. His angel workers will come and sort everybody out. They will save good people, and they will chuck the bad people into a big fire. Those people will cry a lot, and they will be so angry that they will bite their teeth together.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Teachers in God’s family use both new and old messages from God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Matthew 13:19; Matthew 13:11; Mark 4:34"> Then Jesus asked his followers, “Do you understand all these things?” They said, “Yes, we do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="1 John 2:7–8; 2 Timothy 3:16–17; Titus 1:9"> Then Jesus said, “Every teacher of our Jewish law that joins God’s family will teach new messages from God as well as old messages. Every teacher of God’s word is like a man that owns a big house. That man has a store-room in that house, where he keeps his very special things. Sometimes he brings those things out to show to his friends. He shows them new things, as well as old things.”</span>
</div>
</div>
