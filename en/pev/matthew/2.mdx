## Some wise men came to Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Micah 5:2; John 7:42; Luke 2:4–7"> Jesus was born in Bethlehem, in Judea country. At that time, the big boss over that country was called Herod. There were some wise men in a country to the east of Judea. They always looked at the stars, and one night they saw a special star in the sky. So they went west to Jerusalem, the city in Judea country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 24:17; Jeremiah 23:5; Revelation 22:16"> They asked the Jerusalem people, “Where was that baby born that is going to be the big boss over you Jewish people? You see, back in our country, we saw his special star. We saw it come up into the sky, so we came here, to show respect to him. Where will we find him?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 17:6–7; John 11:47–48; Matthew 23:37"> Herod was the big boss over that country, and somebody told him that these wise men were talking about another big boss. Herod got very worried. He thought this other person might fight against him and take over his job. A lot of other people in Jerusalem were worried about it too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Matthew 7:29; Acts 6:12"> So Herod had a meeting with the bosses of the Jewish ceremonies and the Jewish law teachers, and he asked them, “Where will that baby be born? You know, the Christ, the man that God promised to send?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 7:42; Ruth 1:1; 1 Samuel 16:1"> They said, “He will be born in Bethlehem, in Judea country. You see, a long time ago, God told one of his men to write about it. He wrote this in God’s book,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:23–25; Micah 5:2; Isaiah 9:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘Bethlehem is a little town in Judea country,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but it is a very important town,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because a big boss will be born there.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will really look after God’s people, the Israel nation.’”</span>That’s what those Jewish leaders told Herod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 1:10; 1 Samuel 18:21; Psalms 55:21"> So Herod sent for those wise men to come and meet him, but he didn’t tell anyone else about the meeting. He asked them, “When did you first see that star?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:19–20; Jeremiah 41:5–7; Luke 20:20–21"> So they told him when they first saw it. Then he said to them, “Go to Bethlehem and look carefully for that boy. After you find him, come and tell me the place where he’s living, so that I can go there too and show respect to him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 2:2; Proverbs 8:17; Psalms 25:12"> The wise men listened to Herod, then they started to go to Bethlehem. Then they saw that same star that they saw before, and they were very happy. That star moved through the sky, and they followed it until it stopped right above the house that Joseph and Mary were living in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 60:6; Psalms 72:10; Luke 2:16"> The wise men went into that house. They saw Jesus with his mother Mary, and they got down on their knees, to show him respect. Then they opened their bags and gave him some presents. They gave him some gold. And they gave him some powder called frankincense, to burn to make a good smell. And they gave him some rubbing medicine called myrrh, that smells good, like perfume.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 2:22; Matthew 2:19; Matthew 2:13"> After that, the wise men had a dream. In that dream God told them not to go back to Herod, so they went back to their own country along a different road.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The big boss tried to kill Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 10:7; Acts 5:19; Matthew 1:20"> After those wise men went away, Joseph had a dream. He heard God’s angel messenger say, “Herod will try to kill the boy, so get up and take Jesus and Mary to Egypt country, and stay there until I tell you to come back.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 2:20–21; Acts 26:21; Matthew 1:24"> So Joseph got up right then, in the middle of the night, and he got Mary and Jesus, and they left to go to Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 11:1; Exodus 4:22; Numbers 24:8"> They lived in Egypt until Herod died, and then they came out of Egypt and went back to Israel country. So it happened just like God told one of his men to write in God’s book, a long time ago. He wrote,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“God called his son to come out of Egypt.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Proverbs 28:15; Judges 16:10"> At the time when Joseph, Mary and Jesus were going to Egypt, Herod waited for those wise men to come back, but they tricked him. They didn’t come back to him. Then he got really angry. He remembered their story about when they first saw the star, so he sent his soldiers to Bethlehem, and he told them to kill all the little boys up to 2 years old. So they killed all the little boys around Bethlehem. Herod wanted to make sure that Jesus was dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> That all happened the way God’s man, Jeremiah, wrote in God’s book, a long time ago. This is what he wrote,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:15; Jeremiah 9:17–21; Jeremiah 4:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">“In the town called Ramah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a lot of women are really sad, and they are crying out loud.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They are the grand-daughters of Rachel.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They are crying for their babies.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Nobody can cheer them up,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because their babies are dead.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 1:20; Daniel 8:25; Matthew 2:12–13"> Some time later, Herod died. Then, in Egypt, Joseph had another dream. He heard God’s angel messenger say,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 4:19; 1 Kings 12:1–3; Matthew 2:13"> “Get up and take Mary and Jesus back to Israel country. The people that wanted to kill him are dead now.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 11:8; Genesis 6:22"> So Joseph did what God’s angel messenger told him. He got up, and he got Mary and Jesus, and they started to go back to Israel country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 48:17–18; Luke 2:39; John 7:52"> But then somebody told him that Herod’s son Arkelayus was the new big boss over the part of Israel called Judea, and Joseph was too frightened to go there. Then he had another dream. God told him, “Go to Galilee country.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 13:5; John 1:45–46; Mark 1:24"> So he took his family up north to Galilee. He got to the town called Nazareth, and he stayed there. A long time before that, one of God’s men wrote about this, and it is in God’s book. He wrote,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“People will say that God’s special man is from Nazareth.”</span>And that happened after Joseph took his family to live at Nazareth.</span>
</div>
</div>
