## The family line of Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 22:18; Romans 1:3; Galatians 3:16"> A long time ago, God promised to send somebody to save us. Jesus is that person. These names are the fathers and grand-fathers of Jesus, that lived a long time before he was born. This list shows that Jesus was from the family of David. David was the big boss over the Israel people. It also shows that David was from the family of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 7:8; Hebrews 11:17–18; Genesis 46:8–27"> <span className="pl pl-1">This list starts with Abraham, a really long time ago. He was the father of Isaac.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Isaac was the father of Jacob.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Jacob was the father of Judah and his brothers.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Genesis 38:29–30; 1 Chronicles 4:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Judah was the father of Perez and Zerah. Their mother’s name was Tamar.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Perez was the father of Hezron.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Hezron was the father of Ram.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:10–12; Numbers 7:12; Numbers 2:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Ram was the father of Amminadab.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Amminadab was the father of Nahshon.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Nahshon was the father of Salmon.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:31; Ruth 1:16–17; James 2:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">Salmon was the father of Boaz. His mother’s name was Rahab.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Boaz was the father of Obed. His mother’s name was Ruth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Obed was the father of Jesse.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 15:5; 1 Samuel 17:12; 2 Samuel 12:24–25"> <span className="pl pl-1">Jesse was the father of David. He was the big boss over all that mob, the Israel nation.</span>So in that family there were 14 fathers from Abraham to David.<br /><span className="pl pl-1">David was the father of Solomon. At first Solomon’s mother was the wife of another man called Uriah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:1–15; 1 Kings 14:31; 1 Kings 11:43–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Solomon was the father of Riaboam.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Riaboam was the father of Abijah.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Abijah was the father of Asa.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:11; 2 Chronicles 26:1–23; 2 Kings 8:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Asa was the father of Jehoshafat.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Jehoshafat was the father of Jehoram.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Jehoram was the father of Uzziah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 7:1–13; 2 Chronicles 26:21; 2 Chronicles 27:1–32"> <span className="pl pl-1">Uzziah was the father of Jotham.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Jotham was the father of Ahaz.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Ahaz was the father of Hezekiah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:33–27; 1 Kings 13:2; 1 Chronicles 3:13–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hezekiah was the father of Manasseh.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Manasseh was the father of Amon.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Amon was the father of Josiah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:20; Jeremiah 52:28–30; Daniel 1:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Josiah was the father of Jeconiah and his brothers. At that time, the soldiers from Babylon came and fought the Jewish people. The Babylon mob won, and they took the Jewish people as prisoners to their country.</span>So in that family there were 14 fathers from David until the time they all had to go to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:17; Ezra 3:2; Luke 3:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Jeconiah was the father of Shiyaltiel.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Shiyaltiel was the father of Zerubbabel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Zerubbabel was the father of Abihud.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Abihud was the father of Eliakim.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Eliakim was the father of Azor.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Azor was the father of Zadok.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Zadok was the father of Akim.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Akim was the father of Eliud.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Eliud was the father of Eleazar.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Eleazar was the father of Matthan.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Matthan was the father of Jacob.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:17; John 4:25; Luke 3:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Jacob was the father of Joseph. Joseph was the husband of Mary, and Mary was the mother of Jesus. Jesus is the Christ, the man that God promised to send to save us.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 1:11–12; 2 Kings 24:14; Matthew 11:2"> So in that family there were 14 fathers from Abraham to David, and there were 14 fathers from David until the time they all had to go to Babylon, and there were 14 fathers from that Babylon time until the time God sent Jesus to the world.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Joseph had a dream

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 4:4–5; Luke 1:27–38; Job 14:4"> This is the story about how Jesus was born. He is the man that God talked about a long time ago. God promised to send him to us. Mary was promised to a man called Joseph, but they hadn’t slept together yet, like married people do. Then the Holy Spirit made a baby grow inside Mary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:21–24; Deuteronomy 24:1–4; John 8:4–5"> Joseph found out that Mary was going to have a baby, and he was not happy. He knew that the baby wasn’t his, so he wanted to leave Mary. But he was a good man, and he didn’t want to shame her. So he thought, “I will just leave her quietly, without any argument.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Luke 1:19; Matthew 1:18"> While Joseph thought about that, he went to sleep and had a dream. He saw an angel messenger from God. The angel said, “Joseph, you are from the family of David. Look, you can go ahead and marry Mary. Don’t be too frightened to do that. You see, her baby is from the Holy Spirit, not from another man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 13:23; Acts 4:12; John 1:29"> She will have a baby boy, and you will name him Jesus. (That name Jesus means God saves.) You see, he will save his people, so that God will not punish them for the bad things they do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 24:44; Matthew 2:23; 1 Kings 8:15"> That all happened just like God told one of his men to write in God’s book, a long time ago. He wrote,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Isaiah 9:6–7; Psalms 46:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">“There will be a young woman that never slept with a man, like a woman does with her husband, but she will have a baby boy.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">That boy will have a special name, Immanuel. That name means God is with us.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 15:14; James 2:21–26; John 2:5–8"> Joseph woke up and did what God’s angel told him to do. He married Mary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 2:21; Exodus 13:2; Matthew 1:21"> But he did not sleep with her, like a man does with his wife, until after her son was born. And he named the baby Jesus.</span>
</div>
</div>
