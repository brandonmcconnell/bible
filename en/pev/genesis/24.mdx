## Abraham sent his worker to find a wife for his son Isaac

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 112:1–3; Galatians 3:9; Matthew 6:33"> Abraham was very old, and God was very good to him in every way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 47:29; Genesis 39:4–6; Genesis 15:2"> Abraham had workers that looked after everything he owned. One of those workers was older than all the other workers, and he was in charge of all the other workers too. One day, Abraham talked to that oldest worker. He said, “I want you to make me a strong promise. So come here and put your hand under my leg.” You see, in Abraham’s culture, people sometimes made promises to each other that way. The person that made the promise, they put their hand under the other person’s leg, and that showed that they had to keep their promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 28:8"> Abraham said to the worker, “I want you to make this promise with God listening. His name is Yahweh, and he is the boss of heaven and earth. Promise me that you will not get a wife for my son Isaac from this country that we are living in. Don’t get him a wife from the Canaan people that live here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 28:2; Genesis 22:20–23; Genesis 12:7"> No, I want you to go back to my home country and get a wife for Isaac from my relatives.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:2; Exodus 9:2; Exodus 20:7"> Then Abraham’s worker said, “Yes, I will do that. But think about this. Maybe I will pick a woman to be Isaac’s wife, and that woman will not want to leave her home country and come with me here, to Canaan country. Then what do you want me to do? Maybe I can come back here and get Isaac, and then I can take him to your home country to get married?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 5:1; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Hebrews 10:39"> Abraham said, “No way. Make sure that you never take Isaac back to my home country. He has to stay here in Canaan country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 33:2; Genesis 13:15; Hebrews 1:14"> You know that Yahweh my God is the boss in heaven. He took me away from my father’s family and my home country, and he brought me here to Canaan country. And God made me a strong promise. He said, ‘I am going to give this country to all the people that will be born into your family later on.’” Then Abraham said, “I know that God will help you find a wife for my son. Yes, God will send an angel worker to my home country to get things ready for you. When you get there, you will find a woman for my son Isaac to marry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 2:17–20; Numbers 30:5; Numbers 30:8"> But if that woman doesn’t want to leave her home country and come back here with you, then don’t worry. You will not have to keep that promise any more. But I tell you again, you can never take my son Isaac back to my home country.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 24:2"> So Abraham’s worker put his hand under Abraham’s leg, and he made that strong promise, just like Abraham told him to. He said, “I promise that I will not get a wife for your son Isaac from the Canaan people that live here in this country. I promise that I will go back to your home country and get a wife for Isaac from your relatives.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 11:31; Deuteronomy 23:4; Genesis 27:43"> Abraham wanted to give lots of really good presents to his relatives, so the worker got those presents ready, and he got 10 of Abraham’s camels to carry everything, and he got some of Abraham’s other workers to go with him. They all left that place, and they went north, all the way to Abraham’s home country. That country was called Aram Naharayim. When they got to that country, they went towards the town where Abraham’s brother Nahor lived.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Abraham’s worker met Rebekah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:11; Exodus 2:16; John 4:7"> Abraham’s worker stopped near that town, next to a water-hole, and he let the camels lie down there to rest. It was nearly night time, and the young women that lived in that town, they were going to the water-hole to get water. They used to do that at the same time every day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 24:27; Exodus 3:6; Genesis 26:24"> Abraham’s worker saw those young women, and he prayed to God. He said, “Yahweh, you are the God of my boss Abraham. Please be very good to Abraham, just like you promised him. Please let me find a wife for Abraham’s son Isaac today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 24:43; Psalms 37:5; Exodus 2:16"> Look, I am standing next to this water-hole, and the young women that live in the town are coming out to get water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 6:37; Judges 6:17; Genesis 15:8"> Please show me which woman you picked to be Isaac’s wife.” Then Abraham’s worker said to God, “I will talk to one of those women, and I will say, ‘Please give me a drink from your jar of water.’ Maybe that woman will say to me, ‘Yes, I will give you a drink, and I’ll give your camels some water too.’ If she says that, then I will know that she is the woman you picked to be Isaac’s wife. And I will know that you have been very good to my boss Abraham, just like you promised him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 11:29; Genesis 24:45; Isaiah 65:24"> While Abraham’s worker was still praying, a young woman walked out of the town with a jar on her shoulder. That woman’s name was Rebekah, and her father’s name was Bethuel. Bethuel’s mother was Milcah, and Bethuel’s father was Nahor. And Nahor was Abraham’s brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 26:7; Numbers 31:17–18; Genesis 39:6"> Rebekah was a very beautiful young woman, and she wasn’t married. She never slept with a man, like a woman sleeps with her husband. Rebekah got to the water-hole, and she went down to the water and filled up her jar, and then she came back up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 4:7; Isaiah 21:14; Genesis 26:1–35"> Then Abraham’s worker ran to meet Rebekah. He said to her, “Please let me drink a little bit of water from your jar.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 31:26; 1 Peter 3:8; Genesis 24:14"> Rebekah said, “Yes, please have a drink, sir.” Straight away, she took the jar off her shoulder. Then she held the jar in her hands and gave Abraham’s worker a drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 24:14; 1 Peter 4:9; Genesis 24:45–46"> After Rebekah finished giving him a drink, she said, “Now I will get water for your camels too, until they have enough to drink.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Then she quickly poured the rest of the water from her jug for the camels to drink. Then she ran back to the water-hole to get more water. She kept on doing that until all the camels had enough to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 107:43; Genesis 24:12; 2 Samuel 7:18–20"> While Rebekah was getting the water, Abraham’s worker kept on looking at her, but he didn’t say anything. He waited to see if she was the woman that God picked to be Isaac’s wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:11–12; Exodus 32:2–3; Genesis 23:15–16"> After the camels finished drinking, Abraham’s worker got some of Abraham’s presents out, and he gave Rebekah some jewellery. He gave her a gold nose ring and 2 heavy gold bracelets for her arms. That nose ring was made out of 6 grams of gold, and those bracelets were made out of 120 grams of gold each.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Then the worker said to Rebekah, “Please tell me, who is your father?” Then he said, “Me and my men, we want to stay here in this town tonight. Is there enough room for us to stay at your father’s house?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 24:15; Genesis 22:23; Genesis 22:20"> Rebekah said to the worker, “My father’s name is Bethuel. His mother’s name is Milcah, and his father’s name is Nahor.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 32:8; 1 Peter 4:9; Judges 19:19–21"> Then Rebekah said, “We have plenty of food for your camels, and we have plenty of dry grass for them to sleep on. And we have plenty of room for you and your men to stay with us tonight.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 24:48; Exodus 4:31; Genesis 24:52"> Then Abraham’s worker knew that Rebekah was Abraham’s relative, and he knew that God picked her to be Isaac’s wife. So he got down on his knees and put his face near the ground, to thank God and show him respect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 24:48; Genesis 24:12; Psalms 98:3"> He said, “Yahweh, you are really great. You are the God of my boss Abraham. You keep on being very good to him, just like you promised him. We can always trust you. You brought me straight to Abraham’s relatives, so that I can get a wife for Isaac.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Abraham’s worker talked to Rebekah’s family

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 24:67; Genesis 24:55; Genesis 31:33"> Rebekah ran home and told her family what happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 29:5; Genesis 29:13; Genesis 24:55"> She told them everything that Abraham’s worker said to her at the water-hole, and she showed them the gold nose ring and the gold bracelets that Abraham’s worker gave her. Rebekah’s brother Laban was there, and straight away he ran to the water-hole to find Abraham’s worker, and he found him standing there next to his camels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ruth 3:10; Genesis 26:29; Psalms 115:15"> Then Laban said to Abraham’s worker, “Sir, I can see that God is good to you. Please, don’t keep on standing here at the water-hole. Come home with me. I have a room ready for you, and I have a place for your camels to stay too.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Judges 19:21; Genesis 43:24; Genesis 18:4"> So Abraham’s worker, and the other men that were with him, they went to the house with Laban. Then they took the bags off the camels, and Laban got food for the camels to eat and dry grass for them to sleep on. He also got water so that Abraham’s worker and his men could wash their feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 132:3–5; 1 Timothy 6:2; Ephesians 6:5–8"> After that, he got Abraham’s worker and his men some food. But Abraham’s worker said, “I will not eat yet. First I have to tell you why I came here.” Laban said to him, “Yes, tell us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> So Abraham’s worker talked to Laban and the other people in Rebekah’s family. He said, “I work for Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 24:1; Genesis 13:2; Proverbs 10:22"> His God Yahweh is really good to him, and now Abraham is very rich. Yes, God gave him a lot of sheep, and cows, and camels, and donkeys, and a lot of silver and gold. God also gave him a lot of men and women to work for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 25:5; Genesis 21:10; Genesis 21:1–7"> And Abraham has a son too. Yes, Abraham’s wife Sarah had a baby boy when she was very old. That boy is a man now, and later, when Abraham dies, he will get everything that belonged to Abraham.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Genesis 27:46; Ezra 9:1–3; Genesis 24:2–9"> Then Abraham’s worker said, “My boss Abraham got me to make him a strong promise. He said, ‘Promise me that you will not get a wife for my son from this country that we are living in. Don’t get him a wife from the Canaan people that live here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 24:4; Genesis 31:19; Genesis 12:1"> No, you have to go to my father’s family and my other relatives, and get a wife for my son.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 24:5"> Then I said to my boss Abraham, ‘Maybe I will pick a woman, and that woman will not want to leave her home country and come with me here, to Canaan country. Then what do you want me to do?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Exodus 23:20; Psalms 16:8; Genesis 5:24"> Then my boss Abraham said, ‘Listen, I trust Yahweh my God, and I always live his way. I know that he will send an angel worker to go with you and help you, so that you will find a wife for my son from my father’s family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Genesis 24:8; Deuteronomy 29:12"> But if you pick a woman to be my son’s wife, and her relatives will not let her go with you, then don’t worry. You will not have to keep that promise any more.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:11; Genesis 24:12–14; Ezra 8:21"> Then Abraham’s worker said, “Today, I came to the water-hole that is near your town. Then I prayed to God. I said, ‘Yahweh, you are the God of my boss Abraham. Please let me find a wife for Abraham’s son today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Genesis 24:13–14"> Look, I am standing next to this water-hole, and the young women that live in the town, they are coming out to get water. Please show me which one you picked for Abraham’s son. I will talk to one of those women, and I will say, “Please let me drink a little bit of water from your jar.” Maybe that woman will say to me, “Yes, I will give you a drink, and I’ll give your camels some water too.” If she says that, then I will know that she is the woman you picked for Abraham’s son.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 7:7; Acts 12:12–17; Genesis 24:15–20"> While I was still praying quietly to God, Rebekah walked out of the town with a jar on her shoulder. She went down to the water-hole and got some water. Then I said to Rebekah, ‘Please let me have a drink.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Genesis 24:18–19"> Straight away, she took the jar off her shoulder and she said, ‘Yes, have a drink. And I will get water for your camels too.’ So I had a drink, and then she got water for my camels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 45:13–14; Genesis 24:22–24; Isaiah 62:3–5"> Then I said, ‘Who is your father?’ And she said, ‘My father’s name is Bethuel. His father’s name is Nahor, and his mother’s name is Milcah.’ Then I knew that Rebekah is from Abraham’s family, and I knew that God picked her to marry Abraham’s son. So I gave Rebekah a gold nose ring and 2 gold bracelets for her arms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Genesis 24:26–27; Psalms 48:14"> Then I got down on my knees and put my face near the ground, to thank God and show him respect. I said, ‘Yahweh, you are really great. You are the God of my boss Abraham. You brought me straight here to Abraham’s relatives, so that I can get a wife for Abraham’s son.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Genesis 47:29; Joshua 2:14; Proverbs 3:3"> Abraham’s worker kept on talking to Rebekah’s family. He said, “Now I’m asking you to be very good and fair to my boss Abraham. Will you let me take Rebekah to Canaan country, so that she can marry Abraham’s son? Please tell me if you will say yes or no. Then I will know what I have to do after this.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Psalms 118:23; Genesis 31:24; Genesis 31:29"> Then Rebekah’s brother Laban, and her father Bethuel, they talked to Abraham’s worker. They said, “Those things that you told us, we know that God made them happen. So we can’t argue with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Genesis 20:15; Genesis 24:15; 2 Samuel 16:10"> Look, here is Rebekah. Take her with you to Canaan country, so that she can marry Abraham’s son. That is what God wants.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Genesis 24:26; Genesis 24:48; Psalms 116:1–2"> Then Abraham’s worker got down on his knees and put his face near the ground, to thank God and show him respect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Genesis 24:22; Genesis 24:10; Exodus 12:35"> After that, the worker gave Rebekah and her family some presents from Abraham. He gave Rebekah some clothes, and some pretty things made out of silver and gold. And he also gave presents that cost a lot of money to Rebekah’s brother Laban, and to her mother.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Rebekah left her family and went to Canaan country

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Genesis 24:56; Genesis 24:59; Genesis 28:5–6"> Abraham’s worker and his men ate some food, and then they stayed the night with Rebekah’s family. The next morning, they got up, and Abraham’s worker talked to Rebekah’s family. He said, “Let me take Rebekah and go back to my boss Abraham.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Genesis 4:3; Leviticus 25:29; Judges 14:8"> But Rebekah’s brother and mother said, “Let Rebekah stay here a bit longer, maybe 10 more days, and after that she can go with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Proverbs 25:25; Joshua 1:8; Isaiah 48:15"> Abraham’s worker said, “Please don’t make me wait any longer. You know that God picked Rebekah to marry Abraham’s son, and he helped me find her. So please let her come with me now, so that we can go back to my boss Abraham.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> Then Rebekah’s brother and mother said, “All right, we will get Rebekah and ask her what she wants to do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Luke 1:38; Psalms 45:10–11"> So they got Rebekah, and they said to her, “Do you want to go with this man to Canaan country now, so that you can marry Abraham’s son?” Rebekah said, “Yes, I will go now.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Genesis 35:8; Numbers 11:12; Genesis 24:50"> So Rebekah’s family said to Abraham’s worker, “All right, Rebekah can go with you and your men to Canaan country now. And she can take some women that work for our family. They can live in Canaan country with her and work for her there.” One of those women used to look after Rebekah when she was a little girl.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Genesis 17:16; Ruth 4:11–12"> Then Rebekah’s family asked God to be good to Rebekah. They said to her,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“We are asking God to give you lots of kids and grand-kids,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so that your family will become a really big mob of people.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And we are asking God to make them strong,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so that when other people fight against your mob, your mob will win,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and then your mob will take the towns that belonged to those other people.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Psalms 45:10; Genesis 2:24; Esther 8:14"> Then Rebekah and her working women, they got on camels, and they went with Abraham’s worker and his men, to go all the way to Canaan country.</span>
</div>

## Isaac met Rebekah and married her

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Genesis 25:11; Genesis 16:14; Genesis 12:9"> Abraham’s son Isaac was living in Canaan country. You see, before this time, Isaac went south, away from Abraham’s camp, and he made his camp in the south part of the country called the Negev, near a water-hole. That water-hole was called the water-hole that belongs to God, he is alive and sees me. Then when Abraham’s worker was away in Haran, Isaac came back home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Psalms 119:15; Psalms 1:2; Psalms 104:34"> Then one afternoon, when it was nearly night time, Isaac went out to the grass lands to think about things. Then he looked up and saw camels coming, so he walked out to meet them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Judges 1:14; Joshua 15:18"> Rebekah was riding on one of those camels, and she looked up and saw Isaac coming towards them. Straight away, she got off her camel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:9; 1 Corinthians 11:5–6; 1 Corinthians 11:10"> Then she said to Abraham’s worker, “I can see a man walking towards us. Who is he?” The worker said, “He is my boss Isaac.” Rebekah didn’t want Isaac to see her face, so she got a scarf and put it over her head. You see, in that culture, that is what a woman did when she met the man that she was going to marry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Mark 6:30"> Then Abraham’s worker talked to Isaac, and he told Isaac everything that happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Song of Solomon 8:2; Ephesians 5:22–33; Genesis 23:1–2"> There was a tent there that used to belong to Isaac’s mother Sarah. Isaac took Rebekah into that tent, and Rebekah became Isaac’s wife. Isaac loved Rebekah. Some time before this, Isaac’s mother Sarah died, so Isaac was sad. But after he married Rebekah, he was happy again.</span>
</div>
