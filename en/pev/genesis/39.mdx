## Joseph lived in Egypt country

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 37:28; Psalms 105:17; Genesis 37:36"> This is the story about what happened to Joseph, after that time when his brothers sold him to the Midian mob. That Midian mob took Joseph to Egypt country. Then they sold Joseph to a man called Potifar. That man Potifar, he worked for the big boss of Egypt country, called Pharaoh. Potifar was in charge of all the soldiers that guarded Pharaoh’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:14; Psalms 91:15; Jeremiah 15:20"> God was with Joseph, and God helped him. So everything that Joseph did, it all went well. Joseph worked for Potifar, and he lived in Potifar’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 1:3; Genesis 30:27; Matthew 5:16"> Potifar saw that Joseph did everything really well, and he knew that God was helping him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 39:21–22; Proverbs 27:18; Genesis 19:19"> So Potifar liked Joseph a lot, and he got Joseph to be his special worker. He put Joseph in charge of all the other workers in his house, and he got Joseph to look after everything that he owned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 30:27; 2 Samuel 6:11–12; Acts 27:24"> As soon as Potifar put Joseph in charge, God started to be really good to Potifar. God made everything go well in Potifar’s house, and he made lots of food plants grow on his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:12; Luke 19:17; Proverbs 31:11"> So Potifar really trusted Joseph, and he let Joseph look after everything that belonged to him. Potifar didn’t worry about anything, because Joseph was in charge. Potifar just got to think about easy things, like the food he wanted to eat. Joseph looked after everything else. Potifar’s wife made trouble for Joseph Joseph was a really good-looking man, with a strong body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:11; Ezekiel 16:34; 2 Peter 2:14"> So after a while, Potifar’s wife started to look at Joseph a lot, and she wanted to sleep with him, like a woman sleeps with her husband. She said to Joseph, “Come and sleep with me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 23:26–28; Proverbs 22:14; Proverbs 9:13–18"> But Joseph said, “No, I will not sleep with you. You know, I work for your husband, and he trusts me. He got me to look after everything that belongs to him. He doesn’t worry about anything, because I am in charge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:13; Genesis 20:6; Genesis 42:18"> He made me the most important person in his house. He made me the boss of everything here. There’s only one thing he will not let me touch. He will not let me touch you, because you are his wife. So I will not sleep with you. I will not go against God and do that wrong thing. No way.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Genesis 39:8; 1 Thessalonians 5:22"> But Potifar’s wife did not stop. Every day, she kept on asking Joseph to sleep with her. But Joseph didn’t listen to her. He didn’t sleep with her, and he tried to be far away from her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 5:12; Ephesians 5:3; Malachi 3:5"> One day, Joseph went into Potifar’s house to do his work, just like he always did. On that day, there weren’t any other work-men in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:22; Proverbs 7:13–27; Ecclesiastes 7:26"> Then Potifar’s wife came to Joseph. She grabbed him, and she held on to his coat, and she said, “Come and sleep with me.” So Joseph quickly ran out of the house. But Potifar’s wife still had Joseph’s coat in her hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Potifar’s wife saw that Joseph ran out of the house, and she saw that she still had his coat in her hand. So she thought, “I will make trouble for Joseph.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 10:21; 1 Peter 2:20; Psalms 35:11"> Straight away, she shouted to the other work-men, and she said, “Come and see this.” Then those work-men came to her, and she lied to them and said, “You know, my husband brought a man here from the Hebrew tribe. That man doesn’t respect us mob. He wants to make fun of us. He came to me, and he tried to make me sleep with him, like a man sleeps with his wife. But I yelled out really loud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And as soon as he heard me yell out, he left his coat next to me, and he ran out of the house.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 37:32; Jeremiah 4:22; Jeremiah 9:3–5"> After that, Potifar’s wife put down Joseph’s coat next to her, and she waited for Potifar to come home. Potifar was Joseph’s boss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 23:1; 1 Kings 18:17; Psalms 37:14"> Later on, Potifar came home, and his wife said the same things to him that she said to those work-men. She lied and said, “That work-man from the Hebrew tribe, the one that you brought here, he came to me, and he tried to sleep with me and make me shamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> But I yelled out really loud, and straight away, he ran out of the house, and he left his coat next to me.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Potifar put Joseph in jail

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 4:5–6; Proverbs 18:17; Proverbs 6:34–35"> Potifar heard his wife say that Joseph did those bad things to her. And Potifar got really angry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 40:15; 2 Timothy 2:9; Psalms 105:18–19"> Potifar was Joseph’s boss, so he sent his men to grab Joseph, and they put him in jail. That jail belonged to Pharaoh, the big boss of Egypt country. There were other prisoners in that jail too. And Joseph stayed there in the jail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 3:21; Isaiah 41:10; 1 Peter 3:17"> But God was with Joseph, and God kept on being very good to him. God got the boss of that jail to think in a good way about Joseph. So that jail boss liked Joseph a lot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 40:3–4; Genesis 39:4; 1 Samuel 2:30"> and he put Joseph in charge of all the other prisoners in that jail. Whenever those prisoners had work to do, Joseph was their leader, and he made sure that they did their work properly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Genesis 39:2–3; Psalms 1:3"> Joseph did everything well, so that jail boss didn’t worry about anything. He trusted Joseph to look after the prisoners and get all the work done. You see, God was with Joseph, and God made everything go well for him.</span>
</div>
