## The story about Noah’s sons and their families

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 2:4; Genesis 9:7; Genesis 5:1"> This is the story about Noah’s sons and their families. His sons were Shem, and Ham, and Jafeth, and after that big flood, they had kids too.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The tribes from Jafeth’s family

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:2; Ezekiel 38:6; Revelation 20:8"> A long time later, the people that were born into Jafeth’s family split up into different tribes and nations. Each tribe had their own language, and their own country. Jafeth’s sons were Gomer, and Magog, and Madayi, and Javan, and Tubal, and Meshek, and Tiras. The tribes that were from Jafeth’s son Gomer and his family were the Ashkenaz mob, and the Rifath mob, and the Togarmah mob. The tribes that were from Jafeth’s son Javan and his family were the Elishah mob, and the Tarshish mob, and the Kit mob, and the Rodan mob. Javan’s people lived near the sea.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The tribes from Ham’s family

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:8–16; Ezekiel 27:10; Psalms 105:23"> The people that were born into Ham’s family split up into different tribes too, and each tribe had their own language, and their own country. Ham’s sons were Cush, and Egypt, and Put, and Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:22; Ezekiel 27:15; Isaiah 21:13"> The tribes that were from Ham’s son Cush and his family were the Seba mob, and the Havilah mob, and the Sabtah mob, and the Ramah mob, and the Sabteca mob. And the tribes that were from Ramah and his family were the Sheba mob, and the Dedan mob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Micah 5:6"> One of the men that was born into Cush’s family was called Nimrod. After he grew up, he became a strong fighter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:22; Genesis 6:11; Genesis 25:27"> He was the best hunter in the world too. Whenever people talked about good hunters, they said things like this, “That man is a really good hunter. He is just like Nimrod, the best hunter in the world.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 11:9; Genesis 11:2; Genesis 14:1"> Nimrod controlled the country called Babylonia, and he was the boss over the cities called Babel, and Erek, and Akad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Micah 5:6; Psalms 83:8; Nahum 1:1"> After that, Nimrod got the country called Ashur, and he built the towns called Nineveh, and Rehoboth-Ir, and Calah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Then he built a big town called Resen. It was between Nineveh and Calah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:11–12; Jeremiah 46:9; Ezekiel 30:5"> The tribes that were from Ham’s son Egypt and his family were the Lud mob, and the Anam mob, and the Lehab mob, and the Naftuh mob,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 47:4; 1 Chronicles 1:12; Deuteronomy 2:23"> and the Pathrus mob, and the Casluh mob, and the Caftor mob. (Later the Philistia nation were from the Caftor mob.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 34:2–15; Exodus 34:11; Genesis 23:3"> Ham’s youngest son was called Canaan. Canaan had 2 sons. His 1st son was called Sidon, and his other son was called Heth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:18; Judges 1:21; Zechariah 9:7"> The tribes that were from Canaan and his family were the Jebus mob, and the Amor mob, and the Girgash mob,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 34:2"> and the Hiv mob, and the Arek mob, and the Sin mob,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 18:22; 2 Samuel 8:9; Ezekiel 27:8"> and the Arvad mob, and the Zemar mob, and the Hamath mob. All those tribes were from Canaan, and they went as far north as the town called Sidon, and as far south as the town called Gaza, and as far east as the towns called Sodom, and Gomorrah, and Admah, and Zeboyim, near Lasha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 10:6; Genesis 11:1–9"> They were all the tribes and nations that were from Ham and his family. Each tribe had their own country, and their own language.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The tribes from Shem’s family

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 24:24; Genesis 11:10–26"> Shem was Jafeth’s older brother. The Eber mob are from Shem’s family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 66:19; Jeremiah 25:25; Genesis 14:1–9"> Shem’s sons were Elam, and Ashur, and Arfaxad, and Lud, and Aram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 1:1; Jeremiah 25:20"> The tribes that were from Shem’s son Aram and his family were the Uz mob, and the Hul mob, and the Gether mob, and the Mash mob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 3:35; Genesis 11:12–15"> Shem’s son Arfaxad had a son called Shelah, and he had a son called Eber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:19; Genesis 10:32; Deuteronomy 32:8"> Eber had 2 sons. The 1st son was called Peleg, and his younger brother was called Joktan. That name Peleg means split up. He got that name because all the people split up into different tribes after he was born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:20–28"> The tribes that were from Eber’s son Joktan’s family were the Almodad mob, and the Shelef mob, and the Hazarmaveth mob, and the Jerah mob,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:20–28"> and the Hadoram mob, and the Uzal mob, and the Diklah mob,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:20–28; 1 Kings 10:1; Genesis 25:3"> and the Obal mob, and the Abimayel mob, and the Sheba mob,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 9:28; 1 Chronicles 9:13; Genesis 2:11"> and the Ofir mob, and the Havilah mob, and the Jobab mob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 23:7"> All of those people were born into Joktan’s family. Their country was in the mountains in the east, the country you see if you go from Mesha all the way to Sefar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 10:5; Genesis 10:20; Acts 17:26"> They were all the tribes and nations that were from Shem’s family. Each tribe had their own country, and their own language.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 9:19; Acts 17:26; Genesis 10:1"> That is the big list of all the families and tribes that were born into Noah’s family. They split up into their own nations, and they went everywhere after the big flood.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
