## Jesus told his followers to do something different

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 10:9–10; Luke 10:4; Psalms 23:1"> Jesus said to his followers, “Remember that time I told you to go to other towns and tell people God’s story, and I told you not to take a bag, or money, or extra shoes? Did you go short of anything at that time, or did you get everything you needed anyway?” They said, “We got everything we needed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 10:22–25; 1 Thessalonians 3:4; 1 Thessalonians 2:14–15"> Then Jesus said, “But it is different now. Listen, you are going to get a lot of trouble. So if you have a bag, take it with you. And if you have some money, take it with you. And you will need a long knife to fight with too, so if you haven’t got one, sell your coat to get some money and buy one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Luke 23:32; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> You see, something is going to happen now that is in God’s book. A long time ago, God got one of his men to write this,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘The people reckoned God’s man was bad, and they treated him the same way they treat bad criminals.’</span>Listen, I’m telling you, they are going to do that now. They are going to do it to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 22:49; Matthew 26:52–54; John 18:36"> His followers said, “Sir, we’ve got 2 long knives here.” Jesus said, “We don’t need to talk about that any more.”</span>
</div>
