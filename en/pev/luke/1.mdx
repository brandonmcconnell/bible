## An angel messenger told an old man and his wife that they were going to have a baby

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:10; Matthew 2:1; Nehemiah 12:4"> When Herod was the big boss over Judea country, there was a man there that was called Zekariah. He was one of the men that looked after the Jewish ceremonies. There were different groups that did the Jewish ceremonies for the people at different times. He was in the group called the Abijah mob. His wife belonged to a family of Jewish ceremony people too. Her name was Elizabeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 7:1; Genesis 17:1; Philippians 2:15"> Those 2 people, Zekariah and Elizabeth, they were very good people. They did everything in God’s law, and God liked the way they lived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 11:11; Genesis 18:11; 1 Samuel 1:2"> They got very old, but they didn’t have any kids. You see, Elizabeth just couldn’t have a baby.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 24:19; 2 Chronicles 31:2"> The time came for Zekariah’s mob to do the ceremonies in God’s ceremony house, in Jerusalem city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:11; Exodus 30:7–8; 1 Chronicles 23:13"> One day, it was Zekariah’s turn to go into the special room in that house and burn some special perfume to make a good smell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 16:17; Revelation 8:3; Hebrews 4:14"> While he was doing that, a big mob of people were outside, and they were praying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 40:26–27; Acts 10:3–4; Judges 13:3"> Then God sent one of his angel messengers to that room, and he stood on the right-hand side of the metal table where that special perfume was burning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 13:22; Judges 6:22; Luke 2:9–10"> Zekariah saw the angel there and got a big shock. He was really frightened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:21; Acts 10:31; Genesis 25:21"> But the angel told him, “Don’t be frightened, Zekariah. God has heard what you have been asking him, and he will give you what you want. Your wife, Elizabeth, will have a baby boy, and you will name him John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 1:58; Proverbs 23:24; Genesis 21:6"> You will be really happy, and a lot of other people will be happy with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Numbers 6:2–4; Luke 7:33"> God will say that this kid is important, so you have to make sure he never drinks any sort of wine or grog at all. You see, the Holy Spirit will come into this kid before he gets born, while he is still inside his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–5; Matthew 21:32; Malachi 3:1"> And then, after he grows up, he will get lots of you Israel people to turn away from bad things and turn back to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Malachi 4:5–6; Matthew 11:14; Luke 1:76"> Remember Elijah, God’s man that lived a long time ago. He had God’s spirit in him, and he did powerful things. Well, in the same way, your son John will have God’s spirit in him, and he will do powerful things. He will get people ready for God. John will get people to turn around and live the right way. He will get the men that are fathers to love their kids properly. And he will get people to listen properly to the sensible words of good people. Even those people that don’t listen to God now, he will get them to listen to good people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 38:22; Genesis 17:17; Luke 1:7"> Zekariah said to the angel, “But that’s hard for me to believe. I am an old man, and my wife is old too. We are too old now to have a baby. How can I be sure that your message is true?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 1:26; Matthew 18:10; Daniel 9:21–23"> The angel said, “Look, I am Gabriel. I am always close to God, and I do special jobs for him. And he sent me to tell you this good message.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:26; Ezekiel 24:27; Luke 1:22"> And everything I said will happen at the right time. But you didn’t believe me, so now God will shut you up. You will not be able to talk until this kid gets born.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 6:23–27"> While they were talking in that room, the people outside were waiting for Zekariah. They were thinking, “Why is he staying a long time in that room?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 1:62; John 13:24; Acts 21:40"> Then Zekariah came out, and he tried to talk to them, but he couldn’t say anything. He had to make signs with his hands, and at last they understood that he saw something powerful in that room.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:25; 2 Kings 11:5–7"> Then Zekariah finished his days of work in God’s ceremony house, and he went home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Soon after that, his wife, Elizabeth, found out she was going to have a baby. She stayed inside her house for 5 months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 30:22–23; Isaiah 54:1–4; Genesis 21:1–2"> She said, “Wow. God is properly good to me. He will let me have a baby, and nobody will rubbish me any more.”</span>
</div>

## The story about the mother of Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 2:23; Luke 1:19; John 7:41"> That baby kept on growing inside Elizabeth. After about 6 months, God sent Gabriel, that same angel messenger, to Galilee country, to a town called Nazareth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 1:18; Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:16"> He went there to a young girl that was not married. Her name was Mary. Before that time, Mary didn’t ever sleep with any man, like a woman does with her husband. She was promised to a man that was called Joseph. He belonged to the family of David, the man that was the big boss a long time ago.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 6:12; Daniel 10:19; Luke 1:30"> Gabriel came to Mary and said, “Hello, Mary. God loves you and is good to you. He is here with you now, and he will do something good for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 10:17; Luke 1:12; Acts 10:4"> This worried Mary. She thought, “What’s he talking about?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 1:13; Romans 8:31; Luke 12:32"> Gabriel said, “Don’t be frightened, Mary. God is really happy with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:21; Matthew 1:25"> Listen. You will soon have a baby boy, and you will call him Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6–7; Isaiah 16:5; 2 Samuel 7:11–13"> God will make him the biggest boss over you Israel people, like David, his grand-father that lived a long time ago. He will be very important, and he will be the biggest boss for ever. He will never stop being the biggest boss over the Israel people. And people will call him the son of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Judges 13:8–12; Acts 9:6"> Mary said, “But I’m not married. I’ve never slept with a man, like a wife sleeps with her husband. How will I get a baby?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 1:18; John 1:49; Matthew 1:20"> Gabriel said, “The Holy Spirit will come to you, and God’s power will come over you and give you a baby. Your baby will be really special, and people will say he is God’s son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And you know your cousin Elizabeth? Everyone reckoned she is too old to have a baby, but now she is carrying a baby inside her. She has been carrying that baby for the last 6 months, and soon she will have a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 19:26; Jeremiah 32:27; Mark 10:27"> You see, God can do anything. Nothing is too hard for him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Romans 4:20–21; Psalms 119:38; Psalms 116:16"> Mary said, “All right, I will do whatever God wants. I want everything to happen just like you said.” Then Gabriel left her.</span>
</div>

## Mary visited Elizabeth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 1:65; Joshua 15:48–59; Joshua 10:40"> Soon after that, Mary got ready, and she went south quickly to a town in the hilly part of the country called Judea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> Zekariah and Elizabeth lived in that town. Mary went to their house, and she went in and said to Elizabeth, “Hello cousin.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 1:67; Acts 4:8; Luke 1:44"> As soon as Elizabeth heard Mary say those words, she felt her baby jump inside her. And the Holy Spirit came into her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 1:28; Judges 5:24; Genesis 22:18"> and she talked really loud. She said to Mary, “God is really good to you, and he is making you happy. He is being more good to you than to any other woman. And he is being really good to your baby too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 20:28; John 13:13; Luke 2:11"> I’m not important, but you are the mother of my leader, the man from God. It is really good of you to visit me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> You see, as soon as I heard you say ‘hello,’ my baby inside me was so happy he jumped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 20:29; John 11:40; Luke 11:27–28"> Yes, you believed that God will do everything he told you, and so God makes you very happy.”</span>
</div>

## John the Baptiser was born

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Luke 1:13; Genesis 21:2–3; Luke 2:6–7"> Then the time came for Elizabeth to have her baby, and she had a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Luke 1:25; Luke 1:14; Romans 12:15"> All her family, and the people that lived near her, they all heard that she had a baby, and they knew that God felt sorry for Elizabeth, and that he was very good to her. Elizabeth was happy, and they were all happy too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Genesis 17:12; Leviticus 12:3; Philippians 3:5"> When the baby was 8 days old, all those people went to Zekariah and Elizabeth’s place. The baby boy had to have a young man operation on that day, and they used to give a baby his name at that time. The people wanted to call him Zekariah, the same name that his father had.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Luke 1:13; 2 Samuel 12:25; Luke 1:63"> But Elizabeth said, “No. His name is John.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61"> They said, “What? Nobody in your family has that name.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Luke 1:22"> So they made signs with their hands to his father, to ask him what name he wanted to give to the boy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Luke 1:60; Isaiah 30:8; Luke 1:13"> Zekariah got them to give him something to write on, and he wrote, “His name is John.” Everyone was shocked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Luke 1:20; Exodus 4:15–16; Ezekiel 33:22"> Then, straight away, Zekariah started to talk again, and he told everyone that God is very great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Luke 1:39; Acts 5:11; Luke 7:16"> The people there never saw God do things like that before. They were really surprised. They told other people about everything that happened, and soon a lot of people in the hilly part of Judea country were talking about it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Acts 11:21; Luke 2:19; Luke 2:51"> Everyone that heard the news thought about it, and they said, “After this kid grows up, what will he be?” They knew God was powerfully looking after that boy.</span>
