## God is the one that helps us

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">This song is for people to sing as they walk up to God’s house to show him respect.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 120:1; Psalms 123:1; Jeremiah 3:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">I look up at all the hills,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I ask myself, “Who is the one that helps me?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 41:13; Psalms 124:8; Hebrews 13:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then I say, “God is the one that helps me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He is the one that made the earth and the sky.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 3:26; Proverbs 3:23; 1 Samuel 2:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will always look after you so that you don’t get trouble.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He is watching you all the time, to keep you safe.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He never goes to sleep.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 127:1; Isaiah 27:3; Psalms 32:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">That’s right. He always watches the Israel people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he never sleeps.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 25:4; Psalms 91:1; Isaiah 4:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">God is the one that looks after you properly.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">When the sun is really hot,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">you know how good it is to rest under a shady tree.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Well, when life gets tough for you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">God will be close to you and help you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">like he is a shady tree for you.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 7:16; Isaiah 49:10; Psalms 91:5–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yes, God will always look after you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the day time and in the night time.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He will always keep you safe.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will stop anything from hurting you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Not even the hot sun or the moon will hurt you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He will not let anything finish you.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:6; Proverbs 2:8; Proverbs 3:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Whenever you go anywhere,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">God will go with you all the way,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he will keep you safe.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will do that now,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he will keep doing that all the time, for ever.</span></span>
</div>
</div>
