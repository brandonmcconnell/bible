## Only people that live in a good way can visit God’s ceremony house

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">This is one of David’s songs for God.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 24:3–5; Psalms 84:4; Psalms 61:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, who do you let visit your ceremony house to show you respect?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Who do you let live there on your special hill?</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 28:18; Ephesians 4:25; Colossians 3:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">I know the sort of people that God lets in his ceremony house.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You can visit God’s ceremony house, if you are like this.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You always live in a good way,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">and always do the right thing,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">and only say true things.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 13:10; Psalms 101:5–8; Matthew 7:12"> <span className="pl pl-2">You don’t tell lies about people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">and you don’t do bad things to people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">and you don’t say bad things about people to shame them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 11:35; Matthew 5:33; Psalms 101:6"> <span className="pl pl-2">You never respect really bad people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">but you respect people that properly respect God.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You always keep your promises.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">Maybe it will be hard,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">maybe it will hurt you to keep those promises,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">but you still keep them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 22:25; Deuteronomy 16:19; Ezekiel 22:12"> <span className="pl pl-2">And whenever somebody needs help,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">you let them borrow your money.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">And later on, when they pay you back that money,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">you only take that same money that they borrowed from you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">you don’t make them pay you any more money on top of that.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And another thing. If somebody says to you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">“I want you to go to court and tell lies about good people.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">I will pay you money to tell those lies.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">You always say, “No, I can’t tell lies about people.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">All of those things that I just talked about in this song,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">everybody that lives that way will always be really strong for God.</span></span>
