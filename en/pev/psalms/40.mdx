## If people trust God, he makes them happy

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">This song is for the music leader, and it is one of David’s songs for God.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 27:13–14; Psalms 37:7; James 5:7–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">I had really bad trouble.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">It’s like I was in a deep hole that people die in.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It’s like I was stuck in a bad place,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like my legs were stuck in sticky mud.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But I trusted God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I waited for him to help me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I cried out to him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he listened to me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God helped me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He saved me from that trouble.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It’s like he lifted me out of that mud,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and put my feet on good hard ground.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God kept me safe.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">It’s like he gave me a safe path to walk on.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 33:3; Psalms 103:1–5; Psalms 34:1–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then God gave me a new song to sing to him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">That song says that God is great.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Lots of people will hear about everything that God did,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will know that he has all the power.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Then they will only trust him.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:7–8; Psalms 84:11–12; Psalms 34:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">If people trust God properly,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">God makes those people happy.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Whenever they have trouble, they trust God to help them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They don’t go to people that think they are great, or people that tell lies.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:11; Psalms 139:17–18; Exodus 15:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, my God, you have done lots of powerful things for us.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And you have lots and lots of good plans for us.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Nobody is like you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You do so many great things that I can’t say them all.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">If I try to say them all,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I’ll talk for ever and never stop talking.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 10:5–12; Isaiah 1:11; 1 Samuel 15:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Some people kill animals to pay you for the bad things they did.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And they give you animals and food as gifts.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But that does not really make you happy.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You don’t need people to burn animals for you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You don’t need people to give you gifts.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You taught me all of this.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 10:7–9; Luke 24:44; John 5:39"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I said to you, “Here I am. I came.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Your book tells me the things that I should do.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 7:22; John 4:34; Jeremiah 15:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, it makes me really happy to do everything that you want.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I always think about your laws and obey them.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 22:25; Mark 16:15–16; Psalms 22:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Whenever your people come together to respect you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I tell them, “God helps his people.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I always talk like that, I don’t keep my mouth shut.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You know that’s true, God.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 1:16–17; Acts 20:20–21; Romans 10:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">I know that you always do what is right.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I don’t keep it a secret inside of me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I tell everyone, “We can trust God. He saves us.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I don’t hide the true story from your people.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I tell them, “God always loves us. We can always trust him.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 23:6; Psalms 43:3; Psalms 57:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Whenever I have troubles, God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you feel sorry for me, and you are good to me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You always love me, and I can always trust you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You keep me safe.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 38:4; Psalms 73:26; Psalms 69:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I have lots of troubles now.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">It feels like there are troubles all around me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">lots of troubles, so I can’t count them all.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It’s like all the bad things I did are here with me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I don’t know the right thing to do.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I did lots of bad things,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">more bad things than the number of hairs on my head,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so now I feel weak inside.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 25:17–18; Psalms 70:1–5; Psalms 38:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, please save me from all this trouble.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">God, please come quickly, and help me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 35:26; Psalms 35:4; Psalms 71:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Some people want to kill me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">God, please make them feel shame and make them confused.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Those people are happy whenever I get hurt.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">God, please make them run away and be sad.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 35:21; Psalms 69:24–25; Psalms 35:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those people make fun of me, and they laugh at me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">God, please make them shamed, so that they feel really sad.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 35:27; Psalms 68:3; Acts 19:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">But there are some people that always come to you God.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Please make those people really happy.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They say, “God, thank you for saving us.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And I pray that they always say, “God is great.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 70:5; Hebrews 13:6; Isaiah 41:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">But what about me?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I have lots of trouble and I need help,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so I want God to think about me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God, you are the one that helps me and saves me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">God, my God, please come and help me now and don’t wait.</span></span>
</div>
</div>
