## God will help us

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">This is one of the songs of the Korah mob. It’s for the leader of music at God’s ceremony house.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 145:18; Psalms 9:9; Proverbs 18:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">God is strong, and he is always ready to help us when we get trouble.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He is like a safe place for us to hide.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Whenever we get trouble,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he is the one that can help us.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 23:4; Hebrews 13:6; Psalms 27:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">So we will never get frightened.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Even if there is a big earthquake,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the mountains start to break up and fall into the sea,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">even then we will not be frightened.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 93:3–4; Jeremiah 5:22; Job 9:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Even if the waves in the sea get really big and loud,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and if the mountains shake,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">even then we will not be frightened.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 22:1–3; Ezekiel 47:1–12; Psalms 36:8–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is a river in God’s city,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and it makes the people there happy.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">That place is God’s home.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He is the greatest one of all.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 2:5; Zephaniah 3:15; Joel 2:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">God lives in that city.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Nothing can ever knock it down.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God will stop anybody from hurting the people in that city.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He will look after them all the time,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">right from the start of every day.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 9:5; Psalms 18:13; Habakkuk 3:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">You see, God is powerful,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he frightens the nations that don’t follow him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So they go wild and don’t think properly,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they get weak and useless.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It’s like God just yells at those people and they finish up,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like ice that melts in the hot sun.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Romans 8:31; Numbers 14:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">God is the boss of everything,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he is with us and helps us.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He is the God of us Israel people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he will stop anyone from hurting us.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 66:5; Isaiah 24:1; Isaiah 61:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Come and look at what God does.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He does things on the earth that will shock you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 2:4; Micah 4:3–4; Micah 5:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">He stops wars all over the world.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">It’s like he breaks all the things that people use to fight with,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">like their spears and bows,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and it’s like he burns all the shields with fire.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 100:3; Habakkuk 2:20; Ezekiel 38:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">God says, “Stop, you mob. </div>
