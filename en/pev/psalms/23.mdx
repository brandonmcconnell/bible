## God looks after me properly

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">This is one of David’s songs for God.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Philippians 4:19; John 10:11; Isaiah 40:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">God looks after me properly,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he gives me everything that I need.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You know about people that properly look after sheep all the time, called shepherds.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Well, God looks after me like that.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He is a good shepherd for me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 7:17; Revelation 22:1; Ezekiel 34:13–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">God gives me a good place to rest,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">just like a shepherd finds a good place for his sheep to rest,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a place where there is green grass for them to eat</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">and clean water for them to drink.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 42:16; Psalms 31:3; Psalms 51:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">When I get tired, God makes me feel good and strong again.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He leads me on the right track,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so that people will know that he is good.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Psalms 118:6; Psalms 138:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">You know, God is with me when I have trouble too.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Sometimes I have a lot of trouble,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I feel like I am walking in a really dark valley, and something might kill me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but even then, God is with me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will not be frightened at that time.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I know that God is stopping my enemies from hurting me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">just like a shepherd uses his stick and stops wild dogs from hurting his sheep.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">When I remember that he does that,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I feel safe.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 3:20; Psalms 92:10; Psalms 45:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">God does good things for me even when people are giving me trouble,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he makes my enemies watch him do those good things.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It is like I am a visitor at his house,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he cooks a big dinner for me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I sit down and eat it.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And he makes my enemies watch me eat it.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It is like he fills my cup with good drink,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">right to the top.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And it is like he shows everyone that I am his friend,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">by putting olive oil on my head.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 27:4; 2 Timothy 4:18; 2 Corinthians 5:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am sure that God will always love me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he will be good to me all of my life.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And I will always be in God’s house,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will respect him properly for ever.</span></span>
</div>
