## Find out for yourself that God is really good

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">This is one of David’s songs for God. It’s from the time when David pretended he was crazy in front of big boss Abimelek, so Abimelek sent David away.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:18; Colossians 3:17; Psalms 145:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will always say, “God is good.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Yes, I will keep on saying good things about God.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:24; 1 Corinthians 1:31; Psalms 119:74"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will tell people, “God has all the power.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I want everybody that has a hard life to hear that and be happy.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 69:30; Luke 1:46; Psalms 148:1–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Come here everybody. I want us all to say, “God is great.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Let’s come together and say, “God is really powerful.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 18:6; Matthew 7:7; Psalms 116:1–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">You see, I prayed to God, and he listened to me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He took away everything that frightened me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Psalms 123:1–2; Psalms 18:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">People that trust God are really happy.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They will never feel shame.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 34:17–19; Psalms 3:4; Psalms 10:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">I had a lot of trouble, but I prayed to God, and he listened to me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He saved me from all my troubles.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 91:11; Daniel 6:22; Hebrews 1:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Some people know that God is powerful,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so they have strong respect for him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God sends his special angel spirit to be with those people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and that angel keeps those people safe from trouble.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 6:4–5; Psalms 119:103; 1 Peter 2:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Find out for yourself that God is really good.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Look at all the things he does.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Whenever you get trouble, ask God to look after you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He will do that, and he will make you really happy.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 8:32; Philippians 4:19; Psalms 23:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">You are God’s special people.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You know that he is powerful, so you have to really respect him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">If you really respect God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he will give you everything that you need.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 84:11; Matthew 6:32; Psalms 104:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Sometimes even strong young lions can’t find food and they go hungry,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but people that trust God, they have all the good things that they need.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 1:7; Proverbs 2:1–9; Psalms 111:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Come here everyone, and listen to me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will teach you to respect God properly.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:10–12; Deuteronomy 30:20; Deuteronomy 6:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do you want to live well?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Do you want a long and happy life?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 3:5–10; Psalms 141:3; Proverbs 21:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">If that’s what you want,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">don’t say bad things about other people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and don’t lie to them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 37:27; 1 Peter 3:11; Hebrews 12:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Turn away from doing bad things, and do good things.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Don’t fight with people or make trouble.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Try very hard to live happily with other people.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:12; Psalms 34:17; Psalms 33:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">God looks after people that live the right way.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And if they ask him for help, he listens to them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; Jeremiah 17:13; Jeremiah 44:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">But God punishes people that do bad things.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">After they die, God doesn’t want anybody in the world to remember</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">those bad people.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">So God will make everybody forget them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 145:18–20; Isaiah 65:24; Psalms 34:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">But some people live the right way,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and whenever they ask God for help, he listens to them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He saves them from all their troubles.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 147:3; Psalms 145:18; Isaiah 57:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Some people are really sad and feel hurt inside.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">God stays really close to those people.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And some people think nothing good will happen to them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">God helps those people too.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 34:17; 1 Peter 4:12–13; Psalms 34:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">If somebody lives the right way,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that person might still have a lot of troubles.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">But God will save that person from all those troubles.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 19:36; Psalms 35:10; Psalms 91:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">God will keep that person safe,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and nobody will break any of that person’s bones.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 94:23; Proverbs 24:16; Isaiah 3:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">But people that do bad things,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will get trouble and die.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And if somebody hates people that live the right way,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">God will punish that person.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 103:4; 1 Peter 1:18–19; Psalms 31:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">But if people work hard for God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he will keep them safe.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And if people trust God and if they ask him to look after them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">God will not punish them.</span></span>
</div>
