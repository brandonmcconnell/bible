## A woman that was called Hannah asked God for a baby boy of her own

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:34; 1 Chronicles 6:25–27; 1 Samuel 1:19"> There was a man called Elkanah. He was from Ephraim’s tribe, and he lived in a town called Ramah in the hill country. His father was Jeroham, his grand-father was Elihu, and his great grand-father was Tohu. He belonged to the Zuf family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 16:1–2; Judges 13:2; Genesis 29:23–29"> He had 2 wives called Hannah and Penninah. Penninah had some kids, but Hannah had none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 2:41; Joshua 18:1; Deuteronomy 12:5–7"> Every year Elkanah used to go with his family from Ramah to Shiloh. They went to Shiloh to show respect to God, the boss of everything, and to burn meat for him. At that time, God’s ceremony house was in Shiloh. An old man was there called Eli. He was the leader of God’s ceremonies. His 2 sons, Hofni and Finiyas, also worked in God’s house. They looked after God’s ceremonies too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:17–18; Deuteronomy 16:11; Deuteronomy 12:5–7"> When Elkanah was in Shiloh, he used to burn meat to give it to God, and he gave some of it to his family to eat. He gave meat to Penninah and her kids.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 30:2; Genesis 29:30–31; Genesis 45:22"> His other wife, Hannah, had no kids. You see, before this time, God stopped her from having kids. But Elkanah loved her very much, so he used to give lots of meat to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 6:14; Job 24:21; Leviticus 18:18"> But Penninah used to make fun of her because she had no kids. So Hannah got upset.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:19"> Every year it was like that. Whenever they went up to God’s house at Shiloh, Penninah used to make fun of Hannah until she cried. She felt so sad she couldn’t eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ruth 4:15; Job 6:14; Isaiah 54:6"> Her husband used to ask her, “Why are you crying? You have to eat. Don’t be sad. Don’t keep on worrying that you have no sons. I love you very much anyway.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:3; Psalms 27:4; Psalms 5:7"> One time at Shiloh, after they finished eating and drinking, Hannah got up and went to God’s house to talk to God. The old man Eli sat on a chair at the door of God’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 7:11; Luke 22:44; Job 10:1"> Hannah was so sad. She cried and prayed to God like this,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 13:5; Numbers 6:5; Genesis 29:32"> “God, you are the boss of everything. I am just your worker. I’m sad. Please help me. Don’t forget about me. I want to ask you this. Let me have a little boy. If you do that, I’ll give him back to you, so he can work for you until he gets old. Nobody will cut his hair, so that other people will see that he belongs to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:17; James 5:16; Luke 18:1"> She talked quietly to God for a long time. Eli saw her mouth moving, but couldn’t hear anything, so he thought she was drunk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 4:25; Job 8:2; Proverbs 6:9"> He said, “Why did you come here drunk? Throw that wine away.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 62:8; Lamentations 2:19; Psalms 42:4"> Hannah answered, “No, old man. I’m not drunk. I haven’t drunk any wine at all. I’m a sad woman. I talked to God, and I told him my story.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:12; Job 6:2–3; Matthew 12:34–35"> Please don’t call me no good. I am properly sad. I want God to help me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 5:34; Judges 18:6; Psalms 20:3–5"> Eli said, “Don’t worry. Go now. We Israel mob follow a good God. He will do whatever you ask him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ruth 2:13; Romans 15:13; Ecclesiastes 9:7"> Hannah said to him, “Thank you, old man. You have made me feel happy now.” And she left God’s house, and she went and ate some food. Her face was not sad any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 21:1; Genesis 30:22; 1 Samuel 1:11"> The next day, Elkanah got up and went with his family to show respect and pray to God once again. Then they went home to Ramah. Then Elkanah slept with his wife, Hannah, like married people do together. But God didn’t forget that Hannah asked for a boy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 41:51–52; Matthew 1:21; Exodus 2:10"> Some time later, she really did have a little boy, and she named him Samuel. That name means God heard. She said, “I asked God for him, and he heard me, so I’ll name him Samuel.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Hannah promised to give the boy Samuel to God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:3; Deuteronomy 12:11; Joshua 24:15"> A year later Elkanah went with his family to Shiloh as usual, to show respect to God, and to burn meat and give it to him. You see, long before Elkanah promised to do that, so he wanted to keep on doing it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:28; 1 Samuel 1:11; Luke 2:22"> But his wife Hannah stayed at home. She said, “My baby is still drinking my milk. After he stops drinking my milk, I will take him to Shiloh to give him to God. Then he will stay at that place all the time.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:25; Luke 11:27; Genesis 21:7–8"> Elkanah said, “All right, if you like, stay here until your baby stops drinking your milk. After that, God will help you to keep your promise.” So Hannah stayed home with her little boy while he was still drinking her milk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 15:9–10; Joshua 18:1; 1 Samuel 4:3–4"> Some time later, when the boy was bigger, he stopped drinking her milk. Elkanah went with his family as usual to God’s ceremony house at Shiloh. Hannah and her son went too. They took a big bull, a bag of flour and a big leather water bag full of wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 18:15–16; Luke 2:22"> Elkanah and Hannah killed the bull and burned it for God. Then they took the boy to Eli, the old man that looked after God’s ceremonies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 4:30; 2 Kings 2:4; 2 Kings 2:6"> Hannah said to him, “Old man, do you remember me? A long time ago, I stood here with you. You heard the way I cried and prayed to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 John 5:15; Psalms 118:5; Matthew 7:7"> I wanted a little boy, and God heard me. He let me have this one, just like I asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 24:26; Genesis 24:52; 2 Timothy 3:15"> Now I want to give him to God. He will work for God until he gets old.” After she told him that, they showed respect to God.</span>
</div>
</div>
