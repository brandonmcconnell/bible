## Saul was jealous of David

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 15:20; Judges 11:34; Psalms 68:25"> Then the Israel mob all went back home, and women came from lots of places to meet Saul, the big boss. They danced and sang happily. Some played little drums and others played guitars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:11; 1 Samuel 29:5; Exodus 15:21"> They sang like this as they went,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Saul killed dead a thousand enemy soldiers.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">David killed dead 10,000 of them.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:28; Proverbs 13:10; James 4:5"> Saul heard their words, and he was really angry. He thought, “They reckon David killed more enemy soldiers than me. Maybe they want to make him the boss of us Israel mob, instead of me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 7:22; Genesis 31:2; Matthew 20:15"> From then on, he was jealous of David, and he watched him all the time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:9; 2 Thessalonians 2:11; 1 Kings 18:29"> The next day, God sent a bad spirit to Saul. It made him go really mad. He just sat there in his house and screamed. David played the guitar for him as before. Saul was holding a spear in his hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:33; Isaiah 54:17; Luke 4:30"> and he thought, “I will spear David straight through and hang him on the wall.” So he threw it at him. But David quickly jumped out of the way, and the spear missed him. That happened 2 times, and he missed David each time.</span>
