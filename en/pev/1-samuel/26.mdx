## David shamed Saul again

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:19; Psalms 54:1–7; Joshua 15:24"> One day, some men from a place called Zif went to see Saul at his place at Gibeah. They said, “David’s mob are hiding in a cave at the hill called Hakilah near Jeshimon town.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:2; 1 Samuel 13:2; Psalms 140:4–9"> Saul quickly gathered together 3,000 of his best soldiers from Israel. They went off to look for David in the desert called Zif.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:19; 1 Samuel 26:1"> They camped in the hill country beside the road. David’s mob stayed in the bush. After he heard that Saul was at Hakilah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:16; Joshua 2:1"> he sent some men to have a look. They saw that Saul’s mob really was there, so they came back and told David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:55; 1 Samuel 17:20; 1 Samuel 14:50–51"> That night David went close to Saul’s camp. From his hiding place he saw Saul and the boss soldier Abner, Ner’s son. They slept in the middle. The other soldiers all slept around them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 7:10–11; 2 Samuel 2:18; Genesis 15:20"> There were 2 men with David. They were Ahimelek, the man who used to belong to the Heth mob, and Abishai, Zeruiah’s son. Zeruiah was David’s older sister. Her other son was Joab. David said to them, “I am going to go quietly into the middle of their camp, to where Saul is. Which of you 2 will do that with me?” Abishai said, “I’ll go with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2–3"> So in the middle of the night, David and Abishai went quietly into Saul’s camp. Everybody was asleep. They found Saul asleep in the middle of the camp. His spear stood there, stuck in the ground near his head. Other soldiers lay asleep nearby. One of them was Abner, the boss of Saul’s soldiers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:4; 1 Samuel 26:23; Deuteronomy 32:30"> Abishai said, “Boss. Look at this. God wants you to kill that old man. He did wrong to you. I will stab him right through with his spear. I’ll only stab him one time. I’ll use that spear like it is a big pin, and I’ll pin him to the ground, and he will die straight away.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:14; Psalms 105:15; 2 Samuel 1:16"> But David answered, “You can’t kill him. God will punish you. He picked Saul to be our big boss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 47:29; Deuteronomy 31:14; Psalms 37:13"> Some time God himself will finish him up, maybe through sickness, maybe through fighting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:6; 1 Samuel 24:12; 2 Samuel 1:14"> But not us. We can’t kill him. God does not want me to kill this man that he picked. Let’s just take his spear, and that billycan of water that is near his head, and we will go back.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 2:21; Genesis 15:12; Isaiah 29:10"> So David took the spear and the billycan, and they went away quietly. Nobody saw them. God made everybody sleep properly, so nobody woke up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:8; Judges 9:7"> Then they went across to the other side of the valley and stood on the top of a hill a long way away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> David called out to Abner, “Hey, Abner. Can you hear me?” He answered, “Who’s that calling out? Who’s waking up the big boss?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> David made fun of him, and he said, “Abner, what kind of man are you? You call yourself a big boss soldier, greater than all the other soldiers. But you don’t look after your boss. Just now somebody went into your camp and nearly killed him dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 79:11; 2 Samuel 12:5; 1 Samuel 26:9"> You are a boss soldier for nothing. God should kill you, and your soldiers too. You mob don’t look after the big boss, the one God picked. Look there. Where’s the big boss’s spear? Where’s his billycan? I’ve got those 2 things here.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:16; 1 Samuel 24:8"> Saul heard David’s voice, and said, “David, my son. Is that you talking?” David answered, “Yes, boss. It’s me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:9; 1 Samuel 24:11–14; John 10:32"> Why do you always chase me? I did not do anything wrong. Why do you blame me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:16; 2 Samuel 16:11; 2 Samuel 20:19"> Listen carefully to me. Did God tell you to hate me? If yes, then I have to burn meat to give to him, so he will change his mind. But maybe it was some other men that told you to hate me? They hunted me away from my people, so God has to punish them. They don’t let me live in my country. I’m like a stranger. I have to live in another country now. The people there follow other gods. I can’t live for God properly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:14; Matthew 26:55; 1 Samuel 25:29"> This place is a long way from God. I do not want to die here. I’m just nobody, not a boss man. Why do you keep on looking for me? You keep on chasing me like you are hunting for meat.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:24; 1 Samuel 24:17; Exodus 9:27"> Saul said, “I did the wrong thing. You are good to me. I can’t do wrong to you again. You didn’t kill me, but I truly did wrong. I was a stupid fool. You are like my son. Come back to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> David said, “Your spear is here. Send a man to come and get it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 62:12; 1 Samuel 24:6–7; Psalms 18:20–26"> Just now God brought us together, and I did not kill you. He picked you to be boss, so I will not kill you. God is good to people that listen to him. He is good to people that live straight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; 2 Corinthians 1:9–10; Psalms 54:7"> That’s why I want him to be good to me. I want him to keep me alive, just like I was good to you today, and did not kill you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 24:9–10; Proverbs 26:25; 1 Samuel 24:19"> Then Saul said, “Truly God will be good to you. You will do great things for him. Whatever you do will work out well.” Then David left that place, and Saul went back home.</span>
</div>
