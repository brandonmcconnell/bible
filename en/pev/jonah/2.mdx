## Jonah prayed to God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Lamentations 3:53–56; Psalms 130:1–2; Psalms 91:15"> Then Jonah prayed to God from inside that fish. He talked about the things that happened to him in the water before that fish came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 18:4–6; Psalms 120:1; Psalms 34:6"> He said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Yahweh, when I got trouble, I called out to you, and you answered me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I nearly died, but I called out for help, and you listened to me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 42:7; Lamentations 3:54; Psalms 69:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">This is what happened.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">First you made me go down into deep water, into the middle of the sea.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">The water moved all around me. You sent big waves over me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 31:22; Psalms 5:7; 2 Chronicles 6:38"> <span className="pl pl-1">I knew that you sent me away. You didn’t want to look at me any more.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">But I also knew that I will get to go to your good ceremony house again.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 69:1–2; Lamentations 3:54; Psalms 40:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">That water tried to drown me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Deep water was all around me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Seaweed wrapped around my head.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 30:3; Psalms 16:10; Isaiah 38:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">I sank down to the bottom of the sea, deep down in the earth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">It was like gates locked around me, I thought I was stuck there for ever.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But you, my God, you saved my life. You pulled me up from that deep dark place.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 18:6; 2 Chronicles 30:27; Psalms 11:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">My life was finishing up, and I called out to you my God.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">My prayer went up to you, to your special place where you live.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 31:6; 2 Kings 17:15; Jeremiah 2:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, you are good to your people, and you keep your promises.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">But some people don’t respect you, and they turn away from you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Those people show respect to things they think are gods that have no power.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">So those people will miss out on the good things that you do for your people.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 3:8; Psalms 50:14; Ecclesiastes 5:4–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">But God, I will sing to you to say thank you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And I will kill animals to give them to you, to show respect to you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I promise I will do that.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You are the only one that saves people.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 8:26–27; Psalms 33:9; Genesis 1:3"> And then God talked to that fish, and it spat Jonah on to dry ground.</span>
