<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 5:7; Exodus 22:25–27; 1 Corinthians 6:6–8"> And there arose a great outcry of the people and of their wives against their brethren the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Malachi 2:2; Psalms 128:2–4; Psalms 127:3–5"> And there were some that said, Our sons, and our daughters, &#91;and&#93; ourselves are many; and we must buy corn, that we may eat, and live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 25:35–39; Malachi 3:8–11; Genesis 47:15–25"> And others there were that said, We must pledge our fields, our vineyards, and our houses, that we may buy corn, in the famine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezra 4:13; Ezra 4:20; Deuteronomy 28:47–48"> And others there were that said, We have borrowed money for the king's tax on our fields and vineyards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 4:1; Leviticus 25:39–43; Isaiah 58:7"> Yet now our flesh is like the flesh of our brethren, our children are like their children: and, lo, we must force our sons and our daughters to become servants, and some of our daughters are forced &#91;to become so&#93;, and our hand is powerless; and our fields and our vineyards belong to others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 11:8; Nehemiah 13:25; Ephesians 4:26"> And it displeased me greatly when I heard their complaint and these words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:19–20; Leviticus 25:36; Exodus 22:25"> Then did I consult with my heart, and I upbraided the nobles, and the rulers, and said unto them, “Ye exact usury, every one of his brother!” And I brought together a great assembly against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 25:47–49; 1 Corinthians 8:11; Matthew 25:29"> And I said unto them, We have indeed ransomed our brethren the Jews, who had been sold unto the various nations, as far as our means went: and will ye yourselves even sell your brethren, so that they will be sold again unto us? And they remained silent, and found no answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 25:36; 2 Samuel 12:14; Romans 2:24"> Then said I, The thing is not good which ye are doing: ought ye not to walk in the fear of our God, because of the taunting of the nations, our enemies?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:1; Exodus 22:25–27; Ezekiel 18:13"> And also I, my brothers, and my young men, have lent them money and corn: I pray you, let us relinquish this loan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 58:6; Leviticus 6:4–5; Luke 3:8"> Give back to them, I pray you, even this day, their fields, their vineyards, their oliveyards, and their houses, also the hundredth part of the money, and of the corn, the wine, and the oil, that ye have lent them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezra 10:5; Jeremiah 34:8–10; Matthew 19:21–22"> Then said they, We will give &#91;all&#93; back, and of them will we require nothing: so will we do as thou sayest. Then I called the priests, and made them swear, that they would do in accordance with this promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 18:6; 1 Chronicles 16:36; Matthew 10:14"> Also my lap did I shake out, and said, So may God shake out every man that performeth not this promise, from his house and of his toil-gotten wealth, and so let him remain shaken out, and empty. And all the assembly said, Amen, and they praised the Lord. And the people did according to this promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:6; 2 Thessalonians 3:8–9; 1 Corinthians 9:4–15"> Moreover from the day that &#91;the king&#93; had enjoined on me to be governor in the land of Judah, from the twentieth year up to the two and thirtieth year of king Artaxerxes, &#91;full&#93; twelve years, neither I nor my brothers ate the food of the governor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:9; Isaiah 50:10; Proverbs 16:6"> But the former governors that had been before me had made it heavy for the people, and had taken of them bread and wine, beside forty shekels of silver: yea, even their young men ruled over the people; but I myself did not act so, because of the fear of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; 1 Thessalonians 2:5–6; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> And in the work of this wall also did I labor actively; and we bought not any fields; and all my young men were assembled there by the work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 18:19; 1 Peter 4:9–10; 2 Samuel 9:7"> Moreover of the Jews and rulers, one hundred and fifty men, besides those that came unto us from the nations that are about us, &#91;ate&#93; at my table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 4:22–23; Psalms 37:21; Psalms 37:26"> And that which was prepared for one day was one ox and six choice sheep; also birds were prepared for me, and once in ten days all sorts of wine in abundance: yet with all this I required not the food of the governor; because the service lay heavily upon this people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:31; Psalms 106:4; Nehemiah 13:14"> Remember for me, my God, for good, all that I have done for this people.</span>
