<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 1:2; Deuteronomy 4:26; Jeremiah 22:29"> Give ear, O ye heavens, and I will speak; and let the earth hear the words of my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 55:10–11; Psalms 72:6; Micah 5:7"> My doctrine shall drop as the rain, my speech shall distil as the dew, as heavy rains upon the grass, and as showers upon herbs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:6; Psalms 150:2; Ephesians 1:19"> When I call on the name of the Lord, ascribe ye greatness unto our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 18:30–31; Psalms 92:15; Isaiah 26:4"> He is the Rock, his work is perfect; for all his ways are just: the God of truth and without iniquity, just and upright is he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 1:4; Philippians 2:15; Matthew 17:17"> The corruption is not his, it is the defect of his children, of the perverse and crooked generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 63:16; 1 John 3:1; Isaiah 44:2"> Will ye thus requite the Lord, O people, worthless and unwise? is he not thy father who hath bought thee? is it not he who hath made thee, and established thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 78:3–4; Job 8:8–10; Psalms 44:1"> Remember the days of old, consider the years of former generations; ask thy father, and he will tell thee; thy elders, and they will say it unto thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 17:26; Genesis 11:8–9; Daniel 4:17"> When the Most High divided to the nations their inheritance, when he separated the sons of man: he set the bounds of the tribes according to the number of the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:16; 1 Peter 2:9–10; Exodus 19:5–6"> For the portion of the Lord is his people; Jacob is the lot of his inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 2:8; Psalms 17:8; Hosea 13:5"> He found him in a desert land, and in the waste of the howling of the wilderness; he encircled him, he watched him, he guarded him as the apple of his eye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 19:4; Isaiah 46:4; Isaiah 63:9"> As an eagle stirreth up his nest, fluttereth over his young, spreadeth abroad his wings, seizeth them, beareth them aloft on his pinions:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 78:52–53; Isaiah 46:4; Isaiah 44:7–8"> So did the Lord alone lead him, and there was not with him a stranger god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 58:14; Job 29:6; Psalms 81:16"> He caused him to stride on the high places of the earth, and he ate the products of the fields; and he made him to suck honey out of the rock, and oil out of the flinty stone;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 49:11; Psalms 81:16; Psalms 147:14"> Cream of cows, and milk of sheep, with fat of lambs, and rams of the breed of Bashan, and goats, with the fat of the kidneys of wheat; and of the blood of the grape thou drunkest unmixed wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:20; Hosea 13:6; Deuteronomy 33:5"> Thus did Yeshurun grow fat, and he kicked; &#91;thou art grown fat, thick, fleshy;&#93; and then he forsook the God who made him, and lightly esteemed the Rock of his salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 78:58; 1 Corinthians 10:22; Nahum 1:1–2"> They incensed him with strange gods, with abominations they provoked him to anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 5:8; Deuteronomy 28:64; Leviticus 17:7"> They sacrificed unto evil spirits, things that are not god, gods that they know not, new ones lately come up, which your fathers dreaded not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 17:10; Jeremiah 2:32; Psalms 106:21"> Of the Rock that begat thee thou wast unmindful, and forgottest the God that had brought thee forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 106:40; Psalms 5:4; Isaiah 1:2"> And the Lord saw this, and he was angry; because of the provoking of his sons and of his daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:17–18; Luke 7:31–32; Deuteronomy 32:5"> And he said, I will hide my face from them, I will see what their end will be; for a perverse generation are they, children in whom there is no faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 10:19; Romans 9:25; Romans 11:11–14"> They have moved me to wrath with things that are not god; they have provoked me to anger with their vanities; and I too will move them to jealousy with those which are not a people; I will provoke them to anger with a worthless nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Lamentations 4:11; Jeremiah 15:14; Micah 1:4"> For a fire is kindled in my anger, and it burneth unto the lowest deep; and it consumeth the earth with her products, and it setteth on fire the foundations of the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:16; Psalms 7:12–13; Isaiah 24:17–18"> I will heap upon them miseries; all my arrows will I spend upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 26:22; Ezekiel 5:17; Isaiah 65:25"> They shall be wasted with hunger, and devoured with burning heat, and with bitter deadly disease; also the tooth of beasts will I let loose against them, with the poison of serpents that crawl in the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:15; Lamentations 1:20; Isaiah 30:16"> Without shall the sword destroy, and terror within the chambers, both the young man and the virgin, the suckling with the man of gray hairs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:64; Leviticus 26:33; Deuteronomy 28:37"> I said, I would drive them into one corner, I would cause their remembrance to cease from among men:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 14:15–16; Exodus 32:12; Zechariah 1:14–15"> Were it not that I feared the wrath of the enemy, lest their oppressors should mistake the truth, lest they should say, Our hand is high, and the Lord hath not wrought all this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:22; Isaiah 27:11; Job 28:28"> For a nation void of counsel are they, and there is no understanding in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 81:13; Deuteronomy 5:29; Hosea 14:9"> If they were but wise, they would understand this, they would consider their latter end!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 26:8; Joshua 23:10; Isaiah 30:17"> How should one chase a thousand, and two put ten thousand to flight, unless their Rock had sold them, and the Lord had delivered them up?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 14:25; 1 Samuel 2:2; Daniel 2:47"> For not as our Rock is their rock, even our enemies themselves being judges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:18; Ezekiel 16:45–51; Isaiah 1:10"> For from the vine of Sodom is their vine, and from the fields of Gomorrah; their grapes are grapes of gall, they bear bitter clusters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 58:4; Romans 3:13; Psalms 140:3"> The poison of serpents is their wine, and the deadly venom of asps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hosea 13:12; Job 14:17; Jeremiah 2:22"> Behold! this is laid up in store with me, it is sealed up among my treasures!</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Romans 12:19; Hebrews 10:30; Nahum 1:2"> Mine are vengeance and recompense, at the time that their foot shall slip; for nigh draweth the day of their calamity, and the future speedeth along for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 135:14; Psalms 106:45; Judges 2:18"> For the Lord will espouse the cause of his people, and bethink himself concerning his servants: when he seeth that their power is gone, and the guarded and fortified axe no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Judges 10:14; 2 Kings 3:13"> Then will he say, Where are their gods, the rock in whom they trusted,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:18–19; Psalms 50:13; Judges 10:14"> They that ate the fat of their sacrifices, and drank the wine of their drink-offerings? let them arise and help you, let them be a protection over you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:6; Hosea 6:1; Job 5:18"> See now that I, even I, am he, ard there is no god with me: I alone kill, and I make alive; I wound, and I heal; and no one can deliver out of my hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Hebrews 6:17–18; Genesis 14:22; Revelation 10:5–6"> For I lift up my hand to heaven, and say, I live for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 66:16; Isaiah 34:5–6; Isaiah 27:1"> When I whet my glittering sword, and my hand taketh hold on judgment: I will render vengeance unto my enemies, and those that hate me will I requite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:23; Jeremiah 46:10; Ezekiel 38:21–22"> I will make my arrows drunken with blood, and my sword shall devour flesh; from the blood of the slain and of the captives, from the crushed head of the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Revelation 19:2; Romans 12:19; Revelation 6:10"> Speak aloud, O ye nations, the praises of his people; for he will avenge the blood of his servants, and vengeance will he render to his adversaries, and forgive his land, and people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Numbers 13:8; Numbers 13:16; Deuteronomy 31:30"> And Moses came and spoke all the words of this song in the ears of the people, he, and Hoshea the son of Nun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> And when Moses had made an end of speaking all these words to all Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:19; Ezekiel 40:4; Proverbs 3:1–4"> He said unto them, Set your hearts unto all the words which I testify against you this day, so that ye may command them your children, to observe to do all the words of this law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Proverbs 4:22; Leviticus 18:5; 2 Peter 1:16"> For it is not a vain word for you; on the contrary, it is your life; and through this word shall ye live many days in the land, whither ye go over the Jordan to possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Numbers 27:12–13"> And the Lord spoke unto Moses on that self-same day, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Numbers 27:12; Isaiah 33:17; Deuteronomy 34:1–5"> Get thee up into this mountain of 'Abarim, unto mount Nebo, which is in the land of Moab, that is in front of Jericho; and behold the land of Canaan, which I give unto the children of Israel for a possession;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Genesis 25:8; Numbers 33:38; Numbers 20:24–29"> And die on the mount whither thou goest up, and be gathered unto thy people; as Aaron thy brother died on mount Hor, and was gathered unto his people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 27:14; Numbers 20:11–14; Deuteronomy 3:23–27"> Because ye trespassed against me in the midst of the children of Israel at the waters of contention at Kadesh, in the wilderness of Zin; because ye sanctified me not in the midst of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:1–4; Deuteronomy 3:27; Hebrews 11:39"> For from afar shalt thou see the land; but thither shalt thou not go unto the land which I give the children of Israel.</span>
