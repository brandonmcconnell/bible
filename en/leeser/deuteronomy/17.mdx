<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:21; Malachi 1:8; Deuteronomy 23:18"> Thou shalt not sacrifice unto the Lord thy God any bullock, or lamb, whereon there is a blemish, any thing evil; for it is an abomination unto the Lord thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 2:20; Hosea 8:1; Joshua 7:11"> If there be found in the midst of thee, within any one of thy gates, which the Lord thy God giveth thee. a man or a woman, that doth the wickedness in the eyes of the Lord thy God, to transgress his covenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:31; Deuteronomy 4:19; Job 31:26–27"> And he hath gone and served other gods, and worshipped them, either the sun, or the moon, or any of the host of heaven, which I have prohibited;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 7:51; Deuteronomy 13:12–14; Deuteronomy 19:18"> And it be told thee, and thou hearest of it: then shalt thou inquire diligently; and, behold, if it be true, the thing is certain, such abomination hath been wrought in Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 24:14; Joshua 7:25; Leviticus 24:16"> Then shalt thou bring forth that man or that woman, who have committed this wicked thing, unto thy gates, the man or the woman, and thou shalt stone them with stones till they die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 35:30; Hebrews 10:28; 1 Timothy 5:19"> Upon the evidence of two witnesses, or of three witnesses, shall he that is worthy of death be put to death; he shall not be put to death upon the evidence of one witness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:9; 1 Corinthians 5:13; Deuteronomy 17:12"> The hand of the witnesses shall be first upon him to put him to death, and the hand of all the people at the last; and thou shalt put the evil away from the midst of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5; Haggai 2:11; Deuteronomy 1:17"> If a matter be unknown to thee for decision, between blood and blood, between plea and plea, and between bodily injury and injury, &#91;or&#93; matters of controversy within thy gates: then shalt thou arise, and get thee up unto the place which the Lord thy God will choose;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:24; Haggai 2:11; Malachi 2:7"> And thou shalt come unto the priests, the Levites, and unto the judge that may be in those days, and thou shalt inquire, and they shall inform thee of the sentence of the case;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 22:2–3"> And thou shalt do according to the sentence, which they may tell thee from that place which the Lord will choose, and thou shalt observe to do according to all that they may instruct thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 1:7; Deuteronomy 5:32; Romans 13:1–6"> In accordance with the instruction which they may instruct thee, and according to the decision which they may say unto thee, shalt thou do: thou shalt not depart from the sentence which they may tell thee, to the right, or to the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 15:30; Deuteronomy 13:5; Deuteronomy 18:5"> And the man that will act presumptuously, so as not to hearken unto the priest that standeth to minister there before the Lord thy God, or unto the judge, even that man shall die; and thou shalt put away the evil from Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:11; Deuteronomy 19:20; Numbers 15:30–31"> And all the people shall hear, and be afraid, and not act presumptuously any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:19–20; 1 Samuel 12:19; Joshua 1:13"> When thou art come unto the land which the Lord thy God giveth thee, and thou hast taken possession of it, and dwellest therein, and thou sayest, I wish to set a king over me, like all the nations that are round about me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:21; 1 Chronicles 22:10; 1 Chronicles 12:23"> Then mayest thou indeed set a king over thee, the one whom the Lord thy God will choose; &#91;but&#93; from the midst of thy brethren shalt thou set a king over thee; thou mayest not set over thee a stranger, who is not thy brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 31:1–3; Ezekiel 17:15; Deuteronomy 28:68"> Only he shall not acquire for himself many horses; so that he may not cause the people to return to Egypt, in order to acquire many horses; whereas the Lord hath said unto you, Ye shall henceforth not return on that way any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:26; Matthew 19:5; Proverbs 30:8–9"> Neither shall he take to himself many wives, that his heart may not turn away; nor shall he acquire for himself too much silver and gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:9; 2 Kings 22:8; 2 Chronicles 34:15"> And it shall be, when he sitteth upon the throne of his kingdom, that he shall write for himself a copy of this law in a book out of &#91;that which is&#93; before the priests, the Levites;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 1:8; Psalms 119:97–100; Psalms 1:2"> And it shall be with him, and he shall read therein all the days of his life: in order that he may learn to fear the Lord his God, to keep all the words of this law and these statutes, to do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:32; 1 Kings 15:5; Psalms 131:1–2"> So that his heart be not lifted up above his brethren, and so that he turn not aside from the commandment, to the right, or to the left: in order that he may live many days in his kingdom, he, and his children, in the midst of Israel.</span>
