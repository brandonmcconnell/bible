<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:19; Psalms 9:5; Psalms 109:13"> And it shall be, that the first-born whom she may bear shall succeed in the name of his brother who is dead; so that his name be not blotted out of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ruth 4:5–6; Ruth 4:1–2"> And if the man have no desire to take his sister-in-law: then shall his sister-in-law go up to the gate unto the elders, and say, My husband's brother refuseth to raise up unto his brother a name in Israel, he will not perform on me the duty of a husband's brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ruth 4:6"> Then shall the elders of his city call him, and speak unto him; and if he persist, and say, I have no desire to take her:</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And his name shall be called in Israel, The house of the barefooted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:9; Romans 3:8"> When men strive together one with the other, and the wife of the one draweth near to deliver her husband out of the hand of him that smiteth him, and putteth forth her hand, and taketh him by the secrets:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:13; Deuteronomy 19:21; Deuteronomy 7:2"> Then shalt thou cut off her hand, thy eye shall not have pity.</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Thou shalt not have in thy house divers measures, a great and a small.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Deuteronomy 4:40; Ephesians 6:3"> A perfect and just weight shalt thou have, a perfect and just measure shalt thou have; in order that thy days may be prolonged in the land which the Lord thy God giveth thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 11:1; Revelation 21:27; Deuteronomy 22:5"> For an abomination of the Lord thy God is every one that doth such things, every one that acteth unrighteously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 17:8–16; Numbers 25:17–18; Numbers 24:20"> Remember what 'Amalek did unto thee, by the way, at your coming forth out of Egypt;</span>
