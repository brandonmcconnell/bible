<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 21:5; Romans 9:8; Galatians 3:26"> Ye are the children of the Lord your God: ye shall not cut yourselves, nor make any baldness between your eyes for the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6; Leviticus 20:26; Exodus 19:5–6"> For a holy people art thou unto the Lord thy God, and the Lord hath made choice of thee to be unto himself a peculiar nation above all the nations that are upon the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:14; Romans 14:14; Leviticus 20:25"> Thou shalt not eat any abominable thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 11:2–45; Acts 10:14; 1 Kings 4:23"> These are the beasts which ye may eat: The ox, the sheep, and the goat,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The hart, and the roebuck, and the fallow-deer, and the chamois, and the gazelle, and the wild ox, and the antelope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 18:1; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Psalms 1:1–2"> And every beast that hath parted hoofs, and whose feet are cleft into two claws, and cheweth the cud among the beasts—that alone may ye eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:22–23; Titus 1:16; 2 Timothy 3:5"> Nevertheless these shall ye not eat of those that chew the cud, and of those that possess the divided cloven hoof: The camel, and the hare, and the coney: for they chew the cud, but divide not the hoof; unclean are they unto you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 11:26–27; Isaiah 66:3; 2 Peter 2:22"> And the swine, because it divideth the hoof, yet cheweth not the cud, it is unclean unto you; of their flesh shall ye not eat, and their dead carcass shall ye not touch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 11:9–12"> This may ye eat of all that is in the waters: all that hath fins and scales may ye eat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And whatsoever hath not fins and scales shall ye not eat; it is unclean unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Every clean bird may ye eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 11:13–19"> But these are they which ye shall not eat of them: The eagle, and the ossifrage, and the osprey,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the glede, and the kite, and the vulture after his kind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And every raven after his kind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 30:29"> And the ostrich, and the night-hawk, and the cuckoo, and the hawk after his kind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The little owl, and the great owl and the swan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the pelican, and the gier-eagle, and the cormorant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And the stork, and the heron after his kind, and the lapwing, and the bat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philippians 3:19; Leviticus 11:20–23"> And every winged insect is unclean unto you: it shall not be eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> All clean fowls may ye eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 23:19; Exodus 34:26; Leviticus 22:8"> Ye shall not eat any thing that dieth of itself: unto the stranger that is in thy gates canst thou give it, that he may eat it; or thou mayest sell it unto an alien; for thou art a holy people unto the Lord thy God; thou shalt not seethe a kid in its mother's milk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:37; Deuteronomy 12:17; Deuteronomy 26:12–15"> Thou shalt truly tithe all the produce of thy seed, which the field bringeth forth year by year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5–7; Deuteronomy 4:10; Deuteronomy 12:17–18"> And thou shalt eat before the Lord thy God, in the place which he will choose to cause his name to dwell there, the tithe of thy corn, of thy wine, and of thy oil, and the first-born of thy herds and of thy flocks; in order that thou may learn to fear the Lord thy God all the days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:21; Deuteronomy 12:5; Deuteronomy 11:24"> And if the way be too long for thee, so that thou art not able to carry it; because the place is too far from thee, which the Lord thy God will choose to set his name there, because the Lord thy God will bless thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Then shalt thou turn it into money, and bind up the money in thy hand, and thou shalt go unto the place which the Lord thy God will choose;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:7; Deuteronomy 12:7; Matthew 21:12"> And thou shalt lay out that money for whatsoever thy soul longeth after, for oxen, or for sheep, or for wine, or for strong drink, or for whatsoever thy soul asketh of thee; and thou shalt eat it there before the Lord thy God, and thou shalt rejoice, thou, and thy household.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 18:20; Deuteronomy 14:29; Deuteronomy 12:12"> And the Levite, who is within thy gates, him shalt thou not forsake; for he hath no portion nor inheritance with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:22; Amos 4:4; Deuteronomy 26:12–15"> At the end of three years shalt thou bring forth all the tithe of thy produce in the same year, and thou shalt lay it down within thy gates:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:10; Deuteronomy 14:27; Proverbs 19:17"> And then shall come the Levite, because he hath no portion nor inheritance with thee, with the stranger, and the fatherless, and the widow, that are within thy gates, and they shall eat and be satisfied; in order that the Lord thy God may bless thee in all the work of thy hand which thou doest.</span>
