<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 14:30; Proverbs 4:18–19; Job 8:7"> And the war lasted a long time between the house of Saul and the house of David; but David became continually stronger and stronger, and the house of Saul became continually weaker and weaker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:1–4; Genesis 49:3–4; 2 Samuel 13:1–29"> And there were born unto David sons in Hebron: and his first-born was Amnon, of Achino'am the Yizre'elitess;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 27:8; 1 Chronicles 3:1; Deuteronomy 3:14"> And his second was Kilab, of Abigayil the wife of Nabal the Carmelite; and the third, Abshalom, the son of Ma'achah the daughter of Talmai the king of Geshur;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 2:13–25; 1 Kings 1:5–18"> And the fourth, Adoniyah, the son of Chaggith; and the fifth, Shephatyah, the son of Abital;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the sixth, Yithre'am, by 'Eglah, David's wife. These were born to David in Hebron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:8–9; 2 Chronicles 25:8; Isaiah 8:9–10"> And it came to pass, while the war lasted between the house of Saul and the house of David, that Abner upheld with all his strength the house of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:8–11; 1 Kings 2:21–22; 2 Samuel 12:8"> And Saul had a concubine, whose name was Rizpah, the daughter of Ayah: and Ish-bosheth said to Abner, Wherefore hast thou gone in unto my father's concubine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:8; 2 Samuel 16:9; Mark 6:18–19"> And Abner became very wroth because of the words of Ish-bosheth, and said, Am I the chief of the dogs which belong to Judah? unto this day have I shown kindness unto the house of Saul thy father, to his brothers, and to his friends, and have not delivered thee into the hand of David; and yet thou chargest me today with a wrong committed with this woman?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:28; 1 Kings 19:2; Ruth 1:17"> May God do so to Abner, and continue to do yet more to him, that, as the Lord hath sworn to David, even so will I surely do to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 20:1; 1 Samuel 3:20; 1 Kings 4:25"> To transfer the kingdom from the house of Saul, and to establish the throne of David over Israel and over Judah, from Dan even to Beer-sheba'.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And he could not answer Abner a word more in reply, because of his fear of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:38–40; 2 Samuel 3:21; Luke 16:5–8"> And Abner sent messengers to David on his behalf, saying, Whose is the land? saying &#91;also,&#93; Make thy covenant with me, and, behold, my hand shall be with thee, to bring round unto thee all Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 43:3; 1 Samuel 19:11–17; Genesis 44:23"> And he said, Well: I will indeed make a covenant with thee; but one thing I require of thee, namely, Thou shalt not see my face, except thou first bring Michal, Saul's daughter, when thou comest to see my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:27; 1 Samuel 18:25; 2 Samuel 2:10"> And David sent messengers to Ish-bosheth, the son of Saul, saying, Give up to me my wife Michal, whom I espoused to me for a hundred foreskins of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:44"> And Ish-bosheth sent, and took her from the man, from Paltiel the son of Layish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:5; 2 Samuel 19:16; 1 Kings 2:8"> And her husband went with her going along and weeping behind her as far as Bachurim: when Abner said unto him, Go, return. And he returned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Abner had used these words with the elders of Israel, saying, Already yesterday and even before ye have been desiring David as king over you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:28; Psalms 89:19–23; 1 Samuel 9:16"> And now do it; for the Lord hath said of David thus, By the hand of my servant David will I save my people Israel out of the hand of the Philistines, and out of the hand of all their enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:29; 1 Samuel 10:20–21; Psalms 68:27"> And Abner also spoke in the ears of Benjamin; and Abner went also to speak in the ears of David in Hebron all that seemed good in the eyes of Israel, and in the eyes of the whole house of Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 31:54; Genesis 26:30; Esther 1:3"> And Abner came to David to Hebron, and with him were twenty men; and David made for Abner and for the men that were with him a feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:12; 1 Kings 11:37; 2 Samuel 3:10"> And Abner said unto David, I will now arise and go, and I will assemble unto my Lord the king all Israel, that they may make a covenant with thee, and that thou mayest reign over all that thy soul longeth for. And David dismissed Abner: and he went in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And, behold, the servants of David and Joab came from a predatory excursion, and brought in much booty with them; but Abner was no more with David in Hebron; for he had dismissed him, and he was gone in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> When Joab and all the army that was with him were come, they told Joab, saying, Abner the son of Ner came to the king, and he hath dismissed him, and he is gone in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:8; 2 Samuel 19:5–7; John 18:35"> Then came Joab to the king, and said, What hast thou done? behold, Abner came unto thee: why is it that thou hast dismissed him, that he went freely away?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:6; Isaiah 37:28; Numbers 27:17"> Thou knowest Abner the son of Ner, that to deceive thee did he come, and to know thy going out and thy coming in, and to know all that thou art doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 26:23–26; Proverbs 27:4–6"> And Joab went out from David, and he sent messengers after Abner, who brought him back from the well of Sirah; but David knew it not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Kings 2:5; 2 Samuel 20:9–10; 1 Kings 2:32"> And when Abner was returned to Hebron, Joab took him aside in the gate to speak with him in private; and he smote him there under the fifth rib, and he died, for the blood of 'Asahel his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 35:33; Exodus 21:12; Genesis 9:6"> And when David heard it afterward, he said, I and my kingdom are guiltless before the Lord for ever of the blood of Abner the son of Ner:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:16; Psalms 109:8–19; Leviticus 13:44–46"> May it rest on the head of Joab, and on all his father's house; and may there not fail from the house of Joab one that hath an issue, or that is a leper, or that leaneth on a crutch, or that falleth by the sword, or that lacketh bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 28:17; Acts 28:4; 2 Samuel 2:19–23"> But Joab and Abishai his brother slew Abner, because he had killed their brother 'Asahel at Gib'on in the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 37:34; 2 Samuel 1:11; 2 Samuel 1:2"> And David said to Joab, and to all the people that were with him, Rend your clothes, and gird yourselves with sackcloth, and &#91;go&#93; mourning before Abner. And king David walked behind the bier.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 24:17; Job 31:28; 1 Samuel 30:4"> And they buried Abner in Hebron: and the king lifted up his voice, and wept at the grave of Abner; and all the people wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:17; 2 Chronicles 35:25; Luke 12:19–20"> And the king lamented over Abner, and said, O, that Abner had to die, as the worthless dieth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Judges 16:21; 2 Samuel 1:12; Job 24:14"> Thy hands were not bound, and thy feet were not put into fetters: as one falleth before men of wickedness art thou fallen. And all the people wept again over him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:17; 2 Samuel 1:12; Ruth 1:17"> And all the people came to cause David to eat food while it was yet day; but David swore, saying, So do God to me, and thus may he continue, if before the sun be down I taste bread, or the least else.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 7:37; Psalms 62:9; Mark 15:11–13"> And all the people took notice of it, and it was pleasing in their eyes: as whatsoever the king did was pleasing in the eyes of all the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And all the people and all Israel understood on that day that it had not been of the king to slay Abner the son of Ner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Job 32:9; 2 Samuel 2:8; 2 Samuel 3:12"> And the king said unto his servants, Know ye not that a prince and a great man hath fallen this day in Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 2:33–34; Psalms 101:8; Psalms 28:4"> And I am this day yet weak, and just anointed king; and these men, the sons of Zeruyah, are too strong for me: may the Lord pay the doer of evil according to his wickedness.</span>
