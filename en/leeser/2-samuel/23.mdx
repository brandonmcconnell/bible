<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:12–13; 2 Samuel 7:8–9; Psalms 89:20"> And these are the last words of David. Thus saith David the son of Jesse, and thus saith the man who was raised up on high, the anointed of the God of Jacob, and the sweet singer of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Peter 1:21; Matthew 22:43; Mark 12:36"> The Spirit of the Lord spoke through me, and his word was upon my tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:32; Deuteronomy 32:4; Exodus 18:21"> &#91;Thus&#93; said the God of Israel, concerning me spoke the Rock of Israel, That &#91;I should be&#93; ruler over men, be righteous, ruling in the fear of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 5:31; Psalms 72:6; Hosea 6:5"> And as in the light of morning the sun riseth, in a morning without clouds, with more than the brightness &#91;reflected&#93; by rain on the herbs that spring from the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 55:3; 2 Samuel 7:14–16; Isaiah 9:6–7"> Truly is not so my house with God? since he hath made with me an everlasting covenant, firm in all, and sure? yea, will he not cause to grow all my salvation, and all my desire?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:6; Isaiah 33:12; Deuteronomy 13:13"> But the godless are all of them as waving thorns, which cannot be taken in the hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 6:8; 2 Thessalonians 2:8; Isaiah 27:4"> But the man that will touch them must protect his hand with iron and the staff of a spear: and they will be utterly burnt with fire in the dwelling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:2; 1 Chronicles 11:11–47; 1 Chronicles 27:32"> These are the names of the mighty men whom David had: Yosheb-bashebeth, the Thachkemonite, the chief among the captains,—the same as 'Adino the 'Eznite,—because of eight hundred slain at one time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:4; 1 Chronicles 11:12–14; 1 Samuel 17:26"> And after him was El'azar the son of Dodo, the son of Achochi, one of the three mighty men with David, when they defied the Philistines that were there gathered together to battle, and the men of Israel had withdrawn themselves;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:5; 1 Samuel 11:13; Judges 15:14"> He then arose, and smote the Philistines until his hand was weary, and his hand did cleave unto the sword: and the Lord wrought a great victory on that day; and the people returned after him only to strip &#91;the slain&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:13–14; 1 Chronicles 11:27; 2 Samuel 23:33"> And after him was Shammah the son of Age the Hararite. The Philistines were gathered together into a troop, and there was a piece of ground full of lentiles; and the people had fled from the Philistines;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 21:31; 2 Samuel 23:10; Psalms 3:8"> But he placed himself in the midst of the ground, and delivered it, and smote the Philistines: and the Lord wrought a great victory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:1; 2 Samuel 5:18; Joshua 12:15"> And these three, the chiefs of the thirty, went down, and came to David at harvest-time unto the cave of 'Adullam: and the troop of the Philistines was encamped in the valley of Rephaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:4–5; 1 Samuel 13:4; 1 Samuel 10:5"> And David was then in the strong-hold, and an outpost of the Philistines was then in Beth-lechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 4:14; Psalms 42:1–2; Isaiah 41:17"> And David longed, and said, Oh that one would bring me water to drink out of the well at Beth-lechem, which is by the gate!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 35:14; 2 Corinthians 5:14; Philippians 2:17"> And the three mighty men broke through the camp of the Philistines; and drew water out of the well of Beth-lechem, that was by the gate, and took it, and brought it to David; but he would not drink thereof, and poured it out unto the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 17:10; Mark 14:24; 1 Corinthians 15:30"> And he said, Far be it from me, O Lord, that I should do this: &#91;is not this&#93; the blood of the men that went at the risk of their life? and thus he would not drink it. These things did the three mighty men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:10; 2 Samuel 10:14; 1 Chronicles 11:20–21"> And Abishai, the brother of Joab, the son of Zeruyah, was the chief of these three; and he lifted up his spear against three hundred slain, and had a name among the three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 13:8; Matthew 13:23; 1 Corinthians 15:41"> Although he was the most honored of the three, wherefore he became their captain: he nevertheless attained not unto the three &#91;in prowess&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:18; Joshua 15:21; 2 Samuel 20:23"> And Benayahu the son of Yehoyada', the son of a valiant man, great in many acts of Kabzeel; he it was that smote the two lion-like heroes of Moab; he also went down and slew a lion in the midst of a pit on a day when it snowed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:51; 1 Chronicles 11:23; Colossians 2:15"> And he slew an Egyptian, a man of good appearance; and the Egyptian had a spear in his hand; but he went down to him with a staff, and he snatched the spear out of the Egyptian's hand, and slew him with his own spear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> These things did Benayahu the son of Yehoyada', and he had a name among the three mighty men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:23; 1 Chronicles 27:6; 1 Samuel 22:14"> He was more honored than the thirty; but he attained not to these three. And David appointed him in his private council.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:18; 1 Chronicles 27:7; 1 Chronicles 11:26"> 'Asahel the brother of Joab was one of the thirty; Elchanan the son of Dodo of Beth-lechem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Judges 7:1; 1 Chronicles 11:27–28"> Shammah the Charodite, Elika the Charodite,</span>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 21:18; 1 Chronicles 11:19; 1 Chronicles 11:28"> Abi'ezer the 'Anethothite, Mebunnai the Chushathite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:13; 2 Kings 25:23; 1 Chronicles 11:30"> Zalmon the Achochite, Maharai the Netophathite,</span>

<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 12:15; 1 Chronicles 27:14; Judges 12:13"> Benayahu the Pir'athonite, Hiddai of Nachale-Ga'ash,</span>
