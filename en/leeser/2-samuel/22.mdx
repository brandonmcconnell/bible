<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 15:1; Judges 5:1; Isaiah 12:1–6"> And David spoke unto the Lord the words of this song, and on the day that the Lord had delivered him out of the hand of all his enemies, and out of the hand of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 18:2–50; Psalms 31:3; Psalms 71:3"> And he said, Lord, my rock, my fortress, and my deliverer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Isaiah 12:2; Proverbs 18:10"> God, my rock, in whom I trust; my shield, and the horn of my salvation, my high tower, and my refuge, my saviour! from violence dost thou save me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 18:3; Psalms 48:1; Psalms 96:4"> Praised, I cried, be the Lord, and from my enemies was I saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jonah 2:3; Psalms 69:14–15; Revelation 17:15"> For the waves of death encompassed me, the floods of destruction made me afraid;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 116:3; Proverbs 13:14; Psalms 140:5"> The bonds of hell encircled me, the snares of death seized on me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 18:6; Psalms 116:4; Exodus 3:7"> &#91;When&#93; in my distress I called upon the Lord, and to my God I cried; and he heard from his temple my voice, and my complaint &#91;entered&#93; into his ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 26:11; Psalms 97:4; Judges 5:4"> Then shook and trembled the earth; the foundations of the heavens were moved; and they shook, because he was wroth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Psalms 18:15; Psalms 18:8"> Smoke went up in his anger, and consuming fire out of his mouth, coals flamed forth from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 8:12; Psalms 97:2; Psalms 144:5"> And he bent the heavens, and came down, and thick darkness was under his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 104:3; Psalms 18:10; 1 Samuel 4:4"> And he rode upon a cherub, and flew along, and he was seen upon the wings of the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; 2 Samuel 22:10; Job 36:29"> And he made darkness round about him into pavilions, heavy masses of waters, thick clouds of the skies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:9"> From the brightness before him flamed forth coals of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 29:3–9; 1 Samuel 2:10; Job 37:2–5"> The Lord thundered from heaven, and the Most High uttered forth his voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:23; Joshua 10:10; Habakkuk 3:11"> And he sent out arrows, and scattered them; lightning, and discomfited them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nahum 1:4; Habakkuk 3:8–10; Psalms 114:3–7"> And then were seen the channels of the sea, there were laid open the foundations of the world; at the rebuke of the Lord, through the blast of the breath of his nostrils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Psalms 144:7; Psalms 18:16"> He stretched out from above &#91;his hand&#93;, he took me; he drew me out from the mighty waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 3:7; 2 Timothy 4:17; Psalms 56:9"> He delivered me from my enemy, the strong, from those that hated me, when they were too mighty for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 71:20–21; 1 Samuel 23:26–27; Psalms 118:10–13"> They overcame me on the day of my calamity; but the Lord became my stay;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 31:8; Psalms 118:5; 2 Samuel 15:26"> And he brought me forth into a large space: he delivered me, because he had delight in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 24:4; 1 Samuel 26:23; Psalms 7:8"> The Lord rewarded me according to my righteousness: according to the purity of my hands did he recompense me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 8:32; Genesis 18:19; Psalms 128:1"> For I had kept the ways of the Lord, and had not wickedly departed from my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 119:30; Psalms 119:102; John 15:14"> For all his ordinances were before me, and from none of his statutes did I depart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 6:9; Job 1:1; Genesis 17:1"> I was also upright toward him, and I guarded myself against my iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:21; 2 Corinthians 5:10; Romans 2:7–8"> Therefore did the Lord recompense me according to my righteousness, according to my purity before his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; James 2:13"> With the kind thou wilt show thyself kind; with the upright mighty man thou wilt show thyself upright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 5:8; Psalms 18:26; Leviticus 26:23–28"> With the pure thou wilt show thyself pure; and with the perverse thou wilt wage a contest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 72:12–13; Isaiah 5:15; James 4:6–7"> And the afflicted people thou wilt save; but thy eyes are upon the haughty, &#91;that&#93; thou mayest bring &#91;them&#93; down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 27:1; John 8:12; Job 29:3"> For thou art my lamp, O Lord! and the Lord will enlighten my darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 18:29; Philippians 4:13; Psalms 118:10–12"> For &#91;aided&#93; by thee I run through a troop: &#91;helped&#93; by my God I leap over a wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Proverbs 30:5; Psalms 18:30"> As for God—his way is perfect; the word of the Lord is tried; he is a shield to all that trust in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:2; Deuteronomy 32:31; Jeremiah 10:6–7"> For who is God, save the Lord? and who is a rock, save our God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ephesians 6:10; Isaiah 41:10; Philippians 4:13"> God is my strength and power; and he rendereth free from obstruction my way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13; Isaiah 58:14; Habakkuk 3:19"> He maketh my feet like those of the hinds, and upon my high places he causeth me to stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 144:1; Psalms 18:33–34; Ezekiel 39:9–10"> He teaches my hands for the war, so that a brazen bow is bent by my arms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ephesians 6:16; Psalms 115:14; Psalms 84:11"> And thou gavest me the shield of thy salvation; and thy assistance hath made me great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Proverbs 4:12; Psalms 17:5; 1 Samuel 2:9"> Thou enlargest my steps under me, so that my joints do not slip.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Psalms 21:8–9; 2 Samuel 5:18–25; 2 Samuel 10:14"> I pursue my enemies and destroy them; and I return not again until I have made an end of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Malachi 4:3; Malachi 4:1; Psalms 110:1"> And I make an end of them, and I crush them, that they cannot rise; and they fall under my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Psalms 44:5; Isaiah 60:14; Colossians 1:11"> For thou hast girded me with strength for the war; thou subduest my opponents under me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 23:27; Joshua 10:24; 2 Thessalonians 1:8–9"> And my enemies thou causest to turn their back to me; those that hate me,—that I may destroy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Isaiah 1:15; 1 Samuel 28:6; Micah 3:4"> They look about, but there is none to help; unto the Lord—but he answereth them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Micah 7:10; Isaiah 10:6; 2 Kings 13:7"> And I beat them small as the dust of the earth; as the mire of the street, I stamp them, I tread them down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:1–14; Isaiah 55:5; 2 Samuel 3:1"> Thou hast also delivered me from the contests of my people: thou preservest me to be the head of nations, a people which I know not shall serve me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Psalms 81:15; Psalms 66:3; Isaiah 56:3"> The children of the stranger shall utter flattery unto me; as soon as their ear heareth they shall be obedient unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Micah 7:17; James 1:11; Isaiah 64:6"> The children of the stranger shall fade away, and come forth tottering out of their close places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 89:26; 2 Samuel 22:3; Job 19:25"> The Lord liveth, and blessed be my Rock; and exalted be the God, the Rock of my salvation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 94:1; Psalms 144:2; 2 Samuel 18:31"> The God, that granteth me vengeance, and bringeth down nations under me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Psalms 140:1; 2 Samuel 7:8–9; Numbers 24:7"> And that bringeth me forth from my enemies: also above my opponents thou liftest me up, from the man of violence thou deliverest me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Romans 15:9; Psalms 146:1–2; Psalms 145:1–2"> Therefore will I give thanks unto thee, O Lord, among the nations, and unto thy name will I sing praises;</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Psalms 89:29; Psalms 18:50; Psalms 89:20"> &#91;To&#93; the tower of salvation of his king, and who showeth kindness to his anointed, to David and to his seed forever.</span>
