<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 27:6; 1 Samuel 31:6; 1 Samuel 30:17–26"> Now it came to pass after the death of Saul, when David was returned from smiting the 'Amalekites, that David abode in Ziklag two days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:12; 2 Samuel 4:10; Joshua 7:6"> And it came to pass on the third day, that, behold, a man came out of the camp from Saul with his clothes rent, and earth upon his head: and it happened, when he came to David, that he fell to the earth, and prostrated himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 1:15–19; 2 Kings 5:25"> And David said unto him, From where comest thou? And he said unto him, Out of the camp of Israel am I escaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:16; 1 Samuel 31:1–6; 1 Chronicles 10:1–6"> And David said unto him, What took place there? I pray thee, tell me. And he said, That the people are fled from the battle, and that also many of the people are fallen and have died; and that also Saul and Jonathan his son are dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 25:2; Proverbs 14:15"> And David said unto the young man that told him, How knowest thou that Saul is dead as also Jonathan his son?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:4; 2 Samuel 1:21; 1 Chronicles 10:4–6"> And the young man that told him said, I happened entirely by chance to be upon mount Gilboa', when, behold, there was Saul leaning upon his spear; and, lo, the chariots and horsemen had overtaken him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:12; 2 Samuel 9:6; Judges 9:54"> And he turned round, and he saw me, and called unto me, And I said, Here am I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:17; 1 Samuel 30:13; 1 Samuel 30:1"> And he said unto me, Who art thou! And I answered him, An 'Amalekite am I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And he said unto me, Place thyself, I pray thee, by me, and slay me; for a mortal tremor hath seized on me, although my life is yet whole in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Judges 9:54; 2 Kings 11:12"> So I placed myself by him, and slew him, because I was sure that he could not live after his fall; and I took the crown that was upon his head, and the bracelet that was on his arm, and I have brought them unto my lord hither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:31; Genesis 37:29; 2 Samuel 3:31"> David thereupon took hold of his clothes, and rent them; and &#91;so did&#93; likewise all the men that were with him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 24:17; Psalms 35:13–14; Matthew 5:44"> And they lamented, and wept, and fasted until the evening, for Saul, and for Jonathan his son, and for the people of the Lord, and for the house of Israel; because they were fallen by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:8"> And David said unto the young man that told him, Whence art thou? And he said, The son of a stranger, an 'Amalekite, am I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:9; 1 Samuel 31:4; 2 Peter 2:10"> And David said unto him, How wast thou not afraid to stretch forth thy hand to destroy the Lord's anointed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 2:34; Judges 8:20; 2 Samuel 4:10–12"> And David called one of the young men, and said, Come near, and fall upon him. And he smote him that he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; Luke 19:22; Leviticus 20:9"> And David said unto him, Thy blood is upon thy own head; for thy mouth hath testified against thee, saying, I myself have slain the Lord's anointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:25; 2 Samuel 1:19; Genesis 50:11"> And David lamented with this lamentation over Saul and over Jonathan his son:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 10:13; Genesis 49:8; Deuteronomy 4:10"> And he said, That the children of Judah should be taught the bow; behold it is written in the book of Yashar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:27; 2 Samuel 1:25; Deuteronomy 4:7–8"> O beauty of Israel! upon the high places slain: how are the mighty fallen!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Micah 1:10; 1 Samuel 18:6; Judges 11:34"> Tell it not in Gath, publish it not in the streets of Ashkelon; that the daughters of the Philistines may not be glad, that the daughters of the uncircumcised may not rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:1; Isaiah 21:5; 1 Samuel 10:1"> O mountains of Gilboa', no dew, nor rain be upon you, nor fields of offerings; for there the shield of the mighty was stained, the shield of Saul, as though it had not been anointed with oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:4; Isaiah 34:6–7; 1 Samuel 14:6–14"> From the blood of the slain, from the fat of the mighty, the bow of Jonathan turned never back, and the sword of Saul never returned empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:13; Judges 14:18; Lamentations 4:19"> Saul and Jonathan, the beloved and the dear in their lives, were even in their death not divided: more than eagles were they swift, more than lions were they strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Peter 3:3–5; Jeremiah 2:32; Judges 5:30"> O daughters of Israel, weep for Saul, who clothed you in scarlet, with beautiful dresses, who put on ornaments of gold upon your apparel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:19; 2 Samuel 1:27; Judges 5:18"> How are the mighty fallen in the midst of the battle! O Jonathan, on thy high places slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:17; 1 Samuel 23:16; 1 Samuel 20:41"> I am distressed for thee, my brother Jonathan; very dear hast thou been unto me: wonderful was thy love for me, passing the love of women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:25; 2 Samuel 1:19; Ezekiel 39:9–10"> How are the mighty fallen, and lost the instruments of war!</span>
