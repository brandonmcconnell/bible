<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 12:16; 2 Chronicles 10:16; Deuteronomy 13:13"> And there happened to be a worthless man, whose name was Sheba', the son of Bichri, a Benjamite; and he blew the cornet, and said, We have no part in David, nor have we any inheritance in the son of Jesse: every man to his tents, O Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 6:66–68; Acts 11:23; 2 Samuel 19:15"> So every man of Israel went off from David, following Sheba' the son of Bichri; but the men of Judah adhered unto their king, from the Jordan even to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:21–22; 2 Samuel 15:16; Genesis 40:3"> And David came to his house at Jerusalem; and the king took the ten women, the concubines, whom he had left to guard the house, and put them in a guard-house, and provided for them, but went not in unto them. So they were confined until the day of their death, living in widowhood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:13; 2 Samuel 17:25; 1 Chronicles 2:17"> Then said the king to 'Amassa, Call together for me the men of Judah within three days, and thou present thyself here &#91;then&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:13; 1 Samuel 13:8"> So 'Amassa went to call Judah together; but he remained out longer than the set time which he had appointed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:17; 2 Samuel 11:11; 1 Kings 1:33"> And David said to Abishai, Now will Sheba' the son of Bichri do us more harm than &#91;did&#93; Abshalom: take thou the servants of thy Lord, and pursue after him, lest he succeed in reaching fortified cities, and withdraw himself from our eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:18; 1 Kings 1:38; 2 Samuel 20:23"> And there went out after him Joab's men, and the Kerethites, and the Pelethites, and all the mighty men: and they went forth out of Jerusalem, to pursue after Sheba' the son of Bichri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:30; 2 Samuel 2:13; 2 Samuel 20:4–5"> They were close by the great stone which is at Gib'on, as 'Amassa came before them. And Joab was girded with his coat, his &#91;usual&#93; garment, and upon it the girdle of the sword which was fastened upon his loins in its sheath; and as he went forth it fell out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 22:47–48; Matthew 26:48–49; Micah 7:2"> And Joab said to 'Amassa, Art thou in health, my brother? And Joab's right hand took hold of 'Amassa's beard to kiss him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:23; 2 Samuel 3:27; Judges 3:21"> And 'Amassa did not guard himself against the sword that was in Joab's hand: so he smote him therewith in the fifth rib and shed out his bowels to the ground, and struck him not again; and he died. But Joab and Abishai his brother pursued after Sheba' the son of Bichri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:13; 2 Samuel 20:21; 2 Kings 9:32"> And one man of Joab's people remained standing by him, and said, He that favoreth Joab, and he that is for David, follow Joab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 24:21–22; Psalms 9:16; 2 Samuel 17:25"> And 'Amassa was wallowing in his blood in the midst of the highway. And when the man saw that all the people stood still, he put 'Amassa aside out of the highway into the field, and threw a garment over him, when he saw that every one that came by him stood still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:12; Isaiah 7:3; 2 Kings 18:17"> When he was removed out of the highway, every man passed on after Joab, to pursue after Sheba' the son of Bichri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 15:29; 1 Kings 15:20; 2 Chronicles 16:4"> And this one passed through all the tribes of Israel unto Abel, which is of Beth-ma'achah, and all the Berim: and they assembled themselves together, and went also after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 19:32; Jeremiah 32:24; 1 Kings 15:20"> And they came and besieged him in Abel of Beth-ma'achah, and they cast up a trench against the city, and it stood enclosed by the troops: and all the people that were with Joab were battering to throw down the wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:2; Ecclesiastes 9:14–18; 1 Samuel 25:32–33"> Then called a wise woman out of the city, Hear, hear: say, I pray you, unto Joab, Come near as far as hither, that I may speak with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:12; 1 Samuel 25:24"> And when he was come near unto her, the woman said, Art thou Joab? And he said, I am. Then said she unto him, Hear the words of thy hand-maid. And he said, I do hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:10–11"> Then said she, thus, They ought surely first to have spoken, saying, “Let them ask at least in Abel:” and so would they have come to an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:19; 2 Samuel 21:3; 2 Samuel 17:16"> I am one of the peaceful and faithful &#91;cities&#93; in Israel; thou seekest to overthrow a city and a metropolis in Israel: why wilt thou destroy the inheritance of the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:29; Proverbs 28:13; Jeremiah 17:9"> And Joab answered and said, Far be it, far be it from me, that I should destroy or ruin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:9; Judges 2:9; 2 Samuel 17:2–3"> The matter is not so; but a man from the mountains of Ephraim, Sheba' the son of Bichri is his name, hath lifted up his hand against the king, against David: give him up alone, and I will withdraw from the city. And the woman said unto Joab, Behold, his head shall be cast down to thee over the wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:1; Ecclesiastes 7:19; 2 Samuel 11:6–21"> And the woman came unto all the people with her wisdom; and they cut off the head of Sheba' the son of Bichri, and cast it down to Joab: and he blew the cornet, and they scattered themselves from the city, every man to his tents. And Joab returned to Jerusalem unto the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:16–18; 1 Chronicles 18:15–17; 1 Kings 4:3–6"> Now Joab was over all the army of Israel; and Benayah the son of Yehoyada' was over the Kerethites and over the Pelethites;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 4:3; 1 Kings 12:18; 1 Kings 4:6"> And Adoram was over the tribute; and Jehoshaphat the son of Achilud was recorder;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 4:4; 2 Samuel 8:17; 1 Chronicles 18:16"> And Sheva was scribe; and Zadok and Ebyathar were priests;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:38; Exodus 2:14; Genesis 41:45"> And 'Ira also the Yairite was an officer of state unto David.</span>
