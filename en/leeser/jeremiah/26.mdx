<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:4–5; Jeremiah 27:1; 2 Kings 23:34–36"> In the beginning of the reign of Jehoyakim the son of Josiah the king of Judah came this word from the Lord, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:2; Acts 20:27; Matthew 28:20"> Thus hath said the Lord, Place thyself in the court of the house of the Lord, and speak against all of the cities of Judah, who come to prostrate themselves in the house of the Lord, all the words that I have commanded thee to speak unto them: omit not a word &#91;thereof&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:3; Isaiah 1:16–19; Jeremiah 18:7–10"> Perhaps it be that they will hearken, and return every man from his evil way, that I may bethink me of the evil, which I purpose to do unto them, because of the wrongfulness of their doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 9:6; Jeremiah 44:10; Isaiah 1:20"> And thou shalt say unto them, Thus hath said the Lord, if ye will not hearken unto me, to walk in my law, which I have set before you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:7; 2 Kings 9:7; Jeremiah 7:13"> To hearken unto the words of my servants the prophets, whom I send unto you, yea, making them rise up early, and sending them, while ye have not hearkened:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:9; 2 Kings 22:19; Jeremiah 25:18"> Then will I render this house like Shiloh, and this city will I render a curse unto all the nations of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:31; Micah 3:11; Acts 5:17"> And the priests and the prophets and all the people heard Jeremiah speaking these words in the house of the Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 4:13–14; Acts 7:52; Jeremiah 18:18"> And it came to pass, when Jeremiah had finished speaking all that the Lord had commanded &#91;him&#93; to speak unto all the people, that the priests and the prophets and all the people seized on him, saying, Thou shalt surely die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 4:17–19; Acts 19:24–32; Micah 2:6"> Why hast thou prophesied in the name of the Lord, saying, Like Shiloh shall this house be, and this city shall be ruined, &#91;left&#93; without an inhabitant? And all the people assembled themselves against Jeremiah in the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:10; Jeremiah 38:4–6; Jeremiah 37:14–16"> But when the princes of Judah heard these things, they came up from the king's house unto the house of the Lord, and sat down at the entrance of the new gate of the Lord's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:4; Matthew 26:66; Deuteronomy 18:20"> Then said the priests and the prophets unto the princes and unto all the people, as followeth, This man deserveth the punishment of death; for he hath prophesied against this city, as ye have heard with your own ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Jeremiah 26:15; Acts 4:19"> Then said Jeremiah unto all the princes and unto all the people, as followeth, The Lord hath sent me to prophesy against this house and against this city all the words that ye have heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jonah 3:9; Jonah 4:2; Isaiah 1:19"> But now amend your ways and your doings, and hearken to the voice of the Lord your God: and the Lord will bethink him of the evil that he hath spoken against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 9:25; 2 Samuel 15:26; Jeremiah 38:5"> As for me, behold, I am in your hand: do with me as seemeth good and just in your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 35:33; Proverbs 6:17; Matthew 26:25"> But know ye for certain, that if ye put me to death, ye will surely place &#91;the guilt of&#93; innocent blood upon yourselves, and upon this city, and upon its inhabitants; for in truth hath the Lord sent me unto you to speak in your ears all these words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 5:34–39; Acts 23:29; Acts 25:25"> Then said the princes and all the people unto the priests and to the prophets, This man is not deserving the punishment of death; for in the name of the Lord our God hath he spoken unto us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 5:34; Micah 1:1"> And then rose up certain men of the elders of the land, and said to all the assembly of the people, as followeth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Micah 1:1; Nehemiah 4:2; Zechariah 8:3"> Michah the Morashthite prophesied in the days of Hezekiah the king of Judah. and said to all the people of Judah, as followeth, Thus hath said the Lord of hosts, Zion shall be ploughed up like a field, and Jerusalem shall become heaps of ruins, and the mountain of the house, woody high-places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 32:14; 2 Samuel 24:16; Isaiah 37:15–20"> Did Hezekiah the king of Judah and all Judah attempt to put him to death? behold, he did fear the Lord, and besought the Lord, and the Lord bethought him of the evil which he had spoken against them. And shall we bring a great wickedness on our souls?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:2; Joshua 9:17; Joshua 15:60"> And there was also a man that prophesied in the name of the Lord, Uriyah the son of Shema'yahu of Kiryath-ye'arim, who prophesied against this city and against this land in accordance with all the words of Jeremiah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 10:23; Matthew 14:5; Jeremiah 36:26"> And when king Jehoyakim, with all his mighty men, and all the princes, heard his words, the king sought to put him to death; but when Uriyahu heard it, he was afraid, and fled, and arrived in Egypt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:12; 2 Kings 22:12; 2 Kings 22:14"> But king Jehoyakim sent some men into Egypt, namely, Elnathan the son of 'Achbor, and some men with him into Egypt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:30; Revelation 11:7; Ezekiel 19:6"> And they fetched Uriyahu out of Egypt, and brought him unto king Jehoyakim, who slew him with the sword, and cast his dead body upon the graves of the common people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:5–7; Jeremiah 39:14; Jeremiah 1:18–19"> But the hand of Achikam the son of Shaphan was with Jeremiah, so as not to give him up into the hand of the people to put him to death.</span>
