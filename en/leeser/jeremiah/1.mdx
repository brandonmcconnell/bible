<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:60; Jeremiah 11:21; 2 Chronicles 36:21"> The words of Jeremiah the son of Chilkiyahu, one of the priests that were in 'Anathoth in the land of Benjamin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 13:20; Jonah 1:1; Jeremiah 1:11"> To whom the word of the Lord came in the days of Josiah the son of Amon the king of Judah, in the thirteenth year of his reign,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:2; 2 Chronicles 36:5–8; 2 Kings 23:34"> And who continued &#91;prophet&#93; in the days of Jehoyakim the son of Josiah the king of Judah, until the end of the eleventh year of Zedekiah the son of Josiah the king of Judah, until the carrying away into exile of Jerusalem in the fifth month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:16; Ezekiel 1:3; Jeremiah 1:2"> And the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 139:16; Isaiah 49:1; Galatians 1:15–16"> Before yet I had formed thee in thy mother's body I knew thee; and before thou wast yet come forth out of the womb I sanctified thee: a prophet unto the nations did I ordain thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 6:12; Exodus 4:10–16; 1 Kings 3:7–9"> And I said, Ah, Lord Eternal! behold, I know not how to speak; for I am &#91;but&#93; a lad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 16:15–16; Ezekiel 3:27; Numbers 22:20"> And the Lord said unto me, Say not, I am &#91;but&#93; a lad; but to whomsoever I may send thee shalt thou go, and whatsoever I may command thee shalt thou speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Joshua 1:9; Isaiah 51:7"> Be not afraid because of them; for I am with thee to deliver thee, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Exodus 4:11–12; Luke 12:12"> And the Lord stretched forth his hand, and touched &#91;me&#93; therewith on my mouth; and the Lord said unto me, Behold, I have put my words in thy mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:28; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Isaiah 44:26–28"> See, I have appointed thee this day over the nations and over the kingdoms, to root out, and to pull down, and to destroy, and to throw down: to build up, and to plant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Amos 7:8; Amos 8:2; Zechariah 5:2"> And the word of the Lord came unto me, saying, What seest thou, Jeremiah? And I said, A staff of all almond-tree do I see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:28; Ezekiel 12:25; Ezekiel 12:22–23"> And the Lord said unto me, Thou hast well seen; for I am watching over my word to perform it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:3; Ezekiel 11:7; Zechariah 4:2"> And the word of the Lord came unto me the second time, saying, What seest thou? And I said, A seething pot do I see; and the front thereof is turned from the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 41:25; Jeremiah 4:6; Jeremiah 10:22"> And the Lord said unto me, Out of the north shall the evil break forth over all the inhabitants of the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:10; Jeremiah 25:9; Jeremiah 9:11"> For, lo, I will call unto all the families of the kingdoms of the north, saith the Lord; and they shall come, and they shall set every one his throne at the entrance of the gates of Jerusalem, and upon all its walls round about, and upon &#91;those of&#93; all the cities of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:9; Jeremiah 19:4; Acts 7:41"> And I will call them to account touching all their wickedness, in that they have forsaken me, and have burnt incense unto other gods, and have bowed down unto the works of their own hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:7–8; 1 Kings 18:46; 1 Peter 1:13"> But do thou gird up thy loins, and arise, and speak unto them all that I may command thee; be not discouraged because of them, lest I humble thee before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 50:7; Ezekiel 3:8–9; Jeremiah 15:20"> But I, behold, I have made of thee this day a fortified city, and an iron pillar, and brazen walls over the whole land, against the kings of Judah, against its princes, against its priests, and against the people of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Jeremiah 1:8; Jeremiah 20:11"> And &#91;although&#93; they fight against thee, they shall not prevail against thee; for with thee am I saith the Lord, to deliver thee.</span>
