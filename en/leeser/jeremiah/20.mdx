<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezra 2:37–38; 1 Chronicles 24:14; 2 Kings 25:18"> But when Pashchur the son of Immer the priest, who was chief superintendent in the house of the Lord, heard Jeremiah prophesying these words:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:13; Zechariah 14:10; Jeremiah 1:19"> Then smote Pashchur Jeremiah the prophet, and put him in the stocks that were in the upper gate of Benjamin, which was in the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:10; Jeremiah 46:5; Lamentations 2:22"> And it came to pass on the morrow, that Pashchur brought forth Jeremiah out of the stocks. And Jeremiah said unto him, Not Pashchur &#91;Fulness of Freedom&#93; hath the Lord called thy name, but Magor-missabib &#91;&#91;Terror all around&#93;&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:21; Jeremiah 25:9; Jeremiah 21:4–10"> For thus hath said the Lord, Behold, I will make thee a terror to thyself, and to all thy friends; and they shall fall by the sword of their enemies, and thy eyes shall see it; and all Judah will I give into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he shall lead them into exile to Babylon, and shall smite them with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:13; Ezekiel 22:25; Lamentations 1:10"> And I will give up all the wealth of this city, and all its acquisitions, and all its precious things; and all the treasures of the kings of Judah will I give into the hand of their enemies, who shall plunder them, and take them and carry them away to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:14–15; Lamentations 2:14; Ezekiel 22:28"> And thou, Pashchur, and all the inhabitants of thy house, shall go into captivity; and to Babylon shalt thou come, and there thou shalt die, and there shalt thou be buried, thou, and all thy friends, to whom thou hast prophesied with falsehood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 9:7; Jeremiah 20:9; Lamentations 3:14"> Thou didst persuade me, O Lord, and I was persuaded; thou laidst thy &#91;hand&#93; strongly on me, and didst prevail: I am laughed at all the time, every one mocketh me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:16; Jeremiah 6:10; Jeremiah 5:15–17"> For as often as I speak must I cry out, violence and wasting must I proclaim; because the word of the Lord is become unto me a disgrace, and a derision, all the time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 39:3; Job 32:18–20; Acts 4:20"> And I thought, I will not make mention of him, and I will not speak any more in his name. But it became in my heart as a burning fire enclosed within my bones, and I was weary with enduring, and I could not overcome it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; Psalms 31:13; Isaiah 29:21"> For I heard the defaming of many, angry assemblies on every side, “Tell, and we will tell of him.” All the men who ought to seek my welfare, watch for my fall; saying, “Peradventure he may he enticed, and we shall prevail against him, and we will then take our revenge on him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:19; Jeremiah 1:8; Jeremiah 15:20"> But the Lord is with me as a mighty powerful one; therefore my persecutors will stumble, and they will not prevail: they will be greatly ashamed, for they will not prosper; &#91;it is&#93; an everlasting confusion which will never be forgotten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 62:8; Jeremiah 11:20; Psalms 54:7"> But, O Lord of hosts, that probest the righteous, seest the reins and the heart, let me see thy vengeance on them; for unto thee have I laid open my cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 25:4; Psalms 35:9–11; Psalms 69:33"> Sing unto the Lord, praise ye the Lord; for he hath delivered the soul of the needy from the hand of evil-doers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:10; Job 3:3–16"> Cursed be the day whereon I was born: the day on which my mother bore me shall not be blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 21:5–6; Luke 1:14; Jeremiah 1:5"> Cursed be the man who brought tidings to my father, saying, A man-child is born unto thee. How very glad did he make him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:22; Jeremiah 48:3–4; Deuteronomy 29:23"> And may that same man become like the cities which the Lord overthrew, and bethought himself not; and may he hear a cry of anguish in the morning, and a tumultuous noise at noontide;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 10:18–19; Job 3:10–11; Ecclesiastes 6:3"> Because I was not slain &#91;as I escaped&#93; from the womb; or that my mother might have become unto me my grave, and her womb have been affected with a perpetual pregnancy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 3:20; Lamentations 3:1; 1 Peter 4:14–16"> Wherefore was this that I came forth out of the womb to see trouble and sorrow, that my days should pass away in shame?</span>
