<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 4:1; Jeremiah 42:15; Amos 7:16"> Hear ye the word which the Lord hath spoken concerning you, O house of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 20:23; Leviticus 18:3; Luke 21:25–28"> Thus hath said the Lord, Do not habituate yourselves on the way of the nations, and at the signs of the heavens be ye not dismayed; although the nations should be dismayed at them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 44:9–20; Leviticus 18:30; 1 Peter 1:18"> For the statutes of these people concern what is vanity; for it is but a tree which a man hath cut out of a forest, the work of the hands of the workman, with the axe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 46:7; Isaiah 40:19–20; Psalms 115:4"> With silver and with gold do they ornament it; with nails and with hammers do they fasten it, that it move not from its place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 46:7; 1 Corinthians 12:2; Isaiah 41:23–24"> As a wrought-out palm-like column are they, and cannot speak; they must needs be borne, because they cannot step along. Be not afraid of them; for they cannot do any harm, so also to do any good is not in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 96:4; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 48:1"> Forasmuch as there is none like unto thee, O Lord: thou &#91;alone&#93; art great, and great is thy name in might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 15:4; Psalms 22:28; 1 Corinthians 1:19–20"> Who would not fear thee, O King of the nations? for to thee doth it appertain; because among all the wise men of the nations, and in all their kingdoms, &#91;they say&#93; there is none like unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Zechariah 10:2; Jeremiah 10:14; Psalms 115:8"> But at once shall they be shown to be brutish and foolish: it is a doctrine of vanities, it concerneth but wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 115:4; Daniel 10:5; 1 Kings 10:22"> The beaten out silver is brought from Tharshish, and gold from Uphaz, the work of the workman, and of the hands of the goldsmith: blue and purple is their clothing; the work of skilful men are they all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Isaiah 57:15; Nahum 1:6"> But the Lord God is the truth, he is the living God, and the everlasting king: at his wrath the earth shall quake, and nations cannot endure his indignation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 96:5; Isaiah 2:18; Lamentations 3:66"> Thus shall ye say unto them, The gods that have not made the heavens and the earth, shall perish away from the earth, and from under these heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 1:1; Job 9:8; Proverbs 3:19"> He made the earth by his power, he established the world by his wisdom, and by his understanding he stretched out the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 135:7; Job 38:22; Job 38:34–35"> At the sound when he giveth a multitude of waters in the heavens, and causeth clouds to ascend from the ends of the earth; when he maketh lightnings with rain, and bringeth forth the wind out of his treasures:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 135:16–18; Psalms 115:4–8; Isaiah 46:7–8"> Then standeth every man as brutish without knowledge; ashamed is every goldsmith because of the graven image; for falsehood is his molten work, and there is no breath therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:22; Isaiah 41:24; Jeremiah 51:18"> They are vanity, the work of deception; in the time of their punishment shall they vanish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:9; Jeremiah 32:18; Jeremiah 31:35"> Not like these is the portion of Jacob; for he is the former of all things; and Israel is the tribe of his inheritance: The Lord of hosts is his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:3–12; Jeremiah 6:1; Matthew 24:15"> Gather up thy wares from the ground, O inhabitress of the beleaguered city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:29; Zechariah 1:6; Deuteronomy 28:63–64"> For thus hath said the Lord, Behold, I will cast forth as with a sling the inhabitants of the land at this time, and I will enclose them, in order that they may find them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Micah 7:9; Jeremiah 4:31; Jeremiah 14:17"> Woe is me for my breach! my wound is painful; but I thought, This is but pain, and I shall be able to bear it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:20; Jeremiah 31:15; Isaiah 54:2"> &#91;But now&#93; my tent is laid waste, and all my cords are torn asunder; my children are gone away from me, and they are not &#91;here&#93;; there is no one to stretch forth my tent any more, and to set up my curtains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2–10; Jeremiah 12:10; Jeremiah 23:1–2"> For the shepherds were brutish, and the Lord had they not sought; therefore have they not prospered, and all their flocks are scattered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:11; Habakkuk 1:6–9; Jeremiah 6:22"> Behold, the noise of the report is come, and a great commotion out of the north country, to render the cities of Judah desolate, a dwelling for monsters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 20:24; Proverbs 16:1; Psalms 37:23"> I know, O Lord, that the way of man is not in his own power: it is not in man that passeth away to direct his own steps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 38:1; Psalms 6:1; Jeremiah 30:11"> Correct me, O Lord, but with justice; not in thy anger, lest thou bring me to nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 79:6–7; Jeremiah 8:16; Job 18:21"> Pour out thy fury over the nations that know thee not, and over the families that have not called on thy name; for they have eaten up Jacob, and devoured him, and consumed him, and have made his dwelling desolate.</span>
