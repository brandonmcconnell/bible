<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Malachi 3:15; Psalms 92:7; Psalms 37:1"> &#91;Too&#93; righteous art thou, O Lord, that I could plead with thee; yet must I speak of &#91;the principles of&#93; justice with thee: Wherefore is the way of the wicked happy? do all those prosper that deal treacherously?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Titus 1:16; Matthew 15:8"> Thou hast planted them; they have also taken root; they grow; they also bring forth fruit: thou art near, in their mouth, and far from their mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:18; 2 Kings 20:3; Psalms 17:3"> But thou, O Lord, knowest me; thou seest me, and probest my heart toward thee; set them apart like sheep for the slaughter, and destine them for the day of slaying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:10; Psalms 107:34; Jeremiah 4:25"> How long shall the land mourn, and the herb of all the field wither? Because of the wickedness of those that dwell therein are wholly removed the beasts and the birds; because they said, He will not see our last end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 4:12; Proverbs 24:10; Hebrews 12:3–4"> If thou hast run with the footmen, and they have wearied thee, how then canst thou contend with the horses? and if in the land of peace, &#91;wherein&#93; thou trustedst, &#91;they wearied thee,&#93; how then wilt thou do in the swelling of the Jordan?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 26:25; Jeremiah 9:4; Micah 7:5–6"> For even thy brethren, and the house of thy father, even they have dealt treacherously with thee; yea, even they have called forth a crowd of men after thee: believe them not, though they speak kindly unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 2:6; Jeremiah 11:15; Psalms 78:59–60"> I have forsaken my house, I have abandoned my heritage; I have given up the most dearly beloved of my soul into the hand of her enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 6:8; Hosea 9:15; Jeremiah 2:15"> My heritage is become unto me as a lion in the forest; it sent forth its voice against me: therefore do I hate it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 56:9; Jeremiah 7:33; 2 Kings 24:2"> Is my heritage become unto me as a bird of prey stained with blood? so that the birds of prey are all around it? Come ye, assemble all the beasts of the field, bring them hither to devour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 5:1–7; Jeremiah 23:1; Isaiah 63:18"> Many shepherds have destroyed my vineyard, they have trodden under foot my field, they have rendered my pleasant field a desolate wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:10; Isaiah 42:25; Jeremiah 14:2"> They have made it a desert, and being desolate it mourneth before me: the whole land is made desolate, because no man laid it to heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 47:6; Amos 9:4; Ezekiel 14:17"> Over all the mountain-peaks in the wilderness did the destroyers come; for the sword of the Lord devoureth from the one end of the land even to the other end of the land: there is no peace to any flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Micah 6:15; Deuteronomy 28:38; Haggai 1:6"> They have sown wheat, but they reap thorns; they have put themselves to pain &#91;but&#93; have no profit; be therefore ashamed of your products, because of the fierce anger of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:18; Zechariah 2:8; Zechariah 10:6–12"> Thus hath said the Lord against all my bad neighbors, that touch the inheritance which I have caused my people Israel to inherit, Behold, I will pluck them out of their land, and the house of Judah will I pluck out from the midst of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:6; Jeremiah 48:47; Jeremiah 49:39"> And it shall come to pass, that, after I have plucked them out, and I will again have mercy on them, and will restore them, every man to his heritage, and every man to his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:2; Joshua 23:7; Zephaniah 1:5"> And it shall come to pass, if they will truly learn the ways of my people, to swear by my name, As the Lord liveth; as they had taught my people to swear by Ba'al: that they shall then be built up in the midst of my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 60:12; Psalms 2:8–12; Daniel 11:4"> But if they will not hearken, then will I pluck out that nation, plucking out and exterminating &#91;them&#93;, saith the Lord.</span>
