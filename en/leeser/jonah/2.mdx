<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 42:7; Lamentations 3:54; Psalms 69:1–2"> (2:4) For thou hast cast me into the deep, in the heart of the seas; and the stream compasseth me about: all thy billows and thy waves have passed over me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 31:22; Psalms 5:7; 2 Chronicles 6:38"> (2:5) And I thought indeed, I am driven out from before thy eyes: yet I again shall look toward thy holy temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 69:1–2; Lamentations 3:54; Psalms 40:2"> (2:6) The waters surrounded me, to the peril of my life; the deep compassed me about: sea-weeds were bound about my head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 30:3; Psalms 16:10; Isaiah 38:17"> (2:7) To the bottoms of the mountains did I go down; the earth &#91;closed&#93; her bars about me for ever: when thou broughtest up my life from the pit, O Lord my God!</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 31:6; 2 Kings 17:15; Jeremiah 2:13"> (2:9) They that guard false vanities forsake &#91;the source of&#93; their kindness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 3:8; Psalms 50:14; Ecclesiastes 5:4–5"> (2:10) But I will sacrifice unto thee with the voice of thanksgiving; that which I have vowed will I pay; &#91;for&#93; help is with the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 8:26–27; Psalms 33:9; Genesis 1:3"> (2:11) And the Lord commanded the fish, and it vomited out Jonah upon the dry land.</span>
