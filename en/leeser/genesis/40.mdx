<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 40:13; Genesis 39:20–23; Esther 6:1"> And it came to pass after these things, that the butler of the king of Egypt and the baker committed an offense, against their lord the king of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 16:14; Proverbs 19:12; Psalms 76:10"> And Pharaoh was wroth against his two officers, against the chief of the butlers, and against the chief of the bakers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 39:20; Genesis 39:23"> And he put them in ward in the house of the captain of the guards, into the prison, the place where Joseph was confined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 39:1; Psalms 37:5; Genesis 37:36"> And the captain of the guards charged Joseph with them, and he served them; and they continued a season in ward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 41:11; Genesis 40:8; Genesis 37:5–10"> And they dreamed a dream, both of them, each his dream in one night, each in accordance with the interpretation of his dream, the butler and the baker of the king of Egypt, who were confined in the prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 41:8; Daniel 2:1–3; Genesis 40:8"> And Joseph came in unto them in the morning, and looked at them, and, behold, they were sad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:2; 1 Samuel 1:8; Luke 24:17"> And he asked the officers of Pharaoh that were with him in ward in his lord's house, saying, Wherefore look ye so sadly today?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 2:47; Genesis 41:15–16; Daniel 2:28"> And they said unto him, We have dreamed a dream, and there is none to interpret it. And Joseph said unto them, Do not interpretations belong to God? tell it to me, I pray you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 7:13–15; Genesis 37:5–10; Daniel 4:8"> The chief of the butlers then told his dream to Joseph, and said to him, In my dream, behold, a vine was before me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And on the vine were three branches; and it was as though it budded, shot forth its blossoms, and on its clusters the grapes became ripe:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:11–1; 1 Kings 10:5; 2 Chronicles 9:4"> And Pharaoh's cup was in my hand; and I took the grapes, and pressed them out into Pharaoh's cup, and I placed the cup into Pharaoh's hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 41:12; Judges 7:14; Genesis 41:25–26"> And Joseph said unto him, This is its interpretation: The three branches are three days;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 3:3; Jeremiah 52:31; 2 Kings 25:27"> Within yet three days will Pharaoh lift up thy head, and restore thee unto thy office; and thou shalt place Pharaoh's cup into his hand, after the former manner when thou wast his butler.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 2:12; Luke 23:42; 1 Samuel 20:14–15"> Therefore if thou thinkest on me when it shall be well with thee, then show kindness, I pray thee, unto me, and make mention of me unto Pharaoh, and bring me out of this house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:17–18; Genesis 39:20; 1 Timothy 1:10"> For indeed I was stolen away out of the land of the Hebrews; and here also have I not done the least that they should put me into the dungeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 40:1–2"> And when the chief of the bakers saw that he had well interpreted, he said unto Joseph, I also &#91;saw&#93; in my dream, and, behold, I had three baskets with fine bread on my head:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 49:20; 1 Chronicles 12:20"> And in the uppermost basket there was of all manner of bakemeats, used as food for Pharaoh; and the birds did eat them out of the basket from my head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 40:12; Genesis 41:26; 1 Corinthians 11:24"> And Joseph answered and said, This is the interpretation: The three baskets are three days;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 40:22; Acts 20:27; 2 Samuel 21:6"> Within yet three days will Pharaoh lift up thy head from off thee, and will hang thee on a tree; and the birds shall eat thy flesh from off thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 40:19; Genesis 40:13; Matthew 14:6"> And it came to pass on the third day, which was Pharaoh's birthday, that he made a feast unto all his servants: and he lifted up the head of the chief of the butlers and the head of the chief of the bakers among his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 40:13; Nehemiah 2:1"> And he restored the chief of the butlers unto his butlership; and he placed the cup in Pharaoh's hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 40:19; Genesis 41:16; Daniel 2:30"> But the chief of the bakers he hanged, as Joseph had interpreted to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 31:12; Job 19:14; Psalms 105:19"> Yet the chief of the butlers did not remember Joseph, and forgot him.</span>
