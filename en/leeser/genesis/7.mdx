<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Also of the fowls of the heaven, seven pairs of each, the male and the female; to keep seed alive upon the face of all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 6:13; Genesis 7:12; Genesis 7:17"> For after only seven days more, I will cause it to rain upon the earth forty days and forty nights: and I will blot out every living substance that I have made from off the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 6:22; Luke 8:21; Exodus 39:32"> And Noah did all just as the Lord had commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 5:32; Genesis 8:13"> And Noah was six hundred years old when the flood of waters was upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 17:27; Genesis 6:18; Matthew 24:38"> And Noah went in, and his sons, and his wife, and his sons' wives with him, into the ark, because of the waters of the flood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Of the clean beasts, and of the beasts that are not clean, and of the fowls, and of every thing that creepeth upon the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 7:16; Genesis 2:19; Jeremiah 8:7"> One pair of each went in unto Noah into the ark, the male and the female, as God had commanded Noah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 6:17; Genesis 7:4; Luke 17:27"> And it came to pass, after the seven days, that the waters of the flood were upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 8:2; Malachi 3:10; Proverbs 8:28–29"> In the six hundredth year of Noah's life, in the second month, on the seventeenth day of the month, on this same day, were all the fountains of the great deep broken up, and the windows of heaven were opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; Matthew 4:2; Genesis 7:17"> And the rain fell upon the earth forty days and forty nights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 6:18; Genesis 7:1; Genesis 6:10"> On that self-same day entered Noah, and Shem, and Ham, and Japheth, the sons of Noah, and Noah's wife, and the three wives of his sons with them, into the ark;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 7:8–9; Genesis 7:2–3"> They, and every beast after his kind, and all the cattle after their kind, and every creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth after its kind, and every fowl after his kind, every bird, every thing that hath wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 6:19–20; Isaiah 11:6"> And they went in unto Noah into the ark, one pair of each, of all flesh, wherein is the breath of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 25:10; Luke 13:25; Psalms 91:1–10"> And they that went in, went in male and female of all flesh, as God had commanded him: and then the Lord shut him in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; Genesis 7:12"> And the flood was forty days upon the earth; and the waters increased, and bore up the ark, and it was lifted up above the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 14:28; Psalms 69:15; Job 22:16"> And the waters prevailed, and increased greatly upon the earth; and the ark floated along upon the face of the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 104:6–9; Psalms 46:2–3; 2 Peter 3:6"> And the waters prevailed exceedingly upon the earth; and all the high mountains that are under the whole heavens were covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:23; Psalms 104:6"> Fifteen cubits above them did the waters prevail; and the mountains were &#91;thus&#93; covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 6:13; Genesis 7:4; Genesis 6:17"> And all flesh perished that moved upon the earth, both of fowl, and of cattle, and of beast, and of every creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth, and every man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 2:7; Genesis 6:17"> All in whose nostrils was the breath of life, of all that were on the dry land, died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Peter 2:5; 1 Peter 3:20; Hebrews 11:7"> And it swept off every living substance which was upon the face of the ground, both man, and cattle, and creeping things, and fowls of the heaven; and they were swept from the earth; and Noah only was left, together with those that were with him in the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 8:3–4"> And the waters prevailed upon the earth one hundred and fifty days.</span>
