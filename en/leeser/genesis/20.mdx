<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 26:1; Genesis 26:6; Genesis 18:1"> And Abraham journeyed from there toward the south country, and dwelt between Kadesh and Shur and sojourned in Gerar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 12:15; Genesis 26:7; Genesis 12:11–13"> And Abraham said of Sarah his wife, She is my sister; and Abimelech the king of Gerar sent and took Sarah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 20:7; Matthew 27:19; Psalms 105:14"> But God came to Abimelech in a dream by night, and said to him, Behold, thou shalt die for the sake of the woman whom thou hast taken; for she is a man's wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 18:23–25; 1 Chronicles 21:17; 2 Samuel 4:11"> But Abimelech had not come near to her; and he said, Lord, wilt thou then slay also a righteous nation?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 7:8; 1 Kings 9:4; Daniel 6:22"> Said he not unto me, She is my sister? and she, even she herself, said, He is my brother; in the integrity of my heart and the innocency of my hands have I done this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:34; Proverbs 21:1; 1 Samuel 25:26"> And God said unto him in the dream, Yea, I also well know that thou hast done this in the integrity of thy heart; therefore did I also withhold thee from sinning against me; for this cause I suffered thee not to touch her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 42:8; 1 Samuel 7:5; 2 Samuel 24:17"> And now restore the man's wife, for he is a prophet, and he will pray for thee, that thou mayest live; and if thou restore her not, know thou, that thou shalt surety die, thou and all that are thine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And Abimelech rose early in the morning, and called all his servants, and told all these things in their hearing; and the men were greatly afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 12:18; Genesis 26:10; Genesis 39:9"> Then Abimelech called Abraham, and said unto him, What hast thou done unto us? and in what have I offended thee, that thou hast brought on me and on my kingdom a great sin? deeds that ought not to be done thou hast done unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Abimelech said unto Abraham, What sawest thou, that thou didst this thing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 26:7; Genesis 12:12; Job 28:28"> And Abraham said, Because I thought, Surely there is no fear of God in this place, and they will slay me for the sake of my wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 11:29; Genesis 12:13; 1 Thessalonians 5:22"> And yet indeed she is my sister, the daughter of my father, but not the daughter of my mother; and she became my wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 12:1; Genesis 12:9; Acts 7:3–5"> And it came to pass, when God caused me to wander from my father's house, that I said unto her, This is thy kindness which thou shalt show unto me; at every place whither we shall come, say of me, He is my brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 12:16; Genesis 20:2; Genesis 20:7"> And Abimelech took sheep, and oxen, and men-servants, and women-servants, and gave them unto Abraham, and restored to him Sarah his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 34:10; Genesis 13:9; Genesis 47:6"> And Abimelech said, Behold, my land is before thee: dwell where it is pleasing in thy eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 27:5; Genesis 20:5; Genesis 24:65"> And unto Sarah he said, Behold, I have given thy brother a thousand pieces of silver: behold, this is to thee a covering of the eyes unto all that are with thee; and with all others thou canst thus justify thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 5:16; Matthew 21:22; Job 42:9–10"> And Abraham prayed unto God; and God healed Abimelech, and his wife, and his maid-servants, so that they could bear children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 12:17; Genesis 30:2; Genesis 16:2"> For the Lord had fast closed up every womb of the house of Abimelech, because of Sarah, Abraham's wife.</span>
