<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 5:2; Genesis 29:31; Genesis 37:11"> And when Rachel saw that she bore Jacob no children, Rachel envied her sister; and she said unto Jacob, Give me children, and if not, I die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 16:2; Psalms 127:3; 2 Kings 5:7"> And Jacob's anger was kindled against Rachel: and he said, Am I in God's stead, who hath withheld from thee the fruit of the womb?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 50:23; Job 3:12; Ruth 4:11"> And she said, Behold, &#91;here is&#93; my maid Bilhah, go in unto her; and she shall bear upon my knees, that I may also have children by her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 35:22; Genesis 21:10; 2 Samuel 12:11"> And she gave him Bilhah her handmaid for wife, and Jacob went in unto her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And Bilhah conceived, and bore Jacob a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Lamentations 3:59; Psalms 35:24; Psalms 43:1"> And Rachel said, God hath judged me, and hath also heard my voice, and hath given me a son: therefore called she his name Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And Bilhah, Rachel's maid, conceived again, and bore Jacob a second son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 32:24–25; Genesis 23:6; Genesis 49:21"> And Rachel said, Contests of God have I contended with my sister, I have also prevailed: and she called his name Naphtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 30:4; Genesis 30:17; Genesis 29:35"> When Leah now saw that she had left off bearing, she took Zilpah her maid, and gave her to Jacob for wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Zilpah Leah's maid bore Jacob a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 49:19; Isaiah 65:11; Genesis 35:26"> And Leah said, Good luck hath come: and she called his name Gad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And Zilpah Leah's maid bore a second son unto Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 1:48; Proverbs 31:28; Song of Solomon 6:9"> And Leah said, To my happiness; for the daughters will call me blessed: and she called his name Asher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Song of Solomon 7:13; Genesis 25:30"> And Reuben went in the days of the wheat harvest, and found mandrakes in the field, and he brought them unto Leah his mother; then Rachel said to Leah, Give me, I pray thee, &#91;some&#93; of thy son's mandrakes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 16:13; 1 Corinthians 4:3; Numbers 16:9–10"> And she said unto her, Is it not enough that thou hast taken my husband? and wouldst thou also take away my son's mandrakes? And Rachel said, Therefore shall he be with thee to-night for thy son's mandrakes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Jacob came out of the field in the evening, and Leah went out to meet him, and said, Unto me thou must come in; for surely I have obtained thee as a reward with my son's mandrakes. And he lay with her that night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 30:6; Genesis 30:22; Exodus 3:7"> And God hearkened unto Leah, and she conceived and bore Jacob a fifth son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 49:14–15; Deuteronomy 33:18; Genesis 46:13"> And Leah said, God hath given me my reward, because I have given my maid to my husband: and she called his name Issachar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And Leah conceived again, and bore a sixth son unto Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 35:23; Matthew 4:13; Genesis 49:13"> And Leah said, God hath endued me with a good dowry; now will my husband dwell with me, because I have borne him six sons; and she called his name Zebulun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 34:1–3; Genesis 34:26; Genesis 46:15"> And afterward she bore a daughter, and she called her name Dinah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 29:31; Psalms 113:9; Psalms 127:3"> And God remembered Rachel, and God hearkened to her, and opened her womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 1:25; Genesis 29:31; Luke 1:27"> And she conceived, and bore a son; and she said, God hath taken away my reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 49:22–26; Genesis 35:24; Genesis 37:2"> And she called his name Joseph, saying, The Lord shall add to me another son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 24:54; Genesis 24:56; Genesis 28:15"> And it came to pass, when Rachel had borne Joseph, that Jacob said unto Laban, Send me away, that I may go unto my own place, and to my country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 29:30; Hosea 12:12; Genesis 31:26"> Give me my wives and my children, for whom I have served thee, and let me go; for thou knowest my service with which I have served thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ruth 2:13; Acts 7:10; Isaiah 61:9"> And Laban said unto him, If I could but find favor in thy eyes; I have learned by experience that the Lord hath blessed me for thy sake:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 29:15; Genesis 29:19"> And he said, Appoint me thy wages, and I will give them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 31:6; Genesis 31:38–40; 1 Peter 2:18"> And he said unto him, Thou knowest how I have served thee, and what thy cattle hath become with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:8; 2 Corinthians 12:14; Deuteronomy 11:10"> For it was a little which thou hadst before I came, and it is now increased into a multitude; and the Lord hath blessed thee since my coming; and now when shall I provide also for my own house?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:4–6; Hebrews 13:5; Psalms 118:8"> And he said, What shall I give thee? And Jacob said, Thou shalt not give me the least; if thou wilt do this thing for me, I will again feed and keep thy flock:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 31:8; Genesis 31:10; Genesis 30:35"> I will pass through all thy flock today, removing from there every speckled and spotted lamb, and every brown lamb among the sheep, and whatever is spotted and speckled among the goats; and such shall be after this my reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 13:14; 2 Samuel 22:21; 1 Samuel 26:23"> And my righteousness shall testify for me in time to come, when it shall come with my reward before thy face: every one that is not speckled and spotted among the goats, and brown among the sheep, that shall be counted stolen with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:7; Numbers 22:29; 1 Corinthians 14:5"> And Laban said, Well, let it be according to thy word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 31:9"> And he removed on that day the he-goats that were ring-streaked and spotted, and all the she-goats that were speckled and spotted, every one that had some white on it, and all the brown among the sheep, and gave them into the hand of his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And he put a space of three days' journey between himself and Jacob; and Jacob fed the flocks of Laban that were left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Genesis 31:9–13; Ezekiel 31:8"> And Jacob took himself rods of green poplar, and of the hazel and chestnut tree; and peeled thereon white streaks, laying bare the white which was on the rods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And he set the rods which he had peeled in the gutters in the watering troughs, where the flocks came to drink, just before the flocks, and where they conceived, when they came to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 12:35–36; Genesis 31:9–12; Genesis 31:40"> And the flocks conceived before the rods, and brought forth ring-streaked, speckled, and spotted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And these lambs did Jacob separate, and set the faces of the flocks toward the ring-streaked, and whatever was brown in the flock of Laban; and he put his own flocks by themselves, and put them not with Laban's cattle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> And it came to pass, whensoever the stronger cattle did conceive, that Jacob laid the rods before the eyes of the cattle in the gutters, that they might conceive among the rods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> But when the cattle were feeble, he put them not in; so the feebler belonged to Laban, and the stronger to Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Genesis 24:35; Genesis 30:30; Genesis 26:13–14"> And the man increased exceedingly, and he had many flocks, and maid-servants, and men-servants, and camels, and asses.</span>
