<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:8; Genesis 42:20; Deuteronomy 8:16"> And my cup, the silver cup, thou shalt put in the mouth of the sack of the youngest, and the money for his corn. And he did according to the word of Joseph which he had spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> As soon as the morning was light, the men were sent away, they and their asses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 10:32; Psalms 35:12; Proverbs 17:13"> They were gone out of the city, not yet far off, when Joseph said unto the superintendent of his house, Up, follow after the men; and when thou hast overtaken them, say unto them, Wherefore have ye returned evil for good?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 30:27; Leviticus 19:26; 2 Kings 21:6"> Is not this out of which my lord drinketh, and whereby indeed he divineth? ye have done evil in so doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And he overtook them, and he spoke unto them these same words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 38:16–18; Genesis 37:18–32; 2 Kings 8:13"> And they said unto him, Wherefore will my lord speak such words as these? God forbid that thy servants should do any thing like this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 43:12; Genesis 42:35; Romans 13:9"> Behold the money, which we found in the mouth of our sacks, we brought back unto thee out of the land of Canaan: how then should we steal out of thy lord's house silver or gold?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 31:32; Psalms 7:3–5; Job 31:38–40"> With whomsoever of thy servants it be found, let him die; and we also will be bondmen unto thy lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 22:3; Genesis 44:17; Genesis 44:33"> And he said, Now also let it be according to your words: he with whom it is found shall be my servant; but ye shall be blameless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And they made haste, and every one of them took down his sack to the ground, and every one opened his sack.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 44:26–32; Genesis 42:36–38; Genesis 43:14"> And he searched, at the eldest he began, and at the youngest he left off; and the cup was found in Benjamin's sack.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:11; Genesis 37:29–34; 2 Samuel 1:2"> Then they rent their clothes, and every one loaded his ass, and they returned to the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 50:18; Genesis 43:25; Genesis 43:16"> And Judah and his brothers came into Joseph's house, and he was yet there; and they fell down before him on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 4:10; Genesis 21:26; Genesis 3:13"> And Joseph said unto them, What deed is this that ye have done? knew ye not that such a man as I can certainly divine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 44:9; Daniel 9:7; Numbers 32:23"> And Judah said, What shall we say unto my lord? what shall we speak? or how shall we justify ourselves? God hath found out the iniquity of thy servants: behold we are servants unto my lord, both we, as also he in whose hand the cup was found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 17:15; 2 Samuel 23:3; Genesis 18:25"> And he said, God forbid that I should do this: the man in whose hand the cup was found, he shall be my servant; and as for you, go you up in peace unto your father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 32:22; Genesis 41:40; Genesis 18:30"> Then Judah came near unto him, and said, Pardon, my lord, let thy servant, I pray thee, speak a word in my lord's ears, and let not thy anger burn against thy servant; for thou art even as Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 43:7; Genesis 43:29; Genesis 42:7–10"> My lord asked his servants, saying, Have ye a father, or a brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 37:3; Genesis 42:38; Luke 7:12"> And we said unto my lord, We have an old father, and a little child born in his old age; and his brother is dead, and he alone is left of his mother, and his father loveth him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 42:15; Genesis 42:20; Amos 9:4"> And thou saidst unto thy servants, Bring him down unto me, that I may set my eye upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 44:30; Genesis 42:38"> And we said unto my lord, The lad cannot leave his father; for if he should leave his father, he would die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 43:5; Genesis 43:3; Genesis 42:15–20"> And thou saidst unto thy servants, Except your youngest brother come down with you, ye shall not see my face any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 42:29–34"> And it came to pass, when we came up unto thy servant my father, that we told him the words of my lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 43:2; Genesis 43:5"> And our father said, Go back and buy us a little food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 11:7; Genesis 43:4–5"> And we said, We cannot go down: if our youngest brother be with us, then will we go down; for we cannot see the man's face, except our youngest brother be with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 46:19; Genesis 35:16–18; Genesis 30:22–25"> And thy servant my father said unto us, Ye know that my wife bore me two sons;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 37:33; Genesis 37:13–14; Genesis 42:36"> And the one went out from me, and I said, Surely he hath been torn to pieces; and I have not seen him up to this time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 42:38; Genesis 43:14; Deuteronomy 31:17"> And if ye take this one also from me, and mischief befall him, ye will bring down my gray hairs with sorrow to the grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:1; 2 Samuel 18:33; Genesis 44:31"> And now, when I come to thy servant my father, and the lad be not with us; seeing that his life is bound up in the lad's life;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 44:29; Genesis 37:35; 2 Corinthians 7:10"> It will come to pass, that when he seeth that the lad is not with us, he will die: and thy servants would thus bring down the gray hairs of thy servant our father with sorrow to the grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 43:8–9; Genesis 43:16"> For thy servant became surety for the lad unto my father, saying, If I bring him not unto thee, then shall I have sinned against my father all the days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 John 3:16; Exodus 32:32; Hebrews 7:22"> Now therefore, I pray thee, let thy servant abide instead of the lad as bond-man to my lord; and let the lad go up with his brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Esther 8:6; Psalms 116:3; Psalms 119:143"> For how shall I go up to my father, and the lad be not with me? I should perhaps be compelled to witness the evil which would come on my father.</span>
