<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 7:11; Matthew 8:26–27; Proverbs 8:28"> The fountains also of the deep, and the windows of heaven were stopped; and the rain from heaven was restrained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 7:24; Genesis 7:11"> And the waters returned from off the earth, gradually returning; and the waters were abated after the end of the hundred and fifty days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 19:37; Jeremiah 51:27; Isaiah 37:38"> And the ark rested in the seventh month, on the seventeenth day of the month, upon the mountains of Ararat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 7:11"> And the waters decreased continually until the tenth month; in the tenth month, on the first day of the month, were the tops of the mountains seen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 6:16; Daniel 6:10"> And it came to pass at the end of forty days, that Noah opened the window of the ark which he had made;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 147:9; 1 Kings 17:6; 1 Kings 17:4"> And he sent forth a raven which went forth to and fro, until the waters were dried up from off the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:14; Song of Solomon 1:15; Matthew 10:16"> He then sent forth a dove from him, to see if the waters were abated from off the face of the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 11:28; Psalms 116:7; Deuteronomy 28:65"> But the dove found no resting-place for the sole of her foot, and she returned unto him unto the ark; for there was water on the face of the whole earth; then he put forth his hand, and took her, and brought her into the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; Genesis 7:10; Genesis 8:12"> And he stayed yet another seven days, and again he sent forth the dove out of the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:15; Zechariah 4:12–14; Romans 10:15"> And the dove came in to him at the time of the evening; and, lo, an olive-leaf plucked off was in her mouth; so Noah knew that the waters were abated from off the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 25:9; Isaiah 30:18; Genesis 8:10"> And he stayed yet other seven days, and sent forth the dove; but she returned not again unto him any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 7:11"> And it came to pass in the six hundredth and first year, in the first month, on the first day of the month, that the waters were dried up from off the earth; and Noah removed the covering of the ark, and looked, and, behold, the face of the ground was dry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 7:13–14; Genesis 7:11"> And in the second month, on the seven and twentieth day of the month, was the earth perfectly dried up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And God spoke unto Noah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 7:13; Psalms 121:8; Psalms 91:11"> Go forth from the ark, thou, and thy wife, and thy sons, and thy sons' wives with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 1:22; Genesis 9:1; Jeremiah 31:27–28"> Every living thing that is with thee, of all flesh, both of fowl, and of cattle, and of every creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth, bring forth with thee; that they may breed abundantly on the earth, and be fruitful, and multiply upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 121:8"> And Noah went forth, and his sons, and his wife, and his sons' wives with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Every beast, every creeping thing, and every fowl, whatsoever creepeth upon the earth, after their families, went forth out of the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15–16; Romans 12:1; Genesis 22:9"> And Noah built an altar unto the Lord, and he took of every clean cattle, and of every clean fowl, and offered burnt-offerings on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:15; Ephesians 5:2; Isaiah 54:9–10"> And the Lord smelled the sweet savour; and the Lord said in his heart, I will not again curse the ground any more for the sake of man; although the imagination of man's heart is evil from his youth: neither will I again smite any more every thing living, as I have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 5:7; Psalms 74:16–17; Jeremiah 5:24"> All the while the earth remaineth, seed-time and harvest, and cold and heat, and summer and winter, and day and night, shall not cease.</span>
