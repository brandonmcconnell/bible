<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 18:15; Acts 10:41; Genesis 43:30–31"> Then could Joseph not refrain himself before all those that stood by him; and he cried, Cause every man to go out from me. And there remained no man with him, while Joseph made himself known unto his brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 46:29; Acts 20:37; Numbers 14:1"> And he raised his voice in weeping; and the Egyptians heard it, and the house of Pharaoh heard it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 7:13; Acts 9:5; Zechariah 12:10"> And Joseph said unto his brothers, I am Joseph; doth my father yet live? And his brothers could not answer him; for they were terrified at his presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 37:28; Matthew 14:27; Genesis 50:18"> And Joseph said unto his brothers, Come near to me, I pray you; and they came near; and he said, I am Joseph your brother, whom ye sold into Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 50:20; Acts 7:9–15; Genesis 45:7–8"> But now be not grieved, nor be angry with yourselves, that ye sold me hither; for in order to preserve life did God send me before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 47:23; Genesis 41:54; Genesis 41:56"> For these two years hath the famine been already in the land; and there are yet five years, in which there will be neither ploughing nor harvesting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 44:4; Acts 7:35; Psalms 18:50"> And God hath thus sent me before you to prepare for you a permanence on the earth, and to save your lives by a great deliverance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 15:16; Romans 9:16; John 19:11"> So now it was not you that sent me hither, but God; and he hath made me a father to Pharaoh, and a lord for all his house, and a ruler throughout all the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 45:19–20; Genesis 45:13"> Haste ye, and go up to my father, and say unto him, Thus hath said thy son Joseph, God hath made me lord of all Egypt; come down unto me, tarry not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 9:26; Exodus 8:22; Genesis 46:34–6"> And thou shalt dwell in the land of Goshen, and thou shalt be near unto me, thou, and thy children, and thy children's children, and thy flocks, and thy herds, and all that is thine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 47:12; Genesis 47:6; Matthew 15:5–6"> And I will maintain thee there; for there are yet five years of famine; lest thou, and thy household, and all that thou hast, come to poverty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 42:23; Luke 24:39; John 20:27"> And, behold, your own eyes see, and the eyes of my brother Benjamin, that it is my mouth that speaketh unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 7:14; Revelation 21:23; 1 Peter 1:10–12"> And ye shall tell my father of all my honor in Egypt, and of all that ye have seen; and ye shall hasten and bring down my father hither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 33:4; Genesis 29:11; Genesis 46:29"> And he fell upon his brother Benjamin's neck, and wept; and Benjamin wept upon his neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 15:20; 1 Samuel 10:1; Psalms 77:4"> And he kissed all his brothers, and wept upon them; and after that his brothers spoke with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 41:33; Esther 1:21; Genesis 20:15"> And the report thereof was heard in Pharaoh's house, saying, Joseph's brothers are come; and it was pleasing in the eyes of Pharaoh, and in the eyes of his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 42:25–26; Genesis 44:1–2"> And Pharaoh said unto Joseph, Say unto thy brothers, This do ye; load your beasts, and go, get you unto the land of Canaan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 27:28; Numbers 18:12; Genesis 47:6"> And take your father and your households, and come unto me; and I will give you the best of the land of Egypt, and ye shall eat the fat of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 46:5; Genesis 45:27; Genesis 31:17–18"> And thou art commanded, This do ye, take unto yourselves out of the land of Egypt wagons for your little ones, and for your wives, and take up your father, and come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 1:19; Luke 17:31; Deuteronomy 7:16"> And do ye feel no concern on account of your household goods; for the best of all the land of Egypt is yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 45:19; Ezekiel 23:24; Lamentations 1:18"> And the children of Israel did so; and Joseph gave them wagons, according to the command of Pharaoh; and he gave them provision for the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 43:34; 2 Kings 5:5; Judges 14:12"> To all of them he gave to each changes of raiment; but to Benjamin he gave three hundred pieces of silver, and five changes of raiment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 24:10; Exodus 16:3; Genesis 43:11"> And to his father he sent after this manner: ten asses laden with the best things of Egypt, and ten she-asses laden with corn and bread and other food for his father, for the journey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:13; Psalms 133:1–3; Genesis 42:21–22"> And he accompanied his brothers on the way, and they departed: and he said unto them, Do not fall out by the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And they went out of Egypt, and came into the land of Canaan, unto Jacob their father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 44:28; Genesis 37:35; Luke 24:34"> And they told him, saying, Joseph is yet alive; and that he is governor over all the land of Egypt. But his heart remained cold, for he believed them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 85:6; Isaiah 57:15; Genesis 45:19"> But when they told him all the words of Joseph, which he had said unto them; and when he saw the wagons which Joseph had sent to carry him: the spirit of Jacob their father revived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 2:28–30; John 16:21–22; Genesis 46:30"> And Israel said, Enough; Joseph my son is yet alive: I will go and see him before I die.</span>
