<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esther 6:4; Esther 4:16; Revelation 3:21"> And it came to pass on the third day, that Esther put on her royal apparel, and placed herself in the inner court of the king's house, opposite the king's apartment; and the king was sitting upon his royal throne in the royal apartment, opposite to the entrance of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esther 8:4; Esther 4:11; Proverbs 21:1"> And it happened, when the king saw Esther the queen standing in the court, that she obtained grace in his eyes; and the king held out to Esther the golden sceptre that was in his hand; and Esther drew near, and touched the top of the sceptre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 6:23; Esther 7:2; Esther 5:6"> Then said the king unto her, What wilt thou, queen Esther? and what is thy request? if it be equal to half of the kingdom it shall still be given thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esther 5:8; Proverbs 29:11; Esther 3:15"> And Esther said, If it seem good unto the king, let the king and Haman come this day unto the banquet which I have prepared for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esther 6:14"> Then said the king, Bring Haman quickly hither to fulfill the word of Esther: so came the king with Haman to the banquet which Esther had prepared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esther 7:2; Esther 5:3; Esther 9:12"> And the king said unto Esther at the banquet of wine, What is thy petition? and it shall be granted thee: and what is thy request? even if it be equal to half of the kingdom, it shall still be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Then answered Esther, and said, My petition and my request are,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esther 7:3; Proverbs 16:9; Esther 8:5"> If I have found grace in the eyes of the king, and if it please the king to grant my petition, and to fulfill my request, that the king may come with Haman to the banquet which I will prepare for them, and tomorrow will I do according to the word of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esther 3:5; Matthew 2:16; Psalms 27:3"> And Haman went forth on that day joyful and with a glad heart; but when Haman saw Mordecai in the king's gate, who did not rise up, nor move out of the way for him, then was Haman filled against Mordecai with fury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esther 6:13; Ecclesiastes 7:9; 2 Samuel 13:22–23"> Nevertheless Haman refrained himself, and went to his house: he then sent and had his friends brought in with Zeresh his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esther 9:7–10; Esther 3:1; Luke 12:19–20"> And Haman recounted to them the glory of his riches, and the multitude of his children, and all the things wherein the king had made him great, and how he had advanced him above the princes and the servants of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; Psalms 37:35–36; Job 20:5–8"> And Haman said &#91;farther&#93;, Yea, Esther the queen did not let any one come in with the king unto the banquet that she had prepared but myself: and also for tomorrow am I invited unto her with the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 15:20; Job 18:4; Philippians 4:11–12"> Yet all this profiteth me nothing, every time that I see Mordecai the Jew sitting in the king's gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esther 6:4; Psalms 37:14; 1 Kings 21:7"> Then said unto him Zeresh his wife with all his friends, Let them make a gallows of fifty cubits high, and in the mourning speak unto the king that they may hang Mordecai thereon; and then go thou in with the king unto the banquet joyfully. And the thing pleased Haman; and he had the gallows made.</span>
