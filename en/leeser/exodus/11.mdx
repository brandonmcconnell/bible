<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 3:22; Exodus 12:35–36; Haggai 2:8"> Speak now in the ears of the people, and let them ask every man of his neighbor, and every woman of her neighbor, vessels of silver, and vessels of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 3:21; Exodus 12:36; Revelation 3:9"> And the Lord gave the people favor in the eyes of the Egyptians; also the man Moses was very great in the land of Egypt, in the eyes of Pharaoh's servants, and in the eyes of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 12:29; Psalms 60:10; Job 34:20"> And Moses said, Thus hath said the Lord, About midnight will I go out in the midst of Egypt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 78:51; Exodus 12:12; Psalms 105:36"> And there shall die every first-born in the land of Egypt, from the first-born of Pharaoh that is to sit upon his throne, even unto the first-born of the maid-servant that is behind the mill; and every first-born of cattle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 12:30; Amos 5:17; Zephaniah 1:10"> And there shall be a great cry throughout all the land of Egypt, the like of which hath never been, and the like of which will not be any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 10:21; Exodus 10:23; Exodus 8:22"> But against any of the children of Israel not a dog shall move his tongue, neither against man nor beast; in order that ye may know how that the Lord doth distinguish between the Egyptians and Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 12:31–33; Ezekiel 3:14; Daniel 3:19"> And all these thy servants shall come down unto me, and bow themselves down unto me, saying, Get thee out, and all the people that followeth thee; and after that I will go out: and he went out from Pharaoh in a burning anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 7:3–4; Exodus 3:19; Exodus 10:1"> And the Lord said unto Moses, Pharaoh will not hearken unto you; in order that my wonders may be multiplied in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 4:21; Exodus 10:20; Exodus 10:27"> And Moses and Aaron did all these wonders before Pharaoh; and the Lord hardened Pharaoh's heart, and he did not let the children of Israel go out of his land.</span>
