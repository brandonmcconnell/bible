<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 1:10–11; Proverbs 1:15; Luke 23:23–24"> Thou shalt not follow a multitude to do evil; neither shalt thou speak in a cause, to incline after many, to wrest judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 3:17; Leviticus 19:15; Psalms 82:2–3"> Neither shalt thou countenance a poor man in his cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:1–4; 1 Thessalonians 5:15; Matthew 5:44"> If thou meet thy enemy's ox or his ass going astray, thou shalt surely bring it back to him again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:4"> If thou see the ass of him that hateth thee lying under his burden, and wouldest forbear to unload him, &#91;thou must not do so, but&#93; thou shalt surely unload with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 3:5; Jeremiah 5:28; Leviticus 19:15"> Thou shalt not wrest the judgment of thy poor in his cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 4:25; Exodus 34:7; Exodus 23:1"> Keep thyself far from a false speech; and him who hath been declared innocent and righteous thou shalt not slay; for I will not justify the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Proverbs 17:23; Isaiah 5:23"> And thou shalt take no bribe; for the bribe blindeth the clear-sighted, and perverteth the words of the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:19; Exodus 22:21; Deuteronomy 24:14–18"> And a stranger shalt thou not oppress; for ye know well the spirit of the stranger, seeing ye yourselves were strangers in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 25:3–4"> And six years shalt thou sow thy land, and shalt gather in the fruits thereof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 26:34–35; Leviticus 25:2–7; Leviticus 25:11–12"> But the seventh year shalt thou let it rest and lie still; that the needy of thy people may eat &#91;of it&#93;; and what they leave the beasts of the field shall eat: in like manner shalt thou deal with thy vineyard, and with thy olive tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 13:14; Exodus 34:21; Exodus 35:3"> Six days shalt thou do thy work, and on the seventh day shalt thou rest; that thy ox and thy ass may repose, and the son of thy hand-maid, and the stranger, may be refreshed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 23:7; Zechariah 13:2; 1 Timothy 4:16"> And in all things that I have said unto you be on your guard; and of the name of other gods ye shall make no mention, it shall not be heard out of thy mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 34:22–24; Leviticus 23:34"> Three times shalt thou keep a feast unto me in the year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 22:7; Exodus 34:20; Numbers 28:16–25"> The feast of unleavened bread shalt thou keep; seven days shalt thou eat unleavened bread, as I commanded thee, in the time appointed of the month of Abib; for in it thou camest out from Egypt: and none shall appear before me empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 34:22; Exodus 22:29; Leviticus 23:9–21"> And the feast of harvest, of the first-fruits of thy labors, which thou hast sown in thy field: and the feast of ingathering, at the conclusion of the year, when thou gatherest in thy labors out of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 34:23; Exodus 23:14"> Three times in the year shall all thy males appear before the Lord, the Eternal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 34:25; Leviticus 2:11; Deuteronomy 16:4"> Thou shalt not offer the blood of my sacrifice with leavened bread; neither shall the fat of my festive sacrifice remain until morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 34:26; Deuteronomy 14:21; Deuteronomy 26:10"> The first of the first-fruits of thy land shalt thou bring unto the house of the Lord thy God. Thou shalt not seethe a kid in its mother's milk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 33:14; Psalms 91:11; Exodus 32:34"> Behold, I send an angel before thee, to keep thee on the way, and to bring thee unto the place which I have prepared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 78:56; Psalms 78:40; Hebrews 12:25"> Beware of him, and obey his voice, disobey him not; for he will not pardon your transgression, because my name is in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Deuteronomy 30:7; Zechariah 2:8"> But if thou wilt carefully hearken to his voice, and do all that I shall speak: then will I be an enemy unto thy enemies, and afflict those that afflict thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 23:20; Isaiah 5:13; Genesis 15:19–21"> For my angel shall go before thee, and bring thee in unto the Emorites, and the Hittites, and the Perizzites, and the Canaanites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites; and I will cut them off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; Deuteronomy 12:30–31; Numbers 33:52"> Thou shalt not bow down to their gods, nor serve them, nor do after their deeds; but thou shalt utterly overthrow them, and completely break down their statuary images.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:15; Exodus 15:26; Joshua 22:5"> And ye shall serve the Lord your God, and he will bless thy bread, and thy water; and I will remove sickness from the midst of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:14; Job 5:26; Deuteronomy 28:4"> There shall be no one casting her children, nor a barren woman, in thy land; the number of thy days I will make full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:25; Genesis 35:5; Deuteronomy 11:25"> My terror will I send before thee, and will bring in confusion all the people to which thou shalt come; and I will make all thy enemies turn their back unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:20; Joshua 24:11–12"> And I will send hornets before thee, and they shall drive out the Hivite, the Canaanite, and the Hittite, from before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:22; Joshua 17:12–13; Judges 3:1–4"> I will not drive them out from before thee in one year; lest the land become desolate, and the beast of the field multiply against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Little by little will I drive them out from before thee, until thou be increased and canst possess the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; Deuteronomy 11:24; Joshua 21:44"> And I will set thy bounds from the Red Sea unto the sea of the Philistines, and from the desert unto the river; for I will deliver into your hand the inhabitants of the land, and thou shalt drive them out before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Exodus 34:12; Exodus 34:15"> Thou shalt not make a covenant with them, nor with their gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 106:36; Deuteronomy 7:16; Exodus 34:12"> They shall not dwell in thy land, lest they cause thee to sin against me; for thou mightest &#91;be led to&#93; serve their gods, and this would surely be a snare unto thee.</span>
