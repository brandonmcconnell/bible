<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 38:1–7; Exodus 20:24–26; Exodus 40:10"> And thou shalt make the altar of shittim wood: five cubits long, and five cubits broad, a foursquare shall the altar be, and three cubits shall be its height.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 118:27; Exodus 29:12; Leviticus 4:7"> And thou shalt make its horns on its four corners, from itself shall its horns be; and thou shalt overlay it with copper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 7:45; Numbers 4:14; 1 Chronicles 28:17"> And thou shalt make its pots to receive its ashes, and its shovels, and its basins, and its forks, and its fire-pans; all its vessels thou shalt make of copper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 35:16; Exodus 25:12; Exodus 38:4–5"> And thou shalt make for it a grating, of a network of copper; and thou shalt make upon the net four rings of copper, on its four corners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 38:4"> And thou shalt put it under the compass of the altar beneath, and the net shall reach even to the half of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 35:13–15; Numbers 4:44; Exodus 30:4"> And thou shalt make staves for the altar, staves of shittim wood, and overlay them with copper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 25:28; Exodus 30:4; Numbers 4:13–14"> And the staves shall be put into the rings, and the staves shall be upon the two sides of the altar, when they bear it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Exodus 25:9; Hebrews 8:5"> Hollow, of boards, shalt thou make it; as it was shown to thee on the mount, so shall they make it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 38:9–20; Ezekiel 40:32; Ezekiel 42:3"> And thou shalt make the court of the tabernacle: for the south side, on the right, the hangings for the court, of twisted linen, shall be a hundred cubits in length, for the one side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 26:19–21; Jeremiah 52:21; Exodus 36:38"> And its pillars shall be twenty, with their twenty sockets of copper; the hooks of the pillars and their fillets shall be of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And likewise for the north side in the length there shall be hangings one hundred cubits in length, and its pillars twenty with their twenty sockets of copper; the hooks of the pillars and their fillets shall be of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And &#91;for&#93; the breadth of the court on the west side shall be fifty cubits of hangings; their pillars shall be ten, and their sockets ten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the breadth of the court on the front side, eastward, shall be fifty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:9; Exodus 26:36"> And fifteen cubits of hangings shall be on the one wing; their pillars shall be three and their sockets three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And on the other wing shall be fifteen cubits of hangings; their pillars shall be three, and their sockets three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 26:36; Judges 5:30; Exodus 28:39"> And for the gate of the court shall be a hanging of twenty cubits, of blue, and purple, and scarlet yarn, and twisted linen, the work of the embroiderer; with four pillars for the same, and their four sockets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> All the pillars round about the court shall be filleted with silver; their hooks shall be of silver and their sockets of copper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The length of the court shall be one hundred cubits, and the breadth fifty by fifty, and the height five cubits, of twisted linen, and the sockets for the same of copper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 35:18; Isaiah 22:23–25; Numbers 4:32"> All the vessels of the tabernacle in all the service thereof, and all its pins, and all the pins of the court, shall be of copper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 24:1–4; Zechariah 4:11–14; Psalms 23:5"> And thou shalt command the children of Israel, that they bring thee pure olive oil, beaten out, for the lighting, to cause a light to burn always.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 19:21; Numbers 18:23; 1 Samuel 3:3"> In the tabernacle of the congregation, without the vail, which is before the testimony, shall Aaron with his sons arrange it &#91;for&#93; from the evening to the morning, before the Lord; as a statute for ever unto their generations, on behalf of the children of Israel.</span>
