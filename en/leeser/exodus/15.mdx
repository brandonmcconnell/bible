<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 15:3; Exodus 15:21; Psalms 106:12"> Then sang Moses and the children of Israel this song unto the Lord, and thus did they say, I will sing unto the Lord, for he hath triumphed gloriously: the horse and his rider hath he thrown into the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 118:14; Psalms 18:46"> My strength and song is the Lord, and he is become my salvation: he is my God, and I will declare his praise, the God of my father, and I will exalt him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 24:8; Exodus 14:14; Revelation 19:11–21"> The Eternal is the Lord of war; the Eternal is his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 14:13–28; Exodus 14:6–7"> The chariots of Pharaoh and his host hath he hurled into the sea; and the chosen of his captains are sunk in the Red Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:11; Exodus 15:10; Exodus 14:28"> The depths have covered them; they went down to the bottom as a stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 118:15–16; Isaiah 51:9; Revelation 2:27"> Thy right hand, O Lord, is become glorious in power; thy right hand, O Lord, hath dashed in pieces the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 5:24; Isaiah 47:14; Malachi 4:1"> And in the greatness of thy excellency hast thou overthrown those that rose up against thee; thou didst send forth thy wrath, it consumed them as stubble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 4:9; Psalms 78:13; Exodus 14:21–22"> And with the breath of thy nostrils the waters were heaped up together, the floods stood upright as a wall; congealed were the depths in the heart of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 5:30; Exodus 14:5; Isaiah 53:12"> The enemy said, I will pursue, I will overtake, I will divide the spoil; my desire shall be satisfied upon them; I will draw my sword, my hand shall destroy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Deuteronomy 11:4; Exodus 15:5"> Thou didst blow with thy wind, the sea covered them: they sunk as lead in mighty waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 8:23; Psalms 89:5–8; 1 Samuel 2:2"> Who is like unto thee, O Lord, among the mighty? who is like unto thee, glorious in holiness, fearful in praises, doing wonders?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 15:6"> Thou didst stretch out thy right hand, the earth swallowed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 77:20; 1 Peter 1:5; Psalms 77:14–15"> Thou leadest forth in thy kindness the people thou hast redeemed; thou guidest it in thy strength unto the habitation of thy holiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:25; Joshua 2:9–10; Psalms 48:6"> Nations hear it and tremble: sorrow seizeth the inhabitants of Palestine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 5:1; Joshua 2:11; Joshua 2:9"> Then were troubled the dukes of Edom; the mighty men of Moab, trembling seizeth them; faint-hearted become all the inhabitants of Canaan. l</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:25; Psalms 74:2; Joshua 2:9"> Fear and dread shall fall upon them; by the greatness of thy arm they shall be still as a stone: till thy people pass over, O Lord, till this people pass over, which thou hast purchased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 44:2; Psalms 80:8; Psalms 78:54–55"> Thou wilt bring them, and plant them on the mountain of thy inheritance, the place, O Lord, which thou hast wrought for thy residence, the sanctuary, O Lord, which thy hands have established.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 10:16; Psalms 29:10; Psalms 146:10"> The Lord will reign for ever and ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 14:28–29; Exodus 14:22–23; Hebrews 11:29"> For the horse of Pharaoh went in with his chariots and with his horsemen into the sea, and the Lord brought again upon them the waters of the sea; but the children of Israel went on dry ground through the midst of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 150:4; 1 Samuel 18:6; Psalms 68:25"> Then took Miriam the prophetess, the sister of Aaron, a timbrel in her hand; and all the women went out after her with timbrels and with dances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 15:1; Revelation 15:3; Revelation 7:10–12"> And Miriam began her song to them, Sing ye to the Lord, for he hath triumphed gloriously: the horse and his rider hath he thrown into the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 16:7; 1 Samuel 15:7; Genesis 25:18"> And Moses caused Israel to depart from the Red Sea, and they went out into the wilderness of Shur; and they went three days in the wilderness, and found no water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ruth 1:20; Numbers 33:8"> And they came to Marah; but they could not drink the waters of Marah, for they were bitter; therefore they called its name Marah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 16:2; Exodus 14:11; Numbers 20:2–5"> And the people murmured against Moses, saying, What shall we drink?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:2; Psalms 66:10; Judges 3:1"> And he cried unto the Lord; and the Lord showed him a tree, which he cast into the waters, and the waters were made sweet: there he made for them a statute and an ordinance, and there he proved them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 103:3; Deuteronomy 7:15; Exodus 23:25"> And he said, If thou wilt diligently hearken to the voice of the Lord thy God, and wilt do that which is right in his eyes, and wilt give ear to his commandments, and wilt keep all his statutes: I will put none of those diseases upon thee, which I have brought upon the Egyptians; for I the Lord am thy physician.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Revelation 22:2; Revelation 7:17; Numbers 33:9"> And they came to Elim, and there were twelve wells of water, and seventy palm-trees: and they encamped there by the water.</span>
