<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 19:8; Exodus 24:7; Joshua 24:22"> And Moses came and told the people all the words of the Lord, and all the laws of justice; and all the people answered with one voice, and said, all the words which the Lord hath spoken will we do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 28:18; Deuteronomy 31:9; Joshua 4:8–9"> And Moses wrote down all the words of the Lord, and he rose up early in the morning, and built an altar at the foot of the mount, and twelve pillars, according to the twelve tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 18:12; Leviticus 3:1–17; Exodus 19:22"> And he then sent the young men of the children of Israel, and they offered burnt-offerings, and sacrificed peace-offerings unto the Lord, of oxen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 1:19; Exodus 24:8; Hebrews 9:18"> And Moses took the half of the blood, and put it in basins; and the &#91;other&#93; half of the blood he sprinkled on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:11–13; 1 Thessalonians 5:27; Exodus 24:3–4"> And he took the book of the covenant, and read in the hearing of the people; and they said, all that the Lord hath spoken will we do and obey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:4–5; Exodus 24:6; Ezekiel 36:25"> And Moses took the blood and sprinkled it on the people, and said, Behold the blood of the covenant, which the Lord hath made with you concerning all these words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 24:1"> Then went up Moses, with Aaron, Nadab, and Abihu, and seventy of the elders of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 1:18; Ezekiel 10:1; Genesis 32:30"> And they saw the God of Israel; and the place under his feet was like a paved work of brilliant sapphire, and like the color of heaven in clearness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 15:23–24; Exodus 18:12; Genesis 31:54"> And against the nobles of the children of Israel he stretched not forth his hand; and they saw &#91;the glory of&#93; God, and did eat and drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:22; Exodus 31:18; Exodus 32:15–16"> And the Lord said unto Moses, come up to me to the mount, and remain there: and I will give thee the tables of stone, with the law, and the commandment which I have written, to teach them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 33:11; Numbers 11:28; Exodus 3:1"> And Moses rose up, and his servant Joshua; and Moses went up to the mount of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 17:10; Exodus 17:12; Genesis 22:5"> And unto the elders he said, Tarry ye for us here, until the time we come again unto you; and, behold, Aaron and Chur are with you, whoever may have any cause to be decided, let him come unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 19:9; Matthew 17:5; Exodus 19:16"> And Moses went up to the mount, and the cloud covered the mount.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 16:10; Numbers 16:42; Ezekiel 1:28"> And the glory of the Lord abode upon mount Sinai, and the cloud covered it six days; and he called unto Moses on the seventh day out of the midst of the cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Deuteronomy 4:24; Exodus 3:2"> And the appearance of the glory of the Lord was like a devouring fire on the top of the mount, before the eyes of the children of Israel.</span>
