<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 19:8; Exodus 17:6; Psalms 78:70–72"> And Moses was keeping the flock of Jithro his father-in-law, the priest of Midian; and he led the flock far away into the desert, and came to the mountain of God, to Choreb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 7:30–35; Isaiah 43:2; Mark 12:26"> And an angel of the Lord appeared unto him in a flame of fire out of the midst of a thorn-bush; and he looked, and, behold, the thorn-bush was burning with fire, but the thorn-bush was not consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 7:31; Psalms 107:8; Job 37:14"> And Moses said, I must turn aside, and see this great sight, why the thorn-bush is not burnt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:16; 1 Samuel 3:6; Acts 9:4"> And when the Lord saw that he turned aside to see God called unto him out of the midst of the thorn-bush, and said, Moses, Moses; and he said, Here am I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 5:15; Acts 7:33; Exodus 19:12"> And he said, Draw not nigh hither; put off thy shoes from off thy feet; for the place whereon thou standest is holy ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 20:37; Matthew 22:32; Exodus 4:5"> And he said, I am the God of thy father, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob; and Moses hid his face; for he was afraid to look up to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 145:19; Psalms 106:44; Exodus 2:23–25"> And the Lord said, I have truly seen the affliction of my people that is in Egypt, and have heard its cry by reason of its taskmasters; yea, I know its sorrows;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 50:24; Genesis 15:18–21; Exodus 3:17"> And I am come down to deliver it out of the hand of the Egyptians, and to bring it up out of that land unto a land, good and large, unto a land flowing with milk and honey; unto the place of the Canaanites, and the Hittites, and the Emorites, and the Perizzites, and the Hivites, and the Jebusites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 22:22–23; Exodus 2:23; Exodus 1:13–14"> Now therefore, behold, the cry of the children of Israel is come unto me: and I have also seen the oppression wherewith the Egyptians oppress them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 6:4; 1 Samuel 12:6; Psalms 105:26"> And now then go, and I will send thee unto Pharaoh, and thou shalt bring forth my people the children of Israel out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:18; 2 Corinthians 3:5; Exodus 6:12"> And Moses said unto God, Who am I, that I should go unto Pharaoh, and that I should bring forth the children of Israel out of Egypt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Romans 8:31; Isaiah 41:10"> And he said, Because I will be with thee; and this shall be unto thee the token, that I have sent thee: when thou hast brought forth the people out of Egypt, ye shall serve God upon this mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 30:4; Isaiah 9:6; Jeremiah 23:6"> And Moses said unto God, Behold, if I come unto the children of Israel, and say unto them, The God of your fathers hath sent me unto you; and they then say to me, What is his name? what shall I say unto them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 8:58; Hebrews 13:8; Revelation 1:8"> And God said unto Moses, I will be that I will be: and he said, Thus shalt thou say unto the children of Israel, I will be hath sent me unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 17:7–8; Hebrews 13:8; Malachi 3:6"> And God said moreover unto Moses, Thus shalt thou say unto the children of Israel, The Everlasting One, the God of your fathers, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob, hath sent me unto you: this is my name for ever, and this is my memorial unto all generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 8:4; Exodus 4:31; Exodus 4:29"> Go, and assemble the elders of Israel, and say unto them, The Everlasting One, the God of your fathers, the God of Abraham, of Isaac, and of Jacob, hath appeared unto me, saying, I have surely taken cognizance of you and of that which is done to you in Egypt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 15:13–21; Genesis 50:24; Genesis 46:4"> And I have said, I will bring you up out of the affliction of Egypt, unto the land of the Canaanites, and the Hittites, and the Emorites, and the Perizzites, and the Hivites, and the Jebusites, unto a land flowing with milk and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 9:1; Exodus 7:16; Exodus 10:24–26"> And they will hearken to thy voice: and thou shalt come, thou and the elders of Israel, unto the king of Egypt, and ye shall say unto him, The Everlasting One, the God of the Hebrews hath met with us; and now let us go, we beseech thee, a three days' journey into the wilderness, that we may sacrifice to the Lord our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 5:2; Exodus 6:1; Exodus 7:1–14"> But I am sure that the king of Egypt will not let you go, unless it happen through a mighty hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 7:36; Nehemiah 9:10; Exodus 9:15"> And I will stretch out my hand, and smite Egypt with all my wonders which I will do in the midst thereof; and after that he will let you go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 12:36; Psalms 105:37; Exodus 11:3"> And I will give this people favor in the eyes of the Egyptians; and it shall come to pass, that, when ye go, ye shall not go empty:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:10; Exodus 11:2; Exodus 12:35–36"> But every woman shall ask of her neighbor, and of her that sojourneth in her house, vessels of silver, and vessels of gold, and garments; and ye shall put them upon your sons, and upon your daughters; and ye shall empty out Egypt.</span>
