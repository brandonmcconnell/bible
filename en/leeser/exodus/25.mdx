<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spoke unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Nehemiah 11:2; Ezra 2:68"> Speak unto the children of Israel, that they may bring me an offering; from every man whose heart prompteth him thereto shall ye take my offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:9; Job 28:2"> And this is the offering which ye shall take from them: gold, and silver, and copper,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 19:8; Genesis 41:42; Ezekiel 16:10"> And blue, and purple, and scarlet yarn, and linen thread, and goats' hair,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 26:37–1; Exodus 26:14–15; Exodus 36:20"> And rams' skins died red, and badgers' skins, and shittim wood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 27:20; Exodus 25:37; Exodus 40:24–25"> Oil for lighting, spices for the anointing oil, and for the incense of spices,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Exodus 28:6–30"> Onyx stones, and stones for setting, for the ephod, and for the breastplate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Kings 6:13; Revelation 21:3"> And they shall make me a sanctuary; and I will dwell in the midst of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Hebrews 8:5; Acts 7:44"> In accordance with all that I show thee, the pattern of the tabernacle, and the pattern of all the instruments thereof, even so shall ye make it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 9:4; Exodus 37:1–3; Deuteronomy 10:1–3"> And they shall make an ark of shittim wood: two cubits and a half shall be its length, and a cubit and a half its breadth, and a cubit and a half its height.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 25:24; Exodus 30:3; 2 Chronicles 3:4"> And thou shalt overlay it with pure gold, within and without shalt thou overlay it; and thou shalt make upon it a crown of gold round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 27:7; Exodus 25:26; Exodus 25:15"> And thou shalt cast for it four rings of gold, and put them on the four corners thereof; namely, two rings shall be on the one side of it, and two rings on the other side of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:15; Numbers 4:11; Exodus 27:6"> And thou shalt make staves of shittim wood, and overlay them with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And thou shalt place the staves into the rings, upon the sides of the ark, that the ark may be borne with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 8:8; 2 Chronicles 5:9"> In the rings of the ark shall the staves remain: they shall not be removed therefrom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:26; 1 Kings 8:9; Hebrews 9:4"> And thou shalt put into the ark the testimony which I will give unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 9:5; Exodus 37:6; Hebrews 4:16"> And thou shalt make a cover of pure gold; two cubits and a half shall be its length, and a cubit and a half its breadth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:20; Hebrews 9:5; 1 Kings 8:6–7"> And thou shalt make two cherubim of gold, of beaten work shalt thou make them, on the two ends of the cover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And make one cherub on the one end, and the other cherub on the other end; from the cover itself shall ye make the cherubim on the two ends thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:18; 1 Kings 8:7; Hebrews 9:5"> And the cherubim shall be spreading forth their wings on high, overshadowing the cover with their wings, with their faces turned one to the other; toward the cover shall the faces of the cherubim be directed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 26:34; Romans 10:4; Exodus 25:16–17"> And thou shalt put the cover above upon the ark; and in the ark shalt thou put the testimony which I will give unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 29:42–43; Numbers 7:89; Isaiah 37:16"> And I will meet with thee there, and I will speak with thee from above the cover, from between the two cherubim which are upon the ark of the testimony, all that which I will command thee unto the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 9:2; Exodus 37:10–16; 1 Kings 7:48"> Thou shalt also make a table of shittim wood; two cubits shall be its length, and a cubit its breadth, and a cubit and a half its height.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 25:11; 1 Kings 6:20–22"> And thou shalt overlay it with pure gold, and make thereto a crown of gold round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 37:2; Exodus 30:3"> And thou shalt make unto it a rim of a hand's breadth round about; and thou shalt make a golden crown on its rim round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And thou shalt make for it four rings of gold, and thou shalt put the rings on the four corners that are on its four feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 25:14; Exodus 25:28"> Close under the rim shall the rings be; as receptacles for the staves, to bear the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 25:14; Acts 9:15; Numbers 10:17"> And thou shalt make the staves of shittim wood, and overlay them with gold; and the table shall be borne with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 4:7; Exodus 37:16; Revelation 3:20"> And thou shalt make its dishes, and its spoons, and its supporters, and its purifying tubes, wherewith &#91;the bread&#93; is to be covered: of pure gold shalt thou make them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 24:5–9; Matthew 12:4; Exodus 39:36"> And thou shalt set upon the table show-bread before me always.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 7:49; Zechariah 4:2; Exodus 37:17–24"> And thou shalt make a candlestick of pure gold: of beaten work shall the candlestick be made; its shaft, and its branches, its bowls, its knobs, and its flowers, shall be out of one piece with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And six branches shall come out of its sides; three branches of the candlestick out of the one side, and three branches of the candlestick out of the other side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 17:4–8; Jeremiah 1:11–12; Zechariah 4:3"> Three bowls, almond-shaped, shall be on one branch, with a knob and a flower; and three bowls, almond-shaped, on the other branch, with a knob and a flower: so on the six branches that come out of the candlestick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And on the candlestick itself shall be four bowls, almond-shaped, &#91;with&#93; its knobs and its flowers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And there shall be a knob under the two branches that come out of the same, and a knob under the two branches that come out of the same, and a knob under the two branches that come out of the same; for the six branches that proceed out of the candlestick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 25:18; 1 Kings 10:16–17; 2 Chronicles 9:15"> Their knobs and their branches shall be out of one piece with it; all of it shall be one piece of beaten work of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 8:2; Revelation 1:20–1; Leviticus 24:2–4"> And thou shalt make its seven lamps; and when they light its lamps, it shall give light toward the body of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Kings 12:13; Jeremiah 52:18; 2 Kings 25:14"> And its tongs, and its snuff-dishes shall be of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Out of a talent of pure gold shall he make it, with all these vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Hebrews 8:5; Exodus 26:30; Acts 7:44"> And look that thou make them after their pattern, which thou wast shown on the mount.</span>
