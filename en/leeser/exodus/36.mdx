<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 28:3; Exodus 35:30–35; Exodus 31:1–6"> And Bezalel and Aholiab, and every wise-hearted man, in whom the Lord hath put wisdom and understanding to know how to do every manner of work for the service of the sanctuary, shall make all, just as the Lord hath commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 4:17; Hebrews 5:4; Exodus 31:6"> And Moses called for Bezalel and Aholiab, and every wise-hearted man in whose heart the Lord had put wisdom, every one whose heart stirred him up to come near unto the work to do it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:29; Exodus 35:27; Exodus 35:5–21"> And they received from Moses the whole of the offering, which the children of Israel had brought for the work of the service of the sanctuary, to make it; and these brought unto him yet more free-will offerings morning after morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:10; Luke 12:42; Matthew 24:45"> And then came all the wise men, that wrought all the work of the sanctuary, every man from his own work which they were doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:6–10; 2 Corinthians 8:2–3; 2 Chronicles 24:14"> And they said unto Moses, thus, The people bring more than is required for the service of the work, which the Lord hath commanded to make.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And Moses gave the command, and they caused it to be proclaimed throughout the camp, saying, Let neither man nor woman do any more work for the offering of the sanctuary: so the people were restrained from bringing &#91;more&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:10"> And the stuff prepared was sufficient for all the work to make it, and there was some over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 6:23; Exodus 26:1–37; Ezekiel 1:5–28"> And all the wise-hearted men, among those who wrought the work, made the tabernacle of ten curtains; of twisted linen thread, and blue, and purple, and scarlet yarn; with cherubim, of weaver's work, made he them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The length of each curtain was twenty-eight cubits, and the breadth of each curtain four cubits: there was one measure for all the curtains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:27; Exodus 26:3; Psalms 122:3"> And he coupled together five of the curtains one to another: and the other five curtains he coupled one to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 26:4"> And he made loops of blue on the edge of the one curtain, which was the outside in the coupling: the like he made on the border of the curtain, which was the outmost on the second coupling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 26:5; Exodus 26:10"> Fifty loops made he on the one curtain, and fifty loops made he on the edge of the curtain which was in the second coupling: the loops were fixed opposite to each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4–5; Ephesians 2:20–22; 1 Corinthians 12:20"> And he made fifty hooks of gold; and he coupled the curtains together one unto the other with the hooks, and the tabernacle became thus one piece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 26:7–13"> And he made curtains of goats' hair for a tent over the tabernacle; eleven curtains made he the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The length of each curtain was thirty cubits, and four cubits was the breadth of each curtain: there was one measure for the eleven curtains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And he coupled five of the curtains by themselves, and six of the curtains by themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And he made fifty loops on the edge of the curtain that was the outmost in the coupling, and fifty loops made he on the edge of the curtain of the second coupling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And he made fifty hooks of copper, to couple the tent together that it might be one piece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 26:14"> And he made a covering for the tent of rams' skins dyed red, and a covering of badgers' skins above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 25:5; Numbers 25:1; Exodus 40:18–19"> And he made the boards for the tabernacle, of shittim wood, standing up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Ten cubits was the length of each board, and one cubit and a half was the breadth of each one board.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> There were two tenons for every board, fitted in, one against the other: the like made he for all the boards of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And he made the boards for the tabernacle: twenty boards for the south side, on the right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And forty sockets of silver made he under the twenty boards; two sockets under the one board for its two tenons, and two sockets under the other board for its two tenons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And for the other side of the tabernacle, for the north side, he made twenty boards:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And their forty sockets of silver; two sockets under the one board, and two sockets under the other board.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 26:22; Exodus 26:27"> And for the back wall of the tabernacle, westward, he made six boards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And two boards made he for the corners of the tabernacle in the back wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 4:32; Ephesians 3:18–19; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> And they were closely fitting beneath, and they were closely joined together on the top, by means of one ring; thus he did to both of them, for both the corners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 26:25"> And so there were eight boards, and their sockets of silver, sixteen sockets, two sockets under every board.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 26:26–29; Exodus 25:28; Exodus 30:5"> And he made bars of shittim wood; five, for the boards of the one side of the tabernacle;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 26:26"> And five bars for the boards of the other side of the tabernacle, and five bars for the boards of the tabernacle for the back wall, westward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And he made the middle bar to pass through the midst of the boards from the one end to the other end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And the boards he overlaid with gold, and their rings he made of gold, as receptacles for the bars, and he overlaid the bars with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; Hebrews 10:20; Exodus 40:21"> And he made the vail of blue, and purple, and scarlet yarn, and twisted linen; of weaver's work made he it, with cherubim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:18"> And he made thereunto four pillars of shittim wood, and overlaid them with gold, their hooks also were of gold; and he cast for them four sockets of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 26:36–37; Exodus 40:28"> And he made a hanging for the door of the tabernacle, of blue, and purple, and scarlet yarn, and twisted linen; the work of the embroiderer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 27:10"> And its five pillars with their hooks: and he overlaid their tops and made their fillets with gold; and their five sockets were of copper.</span>
