<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 27:1–8; Ezekiel 43:13–17; Exodus 40:6"> And he made the altar of burnt-offering of shittim wood: five cubits was its length, and five cubits its breadth; it was foursquare, and three cubits was its height.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 27:2; Job 6:12"> And he made its horns on its four corners, from itself were its horns; and he overlaid it with copper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 27:3; 1 Samuel 2:13"> And he made all the vessels of the altar, the pots, and the shovels, and the basins, and the forks, and the fire-pans: all its vessels made he of copper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And he made for the altar a grating, a network of copper, under its compass beneath, even unto the half of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 27:4"> And he cast four rings on the four corners of the grating of copper, as receptacles for the staves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:3; Exodus 25:6"> And he made the staves of shittim wood, and overlaid them with copper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Corinthians 2:2; 1 Corinthians 1:24"> And he put the staves into the rings on the sides of the altar, to bear it by means of them; hollow, of boards, made he it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:22; Luke 2:37; Exodus 40:7"> And he made the laver of copper, and its foot of copper, of the mirrors of the assembled women who had assembled in troops at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 27:9–19; Psalms 89:7; 1 Kings 6:36"> And he made the court: on the south side, on the right, the hangings of the court were of twisted linen, of one hundred cubits;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Their pillars were twenty, with their twenty sockets of copper; the hooks of the pillars and their fillets were of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And for the north side one hundred cubits; their pillars were twenty, with their twenty sockets of copper; the hooks of the pillars and their fillets were of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And for the west side were hangings of fifty cubits; their pillars were ten, and their sockets ten; the hooks of the pillars and their fillets were of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And for the front side, eastward, fifty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:14"> Hangings of fifteen cubits, were on the one wing: their pillars were three, and their sockets three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And for the other wing, on both sides of the gate of the court, were hangings of fifteen cubits; their pillars were three, and their sockets three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> all the hangings of the court round about were of twisted linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the sockets for the pillars were of copper; the hooks of the pillars and their fillets, of silver; and the overlaying of their tops was of silver; and all the pillars of the court were filleted with silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:14"> And the hanging for the gate of the court was the work of the embroiderer, of blue, and purple, and scarlet yarn, and twisted linen; and twenty cubits was the length, and the height, in the breadth, was five cubits, answering to the hangings of the court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And the pillars for the same were four, with their four sockets of copper; their hooks were of silver, and the overlaying of their tops and their fillets, of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:9; Exodus 27:19; Ephesians 2:21–22"> And all the pins of the tabernacle, and of the court round about were of copper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 7:44; Numbers 9:15; 2 Chronicles 24:6"> These are the accounts &#91;of the articles furnished&#93; for the tabernacle, even of the tabernacle of the testimony, which were counted, according to the order of Moses, the service of the Levites, by the hand of Ithamar, the son of Aaron, the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 35:30–3; Exodus 31:1–5; Psalms 119:6"> And Bezalel the son of Uri, the son of Chur, of the tribe of Judah, made all that the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 35:34; Exodus 31:6"> And with him was Aholiab the son of Achissamach, of the tribe of Dan, an engraver, and a skilful weaver, and an embroiderer in blue, and in purple, and in scarlet yarn, and in linen thread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 18:16; Numbers 3:47; Leviticus 27:25"> All the gold that was applied to the work in all the work of the sanctuary, to wit, the gold of the offering, was twenty and nine talents, and seven hundred and thirty shekels, after the shekel of the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 30:12"> And the silver of those that were numbered of the congregation was one hundred talents, and a thousand seven hundred and seventy and five shekels, after the shekel of the sanctuary:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 1:46; Exodus 30:13; Exodus 12:37"> A bekah for every head, that is, half a shekel, after the shekel of the sanctuary, for every one that went to be numbered, from twenty years old and upward, for six hundred thousand and three thousand and five hundred and fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 26:19; Exodus 26:25; Exodus 26:21"> And the hundred talents of silver served to cast the sockets of the sanctuary, and the sockets of the vail: one hundred sockets to the hundred talents, a talent for every socket.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 27:17"> And of the thousand seven hundred and seventy and five shekels he made hooks for the pillars, and overlaid their tops and filleted them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And the copper of the offering was seventy talents, and two thousand and four hundred shekels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 26:37; Exodus 27:17; Exodus 27:10"> And he made therewith the sockets of the door of the tabernacle of the congregation, and the altar of copper, and the grating of copper for it, and all the vessels of the altar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And the sockets of the court round about, and the socket of the court gate, and all the pins of the tabernacle, and all the pins of the court round about.</span>
