<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:14; Deuteronomy 6:20; Exodus 24:3–4"> And these are the laws of justice which thou shalt set before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:12–15; Exodus 12:44; Deuteronomy 15:18"> If thou buy a Hebrew servant, six years shall he serve; and in the seventh he shall go out free for nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:12–14"> If he came in by himself, he shall go out by himself; if he was the husband of a woman, then shall his wife go out with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> If his master should give him a wife, and she bear him sons or daughters: the wife and her children shall belong to her master, and he shall go out by himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:14–15; Deuteronomy 15:16–17; Isaiah 26:13"> And if the servant should plainly say, I love my master, my wife, and my children; I will not go out free:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:17; Leviticus 25:40; Deuteronomy 19:17–18"> Then shall his master bring him unto the judges, and he shall bring him to the door, or unto the door-post; and his master shall bore his ear through with an awl; and he shall serve him till the jubilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:5; Exodus 21:2–3"> And if a man sell his daughter for a maid-servant, she shall not go out as the men-servants go out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 28:8; Deuteronomy 20:7; Malachi 2:11–15"> If she please not her master, to whom he hath assigned her, then shall he aid her to be redeemed: unto a strange nation he shall have no power to sell her, seeing he hath dealt faithlessly with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And if he should assign her unto his son, then shall he do unto her after the right of the daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:1–6"> If he take himself another wife, her food, her raiment, and her duty of marriage, shall he not diminish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And if he do not these three things unto her, then shall she go out free, without money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 9:6; Matthew 26:52; Leviticus 24:17"> He that smiteth a man, so that he die, shall surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 20:2–9; 1 Samuel 24:18; Deuteronomy 4:41–43"> And if he did not lie in wait, but God let it come into his hand, then will I appoint thee a place whither he shall flee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 2:28–34; Hebrews 10:26; Numbers 15:30–31"> But if a man come presumptuously upon his neighbor, to slay him with guile, from my altar shalt thou take him, that he may die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:18–21; Proverbs 30:17; 1 Timothy 1:9"> And he that smiteth his father, or his mother, shall surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:7; 1 Timothy 1:10; Exodus 22:4"> And he that stealeth a man, and selleth him, and he be found in his hand, shall surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 20:20; Mark 7:10–11; Deuteronomy 27:16"> And he that curseth his father, or his mother, shall surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 21:20; Deuteronomy 25:11; 2 Samuel 14:6"> And if men strive together, and one smite the other with a stone, or with the fist, and he die not, but keepeth his bed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:29; Zechariah 8:4"> If he rise again, and walk abroad upon his crutch, then shall he that smote him be quit; only he shall pay for the loss of his time, and shall cause him to be thoroughly healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 29:19; Deuteronomy 19:21; Numbers 35:19"> And if a man smite his servant or his maid, with a rod, and he die under his hand, it shall be surely avenged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 25:44–46"> Nevertheless, if he continue alive a day or two, it shall not be avenged; for he is his money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:18–19; Exodus 21:30; Deuteronomy 16:18"> If men strive, and hurt a woman with child, so that her children depart from her, and yet no farther mischief follow: he shall be surely punished, &#91;with a fine,&#93; according as the husband of the woman will lay upon him; and he shall pay this by the decision of the judges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:21; Numbers 35:31; Leviticus 24:19"> And if any mischief follow, then shalt thou give life for life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:21; Matthew 5:38–40; Matthew 7:2"> Eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Burning for burning, wound for wound, bruise for bruise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 21:20; Job 31:13–15; Psalms 72:12–14"> And if a man smite the eye of his servant, or the eye of his maid, that it perish, he shall let him go free for the sake of his eye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And if he strike out his man-servant's tooth, or his maid-servant's tooth, he shall let him go free for the sake of his tooth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 21:32; Leviticus 20:15–16; Genesis 9:5–6"> If an ox gore a man or a woman, that he die: then shall the ox be surely stoned, and his flesh shall not be eaten; but the owner of the ox shall be quit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:1–9"> But if the ox were wont to gore in time past, and warning have been given to his owner, and he hath not kept him in, and he killeth a man or a woman: the ox shall be stoned, and his owner also should of right be put to death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 21:22; Exodus 30:12; Numbers 35:31–33"> But there shall be laid on him a sum of money in atonement, and he shall give the ransom of his life whatsoever may be laid upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> If he gore a son, or gore a daughter, according to this judgment shall be done unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 26:15; Zechariah 11:12–13; Matthew 27:3–9"> If the ox gore a man-servant or a maid-servant, thirty shekels of silver shall he give to his master, and the ox shall be stoned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 28:10; Jeremiah 18:20; Ecclesiastes 10:8"> And if a man open a pit, or if a man dig a pit, and do not cover it, and an ox or an ass fall therein:</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 22:6; Exodus 21:29–30; Exodus 22:14"> The owner of the pit shall make it good, he shall make restitution in money unto the owner thereof; and the dead beast shall be his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And if one man's ox hurt the ox of another, that he die: then shall they sell the live ox, and divide his money; and the dead ox also they shall divide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> But if it be known that the ox was wont to gore in time past, and his owner hath not kept him in: he shall surely pay ox for ox; and the dead shall belong to him.</span>
