<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 34:32; Exodus 31:1–11; Romans 2:13"> And Moses gathered together all the congregation of the children of Israel, and said unto them, These are the things which the Lord hath commanded, that ye should do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 23:3; Luke 13:14–15; Exodus 20:9–10"> Six days shall work be done, but on the seventh day there shall be to you a holy day, a sabbath of rest to the Lord: whosoever doth work thereon shall be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 16:23; Numbers 15:32–36; Exodus 12:16"> Ye shall not kindle any fire throughout your habitations upon the sabbath day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 25:1–2"> And Moses said unto all the congregation of the children of Israel, as followeth, This is the thing which the Lord hath commanded, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Psalms 110:3; Exodus 25:2–7"> Take ye from among you an offering unto the Lord; whosoever is of a willing heart, let him bring it, an offering of the Lord: Gold, and silver, and copper,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 26:7–14; Exodus 26:31; Exodus 28:15"> And blue, and purple, and scarlet yarn, and linen thread, and goats' hair,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And rams' skills dyed red, and badgers' skins, and shittim wood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 27:20; Exodus 25:1–40; Exodus 30:23"> And oil for the lighting, and spices, for the anointing oil, and for the incense of spices,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 39:6–14; Exodus 28:9; Exodus 25:5"> And onyx stones, and stones for setting, for the ephod, and for the breastplate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 31:1–6; Exodus 36:1–4"> And all the wise-hearted among you shall come, and make all that which the Lord hath commanded:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 26:1–37; Exodus 36:8–34; Exodus 31:7–9"> The tabernacle, its tent, and its covering, its hooks, and its boards, its bars, its pillars, and its sockets;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 25:10–22; Exodus 26:31–33; Exodus 37:1–9"> The ark, and its staves, &#91;with&#93; the mercy-seat, and the vail of the separation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 25:23–30; Leviticus 24:5–6; Exodus 37:10–16"> The table, and its staves, and all its vessels, and the showbread;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–39; Matthew 5:14–15; Exodus 37:17–24"> And the candlestick for the lighting, and its vessel, and its lamps, with the oil for the lighting;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 30:1–10; Exodus 30:22–38; Exodus 37:25–28"> And the altar of incense, and its staves, and the anointing oil, and the incense of spices, and the hanging for the door at the entrance of the tabernacle;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 27:1–8; Exodus 30:18–21; Exodus 38:1–8"> The altar of burnt-offering, with its grating of copper, its staves, and all its vessels, the laver and its foot;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:2; Exodus 38:9–20; Exodus 27:9–19"> The hangings of the court, its pillars, and its sockets, and the hanging for the door of the court;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 27:19"> The pins of the tabernacle, and the pins of the court, and their cords;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 31:10; Numbers 4:5–15; Exodus 28:1–43"> The cloths of service, to do service therewith in the holy place, the holy garments for Aaron the priest, and the garments of his sons, to minister in as priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And all the congregation of the children of Israel departed from the presence of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 25:2; Exodus 35:26; 1 Chronicles 29:14"> And they came, every man whose heart stirred him up; and every one whom his spirit made willing, brought the Lord's offering for the work of the tabernacle of the congregation, and for all its service, and for the holy garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:70–72; Matthew 2:11; Ezra 2:68–69"> And they came, the men with the women; whoever was willing-hearted, brought bracelets, and earrings, and finger-rings, and tablets, all kinds of ornaments of gold, and every man that offered an offering of gold unto the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:8; Exodus 25:2–7; Exodus 35:6–10"> And every man, with whom was found blue, and purple, and scarlet yarn, and linen thread, and goats' hair, and rams' skins dyed red, and badgers' skins, brought them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12"> Every one that did offer an offering of silver and copper brought it as the Lord's offering; and every one with whom was found shittim wood for any work of the service, brought it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 16:12; Luke 8:2–3; Proverbs 14:1"> And all the women that were wise-hearted spun with their hands, and they brought that which they had spun, of the blue, and of the purple, and of the scarlet yarn, and of the linen thread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 35:21; Exodus 36:8; Exodus 35:29"> And all the women whose heart stirred them up in wisdom spun the goats' hair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezra 2:68; 1 Chronicles 29:6; Exodus 35:9"> And the princes brought the onyx stones, and the stones for setting, for the ephod, and for the breastplate;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 30:23–38; Exodus 35:8"> And the spice and the oil, for lighting, and for the anointing oil, and for the incense of spices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Exodus 35:21–22; Exodus 35:4"> Every man and woman, whose heart made them willing to bring for all manner of work, which the Lord had commanded to be made, by the hand of Moses, even that brought the children of Israel as a free-will offering unto the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 31:1–6; James 1:17; 1 Corinthians 12:11"> And Moses said unto the children of Israel, See, the Lord hath called by name Bezalel the son of Uri, the son of Chur, of the tribe of Judah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 1:17; Isaiah 11:2–5; Isaiah 61:1–3"> And he hath filled him with the spirit of God, in wisdom, in understanding, and in knowledge, and in all manner of workmanship;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And to devise works of art, to work in gold, and in silver, and in copper,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And in the cutting of stones, to set them, and in the carving of wood, to make any manner of work of art.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 31:6; 2 Chronicles 2:14; Ezra 7:27"> And to teach hath he put in his heart, both to him, and to Aholiab, the son of Achissamach, of the tribe of Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Exodus 35:31; 1 Kings 7:14; Isaiah 28:26"> He hath filled them with wisdom of heart, to execute all manner of work, of the engraver, and of the designing weaver, and of the embroiderer, in blue, and in purple, in scarlet yarn, and in linen thread, and of the weaver, of those that do every species of work, and of those that devise works of art.</span>
