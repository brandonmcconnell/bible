<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 18:21–22; Daniel 5:31; Esther 1:1"> (6:2) Darius deemed it proper, and he set over the kingdom one hundred and twenty lieutenants, who should be over all the kingdom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Daniel 2:48–49; Daniel 5:29; Esther 7:4"> (6:3) And over these, three presidents, of whom Daniel was one; that these lieutenants should give accounts unto them, so that the king might suffer no damage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 5:14; Proverbs 22:29; Genesis 41:38–41"> (6:4) Then did this Daniel excel &#91;all&#93; the presidents and lieutenants, because a superior spirit was in him: and the king thought to appoint him over the whole kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 3:16; Philippians 2:15; 1 Peter 2:12"> (6:5) Then sought the presidents and lieutenants to find a pretext against Daniel on account of the management of the kingdom; but they were not able to find any pretext or fault, forasmuch as he was faithful, and no kind of error or fault was to be found on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 24:13–16; Esther 3:8; Acts 24:20–21"> (6:6) Then said these men, We shall not find any pretext against this Daniel, except we find it against him in the law of his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 2:4; Nehemiah 2:3; Daniel 6:21"> (6:7) Then came these presidents and lieutenants tumultuously to the king, and thus said they unto him, King Darius, live for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 59:3; Psalms 62:4; Daniel 3:27"> (6:8) All the presidents of the kingdom, the superintendents, and the lieutenants, the counsellors, and the governors, have consulted together to establish a royal statute, and to make a firm prohibition, that whosoever will ask any thing by prayer of any God or man within thirty days, save of thee, O king, shall be cast into the den of lions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 6:15; Esther 1:19; Daniel 6:12"> (6:9) Now, O king, establish the prohibition, and sign the writing, that it cannot be changed, according to the law of the Medes and Persians, which is not to be repealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 146:3; Psalms 118:9; Psalms 62:9–10"> (6:10) In view of this king Darius signed the writing and the prohibition.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Psalms 55:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:17–18"> (6:11) Now when Daniel knew that the writing was signed, he went up unto his house, where he had open windows in his upper chamber in the direction of Jerusalem; and three times every day he kneeled upon his knees, and prayed, and offered thanks before his God, as he had been doing before that time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 37:32–33; Psalms 10:9; Daniel 6:6"> (6:12) Then came in these men tumultuously, and found Daniel praying and making supplication before his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 6:8; Daniel 3:8–12; Esther 1:19"> (6:13) Then came they near, and spoke before the king concerning the king's prohibition, Hast thou not signed a prohibition, that every man that will pray &#91;for aught&#93; of any God or man, within thirty days, save of thee, O king, shall be cast into the den of lions? The king answered and said, The thing is certainly so, according to the law of the Medes and Persians, which cannot he repealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 3:12; Acts 5:29; Daniel 2:25"> (6:14) Then answered they and said before the king, That Daniel, who is of the children of the exiles of Judah, hath paid no regard to thee, O king, nor to the prohibition which thou hast signed; but three times every day he offereth up his prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 6:26; Matthew 27:17–24; 2 Samuel 3:28–29"> (6:15) Then the king, when he heard this matter, felt very much distressed within himself, and on account of Daniel he sought an excuse to deliver him; and till the going down of the sun he strove hard to rescue him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 6:12; Psalms 94:20–21; Daniel 6:8"> (6:16) Then came these men tumultuously unto the king, and said unto the king, Know, O king, that it is the law of the Medes and Persians. That every prohibition and statute which the king hath established is not to be changed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 37:39–40; Job 5:19; Psalms 91:14–16"> (6:17) Then gave the king the order, and they brought Daniel, and cast him into the den of lions. The king commenced and said unto Daniel, May thy God whom thou worshippest continually, truly deliver thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Lamentations 3:53; Acts 12:4; Matthew 27:60–66"> (6:18) And a stone was brought, and placed upon the mouth of the den; and the king sealed it with his own signet-ring, and with the signet-ring of his lords, that nothing should be changed in the purpose concerning Daniel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Esther 6:1; Daniel 2:1; 2 Samuel 12:16–17"> (6:19) Then went the king to his palace, and passed the night fasting: and no food was brought before him; and his sleep fled from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:5; Mark 16:2; Matthew 28:1"> (6:20) Then arose the king by the morning-dawn, as soon as it was light, and went in great haste unto the den of lions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:11; Hebrews 7:25; Daniel 6:27"> (6:21) And when he came near to the den, he cried with a mournful voice unto Daniel: the king commenced and said to Daniel, O Daniel, servant of the living God, hath thy God, whom thou worshippest continually, been able to deliver thee from the lions!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 2:4; Daniel 6:6; Nehemiah 2:3"> (6:22) Then spoke Daniel with the king, O king, live for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:17; Hebrews 11:33; Acts 12:11"> (6:23) My God sent his angel, and locked up the mouths of the lions, and they have not hurt me; forasmuch as before him innocency was found in me; and also before thee, O king, had I done nothing injurious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:20; Mark 9:23; Daniel 3:27–28"> (6:24) Then was the king exceedingly glad within himself, and concerning Daniel he ordered to bring him up out of the den. So was Daniel brought up out of the den, and no manner of hurt was found upon him, because he had trusted in his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 38:13; Esther 7:10; Deuteronomy 24:16"> (6:25) And the king gave the order, and they brought those men who had accused Daniel treacherously, and they cast into the den of lions them, their children, and their wives; and they had not yet touched the bottom of the den when the lions had the mastery over them, and ground up all their bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 4:1; Ezra 1:1–2; Esther 3:12"> (6:26) Then wrote king Darius unto all people, nations, and languages, that dwell on all the earth, May your welfare increase.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Daniel 4:34; Luke 1:33; Daniel 3:29"> (6:27) From me is it decreed, That in all the dominion of my kingdom men shall tremble and have fear before the God of Daniel; for he is the living God, and endureth for ever, and it is his kingdom which will not be destroyed, and his dominion will be unto the end &#91;of things&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Daniel 4:2–3; Psalms 18:50; Acts 4:30"> (6:28) He delivereth and rescueth, and he displayeth sings and wonders in heaven and on earth, he who hath delivered Daniel from the power of the lions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Daniel 1:21; 2 Chronicles 36:22–2; Isaiah 44:28–1"> (6:29) So this Daniel prospered in the reign of Darius, and in the reign of Cyrus the Persian.</span>
