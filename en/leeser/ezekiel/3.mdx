<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 10:9–10; Ezekiel 3:17–21; Ezekiel 2:8–9"> And he said unto me, Son of man, eat what thou findest: eat this roll, and go, speak unto the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:17; Acts 26:19"> So I opened my mouth, and he caused me to eat this roll.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:16; Revelation 10:9–10; Psalms 119:103"> And he said unto me, Son of man, feed thy belly, and fill thy bowels with this roll that I am giving unto thee. And I ate it; and it was in my mouth like honey in sweetness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:5–6; Acts 1:8; Ezekiel 2:3"> And he said unto me, Son of man, go, get thee unto the house of Israel, and speak with my words unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jonah 1:2; Isaiah 33:19; Jonah 3:2–4"> For not to a people of an obscure speech and of a difficult tongue art thou sent, but to the house of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 11:20–24; Romans 9:30–33; Jonah 3:5–10"> Not to many people of an obscure speech and of a difficult tongue, whose words thou canst not understand. Surely, had I sent thee to them, they would truly have hearkened unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 10:16; Ezekiel 2:4; 1 Samuel 8:7"> But the house of Israel will not hearken unto thee; for they will not hearken unto me; for all the house of Israel have a bold forehead, and a hard heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:18; Acts 7:51–56; Micah 3:8"> Behold, I have made thy face strong against their face, and thy forehead strong against their forehead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:6; Jeremiah 1:8; Isaiah 50:7"> As an adamant harder than flint have I made thy forehead: thou shalt not fear them, nor shalt thou be dismayed at their presence, though they be a rebellious family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; Job 22:22; Ezekiel 3:1–3"> And he said unto me, Son of man, all my words that I will speak unto thee receive in thy heart, and hear &#91;them&#93; with thy ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:7; Ezekiel 3:27; Ezekiel 33:30"> And go, get thee to those in exile, unto the children of thy people, and speak unto them, and say unto them, Thus hath said the Lord Eternal: whether they will hear, or whether they will forbear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:3; Acts 8:39; Ezekiel 11:1"> Then a spirit took me up, and I heard behind me a voice of a great rushing, &#91;saying,&#93; Blessed be the glory of the Lord from his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:24; Ezekiel 10:16–17; Ezekiel 10:5"> &#91;I heard&#93; also the sound of the wings of the living creatures that touched one another, and the sound of the wheels at the same time with them, and the sound of a great rushing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:1; Jeremiah 20:7–9; 2 Kings 3:15"> So a spirit lifted me up, and took me away, and I walked in bitterness, in the heat of my spirit, and the inspiration of the Lord was strong upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 137:1; Job 2:13; Genesis 50:10"> Then came I to the exiles at Tel-abib, who dwelt by the river Kebar, and I remained where they dwelt, and I remained there in a state of confusion among them seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:7"> And it came to pass at the end of seven days, That the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 52:8; Hebrews 13:17; 2 Chronicles 19:10"> Son of man, I have made thee a watchman unto the house of Israel: and thou shalt hear the word out of my mouth, and give them warning from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:6; James 5:19–20; Ezekiel 3:20"> When I say unto the wicked, Thou shalt surely die; and thou dost not give him warning, and speakest not to warn the wicked from his wicked way, to save his life: the same wicked man shall die through his iniquity; but his blood will I require at thy hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:9; Ezekiel 14:20; 1 Timothy 4:16"> But thou,—if thou hast warned the wicked, and he turn not from his wickedness, nor from his wicked way: he shall indeed die in his iniquity; but thou hast surely delivered thy soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:24; Ezekiel 18:26; Ezekiel 33:12–13"> Again, When a righteous man do turn from his righteousness, and do what is wrong: then will I lay a stumbling-block before him, &#91;so that&#93; he shall die; yet if thou hast not given him warning, he shall die in his sin, and his acts of righteousness which he hath done shall not be remembered; but his blood will I require from thy hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:16; Proverbs 9:9; Titus 2:15"> But if thou hast truly warned him,—the righteous, that the righteous should not sin, and he doth not sin: he shall surely live, because he attended to the warning, and thou hast surely delivered thy own soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 9:6; Ezekiel 37:1; Ezekiel 8:4"> And the inspiration of the Lord came there over me; and he said unto me, Arise, go forth into the valley, and there will I speak with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:28; Acts 7:55; Revelation 1:17"> And I arose, and went forth into the valley; and, behold, there was the glory of the Lord standing, like that glory which I had seen by the river Kebar: and I fell down on my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:2; Daniel 10:19; Daniel 10:8–10"> Then entered a spirit into me, and placed me upright on my feet, and spoke with me, and said unto me, Go, shut thyself up within thy house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:8; Acts 9:16; Mark 3:21"> But thou, O son of man, behold, they put ropes upon thee, and bind thee with them, that thou canst not go out among them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:27; Micah 3:6–7; Amos 8:11–12"> And I will let thy tongue cleave to the roof of thy mouth, that thou shalt be dumb, and shalt not become to them a man who reproveth; for they are a rebellious family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:2–3; Ezekiel 24:27; Ezekiel 3:11"> But when I speak with thee, I will open thy mouth, and thou shalt say unto them, Thus hath said the Lord Eternal, He that heareth, let him hear; and he that forbeareth, let him forebear; for they are a rebellious family.</span>
