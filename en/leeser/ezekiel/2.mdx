<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:33; Ezekiel 3:27; Matthew 10:12–15"> And they, whether they will hear, or whether they will forbear, &#91;for they are a rebellious family,&#93; shall yet know that a prophet hath been among them.</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:28; Ezekiel 3:17; Jeremiah 1:7"> And thou shalt speak my words unto them, whether they will hear, or whether they will forbear; for they are rebellious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 10:9; Jeremiah 15:16; Isaiah 50:5"> But thou, son of man, hear what I am speaking unto thee, Be not thou rebellious like this rebellious family: open thy mouth, and eat what I give unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:3; Revelation 5:1–5; Daniel 10:10"> And then I looked, and behold, a hand was stretched out toward me; and, lo, a roll-book was therein;</span>
