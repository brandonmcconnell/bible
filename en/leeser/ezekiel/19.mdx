<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:2; Ezekiel 26:17; 2 Kings 24:6"> And thou,—do thou lift up a lamentation for the princes of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 11:3; Isaiah 5:29; Job 4:11"> And say, What a noble lioness was thy mother! among lions did she lie down, in the midst of young lions did she raise her whelps!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:25; 2 Kings 23:31–32; 2 Chronicles 36:1–2"> And she brought up one of her whelps: he became a young lion, and he learned to tear in pieces the prey; &#91;even&#93; men he devoured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:4; 2 Kings 23:33–34; 2 Chronicles 36:6"> And when nations heard of him, he was caught in their pit, and they brought him with nose-rings unto the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:3; 2 Kings 23:34–37"> Now when she saw that she had long waited, &#91;that&#93; her hope was lost, she took another one of her whelps, and made him a young lion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:5; 2 Chronicles 36:9; Jeremiah 26:1–24"> And he went up and down in the midst of lions, he became a young lion; and he learned to tear in pieces the prey; even men he devoured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:12; Ezekiel 12:19; Micah 1:2"> And he broke down their palaces, and their cities laid he in ruins: and then was terrified the land, with all that filled it, because of the noise of his roaring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 4:20; Ezekiel 19:4; Ezekiel 12:13"> Then set themselves the nations against him on every side from the provinces; and they spread over him their net: in their pit was he caught.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:6; Ezekiel 6:2; Jeremiah 36:30–31"> And they put him in a cage with nose-rings &#91;in his nose&#93;, and they brought him to the king of Babylon: they brought him into strong-holds, in order that his voice should no more be heard on the mountains of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 80:8–11; Deuteronomy 8:7; Numbers 24:6–7"> Thy mother was like a vine, if I compare thee to aught, planted by the waters: fruitful and full of boughs was she by reason of many waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:3; Psalms 80:15; Daniel 4:11"> And she had strong branches &#91;fit&#93; for the sceptres of rulers, and her stature grew up high between the thick-branched &#91;trees&#93;, and she was seen through her height by means of the multitude of her tendrils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:10; Hosea 13:15; Isaiah 27:11"> But she was plucked up in fury, to the ground was she cast down, and the east wind dried up her fruit: and torn off and dried up were her strong branches, a fire consumed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 2:3; Ezekiel 19:10; Psalms 63:1"> And now is she planted in the wilderness, in a dry and thirsty land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 80:15–16; Ezekiel 19:11; 2 Kings 24:20"> And fire is gone out of a branch of her boughs, and hath devoured her fruit, so that there is no more on her a strong branch for a sceptre to rule. This is a lamentation, and it is become a lamentation.</span>
