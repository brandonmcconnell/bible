<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 10:1; Psalms 80:8–16; Isaiah 5:1–7"> Son of man, What shall become of the wood of the vine more than of any other wood, of the branch which was standing among the trees of the forest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 14:34–35; Jeremiah 24:8; Mark 9:50"> Can wood be taken therefrom to employ it for any work? or will men take from it a pin to hang thereon any vessel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 15:6; Isaiah 27:11; Psalms 80:16"> Behold, if it be given up to the fire to be consumed,—&#91;and&#93; the fire have consumed both its ends, and the middle of it be scorched; will it be fit for any work?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:16"> Behold, when it was yet entire, it could not be employed for any work: how much more &#91;must this be&#93; when the fire hath consumed it, and it is scorched,—and shall it yet be employed for any work?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 15:2; Jeremiah 7:20; Jeremiah 24:8–10"> Therefore thus hath said the Lord Eternal, As the wood of the vine among the trees of the forest, which I have given up to the fire to be consumed: so do I give up the inhabitants of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:8; Psalms 34:16; Isaiah 24:18"> And I will set my face against them: from the fire have they gone forth, yet the fire shall devour them; and ye shall know that I am the Lord, when I set my face against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:14; Zephaniah 1:18; Isaiah 6:11"> And I will render the land a desert; because they have committed a trespass, saith the Lord Eternal.</span>
