<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:1; Jeremiah 3:15; Isaiah 40:11"> Son of man, prophesy concerning the shepherds of Israel; prophesy, and say unto them, unto the shepherds, Thus hath said the Lord Eternal, Woe to the shepherds of Israel who do feed themselves! should not the shepherds feed the flocks?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 11:16; Jeremiah 2:30; Ezekiel 33:25–26"> The fat ye eat, and with the wool ye clothe yourselves, those that are well fed ye slaughter; but the flock ye feed not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 18:12–13; Matthew 9:36; Ezekiel 34:16"> The diseased have ye not strengthened, and the sick have ye not healed, and that which had a limb broken have ye not bound up, and the strayed have ye not brought back again, and what was lost have ye not sought for; but with force have ye ruled them and with rigor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; 2 Chronicles 18:16; Jeremiah 23:2"> And they were scattered for want of a shepherd; and they became food unto all the beasts of the field, and they were scattered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 142:4; 1 Peter 2:25; John 10:16"> My sheep have to wander about on all the mountains, and upon every high hill: yea, over all the face of the land are my flock scattered, and there is none that inquireth and none that seeketh &#91;after them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:2–3; Micah 3:8–9; Psalms 82:1–7"> Therefore, ye shepherds, hear the word of the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Peter 2:13; Acts 20:33; Ezekiel 34:5–6"> As I live, saith the Lord Eternal, surely because my flock became a prey, and my flock became food unto every beast of the field, because there was no shepherd, and my shepherds did not inquire for my flock, but the shepherds fed themselves, and my flock they fed not:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Therefore, O ye shepherds, hear the word of the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2; Zechariah 10:3; Ezekiel 13:8"> Thus hath said the Lord Eternal, Behold, I will be against the shepherds, and I will require my flock from their hand, and I will stop them from feeding the flock; neither shall the shepherds feed themselves any more: and I will deliver my flock out of their mouth, that they may not serve them for food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 10:16; Luke 19:10; Jeremiah 23:3"> For thus hath said the Lord Eternal, Behold, I am here, and I will both inquire for my flocks, and search for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; John 10:11–12; Luke 19:10"> As a shepherd searcheth for his flock on the day that he is among his flocks that are scattered: so will I search for my flocks; and I will deliver them out of all places whither they have been scattered on the day of clouds and &#91;tempestuous&#93; darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:25–26; Ezekiel 11:17; Isaiah 11:11–16"> And I will bring them out from the people, and gather them from the countries, and I will bring them to their own land; and I will feed them upon the mountains of Israel, in the ravines, and in all the inhabited places of the country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 10:9; Jeremiah 31:25; Psalms 31:8–10"> On a good pasture will I feed them, and upon the high mountains of Israel shall be their fold: there shall they lie in a good fold, and in a fat pasture shall they feed on the mountains of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:15; Psalms 23:1–2; John 21:15"> I myself will feed my flock, and I myself will cause them to lie down, saith the Lord Eternal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:10; Ezekiel 34:4; Ezekiel 34:11"> That which was lost will I seek for, and that which was gone astray will I bring back again, and that which had a limb broken will I bind up, and the sick will I strengthen: but the fat and the strong will I destroy; I will feed them in justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:20–22; Matthew 25:32–33; Zechariah 10:3"> And as for you, O my flock, thus hath said the Lord Eternal, Behold, I will judge between lamb and lamb, between the wethers and the he-goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 23:13; Luke 11:52; Numbers 16:13"> Is it too little for you that ye can feed on the good pasture, that ye must tread down the residue of your pastures with your feet? and that ye can drink clear waters, that ye must render muddy with your feet what is left?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And as for my flock, what ye have trodden with your feet must they eat; and what ye have made muddy with your feet must they drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 25:31–46; Ezekiel 34:10; Psalms 22:12–16"> Therefore thus hath said the Lord Eternal unto them, Behold, I am here, and I will judge between the fat lamb and the lean lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zechariah 11:5; Deuteronomy 33:17; Luke 13:14–16"> Because ye thrust with side and with shoulder, and push with your horns all the diseased, till ye have scattered them abroad:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:2–3; Psalms 72:12–14; Ezekiel 34:10"> Therefore will I save my flock, and they shall no more be a prey; and I will judge between lamb and lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:24–25; Jeremiah 30:9; Isaiah 40:11"> And I will appoint over them one shepherd, and he shall feed them, namely, my servant David: he it is that shall feed them, and he it is that shall be unto them for a shepherd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:33; Ezekiel 36:28; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> And I the Lord—I will be unto them for a God, and my servant David shall be a prince in their midst: I the Lord have spoken it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 11:6–9; Ezekiel 37:26; Leviticus 26:6"> And I will make with them a covenant of peace, and I will cause the wild beasts to cease out of the land: and they shall dwell in the wilderness in safety, and sleep in the forests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:12; Isaiah 44:3; Leviticus 26:4"> And I will make them and the environs of my hill a blessing; and I will cause the rain to come down in its season; rains of blessing shall they be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:8; Leviticus 26:4; Leviticus 26:13"> And the tree of the field shall yield its fruit, and the earth shall yield her products, and they shall be on their land in safety: and they shall know that I am the Lord, when I break the bands of their yoke, and deliver them out of the hand of those that had made them labor for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:27; Jeremiah 30:10; Ezekiel 39:26"> And they shall be no more a prey to the nations, and the beasts of the land shall not devour them; but they shall dwell in safety, with none to make them afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:29; Isaiah 4:2; Ezekiel 34:26–27"> And I will raise up for them a plantation for a &#91;perpetual&#93; renown, and they shall be no more taken away by hunger in the land, neither bear the shameful reproach of the nations any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:27; Ezekiel 34:24; Psalms 46:11"> Thus shall they experience that I the Lord their God am with them, and that they, the house of Israel, are my people, saith the Lord Eternal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 100:3; John 10:16; Psalms 78:52"> And ye my flock, the flock of my pasture, are men, &#91;and&#93; I am your God, saith the Lord Eternal.</span>
