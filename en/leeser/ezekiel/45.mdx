<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:29; Zechariah 14:20–21; Numbers 34:13"> And when ye divide the land by lot for an inheritance, shall ye offer an oblation unto the Lord, as a holy portion of the land, five and twenty thousand rods in length, and in breadth ten thousand. This shall be holy in all its extent round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:16–20; Ezekiel 27:28"> Of this there shall be for the sanctuary five hundred &#91;rods&#93; by five hundred, square round about; and fifty cubits as an open space for it round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:10"> And of this measure shalt thou measure, in length five and twenty thousand, and in breadth ten thousand &#91;rods&#93;: and in it shall be the sanctuary &#91;and&#93; the holy of holies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:19; Numbers 16:5; Ezekiel 40:45"> The holy portion of the land shall it be, for the priests the ministers of the sanctuary shall it be, who come near to minister unto the Lord; and it shall be unto them a place for houses, and a holy place for the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:13; Nehemiah 10:38–39; Ezekiel 48:20"> And five and twenty thousand &#91;rods&#93; in length, and ten thousand in breadth, shall also belong unto the Levites, the servants of the house, for themselves, as a possession, with twenty chambers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:15–18; Ezekiel 48:30–35"> And as the possession of the city shall ye assign five thousand rods broad, and five and twenty thousand long, alongside the holy oblation: unto the whole house of Israel shall it belong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:21; Ezekiel 34:24; Ezekiel 46:16–18"> And the prince shall have that on the one side and on the other side of the holy oblation, and of the possession of the city, in front of the holy oblation, and in front of the possession of the city, on the west side westward, and on the east side eastward; and in length alongside one of the portions, both on the west border and on the east border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 11:3–5; Ezekiel 46:18; Ezekiel 22:27"> As landed property shall it be his possession in Israel: and my princes shall no more wrong my people; but the land shall they give to the house of Israel according to their tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 8:16; Jeremiah 22:3; Ezekiel 44:6"> Thus hath said the Lord Eternal, Ye have done enough wrong, O princes of Israel: remove violence and robbery, and execute justice and righteousness; take away your exactions from my people, saith the Lord Eternal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 6:10–11; Amos 8:4–6; Proverbs 16:11"> Just balances, and a just ephah, and a just bath shall ye have.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 5:10"> The ephah and the bath shall contain the same quantity, that the bath may contain the tenth part of a chomer, and the ephah the tenth part of a chomer: after the chomer shall the measure of contents be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Numbers 3:47; Leviticus 27:25"> And the shekel shall be twenty gerahs: &#91;in pieces of&#93; twenty shekels, five and twenty shekels, fifteen shekels, shall be your maneh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> This is the heave-offering that ye shall offer; The sixth part of an ephah of a chomer of wheat; and ye shall give the sixth part of an ephah of a chomer of barley;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And the fixed portion of oil shall be after the bath of oil, the tenth part of a bath out of the cor, ten baths reckoned to the chomer; for ten baths are a chomer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 1:4; Leviticus 6:30; Colossians 1:21"> And one lamb out of the flock, out of two hundred, out of the fat pastures of Israel, for meat-offerings, and for burnt-offerings, and for peace-offerings, to make an atonement for them, saith the Lord Eternal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 30:14–15; Isaiah 16:1"> All the people of the land shall be held bound for this heave-offering for the prince in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:4–12; 2 Chronicles 31:3; Isaiah 66:23"> And upon the prince shall be the duty to furnish the burnt-offerings, the meat-offerings, and the drink-offerings, on the feasts, and on the new-moon days, and on the sabbaths, on all the festive seasons of the house of Israel: he himself shall prepare the sin-offering, and the meat-offering, and the burnt-offering, and the peace-offerings, to make an atonement in behalf of the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 16:16; Exodus 12:2; Ezekiel 43:22"> Thus hath said the Lord Eternal, In the first month, on the first of the month, shalt thou take a young bullock without blemish, and make an expiation for the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:20; Ezekiel 43:14; Leviticus 16:18–20"> And the priest shall take some of the blood of the sin-offering, and put it upon the door-post of the house, and upon the four corners of the projection of the altar, and upon the door-post of the gate of the inner court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 19:12; Leviticus 4:27–35; Ezekiel 45:15"> And so shalt thou do on the seventh day of the month for every one that erreth, and for him that hath sinned unawares; so shall ye atone for the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 23:5–8; 1 Corinthians 5:7–8; Numbers 9:2–14"> In the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, shall ye have the passover: a feast of seven days; unleavened bread shall be eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 4:14; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Matthew 20:28"> And the prince shall prepare on that day in behalf of himself and in behalf of all the people of the land a bullock for a sin-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 42:8; Leviticus 23:8; Numbers 23:1–2"> And on the seven days of the feast shall he prepare a burnt-offering to the Lord, seven bullocks and seven rams without blemish on every day of the seven days; and for a sin-offering a he-goat on every day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:5–7; Numbers 28:12–15"> And as a meat-offering an ephah for a bullock, and an ephah for a ram shall he prepare, and a hin of oil for each ephah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 23:33–43; Numbers 29:12–38; 2 Chronicles 5:3"> In the seventh month, on the fifteenth day of the month, on the feast, shall he prepare the like during the seven days, both the sin-offering, as also the burnt-offering, and the meat-offering, and the oil.</span>
