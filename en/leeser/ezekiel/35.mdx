<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:1; Ezekiel 21:1; Ezekiel 34:1"> And the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:2; Genesis 32:3; Ezekiel 25:8"> Son of man, set thy face against the mountain of Se'ir, and prophecy against it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:12; Ezekiel 35:7; Ezekiel 6:14"> And say unto it, Thus hath said the Lord Eternal, Behold, I will be against thee, O mountain of Se'ir, and I will stretch out my hand over thee, and I will render thee desolate and wasted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:9; Malachi 1:3–4; Joel 3:19"> Thy cities will I lay in ruins, and thou thyself shalt be desolate, and thou shalt know that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 137:7; Obadiah 1:10–16; Amos 1:11"> Because thou hast had an undying hatred, and didst surrender the children of Israel to the power of the sword, at the time of their calamity, at the time of the iniquity of the end:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 63:2–6; Revelation 18:24; Obadiah 1:15"> Therefore, as I live, saith the Lord Eternal, I will surely let thy blood flow, and blood shall pursue thee; since thou didst not hate blood-shedding, so shall blood pursue thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:11; Ezekiel 35:3; Judges 5:6–7"> Thus will I change the mountain of Se'ir into a desolate land and a waste, and I will cut off from it him that travelleth forward and backward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:12; Ezekiel 32:4–5; Ezekiel 39:4–5"> And I will fill his mountains with his slain: as regardeth thy hills, and thy valleys, and all thy ravines, in them shall fall those that are slain by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:13; Malachi 1:3–4; Ezekiel 6:7"> Into perpetual desolations will I change thee, and thy cities shall not be restored: and ye shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:5; Psalms 83:4–12; Ezekiel 48:35"> Because thou hast said, These two nations and these two countries shall indeed be mine, and we will take possession thereof; whereas the Lord was there:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 9:16; Matthew 7:2; Psalms 73:17–18"> Therefore, as I live, saith the Lord God, I will even do according to thy anger, and according to thy envy which thou didst use out of thy hatred against them; and I will make myself known among them, when I judge thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:2; Psalms 83:12; Ezekiel 6:7"> And thou shalt know that I am the Lord: I have heard all thy blasphemies which thou hast spoken against the mountains of Israel, saying, They are laid desolate, they are given unto us to consume them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 11:36; Isaiah 36:20; Jeremiah 29:23"> And ye boasted greatly against me with your mouth, and have multiplied against me your words: I have indeed heard them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:48; Isaiah 14:7–8; Isaiah 65:13–15"> Thus hath said the Lord Eternal, When the whole earth rejoiceth, I will make thee desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:3–4; Lamentations 4:21; Isaiah 34:5–6"> As thou didst rejoice over the inheritance of the house of Israel, because it was made desolate: so will I do unto thee; desolate shalt thou be, O mountain of Se'ir, and all Idumea—altogether; and they shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
