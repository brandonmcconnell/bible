<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:14; Ezekiel 11:24; Ezekiel 11:13"> And a spirit bore me up, and brought me unto the east gate of the house of the Lord, which looketh eastward: and behold, there were at the entrance of the gate five and twenty men; and I saw in the midst of them Yaazanyah the son of 'Azzur, and Pelatyahu the son of Benayahu, princes of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 30:1; Jeremiah 5:5; Psalms 2:1–2"> Then said he unto me, Son of man, these are the men that devise wickedness, and give evil counsel in this city;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:27; Ezekiel 12:22; Ezekiel 11:7–11"> Who say, &#91;The evil&#93; is not near; so let us build houses: this &#91;city&#93; is the pot, and we are the flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:46–47; Ezekiel 21:2; Ezekiel 3:2–15"> Therefore prophesy against them, prophesy, O son of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:10; Jeremiah 16:17; Revelation 2:23"> And the Spirit of the Lord fell upon me, and said unto me, Speak, Thus hath said the Lord, Thus have ye said, O house of Israel; and whatever cometh into your mind, do I know full well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:23; Ezekiel 22:27; Ezekiel 22:12"> Ye have multiplied those slain by you in this city, and ye have filled its streets with the slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:3–13; Micah 3:2–3; 2 Kings 25:18–22"> Therefore thus hath said the Lord Eternal, Those slain by you whom ye have struck down in the midst of it, —they are the flesh, and this place is the pot; but you are to be removed out of the midst of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 10:24; Isaiah 24:17–18; Job 3:25"> The sword have ye feared: and the sword will I bring over you, saith the Lord Eternal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:8; Psalms 106:41; Deuteronomy 28:36"> And I will remove you out of the midst of it, and I will give you up into the hand of strangers, and will execute punishments among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 14:25; Jeremiah 52:9–10; 2 Kings 25:19–21"> By the sword shall ye fall; on the boundary of Israel will I judge you: and ye shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:3; Ezekiel 11:7–10"> This place shall not be unto you as a pot, so that you should be as flesh in the midst of it; but on the boundary of Israel will I judge you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:10; Ezekiel 8:16; Ezekiel 8:14"> And ye shall know that I am the Lord: because in my statutes have ye not walked, and my ordinances have ye not executed; but ye have done after the ordinances of the nations that are round about you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:1; Ezekiel 9:8; Acts 5:5"> And it came to pass, as I was prophesying, that Pelatyahu the son of Benayah died. Then fell I down upon my face, and cried with a loud voice, and said, Ah Lord Eternal! wilt thou make a full end of the remnant of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Then came the word of the Lord unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:24; Isaiah 65:5; John 16:2"> Son of man, thy brethren, even thy brethren, the men of thy kindred, and the whole house of Israel altogether, are they unto whom the inhabitants of Jerusalem have said, Remain you far from the Lord: unto us is this land given for a possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14; Deuteronomy 30:3–4; Psalms 90:1"> Therefore say, Thus hath said the Lord Eternal, Although I have removed them far away among the nations, and although I have scattered them among the countries: yet will I be to them as a minor sanctuary in the countries whither they are come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:25; Jeremiah 3:18; Ezekiel 34:13"> Therefore say, Thus hath said the Lord Eternal, I will both gather you from the people, and assemble you out of the countries whether ye have been scattered, and I will give you the land of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:23; Ezekiel 5:11; Ezekiel 42:7–8"> And they shall come thither, and they shall remove all its detestable things, and all its abominations out of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:26–27; Jeremiah 31:33; Psalms 51:10"> And I will give them one single heart, and a new spirit will I put within you; and I will remove the heart of stone out of their body, and I will give unto them a heart of flesh:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:11; Psalms 105:45; Jeremiah 30:22"> In order that they may walk in my statutes, and keep my ordinances, and do them; and they shall be unto me for a people, and I will indeed be unto them for a God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:10; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Jeremiah 1:16"> But as for those whose heart walketh after the heart of their detestable things and their abominations, their way do I bring upon their own head, saith the Lord Eternal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:19; Ezekiel 1:19–20"> Then did the cherubim lift up their wings, and the wheels at the same time with them; and the glory of the God of Israel was over them above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zechariah 14:4; Ezekiel 8:4; Ezekiel 10:4"> And the glory of the Lord ascended from the midst of the city, and halted upon the mount which is on the east side of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:12; Ezekiel 8:3; Ezekiel 11:1"> But a spirit bore me up, and brought me into Chaldea, to those in exile, in the appearance through the spirit of God: and then ascended away from me the appearance which I had seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:4; Ezekiel 3:27; Ezekiel 3:17"> Then did I speak unto those in exile all the things that the Lord had shown me.</span>
