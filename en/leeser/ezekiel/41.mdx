<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:2–3; Ezekiel 40:17; Ezekiel 41:23"> And he brought me to the temple: and he measured the door-posts, six cubits broad on the one side, and six cubits broad on the other side, &#91;as also&#93; the breadth of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 6:17; 2 Chronicles 3:3; 1 Kings 6:2"> And the breadth of the door was ten cubits; and the sides of the door were five cubits on the one side, and five cubits on the other side: and he measured its length, forty cubits, and the breadth, twenty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:16"> Then went he inward, and measured the posts of the door, two cubits; and the door was six cubits high; and the breadth of the door, was seven cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 6:20; Hebrews 9:3–8; Exodus 26:33–34"> And he measured its length, twenty cubits; and the breadth, twenty cubits, fronting on the temple: and he said unto me, This is the most holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 6:5–6; Ezekiel 42:3–14; Ezekiel 41:6–9"> After this he measured the wall of the house, six cubits; and the breadth of every side-chamber was four cubits, all round about the house on every side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 6:10; 1 Kings 6:5–6; Ezekiel 40:17"> And the side-chambers were one over another, three and thirty times; and they entered into the wall which was on the house for the side-chambers all round about, that they might be fastened on, but they were not fastened on the wall of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 6:8; Hebrews 6:1; Matthew 13:32"> And as one wound upward it became continually wider for the side-chambers; for the row of chambers about the house went more and more upward round about the house; therefore was the breadth of the house greater upward: and so they ascended from the lowest chambers to the highest through the middle ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:5; Revelation 21:16"> And I saw the height of the house all round about: the foundations of the side-chambers were a full rod of six cubits under ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:11; Ezekiel 42:4; Ezekiel 42:1"> The thickness of the wall, which was for the side-chambers without, was five cubits, as also the space which was left open by the row of the side-chambers that were on the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:17"> And between the chambers there was a width of twenty cubits round about the house on every side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:9; Ezekiel 42:4"> And the doors of the side-chambers were on the open space, one door was in a northern direction, and another door on the south; and the breadth of the place that was left open was five cubits all round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:1; Ezekiel 42:13; Ezekiel 41:13–15"> Now the building that was before the main wing on the west side was seventy cubits broad; and the wall of the building was five cubits thick round about, and its length, ninety cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:47"> So he measured the house, in length one hundred cubits; and the main wing, and the building, with its walls, in length one hundred cubits;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Also the breadth in the front of the house, and of the main wing on the east, was one hundred cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:3; Ezekiel 42:1; Ezekiel 41:12"> And he measured the length of the building on the front side of the main wing which was behind it, and its corner-pillars on the one side and on the other side, one hundred cubits; and this included the inner temple, and the porches of the court;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 6:4; Ezekiel 40:25; Ezekiel 40:16"> The thresholds, and the narrow windows, and the corner-pillars were round about on their three sides: opposite the threshold there was a wainscoting of wood all round about, and so from the ground up to the windows; and the windows were covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:15"> On the part above the door, and as far as the inner house, and the outer &#91;house&#93;, was &#91;a wainscoting&#93;, and on all the wall round about within and without, by &#91;the same&#93; measure;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 7:36; Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 40:22"> And it was ornamented with cherubim and palm-trees, a palm-tree being between two cherubim; and every cherub had two faces;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:14; Ezekiel 1:10"> So that a human face was toward the palm-tree on the one side, and a young lion's face toward the palm-tree on the other side: it was so made on all the house round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:18"> From the ground to the part above the door were the cherubim and the palm-trees made, and so on the wall of the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:1; 1 Kings 6:33; Ezekiel 40:14"> The temple had four-cornered door-posts, and the front of the holy of holies had the &#91;same&#93; appearance as the appearance &#91;of the other&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Malachi 1:12; Ezekiel 23:41; Malachi 1:7"> The altar was of wood, three cubits high, and its length was two cubits; and its corners, and its top-piece, and its walls, were of wood: and he spoke unto me, This is the table that is before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 6:31–35; 2 Chronicles 4:22; Ezekiel 41:1"> And the temple and the holy of holies had two doors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 6:34; Ezekiel 40:48"> And the doors had two leaves &#91;apiece&#93;, two turning leaves, two &#91;leaves&#93; for the one door, and two leaves for the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:18"> And there were made on them, on the doors of the temple, cherubim and palm-trees, as they were made upon the walls; and &#91;a covering of&#93; thick wooden planks was upon the front of the porch without.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:16; Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 41:5"> And there were narrow windows and palm-trees on the one side and on the other side, on the sides of the porch, and on the side-chambers of the house, and the &#91;covering of&#93; thick planks.</span>
