<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 58:1; Ezekiel 24:6; Ezekiel 24:9"> And thou, son of man, wilt thou take to task, wilt thou take to task the city of blood? and wilt thou make her know all her abominations?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:27; Romans 2:5; 2 Peter 2:3"> Then say thou, Thus hath said the Lord Eternal, O city that sheddeth blood in her midst, that her time &#91;of sorrow&#93; may come, and that hath made idols for herself to become unclean:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 21:16; Psalms 44:13–14; Ezekiel 22:2"> Through thy blood which thou hast shed, are thou become guilty; and through thy idols which thou hast made art thou become unclean; and thou hast caused thy days &#91;of destruction&#93; to draw near, and thou art come even unto thy years; therefore do I make thee a disgrace unto the nations, and a mocking to all the countries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 22:2; Jeremiah 15:2–3"> Those who are near, and those who are far from thee, shall mock thee, O thou unclean in name, and great in confusion!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:27; Isaiah 1:23; Zechariah 3:3"> Behold, the princes of Israel were each &#91;ready&#93; with his arm within thee, in order to shed blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:16; Exodus 22:21–22; Leviticus 20:9"> Father and mother they esteemed lightly within thee, toward the stranger they acted with extortion in the midst of thee, the fatherless and the widow they oppressed within thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:38–39; Ezekiel 22:26; Ezekiel 20:13"> My holy things thou didst despise, and my sabbaths thou didst profane.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 4:14; Leviticus 19:16; Hosea 4:10"> Tale-bearers &#91;also&#93; were within thee, in order to shed blood; and upon the mountains did they eat within thee; incest did they commit in the midst of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 18:19; Leviticus 18:7–8; Ezekiel 18:6"> Their father's nakedness they uncovered within thee; her that was unclean in her separation did they violate within thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 18:15; 2 Samuel 13:14; Leviticus 20:17"> And one with his neighbor's wife did commit abomination; and another did defile his daughter-in-law with incest; and another did violate his sister, the daughter of his father, within thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:25; Ezekiel 23:35; Psalms 106:21"> Bribes they took within thee, in order to shed blood; interest and increase didst thou take, and thou didst acquire gain off thy neighbors by extortion: and me thou didst forget, saith the Lord Eternal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:17; Isaiah 33:15; Amos 2:6–8"> And behold, I have struck my hands together at thy wrongful gain which thou hast acquired, and at thy blood-guilt which hath been in the midst of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:7; Ezekiel 17:24; Ezekiel 24:14"> Will thy courage endure, or will thy hands remain strong, on the days that I will deal with thee? I the Lord have spoken, and will do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:8; Ezekiel 22:22; Deuteronomy 4:27"> And I will scatter thee among the nations, and disperse thee in the countries, and I will entirely remove thy uncleanness out of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:7; Psalms 83:18; Ezekiel 7:24"> And thou shalt be degraded through thyself before the eyes of nations, and thou shalt know that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 119:119; Isaiah 1:22; Jeremiah 6:28–30"> Son of man, the house of Israel are become to me dross: they all are copper, and tin, and iron, and lead, in the midst of the furnace; the dross of silver are they become.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 13:30; Ezekiel 24:3–6; Micah 4:12"> Therefore thus hath said the Lord Eternal, Because ye are all become dross, therefore, behold, I will gather you into the midst of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:11–12; Isaiah 54:16; Ezekiel 24:13"> As silver, and copper, and iron, and lead, and tin are gathered into the midst of the furnace, to blow the fire upon it, to melt it: so will I gather you in my anger and in my fury and I will lay you down there, and melt you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 64:7; Nahum 1:6; Psalms 50:3"> Yea, I will assemble you in a heap, and blow upon you with the fire of my wrath, and ye shall be melted in the midst of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:8; Ezekiel 20:33; Hosea 5:10"> As silver is melted in the midst of the furnace, so shall ye be melted in the midst of it: and ye shall know that I am the Lord who have poured out my fury over you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:14–16; Jeremiah 2:30; 2 Chronicles 28:22"> Son of man, say unto her, Thou art a land that is not cleansed; &#91;and&#93; which is not rained upon on the day of indignation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hosea 6:9; Jeremiah 2:34; Ezekiel 13:19"> The banded troop of her prophets in the midst of her is like a roaring lion that teareth in pieces the prey: souls do they devour; wealth and precious things do they take away; the number of her widows do they increase in the midst of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 10:10; Ezekiel 44:23; Ezekiel 22:8"> Her priests violate my law, and profane my holy things: between the holy and profane do they make no distinction, and the difference between the unclean and the clean do they not make known, and from &#91;the violation of&#93; my sabbaths do they turn away their eyes, so that I am profaned among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:13; Isaiah 1:23; Ezekiel 22:6"> Her princes in her midst are like wolves that tear in pieces the prey, to shed blood, to destroy souls, in order to obtain selfish gain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:6–7; Ezekiel 13:10–16; Jeremiah 23:25–32"> And her prophets plastered for them with unadhesive mortar, foreseeing falsehood, and divining unto them lies, saying, Thus hath said the Lord Eternal; when the Lord had not spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:7; Exodus 23:9; Exodus 22:21"> The people of the land are guilty of extortion, and practise robbery, and the poor and the needy they defraud: and they extort from the stranger with injustice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 59:16; Jeremiah 5:1; Psalms 106:23"> And I seek now among them for a man, that could erect a fence, and stand in the breach before me in behalf of the land, so that I should not destroy it; but I find none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:43; Ezekiel 7:8–9; Ezekiel 9:10"> And I therefore pour out over them my indignation; with the fire of my wrath do I make an end of them: their own way do I bring upon their head, saith the Lord Eternal.</span>
