<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:30; Ezekiel 33:17; Ezekiel 3:11"> Son of man, speak to the children of thy people, and say unto them, If there be a land over which I bring the sword, and the people of the land take a man from among themselves, and appoint him unto themselves for a watchman;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 8:1; Nehemiah 4:20; Nehemiah 4:18"> And if he see the sword coming over the land, and blow the cornet, and warn the people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:13; Acts 18:6; Jeremiah 6:17"> And whosoever heareth the sound of the cornet, and taketh no warning; and the sword cometh, and taketh him away: his blood shall be upon his own head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 9:19–21; Hebrews 11:7; John 8:39"> The sound of the cornet hath he heard, and he hath taken no warning; his blood shall be upon him. But had he taken warning he would have delivered his soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:18–20; Ezekiel 33:8–9; Genesis 42:22"> But if the watchman see the sword coming, and blow not the cornet, so that the people be not warned, and the sword cometh, and taketh away from among them some person: this one is taken away for his iniquity; but his blood will I require from the watchman's hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:17–21; Jeremiah 26:2; Ezekiel 2:7–8"> But as for thee, O son of man, I have appointed thee a watchman unto the house of Israel: so that when thou hearest a word from my mouth, thou shalt warn them from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 20:26–27; Ezekiel 33:6; Ezekiel 33:14"> When I say unto the wicked, O wicked one, thou shalt surely die; and thou dost not speak to warn the wicked from his way: that wicked one shall die for his iniquity; but his blood will I require from thy hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:19; Acts 20:26; Proverbs 15:10"> Nevertheless, if thou hast indeed warned the wicked of his way to turn away from it, and he do not turn from his way: he shall certainly die for his iniquity; but thou hast delivered thy soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 49:14; Ezekiel 37:11; Ezekiel 24:23"> But thou, O son of man, speak unto the house of Israel, Thus do ye speak, saying, Truly our transgressions and our sins are upon us, and through them do we pine away: how then shall we live?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:4; Ezekiel 18:23; 2 Peter 3:9"> Say unto them, As I live, saith the Lord Eternal, I have no pleasure in the death of the wicked; but in the return of the wicked from his way that he may live: return ye, return ye from your evil ways; for why will ye die, O house of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:14; Ezekiel 33:18–19; Romans 3:25"> But thou, son of man, say unto the children of thy people, The righteousness of the righteous shall not deliver him on the day of his transgression; and as for the wickedness of the wicked, he shall not stumble through it on the day that he returneth from his wickedness; nor shall the righteous be able to live through the other on the day that he sinneth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:24; Hebrews 10:38; Ezekiel 18:4"> When I say of the righteous, that he shall surely live, and he trusteth to his own righteousness, and committeth what is wrong: all his righteous deeds shall not be remembered, and for his wrong that he hath committed,—through this shall he die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:27; Jeremiah 18:7–8; Hosea 14:1"> Again, when I say unto the wicked, Thou shalt surely die; and he turneth from his sin, and executeth justice and righteousness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:11; Luke 19:8; Exodus 22:1–4"> If the wicked restore the pledge, make restitution for what he hath robbed, walk in the statutes of life, so as not to do any wrong: he shall surely live, he shall not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Ezekiel 18:22; Micah 7:18–19"> All his sins that he hath committed shall not be remembered unto him: justice and righteousness hath he executed, he shall surely live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 25:24–26; Ezekiel 18:25; Ezekiel 18:29"> Yet say the children of thy people, The way of the Lord is not equitable: while, as to them, their way is not equitable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:12–13; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Hebrews 10:38"> When the righteous turneth away from his righteousness, and doth what is wrong, he shall even die therefore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:27–28; Ezekiel 33:14"> And when the wicked returneth from his wickedness, and executeth justice and righteousness, he shall surely live therefore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 16:27; Ezekiel 18:25; Ezekiel 33:17"> Yet ye say, The way of the Lord is not equitable. Every one after his own ways will I judge you, O house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:2; Jeremiah 39:1–8; 2 Kings 25:4"> And it came to pass in the twelfth year, in the tenth month, on the fifth day of the month after our exile, that there came unto me one that had escaped out of Jerusalem, saying, The city hath been smitten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:3; Ezekiel 3:26–27; Ezekiel 24:26–27"> Now the inspiration of the Lord was come upon me in the evening, before the coming of the one who had escaped; and he had opened my mouth, before he was come to me in the morning; and my mouth was opened and I was not kept dumb any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:27; Isaiah 51:2; Acts 7:5"> Son of man, they that dwell in these ruins in the land of Israel say as followeth, But one man was Abraham, and he obtained the land as an inheritance; and as we are many, the land must &#91;surely&#93; be given to us for an inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:16; Jeremiah 7:9–10; Genesis 9:4"> Therefore say unto them, Thus hath said the Lord Eternal, By the blood do ye eat, and your eyes do you lift up toward your idols, and blood do ye shed: and ye expect to possess the land?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 27:40; Micah 2:1–2; Zephaniah 3:3"> Ye depend upon your sword, ye commit abomination, and ye defile every one his neighbor's wife: and ye expect to possess the land?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:6; Jeremiah 42:22; Ezekiel 39:4"> Thus shalt thou say unto them, Thus hath said the Lord Eternal, As I live, surely those that are in the ruined places shall fall by the sword, and him that is in the open field will I give to the beasts that they may devour him, and those that are in the strongholds and in the caves shall die through the pestilence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:24; Jeremiah 44:2; Jeremiah 44:6"> And I will render the land desolate and wasted, and the pride of her strength shall cease; and the mountains of Israel shall be desolate, with none to pass through them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:7; Ezekiel 36:17–18; Ezekiel 22:2–15"> And they shall experience that I am the Lord, when I render the land desolate and wasted, because of all their abominations which they have committed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 22:16–17; Jeremiah 23:35; Isaiah 29:13"> And thou, son of man, &#91;there are&#93; the children of thy people, who are talking about thee by the walls and in the entrances of the houses, and speak one to another, every one to his brother, saying, Do come, and hear what the word is which cometh forth from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 13:22; Psalms 78:36–37; James 1:22–24"> And then they come unto thee as the people come, and they sit before thee &#91;as&#93; my people, and they hear thy words, but do not execute them; for as merry songs they carry them in their mouth, while their heart goeth after their unlawful gains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 6:20; Mark 4:16–17; John 5:35"> And, lo, thou art unto them as a merry song of one that hath a pleasant voice, and can play well: and &#91;thus&#93; they hear thy words, but execute them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremiah 28:9; 1 Samuel 3:19–20; Ezekiel 2:5"> But when it cometh to pass, &#91;lo, it will come,&#93; then shall they know that a prophet hath been among them.</span>
