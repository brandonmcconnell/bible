<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:4; Ezekiel 34:14; Ezekiel 37:22"> But thou, son of man, prophesy unto the mountains of Israel, and say, O mountains of Israel, hear ye the word of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13; Ezekiel 25:3; Ezekiel 35:10"> Thus hath said the Lord Eternal, Because the enemy hath said regarding you, Aha, even the ancient high-places are become ours as a possession:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 44:13–14; Lamentations 2:15–16; Jeremiah 18:16"> Therefore prophesy and say, Thus hath said the Lord Eternal, Because, even because men have made you desolate, and sought to swallow you up on every side, that ye might become a possession unto the residue of the nations, and ye are taken up as a talk for tongues, and an evil report of the people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:28; Deuteronomy 11:11; Ezekiel 36:6"> Therefore, O mountains of Israel, hear ye the word of the Lord Eternal, Thus hath said the Lord Eternal to the mountains, and to the hills, to the ravines, and to the valleys, to the desolate ruins, and to the cities that are forsaken, which are become a prey and derision to the residue of the nations that are round about:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 7:8; Ezekiel 36:3; Psalms 137:7"> Therefore thus hath said the Lord Eternal, Surely in the fire of my jealousy have I spoken against the residue of the nations, and against all Idumea, that have appropriated my land unto themselves as a possession with the joy of all their heart, with derision in their soul, in order to drive it out that it may be for a prey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 123:3–4; Ezekiel 34:29; Psalms 74:23"> Therefore prophesy concerning the land of Israel, and say unto the mountains, and to the hills, to the ravines, and to the valleys, Thus hath said the Lord Eternal, Behold, In my zealousness and in my fury have I spoken, because ye have borne the reproach of the nations:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:5; Amos 1:1–15; Ezekiel 20:15"> Therefore thus hath said the Lord Eternal, I have indeed lifted up my hand, that the nations who are round about you— these shall bear their shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 27:6; Hebrews 10:37; Ezekiel 34:26–29"> But ye, O mountains of Israel, ye shall send forth your boughs, and your fruit shall ye bear for my people Israel, for they are near at hand to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 46:11; Hosea 2:21–23; Ezekiel 36:34"> For, behold, I will be for you, and I will turn unto you, and ye shall be tilled and ye shall be sown;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:33; Jeremiah 33:12; Ezekiel 36:37"> And I will multiply upon you men, all the house of Israel— altogether; and the cities shall be inhabited again, and the ruins shall be rebuilt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:12; Jeremiah 23:5–8; Jeremiah 31:27"> And I will multiply upon you men and beast, and they shall increase and be fruitful; and I will cause you to be inhabited after your old estates, and will do more good unto you than at your beginnings: and ye shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:7; Ezekiel 36:13; Jeremiah 32:44"> Yea, I will cause to walk upon you men, even my people Israel, and they shall possess thee, and thou shalt be unto them as an inheritance, and thou shalt not any more henceforth cast them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 13:32"> Thus hath said the Lord Eternal, Because they say unto you, Thou land devourest up men, and hast been one that hath ever cast out thy nations:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:25–28; Amos 9:15; Isaiah 60:21"> Therefore shalt thou not devour up men any more, and thy nations shalt thou not cast out any more, saith the Lord Eternal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:29; Psalms 89:50; Ezekiel 36:6"> And I will not let be heard against thee any more the reproach of the nations, and the disgrace of the people shalt thou not bear any more, and thy nations shalt thou not cast out any more, saith the Lord Eternal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:7; Numbers 35:33–34; Micah 2:10"> Son of man, the house of Israel, when they dwelt in their own land, defiled it through their way and through their doings: like the uncleanness of a woman in her separation was their way before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:21; Ezekiel 7:8; Lamentations 2:4"> And I poured out my fury over them because of the blood that they had shed in the land, and because through their idols they had polluted it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:24; Deuteronomy 28:64; Ezekiel 5:12"> And I scattered them among the nations, and they were dispersed through the countries: according to their way and according to their doings did I judge them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 2:24; Isaiah 52:5; Jeremiah 33:24"> And when they were come unto the nations, whither they were gone, they profaned my holy name; because they said of them, These are the people of the Lord, and out of his land are they gone forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 48:9; Psalms 74:18; Ezekiel 20:9"> But I had pity for my holy name, which the house of Israel had profaned among the nations, whither they were gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 106:8; Ezekiel 36:32; Psalms 115:1–2"> Therefore say unto the house of Israel, Thus hath said the Lord Eternal, Not for your sake do I this, O house of Israel, but for the sake of my holy name, which ye have profaned among the nations, whither ye are gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:41; Isaiah 5:16; Ezekiel 39:7"> And I will sanctify my great name, which was profaned among the nations, which ye have profaned in the midst of them; and the nations shall know that I am the Lord, saith the Lord Eternal, when I will be sanctified through you before your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:21; Ezekiel 34:13; Amos 9:14–15"> And I will take you from among the nations, and I will gather you out of all the countries, and I will bring you unto your own land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; John 3:5; Jeremiah 33:8"> And I will sprinkle upon you clean water, and ye shall be clean: from all your impurities, and from all your idols, will I cleanse you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19–20; Psalms 51:10; 2 Corinthians 5:17"> And I will give you a new heart, and a new spirit will I put within you; and I will remove the heart of stone out of your body, and I will give you a heart of flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Titus 3:3–6; Hebrews 13:21; 1 Corinthians 3:16"> And my spirit I will put within you, and I will cause that you shall walk in my statutes, and that my ordinances ye shall keep, and do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:33; Jeremiah 32:38; Ezekiel 37:27"> And ye shall dwell in the land which I gave to your fathers; and ye shall be unto me for a people, and I truly will be unto you as a God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 1:7–9; Matthew 1:21; Hosea 2:21–23"> And I will save you from all kinds of your impurities; and I will call unto the corn, and increase it, and I will not lay famine upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:27; Joel 2:26; Deuteronomy 29:23–28"> And I will multiply the fruit of the trees, and the products of the field: in order that ye may receive no more reproach on account of famine among the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:9; Ezekiel 20:43; Nehemiah 9:26–35"> Then shall ye remember your ways that they were evil, and your doings that were not good; and ye shall loathe yourselves on account of your iniquities and on account of your abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:5; Ezekiel 36:22; 2 Timothy 1:9"> Not for your sake do I this, saith the Lord Eternal, be it known unto you: be ashamed and confounded because of your ways, O house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 58:12; Ezekiel 36:10; Jeremiah 33:10"> Thus hath said the Lord Eternal, On the day of my cleansing you from all your iniquities, when I cause the cities to be inhabited, and when the ruins are built up,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:14; Jeremiah 25:9–11; 2 Chronicles 36:21"> And when the desolate land is tilled, instead that it was a waste before the eyes of every passer by:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 51:3; Joel 2:3; Jeremiah 33:9"> Then shall they say, This land, that was desolate, is become like the garden of 'Eden; and the cities that were ruined, and desolate, and broken down, are become fortified, and inhabited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Numbers 23:19; Ezekiel 17:24"> And the nations that are left round about you shall know that I the Lord have built up the broken-down &#91;places&#93;, have planted the desolate &#91;land&#93;: I the Lord have spoken this, and have done it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 John 5:14; Hebrews 10:21–22; Ezekiel 14:3"> Thus hath said the Lord Eternal, Also in this will I yet suffer myself to be entreated of by the house of Israel, to do it for them, I will increase them with men like flocks &#91;in multitude&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Kings 8:63; Acts 2:5–11; John 10:16"> As the flocks of the holy things, as the flocks of Jerusalem on her appointed feasts, so shall the ruined cities be full of flocks of men: and they shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
