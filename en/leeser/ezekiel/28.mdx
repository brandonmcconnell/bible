<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:9; 2 Thessalonians 2:4; Isaiah 14:13–14"> Son of man, say unto the prince of Tyre, Thus hath said the Lord Eternal, Whereas thy heart was lifted up, and thou saidst, A god am I, on the seat of the gods do I dwell, in the heart of the seas; yet thou art but a man, and not God, while thou esteemest thy mind equal to the mind of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 1:20; Daniel 2:22; Daniel 2:27–28"> Behold, thou wast wiser than Daniel; no secret was obscure to thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:17–18; Proverbs 23:4–5; Proverbs 18:11"> With thy wisdom and with thy understanding hadst thou gotten thee riches, and hadst gotten gold and silver into thy treasuries;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 62:10; Psalms 52:7; Ezekiel 28:2"> By the abundance of thy wisdom in thy traffic hadst thou increased thy riches; and thy heart was lifted up because of thy riches:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:2; Exodus 9:17; James 1:11"> Therefore thus hath said the Lord Eternal, Because thou hast esteemed thy mind equal to the mind of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:12; Ezekiel 32:12; Ezekiel 30:11"> Therefore, behold, will I bring over thee strangers, the fiercest • of nations; and they shall draw their swords against the beauty of thy wisdom, and they shall profane thy elegance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:26–27; Ezekiel 27:34; Proverbs 1:12"> Down to the grave will they cast thee, and thou shalt die the deaths of the slain in the heart of the seas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:2; Isaiah 31:3; Psalms 82:7"> Wilt thou then say, I am God, before him that slayeth thee? when thou art but a man, and no God, in the hand of him that fatally wounded thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:18; Ezekiel 32:19; Philippians 3:3"> The deaths of the uncircumcised shalt thou die by the hand of strangers; for I have spoken it, saith the Lord Eternal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:1; Ezekiel 26:17; 2 Corinthians 1:22"> Son of man, take up a lamentation concerning the king of Tyre, and say unto him, Thus hath said the Lord Eternal, Thou wast complete in outline, full of wisdom, and perfect in beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:15; Genesis 2:8; Ezekiel 27:16"> In 'Eden the garden of God didst thou abide; every precious stone was thy covering, the sardius, the topaz, and the diamond, the chrysolite, the onyx, and the jasper, the sapphire, the emerald, and the carbuncle, and gold; thy tabrets and thy flutes of artificial workmanship were prepared in thee on the day thou wast created.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 14:12–15; Exodus 30:26; Ezekiel 28:13"> Thou wast a cherub with outspread covering &#91;wings&#93;; and I had set thee upon the holy mountain of God &#91;as&#93; thou wast; in the midst of the stones of fire didst thou wander.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Ezekiel 28:17–18; Romans 7:9"> Perfect wast thou in thy ways from the day that thou wast created, till wickedness was found in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Habakkuk 2:8; Habakkuk 2:17"> By the abundance of thy commerce thou wast filled to thy centre with violence, and thou didst sin: therefore I degraded thee out of the mountain of God; and I destroyed thee, O covering cherub, from the midst of the stones of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:5; Ezekiel 31:10; James 4:6"> Thy heart was lifted up through thy beauty, thou didst corrupt thy wisdom by reason of thy elegance: &#91;therefore&#93; I cast thee down to the ground, before kings did I set thee that they might gaze on thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Malachi 4:3; Revelation 18:8; Amos 1:9–10"> Through the abundance of thy iniquities, through the wickedness of thy commerce didst thou profane thy sanctuaries: therefore brought I forth fire from the midst of thee, this devoured thee, and I changed thee to ashes upon the earth before the eyes of all those that saw thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:21; Revelation 18:21; Revelation 18:15–19"> All that know thee among the people are astonished concerning thee: thou art as though thou hadst not been, and thou shalt not be any more for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:2; Genesis 10:15; Jeremiah 25:22"> Son of man, set thy face against Zidon, and prophesy against it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:13; Exodus 14:17; Ezekiel 20:41"> And thou shalt say, Thus hath said the Lord Eternal, Behold, I am against thee, O Zidon, and I will be honored in the midst of thee: and they shall know that I am the Lord, when I execute judgments on her, and will be sanctified on her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:22; Ezekiel 26:6; Ezekiel 25:11"> And I will send out against her pestilence, and blood-&#91;shedding&#93; into her streets; and the deadly wounded shall be felled in the midst of her by the sword &#91;that is&#93; against her from every side: and they shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 33:55; Joshua 23:13; 2 Corinthians 12:7"> And there shall be no more unto the house of Israel a pricking brier, nor painful thorn from all that are round about them, that despoil them: and they shall know that I am the Lord Eternal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:37; Ezekiel 20:41; Ezekiel 36:28"> Thus hath said the Lord Eternal, When I gather the house of Israel from the people among whom they are scattered, and shall be sanctified on them before the eyes of the nations: then shall they dwell in their land that I have given to my servant Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:24; Ezekiel 28:22; Jeremiah 32:15"> And they shall dwell thereupon in safety, and they shall build houses, and plant vineyards; yea, they shall dwell in safety; when I execute judgments on all those that despoiled them from round about them: and they shall know that I am the Lord their God.</span>
