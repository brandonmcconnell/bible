<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 8:11–12; Psalms 74:9; 1 Samuel 3:21"> And the lad Samuel was ministering unto the Lord before 'Eli. And the word of the Lord was scarce in those days: prophecy was not extended.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:15; Psalms 90:10; Genesis 27:1"> And it came to pass one day, when 'Eli was lying down in his place, and his eyes had begun to grow dim, he could not see;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 27:4; Leviticus 24:2–4; Exodus 27:20–21"> And the lamp of God had not yet gone out, while Samuel was lying down in &#91;the hall of&#93; the temple of the Lord, where the ark of God was;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 6:8; 1 Corinthians 12:6–11; Exodus 3:4"> That the Lord called Samuel: and he said, Here am I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And he ran unto 'Eli, and said, Here am I; for thou didst call me. And he said, I did not call: lie down again. And he went and lay down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 9:2; 1 Samuel 4:16; 2 Samuel 18:22"> And the Lord continued to call again, Samuel. And Samuel arose and went to 'Eli, and said, Here am I; for thou didst call me. And he answered, I did not call, my son: lie down again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 19:2; Jeremiah 9:24"> And Samuel knew not yet the Lord, nor had the word of the Lord been as yet revealed unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 33:14–15; 1 Corinthians 13:11–12"> And the Lord continued to call, Samuel, the third time; and he arose and went to 'Eli, and said, Here am I; for thou didst call me. And 'Eli then perceived that the Lord was calling the lad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 20:19; Isaiah 6:8; Psalms 85:8"> And 'Eli said unto Samuel, Go, lie down; and it shall be, if he call thee, that thou shalt say, Speak, Lord; for thy servant heareth. And Samuel went and lay down in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:8; 1 Samuel 3:4–6"> And the Lord came, and placed himself, and called as at previous times, Samuel, Samuel. And Samuel said, Speak; for thy servant heareth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:5; Jeremiah 19:3; 2 Kings 21:12"> And the Lord said to Samuel, Behold, I will do a thing in Israel, at which both the ears of every one that heareth it shall tingle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:27–36; Zechariah 1:6; Joshua 23:15"> On that day will I fulfill on 'Eli all that I have spoken concerning his house: I will begin and finish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 19:18; Matthew 10:37; 1 Samuel 2:12"> And I tell him that I will judge his house for ever; for the iniquity that he knew that his sons were drawing a curse on themselves, and he restrained them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 22:14; 1 Samuel 2:25; Jeremiah 15:1"> And therefore have I sworn unto the house of 'Eli, that the iniquity of 'Eli's house shall not be atoned for with sacrifice or meat-offering for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:6–8; Malachi 1:10; 1 Samuel 1:9"> And Samuel lay until the morning, when he opened the doors of the house of the Lord; and Samuel feared to tell the vision unto 'Eli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> But 'Eli called Samuel, and said, Samuel, my son. And he said, Here am I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:35; Ruth 1:17; Psalms 141:5"> And he said, What is the word which he hath spoken unto thee? do not, I pray thee, conceal it from me: may God do to thee thus, and continue to do so, if thou conceal any thing from me of all the word that he hath spoken unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 2:10; Isaiah 39:8; Judges 10:15"> And Samuel told him all the words, and concealed nothing from him. And he said, He is the Lord: let him do what seemeth good in his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 39:2; 1 Samuel 9:6; 1 Kings 8:56"> And Samuel grew up, and the Lord was with him, and he did not let fall any one of all his words to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 20:1; 2 Samuel 3:10; 1 Timothy 1:12"> And thus knew all Israel from Dan even to Beer-sheba' that Samuel was accredited as a prophet of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:1; Genesis 12:7; 1 Samuel 3:4"> And the Lord continued to appear in Shiloh; for the Lord revealed himself to Samuel in Shiloh by the word of the Lord.</span>
