<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 6:27; Micah 7:5; 2 Samuel 1:25–26"> Now it happened one day, that Jonathan the son of Saul said unto the young man that bore his armor, Come, and let us go over to the Philistines' outpost, that is on the other side yonder. But unto his father he told nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:15–16; Isaiah 10:28–29"> And Saul tarried in the lower part of Gib'ah under the pomegranate tree which is by Migron: and the people that were with him were about six hundred men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:20; 1 Samuel 2:28; 1 Samuel 4:21"> And Achiyah, the son of Achitub, the brother of I-chabod, the son of Phinehas, the son of 'Eli, the priest of the Lord in Shiloh, wore the ephod. And the people knew not that Jonathan was gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:23"> And between the passes, by which Jonathan sought to go over unto the outpost of the Philistines, there was a sharp point of rock on the one side, and a sharp point of rock on the other side: and the name of the one was Bozez, and the name of the other Seneh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The one point rose up abruptly northward opposite Michmash, and the other southward opposite Geba'.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 19:26; Romans 8:31; Zechariah 4:6"> And Jonathan said to the young man that bore his armor, Come, and let us go over unto the outpost of these uncircumcised: it may be that the Lord will work for us; for there is no restraint to the Lord to save by means of many or by means of few.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:3; Zechariah 8:23; 1 Samuel 10:7"> And his armor-bearer said unto him, Do all that is in thy heart: turn thee; behold, I am with thee according to thy heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 7:9–14"> Then said Jonathan, Behold, we will pass over unto these men, and we will show ourselves unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 6:36–40; Genesis 24:13–14"> If they say thus unto us, Stand still until we come to you: then will we remain standing in our places, and will not go up unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 24:14; 1 Samuel 10:7; Judges 7:11"> But if they say thus, Come up unto us: then will we go up; for the Lord hath delivered them into our hand; and this shall be unto us the sign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:6; 1 Samuel 14:22; Judges 6:2"> And when both of them showed themselves unto the outpost of the Philistines, the Philistines said, Behold, Hebrews are coming forth out of the holes wherein they have hidden themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:43–44; 2 Samuel 5:24; Judges 7:15"> And the men of the outpost addressed Jonathan and his armor-bearer, and said, Come up to us, and we will let you know something. Then said Jonathan unto his armor-bearer, Come up after me; for the Lord hath given them up into the hand of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Joshua 23:10; Leviticus 26:7–8"> And Jonathan then ascended upon his hands and upon his feet, and his armor-bearer after him: and they fell before Jonathan, and his armor-bearer was killing after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And that first defeat, which Jonathan and his armor-bearer caused, was about twenty men, within about the half of a field, which a yoke of oxen might plough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:17; Genesis 35:5; 2 Samuel 5:24"> And there arose a terror in the camp, in the field, and among all the people; the outposts, and the free-booters, they also were terrified, and the earth quaked; and it became a very great terror.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:22–25; Isaiah 19:2; Judges 7:22"> And the watchers of Saul in Gib'ah of Benjamin looked; and, behold, the multitude became scattered, and ran hither and thither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then said Saul unto the people that were with him, Muster now, and see who is gone away from us. And they mustered, and, behold, there was neither Jonathan nor his armor-bearer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:7–8; 1 Samuel 5:2; Judges 20:23"> And Saul said unto Achiyah, Bring hither the ark of God; for the ark of God was on that day with the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:24; Numbers 27:21; Joshua 9:14"> And it happened, while Saul was speaking unto the priest, that the confusion which was in the camp of the Philistines went on and increased more and more: And Saul said unto the priest, Withdraw thy hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 7:22; 2 Chronicles 20:23; 1 Samuel 14:16"> And Saul and all the people that were with him were called together, and they came to the battle; and, behold, the sword of every man was against his fellow, the disorder being very great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 29:4; Judges 7:23"> And the Hebrews that were with the Philistines as before that time, those namely who had gone up with them, were in the camp round about; but these also resolved to be with the Israelites that were with Saul and Jonathan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:6; 1 Samuel 31:7"> And all the men of Israel who had hidden themselves on the mountain of Ephraim, heard that the Philistines had fled; and they also followed hard after them in the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 14:30; 1 Samuel 13:5; Psalms 44:6–8"> So the Lord saved Israel that day: and the battle passed over unto Beth-aven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 6:26; Romans 10:2; Judges 1:28"> And the men of Israel were hard urged that day; and Saul adjured the people, saying, Cursed be the man that will eat food until the evening, until I have been avenged on my enemies. And the whole people tasted thus no food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Numbers 13:27; Deuteronomy 9:28"> And &#91;the men of&#93; all the land came to a forest; and there was honey upon the surface of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:2"> And when the people were come into the forest, behold, there was a stream of honey; but no one put his hand to his mouth; for the people feared the oath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:29; 1 Samuel 14:43; 1 Samuel 30:12"> But Jonathan had not heard his father charging the people with the oath; he therefore put forth the end of the staff that was in his hand, and dipped it in a honey-comb, and carried his hand again to his mouth; and his eyes became clear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:43; 1 Samuel 14:24"> Then commenced one of the people, and said, Thy father strictly charged the people with an oath, saying, Cursed be the man that will eat food this day; though the people were faint.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 18:18; Joshua 7:25"> Then said Jonathan, My father hath troubled the land: see, I pray you, how my eyes become clear, because I have tasted a little of this honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:18"> How much more, if haply the people had eaten freely this day of the spoil of their enemies which they found? for would there not have been now a greater defeat among the Philistines?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 10:12; Joshua 19:42"> And they smote on that day among the Philistines from Michmash to Ayalon; and the people were very faint.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:19; Leviticus 3:17; Leviticus 17:10–14"> And the people flew upon the spoil, and took sheep, and oxen, and young steers, and slew them on the ground: and the people did eat upon the blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Romans 2:1; Matthew 7:5"> And they told Saul, saying, Behold, the people are sinning against the Lord, in eating upon the blood. And he &#91;then&#93; said, Ye have acted treacherously: roll &#91;hither&#93; unto me this day a great stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And Saul said, Disperse yourselves among the people, and say unto them, Bring near unto me every man his ox, and every man his lamb, and slaughter here, and eat; and sin not against the Lord in eating by the blood. And all the people brought near every man his ox by his hand that night, and slaughtered &#91;them&#93; there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:17; 1 Samuel 7:12; 2 Timothy 3:5"> And Saul built an altar unto the Lord: the same was the first altar that he built unto the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 48:1–2; Psalms 73:28; Malachi 2:7"> And Saul said, Let us go down after the Philistines by night, and spoil them until the morning-light, and let us not leave a man of them. And they said, Do whatsoever seemeth good in thy eyes. Then said the priest, Let us draw near hither unto God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:6; 1 Samuel 30:7–8; 1 Samuel 23:9–12"> And Saul asked counsel of God, Shall I go down after the Philistines? wilt thou deliver them into the hand of Israel? But he answered him not on that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Judges 20:2; Joshua 7:14–26; Numbers 24:17"> And Saul said, Draw ye near hither all the chief of the people: and know and see through what this sin hath happened this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:44; 2 Samuel 12:5; 1 Samuel 14:24"> For, as the Lord liveth, who saveth Israel, that if it be in Jonathan my son, he shall surely die. But no one answered him among all the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:15; 1 Samuel 14:7; 1 Samuel 14:36"> Then said he unto all Israel, Ye shall be on one side, and I and Jonathan my son will be on the other side. And the people said unto Saul, Do what seemeth good in thy eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Acts 1:24; Joshua 7:16–18; 1 Samuel 10:20–21"> And Saul said unto the Lord, God of Israel, O, show forth the perfect truth. And Jonathan and Saul were seized; but the people came forth &#91;free&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And Saul said, Cast the lot between me and Jonathan my son. And Jonathan was seized.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Joshua 7:19; 1 Samuel 14:27; Jonah 1:7–10"> Then said Saul to Jonathan, Do tell me what thou hast done. And Jonathan told him, and said, I did but taste with the end of the staff that was in my hand a little honey: lo, I am willing to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:39; Ruth 1:17; 1 Samuel 25:22"> And Saul said, May God do thus now, and in future also; for thou shalt surely die, Jonathan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Luke 21:18; Acts 27:34; 2 Samuel 14:11"> And the people said unto Saul, Shall Jonathan die, who hath wrought this great salvation in Israel? This shall not be: as the Lord liveth, there shall not fall one hair of his head to the ground; for with God hath he wrought this day. So the people rescued Jonathan, and he died not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> Then went Saul up from following the Philistines; and the Philistines went to their own place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:6; 1 Samuel 14:52; 2 Samuel 8:3"> So Saul strengthened himself in the government over Israel; and he fought on every side against all his enemies, against Moab, and against the children of 'Ammon, and against Edom, and against the kings of Zobah, and against the Philistines: and whithersoever he turned himself, he caused terror.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Exodus 17:14; 1 Samuel 15:3–7; Deuteronomy 25:19"> And he gathered an army, and he smote the 'Amalekites, and delivered Israel out of the hands of those that spoiled them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Chronicles 8:33; 1 Samuel 31:2; 2 Samuel 6:20–23"> Now the sons of Saul were Jonathan, and Yishvi, and Malkishua': and the names of his two daughters—the name of the first-born was Merab, and the name of the younger Michal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:8; 1 Samuel 17:55; 2 Samuel 3:27"> And the name of Saul's wife was Achino'am, the daughter of Achima'az: and the name of the captain of his army was Abner, the son of Ner, Saul's uncle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:1; 1 Samuel 9:21"> And Kish the father of Saul, and Ner the father of Abner, were each the son of Abiel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:11; 1 Samuel 8:1"> And the war against the Philistines was violent all the days of Saul: and when Saul saw any strong man, or any valiant man, he took him unto himself.</span>
