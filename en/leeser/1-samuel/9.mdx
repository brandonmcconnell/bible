<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:51; Acts 13:21; 1 Chronicles 9:36–39"> Now there was a man of Benjamin, whose name was Kish, the son of Abiel, the son of Zeror, the son of Bechorath, the son of Aphiach, the son of a Benjamite, a mighty man of valor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 6:2; Numbers 13:33; 1 Samuel 16:7"> And he had a son whose name was Saul, young and handsome; and there was not a man among the children of Israel handsomer than he: from his shoulders and upward he was taller than any of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 5:10; 1 Samuel 10:2; Judges 10:4"> And there were lost the asses belonging to Kish, Saul's father; and Kish said to Saul his son, Do take with thee one of the servants, and arise, go seek the asses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42; Joshua 24:33; John 3:23"> And he passed through the mountain of Ephraim, and passed through the land of Shalisha, but they found &#91;them&#93; not; then they passed through the land of Sha'alim, and there was nothing there; and he passed through the land of Benjamin, but they found them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:2; 1 Samuel 1:1; Luke 12:11"> When they were come in the land of Zuph, Saul said to his servant that was with him, Come, and let us return; lest my father relinquish the care for the asses, and become anxious for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 13:1; Deuteronomy 33:1; 1 Timothy 6:11"> And the other said unto him, Behold now, a man of God is in this city, and the man is honored; all that he ever saith will surely come to pass: now let us go thither; perhaps he can tell us our way that we should go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 14:3; 2 Kings 8:8; 2 Kings 4:42"> Then said Saul to his servant, But, behold, if we should go, what shall we bring to the man? for the bread is spent out of our vessels, and there is not a present to bring to the man of God: what have we with us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the servant answered Saul again, and said, Behold, I have here in my hand the fourth part of a shekel of silver; and I will give this to the man of God, that he may tell us our way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 30:10; 2 Kings 17:13; Amos 7:12"> In former times it was custom in Israel, that when a man went to inquire of God, he said thus, Come, and let us go as far as the seer; for the Prophet of the present day was in former times called a Seer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 5:13–14"> Then said Saul to his servant, Thy word is good: come, let us go. So they went unto the city where the man of God was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 24:11; Exodus 2:16; Genesis 24:18–20"> As they went up the ascent to the city, they found some maidens going out to draw water; and they said unto them, Is the seer here?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 31:54; 1 Samuel 16:2; 1 Samuel 10:5"> And they answered them, and said, He is; behold, he is before you: make haste now, for this day came he to the city; because the people have a sacrifice today on the high-place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 6:11; Matthew 26:26; Luke 24:30"> As soon as ye are come into the city, ye will straightway find him, before yet he can go up to the high-place to eat; for the people will not eat until he be come, because he always blesseth the sacrifice; afterward eat those that are invited; and now go you up; for just today will ye surely find him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And they went up into the city. They were entering into the city, when, behold, Samuel came out toward them, to go up to the high-place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 13:21; 1 Samuel 15:1; Job 33:16"> And the Lord had revealed to Samuel's ear one day before Saul's coming, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:1; Psalms 106:44; Exodus 3:7–9"> About this time tomorrow will I send unto thee a man out of the land of Benjamin, and thou shalt anoint him as chief over my people Israel, that he may save my people out of the hand of the Philistines; for I have beheld my people, because their cry is come unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hosea 13:11; 1 Samuel 16:6–12; Nehemiah 13:25"> And when Samuel saw Saul, the Lord addressed him, Behold, the man of whom I spoke to thee, This one shall rule over my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And Saul drew near to Samuel within the gate, and said, Tell me, I pray thee, where is the house of the seer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:25; John 4:29"> And Samuel answered Saul, and said, I am the seer: go up before me unto the high-place, and ye shall eat with me today; and I will let thee go in the morning, and all that is in thy heart will I tell thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:5; 1 Samuel 12:13; 1 Samuel 9:3"> And as for thy asses that were lost unto thee this day three days ago, do not set thy heart on them; for they have been found. And to whom belongeth all that is desirable in Israel? Is it not to thee, and to all thy father's house?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:17; Judges 20:46–48; Psalms 68:27"> And Saul answered and said, Am not I a son of Benjamin, of one of the smallest tribes of Israel? and &#91;is not&#93; my family the least of all the families of the tribes of Benjamin? wherefore then hast thou spoken to me such thing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 43:32; Luke 14:10"> And Samuel took Saul and his servant, and brought them into the apartment; and he assigned them a place at the head of the invited guests, who were about thirty persons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:5; Genesis 43:34"> And Samuel said unto the cook, Hand here the portion which I gave thee, of which I said unto thee, Put it away by thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:4; Leviticus 7:32–33; Exodus 29:22"> And the cook took up the shoulder, and that which was on it, and set it before Saul; and he said, Behold what is left! set it before thee, and eat; for unto this time hath it been kept from thee, since I said, I have invited the people. And Saul ate with Samuel on that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 10:9; Deuteronomy 22:8; 2 Samuel 11:2"> And they went down from the high-place into the city, and he spoke with Saul upon the roof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 44:4; Joshua 7:13; Judges 19:28"> And they got up early; and it came to pass when the morning-dawn arose, that Samuel called Saul to the roof, saying, Up, that I may send thee away. And Saul arose, and they went out, both of them, he and Samuel, into the street.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 15:14–15; 1 Samuel 20:38–39; 2 Kings 9:5–6"> As they were going down to the end of the city, Samuel said to Saul, Say to the servant that he pass on before us,—and he passed on,—but thou remain standing a while, and I will let thee hear the word of God.</span>
