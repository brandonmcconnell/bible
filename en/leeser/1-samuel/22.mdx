<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 57:1–11; Psalms 142:1–7; 1 Chronicles 11:15"> And David departed thence, and escaped to the cave 'Adullam: and when his brothers and all his father's house heard it, they went down to him thither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 11:28; 1 Samuel 30:22–24; Psalms 72:12–14"> And there gathered themselves unto him every one that was in distress, and every one that had a creditor, and every one that had an embittered spirit; and he became a captain over them: and there were with him about four hundred men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:18; 2 Samuel 15:25–26; Judges 11:29"> And David went thence to Mizpeh of Moab: and he said unto the king of Moab, Let my father and my mother, I pray thee, go forth with you, until I can know what God will do for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 11:1; 1 Chronicles 12:16; 1 Chronicles 29:29"> And he conducted them to the presence of the king of Moab: and they remained with him all the time that David was in the strong-hold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:25; 1 Chronicles 21:9; 1 Chronicles 29:29"> And the prophet Gad said unto David, Thou must not remain in the strong-hold: depart, and get thee into the land of Judah. Then David departed, and came into the forest of Chereth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:9; 1 Samuel 18:10; 1 Samuel 20:33"> And Saul heard that David was discovered, and the men that were with him, &#91;now Saul was sitting in Gib'ah under the tamarisk on the hill, with his spear in his hand, and all his servants were standing about him;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 12:16; 1 Samuel 8:14–15; 1 Samuel 22:13"> Then said Saul unto his servants that stood about him, Hear, I pray you, men of Benjamin! will the son of Jesse give, indeed, to every one of you fields and vineyards? will he appoint you all captains of thousands, and captains of hundreds?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:3; 1 Samuel 23:21; 1 Samuel 20:13–17"> That ye have conspired, all of you, against me, and there is none that informeth me, while my son hath made a covenant with the son of Jesse, and there is none of you that is concerned for me, or informeth me that my son hath stirred up my servant to lie in wait against me, as it is this day?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 52:1–9; 1 Samuel 14:3; Ezekiel 22:9"> Then answered Doeg the Edomite, who was set over the servants of Saul, and said, I saw the son of Jesse coming to Nob, to Achimelech the son of Achitub.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 27:21; 1 Samuel 30:8; 1 Samuel 23:4"> And he asked counsel for him of the Lord, and he gave him provision, and gave him also the sword of Goliath the philistine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 3:15"> Then sent the king to call Achimelech, the son of Achitub, the priest, and all his father's house, the priests that were in Nob: and they came, all of them, to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:6; 1 Samuel 22:7; Isaiah 65:1"> And Saul said, Hear now, thou son of Achitub. And he said, here am I, my Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:8; Luke 23:2–5; Amos 7:10"> And Saul said unto him, Why have ye conspired against me, thou and the son of Jesse, in that thou didst give him bread, and a sword, and hast asked counsel for him of God, that he should rise to lie in wait against me, as it is this day!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:32; 1 Samuel 19:4–5; 1 Samuel 22:13"> Then answered Achimelech the king, and said, And who is among all thy servants so trusted as David, and the king's son-in-law, and freely admitted to thy private council, and is honored in thy house?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:36; 1 Peter 3:16–17; 2 Corinthians 1:12"> Did I this day then begin to ask counsel for him of God? far be it from me; let not the king impute any thing unto his servant, &#91;nor&#93; to all the house of my father; for thy servant knew not of all this, either a little or great thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 28:15; Daniel 3:19–20; 1 Kings 19:2"> And the king said, Thou shalt surely die, Achimelech, thou, and all thy father's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 1:17; 1 Samuel 14:45; 2 Kings 1:13–14"> And the king said unto the runners that stood about him, Turn round and slay the priests of the Lord; because their hand also is with David, and because they knew that he was fleeing, and did not disclose it to me. But the servants of the king would not stretch forth their hand to fall upon the priests of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 26:10–11; Hosea 7:3; 1 Samuel 2:18"> And the king said to Doeg, Turn thou round, and fall upon the priests. And Doeg the Edomite turned round, and he fell upon the priests, and slew on that day eighty and five persons that did wear a linen ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:3; Hosea 10:14; 1 Samuel 21:1"> And Nob, the city of the priests, he smote with the edge of the sword, both men and women, children and sucklings, and oxen, and asses, and lambs, with the edge of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:6; 1 Samuel 30:7; 1 Kings 2:26–27"> And there escaped one of the sons of Achimelech the son of Achitub, whose name was Ebyathar, and he fled after David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And Ebyathar told David that Saul had slain the priests of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:1–9; Psalms 44:22"> And David said unto Ebyathar, I knew on that day, because Doeg the Edomite was there, that he would surely tell Saul: I have myself occasioned the death of all the persons of thy father's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 2:26; Hebrews 12:1–3; John 10:28–30"> Remain thou with me, fear nothing; for he that will seek my life will seek thy life; but thou shalt be well guarded with me.</span>
