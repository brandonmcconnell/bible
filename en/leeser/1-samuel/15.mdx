<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:16; 1 Samuel 12:14; Psalms 2:10–11"> And Samuel said unto Saul, Me did the Lord send to anoint thee as king over his people, over Israel; and now hearken thou unto the voice of the words of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:17–19; Exodus 17:8–16; Numbers 24:20"> Thus hath said the Lord of hosts, I remember what Amalek did to Israel, how he lay in wait for him on the way, when he came up from Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:16–18; Numbers 24:20; Numbers 31:17"> Now go and smite 'Amalek, and devote all that they have, and spare them not; but slay both man and woman, infant and suckling, ox and lamb, camel and ass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 15:24; 1 Samuel 11:8; 1 Samuel 13:15"> And Saul ordered the people to assemble, and he numbered them in Telaim, two hundred thousand men on foot, and ten thousand of the men of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And Saul came to the city of 'Amalek, and he fought in the valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 1:16; Numbers 10:29–32; Exodus 18:19"> And Saul said unto the Kenites, Go, depart, get you down from the midst of the 'Amalekites, lest I destroy you with them; whereas ye acted kindly with all the children of Israel, at their coming up out of Egypt. And the Kenites departed from the midst of the 'Amalekites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 16:7; Genesis 25:18; 1 Samuel 14:48"> And Saul smote the 'Amalekites from Chavilah until thou comest to Shur, that is before Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:1; Esther 3:1; Numbers 24:7"> And he caught Agag the king of the 'Amalekites alive, and all the people he devoted to the edge of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:15; 1 Samuel 15:19; 1 Samuel 15:3"> But Saul together with the people had pity on Agag, and on the best of the flocks, and of the oxen, and of the fatlings, and the fat lambs, and all that was good, and they would not destroy them; but all the cattle that was of little value and weak, that they destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And the word of the Lord came unto Samuel, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:13; Genesis 6:6; 2 Samuel 24:16"> I repent that I have set up Saul as king; for he hath turned back from following me, and my word hath he not performed: and it displeased Samuel, and he cried unto the Lord all the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 15:55; 2 Samuel 18:18; 1 Samuel 25:2"> And Samuel rose up early to meet Saul in the morning; and it was told to Samuel, saying, Saul came to Carmel, and, behold, he set himself up a monument, and then went about, and passed on, and went down to Gilgal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 17:2; Ruth 3:10; Proverbs 31:31"> And Samuel came to Saul; and Saul said unto him, Blessed be thou unto the Lord, I have performed the word of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 3:13–15; Jeremiah 2:18–19; Jeremiah 2:34–37"> And Samuel said, What is then this bleating of the flocks in my ears, and the lowing of the oxen which I hear?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:21; 1 Samuel 15:9; Exodus 32:22–23"> And Saul said, From the 'Amalekites have they brought them; because the people had pity on the best of the flocks and of the oxen, in order to sacrifice unto the Lord thy God; and the rest have we destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:27; 1 Kings 22:16; 1 Samuel 12:7"> And Samuel said unto Saul, Stay, and I will tell thee what the Lord said to me this night: and he said unto him, Speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:22; 1 Samuel 9:21; 1 Samuel 15:1–3"> And Samuel said, Is it not that, however little thou wast in thy own eyes, thou art the head of the tribes of Israel? and the Lord anointed thee as king over Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 10:29; Genesis 13:13; Genesis 15:16"> And the Lord sent thee on a journey, and said, Go and destroy the sinners, the 'Amalekites, and thou shalt fight against them until they be consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:32; 2 Chronicles 33:6; 2 Chronicles 36:12"> Wherefore then didst thou not hearken unto the voice of the Lord, and didst fly upon the spoil, and didst the evil in the eyes of the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:13; Matthew 19:20; Romans 10:3"> And Saul said unto Samuel, Yea, I have fully hearkened unto the voice of the Lord; and I went on the way which the Lord had sent me; and I have brought Agag the king of 'Amalek; and the 'Amalekites have I destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:15; Exodus 32:22–23; Genesis 3:13"> And the people took of the spoil, of the flocks and oxen, the chief of the devoted things, to sacrifice unto the Lord thy God in Gilgal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 6:6; Psalms 51:16–17; Proverbs 21:3"> And Samuel said, Hath the Lord as much delight in burnt-offerings and in sacrifices, as in obeying the voice of the Lord? Behold, to obey is better than sacrifice, and to attend more than the fat of the rams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 28:16; 1 Samuel 13:14; Leviticus 20:6"> For the sin of witchcraft is rebellion, and idolatry and image-worship, stubbornness; inasmuch as thou hast despised the word of the Lord, he hath also despised thee that thou shalt not be king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:13; Isaiah 51:12–13; Numbers 22:34"> And Saul said unto Samuel, I have sinned; for I have transgressed the will of the Lord, and thy words; because I feared the people, and I hearkened to their voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 10:17"> And now, I pray thee, pardon my sin, and return with me, that I may prostrate myself to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hosea 4:6; 1 Samuel 13:14; 1 Samuel 16:1"> And Samuel said unto Saul, I will not return with thee; for thou didst despise the word of the Lord, and the Lord hath despised thee, that thou shalt not be king over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Kings 11:30–31"> And Samuel turned about to go: and he laid hold on the corner of his mantle, and it was rent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:17–18; Acts 13:22; Daniel 4:32"> And Samuel said unto him, The Lord hath rent the kingdom of Israel from off thee this day, and hath given it to thy associate, who is better than thou.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:14; Numbers 23:19; Titus 1:2"> And also the Strength of Israel will not lie nor repent; for he is not a man, that he should repent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 5:44; John 12:43; Isaiah 29:13"> And he said, I have sinned; &#91;yet&#93; honor me now, I pray thee, in the presence of the elders of my people, and in the presence of Israel, and return with me, that I may prostrate myself unto the Lord thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> So Samuel returned, following Saul; and Saul prostrated himself to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Revelation 18:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Jeremiah 48:44"> And Samuel said, Bring ye hither unto me Agag the king of the 'Amalekites: and Agag came unto him cheerfully; and Agag said, Surely the bitterness of death is past.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Judges 1:7; Genesis 9:6; Matthew 7:2"> And Samuel said, As thy sword did make women childless, so shall thy mother be childless among women; and Samuel hewed Agag in pieces before the Lord in Gilgal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:4; 1 Samuel 7:17"> Then Samuel went to Ramah; and Saul went up to his house at Gib'ah of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:11; 1 Samuel 19:24; 1 Samuel 16:1"> And Samuel did not see Saul any more until the day of his death; because Samuel mourned for Saul; and the Lord repented that he had made Saul king over Israel.</span>
