<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Psalms 15:5; 1 Timothy 6:10"> But his sons walked not in his ways, and they inclined after their own advantage, and took bribes, and perverted justice.</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:11; James 1:5; 1 Samuel 12:17"> But the thing was displeasing in the eyes of Samuel, when they said, Give us a king to judge us. And Samuel prayed unto the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 10:16; 1 Samuel 10:19; Exodus 16:8"> And the Lord said unto Samuel, Hearken unto the voice of the people in all that they may say unto thee; for not thee have they rejected, but me have they rejected, that I should not reign over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 106:34–40; Exodus 32:1; Judges 13:1"> In accordance with all the deeds which they have done since the day that I brought them up out of Egypt even until this day, when they forsook me, and served other gods: so do they also unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:25; 1 Samuel 8:11–18; Ezekiel 46:18"> And now hearken unto their voice: nevertheless thou must still solemnly forewarn them, and tell them the manner of the king that will reign over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Samuel said all the words of the Lord unto the people that had asked of him a king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:52; Deuteronomy 17:14–20; 1 Samuel 10:25"> And he said, This will be the manner of the king that will reign over you: Your sons will he take, and appoint them for himself with his chariots, and among his horsemen; and they will have to run before his chariot;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:7; 1 Kings 4:7; 1 Chronicles 27:1–22"> And to appoint for himself captains over thousands, and captains over fifties; and to plough his ground, and to reap his harvest, and to make his instruments of war, and the instruments of his chariots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And your daughters will he take for ointment makers, and for cooks, and for bakers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:18; 1 Kings 21:7; 1 Kings 21:19"> And your fields, and your vineyards, and your olive-yards, yea the best, will he take, and give them to his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 1:3; Isaiah 39:7; Genesis 37:36"> And of your seeds, and of your vineyards will he take the tenth, and give &#91;the same&#93; to his officers, and to his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And your men-servants, and your maid-servants, and your best young men, and your asses will he take, and employ &#91;them&#93; for his work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Of your flocks will he take the tenth: and ye yourselves will become his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Micah 3:4; Isaiah 1:15; Proverbs 1:25–28"> And ye will cry out on that day because of your king whom ye will have chosen for yourselves, but the Lord will not answer you on that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:16; Jeremiah 7:13; Isaiah 66:4"> Nevertheless the people refused to listen to the voice of Samuel; and they said, No; but a king shall be over us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:5; Psalms 106:35; Deuteronomy 7:6"> That we also may ourselves be like all the nations: and that our king may judge us, and go out before us, and fight our battles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 11:11"> And Samuel heard all the words of the people, and he spoke them before the ears of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:7; Hosea 13:11"> And the Lord said to Samuel, Hearken unto their voice, and appoint them a king. And Samuel said unto the men of Israel, Go ye every man unto his city.</span>
