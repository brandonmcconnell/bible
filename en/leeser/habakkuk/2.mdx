<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 30:8; Revelation 14:13; Revelation 1:18–19"> And the Lord answered me, and said, Write down the vision, and make it plain upon the tables, that everyone may read it fluently.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 27:14; Hebrews 10:36–37; Ezekiel 12:25"> For there is yet a vision for the appointed time, and it speaketh of the end, and it will not deceive: though it tarry, wait for it; because it will surely come, it will not be delayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:17; Hebrews 10:38; Galatians 3:11–12"> Behold, disturbed, not at rest is the soul of &#91;the wicked&#93; in him; but the righteous ever liveth in his &#91;trustful&#93; faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 5:11–12; Proverbs 27:20; Proverbs 20:1"> And though the wine-&#91;drunken&#93; traitor, the proud man, whose house will not stand, who enlargeth his desire as the grave, and is like death, which cannot be satisfied,—though he gather unto him all the nations, and assemble unto him all the people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 23:7; Micah 2:4; Jeremiah 50:13"> Will not all these take up a parable against him, and a proverb and a satire concerning him? and they will say, Woe to him that increaseth what is not his! for how long? and to him that loadeth himself with a burden of guilt!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 29:1; Isaiah 47:11; Jeremiah 8:17"> Behold, suddenly will rise up those that afflict thee, and awake those that plague thee, and thou shalt become a booty unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 33:1; Habakkuk 2:17; Jeremiah 27:7"> Because thou hast despoiled many nations will all the remnant of the people despoil thee; because of the blood of men, and the violence against the land, the town, and all that dwell therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:16; Jeremiah 22:13–19; Isaiah 28:15"> Woe to him that obtaineth an evil gain for his house, that he may set his nest on high, that he may be delivered from the grasp of the wicked!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Nahum 1:14; 2 Kings 9:26; Jeremiah 36:31"> Thou hast counselled shame to thy house, by cutting off many people, and sinning &#91;against&#93; thy soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:40; Joshua 24:27; Job 31:38–40"> For the stone will cry out of the wall, and the beam out of the wood &#91;-work&#93; will answer it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 3:10; Daniel 4:27–31; Nahum 3:1"> Woe to him that buildeth a city with blood-guiltiness, and layeth the foundation of a town by wrong-doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:58; Isaiah 50:11; Proverbs 21:30"> Behold, is it not from the Lord of hosts that people shall labor for the very fire, and nations shall weary themselves for naught but vanity!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 11:9; Psalms 22:27; Psalms 72:19"> For the earth shall be filled with knowledge of the glory of the Lord, as the waters cover the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:7; Genesis 9:22; Revelation 18:3"> Woe unto him that maketh his neighbors drink, &#91;to thee&#93; that pourest out thy poisonous &#91;wine&#93;, and makest them also drunken, in order to look on their nakedness!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 47:3; Nahum 3:5–6; Isaiah 51:21–23"> Thou art filled with shame instead of glory; drink thou also, and let thy nakedness be uncovered: there shall be turned around thee the cup of the Lord's right hand, and filthy spittle shall be on thy glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:8; Psalms 55:23; Zechariah 11:1"> For the violence against Lebanon shall cover thee, and the destruction of beasts, which he terrified away; because of the blood of men, and the violence against the land, the town, and all that dwell therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:8; 1 Corinthians 12:2; Isaiah 44:9–10"> What profiteth the graven image that its maker hath graven it? the molten image, and a teacher of falsehood? that the maker of his image trusteth therein, while making dumb idols?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 135:17; Jeremiah 10:4; 1 Kings 18:26–29"> Woe unto him that saith to the wood, Awake! Rouse up to the dumb stone. Shall this teach? Behold, it is overlaid with gold and silver, and no breath whatever is in its bosom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Zechariah 2:13; Zephaniah 1:7; Psalms 46:10"> But the Lord is in his holy temple: be silent before him all the earth.</span>
