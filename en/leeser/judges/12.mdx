<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 11:12–33"> And Yiphthach said unto them, I and my people were greatly engaged in strife with the children of 'Ammon; and I called you, but ye helped me not out of their hand.</span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:10; Deuteronomy 3:12–17; Proverbs 15:1"> Then Yiphthach gathered together all the men of Gil'ad, and fought with Ephraim; and the men of Gil'ad smote Ephraim, because they said, Ye are fugitives of Ephraim: Gil'ad is in the midst, between Ephraim and Menasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 3:28; Joshua 22:11; Joshua 2:7"> And the Gil'adites seized on the passages of the Jordan before the Ephraimites: and it came to pass, when the Ephraimites who had escaped said, Let me pass over; that the men of Gil'ad said unto him, Art thou an Ephrathite? and if he said, No;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 69:2; Psalms 69:15; Mark 14:70"> Then said they unto him, Do say, Shibboleth; but when he said, Sibboleth, and was not able to pronounce it correctly, they laid hold of him and slew him on the passages of the Jordan; and there fell at that time of the Ephraimites forty and two thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And Yiphthach judged Israel six years; and then died Yiphthach the Gil'adite, and was buried in &#91;one of&#93; the cities of Gil'ad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Micah 5:2; 1 Samuel 16:1; Matthew 2:1"> And after him there judged Israel Ibzan of Beth-lechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 10:4; Judges 12:14"> And he had thirty sons; and thirty daughters he sent abroad, and thirty daughters he brought in for his sons from abroad: and he judged Israel seven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then died Ibzan, and was buried at Beth-lechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And after him there judged Israel Elon the Zebulonite; and he judged Israel ten years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 8:13; 1 Chronicles 6:69; Joshua 19:42"> Then died Elon the Zebulonite, and was buried in Ayalon in the country of Zebulun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And after him there judged Israel 'Abdon the son of Hillel the Pir'athonite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 5:10; Judges 10:4"> And he had forty sons and thirty grandsons, that rode on seventy ass-colts; and he judged Israel eight years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 5:14; Judges 3:13; Judges 3:27"> Then died 'Abdon the son of Hillel the Pir'athonite, and was buried in Pir'athon in the land of Ephraim, in the mountain of the 'Amalekites.</span>
