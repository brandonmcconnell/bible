<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 18:1; Judges 17:8; Malachi 2:15"> And it came to pass in those days, when there was no king in Israel, that there was a certain Levite sojourning on the lower edge of the mountain of Ephraim, who took to himself a concubine out of Beth-lechem-judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:28; Deuteronomy 22:21; Leviticus 21:9"> And his concubine became faithless unto him, and she went away from him unto her father's house to Beth-lechem-judah, and was there one year and four months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 34:3; Genesis 50:21; Leviticus 19:17"> And then her husband arose, and went after her, to speak friendly unto her, to bring her back; and he had his servant with him, and a couple of asses: and she brought him into her father's house; and when the father of the damsel saw him, he rejoiced to meet him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And his father-in-law, the damsel's father, detained him; and he abode with him three days: and they ate and drank, and lodged there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 19:8; Genesis 18:5; Psalms 104:15"> And it came to pass on the fourth day, that they arose early in the morning, and he rose up to depart; but the damsel's father said unto his son-in-law, Comfort thy heart with a morsel of bread, and afterward can ye go your way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 19:9; Judges 16:25; Ruth 3:7"> And they sat down, and both of them ate together and drank; and the damsel's father said unto the man, Do consent, I pray thee, and tarry all night, and let thy heart be merry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Still the man rose up to depart; but his father-in-law urged him, and he turned back and lodged there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And when he arose early in the morning on the fifth day to depart, the damsel's father said, Comfort thy heart, I pray thee, and tarry until the decline of the day: and both of them did eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; Luke 24:29; James 4:13–14"> And when the man rose up to depart, he, and his concubine, and his servant, his father-in-law, the damsel's father, said unto him, Behold, now the day draweth toward evening, tarry all night, I pray you: behold, it is the resting time of day, lodge here, and let thy heart be merry; and you may get early tomorrow on your way, and go then to thy tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 15:63; Joshua 15:8; Judges 1:8"> But the man would not tarry that night, but he rose up and went away, and came as far as opposite Jebus, which is Jerusalem; and he had with him two saddled asses, and his concubine also was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:6; Judges 1:21; Judges 19:10"> When they were by Jebus, the day was far spent; and the servant said unto his master, Come, I pray thee, and let us turn in unto this city of the Jebusites, and lodge in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And his master said unto him, We will not turn into one of the cities of the stranger, that are not belonging to the children of Israel; but we will pass on as far as Gib'ah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:26; Hosea 5:8; Isaiah 10:29"> And he said unto his servant, Come, and let us draw near to one of these places; and let us lodge all night in Gib'ah, or in Ramah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And they passed on and went forward; and the sun went down unto them by Gib'ah, which belongeth to Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 19:18; Matthew 25:35; Matthew 25:43"> And they turned aside thither, to go in to lodge in Gib'ah; and when he went in, he sat down in the street of the city; for there was no man that brought them into his house to lodge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 19:1; Psalms 104:23; Genesis 3:19"> And, behold, an old man was coming from his work out of the field at evening, and this man was from the mountain of Ephraim, and he sojourned in Gib'ah; but the men of the place were Benjamites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 32:17; Genesis 16:8"> And he lifted up his eyes, and saw the wayfaring man in the street of the city: and then said the old man, Whither goest thou? and whence comest thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 18:31; John 15:6; 1 Samuel 1:7"> And he said unto him, We are passing from Beth-lechem-judah toward the lower edge of the mountain of Ephraim; from there am I, and I went as far as Beth-lechem-judah; but I am going to the house of the Lord; and there is no man that bringeth me into his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Yet there is both straw and provender for our asses; and there are also bread and wine for me, and for thy handmaid, and for the young man who is with thy servants; there is no want of any thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 6:23; James 2:15–16; Hebrews 13:2"> And the old man said, Peace be with thee; only let all thy wants lie upon me; at least lodge not in the street.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 43:24; Genesis 18:4; Luke 7:44"> So he brought him to his house, and gave provender unto the asses: and they washed their feet, and they ate and drank.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 19:4–5; 1 Corinthians 6:9; Judges 20:5"> As they were making their hearts merry, behold, the men of the city, worthless people, beset the house round about, knocking at the door; and they said to the master of the house, the old man, thus, Bring forth the man that in come to thy house, that we may know him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 34:7; 2 Samuel 13:12; Judges 20:6"> And the man, the master of the house, went out unto them, and said unto them, No, my brethren, I pray you, act not wickedly; since this man is once come into my house, do not this scandalous thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 19:8; Deuteronomy 21:14; Genesis 34:2"> Behold, here is my daughter a virgin, and his concubine; let me bring them out now, and humble ye them, and do to them what seemeth good in your eyes; but unto this man do not this scandalous thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hosea 10:9; Hosea 9:9; Hosea 7:4–7"> But the men would not hearken to him; so the man took hold of his concubine, and brought her forth unto them into the street; and they knew her, and ill-used her all the night until the morning; and they let her go when the day began to dawn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Judges 19:3; Genesis 18:12; 1 Peter 3:6"> Then came the woman &#91;home&#93; in the early part of the morning, and fell down at the door of the man's house where her lord was, &#91;and lay&#93; till it was light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And when her lord rose up in the morning, and opened the doors of the house, and went out to go on his way: behold, the woman, his concubine, was lying at the door of the house, with her hands upon the threshold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 20:5; 1 Kings 18:29"> And he said unto her, Rise up, and let us be going; but no one answered. Then he took her upon the ass, and the man rose up, and went unto his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 20:6–7; 1 Samuel 11:7; Romans 10:2"> And when he was come into his house, he took a knife, and laid hold on his concubine, and divided her, according to her bones, into twelve pieces, and sent her about in all the territory of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 20:7; Proverbs 13:10; Hosea 9:9"> And it happened, that whoever saw it said, There hath no such deed been done or seen from the day that the children of Israel came up out of the land of Egypt until this day: reflect well on it, give advice, and speak.</span>
