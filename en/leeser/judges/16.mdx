<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 15:47; Ezra 9:1–2; Genesis 38:16–18"> Then went Samson to Gazzah, and saw there a harlot, and went in unto her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 118:10–12; 1 Samuel 23:26; Acts 9:24"> And it was told to the Gazzites, saying, Samson is come hither: and they compassed him in, and lay in wait for him all the night in the gate of the city, and held themselves quiet all the night, saying, By the time it is light in the morning will we kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Psalms 107:16; Isaiah 63:1–5"> And Samson lay till midnight; but he arose at midnight, and took hold of the doors of the city-gate, and the two door-posts, and tore them away with the bolt, and put them upon his shoulders, and carried them up to the top of the mount that is before Hebron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:6; Proverbs 22:14; Nehemiah 13:26"> And it came to pass after this, that he loved a woman in the valley of Shorek, whose name was Delilah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 13:3; Judges 14:15; 1 Timothy 6:9–10"> And the lords of the Philistines came up unto her, and said unto her, Persuade him, and see wherein his great strength lieth, and by what means we may prevail over him, that we may bind him to subdue him: and we will give thee every one of us eleven hundred pieces of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 7:21; Proverbs 6:26; Proverbs 22:14"> And Delilah said to Samson, Tell me, I pray thee, wherein thy great strength lieth, and wherewith thou canst be bound to subdue thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:2–3; Romans 3:8; 1 Samuel 27:10"> And Samson said unto her, If they bind me with seven moist cords which have not yet been dried, then shall I become weak, and be like any other of mankind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26"> And the lords of the Philistines brought up to her seven moist cords which had not yet been dried, and she bound him with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 58:9"> And she had men lying in wait, sitting near her in the chamber. And she said unto him, The Philistines are upon thee, Samson. And he tore the cords, as a thread of tow is torn when it toucheth the fire; and his strength was not perceived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 16:13; Luke 22:48; Judges 16:7"> And Delilah said unto Samson, Behold, thou hast deceived me, and told me lies; now do tell me, I pray thee, wherewith thou canst be bound.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 13:5; Ephesians 4:25; Proverbs 13:3"> And he said unto her, If they bind me fast with new ropes that have never been used in work, then shall I become weak, and be like any other of mankind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And Delilah took new ropes, and bound him therewith, and said unto him, The Philistines are upon thee, Samson. And the liers in wait were sitting in the chamber. But he tore them from off his arms like a thread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And Delilah said unto Samson, Hitherto thou hast deceived me, and told me lies; do tell me wherewith thou canst be bound. And he said unto her, If thou weavest the seven locks of my head with the web.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 106:43; Ezra 9:13–14"> And she fastened it with the pin, and said unto him, The Philistines are upon thee, Samson. And he awakened out of his sleep, and tore away the pin of the loom, with the web.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 14:16; 1 John 2:15–16; John 14:21–24"> And she said unto him, How canst thou say, I love thee, when thy heart is not with me? these three times hast thou deceived me, and hast not told me wherein thy great strength lieth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 7:21–23; Luke 18:5; Mark 14:24"> And it came to pass, when she worried him daily with her words, and urged him, that his soul became impatient to die;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Micah 7:5; Judges 13:5; Proverbs 12:23"> And he told her all his heart, and said unto her, A razor hath not passed over my head; for a Nazarite of God have I been from my mother's womb; if I were shaved, my strength would depart from me, and I should become weak, and be like all other men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 26:15; Judges 16:5; 1 Timothy 6:10"> And when Delilah saw that he had told her all his heart, she sent and called for the lords of the Philistines, saying, Come up this once; for he hath told me all his heart. Then came the lords of the Philistines up unto her, and brought the money in their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 7:26–27; Proverbs 7:21–23; Ecclesiastes 7:26"> And she made him sleep upon her knees; and she called a man, and caused him to shave off the seven locks of his head; and she began to subdue him, and his strength departed from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 14:42–43; 1 Samuel 16:14; Joshua 7:12"> And she said, The Philistines are upon thee, Samson. And he awoke out of his sleep, and thought, I will go out as at other times before, and shake myself free. But he knew not that the Lord had departed from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 2:19; Proverbs 14:14; Isaiah 47:2"> And the Philistines seized him, and put out his eyes, and brought him down to Gazzah, and bound him with fetters of copper; and he had to grind in the prison-house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 107:13–14; Psalms 106:44–45; Leviticus 26:44"> But the hair of his head began to grow again after it was shaved off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:2–5; Proverbs 24:17; Psalms 35:15–16"> And the lords of the Philistines gathered themselves together to offer a great sacrifice unto Dagon their god, and to rejoice; and they said, Our god hath delivered into our hand Samson our enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 5:4; Daniel 5:23; Judges 15:16"> And when the people saw him, they praised their god; for they said, Our god hath delivered into our hand our enemy, and the destroyer of our country, and him who hath slain so many of us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:28; Judges 19:6; Judges 9:27"> And it came to pass, when their heart was merry, that they said, Call for Samson, that he may make sport for us. And they called for Samson out of the prison-house; and he made sport before them; and they placed him between the pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And Samson said unto the lad that held him by the hand, Suffer me &#91;to go&#93; and let me feel the pillars whereupon the house is supported, that I may lean upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:8; 2 Samuel 11:2; Joshua 2:8"> Now the house was full of men and women; and there were all the lords of the Philistines; and upon the roof were about three thousand men and women, that looked on while Samson made sport.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:15; Lamentations 3:31–32; Psalms 143:12"> And Samson called unto the Lord, and said, O Lord Eternal, remember me, I pray thee, and do thou strengthen me only this once, O God, that I may be avenged for one of my two eyes on the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And Samson threw his arms around the two middle pillars upon which the house was supported, and he leaned on them, &#91;on&#93; one with his right hand, and &#91;on&#93; the other with his left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Philippians 2:8; Matthew 16:25; Matthew 24:38–39"> And Samson said, Let me die with the Philistines. And he bent &#91;them&#93; with might, and the house fell upon the lords, and upon all the people that were therein. So the dead whom he slew at his death were more than those whom he had slain in his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 15:20; Judges 13:25; Judges 13:2"> Then came down his brothers and all the house of his father, and they took him up, and carried him up, and buried him between Zor'ah and Eshtaol, in the burying-place of Manoach his father. And he had judged Israel twenty years.</span>
