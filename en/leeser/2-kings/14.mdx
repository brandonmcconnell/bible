<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 13:10; 2 Chronicles 25:1–4; 2 Kings 14:15"> In the second year of Joash the son of Joachaz the king of Israel became Amazyahu, the son of Joash the king of Judah, king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Twenty and five years was he old when he became king, and twenty and nine years did he reign in Jerusalem. And his mother's name was Yeho'addan of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:2–3; Zechariah 1:4–6; 1 Kings 11:4"> And he did what is right in the eyes of the Lord, yet not like David his father: in accordance with all that Joash his father had done, &#91;so&#93; did he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 12:3; 2 Kings 16:4; 2 Kings 15:35"> Nevertheless the high-places were not removed: as yet the people sacrificed and burnt incense on the high-places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:3–4; 2 Kings 12:20–21; Numbers 35:33"> And it came to pass, when the kingdom was firmly established in his hand, that he slew his servants who had slain the king his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:16; Ezekiel 18:20; Ezekiel 18:4"> But the children of the murderers he put not to death: as it is written in the book of the law of Moses, that the Lord commanded, saying, The fathers shall not be put to death for the children, nor shall the children be put to death for the fathers; but every man shall be put to death for his own sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:13; 1 Chronicles 18:12; Joshua 15:38"> He it was that smote of Edom in the valley of salt ten thousand men, and seized Sela' in the war, and called its name Yoktheel until this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:17–24; 2 Samuel 2:14–17; 2 Kings 14:11"> Then sent Amazyah messengers to Jehoash, the son of Jehoachaz the son of Jehu, the king of Israel, saying, Come, let us look one another in the face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 9:8–15; 1 Kings 4:33; 2 Chronicles 33:11"> And Jehoash the king of Israel sent to Amazyahu the king of Judah, saying, The thornbush that was in the Lebanon sent to the cedar that was in the Lebanon, saying, Give thy daughter to my son for wife. And there passed along the wild beasts that were in Lebanon, and trod down the thornbush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:16; Deuteronomy 8:14; 2 Chronicles 32:25"> Thou hast indeed smitten Edom, and thy heart hath lifted thee up: keep thy glory and stay in thy house; and why wilt thou meddle with misfortune, that thou mayest fall, thou, and Judah with thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 19:38; 2 Chronicles 25:16; Joshua 15:10"> But Amazyahu would not hear. Therefore Jehoash the king of Israel went up; and he and Amazyahu the king of Judah looked one another in the face at Beth-shemesh, which belongeth to Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:17; 1 Samuel 4:10; 1 Kings 22:36"> And Judah was defeated before Israel; and they fled every man to his tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:16; Nehemiah 12:39; Zechariah 14:10"> And Jehoash the king of Israel caught Amazyahu the king of Judah, the son of Jehoash, the son of Achazyahu, at Beth-shemesh; and he came to Jerusalem, and made a breach in the wall of Jerusalem from the gate of Ephraim unto the corner-gate, four hundred cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 14:26; 1 Kings 7:51; 1 Kings 15:18"> And he took all the gold and the silver, and all the vessels that were found in the house of the Lord, and in the treasures of the king's house, and the children of the chiefs as hostages, and returned to Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 13:12–13; 1 Kings 14:19–20; 2 Kings 10:34–35"> Now the rest of the acts of Jehoash which he did, and his mighty deeds, and how he fought with Amazyahu the king of Judah, behold, they are written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 13:9; 2 Kings 13:13; 1 Kings 1:21"> And Jehoash slept with his fathers, and was buried in Samaria with the kings of Israel: and Jerobo'am his son became king in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:25–28; 2 Kings 13:10; 2 Kings 14:23"> And Amazyahu the son of Joash the king of Judah lived after the death of Jehoash, the son of Jehoachaz the king of Israel, fifteen years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 11:41; 1 Kings 14:29; 2 Kings 13:8"> And the rest of the acts of Amazyahu, behold, they are written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 10:31; 2 Kings 15:25; 2 Kings 12:20–21"> Now they raised a conspiracy against him in Jerusalem: wherefore he fled to Lachish; but they sent after him to Lachish, and slew him there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 9:28; 2 Kings 12:21; 2 Chronicles 33:20"> And they brought him on horses: and he was buried at Jerusalem with his fathers in the city of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 21:24; Matthew 1:8–9; 2 Kings 15:13"> And all the people of Judah took 'Azaryah, who was sixteen years old, and they made him king instead of his father Amazyahu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Kings 16:6; 1 Kings 9:26; Deuteronomy 2:8"> He it was that built Elath, and brought it back to Judah, after the king slept with his fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hosea 1:1; Amos 7:9–11; Amos 1:1"> In the fifteenth year of Amazyahu the son of Joash the king of Judah became Jerobo'am the son of Joash the king of Israel king in Samaria, &#91;for&#93; forty and one years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Kings 13:6; 2 Kings 13:11; Genesis 38:7"> And he did what is evil in the eyes of the Lord: he departed not from all the sins of Jerobo'am the son of Nebat, who induced Israel to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:17; Matthew 12:39–40; Joshua 19:13"> He restored the boundary of Israel from the entrance of Chamath unto the sea of the plain; in accordance with the word of the Lord God of Israel, which he had spoken by means of his servant Jonah the son of Amitthai, the prophet, who was of Gathchepher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:36; Exodus 3:7; 2 Kings 13:4"> For the Lord saw the affliction of Israel, which was very bitter; that the guarded was no more, and that the fortified was no more, and there was no helper for Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 13:23; 2 Kings 13:5; Romans 11:2–12"> And the Lord had not spoken that he would blot out the name of Israel from under the heavens; but he helped them by means of Jerobo'am the son of Joash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Kings 11:24; 1 Chronicles 18:5–6; 2 Samuel 8:6"> Now the rest of the acts of Jerobo'am, and all that he did, and his mighty deeds, how he warred, and how he brought back Damascus and Chamath, &#91;which had belonged&#93; to Judah, to Israel, behold, they are written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Kings 15:8"> And Jerobo'am slept with his fathers, with the kings of Israel: and Zechariah his son became king in his stead.</span>
