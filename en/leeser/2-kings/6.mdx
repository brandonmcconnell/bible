<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 2:3; 2 Kings 4:1; Joshua 17:14"> And the sons of the prophets said unto Elisha', Behold now, the place where we dwell before thee is too narrow for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 21:3; Acts 18:3; 1 Thessalonians 2:9"> Let us go, we pray thee, as far as the Jordan, and take thence every man one beam, and let us prepare for us there a place to dwell therein. And he said, Go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 6:28; Judges 19:6; 2 Kings 5:23"> And one said, Give thy assent, I pray thee, and go with thy servants. And he said, I will indeed go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:5; Deuteronomy 29:11"> So he went with them; and they came to the Jordan, and they cut down trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 37:21; Ecclesiastes 10:10; 2 Kings 6:15"> But as one was felling a beam, the axe-head fell into the water: and he cried, and said, Alas, my Lord! it was also borrowed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 2:21; Mark 8:23–25; 2 Kings 4:41"> And the man of God said, Where did it fall? And he showed him the place. And he cut down a stick, and cast it in thither, and he caused the iron to swim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 4:36; Exodus 4:4; 2 Kings 4:7"> And then said he, Lift it up to thee. And he stretched out his hand, and took it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 6:24; Proverbs 21:30; Proverbs 20:18"> And the king of Syria made war against Israel, and took counsel with his servants, saying, In such and such a place shall be my camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 3:7; Revelation 1:1; 2 Kings 6:12"> And the man of God sent unto the king of Israel, saying, Beware that thou pass not that place; for thither the Syrians are come down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 13:14; 2 Kings 5:14; Acts 27:24"> And the king of Israel sent to the place, which the man of God had told him and warned him of, and he took care of himself there: not once nor twice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 18:7–11; Isaiah 57:20–21; Matthew 2:3–12"> Therefore the heart of the king of Syria was sorely troubled concerning this thing; and he called for his servants, and said unto them, Can ye not tell me who of us is for the king of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 2:28–30; Amos 3:7; 2 Kings 6:9–10"> And one of his servants said, Not so, my Lord, O king; but Elisha', the prophet that is in Israel, can tell unto the king of Israel the words that thou mayest speak in thy sleeping-chamber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 37:17; John 11:47–53; 1 Samuel 23:22–23"> And he said, Go and see where he is, that I may send and fetch him. And it was told unto him, saying, Behold, he is in Dothan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 18:17; 1 Samuel 24:2; 1 Samuel 23:26"> And he sent thither horses, and chariots, and a strong army: and they came by night, and surrounded the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 8:26; 2 Chronicles 20:12; Psalms 53:5"> And the servant of the man of God rose early, and went forth, when, behold, an army compassed the city both with horses and chariots. And his servant said unto him, Alas, my master! how shall we do?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10–14; Romans 8:31; 1 John 4:4"> And he said, Fear not: for those that are with us are more than those that are with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 68:17; Psalms 34:7; Zechariah 6:1–7"> And Elisha' prayed, and said, O Lord, open, I pray thee, his eyes, that he may see. And the Lord opened the eyes of the young man: and he saw, and behold, the mountain was full of horses and chariots of fire round about Elisha'.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 13:11; John 12:40; Genesis 19:11"> And when they came down to him, Elisha' prayed unto the Lord, and said, Smite, I pray thee, this people with blindness. And he smote them with blindness according to the word of Elisha'.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 24:16; 2 Samuel 16:18–19; Luke 9:23"> And Elisha' said unto them, This is not the way, neither is this the city: follow me, and I will lead you to the man whom ye wish to seek. And he led them thus to Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 6:17; Luke 24:31; Luke 16:23"> And it came to pass, when they were come into Samaria, that Elisha' said, Lord, open the eyes of these, that they may see. And the Lord opened their eyes, and they saw, and, behold, they were in the midst of Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 5:13; 2 Kings 8:9; 2 Kings 2:12"> And the king of Israel said unto Elisha', when he saw them, Shall I smite them? Shall I smite them, my father?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 12:20–21; Genesis 48:22; Hosea 2:18"> But he said, Thou shalt not smite them: wouldst thou smite those whom thou hast taken captive with thy sword and with thy bow? set bread and water before them, that they may eat and drink, and go &#91;back&#93; to their master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 5:2; 2 Kings 24:2; Luke 6:35"> And he prepared for them a great meal; and they ate and drank, and he sent them away, and they went &#91;back&#93; to their master. And the predatory bands of Syria came no more into the land of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 20:1; 1 Kings 22:31; Ecclesiastes 9:14"> And it came to pass after this, that Ben-hadad the king of Syria assembled all his camp, and went up, and besieged Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 6:28–29; Jeremiah 14:18; Jeremiah 14:13–15"> And there was a great famine in Samaria; and, behold, they besieged it, until an ass's head was sold for eighty pieces of silver, and the fourth part of a kab of dove's dung for five pieces of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:4; Isaiah 10:3; Luke 18:3"> And it happened as the king of Israel was passing along upon the wall, that a woman cried unto him, saying, Help, my Lord, O king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 118:8–9; Psalms 124:1–3; Psalms 60:11"> And he said, If the Lord do not help thee, whence shall I help thee? shall it be out of the threshing-floor, or out of the wine-press?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:5; Judges 18:23; Lamentations 4:10"> And the king said unto her, What aileth thee? And she said, This woman said unto me, Give up thy son, that we may eat him today, and my son we will eat tomorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 26:29; Deuteronomy 28:53; Deuteronomy 28:57"> So we boiled my son, and ate him; but when I said unto her on the next day, Give up thy son, that we may eat him: she hid her son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Kings 21:27; 2 Kings 5:7; 2 Kings 19:1"> And it came to pass, when the king heard the words of the woman, that he rent his clothes, as he was passing along upon the wall: and the people looked, and, behold, he had sackcloth beneath upon his flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ruth 1:17; 1 Kings 19:2; John 11:50"> Then said he, May God do so unto me and continue so yet farther, if the head of Elisha' the son of Shaphat shall remain on him this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:1; 1 Kings 18:4; Ezekiel 8:1"> But Elisha' was sitting in his house, and the elders were sitting with him; and he sent a man from before him; but before the messenger could yet come to him, he said to the elders, See ye how this son of the murderer hath sent to remove my head? look, when the messenger cometh, shut the door, and hold him back with the door: is not the sound of his master's feet behind him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 2:9; Isaiah 8:21; Genesis 4:13"> And while he was yet speaking with them, behold, the messenger came down unto him: and he said, Behold, this evil is of the Lord; what shall I hope for in the Lord any longer?</span>
