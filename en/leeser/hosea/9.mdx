<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 10:5; Hosea 4:12; Jeremiah 44:17"> Rejoice not, O Israel, for gladness, as other people; for thou art gone astray, unfaithful to thy God: thou hast loved the wages of sin upon every corn-filled threshing-floor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 2:9; Micah 6:13–16; Joel 1:3–7"> The threshing-floor and the wine-press shall not feed them, and the new wine shall deceive them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:13; Hosea 8:13; Leviticus 25:23"> They shall not dwell in the land of the Lord; but Ephraim shall return to Egypt, and in Assyria will they eat unclean things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 8:13; Deuteronomy 26:14; Jeremiah 6:20"> They shall not pour out wine to the Lord, and &#91;their offerings&#93; shall not be pleasing unto him; their sacrifices shall be unto them as the bread of mourners; all that eat thereof shall be polluted: for this their food can only be for themselves, it shall not come into the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 10:3; Jeremiah 5:31; Hosea 2:11"> What will ye do on the day of the appointed festival, and on the day of the feast of the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 10:8; Isaiah 5:6; Isaiah 7:23"> For, lo, they are gone forth because of the desolation: Egypt will gather them up, Moph will bury them: the pleasant chambers for their silver,—these shall nettles take possession of; thorns shall &#91;grow&#93; in their tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 26:24–25; Micah 7:4; 2 Kings 9:11"> Come are the days of the visitation, come are the days of thy recompense; this shall Israel experience: a fool was the prophet, mad the inspired man, because of the greatness of thy iniquity, and the great hatefulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hosea 5:1; Ezekiel 3:17; Isaiah 62:6"> The watchman of Ephraim with my God, the prophet, was a snare of the fowler on all his ways, a hateful thing in the house of his god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 10:9; Judges 19:16–21; Hosea 8:13"> They are deeply corrupt, as in the days of Gib'ah: he will remember their iniquity, he will visit their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:13; Hosea 4:14; Numbers 25:1–18"> Like grapes in the wilderness had I found Israel; as the first ripe fruit on the fig-tree in the first of the season had I seen your fathers; but they too went to Ba'al-pe'or: and devoted themselves unto that shameful idol, and became abominations as those they loved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:18; Hosea 9:14; Hosea 4:7"> As for Ephraim, their glory shall fly away like a bird: there is no more birth, and no pregnancy, and no conception.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 7:13; Deuteronomy 32:25; Deuteronomy 31:17"> But though they were to bring up their children, yet would I bereave them, that there should be no man: yea, woe also to themselves, when I depart from them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:1–21; Ezekiel 27:3; Hosea 13:8"> Ephraim, as I have seen him like Tyre, planted in a pleasant meadow,—yet this Ephraim shall lead forth to the murderer his children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 23:29; 1 Corinthians 7:26; Mark 13:17"> Give them, O Lord, what thou wilt give! give them a miscarrying womb and dried-up breasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 4:15; Isaiah 1:23; Amos 5:5"> All their wickedness is in Gilgal; for there I &#91;learnt to&#93; hate them; for the wickedness of their doings will I drive them out of my house: I will love them no farther; all their princes are rebels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 9:11–13; Ezekiel 24:21; Isaiah 5:24"> Smitten is Ephraim, their root is dried up, they shall bear no fruit; yea, though they should bring forth, yet would I slay the beloved fruit of their body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hosea 7:13; Deuteronomy 28:64–65; Acts 3:23"> My God will reject them, because they did not hearken unto him: and they shall be wanderers among the nations.</span>
