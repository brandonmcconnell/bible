<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 6:1; Joel 2:12–13; Hosea 12:6"> (14:2) Return, O Israel, even unto the Lord thy God; for thou hast stumbled through thy iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 7:18–19; Hebrews 13:15; Job 34:31–32"> (14:3) Take with you swords, and return to the Lord: say unto him, “Pardon all &#91;our&#93; iniquity, and accept &#91;our return to&#93; good; and let us repay the steers &#91;of sacrifice&#93; with &#91;the prayer of&#93; our lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 68:5; Psalms 10:14; Isaiah 31:1"> (14:4) Asshur shall not help us; upon horses will we not ride: and we will no more say, Ye are our gods, to the work of our hands; for in thee alone the fatherless obtaineth mercy.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 57:18; Ephesians 2:4–9; Jeremiah 3:22"> (14:5) I will heal their backsliding, I will love them freely; for my anger is turned away from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 35:2; Isaiah 27:6; Ephesians 3:17"> (14:6) I will be as the dew unto Israel: he shall bloom as the lily; and he shall strike his roots as &#91;the forests of&#93; Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 52:8; John 15:1; 2 Corinthians 2:14–15"> (14:7) His suckers shall spread out, and his beauty shall be as that of the olive-tree, and his smell as that of the Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 91:1; Psalms 85:6; 1 Corinthians 15:36–38"> (14:8) They shall return that sat under his shade; they shall revive as corn, and bloom as the vine: the scent of which shall be as that of the wine of Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 34:32; Isaiah 55:13; James 1:17"> (14:9) Ephraim &#91;shall say&#93;, What have I to do any more with idols? I have answered, and will observe him; I will be &#91;to him&#93; like a green fir-tree; through me is thy fruit found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 10:29; Psalms 107:43; John 8:47"> (14:10) Who is wise, that he may understand these things? intelligent, that he may know them? for righteous are the ways of the Lord; and the just shall walk in them; but the transgressors will stumble through them.</span>
