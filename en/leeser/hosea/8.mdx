<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 6:7; Deuteronomy 28:49; Hosea 5:8"> Set the cornet to thy mouth. &#91;Let the enemy come&#93; like the eagle against the house of the Lord: because they have transgressed my covenant, and against my law have they trespassed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:16; Matthew 7:21; Luke 13:25"> To me will they then cry, My God, we, Israel, know thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 3:66; Deuteronomy 28:25; 1 Timothy 5:12"> &#91;But&#93; Israel did reject the good; so let the enemy pursue him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 15:10–30; Hosea 13:9–10; Hosea 2:8"> They set up kings, but not my advice: they chose princes and I knew it not: of their silver and their gold have they made themselves idols so that they will be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 10:5; Jeremiah 13:27; Proverbs 1:22"> Thy calf, O Samaria, hath caused thy rejection; my anger is kindled against them: how long will it be that they cannot cleanse themselves?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 10:2; Isaiah 44:9–20; Psalms 135:15–18"> For whom Israel did also that &#91;idol&#93; spring; an artisan made it, and no God is it: so then shall it become broken in splinters— that calf of Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 22:8; Job 4:8; Galatians 6:7"> For the wind do they sow, and the whirlwind shall they reap: &#91;their seed&#93; bringeth no standing corn; the plant yieldeth no meal; but should it yield it, strangers would swallow it up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:28; Jeremiah 51:34; Romans 9:22"> Swallowed up is Israel: now are they among the nations as a vessel without any value.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:24; Hosea 7:11; Ezekiel 16:33–34"> For they are needs gone up to Assyria, they who like a wild ass should dwell alone: Ephraim spendeth lovers' gifts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:37; Ezekiel 23:22–26; Isaiah 10:8"> But even though they should spend gifts among the nations, now will I gather them: and they shall be humbled a little through the burden of the king of princes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 12:11; Hosea 10:1–2; Hosea 10:8"> Because Ephraim hath multiplied altars to sin, the altars have been unto him the means of sinning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:6–8; Nehemiah 9:26; Proverbs 22:20"> I ever wrote down for him the great things of my law; but as a strange thing are they accounted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 9:9; Hosea 9:6; Amos 8:7"> My sacrificial offerings they slay as common flesh that they may eat it; the Lord accepteth them not in favor: now will he remember their iniquity, and visit their sins; they shall indeed return to Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:18; Jeremiah 17:27; Amos 2:5"> For Israel forgot his Maker, and built palaces; and Judah multiplied fortified cities; but I will send a fire among his cities, and it shall devour their fine edifices.</span>
