<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 9:15; Isaiah 29:15; Hosea 6:9"> And for murdering they who had rebelled &#91;against God&#93; concealed themselves in deep places; but I will inflict correction on them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 3:2; Hebrews 4:13; Hosea 13:1"> I well know Ephraim, and Israel is not hidden from me; for now, O Ephraim, hast thou played the harlot, &#91;and&#93; Israel is defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 4:12; 1 John 2:3–4; Hosea 4:1"> Their doings will not permit them to return unto their God; for the spirit of lewdness is in their bosom, and the Lord they have not known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 7:10; Amos 5:2; Ezekiel 23:31–35"> Therefore shall the pride of Israel be humbled before his face: and Israel and Ephraim shall stumble in their iniquity; Judah also shall stumble with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Micah 6:6–7; John 7:34; Proverbs 1:28"> With their flocks and with their herds will they go to seek the Lord; but they shall not find him: he hath withdrawn himself from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 6:7; Isaiah 48:8; Jeremiah 3:20"> Against the Lord have they dealt treacherously; for strange children have they begotten: now shall one month devour them together with their possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hosea 4:15; Hosea 9:9; 1 Samuel 15:34"> Blow ye the cornet in Gib'ah, the trumpet in Ramah: blow the alarm at Beth-aven, &#91;The enemy is&#93; after thee, O Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 1:6; Isaiah 46:10; Hosea 9:11–17"> Ephraim shall be made desolate on the day of chastisement: among the tribes of Israel had I made known that which is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:14; Deuteronomy 27:17; Psalms 93:3–4"> The princes of Judah were like those that remove the landmark: my wrath, therefore, will I pour out upon them like water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:33; Micah 6:16; 1 Kings 12:26–33"> Oppressed is Ephraim, broken through punishment; because he willingly walked after the commandment &#91;of false prophets&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 51:8; Job 13:28; Isaiah 50:9"> But like the moth became I unto Ephraim, and like rottenness to the house of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 7:11; Hosea 12:1; Hosea 10:6"> Then saw Ephraim his sickness, and Judah his wound, and Ephraim went to Asshur, and &#91;the other&#93; sent to the king that should contend &#91;&#91;Jareb&#93;&#93;; but he will never be able to heal you, nor remove from you your wound.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 7:2; Micah 5:8; Hosea 13:7–8"> For I am as a lion unto Ephraim, and as a young lion to the house of Judah: I, even I myself will tear in pieces and go away; I will bear away, and none shall deliver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 26:16; Jeremiah 29:12–14; Deuteronomy 4:29–31"> I will go &#91;from here, and&#93; return to my place, till they acknowledge their guilt, and seek my presence: in their affliction will they seek for me.</span>
