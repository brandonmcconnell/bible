<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 2:15; Exodus 4:22; Hosea 13:4"> When Israel was yet young, then I loved him, and out of Egypt did I call my son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:15; Hosea 2:13; Isaiah 65:7"> The &#91;prophets&#93; called them; but the more they went from them: unto the Be'alim would they sacrifice, and to the graven images would they burn incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:17; Deuteronomy 1:31; Exodus 15:26"> Yet I myself appointed a leader for Ephraim, who took them up in his arms; but they would not acknowledge that I healed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 12:32; Leviticus 26:13; 2 Corinthians 5:14"> With human cords I ever drew them forward, with leading-strings of love: and I was to them as those that lift off the yoke from their jaws, and I held out unto them food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 7:16; Hosea 8:13; 2 Kings 17:13–14"> He should not return unto the land of Egypt: yet &#91;now&#93; is the Assyrian his king; because they refused to repent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 13:16; Hosea 10:6; Hosea 10:14"> And the sword shall fall on his cities, and shall make an end of his boughs, and consume them, because of their &#91;evil&#93; counsels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:5; Amos 5:14–15; Hosea 7:16"> For my people are only bent on backsliding from me; and though upward they call them, they altogether will not elevate themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:20; Matthew 23:37; Hosea 6:4"> How shall I give thee up, Ephraim? how shall I surrender thee, Israel? how shall I make thee as Admah? how shall I change thee as Zeboyim? turned is my heart within me, all my compassion is enkindled together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Isaiah 55:8–9; Micah 7:18–20"> I will not execute the fierceness of my anger, I will not again destroy Ephraim; for God am I, and not man, the Holy One in the midst of thee, and I will not come with an enemy's hatred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 31:4; Joel 3:16; Amos 1:2"> They shall follow after the Lord, when he will roar like a lion; for he will roar, and the children shall hasten together from the west;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 11:11; Isaiah 60:8; Zechariah 10:10"> They shall hasten together as birds out of Egypt, and as doves out of the land of Assyria: and I will cause them to dwell in their houses, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 12:7; Revelation 3:21; 2 Chronicles 29:1–32"> (12:1) With lies hath Ephraim encompassed me about, and with deceit, the house of Israel; but Judah yet ruleth with God, and is faithful to the Holy One.</span>
