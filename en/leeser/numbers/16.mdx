<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jude 1:11; Deuteronomy 11:6; Exodus 6:21"> Now Korach, the son of Yizhar, the son of Kehath, the son of Levi, was presumptuous, together with Dathan and Abiram, the sons of Eliab, and On, the son of Peleth, sons of Reuben:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 26:9; Ezekiel 23:10; Genesis 6:4"> So that they rose up before Moses, with certain men of the children of Israel, in number two hundred and fifty; &#91;who were&#93; princes of the congregation, called to the assembly, men of renown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 106:16; Numbers 14:14; Exodus 19:6"> And they assembled themselves against Moses, and against Aaron, and said unto them, Ye assume too much; for the whole of the congregation are all of them holy, and the Lord is among them; wherefore then will you lift yourselves up above the congregation of the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 14:5; Numbers 20:6; Joshua 7:6"> And when Moses heard it, he fell upon his face:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 65:4; Numbers 17:5; 2 Timothy 2:19"> And he spoke unto Korach and unto all his company, saying, Tomorrow,—then will the Lord make known who is his, and who is holy, that he may cause them to come near unto him; and him whom he shall choose will he cause to come near unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1; Numbers 16:46–48; 1 Kings 18:21–23"> This do ye: Take yourselves censers, Korach and all his company;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 16:3; Ephesians 1:4; 1 Kings 18:17–18"> And put therein fire, and put upon them incense before the Lord, tomorrow; and it shall be that the man whom the Lord will choose, he shall be the holy one; you assume too much, ye sons of Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And Moses said unto Korach, Hear, I pray you, ye sons of Levi:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 7:13; Deuteronomy 10:8; Ezekiel 44:10–11"> It is too little for you, that the God of Israel hath separated you from the congregation of Israel, to bring you near unto himself, to do the service of the tabernacle of the Lord, and to stand before the congregation to minister for them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 13:10; 3 John 1:9; Numbers 18:7"> And he hath brought thee near, and all thy brethren the sons of Levi with thee: and now will ye seek the priesthood also?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 16:7–8; Exodus 17:2; Numbers 16:3"> For which cause &#91;beware&#93;, thou and all thy company that are gathered together against the Lord; for Aaron, what is he, that ye should murmur against him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 2:13–14; Jude 1:8; Proverbs 29:9"> And Moses sent to call Dathan and Abiram, the sons of Eliab; but they said, We will not come up:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 7:35; Exodus 2:14; Numbers 11:5"> Is it too little that thou hast brought us up out of a land flowing with milk and honey, to kill us in the wilderness, that thou wilt assume to make thyself also a prince over us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 20:24; Numbers 36:8–10; Numbers 20:5"> Moreover thou hast not brought us into a land flowing with milk and honey, and thou hast not given us inheritance of fields and vineyards: wilt thou bore out the eyes of these men? we will not come up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 4:4–5; 2 Corinthians 7:2; 1 Thessalonians 2:10"> And this displeased Moses greatly, and he said unto the Lord, Have no respect unto their offering: I have not taken away an ass of any one of them, nor have I done wrong to any one of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:3; 1 Samuel 12:7; 2 Timothy 2:14"> And Moses said unto Korach, Thou and all thy company, be ye before the Lord, thou, and they, and Aaron, tomorrow:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:7"> And take ye every man his censer, and put incense upon them, and bring ye near before the Lord every man his censer, two hundred and fifty censers; thou also, and Aaron, each his censer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And they took every man his censer, and put fire on them, and laid incense thereupon; and they stood at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation with Moses and Aaron.—</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 14:10; Numbers 16:42; Exodus 16:7"> And Korach assembled against them all the congregation unto the door of the tabernacle of the congregation; and the glory of the Lord then appeared unto all the congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And the Lord spoke unto Moses and unto Aaron, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 16:45; Exodus 32:10; Psalms 73:19"> Separate yourselves from the midst of this congregation, and I will make an end of them in a moment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 27:16; Zechariah 12:1; Job 12:10"> And they fell upon their faces, and said, O God, the God of the spirits of all flesh, this one man doth sin, and with all the congregation wouldst thou be wroth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And the Lord spoke unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 16:21"> Speak unto the congregation, saying, Get you away from about the dwelling of Korach, Dathan, and Abiram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 11:16–17; Numbers 11:25; Numbers 11:30"> And Moses rose up and went unto Dathan and Abiram; and there went after him the elders of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Revelation 18:4"> And he spoke unto the congregation, saying, Depart, I pray you, from the tents of these wicked men, and touch nothing which belongeth to them, lest ye be destroyed through all their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 9:4; Isaiah 28:14; Job 40:10–11"> So they got away from the dwelling of Korach, Dathan, and Abiram, on every side: and Dathan and Abiram came out, standing &#91;boldly&#93; at the door of their tents, with their wives, and their sons, and their little ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 6:38; Exodus 3:12; John 14:11"> And Moses said, Through this shall ye know that the Lord hath sent me to do all these deeds; that &#91;I have&#93; not done them out of my own heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Lamentations 4:22; 1 Kings 22:28; Exodus 32:34"> If these men die as all men die, and if the visitation of all men be visited on them: then hath the Lord not sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 55:15; Numbers 16:33; Job 31:3"> But if the Lord do create a new thing, and the earth open her mouth, and swallow them up, with all that appertaineth unto them, and they go down alive into the pit: then shall ye understand that these men have provoked the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 106:17–18; Numbers 27:3; Deuteronomy 11:6"> And it came to pass, when he had made an end of speaking all these words, that the ground that was under them was cloven asunder:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 106:17; Numbers 26:11; Revelation 12:16"> And the earth opened her mouth, and swallowed them up, and their houses, and all the men that appertained unto Korach, and all their goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:18; Isaiah 14:9; Psalms 9:15"> And they went down, they, and all they that appertained to them, alive into the pit; and the earth closed over them, and they disappeared from the midst of the congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Zechariah 14:5; Isaiah 33:3; Revelation 6:15–17"> And all Israel that were round about them fled at their cry; for they said, Perhaps the earth may swallow us up &#91;also&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 26:10; Leviticus 10:2; Psalms 106:18"> And there came out a fire from the Lord, and consumed the two hundred and fifty men that had offered the incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> (17:1) And the Lord spoke unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Leviticus 27:28; Numbers 16:18; Numbers 16:7"> (17:2) Speak unto Elazar the son of Aaron the priest, that he lift up the censers out of the burning, and throw out the fire far away; for they have been hallowed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 26:10; Ezekiel 14:8; 2 Peter 2:6"> (17:3) The censers of these sinners against their own lives; and they shall make of them broad plates for a covering for the altar; for they brought them near before the Lord, and they have thus become hallowed; and they shall serve for a sign unto the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> (17:4) And Elazar the priest took the copper censers, which they that were burnt had brought near; and they beat them out for a covering unto the altar:</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 3:10; Numbers 18:4–7; 2 Chronicles 26:16–21"> (17:5) As a memorial unto the children of Israel, in order that no stranger, who is not of the seed of Aaron, should come near to burn incense before the Lord; that he become not as Korach, and as his company; as the Lord had spoken to him by the hand of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 16:1–7; Psalms 106:23; Numbers 14:2"> (17:6) And all the congregation of the children of Israel murmured against Moses and Aaron, on the morrow, saying, It is you who have caused the people of the Lord to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Numbers 16:19; Leviticus 9:23; Numbers 20:6"> (17:7) And it came to pass, when the congregation assembled against Moses and against Aaron, that they looked toward the tabernacle of the congregation, and, behold, the cloud covered it; and the glory of the Lord appeared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> (17:8) And Moses came with Aaron before the tabernacle of the congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> (17:9) And the Lord spoke unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Numbers 16:21–22; Numbers 16:24; Numbers 20:6"> (17:10) Remove yourselves from the midst of this congregation, that I may consume them in a moment. And they fell upon their faces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Numbers 8:19; 1 Chronicles 27:24; Leviticus 10:6"> (17:11) And Moses said unto Aaron, Take the censer, and put therein fire from off the altar, and put on incense, and carry &#91;it&#93; quickly unto the congregation, and make an atonement for them; for the wrath is gone forth from the Lord; the plague hath begun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; Deuteronomy 33:10–11; Psalms 106:29"> (17:12) And Aaron took as Moses had commanded, and he ran into the midst of the assembly; and, behold, the plague had begun among the people: and he put on the incense, and made an atonement for the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:25; Hebrews 7:24–25; Numbers 16:18"> (17:13) And he stood between the dead and the living; and the plague was stayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Numbers 25:9; Numbers 16:32–35; Hebrews 2:1–3"> (17:14) And those who died in the plague were fourteen thousand and seven hundred, besides those that had died about the matter of Korach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:26–30; Numbers 16:43"> (17:15) And Aaron returned unto Moses, to the door of the tabernacle of the congregation, after the plague had been stayed.</span>
