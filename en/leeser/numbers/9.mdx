<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 1:1; Exodus 40:2"> And the Lord spoke unto Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, in the second year after their coming out of the land of Egypt, in the first month, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:1–2; 1 Corinthians 5:7–8; 2 Chronicles 35:1"> That the children of Israel shall prepare the passover-lamb at its appointed season.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 12:6–11; Leviticus 23:5; 2 Chronicles 30:2"> On the fourteenth day of this month, toward evening, shall ye prepare it at its appointed season: according to all its ordinances, and according to all its prescribed rules, shall ye prepare it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And Moses spoke unto the children of Israel, that they should prepare the passover-lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 5:10; Matthew 28:20; Exodus 12:1–13"> And they prepared the passover-lamb on the fourteenth day of the first month toward evening in the wilderness of Sinai: according to all that the Lord had commanded Moses, so did the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 27:2; Exodus 18:15; Numbers 5:2"> But there were certain men, who had been defiled by the dead body of a man, and they could not prepare the passover-lamb on that day: and they came before Moses and before Aaron on that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:2; 1 Corinthians 5:7–8; Exodus 12:27"> And these men said unto him, We are defiled by the dead body of a man: wherefore shall we be kept back, so as not to offer the sacrifice of the Lord at its appointed season in the midst of the &#91;other&#93; children of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 85:8; Numbers 27:5; 1 Corinthians 4:4"> And Moses said unto them, Wait ye, and I will hear what the Lord will command concerning you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And the Lord spoke unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 2:1–2; Ephesians 2:12–13; 1 Corinthians 11:28"> Speak unto the children of Israel, saying, If any man whatever should be unclean by reason of a dead body, or be on a distant journey, among you or your posterity: yet shall he prepare the passover-lamb unto the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:2–15; Exodus 12:43–49; John 19:36"> In the second month on the fourteenth day toward evening shall they prepare it, with unleavened bread and bitter herbs shall they eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 19:36; Exodus 12:46; Exodus 12:10"> They shall leave none of it until morning, and no bone shall they break on it: according to the whole ordinance of the passover-lamb shall they prepare it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 17:14; Exodus 12:15; Numbers 9:7"> But the man that is clean, and is not on a journey, and forbeareth to prepare the passover-lamb, even that same soul shall be cut off from his people; because the offering of the Lord hath he not brought at its appointed season, his sin shall that man bear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 12:48–49; Leviticus 24:22; Isaiah 56:3–7"> And if a stranger sojourn among you, and will prepare the passover-lamb unto the Lord: according to the ordinance of the passover-lamb, and according to its prescribed rule, so shall he prepare it; one statute shall be for you, both for the stranger, and for the native born in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 13:21; Exodus 40:34; Nehemiah 9:19"> And on the day that the tabernacle was reared up the cloud covered the tabernacle of the tent of the testimony: and in the evening there was upon the tabernacle as it were the appearance of fire, until morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:12; Exodus 40:38; 1 Corinthians 10:1"> So it used to be always: the cloud covered it &#91;by day&#93;, and the appearance of fire by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 40:36–38; Psalms 32:8; John 10:9"> And as the cloud was taken up from the tabernacle, then after that did the children of Israel journey forward: and in the place where the cloud halted, there did the children of Israel encamp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:1; 2 John 1:6; Exodus 17:1"> At the order of the Lord did the children of Israel journey forward, and at the order of the Lord they encamped: all the days that the cloud abode upon the tabernacle did they remain in camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 3:7; Numbers 1:52–53; Numbers 3:8"> And when the cloud tarried upon the tabernacle many days, then did the children of Israel keep the charge of the Lord, and journeyed not forward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And at times it was, that the cloud remained but a few days upon the tabernacle; at the order of the Lord they abode in camp, and at the order of the Lord they journeyed forward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:19; Nehemiah 9:12"> And at times it was, that the cloud remained from evening until morning; and when the cloud was taken up in the morning, they journeyed forward; or a day and a night, and when the cloud was taken up, they journeyed forward;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 32:8; Exodus 40:36–37; Exodus 40:16"> Or two days, or a month, or a year; so long as the cloud tarried upon the tabernacle, to remain thereon, did the children of Israel remain encamped, and journeyed not forward; but when it was taken up, they journeyed forward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 22:3; Numbers 9:19; Genesis 26:5"> At the order of the Lord they remained in camp, and at the order of the Lord they journeyed forward: the charge of the Lord they kept, at the order of the Lord by the hand of Moses.</span>
