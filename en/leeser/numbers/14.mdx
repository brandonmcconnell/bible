<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:45; Numbers 11:1–4"> And all the congregation lifted up their voice, and cried aloud; and the people wept that night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 15:24; Exodus 17:3; Numbers 16:41"> And all the people murmured against Moses and against Aaron; and the whole congregation said unto them, Oh who would grant that we had died in the land of Egypt! or that we might but die in this wilderness!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 14:31–32; Jeremiah 9:3; Psalms 78:40"> And wherefore doth the Lord bring us unto yonder land, to fall by the sword? that our wives and our children may become a prey? is it not better for us to return to Egypt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 7:39; Nehemiah 9:16–17; Deuteronomy 17:16"> And they said one to another, Let us appoint a chief, and let us return to Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 16:4; Numbers 16:22; Numbers 16:45"> Then fell Moses and Aaron on their faces before all the assembly of the congregation of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:30; Numbers 13:30; Numbers 14:38"> And Joshua, the son of Nun, and Caleb the son of Yephunneh, of those that had spied out the land, rent their garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 13:27; Deuteronomy 1:25; Deuteronomy 8:7–9"> And they said unto all the congregation of the children of Israel, as followeth, The land, through which we have passed to spy it out, this land is exceedingly good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:20; Isaiah 62:4; Numbers 13:27"> If the Lord have delight in us, then will he bring us into this land, and give it to us: a land which is flowing with milk and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:18; Romans 8:31; Deuteronomy 31:6"> Only against the Lord do ye not rebel; and then ye need not fear the people of the land; for they are our bread: their shadow is departed from them, while the Lord is with us; fear them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 17:4; 1 Samuel 30:6; Leviticus 9:23"> But all the congregation said to stone them with stones: when the glory of the Lord appeared in the tabernacle of the congregation unto all the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 78:32; Psalms 106:24; Psalms 78:22"> And the Lord said unto Moses, How long yet shall this people provoke me? and how long yet will they not believe in me, with all the signs which I have shown in the midst of them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 32:10; Numbers 25:9; 2 Samuel 24:12–15"> I will smite them with the pestilence, and root them out, and I will make of thee a nation greater and mightier than they.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 106:23; Ezekiel 20:9; Deuteronomy 32:27"> And Moses said unto the Lord, But when the Egyptian hear, from the midst of whom thou hast brought up in thy might this people;—</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 15:14; Exodus 33:11; Deuteronomy 5:4"> And when they tell to the inhabitants of this land, who have heard that thou, Lord, art in the midst of this people, that face to face thou, Lord, art seen, and that thy cloud standeth over them, and that in a pillar of cloud thou goest before them by day, and in a pillar of fire by night;—</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 6:16"> That thou hast killed this people as one man: then will the nations that have heard thy fame, say in this manner,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:28; Joshua 7:9; Joshua 7:7"> That because the Lord was not able to bring this people into the land which he had sworn unto them, hath he slain them in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 9:6; Matthew 9:8; Micah 3:8"> And now, I beseech thee, let the greatness of the power of the Lord be made manifest, as thou hast spoken, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 34:6–7; Psalms 145:8; Exodus 20:5"> The Eternal is long-suffering, and abundant in beneficence, forgiving iniquity and transgression; but who will by no means clear the guilty, visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children, upon the third and upon the fourth generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 34:9; Psalms 78:38; Psalms 106:45"> Pardon, I beseech thee, the iniquity of this people, according to the greatness of thy beneficence, and as thou hast been indulgent to this people, from Egypt even until hitherto.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Micah 7:18–20; James 5:16; Psalms 106:23"> And the Lord said, I have pardoned according to thy word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 72:19; Habakkuk 2:14; Isaiah 6:3"> But as truly as I live, and as all the earth is filled with the glory of the Lord:—</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 19:3; Genesis 31:7; Exodus 32:1"> That all the men who have seen my glory, and my signs, which I have displayed in Egypt and in the wilderness, and have tempted me these ten times, and have not hearkened to my voice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 32:11; Numbers 26:64; Ezekiel 20:15"> Shall surely not see the land which I have sworn unto their fathers, yea all those that have provoked me shall not see it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 14:6–9; Joshua 14:6–14; Ephesians 6:6"> But my servant Caleb, as a reward that he had another spirit with him, and followed me fully,—therefore will I bring him into the land whereinto he went; and his seed shall possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:40; Numbers 13:29; Psalms 81:11–13"> And the Amalekites and the Canaanites dwell in the valley: tomorrow turn you, and set forward into the wilderness by the way to the Red Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And the Lord spoke unto Moses and unto Aaron, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 16:12; 1 Corinthians 10:10; Exodus 16:28"> How long &#91;shall indulgence be given&#93; to this evil congregation, that murmur against me? the murmurings of the children of Israel, which they murmur against me, have I heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 14:21; Hebrews 3:17; Numbers 14:2"> Say unto them, As truly as I live, saith the Lord, as ye have spoken in my ears, so will I do to you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 1:45; 1 Corinthians 10:5; Hebrews 3:17"> In this wilderness shall your carcasses fall, and all that were numbered of you, according to your whole number, from twenty years old and upward; ye who have murmured against me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 26:65; Numbers 14:38; Deuteronomy 1:36–38"> Truly ye shall not come into the land, concerning which I have lifted up my hand to let you dwell therein; save Caleb the son of Yephunneh, and Joshua the son of Nun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:39; Psalms 106:24; Numbers 26:64"> But your little ones of which ye said, They would become a prey, them will I bring in, and they shall know the land which ye have despised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:5; Numbers 14:29; Hebrews 3:17"> But as for you, your carcasses shall fall in this wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:35; Numbers 32:13; Deuteronomy 2:14"> And your children shall wander about in the wilderness forty years, and bear your backslidings, until your carcasses be spent in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:6; Numbers 13:25; Jeremiah 18:9–10"> After the number of the days in which ye spied out the land, forty days, yea, each one day for a year, shall ye bear for your iniquities, forty years; and ye shall experience my withdrawal &#91;of protection&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; 1 Corinthians 10:5; Hebrews 3:19"> I the Lord have spoken it, surely, this will I do unto all this evil congregation that have assembled against me: in this wilderness shall they be spent, and therein shall they die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Numbers 13:31–33; Numbers 13:4–16"> And the men whom Moses had sent to spy out the land, and who returned, and caused all the congregation to murmur against him, by bringing up an evil report against the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:10; Numbers 16:49; Hebrews 3:17"> Even these men, that had brought up the evil report of the land, died by the plague before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Joshua 14:6–10; Numbers 26:65"> But Joshua the son of Nun, and Caleb the son of Yephunneh, remained alive of those men, who had gone to spy out the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 33:4; Matthew 8:12; Hebrews 12:17"> And Moses spoke these words unto all the children of Israel; and the people mourned greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:41; Matthew 7:21–23; Luke 13:25"> And they rose up early in the morning, and went up to the top of the mountain, saying, Lo, here we are, and we will go up unto the place of which the Lord hath spoken; for we have sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:20; Job 4:9; Jeremiah 2:37"> And Moses said, Wherefore now do ye transgress the order of the Lord? and it will not prosper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:42; Joshua 7:8; Joshua 7:12"> Do not go up, for the Lord is not among you; that ye may not be smitten before your enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 13:29; Numbers 14:25; 2 Chronicles 15:2"> For the Amalekites and the Canaanites are there before you, and ye will fall by the sword; since, because ye are turned away from the Lord, the Lord also will not be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:43; 1 Samuel 4:3–11; Numbers 31:6"> Yet they persisted to go up unto the top of the mountain; but the ark of the covenant of the Lord, and Moses, did not move out of the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Numbers 21:3; Judges 1:17; Deuteronomy 1:44"> Then came down the Amalekites, and the Canaanites that dwelt on that mountain, and smote them, and discomfited them, even unto Chormah.</span>
