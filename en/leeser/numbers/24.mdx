<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 22:31; Numbers 24:16; Numbers 23:7"> And he took up his parable, and said, Thus saith Bil'am the son of Beor, and thus saith the man whose eyes are open;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Revelation 1:10; Numbers 12:6"> Thus saith he who heareth the sayings of God, who seeth the vision of the Almighty, falling down, with unvailed eyes:</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 1:3; Isaiah 58:11; Psalms 104:16"> As streams are they spread forth, as gardens by the river's side, as aloe-trees, which the Lord hath planted, as cedar-trees beside the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:12; 1 Chronicles 14:2; Revelation 17:1"> Water runneth out of His buckets, that his seed may be moistened by abundance of water; and exalted above Agag shall be his king, and raised on high shall be his kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 45:5; Psalms 2:9; Numbers 14:9"> God, who brought him forth out of Egypt, is to him like the heights of the reem; he will devour nations, his oppressors, and their bones will he break, and pierce &#91;them&#93; through with his arrows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Genesis 27:29; Genesis 49:9"> He coucheth, he lieth down as a lion, and as a lioness: who shall make him rise up? They that bless thee be blessed, and they that curse thee be cursed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 23:11; Ezekiel 21:14; Ezekiel 21:17"> And the anger of Balak was kindled against Bil'am, and he struck his hands together: and Balak said unto Bil'am, To denounce my enemies did I call thee, and, behold, thou hast even blessed them these three times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 22:17; Numbers 22:37; Hebrews 11:24–26"> And now flee thou to thy place: I thought to honor thee greatly; but, lo, the Lord hath kept thee back from honor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 22:18; Numbers 22:38"> And Bil'am said unto Balak, Did I not already speak to thy messengers, whom thou sentest unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 16:28; Numbers 22:18; Numbers 22:20"> If Balak would give me his house full of silver and gold, I could not transgress the order of the Lord, to do good or evil out of my own heart: what the Lord will speak, that must I speak?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Micah 6:5; Revelation 2:14; Genesis 49:1"> And now, behold, I am going unto my people: come, I will advise thee against what this people will do to thy people in the end of days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 24:3–4; Numbers 23:7; Matthew 13:35"> And he took up his parable and said, Thus saith Bil'am the son of Beor, and thus saith the man whose eyes are open;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 24:4; 1 Corinthians 8:1; 2 Samuel 23:1–2"> Thus saith he who heareth the sayings of God, and knoweth the knowledge of the Most High, who seeth the vision of the Almighty, falling down, with unvailed eyes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 22:16; Luke 1:32–33; Genesis 49:10"> I see him, but not now; I behold him, but not nigh; there steppeth forth a star out of Jacob, and there ariseth a sceptre out of Israel, and he pierceth the chiefs of Moab, and destroyeth all the children of Sheth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Amos 9:12; Genesis 27:29; 2 Samuel 8:14"> And Edom shall be a conquest, and Seir shall be a conquest for his enemies; and Israel shall do valiantly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 49:10; Micah 5:2; Psalms 2:1–12"> And there shall rule the one from Jacob, and he shall destroy whatever escapeth out of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 17:14; Judges 6:3; 1 Chronicles 4:43"> And he looked on Amalek, and he took up his parable, and said, The first of nations is Amalek; but his latter end shall be destruction for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 15:19; Judges 1:16; Job 29:18"> And he looked on the Kenites, and took up his parable, and said, Strong is thy dwelling-place, and placed on the rock is thy nest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 83:8; Genesis 10:22; Ezra 4:2"> Nevertheless the Kenite shall be wasted: whither will Asshur carry thee away captive?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 23:23; Malachi 3:2; 2 Kings 5:1"> And he took up his parable, and said, Alas, who shall live when God doth appoint this one?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 10:4; Numbers 24:20; Daniel 10:20"> But ships will come from the coast of Kittim, and will afflict Asshur, and will afflict 'Eber; and he also will be given to destruction for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 31:8; Joshua 13:22; Numbers 24:11"> And Bil'am rose up, and went and returned to his place; and Balak also went his way.</span>
