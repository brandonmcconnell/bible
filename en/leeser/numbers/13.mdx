<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spoke unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:22–25; Numbers 32:8; Numbers 34:18"> Send thou out some men that they may spy out the land of Canaan, which I give unto the children of Israel: one man each of every tribe of their fathers shall ye send, every one who is a prince among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 12:16; Numbers 32:8; Deuteronomy 1:19"> And Moses sent them out from the wilderness of Paran by the order of the Lord: they all were men, &#91;who&#93; were heads of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And these are their names: Of the tribe of Reuben, Shammua the son of Zaccur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Of the tribe of Simeon, Shaphat the son of Chori.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 34:19; Numbers 14:6; Numbers 13:30"> Of the tribe of Judah, Caleb the son of Yephunneh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Of the tribe of Issachar, Yigal the son of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 24:13; Numbers 11:28; Numbers 13:16"> Of the tribe of Ephraim, Hoshea, the son of Nun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Of the tribe of Benjamin, Palti the son of Raphu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Of the tribe of Zebulun, Gaddiel the son of Sodi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Of the tribe of Joseph, of the tribe of Menasseh, Gaddi the son of Sussi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Of the tribe of Dan, 'Ammiel the son of Gemalli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Of the tribe of Asher, Sethur the son of Michael.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Of the tribe of Naphtali, Nachbi the son of Vophsi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Of the tribe of Gad, Geuel the son of Machi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 13:8; Exodus 17:9; Numbers 14:6"> These are the names of the men whom Moses sent to spy out the land; and Moses called Hoshea the son of Nun, Joshua &#91;&#91;Yehoshua'&#93;&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 1:9; Genesis 12:9; Judges 1:19"> And Moses sent them to spy out the land of Canaan, and he said unto them, Go you up this way at the south side, and go up into the mountain;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:14; Exodus 3:8"> And see the land, what it is; and the people that dwell therein, whether they be strong or weak, whether they be few or many;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And what the land is on which they dwell, whether it be good or bad; and what the cities are in which they dwell, whether in open places, or in strongholds;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:23; Deuteronomy 31:6–8; Ezekiel 34:14"> And what the land is, whether it be fat or lean, whether there be trees therein, or not; and take ye courage, and take away some of the fruit of the land. Now the time was the season of the first ripening of grapes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 20:1; Numbers 33:36; Numbers 27:14"> And they went up, and spied out the land from the wilderness of Zin unto Rechob, on the road to Chamath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 78:12; Psalms 78:43; Isaiah 19:11"> And they ascended on the south side, and came unto Hebron; and there were Achiman, Sheshai, and Talmai, the children of 'Anak; &#91;now Hebron had been built seven years before Zoan in Egypt.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 13:24; Numbers 32:9; Deuteronomy 1:24–25"> And they came unto the valley of Eshcol, and they cut down from there a branch with one cluster of grapes, and they bore it upon a barrow between two; and &#91;they took some&#93; of the pomegranates and of the figs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> That place was called the valley of Eshcol, on account of the cluster which the children of Israel cut down from there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 34:28; Exodus 24:18; Numbers 14:33–34"> And they returned from spying out the land at the end of forty days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 32:8; Numbers 20:1; Deuteronomy 1:19"> And they went and came to Moses, and to Aaron, and to all the congregation of the children of Israel, unto the wilderness of Paran, to Kadesh; and they brought back word unto them, and unto all the congregation, and showed them the fruit of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Exodus 3:17; Exodus 13:5"> And they told him, and said, We came unto the land whither thou didst send us, and truly doth it flow with milk and honey; and this is its fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:1–2; Deuteronomy 1:28; Numbers 13:33"> Nevertheless the people are strong that dwell in the land, and the cities are very strongly walled, and great; and the children of 'Anak also have we seen there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 14:43; Judges 6:3; Genesis 15:19–21"> The Amalekites dwell in the southern country; and the Hittites, and the Jebusites, and the Emorites, dwell in the mountains; and the Canaanites dwell by the sea, and by the margin of the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 14:24; Romans 8:31; Philippians 4:13"> And Caleb stilled the people toward Moses, and he said, We can easily go up, and take possession of it; for we are well able to overcome it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; Joshua 14:8; Hebrews 3:19"> But the men who had gone up with him said, We are not able to go up against the people; for they are stronger than we.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Amos 2:9; Numbers 14:36–37; Ezekiel 36:13–14"> And they brought up an evil report of the land which they had spied out unto the children of Israel, saying, The land through which we have passed to spy it out, is a land that consumeth its inhabitants; and all the people that we saw in it are men of a great stature.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; Isaiah 40:22; Genesis 6:4"> And there we saw the giants, the sons of 'Anak, of the giants' &#91;family&#93;: and we were in our own eyes as grasshoppers, and so were we in their eyes.</span>
