<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 22:1; Numbers 31:12; Numbers 33:50"> And the Lord spoke unto Moses in the plains of Moab by the Jordan opposite Jericho, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 14:3–4; Ezekiel 48:8; Joshua 21:2–42"> Command the children of Israel, that they give unto the Levites from the inheritance of their possession cities to dwell in; and an open space for the cities round about them shall ye give &#91;also&#93; unto the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:14; Joshua 21:11; Ezekiel 45:2"> And the cities shall serve them to dwell in; and their open spaces shall be for their cattle, and for their goods, and for all their requirements.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And the open spaces of the cities, which ye shall give unto the Levites, shall reach from the wall of the city and outward a thousand cubits round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And ye shall measure from without the city on the east side two thousand cubits, and on the south side two thousand cubits, and on the west side two thousand cubits, and on the north side two thousand cubits, with the city in the midst: this shall be to them the open spaces of the cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 21:13; Joshua 21:3; Psalms 9:9"> And the cities which ye shall give unto the Levites shall be the six cities of refuge, which ye shall appoint that the manslayer may flee thither; and in addition to them shall ye give forty and two cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 21:3–42; 1 Chronicles 6:54–81"> All the cities which ye shall &#91;thus&#93; give to the Levites shall be forty and eight cities, they with their open spaces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 26:54; Numbers 33:54; Joshua 21:1–42"> And the cities which ye shall give of the possession of the children of Israel, from the tribe that hath many shall ye give many; but from the one that hath few shall ye give few: every one according to its inheritance which it may inherit shall give of its cities unto the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And the Lord spoke unto Moses. saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:9; Deuteronomy 19:1–2; Leviticus 14:34"> Speak unto the children of Israel, and say unto them, When ye have passed over the Jordan into the land of Canaan:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 21:13; Joshua 20:2; Numbers 35:6"> Then ye shall appoint unto yourselves proper cities, that they be cities of refuge for you; and thither shall flee the manslayer who killeth any person at unawares.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 20:3–6; Deuteronomy 19:6; Joshua 20:9"> And these cities shall be unto you for a refuge from the avenger; that the manslayer die not, until he have stood before the congregation for trial.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the cities which ye shall give, shall be six cities of refuge unto you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:8–10; Deuteronomy 4:41–43; Joshua 20:7–9"> Three of these cities shall ye give on this side of the Jordan, and the three other cities shall ye give in the land of Canaan: cities of refuge shall they be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 15:16; Galatians 3:28; Romans 3:29"> For the children of Israel, and for the stranger, and for the sojourner among them, shall these six cities be for a refuge: that every one may flee thither that killeth any person at unawares.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 24:17; Genesis 9:5–6; Deuteronomy 19:11–13"> And if he have smitten him with an instrument of iron, so that he die, he is a murderer: the murderer shall surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 21:18"> And if he have smitten him with a stone which one can take in the hand, wherewith he may die, and he die, he is a murderer: the murderer shall surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Or if he have smitten him with an article of wood, which one can take in the hand, wherewith he may die, and he die, he is a murderer: the murderer shall surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 20:3; Deuteronomy 19:6; Deuteronomy 19:12"> The avenger of the blood himself shall slay the murderer; when he meeteth him, shall he slay him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 21:14; Deuteronomy 19:11; 2 Samuel 3:27"> And if he push against him out of hatred, or he have hurled at him &#91;any thing&#93; by lying in wait, that he die;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Or if in enmity he have smitten him with his hand, that he died, he that smote him shall surely be put to death; &#91;for&#93; he is a murderer: the avenger of the blood shall slay the murderer, when he meeteth with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 35:11; Exodus 21:13; Joshua 20:3"> But if he have pushed against him accidentally without enmity, or have cast upon him any thing without lying in wait,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Or with any stone wherewith a man may die, without seeing him, and he have let it fall upon him that he died; whereas he was not his enemy, and did not seek his harm:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 20:6; Numbers 35:12"> Then shall the congregation judge between the slayer and the avenger of the blood according to these institutions;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 29:7; Leviticus 4:3; Romans 3:24–26"> And the congregation shall deliver the manslayer out of the hand of the avenger of the blood, and the congregation shall restore him to the city of his refuge, whither he had fled; and he shall abide in it until the death of the high-priest, who hath been anointed with the holy oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> But if the manslayer should at any time pass the boundary of the city of his refuge, whither he may have fled;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 22:2; Deuteronomy 19:10; Deuteronomy 19:6"> And the avenger of the blood should find him beyond the boundary of the city of his refuge, and the avenger of the blood should kill the manslayer: he shall not be guilty of blood;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 3:14; Hebrews 10:26–30; Hebrews 6:4–8"> Because in the city of his refuge shall be remain until the death of the high-priest; but after the death of the high-priest the manslayer may return unto the land of his possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 27:11; Numbers 27:1"> And these things shall be unto you for a statute of justice throughout your generations, in all your dwellings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 10:28; Deuteronomy 19:15; Matthew 18:16"> Whoever it be that killeth a person, according to the testimony of witnesses shall the murderer be put to death; but one witness shall not testify against any person to cause him to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:11–13; Genesis 9:5–6; Psalms 51:14"> Moreover ye shall take no redemption money for the person of a murderer, who is guilty of death; but he shall surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 4:12; Galatians 3:10–13; Galatians 2:21"> And ye shall take no redemption money for him that hath fled to the city of his refuge, that he should come again to dwell in the land, until the death of the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 11:50–51; Psalms 106:38; Hosea 4:2–3"> And ye shall not defile the land wherein ye are; for the blood it is which defileth the land: and no atonement can be made unto the land for the blood which hath been shed therein, except through the blood of him that hath shed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 5:3; Psalms 135:21; Revelation 21:27"> And ye shall not render unclean the land which ye inhabit, in the midst of which I dwell; for I the Lord dwell in the midst of the children of Israel.</span>
