<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:1; Deuteronomy 3:8; Numbers 21:20"> And the children of Israel set forward, and encamped in the plains of Moab, on this side of the Jordan, opposite Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 11:25; Numbers 21:20–35; Numbers 21:3"> And Balak the son of Zippor saw all that Israel had done to the Emorites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 15:15; Isaiah 23:5; Joshua 9:24"> And Moab was greatly afraid of the people, because it was numerous; and Moab was horrified because of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 25:15–18; Numbers 31:8; Numbers 24:17"> And Moab said unto the elders of Midian, Now will this assemblage devour all that is round about us, as the ox devoureth the grass of the field; and Balak the son of Zippor was king of Moab at that time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:4; Joshua 24:9; Micah 6:5"> And he sent messengers unto Bil'am the son of Beor to Pethor, which is by the river, in the land of the children of his people, to have him called; saying, Behold, there is a people come out from Egypt; behold, it covereth the surface of the earth, and it is abiding opposite to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 24:9; Numbers 23:7–8; Acts 16:16"> And now do but come, curse me this people; for it is too mighty for me; peradventure I may be able to smite it, that I may drive it out of the land; for I know that he whom thou blessest is blessed, and he whom thou cursest is cursed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:7–8; Micah 3:11; Romans 16:18"> And the elders of Moab and the elders of Midian departed with the rewards of divination in their hand; and they came unto Bil'am, and spoke unto him the words of Balak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:12; Numbers 22:19–20; Jeremiah 12:2"> And he said unto them, Remain you here this night, and I will bring you word again, as the Lord may speak unto me; and the princes of Moab abode with Bil'am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 20:3; Numbers 22:20; Genesis 16:8"> And God came unto Bil'am, and said, Who are these men with thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Bil'am said unto God, Balak the son of Zippor, the king of Moab, hath sent unto me, &#91;saying,&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Behold, there is the people that is come out of Egypt, and covereth the face of the earth: now come, denounce it for me; peradventure I shall be able to fight against it, and drive it away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:5; Ephesians 1:3; Genesis 12:2"> And God said unto Bil'am, Thou shalt not go with them: thou shalt not curse the people; for it is blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:5; Numbers 22:14"> And Bil'am rose up in the morning, and said unto the princes of Balak, Go back to your land; for the Lord refuseth to give me leave to go with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 22:37; Numbers 22:13"> And the princes of Moab rose up, and they went unto Balak, and said, Bil'am refuseth to come with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 10:7–8; Numbers 22:7–8"> And Balak sent yet again princes, more in number, and more honorable than those.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And they came to Bil'am, and said to him, Thus hath said Balak the son of Zippor, Do not suffer thyself, I pray thee, to be prevented from coming unto me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 24:11; Esther 5:11; Matthew 4:8–9"> For I will honor thee greatly, and whatsoever thou mayest say unto me will I do: and only come, I pray thee, denounce me this people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 24:13; 1 Kings 22:14; 2 Chronicles 18:13"> And Bil'am answered and said unto the servants of Balak, If Balak would give me his house full of silver and gold, I could not transgress the order of the Lord my God, to do a small or a great thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jude 1:11; 2 Peter 2:3; 1 Timothy 6:9–10"> And now, I pray you, tarry ye also here this night, that I may know what the Lord will farther speak with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 23:12; Numbers 24:13; Numbers 23:26"> And God came unto Bil'am at night, and said unto him, If to call thee the men have come, rise up, go with them; but only the word which I shall speak unto thee, that shalt thou do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 1:15–16"> And Bil'am rose up in the morning, and saddled his ass, and went with the princes of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 4:24; Lamentations 2:4; Numbers 22:32"> And the anger of God was kindled, because he went; and an angel of the Lord placed himself in the way to be a hindrance to him; and he was riding upon his ass, and his two servants were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jude 1:11; 2 Peter 2:16; 1 Corinthians 1:27–29"> And the ass saw the angel of the Lord standing in the way, with his sword drawn in his hand; and the ass turned aside out of the way, and went into the field: and Bil'am smote the ass, to make her turn into the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> But the angel of the Lord stepped into a path between the vineyards, &#91;with&#93; a wall on this side, and a wall on that side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 47:12; Job 5:13–15"> And when the ass saw the angel of the Lord, she forced herself against the wall, and pressed Bil'am's foot against the wall: and he smote her again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hosea 2:6; Isaiah 26:11"> And the angel of the Lord went yet farther, and stood in a narrow place, where there was no way to turn either to the right or to the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 14:16; James 1:19; Proverbs 27:3–4"> And when the ass saw the angel of the Lord, she lay down under Bil'am: whereupon Bil'am's anger was kindled, and he smote the ass with a stick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Peter 2:16; Luke 1:37; Exodus 4:11"> And the Lord opened the mouth of the ass, and she said unto Bil'am, What have I done unto thee, that thou hast smitten me these three times?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 12:10; Matthew 15:19; Proverbs 12:16"> And Bil'am said unto the ass, Because thou hast mocked me: had I but a sword in my hand, I would assuredly have now killed thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Peter 2:16; 1 Corinthians 1:27–28"> And the ass said unto Bil'am, Am not I thy ass, upon which thou hast ridden from thy commencement unto this day? was I ever wont to do so unto thee? and he said, No.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 21:19; Luke 24:31; Acts 26:18"> Then the Lord opened the eyes of Bil'am, and he saw the angel of the Lord standing in the way, with his sword drawn in his hand: and he bowed down his head and prostrated himself on his face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 28:18; Acts 13:10; Numbers 22:22"> And the angel of the Lord said unto him, Wherefore hast thou smitten thy ass these three times? behold, I went out to be a hindrance; because the journey which is odious to me was too quickly begun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 16:33–35; Numbers 14:37; 1 Kings 13:24–28"> And the ass saw me, and turned aside before me these three times; if she had not turned aside from me, I would surely now not only have slain thee, but saved her alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 34:31–32; 2 Samuel 12:13; 1 Samuel 15:24"> And Bil'am said unto the angel of the Lord, I have sinned; for I knew not that thou wast standing against me in the way; but now, if it be evil in thy eyes, I will return home again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:9–12; Isaiah 37:26–29; Numbers 22:20–21"> And the angel of the Lord said unto Bil'am, Go with the men; however, only the word which I will speak unto thee, that shalt thou speak: and Bil'am went with the princes of Balak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 16:2; Acts 28:15; Jeremiah 48:20"> And when Balak heard that Bil'am was come, he went out to meet him unto 'Ir-Moab, which is on the border of Arnon, which is at the outmost end of the boundary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 24:11; John 5:44; Matthew 4:8–9"> And Balak said unto Bil'am, Did I not earnestly send unto thee to have thee called? wherefore camest thou not unto me? in truth, am I not able to honor thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 22:18; Proverbs 19:21; Numbers 23:16"> And Bil'am said unto Balak, Lo, I am come unto thee; have I now any power whatever to speak the least? the word that God may put in my mouth, that alone must I speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> And Bil'am went with Balak, and they came unto Kiryath-chuzoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 23:14; Proverbs 1:16; Numbers 23:2"> And Balak slew oxen and sheep, and sent to Bil'am and to the princes that were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 23:13; Numbers 25:2–3; 2 Chronicles 11:15"> And it came to pass in the morning, that Balak took Bil'am, and brought him up into the high places of Baal, and he saw thence a portion of the people.</span>
