<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:12; Jeremiah 31:28; Daniel 9:14"> Can a bird fall in a snare upon the earth, when there is no gin for him? is ever a snare taken up from the ground, when it hath caught nothing at all?</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:22; Zephaniah 1:9; Zechariah 5:3–4"> For they know not how to act rightly, saith the Lord, who treasure up violence and robbery in their palaces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 18:9–11; 2 Kings 17:3–6; 2 Kings 15:19"> Therefore thus hath said the Lord Eternal, The adversary &#91;is there&#93; and surroundeth the land: and he shall bring down from thee thy strength, and thy palaces shall be plundered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:34–37; Romans 11:4–5; Isaiah 31:4"> Thus hath said the Lord, As the shepherd snatcheth out of the mouth of the lion &#91;at most&#93; two leg-bones, or a tip of the ear: so shall be delivered the children of Israel that sit in Samaria on the corner of a bed, and on Damascus couches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:18–19; 2 Chronicles 24:19; Acts 20:21"> Hear ye, and give warning in the house of Jacob, saith the Lord Eternal, the God of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 23:15; Hosea 10:5–8; Hosea 10:14–15"> For on the day when I visit the transgressions of Israel upon him, will I also inflict punishment on the altars of Beth-el: and then shall be hewn off the horns of the altar, and they shall fall to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 22:39; Jeremiah 36:22; Amos 6:11"> And I will smite the winter-house together with the summer-house: and the houses of ivory shall disappear, and the great houses shall be no more, saith the Lord.</span>
