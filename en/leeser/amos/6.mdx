<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 32:9–11; James 5:5; Luke 12:17–20"> Woe to those that are free from care in Zion, and that are in safety on the mount of Samaria, who are named the chief of the nations, to whom the house of Israel come!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nahum 3:8; Genesis 10:10; 2 Kings 18:34"> Pass ye over unto Calneh, and see; and go from there to Chamath-rabbah; then go down to Gath of the Philistines: whether they be better than these kingdoms? or whether their territory be greater than your territory?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 9:10; Isaiah 56:12; Ezekiel 12:27"> &#91;Ye&#93; that deem far away the evil day, and cause the seat of violence to come near;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 3:12; Luke 16:19; 1 Samuel 25:36–38"> That lie upon beds of ivory, and are stretched out upon their couches, and eat lambs out of the flock, and calves out of the midst of the stall;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 5:12; Amos 5:23; Amos 8:3"> That chant to the sound of the psaltery, and like David's do they imagine their instrument of music to be;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 49:22; 1 Corinthians 12:26; Matthew 26:7–9"> That drink out of wine-bowls, and anoint themselves with the costliest of ointments; but who feel no pain for the wound of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 7:11; Daniel 5:4–6; Amos 5:5"> Therefore now shall they go into exile at the head of exiles, and the noisy banquet of those that were stretched out shall pass away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:14; Amos 4:2; Amos 8:7"> The Lord Eternal hath sworn by his own existence, saith the Lord the God of hosts, I abhor the pride of Jacob, and his palaces do I hate: therefore will I surrender up &#91;to the enemy&#93; the city with all that filleth it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 5:3; Job 1:2; Psalms 109:13"> And it shall come to pass, that if there remain ten men in one house, they shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 8:3; 1 Samuel 31:12; Amos 5:13"> And should a man's uncle or relative carry him forth, to bring out the bones out of the house, and say unto him that is in the recesses of the house, Is there yet any one with thee? he will say, There is no one left. Then will he say, Be silent; for we may not make mention of the name of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Amos 3:15; Isaiah 55:11; 2 Kings 25:9"> For, behold, the Lord commandeth, and he will smite the great house with breaches, and the little house with clefts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 5:7; Hosea 10:4; Amos 5:11–12"> Do horses ever run upon the rock? or will one plough there with oxen? that ye have turned justice into poison, and the fruit of righteousness into wormwood;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 28:14–15; Luke 12:19–20; 2 Kings 14:25"> Ye who rejoice for a thing of naught, who say, Have we not through our own strength procured ourselves horns?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 8:65; 2 Kings 14:25; Jeremiah 5:15–17"> For, behold, I will raise up against you a nation, O house of Israel, saith the Lord the God of hosts, and they shall oppress you from the entrance of Chamath unto the brook of the wilderness.</span>
