<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 7:1; Amos 7:7; Amos 7:4"> Thus did the Lord Eternal show unto me: and, behold, there was a basket of summer fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Amos 7:8; Lamentations 4:18; Jeremiah 24:1–3"> And he said, What dost thou see, 'Amos? And I said, A basket of summer fruit. Then said the Lord unto me, The end is come for my people Israel: I will not farther indulge them any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 5:23; Amos 6:9–10; Jeremiah 9:21–22"> And the songs of the temple shall become a wailing on that day, saith the Lord Eternal: many shall be the dead bodies; in every place shall men throw them down, &#91;saying,&#93; Be silent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 30:14; Psalms 14:4; Amos 5:11"> Hear this, O ye that are greedy to swallow the needy, and to ruin the poor of the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:15–21; Micah 6:10–11; Hosea 12:7"> Saying, When will the new moon be gone, that we may sell provision? and the sabbath, that we may open the corn-warehouses, making the ephah small, and increasing the shekel, and cheating with deceitful balances?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 2:6; Leviticus 25:39–42; Nehemiah 5:8"> That we may buy the poor for silver, and the needy for a pair of shoes; and even sell the refuse of the corn?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 6:8; Hosea 9:9; Hosea 8:13"> Sworn hath the Lord by the excellency of Jacob, Surely I will not forget to eternity all their works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 9:5; Hosea 4:3; Jeremiah 46:8"> Shall because of this the land not tremble, and mourn every one that dwelleth therein? and shall it not rise up like a stream wholly, and roll onward and sink again like the stream of Egypt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 15:33; Matthew 27:45; Jeremiah 15:9"> And it shall come to pass on that day, saith the Lord Eternal, that I will cause the sun to set at noon, and I will bring darkness over the earth on a bright day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:26; Zechariah 12:10; Job 20:23"> And I will change your feasts into mourning, and all your songs into lamentations; and I will bring upon all loins sackcloth, and upon every head baldness; and I will cause &#91;the land&#93; to mourn as one doth for an only son, and its end to be as a day of bitter &#91;complaint&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 74:9; 1 Samuel 3:1; 2 Chronicles 15:3"> Behold, days are coming, saith the Lord Eternal, when I will send a famine in the land, not a famine for bread, nor a thirst for water, but to hear the words of the Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:7–10; Matthew 24:23–26; Daniel 12:4"> And they will wander about from sea to sea, and from the north even to the east, they will roam about to seek the word of the Lord; but they shall not find it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 2:3; Lamentations 1:18; Lamentations 2:21"> On that day shall the fair virgins and the young men faint for thirst;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 5:5; 1 Kings 12:28–29; Deuteronomy 9:21"> Those that swear by the guilt of Samaria, and say, As thy god liveth, O Dan; and, As liveth the worshipped idol of Beer-sheba',—yea, they shall fall, and never rise up again.</span>
