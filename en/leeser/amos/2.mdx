<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:8–9; Ezekiel 25:8–9; Amos 1:3"> Thus hath said the Lord, For three transgressions of Moab, and for four, will I not turn away their punishment; because he burnt the bones of the king of Edom into lime;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:24; Jeremiah 48:41; Amos 1:14"> And I will send a fire against Moab, which shall devour the palaces of Keriyoth: and Moab shall die in the tumult, in the shouting, amidst the sound of the cornet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 24:17; Jeremiah 48:7; Jeremiah 48:25"> And I will cut off the judges from her midst, and all her princes will I slay, with him, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:24; Ezekiel 20:16; Ezekiel 20:13"> Thus hath said the Lord, For three transgressions of Judah, and for four, will I not turn away their punishment; because they have despised the law of the Lord, and did not keep his statutes, and their lying idols caused them to err, after which their fathers had walked;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 8:14; Jeremiah 17:27; Jeremiah 21:10"> And I will send out a fire against Judah, which shall devour the palaces of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 3:3; Micah 3:2–3; Amos 5:11–12"> Thus hath said the Lord, For three transgressions of Israel, and for four, will I not turn away their punishment; because they sold for silver the righteous, and the needy for a pair of shoes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 5:12; Isaiah 10:2; Ezekiel 22:11"> That are eager after the dust of the earth on the head of the poor, and turn aside the way of the meek: and a man and his father will go in unto the same young woman, in order to profane my holy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:12–17; Exodus 22:26–27; Amos 6:6"> And upon pledged garments they stretch themselves out by every altar, and the wine of the condemned do they drink in the house of their gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 18:16; Malachi 4:1; Deuteronomy 3:11"> Yet have I destroyed the Emorite from before them, whose height was like the height of cedars, and who was strong as the oaks; but I destroyed his fruit from above, and his roots from beneath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:7; Exodus 12:51; Amos 3:1"> And it was I who have brought you up from the land of Egypt, and led you forty years through the wilderness, to take possession of the land of the Emorite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:25; Numbers 6:2–3; 1 Kings 20:41"> And I have raised up of your sons for prophets, and of your young men for nazarites: is it not even thus, O ye children of Israel? saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 7:13; Isaiah 30:10; Jeremiah 11:21"> But ye have given the nazarites wine to drink; and concerning the prophets have ye commanded, saying, Ye shall not prophesy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:14; Isaiah 43:24; Ezekiel 16:43"> Behold, I press down &#91;the ground&#93; under you, as the wagon presseth &#91;it&#93; down that is full of sheaves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23; Psalms 33:16; Ecclesiastes 9:11"> And refuge shall vanish from the swift, and the strong shall not make use of his force, neither shall the mighty man escape with his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 33:16–17; Ezekiel 39:3"> And he that handleth the bow shall not be able to stand; and he that is swift of foot shall not escape: neither shall he that rideth the horse escape with his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 14:52; Jeremiah 48:41; 2 Kings 7:8–20"> And he that is most courageous hearted among the mighty shall flee away naked on that day, saith the Lord.</span>
