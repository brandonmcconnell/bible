<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 22:12; Amos 6:1; Amos 5:11"> Hear this word, O ye cows of Bashan, that are on the mount of Samaria, who oppress the poor, who crush the needy, who say to their lords, Bring, and let us drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:16; Psalms 89:35; Habakkuk 1:15–16"> Sworn hath the Lord Eternal by his holiness, that, lo, days are coming over you, when men will carry you away with hooks, and your posterity with fishhooks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:5; Ezekiel 12:12; Matthew 16:26"> And through breaches in the wall shall ye go out, every one through that before her: and ye shall cast off your proud greatness, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:39; Hosea 4:15; Numbers 28:3–4"> Go then to Beth-el, and transgress; to Gilgal, &#91;and&#93; multiply transgression; and bring in the morning your sacrifices, after three days your tithes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 22:18–21; Psalms 81:12; Hosea 9:10"> And burn of leaven a sacrifice of thanksgiving, and proclaim and publish freewill-offerings; for so do you love &#91;to do&#93;, O ye children of Israel, saith the Lord Eternal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Haggai 2:17; Jeremiah 5:3; Isaiah 9:13"> But, I also had indeed given you cleanness of teeth in all your cities, and want of bread in all your places: and yet have ye not returned unto me, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 9:26; Jeremiah 3:3; 2 Chronicles 7:13–14"> And I also had indeed withholden from you the rain, when it was yet three months to the harvest; and I caused it to rain upon one city, and upon another city I caused it not to rain; one piece of land was rained upon, and another piece whereupon it rained not became dried up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 4:6; 1 Kings 18:5; Haggai 1:6"> And two or three cities wandered unto one city, to drink water; but they were not satisfied: and yet have ye not returned unto me, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:22; Haggai 2:17; Joel 1:4"> I had smitten you with blasting and mildew; the multitude of your gardens and your vineyards and your fig-trees and your olive-trees did the caterpillar devour: and yet have ye not returned unto me, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:60; Leviticus 26:25; Joel 2:20"> I had sent out against you the pestilence after the manner of Egypt; I slew your young men with the sword, together with your captive horses; and I had caused the stench of your camps to ascend even into your nostrils; and yet ye have not returned unto me, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 13:19; Zechariah 3:2; Genesis 19:24–25"> I had produced an overthrow among you, like the overthrow by God of Sodom and Gomorrah, and ye became like a fire-brand snatched out of the burning: and yet have ye not returned unto me, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2–4; James 4:1–10; Amos 9:1–4"> Therefore thus will I do unto thee, O Israel: because then I will do this unto thee, prepare to meet thy God, O Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Amos 5:8; Micah 1:3; Amos 9:6"> For, lo, he that formeth the mountains, and createth the wind, and declareth unto man what is his thought, that maketh the morning-dawn &#91;and&#93; darkness, and treadeth upon the high places of the earth, — The Lord, the God of hosts, is his name.</span>
