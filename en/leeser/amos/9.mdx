<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:53; Psalms 139:7–10; Obadiah 1:4"> Though they were to creep down into the nether world, thence would my hand fetch them; and though they were to climb up to the heavens, thence would I bring them down;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:16; Jeremiah 23:23–24; Isaiah 27:1"> And though they were to hide themselves on the top of Carmel, thence would I search and take them out; and though they were to conceal themselves from before my eyes in the bottom of the sea, thence would I command the serpent, that he should bite them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:33; Jeremiah 21:10; Jeremiah 44:11"> And though they were to go into captivity before their enemies, thence would I command the sword, that it should slay them: and I will set my eye upon them for evil, and not for good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 8:8; Psalms 46:6; Isaiah 64:1"> And the Lord Eternal of hosts it is that toucheth the earth, and she melteth away, and all that dwell thereon shall mourn; and she riseth up like a stream wholly; and she sinketh like the stream of Egypt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 5:8; Amos 4:13; Psalms 104:3"> That buildeth in the heavens his steps, and hath founded his vault over the earth; that calleth for the waters of the sea, and poureth them out over the face of the earth: The Lord is his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 47:4; Deuteronomy 2:23; 2 Kings 16:9"> Are ye not like the children of the Ethiopians unto me, O children of Israel? saith the Lord: have I not brought up Israel out of the land of Egypt? and the Philistines from Caphtor, and the Syrians from Kir?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:11; Amos 9:4; Jeremiah 44:27"> Behold, the eyes of the Lord Eternal are upon the sinful kingdom, and I will destroy it from off the face of the earth; save only that I will not utterly destroy the house of Jacob, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 22:31; Deuteronomy 28:64; Isaiah 30:28"> For, lo, I will give the command, and I will shake about among all the nations the house of Israel, as one shaketh things in a sieve, while not the least piece falleth down upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 6:3; Isaiah 33:14; Amos 6:1"> By the sword shall die all the sinners of my people, who say, The evil will not come near &#91;us&#93;, nor hasten along for our sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 15:15–17; Jeremiah 33:14–16; Isaiah 16:5"> On that day will I raise up the tabernacle of David which is fallen; and I will close up its breaches; and its ruins will I raise up, and I will rebuild it as in days of old:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 43:7; Isaiah 65:1; Acts 15:17"> In order that they may take possession of the remnant of Edom, and of all the nations, which are called by my name, saith the Lord that doth this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joel 3:18; John 4:35; Leviticus 26:5"> Behold, days are coming, saith the Lord, when the ploughman shall come close up to the harvester, and the treader of the grapes to the one that scattereth the seed: and the mountains shall drop with sweet new wine, and all the hills shall melt away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 61:4; Jeremiah 30:3; Jeremiah 30:18"> And I will bring back the captivity of my people Israel, and they shall build the wasted cities, and dwell therein; and they shall plant vineyards, and drink their wine; and they shall lay out gardens, and eat their fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:6; Ezekiel 37:25; Isaiah 60:21"> And I will plant them upon their own soil, and they shall not be pulled up any more out of their land which I have given unto them, saith the Lord thy God.</span>
