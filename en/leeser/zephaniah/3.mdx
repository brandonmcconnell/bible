<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:6; Isaiah 30:12; Isaiah 59:13"> Woe to her that is rebellious and polluted, to the oppressing city!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:3; Jeremiah 22:21; Jeremiah 7:23–28"> She hearkened not to any voice; she accepted no correction; in the Lord she did not trust; to her God she drew not near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:8; Ezekiel 22:25–27; Jeremiah 5:6"> Her princes in her midst are roaring lions: her judges are evening wolves, they leave not a bone for the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:26; Hosea 9:7; Malachi 2:8"> Her prophets are thoughtless men of treachery: her priests have profaned the sanctuary, they have done violence to the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Psalms 145:17; Lamentations 3:23"> The just Lord is in her midst, he will not do wrong; morning after morning doth he bring his justice to the light &#91;of day&#93;, it never faileth; but the unjust knoweth no shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:6; Zephaniah 2:5; 1 Corinthians 10:11"> I have cut off nations; destroyed are their battlements; I have laid in ruins their streets, so that none passeth through; their cities are wasted, without a man, without an inhabitant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 9:9; Jeremiah 36:3; Micah 2:1–2"> I said, Surely thou wilt fear me, thou wilt accept correction; so that her dwelling should not be cut off, all that I had decreed to bring over her; but they rose up early, they acted corruptly in all their doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joel 3:2; Zephaniah 1:18; Micah 7:7"> Therefore wait but for me, saith the Lord, for the day that I rise up to the prey; for my judgment &#91;cometh&#93; to gather the nations, for me to assemble the kingdoms, to pour over them my indignation, all the fierceness of my anger; for through the fire of my jealousy shall all the earth be devoured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 11:1; Psalms 22:27; Isaiah 19:18"> Yea then will I change unto the people a pure language, that they may all call on the name of the Lord, to serve him with one accord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 11:11; Psalms 68:31; Isaiah 18:1"> From beyond the rivers of Cush shall they bring my suppliants, even the assembly of my dispersed, as an offering unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 54:4; Joel 2:26–27; Isaiah 11:9"> On that day shalt thou not be ashamed because of all thy doings, whereby thou hast transgressed against me; for then will I remove out of the midst of thee those that rejoice in thy pride, and thou shalt never more be haughty again on my holy mount.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 14:32; Nahum 1:7; Zechariah 13:8–9"> And I will leave remaining in the midst of thee a humble and poor people, and they shall trust in the name of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 14:5; Zephaniah 2:7; Micah 4:7"> The remnant of Israel shall not do injustice, nor speak lies; and there shall not be found in their mouth a deceitful tongue; for they shall feed and lie down, with none to make them afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 12:6; Zechariah 2:10–11; Micah 4:8"> Sing, O daughter of Zion; shout, O Israel; rejoice and be glad with all thy heart, O daughter of Jerusalem!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 51:22; Zephaniah 3:5; Isaiah 33:22"> The Lord hath removed thy punishment, he hath cleared away thy enemy: the king of Israel, the Lord, is in the midst of thee; thou shalt not see evil any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 12:12; Isaiah 35:3–4; Isaiah 41:10"> On that day shall it be said to Jerusalem, Fear thou not: &#91;to&#93; Zion, Let not thy hands become weak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 149:4; Psalms 147:11; Jeremiah 32:41"> The Lord thy God is in the midst of thee, the mighty one who will save; he will be glad over thee with rejoicing; he will be silent in his love, he will exult over thee with song.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 42:2–4; Ezekiel 36:24; Lamentations 2:6–7"> Those that mourn far away from the festive assembly do I gather, those that were separated from thee, &#91;that have borne&#93; for thee the burden of reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 49:25–26; Isaiah 60:14; Jeremiah 30:16"> Behold, I will deal &#91;severely&#93; with all that afflict thee at that time: and I will save her that halteth, and her that was driven off will I gather; and I will render them a praise and a famous name on all the earth where they have been put to shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:14; Malachi 3:12; Isaiah 56:5"> At that time will I bring you back, even in the time that I gather you; for I will make you for a name and for a praise among all people of the earth, when I bring back again your captives before your eyes, saith the Lord.</span>
