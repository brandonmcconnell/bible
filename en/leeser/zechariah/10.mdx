<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="James 5:16–18; Matthew 7:7–8; Isaiah 44:3"> Ask from the Lord rain at the time of the latter rain, &#91;from&#93; the Lord who maketh lightning-clouds: and he will give unto them showers of rain, to every herb in the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; Jeremiah 27:9; Ezekiel 34:5"> For the Theraphim speak vanity, and the diviners foresee a lie, and those that have dreams speak what is false, with nought do they comfort: therefore do they move about like a flock, they bleat, because &#91;they have&#93; no shepherd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2; Ezekiel 34:7–10; Jeremiah 23:1–2"> Against the shepherds is my anger kindled, and on the he-goats will I inflict punishment; for the Lord of hosts thinketh of his flock, the house of Judah, and maketh them as his elegant horse in the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 9:10; Zechariah 9:8; Numbers 24:17"> Out of him cometh forth the corner &#91;-stone&#93;, out of him the tent-nail, out of him the battle-bow, out of him every ruler &#91;of others&#93; together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Haggai 2:22; Zechariah 9:13; Psalms 20:7"> And they shall be like mighty men, treading down &#91;their enemies&#93; in the mire of the streets in the battle, and they shall fight, because the Lord is with them: and the riders on horses shall be made ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 13:9; Zechariah 10:12; Jeremiah 3:18"> And I will strengthen the house of Judah, and the house of Joseph will I save, and I will bring them again to their own homes; for I have mercy upon them, and they shall be as though I had never cast them off; for I am the Lord their God, and I will answer their prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 9:15; Zechariah 9:17; 1 Samuel 2:1"> And they of Ephraim shall be like a mighty man, and their heart shall rejoice as though from wine: and their children shall see it, and be rejoiced; their heart shall be glad in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 5:26; Isaiah 7:18; Jeremiah 33:22"> I will call for them, and gather them; for I have redeemed them: and they shall increase as they have increased &#91;formerly&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:9; Hosea 2:23; Esther 8:17"> And when I shall have scattered them among the people, they will remember me in the far-off countries: therefore shall they live with their children, and return again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 11:11; Micah 7:14; Zechariah 8:7"> And I will bring them back again out of the land of Egypt, and out of Assyria will I gather them; and into the land of Gil'ad and Lebanon will I bring them, and it shall not be sufficient for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:13; Micah 5:5–6; Isaiah 43:2"> And he will pass through the sea &#91;with&#93; distress, and he will smite in the sea the waves, and there shall dry up all the deeps of the stream: and there shall be brought down the pride of Assyria, and the sceptre of Egypt shall depart away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Isaiah 2:5; Zechariah 10:6"> And I will strengthen them in the Lord, and in his name shall they ever walk, saith the Lord.</span>
