<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:3; Zechariah 1:16; Ezekiel 47:4"> (2:5) And I lifted up my eyes, and looked, and behold, there was a man with a measure-cord in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:39; Ezekiel 45:6; Revelation 21:15–17"> (2:6) Then said I, Whither art thou going? And he said unto me, To measure Jerusalem, to see what is her breadth, and what is her length.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 1:19; Zechariah 1:8–11; Zechariah 4:5"> (2:7) And, behold, the angel that spoke with me went out, and another angel came out to meet him;</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 4:5; Zechariah 9:8; Psalms 3:3"> (2:9) But I—I will be unto her, saith the Lord, a wall of fire round about, and for glory will I be in the midst of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 48:20; Ezekiel 17:21; Isaiah 52:11–12"> (2:10) Up! up! and flee away from the land of the north saith the Lord; for as the four winds of the heaven have I spread you abroad, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 48:20; Genesis 19:17; Isaiah 52:11"> (2:11) Up, Zion, escape, thou that dwellest with the daughter of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 17:8; Deuteronomy 32:10; John 15:21–23"> (2:12) For thus hath said the Lord of hosts, For the sake of &#91;your&#93; glory hath he sent me unto the nations that have despoiled you; for whoever toucheth you, toucheth the apple of his eye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 19:16; Isaiah 14:2; Zechariah 4:9"> (2:13) For, behold, I will swing my hand over them, and they shall be a spoil to those that served them: and ye shall acknowledge that the Lord of hosts hath sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 28:20; John 1:14; Zephaniah 3:14–15"> (2:14) Sing, and rejoice, O daughter of Zion; for, lo, I am coming, and I will dwell in the midst of thee, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 11:15; 1 Peter 2:9–10; Acts 28:28"> (2:15) And many nations will join themselves to the Lord on that day, and they shall be unto me for a people: and I will dwell in the midst of thee, and thou shalt know that the Lord of hosts hath sent me unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zechariah 1:17; Deuteronomy 32:9; Jeremiah 10:16"> (2:16) And the Lord will take Judah as his inheritance upon the holy land; and he will again make choice of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:20; Psalms 68:5; Psalms 78:65"> (2:17) Be silent, all flesh! before the Lord; for he is waked up out of his holy habitation.</span>
