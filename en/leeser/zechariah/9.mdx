<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 1:3–5; Jeremiah 49:23–27; Amos 3:12"> The prophecy of the word of the Lord concerning the land of Chadrach, and Damascus his resting-place; for unto the Lord &#91;will look&#93; the eye of men, and &#91;that of&#93; all the tribes of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:23; Ezekiel 28:12; Ezekiel 28:2–5"> And also concerning Chamath that is bordering thereon, Tyre, and Zidon, though it be very wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:33; Job 27:16; Ezekiel 28:4–5"> And though Tyre have built herself a strong-hold, and heaped up silver as the dust, and fine gold as the mire of the streets:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:18; Ezekiel 26:17; Isaiah 23:1–7"> Behold, the Lord will drive her out, and he will strike down her power into the sea; and she herself shall be devoured with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 8:26; Revelation 18:9–17; Jeremiah 47:1"> Ashkelon shall see it, and fear; Gazzah also, and tremble greatly: and 'Ekron, for her trust will be made ashamed: and the king shall vanish from Gazzah, and Ashkelon shall not be inhabited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 1:8; Zephaniah 2:10; 1 Peter 5:5"> And aliens shall dwell in Ashdod, and I will cut off the pride of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 3:12; Ezekiel 16:57–61; Isaiah 60:14–16"> And I will remove their bloody &#91;-sacrifices&#93; out of their mouth, and their abominations from between their teeth; and their land also shall be left for our God, and it shall be as a prince's &#91;dwelling&#93; in Judah, and 'Ekron shall be like Jebusi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 54:14; Isaiah 60:18; Isaiah 52:1"> And I will encamp about my house against armies, against those that pass to and fro, and there shall not pass over them any more an oppressor; for now do I look &#91;on them&#93; with my eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 21:4–7; Zephaniah 3:14–15; John 12:13–16"> Be greatly glad, O daughter of Zion; shout, O daughter of Jerusalem! behold, thy King will come unto thee, righteous and victorious is he, lowly, and riding upon an ass, and upon a colt the foal of a she-ass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 1:7; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Hosea 2:18"> And I will cut off chariots from Ephraim, and horses from Jerusalem, and there shall be cut off the battle-bow, and he shall speak peace unto the nations; and his dominion shall be from sea to sea, and from the river to the ends of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 51:14; Exodus 24:8; Psalms 102:19–21"> As for thee also, because of the blood of thy covenant, do I send forth thy prisoners out of the pit wherein there is no water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 42:10; Isaiah 61:7; Lamentations 3:21–22"> Return you to the strong-hold, ye hopeful prisoners: even today do I declare, that I will recompense twofold &#91;good&#93; unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 17:14; Zechariah 10:3–7; Isaiah 49:2"> For I do bend Judah for me, grasp Ephraim &#91;as&#93; a bow; and I will stir up thy sons, O Zion, against thy sons, O Javan, and I will render thee as the sword of a mighty man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 18:14; Isaiah 66:15; Psalms 144:5–6"> And the Lord will appear over them, and then will go forth like the lightning his arrow: and the Lord Eternal will blow on the cornet, and he will go along in the tempests of the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 12:6; Zechariah 10:7; Zechariah 10:5"> The Lord of hosts will be a shield over them; and they shall devour &#91;the prey&#93;, and subdue the sling-stones; and they shall drink, make a noise as one &#91;drunken with&#93; wine; and they shall be filled like the offering-bowls, like the corners of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 62:3; Luke 12:32; Haggai 2:23"> And the Lord their God will save them on that day as the flock of his people; for &#91;like&#93; the stones of a crown, will they elevate themselves over his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 33:17; Isaiah 62:8–9; 1 John 4:8–11"> For how great will be &#91;that generation's&#93; happiness, and how great its beauty! corn shall make the young men sing joyfully, and new wine the virgins.</span>
