<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:11; John 1:29; Psalms 51:2"> On that day shall there be a fountain opened to the house of David and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem, for cleansing from sin and for purification.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 22:22; Jeremiah 23:14–15; Hosea 2:17"> And it shall come to pass on that day, saith the Lord of hosts, that I will cut off the names of the idols out of the land, and they shall not be remembered any more: and also the prophets and the unclean spirit will I remove out of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:6–11; Deuteronomy 18:20; Ezekiel 14:9"> And it shall come to pass, if any one should yet prophesy, that his father and his mother who have begotten him shall say unto him, Thou shalt not live; for falsely hast thou spoken in the name of the Lord: and his father and his mother who have begotten him shall thrust him through when he prophesieth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 3:6–7; Matthew 3:4; Revelation 11:3"> And it shall come to pass on that day, that the prophets shall be ashamed every one because of his vision, when he prophesieth; and they shall not clothe themselves with a hairy cloak in order to deceive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 7:14; Acts 19:17–20"> But he will say, I am no prophet, a man that tilleth the ground am I; for some one hath taught me to keep cattle from my youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:35; Psalms 22:16; Proverbs 27:5–6"> And should some one say unto him, What are these wounds between thy hands? Then will he say, Those with which I have been wounded in the house of my &#91;vicious&#93; friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 14:27; Matthew 26:31; John 16:32"> Awake, O sword, against my shepherd, and against the man whom I have associated with me, saith the Lord of hosts: smite the shepherd, and the sheep shall be scattered; but I will turn my hand toward the feeble ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:12; Revelation 16:19; Ezekiel 5:2–4"> And it shall come to pass, that in all the land, saith the Lord, two parts &#91;of those&#93; therein shall be cut off, shall perish; but the third part shall be left therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Jeremiah 29:11–12; Acts 2:21"> And I will bring the third part into the fire, and I will refine them as one refineth silver, and will probe them as gold is probed: they will call on my name, and I will answer their prayer; I will say, They arc my people; and they will say, The Lord is my God.</span>
