<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 42:5; Jeremiah 51:15; Isaiah 57:16"> The prophecy of the word of the Lord concerning Israel, Saith the Lord, who stretcheth forth the heavens, and layeth the foundation of the earth, and formeth the spirit of man within him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 14:14; Psalms 75:8; Isaiah 51:22–23"> Behold, I will make Jerusalem a cup of confusion unto all the people round about; and also against Judah, who will have to be at the siege against Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 21:44; Zechariah 14:2–4; Daniel 2:34–35"> And it shall come to pass on that day that I will make Jerusalem a burdensome stone for all the people; all that burden themselves with it shall be severely cut: yet then will be gathered together against it all the nations of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:28; Zechariah 12:3; Zechariah 12:6"> On that day, saith the Lord, will I smite every horse with dizziness, and his rider with madness; but over the house of Judah will I open my eyes, while I will smite every horse of the people with blindness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 10:12; Joel 3:16; Psalms 18:32"> And the chiefs of Judah will say in their heart, A strong support for me are the inhabitants of Jerusalem through the Lord of hosts their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Obadiah 1:18; Micah 5:5–8; Zechariah 2:4"> On that day will I make the chiefs of Judah like a fire-hearth among the wood, and like a torch of fire among sheaves; and they shall devour on the right hand and on the left all the people round about; and Jerusalem shall be inhabited again in her own place, even in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:18; Romans 3:27; Luke 1:51–53"> The Lord also will save the tents of Judah first: in order that the glory of the house of David and the glory of the inhabitants of Jerusalem shall not become boastful over Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Micah 5:2–4; Zechariah 9:8; Joel 3:16–17"> On that day will the Lord be a shield around the inhabitants of Jerusalem: and the feeblest among them shall be on that day like David; and the house of David shall be like divine beings, like an angel of the Lord before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 54:17; Zechariah 14:2–3; Haggai 2:22"> And it shall come to pass on that day, that I will seek to destroy all the nations that are come against Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; Ezekiel 39:29; Joel 2:28–29"> But I will pour out over the house of David, and over the inhabitants of Jerusalem, the spirit of grace and of supplications: and they will look up toward me &#91;for every one&#93; whom they have thrust through, and they will lament for him, as one lamenteth for an only son, and weep bitterly for him, as one weepeth bitterly for the first-born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:24; 2 Kings 23:29"> On that day will the lamentation be great in Jerusalem, like the lamentation at Hadadrimmon in the valley of Megiddon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zechariah 7:3; Luke 3:31; 2 Samuel 5:14"> And the land will mourn, every family apart by itself: the family of the house of David apart, and their wives apart; the family of the house of Nathan apart, and their wives apart;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:7; 2 Samuel 16:5; 1 Chronicles 23:10"> The family of the house of Levi apart, and their wives apart; the family of Shim'i apart, and their wives apart;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 9:12"> All the families that remain, every family apart by itself, and their wives apart.</span>
