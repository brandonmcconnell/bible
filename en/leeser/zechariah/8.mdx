<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of the Lord of hosts came, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joel 2:18; Zechariah 1:14–16; Isaiah 42:13–14"> Thus hath said the Lord of hosts, I am jealous for Zion with a great jealousy, and with great fury am I jealous for her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 1:16; Zechariah 2:10–11; John 1:14"> Thus hath said the Lord, I return unto Zion, and will dwell in the midst of Jerusalem: and Jerusalem shall be called, The city of truth; and the mount of the Lord of hosts, The holy mount.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 65:20–22; Job 5:26; Lamentations 5:11–15"> Thus hath said the Lord of hosts, Again shall there sit old men and old women in the streets of Jerusalem, and every one with his staff in his hand because of their multitude of years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:13; Matthew 11:16–17; Jeremiah 30:19–20"> And the streets of the city shall be full of boys and girls playing in her streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:27; Romans 4:20–21; Numbers 11:22–23"> Thus hath said the Lord of hosts, If it should be marvelous in the eyes of the remnant of this people in those days, should it also be marvelous in my eyes? saith the Lord of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:8; Romans 11:25–27; Malachi 1:11"> Thus hath said the Lord of hosts, Behold, I will save my people from the east country and from the country of the setting of the sun;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:28; Ezekiel 11:20; Jeremiah 31:33"> And I will bring them &#91;back&#93;, that they may dwell in the midst of Jerusalem: and they shall be unto me for a people, and I will be unto them for a God, in truth and in righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 35:4; 1 Chronicles 22:13; Ezra 5:1–2"> Thus hath said the Lord of hosts, Let your hands be strong, ye that hear in these days these words out of the mouth of the prophets, who &#91;spoke&#93; on the day that the foundation of the house of the Lord of hosts was laid, when the temple was to be built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Haggai 1:6–11; Haggai 2:16–18; Amos 3:6"> For before those days there was no reward for man, nor any reward for beast; and for him that went out or came in there was no peace, because of the oppressor: and I let loose all men, every one against his neighbor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 12:1; Haggai 2:19; Zechariah 8:8–9"> But now I am no more as in the former days unto the residue of this people, saith the Lord of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 26:4–5; Deuteronomy 33:13; Genesis 27:28"> For the seed shall be undisturbed; the vine shall give its fruit, and the ground shall give her production, and the heavens shall give their dew: and I will bestow on the remnant of this people all these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:20; Zechariah 8:9; Psalms 72:17"> And it shall come to pass, that, in the same degree as ye have been a curse among the nations, O house of Judah, and house of Israel, so will I save you and ye shall be a blessing: fear not; let your hands be strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:28; Ezekiel 24:14; Jeremiah 4:28"> For thus hath said the Lord of hosts, As I had purposed to do you evil, when your fathers incensed me, saith the Lord of hosts, and I bethought myself not:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 8:13; Micah 7:18–20; Luke 12:32"> So do I again purpose in these days to do well unto Jerusalem and to the house of Judah; fear ye not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ephesians 4:25; Zechariah 7:9; Psalms 15:2"> These are the things that ye shall do, Speak ye the truth every man to his neighbor; &#91;with&#93; truth and the judgment of peace judge ye in your gates;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 6:16–19; Micah 2:1–3; Jeremiah 44:4"> And let none of you think evil in your hearts against his neighbor; and love not a false oath, for all these are what I hate, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And the word of the Lord of hosts came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 8:16; Jeremiah 39:2; Zechariah 7:5"> Thus hath said the Lord of hosts, The fast of the fourth, and the fast of the fifth, and the fast of the seventh, and the fast of the tenth &#91;month&#93;, shall become to the house of Judah gladness and joy, and merry festivals: only love ye the truth and peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 2:2–3; Micah 4:1–2; Zechariah 2:11"> Thus hath said the Lord of hosts, &#91;A time&#93; shall yet be !!when there shall come people, and the inhabitants of many cities;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zechariah 7:2; Hosea 6:3; Psalms 146:1–2"> And the inhabitants of one &#91;city&#93; shall go to another, saying, Let us only go to pray before the Lord, and to seek the Lord of hosts: I too will likewise go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zechariah 8:21; Isaiah 60:3–22; Revelation 21:24"> And many people and strong nations shall come to seek the Lord of hosts in Jerusalem, and to pray before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 55:5; 1 Corinthians 14:25; Isaiah 60:3"> Thus hath said the Lord of hosts, In those days &#91;it shall happen&#93;, that ten men out of all the languages of the nations shall take hold—yea, they shall take hold of the skirt of him that is a Jew, saying, Let us go with you; for we have heard that God is with you.</span>
