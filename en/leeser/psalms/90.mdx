<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:27; Psalms 71:3; 1 John 4:16"> BOOK FOURTH: “A prayer of Moses the man of God.” Lord, a place of refuge hast thou been unto us in all generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 13:8; Genesis 1:1; Psalms 102:24–27"> Before yet the mountains were brought forth, or thou hadst ever produced the earth and the world, even from everlasting to everlasting, thou art God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Psalms 146:4"> Thou turnest man to contrition, and sayest, Return ye children of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Peter 3:8; Psalms 39:5; Matthew 24:43"> For a thousand years are in thy eyes but as the yesterday when it is past, and as a watch in the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 40:6; Psalms 73:20; Job 22:16"> Thou carriest them away as with a flood; they are as a sleep: in the morning &#91;they grow&#93; like the grass which changeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 14:2; Psalms 92:7; James 1:11"> In the morning it blossometh, and is changed: in the evening it is mowed off, and withereth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 39:11; Psalms 59:13; Exodus 14:24"> For &#91;thus&#93; are we consumed by thy anger, and by thy fury are we terrified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:17; Ecclesiastes 12:14; Hebrews 4:12–13"> Thou hast set our iniquities before thee, our concealed sins before the light of thy countenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 78:33; Psalms 39:5; Psalms 90:4"> For all our days are passed away in thy wrath: we consume our years like a word that is spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 4:14; Psalms 78:39; 2 Samuel 19:35"> The days of our years in this life are seventy years; and if by uncommon vigor they be eighty, yet is their greatness trouble and mishap; for it soon hasteneth off, and we fly away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 26:24; Isaiah 33:14; Nahum 1:6"> Who knoweth the strength of thy anger, and thy wrath which is like the fear of thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 5:16–17; Psalms 39:4; John 9:4"> Let us then know how to number our days, that we may obtain a heart endowed with wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 106:45; Psalms 135:14; Deuteronomy 32:36"> Return, O Lord, how long yet? and bethink thee concerning thy servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 23:6; Psalms 86:4; Psalms 31:7"> O satisfy us in the morning with thy kindness, that we may be glad and rejoice throughout all our days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 126:5–6; Psalms 30:5; John 16:20"> Cause us to rejoice as many days as those wherein thou hast afflicted us, the years wherein we have seen unhappiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:2; Psalms 44:1; Deuteronomy 32:4"> Let thy act be visible on thy servants, and thy majesty over their children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 16:3; 1 Corinthians 3:7; Job 22:28"> And may the beauty of the Lord our God be upon us; and the work of our hands do thou firmly establish upon us: yea, the work of our hands—firmly establish thou it.</span>
