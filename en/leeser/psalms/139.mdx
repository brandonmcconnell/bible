<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Jeremiah 12:3; Jeremiah 17:9–10"> “To the chief musician, by David, a psalm.” O Lord! thou hast searched me through, and thou knowest &#91;me&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 9:4; 2 Kings 19:27; Psalms 94:11"> Thou indeed knowest my sitting down and my rising up, thou understandest my thinking while yet afar off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:24; Job 31:4; Genesis 28:10–17"> My walking and my lying down hast thou limited, and with all my ways art thou acquainted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 19:14; Hebrews 4:12–13; Psalms 50:19–21"> For, while there is not a word on my tongue, lo, thou, O Lord, knowest it entirely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:27; Psalms 34:7; Revelation 1:17"> Behind and before hast thou hedged me in, and thou placest upon me thy hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Job 42:3; Job 26:14"> Too wonderful is such knowledge for me: it is too exalted, I cannot attain unto it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:23–24; Jonah 1:3; Jonah 1:10"> Whither shall I go from thy spirit? or whither shall I flee away from thy presence?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 9:2–4; Jonah 2:2; Job 34:21–22"> If I should ascend into heaven, thou art there; and if I should make my bed in the nether world, behold, thou art there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 19:6; Psalms 74:16–17; Malachi 4:2"> If I should lift up the wings of the morning-dawn, if I should dwell in the uttermost parts of the sea:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 23:3; Isaiah 41:13; Psalms 73:23"> Even there would thy hand lead me, and thy right hand would seize hold of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 12:22; Jeremiah 23:24; Psalms 94:7"> If I said, Surely darkness shall enshroud me, and into night &#91;be turned&#93; the light about me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 2:22; Job 34:22; Hebrews 4:13"> Yet even darkness can obscure nothing from thee; but the night will shine like the day; both the darkness and the light are alike &#91;to thee&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Psalms 119:73; Isaiah 44:2"> For thou possessest my reins: thou hast covered me in my mother's womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 1:26–27; Psalms 111:2; Psalms 104:24"> I will thank thee therefore, that I am &#91;so&#93; fearfully &#91;and&#93; wonderfully made: wonderful are thy works; and that my soul knoweth right well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:5; Psalms 139:13; Job 10:8–11"> My being was not concealed from thee, when I was made in secret, when I was &#91;so to say&#93; embroidered in the lowest parts of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 56:8; Malachi 3:16; Revelation 20:12"> My undeveloped substance did thy eyes see; and in thy book were all of them written down—the days which have been formed, while yet not one of them was here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 40:5; Jeremiah 29:11; Isaiah 55:8–9"> And how precious are unto me thy thoughts, O God! how mightily great is their sum!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 3:5; Psalms 139:3; Psalms 40:5"> Should I count them, they would be more numerous than the sand: I awake, and I am still with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 5:6; Psalms 119:115; Isaiah 11:4"> If thou wouldst but slay the wicked, O God! and ye men of blood, depart from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jude 1:15; Exodus 20:7; Psalms 74:18"> Who speak of thee for a wicked end, thy enemies, that bear &#91;thy name&#93; for a vain purpose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 119:158; Psalms 31:6; Psalms 26:5"> Behold, those that hate thee I ever hate, O Lord; and for those that rise up against thee do I feel loathing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 101:3–8; Luke 14:26"> With the utmost hatred do I hate them: enemies are they become unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 26:2; Job 31:6; 1 Peter 1:7"> Search me through, O God, and know my heart; probe me, and know my thoughts:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 143:10; Jeremiah 17:9–10; Psalms 25:8–9"> And see if there be a way of perverseness in me, and lead me on the way of eternity.</span>
