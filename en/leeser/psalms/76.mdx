<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 14:18; Isaiah 12:6; Psalms 132:13–14"> (76:3) And in Salem was his tabernacle made, and his dwelling-place in Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 46:9; Ezekiel 39:3–4; Ezekiel 39:9–10"> (76:4) There broke he the shining arrows of the bow, shield, and sword, and battle. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:1–4; Daniel 7:17–28; Ezekiel 19:6"> (76:5) Thou art more brilliant, more excellent than the mountains &#91;full&#93; of prey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nahum 3:18; Psalms 13:3; Isaiah 46:12"> (76:6) Bereft of reason are the stouthearted, they slumber their sleep: and none of the men of might have found &#91;the use of&#93; their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 15:1; Psalms 80:16; Exodus 15:21"> (76:7) From thy rebuke, O God of Jacob, lie in deep sleep both chariot and horse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nahum 1:6; Psalms 2:12; Psalms 89:7"> (76:8) Thou—thou art to be feared: and who may stand in thy sight when once thy anger &#91;is kindled&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:29–30; Habakkuk 2:20; Judges 5:20"> (76:9) From heaven hast thou caused &#91;thy&#93; sentence to be heard: the earth feared, and became still,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 5:5; Zephaniah 2:3; Isaiah 11:4"> (76:10) When God rose to judgment, to save all the lowly of the earth. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 9:17; Matthew 24:22; Daniel 3:19–20"> (76:11) For the fury of man shall praise thee: the remainder of the fury wilt thou gird about thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:4–6; Psalms 50:14; Psalms 68:29"> (76:12) Make vows, and pay &#91;them&#93; unto the Lord your God, all ye that are round about him: let men bring presents unto him that exciteth fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:6; 2 Chronicles 32:21; Psalms 68:12"> (76:13) He will cut down the spirit of the powerful: he is terrible to the kings of the earth.</span>
