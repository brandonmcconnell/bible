<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Proverbs 14:21; 2 Corinthians 9:8–14"> “To the chief musician, a psalm of David.” (41:2) Happy is he that careth for the poor: on the day of evil will the Lord deliver him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 45:4–5; Psalms 27:12; 1 Timothy 4:8"> (41:3) The Lord will preserve him, and keep him alive; he shall be made happy on the earth: and thou wilt not deliver him unto the revengeful desire of his enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 20:5–6; Psalms 73:26; Philippians 2:26–27"> (41:4) The Lord will sustain him upon the bed of painful disease: thou changest all his couch in his sickness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 103:3; James 5:15–16; 2 Chronicles 30:18–20"> (41:5) I said, Lord, be gracious unto me: heal my soul; for I have sinned against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; Psalms 102:8; Psalms 38:12"> (41:6) My enemies speak evil of me, When will he die, and his name perish?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 12:2; Luke 11:53–54; Proverbs 26:24–26"> (41:7) And if he come to see &#91;me&#93;, he speaketh falsely: his heart gathereth wickedness to itself; he goeth abroad &#91;and&#93; speaketh it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 16:28; Psalms 56:5–6; Psalms 31:13"> (41:8) Altogether whisper against me all that hate me: against me do they devise my injury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 71:11; Job 2:7–8; Matthew 27:41–46"> (41:9) “His godless deed &#91;say they&#93; is poured out over him: as he lieth there he will never more rise up again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 13:18; Psalms 55:12–14; Matthew 26:23"> (41:10) Yea, even the man that should have sought my welfare, in whom I trusted, who eateth my bread, hath lifted up his heel against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 21:8–10; Luke 19:27; Psalms 18:37–42"> (41:11) But thou, O Lord, be gracious unto me, and raise me up, that I may requite it unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Colossians 2:15; Psalms 124:6; Psalms 147:11"> (41:12) By this do I know that thou art pleased with me, that my enemy doth not triumph over me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 36:7; Psalms 73:23–24; Psalms 37:17"> (41:13) But as for me, thou upholdest me in my integrity, and placest me before thy presence for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 72:18–19; Ephesians 1:3; Revelation 4:8"> (41:14) Blessed be the Lord the God of Israel from everlasting, and to everlasting. Amen, and Amen. BEGIM</span>
