<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 31:15; Habakkuk 3:1; 1 Peter 4:19"> “A Shiggayon of David, which he sang unto the Lord, concerning the affairs of Cush the Benjamite.” (7:2) O Lord my God, in thee do I put my trust; save me from every one of my persecutors, and deliver me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 50:22; Isaiah 38:13; 2 Timothy 4:17"> (7:3) Lest he tear like a lion my soul, rending it in pieces, with none to deliver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 59:3; 1 Samuel 24:11; 1 Samuel 26:18–19"> (7:4) O Lord my God, if I have done this; if there be injustice in my hands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 55:20; 1 Samuel 22:14; 1 Samuel 24:17–19"> (7:5) If I have recompensed him that was at peace with me with evil; If I have taken aught from my assailants without cause:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 10:6; Zechariah 10:5; Jeremiah 17:13"> (7:6) May the enemy hotly pursue my soul, and overtake it; and tread down upon the earth my life, and cause my honor to be in the dust. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 44:23; Psalms 35:23; Psalms 3:7"> (7:7) Arise, O Lord, in thy anger, lift up thyself because of the rage of my enemies: and awake for me &#91;to&#93; the judgment that thou hast commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 57:15; Revelation 16:5–7; Revelation 18:20"> (7:8) So shall the congregation of nations compass thee about: and for their sakes return thou to the height.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 96:13; Romans 14:10–12; Psalms 43:1"> (7:9) The Lord will judge the people: judge me, O Lord, according to my righteousness, and according to my integrity &#91;grant&#93; me &#91;recompense&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:20; Jeremiah 17:10; Jeremiah 20:12"> (7:10) Oh let the evil of the wicked come to an end; but establish the just, O thou, who triest the hearts and reins, O righteous God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 125:4; Psalms 84:11; Proverbs 28:18"> (7:11) My protection is by God, who saveth the upright in heath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nahum 1:2; Nahum 1:6; Psalms 94:15"> (7:12) God is a righteous judge, and a God who is indignant &#91;with the wicked&#93; every day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 3:19; Matthew 3:10; Deuteronomy 32:41"> (7:13) If he turn not, He will whet his sword, he bendeth his bow, and maketh it ready.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:6; Psalms 45:5; Psalms 64:7"> (7:14) Also for him he prepareth the instruments of death; he fashioneth his arrows against the persecutors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 15:35; James 1:15; Isaiah 33:11"> (7:15) Behold, he travaileth with wrong doing; but he hath conceived mischief, and bringeth forth falsehood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 4:8; Psalms 141:10; Proverbs 26:27"> (7:16) He hath hollowed out a pit, and dug it, and is fallen into the ditch which he hath wrought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esther 9:25; Psalms 37:12–13; 1 Kings 2:32"> (7:17) His mischief will return upon his own head, and upon his own skull will his violence come down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 9:2; Psalms 98:2; Psalms 71:15–16"> (7:18) I will thank the Lord according to his righteousness; and I will sing praises to the name of the Lord the Most High.</span>
