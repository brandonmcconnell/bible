<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 147:1; Psalms 107:1; Ephesians 5:19"> “A psalm or song for the sabbath day.” (92:2) It is a good thing to give thanks unto the Lord, and to sing praises unto thy name, O Most High:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Lamentations 3:22–23; Psalms 89:1–2; John 1:17"> (92:3) To tell in the morning of thy kindness, and of thy faithfulness in the nights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 33:2; 1 Chronicles 13:8; Nehemiah 12:27"> (92:4) Upon a ten-stringed instrument, and upon the psaltery; and with the sweet sound of the harp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14; Psalms 8:6; Luke 1:47"> (92:5) For thou hast caused me to rejoice, O Lord, through thy doing: because of the works of thy hands will I triumph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 11:33–34; Isaiah 55:8–9; Psalms 40:5"> (92:6) How great are thy works, O Lord! exceedingly profound are thy thoughts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 73:22; 1 Corinthians 2:14; Psalms 94:8"> (92:7) A brutish man knoweth it not, and a fool cannot understand this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 73:18–20; Psalms 37:38; Psalms 37:35–36"> (92:8) When the wicked spring up like herbs, and when all the workers of wickedness do flourish; it is that they may be destroyed evermore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 83:18; Acts 12:22–24; Acts 12:1"> (92:9) But thou art exalted to eternity, O Lord!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 37:20; Psalms 89:10; 2 Thessalonians 1:7–9"> (92:10) For, lo, thy enemies, O Lord, for, lo, thy enemies shall perish: all the workers of wickedness shall be scattered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 23:5; Psalms 89:17; Psalms 45:7"> (92:11) But thou exaltest my horn like that of a reem: I am anointed with fresh oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 91:8; Psalms 54:7; Psalms 59:10"> (92:12) And my eye looketh on &#91;the punishment of&#93; those that regard me with envy: of the evildoers that rise up against me my ears shall bear it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:8; Psalms 1:3; Psalms 52:8"> (92:13) The righteous shall spring up like the palm-tree: like a cedar in Lebanon shall he grow high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Peter 3:18; Isaiah 61:3; Ephesians 3:17"> (92:14) Planted in the house of the Lord, in the courts of our God shall they spring up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 46:4; Jeremiah 17:8; Psalms 71:18"> (92:15) They shall still flourish in high old age; they shall be vigorous and covered with foliage;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 18:2; Romans 9:14; Psalms 62:6"> (92:16) To declare that the Lord is upright: he is my rock, and there is no faultiness in him.</span>
