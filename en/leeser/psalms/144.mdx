<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 18:34; 2 Samuel 22:35; Psalms 18:2"> “By David.” Blessed be the Lord my Rock, who exerciseth my hands for the battle, my fingers for the war:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:2–3; Psalms 18:2; Psalms 91:2"> My kindness, and my strong-hold; my high tower, and my deliverer; my shield, and he in whom I trust; who subdueth my people under me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 8:4; Job 7:17; Hebrews 2:6"> Lord, what is man, that thou takest cognizance of him: the son of a mortal, that thou regardest him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 102:11; Psalms 39:5–6; Psalms 109:23"> Man is like the breath: his days are like a passing shadow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 104:32; Psalms 18:9; Exodus 19:18"> O Lord, bend thy heavens, and come down: touch the mountains, that they may smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:23; Psalms 18:13–14; Deuteronomy 32:42"> Cast forth lightning, and scatter them: send out thy arrows, and confound them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 69:1–2; Psalms 18:16; Psalms 54:3"> Stretch out thy hands from above; rid me, and deliver me out of great waters, from the hand of the children of the stranger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 12:2; Psalms 41:6; Psalms 62:4"> Whose mouth speaketh vanity, and whose right hand is the right hand of falsehood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 33:2–3; Psalms 40:3; Psalms 149:1"> O God, a new song will I sing unto thee: upon the ten-stringed psaltery will I sing praises unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 18:50; Psalms 140:7; Psalms 33:16–18"> &#91;Thou art he&#93; that giveth victory unto kings: who riddeth David his servant from the evil-bringing sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 144:7–8; Isaiah 44:20; Psalms 12:2"> Rid me, and deliver me from the hand of the children of the stranger, whose mouth speaketh vanity, and whose right hand is the right hand of falsehood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 128:3; 1 Peter 3:3–6; Isaiah 44:3–5"> So that our sons may be like plants, grown up in their youth: our daughters, like corner-pillars, sculptured in the model of a palace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:13–14; Leviticus 26:10; Malachi 3:10"> May our garners be full, furnishing all manner of store: our sheep bringing forth thousands and ten thousands in our open pastures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Zechariah 8:3–5; Deuteronomy 28:7; Isaiah 24:11"> May our oxen be strong to labor: may there be no breach, nor migration, nor loud complaint in our streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 33:12; Ephesians 1:3; Psalms 146:5"> Happy the people, that fare thus: happy the people, whose God is the Lord.</span>
