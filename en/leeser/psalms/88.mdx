<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 22:2; Luke 18:7"> “A song or psalm of the sons of Korach, to the chief musician upon Machalath-le'annoth, a Maskil of Heman the Ezrachite.” (88:2) O Lord the God of my salvation, by day do I cry, &#91;and&#93; at night I am before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 31:2; Psalms 141:1–2; Lamentations 3:8"> (88:3) Let my prayer come unto thy presence; incline thy ear unto my entreaty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 107:18; Lamentations 3:15–19; Mark 14:33–34"> (88:4) For my soul is sated with troubles; and my life draweth nigh unto the nether world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 28:1; 2 Corinthians 1:9; Psalms 143:7"> (88:5) I am counted with those that descend into the pit; I am become as a man without vigor:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 53:8; Psalms 31:22; Isaiah 14:9–12"> (88:6) Free among the dead, like the slain that dwell in the grave, whom thou rememberest no more; and those that are cut off by thy hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 69:15; Psalms 86:13; Psalms 143:3"> (88:7) Thou hast laid me in the pit of the lower world, in darkness, in the depths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 42:7; Job 6:4; Romans 2:5–9"> (88:8) Upon me lieth heavily thy fury, and with all thy billows hast thou afflicted me. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 31:11; Isaiah 49:7; Jeremiah 32:2"> (88:9) Thou hast removed my acquaintances far from me; thou hast rendered me an abomination unto them: I am imprisoned, and I cannot go forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 11:13; Psalms 38:10; Psalms 86:3"> (88:10) My eye languisheth by reason of affliction: I call on thee, O Lord, every day, I stretch out unto thee my hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 6:5; Isaiah 26:19; Psalms 30:9"> (88:11) Wilt thou display wonders to the dead? shall the departed arise &#91;and&#93; thank thee? Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 26:6; Proverbs 15:11; Psalms 73:18"> (88:12) Shall thy kindness be related in the grave? thy faithfulness in the place of corruption?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:5; Ecclesiastes 2:16; Isaiah 8:22"> (88:13) Shall thy wonders be acknowledged in the darkness? and thy righteousness in the land of forgetfulness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 5:3; Psalms 30:2; Mark 1:35"> (88:14) Yet I cry indeed aloud unto thee, O Lord; and in the morning shall my prayer come before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 13:24; Psalms 13:1; Psalms 44:24"> (88:15) Why, O Lord, wilt thou cast off my soul? &#91;why&#93; wilt thou hide thy face from me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 6:4; Luke 22:44; Job 7:11–16"> (88:16) I am afflicted and perishing from my youth up: I bear thy terrors, I am distracted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 90:11; Isaiah 53:4–6; Romans 8:32"> (88:17) Over me have the fires of thy wrath passed; thy terrors have destroyed me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 22:16; Psalms 116:3; Psalms 69:1–2"> (88:18) They encompass me like water all the time; they have closed in round about me together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 88:8; Psalms 38:11; Job 19:12–15"> (88:19) Thou hast removed far from me lover and friend, my acquaintances &#91;are in&#93; darkness.</span>
