<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 31:1–3; Psalms 25:2–3; 1 Peter 2:6"> In thee, O Lord, do I put my trust; let me never be made ashamed.</span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 18:2; Psalms 91:9; Proverbs 18:10"> Be thou unto me a rocky habitation, whereunto I may continually resort, which thou hast ordained to save me; for my rock and my strong-hold art thou.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 140:1–4; Psalms 59:1–2; 2 Samuel 17:1–2"> O my God, release me out of the hand of the wicked, out of the grasp of the unrighteous and violent man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:7; Psalms 39:7; Psalms 119:81"> For thou art my hope, O Lord Eternal: thou art my trust from my youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 22:9–10; Jeremiah 1:5; Psalms 139:15–16"> By thee have I been supported from my birth: thou art he that took me out of my mother's womb: of thee is my praise continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 8:18; 1 Corinthians 4:9; Acts 4:13"> As a wonderful token have I been unto many; but thou art my strong refuge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 35:28; Psalms 146:2; Psalms 104:1"> My mouth shall be filled with thy praise, and with thy glory all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 46:4; 2 Timothy 1:12; Psalms 71:18"> Cast me not off in the time of old age: when my strength faileth, forsake me not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 83:3; Psalms 56:6; Jeremiah 20:10"> For my enemies speak of me; and they that watch for my soul take counsel together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 7:2; Psalms 3:2; Matthew 27:46"> Saying, “God hath forsaken him: pursue and seize him: for there is none to deliver.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 35:22; Psalms 38:21–22; Psalms 70:5"> O God, be not far from me: O my God, hasten to my help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:11; Psalms 71:24; Psalms 35:26"> Let those be made ashamed, let them perish, that are adversaries to my soul: let those be covered with reproach and dishonor that seek my unhappiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 130:7; 1 John 3:3; Psalms 71:6"> But I will continually hope, and will add yet more to all thy praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 40:5; Psalms 35:28; Psalms 71:8"> My mouth shall relate thy righteousness, all the day thy salvation; for I know not their numbers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Psalms 51:14; Philippians 3:9"> I will come to praise the mighty deeds of the Lord Eternal: I will make mention of thy righteousness, yea, thine only.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 71:5; Psalms 119:9; Psalms 119:102"> O God, thou hast taught me from my youth: and hitherto I ever tell of thy wondrous deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 46:4; Psalms 71:9; Psalms 78:4"> Therefore also even in old age, and when I am grayheaded, O God, forsake me not: until I have told of thy strength unto &#91;this&#93; generation, to every one that may come of thy might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 35:10; Psalms 36:5–6; Psalms 57:10"> And thy righteousness, O God, reacheth even to the height, thou, who hast done great things: O God, who is like thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 138:7; Psalms 60:3; Psalms 86:13"> Thou, who hast shown me great distresses and misfortunes, wilt again revive me; and from the depths of the earth wilt thou bring me up again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 12:1; 2 Samuel 3:1; 2 Corinthians 2:14"> Thou wilt increase my greatness, and wilt turn round and comfort me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 92:1–3; 2 Kings 19:22; Psalms 33:2"> Also I, I will thank thee with the psaltery, &#91;for&#93; thy truth, O my God: I will sing unto thee with the harp, O thou Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 104:33; Psalms 103:4; Luke 1:46–47"> My lips shall shout joyfully when I sing unto thee; and my soul &#91;too&#93;, which thou hast redeemed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 71:13; Psalms 18:37–43; 1 Corinthians 15:25"> Also my tongue shall speak all the day of thy righteousness; for ashamed, for put to the blush are those that seek my unhappiness.</span>
