<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 3:12; Psalms 86:6; Psalms 61:1"> “To the chief musician on Neginoth, a Maskil of David.” (55:2) Give ear, O God, to my prayer, and hide not thyself from my supplication.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 38:14; Psalms 66:19; Psalms 77:3"> (55:3) Listen unto me, and answer me: I mourn in my grief, and moan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:7–8; Psalms 27:12; Psalms 35:11"> (55:4) Because of the voice of the enemy, because of the oppression of the wicked; for they cast wrong upon me, and in wrath they attack me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 116:3; Mark 14:33–34; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> (55:5) My heart is sorely pained within me, and the terrors of death are fallen upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; Job 21:6; 2 Samuel 15:14"> (55:6) Fearfulness and trembling are come upon me, and shuddering hath covered me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 11:1; Revelation 12:14; Psalms 139:9"> (55:7) And I said, Oh that some one would give me wings like a dove! I would fly away and dwell &#91;quietly&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:2; 2 Samuel 17:21–22; Jeremiah 37:12"> (55:8) Lo, I would flee far away, I would spend my night in the wilderness. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 4:6; Matthew 7:25–27; Isaiah 17:12–13"> (55:9) I would prepare hastily a refuge for me from the sweeping wind &#91;and&#93; from storm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:7; Genesis 11:7–9; Jeremiah 23:14"> (55:10) Destroy, O Lord, divide their tongue; for I have seen violence and strife in the city;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 59:14–15; Isaiah 59:6–15; Ezekiel 9:4"> (55:11) Day and night do these encompass it upon her walls: and wrong and trouble are in her midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:26–27; Psalms 10:7; Psalms 5:9"> (55:12) Mischief is in her midst: guile and deceit depart not from her streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; John 13:18; Matthew 26:21–23"> (55:13) For it is not an enemy that reproached me; then I could bear it: not he that hateth me hath magnified himself against me; then I would have hidden myself from him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:12; Micah 7:5; Jeremiah 9:4"> (55:14) But it is thou, a man my equal, my guide, and my acquaintance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 42:4; Isaiah 2:3; Ezekiel 33:31"> (55:15) So that we took sweet secret counsel together, and walked unto the house of God in tumultuous company.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 59:13; Psalms 64:7; Psalms 9:17"> (55:16) Let him dispense death over them; let them go down alive into the nether world; for evil is in their dwelling, in the midst of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Psalms 109:4; Psalms 73:28"> (55:17) I, however, will call on God: and the Lord will save me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; 1 Thessalonians 5:17; Psalms 141:2"> (55:18) At evening and morning and noon will I make my complaint and moan: and he heareth my voice.—</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 57:3; Psalms 3:6–7; 2 Samuel 22:1"> (55:19) He delivereth my soul in peace from the battle against me; for in multitudes are they &#91;contending&#93; with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:27; Revelation 6:10–11; Jeremiah 48:11"> (55:20) God will hear, and humble them—yea, he that sitteth enthroned from the oldest time Selah—those who dread no changes, and fear not God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 12:1; Psalms 89:34; Psalms 7:4"> (55:21) He stretcheth out his hands against those at peace with him: he violateth his covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Psalms 28:3; Proverbs 5:3–4"> (55:22) The creamy words of his mouth are smooth, yet there is war &#91;in&#93; his heart; his words are softer than oil, yet are they drawn swords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 5:7; Psalms 37:5; Philippians 4:6–7"> (55:23) Cast thy burden upon the Lord, and he will sustain thee: he will never suffer the righteous to be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 10:27; Psalms 5:6; Job 15:32"> (55:24) But thou, O God, thou wilt bring them down into the pit of destruction: let not the men of blood and deceit live out half their days; but I will indeed trust in thee.</span>
