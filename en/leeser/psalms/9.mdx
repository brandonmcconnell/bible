<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 86:12; 1 Chronicles 16:24; Psalms 145:1–3"> “To the chief musician upon Muthlabben, a psalm of David.” (9:2) I will thank the Lord with all my heart: I will relate all thy marvelous deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 83:18; Psalms 5:11; Psalms 7:17"> (9:3) I will rejoice and exult in thee: I will sing praise to thy name, O thou Most High:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:9; Revelation 6:12–17; Psalms 80:16"> (9:4) When my enemies are turned backward, &#91;when&#93; they fall and perish at thy presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 140:12; 1 Peter 2:23; Psalms 98:9"> (9:5) For thou hast conducted my dispute and my cause; thou hast sat on the throne as a righteous judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; Deuteronomy 9:14; Proverbs 13:9"> (9:6) Thou hast rebuked nations, thou hast caused the wicked to perish, their name hast thou blotted out for ever and ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 14:22–23; Exodus 14:13; Micah 7:8"> (9:7) O thou enemy, the ruins are passed away for ever, and the cities which thou hast destroyed,—lost is their memorial, yea, theirs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 103:19; Hebrews 13:8; Psalms 102:12"> (9:8) But the Lord will sit enthroned for ever: he hath established for giving judgment his throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 96:13; Psalms 98:9; Revelation 20:12–13"> (9:9) And he will judge the world with righteousness, he shall decide for the people, with equity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 18:10; Psalms 91:1–2; Nahum 1:7"> (9:10) The Lord also will be a strong-hold for the oppressed, a strong-hold in times of distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 91:14; Proverbs 18:10; Isaiah 26:3–4"> (9:11) And they that know thy name will put their trust in thee; for thou hast not forsaken those that seek thee, O Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 107:22; Psalms 96:1–2; Psalms 148:1–5"> (9:12) Sing praises to the Lord, who dwelleth in Zion: announce among the people his deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 34:6; Exodus 3:7; Revelation 16:6"> (9:13) For he, that inquireth after acts of blood, hath remembered them: he hath not forgotten the cry of the afflicted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 86:13; Psalms 30:3; Psalms 107:18"> (9:14) Be gracious unto me, O Lord; have regard to my affliction &#91;coming&#93; from those that hate me, thou who liftest me up from the gates of death:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 13:5; Psalms 51:12; Psalms 106:2"> (9:15) In order that I may relate all thy praises in the gates of the daughters of Zion: I will be glad in thy salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 7:15–16; Psalms 57:6; Psalms 35:8"> (9:16) Sunk are nations in the ditch that they have prepared: in this net which they had laid in secret is their own foot caught.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 7:5; Judges 1:7; Proverbs 12:13"> (9:17) The Lord is made known: he executed justice: through the doing of his own hands is the wicked ensnared. Higgayon, Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 50:22; Job 8:13; Romans 2:8–9"> (9:18) The wicked shall return into hell, all the nations that are forgetful of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 12:5; Proverbs 23:18; Proverbs 24:14"> (9:19) For not everlastingly shall the needy be forgotten: the expectation of the poor shall not perish for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 3:7; Psalms 44:23; Psalms 7:6"> (9:20) Arise, O Lord; let not the mortal boast of his strength; let nations be judged before thy face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 62:9; Psalms 82:6–7; Isaiah 31:3"> (9:21) Place, O Lord, fear over them: let nations know, that they are but mortals. Selah.</span>
