<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 21:23; Psalms 50:2; Psalms 77:20"> “To the chief musician upon Shoshannim; an 'Eduth by Assaph; a psalm.” (80:2) O Shepherd of Israel, give ear, thou that leadest Joseph like a flock; thou that dwellest between the cherubims, shine forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 35:23; Numbers 2:18–24; Psalms 44:23–26"> (80:3) Before Ephraim and Benjamin and Menasseh awaken thy might, and come to our help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 5:21; Numbers 6:25–26; Psalms 85:4"> (80:4) O God, cause us to return, and let thy countenance shine, that we may be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 85:5; Isaiah 58:2–3; Psalms 59:5"> (80:5) O Lord of hosts, how long shall thy anger smoke against the prayer of thy people?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 42:3; Isaiah 30:20; Psalms 102:9"> (80:6) Thou feedest them with the bread of tears, and givest them tears to drink in great measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 79:4; Isaiah 36:8; Isaiah 37:23"> (80:7) Thou renderest us a contest unto our neighbors: and our enemies hold derision among themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 51:10; Isaiah 64:5; Jeremiah 4:14"> (80:8) O God of hosts, cause us to return, and let thy countenance shine, that we may be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:21; Psalms 44:2; Ezekiel 17:6"> (80:9) A vine didst thou remove out of Egypt: thou drovest out nations, and plantedst it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 24:12; Isaiah 27:6; 1 Kings 4:20"> (80:10) Thou didst clear out a place before it, and it struck its root deeply, and it filled the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 104:16"> (80:11) Mountains were covered with its shadow, and with its boughs the cedars of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 72:8; 1 Chronicles 18:3; 1 Kings 4:24"> (80:12) It sent out its tendrils as far as the sea, and unto the river its suckers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 5:5; Psalms 89:40–41; Isaiah 18:5–6"> (80:13) Wherefore hast thou now broken down its fences, so that all who pass by the way pluck the fruit from it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:6; Jeremiah 4:7; Jeremiah 39:1–3"> (80:14) The boar out of the forest doth gnaw at it, and what moveth on the field feedeth on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 63:15; Psalms 90:13; Daniel 9:16–19"> (80:15) O God of hosts, return; I pray thee, look down from heaven, and behold, and think of this vine;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 15:1; Psalms 80:8; Jeremiah 2:21"> (80:16) And of the sprout which thy right hand hath planted, and of the branch that thou hast made strong for thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 39:11; John 15:6; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> (80:17) It is burnt with fire, it is hewn down; because of the rebuke of thy countenance do they perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Daniel 7:13–14; Psalms 89:21; Psalms 110:1"> (80:18) Let thy hand be over the man of thy right hand, over the son of man whom thou hast made strong for thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 119:107; Psalms 119:25; Psalms 119:40"> (80:19) Then will we not swerve from thee: revive us again, and we will call on thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:22–23; Psalms 80:3; Psalms 27:4"> (80:20) O Lord God of hosts, cause us to return: let thy countenance shine, that we may be saved.</span>
