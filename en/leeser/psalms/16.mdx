<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 26:3–4; Psalms 56:1; Psalms 31:23"> “A Michtham of David.” Preserve me, O God; for I have placed my trust in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 73:25; Psalms 31:14; Psalms 89:26"> &#91;O my soul&#93;, thou hast said unto the Lord, Thou art my Lord: my happiness is not without thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 12:26; Hebrews 6:10; Proverbs 13:20"> &#91;But&#93; in the saints who are on the earth, and in the excellent—in them is all my delight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 23:13; Joshua 23:7; Psalms 32:10"> Multiplied shall be the sorrows of those who give presents to another god: I will not pour out their drink-offerings of blood, nor bear their names upon my lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 73:26; Psalms 142:5; Lamentations 3:24"> Thou, O Lord, art the portion of my inheritance, and my cup; thou hast drawn my lot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:19; Ephesians 1:18; Psalms 21:1–3"> My possessions are fallen in agreeable places: yea, my heritage is pleasant to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 73:24; Isaiah 50:4; Psalms 77:6"> I will bless the Lord, who hath given me counsel: also in the night seasons my reins admonish me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 62:6; Acts 2:25–28; Psalms 121:5"> I have always set the Lord before me, that, being at my right hand, I might not be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 2:26; Psalms 30:12; Psalms 57:8"> Therefore is rejoiced my heart, and my spirit is glad: also my flesh shall rest in safety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 2:27–31; Acts 13:35–38; Psalms 49:15"> For thou wilt not abandon my soul to the grave: thou wilt not suffer thy pious &#91;servant&#93; to see corruption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 2:28; Jude 1:24; Psalms 36:7–8"> Thou wilt let me know the path of life: fulness of joy is in thy presence; pleasures are at thy right hand for evermore.</span>
