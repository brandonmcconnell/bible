<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 6:1; Psalms 30:2; Psalms 31:10"> (6:3) Be gracious unto me, O Lord; for I am withering away; heal me, O Lord; for my bones are terrified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 90:13; John 12:27; Psalms 42:11"> (6:4) And my soul is greatly terrified; but thou, O Lord, how long yet—?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 9:18; Psalms 116:4; Ephesians 2:7–8"> (6:5) Return, O Lord, deliver my soul: help me for the sake of thy kindness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 88:10–12; Psalms 115:17; Ecclesiastes 9:10"> (6:6) For in death men do not remember thee: in the nether world, who shall give thee thanks?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 69:3; Psalms 42:3; Psalms 38:9"> (6:7) I am weary with my sighing; I flood every night my bed; With my tears I moisten my couch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 38:10; Job 17:7; Lamentations 5:17"> (6:8) My eye is consumed because of grief; it waxeth old because of all my assailants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 13:27; Psalms 145:18; Psalms 119:115"> (6:9) Depart from me, all ye workers of wickedness; for the Lord hath heard the voice of my weeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 66:19–20; Psalms 120:1; 2 Corinthians 12:8–10"> (6:10) The Lord hath heard my supplication; the Lord will accept my prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Malachi 3:18; Psalms 86:17; Jeremiah 20:11"> (6:11) Ashamed and greatly terrified shall become all my enemies; they will turn round, and be made ashamed in a moment.</span>
