<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 118:10–12; Psalms 57:3; Psalms 92:8"> (56:3) Those that regard me with envy long to swallow &#91;me&#93; up all the time; for many are they that fight against me, O thou Most High.</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 11:54; Matthew 22:15; Isaiah 29:20–21"> (56:6) All the day they wrest my words: against me are all their thoughts for evil.</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 55:23; Psalms 94:20–21; Isaiah 28:15"> (56:8) Because of their wrong-doing let me escape from them: in anger cast down the people, O God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 20:5; Psalms 39:12; Psalms 126:5–6"> (56:9) My wanderings hast thou well numbered: put thou my tears into thy bottle; behold, they are numbered by thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Psalms 46:11; Psalms 118:11–13"> (56:10) Then shall my enemies retire backward on the day when I call &#91;on thee&#93;: this I know—that God is for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; 2 Peter 1:4; Genesis 32:11"> (56:11) In God will I praise the word: in the Lord will I praise the word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 27:1; Isaiah 51:12–13; Isaiah 51:7–8"> (56:12) In God have I put my trust; I will not be afraid: what can man do unto me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 9:1–3; 1 Samuel 1:24–28; Psalms 50:14"> (56:13) Upon me, O God, &#91;rest&#93; thy vows: I will pay thanksgiving offerings unto thee.</span>
