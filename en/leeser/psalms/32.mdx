<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 55:6; Isaiah 43:2; 2 Corinthians 6:2"> For this shall every pious one pray unto thee in a time when thou mayest be found: surely &#91;then&#93; when great waters overflow, they shall never reach unto him.</span>
