<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 113:3; Joshua 22:22; Matthew 25:32"> “A psalm of Assaph.” The God of gods, the Lord, speaketh, and calleth the earth, from the rising of the sun unto his setting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 48:2; Psalms 80:1; Revelation 21:23"> Out of Zion, the perfection of beauty, God shineth forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 7:10; Numbers 16:35; Leviticus 10:2"> Our God is coming, and will not keep silence: a fire devoureth before him, and round him there rageth a mighty storm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Isaiah 1:2; Deuteronomy 31:28"> He will call to the heavens above, and to the earth, to judge his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 26:28; Exodus 24:3–8; Hebrews 13:20"> “Gather together unto me my pious servants, who make a covenant with me by sacrifice.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 97:6; Psalms 75:7; Psalms 89:5"> And the heavens tell of his righteousness; for God is judge himself. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 20:2; Psalms 81:8; Jeremiah 2:9"> “Hear, O my people, and I will speak; O Israel, and I will testify against thee: God, thy God, am I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 1:11–31; Hebrews 10:4–10; Hosea 6:6"> Not because of thy sacrifices will I reprove thee; and thy burnt-offerings are continually before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Micah 6:6–8; Hebrews 10:4–6; Psalms 69:31"> I will not take a bullock out of thy house, nor he-goats out of thy folds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:5–6; Psalms 8:6–8; Psalms 104:14"> For mine are all the beasts of the forest, the cattle upon a thousand mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 1:20–22; Matthew 6:26; Luke 12:24"> I know all the fowls of the mountains: whatever moveth on the fields is with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 19:5; Deuteronomy 10:14; Psalms 24:1–2"> If I were hungry, I would not say it to thee; for mine is the world, and what filleth it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Do I eat the flesh of fatted bulls, or drink the blood of he-goats?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15; Psalms 56:12; Deuteronomy 23:21"> Offer unto God thanksgiving; and pay unto the Most High thy vows;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 107:28; Psalms 91:15; Psalms 34:3–4"> And call on me on the day of distress: I will deliver thee,— and so wilt thou glorify me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Psalms 78:36–38; Romans 2:17–24"> But unto the wicked God saith, “What hast thou to do to relate my statutes, and why bearest thou my covenant upon thy mouth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:26; Proverbs 12:1; 2 Timothy 4:3–4"> And yet thou hatest instruction, and castest my words behind thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 1:32; 1 Timothy 5:22; Hebrews 13:4"> When thou seest a thief, then art thou pleased with him, and with adulterers hast thou thy portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Psalms 52:2–4; Psalms 55:21"> Thou lettest loose thy mouth with evil, and thy tongue frameth deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 10:21; Proverbs 10:18; Leviticus 19:16"> Thou sittest and speakest against thy brother; against thy own mother's son thou utterest slander.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 2:4–5; Proverbs 29:1; Isaiah 57:11"> These things hast thou done, and I kept silence: thou didst ween that I am like thyself; &#91;but&#93; I will reprove thee, and set it in order before thy eyes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 8:13; Psalms 9:17; Hosea 4:6"> Do but reflect on this, ye that forget God, lest I tear &#91;you&#93; in pieces, with none to deliver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 91:16; James 3:13; Psalms 24:4–5"> Whoso offereth thanksgiving glorifieth me: and to him that ordereth his course aright, will I show the salvation of God.</span>
