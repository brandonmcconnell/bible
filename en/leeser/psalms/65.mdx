<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 62:1; Psalms 76:11; Psalms 116:17–18"> “To the chief musician, a psalm &#91;and&#93; song of David.” (65:2) For thee praise is waiting, O God, in Zion: and unto thee shall vows be paid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 65:24; Jeremiah 29:12–13; Psalms 86:9"> (65:3) O thou that hearest prayer, unto thee all flesh shall come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 38:4; Psalms 79:9; Hebrews 9:14"> (65:4) The iniquitous things have become too mighty for me: our transgressions—these wilt thou wipe away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 1:4; Psalms 84:4; Psalms 23:6"> (65:5) Happy is he whom thou choosest, and causest to approach, that he may dwell in thy courts: let us be satisfied with the happiness of thy house, the holiness of thy temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:21; Psalms 66:3; Revelation 15:3–4"> (65:6) With terrific deeds in righteousness wilt thou answer us, O God of our salvation, who art the confidence of all the ends of the earth, and of the sea, that are far away;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 93:1; Micah 6:2; Habakkuk 3:6"> (65:7) Who setteth firmly the mountains by his power, who is girded with might;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 89:9; Isaiah 17:12–13; Psalms 93:3–4"> (65:8) Who assuageth the roaring of the seas, the roaring of their waves, and the tumult of nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 148:3; Psalms 136:8; Psalms 104:20–23"> (65:9) And they that dwell in the uttermost parts are afraid of thy wondrous signs: the outgoings of the morning and evening thou causest to rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 46:4; Psalms 104:13–15; Psalms 68:9–10"> (65:10) Thou hast thought of the earth, and waterest her abundantly; thou greatly enrichest her; the brook of God is full of water: thou preparest their corn, when thou hast thus prepared her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 147:8; 1 Corinthians 3:6–7; Deuteronomy 32:2"> (65:11) Watering her furrows abundantly, smoothing down her ridges, thou softenest her with showers; thou blessest her growth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joel 2:21–26; Psalms 103:4; Psalms 5:12"> (65:12) Thou hast crowned the year of thy goodness; and thy tracks drop fatness:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 38:26–27; Psalms 104:10–13; Joel 2:22"> (65:13) The pastures of the wilderness are dropping &#91;with plenty&#93;: and the hills are girt with gladness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 55:12; Acts 14:17; Isaiah 35:1–2"> (65:14) The meadows are clothed with flocks, and the valleys are enveloped with corn: men shout for joy, &#91;yea,&#93; they also sing.</span>
