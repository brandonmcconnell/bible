<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:7; Psalms 66:1; Psalms 100:1–2"> “To the chief musician upon Gittith; by Assaph.” (81:2) Sing aloud unto God our strength: shout joyfully unto the God of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 149:1–3; Psalms 92:3; Colossians 3:16"> (81:3) Lift up psalm, and bring hither the timbrel, the pleasant harp with the psaltery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 10:1–10; Numbers 15:3; 2 Chronicles 2:4"> (81:4) Blow on the new moon the cornet, at the time appointed, on the day of our feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> (81:5) For this is a statute for Israel, an ordinance by the God of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 114:1; Deuteronomy 28:49; 1 Corinthians 14:21–22"> (81:6) As a testimony in Joseph did he ordain it, when he went out over the land of Egypt. The language of one I had not known did I hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 9:4; Isaiah 10:27; Exodus 6:6"> (81:7) I removed from the burden his shoulder: his hands left behind the burden-basket.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 19:19; Numbers 20:13; Exodus 2:23"> (81:8) In distress thou didst call, and I delivered thee; I answered thee in the secret of the thunder: I proved thee at the waters of Meribah. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 50:7; John 3:11; Isaiah 1:19"> (81:9) Hear, O my people, and I will give warning unto thee; O Israel, if thou wouldst but hearken unto me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 43:12; Deuteronomy 32:12; Deuteronomy 6:14"> (81:10) There shall not be among thee a foreign God; nor shalt thou bow thyself down to any strange God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 15:7; Psalms 107:9; Exodus 20:2"> (81:11) I am the Lord thy God, who have brought thee up out of the land of Egypt: open wide thy mouth, and I will fill it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:18; Jeremiah 7:23–24; Jeremiah 2:11–13"> (81:12) But my people did not hearken to my voice; and Israel was not willing &#91;to follow&#93; me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 1:24; Jeremiah 7:24; Isaiah 30:1"> (81:13) So I let them go in the stubbornness of their own hearts: and they walked in their own counsels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 48:18; Deuteronomy 5:29; Deuteronomy 10:12–13"> (81:14) Oh that my people would hearken unto me, that Israel would walk in my ways!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 1:8; Judges 2:20–23; Numbers 14:9"> (81:15) In a little while would I subdue their enemies, and against their adversaries would I turn my hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:10; Romans 1:30; Exodus 20:5"> (81:16) The haters of the Lord should offer flattery unto him; but their time should endure for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13–14; Psalms 147:14; 1 Samuel 14:25–26"> (81:17) And he would feed him with the best of wheat: and out of the rock would I satisfy thee with honey.</span>
