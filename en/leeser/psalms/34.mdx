<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:18; Colossians 3:17; Psalms 145:1–2"> “By David, when he disguised his reason before Abimelech, who drove him away, and he departed.” (34:2) I will bless the Lord at all times: continually shall his praise be in my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:24; 1 Corinthians 1:31; Psalms 119:74"> (34:3) My soul shall make her boast in the Lord: the humble shall hear it, and be rejoiced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 69:30; Luke 1:46; Psalms 148:1–14"> (34:4) Oh magnify the Lord with me, and let us exalt his name together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 18:6; Matthew 7:7; Psalms 116:1–6"> (34:5) I sought the Lord, and he answered me, and from all that I dreaded did he deliver me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Psalms 123:1–2; Psalms 18:28"> (34:6) They &#91;who&#93; looked unto him, were indeed enlightened, and their faces were not put to the blush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 34:17–19; Psalms 3:4; Psalms 10:17"> (34:7) This poor man cried, and the Lord heard him, and out of all his distresses did he save him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 91:11; Daniel 6:22; Hebrews 1:14"> (34:8) The angel of the Lord encampeth round about those who fear him, and delivereth them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 6:4–5; Psalms 119:103; 1 Peter 2:2–3"> (34:9) Experience and see that the Lord is good: happy is the man that trusteth in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 8:32; Philippians 4:19; Psalms 23:1"> (34:10) Oh fear the Lord, ye his saints: for there is no want to those who fear him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 84:11; Matthew 6:32; Psalms 104:21"> (34:11) The young lions do lack, and suffer hunger; but those who seek the Lord shall not want any good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 1:7; Proverbs 2:1–9; Psalms 111:10"> (34:12) Come, ye children, hearken unto me: the fear of the Lord will I teach you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:10–12; Deuteronomy 30:20; Deuteronomy 6:2"> (34:13) Who is the man that desireth life, loveth &#91;many&#93; days, that he may see happiness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 3:5–10; Psalms 141:3; Proverbs 21:23"> (34:14) Guard thy tongue from evil, and thy lips from speaking deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 37:27; 1 Peter 3:11; Hebrews 12:14"> (34:15) Depart from evil, and do good; seek peace, and pursue it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:12; Psalms 34:17; Psalms 33:18"> (34:16) The eyes of the Lord are directed unto the righteous, and his ears unto their cry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; Jeremiah 17:13; Jeremiah 44:11"> (34:17) &#91;But&#93; the anger of the Lord is against those that do evil, to cut off from the earth their remembrance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 145:18–20; Isaiah 65:24; Psalms 34:19"> (34:18) Those cry, and the Lord heareth, and from all their distresses he delivereth them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 147:3; Psalms 145:18; Isaiah 57:15"> (34:19) The Lord is nigh unto those that are broken-hearted: and he saveth those that are of a contrite spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 34:17; 1 Peter 4:12–13; Psalms 34:6"> (34:20) Many are the afflictions of the righteous; but out of them all the Lord ever delivereth him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 19:36; Psalms 35:10; Psalms 91:12"> (34:21) He watcheth all his bones: not one of them is broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 94:23; Proverbs 24:16; Isaiah 3:11"> (34:22) The evil will slay the wicked: and they who hate the righteous shall incur guiltiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 103:4; 1 Peter 1:18–19; Psalms 31:5"> (34:23) The Lord redeemeth the soul of his servants: and all that trust in him shall not incur guiltiness.</span>
