<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 140:3; James 3:8; Ecclesiastes 10:11"> (58:5) They have poison like the poison of a serpent: &#91;they are&#93; like the deaf adder that stoppeth her ear;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:17; Isaiah 19:3; Deuteronomy 18:11"> (58:6) Which will not hearken to the voice of conjurers, yea, that of the wisest of all charmers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 3:7; Job 4:10–11; Hosea 5:14"> (58:7) O God, break out their teeth in their mouth: the jaw-teeth of the young lions tear thou out, O Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 112:10; Joshua 7:5; Psalms 64:3"> (58:8) Let them melt away as water &#91;which&#93; runneth off: when each one bendeth &#91;his bow to shoot&#93; his arrows, let them be as if cut in pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 3:16; Ecclesiastes 6:3; Psalms 37:35–36"> (58:9) As a snail which melteth, let him pass away; like the untimely birth of a woman which hath not seen the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 118:12; Proverbs 10:25; Ecclesiastes 7:6"> (58:10) Before your pots can feel the thorns, will he take them away with a whirlwind, both the green and the burning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 68:23; Psalms 91:8; Psalms 64:10"> (58:11) The righteous will rejoice when he seeth the vengeance: he will bathe his steps in the blood of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 67:4; Isaiah 3:10; Psalms 64:9"> (58:12) So that a man shall say, Verily fruit will come for the righteous: verily there is a God that judgeth on the earth.</span>
