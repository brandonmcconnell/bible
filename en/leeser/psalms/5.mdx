<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 65:2; Psalms 3:4; Isaiah 33:22"> (5:3) Listen unto the voice of my loud cry, my King, and my God, when unto thee I pray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 119:147; Psalms 88:13; Psalms 130:6"> (5:4) O Lord! in the morning do thou hear my voice: in the morning will I set in order &#91;my prayer&#93; before thee, and 1ook up with hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 11:5; Hebrews 12:14; Malachi 2:17"> (5:5) For thou art not a God that hath pleasure in wickedness: evil cannot abide with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 11:5; Psalms 1:5; Hosea 9:15"> (5:6) The arrogant cannot stand up before thy eyes: thou hatest all workers of wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 55:23; Revelation 21:8; Revelation 22:15"> (5:7) Thou wilt destroy those that speak lies: the man of blood and deceit the Lord abhorreth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 132:7; Psalms 138:2; Hebrews 4:16"> (5:8) But as for me—in the abundance of thy kindness will I enter thy house: I will bow myself down before thy holy temple in the fear of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 27:11; Psalms 25:4–5; Proverbs 3:5–6"> (5:9) O Lord, lead me in thy righteousness because of those that regard me enviously; make straight before me thy way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 3:13; Jeremiah 9:3–6; Psalms 62:4"> (5:10) For there is not in their mouth any sincerity; their inward part is full of deception; an open sepulchre is their throat: they flatter with their tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 63:10; Psalms 140:9–10; 2 Samuel 17:14"> (5:11) Condemn them, O God: let them fall through their own counsels; for the multitude of their transgressions cast them down; for they have rebelled against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 8:28; Revelation 19:1–7; Psalms 68:3"> (5:12) Then will rejoice all those that put their trust in thee; for ever will they shout for joy, when thou protectest them: and then will exult in thee those that love thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 84:11; Psalms 32:10; Psalms 115:13"> (5:13) For thou wilt bless the righteous, O Lord; as with a shield wilt thou encompass him with favor.</span>
