<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 22:1; Psalms 27:9; Psalms 13:1–3"> Wherefore, O Lord, standest thou afar off? hidest thyself in times of distress?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 59:12; Psalms 36:11; Psalms 7:16"> In the pride of the wicked doth he hotly pursue the poor: they are seized through the plans that those have devised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 94:4; Luke 12:19; Zechariah 11:5–8"> For the wicked boasteth of the longing of his soul, and the robber blesseth himself when he hath despised the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 14:1–2; Acts 8:22; Romans 1:28"> The wicked, according to the pride of his wrathfulness, &#91;saith,&#93; He will not require, There is no God &#91;in&#93; all his plans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 92:5–6; Isaiah 26:11; Psalms 12:5"> Prosperous are his ways at all times; far in the height &#91;remain&#93; thy punishments away from him: all his assailants—he puffeth at them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:11; Matthew 24:48; Isaiah 56:12"> He saith in his heart, I shall not be moved; I shall be for many generations, and without adversity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 7:14; Psalms 140:9; Psalms 140:3"> Of the false oaths is his mouth full, and deceit and fraud: under his tongue is mischief and wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:14; 1 Samuel 22:18; Jeremiah 22:17"> He sitteth in the lurking-places of the villages; in the secret places doth he murder the innocent: his eyes search for the unfortunate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 17:12; Psalms 59:3; Micah 7:2"> He lieth in wait in a secret place like a lion in his den; he lieth in wait to snatch up the poor: he snatcheth up the poor, as they draweth him into his net.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:21–22; 1 Samuel 18:21–26; 2 Samuel 15:5"> He croucheth, he bendeth himself, and the unfortunate fall through his might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:12; Psalms 94:7; Psalms 73:11"> He saith in his heart, God hath forgotten; he hath hidden his face; he will never see it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 5:9; Psalms 9:12; Psalms 94:2"> Arise, O Lord; O God, lift up thy hand: forget not the afflicted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:9–10; Psalms 74:10; Genesis 9:5"> For what doth the wicked despise God? &#91;why&#93; doth he say in his heart, Thou wilt not require it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 68:5; Psalms 146:9; Psalms 55:22"> Thou hast seen it: for thou beholdest mischief and despiteful acts, to requite it with thy hand: unto thee the unfortunate committeth himself; unto the fatherless hast thou &#91;ever&#93; been a helper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:12; Psalms 37:17; Job 38:15"> Break thou the arm of the wicked; and of the bad man—thou wilt inquire for his wickedness &#91;till&#93; thou find none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 29:10; Jeremiah 10:10; 1 Timothy 1:17"> The Lord is King for ever and ever: nations are perished out of his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 145:19; 2 Chronicles 34:27; Psalms 34:15"> The longing of the afflicted dost thou hear, O Lord! thou wilt strengthen their heart, thou wilt cause thy ear to listen:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Psalms 82:3; Isaiah 11:4"> To judge the fatherless and the oppressed, that not farther more shall be arrogant the mortal from the earth.</span>
