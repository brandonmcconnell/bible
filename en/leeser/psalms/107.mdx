<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 106:1; 1 Chronicles 16:34; 2 Chronicles 7:3"> BOOK FIFTH: Oh give thanks unto the Lord; for he is good; for unto eternity endureth his kindness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 1:18–19; Luke 1:74; Galatians 3:13"> Thus let the Lord's redeemed say, even those whom he hath redeemed from the hand of the adversary;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 106:47; Isaiah 43:5–6; Revelation 5:9"> And whom he hath gathered out of the &#91;various&#93; lands, from the east, and from the west, from the north, and from the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:10; Ezekiel 34:12; Ezekiel 34:6"> They wandered about in the wilderness, in the desert path; they could not find an inhabited city:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 8:2–3; 1 Samuel 30:11–12; Lamentations 2:19"> Hungry and thirsty, their soul within them fainted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Psalms 91:15; Jeremiah 29:12–14"> Then they cried unto the Lord when they were in distress, &#91;and&#93; out of their afflictions he delivered them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:9; Psalms 77:20; Hebrews 11:9–10"> And he led them forth on the right way, that they might go to an inhabited city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 107:31; Psalms 147:1; Daniel 4:2–3"> They &#91;therefore&#93; shall give thanks unto the Lord for his kindness, and &#91;proclaim&#93; his wonders to the children of men!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 5:6; Jeremiah 31:25; Psalms 34:10"> For he satisfied the longing soul, and the hungry soul he filled with good.—</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 1:79; Micah 7:8; Romans 6:20–21"> Such as sit in darkness and in the shadow of death, bound in misery and &#91;fetters of&#93; iron;—</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 106:43; Psalms 78:40; Romans 1:28"> Because they have rebelled against the words of God, and have contemned the counsel of the Most High;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 22:11; Exodus 5:18–19; Exodus 2:23"> And he humbled with trouble their heart; they stumbled, and there was none to help;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 107:28; Psalms 107:6; Judges 4:3"> But when they cry unto the Lord when they are in distress, he saveth them out of their afflictions;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 42:16; Ephesians 5:8; Psalms 116:16"> He bringeth them out of darkness and the shadow of death, and teareth their bands asunder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 107:21; Psalms 107:31; Psalms 107:8"> They &#91;therefore&#93; shall give thanks unto the Lord for his kindness, and &#91;proclaim&#93; his wonders to the children of men!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 45:1–2; Judges 16:3; Micah 2:13"> For he hath broken the doors of copper, and the bolts of iron hath he hewn asunder.—</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Lamentations 3:39; Proverbs 1:22; Psalms 38:1–8"> Fools, because of their transgression, and because of their iniquities, are afflicted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 9:13; Psalms 88:3; Job 33:19–22"> All manner of food their soul abhorreth; and they draw near unto the gates of death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 78:34–35; Psalms 34:4–6; Psalms 107:13"> But when they cry unto the Lord when they are in distress, he saveth them out of their afflictions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 8:8; Psalms 103:3–4; Psalms 147:15"> He sendeth his word and healeth them, and delivereth them from their graves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 107:8; Psalms 107:15; 2 Chronicles 32:25"> They &#91;therefore&#93; shall give thanks unto the Lord for his kindness, and &#91;proclaim&#93; his wonders to the children of men!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 50:14; Psalms 118:17; Psalms 9:11"> They shall also sacrifice the sacrifices of thanksgiving, and relate his deeds with joyful song.—</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 18:17; Psalms 48:7; Ezekiel 27:26"> They who go down to the sea in ships, who do business on great waters;—</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 104:24–27; Psalms 95:5; Job 38:8–11"> These have seen the works of the Lord, and his wonders on the deep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jonah 1:4; Matthew 8:24; Psalms 148:8"> For he spoke, and he raised the stormy wind, which lifteth up its waves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 22:14; Psalms 119:28; Isaiah 13:7"> They would mount up to heaven, they would go down to the depths: their soul was melted because of their danger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 12:25; Isaiah 29:9; Isaiah 19:3"> They would reel to and fro, and stagger like a drunken man, and all their wisdom was exhausted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 8:25; Psalms 107:13; Psalms 107:19"> And they cried unto the Lord when they were in distress, and he brought them out of their afflictions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 8:26; Psalms 65:7; Luke 8:23–25"> He calmed the storm into a whisper, and stilled were the waves of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 6:21"> And they were rejoiced because they were silent: and then he guided them unto their desired haven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 107:8; Psalms 103:2; Psalms 107:21"> They &#91;therefore&#93; shall give thanks unto the Lord for his kindness, and &#91;proclaim&#93; his wonders to the children of men!</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 22:25; Psalms 22:22; Psalms 99:5"> And they must exalt him in the congregation of the people, and in the assembly of the elders must they praise him.—</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 50:2; Isaiah 42:15; Amos 4:7–8"> He changeth rivers into a wilderness, and water-springs into parched ground;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 14:3; Genesis 13:10; Ezekiel 47:11"> A fruitful land into a salty waste, for the wickedness of those that dwell therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 114:8; Isaiah 35:6–7; Isaiah 41:17–19"> He changeth the wilderness into a pool of water, and desert land into water-springs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 107:7; Luke 1:53; Acts 17:26"> And there he causeth to dwell the hungry, that they may found an inhabited city;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:7; 2 Corinthians 9:10; Jeremiah 29:5"> And they sow fields, and plant vineyards, that they may yield the fruits of the &#91;annual&#93; product.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 1:7; Genesis 12:2; Genesis 17:20"> He also blesseth them, and they multiply greatly, and he suffereth not their cattle to diminish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 2:23–24; 2 Kings 10:32; 2 Chronicles 15:5–6"> They were also diminished and bowed low through oppression, misfortune, and sorrow:</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Job 12:24; Job 12:21; Daniel 4:33"> He &#91;then&#93; poureth contempt upon princes, and causeth them to wander in a pathless wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:8; Job 21:11; Esther 8:15–17"> And he exalteth the needy from misery, and maketh &#91;his&#93; families like flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 63:11; Job 22:19; Romans 3:19"> The righteous shall see it, and rejoice; but all wickedness shall stop her mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Hosea 14:9; Jeremiah 9:12; Psalms 64:9"> Whoever is wise, let him observe these things, and let &#91;all&#93; understand the kindness of the Lord.</span>
