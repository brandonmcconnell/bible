<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 3:18; Proverbs 16:6; Psalms 143:12"> “To the chief musician, by the servant of the Lord, by David.” (36:2) Saith vice itself to the wicked—so I feel it within my heart— that he should have no dread of God before his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:19; Psalms 10:3; Psalms 49:18"> (36:3) For he flattereth himself in his own eyes &#91;too much&#93; to find out his iniquity to hate it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 55:21; Psalms 10:7; Jeremiah 4:22"> (36:4) The words of his mouth are wickedness and deceit: he hath left off to be wise, to do good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 2:1; Proverbs 4:16; Romans 12:9"> (36:5) He deviseth wickedness upon his couch; he placeth himself on a way that is not good; evil he despiseth not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 100:5; Psalms 108:4; Psalms 103:11"> (36:6) O Lord, into the heavens reacheth thy kindness, thy faithfulness even into the skies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 77:19; Romans 11:33; Psalms 71:19"> (36:7) Thy righteousness is like the mountains of God; thy acts of justice like the great deep: man and beast dost thou ever help, O Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 86:5; Psalms 91:4; Ruth 2:12"> (36:8) How precious is thy kindness, O God! And the children of men that seek shelter under the shadow of thy wings,—</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 16:11; Psalms 65:4; Isaiah 58:11"> (36:9) These will be abundantly satisfied with the fatness of thy house; and of the stream of thy delights wilt thou give them to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 8:12; John 4:14; 1 John 1:7"> (36:10) For with thee is the source of life: in thy light shall we see light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:7; Hebrews 8:11; Psalms 97:10–11"> (36:11) Draw down continuously thy kindness unto those that acknowledge thee; and thy righteousness to the upright in heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 16:8; Psalms 17:8–14; Psalms 119:51"> (36:12) Let not come against me the foot of pride, and let not the hand of the wicked chase me off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 1:5; Psalms 58:10–11; Psalms 55:23"> (36:13) There are fallen the workers of wickedness: they are thrust down, and shall not be able to rise.</span>
