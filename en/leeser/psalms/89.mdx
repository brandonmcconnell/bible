<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 101:1; Psalms 89:8; Psalms 119:90"> “A Maskil of Ethan the Ezrachite.” (89:2) The kindnesses of the Lord will I for ever sing: from generation to generation will I make known thy faithfulness with my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 36:5; Nehemiah 9:31; Psalms 89:5"> (89:3) For I have said, To eternity will kindness be built up: the heavens—yea, in these wilt thou establish thy faithfulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 132:11; 2 Samuel 7:10–16; Psalms 89:34–35"> (89:4) “I have made a covenant with my elect, I have sworn unto David my servant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:12–16; Luke 1:32–33; Psalms 132:12"> (89:5) Unto eternity will I establish thy seed, and I will build up thy throne, from generation to generation.” Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 97:6; Psalms 19:1; Isaiah 44:23"> (89:6) And the heavens praise thy wonder, O Lord: also thy faithfulness in the assembly of holy ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 86:8; Psalms 71:19; Psalms 113:5"> (89:7) For who in the sky can be compared unto the Lord? who can be likened unto the Lord among the sons of the mighty?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 47:2; Psalms 76:7–11; Hebrews 12:28–29"> (89:8) God is greatly terrific in the secret council of the holy ones and fear-inspiring over all that are about him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 71:19; 1 Samuel 2:2; Jeremiah 32:17"> (89:9) O Lord God of hosts, who is powerful, like thee, Eternal! and thy faithfulness is round about thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 65:7; Psalms 107:25–29; Mark 4:41"> (89:10) Thou rulest over the pride of the sea: when its waves are lifted up, thou assuagest them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 87:4; Psalms 68:30; Psalms 144:6"> (89:11) Thou didst crush Rahab as one that is slain: with thy strong arm didst thou scatter thy enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:11; Genesis 1:1; Psalms 115:16"> (89:12) Thine are the heavens, also thine is the earth: as for the world and what filleth it, thou hast founded them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 12:1; Job 26:7; Joshua 19:22"> (89:13) The north and the south—these hast thou created: Tabor and Chermon shall rejoice in thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 4:34–35; Psalms 89:10; Psalms 62:11"> (89:14) Thine is the powerful arm, with might: strong is thy hand, and exalted is thy right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; Proverbs 16:12; Psalms 45:6–7"> (89:15) Righteousness and justice are the prop of thy throne: kindness and truth precede thy presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 44:3; Job 29:3; Psalms 4:6"> (89:16) Happy is the people that know the cornet's sound: O Lord, in the light of thy countenance will they ever walk firmly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Philippians 4:4; Philippians 3:9; Luke 1:47"> (89:17) In thy name will they be glad all the day, and in thy righteousness will they be exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 75:10; Psalms 92:10; Psalms 148:14"> (89:18) For thou art the glory of their strength; and through thy favor will our horn be exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 47:9; Psalms 71:22; Isaiah 33:22"> (89:19) For of the Lord is our shield; and of the Holy One of Israel is our king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:18; 1 Kings 11:34; Psalms 89:3"> (89:20) Then spokest thou in a vision to thy pious &#91;servant&#93;, and saidst, “I have bestowed help to one that is mighty; I have exalted a youth out of the people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:12–13; Isaiah 61:1–3; 1 Samuel 16:1"> (89:21) I have found David my servant; with my holy oil have I anointed him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 42:1; Psalms 18:32–39; Psalms 89:13"> (89:22) With whom my hand shall be firmly established; also my arm shall strengthen him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:9; 2 Samuel 7:10; Matthew 4:1–10"> (89:23) The enemy shall not exact from him like a lender: and the son of injustice shall not afflict him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:9; 2 Samuel 3:1; Psalms 18:40"> (89:24) And I will beat down before his face his assailants, and those that hate him will I plague.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 89:16–17; 2 Corinthians 1:20; Psalms 91:14"> (89:25) But my faithfulness and my kindness shall be with him: and through my name shall his horn be exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 80:11; Psalms 2:8; Psalms 72:8–11"> (89:26) And I will place on the sea his hand, and on the rivers his right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:14; 2 Samuel 22:47; Psalms 95:1"> (89:27) He will call unto me, Thou art my father, my God, and the rock of my salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 1:15; Colossians 1:18; Psalms 2:7"> (89:28) Also I will appoint my first-born, the highest among the kings of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 55:3; Jeremiah 33:20–21; Acts 13:32–34"> (89:29) For evermore will I keep for him my kindness, and my covenant shall stand faithfully with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 89:4; Deuteronomy 11:21; Psalms 89:36"> (89:30) And I appoint for ever his seed, and his throne as the days of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:14; 1 Chronicles 28:9; Psalms 119:53"> (89:31) If his children forsake my law, and walk not in my ordinances;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 55:20"> (89:32) If they profane my statutes, and keep not my commandments:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:14; Hebrews 12:6–11; Proverbs 3:11–12"> (89:33) Then will I visit with the rod their transgressions, and with plagues their iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 54:8–10; 2 Samuel 7:15; Lamentations 3:31–32"> (89:34) Nevertheless my kindness will I not make utterly void from him, and I will not act falsely against my faithfulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Matthew 24:35; James 1:17"> (89:35) I will not profane my covenant, and what is gone out of my lips will I not alter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 132:11; Hebrews 6:13; Titus 1:2"> (89:36) One thing have I sworn by my holiness, that I will not lie unto David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 72:5; Psalms 89:29; Isaiah 59:21"> (89:37) His seed shall endure for ever, and his throne shall be like the sun before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:35–36; Genesis 1:14–18; Psalms 104:19"> (89:38) Like the moon shall it be firmly established for ever, and as this faithful witness in the sky.” Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:9; Deuteronomy 32:19; Psalms 106:40"> (89:39) And yet thou hast east off and despised, thou hast become wroth with thy anointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Lamentations 5:16; Psalms 74:7; Psalms 89:34–36"> (89:40) Thou hast made void the covenant of thy servant: thou hast profaned, down to the ground, his crown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Psalms 80:12; Lamentations 2:2; Lamentations 2:5"> (89:41) Thou hast broken down all his fences: thou hast brought his strong-holds to terror.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Psalms 79:4; Lamentations 5:1; Ezekiel 5:14–15"> (89:42) All that pass by the way plunder him: he is become a reproach to his neighbors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 80:6; Revelation 11:10; Deuteronomy 28:43"> (89:43) Thou hast raised up the right hand of his assailants: thou hast caused all his enemies to rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Joshua 7:8–12; Numbers 14:42; Psalms 44:10"> (89:44) Thou hast also turned the edge of his sword, and hast not let him stand erect in the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Lamentations 4:1–2; 2 Thessalonians 2:3–10; Psalms 89:39"> (89:45) Thou hast made his brilliancy cease; and his throne hast thou thrown down to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Psalms 109:29; Psalms 44:15; Micah 7:10"> (89:46) Thou hast shortened the days of his youth: thou hast enshrouded him with shame. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 79:5; Psalms 78:63; Psalms 88:14"> (89:47) How long, Lord, wilt thou hide thyself, continually? how long shall thy fury burn like fire?</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Job 14:1; Job 10:9; Job 7:7"> (89:48) Remember &#91;what&#93; I am, what my duration is &#91;here&#93;, for what nothingness thou hast created all sons of men!</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 49:15; Hebrews 11:5; Ecclesiastes 9:5"> (89:49) What man is there that can live, and shall not see death? that can deliver his soul from the power of the nether word? Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:15; Hebrews 7:21; Isaiah 55:3"> (89:50) Where are thy former kindnesses, O Lord, which thou hast sworn unto David by thy truth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Psalms 74:22; Psalms 74:18; Psalms 69:9"> (89:51) Remember, Lord, the disgrace of thy servants; that I bear in my bosom the &#91;burden&#93; of all the many nations;</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="1 Peter 4:14–16; Psalms 74:10; 2 Samuel 16:7–8"> (89:52) That thy enemies have defied, O Lord; that they have defied the footsteps of thy anointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Psalms 72:18–19; Psalms 41:13; 1 Timothy 1:17"> (89:53) Blessed be the Lord for evermore. Amen, and Amen.</span>
