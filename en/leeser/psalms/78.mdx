<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 51:4; Isaiah 55:3; Psalms 74:1"> “A Maskil of Assaph.” Give ear, O my people, to my instruction: incline thy ear to the words of my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 13:34–35; Mark 4:34; Matthew 13:11–13"> I will open with a parable my mouth: I will utter riddles out of ancient times;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 44:1; Exodus 13:14–15; Exodus 12:26–27"> Which we have heard and know, and which our fathers have related unto us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:19; Psalms 145:4–6; Deuteronomy 4:9"> We will not conceal them from their children, relating to the latest generation the praises of the Lord, and his strength, and his wonderful deeds which he hath done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Ephesians 6:4; Deuteronomy 4:45"> Yea, he established a testimony in Jacob, and instituted a law in Israel, which he commanded our fathers, that they should make them known to their children:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 102:18; Joel 1:3; Esther 9:28"> In order that the latest generation might know them, even the children that are to be born; that they may arise and relate them to their children;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:29; Revelation 14:12; 1 John 5:3"> That they may place in God their hope, and not forget the doings of God, but observe his commandments;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 78:37; Exodus 32:9; 2 Kings 17:14"> And that they may not be like their fathers, a stubborn and rebellious generation; a generation that had not directed their heart firmly, and whose spirit was not faithful to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:41–44; 1 Samuel 31:1; Psalms 78:57"> The children of Ephraim, like well-armed archers, that turn round on the day of battle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:20; Deuteronomy 31:16; Nehemiah 9:26–29"> Kept not the covenant of God, and in his law they refused to walk;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 106:13; Deuteronomy 32:18; Psalms 106:21–22"> And they forgot his deeds, as also his wonders, which he had permitted them to see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 13:22; Ezekiel 30:14; Isaiah 19:11"> In the presence of their fathers did he do wonders, in the land of Egypt, in the fields of Zo'an.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Exodus 15:8; Psalms 66:6"> He divided the sea, and caused them to pass through; and he made the waters stand upright as a wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 105:39; Exodus 14:24; Exodus 13:21–22"> And he led them with the cloud by day, and all the night with a light of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 105:41; Numbers 20:11; Exodus 17:6"> He split rocks in the wilderness, and gave them drink as out of the mighty deep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 20:8; Numbers 20:10–11; Deuteronomy 8:15"> And he brought forth running streams out of the rock, and caused water to run down like rivers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 63:10; Hebrews 3:16–19; Deuteronomy 9:12–22"> But they repeated to sin yet more against him, rebelling against the Most High in the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:16; Numbers 11:4; 1 Corinthians 10:9"> And they tempted God in their heart, by asking food for their desire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 21:5; Numbers 11:4; Exodus 16:3"> Yea, they spoke against God: they said, Will God be able to set in order a table in the wilderness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 20:11; Numbers 11:21–23; Psalms 78:41"> Behold, he smote the rock, so that waters gushed out, and streams overflowed: shall he also be able to give bread? or can he provide flesh for his people?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jude 1:5; 1 Corinthians 10:11; Numbers 11:1–3"> Therefore, when the Lord heard this, he became wroth: and a fire was kindled against Jacob, and anger also ascended against Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 3:18–19; Hebrews 3:12; Jude 1:5"> Because they had not believed in God, and had not trusted in his salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 7:11; Malachi 3:10; Psalms 33:9"> Then he ordained the skies from above, and the doors of heaven he opened;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 16:4; Psalms 105:40; John 6:31–71"> And he let rain down upon them manna to eat, and the corn of heaven gave he unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 14:20; Matthew 15:37; Psalms 103:20"> Angels' bread did man eat: he sent them provision to satisfaction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 11:31; Psalms 135:7"> He caused an east wind to pass along the heavens; and he led forth by his strength the south wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 11:18–19; Exodus 16:12–13; Numbers 11:32"> And he let rain upon them flesh &#91;as plentiful&#93; as the dust, and winged birds like the sand of the sea;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And he let them fall in the midst of their camp, round about their habitations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 106:15; Numbers 11:19–20"> And they ate, and were greatly satisfied, and what they longed for he brought unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 11:33–34; Luke 16:19–23; Numbers 22:20–22"> They were not estranged from their longing, yet was their food in their mouth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 10:16"> When the wrath of God ascended against them, and he slew some of the fattest of them, and the young men of Israel did he strike down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 12:37; Psalms 78:22; Psalms 78:11"> With all this they sinned again, and believed not in his wonders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 14:35; Numbers 14:29; Numbers 26:64–65"> Therefore he caused their days to come to an end in nought, and their years in dread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hosea 5:15; Numbers 21:7; Judges 3:12–15"> When he slew them, then did they seek him, and they returned and inquired earnestly after God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Isaiah 41:14; Exodus 15:13"> And they remembered that God was their rock, and the most high God their redeemer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31; Isaiah 29:13; Hosea 11:12"> Nevertheless they prayed insincerely to him with their mouth, and with their tongue they lied unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 8:21; Psalms 78:8; Psalms 51:10"> For their heart was not firm with him, and they were not faithful in his covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 48:9; Numbers 14:18–20; Ezekiel 20:17"> But he, being merciful, forgave the iniquity, and destroyed &#91;them&#93; not: yea, many a time turned he his anger away, and did not awaken all his fury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 6:3; James 4:14; Job 7:7"> And he remembered that they are but flesh, a spirit that passeth away, and returneth not again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Ephesians 4:30; Psalms 95:8–10; Hebrews 3:15–17"> How oft did they rebel against him in the wilderness, grieve him in the desert!</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Hebrews 3:8–11; Numbers 14:22; Acts 7:39"> Yea, they once more tempted God, and set limits to the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Judges 8:34; Psalms 78:11; Jeremiah 32:21"> They remembered not his hand, the day when he ransomed them from the adversary;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:22; Exodus 4:21; Exodus 3:19–20"> When he displayed in Egypt his signs, and his wonderful tokens in the fields of Zo'an.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 105:29; Revelation 16:3–6; Exodus 7:17–21"> And he changed their rivers into blood; and their running streams, that they could not drink &#91;of them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Exodus 8:21–24; Psalms 105:30–31; Exodus 8:2–15"> He sent out among them various wild beasts, which devoured them; and frogs, which destroyed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Exodus 10:12–15; Psalms 105:34–35; Amos 7:1–2"> And he gave unto the cricket their products, and their labor unto the locust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 105:32–33; Exodus 9:18–34"> He slew with hail their vines, and their sycamore-trees with ice-bolts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Exodus 9:19; Exodus 9:28"> And he surrendered to the hail their cattle, and their herds to the lightning's flashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Exodus 12:13; 1 Kings 22:21–22; Lamentations 4:11"> He let loose against them the fierceness of his anger, wrath and indignation, and distress, a host of angels of misfortune.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:4; 2 Peter 2:4–5; Romans 8:32"> He leveled a path for his anger; he withheld not from death their soul, and their life he surrendered to the pestilence;</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Psalms 105:23; Psalms 135:8; Psalms 106:22"> And he smote all the first-born in Egypt; the first of their strength in the tents of Ham;</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:12; Psalms 77:20; John 10:11–42"> But he caused his own people to depart like flocks, and guided them like a drove in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Exodus 14:19–20; Exodus 15:10; Exodus 14:27–28"> And he led them in safety, so that they felt no dread; but the sea covered over their enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Psalms 44:3; Exodus 15:17; Isaiah 11:9"> And he brought them to his holy territory, even to this mount, which his right hand had acquired.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Joshua 13:7; Psalms 44:2; Nehemiah 9:22–25"> And he drove out from before them nations, and divided them by the measuring-line as an inheritance, and he caused to dwell in their tents the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Judges 2:11–12; Deuteronomy 32:15–21; Psalms 78:40–41"> Yet they tempted and rebelled against the most high God, and his testimonies they kept not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Hosea 7:16; Ezekiel 20:27–28; Psalms 78:8"> But swerved aside, and dealt unfaithfully like their fathers; they turned about like a deceitful bow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Leviticus 26:30; 1 Kings 12:31"> And they provoked him to anger with their high-places, and with their graven images they moved him to jealousy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Psalms 106:40; Lamentations 2:7; Deuteronomy 32:19"> God heard this, and he became wroth, and felt greatly disgusted with Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; 1 Samuel 4:4–11; Jeremiah 26:6–9"> And he cast off the dwelling at Shiloh, the tabernacle where he had dwelt among men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Psalms 132:8; 1 Samuel 4:21–2; 1 Samuel 4:17"> And he gave up his strength unto captivity, and his glory into the adversary's hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Psalms 89:38; Isaiah 64:9; 1 Samuel 4:10–11"> And he surrendered his people unto the sword; and with his inheritance was he wroth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:34; Jeremiah 16:9; Deuteronomy 32:22"> His young men the fire devoured; and his virgins were not demanded in marriage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:17; Job 27:15; Ezekiel 24:23"> His priests fell by the sword; and his widows did not weep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Psalms 44:23; Psalms 7:6; Isaiah 51:9"> Then awoke the Lord as one that sleepeth, like a mighty man that shouteth by reason of wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:6; Jeremiah 23:40; Job 40:12"> And he smote his enemies backward: a perpetual disgrace on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:21–1; 2 Samuel 6:2; 2 Samuel 6:17"> Yet was he disgusted with the tent of Joseph, and of the tribe of Ephraim he made not choice;</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Psalms 87:2; Psalms 132:12–14; Ruth 4:17–22"> But he chose the tribe of Judah, the mount Zion which he loved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="Job 26:7; 1 Chronicles 29:19; 1 Samuel 2:8"> And he built like high &#91;mountains&#93; his sanctuary, like the earth which he hath founded for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:11–12; Acts 13:22; Exodus 3:1"> And he made choice of David his servant, and took him from the sheep-folds:</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:2; 1 Chronicles 11:2; Ezekiel 34:23–24"> From following the ewes with young he brought him, to feed Jacob his people, and Israel his inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72" data-xref="1 Kings 9:4; 2 Timothy 2:15; Isaiah 11:2–4"> And he fed them according to the integrity of his heart; and by the skilfulness of his hands did he lead them.</span>
