<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 105:2–3; Revelation 5:13; Isaiah 49:13"> Sing forth the glory of his name; make glorious his praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 18:44; Psalms 65:5; Psalms 81:15"> Say unto God, How fear-inspiring is every one of thy works! through the greatness of thy strength will thy enemies yield feigned obedience unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 22:27; Psalms 117:1; Psalms 96:1–2"> All the lands shall bow themselves down unto thee, and shall sing praises unto thee; they shall sing praises to thy name. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 126:1–3; Numbers 23:23; Psalms 66:16"> Come and see the deeds of God: fear-inspiring is his doing toward the children of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 3:16; Exodus 15:1–21; Joshua 3:14"> He changed the sea into dry land: through the river they went on foot: there did we rejoice in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 11:4; Matthew 28:18; Psalms 140:8"> He ruleth by his might for ever; his eyes look upon the nations: the rebellious—these shall not be exalted. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 98:4; Revelation 19:1; Jeremiah 33:11"> Bless, O ye people, our God, and cause the voice of his praise to be heard:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 121:3; Psalms 112:6; Psalms 94:18"> Who hath appointed our soul to life, and hath not suffered our foot to slip.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 48:10; Psalms 17:3; 1 Peter 1:6–7"> For thou hast proved us, O God: thou hast refined us, as silver is refined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Lamentations 1:13; Ezekiel 12:13; Lamentations 3:2–66"> Thou hast brought us into the net; thou hast placed fetters upon our loins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 43:1–2; Isaiah 51:23; Psalms 40:2–3"> Thou hast caused men to ride on our head: we entered into fire and into water; but thou broughtest us out to &#91;the enjoyment&#93; of overflowing plenty. a</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 100:4; Psalms 56:12; Ecclesiastes 5:4"> I will enter thy house with burnt-offerings: I will pay unto thee my vows,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:11; Judges 11:35–36; Psalms 18:6"> Which my lips have uttered, and my mouth hath spoken, when I was in distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 51:19; 2 Samuel 6:17–19; 1 Chronicles 16:1–3"> Burnt-offerings of fatlings will I offer up unto thee, with the incense of rams; I will prepare steers with he-goats. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 71:15; Psalms 34:11; Psalms 71:18"> Come, hear, and I will relate, all ye that fear God, what he hath done for my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 34:6; Psalms 145:1; Psalms 116:12"> Unto him I cried with my mouth, and a song of extolling was on my tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 9:31; Proverbs 28:9; Proverbs 15:29"> If I had looked on wickedness with my heart, the Lord would not have heard;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 116:1–2; Hebrews 5:7; Lamentations 3:55–56"> But verily God hath heard; he hath listened to the voice of my prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 86:12–13; Psalms 68:35; Psalms 51:11"> Blessed be God, who hath not removed my prayer &#91;from him&#93;, nor his kindness from me.</span>
