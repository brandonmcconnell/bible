<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 15:34; Matthew 27:46; Hebrews 5:7"> “To the chief musician upon Ayeleth-hashachar, a psalm of David.” (22:2) My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me? why art thou so far from saving me, and from the words of my loud complaint?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 42:3; Luke 18:7; Luke 22:41–46"> (22:3) O my God! I call in the day-time, but thou answerest not; and in the night I find no rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 50:23; Deuteronomy 10:21; Isaiah 6:3"> (22:4) But thou art holy, O thou that dwellest amidst the praises of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 14:31; Exodus 14:13–14; Hebrews 11:8–32"> (22:5) In thee did our fathers trust: they trusted, and thou didst deliver them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 10:11; Isaiah 49:23; Romans 9:33"> (22:6) Unto thee they cried, and were delivered: in thee they trusted, and were not put to shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 53:3; Job 25:6; Isaiah 49:7"> (22:7) But I am a worm, and not a man, a reproach of men, and despised of people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 15:29; Psalms 109:25; Matthew 27:39–40"> (22:8) All those who see me laugh me to scorn: they draw open their lips, they shake their head, &#91;saying,&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 27:42–43; Mark 15:30–32; Psalms 91:14"> (22:9) “Let him throw himself on the Lord, that he may deliver him: he will save him, for he delighteth in him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 71:6; Isaiah 49:1–2; Psalms 71:17"> (22:10) Yea, thou art he that took me from the womb: thou hast been my trust when I hung on my mother's breasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 1:15; Jeremiah 1:5; Isaiah 49:1"> (22:11) Upon thee was I cast from my birth: from my mother's womb art thou my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 71:12; Psalms 72:12; Psalms 10:1"> (22:12) Oh be not far from me &#91;now&#93;; for distress is near; for there is none to help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 4:1–3; Psalms 68:30; Acts 4:27"> (22:13) Many steers have encompassed me: the strong bulls of Bashan have beset me round.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Psalms 35:21; Lamentations 3:46"> (22:14) They have opened wide against me their mouth, &#91;as&#93; a ravenous and roaring lion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 31:10; Daniel 5:6; Job 30:16"> (22:15) Like water am I poured out, and all my bones are disjointed: my heart is become like wax, it is melted in the midst of my entrails.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 19:28; Psalms 69:3; Psalms 69:21"> (22:16) Like a potsherd is my strength dried up; and my tongue cleaveth to my palate; and into the dust of death hast thou laid me down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 19:37; Zechariah 12:10; Matthew 27:35"> (22:17) For dogs have encompassed me; the assembly of the wicked have enclosed me: like lions &#91;they threaten&#93; my hands and my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 23:35; Job 33:21; Matthew 27:39–41"> (22:18) I may number all my bones: &#91;while&#93; they stare and look upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 27:35; Luke 23:34; Mark 15:24"> (22:19) They divide my clothes among themselves, and for my garment do they cast lots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 22:11; Psalms 40:13; Psalms 40:17"> (22:20) But thou, O Lord, be not far from me: O &#91;thou who art&#93; my strength, hasten to my aid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 35:17; Zechariah 13:7; Psalms 17:13"> (22:21) Deliver from the sword my life, from the power of the dog my solitary soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:17; Numbers 23:22; 1 Peter 5:8"> (22:22) Save me from the lion's mouth; as thou hast answered me from the horns of the reem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 2:11–12; Romans 8:29; Psalms 40:9–10"> (22:23) I will relate thy name unto my brethren: in the midst of the congregation will I praise thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 135:19–20; Revelation 15:4; Psalms 107:1–2"> (22:24) Ye that fear the Lord, praise him; all ye the seed of Jacob, glorify him; and be in dread of him, all ye the seed of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Isaiah 50:6–9; Psalms 116:3–6"> (22:25) For he hath not despised nor abhorred the affliction of the poor; neither hath he hidden his face from him; but when he cried unto him, he heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 40:9–10; Psalms 35:18; Psalms 111:1"> (22:26) Of thee shall be my praise in a great assembly: my vows will I pay in the presence of those who fear him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 69:32; Psalms 40:16; Isaiah 65:13"> (22:27) The meek shall eat and be satisfied; they who seek him shall praise the Lord: may your heart live for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 86:9; Isaiah 45:22; Psalms 2:8"> (22:28) All the ends of the world shall remember and return unto the Lord: and all the families of the nations shall bow themselves down before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Zechariah 14:9; Psalms 47:7–8; Revelation 11:15"> (22:29) For the kingdom is the Lord's, and he governeth the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Philippians 2:10; Isaiah 26:19; Psalms 45:12"> (22:30) All they that eat the fat of the earth shall bow themselves down; before him shall bend the knee all that are going down into the dust; for none can keep alive his own soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 2:13; Psalms 14:5; 1 Peter 2:9"> (22:31) Distant ages shall serve him; there shall be related of the Lord unto future generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 78:6; Psalms 86:9; Romans 1:17"> (22:32) They will come, and will tell his righteousness unto a people yet unborn, that he hath done this.</span>
