<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 34:4; Psalms 143:1–3; Lamentations 3:55–56"> “To the chief musician, a psalm of David.” (64:2) Hear my voice, O God, in my complaint: preserve my life from the dread of the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 143:9; Psalms 59:2; Psalms 109:2–3"> (64:3) Hide me from the secret counsel of evil-doers, from the tumultuous assault of the workers of wickedness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Psalms 58:7; James 3:6–8"> (64:4) Who whet their tongue like a sword, who aim with their arrow, the bitter word:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 55:19; Psalms 11:2; 1 Peter 2:22–23"> (64:5) To shoot in secret at the innocent; suddenly do they shoot at him, and fear not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 140:5; Psalms 10:11; Ezekiel 8:12"> (64:6) They encourage themselves in an evil plan; they tell secretly of laying snares: they say, Who will see them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:9–10; Matthew 26:59; Psalms 5:9"> (64:7) They search out iniquities; “We are ready with the carefully searched out device:” and the inward thought and heart of each is deeply &#91;hidden&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 7:12–13; 1 Thessalonians 5:2–3; Proverbs 6:15"> (64:8) But God shooteth at them suddenly &#91;his&#93; arrow;—&#91;thence&#93; are come their wounds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 18:7; Proverbs 12:13; Psalms 140:9"> (64:9) And their own tongues will stumble over themselves: all that look on them will shake their head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:10; Jeremiah 50:28; Psalms 40:3"> (64:10) All men shall fear, and shall declare the deeds of God, and understand his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 32:11–1; Psalms 40:3; 1 Corinthians 1:30–31"> (64:11) The righteous shall rejoice in the Lord, and shall trust in him; and all the upright in heart shall glorify themselves.</span>
