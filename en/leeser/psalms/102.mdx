<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 18:6; Psalms 130:1–2; Psalms 142:2–3"> “A prayer of the afflicted, when he is overwhelmed, and poureth out before the Lord his complaint.” (102:2) O Lord, hear my prayer, and let my cry come unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 69:17; Psalms 27:9; Isaiah 8:17"> (102:3) Hide not thy face from me on the day when I am distressed; incline unto me thy ear; on the day when I call, answer me speedily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 4:14; Psalms 37:20; Job 30:30"> (102:4) For my days vanish in smoke, and my bones are burning like a hearth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 40:7; Psalms 37:2; Psalms 77:3"> (102:5) Struck &#91;by heat&#93; like the herb and dried up is my heart; for I forget to eat my bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 19:20; Lamentations 4:8; Psalms 6:8"> (102:6) Because of the voice of my groaning my bones cleave to my flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:14; Isaiah 38:14; Isaiah 34:11–15"> (102:7) I am like the pelican of the wilderness: I am become like the owl amid ruins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 77:4; Psalms 38:11; Deuteronomy 28:66–67"> (102:8) I watch, and I am become like a &#91;night-&#93;bird sitting alone upon the housetop.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 26:11; Psalms 69:9–10; Isaiah 65:15"> (102:9) All the day my enemies reproach me: they that mock me swear by me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 42:3; Psalms 80:5; Micah 1:10"> (102:10) For ashes do I eat like bread, and my drink I mingle with weeping;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 38:3; Psalms 90:7–9; Psalms 147:6"> (102:11) Because of thy indignation and thy wrath; for thou hadst lifted me up, and hast cast me down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 14:2; Psalms 109:23; Psalms 144:4"> (102:12) My days are like a shadow that declineth; and like the herb I wither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 135:13; Lamentations 5:19; Exodus 3:15"> (102:13) But thou, O Lord, wilt sit enthroned for ever; and thy memorial is unto all generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1; Galatians 4:4; Isaiah 40:2"> (102:14) Thou wilt indeed arise; thou wilt have mercy upon Zion; for it is time to favor her, for the appointed time is coming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:2; Ezra 7:27; Ezra 3:1–3"> (102:15) For thy servants hold dear her stones, and her very dust they cherish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 8:43; Psalms 138:4; Isaiah 60:3–22"> (102:16) Then shall nations fear the name of the Lord, and all the kings of the earth thy glory:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1–2; Psalms 147:2; Jeremiah 33:7"> (102:17) When the Lord shalt have built up Zion, he appeareth in his glory;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:6; Psalms 22:24; Jeremiah 29:11–14"> (102:18) &#91;When&#93; he hath regarded the prayer of the forsaken, and doth not despise their prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 22:30–31; Romans 15:4; Isaiah 43:21"> (102:19) This shall be written down for the latest generation; and the people which shall be created shall praise the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:15; Psalms 14:2; 1 Kings 8:43"> (102:20) For he hath looked down from the height of his sanctuary; the Lord hath cast from heaven his view to the earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 79:11; Psalms 146:7; Exodus 3:7"> (102:21) To hear the sighing of the prisoner; to loosen those that are doomed to death:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 22:22; Isaiah 51:11; Psalms 79:13"> (102:22) That men may proclaim in Zion the name of the Lord, and his praise in Jerusalem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zechariah 8:20–23; Matthew 24:14; Psalms 72:8–11"> (102:23) When people are gathered together, and kingdoms, to serve the Lord.—</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 89:38–47; 1 Timothy 4:1–3; 2 Thessalonians 2:3–12"> (102:24) He hath weakened on the way my strength; he hath shortened my days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 39:13; Habakkuk 1:12; Psalms 102:12"> (102:25) I will say, O my God! take me not away in the midst of my days: throughout all generations are thy years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 1:1; Exodus 20:11; Hebrews 1:10–12"> (102:26) In olden times didst thou lay the foundations of the earth; and the heavens are the work of thy hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 51:6; Matthew 24:35; Isaiah 34:4"> (102:27) These will indeed perish, but thou wilt ever exist: yea, all of them will wear out like a garment; as a vesture wilt thou change them, and they will be changed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 13:8; James 1:17; Malachi 3:6"> (102:28) But thou art ever the same, and thy years will have no end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 69:35–36; Psalms 45:16–17; Psalms 22:30–31"> (102:29) The children of thy servants will dwell &#91;securely&#93;, and their seed will be firmly established before thee.</span>
