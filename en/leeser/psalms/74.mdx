<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:20; Psalms 79:13; Psalms 100:3"> “A Maskil of Assaph.” Why, O God, hast thou cast us off for ever? why will thy anger smoke against the flock of thy pasture?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:16; Exodus 15:16; Exodus 15:13"> Remember thy congregation, which thou didst acquire of old; which thou didst redeem as the tribe of thy inheritance: this mount Zion, whereon thou hast dwelt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 1:10; Psalms 79:1; Daniel 9:17"> Lift up thy steps unto the perpetual heaps of ruins: the enemy hath ill-used every thing in the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Lamentations 2:7; Matthew 24:15; Daniel 6:27"> Thy adversaries have roared in the midst of thy places of assembly: they have set up their signs for signs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 5:6; 2 Chronicles 2:14; Jeremiah 46:22–23"> &#91;The enemy&#93; is known as one that lifteth up high axes against the thickets of a forest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 6:18; 1 Kings 6:29; 1 Kings 6:35"> And now they hew in pieces the carved work thereof altogether with hatchets and hammers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 25:9; Psalms 89:39; Ezekiel 24:21"> They have set on fire thy sanctuary; to the ground have they profaned the dwelling-place of thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 83:4; 2 Kings 4:23; Esther 3:8–9"> They have said in their heart, We will oppress them altogether: they have burnt up all the places of assembly of God in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 8:11; Ezekiel 7:26; 1 Samuel 3:1"> Our signs do we not see: there is no more any prophet: and there is no one among us that knoweth how long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 89:50–51; Psalms 44:16; Psalms 89:46"> How long, O God, shall the adversary utter defiance? shall the enemy blaspheme thy name for ever?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Lamentations 2:3; Psalms 78:65–66; Psalms 44:23"> Why withdrawest thou thy hand, and thy right hand? &#91;draw it&#93; out of thy bosom—exterminate &#91;them&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 44:4; Exodus 19:5–6; Isaiah 33:22"> Since &#91;thou&#93; God art my King from olden days, working salvation in the midst of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Psalms 78:13; Ezekiel 29:3"> It was thou that didst divide by thy strength the sea: thou brokest in pieces the heads of the crocodiles on the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 27:1; Psalms 72:9; Psalms 104:25–26"> Thou didst crush the heads of leviathan, and gavest them as food to the people inhabiting the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 20:11; Isaiah 48:21; Psalms 105:41"> Thou didst cleave fountain and stream: thou didst dry up ever-flowing rivers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 136:7–9; Matthew 5:45; Psalms 104:19"> Thine is the day and thine is the night: it is thou who hast prepared the luminary and the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:8; Acts 17:26; Genesis 8:22"> It is thou who hast set up all the boundaries of the earth: summer and winter—thou thyself hast formed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 39:8; Revelation 16:19; Deuteronomy 32:6"> Remember this, that the enemy hath defied the Lord, and that a worthless foolish people have blasphemed thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:14; Matthew 10:16; Psalms 9:18"> Oh give not up unto the multitude of enemies the soul of thy turtle-dove: the congregation of thy afflicted do not thou forget for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 106:45; Genesis 17:7–8; Hebrews 8:10"> Look unto the covenant; for the dark places of the earth are full of habitations of violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 12:5; Psalms 35:10; Psalms 9:18"> Oh let not the oppressed return confounded: let the poor and needy praise thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 74:18; Psalms 53:1; Isaiah 52:5"> Arise, O God, plead thy own cause: remember thy defiance from the worthless fool all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 37:29; Psalms 65:7; Jonah 1:2"> Forget not the voice of thy adversaries: the tumult of those that rise up against thee ascendeth continually.</span>
