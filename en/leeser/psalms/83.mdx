<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 28:1; Psalms 35:22; Psalms 50:3"> “A song or psalm of Assaph.” (83:2) O God, take no rest for thyself: be not silent and keep not still, O God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 81:15; Matthew 27:24; Judges 8:28"> (83:3) For, lo, thy enemies make a tumult, and they that hate thee have lifted up their head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 64:2; Psalms 27:5; Psalms 31:20"> (83:4) Against thy people they take crafty secret device, and they consult against those whom thou protectest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:2; Jeremiah 11:19; Psalms 74:8"> (83:5) They have said, Come, and let us cut them off from being a nation; and the name of Israel shall be remembered no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 2:2; Revelation 17:13; Isaiah 8:9–10"> (83:6) For they have consulted cordially together; against thee they make a covenant:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:10; 2 Chronicles 20:1; Psalms 137:7"> (83:7) The tents of Edom, and the Ishmaelites: Moab, and the Hagarenes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 13:5; Ezekiel 27:3; Ezekiel 27:9"> (83:8) Gebal, and 'Ammon, and 'Amalek; the Philistines with the inhabitants of Tyre;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:9; Genesis 25:3; 2 Kings 15:19"> (83:9) Also Asshur is joined with them; they have become an arm unto the children of Lot. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 4:15–24; Isaiah 9:4; Judges 5:21"> (83:10) Do unto them as &#91;unto&#93; Midian; as to Sissera, as to Jabin, at the brook Kishon:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:17; Joshua 17:11; 1 Samuel 28:7"> (83:11) Who were annihilated at 'En-dor; they became as dung for the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 7:25; Judges 8:12–21"> (83:12) Render them, their nobles, like 'Oreb, and like Zeeb; yea, like Zebach and like Zalmunna' all their princes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:11; Psalms 83:4; Psalms 74:7–8"> (83:13) Who said, Let us conquer for ourselves the dwellings of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 21:18; Psalms 35:5; Jeremiah 13:24"> (83:14) O my God, render them like the thistle-down, like stubble before the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:22; Isaiah 9:18; Malachi 4:1"> (83:15) As the fire burneth up a forest, and as the flame setteth the mountains on fire:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 9:17; Psalms 50:3; Psalms 58:9"> (83:16) So pursue them with thy storm, and with thy whirlwind do thou terrify them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 9:19–20; Psalms 6:10; Psalms 109:29"> (83:17) Fill their faces with shame, that they may seek thy name, O Lord!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 35:4; Psalms 40:14–15; Psalms 35:26"> (83:18) Let them be made ashamed and terrified for ever and aye; yea, let them be put to the blush and perish:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 6:3; Psalms 59:13; Jeremiah 16:21"> (83:19) That they may know that thou, whose name is the Eternal, art by thyself alone, the Most High over all the earth.</span>
