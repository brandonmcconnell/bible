<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 49:3; 2 Samuel 23:2; Job 33:3"> “To the chief musician upon Shoshannim, by the sons of Korach, a Maskil, a song of love.” (45:2) My heart swelleth with a good speech; I say, “My works shall be for the king:” my tongue is the pen of a ready writer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 4:22; John 7:46; Hebrews 7:26"> (45:3) Thou art more fair than the children of men; grace is poured out over thy lips: therefore hath God blessed thee for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Revelation 1:16; Isaiah 49:2"> (45:4) Gird thy sword upon the thigh, O mighty one, &#91;it is&#93; thy glory and thy majesty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 65:5; Revelation 6:2; Revelation 19:11"> (45:5) Yea, it is thy majesty: be prosperous, ride along for the cause of truth and meekness and righteousness; and fearful things shall thy right hand teach thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 21:12; Numbers 24:8; Psalms 38:2"> (45:6) Thy sharpened arrows—people will fall down beneath thee—&#91;will enter&#93; into the heart of the king's enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 1:8–9; Psalms 93:2; John 1:1"> (45:7) Thy throne, given of God, endureth for ever and ever: the sceptre of equity is the sceptre of thy kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 1:9; Psalms 21:6; Isaiah 61:1–3"> (45:8) Thou lovest righteousness, and hatest wickedness: therefore hath God, thy God, anointed thee with the oil of gladness above thy associates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:3; 2 Corinthians 2:14–16; John 19:39"> (45:9) Of myrrh, and aloes, and cassia are &#91;fragrant&#93; all thy garments: out of palaces of ivory have they made thee joyful with the sound of music.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 2:19; Revelation 21:9; Ephesians 5:26–27"> (45:10) Kings' daughters are among those dear to thee: the queen standeth on thy right hand in fine gold of Ophir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:10–13; Genesis 12:1; Matthew 10:37"> (45:11) Hearken, O daughter, and look, and incline thy ear, and forget thy own people, and thy father's house:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 95:6; Isaiah 54:5; Song of Solomon 4:1–5"> (45:12) Then will the king long for thy beauty; for he is thy Lord; and bow thyself to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 22:29; Isaiah 60:3; Isaiah 49:23"> (45:13) And the daughter of Tyre shall be there with a gift: the rich among the people shall entreat thy favor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 19:7–8; Isaiah 61:10; Romans 3:22"> (45:14) All gloriously attired awaiteth the king's daughter in the inner chamber: of wrought gold is her garment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:2; Song of Solomon 8:13; Exodus 28:39"> (45:15) In embroidered clothes will she be brought unto the king: virgins that follow her, her companions, are brought unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 7:15–17; Jude 1:24; Isaiah 55:12–13"> (45:16) They are led forth with rejoicings and gladness, they enter into the palace of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; 1 Peter 2:9; Revelation 1:6"> (45:17) Instead of thy fathers shall be thy children: thou wilt appoint them as princes in all the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 62:3; Malachi 1:11; Psalms 72:17–19"> (45:18) I will make thy name to be remembered in all generations: therefore shall the people praise thee for ever and ever.</span>
