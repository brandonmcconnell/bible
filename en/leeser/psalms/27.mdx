<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 13:6; Psalms 118:6; Exodus 15:2"> “Of David.” The Lord is my light and my salvation; of whom shall I be afraid? the Lord is the fortress of my life; of whom shall I have dread?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 118:12; Psalms 14:4; Psalms 9:3"> When evil-doers come near against me to eat up my flesh, my assailants and my enemies at me: then do they stumble and fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 3:6; 1 Peter 3:14; Romans 8:35–37"> If an army should encamp against me, my heart shall not fear: if war should arise against me, even then will I have trust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 26:8; Psalms 23:6; Psalms 65:4"> One thing have I asked of the Lord, that I will seek for: that I may dwell in the house of the Lord all the days of my life, to behold the loveliness of the Lord, and to be every morning early in his temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 18:10; Psalms 31:20; Psalms 138:7"> For he will hide me in his pavilion on the day of evil; he will conceal me in the secret of his tabernacle; upon a rock will he place me high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 3:3; Hebrews 13:15; Psalms 107:22"> And now will my head be lifted up above my enemies all round about me; and I will sacrifice in his tabernacle sacrifices of joy: I will sing, and I will triumphantly play unto the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 130:2–4; Psalms 13:3; Psalms 5:2"> Hear, O Lord, my voice, &#91;when&#93; I call, and be gracious unto me, and answer me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 105:4; Jeremiah 29:12–13; Isaiah 55:6–7"> Of thee, said my heart, “Seek ye my presence”: thy presence, Lord, will I seek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 69:17; Psalms 143:7; Psalms 102:2"> Hide &#91;then&#93; not thy face from me; reject not in anger thy servant, thou &#91;who&#93; hast been my help: cast me not off, nor forsake me, O God of my salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 49:15; Isaiah 40:11; John 16:32"> For my father and my mother have forsaken me; but the Lord will take me up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 25:4–5; Psalms 5:8; Psalms 86:11"> Point me out thy way, O Lord! and guide me on a level path, because of those that regard me enviously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 31:8; Psalms 41:11; Matthew 26:59–60"> Give me not up to the &#91;revengeful&#93; desire of my assailants; for there are risen up against me false witnesses, and such as utter violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 142:5; 2 Corinthians 4:16; 2 Corinthians 4:8–14"> Unless I had believed to see the goodness of the Lord in the land of life—</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:31; Psalms 31:24; Isaiah 30:18"> Wait on the Lord; be strong, and let thy heart be of good courage; and only wait on the Lord.</span>
