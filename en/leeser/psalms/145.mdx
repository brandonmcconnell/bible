<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 12:4; Psalms 119:27; Psalms 72:18"> On the majestic glory of thy excellence, and on thy wondrous deeds will I meditate.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6–7; 1 Chronicles 29:11–12; Psalms 93:1–2"> Of the glory of thy kingdom shall they converse, and of thy might shall they speak:</span>
