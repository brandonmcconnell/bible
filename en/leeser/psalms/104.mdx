<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 103:22; Psalms 145:3; Psalms 96:6"> Bless, O my soul, the Lord. O Lord my God, thou art very great; with glory and majesty art thou clothed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 40:22; Daniel 7:9; Isaiah 45:12"> &#91;Thou art he&#93; who wrappeth himself in light as with a garment; who stretcheth out the heavens like a curtain;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 9:6; 2 Samuel 22:11; Isaiah 19:1"> Who frameth of the waters the beams of his upper-chambers; who maketh the clouds his chariot; who walketh along upon the wings of the wind:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 1:7; 2 Kings 2:11; 2 Kings 6:17"> Who maketh the winds his messengers; the flaming fire his ministers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 26:7; Psalms 24:2; Psalms 33:9"> Who hath founded the earth upon her bases, that she should not be moved to all eternity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 7:19; 2 Peter 3:5; Genesis 1:2–10"> Thou hadst covered the deep as with a garment: above the mountains stood the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 18:15; Genesis 8:1; Mark 4:39"> At thy rebuke they fled, at the voice of thy thunder they hastened away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 8:5; Psalms 33:7"> They ascended mountains; they went down valleys, unto the place which thou hadst founded for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 38:10–11; Genesis 9:11–15; Jeremiah 5:22"> Bounds hast thou set which they cannot pass over, that they return not again to cover the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 41:18; Psalms 107:35; Isaiah 35:7"> &#91;Thou art he&#93; who sendeth springs into the valleys, between mountains they run along.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 145:16; Job 39:5–8; Psalms 104:13"> They give drink to all the beasts of the field: the wild asses quench &#91;thereon&#93; their thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 50:11; Psalms 148:10; Matthew 8:20"> By them have the fowls of the heaven ever their habitation, from between the branches they send forth their voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 147:8; Jeremiah 10:13; Jeremiah 14:22"> Who watereth the mountains from his upper-chambers: from the fruit of thy works is the earth satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 136:25; Job 28:5; Psalms 147:8–9"> &#91;Thou art he&#93; who causeth grass to grow for the cattle, and herbs by the service of man, that he may bring forth bread out of the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 23:5; Ecclesiastes 10:19; Ecclesiastes 9:7"> And wine that maketh joyful the heart of man, &#91;and&#93; oil to brighten his face, and bread which strengtheneth the heart of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 24:6; Psalms 92:2; Psalms 29:5"> Full of sap are the trees of the Lord, the cedars of Lebanon which he hath planted;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 104:12; Leviticus 11:19; Daniel 4:21"> Where the birds make their nests: the stork—fir-trees are her house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 30:26; Job 39:1; Leviticus 11:5"> The high mountains are for the wild goats: the rocks are a shelter for the conies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:35; Genesis 1:14–18; Psalms 136:7–9"> He hath made the moon for seasons: the sun knoweth his going down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Psalms 74:16; Amos 1:13"> Thou causest darkness, and it becometh night, wherein creep forth all the beasts of the forest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 38:39; Joel 1:20; Psalms 34:10"> The young lions roar after their prey, and ask from God their food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 37:8; Nahum 3:17; John 3:20"> The sun ariseth, they withdraw &#91;to their lairs&#93;, and lie down in their dens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 5:12; Ephesians 4:28"> Man goeth &#91;then&#93; forth unto his work, and to his labor until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:12; Psalms 40:5; Psalms 50:10–12"> How manifold are thy works, O Lord! in wisdom hast thou made them all: the earth is full of thy riches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 69:34; Psalms 95:4–5; Genesis 1:28"> Here is this great and wide-extended sea; therein are moving things without number, living creatures both small and great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 107:23; Isaiah 27:1; Psalms 74:14"> There the ships make their way: &#91;there also&#93; is the leviathan, whom thou hast made to sport therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 136:25; Psalms 147:9; Job 38:41"> All of these wait upon thee, to give them their food in its due season.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 145:16"> What thou givest them they gather: thou openest thy hand, they are satisfied with good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 34:14–15; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Psalms 146:4"> Thou hidest thy face, they suddenly vanish: thou takest away their spirit, they perish, and to their dust they return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 33:4; Ezekiel 37:9; Revelation 21:5"> Thou sendest forth thy spirit, they are created; and thou renewest the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 1:31; 2 Timothy 4:18; Ephesians 3:21"> The glory of the Lord will endure for ever; the Lord will rejoice in his works:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 144:5; Psalms 97:4–5; Exodus 19:18"> He who looketh down on the earth, and she trembleth; who toucheth the mountains, and they smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 63:4; Psalms 146:2; Psalms 145:1–2"> I will sing unto the Lord while I live: I will sing praises to my God while I exist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:17–18; Philippians 4:4; Psalms 1:2"> May my speech be agreeable to him: I will indeed rejoice in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 103:22–1; Psalms 37:38; Psalms 106:48"> May the sinners cease from off the earth, and the wicked be no more. Bless, O my soul, the Lord. Hallelujah.</span>
