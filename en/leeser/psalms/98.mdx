<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 15:6; Isaiah 52:10; Psalms 33:3"> “A psalm.” Oh sing unto the Lord a new song; for he hath done wonderful things: his right hand and his holy arm have gotten him the victory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 52:10; Isaiah 62:2; Psalms 22:31"> The Lord hath made known his salvation: before the eyes of the nations hath he revealed his righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 22:27; Acts 13:47; Deuteronomy 4:31"> He hath remembered his kindness and his truth toward the house of Israel: all the ends of the earth have seen the salvation of our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 44:23; Psalms 100:1; Psalms 66:1"> Shout joyfully unto the Lord, all the lands: break forth, and rejoice, and sing praises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 51:3; Revelation 5:8; Psalms 33:2"> Sing praises unto the Lord with the harp,—with the harp, and the voice of psalmody.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 81:2–4; Revelation 19:16; 2 Chronicles 15:14"> With trumpets and the sound of cornet shout joyfully before the King, the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 49:13; Psalms 24:1; Psalms 96:11–1"> Let the sea roar, with all that filleth it; the world, with those that dwell therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 55:12; Psalms 89:12; Psalms 65:12–13"> Let the rivers clap their hands; let the mountains be joyful together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 96:13; Psalms 96:10; Acts 17:31"> Before the Lord; for he cometh to judge the earth: he will judge the world with righteousness, and people with equity.</span>
