<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5–6; 1 Chronicles 22:12–13; Isaiah 11:2"> “By Solomon.” O God, give unto the king thy decisions, and thy righteousness unto the king's son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 32:1; Psalms 72:12–14; Revelation 19:11"> He shall decide for thy people with righteousness, and for thy afflicted with justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Psalms 96:11–13; Psalms 72:16"> The mountains shall bear peace for the people, and the hills &#91;the same&#93;, through righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 11:4; Ezekiel 34:15–16; Psalms 109:31"> He shall judge the afflicted of the people, he shall give help to the children of the needy; but he shall crush the oppressor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 89:36–37; Psalms 72:17; Daniel 7:14"> They shall fear thee as long as the sun shineth, and in the presence of the moon, throughout all generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 6:3; Deuteronomy 32:2; 2 Samuel 23:4"> He shall come down like rain upon the mown grass, as showers which are dropping on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 2:4; Malachi 4:2; Psalms 92:12"> In his days shall the righteous flourish; and abundance of peace &#91;shall be&#93; till the moon shall be no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 23:31; Zechariah 9:10; Psalms 80:11"> And he shall have dominion from sea to sea, and from the river unto the ends of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; Micah 7:17; Luke 19:27"> Before him shall bend down those that dwell in the wilderness; and his enemies shall lick the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 60:6; Psalms 68:29; Isaiah 49:7"> The kings of Tharshish and of the isles shall bring presents: the kings of Sheba and Seba shall offer gifts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 86:9; Isaiah 49:22–23; Revelation 11:15"> Yea, there shall bow down before him all kings: all nations shall serve him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 29:12; Revelation 3:17–18; Psalms 102:17"> For he will deliver the needy when he crieth; the afflicted also, who hath no helper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Psalms 109:31; Matthew 5:3"> He will spare the poor and needy; and the souls of the needy will he assist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 116:15; Revelation 19:2; Psalms 69:18"> From wrong and violence will he deliver their soul; and precious shall their blood be in his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 10:14; Revelation 5:8–14; Jude 1:25"> And he shall live; and he will give him of the gold of Sheba: and he will pray in his behalf continually; all the time will he bless him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 7:9; Psalms 92:12–14; Isaiah 30:23"> There shall be an abundance of corn in the land; upon the top of the mountains its fruit shall shake like &#91;the trees of&#93; Lebanon; and &#91;men&#93; shall blossom out of the city like herbs of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:23; Psalms 89:36"> His name will endure for ever; in the presence of the sun his name shall flourish: and men shall bless themselves with him: all nations shall call him happy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 41:13; Psalms 106:48; 1 Chronicles 29:10"> Blessed be the Lord the God, the God of Israel, who alone doth wondrous things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 14:21; Matthew 6:10; Psalms 41:13"> And blessed he his glorious name for ever; and with his glory may the whole earth be filled: Amen, and Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:1; Job 31:40; Jeremiah 51:64"> Here are ended the prayers of David the son of Jesse.</span>
