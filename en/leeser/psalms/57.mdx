<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 138:8; Philippians 1:6; Hebrews 13:21"> (57:3) I will call unto God, the Most High; unto God that accomplisheth &#91;his kindness&#93; on me.</span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 58:6; Proverbs 30:14; Psalms 35:17"> (57:5) My soul is in the midst of lions; I lie down &#91;in the midst of&#93; those that send out flames, &#91;those&#93; sons of men, whose teeth are spears and arrows, and whose tongue is a sharpened sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:14; Isaiah 6:3; Psalms 72:19"> (57:6) Be thou exalted above the heavens, O God: above all the earth let thy glory be.</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 112:7; Ephesians 5:20; Psalms 34:4"> (57:8) Firm is my heart, O God, firm is my heart: I will sing and play.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 16:9; Psalms 108:1–3; Psalms 30:12"> (57:9) Awake, my spirit; awake, psaltery and harp: I will wake up the morning-dawn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 96:3; Psalms 22:22–23; Psalms 18:49"> (57:10) I will thank thee among the people, O Lord: I will sing praises unto thee among the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 108:4; Psalms 103:11; Psalms 36:5"> (57:11) For great, even unto the heavens, is thy kindness, and even unto the skies &#91;extendeth&#93; thy truth.</span>
