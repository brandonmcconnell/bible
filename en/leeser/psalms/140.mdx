<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 18:48; Psalms 140:4; Psalms 71:4"> “To the chief musician, a psalm of David.” (140:2) Deliver me, O Lord, from an evil man; from a man of violence do thou keep me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 56:6; Psalms 36:4; Psalms 64:5–6"> (140:3) Who think over evil &#91;resolves&#93; in their heart, &#91;who&#93; every day are gathered together for war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 58:4; 2 Corinthians 11:3; Romans 3:13–14"> (140:4) They have sharpened their tongues like a serpent: the poison of the adder is under their lips. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 36:11; Psalms 71:4; Psalms 17:5"> (140:5) Preserve me, O Lord, from the hands of the wicked, from the man of violence do thou keep me, who think of overthrowing my steps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 142:3; Psalms 35:7; Jeremiah 18:22"> (140:6) The proud have hidden a snare for me, and cords; they have spread a net by the side of &#91;my&#93; track; traps have they set for me. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 16:2; Psalms 31:14; Psalms 119:57"> (140:7) I have said unto the Lord, Thou art my God: give ear, O Lord, to the voice of my supplications.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 62:7; Psalms 89:26; Psalms 18:1–2"> (140:8) O thou Eternal Lord, the strength of my salvation, thou hast covered my head on the day of battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 10:2–3; Deuteronomy 32:27; Psalms 27:12"> (140:9) Grant not, O Lord, the longings of the wicked; suffer not his wicked device to succeed: lest they exalt themselves. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 7:16; Proverbs 18:7; Proverbs 12:13"> (140:10) &#91;As for&#93; the heads of those that encompass me about, let the mischief of their own lips cover them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 11:6; Psalms 21:9; Revelation 21:8"> (140:11) Let burning coals be cast upon them: let them be thrown into the fire; into deep pits, that they rise not up again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 34:21; Proverbs 18:21; Isaiah 3:11"> (140:12) Let not the man of an &#91;evil&#93; tongue be established on the earth: may evil hunt down the violent man to his downfall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 9:4; 1 Kings 8:49; Proverbs 23:10–11"> (140:13) I know that the Lord will procure right for the afflicted, &#91;and&#93; justice for the needy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 16:11; Psalms 11:7; Isaiah 3:10"> (140:14) Surely the righteous shall give thanks unto thy name: the upright shall dwell before thy presence.</span>
