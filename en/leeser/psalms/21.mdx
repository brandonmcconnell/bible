<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 2:2; Psalms 62:7; Psalms 28:7"> “To the chief musician, a psalm of David.” (21:2) O Lord, because of thy strength will the king rejoice; and through thy salvation how greatly will he be glad!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; Psalms 20:4–5; Psalms 2:8–9"> (21:3) The longing of his heart hast thou given him, and the request of his lips hast thou not withholden. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:30; Ephesians 1:3; Psalms 59:10"> (21:4) For thou meetest him unasked with the blessings of happiness: thou settest on his head a crown of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 91:16; Revelation 1:18; Psalms 119:77"> (21:5) Life hath he asked of thee, thou gavest it to him, length of days for ever and ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 3:22; John 17:22; Revelation 5:8–13"> (21:6) Great is his honor through thy help: glory and majesty thou layest upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 2:28; Psalms 45:7; Psalms 16:11"> (21:7) For thou appointest him to be a blessing for ever: thou makest him glad with joy from thy presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 16:8; Psalms 91:2; Psalms 20:7–8"> (21:8) For the king trusteth in the Lord; and through the kindness of the Most High shall he not be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 10:10; Psalms 72:9; Psalms 110:1–2"> (21:9) Thy hand will reach all thy enemies: thy right hand will reach those that hate thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Psalms 50:3; Psalms 18:8"> (21:10) Thou wilt render them as a fiery oven at the time of thy anger: the Lord in his wrath will destroy them, and a fire will devour them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 37:28; 1 Kings 13:34; Job 18:16–19"> (21:11) Their fruit wilt thou cause to perish from the earth, and their seed from among the children of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 10:2; Psalms 2:1; Psalms 31:13"> (21:12) For they directed against thee evil: they devised a mischievous purpose, which they were not able to perform.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 7:12–13; Psalms 18:40; Psalms 64:7"> (21:13) For thou wilt make them turn their back; upon thy bow-strings thou wilt make ready &#91;thy arrows&#93; against their face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 16:5–7; Revelation 19:1–6; Revelation 15:3–4"> (21:14) Exalt thyself, O Lord, in thy strength; &#91;and&#93; we will sing and praise thy power.</span>
