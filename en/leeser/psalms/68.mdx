<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 10:35; Isaiah 51:9–10; Isaiah 42:13–14"> “To the chief musician, by David, a Psalm or song.” (68:2) Oh that God would arise, that his enemies might be scattered, and those that hate him might flee before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 1:4; Psalms 37:20; Isaiah 9:18"> (68:3) As smoke is driven off, so drive them away: as wax melteth before the fire, so let the wicked perish at the presence of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 64:10; Psalms 100:1–2; 1 Thessalonians 5:16"> (68:4) But the righteous shall rejoice; they shall exult before God: yea, they shall be exceedingly joyful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:26; Isaiah 12:4–6; Psalms 66:4"> (68:5) Sing unto God, sing praises to his name; extol him who rideth upon the heavens: the Everlasting is his name, and rejoice before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 10:14; Jeremiah 49:11; Psalms 82:3–4"> (68:6) A father of the fatherless, and a judge of the widows, is God in his holy habitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 69:33; Psalms 113:9; Psalms 107:10"> (68:7) God places those who are solitary in the midst of their families: he bringeth out those who are bound unto happiness; but the rebellious dwell in a dry land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 13:21; Judges 5:4; Judges 4:14"> (68:8) O God, when thou didst go forth before thy people, when thou didst tread along through the wilderness—Selah—</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 19:18; Exodus 19:16; Deuteronomy 5:23–25"> (68:9) The earth quaked, also the heavens dropped at the presence of God, yea, this Sinai, at the presence of God, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:14; Psalms 65:9–13; Psalms 78:24–27"> (68:10) Rain of beneficence didst thou pour down, O God, whereby thou didst truly strengthen thy heritage, when it was weary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:9–10; Psalms 74:19; Matthew 11:5"> (68:11) Thy assembly dwelt therein: thou didst prepare it with thy goodness for the afflicted &#91;people&#93;. O God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 15:20; Psalms 40:3; Exodus 17:9–16"> (68:12) The Lord gave &#91;happy&#93; tidings; they are published by the female messengers, a numerous host.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 10:16; Judges 5:19; 1 Samuel 30:24"> (68:13) The kings of the armies flee away—flee away: yet she that tarried at home divideth the spoil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 105:37; Genesis 49:14; 1 Corinthians 6:9–11"> (68:14) When ye lie still between the folds &#91;of your cattle&#93;, &#91;ye will be like&#93; the wings of the dove covered with sliver, and her pinions shining with flaming gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 9:48; Psalms 51:7; Isaiah 1:18"> (68:15) When the Almighty scattered kings in the midst of her, then even in darkness shone light &#91;as pure&#93; as snow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 87:1–2; Psalms 78:68–69; Deuteronomy 3:10"> (68:16) A mountain of God is the mount of Bashan; many peaks hath the mount of Bashan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5; Psalms 132:13–14; Revelation 21:2–3"> (68:17) Why watch ye enviously, ye many-peaked mountains, yonder mountain which God hath chosen for his residence? yea, the Lord will also dwell &#91;there&#93; for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:2; Daniel 7:10; Revelation 5:11"> (68:18) The chariots of God are two myriads; thousands of angels &#91;follow him&#93;: the Lord is among them: so is Sinai holy &#91;among mountains&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 4:8–10; 2 Corinthians 6:16; Revelation 21:3"> (68:19) Thou didst ascend on high, lead away captives, receive gifts among men, yea, even the rebellious, to dwell among them. O Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 46:4; Lamentations 3:23; Psalms 55:22"> (68:20) Blessed be the Lord; day by day he loadeth us &#91;with benefits&#93;; our God is our salvation. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Revelation 1:18; 1 Samuel 2:6"> (68:21) Our God is to us the God of salvation: and by the Eternal the Lord are the escapes from death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:13; Psalms 7:12; Psalms 110:6"> (68:22) But God will crush the head of his enemies, the hairy skull of him who walketh in his guiltiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 21:33; Ezekiel 36:24; Amos 9:2–3"> (68:23) The Lord hath said, From Bashan will I bring back, I will bring back from the depths of the sea:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 21:19; Psalms 58:10; 1 Kings 22:38"> (68:24) In order that thou mayest wade with thy feet in blood, feeding the tongue of thy dogs from the enemies' blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 63:2; 2 Samuel 6:12–17; 1 Chronicles 15:16–24"> (68:25) Men see thy goings forth, O God! the goings forth of my God, my King, into the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:8; Judges 11:34; Exodus 15:20"> (68:26) First come singers, then follow players on instruments, in the midst of maidens playing on timbrels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 48:1; Deuteronomy 33:28; Psalms 26:12"> (68:27) In assemblies bless ye God, &#91;praise&#93; the Lord, ye sprung from Israel's fountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:21; Judges 20:35; Judges 21:6–25"> (68:28) There Benjamin the youngest leadeth them on, the princes of Judah in purple robes, the princes of Zebulun, the princes of Naphtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 138:8; Psalms 71:3; Psalms 42:8"> (68:29) Thy God hath ordained the rule to thee: strengthen, O God, what thou hast wrought for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 10:10; Psalms 76:11; Isaiah 60:6–11"> (68:30) Because of thy temple over Jerusalem shall kings bring presents unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 89:10; Psalms 22:12–13; Jeremiah 50:11"> (68:31) Rebuke the wild beasts hiding among the reeds, the troops of steers among the calves of nations, that hasten along with presents of silver. He scattereth nations that are eager for the fight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 45:14; Zephaniah 3:10; Psalms 44:20"> (68:32) Nobles will come out of Egypt: Ethiopia will stretch forth eagerly her hands unto God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 67:2–5; Romans 15:10–11; Psalms 117:1–2"> (68:33) Kingdoms of the earth, sing unto God; sing praises unto the Lord: Selah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 104:3; Psalms 18:10; Psalms 68:4"> (68:34) To him who rideth over the highest heavens, of ancient days: hear! he sendeth forth his voice, the voice of might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Revelation 19:6; 2 Peter 1:17; 1 Chronicles 16:28–29"> (68:35) Ascribe ye strength unto God: his excellency is over Israel, and his strength is in the skies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 29:11; Colossians 1:11; Philippians 4:13"> (68:36) Thou art tremendous, O God, from thy holy residences: O God of Israel, &#91;thou art&#93; he that givest strength and power unto &#91;thy&#93; people. Blessed be God.</span>
