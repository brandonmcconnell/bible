<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 3:19; Isaiah 44:22; Isaiah 43:25"> “To the chief musician, a psalm of David, (51:2) When Nathan the prophet came unto him, after he had gone in unto Bath-sheba'.” (51:3) Be gracious unto me, O God, according to thy kindness: according to the greatness of thy mercies blot out my transgressions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 1:7–9; Psalms 51:7; Hebrews 10:21–22"> (51:4) Wash me thoroughly from my iniquity, and cleanse me from my sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; Isaiah 59:12; Psalms 38:18"> (51:5) For of my transgressions I have full knowledge; and my sin is before me continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:13–14; Luke 15:21; Genesis 20:6"> (51:6) To thee, thee only, have I sinned, and what is evil in thy eyes have I done:—in order that thou mightest be righteous when thou speakest, be justified when thou judgest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 5:12; Job 14:4; Psalms 58:3"> (51:7) Behold, in iniquity was I brought forth; and in sin did my mother conceive me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 15:2; 1 Samuel 16:7; 1 Peter 3:4"> (51:8) Behold, thou desirest truth in the inward parts: therefore do thou cause me to know wisdom in the recesses &#91;of the heart&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 1:7; Isaiah 1:18; Hebrews 9:13–14"> (51:9) Cleanse me from sin with hyssop, and I shall be clean: wash me, and I shall be whiter than snow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 5:17–18; Hosea 6:1–2; Matthew 5:4"> (51:10) Cause me to hear gladness and joy; that the bones which thou hast crushed may rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:17; Micah 7:18–19; Psalms 51:1"> (51:11) Hide thy face from my sins, and all my iniquities do thou blot out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19; 2 Corinthians 5:17; Ephesians 4:22–24"> (51:12) Create unto me a clean heart, O God; and a firm spirit renew thou within me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 4:30; 2 Kings 13:23; Isaiah 63:10–11"> (51:13) Cast me not away from thy presence; and thy holy spirit do not take from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Psalms 13:5; Isaiah 61:10"> (51:14) Restore unto me the gladness of thy salvation; and with a liberal spirit do thou support me.—</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 22:32; Acts 26:18–20; Acts 3:19"> (51:15) I will teach transgressors thy ways, and sinners shall return unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 35:28; 2 Samuel 12:9; Psalms 26:9"> (51:16) Deliver me from blood-guiltiness, O God, thou God of my salvation; &#91;that&#93; my tongue may sing aloud of thy righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 63:3–5; Hebrews 13:15; Psalms 9:14"> (51:17) O Lord, open thou my lips, and my mouth shall declare thy praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 40:6; Hosea 6:6; Hebrews 10:5–6"> (51:18) For thou desirest not sacrifice; else would I give it: in burnt-offering hast thou no delight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 34:18; Isaiah 66:2; Psalms 147:3"> (51:19) The sacrifices of God are a broken spirit: a broken and a contrite heart, O God, wilt thou not despise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 102:16; Psalms 122:6–9; Luke 12:32"> (51:20) Do good in thy favor unto Zion; build thou the walls of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 4:5; Ephesians 5:2; Malachi 3:3"> (51:21) Then wilt thou be pleased with the sacrifices of righteousness, with burnt-offering and entire offering: then shall bullocks be offered upon thy altar.</span>
