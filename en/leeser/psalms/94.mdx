<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 20:5; Revelation 6:10; Jeremiah 12:1–2"> How long shall the wicked, O Lord—how long shall the wicked exult?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 31:18; Psalms 52:1; Revelation 13:5–6"> They sputter, they speak hard things: all the workers of wickedness boast themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 3:15; Psalms 79:2–3; Jeremiah 51:20–23"> Thy people, O Lord! they crush, and thy heritage they afflict.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 10:2; Malachi 3:5; Jeremiah 7:6"> The widow and the stranger they slay, and the fatherless they murder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 10:11–13; Luke 18:3–4; Zephaniah 1:12"> And they say, The Lord will not see, and the God of Jacob will not take notice of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 92:6; Titus 3:3; Psalms 49:10"> Understand, ye brutish among the people: and ye fools, when will ye become intelligent!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 4:11; Jeremiah 23:23–24; Psalms 11:4"> He that hath planted the ear, shall he not hear? or he that hath formed the eye, shall he not see?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 35:11; Isaiah 28:26; Psalms 44:2"> He that admonisheth nations, shall he not correct? is it not he that teacheth man knowledge!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:20; 1 Corinthians 1:19; 1 Corinthians 1:21"> The Lord knoweth the thoughts of man, that they are nought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 3:11–12; Job 5:17; Hebrews 12:5–11"> Happy is the man whom thou admonisheth, O Lord, and teachest him out of thy law:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 55:23; 2 Peter 2:9; Psalms 9:15"> That thou mayest grant him repose from the days of evil, until the pot be dug for the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:22; Psalms 37:28; Romans 11:1–2"> For the Lord will not cast off his people, and his inheritance will he not forsake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Micah 7:9; Psalms 97:2; Psalms 37:34"> For unto righteousness will justice return; and it shall be followed by all the upright in heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 5:23; Numbers 25:6–13; John 7:50–51"> Who will rise up for me against evil-doers? or who will stand forward for me against the workers of wickedness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 124:1–2; Psalms 31:17; Psalms 118:13"> Unless the Lord had been a help unto me, but a little would have been wanting that my soul had dwelt in the silence of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 121:3; Isaiah 41:10; Psalms 119:116–117"> When I said, My foot hath slipped: thy kindness, O Lord, sustained me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 61:2; 2 Corinthians 1:4–5; Psalms 77:2–10"> In the multitude of my &#91;painful&#93; thoughts within me, thy consolations delight my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 58:2; Isaiah 10:1; Ecclesiastes 3:16"> Can there be associated with thee the throne of destructive wickedness, which frameth mischief as a law?</span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 18:2; Psalms 27:1–3; Psalms 59:16–17"> But the Lord is become my defence, and my God, the rock of my refuge.</span>
