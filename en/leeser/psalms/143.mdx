<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 71:2; Psalms 31:1; Psalms 140:6"> “A psalm of David.” O Lord, hear my prayer, give ear to my supplications: in thy faithfulness answer me, in thy righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; Romans 3:20; Job 25:4"> And enter not into judgment with thy servant; for no living man can be regarded righteous before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 3:6; Psalms 7:1–2; Psalms 7:5"> For the enemy hath pursued my soul; he hath crushed to the ground my life; he hath made me dwell in darkness, as those that are dead eternally.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 142:3; Psalms 77:3; Psalms 102:3–4"> And my spirit within me is overwhelmed: in my bosom is my heart astounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 77:5–6; Psalms 77:10–12; Micah 6:5"> I remember the days of olden times: I meditate on all thy doings: on the work of thy hands do I reflect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 63:1; Job 11:13; Psalms 84:2"> I spread forth my hands unto thee: my soul &#91;longeth&#93; for thee, as a thirsty land. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 69:17; Psalms 28:1; Psalms 27:9"> Hasten, answer me, O Lord, my spirit falleth: hide not thy face from me, that I may not become like those that go down into the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 25:4–5; Proverbs 3:5–6; Isaiah 48:17"> Cause me to hear in the morning thy kindness; for in thee do I trust: cause me to know the way whereon I should walk; for unto thee do I lift up my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 18:10; Psalms 61:3–4; Psalms 59:1"> Deliver me, O Lord, from my enemies: by thee do I seek shelter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 25:4–5; John 14:26; Hebrews 13:21"> Teach me to do thy will; for thou art my God: thy spirit is good; guide me on a level land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 31:1; Ephesians 2:4–5; Psalms 71:2"> For thy sake, O Lord, revive me: in thy righteousness bring forth out of distress my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 54:5; Psalms 116:16; Psalms 119:94"> And in thy kindness destroy my enemies, and annihilate all the adversaries of my soul; far I am thy servant.</span>
