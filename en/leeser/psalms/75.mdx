<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 145:18; Psalms 138:2; Jeremiah 10:6"> “To the chief musician, Al-tashcheth, a psalm or song of Asaph.” (75:2) We give thanks unto thee, O God, we give thanks, and nigh is thy name: men relate thy wondrous deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:17; Acts 17:31; Acts 1:7"> (75:3) “For I will take up a fixed time when I will judge uprightly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:8; Isaiah 24:19; Hebrews 1:3"> (75:4) The earth and all her inhabitants are melting away: I myself establish firmly her pillars.” Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 1:21; Proverbs 1:22; Proverbs 8:5"> (75:5) I said unto the arrogant, Deal not arrogantly: and to the wicked, Lift not up the horn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Ezekiel 2:4; Isaiah 48:4"> (75:6) Lift not up on high your horn, speak not with a stiffly erect neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 3:3"> (75:7) For neither from the rising &#91;of the sun&#93;, nor from &#91;his&#93; setting, nor from the wilderness of mountains &#91;cometh this&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 1:52; Daniel 2:21–22; Psalms 147:6"> (75:8) But God is the judge: he lowereth one, and lifteth up another,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 21:20; Jeremiah 25:15; Psalms 11:6"> (75:9) For there is a cup in the hand of the Lord, and the wine foameth, it is full of mixture: and he poureth out of the same; but its lees they drain, they drink—all the wicked of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 9:14; Psalms 145:1–2; Psalms 40:10"> (75:10) But I will declare for ever, I will sing praises to the God of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 92:10; Psalms 89:17; Jeremiah 48:25"> (75:11) And all the horns of the wicked will I hew off; but the horns of the righteous shall be exalted.</span>
