<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 6:1; Isaiah 54:8; Hebrews 12:5–11"> “A psalm of David, to bring to remembrance.” (38:2) O Lord, correct me not in thy wrath, nor chastise me in thy fury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 6:4; Psalms 32:4; Psalms 64:7"> (38:3) For thy arrows have penetrated into me, and thy hand presseth down upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 6:2; Psalms 51:8; Psalms 31:9–10"> (38:4) There is no soundness in my flesh because of thy indignation: there is no peace in my bones because of my sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 40:12; Ezra 9:6; Matthew 11:28"> (38:5) For my iniquities are passed over my head, as a heavy burden are they too heavy for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 69:5; Jeremiah 8:22; Isaiah 1:5–6"> (38:6) Foul, corrupt are my bruises because of my folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 30:28; Psalms 35:14; Psalms 42:9"> (38:7) I am bent double; I am bowed down to the utmost; all the day long I go about full of grief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:18–19; Acts 12:23; Psalms 102:3"> (38:8) For my loins are filled with a burning disease, and there is no soundness in my flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 3:24; Psalms 32:3; Job 30:28"> (38:9) I am made faint and crushed to the utmost; I cry aloud because of the groaning of my heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 10:17; Psalms 6:6; 2 Corinthians 5:2"> (38:10) Lord, before thee is all my longing; and my sighing is from thee not hidden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 6:7; Psalms 88:9; Psalms 69:3"> (38:11) My heart is restless, my strength hath left me; and the light of my eyes—that also is no more with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 31:11; Job 19:13–17; Luke 23:49"> (38:12) My lovers and my friends stand aloof from my plague; and my kinsmen stand afar off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 35:20; Psalms 140:5; 2 Samuel 17:1–3"> (38:13) They also that seek after my life lay snares; and they that wish for my mishap speak wicked falsehoods; and deceits do they devise all the day long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 39:2; Isaiah 53:7; Psalms 39:9"> (38:14) But I, as a deaf man, hear not; and I am as a dumb man that cannot open his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 5:13; John 8:6; Mark 15:3–5"> (38:15) Thus am I as a man that heareth not, and in whose mouth are no words of defence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 39:7; Psalms 17:6; Psalms 138:3"> (38:16) For in thee, O Lord, do I hope: thou wilt answer, O Lord my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 94:18; Deuteronomy 32:35; Psalms 35:24–26"> (38:17) For I said, Perhaps they might rejoice over me: when my foot slippeth, they might magnify themselves over me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 38:6; Psalms 6:6; Psalms 35:15"> (38:18) For I am prepared for &#91;my&#93; downfall, and my pain is continually before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 32:5; Proverbs 28:13; Job 33:27"> (38:19) For I will tell of my iniquity; I will be grieved because of my sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; John 15:18–25; Psalms 69:4"> (38:20) But my enemies are strong in life; and numerous are those that hate me wrongfully;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 35:12; 1 John 3:12; Jeremiah 18:20"> (38:21) They also that repay &#91;me&#93; evil in lieu of good; they hate me bitterly because I pursue what is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 22:19; Psalms 22:24; Psalms 22:11"> (38:22) Forsake me not, O Lord: O my God, be not far from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 27:1; Psalms 40:13; Isaiah 12:2"> (38:23) Make haste to help me, O Lord, my salvation.</span>
