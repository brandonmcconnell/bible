<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:1–51; Colossians 1:11; 1 John 4:19"> “To the chief musician, by the servant of the Lord, by David, who spoke unto the Lord the words of this song on the day that the Lord had delivered him out of the power of all his enemies, and from the hand of Saul; (18:2) And he said,” I ever love thee, O Lord, my strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 144:2; Psalms 62:7; Psalms 19:14"> (18:3) The Lord is my rock, and my fortress, and my deliverer; my God, my rock, in whom I trust; my shield, and the horn of my salvation, and my high tower.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 91:15; Psalms 145:3; Psalms 62:8"> (18:4) Praised, I cried, be the Lord, and from my enemies was I saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 116:3; Matthew 27:24–25; Mark 14:33–34"> (18:5) The bonds of death encompassed me, and the floods of destruction made me afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 86:13; Psalms 88:3–8; Acts 2:24"> (18:6) The bonds of hell encircled me: the snares of death seized on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; 2 Samuel 22:7; Psalms 34:15"> (18:7) &#91;When&#93; in my distress I called upon the Lord, and unto my God I cried: he heard from his temple my voice, and my complaint came before him, even into his ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 4:31; Psalms 68:7–8; Acts 16:25–26"> (18:8) Then shook and trembled the earth; and the foundations of the mountains were moved; and they were shaken, because he was wroth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 21:9; Psalms 11:6; Nahum 1:5–6"> (18:9) Smoke went up in his anger and consuming fire out of his mouth: coals flamed forth from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 144:5–15; Deuteronomy 33:26; 2 Samuel 22:10"> (18:10) And he bent the heavens, and came down: and thick darkness was under his feet:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 104:3; 2 Samuel 22:11–12; Psalms 99:1"> (18:11) And he rode upon a cherub, and flew along, and he flitted by upon the wings of the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; Deuteronomy 4:11; Psalms 91:1"> (18:12) He made darkness his hiding-place, round about him as his pavilion, dark waters, thick clouds of the skies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 10:11; Revelation 16:21; Matthew 17:2"> (18:13) From the brightness before him his thick clouds passed away, &#91;with&#93; hail-stones and coals of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 104:7; 1 Samuel 7:10; Psalms 140:10"> (18:14) And the Lord thundered in the heavens, and the Most High uttered forth his voice, &#91;with&#93; hail-stones and coals of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 144:6; Habakkuk 3:11; Numbers 24:8"> (18:15) And he sent out his arrows, and scattered them; and he shot forth lightnings, and discomfited them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 106:9; Exodus 15:8; Micah 6:2"> (18:16) And then were seen the channels of the waters, and there were laid open the foundations of the world, through thy rebuke, O Lord, through the blast of the breath of thy nostrils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:17; Psalms 144:7; Psalms 57:3"> (18:17) He stretched out from above &#91;his hand&#93;, he took me; he drew me out from mighty waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 2:14–15; Psalms 35:10; Psalms 142:6"> (18:18) He delivered me from my enemy, the strong, and from those that hated me, when they were too mighty for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Obadiah 1:10–14; Deuteronomy 32:35; Psalms 46:11"> (18:19) They overcame me on the day of my calamity; but the Lord became my stay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 118:5; Psalms 31:8; Psalms 18:36"> (18:20) And he brought me forth into a large space; he delivered me, because he had delight in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 24:4; Matthew 6:4; Psalms 58:11"> (18:21) The Lord rewarded me according to my righteousness; according to the purity of my hands did he recompense me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 119:102; Psalms 17:4; Psalms 26:1"> (18:22) For I have kept the ways of the Lord, and have not wickedly departed from my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 119:13; Psalms 119:112; Psalms 119:30"> (18:23) For all his ordinances were before me, and his statutes had I not put away from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 18:8–9; Psalms 11:7; 1 Chronicles 29:17"> (18:24) I was also upright with him, and I guarded myself against my iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:41–42; Ruth 2:12; 1 Samuel 26:23"> (18:25) Therefore did the Lord recompense me according to my righteousness, according to the purity of my hands before his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; Psalms 62:12; Matthew 18:33–35"> (18:26) With the kind thou wilt show thyself kind; with the upright man thou wilt show thyself upright;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 3:34; Leviticus 26:23–24; James 2:13"> (18:27) With the pure thou wilt show thyself pure; and with the perverse thou wilt wage a contest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 66:2; James 2:5; Isaiah 57:15"> (18:28) For thou wilt indeed save the afflicted people; but haughty eyes wilt thou bring down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:29; Job 29:3; Isaiah 42:16"> (18:29) For thou wilt cause my light to shine: the Lord my God will enlighten my darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:30; Ephesians 6:10–13; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> (18:30) For &#91;aided&#93; by thee I run through a troop; and &#91;helped&#93; by my God I leap over a wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Proverbs 30:5; 2 Samuel 22:31"> (18:31) As for God,—his way is perfect; the word of the Lord is tried: he is a shield to all those that trust in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:31; Psalms 86:8; 2 Samuel 22:32"> (18:32) For who is God save the Lord? or who is a rock beside our God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 45:5; Psalms 28:7; 2 Samuel 22:33"> (18:33) He is the God that girdeth me with strength, and rendereth my way unobstructed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:19; Deuteronomy 32:13; Deuteronomy 33:29"> (18:34) He maketh my feet like those of the hinds, and upon my high-places he causeth me to stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 144:1; Psalms 46:9; 2 Samuel 22:36"> (18:35) He teacheth my hands for the war, so that a brazen bow is bent by my arms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:36; James 3:17–18; Psalms 28:7"> (18:36) And thou gavest me the shield of thy salvation, and thy right hand supported me: and thy meekness hath made me great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 4:12; 2 Samuel 22:37; Job 36:16"> (18:37) Thou enlargest my steps under me, so that my joints do not slip.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 53:10–12; Psalms 44:5; Psalms 35:2"> (18:38) I pursue my enemies, and overtake them; and I return not again till I have made an end of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:1–25; 2 Samuel 22:39; 1 Samuel 23:5"> (18:39) I crush them that they are not able to rise: they fall under my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:40–41; Psalms 18:32; 1 Corinthians 15:25–28"> (18:40) For thou hast girded me with strength for the war: thou subduest my opponents under me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Psalms 21:12; Psalms 94:23"> (18:41) And my enemies thou causest to turn their back to me; and those that hate me,—that I may destroy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Proverbs 1:28; Job 27:9; Job 35:12–13"> (18:42) They cry, but there is none to help; unto the Lord,—but he answereth them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Isaiah 10:6; Zechariah 10:5; 2 Kings 13:7"> (18:43) And I beat them small as the dust before the wind: like the dirt in the streets do I cast them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Isaiah 55:5; 2 Samuel 3:1; Isaiah 52:15"> (18:44) Thou deliverest me from the contests of the people; thou appointest me to be the head of nations: a people that I know not shall serve me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 66:3; Psalms 81:15; Deuteronomy 33:29"> (18:45) As soon as their ear heareth they shall be obedient to me: the children of the stranger shall utter flattery unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Micah 7:17; James 1:11; Isaiah 24:4"> (18:46) The children of the stranger shall fade away, and come forth trembling out of their close places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:47; Psalms 57:5; Psalms 18:2"> (18:47) The Lord liveth, and blessed be my Rock; and exalted be the God of my salvation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 47:3; Romans 12:19; Nahum 1:2"> (18:48) The God that granteth me vengeance, and subdueth nations under me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 140:1; Psalms 140:4; Psalms 86:14"> (18:49) That delivereth me from my enemies: also above my opponents thou liftest me up; thou deliverest me from the man of violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Romans 15:9; 2 Samuel 22:50–51; Psalms 72:18–19"> (18:50) Therefore will I give thanks unto thee among the nations, O Lord, and unto thy name will I sing praises,—</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Psalms 144:10; Psalms 132:10; 2 Samuel 7:13"> (18:51) &#91;To him&#93; that maketh great the salvation of his king, and who sheweth kindness to his anointed, to David, and to his seed for ever.</span>
