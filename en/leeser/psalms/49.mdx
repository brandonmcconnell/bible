<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 78:1; Psalms 33:8; Matthew 11:15"> “To the chief musician, by the sons of Korach, a psalm.” (49:2) Hear this, all ye people; give ear, all ye inhabitants of the perishable world:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 62:9; Proverbs 22:2; Jeremiah 5:4–5"> (49:3) Both the sons of the low and the sons of the high, rich and needy, altogether.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 19:14; Proverbs 22:17; Matthew 12:35"> (49:4) My mouth shall speak wisdom; and the meditation of my heart shall be of understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 78:2; Matthew 13:35; Numbers 12:8"> (49:5) I will incline my ear to a parable: I will open with the harp my riddle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 27:24; Romans 8:33–34; Amos 5:13"> (49:6) Wherefore should I fear in the days of evil, when the iniquity of my oppressors encompasseth me?—</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:17; Psalms 52:7; Jeremiah 9:23"> (49:7) Of those that trust in their wealth, and boast themselves of the multitude of their riches?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; 1 Timothy 2:6; 1 Peter 1:18"> (49:8) No one can in any wise redeem his brother, nor can he give to God redemption money for himself;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; Job 36:18–19"> (49:9) For the ransom of their soul is too costly, and it is omitted for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:48; Psalms 16:10; Ecclesiastes 8:8"> (49:10) And should he still live for ever? not see the pit?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 12:20; Psalms 39:6; Hebrews 9:27"> (49:11) For he must see that wise men die, that together the fool and the brutish person perish and leave to others their wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 4:17; Psalms 5:9; Psalms 64:6"> (49:12) Their inward thought is, that their houses are to be for ever, their dwelling-places, from generation to generation; they call them by their own names in &#91;various&#93; countries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:18–21; Psalms 49:20; Psalms 82:7"> (49:13) Nevertheless man in &#91;his&#93; splendor endureth not: he is like the beasts &#91;that&#93; perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 12:20; 1 Corinthians 3:19; Luke 16:27–28"> (49:14) This is their way, their folly: yet their posterity will take pleasure in their sayings. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 4:3; 1 Corinthians 6:2; Daniel 7:22"> (49:15) Like flocks are they thrust into the nether world; death will feed them; but the upright shall have dominion over them in that morning, and their form wasteth away in the nether world, &#91;taken away&#93; from their own dwelling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 73:24; Psalms 56:13; Psalms 86:13"> (49:16) But God will redeem my soul from the power of the nether world; for he will take me away. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Psalms 37:1; Revelation 21:26"> (49:17) Be not thou afraid when a man becometh rich, when the glory of his house is increased;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:7; Job 27:19; Luke 16:24"> (49:18) For when he dieth he can take nothing away; his glory will not descend after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 12:19; Deuteronomy 29:19; Psalms 10:3"> (49:19) For though he bless his soul during his life, and men praise thee, when thou doest well to thyself:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 33:30; Genesis 15:15; Psalms 56:13"> (49:20) She shall go to the generation of his fathers, unto eternity will these never see light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 49:12; Ecclesiastes 3:18–19; Job 4:21"> (49:21) Man, though in splendor, who understandeth not, is like the beasts that perish. °</span>
