<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 18:21; Psalms 57:4; Jeremiah 9:3–4"> (52:4) Thy tongue deviseth mischiefs, like a sharpened razor, thou worker of deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:3–5; 2 Timothy 3:4; John 8:44"> (52:5) Thou lovest evil more than good; falsehood more than speaking righteousness. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 120:3; James 3:6–9; 1 Samuel 22:18–19"> (52:6) Thou lovest all words of destruction, the tongue of deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 2:22; Psalms 27:13; Revelation 21:8"> (52:7) &#91;Therefore&#93; God will also destroy thee for ever: he will take thee away, and pluck thee out of his tent, and root thee out of the land of life. Selah.—</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5; Psalms 73:18–20; Ecclesiastes 8:8"> (52:9) “Lo, this is the man that made not God his fortress; but trusted in the abundance of his riches, relied proudly on his mischievous wickedness.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 92:12–14; Psalms 1:3; Hosea 14:6–8"> (52:10) But I am like a green olive-tree in the house of God: I trust in the kindness of God for ever and ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 130:5–6; Lamentations 3:25–26; Psalms 54:6"> (52:11) I will thank thee for ever, because thou hast done it: and I will wait on thy name, for &#91;it is&#93; good, before thy pious ones.</span>
