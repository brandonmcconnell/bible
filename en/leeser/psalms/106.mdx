<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:18; Psalms 105:1; 1 Chronicles 16:34"> Hallelujah. Oh give thanks unto the Lord; for he is good: for to eternity endureth his kindness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Ephesians 1:19; Psalms 145:3–12"> Who can utter the mighty acts of the Lord? who can publish all his praise?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 15:2; James 1:25; John 15:14"> Happy are those that observe justice, that execute righteousness at all times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 119:132; Luke 23:42; Nehemiah 13:14"> Remember me, O Lord, when thou favorest thy people: oh visit me with thy salvation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:16; Psalms 105:43; Psalms 1:3"> That I may look on the happiness of thy elect, that I may rejoice in the joy of thy nation, that I may glorify myself with thy inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:16; Psalms 78:8; 1 Kings 8:47"> We have sinned together with our fathers, we have committed iniquity, we have done wickedly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 14:11–12; Psalms 78:42; Psalms 78:11"> Our fathers did not reflect on thy wonders in Egypt: they remembered not the multitude of thy kindnesses; but rebelled at the sea, even at the Red Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 9:16; Ezekiel 20:9; Ezekiel 20:14"> Nevertheless he saved them for the sake of his name, to make known his might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 18:15; Isaiah 51:10; Isaiah 63:11–14"> He rebuked the Red Sea also, and it was dried up; and he led them through the depths, as through the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 14:30; Psalms 107:2; Micah 6:4"> And he saved them from the hand of him that hated them, and redeemed them from the hand of the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 15:5; Psalms 78:53; Exodus 14:13"> And the waters covered their adversaries: not one of them was left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 14:31–21; Luke 8:13; John 8:30–31"> Then believed they in his words, they sang his praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 78:11; Exodus 15:24; Isaiah 48:17–18"> Speedily they forgot his works, they waited not for his counsel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:9; Numbers 11:4; 1 Corinthians 10:6"> And they felt a lustful longing in the wilderness, and tempted God in the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 11:31–34; Isaiah 10:16; Psalms 78:29–31"> And he gave them what they had asked; but sent dryness into their soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 16:1–50; Leviticus 21:10–12; Leviticus 21:6–8"> Moreover they envied Moses in the camp, and Aaron the holy one of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:6; Numbers 16:29–33; Numbers 26:10"> The earth opened and swallowed up Dathan, and covered over the company of Abiram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Numbers 16:35–40; Numbers 16:46"> And a fire was kindled in their company: the flame burnt up the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 32:4–8; Exodus 32:35; Nehemiah 9:18"> They made a calf in Horeb, and bowed themselves down to a molten image.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:11; Exodus 20:4–5; Romans 1:22–23"> And they exchanged their glory for the similitude of an ox that eateth herbs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:32; Psalms 78:42–51; Psalms 106:13"> They forgot God their saviour, who had done great things in Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 105:23; Psalms 78:51; Exodus 14:25–28"> Wonders in the land of Ham, terrible things by the Red Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:13–14; Deuteronomy 9:25; Ezekiel 22:30"> He therefore spoke of destroying them: had not Moses his elect stood in the breach before him, to turn away his fury, that he might not destroy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:32; Ezekiel 20:6; Hebrews 3:18–19"> And they despised the pleasant land, they believed not in his word;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 14:22; Deuteronomy 1:26–27; Hebrews 3:15"> But they murmured in their tents, they hearkened not unto the voice of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:15; Numbers 14:28–35; Hebrews 3:11"> He therefore lifted up his hand against them, to cause them to fall in the wilderness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 44:11; Leviticus 26:33; Deuteronomy 28:64–65"> And to let their seed fall among the nations, and to scatter them in the lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hosea 9:10; Numbers 25:1–3; Numbers 25:5"> And they joined themselves unto Ba'al-pe'or, and ate the sacrifices of the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:16–21; 1 Corinthians 10:8; Romans 1:21–24"> And they provoked him to anger with their deeds: and there broke in among them the plague.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 25:6–8; Joshua 7:12; Numbers 25:14–15"> Then stood up Phinehas, and executed judgment: and the plague was stayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 25:11–13; Mark 14:3–9; Deuteronomy 24:13"> And it was accounted unto him for righteousness, unto all generations for evermore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 20:2–13; Numbers 20:23–24; Deuteronomy 4:21"> They angered him also at the waters of Meribah, and evil happened to Moses for their sake;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 20:10–11; Psalms 107:11; James 3:2"> Because they had embittered his spirit, and so he spoke thoughtlessly with his lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Deuteronomy 7:16; Judges 1:21"> They did not exterminate the nations, that the Lord had indicated to them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Judges 3:5–6; 1 Corinthians 15:33; 1 Corinthians 5:6"> But they mingled themselves among the nations, and learned their doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:16; Judges 2:3; Exodus 23:33"> And they served their idols, and these became unto them a snare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Kings 17:17; 2 Kings 16:3; Deuteronomy 32:17"> Yea, they sacrificed their sons and their daughters unto the evil spirits;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 35:33; Deuteronomy 21:9; Isaiah 26:21"> And they shed innocent blood, the blood of their sons and of their daughters, whom they sacrificed unto the idols of Canaan: and the land was polluted with blood-guiltiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:18; Numbers 15:39; Leviticus 17:7"> Thus were they made unclean through their own doings, and went astray with their own deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Deuteronomy 9:29; Judges 2:20"> therefore was the wrath of the Lord kindled against his people, and be felt disgust for his own inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Nehemiah 9:27–38; Judges 3:8"> And he gave them up into the hand of the nations: and there ruled over them those that hated them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Judges 4:3; Judges 10:12"> And their enemies also oppressed them: and they were subdued under their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalms 81:12; Judges 2:16–18; Psalms 1:1"> Many times did he deliver them; but they rebelled with their counsel, and they were brought low through their iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Judges 3:9; Judges 4:3; 1 Samuel 7:8–12"> Nevertheless he looked on when they were in distress, when he heard their entreaty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Psalms 105:8; Lamentations 3:32; Judges 2:18"> And he remembered unto them his covenant, and he bethought himself according to the abundance of his kindnesses;</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ezra 9:9; Jeremiah 42:12; Jeremiah 15:11"> And be caused them to find mercy before all those that had carried them away captive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:35–36; Revelation 7:10–12; 2 Corinthians 2:14"> Save us, O Lord our God, and gather us from among the nations, to give thanks unto thy holy name, to triumph in thy praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 41:13; Psalms 89:52; Psalms 105:45–1"> Blessed be the Lord the God of Israel from everlasting even to everlasting: and let all the people say, Amen, Hallelujah.</span>
