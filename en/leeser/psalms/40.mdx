<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 27:13–14; Psalms 37:7; James 5:7–11"> “To the chief musician, a psalm of David.” (40:2) I had waited patiently for the Lord, when he inclined himself unto me, and heard my cry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 37:23; Psalms 119:133; Psalms 27:5"> (40:3) And he brought me up out of the noiseful deep, out of the miry clay, and he set up my feet upon a rock, making firm my steps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 33:3; Psalms 103:1–5; Psalms 34:1–6"> (40:4) And he placed in my mouth a new song, a praise unto our God: many will see it, and fear; and they will trust in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:7–8; Psalms 84:11–12; Psalms 34:8"> (40:5) Happy is the man that maketh the Lord his trust, and turneth not unto the proud, nor such as stray aside unto lies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:11; Psalms 139:17–18; Exodus 15:11"> (40:6) Many things hast thou done, O Lord my God; thy wonderful deeds and thy thoughts toward us—there is none to be compared unto thee—will I tell and speak of, &#91;though&#93; they are too numerous to be counted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 10:5–12; Isaiah 1:11; 1 Samuel 15:22"> (40:7) Sacrifice and meat-offering thou desirest not—ears hast thou hollowed out unto me—burnt-offering and sin-offering thou demandest not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 10:7–9; Luke 24:44; John 5:39"> (40:8) Then said I, Lo, I come: in the roll of the book it is written down for me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 7:22; John 4:34; Jeremiah 15:16"> (40:9) To fulfill thy will, O my God, do I desire; and thy law is within my heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 22:25; Mark 16:15–16; Psalms 22:22"> (40:10) I announce &#91;thy&#93; righteousness in the great assembly: lo, I will not refrain my lips, O Lord, thou well knowest it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 1:16–17; Acts 20:20–21; Romans 10:9–10"> (40:11) Thy righteousness have I never hidden within my heart; thy faithfulness and thy salvation have I spoken of openly: I have not concealed thy kindness and thy truth before the great assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 23:6; Psalms 43:3; Psalms 57:3"> (40:12) Do thou, O Lord, not withhold thy mercies from me: let thy kindness and thy truth continually watch over me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 38:4; Psalms 73:26; Psalms 69:4"> (40:13) For evils without number have compassed me about; my iniquities have overtaken me, so that I am not able to see: they are more numerous than the hairs of my head; and my courage hath forsaken me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 25:17–18; Psalms 70:1–5; Psalms 38:22"> (40:14) Be pleased, O Lord, to deliver me; O Lord, hasten to my help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 35:26; Psalms 35:4; Psalms 71:13"> (40:15) May those be made ashamed and put to the blush together that seek after my soul to take her away: let them be driven backward and be confounded, that wish for my mishap.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 35:21; Psalms 69:24–25; Psalms 35:25"> (40:16) May they be astonished in consequence of their shame that say unto me, Aha, aha!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 35:27; Psalms 68:3; Acts 19:17"> (40:17) &#91;But&#93; may all those that seek thee be glad and rejoice in thee: may they say continually, The Lord be magnified,—those that love thy salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 70:5; Hebrews 13:6; Isaiah 41:17"> (40:18) But though I be poor and needy, the Lord will think of me: my help and my deliverer art thou: O my God, delay not.</span>
