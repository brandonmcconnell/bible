<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 90:1; Psalms 91:1; Matthew 8:20"> (84:4) Even as the sparrow hath found a house, and the swallow a nest for herself, where she may lay her young:—&#91;have I found&#93; thy altars, O Lord of hosts, my King, and my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 23:6; Psalms 65:4; Psalms 27:4"> (84:5) Happy are they who dwell in thy house: they will be continually praising thee. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 28:7–8; 2 Corinthians 12:9; Zechariah 10:12"> (84:6) Happy is the man whose strong confidence is in thee, &#91;all&#93; whose heart reflecteth on the paths &#91;of righteousness&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:17; Romans 5:3–5; John 16:33"> (84:7) Passing through the valley of weeping, they will change it into a spring: also the early rain covereth it with blessings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 4:18; Isaiah 40:31; 2 Corinthians 3:18"> (84:8) They go from strength to strength, each of them appeareth before God in Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 59:5"> (84:9) O Lord God of hosts, hear my prayer: give ear, O God of Jacob. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 2:2; Genesis 15:1; Psalms 84:11"> (84:10) &#91;Thou,&#93; our shield, behold, O God, and look upon the face of thy anointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 27:4; Psalms 26:8–10; Psalms 63:2"> (84:11) For better is a day in thy courts than a thousand &#91;elsewhere&#93;: I would rather choose to wait at the threshold of the house of my God, than to dwell in the tents of wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 34:9–10; Proverbs 2:7; Matthew 6:33"> (84:12) For a sun and shield is the Lord God; grace and glory will the Lord give; he will not withhold any good from those that walk with integrity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 62:8; Jeremiah 17:7–8; Isaiah 30:18"> (84:13) O Lord of hosts, happy is the man that trusteth in thee.</span>
