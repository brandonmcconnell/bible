<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 5:8; Psalms 51:10; Luke 12:32"> BOOK THIRD: “A Psalm of Assaph.” Truly God is good to Israel, to such as are pure of heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 94:18; 1 Samuel 2:9; Psalms 116:8"> But as for me, it lacked but little that my feet had been moved: almost nothing was needed that my steps had slipped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 21:7; Psalms 37:7; Proverbs 23:17"> For I was envious at the arrogant, when I saw the prosperity of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 21:23–24; Ecclesiastes 2:16; Luke 16:22"> For there are no deadly fetters for them, but their strength is firm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 21:9; Psalms 73:12; Revelation 3:19"> They share not in the trouble of mortals, and with men are they not afflicted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 109:18; 1 Peter 5:5; Daniel 4:30"> Therefore is pride their neck-chain: violence envelopeth them as a garment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:28; Psalms 17:10; Job 15:27"> Their eyes start out from fatness: they have exceeded their heart's imaginings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jude 1:16; Psalms 17:10; Psalms 10:2"> They scorn, and speak wickedly of oppression: loftily do they speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 13:6; James 3:6; Exodus 5:2"> They set their mouth in the heavens, and their tongue walketh busily on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 75:8"> Therefore do his people turn away hither: and waters of a full cup are drained by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 7:2; Zephaniah 1:12; Job 22:13–14"> And they say, How should God know? and is there knowledge in the Most High?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 52:7; Psalms 49:6; Hosea 12:7–8"> Behold, these are the wicked; and yet prospering continually they increase in wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 21:15; Psalms 26:6; Job 34:9"> Verily in vain have I thus cleansed my heart, and have washed in innocency my hands:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 12:5; Psalms 94:12; Job 7:3–4"> While I was afflicted all the day, and my chastisement &#91;came&#93; every morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:6–7; Malachi 2:8; 1 Peter 2:9"> But if I were to say, I will speak thus: behold, I would be treacherous against the generation of thy children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:17; John 16:18–19; Psalms 36:6"> And when I should think to know this, it would be trouble in my eyes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 12:20; Psalms 77:13; Ecclesiastes 8:12–13"> Until I enter into the sanctuary of God; and understand what their future will be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 35:6; Jeremiah 23:12; Deuteronomy 32:35"> Surely thou placest them on slippery spots: thou lettest them fall down into destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 28:1; Isaiah 47:11; Daniel 5:6"> How are they brought into desolation, as in a moment! they perish, they come to their end with terrific events.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 20:8; Psalms 78:65; Psalms 90:5"> As a dream after awaking, O Lord, reject thou in wrath their image.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Psalms 73:3; Job 16:13"> For it fermented in my heart, and in my reins I felt sharp thrusts;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:18; Psalms 92:6; Psalms 49:10"> But I was indeed foolish, and I knew it not: I was as a &#91;thoughtless&#93; beast with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 41:13; Psalms 63:8; Matthew 28:20"> Nevertheless I am continually with thee: thou hast seized hold of me by my right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 32:8; Isaiah 58:11; Proverbs 3:5–6"> With thy counsel wilt thou guide me, and afterward take me on to glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Philippians 3:8; Psalms 16:2; Psalms 63:3"> Whom have I in heaven? and beside thee I desire nothing upon earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Isaiah 40:29–31; Psalms 138:3"> Though my flesh and my heart should fall; yet the rock of my heart, and my portion will be God for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 4:4; Psalms 119:155; Exodus 34:15"> For, lo, those that are far from thee shall perish: thou destroyest every one that strayeth away from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="James 4:8; Psalms 40:5; Hebrews 10:19–22"> But as regardeth me, to draw near to God is good for me: I have put in the Lord Eternal my trust, that I may relate all thy works.</span>
