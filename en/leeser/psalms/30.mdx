<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 25:2; Daniel 4:37; Psalms 35:19"> “A psalm, a song at the dedication of the temple, of David.” (30:2) I will extol thee, O Lord; for thou hast lifted me up, and hast not suffered my enemies to rejoice over me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 103:3–4; 2 Kings 20:5; Psalms 147:3"> (30:3) O Lord my God, I cried loudly unto thee, and thou hast healed me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 86:13; Psalms 16:10; Jonah 2:4–6"> (30:4) O Lord, thou hast brought up from the nether world my soul: thou hast kept me alive, that I should not go down to the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 97:12; Revelation 4:8; 1 Chronicles 16:4"> (30:5) Sing unto the Lord, O ye his pious ones, and give thanks to the memorial of his holiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 54:7–8; 2 Corinthians 4:17; Psalms 126:5–6"> (30:6) For his anger is momentary, &#91;but&#93; life is in his favor: in the evening &#91;cometh&#93; weeping to stay for a night, but in the morning, there is joyful song.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 12:19; 2 Corinthians 12:7; Psalms 119:117"> (30:7) And I had said indeed in my prosperity, I shall never be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 104:29; Psalms 143:7; Psalms 30:5"> (30:8) Lord, by thy favor hadst thou caused my mountain to stand in strength: thou didst hide thy face, &#91;and&#93; I was terrified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 4:6–7; Psalms 34:6; Psalms 130:1–2"> (30:9) Unto thee, O Lord, will I call; and unto the Lord will I make supplication.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 6:5; Psalms 115:17–18; Isaiah 38:18"> (30:10) What profit is there in my blood, when I go down to the grave? Shall the dust thank thee? shall it announce thy truth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 54:4; Psalms 143:1; Psalms 143:7–9"> (30:11) Hear, O Lord, and be gracious unto me: Lord, be thou a helper unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:4; Jeremiah 31:13–14; Ecclesiastes 3:4"> (30:12) Thou hast changed my mourning into dancing for me; thou hast loosened my sack-cloth, and girded me with joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 145:2; Psalms 146:1–2; Psalms 44:8"> (30:13) To the end that my glorious soul may sing praise to thee, and never be silent. O Lord my God, for ever will I give thanks unto thee.</span>
