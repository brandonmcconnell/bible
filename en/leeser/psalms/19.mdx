<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 1:19–20; Psalms 8:3; Psalms 50:6"> “To the chief musician, a psalm of David.” (19:2) The heavens relate the glory of God; and the expanse telleth of the works of his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 74:16; Psalms 134:1–3; Genesis 8:22"> (19:3) Day unto day uttereth speech, and night unto night showeth knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:19"> (19:4) There is no speech, there are no words, their voice is not heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 10:18; Genesis 1:14–18; Psalms 98:3"> (19:5) &#91;But&#93; their melody extendeth through all the earth, and to the end of the world their words. For the sun hath he set a tabernacle among them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 62:5; Philippians 3:13–14; 1 Corinthians 9:24–26"> (19:6) And he goeth out as a bridegroom from his chamber, he is glad like a strong man to run his course;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:5; Job 22:14; Colossians 1:23"> (19:7) From the end of the heavens is his going forth, and his circuit is unto their ends: and there is nothing hidden from his heat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 119:130; Psalms 111:7; Colossians 3:16"> (19:8) The law of the Lord is perfect, quieting the soul: the testimony of the Lord is sure, making wise the simple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 119:105; Psalms 12:6; Jeremiah 15:16"> (19:9) The precepts of the Lord are upright, rejoicing the heart; the commandment of the Lord is clear, enlightening the eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:24; Psalms 119:142; Psalms 119:137–138"> (19:10) The fear of the Lord is pure, enduring for ever: the ordinances of the Lord are the truth, they are just altogether.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 119:103; Proverbs 3:13–15; Psalms 119:72"> (19:11) They are those which are to be desired more than gold, and much fine gold; and they are sweeter than honey and the dropping of honeycomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; 2 John 1:8; Hebrews 11:6–7"> (19:12) Moreover thy servant is admonished by them: in keeping them there is great reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 90:8; Job 6:24; Psalms 139:23–24"> (19:13) Who can guard against errors? from secret &#91;faults&#93; do thou cleanse me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 119:133; Romans 6:12–14; 1 Samuel 25:39"> (19:14) Also from presumptuous &#91;sins&#93; withhold thy servant; let them not have dominion over me: then shall I be blameless, and I shall be clear from any great transgression.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 104:34; Psalms 51:15; Psalms 119:108"> (19:15) May the words of my mouth, and the meditation of my heart, be acceptable before thee, O Lord, my Rock, and my Redeemer.</span>
