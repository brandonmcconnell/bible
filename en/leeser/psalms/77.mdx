<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 3:4; Psalms 142:1–3; Psalms 50:1"> “To the chief musician on Jeduthun, by Assaph a psalm.” (77:2) &#91;I lift up&#93; my voice unto God, and I cry; &#91;I lilt up&#93; my voice unto God: do then give ear unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Genesis 37:35; Isaiah 26:9"> (77:3) On the day of my distress I sought the Lord; in the night my hand was stretched out, and did not cease: my soul refused to be comforted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 61:2; Psalms 142:2–3; Job 31:23"> (77:4) I think of God, and moan: I reflect, and my spirit is overwhelmed. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 7:13–15; Psalms 6:6; Job 2:13"> (77:5) Thou holdest my eyes awake: I am troubled and I cannot speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:7; Isaiah 51:9; Psalms 143:5"> (77:6) I think over the days of old, the years of ancient times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 42:8; Psalms 4:4; Psalms 139:23–24"> (77:7) I call to remembrance my song in the night; with my own heart I reflect: and my spirit maketh diligent search.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 85:1; Lamentations 3:31–32; Psalms 85:5"> (77:8) Will the Lord cast me off for ever? and will he never more give his favor again?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Romans 9:6; 2 Peter 3:9"> (77:9) Is his kindness spent for ever? is his promise come to an end for all generations?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 40:11; Isaiah 49:14–15; Isaiah 63:15"> (77:10) Hath God forgotten to be gracious? or hath he shut up in anger his mercies? Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 42:3; Mark 9:24; Exodus 15:6"> (77:11) And I said, This shall be my entreaty, &#91;for&#93; the years of the right hand of the Most High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 143:5; Psalms 105:5; 1 Chronicles 16:12"> (77:12) I will remember the deeds of the Lord; for I will remember out of ancient times thy wonders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 71:24; Psalms 145:11; Deuteronomy 6:7"> (77:13) I will meditate also of all thy work, and on thy deeds will I reflect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 73:17; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 89:6–8"> (77:14) O God, in holiness is thy way: where is there a god so great as God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 6:27; Psalms 136:4; Exodus 15:11"> (77:15) Thou art the God that dost wonders: thou hast made known among the people thy strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 6:6; Deuteronomy 9:29; Deuteronomy 9:26"> (77:16) Thou hast redeemed with &#91;a mighty&#93; arm thy people, the sons of Jacob and Joseph. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Habakkuk 3:15; Joshua 3:15–16"> (77:17) The waters saw thee, O God, the waters saw thee; they shook: also the depths trembled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 18:14; 2 Samuel 22:15; Habakkuk 3:11"> (77:18) The clouds poured out water; the skies sent forth thunder: also thy arrows sped along.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 97:4; 2 Samuel 22:14; Revelation 11:19"> (77:19) The voice of thy thunder was in the whirlwind; lightnings gave light to the world; the earth trembled and quaked,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 14:28; Psalms 29:10; Nahum 1:3–4"> (77:20) Through the sea led thy way, and thy path was through mighty waters, and thy footsteps could not be known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 13:21; Psalms 78:52; Exodus 14:19"> (77:21) Thou didst lead like a flock thy people by means of Moses and Aaron.</span>
