<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 37:6; Ezekiel 18:25; 2 Thessalonians 1:6–9"> Let from thy presence my sentence come forth; let thy eyes behold what is right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 11:23; Job 23:10; Psalms 26:2"> Thou hast proved my heart; thou hast thought of me in the night; thou hast refined me—thou couldst find nothing: my purpose doth not pass beyond &#91;the words of&#93; my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Psalms 119:9–11; 1 Corinthians 3:3"> Among the deeds of men did I observe, by the word of thy lips, the paths of the dissolute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 44:18; Psalms 119:133; 1 Samuel 2:9"> My steps held firmly to thy tracks, &#91;and&#93; my footsteps did not slip.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 116:2; Psalms 86:7; Psalms 88:2"> I call on thee, for thou wilt answer me, O God: incline thy ear unto me, hear my speech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 60:5; Psalms 5:11–12; Psalms 31:21"> Show marvelously thy loving-kindnesses, O thou that savest those who put their trust &#91;in thee&#93; from those that rise up &#91;against them&#93; by thy right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:10; Psalms 91:4; Psalms 91:1"> Keep me as the apple of the eye; conceal me under the shadow of thy wings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 35:4; Psalms 35:7; Psalms 7:5"> From the wicked that despoil me, my enemies, who, to take my life, compass me about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:3; Psalms 123:4; Psalms 31:18"> They are enclosed in their own fat: with their mouth they speak proudly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 37:14; Proverbs 6:13–14; Psalms 10:8–10"> On our steps they now encompass us: they direct their eyes to turn aside in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 7:2; 1 Peter 5:8; Psalms 10:9"> Every man is just like a lion that is greedy to tear his prey, and like a young lion lurking in a covert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 44:26; Psalms 44:23; Psalms 7:6"> Arise, O Lord, prevent him, cast him down; deliver my soul from the wicked, who is thy sword,—</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:18; John 17:14; Luke 16:25"> From these men—thy hand—O Lord, from the men of this world, whose portion is in this life, and whose belly thou fillest with thy hidden treasure: they have children in plenty, and leave the rest of their substance to their babes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:18; Matthew 5:6; Psalms 11:7"> As for me, in righteousness shall I behold thy face: I shall be satisfied, when I awake, with contemplating thy likeness.</span>
