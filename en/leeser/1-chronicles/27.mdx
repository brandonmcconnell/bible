<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 4:27; 2 Chronicles 26:11–13; Deuteronomy 1:15"> And &#91;these are&#93; the children of Israel after their number; &#91;to wit,&#93; the chiefs of the family divisions and the captains of the thousands and the hundreds, and their officers that served the king in every matter of the divisions, that came in and went out month by month, throughout all the months of the year, every division being twenty and four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:11; 2 Samuel 23:8"> Over the first division for the first month was Jashob'am the son of Zabdiel; and in his division were twenty and four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 26:20; Numbers 10:14; Genesis 49:8–10"> &#91;He&#93; of the children of Perez was the chief of all the captains of the armies for the first month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:9; 1 Chronicles 11:12"> And over the division of the second month was Dodai the Achochite, and of his division was Mikloth also the ruler; and in his division were twenty and four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 18:17; 1 Kings 4:4–5"> The third captain of the army for the third month was Benayahu the son of Jehoyada', the priest, the chief; and in his division were twenty and four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:20–23; 1 Chronicles 11:22–25; 2 Samuel 22:20–23"> This Benayahu was the mighty among the thirty, and &#91;set&#93; over the thirty'; and of his division was 'Ammizabad his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:26; 2 Samuel 23:24; 2 Samuel 2:18–23"> The fourth for the fourth month was 'Assahel the brother of Joab, with Zebadyah his son after him; and in his division were twenty and four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:27; 2 Samuel 23:25; 1 Chronicles 26:29"> The fifth for the fifth month was the captain Shamhuth the Yizrachite; and in his division were twenty and four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:28; 2 Samuel 23:26"> The sixth for the sixth month was 'Ira the son of 'Ikkesh the Theko'ite; and in his division were twenty and four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:27; 2 Samuel 23:26"> The seventh for the seventh month was Chelez the Pelonite, of the children of Ephraim; and in his division were twenty and four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:18; 1 Chronicles 11:29; Numbers 26:20"> The eighth for the eighth month was Sibbechai the Chushathite, of the Zarchites: and in his division were twenty and four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:28; 2 Samuel 23:27"> The ninth for the ninth month was Abi'ezer the 'Anthothite, of Benjamin; and in his division were twenty and four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:30; 2 Samuel 23:28"> The tenth for the tenth month was Maharai the Netophathite, of the Zarchites; and in his division were twenty and four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:31; 2 Samuel 23:30"> The eleventh for the eleventh month was Benayah the Pir'athonite, of the children of Ephraim; and in his division were twenty and four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:29; 1 Chronicles 4:13; Judges 3:9"> The twelfth for the twelfth month was Cheldai the Netophathite, of 'Othniel; and in his division were twenty and four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Moreover over the tribes of Israel: Of the Reubenites was ruler Eli'ezer the son of Zichri; of the Simeonites, Shephatyahu the son of Ma'achah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:30; 1 Chronicles 24:31; 1 Chronicles 12:27–28"> Of the Levites, Chashabyah the son of Kamuel; of &#91;the sons of&#93; Aaron, Zadok;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:6; 1 Samuel 17:29; 1 Samuel 17:13"> Of Judah, Elihu, one of the brothers of David; of Issachar, 'Omri the son of Michael;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Of Zebulun, Yishma'yahu the son of 'Obadyahu; of Naphtali, Jerimoth the son of 'Azriel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Of the children of Ephraim, Hoshea, the son of 'Azazyahu; of the half tribe of Menasseh, Joel the son of Pedayahu;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:27; 1 Samuel 14:50–51; 2 Samuel 3:37"> Of the half tribe of Menasseh in Gil'ad, Yiddo the son of Zecharyahu; of Benjamin, 'Ja'assiel the son of Abner;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:1"> Of Dan, 'Azarel the son of Jerocham. These were the princes of the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 15:5; 1 Chronicles 21:2–5; Hebrews 11:12"> But David took not their number from twenty years old and under; because the Lord had said he would multiply Israel like the stars of the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:1–17; 2 Samuel 24:1–15"> Joab the son of Zeruyah began to number &#91;them&#93;; but he finished not, and there came wrath because of it against Israel: and the number was not entered in the account of the chronicles of king David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:2; Exodus 1:11; 2 Chronicles 26:10"> And over the king's treasures was 'Azmaveth the son of 'Adiel; and over the storehouses in the fields, in the cities, and in the villages, and in the castles, was Jehonathan the son of 'Uzziyahu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And over those that did the work of the field, in the tillage of the ground, was 'Ezri the son of Kelub.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And over the vineyards was Shim'i the Ramathite; and over what was in the vineyards, as regardeth the supplies of wine, was Zabdi the Shiphmite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Kings 10:27; 2 Chronicles 1:15; 1 Kings 4:7"> And over the olive-trees and the sycamore-trees that were in the lowlands was Ba'al-chanan the Gederite: and over the supplies of oil was Jo'ash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:16; Isaiah 65:10"> And over the herds that fed in Sharon was Shitrai the Sharonite; and over the herds that were in the valleys was Shaphat the son of 'Adlai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 47:6; Job 1:3"> And over the camels was Obil the Ishma'elite; and over the she-asses was Yechdeyahu the Meronothite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:10"> And over the flocks was Jaziz the Hagerene. All these were the rulers of the property which belonged to king David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:21; 2 Samuel 13:3; 1 Chronicles 11:11"> Also Jonathan David's uncle was a counsellor, being a man of understanding and acquainted with law; and Jeshiel the son of Chachmoni was with the king's sons;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:12; 2 Samuel 15:37; 2 Samuel 15:32"> And Achithophel was the king's counsellor; and Chushai the Arkite was the king's friend;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:6; 1 Kings 1:7; 1 Chronicles 27:5"> And after Achithophel &#91;came&#93; Jehoyada' the son of Benayahu and Ebyathar; and the captain of the king's army was Joab.</span>
