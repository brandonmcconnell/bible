<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:12–13; 1 Samuel 16:1; 2 Samuel 5:3"> Thus came all the elders of Israel to the king to Hebron; and David made a covenant with them in Hebron before the Lord; and they anointed David as king over Israel, according to the word of the Lord through means of Samuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 1:21; Genesis 15:21; Joshua 15:8"> And David and all Israel went to Jerusalem, which is Jebus; and there were the Jebusites, the inhabitants of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 5:2; 1 Kings 8:1; Psalms 87:2"> And the inhabitants of Jebus said to David, Thou shalt not come in hither. Nevertheless David captured the strong-hold of Zion, the same is the City of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:16; 2 Samuel 20:23; 2 Samuel 3:27"> And David said, Whosoever doth smite the Jebusites at first shall be head and chief. Then did Joab the son of Zeruyah go up at first, and became head-man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:5; 2 Samuel 5:7; Psalms 2:6"> And David dwelt in the castle: therefore they called it, “The City of David.”</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:3; 1 Samuel 16:1; 1 Chronicles 12:38"> These also are the principals of the mighty men whom David had, who held firmly with him in his kingdom, with all Israel, to make him king, according to the word of the Lord concerning Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:8; 1 Chronicles 27:2; 1 Chronicles 12:18"> And this is the number of the mighty men whom David had: Jashoh'am, the son of Chachmoni, the chief of the captains, who lifted up his spear against three hundred slain at one time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:4; 2 Samuel 23:17–19; 2 Samuel 23:23"> And after him was El'asar the son of Dodo, the Achochite, who was one of the three mighty men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:11–12; 1 Samuel 17:1"> He was with David at Pass-dammim, and the Philistines were gathered together there to battle, and there was a piece of ground full of barley; and the people had fled from before the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 144:10; 2 Kings 5:1; 1 Samuel 14:23"> And they placed themselves in the midst of that piece &#91;of ground&#93;, and they delivered it, and smote the Philistines: and the Lord helped &#91;them&#93; with a great victory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 14:9; 2 Samuel 5:18; Isaiah 17:5"> And these three, the chiefs of the thirty, went down to the rock to David, to the cave of 'Adullam; and the camp of the Philistines was pitched in the valley of Rephaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:5; 1 Samuel 13:4; 1 Samuel 13:23"> And David was then in the strong-hold, and an outpost of the Philistines was then at Beth-lechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 4:14; Isaiah 12:3; 2 Samuel 23:15–16"> And David longed, and said, Oh that some one would bring me water to drink out of the well of Beth-lechem, which is by the gate!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 21:13; 1 Samuel 19:5; Acts 20:24"> And the three broke through the camp of the Philistines, and drew water out of the well of Beth-lechem, which was by the gate, and carried it, and brought it to David; but David would not drink thereof, and poured it out unto the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:17; Psalms 72:14; Judges 9:17"> And he said, Far be it from me, before my God, that I should do this: shall I drink the blood of these men that went at the risk of their lives? for at the risk of their lives did they bring it; and thus he would not drink it. These things did the three mighty men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:6; 1 Chronicles 2:16; 2 Samuel 20:6"> And Abshai the brother of Joab was the chief of these three; and he lifted up his spear against three hundred slain, and had a name among the three.</span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:20–23; 2 Samuel 8:18; Joshua 15:21"> Banayah the son of Jehoyada', the son of a valiant man, great in many acts, of Kah-zeel: he it was that smote the two lion-like heroes of Moab; he also went down and smote a lion in the midst of a pit on a day when it snowed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:7; 1 Samuel 17:4; Deuteronomy 3:11"> And he smote an Egyptian, a man of great stature, five cubits high; and in the Egyptian's hand was a spear like a weaver's beam; and he went down to him with a staff, and he snatched the spear out of the Egyptian's hand, and slew him with his own spear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> These things did Benayah the son of Jehoyada'; and he had a name among the mighty men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:23"> Behold, he was indeed more honored than the thirty; but he attained not to the first three. And David appointed him over his private council.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:7; 2 Samuel 21:19; 2 Samuel 2:18–23"> And the mighty men of the armies were, 'Assahel the brother of Joab, Elchanan the son of Dodo of Beth-lechem.</span>
