<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:20; 2 Chronicles 34:29–30; 2 Chronicles 29:20"> And David consulted with the officers of the thousands and hundreds, and with every leader.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 4:4–20; 1 Samuel 31:1; 1 Chronicles 10:7"> And David said unto all the congregation of Israel, if it seem good unto you, and if it be of the Lord our God, let us send widely about unto our brethren who are left in all the lands of Israel, and with them unto the priests and Levites who are in their cities and open districts, that they may gather themselves together unto us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:1–2; 1 Samuel 23:2; 1 Samuel 14:18"> And let us bring round the ark of our God to us; for we have inquired not at it in the days of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esther 8:5; 2 Samuel 3:36; 1 Samuel 18:20"> And all the congregation said that this should be done; for the king was right in the eyes of all the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:21–1; 1 Kings 8:65; 2 Samuel 6:1"> So David assembled all Israel together, from Shichor of Egypt even unto the entrance of Chemath, to bring the ark of God from Kiryath-ye'arim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 25:22; Joshua 15:9; 2 Kings 19:15"> And David went up, with all Israel, to Ba'alah, &#91;that is&#93; to Kiryath-ye'arim, which belonged to Judah, to bring up thence the ark of God the Lord, that dwelleth between the cherubim, whose name is called &#91;on it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; 1 Samuel 6:7; 1 Chronicles 15:2"> And they conveyed the ark of God in a new wagon out of the house of Abinadab: and 'Uzza and Achyo guided the wagon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 150:3–5; Psalms 68:25–27; Amos 6:5"> And David and all Israel played before God with all their might, and with singing, and on harps, and on psalteries, and on tambourines, and with cymbals, and with trumpets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:6"> And when they came as far as the threshing-floor of Kidon, 'Uzza put forth his hand to take hold of the ark; for the oxen shook it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:15; 1 Chronicles 15:13; Numbers 4:15"> And the anger of the Lord was kindled against 'Uzza, and he smote him, because he had put forth his hand toward the ark: and he died there before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:7; 2 Samuel 6:9; Genesis 32:32"> And it was grievious to David, because the Lord had suddenly taken away 'Uzza; and he called that place Perez-'uzza &#91;&#91;Breach of 'Uzza&#93;&#93; until this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; 1 Kings 8:27; Job 25:5–6"> And David was afraid of God that day, saying, How shall I bring home to me the ark of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:4; 1 Chronicles 15:18; 2 Samuel 4:3"> So David removed not the ark unto himself into the city of David, but had it carried round into the house of 'Obed-edom the Gittite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:4–5; Proverbs 10:22; Proverbs 3:9–10"> And the ark of God remained in the house of 'Obed-edom, in his house, three months. And the Lord blessed the house of 'Obed-edom, and all that belonged to him.</span>
