<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:28; 1 Chronicles 28:5; 1 Kings 1:33–39"> And when David was old and full of days, he made Solomon his son king over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 24:1; 2 Chronicles 34:29–30; 1 Chronicles 13:1"> And he gathered together all the princes of Israel, with the priests and the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:24; Numbers 4:2–49"> And then were numbered the Levites from thirty years old and upward: and their number by their polls, of men, was thirty and eight thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:8; Deuteronomy 16:18; 1 Chronicles 26:29–31"> Of these were twenty and four thousand to superintend the work of the house of the Lord; and six thousand were officers and judges;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:25–26; 2 Chronicles 8:14; Psalms 87:7"> And four thousand were gatekeepers: and four thousand those who praised the Lord with the instruments which I have made, to praise therewith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 6:1; 1 Chronicles 6:16"> And David divided them into divisions after the sons of Levi, after Gershon, Kehath, and Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:21; Exodus 6:17; 1 Chronicles 6:17–20"> Of the Gershunites: La'dan, and Shim'i.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:33–34; 1 Chronicles 15:20–21; 1 Chronicles 15:7"> The sons of La'dan: The chief was Jechiel, and Zetham, and Joel, three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The sons of Shim'i: Shelomith, and Chaziel, and Haran, three. These were the chiefs of the families of La'dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And the sons of Shim'i were, Jachath, Zina, and Je'ush, and Beri'ah. These four were the sons of Shim'i.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Jachath was the chief, and Zizah the second; but Je'ush and Beri'ah had not many sons: therefore were they accounted as one family division in the numbering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 6:18; Numbers 3:27; Numbers 26:58"> The sons of Kehath: 'Amram, Yizhar, Hebron, and 'Uzziel, four.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:5; Exodus 6:20; 1 Samuel 2:28"> The sons of 'Amram, Aaron and Moses: and Aaron was set apart, to sanctify him as most holy, he with his sons for ever, to burn incense before the Lord, to minister unto him, and to bless in his name for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:1; Psalms 90:1; 1 Chronicles 26:23–25"> But as regardeth Moses the man of God, his sons were named after the tribe of Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 18:3–4; Exodus 2:22; Exodus 4:20"> The sons of Moses were, Gershom, and Eli'ezer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:24; 1 Chronicles 25:20; 1 Chronicles 24:20"> Of the sons of Gershom, Shebuel was the chief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:25"> And the sons of Eli'ezer were, Rechahyah the chief. And Eli'ezer had no other sons; but the sons of Rechahyah became exceedingly numerous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:26; 1 Chronicles 24:22"> Of the sons of Yizhar, was Shelomith the chief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:23; 1 Chronicles 15:9; 1 Chronicles 23:12"> The sons of Hebron: Jeriyahu the chief, Amaryah the second, Jachaziel the third, and Jekam'am the fourth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The sons of 'Uzziel: Michah the chief, and Yishiyah the second.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 6:19; 1 Chronicles 6:20; 1 Chronicles 24:26–30"> The sons of Merari: Machli, and Mushi. The sons of Machli: El'azar, and Kish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:28; Numbers 36:6–8"> And El'azar died, and had no sons, but daughters; and the sons of Kish, their brethren, took them &#91;for wives&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:30"> The sons of Mushi: Machli, and 'Eder, and Jeremoth, three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 10:17; Numbers 10:21; 1 Chronicles 23:3"> These were the sons of Levi after their family divisions, even the chiefs of the families, as they were counted by numbering the names after their polls, that did the work for the service of the house of the Lord, from twenty years old and upward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 132:13–14; 1 Chronicles 22:18; 2 Samuel 7:11"> For David said, The Lord the God of Israel hath given rest unto his people, and he dwelleth in Jerusalem for evermore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 4:5; Numbers 7:9; Numbers 4:15"> And also the Levites are no more bound to carry the tabernacle, and all its vessels for the service thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:24; Psalms 72:20; 2 Samuel 23:1"> Therefore by the last words of David were there numbered of the Levites those who were from twenty years old and above;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:3–6; 2 Chronicles 31:11; 2 Chronicles 35:11–14"> Because their station was to be at the side of the sons of Aaron for the service of the house of the Lord, in the courts, and in the chambers, and by the purification of all holy things, and the work of the service of the house of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 19:35–36; Exodus 25:30; Leviticus 6:20–23"> And for the shew-bread, which was put in rows, and for the fine flour for meat-offering, and for the unleavened cakes, and for that which is baked in the pan, and for that which is sodden, and for all manner of dry and wet measure;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 92:1–3; Ezra 3:10–11; 1 Chronicles 9:33"> And to stand every morning to thank and praise the Lord, and so also at evening,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 1:13–14; Numbers 28:1–29; Numbers 10:10"> And at all the offering of burnt-offerings unto the Lord on the sabbaths, on the new moons, and on the appointed feasts, according to the number, and after the manner prescribed for them, continually before the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 3:38; Numbers 3:6–9"> And that they should keep the charge of the tabernacle of the congregation, and the charge of the sanctuary, and the charge of the sons of Aaron their brethren, in the service of the house of the Lord.</span>
