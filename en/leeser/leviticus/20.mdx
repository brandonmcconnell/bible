<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spoke unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:21; Leviticus 18:21; Leviticus 20:27"> And to the children of Israel shalt thou say, Whatsoever man of the children of Israel, or of the strangers that sojourn in Israel, that giveth any of his seed unto Molech, shall surely be put to death; the people of the land shall stone him with stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 18:21; Ezekiel 5:11; Ezekiel 23:38–39"> And I will set my face against that man, and I will cut him off from among his people; because of his seed hath he given unto Molech, in order to defile my sanctuary, and to profane my holy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:2–5; Acts 17:30; 1 Kings 20:42"> And if the people of the land should in any way hide their eyes from that man, when he giveth of his seed unto Molech, so as not to kill him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 106:39; Hosea 2:13; Hosea 2:5"> Then will I set my face against that man, and against his family, and I will cut him off, and all that go astray after him, to go astray after Molech, from among their people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; Isaiah 8:19; Leviticus 20:27"> And the person that turneth unto such as have familiar spirits, and unto wizards, to go astray after them,—then will I set my face against that person, and will cut him off from among his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44; Leviticus 19:2; 1 Peter 1:15–16"> Sanctify yourselves therefore, and be ye holy; for I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 31:13; James 1:22; 1 Thessalonians 5:23"> And ye shall keep my statutes, and do them: I am the Lord who sanctify you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 21:17; Mark 7:10; Deuteronomy 27:16"> For every one whatever that curseth his father or his mother shall be put to death: his father or his mother hath he cursed, his blood shall be upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 8:4–5; Deuteronomy 22:22–24; Deuteronomy 5:18"> And if there be a man that committeth adultery with a man's wife, &#91;whoever it be&#93; that committeth adultery with his neighbor's wife: then shall the adulterer be put to death, together with the adulteress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:20; Deuteronomy 27:23; Amos 2:7"> And a man that lieth with his father's wife, hath uncovered his father's nakedness: both of them shall be put to death; their blood shall be upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 18:15; Leviticus 18:23; Genesis 38:18"> And if a man lie with his daughter-in-law, both of them shall be put to death: they have committed an unnatural deed; their blood shall be upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9; Leviticus 18:22; Jude 1:7"> And if a man lie with a male, as they lie with a woman, both of them have committed an abomination: they shall be put to death; their blood shall be upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 18:17; Deuteronomy 27:23; Joshua 7:15"> And if a man take a woman and her mother, it is incest: in fire shall they burn him and them; that there be no incest among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 18:23; Deuteronomy 27:21; Exodus 22:19"> And a man that lieth with a beast shall be put to death: and the beast also shall ye slay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 21:32; Exodus 19:13; Hebrews 12:20"> And if a woman approach unto any beast to lie down thereto, then shalt thou kill the woman, and the beast; they shall be put to death; their blood shall be upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 18:9; Deuteronomy 27:22; Ezekiel 22:11"> And if a man take his sister, the daughter of his father, or the daughter of his mother, and he see her nakedness, and she see his nakedness: it is a disgraceful deed; and they shall be cut off before the eyes of their people: the nakedness of his sister hath he uncovered; his iniquity shall he bear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 15:24; Leviticus 18:19; Ezekiel 18:6"> And if a man lie with a woman suffering of her separation, and uncover her nakedness, and he lay open her fountain, and she uncover the fountain of her blood: then shall both of them be cut off from the midst of their people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 18:6; Leviticus 18:12–30; Exodus 6:20"> And the nakedness of thy mother's sister, or of thy father's sister shalt thou not uncover; for his near of kin he uncovereth: their iniquity shall they bear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 18:14; Job 18:19; Luke 23:29"> And the man that lieth with his uncle's wife, hath uncovered his uncle's nakedness: their sin shall they bear; childless shall they die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 18:16; Matthew 14:3–4"> And if a man do take his brother's wife, it is an abominable act: the nakedness of his brother hath he uncovered; childless shall they remain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 18:25–28; Ezekiel 36:27; Leviticus 18:4–5"> And keep ye all my statutes, and all my ordinances, and do them; that the land, whither I bring you to dwell therein, may not vomit you forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:5; Leviticus 18:24; Leviticus 18:30"> And ye shall not walk in the customs of the nation which I cast out before you; for all these things they committed, and therefore I felt loathing for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:2; Exodus 6:8; Deuteronomy 7:6"> And I said unto you, Ye shall possess their land, and I will give it unto you to possess it, a land flowing with milk and honey: I am the Eternal your God, who have separated you from the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:3–21; Leviticus 11:1–47; Acts 10:11–15"> Ye shall therefore make a difference between the clean beast and the unclean, and between the unclean fowl and the clean; and ye shall not make your souls abominable by the beast, or by the fowl, or by any manner of thing that creepeth on the ground, which I have separated from you as unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 1:15–16; Leviticus 20:7; Leviticus 20:24"> And ye shall be holy unto me, for I the Lord am holy; and I have separated you from the nations, that ye should be mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; Leviticus 20:6; 1 Samuel 28:7–9"> And if there be among men or women one that hath a familiar spirit, or that is a wizard, they shall be put to death; with stones shall they stone them; their blood shall be upon them.</span>
