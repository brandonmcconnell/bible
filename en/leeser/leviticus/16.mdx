<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1–2"> And the Lord spoke unto Moses after the death of the two sons of Aaron, when they had come near before the Lord, and died:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 30:10; 1 Kings 8:10–12; Hebrews 10:19–20"> And the Lord said unto Moses, Speak unto Aaron thy brother, that he come not at all times into the holy place within the vail, before the mercy-seat, which is upon the ark, that he die not; for in the cloud will I appear upon the mercy-seat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 4:3; Hebrews 9:24–25; Hebrews 9:7"> With this shall Aaron come into the holy place: with a young bullock for a sin-offering, and a ram for a burnt-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 28:39–43; Ezekiel 44:17–18; Exodus 30:20"> A holy linen coat shall be put on, and linen breeches shall he have upon his flesh, and with a linen girdle shall he gird himself, and a linen mitre shall he bind on his head; these are holy garments; therefore shall he wash his flesh in water, and then put them on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:21; Leviticus 8:2; Ezra 6:17"> And from the congregation of the children of Israel shall he take two goats for a sin-offering, and one ram for a burnt-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 9:7; Hebrews 9:7; Hebrews 7:27"> And Aaron shall bring near the bullock of the sin-offering, which is for himself, and make an atonement for himself, and for his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 12:1; Leviticus 4:4; Matthew 16:21"> And he shall take the two goats, and place them before the Lord at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:23–26; Jonah 1:7; Proverbs 16:33"> And Aaron shall put lots upon the two goats; one lot “for the Lord,” and the other lot “for 'Azazel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 4:27–28; Acts 2:23"> And Aaron shall bring near the goat upon which fell the lot “for the Lord,” and offer him for a sin-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 2:2; Leviticus 16:21–22; Hebrews 9:23–24"> But the goat on which fell the lot “for 'Azazel,” shall be placed alive before the Lord, to make an atonement with him, by sending him away to 'Azazel into the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 16:6; Hebrews 7:27; Hebrews 9:7"> And Aaron shall bring near the bullock of the sin-offering, which is for himself, and he shall make an atonement for himself, and for his house; and he shall kill the bullock of the sin-offering which is for himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1; Exodus 30:34–38; Revelation 8:3–4"> And he shall take a censer full of burning coals of fire from off the altar before the Lord, and both his hands full of incense of spices, pounded fine, and bring it within the vail;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 25:21; Exodus 30:1; Exodus 30:7–8"> And he shall put the incense upon the fire, before the Lord; that the cloud of the incense may envelop the mercy-seat that is upon the testimony, that he die not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 9:25; Leviticus 4:5–6; Hebrews 9:13"> And he shall take of the blood of the bullock, and sprinkle it with his finger above toward the mercy-seat, eastward; and before the mercy-seat shall he sprinkle seven times of the blood with his finger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 9:7; Hebrews 2:17; Hebrews 9:3"> And he shall kill the goat of the sin-offering, that is for the people, and bring his blood to within the vail, and do with that blood as he did with the blood of the bullock, and sprinkle it above the mercy-seat, and before the mercy-seat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 9:22–23; Leviticus 16:18; Ezekiel 45:18–19"> And he shall make an atonement for the holy place, because of the uncleanness of the children of Israel, and because of their transgressions in all their sins: and so shall he do for the tabernacle of the congregation, that abideth among them in the midst of their uncleanness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 53:6; Luke 1:10; Exodus 34:3"> And there shall not be any man in the tabernacle of the congregation when he goeth in to make an atonement in the holy place, until he come out: and so shall he make an atonement for himself, and for his household, and for the whole congregation of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 4:7; Hebrews 5:7–8; Leviticus 16:16"> And he shall then go out unto the altar that is before the Lord, and make an atonement upon it; and he shall take of the blood of the bullock, and of the blood of the goat, and put it upon the horns of the altar round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 13:1; Ezekiel 43:18–22"> And he shall sprinkle upon it of the blood with his finger seven times; and he shall cleanse it, and hallow it from the uncleanness of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 16:16; Ezekiel 45:20; Colossians 1:20"> And when he hath made an end of atoning for the holy place, and the tabernacle of the congregation, and the altar: then shall he bring near the live goat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 53:6; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Ezra 10:1"> And Aaron shall lay both his hands upon the head of the live goat, and confess over him all the iniquities of the children of Israel, and all their transgressions in all their sins, putting them upon the head of the goat, and he shall send him away by the hand of a man appointed thereto into the wilderness:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 9:28; Isaiah 53:11–12; John 1:29"> And the goat shall bear upon him all their iniquities unto a land not inhabited; and so shall he send away the goat into the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:19; Ezekiel 42:14; Leviticus 16:4"> And Aaron shall then go into the tabernacle of the congregation, and he shall take off the linen garments, which he had put on when he went into the holy place, and he shall leave them there:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 16:3–5; Hebrews 10:19–22; Exodus 28:4–14"> And he shall bathe his flesh with water in a holy place, and put on his garments; and come then forth, and offer his burnt-offering, and the burnt-offering of the people, and make an atonement for himself, and for the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 29:13; Leviticus 4:8–10; Leviticus 16:6"> And the fat of the sin-offering shall he burn upon the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 16:10; Hebrews 7:19; Numbers 19:21"> And he that carrieth the goat to 'Azazel shall wash his clothes, and bathe his flesh in water, and afterward he may come into the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 4:21; Leviticus 6:30; Leviticus 4:11–12"> And the bullock for the sin-offering, and the goat for the sin-offering, the blood of which was brought in to make atonement in the holy place, shall one carry forth without the camp; and they shall burn in fire their skins, and their flesh, and their dung.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And he that burneth them shall wash his clothes, and bathe his flesh in water, and afterward he may come into the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 58:3; Numbers 29:7; Exodus 20:10"> And it shall be unto you a statute for ever: in the seventh month, on the tenth of the month, ye shall afflict yourselves &#91;by fasting&#93;, and no work shall ye do, whether it be one of your own country, or the stranger that sojourneth among you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:8; Psalms 51:2; Ephesians 5:26"> For on that day shall &#91;the high-priest&#93; make an atonement for you, to cleanse you; from all your sins before the Lord shall ye be clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 23:32; Isaiah 58:5; Leviticus 25:4"> It shall be a sabbath of rest unto you, and ye shall afflict yourselves &#91;by fasting&#93;, as a statute for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 16:4; Exodus 29:29–30; Leviticus 4:5"> And the priest, who shall be anointed, and who shall be consecrated to minister as priest in his father's stead, shall make the atonement; and he shall put on the linen clothes, the holy garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 16:16; Leviticus 16:24; Leviticus 16:6"> And he shall make an atonement for the holy of holies; and for the tabernacle of the congregation, and for the altar shall he make an atonement; and also for the priests, and for all the people of the congregation shall he make an atonement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hebrews 9:7; Hebrews 9:25; Exodus 30:10"> And this shall be unto you as a statute for everlasting, to make an atonement for the children of Israel for all their sins once a year: and he did as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
