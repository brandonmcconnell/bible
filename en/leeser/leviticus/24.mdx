<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spoke unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:6; Ephesians 5:8–14; Exodus 27:20–21"> Command the children of Israel, that they bring unto thee pure beaten olive-oil, for the lighting, to cause the lamp to burn continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Without the vail of the testimony, in the tabernacle of the congregation, shall Aaron put it in order &#91;for&#93; from evening unto morning before the Lord continually; as a statute for ever in your generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 31:8; Exodus 37:17–24; Exodus 39:37"> Upon the pure candlestick shall be put in order the lamps, before the Lord, continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 25:30; 1 Kings 18:31; Exodus 40:23"> And thou shalt take fine flour, and bake thereof twelve cakes: of two tenth parts shall each one cake be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 7:48; Exodus 25:23–24; 2 Chronicles 4:19"> And thou shalt place them in two rows, six in a row, upon the pure table before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 2:2; Revelation 8:3–4; Genesis 9:16"> And thou shalt put upon each row pure frankincense, that it may be unto the bread for a memorial, as a fire-offering unto the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:4; Numbers 4:7; 1 Chronicles 9:32"> On every and each sabbath day shall he place it in order before the Lord continually, &#91;obtained&#93; from the children of Israel as an everlasting covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 8:31; Luke 6:4; Mark 2:26"> And it shall belong to Aaron and to his sons; and they shall eat it in a holy place; for it is most holy unto him, from the fire-offerings of the Lord, as a perpetual fixed portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 11:4; Exodus 12:38"> And there went forth a son of an Israelitish woman, but who was the son of an Egyptian man, among the children of Israel; and there quarrelled together in the camp this son of the Israelitish woman and an Israelitish man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 20:7; Leviticus 24:15–16; Exodus 3:15"> And the son of the Israelitish woman pronounced the &#91;holy&#93; Name, and blasphemed; and they brought him unto Moses: &#91;and his mother's name was Shelomith, the daughter of Dibri, of the tribe of Dan;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 18:15–16; Numbers 15:34; Numbers 27:5"> And they placed him in ward, until the decision of the Lord could be explained to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the Lord spoke unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:7; Leviticus 20:27; Deuteronomy 21:21"> Lead forth the blasphemer to without the camp; and all that have heard him shall lay their hands upon his head; and all the congregation shall stone him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 9:13; Exodus 22:28; Leviticus 5:1"> And unto the children of Israel shalt thou speak, saying, Whatsoever man that blasphemeth his God shall bear his sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 12:31; James 2:7; John 10:33–36"> But he that pronounced the name of the Lord &#91;with blasphemy&#93; shall be put to death, all the congregation shall stone him; be he a stranger, or be he one that is born in the land, when he pronounceth the &#91;holy&#93; Name &#91;with blasphemy,&#93; he shall be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 9:5–6; Deuteronomy 19:11–12; Deuteronomy 27:24"> And he that taketh the life of any man shall surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 24:21; Exodus 21:34–36"> And he that taketh the life of a beast shall make it good: beast for beast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 5:38; Matthew 7:2; Deuteronomy 19:21"> And if a man cause a bodily defect in his neighbor, as he hath done, so shall be done to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:21; Matthew 5:38; Exodus 21:23–25"> Breach for breach, eye for eye, tooth for tooth: in the manner as he hath caused a bodily defect in a man, so shall it be done to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 24:17–18; Exodus 21:33"> And he that killeth a beast, shall make restitution for it; and he that killeth a man, shall be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 12:49; Numbers 15:15–16; Numbers 9:14"> One manner of judicial law shall ye have, the stranger shall be equal with one of your own country; for I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 10:28–29; Hebrews 2:2–3; Numbers 15:35–36"> And Moses spoke to the children of Israel; and they led forth the blasphemer to without the camp, and they stoned him with stones; and the children of Israel did as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
