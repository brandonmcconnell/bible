<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 5:17; Proverbs 29:24; Leviticus 19:8"> And if any person sin, because he heareth the voice of adjuration, and he is a witness, since he hath either seen or knoweth something; if he do not tell it, and thus bear his iniquity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 5:17; Deuteronomy 14:8; Haggai 2:13"> Or if there be a person who toucheth any unclean thing, whether it be the carcass of an unclean beast, or the carcass of unclean cattle, or the carcass of an unclean creeping thing, and it escape his recollection; but &#91;he becometh aware that&#93; he is unclean, and hath &#91;thus&#93; incurred guilt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 19:11–16; Leviticus 15:1–33; Leviticus 12:1–8"> Or if he touch the uncleanness of man, whatsoever uncleanness of the kind it be by which he can be defiled, and it escape his recollection; but he becometh aware of it, and &#91;that&#93; he hath &#91;thus&#93; incurred guilt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:2–6; Acts 23:12; Judges 11:31"> Or if any person swear, by pronouncing with his lips to do evil, or to do good &#91;to himself&#93;, in whatsoever it be that a man pronounceth with an oath, and it escape his recollection; but he becometh aware of it that he hath incurred guilt by any one of these:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; Numbers 5:7; Leviticus 26:40"> And it shall be, if he have incurred guilt by any one of these &#91;things,&#93; that he shall confess that concerning which he hath sinned;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 4:28; Leviticus 4:32; Leviticus 4:20"> And he shall bring his trespass-offering unto the Lord for his sin which he hath committed, a female from the flocks, a sheep or a goat, for a sin-offering; and the priest shall make an atonement for him concerning his sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 12:8; Leviticus 14:21–22; Matthew 3:16"> And if his means be not sufficient for a sheep, then shall he bring as his offering &#91;for the trespass&#93; which he hath committed, two turtle-doves, or two young pigeons, unto the Lord; one for a sin-offering, and the other for a burnt-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 1:15; Leviticus 1:17; Romans 4:25"> And he shall bring them unto the priest, and he shall offer that which is for the sin-offering first, and pinch off its head by the back of its neck, but shall not divide it asunder:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 4:30; Leviticus 4:34; Leviticus 4:18"> And he shall sprinkle some of the blood of the sin-offering upon the wall of the altar: and the rest of the blood shall be wrung out at the bottom of the altar; it is a sin-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 1:14–17; Leviticus 4:26; Leviticus 4:20"> And the second shall he prepare as a burnt-offering, according to the prescribed order; and the priest shall make an atonement for him for his sin which he hath committed, and it shall be forgiven unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 5:15; Leviticus 2:1–2; Exodus 16:36"> But if his means be not sufficient for two turtle-doves, or two young pigeons, then shall he bring as his offering for that which he hath sinned, the tenth part of an ephah of fine flour for a sin-offering; he shall not put upon it any oil, nor shall he put thereupon any frankincense; for it is a sin-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 4:35; Leviticus 2:9; Leviticus 2:2"> And he shall bring it to the priest; and the priest shall take from it his handful, as its memorial, and burn it on the altar, upon the fire-offerings of the Lord: it is a sin-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3; Leviticus 4:26; 1 Corinthians 9:13"> And the priest shall make an atonement for him concerning his sin that he hath committed in one of these, and it shall be forgiven unto him; and it shall belong to the priest, as the meat-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And the Lord spoke unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Leviticus 4:2; Leviticus 6:6"> If any person commit a trespass, and sin through ignorance, against the holy things of the Lord: then shall he bring as his trespass-offering unto the Lord a ram without blemish out of the flocks, in value of two shekels of silver, after the shekel of the sanctuary, for a trespass-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 22:14; Numbers 5:7; Leviticus 27:27"> And that, in which he hath sinned against the holy thing, shall he pay, and shall add its fifth part thereto, and give it unto the priest; and the priest shall make an atonement for him with the ram of the trespass-offering, and it shall be forgiven unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 4:27; Leviticus 4:13; Leviticus 4:2–4"> And if any person sin, and commit any one of the prohibitions of the Lord which ought not to be done; and he know not whether he have incurred guilt, and so bear his iniquity:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 5:15–16; 1 Timothy 2:5–6; Leviticus 1:4"> Then shall he bring a ram without blemish out of the flocks, of the usual value, for a trespass-offering, unto the priest: and the priest shall make an atonement for him concerning his sin of ignorance, wherein he hath erred and knoweth it not, and it shall be forgiven unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 51:4; Malachi 3:8; 2 Corinthians 5:19–21"> It is a trespass-offering: he hath in trespassing trespassed against the Lord.</span>
