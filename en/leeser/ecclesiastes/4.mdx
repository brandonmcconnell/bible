<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:16; Ecclesiastes 5:8; Isaiah 5:7"> And I turned about, and beheld all the oppressed that are made so under the sun: and, behold, there are the tears of the oppressed, and they have no comforter; and from the hand of their oppressors they suffer violence; and they have no comforter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 3:11–26; Ecclesiastes 2:17; Ecclesiastes 9:4–6"> Thereupon praised I the dead that are already dead, more than the living who are still alive;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 23:29; Ecclesiastes 6:3–5; Matthew 24:19"> And as happier than both of them, him who hath not yet come into being, who hath not seen the evil-doing that is done under the sun.—</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 3:12; Ecclesiastes 1:14; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> Again, I beheld all the toil, and all the energy in doing, that it is &#91;from&#93; the envy of one man of his neighbor. Also this is vanity and a torture of the spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 9:20; Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 6:10–11"> The fool foldeth his hands together, and eateth his own flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 16:8; Proverbs 15:16–17; Psalms 37:16"> Better is a handful of quiet, than both the hands full of toil and torture of spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:1; Psalms 78:33; Zechariah 1:6"> Then I turned about, and I saw a vanity under the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 2:16; Proverbs 27:20; Luke 12:20"> There is one alone, and he hath not a companion; yea, he hath neither son nor brother: yet is there no end to all his toil; his eye also is not satisfied with riches. Yet for whom do I toil, and deprive my soul of good? Also this is vanity, yea, it is a bad employment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 27:17; Genesis 2:18; 1 Corinthians 12:18–21"> Two are better than one; because they will have a good reward for their toil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; 1 Thessalonians 4:18; Isaiah 35:3–4"> For if they fall, the one will lift up his fellow; but woe to the single one that falleth; for he hath no companion to lift him up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 1:1–4"> Also, if two lie together, then will they become warm; but how can one person alone become warm?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 3:16–17; Ephesians 4:3; 2 Samuel 23:9"> And if a man could overpower him, the single one, two would stand up against him: and a threefold cord cannot quickly be torn asunder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:16; 2 Chronicles 16:9–10; Proverbs 19:1"> Better is a poor and a wise youth than an old and foolish king, who knoweth not how to be admonished any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 113:7–8; Genesis 41:14; Job 5:11"> For out of the prison cometh the one to reign: whereas also in his kingdom the other becometh poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:6"> I have seen all the living who walk under the sun, being with the second child that is to stand up in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:14; 1 Kings 1:40; Ecclesiastes 2:17"> There was no end to all the people, &#91;belonging&#93; to all that have been before them: they also that come after will not rejoice in him. Surely this also is vanity and a torture of the spirit.—</span>
