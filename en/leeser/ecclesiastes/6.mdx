<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:13"> There is an evil which I have seen under the sun, and it is great on men:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:19; Psalms 17:14; Psalms 73:7"> &#91;There is many&#93; a man to whom God hath given riches, property, and honor, and nothing is wanting for his soul of all that he longeth for: yet God empowereth him not to eat thereof, but a stranger will consume it. This is vanity, and it is an evil disease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 3:16; Jeremiah 22:19; Ecclesiastes 4:3"> If a man were to beget a hundred children, and live many years, so that the days of his years were many, and his soul were not satisfied with what is good, and he have not had even a burial: then do I say, that an untimely birth is better than he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 109:13"> For in vanity it came, and in darkness it departeth, and with darkness will its name be covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 58:8; Job 14:1; Job 3:10–13"> Moreover it never saw the sun, and knew nothing: this hath more rest than the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 9:27; Ecclesiastes 3:20; Job 30:23"> Yea, though he were to live a thousand years twice told, and had not seen any good— doth not every one go to one place?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 6:27; Proverbs 16:26; 1 Timothy 6:6–8"> All the toil of a man is for his mouth; and yet is his desire never filled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 19:1; Ecclesiastes 2:14–16; Psalms 101:2"> For what hath the wise more than the fool? what hath the poor, that knoweth to walk &#91;properly&#93; before the living?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:14; Job 31:7; Ecclesiastes 1:2"> Better is what one seeth with the eyes than the wandering of the desire. Also this is vanity and a torture of the spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:32; Isaiah 45:9–10; Job 40:2"> That which hath been is already called by its name, and it is known that he is a man: and he is not able to contend with him that is mightier than he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:16; Ecclesiastes 5:7; Ecclesiastes 2:3–11"> For there are many things that increase vanity: what advantage &#91;cometh thence&#93; for man?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 4:14; Job 14:2; Ecclesiastes 3:22"> For who knoweth what is good for man in this life, the number of the days of his vain life, that he should spend them as a shadow? for who can tell a man what will be after him under the sun?</span>
