<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 90:12; Deuteronomy 32:29; Hebrews 9:27"> It is better to go to the house of mourning than to go to the house of feasting; inasmuch as that is the end of all men: and let the living lay it to his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:9–11; Romans 5:3–4; James 4:8–10"> Better is vexation than laughing; for through the sadness of the countenance the heart is made better.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 8:14–16; 1 Samuel 25:36; Luke 7:12–13"> The heart of the wise is in the house of mourning; but the heart of fools is in the house of joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 15:31–32; Psalms 141:5; Revelation 3:19"> It is better to hear the rebuke of the wise, than that a man should hear the song of fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 58:9; Psalms 118:12; Ecclesiastes 2:2"> For as the crackling of thorns under a pot, so is the laughter of the fool. Also this is vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Exodus 23:8; Proverbs 17:23"> For &#91;exercising&#93; oppression maketh a wise man mad; and bribery corrupteth the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Proverbs 14:29; Psalms 126:5–6"> Better is the end of a thing than the beginning thereof: better is the patient in spirit than the proud in spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 1:19; Proverbs 14:17; Ephesians 4:26–27"> Be not rash in thy spirit to be angry; for anger resteth in bosom of fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 6:13; Psalms 14:2–3; Genesis 6:11–12"> Thou must not say, How was it that the former days were better than these? for it is not out of wisdom that thou askest concerning this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:7; 1 Timothy 6:17–19; Ecclesiastes 2:13"> Wisdom is better than an inheritance, yea, preferable for those that see the sun;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 8:35; Proverbs 2:7; Proverbs 3:18"> For under the shadow of wisdom &#91;a man is equally well as&#93; under the shadow of money; but the superior excellency of knowledge is, that wisdom giveth life to him that possesseth it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:15; Job 12:14; Psalms 107:43"> Consider &#91;then&#93; the work of God; for who can make straight what he hath made crooked?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:4; Ecclesiastes 3:22; Ecclesiastes 12:13"> On the day of prosperity be happy, but on the day of adversity look on: also this hath God made in equal measure with the other, to the end that man should not find the least to censure him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:12–14; Ecclesiastes 6:12; Psalms 73:3–13"> All things have I seen in the days of my vanity: there is many a righteous man that perisheth in his righteousness, and there is many a wicked man that liveth long in his wickedness.—</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:3; Proverbs 25:16; Colossians 2:18"> Be not righteous over much; neither show thyself over wise: why wouldst thou destroy thyself?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 10:27; Psalms 55:23; Job 22:16"> Be not wicked over much, and be no fool: why wouldst thou die before thy time?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:13; Malachi 4:2; Ecclesiastes 11:6"> It is good that thou shouldst take hold of that, and that also from this thou withdraw not thy hand; for he that feareth God will come forth out of them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 21:22; Colossians 1:9–11; Proverbs 24:5"> Wisdom giveth more strength to the wise than ten rulers which were in the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 3:23; Isaiah 53:6; 1 John 1:8–10"> For no man is so righteous upon earth, that he should do always good, and never sin.—</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 30:10; Isaiah 29:21; 2 Samuel 16:10"> Also take no heed unto all the words that are spoken: lest thou hear thy servant cursing thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 3:9; Matthew 18:32–35; John 8:7–9"> For oftentimes also doth thy own heart know that thou thyself likewise hast cursed others.—</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 1:22; Genesis 3:5; 1 Corinthians 1:20"> All this have I proved by wisdom: I said, I will be wise; but it was far from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 11:33; 1 Timothy 6:16; Isaiah 55:8–9"> Far is what formerly was so, and what was deep remaineth deep: who can find it out?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:13; Joshua 7:13; Ecclesiastes 2:20"> Then I turned myself about together with my heart to know, and to search, and to seek out wisdom, and experience, and to know the wickedness of folly, and the foolishness of madness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 22:14; Proverbs 5:3–5; Proverbs 2:18–19"> And I find as more bitter than death the woman, whose heart is snares and nets, and whose hands are bonds: he that is deemed good before God will escape from her; but the sinner will be caught by her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:8–10; Ecclesiastes 1:1–2"> Behold, this have I found, saith Koheleth, &#91;adding&#93; one to the other, to find experience,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 33:23; 1 Kings 11:1–3; Psalms 12:1"> What my soul constantly sought, but I found it not; one man among a thousand did I find; but a woman among all these did I not find.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:22; Mark 7:8–9; Titus 3:3"> Lo, this only did I find, that God hath made man upright; but they have sought for many &#91;sinful&#93; devices.</span>
