<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:9; Ecclesiastes 5:16; Ecclesiastes 2:22"> What profit hath a man of all his toil which he toil-eth under the sun?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 104:5; Zechariah 1:5; Ecclesiastes 6:12"> One generation passeth away, and another generation cometh; but the earth endureth for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 19:4–6; Habakkuk 3:11; Psalms 42:1"> The sun also riseth, and the sun goeth down, and striving to reach his place he riseth again there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 3:8; Ecclesiastes 11:5; Psalms 107:25"> Going toward the south, and turning round toward the north, the wind moveth round about continually; and around its circles doth the wind return again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 38:10–11; Psalms 104:6–9"> All the rivers run into the sea; yet the sea is never full: unto the place whither the rivers go, thither will they continue to go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 27:20; Matthew 11:28; Ecclesiastes 4:8"> All things weary themselves &#91;constantly&#93;; man can not utter them: the eye is never satisfied with seeing, nor the ear filled with hearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:15; Ecclesiastes 6:10; Ecclesiastes 2:12"> That which hath been, is the same which will be; and that which hath been done, is the same which will be done; and there is nothing new under the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Luke 17:26–30; Matthew 23:30–32"> If there be any thing whereof it is said, See, this is new: It hath already been in olden times which were before us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:16; Psalms 9:6; Ecclesiastes 9:5"> &#91;Only&#93; there is no recollection of former &#91;generations&#93;; and also of the later ones, that are to be—of these &#91;likewise&#93; there will be no recollection with those that will be still later.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:1; 1 Kings 4:1–19"> I Koheleth was king over Israel in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:10; Genesis 3:19; 1 Timothy 4:15"> And I directed my heart to inquire and to search out by wisdom concerning all that is done under the heavens: this is an evil employment which God hath given to the sons of man to busy themselves therewith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:11; Ecclesiastes 2:17; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> I saw all the deeds that are done under the sun: and, behold, all is vanity and a torture of the spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:12–13; Isaiah 40:4; Ecclesiastes 3:14"> What is crooked cannot be made straight; and that which is defective cannot be numbered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:9; 1 Kings 4:30; 1 Kings 10:7"> I spoke with my own heart, saying, Lo, I have truly obtained greater and more wisdom than all those who have been before me over Jerusalem: yea, my heart had seen much wisdom and knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:21; Ecclesiastes 1:13–14"> And I directed my heart to know wisdom, and to know madness and folly; &#91;but&#93; I have perceived that this also is a torture of the spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 28:28; Ecclesiastes 12:12–13; 1 Corinthians 3:18–20"> For where there is much wisdom there is much vexation: and he that increaseth knowledge increaseth pain.</span>
