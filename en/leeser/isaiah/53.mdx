<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 12:38; Romans 10:16–17; 1 Corinthians 1:18"> Who would have believed our report? and the arm of the Lord—over whom hath it been revealed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 2:6–7; Isaiah 52:14; John 1:10–14"> Yea, he grew up like a small shoot before him, and as a root out of a dry land: he had no form nor comeliness, so that we should look at him; and no countenance, so that we should desire him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 26:67; John 1:10–11; Isaiah 53:10"> He was despised and shunned by men; a man of pains, and acquainted with disease; and as one who hid his face from us was he despised, and we esteemed him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Matthew 8:17; Galatians 3:13"> But only our diseases did he bear himself, and our pains he carried: while we indeed esteemed him stricken, smitten of God, and afflicted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24–25; 1 Peter 3:18; Matthew 20:28"> Yet he was wounded for our transgressions, he was bruised for our iniquities: the chastisement for our peace was upon him; and through his bruises was healing granted to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 2:25; 1 Peter 3:18; Psalms 119:176"> We all like sheep went astray; every one to his own way did we turn; and the Lord let befall him the guilt of us all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 8:32–33; Matthew 26:63; 1 Peter 2:23"> He was oppressed, and he was also taunted, yet he opened not his mouth; like the lamb which is led to the slaughter, and like an ewe before her shearers is dumb; and he opened not his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Peter 3:18; Acts 8:33; Matthew 26:65–66"> Through oppression and through judicial punishment was he taken away; but his generation—who could tell, that he was cut away out of the land of life, &#91;that&#93; for the transgressions of my people the plague was laid on him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 27:57–60; 1 Peter 2:22; 1 John 3:5"> And he let his grave be made with the wicked, and with the &#91;godless&#93; rich at his death; although he had done no violence, and there was no deceit in his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 8:32; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Galatians 3:13"> But the Lord was pleased to crush him through disease: when &#91;now&#93; his soul hath brought the trespass-offering, then shall he see &#91;his&#93; seed, live many days, and the pleasure of the Lord shall prosper in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 5:18–19; 1 Peter 2:24; Isaiah 53:4–6"> &#91;Freed&#93; from the trouble of his soul shall he see &#91;the good&#93; and be satisfied: through his knowledge shall my righteous servant bring the many to righteousness, while he will bear their iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 22:37; Hebrews 9:28; Luke 23:32–34"> Therefore will I divide him &#91;a portion&#93; with the many, and with the strong shall he divide the spoil; because he poured out his soul unto death, and with transgressors was he numbered: while he bore the sin of many, and for the transgressors he let &#91;evil&#93; befall him.</span>
