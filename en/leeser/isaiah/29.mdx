<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 4:4–5; 2 Samuel 5:9; Micah 6:6–7"> Woe to Ariel, to Ariel, the town where David dwelt! add ye year to year; let the festivals come round in order;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 5:25–30; Isaiah 3:26; Isaiah 10:32"> Yet will I distress Ariel, and there shall be groaning and wailing: and it shall be unto me like Ariel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 19:43–44; Ezekiel 21:22; 2 Kings 25:1–4"> And I will encamp against thee round about, and will lay siege against thee with hostile posts, and I will raise up entrenchments against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 8:19; Psalms 44:25; Isaiah 3:8"> And brought down low, shalt thou speak &#91;as though&#93; out of the earth, and out of the dust shall come forth thy speech; and like one of a familiar spirit out of the earth shall be thy voice, and out of the dust shalt thou whisper forth thy speech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Isaiah 17:13–14; Isaiah 37:36"> And like the small dust shall be the multitude of thy barbarian enemies, and like the passing chaff the multitude of tyrants; and &#91;this&#93; shall be at unawares, suddenly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 13:8; 1 Samuel 2:10; Matthew 24:7"> From the Lord of hosts shall the visitation come with thunder, and with earthquake, and great noise, with storm and tempest, and the devouring flame of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 20:8; Micah 4:11–12; Zechariah 12:9"> And as a dream of a night-vision shall be the multitude of all the nations that go to war against Ariel, even all that fight against her and raise towers against her, and that distress her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 44:12; Psalms 73:20; Isaiah 10:7–16"> And it shall even be as when a hungry man dreameth, that, behold, he eateth; but he awaketh, and his soul is empty; or as when a thirsty man dreameth, that, behold, he drinketh; but he awaketh, and, behold, he is faint, and his soul yet longeth: so shall it be with the multitude of all the nations, that go to war against mount Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 51:21–22; Isaiah 19:14; Revelation 17:6"> Stay but still and wonder; turn your eyes away, and be blinded; they are drunken, but not with wine; they stagger, but not with strong drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 11:8; Isaiah 44:18; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–12"> For the Lord hath poured out over you the spirit of deep sleep, and hath closed your eyes: &#91;over&#93; the prophets, and your chiefs, the seers, hath he cast a vail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 13:11; Daniel 12:4; Isaiah 8:16"> And the vision of every thing is become unto you as the words of a book that is sealed, which men deliver to one that can read, saying, Read this, I pray thee; and he saith, I cannot; for it is sealed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 7:15–16; Isaiah 29:18; Jeremiah 5:4"> And the book is then delivered to one that cannot read, saying, Read this, I pray thee; and he saith, I cannot read.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:2; Ezekiel 33:31–33; Mark 7:2–13"> And the Lord said, Forasmuch as this people draw near with their mouth, and with their lips do honor me, but have removed their heart far from me, and their fear toward me is but the acquired precept of men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:5; 1 Corinthians 1:19–24; Isaiah 28:21"> Therefore, behold, I will do yet farther a marvelous work with this people, doing wonder on wonder; so that the wisdom of their wise men shall be lost, and the understanding of their prudent men shall be hidden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:12; Isaiah 47:10; Isaiah 30:1"> Woe unto those that seek to hide deeply their counsel from the Lord, so that their works may be in the dark, and they say, Who seeth us? and who knoweth us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 9:19–21; Isaiah 64:8; Isaiah 45:9–11"> Oh your perverseness! shall the potter be esteemed as the clay? that the work shall say of its maker, He hath not made me? or shall the thing framed say of its framer, He had no understanding?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 32:15; Matthew 19:30; Hebrews 10:37"> Lo! but yet a very little while more, and Lebanon shall be turned into a fruitful field, and the fruitful field shall be esteemed as a forest!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5; Matthew 11:5; Mark 7:37"> And on that day shall the deaf hear the words of the book, and out of obscurity, and out of darkness, shall the eyes of the blind see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="James 2:5; Isaiah 61:1; Matthew 11:29"> And the sufferers shall have abundant joy in the Lord, and the needy among men shall be glad in the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Micah 2:1; Isaiah 28:14–22; Luke 16:14"> For the tyrant is no more, and consumed is the scorner, and cut off are all that watch for injustice;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Amos 5:10–12; James 5:6; Acts 3:14"> That cause mankind to sin by &#91;their&#93; word; and lay a snare for him that reproveth &#91;them&#93; in the gate; and pervert through fraud the cause of the just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 45:17; Isaiah 51:2; Jeremiah 30:10"> Therefore thus hath said the Lord unto the house of Jacob, he who hath redeemed Abraham, Not now shall Jacob be ashamed, and not now shall his face be made pale.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; Isaiah 19:25; Isaiah 45:11"> For when he seeth his children, the work of my hands in the midst of him, how they sanctify my name: then will they sanctify the Holy One of Jacob, and the God of Israel will they reverence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 15:17–19; Hebrews 5:2; Isaiah 28:7"> They also that were erring in spirit shall acquire understanding, and they that murmured shall obtain instruction.</span>
