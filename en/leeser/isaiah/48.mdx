<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:2; Psalms 68:26; Isaiah 65:16"> Hear ye this, O house of Jacob, who are called by the name of Israel, and are come forth out of the spring of Judah, who swear by the name of the Lord, and make mention of the God of Israel,—not in truth, nor in righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Romans 2:17; Isaiah 52:1"> For of the holy city they call themselves, and upon the God of Israel they stay themselves,—The Lord of hosts is his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 42:9; Joshua 21:45; Isaiah 41:22"> The former things have I declared from the beginning; and out of my mouth went they forth, and I announced them: suddenly did I accomplish them, and they came to pass;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Exodus 32:9; Zechariah 7:11–12"> Because I knew that thou art obstinate, that like an iron sinew is thy neck, and thy brow of copper;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 48:3; Luke 1:70; Acts 15:18"> And I declared it to thee from the beginning; before yet it came to pass did I let thee hear it: lest thou should say, My idol hath done these things, and my graven image, and my molten image, have ordained them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 4:1; Isaiah 42:9; Romans 16:25–26"> Thou hast heard it; see it all now; and you—will you not declare it? I caused thee to hear new things, from this time, even hidden things which thou hadst not known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Now are they created, and not from the beginning; and before the day &#91;that I announced them&#93; thou heardest them not; lest thou shouldest say, Behold, I knew them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 58:3; Hosea 5:7; Jeremiah 6:10"> But neither hadst thou heard it; nor didst thou know; nor had in ancient times thy ear been opened; for I knew that thou wouldst deal very treacherously, and a transgressor wast thou called from thy birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 79:9; Psalms 106:8; Psalms 143:11"> For the sake of my name will I defer my anger, and because of my praise will I restrain it toward thee, so that I may not cut thee off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 4:12; Proverbs 17:3; Job 23:10"> Behold, I have refined thee, though not into silver: I have approved thee in the crucible of affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 42:8; Ezekiel 20:9; Isaiah 37:35"> For my own sake will I do it: for how would &#91;my name&#93; be dishonored? and my glory will I not give unto another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 41:4; Revelation 22:13; Revelation 1:8"> Hearken unto me, O Jacob, and Israel, my called one; I am he; I am the first, I also am the last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 1:10–12; Psalms 102:25; Isaiah 40:26"> My hand also hath laid the foundation of the earth, and my right hand hath spanned out the heavens: I call unto them, they stand forward together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 43:9; Jeremiah 50:21–29; Isaiah 13:17–18"> Assemble yourselves, all of you, and hear: Who among them hath told these things? He whom the Lord loveth, will do his pleasure on Babylon, and &#91;display&#93; his arm &#91;on&#93; the Chaldeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 1:8; Isaiah 45:1–2; Psalms 45:4"> I, even I, have spoken it, I have also called him: I have brought him, and he shall be prosperous on his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 18:20; John 20:21–22; John 3:34"> Come ye near unto me, hear ye this; never from the beginning have I spoken in secret; from the time that it occurred, was I there. And now the Lord Eternal hath sent me, and his Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 32:8; Psalms 25:12; Job 22:21–22"> Thus hath said the Lord, thy Redeemer, the Holy One of Israel, I am the Lord thy God who teach thee for thy profit, who lead thee by the way thou shouldst go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:29; Isaiah 66:12; Psalms 119:165"> Oh that thou hadst but listened to my commandments! then would have been as a river thy peace, and thy prosperity as the waves of the sea:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 10:22; Genesis 22:17; Romans 9:27"> And then would have been as the sand thy seed, and the offspring of thy body like the pebbles of the sea-shore; yet shall his name not be cut off nor destroyed from before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:45; Jeremiah 50:8; Isaiah 52:9"> Go forth out of Babylon, flee away from the Chaldeans, with the voice of singing declare, announce this, carry it forth as far as the end of the earth; say, The Lord hath redeemed his servant Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 17:6; Isaiah 43:19–20; Psalms 105:41"> And they thirsted not when he led them through the deserts; waters out of the rock he let drop down for them: and he cleaved the rock, and the waters gushed out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 57:21; Job 15:20–24; Luke 19:42"> There is no peace, saith the Lord, unto the wicked.</span>
