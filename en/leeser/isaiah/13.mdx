<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 47:1–15; Jeremiah 50:1–64; Isaiah 1:1"> The doom of Babylon, which Isaiah Amoz' son foresaw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:58; Isaiah 5:26; Jeremiah 50:2"> Upon a high mountain lift ye up a banner, raise high your voice unto them, wave the hand, that they may enter into the gates of the princes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joel 3:11; Jeremiah 51:20–24; Revelation 17:12–18"> I have charged my prepared ones, I have also called my heroes for my anger, those that rejoice in my highness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joel 3:14; Revelation 19:11–21; Joel 2:25"> There is a noise of tumult on the mountains, like that of a numerous people; a noise of shouting of kingdoms of nations assembled; the Lord of hosts mustereth a host of battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:20–46; Matthew 24:31; Jeremiah 50:9"> They are coming from a far-off country, from the end of the heavens, &#91;here is&#93; the Lord, with the weapons of his indignation, to destroy all the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 1:15; Isaiah 13:9; Amos 5:18"> Wail ye; for nigh is the day of the Lord; like wasting from the Almighty shall it come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:7; Nahum 2:10; Nahum 1:6"> Therefore all hands shall become weak, and every mortal's heart shall melt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 26:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Nahum 2:10"> And they shall be affrighted, pangs and pains shall seize on them; they shall have throes as a woman that travaileth; one at the other shall they look amazed; red like flames shall their faces glow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nahum 1:6; Malachi 4:1; Isaiah 13:15–18"> Behold, the day of the Lord cometh, direful, &#91;laden&#93; with wrath and the fierceness of anger, to render the earth desolate: and its sinners will he destroy out of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joel 2:31; Matthew 24:29; Mark 13:24"> For the stars of the heavens and their constellations shall not give forth their light: the sun shall be darkened in his going forth, and the moon shall not shed abroad her light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 2:11; Isaiah 5:15; Daniel 5:22–23"> And I will visit on the world its evil, and on the wicked their iniquity; and I will stop the arrogance of the presumptuous, and the haughtiness of the tyrants will I humble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 4:1; Isaiah 24:6; Job 28:16"> I will make the mortal more precious than fine gold; and man, more than the valued metal of Ophir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 12:26–27; Joel 3:16; Matthew 24:29"> Therefore will I shake the heavens, and the earth shall start quaking out of her place, at the wrath of the Lord of hosts, and on the day of his fierce anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 22:17; Jeremiah 50:16; 1 Kings 22:36"> And the people shall be as the chased roe, and as flocks which no one gathereth up; every man to his own people shall they turn, and every man into his own land shall they flee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 14:19–22; Jeremiah 50:27; Isaiah 47:9–14"> Every one that is found shall be thrust through; and every one that is joined unto them shall fall by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nahum 3:10; Hosea 10:14; Psalms 137:8–9"> And their babes shall be dashed to pieces before their eyes: spoiled shall be their houses, and their wives ravished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Daniel 5:28–31; Jeremiah 51:11; Isaiah 41:25"> Behold, I will stir up against them the Medes; who will not regard silver, and who will not delight in gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 8:12; 2 Chronicles 36:17; Isaiah 13:16"> And their bows will dash young men to pieces; and on the fruit of the womb will they have no mercy; on children their eye will not look with pity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 4:30; Zephaniah 2:9; Jeremiah 51:41"> And &#91;thus&#93; shall Babylon, the glory of kingdoms, the proud ornament of the Chaldeans, become like the overthrow through God of Sodom and Gomorrah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:43; Isaiah 14:23; Jeremiah 50:3"> It shall not be inhabited for ever, and it shall not be dwelt in from generation to generation; nor shall the Arabian pitch there his tent; and shepherds shall not let their flocks rest there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 18:2; Isaiah 34:11–15"> But there shall rest the wild beasts of the desert; and their houses shall be full of owls; and ostriches shall dwell there, and evil spirits shall dance there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:33; Isaiah 25:2; Isaiah 35:7"> And jackals shall howl in her palaces, and monsters in the temples of pleasure; and near to come is her time, and her days shall not be extended.</span>
