<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:17; Acts 10:34; Matthew 19:12–30"> And let not say the son of the stranger, that joineth himself unto the Lord, saying, Surely the Lord will exclude me from his people; nor let the eunuch say, Behold, I am a dry tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 119:111; Luke 10:42; Isaiah 27:5"> For thus hath said the Lord concerning the eunuchs that keep my sabbaths, and make choice of what pleaseth me, and take hold of my covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 3:12; 1 John 3:1; Revelation 3:5"> I will indeed give unto them in my house and within my walls a place and a name, better than sons and daughters: an everlasting name will I give them, that shall not be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:5; Isaiah 56:2–4; Isaiah 58:13"> Also the sons of the stranger, that join themselves unto the Lord, to serve him, and to love the name of the Lord, to be unto him as servants, every one that keepeth the sabbath by not violating it, and those who take hold of my covenant:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 11:17; Isaiah 2:2–3; Malachi 1:11"> Even these will I bring to my holy mountain, and make them joyful in my house of prayer; their burnt-offerings and their sacrifices shall be accepted upon my altar; for my house shall be called a house of prayer for all the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:16; Isaiah 54:7; Isaiah 60:3–11"> Thus saith the Lord Eternal who gathereth the outcasts of Israel, I will yet gather &#91;others&#93; to him, beside his own gathered &#91;outcasts&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:9; Revelation 19:17–18; Isaiah 18:6"> All ye beasts of the field, come to devour, &#91;yea,&#93; all ye beasts in the forest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 29:10; Philippians 3:2; Isaiah 58:1"> His watchmen are all of them blind, they know nothing; they all are dumb dogs, they cannot bark; dreamers, lying down, loving to slumber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2–3; Ezekiel 13:19; Micah 3:11"> But the dogs are of a greedy disposition, they know not how to be satisfied; and those are shepherds that know how to understand: they all turn to their own way, every one after his gain, from all quarters.</span>
