<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 4:15–16; 2 Kings 15:29; Isaiah 8:22"> (8:23) For no fatigue &#91;befalleth&#93; him that oppresseth it; in the first time he made light of the land of Zebulun, and the land of Naphtali, and at the last he will deal hard, with the way by the sea, on the other side of the Jordan, &#91;up to&#93; the Galilee of the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 4:16; Ephesians 5:8; John 8:12"> (9:1) The people that walk in darkness have seen a great light: they that dwell in the land of the shadow of death—a light shineth brightly over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 35:10; Psalms 119:162; Isaiah 26:15"> (9:2) Thou hast multiplied the nation, made great their joy: they rejoice before thee as with the joy in harvest, as men are glad when they divide the spoil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:8; Isaiah 14:25; Isaiah 10:26–27"> (9:3) For the yoke of their burden, and the staff on their shoulder, the rod of their oppressor, hast thou broken, as on the day of Midian.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:8; Psalms 46:9; Isaiah 30:33"> (9:4) For all the weapons of the fighter in the battle's tumult, and the garment rolled in blood, shall be burnt, become food for fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 2:11; Matthew 28:18; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> (9:5) For a child is born unto us, a son hath been given unto us, and the government is placed on his shoulders; and his name is called, Wonderful, counsellor of the mighty God, of the everlasting Father, the prince of peace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 1:32–33; Daniel 2:44; Daniel 7:14"> (9:6) For &#91;promoting&#93; the increase of the government, and for peace without end, upon the throne of David and upon his kingdom, to establish it and to support it through justice and righteousness, from henceforth and unto eternity: the zeal of the Lord of hosts will do this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 8:4–8; Zechariah 1:6; Zechariah 5:1–4"> (9:7) A word hath the Lord sent against Jacob, and it hath alighted upon Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 46:12; 1 Peter 5:5; Ezekiel 7:27"> (9:8) And experience it shall all the people together, Ephraim and the inhabitants of Samaria; because they say, in the pride and haughtiness of heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Malachi 1:4; 1 Kings 10:27; 1 Kings 7:9–12"> (9:9) “The bricks are fallen down, but with hewn stones will we re-build: the sycamores are cut down, but with cedars will we replace them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 8:4–7; Isaiah 17:1–5; Isaiah 7:8"> (9:10) Therefore will the Lord strengthen the adversaries of Rezin above him, and will stir up his enemies;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 5:25; 2 Chronicles 28:18; Psalms 79:7"> (9:11) The Syrians in front, and the Philistines behind; and they shall devour Israel with a full mouth. For all this his anger is not turned away, but his hand still remaineth stretched out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 7:10; Isaiah 31:1; Jeremiah 5:3"> (9:12) And the people return not unto him that smiteth them, and the Lord of hosts do they not seek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 19:15; Revelation 18:8; Hosea 1:6"> (9:13) And the Lord cutteth off from Israel head and tail, palm-branch and rush, on one day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 29:10; Matthew 24:24; 1 Samuel 9:6"> (9:14) The ancient and honorable is the head; and the prophet that teacheth lies is the tail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 15:14; Isaiah 3:12; Matthew 23:16–36"> (9:15) And the guides of this people are those that guide to error; and those that are led of them are misled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:21; Isaiah 10:6; Isaiah 5:25"> (9:16) Therefore will the Lord have no joy in their young men, and on their fatherless and widows will he have no mercy; for every one is a hypocrite and an evil-doer, and every mouth speaketh scandalous words. For all this his anger is not turned away, but his hand still remaineth stretched out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 83:14; Malachi 4:1; Isaiah 5:24"> (9:17) For wickedness burneth like the fire; it shall devour the briers and thorns: and it shall kindle &#91;a flame&#93; in the thickets of the forest, and they shall be shrouded by the wreaths of smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Micah 7:2; Micah 7:6; Joel 2:2"> (9:18) Through the fury of the Lord of hosts is the land scorched, and the people have become as food for the fire; no man will spare his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 49:26; Jeremiah 19:9; Lamentations 4:10"> (9:19) And he snatcheth on the right hand, and is &#91;yet&#93; hungry; and he eateth on the left hand, and are not &#91;yet&#93; satisfied; they shall eat every man the flesh of his own arm:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 5:25; Galatians 5:15; Isaiah 9:12"> (9:20) Menasseh &#91;against&#93; Ephraim: and Ephraim &#91;against&#93; Menasseh; and they together against Judah. For all this his anger is not turned away, but his hand still remaineth stretched out.</span>
