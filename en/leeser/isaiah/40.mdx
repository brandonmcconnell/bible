<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:4; Isaiah 51:12; Isaiah 51:3"> Comfort ye, comfort ye my people, saith your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 9:12; Isaiah 61:7; Hosea 2:14"> Speak ye &#91;comfort&#93; to the heart of Jerusalem, and call out unto her, that her time of sorrow is accomplished, that her iniquity is atoned for; for she hath received from the hand of the Lord double for all her sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Mark 1:2–5; John 1:23"> A voice calleth out, In the wilderness make ye clear the way of the Lord, make straight in the desert a highway for God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 3:5; Ezekiel 21:26; Luke 18:14"> Every valley shall be raised, and every mountain and hill shall be made low; and the crooked shall be made a straight path, and the rough places a plain:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:14; Joel 2:28; Isaiah 52:10"> And the glory of the Lord shall be revealed; and all flesh shall see it together; for the mouth of the Lord hath spoken it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 103:15–16; 1 Peter 1:24–25; James 1:10–11"> A voice saith, Proclaim; and he saith, What shall I proclaim? all flesh is grass, and all its goodliness is as the flower of the field:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 41:21"> The grass withereth, the flower fadeth; because the breath of the Lord hath blown upon it; surely the people is grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 13:31; 1 Peter 1:25; Matthew 24:35"> The grass withereth, the flower fadeth; but the word of our God will stand firm for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7–8; Isaiah 12:2; Isaiah 25:9"> Upon a high mountain get thee up, thou that bringest good tidings to Zion; lift up with strength thy voice, thou who bringest good tidings to Jerusalem; lift it up, be not afraid; say unto the cities of Judah, Behold, &#91;here is&#93; your God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 22:12; Isaiah 62:11; Revelation 17:14"> Behold, the Lord Eternal will come with might, and his arm ruleth for him: behold, his reward is with him, and his recompense before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 7:17; Micah 5:4; Psalms 23:1–6"> Like a shepherd will he feed his flock: with his arm will he gather the lambs, and in his bosom will he carry them, will he lead gently those that suckle their young.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 30:4; Hebrews 1:10–12; Psalms 102:25–26"> Who hath measured in the hollow of his hand the waters, and meted out the heavens with the span, and comprised in a measure the dust of the earth, and weighed in the scale-beam the mountains, and the hills in balances?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:16; Romans 11:34; Job 21:22"> Who hath meted out the Spirit of the Lord? and &#91;who was&#93; his counsellor that he could have given him information?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 21:22; Colossians 2:3; 1 Corinthians 12:4–6"> With whom took he counsel, that he gave him understanding, and taught him the path of justice, and taught him knowledge, and caused him to know the way of understanding?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 40:22; Job 34:14–15; Jeremiah 10:10"> Behold, nations are as a drop out of a bucket, and as the small dust of the balance are they accounted: behold, isles are like the flying dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Micah 6:6–7; Psalms 40:6; Psalms 50:9–12"> And Lebanon is not sufficient for burning, and its beasts do not suffice for burnt-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 62:9; Daniel 4:34–35; Isaiah 29:7"> All the nations are as naught before him; less than nothing, and vanity are they accounted to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 46:5; 1 Samuel 2:2; Isaiah 40:25"> To whom then will ye liken God? or what likeness will ye compare unto him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:9; Habakkuk 2:18–19; Isaiah 46:6–7"> The graven image the artificer hath cast and the goldsmith hath overspread it with gold, and fabricated &#91;on it&#93; silver chains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:3–4; Isaiah 41:7; Isaiah 46:7"> He that is skilled in the choice chooseth a wood that will not rot; he seeketh unto himself a skilful workman to prepare a graven image, that shall not be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 48:13; Psalms 50:6; Acts 14:17"> Know ye not? hath it not been told you from the beginning? have ye not paid attention to the foundations of the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 8:27; Psalms 104:2; Isaiah 42:5"> &#91;It is he&#93; that dwelleth above the circle of the earth, while its inhabitants are as grasshoppers; that stretched out the heavens as a curtain, and spreadeth them out as a tent to dwell in;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 12:21; Psalms 107:40; Isaiah 34:12"> That bringeth princes to naught; rendering the judges of the earth as vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 41:16; Isaiah 17:13; Haggai 1:9"> Yes, they were not yet planted; yea, they were not yet sown; yea, their stem had not yet taken root in the earth: when he but breathed upon them, and they withered, and the storm-wind carrieth them away as stubble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 40:18; Deuteronomy 4:33; Deuteronomy 4:15–18"> To whom then will ye liken me, that I should be equal to? saith the Holy One.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 1:16–17; Jeremiah 32:17–19; Isaiah 48:13"> Lift up your eyes on high, and see who hath created these? he that bringeth out their host by number; that calleth them all by name; from the Mighty One not one escapeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 18:7–8; Isaiah 49:4; Isaiah 49:14"> Why wilt thou say, O Jacob, and speak, O Israel, My way is hidden from the Lord, and my cause hath passed from the cognizance of my God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 11:33–34; Psalms 147:5; Isaiah 55:8–9"> Dost thou not know? hast thou not heard? The God of everlasting is the Lord, is the Creator of the ends of the earth; he will not be faint and weary; unsearchable is his understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Philippians 4:13; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> He giveth to the faint strength; and to the powerless he imparteth much might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Amos 2:14; Psalms 34:10; Psalms 33:16"> Though youths should grow faint and be weary, and young men should utterly stumble:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25–26; 2 Corinthians 4:16; Psalms 27:13–14"> Yet they that wait upon the Lord shall acquire new strength, they shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run and not be weary, they shall walk, and not become faint.</span>
