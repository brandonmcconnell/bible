<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 25:2; Zechariah 9:1; Amos 1:3–5"> The doom of Damascus. Behold, Damascus is deprived of being a city, and it shall become decaying ruins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:5; Jeremiah 7:33; Numbers 32:34"> Forsaken are the cities of 'Aro'er: they shall be &#91;given up&#93; to flocks, which shall lie down, and none shall make them afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 9:11; Isaiah 8:4; Isaiah 7:16"> And there shall cease the fortress from Ephraim, and the kingdom from Damascus, and the remnant of Syria: like the glory of the children of Israel shall they be, saith the Lord of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 10:16; Deuteronomy 32:15–27; Isaiah 24:13"> And it shall come to pass on that day, that the glory of Jacob shall vanish, and the fatness of his flesh shall become lean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 13:30; Joel 3:13; Jeremiah 51:33"> And it shall be as when the harvestman gathereth the standing-corn, and reapeth the ears with his arm; and it shall be as when one gleaneth ears in the valley of Rephaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 24:13; Deuteronomy 4:27; Romans 9:27"> And there shall be left on it gleaning-fruit, as one shaketh an olive-tree, two or three berries on the top of the uppermost bough, four or five on the outmost branches of a fruitful tree, saith the Lord the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Micah 7:7; Hosea 6:1; Hosea 3:5"> On that day shall a man turn his regard up to his Maker, and his eyes shall look toward the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 30:22; Isaiah 27:9; Isaiah 2:8"> And he shall not turn his regard to the altars, the work of his hands; and he shall not look at what his fingers have made, both the groves and the sun-images.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 3:11–15; Isaiah 27:10; Isaiah 24:1–12"> On that day shall his strong cities be as a forsaken ruin in a forest, and on a mountain-peak, which they left because of the children of Israel: and the land shall be desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 106:21; Deuteronomy 32:18; Isaiah 26:4"> Because thou hast forgotten the God of thy salvation, and the rock of thy strength thou hast not remembered; therefore wouldst thou plant luxuriant plantings, and wouldst set out the shoots of the stranger therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 4:8; Hosea 8:7; Galatians 6:7–8"> &#91;Already&#93; on the day that thou plantedst thou causedst to grow, and in the morning thou madest thy seed to blossom; but now fleeth the harvest on the day of disease and of incurable pain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:23; Luke 21:25; Psalms 18:4"> Woe to the raging of many people, who rage like the raging of the seas; and to the noise of nations, that make a noise like the roar of mighty waters!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 9:5; Psalms 1:4; Isaiah 41:15–16"> The nations will make a noise like the roaring of many waters; but He will rebuke it, and it shall flee afar off, and shall be chased as the chaff of the mountains before the wind, and as thistle-down before the tempest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 19:35; Proverbs 22:23; Jeremiah 13:25"> At eveningtide, behold, there is trouble; before yet it is morning it is no more. This is the portion of our spoilers, and the lot of those that plunder us.</span>
