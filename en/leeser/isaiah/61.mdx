<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 4:18–19; Luke 7:22; Isaiah 42:7"> The Spirit of the Lord Eternal is upon me; because the Lord hath anointed me to announce good tidings unto the meek: he hath sent me to bind up the broken-hearted, to proclaim to captives Liberty, and to prisoners Release;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 5:4; Isaiah 34:8; Luke 4:19"> To proclaim a year of favor of the Lord, and the day of vengeance of our God, to comfort all mourners;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 30:11; Jeremiah 17:7–8; John 16:20"> To grant unto the mourners of Zion,—to give unto them ornament in the place of ashes, oil of gladness in the place of mourning, garments of praise in the place of a grieved spirit; that they may be called, Oaks of righteousness, the planting of the Lord, that he may be glorified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 58:12; Ezekiel 36:33–36; Amos 9:14–15"> And they shall build up the ancient ruins, desolate places of former times shall they raise up, and they shall renew ruined cities, the desolate places of many generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 60:10–14; Isaiah 14:1–2; Ephesians 2:12–20"> And strangers shall stand and feed your flocks, and the sons of the alien shall be your ploughmen and your vintners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 19:6; 1 Peter 2:5; 1 Peter 2:9"> But ye, ye shall be called, Priests of the Lord; Ministers of our God, shall be said unto you: the wealth of nations shall ye consume, and in their glory shall ye be placed as possessors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 42:10; Zechariah 9:12; Isaiah 40:2"> In the place of your twofold shame,—and the confusion of which they loudly complained as their portion: therefore in their land shall they possess a twofold &#91;portion&#93;; everlasting joy shall be granted unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 11:7; Jeremiah 9:24; Zechariah 8:16–17"> For I the Lord love justice, I hate robbery with burnt-offering: therefore will I give them the recompense of their work in truth, and an everlasting covenant will I make with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 44:3; Psalms 115:14; Genesis 22:18"> And among the nations shall their seed be known, and their offspring in the midst of the people: all that see them shall acknowledge them, that they are the seed whom the Lord hath blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 19:7–8; Psalms 132:9; Psalms 132:16"> I will be greatly glad in the Lord, my soul shall be joyful in my God; for he hath clothed me with the garments of salvation, with the mantle of righteousness hath he enveloped me, as a bridegroom decketh himself with elegant attire, and as a bride adorneth herself with her bridal array.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 4:26–32; Isaiah 58:11; 1 Peter 2:9"> For, as the earth bringeth forth her growth, and as a garden causeth what is sown therein to spring forth: thus will the Lord Eternal cause righteousness and praise to spring forth in the presence of all the nations.</span>
