<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 15:37; 2 Kings 16:1; Isaiah 1:1"> And it came to pass in the days of Achaz the son of Jotham, the son of 'Uzziyahu, the king of Judah, that Rezin the king of Syria, and Pekach the son of Remalyahu, the king of Israel, went up toward Jerusalem to war against it; but were not able to make an attack upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 8:12; Isaiah 7:13; Isaiah 9:9"> And it was told unto the house of David, saying, Syria is encamped with Ephraim; and his heart trembled, with the heart of his people, as the trees of the forest are shaken before the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 18:17; Isaiah 36:2; Romans 9:27"> And the Lord said unto Isaiah, Go forth now to meet Achaz, thou with Shear-yashub thy son, to the end of the aqueduct of the upper pool, on the highway of the washer's field;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 35:4; Lamentations 3:26; Deuteronomy 20:3"> And thou shalt say unto him, Take heed, and be quiet; fear not, and let thy heart not become faint because of these two stumps of smoking firebrands, before the fierce anger of Rezin with Syria, and of the son of Remalyahu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 83:3–4; Zechariah 1:15; Psalms 2:2"> Forasmuch as Syria, &#91;with&#93; Ephraim and the son of Remalyahu, have taken evil counsel against thee, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Let us go up against Judah, and besiege it, and let us make a breach therein for us, and set up as king in the midst of it the son of Tabeal:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 8:10; Lamentations 3:37; Isaiah 46:10–11"> Thus hath said the Lord Eternal, It shall not succeed, and it shall not come to pass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 17:1–3; Genesis 14:15; 2 Samuel 8:6"> For the head of Syria is Damascus, and the head of Damascus is Rezin: and within sixty and five years shall Ephraim be broken, to be no more a people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 11:6; Romans 11:20; 2 Chronicles 20:20"> And the head of Ephraim is Samaria, and the head of Samaria is Remalyahu's son. If ye will not believe, surely ye shall not have permanence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 8:5; Isaiah 1:5; Isaiah 10:20"> And the Lord continued to speak unto Achaz, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 37:30; Isaiah 38:7–8; Isaiah 38:22"> Ask thee a sign from the Lord thy God; ask it in the depth, or high up above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31; 2 Chronicles 28:22; Malachi 3:15"> But Achaz said, I will not ask, and I will not tempt the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 43:24; Isaiah 63:10; Ezekiel 34:18"> And he said, Hear ye now, O house of David! Is it too little for you to weary men, that ye will weary also my God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 1:23; Isaiah 9:6; Luke 1:31"> Therefore will the Lord himself give you a sign: behold, this young woman shall conceive, and bear a son, and she shall call his name 'Immanu-el, &#91;God with us&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 7:22; Amos 5:15; Matthew 3:4"> Cream and honey shall he eat, so soon as he knoweth to refuse the evil, and to choose the good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 8:4; Deuteronomy 1:39; Isaiah 9:11"> For before yet the child shall know to refuse the evil, and to choose the good, shall be forsaken the land, of the kings of which thou feelest dread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–8; Isaiah 10:5–6; 2 Chronicles 28:19–21"> The Lord will bring over thee, and over thy people, and over thy father's house, days that have not come, from the day that Ephraim withdrew from Judah,—the king of Assyria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 5:26; Joshua 24:12; Deuteronomy 1:44"> And it shall come to pass on that day, that the Lord will call for the fly that is in the uttermost end of the streams of Egypt, and for the bee that is in the land of Assyria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 2:19; Jeremiah 16:16; Isaiah 2:21"> And they shall come, and shall encamp, all of them, in the desolate valleys, and in the clefts of the rocks, and upon all thorn-hedges, and upon all bushes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7; Ezekiel 29:18; Isaiah 10:15"> On the same day will the Lord shave with the razor that is hired, from among those on the other side of the river, with the king of Assyria, the head, and the hair of the feet: and also the beard shall it entirely remove.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 5:17; Jeremiah 39:10; Isaiah 37:30"> And it shall come to pass on that day, that a man shall nourish &#91;but&#93; one young cow, and two sheep;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 7:15; Matthew 3:4; 2 Samuel 17:29"> And it shall happen, that for the abundance of milk which they shall give he shall eat cream; for cream and honey shall eat every one that is left in the midst of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 5:6; Song of Solomon 8:11–12; Matthew 21:33"> And it shall come to pass on that day, that every place, where there are &#91;now&#93; a thousand vines worth a thousand silver shekels, shall be,—yea, this shall be &#91;given up&#93; to briers and thorns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 27:3"> With arrows and with bows shall men enter thither; because all the land shall become &#91;covered with&#93; briers and thorns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 7:21–22; Isaiah 13:20–22; Isaiah 5:17"> And all mountains that are worked with the mattock, there shall not come thither the fear of briers and thorns: and they shall serve for the pasture of oxen, and for the treading of sheep.</span>
