<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 8:18; Numbers 14:34; Ezekiel 4:5–6"> And the Lord said, Just as my servant Isaiah hath walked naked and barefoot three years as a sign and token for Egypt and for Cush:</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 30:5; Isaiah 30:3; 2 Kings 18:21"> And they shall be terrified, and ashamed of Cush their trust, and of Egypt their vaunt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 23:33; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Hebrews 2:3"> And the inhabitant of this isle shall say on that day, Behold, such is our trust, whither we fled for help to be delivered from the king of Assyria: and how shall we then escape?</span>
