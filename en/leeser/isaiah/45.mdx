<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:20–24; Isaiah 44:28; Isaiah 42:6"> Thus hath said the Lord to his anointed, to Cyrus, whom I have taken hold of by his right hand, to subdue nations before him, even the loins of kings will I ungird, to open before him &#91;city-&#93;doors, and gates that they shall not be shut;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 40:4; Luke 3:5; Isaiah 42:16"> I myself will go before thee, and proud eminences will I level: doors of brass will I break in pieces, and bolts of iron will I cut asunder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 48:15; Isaiah 43:1; Exodus 33:17"> And I will give unto thee the treasures of darkness, and riches hidden in secret places; in order that thou mayest know that I am the Lord, who call thee by thy name, the God of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 41:8–9; Acts 17:23; Isaiah 44:1"> For the sake of my servant Jacob, and Israel my elect; and I have called thee by thy name: I have designated thee, though thou hast not known me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 44:8; Isaiah 44:6; Isaiah 46:9"> I am the Lord, and there is none else, beside me there is no god; I assisted thee, though thou hast not known me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 1:11; Isaiah 45:5; Psalms 46:10"> In order that they may know from the rising of the sun, and from its setting, that there is nothing without me. I am the Lord, and there is no one else;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:13–14; Amos 3:6; Psalms 75:7"> Forming the light, and creating darkness; making peace, and creating evil: I the Lord do all these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hosea 10:12; Isaiah 61:11; Psalms 72:6"> Drop down ye heavens, and let the skies distil blessing; let the earth open and let them &#91;all&#93; be fruitful of prosperity, and let righteousness spring up likewise: I the Lord have created it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; Romans 9:20–21; Isaiah 10:15"> Woe unto him that contendeth with the one who formed him—a potsherd among the potsherds of the earth. Shall the clay say to him that fashioneth it, What makest thou? or thy work, He hath no hands?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:16; Hebrews 12:9; Malachi 1:6"> Woe unto him that saith unto &#91;his&#93; father, What begettest thou? or to the woman, What bringest thou forth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:3; Isaiah 48:17; Mark 11:24"> Thus hath said the Lord, the Holy One of Israel, and he who hath formed him, About events to come will you ask me? concerning my sons, and concerning the work of my hands will ye command me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 42:5; Isaiah 44:24; Jeremiah 27:5"> &#91;When&#93; I myself have made the earth, and created man upon it; &#91;when&#93; I, even my hands, have stretched out the heavens, and I have ordained all their host.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 41:2; Isaiah 13:17; Isaiah 41:25"> I myself have waked him up in righteousness, and all his ways will I make straight: he shall build my city, and my exiles shall he dismiss free, not for purchase-money nor for presents, saith the Lord of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; 1 Corinthians 14:25; Jeremiah 16:19"> Thus hath said the Lord, The labor of Egypt, and the merchandise of Ethiopia, and of the Sabeans, men of high stature, shall pass over unto thee, and thine shall they be: behind thee shall they walk; in chains shall they pass along, and unto thee shall they bow, unto thee shall they pray, &#91;saying&#93; Yea, only among thee is God; and there is no one else beside God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 8:17; Psalms 44:24; Isaiah 57:17"> Verily thou art a God that hidest thyself, O God of Israel, the Saviour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 44:9; Isaiah 44:11; Isaiah 42:17"> They are ashamed, and also confounded, all of them: together shall they go to confusion that are makers of idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 10:11; Psalms 25:3; Isaiah 54:4"> &#91;But&#93; Israel shall be helped by the Lord with an everlasting salvation: ye shall not be ashamed and not be confounded unto all eternity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 115:16; Jeremiah 10:12; Isaiah 45:12"> For thus hath said the Lord the creator of the heavens; he, the God that formed the earth and made it; he that hath established it,—not for naught did he create it, to be inhabited did he form it: I am the Lord; and there is no one else.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 48:16; Amos 5:4; Numbers 23:19–20"> Not on a secret spot have I spoken, in a dark place of the earth; I said not unto the seed of Jacob, Seek ye me for naught; &#91;but&#93; I the Lord speak righteousness, I declare things that are right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:5; Isaiah 44:17–20; Isaiah 43:9"> Assemble yourselves and come; draw near together, ye escaped fugitives of the nations! They have no knowledge that carry the wood of their graven image, and pray unto a god that cannot save.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 45:5; Isaiah 43:11; Isaiah 44:7–8"> Tell ye, and bring them near; yea, let them take counsel together: Who hath announced this in ancient times? told it from the beginning? is it not I the Lord? and there is no other god without me, a just god and a saviour; there is none beside me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 6:40; Micah 7:7; Hebrews 12:2"> Turn unto me, so that ye may be helped, all ye ends of the earth; for I am God, and there is no one else.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 2:10; Romans 14:10–12; Isaiah 55:11"> By myself have I sworn, righteousness is gone out of my mouth, a word &#91;which&#93; shall not return, That unto me every knee shall bend, every tongue shall swear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 41:11; Isaiah 54:17; Ephesians 6:10"> Only in the Lord,—shall men say of me,—there are righteousness and strength. Unto him shall come and be ashamed all that are incensed against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 41:16; Galatians 3:27–29; 1 Corinthians 6:11"> In the Lord shall be justified, and shall glory themselves all the seed of Israel.</span>
