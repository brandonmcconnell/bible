<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 15:2; Psalms 118:28; Numbers 23:19"> O Lord, my God art thou; I will exalt thee, I will praise thy name; for thou hast done wonderful things, resolves of distant times &#91;are become&#93; faithful confirmation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 17:1; Isaiah 13:22; Isaiah 25:12"> For thou hast made of a city a stone-heap; of a fortified town a falling ruin; the palace of barbarians ceaseth out of the city, to eternity shall it not be rebuilt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 14:9; Isaiah 49:23–26; Psalms 46:10–11"> Therefore shall a strong people honor thee; the town of the tyrannical nations shall fear thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:12; Isaiah 32:2; James 2:5"> For thou hast become a stronghold to the poor, a stronghold to the needy when he is distressed; a protection from the tempest, a shadow from the heat; for the &#91;wrathful&#93; breath of the tyrants is like the tempest against a wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 20:8–9; Isaiah 49:10; Psalms 105:39"> Like heat in a dry land, wilt thou subdue the tumult of the barbarians; as the heat &#91;is lessened&#93; by the shadow of the cloud, so will he subdue the song of the tyrants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 8:11; Psalms 63:5; Zechariah 9:16–17"> And the Lord of hosts will make unto all the nations on this mountain a feast of fat things, a feast of wines on the lees, of fat things full of marrow, of wines on the lees well refined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 4:18; Isaiah 60:1–3; Matthew 27:51"> And he will destroy on this mountain the face of the covering which covereth all the people, and the vail that is spread over all the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; Revelation 7:17; 1 Corinthians 15:54"> He will destroy death to eternity: and the Lord Eternal will wipe away the tear from off all faces; and the shame of his people will he remove from off all the earth; for the Lord hath spoken it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 27:14; Genesis 49:18; Micah 7:7"> And men will say on that day, Lo, this is our God, for whom we have waited that he would help us; this is the Lord for whom we have waited, we will be glad and we will rejoice in his salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 25:6; Isaiah 18:4; Zephaniah 2:9"> For the hand of the Lord will rest on this mountain, and Moab shall be trodden down on his own place, even as straw is trodden down upon the dunghill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 16:14; Isaiah 2:11; Isaiah 16:6"> And he will spread forth his hands in the midst thereof, as the swimmer spreadeth them forth to swim: and he will bring down his pride together with the joints of his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 26:5; Revelation 18:21; Isaiah 14:23"> And the fortress of the stronghold of thy walls he bringeth down, layeth low, casteth it to the ground, even to the dust.</span>
