<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 12:12; Revelation 1:7; Joshua 2:11"> The doom of Egypt. Behold, the Lord rideth upon a swift cloud, and is coming to Egypt: and the idols of Egypt shall be moved because of his presence, and the heart of Egypt shall melt in its inward parts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 7:22; 1 Samuel 14:20; Matthew 10:36"> And I will stir up Egyptians against Egyptians: and they shall fight every one against his brother, and every one against his fellow; city against city, kingdom against kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 8:19; Daniel 2:2; 1 Chronicles 10:13"> And the spirit of Egypt shall be emptied out in its inward parts, and its counsel will I frustrate; and they will inquire of the idols, and of the charmers, and of those that have familiar spirits, and of the wizards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4; Jeremiah 46:26; Ezekiel 29:19"> And I will surrender the Egyptians into the hand of a cruel lord; and a rigorous king shall rule over them, saith the Lord, the Eternal of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:36; Ezekiel 30:12; Zechariah 14:18"> And the waters shall fail from the sea, and the river shall be wasted and dried up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 37:25; Exodus 7:18; 2 Kings 19:24"> And the rivers shall become foul; and shallow and dried up shall become the deep streams: reeds and flags shall wither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 32:20; Joel 1:17–18; Isaiah 23:3"> The well-rooted plants by the stream, by the mouth of the stream, and every thing sown by the stream, shall dry up, be scattered &#91;by the wind,&#93; and be no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:10; Habakkuk 1:15; Numbers 11:5"> The fishermen also shall lament, and all they that cast an angle into the stream shall mourn; and they that spread nets upon the face of the waters shall languish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:7; Proverbs 7:16; 1 Kings 10:28"> And ashamed shall be they that work in fine flax, and they that weave white cloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:10; Exodus 7:19; Exodus 8:5"> And its foundations shall be beaten down, all that build sluices shall be grieved in soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 13:22; Acts 7:22; 1 Kings 4:30"> Surely fools are the princes of Zo'an, !!the wise of the counsellors of Pharaoh &#91;impart&#93; silly counsel: how can ye say unto Pharaoh, I am a son of the wise, a son of the ancient kings?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:20; Isaiah 14:24; Judges 9:38"> Where are they, these, thy wise men? that they may tell thee now, that they know what the Lord of hosts hath resolved on over Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:13; Jeremiah 2:16; Jeremiah 46:14"> The princes of Zo'an are become fools, deceived are the princes of Noph; and Egypt is led astray by the chiefs of its tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:11; 1 Kings 22:20–23; Ezekiel 14:7–9"> The Lord hath poured out in the midst thereof a spirit of perverseness: and they have led Egypt astray in all its work, as a drunkard reeleth astray in his vomit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 9:14–15; 1 Thessalonians 4:11–12; Proverbs 14:23"> And there shall not be for Egypt &#91;successful&#93; work, which the head or tail, palm-branch or rush, may do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:30; Nahum 3:13; Isaiah 11:15"> On that day shall Egypt be like the women: and it shall tremble and be in dread because of the waving of the hand of the Lord of hosts, which he waveth over it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 14:24; Daniel 4:35; Ezekiel 29:6–7"> And the land of Judah shall become unto Egypt a terror, every one that maketh mention thereof shall be in dread, because of the counsel of the Lord of hosts, which he hath resolved against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:9; Zechariah 2:11; Isaiah 19:21"> On that day shall be five cities in the land of Egypt speaking the language of Canaan, and swearing by the Lord of hosts; “The city of destruction” shall one be called.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 28:18; Joshua 22:10; Joshua 22:26"> On that day shall there be all altar to the Lord in the midst of the land of Egypt, and a pillar at its border to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Titus 2:13; Isaiah 45:21–22"> And it shall be for a sign and for a testimony unto the Lord of hosts in the land of Egypt; for they shall cry unto the Lord because of the oppressors, and he will send them a helper, and a chief, and he shall deliver them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 11:9; Malachi 1:11; John 17:3"> And the Lord will be made known to the Egyptians, and the Egyptians shall know the Lord on that day, and will do service &#91;with&#93; sacrifice and oblation; yea, they will make vows unto the Lord and perform &#91;them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Hebrews 12:11; Hosea 14:1"> And the Lord will thus strike Egypt, striking and healing it: and they will return unto the Lord, and he will be entreated of them, and heal them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 11:16; Ephesians 2:18–22; Isaiah 35:8–10"> On that day there shall be a highway out of Egypt to Asshur, and Asshur shall come into Egypt, and the Egyptians into Asshur, and the Egyptians shall serve with Asshur &#91;the Lord&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 65:8; Genesis 12:2; Galatians 3:14"> On that day shall Israel be the third with Egypt and with Asshur, a blessing in the midst of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 29:23; Hosea 2:23; Romans 9:24–25"> Whom the Lord of hosts will have blessed, saying, Blessed be my people Egypt, and Asshur the work of my hands, and my heritage Israel.</span>
