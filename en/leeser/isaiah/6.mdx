<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 12:41; Revelation 15:8; Revelation 7:15–17"> In the year of king 'Uzziyahu's death I saw the Lord sitting upon a high and exalted throne, and his train filled the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 4:8; Ezekiel 1:11; Psalms 104:4"> Seraphim were standing around him, each one had six wings; with two he covered his face, and with two he covered his feet, and with two did he fly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 4:8–9; Psalms 72:19; Numbers 14:21"> And one called unto the other, and said, Holy, holy, holy is the Lord of hosts; the whole earth is full of his glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 15:8; Exodus 40:34; 2 Chronicles 5:13–1"> And the posts of the threshold shook at the voice of those that called aloud, and the house was filled with smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 5:8–9; James 3:6–10; Jeremiah 9:3–8"> And I said, Woe is me! for I am lost; because a man of unclean lips am I, and in the midst of a people of unclean lips do I dwell; for the King, the Lord of hosts have my eyes seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 8:3–5; Hebrews 9:22–26; Ezekiel 10:2"> Then flew unto me one of the seraphim, and in his hand was a live coal, with the tongs had he taken it from off the altar:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:9; 1 John 1:7; Isaiah 43:25"> And he touched therewith upon my mouth, and said, Lo, this hath touched thy lips; and thy iniquity is departed, and thy sin is forgiven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 4:10–13; Acts 26:16–17; Ephesians 3:8"> And I heard the voice of the Lord, saying, Whom shall I send, and who will go for us? And I said, Here am I: send me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 13:14–15; Luke 8:10; Mark 4:12"> And he said, Go, and say unto this people, Hear indeed, but understand not; and see indeed, but know not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 13:15; Acts 28:27; Jeremiah 5:21"> Obdurate will remain the heart of this people, and their ears will be heavy, and their eyes will be shut: so that they will not see with their eyes, nor hear with their ears, nor their hearts be understanding, so that they be converted, and healing be granted them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 24:1–12; Psalms 94:3; Leviticus 26:31"> And I said, How long, O Lord? And he said, Until that cities be left waste without an inhabitant, and houses without man, and the soil be made desolate as a wilderness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:29; Deuteronomy 28:64; 2 Kings 25:11"> And the Lord will have removed far away the men, and the depopulation be great in the midst of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 14:7–9; Romans 11:5–6; Genesis 22:18"> And should a tenth part thereof yet remain, it will again be swept away: &#91;yet&#93; like the terebinth and the oak, which, when they cast their leaves, retain their stems, so remaineth the holy seed, its stem.</span>
