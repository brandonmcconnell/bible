<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 19:13; Isaiah 34:5–6; Psalms 137:7"> Who is this that cometh from Edom, dyed red in his garments from Bozrah? this—glorious in his apparel, moving along in the greatness of his strength? “I who speak in righteousness, mighty to save.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 19:13; Revelation 19:15"> Why is redness on thy apparel, and &#91;why are&#93; thy garments as of one that treadeth the wine-press?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 19:13–15; Revelation 14:19–20; Malachi 4:3"> “I have trodden the vat alone, and of the nations there was no man with me; and I trod them down in my anger, and I trampled on them in my fury; and their blood was sprinkled on my garments, and all my raiments have I stained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 34:8; Jeremiah 51:6; Isaiah 35:4"> For the day of vengeance was in my heart, and the year of my redeemed was come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 44:3; Isaiah 40:10; Psalms 98:1"> And I looked, and there was no one to help, and I was astonished, and there was no one to support; and then my own arm aided me, and my fury—this it was that upheld me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 14:10; Revelation 16:19; Isaiah 49:26"> And I stamped down nations in my anger, and I made them drunken with my fury, and brought down to the earth their victorious strength.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4; Psalms 86:5; Psalms 86:15"> The kindnesses of the Lord will I mention, the praises of the Lord, in accordance with all that the Lord hath bestowed on us, and the abundant goodness toward the house of Israel, which he hath bestowed on them according to his mercies, and the abundance of his kindnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 17:7; Isaiah 12:2; Exodus 6:7"> And he said, Surely they are my people, children that will not lie; and he became to them a Saviour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 33:14; Exodus 23:20–21; Malachi 3:1"> In all their affliction he was afflicted, and the angel of his presence saved them; in his love and in his pity he redeemed them; and he bore them, and he carried them all the days of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Ephesians 4:30; Psalms 78:56"> But they rebelled, and grieved his holy Spirit; and he changed himself to become their enemy, and he fought against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 11:17; Exodus 14:22; Psalms 77:20"> Then remembered his people the ancient days of Moses, Where is he that brought them up out of the sea with the shepherd of his flock? where is he that put within him his holy Spirit?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 15:16; Psalms 78:13; Joshua 3:16"> That displayed by the right hand of Moses his glorious arm; that divided the water before them, to make unto himself an everlasting name?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:9; Psalms 106:9; Habakkuk 3:15"> That led them through the deeps, as a horse through the wilderness, that they should not stumble?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 63:12; Hebrews 4:8–11; Joshua 23:1"> As a beast goeth down into the valley, so did the spirit of the Lord bring them to rest; thus didst thou guide thy people, to make unto thyself a glorious name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:20; Deuteronomy 26:15; Psalms 80:14"> Look down from heaven, and behold, from the habitation of thy holiness and of thy glory: where are thy zeal and thy mights, the yearning of thy bowels and of thy mercy which are now restrained from me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; Malachi 2:10; Jeremiah 3:19"> For thou art our father; for Abraham knoweth nothing of us, and Israel recogniseth us not: thou, O Lord, art our father, our Redeemer from everlasting is thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10; Joshua 11:20; Ezekiel 14:7–9"> Why hast thou let us go astray, O Lord, from thy ways, suffered our heart to be hardened against thy fear? Return for the sake of thy servants, the tribes of thy heritage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 74:3–7; Revelation 11:2; Deuteronomy 7:6"> But a brief space were thy holy people in possession, when our adversaries trod down thy sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 135:4; Acts 14:16; Romans 9:4"> We are become as though we are those over whom thou hast never ruled, over whom thy name hath not been called.</span>
