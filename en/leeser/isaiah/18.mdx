<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:10; 2 Kings 19:9; Zephaniah 2:12"> Woe to the land with spreading wings, which is beyond the rivers of Cush,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 18:7; Genesis 10:8–9; 2 Chronicles 16:8"> That sendeth on the sea ambassadors, and in vessels of bulrushes messengers over the face of the waters. Go, ye swift messengers, to a nation pulled and torn, to a people terrible from their beginning and onward; a nation meted out and trodden down, whose land the rivers have spoiled!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 5:26; Isaiah 26:11; Matthew 13:9"> All ye inhabitants of the world, and dwellers on the earth, when the ensign is lifted up on the mountains, see ye; and when the cornet is blown, hear ye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 5:15; Isaiah 26:21; Isaiah 18:7"> For so hath said the Lord unto me, I will take my rest, and I will look down on my dwelling-place like a clear heat upon herbs, and like a cloud of dew in the heat of harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 17:10–11; Song of Solomon 2:13; Ezekiel 17:6–10"> For before the harvest, when the blossom is past, and the flower becometh a ripening grape, will he both cut off the tendrils with pruning-knives, and the sprigs will he remove and cut down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:33; Ezekiel 39:17–20; Ezekiel 32:4–6"> They shall be left together unto the birds of prey of the mountains, and to the beasts of the earth: and the birds of prey shall summer upon them, and all the beasts of the earth shall winter upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:10; Acts 8:27–28; Zechariah 14:16–17"> At that time shall be brought as a present unto the Lord of hosts a people pulled and torn, and a people terrible from their beginning and onward; a nation meted out and trodden under foot, whose land the rivers have spoiled, to the place of the name of the Lord of hosts, the mount Zion.</span>
