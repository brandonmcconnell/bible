<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 2:1–3; Jeremiah 48:1–47; Isaiah 11:14"> The doom of Moab. Truly in a night is 'Ar of Moab plundered, it is laid waste; truly in a night is Kir of Moab plundered, it is laid waste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:1; Leviticus 21:5; Isaiah 3:24"> It goeth up to the &#91;idol-&#93;house, and Dibon &#91;goeth&#93; up to the high-places to weep, on Nebo and on Medeba shall Moab wail: on all its heads there is baldness, and every beard is hewn off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jonah 3:6–8; Jeremiah 19:13; Deuteronomy 22:8"> In its streets they are girded with sack-cloth, on its roofs, and in its public places every one shall wail, groan with weeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:34; 1 Kings 19:4; Jonah 4:8"> And loud crieth Cheshbon with El'aleh; as far as Yahaz is heard their voice: therefore the armed men of Moab shall howl; its soul is grieved for itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:5; Jeremiah 4:20; Luke 19:41–44"> My heart will cry for Moab, whose fugitives are as far as Zo'ar, &#91;and&#93; the third 'Eglarth; for the ascent of Luchith—with weeping is it ascended; for on the way to Choronayim they let resound the cry of defeat &#91;in battle&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 1:10–12; Isaiah 19:5–7; Numbers 32:36"> For the waters of Nimrim shall be desolate; for dry is the grass, gone are the herbs, and green things are no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:36; Nahum 2:12–13; Isaiah 10:6"> Therefore the rest of their acquisitions and what they possess shall they carry away over the brook of the willows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:10; Isaiah 15:2–5; Jeremiah 48:31–34"> For the cry hath encompassed the boundary of Moab; up to Eglayim &#91;is heard&#93; its wail, and at Beer-elim &#91;is heard&#93; its wail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 17:25; Leviticus 26:28; Leviticus 26:24"> For the waters of Dimon are filled with blood; for I will bring over Dimon armed bands; over the escaped of Moab &#91;cometh&#93; a lion, and over the remnant of the land.</span>
