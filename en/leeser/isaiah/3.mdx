<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 26:26; Ezekiel 14:13; Isaiah 1:24"> For, behold, the Lord, the Eternal of hosts, doth remove from Jerusalem and from Judah stay and staff, every stay of bread, and every stay of water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 9:14–15; 2 Kings 24:14–16; Lamentations 5:12–14"> The hero, and the man of war, the judge, and the prophet, and the prudent, and the ancient,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 8:18; Deuteronomy 1:15; Exodus 18:21"> The captain of fifty, and the honorable man, and the counsellor, and the skilful artificer, and the eloquent orator.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:16; 2 Chronicles 33:1; 1 Kings 3:7–9"> And I will set up boys as their princes, and children shall rule over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:3–8; Malachi 3:5; Matthew 27:28–30"> And so shall the people press man against man, and one against the other: the boy shall demean himself proudly against the ancient, and the base against the honorable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 4:1; John 6:15; Judges 11:6–8"> When a man will seize hold on his brother in the house of his father, &#91;saying,&#93; Thou hast a garment, thou shalt be our ruler, and let this ruin be under thy hand:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 5:13; Lamentations 2:13; Ezekiel 34:4"> He will swear on that day, saying, I will not be a chief; and in my house is neither bread nor clothing; you shall not appoint me a ruler of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:22; Ezekiel 9:9; Malachi 3:13–15"> For Jerusalem is sunk to decay, and Judah is fallen; because their tongue and their doings are against the Lord, to incense the eyes of his glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 13:13; Genesis 18:20–21; 2 Kings 9:30"> The boldness of their face testifieth against them; and like Sodom they tell openly their sin, they conceal it not. Woe unto their soul! for they have prepared evil unto themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 128:1–2; Hebrews 6:10; Ecclesiastes 8:12"> Say ye to the righteous, that he hath done well; for the fruit of their doings shall they eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:13; James 2:13; Psalms 62:12"> Woe unto the wicked who doeth evil; for the recompense of his hands shall be bestowed on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 3:4; Nahum 3:13; 2 Kings 11:1"> My people! their oppressors are children, and women rule over them. O my people! thy leaders cause thee to err, and the direction of thy paths they corrupt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Micah 6:2; Hosea 4:1–2; Proverbs 22:22–23"> The Lord is stepped forth to plead, and standeth up to judge the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:4; Psalms 143:2; Isaiah 3:2–3"> The Lord will enter into judgment with the ancients of his people, and their princes; but ye—ye have eaten up the vineyard; the plunder of the poor is in your houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Micah 3:2–3; Psalms 94:5; Amos 2:6–7"> What mean ye that ye crush my people, and grind down the faces of the poor? saith the Lord the Eternal of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 32:9–11; Ezekiel 16:49–50; Isaiah 4:4"> And the Lord said, Forasmuch as the daughters of Zion are proud, and walk with stretched forth necks and casting about their eyes, walking and mincing as they go, and making a tinkling with their feet:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:22; Micah 1:11; Revelation 16:2"> Therefore will the Lord smite with leprosy the crown of the head of the daughters of Zion, and the Lord will lay open their nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 8:21; Judges 8:26"> On that day will the Lord take away the beauty of their tinkling shoe-buckles, and the hair-nets, and the crescent-shaped ornaments,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:11; Numbers 31:50; Exodus 35:22"> The drops, and the bracelets, and the mufflers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 39:28; Genesis 35:4; Ezekiel 16:12"> The bonnets, and the foot-chains, and the head-bands, and the tablets, and the earrings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 24:47; 1 Peter 3:3–4; Luke 15:22"> The finger-rings, and nose-jewels,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The changeable suits of apparel, and the mantles, and the shawls, and the pockets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 38:8; Ruth 3:15; Ezekiel 16:10"> The mirrors, and the chemisettes, and the turbans, and the long vails.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Lamentations 2:10; Ezekiel 27:31; Isaiah 22:12"> And it shall come to pass, that instead of sweet smell there shall be corruption; and instead of a girdle a rope; and instead of curled hair baldness; and instead of a wide garment a girding of sackcloth, a mark of burning instead of beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:9; Isaiah 1:20; Amos 9:10"> Thy men shall fall by the sword, and thy mighty ones in the war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Lamentations 2:10; Jeremiah 14:2; Lamentations 1:4"> And then shall her gates lament and mourn; and stript of all shall she sit upon the ground.</span>
