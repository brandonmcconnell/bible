<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 12:1; Luke 20:9; John 15:1"> I will sing now for my beloved, the song of my dear one touching his vineyard. A vineyard had my beloved on a rich hill-top;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:21; Deuteronomy 32:32–33; Mark 11:13"> And he fenced it in, and cleared it of stones, and planted it with the choicest vines, and built a tower in its midst, and also a winepress he hewed out therein: and he hoped that it should bring forth grapes, and it brought forth worthless fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 2:5; Psalms 51:4; Micah 6:2–3"> And now, O inhabitants of Jerusalem, and men of Judah, judge, I pray you, between me and my vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 23:37; Micah 6:3–4; Jeremiah 2:5"> What more was to be done to my vineyard, that I had not done in it? Why then did I hope that it should bring forth grapes, while it brought forth worthless fruit?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Lamentations 1:15; Luke 21:24; Isaiah 28:18"> And now I will let you know also what I will do to my vineyard: I will take away its hedge, and it shall be eaten off; I will break down its wall, and it shall be trodden down;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 7:23–25; Jeremiah 25:11; Isaiah 24:1–3"> And I will lay it quite waste; it shall not be pruned, nor hoed around; and it shall be overgrown with briers and thorns; and the clouds will I command that they send down no rain upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 80:8–11; Jeremiah 12:10; James 5:4"> For the vineyard of the Lord of hosts is the house of Israel, and the men of Judah are the plant of his delight: and he hoped for justice, but behold injustice; for equity, but behold iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Micah 2:2; Habakkuk 2:9–12; Jeremiah 22:13–17"> Woe unto those that cause house to join on house, bring field near to field, till there is no more room, so that ye may be left alone as the inhabitants in the midst of the land!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 23:38; Isaiah 22:14; Amos 5:11"> In my ears &#91;spoke&#93; the Lord of hosts, Truly many houses shall become desolate, yea, great and beautiful ones without an inhabitant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 26:26; Haggai 2:16; Haggai 1:6"> Yea, ten acres of vineyard shall yield &#91;but&#93; one bath, and a chomer-seed shall yield &#91;but&#93; an ephah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 23:29–30; Romans 13:13; Proverbs 20:1"> Woe unto those that rise up early in the morning, that they may run after strong drink; that continue until late in the twilight, till wine inflame them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 21:11–14; Psalms 28:5; Amos 6:4–6"> And there are harp and psaltery, tambourine and flute, and wine at their drinking-feasts; but the deeds of the Lord they regard not, and the works of his hands they behold not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 4:6; Isaiah 1:3; Isaiah 27:11"> Therefore are my people led into exile, for want of knowledge: and their honorable men suffer of famine, and their multitude are panting with thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 30:16; Luke 21:34; Habakkuk 2:5"> Therefore hath the deep enlarged her desire, and opened her mouth without measure: and there descend &#91;Jerusalem's&#93; glory, and her multitude, and her noise, and whoever rejoiced therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 2:11; Isaiah 2:9; Jeremiah 5:4–5"> And bent down shall be the son of earth, and humbled shall be the man, and the eyes of the lofty shall be humbled;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 46:10; Isaiah 12:4; 1 Chronicles 29:11"> And the Lord of hosts shall be exalted in judgment, and the holy God shall be sanctified by &#91;displaying his&#93; righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:6; Zephaniah 2:14; Isaiah 7:25"> Then shall the sheep feed according to their wont, and the ruins of the fat ones shall sojourners eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:14; Ezekiel 13:22; Jeremiah 5:31"> Woe unto those that draw iniquity with the cords of falsehood, and as with a wagon-rope, sinfulness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:22; Jeremiah 17:15; Jeremiah 23:36"> That say, Let him make speed, let him hasten his work, that we may see it: and let draw nigh and come the counsel of the Holy One of Israel, that we may know it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 17:15; 2 Timothy 3:1–5; Luke 16:15"> Woe unto those that say of the evil it is good, and of the good it is evil; that put darkness for light, and light for darkness; that put bitter for sweet, and sweet for bitter!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:18–20; Proverbs 3:7; Romans 12:16"> Woe unto those that are wise in their own eyes, and intelligent in their own esteem!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 5:11; Habakkuk 2:15; Isaiah 28:7"> Woe unto those that are heroes to drink wine, and men of might to mingle strong drink;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 10:2; James 5:6; Psalms 94:21"> Who justify the wicked in lieu of a bribe, and who deprive the righteous of their right!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 30:12; Job 18:16; Joel 2:5"> Therefore as the tongue of the fire devoureth the stubble, and dry hay sinketh before the flame: so shall their root be as rotten things, and their blossom shall fly up as the dust; because they have despised the law of the Lord of hosts, and the word of the Holy One of Israel they have rejected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 10:4; Isaiah 9:21; Isaiah 9:17"> For this cause is kindled the anger of the Lord against his people, and he stretcheth forth his hand against them, and he smiteth them; and the mountains tremble, and their carcasses lie like sweepings in the midst of the streets: with all this his anger is not turned away, but still is his hand stretched out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Zechariah 10:8; Deuteronomy 28:49; Isaiah 7:18"> And he will lift up an ensign to the nations from afar, and will call for one of them from the end of the earth; and, behold, with speed, swiftly, it cometh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joel 2:7–8; Job 12:18; Job 12:21"> There is none weary, nor stumbling among its men; it slumbereth not, it sleepeth not; not loosened is the girdle of its loins, not broken is the latchet of its shoes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 45:5; Nahum 2:3–4; Ezekiel 21:9–11"> Whose arrows are sharpened, and all whose bows are bent; its horses hoofs are hard like the flint, and its wheels like the whirlwind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 42:22; Zechariah 11:3; Micah 5:8"> It hath a roar like the lioness, it roareth like the young lions: yea, it growleth, and layeth hold of the prey, and carrieth it safely off, with none to deliver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 8:22; Joel 2:10; Jeremiah 4:23–28"> And it will rage against them on that day like with the raging of the sea: and if one look unto the earth, behold, there is darkness, oppression, and the light is darkened through the darkness of its clouds.</span>
