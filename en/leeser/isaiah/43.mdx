<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 100:3; Isaiah 43:7; Zechariah 13:9"> But now thus hath said the Lord that created thee, O Jacob, and he that formed thee, O Israel, Fear not; for I have redeemed thee, I have called thee by thy name; mine art thou.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Joshua 1:9; Psalms 66:12"> Whenever thou passest through the waters, I am with thee; and through the rivers,—they shall not overflow thee: whenever thou walkest through the fire, thou shalt not be scorched; neither shall the flame burn on thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 60:16; Isaiah 45:15; 2 Chronicles 14:9–14"> For I am the Lord thy God, the Holy One of Israel, thy Saviour; I have given Egypt for thy ransom, Cush and Seba in place of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6–8; 1 Peter 2:9; Jeremiah 31:3"> Since thou art precious in my eyes, art honorable, and I indeed do love thee: therefore will I give men in place of thee, and nations instead of thy soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:3; Isaiah 49:12; Luke 13:29"> Fear not, for I am with thee; from the east will I bring thy seed, and from the west will I gather thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:17–18; Jeremiah 3:14; Jeremiah 3:18–19"> I will say to the north, Give up; and to the south, Withhold not: bring my sons from afar, and my daughters from the ends of the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; Isaiah 43:21; Ephesians 1:12"> Every one that is called by my name, and whom I have created for my glory; whom I have formed; yea, whom I have made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9; Ezekiel 12:2; Isaiah 42:18–20"> Bring forward the blind people that have eyes, and the deaf that have ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 46:10; Isaiah 44:7–9; Isaiah 41:21–26"> Let all the nations be gathered together, and let the people be assembled: who among them can announce this? and cause us to hear former things? let them bring forth their witnesses, that they may be justified: or let them hear, and say, It is truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 41:4; Isaiah 45:6; Isaiah 46:8–9"> Ye are my witnesses, saith the Lord, and my servant whom I have chosen: in order that ye may know and believe me, and understand, that I am he; before me there was no god formed, and after me there will be none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 13:4; Isaiah 44:6; Isaiah 45:21–22"> I, I am the Lord; and beside me there is no saviour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:12; Psalms 81:9–10; Isaiah 43:10"> I myself have announced it, and I have saved, and I have let it be heard, and there was no strange &#91;god&#93; among you; and ye are my witnesses, saith the Lord, and I am God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 14:27; Isaiah 46:10; Psalms 90:2"> Yea, from the &#91;first&#93; day am I he; and there is none that can deliver out of my hand: if I will work, is there one that can hinder it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; Isaiah 23:13; Jeremiah 51:24"> Thus hath said the Lord, the Holy One of Israel, For your sake did I send to Babylon, and in swift vessels brought I them all down, and the Chaldeans, in the ships of their joyful song.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 43:3; Matthew 25:34; Isaiah 48:17"> I am the Lord, the Holy One, the Creator of Israel, your King.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 51:10; Isaiah 43:2; Psalms 77:19"> Thus hath said the Lord, who maketh a way in the sea, and a path in the mighty waters;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 14:4–9; Isaiah 1:31; Ezekiel 38:8–18"> Who bringeth forth chariot and horse, army and power: together shall they lie down, they shall not rise up again; they are extinct, like a wick are they quenched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 65:17; 2 Corinthians 3:10; 1 Chronicles 16:12"> Remember not the former things, and ancient events regard no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 48:6; Isaiah 42:9; 2 Corinthians 5:17"> Behold, I will do a new thing; now shall it spring forth; will ye not acknowledge it? I will even make in the wilderness a way, and in the desert rivers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 41:17; Isaiah 48:21; Isaiah 49:10"> The beasts of the field shall honor me, the monsters and the ostriches; because I give waters in the wilderness, rivers in the desert, to give drink to my people, my elect;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Hebrews 13:15; Psalms 102:18"> This people which I have formed for myself; my praise shall they relate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Micah 6:3; Malachi 3:14; Malachi 1:13"> But on me hast thou not called, O Jacob; for thou art become weary of me, O Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Amos 5:25; Zechariah 7:5–6; Malachi 3:8"> Thou hast not brought unto me the lamb of thy burnt-offerings; and with thy sacrifices hast thou not honored me: I have not troubled thee with meat-offerings, nor wearied thee with frankincense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 7:13; Jeremiah 6:20; Isaiah 1:14"> Thou hast not bought for me sweet cane, and with the fat of thy sacrifices hast thou not satisfied me; but thou hast troubled me with thy sins, thou hast wearied me with thy iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 8:12; Micah 7:18–19; Hebrews 10:17"> I, it is I that blot out thy transgressions for my own sake, and thy sins I will not remember.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 1:18; Isaiah 43:9; Romans 8:33"> Put me in remembrance; let us plead together: relate thou, in order that thou mayest be justified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 28:7; Jeremiah 5:31; Ezekiel 22:25–28"> Thy first father did sin, and they that plead for thee transgressed against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:9; Isaiah 65:15; Zechariah 8:13"> Therefore do I profane the holy princes, and I give up Jacob to the curse, and Israel to reproaches.</span>
