<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:14–15; Psalms 106:33; Psalms 39:2–3"> After this time Job opened his mouth, and cursed his day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 18:14"> And Job commenced, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:14–18; Jeremiah 15:10; Job 10:18–19"> Oh that the day whereon I was born might perish, and the night when it was said, There hath been a male child conceived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 27:20; Exodus 10:22–23; Joel 2:2"> May that day be &#91;covered with&#93; darkness; may not God from above inquire for it, and may no light beam upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 10:21–22; Psalms 23:4; Job 28:3"> Oh that darkness and the shadow of death might defile it; may a cloud rest upon it; may the blackness of the day terrify it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Yon night — let darkness seize upon it; let it not be united to the days of the year; let it not come into the number of the &#91;periods lighted by the&#93; moon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 24:8; Jeremiah 7:34; Revelation 18:22–23"> Lo, may that night be solitary, let no song of joy occur thereon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 41:1; Job 41:10; Mark 5:38"> Let those denounce it that curse the day, who are ready to raise up their mourning cry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 41:18; Jeremiah 13:16; Job 30:26"> Let the stars of its twilight be darkened; let it hope for light, and there be none; and let it not behold the eyelids of the morning-dawn;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:17; Ecclesiastes 6:3–5; Genesis 20:18"> Because God closed not against me the doors of the womb, and thus concealed trouble from my eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 10:18–19; Isaiah 46:3; Psalms 71:6"> Why did I not die &#91;the moment I issued&#93; from the womb, and &#91;why&#93; was I not born merely to perish at once?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 66:12; Genesis 30:3; Genesis 50:23"> Wherefore were knees ready to receive me? and for what purpose were breasts there that I might suck?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:10; Job 21:13; Job 14:10–12"> For now should I be lying still and be quiet; I should sleep: then would I be at rest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 15:28; Psalms 89:48; Psalms 49:14"> With kings and counsellors of the earth, who build up ruined places for themselves;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 10:27; Job 22:25; Zephaniah 1:18"> Or with princes possessing gold, who fill their houses with silver;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 58:8; Ecclesiastes 6:3; 1 Corinthians 15:8"> Or as an untimely birth, hidden &#91;from view&#93; I should not exist; as infants that never have seen the light;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Job 17:16; Hebrews 4:11"> There &#91;where&#93; the wicked cease from troubling; and where the exhausted weary are at rest;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 39:7; Isaiah 14:3–4; Exodus 5:6–8"> &#91;Where&#93; the prisoners repose together, &#91;and&#93; they hear no more the taskmaster's voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:8; Job 30:23; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> The small with the great is there, and the servant free from his master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:18; Job 7:15–16; 1 Samuel 1:10"> Wherefore giveth He now light to the labor-laden, and life unto the bitter in soul?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 9:6; Proverbs 2:4; 1 Kings 19:4"> Who wait for death, which &#91;cometh&#93; not; and who dig for it sooner than for hidden treasures;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Who would rejoice even to exulting, who would be glad could they but find a grave?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 19:8; Lamentations 3:7; Isaiah 40:27"> &#91;Why is light given&#93; to a man whose way is hidden, and around whom God hath placed a fence?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 102:9; Psalms 42:3–4; Psalms 80:5"> For before my food cometh my groaning, and like the water are poured forth my loud complaints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 1:5; Job 30:15; Job 31:23"> Because what I greatly dreaded is come upon me, and what I apprehended is come unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 7:14; Job 27:9; Psalms 143:11"> I have had no safety, and no quiet, and no rest; and &#91;now&#93; harrowing trouble is come.</span>
