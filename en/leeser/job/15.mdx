<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 42:7; Job 2:11; Job 22:1"> Then answered Eliphaz the Themanite, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 6:26; Job 8:2; Hosea 12:1"> Should a wise man utter windy knowledge, and fill his inward parts with the east wind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Matthew 12:36–37; Job 13:4–5"> Should he reason with a speech which availeth nothing? and with words in which there is no profit?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 3:31; Luke 18:1; Amos 6:10"> Yea, thou truly makest void the fear &#91;of God&#93;, and diminishest devotion before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:3–5; James 3:5–8; Luke 6:45"> For thy iniquity teacheth thy mouth, so that thou choosest the language of the crafty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 19:22; Matthew 12:37; Job 9:20"> Thy own mouth must condemn thee, but not I: yea, thy own lips will testify against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 90:2; Job 12:12; Job 38:4–41"> Wast thou born as the first man? or wast thou brought forth before the hills?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 11:34; Jeremiah 23:18; Job 13:5–6"> Hast thou listened to the secret counsel of God? and is wisdom therefore of little esteem with thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 13:2; 2 Corinthians 11:5; Job 12:3"> What knowest thou, that we do not know? what understandest thou, which is not with us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 32:6–7; Job 8:8–10; Job 12:12"> Both the grayheaded and the very aged are among us,— richer than thy father in days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:2; 2 Corinthians 1:3–5; 1 Kings 22:24"> Are the divine consolations too little for thee? and the word that was so mild with thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Mark 7:21–22"> Whither doth thy heart carry thee away? and what do thy eyes gaze at?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 15:25–27; James 3:2–6; Job 9:4"> That thou shouldst turn against God thy spirit, and utter &#91;such&#93; words out of thy mouth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; Job 14:4; Proverbs 20:9"> What is man, that he should be pure? and that he who is born of woman should be declared righteous?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 25:5; Job 4:18; Job 5:1"> Behold, in his holy ones he putteth no trust; and the heavens are not pure in his eyes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 34:7; Proverbs 19:28; Romans 3:9–19"> How much more then the abominable and corrupt, the man who drinketh like water wrong-doing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 36:2; Job 34:2; Job 13:5–6"> I will instruct thee, hear me; and what I have seen will I relate;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 8:8; Isaiah 38:19; Psalms 71:18"> Which wise men have ever told, and have not concealed, as they obtained it from their fathers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joel 3:17; Genesis 10:32; Genesis 10:25"> Unto whom alone the earth was given, and into whose midst no stranger ever entered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 27:13; James 5:1–6; Luke 12:19–21"> All his days is the wicked plagued with pain, and the number of years which are laid by for the tyrant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 18:11; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Leviticus 26:36"> A sound of terrors is in his ears: during peace will the waster come over him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 20:24–25; Matthew 27:5; Job 9:16"> He believeth not that he shall return out of darkness, and he is looked for by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 109:10; Psalms 59:15; Job 18:12"> He wandereth abroad for bread, &#91;saying,&#93; Where is it? he knoweth that there is ready at his hand the day of darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 24:34; Proverbs 6:11; Job 6:2–4"> Distress and anguish terrify him: they assail him with might, as a king prepared for the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 5:23; Psalms 52:7; Psalms 73:9"> Because he had stretched out against God his hand, and strengthened himself against the Almighty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 18:40; Job 16:12; 2 Chronicles 32:13–17"> &#91;And&#93; he had run against him, with an &#91;extended&#93; neck, with the thick roundings of his bucklers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 17:10; Jeremiah 5:28; Psalms 73:7"> Because he had covered his face with his fat, and had made thick folds of fat on his flanks;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 3:14; Jeremiah 51:37; Micah 7:18"> And he dwelt in abandoned cities, in houses which none inhabited, which were destined to be ruinous heaps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 27:16–17; Psalms 49:16–17; James 1:11"> &#91;Yet&#93; will he not remain rich, neither will his wealth endure, nor will he attain their perfection on earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 4:9; Job 15:22; Job 18:5–6"> He will never depart out of darkness: the flame shall dry up his shoots, and he will depart by the breath of God's mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 59:4; Galatians 6:3; Psalms 62:10"> Let him that goeth astray not trust in vanity; for vanity will be what he obtaineth thereby.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:17; Psalms 55:23; Hosea 9:16"> Even before his time will it be overfull, and his branches will not be green.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:39–40; Isaiah 33:9; Revelation 6:13"> He will shake off like the vine his unripe grapes, and cast off like the olive his blossoms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 8:13; Job 27:8; Job 8:22"> For the assembly of hypocrites will remain desolate, and fire will consume the tents of bribery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 7:14; Hosea 10:13; Isaiah 59:4–5"> They conceive trouble, and bring forth wrong-doing, and their body prepareth deceit.</span>
