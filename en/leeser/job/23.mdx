<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then answered Job, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 10:1; Psalms 32:4; Job 7:11"> Even now is my complaint bitter: my suffering is heavier than my groans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 16:21; Jeremiah 14:7; Job 13:3"> Oh who would grant that I knew where I might find him! that I might attain to his Judgment throne!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 13:18; Daniel 9:18–19; Exodus 32:12–13"> I would put in order before him my cause, and my mouth would I fill with arguments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 13:22–23; Job 10:2; 1 Corinthians 4:3–4"> I should know the words which he might answer me, and understand what he might say unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 13:21; Job 9:33–34; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> Would he with his power contend against me? he would truly not lay such doings to my charge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 13:3; Romans 8:1; Isaiah 1:18"> There would an upright one argue with him; and I should be allowed to escape for ever by my judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 10:1; Job 9:11; Job 35:14"> But, lo, I go eastward—and he is not there; and to the west— and I cannot perceive him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:46; Isaiah 8:17"> When he doth great things at the north, I behold him not; he hideth himself in the south—and I see him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 1:12; James 1:2–4; 1 Peter 1:7"> But he knoweth the way that I take: were he to probe me, I should come forth as gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 44:18; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Job 17:9"> On his steps my foot hath held fast: his way have I kept, and swerved not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:16; Psalms 119:103; Psalms 119:11"> From the commandment of his lips have I also not moved away: as a fixed statute for me have I treasured up the sayings of his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 115:3; Proverbs 19:21; Isaiah 46:10"> But he is unchangeably one, and who can turn him? And what his will desireth, even that he doth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:9; Romans 11:33"> For he will bring to completion what hath been destined for me: and like these hath he many other things with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; Psalms 77:3; Job 31:23"> Therefore am I terrified at his presence: I will reflect, and be in dread of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 22:14; Job 27:2; Psalms 88:16"> Still God hath made timid my heart, and the Almighty hath terrified me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 19:8; Isaiah 57:1; 2 Kings 22:20"> Because I was not destroyed before this darkness, and because he hath not hidden from my face &#91;this&#93; gloom.</span>
