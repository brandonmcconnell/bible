<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 3:1; Matthew 5:2; Psalms 78:2"> Behold now, I have opened my mouth, my tongue speaketh in my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 27:4; Proverbs 20:15; 1 Thessalonians 2:3–4"> Out of my straightforward heart &#91;come&#93; my sayings, and my lips utter knowledge clearly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 32:8; Genesis 2:7; Job 27:3"> The spirit of God hath made me, and the breath of the Almighty giveth me life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 33:32–33; Job 32:1; Psalms 50:21"> If thou canst, answer me, array thyself before me, stand forward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 4:19; Job 10:9; Job 13:12"> Behold, I am in the same relation as thyself toward God: I myself also am cut out of the clay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 13:21; Job 9:34; Psalms 32:4"> Behold, dread of me cannot terrify thee, and my pressure will not be too heavy upon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:14; Jeremiah 29:23"> But thou hast said before my ears, and the sound of the words I still hear,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 10:7; Job 16:17; Job 9:21"> “I am pure without transgression, I am quite clean; and there is no iniquity in me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 19:11; Job 13:24–25; Job 9:30–31"> Yet, behold, he findeth hateful backsliding on me, he regardeth me as an enemy unto him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:27; Job 31:4; Daniel 4:35"> He putteth my feet in the stocks, he watcheth all my paths.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:25; Job 36:5; Job 1:22"> Behold, In this thou art not just: I will answer thee; for God is far greater than a mortal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 40:2; Isaiah 45:9; Romans 11:34"> Why dost thou contend against him? for with all his words will he not give an answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24; 2 Chronicles 33:10; Job 33:29"> For God speaketh once, yea twice: &#91;yet man&#93; regardeth it not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 12:6; Job 4:13; Genesis 20:3"> In a dream, in a vision of the night, when deep sleep falleth upon men, in slumbers upon the couch:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 36:10; Job 36:15; Psalms 40:6"> Then doth he lay open the ear of men, and sealeth it with their warning;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 2:11; James 4:10; Genesis 20:6"> To remove the son of earth &#91;from his intended&#93; deed and he covereth up pride from man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 33:30; Job 33:28; 2 Peter 3:9"> He withholdeth his soul from the pit, and his life from passing away by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 30:17; Job 5:17–18; 1 Corinthians 11:32"> And so is he admonished by pain upon his couch, and all his bones with violent &#91;aches&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 107:17–18; Job 3:24; Genesis 3:6"> So that his inclination abhorreth bread, and his soul, the most agreeable food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 19:20; Job 13:28; Proverbs 5:11"> His flesh is consumed away, that it cannot be seen, and his bones that were not seen stick out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 33:28; 2 Samuel 24:16; Job 17:13–16"> Yea, his soul draweth near unto the pit, and his life to those that slay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Ecclesiastes 7:28; 2 Corinthians 5:20"> If there be now about him one single angel, as defender, one out of a thousand, to tell for man his uprightness:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 49:7–8; Matthew 20:28; 1 Peter 1:18–19"> Then is he gracious unto him, and saith, Release him from going down to the pit, I have found an atonement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 103:5; 2 Kings 5:14; Joshua 14:10–11"> His flesh becometh full again as in youth: he returneth to the days of his boyhood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Psalms 30:5; Acts 9:11"> He will offer his entreaty unto God, and he will receive him in favor, that he may see his face with joy: so doth He recompense unto the mortal his righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 6:21; 2 Samuel 12:13; Psalms 14:2"> He then should assemble men around, and say, “I had sinned, and perverted what is right, yet have I not received a like return.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 33:24; Job 3:16; Job 33:22"> Thus he redeemeth his soul from passing into the pit, and his life will look joyously on the light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 1:11; 1 Corinthians 12:6; Philippians 2:13"> Lo, all these things doth God two or three times with man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 56:13; Isaiah 38:17; Job 33:28"> To bring back his soul from the pit, that she may shine in the light of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Job 18:2; Job 13:6; Job 21:2"> Listen well, O Job, hearken unto me: keep silence, and I will truly speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 21:27; Job 15:4–5; Job 22:5–9"> If thou hast any words, answer me: speak, for I wish to justify thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 34:11; Proverbs 5:1–2; Psalms 49:3"> If not, hearken thou unto me: keep silence, and I will teach thee wisdom.</span>
