<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 144:3; Psalms 143:2; Psalms 8:4"> And yet on such a one dost thou open thy eyes, and me thou bringest into judgment with thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 3:6; Job 15:14; Ephesians 2:3"> Who can make a clean thing out of an unclean? not one &#91;thing&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 39:4; Acts 17:26; Hebrews 9:27"> Seeing that his days are determined, the number of his months are &#91;fixed&#93; with thee, that thou hast set his bounds which he cannot pass:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 7:19; Psalms 39:13; Job 7:1–2"> Turn thyself from him that he may recover from his pain, and be able to enjoy like a hired laborer his day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 27:6; Isaiah 11:1; Job 14:14"> For there is hope for the tree: if it be cut down, it may still sprout again, while its young shoot will not cease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:36; Isaiah 26:19; John 12:24"> If even its root become old in the earth, and its stock die in the dust:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 11:17–24; Ezekiel 17:3–10; Ezekiel 17:22–24"> Yet through the scent of water will it flourish &#91;again&#93;, and produce boughs as though It were newly planted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 19:26; Job 7:7–10; Job 14:12"> But man dieth, and lieth powerless: yea, the son of earth departeth—and where is he?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 19:5; Jeremiah 15:18; Job 6:15–18"> The waters run off from the sea, and the river faileth and drieth up:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 10:21–22; Job 3:13; Ecclesiastes 3:19–21"> So doth man lie down, and riseth not: till the heavens be no more, they will not awake, and will not be roused out of their sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 13:32; Isaiah 57:1–2; Isaiah 26:20–21"> Oh who would grant that thou mightest hide me in the nether world, that thou mightest conceal me, until thy wrath be appeased, that thou mightest set for me a fixed time, and remember me then!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:51–52; Job 7:1; Acts 26:8"> Or, when a man dieth, will he live again? all the days of my time of service would I then wait, till &#91;the hour of&#93; my release were come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 13:22; Psalms 138:8; Job 10:8"> Do thou call, and I will truly answer thee: have a desire for the work of thy hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 10:6; Job 31:4; Psalms 139:1–4"> Yet now thou numberest my steps: and thou waitest not with &#91;the punishment of&#93; my sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:34; Hosea 13:12; Job 21:19"> Sealed up in a bag is my transgression, and thou yet addest to my iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 6:14; Job 18:4; Jeremiah 4:24"> But truly a falling mountain will crumble, and &#91;even&#93; a rock is moved out of its place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 7:6; Job 27:8; Job 19:10"> The water weareth out stones; thou sweepest away their fragments &#91;like&#93; the dust of the earth: and so thou destroyest the hope of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 14:14; Job 2:12; Ecclesiastes 8:8"> Thou assailest him with might without ceasing, till he passeth away: thou changest his countenance, and sendest him off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:5; Ecclesiastes 2:18–19; Isaiah 63:16"> His children acquire honor, but he knoweth it not: and they are esteemed little, but he perceiveth nothing of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 19:26; Job 19:20; Luke 16:23–24"> But his body. on him, feeleth pain, and his soul will mourn for him.</span>
