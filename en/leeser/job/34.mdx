<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Elihu commenced, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 1:5; 1 Corinthians 14:20; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> Hear, O ye wise men, my words; and ye that have knowledge, give ear unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 12:11; 1 Corinthians 2:15; Job 33:2"> For the ear proveth words, as the palate tasteth the food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:21; Judges 19:30; Romans 12:2"> Let us choose for ourselves what is just: let us acknowledge between ourselves what is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 27:2; Job 33:9; Job 16:17"> For Job hath said, “I am righteous; and God hath taken away justice from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 6:4; Job 27:4–6; Job 16:13"> Should I lie concerning the justice due me? incurable is &#91;my wound from&#93; the arrow I bear in me without any transgression.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 15:16; Deuteronomy 29:19; Proverbs 1:22"> What man is there like Job, who drinketh scorning like water,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 1:1; 1 Corinthians 15:33; Proverbs 13:20"> And is on the road to keep company with the wrong-doers, and to walk with men of wickedness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 21:14–16; Job 35:3; Malachi 3:14"> For he hath said, “It profiteth a man nothing when he acteth according to the pleasure of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 9:14; Deuteronomy 32:4; Job 8:3"> Therefore ye men of sense hearken unto me: far is it from God to practise wickedness; and from the Almighty to do wrong!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Romans 2:6; Revelation 22:12"> For the work of a son of earth doth he recompense unto him, and according to the path of man doth he permit things to occur to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 8:3; Psalms 11:7; Psalms 145:17"> Yea, surely God will not condemn unjustly, nor will the Almighty pervert justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:11; Isaiah 40:13–14; Romans 11:34–36"> Who hath given him a charge concerning the earth? or who hath intrusted &#91;him&#93; the whole world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 104:29; Job 7:17; Job 9:4"> If he were to set his heart upon man, he would gather unto himself his spirit and his breath:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Isaiah 57:16"> All flesh would perish together, and the son of earth would return again unto dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 13:2–6; Job 12:3"> If then thou wishest to understand, hear this: give ear to the sound of my words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 40:8; Genesis 18:25; 2 Samuel 23:3"> Is it possible that he who hateth justice should govern? or wilt thou condemn the righteous mighty One?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 22:28; Proverbs 17:26; Romans 13:7"> &#91;Is it fit&#93; to say to a king, Thou art worthless? and to princes, Ye are wicked?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:17; Acts 10:34; James 2:5"> Whereas he is one that showeth no favor to chieftains, and distinguisheth not the rich before the indigent; for all of them are the work of his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 73:19; 1 Samuel 25:37–39; 1 Samuel 26:10"> In a moment will they die, and in the midst of the night; people are moved, and pass away: and the mighty will be removed without a human hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 15:3; Job 31:4; Jeremiah 16:17"> For big eyes are upon the ways of man, and all his steps doth he see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Amos 9:2–3; Proverbs 10:29"> There is no darkness, nor shadow of death, where the evil-doers can hide themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezra 9:13; Psalms 119:137; Romans 9:20"> For he need not direct &#91;his attention&#93; a long time upon man, that he should enter into judgment before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 113:7–8; Psalms 94:5; Daniel 2:21"> He breaketh down mighty men without &#91;long&#93; searching, and placeth others in their stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hosea 7:2; Job 34:20; Amos 8:7"> For the reason that he knoweth their deeds: therefore he overturneth them in the night, and they are crushed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 58:10–11; Deuteronomy 21:21; Isaiah 66:24"> Among wicked men doth he strike them, in the place where &#91;many&#93; see them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 28:5; 1 Samuel 15:11; Isaiah 5:12"> Because they have departed from following him, and have not considered all his ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="James 5:4; Exodus 3:7; Job 35:9"> Bringing before them the cry of the indigent, and the cry of the afflicted which he had to hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 23:13; John 14:27; Psalms 27:9"> When he now granteth rest, who will condemn &#91;him&#93;! and when he hideth his face, who can behold him? whether it be against a nation, or against one man, it is the same:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 29:2–12; Job 34:21; 2 Kings 21:9"> That no hypocritical man may reign, that such shall not be a mare to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Micah 7:9; Job 33:27; Job 42:6"> For truly it is only fitting to say unto God, “I bear &#91;cheerfully&#93;, I will not do any wrong;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 32:8; Psalms 25:4–5; Psalms 139:23–24"> What I cannot see myself, do thou truly teach me; if I have done what is unjust, I will do so no more.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 33:32; Isaiah 45:9; Psalms 135:6"> Should he then according to thy view send a recompense, because thou hast rejected him? “Because thou must choose, and not I?” and what thou knowest, do speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:15; Job 34:4; Job 34:16"> Men of sense will say unto me, and every wise man who heareth me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 35:16; Job 38:2; Job 15:2"> That Job hath not spoken with knowledge, and that his words are without intelligence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Job 21:7; Job 23:16; Job 12:6"> Oh that Job may therefore be probed continually, in order to give answers against sinful men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Job 27:23; Isaiah 1:19–20; 1 Samuel 15:23"> For he addeth unto his sin transgression: among us he uttereth too many loud words, and multiplieth his speeches against God.</span>
