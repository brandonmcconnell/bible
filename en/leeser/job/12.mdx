<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then answered Job, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 5:21; Job 8:8–10; 1 Corinthians 6:5"> Truly ye are indeed the &#91;right kind of&#93; people, and with you wisdom must die out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 13:2–5; Proverbs 26:4; 2 Corinthians 11:21–23"> I also have sense like you; I do not fall short compared with you: and who possesseth not such things as these?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 30:1; Job 17:6; Psalms 91:15"> I am as one laughed at by his friend, who calleth upon God, while he answered him: &#91;yea,&#93; a laughing-stock though righteous and innocent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 25:8; Proverbs 13:9; Psalms 17:5"> To the unfortunate there is given contempt— according to the thoughts of him that is at ease— prepared &#91;also&#93; for those whose foot slippeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 9:24; Psalms 73:11–12; Psalms 17:14"> Prosperous are the tents of robbers, and security is given to those that provoke God. to him who carrieth his god in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:3; Proverbs 6:6; Jeremiah 8:7"> Yet, do only ask of the beasts, and they will instruct thee; and the fowls of the heavens, and they will tell it thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Or speak to the earth, and she will instruct thee; and the fishes of the sea will inform thee</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 41:20; Jeremiah 27:5–6; Romans 11:36"> Who knoweth not through all these that the hand of the Lord hath wrought this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 17:28; Job 33:4; Acts 17:25"> &#91;He&#93; in whose hand is the soul of every living thing, and the spirit of all the bodies of men?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 34:3; Hebrews 5:14; Job 6:30"> Doth not the ear try words, as the palate tasteth food for itself?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 32:7; Job 15:10; Job 8:8"> So It with the ancients wisdom, and with &#91;those of&#93; length of days understanding,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 9:4; Job 36:5; Proverbs 2:6–7"> That with him are wisdom and strength, his are counsel and understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 3:7; Isaiah 22:22; Malachi 1:4"> Behold, he pulleth down, and there can be no rebuilding: he locketh &#91;the prison&#93; upon a man, and there can be no opening,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; Amos 5:8; Genesis 6:13"> Behold, he restraineth the waters, and they dry up; or he suffereth them to flow, and they overturn the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 13:9; Ezekiel 14:9; 1 Kings 22:22–23"> With him are strength and counsel: his are the deceived and the deceiver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 29:14; 1 Corinthians 1:19–20; 2 Samuel 17:23"> He leadeth counsellors away bereft of sense, and maketh the judges fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 2:21; Psalms 116:16; 2 Chronicles 33:11–14"> He looseth the bond of kings, and bindeth a girdle around their loins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 45:1; Isaiah 37:36–38; Revelation 17:14"> He leadeth priests away bereft of sense, and the powerful he causeth to walk on crooked paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 32:9; Job 17:4; Job 12:24"> He removeth the speech from trusty speakers, and taketh away the intelligence of the aged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 107:40; Isaiah 23:9; 2 Kings 9:34–37"> He poureth contempt upon princes, and the belt of the mighty be looseneth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 2:22; 1 Corinthians 4:5; 1 Corinthians 2:10"> He layeth open deep things from the midst of darkness, and bringeth out unto light the shadow of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 9:3; Isaiah 26:15; Isaiah 60:22"> He permitteth the nations to become great, and destroyeth them: he spreadeth out the nations, and leadeth them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 107:40; Job 12:20; Hosea 7:11"> He taketh away the sense of the chiefs of the people of the land, and causeth them to wander astray in a wilderness when there is no way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 5:14; Psalms 107:27; Isaiah 19:14"> They grope in the dark without light, and he causeth them to wander astray like a drunken man.</span>
