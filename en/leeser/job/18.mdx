<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 73:22; Job 17:10; Job 17:4"> For what cause are we counted as beasts, reputed stupid your eyes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 16:9; Job 14:18; Job 13:14"> Thou, the one that teareth himself to pieces in his anger— shall for thy sake the earth be forsaken, and the rock be moved away out of its place?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 13:9; Proverbs 24:20; Proverbs 20:20"> Ah, truly the light of the wicked will be quenched, and the spark of his fire shall not give light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 18:23; Job 21:17; Psalms 18:28"> The light becometh dark in his tent, and his lamp will be quenched above him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 4:12; Psalms 33:10; Psalms 18:36"> His powerful steps will be narrowed, and his own counsel will cast him down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 9:15; Job 22:10; Psalms 35:8"> For he is driven into the net by his own feet, and he taketh his walk upon a snare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14–15; Job 1:15; Job 1:17"> The trap will seize him by the heel, and the robber will prevail over him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Romans 11:9; Psalms 11:6"> The cord is hidden for him in the ground, and a trap is set for him on the pathway.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 15:21; Jeremiah 6:25; Leviticus 26:36"> All around do terrors scare him, and chase him as he walketh along.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:36; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Psalms 34:10"> His first-born will suffer hunger, and calamity will be ready for his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jonah 2:6; Zechariah 14:12; Revelation 6:8"> It will devour the limbs of his body: yea, the first-born of death will devour his limbs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 10:28; Psalms 55:4; Job 11:20"> Then will be plucked up out of his tent his confidence, and &#91;the evil&#93; will urge him forward to the king of terrors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 11:6; Deuteronomy 29:23; Isaiah 34:9–10"> It will dwell in his tent, because it is no more his: there will be strewed sulphur on his habitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 9:16; Isaiah 5:24; Job 15:30"> Beneath, his roots will be dried up, and above will his boughs he cut away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 34:16; Proverbs 10:7; Psalms 109:13"> His resemblance vanisheth from the earth, and no name remaineth for him in the streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 20:8; Job 10:22; Job 3:20"> Men will thrust him out from light into darkness, and out of the world will they drive him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:30; Isaiah 14:21–22; Job 20:26–28"> He will have neither son nor grandson among his people, nor any that escapeth in the places of his sojourning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 37:13; Jeremiah 50:27; Ezekiel 21:25"> Because of his &#91;calamitous&#93; day are they that come after him astonished, and they that went before are seized with shuddering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Titus 1:16; Jeremiah 9:3; Jeremiah 10:25"> Yea, such are the dwellings of the unjust, and this is the place of one that knew not God.</span>
