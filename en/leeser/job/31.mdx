<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="James 1:14–15; 1 John 2:16; Matthew 5:28–29"> A covenant had I made with my eyes: how then should I fix my look on a virgin?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 20:29; Job 27:13; Hebrews 13:4"> And what then would have been my portion of God from above? and what lot of the Almighty from on high?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 21:30; Job 34:22; Matthew 7:13"> Is not calamity &#91;ready&#93; for the unjust? and misfortune for the wrong-doers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 34:21; Proverbs 5:21; 2 Chronicles 16:9"> Behold, he truly seeth my ways, and numbereth all my steps;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Psalms 44:20–21; Psalms 12:2"> &#91;And knoweth&#93; whether I have walked with vain desires, or if my foot hath hastened after deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 7:8–9; Daniel 5:27; Isaiah 26:7"> Let him weigh me then in a righteous balance, and let God acknowledge my integrity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 15:39; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Matthew 5:29"> If my step have turned aside from the &#91;proper&#93; way, and my heart have walked after my eyes, and if any blemish have cleaved to my hands:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Micah 6:15; Leviticus 26:16; Deuteronomy 28:38"> Then let me sow, and let another eat; and let what I have growing be rooted out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 11:4; Ecclesiastes 7:26; Judges 16:5"> If my heart have been beguiled toward a woman, or if I have lain in wait at my neighbor's door:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:10; 2 Samuel 12:11; Isaiah 47:2"> Then may my wife labor at the mill for another, and may strangers ill-use her;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 20:10; Deuteronomy 22:22–24; Job 31:28"> For this would be incest; yea, it would be an iniquity &#91;to be punished by&#93; the judges;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 6:27; Jeremiah 5:7–9; Job 15:30"> For it would be a fire that consumeth down to the place of corruption, and would root out all my products.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:14–15; Exodus 21:26–27; Leviticus 25:46"> If ever I cast aside the justice due to my man-servant and my maid-servant, when they contended with me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 2:13; Psalms 9:12; Zechariah 2:13"> What then could I do when God should rise up? and when he should investigate, what could I answer him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 14:31; Malachi 2:10; Job 34:19"> Did not he that made me make him born or a woman? and did not the same one fashion us in the womb?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 38:14; Deuteronomy 15:7–10; Deuteronomy 28:32"> If ever I denied the wish of the indigent, or ever allowed the eyes of the widow to fall &#91;in vain hopes&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 29:12–16; John 13:29; Job 22:7"> Or if ever I ate my bread by myself alone, and the fatherless did not eat thereof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> (For from my youth he was brought up with me, as though we were of one father, and I have guided her &#91;as though she was sprung&#93; from my mother's womb;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 22:6; Luke 3:11; Job 24:4"> If ever I saw any one perishing for want of clothing, or the needy without covering:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:13; Job 29:11"> If his loins have not blessed me, and if he have not been warmed with the fleece of my sheep;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 22:9; Job 29:12; Micah 7:3"> If I have swung my hand against the fatherless, because I saw in the gate those that would help me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 137:6; Job 38:15; Job 31:40"> Then may my shoulder fall from my shoulder-blade, and my arm be broken from the channel-bone;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 13:11; Psalms 119:120; 2 Corinthians 5:11"> For dreaded by me was the calamitous punishment of God, and against his highness I can accomplish nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 49:6–7; Deuteronomy 8:12–14; Proverbs 11:28"> If I have made gold my confidence, or have said to the fine gold, Thou art my trust:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 62:10; Habakkuk 1:16; Isaiah 10:13–14"> If ever I rejoiced because my wealth was abundant, and because my hand had gotten much;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:16; Deuteronomy 4:19; Deuteronomy 17:3"> If ever I looked at the light &#91;of the sun&#93; when he shone brightly and on the moon walking in splendor:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:6; Deuteronomy 11:16; Psalms 2:12"> And my heart became misled in secret, and my hand kissed my mouth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:2–7; Job 31:11; Psalms 50:6"> This also were an iniquity to be punished by the judge; for thus would I have denied the God that is above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 24:17–18; Proverbs 17:5; Psalms 35:13–14"> If ever I rejoiced at the downfall of him that hated me, or was elated when evil befell him;—</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Romans 12:14; Exodus 23:4–5"> But I suffered not my mouth to sin by denouncing with a curse his soul:—</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 1:18; Proverbs 1:11–12; Micah 3:2–3"> If the men of my tent said not, Oh is there one that is not satisfied of his flesh;—</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 13:2; Genesis 19:2–3; Judges 19:20–21"> In the street a stranger had not to lodge; my doors I held open to the roadside;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; Hosea 6:7; Acts 5:8"> If I covered up my transgressions like a common man, by hiding in my bosom my iniquity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 23:2; Proverbs 29:25; Esther 4:11"> Because I dreaded the great multitude, or because the contempt of families did terrify me, so that I kept silence, and dared not to go out of the door;—</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 13:21–22; Job 40:4–5; Job 17:3"> Oh who will bring me one that would hear me! behold, here is my plea; may the Almighty answer me; and any record which my opponent may have written,—</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 22:22; Job 29:14; Isaiah 62:3"> Surely upon my shoulder would I carry it: I would bind it as a crown unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Job 13:15; Ephesians 3:12; Hebrews 4:15–16"> The number of my steps would I tell him: as &#91;to&#93; a prince would I go near unto him.—</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:11; James 5:4; Psalms 65:13"> If my land ever cried out because of me, or if its furrows wept together;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 21:19; James 5:4; Isaiah 26:21"> If I ever consumed its strength without payment, or caused the soul of its owners to grieve:</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Genesis 3:17–18; Malachi 1:3; Psalms 72:20"> Then may instead of wheat, thorns come forth, and instead of barley, cockle. (Here end the words of Job.)</span>
