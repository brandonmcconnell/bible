<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then commenced Elihu, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 12:36–37; Job 19:6–7; Job 16:17"> Dost thou deem this to be just, that thou hast said, “My righteousness is more than God's?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 34:9; Malachi 3:14; Job 9:30–31"> For thou sayest, “What benefit will it be unto thee? what more profit shall I have, than if I had sinned?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; Job 34:8"> I will truly reply unto thee with words, and unto thy friends with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 22:12; Isaiah 55:9; Nahum 1:3"> Look unto the heavens, and see; and gaze on the skies which are higher than thou.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:19; Proverbs 8:36; Proverbs 9:12"> If thou sin, what dost thou effect against him? and if thy transgressions be multiplied, what canst thou do unto him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 22:2–3; Psalms 16:2; Luke 17:10"> If thou be righteous, what givest thou him? or what doth he accept out of thy hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:30; Job 42:8; Acts 27:24"> A man like thyself thy wickedness may reach, and a son of earth thy righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 2:23; Job 34:28; Exodus 3:7"> By reason of the multitude of oppressions &#91;the wicked&#93; cause men to cry: these complain aloud because of the arm of the mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 42:8; Acts 16:25; Psalms 149:5"> But &#91;man&#93; saith not, Where is God my maker, who bestoweth joyful songs even in the night;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 94:12; Job 36:22; Job 32:8"> Who teacheth us more than the beasts of the earth, and maketh us wiser than the fowls of the heavens?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 1:28; John 9:31; Psalms 123:3–4"> There do they cry, but he answereth not: because of the pride of evil men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 15:29; Isaiah 1:15; James 4:3"> Only what is false will God not hear, nor will the Almighty regard it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 9:11; Micah 7:7–9; Psalms 37:5–6"> Although thou sayest, thou canst not see him: yet the decision is before him; and do thou wait for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 89:32; Job 9:14; Job 13:15"> But now, because his anger hath punished nothing, shall he not greatly take cognizance of the multitude of sins?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 38:2; Job 33:8–12; Job 34:35–37"> But Job openeth wide his mouth for nought: without knowledge he heapeth up words.</span>
