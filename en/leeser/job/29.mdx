<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 27:1; Job 13:12; Numbers 23:7"> And Job continued to take up his parable, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 1:10; Job 1:1–5; Jeremiah 31:28"> Who will give me back months like those which are past, days like those when God guarded me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 18:28; John 8:12; Job 18:6"> When his lamp shone over my head, when by his light I could walk in darkness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 25:14; Proverbs 3:32; Psalms 27:5"> As I was in the days of my abundance, when the confidence of God was upon my tent:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 128:3; Psalms 127:3–5; Deuteronomy 33:27–29"> When the Almighty was yet with me, when my servants stood round about me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 81:16; Job 20:17; Deuteronomy 32:13–14"> When I bathed my steps in cream, and the rock poured out near me streamlets of oil!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 8:16; Ruth 4:1–2; Deuteronomy 16:18"> When I went out to the gate close by the city, when in the open place I established my seat:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 19:32; Proverbs 16:31; Romans 13:7"> Young men saw me, and hid themselves; and the aged rose up, and remained standing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 21:5; Job 40:4; Proverbs 10:19"> Princes stopped in the midst of &#91;their&#93; words, and laid their hand on their mouth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 137:6; Ezekiel 3:26"> The voice of nobles was arrested, and their tongue cleaved to their palate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 4:22; Job 31:20; Proverbs 29:2"> For the ear that heard me called me happy; and the eye that saw me bore witness for me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 72:12; Proverbs 21:13; James 1:27"> Because I delivered the poor that cried, and the fatherless, yea, that had none to help him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philemon 1:7; Job 22:9; Job 31:19–20"> The blessing of him that was ready to perish came upon me; and the heart of the widow I caused to sing for joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; Isaiah 59:17; Psalms 132:9"> I took righteousness as my garment, and it clothed me: as a robe and a mitre was justice unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 10:31; Matthew 11:5; 1 Corinthians 12:12–31"> Eyes was I to the blind; and feet to the lame was I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 29:7; Psalms 68:5; Job 31:18"> A father was I to the needy; and the cause of him I knew not I used to investigate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 3:7; Proverbs 30:14; 1 Samuel 17:35"> And I broke the cutting-teeth of the wrong-doer, and out of his teeth I cast down his prey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 30:6–7; Genesis 32:12; Job 42:16–17"> And I said then, “In the midst of my nest shall I depart hence, and like the sand shall I have many days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 1:3; Jeremiah 17:8; Hosea 14:5–7"> My root will stand open for the water, and the dew will lodge on my boughs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 49:24; Isaiah 40:31; Psalms 18:34"> My glory will ever be new with me, and my bow will acquire fresh strength in my hand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 32:11–12; Job 29:9–10"> Unto me men listened, and waited, and watched in silence for my counsel</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:2; Song of Solomon 4:11; Matthew 22:46"> After my words they made no reply, and my speech dropped on them &#91;like dew&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hosea 6:3; Psalms 72:6; Zechariah 10:1"> And they waited for me as for the rain, and they opened wide their mouth as for the latter rain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 89:15; Psalms 4:6; Psalms 126:1"> I smiled on those that had lost their confidence; and the light of my countenance they never cast down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:3–4; 1 Thessalonians 3:2–3; Genesis 14:14–17"> I chose their way for them, and I sat as chief, and dwelt as king in his army, as one that comforteth mourners.</span>
