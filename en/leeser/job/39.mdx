<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> They bend themselves: they drop their young ones; throw off their pains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Their little ones become strong; they grow up in the open field; they go forth, and return not unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 11:12; Job 24:5; Job 6:5"> Who sent out the wild ass free? or who loosened the bonds of the forest-ass?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 107:34; Job 24:5; Deuteronomy 29:23"> To whom I assigned the wilderness as his house, and the salty land as his dwellings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 5:18; Job 3:18; Exodus 5:13–16"> He laugheth at the noise of a town, and the shoutings of the driver he heareth not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 104:27–28; Psalms 145:15–16; Job 40:15"> What he espieth on the mountains is his pasture, and after every green thing doth he search.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 23:22; Deuteronomy 33:17; Psalms 92:10"> Will the forest-ox be willing to serve thee, or will he stay over night at thy crib?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 10:10–11; Psalms 129:3; Job 39:7"> Canst thou bind the forest-ox with a rope &#91;to labor&#93; in the furrow? or will he harrow valleys, following after thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 1:26; Psalms 144:14; Genesis 42:26"> Wilt thou trust him, because his strength is great? and wilt thou leave to him thy labor?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:15; Proverbs 3:16; Amos 2:13"> Wilt thou confide in him, that he should bring home thy seed, and gather it into thy threshing-floor?—</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:7; Zechariah 5:9; 2 Chronicles 9:21"> The wing of the ostrich moveth joyfully: hath she the pinions and plumage of the careful stork?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> &#91;No,&#93; for she intrusteth her eggs to the earth, and letteth them be hatched out on the dust:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And she forgetteth that a foot may crush them, or that the beast of the field may stamp them down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Lamentations 4:3; Deuteronomy 28:56–57; 1 Kings 3:26–27"> He hath made her callous against her young, as though they were not hers: her labor is in vain, &#91;but she feeleth&#93; no dread;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 35:11; Job 17:4; Isaiah 57:17"> Because God hath denied her wisdom, and he hath not imparted to her understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 41:29; Job 39:22; Job 5:22"> At the time she raiseth herself up on high, she laugheth at the horse and his rider.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 147:10; Psalms 93:1; Psalms 104:1"> Dost thou give the horse strength? dost thou clothe his neck with the rolling mane?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:16; Job 41:20–21; Joel 2:5"> Canst thou make him jump like a locust? his majestic snort is terrible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:6; Psalms 19:5; 1 Samuel 17:4–10"> Men spy about in the valley, and he rejoiceth in his strength: he goeth forth to meet the armed array.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 41:33; Job 39:16; Job 39:18"> He laugheth at fear, and is not dismayed; and turneth not back from before the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 41:26–29"> Over him rattle the quiver, the glittering spear and the lance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Amos 3:6; Luke 24:41; Job 37:20"> With impatient noise and rage he holloweth &#91;with his hoof&#93; the ground, and keepeth not quiet when the cornet's voice &#91;is heard&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 70:3; Ezekiel 26:2; Ezekiel 36:2"> Midst the sound of the cornet he uttereth his joyful neigh; and from afar he perceiveth the battle, the loud call of the captains, and the battle-cry.—</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:15; Song of Solomon 2:12; Leviticus 16:11"> Is it through thy understanding that the hawk flieth along, and spreadeth out his wings toward the south?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Obadiah 1:4; Jeremiah 49:16; Exodus 19:4"> Or is it by your order that the eagle doth mount upward, and buildeth high up his nest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:4"> On a rock he dwelleth, and spendeth his nights, on a rocky crag and mountain fastness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 9:26"> From there he espieth his food, from afar can his eyes behold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 24:28; Luke 17:37; Ezekiel 39:17–19"> His young ones, also, sip up blood: and where the slain be, there is he.</span>
