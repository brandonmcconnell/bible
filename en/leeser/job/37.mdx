<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 38:13; Matthew 24:27; Isaiah 11:12"> Under the whole heavens he letteth it loose, and his lightning over the ends of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 29:3–9; Psalms 68:33; Exodus 15:7–8"> Behind it roareth the thunder; he thundereth with his majestic voice; and he holdeth them not back when his voice is heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 5:9; Romans 11:33; Isaiah 40:28"> God thundereth with his marvelous voice: he doth great things, which we cannot comprehend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 38:22; Job 36:27; Ezekiel 13:13"> For to the snow he saith, Be thou on the earth: likewise the pouring rain, and to the pouring rains of his strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 111:2; Psalms 109:27; Isaiah 26:11"> He sealeth it on the hand of every man, that all men whom he hath made may know it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 104:22; Job 38:40"> Then retire the beasts into &#91;their&#93; dens, and rest in their lairs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 9:9; Psalms 104:3; Isaiah 21:1"> Out of &#91;his&#93; chamber cometh the whirlwind, and out of the north, the cold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 38:29–30; Psalms 147:16–18; Psalms 78:47"> From the breathing of God ice is given, and the broad waters become solid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 36:27–30; Matthew 17:5; Isaiah 18:4"> Also with moisture he loadeth the cloud; &#91;and&#93; he scattereth the cloud of his lightning;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 148:8; Joel 2:23; James 5:17–18"> And it is turned round about by his guidance, to execute what he commandeth it upon the face of the world, the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 18:45; Job 38:26–27; 1 Samuel 12:18–19"> Whether it be as a chastising rod—if this be destined for his earth—or for kindness, doth he cause it to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 111:2; Psalms 145:10–12; Exodus 14:13"> Give ear unto this, O Job: stand still, and consider well the wonders of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 36:30–32; Isaiah 40:26; Job 37:11"> Dost thou know how God hath imposed &#91;a law&#93; on them, and &#91;how&#93; he hath caused the light of his cloud to shine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 36:4; Proverbs 3:19–20; Isaiah 40:22"> Dost thou know aught about the balancings of the clouds, the wondrous works of him who is perfect in knowledge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 147:18; Job 6:17; Job 38:31"> &#91;Thou&#93; who clothest thyself with warm garments, when He giveth the earth rest from the south wind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 44:24; Psalms 104:2; Isaiah 40:22"> Hast thou with him spread out the skies, which are strong even as a molten mirror?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 38:2; Psalms 73:22; Job 42:3"> Let us know what we shall say unto him: we cannot set aught in order &#91;before him&#93; because of darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 11:7–8; Psalms 139:4; Job 6:3"> Can &#91;all&#93; be related of him, when I speak &#91;ever so much&#93;? or if a man talk &#91;of him&#93; even till he be swallowed up &#91;in death&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 36:32; Job 26:9; Job 38:25"> Yet now men see not the light which is bright in the skies, when the wind hath passed along, and purified them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 104:1; Jude 1:25; Hebrews 12:29"> The golden &#91;light&#93; that cometh out of the north: around God is terrible majesty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:16; Romans 11:33; Psalms 99:4"> The Almighty, whom we cannot find out, excellent in power, and in justice, and abounding in righteousness, will not afflict:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:28; 1 Corinthians 1:26; Psalms 130:4"> Therefore do men fear him; he respecteth not any that are wise of heart.</span>
