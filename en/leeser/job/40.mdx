<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 30:32; Job 29:9; Job 21:5"> Behold, I am too vile: what shall I answer thee? my hand do I place on my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 33:14; Job 9:3; Job 9:15"> Once have I spoken; but I will not answer: yea, twice; but I will not repeat it again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 38:1; 2 Peter 3:10–12; Psalms 50:3–4"> Then answered the Lord unto Job out of the storm-wind, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 38:3; Job 42:4; Job 23:3–4"> Do but gird up like a mighty man thy loins: I will ask thee, and do thou inform me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:4; Isaiah 28:18; Job 32:2"> Wilt thou indeed annul my decree? wilt thou condemn me, in order that thou mayest appear righteous?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:13; Job 37:4–5; Isaiah 45:9"> But if thou hast an arm like God, or if thou canst thunder loudly like him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 93:1; Psalms 149:4; Isaiah 59:17"> Then do deck thyself with excellence and greatness, and clothe thyself in majesty and glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 4:37; Isaiah 2:11–12; Isaiah 2:17"> Scatter abroad the ragings of thy wrath, and look on every proud one, and humble him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 63:3; Malachi 4:3; Psalms 60:12"> Look on every proud one, and bend him low; and tread down the wicked in their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 2:10; Esther 7:8; Job 14:13"> Hide them in the dust altogether: bind up their faces in concealment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4–9; Romans 5:6; Psalms 44:6"> Then will I also myself praise thee, when thy own right hand hath helped thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 40:20; Psalms 104:14; Genesis 1:24–26"> Only behold Behemoth, which I made near thee: grass he eateth like the ox.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Only see, &#91;how great&#93; is his strength in his loins, and his force, in the muscles of his belly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 41:23"> He stretcheth out his tail like a cedar: the sinews of his loins are closely wrapped together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 48:4; Job 7:12"> His bones are like pipes of brass: his frame is like bars of iron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 41:33; Job 26:13; Isaiah 27:1"> He is the first in rank of the works of God: he that made him can alone bring his sword near unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 104:26; Psalms 104:14; Job 40:15"> But truly the mountains bear for him his food, and all the beasts of the field play there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 35:7; Isaiah 19:6–7"> Under shady trees he lieth down, in the covert of the reeds, and swamp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:5; Leviticus 23:40; Isaiah 15:7"> Shady trees cover him as his shadow: willows of the brook encompass him about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 13:10; Isaiah 37:25; Isaiah 28:16"> Behold, a river sweepeth violently along, but he hasteneth not away: he remaineth quiet, though a Jordan rusheth up to his mouth.</span>
