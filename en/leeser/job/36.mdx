<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then continued Elihu, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 21:3; 2 Corinthians 5:20; Job 13:7–8"> Wait for me a little, and I will instruct thee; for &#91;I have&#93; still some words on God's behalf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 3:17; James 1:17; Deuteronomy 32:4"> I will lift up my knowledge for him who is afar, and for my Maker will I obtain righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 8:7–8; Job 37:16; Psalms 49:3"> For truly no falsehood is &#91;in&#93; my words: one that is upright in &#91;his&#93; opinions &#91;dealeth now&#93; with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 22:24; Psalms 138:6; Jeremiah 10:12"> Behold, God is mighty, and despiseth not any: he is mighty in strength of intellect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 72:12–14; Psalms 140:12; 2 Peter 2:9"> He permitteth not the wicked to live; but he procureth justice for the afflicted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 34:15; Psalms 33:18; 1 Samuel 2:8"> He withdraweth not his eyes from the righteous; but &#91;he placeth them&#93; with kings on the throne; yea, he doth establish them for ever, and they are exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 5:22; Psalms 107:10; Lamentations 3:9"> And if they be bound in fetters, and if they be entangled in the cords of affliction:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 119:71; Job 15:25; 1 Corinthians 11:32"> Then doth he tell them of their work, and of their transgressions, when they had become strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 3:8; Job 36:15; Isaiah 55:6–7"> And he openeth thus their ear to correction, and saith that they should return from wrong-doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:23; Isaiah 1:19; Jeremiah 26:13"> If they hearken and serve &#91;him&#93;, they will spend their days in happiness, and their years in pleasures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 4:21; Romans 2:8–9; Isaiah 3:11"> But if they hearken not, they will pass away through the sword, and they will perish in want of knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 2:5; Job 36:8; Job 27:8–10"> But the hypocrites in heart persevere in wrath; they will not offer entreaty when he bindeth them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:16; Job 15:32; Deuteronomy 23:17"> Their soul will die in youth, and their life, among the incestuous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 36:10; 2 Chronicles 12:8"> He delivereth the afflicted through his affliction, and openeth through oppression his ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 23:5; Psalms 118:5; Psalms 18:19"> And also thee hath he incited away from the jaws of distress into a wide space, on the site of which there is no straitness; and what is set on thy table is full of fatness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 18:4; Romans 1:32; Job 34:36"> But if thou art full of the judgment of the wicked: &#91;divine&#93; judgment and decree will support each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 33:24; Ephesians 5:6; Romans 1:18"> For there would be fury, If aught were to incite thee to utter an abundance &#91;of rebellious words&#93;; and the greatness of the infliction must not mislead thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 11:4; Proverbs 11:21; James 5:3"> Will he esteem thy riches? no, not gold ore, nor all the highest forces of strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 19:35; Job 34:20; Exodus 12:29"> Desire then not eagerly the night, when nations pass away in their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 11:25; Psalms 66:18; 1 Peter 3:17"> Take heed, turn not thyself to wrong-doing, so that thou wouldst choose this because of &#91;thy&#93; affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:7–8; Luke 1:52; Romans 13:1"> Behold, God is exalted by his power: who is an instructer like him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:16; Isaiah 40:13–14; Job 8:3"> Who hath given him a charge concerning his way? or who hath ever said, Thou hast acted unjustly?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 15:3; Psalms 111:2–4; Psalms 72:18"> Reflect, that thou shouldst magnify his work, which &#91;other&#93; men have beheld.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> All men have looked at it &#91;with astonishment&#93;; the mortal gazeth at it from afar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 90:2; Psalms 145:3; Job 11:7–9"> Behold, God is great, and we comprehend him not, the number of his years can truly not be searched out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 147:8; Psalms 148:8; Psalms 65:9–13"> For he taketh away drops of water, which are purified into rain in his mist:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 3:20; Genesis 7:11–12; Job 37:11–13"> These drop down out of the skies; they distil upon the multitude of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 37:16; Job 37:2–5; Psalms 18:13"> But &#91;what man&#93; can understand the outspreadings of the clouds? the tumult of his tabernacle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 38:8–11; Job 38:34–35; Genesis 1:9"> Behold, he spreadeth out over it his light, and covereth up the roots of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 136:25; Acts 14:17; Job 37:13"> For by means of them he judgeth nations, he giveth food in superfluity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 135:7; Psalms 147:8–9; Psalms 148:8"> &#91;His&#93; hands he covereth with light; and he commandeth it to strike the one who striveth against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 37:2; Job 36:29; 2 Samuel 22:14"> The noise of his storm telleth of it, yea, the cattle also, of the rising tempest.</span>
