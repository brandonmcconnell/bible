<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then answered Job, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3–4; Job 4:3–4; Isaiah 41:5–7"> What assistance hast thou given to the powerless? &#91;how&#93; hast thou helped the arm without strength?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 71:15–18; Job 32:11–13; Job 33:3"> How hast thou counselled the unwise? and what sound wisdom hast thou made known so plentifully?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 22:23–24; Job 32:18; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> To whom hast thou told words? and whose spirit came from thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 88:10; Genesis 6:4; Job 41:1–34"> The departed are called into being beneath the waters, and their inhabitants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 139:8; Proverbs 15:11; Hebrews 4:13"> Naked is the nether world before him, and there is no covering for the place of corruption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 9:8; Psalms 104:2–5; Isaiah 40:22"> He stretched out the north over empty space; he suspended the earth on nothing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 30:4; Jeremiah 10:13; Isaiah 5:6"> He bound up the waters in his clouds; and the cloud bursteth not under their weight;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; Job 22:14; Habakkuk 3:3–5"> He closed up the surface of his throne, spreading over it his cloud;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 8:29; Job 38:8–11; Jeremiah 5:22"> A fixed limit he compassed off over the face of the waters, for the division of the light and darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:8; Psalms 18:7; Job 15:15"> The pillars of heaven tremble greatly, and are astounded at his rebuke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 51:15; Jeremiah 31:35; Psalms 89:9–10"> By his power he split in pieces the sea, and by his understanding he crushed &#91;its&#93; pride:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 27:1; Psalms 104:30; Psalms 74:13–14"> By his breath the heavens &#91;acquired&#93; beauty; his hand hath created the flying serpent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:26–29; Psalms 145:3; Romans 11:33"> Lo, these are ends of his ways; for how slight a whisper is heard &#91;by us&#93; of him! but the thunder of his mighty deeds who can understand?</span>
