<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then answered Job, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 13:4–5; Psalms 69:26; Job 11:2–3"> I have heard many things such as these: troublesome comforters are ye all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 6:26; Job 15:2; Titus 2:8"> Shall there be any end to words of wind? or what compelleth thee that thou shouldst answer?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 109:25; Psalms 22:7; Lamentations 2:15"> I also could well speak as ye do: if your soul were but in my soul's stead, I could overwhelm you with words, and could shake my head at you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 27:9; Galatians 6:1; Isaiah 35:3–4"> But I would strengthen you with my mouth, and the condolence of my lips should restrain &#91;your grief&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 10:1; Psalms 88:15–18; Psalms 77:1–9"> Though I were to speak, my pain would not be restrained; and though I should forbear, what will go away from me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 7:3; Job 1:15–19; Psalms 6:6–7"> But now he hath made me weary: thou hast made desolate all my company.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 10:17; Job 19:20; Ruth 1:21"> And thou hast filled me with wrinkles, they are my witnesses; and my leanness riseth up for me, giveth its testimony to my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 35:16; Job 13:24; Lamentations 2:16"> In his wrath he teareth me to pieces, and assaileth me: he gnasheth over me with his teeth; my adversary sendeth threatening looks at me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 35:15; Psalms 22:13; Isaiah 50:6"> They now open wide against me their mouth; reproachfully they smite my cheek: altogether do they assemble against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 27:12; Psalms 7:14; Psalms 31:8"> God hath surrendered me to the unjust, and cast me down into the hands of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 7:20; Lamentations 3:11–12; Lamentations 3:4"> I was at ease, but he hath crushed me; he hath also grasped me by the neck, and shaken me to pieces, and set me up unto himself as a mark;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 20:25; Job 6:4; Lamentations 2:11"> His archers encompass me round about; he cleaveth my reins sunder, and doth not pity; he poureth out upon the ground my gall;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 15:8; Lamentations 3:3–5; Joel 2:7"> He breaketh me down with breach upon breach; he runneth against me like a mighty man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 37:34; Psalms 7:5; Psalms 75:5"> Sackcloth have I sewed upon my skin, and my horn I roll in the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 14:34; Psalms 102:3–5; Lamentations 1:16"> My face gloweth from weeping, and on my eyelids resteth the shadow of death:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 15:8; Psalms 66:18–19; Job 27:6–7"> Not because any violence is in my hands, and while my prayer is pure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 26:21; Ezekiel 24:7; Genesis 4:11"> Earth! do thou not cover up my blood, and let no place restrain my cry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 1:9; 1 Thessalonians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 11:31"> Even now, behold, my witness is in the heavens, and one that testifieth for me is on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Psalms 142:2; Job 16:4"> Are my friends my defenders? unto God my eye poureth out &#91;its tears&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 6:10; Isaiah 45:9; Romans 9:20"> And oh that a man might plead with God, as one son of earth with the other!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 14:10; Job 14:14; Job 14:5"> For when the numbered years are passed, then must I travel a path whence I cannot return.</span>
