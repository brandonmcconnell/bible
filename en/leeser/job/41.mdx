<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 37:29; Ezekiel 29:4–5; 2 Kings 19:28"> (40:26) Canst thou put a reed through his nose? or bore his jaw through with a thorn?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 30:10; Proverbs 25:15; Psalms 55:21"> (40:27) Will he address many supplications unto thee? or will he speak submissively unto thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 21:6; Deuteronomy 15:17; Psalms 8:5–6"> (40:28) Will he make a covenant with thee? that thou couldst take him as a servant for ever?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 16:25–30; Job 28:11"> (40:29) Canst thou play with him as with a bird? and tie him up for thy maidens?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 14:11"> (40:30) Can companions waylay him? can they divide him among merchants?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> (40:31) Canst thou fill his skin with barbed irons? and &#91;pierce&#93; with a fish-spear his head?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 20:11; Luke 14:31–32; 2 Kings 10:4"> (40:32) Lay thy hand upon him; think of the battle: thou wilt never do it again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 28:19; 1 Samuel 3:11; Luke 21:11"> (41:1) Behold, his expectation was deceived: even at his mere sight is he cast down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:4; Psalms 2:11–12; 1 Corinthians 10:22"> (41:2) None is so daring that he would stir him up: and who is there that will stand up before me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 11:35; Psalms 24:1; Job 35:7"> (41:3) Who hath shown me favor, that I should repay him! whatsoever is under the whole heaven is mine.—</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 1:25"> (41:4) I will not conceal &#91;the account of&#93; his limbs, nor the relation of his might, nor the grace of his proportion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 19:28; Psalms 32:9; James 3:3"> (41:5) Who hath ever laid open the front of his garment? or who can penetrate into his double row of teeth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:4; Psalms 57:4; Psalms 58:6"> (41:6) Who hath opened the doors of his face? all round about his teeth abideth terror.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 5:5; Revelation 5:2–3; Jeremiah 9:23"> (41:7) What pride is there in &#91;his&#93; strong shields; he is locked up as with a close seal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> (41:8) One is joined to another; and no breath can come between them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> (41:9) They are fitted closely one to another: they are interlocked, that they cannot be severed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 3:9; Revelation 1:14"> (41:10) From his sneezing there beameth forth a light, and his eyes are like the eyelids of the morning-dawn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 18:8"> (41:11) Out of his mouth issue burning torches, sparks of fire escape &#91;therefrom&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:13–14"> (41:12) Out of his nostrils cometh forth smoke, as out of a seething pot or caldron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 18:8; Isaiah 30:33; Habakkuk 3:5"> (41:13) His breath kindleth coals, and a flame cometh out of his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 13:14; Job 40:16; 1 Corinthians 15:55–57"> (41:14) In his neck abideth strength, and before him danceth terror joyfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> (41:15) The flakes of his flesh are fitted closely together: they are as molten metal on him, immovable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:3; Isaiah 48:4; Zechariah 7:12"> (41:16) His heart is firm like a stone: yea, as firm as the nether millstone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jonah 1:4–6; Psalms 107:28"> (41:17) At his lifting himself up the mighty are terrified: the waves also are lessened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 39:21–24"> (41:18) If one overtake him with the sword, it cannot hold; nor the spear, the dart, and armor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> (41:19) He esteemeth iron as straw, &#91;and&#93; brass as rotten wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:10; Job 39:7"> (41:20) The child of the bow cannot make him flee: into stubble are slingstones changed unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:14"> (41:21) Clubs are esteemed as stubble, and he laugheth at the whirring of the lance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 41:15"> (41:22) Beneath him are sharp-pointed potsherds, he spreadeth out, &#91;as it were, a&#93; threshing-roller upon the mire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> (41:23) He causeth the deep to boil like a pot: he rendereth the sea like an apothecary's mixture.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 38:30; Genesis 1:2; Genesis 15:15"> (41:24) Behind him he causeth his pathway to shine, &#91;so that&#93; men esteem the deep to be hoary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 40:19"> (41:25) There is none upon earth that ruleth over him, who is made to be without dread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:3; Psalms 73:6; Job 28:8"> (41:26) He looketh upon all that is high: he is the king over all the ravenous beasts.</span>
