<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 20:1"> Then answered Zophar the Na'amathite, and said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 10:19; James 1:19; Job 18:2"> Shall a multitude of words not be answered? and is it so that a man full of talk shall be deemed in the right?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 17:2; Job 24:25; Titus 2:8"> Thy inventions are to bring men to silence; and when thou utterest thy mocking no one is to cause thee to feel abashed!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 10:7; Job 6:10; Job 35:2"> For thou hast said &#91;to God&#93;, My doctrine is pure, and I am become clean in thy eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 38:1–2; Job 31:35; Job 33:6–18"> But oh that God would but speak, and open his lips against thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra 9:13; Lamentations 3:22; Daniel 2:28"> And that he would declare unto thee the secrets of wisdom; for it is double to that which is really in our possession: and thou wouldst experience that God overlooketh unto thee much of thy iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Ecclesiastes 3:11; Psalms 145:3"> Canst thou find out the experience of God? or canst thou find &#91;the way&#93; unto the utmost limit of the Almighty?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 22:12; Psalms 139:6–8; Isaiah 55:9"> It is as high as heaven; what canst thou effect? it is deeper than the nether world; what canst thou know?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 139:9–10; Job 28:24–25; Psalms 65:5–8"> Longer than the earth is its measure, and broader than the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:12–13; Job 12:14; Revelation 3:7"> If he pass by, and surrender &#91;one to suffering&#93;, and call together an assembly, who can hinder him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 10:14; Hebrews 4:13; Revelation 2:23"> For he knoweth the men of vanity: he seeth the wrong-doer and him who considereth not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 2:20; Ecclesiastes 3:18; James 3:13–17"> And the heartless who acquireth intelligence, and him who is &#91;like&#93; the colt of the wild ass who is transformed into a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 143:6; Psalms 88:9; 1 Samuel 7:3"> If thou truly direct &#91;aright&#93; thy heart, and spread out thy hands toward him: —</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:23; James 4:8; Job 4:7"> If wrong be in thy hand, put it far away, and let not wickedness dwell in thy tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 22:26; 1 John 2:28; 1 John 3:19–22"> For then canst thou lift up thy face free from blemish: yea, thou wilt stand steadfast, and needest not to fear;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 65:16; Ecclesiastes 5:20; John 16:21"> Because thou wilt truly forget thy trouble, and as a waterflood that is passed away wilt thou remember it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 58:8–10; Psalms 37:6; Proverbs 4:18"> And brighter than the noon of day will thy earthly existence arise; and thy obscurity will be like thy morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:24–26; Psalms 43:5; Psalms 3:5"> And thou wilt feel trust, because there is hope: yea, thou wilt search about carefully, and thou wilt lie down in safety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 45:12; Isaiah 60:14; Job 42:8–9"> Also thou wilt stretch thyself out &#91;to rest&#93;, with none to make thee afraid; and many will entreat thy favor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:65; Job 31:16; Job 17:5"> But the eyes of the wicked shall fail, and the means of escape will vanish from them, and their &#91;sole&#93; hope shall be the breathing out of their soul.</span>
