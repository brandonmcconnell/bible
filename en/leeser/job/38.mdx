<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 40:6; Job 37:1–2; 2 Kings 2:11"> Then did the Lord address Job out of the storm-wind, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 42:3; 1 Timothy 1:7; Job 35:16"> Who is this that casteth darkness &#91;on my&#93; counsel by words without knowledge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 40:7; Exodus 12:11; Jeremiah 1:17"> Do but gird up like a mighty man thy loins: and I will ask thee, and do thou inform me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 104:5; Hebrews 1:10; Proverbs 30:4"> Where wast thou when I laid the foundations of the earth? tell it, if thou hast any understanding &#91;of it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 40:12; Zechariah 2:1–2; Job 11:9"> Who fixed her measurements, if thou knowest it? or who stretched the measuring-line over her?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 26:7; Psalms 104:5; Psalms 93:1"> Upon what are her foundation-pillars placed at rest? or who laid her corner-stone:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 5:11; Job 1:6; Ezra 3:11–12"> When altogether sang the morning stars in gladness, and shouted for joy all the sons of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 1:9; Proverbs 8:29; Jeremiah 5:22"> And who closed up with doors the sea, when, issuing forth, it came out of the deep bosom of the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 1:2"> When I made the clouds its garment, and thick fog its swaddling-cloth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:22; Psalms 104:9; Psalms 33:7"> And when I decreed for it my law, and set &#91;for it&#93; bars and doors,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 89:9; Proverbs 8:29; Psalms 65:6–7"> And said, Thus far mayest thou come, but no farther; and here shall be stayed &#91;thy strength&#93; in the pride of thy waves?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 74:16; Psalms 148:3–5; Genesis 1:5"> Didst thou ever, in all thy days, command the morning; didst thou ever assign the morning-dawn its place:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 104:35; Job 37:3; Exodus 14:27"> That it might lay hold of the ends of the earth, so that the wicked might be shaken out therefrom?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 104:6; Psalms 104:2"> She is changed as the sealing-clay: and &#91;all things&#93; stand as though newly clad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 10:15; Psalms 37:17; Job 18:5"> And from the wicked is their light withdrawn, and the high-raised arm is broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 8:24; Job 26:5–6; Psalms 77:19"> Didst thou ever penetrate as far as the springs of the sea? or wander through the bottom of the deep?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 9:13; Psalms 107:18; Job 3:5"> Were the gates of death ever laid open unto thee? or canst thou see the doors of the shadow of death?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 89:11–12; Revelation 20:9; Jeremiah 31:37"> Hast thou a clear understanding of the breadth of the earth? Tell it, if thou knowest it all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:8; Genesis 1:14–18; Genesis 1:3–4"> Where is the way &#91;to the spot where&#93; the light dwelleth? and the darkness—where is its place,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 10:19; Job 26:10; Genesis 23:17"> That thou mightest take each to its boundary, and that thou mightest mark the pathways to its house?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 15:7; Job 38:12; Job 38:4"> Thou &#91;surely&#93; knowest it; because thou wast then born, and the number of thy days is great!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 37:6; Psalms 135:7; Psalms 33:7"> Didst thou ever enter into the treasuries of the snow? or canst thou see the treasuries of the hail,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 10:11; Isaiah 30:30; Exodus 9:18"> Which I have reserved for the time of distress, for the day of fight and battle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 38:12–13; Jonah 4:8; Matthew 24:27"> Where is the way &#91;to the spot where&#93; the light divideth itself, &#91;where&#93; the east wind is scattered over the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 28:26; Psalms 29:3–10; Job 37:3–6"> Who hath divided off watercourses for the overflowing rain, and a way for the lightning &#91;that is followed by&#93; thunders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:22; Hebrews 6:7–8; Psalms 107:35"> To bring rain on a land, void of men; on a wilderness wherein no son of earth &#91;is found&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 107:35; Psalms 104:14"> To satisfy waste and desolate lands; and to promote the growth of the tender grass?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:22; Psalms 147:8; Jeremiah 10:13"> Hath the rain a father? or who hath begotten the drops of the dew?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 147:16–17; Job 37:10; Job 38:8"> Out of whose womb cometh forth the ice? and the hoary frost of heaven—who giveth birth to it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 37:10"> &#91;When&#93; like a stone the waters are congealed, and the face of the deep is bound in fetters?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Amos 5:8; Job 9:9"> Canst thou bind together the chains of the Pleiades, or loosen the bands of Orion?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 38:31; Job 9:9; 2 Kings 23:5"> Canst thou bring forth the constellations of the zodiac, each in its season? or canst thou guide the Bear with its young?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:35–36; Genesis 8:22; Job 38:12–13"> Knowest thou the laws of heaven? or dost thou appoint its rule on the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 22:11; Job 36:27–28; Amos 5:8"> Canst thou lift up to the clouds thy voice, that the abundance of waters may cover thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 36:32; Job 37:3; Isaiah 65:1"> Canst thou send out lightnings, that they may go, and say unto thee, Here are we?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Job 32:8; Psalms 51:6; Proverbs 2:6"> Who hath put wisdom in the dark clouds? or who hath given understanding to the bright meteors?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Psalms 147:4; Genesis 15:5; Genesis 8:1"> Who ordaineth the skies with wisdom? or who emptieth out the bottles of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> When the dust is poured out as molten metal, and the clods are made to cleave fast together?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 104:21; Job 4:10–11; Psalms 34:10"> Dost thou hunt for the lioness her prey? and suppliest thou the food for the young lions,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Job 37:8; Genesis 49:9; Numbers 24:9"> When they are couched in their lairs, rest in the thicket, lying in wait?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 12:24; Matthew 6:26; Psalms 147:9"> Who provideth for the raven his provision? when his young ones cry unto God, and wander about for lack of food?</span>
