<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 42:9; Job 11:1"> Then answered Zophar the Na'amathite, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 14:29; Psalms 31:22; Job 20:3"> Even therefore do my inmost thoughts give me an answer, and for this reason do I feel a strong excitement within me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 33:3; Job 20:2; Psalms 78:2–5"> Reproof which casteth shame on me must I hear; yet out of my understanding will the spirit give me an answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 9:1–3; Job 15:10; Job 32:7"> Dost thou know this? from the very beginning of things, from the very time when man was placed upon earth it was,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 37:35–36; Job 18:5–6; Acts 12:22–23"> That the triumphal shouting of the wicked is ever of but a recent date, and the joy of the hypocrite endureth only for a moment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 14:13–14; Obadiah 1:3–4; Genesis 11:4"> Though his exaltation should mount up to the heavens, and his head should reach unto the clouds;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 7:10; 1 Kings 14:10; Job 4:20"> Yet when he but turneth round will he vanish for ever; those who have seen him will say, Where is he?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 90:5; Psalms 73:20; Isaiah 29:7–8"> Like a dream will he fly away, and men will find him no more; yea, he will be chased away like a vision of the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 7:8; Job 7:10; Job 8:18"> If an eye have surveyed him, it will not do so again, and it will not behold him any more in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 20:18; Luke 19:8; Job 27:16–17"> His children will suffer oppression from the indigent, and his hands will have to restore his &#91;ill-gotten&#93; wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:26; Psalms 25:7; Job 21:26"> &#91;Now&#93; his bones are full of his youthful vigor; but it will &#91;suddenly&#93; lie down with him in the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 15:16; Psalms 10:7; Ecclesiastes 11:9"> If the evil be sweet in his mouth, he will conceal it under his tongue;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 9:43–49; Romans 8:13; Matthew 5:29–30"> He will cherish it, and not forsake it; and hold it back within his palate;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 3:13; Psalms 51:8–9; Psalms 32:3–4"> His food is thus changed within his bowels, and becometh the venom of asps within him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 23:8; Matthew 27:3–4"> The wealth which he hath swallowed, will he have to vomit up again; God will drive it out of his belly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 28:3–6; Romans 3:13; Deuteronomy 32:24"> The poison of asps will he have to suck; the viper's tongue will slay him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13–14; Job 29:6; Luke 16:24"> He shall not look with pleasure on streams, on flowing brooks of honey and cream.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 20:10; Job 20:15; Jeremiah 51:44"> He restoreth what he hath labored for, and will not swallow it down; however much he may have obtained by toil, he will not have any joy of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 35:9; Proverbs 14:31; James 5:4"> Because he oppressed and forsook the indigent; because he took violently away a house, shall he not rebuild it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:13–14; Isaiah 57:20–21"> Because he knew not quietness in his bosom, shall he not escape through what is the most precious to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:11; Job 15:29; Luke 16:24–25"> Nothing was spared from his craving to eat: therefore shall his wealth not prosper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 15:29; Revelation 18:7; 2 Kings 24:2"> In the fulness of his abundance will distress assail him: every hand of &#91;those he&#93; troubled will come against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 78:30–31; Numbers 11:33; Psalms 11:6"> In order to fill his belly, &#91;God&#93; will send out against him the fury of his wrath, and will rain it upon him for his eating.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 24:18; Amos 5:19; 2 Samuel 22:35"> If he flee from the iron weapon, the brazen bow will strike him through.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 16:13; Job 18:11; Deuteronomy 32:41"> He draweth it, and it cometh out of the body; yea, out of his gall the glittering &#91;arrow&#93; cometh forth: over him come the terrors &#91;of death&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 21:9; Psalms 120:4; Isaiah 30:33"> Entire darkness is laid by for his treasures: a fire not urged by blowing will consume him; it will destroy any one that is left in his tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 26:21; Luke 12:2–3; Job 16:18"> The heavens will lay open his iniquity; and the earth will raise herself up against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 27:14–19; Revelation 18:17; James 5:1–3"> The product of his house will be banished, flowing away on the day of his wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 27:13; Job 31:2–3; Psalms 11:5–6"> This is the portion of a wicked man from God, and his decreed heritage from God.</span>
