<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 12:4; Psalms 35:15–16; Psalms 69:12"> But now they who are younger than I in years laugh at me, whose fathers I scorned to put as equals with the dogs of my flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Yea, what possible use can the strength of their hands be unto me, over whom old age hath passed fruitlessly?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 24:5; Hebrews 11:38; Job 24:13–16"> Who suffer for want and famine in solitude; who flee into the wilderness &#91;where all is&#93; darkness, ruin, and desolation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 15:16; 2 Kings 4:38–39; Amos 7:14"> Who crop off mallows by the bushes, and have broom-bush roots as their bread;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 4:25; Daniel 4:32–33; Psalms 109:10"> Who are driven forth from among &#91;men&#93;, who are shouted after as though they were thieves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:1–2; Judges 6:2; Isaiah 2:19"> To dwell in the caverns of the valleys, in holes of the earth, and on naked cliffs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 6:5; Job 11:12; Genesis 16:12"> Among the bushes they shriek; under briers they are huddled together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 16:22; Job 40:4; Isaiah 32:6"> The children of the worthless, yea, the children of the nameless, who were outcasts from the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Lamentations 3:63; Job 17:6; Lamentations 3:14"> But now I am become their song, and I am become a byword unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 26:67; Isaiah 50:6; Numbers 12:14"> They loathe me, they keep themselves far from me, and from my face they withhold not their spittle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 12:21; Ruth 1:21; Job 12:18"> Because he hath loosened the cord of my bow, and afflicted me, they have also cast off the bridle before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 19:12; Isaiah 3:5; Job 19:18"> Against my right hand rise up this swarm of worthless youths: they push away my feet, and they level against me their calamity-bringing paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 3:12; Zechariah 1:15; Psalms 69:26"> They destroy my footpath, they help forward my downfall, without any one to aid them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:16; Psalms 18:4; Psalms 69:14–15"> As &#91;through&#93; a broad breach they come: amidst a loud noise they rolled themselves along.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 13:3; Job 6:4; Isaiah 44:22"> Terrors have turned their face against me; they chase like the wind my glory: and like a cloud is my happiness passed away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 42:4; Psalms 22:14; Isaiah 53:12"> And now my soul is poured out over me; the days of affliction have seized on me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 7:4; Job 33:19–21; Isaiah 38:13"> All night it holloweth out my bones out of my body; and my pursuers take no rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 7:5; Job 19:20; Job 2:7"> Through the Almlghty's power is my garment made unknown: like the opening of my coat hath he enclosed me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 9:31; Job 42:6; Genesis 18:27"> He hath cast me into the mire, and I am become like dust and ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 19:7; Psalms 80:4–5; Psalms 22:2"> I cry aloud unto thee, but thou answerest me not: I stand up, and thou fixest thy regard against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 10:14–17; Job 16:9–14; Job 19:6–9"> Thou art changed into a cruel master toward me: with the strength of thy hand thou assailest me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 27:21; Psalms 1:4; Hosea 4:19"> Thou liftest me up to the wind; thou causest me to pass away, and dissolvest in me all wise counsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:5–7; Hebrews 9:27; Ecclesiastes 9:5"> For I know that thou wilt bring me back to death, and to the house of assembly for all the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Judges 5:31; Matthew 27:39–44; Job 19:7"> But doth not a man stretch out his hand among ruins? or doth one not cry out therefrom &#91;for help&#93; when he meeteth his downfall?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Psalms 35:13–14; Jeremiah 13:17"> Did I not weep for him that was hard pressed by misfortune? was not my soul grieved for the needy?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:15; Job 3:25–26; Jeremiah 14:19"> That I hoped for good, but there came evil; and I waited for light, and there came darkness!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Lamentations 2:11; Jeremiah 31:20; Jeremiah 4:19"> My bowels heave, and rest not: the days of affliction have overcome me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 42:9; Psalms 38:6; Psalms 43:2"> I walk about mournfully without sunlight: when I rise up, in the assembly, I cry with pain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Micah 1:8; Psalms 102:6; Psalms 44:19"> I am a brother to &#91;howling&#93; monsters, and a companion to ostriches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Lamentations 4:8; Psalms 102:3; Psalms 119:83"> My skin hangeth down black from me, and my bones are burnt from heat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Lamentations 5:15; Isaiah 21:4; Isaiah 22:12"> And thus is changed to mourning my harp, and my pipe to the sound of weeping.</span>
