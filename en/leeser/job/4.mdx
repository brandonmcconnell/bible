<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 8:1; Job 22:1; Job 15:1"> Then answered Eliphaz the Themanite, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 32:18–20; Acts 4:20; Jeremiah 6:11"> If we essay to address a word to thee, wilt thou be wearied? yet who is able to refrain from speaking?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3; Hebrews 12:12; Proverbs 10:21"> Behold, thou hast &#91;ere this&#93; corrected many, and weak hands thou wast wont to strengthen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 12:18; Psalms 145:14; Hebrews 12:12"> Him that stumbled thy words used to uphold, and to sinking knees thou gavest vigor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 19:21; 2 Corinthians 4:16; Job 2:5"> Yet now, when it cometh to thee, thou art wearied: it toucheth even thee, and thou art terrified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 3:26; Proverbs 14:26; Job 1:1"> Is not then thy fear of God still thy confidence, thy hope equal to the integrity of thy ways?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 37:25; 2 Peter 2:9; Job 36:7"> Remember, I pray thee, who ever perished, being innocent? or where were the righteous destroyed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 6:7–8; Proverbs 22:8; Hosea 8:7"> Even as I have seen, that those who plough wrong-doing, and sow trouble, have to reap the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 15:30; Isaiah 11:4; Isaiah 30:33"> Before the breathing of God they perish, and before the breath of his nostrils they come to their end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 58:6; Proverbs 30:14; Psalms 3:7"> The roaring of the lion, and the voice of the fierce lion, and the teeth of the young lions, are broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 34:10; Job 8:3–4; Job 1:19"> The old lion perisheth for lack of prey, and the whelps of the lioness have to scatter themselves abroad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 26:14; Psalms 62:11; 1 Corinthians 13:12"> But to me a word came by stealth, and my ear took in a scarcely perceptible whisper thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 33:14–16; Genesis 2:21; Genesis 31:24"> In intense thoughts out of visions of the night, when deep sleep falleth on men:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Job 33:19; Luke 1:12"> Dread came over me, with trembling, and it caused all my bones to shudder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 1:7; Daniel 5:6; Psalms 104:4"> Then flitted a spirit past before my face; the hair of my body stood up:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 19:12"> It stood still, but I could not recognize its form; a figure was before my eyes, a slight whisper, then a &#91;louder&#93; voice I heard, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 9:2; Job 25:4; Ecclesiastes 7:20"> Can a mortal be more righteous than God? or can a man be more pure than his Maker?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 25:5–6; 2 Peter 2:4; Jude 1:6"> Behold, in his servants he putteth no trust, and his angels he chargeth with folly:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 10:9; Genesis 2:7; Genesis 3:19"> How much less in those that dwell in houses of clay, whose foundation is in the dust, who are crushed till they come to be eaten by the moth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 20:7; Job 14:14; Job 16:22"> From morning to evening are they broken to pieces: without laying it &#91;to heart&#93; they perish for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 36:12; Psalms 39:11; Job 8:22"> Behold, their excellency which is in them is torn away: they die, and this without wisdom.</span>
