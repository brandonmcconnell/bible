<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then answered Eliphaz the Themanite, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 17:10; Galatians 6:7–8; Job 35:6–8"> Can a man be serviceable unto God? Truly the intelligent is serviceable unto himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 15:8; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Psalms 147:10–11"> Is it any pleasure to the Almighty that thou art righteous? or is it any gain to him, that thou makest thy ways perfect?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 14:3; Psalms 143:2; Revelation 3:19"> Is it out of fear of thee that he will reproach thee? or go with thee into judgment?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 40:12; Job 15:5–6; Job 15:31–34"> Is not thy evil great? and no end to thy iniquities?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 22:26; Ezekiel 18:12; Ezekiel 18:16"> For thou hast taken a pledge from thy brothers for nought, and stripped the naked of their clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 31:17; Matthew 25:42; Isaiah 58:7"> Thou hast not given water to the weary to drink, and from the hungry thou hast withholden bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 9:15; Job 29:7–17; Job 13:8"> But as for the man of a strong arm, he obtained the land, and the highly honored could dwell therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 24:21; Job 24:3; Ezekiel 22:7"> Widows hast thou sent away empty, and the arms of the fatherless have been broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 18:8–10; Job 13:21; Job 6:4"> Therefore are snares round about thee, and sudden dread terrifieth thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 69:1–2; Lamentations 3:54; Jonah 2:3"> Or seest thou not the darkness? and the abundance of water which covereth thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 11:8; Isaiah 66:1; Psalms 8:3–4"> Is not God in the height of heaven? and beholding the highest elevation of the stars, however high they are?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:12; Psalms 10:11; Ezekiel 9:9"> But thou sayest, “What doth God know? can he judge behind the darkness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; Psalms 139:11–12; Psalms 33:14"> Thick clouds are a covering for him, so that he will not see; and he walketh along on the circle of heaven.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 17:26–27; Genesis 6:5; Genesis 6:11–13"> Wilt thou &#91;thus&#93; observe the path of ancient times which the men of injustice have trodden?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 15:32; Genesis 7:17–24; Psalms 55:23"> Who were shrivelled up before their time, whose foundation was flooded away like a river;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 21:14–15; Romans 1:28; Psalms 4:6"> Who said unto God, “'Depart from us:” and what wrought the Almighty for them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 21:16; Job 12:6; Acts 14:17"> And yet it was he that filled their houses with good things; but the counsel of the wicked be &#91;still&#93; far from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 58:10; Psalms 107:42; Psalms 52:6"> The righteous will see it, and be glad; and the innocent will laugh them to scorn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 2:6–7; Job 1:16; Job 20:26"> “Is not he destroyed that rose up against us, and hath not the fire consumed what they had left?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:9; Isaiah 27:5; Philippians 4:7"> Do but become acquainted with him, and be at peace: thereby will happiness come unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; Job 23:12; Deuteronomy 4:1–2"> Do but accept instruction from his mouth, and lay up his saying in thy heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 11:13–14; Zechariah 1:3; Job 8:5–6"> If thou return to the Almighty, thou shalt be built up, so thou wilt but remove wrong-doing far away from thy tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 13:12; 1 Kings 9:28; Genesis 10:29"> And throw down in the dust precious metals, and &#91;the gold of&#93; Ophir to the stones of the brooks:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 33:6; Psalms 16:5–6; Genesis 15:1"> Then will the Almighty be thy precious metal, and brightly-shining silver unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; Isaiah 58:14; Job 27:10"> For then wilt thou have in the Almighty thy delight, and thou canst lift up unto God thy face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 58:9; Psalms 116:1; 1 John 5:14–15"> Thou wilt make entreaty unto him, and he will hear thee, and thy vows wilt thou pay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; Psalms 90:17; Psalms 20:4"> And if thou decree a thing, it will be fulfilled unto thee; and upon thy ways the light will shine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="James 4:6; Job 5:19–27; Luke 1:52"> For when men are brought low, thou wilt say, Pride &#91;hath done it&#93;; but those of lowly eyes &#91;God&#93; will help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 42:7–8; James 5:15–16; Genesis 18:26–32"> He will even deliver him who is not guiltless: and thou wilt be delivered by the purity of thy hands.</span>
