<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then answered Job, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 4:17; Romans 3:20; Job 25:4"> Truly I know that it is so: and how could a mortal be righteous before God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 10:2; Job 40:2; Isaiah 57:15–16"> If he were desirous to enter into a contest with him, he could not give him one answer out of a thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 36:5; Romans 11:33; Ephesians 3:20"> He is wise of heart, and mighty in strength: who hath hardened himself against him, and escaped unscathed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; Luke 21:11; Habakkuk 3:10"> &#91;He it is&#93; who removeth mountains, and they know it not, yea, when he overturneth them in his anger;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Haggai 2:6; Hebrews 12:26; Psalms 75:3"> Who shaketh the earth loose out of her place, that her pillars tremble;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Amos 8:9; Luke 21:25–26"> Who speaketh to the sun, and he shineth not, and around the stars he placeth a seal;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 44:24; Isaiah 40:22; Psalms 104:2–3"> Who spread out the heavens by himself alone, and treadeth upon the hillocks of the sea;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 5:8; Genesis 1:16; Job 38:31–41"> Who made the Bear, Orion, and the Pleiades, and the chambers the south;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 5:9; Psalms 72:18; Ephesians 3:20"> Who doth great things which are quite unsearchable, and wonders which are quite without number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 23:8–9; Job 35:14; Psalms 77:19"> Lo, were he to go past by me, I should not see him; and were he to pass along, I should not perceive him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 45:9; Job 11:10; Job 23:13"> Behold, were he to snatch aught away, who could hold him back? who would say unto him, What dost thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 89:10; Job 26:12; Isaiah 30:7"> God will not withdraw his anger: beneath him sink down the helpers of the proud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 8:27; Job 9:3; Job 4:19"> How much less then could I answer him, and select my words &#91;to contend&#93; with him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 10:15; Job 8:5; Job 10:2"> Whom, were I even righteous, I could not answer? to him that condemneth me I could &#91;only&#93; make supplication.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 29:24; Exodus 6:9; Psalms 66:18–20"> Or were I to call, and he would answer me, I could yet not believe that he would give ear unto my voice—</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 16:14; Job 2:3; Job 34:6"> He that bruiseth me with &#91;his&#93; tempest, and multiplieth my wounds without a cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 7:19; Lamentations 3:15; Psalms 88:15–18"> He suffereth me not to recover my breath; but feedeth me overmuch with bitter things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 9:32–33; Job 9:4; 1 Corinthians 1:25"> If it regard strength, lo, he is the powerful; and if justice, who will cite him for me to appear?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 15:5–6; Philippians 3:12–15; Psalms 143:2"> If I were righteous even, my own mouth would condemn me: were I innocent, it would still prove me perverse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 1:1; Psalms 139:23–24; Proverbs 28:26"> I am innocent; I will not have regard for myself: I will despise my life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:1–3; Luke 13:2–4; Job 10:8"> One thing is &#91;certain&#93;, therefore have I said it, The innocent and the wicked he bringeth to their end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 24:12; Job 1:13–19; Job 4:7"> If a scourge should slay suddenly, he will mock at the trial of the guiltless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 10:3; Jeremiah 12:1–2; Psalms 73:3–7"> Is a land given up into the hand of the wicked? he covereth the faces of its judges: if this be not the truth, who is it then?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 7:6–7; James 4:14; Psalms 90:9–10"> And my days pass swifter than a runner: they flee away, they see no happiness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:8; Isaiah 18:2; Job 39:27–30"> They hasten along like pirate ships: like the eagle that stoopeth down upon his food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 7:13; Jeremiah 8:18; Job 7:11"> If I say, I will forget my complaint, I will leave off my sorrowful countenance, and recover my cheerfulness:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; Psalms 88:15–16; Job 21:6"> O then would I be in dread of all my pains; I know that thou wilt not declare me innocent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 10:7; Job 21:27; Job 10:14–17"> I must ever be guilty: why then should I fatigue myself for nought?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:22; Psalms 26:6; 1 John 1:8"> If I were to wash myself in snow-water, to cleanse myself in the purity of my hands:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 64:6; Isaiah 59:6; Job 15:6"> Even then wouldst thou plunge me in the ditch, that my own clothes would render me abhorred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 9:20; Ecclesiastes 6:10; Psalms 143:2"> For he is not a man, like me, that I could answer him, that we should enter together into a contest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:25; 1 John 2:1–2; Job 9:19"> There is no one who can decide between us, who could lay his hand upon us both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 39:10; Job 13:11; Job 33:7"> Let him but remove from me his rod, and let not his dread terrify me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Then would I speak, and not fear him; for the like I feel not within me.</span>
