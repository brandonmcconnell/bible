<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then answered Job, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 64:3; Job 18:2; Job 27:2"> How long will ye grieve my soul, and crush me with words?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 31:7; Nehemiah 4:12; Job 18:4–21"> These ten times have ye reproached me: ye are not ashamed when ye show yourselves as strangers to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Proverbs 9:12; Ezekiel 18:4"> Yea, if it be indeed that I have erred, let my error remain with myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 35:26; Psalms 38:16; Psalms 55:12"> But if indeed ye wish to magnify yourselves above me, and to prove against me my disgrace:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 18:8–10; Job 27:2; Job 16:11–14"> Then know for certain that God hath bent me down, and hath laid his net all around me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:2–3; Lamentations 3:8; Job 31:35–36"> Behold, I cry out concerning the violence &#91;done me&#93;, but I am not answered: I entreat aloud, but there is no justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 3:7; Job 3:23; Proverbs 4:19"> My road hath he fenced up, so that I cannot pass out; and on my paths he placeth darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:44; Job 29:7–14; Psalms 89:39"> My glory hath he stripped from me, and removed the crown of my head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 24:20; Psalms 37:35–36; 2 Corinthians 4:8–9"> He hath pulled me down on every side, and I am going hence; and he hath rooted up like a tree my hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:24; Job 16:9; Job 33:10"> He hath also kindled against me his wrath, and he counteth me with himself as one of his adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 30:12; Job 16:13; Isaiah 51:23"> Altogether come on his troops, and make level against me their way, and encamp round about my tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 88:8; Psalms 88:18; Psalms 69:8"> My brothers hath he removed far from me, and my acquaintance are entirely estranged from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 38:11; Jeremiah 20:10; 2 Samuel 16:23"> My near of kin have withdrawn, and those befriended by me have forgotten me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 31:31–32; Psalms 123:3"> Ye that sojourn in my house, and my maid-servants, regard me as a stranger: an alien am I become in their eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 1:15–17; Job 1:19"> I call for my servant, but he will not answer, though I were to entreat him with my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 2:9–10; Job 17:1"> My breath is become nauseous to my wife, and my caressing, to the children of my own body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 2:23; Job 30:12; Job 30:1"> Yea, children even despise me: I rise up, but they speak against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; Psalms 109:4–5; Psalms 55:12–14"> All that have had my confidence abominate me; and those whom I have loved are turned against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 102:5; Lamentations 4:8; Job 33:19–22"> To my skin and to my flesh my bones do cleave, and I must sustain myself with the gums of my teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 1:11; Psalms 38:2; Job 2:5"> Spare me, spare me, O ye, my friends; for the hand of God hath touched me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 69:26; Job 31:31; Job 16:13–14"> Why will ye persecute me as God &#91;hath done&#93;, and will never be satisfied with my flesh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 30:8; Job 31:35; Isaiah 8:1"> Oh who would but grant, that my words might be written down! oh who would grant that they were entered in a book!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:1; Exodus 28:21; Exodus 32:16"> That they were hewn with an iron pen and &#91;blackened with&#93; lead for eternity in the hard rock!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ephesians 1:7; Isaiah 54:5; Psalms 78:35"> And well I know that my redeemer liveth, and that he will remain as the last after the creatures of the dust &#91;are passed away&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 5:8; 1 John 3:2; Psalms 17:15"> And after my skin is cut to pieces will this be: and then freed from my body shall I behold God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 73:26; Isaiah 26:19; Numbers 24:17"> Whom I shall myself behold to my happiness, and whom my eyes will see, and not as a stranger, &#91;when even&#93; my reins are consumed within my bosom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 19:22; Psalms 69:26; 1 Kings 14:13"> But if ye should say, How will we pursue him? seeing the root of the matter is found in me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:14; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Psalms 58:10–11"> Then have dread for yourselves of the sword; for the wrath &#91;which ye excite&#93; is an iniquity that bringeth the sword; in order that ye may know there is one that judgeth &#91;in the world&#93;.</span>
