<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 1:7; Ecclesiastes 3:17; 2 Peter 3:7–8"> Why are not times &#91;of punishment&#93; treasured up by the Almighty, and why do his adorers not see his days &#91;of retribution&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:14; Deuteronomy 27:17; Proverbs 23:10"> &#91;The wicked&#93; remove landmarks: they rob flocks, and feed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:6; Deuteronomy 24:10–13; 1 Samuel 12:3"> They drive away the ass of the fatherless, they take in pledge the widow's ox.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 28:28; Job 24:14; Ezekiel 22:29"> They chase the needy out of the highway: altogether hide themselves the poor of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 2:1; Job 39:5–7; Psalms 104:23"> Behold, as wild asses in the wilderness go they forth to their work, rising betimes after &#91;their&#93; prey: the desert yieldeth food for them and for their young men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Micah 6:15; Deuteronomy 28:51; Deuteronomy 28:33"> In the field they reap their food; and in the vineyard of the wicked they gather the fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 22:26–27; Job 22:6; Deuteronomy 24:11–13"> They cause &#91;the poor&#93; to spend the night naked, without clothing and without any covering in the cold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 4:5; Song of Solomon 5:2; Hebrews 11:38"> Through the sweeping rain of the mountains are they made wet, and for want of a shelter do they embrace the rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:5; 2 Kings 4:1"> The others pluck from the breast the fatherless, and the garment of the poor they take in pledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 2:7–8; Deuteronomy 24:19; Amos 5:11–12"> They cause him to go naked without clothing, and from the hungry they take away the sheaf:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="James 5:4; Jeremiah 22:13; Deuteronomy 25:4"> Within their walls do they make oil, they tread their wine-presses, and suffer thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:1; Malachi 2:17; Romans 2:4–5"> Out of a populous city is groaning heard, and the soul of the deadly wounded crieth out: yet God regardeth it not as an offence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 3:19–20; Proverbs 4:19; Jude 1:6"> Yon men are of those that rebel against the light: they know not its ways, nor abide in its paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Micah 2:1–2; Psalms 10:8–10; Revelation 3:3"> With the earliest light riseth the murderer, he slayeth the poor and needy, and in the night he becometh like the thief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 10:11; Proverbs 7:9–10; Psalms 94:7"> And the eye of the adulterer watcheth for the twilight, saying, No eye will see me; and placeth a covering on his face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 3:20; Ephesians 5:11–13; Matthew 24:43"> They break into houses in the dark, in the daytime they lock themselves in: they know not the light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 3:5; Jeremiah 2:26; Psalms 73:18–19"> For to all of these alike is the morning as the shadow of death; for they are familiar with the terrors of the shadow of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 69:22; Psalms 58:7; Job 9:26"> Swift are such men &#91;to flee&#93; on the face of the water; accursed is their field on the land; none of them turneth himself to the way, of the vineyards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 49:14; Luke 16:22; Proverbs 14:32"> Drought and heat speedily consume the snow-waters: so doth the grave those who have sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; Daniel 4:14; Job 18:16–17"> The mother that bore such a one will forget him; the worm will feed sweetly on him; he will be no more remembered; and like a tree will wickedness be broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:6–7; Job 22:9; Job 29:13"> He ill-treateth the barren that heareth not; and to the widow he acteth not well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 6:4–9; Esther 3:8–10; John 19:12–16"> But he also draweth down the mighty with his power: he riseth up, no one is sure of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 15:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Revelation 2:23"> To such &#91;God&#93; granteth to be in safety, that he may find support; and His eyes are upon their ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 37:10; Psalms 37:35–36; James 5:1–3"> They are exalted; in but a little while they are no more; and they are brought down low: like all others are they gathered in, and like the top of the ear of corn are they cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 9:24; Job 11:2–3; Job 6:28"> But if it be not so, who will prove me a liar, and render nought my word?</span>
