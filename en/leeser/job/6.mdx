<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 4:1"> Then answered Job, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 31:6; Job 23:2; Job 4:5"> Oh that my vexation could be truly weighed, and my calamity; oh that men might lift it up in the balances at once!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 27:3; Job 23:2; Psalms 40:5"> For now it is already heavier than the sand of the sea: therefore are my words confused.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 38:2; Job 30:15; Psalms 88:15–16"> For the arrows of the Almighty are within me, the poison whereof my spirit drinketh it: the terrors of God set themselves in array against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joel 1:18–20; Jeremiah 14:6; Psalms 42:1"> Doth the wild ass bray over the grass? or loweth the ox over his fodder?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 34:3; Luke 14:34; Colossians 4:6"> Is ever tasteless food eaten without salt? or is there any flavor in the white of an egg?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 3:24; Ezekiel 4:16; Daniel 10:3"> My soul refuseth to touch them: they are unto me like disgusting food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 119:81; Job 17:14–16; Job 6:11–13"> Oh that some one would grant the accomplishment of my request; and that God would grant me the fulfillment of my hope!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 19:4; Jonah 4:3; Jonah 4:8"> Yea, that it would please God that he might crush me: that he would let loose his hand, and make an end of me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 23:12; Leviticus 19:2; Isaiah 57:15"> Then would this be still my comfort; yea, I would rejoice in my pain while be would not spare: that I have not gainsaid the commands of the Holy One.—</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 103:14–16; Psalms 102:23; Psalms 39:5"> What is my strength, that I should wait? and what my end, that I should yet longer retain my patience?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 41:24; Job 40:18"> Is the strength of stones my strength? or is my flesh brazen?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 26:2; 2 Corinthians 1:12; Job 19:28"> Truly, am I not without my help in me? and is not wise counsel driven far away from me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 17:17; 1 Corinthians 12:26; Galatians 6:2"> As though I were one who refuseth kindness to his friend, and forsaketh the fear of the Almighty:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 38:11; Jeremiah 15:18; Psalms 41:9"> My brothers are treacherous as a brook, like flowing brooks they pass along;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Which are made turbid by reason of the ice, wherein the snow hideth itself;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; Job 24:19"> At the time when they feel the warmth, they vanish; when it is hot, they are quenched out of their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The paths of their course wind themselves along; they go in the wilderness and are lost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 21:14; Genesis 25:15; Jeremiah 25:23"> The caravans of Thema look hither, the travelling companies Sheba hope for them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:3–4; Romans 5:5; Romans 9:33"> But they stand ashamed because they had trusted; they come thither and are made to blush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 38:11; 2 Timothy 4:16; Jeremiah 17:5–6"> For truly now ye are like such a one: ye see my terrible state and are afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 20:33; Job 42:11; 1 Samuel 12:3"> Have I then ever said, Give me something, and out of your property offer a bribe in my behalf?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 49:15; Psalms 49:7–8; Psalms 107:2"> And deliver me from the hand of the adversary? and redeem from the hand of tyrants?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="James 3:2; Proverbs 9:9; Psalms 39:1–2"> Teach me, and I will indeed remain silent; and wherein I erred give me to understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 32:3; Job 13:5; Proverbs 25:11"> How pleasant are straightforward words! but what doth arguing prove?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 8:2; Ephesians 4:14; Job 42:3"> Do ye think to reprove words, and &#91;to regard&#93; as wind the speeches of one that is despairing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 24:9; Job 24:3; 2 Peter 2:3"> Yea, ye would cast any thing upon the fatherless, and ye would dig a pit against your friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 13:4; Job 33:3; Job 27:4"> But now, if it please you, turn yourselves toward me, and &#91;say&#93; whether I would lie before your face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 17:10; Job 34:5; Job 27:4–6"> Reflect again, I pray you, there will be no wrong: yea, reflect once more, my righteousness &#91;will be found&#93; therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 12:11; Job 6:6; Job 34:3"> Is there any wrong on my tongue? or should my palate not understand &#91;if I spoke&#93; what is iniquitous?</span>
