<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 21:3; Job 16:3; Job 19:2–3"> Lo! ye yourselves have all beheld it: why is it then that ye deal in such vanities?</span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 1:8; Job 8:14–15; Isaiah 51:8"> He buildeth his house like the moth, and like a hut that a keeper hath made.</span>
