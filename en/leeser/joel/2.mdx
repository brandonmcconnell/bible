<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 2:15; Joel 1:15; 1 Peter 4:7"> Blow ye the cornet in Zion, and sound an alarm on my holy mount; let all the inhabitants of the land tremble; for the day of the Lord cometh, for it is nigh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joel 1:6; Zephaniah 1:14–15; Joel 2:10–11"> It is a day of darkness and of gloom, a day of clouds and of tempestuous obscurity, like the morning-dawn spread out upon the mountains: a people numerous and strong, the like of which hath never been and after it there will be none any more, even to the years of all coming generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 51:3; Joel 1:19–20; Genesis 2:8"> Before it devoureth a fire; and behind it singeth a flame: like the garden of 'Eden was the land before it &#91;came&#93;, and after it is a desolate wilderness; yea, and nothing escapeth from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 9:7"> Like the appearance of horses is its appearance; and like horsemen, so do they run.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 9:9; Isaiah 5:24; Isaiah 30:30"> Like the noise of chariots on the tops of mountains do they leap; they are like the noise of a flame of fire consuming the stubble, as a strong people arrayed for a battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nahum 2:10; Isaiah 13:8; Jeremiah 30:6"> At its presence the people are much pained: all the faces are covered with blackness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 30:27; 2 Samuel 1:23; 2 Samuel 2:18–19"> Like mighty men do they run; like men of war they climb up a wall; and they march every one on his own ways, and they turn not aside on their paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 23:10; Job 33:18; 2 Chronicles 32:5"> And they do not press one another; every one on his beaten track do they go forward: and they pass through between war-like weapons, and change not their purpose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:21; John 10:1; Exodus 10:6"> Into the city they hasten forward; on the wall they run; into the houses they climb up; through the windows they make their entrance like a thief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 24:29; Isaiah 13:10; Joel 3:15–16"> Before them trembleth the earth; the heavens quake; the sun and the moon are obscured, and the stars withdraw their brightness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Malachi 3:2; Nahum 1:6; Jeremiah 30:7"> And the Lord uttereth his voice before his army; for very numerous is his camp; for strong is he that executeth his word; for great is the day of the Lord and very terrible; and who is able to endure it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 4:8–9; Isaiah 55:6–7; 2 Chronicles 7:13–14"> But even now also, saith the Lord, return ye fully to me with all your heart, and with fasting, and with weeping, and with mourning:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 86:15; Psalms 34:18; Exodus 34:6–7"> And rend your heart, and not your garments, and return unto the Lord your God; for gracious and merciful is he, long suffering, and of great kindness, and he bethinketh himself of the evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jonah 3:9; Haggai 2:19; 2 Kings 19:4"> He that is conscious &#91;of guilt&#93;, let him return and repent: when &#91;the plague&#93; may leave behind it a blessing; even a meat-offering and a drink-offering unto the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joel 1:14; Joel 2:1; 1 Kings 21:12"> Blow the cornet in Zion, sanctify a fast, proclaim a solemn assembly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:5; 2 Chronicles 20:13; 1 Corinthians 7:5"> Gather the people, sanctify the congregation, assemble the elders, gather the children, and those that suck the breasts; let the bridegroom go forth out of his chamber, and the bride out of her closet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 115:2; Ezekiel 8:16; Psalms 79:10"> Between the porch and the altar let the priests weep, the ministers of the Lord, and let them say, Spare, O Lord, thy people, and give not up thy heritage to reproach, for nations to make a by-word of them: wherefore should they say among the people, Where is their God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 8:2; Psalms 103:13; Zechariah 1:14"> And the Lord was zealous for his land, and he had pity for his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:29; Ezekiel 36:15; Isaiah 62:8–9"> And the Lord answered and said unto his people, “Behold, I will send you the corn, and the young wine, and the oil, and ye shall be satisfied therewith; and I will not give you up any more to be a reproach among the nations:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Zechariah 14:8; Amos 4:10; Isaiah 34:3"> And the host of the north will I remove far away from you, and I will drive it off into a land barren and desolate, with its advance toward the eastern sea, and its rereward toward the western sea; and its stench shall ascend, and its ill savor shall come up, because it hath done great things.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:16–17; Isaiah 54:4; Isaiah 41:10"> Fear not, O land; be glad and rejoice; for the Lord hath done great things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zechariah 8:12; Psalms 65:12; Psalms 67:6"> Be not afraid, ye beasts of the field; for the pastures of the wilderness have become green; for the tree beareth its fruit, the fig-tree and the vine yield their strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="James 5:7–8; Philippians 4:4; Leviticus 26:4"> And ye children of Zion, be glad, and rejoice in the Lord your God; for he hath given you the first rain in beneficence, and he hath caused to come down for you the rain, the first rain, and the latter rain in the first &#91;month&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Malachi 3:10; Amos 9:13; Joel 3:18"> And the threshing-floors are full of corn, and the vats overflow with young wine and oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Zechariah 10:6; Joel 1:4–7; Joel 2:2–11"> And I will repay to you the years &#91;in&#93; which the locust hath eaten &#91;all&#93;, with the cankerworm, and the cricket, and the caterpillar, my great army, which I had sent against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 10:11; Psalms 22:26; Romans 5:5"> And ye shall eat in plenty, and be satisfied, and praise the name of the Lord your God, who hath dealt wondrously with you: and my people shall not be made ashamed unto eternity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:26–28; Leviticus 26:11–12; Zephaniah 3:17"> And ye shall know that I am in the midst of Israel, and that I am the Lord your God, and none else; and my people shall not be made ashamed unto eternity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 2:16–21; Ezekiel 39:29; Isaiah 32:15"> (3:1) And it shall come to pass after this, that I will pour out my spirit over all flesh: and your sons and your daughters shall prophesy: your old men shall dream dreams: your young men shall see visions:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:13; Galatians 3:28; Colossians 3:11"> (3:2) And also over the men-servants and over the maid-servants in those days will I pour out my spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 2:19–20; Luke 21:11; Matthew 24:29"> (3:3) And I will display wonderful tokens in the heavens and on the earth, blood, and fire, and pillars of smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Revelation 6:12–13; Matthew 24:29; Malachi 4:1"> (3:4) The sun shall be changed into darkness, and the moon into blood, before the coming of the day of the Lord, the great and the terrible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 10:11–14; Acts 2:20–21; Isaiah 46:13"> (3:5) And it shall come to pass, that whosoever shall call on the name of the Lord shall escape; for on mount Zion and in Jerusalem there shall be deliverance, as the Lord hath said, and among the remnant whom the Lord calleth.</span>
