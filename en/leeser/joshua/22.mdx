<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 32:18–33; Deuteronomy 29:7–8"> Then did Joshua call the Reubenites, and the Gadites, and the half tribe of Menasseh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 1:12–18; Deuteronomy 3:16–20; Numbers 32:20–29"> And he said unto them, Ye have indeed kept all that Moses the servant of the Lord commanded you, and ye have obeyed my voice in all that I commanded you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 1:23–27"> Ye have not forsaken your brethren these many days, until this day; but ye have kept the obligation of the commandment of the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 13:8; Numbers 32:18; Deuteronomy 3:20"> And now the Lord your God hath given rest unto your brethren, as he promised them; therefore now turn yourselves, and get you unto your tents, unto the land of your possession, which Moses the servant of the Lord gave unto you on the other side of the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 22:37; Exodus 20:6; Romans 8:28"> Only take diligent heed to practise the commandment and the law, which Moses the servant of the Lord hath commanded you, to love the Lord your God, and to walk in all his ways, and to keep his commandments, and to cleave unto him, and to serve him with all your heart and with all your soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 14:13; Luke 24:50; Genesis 47:7"> And Joshua blessed them, and sent them away; and they went unto their tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 17:1–12; Numbers 32:33; Joshua 13:29–31"> Now to the one half of the tribe of Menasseh Moses had given possession in Bashan; but unto the other half thereof gave Joshua with their brethren on this side of the Jordan westward; and when Joshua sent them also away unto their tents, he blessed them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 31:27; Deuteronomy 8:9–14; Proverbs 3:16"> And he said unto them, as followeth, With much riches return unto your tents, and with very much cattle, with silver, and with gold, and with copper, and with iron, and with very many garments: divide the spoil of your enemies with your brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 32:29; Numbers 32:26; Numbers 32:1"> And the children of Reuben and the children of Gad and the half tribe of Menasseh returned, and departed from the children of Israel from Shiloh, which is in the land of Canaan, to go unto the country of Gil'ad, to the land of their possession, whereof they were possessed, according to the order of the Lord by the hand of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 24:26–27; Genesis 31:46–52; Joshua 4:5–9"> And when they came unto the districts of the Jordan, that are in the land of Canaan, the children of Reuben and the children of Gad and the half tribe of Menasseh built there an altar by the Jordan, a great altar for a show.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:12–14; Leviticus 17:8–9; Joshua 2:7"> And the children of Israel heard, as followeth, Behold, the children of Reuben and the children of Gad and the half tribe of Menasseh have built an altar in the front of the land of Canaan, in the districts of the Jordan, at the side belonging to the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 20:1–11; Deuteronomy 13:15; Galatians 4:17–18"> And when the children of Israel heard it, the whole congregation of the children of Israel assembled themselves together at Shiloh, to go up against them to war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 25:7; Psalms 106:30–31; Exodus 6:25"> And the children of Israel sent unto the children of Reuben, and to the children of Gad, and the half tribe of Menasseh, into the land of Gil'ad, Phinehas the son of Elazar the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 1:4; Exodus 18:25"> And ten princes with him, one prince each for every division of all the tribes of Israel; and each one was a head of their family divisions among the thousands of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:3; 1 Samuel 15:23; Deuteronomy 30:17"> And they came unto the children of Reuben, and to the children of Gad, and to the half tribe of Menasseh, unto the land of Gil'ad, and they spoke with them, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:13–14"> Thus have said the whole congregation of the Lord, What trespass is this that ye have committed against the God of Israel to turn away this day from following the Lord, in that ye have built yourselves an altar, that ye might rebel this day against the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 25:1–18; Psalms 106:28–29; Deuteronomy 4:3–4"> Have we had too little in the iniquity of Peor,—from which we are not yet cleansed until this day,—when there was a plague in the congregation of the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 16:22; Joshua 22:16; Joshua 7:11"> And will ye turn away this day from following the Lord? and it will be, that when ye will rebel this day against the Lord, tomorrow he will be wroth with the whole congregation of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; 2 Chronicles 11:16–17; Acts 10:14–15"> But, notwithstanding, if the land of your possession be unclean, then pass ye over unto the land of the possession of the Lord, wherein dwelleth the tabernacle of the Lord, and take possession in the midst of us; but rebel not against the Lord and against us do not rebel, in building yourselves an altar beside the altar of the Lord our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 7:1; Joshua 7:5; Joshua 7:18"> Did not 'Achan the son of Zerach commit a trespass on the devoted things, and wrath fell on all the congregation of Israel? and he, though but one man, perished not alone in his iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="James 1:19; Proverbs 16:1; 1 Peter 3:15"> But the children of Reuben and the children of Gad and the half tribe of Menasseh answered, and they spoke unto the heads of the thousands of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Kings 8:39; Psalms 44:21; Deuteronomy 10:17"> The God of gods, the Eternal, the God of gods, the Eternal, he knoweth, and Israel also shall know: if it be in rebellion, or if in transgression against the Lord, &#91;aid us not this day,&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:16; Deuteronomy 18:19; Ezekiel 3:18"> That we have built us an altar to turn away from following the Lord; or if to offer thereon burnt-offering or meat-offering, or if to offer thereon peace-offerings, may the Lord himself require it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 4:6; Deuteronomy 6:20; Genesis 30:33"> Or whether we have not done it for fear of this thing, saying, In time to come your children might say unto our children, as followeth, What have ye to do with the Lord, the God of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 12:16; Ezra 4:2–3; 1 Samuel 26:19"> For the Lord hath made a boundary between us and you, ye children of Reuben and children of Gad,—the Jordan; ye have no part in the Lord: thus might your children make our children cease so as not to fear the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Wherefore we said, Let us now act for ourselves to build this altar, not for burnt-offering, nor for sacrifice;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 24:27; Genesis 31:48; Deuteronomy 12:17–18"> But it shall be a witness between us, and you, and our generations after us, that we may perform the service of the Lord before him with our burnt-offerings, and with our sacrifices, and with our, peace-offerings; and that your children may not say in time to come to our children, Ye have no portion in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Ezekiel 43:10–11; Hebrews 8:5"> And we said, that it shall be !!when they should say this to us and to our generations in time to come, that we may say, Behold the pattern of the altar of the Lord, which our fathers made, not for burnt-offering, nor for sacrifice; but it is a witness between us and you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 24:16; Deuteronomy 12:13–14; Joshua 22:23"> Far be it from us that we should rebel against the Lord, and turn away this day from following the Lord, to build an altar for burnt-offering, for meat-offering, or for sacrifice, beside the altar of the Lord our God that is before his dwelling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 22:33; Esther 1:21; Proverbs 15:1"> And when Phinehas the priest, and the princes of the congregation and the heads of the thousands of Israel who were with him, heard the words which the children of Reuben and the children of Gad and the children of Menasseh had spoken, it was pleasing in their eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; Leviticus 26:11–12; Isaiah 12:6"> And Phinehas the son of Elazar the priest said unto the children of Reuben, and to the children of Gad, and to the children of Menasseh, This day do we know that the Lord is in our midst, because ye have not committed this trespass against the Lord: now have ye delivered the children of Israel out of the hand of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Joshua 22:12–14; Proverbs 25:13"> And Phinehas the son of Elazar the priest, and the princes, returned from the children of Reuben, and from the children of Gad, out of the land of Gil'ad, unto the land of Canaan, to the children of Israel, and they brought them word again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:20; Luke 2:28; Daniel 2:19"> And the thing was pleasing in the eyes of the children of Israel; and the children of Israel blessed God; and they did not speak any more to go up against them to battle, to destroy the land wherein the children of Reuben and Gad dwelt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Joshua 22:27; Matthew 4:10; Isaiah 43:10"> And the children of Reuben and the children of Gad called the altar &#91;“'Ed”&#93;; for &#91;they said&#93; it is a witness between us that the Eternal is God.</span>
