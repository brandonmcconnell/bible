<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 6:21; Hebrews 7:1; Joshua 8:2"> Now it came to pass, when Adoni-zedek, the king of Jerusalem heard that Joshua had captured 'Ai, and had utterly destroyed it; &#91;that&#93; as he had done to Jericho and its king, so had he done to 'Ai and its king; and that the inhabitants of Gib'on had made peace with Israel, and were in the midst of them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:25; Joshua 2:24; Exodus 15:14–16"> That they were greatly afraid; because Gib'on was a great city, like one of the royal cities, and because it was greater than 'Ai, and all the men thereof were mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:9; Micah 1:13; Joshua 10:1"> Therefore Adoni-zedek the king of Jerusalem sent unto Hoham the king of Hebron, and unto Piram the king of Yarmuth, and unto Yaphia', the king of Lachish, and unto Debir the king of 'Eglon, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 9:15; Joshua 10:1; Isaiah 8:9–10"> Come up unto me, and help me, that we may smite Gib'on; for it hath made peace with Joshua and with the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 8:9–10; Genesis 15:16; Joshua 10:6"> And the five kings of the Emorites, the king of Jerusalem, the king of Hebron, the king of Yarmuth, the king of Lachish, the king of 'Eglon, assembled themselves together, and went up, they and all their camps, and encamped before Gib'on, and made war against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 9:6; Joshua 5:10; Deuteronomy 1:15"> And the men of Gib'on sent unto Joshua to the camp to Gilgal, saying, Do not withdraw thy hand from thy servants: come up to us quickly, and save us, and help us; for all the kings of the Emorites that dwell in the mountains are assembled together against us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 8:1; Isaiah 8:12; Isaiah 8:14"> And Joshua went up from Gilgal, he, and all the people of war with him, and all the mighty men of valor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Deuteronomy 3:2; Psalms 27:1–2"> And the Lord said unto Joshua, Be not afraid of them; for into thy hand have I delivered them: there shall not stand a man of them before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:10; 2 Timothy 2:3; Proverbs 24:11–12"> And Joshua came unto them suddenly; the whole night he went up from Gilgal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 18:14; Joshua 16:3; Joshua 16:5"> And the Lord brought them in confusion before Israel, and they smote them with a great slaughter at Gib'on, and pursued them by the way of the ascent to Beth-choron, and smote them up to 'Azekah, and up to Makkedah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 28:2; Judges 5:20; Psalms 18:12–14"> And it came to pass, as they fled from before Israel, while they were in the declivity of Beth-choron, that the Lord cast down upon them great stones from heaven, up to 'Azekah, and they died: there were more who died by means of the hailstones than those whom the children of Israel had slain with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:11; Isaiah 38:8; Isaiah 60:20"> Then spoke Joshua to the Lord on the day when the Lord delivered up the Emorites before the children of Israel, and he said before the eyes of Israel, Sun, stand thou still upon Gib'on; and thou, Moon, in the valley of Ayalon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:11; Isaiah 38:8; Joshua 10:14"> And the sun stood still, and the moon stayed, until the people had avenged themselves upon their enemies. Is not this written in the book of Yashar? And the sun stood still in the midst of the heavens, and hastened not to go down about a whole day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:30; Exodus 14:14; Joshua 10:42"> And there was no day like that before it or after it, that the Lord hearkened unto the voice of a man; for the Lord fought for Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 10:43; Joshua 10:6"> And Joshua returned, and all Israel with him, unto the camp to Gilgal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:8; Amos 9:2; Psalms 139:7–10"> But these five kings fled, and hid themselves in the cave at Makkedah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And it was told to Joshua, saying, The five kings have been found hidden in the cave at Makkedah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 27:66; Job 21:30; Amos 9:1"> And Joshua said, Roll great stones to the mouth of the cave, and set men over it to guard them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 18:37–41; Jeremiah 8:14; Isaiah 9:14–15"> But you, do ye not stay, pursue after your enemies, and smite the hindmost of them: suffer them not to enter into their cities; for the Lord your God hath delivered them into your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 8:24; 2 Samuel 20:6; Joshua 10:10"> And it came to pass, when Joshua and the children of Israel had made an end of smiting them with a very great defeat, till they were all spent, and those that escaped had fled from them and entered into the fortified cities,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 11:7; Isaiah 54:17; Isaiah 57:4"> That all the people returned to the camp to Joshua at Makkedah in peace: no one pointed against any man of the children of Israel his tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:32"> Then said Joshua, Open the mouth of the cave, and bring out unto me those five kings out of the cave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 10:1; Joshua 10:5; Joshua 10:3"> And they did so, and brought forth unto him those five kings out of the cave, the king of Jerusalem, the king of Hebron, the king of Yarmuth, the king of Lachish, the king of 'Eglon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Malachi 4:3; Romans 16:20; Revelation 2:26–27"> And it came to pass, when they brought out those kings unto Joshua, that Joshua called for all the men of Israel, and said unto the chiefs of the men of war who had gone with him, Come near, put your feet upon the necks of these kings. And they came near, and put their feet upon their necks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:10; Joshua 1:9; Ephesians 6:10"> And Joshua said unto them, Fear not, nor be disheartened, be strong and of good courage; for thus will the Lord do unto all your enemies against whom ye fight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Joshua 8:29; 2 Samuel 21:9; Matthew 27:25"> And Joshua smote them afterward, and slew them, and hanged them on five trees; and they remained hanging upon the trees until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 8:29; Deuteronomy 21:22–23; Joshua 7:26"> And it came to pass at the time of the going down of the sun, that Joshua commanded, and they took them down from the trees, and cast them into the cave wherein they had been hidden; and they placed great stones upon the mouth of the cave, &#91;which remain&#93; even until this very day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 6:21; Deuteronomy 7:16; Deuteronomy 20:16–17"> And Joshua captured Makkedah on that day, and smote it with the edge of the sword, and its king he devoted, them, and all the souls that were therein; he left none that escaped; and he did to the king of Makkedah as he had done unto the king of Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 21:13; Joshua 15:42; Jeremiah 52:1"> Then did Joshua, and all Israel with him, pass from Makkedah unto Libnah; and he fought against Libnah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And the Lord delivered it also into the hand of Israel, with its king; and he smote it with the edge of the sword, and all the souls that were therein; he left none in it that escaped; and he did unto its king as he had done unto the king of Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 10:3; Joshua 12:11; Joshua 10:5"> And Joshua, and all Israel with him, passed from Libnah unto Lachish, and encamped against it, and fought against it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And the Lord delivered Lachish into the hand of Israel; and he captured it on the second day, and smote it with the edge of the sword, and all the souls that were therein: just as he had done to Libnah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 16:10; Judges 1:29; Joshua 16:3"> Then came up Horam the king of Gezer to help Lachish; and Joshua smote him and his people, until he had left him none that escaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Joshua 10:3; Joshua 12:12; Joshua 15:39"> And Joshua and all Israel with him passed from Lachish unto 'Eglon; and they encamped against it, and fought against it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 26:44; Joshua 10:37; Job 19:10"> And they captured it on that day, and smote it with the edge of the sword; and all the souls that were therein he devoted on that day: just as he had done to Lachish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Judges 1:10; Joshua 15:13; Joshua 14:13–14"> And Joshua and all Israel with him went up from 'Eglon unto Hebron; and they fought against it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And they captured it, and smote it with the edge of the sword, and its king, and all its cities, and all the souls that were therein; he left none that escaped, just as he had done to 'Eglon; and he devoted it, and all the souls that were therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Joshua 15:15; Joshua 12:13; Joshua 15:49"> And Joshua and all Israel with him returned to Debir; and fought against it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Obadiah 1:18; Joshua 11:8; Deuteronomy 3:3"> And he captured it, and its king, and all its cities; and they smote them with the edge of the sword, and devoted all the souls that were therein; he left none that escaped: as he had done to Hebron, so did he to Debir and to its king; and as he had done to Libnah and to its king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2–16; Exodus 23:31–33; Joshua 8:27"> And Joshua smote all the country, the mountain, and the south, and the lowlands, and the declivities, and all their kings; he left none that escaped; and all that breathed he utterly destroyed, as the Lord, the God of Israel had commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Joshua 11:16; Joshua 15:51; Deuteronomy 9:23"> And Joshua smote them from Kadesh-barnea' even unto Gazzah, and all the country of Goshen, even up to Gib'on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Joshua 10:14; Psalms 46:7; Psalms 80:3"> And all these kings and their land did Joshua capture at one time; because the Lord, the God of Israel fought for Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Joshua 10:15; Joshua 4:19; 1 Samuel 11:14"> And Joshua returned, and all Israel with him, unto the camp to Gilgal.</span>
