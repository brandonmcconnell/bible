<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 11:10; Psalms 2:1–4; Judges 4:2"> And it came to pass, that, when Yabin the king of Chazor heard these things, he sent to Yobab the king of Madon, and to the king of Shimron, and to the king of Achshaph,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 12:3; Judges 1:27; Joshua 17:11"> And to the kings that were at the north, on the mountains, and in the plains, south of Kinneroth, and in the lowlands, and in the district of Dor on the west,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 3:3; Joshua 18:26; Genesis 31:49"> To the Canaanites on the east and on the west, and to the Emorites, and the Hittites, and the Perizzites, and the Jebusites in the mountains, and to the Hivites under Chermon in the land of Mizpah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 7:12; 1 Samuel 13:5; Genesis 32:12"> And they went out, they and all their camps with them, much people, even as the sand that is upon the sea-shore in multitude, and with very many horses and chariots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 118:10–12; Revelation 16:14; Isaiah 8:9"> And all these kings assembled themselves together, and they came and encamped together at the waters of Merom, to fight against Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 10:8; 2 Samuel 8:4; Judges 20:28"> And the Lord said unto Joshua, Be not afraid because of them; for tomorrow about this time will I give all of them up slain before Israel: their horses shalt thou hamstring and their chariots shalt thou burn with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 10:9; 1 Thessalonians 5:2–3"> And Joshua and all the people of war with him came upon them by the waters of Merom suddenly; and they fell upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 13:6; Joshua 19:28; Zechariah 9:2"> And the Lord delivered them into the hand of Israel, who smote them, and pursued them unto great Zidon, and unto Missrephoth-mayim, and unto the valley of Mizpeh eastward; and they smote them, until they left them none that escaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 11:6; Ezekiel 39:9–10"> And Joshua did unto them as the Lord had said unto him: their horses he hamstringed and their chariots he burnt with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 11:1; Judges 4:2"> And Joshua at that time turned back, and captured Chazor, and its kings he smote with the sword; for Chazor aforetimes was the head of all these kingdoms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 10:40; Deuteronomy 20:16"> And they smote all the souls that were therein with the edge of the sword, and devoted them; there was not left any one having breath; and Chazor he burnt with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:16–17; Deuteronomy 7:2; Numbers 33:50–53"> And all the cities of these kings, and all their kings, did Joshua capture, and he smote them with the edge of the sword, and he devoted them, as Moses the servant of the Lord had commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:18"> But as for the cities that had been left standing in their strength, these did Israel not burn: save Chazor only did Joshua burn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Numbers 31:9; Joshua 8:27"> And all the spoil of these cities, and the cattle, did the children of Israel take as booty unto themselves; but all the men they smote with the edge of the sword, until they had destroyed them: they left not any one having breath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 1:7; Exodus 34:11–13; Acts 20:20"> As the Lord had commanded Moses his servant, so did Moses command Joshua, and so did Joshua: he left nothing undone of all that the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 10:41; Joshua 11:21; Joshua 12:8"> And Joshua took all that land, the mountain, and all the south country, and all the land of Goshen, and the lowlands, and the plain, and the mountain of Israel, and its lowlands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:24; Joshua 11:3; Joshua 13:5"> From the bald mountain that goeth up to Seir, even unto Baal-gad in the valley of Lebanon under mount Chermon; and all their kings he captured, and smote them, and slew them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 14:7–10; Joshua 11:23"> A long time did Joshua make war with all these kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 9:3–27"> There was not a city that made peace with the children of Israel, save the Hivites the inhabitants of Gib'on: the whole they took by war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 9:18; Deuteronomy 20:16–17; Exodus 4:21"> For of the Lord it was to harden their heart, that they should come against Israel in battle, in order to destroy them utterly, that they might obtain no favor: but in order that he might exterminate them, as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:2; Numbers 13:22–23; Joshua 15:13–14"> And Joshua came at that time, and cut off the 'Anakim from the mountains, from Hebron, from Debir, from 'Anab, and from the whole mountain of Judah, and from the whole mountain of Israel: with their cities did Joshua destroy them utterly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:4; 1 Chronicles 18:1; Joshua 15:46"> There was none of the 'Anakim left in the land of the children of Israel: only in Gazzah, in Gath, and in Ashdod, there remained some.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 21:44–45; Numbers 26:52–55; Joshua 14:1–15"> And Joshua' took the whole land, all just as the Lord had spoken unto Moses; and Joshua gave it for an inheritance unto Israel, according to their divisions by their tribes. And the land rested from war.</span>
