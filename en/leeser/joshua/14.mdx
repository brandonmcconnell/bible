<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 34:17–29"> And these are the countries which the children of Israel obtained as an inheritance in the land of Canaan, which Elazar the priest, and Joshua the son of Nun, and the heads of the divisions of the tribes of the children of Israel, distributed for an inheritance unto them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 33:54; Numbers 34:13; Numbers 26:55–56"> By the lot as their inheritance: as the Lord had commanded by the hand of Moses, for the nine tribes, and the half tribe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 13:14; Joshua 13:8; Joshua 13:32–33"> For Moses had given the inheritance of the two tribes and the half tribe on the other side of the Jordan; but unto the Levites he had given no inheritance among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 48:5; 1 Chronicles 5:1–2; Numbers 35:2–8"> For the children of Joseph were two tribes, Menasseh and Ephraim; therefore they gave no portion unto the Levites in the land, save cities to dwell in, with their open spaces for their cattle and for their substance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 21:2; Numbers 35:1–2"> As the Lord had commanded Moses, so did the children of Israel, and so divided they the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:30; Numbers 13:6; Numbers 14:24"> And the children of Judah came near unto Joshua in Gilgal; and Caleb the son of Yephunneh the Kenizzite said unto him, Thou well knowest the word which the Lord spoke unto Moses the man of God concerning me and thyself in Kadesh-barnea'.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 13:6; Numbers 13:26–33; Numbers 14:6–10"> Forty years old was I when Moses the servant of the Lord sent me from Kadesh-barnea' to spy out the land; and I brought him word again as it was in my heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 14:24; Deuteronomy 1:36; Numbers 13:31"> But my brethren who had gone up with me caused the heart of the people to be faint; but I wholly followed the Lord my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 1:3; Deuteronomy 1:36; Numbers 14:22–24"> And Moses swore on that day, saying, Surely the land whereon thy foot hath trodden shall belong to thee for an inheritance, and to thy children for ever; because thou hast wholly followed the Lord my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 14:33–34; Numbers 14:30; Joshua 11:18"> And now, behold, the Lord hath kept me alive, as he hath spoken: it is now forty and five years since the Lord spoke this word unto Moses, while Israel wandered in the wilderness; and now, behold, I am this day eighty and five years old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:7; Psalms 103:5; Deuteronomy 31:2"> I am yet this day as strong as I was on the day that Moses sent me: as my strength was then, even so is my strength now, for war, and to go out, and to come in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 11:33; Philippians 4:13; Numbers 13:33"> Now therefore give me this mountain, whereof the Lord spoke on that day; for thou didst hear on that day that the 'Anakim were there, and great fortified cities: perhaps the Lord will be with me, and I shall drive them out, as the Lord hath spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 1:20; Joshua 22:6; 1 Chronicles 6:55–56"> And Joshua blessed him, and gave Hebron unto Caleb the son of Yephunneh for an inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 14:8–9; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> Therefore did Hebron become the inheritance of Caleb the son of Yephunneh the Kenizzite unto this day; for the cause that he had wholly followed the Lord the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 11:23; Genesis 23:2; Joshua 15:13"> And the name of Hebron was aforetimes Kiryatharba', who was the greatest man among the 'Anakim. And the land had rest from war.</span>
