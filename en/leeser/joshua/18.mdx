<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 19:51; 1 Samuel 1:3; Joshua 21:2"> And the whole congregation of the children of Israel assembled together at Shiloh, and set up there the tabernacle of the congregation. And the land was subdued before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And there remained among the children of Israel, that had not yet received their inheritance, seven tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 18:9; Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 10:4"> And Joshua said unto the children of Israel, How long will ye show yourselves slack to go to take possession of the land, which the Lord the God of your fathers hath given to you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 18:6; Joshua 4:2; Joshua 18:3"> Furnish for yourselves three men for each tribe; and I will send them out, and they shall arise, and walk through the land, and describe it according to their inheritance, and come again to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 16:1–10; Joshua 19:1–9; Joshua 15:1–12"> And they shall divide it into seven parts: Judah shall remain on his boundary at the south, and the house of Joseph shall remain on their boundary at the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 14:2; Joshua 18:10; Acts 13:19"> But you shall make a description of the land in seven parts, and bring it hither to me; and I will cast the lot for you here, before the Lord our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 13:33; Joshua 13:8–31; Numbers 18:20"> For to the Levites there is no portion among you; for the priesthood of the Lord is their inheritance; and Gad, and Reuben, and half the tribe of Menasseh have already received their inheritance beyond the Jordan, at the east, which Moses the servant of the Lord gave to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 18:10; Romans 14:19; 1 Samuel 14:41"> And the men arose, and went away; and Joshua charged those that went to describe the land, saying, Go and walk through the land, and describe it, and return again to me; and here will I cast the lot for you before the Lord, in Shiloh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 13:19"> And the men went and passed through the land, and described it according to the cities in seven parts in a book; and they came again to Joshua to the camp at Shiloh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 1:12; Acts 13:19; Joshua 18:6"> And Joshua cast the lot for them in Shiloh before the Lord; and Joshua divided there the land unto the children of Israel according to their divisions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 16:1–10; Joshua 15:1–8; Deuteronomy 10:1–22"> And the lot of the tribe of the children of Benjamin came up according to their families; and the boundary of their lot came forth between the children of Judah and the children of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 16:1; Joshua 7:2; Joshua 2:1"> And their boundary was on the north side &#91;starting&#93; from the Jordan; and the boundary went up to the side of Jericho on the north, and went up through the mountains westward; and its terminations were at the wilderness of Beth-aven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 28:19; Joshua 16:2–3; Joshua 10:11"> And the boundary went over from there toward Luz, to the south side of Luz, which is Beth-el; and the boundary descended to 'Atroth-addar, upon the mount that is on the south side of the lower Beth-choron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 15:9; 1 Chronicles 13:5–6; 1 Samuel 7:1–2"> And the border extended &#91;thence&#93;, and fetched a compass to the west side, to the south of the mount that is before Beth-choron at the south; and its terminations were at Kiryath-ba'al, which is Kiryath-ye'arim, a city of the children of Judah: this was the west side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 15:9"> And the south side commenced from the end of Kiryath-ye'arim, and the boundary went out on the west, and went out to the spring of the waters of Nephtoach:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 23:10; Joshua 15:7–8; Isaiah 30:33"> And the boundary went down to the end of the mountain that is before the valley of the son of Hinnom, and which is in the valley of Rephaim at the north, and descended to the valley of Hinnom, to the side of the Jebusi on the south, and descended to 'Enrogel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 15:6"> And extended northwardly, and went forth to 'En-shemesh, and went forth toward Geliloth, which is opposite to the ascent of Adummim, and descended to Eben-bohan the son of Reuben,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 15:6; Joshua 15:61"> And passed along on this side opposite to 'Arabah northward; and went down unto 'Arabah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 14:3; Joshua 15:2; Numbers 34:3"> And the boundary passed along to the side of Beth-choglah northward; and the terminations of the border were at the north bay of the Salt Sea at the south end of the Jordan: this was the south boundary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And the Jordan bounded it on the east side. This was the inheritance of the children of Benjamin, by its boundaries round about, according to their families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 18:12; Joshua 6:1; Joshua 18:19"> Now these were the cities of the tribe of the children of Benjamin according to their families, Jericho, and Beth-choglah, and 'Emekkeziz,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 15:6; 1 Kings 12:29–32; Joshua 18:18"> And Beth-ha'arabah, and Zemarayim, and Beth-el,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:17"> And Avvim, and Parah, and 'Ophrah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezra 2:26; Joshua 21:17; Isaiah 10:29"> And Kephar-ha'ammonah, and 'Ophni, and Geba': twelve cities with their villages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 9:17; 1 Kings 3:4–5; Joshua 10:2"> Gib'on, and Ramah, and Beeroth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Judges 10:17; Ezra 2:25; Joshua 9:17"> And Mizpeh, and Kephirah, and Mozah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And Rekem, and Yirpeel, and Taralah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:14; Joshua 15:8; Judges 20:4–5"> And Zela', Eleph, and Jebusi, which is Jerusalem, Gib'ath, and Kiryath: fourteen cities with their villages. This is the inheritance of the children of Benjamin according to their families.</span>
