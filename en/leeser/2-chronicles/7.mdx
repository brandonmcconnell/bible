<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 18:38; 1 Chronicles 21:26; Leviticus 9:23–24"> And when Solomon had made an end of praying, a fire came down from heaven, and consumed the burnt-offering and the sacrifices; and the glory of the Lord filled the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 15:8; 2 Chronicles 5:14; Exodus 24:17"> And the priests were not able to enter into the house of the Lord; because the glory of the Lord had filled the Lord's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 5:13; 2 Chronicles 20:21; Luke 1:50"> And all the children of Israel were looking on as the fire came down, and the glory of the Lord &#91;was resting&#93; upon the house; and they kneeled down with their faces to the ground upon the pavement, and prostrated themselves, and gave thanks unto the Lord, for he is good; because unto everlasting endureth his kindness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 8:62–63"> And the king and all the people offered sacrifices before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 10:22; Ezekiel 45:17; 2 Chronicles 30:24"> And king Solomon offered a sacrifice of twenty and two thousand oxen, and a hundred and twenty thousand sheep: and so they dedicated the house of God, the king and all the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 5:12; 1 Chronicles 15:16–21; 2 Chronicles 7:3"> And the priests were standing on their stations, and the Levites with the instruments of the music of the Lord, which king David had made to give thanks unto the Lord, because unto everlasting endureth his kindness, with the song of praise of David in their hand; and the priests blew the trumpets opposite to them, and all Israel were standing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 8:64–66; Hebrews 13:10–12; 2 Chronicles 36:14"> And Solomon hallowed the interior of the court that was before the house of the Lord; for he prepared there the burnt-offerings, and the fat of the peace-offerings; because the copper altar which Solomon had made was not able to contain the burnt-offerings, and the meat-offerings, and the fat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; 1 Kings 8:65; Nehemiah 8:13–18"> And Solomon held the feast at that time seven days, and all Israel with him, a very great assembly, from the entrance of Chamath unto the river of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 23:36; 1 Kings 8:65; Joel 1:14"> And they held on the eighth day a solemn assembly; for the dedication of the altar they held seven days, and the feast seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 8:66; Psalms 100:2; Deuteronomy 12:18"> And on the three-and-twentieth day of the seventh month he dismissed the people unto their tents, joyful and glad of heart because of the good that the Lord had done for David, and for Solomon, and for Israel his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 9:1–9; Ecclesiastes 2:10–11; 2 Chronicles 2:1"> Thus did Solomon complete the house of the Lord, and the king's house; and &#91;in&#93; all that came into Solomon's heart to make in the house of the Lord, and in his own house, he prospered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 17:1; 1 Kings 9:2; 2 Chronicles 7:16"> Then appeared the Lord to Solomon during the night, and said unto him, I have heard thy prayer, and I have made choice of this place for myself as a house of sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:26–28; Deuteronomy 11:17; Revelation 11:6"> If I shut up the heavens that there be no rain, or if I give a charge to the locusts to devour off the land, or if I send a pestilence among my people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40–41; James 4:9–10; Isaiah 55:6–7"> And if my people, over whom my name is called, do then humble themselves, and pray, and seek my presence, and turn away from their evil ways: then will I also hear from heaven, and I will forgive their sin, and will heal their land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:12; 2 Chronicles 6:40; Nehemiah 1:6"> Now, my eyes shall be open, and my ears attentive unto the prayer on this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:15; 2 Chronicles 7:12; 2 Chronicles 6:5–6"> And now I have chosen and hallowed this house, that my name may be there for ever; and my eyes and my heart shall be there at all times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zechariah 3:7; Ezekiel 36:27; 1 Kings 11:38"> And as for thee, if thou wilt walk before me, as David thy father hath walked, so as to do in accordance with all that I have commanded thee, and wilt keep my statutes and my ordinances:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:16; Jeremiah 33:20–21; 2 Samuel 7:13–16"> Then will I establish the throne of thy kingdom, just as I have covenanted with David thy father, saying, There shall never fail thee a man to be ruler in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 26:14; Deuteronomy 28:15; Leviticus 26:33–46"> But if ye will indeed turn away, and forsake my statutes and my commandments, which I have set before you, and will go and serve other gods, and bow down to them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 17:20; Deuteronomy 28:37; Jeremiah 24:9"> Then will I pluck them up out of my land which I have given unto them; and this house, which I have hallowed for my name, will I cast away from my sight, and I will render it to be for a proverb and for a by-word among all the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:8–9; Jeremiah 16:10–12; Jeremiah 19:8"> And this house, which hath been so exalted, shall become an astonishment to every one that passeth by it: so that he will say, Why hath the Lord done thus unto this land, and unto this house?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 9:12; Lamentations 2:16–17; Ezekiel 14:23"> And men shall then say, For the cause that they forsook the Lord the God of their fathers, who had brought them forth out of the land of Egypt, and they took hold of other gods, and bowed down to them, and served them: therefore hath he brought upon them all this evil.</span>
