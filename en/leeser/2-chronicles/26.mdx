<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:1; Matthew 1:8–9; 2 Chronicles 33:25"> And all the people of Judah took 'Uzziyahu, who was then sixteen years old, and made him king instead of his father Amazyahu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 16:6; 2 Chronicles 25:28; 2 Chronicles 25:23"> He it was that built Eloth, and brought it back to Judah, after the king slept with his fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 15:2–3; Amos 1:1; Hosea 1:1"> Sixteen years old was 'Uzziyahu when he became king, and fifty and two years did he reign in Jerusalem. And his mother's name was Yecholyah of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:2"> And he did what is right in the eyes of the Lord, in accordance with all that his father Amazyahu had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; 2 Chronicles 24:2; 1 Chronicles 22:13"> And he was &#91;inclined&#93; to seek God in the days of Zecharyahu, who had understanding in the visions of God; and during the time that he sought the Lord, God caused him to prosper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 14:29; 1 Samuel 5:6; 1 Samuel 5:1"> And he went forth and made war against the Philistines, and he broke down the wall of Gath, and the wall of Jabneh, and the wall of Ashdod; and he built cities in &#91;the country of&#93; Ashdod, and among the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:16; 1 Chronicles 5:20; 2 Chronicles 14:11"> And God helped him against the Philistines, and against the Arabians that dwelt in Gur-ba'al, and the Me'unim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:2; 2 Chronicles 17:11; Genesis 19:38"> And the 'Ammonites gave presents to 'Uzziyahu: and his name extended even to the entrance of Egypt; for he became exceedingly strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:13; 2 Chronicles 25:23; 2 Kings 14:13"> And 'Uzziyahu built towers in Jerusalem, above the corner-gate, and above the valley-gate, and at the angle, and made them strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 26:18–21; Isaiah 29:17; 2 Kings 3:4"> He built also towers in the desert, and hewed out many wells; for he had much cattle, both in the lowlands and in the plain; &#91;also&#93; husbandmen, and vintners in the mountains, and in Carmel; for he loved husbandry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 5:2"> Moreover 'Uzziyah had an army of fighting men, that went out to the host by bands, according to the number of those mustered of them through the hand of Je'iel the scribe and Ma'asseyahu the overseer, under the supervision of Chananyahu, one of the king's captains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The whole number of the chiefs of the family divisions of the mighty men of valor was two thousand and six hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:5; 2 Chronicles 14:8; 2 Chronicles 11:1"> And under their supervision was an efficient army, &#91;of&#93; three hundred thousand and seven thousand and five hundred, that made war with mighty power, to help the king against the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 20:16; 1 Samuel 17:49"> And 'Uzziyahu prepared for them, for all the host, shields, and spears, and helmets, and coats of mail, and bows, and stones for slinging.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 31:4; Matthew 4:24; 2 Chronicles 2:7"> And he made in Jerusalem artificial contrivances, contrived by a skilful man, to be &#91;stationed&#93; on the towers and upon the ramparts, to shoot off arrows and great stones. And his name extended ever so far abroad; for he was marvelously assisted, till he became strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:25; 2 Chronicles 25:19; Proverbs 16:18"> But when he was strong, his heart was lifted up to his destruction; and he became unfaithful against the Lord his God, and went into the temple of the Lord to burn incense upon the altar of incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:10; 1 Chronicles 26:6; 1 Chronicles 12:28"> And there went in after him 'Azaryahu the priest, and with him were priests of the Lord, valiant men, &#91;to the number of&#93; eighty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 30:7–8; Numbers 18:7; Jeremiah 13:18"> And they stood forward against king 'Uzziyahu, and they said unto him, It is not for thee, O 'Uzziyahu, to burn incense unto the Lord, but for the priests the sons of Aaron, who are consecrated to burn incense: go out of the sanctuary; for thou hast trespassed; and it will not be for thy honor from the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 12:10; 2 Chronicles 25:16; 2 Kings 5:25–27"> But 'Uzziyahu became wroth, and in his hand was a censer to burn incense: and while he was wroth with the priests, the leprosy even broke out on his forehead before the priests in the house of the Lord, above the altar of the incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:22; Esther 6:12; Leviticus 14:34"> And when 'Azaryahu the chief priest, with all the priests, turned about toward him, behold, he was leprous on his forehead, and they hurried him away from there: yea, he also made haste to go out, because the Lord had afflicted him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 13:46; Numbers 12:15; 2 Kings 7:3"> And king 'Uzziyahu was a leper until the day of his death, and dwelt in the leper-house, as a leper; for he was excluded from the house of the Lord: and Jotham his son was over the king's house, &#91;and&#93; judged the people of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 1:1; Isaiah 6:1; 2 Chronicles 9:29"> And the rest of the acts of 'Uzziyahu, the first and the last, did Isaiah the prophet, the son of Amos write.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:27; 2 Chronicles 21:20; 2 Chronicles 26:18"> And 'Uzziyahu slept with his fathers, and they buried him with his fathers in the burial-field which belonged to the kings; for they said, He is a leper: and Jotham his son became king in his stead.</span>
