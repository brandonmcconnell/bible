<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 15:24; Ephesians 6:10; Matthew 1:8"> And Jehoshaphat his son became king in his stead, and strengthened himself against Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:8; 2 Chronicles 11:5; 2 Chronicles 11:11–12"> And he placed forces in all the fortified cities of Judah, and placed garrisons in the land of Judah, and in the cities of Ephraim, which Assa his father had captured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 22:2; Isaiah 41:10; 2 Timothy 4:22"> And the Lord was with Jehoshaphat; because he walked in the first ways of David his father, and sought not after the Be'alim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 12:28; Luke 1:6; 1 Thessalonians 2:12"> On the contrary, after the God of his father did he seek, and in his commandments did he walk, but not after the doings of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 18:1; Matthew 6:33; Psalms 127:1"> Therefore did the Lord establish the kingdom in his hand; and all Judah gave presents to Jehoshaphat; and he had riches and honor in abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 14:9; 2 Chronicles 15:17; 2 Chronicles 20:33"> And his heart raised itself up in the ways of the Lord, and he removed moreover the high-places and groves out of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:3; Nehemiah 8:7–8; Ecclesiastes 12:9–10"> And in the third year of his reign he sent his princes, even Ben-chayil, and 'Obadiah, and Zechariah, and Nethanel, and Michayahu, to teach in the cities of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 7:1–6; 2 Chronicles 19:8; Malachi 2:7"> And with them were the Levites, Shema'yahu, and Nethanyahu, and Zebadyahu, and 'Assahel, and Shemiramoth, and Jehonathan, and Adoniyahu, and Tobiyahu, and Tobadoniyah, the Levites; and with them Elishama' and Jehoram, the priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 5:46; Romans 3:2; Acts 8:40"> And they taught in Judah, and with them was the book of the law of the Lord, and they moved about through all the cities of Judah, and taught the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:14; Exodus 15:14–16; Proverbs 16:7"> And the dread of the Lord was upon all the kingdoms of the lands that were round about Judah, so that they made no war with Jehoshaphat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:8; 2 Chronicles 9:14; 2 Samuel 8:2"> Also from the Philistines did people bring unto Jehoshaphat presents and silver, as tribute: also the Arabians brought him small cattle, rams seven thousand and seven hundred, and he-goats seven thousand and seven hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:2–6; 2 Chronicles 14:6–7; 2 Chronicles 32:5"> And Jehoshaphat went on becoming exceedingly great, and he built in Judah castles and treasure-cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:25–31; 2 Chronicles 26:10–15"> And he had great works in the cities of Judah, and men of war, mighty in valor, in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:1; Genesis 15:5; Numbers 1:18"> And these are their numbers according to their family divisions: Of Judah, of the captains of the thousands was 'Adnah the chief, and with him were mighty men of valor, three hundred thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And next to him was Jehochanan the chief, and with him were two hundred and eighty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 5:9; Judges 5:2; 1 Chronicles 29:9"> And next to him was 'Amassyah the son of Zichri, who voluntarily offered himself unto the Lord; and with him were two hundred thousand mighty men of valor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:8; 2 Samuel 1:21–22"> And of Benjamin, the mighty valiant Elyada', and with him were those armed with bow and shield two hundred thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And next him was Jehozabad, and with him were one hundred and eighty thousand ready armed for the host.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:2; 2 Chronicles 11:12; 2 Chronicles 11:23"> These were those that ministered to the king, besides those whom the king had placed in the fortified cities throughout all Judah.</span>
