<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:31; 1 Kings 2:10; 1 Kings 14:31"> (13:23) And Abiyah slept with his fathers, and they buried him in the city of David: and Assa his son became king in his stead. In his days the land had repose ten years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 15:11; 2 Chronicles 31:20; 1 Kings 15:14"> (14:1) And Assa did what is good and right in the eyes of the Lord his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:5; Exodus 34:13; 2 Kings 23:14"> (14:2) And he removed the altars of the strange &#91;gods&#93;, and the high-places, and broke up the statues, and cut down the groves;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:19; Psalms 119:10; Psalms 101:2–8"> (14:3) And he ordered Judah to seek the Lord the God of their fathers, and to execute the law and the commandment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:4; 2 Chronicles 34:7"> (14:4) Also he removed out of all the cities of Judah the high-places and the sun-images: and the kingdom had repose before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:15; 1 Chronicles 22:9; Job 34:29"> (14:5) And he built fortified cities in Judah; for the land had repose, and no one had war with him in those years; because the Lord had given him rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 3:12; 2 Chronicles 14:6; Jeremiah 29:12–14"> (14:6) Therefore he said unto Judah, Let us build these cities, and surround them with walls, and towers, gates, and bars: the land is yet before us; because we have sought the Lord our God,—we have sought him, and he hath given us rest on every side. So they built and prospered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:3; 2 Chronicles 17:14–19; 2 Chronicles 11:1"> (14:7) And Assa had an army that bore targets and spears, out of Judah three hundred thousand; and out of Benjamin, that bore shields and drew the bow, two hundred and eighty thousand: all these were mighty men of valor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:8; 2 Chronicles 12:2–3; 2 Chronicles 11:8"> (14:8) And there came out against them Zerach the Ethiopian with an army of a thousand times thousand, and three hundred chariots; and he came as far as Mareshah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 1:17; Joshua 19:4"> (14:9) Then went Assa out against him, and they set themselves in battle-array in the valley of Zephathah near Mareshah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:19; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Psalms 22:5"> (14:10) And Assa called unto the Lord his God, and said, Lord, nothing can hinder thee to help, whether it be the mighty, or those that have no power: help us, O Lord our God; for on thee do we rely, and in thy name are we come against this multitude. O Lord, thou art our God! no mortal can place a restraint against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:15; Psalms 136:17–18; Deuteronomy 28:7"> (14:11) Thereupon did the Lord strike down the Ethiopians before Assa, and before Judah: and the Ethiopians fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 10:19; Genesis 26:1; Genesis 20:1"> (14:12) And Assa and the people that were with him pursued them as far as Gerar: and there fell of the Ethiopians &#91;so many&#93;, that they could not recover themselves; for they were broken down before the Lord, and before his camp; and they carried away exceedingly much booty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:10; Genesis 35:5; 2 Chronicles 20:29"> (14:13) And they smote all the cities round about Gerar; for the dread of the Lord was upon them: and they plundered all the cities; for abundant spoil was in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 31:30–47; Numbers 31:9; 1 Samuel 30:20"> (14:14) And also the tents of &#91;the owners of&#93; cattle did they smite, and they carried away sheep in abundance, and camels, and returned to Jerusalem.</span>
