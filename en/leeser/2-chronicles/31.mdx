<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 18:4; 2 Chronicles 32:12; Genesis 19:15"> And when all this was finished, all Israel that were present went out to the cities of Judah, and broke in pieces the statues, and cut down the groves, and pulled down the high-places and the altars out of all Judah and Benjamin, and in Ephraim and Menasseh, until they had made an end of them all. Then returned all the children of Israel every man to his possession, to their own cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:1; 1 Chronicles 16:4–6; 2 Chronicles 29:24–26"> And Hezekiah stationed the divisions of the priests and the Levites after their divisions, every man according to his service, of the priests and the Levites, for burnt-offerings and for peace-offerings, to minister, and to give thanks, and to praise in the gates of the camps of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:17; 2 Chronicles 35:7; Colossians 2:16–17"> The king also gave a portion from his own property for the burnt-offerings, &#91;namely,&#93; for the morning and evening burnt-offerings, and the burnt-offerings for the sabbaths, and for the new-moons, and for the appointed feasts, as it is written in the law of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Malachi 2:7; Numbers 18:8–21; Nehemiah 13:10–13"> Moreover he said to the people, to those who dwelt in Jerusalem, to give the portion of the priests and the Levites, in order that they might hold firmly to the law of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:12; 2 Chronicles 24:10–11; Nehemiah 10:35–39"> And when the matter was spread abroad, the children of Israel brought in abundance the first-fruits of corn, of the new wine, and of oil, and of honey, and of all the products of the field: and the tithe of all things did they bring &#91;likewise&#93; in abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 27:30; 2 Chronicles 11:16–17; Deuteronomy 14:28"> And as for the children of Israel and Judah, that dwelt in the cities of Judah, they also brought in the tithe of oxen and sheep, and the tithe of holy things which were hallowed unto the Lord their God, and gave &#91;them&#93; by heaps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 23:16–24"> In the third month did they begin to lay the foundation of the heaps, and in the seventh month did they finish them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 1:3; Genesis 14:19–20; Philippians 4:10"> And when Hezekiah and the princes came and saw the heaps, they blessed the Lord, and his people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Then made Hezekiah inquiry of the priests and the Levites concerning the heaps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Malachi 3:10; 1 Chronicles 6:8; 2 Corinthians 9:8–11"> Then spoke to him 'Azaryahu the chief priest of the house of Zadok, and said, Since it was begun to bring the heave-offerings into the house of the Lord, there hath been enough to eat, and to leave in great abundance; for the Lord hath blessed his people: and that which is left is this great mass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:38–39; Nehemiah 13:5; Nehemiah 13:12–13"> Then ordered Hezekiah to prepare chambers in the house of the Lord: and they prepared them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:9; 2 Kings 12:15; 1 Chronicles 26:20–26"> And they brought in the heave-offerings, and the tithes, and the sanctified things, in faithfulness: and over them were appointed the ruler Conanyahu the Levite, and Shim'i his brother the second in rank.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:5; 2 Chronicles 31:10–11; 1 Chronicles 9:11"> And Jechiel, and 'Azazyahu, and Nachath, and 'Assahel, and Jerimoth. and Jozabad, and Eliel, and Yissmachyahu, and Machath, and Benayahu, were overseers under the supervision of Conanyahu and Shim'i his brother, by the appointment of king Hezekiah, and 'Azazyahu the ruler of the house of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 23:38; 1 Chronicles 26:12; Leviticus 27:28"> And Kore the son of Yimnah the Levite, the gatekeeper at the east side. was over the freewill-offerings of God, to give &#91;to him&#93; the heave-offerings of the Lord, and the most holy things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 21:9–19; 1 Chronicles 9:22; 2 Chronicles 29:12"> And under his supervision were 'Eden, and Minyamin, and Jeshua', and Shema-yahu, Amaryahu, and Shechanyahu, in the cities of the priests, in faithfulness, to give to their brethren after the divisions, equally to the great as to the small;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezra 3:4; Leviticus 21:22–23; 1 Chronicles 23:3"> Besides &#91;these&#93; to those recorded by their genealogies of males, from three years old and upward, of all that entered into the house of the Lord, the daily portion on its day, for their service in their charges according to their divisions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:24; 1 Chronicles 23:27; Numbers 3:15"> And likewise to the priests recorded by their genealogies after their family divisions, and the Levites from twenty years old and upward, in their charges by their divisions;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 15:16; Isaiah 5:16; 2 Chronicles 31:15"> And to those recorded by their genealogies of all their little ones, their wives, and their sons, and their daughters, of all the assembly; for in their faithfulness they devoted themselves in the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:12–15; Leviticus 25:34; Numbers 35:2–5"> Also of the sons of Aaron the priests, who were in the fields of the open districts of their cities, in each and every city, there were men, expressed by name, who had to give portions to all the males among the priests, and to all that were recorded by their genealogies among the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 20:3; 2 Kings 22:2; Acts 24:16"> And the like did Hezekiah in all Judah, and he did what is good and right and true before the Lord his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Jeremiah 29:13; Joshua 1:7–8"> And in every work that he began in the service of the house of God, and in the law, and in the commandments, to seek his God, he acted with all his heart, and prospered.</span>
