<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:29; Deuteronomy 32:1; Micah 1:2"> Do but hear now what the Lord saith, Arise, contend thou before the mountains, and let the hills hear thy voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 4:1; Hosea 12:2; Isaiah 1:18"> Hear ye, O mountains, the controversy of the Lord, and ye strong foundations of the earth! for the Lord hath a controversy with his people, and with Israel will he plead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:5; Isaiah 43:22–23; Psalms 81:13"> O my people, what have I done unto thee? and wherewith have I wearied thee? testify against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 12:51; Deuteronomy 7:8; Amos 2:10"> Although I had brought thee up out of the land of Egypt, and redeemed thee out of the house of bond-men; and I sent before thee Moses, Aaron, and Miriam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 5:9–10; Numbers 25:1; Judges 5:11"> O my people, do but remember what Balak the king of Moab resolved, and what Bil'am the son of Be'or answered him, from Shittim unto Gilgal, in order that ye may know the gracious benefits of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 51:16–17; Romans 10:2–3; Psalms 40:6–8"> Wherewith shall I come before the Lord, bow myself before the God on high? shall I come before him with burnt-offerings, with calves of a year old!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 16:3; 1 Samuel 15:22; Psalms 51:16"> Will the Lord be pleased with thousands of rams, or with myriads of streams of oil? shall I give my first-born for my transgression, the fruit of my body for the sin of my soul?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:12–13; Proverbs 21:3; Colossians 3:12"> He hath told thee, O man, what is good; and what the Lord doth require of thee: &#91;nothing&#93; but to do justice, and to love kindness, and to walk humbly with thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Hosea 14:9; Isaiah 30:27"> The voice of the Lord calleth unto the city,—and &#91;the man of&#93; wisdom shall see thy name:—hear ye the rod &#91;of punishment&#93;, and who hath ordained it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 3:10; Amos 8:5–6; Jeremiah 5:26–27"> Are there yet in the house of the wicked man the treasures of wickedness, and the scant accursed measure?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 12:7; Leviticus 19:36; Proverbs 16:11"> Can I be pure with wicked balances, and with a bag &#91;full&#93; of deceptive weights?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 1:23; Jeremiah 9:8; Hosea 7:13"> For her rich men are full of violence, and her inhabitants have spoken falsehood, and their tongue is deceit &#91;itself&#93; in their mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 12:23; Deuteronomy 28:21–22; Hosea 13:16"> Therefore have I also smitten thee with sore wounds, making &#91;thee&#93; desolate because of thy sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hosea 4:10; Leviticus 26:26; Isaiah 65:13"> Thou wilt indeed eat, but not be satisfied; and what thou hast eaten shall bend thee down; and thou wilt overtake &#91;the enemy&#93;, but thou shalt not deliver; and that which thou deliverest will I give up to the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:13; Amos 5:11; Jeremiah 12:13"> Thou wilt indeed sow, but thou shalt not reap; thou wilt indeed tread out olives, but thou shalt not anoint thyself with oil; and the juice of the grapes, but thou shalt not drink wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:51; 1 Kings 16:25–33; Jeremiah 7:24"> For there are observed the statutes of 'Omri, and all the works of the house of Achab, and ye walk in their counsels: in order that I should give thee up unto desolation, and thy inhabitants to derision; and ye shall bear the reproach of my people.</span>
