<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 28:4; Isaiah 24:13; Hosea 9:10"> Woe is me! for I am as in the gathering of the summer-fruits, as in the grape-gleanings of the vintage: there is no cluster to eat, no first-ripe fruit for which my soul longeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 12:1; Isaiah 57:1; Isaiah 59:7"> The pious hath disappeared out of the land; and the upright among men there is none; all of them lie in wait for blood; they hunt every man his brother with a net.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Proverbs 4:16–17; Amos 5:12"> For the evil of your hands you expect good? while the prince demandeth &#91;bribes&#93;, and the judge acteth for pay; and the great man is only speaking the wilful pleasure of his soul: and so do they make a network &#91;of wrong&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 22:5; Ezekiel 2:6; Isaiah 10:3"> The best of them is like a brier; the most upright is &#91;sharper&#93; than a thorn-hedge: the day of thy watchmen, thy punishment, is come; now shall be perplexity among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:4; Psalms 118:8–9; Matthew 10:16"> Trust ye not in a friend, put ye not confidence in a confidant: from her that lieth in thy bosom guard the doors of thy mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 10:35–36; Luke 12:53; Matthew 10:21"> For the son disgraceth the father, the daughter riseth up against her mother, the daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law; a man's enemies are the men of his own house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25–26; Psalms 37:7; 1 John 5:14–15"> But I—I will look unto the Lord; I will wait for the God of my salvation: my God will hear me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 8:12; Isaiah 9:2; Psalms 27:1"> Rejoice not, O my enemy, over me: though I am fallen, I rise again: though I should sit in darkness, the Lord will be a light unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 37:6; 1 Corinthians 4:5; 1 Samuel 24:15"> The indignation of the Lord will I bear, because I have sinned against him; until that he plead my cause, and execute justice for me: &#91;when&#93; he will bring me forth to the light, and I shall behold his righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 10:5; Jeremiah 50:33–34; Psalms 42:10"> Then she that is my enemy will see it, and shame shall cover her, who said unto me, Where is the Lord thy God? My eyes shall complacently see her &#91;suffer&#93;: now shall she be trodden down as the mire of the streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Amos 9:11–15; Nehemiah 2:8; Nehemiah 2:17"> The day that thy fences are to be built—that same day, the ordained, is yet far removed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 19:23–25; Isaiah 11:16; Hosea 11:11"> It is a day when men shall come to thee from Assyria, and the cities of Mazor, and from Mazor even to the river, and from sea to sea, and &#91;from&#93; mountain to mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 3:10–11; Jeremiah 25:11; Micah 6:13"> While the land &#91;of the nations&#93; shall be made desolate because of its inhabitants, for the fruit of their doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 23:1–4; Micah 5:4; Psalms 100:3"> Feed thy people with thy rod, the flock of thy heritage, which dwell in solitude in the wood, in the midst of Carmel: let them feed in Bashan and Gil'ad, as in the days of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 78:12–72; Exodus 3:20; Psalms 68:22"> As in the days of thy coming out of the land of Egypt will I let them see marvelous things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 26:11; Isaiah 52:15; Job 29:9–10"> Nations shall see and be ashamed of all their might: they shall lay their hand upon their mouth, their ears shall be deafened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; Psalms 72:9; Psalms 18:45"> They shall lick the dust like the serpent; like those that crawl on the earth, shall they come forth trembling out of their close places: unto the Lord our God shall they hasten in dread, and shall be afraid of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Isaiah 44:22; Psalms 103:8–9"> Who is a god like unto thee, pardoning iniquity, and forgiving transgression to the residue of his heritage? he retaineth not his anger for ever, because he delighteth in kindness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 38:17; Romans 6:14; Isaiah 43:25"> He will again have mercy on us, he will suppress our iniquities; yea, thou wilt cast all their sins into the depths of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 28:13–14; Luke 1:72–74; Deuteronomy 7:8"> Thou wilt show faithfulness unto Jacob, and kindness unto Abraham, which thou hast sworn unto our fathers in the days of old.</span>
