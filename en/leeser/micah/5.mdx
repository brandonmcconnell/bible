<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Lamentations 3:30; Job 16:10; Matthew 26:67"> (4:14) Now gather thyself in troops, O daughter of troops; they lay siege against us: with the rod they smite upon the cheek the judge of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 2:6; John 7:42; Luke 1:31–33"> (5:1) But thou, Beth-lechem Ephratah, the least &#91;though&#93; thou be among the thousands of Judah, &#91;yet&#93; out of thee shall he come forth unto me that is to be ruler in Israel, whose origin is from olden times, from most ancient days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 12:1–2; Romans 9:27–28; Micah 4:7"> (5:2) Therefore will he give them up, until the time that she who travaileth hath brought forth: then shall the remnant of his brethren return with the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 40:10–11; Micah 7:14; Luke 1:32"> (5:3) And he shall stand forward and feed &#91;Israel&#93; through the strength of the Lord, through the excellency of the name of the Lord his God: and they shall abide &#91;safely&#93;; for now shall he be great even unto the ends of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 2:14; Zechariah 10:3; Ephesians 2:14–17"> (5:4) And in this &#91;manner&#93; shall there be peace: If Asshur should come into our land; and if he should tread in our palaces, then will we raise up against him seven shepherds, and eight anointed men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 10:8–11; Isaiah 14:25; Isaiah 14:2"> (5:5) And they shall lay waste the land of Asshur with the sword, and the land of Nimrod in the gates of its &#91;cities&#93;: thus will he deliver us from Asshur, if he should come into our land, and if he should tread within our borders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Micah 5:3; Isaiah 44:3; Psalms 72:6"> (5:6) And the remnant of Jacob shall be in the midst of many people like dew from the Lord, like showers upon the herbs, that wait not for man, nor hope for the sons of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Zechariah 10:5; Micah 4:13; Zechariah 9:15"> (5:7) And the remnant of Jacob shall be among the nations, in the midst of many people, like a lion among the beasts of the forest, like a young lion among flocks of sheep: who, if he break in, both treadeth down, and teareth in pieces, while none can deliver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 26:11; Psalms 21:8; Isaiah 14:2–4"> (5:8) High shall thy hand be lifted up above thy adversaries, and all thy enemies shall be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 9:10; Hosea 14:3; Psalms 20:7–8"> (5:9) And it shall come to pass on that day, saith the Lord, that I will cut off thy horses out of the midst of thee, and I will destroy thy chariots;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 2:12–17; Ezekiel 38:11; Hosea 10:14"> (5:10) And I will cut off the cities of thy land, and I will throw down all thy strongholds;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:10–12; Isaiah 8:19–20; Isaiah 2:20"> (5:11) And I will cut off the arts of witchcraft out of thy hand; and soothsayers shalt thou have no more;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:9; Ezekiel 36:25; Hosea 14:8"> (5:12) And I will cut off thy graven images, and thy statues out of the midst of thee; and thou shalt no more prostrate thyself to the work of thy hands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 34:13"> (5:13) And I will pluck up thy groves out of the midst of thee; and I will destroy thy enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 149:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:8; Micah 5:8"> (5:14) And I will in anger and in fury execute vengeance upon the nations, upon those that have not hearkened.</span>
