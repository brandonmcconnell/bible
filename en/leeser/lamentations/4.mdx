<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:19–22; Lamentations 2:19; Isaiah 1:21"> Oh how is the gold become dim! how is the most fine gold changed! how are the stones of the sanctuary poured out at the corners of every street.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 30:14; Jeremiah 19:11; Romans 9:21–23"> The precious sons of Zion, valued equal to pure gold, how are they now esteemed as earthen pitchers, the work of the hands of the potter!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:10; Luke 23:28–29; Lamentations 2:20"> Even wild beasts offer the breast, they give suck to their young ones: the daughter of my people is become cruel, like the ostriches in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 22:15; Lamentations 2:11–12; Matthew 7:9–11"> The tongue of the suckling cleaveth to its palate by reason of thirst: babes ask for bread, there is not one to break it for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 6:3–7; Luke 16:19; Luke 15:16"> Those that used to eat dainty food are desolate in the streets: they that were reared up on scarlet now embrace dunghills.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 19:25; Luke 12:47; Luke 10:12"> For greater is the iniquity of the daughter of my people than the sin of Sodom, that was overthrown as it were in a moment, and no human hands were laid on her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 51:7; Song of Solomon 5:10; Psalms 144:12"> Her crowned princes were purer than snow, they were whiter than milk, they were more brilliant in body than pearls, more than the sapphire, their countenance:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 5:10; Psalms 102:3–5; Job 30:30"> Darker than black is now their visage; they are not to be recognized in the streets: their skin is shriveled fast upon their bones; it is dry, it is become like wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:23; Leviticus 26:39; Jeremiah 16:4"> Happier are those slain by the sword than those slain by hunger; for those poured forth their blood, being pierced through, —&#91;these perished&#93; without the fruits of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Lamentations 2:20; Isaiah 49:15; 2 Kings 6:26–29"> The hands of merciful women cooked their own children: they became food unto them in the downfall of the daughter of my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:20; Ezekiel 22:31; Jeremiah 17:27"> The Lord hath let loose all his fury: he hath poured out the fierceness of his anger: and he hath kindled a fire in Zion, which hath devoured her foundations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:13; Deuteronomy 29:24–28; Psalms 48:4–6"> The kings of the earth, and all the inhabitants of the world, would not believe that an adversary or an enemy could ever enter within the gates of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:31; Jeremiah 6:13; Jeremiah 26:8–9"> &#91;But it hath happened&#93; because of the sins of her prophets, the iniquities of her priests, that had shed in the midst of her the blood of the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:34; Isaiah 56:10; Isaiah 1:15"> They wandered about blindly in the streets, they became defiled with blood; so that men were not able to touch their garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 13:45–46"> Depart, ye unclean, they called out unto them: depart, depart, touch not. So they flee away and also wander about: men say among the nations, They shall no more sojourn there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Lamentations 5:12; Isaiah 9:14–16; Deuteronomy 28:64–65"> The anger of the Lord hath divided them; he will no more look at them: the faces of the priests they respected not, and the elders they spared not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:16; Isaiah 20:5; 2 Kings 24:7"> Even now our eyes anxiously wait for our valueless help: in our waiting have we waited for a nation that cannot help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Amos 8:2; Ezekiel 7:2–12; Lamentations 3:52"> They hunt our steps, that we cannot walk in our streets: our end is near, our days are full: for our end is come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:8; Jeremiah 4:13; Isaiah 5:26–28"> Swifter were our pursuers than the eagles of heaven: upon the mountains did they hotly follow us: in the wilderness did they lie in wait for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:21; Ezekiel 12:13; Jeremiah 39:5"> The breath of our nostrils, the anointed of the Lord, was caught in their pits, he, of whom we said, Under his shadow shall we live among the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Amos 1:11; Revelation 16:15; Job 1:1"> Be glad and rejoice, O daughter of Edom, that dwellest in the land of 'Uz: also unto thee shall the cup pass; thou wilt be drunken, and make thyself naked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 40:2; Psalms 137:7; Jeremiah 50:20"> Brought to an end is thy iniquity, O daughter of Zion; He will no more carry thee away into exile: He visiteth thy iniquity, O daughter of Edom; He layeth open thy sins.</span>
