<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 12:1; Hosea 9:3; Hosea 7:11"> To Egypt do we stretch out our hand, to Asshur, to be satisfied with bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:29; Jeremiah 16:12; Ezekiel 18:2"> Our fathers have sinned, and are no more; but we have indeed to bear their iniquities.</span>
