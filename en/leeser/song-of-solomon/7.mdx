<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 45:13; Exodus 35:35; Exodus 28:15"> (7:2) How beautiful are thy steps in sandals, O prince's daughter! the roundings of thy thighs are like jewelled ornaments, the work of the hands of the artificer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 46:3; Psalms 45:16; Romans 7:4"> (7:3) Thy navel is like a round goblet which lacketh not the mixed wine: thy body is like a heap of wheat fenced about with lilies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Song of Solomon 4:5; Song of Solomon 6:6"> (7:4) Thy two breasts are like two fawns, the twins of the roe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Song of Solomon 4:4; Song of Solomon 4:8–9; Psalms 144:12"> (7:5) Thy neck is like a tower of ivory; thy eyes are like the pools in Cheshbon, by the gate of Bath-rabbim; thy nose is like the tower of Lebanon which looketh toward Damascus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 35:2; Song of Solomon 4:1; Micah 7:14"> (7:6) Thy head upon thee is like Carmel, and the hair of thy head like purple: a king is held bound in the tresses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:15–16; Psalms 45:11; Song of Solomon 4:10"> (7:7) How beautiful and how pleasant art thou, O love, in thy attractions!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Song of Solomon 4:5; Song of Solomon 1:13; Ephesians 4:13"> (7:8) This thy stature is like a palm-tree, and thy breasts are like clusters of grapes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14; John 14:21–23; Song of Solomon 2:5"> (7:9) I thought, I wish to climb up the palm-tree, I wish to take hold of its boughs; and, oh, that thy breasts might be like clusters of the vine, and the smell of thy nose like apples;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Song of Solomon 5:16; Isaiah 62:8–9; Acts 4:31–32"> (7:10) And thy palate like the best wine, that glideth down for my friend gently, exciting the lips of those that are asleep.—</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 45:11; Song of Solomon 2:16; Song of Solomon 6:3"> (7:11) I am my friend's, and toward me is his desire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Song of Solomon 4:8; Song of Solomon 2:10–13; Song of Solomon 1:4"> (7:12) Come, my friend, let us go forth into the field; let us spend the night in the villages;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Song of Solomon 6:11; Ephesians 6:24; Song of Solomon 7:6"> (7:13) Let us get up early to the vineyards; let us see if the vine have blossomed, whether the young grape have opened &#91;to the view&#93;, whether the pomegranates have budded: there will I give my caresses unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 30:14; Matthew 13:52; 1 Peter 4:11"> (7:14) The mandrakes give forth &#91;their&#93; smell, and at our doors are all manner of precious fruits, new and also old: O my friend, these have I laid up for thee.</span>
