<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Song of Solomon 8:5; Hebrews 1:1–6; Isaiah 32:2"> Like the apple-tree among the trees of the forest, so is my friend among the young men: under his shadow do I ardently wish to sit, and his fruit is sweet to my palate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 8:28–39; John 15:9–15; Romans 5:8–10"> He brought me to the banqueting-house, and his banner over me was love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Song of Solomon 5:8; Hosea 3:1; 2 Samuel 6:19"> Strengthen me with flagons of wine, refresh me with apples; for sick of love am I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:17; Isaiah 54:5–10; Jeremiah 32:41"> Oh that his left hand might be under my head, and that his right might embrace me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Song of Solomon 3:5; Song of Solomon 8:4; Song of Solomon 1:5"> I adjure you, O daughters of Jerusalem, by the roes, and by the hinds of the field, that ye awaken not, nor excite my love, till it please &#91;to come of itself&#93;.—</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:27; Revelation 3:20; John 3:29"> The voice of my friend! behold, there he cometh, leaping over the mountains, skipping over the hills.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Song of Solomon 8:14; Song of Solomon 2:17; John 5:39"> My friend is like a roebuck or the fawn of the hinds: behold, there he standeth behind our wall, looking in at the windows, seeing through the lattice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:13; Song of Solomon 4:7–8; Revelation 22:17"> My friend commenced, and said unto me, Rise thee up, my beloved, my fair one, and come along.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1–2; Ecclesiastes 3:11; Isaiah 54:6–8"> For, lo, the winter is past, the rain is over and gone its way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 55:12; Colossians 3:16; Psalms 89:15"> The flowers are seen in the land; the time of the &#91;birds'&#93; singing is come, and the voice of the turtle-dove is heard in our land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:10; Song of Solomon 7:11–13; Isaiah 55:10–11"> The fig-tree perfumeth its green figs, and the vines with young grapes give forth a &#91;pleasant&#93; smell. Arise thee, my beloved, my fair one, and come along.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezra 9:5–6; Song of Solomon 5:2; Song of Solomon 1:5"> O my dove, who art in the clefts of the rock, in the recesses of the cliffs, let me see thy countenance, let me hear thy voice; for thy voice is sweet, and thy countenance is comely.—</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 13:32; 2 Peter 2:1–3; Song of Solomon 7:12"> Seize for us the foxes, the little foxes, that injure the vineyards; for our vineyards have young grapes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Song of Solomon 6:3; Song of Solomon 7:10; Psalms 63:1"> My friend is mine, and I am his—that feedeth among the lilies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Song of Solomon 4:6; Song of Solomon 2:8–9; 2 Peter 1:19"> Until the day become cool, and the shadows flee away, turn about, my friend, and be thou like the roebuck or the fawn of the hinds upon the mountains of separation.</span>
