<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:16; Song of Solomon 6:3; Song of Solomon 7:3"> Thy two breasts are like two fawns, the twins of the roe, that feed among the lilies.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Song of Solomon 5:1–2; Song of Solomon 4:12; Proverbs 5:19–20"> Thou hast ravished my heart, O my sister, &#91;my&#93; bride; thou hast ravished my heart with one of thy eyes, with one chain of thy neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:2–4; Song of Solomon 7:6; Galatians 5:22"> How beautiful are thy caresses, O my sister, &#91;my&#93; bride! how much more pleasant are thy caresses than wine! and the smell of thy fragrant oils more than all spices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Song of Solomon 5:1; Song of Solomon 7:9; Genesis 27:27"> Of sweet honey drop thy lips, O bride: honey and milk are under thy tongue; and the scent of thy garments is like the scent of Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 5:15–18; Revelation 21:27; 1 Corinthians 6:13"> A locked-up garden is my sister, &#91;my&#93; bride; a locked-up spring, a sealed fountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:14; Song of Solomon 6:11; Ecclesiastes 2:5"> Thy sprouts are an orchard of pomegranates, with precious fruits, copher and spikenard;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 30:23; Song of Solomon 4:6; Song of Solomon 6:2"> Spikenard and saffron; calamus and cinnamon, with all the trees of frankincense; myrrh and aloes, with all the chief of spices;</span>
