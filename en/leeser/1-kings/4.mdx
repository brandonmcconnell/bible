<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; 1 Corinthians 12:28; 2 Samuel 8:15–18"> And these were the princes whom he had: 'Azaryahu the son of Zadok the priest;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:16; Isaiah 62:6; 1 Chronicles 18:15"> Elichoreph and Achiyah, the sons of Shisha, scribes; Jehoshaphat the son of Achilud, the recorder;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 2:35; 1 Kings 2:26–27"> And Benayahu the son of Yehoyada' over the army; and Zadok and Ebyathar priests;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:33; 2 Samuel 15:37; 2 Samuel 16:16"> And Azaryahu the son of Nathan was over the superintendents; and Zabud the son of Nathan was an officer of state and the king's friend;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 9:15; 2 Samuel 20:24; 1 Kings 12:18"> And Achishar was over the household; and Adoniram the son of 'Abda was over the tribute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:1–15"> And Solomon had twelve superintendents over all Israel, who provided for the king and his household: one month in the year had the particular one to procure provisions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 24:33; Judges 19:1; Judges 17:1"> And these are their names: Ben-chur on the mountain of Ephraim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 21:16; 1 Samuel 6:20; 1 Samuel 6:12"> Ben-deker, in Makaz, and in Sha'albim, and Beth-shemesh, and Elon-beth-chanan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 15:35; Joshua 12:17; Joshua 17:2"> Ben-chessed, in Arubboth; to him pertained Sochoh and all the land of Chepher;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 11:2; Joshua 12:23; Joshua 17:11"> Ben-abinadab, in all the district of Dor; he had Taphath the daughter of Solomon for wife;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 17:11; Judges 5:19; Joshua 3:16"> Ba'ana the son of Achilud in Tha'anach and Megiddo, and all Beth-shean, which is by Zarethanah beneath Yizre'el, from Beth-shean to Abel-mecholah, as far as beyond Yokme'am;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:4; Numbers 32:41; 1 Kings 22:3"> Ben-geber, in Ramoth-gil'ad; to him pertained the villages of Ya'ir the son of Menasseh, which are in Gil'ad; to him also pertained the region of Argob, which is in Bashan, sixty great cities with walls and brazen bars;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 13:26; 2 Samuel 17:24; 2 Samuel 2:8"> Achinadab the son of 'Iddo in Machanayim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 19:32–39; 2 Samuel 15:27; 1 Samuel 18:18"> Achima'az in Naphtali; he also took Bahsemath the daughter of Solomon for wife;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 19:24–31; 2 Samuel 15:32"> Ba'anah the son of Chushai in Asher and in Be'aloth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 19:17–23"> Jehoshaphat the son of Paruach, in Issachar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 1:8; Joshua 18:20–28; Zechariah 12:13"> Shim'i the son of Ela, in Benjamin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:26–17; Joshua 13:9–12; Numbers 21:21–35"> Geber the son of Uri in the country of Gil'ad, the country of Sichon the king of the Emorites, and of 'Og the king of Bashan; besides the one superintendent who was in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; 1 Kings 3:8; Genesis 32:12"> Judah and Israel were numerous, as the sand which is by the sea in multitude; and they ate and drank, and made merry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; Joshua 1:4; Psalms 68:29"> (5:1) And Solomon was ruling over all the kingdoms from the river unto the land of the Philistines, and as far as the boundary of Egypt, which brought presents, and served Solomon all the days of his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> (5:2) And Solomon's provision for one day was thirty kors of fine flour, and sixty kors of meal,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:17–18"> (5:3) Ten fatted oxen, and twenty pasture oxen, and a hundred sheep, besides harts, and roebucks, and fallow-deer, and fatted fowl.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:9; Psalms 72:11; Psalms 72:3"> (5:4) For he had dominion over all the region on this side the river, from Thiphsach even to Gazzah, over all the kings on this side the river: and he had peace on all sides round about him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Micah 4:4; Zechariah 3:10; Judges 20:1"> (5:5) And Judah and Israel dwelt in safety, every man under his vine and under his fig-tree, from Dan even to Beer-sheba', all the days of Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:14; 2 Chronicles 9:25; 2 Samuel 8:4"> (5:6) And Solomon had forty thousand stalls for the horses for his chariots, and twelve thousand horsemen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> (5:7) And those superintendents provided for king Solomon, and for all that came near unto king Solomon's table, every man in his month: they let nothing be wanting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Esther 8:10; Esther 8:14; Micah 1:13"> (5:8) The barley also and the straw for the horses and the runners did they bring unto the place where he might be, every man according to his prescription.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 3:12; James 3:17; 1 Kings 4:20"> (5:9) And God gave unto Solomon wisdom and understanding exceedingly much, and largeness of heart, even as the sand that is on the sea-shore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Daniel 5:11–12; Genesis 25:6; Daniel 1:20"> (5:10) And Solomon's wisdom excelled the wisdom of all the children of the east country, and all the wisdom of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:6; 1 Chronicles 15:19; 1 Kings 3:12"> (5:11) And he was wiser than all men; than Ethan the Ezrachite, and Heman, and Kalkol, and Darda', the sons of Machol: and his name was &#91;spread&#93; among all the nations round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:1–17; Matthew 13:35; Proverbs 1:1–31"> (5:12) And he spoke three thousand proverbs; and his songs were a thousand and five.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Kings 19:23; Psalms 51:7; Psalms 92:12"> (5:13) And he spoke concerning the trees, from the cedar-tree that is on the Lebanon even unto the hyssop that springeth out of the wall; he spoke also concerning the beasts, and concerning the fowl, and concerning the creeping things, and concerning the fishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:23; 1 Kings 10:1; 2 Chronicles 9:1"> (5:14) And men came from all the people to hear the wisdom of Solomon, from all the kings of the earth, who had heard of his wisdom.</span>
