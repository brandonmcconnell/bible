<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 9:6; 2 Chronicles 10:1–19; Judges 9:1"> And Rehobo'am went to Shechem; for all Israel were come to Shechem to make him king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 11:40; 2 Chronicles 10:2–3; 1 Kings 11:26–31"> And it came to pass, when Jerobo'am the son of Nebat, heard of it, (but he was yet in Egypt, whither he was fled from the presence of king Solomon, and Jerobo'am dwelt in Egypt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And they had sent and called him;) that Jerobo'am and all the congregation of Israel came, and spoke unto Rehobo'am, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:11–18; 1 Kings 4:7; Matthew 11:29–30"> Thy father made our yoke hard; but do thou now make lighter the hard service of thy father, and his heavy yoke which he put upon us, and we will serve thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 12:12"> And he said unto them, Go away yet for three days, and then return to me. And the people went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 12:12; Job 32:7; 2 Samuel 16:20"> Then consulted king Rehobo'am with the old men, that had stood before Solomon his father while he yet lived, and said, How do ye advise that I should give an answer to this people?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; 2 Chronicles 10:6–7; 1 Kings 12:13"> And they spoke unto him, saying, If thou wilt this day be a servant unto this people, and wilt serve them, and be attentive to them, and speak to them good words; then will they be servants unto thee for all times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 19:20; Ecclesiastes 10:2–3; 2 Chronicles 10:8"> But he forsook the counsel of the old men, which they had given him, and consulted with the young men that were grown up with him, those who stood before him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 22:6–8; 2 Samuel 17:5–6; 2 Chronicles 10:9"> And he said unto them, What do you counsel how we should give an answer to this people, who have spoken to me, saying, Make lighter the yoke which thy father did put upon us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 10:10–11; Proverbs 10:14; Isaiah 47:6"> Then spoke unto him the young men that were grown up with him, saying, Thus must thou say unto this people that have spoken unto thee, saying, Thy father made our yoke heavy, but do thou make it lighter unto us: thus must thou speak unto them, My little finger is thicker than my father's loins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:11; Ezekiel 2:6; Jeremiah 28:13–14"> And now, if my father hath burdened you with a heavy yoke, I will even add to your yoke; if my father hath chastised you with whips, then will I chastise you with scorpion-thorns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 12:5; 2 Chronicles 10:12–14"> When now Jerobo'am and all the people came to Rehobo'am on the third day, as the king had spoken, saying, Return to me on the third day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 3:17; Proverbs 13:20; Ecclesiastes 10:12"> The king answered the people harshly, and forsook the old men's counsel that they had advised him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 5:16–18; 1 Kings 12:10–11; Exodus 5:5–9"> And he spoke to them after the counsel of the young men, saying, My father made your yoke heavy, and I will even add to your yoke; my father chastised you with whips, but I will chastise you with scorpion-thorns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 12:24; Deuteronomy 2:30; Judges 14:4"> Thus the king hearkened not unto the people; for it was so brought about from the Lord, in order that he might fulfill his word, which the Lord had spoken by means of Achiyah the Shilonite unto Jerobo'am the son of Nebat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:1; Jeremiah 23:5–6; 2 Chronicles 10:16"> So when all Israel saw that the king hearkened not unto them, the people gave the king an answer saying, What portion have we in David? nor have we an inheritance in the son of Jesse; to your tents, O Israel: now see to thy own house, David. So did Israel go away unto their tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 11:36; 1 Kings 11:13; 2 Chronicles 11:13–17"> But as for the children of Israel who dwelt in the cities of Judah, over them did Rehobo'am reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 4:6; 2 Samuel 20:24; 1 Kings 5:14"> Then sent king Rehobo'am Adoram, who was over the tribute; but all Israel stoned him with stones, that he died. Therefore king Rehobo'am made speed with all his might to get up into his chariot, to flee to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 17:21; 2 Chronicles 10:19; Joshua 4:9"> So did Israel rebel against the house of David unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 11:13; 1 Kings 11:32; Hosea 8:4"> And it came to pass, when all Israel heard that Jerobo'am was returned, that they sent and called him unto the congregation, and made him king over all Israel: there was none that followed the house of David, save the tribe of Judah alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:1–3; 1 Chronicles 21:5; 2 Chronicles 14:8"> And when Rehobo'am was come to Jerusalem, he assembled all the house of Judah, with the tribe of Benjamin, a hundred and eighty thousand chosen men, warriors, to fight against the house of Israel, to bring back the kingdom to Rehobo'am the son of Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:2; 2 Chronicles 12:5–7; 1 Kings 13:1"> But the word of God came unto Shema'yah the man of God, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 12:17"> Say unto Rehobo'am, the son of Solomon, the king of Judah, and unto all the house of Judah and Benjamin, and to the remnant of the people, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 12:15; 2 Chronicles 25:7–8; Hosea 8:4"> Thus hath said the Lord, Ye shall not go up, nor fight with your brethren the children of Israel: return every man to his house; for from me hath this thing been brought about. And they hearkened to the word of the Lord, and returned to go home, according to the word of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Judges 8:17; Judges 8:8; Judges 9:45–49"> And Jerobo'am built Shechem in the mountain of Ephraim, and dwelt therein; and he went out from there and built Penuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:18–21; Isaiah 7:9; Mark 2:6–8"> And Jerobo'am said in his heart, Now may the kingdom return to the house of David:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5–7; Proverbs 29:25; Deuteronomy 12:14"> If this people go up to prepare sacrifices in the house of the Lord at Jerusalem, then may the heart of this people turn again unto their Lord, even unto Rehobo'am the king of Judah, and they might kill me, and return to Rehobo'am the king of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Kings 10:29; Exodus 32:8; 2 Kings 17:16"> Whereupon the king took counsel, and he made two calves of gold, and said unto the people, You have been long enough going up to Jerusalem: behold, here are thy gods, O Israel, which have brought thee up out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 28:19; Judges 18:27–31; Hosea 4:15"> And he placed the one in Beth-el, and the other put he in Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 17:21; 1 Kings 13:34; 2 Kings 10:31"> And this thing became a sin; and the people went before the one, as far as Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:9; 2 Kings 17:32; 1 Kings 13:32–33"> And he made a house of the high-places, and made priests of the lowest of the people, who were not of the sons of Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 8:5; 1 Kings 8:2; Amos 7:10–13"> And Jerobo'am made a feast in the eighth month, on the fifteenth day of the month, like unto the feast that is in Judah, and he offered upon the altar; so did he in Beth-el, sacrificing unto the calves that he had made: and he stationed at Beth-el the priests of the high-places whom he had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Kings 13:1; Numbers 15:39; Matthew 15:6"> So he offered upon the altar which he had made in Beth-el on the fifteenth day of the eighth month, in the month which he had falsely devised of his own heart; and he ordained a feast unto the children of Israel, and he went up to the altar, to burn incense.</span>
