<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:1–2; Acts 7:47; 1 Chronicles 29:19"> And it came to pass, in the four hundred and eightieth year after the going forth of the children of Israel out of the land of Egypt, in the fourth year, in the month Ziv, which is the second month, of the reign of Solomon over Israel, that he built the house unto the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:1–41; Ezekiel 41:1–15; Revelation 21:16–17"> And the house which king Solomon built unto the Lord, was sixty cubits in length, and twenty in breadth, and thirty cubits in height.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:11; 2 Chronicles 3:3–4; Ezekiel 41:15"> And the porch before the temple of the house, was twenty cubits in length, in front of the breadth of the house; and ten cubits in breadth at the east side of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 41:26; Ezekiel 41:16"> And he made for the house windows wide without and narrow within.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 6:16; 1 Kings 6:19–21; 1 Kings 6:31"> And he built on the wall of the house a gallery round about, on the walls of the house round about, of the temple and of the most holy place: and he made side-chambers round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> The nethermost gallery was five cubits in breadth, and the middle was six cubits in breadth, and the third was seven cubits in breadth; for projections had he made to the house round about on the outside, so as to fasten nothing in the walls of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:5–6; 1 Peter 2:5; Exodus 20:25"> And the house, when it was in building, was built of entire stones as they had been prepared at the quarry: so that neither hammer, nor axe, nor any tool of iron was heard in the house, while it was in building.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:6–7"> The door for the middle &#91;gallery&#93; sidechamber was in the right side of the house: and with winding stairs they went up into the middle chamber, and out of the middle into the third.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 6:14; 1 Kings 6:38"> So he built the house, and finished it; and covered the house with hollow tiles, and with boards of cedar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And he built the gallery against all the house, &#91;each&#93; five cubits in height; and it was fastened on to the house with timber of cedar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the word of the Lord came to Solomon, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:10; Colossians 1:23; 1 Kings 3:14"> This house which thou art building—if thou wilt walk in my statutes, and execute my ordinances, and keep all my commandments to walk in them: then will I perform my word with thee, which I have spoken unto David thy father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:6; Exodus 25:8; Leviticus 26:11"> And I will dwell in the midst of the children of Israel, and I will not forsake my people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 6:9; 1 Kings 6:38; Acts 7:47–48"> So did Solomon build the house, and finish it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 7:7"> And he built the walls of the house within with boards of cedar; from the floor of the house to where the walls touched the ceiling did he overlay it on the inside with wood; and he overlaid the floor of the house with boards of fir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:8; 1 Kings 8:6; Hebrews 9:3"> And he built the twenty cubits on the lower side of the house with boards of cedar, from the floor to the battlements; and he built it within, for the debir, for the holy of holies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the house, that is, the temple before it, was forty cubits long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 7:24"> And the cedar on the house within was carved with colocynths and opening flowers; all was cedar, no stone was seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 8:6–10; Psalms 28:2; 1 Kings 6:5"> And the debir in the house within did he prepare, to set therein the ark of the covenant of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 6:2–3; 1 Kings 6:22; 1 Kings 7:48"> And the interior of the debir was twenty cubits in length, and twenty cubits in breadth, and twenty cubits was its height: and he overlaid it with pure gold; and he overlaid the altar with cedar-wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:7–9; 2 Chronicles 3:14–16; 1 Kings 6:5"> And Solomon overlaid the house within with pure gold: and he barred by means of chains of gold the front of the debir; and he overlaid it with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 30:1; Exodus 30:3; 2 Chronicles 3:7–14"> And the whole house he overlaid with gold, until all the house was finished: also the whole altar that was before the debir did he overlay with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 37:7–9; Genesis 3:24; 1 Peter 1:12"> And within the debir he made two cherubims of oleaster-wood, each ten cubits high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And five cubits was the one wing of the cherub, and five cubits the other wing of the cherub: &#91;there were&#93; ten cubits from the uttermost part of the one wing unto the uttermost part of the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And the other cherub was also ten cubits: both the cherubim were of one measure and one form.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> The height of the one cherub was ten cubits, and so that of the other cherub.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 37:9; Exodus 25:20; 2 Chronicles 5:8"> And he set the cherubim within the innermost part of the house: and they spread forth the wings of the cherubim, so that the wing of the one touched the one wall, and the wing of the other cherub touched the other wall; and their wings toward the middle of the house touched one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And he overlaid the cherubim with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 6:32; Psalms 92:12–15; 2 Chronicles 4:2–5"> And on all the wall of the house round about he carved figures of cherubim and palm-trees and opening flowers, in the debir and in the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 21:18–21; Isaiah 60:17; Isaiah 54:11–12"> And the floor of the house he overlaid with gold for the debir and for the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–20; Ephesians 3:18; John 14:6"> And for the entrance of the debir he made doors of oleaster-wood: the lintel with the side-posts forming five sides.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 6:18; 1 Kings 6:29"> And also upon the two doors of oleaster-wood he carved figures of cherubim and palm-trees and opening flowers, and overlaid them with gold, and spread the gold, by beating, upon the cherubim, and upon the palm-trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> So also made he for the entrance of the temple door-posts of oleaster-wood in shape of a square;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:23–25; 1 Kings 5:8"> And two doors of fir-wood: the one door having two leaves which were folding, and the other door having two leaves which were folding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And he carved thereon cherubim, and palm-trees and opening flowers: and he overlaid them with gold fitting upon the carved work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 7:12; 2 Chronicles 7:7; Exodus 27:9–19"> And he built the inner court of three rows of hewn stone, and one row of cedar beams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Kings 6:1; 2 Chronicles 3:2"> In the fourth year was the foundation of the house of the Lord laid, in the month Ziv;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Ezra 6:14–15; 1 Kings 7:1; Zechariah 4:9"> And in the eleventh year, in the month Bul, which is the eighth month, was the house finished throughout all its parts, and according to all its requirements. So was he building it seven years.</span>
