<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jude 1:12; Genesis 43:32–34"> When thou sittest to eat with a ruler, consider diligently what is before thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 3:19; Matthew 18:8–9; 1 Corinthians 9:27"> For thou puttest a knife to thy throat, if thou be a man of a craving desire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 23:6; Psalms 141:4; Ephesians 4:22"> Do not long for his savory meats; they are deceitful food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5; Proverbs 28:20; Matthew 6:19"> Fatigue thyself not to become rich; because thou hast understanding, forbear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 27:24; 1 John 2:16; James 5:1–2"> When thou lettest merely thy eyes fly over it, it is no more: for it will ever make itself wings: like an eagle will it fly toward heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 141:4; Proverbs 23:3; Daniel 1:8–10"> Eat not the bread of a man with an evil eye, and do not long for his savory meats;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 12:2; Psalms 55:21; Judges 16:15"> For as though there were a division in his soul, so doth he act: Eat and drink, saith he to thee; but his heart is not with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Thy morsel which thou hast eaten must thou spit out, and thou hast wasted thy pleasant words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 9:7–8; Matthew 7:6; Proverbs 26:4–5"> Speak not before the ears of a fool; for he will despise the intelligence of thy words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 22:28; Zechariah 7:10; Deuteronomy 27:17"> Remove not the ancient landmark, and into the fields of the fatherless must thou not enter;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 22:23; Job 19:25; Jeremiah 51:36"> For their redeemer is strong; he will indeed plead their cause with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 5:1–2; Proverbs 2:2–6; Proverbs 23:19"> Apply thy heart unto instruction, and thy ears to the sayings of knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 13:24; Proverbs 19:18; Proverbs 29:15"> Withhold not from a lad correction; for if thou beat him with the rod, he will not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:5; Proverbs 22:15; 1 Corinthians 11:32"> Thou wilt indeed beat him with the rod; but thou wilt deliver his soul from perdition.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 23:24–25; 2 John 1:4; Proverbs 29:3"> My son, If thy heart be wise, my heart shall rejoice, even mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 8:6; Ephesians 5:4; James 3:2"> And my reins shall exult when thy lips speak what is equitable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 28:14; 2 Corinthians 7:1; 1 Peter 1:17"> Let not thy heart be envious against sinners; but &#91;remain&#93; in the fear of the lord all the time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 10:35; Jeremiah 29:11; Psalms 9:18"> For surely there is a future, and thy hope will not be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 23:26; Proverbs 23:12; Proverbs 4:10–23"> Hear thou, my son, and become wise, and guide thy heart on the right way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 21:34; Ephesians 5:18; Isaiah 5:22"> Be not among those that drink wine immoderately, among those that over-indulge in eating flesh:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 21:17; Proverbs 19:15; Deuteronomy 21:20"> For the drunkard and the glutton will come to poverty; and drowsiness clotheth a man in rags.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 30:17; Proverbs 1:8; John 19:26–27"> Hearken unto thy father that hath begotten thee, and despise not thy mother although she be old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 18:15; Matthew 13:44; Psalms 119:72"> Buy the truth and sell it not; &#91;also&#93; wisdom, and instruction, and understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 15:20; Proverbs 23:15–16"> The father of the righteous will be greatly glad, and he that begetteth a wise child will have joy through him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 17:25; Luke 11:27–28; Luke 1:58"> Let &#91;then&#93; thy father and thy mother rejoice, and let her that hath born thee be glad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 119:2; Psalms 119:9–11; Deuteronomy 6:5"> Give, my son, thy heart unto me, and let thy eyes watch my ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 22:14"> For a harlot is a deep ditch, and a strange woman is a narrow well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Proverbs 7:12; Proverbs 22:14"> She also lieth in wait like a robber, and she increaseth the treacherous among men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 5:18; Isaiah 5:11; Proverbs 23:21"> Who hath woe? who hath sorrow? who hath quarrels? who hath complaints? who hath wounds without cause? who hath redness of eyes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ephesians 5:18; Isaiah 5:11; Proverbs 20:1"> They that tarry late over the wine: they that come to seek for mixed drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 John 2:16; 2 Samuel 11:2; Psalms 119:37"> Do not look on the wine when it looketh red, when it giveth its color in the cup, when it glideth down so readily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:8; Isaiah 28:7–8; Amos 9:3"> At the last it will bite like a serpent, and like a basilisk will it sting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jude 1:12–13; Psalms 69:12; Daniel 5:4"> Thy eyes will see strange forms, and thy heart will speak perverse things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2–7; 1 Kings 20:16–22; 1 Samuel 30:16–17"> And thou wilt be like one that lieth down in the heart of the sea, or as he that lieth on the top of a mast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 56:12; Jeremiah 5:3; 2 Peter 2:22"> “They smote me, &#91;but&#93; I suffered no pain; they struck me hard, &#91;but&#93; I felt it not: when shall I awake? I will continue to seek it again.”</span>
