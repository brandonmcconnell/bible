<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 22:17; Proverbs 4:20; Proverbs 2:1"> My son, attend unto my wisdom; to my understanding incline thou thy ear:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 16:23; Proverbs 15:7; Malachi 2:6–7"> That thou mayest observe discretion, and that thy lips may keep knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 2:16; Proverbs 6:24; Proverbs 7:5"> For as of fine honey drop the lips of an adulterous woman, and smoother than oil is her palate;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Psalms 57:4; Psalms 55:21"> But her end is bitter as wormwood, it is sharp as a two-edged sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 7:27; Proverbs 2:18–19"> Her feet go down to death, her steps take firm hold on the nether world:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 4:26; Proverbs 5:21; Psalms 16:11"> So that she cannot balance the path of life; her tracks are unsteady, and she knoweth it not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 4:1; Proverbs 7:24; Proverbs 22:17–21"> And now, O ye children, hearken unto me, and depart not from the sayings of my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 7:25; Ephesians 5:11; Matthew 6:13"> Remove far from her thy way, and come not nigh to the door of her house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:26; Hosea 4:13–14; Genesis 38:23–26"> That thou mayest not give up unto others thy vigor, and thy years unto the cruel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 7:9; Proverbs 6:35; Proverbs 31:3"> That strangers may not satisfy themselves with thy strength, and with thy exertions, in the house of an alien:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 6:21; Revelation 22:15; Hebrews 13:4"> While thou moanest at thy end, when thy flesh and thy body are coming to their end,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 12:1; Proverbs 1:7; Proverbs 15:5"> And thou sayest, How have I hated correction, and how hath my heart rejected reproof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:8; Luke 15:18; Hebrews 13:7"> While I hearkened not to the voice of my instructors, and to my teachers I inclined not my ear;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; 2 Peter 2:10–18; 1 Corinthians 10:6–8"> But little more was wanting, and I had been in all &#91;kinds of&#93; unhappiness in the midst of the congregation and assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:2–5; Hebrews 13:4; Proverbs 5:18–19"> Drink water out of thy own cistern, and running waters out of thy own well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 128:3; Psalms 127:3; Isaiah 48:21"> So will thy springs overflow abroad; and in the open streets will be thy rivulets of water;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> They will be thy own only, and not those of strangers with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:9; Malachi 2:14–15; Song of Solomon 4:15"> Thy fountain will be blessed: and rejoice with the wife of thy youth,—</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Song of Solomon 7:3; Song of Solomon 8:14; Song of Solomon 4:5"> The lovely gazelle and the graceful chamois: let her bosom satisfy thee abundantly at all times; with her love be thou ravished continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 7:5; Proverbs 6:24; Proverbs 23:33"> And why wilt thou, my son, be ravished with an adulteress, and embrace the bosom of an alien woman?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Proverbs 15:3; Job 34:21"> For before the eyes of the Lord are the ways of man, and all his tracks doth he weigh in the balance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 11:5; Psalms 9:15; Proverbs 1:18"> His own iniquities will truly catch the wicked, and with the cords of his sin will he be held firmly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 36:12; Job 4:21; Proverbs 10:21"> He will indeed die for want of correction; and through the abundance of his folly will he sink into error.</span>
