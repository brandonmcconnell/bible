<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 1:1; 1 Kings 4:32; Micah 1:1"> Also these are the proverbs of Solomon, which the men of Hezekiah the king of Judah have collected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:29; Romans 11:33–34; Job 11:7–8"> It is the honor of God to conceal a thing; but the honor of kings is to search out a matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 55:9; Psalms 103:11; 1 Kings 4:29"> As are the heavens for height, and the earth is for depth, so should the heart of kings be unsearchable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:20–21; Ezekiel 22:18; Proverbs 17:3"> Take away the dross from the silver, and there will come forth a vessel for the melter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 16:12; Proverbs 20:8; Psalms 101:7–8"> Take away the wicked from before the king, and his throne will be firmly established in righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 27:2; Proverbs 25:27; Proverbs 16:19"> Do not glorify thyself in the presence of the king, and force thyself not into the place of great men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 14:7–11; Luke 18:14; 1 Peter 5:5"> For better it is that it be said unto thee, “Come up hither,” than that thou shouldst be put lower in the presence of the prince, which thy own eyes have &#91;often&#93; seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 5:25; Proverbs 17:14; Proverbs 18:6"> Do not proceed to a contest hastily, lest &#91;thou know not&#93; what thou wilt have to do at its end, when thy neighbor hath put thee to confusion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 18:5–17; Proverbs 11:13; Proverbs 20:19"> Carry on thy cause with thy neighbor; but lay not open the secret of another:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 119:39"> Lest he that heareth it put thee to shame, and thy infamy never be removed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 15:23; Isaiah 50:4; Ecclesiastes 12:10"> Like apples of gold among figures of silver is a word spoken in a proper manner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 141:5; Proverbs 15:31–32; Proverbs 15:5"> As an earring of gold, and a pendant of fine gold, so is a wise reprover toward an ear that listeneth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 13:17; Proverbs 25:25; Philippians 2:25–30"> As the cooling of snow on a harvest-day, so is a faithful messenger to those that send him; for he refresheth the soul of his master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 20:6; Luke 14:11; 2 Peter 2:15–19"> Like clouds and wind without rain, so is a man that vaunteth falsely of a gift.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; Ecclesiastes 10:4; Proverbs 16:14"> By long forbearing is a prince persuaded, and a soft tongue breaketh bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 25:27; Ephesians 5:18; Proverbs 24:13–14"> Hast thou found honey: eat so much as is sufficient for thee: lest thou consume too much of it, and have to vomit it forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 19:2–3; Judges 19:18–21; Romans 15:24"> Make thy foot scarce in the house of thy friend: lest he have too much of thee, and so hate thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Proverbs 12:18; James 3:6"> A battle-axe, and a sword, and a sharpened arrow is a man that testifieth as a false witness against his neighbor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 36:6; Ezekiel 29:6–7; Job 6:14–20"> Like a broken tooth and a foot out of joint, is confidence in a treacherous man in a time of distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Ecclesiastes 3:4; Isaiah 58:7"> &#91;As&#93; he that taketh off his garment on a cold day, &#91;as&#93; vinegar is upon natron: so is he that singeth songs before an unhappy heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 12:20–21; Matthew 5:44; Exodus 23:4–5"> If thy enemy be hungry, give him bread to eat; and if he be thirsty, give him water to drink;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:12; Matthew 10:13; 1 Corinthians 15:18"> For though thou gatherest coals of fire upon his head, yet will the Lord repay it unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 37:22; Psalms 15:3; Romans 1:30"> The north wind bringeth forth rain: so doth secret talking, angry countenances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 21:19; Proverbs 21:9; Proverbs 27:15–16"> It is better to dwell in the corner of a roof, than with a quarrelsome woman even in a roomy house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 15:30; Romans 10:15; Psalms 63:1"> As cold water is to a fainting soul, so are good news from a far-off country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:18–19; 1 Samuel 22:14–18; 1 Thessalonians 2:15"> Like a turbid spring and a corrupt fountain, is a righteous man that giveth way before the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 5:44; Proverbs 27:2; Philippians 2:3"> To eat too much honey is not good: so is it honor to set a limit to men's honor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 16:32; Proverbs 22:24; 2 Chronicles 32:5"> Like a city that is broken in, and is without walls: so is the man that hath no control over his spirit.</span>
