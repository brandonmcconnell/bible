<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 31:1; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> The words of Agur the son of Yakeh, even the prophecy: the man said unto Ithiel, even unto Ithiel and Ukkal,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 73:22; 1 Corinthians 3:18; Isaiah 6:5"> “Surely I am more brutish than any man, and have not the understanding of a common man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 3:7; Ephesians 3:18–19; Romans 11:33"> Nor have I learned wisdom, so that I should have knowledge of the Holy One.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 3:13; Ephesians 4:8–10; Job 26:8"> Who was it that ascended into heaven, and came down again? who gathered the wind in his fists? who bound the waters in a garment? who set up all the ends of the earth? what is his name, and what is his son's name, if thou knowest it?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 18:30; James 3:17; Psalms 12:6"> Every saying of God is purified: he is a shield unto those that put their trust in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; Deuteronomy 4:2; Revelation 22:18–19"> Do not add aught unto his words: lest he reprove thee, and thou be found a liar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 10:42; 2 Kings 2:9; Psalms 27:4"> Two things do I request of thee: deny them not to me before I die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:6–8; Psalms 62:9–10; Psalms 119:37"> Vanity and lying words do thou remove far from me: neither poverty nor riches give thou unto me; let me eat the bread appointed unto me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 13:6; Deuteronomy 31:20; Ezekiel 16:49–50"> Lest I become over-full, and deny thee, and say, Who is the Lord? or lest I become poor, and steal, and trespass against the name of my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 14:4; Ecclesiastes 7:21; Deuteronomy 23:15"> Do not calumniate a servant unto his master: lest he curse thee, and thou incur guilt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 20:20; Proverbs 30:17; Exodus 21:17"> There is a generation that curseth its father, and doth not bless its mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 18:11; Proverbs 16:2; 2 Timothy 3:5"> There is a generation that is pure in its own eyes, and yet is not washed &#91;clean&#93; of its filthiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 6:17; Isaiah 2:11; Psalms 101:5"> There is a generation—O how lofty are its eyes! and its eyelids are so lifted up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Psalms 3:7; Psalms 14:4"> There is a generation, whose teeth are as swords, and whose string teeth are as knives, to devour the poor from off the earth, and the needy from among men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 30:21; Proverbs 30:29; Proverbs 6:16"> Insatiability hath two daughters, &#91;crying,&#93; Give, give. There are three things that are never satisfied, yea, four things which never say, Enough:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 27:20; Habakkuk 2:5; Genesis 30:1"> The nether world; and a barren womb; the earth which is not satisfied with water; and the fire which never saith, Enough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 23:22; Deuteronomy 21:18–21; Proverbs 30:11"> The eye that mocketh at his father, and despiseth to obey his mother, this shall the ravens of the valley pick out; and the young eagles shall eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 42:3; Psalms 139:6"> Three things there are which are too wonderful for me; and four, which I know not:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 40:31; Job 39:27; Exodus 22:16"> The way of the eagle in the air; the way of a serpent upon a rock; the way of a ship in the heart of the sea; and the way of a man with a young woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 5:6; Proverbs 7:13–23; Numbers 5:11–30"> Such is the way of an adulterous woman: she eateth, and wipeth her mouth, and saith, I have done no wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Under three things the earth trembleth, and under four which she cannot bear:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 19:10; Ecclesiastes 10:7; 1 Samuel 25:10–11"> Under a servant when he becometh king, and a worthless fool when he hath eaten enough bread;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 21:19; Proverbs 27:15; Proverbs 29:21"> Under an odious woman when she is married, and a bond-woman when she dispossesseth her mistress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 12:7"> There are four which are the little ones of the earth, and they are nevertheless exceedingly wise:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 6:6–8"> The ants are a people not strong, therefore do they prepare in the summer their food;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 104:18; Leviticus 11:5"> The conies are but a feeble people, therefore do they place on the rocks their houses;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joel 2:25; Joel 1:6–7; Joel 2:7–11"> The locusts have no king, and yet they go forth in troops altogether;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> The spider thou canst catch with &#91;thy&#93; hands, and yet she is in the palaces of a king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Three there are that have a stately step, and four, that are stately in going:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 23:24; Judges 14:18"> The lion, the mightiest among beasts, who turneth not round from before any one;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Daniel 3:15–18; Proverbs 20:2; Proverbs 16:14"> The light-legged greyhound, and the he-goat; and a king, against whom there is no rising up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 21:5; Job 40:4; Proverbs 26:12"> If thou hast become degraded by lifting up thyself, or if thou hast devised evil, put thy hand to thy mouth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 17:14; Proverbs 29:22; Proverbs 28:25"> For the pressure of milk bringeth forth butter, and the pressure of the nose bringeth forth blood: so the pressure of wrath bringeth forth strife.</span>
