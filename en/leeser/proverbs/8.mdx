<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 1:20–21; Matthew 3:3; John 7:37"> Behold, wisdom calleth, and understanding sendeth forth her voice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 9:14; Proverbs 9:3"> On the top of high places, by the wayside, at the house where there are &#91;many&#93; paths doth she place herself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 18:20; Acts 5:20; Job 29:7"> Alongside of gates, at the opening of the city, at the entrance of the town&#91;-doors&#93; doth she call loudly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 11:15; John 3:16; Psalms 50:1"> Unto you, O men, I call, and my voice &#91;goeth forth&#93; to the sons of men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 1:22; Acts 26:18; Revelation 3:17–18"> Learn, O ye simple, to understand prudence: and, ye fools, be ye of an understanding heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 1:26; Matthew 7:28–29; Proverbs 23:16"> Hear! for of noble things will I speak; and the opening of my lips shall be of what is equitable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 37:30; John 8:14; John 17:17"> For truth uttereth my palate ever, and the abomination of my lips is wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 7:46; Psalms 12:6; Proverbs 8:13"> In righteousness are all the sayings of my mouth, there is in them nothing crooked or perverse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 14:6; James 1:5; John 7:17"> They are all evident to the man of understanding, and correct to those that have obtained knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 2:4–5; Psalms 119:127; Psalms 119:72"> Accept my correction, and not silver; and knowledge rather than choice gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 3:14–15; Proverbs 4:5–7; Proverbs 16:16"> For wisdom is better than pearls; and all the things that men wish for are not equal to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 28:26; Exodus 35:30–4; Romans 11:33"> I wisdom dwell with prudence, and the knowledge of discreet thoughts do I discover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 97:10; Proverbs 16:6; 1 Samuel 2:3"> The fear of the Lord is to hate evil, pride and arrogance, and the evil way: and the mouth of perverseness do I hate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:19; Isaiah 9:6; Isaiah 40:14"> Mine are counsel and sound wisdom: I am understanding; mine is might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 13:1; Daniel 2:21; Matthew 28:18"> Through me do kings reign, and chieftains give decrees &#91;in&#93; righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Through me do princes rule, and the nobles, even all the judges of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 14:23; John 14:21; Psalms 91:14"> I indeed love those that love me: and those that seek me earnestly shall find me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:16; Matthew 6:33; Luke 16:11–12"> Riches and honor are with me, yea, enduring wealth and righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 3:14; Proverbs 10:20; Ecclesiastes 7:12"> My fruit is better than gold, and than fine gold; and my products, than choice silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:32; John 10:27–28; Psalms 32:8"> On the road of righteousness do I walk firmly, in the midst the paths of justice:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 1:4; Proverbs 24:4; Matthew 25:46"> That I may cause those that love me to inherit a lasting possession; and their treasures will I fill</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 1:17; John 1:1–2; Proverbs 3:19"> The Lord created me as the beginning of his way, the first of his works from the commencement,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 17:24; Psalms 2:6; Genesis 1:26"> From eternity was I appointed chief, from the beginning, from the earliest times of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 3:20; Genesis 1:2; 1 John 4:9"> When there were yet no depths, was I brought forth; when there were yet no springs laden heavily with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 90:2; Psalms 102:25–28; Job 38:4–11"> Before the mountains were yet sunk down, before the hills was I brought forth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 1:1–31"> While as yet he had not made the land and open fields, nor the chief of the dust of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 40:22; Job 26:10; Proverbs 3:19"> When he prepared the heavens, I was there; when he drew a circle over the face of the deep;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> When he fastened the skies above; when the springs of the deep became strong;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 104:9; Jeremiah 5:22; Genesis 1:9–10"> When he assigned to the sea his decree, that the waters should not transgress his order: when he established firmly the foundations of the earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 1:1–3; John 1:18; Isaiah 42:1"> Then was I near him, as a nursling: and I was day by day &#91;his&#93; delights, playing before him at all times;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 16:3; John 13:1; Psalms 40:6–8"> Playing in the world, his earth; and having my delights with the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 11:28; Psalms 119:1–2; Psalms 1:1–4"> And now, O children &#91;of men&#93;, hearken unto me! for happy those that observe my ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 4:1; Psalms 81:11–12; Proverbs 5:1"> Hear correction, and be wise, and reject it not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 11:28; Matthew 7:24; Proverbs 3:13"> Happy is the man that hearkeneth unto me, watching day by day at my gates, waiting at the posts of my doors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 3:36; 1 John 5:11–12; Proverbs 12:2"> For he who findeth me findeth life, and he obtaineth favor from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 15:32; Proverbs 1:31; 1 Corinthians 16:22"> But he that sinneth against me doth violence to his own soul: all those that hate me love death.</span>
