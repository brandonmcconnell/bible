<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 19:10; Proverbs 28:16; Esther 4:6"> As snow is in summer, and as rain in harvest: so is honor not seemly to a fool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 23:8; Nehemiah 13:2; Psalms 109:28"> As the bird &#91;cometh&#93; to flit away, as the swallow, to fly off: so will an undeserved curse not come &#91;to fulfillment&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 10:13; Psalms 32:9; 1 Corinthians 4:21"> A whip is for the horse, a bridle for the ass, and a rod for the fool's back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 26:5; 1 Peter 3:9; Jude 1:9"> Do not answer a fool according to his folly, lest thou also become equal unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 12:16; Matthew 16:1–4; Proverbs 28:11"> Answer a fool according to his folly, lest he be wise in his own eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 10:26; Proverbs 25:13; Proverbs 13:17"> He choppeth off the feet, and drinketh vexation, that sendeth important messages by the hand of a fool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 26:9; Psalms 64:8; Proverbs 17:7"> Too feebly hang down the thighs on a lame man: so is a parable in the mouth of fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 26:1; Proverbs 30:22; Proverbs 19:10"> As is the one that bindeth a stone fast in a sling, so is he that giveth honor to a fool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 23:35"> &#91;As&#93; a thorn that is come into the hand of a drunkard, so is a parable in the mouth of fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 2:6; Proverbs 11:31"> A master injureth all things when he hireth a fool or hireth mere rovers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 2:22; Matthew 12:45; Exodus 8:15"> As a dog returneth to his vomit, so doth a fool repeat to act in his folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 29:20; 1 Corinthians 3:18–19; Proverbs 26:5"> When thou seest a man wise in his own eyes, then is there more hope for a fool than for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 22:13; Proverbs 19:15; Proverbs 15:19"> The slothful saith, There is a leopard in the way: a lion is between the streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 6:12; Proverbs 12:27; Proverbs 12:24"> As a door turneth upon its hinges, so doth the slothful upon his bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 19:24"> Hath the slothful hidden his hand in the dish, it wearieth him to bring it back again to his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 26:12; 1 Peter 3:15; Proverbs 12:15"> The slothful is wiser in his own eyes, than seven men that can give wise answers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 20:3; 2 Timothy 2:23–24; Luke 12:14"> As is one that taketh hold of a dog by the ears, so is he that passing by becometh excited about a dispute which concerneth him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 25:18; Isaiah 50:11; Proverbs 7:23"> As one fatigueth himself shooting off firebrands, arrows, and death:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 5:4; Proverbs 10:23; Proverbs 15:21"> So is the man that hath cheated his neighbor, and saith, Behold, I am only jesting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 22:10; James 3:6; Proverbs 16:28"> Where there is no wood, the fire goeth out: so where there is no whisperer, strife is silenced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 15:18; Proverbs 29:22; 2 Samuel 20:1"> As charcoals are added to burning coals, and wood to fire, so is a contentious man fitted to enkindle a dispute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 18:8; Proverbs 20:19; Ezekiel 22:9"> The words of a whisperer are as wounds, and they go down into the innermost chambers of the body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 22:47–48; Luke 11:39; Proverbs 10:18"> Like silver dross laid over an earthen vessel, so are burning lips with a bad heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 12:20; Proverbs 10:18; Psalms 41:6"> With his lips dissembleth he that hateth, and within himself layeth he up deceit:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 6:16–19; Jeremiah 12:6; Psalms 28:3"> Though he make his voice sound ever so graciously, believe him not; for there are seven abominations in his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 55:21–23; 1 Samuel 18:17; 1 Samuel 18:21"> If one's hatred be covered by deception, then shall be laid bare his wickedness before a &#91;whole&#93; assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 28:10; Psalms 9:15; Psalms 7:15–16"> Whoso diggeth a pit will fall therein; and upon him that rolleth a stone, will it return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 8:44–49; Luke 20:20–21; John 10:32–33"> A lying tongue hateth those that are crushed by it; and a flattering mouth prepareth &#91;others'&#93; downfall.</span>
