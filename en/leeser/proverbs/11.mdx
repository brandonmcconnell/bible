<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:13–16; Proverbs 16:11; Proverbs 20:23"> Balances of deceit are an abomination of the Lord; but a full weight &#91;obtaineth&#93; his favor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 29:23; Proverbs 15:33; Proverbs 16:18–19"> When pride cometh, then cometh disgrace; but with the modest there is wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 13:6; Proverbs 28:18; Psalms 26:1"> The integrity of the upright guideth them; but the cunning of the treacherous destroyeth them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:18; Proverbs 10:2; Genesis 7:1"> Wealth cannot profit on the day of wrath; but righteousness deliver from death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 5:22; Psalms 9:15–16; Proverbs 1:31–32"> The righteousness of the perfect maketh even his way; but by his own wickedness will the wicked fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:8; 1 Kings 2:44; Psalms 7:15–16"> The righteousness of the upright will deliver them; but through their own sinful desires are the treacherous caught.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 10:28; Job 8:13–14; Job 11:20"> When a wicked man dieth, &#91;his&#93; hope vanisheth; and the expectation of his children is lost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 6:23–24; Proverbs 21:18; Esther 7:9–10"> The righteous is delivered out of distress, and the wicked cometh in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 2:10–16; Proverbs 4:5–6; 1 John 2:27"> With his mouth doth the hypocrite destroy his neighbor; but through knowledge are the righteous delivered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 28:12; Esther 8:15–16; Psalms 58:10–11"> When it goeth well with the righteous, the town rejoiceth loudly: and when the wicked perish, there is joyful shouting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 14:34; Proverbs 29:8; James 3:6"> Through the blessing of the upright a city is exalted; but through the mouth of the wicked it is pulled down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 10:19; 1 Peter 2:23; Luke 18:9"> He that despiseth his neighbor is void of sense; but a man of understanding maintaineth silence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 20:19; Proverbs 25:9; Leviticus 19:16"> He that walketh about as talebearer revealeth secrets; but he that is of a faithful spirit concealeth the matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 24:6; Proverbs 15:22; Proverbs 20:18"> Where there is no wise guidance, a people must fall: but &#91;it will obtain&#93; help through the multitude of counsellors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 17:18; Proverbs 6:1–5; Proverbs 22:26–27"> With evil will he be overwhelmed that is surety for a stranger: but he that hateth giving the hand as pledge is safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 31:30–31; Luke 10:42; Acts 16:14–15"> A woman endowed with grace will surely obtain honor; and the powerful will obtain riches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; Matthew 25:34–40; James 2:13"> The man of kindness doth good to his own soul; but he that troubleth his own flesh is cruel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 6:8–9; Ecclesiastes 10:8; Hosea 10:12–13"> The wicked practiseth a work of falsehood; but he that soweth righteousness &#91;obtaineth&#93; the reward of truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 10:16; Romans 2:8–9; Proverbs 12:28"> He who is firm in righteousness attaineth to life: and he that pursueth evil &#91;doth it&#93; to his own death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 119:1; Proverbs 21:29; 1 Chronicles 29:17"> An abomination of the Lord are those of a perverse heart; but his favor is for those who are unblemished in their way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 112:1–2; Acts 2:39; Jeremiah 32:39"> The hand &#91;of God&#93; being against &#91;his&#93; hand, the bad man shall not go unpunished; but the seed of the righteous shall escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 3:3–4; Proverbs 31:30; Nahum 3:4–6"> As a golden ring in a swine's snout, so is a handsome woman that hath thrown off discretion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; Romans 2:8–9; Matthew 5:6"> The desire of the righteous is only good: but the hope of the wicked is the wrath &#91;of God&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Luke 6:38; Deuteronomy 15:10"> There is a man that scattereth gifts, and yet his wealth is increased: and there is one that withholdeth more than is proper, and still cometh only to want.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; 2 Corinthians 9:6–9; Isaiah 58:7–11"> A beneficent soul will be abundantly gratified; and he that refresheth &#91;others&#93; will be also refreshed himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 29:13; Genesis 42:6; Amos 8:4–6"> Him that withholdeth corn, the people will denounce; but blessing will be heaped upon the head of the one that selleth it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 7:15–16; Proverbs 17:11; Psalms 9:15–16"> He that diligently searcheth after good seeketh favor; but if one inquireth after evil, it will come unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:17; Jeremiah 17:8; Psalms 52:7–8"> He that trusteth in his riches will surely fall; but the righteous shall grow like the leaves &#91;of a tree&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:16; Proverbs 14:19; Proverbs 15:27"> He that troubleth his own house will inherit the wind; and the fool will become the servant to the wise of heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Daniel 12:3; James 5:20; 1 Corinthians 9:19–23"> The fruit of the righteous is of the tree of life, and the wise draweth souls to himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17–18; Jeremiah 25:29; 1 Corinthians 11:30–32"> Behold, the righteous is recompensed on the earth: how much more the wicked and the sinner.</span>
