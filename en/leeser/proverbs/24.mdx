<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Proverbs 23:17; Proverbs 3:31"> Be thou not envious of bad men, and do not long to be with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Psalms 7:14; Psalms 28:3"> For their heart meditateth destruction, and of mischief do their lips speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 14:1; 1 Corinthians 3:9; Proverbs 9:1"> Through wisdom is a house built; and through understanding is it firmly established;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 15:6; Proverbs 21:20; Proverbs 20:15"> And through knowledge are chambers filled with all manner of precious and pleasant wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:19; Colossians 1:11; Proverbs 21:22"> A wise man is &#91;always&#93; in power; and a man of knowledge fortifieth &#91;his&#93; strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 11:14; Proverbs 20:18; Proverbs 15:22"> For by wise counsel canst thou conduct thy war; and there is help in a multitude of counsellors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 14:6; Psalms 10:5; 1 Corinthians 2:14"> Wisdom is too high for a fool: in the gate can he not open his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 21:11; Ezekiel 38:10–11; Proverbs 14:22"> Him that deviseth to do evil, men call a master of wicked devices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 15:19; 2 Corinthians 10:5; Isaiah 55:7"> The counsel of folly is sin; and an abomination to men is the scorner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 40:28–31; Ephesians 3:13; Jeremiah 51:46"> If thou despond on the day of distress, thy strength is small.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 82:4; Isaiah 58:6–7; 1 John 3:16–17"> Deliver those that are taken unto death, and those that are moved away to the slaughter hold back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 34:11; Revelation 2:23; Jeremiah 17:10"> If thou shouldst say, Behold, we know not this man: lo, he that weigheth hearts will truly regard it, and he that keepeth thy soul will surely know it; and he will give a recompense to man according to his doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 7:15; Song of Solomon 5:1; Proverbs 25:16"> Eat honey, my son, because it is good; and the fine honey, which is sweet to thy palate:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 1:25; Jeremiah 15:16; Proverbs 2:10"> So obtain the knowledge of wisdom for thy soul: when thou hast found her, then shall there be a &#91;happy&#93; future, and thy hope shall not be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 9:24; Isaiah 32:18; 1 Samuel 23:20–23"> Lie not in wait, O wicked man! against the dwelling of the righteous; waste not his resting-place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 34:19; Psalms 37:24; Micah 7:8–10"> For though the righteous were to fall seven times, he will rise up again; but the wicked shall stumble into misfortune.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Obadiah 1:12; Job 31:29; Proverbs 17:5"> At the fall of thy enemy do not rejoice; and at his stumbling let not thy heart be glad:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 1:15–16; Lamentations 4:21–22"> Lest the Lord see it and it be displeasing in his eyes, and he turn away from him his wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 119:115; Psalms 37:1; Proverbs 23:17"> Fret not thyself because of evil-doers, neither be thou envious of the wicked;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 13:9; Job 18:5–6; Psalms 11:6"> For there will be no &#91;happy&#93; future for the bad man: the lamp of the wicked will be quenched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 13:1–7; 1 Peter 2:13–17; Titus 3:1"> My son, fear the Lord and the king: with those that are desirous to change do not mingle thyself;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 90:11; Hosea 5:11; Numbers 16:31–35"> For suddenly will their calamity arise; and who knoweth the ruin of both of them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 28:21; Proverbs 18:5; John 7:24"> These things also are for the wise. To have respect of persons in judgment is not good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 17:15; Isaiah 5:23; Proverbs 11:26"> Him that saith unto the wicked, Thou art righteous, will the people denounce, him will nations hold accursed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:20; 1 Samuel 3:13; Proverbs 28:23"> But to those that punish delight shall be given, and upon them shall come the blessing of the good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 15:23; Genesis 41:38–57; Proverbs 25:11–12"> Men will kiss the lips of him that giveth a proper answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 14:28–30; 1 Kings 5:17–18; 1 Kings 6:7"> Prepare without thy work, and make it fit in the field for thyself: and afterward build thy house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ephesians 4:25; Matthew 26:59–60; Proverbs 25:18"> Be not without cause a witness against thy neighbor; for wouldst thou beguile with thy lips?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 20:22; Romans 12:17–21; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> Say not, As he hath done to me so will I do to him: I will recompense every man according to his doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Proverbs 6:6–19; Job 4:8"> By the field of a slothful man I once passed along, and by the vineyard of a man void of sense:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:18; Isaiah 5:5; Jeremiah 4:3"> And, lo, it was all grown over with thorns, nettles had covered its surface, and its stone-wall was broken down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:11; Deuteronomy 13:11; 1 Corinthians 10:6"> And when I had indeed beheld &#91;this&#93; I took it to my heart: I saw it, and received a warning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:6–8; Proverbs 6:4–11; Romans 13:11"> “A little &#91;more&#93; sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands in lying down;”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 13:4"> But then will thy poverty come like a rover; and thy wants as a man armed with a shield.</span>
