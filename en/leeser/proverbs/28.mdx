<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:7; Psalms 53:5; Isaiah 26:3–4"> Every wicked fleeth when no man pursueth; but the righteous are like the confident young lion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 15:28; 1 Kings 16:8–29; 2 Kings 15:8–31"> When there is transgression in a land, it hath many for its princes; but under a man of understanding and knowledge &#91;its&#93; prosperity will long continue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 18:28–30"> A poor man that oppresseth the indigent is like a sweeping rain which bringeth no bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:32; Ephesians 5:11; Matthew 14:4"> They that forsake the law praise the wicked; but such as observe the law contend with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 92:6; James 1:5; 1 John 2:27"> Bad men understand not justice; but they that seek the Lord understand all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 19:1; Proverbs 28:18; Luke 16:19–23"> Better is the poor that walketh in his integrity, than he that is perverse in his ways, though he be rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:3–4; Proverbs 29:3; Proverbs 3:1–35"> Whoso keepeth the law is an intelligent son; but he that is a companion of gluttons bringeth dishonor on his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 27:16–17; Proverbs 13:22; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> He that increaseth his wealth by interest and usury will gather it for him that will be kind to the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 66:18; 2 Timothy 4:3–4; Zechariah 7:11–13"> When one turneth away his ear so as not to listen to the law, even his prayer becometh an abomination.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 26:27; Psalms 37:25–26; Galatians 2:4"> Whoso causeth the upright to go astray on an evil way, will surely fall into his own ditch; but the men of integrity will inherit what is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 18:17; Ecclesiastes 9:15–17; Proverbs 26:16"> The rich man is wise in his own eyes; but the indigent that hath understanding can search him through.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 29:2; Proverbs 11:10; Proverbs 28:28"> When the righteous exult, there is great splendor; but when the wicked rise up, a man hath to be sought for.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 John 1:8–10; Psalms 32:3–5; Job 31:33"> He that concealeth his transgressions will not prosper; but whoso confesseth and forsaketh them will obtain mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 23:17; Psalms 2:11; Isaiah 66:2"> Happy is the man that always dreadeth &#91;to do evil&#93;; but he that hardeneth his heart will fall into unhappiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Matthew 2:16; Proverbs 19:12"> As a roaring lion, and a greedy bear, so is a wicked ruler over an indigent people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; Jeremiah 22:15–17; Ecclesiastes 4:1"> The prince that is void of understanding is also a great oppressor; &#91;but&#93; he that hateth unjust gain will prolong his days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 9:6; 1 Kings 21:19; Exodus 21:14"> A man oppressed by the load of having shed human blood will flee even to the pit: let no man hold him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 10:9; Proverbs 28:6; Revelation 3:3"> Whoso walketh in integrity will be saved; but he that walketh perversely on two paths, will fall on one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Judges 9:4; Proverbs 27:23–27"> He that tilleth his ground will have plenty of bread; but he that runneth after idle persons will have enough of poverty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 16:10–12; Proverbs 13:11; Revelation 2:10"> A faithful man will abound with blessings; but he that maketh haste to be rich will not go unpunished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 24:23; Proverbs 18:5; Ezekiel 13:19"> To have respect to persons is not good; because even for a piece of bread will a man transgress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 23:6; 1 Timothy 6:9; Proverbs 28:20"> He that is eager for wealth is a man of an evil eye, and he knoweth not that want will come upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 27:5–6; Matthew 18:15; Psalms 141:5"> He that reproveth a man after &#91;my example&#93; will obtain more grace than he that flattereth with the tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 15:4–6; Proverbs 18:9; Proverbs 19:26"> Whoso robbeth his father or his mother, and saith, it is no transgression,—the same is a companion of a destroyer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 11:25; 1 Timothy 6:6; Proverbs 13:10"> He that hath an insatiable desire stirreth up strife: but he that putteth his trust in the Lord will be abundantly gratified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5; Jeremiah 17:9; Job 28:28"> He that trusteth in his own sense is a fool; but whoso walketh in wisdom, will ever escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Deuteronomy 15:7; Psalms 41:1–3"> He that giveth unto the poor will not have any want; but he that hideth his eyes will have an abundance of curses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 29:2; Job 24:4; Acts 12:23–24"> When the wicked rise, men conceal themselves; but when they perish, the righteous increase.</span>
