<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 6:20; Proverbs 1:8; 2 Timothy 1:5"> The words of king Lemuel, the prophecy with which his mother instructed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 49:15; 1 Samuel 1:11; 1 Samuel 1:28"> What &#91;hast thou done&#93;, O my son: and what, O son of my body? and what, O son of my vows?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:17; Nehemiah 13:26; Proverbs 7:26–27"> Give not unto women thy vigor, nor thy ways to those that ruin kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Ecclesiastes 10:17; Isaiah 5:22"> Not for kings, O Lemoel, not for kings &#91;it is fitting&#93; to drink wine, nor for princes, strong drink:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:5"> Lest either might drink, and forget what is written in the law, and pervert the cause of all the afflicted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:23; Psalms 104:15; 1 Samuel 1:10"> Give strong drink unto him that is ready to perish, and wine unto those who have an embittered soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 5:18"> Let such a one drink, and forget his poverty, and remember his trouble no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 82:3–4; Proverbs 24:11–12; Esther 4:13–16"> Open thy mouth for the dumb, for the cause of all fatherless children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 1:17; Leviticus 19:15; Amos 5:11–12"> Open thy mouth, judge righteously, and decide the cause of the poor and needy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 19:14; Proverbs 12:4; Proverbs 18:22"> Who can find a virtuous woman: for far above pearls is her value.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 3:1–7; 2 Kings 4:22–23; 2 Kings 4:9–10"> The heart of her husband doth safely trust in her, and he will not see his gain diminish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:18–22; 1 Samuel 25:26–27"> She treateth him well and not ill, all the days of her life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Titus 2:5; 1 Timothy 5:10; 2 Thessalonians 3:10–12"> She seeketh for wool and flax, and worketh with her willing hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 31:24; 1 Kings 9:26–28; Ezekiel 27:3–36"> She is become like the merchant's ships: from afar doth she bring her food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 12:11; Mark 1:35; Luke 12:42"> And she riseth while it is yet night, and giveth provision to her household, and a task to her maidens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 13:44; Song of Solomon 8:12; Joshua 15:18"> She thinketh of a field, and buyeth it: with the fruit of her hands she planteth a vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 38:3; 1 Peter 1:13; Ephesians 6:10"> She girdeth with strength her loins, and giveth vigor to her arms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:9; Matthew 25:3–10; Psalms 127:2"> She perceiveth that her profit is good: &#91;therefore&#93; her lamp goeth not out by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 35:25–26"> She stretcheth out her hands to the spindle, and her palms hold fast the distaff.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 4:28; Hebrews 13:16; Proverbs 22:9"> She spreadeth out wide her open palm to the poor: yea, her hands she stretcheth forth to the needy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 45:22; 2 Samuel 1:24; Proverbs 25:20"> She hath no fear for her household of the snow: for all her household are clothed in scarlet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 19:8; Proverbs 7:16; Genesis 41:42"> Tapestry-covering she maketh for herself: of linen and purple is her attire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 12:4; Ruth 4:1; Deuteronomy 16:18"> Well known is in the gates her husband, when he sitteth with the elders of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:16; Luke 16:19; Proverbs 31:19"> Fine tunics she maketh, and selleth them, and girdles she furnisheth unto the merchant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 40:10; Romans 13:14; Job 29:14"> Strength and dignity are her clothing: and she smileth at the coming of the last day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Proverbs 16:24; Proverbs 10:31"> She openeth her mouth with wisdom, and the law of kindness is on her tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:10; Titus 2:4; 2 Thessalonians 3:6"> She looketh well to the ways of her household, and the bread of idleness she doth not eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:15–17; 2 Timothy 1:5; 1 Kings 2:19"> Her children rise up, and call her blessed; her husband, also, and he praiseth her:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Song of Solomon 6:8–9; Ephesians 5:27"> “Many daughters have done virtuously; but thou excellest them all.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 1:7; 1 Peter 3:4–5; Proverbs 11:22"> False is grace, and vain is beauty: a woman only that feareth the Lord shall indeed be praised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 128:2; Hebrews 6:10; Matthew 7:16"> Give her of the fruit of her hands, and let her own works praise her in the gates.</span>
