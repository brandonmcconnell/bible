<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 1:5; Ephesians 1:17–18; Proverbs 3:6"> For if thou wilt call after intelligence; if after understanding thou wilt lift up thy voice;</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 1:7; Jeremiah 24:7; Jeremiah 32:40–41"> Then wilt thou understand the fear of the Lord, and the knowledge of God wilt thou find.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 1:17–18; James 1:5; Job 32:8"> For the Lord giveth wisdom: out of his mouth &#91;come&#93; knowledge and understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 3:15–17; Proverbs 30:5; Psalms 84:11"> He treasureth up sound wisdom for the righteous, as a shield to those that walk in integrity:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 23:3; 1 Samuel 2:9; John 10:28–29"> That men may keep the paths of justice; and the way of his pious servants doth he guard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:16; Psalms 143:8–10; Matthew 7:13–14"> Then wilt thou understand righteousness, and justice, and equity: yea, every track of goodness.</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; Proverbs 8:13; Proverbs 13:20"> To deliver thee from the way of the bad, from the man that speaketh perverse things;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 3:19–20; Proverbs 4:19; Proverbs 21:16"> &#91;From those&#93; who leave the paths of uprightness, to walk in the ways of darkness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 1:32; Proverbs 10:23; Jeremiah 11:15"> Who rejoice to do evil, who are delighted in the perverseness of the bad;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 125:5; Proverbs 21:8; Philippians 2:15"> Who as regardeth their paths are crooked, and froward in their tracks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Proverbs 22:14; Proverbs 23:27"> To deliver thee from the adulteress, from the alien woman that useth flattering speeches;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:59–60; Ezekiel 16:8; Jeremiah 3:4"> That forsaketh the friend of her youth, and forgetteth the covenant of her God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 9:18; 1 Corinthians 6:9–11; Proverbs 5:4–14"> For she sinketh unto death—her house, and unto the departed &#91;lead&#93; her tracks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Psalms 81:12; Hosea 4:14"> All that come unto her return not again, and they will not reach the paths of life.</span>
