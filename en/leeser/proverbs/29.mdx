<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 6:15; 1 Samuel 2:25; Proverbs 1:24–31"> A man that, having received many admonitions, still hardeneth his neck, will suddenly be broken, and this without remedy.</span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 27:11; Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 15:20"> The man that loveth wisdom causeth his father to rejoice; but he that keepeth company with harlots wasteth &#91;his&#93; wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 29:14; Proverbs 20:8; Daniel 11:20"> A king will through the exercise of justice establish &#91;the welfare of&#93; a land; but one that loveth gifts overthroweth it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 5:1; Job 17:5; Psalms 5:9"> A man that flattereth his neighbor spreadeth a net for his steps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:12; Exodus 15:1; 2 Timothy 2:26"> In the transgression of a man there is an evil snare: but the righteous ever singeth and rejoiceth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 29:16; Psalms 41:1; Proverbs 21:13"> The righteous considereth the cause of the indigent: but the wicked will not understand the knowledge &#91;of justice&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 11:11; Jeremiah 15:1; James 5:15–18"> Scornful men will kindle &#91;confusion&#93; in a town; but the wise turn away wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 26:4; Ecclesiastes 10:13; Matthew 7:6"> If a wise man contend with a foolish man, whether he be angry or whether he laugh, &#91;he will have&#93; no rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 15:18–19; 1 John 3:12–13; Genesis 4:5–8"> Men of blood hate the guiltless one; but the upright seek &#91;to preserve&#93; his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 19:11; Proverbs 12:16; Micah 7:5"> A fool uttereth all his mind; but the wise holdeth it back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 20:8; 2 Kings 10:6–7; Psalms 52:2–4"> If a ruler listen to the word of falsehood, all his servants become wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 5:45; Proverbs 22:2; Psalms 13:3"> The poor and the man of exactions meet together: the Lord enlighteneth the eyes of both of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 16:12; Isaiah 11:4; Psalms 72:2–4"> When a king judgeth in truth the indigent, his throne shall stand firmly for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 17:25; Proverbs 29:17"> The rod and reproof impart wisdom; but a lad abandoned to himself bringeth shame on his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 37:36; Psalms 91:8; Psalms 58:10"> With the increase of the wicked transgression increaseth; but the righteous shall yet look on their downfall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 29:15; Proverbs 23:13–14"> Correct thy son, and he will procure thee rest: yea, he will give delight unto thy soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 1:25; Luke 11:28; John 13:17"> Without a prophetic vision a people become unruly; but when it observeth the law, then will it be happy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 26:3; Job 19:16; Proverbs 30:22"> Not with words &#91;alone&#93; can a servant be corrected; for though he understand, there will be no response.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 1:19; Proverbs 26:12; Proverbs 14:29"> Seest then a man that is hasty in his words? there is more hope for a fool than for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> If one rear his servant delicately from his youth, then will he at length become as &#91;his&#93; son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 15:18; Proverbs 17:19; Proverbs 26:21"> A man of anger stirreth up strife; and a man of fury aboundeth in transgression.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 23:12; Luke 14:11; Proverbs 11:2"> The pride of a man will humble him; but the humble in spirit will attain to honor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 5:1; Proverbs 1:11–19; Proverbs 8:36"> Whoso divideth with a thief hateth his own soul: he heareth the adjuration and dareth not to tell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 118:8; Luke 12:4; Proverbs 18:10"> The dread of man bringeth a snare; but whoso putteth his trust in the Lord will be upheld in safety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 19:6; Proverbs 19:21; Psalms 20:9"> Many seek the favor of a ruler; but from the Lord cometh justice for man.</span>
