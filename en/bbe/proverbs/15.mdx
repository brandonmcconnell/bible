<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 10:12; Proverbs 25:15; Proverbs 15:18"> By a soft answer wrath is turned away, but a bitter word is a cause of angry feelings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 12:23; Proverbs 15:28; Proverbs 13:16"> Knowledge is dropping from the tongue of the wise; but from the mouth of the foolish comes a stream of foolish words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Jeremiah 16:17; Jeremiah 23:24"> The eyes of the Lord are in every place, keeping watch on the evil and the good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 12:18; Proverbs 16:24; Proverbs 18:8"> A comforting tongue is a tree of life, but a twisted tongue is a crushing of the spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 15:31–32; Proverbs 13:1; Proverbs 13:18"> A foolish man puts no value on his father's training; but he who has respect for teaching has good sense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 8:21; Proverbs 21:20; Proverbs 10:22"> In the house of the upright man there is a great store of wealth; but in the profits of the sinner there is trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 12:34; James 3:6; Ephesians 4:29"> The lips of the wise keep knowledge, but the heart of the foolish man is not right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 15:29; Proverbs 21:27; 1 Chronicles 29:17"> The offering of the evil-doer is disgusting to the Lord, but the prayer of the upright man is his delight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:11; Proverbs 21:21; Matthew 7:13"> The way of the evil-doer is disgusting to the Lord, but he who goes after righteousness is dear to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 12:1; John 3:20; Proverbs 13:1"> There is bitter punishment for him who is turned from the way; and death will be the fate of the hater of teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 44:21; Hebrews 4:13; Psalms 139:8"> Before the Lord are the underworld and destruction: how much more, then, the hearts of the children of men!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 5:10; Job 21:14; Proverbs 9:7–8"> The hater of authority has no love for teaching: he will not go to the wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 17:22; John 14:1; Proverbs 18:14"> A glad heart makes a shining face, but by the sorrow of the heart the spirit is broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 9:9; Isaiah 30:10; Proverbs 18:15"> The heart of the man of good sense goes in search of knowledge, but foolish things are the food of the unwise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:10; Romans 12:12; Proverbs 15:13"> All the days of the troubled are evil; but he whose heart is glad has an unending feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 16:8; Psalms 37:16; Ecclesiastes 5:10–12"> Better is a little with the fear of the Lord, than great wealth together with trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 17:1; Proverbs 21:19; 1 John 4:16"> Better is a simple meal where love is, than a fat ox and hate with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; Proverbs 26:21; Proverbs 29:22"> An angry man makes men come to blows, but he who is slow to get angry puts an end to fighting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 35:8; Proverbs 22:5; Isaiah 30:21"> Thorns are round the way of the hater of work; but the road of the hard worker becomes a highway.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 29:3; Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 23:15–16"> A wise son makes a glad father, but a foolish man has no respect for his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 5:15; Proverbs 10:23; Proverbs 14:16"> Foolish behaviour is joy to the unwise; but a man of good sense makes his way straight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 11:14; Proverbs 20:18; Ecclesiastes 8:6"> Where there are no wise suggestions, purposes come to nothing; but by a number of wise guides they are made certain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 50:4; Proverbs 25:11–12; Ephesians 4:29"> A man has joy in the answer of his mouth: and a word at the right time, how good it is!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Colossians 3:1–2; Psalms 16:11; Matthew 7:14"> Acting wisely is the way of life, guiding a man away from the underworld.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 146:9; Psalms 68:5–6; Proverbs 14:11"> The house of the man of pride will be uprooted by the Lord, but he will make safe the heritage of the widow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 6:16–19; Jeremiah 4:14; Proverbs 15:23"> Evil designs are disgusting to the Lord, but the words of the clean-hearted are pleasing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 23:8; Proverbs 1:19; Jeremiah 17:11"> He whose desires are fixed on profit is a cause of trouble to his family; but he who has no desire for offerings will have life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Peter 3:15; Proverbs 29:11; Ecclesiastes 5:2"> The heart of the upright gives thought to his answer; but from the mouth of the evil-doer comes a stream of evil things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 9:31; Psalms 145:18–19; Proverbs 15:8"> The Lord is far from sinners, but his ear is open to the prayer of the upright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 58:11; Revelation 22:5; Revelation 21:23"> The light of the eyes is a joy to the heart, and good news makes the bones fat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 15:5; Proverbs 25:12; John 15:3–4"> The man whose ear is open to the teaching of life will have his place among the wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24–33; Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 1:7"> He who will not be controlled by training has no respect for his soul, but he who gives ear to teaching will get wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; Proverbs 18:12; Proverbs 1:7"> The fear of the Lord is the teaching of wisdom; and a low opinion of oneself goes before honour.</span>
