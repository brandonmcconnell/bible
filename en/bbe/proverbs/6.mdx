<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 22:26; Proverbs 17:18; Proverbs 20:16"> My son, if you have made yourself responsible for your neighbour, or given your word for another,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 18:7; Proverbs 12:13"> You are taken as in a net by the words of your mouth, the sayings of your lips have overcome you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 4:10; Psalms 31:8; 2 Samuel 24:14"> Do this, my son, and make yourself free, because you have come into the power of your neighbour; go without waiting, and make a strong request to your neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 132:4; Proverbs 6:10–11; Mark 13:35–36"> Give no sleep to your eyes, or rest to them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 91:3; Psalms 124:7; Proverbs 1:17"> Make yourself free, like the roe from the hand of the archer, and the bird from him who puts a net for her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 20:4; Proverbs 18:9; Proverbs 6:9"> Go to the ant, you hater of work; give thought to her ways and be wise:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 38:39–12; Proverbs 30:27; Job 39:26–30"> Having no chief, overseer, or ruler,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 10:5; 1 Timothy 6:19; Proverbs 30:25"> She gets her meat in the summer, storing up food at the time of the grain-cutting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 24:33–34; Jeremiah 4:14; Ephesians 5:14"> How long will you be sleeping, O hater of work? when will you get up from your sleep?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 24:33–34; Proverbs 6:6; Proverbs 23:33–34"> A little sleep, a little rest, a little folding of the hands in sleep:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 24:34"> Then loss will come on you like an outlaw, and your need like an armed man</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:34; 1 Timothy 5:13; Proverbs 16:27"> A good-for-nothing man is an evil-doer; he goes on his way causing trouble with false words;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; Proverbs 10:10; Job 15:12"> Making signs with his eyes, rubbing with his feet, and giving news with his fingers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 16:17; Micah 2:1; Hosea 8:7"> His mind is ever designing evil: he lets loose violent acts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:16; Jeremiah 19:11; 1 Thessalonians 5:3"> For this cause his downfall will be sudden; quickly he will be broken, and there will be no help for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 11:5; Proverbs 8:13; Proverbs 11:20"> Six things are hated by the Lord; seven things are disgusting to him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 120:2–3; Proverbs 12:22; Psalms 101:5"> Eyes of pride, a false tongue, hands which take life without cause;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 6:5; Proverbs 1:16; Romans 3:15"> A heart full of evil designs, feet which are quick in running after sin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 19:5; Proverbs 6:14; James 3:18"> A false witness, breathing out untrue words, and one who lets loose violent acts among brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1; Deuteronomy 27:16; Proverbs 7:1–4"> My son, keep the rule of your father, and have in memory the teaching of your mother:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 3:3; Proverbs 7:3–4; 2 Corinthians 3:3"> Keep them ever folded in your heart, and have them hanging round your neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; Proverbs 3:23–24; Psalms 119:24"> In your walking, it will be your guide; when you are sleeping, it will keep watch over you; when you are awake, it will have talk with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 119:105; 2 Peter 1:19; Psalms 19:8"> For the rule is a light, and the teaching a shining light; and the guiding words of training are the way of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 2:16; Proverbs 7:5; Ecclesiastes 7:26"> They will keep you from the evil woman, from the smooth tongue of the strange woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 1:14–15; Matthew 5:28; 2 Kings 9:30"> Let not your heart's desire go after her fair body; let not her eyes take you prisoner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 15:30; Proverbs 29:3; 1 Samuel 2:36"> For a loose woman is looking for a cake of bread, but another man's wife goes after one's very life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 31:9–12; Hosea 7:4–7; James 3:5"> May a man take fire to his breast without burning his clothing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Or may one go on lighted coals, and his feet not be burned?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:1; Malachi 3:5; 2 Samuel 12:9–10"> So it is with him who goes in to his neighbour's wife; he who has anything to do with her will not go free from punishment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 38:39"> Men do not have a low opinion of a thief who takes food when he is in need of it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 19:8; Job 20:18; 2 Samuel 12:6"> But if he is taken in the act he will have to give back seven times as much, giving up all his property which is in his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 7:7; Hebrews 13:4; Romans 1:22–24"> He who takes another man's wife is without all sense: he who does it is the cause of destruction to his soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 5:9–11; Psalms 51:8; Judges 16:19–21"> Wounds will be his and loss of honour, and his shame may not be washed away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 27:4; Numbers 5:14; Song of Solomon 8:6"> For bitter is the wrath of an angry husband; in the day of punishment he will have no mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 8:25; Malachi 2:9; Proverbs 7:13"> He will not take any payment; and he will not make peace with you though your money offerings are increased.</span>
