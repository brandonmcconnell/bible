<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 4:1; Proverbs 7:24; Proverbs 22:17–21"> Give ear to me then, my sons, and do not put away my words from you.</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 12:1; Proverbs 1:7; Proverbs 15:5"> And you will say, How was teaching hated by me, and my heart put no value on training;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:8; Luke 15:18; Hebrews 13:7"> I did not give attention to the voice of my teachers, my ear was not turned to those who were guiding me!</span>
