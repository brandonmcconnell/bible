<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 15:5; Proverbs 9:7–8; Proverbs 15:20"> A wise son is a lover of teaching, but the ears of the haters of authority are shut to sharp words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 12:14; Proverbs 18:20; Proverbs 10:11"> A man will get good from the fruit of his lips, but the desire of the false is for violent acts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 21:23; Proverbs 18:21; Proverbs 18:7"> He who keeps a watch on his mouth keeps his life; but he whose lips are open wide will have destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 12:24; Proverbs 12:11; 2 Peter 1:5–11"> The hater of work does not get his desires, but the soul of the hard workers will be made fat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 3:9; Ephesians 4:25; Psalms 119:163"> The upright man is a hater of false words: the evil-doer gets a bad name and is put to shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 11:3; Proverbs 11:5–6; Psalms 140:11"> Righteousness keeps safe him whose way is without error, but evil-doers are overturned by sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 18:11–14; Revelation 3:17; Proverbs 11:24"> A man may be acting as if he had wealth, but have nothing; another may seem poor, but have great wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; 1 Peter 1:18–19; Psalms 49:6–10"> A man will give his wealth in exchange for his life; but the poor will not give ear to sharp words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 4:18; Proverbs 24:20; Proverbs 20:20"> There is a glad dawn for the upright man, but the light of the sinner will be put out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 19:20; Proverbs 12:15–16; Proverbs 17:14"> The only effect of pride is fighting; but wisdom is with the quiet in spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 20:21; Proverbs 10:2; James 5:1–5"> Wealth quickly got will become less; but he who gets a store by the work of his hands will have it increased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 16:22; Proverbs 13:19; Psalms 119:81–83"> Hope put off is a weariness to the heart; but when what is desired comes, it is a tree of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 15:31; 2 Chronicles 36:16; 2 John 1:8"> He who makes sport of the word will come to destruction, but the respecter of the law will be rewarded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 18:5; Proverbs 10:11; Proverbs 14:27"> The teaching of the wise is a fountain of life, turning men away from the nets of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:19; Proverbs 4:19; Acts 7:10"> Wise behaviour gets approval, but the way of the false is their destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 15:2; Proverbs 12:22–23; Ephesians 5:17"> A sharp man does everything with knowledge, but a foolish man makes clear his foolish thoughts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 25:13; 1 Corinthians 4:2; 2 Corinthians 5:20"> A man taking false news is a cause of trouble, but he who gives news rightly makes things well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 15:5; Proverbs 12:1; Proverbs 15:31–32"> Need and shame will be the fate of him who is uncontrolled by training; but he who takes note of teaching will be honoured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 7:14–17; Proverbs 13:12; Proverbs 29:27"> To get one's desire is sweet to the soul, but to give up evil is disgusting to the foolish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33–34; Proverbs 15:31; Proverbs 9:6"> Go with wise men and be wise: but he who keeps company with the foolish will be broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 32:10; Isaiah 3:10–11; Romans 2:7–10"> Evil will overtake sinners, but the upright will be rewarded with good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:26; Job 27:16–17; Proverbs 28:8"> The heritage of the good man is handed down to his children's children; and the wealth of the sinner is stored up for the upright man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Proverbs 28:19; Ecclesiastes 5:9"> There is much food in the ploughed land of the poor; but it is taken away by wrongdoing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 29:17; Proverbs 23:13–14; Proverbs 29:15"> He who keeps back his rod is unkind to his son: the loving father gives punishment with care.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 34:10; Proverbs 10:3; Hebrews 13:5"> The upright man has food to the full measure of his desire, but there will be no food for the stomach of evil-doers.</span>
