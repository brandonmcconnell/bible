<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 1:1; 1 Kings 4:32; Micah 1:1"> These are more wise sayings of Solomon, copied out by the men of Hezekiah, king of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:29; Romans 11:33–34; Job 11:7–8"> It is the glory of God to keep a thing secret: but the glory of kings is to have it searched out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 55:9; Psalms 103:11; 1 Kings 4:29"> The heaven is high and the earth is deep, and the hearts of kings may not be searched out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:20–21; Ezekiel 22:18; Proverbs 17:3"> Take away the waste from silver, and a vessel will come out for the silver-worker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 16:12; Proverbs 20:8; Psalms 101:7–8"> Take away evil-doers from before the king, and the seat of his power will be made strong in righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 27:2; Proverbs 25:27; Proverbs 16:19"> Do not take glory for yourself before the king, and do not put yourself in the place of the great:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 14:7–11; Luke 18:14; 1 Peter 5:5"> For it is better to have it said to you, Come up here; than for you to be put down in a lower place before the ruler.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 5:25; Proverbs 17:14; Proverbs 18:6"> Do not be quick to go to law about what you have seen, for what will you do in the end, when your neighbour has put you to shame?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 18:5–17; Proverbs 11:13; Proverbs 20:19"> Have a talk with your neighbour himself about your cause, but do not give away the secret of another:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 119:39"> Or your hearer may say evil of you, and your shame will not be turned away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 15:23; Isaiah 50:4; Ecclesiastes 12:10"> A word at the right time is like apples of gold in a network of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 141:5; Proverbs 15:31–32; Proverbs 15:5"> Like a nose-ring of gold and an ornament of the best gold, is a wise man who says sharp words to an ear ready to give attention.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 13:17; Proverbs 25:25; Philippians 2:25–30"> As the cold of snow in the time of grain-cutting, so is a true servant to those who send him; for he gives new life to the soul of his master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 20:6; Luke 14:11; 2 Peter 2:15–19"> As clouds and wind without rain, so is one who takes credit for an offering he has not given.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; Ecclesiastes 10:4; Proverbs 16:14"> A judge is moved by one who for a long time undergoes wrongs without protest, and by a soft tongue even bone is broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 25:27; Ephesians 5:18; Proverbs 24:13–14"> If you have honey, take only as much as is enough for you; for fear that, being full of it, you may not be able to keep it down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 19:2–3; Judges 19:18–21; Romans 15:24"> Let not your foot be frequently in your neighbour's house, or he may get tired of you, and his feeling be turned to hate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Proverbs 12:18; James 3:6"> One who gives false witness against his neighbour is a hammer and a sword and a sharp arrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 36:6; Ezekiel 29:6–7; Job 6:14–20"> Putting one's faith in a false man in time of trouble is like a broken tooth and a shaking foot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Ecclesiastes 3:4; Isaiah 58:7"> Like one who takes off clothing in cold weather and like acid on a wound, is he who makes melody to a sad heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 12:20–21; Matthew 5:44; Exodus 23:4–5"> If your hater is in need of food, give him bread; and if he is in need of drink, give him water:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:12; Matthew 10:13; 1 Corinthians 15:18"> For so you will put coals of fire on his head, and the Lord will give you your reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 37:22; Psalms 15:3; Romans 1:30"> As the north wind gives birth to rain, so is an angry face caused by a tongue saying evil secretly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 21:19; Proverbs 21:9; Proverbs 27:15–16"> It is better to be living in an angle of the house-top, than with a bitter-tongued woman in a wide house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 15:30; Romans 10:15; Psalms 63:1"> As cold water to a tired soul, so is good news from a far country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:18–19; 1 Samuel 22:14–18; 1 Thessalonians 2:15"> Like a troubled fountain and a dirty spring, is an upright man who has to give way before evil-doers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 5:44; Proverbs 27:2; Philippians 2:3"> It is not good to take much honey: so he who is not looking for honour will be honoured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 16:32; Proverbs 22:24; 2 Chronicles 32:5"> He whose spirit is uncontrolled is like an unwalled town which has been broken into.</span>
