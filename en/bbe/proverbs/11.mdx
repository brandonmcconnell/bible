<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:13–16; Proverbs 16:11; Proverbs 20:23"> Scales of deceit are hated by the Lord, but a true weight is his delight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 29:23; Proverbs 15:33; Proverbs 16:18–19"> When pride comes, there comes shame, but wisdom is with the quiet in spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 13:6; Proverbs 28:18; Psalms 26:1"> The righteousness of the upright will be their guide, but the twisted ways of the false will be their destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:18; Proverbs 10:2; Genesis 7:1"> Wealth is of no profit in the day of wrath, but righteousness keeps a man safe from death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 5:22; Psalms 9:15–16; Proverbs 1:31–32"> The righteousness of the good man will make his way straight, but the sin of the evil-doer will be the cause of his fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:8; 1 Kings 2:44; Psalms 7:15–16"> The righteousness of the upright will be their salvation, but the false will themselves be taken in their evil designs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 10:28; Job 8:13–14; Job 11:20"> At the death of an upright man his hope does not come to an end, but the hope of the evil-doer comes to destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 6:23–24; Proverbs 21:18; Esther 7:9–10"> The upright man is taken out of trouble, and in his place comes the sinner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 2:10–16; Proverbs 4:5–6; 1 John 2:27"> With his mouth the evil man sends destruction on his neighbour; but through knowledge the upright are taken out of trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 28:12; Esther 8:15–16; Psalms 58:10–11"> When things go well for the upright man, all the town is glad; at the death of sinners, there are cries of joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 14:34; Proverbs 29:8; James 3:6"> By the blessing of the upright man the town is made great, but it is overturned by the mouth of the evil-doer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 10:19; 1 Peter 2:23; Luke 18:9"> He who has a poor opinion of his neighbour has no sense, but a wise man keeps quiet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 20:19; Proverbs 25:9; Leviticus 19:16"> He who goes about talking of others makes secrets public, but the true-hearted man keeps things covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 24:6; Proverbs 15:22; Proverbs 20:18"> When there is no helping suggestion the people will have a fall, but with a number of wise guides they will be safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 17:18; Proverbs 6:1–5; Proverbs 22:26–27"> He who makes himself responsible for a strange man will undergo much loss; but the hater of such undertakings will be safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 31:30–31; Luke 10:42; Acts 16:14–15"> A woman who is full of grace is honoured, but a woman hating righteousness is a seat of shame: those hating work will undergo loss, but the strong keep their wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; Matthew 25:34–40; James 2:13"> The man who has mercy will be rewarded, but the cruel man is the cause of trouble to himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 6:8–9; Ecclesiastes 10:8; Hosea 10:12–13"> The sinner gets the payment of deceit; but his reward is certain who puts in the seed of righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 10:16; Romans 2:8–9; Proverbs 12:28"> So righteousness gives life; but he who goes after evil gets death for himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 119:1; Proverbs 21:29; 1 Chronicles 29:17"> The uncontrolled are hated by the Lord, but those whose ways are without error are his delight</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 112:1–2; Acts 2:39; Jeremiah 32:39"> Certainly the evil-doer will not go free from punishment, but the seed of the upright man will be safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 3:3–4; Proverbs 31:30; Nahum 3:4–6"> Like a ring of gold in the nose of a pig, is a beautiful woman who has no sense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; Romans 2:8–9; Matthew 5:6"> The desire of the upright man is only for good, but wrath is waiting for the evil-doer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Luke 6:38; Deuteronomy 15:10"> A man may give freely, and still his wealth will be increased; and another may keep back more than is right, but only comes to be in need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; 2 Corinthians 9:6–9; Isaiah 58:7–11"> He who gives blessing will be made fat, but the curser will himself be cursed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 29:13; Genesis 42:6; Amos 8:4–6"> He who keeps back grain will be cursed by the people; but a blessing will be on the head of him who lets them have it for a price.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 7:15–16; Proverbs 17:11; Psalms 9:15–16"> He who, with all his heart, goes after what is good is searching for grace; but he who is looking for trouble will get it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:17; Jeremiah 17:8; Psalms 52:7–8"> He who puts his faith in wealth will come to nothing; but the upright man will be full of growth like the green leaf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:16; Proverbs 14:19; Proverbs 15:27"> The troubler of his house will have the wind for his heritage, and the foolish will be servant to the wise-hearted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Daniel 12:3; James 5:20; 1 Corinthians 9:19–23"> The fruit of righteousness is a tree of life, but violent behaviour takes away souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17–18; Jeremiah 25:29; 1 Corinthians 11:30–32"> If the upright man is rewarded on earth, how much more the evil-doer and the sinner!</span>
