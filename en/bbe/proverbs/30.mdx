<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 31:1; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> The words of Agur, the son of Jakeh, from Massa. The man says: I am full of weariness, O God, I am full of weariness; O God, I have come to an end:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 73:22; 1 Corinthians 3:18; Isaiah 6:5"> For I am more like a beast than any man, I have no power of reasoning like a man:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 3:7; Ephesians 3:18–19; Romans 11:33"> I have not got wisdom by teaching, so that I might have the knowledge of the Holy One.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 3:13; Ephesians 4:8–10; Job 26:8"> Who has gone up to heaven and come down? who has taken the winds in his hands, prisoning the waters in his robe? by whom have all the ends of the earth been fixed? what is his name, and what is his son's name, if you are able to say?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 18:30; James 3:17; Psalms 12:6"> Every word of God is tested: he is a breastplate to those who put their faith in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; Deuteronomy 4:2; Revelation 22:18–19"> Make no addition to his words, or he will make clear your error, and you will be seen to be false.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 10:42; 2 Kings 2:9; Psalms 27:4"> I have made request to you for two things; do not keep them from me before my death:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:6–8; Psalms 62:9–10; Psalms 119:37"> Put far from me all false and foolish things: do not give me great wealth or let me be in need, but give me only enough food:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 13:6; Deuteronomy 31:20; Ezekiel 16:49–50"> For fear that if I am full, I may be false to you and say, Who is the Lord? or if I am poor, I may become a thief, using the name of my God wrongly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 14:4; Ecclesiastes 7:21; Deuteronomy 23:15"> Do not say evil of a servant to his master, or he will put a curse on you, and you will get into trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 20:20; Proverbs 30:17; Exodus 21:17"> There is a generation who put a curse on their father, and do not give a blessing to their mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 18:11; Proverbs 16:2; 2 Timothy 3:5"> There is a generation who seem to themselves to be free from sin, but are not washed from their unclean ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 6:17; Isaiah 2:11; Psalms 101:5"> There is a generation, O how full of pride are their eyes! O how their brows are lifted up!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Psalms 3:7; Psalms 14:4"> There is a generation whose teeth are like swords, their strong teeth like knives, for the destruction of the poor from the earth, and of those who are in need from among men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 30:21; Proverbs 30:29; Proverbs 6:16"> The night-spirit has two daughters, Give, give. There are three things which are never full, even four which never say, Enough:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 27:20; Habakkuk 2:5; Genesis 30:1"> The underworld, and the woman without a child; the earth which never has enough water, and the fire which never says, Enough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 23:22; Deuteronomy 21:18–21; Proverbs 30:11"> The eye which makes sport of a father, and sees no value in a mother when she is old will be rooted out by the ravens of the valley, and be food for the young eagles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 42:3; Psalms 139:6"> There are three things, the wonder of which overcomes me, even four things outside my knowledge:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 40:31; Job 39:27; Exodus 22:16"> The way of an eagle in the air; the way of a snake on a rock; the way of a ship in the heart of the sea; and the way of a man with a girl.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 5:6; Proverbs 7:13–23; Numbers 5:11–30"> This is the way of a false wife; she takes food, and, cleaning her mouth, says, I have done no wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> For three things the earth is moved, and there are four which it will not put up with:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 19:10; Ecclesiastes 10:7; 1 Samuel 25:10–11"> A servant when he becomes a king; a man without sense when his wealth is increased;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 21:19; Proverbs 27:15; Proverbs 29:21"> A hated woman when she is married; and a servant-girl who takes the place of her master's wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 12:7"> There are four things which are little on the earth, but they are very wise:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 6:6–8"> The ants are a people not strong, but they put by a store of food in the summer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 104:18; Leviticus 11:5"> The conies are only a feeble people, but they make their houses in the rocks;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joel 2:25; Joel 1:6–7; Joel 2:7–11"> The locusts have no king, but they all go out in bands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> You may take the lizard in your hands, but it is in kings' houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> There are three things whose steps are good to see, even four whose goings are fair:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 23:24; Judges 14:18"> The lion, which is strongest among beasts, not turning from his way for any;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Daniel 3:15–18; Proverbs 20:2; Proverbs 16:14"> The war-horse, and the he-goat, and the king when his army is with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 21:5; Job 40:4; Proverbs 26:12"> If you have done foolishly in lifting yourself up, or if you have had evil designs, put your hand over your mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 17:14; Proverbs 29:22; Proverbs 28:25"> The shaking of milk makes butter, and the twisting of the nose makes blood come: so the forcing of wrath is a cause of fighting.</span>
