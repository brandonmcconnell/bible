<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:1; Luke 10:20; 1 Kings 1:47"> A good name is more to be desired than great wealth, and to be respected is better than silver and gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 31:15; Proverbs 14:31; 1 Samuel 2:7"> The man of wealth and the poor man come face to face: the Lord is the maker of them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 27:12; Proverbs 14:16; Proverbs 29:1"> The sharp man sees the evil and takes cover: the simple go straight on and get into trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; James 4:10; Psalms 34:9–10"> The reward of a gentle spirit and the fear of the Lord is wealth and honour and life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 15:19; 1 John 5:18; Proverbs 16:17"> Thorns and nets are in the way of the twisted: he who keeps watch over his soul will be far from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 6:4; Deuteronomy 6:7; 2 Timothy 3:15"> If a child is trained up in the right way, even when he is old he will not be turned away from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 4:1; Proverbs 22:16; Matthew 18:25"> The man of wealth has rule over the poor, and he who gets into debt is a servant to his creditor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 6:7–8; Job 4:8; Isaiah 30:31"> By planting the seed of evil a man will get in the grain of sorrow, and the rod of his wrath will be broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Hebrews 6:10; 1 Timothy 6:18–19"> He who is kind will have a blessing, for he gives of his bread to the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 26:20–21; Genesis 21:9–10; 1 Corinthians 5:5–6"> Send away the man of pride, and argument will go out; truly fighting and shame will come to an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 16:13; Matthew 5:8; Psalms 101:6"> He whose heart is clean is dear to the Lord; for the grace of his lips the king will be his friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:9; 2 Timothy 3:8–9; 2 Thessalonians 2:8"> The eyes of the Lord keep knowledge, but by him the acts of the false man will be overturned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 15:19; Proverbs 26:13–16; Numbers 13:32–33"> The hater of work says, There is a lion outside: I will be put to death in the streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Proverbs 23:27; Proverbs 6:24–29"> The mouth of strange women is a deep hole: he with whom the Lord is angry will go down into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 13:24; Proverbs 29:15; Proverbs 19:18"> Foolish ways are deep-seated in the heart of a child, but the rod of punishment will send them far from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 22:22–23; Psalms 12:5; James 2:13"> He who is cruel to the poor for the purpose of increasing his profit, and he who gives to the man of wealth, will only come to be in need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 23:12; Psalms 90:12; Proverbs 3:1"> Let your ear be bent down for hearing my words, and let your heart give thought to knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:16; Proverbs 2:10; Psalms 119:103"> For it is a delight to keep them in your heart, to have them ready on your lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 62:8; Jeremiah 17:7; 1 Peter 1:21"> So that your faith may be in the Lord, I have made them clear to you this day, even to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 8:6; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> Have I not put in writing for you thirty sayings, with wise suggestions and knowledge,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:3–4; 1 Peter 3:15; John 20:31"> To make you see how certain are true words, so that you may give a true answer to those who put questions to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zechariah 7:10; Malachi 3:5; Exodus 23:6"> Do not take away the property of the poor man because he is poor, or be cruel to the crushed ones when they come before the judge:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 12:5; Psalms 140:12; Proverbs 23:11"> For the Lord will give support to their cause, and take the life of those who take their goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 29:22; 2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Proverbs 21:24"> Do not be friends with a man who is given to wrath; do not go in the company of an angry man:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; Proverbs 13:20; Psalms 106:35"> For fear of learning his ways and making a net ready for your soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 11:15; Proverbs 17:18; Proverbs 6:1–5"> Be not one of those who give their hands in an agreement, or of those who make themselves responsible for debts:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 20:16; 2 Kings 4:1; Exodus 22:26–27"> If you have nothing with which to make payment, he will take away your bed from under you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:17; Deuteronomy 19:14; Proverbs 23:10"> Let not the old landmark be moved which your fathers have put in place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 11:28; Matthew 25:21; Proverbs 12:24"> Have you seen a man who is expert in his business? he will take his place before kings; his place will not be among low persons.</span>
