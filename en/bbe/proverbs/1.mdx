<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; 1 Chronicles 22:9; 1 Chronicles 28:5"> The wise sayings of Solomon, the son of David, king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 7:4; Deuteronomy 4:5–6; 2 Timothy 3:15–17"> To have knowledge of wise teaching; to be clear about the words of reason:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 2:1–9; Job 22:22; Proverbs 8:10–11"> To be trained in the ways of wisdom, in righteousness and judging truly and straight behaviour:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 8:5; Proverbs 8:17; Psalms 119:9"> To make the simple-minded sharp, and to give the young man knowledge, and serious purpose:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 12:1; Proverbs 9:9; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> (The wise man, hearing, will get greater learning, and the acts of the man of good sense will be wisely guided:)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 78:2; Mark 4:11; Psalms 49:4"> To get the sense of wise sayings and secrets, and of the words of the wise and their dark sayings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 28:28; Psalms 111:10–1; Ecclesiastes 12:13"> The fear of the Lord is the start of knowledge: but the foolish have no use for wisdom and teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 6:20; Proverbs 30:17; Proverbs 5:1–2"> My son, give ear to the training of your father, and do not give up the teaching of your mother:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 5:29; Proverbs 4:9; Genesis 41:42"> For they will be a crown of grace for your head, and chain-ornaments about your neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; Proverbs 16:29; Psalms 1:1"> My son, if sinners would take you out of the right way, do not go with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:26; Proverbs 1:18; Proverbs 12:6"> If they say, Come with us; let us make designs against the good, waiting secretly for the upright, without cause;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 124:3; Psalms 28:1; Romans 3:13"> Let us overcome them living, like the underworld, and in their strength, as those who go down to death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Luke 12:15; Job 24:2–3"> Goods of great price will be ours, our houses will be full of wealth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Take your chance with us, and we will all have one money-bag:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:101; Psalms 1:1; Proverbs 4:14–15"> My son, do not go with them; keep your feet from their ways:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Proverbs 6:18; Proverbs 4:16"> For their feet are running after evil, and they are quick to take a man's life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 35:11; Proverbs 7:23; Jeremiah 8:7"> Truly, to no purpose is the net stretched out before the eyes of the bird:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 9:16; Esther 7:10; Proverbs 5:22–23"> And they are secretly waiting for their blood and making ready destruction for themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 15:27; 2 Peter 2:3; James 5:1–4"> Such is the fate of everyone who goes in search of profit; it takes away the life of its owners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 9:3; Colossians 2:3; Luke 11:49"> Wisdom is crying out in the street; her voice is loud in the open places;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 5:20; Proverbs 9:3; Matthew 13:2"> Her words are sounding in the meeting-places, and in the doorways of the town:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 1:29; Psalms 1:1; Proverbs 5:12"> How long, you simple ones, will foolish things be dear to you? and pride a delight to the haters of authority? how long will the foolish go on hating knowledge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 3:19; Ezekiel 33:11; Ezekiel 18:27–30"> Be turned again by my sharp words: see, I will send the flow of my spirit on you, and make my words clear to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 65:12; Romans 10:21; Jeremiah 7:13"> Because your ears were shut to my voice; no one gave attention to my out-stretched hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 81:11; Luke 7:30; Proverbs 1:30"> You were not controlled by my guiding, and would have nothing to do with my sharp words:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 2:4; Proverbs 10:24; Psalms 37:13"> So in the day of your trouble I will be laughing; I will make sport of your fear;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 3:25–26; Luke 21:34–35; Psalms 58:9"> When your fear comes on you like a storm, and your trouble like a rushing wind; when pain and sorrow come on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Micah 3:4; James 4:3; Jeremiah 11:11"> Then I will give no answer to their cries; searching for me early, they will not see me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 1:22; Job 21:14–15; Proverbs 6:23"> For they were haters of knowledge, and did not give their hearts to the fear of the Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 81:11; Proverbs 1:25; Luke 14:18–20"> They had no desire for my teaching, and my words of protest were as nothing to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 14:14; Jeremiah 6:19; Proverbs 22:8"> So the fruit of their way will be their food, and with the designs of their hearts they will be made full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 8:36; Jeremiah 2:19; John 3:36"> For the turning back of the simple from teaching will be the cause of their death, and the peace of the foolish will be their destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 25:12–13; Psalms 112:7–8; Isaiah 26:3"> But whoever gives ear to me will take his rest safely, living in peace without fear of evil.</span>
