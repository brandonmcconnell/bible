<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 48:10; Jeremiah 17:10; Psalms 26:2"> The heating-pot is for silver and the oven-fire for gold, but the Lord is the tester of hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3–4; 1 John 4:5; Proverbs 28:4"> A wrongdoer gives attention to evil lips, and a man of deceit gives ear to a damaging tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 14:31; Job 31:29; 1 John 3:17"> Whoever makes sport of the poor puts shame on his Maker; and he who is glad because of trouble will not go free from punishment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 127:3–5; Proverbs 13:22; Proverbs 16:31"> Children's children are the crown of old men, and the glory of children is their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 29:12; Proverbs 12:19; Psalms 50:16–17"> Fair words are not to be looked for from a foolish man, much less are false lips in a ruler.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 23:8; Micah 7:3; 1 Samuel 25:35"> An offering of money is like a stone of great price in the eyes of him who has it: wherever he goes, he does well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 10:12; 1 Peter 4:8; Proverbs 16:28"> He who keeps a sin covered is looking for love; but he who keeps on talking of a thing makes division between friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 9:8–9; Proverbs 15:5"> A word of protest goes deeper into one who has sense than a hundred blows into a foolish man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 2:46; 2 Samuel 16:5–9; 2 Samuel 18:15"> An uncontrolled man is only looking for trouble, so a cruel servant will be sent against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 13:8; 2 Samuel 17:8; Proverbs 28:15"> It is better to come face to face with a bear whose young ones have been taken away than with a foolish man acting foolishly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 109:4–13; 1 Peter 3:9; Romans 12:17"> If anyone gives back evil for good, evil will never go away from his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 20:3; Proverbs 25:8; 2 Timothy 2:23–24"> The start of fighting is like the letting out of water: so give up before it comes to blows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 23:7; Proverbs 18:5; Isaiah 5:23"> He who gives a decision for the evil-doer and he who gives a decision against the upright, are equally disgusting to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 21:25–26; Proverbs 23:23; Proverbs 18:15"> How will money in the hand of the foolish get him wisdom, seeing that he has no sense?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 18:24; John 15:13–14; 1 Samuel 20:17"> A friend is loving at all times, and becomes a brother in times of trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 11:15; Proverbs 6:1–5; Proverbs 22:26–27"> A man without sense gives his hand in an agreement, and makes himself responsible before his neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 16:18; Proverbs 18:12; 1 Samuel 25:36–38"> The lover of fighting is a lover of sin: he who makes high his doorway is looking for destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 3:6–8; Proverbs 10:31; Proverbs 6:12–15"> Nothing good comes to him whose heart is fixed on evil purposes: and he who has an evil tongue will come to trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 19:13; Proverbs 17:25"> He who has an unwise son gets sorrow for himself, and the father of a foolish son has no joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 15:13; Proverbs 12:25; Proverbs 18:14"> A glad heart makes a healthy body, but a crushed spirit makes the bones dry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 17:8; Deuteronomy 16:19; Exodus 23:8"> A sinner takes an offering out of his robe, to get a decision for himself in a cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:14; Ecclesiastes 8:1; Proverbs 14:6"> Wisdom is before the face of him who has sense; but the eyes of the foolish are on the ends of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; 2 Samuel 13:1–22; Proverbs 19:13"> A foolish son is a grief to his father, and bitter pain to her who gave him birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 17:15; Proverbs 18:5; John 18:22"> To give punishment to the upright is not good, or to give blows to the noble for their righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 3:18; James 1:19; Proverbs 10:19"> He who has knowledge says little: and he who has a calm spirit is a man of good sense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 13:5; Proverbs 15:2; Ecclesiastes 10:14"> Even the foolish man, when he keeps quiet, is taken to be wise: when his lips are shut he is credited with good sense.</span>
