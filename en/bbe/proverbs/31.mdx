<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 6:20; Proverbs 1:8; 2 Timothy 1:5"> The words of Lemuel, king of Massa: the teaching which he had from his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 49:15; 1 Samuel 1:11; 1 Samuel 1:28"> What am I to say to you, O Lemuel, my oldest son? and what, O son of my body? and what, O son of my oaths?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:17; Nehemiah 13:26; Proverbs 7:26–27"> Do not give your strength to women, or your ways to that which is the destruction of kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Ecclesiastes 10:17; Isaiah 5:22"> It is not for kings, O Lemuel, it is not for kings to take wine, or for rulers to say, Where is strong drink?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:5"> For fear that through drinking they may come to have no respect for the law, wrongly judging the cause of those who are in trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:23; Psalms 104:15; 1 Samuel 1:10"> Give strong drink to him who is near to destruction, and wine to him whose soul is bitter:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 5:18"> Let him have drink, and his need will go from his mind, and the memory of his trouble will be gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 82:3–4; Proverbs 24:11–12; Esther 4:13–16"> Let your mouth be open for those who have no voice, in the cause of those who are ready for death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 1:17; Leviticus 19:15; Amos 5:11–12"> Let your mouth be open, judging rightly, and give right decisions in the cause of the poor and those in need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 19:14; Proverbs 12:4; Proverbs 18:22"> Who may make discovery of a woman of virtue? For her price is much higher than jewels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 3:1–7; 2 Kings 4:22–23; 2 Kings 4:9–10"> The heart of her husband has faith in her, and he will have profit in full measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:18–22; 1 Samuel 25:26–27"> She does him good and not evil all the days of her life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Titus 2:5; 1 Timothy 5:10; 2 Thessalonians 3:10–12"> She gets wool and linen, working at the business of her hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 31:24; 1 Kings 9:26–28; Ezekiel 27:3–36"> She is like the trading-ships, getting food from far away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 12:11; Mark 1:35; Luke 12:42"> She gets up while it is still night, and gives meat to her family, and their food to her servant-girls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 13:44; Song of Solomon 8:12; Joshua 15:18"> After looking at a field with care, she gets it for a price, planting a vine-garden with the profit of her work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 38:3; 1 Peter 1:13; Ephesians 6:10"> She puts a band of strength round her, and makes her arms strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:9; Matthew 25:3–10; Psalms 127:2"> She sees that her marketing is of profit to her: her light does not go out by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 35:25–26"> She puts her hands to the cloth-working rod, and her fingers take the wheel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 4:28; Hebrews 13:16; Proverbs 22:9"> Her hands are stretched out to the poor; yes, she is open-handed to those who are in need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 45:22; 2 Samuel 1:24; Proverbs 25:20"> She has no fear of the snow for her family, for all those in her house are clothed in red.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 19:8; Proverbs 7:16; Genesis 41:42"> She makes for herself cushions of needlework; her clothing is fair linen and purple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 12:4; Ruth 4:1; Deuteronomy 16:18"> Her husband is a man of note in the public place, when he takes his seat among the responsible men of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:16; Luke 16:19; Proverbs 31:19"> She makes linen robes and gets a price for them, and traders take her cloth bands for a price.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 40:10; Romans 13:14; Job 29:14"> Strength and self-respect are her clothing; she is facing the future with a smile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Proverbs 16:24; Proverbs 10:31"> Her mouth is open to give out wisdom, and the law of mercy is on her tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:10; Titus 2:4; 2 Thessalonians 3:6"> She gives attention to the ways of her family, she does not take her food without working for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:15–17; 2 Timothy 1:5; 1 Kings 2:19"> Her children get up and give her honour, and her husband gives her praise, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Song of Solomon 6:8–9; Ephesians 5:27"> Unnumbered women have done well, but you are better than all of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 1:7; 1 Peter 3:4–5; Proverbs 11:22"> Fair looks are a deceit, and a beautiful form is of no value; but a woman who has the fear of the Lord is to be praised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 128:2; Hebrews 6:10; Matthew 7:16"> Give her credit for what her hands have made: let her be praised by her works in the public place.</span>
