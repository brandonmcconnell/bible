<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 16:9–11; Psalms 37:7; Numbers 16:19–41"> By his foolish behaviour a man's ways are turned upside down, and his heart is bitter against the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 14:20; Proverbs 19:6–7; Luke 15:13–15"> Wealth makes a great number of friends; but the poor man is parted from his friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 21:28; Proverbs 19:9; Proverbs 6:19"> A false witness will not go without punishment, and the breather out of deceit will not go free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 29:26; Proverbs 18:16; Proverbs 17:8"> Great numbers will make attempts to get the approval of a ruler: and every man is the special friend of him who has something to give.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 19:4; 1 John 3:17–18; James 2:15–16"> All the brothers of the poor man are against him: how much more do his friends go far from him! ...</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 16:20; Proverbs 8:35–36; Proverbs 3:18"> He who gets wisdom has love for his soul: he who keeps good sense will get what is truly good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 22:15; Proverbs 19:5; Revelation 19:20"> A false witness will not go without punishment, and the breather out of deceit will be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 30:21–22; Proverbs 26:1; Luke 16:19"> Material comfort is not good for the foolish; much less for a servant to be put over rulers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 16:32; Proverbs 14:29; James 1:19"> A man's good sense makes him slow to wrath, and the overlooking of wrongdoing is his glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 14:5; Proverbs 20:2; Proverbs 16:14–15"> The king's wrath is like the loud cry of a lion, but his approval is like dew on the grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 27:15; Proverbs 21:9; Proverbs 10:1"> A foolish son is the destruction of his father; and the bitter arguments of a wife are like drops of rain falling without end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 18:22; Proverbs 31:10–31; Proverbs 13:22"> House and wealth are a heritage from fathers, but a wife with good sense is from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 23:21; Proverbs 20:13; 2 Thessalonians 3:10"> Hate of work sends deep sleep on a man: and he who has no industry will go without food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 10:28; Proverbs 13:13; Luke 11:28"> He who keeps the law keeps his soul; but death will be the fate of him who takes no note of the word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 28:27; Hebrews 6:10; Luke 6:38"> He who has pity on the poor gives to the Lord, and the Lord will give him his reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 13:24; Proverbs 29:15; Proverbs 22:15"> Give your son training while there is hope; let not your heart be purposing his death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 25:28; 2 Samuel 16:5–6; 1 Samuel 24:17–22"> A man of great wrath will have to take his punishment: for if you get him out of trouble you will have to do it again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 12:15; Proverbs 8:34–35; Proverbs 2:1–9"> Let your ear be open to suggestion and take teaching, so that at the end you may be wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 16:9; Isaiah 14:24; Proverbs 16:1"> A man's heart may be full of designs, but the purpose of the Lord is unchanging.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 14:6–8; 2 Corinthians 8:12; 2 Chronicles 6:8"> The ornament of a man is his mercy, and a poor man is better than one who is false.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 5:6; Proverbs 14:26–27; Proverbs 12:21"> The fear of the Lord gives life: and he who has it will have need of nothing; no evil will come his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 15:19; Proverbs 6:9–10; Proverbs 24:30–34"> The hater of work puts his hand deep into the basin, and will not even take it to his mouth again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 21:11; Proverbs 17:10; Proverbs 15:5"> When blows overtake the man of pride, the simple will get sense; say sharp words to the wise, and knowledge will be made clear to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 10:5; Proverbs 17:2; Proverbs 28:24"> He who is violent to his father, driving away his mother, is a son causing shame and a bad name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 4:24; 1 Timothy 6:3–5; Matthew 7:15"> A son who no longer gives attention to teaching is turned away from the words of knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 15:16; Job 34:7; Luke 18:2–4"> A good-for-nothing witness makes sport of the judge's decision: and the mouth of evil-doers sends out evil like a stream.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 26:3; Proverbs 10:13; Proverbs 9:12"> Rods are being made ready for the man of pride, and blows for the back of the foolish.</span>
