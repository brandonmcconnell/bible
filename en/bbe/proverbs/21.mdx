<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 3:2–3; Proverbs 3:29; Psalms 36:4"> The desire of the evil-doer is fixed on evil: he has no kind feeling for his neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 19:25; Proverbs 18:15; Proverbs 9:9"> When the man of pride undergoes punishment, the simple man gets wisdom; and by watching the wise he gets knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 14:11; Psalms 37:35–36; 2 Peter 3:6–7"> The Upright One, looking on the house of the evil-doer, lets sinners be overturned to their destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 28:27; Matthew 6:14; Proverbs 1:28"> He whose ears are stopped at the cry of the poor, will himself get no answer to his cry for help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 18:16; Proverbs 19:6; Proverbs 17:8"> By a secret offering wrath is turned away, and the heat of angry feelings by money in the folds of the robe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 10:29; Matthew 7:23; Matthew 13:41–42"> It is a joy to the good man to do right, but it is destruction to the workers of evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 10:38; Psalms 125:5; Psalms 49:14"> The wanderer from the way of knowledge will have his resting-place among the shades.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 23:21; 1 Timothy 5:6; 2 Timothy 3:4"> The lover of pleasure will be a poor man: the lover of wine and oil will not get wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 11:8; Isaiah 43:3–4; Isaiah 53:4–5"> The evil-doer will be given as a price for the life of the good man, and the worker of deceit in the place of the upright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 21:9; Psalms 120:5–6; Psalms 55:6–7"> It is better to be living in a waste land, than with a bitter-tongued and angry woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 25:3–4; Psalms 112:3; Ecclesiastes 7:11"> There is a store of great value in the house of the wise, but it is wasted by the foolish man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 5:6; 1 Corinthians 15:58; Proverbs 22:4"> He who goes after righteousness and mercy will get life, righteousness, and honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:19; Proverbs 24:5; Ecclesiastes 9:13–18"> A wise man goes up into the town of the strong ones, and overcomes its strength in which they put their faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 13:3; Proverbs 18:21; James 1:26"> He who keeps watch over his mouth and his tongue keeps his soul from troubles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 1:1; Proverbs 18:12; Proverbs 1:22"> The man of pride, lifted up in soul, is named high-hearted; he is acting in an outburst of pride.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 12:24; Matthew 25:26"> The desire of the hater of work is death to him, for his hands will do no work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 37:26; Acts 20:33–35; Ephesians 4:28"> All the day the sinner goes after his desire: but the upright man gives freely, keeping nothing back.</span>
