<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 6:15; 1 Samuel 2:25; Proverbs 1:24–31"> A man hating sharp words and making his heart hard, will suddenly be broken and will not be made well again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 28:12; Proverbs 11:10; Proverbs 28:28"> When the upright have power, the people are glad; when an evil man is ruler, grief comes on the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 27:11; Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 15:20"> A man who is a lover of wisdom is a joy to his father: but he who goes in the company of loose women is a waster of wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 29:14; Proverbs 20:8; Daniel 11:20"> A king, by right rule, makes the land safe; but one full of desires makes it a waste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 5:1; Job 17:5; Psalms 5:9"> A man who says smooth things to his neighbour is stretching out a net for his steps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:12; Exodus 15:1; 2 Timothy 2:26"> In the steps of an evil man there is a net for him, but the upright man gets away quickly and is glad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 29:16; Psalms 41:1; Proverbs 21:13"> The upright man gives attention to the cause of the poor: the evil-doer gives no thought to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 11:11; Jeremiah 15:1; James 5:15–18"> Men of pride are the cause of violent acts in a town, but by wise men wrath is turned away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 26:4; Ecclesiastes 10:13; Matthew 7:6"> If a wise man goes to law with a foolish man, he may be angry or laughing, but there will be no rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 15:18–19; 1 John 3:12–13; Genesis 4:5–8"> Men of blood are haters of the good man, and evil-doers go after his soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 19:11; Proverbs 12:16; Micah 7:5"> A foolish man lets out all his wrath, but a wise man keeps it back quietly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 20:8; 2 Kings 10:6–7; Psalms 52:2–4"> If a ruler gives attention to false words, all his servants are evil-doers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 5:45; Proverbs 22:2; Psalms 13:3"> The poor man and his creditor come face to face: the Lord gives light to their eyes equally.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 16:12; Isaiah 11:4; Psalms 72:2–4"> The king who is a true judge in the cause of the poor, will be safe for ever on the seat of his power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 17:25; Proverbs 29:17"> The rod and sharp words give wisdom: but a child who is not guided is a cause of shame to his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 37:36; Psalms 91:8; Psalms 58:10"> When evil men are in power, wrongdoing is increased; but the upright will have pleasure when they see their downfall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 29:15; Proverbs 23:13–14"> Give your son training, and he will give you rest; he will give delight to your soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 1:25; Luke 11:28; John 13:17"> Where there is no vision, the people are uncontrolled; but he who keeps the law will be happy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 26:3; Job 19:16; Proverbs 30:22"> A servant will not be trained by words; for though the sense of the words is clear to him, he will not give attention.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 1:19; Proverbs 26:12; Proverbs 14:29"> Have you seen a man who is quick with his tongue? There is more hope for a foolish man than for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> If a servant is gently cared for from his early years, he will become a cause of sorrow in the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 15:18; Proverbs 17:19; Proverbs 26:21"> An angry man is the cause of fighting, and a man given to wrath does much wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 23:12; Luke 14:11; Proverbs 11:2"> A man's pride will be the cause of his fall, but he who has a gentle spirit will get honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 5:1; Proverbs 1:11–19; Proverbs 8:36"> A man who takes part with a thief has hate for his soul; he is put under oath, but says nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 118:8; Luke 12:4; Proverbs 18:10"> The fear of man is a cause of danger: but whoever puts his faith in the Lord will have a safe place on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 19:6; Proverbs 19:21; Psalms 20:9"> The approval of a ruler is desired by great numbers: but the decision in a man's cause comes from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 John 3:13; John 15:17–19; Psalms 139:21"> An evil man is disgusting to the upright, and he who is upright is disgusting to evil-doers.</span>
