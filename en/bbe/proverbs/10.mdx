<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 1:1; Proverbs 29:15; Proverbs 17:25"> A wise son makes a glad father, but a foolish son is a sorrow to his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 11:4; Proverbs 12:28; Psalms 49:6–10"> Wealth which comes from sin is of no profit, but righteousness gives salvation from death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 37:3; Matthew 6:30–33; Psalms 37:25"> The Lord will not let the upright be in need of food, but he puts far from him the desire of the evil-doers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 21:5; Proverbs 20:4; Proverbs 19:15"> He who is slow in his work becomes poor, but the hand of the ready worker gets in wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 6:8; Proverbs 30:25; Proverbs 17:2"> He who in summer gets together his store is a son who does wisely; but he who takes his rest when the grain is being cut is a son causing shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:2; Proverbs 28:20; Job 29:13"> Blessings are on the head of the upright, but the face of sinners will be covered with sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 112:6; Luke 1:48; Mark 14:9"> The memory of the upright is a blessing, but the name of the evil-doer will be turned to dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 7:24–25; Proverbs 12:1; James 3:13"> The wise-hearted man will let himself be ruled, but the man whose talk is foolish will have a fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 28:18; Matthew 10:26; Psalms 26:11–12"> He whose ways are upright will go safely, but he whose ways are twisted will be made low.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; Proverbs 6:13; 3 John 1:10"> He who makes signs with his eyes is a cause of trouble, but he who makes a man see his errors is a cause of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 13:14; Ephesians 4:29; Proverbs 10:6"> The mouth of the upright man is a fountain of life, but the mouth of the evil-doer is a bitter cup.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 4:8; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7; Proverbs 17:9"> Hate is a cause of violent acts, but all errors are covered up by love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 26:3; Proverbs 6:32; Isaiah 50:4"> In the lips of him who has knowledge wisdom is seen; but a rod is ready for the back of him who is without sense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 18:7; Proverbs 10:8; Proverbs 13:3"> Knowledge is stored up by the wise, but the mouth of the foolish man is a destruction which is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 18:11; Proverbs 19:7; Psalms 52:7"> The property of the man of wealth is his strong town: the poor man's need is his destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 6:23; Isaiah 3:10–11; Hebrews 6:10"> The work of the upright gives life: the increase of the evil-doer is a cause of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Peter 1:5–11; Luke 11:28; Proverbs 6:23"> He who takes note of teaching is a way of life, but he who gives up training is a cause of error.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 26:24–26; Psalms 55:21; 2 Samuel 3:27"> Hate is covered up by the lips of the upright man, but he who lets out evil about another is foolish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="James 1:19; James 3:2; Proverbs 17:27–28"> Where there is much talk there will be no end to sin, but he who keeps his mouth shut does wisely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:9; Proverbs 12:18; Genesis 6:5"> The tongue of the upright man is like tested silver: the heart of the evil-doer is of little value.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hosea 4:6; Proverbs 17:16; Proverbs 1:29"> The lips of the upright man give food to men, but the foolish come to death for need of sense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 107:38; Genesis 26:12; 1 Samuel 2:7–8"> The blessing of the Lord gives wealth: hard work makes it no greater.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 15:21; Proverbs 2:14; Proverbs 14:9"> It is sport to the foolish man to do evil, but the man of good sense takes delight in wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 145:19; Isaiah 66:4; Matthew 5:6"> The thing feared by the evil-doer will come to him, but the upright man will get his desire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 15:5; Psalms 58:9; Matthew 7:24–27"> When the storm-wind is past, the sinner is seen no longer, but the upright man is safe for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 25:13; Proverbs 26:6; Matthew 25:26"> Like acid drink to the teeth and as smoke to the eyes, so is the hater of work to those who send him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 9:11; Proverbs 3:2; Psalms 91:16"> The fear of the Lord gives long life, but the years of the evil-doer will be cut short.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 8:13; Romans 15:13; Psalms 73:24–26"> The hope of the upright man will give joy, but the waiting of the evil-doer will have its end in sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 21:15; Romans 2:8–9; Philippians 4:13"> The way of the Lord is a strong tower for the upright man, but destruction to the workers of evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 37:22; Psalms 125:1; Psalms 37:9–10"> The upright man will never be moved, but evil-doers will not have a safe resting-place in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 37:30; Proverbs 10:13; Proverbs 10:20–21"> The mouth of the upright man is budding with wisdom, but the twisted tongue will be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 15:28; Proverbs 12:6; Proverbs 11:11"> The lips of the upright man have knowledge of what is pleasing, but twisted are the mouths of evil-doers.</span>
