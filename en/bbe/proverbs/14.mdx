<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 31:10–31; Proverbs 24:3–4; Proverbs 21:9"> Wisdom is building her house, but the foolish woman is pulling it down with her hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:2–3; Acts 10:35; Proverbs 28:6"> He who goes on his way in righteousness has before him the fear of the Lord; but he whose ways are twisted gives him no honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 12:6; James 3:5–6; Revelation 12:11"> In the mouth of the foolish man is a rod for his back, but the lips of the wise will keep them safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:9–11; Proverbs 13:23; Amos 4:6"> Where there are no oxen, their food-place is clean; but much increase comes through the strength of the ox.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 6:19; Proverbs 19:5; Exodus 23:1"> A true witness does not say what is false, but a false witness is breathing out deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 1:5; Matthew 13:11–12; Matthew 11:25–27"> The hater of authority, searching for wisdom, does not get it; but knowledge comes readily to the open-minded man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; Ephesians 5:11; 1 Corinthians 5:11"> Go away from the foolish man, for you will not see the lips of knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 5:17; 2 Timothy 3:13; James 3:13"> The wisdom of the man of good sense makes his way clear; but the unwise behaviour of the foolish is deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 26:18–19; Proverbs 10:23; Romans 14:17–18"> In the tents of those hating authority there is error, but in the house of the upright man there is grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 4:7; Proverbs 18:14; Proverbs 15:13"> No one has knowledge of a man's grief but himself; and a strange person has no part in his joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 3:33; Proverbs 12:7; Matthew 7:26–27"> The house of the sinner will be overturned, but the tent of the upright man will do well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 16:25; Matthew 7:13–14; Proverbs 12:15"> There is a way which seems straight before a man, but its end is the ways of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:2; James 4:9; Ecclesiastes 2:10–11"> Even while laughing the heart may be sad; and after joy comes sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 6:8; Proverbs 12:14; Hebrews 3:12"> He whose heart is turned away will have the reward of his ways in full measure; but a good man will have the reward of his doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 5:17; Proverbs 14:8; 1 John 4:1"> The simple man has faith in every word, but the man of good sense gives thought to his footsteps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 22:3; Proverbs 3:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:22"> The wise man, fearing, keeps himself from evil; but the foolish man goes on in his pride, with no thought of danger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 14:29; James 1:19; Ecclesiastes 7:9"> He who is quickly angry will do what is foolish, but the man of good sense will have quiet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Peter 1:18; 1 Peter 5:4; Jeremiah 44:17"> Foolish behaviour is the heritage of the simple, but men of good sense are crowned with knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 42:6; Malachi 4:3; Exodus 8:8"> The knees of the evil are bent before the good; and sinners go down in the dust at the doors of the upright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 19:4; Proverbs 10:15; Proverbs 19:6–7"> The poor man is hated even by his neighbour, but the man of wealth has numbers of friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Proverbs 28:27; Proverbs 11:12"> He who has no respect for his neighbour is a sinner, but he who has pity for the poor is happy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 12:2; John 1:17; Psalms 61:7"> Will not the designers of evil come into error? But mercy and good faith are for the designers of good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 28:19; Proverbs 12:24; 2 Thessalonians 3:10–12"> In all hard work there is profit, but talk only makes a man poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:11–12; Luke 12:19–20; Luke 16:19–25"> Their wisdom is a crown to the wise, but their foolish behaviour is round the head of the unwise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 14:5; Acts 26:16–20; 2 Peter 3:3"> A true witness is the saviour of lives; but he who says false things is a cause of deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 19:23; Jeremiah 32:39–40; Psalms 115:13–14"> For him in whose heart is the fear of the Lord there is strong hope: and his children will have a safe place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Revelation 21:6; Proverbs 13:14; Proverbs 2:10–18"> The fear of the Lord is a fountain of life, by which one may be turned from the nets of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Kings 4:20–21; 2 Kings 13:7; Exodus 1:22"> A king's glory is in the number of his people: and for need of people a ruler may come to destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="James 1:19; Ecclesiastes 7:9; Proverbs 14:17"> He who is slow to be angry has great good sense; but he whose spirit is over-quick gives support to what is foolish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 17:22; Proverbs 4:23; Proverbs 3:8"> A quiet mind is the life of the body, but envy is a disease in the bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 22:2; Proverbs 17:5; Proverbs 22:16"> He who is hard on the poor puts shame on his Maker; but he who has mercy on those who are in need gives him honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:18; 2 Corinthians 5:8; Psalms 23:4"> The sinner is overturned in his evil-doing, but the upright man has hope in his righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 12:16; Proverbs 13:16; Proverbs 12:23"> Wisdom has her resting-place in the mind of the wise, but she is not seen among the foolish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 107:34; Deuteronomy 28:1–68; Deuteronomy 29:18–28"> By righteousness a nation is lifted up, but sin is a cause of shame to the peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 24:45–51; Luke 12:42–48; Matthew 25:23"> The king has pleasure in a servant who does wisely, but his wrath is against him who is a cause of shame.</span>
