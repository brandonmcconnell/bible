<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:7; Psalms 53:5; Isaiah 26:3–4"> The evil man goes running away when no man is after him, but the upright are without fear, like the lion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 15:28; 1 Kings 16:8–29; 2 Kings 15:8–31"> Because of the sin of the land, its troubles are increased; but by a man of wisdom and knowledge they will be put out like a fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 18:28–30"> A man of wealth who is cruel to the poor is like a violent rain causing destruction of food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:32; Ephesians 5:11; Matthew 14:4"> Those who have no respect for the law give praise to the evil-doer; but such as keep the law are against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 92:6; James 1:5; 1 John 2:27"> Evil men have no knowledge of what is right; but those who go after the Lord have knowledge of all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 19:1; Proverbs 28:18; Luke 16:19–23"> Better is the poor man whose ways are upright, than the man of wealth whose ways are not straight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:3–4; Proverbs 29:3; Proverbs 3:1–35"> He who keeps the law is a wise son, but he who keeps company with feasters puts shame on his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 27:16–17; Proverbs 13:22; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> He who makes his wealth greater by taking interest, only gets it together for him who has pity on the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 66:18; 2 Timothy 4:3–4; Zechariah 7:11–13"> As for the man whose ear is turned away from hearing the law, even his prayer is disgusting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 26:27; Psalms 37:25–26; Galatians 2:4"> Anyone causing the upright to go wandering in an evil way, will himself go down into the hole he has made; but the upright will have good things for their heritage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 18:17; Ecclesiastes 9:15–17; Proverbs 26:16"> The man of wealth seems to himself to be wise, but the poor man who has sense has a low opinion of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 29:2; Proverbs 11:10; Proverbs 28:28"> When the upright do well, there is great glory; but when evil-doers are lifted up, men do not let themselves be seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 John 1:8–10; Psalms 32:3–5; Job 31:33"> He who keeps his sins secret will not do well; but one who is open about them, and gives them up, will get mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 23:17; Psalms 2:11; Isaiah 66:2"> Happy is the man in whom is the fear of the Lord at all times; but he whose heart is hard will come into trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Matthew 2:16; Proverbs 19:12"> Like a loud-voiced lion and a wandering bear, is an evil ruler over a poor people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; Jeremiah 22:15–17; Ecclesiastes 4:1"> The prince who has no sense is a cruel ruler; but he who has no desire to get profit for himself will have long life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 9:6; 1 Kings 21:19; Exodus 21:14"> One who has been the cause of a man's death will go in flight to the underworld: let no man give him help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 10:9; Proverbs 28:6; Revelation 3:3"> He whose ways are upright will be safe, but sudden will be the fall of him whose ways are twisted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Judges 9:4; Proverbs 27:23–27"> By ploughing his land a man will have bread in full measure; but he who goes after good-for-nothing persons will be poor enough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 16:10–12; Proverbs 13:11; Revelation 2:10"> A man of good faith will have great blessing, but one attempting to get wealth quickly will not go free from punishment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 24:23; Proverbs 18:5; Ezekiel 13:19"> It is not good to have respect for a man's position: for a man will do wrong for a bit of bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 23:6; 1 Timothy 6:9; Proverbs 28:20"> He who is ever desiring wealth goes running after money, and does not see that need will come on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 27:5–6; Matthew 18:15; Psalms 141:5"> He who says words of protest to a man will later have more approval than one who says smooth words with his tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 15:4–6; Proverbs 18:9; Proverbs 19:26"> He who takes from his father or his mother what is theirs by right, and says, It is no sin; is the same as a taker of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 11:25; 1 Timothy 6:6; Proverbs 13:10"> He who is ever desiring profit is a cause of fighting; but he who puts his faith in the Lord will be made fat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5; Jeremiah 17:9; Job 28:28"> He whose faith is in himself is foolish; but everyone walking wisely will be kept safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Deuteronomy 15:7; Psalms 41:1–3"> He who gives to the poor will never be in need, but great curses will be on him who gives no attention to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 29:2; Job 24:4; Acts 12:23–24"> When evil-doers are lifted up, men take cover; but when destruction overtakes them, the upright are increased.</span>
