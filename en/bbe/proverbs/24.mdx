<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Proverbs 23:17; Proverbs 3:31"> Have no envy for evil men, or any desire to be with them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Psalms 7:14; Psalms 28:3"> For the purposes of their hearts are destruction, and their lips are talking of trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 14:1; 1 Corinthians 3:9; Proverbs 9:1"> The building of a house is by wisdom, and by reason it is made strong:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 15:6; Proverbs 21:20; Proverbs 20:15"> And by knowledge its rooms are full of all dear and pleasing things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:19; Colossians 1:11; Proverbs 21:22"> A wise man is strong; and a man of knowledge makes strength greater.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 11:14; Proverbs 20:18; Proverbs 15:22"> For by wise guiding you will overcome in war: and in a number of wise guides there is salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 14:6; Psalms 10:5; 1 Corinthians 2:14"> Wisdom is outside the power of the foolish: he keeps his mouth shut in the public place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 21:11; Ezekiel 38:10–11; Proverbs 14:22"> He whose purposes are bad will be named a man of evil designs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 15:19; 2 Corinthians 10:5; Isaiah 55:7"> The purpose of the foolish is sin: and the hater of authority is disgusting to others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 40:28–31; Ephesians 3:13; Jeremiah 51:46"> If you give way in the day of trouble, your strength is small.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 82:4; Isaiah 58:6–7; 1 John 3:16–17"> Be the saviour of those who are given up to death, and do not keep back help from those who are slipping to destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 34:11; Revelation 2:23; Jeremiah 17:10"> If you say, See, we had no knowledge of this: does not the tester of hearts give thought to it? and he who keeps your soul, has he no knowledge of it? and will he not give to every man the reward of his work?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 7:15; Song of Solomon 5:1; Proverbs 25:16"> My son, take honey, for it is good; and the flowing honey, which is sweet to your taste:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 1:25; Jeremiah 15:16; Proverbs 2:10"> So let your desire be for wisdom: if you have it, there will be a future, and your hope will not be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 9:24; Isaiah 32:18; 1 Samuel 23:20–23"> Do not keep a secret watch, O evil-doer, against the fields of the upright man, or send destruction on his resting-place:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 34:19; Psalms 37:24; Micah 7:8–10"> For an upright man, after falling seven times, will get up again: but trouble is the downfall of the evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Obadiah 1:12; Job 31:29; Proverbs 17:5"> Do not be glad at the fall of your hater, and let not your heart have joy at his downfall:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 1:15–16; Lamentations 4:21–22"> For fear that the Lord may see it, and it may be evil in his eyes, and his wrath may be turned away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 119:115; Psalms 37:1; Proverbs 23:17"> Do not be troubled because of evil-doers, or have envy of sinners:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 13:9; Job 18:5–6; Psalms 11:6"> For there will be no future for the evil man; the light of sinners will be put out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 13:1–7; 1 Peter 2:13–17; Titus 3:1"> My son, go in fear of the Lord and the king: have nothing to do with those who are in high positions:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 90:11; Hosea 5:11; Numbers 16:31–35"> For their downfall will come suddenly; and who has knowledge of the destruction of those in high positions?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 28:21; Proverbs 18:5; John 7:24"> These are more sayings of the wise: To have respect for a person's position when judging is not good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 17:15; Isaiah 5:23; Proverbs 11:26"> He who says to the evil-doer, You are upright, will be cursed by peoples and hated by nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:20; 1 Samuel 3:13; Proverbs 28:23"> But those who say sharp words to him will have delight, and a blessing of good will come on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 15:23; Genesis 41:38–57; Proverbs 25:11–12"> He gives a kiss with his lips who gives a right answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 14:28–30; 1 Kings 5:17–18; 1 Kings 6:7"> Put your work in order outside, and make it ready in the field; and after that, see to the building of your house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ephesians 4:25; Matthew 26:59–60; Proverbs 25:18"> Do not be a violent witness against your neighbour, or let your lips say what is false.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 20:22; Romans 12:17–21; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> Say not, I will do to him as he has done to me; I will give the man the reward of his work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Proverbs 6:6–19; Job 4:8"> I went by the field of the hater of work, and by the vine-garden of the man without sense;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:18; Isaiah 5:5; Jeremiah 4:3"> And it was all full of thorns, and covered with waste plants, and its stone wall was broken down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:11; Deuteronomy 13:11; 1 Corinthians 10:6"> Then looking at it, I gave thought: I saw, and I got teaching from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:6–8; Proverbs 6:4–11; Romans 13:11"> A little sleep, a little rest, a little folding of the hands in sleep:</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 13:4"> So loss will come on you like an outlaw, and your need like an armed man.</span>
