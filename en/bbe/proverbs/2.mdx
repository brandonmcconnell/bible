<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 1:5; Ephesians 1:17–18; Proverbs 3:6"> Truly, if you are crying out for good sense, and your request is for knowledge;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 13:44; Proverbs 16:16; Matthew 19:29"> If you are looking for her as for silver, and searching for her as for stored-up wealth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 1:7; Jeremiah 24:7; Jeremiah 32:40–41"> Then the fear of the Lord will be clear to you, and knowledge of God will be yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 1:17–18; James 1:5; Job 32:8"> For the Lord gives wisdom; out of his mouth come knowledge and reason:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 3:15–17; Proverbs 30:5; Psalms 84:11"> He has salvation stored up for the upright, he is a breastplate to those in whom there is no evil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 23:3; 1 Samuel 2:9; John 10:28–29"> He keeps watch on the ways which are right, and takes care of those who have the fear of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:16; Psalms 143:8–10; Matthew 7:13–14"> Then you will have knowledge of righteousness and right acting, and upright behaviour, even of every good way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:16; Colossians 3:16; Job 23:12"> For wisdom will come into your heart, and knowledge will be pleasing to your soul;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 4:6; Ephesians 5:15; Psalms 25:21"> Wise purposes will be watching over you, and knowledge will keep you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; Proverbs 8:13; Proverbs 13:20"> Giving you salvation from the evil man, from those whose words are false;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 3:19–20; Proverbs 4:19; Proverbs 21:16"> Who give up the way of righteousness, to go by dark roads;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 1:32; Proverbs 10:23; Jeremiah 11:15"> Who take pleasure in wrongdoing, and have joy in the evil designs of the sinner;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 125:5; Proverbs 21:8; Philippians 2:15"> Whose ways are not straight, and whose footsteps are turned to evil:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Proverbs 22:14; Proverbs 23:27"> To take you out of the power of the strange woman, who says smooth words with her tongue;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:59–60; Ezekiel 16:8; Jeremiah 3:4"> Who is false to the husband of her early years, and does not keep the agreement of her God in mind:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 9:18; 1 Corinthians 6:9–11; Proverbs 5:4–14"> For her house is on the way down to death; her footsteps go down to the shades:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Psalms 81:12; Hosea 4:14"> Those who go to her do not come back again; their feet do not keep in the ways of life:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 6:12; 3 John 1:11; Psalms 119:63"> So that you may go in the way of good men, and keep in the footsteps of the upright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 84:11; Psalms 37:29; Psalms 37:9"> For the upright will be living in the land, and the good will have it for their heritage.</span>
