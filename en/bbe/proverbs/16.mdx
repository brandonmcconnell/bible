<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 20:2; Proverbs 19:12; Ecclesiastes 10:4"> The wrath of the king is like those who give news of death, but a wise man will put peace in place of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 72:6; Job 29:23–24; Hosea 6:3"> In the light of the king's face there is life; and his approval is like a cloud of spring rain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 8:10–11; Matthew 16:26; Proverbs 8:19"> How much better it is to get wisdom than gold! and to get knowledge is more to be desired than silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 35:8; Proverbs 19:16; Proverbs 10:9"> The highway of the upright is to be turned away from evil: he who takes care of his way will keep his soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 11:2; Isaiah 2:11–12; Proverbs 18:12"> Pride goes before destruction, and a stiff spirit before a fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 5:3; Isaiah 57:15; Psalms 138:6"> Better it is to have a gentle spirit with the poor, than to take part in the rewards of war with men of pride.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:7–8; Psalms 2:12; Isaiah 30:18"> He who gives attention to the law of right will get good; and whoever puts his faith in the Lord is happy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 16:23–24; Isaiah 50:4; Proverbs 15:7"> The wise-hearted will be named men of good sense: and by pleasing words learning is increased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 13:14; John 6:63; John 5:24"> Wisdom is a fountain of life to him who has it; but the punishment of the foolish is their foolish behaviour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 15:28; Matthew 12:34–35; Colossians 3:16"> The heart of the wise man is the teacher of his mouth, and gives increased learning to his lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 12:18; Proverbs 24:13–14; Proverbs 25:11–12"> Pleasing words are like honey, sweet to the soul and new life to the bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 14:12; Proverbs 12:15; 2 Corinthians 13:5"> There is a way which seems straight before a man, but its end is the ways of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 6:7; Proverbs 14:23; Proverbs 9:12"> The desire of the working man is working for him, for his need of food is driving him on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 3:6; Proverbs 6:14; Isaiah 5:18"> A good-for-nothing man is a designer of evil, and in his lips there is a burning fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 17:9; Proverbs 18:8; Proverbs 15:18"> A man of twisted purposes is a cause of fighting everywhere: and he who says evil secretly makes trouble between friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 2:12–15; Proverbs 12:26; Proverbs 1:10–14"> A violent man puts desire of evil into his neighbour's mind, and makes him go in a way which is not good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 13:15; Micah 7:3; Matthew 14:7–8"> He whose eyes are shut is a man of twisted purposes, and he who keeps his lips shut tight makes evil come about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 20:29; Leviticus 19:32; Proverbs 3:1–2"> The grey head is a crown of glory, if it is seen in the way of righteousness.</span>
