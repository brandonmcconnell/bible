<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jude 1:12; Genesis 43:32–34"> When you take your seat at the feast with a ruler, give thought with care to what is before you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 3:19; Matthew 18:8–9; 1 Corinthians 9:27"> And put a knife to your throat, if you have a strong desire for food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 23:6; Psalms 141:4; Ephesians 4:22"> Have no desire for his delicate food, for it is the bread of deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5; Proverbs 28:20; Matthew 6:19"> Take no care to get wealth; let there be an end to your desire for money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 27:24; 1 John 2:16; James 5:1–2"> Are your eyes lifted up to it? it is gone: for wealth takes to itself wings, like an eagle in flight up to heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 141:4; Proverbs 23:3; Daniel 1:8–10"> Do not take the food of him who has an evil eye, or have any desire for his delicate meat:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 12:2; Psalms 55:21; Judges 16:15"> For as the thoughts of his heart are, so is he: Take food and drink, he says to you; but his heart is not with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The food which you have taken will come up again, and your pleasing words will be wasted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 9:7–8; Matthew 7:6; Proverbs 26:4–5"> Say nothing in the hearing of a foolish man, for he will put no value on the wisdom of your words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 22:28; Zechariah 7:10; Deuteronomy 27:17"> Do not let the landmark of the widow be moved, and do not go into the fields of those who have no father;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 22:23; Job 19:25; Jeremiah 51:36"> For their saviour is strong, and he will take up their cause against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 5:1–2; Proverbs 2:2–6; Proverbs 23:19"> Give your heart to teaching, and your ears to the words of knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 13:24; Proverbs 19:18; Proverbs 29:15"> Do not keep back training from the child: for even if you give him blows with the rod, it will not be death to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:5; Proverbs 22:15; 1 Corinthians 11:32"> Give him blows with the rod, and keep his soul safe from the underworld.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 23:24–25; 2 John 1:4; Proverbs 29:3"> My son, if your heart becomes wise, I, even I, will be glad in heart;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 8:6; Ephesians 5:4; James 3:2"> And my thoughts in me will be full of joy when your lips say right things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 28:14; 2 Corinthians 7:1; 1 Peter 1:17"> Have no envy of sinners in your heart, but keep in the fear of the Lord all through the day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 10:35; Jeremiah 29:11; Psalms 9:18"> For without doubt there is a future, and your hope will not be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 23:26; Proverbs 23:12; Proverbs 4:10–23"> Give ear, my son, and be wise, guiding your heart in the right way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 21:34; Ephesians 5:18; Isaiah 5:22"> Do not be among those who give themselves to wine-drinking, or among those who make themselves full with meat:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 21:17; Proverbs 19:15; Deuteronomy 21:20"> For those who take delight in drink and feasting will come to be in need; and through love of sleep a man will be poorly clothed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 30:17; Proverbs 1:8; John 19:26–27"> Give ear to your father whose child you are, and do not keep honour from your mother when she is old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 18:15; Matthew 13:44; Psalms 119:72"> Get for yourself that which is true, and do not let it go for money; get wisdom and teaching and good sense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 15:20; Proverbs 23:15–16"> The father of the upright man will be glad, and he who has a wise child will have joy because of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 17:25; Luke 11:27–28; Luke 1:58"> Let your father and your mother be glad, let her who gave you birth have joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 119:2; Psalms 119:9–11; Deuteronomy 6:5"> My son, give me your heart, and let your eyes take delight in my ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 22:14"> For a loose woman is a deep hollow, and a strange woman is a narrow water-hole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Proverbs 7:12; Proverbs 22:14"> Yes, she is waiting secretly like a beast for its food, and deceit by her is increased among men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 5:18; Isaiah 5:11; Proverbs 23:21"> Who says, Oh! who says, Ah! who has violent arguments, who has grief, who has wounds without cause, whose eyes are dark?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ephesians 5:18; Isaiah 5:11; Proverbs 20:1"> Those who are seated late over the wine: those who go looking for mixed wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 John 2:16; 2 Samuel 11:2; Psalms 119:37"> Keep your eyes from looking on the wine when it is red, when its colour is bright in the cup, when it goes smoothly down:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:8; Isaiah 28:7–8; Amos 9:3"> In the end, its bite is like that of a snake, its wound like the wound of a poison-snake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jude 1:12–13; Psalms 69:12; Daniel 5:4"> Your eyes will see strange things, and you will say twisted things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2–7; 1 Kings 20:16–22; 1 Samuel 30:16–17"> Yes, you will be like him who takes his rest on the sea, or on the top of a sail-support.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 56:12; Jeremiah 5:3; 2 Peter 2:22"> They have overcome me, you will say, and I have no pain; they gave me blows without my feeling them: when will I be awake from my wine? I will go after it again.</span>
