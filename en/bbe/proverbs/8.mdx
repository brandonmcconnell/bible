<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 1:20–21; Matthew 3:3; John 7:37"> Is not wisdom crying out, and the voice of knowledge sounding?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 9:14; Proverbs 9:3"> At the top of the highways, at the meeting of the roads, she takes her place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 18:20; Acts 5:20; Job 29:7"> Where the roads go into the town her cry goes out, at the doorways her voice is loud:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 11:15; John 3:16; Psalms 50:1"> I am crying out to you, O men; my voice comes to the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 1:22; Acts 26:18; Revelation 3:17–18"> Become expert in reason, O you simple ones; you foolish ones, take training to heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 1:26; Matthew 7:28–29; Proverbs 23:16"> Give ear, for my words are true, and my lips are open to give out what is upright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 37:30; John 8:14; John 17:17"> For good faith goes out of my mouth, and false lips are disgusting to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 7:46; Psalms 12:6; Proverbs 8:13"> All the words of my mouth are righteousness; there is nothing false or twisted in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 14:6; James 1:5; John 7:17"> They are all true to him whose mind is awake, and straightforward to those who get knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 2:4–5; Psalms 119:127; Psalms 119:72"> Take my teaching, and not silver; get knowledge in place of the best gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 3:14–15; Proverbs 4:5–7; Proverbs 16:16"> For wisdom is better than jewels, and all things which may be desired are nothing in comparison with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 28:26; Exodus 35:30–4; Romans 11:33"> I, wisdom, have made wise behaviour my near relation; I am seen to be the special friend of wise purposes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 97:10; Proverbs 16:6; 1 Samuel 2:3"> The fear of the Lord is seen in hating evil: pride, a high opinion of oneself, the evil way, and the false tongue, are unpleasing to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:19; Isaiah 9:6; Isaiah 40:14"> Wise design and good sense are mine; reason and strength are mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 13:1; Daniel 2:21; Matthew 28:18"> Through me kings have their power, and rulers give right decisions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Through me chiefs have authority, and the noble ones are judging in righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 14:23; John 14:21; Psalms 91:14"> Those who have given me their love are loved by me, and those who make search for me with care will get me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:16; Matthew 6:33; Luke 16:11–12"> Wealth and honour are in my hands, even wealth without equal and righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 3:14; Proverbs 10:20; Ecclesiastes 7:12"> My fruit is better than gold, even than the best gold; and my increase is more to be desired than silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:32; John 10:27–28; Psalms 32:8"> I go in the road of righteousness, in the way of right judging:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 1:4; Proverbs 24:4; Matthew 25:46"> So that I may give my lovers wealth for their heritage, making their store-houses full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 1:17; John 1:1–2; Proverbs 3:19"> The Lord made me as the start of his way, the first of his works in the past.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 17:24; Psalms 2:6; Genesis 1:26"> From eternal days I was given my place, from the birth of time, before the earth was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 3:20; Genesis 1:2; 1 John 4:9"> When there was no deep I was given birth, when there were no fountains flowing with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 90:2; Psalms 102:25–28; Job 38:4–11"> Before the mountains were put in their places, before the hills was my birth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 1:1–31"> When he had not made the earth or the fields or the dust of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 40:22; Job 26:10; Proverbs 3:19"> When he made ready the heavens I was there: when he put an arch over the face of the deep:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> When he made strong the skies overhead: when the fountains of the deep were fixed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 104:9; Jeremiah 5:22; Genesis 1:9–10"> When he put a limit to the sea, so that the waters might not go against his word: when he put in position the bases of the earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 1:1–3; John 1:18; Isaiah 42:1"> Then I was by his side, as a master workman: and I was his delight from day to day, playing before him at all times;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 16:3; John 13:1; Psalms 40:6–8"> Playing in his earth; and my delight was with the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 11:28; Psalms 119:1–2; Psalms 1:1–4"> Give ear to me then, my sons: for happy are those who keep my ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 4:1; Psalms 81:11–12; Proverbs 5:1"> Take my teaching and be wise; do not let it go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 11:28; Matthew 7:24; Proverbs 3:13"> Happy is the man who gives ear to me, watching at my doors day by day, keeping his place by the pillars of my house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 3:36; 1 John 5:11–12; Proverbs 12:2"> For whoever gets me gets life, and grace from the Lord will come to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 15:32; Proverbs 1:31; 1 Corinthians 16:22"> But he who does evil to me, does wrong to his soul: all my haters are in love with death.</span>
