<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 4:5; John 14:21–24; Deuteronomy 6:6–9"> My son, keep my teaching in your memory, and my rules in your heart:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1–3; Proverbs 4:10; Psalms 91:16"> For they will give you increase of days, years of life, and peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 7:3; 2 Corinthians 3:3; Hebrews 10:16"> Let not mercy and good faith go from you; let them be hanging round your neck, recorded on your heart;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 2:52; 1 Samuel 2:26; Romans 14:18"> So you will have grace and a good name in the eyes of God and men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 37:5; Jeremiah 17:7–8; Proverbs 28:26"> Put all your hope in God, not looking to your reason for support.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 16:3; Psalms 32:8; Philippians 4:6"> In all your ways give ear to him, and he will make straight your footsteps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 28:28; Romans 12:16; Proverbs 26:12"> Put no high value on your wisdom: let the fear of the Lord be before you, and keep yourself from evil:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 4:22; Psalms 147:3; Proverbs 16:24"> This will give strength to your flesh, and new life to your bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:2–15; 1 Corinthians 16:2; Exodus 23:19"> Give honour to the Lord with your wealth, and with the first-fruits of all your increase:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Deuteronomy 28:8; Proverbs 22:9"> So your store-houses will be full of grain, and your vessels overflowing with new wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 5:17; Revelation 3:19; Hebrews 12:5–12"> My son, do not make your heart hard against the Lord's teaching; do not be made angry by his training:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:5; Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 29:17"> For to those who are dear to him the Lord says sharp words, and makes the son in whom he has delight undergo pain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 8:32–35; Proverbs 4:5–9; Ecclesiastes 9:15–18"> Happy is the man who makes discovery of wisdom, and he who gets knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 16:16; Proverbs 8:19; Job 28:13–19"> For trading in it is better than trading in silver, and its profit greater than bright gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 8:11; Job 28:18; Psalms 73:25–26"> She is of more value than jewels, and nothing for which you may have a desire is fair in comparison with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 3:2; 2 Corinthians 6:10; Proverbs 22:4"> Long life is in her right hand, and in her left are wealth and honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 1:79; Isaiah 26:3; Philippians 4:8–9"> Her ways are ways of delight, and all her goings are peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 11:30; Revelation 2:7; Proverbs 15:4"> She is a tree of life to all who take her in their hands, and happy is everyone who keeps her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 104:24; Jeremiah 10:12; John 1:3"> The Lord by wisdom put in position the bases of the earth; by reason he put the heavens in their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 7:11; Psalms 65:9–12; Job 36:27–28"> By his knowledge the deep was parted, and dew came dropping from the skies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 1:8; Deuteronomy 6:6–9; Deuteronomy 32:46–47"> My son, keep good sense, and do not let wise purpose go from your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 4:22; Proverbs 1:9; Isaiah 38:16"> So they will be life for your soul, and grace for your neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 4:12; Proverbs 10:9; Psalms 121:3"> Then you will go safely on your way, and your feet will have no cause for slipping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 3:5; Psalms 4:8; Proverbs 6:22"> When you take your rest you will have no fear, and on your bed sleep will be sweet to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10–14; Psalms 112:7; 1 Peter 3:14"> Have no fear of sudden danger, or of the storm which will come on evil-doers:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 91:3; 1 Samuel 2:9; Proverbs 14:26"> For the Lord will be your hope, and will keep your foot from being taken in the net.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Galatians 6:10; James 2:15–16; Romans 13:7"> Do not keep back good from those who have a right to it, when it is in the power of your hand to do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 19:13; Deuteronomy 24:12–15; 2 Corinthians 8:11"> Say not to your neighbour, Go, and come again, and tomorrow I will give; when you have it by you at the time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 6:14; Proverbs 16:29–30; Psalms 55:20"> Do not make evil designs against your neighbour, when he is living with you without fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:24; Proverbs 29:22; Romans 12:18–21"> Do not take up a cause at law against a man for nothing, if he has done you no wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 24:1; Psalms 37:1; Galatians 5:21"> Have no envy of the violent man, or take any of his ways as an example.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 25:14; John 15:15; Proverbs 11:20"> For the wrong-hearted man is hated by the Lord, but he is a friend to the upright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 37:22; Leviticus 26:14–46; Job 8:6–7"> The curse of the Lord is on the house of the evil-doer, but his blessing is on the tent of the upright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="James 4:6; 1 Peter 5:5; Psalms 138:6"> He makes sport of the men of pride, but he gives grace to the gentle-hearted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 132:18; Isaiah 65:13–15; Psalms 73:24"> The wise will have glory for their heritage, but shame will be the reward of the foolish.</span>
