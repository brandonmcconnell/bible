<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 15:10; Proverbs 9:7–8; Psalms 119:97–100"> A lover of training is a lover of knowledge; but a hater of teaching is like a beast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 8:35; Acts 11:24; Psalms 112:5"> A good man has grace in the eyes of the Lord; but the man of evil designs gets punishment from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 10:25; Ephesians 3:17; Proverbs 12:12"> No man will make himself safe through evil-doing; but the root of upright men will never be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 14:30; Proverbs 31:10–25; Proverbs 14:1"> A woman of virtue is a crown to her husband; but she whose behaviour is a cause of shame is like a wasting disease in his bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 139:23; Isaiah 55:7; Psalms 140:1–3"> The purposes of upright men are right, but the designs of evil-doers are deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 14:3; Jeremiah 5:26; Proverbs 1:11–19"> The words of sinners are destruction for the upright; but the mouth of upright men is their salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:24–27; Proverbs 11:21; Proverbs 10:25"> Evil-doers are overturned and never seen again, but the house of upright men will keep its place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:18; 1 Corinthians 4:5; 1 Corinthians 3:10–15"> A man will be praised in the measure of his wisdom, but a wrong-minded man will be looked down on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 14:11; Proverbs 13:7"> He who is of low position and has a servant, is better than one who has a high opinion of himself and is in need of bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:4; Genesis 33:13–14; Numbers 22:28–32"> An upright man has thought for the life of his beast, but the hearts of evil-doers are cruel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 28:19; Proverbs 14:23; 1 Thessalonians 4:11–12"> He who does work on his land will not be short of bread; but he who goes after foolish men is without sense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:7–8; John 15:5; Luke 8:13–15"> The resting-place of the sinner will come to destruction, but the root of upright men is for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 21:23; Proverbs 11:8; Psalms 64:8"> In the sin of the lips is a net which takes the sinner, but the upright man will come out of trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 18:20–21; Isaiah 3:10–11; Proverbs 13:2"> From the fruit of his mouth will a man have good food in full measure, and the work of a man's hands will be rewarded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 3:7; Proverbs 26:12; Proverbs 14:16"> The way of the foolish man seems right to him? but the wise man gives ear to suggestions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 29:11; James 1:19; Proverbs 10:12"> A foolish man lets his trouble be openly seen, but a sharp man keeps shame secret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 14:5; Proverbs 14:25; Proverbs 24:28"> The breathing out of true words gives knowledge of righteousness; but a false witness gives out deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 16:24; Proverbs 15:4; Psalms 57:4"> There are some whose uncontrolled talk is like the wounds of a sword, but the tongue of the wise makes one well again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 19:9; Psalms 52:4–5; Job 20:5"> True lips are certain for ever, but a false tongue is only for a minute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 5:9; 1 Peter 3:8–13; Proverbs 12:12"> Deceit is in the heart of those whose designs are evil, but for those purposing peace there is joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 3:13; Psalms 91:10; Romans 8:28"> No trouble will come to upright men, but sinners will be full of evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 5:6; Proverbs 11:20; Revelation 22:15"> False lips are hated by the Lord, but those whose acts are true are his delight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 13:16; Proverbs 11:13; Proverbs 10:19"> A sharp man keeps back his knowledge; but the heart of foolish men makes clear their foolish thoughts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 12:27; Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 21:25–26"> The hand of the ready worker will have authority, but he who is slow in his work will be put to forced work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 16:24; Proverbs 15:13; Proverbs 17:22"> Care in the heart of a man makes it weighted down, but a good word makes it glad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 John 2:26; Matthew 5:46–48; Proverbs 17:27"> The upright man is a guide to his neighbour, but the way of evil-doers is a cause of error to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 26:15; Proverbs 15:16"> He who is slow in his work does not go in search of food; but the ready worker gets much wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="3 John 1:11; Romans 5:21; Ezekiel 18:20–24"> In the road of righteousness is life, but the way of the evil-doer goes to death.</span>
