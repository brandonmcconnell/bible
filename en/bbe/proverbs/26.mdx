<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 19:10; Proverbs 28:16; Esther 4:6"> Like snow in summer and rain when the grain is being cut, so honour is not natural for the foolish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 23:8; Nehemiah 13:2; Psalms 109:28"> As the sparrow in her wandering and the swallow in her flight, so the curse does not come without a cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 10:13; Psalms 32:9; 1 Corinthians 4:21"> A whip for the horse, a mouth-bit for the ass, and a rod for the back of the foolish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 26:5; 1 Peter 3:9; Jude 1:9"> Do not give to the foolish man a foolish answer, or you will be like him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 12:16; Matthew 16:1–4; Proverbs 28:11"> Give a foolish man a foolish answer, or he will seem wise to himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 10:26; Proverbs 25:13; Proverbs 13:17"> He who sends news by the hand of a foolish man is cutting off his feet and drinking in damage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 26:9; Psalms 64:8; Proverbs 17:7"> The legs of one who has no power of walking are hanging loose; so is a wise saying in the mouth of the foolish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 26:1; Proverbs 30:22; Proverbs 19:10"> Giving honour to a foolish man is like attempting to keep a stone fixed in a cord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 23:35"> Like a thorn which goes up into the hand of a man overcome by drink, so is a wise saying in the mouth of a foolish man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 2:6; Proverbs 11:31"> Like an archer wounding all who go by, is a foolish man overcome by drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 2:22; Matthew 12:45; Exodus 8:15"> Like a dog going back to the food which he has not been able to keep down, is the foolish man doing his foolish acts over again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 29:20; 1 Corinthians 3:18–19; Proverbs 26:5"> Have you seen a man who seems to himself to be wise? There is more hope for the foolish than for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 22:13; Proverbs 19:15; Proverbs 15:19"> The hater of work says, There is a lion in the way; a lion is in the streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 6:12; Proverbs 12:27; Proverbs 12:24"> A door is turned on its pillar, and the hater of work on his bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 19:24"> The hater of work puts his hand deep into the basin: lifting it again to his mouth is a weariness to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 26:12; 1 Peter 3:15; Proverbs 12:15"> The hater of work seems to himself wiser than seven men who are able to give an answer with good sense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 20:3; 2 Timothy 2:23–24; Luke 12:14"> He who gets mixed up in a fight which is not his business, is like one who takes a dog by the ears while it is going by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 25:18; Isaiah 50:11; Proverbs 7:23"> As one who is off his head sends about flaming sticks and arrows of death,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 5:4; Proverbs 10:23; Proverbs 15:21"> So is the man who gets the better of his neighbour by deceit, and says, Am I not doing so in sport?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 22:10; James 3:6; Proverbs 16:28"> Without wood, the fire goes out; and where there is no secret talk, argument is ended.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 15:18; Proverbs 29:22; 2 Samuel 20:1"> Like breath on coals and wood on fire, so a man given to argument gets a fight started.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 18:8; Proverbs 20:19; Ezekiel 22:9"> The words of one who says evil of his neighbour secretly are like sweet food, they go down into the inner parts of the stomach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 22:47–48; Luke 11:39; Proverbs 10:18"> Smooth lips and an evil heart are like a vessel of earth plated with silver waste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 12:20; Proverbs 10:18; Psalms 41:6"> With his lips the hater makes things seem what they are not, but deceit is stored up inside him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 6:16–19; Jeremiah 12:6; Psalms 28:3"> When he says fair words, have no belief in him; for in his heart are seven evils:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 55:21–23; 1 Samuel 18:17; 1 Samuel 18:21"> Though his hate is covered with deceit, his sin will be seen openly before the meeting of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 28:10; Psalms 9:15; Psalms 7:15–16"> He who makes a hole in the earth will himself go falling into it: and on him by whom a stone is rolled the stone will come back again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 8:44–49; Luke 20:20–21; John 10:32–33"> A false tongue has hate for those who have clean hearts, and a smooth mouth is a cause of falling.</span>
