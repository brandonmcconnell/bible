<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Luke 1:76; Mark 1:2–3"> See, I am sending my servant, and he will make ready the way before me; and the Lord, whom you are looking for, will suddenly come to his Temple; and the angel of the agreement, in whom you have delight, see, he is coming, says the Lord of armies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 13:9; Isaiah 4:4; 1 Corinthians 3:13–15"> But by whom may the day of his coming be faced? and who may keep his place when he is seen? for he is like the metal-tester's fire and the cleaner's soap.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 1:25; Proverbs 17:3; Zechariah 13:9"> He will take his seat, testing and cleaning the sons of Levi, burning away the evil from them as from gold and silver; so that they may make offerings to the Lord in righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:1–3; Isaiah 56:7; Zechariah 8:3"> Then the offering of Judah and Jerusalem will be pleasing to the Lord, as in days gone by, and as in past years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 5:8–9; Hebrews 13:4; Deuteronomy 5:11"> And I will come near to you for judging; I will quickly be a witness against the wonder-workers, against those who have been untrue in married life, against those who take false oaths; against those who keep back from the servant his payment, and who are hard on the widow and the child without a father, who do not give his rights to the man from a strange country, and have no fear of me, says the Lord of armies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Hebrews 13:8; James 1:17"> For I am the Lord, I am unchanged; and so you, O sons of Jacob, have not been cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 1:3; Romans 10:21; Romans 10:3"> From the days of your fathers you have been turned away from my rules and have not kept them. Come back to me, and I will come back to you, says the Lord of armies. But you say, How are we to come back?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 3:9–10; Matthew 22:21; Mark 12:17"> Will a man keep back from God what is right? But you have kept back what is mine. But you say, What have we kept back from you? Tenths and offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:15–19; Haggai 1:6–11; Haggai 2:14–17"> You are cursed with a curse; for you have kept back from me what is mine, even all this nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:6–8; Matthew 6:33; Proverbs 3:9–10"> Let your tenths come into the store-house so that there may be food in my house, and put me to the test by doing so, says the Lord of armies, and see if I do not make the windows of heaven open and send down such a blessing on you that there is no room for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:14; Joel 2:22; Zechariah 8:12"> And on your account I will keep back the locusts from wasting the fruits of your land; and the fruit of your vine will not be dropped on the field before its time, says the Lord of armies</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 61:9; Isaiah 62:4; Zechariah 8:23"> And you will be named happy by all nations: for you will be a land of delight, says the Lord of armies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Malachi 2:17; Isaiah 37:23; Romans 9:20"> Your words have been strong against me, says the Lord. And still you say, What have we said against you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 58:3; Zephaniah 1:12; Psalms 73:8–13"> You have said, It is no use worshipping God: what profit have we had from keeping his orders, and going in clothing of sorrow before the Lord of armies?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Jeremiah 12:1–2; Malachi 2:17"> And now to us the men of pride seem happy; yes, the evil-doers are doing well; they put God to the test and are safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 20:12; Psalms 56:8; Malachi 4:2"> Then those in whom was the fear of the Lord had talk together: and the Lord gave ear, and it was recorded in a book to be kept in mind before him, for those who had the fear of the Lord and gave thought to his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 19:5; Deuteronomy 7:6; 2 Corinthians 6:18"> And they will be mine, says the Lord, in the day when I make them my special property; and I will have mercy on them as a man has mercy on his son who is his servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 25:46; Psalms 58:10–11; Genesis 18:25"> Then you will again see how the upright man is different from the sinner, and the servant of God from him who is not.</span>
