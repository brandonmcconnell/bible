<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:1; Philippians 4:16; Acts 20:4"> Now when they had gone through Amphipolis and Apollonia they came to Thessalonica, where there was a Synagogue of the Jews:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 9:20; Acts 17:17; Acts 18:4"> And Paul, as he generally did, went in to them, and on three Sabbath days had discussions with them from the holy Writings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 18:28; Acts 9:22; Luke 24:32"> Saying to them clearly and openly that Christ had to be put to death and come back to life again; and that this Jesus, whom, he said, I am preaching to you, is the Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 17:12; Acts 13:43; Acts 14:4"> And some of them had faith, and were joined to Paul and Silas; and a number of the God-fearing Greeks, and some of the chief women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 5:26; Acts 17:13; Romans 16:21"> But the Jews, being moved with envy, took with them certain low persons from among the common people, and getting together a great number of people, made an outcry in the town, attacking the house of Jason with the purpose of taking them out to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 24:5; Acts 16:19–21; Acts 28:22"> And when they were not able to get them, they took Jason and some of the brothers by force before the rulers of the town, crying, These men, who have made trouble all over the world have now come here;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 23:2; Acts 16:21; John 19:12"> Whom Jason has taken into his house: and they are acting against the orders of Caesar, saying that there is another king, Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 2:3; John 11:48"> And hearing these things the people and the rulers of the town were troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 17:5"> And having made Jason and the others give an undertaking to keep the peace, they let them go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 17:2; 1 Thessalonians 2:2; Acts 17:13–14"> And the brothers straight away sent Paul and Silas away by night to Beroea: and they, when they came there, went to the Synagogue of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 34:16; Proverbs 2:1–5; John 5:39"> Now these were more noble than the Jews of Thessalonica, for they gave serious attention to the word, searching in the holy Writings every day, to see if these things were so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 5:14; Acts 14:1; Acts 13:50"> And a number of them had faith, and no small number of the Greek women of high position and of the men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:14–16; Proverbs 28:25; Acts 6:12"> But when the Jews of Thessalonica had news that Paul was preaching the word at Beroea, they came there, troubling the people and working them up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 17:10; Matthew 10:23; Acts 9:25"> So the brothers sent Paul straight away to the sea: but Silas and Timothy kept there still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 18:5; 1 Thessalonians 3:1; Acts 18:1"> But those who went with Paul took him as far as Athens, and then went away, with orders from him to Silas and Timothy to come to him quickly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 119:158; 2 Peter 2:7; Psalms 119:136"> Now while Paul was waiting for them at Athens, his spirit was troubled, for he saw all the town full of images of the gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 13:16; Acts 14:1–4; Acts 17:2–4"> So he had discussions in the Synagogue with the Jews and God-fearing Gentiles, and every day in the market-place with those who were there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:20–21; Colossians 2:8; Luke 11:53"> And some of those who were supporters of the theories of the Epicureans and the Stoics, had a meeting with him. And some said, What is this talker of foolish words saying? And others, He seems to be a preacher of strange gods: because he was preaching of Jesus and his coming back from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 1:27; Acts 17:22; Acts 24:24"> And they took him to Mars' Hill, saying, Will you make clear to us what is this new teaching of yours?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:23; Hosea 8:12; 1 Corinthians 2:14"> For you seem to us to say strange things, and we have a desire to get the sense of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:16–17; Colossians 4:5; Ephesians 5:16"> (Now all the Athenians and the men from other lands who come there were giving all their time to talking or hearing of anything new.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 25:19; Acts 19:35; Acts 17:16"> And Paul got to his feet on Mars' Hill and said, O men of Athens, I see that you are overmuch given to fear of the gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 4:22; Galatians 4:8–9; John 17:3"> For when I came by, I was looking at the things to which you give worship, and I saw an altar with this writing on it, TO THE GOD OF WHOM THERE IS NO KNOWLEDGE. Now, what you, without knowledge, give worship to, I make clear to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 8:27; Deuteronomy 10:14; Acts 7:48"> The God who made the earth and everything in it, he, being Lord of heaven and earth, is not housed in buildings made with hands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 50:8–13; Genesis 2:7; Isaiah 42:5"> And he is not dependent on the work of men's hands, as if he had need of anything, for he himself gives to all life and breath and all things;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:7–8; Genesis 3:20; Romans 5:12–19"> And he has made of one blood all the nations of men living on all the face of the earth, ordering their times and the limits of their lands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:23–24; Deuteronomy 4:7; Acts 14:17"> So that they might make search for God, in order, if possible, to get knowledge of him and make discovery of him, though he is not far from every one of us:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 12:10; Colossians 1:17; Hebrews 1:3"> For in him we have life and motion and existence; as certain of your verse writers have said, For we are his offspring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 40:25; Exodus 20:4; Psalms 115:4–8"> If then we are the offspring of God, it is not right for us to have the idea that God is like gold or silver or stone, formed by the art or design of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 14:16; Titus 2:11–12; Luke 24:47"> Those times when men had no knowledge were overlooked by God; but now he gives orders to all men in every place to undergo a change of heart:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 25:31–46; 2 Peter 3:7; Romans 2:16"> Because a day has been fixed in which all the world will be judged in righteousness by the man who has been marked out by him for this work; of which he has given a sign to all men by giving him back from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 17:18; Acts 26:8; Acts 25:19"> Now on hearing about the coming back from death, some of them made sport of it, but others said, Let us go more fully into this another time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And so Paul went away from among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 17:19; Romans 11:5–6; Acts 13:48"> But some men gave him their support: among whom was Dionysius the Areopagite, and a woman named Damaris, and others with them.</span>
