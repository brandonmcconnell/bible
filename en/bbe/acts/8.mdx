<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 22:20; Daniel 3:16–18; Acts 5:40"> And Saul gave approval to his death. Now at that time a violent attack was started against the church in Jerusalem; and all but the Apostles went away into all parts of Judaea and Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 50:10–11; Genesis 23:2; Jeremiah 22:18"> And God-fearing men put Stephen's body in its last resting-place, making great weeping over him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13; Galatians 1:13; 1 Corinthians 15:9"> But Saul was burning with hate against the church, going into every house and taking men and women and putting them in prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:23; Acts 11:19; Acts 8:1"> But those who had gone in flight went everywhere preaching the word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 6:5; Acts 21:8; Acts 8:35–36"> And Philip went down to Samaria and was teaching them about Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 4:41–42; 2 Chronicles 30:12; Matthew 20:15–16"> And all the people gave attention to the words which Philip said, when they saw the signs which he did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:1; Acts 14:8–10; Mark 16:17–18"> For unclean spirits came out from those who had them, crying with a loud voice; and a number of those who were ill and broken in body were made well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 13:52; Acts 13:48; Acts 8:39"> And there was much joy in that town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 13:6; Acts 5:36; John 7:18"> But there was a certain man named Simon, who in the past had been a wonder-worker and a cause of surprise to the people of Samaria, saying that he himself was a great man:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; 2 Peter 2:2; Revelation 13:3"> To whom they all gave attention, from the smallest to the greatest, saying, This man is that power of God which is named Great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 8:9; Galatians 3:1; Isaiah 44:25"> And they gave attention to him, because for a long time his wonder-working powers had kept them under his control.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 1:3; Matthew 28:19"> But when they had faith in the good news given by Philip about the kingdom of God and the name of Jesus Christ, a number of men and women had baptism.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 8:6–7; John 5:20; Acts 19:11"> And Simon himself had faith and, having had baptism, he went with Philip and, seeing the signs and the great wonders which he did, he was full of surprise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:13; 1 Thessalonians 3:2; Galatians 2:9"> Now when the Apostles at Jerusalem had news that the people of Samaria had taken the word of God into their hearts, they sent to them Peter and John;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 2:38; John 16:23–24; John 14:13–14"> Who, when they came there, made prayer for them, that the Holy Spirit might be given to them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 19:2; Matthew 28:19"> For up to that time he had not come on any of them; only baptism had been given to them in the name of the Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 19:6; Acts 6:6; 2 Timothy 1:6"> Then they put their hands on them, and the Holy Spirit came on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:5; Matthew 10:8; 2 Kings 8:9"> Now when Simon saw that the Holy Spirit was given through the touch of the Apostles' hands, he made them an offering of money, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 14:7–11; 3 John 1:9; John 5:44"> Give me this power, so that when I put my hands on anyone he may get the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 5:17; Matthew 10:8; Acts 2:38"> But Peter said, May your money come to destruction with you, because you had the idea that what is freely given by God may be got for a price.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Psalms 36:1; Matthew 6:22–24"> You have no part in this business, because your heart is not right before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:25–26; Daniel 4:27; Hebrews 4:12"> Let your heart be changed, and make prayer to God that you may have forgiveness for your evil thoughts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 58:6; Proverbs 5:22; John 8:34"> For I see that you are prisoned in bitter envy and the chains of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 8:8; Numbers 21:7; Job 42:8"> And Simon, answering, said, Make prayer for me to the Lord, so that these things which you have said may not come on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 20:21; Acts 26:22–23; Acts 1:8"> So they, having given their witness and made clear the word of the Lord, went back to Jerusalem, giving the good news on their way in a number of the small towns of Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 5:19; Hebrews 1:14; 2 Kings 1:3"> But an angel of the Lord said to Philip, Get up, and go to the south, to the road which goes from Jerusalem to Gaza, through the waste land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:10; Psalms 68:31; Isaiah 56:3–8"> And he went and there was a man of Ethiopia, a servant of great authority under Candace, queen of the Ethiopians, and controller of all her property, who had come up to Jerusalem for worship;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; Isaiah 1:1; Acts 28:25"> He was going back, seated in his carriage, and was reading the book of the prophet Isaiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 10:19; Acts 11:12; Acts 20:22–23"> And the Spirit said to Philip, Go near, and get on his carriage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 24:44–45; John 5:39; Ephesians 5:17"> And Philip, running up to him, saw that he was reading Isaiah the prophet, and said to him, Is the sense of what you are reading clear to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 10:14; Psalms 73:16–17; Isaiah 29:18–19"> And he said, How is that possible when I have no guide? And he made Philip get up by his side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7–8; 1 Peter 2:21–24; Jeremiah 51:40"> Now the place in the book where he was reading was this: He was taken, like a sheep, to be put to death; and as a lamb is quiet when its wool is being cut, so he made no sound:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Isaiah 53:8; Zechariah 13:7"> Being of low degree, his cause was not given a hearing: who has knowledge of his family? for his life is cut off from the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 15:15; Matthew 13:36; Matthew 2:2–4"> And the Ethiopian said to Philip, About whom are these words said by the prophet? about himself, or some other?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 24:27; Acts 18:28; Acts 28:23"> So Philip, starting from this writing, gave him the good news about Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 10:47; John 3:5; Titus 3:5–6"> And while they were going on their way, they came to some water, and the Ethiopian said, See, here is water; why may I not have baptism?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> &#91;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 4:1; John 3:22–23"> And he gave orders for the carriage to be stopped, and the two of them went down into the water, and Philip gave him baptism.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 18:12; 2 Kings 2:16; Ezekiel 8:3"> And when they came up out of the water, the Spirit of the Lord took Philip away; and the Ethiopian saw him no more, for he went on his way full of joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Acts 21:8; Acts 23:23; Acts 23:33"> But Philip came to Azotus, and went through all the towns, preaching the good news, till he came to Caesarea.</span>
