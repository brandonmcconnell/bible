<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 24:11; Acts 23:2; Acts 25:2"> And after five days, the high priest, Ananias, came with certain of the rulers, and an expert talker, one Tertullus; and they made a statement to Felix against Paul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 29:5; Proverbs 26:28; Acts 24:26–27"> And when he had been sent for, Tertullus, starting his statement, said, Because by you we are living in peace, and through your wisdom wrongs are put right for this nation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 23:26; Acts 26:25; Luke 1:3"> In all things and in all places we are conscious of our great debt to you, most noble Felix.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 11:32"> But, so that I may not make you tired, I make a request to you of your mercy, to give hearing to a short statement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 28:22; Acts 24:14; Acts 26:5"> For this man, in our opinion, is a cause of trouble, a maker of attacks on the government among Jews through all the empire, and a chief mover in the society of the Nazarenes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 24:12; Acts 21:27–32; John 18:31"> Who, in addition, was attempting to make the Temple unclean: whom we took,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> &#91;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 23:35; Acts 25:15–16; Acts 25:5"> And from whom you will be able, by questioning him yourself, to get knowledge of all the things which we say against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:16; Micah 7:2–3; Psalms 4:2"> And the Jews were in agreement with his statement, saying that these things were so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 3:15; 1 Samuel 2:25; Luke 18:2"> Then when the ruler had given him a sign to make his answer, Paul said, Because I have knowledge that you have been a judge over this nation for a number of years, I am glad to make my answer:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 24:1; Acts 21:18; Acts 23:11"> Seeing that you are able to make certain of the fact that it is not more than twelve days from the time when I came up to Jerusalem for worship;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 25:8; Acts 28:17; Acts 24:18"> And they have not seen me in argument with any man in the Temple, or working up the feelings of the people, in the Synagogues or in the town:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 25:7; 1 Peter 3:16"> And they are not able to give facts in support of the things which they say against me now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 9:2; Romans 3:21; Acts 3:13"> But this I will say openly to you, that I do give worship to the God of our fathers after that Way, which to them is not the true religion: but I have belief in all the things which are in the law and in the books of the prophets:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; John 5:28–29; 1 Thessalonians 4:14–16"> Hoping in God for that which they themselves are looking for, that there will be a coming back from the dead for upright men and wrongdoers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 23:1; 1 Timothy 1:5; 2 Corinthians 4:2"> And in this, I do my best at all times to have no reason for shame before God or men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 2:10; Romans 15:25–28; 1 Corinthians 16:1–4"> Now after a number of years I came to give help and offerings to my nation:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 26:21; Acts 21:26–30; Acts 24:12"> And having been made clean, I was in the Temple, but not with a great number of people, and not with noise: but there were certain Jews from Asia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 23:30; Acts 25:16"> And it would have been better if they had come here to make a statement, if they have anything against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Or let these men here present say what wrongdoing was seen in me when I was before the Sanhedrin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 23:6; Acts 4:2; Acts 26:6–8"> But only this one thing which I said among them in a loud voice, I am this day being judged on the question of the coming back from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 24:6; Acts 24:24; Acts 25:26"> But Felix, who had a more detailed knowledge of the Way, put them off, saying, When Lysias, the chief captain, comes down, I will give attention to your business.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 28:16; Acts 27:3; Proverbs 16:7"> And he gave orders to the captain to keep Paul under his control, and to let him have everything he had need of; and not to keep his friends from coming to see him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 20:21; Galatians 2:16; Mark 6:20"> But after some days, Felix came with Drusilla his wife, who was of the Jews by birth, and sent for Paul, and gave hearing to him about faith in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 16:8; 1 Peter 3:15; Acts 10:42"> And while he was talking about righteousness and self-control and the judging which was to come, Felix had great fear and said, Go away for the present, and when the right time comes I will send for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Proverbs 17:23; Isaiah 56:11"> For he was hoping that Paul would give him money: so he sent for him more frequently and had talk with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 25:9; Acts 25:14; Acts 12:3"> But after two years Porcius Festus took the place of Felix, who, desiring to have the approval of the Jews, kept Paul in chains.</span>
