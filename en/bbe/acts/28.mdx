<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 27:26; Acts 27:44; Acts 27:39"> And when we were safe, we made the discovery that the island was named Melita.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 1:14; Hebrews 13:2; Colossians 3:11"> And the simple people living there were uncommonly kind to us, for they made a fire for us, and took us in, because it was raining and cold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 23:33; Matthew 12:34; Acts 28:4"> But when Paul had got some sticks together and put them on the fire, a snake came out, because of the heat, and gave him a bite on the hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 13:2; Luke 13:4; John 7:24"> And when the people saw it hanging on his hand, they said to one another, Without doubt this man has put someone to death, and though he has got safely away from the sea, God will not let him go on living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 10:19; Mark 16:18; Psalms 91:13"> But shaking off the beast into the fire, he got no damage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 12:22; Acts 14:11–13; Matthew 21:9"> But they had the idea that they would see him becoming ill, or suddenly falling down dead; but after waiting a long time, and seeing that no damage came to him, changing their opinion, they said he was a god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:40–41; Luke 19:6–9; Acts 28:2"> Now near that place there was some land, the property of the chief man of the island, who was named Publius; who very kindly took us into his house as his guests for three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 9:40; Mark 6:5; James 5:14–16"> And the father of Publius was ill, with a disease of the stomach; to whom Paul went, and put his hands on him, with prayer, and made him well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 5:12; Mark 6:54–56; Acts 5:15"> And when this took place, all the others in the island who had diseases came and were made well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:6; Philippians 4:11–12; Matthew 6:31–34"> Then they gave us great honour, and, when we went away, they put into the ship whatever things we were in need of.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 27:6; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Isaiah 45:20"> And after three months we went to sea in a ship of Alexandria sailing under the sign of the Dioscuri, which had been at the island for the winter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And going into the harbour at Syracuse, we were waiting there for three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 27:13"> And from there, going about in a curve, we came to Rhegium: and after one day a south wind came up and on the day after we came to Puteoli:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 21:4; Acts 20:6; Acts 19:1"> Where we came across some of the brothers, who kept us with them for seven days; and so we came to Rome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 13:3; Romans 15:24; Exodus 4:14"> And the brothers, when they had news of us, came out from town as far as Appii Forum and the Three Taverns to have a meeting with us: and Paul, seeing them, gave praise to God and took heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 27:3; Acts 24:23; Acts 28:30–31"> And when we came into Rome, they let Paul have a house for himself and the armed man who kept watch over him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 25:8; Acts 6:14; Acts 25:10"> Then after three days he sent for the chief men of the Jews: and when they had come together, he said to them, My brothers, though I had done nothing against the people or the ways of our fathers, I was given, a prisoner from Jerusalem, into the hands of the Romans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 26:31–32; Acts 23:29; Acts 22:24–25"> Who, when they had put questions to me, were ready to let me go free, because there was no cause of death in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 26:32; Acts 25:21; Acts 25:10–12"> But when the Jews made protest against it, I had to put my cause into Caesar's hands; not because I have anything to say against my nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 6:20; 2 Timothy 2:9; Acts 26:6–7"> But for this reason I sent for you, to see and have talk with you: for because of the hope of Israel I am in these chains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 54:17; Isaiah 41:11; Isaiah 50:8"> And they said to him, We have not had letters from Judaea about you, and no one of the brothers has come to us here to give an account or say any evil about you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; Acts 24:14; 1 Peter 3:16"> But we have a desire to give hearing to your opinion: for as to this form of religion, we have knowledge that in all places it is attacked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 17:2–3; Acts 19:8; Acts 18:28"> And when a day had been fixed, they came to his house in great numbers; and he gave them teaching, giving witness to the kingdom of God, and having discussions with them about Jesus, from the law of Moses and from the prophets, from morning till evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 14:4; Acts 17:4–5; Acts 19:8–9"> And some were in agreement with what he said, but some had doubts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 15:7; 2 Peter 1:21; Mark 7:6"> And they went away, for there was a division among them after Paul had said this one thing: Well did the Holy Spirit say by the prophet Isaiah to your fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; Ezekiel 12:2; Isaiah 42:19–20"> Go to this people and say, Though you give ear, you will not get knowledge; and seeing, you will see, but the sense will not be clear to you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10"> For the heart of this people has become fat and their ears are slow in hearing and their eyes are shut; for fear that they might see with their eyes and give hearing with their ears and become wise in their hearts and be turned again to me, so that I might make them well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 2:30–32; Acts 26:17–18; Romans 3:29–30"> Be certain, then, that the salvation of God is sent to the Gentiles, and they will give hearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> &#91;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And for the space of two years, Paul was living in the house of which he had the use, and had talk with all those who went in to see him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 4:29; Ephesians 6:19–20; Matthew 4:23"> Preaching the kingdom of God and teaching about the Lord Jesus Christ without fear, and no orders were given that he was not to do so.</span>
