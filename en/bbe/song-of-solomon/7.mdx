<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 45:13; Exodus 35:35; Exodus 28:15"> How beautiful are your feet in their shoes, O king's daughter! The curves of your legs are like jewels, the work of the hands of a good workman:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 46:3; Psalms 45:16; Romans 7:4"> Your stomach is a store of grain with lilies round it, and in the middle a round cup full of wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Song of Solomon 4:5; Song of Solomon 6:6"> Your two breasts are like two young roes of the same birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Song of Solomon 4:4; Song of Solomon 4:8–9; Psalms 144:12"> Your neck is as a tower of ivory; your eyes like the waters in Heshbon, by the doorway of Bath-rabbim; your nose is as the tower on Lebanon looking over Damascus:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 35:2; Song of Solomon 4:1; Micah 7:14"> Your head is like Carmel, and the hair of your head is like purple, in whose net the king is prisoner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:15–16; Psalms 45:11; Song of Solomon 4:10"> How beautiful and how sweet you are, O love, for delight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Song of Solomon 4:5; Song of Solomon 1:13; Ephesians 4:13"> You are tall like a palm-tree, and your breasts are like the fruit of the vine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14; John 14:21–23; Song of Solomon 2:5"> I said, Let me go up the palm-tree, and let me take its branches in my hands: your breasts will be as the fruit of the vine, and the smell of your breath like apples;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Song of Solomon 5:16; Isaiah 62:8–9; Acts 4:31–32"> And the roof of your mouth like good wine flowing down smoothly for my loved one, moving gently over my lips and my teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 45:11; Song of Solomon 2:16; Song of Solomon 6:3"> I am for my loved one, and his desire is for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Song of Solomon 4:8; Song of Solomon 2:10–13; Song of Solomon 1:4"> Come, my loved one, let us go out into the field; let us take rest among the cypress-trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Song of Solomon 6:11; Ephesians 6:24; Song of Solomon 7:6"> Let us go out early to the vine-gardens; let us see if the vine is in bud, if it has put out its young fruit, and the pomegranate is in flower. There I will give you my love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 30:14; Matthew 13:52; 1 Peter 4:11"> The mandrakes give out a sweet smell, and at our doors are all sorts of good fruits, new and old, which I have kept for my loved one.</span>
