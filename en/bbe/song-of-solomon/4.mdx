<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Song of Solomon 7:4; Ephesians 4:15–16; Nehemiah 3:19"> Your neck is like the tower of David made for a store-house of arms, in which a thousand breastplates are hanging, breastplates for fighting-men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:16; Song of Solomon 6:3; Song of Solomon 7:3"> Your two breasts are like two young roes of the same birth, which take their food among the lilies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:17; Song of Solomon 4:14; Luke 1:78"> Till the evening comes, and the sky slowly becomes dark, I will go to the mountain of myrrh, and to the hill of frankincense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:15; Song of Solomon 4:1; Jude 1:24"> You are all fair, my love; there is no mark on you.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Song of Solomon 5:1–2; Song of Solomon 4:12; Proverbs 5:19–20"> You have taken away my heart, my sister, my bride; you have taken away my heart, with one look you have taken it, with one chain of your neck!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:2–4; Song of Solomon 7:6; Galatians 5:22"> How fair is your love, my sister! How much better is your love than wine, and the smell of your oils than any perfume!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Song of Solomon 5:1; Song of Solomon 7:9; Genesis 27:27"> Your lips are dropping honey; honey and milk are under your tongue; and the smell of your clothing is like the smell of Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 5:15–18; Revelation 21:27; 1 Corinthians 6:13"> A garden walled-in is my sister, my bride; a garden shut up, a spring of water stopped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:14; Song of Solomon 6:11; Ecclesiastes 2:5"> The produce of the garden is pomegranates; with all the best fruits, henna and spikenard,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 30:23; Song of Solomon 4:6; Song of Solomon 6:2"> Spikenard and safron; calamus and cinnamon, with all trees of frankincense; myrrh and aloes, with all the chief spices.</span>
