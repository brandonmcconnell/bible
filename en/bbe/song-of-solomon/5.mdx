<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 11:7; Romans 7:22–23; Proverbs 13:4"> I have put off my coat; how may I put it on? My feet are washed; how may I make them unclean?</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Song of Solomon 3:3; Matthew 21:33–41; Acts 5:40–41"> The keepers who go about the town overtook me; they gave me blows and wounds; the keepers of the walls took away my veil from me.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:8; Song of Solomon 6:1; Song of Solomon 6:9–10"> What is your loved one more than another, O fairest among women? What is your loved one more than another, that you say this to us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 45:2; Song of Solomon 2:1; Philippians 2:9–11"> My loved one is white and red, the chief among ten thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Song of Solomon 7:5; Song of Solomon 5:2; Daniel 7:9"> His head is as the most delicate gold; his hair is thick, and black as a raven.</span>
