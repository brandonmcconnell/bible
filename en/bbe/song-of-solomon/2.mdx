<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:13; Song of Solomon 4:7–8; Revelation 22:17"> My loved one said to me, Get up, my love, my fair one, and come away.</span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:10; Song of Solomon 7:11–13; Isaiah 55:10–11"> The fig-tree puts out her green fruit and the vines with their young fruit give a good smell. Get up from your bed, my beautiful one, and come away.</span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 13:32; 2 Peter 2:1–3; Song of Solomon 7:12"> Take for us the foxes, the little foxes, which do damage to the vines; our vines have young grapes.</span>
