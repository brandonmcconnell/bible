<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> All the sons of Perez living in Jerusalem were four hundred and sixty-eight men of good position.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And after him Gabbai, Sallai, nine hundred and twenty-eight.</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:12–13"> And their brothers who did the work of the house, eight hundred and twenty-two; and Adaiah, the son of Jeroham, the son of Pelaliah, the son of Amzi, the son of Zechariah, the son of Pashhur, the son of Malchijah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And his brothers, heads of families, two hundred and forty-two; and Amashsai, the son of Azarel, the son of Ahzai, the son of Meshillemoth, the son of Immer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And their brothers, men of war, a hundred and twenty-eight; and their overseer was Zabdiel, the son of Haggedolim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:14; 1 Chronicles 9:19"> And of the Levites: Shemaiah, the son of Hasshub, the son of Azrikam, the son of Hashabiah, the son of Bunni,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 6:2–3; Nehemiah 8:7; Ezra 8:33"> And Shabbethai and Jozabad, of the chiefs of the Levites, who were responsible for the outside business of the house of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:15; 1 Chronicles 25:1–6; Nehemiah 12:8–9"> And Mattaniah, the son of Mica, the son of Zabdi, the son of Asaph, who had to give the first note of the song of praise in prayer, and Bakbukiah, the second among his brothers, and Abda, the son of Shammua, the son of Galal, the son of Jeduthun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:1; Revelation 21:2; Daniel 9:24"> All the Levites in the holy town were two hundred and eighty-four.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:45; 1 Chronicles 9:17–22; Psalms 84:10"> In addition the door-keepers, Akkub, Talmon, and their brothers who kept watch at the doors, were a hundred and seventy-two.</span>

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 14:15; Joshua 15:21–22"> And for the daughter-towns with their fields, some of the men of Judah were living in Kiriath-arba and its daughter-towns, and in Dibon and its daughter-towns, and in Jekabzeel and its daughter-towns,</span>
