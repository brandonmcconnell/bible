<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:3; Matthew 6:9–10; James 5:16"> My desire is, first of all, that you will make requests and prayers and give praise for all men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9–13; Jeremiah 29:7; Ezra 6:10"> For kings and all those in authority; so that we may have a calm and quiet life in all fear of God and serious behaviour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; 1 Timothy 1:1; 1 Peter 2:20"> This is good and pleasing in the eyes of God our Saviour;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:32; 2 Peter 3:9; Ezekiel 18:23"> Whose desire is that all men may have salvation and come to the knowledge of what is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:6; Galatians 3:20; Hebrews 9:15"> For there is one God and one peacemaker between God and men, the man Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; 1 Peter 3:18; 1 John 2:1–2"> Who gave himself as an offering for all; witness of which was to be given at the right time;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 2 Timothy 1:11; Romans 9:1"> And of this I became a preacher and an Apostle (what I say is true, not false,) and a teacher of the Gentiles in the true faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 134:2; Psalms 63:4; Hebrews 10:22"> It is my desire, then, that in every place men may give themselves to prayer, lifting up holy hands, without wrath or argument.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 3:3–5; Titus 2:3–5; Psalms 149:4"> And that women may be dressed in simple clothing, with a quiet and serious air; not with twisted hair and gold or jewels or robes of great price;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; 1 Peter 3:3–5; Revelation 2:19"> But clothed with good works, as is right for women who are living in the fear of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:34–35; Ephesians 5:22–24; 1 Corinthians 11:3"> Let a woman quietly take the place of a learner and be under authority.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:34"> In my opinion it is right for a woman not to be a teacher, or to have rule over a man, but to be quiet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 2:18; 1 Corinthians 11:8–9; Genesis 1:27"> For Adam was first formed, then Eve;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:3; Genesis 3:6; Genesis 3:12–13"> And Adam was not taken by deceit, but the woman, being tricked, became a wrongdoer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Titus 2:12; Genesis 3:15–16; 1 Peter 4:7"> But if they go on in faith and love and holy self-control, she will be kept safe at the time of childbirth.</span>
