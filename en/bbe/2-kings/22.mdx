<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 15:39; 2 Chronicles 34:1–33; Jeremiah 1:2"> Josiah was eight years old when he became king; and he was ruling in Jerusalem for thirty-one years; his mother's name was Jedidah, daughter of Adaiah of Bozkath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:32; Joshua 1:7; 2 Kings 18:3"> He did what was right in the eyes of the Lord, walking in the ways of David his father, without turning to the right hand or to the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:3–33"> Now in the eighteenth year after he became king, Josiah sent Shaphan, the son of Azaliah, the son of Meshullam, the scribe, to the house of the Lord, saying to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 12:4; Mark 12:41–42; 2 Chronicles 34:9–18"> Go up to Hilkiah, the chief priest, and let him give out the money which is taken into the house of the Lord, which the keepers of the door have got together from the people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 12:11–14; 2 Kings 12:5; 2 Chronicles 24:12–13"> And let it be given to the overseers of the work of the Lord's house, to give to the workmen who are making good what was damaged in the house of the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> To the woodworkers and the builders and the stone-cutters; and for getting wood and cut stones for the building up of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 12:15; 1 Corinthians 4:2; Nehemiah 7:2"> They did not have to give any account of the money which was handed to them, for they made use of it with good faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:24–26; 2 Chronicles 34:14–28"> Then Hilkiah, the chief priest, said to Shaphan the scribe, I have made discovery of the book of the law in the house of the Lord. So Hilkiah gave it to Shaphan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:11; 2 Kings 25:22; 2 Kings 18:18"> Then, after reading it, Shaphan the scribe went in to the king and gave him an account of what had been done, saying, Your servants have given out the money which was in the house, and have given it to the overseers of the work of the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:21; Jeremiah 13:18; Deuteronomy 17:18–20"> Then Shaphan the scribe said to the king, Hilkiah the priest has given me a book; and he was reading it before the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:19; Joel 2:13; 2 Kings 22:19"> And the king, hearing the words of the book of the law, took his robe in his hands, violently parting it as a sign of his grief;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:24; 2 Kings 25:22; 2 Kings 19:2–3"> And he gave orders to Hilkiah the priest, and Ahikam, the son of Shaphan, and Achbor, the son of Micaiah, and Shaphan the scribe, and Asaiah the king's servant, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:23–28; Deuteronomy 31:17–18; Jeremiah 37:17"> Go and get directions from the Lord for me and for the people and for all Judah, about the words of this book which has come to light; for great is the wrath of the Lord which is burning against us, because our fathers have not given ear to the words of this book, to do all the things which are recorded in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 4:4; 2 Chronicles 34:22; Micah 6:4"> So Hilkiah the priest, and Ahikam and Achbor and Shaphan and Asaiah, went to Huldah the woman prophet, the wife of Shallum, the son of Tikvah, the son of Harhas, keeper of the robes, (now she was living in Jerusalem, in the second part of the town;) and they had talk with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 1:6; 2 Kings 1:16; Jeremiah 23:28"> And she said to them, The Lord, the God of Israel, says, Say to the man who sent you to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 9:11–14; Joshua 23:15; Deuteronomy 29:27"> These are the words of the Lord: See, I will send evil on this place and on its people, even everything which the king of Judah has been reading in the book;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 21:22; Jeremiah 7:20; Deuteronomy 29:24–28"> Because they have given me up, burning offerings to other gods and moving me to wrath by all the work of their hands; so my wrath will be on fire against this place, and will not be put out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:26–28; Malachi 3:16–17; Isaiah 3:10"> But to the king of Judah who sent you to get directions from the Lord, say, This is what the Lord, the God of Israel, has said: As to the words which have come to your ears,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 51:17; 1 Kings 21:29; Isaiah 57:15"> Because your heart was soft, and you made yourself low before me, when you had word of what I said against this place and its people, that they would become a waste and a curse, and you gave signs of grief, weeping before me: truly, I have given ear to you, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 37:37; Isaiah 57:1–2; 1 Chronicles 17:11"> For this cause I will let you go to your fathers and be put in your last resting-place in peace, and your eyes will not see all the evil which I will send on this place. So they took this news back to the king.</span>
