<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 18:25; 2 Kings 2:3; Nehemiah 5:2–5"> Now a certain woman, the wife of one of the sons of the prophets, came crying to Elisha and said, Your servant my husband is dead; and to your knowledge he was a worshipper of the Lord; but now, the creditor has come to take my two children as servants in payment of his debt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 17:12; James 2:5; 2 Kings 6:26–27"> Then Elisha said to her, What am I to do for you? say now, what have you in the house? And she said, Your servant has nothing in the house but a pot of oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 2:7; Psalms 81:10; John 16:24"> Then he said, Go out to all your neighbours and get vessels, a very great number of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 8:5–9; Matthew 6:6; Ephesians 3:20"> Then go in, and, shutting the door on yourself and your sons, put oil into all these vessels, putting on one side the full ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 5:11; Hebrews 11:7–8; 1 Kings 17:15–16"> So she went away, and when the door was shut on her and her sons, they took the vessels to her and she put oil into them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 14:20; 2 Kings 13:19; Luke 6:19"> And when all the vessels were full, she said to her son, Get me another vessel. And he said, There are no more. And the flow of oil was stopped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:7–12; 1 Kings 12:22; Romans 12:17"> So she came to the man of God and gave him word of what she had done. And he said, Go and get money for the oil and make payment of your debt, and let the rest be for the needs of yourself and your sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 19:18; Genesis 19:3; 2 Kings 4:11–12"> Now there came a day when Elisha went to Shunem, and there was a woman of high position living there, who made him come in and have a meal with her. And after that, every time he went by, he went into her house for a meal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 17:24; Proverbs 31:10–11; 2 Kings 4:7"> And she said to her husband, Now I see that this is a holy man of God, who comes by day after day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:13; Matthew 10:41–42; Matthew 25:40"> So let us make a little room on the wall; and put a bed there for him, and a table and a seat and a light; so that when he comes to us, he will be able to go in there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Now one day, when he had gone there, he went into the little room and took his rest there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 8:4–5; 2 Kings 3:11; 2 Kings 4:29–31"> And he said to Gehazi, his servant, Send for this Shunammite. So in answer to his voice she came before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:16–18; Philippians 4:18–19; Hebrews 6:10"> And he said to him, Now say to her, See, you have taken all this trouble for us; what is to be done for you? will you have any request made for you to the king or the captain of the army? But she said, I am living among my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 1:7; Genesis 15:2–3; 1 Samuel 1:8"> So he said, What then is to be done for her? And Gehazi made answer, Still there is this, she has no son and her husband is old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then he said, Send for her. And in answer to his voice she took her place at the door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 18:10; Luke 1:13; 2 Kings 4:28"> And Elisha said, At this time in the coming year you will have a son in your arms. And she said, No, my lord, O man of God, do not say what is false to your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 113:9; Luke 1:36; Genesis 21:1"> Then the woman became with child and gave birth to a son at the time named, in the year after, as Elisha had said to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ruth 2:4"> Now one day, when the child was older, he went out to his father to where the grain was being cut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 14:1–2; Jeremiah 4:19"> And he said to his father, My head, my head! And the father said to a servant, Take him in to his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 7:12; John 11:3; John 11:14"> And he took him in to his mother, and she took him on her knees and kept him there till the middle of the day, when his life went from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 4:32; 2 Kings 4:10; 2 Kings 4:7"> Then she went up and put him on the bed of the man of God, shutting the door on him, and went out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 9:38; 2 Kings 4:26; John 11:3"> And she said to her husband, Send me one of the servants and one of the asses so that I may go quickly to the man of God and come back again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 10:10; Numbers 28:11; 1 Chronicles 23:31"> And he said, Why are you going to him today? it is not a new moon or a Sabbath. But she said, It is well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 4:20; 1 Kings 13:13; 1 Samuel 25:19–20"> Then she made the ass ready and said to her servant, Keep driving on; do not make a stop without orders from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 2:25; Isaiah 35:2; 1 Kings 18:19"> So she went, and came to Mount Carmel, to the man of God. And when the man of God saw her coming in his direction, he said to Gehazi, his servant, See, there is the Shunammite;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 15:36; 2 Kings 4:23; 1 Samuel 17:18"> Go quickly to her, and on meeting her say to her, Are you well? and your husband and the child, are they well? And she said in answer, All is well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:3; Genesis 18:17; Amos 3:7"> And when she came to where the man of God was on the hill, she put her hands round his feet; and Gehazi came near with the purpose of pushing her away; but the man of God said, Let her be, for her soul is bitter in her; and the Lord has kept it secret from me, and has not given me word of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 30:1; 2 Kings 4:16"> Then she said, Did I make a request to my lord for a son? did I not say, Do not give me false words?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Kings 2:14; 1 Kings 18:46; Exodus 14:16"> Then he said to Gehazi, Make yourself ready, and take my stick in your hand, and go: if you come across anyone on the way, give him no blessing, and if anyone gives you a blessing, give him no answer. And put my stick on the child's face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ruth 1:16–18; 2 Kings 2:2; Exodus 33:12–16"> But the mother of the child said, As the Lord is living and as your soul is living, I will not go back without you. So he got up and went with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 11:11; Mark 5:39; Mark 9:19–29"> And Gehazi went on before them and put the stick on the child's face; but there was no voice, and no one gave attention. So he went back, and meeting him gave him the news, saying, The child is not awake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 8:52–53; John 11:17; 1 Kings 17:17"> And when Elisha came into the house he saw the child dead, stretched on his bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 6:6; 2 Kings 4:4; Acts 9:40"> So he went in, and shutting the door on the two of them, made prayer to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 17:21; Acts 20:10"> Then he got up on the bed, stretching himself out on the child, and put his mouth on the child's mouth, his eyes on his eyes and his hands on his hands; and the child's body became warm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Kings 8:5; 2 Kings 13:21; 1 Kings 17:22"> Then he came back, and after walking once through the house and back, he went up, stretching himself out on the child seven times; and the child's eyes became open.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Hebrews 11:35; 1 Kings 17:23; Luke 7:15"> And he gave orders to Gehazi, and said, Send for the Shunammite. And she came in answer to his voice. And he said, Take up your son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Hebrews 11:35; 2 Kings 2:15; 1 Kings 17:24"> And she came in, and went down on her face to the earth at his feet; then she took her son in her arms and went out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Kings 8:1; 2 Kings 2:3; 2 Kings 2:1"> And Elisha went back to Gilgal, now there was very little food in the land; and the sons of the prophets were seated before him. And he said to his servant, Put the great pot on the fire, and make soup for the sons of the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:21; Isaiah 5:4; Matthew 15:13"> And one went out into the field to get green plants and saw a vine of the field, and pulling off the fruit of it till the fold of his robe was full, he came back and put the fruit, cut up small, into the pot of soup, having no idea what it was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 16:18; Exodus 15:23; Exodus 10:17"> Then they gave the men soup from the pot. And while they were drinking the soup, they gave a cry, and said, O man of God, there is death in the pot; and they were not able to take any more food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Kings 2:21; Exodus 15:25; 1 Corinthians 1:25"> But he said, Get some meal. And he put it into the pot, and said, Now give it to the people so that they may have food. And there was nothing bad in the pot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:7; John 6:9; Matthew 14:16–21"> Now a man came from Baal-shalishah with an offering of first-fruits for the man of God, twenty barley cakes and garden fruit in his bag. And he said, Give these to the people for food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 9:13; Matthew 14:20; John 6:9"> But his servant said, How am I to put this before a hundred men? But he said, Give it to the people for food; for the Lord says, There will be food for them and some over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> So he put it before them, and they had a meal and there was more than enough, as the Lord had said.</span>
