<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Haggai 1:11; 2 Kings 4:31–35; Ruth 1:1"> Now Elisha had said to the woman whose son he had given back to life, Go now, with all the people of your house, and get a living-place for yourselves wherever you are able; for by the word of the Lord, there will be great need of food in the land; and this will go on for seven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:8; Judges 3:3; 1 Samuel 27:1–3"> So the woman got up and did as the man of God said; and she and the people of her house were living in the land of the Philistines for seven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:16; Luke 18:3–5; 2 Kings 4:13"> And when the seven years were ended, the woman came back from the land of the Philistines and went to the king with a request for her house and her land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 5:20–27; 2 Kings 2:14; 2 Kings 6:32"> Now the king was talking with Gehazi, the servant of the man of God, saying, Now, give me an account of all the great things Elisha has done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 4:35; Matthew 10:29–30; 2 Kings 6:26"> And while he was giving the king the story of how Elisha had given life to the dead, the woman whose son had come back to life came to the king with a request for her house and her land. And Gehazi said, My lord king, this is the woman and this is her son, whose life Elisha gave back to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Proverbs 21:1; 2 Samuel 9:7"> And in answer to the king's questions, the woman gave him all the story. So the king gave orders to one of his unsexed servants, saying, Give her back all her property, and all the produce of her fields from the day when she went away from the land up till now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 11:24; 2 Kings 6:24; 1 Kings 20:1"> And Elisha came to Damascus; and Ben-hadad, king of Aram, was ill; and they said to him, The man of God has come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 19:15; 2 Kings 1:2; 1 Samuel 9:7"> Then the king said to Hazael, Take an offering with you, and go to see the man of God and get directions from the Lord by him, saying, Am I going to get better from my disease?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 5:13; 2 Kings 13:14; 1 Kings 19:15"> So Hazael went to see him, taking with him forty camels with offerings on their backs of every sort of good thing from Damascus; and when he came before him, he said, Your son Ben-hadad, king of Aram, has sent me to you, saying, Will I get better from this disease?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 7:7; Genesis 2:17; Amos 7:1"> And Elisha said to him, Go, say to him, You will certainly get better; but the Lord has made it clear to me that only death is before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:41; Romans 9:2; Jeremiah 9:18"> And he kept his eyes fixed on him till he was shamed, and the man of God was overcome with weeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 13:16; Nahum 3:10; 2 Kings 15:16"> And Hazael said, Why is my lord weeping? Then he said in answer, Because I see the evil which you will do to the children of Israel: burning down their strong towns, putting their young men to death with the sword, smashing their little ones against the stones, and cutting open the women who are with child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:43; 1 Kings 19:15; 2 Samuel 9:8"> And Hazael said, How is it possible that your servant, who is only a dog, will do this great thing? And Elisha said, The Lord has made it clear to me that you will be king over Aram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 8:10; Matthew 26:16; 2 Kings 5:25"> Then he went away from Elisha and came in to his master, who said to him, What did Elisha say to you? And his answer was, He said that you would certainly get well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 8:13; 1 Kings 15:28; 1 Kings 11:26–37"> Now on the day after, Hazael took the bed-cover, and making it wet with water, put it over Ben-hadad's face, causing his death: and Hazael became king in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 1:17; 2 Kings 3:1; 2 Chronicles 21:1–20"> In the fifth year of Joram, the son of Ahab, king of Israel, Jehoram, the son of Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, became king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:5–10"> He was thirty-two years old when he became king; and he was ruling in Jerusalem for eight years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 8:26; 1 Kings 11:1–5; 2 Chronicles 21:13"> He went in the ways of the kings of Israel, as the family of Ahab did: for the daughter of Ahab was his wife; and he did evil in the eyes of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 11:36; 2 Samuel 7:12–13; Luke 1:32–33"> But it was not the Lord's purpose to send destruction on Judah, because of David his servant, to whom he had given his word that he would have a light for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 22:47; 2 Kings 3:9; 2 Kings 8:22"> In his time, Edom made themselves free from the rule of Judah, and took a king for themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:17"> Then Joram went over to Zair, with all his war-carriages; ... made an attack by night on the Edomites, whose forces were all round him, ... the captains of the war-carriages; and the people went in flight to their tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Kings 19:8; Joshua 21:13; Genesis 27:40"> So Edom made themselves free from the rule of Judah to this day. And at the same time, Libnah made itself free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 15:36; 1 Kings 11:41; 2 Chronicles 21:11–20"> Now the rest of the acts of Joram, and all he did, are they not recorded in the book of the history of the kings of Judah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:6; 2 Chronicles 25:23; 2 Chronicles 21:1"> And Joram went to rest with his fathers and was put into the earth with his fathers in the town of David: and Ahaziah his son became king in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 9:29; 2 Kings 8:16–17; 2 Chronicles 21:20–6"> In the twelfth year that Joram, the son of Ahab, was king of Israel, Ahaziah, the son of Jehoram, king of Judah, became king;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:2; 2 Kings 9:21–27; 2 Kings 11:1"> Ahaziah was twenty-two years old when he became king, and he was ruling in Jerusalem for one year. His mother's name was Athaliah, the daughter of Omri, king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:3–4; 2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Ecclesiastes 7:26"> He went in the ways of the family of Ahab, and did evil in the eyes of the Lord as the family of Ahab did, for he was a son-in-law of the family of Ahab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:5; 1 Kings 4:13; 2 Chronicles 19:2"> He went with Joram, the son of Ahab, to make war on Hazael, king of Aram, at Ramoth-gilead: and Joram was wounded by the Aramaeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Kings 9:15–16; 2 Kings 8:28; 2 Chronicles 22:5–7"> So King Joram went back to Jezreel to get well from the wounds which the bowmen had given him at Ramah, when he was fighting against Hazael, king of Aram. And Ahaziah, the son of Jehoram, king of Judah, went down to see Joram, the son of Ahab, in Jezreel, because he was ill.</span>
