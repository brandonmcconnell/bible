<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 3:9–11; 2 Chronicles 25:8; Jeremiah 51:20–23"> A crusher has come up before your face: keep a good look-out, let the way be watched, make yourself strong, let your power be greatly increased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 80:12–13; Ezekiel 37:23; Zephaniah 3:11"> For the Lord will make good the vine of Jacob, as well as the vine of Israel: for the wasters have made them waste and sent destruction on the branches of their vine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:14–15; Isaiah 63:1–3; Zechariah 6:2"> The body-covers of his fighting men have been made red, the men of war are clothed in bright red: the war-carriages are like flames of fire in the day when he gets ready, the horses are shaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:13; Ezekiel 26:10; Nahum 3:2–3"> The war-carriages are rushing through the streets, pushing against one another in the wide ways, looking like burning lights, running like thunder-flames.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:12; Jeremiah 51:27–28; Jeremiah 50:29"> He takes the record of his great men: they go falling on their way; they go quickly to the wall, the cover is made ready.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 45:1–2; 2 Peter 3:10–11"> The river doorways are forced open, and the king's house is flowing away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 38:14; Isaiah 59:11; Luke 23:48"> And the queen is uncovered, she is taken away and her servant-girls are weeping like the sound of doves, hammering on their breasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:13; Revelation 17:1; Jeremiah 46:5"> But Nineveh is like a pool of water whose waters are flowing away; Keep your place, they say; but no one is turning back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:12; Daniel 11:8; Nahum 2:12–13"> Take silver, take gold; for there is no end to the store; take for yourselves a weight of things to be desired.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joel 2:6; Psalms 22:14; Daniel 5:6"> Everything has been taken from her, all is gone, she has nothing more: the heart is turned to water, the knees are shaking, all are twisted in pain, and colour has gone from all faces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 5:29; Jeremiah 2:15; Isaiah 31:4"> Where is the lions' hole, the place where the young lions got their food, where the lion and the she-lion were walking with their young, without cause for fear?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:34; Psalms 17:12; Isaiah 10:6–14"> Food enough for his young and for his she-lions was pulled down by the lion; his hole was full of flesh and his resting-place stored with meat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:13; Psalms 46:9; Nahum 3:5"> See, I am against you, says the Lord of armies, and I will have your war-carriages burned in the smoke, and your young lions will be food for the sword: you will no longer get your food by force on the earth, and the voice of your she-lions will be stopped for ever.</span>
