<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 3:4; Isaiah 10:32; 2 Kings 14:7"> And they will send ... to the mountain of the daughter of Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 27:8; Judges 11:18; Jeremiah 48:20"> For the daughters of Moab will be like wandering birds, like a place from which the young birds have gone in flight, at the ways across the Arnon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 32:2; 1 Kings 18:4; Isaiah 25:4"> Give wise directions, make a decision; let your shade be as night in full day: keep safe those who are in flight; do not give up the wandering ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 14:4; Isaiah 51:13; Isaiah 9:4"> Let those who have been forced out of Moab have a resting-place with you; be a cover to them from him who is making waste their land: till the cruel ones are cut off, and wasting has come to an end, and those who take pleasure in crushing the poor are gone from the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 7:14; Micah 4:7; Isaiah 9:6–7"> Then a king's seat will be based on mercy, and one will be seated on it in the tent of David for ever; judging uprightly, and quick to do righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 2:1; Obadiah 1:3–4; Jeremiah 48:29–30"> We have had word of the pride of Moab, how great it is; how he is lifted up in pride and passion: his high words about himself are false.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 3:25; 1 Chronicles 16:3; Jeremiah 48:20"> For this cause everyone in Moab will give cries of grief for Moab: crushed to the earth, they will be weeping for the men of Kir-hareseth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 15:4; Numbers 32:38; Jeremiah 48:32"> For the fields of Heshbon are waste, the vine of Sibmah is dead; the lords of nations were overcome by the produce of her vines; her vine-plants went as far as Jazer, and came even to the waste land; her branches were stretched out to the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:12; Isaiah 15:4–5; Jeremiah 40:10"> For this cause my sorrow for the vine of Sibmah will be like the weeping for Jazer: my eyes are dropping water on you, O Heshbon and Elealeh! For they are sounding the war-cry over your summer fruits and the getting in of your grain;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:33; Judges 9:27; Amos 5:17"> And all joy is gone; no longer are they glad for the fertile field; and in the vine-gardens there are no songs or sounds of joy: the crushing of grapes has come to an end, and its glad cry has been stopped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 15:5; Jeremiah 48:36; Hosea 11:8"> For this cause the cords of my heart are sounding for Moab, and I am full of sorrow for Kir-heres.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 15:2; 1 Kings 18:29; Jeremiah 48:35"> And when Moab goes up to the high place, and makes prayer in the house of his god, it will have no effect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 44:8"> This is the word which the Lord said about Moab in the past.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 21:16; Jeremiah 48:46–47; Isaiah 23:9"> But now the Lord has said, In three years, the years of a servant working for payment, the glory of Moab, all that great people, will be turned to shame, and the rest of Moab will be very small and without honour.</span>
