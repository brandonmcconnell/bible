<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6–7; Jeremiah 33:15; Hebrews 1:8–9"> See, a king will be ruling in righteousness, and chiefs will give right decisions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 25:4; Isaiah 44:3; Isaiah 9:6"> And a man will be as a safe place from the wind, and a cover from the storm; as rivers of water in a dry place, as the shade of a great rock in a waste land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 29:18; Acts 26:18; 2 Corinthians 4:6"> And the eyes of those who see will not be shut, and those who have hearing will give ear to the word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 29:24; Luke 21:14–15; Matthew 16:17"> The man of sudden impulses will become wise in heart, and he whose tongue is slow will get the power of talking clearly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 5:20; Psalms 15:4; Malachi 3:18"> The foolish man will no longer be named noble, and they will not say of the false man that he is a man of honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 15:19; Matthew 23:13; Isaiah 3:15"> For the foolish man will say foolish things, having evil thoughts in his heart, working what is unclean, and talking falsely about the Lord, to keep food from him who is in need of it, and water from him whose soul is desiring it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:26–28; Matthew 26:59–60; Matthew 26:14–16"> The designs of the false are evil, purposing the destruction of the poor man by false words, even when he is in the right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:2; Acts 11:29–30; Luke 6:33–35"> But the noble-hearted man has noble purposes, and by these he will be guided.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 13:9; Isaiah 3:16; Isaiah 28:23"> Give ear to my voice, you women who are living in comfort; give attention to my words, you daughters who have no fear of danger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 7:23; Jeremiah 8:13; Zephaniah 1:13"> In not much more than a year, you, who are not looking for evil, will be troubled: for the produce of the vine-gardens will be cut off, and there will be no getting in of the grapes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 3:24; Isaiah 33:14; Isaiah 20:4"> Be shaking with fear, you women who are living in comfort; be troubled, you who have no fear of danger: take off your robes and put on clothing of grief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:15; Ezekiel 20:6; Nahum 2:7"> Have sorrow for the fields, the pleasing fields, the fertile vine;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 22:2; Hosea 9:6; Isaiah 7:23"> And for the land of my people, where thorns will come up; even for all the houses of joy in the glad town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 24:12; Isaiah 24:10; Isaiah 25:2"> For the fair houses will have no man living in them; the town which was full of noise will become a waste; the hill and the watchtower will be unpeopled for ever, a joy for the asses of the woods, a place of food for the flocks;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 29:17; Ezekiel 39:29; Isaiah 44:3"> Till the spirit comes on us from on high, and the waste land becomes a fertile field, and the fertile field is changed into a wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 94:14–15; 1 Peter 2:9–12; Isaiah 60:21"> Then in the waste land there will be an upright rule, and righteousness will have its place in the fertile field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 26:3; Psalms 119:165; Philippians 4:6–9"> And the work of righteousness will be peace; and the effect of an upright rule will be to take away fear for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 2:5; Hosea 2:18–23; Jeremiah 33:16"> And my people will be living in peace, in houses where there is no fear, and in quiet resting-places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 28:17; Isaiah 30:30; Isaiah 28:2"> But the tall trees will come down with a great fall, and the town will be low in a low place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:1; Isaiah 30:23–24; Isaiah 55:10–11"> Happy are you who are planting seed by all the waters, and sending out the ox and the ass.</span>
