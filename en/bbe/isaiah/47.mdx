<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:33; Psalms 137:8; Zechariah 2:7"> Come and take your seat in the dust, O virgin daughter of Babylon; come down from your seat of power, and take your place on the earth, O daughter of the Chaldaeans: for you will never again seem soft and delicate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4; Matthew 24:41; Exodus 11:5"> Take the crushing-stones and get the meal crushed: take off your veil, put away your robe, let your legs be uncovered, go through the rivers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nahum 3:5; Jeremiah 51:20–24; Jeremiah 50:27–28"> The shame of your unclothed condition will be seen by all: I will give punishment without mercy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; Isaiah 43:14; Isaiah 49:26"> Says the Lord who takes up our cause; the Lord of armies is his name, the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 18:16–19; Habakkuk 2:20; Isaiah 47:7"> Be seated in the dark without a word, O daughter of the Chaldaeans: for you will no longer be named, The Queen of Kingdoms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:9; Zechariah 1:15; Deuteronomy 28:50"> I was angry with my people, I put shame on my heritage, and gave them into your hands: you had no mercy on them; you put a cruel yoke on those who were old;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:29; Isaiah 47:5; Jeremiah 5:31"> And you said, I will be a queen for ever: you did not give attention to these things, and did not keep in mind what would come after.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:15; Isaiah 32:9; Isaiah 47:10"> So now take note of this, you who are given up to pleasure, living without fear of evil, saying in your heart, I am, and there is no one like me; I will never be a widow, or have my children taken from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 73:19; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Isaiah 47:12–13"> But these two things will come on you suddenly in one day, the loss of children and of husband: in full measure they will come on you, for all your secret arts, and all your wonders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 5:21; Isaiah 29:15; Romans 1:22"> For you had faith in your evil-doing; you said, No one sees me; by your wisdom and knowledge you have been turned out of the way: and you have said in your heart, I am, and there is no other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Revelation 18:9–10; Daniel 5:25–30"> Because of this evil will come on you, which may not be turned away for any price: and trouble will overtake you, from which no money will give salvation: destruction will come on you suddenly, without your knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 13:8–12; Nahum 3:4; Jeremiah 2:28"> Go on now with your secret arts, and all your wonder-working, to which you have given yourself up from your earliest days; it may be that they will be of profit to you, or by them you may put fear into your attackers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 44:25; Daniel 2:2–10; Ezekiel 24:12"> But your mind is troubled by the number of your guides: let them now come forward for your salvation: the measurers of the heavens, the watchers of the stars, and those who are able to say from month to month what things are coming on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Nahum 1:10; Ezekiel 15:7"> Truly, they have become like dry stems, they have been burned in the fire; they are not able to keep themselves safe from the power of the flame: it is not a coal for warming them, or a fire by which a man may be seated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 56:11; Ezekiel 27:12–25; Jeremiah 51:6–9"> Small profit have you had from those who, from your earliest days, got great profit out of you; they have gone in flight, every one straight before him, and you have no saviour.</span>
