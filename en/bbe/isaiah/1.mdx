<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 18:1–21; 2 Chronicles 26:1–33; Isaiah 6:1"> The vision of Isaiah, the son of Amoz, which he saw about Judah and Jerusalem, in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Deuteronomy 30:19; Micah 1:2"> Give ear, O heavens, and you, O earth, to the word which the Lord has said: I have taken care of my children till they became men, but their hearts have been turned away from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:7; Jeremiah 4:22; Isaiah 44:18"> Even the ox has knowledge of its owner, and the ass of the place where its master puts its food: but Israel has no knowledge, my people give no thought to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 5:24; Jeremiah 2:17; Jeremiah 2:13"> O nation full of sin, a people weighted down with crime, a generation of evil-doers, false-hearted children: they have gone away from the Lord, they have no respect for the Holy One of Israel, their hearts are turned back from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 9:8–11; Ezekiel 24:13; Jeremiah 5:3"> Why will you have more and more punishment? why keep on in your evil ways? Every head is tired and every heart is feeble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:14; Jeremiah 33:6; Matthew 9:12"> The body, from head to foot, is all diseased; it is a mass of open wounds, marks of blows, and broken flesh: the flow of blood has not been stopped, and no oil has been put on the wounds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Lamentations 5:2; Deuteronomy 28:43; Deuteronomy 28:33"> Your country has become waste; your towns are burned with fire; as for your land, it is overturned before your eyes, made waste and overcome by men from strange lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; John 12:15; Zechariah 2:10"> And the daughter of Zion has become like a tent in a vine-garden, like a watchman's house in a field of fruit, like a town shut in by armies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 9:29; Isaiah 37:31–32; Genesis 19:24"> If the Lord of armies had not kept some at least of us safe, we would have been like Sodom, and the fate of Gomorrah would have been ours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 11:8; Ezekiel 16:49; Amos 9:7"> Give ear to the word of the Lord, you rulers of Sodom; let your hearts be turned to the law of our God, you people of Gomorrah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:22; Amos 5:21; Jeremiah 6:20"> What use to me is the number of the offerings which you give me? says the Lord; your burned offerings of sheep, and the best parts of fat cattle, are a weariness to me; I take no pleasure in the blood of oxen, or of lambs, or of he-goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 34:23; Exodus 23:17; Micah 6:8"> At whose request do you come before me, making my house unclean with your feet?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 66:3; Malachi 1:10; Matthew 15:9"> Give me no more false offerings; the smoke of burning flesh is disgusting to me, so are your new moons and Sabbaths and your holy meetings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 5:21; Isaiah 43:24; Isaiah 7:13"> Your new moons and your regular feasts are a grief to my soul: they are a weight in my spirit; I am crushed under them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Micah 3:4; Isaiah 59:2–3; Proverbs 1:28"> And when your hands are stretched out to me, my eyes will be turned away from you: even though you go on making prayers, I will not give ear: your hands are full of blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 3:11; Isaiah 55:6–7; 1 Peter 2:1"> Be washed, make yourselves clean; put away the evil of your doings from before my eyes; let there be an end of sinning;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Micah 6:8; Jeremiah 22:3; Psalms 82:3–4"> Take pleasure in well-doing; let your ways be upright, keep down the cruel, give a right decision for the child who has no father, see to the cause of the widow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 44:22; Revelation 7:14; Psalms 51:7"> Come now, and let us have an argument together, says the Lord: how may your sins which are red like blood be white as snow? how may their dark purple seem like wool?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:15–16; Isaiah 3:10; Joel 2:26"> If you will give ear to my word and do it, the good things of the land will be yours;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 65:12; Isaiah 58:14; Numbers 23:19"> But if your hearts are turned against me, I will send destruction on you by the sword; so the Lord has said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:1–23; Zechariah 8:3; Zephaniah 3:1–3"> The upright town has become untrue; there was a time when her judges gave right decisions, when righteousness had a resting-place in her, but now she is full of those who take men's lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:28–30; Lamentations 4:1–2; Hosea 4:18"> Your silver is no longer true metal, your wine is mixed with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Micah 7:3; Exodus 23:8; Zechariah 7:10"> Your chiefs have gone against the Lord, they have become friends of thieves; every one of them is looking for profit and going after rewards; they do not give right decisions for the child who has no father, and they do not let the cause of the widow come before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 60:16; Isaiah 35:4; Deuteronomy 28:63"> For this reason the Lord, the Lord of armies, the Strong One of Israel, has said, I will put an end to my haters, and send punishment on those who are against me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Malachi 3:3; Jeremiah 9:7; Isaiah 4:4"> And my hand will again be on you, washing away what is unclean as with soap, and taking away all your false metal;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:7; Zechariah 8:3; Isaiah 62:1–2"> And I will give you judges again as at the first, and wise guides as in the past; then you will be named, The Town of Righteousness, the true town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 62:12; Isaiah 45:21–25; Isaiah 5:16"> Upright acts will be the price of Zion's forgiveness, and by righteousness will men be living there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:8–9; Psalms 1:6; Isaiah 30:13"> But a common destruction will overtake sinners and evil-doers together, and those who have gone away from the Lord will be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 65:3; Isaiah 57:5; Isaiah 66:17"> For you will be put to shame because of the trees of your desire, and because of the gardens of your pleasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5–6; Ezekiel 17:9–10; Matthew 21:19"> For you will be like a tree whose leaves have become dry, and like a garden without water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 66:24; Ezekiel 32:21; Malachi 4:1"> And the strong will be as food for the fire, and his work as a flame; and they will be burned together, with no one to put out the fire.</span>
