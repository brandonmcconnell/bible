<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 51:9; Ezekiel 29:3; Isaiah 66:16"> In that day the Lord, with his great and strong and cruel sword, will send punishment on Leviathan, the quick-moving snake, and on Leviathan, the twisted snake; and he will put to death the dragon which is in the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:21; Luke 20:9–18; Isaiah 5:1–7"> In that day it will be said, A vine-garden of delight, make a song about it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 58:11; John 15:1–2; 1 Samuel 2:9"> I, the Lord, am watching it; I will give it water at all times: I will keep it night and day, for fear that any damage comes to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 10:17; Hebrews 6:8; Matthew 3:12"> My passion is over: if the thorns were fighting against me, I would make an attack on them, and they would be burned up together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 25:4; Job 22:21; Ephesians 2:16–17"> Or let him put himself under my power, and make peace with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 37:31; Hosea 14:5–6; Romans 11:16–26"> In days to come Jacob will take root: Israel will put out buds and flowers; and the face of the world will be full of fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 37:36–38; Jeremiah 51:24; Isaiah 10:20–25"> Is his punishment like the punishment of those who overcame him? or are his dead as great in number as those he put to the sword?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:24; Jeremiah 4:11; Ezekiel 19:12"> Your anger against her has been made clear by driving her away; he has taken her away with his storm-wind in the day of his east wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 11:27; Isaiah 48:10; Isaiah 17:8"> So by this will the sin of Jacob be covered, and this is all the fruit of taking away his punishment; when all the stones of the altar are crushed together, so that the wood pillars and the sun-images will not be put up again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 17:2; Jeremiah 26:18; Isaiah 32:13–14"> For the strong town is without men, an unpeopled living-place; and she has become a waste land: there the young ox will take his rest, and its branches will be food for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:7; Isaiah 1:3; Isaiah 43:1"> When its branches are dry they will be broken off; the women will come and put fire to them: for it is a foolish people; for this cause he who made them will have no mercy on them, and he whose work they are will not have pity on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; Deuteronomy 30:3–4; Isaiah 56:8"> And it will be in that day that the Lord will get together his grain, from the River to the stream of Egypt, and you will be got together with care, O children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 24:31; Leviticus 25:9; Hebrews 12:22"> And it will be in that day that a great horn will be sounded; and those who were wandering in the land of Assyria, and those who had been sent away into the land of Egypt, will come; and they will give worship to the Lord in the holy mountain at Jerusalem.</span>
