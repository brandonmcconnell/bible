<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 51:7; Psalms 94:15; Hebrews 12:14"> Give ear to me, you who are searching for righteousness, who are looking for the Lord: see the rock from which you were cut out, and the hole out of which you were taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:24; Galatians 3:9–14; Romans 4:1–5"> Let your thoughts be turned to Abraham, your father, and to Sarah, who gave you birth: for when he was but one, my voice came to him, and I gave him my blessing, and made him a great people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 49:13; Joel 2:3; Isaiah 52:9"> For the Lord has given comfort to Zion: he has made glad all her broken walls; making her waste places like Eden, and changing her dry land into the garden of the Lord; joy and delight will be there, praise and the sound of melody.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Isaiah 2:3; 1 Corinthians 9:21"> Give attention to me, O my people; and give ear to me, O my nation; for teaching will go out from me, and the knowledge of the true God will be a light to the peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 10:17–18; Isaiah 46:13; Isaiah 42:4"> Suddenly will my righteousness come near, and my salvation will be shining out like the light; the sea-lands will be waiting for me, and they will put their hope in my strong arm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Psalms 102:25–26; Isaiah 34:4"> Let your eyes be lifted up to the heavens, and turned to the earth which is under them: for the heavens will go in flight like smoke, and the earth will become old like a coat, and its people will come to destruction like insects: but my salvation will be for ever, and my righteousness will not come to an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 5:11; Psalms 37:31; Ezekiel 2:6"> Give ear to me, you who have knowledge of righteousness, in whose heart is my law; have no fear of the evil words of men, and give no thought to their curses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 51:6; Isaiah 50:9; Hosea 5:12"> For like a coat they will be food for the insect, the worm will make a meal of them like wool: but my righteousness will be for ever, and my salvation to all generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 74:13–14; Isaiah 52:1; Psalms 89:10"> Awake! awake! put on strength, O arm of the Lord, awake! as in the old days, in the generations long past. Was it not by you that Rahab was cut in two, and the dragon Wounded?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 43:16; Isaiah 63:11–12; Isaiah 50:2"> Did you not make the sea dry, the waters of the great deep? did you not make the deep waters of the sea a way for the Lord's people to go through?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 25:8; Revelation 21:4; Revelation 7:17"> Those whom the Lord has made free will come back with songs to Zion; and on their heads will be eternal joy: delight and joy will be theirs, and sorrow and sounds of grief will be gone for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 118:6; 1 Peter 1:24; Isaiah 2:22"> I, even I, am your comforter: are you so poor in heart as to be in fear of man who will come to an end, and of the son of man who will be like grass?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 45:12; Isaiah 40:22; Psalms 104:2"> And you have given no thought to the Lord your Maker, by whom the heavens were stretched out, and the earth placed on its base; and you went all day in fear of the wrath of the cruel one, when he was making ready for your destruction. And where is the wrath of the cruel one?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 48:20; Zechariah 9:11; Isaiah 52:2"> The prisoner, bent under his chain, will quickly be made free, and will not go down into the underworld, and his bread will not come to an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:35; Isaiah 54:5; Amos 9:5–6"> For I am the Lord your God, who makes the sea calm when its waves are thundering: the Lord of armies is his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:21; Isaiah 49:2; John 17:8"> And I have put my words in your mouth, covering you with the shade of my hand, stretching out the heavens, and placing the earth on its base, and saying to Zion, You are my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 52:1; Revelation 14:10; Psalms 60:3"> Awake! awake! up! O Jerusalem, you who have taken from the Lord's hand the cup of his wrath; tasting in full measure the wine which overcomes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 49:21; Psalms 88:18; Psalms 142:4"> She has no one among all her children to be her guide; not one of the sons she has taken care of takes her by the hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Amos 7:2; Isaiah 47:9; Ezekiel 14:21"> These two things have come on you; who will be weeping for you? wasting and destruction; death from need of food, and from the sword; how may you be comforted?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Lamentations 2:11–12; Revelation 16:9–11; Ezekiel 39:19"> Your sons are overcome, like a roe in a net; they are full of the wrath of the Lord, the punishment of your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 29:9; Isaiah 54:11; Isaiah 51:17"> So now give ear to this, you who are troubled and overcome, but not with wine:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 22:23; Isaiah 51:17; Jeremiah 50:34"> This is the word of the Lord your master, even your God who takes up the cause of his people: See, I have taken out of your hand the cup which overcomes, even the cup of my wrath; it will not again be given to you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 10:24; Zechariah 12:2; Isaiah 49:25–26"> And I will put it into the hand of your cruel masters, and of those whose yoke has been hard on you; who have said to your soul, Down on your face! so that we may go over you: and you have given your backs like the earth, even like the street, for them to go over.</span>
