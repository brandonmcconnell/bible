<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:4; Isaiah 51:12; Isaiah 51:3"> Give comfort, give comfort, to my people, says your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 9:12; Isaiah 61:7; Hosea 2:14"> Say kind words to the heart of Jerusalem, crying out to her that her time of trouble is ended, that her punishment is complete; that she has been rewarded by the Lord's hand twice over for all her sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Mark 1:2–5; John 1:23"> A voice of one crying, Make ready in the waste land the way of the Lord, make level in the lowland a highway for our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 3:5; Ezekiel 21:26; Luke 18:14"> Let every valley be lifted up, and every mountain and hill be made low, and let the rough places become level, and the hilltops become a valley,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:14; Joel 2:28; Isaiah 52:10"> And the glory of the Lord will be made clear, and all flesh will see it together, for the mouth of the Lord has said it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 103:15–16; 1 Peter 1:24–25; James 1:10–11"> A voice of one saying, Give a cry! And I said, What is my cry to be? All flesh is grass, and all its strength like the flower of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 41:21"> The grass becomes dry, the flower is dead; because the breath of the Lord goes over it: truly the people is grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 13:31; 1 Peter 1:25; Matthew 24:35"> The grass is dry, the flower is dead; but the word of our God is eternal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7–8; Isaiah 12:2; Isaiah 25:9"> You who give good news to Zion, get up into the high mountain; you who give good news to Jerusalem, let your voice be strong; let it be sounding without fear; say to the towns of Judah, See, your God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 22:12; Isaiah 62:11; Revelation 17:14"> See, the Lord God will come as a strong one, ruling in power: see, those made free by him are with him, and those whom he has made safe go before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 7:17; Micah 5:4; Psalms 23:1–6"> He will give food to his flock like a keeper of sheep; with his arm he will get it together, and will take up the lambs on his breast, gently guiding those which are with young.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 30:4; Hebrews 1:10–12; Psalms 102:25–26"> In the hollow of whose hand have the waters been measured? and who is able to take the heavens in his stretched-out fingers? who has got together the dust of the earth in a measure? who has taken the weight of the mountains, or put the hills into the scales?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:16; Romans 11:34; Job 21:22"> By whom has the spirit of the Lord been guided, or who has been his teacher?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 21:22; Colossians 2:3; 1 Corinthians 12:4–6"> Who gave him suggestions, and made clear to him the right way? who gave him knowledge, guiding him in the way of wisdom?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 40:22; Job 34:14–15; Jeremiah 10:10"> See, the nations are to him like a drop hanging from a bucket, and like the small dust in the scales: he takes up the islands like small dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Micah 6:6–7; Psalms 40:6; Psalms 50:9–12"> And Lebanon is not enough to make a fire with, or all its cattle enough for a burned offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 62:9; Daniel 4:34–35; Isaiah 29:7"> All the nations are as nothing before him; even less than nothing, a thing of no value.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 46:5; 1 Samuel 2:2; Isaiah 40:25"> Whom then is God like, in your opinion? or what will you put forward as a comparison with him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:9; Habakkuk 2:18–19; Isaiah 46:6–7"> The workman makes an image, and the gold-worker puts gold plates over it, and makes silver bands for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:3–4; Isaiah 41:7; Isaiah 46:7"> The wise workman makes selection of the mulberry-tree of the offering, a wood which will not become soft; so that the image may be fixed to it and not be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 48:13; Psalms 50:6; Acts 14:17"> Have you no knowledge of it? has it not come to your ears? has not news of it been given to you from the first? has it not been clear to you from the time when the earth was placed on its base?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 8:27; Psalms 104:2; Isaiah 42:5"> It is he who is seated over the arch of the earth, and the people in it are as small as locusts; by him the heavens are stretched out like an arch, and made ready like a tent for a living-place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 12:21; Psalms 107:40; Isaiah 34:12"> He makes rulers come to nothing; the judges of the earth are of no value.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 41:16; Isaiah 17:13; Haggai 1:9"> They have only now been planted, and their seed put into the earth, and they have only now taken root, when he sends out his breath over them and they become dry, and the storm-wind takes them away like dry grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 40:18; Deuteronomy 4:33; Deuteronomy 4:15–18"> Who then seems to you to be my equal? says the Holy One.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 1:16–17; Jeremiah 32:17–19; Isaiah 48:13"> Let your eyes be lifted up on high, and see: who has made these? He who sends out their numbered army: who has knowledge of all their names: by whose great strength, because he is strong in power, all of them are in their places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 18:7–8; Isaiah 49:4; Isaiah 49:14"> Why do you say, O Jacob, such words as these, O Israel, The Lord's eyes are not on my way, and my God gives no attention to my cause?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 11:33–34; Psalms 147:5; Isaiah 55:8–9"> Have you no knowledge of it? has it not come to your ears? The eternal God, the Lord, the Maker of the ends of the earth, is never feeble or tired; there is no searching out of his wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Philippians 4:13; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> He gives power to the feeble, increasing the strength of him who has no force.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Amos 2:14; Psalms 34:10; Psalms 33:16"> Even the young men will become feeble and tired, and the best of them will come to the end of his strength;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25–26; 2 Corinthians 4:16; Psalms 27:13–14"> But those who are waiting for the Lord will have new strength; they will get wings like eagles: running, they will not be tired, and walking, they will have no weariness.</span>
