<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 60:18; Isaiah 62:11; Ephesians 5:19–20"> In that day will this song be made in the land of Judah: We have a strong town; he will make salvation our walls and towers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 5:9; Isaiah 60:11; 1 Peter 2:9"> Let the doors be open, so that the upright nation which keeps faith may come in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 4:7; John 16:33; John 14:27"> The man whose heart is unmoved you will keep in peace, because his hope is in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 62:8; Psalms 55:22"> Let your hope be in the Lord for ever: for the Lord Jah is an unchanging Rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:37; Revelation 18:2; Job 40:11–13"> For he has made low those who are lifted up, all the people of the town of pride: he makes it low, crushing it down to the earth; he makes it low in the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 16:20; Luke 10:19; Revelation 2:26"> It will be crushed under the feet of the poor and the steps of those who are in need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; Isaiah 42:16; 1 John 3:7"> The way of the good man is straight; the road of the upright is made smooth by you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 37:3–7; James 5:7–11; Isaiah 33:2"> We have been waiting for you, O Lord; the desire of our soul is for the memory of your name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Psalms 63:1; Psalms 119:62"> In the night the desire of my soul has been for you; early will my spirit be searching for you; for when your punishments come on the earth, the people of the world will get the knowledge of righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 11:7; John 5:37–38; Romans 2:4–5"> Even if you are kind to the evil-doer, he will not go after righteousness; even in the land of the upright he will still go on in his wrongdoing, and will not see the glory of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:27; Micah 5:9; Isaiah 5:24"> Lord, your hand is lifted up, but they do not see: let them see ... yes, your haters will be burned up in the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; Psalms 57:2; Psalms 29:11"> Lord, you will give us peace: for all our works are the outcome of your purpose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 63:7; Isaiah 2:8; Isaiah 10:11"> O Lord, our God, other lords than you have had rule over us; but in you only is our salvation, and no other name will we take on our lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 18:2–3; Proverbs 10:7; Isaiah 51:12–13"> The dead will not come back to life: their spirits will not come back to earth; for this cause you have sent destruction on them, so that the memory of them is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 9:3; Isaiah 33:17; Nehemiah 9:23"> You have made the nation great, O Lord, you have made it great; glory is yours: you have made wide the limits of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 5:15; Psalms 50:15; Psalms 77:1–2"> Lord, in trouble our eyes have been turned to you, we sent up a prayer when your punishment was on us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 21:3; Isaiah 13:8; John 16:21"> As a woman with child, whose time is near, is troubled, crying out in her pain; so have we been before you, O Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hosea 13:13; Psalms 17:14; 2 Kings 19:3"> We have been with child, we have been in pain, we have given birth to wind; no salvation has come to the earth through us, and no children have come into the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; Ezekiel 37:1–14; Matthew 27:52"> Your dead will come back; their dead bodies will come to life again. Those in the dust, awaking from their sleep, will send out a song; for your dew is a dew of light, and the earth will give birth to the shades.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 30:5; Psalms 27:5; Isaiah 54:7–8"> Come, my people, into your secret places, and let your doors be shut: keep yourself safe for a short time, till his wrath is over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 6:9–11; Isaiah 13:11; Jude 1:14–15"> For the Lord is coming out of his place to send punishment on the people of the earth for their evil-doing: the earth will let the blood drained out on her be seen, and will keep her dead covered no longer.</span>
