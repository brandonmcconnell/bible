<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 12:38; Romans 10:16–17; 1 Corinthians 1:18"> Who would have had faith in the word which has come to our ears, and to whom had the arm of the Lord been unveiled?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 2:6–7; Isaiah 52:14; John 1:10–14"> For his growth was like that of a delicate plant before him, and like a root out of a dry place: he had no grace of form, to give us pleasure;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 26:67; John 1:10–11; Isaiah 53:10"> Men made sport of him, turning away from him; he was a man of sorrows, marked by disease; and like one from whom men's faces are turned away, he was looked down on, and we put no value on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Matthew 8:17; Galatians 3:13"> But it was our pain he took, and our diseases were put on him: while to us he seemed as one diseased, on whom God's punishment had come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24–25; 1 Peter 3:18; Matthew 20:28"> But it was for our sins he was wounded, and for our evil doings he was crushed: he took the punishment by which we have peace, and by his wounds we are made well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 2:25; 1 Peter 3:18; Psalms 119:176"> We all went wandering like sheep; going every one of us after his desire; and the Lord put on him the punishment of us all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 8:32–33; Matthew 26:63; 1 Peter 2:23"> Men were cruel to him, but he was gentle and quiet; as a lamb taken to its death, and as a sheep before those who take her wool makes no sound, so he said not a word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Peter 3:18; Acts 8:33; Matthew 26:65–66"> They took away from him help and right, and who gave a thought to his fate? for he was cut off from the land of the living: he came to his death for the sin of my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 27:57–60; 1 Peter 2:22; 1 John 3:5"> And they put his body into the earth with sinners, and his last resting-place was with the evil-doers, though he had done no wrong, and no deceit was in his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 8:32; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Galatians 3:13"> And the Lord was pleased ... see a seed, long life, ... will do well in his hand. ...</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 5:18–19; 1 Peter 2:24; Isaiah 53:4–6"> ... made clear his righteousness before men ... had taken their sins on himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 22:37; Hebrews 9:28; Luke 23:32–34"> For this cause he will have a heritage with the great, and he will have a part in the goods of war with the strong, because he gave up his life, and was numbered with the evil-doers; taking on himself the sins of the people, and making prayer for the wrongdoers.</span>
