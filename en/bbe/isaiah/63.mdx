<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 19:13; Isaiah 34:5–6; Psalms 137:7"> Who is this who comes from Edom, with blood-red robes from Bozrah? he whose clothing is fair, stepping with pride in his great strength? I whose glory is in the right, strong for salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 19:13; Revelation 19:15"> Why is your clothing red, and why are your robes like those of one who is crushing the grapes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 19:13–15; Revelation 14:19–20; Malachi 4:3"> I have been crushing the grapes by myself, and of the peoples there was no man with me: in my wrath and in my passion, they were crushed under my feet; and my robes are marked with their life-blood, and all my clothing is red.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 34:8; Jeremiah 51:6; Isaiah 35:4"> For the day of punishment is in my heart, and the year for the payment of the price for my people has come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 44:3; Isaiah 40:10; Psalms 98:1"> And I saw that there was no helper, and I was wondering that no one gave them support: so my arm did the work of salvation, and my wrath was my support.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 14:10; Revelation 16:19; Isaiah 49:26"> And in my passion the peoples were crushed under my feet, and broken in my wrath, and I put down their strength to the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4; Psalms 86:5; Psalms 86:15"> I will give news of the mercies of the Lord, and his great acts, even all the things the Lord has done for us, in his great grace to the house of Israel; even all he has done for us in his unnumbered mercies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 17:7; Isaiah 12:2; Exodus 6:7"> For he said, Truly they are my people, children who will not be false: so he was their saviour out of all their trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 33:14; Exodus 23:20–21; Malachi 3:1"> It was no sent one or angel, but he himself who was their saviour: in his love and in his pity he took up their cause, and he took them in his arms, caring for them all through the years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Ephesians 4:30; Psalms 78:56"> But they went against him, causing grief to his holy spirit: so he was turned against them, and made war on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 11:17; Exodus 14:22; Psalms 77:20"> Then the early days came to their minds, the days of Moses his servant: and they said, Where is he who made the keeper of his flock come up from the sea? where is he who put his holy spirit among them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 15:16; Psalms 78:13; Joshua 3:16"> He who made the arm of his glory go at the right hand of Moses, by whom the waters were parted before them, to make himself an eternal name;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:9; Psalms 106:9; Habakkuk 3:15"> He who made them go through the deep waters, like a horse in the waste land?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 63:12; Hebrews 4:8–11; Joshua 23:1"> Like the cattle which go down into the valley, they went without falling, the spirit of the Lord guiding them: so you went before your people, to make yourself a great name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:20; Deuteronomy 26:15; Psalms 80:14"> Let your eyes be looking down from heaven, from your holy and beautiful house: where is your deep feeling, the working of your power? do not keep back the moving of your pity and your mercies:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; Malachi 2:10; Jeremiah 3:19"> For you are our father, though Abraham has no knowledge of us, and Israel gives no thought to us: you, O Lord, are our father; from the earliest days you have taken up our cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10; Joshua 11:20; Ezekiel 14:7–9"> O Lord, why do you send us wandering from your ways, making our hearts hard, so that we have no fear of you? Come back, because of your servants, the tribes of your heritage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 74:3–7; Revelation 11:2; Deuteronomy 7:6"> Why have evil men gone over your holy place, so that it has been crushed under the feet of our haters?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 135:4; Acts 14:16; Romans 9:4"> We have become as those who were never ruled by you, on whom your name was not named.</span>
