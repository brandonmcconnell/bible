<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:13; Psalms 125:2; Joel 3:12"> The word about the valley of vision. Why have all your people gone up to the house-tops?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 32:13; Isaiah 23:7; Lamentations 2:20"> You, who are full of loud voices, a town of outcries, given up to joy; your dead men have not been put to the sword, or come to their death in war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 25:4–7; Isaiah 3:1–8; Jeremiah 39:4–7"> All your rulers ... have gone in flight; all your strong ones have gone far away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:1; Matthew 2:18; Psalms 77:2"> For this cause I have said, Let your eyes be turned away from me in my bitter weeping; I will not be comforted for the wasting of the daughter of my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 7:4; Isaiah 37:3; Luke 23:30"> For it is a day of trouble and of crushing down and of destruction from the Lord, the Lord of armies, in the valley of vision; ...</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 16:9; Isaiah 21:2; Amos 1:5"> And Elam was armed with arrows, and Aram came on horseback; and the breastplate of Kir was uncovered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–8; Isaiah 10:28–32; Jeremiah 39:1–3"> And your most fertile valleys were full of war-carriages, and the horsemen took up their positions in front of the town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 7:2; 1 Kings 10:17; Isaiah 36:1–3"> He took away the cover of Judah; and in that day you were looking with care at the store of arms in the house of the woods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 20:20; Nehemiah 3:16; 2 Chronicles 32:1–6"> And you saw all the broken places in the wall of the town of David: and you got together the waters of the lower pool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And you had the houses of Jerusalem numbered, pulling down the houses to make the wall stronger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Micah 7:7; Jeremiah 33:2–3; Isaiah 31:1"> And you made a place between the two walls for storing the waters of the old pool: but you gave no thought to him who had done this, and were not looking to him by whom it had been purposed long before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joel 2:17; Micah 1:16; Joel 1:13"> And in that day the Lord, the Lord of armies, was looking for weeping, and cries of sorrow, cutting off of the hair, and putting on the clothing of grief:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 56:12; 1 Corinthians 15:32; Luke 17:26–29"> But in place of these there was joy and delight, oxen and sheep were being made ready for food, there was feasting and drinking: men said, Now is the time for food and wine, for tomorrow death comes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:13; 1 Samuel 3:14; Isaiah 5:9"> And the Lord of armies said to me secretly, Truly, this sin will not be taken from you till your death, says the Lord, the Lord of armies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 36:3; 2 Kings 18:18; 2 Kings 19:2"> The Lord, the Lord of armies, says, Go to this person in authority, this Shebna, who is over the house; who has made himself a resting-place on high, cutting out a place for himself in the rock, and say,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:60; 2 Chronicles 16:14; 2 Samuel 18:18"> Who are you, and by what right have you made for yourself a resting-place here?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:3; Job 9:24; Esther 7:8"> See, O strong man, the Lord will send you violently away, gripping you with force,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 17:13; Amos 7:17"> Twisting you round and round like a ball he will send you out into a wide country: there you will come to your end, and there will be the carriages of your pride, O shame of your lord's house!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:24; Psalms 75:6–7; Job 40:11–12"> And I will have you forced out of your place of authority, and pulled down from your position.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 36:3; 2 Kings 18:18; Isaiah 37:2"> And in that day I will send for my servant, Eliakim, the son of Hilkiah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 45:8; Isaiah 9:6–7; Genesis 41:42–43"> And I will put your robe on him, and put your band about him, and I will give your authority into his hand: and he will be a father to the men of Jerusalem, and to the family of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 16:18–19; Revelation 3:7; Job 12:14"> And I will give the key of the family of David into his care; and what he keeps open will be shut by no one, and what he keeps shut no one will make open.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezra 9:8; Job 36:7; Zechariah 10:4"> And I will put him like a nail in a safe place; and he will be for a seat of glory to his father's family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 6:1–3; John 20:21–23; Matthew 28:18"> And all the glory of his father's family will be hanging on him, all their offspring, every small vessel, even the cups and the basins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 46:11; Esther 9:24–25; Psalms 146:3"> In that day, says the Lord of armies, will the nail fixed in a safe place give way; and it will be cut down, and in its fall the weight hanging on it will be cut off, for the Lord has said it.</span>
