<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 4:27; Isaiah 62:4; 1 Samuel 2:5"> Let your voice be loud in song, O woman without children; make melody and sounds of joy, you who did not give birth: for the children of her who had no husband are more than those of the married wife, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 33:20; Jeremiah 10:20; Isaiah 49:19–20"> Make wide the place of your tent, and let the curtains of your house be stretched out without limit: make your cords long, and your tent-pins strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:35–36; Genesis 28:14; Isaiah 43:5–6"> For I will make wide your limits on the right hand and on the left; and your seed will take the nations for a heritage, and make the waste towns full of people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; 1 Peter 2:6; Jeremiah 31:19"> Have no fear; for you will not be shamed or without hope: you will not be put to shame, for the shame of your earlier days will go out of your memory, and you will no longer keep in mind the sorrows of your widowed years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 5:25–27; Hosea 2:19–20; 2 Corinthians 11:2–3"> For your Maker is your husband; the Lord of armies is his name: and the Holy One of Israel is he who takes up your cause; he will be named the God of all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 62:4; Ecclesiastes 9:9; Hosea 2:14–15"> For the Lord has made you come back to him, like a wife who has been sent away in grief of spirit; for one may not give up the wife of one's early days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 30:5; 2 Peter 3:8; Micah 4:6"> For a short time I gave you up; but with great mercies I will take you back again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:3; Isaiah 60:10; Ezekiel 39:23–24"> In overflowing wrath my face was veiled from you for a minute, but I will have pity on you for ever, says the Lord who takes up your cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 12:1; Genesis 8:21; Hebrews 6:16–18"> For this is like the days of Noah to me: for as I took an oath that the waters of Noah would never again go over the earth, so have I taken an oath that I will not again be angry with you, or say bitter words to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 89:33–34; Psalms 46:2; Ephesians 2:4–5"> For the mountains may be taken away, and the hills be moved out of their places, but my love will not be taken from you, or my agreement of peace broken, says the Lord, who has had mercy on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4–6; Acts 14:22; Jeremiah 30:17"> O troubled one, storm-crushed, uncomforted! see, your stones will be framed in fair colours, and your bases will be sapphires.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> I will make your towers of rubies, and your doors of carbuncles, and the wall round you will be of all sorts of beautiful stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 6:45; Philippians 4:7; Psalms 119:165"> And all your builders will be made wise by the Lord; and great will be the peace of your children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 3:25–26; Jeremiah 30:10; Zechariah 9:8"> All your rights will be made certain to you: have no fear of evil, and destruction will not come near you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 41:11–16; Psalms 37:12–13; Zechariah 12:9"> See, they may be moved to war, but not by my authority: all those who come together to make an attack on you, will be broken against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 9:16; Proverbs 16:4; Isaiah 46:11"> See, I have made the iron-worker, blowing on the burning coals, and making the instrument of war by his work; and I have made the waster for destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 8:28–39; Isaiah 54:15; John 10:28–30"> No instrument of war which is formed against you will be of any use; and every tongue which says evil against you will be judged false. This is the heritage of the servants of the Lord, and their righteousness comes from me, says the Lord.</span>
