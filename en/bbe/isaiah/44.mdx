<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 41:8; Romans 11:5–6; Jeremiah 30:10"> And now, give ear, O Jacob my servant, and Israel whom I have taken for myself:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Isaiah 43:1; Isaiah 46:3–4"> The Lord who made you, forming you in your mother's body, the Lord, your helper, says, Have no fear, O Jacob my servant, and you, Jeshurun, whom I have taken for myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joel 2:28; Acts 2:17; Isaiah 59:21"> For I will send water on the land needing it, and streams on the dry earth: I will let my spirit come down on your seed, and my blessing on your offspring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 58:11; Psalms 1:3; Psalms 92:13–15"> And they will come up like grass in a well-watered field, like water-plants by the streams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:5; Zechariah 13:9; 1 Peter 2:9"> One will say, I am the Lord's; and another will give himself the name, Jacob; another will put a mark on his hand, I am the Lord's, and another will take the name of Israel for himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 41:4; Isaiah 43:10–11; Revelation 1:8"> The Lord, the King of Israel, even the Lord of armies who has taken up his cause, says, I am the first and the last, and there is no God but me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 41:26; Isaiah 41:22; Isaiah 46:9–10"> If there is one like me, let him come forward and say it, let him make it clear and put it in order before me: who has made clear in the past the things to come? let him make clear the future to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 43:10; 1 Samuel 2:2; Deuteronomy 4:35"> Have no fear, be strong in heart; have I not made it clear to you in the past, and let you see it? and you are my witnesses. Is there any God but me, or a Rock of whom I have no knowledge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 41:24; Jeremiah 10:14–15; Psalms 97:7"> Those who make a pictured image are all of them as nothing, and the things of their desire will be of no profit to them: and their servants see not, and have no knowledge; so they will be put to shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:5; Habakkuk 2:18; Acts 19:26"> Whoever makes a god, makes nothing but a metal image in which there is no profit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 42:17; Isaiah 45:16; Psalms 97:7"> Truly, all those who make use of secret arts will be put to shame, and their words of power are only words of men: let them all come forward together; they will all be in fear and be put to shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 41:6–7; Isaiah 40:19; Isaiah 46:6–7"> The iron-worker is heating the metal in the fire, giving it form with his hammers, and working on it with his strong arm: then for need of food his strength gives way, and for need of water he becomes feeble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 17:4–5; Psalms 115:5–7; Acts 17:29"> The woodworker is measuring out the wood with his line, marking it out with his pencil: after smoothing it with his plane, and making circles on it with his instrument, he gives it the form and glory of a man, so that it may be placed in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:20; Jeremiah 10:3–8; Hosea 4:12"> He has cedars cut down for himself, he takes an oak and lets it get strong among the trees of the wood; he has an ash-tree planted, and the rain gives it growth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:14; Revelation 9:20; Judges 2:19"> Then it will be used to make a fire, so that a man may get warm; he has the oven heated with it and makes bread: he makes a god with it, to which he gives worship: he makes a pictured image out of it, and goes down on his face before it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> With part of it he makes a fire, and on the fire he gets meat cooked and takes a full meal: he makes himself warm, and says, Aha! I am warm, I have seen the fire:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 45:20; 1 Kings 18:26; Daniel 6:27"> And the rest of it he makes into a god, even his pictured image: he goes down on his face before it, giving worship to it, and making prayer to it, saying, Be my saviour; for you are my god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4; John 12:39–40"> They have no knowledge or wisdom; for he has put a veil over their eyes, so that they may not see; and on their hearts, so that they may not give attention.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:15; 1 Kings 11:7; 1 Kings 11:5"> And no one takes note, no one has enough knowledge or wisdom to say, I have put part of it in the fire, and made bread on it; I have had a meal of the flesh cooked with it: and am I now to make the rest of it into a false god? am I to go down on my face before a bit of wood?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 1:28; Job 15:31; Hosea 12:1"> As for him whose food is the dust of a dead fire, he has been turned from the way by a twisted mind, so that he is unable to keep himself safe by saying, What I have here in my hand is false.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 44:1–2; Isaiah 49:15–16; Isaiah 43:15"> Keep these things in mind, O Jacob; and you Israel, for you are my servant: I have made you; you are my servant; O Israel, I will not let you go out of my memory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; 1 Corinthians 6:20; Acts 3:18–19"> I have put your evil doings out of my mind like a thick cloud, and your sins like a mist: come back to me; for I have taken up your cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ephesians 1:6–7; Revelation 5:8–14; Psalms 69:34"> Make a song, O heavens, for the Lord has done it: give a loud cry, you deep parts of the earth: let your voices be loud in song, you mountains, and you woods with all your trees: for the Lord has taken up the cause of Jacob, and will let his glory be seen in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 45:12; Isaiah 42:5; Isaiah 54:5"> The Lord, who has taken up your cause, and who gave you life in your mother's body, says, I am the Lord who makes all things; stretching out the heavens by myself, and giving the earth its limits; who was with me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 33:10; Job 5:12–14; Jeremiah 50:36"> Who makes the signs of those who give word of the future come to nothing, so that those who have knowledge of secret arts go off their heads; turning the wise men back, and making their knowledge foolish:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 18:36–38; Isaiah 42:9; Nehemiah 1:3"> Who makes the word of his servants certain, and gives effect to the purposes of his representatives; who says of Jerusalem, Her people will come back to her; and of the towns of Judah, I will give orders for their building, and will make her waste places fertile again:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 42:15; Jeremiah 51:36; Jeremiah 50:38"> Who says to the deep, Be dry, and I will make your rivers dry:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:22–3; Isaiah 45:1; Isaiah 45:13"> Who says of Cyrus, He will take care of my sheep, and will do all my pleasure: who says of Jerusalem, I will give the word for your building; and of the Temple, Your bases will be put in place.</span>
