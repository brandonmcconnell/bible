<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 30:8; Job 19:23–24; Habakkuk 2:2–3"> And the Lord said to me, Take a great writing-board, and on it put down in common letters, Maher-shalal-hash-baz;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 16:10–11; 2 Kings 18:2; 2 Corinthians 13:1"> And take true witnesses to the writing, Uriah the priest, and Zechariah, the son of Jeberechiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 1:3–9; Judges 4:4; Isaiah 7:13–14"> And I went in to my wife, and she became with child, and gave birth to a son. Then the Lord said to me, Give him the name Maher-shalal-hash-baz,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 9:11; 2 Kings 16:9; Deuteronomy 1:39"> For before the child is able to say, Father, or, Mother, the wealth of Damascus and the goods of Samaria will be taken away by the king of Assyria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 7:10"> And the Lord said again to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 9:7; Nehemiah 3:15; Isaiah 7:6"> Because this people will have nothing to do with the softly-flowing waters of Shiloah, and have fear of Rezin and Remaliah's son;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 17:12–13; Genesis 6:17; Isaiah 7:17"> For this cause the Lord is sending on them the waters of the River, deep and strong, even the king of Assyria and all his glory: and it will come up through all its streams, overflowing all its edges:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Isaiah 30:28; Matthew 1:23"> And it will come on into Judah; rushing on and overflowing, till the waters are up to the neck; &#42;&#42;&#42;and his outstretched wings will be covering the land from side to side: for God is with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 14:5–6; Proverbs 11:21; Psalms 37:14–15"> Have knowledge, O peoples, and be in fear; give ear, all you far-off parts of the earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Lamentations 3:37; Romans 8:31; Job 5:12"> Let your designs be formed, and they will come to nothing; give your orders, and they will not be effected: for God is with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:14; Proverbs 1:15; Jeremiah 15:19"> For the Lord, controlling me with a strong hand, gave me orders not to go in the way of this people, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:14–15; Luke 21:9; Psalms 53:5"> Do not say, It is holy, about everything of which this people says, It is holy; and do not be in fear of what they go in fear of.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 12:5; Isaiah 26:3–4; Revelation 15:4"> But let the Lord of armies be holy to you, and go in fear of him, giving honour to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 2:8; Romans 9:32–33; Isaiah 28:16"> And he will be for a holy place: but for a stone of falling and a rock of trouble to the two houses of Israel, and to the men of Jerusalem, for a net in which they may be taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 21:44; Luke 20:17–18; Isaiah 28:13"> And numbers of them, falling on the stone, will be broken, and will be taken in the net.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 12:4; Hebrews 3:5; Mark 4:10–11"> Let my teaching be kept secret: and my words be given to my disciples only.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36–39; Habakkuk 2:3; Lamentations 3:25–26"> And I will be waiting for the Lord, whose face is veiled from the house of Jacob, and I will be looking for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 2:13–14; Luke 2:34; Zechariah 8:3"> See, I and the children whom the Lord has given me, are for signs and for wonders in Israel from the Lord of armies, whose resting-place is in Mount Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 10:13; Leviticus 20:6; 2 Chronicles 33:6"> And when they say to you, Make request for us to those who have control of spirits, and to those wise in secret arts, who make hollow bird-like sounds; is it not right for a people to make request to their gods, to make request for the living to the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 1:19; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; Isaiah 30:8–11"> Then say to them, Put your faith in the teaching and the witness. ... If they do not say such things. ... For him there is no dawn. ...</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 9:20; Proverbs 19:3; Lamentations 4:4–5"> And he will go through the land in bitter trouble and in need of food; and when he is unable to get food, he will become angry, cursing his king and his God, and his eyes will be turned to heaven on high;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 5:30; Matthew 22:13; Jeremiah 13:16"> And he will be looking down on the earth, and there will be trouble and dark clouds, black night where there is no seeing.</span>
