<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 102:13; Zechariah 1:17; Zechariah 8:22–23"> For the Lord will have mercy on Jacob, and will again make Israel his special people, and will put them in their land; and the man from a strange country will take his place among them and be joined to the family of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 61:5; Isaiah 60:14; Jeremiah 30:16"> And the people will take them with them to their place: and the children of Israel will give them a heritage in the Lord's land as men-servants and women-servants, making them prisoners whose prisoners they were; and they will be rulers over their masters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:10; Isaiah 32:18; Ezra 9:8–9"> And it will be, in the day when the Lord gives you rest from your sorrow, and from your trouble, and from the hard yoke which they had put on you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 49:26; Habakkuk 1:2–10; Jeremiah 51:34–35"> That you will take up this bitter song against the king of Babylon, and say, How has the cruel overseer come to an end! He who was lifted up in pride is cut off;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 125:3; Isaiah 9:4; Jeremiah 48:15–17"> The stick of the evil-doers, the rod of the rulers, is broken by the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 7:19–21; James 2:13; Revelation 17:16–17"> He whose rod was on the peoples with an unending wrath, ruling the nations in passion, with an uncontrolled rule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 126:1–3; Psalms 98:7–9; Psalms 96:11–13"> All the earth is at rest and is quiet: they are bursting into song.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:16; Isaiah 55:12–13; Zechariah 11:2"> Even the trees of the wood are glad over you, the trees of Lebanon, saying, From the time of your fall no wood-cutter has come up against us with an axe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:21–32; Jeremiah 50:8; Proverbs 15:24"> The underworld is moved at your coming: the shades of the dead are awake before you, even the strong ones of the earth; all the kings of the world have got up from their seats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 49:6–14; Ecclesiastes 2:16; Psalms 82:6–7"> They all make answer and say to you, Have you become feeble like us? have you been made even as we are?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 5:25–30; Job 21:11–15; Isaiah 66:24"> Your pride has gone down into the underworld, and the noise of your instruments of music; the worms are under you, and your body is covered with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13–17; Revelation 12:7–10; Luke 10:18"> How great is your fall from heaven, O shining one, son of the morning! How are you cut down to the earth, low among the dead bodies!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 8:10–12; Ezekiel 28:2; Ezekiel 28:12–16"> For you said in your heart, I will go up to heaven, I will make my seat higher than the stars of God; I will take my place on the mountain of the meeting-place of the gods, in the inmost parts of the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 47:8; Genesis 3:5; 2 Thessalonians 2:4"> I will go higher than the clouds; I will be like the Most High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 11:23; Ezekiel 32:23; Acts 12:22–23"> But you will come down to the underworld, even to its inmost parts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:23; Psalms 58:10–11; Jeremiah 51:20–23"> Those who see you will be looking on you with care, they will be in deep thought, saying, Is this the troubler of the earth, the shaker of kingdoms?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joel 2:3; Isaiah 13:19–22; Isaiah 45:13"> Who made the world a waste, overturning its towns; who did not let his prisoners loose from the prison-house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:25; Isaiah 22:16; Ezekiel 32:18–32"> All the kings of the earth are at rest in glory, every man in his house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 41:9; Jeremiah 41:7; 1 Kings 21:24"> But you, like a birth before its time, are stretched out with no resting-place in the earth; clothed with the bodies of the dead who have been put to the sword, who go down to the lowest parts of the underworld; a dead body, crushed under foot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 21:10; Job 18:19; Psalms 37:28"> As for your fathers, you will not be united with them in their resting-place, because you have been the cause of destruction to your land, and of death to your people; the seed of the evil-doer will have no place in the memory of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 23:35; Exodus 20:5; Habakkuk 2:8–12"> Make ready a place of death for his children, because of the evil-doing of their father; so that they may not come up and take the earth for their heritage, covering the face of the world with waste places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:29–35; Proverbs 10:7; 1 Kings 14:10"> For I will come up against them, says the Lord of armies, cutting off from Babylon name and offspring, son and son's son, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:14; Isaiah 13:21–22; 1 Kings 14:10"> And I will make you a heritage for the hedgehog, and pools of water: and I will go through it with the brush of destruction, says the Lord of armies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Job 23:13; Isaiah 46:10–11"> The Lord has taken an oath, saying, My design will certainly come about, and my purpose will be effected:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 9:4; Nahum 1:13; Isaiah 37:36–38"> To let the Assyrian be broken in my land, and crushed under foot on my mountains: there will his yoke be taken away from them, and his rule over them come to an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 23:9; Zephaniah 3:6–8; Exodus 15:12"> This is the purpose for all the earth: and this is the hand stretched out over all nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 43:13; Job 23:13; 2 Chronicles 20:6"> For it is the purpose of the Lord of armies, and who will make it of no effect? when his hand is stretched out, by whom may it be turned back?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Kings 16:20; Isaiah 13:1; 2 Chronicles 28:27"> In the year of the death of King Ahaz this word came to the prophet:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 30:6; Isaiah 11:8; 2 Chronicles 26:6"> Be not glad, O Philistia, all of you, because the rod which was on you is broken: for out of the snake's root will come a poison-snake, and its fruit will be a winged poison-snake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 7:21–22; Zephaniah 2:4–7; Isaiah 5:17"> And the poorest of the land will have food, and those in need will be given a safe resting-place: but your seed will come to an end for need of food, and the rest of you will be put to the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:14; Isaiah 20:1; Isaiah 14:29"> Send out a cry, O door! Make sounds of sorrow, O town! All your land has come to nothing, O Philistia; for there comes a smoke out of the north, and everyone keeps his place in the line.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 102:16; Psalms 87:5; James 2:5"> What answer, then, will my people give to the representatives of the nation? That the Lord is the builder of Zion, and she will be a safe place for the poor of his people.</span>
