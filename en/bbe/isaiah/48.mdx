<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:2; Psalms 68:26; Isaiah 65:16"> Give ear to this, O family of Jacob, you who are named by the name of Israel, and have come out of the body of Judah; who take oaths by the name of the Lord, and make use of the name of the God of Israel, but not truly and not in good faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Romans 2:17; Isaiah 52:1"> For they say that they are of the holy town, and put their faith in the God of Israel: the Lord of armies is his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 42:9; Joshua 21:45; Isaiah 41:22"> I gave word in the past of the things which came about; they came from my mouth, and I made them clear: suddenly I did them, and they came about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Exodus 32:9; Zechariah 7:11–12"> Because I saw that your heart was hard, and that your neck was an iron cord, and your brow brass;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 48:3; Luke 1:70; Acts 15:18"> For this reason I made it clear to you in the past, before it came I gave you word of it: for fear that you might say, My god did these things, and my pictured and metal images made them come about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 4:1; Isaiah 42:9; Romans 16:25–26"> All this has come to your ears and you have seen it; will you not give witness to it? I am now making clear new things, even secret things, of which you had no knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> They have only now been effected, and not in the past: and before this day they had not come to your ears; for fear that you might say, I had knowledge of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 58:3; Hosea 5:7; Jeremiah 6:10"> Truly you had no word of them, no knowledge of them; no news of them in the past had come to your ears; because I saw how false was your behaviour, and that your heart was turned against me from your earliest days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 79:9; Psalms 106:8; Psalms 143:11"> Because of my name I will put away my wrath, and for my praise I will keep myself from cutting you off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 4:12; Proverbs 17:3; Job 23:10"> See, I have been testing you for myself like silver; I have put you through the fire of trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 42:8; Ezekiel 20:9; Isaiah 37:35"> For myself, even because of my name, I will do it; for I will not let my name be shamed; and my glory I will not give to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 41:4; Revelation 22:13; Revelation 1:8"> Give ear to me, Jacob, and Israel, my loved one; I am he, I am the first and I am the last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 1:10–12; Psalms 102:25; Isaiah 40:26"> Yes, by my hand was the earth placed on its base, and by my right hand the heavens were stretched out; at my word they take up their places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 43:9; Jeremiah 50:21–29; Isaiah 13:17–18"> Come together, all of you, and give ear; who among you has given news of these things? the Lord's loved one will do his pleasure with Babylon, and with the seed of the Chaldaeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 1:8; Isaiah 45:1–2; Psalms 45:4"> I, even I, have given the word; I have sent for him: I have made him come, and have given effect to his undertakings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 18:20; John 20:21–22; John 3:34"> Come near to me, and give ear to this; from the start I did not keep it secret; from the time of its coming into existence I was there: and now the Lord God has sent me, and given me his spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 32:8; Psalms 25:12; Job 22:21–22"> The Lord who takes up your cause, the Holy One of Israel, says, I am the Lord your God, who is teaching you for your profit, guiding you by the way in which you are to go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:29; Isaiah 66:12; Psalms 119:165"> If only you had given ear to my orders, then your peace would have been like a river, and your righteousness as the waves of the sea:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 10:22; Genesis 22:17; Romans 9:27"> Your seed would have been like the sand, and your offspring like the dust: your name would not be cut off or come to an end before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:45; Jeremiah 50:8; Isaiah 52:9"> Go out of Babylon, go in flight from the Chaldaeans; with the sound of song make it clear, give the news, let the word go out even to the end of the earth: say, The Lord has taken up the cause of his servant Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 17:6; Isaiah 43:19–20; Psalms 105:41"> They had no need of water when he was guiding them through the waste lands: he made water come out of the rock for them: the rock was parted and the waters came flowing out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 57:21; Job 15:20–24; Luke 19:42"> There is no peace, says the Lord, for the evil-doers.</span>
