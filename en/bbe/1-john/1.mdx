<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:9; John 17:3; John 17:21"> We give you word of all we have seen and everything which has come to our ears, so that you may be united with us; and we are united with the Father and with his Son Jesus Christ:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 15:11; John 16:24; 2 John 1:12"> And we are writing these things to you so that our joy may be made complete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 8:12; James 1:17; Isaiah 60:19"> This is the word which came to us from him and which we give to you, that God is light and in him there is nothing dark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 John 4:20; 1 John 2:4; John 3:19–21"> If we say we are joined to him, and are walking still in the dark, our words are false and our acts are untrue:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Romans 13:12; Revelation 1:5"> But if we are walking in the light, as he is in the light, we are all united with one another, and the blood of Jesus his Son makes us clean from all sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:23; Ecclesiastes 7:20; James 3:2"> If we say that we have no sin, we are false to ourselves and there is nothing true in us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 32:5; Proverbs 28:13; Jeremiah 33:8"> If we say openly that we have done wrong, he is upright and true to his word, giving us forgiveness of sins and making us clean from all evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 1:8; 1 John 5:10; 1 John 2:4"> If we say that we have no sin, we make him false and his word is not in us.</span>
