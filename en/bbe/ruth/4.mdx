<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:7; Ruth 3:12; Deuteronomy 16:18"> And Boaz went up to the public place of the town, and took his seat there: and the near relation of whom he had been talking came by; and Boaz, crying out to him by name, said, Come and be seated here. And he came and was seated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 31:23; 1 Kings 21:8; Acts 6:12"> Then he got ten of the responsible men of the town, and said, Be seated here. And they took their seats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 13:10; Psalms 112:5"> Then he said to the near relation, Naomi, who has come back from the country of Moab, is offering for a price that bit of land which was our brother Elimelech's:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 25:25–29; Jeremiah 32:7–12; Romans 12:17"> And it was in my mind to give you the chance of taking it, with the approval of those seated here and of the responsible men of my people. If you are ready to do what it is right for a relation to do, then do it: but if you will not do it, say so to me now; for there is no one who has the right to do it but you, and after you myself. And he said, I will do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 38:8; Deuteronomy 25:5–6; Matthew 22:24"> Then Boaz said, On the day when you take this field, you will have to take with it Ruth, the Moabitess, the wife of the dead, so that you may keep the name of the dead living in his heritage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 25:25; Ruth 3:13"> And the near relation said, I am not able to do the relation's part, for fear of damaging the heritage I have: you may do it in my place, for I am not able to do it myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:7–10"> Now, in earlier times this was the way in Israel when property was taken over by a near relation, or when there was a change of owner. To make the exchange certain one man took off his shoe and gave it to the other; and this was a witness in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> So the near relation said to Boaz, Take it for yourself. And he took off his shoe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:10–12; Genesis 23:16–18"> Then Boaz said to the responsible men and to all the people, You are witnesses today that I have taken at a price from Naomi all the property which was Elimelech's, and everything which was Chilion's and Mahlon's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:6; Ephesians 5:25; Genesis 29:27"> And, further, I have taken Ruth, the Moabitess, who was the wife of Mahlon, to be my wife, to keep the name of the dead man living in his heritage, so that his name may not be cut off from among his countrymen, and from the memory of his town: you are witnesses this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 29:32–24; Micah 5:2; Matthew 2:6"> And all the people who were in the public place, and the responsible men, said, We are witnesses. May the Lord make this woman, who is about to come into your house, like Rachel and Leah, which two were the builders of the house of Israel: and may you have wealth in Ephrathah, and be great in Beth-lehem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 38:29; 1 Samuel 2:20; 1 Chronicles 2:4"> May your family be like the family of Perez, the son whom Tamar gave to Judah, from the offspring which the Lord may give you by this young woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 33:5; Genesis 29:31; Ruth 3:11"> So Boaz took Ruth and she became his wife; and he went in to her, and the Lord made her with child and she gave birth to a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 1:5–20; Luke 1:58; Genesis 12:2"> And the women said to Naomi, A blessing on the Lord, who has not let you be this day without a near relation, and may his name be great in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 55:22; Isaiah 46:4; Genesis 45:11"> He will be a giver of new life to you, and your comforter when you are old, for your daughter-in-law, who, in her love for you, is better than seven sons, has given birth to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Naomi took the child and put her arms round it, and she took care of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 1:58–63"> And the women who were her neighbours gave it a name, saying, Naomi has a child; and they gave him the name of Obed: he is the father of Jesse, the father of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 1:3–6; 1 Chronicles 2:4–8; Luke 3:33"> Now these are the generations of Perez: Perez became the father of Hezron;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 3:33; 1 Chronicles 2:9–10; Matthew 1:4"> And Hezron became the father of Ram, and Ram became the father of Amminadab;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 1:7; Luke 3:32; Matthew 1:4"> And Amminadab became the father of Nahshon, and Nahshon became the father of Salmon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 3:32; Matthew 1:5; 1 Chronicles 2:11–12"> And Salmon became the father of Boaz, and Boaz became the father of Obed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:15; Isaiah 11:1; Matthew 1:6"> And Obed became the father of Jesse, and Jesse became the father of David.</span>
