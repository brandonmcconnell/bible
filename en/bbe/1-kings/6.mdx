<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:1–2; Acts 7:47; 1 Chronicles 29:19"> In the four hundred and eightieth year after the children of Israel came out of the land of Egypt, in the fourth year that Solomon was king of Israel, in the month Ziv, which is the second month, the building of the Lord's house was started.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:1–41; Ezekiel 41:1–15; Revelation 21:16–17"> The house which Solomon made for the Lord was sixty cubits long, twenty cubits wide and thirty cubits high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:11; 2 Chronicles 3:3–4; Ezekiel 41:15"> The covered way before the Temple of the house was twenty cubits long, as wide as the house, and ten cubits wide in front of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 41:26; Ezekiel 41:16"> And for the house he made windows, with network across.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 6:16; 1 Kings 6:19–21; 1 Kings 6:31"> And against the walls all round, and against the walls of the Temple and of the inmost room, he put up wings, with side rooms all round:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> The lowest line of them being five cubits wide, the middle six cubits wide and the third seven cubits; for there was a space all round the outside walls of the house so that the boards supporting the rooms did not have to be fixed in the walls of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:5–6; 1 Peter 2:5; Exodus 20:25"> (And the stones used in the building of the house were squared at the place where they were cut out; there was no sound of hammer or axe or any iron instrument while they were building the house.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:6–7"> The door to the lowest side rooms was in the right side of the house; and they went up by twisting steps into the middle rooms, and from the middle into the third.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 6:14; 1 Kings 6:38"> So he put up the house and made it complete, roofing it with boards of cedar-wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And he put up the line of side rooms against the walls of the house, fifteen cubits high, resting against the house on boards of cedar-wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> (And the word of the Lord came to Solomon, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:10; Colossians 1:23; 1 Kings 3:14"> About this house which you are building: if you will keep my laws and give effect to my decisions and be guided by my rules, I will give effect to my word which I gave to David your father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:6; Exodus 25:8; Leviticus 26:11"> And I will be ever among the children of Israel, and will not go away from my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 6:9; 1 Kings 6:38; Acts 7:47–48"> So Solomon made the building of the house complete.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 7:7"> The walls of the house were covered inside with cedar-wood boards; from the floor to the roof of the house they were covered inside with wood; and the floor was covered with boards of cypress-wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:8; 1 Kings 8:6; Hebrews 9:3"> And at the back of the house a further space of twenty cubits was shut in with boards of cedar-wood, for the inmost room.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the house, that is, the Temple, in front of the holy place was forty cubits long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 7:24"> (All the inside of the house was cedar-wood, ornamented with designs of buds and flowers; no stonework was to be seen inside.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 8:6–10; Psalms 28:2; 1 Kings 6:5"> And he made ready an inmost room in the middle of the house, in which to put the ark of the agreement of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 6:2–3; 1 Kings 6:22; 1 Kings 7:48"> And the inmost room was twenty cubits square and twenty cubits high, plated over with clear gold, and he made an altar of cedar-wood, plating it with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:7–9; 2 Chronicles 3:14–16; 1 Kings 6:5"> Solomon had all the inside of the house covered with gold, and he put chains of gold across in front of the inmost room, which itself was covered with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 30:1; Exodus 30:3; 2 Chronicles 3:7–14"> Plates of gold were put all through the house till it was covered completely (and the altar in the inmost room was all covered with gold).</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 37:7–9; Genesis 3:24; 1 Peter 1:12"> In the inmost room he made two winged beings of olive-wood, ten cubits high;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> With outstretched wings five cubits wide; the distance from the edge of one wing to the edge of the other was ten cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> The two winged ones were ten cubits high, of the same size and form.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> The two of them were ten cubits high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 37:9; Exodus 25:20; 2 Chronicles 5:8"> These were placed inside the inner house, their outstretched wings touching the walls of the house, one touching one wall and one the other, while their other wings were touching in the middle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> These winged ones were plated over with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 6:32; Psalms 92:12–15; 2 Chronicles 4:2–5"> And all the walls of the house inside and out were ornamented with forms of winged ones and palm-trees and open flowers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 21:18–21; Isaiah 60:17; Isaiah 54:11–12"> And the floor of the house was covered with gold, inside and out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–20; Ephesians 3:18; John 14:6"> For the way into the inmost room he made doors of olive-wood, the arch and the door supports forming a five-sided opening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 6:18; 1 Kings 6:29"> On the olive-wood doors were cut designs of winged ones and palm-trees and open flowers, all of them, with the doors, plated with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Then he made pillars of olive-wood for the way into the Temple; the pillars were square:</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:23–25; 1 Kings 5:8"> And two folding doors of cypress-wood, with two leaves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> These were ornamented with designs of winged ones and palm-trees and open flowers, plated over with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 7:12; 2 Chronicles 7:7; Exodus 27:9–19"> And the inner space was walled with three lines of squared stones and a line of cedar-wood boards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Kings 6:1; 2 Chronicles 3:2"> In the fourth year the base of the house was put in its place, in the month Ziv.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Ezra 6:14–15; 1 Kings 7:1; Zechariah 4:9"> And in the eleventh year, in the month Bul, which is the eighth month, the building of the house was complete in every detail, as it had been designed. So he was seven years building it.</span>
