<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 12:42; Luke 11:31; 2 Chronicles 9:1–12"> Now the queen of Sheba, hearing great things of Solomon, came to put his wisdom to the test with hard questions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 25:23; Psalms 4:4; Job 4:2"> And she came to Jerusalem with a very great train, with camels weighted down with spices, and stores of gold and jewels: and when she came to Solomon she had talk with him of everything in her mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 1:5–6; Matthew 13:11; 1 Kings 10:1"> And Solomon gave her answers to all her questions; there was no secret which the king did not make clear to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 12:42; 1 Kings 3:28; 2 Chronicles 9:3–4"> And when the queen of Sheba had seen all the wisdom of Solomon, and the house which he had made,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:16; 1 Kings 4:22–23; 2 Kings 16:18"> And the food at his table, and all his servants seated there, and those who were waiting on him in their places, and their robes, and his wine-servants, and the burned offerings which he made in the house of the Lord, there was no more spirit in her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:5–6"> And she said to the king, The account which was given to me in my country of your acts and your wisdom was true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:9; John 20:25–29; 1 John 3:2"> But I had no faith in what was said about you, till I came and saw for myself; and now I see that it was not half the story; your wisdom and your wealth are much greater than they said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 8:34; Proverbs 10:21; Luke 11:31"> Happy are your wives, happy are these your servants whose place is ever before you, hearing your words of wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 72:2; 1 Kings 5:7; 2 Samuel 8:15"> May the Lord your God be praised, whose pleasure it was to put you on the seat of the kingdom of Israel; because the Lord's love for Israel is eternal, he has made you king, to be their judge in righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 10:2; Matthew 2:11; 1 Kings 9:14"> And she gave the king a hundred and twenty talents of gold, and a great store of spices and jewels: never again was such a wealth of spices seen as that which the queen of Sheba gave King Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 9:27–28; 2 Chronicles 8:18; Psalms 45:9"> And the sea-force of Hiram, in addition to gold from Ophir, came back with much sandal-wood and jewels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:1–31; Psalms 150:3–5; Revelation 14:2–3"> And from the sandal-wood the king made pillars for the house of the Lord, and for the king's house, and instruments of music for the makers of melody: never has such sandal-wood been seen to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; Matthew 15:28; Psalms 20:4"> And King Solomon gave the queen of Sheba all her desire, whatever she made request for, in addition to what he gave her freely from the impulse of his heart. So she went back to her country, she and her servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:13–28; 1 Kings 9:28"> Now the weight of gold which came to Solomon in one year was six hundred and sixty-six talents;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 72:10; 1 Chronicles 9:24; Isaiah 21:13"> In addition to what came to him from the business of the traders, and from all the kings of the Arabians, and from the rulers of the country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 14:26–28; 2 Chronicles 12:9–10; 2 Chronicles 9:15–16"> And Solomon made two hundred body-covers of hammered gold, every one having six hundred shekels of gold in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 7:2; 1 Kings 14:26"> And he made three hundred smaller body-covers of hammered gold, with three pounds of gold in every cover: and the king put them in the house of the Woods of Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 45:8; 1 Kings 10:22; Revelation 20:11"> Then the king made a great ivory seat, plated with the best gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> There were six steps going up to it, and the top of it was round at the back, there were arms on the two sides of the seat, and two lions by the side of the arms;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 24:9; Genesis 49:9; Numbers 23:24"> And twelve lions were placed on the one side and on the other side on the six steps: there was nothing like it in any kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 10:17; 1 Kings 7:2; 2 Chronicles 9:20–22"> And all King Solomon's drinking-vessels were of gold, and all the vessels of the house of the Woods of Lebanon were of the best gold; not one was of silver, for no one gave a thought to silver in the days of King Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Kings 22:48; Genesis 10:4; 2 Chronicles 20:36–37"> For the king had Tarshish-ships at sea with the ships of Hiram; once every three years the Tarshish-ships came with gold and silver and ivory and monkeys and peacocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 3:12–13; Colossians 2:2–3; 1 Kings 4:29–34"> And King Solomon was greater than all the kings of the earth in wealth and in wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 3:12; James 1:5; 1 Kings 3:28"> And from all over the earth they came to see Solomon and to give ear to his wisdom, which God had put in his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 42:11; Psalms 72:10; Psalms 72:15"> And everyone took with him an offering, vessels of silver and vessels of gold, and robes, and coats of metal, and spices, and horses, and beasts of transport, regularly year by year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:25; 1 Kings 4:26; Deuteronomy 17:16"> And Solomon got together war-carriages and horsemen; he had one thousand, four hundred carriages and twelve thousand horsemen, whom he kept, some in the carriage-towns and some with the king at Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:15–17; Deuteronomy 17:17; 2 Chronicles 9:27"> And the king made silver as common as stones in Jerusalem and cedars like the sycamore-trees of the lowlands in number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:16; 2 Chronicles 9:28; Isaiah 31:1–3"> And Solomon's horses came from Egypt and from Kue; the king's traders got them at a price from Kue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 1:4; 2 Kings 7:6–7; Malachi 1:1"> A war-carriage might be got from Egypt for six hundred shekels of silver, and a horse for a hundred and fifty; they got them at the same rate for all the kings of the Hittites and the kings of Aram.</span>
