<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 47:29; Deuteronomy 31:14; 1 Timothy 1:18"> Now the time of David's death came near; and he gave orders to Solomon his son, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 1:6–7; Joshua 23:14; Ephesians 6:10"> I am going the way of all the earth: so be strong and be a man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:9; Deuteronomy 17:18–20; Proverbs 3:1–4"> And keep the orders of the Lord your God, walking in his ways, keeping his laws and his orders and his rules and his words, as they are recorded in the law of Moses; so that you may do well in all you do and wherever you go,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 20:3; 2 Samuel 7:25; 1 Kings 8:25"> So that the Lord may give effect to what he said of me, If your children give attention to their ways, living uprightly before me with all their heart and their soul, you will never be without a man to be king in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:27; 2 Samuel 20:10; 2 Samuel 18:5"> Now you have knowledge of what Joab, the son of Zeruiah, did to me, and to the two captains of the army of Israel, Abner, the son of Ner, and Amasa, the son of Jether, whom he put to death, taking payment for the blood of war in time of peace, and making the band of my clothing and the shoes on my feet red with the blood of one put to death without cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 2:9; Isaiah 57:21; Psalms 37:37"> So be guided by your wisdom, and let not his white head go down to the underworld in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:27–29; 2 Samuel 9:7; 2 Samuel 19:31–40"> But be good to the sons of Barzillai the Gileadite, and let them be guests at your table; for so they came to me when I went in flight from Absalom your brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:5–8; 1 Kings 2:36–46; 2 Samuel 19:16–23"> Now you have with you Shimei, the son of Gera the Benjamite of Bahurim, who put a bitter curse on me on the day when I went to Mahanaim; but he came down to see me at Jordan, and I gave him my oath by the Lord, saying, I will not put you to death by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 2:6; Genesis 42:38; Genesis 44:31"> But do not let him be free from punishment, for you are a wise man; and it will be clear to you what you have to do with him; see that his white head goes down to the underworld in blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 2:29; Acts 13:36; 1 Kings 1:21"> Then David went to rest with his fathers, and his body was put into the earth in the town of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:4–5; 1 Chronicles 29:26–27"> David was king over Israel for forty years: for seven years he was king in Hebron and for thirty-three years in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:1; 1 Chronicles 29:23–25; Psalms 132:12"> And Solomon took his place on the seat of David his father, and his kingdom was made safe and strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:4–5; 1 Kings 1:5–10; Luke 10:5–6"> Then Adonijah, the son of Haggith, came to Bath-sheba, the mother of Solomon. And she said, Come you in peace? And he said, Yes, in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:12; Luke 7:40"> Then he said, I have something to say to you. And she said, Say on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:9–10; 1 Chronicles 28:5–7; 1 Kings 1:25"> And he said, You saw how the kingdom was mine, and all Israel had the idea that I would be their king; but now the kingdom is turned about, and has become my brother's, for it was given to him by the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 30:7; Psalms 132:10"> Now I have one request to make to you, and do not say, No, to me. And she said to him, Say on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:8; 1 Kings 1:2–4; 2 Samuel 3:7"> Then he said, Will you go to Solomon the king (for he will not say, No, to you) and put before him my request that he will give me Abishag the Shunammite for a wife?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 14:15"> And Bath-sheba said, Good! I will make your request to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 45:9; 1 Kings 15:13; Leviticus 19:32"> So Bath-sheba went to King Solomon to have talk with him on Adonijah's account. And the king got up to come to her, and went down low to the earth before her; then he took his place on the king's seat and had a seat made ready for the king's mother and she took her place at his right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–11; Matthew 11:24; John 2:3–4"> Then she said, I have one small request to make to you; do not say, No, to me. And the king said, Say on, my mother, for I will not say, No, to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 1:3; 2 Samuel 16:21–22"> And she said, Let Abishag the Shunammite be given to Adonijah your brother for a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:2; 1 Chronicles 3:5; Matthew 20:22"> Then King Solomon made answer and said to his mother, Why are you requesting me to give Abishag the Shunammite to Adonijah? Take the kingdom for him in addition, for he is my older brother, and Abiathar the priest and Joab, the son of Zeruiah, are on his side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ruth 1:17; Psalms 140:9; Psalms 64:8"> Then King Solomon took an oath by the Lord, saying, May God's punishment be on me if Adonijah does not give payment for these words with his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:10; 1 Chronicles 17:10; 1 Chronicles 17:17"> Now by the living Lord, who has given me my place on the seat of David my father, and made me one of a line of kings, as he gave me his word, truly Adonijah will be put to death this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:18; 1 Kings 2:46; Judges 8:20–21"> And King Solomon sent Benaiah, the son of Jehoiada, and he made an attack on him and put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Joshua 21:18; 1 Samuel 22:20–23; Jeremiah 1:1"> And to Abiathar the priest the king said, Go to Anathoth, to your fields; for death would be your right reward; but I will not put you to death now, because you took up the ark of the Lord God before David my father, and you were with him in all his troubles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:27–36; 1 Samuel 3:12–14; Jeremiah 7:12–14"> So Solomon let Abiathar be priest no longer, so that he might make the word of the Lord come true which he said about the sons of Eli in Shiloh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Kings 1:50; 1 Kings 1:7; 2 Samuel 18:2"> And news of this came to Joab; for Joab had been one of Adonijah's supporters, though he had not been on Absalom's side. Then Joab went in flight to the Tent of the Lord, and put his hands on the horns of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 2:25; Exodus 21:14; 1 Kings 2:46"> And they said to King Solomon, Joab has gone in flight to the Tent of the Lord and is by the altar. Then Solomon sent Benaiah, the son of Jehoiada, saying, Go, make an attack on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And Benaiah came to the Tent of the Lord and said to him, The king says, Come out. And he said, No; but let death come to me here. And Benaiah went back to the king and gave him word of the answer which Joab had given.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:8–9; Numbers 35:33; Exodus 21:14"> And the king said, Do as he has said and make an attack on him there, and put his body into the earth; so that you may take away from me and from my family the blood of one put to death by Joab without cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Judges 9:24; Judges 9:57; Psalms 7:16"> And the Lord will send back his blood on his head, because of the attack he made on two men more upright and better than himself, putting them to the sword without my father's knowledge; even Abner, the son of Ner, captain of the army of Israel, and Amasa, the son of Jether, captain of the army of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; 1 Kings 2:32; Luke 2:14"> So their blood will be on the head of Joab, and on the head of his seed for ever; but for David and his seed and his family and the seat of his kingdom, there will be peace for ever from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 3:1; Joshua 15:61; 1 Kings 2:25"> So Benaiah, the son of Jehoiada, went up, and falling on him, put him to death; and his body was put to rest in his house in the waste land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 2:27; 1 Chronicles 24:3; 1 Chronicles 29:22"> And the king put Benaiah, the son of Jehoiada, in his place over the army; and Zadok the priest he put in the place of Abiathar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:5–9; 1 Kings 2:8–9; Proverbs 20:26"> Then the king sent for Shimei, and said to him, Make a house for yourself in Jerusalem and keep there and go to no other place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:16; 2 Samuel 15:23; Joshua 2:19"> For be certain that on the day when you go out and go over the stream Kidron, death will overtake you: and your blood will be on your head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Kings 20:4; 2 Kings 20:19"> And Shimei said to the king, Very well! as my lord the king has said, so will your servant do. And for a long time Shimei went on living in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:10; 1 Samuel 27:2–3"> But after three years, two of the servants of Shimei went in flight to Achish, son of Maacah, king of Gath. And word was given to Shimei that his servants had gone to Gath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 12:15; Proverbs 15:27; 1 Timothy 6:10"> Then Shimei got up, and making ready his ass, he went to Gath, to Achish, in search of his servants; and he sent and got them from Gath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> And news was given to Solomon that Shimei had gone from Jerusalem to Gath and had come back again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 15:4; 1 Kings 2:36–38; Luke 15:22"> Then the king sent for Shimei, and said to him, Did I not make you take an oath by the Lord, protesting to you and saying, Be certain that on the day when you go out from here, wherever you go, death will overtake you? and you said to me, Very well!</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Romans 13:5; 2 Chronicles 30:12; Ecclesiastes 8:2"> Why then have you not kept the oath of the Lord and the order which I gave you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:5–13; Psalms 7:16; Ezekiel 17:19"> And the king said to Shimei, You have knowledge of all the evil which you did to David my father; and now the Lord has sent back your evil on yourself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Proverbs 25:5; 1 Kings 2:24; Isaiah 9:6–7"> But a blessing will be on King Solomon, and the kingdom of David will keep its place before the Lord for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:1; 1 Kings 2:12; 1 Kings 2:45"> So the king gave orders to Benaiah, the son of Jehoiada; and he went out and, falling on him, put him to death. And Solomon's authority over the kingdom was complete.</span>
