<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 2:8; Matthew 13:21; Matthew 26:31–33"> I have said these things to you so that you may not be in doubt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 9:22; Acts 26:9–11; Romans 10:2–3"> They will put you out of the Synagogues: yes, the time is coming when whoever puts you to death will have the belief that he is doing God's pleasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 15:21; John 17:25; John 8:55"> They will do these things to you because they have not had knowledge of the Father or of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 14:29; Mark 13:23; John 17:12–13"> I have said these things to you so that when the time comes, what I have said may come to your mind. I did not say them to you at the first, because then I was still with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 13:36; John 7:33; John 16:28"> But now I am going to him who sent me; and not one of you says to me, Where are you going?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 14:1; John 16:20–22; Luke 24:17"> But your hearts are full of sorrow because I have said these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 14:26; John 14:16–17; John 15:26"> But what I am saying is true: my going is for your good: for if I do not go away, the Helper will not come to you; but if I go, I will send him to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 2:37; Jude 1:15; Zechariah 12:10"> And he, when he comes, will make the world conscious of sin, and of righteousness, and of being judged:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 15:22–25; John 8:23–24; John 12:47–48"> Of sin, because they have not faith in me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:21; 1 Peter 3:18; Romans 10:3–4"> Of righteousness, because I go to the Father and you will see me no more;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 12:31; 1 John 3:8; Hebrews 2:14"> Of being judged, because the ruler of this world has been judged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 4:33; John 15:15; 1 Corinthians 3:1–2"> I have still much to say to you, but you are not strong enough for it now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 14:26; 1 Corinthians 2:10–13; John 14:17"> However, when he, the Spirit of true knowledge, has come, he will be your guide into all true knowledge: for his words will not come from himself, but whatever has come to his hearing, that he will say: and he will make clear to you the things to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 John 4:13–14; 2 Corinthians 3:14–18; John 15:26"> He will give me glory, because he will take of what is mine, and make it clear to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 17:10; Matthew 11:27; John 3:35"> Everything which the Father has is mine: that is why I say, He will take of what is mine and will make it clear to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 7:33; Acts 1:3; John 14:18–24"> After a little time you will see me no longer; and then again, after a little time, you will see me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 16:16; Mark 9:32; John 16:5"> So some of the disciples said one to another, What is this he is saying, After a little time, you will see me no longer; and then again, after a little time, you will see me? and, I am going to the Father?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 24:25; Matthew 16:9–11; Hebrews 5:12"> So they said again and again, What is this he is saying, A little time? His words are not clear to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 16:30; John 16:16; John 14:19"> Jesus saw that they had a desire to put the question to him, so he said to them, Is this what you are questioning one with another, why I said, After a little time, you will see me no longer; and then again, after a little time, you will see me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 1:2; Matthew 5:4; Psalms 126:5–6"> Truly I say to you, You will be weeping and sorrowing, but the world will be glad: you will be sad, but your sorrow will be turned into joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 113:9; Isaiah 13:8; 1 Thessalonians 5:3"> When a woman is about to give birth she has sorrow, because her hour is come; but when she has given birth to the child, the pain is put out of her mind by the joy that a man has come into the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 1:8; Acts 13:52; John 16:6"> So you have sorrow now: but I will see you again, and your hearts will be glad, and no one will take away your joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 15:7; John 14:13–14; Matthew 21:22"> And on that day you will put no questions to me. Truly I say to you, Whatever request you make to the Father, he will give it to you in my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–8; James 4:2–3; John 15:11"> Up to now you have made no request in my name: do so, and it will be answered, so that your hearts may be full of joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 16:12; Mark 4:13; Matthew 13:34–35"> All this I have said to you in veiled language: but the time is coming when I will no longer say things in veiled language but will give you knowledge of the Father clearly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 8:34; John 17:24; John 17:19"> In that day you will make requests in my name: and I do not say that I will make prayer to the Father for you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 14:21; John 14:23; John 17:23"> For the Father himself gives his love to you, because you have given your love to me and have had faith that I came from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 13:1; John 13:3; John 8:14"> I came out from the Father and have come into the world: again, I go away from the world and go to the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 16:25"> His disciples said, Now you are talking clearly and not in veiled language.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 21:17; John 17:8; John 16:17"> Now we are certain that you have knowledge of all things and have no need for anyone to put questions to you: through this we have faith that you came from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 9:44–45; John 13:38"> Jesus made answer, Have you faith now?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; Matthew 26:31; John 8:29"> See, a time is coming, yes, it is now here, when you will go away in all directions, every man to his house, and I will be by myself: but I am not by myself, because the Father is with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 14:27; 1 John 5:4; 2 Thessalonians 3:16"> I have said all these things to you so that in me you may have peace. In the world you have trouble: but take heart! I have overcome the world.</span>
