<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 1:1; John 17:5; Revelation 19:13"> From the first he was the Word, and the Word was in relation with God and was God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> This Word was from the first in relation with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 1:16–17; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Revelation 4:11"> All things came into existence through him, and without him nothing was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 8:12; John 12:46; John 5:26"> What came into existence in him was life, and the life was the light of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 3:19–20; John 12:36–40; 1 Corinthians 2:14"> And the light goes on shining in the dark; it is not overcome by the dark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; John 3:28; Isaiah 40:3–5"> There was a man sent from God, whose name was John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 19:4; John 1:36; John 5:33–35"> He came for witness, to give witness about the light, so that all men might have faith through him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:20; John 3:28; Acts 19:4"> He himself was not the light: he was sent to give witness about the light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 12:46; John 1:4; 1 John 2:8"> The true light, which gives light to every man, was then coming into the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 17:25; 1 John 3:1; 1 Corinthians 2:8"> He was in the world, the world which came into being through him, but the world had no knowledge of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:14; Isaiah 53:2–3; John 3:32"> He came to the things which were his and his people did not take him to their hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 3:26; Romans 8:14; 1 John 3:1"> To all those who did so take him, however, he gave the right of becoming children of God — that is, to those who had faith in his name:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 John 3:9; James 1:18; 1 Peter 1:23"> Whose birth was from God and not from blood, or from an impulse of the flesh and man's desire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 John 4:9; 1 Timothy 3:16; Isaiah 40:5"> And so the Word became flesh and took a place among us for a time; and we saw his glory — such glory as is given to an only son by his father — saw it to be true and full of grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 3:11; Colossians 1:17; Luke 3:16"> John gave witness about him, crying, This is he of whom I said, He who is coming after me is put over me because he was in existence before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 1:19; Romans 5:2; Ephesians 2:5–10"> From his full measure we have all been given grace on grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 6:14; John 14:6; Romans 3:19–26"> For the law was given through Moses; grace and the true way of life are ours through Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 1:15; John 6:46; Matthew 11:27"> No man has seen God at any time; the only Son, who is on the breast of the Father, he has made clear what God is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 3:15–18; John 10:24; Acts 19:4"> And this is the witness of John when the Jews sent priests and Levites from Jerusalem to him with the question, Who are you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 1:7–8; Matthew 3:11–12; John 3:28–36"> He said quite openly and straightforwardly, I am not the Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Deuteronomy 18:15–18; Matthew 11:14"> And they said to him, What then? Are you Elijah? And he said, I am not. Are you the prophet? And his answer was, I am not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:13"> So they said to him, Who are you then? We have to give some answer to those who sent us. What have you to say about yourself?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–5; Mark 1:3; Matthew 3:3"> He said, I am the voice of one crying in the waste land, Make straight the way of the Lord, as said Isaiah the prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 11:53; Matthew 23:13–15; Luke 11:39–44"> Those who had been sent came from the Pharisees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:18; Deuteronomy 18:15; John 1:20–22"> And they put this question to him, saying, Why then are you giving baptism if you are not the Christ, or Elijah, or the prophet?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 1:10–11; Luke 3:16; Acts 1:5"> John's answer was: I give baptism with water; but there is one among you of whom you have no knowledge;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 1:30; Mark 1:7; Matthew 3:11"> It is he who is coming after me; I am not good enough to undo his shoes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 10:40; John 3:23; Judges 7:24"> These things took place at Bethany on the other side of the Jordan, where John was giving baptism.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; 1 John 2:2; 1 John 3:5"> The day after, John sees Jesus coming to him and says, See, here is the Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 1:27; John 1:15; Luke 3:16"> This is he of whom I said, One is coming after me who is put over me because he was in existence before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Luke 1:17; Malachi 4:2–5"> I myself had no knowledge of him, but I came giving baptism with water so that he might be seen openly by Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 1:10; Luke 3:22; Matthew 3:16"> And John gave this witness, saying, I saw the Spirit coming down from heaven like a dove and resting on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 1:5; Matthew 3:11; Luke 3:16"> I had no knowledge who he was, but he who sent me to give baptism with water said to me, The one on whom you see the Spirit coming down and resting, it is he who gives baptism with the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 2:7; John 1:49; Psalms 89:26–27"> This I saw myself and my witness is that he is the Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 3:25–26; Malachi 3:16"> The day after, John was there again with two of his disciples;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 1:29; 1 Peter 1:19–20; Isaiah 65:1–2"> And looking at Jesus while he was walking he said, See, there is the Lamb of God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Romans 10:17; John 1:43; Zechariah 8:21"> Hearing what he said, the two disciples went after Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 18:7; John 3:2; John 1:49"> And Jesus, turning round, saw them coming after him and said to them, What are you looking for? They said to him, Rabbi (which is to say, Master), where are you living?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 24:29; Matthew 11:28–30; John 6:37"> He said to them, Come and see. They went with him then and saw where he was living; and they were with him all that day: it was then about the tenth hour of the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 1:16–20; Matthew 4:18–22; John 1:40–42"> Andrew, Simon Peter's brother, was one of the two men who, hearing what John said, went after Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 4:25; John 1:45; Psalms 89:20"> Early in the morning he came across his brother and said to him, We have made discovery! It is the Messiah! (which is to say, the Christ).</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 21:15–17; 1 Corinthians 3:22; 1 Corinthians 15:5"> And he took him to Jesus. Looking at him fixedly Jesus said, You are Simon, the son of John; your name will be Cephas (which is to say, Peter).</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 9:9; John 14:8; Isaiah 65:1"> The day after this, Jesus had a desire to go into Galilee. He came across Philip and said to him, Come and be my disciple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 12:21; Matthew 11:21; Mark 8:22"> Now Philip's town was Beth-saida, where Andrew and Peter came from.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:18–22; Luke 24:27; Micah 5:2"> Philip came across Nathanael and said to him, We have made a discovery! It is he of whom Moses, in the law, and the prophets were writing, Jesus of Nazareth, the son of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 7:52; John 7:41–42; John 4:29"> Nazareth! said Nathanael, Is it possible for any good to come out of Nazareth? Philip said to him, Come and see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Revelation 14:5; Psalms 32:2; Psalms 73:1"> Jesus saw Nathanael coming to him and said of him, See, here is a true son of Israel in whom there is nothing false.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Isaiah 65:24; John 2:25; Psalms 139:1–2"> Nathanael said to him, Where did you get knowledge of me? In answer Jesus said, Before Philip was talking with you, while you were still under the fig-tree, I saw you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:15; Zechariah 9:9; Matthew 27:42"> Nathanael said to him, Rabbi, you are the Son of God, you are King of Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 11:40; John 20:29; Luke 1:45"> In answer Jesus said to him, You have faith because I said to you, I saw you under the fig-tree. You will see greater things than these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Genesis 28:12; Acts 7:56; Luke 22:43"> And he said to him, Truly I say to you all, You will see heaven opening and God's angels going up and coming down on the Son of man.</span>
</div>
