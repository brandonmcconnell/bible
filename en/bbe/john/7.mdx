<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 5:16–18; John 4:3; Matthew 21:38"> After this, Jesus went from place to place in Galilee. He did not go about in Judaea, because the Jews were looking for a chance to put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 14:16–19; Deuteronomy 16:13–16; Exodus 23:16–17"> But the feast of the Jews, the feast of tents, was near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 12:46; John 7:5; Luke 8:19"> So his brothers said to him, Go away from here into Judaea so that your disciples may see the works which you do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 18:20; Proverbs 18:1–2; Matthew 23:5"> Because no man does things secretly if he has a desire that men may have knowledge of him. If you do these things, let yourself be seen by all men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 3:21; John 1:11–13; Micah 7:5–6"> For even his brothers had no belief in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 7:8; John 2:4; John 7:30"> Jesus said to them, My time is still to come, but any time is good for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:18–19; John 3:19; Galatians 4:16"> It is not possible for you to be hated by the world; but I am hated by it, because I give witness that what it does is evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 7:6; John 8:20; John 7:30"> Go you up to the feast: I am not going up now to the feast because my time has not fully come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Having said these things to them, he still kept in Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 5:13; Matthew 10:16; Isaiah 42:2–3"> But after his brothers had gone up to the feast, then he went up, not publicly, but in secret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 11:56"> At the feast the Jews were looking for him and saying, Where is he?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 7:40–43; John 9:16; John 7:32"> And there was much discussion about him among the mass of the people. Some said, He is a good man; but others said, No, he is giving people false ideas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 9:22; John 19:38; John 20:19"> But no man said anything about him openly for fear of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 7:28; Luke 19:47; John 7:37"> Now in the middle of the feast Jesus went up to the Temple and was teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 4:22; Matthew 7:28–29; Matthew 13:54"> Then the Jews were surprised and said, How has this man got knowledge of books? He has never been to school.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 14:10; John 14:24; John 17:8"> Jesus gave them this answer: It is not my teaching, but his who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 8:31–32; John 8:43; Jeremiah 31:33–34"> If any man is ready to do God's pleasure he will have knowledge of the teaching and of where it comes from — from God or from myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 5:41; 1 Corinthians 10:31–33; John 13:31–32"> The man whose words come from himself is looking for glory for himself, but he who is looking for the glory of him who sent him — that man is true and there is no evil in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 1:17; John 11:53; Matthew 12:14"> Did not Moses give you the law? Even so, not one of you keeps the law. Why have you a desire to put me to death?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 10:20; John 8:48; John 8:52"> The people said in answer, You have an evil spirit: who has any desire to put you to death?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 7:23; John 5:2–11"> This was the answer of Jesus: I have done one work and you are all surprised at it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 12:3; Genesis 17:10–14; Romans 4:9–11"> Moses gave you circumcision — not that it comes from Moses, but from the fathers — and even on the Sabbath you give a child circumcision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 12:5; John 5:14–16; Matthew 12:2"> If a child is given circumcision on the Sabbath so that the law of Moses may not be broken, why are you angry with me because I made a man completely well on the Sabbath?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 11:3–4; James 2:9; John 8:15"> Let not your decisions be based on what you see, but on righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 7:10–11"> Then some of the people of Jerusalem said, Is not this the man whose death is desired?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7–8; John 7:48; John 12:42"> And here he is talking openly and they say nothing to him! Is it possible that the rulers have knowledge that this is truly the Christ?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Micah 5:2; John 7:41–42; John 6:42"> However, it is clear to us where this man comes from: but when the Christ comes no one will have knowledge where he comes from.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 8:42; John 8:19; John 8:26"> Then, when he was teaching in the Temple, Jesus said with a loud voice, You have knowledge of me and you have knowledge of where I come from; and I have not come of myself; but there is One who has sent me; he is true, but you have no knowledge of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:55; Matthew 11:27; John 17:18"> I have knowledge of him because I came from him and he sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 8:20; John 10:39; John 7:6"> Then they had a desire to take him: but no man put hands on him because his hour was still to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 12:42; Matthew 12:23; John 10:41–42"> And numbers of the people had belief in him, and they said, When the Christ comes will he do more signs than this man has done?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 18:3; Luke 22:52–53; John 11:47–48"> This discussion of the people came to the ears of the Pharisees; and the chief priests and the Pharisees sent servants to take him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 16:5; John 13:33; Mark 16:19"> Then Jesus said, I will be with you a little longer and then I go to him who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24–31; John 13:33–36; John 14:6"> You will be looking for me, and you will not see me: and where I am you may not come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="James 1:1; Isaiah 11:12; Zephaniah 3:10"> So the Jews said among themselves, To what place is he going where we will not see him? will he go to the Jews living among the Greeks and become the teacher of the Greeks?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 3:9; John 7:34; John 12:34"> What is this saying of his, You will be looking for me and will not see me, and where I am you may not come?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 55:1; John 4:14; John 6:35"> On the last day, the great day of the feast, Jesus got up and said in a loud voice, If any man is in need of drink let him come to me and I will give it to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 4:14; Isaiah 44:3; Isaiah 58:11"> He who has faith in me, out of his body, as the Writings have said, will come rivers of living water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 2:17; Joel 2:28; John 14:16–17"> This he said of the Spirit which would be given to those who had faith in him: the Spirit had not been given then, because the glory of Jesus was still to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 21:11; John 1:21; John 6:14"> When these words came to their ears, some of the people said, This is certainly the prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 7:52; John 1:46; John 1:49"> Others said, This is the Christ. But others said, Not so; will the Christ come from Galilee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Matthew 2:5; Luke 2:4"> Do not the Writings say that the Christ comes of the seed of David and from Beth-lehem, the little town where David was?</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 9:16; John 10:19; John 7:12"> So there was a division among the people because of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 7:30; Acts 23:11; John 8:20"> And some of them had a desire to take him; but no man put hands on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 7:32; Acts 5:21–27"> Then the servants went back to the chief priests and Pharisees, who said to them, Why have you not got him with you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Matthew 7:28–29; Luke 4:22; John 7:26"> The servants made answer, No man ever said things like this man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 7:12; 2 Corinthians 6:8; 2 Kings 18:32"> Then the Pharisees said to them, Have you, like the others, been given false ideas?</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 12:42; 1 Corinthians 1:20; 1 Corinthians 2:8"> Have any of the rulers belief in him, or any one of the Pharisees?</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:20–21; John 9:40; John 9:34"> But these people who have no knowledge of the law are cursed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 19:39; John 3:1–2"> Nicodemus — he who had come to Jesus before, being himself one of them — said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Proverbs 18:13; Deuteronomy 1:17; Deuteronomy 19:15–19"> Is a man judged by our law before it has given him a hearing and has knowledge of what he has done?</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 7:41; John 1:46; Isaiah 9:1–2"> This was their answer: And do you come from Galilee? Make search and you will see that no prophet comes out of Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Psalms 33:10; Job 5:12–13; Psalms 76:10"> &#91;And every man went to his house;</span>
