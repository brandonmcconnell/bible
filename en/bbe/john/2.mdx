<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 4:46; Proverbs 31:10–12; John 21:2"> On the third day two people were going to be married at Cana in Galilee. The mother of Jesus was there:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:40–49; Matthew 10:40–42; 1 Corinthians 7:39"> And Jesus with his disciples came as guests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 104:15; Philippians 4:6; Isaiah 24:11"> When they had not enough wine, the mother of Jesus said to him, They have no wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 7:6; John 13:1; John 7:30"> Jesus said to her, Woman, this is not your business; my time is still to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:14; Genesis 6:22; Hebrews 11:8"> His mother said to the servants, Whatever he says to you, do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 3:25; Hebrews 9:10; Ephesians 5:26"> Now six pots of stone, every one taking two or three firkins of water, were placed there for the purpose of washing, as is the way of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 4:2–6; John 2:5; John 2:3"> Jesus said to the servants, Make the pots full of water. And they made them full to the top.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 2:9; Proverbs 3:5–6; Romans 13:7"> Then he said to them, Now take some, and give it to the master of the feast. So they took it to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 4:46; John 7:17; Psalms 119:100"> After tasting the water which had now become wine, the master of the feast (having no idea where it came from, though it was clear to the servants who took the water out) sent for the newly-married man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 7:16–17; Proverbs 9:1–6; Luke 16:25"> And said to him, Every man first puts out his best wine and when all have had enough he puts out what is not so good; but you have kept the good wine till now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 20:30–31; John 1:14; John 3:2"> This, the first of his signs, Jesus did at Cana in Galilee and let his glory be seen openly; and his disciples put their faith in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:46; Matthew 13:55–56; Mark 6:3"> After this he went down to Capernaum, with his mother, his brothers, and his disciples, and they were there not more than two or three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 2:41; John 11:55; John 2:23"> The time of the Passover of the Jews was near and Jesus went up to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 19:45–46; Mark 11:15; Mark 11:17"> And there in the Temple he saw men trading in oxen and sheep and doves, and he saw the changers of money in their seats:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 18:6; Zechariah 4:6; 2 Corinthians 10:4"> And he made a whip of small cords and put them all out of the Temple, with the sheep and the oxen, sending in all directions the small money of the changers and overturning their tables;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 21:13; Jeremiah 7:11; Mark 11:17"> And to those who were trading in doves he said, Take these things away; do not make my Father's house a market.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 69:9; Psalms 119:139"> And it came to the minds of the disciples that the Writings say, I am on fire with passion for your house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Mark 8:11; Mark 11:27–28"> Then the Jews put this question to him: What sign of authority have you to give us, seeing that you do these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 14:58; Mark 15:29; Matthew 27:40"> And Jesus said to them, Send destruction on this Temple and I will put it up again in three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The Jews said, The building of this Temple took forty-six years; and you will put it up in three days!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:19; 1 Corinthians 3:16; Ephesians 2:20–22"> But his words were about that holy building which was his body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 12:16; John 16:4; Luke 24:7–8"> So when he had come back again from the dead, the memory of these words came back to the disciples, and they had faith in the holy Writings and in the word which Jesus had said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 7:31; Luke 8:13; John 6:14"> Now while he was in Jerusalem at the feast of the Passover, a great number of people came to have faith in his name, after seeing the signs which he did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:64; Hebrews 4:13; Acts 1:24"> But Jesus did not have faith in them, because he had knowledge of them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 13:11; Matthew 9:4; John 6:64"> He had no need for any witness about man; for he himself had knowledge of what was in man.</span>
