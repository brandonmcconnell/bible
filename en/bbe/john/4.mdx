<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 3:26; John 3:22; 2 Corinthians 4:5"> Now when it was clear to the Lord that word had come to the ears of the Pharisees that Jesus was making more disciples than John and was giving them baptism</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 10:48; 1 Corinthians 1:13–17"> (Though, in fact, it was his disciples who gave baptism, not Jesus himself),</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 10:23; John 10:40; John 2:11"> He went out of Judaea into Galilee again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 17:11; Luke 2:49; Luke 9:51–52"> And it was necessary for him to go through Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 24:32; Genesis 33:19; Genesis 48:22"> So he came to a town of Samaria which was named Sychar, near to the bit of land which Jacob gave to his son Joseph:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; Hebrews 2:17; Matthew 8:24"> Now Jacob's fountain was there. Jesus, being tired after his journey, was resting by the fountain. It was about the sixth hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:42; 1 Kings 17:10; Genesis 24:43"> A woman of Samaria came to get water, and Jesus said to her, Give me some water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 9:13; John 4:39; John 4:5"> For his disciples had gone to the town to get food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 10:28; Matthew 10:5; 2 Kings 17:24–41"> The woman of Samaria said to him, Why do you, a Jew, make a request for water to me, a woman of Samaria? She said this because Jews have nothing to do with the people of Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 4:14; Revelation 7:17; John 7:37–39"> In answer Jesus said, If you had knowledge of what God gives freely and who it is who says to you, Give me water, you would make your prayer to him, and he would give you living water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; John 3:4"> The woman said to him, Sir, you have no vessel and the fountain is deep; from where will you get the living water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 4:6; John 8:53; Matthew 12:42"> Are you greater than our father Jacob who gave us the fountain and took the water of it himself, with his children and his cattle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 6:27; Isaiah 65:13–14; Luke 16:24"> Jesus said to her, Everyone who takes this water will be in need of it again:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 7:38–39; John 6:35; Isaiah 49:10"> But whoever takes the water I give him will never be in need of drink again; for the water I give him will become in him a fountain of eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 6:34; Romans 8:5; Psalms 4:6"> The woman said to him, Sir, give me this water, so that I may not be in need again of drink and will not have to come all this way for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Revelation 2:23; John 4:18"> Jesus said to her, Go, get your husband and come back here with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> In answer, the woman said, I have no husband. Jesus said to her, You have said rightly, I have no husband:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:20; 1 Corinthians 7:10–11; Romans 7:3"> You have had five husbands, and the man you have now is not your husband: that was truly said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 7:39; John 6:14; John 9:17"> The woman said to him, Sir, I see that you are a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 12:6–7; Deuteronomy 27:12; Deuteronomy 11:29"> Our fathers gave worship on this mountain, but you Jews say that the right place for worship is in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:8; Malachi 1:11; John 4:23"> Jesus said to her, Woman, take my word for this; the time is coming when you will not give worship to the Father on this mountain or in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 17:23; Isaiah 2:3; Romans 9:4–5"> You give worship, but without knowledge of what you are worshipping: we give worship to what we have knowledge of: for salvation comes from the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 3:3; John 5:25; Matthew 15:7–9"> But the time is coming, and is even now here, when the true worshippers will give worship to the Father in the true way of the spirit, for these are the worshippers desired by the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:17; Matthew 15:8–9; Philippians 3:3"> God is Spirit: then let his worshippers give him worship in the true way of the spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 1:49; Daniel 9:24–26; John 1:41–42"> The woman said to him, I am certain that the Messiah, who is named Christ, is coming; when he comes he will make all things clear to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 8:24; Mark 14:61–62; Romans 10:20–21"> Jesus said to her, I, who am talking to you, am he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 7:39; John 4:8–9"> At that point the disciples came back, and they were surprised to see him talking to a woman; but not one of them said to him, What is your purpose? or, Why are you talking to her?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 4:7; Luke 24:33; Mark 16:8–10"> Then the woman put down her water-pot and went into the town, and said to the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Revelation 22:17; 1 Corinthians 14:24–25; John 4:25"> Come and see a man who has been talking to me of everything I ever did! Is it possible that this is the Christ?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Romans 5:20; Acts 8:5–8; Luke 17:16–18"> So they went out of the town and came to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 1:38; Genesis 24:33; Acts 16:30–34"> While this was taking place, the disciples were saying to Jesus, Master, take some food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 23:12; Psalms 119:103; John 4:34"> But he said to them, I have food of which you have no knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 16:6–11; Luke 9:45"> So the disciples said one to another, Did anyone give him food?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 6:38; John 5:30; Job 23:12"> Jesus said, My food is to do the pleasure of him who sent me and to make his work complete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 10:2–3; Matthew 9:37–38; John 4:30"> You would say, Four months from now is the time of the grain-cutting. Take a look, I say to you, at the fields; they are even now white for cutting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="James 5:19–20; Romans 1:13; 1 Corinthians 3:5–9"> He who does the cutting now has his reward; he is getting together fruit for eternal life, so that he who did the planting and he who gets in the grain may have joy together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Micah 6:15; Job 31:8; Luke 19:21"> In this the saying is a true one, One does the planting, and another gets in the grain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 1:7; 2 Chronicles 36:15; Acts 10:42–43"> I sent you to get in grain which you had no hand in planting: other men did that work, and you take the reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 4:29; John 4:5; John 10:41–42"> Now a number of the people of that town had faith in him because of the woman's witness: He has been talking to me of everything I ever did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Revelation 3:20; Acts 16:15; Luke 24:29"> So when the people came to him they made request to him to be among them for a time, and he was there two days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:4–5; Acts 1:8; Acts 8:12"> And a great number more of them came to have faith in him because of what he himself said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 John 4:14; John 1:29; 1 John 5:20"> And they said to the woman, Now we have faith, but not because of your story: we ourselves have given ear to his words, and we are certain that he is truly the Saviour of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 4:13; Romans 15:8; Mark 7:27–28"> And after the two days he went on from there into Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 13:57; Luke 4:24; Mark 6:4"> For Jesus himself said that a prophet has no honour in the country of his birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 2:23; John 2:13–16; Deuteronomy 16:16"> So when he came into Galilee, the Galilaeans took him to their hearts because of the things which they had seen him do in Jerusalem at the feast — they themselves having been there at the feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 2:1–11; Luke 7:2; Hosea 5:15"> So he came to Cana in Galilee, where he had made the water wine. And there was a certain man of high position whose son was ill at Capernaum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 4:3; John 11:21; John 11:32"> When it came to his ears that Jesus had come from Judaea into Galilee, he went to him and made a request that he would come down to his son, who was near to death, and make him well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 20:29; Hebrews 2:4; 1 Corinthians 1:22"> Then Jesus said to him, You will not have faith if you do not see signs and wonders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Mark 5:23; Psalms 88:10–12; Psalms 40:17"> The man said, Sir, come down before my boy is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 8:13; Mark 7:29–30; Romans 4:20–21"> And Jesus said, Go in peace; your son is living. The man had faith in the word which Jesus said to him and went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="1 Kings 17:23; John 4:50; John 4:53"> And while he was going down, his servants came to him and said, Your boy is living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> So he put a question to them as to the hour when he became better; and they said to him, The disease went from him yesterday at the seventh hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Psalms 107:20; Luke 19:9; Acts 16:34"> It was clear then to the father that this was the very time at which Jesus said to him, Your son is living. And he had faith in Jesus, he and all his family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 4:45; John 2:1–11"> Now this is the second sign which Jesus did after he had come out of Judaea into Galilee.</span>
