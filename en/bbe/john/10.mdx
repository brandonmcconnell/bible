<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 7:15; John 10:8–10; Romans 16:18"> Truly I say to you, He who does not go through the door into the place where the sheep are kept, but gets in by some other way, is a thief and an outlaw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 10:14; Acts 20:28; 1 Peter 5:4"> He who goes in by the door is the keeper of the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:11–16; 2 Timothy 2:19; John 10:4"> The porter lets him in; and the sheep give ear to his voice; he says over the names of the sheep, and takes them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:30; John 10:16; John 12:26"> When he has got them all out, he goes before them, and the sheep go after him, for they have knowledge of his voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 John 4:5–6; Colossians 2:6–10; Mark 4:24"> They will not go after another who is not their keeper, but will go from him in flight, because his voice is strange to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 56:11; Psalms 82:5; Proverbs 28:5"> In this Jesus was teaching them in the form of a story: but what he said was not clear to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 14:6; Ephesians 2:18; John 10:1"> So Jesus said again, Truly I say to you, I am the door of the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2; Jeremiah 23:1; John 10:1"> All who came before me are thieves and outlaws: but the sheep did not give ear to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 14:6; Ephesians 2:18; John 10:7"> I am the door: if any man goes in through me he will have salvation, and will go in and go out, and will get food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 19:10; John 6:51; John 12:47"> The thief comes only to take the sheep and to put them to death: he comes for their destruction: I have come so that they may have life and have it in greater measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 10:14–15; Psalms 23:1; John 15:13"> I am the good keeper of sheep: the good keeper gives his life for the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 20:29; Zechariah 11:16–17; Ezekiel 34:2–6"> He who is a servant, and not the keeper or the owner of the sheep, sees the wolf coming and goes in flight, away from the sheep; and the wolf comes down on them and sends them in all directions:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 2:20; John 12:6; Acts 18:17"> Because he is a servant he has no interest in the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 10:27; John 10:11; 2 Timothy 2:19"> I am the good keeper; I have knowledge of my sheep, and they have knowledge of me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 1:4; John 10:11; Isaiah 53:10"> Even as the Father has knowledge of me and I of the Father; and I am giving my life for the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 56:8; John 11:52; 1 Peter 2:25"> And I have other sheep which are not of this field: I will be their guide in the same way, and they will give ear to my voice, so there will be one flock and one keeper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 10:18; John 10:11; Hebrews 2:9"> For this reason am I loved by the Father, because I give up my life so that I may take it again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 2:9; Titus 2:14; John 2:19–21"> No one takes it away from me; I give it up of myself. I have power to give it up, and I have power to take it again. These orders I have from my Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 9:16; 1 Corinthians 3:3; John 7:40–43"> There was a division again among the Jews because of these words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:20; Mark 3:21; John 8:47–48"> And a number of them said, He has an evil spirit and is out of his mind; why do you give ear to him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 4:11; Isaiah 35:5–6; Psalms 94:9"> Others said, These are not the words of one who has an evil spirit. Is it possible for an evil spirit to make blind people see?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Then came the feast of the opening of the Temple in Jerusalem: it was winter;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 5:12; Acts 3:11"> And Jesus was walking in the Temple, in Solomon's covered way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 22:67–70; John 1:19; John 8:25"> Then the Jews came round him, saying, how long are you going to keep us in doubt? If you are the Christ, say so clearly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 10:38; John 8:58; John 14:11"> Jesus said in answer, I have said it and you have no belief: the works which I do in my Father's name, these give witness about me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 8:47; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4; John 12:37–40"> But you have no belief because you are not of my sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 10:16; John 10:14; Revelation 3:20"> My sheep give ear to my voice, and I have knowledge of them, and they come after me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 6:39–40; John 6:37; John 3:15–16"> And I give them eternal life; they will never come to destruction, and no one will ever take them out of my hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 6:37; John 17:2; John 17:9"> That which my Father has given to me has more value than all; and no one is able to take anything out of the Father's hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 1:1–2; John 14:9; John 17:21–23"> I and my Father are one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 5:18; John 8:59; 1 Samuel 30:6"> Then the Jews took up stones again to send at him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 John 3:12; John 5:36; Psalms 35:12"> Jesus said to them in answer, I have let you see a number of good works from the Father; for which of those works are you stoning me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 5:18; Leviticus 24:16; John 10:30"> This was their answer: We are not stoning you for a good work but for evil words; because being a man you make yourself God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 82:6–7; Exodus 7:1; Psalms 82:1"> In answer, Jesus said, Is there not a saying in your law, I said, You are gods?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Romans 13:1; Luke 16:17"> If he said they were gods, to whom the word of God came (and the Writings may not be broken),</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 5:17–18; John 3:17; Jeremiah 1:5"> Do you say of him whom the Father made holy and sent into the world, Your words are evil; because I said, I am God's Son?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 15:24; John 10:25; John 12:37–40"> If I am not doing the works of my Father, do not have belief in me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 14:20; John 10:25; John 10:30"> But if I am doing them, then have belief in the works even if you have no belief in me; so that you may see clearly and be certain that the Father is in me and I am in the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 7:30; John 8:59; John 7:44"> Then again they made an attempt to take him; but he got away from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 1:28; John 11:54; John 7:1"> And he went again to the other side of the Jordan, to the place where John first gave baptism; and he was there for a time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 1:33–34; John 1:29–30; Mark 1:37"> And a great number of people came to him, saying, John did no sign: but everything John said of this man was true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 4:41; John 2:23; John 7:31"> And a number came to have faith in him there.</span>
