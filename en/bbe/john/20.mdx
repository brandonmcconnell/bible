<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 16:9; Matthew 27:60; John 19:25"> Now on the first day of the week, very early, while it was still dark, Mary Magdalene came to the place and saw that the stone had been taken away from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 13:23; John 20:13; John 21:24"> Then she went running to Simon Peter, and to the other disciple who was loved by Jesus, and said to them, They have taken away the Lord out of the place of the dead and we have no knowledge where they have put him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 24:12"> So Peter and the other disciple went out to the place of the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:23; 2 Corinthians 8:12; 1 Corinthians 9:24"> They went running together, and the other disciple got in front of Peter and came first to the hole in the rock;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 19:40; John 20:11; John 11:44"> And looking in, he saw the linen bands on the earth; but he did not go in,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:17; John 21:7; Matthew 16:15–16"> Then Simon Peter came after him and went into the hole in the rock; and he saw the linen bands on the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 11:44"> And the cloth, which had been round his head, not with the linen bands but rolled up in a place by itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 20:25; John 20:4; John 1:50"> Then the other disciple who came there first went in; and he saw and belief came to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 16:10; Isaiah 25:8; Isaiah 53:10–12"> For at that time they had no knowledge that the Writings said that he would have to come again from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 7:53; John 16:32"> So then the disciples went away again to their houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 20:5"> But Mary was still there outside the hole in the rock, weeping; and while she was weeping and looking into the hole,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 24:3–7; Revelation 3:4; Mark 16:5–6"> She saw two angels in white seated where the body of Jesus had been, one at the head and the other at the feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 20:15; John 2:4; 1 Samuel 1:8"> They said to her, Woman, why are you weeping? She said to them, Because they have taken away my Lord, and I have no knowledge where they have put him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 24:16; John 21:4; Matthew 28:9"> And then looking round, she saw Jesus there, but had no idea that it was Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 20:13; John 18:7; John 1:38"> Jesus said to her, Woman, why are you weeping? who are you looking for? She, taking him for the gardener, said to him, Sir, if you have taken him away from here, say where you have put him and I will take him away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 10:3; John 1:38; Acts 10:3"> Jesus said to her, Mary! Turning, she said to him in Hebrew, Rabboni! (which is to say, Master).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 16:28; Ephesians 4:8–10; Galatians 4:6–7"> Jesus said to her, Do not put your hand on me, for I have not gone up to the Father: but go to my brothers and say to them, I go up to my Father and your Father, to my God and your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 24:10; Matthew 28:10; John 20:1"> Mary Magdalene went with the news to the disciples, and said she had seen the Lord and that he had said these things to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 20:26; John 14:27; John 20:21"> At evening on that day, the first day of the week, when, for fear of the Jews, the doors were shut where the disciples were, Jesus came among them and said to them, May peace be with you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 16:22; John 16:20; John 20:27"> And when he had said this, he let them see his hands and his side. Then the disciples were glad when they saw the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 17:18–19; John 13:20; John 3:17"> And Jesus said to them again, May peace be with you! As the Father sent me, even so I now send you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 33:4; Genesis 2:7; Acts 8:15"> And when he had said this, breathing on them, he said to them, Let the Holy Spirit come on you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 18:18; Matthew 16:19; Mark 2:5–10"> Any to whom you give forgiveness, will be made free from their sins; and any from whom you keep back forgiveness, will still be in their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 11:16; John 6:66–67; Hebrews 10:25"> Now Thomas, one of the twelve, named Didymus, was not with them when Jesus came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 6:30; Mark 16:11; Hebrews 3:12"> So the other disciples said to him, We have seen the Lord. But he said to them, If I do not see in his hands the print of the nails and put my finger into the print of the nails, and if I do not put my hand into his side, I will never have belief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 20:19; Isaiah 54:10; John 20:21"> And after eight days, his disciples were again in the house and Thomas was with them. Though the doors were shut, Jesus came, and taking his place in the middle of them, he said, May peace be with you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 20:25; 1 John 1:1–2; John 20:20"> Then he said to Thomas, Put out your finger, and see my hands; and put your hand here into my side: and be no longer in doubt but have belief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 1:1; Isaiah 9:6; Isaiah 7:14"> And Thomas said in answer, My Lord and my God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Peter 1:8; 2 Corinthians 5:7; Hebrews 11:1"> Jesus said to him, Because you have seen me you have belief: a blessing will be on those who have belief though they have not seen me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 21:25; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; Romans 15:4"> A number of other signs Jesus did before his disciples which are not recorded in this book:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 10:43; John 3:15–16; 1 John 5:1"> But these are recorded, so that you may have faith that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and so that, having this faith you may have life in his name.</span>
