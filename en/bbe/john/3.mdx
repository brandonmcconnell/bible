<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 19:39; John 7:47–50; John 3:10"> Now there was among the Pharisees a man named Nicodemus, who was one of the rulers of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 5:36; Acts 10:38; Acts 2:22"> He came to Jesus by night and said to him, Rabbi, we are certain that you have come from God as a teacher, because no man would be able to do these signs which you do if God was not with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:17; John 3:5–6; 1 Peter 1:23–25"> Jesus said to him, Truly, I say to you, Without a new birth no man is able to see the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; 1 Corinthians 1:18; John 3:3"> Nicodemus said to him, How is it possible for a man to be given birth when he is old? Is he able to go into his mother's body a second time and come to birth again?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:25–27; Ephesians 5:26; Acts 2:38"> Jesus said in answer, Truly, I say to you, If a man's birth is not from water and from the Spirit, it is not possible for him to go into the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 5:16–21; Ezekiel 36:26–27; Romans 8:4–9"> That which has birth from the flesh is flesh, and that which has birth from the Spirit is spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 3:3; Ephesians 4:22–24; Romans 12:1–2"> Do not be surprised that I say to you, It is necessary for you to have a second birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:4–5; 1 Corinthians 2:11; Acts 2:2"> The wind goes where its pleasure takes it, and the sound of it comes to your ears, but you are unable to say where it comes from and where it goes: so it is with everyone whose birth is from the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 6:60; Luke 1:34; Isaiah 42:16"> And Nicodemus said to him, How is it possible for these things to be?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 22:29; Jeremiah 8:8–9; Ezekiel 11:19"> And Jesus, answering, said, Are you the teacher of Israel and have no knowledge of these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 8:38; 1 John 5:6–12; John 12:49"> Truly, I say to you, We say that of which we have knowledge; we give witness of what we have seen; and you do not take our witness to be true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:1–2; 1 Corinthians 2:7–9; Hebrews 5:11"> If you have no belief when my words are about the things of earth, how will you have belief if my words are about the things of heaven?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 30:4; John 6:38; Acts 2:34"> And no one has ever gone up to heaven but he who came down from heaven, the Son of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 21:7–9; John 8:28; John 12:32–34"> As the snake was lifted up by Moses in the waste land, even so it is necessary for the Son of man to be lifted up:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 3:16; John 6:40; John 12:44–46"> So that whoever has faith may have in him eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 5:8; 1 John 4:9–10; John 3:15"> For God had such love for the world that he gave his only Son, so that whoever has faith in him may not come to destruction but have eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 John 4:14; Luke 19:10; John 12:47–48"> God did not send his Son into the world to be judge of the world; he sent him so that the world might have salvation through him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 16:16; 1 John 5:10; Romans 8:1"> The man who has faith in him does not come up to be judged; but he who has no faith in him has been judged even now, because he has no faith in the name of the only Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 8:12; 2 Thessalonians 2:12; John 9:39–41"> And this is the test by which men are judged: the light has come into the world and men have more love for the dark than for the light, because their acts are evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11–13; John 7:7; Job 24:13–17"> The light is hated by everyone whose acts are evil and he does not come to the light for fear that his acts will be seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 1:6; Psalms 139:23–24; 3 John 1:11"> But he whose life is true comes to the light, so that it may be clearly seen that his acts have been done by the help of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 4:1–3; John 3:26; John 2:13"> After these things Jesus and his disciples went into the land of Judaea, and there he was with them for some time, giving baptism.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 1:4–5; Ezekiel 43:2; Matthew 3:5–6"> Now John was then giving baptism at Aenon near Salim, because there was much water there; and people came and were given baptism.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 4:12; Matthew 14:3; Mark 6:17"> For at this time John had not been put into prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 2:6; Matthew 3:11; Hebrews 6:2"> Then a question came up between John's disciples and a Jew about washing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 1:7; John 1:15; John 12:19"> And they went to John and said to him, Rabbi, the man who was with you on the other side of the Jordan, the man to whom you gave witness, is now giving baptism, and everyone is going to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 1:17; 1 Corinthians 4:7; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> And this was John's answer: A man is unable to have anything if it is not given to him from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 1:20; John 1:23; Matthew 3:11–12"> You yourselves give witness that I said, I am not the Christ. What I said was, I am sent before the Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 9:15; Hosea 2:19; Isaiah 54:5"> He who has the bride is the husband: but the husband's friend, whose place is by his side and whose ears are open to him, is full of joy because of the husband's voice: such is my joy, and it is complete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 1:18; Psalms 72:17–19; Hebrews 3:2–6"> He has to become greater while I become less.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 8:23; Matthew 28:18; Romans 9:5"> He who comes from heaven is greater than all others: he who comes from earth is of the earth, and of the earth are his words: he who comes from heaven is over all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 3:11; John 15:15; John 1:11"> He gives witness of what he has seen and of what has come to his ears; and no man takes his witness as true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 6:17; Ephesians 1:13; Titus 1:1–2"> He who so takes his witness has made clear his faith that God is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 10:38; Matthew 12:18; John 15:26"> For he whom God has sent says God's words; and God does not give him the Spirit by measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 28:18; John 5:22; John 5:20"> The Father has love for the Son and has put all things into his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 5:24; John 1:12; 1 Thessalonians 5:9"> He who has faith in the Son has eternal life; but he who has not faith in the Son will not see life; God's wrath is resting on him.</span>
