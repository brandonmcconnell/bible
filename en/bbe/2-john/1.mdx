<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:4; 1 John 3:18; John 8:32"> I, a ruler in the church, send word to the noble sister who is of God's selection, and to her children, for whom I have true love; and not only I, but all who have knowledge of what is true;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 2:14; 1 John 1:8; 1 John 2:17"> Because of this true knowledge which is in us, and will be with us for ever:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 4:10; Romans 1:7; 1 Timothy 1:14"> May grace, mercy, and peace be with us from God the Father, and from Jesus Christ, the Son of the Father, in all true love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="3 John 1:3–4; Ephesians 5:8; Ephesians 5:2"> It gave me great joy to see some of your children walking in the true way, even as we were ordered to do by the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 John 3:11; John 13:34–35; John 15:12"> And now, my sister, I make a request to you, not sending you a new law, but the law which we had from the first, that we have love for one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 John 5:3; 1 John 2:24; John 15:10"> And love is the keeping of his laws. This is the law which was given to you from the first, so that you might keep it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 2:26; 1 John 4:1–3; 1 John 2:18–22"> Because a number of false teachers have gone out into the world, who do not give witness that Jesus Christ came in the flesh. Such a one is a false teacher and Antichrist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:35; Revelation 3:11; 1 Corinthians 3:14"> Keep watch over yourselves, so that you do not make our work of no effect, but may get your full reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 7:16–17; Colossians 3:16; 1 John 2:22–24"> Anyone who goes on and does not keep to the teaching of Christ, has not God: he who keeps to the teaching has the Father and the Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Titus 3:10; Romans 16:17–18; 2 Thessalonians 3:6"> If anyone comes to you not having this teaching, do not take him into your house or give him words of love:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:22; Ephesians 5:11; Revelation 18:4"> For he who gives him words of love has a part in his evil works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="3 John 1:13–14; John 16:12; 1 Corinthians 16:5–7"> Having much to say to you, it is not my purpose to put it all down with paper and ink: but I am hoping to come to you, and to have talk with you face to face, so that your joy may be full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 5:13; 2 John 1:1–13"> The children of your noble sister, who is of God's selection, send you their love.</span>
