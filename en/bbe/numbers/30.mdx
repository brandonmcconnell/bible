<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 7:2; Deuteronomy 1:13–17; Exodus 18:25"> And Moses said to the heads of the tribes of the children of Israel, This is the order of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 50:14; Psalms 116:14; Ecclesiastes 5:4–5"> When a man takes an oath to the Lord, or gives an undertaking having the force of an oath, let him not go back from his word, but let him do whatever he has said he will do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> If a woman, being young and under the authority of her father, takes an oath to the Lord or gives an undertaking;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> If her father, hearing of her oath or the undertaking she has given, says nothing to her, then all her oaths and every undertaking she has given will have force.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 7:10–13; Matthew 15:4–6; Ephesians 6:1"> But if her father, hearing of it, makes her take back her word, then the oaths or the undertakings she has given will have no force; and she will have forgiveness from the Lord, because her oath was broken by her father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 56:12"> And if she is married to a husband at the time when she is under an oath or an undertaking given without thought;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> If her husband, hearing of it, says nothing to her at the time, then the oaths she made and the undertakings she gave will have force.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 3:16; Ephesians 5:22–24; 1 Corinthians 14:34"> But if her husband, hearing of it, makes her take it back, then the oath she made and the undertaking she gave without thought will have no force or effect, and she will have the Lord's forgiveness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 2:37; Romans 7:2; Leviticus 21:7"> But an oath made by a widow or one who is no longer married to her husband, and every undertaking she has given, will have force.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> If she made an oath while she was under the authority of her husband,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And her husband, hearing of it, said nothing to her and did not put a stop to it, then all her oaths and every undertaking she gave will have force.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 30:8; 1 Corinthians 11:3; Numbers 15:25"> But if her husband, on hearing of it, made them without force or effect, then whatever she has said about her oaths or her undertaking has no force: her husband has made them without effect, and she will have the Lord's forgiveness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:3; 1 Peter 3:1–6; Isaiah 58:5"> Every oath, and every undertaking which she gives, to keep herself from pleasure, may be supported or broken by her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> But if the days go on, and her husband says nothing whatever to her, then he is giving the support of his authority to her oaths and undertakings, because at the time of hearing them he said nothing to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 5:1; Numbers 30:5; Numbers 30:8"> But if at some time after hearing of them, he makes them without force, then he is responsible for her wrongdoing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 14:54–57; Leviticus 11:46–47; Numbers 5:29–30"> These are the laws which the Lord gave Moses in relation to a man and his wife, or a father and a young daughter who is under his authority.</span>
