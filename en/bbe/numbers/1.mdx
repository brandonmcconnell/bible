<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 26:26–27; Genesis 49:13; Genesis 30:20"> The generations of the sons of Zebulun were numbered by their families and their fathers' houses, every male of twenty years old and over who was able to go to war;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 26:27; Numbers 2:8"> Fifty-seven thousand, four hundred of the tribe of Zebulun were numbered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 26:35–37; Deuteronomy 33:17; Genesis 46:20"> The generations of the sons of Joseph were numbered by their families and their fathers' houses, every male of twenty years old and over who was able to go to war;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 26:37; Deuteronomy 33:17; Numbers 2:19"> Forty thousand, five hundred of the tribe of Ephraim were numbered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 26:28–34"> The generations of the sons of Manasseh were numbered by their families and their fathers' houses, every male of twenty years old and over who was able to go to war;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 26:34; Genesis 48:19–20; Numbers 2:21"> Thirty-two thousand, two hundred of the tribe of Manasseh were numbered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 49:27; Genesis 44:20; Revelation 7:8"> The generations of the sons of Benjamin were numbered by their families and their fathers' houses, every male of twenty years old and over who was able to go to war;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 26:41; Judges 20:44–46; 2 Chronicles 17:17"> Thirty-five thousand, four hundred of the tribe of Benjamin were numbered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 46:23; Genesis 30:5–6; Genesis 49:16–17"> The generations of the sons of Dan were numbered by their families and their fathers' houses, every male of twenty years and over who was able to go to war;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Numbers 26:43; Numbers 2:26"> Sixty-two thousand, seven hundred of the tribe of Dan were numbered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Genesis 30:12–13; Genesis 46:27; Genesis 49:20"> The generations of the sons of Asher were numbered by their families and their fathers' houses, every male of twenty years old and over who was able to go to war;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 26:47; Numbers 2:28"> Forty-one thousand, five hundred of the tribe of Asher were numbered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Genesis 49:21; Numbers 26:48–50; Genesis 46:24"> The generations of the sons of Naphtali were numbered by their families and their fathers' houses, every male of twenty years old and over who was able to go to war;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 26:50; Numbers 2:30"> Fifty-three thousand, four hundred of the tribe of Naphtali were numbered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Numbers 26:64; Numbers 1:2–16"> These are they who were numbered by Moses and Aaron and by the twelve chiefs of Israel, one from every tribe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> So all those who were numbered of the children of Israel, by their families, all those of twenty years old and over who were able to go to war,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Exodus 12:37; Numbers 26:51; Exodus 38:26"> Were six hundred and three thousand, five hundred and fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Numbers 2:33; Numbers 3:1–49; Numbers 26:57–62"> But the Levites, of the tribe of their fathers, were not numbered among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> For the Lord said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Numbers 26:62; Numbers 2:33"> Only the tribe of Levi is not to be numbered among the children of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Exodus 38:21; Exodus 31:18; Numbers 4:15"> But to them you are to give the care of the Tent of meeting with its vessels and everything in it: they are to take up the Tent, and be responsible for everything to do with it, and put up their tents round it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 3:38; Numbers 3:10; 1 Samuel 6:19"> And when the Tent of meeting goes forward, the Levites are to take it down; and when it is to be put up, they are to do it: any strange person who comes near it is to be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Numbers 2:2; Numbers 2:34; Numbers 10:1–36"> The children of Israel are to put up their tents, every man in his tent-circle round his flag.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Numbers 16:46; 1 Chronicles 23:32; Leviticus 10:6"> But the tents of the Levites are to be round the Tent of meeting, so that wrath may not come on the children of Israel: the Tent of meeting is to be in the care of the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; Exodus 40:16; Exodus 39:32"> So the children of Israel did as the Lord had given orders to Moses.</span>
