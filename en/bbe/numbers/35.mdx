<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 22:1; Numbers 31:12; Numbers 33:50"> And the Lord said to Moses in the lowlands of Moab by Jordan at Jericho,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 14:3–4; Ezekiel 48:8; Joshua 21:2–42"> Give orders to the children of Israel to give to the Levites, from the heritage which is theirs, towns for themselves, with land on the outskirts of the towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:14; Joshua 21:11; Ezekiel 45:2"> These towns are to be their living-places, with land round them for their cattle and their food and all their beasts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Stretching from the wall of the towns a distance of a thousand cubits all round.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The measure of this space of land is to be two thousand cubits outside the town on the east, and two thousand cubits on the south and on the west and on the north, the town being in the middle. This space will be the outskirts of their towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 21:13; Joshua 21:3; Psalms 9:9"> And the towns which you give the Levites are to be the six safe places to which the taker of life may go in flight; and in addition you are to give them forty-two towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 21:3–42; 1 Chronicles 6:54–81"> Forty-eight towns are to be given to the Levites, all with land round them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 26:54; Numbers 33:54; Joshua 21:1–42"> And these towns are to be given out of the heritage of the children of Israel, taking the greater number from those who have much, and a smaller number from those who have little: everyone, in the measure of his heritage, is to give of his property to the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And the Lord said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:9; Deuteronomy 19:1–2; Leviticus 14:34"> Say to the children of Israel, when you have gone over Jordan into the land of Canaan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 21:13; Joshua 20:2; Numbers 35:6"> Then let certain towns be marked out as safe places to which anyone who takes the life of another in error may go in flight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 20:3–6; Deuteronomy 19:6; Joshua 20:9"> In these towns you may be safe from him who has the right of punishment; so that death may not overtake the taker of life till he has been judged by the meeting of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Six of the towns which you give will be such safe places;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:8–10; Deuteronomy 4:41–43; Joshua 20:7–9"> Three on the other side of Jordan and three in the land of Canaan, to be safe places for flight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 15:16; Galatians 3:28; Romans 3:29"> For the children of Israel and for the man from another country who is living among them, these six towns are to be safe places, where anyone causing the death of another through error may go in flight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 24:17; Genesis 9:5–6; Deuteronomy 19:11–13"> But if a man gives another man a blow with an iron instrument, causing his death, he is a taker of life and is certainly to be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 21:18"> Or if he gives him a blow with a stone in his hand, causing his death, he is a taker of life and is certainly to be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Or if he gave him blows with a wood instrument in his hands, causing his death, he is a taker of life and is certainly to be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 20:3; Deuteronomy 19:6; Deuteronomy 19:12"> He whose right it is to give punishment for blood, may himself put to death the taker of life when he comes face to face with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 21:14; Deuteronomy 19:11; 2 Samuel 3:27"> If in his hate he put a sword through him, or waiting secretly for him sent a spear or stone at him, causing his death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Or in hate gave him blows with his hand, causing death; he who gave the death-blow is to be put to death; he is a taker of life: he whose right it is to give punishment for blood may put to death the taker of life when he comes face to face with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 35:11; Exodus 21:13; Joshua 20:3"> But if a man has given a wound to another suddenly and not in hate, or without design has sent something against him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Or has given him a blow with a stone, without seeing him, so causing his death, though he had nothing against him and no desire to do him evil:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 20:6; Numbers 35:12"> Then let the meeting of the people be judge between the man responsible for the death and him who has the right of punishment for blood, acting by these rules:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 29:7; Leviticus 4:3; Romans 3:24–26"> And let the people keep the man responsible for the death safe from the hands of him who has the right of punishment for blood, and send him back to his safe town where he had gone in flight: there let him be till the death of the high priest who was marked with the holy oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> But if ever he goes outside the walls of the safe town where he had gone in flight,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 22:2; Deuteronomy 19:10; Deuteronomy 19:6"> And the giver of punishment, meeting him outside the walls of the town, puts him to death, he will not be responsible for his blood:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 3:14; Hebrews 10:26–30; Hebrews 6:4–8"> Because he had been ordered to keep inside the safe town till the death of the high priest: but after the death of the high priest the taker of life may come back to the place of his heritage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 27:11; Numbers 27:1"> These rules are to be your guide in judging through all your generations wherever you may be living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 10:28; Deuteronomy 19:15; Matthew 18:16"> Anyone causing the death of another is himself to be put to death on the word of witnesses: but the word of one witness is not enough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:11–13; Genesis 9:5–6; Psalms 51:14"> Further, no price may be given for the life of one who has taken life and whose right reward is death: he is certainly to be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 4:12; Galatians 3:10–13; Galatians 2:21"> And no price may be offered for one who has gone in flight to a safe town, for the purpose of letting him come back to his place before the death of the high priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 11:50–51; Psalms 106:38; Hosea 4:2–3"> So do not make the land where you are living unholy: for blood makes the land unholy: and there is no way of making the land free from the blood which has come on it, but only by the death of him who was the cause of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 5:3; Psalms 135:21; Revelation 21:27"> Do not make unclean the land where you are living and in which is my House: for I the Lord am present among the children of Israel.</span>
