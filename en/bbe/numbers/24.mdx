<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 23:3; Numbers 23:23; Numbers 23:28"> Now when Balaam saw that it was the Lord's pleasure to give his blessing to Israel, he did not, as at other times, make use of secret arts, but turning his face to the waste land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:20; 2 Chronicles 15:1; 1 Samuel 19:23"> And lifting up his eyes, he saw Israel there, with their tents in the order of their tribes: and the spirit of God came on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 22:31; Numbers 24:16; Numbers 23:7"> And moved by the spirit, he said, These are the words of Balaam, son of Beor, the words of the man whose eyes are open:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Revelation 1:10; Numbers 12:6"> He says, whose ears are open to the words of God, who has seen the vision of the Ruler of all, falling down, but having his eyes open:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> How fair are your tents, O Jacob, your houses, O Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 1:3; Isaiah 58:11; Psalms 104:16"> They are stretched out like valleys, like gardens by the riverside, like flowering trees planted by the Lord, like cedar-trees by the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:12; 1 Chronicles 14:2; Revelation 17:1"> Peoples will be in fear before his strength, his arm will be on great nations: his king will be higher than Agag, and his kingdom made great in honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 45:5; Psalms 2:9; Numbers 14:9"> It is God who has taken him out of Egypt; his horns are like those of the mountain ox; the nations warring against him will be his food, their bones will be broken, they will be wounded with his arrows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Genesis 27:29; Genesis 49:9"> He took his sleep stretched out like a lion, and like a she-lion: by whom will his rest be broken? May a blessing be on everyone who gives you blessing, and a curse on everyone by whom you are cursed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 23:11; Ezekiel 21:14; Ezekiel 21:17"> Then Balak was full of wrath against Balaam, and angrily waving his hands he said to Balaam, I sent for you so that those who are against me might be cursed, but now, see, three times you have given them a blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 22:17; Numbers 22:37; Hebrews 11:24–26"> Go back quickly to the place you came from: it was my purpose to give you a place of honour, but now the Lord has kept you back from honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 22:18; Numbers 22:38"> Then Balaam said to Balak, Did I not say to the men you sent to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 16:28; Numbers 22:18; Numbers 22:20"> Even if Balak gave me his house full of silver and gold, it would not be possible for me to go outside the orders of the Lord, doing good or evil at the impulse of my mind; whatever the Lord says I will say?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Micah 6:5; Revelation 2:14; Genesis 49:1"> So now I will go back to my people: but first let me make clear to you what this people will do to your people in days to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 24:3–4; Numbers 23:7; Matthew 13:35"> Then he went on with his story and said, These are the words of Balaam, the son of Beor, the words of him whose eyes are open:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 24:4; 1 Corinthians 8:1; 2 Samuel 23:1–2"> He says, whose ear is open to the words of God, who has knowledge of the Most High, who has seen the vision of the Ruler of all, falling down and having his eyes open:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 22:16; Luke 1:32–33; Genesis 49:10"> I see him, but not now: looking on him, but not near: a star will come out of Jacob, and a rod of authority out of Israel, sending destruction to the farthest limits of Moab and on the head of all the sons of Sheth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Amos 9:12; Genesis 27:29; 2 Samuel 8:14"> Edom will be his heritage, and he will put an end to the last of the people of Seir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 49:10; Micah 5:2; Psalms 2:1–12"> And Israel will go on in strength, and Jacob will have rule over his haters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 17:14; Judges 6:3; 1 Chronicles 4:43"> Then, turning his eyes to Amalek, he went on with his story and said, Amalek was the first of the nations, but his part will be destruction for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 15:19; Judges 1:16; Job 29:18"> And looking on the Kenites he went on with his story and said, Strong is your living-place, and your secret place is safe in the rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 83:8; Genesis 10:22; Ezra 4:2"> But still the Kenites will be wasted, till Asshur takes you away prisoner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 23:23; Malachi 3:2; 2 Kings 5:1"> Then he went on with his story and said, But who may keep his life when God does this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 10:4; Numbers 24:20; Daniel 10:20"> But ships will come from the direction of Kittim, troubling Asshur and troubling Eber, and like the others their fate will be destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 31:8; Joshua 13:22; Numbers 24:11"> Then Balaam got up and went back to his place: and Balak went away.</span>
