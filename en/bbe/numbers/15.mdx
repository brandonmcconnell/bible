<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 23:10; Numbers 15:18; Leviticus 25:2"> Say to the children of Israel, When you have come into the land which I am giving to you for your resting-place,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 29:18; Genesis 8:21; Philippians 4:18"> And are going to make an offering by fire to the Lord, a burned offering or an offering in connection with an oath, or an offering freely given, or at your regular feasts, an offering for a sweet smell to the Lord, from the herd or the flock:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 29:40; Leviticus 23:13; Leviticus 6:14"> Then let him who is making his offering, give to the Lord a meal offering of a tenth part of a measure of the best meal mixed with a fourth part of a hin of oil:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 28:7; Numbers 28:14; Judges 9:13"> And for the drink offering, you are to give with the burned offering or other offering, the fourth part of a hin of wine for every lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 28:12–14; Numbers 15:4"> Or for a male sheep, give as a meal offering two tenth parts of a measure of the best meal mixed with a third part of a hin of oil:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And for the drink offering give a third part of a hin of wine, for a sweet smell to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 3:1; Leviticus 7:11–18; Leviticus 1:3"> And when you make ready a young ox for a burned or other offering, or for the effecting of an oath, or for peace-offerings to the Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 28:14; Numbers 28:12; Leviticus 6:14"> Then with the ox give a meal offering of three tenth parts of a measure of the best meal mixed with half a hin of oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 15:5; Numbers 6:15"> And for the drink offering: give half a hin of wine, for an offering made by fire for a sweet smell to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> This is to be done for every young ox and for every male sheep or he-lamb or young goat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Whatever number you make ready, so you are to do for every one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> All those who are Israelites by birth are to do these things in this way, when giving an offering made by fire of a sweet smell to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And if a man from another country or any other person living among you, through all your generations, has the desire to give an offering made by fire of a sweet smell to the Lord, let him do as you do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 9:14; Numbers 15:29; Exodus 12:49"> There is to be one law for you and for the man of another country living with you, one law for ever from generation to generation; as you are, so is he to be before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The law and the rule are to be the same for you and for those from other lands living with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the Lord said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 15:2; Deuteronomy 26:1–15"> Say to the children of Israel, When you come into the land where I am guiding you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 5:11–12"> Then, when you take for your food the produce of the land, you are to give an offering lifted up before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 2:14; Exodus 34:26; Deuteronomy 26:2–10"> Of the first of your rough meal you are to give a cake for a lifted offering, lifting it up before the Lord as the offering of the grain-floor is lifted up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 29:28; Numbers 18:26"> From generation to generation you are to give to the Lord a lifted offering from the first of your rough meal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 4:2; Leviticus 4:27; Leviticus 5:15–17"> And if in error you go against any of these laws which the Lord has given to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> All the laws which the Lord has given you by the hand of Moses, from the day when the Lord gave them, and ever after from generation to generation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 4:23; Leviticus 4:13–21; Leviticus 5:15"> Then, if the wrong is done in error, without the knowledge of the meeting of the people, let all the meeting give a young ox as a burned offering, a sweet smell to the Lord, with its meal offering and its drink offering, as is ordered in the law, together with a he-goat for a sin-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 4:20; Romans 3:25; Hebrews 2:17"> So the priest will make the people free from sin, and they will have forgiveness; for it was an error, and they have given their offering made by fire to the Lord, and their sin-offering before the Lord, on account of their error:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 15:24"> And all the meeting of the children of Israel, as well as those from other lands living among them, will have forgiveness; for it was an error on the part of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 4:27–28; Acts 17:30; Acts 3:17"> And if one person does wrong, without being conscious of it, then let him give a she-goat of the first year for a sin-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 4:35"> And the priest will take away the sin of the person who has done wrong, if the wrong was done unconsciously, and he will have forgiveness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 15:15; Leviticus 16:29; Leviticus 17:15"> The law in connection with wrong done unconsciously is to be the same for him who is an Israelite by birth and for the man from another country who is living among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26; Psalms 19:13; Deuteronomy 1:43"> But the person who does wrong in the pride of his heart, if he is one of you or of another nation by birth, is acting without respect for the Lord, and will be cut off from his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:9; Proverbs 13:13; Ezekiel 18:20"> Because he had no respect for the word of the Lord, and did not keep his law, that man will be cut off without mercy and his sin will be on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 35:2–3; Exodus 31:14–15; Exodus 20:8–10"> Now while the children of Israel were in the waste land, they saw a man who was getting sticks on the Sabbath day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 8:3–20"> And those who saw him getting sticks took him before Moses and Aaron and all the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Leviticus 24:12; Numbers 9:8"> And they had him shut up, because they had no directions about what was to be done with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 7:58; Exodus 31:14–15; 1 Kings 21:13"> Then the Lord said to Moses, Certainly the man is to be put to death: let him be stoned by all the people outside the tent-circle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Joshua 7:25"> So all the people took him outside the tent-circle and he was stoned to death there, as the Lord gave orders to Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And the Lord said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 23:5; Deuteronomy 22:12; Matthew 9:20"> Say to the children of Israel that through all their generations they are to put on the edges of their robes an ornament of twisted threads, and in every ornament a blue cord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 106:39; Proverbs 28:26; Psalms 73:27"> So that, looking on these ornaments, you may keep in mind the orders of the Lord and do them; and not be guided by the desires of your hearts and eyes, through which you have been untrue to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Romans 12:1; 1 Thessalonians 4:7; Leviticus 11:44–45"> And that you may keep in mind all my orders and do them and be holy to your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9–10; Ezekiel 36:25–27; Hebrews 11:16"> I am the Lord your God, who took you out of the land of Egypt, so that I might be your God: I am the Lord your God.</span>
