<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 6:20; Genesis 10:1; Exodus 6:16"> Now these are the generations of Aaron and Moses, in the day when the word of the Lord came to Moses on Mount Sinai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 6:23; Numbers 26:60; Exodus 28:1"> These are the names of the sons of Aaron: Nadab the oldest, and Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 28:41; Leviticus 8:30; Leviticus 8:12"> These are the names of the sons of Aaron, the priests, on whom the holy oil was put, who were marked out as priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1–2; Numbers 26:61"> And Nadab and Abihu were put to death before the Lord when they made an offering of strange fire before the Lord, in the waste land of Sinai, and they had no children: and Eleazar and Ithamar did the work of priests before Aaron their father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the Lord said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:8; Numbers 2:33; Numbers 1:49–53"> Make the tribe of Levi come near, and put them before Aaron the priest, to be his helpers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 1:50; Numbers 8:15; Numbers 8:11"> In order that they may be responsible to him and to all Israel for the care of the Tent of meeting, and to do the work of the House;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 4:33; Numbers 10:17; Isaiah 52:11"> And they will have the care of all the vessels of the Tent of meeting, and will do for the children of Israel all the necessary work of the House.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 8:19; Numbers 18:6–7; Ephesians 4:8"> Give the Levites to Aaron and his sons; so that they may be his without question from among the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 1:51; Numbers 3:38; Romans 12:7"> And give orders that Aaron and his sons are to keep their place as priests; any strange person who comes near is to be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the Lord said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 3:41; Numbers 8:16; Numbers 18:6"> See, I have taken the Levites out of the children of Israel to be mine in place of the first sons of the children of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 2:23; Exodus 13:12; Exodus 13:2"> For all the first sons are mine; on the day when I put to death all the first sons in the land of Egypt, I took for myself every first male birth of man and beast. They are mine; I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And the Lord said to Moses in the waste land of Sinai,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 26:62; Mark 10:14; 2 Timothy 3:15"> Let all the children of Levi be numbered by their families and their fathers' houses; let every male of a month old and over be numbered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 3:39; Numbers 4:37; Numbers 4:49"> So Moses did as the Lord said, numbering them as he had been ordered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 46:11; Exodus 6:16–19; Numbers 15:5–23"> These were the sons of Levi by name: Gershon and Kohath and Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:17; 1 Chronicles 26:1–32; Exodus 6:17–19"> And these are the names of the sons of Gershon, by their families: Libni and Shimei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 6:18; 1 Chronicles 6:18; 1 Chronicles 15:17–21"> And the sons of Kohath, by their families: Amram and Izhar and Hebron and Uzziel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 6:19; 1 Chronicles 6:19; Numbers 3:33"> And the sons of Merari by their families: Mahli and Mushi. These are the families of the Levites in the order of their fathers' houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> From Gershon come the Libnites and the Shimeites; these are the families of the Gershonites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 4:38–40"> Those who were numbered of them, the males from one month old and over, were seven thousand, five hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 2:17"> The tents of the Gershonites are to be placed at the back of the House, to the west.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> The chief of the Gershonites is Eliasaph, the son of Lael.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 25:9; 1 Chronicles 23:32; 1 Chronicles 9:14–33"> In the Tent of meeting, the Gershonites are to have the care of the House, and the Tent with its cover, and the veil for the door of the Tent of meeting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 35:18; Exodus 38:9–16; Exodus 27:9–16"> And the hangings for the open space round the House and the altar, and the curtain for its doorway, and all the cords needed for its use.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:23; Numbers 3:19; 1 Chronicles 23:12"> From Kohath come the Amramites and the Izharites and the Hebronites and the Uzzielites; these are the families of the Kohathites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 3:31; Numbers 4:35–36; Numbers 3:7"> Those who were numbered of them, the males from one month old and over, were eight thousand, six hundred, who were responsible for the care of the holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 3:23; Numbers 2:10"> The tents of the Kohathites are to be placed on the south side of the House.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Their chief is Elizaphan, the son of Uzziel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 36:35–36; Exodus 27:1–8; Exodus 26:31–33"> In their care are the ark, and the table, and the lights, and the altars, and all the vessels used in the holy place, and the veil, and all they are used for.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 4:27; Numbers 4:16; Numbers 20:25–28"> Eleazar, the son of Aaron the priest, will be head over all the Levites and overseer of those responsible for the care of the holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 3:20; 1 Chronicles 6:19; 1 Chronicles 23:21"> From Merari come the Mahlites and the Mushites; these are the families of Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 2:9; Numbers 2:11; Numbers 1:21"> Those who were numbered of them, the males of a month old and over, were six thousand, two hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 2:25; Numbers 3:28–29"> The chief of the families of Merari was Zuriel, the son of Abihail: their tents are to be placed on the north side of the House.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 26:32; Exodus 26:37; Exodus 26:15–29"> And in their care are to be all the boards of the Tent, with their rods and pillars and bases, and all the instruments, and all they are used for,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And the pillars of the open space round it, with their bases and nails and cords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 3:10; Numbers 1:53; Numbers 2:3"> And those whose tents are to be placed on the east side of the House in front of the Tent of meeting, looking to the dawn, are Moses and Aaron and his sons, who will do the work of the holy place for the children of Israel; and any strange person who comes near will be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Numbers 26:62; Matthew 7:14; Numbers 4:47–48"> All the Levites numbered by Moses and Aaron at the order of the Lord, all the males of one month old and over numbered in the order of their families, were twenty-two thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 3:15; Luke 10:20; Psalms 87:6"> And the Lord said to Moses, Let all the first male children be numbered, and take the number of their names.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 3:12; Numbers 3:45; Numbers 8:16"> And give to me the Levites (I am the Lord) in place of the first sons of the children of Israel; and the cattle of the Levites in place of the first births among the cattle of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> So Moses had all the first sons among the children of Israel numbered, as the Lord said to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 3:39"> Every first son from a month old and over was numbered by name, and the number came to twenty-two thousand, two hundred and seventy-three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And the Lord said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Numbers 3:12; Numbers 3:40–41"> Take the Levites in place of all the first sons of the children of Israel, and the cattle of the Levites in place of their cattle; the Levites are to be mine; I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Exodus 13:13; Numbers 18:15–16"> And the price you have to give for the two hundred and seventy-three first sons of the children of Israel which are in addition to the number of the Levites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Leviticus 27:6; Numbers 18:16"> Will be five shekels for every one, by the scale of the holy place (the shekel is twenty gerahs);</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> And this money, the price of those over the number of the Levites, is to be given to Aaron and his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> So Moses took the money, the price of those whose place had not been taken by the Levites;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Numbers 3:46–47; 1 Peter 3:18; Matthew 20:28"> From the first sons of Israel he took it, a thousand, three hundred and sixty-five shekels, by the scale of the holy place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 3:48; Numbers 16:15; 1 Samuel 12:3–4"> And he gave the money to Aaron and his sons, as the Lord had said.</span>
