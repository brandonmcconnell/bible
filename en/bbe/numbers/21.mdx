<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 1:16; Numbers 33:40; Joshua 12:14"> And it came to the ears of the Canaanite, the king of Arad, living in the South, that Israel was coming by the way of Atharim, and he came out against them and took some of them prisoners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 11:30; Genesis 28:20; 1 Samuel 1:11"> Then Israel made an oath to the Lord, and said, If you will give up this people into my hands, then I will send complete destruction on all their towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 14:45; Deuteronomy 1:44; Psalms 10:17"> And the Lord, in answer to the voice of Israel, gave the Canaanites up to them; and they put them and their towns completely to destruction: and that place was named Hormah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 6:9; Judges 11:18; Numbers 32:7"> Then they went on from Mount Hor by the way to the Red Sea, going round the land of Edom: and the spirit of the people was overcome with weariness on the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 78:19; Numbers 16:13–14; Exodus 17:2–3"> And crying out against God and against Moses, they said, Why have you taken us out of Egypt to come to our death in the waste land? For there is no bread and no water, and this poor bread is disgusting to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:9; Jeremiah 8:17; Deuteronomy 8:15"> Then the Lord sent poison-snakes among the people; and their bites were a cause of death to numbers of the people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 8:24; Numbers 11:2; Psalms 78:34"> Then the people came to Moses and said, We have done wrong in crying out against the Lord and against you: make prayer to the Lord to take away the snakes from us. So Moses made prayer for the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 3:14; Psalms 106:43–45; Psalms 145:8"> And the Lord said to Moses, Make an image of a snake and put it on a rod, and anyone who has been wounded by the snakes, looking on it will be made well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 3:14–15; Hebrews 12:2; John 6:40"> So Moses made a snake of brass and put it on a rod; and anyone who had a snakebite, after looking on the snake of brass, was made well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 33:43–45"> Then the children of Israel went on and put up their tents in Oboth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And journeying on again from Oboth, they put up their tents in Iye-abarim, in the waste land before Moab looking east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:13–14"> And moving on from there, they put up their tents in the valley of Zered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 11:18; Numbers 22:36; Isaiah 16:2"> From there they went on and put up their tents on the other side of the Arnon, which is on the waste land at the edge of the land of the Amorites; for the Arnon is the line of division between Moab and the Amorites:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 10:13; 2 Samuel 1:18"> As it says in the book of the Wars of the Lord, Vaheb in Suphah, and the valley of the Amon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 21:28; Deuteronomy 2:18; Deuteronomy 2:9"> The slope of the valleys going down to the tents of Ar and touching the edge of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 20:8; Isaiah 12:3; Isaiah 41:17–18"> From there they went on to Beer, the water-spring of which the Lord said to Moses, Make the people come together and I will give them water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 5:13; Exodus 15:1–2; Psalms 106:12"> Then Israel gave voice to this song: Come up, O water-spring, let us make a song to it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 33:22; 1 Timothy 6:17–18; James 4:12"> The fountain made by the chiefs, made deep by the great ones of the people, with the law-givers' rod, and with their sticks. Then from the waste land they went on to Mattanah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And from Mattanah to Nahaliel: and from Nahaliel to Bamoth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 23:28; Numbers 23:14; Deuteronomy 1:5"> And from Bamoth to the valley in the open country of Moab, and to the top of Pisgah looking over Jeshimon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:26–28; Numbers 20:14–19; Judges 11:19–21"> And Israel sent men to Sihon, king of the Amorites, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 20:17"> Let me go through your land: we will not go into field or vine-garden, or take the water of the springs; we will go by the highway till we have gone past the limits of your land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 11:20; Deuteronomy 29:7–8; Isaiah 15:4"> And Sihon would not let Israel go through his land; but got all his people together and went out against Israel into the waste land, as far as Jahaz, to make war on Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Amos 2:9; Joshua 24:8; Genesis 32:22"> But Israel overcame him, and took all his land from the Arnon to the Jabbok, as far as the country of the children of Ammon, for the country of the children of Ammon was strongly armed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 32:33–42; Isaiah 16:8–9; Jeremiah 48:2"> And Israel took all their towns, living in Heshbon and all the towns and small places of the Amorites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> For Heshbon was the town of Sihon, king of the Amorites, who had made war against an earlier king of Moab and taken from him all his land as far as the Arnon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 14:4; Numbers 21:14; Habakkuk 2:6"> So the makers of wise sayings say, Come to Heshbon, building up the town of Sihon and making it strong:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 21:15; Jeremiah 48:45–46; Isaiah 15:1–2"> For a fire has gone out of Heshbon, a flame from the town of Sihon: for the destruction of Ar in Moab, and the lords of the high places of the Arnon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 11:24; 2 Kings 23:13; Jeremiah 48:46"> Sorrow is yours, O Moab! Destruction is your fate, O people of Chemosh: his sons have gone in flight, and his daughters are prisoners, in the hands of Sihon, king of the Amorites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:18; Isaiah 15:2; Jeremiah 48:22"> They are wounded with our arrows; destruction has come on Heshbon, even to Dibon; and we have made the land waste as far as Nophah, stretching out to Medeba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 13:8–12; Numbers 32:33–42; Deuteronomy 3:16–17"> So Israel put up their tents in the land of the Amorites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:32; Numbers 32:1; Numbers 32:35"> And Moses sent men secretly to Jazer, and they took its towns, driving out the Amorites who were living there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 13:12; Deuteronomy 1:4; Deuteronomy 29:7"> Then turning they went up by the way of Bashan; and Og, king of Bashan, went out against them with all his people, to the fight at Edrei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 135:10–11; Joshua 8:7; Deuteronomy 3:2–3"> And the Lord said to Moses, Have no fear of him: for I have given him up into your hands, with all his people and his land; do to him as you did to Sihon, king of the Amorites, at Heshbon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:7–8; Joshua 13:12; Deuteronomy 3:3–17"> So they overcame him and his sons and his people, driving them all out: and they took his land for their heritage.</span>
