<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord said to Moses and Aaron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 22:20–25; Deuteronomy 21:3; 1 Samuel 6:7"> This is the rule of the law which the Lord has made, saying, Give orders to the children of Israel to give you a red cow without any mark on her, and on which the yoke has never been put:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 4:12; Leviticus 4:21; Leviticus 16:27"> Give her to Eleazar the priest and let him take her outside the tent-circle and have her put to death before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 4:17; Leviticus 4:6; Leviticus 16:14"> Then let Eleazar the priest take some of her blood on his finger, shaking the blood seven times in the direction of the front of the Tent of meeting:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 29:14; Leviticus 4:11–12; Psalms 22:14"> And the cow is to be burned before him, her skin and her flesh and her blood and her waste are to be burned:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 14:4; Leviticus 14:49; Psalms 51:7"> Then let the priest take cedar-wood and hyssop and red thread, and put them into the fire where the cow is burning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 11:25; Leviticus 11:40; Numbers 19:8"> And the priest, after washing his clothing and bathing his body in water, may come back to the tent-circle, and will be unclean till evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And he who does the burning is to have his clothing washed and his body bathed in water and be unclean till evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 9:13; Numbers 19:13; Numbers 19:20–21"> Then let a man who is clean take the dust of the burned cow and put it outside the tent-circle in a clean place, where it is to be kept for the children of Israel and used in making the water which takes away what is unclean: it is a sin-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 12:49; Numbers 19:7–8; Colossians 3:11"> And he who takes up the dust of the burned cow is to have his clothing washed with water and be unclean till evening: this is to be a law for ever, for the children of Israel as well as for the man from another country who is living among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 5:2; Numbers 31:19; Leviticus 21:1"> Anyone touching a dead body will be unclean for seven days:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 31:19; Revelation 7:14; Leviticus 7:17"> On the third day and on the seventh day he is to make himself clean with the water, and so he will be clean: but if he does not do this on the third day and on the seventh day, he will not be clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 15:31; Leviticus 22:3; Leviticus 7:20"> Anyone touching the body of a dead man without making himself clean in this way, makes the House of the Lord unclean; and that man will be cut off from Israel: because the water was not put on him, he will be unclean; his unclean condition is unchanged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> This is the law when death comes to a man in his tent: everyone who comes into the tent, and everyone who is in the tent, will be unclean for seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 14:36; Leviticus 11:32; Numbers 31:20"> And every open vessel without a cover fixed on it will be unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 31:19; Matthew 23:27; Luke 11:44"> And anyone touching one who has been put to death with the sword in the open country, or the body of one who has come to his end by a natural death, or a man's bone, or the resting-place of a dead body, will be unclean for seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 7:38; Genesis 26:19; Revelation 7:17"> And for the unclean, they are to take the dust of the burning of the sin-offering, and put flowing water on it in a vessel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:25–27; Hebrews 9:14; Numbers 19:9"> And a clean person is to take hyssop and put it in the water, shaking it over the tent, and all the vessels, and the people who were there, and over him by whom the bone, or the body of one who has been put to death with the sword, or the body of one who has come to his end by a natural death, or the resting-place was touched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 1:7; Hebrews 10:22; Ezekiel 36:25"> Let the clean person do this to the unclean on the third day and on the seventh day: and on the seventh day he is to make him clean; and after washing his clothing and bathing himself in water, he will be clean in the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 16:16; Genesis 17:14; Numbers 19:13"> But the man who, being unclean, does not make himself clean in this way, will be cut off from the meeting of the people, because he has made the holy place of the Lord unclean: the water has not been put on him, he is unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 9:13–14; Hebrews 10:4; Hebrews 9:10"> This is to be a law for them for ever: he who puts the water on the unclean person is to have his clothing washed; and anyone touching the water will be unclean till evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Haggai 2:13; Leviticus 7:19; Matthew 15:19–20"> Anything touched by the unclean person will be unclean; and any person touching it will be unclean till evening.</span>
