<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 13:21; Exodus 15:20; Numbers 33:36"> In the first month all the children of Israel came into the waste land of Zin, and put up their tents in Kadesh; there death came to Miriam, and they put her body to rest in the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 16:19; Numbers 16:42; Exodus 15:23–24"> And there was no water for the people: and they came together against Moses and against Aaron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 17:2; Numbers 14:1–2; Numbers 16:31–35"> And the people were angry with Moses and said, If only death had overtaken us when our brothers came to their death before the Lord!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 17:3; Psalms 106:21; Numbers 16:13–14"> Why have you taken the Lord's people into this waste, for death to come to us and to our cattle there?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 16:14; Jeremiah 2:2; Jeremiah 2:6"> Why have you made us come out of Egypt into this evil place? This is no place of seed or figs or vines or other fruits, and there is no water for drinking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:5; Numbers 16:4; Numbers 16:19"> Then Moses and Aaron went away from the people to the door of the Tent of meeting; and, falling on their faces there, they saw the glory of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And the Lord said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 4:17; Acts 2:1–4; Revelation 22:17"> Take the rod, you and Aaron, your brother, and make all the people come together, and before their eyes give orders to the rock to give out its water; and so make water come out of the rock for them, and give the people and their cattle drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 17:10"> And Moses took the rod from before the Lord as he gave him orders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 106:32–33; Acts 14:9–15; Luke 9:54–55"> Then Moses and Aaron made the people come together in front of the rock, and he said to them, Give ear now, you people whose hearts are turned from the Lord; are we to get water for you out of the rock?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:4; Exodus 17:6; Deuteronomy 8:15"> And lifting up his hand, Moses gave the rock two blows with his rod: and water came streaming out, and the people and their cattle had drink enough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:37; Numbers 27:14; Ezekiel 36:23"> Then the Lord said to Moses and Aaron, Because you had not enough faith in me to keep my name holy before the children of Israel, you will not take this people into the land which I have given them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 17:7; Psalms 95:8; Deuteronomy 33:8"> These are the waters of Meribah; because the children of Israel went against the Lord, and they saw that he was holy among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 11:16–17; Deuteronomy 23:7; Obadiah 1:10–12"> Then Moses sent men from Kadesh to the king of Edom to say to him, Your brother Israel says, You have knowledge of all the things we have been through;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 12:40; Deuteronomy 26:6; Genesis 46:6"> How our fathers went down into Egypt, and we were living in Egypt for a long time; and the Egyptians were cruel to us and to our fathers:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 14:19; Exodus 23:20; Exodus 33:2"> And the Lord gave ear to the voice of our cry, and sent an angel and took us out of Egypt: and now we are in Kadesh, a town on the edge of your land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:27; Deuteronomy 2:29; Numbers 21:22–24"> Let us now go through your land: we will not go into field or vine-garden, or take the water of the springs; we will go by the highway, not turning to the right or to the left, till we have gone past the limits of your land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And Edom said, You are not to go through my land, for if you do I will come out against you with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:28; Deuteronomy 2:6; Exodus 12:38"> And the children of Israel said to him, We will go up by the highway: and if we or our cattle take of your water, we will give you a price for it: only let us go through on our feet, nothing more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 11:17; Amos 1:11; Genesis 32:6"> But he said, You are not to go through. And Edom came out against them in his strength, with a great army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 11:18; Deuteronomy 2:29; Deuteronomy 2:4–8"> So Edom would not let Israel go through his land; and Israel went in another direction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 20:1; Numbers 20:14; Numbers 21:4"> And they went on from Kadesh, and came, with all their people, to Mount Hor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 33:37"> And at Mount Hor, at the edge of the land of Edom, the Lord said to Moses and Aaron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 25:8; Deuteronomy 32:50; Numbers 31:2"> Aaron will be put to rest with his people; he will not go into the land which I have given to the children of Israel, because you went against my word at the waters of Meribah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 33:38–39"> So take Aaron and Eleazar, his son, up into Mount Hor;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 20:24; Isaiah 22:21–22; Hebrews 7:11"> And take Aaron's robes off him and put them on Eleazar, his son: and death will come to Aaron there, and he will be put to rest with his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> So Moses did as the Lord had said, and before the eyes of all the people they went up Mount Hor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:6; Exodus 29:29–30; Deuteronomy 34:9"> And Moses took off Aaron's robes, and put them on Eleazar, his son; and there on the top of the mountain death came to Aaron: then Moses and Eleazar came down from the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:8; 2 Chronicles 35:24–25; Genesis 1:10"> And when the people saw that Aaron was dead, all the children of Israel gave themselves up to weeping for him for thirty days.</span>
