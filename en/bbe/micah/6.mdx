<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:29; Deuteronomy 32:1; Micah 1:2"> Give ear now to the words of the Lord: Up! put forward your cause before the mountains, let your voice be sounding among the hills.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 4:1; Hosea 12:2; Isaiah 1:18"> Give ear, O you mountains, to the Lord's cause, and take note, you bases of the earth: for the Lord has a cause against his people, and he will take it up with Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:5; Isaiah 43:22–23; Psalms 81:13"> O my people, what have I done to you? how have I been a weariness to you? give answer against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 12:51; Deuteronomy 7:8; Amos 2:10"> For I took you up out of the land of Egypt and made you free from the prison-house; I sent before you Moses, Aaron, and Miriam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 5:9–10; Numbers 25:1; Judges 5:11"> O my people, keep in mind now what was designed by Balak, king of Moab, and the answer which Balaam, son of Beor, gave him; the events, from Shittim to Gilgal, so that you may be certain of the upright acts of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 51:16–17; Romans 10:2–3; Psalms 40:6–8"> With what am I to come before the Lord and go with bent head before the high God? am I to come before him with burned offerings, with young oxen a year old?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 16:3; 1 Samuel 15:22; Psalms 51:16"> Will the Lord be pleased with thousands of sheep or with ten thousand rivers of oil? am I to give my first child for my wrongdoing, the fruit of my body for the sin of my soul?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:12–13; Proverbs 21:3; Colossians 3:12"> He has made clear to you, O man, what is good; and what is desired from you by the Lord; only doing what is right, and loving mercy, and walking without pride before your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Hosea 14:9; Isaiah 30:27"> The voice of the Lord is crying out to the town: Give ear, you tribes and the meeting of the town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 3:10; Amos 8:5–6; Jeremiah 5:26–27"> Am I to let the stores of the evil-doer go out of my memory, and the short measure, which is cursed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 12:7; Leviticus 19:36; Proverbs 16:11"> Is it possible for me to let wrong scales and the bag of false weights go without punishment?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 1:23; Jeremiah 9:8; Hosea 7:13"> For its men of wealth are cruel, and its people have said what is not true, and their tongue is false in their mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 12:23; Deuteronomy 28:21–22; Hosea 13:16"> So I have made a start with your punishment; I have made you waste because of your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hosea 4:10; Leviticus 26:26; Isaiah 65:13"> You will have food, but not enough; your shame will be ever with you: you will get your goods moved, but you will not take them away safely; and what you do take away I will give to the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:13; Amos 5:11; Jeremiah 12:13"> You will put in seed, but you will not get in the grain; you will be crushing olives, but your bodies will not be rubbed with the oil; and you will get in the grapes, but you will have no wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:51; 1 Kings 16:25–33; Jeremiah 7:24"> For you have kept the laws of Omri and all the works of the family of Ahab, and you have been guided by their designs: so that I might make you a cause of wonder and your people a cause of hisses; and the shame of my people will be on you.</span>
