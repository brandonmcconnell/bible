<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 36:4; Isaiah 32:7; Psalms 7:14–16"> A curse on the designers of evil, working on their beds! in the morning light they do it, because it is in their power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 5:8; Jeremiah 22:17; Amos 8:4"> They have a desire for fields and take them by force; and for houses and take them away: they are cruel to a man and his family, even to a man and his heritage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:3; Amos 5:13; Isaiah 2:11–12"> For this cause the Lord has said, See, against this family I am purposing an evil from which you will not be able to take your necks away, and you will be weighted down by it; for it is an evil time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:6; Jeremiah 9:10; Jeremiah 9:17–21"> In that day this saying will be said about you, and this song of grief will be made: The heritage of my people is measured out, and there is no one to give it back; those who have made us prisoners have taken our fields from us, and complete destruction has come to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:8; Joshua 18:4; Joshua 18:10"> For this cause you will have no one to make the decision by the measuring line in the meeting of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 2:12; Isaiah 30:10; Acts 5:40"> Let not words like these be dropped, they say: Shame and the curse will not come to the family of Jacob!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 84:11; Psalms 15:2; Jeremiah 15:16"> Is the Lord quickly made angry? are these his doings? do not his words do good to his people Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 120:6–7; 2 Samuel 20:19; Isaiah 9:21"> As for you, you have become haters of those who were at peace with you: you take the clothing of those who go by without fear, and make them prisoners of war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:14; Ezekiel 39:21; 2 Corinthians 4:6"> The women of my people you have been driving away from their dearly loved children; from their young ones you are taking my glory for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:9; Psalms 106:38; 2 Kings 17:6"> Up! and go; for this is not your rest: because it has been made unclean, the destruction ordered will come on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:31; Isaiah 30:10–11; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15"> If a man came with a false spirit of deceit, saying, I will be a prophet to you of wine and strong drink: he would be the sort of prophet for this people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 4:6–7; Micah 7:18; Hosea 1:11"> I will certainly make all of you, O Jacob, come together; I will get together the rest of Israel; I will put them together like the sheep in their circle: like a flock in their green field; they will be full of the noise of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 52:12; Zechariah 9:14–15; Isaiah 45:1–2"> The opener of the way will go up before them: forcing their way out they will go on to the doorway and out through it: their king will go on before them, and the Lord at their head.</span>
