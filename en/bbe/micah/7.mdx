<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 28:4; Isaiah 24:13; Hosea 9:10"> Sorrow is mine! for I am as when they have got in the summer fruits, like the last of the grapes: there is nothing for food, not even an early fig for my desire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 12:1; Isaiah 57:1; Isaiah 59:7"> The good man is gone from the earth, there is no one upright among men: they are all waiting secretly for blood, every man is going after his brother with a net.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Proverbs 4:16–17; Amos 5:12"> Their hands are made ready to do evil; the ruler makes requests for money, and the judge is looking for a reward; and the great man gives decisions at his pleasure, and the right is twisted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 22:5; Ezekiel 2:6; Isaiah 10:3"> The best of them is like a waste plant, and their upright ones are like a wall of thorns. Sorrow! the day of their fate has come; now will trouble come on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:4; Psalms 118:8–9; Matthew 10:16"> Put no faith in a friend, do not let your hope be placed in a relation: keep watch on the doors of your mouth against her who is resting on your breast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 10:35–36; Luke 12:53; Matthew 10:21"> For the son puts shame on his father, the daughter goes against her mother and the daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law; and a man's haters are those of his family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25–26; Psalms 37:7; 1 John 5:14–15"> But as for me, I am looking to the Lord; I am waiting for the God of my salvation: the ears of my God will be open to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 8:12; Isaiah 9:2; Psalms 27:1"> Do not be glad because of my sorrow, O my hater: after my fall I will be lifted up; when I am seated in the dark, the Lord will be a light to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 37:6; 1 Corinthians 4:5; 1 Samuel 24:15"> I will undergo the wrath of the Lord, because of my sin against him; till he takes up my cause and does what is right for me: when he makes me come out into the light, I will see his righteousness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 10:5; Jeremiah 50:33–34; Psalms 42:10"> And my hater will see it and be covered with shame; she who said to me, Where is the Lord your God? my eyes will see their desire effected on her, now she will be crushed under foot like the dust of the streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Amos 9:11–15; Nehemiah 2:8; Nehemiah 2:17"> A day for building your walls! in that day will your limits be stretched far and wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 19:23–25; Isaiah 11:16; Hosea 11:11"> In that day they will come to you from Assyria and the towns of Egypt, and from Egypt even to the River, and from sea to sea and from mountain to mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 3:10–11; Jeremiah 25:11; Micah 6:13"> But the land will become a waste because of its people, as the fruit of their works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 23:1–4; Micah 5:4; Psalms 100:3"> Keep your people safe with your rod, the flock of your heritage, living by themselves in the woods in the middle of Carmel: let them get their food in Bashan and Gilead as in the past.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 78:12–72; Exodus 3:20; Psalms 68:22"> As in the days when you came out from the land of Egypt, let us see things of wonder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 26:11; Isaiah 52:15; Job 29:9–10"> The nations will see and be shamed because of all their strength; they will put their hands on their mouths, their ears will be stopped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; Psalms 72:9; Psalms 18:45"> They will take dust as their food like a snake, like the things which go flat on the earth; they will come shaking with fear out of their secret places: they will come with fear to the Lord our God, full of fear because of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Isaiah 44:22; Psalms 103:8–9"> Who is a God like you, offering forgiveness for evil-doing and overlooking the sins of the rest of his heritage? he does not keep his wrath for ever, because his delight is in mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 38:17; Romans 6:14; Isaiah 43:25"> He will again have pity on us; he will put our sins under his feet: and you will send all our sins down into the heart of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 28:13–14; Luke 1:72–74; Deuteronomy 7:8"> You will make clear your good faith to Jacob and your mercy to Abraham, as you gave your oath to our fathers from times long past.</span>
