<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jonah 1:1; John 21:15–17"> And the word of the Lord came to Jonah a second time, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:7; Jeremiah 1:17; Jonah 3:3"> Up! go to Nineveh, that great town, and give it the word which I have given you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 21:28–29; Psalms 80:10; Genesis 22:3"> So Jonah got up and went to Nineveh as the Lord had said. Now Nineveh was a very great town, three days' journey from end to end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:22; Jeremiah 18:7–10; 2 Kings 20:1"> And Jonah first of all went a day's journey into the town, and crying out said, In forty days destruction will overtake Nineveh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 11:32; Matthew 12:41; Joel 2:12–17"> And the people of Nineveh had belief in God; and a time was fixed for going without food, and they put on haircloth, from the greatest to the least.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 4:6–10; Esther 4:1–4; Daniel 9:3"> And the word came to the king of Nineveh, and he got up from his seat of authority, and took off his robe, and covering himself with haircloth, took his seat in the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:3; Joel 1:18; Joel 2:15–16"> And he had it given out in Nineveh, By the order of the king and his great men, no man or beast, herd or flock, is to have a taste of anything; let them have no food or water:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jonah 1:6; Isaiah 55:6–7; Ezekiel 18:21–24"> And let man and beast be covered with haircloth, and let them make strong prayers to God: and let everyone be turned from his evil way and the violent acts of their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:22; Joel 2:13–14; Psalms 106:45"> Who may say that God will not be turned, changing his purpose and turning away from his burning wrath, so that destruction may not overtake us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:8; 1 Kings 21:27–29; Luke 15:20"> And God saw what they did, how they were turned from their evil way; and God's purpose was changed as to the evil which he said he would do to them, and he did it not.</span>
