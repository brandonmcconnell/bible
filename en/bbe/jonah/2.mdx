<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 31:22; Psalms 5:7; 2 Chronicles 6:38"> And I said, I have been sent away from before your eyes; how may I ever again see your holy Temple?</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 30:3; Psalms 16:10; Isaiah 38:17"> I went down to the bases of the mountains; as for the earth, her walls were about me for ever: but you have taken up my life from the underworld, O Lord my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 18:6; 2 Chronicles 30:27; Psalms 11:4"> When my soul in me was overcome, I kept the memory of the Lord: and my prayer came in to you, into your holy Temple.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 8:26–27; Psalms 33:9; Genesis 1:3"> And at the Lord's order, the fish sent Jonah out of its mouth on to the dry land.</span>
