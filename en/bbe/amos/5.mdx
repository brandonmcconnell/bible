<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:1; Jeremiah 9:10; Jeremiah 7:29"> Give ear to this word, my song of sorrow over you, O children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:17; Lamentations 2:13; Jeremiah 2:27"> The virgin of Israel has been made low, never again to be lifted up: she is stretched out by herself on her land; there is no one to put her on her feet again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 10:22; Ezekiel 12:16; Isaiah 6:13"> For these are the words of the Lord God: The town which was able to send out a thousand, will have only a hundred; and that which sent out a hundred, will have only ten, in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; Zephaniah 2:3; Jeremiah 29:12–13"> For these are the words of the Lord to the children of Israel: Let your hearts be turned to me, so that you may have life:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 4:15; Amos 4:4; Amos 8:14"> Do not be looking for help to Beth-el, and do not go to Gilgal, or make your way to Beer-sheba: for Gilgal will certainly be taken prisoner, and Beth-el will come to nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:11; Isaiah 55:6; Ezekiel 20:47–48"> Go to the Lord for help so that you may have life; for fear that he may come like fire bursting out in the family of Joseph, causing destruction, and there will be no one to put it out in Beth-el.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 6:12; Amos 5:11–12; Zephaniah 1:6"> You who make the work of judging a bitter thing, crushing down righteousness to the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 4:13; Job 9:9; Amos 9:6"> Go for help to him who makes Orion and the Pleiades, by whom the deep dark is turned into morning, who makes the day black with night; whose voice goes out to the waters of the sea, sending them out over the face of the earth: the Lord is his name;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 13:17; Jeremiah 37:10; Micah 5:11"> Who sends sudden destruction on the strong, so that destruction comes on the walled town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 29:21; 1 Kings 22:8; John 8:45–47"> They have hate for him who makes protest against evil in the public place, and he whose words are upright is disgusting to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:30; Micah 6:15; Zephaniah 1:13"> So because the poor man is crushed under your feet, and you take taxes from him of grain: you have made for yourselves houses of cut stone, but you will not take your rest in them; the fair vine-gardens planted by your hands will not give you wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 2:6–7; Amos 5:10; Isaiah 1:23"> For I have seen how your evil-doing is increased and how strong are your sins, you troublers of the upright, who take rewards and do wrong to the cause of the poor in the public place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 4:4; 2 Timothy 3:1; Ecclesiastes 3:7"> So the wise will say nothing in that time; for it is an evil time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Matthew 6:33; Micah 6:8"> Go after good and not evil, so that life may be yours: and so the Lord, the God of armies, will be with you, as you say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 12:9; 3 John 1:11; Psalms 97:10"> Be haters of evil and lovers of good, and let right be done in the public place: it may be that the Lord, the God of armies, will have mercy on the rest of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joel 1:11; Jeremiah 9:17–20; Jeremiah 9:10"> So these are the words of the Lord, the God of armies, the Lord: There will be weeping in all the open spaces; and in all the streets they will say, Sorrow! sorrow! and they will get in the farmer to the weeping, and the makers of sad songs to give cries of grief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 12:12; Jeremiah 48:33; Isaiah 16:10"> In all the vine-gardens there will be cries of grief: for I will go through among you, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joel 1:15; Joel 2:1–2; Joel 2:31"> Sorrow to you who are looking for the day of the Lord! what is the day of the Lord to you? it is dark and not light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 24:17–18; Jeremiah 15:2–3; Acts 28:4"> As if a man, running away from a lion, came face to face with a bear; or went into the house and put his hand on the wall and got a bite from a snake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Ezekiel 34:12; Zephaniah 1:15"> Will not the day of the Lord be dark and not light? even very dark, with no light shining in it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 1:11–16; Proverbs 28:9; Leviticus 26:31"> Your feasts are disgusting to me, I will have nothing to do with them; I will take no delight in your holy meetings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Micah 6:6–7; Amos 4:4–5; Isaiah 66:3"> Even if you give me your burned offerings and your meal offerings, I will not take pleasure in them: I will have nothing to do with the peace-offerings of your fat beasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Amos 6:5; Amos 8:10; Amos 8:3"> Take away from me the noise of your songs; my ears are shut to the melody of your instruments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Micah 6:8; Jeremiah 22:3; Hosea 6:6"> But let the right go rolling on like waters, and righteousness like an ever-flowing stream.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 7:42–43; Ezekiel 20:8; Nehemiah 9:18"> Did you come to me with offerings of beasts and meal offerings in the waste land for forty years, O Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 23:12–13; 1 Kings 11:33; Leviticus 20:2–5"> Truly, you will take up Saccuth your king and Kaiwan your images, the star of your god, which you made for yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 7:43; 2 Kings 17:6; Amos 4:13"> And I will send you away as prisoners farther than Damascus, says the Lord, whose name is the God of armies.</span>
