<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 2:6; Micah 5:2; Hebrews 7:14"> Though Judah became stronger than his brothers, and from him came the ruler, the birthright was Joseph's:)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 46:9; Exodus 6:14; Numbers 26:5–9"> The sons of Reuben, the oldest son of Israel: Hanoch and Pallu, Hezron and Carmi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> The sons of Joel: Shemaiah his son, Gog his son, Shimei his son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Micah his son, Reaiah his son, Baal his son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:26; 2 Kings 15:29; 2 Chronicles 28:20"> Beerah his son, whom Tiglath-pileser, king of Assyria, took away as a prisoner: he was chief of the Reubenites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:17"> And his brothers by their families, when the list of their generations was made up: the chief, Jeiel, and Zechariah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:4; Numbers 32:34; Numbers 32:38"> And Bela, the son of Azaz, the son of Shema, the son of Joel, who was living in Aroer, as far as Nebo and Baal-meon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 22:8–9"> And to the east his limits went as far as the starting point of the waste land, ending at the river Euphrates, because their cattle were increased in number in the land of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 21:9; 1 Chronicles 5:18–21; Psalms 83:6"> And in the days of Saul they made war on the Hagarites, and overcame them; and they put up their tents through all the land east of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 13:11; Joshua 13:24–28; Deuteronomy 3:10–17"> And the sons of Gad were living opposite to them, in the land of Bashan as far as Salecah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Joel the chief, and Shapham the second, and Janai and Shaphat in Bashan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And their brothers, the men of their family: Michael and Meshullam and Sheba and Jorai and Jacan and Zia and Eber, seven of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> These were the sons of Abihail, the son of Huri, the son of Jaroah, the son of Gilead, the son of Michael, the son of Jeshishai, the son of Jahdo, the son of Buz;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Ahi, the son of Abdiel, the son of Guni, head of their families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:29; Song of Solomon 2:1; Isaiah 35:2"> And they were living in Gilead in Bashan, in its small towns and in all the grass-land of Sirion as far as its limits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 14:16; 2 Kings 14:28; 2 Kings 15:32"> All these were listed under the names of their families, in the time of Jotham, king of Judah, and in the time of Jeroboam, king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 1:3; Joshua 4:12–13"> There were forty-four thousand, seven hundred and sixty of the sons of Reuben and of the Gadites and of the half-tribe of Manasseh, all strong men, expert in the use of the body-cover, the sword, and the bow, and in the art of war, all able to take up arms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 25:15; 1 Chronicles 1:31; 1 Chronicles 5:10"> And they went to war against the Hagarites, with Jetur and Naphish and Nodab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 22:4–5; Psalms 9:10; 2 Chronicles 18:31"> And they were helped against them, so that the Hagarites, and those with them, were given into their power. For they sent up prayers to God in the fight, and he gave ear to them, because they put their faith in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 18:13; Numbers 31:35"> And they took away their cattle: fifty thousand camels, two hundred and fifty thousand sheep, and two thousand asses, and a hundred thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:8; Romans 8:31; Joshua 23:10"> And a very great number went to their death, because the war was God's purpose. And they went on living in their place till they were taken away as prisoners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:8–9; Song of Solomon 4:8; Psalms 133:3"> And the men of the half-tribe of Manasseh were living in the land: and their numbers were increased till all the land from Bashan to Baal-hermon and Senir and the mountain Hermon was theirs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 4:38"> And these were the heads of their families: Epher and Ishi and Eliel and Azriel and Jeremiah and Hodaviah and Jahdiel, men of war, of great name, heads of families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Revelation 17:5; Psalms 106:34–39; Exodus 34:15"> And they did evil against the God of their fathers, worshipping the gods of the people of the land, whom God had put to destruction before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 15:19; 2 Kings 17:6; 2 Kings 15:29"> And the God of Israel put an impulse into the heart of Pul, king of Assyria, and of Tiglath-pileser, king of Assyria, who took them away as prisoners, all the Reubenites and the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh, to Halah and Habor and Hara and to the river of Gozan, to this day.</span>
