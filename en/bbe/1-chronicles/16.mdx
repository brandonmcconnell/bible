<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:1; Ezra 6:16–18; 2 Chronicles 5:6–7"> Then they took in the ark of God and put it inside the tent which David had put up for it; and they made offerings, burned offerings and peace-offerings before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 47:10; Genesis 14:19; Leviticus 1:3"> And when David had come to an end of making the burned offerings and peace-offerings, he gave the people a blessing in the name of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:10; Ezekiel 45:17; 2 Chronicles 35:7–8"> And he gave to everyone, every man and woman of Israel, a cake of bread, some meat, and a cake of dry grapes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:27–32; Psalms 72:18; Psalms 103:2"> And he put some of the Levites before the ark of the Lord as servants, to keep the acts of the Lord in memory, and to give worship and praise to the Lord, the God of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:39; 2 Chronicles 29:25; 1 Chronicles 15:16–24"> Asaph the chief, and second to him Zechariah, Uzziel and Shemiramoth and Jehiel and Mattithiah and Eliab and Benaiah and Obed-edom and Jeiel, with corded instruments of music; and Asaph, with brass instruments sounding loudly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:12; 2 Chronicles 29:26–28; 2 Chronicles 5:12–13"> And Benaiah and Jahaziel the priests, blowing horns all the time before the ark of the agreement of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:1; Psalms 18:1; 2 Samuel 23:1–2"> Then on that day David first made the giving of praise to the Lord the work of Asaph and his brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 105:1–15; Isaiah 12:4; 2 Kings 19:19"> O give praise to the Lord; give honour to his name, talking of his doings among the peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; Psalms 96:1–3; Psalms 98:1–4"> Let your voice be sounded in songs and melody; let all your thoughts be of the wonder of his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–8; Jeremiah 29:13; Psalms 34:2"> Have glory in his holy name; let the hearts of those who are searching after the Lord be glad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 27:8–9; Zephaniah 2:2–3; Psalms 68:35"> Let your search be for the Lord and for his strength; let your hearts ever be turned to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 111:4; Psalms 119:20; Psalms 103:2"> Keep in mind the great works which he has done; his wonders, and the decisions of his mouth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Exodus 19:5–6; Psalms 135:4"> O you seed of Israel his servant, you children of Jacob, his loved ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 48:10–11; Isaiah 26:9; Exodus 15:2"> He is the Lord our God: he is judge of all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 105:8; Malachi 4:4; Deuteronomy 7:9"> He has kept his agreement in mind for ever, the word which he gave for a thousand generations;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 26:3; Genesis 17:2; Genesis 35:11"> The agreement which he made with Abraham, and his oath to Isaac;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 17:7–8; Genesis 35:11–12; Joshua 24:11–13"> And he gave it to Jacob for a law, and to Israel for an eternal agreement;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 13:15; Micah 2:5; Genesis 17:8"> Saying, To you will I give the land of Canaan, the measured line of your heritage:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; Deuteronomy 7:7; Acts 7:5"> When you were still small in number, and strange in the land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 46:6; Genesis 20:1; Genesis 46:3"> When they went about from one nation to another, and from one kingdom to another people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 12:17; Genesis 20:3; Exodus 7:15–18"> He would not let anyone do them wrong; he even kept back kings because of them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 20:7; Psalms 105:15; Genesis 49:8–10"> Saying, Put not your hand on those who have been marked with my holy oil, and do my prophets no wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 96:1–13; Psalms 71:15; Isaiah 51:6–8"> Make songs to the Lord, all the earth; give the good news of his salvation day by day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2–6; Daniel 4:1–3; Psalms 22:27"> Make clear his glory to the nations, and his wonders to all the peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 89:7; Psalms 76:7; Psalms 66:3–5"> For the Lord is great, and greatly to be praised; and he is more to be feared than all other gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 102:25; Leviticus 19:4; Isaiah 42:5"> For all the gods of the nations are false gods; but the Lord made the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 16:11; Psalms 96:6; Psalms 28:7–8"> Honour and glory are before him: strength and joy are in his holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:10; Philippians 4:13; Psalms 100:1–2"> Give to the Lord, O you families of the peoples, give to the Lord glory and strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 100:4; Psalms 96:9; Isaiah 6:3"> Give to the Lord the glory of his name; take with you an offering and come before him; give worship to the Lord in holy robes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 33:9; Psalms 148:5–6; Hebrews 1:3"> Be in fear before him, all the earth: the world is ordered so that it may not be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 96:10; Revelation 19:6; Psalms 97:1"> Let the heavens have joy and let the earth be glad; let them say among the nations, The Lord is King.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 148:9–10; Isaiah 44:23; Psalms 98:7–8"> Let the sea be thundering with all its waters; let the field be glad, and everything which is in it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 98:9; Psalms 96:12–13; Revelation 11:17–18"> Then let all the trees of the wood be sounding with joy before the Lord, for he is come to be the judge of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 106:1; 2 Chronicles 7:3; Psalms 107:1"> O give praise to the Lord, for he is good: for his mercy is unchanging for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 106:47–48; 1 Peter 2:9; Psalms 14:7"> And say, Be our saviour, O God of our salvation, and let us come back, and give us salvation from the nations, so that we may give honour to your holy name and have glory in your praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 8:15; Psalms 72:18–19; 1 Kings 8:56"> Praise be to the Lord, the God of Israel, for ever and for ever. And all the people said, So be it; and gave praise to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; Ezra 3:4; 1 Chronicles 16:4–6"> So he made Asaph and his brothers keep their places there before the ark of the agreement of the Lord, to do whatever had to be done before the ark at all times day by day:</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:10; 1 Chronicles 13:14; 1 Chronicles 25:3"> And Obed-edom, the son of Jeduthun, and Hosah, with their brothers, sixty-eight of them, to be door-keepers:</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 3:4; 1 Chronicles 15:11; 2 Chronicles 1:3–4"> And Zadok the priest, with his brothers the priests, before the House of the Lord in the high place at Gibeon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 28:3–8; Exodus 29:38–42; 2 Chronicles 31:3"> To give burned offerings to the Lord on the altar of burned offerings morning and evening, every day, as it is ordered in the law of the Lord which he gave to Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Chronicles 5:13; 1 Chronicles 25:1–6; 2 Chronicles 20:21"> And with them Heman and Jeduthun, and the rest who were marked out by name to give praise to the Lord, for his mercy is unchanging for ever;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 150:3–6; 1 Chronicles 25:6–7; 2 Chronicles 29:25–28"> And Heman and Jeduthun had horns and brass instruments sounding loudly, and instruments of music for the songs of God; and the sons of Jeduthun were to be at the door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:19–20; Genesis 18:19; Psalms 101:2"> And all the people went away, every man to his house; and David went back to give a blessing to his family.</span>
