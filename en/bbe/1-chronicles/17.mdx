<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:7–9; 1 Chronicles 15:1; 1 Chronicles 17:5"> Now when David was living in his house, he said to Nathan the prophet, See, I am living in a house of cedar-wood, but the ark of the Lord's agreement is under the curtains of a tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:2; 1 Corinthians 13:9; Zechariah 8:23"> And Nathan said to David, Do whatever is in your heart, for God is with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 3:7; Numbers 12:6; Isaiah 30:21"> But that same night, the word of God came to Nathan, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:2–3; Isaiah 55:8–9; 2 Chronicles 6:8–9"> Go and say to David my servant, The Lord says, You are not to make me a house for my living-place:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:6; Exodus 40:2–3; 1 Kings 8:16"> For from the day when I took Israel up, till this day, I have had no house, but have gone from tent to tent, and from living-place to living-place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:7; Numbers 10:33–36; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> In all the places where I have gone with all Israel, did I ever say to any of the judges of Israel, whom I made the keepers of my people, Why have you not made for me a house of cedar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:21; Exodus 3:1–10; 1 Samuel 17:15"> So now, say to my servant David, The Lord of armies says, I took you from the fields, from keeping sheep, so that you might be a ruler over my people Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:14; 1 Chronicles 17:2; Psalms 113:7–8"> And I have been with you wherever you went, cutting off before you all those who were against you; and I will make your name like the name of the greatest ones of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 2:23; Ezekiel 28:4; Ephesians 5:6"> And I will make a resting-place for my people Israel, planting them there, so that they may be in the place which is theirs and never again be moved; and never again will they be made waste by evil men, as they were at first,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:11; Judges 2:14–18; Exodus 1:21"> From the time when I put judges over my people Israel; and I will overcome all those who are against you; and I will make you great and the head of a line of kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 132:11; 1 Kings 1:21; 1 Chronicles 28:5"> And when the time comes for you to go to your fathers, I will put in your place your seed after you, one of your sons, and I will make his kingdom strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 5:5; Daniel 2:44; Isaiah 9:7"> He will be the builder of my house, and I will make the seat of his authority certain for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 1:5; Isaiah 55:3; 1 Chronicles 17:12"> I will be to him a father and he will be to me a son; and I will not take my mercy away from him as I took it from him who was before you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 89:36; Luke 1:32–33; Psalms 72:17"> But I will make his place in my house and in my kingdom certain for ever; and the seat of his authority will never be overturned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:28; Acts 20:27; 2 Samuel 7:17"> So Nathan gave David an account of all these words and this vision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:18; Genesis 32:10; Ephesians 3:8"> Then David the king went in and took his seat before the Lord, and said, Who am I, O Lord God, and what is my family, that you have been my guide till now?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:11–15; Philippians 2:8–11; Isaiah 49:6"> And this was only a small thing to you, O God; but your words have even been about the far-off future of your servant's family, looking on me as on one of high position, O Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 21:17; 1 Samuel 2:30; 2 Samuel 7:20–24"> What more may David say to you? for you have knowledge of your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 37:35; 2 Samuel 7:21; Daniel 9:17"> O Lord, because of your servant, and from your heart, you have done all these great things and let them be seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; Jeremiah 10:6–7; Ephesians 3:20"> O Lord, there is no one like you, and no other God but you, as is clear from everything which has come to our ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:9–10; Isaiah 63:9; Isaiah 48:9"> And what other nation in the earth, like your people Israel, did a god go out to take for himself, to be his people, making his name great and to be feared, driving out the nations from before your people whom you made free and took out of Egypt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 19:5–6; 1 Peter 2:9; Romans 11:1–12"> For your people Israel you made yours for ever; and you, Lord, became their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 119:49; Jeremiah 11:5; Luke 1:38"> And now, Lord, let your words about your servant and about his family be made certain for ever, and do as you have said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 6:13; Jeremiah 31:1; Revelation 21:3"> So let your words be made certain and your name be made great, when men say, The Lord of armies is the God of Israel; and when the family of David your servant is made strong before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:15; Ezekiel 36:37; Psalms 10:17"> For you, O my God, have let your servant see that you will make him head of a line of kings; and so it has come into your servant's heart to make his prayer to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 34:6–7; Hebrews 6:18; Titus 1:2"> And now, O Lord, you are God, and you have said you will give this good thing to your servant:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ephesians 1:3; Romans 11:29; Psalms 72:17"> And now you have been pleased to give your blessing to the family of your servant, so that it may go on for ever before you; you, O Lord, have given your blessing, and a blessing will be on it for ever.</span>
