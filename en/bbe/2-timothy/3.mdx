<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Peter 3:3; 2 Timothy 4:3; 1 Timothy 4:1–3"> But be certain of this, that in the last days times of trouble will come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 2:21; Romans 1:29–31; Jude 1:16"> For men will be lovers of self, lovers of money, uplifted in pride, given to bitter words, going against the authority of their fathers, never giving praise, having no religion,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jude 1:18; Romans 1:31; Matthew 10:21"> Without natural love, bitter haters, saying evil of others, violent and uncontrolled, hating all good,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Philippians 3:18–19; 1 Timothy 3:6; Jude 1:4"> False to their friends, acting without thought, lifted up in mind, loving pleasure more than God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Titus 1:16; Matthew 23:27–28"> Having a form of religion, but turning their backs on the power of it: go not with these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jude 1:4; 2 Peter 3:17; Titus 3:3"> For these are they who go secretly into houses, making prisoners of foolish women, weighted down with sin, turned from the way by their evil desires,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3–4; Ephesians 4:14; 1 Timothy 2:4"> Ever learning, and never coming to the knowledge of what is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 13:8–11; Revelation 2:20; 1 Timothy 6:5"> And as James and Jambres went against Moses, so do these go against what is true: men of evil minds, who, tested by faith, are seen to be false.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:8; Exodus 7:12; Jeremiah 28:15–17"> But they will go no farther: for their foolish behaviour will be clear to all men, as theirs was in the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:11; Titus 2:7; 2 Peter 1:5–7"> But you took as your example my teaching, behaviour, purpose, and faith; my long waiting, my love, my quiet undergoing of trouble;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 34:19; Acts 14:5–6; 2 Corinthians 11:23–28"> My punishments and pain; the things which came to me at Antioch, at Iconium, at Lystra; the cruel attacks made on me: and the Lord made me free from them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:14; 1 Peter 4:12–16; 1 Peter 2:20–21"> Yes, and all whose purpose is to be living in the knowledge of God in Christ Jesus, will be cruelly attacked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:1; 2 Peter 2:20; Titus 3:3"> Evil and false men will become worse and worse, using deceit and themselves overcome by deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:13; 2 Timothy 2:2; 2 Timothy 1:13"> But see that you keep to the teaching you have been given and the things of which you are certain, conscious of who has been your teacher;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:98–99; Luke 24:45; Proverbs 22:6"> And that from the time when you were a child, you have had knowledge of the holy Writings, which are able to make you wise to salvation, through faith in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 15:4; Hebrews 4:12; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> Every holy Writing which comes from God is of profit for teaching, for training, for guiding, for education in righteousness:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:21; Ephesians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 9:8"> So that the man of God may be complete, trained and made ready for every good work.</span>
