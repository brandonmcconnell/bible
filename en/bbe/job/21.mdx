<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Job made answer and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 2:1; Job 34:2; Job 15:11"> Give attention with care to my words; and let this be your comfort.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 17:2; Job 16:10; Job 16:20"> Let me say what is in my mind, and after that, go on making sport of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 102:1; Psalms 42:11; Psalms 142:2–3"> As for me, is my outcry against man? is it then to be wondered at if my spirit is troubled?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 29:9; Job 40:4; Judges 18:19"> Take note of me and be full of wonder, put your hand on your mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:16; Psalms 119:120; Psalms 88:15"> At the very thought of it my flesh is shaking with fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 73:3–12; Job 12:6; Jeremiah 12:1–3"> Why is life given to the evil-doers? why do they become old and strong in power?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 17:6; Job 5:3–4; Job 20:10"> Their children are ever with them, and their offspring before their eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 9:34; Psalms 73:5; Psalms 73:19"> Their houses are free from fear, and the rod of God does not come on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 23:26; Luke 16:19; Deuteronomy 7:13–14"> Their ox is ready at all times to give seed; their cow gives birth, without dropping her young.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 107:41; Psalms 127:3–5"> They send out their young ones like a flock, and their children have pleasure in the dance,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 6:4–6; Isaiah 5:12; Genesis 4:21"> They make songs to the instruments of music, and are glad at the sound of the pipe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 36:11; Luke 12:19–20; Psalms 73:4"> Their days come to an end without trouble, and suddenly they go down to the underworld.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:17; Romans 8:7; 2 Thessalonians 2:10–12"> Though they said to God, Go away from us, for we have no desire for the knowledge of your ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 5:2; Job 34:9; Malachi 1:13–14"> What is the Ruler of all, that we may give him worship? and what profit is it to us to make prayer to him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 1:1; Job 22:18; Genesis 49:6"> Truly, is not their well-being in their power? (The purpose of the evil-doers is far from me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 18:5–6; Proverbs 20:20; Proverbs 13:9"> How frequently is the light of the evil-doers put out, or does trouble come on them? how frequently does his wrath take them with cords?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 1:4; Psalms 83:13; Job 13:25"> How frequently are they as dry stems before the wind, or as grass taken away by the storm-wind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; Ezekiel 18:19–20; Ezekiel 18:2"> You say, God keeps punishment stored up for his children. Let him send it on the man himself, so that he may have the punishment of it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 14:10; Isaiah 51:17; Psalms 75:8"> Let his eyes see his trouble, and let him be full of the wrath of the Ruler of all!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 14:5; Ecclesiastes 2:18–19; Psalms 55:23"> For what interest has he in his house after him, when the number of his months is ended?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 11:34; 1 Corinthians 2:16; Isaiah 40:13–14"> Is anyone able to give teaching to God? for he is the judge of those who are on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 20:22–23; Luke 12:19–21; Psalms 73:4–5"> One comes to his end in complete well-being, full of peace and quiet:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 3:8; Psalms 17:10; Job 15:27"> His buckets are full of milk, and there is no loss of strength in his bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:16–17; Job 7:11; Proverbs 14:10"> And another comes to his end with a bitter soul, without ever tasting good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:2; Isaiah 14:11; Job 20:11"> Together they go down to the dust, and are covered by the worm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 5:22; Psalms 119:86; Psalms 59:4"> See, I am conscious of your thoughts, and of your violent purposes against me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 8:22; Job 20:7; Habakkuk 2:9–11"> For you say, Where is the house of the ruler, and where is the tent of the evil-doer?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 129:8"> Have you not put the question to the travellers, and do you not take note of their experience?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 16:4; 2 Peter 2:9–17; 2 Peter 3:7"> How the evil man goes free in the day of trouble, and has salvation in the day of wrath?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Galatians 2:11; Deuteronomy 7:10; Romans 12:19"> Who will make his way clear to his face? and if he has done a thing, who gives him punishment for it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 16:22; Psalms 49:14; Ezekiel 32:21–32"> He is taken to his last resting-place, and keeps watch over it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 9:27; Job 24:24; Job 30:23"> The earth of the valley covering his bones is sweet to him, and all men come after him, as there were unnumbered before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 16:2; Job 32:3; Job 13:4"> Why then do you give me comfort with words in which there is no profit, when you see that there is nothing in your answers but deceit?</span>
