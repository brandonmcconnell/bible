<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 40:6; Job 37:1–2; 2 Kings 2:11"> And the Lord made answer to Job out of the storm-wind, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 42:3; 1 Timothy 1:7; Job 35:16"> Who is this who makes the purpose of God dark by words without knowledge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 40:7; Exodus 12:11; Jeremiah 1:17"> Get your strength together like a man of war; I will put questions to you, and you will give me the answers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 104:5; Hebrews 1:10; Proverbs 30:4"> Where were you when I put the earth on its base? Say, if you have knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 40:12; Zechariah 2:1–2; Job 11:9"> By whom were its measures fixed? Say, if you have wisdom; or by whom was the line stretched out over it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 26:7; Psalms 104:5; Psalms 93:1"> On what were its pillars based, or who put down its angle-stone,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 5:11; Job 1:6; Ezra 3:11–12"> When the morning stars made songs together, and all the sons of the gods gave cries of joy?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 1:9; Proverbs 8:29; Jeremiah 5:22"> Or where were you when the sea came to birth, pushing out from its secret place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 1:2"> When I made the cloud its robe, and put thick clouds as bands round it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:22; Psalms 104:9; Psalms 33:7"> Ordering a fixed limit for it, with locks and doors;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 89:9; Proverbs 8:29; Psalms 65:6–7"> And said, So far you may come, and no farther; and here the pride of your waves will be stopped?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 74:16; Psalms 148:3–5; Genesis 1:5"> Have you, from your earliest days, given orders to the morning, or made the dawn conscious of its place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 104:35; Job 37:3; Exodus 14:27"> So that it might take a grip of the skirts of the earth, shaking all the evil-doers out of it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 104:6; Psalms 104:2"> It is changed like wet earth under a stamp, and is coloured like a robe;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 10:15; Psalms 37:17; Job 18:5"> And from the evil-doers their light is kept back, and the arm of pride is broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 8:24; Job 26:5–6; Psalms 77:19"> Have you come into the springs of the sea, walking in the secret places of the deep?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 9:13; Psalms 107:18; Job 3:5"> Have the doors of death been open to you, or have the door-keepers of the dark ever seen you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 89:11–12; Revelation 20:9; Jeremiah 31:37"> Have you taken note of the wide limits of the earth? Say, if you have knowledge of it all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:8; Genesis 1:14–18; Genesis 1:3–4"> Which is the way to the resting-place of the light, and where is the store-house of the dark;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 10:19; Job 26:10; Genesis 23:17"> So that you might take it to its limit, guiding it to its house?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 15:7; Job 38:12; Job 38:4"> No doubt you have knowledge of it, for then you had come to birth, and the number of your days is great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 37:6; Psalms 135:7; Psalms 33:7"> Have you come into the secret place of snow, or have you seen the store-houses of the ice-drops,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 10:11; Isaiah 30:30; Exodus 9:18"> Which I have kept for the time of trouble, for the day of war and fighting?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 38:12–13; Jonah 4:8; Matthew 24:27"> Which is the way to the place where the wind is measured out, and the east wind sent out over the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 28:26; Psalms 29:3–10; Job 37:3–6"> By whom has the way been cut for the flowing of the rain, and the flaming of the thunder;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:22; Hebrews 6:7–8; Psalms 107:35"> Causing rain to come on a land where no man is living, on the waste land which has no people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 107:35; Psalms 104:14"> To give water to the land where there is waste and destruction, and to make the dry land green with young grass?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:22; Psalms 147:8; Jeremiah 10:13"> Has the rain a father? or who gave birth to the drops of night mist?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 147:16–17; Job 37:10; Job 38:8"> Out of whose body came the ice? and who gave birth to the cold mist of heaven?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 37:10"> The waters are joined together, hard as a stone, and the face of the deep is covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Amos 5:8; Job 9:9"> Are the bands of the Pleiades fixed by you, or are the cords of Orion made loose?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 38:31; Job 9:9; 2 Kings 23:5"> Do you make Mazzaroth come out in its right time, or are the Bear and its children guided by you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:35–36; Genesis 8:22; Job 38:12–13"> Have you knowledge of the laws of the heavens? did you give them rule over the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 22:11; Job 36:27–28; Amos 5:8"> Is your voice sent up to the cloud, so that you may be covered by the weight of waters?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 36:32; Job 37:3; Isaiah 65:1"> Do you send out the thunder-flames, so that they may go, and say to you, Here we are?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Job 32:8; Psalms 51:6; Proverbs 2:6"> Who has put wisdom in the high clouds, or given knowledge to the lights of the north?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Psalms 147:4; Genesis 15:5; Genesis 8:1"> By whose wisdom are the clouds numbered, or the water-skins of the heavens turned to the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> When the earth becomes hard as metal, and is joined together in masses?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 104:21; Job 4:10–11; Psalms 34:10"> Do you go after food for the she-lion, or get meat so that the young lions may have enough,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Job 37:8; Genesis 49:9; Numbers 24:9"> When they are stretched out in their holes, and are waiting in the brushwood?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 12:24; Matthew 6:26; Psalms 147:9"> Who gives in the evening the meat he is searching for, when his young ones are crying to God; when the young lions with loud noise go wandering after their food?</span>
