<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 20:29; Job 27:13; Hebrews 13:4"> For what is God's reward from on high, or the heritage given by the Ruler of all from heaven?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 21:30; Job 34:22; Matthew 7:13"> Is it not trouble for the sinner, and destruction for the evil-doers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 34:21; Proverbs 5:21; 2 Chronicles 16:9"> Does he not see my ways, and are not my steps all numbered?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Psalms 44:20–21; Psalms 12:2"> If I have gone in false ways, or my foot has been quick in working deceit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 7:8–9; Daniel 5:27; Isaiah 26:7"> (Let me be measured in upright scales, and let God see my righteousness:)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 15:39; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Matthew 5:29"> If my steps have been turned out of the way, or if my heart went after my eyes, or if the property of another is in my hands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Micah 6:15; Leviticus 26:16; Deuteronomy 28:38"> Let me put seed in the earth for another to have the fruit of it, and let my produce be uprooted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 11:4; Ecclesiastes 7:26; Judges 16:5"> If my heart went after another man's wife, or if I was waiting secretly at my neighbour's door;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:10; 2 Samuel 12:11; Isaiah 47:2"> Then let my wife give pleasure to another man and let others make use of her body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 20:10; Deuteronomy 22:22–24; Job 31:28"> For that would be a crime; it would be an act for which punishment would be measured out by the judges:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 6:27; Jeremiah 5:7–9; Job 15:30"> It would be a fire burning even to destruction, and taking away all my produce.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:14–15; Exodus 21:26–27; Leviticus 25:46"> If I did wrong in the cause of my man-servant, or my woman-servant, when they went to law with me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 2:13; Psalms 9:12; Zechariah 2:13"> What then will I do when God comes as my judge? and what answer may I give to his questions?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 14:31; Malachi 2:10; Job 34:19"> Did not God make him as well as me? did he not give us life in our mothers' bodies?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 38:14; Deuteronomy 15:7–10; Deuteronomy 28:32"> If I kept back the desire of the poor; if the widow's eye was looking for help to no purpose;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 29:12–16; John 13:29; Job 22:7"> If I kept my food for myself, and did not give some of it to the child with no father;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> (For I was cared for by God as by a father from my earliest days; he was my guide from the body of my mother;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 22:6; Luke 3:11; Job 24:4"> If I saw one near to death for need of clothing, and that the poor had nothing covering him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:13; Job 29:11"> If his back did not give me a blessing, and the wool of my sheep did not make him warm;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 22:9; Job 29:12; Micah 7:3"> If my hand had been lifted up against him who had done no wrong, when I saw that I was supported by the judges;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 137:6; Job 38:15; Job 31:40"> May my arm be pulled from my body, and be broken from its base.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 13:11; Psalms 119:120; 2 Corinthians 5:11"> For the fear of God kept me back, and because of his power I might not do such things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 49:6–7; Deuteronomy 8:12–14; Proverbs 11:28"> If I made gold my hope, or if I ever said to the best gold, I have put my faith in you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 62:10; Habakkuk 1:16; Isaiah 10:13–14"> If I was glad because my wealth was great, and because my hand had got together a great store;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:16; Deuteronomy 4:19; Deuteronomy 17:3"> If, when I saw the sun shining, and the moon moving on its bright way,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:6; Deuteronomy 11:16; Psalms 2:12"> A secret feeling of worship came into my heart, and my hand gave kisses from my mouth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:2–7; Job 31:11; Psalms 50:6"> That would have been another sin to be rewarded with punishment by the judges; for I would have been false to God on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 24:17–18; Proverbs 17:5; Psalms 35:13–14"> If I was glad at the trouble of my hater, and gave cries of joy when evil overtook him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Romans 12:14; Exodus 23:4–5"> (For I did not let my mouth give way to sin, in putting a curse on his life;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 1:18; Proverbs 1:11–12; Micah 3:2–3"> If the men of my tent did not say, Who has not had full measure of his meat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 13:2; Genesis 19:2–3; Judges 19:20–21"> The traveller did not take his night's rest in the street, and my doors were open to anyone on a journey;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; Hosea 6:7; Acts 5:8"> If I kept my evil doings covered, and my sin in the secret of my breast,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 23:2; Proverbs 29:25; Esther 4:11"> For fear of the great body of people, or for fear that families might make sport of me, so that I kept quiet, and did not go out of my door;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 13:21–22; Job 40:4–5; Job 17:3"> If only God would give ear to me, and the Ruler of all would give me an answer! or if what he has against me had been put in writing!</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 22:22; Job 29:14; Isaiah 62:3"> Truly I would take up the book in my hands; it would be to me as a crown;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Job 13:15; Ephesians 3:12; Hebrews 4:15–16"> I would make clear the number of my steps, I would put it before him like a prince! The words of Job are ended.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:11; James 5:4; Psalms 65:13"> If my land has made an outcry against me, or the ploughed earth has been in sorrow;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 21:19; James 5:4; Isaiah 26:21"> If I have taken its produce without payment, causing the death of its owners;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Genesis 3:17–18; Malachi 1:3; Psalms 72:20"> Then in place of grain let thorns come up, and in place of barley evil-smelling plants.</span>
