<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Job made answer and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 5:21; Job 8:8–10; 1 Corinthians 6:5"> No doubt you have knowledge, and wisdom will come to an end with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 13:2–5; Proverbs 26:4; 2 Corinthians 11:21–23"> But I have a mind as well as you; I am equal to you: yes, who has not knowledge of such things as these?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 30:1; Job 17:6; Psalms 91:15"> It seems that I am to be as one who is a cause of laughing to his neighbour, one who makes his prayer to God and is answered! the upright man who has done no wrong is to be made sport of!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 25:8; Proverbs 13:9; Psalms 17:5"> In the thought of him who is in comfort there is no respect for one who is in trouble; such is the fate of those whose feet are slipping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 9:24; Psalms 73:11–12; Psalms 17:14"> There is wealth in the tents of those who make destruction, and those by whom God is moved to wrath are safe; even those whose god is their strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:3; Proverbs 6:6; Jeremiah 8:7"> But put now a question to the beasts, and get teaching from them; or to the birds of the heaven, and they will make it clear to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Or to the things which go flat on the earth, and they will give you wisdom; and the fishes of the sea will give you news of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 41:20; Jeremiah 27:5–6; Romans 11:36"> Who does not see by all these that the hand of the Lord has done this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 17:28; Job 33:4; Acts 17:25"> In whose hand is the soul of every living thing, and the breath of all flesh of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 34:3; Hebrews 5:14; Job 6:30"> Are not words tested by the ear, even as food is tasted by the mouth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 32:7; Job 15:10; Job 8:8"> Old men have wisdom, and a long life gives knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 9:4; Job 36:5; Proverbs 2:6–7"> With him there is wisdom and strength; power and knowledge are his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 3:7; Isaiah 22:22; Malachi 1:4"> Truly, there is no building up of what is pulled down by him; when a man is shut up by him, no one may let him loose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; Amos 5:8; Genesis 6:13"> Truly, he keeps back the waters and they are dry; he sends them out and the earth is overturned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 13:9; Ezekiel 14:9; 1 Kings 22:22–23"> With him are strength and wise designs; he who is guided into error, together with his guide, are in his hands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 29:14; 1 Corinthians 1:19–20; 2 Samuel 17:23"> He takes away the wisdom of the wise guides, and makes judges foolish;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 2:21; Psalms 116:16; 2 Chronicles 33:11–14"> He undoes the chains of kings, and puts his band on them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 45:1; Isaiah 37:36–38; Revelation 17:14"> He makes priests prisoners, overturning those in safe positions;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 32:9; Job 17:4; Job 12:24"> He makes the words of responsible persons without effect, and takes away the good sense of the old;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 107:40; Isaiah 23:9; 2 Kings 9:34–37"> He puts shame on chiefs, and takes away the power of the strong;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 2:22; 1 Corinthians 4:5; 1 Corinthians 2:10"> Uncovering deep things out of the dark, and making the deep shade bright;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 9:3; Isaiah 26:15; Isaiah 60:22"> Increasing nations, and sending destruction on them; making wide the lands of peoples, and then giving them up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 107:40; Job 12:20; Hosea 7:11"> He takes away the wisdom of the rulers of the earth, and sends them wandering in a waste where there is no way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 5:14; Psalms 107:27; Isaiah 19:14"> They go feeling about in the dark without light, wandering without help like those overcome with wine.</span>
