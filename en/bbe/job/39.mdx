<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 29:9; 1 Samuel 24:2; Psalms 104:18"> Have you knowledge of the rock-goats? or do you see the roes giving birth to their young?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:24"> Is the number of their months fixed by you? or is the time when they give birth ordered by you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> They are bent down, they give birth to their young, they let loose the fruit of their body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Their young ones are strong, living in the open country; they go out and do not come back again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 11:12; Job 24:5; Job 6:5"> Who has let the ass of the fields go free? or made loose the bands of the loud-voiced beast?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 107:34; Job 24:5; Deuteronomy 29:23"> To whom I have given the waste land for a heritage, and the salt land as a living-place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 5:18; Job 3:18; Exodus 5:13–16"> He makes sport of the noise of the town; the voice of the driver does not come to his ears;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 104:27–28; Psalms 145:15–16; Job 40:15"> He goes looking for his grass-lands in the mountains, searching out every green thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 23:22; Deuteronomy 33:17; Psalms 92:10"> Will the ox of the mountains be your servant? or is his night's resting-place by your food-store?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 10:10–11; Psalms 129:3; Job 39:7"> Will he be pulling your plough with cords, turning up the valleys after you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 1:26; Psalms 144:14; Genesis 42:26"> Will you put your faith in him, because his strength is great? will you give the fruit of your work into his care?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:15; Proverbs 3:16; Amos 2:13"> Will you be looking for him to come back, and get in your seed to the crushing-floor?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:7; Zechariah 5:9; 2 Chronicles 9:21"> Is the wing of the ostrich feeble, or is it because she has no feathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> That she puts her eggs on the earth, warming them in the dust,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Without a thought that they may be crushed by the foot, and broken by the beasts of the field?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Lamentations 4:3; Deuteronomy 28:56–57; 1 Kings 3:26–27"> She is cruel to her young ones, as if they were not hers; her work is to no purpose; she has no fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 35:11; Job 17:4; Isaiah 57:17"> For God has taken wisdom from her mind, and given her no measure of knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 41:29; Job 39:22; Job 5:22"> When she is shaking her wings on high, she makes sport of the horse and of him who is seated on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 147:10; Psalms 93:1; Psalms 104:1"> Do you give strength to the horse? is it by your hand that his neck is clothed with power?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:16; Job 41:20–21; Joel 2:5"> Is it through you that he is shaking like a locust, in the pride of his loud-sounding breath?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:6; Psalms 19:5; 1 Samuel 17:4–10"> He is stamping with joy in the valley; he makes sport of fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 41:33; Job 39:16; Job 39:18"> In his strength he goes out against the arms of war, turning not away from the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 41:26–29"> The bow is sounding against him; he sees the shining point of spear and arrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Amos 3:6; Luke 24:41; Job 37:20"> Shaking with passion, he is biting the earth; he is not able to keep quiet at the sound of the horn;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 70:3; Ezekiel 26:2; Ezekiel 36:2"> When it comes to his ears he says, Aha! He is smelling the fight from far off, and hearing the thunder of the captains, and the war-cries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:15; Song of Solomon 2:12; Leviticus 16:11"> Is it through your knowledge that the hawk takes his flight, stretching out his wings to the south?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Obadiah 1:4; Jeremiah 49:16; Exodus 19:4"> Or is it by your orders that the eagle goes up, and makes his resting-place on high?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:4"> On the rock is his house, and on the mountain-top his strong place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 9:26"> From there he is watching for food; his eye sees it far off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 24:28; Luke 17:37; Ezekiel 39:17–19"> His young have blood for their drink, and where the dead bodies are, there is he to be seen.</span>
