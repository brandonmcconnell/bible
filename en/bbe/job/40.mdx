<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 38:3; Job 42:4; Job 23:3–4"> Get your strength together like a man of war: I will put questions to you, and you will give me the answers.</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4–9; Romans 5:6; Psalms 44:6"> Then I will give praise to you, saying that your right hand is able to give you salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 40:20; Psalms 104:14; Genesis 1:24–26"> See now the Great Beast, whom I made, even as I made you; he takes grass for food, like the ox.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> His strength is in his body, and his force in the muscles of his stomach.</span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 41:33; Job 26:13; Isaiah 27:1"> He is the chief of the ways of God, made by him for his pleasure.</span>
