<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:25; Job 36:5; Job 1:22"> Truly, in saying this you are wrong; for God is greater than man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 40:2; Isaiah 45:9; Romans 11:34"> Why do you put forward your cause against him, saying, He gives no answer to any of my words?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24; 2 Chronicles 33:10; Job 33:29"> For God gives his word in one way, even in two, and man is not conscious of it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 12:6; Job 4:13; Genesis 20:3"> In a dream, in a vision of the night, when deep sleep comes on men, while they take their rest on their beds;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 36:10; Job 36:15; Psalms 40:6"> Then he makes his secrets clear to men, so that they are full of fear at what they see;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 2:11; James 4:10; Genesis 20:6"> In order that man may be turned from his evil works, and that pride may be taken away from him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 33:30; Job 33:28; 2 Peter 3:9"> To keep back his soul from the underworld, and his life from destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 30:17; Job 5:17–18; 1 Corinthians 11:32"> Pain is sent on him as a punishment, while he is on his bed; there is no end to the trouble in his bones;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 107:17–18; Job 3:24; Genesis 3:6"> He has no desire for food, and his soul is turned away from delicate meat;</span>

<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 49:7–8; Matthew 20:28; 1 Peter 1:18–19"> And if he has mercy on him, and says, Let him not go down to the underworld, I have given the price for his life:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 103:5; 2 Kings 5:14; Joshua 14:10–11"> Then his flesh becomes young again, and he comes back to the days of his early strength;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Psalms 30:5; Acts 9:11"> He makes his prayer to God, and he has mercy on him; he sees God's face with cries of joy; he gives news of his righteousness to men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 6:21; 2 Samuel 12:13; Psalms 14:2"> He makes a song, saying, I did wrong, turning from the straight way, but he did not give me the reward of my sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 33:24; Job 3:16; Job 33:22"> He kept my soul from the underworld, and my life sees the light in full measure.</span>

<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 21:27; Job 15:4–5; Job 22:5–9"> If you have anything to say, give me an answer; for it is my desire that you may be judged free from sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 34:11; Proverbs 5:1–2; Psalms 49:3"> If not, give attention to me, and keep quiet, and I will give you wisdom.</span>
