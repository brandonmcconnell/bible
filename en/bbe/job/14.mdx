<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 5:7; Ecclesiastes 2:23; Job 25:4"> As for man, the son of woman, his days are short and full of trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 40:6–8; 1 Peter 1:24; Job 8:9"> He comes out like a flower, and is cut down: he goes in flight like a shade, and is never seen again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 144:3; Psalms 143:2; Psalms 8:4"> Is it on such a one as this that your eyes are fixed, with the purpose of judging him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 3:6; Job 15:14; Ephesians 2:3"> If only a clean thing might come out of an unclean! But it is not possible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 39:4; Acts 17:26; Hebrews 9:27"> If his days are ordered, and you have knowledge of the number of his months, having given him a fixed limit past which he may not go;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 7:19; Psalms 39:13; Job 7:1–2"> Let your eyes be turned away from him, and take your hand from him, so that he may have pleasure at the end of his day, like a servant working for payment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 27:6; Isaiah 11:1; Job 14:14"> For there is hope of a tree; if it is cut down, it will come to life again, and its branches will not come to an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:36; Isaiah 26:19; John 12:24"> Though its root may be old in the earth, and its cut-off end may be dead in the dust;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 11:17–24; Ezekiel 17:3–10; Ezekiel 17:22–24"> Still, at the smell of water, it will make buds, and put out branches like a young plant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 19:26; Job 7:7–10; Job 14:12"> But man comes to his death and is gone: he gives up his spirit, and where is he?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 19:5; Jeremiah 15:18; Job 6:15–18"> The waters go from a pool, and a river becomes waste and dry;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 10:21–22; Job 3:13; Ecclesiastes 3:19–21"> So man goes down to his last resting-place and comes not again: till the heavens come to an end, they will not be awake or come out of their sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 13:32; Isaiah 57:1–2; Isaiah 26:20–21"> If only you would keep me safe in the underworld, putting me in a secret place till your wrath is past, giving me a fixed time when I might come to your memory again!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:51–52; Job 7:1; Acts 26:8"> If death takes a man, will he come to life again? All the days of my trouble I would be waiting, till the time came for me to be free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 13:22; Psalms 138:8; Job 10:8"> At the sound of your voice I would give an answer, and you would have a desire for the work of your hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 10:6; Job 31:4; Psalms 139:1–4"> For now my steps are numbered by you, and my sin is not overlooked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:34; Hosea 13:12; Job 21:19"> My wrongdoing is corded up in a bag, and my sin is shut up safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 6:14; Job 18:4; Jeremiah 4:24"> But truly a mountain falling comes to dust, and a rock is moved from its place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 7:6; Job 27:8; Job 19:10"> The stones are crushed small by the force of the waters; the dust of the earth is washed away by their overflowing: and so you put an end to the hope of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 14:14; Job 2:12; Ecclesiastes 8:8"> You overcome him for ever, and he is gone; his face is changed in death, and you send him away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:5; Ecclesiastes 2:18–19; Isaiah 63:16"> His sons come to honour, and he has no knowledge of it; they are made low, but he is not conscious of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 19:26; Job 19:20; Luke 16:23–24"> Only his flesh still has pain, and his soul is sad.</span>
