<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 28:2–4; Jeremiah 5:22; Exodus 19:16"> At this my heart is shaking; it is moved out of its place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 37:5; Job 36:33; Psalms 104:7"> Give ear to the rolling noise of his voice; to the hollow sound which goes out of his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 38:13; Matthew 24:27; Isaiah 11:12"> He sends it out through all the heaven, and his thunder-flame to the ends of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 29:3–9; Psalms 68:33; Exodus 15:7–8"> After it a voice is sounding, thundering out the word of his power; he does not keep back his thunder-flames; from his mouth his voice is sounding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 5:9; Romans 11:33; Isaiah 40:28"> He does wonders, more than may be searched out; great things of which we have no knowledge;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 38:22; Job 36:27; Ezekiel 13:13"> For he says to the snow, Make the earth wet; and to the rain-storm, Come down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 111:2; Psalms 109:27; Isaiah 26:11"> He puts an end to the work of every man, so that all may see his work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 104:22; Job 38:40"> Then the beasts go into their holes, and take their rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 9:9; Psalms 104:3; Isaiah 21:1"> Out of its place comes the storm-wind, and the cold out of its store-houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 38:29–30; Psalms 147:16–18; Psalms 78:47"> By the breath of God ice is made, and the wide waters are shut in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 36:27–30; Matthew 17:5; Isaiah 18:4"> The thick cloud is weighted with thunder-flame, and the cloud sends out its light;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 148:8; Joel 2:23; James 5:17–18"> And it goes this way and that, round about, turning itself by his guiding, to do whatever he gives orders to be done, on the face of his world of men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 18:45; Job 38:26–27; 1 Samuel 12:18–19"> For a rod, or for a curse, or for mercy, causing it to come on the mark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 111:2; Psalms 145:10–12; Exodus 14:13"> Give ear to this, O Job, and keep quiet in your place; and take note of the wonders worked by God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 36:30–32; Isaiah 40:26; Job 37:11"> Have you knowledge of God's ordering of his works, how he makes the light of his cloud to be seen?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 36:4; Proverbs 3:19–20; Isaiah 40:22"> Have you knowledge of the balancings of the clouds, the wonders of him who has all wisdom?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 147:18; Job 6:17; Job 38:31"> You, whose clothing is warm, when the earth is quiet because of the south wind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 44:24; Psalms 104:2; Isaiah 40:22"> Will you, with him, make the skies smooth, and strong as a polished looking-glass?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 38:2; Psalms 73:22; Job 42:3"> Make clear to me what we are to say to him; we are unable to put our cause before him, because of the dark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 11:7–8; Psalms 139:4; Job 6:3"> How may he have knowledge of my desire for talk with him? or did any man ever say, May destruction come on me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 36:32; Job 26:9; Job 38:25"> And now the light is not seen, for it is dark because of the clouds; but a wind comes, clearing them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 104:1; Jude 1:25; Hebrews 12:29"> A bright light comes out of the north; God's glory is greatly to be feared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:16; Romans 11:33; Psalms 99:4"> There is no searching out of the Ruler of all: his strength and his judging are great; he is full of righteousness, doing no wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:28; 1 Corinthians 1:26; Psalms 130:4"> For this cause men go in fear of him; he has no respect for any who are wise in heart.</span>
