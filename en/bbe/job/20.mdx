<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 42:9; Job 11:1"> Then Zophar the Naamathite made answer and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 14:29; Psalms 31:22; Job 20:3"> For this cause my thoughts are troubling me and driving me on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 33:3; Job 20:2; Psalms 78:2–5"> I have to give ear to arguments which put me to shame, and your answers to me are wind without wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 9:1–3; Job 15:10; Job 32:7"> Have you knowledge of this from early times, when man was placed on the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 37:35–36; Job 18:5–6; Acts 12:22–23"> That the pride of the sinner is short, and the joy of the evil-doer but for a minute?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 14:13–14; Obadiah 1:3–4; Genesis 11:4"> Though he is lifted up to the heavens, and his head goes up to the clouds;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 7:10; 1 Kings 14:10; Job 4:20"> Like the waste from his body he comes to an end for ever: those who have seen him say, Where is he?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 90:5; Psalms 73:20; Isaiah 29:7–8"> He is gone like a dream, and is not seen again; he goes in flight like a vision of the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 7:8; Job 7:10; Job 8:18"> The eye which saw him sees him no longer; and his place has no more knowledge of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 20:18; Luke 19:8; Job 27:16–17"> His children are hoping that the poor will be kind to them, and his hands give back his wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:26; Psalms 25:7; Job 21:26"> His bones are full of young strength, but it will go down with him into the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 15:16; Psalms 10:7; Ecclesiastes 11:9"> Though evil-doing is sweet in his mouth, and he keeps it secretly under his tongue;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 9:43–49; Romans 8:13; Matthew 5:29–30"> Though he takes care of it, and does not let it go, but keeps it still in his mouth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 3:13; Psalms 51:8–9; Psalms 32:3–4"> His food becomes bitter in his stomach; the poison of snakes is inside him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 23:8; Matthew 27:3–4"> He takes down wealth as food, and sends it up again; it is forced out of his stomach by God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 28:3–6; Romans 3:13; Deuteronomy 32:24"> He takes the poison of snakes into his mouth, the tongue of the snake is the cause of his death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13–14; Job 29:6; Luke 16:24"> Let him not see the rivers of oil, the streams of honey and milk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 20:10; Job 20:15; Jeremiah 51:44"> He is forced to give back the fruit of his work, and may not take it for food; he has no joy in the profit of his trading.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 35:9; Proverbs 14:31; James 5:4"> Because he has been cruel to the poor, turning away from them in their trouble; because he has taken a house by force which he did not put up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:13–14; Isaiah 57:20–21"> There is no peace for him in his wealth, and no salvation for him in those things in which he took delight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:11; Job 15:29; Luke 16:24–25"> He had never enough for his desire; for this cause his well-being will quickly come to an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 15:29; Revelation 18:7; 2 Kings 24:2"> Even when his wealth is great, he is full of care, for the hand of everyone who is in trouble is turned against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 78:30–31; Numbers 11:33; Psalms 11:6"> God gives him his desire, and sends the heat of his wrath on him, making it come down on him like rain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 24:18; Amos 5:19; 2 Samuel 22:35"> He may go in flight from the iron spear, but the arrow from the bow of brass will go through him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 16:13; Job 18:11; Deuteronomy 32:41"> He is pulling it out, and it comes out of his back; and its shining point comes out of his side; he is overcome by fears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 21:9; Psalms 120:4; Isaiah 30:33"> All his wealth is stored up for the dark: a fire not made by man sends destruction on him, and on everything in his tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 26:21; Luke 12:2–3; Job 16:18"> The heavens make clear his sin, and the earth gives witness against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 27:14–19; Revelation 18:17; James 5:1–3"> The produce of his house is taken away into another country, like things given into the hands of others in the day of wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 27:13; Job 31:2–3; Psalms 11:5–6"> This is the reward of the evil man, and the heritage given to him by God.</span>
