<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 30:10; Proverbs 25:15; Psalms 55:21"> Will he make prayers to you, or say soft words to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 21:6; Deuteronomy 15:17; Psalms 8:5–6"> Will he make an agreement with you, so that you may take him as a servant for ever?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 16:25–30; Job 28:11"> Will you make sport with him, as with a bird? or put him in chains for your young women?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 14:11"> Will the fishermen make profit out of him? will they have him cut up for the traders?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Will you put sharp-pointed irons into his skin, or fish-spears into his head?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 20:11; Luke 14:31–32; 2 Kings 10:4"> Only put your hand on him, and see what a fight you will have; you will not do it again!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 28:19; 1 Samuel 3:11; Luke 21:11"> Truly, the hope of his attacker is false; he is overcome even on seeing him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:4; Psalms 2:11–12; 1 Corinthians 10:22"> He is so cruel that no one is ready to go against him. Who then is able to keep his place before me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 11:35; Psalms 24:1; Job 35:7"> Who ever went against me, and got the better of me? There is no one under heaven!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 1:25"> I will not keep quiet about the parts of his body, or about his power, and the strength of his frame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 19:28; Psalms 32:9; James 3:3"> Who has ever taken off his outer skin? who may come inside his inner coat of iron?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:4; Psalms 57:4; Psalms 58:6"> Who has made open the doors of his face? Fear is round about his teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 5:5; Revelation 5:2–3; Jeremiah 9:23"> His back is made of lines of plates, joined tight together, one against the other, like a stamp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> One is so near to the other that no air may come between them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> They take a grip of one another; they are joined together, so that they may not be parted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 3:9; Revelation 1:14"> His sneezings give out flames, and his eyes are like the eyes of the dawn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 18:8"> Out of his mouth go burning lights, and flames of fire are jumping up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:13–14"> Smoke comes out of his nose, like a pot boiling on the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 18:8; Isaiah 30:33; Habakkuk 3:5"> His breath puts fire to coals, and a flame goes out of his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 13:14; Job 40:16; 1 Corinthians 15:55–57"> Strength is in his neck, and fear goes dancing before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> The plates of his flesh are joined together, fixed, and not to be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:3; Isaiah 48:4; Zechariah 7:12"> His heart is as strong as a stone, hard as the lower crushing-stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jonah 1:4–6; Psalms 107:28"> When he gets ready for the fight, the strong are overcome with fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 39:21–24"> The sword may come near him but is not able to go through him; the spear, or the arrow, or the sharp-pointed iron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Iron is to him as dry grass, and brass as soft wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:10; Job 39:7"> The arrow is not able to put him to flight: stones are no more to him than dry stems.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:14"> A thick stick is no better than a leaf of grass, and he makes sport of the onrush of the spear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 41:15"> Under him are sharp edges of broken pots: as if he was pulling a grain-crushing instrument over the wet earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> The deep is boiling like a pot of spices, and the sea like a perfume-vessel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 38:30; Genesis 1:2; Genesis 15:15"> After him his way is shining, so that the deep seems white.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 40:19"> On earth there is not another like him, who is made without fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:3; Psalms 73:6; Job 28:8"> Everything which is high goes in fear of him; he is king over all the sons of pride.</span>
