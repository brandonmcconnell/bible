<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Job made answer and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 10:1; Psalms 32:4; Job 7:11"> Even today my outcry is bitter; his hand is hard on my sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 16:21; Jeremiah 14:7; Job 13:3"> If only I had knowledge of where he might be seen, so that I might come even to his seat!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 13:18; Daniel 9:18–19; Exodus 32:12–13"> I would put my cause in order before him, and my mouth would be full of arguments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 13:22–23; Job 10:2; 1 Corinthians 4:3–4"> I would see what his answers would be, and have knowledge of what he would say to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 13:21; Job 9:33–34; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> Would he make use of his great power to overcome me? No, but he would give attention to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 13:3; Romans 8:1; Isaiah 1:18"> There an upright man might put his cause before him; and I would be free for ever from my judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 10:1; Job 9:11; Job 35:14"> See, I go forward, but he is not there; and back, but I do not see him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:46; Isaiah 8:17"> I am looking for him on the left hand, but there is no sign of him; and turning to the right, I am not able to see him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 1:12; James 1:2–4; 1 Peter 1:7"> For he has knowledge of the way I take; after I have been tested I will come out like gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 44:18; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Job 17:9"> My feet have gone in his steps; I have kept in his way, without turning to one side or to the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:16; Psalms 119:103; Psalms 119:11"> I have never gone against the orders of his lips; the words of his mouth have been stored up in my heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 115:3; Proverbs 19:21; Isaiah 46:10"> But his purpose is fixed and there is no changing it; and he gives effect to the desire of his soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:9; Romans 11:33"> For what has been ordered for me by him will be gone through to the end: and his mind is full of such designs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; Psalms 77:3; Job 31:23"> For this cause I am in fear before him, my thoughts of him overcome me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 22:14; Job 27:2; Psalms 88:16"> For God has made my heart feeble, and my mind is troubled before the Ruler of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 19:8; Isaiah 57:1; 2 Kings 22:20"> For I am overcome by the dark, and by the black night which is covering my face.</span>
