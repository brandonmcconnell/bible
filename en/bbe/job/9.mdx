<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Job made answer and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 4:17; Romans 3:20; Job 25:4"> Truly, I see that it is so: and how is it possible for a man to get his right before God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 10:2; Job 40:2; Isaiah 57:15–16"> If a man was desiring to go to law with him, he would not be able to give him an answer to one out of a thousand questions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 36:5; Romans 11:33; Ephesians 3:20"> He is wise in heart and great in strength: who ever made his face hard against him, and any good came of it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; Luke 21:11; Habakkuk 3:10"> It is he who takes away the mountains without their knowledge, overturning them in his wrath:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Haggai 2:6; Hebrews 12:26; Psalms 75:3"> Who is moving the earth out of its place, so that its pillars are shaking:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Amos 8:9; Luke 21:25–26"> Who gives orders to the sun, and it does not give its light; and who keeps the stars from shining.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 44:24; Isaiah 40:22; Psalms 104:2–3"> By whose hand the heavens were stretched out, and who is walking on the waves of the sea:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 5:8; Genesis 1:16; Job 38:31–41"> Who made the Bear and Orion, and the Pleiades, and the store-houses of the south:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 5:9; Psalms 72:18; Ephesians 3:20"> Who does great things not to be searched out; yes, wonders without number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 23:8–9; Job 35:14; Psalms 77:19"> See, he goes past me and I see him not: he goes on before, but I have no knowledge of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 45:9; Job 11:10; Job 23:13"> If he puts out his hand to take, by whom may it be turned back? who may say to him, What are you doing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 89:10; Job 26:12; Isaiah 30:7"> God's wrath may not be turned back; the helpers of Rahab were bent down under him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 8:27; Job 9:3; Job 4:19"> How much less may I give an answer to him, using the right words in argument with him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 10:15; Job 8:5; Job 10:2"> Even if my cause was good, I would not be able to give an answer; I would make request for grace from him who was against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 29:24; Exodus 6:9; Psalms 66:18–20"> If I had sent for him to be present, and he had come, I would have no faith that he would give ear to my voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 16:14; Job 2:3; Job 34:6"> For I would be crushed by his storm, my wounds would be increased without cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 7:19; Lamentations 3:15; Psalms 88:15–18"> He would not let me take my breath, but I would be full of bitter grief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 9:32–33; Job 9:4; 1 Corinthians 1:25"> If it is a question of strength, he says, Here I am! and if it is a question of a cause at law, he says, Who will give me a fixed day?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 15:5–6; Philippians 3:12–15; Psalms 143:2"> Though I was in the right, he would say that I was in the wrong; I have done no evil; but he says that I am a sinner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 1:1; Psalms 139:23–24; Proverbs 28:26"> I have done no wrong; I give no thought to what becomes of me; I have no desire for life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:1–3; Luke 13:2–4; Job 10:8"> It is all the same to me; so I say, He puts an end to the sinner and to him who has done no wrong together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 24:12; Job 1:13–19; Job 4:7"> If death comes suddenly through disease, he makes sport of the fate of those who have done no wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 10:3; Jeremiah 12:1–2; Psalms 73:3–7"> The land is given into the power of the evil-doer; the faces of its judges are covered; if not by him, then who has done it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 7:6–7; James 4:14; Psalms 90:9–10"> My days go quicker than a post-runner: they go in flight, they see no good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:8; Isaiah 18:2; Job 39:27–30"> They go rushing on like reed-boats, like an eagle dropping suddenly on its food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 7:13; Jeremiah 8:18; Job 7:11"> If I say, I will put my grief out of mind, I will let my face be sad no longer and I will be bright;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; Psalms 88:15–16; Job 21:6"> I go in fear of all my pains; I am certain that I will not be free from sin in your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 10:7; Job 21:27; Job 10:14–17"> You will not let me be clear of sin! why then do I take trouble for nothing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:22; Psalms 26:6; 1 John 1:8"> If I am washed with snow water, and make my hands clean with soap;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 64:6; Isaiah 59:6; Job 15:6"> Then you will have me pushed into the dust, so that I will seem disgusting to my very clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 9:20; Ecclesiastes 6:10; Psalms 143:2"> For he is not a man as I am, that I might give him an answer, that we might come together before a judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:25; 1 John 2:1–2; Job 9:19"> There is no one to give a decision between us, who might have control over us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 39:10; Job 13:11; Job 33:7"> Let him take away his rod from me and not send his fear on me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Then I would say what is in my mind without fear of him; for there is no cause of fear in myself.</span>
