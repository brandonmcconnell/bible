<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Job made answer and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3–4; Job 4:3–4; Isaiah 41:5–7"> How have you given help to him who has no power! how have you been the salvation of the arm which has no strength!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 71:15–18; Job 32:11–13; Job 33:3"> How have you given teaching to him who has no wisdom, and fully made clear true knowledge!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 22:23–24; Job 32:18; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> To whom have your words been said? and whose spirit came out from you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 88:10; Genesis 6:4; Job 41:1–34"> The shades in the underworld are shaking; the waters and those living in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 139:8; Proverbs 15:11; Hebrews 4:13"> The underworld is uncovered before him, and Destruction has no veil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 9:8; Psalms 104:2–5; Isaiah 40:22"> By his hand the north is stretched out in space, and the earth is hanging on nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 30:4; Jeremiah 10:13; Isaiah 5:6"> By him the waters are shut up in his thick clouds, and the cloud does not give way under them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; Job 22:14; Habakkuk 3:3–5"> By him the face of his high seat is veiled, and his cloud stretched out over it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 8:29; Job 38:8–11; Jeremiah 5:22"> By him a circle is marked out on the face of the waters, to the limits of the light and the dark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:8; Psalms 18:7; Job 15:15"> The pillars of heaven are shaking, and are overcome by his sharp words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 51:15; Jeremiah 31:35; Psalms 89:9–10"> By his power the sea was made quiet; and by his wisdom Rahab was wounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 27:1; Psalms 104:30; Psalms 74:13–14"> By his wind the heavens become bright: by his hand the quickly moving snake was cut through.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:26–29; Psalms 145:3; Romans 11:33"> See, these are only the outskirts of his ways; and how small is that which comes to our ears about him! But the thunder of his acts of power is outside all knowledge.</span>
