<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 15:15; Job 15:8–10; Job 4:18"> Give now a cry for help; is there anyone who will give you an answer? and to which of the holy ones will you make your prayer?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:9; Hosea 7:11; Genesis 30:1"> For wrath is the cause of death to the foolish, and he who has no wisdom comes to his end through passion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 73:18–20; Psalms 37:35–36; Psalms 92:7"> I have seen the foolish taking root, but suddenly the curse came on his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 127:5; Job 18:16–19; Psalms 119:155"> Now his children have no safe place, and they are crushed before the judges, for no one takes up their cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Lamentations 2:16; Hosea 8:7; Jeremiah 51:44"> Their produce is taken by him who has no food, and their grain goes to the poor, and he who is in need of water gets it from their spring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Lamentations 3:38; 1 Samuel 6:9"> For evil does not come out of the dust, or trouble out of the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 14:1; Genesis 3:17–19; Ecclesiastes 5:15–17"> But trouble is man's fate from birth, as the flames go up from the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Job 22:21; Psalms 37:5"> But as for me, I would make my prayer to God, and I would put my cause before him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 40:5; Job 9:10; Psalms 86:10"> Who does great things outside our knowledge, wonders without number:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:24; Acts 14:17; Psalms 147:8"> Who gives rain on the earth, and sends water on the fields:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 113:7; 1 Samuel 2:7–8; Luke 6:21"> Lifting up those who are low, and putting the sad in a safe place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 33:10–11; Psalms 21:11; Isaiah 8:10"> Who makes the designs of the wise go wrong, so that they are unable to give effect to their purposes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:19; Psalms 9:15–16; 1 Corinthians 1:19–20"> He takes the wise in their secret designs, and the purposes of the twisted are cut off suddenly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 12:25; Isaiah 59:10; Deuteronomy 28:29"> In the daytime it becomes dark for them, and in the sunlight they go feeling about as if it was night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 35:10; Psalms 140:12; Psalms 109:31"> But he keeps safe from their sword those who have no father, and the poor from the power of the strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 107:42; 1 Samuel 2:8–9; Psalms 63:11"> So the poor man has hope, and the mouth of the evil-doer is stopped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 1:12; Psalms 94:12; Proverbs 3:11–12"> Truly, that man is happy who has training from the hand of God: so do not let your heart be shut to the teaching of the Ruler of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hosea 6:1; Deuteronomy 32:39; Isaiah 30:26"> For after his punishment he gives comfort, and after wounding, his hands make you well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 34:19; 1 Corinthians 10:13; Proverbs 24:16"> He will keep you safe from six troubles, and in seven no evil will come near you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 33:19; Hosea 13:14; Psalms 37:19"> When there is need of food he will keep you from death, and in war from the power of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 31:20; Psalms 91:5–7; Jeremiah 18:18"> He will keep you safe from the evil tongue; and you will have no fear of wasting when it comes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:25; Isaiah 35:9; Psalms 91:13"> You will make sport of destruction and need, and will have no fear of the beasts of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 26:6; Hosea 2:18; Daniel 6:22"> For you will be in agreement with the stones of the earth, and the beasts of the field will be at peace with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 121:7–8; Psalms 91:10; Isaiah 4:5–6"> And you will be certain that your tent is at peace, and after looking over your property you will see that nothing is gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 112:2; Psalms 72:16; Deuteronomy 28:4"> You will be certain that your seed will be great, and your offspring like the plants of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 42:16–17; Proverbs 9:11; Psalms 91:16"> You will come to your last resting-place in full strength, as the grain is taken up to the crushing-floor in its time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 111:2; Job 32:11–12; Proverbs 9:12"> See, we have made search with care, and it is so; it has come to our ears; see that you take note of it for yourself.</span>
