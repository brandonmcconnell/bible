<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 42:7; Job 2:11; Job 22:1"> And Eliphaz the Temanite made answer and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 6:26; Job 8:2; Hosea 12:1"> Will a wise man make answer with knowledge of no value, or will he give birth to the east wind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Matthew 12:36–37; Job 13:4–5"> Will he make arguments with words in which is no profit, and with sayings which have no value?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 3:31; Luke 18:1; Amos 6:10"> Truly, you make the fear of God without effect, so that the time of quiet worship before God is made less by your outcry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:3–5; James 3:5–8; Luke 6:45"> For your mouth is guided by your sin, and you have taken the tongue of the false for yourself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 19:22; Matthew 12:37; Job 9:20"> It is by your mouth, even yours, that you are judged to be in the wrong, and not by me; and your lips give witness against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 90:2; Job 12:12; Job 38:4–41"> Were you the first man to come into the world? or did you come into being before the hills?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 11:34; Jeremiah 23:18; Job 13:5–6"> Were you present at the secret meeting of God? and have you taken all wisdom for yourself?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 13:2; 2 Corinthians 11:5; Job 12:3"> What knowledge have you which we have not? is there anything in your mind which is not in ours?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 32:6–7; Job 8:8–10; Job 12:12"> With us are men who are grey-haired and full of years, much older than your father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:2; 2 Corinthians 1:3–5; 1 Kings 22:24"> Are the comforts of God not enough for you, and the gentle word which was said to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Mark 7:21–22"> Why is your heart uncontrolled, and why are your eyes lifted up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 15:25–27; James 3:2–6; Job 9:4"> So that you are turning your spirit against God, and letting such words go out of your mouth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; Job 14:4; Proverbs 20:9"> What is man, that he may be clean? and how may the son of woman be upright?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 25:5; Job 4:18; Job 5:1"> Truly, he puts no faith in his holy ones, and the heavens are not clean in his eyes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 34:7; Proverbs 19:28; Romans 3:9–19"> How much less one who is disgusting and unclean, a man who takes in evil like water!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 36:2; Job 34:2; Job 13:5–6"> Take note and give ear to my words; and I will say what I have seen:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 8:8; Isaiah 38:19; Psalms 71:18"> (The things which wise men have got from their fathers, and have not kept secret from us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joel 3:17; Genesis 10:32; Genesis 10:25"> For only to them was the land given, and no strange people were among them:)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 27:13; James 5:1–6; Luke 12:19–21"> The evil man is in pain all his days, and the number of the years stored up for the cruel is small.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 18:11; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Leviticus 26:36"> A sound of fear is in his ears; in time of peace destruction will come on him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 20:24–25; Matthew 27:5; Job 9:16"> He has no hope of coming safe out of the dark, and his fate will be the sword;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 109:10; Psalms 59:15; Job 18:12"> He is wandering about in search of bread, saying, Where is it? and he is certain that the day of trouble is ready for him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 24:34; Proverbs 6:11; Job 6:2–4"> He is greatly in fear of the dark day, trouble and pain overcome him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 5:23; Psalms 52:7; Psalms 73:9"> Because his hand is stretched out against God, and his heart is lifted up against the Ruler of all,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 18:40; Job 16:12; 2 Chronicles 32:13–17"> Running against him like a man of war, covered by his thick breastplate; even like a king ready for the fight,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 17:10; Jeremiah 5:28; Psalms 73:7"> Because his face is covered with fat, and his body has become thick;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 3:14; Jeremiah 51:37; Micah 7:18"> And he has made his resting-place in the towns which have been pulled down, in houses where no man had a right to be, whose fate was to become masses of broken walls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 27:16–17; Psalms 49:16–17; James 1:11"> He does not get wealth for himself, and is unable to keep what he has got; the heads of his grain are not bent down to the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 4:9; Job 15:22; Job 18:5–6"> He does not come out of the dark; his branches are burned by the flame, and the wind takes away his bud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 59:4; Galatians 6:3; Psalms 62:10"> Let him not put his hope in what is false, falling into error: for he will get deceit as his reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:17; Psalms 55:23; Hosea 9:16"> His branch is cut off before its time, and his leaf is no longer green.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:39–40; Isaiah 33:9; Revelation 6:13"> He is like a vine whose grapes do not come to full growth, or an olive-tree dropping its flowers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 8:13; Job 27:8; Job 8:22"> For the band of the evil-doers gives no fruit, and the tents of those who give wrong decisions for reward are burned with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 7:14; Hosea 10:13; Isaiah 59:4–5"> Evil has made them with child, and they give birth to trouble; and the fruit of their body is shame for themselves.</span>
