<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 19:21; 2 Corinthians 4:16; Job 2:5"> But now it has come on you and it is a weariness to you; you are touched by it and your mind is troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 3:26; Proverbs 14:26; Job 1:1"> Is not your fear of God your support, and your upright way of life your hope?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 37:25; 2 Peter 2:9; Job 36:7"> Have you ever seen destruction come to an upright man? or when were the god-fearing ever cut off?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 6:7–8; Proverbs 22:8; Hosea 8:7"> What I have seen is that those by whom trouble has been ploughed, and evil planted, get the same for themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 15:30; Isaiah 11:4; Isaiah 30:33"> By the breath of God destruction takes them, and by the wind of his wrath they are cut off.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 34:10; Job 8:3–4; Job 1:19"> The old lion comes to his end for need of food, and the young of the she-lion go wandering in all directions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 26:14; Psalms 62:11; 1 Corinthians 13:12"> A word was given to me secretly, and the low sound of it came to my ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 33:14–16; Genesis 2:21; Genesis 31:24"> In troubled thoughts from visions of the night, when deep sleep comes on men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Job 33:19; Luke 1:12"> Fear came on me and shaking, and my bones were full of trouble;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 1:7; Daniel 5:6; Psalms 104:4"> And a breath was moving over my face; the hair of my flesh became stiff:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 19:12"> Something was present before me, but I was not able to see it clearly; there was a form before my eyes: a quiet voice came to my ears, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 9:2; Job 25:4; Ecclesiastes 7:20"> May a man be upright before God? or a man be clean before his Maker?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 25:5–6; 2 Peter 2:4; Jude 1:6"> Truly, he puts no faith in his servants, and he sees error in his angels;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 10:9; Genesis 2:7; Genesis 3:19"> How much more those living in houses of earth, whose bases are in the dust! They are crushed more quickly than an insect;</span>
