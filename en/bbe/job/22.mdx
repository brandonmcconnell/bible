<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Eliphaz the Temanite made answer and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 17:10; Galatians 6:7–8; Job 35:6–8"> Is it possible for a man to be of profit to God? No, for a man's wisdom is only of profit to himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 15:8; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Psalms 147:10–11"> Is it of any interest to the Ruler of all that you are upright? or is it of use to him that your ways are without sin?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 14:3; Psalms 143:2; Revelation 3:19"> Is it because you give him honour that he is sending punishment on you and is judging you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 40:12; Job 15:5–6; Job 15:31–34"> Is not your evil-doing great? and there is no end to your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 22:26; Ezekiel 18:12; Ezekiel 18:16"> For you have taken your brother's goods when he was not in your debt, and have taken away the clothing of those who have need of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 31:17; Matthew 25:42; Isaiah 58:7"> You do not give water to the tired traveller, and from him who has no food you keep back bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 9:15; Job 29:7–17; Job 13:8"> For it was the man with power who had the land, and the man with an honoured name who was living in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 24:21; Job 24:3; Ezekiel 22:7"> You have sent widows away without hearing their cause, and you have taken away the support of the child who has no father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 18:8–10; Job 13:21; Job 6:4"> For this cause nets are round your feet, and you are overcome with sudden fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 69:1–2; Lamentations 3:54; Jonah 2:3"> Your light is made dark so that you are unable to see, and you are covered by a mass of waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 11:8; Isaiah 66:1; Psalms 8:3–4"> Is not God as high as heaven? and see the stars, how high they are!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:12; Psalms 10:11; Ezekiel 9:9"> And you say, What knowledge has God? is he able to give decisions through the deep dark?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; Psalms 139:11–12; Psalms 33:14"> Thick clouds are covering him, so that he is unable to see; and he is walking on the arch of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 17:26–27; Genesis 6:5; Genesis 6:11–13"> Will you keep the old way by which evil men went?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 15:32; Genesis 7:17–24; Psalms 55:23"> Who were violently taken away before their time, who were overcome by the rush of waters:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 21:14–15; Romans 1:28; Psalms 4:6"> Who said to God, Go away from us; and, What is the Ruler of all able to do to us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 21:16; Job 12:6; Acts 14:17"> Though he made their houses full of good things: but the purpose of the evil-doers is far from me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 58:10; Psalms 107:42; Psalms 52:6"> The upright saw it and were glad: and those who had done no wrong made sport of them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 2:6–7; Job 1:16; Job 20:26"> Saying, Truly, their substance is cut off, and their wealth is food for the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:9; Isaiah 27:5; Philippians 4:7"> Put yourself now in a right relation with him and be at peace: so will you do well in your undertakings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; Job 23:12; Deuteronomy 4:1–2"> Be pleased to take teaching from his mouth, and let his words be stored up in your heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 11:13–14; Zechariah 1:3; Job 8:5–6"> If you come back to the Ruler of all, making yourself low before him; if you put evil far away from your tents;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 13:12; 1 Kings 9:28; Genesis 10:29"> And put your gold in the dust, even your gold of Ophir among the rocks of the valleys;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 33:6; Psalms 16:5–6; Genesis 15:1"> Then the Ruler of all will be your gold, and his teaching will be your silver;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; Isaiah 58:14; Job 27:10"> For then you will have delight in the Ruler of all, and your face will be lifted up to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 58:9; Psalms 116:1; 1 John 5:14–15"> You will make your prayer to him, and be answered; and you will give effect to your oaths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; Psalms 90:17; Psalms 20:4"> Your purposes will come about, and light will be shining on your ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="James 4:6; Job 5:19–27; Luke 1:52"> For God makes low those whose hearts are lifted up, but he is a saviour to the poor in spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 42:7–8; James 5:15–16; Genesis 18:26–32"> He makes safe the man who is free from sin, and if your hands are clean, salvation will be yours.</span>
