<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 4:1"> And Job made answer and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 31:6; Job 23:2; Job 4:5"> If only my passion might be measured, and put into the scales against my trouble!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 27:3; Job 23:2; Psalms 40:5"> For then its weight would be more than the sand of the seas: because of this my words have been uncontrolled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 38:2; Job 30:15; Psalms 88:15–16"> For the arrows of the Ruler of all are present with me, and their poison goes deep into my spirit: his army of fears is put in order against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joel 1:18–20; Jeremiah 14:6; Psalms 42:1"> Does the ass of the fields give out his voice when he has grass? or does the ox make sounds over his food?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 34:3; Luke 14:34; Colossians 4:6"> Will a man take food which has no taste without salt? or is there any taste in the soft substance of purslain?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 3:24; Ezekiel 4:16; Daniel 10:3"> My soul has no desire for such things, they are as disease in my food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 119:81; Job 17:14–16; Job 6:11–13"> If only I might have an answer to my prayer, and God would give me my desire!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 19:4; Jonah 4:3; Jonah 4:8"> If only he would be pleased to put an end to me; and would let loose his hand, so that I might be cut off!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 23:12; Leviticus 19:2; Isaiah 57:15"> So I would still have comfort, and I would have joy in the pains of death, for I have not been false to the words of the Holy One.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 103:14–16; Psalms 102:23; Psalms 39:5"> Have I strength to go on waiting, or have I any end to be looking forward to?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 41:24; Job 40:18"> Is my strength the strength of stones, or is my flesh brass?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 26:2; 2 Corinthians 1:12; Job 19:28"> I have no help in myself, and wisdom is completely gone from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 17:17; 1 Corinthians 12:26; Galatians 6:2"> He whose heart is shut against his friend has given up the fear of the Ruler of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 38:11; Jeremiah 15:18; Psalms 41:9"> My friends have been false like a stream, like streams in the valleys which come to an end:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Which are dark because of the ice, and the snow falling into them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; Job 24:19"> Under the burning sun they are cut off, and come to nothing because of the heat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The camel-trains go out of their way; they go up into the waste and come to destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 21:14; Genesis 25:15; Jeremiah 25:23"> The camel-trains of Tema were searching with care, the bands of Sheba were waiting for them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:3–4; Romans 5:5; Romans 9:33"> They were put to shame because of their hope; they came and their hope was gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 38:11; 2 Timothy 4:16; Jeremiah 17:5–6"> So have you now become to me; you see my sad condition and are in fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 20:33; Job 42:11; 1 Samuel 12:3"> Did I say, Give me something? or, Make a payment for me out of your wealth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 49:15; Psalms 49:7–8; Psalms 107:2"> Or, Get me out of the power of my hater? or, Give money so that I may be free from the power of the cruel ones?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="James 3:2; Proverbs 9:9; Psalms 39:1–2"> Give me teaching and I will be quiet; and make me see my error.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 32:3; Job 13:5; Proverbs 25:11"> How pleasing are upright words! but what force is there in your arguments?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 8:2; Ephesians 4:14; Job 42:3"> My words may seem wrong to you, but the words of him who has no hope are for the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 24:9; Job 24:3; 2 Peter 2:3"> Truly, you are such as would give up the child of a dead man to his creditors, and would make a profit out of your friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 13:4; Job 33:3; Job 27:4"> Now then, let your eyes be turned to me, for truly I will not say what is false to your face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 17:10; Job 34:5; Job 27:4–6"> Let your minds be changed, and do not have an evil opinion of me; yes, be changed, for my righteousness is still in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 12:11; Job 6:6; Job 34:3"> Is there evil in my tongue? is not the cause of my trouble clear to me?</span>
