<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Job made answer and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 13:4–5; Psalms 69:26; Job 11:2–3"> Such things have frequently come to my ears: you are comforters who only give trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 6:26; Job 15:2; Titus 2:8"> May words which are like the wind be stopped? or what is troubling you to make answer to them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 109:25; Psalms 22:7; Lamentations 2:15"> It would not be hard for me to say such things if your souls were in my soul's place; joining words together against you, and shaking my head at you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 27:9; Galatians 6:1; Isaiah 35:3–4"> I might give you strength with my mouth, and not keep back the comfort of my lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 10:1; Psalms 88:15–18; Psalms 77:1–9"> If I say what is in my mind, my pain becomes no less: and if I keep quiet, how much of it goes from me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 7:3; Job 1:15–19; Psalms 6:6–7"> But now he has overcome me with weariness and fear, and I am in the grip of all my trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 10:17; Job 19:20; Ruth 1:21"> It has come up as a witness against me, and the wasting of my flesh makes answer to my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 35:16; Job 13:24; Lamentations 2:16"> I am broken by his wrath, and his hate has gone after me; he has made his teeth sharp against me: my haters are looking on me with cruel eyes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 35:15; Psalms 22:13; Isaiah 50:6"> Their mouths are open wide against me; the blows of his bitter words are falling on my face; all of them come together in a mass against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 27:12; Psalms 7:14; Psalms 31:8"> God gives me over to the power of sinners, sending me violently into the hands of evil-doers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 7:20; Lamentations 3:11–12; Lamentations 3:4"> I was in comfort, but I have been broken up by his hands; he has taken me by the neck, shaking me to bits; he has put me up as a mark for his arrows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 20:25; Job 6:4; Lamentations 2:11"> His bowmen come round about me; their arrows go through my body without mercy; my life is drained out on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 15:8; Lamentations 3:3–5; Joel 2:7"> I am broken with wound after wound; he comes rushing on me like a man of war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 37:34; Psalms 7:5; Psalms 75:5"> I have made haircloth the clothing of my skin, and my horn is rolled in the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 14:34; Psalms 102:3–5; Lamentations 1:16"> My face is red with weeping, and my eyes are becoming dark;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 15:8; Psalms 66:18–19; Job 27:6–7"> Though my hands have done no violent acts, and my prayer is clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 26:21; Ezekiel 24:7; Genesis 4:11"> O earth, let not my blood be covered, and let my cry have no resting-place!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 1:9; 1 Thessalonians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 11:31"> Even now my witness is in heaven, and the supporter of my cause is on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Psalms 142:2; Job 16:4"> My friends make sport of me; to God my eyes are weeping,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 6:10; Isaiah 45:9; Romans 9:20"> So that he may give decision for a man in his cause with God, and between a son of man and his neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 14:10; Job 14:14; Job 14:5"> For in a short time I will take the journey from which I will not come back.</span>
