<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Elihu made answer and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 12:36–37; Job 19:6–7; Job 16:17"> Does it seem to you to be right, and righteousness before God, to say,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 34:9; Malachi 3:14; Job 9:30–31"> What profit is it to me, and how am I better off than if I had done wrong?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; Job 34:8"> I will make answer to you and to your friends:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 22:12; Isaiah 55:9; Nahum 1:3"> Let your eyes be turned to the heavens, and lifted up to see the skies; they are higher than you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:19; Proverbs 8:36; Proverbs 9:12"> If you have done wrong, is he any the worse for it? and if your sins are great in number, what is it to him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 22:2–3; Psalms 16:2; Luke 17:10"> If you are upright, what do you give to him? or what does he take from your hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:30; Job 42:8; Acts 27:24"> Your evil-doing may have an effect on a man like yourself, or your righteousness on a son of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 2:23; Job 34:28; Exodus 3:7"> Because the hand of the cruel is hard on them, men are making sounds of grief; they are crying out for help because of the arm of the strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 42:8; Acts 16:25; Psalms 149:5"> But no one has said, Where is God my Maker, who gives songs in the night;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 94:12; Job 36:22; Job 32:8"> Who gives us more knowledge than the beasts of the earth, and makes us wiser than the birds of the heaven?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 1:28; John 9:31; Psalms 123:3–4"> There they are crying out because of the pride of the evil-doers, but he gives them no answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 15:29; Isaiah 1:15; James 4:3"> But God will not give ear to what is false, or the Ruler of all take note of it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 9:11; Micah 7:7–9; Psalms 37:5–6"> How much less when you say that you do not see him; that the cause is before him, and you are waiting for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 89:32; Job 9:14; Job 13:15"> And now ... ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 38:2; Job 33:8–12; Job 34:35–37"> And Job's mouth is open wide to give out what is of no profit, increasing words without knowledge.</span>
