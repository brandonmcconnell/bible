<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 27:1; Job 13:12; Numbers 23:7"> And Job again took up the word and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 1:10; Job 1:1–5; Jeremiah 31:28"> If only I might again be as I was in the months which are past, in the days when God was watching over me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 18:28; John 8:12; Job 18:6"> When his light was shining over my head, and when I went through the dark by his light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 25:14; Proverbs 3:32; Psalms 27:5"> As I was in my flowering years, when my tent was covered by the hand of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 128:3; Psalms 127:3–5; Deuteronomy 33:27–29"> While the Ruler of all was still with me, and my children were round me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 81:16; Job 20:17; Deuteronomy 32:13–14"> When my steps were washed with milk, and rivers of oil were flowing out of the rock for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 8:16; Ruth 4:1–2; Deuteronomy 16:18"> When I went out of my door to go up to the town, and took my seat in the public place,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 19:32; Proverbs 16:31; Romans 13:7"> The young men saw me, and went away, and the old men got up from their seats;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 21:5; Job 40:4; Proverbs 10:19"> The rulers kept quiet, and put their hands on their mouths;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 137:6; Ezekiel 3:26"> The chiefs kept back their words, and their tongues were joined to the roofs of their mouths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 4:22; Job 31:20; Proverbs 29:2"> For when it came to their ears, men said that I was truly happy; and when their eyes saw, they gave witness to me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 72:12; Proverbs 21:13; James 1:27"> For I was a saviour to the poor when he was crying for help, to the child with no father, and to him who had no supporter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philemon 1:7; Job 22:9; Job 31:19–20"> The blessing of him who was near to destruction came on me, and I put a song of joy into the widow's heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; Isaiah 59:17; Psalms 132:9"> I put on righteousness as my clothing, and was full of it; right decisions were to me a robe and a head-dress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 10:31; Matthew 11:5; 1 Corinthians 12:12–31"> I was eyes to the blind, and feet to him who had no power of walking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 29:7; Psalms 68:5; Job 31:18"> I was a father to the poor, searching out the cause of him who was strange to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 3:7; Proverbs 30:14; 1 Samuel 17:35"> By me the great teeth of the evil-doer were broken, and I made him give up what he had violently taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 30:6–7; Genesis 32:12; Job 42:16–17"> Then I said, I will come to my end with my children round me, my days will be as the sand in number;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 1:3; Jeremiah 17:8; Hosea 14:5–7"> My root will be open to the waters, and the night mist will be on my branches,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 49:24; Isaiah 40:31; Psalms 18:34"> My glory will be ever new, and my bow will be readily bent in my hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 32:11–12; Job 29:9–10"> Men gave ear to me, waiting and keeping quiet for my suggestions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:2; Song of Solomon 4:11; Matthew 22:46"> After I had said what was in my mind, they were quiet and let my words go deep into their hearts;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hosea 6:3; Psalms 72:6; Zechariah 10:1"> They were waiting for me as for the rain, opening their mouths wide as for the spring rains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 89:15; Psalms 4:6; Psalms 126:1"> I was laughing at them when they had no hope, and the light of my face was never clouded by their fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:3–4; 1 Thessalonians 3:2–3; Genesis 14:14–17"> I took my place as a chief, guiding them on their way, and I was as a king among his army. ...</span>
