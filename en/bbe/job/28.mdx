<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:13; Habakkuk 2:13; Matthew 6:33"> Man puts an end to the dark, searching out to the farthest limit the stones of the deep places of the dark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> He makes a deep mine far away from those living in the light of day; when they go about on the earth, they have no knowledge of those who are under them, who are hanging far from men, twisting from side to side on a cord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 28:25–29; Psalms 104:14–15; Genesis 1:11–12"> As for the earth, bread comes out of it; but under its face it is turned up as if by fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 28:16; Exodus 24:10; Isaiah 54:11"> Its stones are the place of sapphires, and it has dust of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 11:6; Job 28:21–23; Job 38:24"> No bird has knowledge of it, and the hawk's eye has never seen it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The great beasts have not gone over it, and the cruel lion has not taken that way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nahum 1:4–6"> Man puts out his hand on the hard rock, overturning mountains by the roots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:9; Proverbs 14:23; Proverbs 24:4"> He makes deep ways, cut through the rock, and his eye sees everything of value.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:5; Isaiah 45:2–3; Isaiah 44:27"> He keeps back the streams from flowing, and makes the secret things come out into the light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 2:4–6; Job 28:20; James 1:5"> But where may wisdom be seen? and where is the resting-place of knowledge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 8:11; Proverbs 8:18–19; Proverbs 3:14–15"> Man has not seen the way to it, and it is not in the land of the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 11:33–34"> The deep waters say, It is not in me: and the sea says, It is not with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 16:16; Proverbs 8:10–11; Proverbs 8:19"> Gold may not be given for it, or a weight of silver in payment for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 13:12; Ezekiel 28:13; Psalms 45:9"> It may not be valued with the gold of Ophir, with the onyx of great price, or the sapphire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; Revelation 4:6; Revelation 21:11"> Gold and glass are not equal to it in price, and it may not be exchanged for jewels of the best gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:15; Proverbs 31:10; Lamentations 4:7"> There is no need to say anything about coral or crystal; and the value of wisdom is greater than that of pearls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 8:19; Exodus 39:10; Ezekiel 28:13"> The topaz of Ethiopia is not equal to it, and it may not be valued with the best gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 28:12; James 1:5; James 1:17"> From where then does wisdom come, and where is the resting-place of knowledge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 49:3–4; 1 Corinthians 2:7–10; Matthew 11:25"> For it is kept secret from the eyes of all living, unseen by the birds of the air.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 83:10–12; Job 26:6; Job 28:14"> Destruction and Death say, We have only had word of it with our ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 8:22–31; 1 Corinthians 1:30; Jude 1:25"> God has knowledge of the way to it, and of its resting-place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 15:3; Zechariah 4:10; 2 Chronicles 16:9"> For his eyes go to the ends of the earth, and he sees everything under heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 135:7; Isaiah 40:12; Job 12:15"> When he made a weight for the wind, measuring out the waters;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 38:25; Job 37:3; Zechariah 10:1"> When he made a law for the rain, and a way for the thunder-flames;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 19:1; Proverbs 8:22–29"> Then he saw it, and put it on record; he gave it its fixed form, searching it out completely.</span>
