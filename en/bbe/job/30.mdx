<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 12:4; Psalms 35:15–16; Psalms 69:12"> But now those who are younger than I make sport of me; those whose fathers I would not have put with the dogs of my flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Of what use is the strength of their hands to me? all force is gone from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 24:5; Hebrews 11:38; Job 24:13–16"> They are wasted for need of food, biting the dry earth; their only hope of life is in the waste land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 15:16; 2 Kings 4:38–39; Amos 7:14"> They are pulling off the salt leaves from the brushwood, and making a meal of roots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 4:25; Daniel 4:32–33; Psalms 109:10"> They are sent out from among their townsmen, men are crying after them as thieves</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:1–2; Judges 6:2; Isaiah 2:19"> They have to get a resting-place in the hollows of the valleys, in holes of the earth and rocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 6:5; Job 11:12; Genesis 16:12"> They make noises like asses among the brushwood; they get together under the thorns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 16:22; Job 40:4; Isaiah 32:6"> They are sons of shame, and of men without a name, who have been forced out of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Lamentations 3:63; Job 17:6; Lamentations 3:14"> And now I have become their song, and I am a word of shame to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 26:67; Isaiah 50:6; Numbers 12:14"> I am disgusting to them; they keep away from me, and put marks of shame on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 12:21; Ruth 1:21; Job 12:18"> For he has made loose the cord of my bow, and put me to shame; he has sent down my flag to the earth before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 19:12; Isaiah 3:5; Job 19:18"> The lines of his men of war put themselves in order, and make high their ways of destruction against me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 3:12; Zechariah 1:15; Psalms 69:26"> They have made waste my roads, with a view to my destruction; his bowmen come round about me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:16; Psalms 18:4; Psalms 69:14–15"> As through a wide broken place in the wall they come on, I am overturned by the shock of their attack.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 13:3; Job 6:4; Isaiah 44:22"> Fears have come on me; my hope is gone like the wind, and my well-being like a cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 42:4; Psalms 22:14; Isaiah 53:12"> But now my soul is turned to water in me, days of trouble overtake me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 7:4; Job 33:19–21; Isaiah 38:13"> The flesh is gone from my bones, and they give me no rest; there is no end to my pains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 7:5; Job 19:20; Job 2:7"> With great force he takes a grip of my clothing, pulling me by the neck of my coat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 9:31; Job 42:6; Genesis 18:27"> Truly God has made me low, even to the earth, and I have become like dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 19:7; Psalms 80:4–5; Psalms 22:2"> You give no answer to my cry, and take no note of my prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 10:14–17; Job 16:9–14; Job 19:6–9"> You have become cruel to me; the strength of your hand is hard on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 27:21; Psalms 1:4; Hosea 4:19"> Lifting me up, you make me go on the wings of the wind; I am broken up by the storm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:5–7; Hebrews 9:27; Ecclesiastes 9:5"> For I am certain that you will send me back to death, and to the meeting-place ordered for all living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Judges 5:31; Matthew 27:39–44; Job 19:7"> Has not my hand been stretched out in help to the poor? have I not been a saviour to him in his trouble?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Psalms 35:13–14; Jeremiah 13:17"> Have I not been weeping for the crushed? and was not my soul sad for him who was in need?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:15; Job 3:25–26; Jeremiah 14:19"> For I was looking for good, and evil came; I was waiting for light, and it became dark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Lamentations 2:11; Jeremiah 31:20; Jeremiah 4:19"> My feelings are strongly moved, and give me no rest; days of trouble have overtaken me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 42:9; Psalms 38:6; Psalms 43:2"> I go about in dark clothing, uncomforted; I get up in the public place, crying out for help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Micah 1:8; Psalms 102:6; Psalms 44:19"> I have become a brother to the jackals, and go about in the company of ostriches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Lamentations 4:8; Psalms 102:3; Psalms 119:83"> My skin is black and dropping off me; and my bones are burning with the heat of my disease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Lamentations 5:15; Isaiah 21:4; Isaiah 22:12"> And my music has been turned to sorrow, and the sound of my pipe into the noise of weeping.</span>
