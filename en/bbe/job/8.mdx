<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; 2 Chronicles 19:7; Genesis 18:25"> Does God give wrong decisions? or is the Ruler of all not upright in his judging?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 1:5; Job 1:18–19; Job 5:4"> If your children have done evil against him, then their punishment is from his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 4:7–10; Hebrews 3:7–8; Job 11:13"> If you will make search for God with care, and put your request before the Ruler of all;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 51:9; Isaiah 3:10; Proverbs 15:8"> If you are clean and upright; then he will certainly be moved to take up your cause, and will make clear your righteousness by building up your house again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 42:12–13; Matthew 13:31–32; Proverbs 19:20"> And though your start was small, your end will be very great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:7; Deuteronomy 4:32; Job 15:18"> Put the question now to the past generations, and give attention to what has been searched out by their fathers:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:15; Psalms 144:4; Job 14:2"> (For we are but of yesterday, and have no knowledge, because our days on earth are gone like a shade:)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 18:15; Matthew 12:35; Job 12:7–8"> Will they not give you teaching, and say words of wisdom to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 2:3; Isaiah 19:5–7"> Will the river-plant come up in its pride without wet earth? will the grass get tall without water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:6; Psalms 129:6–7; Matthew 13:20"> When it is still green, without being cut down, it becomes dry and dead before any other plant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 9:17; Job 11:20; Job 13:16"> So is the end of all who do not keep God in mind; and the hope of the evil-doer comes to nothing:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 59:5–6"> Whose support is cut off, and whose hope is no stronger than a spider's thread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 27:18; Luke 6:47–49; Matthew 7:24–27"> He is looking to his family for support, but it is not there; he puts his hope in it, but it comes to nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 80:11; Job 21:7–15; Job 5:3"> He is full of strength before the sun, and his branches go out over his garden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 5:24; Mark 11:20; Jude 1:12"> His roots are twisted round the stones, forcing their way in between them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 7:10; Psalms 37:36; Job 7:8"> If he is taken away from his place, then it will say, I have not seen you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 20:5; Psalms 113:7; 1 Samuel 2:8"> Such is the joy of his way, and out of the dust another comes up to take his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 4:7; Psalms 37:37; Psalms 37:24"> Truly, God will not give up him who is without sin, and will not take evil-doers by the hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 126:2; Genesis 21:6; Psalms 126:6"> The time will come when your mouth will be full of laughing, and cries of joy will come from your lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 132:18; Psalms 109:29; Psalms 35:26"> Your haters will be clothed with shame, and the tent of the sinner will not be seen again.</span>
