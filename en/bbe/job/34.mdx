<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Elihu made answer and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 1:5; 1 Corinthians 14:20; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> Give ear, you wise, to my words; and you who have knowledge, give attention to me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 12:11; 1 Corinthians 2:15; Job 33:2"> For words are tested by the ear, as food is tasted by the mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:21; Judges 19:30; Romans 12:2"> Let us make the decision for ourselves as to what is right; let us have the knowledge among ourselves of what is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 27:2; Job 33:9; Job 16:17"> For Job has said, I am upright, and it is God who has taken away my right;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 6:4; Job 27:4–6; Job 16:13"> Though I am right, still I am in pain; my wound may not be made well, though I have done no wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 15:16; Deuteronomy 29:19; Proverbs 1:22"> What man is like Job, a man who freely makes sport of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 1:1; 1 Corinthians 15:33; Proverbs 13:20"> And goes in the company of evil-doers, walking in the way of sinners?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 21:14–16; Job 35:3; Malachi 3:14"> For he has said, It is no profit to a man to take delight in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 9:14; Deuteronomy 32:4; Job 8:3"> Now then, you wise, take note; you men of knowledge, give ear to me. Let it be far from God to do evil, and from the Ruler of all to do wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Romans 2:6; Revelation 22:12"> For he gives to every man the reward of his work, and sees that he gets the fruit of his ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 8:3; Psalms 11:7; Psalms 145:17"> Truly, God does not do evil, and the Ruler of all is not a false judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:11; Isaiah 40:13–14; Romans 11:34–36"> Who put the earth into his care, or made him responsible for the world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 104:29; Job 7:17; Job 9:4"> If he made his spirit come back to him, taking his breath into himself again,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Isaiah 57:16"> All flesh would come to an end together, and man would go back to the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 13:2–6; Job 12:3"> If you are wise, take note of this; give ear to the voice of my words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 40:8; Genesis 18:25; 2 Samuel 23:3"> How may a hater of right be a ruler? and will you say that the upright Ruler of all is evil?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 22:28; Proverbs 17:26; Romans 13:7"> He who says to a king, You are an evil-doer; and to rulers, You are sinners;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:17; Acts 10:34; James 2:5"> Who has no respect for rulers, and who gives no more attention to those who have wealth than to the poor, for they are all the work of his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 73:19; 1 Samuel 25:37–39; 1 Samuel 26:10"> Suddenly they come to an end, even in the middle of the night: the blow comes on the men of wealth, and they are gone, and the strong are taken away without the hand of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 15:3; Job 31:4; Jeremiah 16:17"> For his eyes are on the ways of a man, and he sees all his steps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Amos 9:2–3; Proverbs 10:29"> There is no dark place, and no thick cloud, in which the workers of evil may take cover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezra 9:13; Psalms 119:137; Romans 9:20"> For he does not give man a fixed time to come before him to be judged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 113:7–8; Psalms 94:5; Daniel 2:21"> He sends the strong to destruction without searching out their cause, and puts others in their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hosea 7:2; Job 34:20; Amos 8:7"> For he has knowledge of their works, overturning them in the night, so that they are crushed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 58:10–11; Deuteronomy 21:21; Isaiah 66:24"> The evil-doers are broken by his wrath, he puts his hand on them with force before the eyes of all onlookers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 28:5; 1 Samuel 15:11; Isaiah 5:12"> Because they did not go after him, and took no note of his ways,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="James 5:4; Exodus 3:7; Job 35:9"> So that the cry of the poor might come up to him, and the prayer of those in need come to his ears.</span>
