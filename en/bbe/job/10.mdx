<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 139:23–24; Job 9:29; Psalms 143:2"> I will say to God, Do not put me down as a sinner; make clear to me what you have against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; Psalms 138:8; Job 14:15"> What profit is it to you to be cruel, to give up the work of your hands, looking kindly on the design of evil-doers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:7; Luke 16:15; Job 9:32"> Have you eyes of flesh, or do you see as man sees?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Peter 3:8; Psalms 90:2–4; Psalms 102:24–27"> Are your days as the days of man, or your years like his,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 14:16; Psalms 10:15; Zephaniah 1:12"> That you take note of my sin, searching after my wrongdoing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Daniel 3:15; Psalms 139:1–2"> Though you see that I am not an evil-doer; and there is no one who is able to take a man out of your hands?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 119:73; Isaiah 43:7; Job 10:3"> Your hands made me, and I was formed by you, but then, changing your purpose, you gave me up to destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 2:7; Isaiah 64:8; Genesis 3:19"> O keep in mind that you made me out of earth; and will you send me back again to dust?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 139:14–16"> Was I not drained out like milk, becoming hard like cheese?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:4–8; Ephesians 4:16; Job 40:17–18"> By you I was clothed with skin and flesh, and joined together with bones and muscles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 17:28; Job 33:4; Acts 17:25"> You have been kind to me, and your grace has been with me, and your care has kept my spirit safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 23:13; Deuteronomy 32:39; Isaiah 45:15"> But you kept these things in the secret of your heart; I am certain this was in your thoughts:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 7:21; Psalms 130:3; Exodus 34:7"> That, if I did wrong, you would take note of it, and would not make me clear from sin:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 3:11; Job 9:15; Job 9:20–21"> That, if I was an evil-doer, the curse would come on me; and if I was upright, my head would not be lifted up, being full of shame and overcome with trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 38:13; Lamentations 3:10; Job 5:9"> And that if there was cause for pride, you would go after me like a lion; and again put out your wonders against me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ruth 1:21; Job 16:8; Job 16:11–16"> That you would send new witnesses against me, increasing your wrath against me, and letting loose new armies on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:14–18; Jeremiah 15:10; Job 11:20"> Why then did you make me come out of my mother's body? It would have been better for me to have taken my last breath, and for no eye to have seen me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 58:8"> And for me to have been as if I had not been; to have been taken from my mother's body straight to my last resting-place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 14:1; Psalms 39:13; Psalms 103:15–16"> Are not the days of my life small in number? Let your eyes be turned away from me, so that I may have a little pleasure,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 3:5; 2 Samuel 12:23; Job 16:22"> Before I go to the place from which I will not come back, to the land where all is dark and black,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 88:12; Jeremiah 2:6; Jeremiah 13:16"> A land of thick dark, without order, where the very light is dark.</span>
