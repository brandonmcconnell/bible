<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 20:1"> Then Zophar the Naamathite made answer and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 10:19; James 1:19; Job 18:2"> Are all these words to go unanswered? and is a man seen to be right because he is full of talk?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 17:2; Job 24:25; Titus 2:8"> Are your words of pride to make men keep quiet? and are you to make sport, with no one to put you to shame?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 10:7; Job 6:10; Job 35:2"> You may say, My way is clean, and I am free from sin in your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 38:1–2; Job 31:35; Job 33:6–18"> But if only God would take up the word, opening his lips in argument with you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra 9:13; Lamentations 3:22; Daniel 2:28"> And would make clear to you the secrets of wisdom, and the wonders of his purpose!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Ecclesiastes 3:11; Psalms 145:3"> Are you able to take God's measure, to make discovery of the limits of the Ruler of all?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 22:12; Psalms 139:6–8; Isaiah 55:9"> They are higher than heaven; what is there for you to do? deeper than the underworld, and outside your knowledge;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 139:9–10; Job 28:24–25; Psalms 65:5–8"> Longer in measure than the earth, and wider than the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:12–13; Job 12:14; Revelation 3:7"> If he goes on his way, shutting a man up and putting him to death, who may make him go back from his purpose?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 10:14; Hebrews 4:13; Revelation 2:23"> For in his eyes men are as nothing; he sees evil and takes note of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 2:20; Ecclesiastes 3:18; James 3:13–17"> And so a hollow-minded man will get wisdom, when a young ass of the field gets teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 143:6; Psalms 88:9; 1 Samuel 7:3"> But if you put your heart right, stretching out your hands to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:23; James 4:8; Job 4:7"> If you put far away the evil of your hands, and let no wrongdoing have a place in your tent;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 22:26; 1 John 2:28; 1 John 3:19–22"> Then truly your face will be lifted up, with no mark of sin, and you will be fixed in your place without fear:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 65:16; Ecclesiastes 5:20; John 16:21"> For your sorrow will go from your memory, like waters flowing away:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 58:8–10; Psalms 37:6; Proverbs 4:18"> And your life will be brighter than day; though it is dark, it will become like the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:24–26; Psalms 43:5; Psalms 3:5"> And you will be safe because there is hope; after looking round, you will take your rest in quiet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 45:12; Isaiah 60:14; Job 42:8–9"> Sleeping with no fear of danger; and men will be desiring to have grace in your eyes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:65; Job 31:16; Job 17:5"> But the eyes of the evil-doers will be wasting away; their way of flight is gone, and their only hope is the taking of their last breath.</span>
