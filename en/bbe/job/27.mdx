<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 34:5; Numbers 14:21; 2 Kings 4:27"> By the life of God, who has taken away my right; and of the Ruler of all, who has made my soul bitter;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 2:7; Job 33:4; Job 32:8"> (For all my breath is still in me, and the spirit of God is my life;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 13:7; 2 Corinthians 11:10; John 8:55"> Truly, there is no deceit in my lips, and my tongue does not say what is false.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 2:9; Job 13:15; Job 42:7"> Let it be far from me! I will certainly not say that you are right! I will come to death before I give up my righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 2:3; 1 John 3:20–21; Acts 24:16"> I will keep it safe, and will not let it go: my heart has nothing to say against any part of my life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 4:19; 1 Samuel 25:26; 2 Samuel 18:32"> Let my hater be like the evil man, and let him who comes against me be as the sinner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; Job 8:13; Job 11:20"> For what is the hope of the sinner when he is cut off, when God takes back his soul?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 1:15; Proverbs 1:28; Jeremiah 14:12"> Will his cry come to the ears of God when he is in trouble?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 22:26–27; Psalms 37:4; Habakkuk 3:18"> Will he take delight in the Ruler of all, and make his prayer to God at all times?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 71:17; Isaiah 8:11; Job 4:3–4"> I will give you teaching about the hand of God; I will not keep secret from you what is in the mind of the Ruler of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 21:3; Job 16:3; Job 19:2–3"> Truly, you have all seen it yourselves; why then have you become completely foolish?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 20:19–29; Job 15:20–35; Isaiah 3:11"> This is the punishment of the evil-doer from God, and the heritage given to the cruel by the Ruler of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:41; Job 20:10; Luke 23:29"> If his children are increased, it is for the sword; and his offspring have not enough bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 78:64; Jeremiah 22:18; 1 Kings 14:10–11"> When those of his house who are still living come to their end by disease, they are not put into the earth, and their widows are not weeping for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 9:3; Matthew 6:19; Habakkuk 2:6"> Though he may get silver together like dust, and make ready great stores of clothing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 13:22; Ecclesiastes 2:26; Proverbs 28:8"> He may get them ready, but the upright will put them on, and he who is free from sin will take the silver for a heritage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 1:8; Job 8:14–15; Isaiah 51:8"> His house has no more strength than a spider's thread, or a watchman's tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 24:24; Psalms 58:9; Psalms 73:19–20"> He goes to rest full of wealth, but does so for the last time: on opening his eyes, he sees it there no longer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 15:21; Job 18:11; Job 21:18"> Fears overtake him like rushing waters; in the night the storm-wind takes him away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 11:6; Psalms 83:15; Psalms 58:9"> The east wind takes him up and he is gone; he is forced violently out of his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4–5; Ezekiel 24:14; Job 11:20"> God sends his arrows against him without mercy; he goes in flight before his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Lamentations 2:15; 1 Kings 9:8; Zephaniah 2:15"> Men make signs of joy because of him, driving him from his place with sounds of hissing.</span>
