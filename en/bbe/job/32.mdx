<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 10:7; Job 33:9; Job 10:2"> So these three men gave no more answers to Job, because he seemed to himself to be right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 22:21; Job 40:8; Job 27:2"> And Elihu, the son of Barachel the Buzite, of the family of Ram, was angry, burning with wrath against Job, because he seemed to himself more right than God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 8:6; Job 24:25; Job 22:5–30"> And he was angry with his three friends, because they had been unable to give him an answer, and had not made Job's sin clear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 32:11–12; Proverbs 18:13"> Now Elihu had kept quiet while Job was talking, because they were older than he;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 32:2; Exodus 32:19"> And when Elihu saw that there was no answer in the mouth of the three men, he was very angry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 15:10; 1 Timothy 5:1; Titus 2:6"> And Elihu, the son of Barachel the Buzite, made answer and said, I am young, and you are very old, so I was in fear, and kept myself from putting my knowledge before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 16:31; Job 12:12; Hebrews 5:12"> I said to myself, It is right for the old to say what is in their minds, and for those who are far on in years to give out wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 38:36; Job 33:4; 2 Timothy 3:16"> But truly it is the spirit in man, even the breath of the Ruler of all, which gives them knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 11:25; Job 12:20; Ecclesiastes 4:13"> It is not the old who are wise, and those who are full of years have not the knowledge of what is right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:25; 1 Corinthians 7:40"> So I say, Give ear to me, and I will put forward my knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 29:21; Job 5:27; Proverbs 28:11"> I was waiting for your words, I was giving ear to your wise sayings; while you were searching out what to say,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 32:3; 1 Timothy 1:7"> I was taking note; and truly not one of you was able to make clear Job's error, or to give an answer to his words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23; 1 Corinthians 1:27–29; 1 Corinthians 3:18"> Take care that you do not say, Wisdom is here; God may overcome him, but not man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> I will not put forward words like these, or make use of your sayings in answer to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 6:24–25; Matthew 22:46; Job 29:22"> Fear has overcome them, they have no more answers to give; they have come to an end of words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 1:19; Job 13:5; Amos 5:13"> And am I to go on waiting while they have nothing to say? while they keep quiet and give no more answers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 35:3–4; Job 33:12; Job 32:10"> I will give my answer; I will put forward my knowledge:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 39:3; Acts 4:20; 2 Corinthians 5:13–14"> For I am full of words, I am unable to keep in my breath any longer:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 9:17"> My stomach is like wine which is unable to get out; like skins full of new wine, it is almost burst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 8:6–7; Job 21:3; Job 13:13"> Let me say what is in my mind, so that I may get comfort; let me give answer with open mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 19:15; Job 13:8; Matthew 22:16"> Let me not give respect to any man, or give names of honour to any living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 12:2–3; 1 Thessalonians 2:5; Proverbs 29:5"> For I am not able to give names of honour to any man; and if I did, my Maker would quickly take me away.</span>
