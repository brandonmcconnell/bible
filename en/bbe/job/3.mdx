<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:14–15; Psalms 106:33; Psalms 39:2–3"> Then, opening his mouth, and cursing the day of his birth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 18:14"> Job made answer and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:14–18; Jeremiah 15:10; Job 10:18–19"> Let destruction take the day of my birth, and the night on which it was said, A man child has come into the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 27:20; Exodus 10:22–23; Joel 2:2"> That day — let it be dark; let not God take note of it from on high, and let not the light be shining on it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 10:21–22; Psalms 23:4; Job 28:3"> Let the dark and the black night take it for themselves; let it be covered with a cloud; let the dark shades of day send fear on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> That night — let the thick dark take it; let it not have joy among the days of the year; let it not come into the number of the months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 24:8; Jeremiah 7:34; Revelation 18:22–23"> As for that night, let it have no fruit; let no voice of joy be sounded in it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 41:1; Job 41:10; Mark 5:38"> Let it be cursed by those who put a curse on the day; who are ready to make Leviathan awake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 41:18; Jeremiah 13:16; Job 30:26"> Let its morning stars be dark; let it be looking for light, but may it not have any; let it not see the eyes of the dawn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:17; Ecclesiastes 6:3–5; Genesis 20:18"> Because it did not keep the doors of my mother's body shut, so that trouble might be veiled from my eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 10:18–19; Isaiah 46:3; Psalms 71:6"> Why did death not take me when I came out of my mother's body, why did I not, when I came out, give up my last breath?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 66:12; Genesis 30:3; Genesis 50:23"> Why did the knees take me, or why the breasts that they might give me milk?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:10; Job 21:13; Job 14:10–12"> For then I might have gone to my rest in quiet, and in sleep have been in peace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 15:28; Psalms 89:48; Psalms 49:14"> With kings and the wise ones of the earth, who put up great houses for themselves;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 10:27; Job 22:25; Zephaniah 1:18"> Or with rulers who had gold, and whose houses were full of silver;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 58:8; Ecclesiastes 6:3; 1 Corinthians 15:8"> Or as a child dead at birth I might never have come into existence; like young children who have not seen the light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Job 17:16; Hebrews 4:11"> There the passions of the evil are over, and those whose strength has come to an end have rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 39:7; Isaiah 14:3–4; Exodus 5:6–8"> There the prisoners are at peace together; the voice of the overseer comes not again to their ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:8; Job 30:23; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> The small and the great are there, and the servant is free from his master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:18; Job 7:15–16; 1 Samuel 1:10"> Why does he give light to him who is in trouble, and life to the bitter in soul;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 9:6; Proverbs 2:4; 1 Kings 19:4"> To those whose desire is for death, but it comes not; who are searching for it more than for secret wealth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Who are glad with great joy, and full of delight when they come to their last resting-place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 19:8; Lamentations 3:7; Isaiah 40:27"> To a man whose way is veiled, and who is shut in by God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 102:9; Psalms 42:3–4; Psalms 80:5"> In place of my food I have grief, and cries of sorrow come from me like water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 1:5; Job 30:15; Job 31:23"> For I have a fear and it comes on me, and my heart is greatly troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 7:14; Job 27:9; Psalms 143:11"> I have no peace, no quiet, and no rest; nothing but pain comes on me.</span>
