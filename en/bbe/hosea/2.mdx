<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 2:10; Hosea 2:23; Zechariah 13:9"> Say to your brothers, Ammi; and to your sisters, Ruhamah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 50:1; Ezekiel 16:25; Ezekiel 23:45"> Take up the cause against your mother, take it up, for she is not my wife, and I am not her husband; let her put away her loose ways from her face, and her false ways from between her breasts;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:22; Isaiah 32:13–14; Ezekiel 19:13"> For fear that I may take away her robe from her, making her uncovered as in the day of her birth; making her like a waste place and a dry land, causing her death through need of water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:14; Hosea 1:6; Ezekiel 8:18"> And I will have no mercy on her children, for they are the children of her loose ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 1:21; Hosea 3:1; Jeremiah 44:17–18"> For their mother has been untrue; she who gave them birth has done things of shame, for she said, I will go after my lovers, who give me my bread and my water, my wool and my linen, my oil and my wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 19:8; Job 3:23; Lamentations 3:7–9"> For this cause I will put thorns in her road, building up a wall round her so that she may not go on her way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:2; Hosea 5:13; Ezekiel 23:4"> And if she goes after her lovers she will not overtake them; if she makes search for them she will not see them; then will she say, I will go back to my first husband, for then it was better for me than now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 1:3; Hosea 4:11; Hosea 13:2"> For she had no knowledge that it was I who gave her the grain and the wine and the oil, increasing her silver and gold which they gave to the Baal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 2:3; Haggai 1:6–11; Malachi 1:4"> So I will take away again my grain in its time and my wine, and I will take away my wool and my linen with which her body might have been covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:29; Jeremiah 13:26; Hosea 13:7–8"> And now I will make her shame clear before the eyes of her lovers, and no one will take her out of my hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 1:13–14; Amos 5:21; Jeremiah 7:34"> And I will put an end to all her joy, her feasts, her new moons, and her Sabbaths, and all her regular meetings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 7:23; Isaiah 5:5; Hosea 2:5"> And I will make waste her vines and her fig-trees, of which she has said, These are the payments which my lovers have made to me; and I will make them a waste of trees, and the beasts of the field will take them for food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 13:6; Judges 3:7; Hosea 13:1"> And I will give her punishment for the days of the Baals, to whom she has been burning perfumes, when she made herself fair with her nose-rings and her jewels, and went after her lovers, giving no thought to me, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:1–2; Revelation 12:6; Ezekiel 20:35–36"> For this cause I will make her come into the waste land and will say words of comfort to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:2; Ezekiel 28:26; Hosea 11:1"> And I will give her vine-gardens from there, and the valley of Achor for a door of hope; and she will give her answer there as in the days when she was young, and as in the time when she came up out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 54:5; Hosea 2:7; Jeremiah 3:14"> And in that day, says the Lord, you will say to me, Ishi; and you will never again give me the name of Baali;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 23:13; Joshua 23:7; Psalms 16:4"> For I will take away the names of the Baals out of her mouth, and never again will she say their names.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:25; Ezekiel 39:9–10; Psalms 46:9"> And in that day I will make an agreement for them with the beasts of the field and the birds of heaven and the things which go low on the earth; I will put an end to the bow and the sword and war in all the land, and will make them take their rest in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:2; Revelation 21:2; Isaiah 54:8–10"> And I will take you as my bride for ever; truly, I will take you as my bride in righteousness and in right judging, in love and in mercies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 John 5:20; John 17:3; Jeremiah 24:7"> I will take you as my bride in good faith, and you will have knowledge of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zechariah 8:12; Isaiah 65:24; Romans 8:32"> And it will be, in that day, says the Lord, that I will give an answer to the heavens, and the heavens to the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joel 2:19; Hosea 1:4; Hosea 1:11"> And the earth will give its answer to the grain and the wine and the oil, and they will give an answer to Jezreel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 9:25–26; 1 Peter 2:9–10; Zechariah 13:9"> And I will put her as seed in the earth, and I will have mercy on her to whom no mercy was given; and I will say to those who were not my people, You are my people, and they will say, My God.</span>
