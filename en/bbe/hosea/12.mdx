<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 11:12; Hosea 8:7; Ezekiel 17:10"> Ephraim's food is the wind, and he goes after the east wind: deceit and destruction are increasing day by day; they make an agreement with Assyria, and take oil into Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 6:2; Isaiah 10:6; Hosea 4:1"> The Lord has a cause against Judah, and will give punishment to Jacob for his ways; he will give him the reward of his acts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 25:26; Genesis 32:24–28; Romans 9:11"> In the body of his mother he took his brother by the foot, and in his strength he was fighting with God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 35:15; Malachi 3:1; Genesis 35:9–10"> He had a fight with the angel and overcame him; he made request for grace to him with weeping; he came face to face with him in Beth-el and there his words came to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 3:15; Isaiah 42:8; Psalms 135:13"> Even the Lord, the God of armies; the Lord is his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Micah 6:8; Hosea 14:1; Micah 7:7"> So then, come back to your God; keep mercy and right, and be waiting at all times on your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Micah 6:10–11; Amos 8:5–6; Proverbs 11:1"> As for Canaan, the scales of deceit are in his hands; he takes pleasure in twisted ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 3:17; Psalms 62:10; Psalms 52:7"> And Ephraim said, Now I have got wealth and much property; in all my works no sin may be seen in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 23:40–43; Nehemiah 8:15–17; Hosea 13:4"> But I am the Lord your God from the land of Egypt; I will give you tents for your living-places again as in the days of the holy meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 17:13; Ezekiel 20:49; Jeremiah 19:1"> My word came to the ears of the prophets and I gave them visions in great number, and by the mouths of the prophets I made use of comparisons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 4:15; Hosea 6:8; Hosea 8:11"> In Gilead there is evil. They are quite without value; in Gilgal they make offerings of oxen; truly their altars are like masses of stones in the hollows of a ploughed field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 31:41; Genesis 27:43; Genesis 28:1–22"> And Jacob went in flight into the field of Aram, and Israel became a servant for a wife, and for a wife he kept sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 12:50–51; Acts 3:22–23; Psalms 77:20"> And by a prophet the Lord made Israel come up out of Egypt, and by a prophet he was kept safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:13; Daniel 11:18; 2 Kings 17:7–18"> I have been bitterly moved to wrath by Ephraim; so that his blood will be on him, and the Lord will make his shame come back on him.</span>
