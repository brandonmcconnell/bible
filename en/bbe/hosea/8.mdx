<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:28; Jeremiah 51:34; Romans 9:22"> Israel has come to destruction; now they are among the nations like a cup in which there is no pleasure.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:37; Ezekiel 23:22–26; Isaiah 10:8"> But though they give money to the nations for help, still I will send them in all directions; and in a short time they will be without a king and rulers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 12:11; Hosea 10:1–2; Hosea 10:8"> Because Ephraim has been increasing altars for sin, altars have become a cause of sin to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:6–8; Nehemiah 9:26; Proverbs 22:20"> Though I put my law in writing for him in ten thousand rules, they are to him as a strange thing.</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:18; Jeremiah 17:27; Amos 2:5"> For Israel has no memory of his Maker, and has put up the houses of kings; and Judah has made great the number of his walled towns.
