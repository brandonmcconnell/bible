<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:22; Micah 6:2; Isaiah 3:13–14"> Give ear to the word of the Lord, O children of Israel; for the Lord has a cause against the people of this land, because there is no good faith in it, and no mercy and no knowledge of God in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 6:9; Jeremiah 6:7; Hosea 7:1"> There is cursing and broken faith, violent death and attacks on property, men are untrue in married life, houses are broken into, and there is blood touching blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:3; Isaiah 24:4–12; Nahum 1:4"> Because of this the land will be dry, and everyone living in it will be wasted away, with the beasts of the field and the birds of heaven; even the fishes of the sea will be taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:12; Hosea 4:17; Amos 5:13"> Let no man go to law or make protests, for your people are like those who go to law with a priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:8; Hosea 2:2; Galatians 4:26"> You will not be able to keep on your feet by day, and by night the prophet will be falling down with you, and I will give your mother to destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 5:13; Proverbs 19:2; Hosea 6:6"> Destruction has overtaken my people because they have no knowledge; because you have given up knowledge, I will give you up, so that you will be no priest to me, because you have not kept in mind the law of your God, I will not keep your children in my memory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 13:6; Philippians 3:19; 1 Samuel 2:30"> Even while they were increasing in number they were sinning against me; I will let their glory be changed into shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Isaiah 56:11; Titus 1:11"> The sin of my people is like food to them; and their desire is for their wrongdoing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 24:2; Jeremiah 5:31; Matthew 15:14"> And the priest will be like the people; I will give them punishment for their evil ways, and the reward of their acts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 6:14; Leviticus 26:26; Haggai 1:6"> They will have food, but they will not be full; they will be false to me, but they will not be increased, because they no longer give thought to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Luke 21:34; Isaiah 28:7"> Loose ways and new wine take away wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:27; Hosea 5:4; Habakkuk 2:19"> My people get knowledge from their tree, and their rod gives them news; for a false spirit is the cause of their wandering, and they have been false to their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:29; Jeremiah 3:6; Ezekiel 6:13"> They make offerings on the tops of mountains, burning perfumes in high places, under trees of every sort, because their shade is good: and so your daughters are given up to loose ways and your brides are false to their husbands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hosea 4:1; John 8:43; Ephesians 4:18"> I will not give punishment to your daughters or your brides for their evil behaviour; for they make themselves separate with loose women, and make offerings with those who are used for sex purposes in the worship of the gods: the people who have no wisdom will be sent away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 12:11; Hosea 9:15; Amos 4:4"> Do not you, O Israel, come into error; do not you, O Judah, come to Gilgal, or go up to Beth-aven, or take an oath, By the living Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:24; Jeremiah 8:5; Jeremiah 3:11"> For Israel is uncontrolled, like a cow which may not be controlled; now will the Lord give them food like a lamb in a wide place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 81:12; Revelation 22:11; Hosea 13:2"> Ephraim is joined to false gods; let him be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Amos 5:12; 2 Kings 17:7–17"> Their drink has become bitter; they are completely false; her rulers take pleasure in shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 1:29; Jeremiah 4:11–12; Hosea 13:15"> They are folded in the skirts of the wind; they will be shamed because of their offerings.</span>
