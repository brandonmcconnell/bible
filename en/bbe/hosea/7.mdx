<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 7:13; Micah 7:3–7; Hosea 6:4"> When my desire was for the fate of my people to be changed and to make Israel well, then the sin of Ephraim was made clear, and the evil-doing of Samaria; for their ways are false, and the thief comes into the house, while the band of outlaws takes property by force in the streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 12:2; Amos 8:7; Jeremiah 14:10"> And they do not say to themselves that I keep in mind all their sin; now their evil acts come round them on every side; they are before my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Micah 7:3; Romans 1:32; Jeremiah 28:1–4"> In their sin they make a king for themselves, and rulers in their deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:2; James 4:4; Jeremiah 5:7–8"> They are all untrue; they are like a burning oven; the bread-maker does not make up the fire from the time when the paste is mixed till it is leavened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 28:1; Matthew 14:6; Genesis 40:20"> On the day of our king, the rulers made him ill with the heat of wine; his hand was stretched out with the men of pride.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 7:7; Hosea 7:4; 1 Samuel 19:11–15"> For they have made their hearts ready like an oven, while they are waiting secretly; their wrath is sleeping all night; in the morning it is burning like a flaming fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 15:25; 2 Kings 15:30; 2 Kings 15:10"> They are all heated like an oven, and they put an end to their judges; all their kings have been made low; not one among them makes prayer to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 106:35; Ezra 9:12; Nehemiah 13:23–25"> Ephraim is mixed with the peoples; Ephraim is a cake not turned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 8:7; Isaiah 42:22–25; 2 Kings 13:3–7"> Men from other lands have made waste his strength, and he is not conscious of it; grey hairs have come on him here and there, and he has no knowledge of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 5:5; Isaiah 9:13; Amos 4:6–13"> And the pride of Israel gives an answer to his face; but for all this, they have not gone back to the Lord their God, or made search for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 12:1; Hosea 5:13; Hosea 11:11"> And Ephraim is like a foolish dove, without wisdom; they send out their cry to Egypt, they go to Assyria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Deuteronomy 31:16–29"> When they go, my net will be stretched out over them; I will take them like the birds of heaven, I will give them punishment, I will take them away in the net for their sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 9:12; Hosea 11:12; Isaiah 63:8"> May trouble be theirs! for they have gone far away from me; and destruction, for they have been sinning against me; I was ready to be their saviour, but they said false words against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 4:3; Job 35:9–10; Jeremiah 3:10"> And they have not made prayer to me in their hearts, but they make loud cries on their beds; they are cutting themselves for food and wine, they are turned against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Nahum 1:9; Psalms 62:3; Jeremiah 17:9"> Though I have given training and strength to their arms, they have evil designs against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 78:57; Hosea 9:3; Ezekiel 23:32"> They have gone to what is of no value; they are like a false bow; their captains will come to destruction by the sword, and their ruler by my wrath; for this, the land of Egypt will make sport of them.</span>
