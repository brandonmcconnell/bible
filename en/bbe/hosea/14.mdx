<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 68:5; Psalms 10:14; Isaiah 31:1"> Assyria will not be our salvation; we will not go on horses; we will not again say to the work of our hands, You are our gods; for in you there is mercy for the child who has no father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 57:18; Ephesians 2:4–9; Jeremiah 3:22"> I will put right their errors; freely will my love be given to them, for my wrath is turned away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 35:2; Isaiah 27:6; Ephesians 3:17"> I will be as the dew to Israel; he will put out flowers like a lily, and send out his roots like Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 52:8; John 15:1; 2 Corinthians 2:14–15"> His branches will be stretched out, he will be beautiful as the olive-tree and sweet-smelling as Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 91:1; Psalms 85:6; 1 Corinthians 15:36–38"> They will come back and have rest in his shade; their life will be made new like the grain, and they will put out flowers like the vine; his name will be like the wine of Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 34:32; Isaiah 55:13; James 1:17"> As for Ephraim, what has he to do with false gods any longer? I have given an answer and I will keep watch over him; I am like a branching fir-tree, from me comes your fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 10:29; Psalms 107:43; John 8:47"> He who is wise will see these things; he who has good sense will have knowledge of them. For the ways of the Lord are straight, and the upright will go in them, but sinners will be falling in them.</span>
