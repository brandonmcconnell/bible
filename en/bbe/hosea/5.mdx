<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 9:15; Isaiah 29:15; Hosea 6:9"> They have gone deep in the evil ways of Shittim, but I am the judge of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 3:2; Hebrews 4:13; Hosea 13:1"> I have knowledge of Ephraim, and Israel is not secret from me; for now, O Ephraim, you have been false to me, Israel has become unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 4:12; 1 John 2:3–4; Hosea 4:1"> Their works will not let them come back to their God, for a false spirit is in them and they have no knowledge of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 7:10; Amos 5:2; Ezekiel 23:31–35"> And the pride of Israel gives an answer to his face; and Ephraim will have a fall through his sins, and the fall of Judah will be the same as theirs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Micah 6:6–7; John 7:34; Proverbs 1:28"> They will go, with their flocks and their herds, in search of the Lord, but they will not see him; he has taken himself out of their view.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 6:7; Isaiah 48:8; Jeremiah 3:20"> They have been false to the Lord; they have given birth to strange children; now the new moon will make them waste with their fields.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hosea 4:15; Hosea 9:9; 1 Samuel 15:34"> Let the horn be sounded in Gibeah and in Ramah; give a loud cry in Beth-aven, They are after you, O Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 1:6; Isaiah 46:10; Hosea 9:11–17"> Ephraim will become a waste in the day of punishment; I have given knowledge among the tribes of Israel of what is certain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:14; Deuteronomy 27:17; Psalms 93:3–4"> The rulers of Judah are like those who take away a landmark; I will let loose my wrath on them like flowing water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:33; Micah 6:16; 1 Kings 12:26–33"> Ephraim is troubled; he is crushed by his judges, because he took pleasure in walking after deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 51:8; Job 13:28; Isaiah 50:9"> And so to Ephraim I am like a wasting insect, and a destruction to the children of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 7:11; Hosea 12:1; Hosea 10:6"> When Ephraim saw his disease and Judah his wound, then Ephraim went to Assyria and sent to the great king; but he is not able to make you well or give you help for your wound.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 7:2; Micah 5:8; Hosea 13:7–8"> For I will be to Ephraim as a lion, and as a young lion to the children of Judah; I, even I, will give him wounds and go away; I will take him away, and there will be no helper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 26:16; Jeremiah 29:12–14; Deuteronomy 4:29–31"> I will go back to my place till they are made waste; in their trouble they will go after me early and will make search for me.</span>
