<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 9:17; 1 Timothy 2:7; 1 Corinthians 9:19"> Am I not free? am I not an Apostle? have I not seen Jesus our Lord? are you not my work in the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:12; John 6:27; 2 Corinthians 3:1–3"> If to others I am not an Apostle, at least I am one to you: for the fact that you are Christians is the sign that I am an Apostle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:16–19; 2 Corinthians 13:3; 2 Corinthians 13:10"> My answer to those who are judging me is this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:6; 2 Thessalonians 3:8–9; Luke 10:7"> Have we no right to take food and drink?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 1:42; Hebrews 13:4; Matthew 8:14"> Have we no right to take about with us a Christian wife, like the rest of the Apostles, and the brothers of the Lord, and Cephas?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:7–9; Acts 13:1–2; Acts 4:36"> Or I only and Barnabas, have we no right to take a rest from work?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 27:18; Deuteronomy 20:6; 2 Timothy 2:3–4"> Who ever goes to war without looking to someone to be responsible for his payment? who puts in vines and does not take the fruit of them? or who takes care of sheep without drinking of their milk?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 8:20; 1 Thessalonians 4:8; Romans 3:31"> Am I talking as a man? does not the law say the same?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:4; 1 Timothy 5:18; Psalms 104:27"> For it says in the law of Moses, It is not right to keep the ox from taking the grain when he is crushing it. Is it for the oxen that God is giving orders?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:6; Romans 15:4; Romans 4:23–24"> Or has he us in mind? Yes, it was said for us; because it is right for the ploughman to do his ploughing in hope, and for him who is crushing the grain to do his work hoping for a part in the fruits of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 15:27; Galatians 6:6; 2 Corinthians 11:15"> If we have been planting the things of the Spirit for you, does it seem a great thing for you to give us a part in your things of this world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:15; 1 Corinthians 9:18; 2 Corinthians 11:12"> If others have a part in this right over you, have we not even more? But we did not make use of our right, so that we might put nothing in the way of the good news of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 18:8–20; Numbers 5:9–10; Leviticus 6:26"> Do you not see that the servants of the holy things get their living from the Temple, and the servants of the altar have their part in the food which is offered on the altar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 10:10; 1 Timothy 5:17–18; 1 Corinthians 9:4"> Even so did the Lord give orders that the preachers of the good news might get their living from the good news.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:9; 2 Thessalonians 3:8; 1 Corinthians 9:12"> But I have not made use of any of these things: and I am not writing this in the hope that it may be so for me: for it would be better for me to undergo death, than for any man to make this pride of mine of no effect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 1:14; Jeremiah 20:9; Colossians 4:17"> For if I am a preacher of the good news, I have no cause for pride in this; because I am forced to do so, for a curse is on me if I do not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 2:7; 1 Corinthians 3:8; 1 Chronicles 28:9"> But if I do it gladly, I have a reward; and if not, I am under orders to do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:7–9; 1 Corinthians 8:9; 1 Thessalonians 2:6"> What then is my reward? This, that when I am giving the good news, I may give it without payment, not making use of my rights as a preacher of the good news.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 5:13; Matthew 18:15; James 5:19–20"> For though I was free from all men, I made myself a servant to all, so that more might have salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 16:3; Galatians 5:18; Acts 21:20–26"> And to the Jews I was as a Jew, so that I might give the good news to them; to those under the law I was the same, not as being myself under the law, but so that I might give the good news to those under the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 2:12; Romans 2:14; Galatians 3:2"> To those without the law I was as one without the law, not as being without law to God, but as under law to Christ, so that I might give the good news to those without the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:33; Romans 15:1; 1 Corinthians 9:19"> To the feeble, I was as one who is feeble, so that they might have salvation: I have been all things to all men, so that some at least might have salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:10; Mark 8:35; 1 Corinthians 9:25–27"> And I do all things for the cause of the good news, so that I may have a part in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Philippians 3:14; Hebrews 12:1; 2 Timothy 4:7–8"> Do you not see that in a running competition all take part, but only one gets the reward? So let your minds be fixed on the reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:5; Revelation 2:10; 2 Timothy 4:7–8"> And every man who takes part in the sports has self-control in all things. Now they do it to get a crown which is of this world, but we for an eternal crown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Peter 1:10; Philippians 1:21; 2 Timothy 2:5"> So then I am running, not uncertainly; so I am fighting, not as one who gives blows in the air:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 8:13; 1 Corinthians 9:25; Colossians 3:5"> But I give blows to my body, and keep it under control, for fear that, after having given the good news to others, I myself might not have God's approval.</span>
