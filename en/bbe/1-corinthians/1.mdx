<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 1:1; Ephesians 1:1; 2 Corinthians 1:1"> Paul, an Apostle of Jesus Christ by the purpose of God, and Sosthenes the brother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 1:7; 1 Corinthians 6:9–11; 1 Corinthians 1:30"> To the church of God which is in Corinth, to those who have been made holy in Christ Jesus, saints by the selection of God, with all those who in every place give honour to the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, their Lord and ours:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 1:7; 2 Corinthians 1:2; 1 Peter 1:2"> Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:8; John 14:26; 1 Timothy 1:14"> I give praise to my God for you at all times, because of the grace of God which has been given to you in Christ Jesus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:11; 2 Corinthians 8:7; Colossians 2:3"> So that in him you have wealth in all things, in word and in knowledge of every sort;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 1:2; 2 Timothy 1:8; 1 Timothy 2:6"> Even as the witness of the Christ has been made certain among you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Philippians 3:20; 2 Peter 3:12; Hebrews 9:28"> So that having every grace you are living in the hope of the revelation of our Lord Jesus Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:23–24; 1 Thessalonians 3:13; Colossians 1:22"> Who will give you strength to the end, to be free from all sin in the day of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:3; Hebrews 10:23; Deuteronomy 7:9"> God is true, through whom you have been given a part with his Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 2:1–4; Ephesians 4:1–7; 1 Corinthians 12:25"> Now I make request to you, my brothers, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that you will all say the same thing, and that there may be no divisions among you, so that you may be in complete agreement, in the same mind and in the same opinion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 18:6; 1 Timothy 6:4; 2 Timothy 2:23–25"> Because it has come to my knowledge, through those of the house of Chloe, that there are divisions among you, my brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:21–23; John 1:42; Matthew 23:9–10"> That is, that some of you say, I am of Paul; some say, I am of Apollos; some say, I am of Cephas; and some say, I am Christ's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 4:5; 2 Corinthians 11:4; Acts 2:38"> Is there a division in Christ? was Paul nailed to the cross for you? or were you given baptism in the name of Paul?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 18:8; Romans 16:23; 1 Corinthians 1:4"> I give praise to God that not one of you had baptism from me, but Crispus and Gaius;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:2; John 3:28–29; John 7:18"> So that no one may be able to say that you had baptism in my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:15; 1 Corinthians 16:17; Acts 16:33"> And I gave baptism to the house of Stephanas; but I am not certain that any others had baptism from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:1; 1 Corinthians 2:13; 2 Peter 1:16"> For Christ sent me, not to give baptism, but to be a preacher of the good news: not with wise words, for fear that the cross of Christ might be made of no value.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; Romans 1:16; 2 Thessalonians 2:10"> For the word of the cross seems foolish to those who are on the way to destruction; but to us who are on the way to salvation it is the power of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 29:14; Jeremiah 8:9; 1 Corinthians 3:19"> As it says in the holy Writings, I will put an end to the wisdom of the wise, and will put on one side the designs of those who have knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 1:22; Job 12:17; 1 Corinthians 3:19"> Where is the wise? where is he who has knowledge of the law? where is the man of this world who has a love of discussion? has not God made foolish the wisdom of this world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 10:21; Romans 1:20–22; Matthew 11:25"> For because, by the purpose of God, the world, with all its wisdom, had not the knowledge of God, it was God's pleasure, by so foolish a thing as preaching, to give salvation to those who had faith in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 16:1–4; Matthew 12:38–39; Mark 8:11"> Seeing that the Jews make request for signs, and the Greeks are looking for knowledge:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; 1 Corinthians 2:2; 1 Peter 2:8"> But we give the good news of Christ on the cross, a hard thing to the Jews, and a foolish thing to the Gentiles;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:30; Colossians 2:3; 1 Corinthians 1:18"> But to those of God's selection, Jews and Greeks, Christ is the power and the wisdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 11:33–36; 1 Corinthians 1:18; Zechariah 4:6–7"> Because what seems foolish in God is wiser than men; and what seems feeble in God is stronger than men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 2:5; 1 Corinthians 3:18–20; James 1:9–11"> For you see God's design for you, my brothers, that he has not taken a great number of the wise after the flesh, not the strong, not the noble:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 2:5; Matthew 11:25; 1 Corinthians 1:20"> But God made selection of the foolish things of this world so that he might put the wise to shame; and the feeble things that he might put to shame the strong;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 34:24; Romans 4:17; Isaiah 2:11"> And the low things of the world, and the things without honour, did God make selection of, yes, even the things which are not, so that he might make as nothing the things which are:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Romans 3:27; Jeremiah 9:23; Ephesians 2:9"> So that no flesh might have glory before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ephesians 1:7; Philippians 3:9; Romans 8:1"> But God has given you a place in Christ Jesus, through whom God has given us wisdom and righteousness and salvation, and made us holy:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23–24; 2 Corinthians 10:17; Philippians 3:3"> So that, as it is said in the holy Writings, Whoever has a desire for glory, let his glory be in the Lord.</span>
