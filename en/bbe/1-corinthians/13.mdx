<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:5; 1 Peter 4:8; Galatians 5:22"> If I make use of the tongues of men and of angels, and have not love, I am like sounding brass, or a loud-tongued bell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 4:8; 1 Corinthians 13:3; 1 John 4:20–21"> And if I have a prophet's power, and have knowledge of all secret things; and if I have all faith, by which mountains may be moved from their place, but have not love, I am nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 2:14–17; John 15:13; Matthew 23:5"> And if I give all my goods to the poor, and if I give my body to be burned, but have not love, it is of no profit to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 4:8; Ephesians 4:32; Galatians 5:21–22"> Love is never tired of waiting; love is kind; love has no envy; love has no high opinion of itself, love has no pride;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:24; Philippians 2:3–5; 1 John 3:16–17"> Love's ways are ever fair, it takes no thought for itself; it is not quickly made angry, it takes no account of evil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 12:9; 3 John 1:3–4; 2 John 1:4"> It takes no pleasure in wrongdoing, but has joy in what is true;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:8; 1 Corinthians 13:4; 2 Timothy 2:24"> Love has the power of undergoing all things, having faith in all things, hoping all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:13; 1 Corinthians 13:1–2; Galatians 5:6"> Though the prophet's word may come to an end, tongues come to nothing, and knowledge have no more value, love has no end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 2:2–3; Ephesians 3:18–19; 1 Corinthians 2:9"> For our knowledge is only in part, and the prophet's word gives only a part of what is true:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:12; Isaiah 60:19–20; 2 Corinthians 5:7–8"> But when that which is complete is come, then that which is in part will be no longer necessary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:20; 1 Corinthians 3:1–2; Galatians 4:1"> When I was a child, I made use of a child's language, I had a child's feelings and a child's thoughts: now that I am a man, I have put away the things of a child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 3:2; 2 Corinthians 3:18; 1 Corinthians 13:9–10"> For now we see things in a glass, darkly; but then face to face: now my knowledge is in part; then it will be complete, even as God's knowledge of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:14; Colossians 3:14; 1 John 4:7–18"> But now we still have faith, hope, love, these three; and the greatest of these is love.</span>
