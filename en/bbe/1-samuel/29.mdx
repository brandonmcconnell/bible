<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:1; Joshua 19:30; 1 Kings 20:30"> Now the Philistines got all their army together at Aphek: and the Israelites put their forces in position by the fountain in Jezreel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:1–2; Joshua 13:3; 1 Samuel 29:6–7"> And the lords of the Philistines went on with their hundreds and their thousands, and David and his men came after with Achish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 6:5; 1 Samuel 27:7; John 19:6"> Then the rulers of the Philistines said, What are these Hebrews doing here? And Achish said to the rulers of the Philistines, Is this not David, the servant of Saul the king of Israel, who has been with me for a year or two, and I have never seen any wrong in him from the time when he came to me till now?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:21; 1 Chronicles 12:19; Luke 16:8"> But the rulers of the Philistines were angry with him, and said to him, Make the man go back to the place you have given him; do not let him go down with us to the fight, or he may be turned against us and be false to us: for how will this man make peace with his lord? will it not be with the heads of these men?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:11; 1 Samuel 18:6–7; Proverbs 27:14"> Is this not David, who was named in their songs, when in the dance they said to one another, Saul has put to death thousands, and David tens of thousands?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:25; 2 Kings 19:27; 1 Samuel 20:3"> Then Achish sent for David and said to him, By the living Lord, you are upright, and everything you have done with me in the army has been pleasing to me: I have seen no evil in you from the day when you came to me till now: but still, the lords are not pleased with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 22:34"> So now go back, and go in peace, so that you do not make the lords of the Philistines angry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 6:13; Psalms 34:13–14; 1 Samuel 28:2"> And David said to Achish, But what have I done? what have you seen in your servant while I have been with you till this day, that I may not go and take up arms against those who are now making war on my lord the king?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:27; 2 Samuel 14:17; 2 Samuel 14:20"> And Achish in answer said, It is true that in my eyes you are good, like an angel of God: but still, the rulers of the Philistines have said, He is not to go up with us to the fight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:19; 1 Samuel 30:1–2; 1 Chronicles 12:22"> So get up early in the morning, with the servants of your lord who are with you, and go to the place I have given you, and have no evil design in your heart, for you are good in my eyes; but when there is light enough in the morning, go away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 29:1; 2 Samuel 4:4; Joshua 19:18"> So David and his men got up early in the morning to go back to the land of the Philistines. And the Philistines went up to Jezreel.</span>
