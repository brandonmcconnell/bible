<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:18–19; 1 Timothy 5:21; Nehemiah 7:2"> Now when Samuel was old, he made his sons judges over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:38; Genesis 22:19; 1 Chronicles 6:28"> The name of his first son was Joel and the name of his second Abijah: they were judges in Beer-sheba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Psalms 15:5; 1 Timothy 6:10"> And his sons did not go in his ways, but moved by the love of money took rewards, and were not upright in judging.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:17; 2 Samuel 5:3; Exodus 3:16"> Then all the responsible men of Israel got together and went to Samuel at Ramah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:14–15; Hosea 13:10–11; 1 Samuel 8:6–8"> And said to him, See now, you are old, and your sons do not go in your ways: give us a king now to be our judge, so that we may be like the other nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:11; James 1:5; 1 Samuel 12:17"> But Samuel was not pleased when they said to him, Give us a king to be our judge. And Samuel made prayer to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 10:16; 1 Samuel 10:19; Exodus 16:8"> And the Lord said to Samuel, Give ear to the voice of the people and what they say to you: they have not been turned away from you, but they have been turned away from me, not desiring me to be king over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 106:34–40; Exodus 32:1; Judges 13:1"> As they have done from the first, from the day when I took them out of Egypt till this day, turning away from me and worshipping other gods, so now they are acting in the same way to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:25; 1 Samuel 8:11–18; Ezekiel 46:18"> Give ear now to their voice: but make a serious protest to them, and give them a picture of the sort of king who will be their ruler.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Samuel said all these words of the Lord to the people who were desiring a king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:52; Deuteronomy 17:14–20; 1 Samuel 10:25"> And he said, This is the sort of king who will be your ruler: he will take your sons and make them his servants, his horsemen, and drivers of his war-carriages, and they will go running before his war-carriages;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:7; 1 Kings 4:7; 1 Chronicles 27:1–22"> And he will make them captains of thousands and of fifties; some he will put to work ploughing and cutting his grain and making his instruments of war and building his war-carriages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Your daughters he will take to be makers of perfumes and cooks and bread-makers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:18; 1 Kings 21:7; 1 Kings 21:19"> He will take your fields and your vine-gardens and your olive-gardens, all the best of them, and give them to his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 1:3; Isaiah 39:7; Genesis 37:36"> He will take a tenth of your seed and of the fruit of your vines and give it to his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> He will take your men-servants and your servant-girls, and the best of your oxen and your asses and put them to his work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> He will take a tenth of your sheep: and you will be his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Micah 3:4; Isaiah 1:15; Proverbs 1:25–28"> Then you will be crying out because of your king whom you have taken for yourselves; but the Lord will not give you an answer in that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:16; Jeremiah 7:13; Isaiah 66:4"> But the people gave no attention to the voice of Samuel; and they said, No, but we will have a king over us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:5; Psalms 106:35; Deuteronomy 7:6"> So that we may be like the other nations, and so that our king may be our judge and go out before us to war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 11:11"> Then Samuel, after hearing all the people had to say, went and gave an account of it to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:7; Hosea 13:11"> And the Lord said to Samuel, Give ear to their voice and make a king for them. Then Samuel said to the men of Israel, Let every man go back to his town.</span>
