<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 15:44; Micah 6:15; Nehemiah 3:17–18"> And they sent word to David, saying, The Philistines are fighting against Keilah and taking the grain from the grain-floors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:4; 2 Samuel 5:19; 2 Samuel 5:23"> So David, questioning the Lord, said, Am I to go and make an attack on these Philistines? And the Lord said to David, Go and make an attack on the Philistines so that Keilah may be kept from falling into their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:5; Psalms 11:1; 1 Samuel 23:15"> And David's men said to him, Even here in Judah we are full of fear: how much more then if we go to Keilah against the armies of the Philistines?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 7:7; Joshua 8:7; 2 Kings 3:18"> Then David put the question to the Lord again, and the Lord answering said, Up! go down to Keilah; for I will give the Philistines into your hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> So David and his men went to Keilah, and had a fight with the Philistines, and took away their cattle, and put them to the sword with great destruction. So David was the saviour of the people of Keilah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:20; 1 Samuel 14:3; 1 Samuel 14:18"> Now when Abiathar, the son of Ahimelech, went in flight to David, he came down to Keilah with the ephod in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:4–6; Job 20:5; 1 Samuel 26:8–9"> And news was given to Saul that David had come to Keilah. And Saul said, Now God has given him into my hands; for by going into a walled town with locked doors, he has let himself be shut in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And Saul sent for all the people to come to the fight, and go down to Keilah to make an attack on David and his men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:7; 1 Samuel 23:6; Numbers 27:21"> And it was clear to David that Saul had evil designs against him, and he said to Abiathar the priest, Come here with the ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 3:15–16; 1 Samuel 23:8; Genesis 18:24"> Then David said, O Lord, the God of Israel, news has been given to your servant that it is Saul's purpose to come to Keilah and send destruction on the town because of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:3; Psalms 50:15; Matthew 7:7–8"> And now, is it true, as they have said to me, that Saul is coming? O Lord, the God of Israel, give ear to your servant, and say if these things are so. And the Lord said, He is coming down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:20; Psalms 31:8; Psalms 62:1"> Then David said, Will the men of Keilah give me and my men up to Saul? and the Lord said, They will give you up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:2; 1 Samuel 25:13; 1 Samuel 30:9–10"> Then David and his men, about six hundred of them, went out of Keilah, and got away wherever they were able to go. And Saul, hearing that David had got away from Keilah, did not go there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 54:3–4; Psalms 32:7; Joshua 15:24"> And David kept in the waste land, in safe places, waiting in the hill-country in the waste land of Ziph. And Saul was searching for him every day, but God did not give him up into his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And David was full of fear, in the knowledge that Saul had come out to take his life; and David was in the waste land of Ziph, in Horesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3–4; Hebrews 12:12–13; Job 4:3–4"> And Saul's son Jonathan went to David in Horesh, and made his hands strong in God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:31; 1 Samuel 24:20; Acts 28:16"> And said to him, Have no fear, for Saul my father will not get you into his power; and you will be king of Israel, and I will be by your side, and my father Saul is certain of this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:7; 1 Samuel 20:42; 1 Samuel 18:3"> And the two of them made an agreement before the Lord: and David went on living in Horesh, and Jonathan went back to his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:1; 1 Samuel 26:3; Psalms 54:1–7"> Then the Ziphites came up to Gibeah to see Saul, and said, Is not David living secretly among us in the strong places in Horesh, in the hill of Hachilah to the south of the waste land?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:12; Proverbs 29:26; Proverbs 11:23"> So now, O king, have your soul's desire and come down, and we, for our part, will give him up into the king's hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:8; Isaiah 66:5; Micah 3:11"> And Saul said, The Lord's blessing will be yours, for you have had pity on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 5:13"> Go now, and take more steps, and see where he is living: for they say that he is expert in deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 3:15–16; Mark 14:1; Numbers 10:36"> So take care to get knowledge of all the secret places where he is taking cover, and be certain to come back to me, and I will go with you: and without doubt, if he is anywhere in the land, I will get him, among all the families of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:2; Joshua 15:55"> And they went back and came to Ziph before Saul: but David and his men were in the waste land of Maon, in the dry land south of the waste land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Judges 15:8; 1 Samuel 23:28"> And Saul and his men went in search of him. And David had word of it, so he came down to the rock in the waste land of Maon. And Saul, hearing of this, went after David into the waste land of Maon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 17:9; 2 Corinthians 1:8; Psalms 140:1–9"> And Saul and his men went on one side of the mountain, and David and his men went on the other: and David's purpose was to get away as quickly as possible, for fear of Saul; for Saul and his men were making a circle round David and his men in order to take them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 19:9; Psalms 116:3; Deuteronomy 32:36"> But a man came to Saul saying, Be quick and come; for the Philistines have made an attack on the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> So turning back from going after David, Saul went against the Philistines: so that place was named Sela-hammah-lekoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:2; Joshua 15:62; Ezekiel 47:10"> And from there, David went up and took cover in the safe place of En-gedi.</span>
