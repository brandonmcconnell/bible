<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:7; 1 Samuel 29:11; 1 Samuel 27:8–10"> Now when David and his men came to Ziklag on the third day, the Amalekites had made an attack on the South and on Ziklag, and had overcome Ziklag and put it on fire;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 27:11; Job 38:11; Psalms 76:10"> And had made the women and all who were there, small and great, prisoners: they had not put any of them to death, but had taken them all away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 34:19; 1 Peter 1:6–7; Revelation 3:9"> And when David and his men came to the town, they saw that it had been burned down, and their wives and their sons and daughters had been made prisoners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:4; Numbers 14:1; Judges 21:2"> Then David and the people who were with him gave themselves up to weeping till they were able to go on weeping no longer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:42–43; 2 Samuel 2:2; 1 Samuel 1:2"> And David's two wives, Ahinoam of Jezreel and Abigail, the wife of Nabal of Carmel, had been made prisoners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 56:3–4; Psalms 56:11; Psalms 62:5"> And David was greatly troubled; for the people were talking of stoning him, because their hearts were bitter, every man sorrowing for his sons and his daughters: but David made himself strong in the Lord his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 2:26; 1 Samuel 22:20–21; 1 Samuel 23:2–9"> And David said to Abiathar the priest, the son of Ahimelech, Come here to me with the ephod. And Abiathar took the ephod to David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:18; Proverbs 3:5–6; 1 Samuel 23:2"> Then David, questioning the Lord, said, Am I to go after this band? will I be able to overtake them? And in answer he said, Go after them, for you will certainly overtake them, and get back everything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 27:2"> So David went, and his six hundred men went with him, and they came to the stream Besor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:9; 1 Samuel 30:21; 1 Samuel 14:31"> And David, with four hundred men, went on: but two hundred of them were overcome with weariness, and not able to go across the stream.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 12:20–21; Deuteronomy 23:7; Matthew 25:35"> And in the fields they saw an Egyptian whom they took to David, and they gave him bread, and he had a meal, and they gave him water for drink;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 15:19; 1 Samuel 14:27; 1 Samuel 30:13"> And they gave him part of a cake of figs and some dry grapes; and after the food, his spirit came back to him, for he had had no food or drink for three days and nights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 2:13; Proverbs 12:10; Job 31:13–15"> And David said to him, Whose man are you and where do you come from? And he said, I am a young man of Egypt, servant to an Amalekite; and my master went on without me because three days back I became ill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 1:38; 2 Samuel 8:18; 1 Kings 1:44"> We made an attack on the south part of the country of the Cherethites, and on the land which is Judah's, and on the south of Caleb; and we put Ziklag on fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 2:12; Joshua 9:19–20; Ezekiel 17:16"> And David said to him, Will you take me down to this band? And he said, If you give me your oath that you will not put me to death or give me up to my master, I will take you to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 11:10–13; 2 Samuel 13:28; Judges 16:23–30"> And when he had taken him down, they saw them all, seated about on all sides, feasting and drinking among all the mass of goods which they had taken from the land of the Philistines and the land of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:11; Psalms 18:42; 1 Samuel 15:3"> And David went on fighting them from evening till the evening of the day after; and not one of them got away but only four hundred young men who went in flight on camels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 14:16"> And David got back everything the Amalekites had taken; and he got back his two wives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 91:9–10; 1 Samuel 30:8; Job 1:10"> There was no loss of anything, small or great, sons or daughters or goods or anything which they had taken away: David got it all back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:26; Numbers 31:9–12; 2 Chronicles 20:25"> And they took all the flocks and herds, and driving them in front of him, said, These are David's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 13:1; 1 Samuel 30:10; Judges 8:15"> And David came to the two hundred men, who because of weariness had not gone with him, but were waiting at the stream Besor: and they went out, meeting David and the people who were with him; and when they came near them, they said, How are you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 7:12; Deuteronomy 13:13; 1 Kings 21:10"> Then the bad and good-for-nothing men among those who went with David said, Because they did not go with us, we will give them nothing of the goods which we have got back, but only to every man his wife and children, so that he may take them and go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:18; Psalms 44:2–7; Acts 7:2"> Then David said, You are not to do this, my brothers, after what the Lord has given us, who has kept us safe and given up the band which came against us into our hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 22:8; Numbers 31:27; Psalms 68:12"> Who is going to give any attention to you in this question? for an equal part will be given to him who went to the fight and to him who was waiting by the goods: they are all to have the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:13"> And so he made it a rule and an order for Israel from that day till now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:27; Genesis 33:11; Isaiah 32:8"> And when David came to Ziklag, he sent some of the goods to the responsible men of Judah, and to his friends, saying, Here is an offering for you from the goods of those who were fighting against the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 15:48; Joshua 19:8; Judges 1:22–23"> He sent to those who were in Beth-el, and in Ramah of the South, and in Jattir;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 13:16; Joshua 15:50; Joshua 21:14"> And to those in Arara and Eshtemoa</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 1:16; 1 Samuel 27:10; 1 Samuel 15:6"> and Carmel and in the towns of the Jerahmeelites, and in the towns of the Kenites;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 1:17; Joshua 19:4; Joshua 15:42"> And to those who were in Hormah and in Bor-ashan and in Athach;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:1; 2 Samuel 15:10; 2 Samuel 4:1"> And in Hebron, and to all the places where David and his men had been living.</span>
