<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 15:35; 2 Chronicles 28:18; 1 Chronicles 11:13"> Now the Philistines got their armies together for war, and came together at Socoh in the land of Judah, and took up their position between Socoh and Azekah in Ephes-dammim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:9; 1 Samuel 17:19"> And Saul and the men of Israel came together and took up their position in the valley of Elah, and put their forces in order against the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> The Philistines were stationed on the mountain on one side and Israel on the mountain on the other side: and there was a valley between them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 11:22; 2 Samuel 21:16–22; 1 Chronicles 20:4–8"> And a fighter came out from the tents of the Philistines, named Goliath of Gath; he was more than six cubits tall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And he had a head-dress of brass on his head, and he was dressed in a coat of metal, the weight of which was five thousand shekels of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:45; 2 Chronicles 9:15; 1 Kings 10:16"> His legs were covered with plates of brass and hanging on his back was a javelin of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:19; 1 Samuel 17:41; 1 Chronicles 11:23"> The stem of his spear was as long as a cloth-worker's rod, and its head was made of six hundred shekels' weight of iron: and one went before him with his body-cover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:17; 1 Chronicles 21:3; 1 Samuel 17:26"> He took up his position and in a loud voice said to the armies of Israel, Why have you come out to make war? Am I not a Philistine and you servants of Saul? Send out a man for yourselves and let him come down to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:1"> If he is able to have a fight with me and overcome me, then we will be your servants: but if I am able to overcome him, then you will be our servants and do work for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:25–26; 1 Samuel 17:45; 1 Samuel 17:36"> And the Philistine said, I have put to shame the armies of Israel this day; give me a man so that we may have a fight together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Deuteronomy 31:8; Proverbs 28:1"> And Saul and all Israel, hearing those words of the Philistine, were troubled and full of fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:10–11; Genesis 35:19; 1 Samuel 16:18"> Now David was the son of that Ephrathite of Beth-lehem-judah named Jesse, who had eight sons; and he was an old man in Saul's day, and far on in years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:13; 2 Samuel 21:21; 1 Samuel 17:28"> And the three oldest sons of Jesse had gone with Saul to the fight: the names of the three who went to the fight were Eliab, the oldest, and Abinadab the second, and Shammah the third.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:11; Genesis 25:23"> And David was the youngest: and the three oldest were with Saul's army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:11; 1 Samuel 16:19–23"> Now David went to and from Saul, looking after his father's sheep at Beth-lehem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 4:2; Luke 4:2"> And the Philistine came near every morning and evening for forty days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:18; 2 Samuel 17:28; Ruth 2:14"> And Jesse said to his son David, Take now for your brothers an ephah of this dry grain and these ten cakes of bread, and go quickly with them to the tents to your brothers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 37:14; 1 Samuel 16:20; 2 Samuel 17:29"> And take these ten cheeses to the captain of their thousand, and see how your brothers are and come back with a sign to say how they are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Now Saul, and they, and all the men of Israel were in the valley of Elah, fighting with the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:5; Luke 19:43; Ephesians 6:1–2"> And David got up early in the morning, and, giving the sheep into the care of a keeper, took the things and went as Jesse had said; and he came to the lines where the carts were, when the army was going out to the fight giving their war-cry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And Israel and the Philistines had put their forces in position, army against army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 10:5–6; Judges 18:15; Isaiah 10:28"> And David gave his parcels into the hands of the keeper of the army stores, and went running to the army and came to his brothers to get knowledge about them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:8–10"> And while he was talking to them, the fighter, the Philistine of Gath, Goliath by name, came out from the Philistines' lines and said the same words, in David's hearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:6–7; Leviticus 26:36; Isaiah 7:2"> And all the men of Israel, when they saw him, went in flight, overcome with fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 15:16; Revelation 2:7; Revelation 3:12"> And the men of Israel said, Have you seen this man? Clearly he has come out to put shame on Israel: and it is certain that if any man overcomes him, the king will give that man great wealth, and will give him his daughter, and make his father's family free in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:26; 1 Samuel 11:2; 1 Samuel 14:6"> And David said to the men near him, What will be done to the man who overcomes this Philistine and takes away the shame from Israel? for who is this Philistine, a man without circumcision, that he has put shame on the armies of the living God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:25"> And the people gave him this answer, So it will be done to the man who overcomes him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 10:36; Genesis 37:11; Proverbs 18:19"> And Eliab, his oldest brother, hearing what David said to the men, was moved to wrath against David, and said, Why have you come here? Into whose care have you given that little flock of sheep in the waste land? I have knowledge of your pride and the evil of your heart, you have come down to see the fight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; 1 Corinthians 2:15; Proverbs 15:1"> And David said, What have I done now? was it not only a word?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:26–27"> And turning away from him to one of the other men, he said the same words: and the people gave him the same answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 22:29"> And, hearing what David said, they gave Saul word of it: and he sent for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:18; Deuteronomy 20:1–3; Psalms 3:6"> And David said to Saul, Let no man's heart become feeble because of him; I, your servant, will go out and have a fight with this Philistine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 13:31; Deuteronomy 9:2; Revelation 13:4"> And Saul said to David, You are not able to go out against this Philistine and have a fight with him: for you are only a boy, and he has been a man of war from his earliest days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And David said to Saul, Your servant has been keeper of his father's sheep; and if a lion or a bear came and took a lamb from the flock,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Amos 3:12; 2 Timothy 4:17–18; Daniel 6:22"> I went out after him, and overcame him, and took it out of his mouth: and if, turning on me, he came at me, I took him by the hair and overcame him and put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:10; Acts 5:38–39; Romans 2:28–29"> Your servant has overcome lion and bear: and the fate of this Philistine, who is without circumcision, will be like theirs, seeing that he has put shame on the armies of the living God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:17–18; Psalms 138:7–8; 2 Corinthians 1:9–10"> And David said, The Lord, who kept me safe from the grip of the lion and the bear, will be my saviour from the hands of this Philistine. And Saul said to David, Go! and may the Lord be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Then Saul gave David his clothing of war, and put a head-dress of brass on his head and had him clothed with a coat of metal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Zechariah 4:6; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Hosea 1:7"> And David took Saul's sword and put the band round him over the metal coat, and was unable to go forward; for he was not used to them. Then David said to Saul, It is not possible for me to go out with these, for I am not used to them. So David took them off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:27–29; Matthew 10:10; Judges 15:15–16"> Then he took his stick in his hand, and got five smooth stones from the bed of the stream and put them in a bag such as is used by sheep-keepers; and in his hand was a leather band used for sending stones: and so he went in the direction of the Philistine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> And the Philistine came nearer to David; and the man who had his body-cover went before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:12; 1 Samuel 17:33; Psalms 123:3–4"> And when the Philistine, taking note, saw David, he had a poor opinion of him: for he was only a boy, red-haired and good-looking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:8; 2 Samuel 3:8; 1 Samuel 24:14"> And the Philistine said to David, Am I a dog, that you come out to me with sticks? And the Philistine put curses on David by all his gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:9–10; Proverbs 18:12; 1 Kings 20:10–11"> And the Philistine said to David, Come here to me, and I will give your flesh to the birds of the air and the beasts of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4; 2 Chronicles 32:8; 2 Corinthians 3:5"> Then David said to the Philistine, You come to me with a sword and a spear and a javelin: but I come to you in the name of the Lord of armies, the God of the armies of Israel on which you have put shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 52:10; Joshua 4:24; 2 Kings 19:19"> This day the Lord will give you up into my hands, and I will overcome you, and take your head off you; and I will give the bodies of the Philistine army to the birds of the air and the beasts of the earth today, so that all the earth may see that Israel has a God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 44:6–7; Zechariah 4:6; Hosea 1:7"> And all these people who are here today may see that the Lord does not give salvation by sword and spear: for the fight is the Lord's, and he will give you up into our hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Proverbs 28:1; Psalms 27:1"> Now when the Philistine made a move and came near to David, David quickly went at a run in the direction of the army, meeting the Philistine face to face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:27–28; 2 Kings 9:24; 1 Kings 22:34"> And David put his hand in his bag and took out a stone and sent it from his leather band straight at the Philistine, and the stone went deep into his brow, and he went down to the earth, falling on his face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Judges 15:15; 1 Samuel 17:39; 1 Samuel 23:21"> So David overcame the Philistine with his leather band and a stone, wounding the Philistine and causing his death: but David had no sword in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Hebrews 11:34; 1 Samuel 21:9; 2 Samuel 23:21"> So running up to the Philistine and putting his foot on him, David took his sword out of its cover, and put him to death, cutting off his head with it. And when the Philistines saw that their fighter was dead, they went in flight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Joshua 15:11; Joshua 15:33–36; Joshua 15:45–46"> And the men of Israel and of Judah got up, and gave a cry, and went after the Philistines as far as Gath and the town doors of Ekron. And the wounded of the Philistines were falling down by the road from Shaaraim all the way to Gath and Ekron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:20; 2 Kings 7:7–16; Jeremiah 30:16"> Then the children of Israel came back from going after the Philistines, and took their goods from the tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Exodus 16:33; Joshua 4:7–8; 1 Samuel 21:9"> And David took the head of the Philistine to Jerusalem, but the metal war-dress and the arms he put in his tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:21–22; 1 Samuel 17:58"> And when Saul saw David going out against the Philistine, he said to Abner, the captain of the army, Abner, whose son is this young man? And Abner said, On your life, O king, I have no idea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> And the king said, Make search and see whose son this young man is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:54"> And when David was coming back after the destruction of the Philistine, Abner took him to Saul, with the head of the Philistine in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:12; 1 Samuel 16:18–19"> And Saul said to him, Young man, whose son are you? And David in answer said, I am the son of your servant Jesse of Beth-lehem.</span>
