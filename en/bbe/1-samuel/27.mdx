<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 40:27–31; 1 Samuel 21:10–15; Isaiah 51:12"> And David said to himself, Some day death will come to me by the hand of Saul: the only thing for me to do is to get away into the land of the Philistines; then Saul will give up hope of taking me in any part of the land of Israel: and so I may be able to get away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:10; 1 Samuel 25:13; 1 Kings 2:39–40"> So David and the six hundred men who were with him went over to Achish, the son of Maoch, king of Gath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:42–43; 1 Samuel 30:5; 1 Samuel 25:18–35"> And David and his men were living with Achish at Gath; every man had his family with him, and David had his two wives, Ahinoam of Jezreel, and Abigail of Carmel, who had been the wife of Nabal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:21"> And Saul, hearing that David had gone to Gath, went after him no longer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 46:34; 2 Corinthians 6:17"> Then David said to Achish, If now I have grace in your eyes, let me have a place in one of the smaller towns of your land, to be my living-place; for it is not right for your servant to be living with you in the king's town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:28; Joshua 15:31; 1 Chronicles 12:20"> So Achish straight away gave him Ziklag: and for that reason Ziklag has been the property of the kings of Judah to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 29:3"> And David was living in the land of the Philistines for the space of a year and four months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:7–8; Joshua 13:2; Joshua 13:13"> And David and his men went up and made attacks on the Geshurites and the Girzites and the Amalekites; for these were the people who were living in the land from Telam on the way to Shur, as far as Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:3; Joshua 6:21; Deuteronomy 25:17–19"> And David again and again made attacks on the land till not a man or a woman was still living; and he took away the sheep and the oxen and the asses and the camels and the clothing; and he came back to Achish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 1:16; 1 Chronicles 2:9; 1 Chronicles 2:25"> And every time Achish said, Where have you been fighting today? David said, Against the South of Judah and the South of the Jerahmeelites and the South of the Kenites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 29:25; Proverbs 12:19; 1 Samuel 22:22"> Not one living man or woman did David ever take back with him to Gath, fearing that they might give an account of what had taken place, and say, This is what David did, and so has he been doing all the time while he has been living in the land of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; 1 Samuel 13:4"> And Achish had belief in what David said, saying, He has made himself hated by all his people Israel, and so he will be my servant for ever.</span>
