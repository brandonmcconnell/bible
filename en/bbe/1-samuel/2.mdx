<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 1:46–56; Psalms 13:5; Isaiah 12:2–3"> And Hannah, in prayer before the Lord, said, My heart is glad in the Lord, my horn is lifted up in the Lord: my mouth is open wide over my haters; because my joy is in your salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:32; Isaiah 44:6; Psalms 86:8"> No other is holy as the Lord, for there is no other God but you: there is no Rock like our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 8:13; 1 Kings 8:39; Proverbs 24:12"> Say no more words of pride; let not uncontrolled sayings come out of your mouths: for the Lord is a God of knowledge, by him acts are judged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 76:3; Psalms 37:15; Psalms 46:9"> The bows of the men of war are broken, and the feeble are clothed with strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:9; Luke 1:53; Psalms 113:9"> Those who were full are offering themselves as servants for bread; those who were in need are at rest; truly, she who had no children has become the mother of seven; and she who had a family is wasted with sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Isaiah 26:19; Revelation 1:18"> The Lord is the giver of death and life: sending men down to the underworld and lifting them up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 75:7; Job 5:11; Deuteronomy 8:17–18"> The Lord gives wealth and takes a man's goods from him: crushing men down and again lifting them up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 113:7–8; Daniel 2:48; James 2:5"> Lifting the poor out of the dust, and him who is in need out of the lowest place, to give them their place among rulers, and for their heritage the seat of glory: for the pillars of the earth are the Lord's and he has made them the base of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 121:3; Psalms 91:11–12; Psalms 33:16–17"> He will keep the feet of his holy ones, but the evil-doers will come to their end in the dark night, for by strength no man will overcome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 2:9; Psalms 89:24; Psalms 18:13–14"> Those who make war against the Lord will be broken; against them he will send his thunder from heaven: the Lord will be judge of the ends of the earth, he will give strength to his king, lifting up the horn of him on whom the holy oil has been put.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:1; 1 Samuel 2:18; 1 Samuel 1:28"> Then Elkanah went to Ramah to his house. And the child became the servant of the Lord under the direction of Eli the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 1:28–30; Jeremiah 2:8; Judges 2:10"> Now the sons of Eli were evil and good-for-nothing men, having no knowledge of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 7:29–34"> And the priests' way with the people was this: when any man made an offering, the priest's servant came while the flesh was being cooked, having in his hand a meat-hook with three teeth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 29:27–28; Isaiah 56:11; 1 Samuel 2:29"> This he put into the pot, and everything which came up on the hook the priest took for himself. This they did in Shiloh to all the Israelites who came there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 3:16; Leviticus 3:3–5; Romans 16:18"> And more than this, before the fat was burned, the priest's servant would come and say to the man who was making the offering, Give me some of the flesh to be cooked for the priest; he has no taste for meat cooked in water, but would have you give it uncooked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 7:23–25; Leviticus 3:16; 1 Peter 5:2–3"> And if the man said to him, First let the fat be burned, then take as much as you will; then the servant would say, No, you are to give it to me now, or I will take it by force.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Malachi 2:7–9; Genesis 13:13; Malachi 2:13"> And the sin of these young men was very great before the Lord; for they gave no honour to the Lord's offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:1; Exodus 28:4; 1 Samuel 2:11"> But Samuel did the work of the Lord's house, while he was a child, dressed in a linen ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:3; 1 Samuel 1:21; Exodus 23:14"> And his mother made him a little robe and took it to him every year when she came with her husband for the year's offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:27–28; Luke 2:34; Ruth 4:11"> And every year Eli gave Elkanah and his wife a blessing, saying, May the Lord give you offspring by this woman in exchange for the child you have given to the Lord. And they went back to their house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 2:40; 1 Samuel 3:19; Genesis 21:1"> And the Lord had mercy on Hannah and she gave birth to three sons and two daughters. And the young Samuel became older before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 38:8; 1 Samuel 2:13–17; Jeremiah 7:9–10"> Now Eli was very old; and he had news from time to time of what his sons were doing to all Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:12; Philippians 3:19; Acts 9:4"> And he said to them, Why are you doing such things? for from all this people I get accounts of your evil ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Malachi 2:8; 1 Timothy 3:7; Acts 6:3"> No, my sons, the account which is given me, which the Lord's people are sending about, is not good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:14; Joshua 11:20; Hebrews 10:26"> If one man does wrong to another, God will be his judge: but if a man's sin is against the Lord, who will take up his cause? But they gave no attention to the voice of their father, for it was the Lord's purpose to send destruction on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 2:52; Luke 2:40; Acts 2:47"> And the young Samuel, becoming older, had the approval of the Lord and of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:1; Exodus 4:27; Exodus 4:14–16"> And a man of God came to Eli and said to him, The Lord says, Did I let myself be seen by your father's people when they were in Egypt, servants in Pharaoh's house?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 28:1; Leviticus 8:7–8; Numbers 18:19"> Did I take him out of all the tribes of Israel to be my priest and to go up to my altar to make the smoke of the offerings go up and to take up the ephod? Did I give to your father's family all the offerings made by fire by the children of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; Deuteronomy 32:15; 1 Samuel 2:13–17"> Why then are you looking with envy on my offerings of meat and of meal which were ordered by my word, honouring your sons before me, and making yourselves fat with all the best of the offerings of Israel, my people?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 12:26; Jeremiah 18:9–10; Proverbs 3:9–10"> For this reason the Lord God of Israel has said, Truly I did say that your family and your father's people would have their place before me for ever: but now the Lord says, Let it not be so; I will give honour to those by whom I am honoured, and those who have no respect for me will be of small value in my eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:17–20; 1 Kings 2:26–27; Psalms 37:17"> See, the days are coming when your arm and the arm of your father's people will be cut off;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Zechariah 8:4; 1 Samuel 4:11; Psalms 78:59–64"> And never again will there be an old man in your family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Kings 2:26–27; 1 Kings 1:7; 1 Samuel 22:21–23"> But one man of your family will not be cut off by my hand, and his eyes will be made dark, and grief will be in his heart: and all the offspring of your family will come to their end by the sword of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:11; 1 Kings 13:3; 1 Samuel 4:17"> And this will be the sign to you, which will come on Hophni and Phinehas, your sons; death will overtake them on the same day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:27; 2 Samuel 7:11; 1 Kings 11:38"> And I will make a true priest for myself, one who will do what is in my heart and in my mind: and I will make for him a family which will not come to an end; and his place will be before my holy one for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 2:27; Malachi 1:13; Ezekiel 44:10–12"> Then it will be that the rest of your family, anyone who has not been cut off, will go down on his knees to him for a bit of silver or a bit of bread, and say, Be pleased to put me into one of the priest's places so that I may have a little food.</span>
