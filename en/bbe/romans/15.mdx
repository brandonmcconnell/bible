<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:14; Romans 14:1; Galatians 6:1–2"> We who are strong have to be a support to the feeble, and not give pleasure to ourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 14:19; 1 Corinthians 10:24; Philippians 2:4–5"> Let every one of us give pleasure to his neighbour for his good, to make him strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 69:9; Philippians 2:8; John 4:34"> For Christ did not give pleasure to himself, but, as it is said, The bitter words of those who were angry with you came on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:11; 2 Timothy 3:16–17; Romans 4:23–24"> Now those things which were put down in writing before our time were for our learning, so that through quiet waiting and through the comfort of the holy Writings we might have hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 2:4–5; 1 Peter 3:8; Romans 12:16"> Now may the God who gives comfort and strength in waiting make you of the same mind with one another in harmony with Christ Jesus:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 10:29–30; Revelation 1:6; Ephesians 1:3"> So that with one mouth you may give glory to the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:40; John 13:34; Romans 5:2"> So then, take one another to your hearts, as Christ took us, to the glory of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 4:4–5; Matthew 15:24; John 1:11"> Now I say that Christ has been made a servant of the circumcision to give effect to the undertakings given by God to the fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:50; Psalms 18:49; Romans 3:29"> And so that the Gentiles might give glory to God for his mercy; as it is said, For this reason I will give praise to you among the Gentiles, and I will make a song to your name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 67:3–4; Deuteronomy 32:43; Isaiah 42:10–12"> And again he says, Take part, you Gentiles, in the joy of his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 117:1"> And again, Give praise to the Lord, all you Gentiles; and let all the nations give praise to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 11:10; Isaiah 11:1; Matthew 12:21"> And again Isaiah says, There will be the root of Jesse, and he who comes to be the ruler over the Gentiles; in him will the Gentiles put their hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:16–17; Romans 12:12; 2 Corinthians 9:8"> Now may the God of hope make you full of joy and peace through faith, so that all hope may be yours in the power of the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; 2 Peter 1:12; 1 John 2:21"> And I myself am certain of you, brothers, that you are full of what is good, complete in all knowledge, able to give direction to one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 15:10; Romans 1:5"> But I have, in some measure, less fear in writing to you to put these things before you again, because of the grace which was given to me by God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 11:13; Romans 1:1; Acts 9:15"> To be a servant of Christ Jesus to the Gentiles, doing the work of a priest in the good news of God, so that the offering of the Gentiles might be pleasing to God, being made holy by the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14–16; 2 Corinthians 12:11–21; Hebrews 5:1"> So I have pride in Christ Jesus in the things which are God's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 15:12; Romans 1:5; Acts 21:19"> And I will keep myself from talking of anything but those things which Christ has done by me to put the Gentiles under his rule in word and in act,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:12; Hebrews 2:4; Acts 19:11–12"> By signs and wonders, in the power of the Holy Spirit; so that from Jerusalem and round about as far as Illyricum I have given all the good news of Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:13–16; 1 Corinthians 3:9–15; Ephesians 2:20–22"> Making it my purpose not to take the good news where Christ was named, so that my work might not be resting on that of others;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 52:15; Isaiah 65:1"> But as it is said in the holy Writings, They will see, to whom the news of him had not been given, and those to whose ears it had not come will have knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 1:13; 1 Thessalonians 2:17–18"> For which reason I was frequently kept from coming to you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 15:32; Romans 1:10–12; Romans 15:29"> But now, having no longer any place in these parts and having had for a number of years a great desire to come to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 15:28; Romans 1:12; Acts 15:3"> Whenever I go to Spain (for it is my hope to see you on my way, and to be sent on there by you, if first I may in some measure have been comforted by your company) —</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 24:17; Acts 19:21; Acts 20:22"> But now I go to Jerusalem, taking help for the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:1–9; Acts 11:27–30; 2 Corinthians 9:2"> For it has been the good pleasure of those of Macedonia and Achaia to send a certain amount of money for the poor among the saints at Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:11; Galatians 6:6; Philemon 1:19"> Yes, it has been their good pleasure; and they are in their debt. For if the Gentiles have had a part in the things of the Spirit which were theirs, it is right for them, in the same way, to give them help in the things of the flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 15:24; Philippians 4:17; Colossians 1:6"> So when I have done this, and have given them this fruit of love, I will go on by you into Spain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 4:13; Romans 1:10–12; Psalms 16:11"> And I am certain that when I come, I will be full of the blessing of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 4:12; 2 Corinthians 1:11; 2 Corinthians 4:11"> Now I make request to you, brothers, by our Lord Jesus Christ, and by the love of the Spirit, that you will be working together with me in your prayers to God for me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:2; 2 Corinthians 8:4; 2 Corinthians 9:1"> So that I may be kept safe from those in Judaea who have not put themselves under the rule of God, and that the help which I am taking for Jerusalem may be pleasing to the saints;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:18; Philemon 1:20; Philemon 1:7"> So that I may come to you in joy by the good pleasure of God, and have rest with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:11; Philippians 4:9; 1 Thessalonians 5:23"> Now may the God of peace be with you all. So be it.</span>
