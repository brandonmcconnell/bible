<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 18:18; 1 Peter 1:22–23; 1 Timothy 5:9–10"> It is my desire to say a good word for Phoebe, who is a servant of the church in Cenchreae:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 2:29; Philippians 1:27; Matthew 10:40–42"> That you will take her in kindly, after the way of the saints, as one who is the Lord's, and give her help in anything in which she may have need of you: because she has been a help to a great number and to myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:19; 2 Timothy 4:19; Acts 18:26"> Give my love to Prisca and Aquila, workers with me in Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Philippians 2:30; 1 John 3:16; 1 Corinthians 16:1"> Who for my life put their necks in danger; to whom not only I but all the churches of the Gentiles are in debt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 4:15; Philemon 1:2; 1 Corinthians 16:19"> And say a kind word to the church which is in their house. Give my love to my dear Epaenetus, who is the first fruit of Asia to Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 16:12; Matthew 27:55; 1 Timothy 5:10"> Give my love to Mary, who gave much care to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 16:11; Romans 16:21; Philemon 1:23"> Give my love to Andronicus and Junia, my relations, who were in prison with me, who are noted among the Apostles, and who were in Christ before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 4:1; 1 John 3:14; Romans 16:5"> Give my love to Ampliatus, who is dear to me in the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 16:2–3; Romans 16:21"> Give my love to Urbanus, a worker in Christ with us, and to my dear Stachys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 14:18; 1 Peter 1:7; Deuteronomy 8:2"> Give my love to Apelles, who has the approval of Christ. Say a kind word to those who are of the house of Aristobulus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 16:21; Romans 16:7"> Give my love to Herodion, my relation. Say a kind word to those of the house of Narcissus, who are in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:12–13; Hebrews 6:10–11; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> Give my love to Tryphaena and Tryphosa, workers in the Lord. Give my love to my dear Persis, who did much work in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 15:21; 1 Timothy 5:2; 2 John 1:1"> Give my love to Rufus, one of the Lord's selection, and to his mother and mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 1:2; Romans 8:29; Hebrews 3:1"> Give my love to Asyncritus, Phlegon, Hermes, Patrobas, Hermas, and the brothers who are with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 16:2; Romans 1:7; Romans 16:14"> Give my love to Philologus and Julia, Nereus and his sister, and Olympas, and all the saints who are with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:26; 1 Corinthians 16:20; 2 Corinthians 13:12"> Give one another a holy kiss. All the churches of Christ send their love to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jude 1:19; 2 Thessalonians 3:6; 1 Timothy 6:3–5"> Now, it is my desire, brothers, that you will take note of those who are causing division and trouble among you, quite against the teaching which was given to you: and keep away from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 2:4; Matthew 7:15; 1 Timothy 6:5"> For such people are not servants of the Lord Christ, but of their stomachs; and by their smooth and well-said words the hearts of those who have no knowledge of evil are tricked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 10:16; 1 Corinthians 14:20; Philippians 2:15"> For all have knowledge of how you do what you are ordered. For this reason I have joy in you, but it is my desire that you may be wise in what is good, and without knowledge of evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:19; 1 John 3:8; Genesis 3:15"> And the God of peace will be crushing Satan under your feet before long. The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 20:4; Acts 17:5; Acts 13:1"> Timothy, who is working with me, sends his love to you, so do Lucius and Jason and Sosipater, my relations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Galatians 6:11; 1 Corinthians 16:21; Romans 16:8"> I, Tertius, who have done the writing of this letter, send love in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:14; Acts 19:22; 2 Timothy 4:20"> Gaius, with whom I am living, whose house is open to all the church, sends his love, so does Erastus, the manager of the accounts of the town, and Quartus, the brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> &#91;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:7; Romans 2:16; Colossians 1:26–27"> Now to him who is able to make you strong in agreement with the good news which I gave you and the preaching of Jesus Christ, in the light of the revelation of that secret which has been kept through times eternal,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 1:5; Galatians 3:8; Romans 15:18"> But is now made clear; and by the writings of the prophets, by the order of the eternal God, the knowledge of it has been given to all the nations, so that they may come under the rule of the faith;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:17; Romans 11:36; Revelation 7:10–12"> To the only wise God, through Jesus Christ, be the glory for ever. So be it.</span>
