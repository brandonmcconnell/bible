<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 2:25–29; Genesis 25:32; Ecclesiastes 6:11"> How then is the Jew better off? or what profit is there in circumcision?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 9:4; Acts 7:38; 1 Corinthians 9:17"> Much in every way: first of all because the words of God were given to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:13; Hebrews 4:2; Romans 9:6"> And if some have no faith, will that make the faith of God without effect?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 51:4; Psalms 116:11; Galatians 2:17"> In no way: but let God be true, though every man is seen to be untrue; as it is said in the Writings, That your words may be seen to be true, and you may be seen to be right when you are judged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 6:19; Galatians 3:15; Romans 9:18–20"> But if the righteousness of God is supported by our wrongdoing what is to be said? is it wrong for God to be angry (as men may say)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 18:25; Acts 17:31; Job 8:3"> In no way: because if it is so, how is God able to be the judge of all the world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 3:4; Romans 9:19–20; Acts 13:27–29"> But if, because I am untrue, God being seen to be true gets more glory, why am I to be judged as a sinner?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 6:1; Romans 6:15; Romans 5:20"> Let us not do evil so that good may come (a statement which we are falsely said by some to have made), because such behaviour will have its right punishment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:22; Romans 6:15; Isaiah 65:5"> What then? are we worse off than they? In no way: because we have before made it clear that Jews as well as Greeks are all under the power of sin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 53:1–3; Psalms 14:1–3; Romans 3:23"> As it is said in the holy Writings, There is not one who does righteousness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 14:2–4; Psalms 53:2; Titus 3:3"> Not one who has the knowledge of what is right, not one who is a searcher after God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 14:3; Ecclesiastes 7:20; Isaiah 64:6"> They have all gone out of the way, there is no profit in any of them; there is not one who does good, not so much as one:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 5:9; Psalms 140:3; Matthew 23:27–28"> Their throat is like an open place of death; with their tongues they have said what is not true: the poison of snakes is under their lips:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Psalms 109:17–18; Psalms 59:12"> Whose mouth is full of curses and bitter words:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7–8; Proverbs 1:16; Proverbs 6:18"> Their feet are quick in running after blood;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Destruction and trouble are in their ways;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 59:8; Romans 5:1; Luke 1:79"> And of the way of peace they have no knowledge:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 36:1; Proverbs 23:17; Proverbs 16:6"> There is no fear of God before their eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Galatians 3:22–23; Romans 2:12–18"> Now, we have knowledge that what the law says is for those who are under the law, so that every mouth may be stopped, and all men may be judged by God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Galatians 2:16; Acts 13:39; Romans 3:28"> Because by the works of the law no man is able to have righteousness in his eyes, for through the law comes the knowledge of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 10:43; Jeremiah 33:16; Philippians 3:9"> But now without the law there is a revelation of the righteousness of God, to which witness is given by the law and the prophets;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 10:12; Galatians 3:28; Romans 8:1"> That is, the righteousness of God through faith in Jesus Christ, to all those who have faith; and one man is not different from another,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; 1 John 1:8–10; Romans 3:9"> For all have done wrong and are far from the glory of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Peter 1:18–19; Titus 3:5–7; Colossians 1:14"> And they may have righteousness put to their credit, freely, by his grace, through the salvation which is in Christ Jesus:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 3:23–24; 1 Peter 1:18–20; 1 John 4:10"> Whom God has put forward as the sign of his mercy, through faith, by his blood, to make clear his righteousness when, in his pity, God let the sins of earlier times go without punishment;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Romans 4:5; Romans 3:30"> And to make clear his righteousness now, so that he might himself be upright, and give righteousness to him who has faith in Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 4:2; Romans 2:23; 1 Corinthians 1:29–31"> What reason, then, is there for pride? It is shut out. By what sort of law? of works? No, but by a law of faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Galatians 2:16; Galatians 3:8; Titus 3:7"> For this reason, then, a man may get righteousness by faith without the works of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; Ephesians 3:6; Zechariah 2:11"> Or is God the God of Jews only? is he not in the same way the God of Gentiles? Yes, of Gentiles:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Galatians 3:8; Galatians 3:20; Philippians 3:3"> If God is one; and he will give righteousness because of faith to those who have circumcision, and through faith to those who have not circumcision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Galatians 2:21; Matthew 5:17; Romans 13:8–10"> Do we, then, through faith make the law of no effect? in no way: but we make it clear that the law is important.</span>
