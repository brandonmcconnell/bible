<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:20–22; Romans 9:1–3; John 5:34"> Brothers, my heart's desire and my prayer to God for them is, that they may get salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 21:20; Romans 9:31–32; Proverbs 19:2"> For I give witness of them that they have a strong desire for God, but not with knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 3:9; Luke 16:15; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> Because, not having knowledge of God's righteousness, and desiring to give effect to their righteousness, they have not put themselves under the righteousness of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 3:24; Romans 8:3–4; Matthew 5:17–18"> For Christ is the end of the law for righteousness to everyone who has faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Ezekiel 20:11; Nehemiah 9:29"> For Moses says that the man who does the righteousness which is of the law will get life by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:11–14; Proverbs 30:4; Ephesians 4:8–10"> But the righteousness which is of faith says these words, Say not in your heart, Who will go up to heaven? (that is, to make Christ come down:)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 13:20; Revelation 1:18; Romans 4:25"> Or, Who will go down into the deep? (that is, to make Christ come again from the dead:)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:14; Romans 1:16–17; 1 Timothy 4:6"> But what does it say? The word is near you, in your mouth and in your heart: that is, the word of faith of which we are the preachers:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 12:8; Acts 16:31; Philippians 2:11"> Because, if you say with your mouth that Jesus is Lord, and have faith in your heart that God has made him come back from the dead, you will have salvation:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 4:15; Romans 10:9; Galatians 2:16"> For with the heart man has faith to get righteousness, and with the mouth he says that Jesus is Lord to get salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 28:16; Romans 9:33; 1 Peter 2:6"> Because it is said in the holy Writings, Whoever has faith in him will not be shamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Philippians 4:19; Revelation 17:14; Galatians 3:28"> And the Jew is not different from the Greek: for there is the same Lord of all, who is good to all who have hope in his name:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 2:21; Joel 2:32"> Because, Whoever will give worship to the name of the Lord will get salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 16:15–16; Acts 26:17–18; Titus 1:3"> But how will they give worship to him in whom they have no faith? and how will they have faith in him of whom they have not had news? and how will they have news without a preacher?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Nahum 1:15; Ephesians 2:17"> And how will there be preachers if they are not sent? As it is said, How beautiful are the feet of those who give the glad news of good things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1; Romans 3:3; John 12:38–40"> But they have not all given ear to the good news. For Isaiah says, Lord, who has had faith in our word?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 3:2; Galatians 3:5; 1 Thessalonians 2:13"> So faith comes by hearing, and hearing by the word of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 19:4; Romans 1:8; Matthew 24:14"> But I say, Did not the word come to their ears? Yes, certainly: Their sound has gone out into all the earth, and their words to the ends of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Romans 11:11; Romans 11:14"> But I say, Had Israel no knowledge? First Moses says, You will be moved to envy by that which is not a nation, and by a foolish people I will make you angry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 65:1–2; Romans 9:30; Isaiah 55:4–5"> And Isaiah says without fear, Those who were not searching for me made discovery of me; and I was seen by those whose hearts were turned away from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 65:2–5; 1 Peter 2:8; Jeremiah 35:15"> But about Israel he says; All the day my hands have been stretched out to a people whose hearts were turned away, and who put themselves against my word.</span>
