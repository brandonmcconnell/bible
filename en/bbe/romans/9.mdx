<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:7; Romans 1:9; Galatians 1:20"> I say what is true in Christ, and not what is false, my mind giving witness with me in the Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 3:18; Psalms 119:136; Jeremiah 13:17"> That I am full of sorrow and pain without end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 32:32; Esther 8:6; Romans 11:14"> For I have a desire to take on myself the curse for my brothers, my family in the flesh:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 2:12; Psalms 147:19; Romans 9:6"> Who are Israelites: who have the place of sons, and the glory, and the agreements with God, and the giving of the law, and the worship, and the hope offered by God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 10:30; John 1:1–3; Colossians 1:16–19"> Whose are the fathers, and of whom came Christ in the flesh, who is over all, God, to whom be blessing for ever. So be it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 2:28–29; Galatians 6:16; Romans 11:1–2"> But it is not as if the word of God was without effect. For they are not all Israel, who are of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 21:12; Hebrews 11:18; John 8:37–39"> And they are not all children because they are the seed of Abraham; but, In Isaac will your seed be named.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:13; Romans 4:11–16; Galatians 3:26–29"> That is, it is not the children of the flesh, but the children of God's undertaking, who are named as the seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 18:10; Genesis 18:14; Genesis 17:21"> For this is the word of God's undertaking, At this time will I come, and Sarah will have a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 25:21–23; Luke 16:26; Romans 5:11"> And not only so, but Rebecca being about to have a child by our father Isaac —</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Ephesians 1:4–5; 2 Peter 1:10"> Before the children had come into existence, or had done anything good or bad, in order that God's purpose and his selection might be effected, not by works, but by him whose purpose it is,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 25:22–23; 2 Samuel 8:14; 1 Kings 22:47"> It was said to her, The older will be the servant of the younger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Malachi 1:2–3; Deuteronomy 21:15; Genesis 29:33"> Even as it is said, I had love for Jacob, but for Esau I had hate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:7; Psalms 145:17; Deuteronomy 32:4"> What may we say then? is God not upright? let it not be said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 33:19; Romans 9:16; Micah 7:18"> For he says to Moses, I will have mercy on whom I will have mercy, and pity on whom I will have pity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ephesians 2:8; Matthew 11:25–26; 1 Peter 2:9–10"> So then, it is not by the desire or by the attempt of man, but by the mercy of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 9:16; Proverbs 16:4; Exodus 14:17–18"> For the holy Writings say to Pharaoh, For this same purpose did I put you on high, so that I might make my power seen in you, and that there might be knowledge of my name through all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 11:20; Exodus 4:21; Romans 9:15–16"> So then, at his pleasure he has mercy on a man, and at his pleasure he makes the heart hard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 4:35; 2 Chronicles 20:6; 1 Corinthians 15:12"> But you will say to me, Why does he still make us responsible? who is able to go against his purpose?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 29:16; Isaiah 45:9–11; Isaiah 64:8"> But, O man, who are you, to make answer against God? May the thing which is made say to him who made it, Why did you make me so?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; Proverbs 16:4; 2 Timothy 2:20–21"> Or has not the potter the right to make out of one part of his earth a vessel for honour, and out of another a vessel for shame?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 16:4; Jude 1:4; Romans 9:21"> What if God, desiring to let his wrath and his power be seen, for a long time put up with the vessels of wrath which were ready for destruction:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 2:4; 1 Peter 1:2–5; Colossians 1:27"> And to make clear the wealth of his glory to vessels of mercy, which he had before made ready for glory,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; Acts 13:47–48; Ephesians 3:6–8"> Even us, who were marked out by him, not only from the Jews, but from the Gentiles?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hosea 2:23; 1 Peter 2:10; John 16:27"> As he says in Hosea, They will be named my people who were not my people, and she will be loved who was not loved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hosea 1:9–10; 1 John 3:1–3; Galatians 3:26"> And in the place where it was said to them, You are not my people, there they will be named the sons of the living God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 10:20–23; Romans 11:4–6; Isaiah 1:9"> And Isaiah says about Israel, Even if the number of the children of Israel is as the sand of the sea, only a small part will get salvation:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 10:23; Revelation 19:11; Acts 17:31"> For the Lord will give effect to his word on the earth, putting an end to it and cutting it short.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 1:9; Isaiah 13:19; Amos 4:11"> And, as Isaiah had said before, If the Lord of armies had not given us a seed, we would have been like Sodom and Gomorrah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Philippians 3:9; Hebrews 11:7; Galatians 2:16"> What then may we say? That the nations who did not go after righteousness have got righteousness, even the righteousness which is of faith:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 11:7; Romans 3:20; James 2:10–11"> But Israel, going after a law of righteousness, did not get it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:23; 1 Peter 2:8; Luke 7:23"> Why? Because they were not searching for it by faith, but by works. They came up against the stone which was in the way;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 28:16; Isaiah 8:14–15; Romans 10:11"> As it is said, See, I am putting in Zion a stone causing a fall, and a rock in the way: but he who has faith in him will not be put to shame.</span>
