<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 15:1; 1 Corinthians 8:7–13; 1 Corinthians 9:22"> Do not put on one side him who is feeble in faith, and do not put him in doubt by your reasonings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:4; Romans 14:22–23; Romans 14:14"> One man has faith to take all things as food: another who is feeble in faith takes only green food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 14:10; Colossians 2:16–17; Luke 18:9"> Let not him who takes food have a low opinion of him who does not: and let not him who does not take food be a judge of him who does; for he has God's approval.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 4:11–12; Romans 9:20; Romans 8:31–39"> Who are you to make yourself a judge of another man's servant? it is to his master that he is responsible for good or bad. Yes, his place will be safe, because the Lord is able to keep him from falling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 2:16–17; Romans 14:23; Galatians 4:9–10"> This man puts one day before another: to that man they are the same. Let every man be certain in his mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:30–31; 1 Timothy 4:3–5; Matthew 14:19"> He who keeps the day, keeps it to the Lord; and he who takes food, takes it as to the Lord, for he gives praise to God; and he who does not take food, to the Lord he takes it not, and gives praise to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:2; 2 Corinthians 5:15; 1 Corinthians 6:19–20"> For every man's life and every man's death has a relation to others as well as to himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 1:20; 1 Thessalonians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 15:23"> As long as we have life we are living to the Lord; or if we give up our life it is to the Lord; so if we are living, or if our life comes to an end, we are the Lord's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 1:18; Luke 24:26; Hebrews 12:2"> And for this purpose Christ went into death and came back again, that he might be the Lord of the dead and of the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 4:5; Ecclesiastes 12:14"> But you, why do you make yourself your brother's judge? or again, why have you no respect for your brother? because we will all have to take our place before God as our judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 45:22–25; Philippians 2:10–11; Psalms 72:11"> For it is said in the holy Writings, By my life, says the Lord, to me every knee will be bent, and every tongue will give worship to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 4:5; Matthew 12:36; Matthew 16:27"> So every one of us will have to give an account of himself to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:3; James 4:11; 1 Corinthians 8:9–13"> Then let us not be judges of one another any longer: but keep this in mind, that no man is to make it hard for his brother, or give him cause for doubting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Romans 14:2; 1 Timothy 4:4"> I am conscious of this, and am certain in the Lord Jesus, that nothing is unclean in itself; but for the man in whose opinion it is unclean, for him it is unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:11–12; Philippians 2:2–4; Galatians 5:13"> And if because of food your brother is troubled, then you are no longer going on in the way of love. Do not let your food be destruction to him for whom Christ went into death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:22; 1 Corinthians 10:29–30"> Let it not be possible for men to say evil about your good:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Romans 15:13; 1 Corinthians 4:20"> For the kingdom of God is not food and drink, but righteousness and peace and joy in the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:21; 2 Corinthians 5:11; 1 Peter 2:20"> And he who in these things is Christ's servant, is pleasing to God and has the approval of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Romans 12:18; Psalms 34:14"> So then, let us go after the things which make peace, and the things by which we may be a help to one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 14:14–15; Matthew 18:6; Titus 1:15"> Do not let the work of God come to nothing on account of food. All things are certainly clean; but it is evil for that man who by taking food makes it hard for another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:13; Romans 14:13; Romans 15:1–2"> It is better not to take meat or wine or to do anything which might be a cause of trouble to your brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 John 3:21; Romans 14:5; James 3:13"> The faith which you have, have it to yourself before God. Happy is the man who is not judged by that to which he gives approval.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 11:6; Titus 1:15; 1 Corinthians 11:29–31"> But he who is in doubt is judged if he takes food, because he does it not in faith; and whatever is not of faith is sin.</span>
