<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 4:32; Leviticus 23:24; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> And when the seventh month came, and the children of Israel were in the towns, the people came together like one man to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:17; Ezra 2:2; Haggai 1:1"> Then Jeshua, the son of Jozadak, and his brothers the priests, and Zerubbabel, the son of Shealtiel, with his brothers, got up and made the altar of the God of Israel for burned offerings as is recorded in the law of Moses, the man of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezra 4:4; Numbers 28:2–8; Ezra 8:21–22"> They put the altar on its base; for fear was on them because of the people of the countries: and they made burned offerings on it to the Lord, even burned offerings morning and evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:14–17; Numbers 29:12–38; Exodus 23:16"> And they kept the feast of tents, as it is recorded, making the regular burned offerings every day by number, as it is ordered; for every day what was needed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 29:39; Numbers 28:19; Exodus 29:38–42"> And after that, the regular burned offering and the offerings for the new moons and all the fixed feasts of the Lord which had been made holy, and the offering of everyone who freely gave his offering to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> From the first day of the seventh month they made a start with the burned offerings, but the base of the Temple of the Lord had still not been put in its place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 5:6; Acts 9:36; Acts 12:20"> And they gave money to the stoneworkers and woodworkers; and meat and drink and oil to the people of Zidon and of Tyre, for the transport of cedar-trees from Lebanon to the sea, to Joppa, as Cyrus, king of Persia, had given them authority to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 4:3; 1 Chronicles 23:4; 1 Chronicles 23:24–32"> Now in the second year of their coming into the house of God in Jerusalem, in the second month, the work was taken in hand by Zerubbabel, the son of Shealtiel, and Jeshua, the son of Jozadak, and the rest of their brothers the priests and the Levites, and all those who had come from the land where they were prisoners to Jerusalem: and they made the Levites, of twenty years old and over, responsible for overseeing the work of the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezra 2:40; Ezra 3:8; Nehemiah 7:43"> Then Jeshua with his sons and his brothers, Kadmiel with his sons, the sons of Hodaviah, together took up the work of overseeing the workmen in the house of God: the sons of Henadad with their sons and their brothers, the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:1–7; 1 Chronicles 15:27; 1 Chronicles 16:37"> And when the builders put in position the base of the Temple of the Lord, the priests, dressed in their robes, took their places with horns, and the Levites, the sons of Asaph, with brass instruments, to give praise to the Lord in the way ordered by David, king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:34; Psalms 106:1; Psalms 107:1"> And they gave praise to the Lord, answering one another in their songs and saying, For he is good, for his mercy to Israel is eternal. And all the people gave a great cry of joy, when they gave praise to the Lord, because the base of the Lord's house was put in place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Haggai 2:3; Matthew 13:31–32; Isaiah 60:22"> But a number of the priests and Levites and the heads of families, old men who had seen the first house, when the base of this house was put down before their eyes, were overcome with weeping; and a number were crying out with joy:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 19:37–40; 1 Samuel 4:5; Nehemiah 12:43"> So that in the ears of the people the cry of joy was mixed with the sound of weeping; for the cries of the people were loud and came to the ears of those who were a long way off.</span>
