<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 27:6; 1 Samuel 31:6; 1 Samuel 30:17–26"> Now after the death of Saul, when David, having come back from the destruction of the Amalekites, had been in Ziklag for two days;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:12; 2 Samuel 4:10; Joshua 7:6"> On the third day a man came from Saul's tents, with his clothing out of order and earth on his head: and when he came to David, he went down on the earth and gave him honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 1:15–19; 2 Kings 5:25"> And David said to him, Where have you come from? And he said, I have come in flight from the tents of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:16; 1 Samuel 31:1–6; 1 Chronicles 10:1–6"> And David said to him, How did things go? Give me the news. And in answer he said, The people have gone in flight from the fight, and a great number of them are dead; and Saul and his son Jonathan are dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 25:2; Proverbs 14:15"> And David said to the young man who gave him the news, Why are you certain that Saul and his son Jonathan are dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:4; 2 Samuel 1:21; 1 Chronicles 10:4–6"> And the young man said, I came by chance to Mount Gilboa, and I saw Saul supporting himself on his spear; and the war-carriages and horsemen overtook him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:12; 2 Samuel 9:6; Judges 9:54"> And looking back, he saw me and gave a cry to me. And answering him I said, Here am I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:17; 1 Samuel 30:13; 1 Samuel 30:1"> And he said to me, Who are you? And I said, I am an Amalekite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Then he said to me, Come here to my side, and put me to death, for the pain of death has me in its grip but my life is still strong in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Judges 9:54; 2 Kings 11:12"> So I put my foot on him and gave him his death-blow, because I was certain that he would not go on living after his fall: and I took the crown from his head and the band from his arm, and I have them here for my lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:31; Genesis 37:29; 2 Samuel 3:31"> Then David gave way to bitter grief, and so did all the men who were with him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 24:17; Psalms 35:13–14; Matthew 5:44"> And till evening they gave themselves to sorrow and weeping, and took no food, weeping for Saul and for Jonathan, his son, and for the people of the Lord and for the men of Israel; because they had come to their end by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:8"> And David said to the young man who had given him the news, Where do you come from? And he said, I am the son of a man from a strange land; I am an Amalekite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:9; 1 Samuel 31:4; 2 Peter 2:10"> And David said to him, Had you no fear of stretching out your hand to put to death the one marked with the holy oil?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 2:34; Judges 8:20; 2 Samuel 4:10–12"> And David sent for one of his young men and said, Go near and put an end to him. And he put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; Luke 19:22; Leviticus 20:9"> And David said to him, May your blood be on your head; for your mouth has given witness against you, saying, I have put to death the man marked with the holy oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:25; 2 Samuel 1:19; Genesis 50:11"> Then David made this song of grief for Saul and Jonathan, his son:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 10:13; Genesis 49:8; Deuteronomy 4:10"> (It is recorded in the book of Jashar for teaching to the sons of Judah) and he said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:27; 2 Samuel 1:25; Deuteronomy 4:7–8"> The glory, O Israel, is dead on your high places! How have the great ones been made low!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Micah 1:10; 1 Samuel 18:6; Judges 11:34"> Give no news of it in Gath, let it not be said in the streets of Ashkelon; or the daughters of the Philistines will be glad, the daughters of men without circumcision will be uplifted in joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:1; Isaiah 21:5; 1 Samuel 10:1"> O mountains of Gilboa, let there be no dew or rain on you, you fields of death: for there the arms of the strong have been shamed, the arms of Saul, as if he had not been marked with the holy oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:4; Isaiah 34:6–7; 1 Samuel 14:6–14"> From the blood of the dead, from the fat of the strong, the bow of Jonathan was not turned back, the sword of Saul did not come back unused.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:13; Judges 14:18; Lamentations 4:19"> Saul and Jonathan were loved and pleasing; in their lives and in their death they were not parted; they went more quickly than eagles, they were stronger than lions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Peter 3:3–5; Jeremiah 2:32; Judges 5:30"> O daughters of Israel, have sorrow for Saul, by whom you were delicately clothed in robes of red, with ornaments of gold on your dresses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:19; 2 Samuel 1:27; Judges 5:18"> How have the great ones been made low in the fight! Jonathan is dead on your high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:17; 1 Samuel 23:16; 1 Samuel 20:41"> I am full of grief for you, my brother Jonathan: very dear have you been to me: your love for me was a wonder, greater than the love of women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:25; 2 Samuel 1:19; Ezekiel 39:9–10"> How have the great ones been made low, and the arms of war broken!</span>
