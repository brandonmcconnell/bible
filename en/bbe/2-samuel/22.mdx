<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 24:4; 1 Samuel 26:23; Psalms 7:8"> The Lord gives me the reward of my righteousness, because my hands are clean before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 8:32; Genesis 18:19; Psalms 128:1"> For I have kept the ways of the Lord; I have not been turned away in sin from my God.</span>

<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 72:12–13; Isaiah 5:15; James 4:6–7"> For you are the saviour of those who are in trouble; but your eyes are on men of pride, to make them low.</span>

<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 18:29; Philippians 4:13; Psalms 118:10–12"> By your help I have made a way through the wall which was shutting me in: by the help of my God I have gone over a wall.</span>

<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Psalms 21:8–9; 2 Samuel 5:18–25; 2 Samuel 10:14"> I go after my haters and overtake them; not turning back till they are all overcome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Malachi 4:3; Malachi 4:1; Psalms 110:1"> I have sent destruction on them and given them wounds, so that they are not able to get up: they are stretched under my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Psalms 44:5; Isaiah 60:14; Colossians 1:11"> For I have been armed by you with strength for the fight: you have made low under me those who came out against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 23:27; Joshua 10:24; 2 Thessalonians 1:8–9"> By you their backs are turned in flight, so that my haters are cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Isaiah 1:15; 1 Samuel 28:6; Micah 3:4"> They were crying out, but there was no one to come to their help: even to the Lord, but he gave them no answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Micah 7:10; Isaiah 10:6; 2 Kings 13:7"> Then they were crushed as small as the dust of the earth, stamped down under my feet like the waste of the streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:1–14; Isaiah 55:5; 2 Samuel 3:1"> You have made me free from the fightings of my people; you have made me the head of the nations: a people of whom I had no knowledge will be my servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Psalms 81:15; Psalms 66:3; Isaiah 56:3"> Men of other countries will, with false hearts, put themselves under my authority: from the time when my name comes to their ears, they will be ruled by me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Micah 7:17; James 1:11; Isaiah 64:6"> They will be wasted away, they will come out of their secret places shaking with fear.</span>

<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Psalms 140:1; 2 Samuel 7:8–9; Numbers 24:7"> He makes me free from my haters: I am lifted up over those who come up against me: you have made me free from the violent man.</span>
