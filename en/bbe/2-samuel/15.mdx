<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 1:5; 2 Samuel 12:11; 1 Samuel 8:11"> Now after this, Absalom got for himself a carriage and horses, and fifty runners to go before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 18:14; Ruth 4:1; Job 24:14"> And Absalom got up early, morning after morning, and took his place at the side of the public meeting-place: and when any man had a cause which had to come to the king to be judged, then Absalom, crying out to him, said, What is your town? and he would say, Your servant is of one of the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:15; Ezekiel 22:7; 1 Peter 2:17"> And Absalom would say to him, See, your cause is true and right; but no man has been named by the king to give you a hearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 9:29; Judges 9:1–5; Proverbs 27:2"> And more than this, Absalom said, If only I was made judge in the land, so that every man who has any cause or question might come to me, and I would give a right decision for him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:33; Psalms 55:21; Proverbs 26:25"> And if any man came near to give him honour, he took him by the hand and gave him a kiss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 16:18; 2 Peter 2:3; Proverbs 11:9"> And this Absalom did to everyone in Israel who came to the king to have his cause judged: so Absalom, like a thief, took away the hearts of the men of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 58:4; 1 Samuel 16:13; 1 Samuel 16:1–2"> Now at the end of four years, Absalom said to the king, Let me go to Hebron and give effect to the oath which I made to the Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 28:20–21; 2 Samuel 13:37–38; 1 Samuel 1:11"> For while I was living in Geshur in Aram, your servant made an oath, saying, If ever the Lord lets me come back to Jerusalem, I will give him worship in Hebron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And the king said to him, Go in peace. So he got up and went to Hebron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:38; 2 Samuel 5:5; 2 Samuel 3:2–3"> But Absalom at the same time sent watchers through all the tribes of Israel to say, At the sound of the horn you are to say, Absalom is king in Hebron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:13; 1 Samuel 22:15; Romans 16:18–19"> And with Absalom, at his request, went two hundred men from Jerusalem, who were completely unconscious of his designs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; 2 Samuel 15:31; Joshua 15:51"> And Absalom sent for Ahithophel the Gilonite, one of David's helpers, from Giloh his town, while he was making the offerings. And the design against David became strong, for more and more people were joined to Absalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 9:3; Matthew 27:22; 2 Samuel 15:6"> And one came to David and said, The hearts of the men of Israel have gone after Absalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:9; Ezekiel 46:18; Psalms 55:3–11"> And David said to all his servants who were with him at Jerusalem, Come, let us go in flight, or not one of us will be safe from Absalom: let us go without loss of time, or he will overtake us quickly and send evil on us, and put the town to the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 6:66–69; Proverbs 18:24; Luke 22:28–29"> And the king's servants said to the king, See, your servants are ready to do whatever the king says is to be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:21–22; 2 Samuel 20:3; Psalms 3:1"> So the king went out, taking with him all the people of his house, but for ten of his women, who were to take care of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 3:1–2; Ecclesiastes 10:7; Psalms 66:12"> And the king went out, and all his servants went after him, and made a stop at the Far House.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:18; 1 Samuel 30:14; 2 Samuel 20:7"> And all the people went on by his side; and all the Cherethites and all the Pelethites and all the men of Ittai of Gath, six hundred men who came after him from Gath, went on before the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:2; Ruth 1:11–13"> Then the king said to Ittai the Gittite, Why are you coming with us? go back and keep with the king: for you are a man of another country, you are far from the land of your birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:13; 2 Samuel 2:6; John 1:17"> It was only yesterday you came to us; why then am I to make you go up and down with us? for I have to go where I may; go back then, and take your countrymen with you, and may the Lord's mercy and good faith be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ruth 1:16–17; Proverbs 17:17; 2 Kings 4:30"> And Ittai the Gittite in answer said, By the living Lord, and by the life of my lord the king, in whatever place my lord the king may be, for life or death, there will your servant be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And David said to Ittai, Go forward, then. And Ittai the Gittite went on, with all his men and all the little ones he had with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:2; 1 Kings 2:37; John 18:1"> And there was great weeping in all the country when all the people went through; and the king himself was waiting in the Kidron valley and all the people went by him in the direction of the olive-tree on the edge of the waste land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; 2 Samuel 8:17; 2 Samuel 20:25"> Then Zadok came, and Abiathar, and with them the ark of God's agreement: and they put down the ark of God, till all the people from the town had gone by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:30; Psalms 26:8; Psalms 43:3–4"> And the king said to Zadok, Take the ark of God back into the town: if I have grace in the eyes of the Lord, he will let me come back and see it and his House again:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:18; 2 Samuel 22:20; 1 Kings 10:9"> But if he says, I have no delight in you: then, here I am; let him do to me what seems good to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:9; 2 Samuel 17:17; 2 Samuel 24:11"> The king said further to Zadok the priest, See, you and Abiathar are to go back to the town in peace, with your two sons, Ahimaaz, your son, and Jonathan, the son of Abiathar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:16; 2 Samuel 15:23; 2 Samuel 17:1"> See, I will be waiting at the way across the river, in the waste land, till I get news from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> So Zadok and Abiathar took the ark of God back to Jerusalem, and did not go away from there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Esther 6:12; 2 Samuel 19:4; Luke 19:41"> And David went up the slopes of the Mount of Olives weeping all the way, with his head covered and no shoes on his feet: and all the people who were with him, covering their heads, went up weeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:14; 2 Samuel 17:23; James 3:15"> And word came to David, saying, Ahithophel is among those who are joined to Absalom. And David said, O Lord, let the wisdom of Ahithophel be made foolish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Joshua 16:2; 2 Samuel 1:2; Psalms 3:3–5"> Now when David had come to the top of the slope, where they gave worship to God, Hushai the Archite came to him in great grief with dust on his head:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:35"> David said to him, If you go on with me, you will be a trouble to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Joshua 8:2; 2 Samuel 16:16–19; Matthew 10:16"> But if you go back to the town and say to Absalom, I will be your servant, O king; as in the past I have been your father's servant, so now I will be yours: then you will be able to keep Ahithophel's designs against me from being put into effect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:15–16"> And have you not there Zadok and Abiathar the priests? so whatever comes to your ears from the king's house, give word of it to Zadok and Abiathar the priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:17; 2 Samuel 15:27; 2 Samuel 18:19–33"> See, they have with them their two sons, Ahimaaz, Zadok's son, and Jonathan, the son of Abiathar; by them you may send word to me of everything which comes to your ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:15–16; 1 Chronicles 27:33"> So Hushai, David's friend, went into the town, and Absalom came to Jerusalem.</span>
