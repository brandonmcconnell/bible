<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:12–13; 2 Samuel 7:8–9; Psalms 89:20"> Now these are the last words of David. David, the son of Jesse, says, the man who was lifted up on high, the man on whom the God of Jacob put the holy oil, the loved one of Israel's songs, says:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Peter 1:21; Matthew 22:43; Mark 12:36"> The spirit of the Lord had voice through me, his word was on my tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:32; Deuteronomy 32:4; Exodus 18:21"> The God of Israel said, the word of the Rock of Israel came to me: When an upright king is ruling over men, when he is ruling in the fear of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 5:31; Psalms 72:6; Hosea 6:5"> It is as the light of the morning, when the sun comes up, a morning without clouds; making young grass come to life from the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 55:3; 2 Samuel 7:14–16; Isaiah 9:6–7"> For is not my house so with God? For he has made with me an eternal agreement, ordered in all things and certain: as for all my salvation and all my desire, will he not give it increase?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:6; Isaiah 33:12; Deuteronomy 13:13"> But the evil-doers, all of them, will be like thorns to be pushed away, because they may not be gripped in the hand:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 6:8; 2 Thessalonians 2:8; Isaiah 27:4"> But anyone touching them has to be armed with iron and the rod of a spear; and they will be burned with fire, every one of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:2; 1 Chronicles 11:11–47; 1 Chronicles 27:32"> These are the names of David's men of war: Ishbaal the Hachmonite, chief of the three; his axe was lifted up against eight hundred put to death at one time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:4; 1 Chronicles 11:12–14; 1 Samuel 17:26"> After him was Eleazar, the son of Dodai the Ahohite, one of the three great fighters, who was with David in Pas-dammim when the Philistines came together there for the fight; and when the men of Israel had gone in flight,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:5; 1 Samuel 11:13; Judges 15:14"> He was with David and went on fighting the Philistines till his hand became tired and stiff from gripping his sword: and that day the Lord gave a great salvation, and the people came back after him only to take the goods of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:13–14; 1 Chronicles 11:27; 2 Samuel 23:33"> After him was Shammah, the son of Ela the Hararite. And the Philistines came together in Lehi, where there was a bit of land full of seed; and the people went in flight from the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 21:31; 2 Samuel 23:10; Psalms 3:8"> But he kept his place in the middle of the bit of land, and kept back their attack and overcame the Philistines: and the Lord gave a great salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:1; 2 Samuel 5:18; Joshua 12:15"> And three of the thirty went down at the start of the grain-cutting, and they came to David at the strong place of Adullam; and the band of Philistines had taken up their position in the valley of Rephaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:4–5; 1 Samuel 13:4; 1 Samuel 10:5"> And at that time David had taken cover in the strong place, and an armed force of the Philistines was in Beth-lehem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 4:14; Psalms 42:1–2; Isaiah 41:17"> And David, moved by a strong desire, said, If only someone would give me a drink of water from the water-hole of Beth-lehem, by the doorway into the town!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 35:14; 2 Corinthians 5:14; Philippians 2:17"> And the three men, forcing their way through the Philistine army, got water from the water-hole of Beth-lehem, by the doorway into the town, and took it back to David: but he would not take it, but, draining it out, made an offering of it to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 17:10; Mark 14:24; 1 Corinthians 15:30"> And he said, Far be it from me, O Lord, to do this; how may I take as my drink the life-blood of men who have put their lives in danger? So he would not take it. These things did the three great men of war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:10; 2 Samuel 10:14; 1 Chronicles 11:20–21"> And Abishai, the brother of Joab, the son of Zeruiah, was chief of the thirty. He put to death three hundred with his spear, and he got for himself a name among the thirty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 13:8; Matthew 13:23; 1 Corinthians 15:41"> Was he not the noblest of the thirty? so he was made their captain: but he was not equal to the first three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:18; Joshua 15:21; 2 Samuel 20:23"> And Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, a fighting man of Kabzeel, had done great acts; he put to death the two sons of Ariel of Moab: he went down into a hole and put a lion to death in time of snow:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:51; 1 Chronicles 11:23; Colossians 2:15"> And he made an attack on an Egyptian, a tall man: and the Egyptian had a spear in his hand; but he went down to him with a stick, and pulling the spear out of the hands of the Egyptian, put him to death with that same spear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> These were the acts of Benaiah, the son of Jehoiada, who had a great name among the thirty men of war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:23; 1 Chronicles 27:6; 1 Samuel 22:14"> He was honoured over the rest of the thirty, but he was not equal to the first three. And David put him over the fighting men who kept him safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:18; 1 Chronicles 27:7; 1 Chronicles 11:26"> Asahel, the brother of Joab, was one of the thirty; and Elhanan, the son of Dodai, of Beth-lehem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Judges 7:1; 1 Chronicles 11:27–28"> Shammah the Harodite, Elika the Harodite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:2; 1 Chronicles 11:27–28; 1 Chronicles 27:9–10"> Helez the Paltite, Ira, the son of Ikkesh the Tekoite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 21:18; 1 Chronicles 11:19; 1 Chronicles 11:28"> Abiezer the Anathothite, Sibbecai the Hushathite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:13; 2 Kings 25:23; 1 Chronicles 11:30"> Zalmon the Ahohite, Maharai the Netophathite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:30–31; Joshua 18:28; 1 Chronicles 27:15"> Heldai, the son of Baanah the Netophathite, Ittai, the son of Ribai of Gibeah of the children of Benjamin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 12:15; 1 Chronicles 27:14; Judges 12:13"> Benaiah the Pirathonite, Hiddai of the valleys of Gaash,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:16; 1 Chronicles 11:32–33"> Abiel the Arbathite, Azmaveth of Bahurim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:34"> Eliahba the Shaalbonite, Jashen the Gunite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:27; 2 Samuel 23:11; 1 Chronicles 11:35"> Jonathan, the son of Shammah the Hararite, Ahiam, the son of Sharar the Hararite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Samuel 11:3; 2 Samuel 15:12; 2 Samuel 10:6"> Eliphelet, the son of Ahasbai the Maacathite, Eliam, the son of Ahithophel the Gilonite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:37; Joshua 15:55"> Hezrai the Carmelite, Paarai the Archite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:3; 1 Chronicles 11:38"> Igal, the son of Nathan of Zobah, Bani the Gadite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:37; 1 Chronicles 11:39; 2 Samuel 4:2"> Zelek the Ammonite, Naharai the Beerothite, who had the care of the arms of Joab, son of Zeruiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:53; 2 Samuel 20:26; 1 Chronicles 11:40"> Ira the Ithrite, Gareb the Ithrite,</span>
