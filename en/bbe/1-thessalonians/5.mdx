<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 1:7; 1 Thessalonians 4:9; Matthew 24:36"> But about the times and their order, my brothers, there is no need for me to say anything to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 16:15; 2 Peter 3:10; Matthew 24:42–44"> For you yourselves have the knowledge that the day of the Lord will come like a thief in the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 21:34–35; Matthew 24:37–39; Luke 17:26–30"> When they say, There is peace and no danger, then sudden destruction will come on them, as birth-pains on a woman with child; and they will not be able to get away from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 2:8; 2 Peter 3:10; Deuteronomy 19:6"> But you, my brothers, are not in the dark, for that day to overtake you like a thief:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Acts 26:18; John 12:36"> For you are all sons of light and of the day: we are not of the night or of the dark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 1:13; Matthew 24:42; Luke 22:46"> So then, let us not take our rest as the others do, but let us be self-controlled and awake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 13:13; 1 Corinthians 15:34; 2 Peter 2:13"> For those who are sleeping do so in the night; and those who are the worse for drink are so in the night;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 13:12–13; Ephesians 6:11; 1 Peter 1:13"> But let us, who are of the day, be serious, putting on the breastplate of faith and love, and on our heads, the hope of salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:10; 1 Peter 2:10; 2 Timothy 2:10"> For God's purpose for us is not wrath, but salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 14:8–9; 1 Peter 3:18; Romans 8:34"> Who was put to death for us, so that, awake or sleeping, we may have a part in his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Hebrews 10:25; Romans 15:2"> So then, go on comforting and building up one another, as you have been doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 13:17; Hebrews 13:7; 1 Timothy 5:17–18"> But we make this request to you, my brothers: give attention to those who are working among you, who are over you in the Lord to keep order among you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 9:50; Hebrews 12:14; Galatians 6:6"> And have a high opinion of them in love because of their work. Be at peace among yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:2; Romans 14:1; Acts 20:35"> And our desire is that you will keep control over those whose lives are not well ordered, giving comfort to the feeble-hearted, supporting those with little strength, and putting up with much from all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Galatians 6:10; Leviticus 19:18"> Let no one give evil for evil; but ever go after what is good, for one another and for all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:12; Philippians 4:4; Matthew 5:12"> Have joy at all times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 12:12; Colossians 4:2; Ephesians 6:18"> Keep on with your prayers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 5:20; Philippians 4:6; Colossians 3:17"> In everything give praise: for this is the purpose of God in Christ Jesus for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 4:30; Acts 7:51; 2 Timothy 1:6"> Do not put out the light of the Spirit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:3–6; 1 Corinthians 14:1; Numbers 11:25–29"> Do not make little of the words of the prophets;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 4:1; Romans 12:2; Ephesians 5:10"> Let all things be tested; keep to what is good;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:3; Philippians 4:8; 1 Corinthians 10:31–33"> Keep from every form of evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jude 1:24; Hebrews 4:12; 1 Corinthians 1:8–9"> And may the God of peace himself make you holy in every way; and may your spirit and soul and body be free from all sin at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:3; Numbers 23:19; 1 Corinthians 1:9"> God, by whom you have been marked out in his purpose, is unchanging and will make it complete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 13:18–19; Philippians 1:19; Philemon 1:22"> Brothers, keep us in mind in your prayers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 16:16; 1 Corinthians 16:20"> Give all the brothers a holy kiss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 4:16; Acts 19:13; 1 Timothy 1:3"> I give orders in the name of the Lord that all the brothers are to be present at the reading of this letter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 16:20; 2 Thessalonians 3:18; Romans 16:23"> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you.</span>
