<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 17:15; 1 Thessalonians 3:5; 1 Thessalonians 2:17"> At last our desire to have news of you was so strong that, while we ourselves were waiting at Athens,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 14:22–23; Ephesians 6:21–22; Philippians 2:19–25"> We sent Timothy, our brother and God's servant in the good news of Christ, to give you strength and comfort in your faith;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 14:22; Romans 5:3; Acts 9:16"> So that no man might be moved by these troubles; because you see that these things are part of God's purpose for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:14; 2 Corinthians 8:1–2; 1 Thessalonians 2:2"> And when we were with you, we said to you that trouble was before us; and so it came about, as you see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 1:13–14; Matthew 4:3; 2 Corinthians 2:11"> For this reason, when I was no longer able to keep quiet, I sent to get news of your faith, fearing that you might be tested by the Evil One and that our work might come to nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:2; Isaiah 52:7; Proverbs 25:25"> But now that Timothy has come to us from you, and has given us good news of your faith and love, and that you have happy memories of us, desiring greatly to see us, even as we do to see you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:4; 2 John 1:4; 1 Thessalonians 3:8–9"> For this cause, brothers, in all our trouble and grief we were comforted about you because of your faith;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Colossians 1:23; 1 Corinthians 16:13; Revelation 3:11"> For it is life to us if you keep your faith in the Lord unchanged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 71:14–15; Psalms 96:12–13; 2 Corinthians 9:15"> For how great is the praise which we give to God for you, and how great the joy with which we are glad because of you before our God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:9; 1 Thessalonians 3:11; Philippians 1:25"> Night and day requesting God again and again that we may see your face and make your faith complete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Matthew 6:32; 2 Thessalonians 2:16"> Now may our God and Father himself and our Lord Jesus make a way for us to come to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Philippians 1:9; 1 John 4:7–16; Matthew 22:39"> And the Lord give you increase of love in fullest measure to one another and to all men, even as our love to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 1:22; 1 Thessalonians 5:23; Ephesians 5:27"> So that your hearts may be strong and free from all sin before our God and Father, at the coming of our Lord Jesus with all his saints.</span>
