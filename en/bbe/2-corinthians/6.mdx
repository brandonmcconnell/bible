<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:9; Hebrews 12:15; 1 Peter 4:10"> We then, working together with God, make our request to you not to take the grace of God to no purpose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 49:8; Hebrews 4:7; Hebrews 3:13"> (For he says, I have given ear to you at a good time, and I have been your helper in a day of salvation: see, now is the good time; now is the day of salvation):</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 14:13; 1 Corinthians 9:12; Matthew 17:27"> Giving no cause for trouble in anything, so that no one may be able to say anything against our work;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:10; 2 Corinthians 4:8; 2 Corinthians 11:27"> But in everything making it clear that we are the servants of God, in quiet strength, in troubles, in need, in sorrow,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:23–25; 2 Corinthians 11:27; Acts 19:23–34"> In blows, in prisons, in attacks, in hard work, in watchings, in going without food;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Colossians 1:27; Galatians 5:22"> In a clean heart, in knowledge, in long waiting, in being kind, in the Holy Spirit, in true love,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:2; 2 Timothy 2:15; Ephesians 6:11–20"> In the true word, in the power of God; with the arms of righteousness on the right hand and on the left,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 27:63; 1 Peter 4:14; 1 Timothy 4:10"> By glory and by shame, by an evil name and a good name; as untrue, and still true;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:10–11; Romans 8:36; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> Unnoted, but still kept fully in mind; as near to death, but still living; as undergoing punishment, but not put to death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 16:22; 2 Corinthians 8:9; Revelation 2:9"> As full of sorrow, but ever glad; as poor, but giving wealth to others; as having nothing, but still having all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:3–4; Philippians 1:8; Psalms 119:32"> Our mouth is open to you, O Corinthians, our heart is wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:2; Micah 2:7; Philippians 1:8"> It is not our feelings to you which are narrow, but yours to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="3 John 1:4; 1 John 3:18; 1 Thessalonians 2:11"> Now to give me back payment of the same sort (I am talking as to my children), let your hearts be wide open to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; 1 Corinthians 10:21; James 4:4"> Do not keep company with those who have not faith: for what is there in common between righteousness and evil, or between light and dark?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 18:21; 1 Corinthians 10:20–21; 1 John 5:11–13"> And what agreement is there between Christ and the Evil One? or what part has one who has faith with one who has not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 21:3; Exodus 29:45; Hebrews 8:10"> And what agreement has the house of God with images? for we are a house of the living God; even as God has said, I will be living among them, and walking with them; and I will be their God, and they will be my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; Revelation 18:4; 2 Corinthians 7:1"> For which cause, Come out from among them, and be separate, says the Lord, and let no unclean thing come near you; and I will take you for myself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 21:7; John 1:12; 2 Samuel 7:14"> And will be a Father to you; and you will be my sons and daughters, says the Lord, the Ruler of all.</span>
