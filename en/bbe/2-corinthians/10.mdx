<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:10; Matthew 11:29; Galatians 5:2"> Now I, Paul, myself make request to you by the quiet and gentle behaviour of Christ, I who am poor in spirit when with you, but who say what is in my mind to you without fear when I am away from you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:10; 2 Corinthians 13:2; Galatians 5:16–25"> Yes, I make my request to you, so that when I am with you I may not have to make use of the authority which may be needed against some to whom we seem to be walking after the flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4; Galatians 2:20; 1 Peter 4:1–2"> For though we may be living in the flesh, we are not fighting after the way of the flesh</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:7; Ephesians 6:13–18; Jeremiah 1:10"> (For the arms with which we are fighting are not those of the flesh, but are strong before God for the destruction of high places);</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Isaiah 55:7; 1 Peter 1:14–15"> Putting an end to reasonings, and every high thing which is lifted up against the knowledge of God, and causing every thought to come under the authority of Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:9; Numbers 16:26–30; 2 Corinthians 7:15"> Being ready to give punishment to whatever is against his authority, after you have made it clear that you are completely under his control.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:12; John 7:24; 1 Corinthians 14:37"> Give attention to the things which are before you. If any man seems to himself to be Christ's, let him keep in mind that we are as much Christ's as he is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:10; 2 Corinthians 12:6; Galatians 1:1"> For though I might take pride in our authority (which the Lord gave for building you up, and not for your destruction), it will not be a cause of shame to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:10; 1 Corinthians 4:19–21; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> That I may not seem to have the desire of causing you fear by my letters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:17; Exodus 4:10; Galatians 4:13–14"> For his letters, they say, have weight and are strong; but in body he is feeble, and his way of talking has little force.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:20; 2 Corinthians 13:2–3; 2 Corinthians 13:10"> Let those who say this keep in mind that, what we are in word by letters when we are away, so will we be in act when we are present.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 27:2; Proverbs 26:12; Luke 18:11"> For we will not make comparison of ourselves with some of those who say good things about themselves: but these, measuring themselves by themselves, and making comparison of themselves with themselves, are not wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 12:6; Romans 15:20; 1 Peter 4:10"> We will not give glory to ourselves in over-great measure, but after the measure of the rule which God has given us, a measure which comes even to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:15; 1 Timothy 1:11; Romans 1:16"> For we have no need to make ourselves seem more than we are, as if our authority did not come as far as to you: for we came even as far as you with the good news of Christ:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 15:20; 2 Thessalonians 1:3; 2 Corinthians 10:13"> Not taking credit to ourselves for what is not our business, that is, for the work of others; but having hope that, with the growth of your faith, we may get the credit for an increase which is the effect of our work,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 15:24–28; Acts 19:21"> So that we may be able to go on and take the good news to countries still farther away than you are, and not take credit for another man's work in making things ready to our hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23–24; 1 Corinthians 1:31; James 1:9–10"> But whoever has a desire for glory, let his glory be in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 16:15; Proverbs 21:2; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> For the Lord's approval of a man is not dependent on his opinion of himself, but on the Lord's opinion of him.</span>
