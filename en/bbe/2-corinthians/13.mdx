<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 18:16; Deuteronomy 19:15; Deuteronomy 17:6"> This is the third time that I am coming to you. From the mouth of two or three witnesses will every word be made certain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:23; 2 Corinthians 12:20–21; 1 Corinthians 5:5"> I said before, and still say it before I come, as being present for the second time, though I am still away from you, to those who have done wrong before, and to all the others, that if I come again I will not have pity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 10:20; 2 Corinthians 12:12; Luke 21:15"> Seeing that you are looking for a sign of Christ giving out his word in me; who is not feeble in relation to you, but is strong in you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 3:18; Romans 6:4; 2 Corinthians 10:3–4"> For he was feeble in that he was put to death on the cross, but he is living by the power of God. And we are feeble in him, but we will be living with him through the power of God in relation to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40; 1 Corinthians 11:28; Psalms 139:23–24"> Make a test of yourselves, if you are in the faith; make certain of yourselves. Or are you not conscious in yourselves that Jesus Christ is in you, if you are truly Christ's?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:20; 2 Corinthians 13:10; 2 Corinthians 13:3–4"> But it is my hope that you will have no doubt that we are truly Christ's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:12; 1 Timothy 2:2; 2 Corinthians 6:8–9"> Now our prayer to God is that you may do no evil; not in order that it may be put to our credit, but so that you may do what is right, whatever we may seem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 21:30; Mark 9:39; Acts 4:28–30"> Because we are able to do nothing against what is true, but only for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:11; 1 Corinthians 4:10; 1 Thessalonians 3:10"> For we are glad when we are feeble and you are strong: and this is our prayer, even that you may be made complete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:8; 2 Corinthians 2:3; Titus 1:13"> For this cause I am writing these things while I am away, so that there may be need for me, when I am present, to make use of sharp measures, by the authority which the Lord has given me for building up and not for destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 12:14; Romans 12:16; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> Let this be my last word, brothers; be glad; be complete; be comforted; be of the same mind; be at peace with one another: and the God of love and peace will be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 16:16; 1 Peter 5:14; 1 Thessalonians 5:26"> Give one another a holy kiss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 16:16; Romans 16:21–23; Hebrews 13:24"> All the saints send their love to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 2:22; Romans 5:5; Romans 16:20"> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, and the love of God, and the harmony of the Holy Spirit, be with you all.</span>
