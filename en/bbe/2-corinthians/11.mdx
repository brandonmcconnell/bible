<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:4; 1 Corinthians 1:21; 2 Corinthians 11:19"> Put up with me if I am a little foolish: but, truly, you do put up with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 2:19–20; Ephesians 5:26–27; Isaiah 54:5"> For I have a very great care for you: because you have been married by me to one husband, and it is my desire to give you completely holy to Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; Galatians 1:6; Ephesians 4:14"> But I have a fear, that in some way, as Eve was tricked by the deceit of the snake, your minds may be turned away from their simple and holy love for Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 1:6–8; 1 Corinthians 3:11; 1 Timothy 2:5"> For if anyone comes preaching another Jesus from the one whose preachers we are, or if you have got a different spirit, or a different sort of good news from those which came to you, how well you put up with these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:11–12; 1 Corinthians 15:10; Galatians 2:6–9"> For in my opinion, I am in no way less than the most important of the Apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 3:4; 1 Corinthians 1:17; 2 Corinthians 4:2"> But though I am rough in my way of talking, I am not so in knowledge, as we have made clear to all by our acts among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:13; 1 Thessalonians 2:9; 2 Corinthians 10:1"> Or did I do wrong in making myself low so that you might be lifted up, because I gave you the good news of God without reward?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 4:18; 2 Corinthians 11:9; Philippians 4:14–16"> I took money from other churches as payment for my work, so that I might be your servant;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:8–9; 1 Thessalonians 2:9; 1 Thessalonians 2:6"> And when I was present with you, and was in need, I let no man be responsible for me; for the brothers, when they came from Macedonia, gave me whatever was needed; and in everything I kept myself from being a trouble to you, and I will go on doing so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 9:1; Romans 1:9; Acts 18:12"> As the true word of Christ is in me, I will let no man take from me this my cause of pride in the country of Achaia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:15; 2 Corinthians 6:11–12; 2 Corinthians 7:3"> Why? because I have no love for you? let God be judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:12; 1 Corinthians 5:6; 2 Corinthians 5:12"> But what I do, that I will go on doing, so that I may give no chance to those who are looking for one; so that, in the cause of their pride, they may be seen to be the same as we are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 1:7; Revelation 2:2; 1 John 4:1"> For such men are false Apostles, workers of deceit, making themselves seem like Apostles of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 1:8; 2 Corinthians 2:11; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> And it is no wonder; for even Satan himself is able to take the form of an angel of light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philippians 3:19; Revelation 9:11; Isaiah 9:14–15"> So it is no great thing if his servants make themselves seem to be servants of righteousness; whose end will be the reward of their works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:1; 2 Corinthians 12:6; 2 Corinthians 12:11"> I say again, Let me not seem foolish to anyone; but if I do, put up with me as such, so that I may take a little glory to myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:12; Philippians 3:3–6; 2 Corinthians 9:4"> What I am now saying is not by the order of the Lord, but as a foolish person, taking credit to myself, as it seems.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:10; 2 Corinthians 11:21–23; 2 Corinthians 10:12–18"> Seeing that there are those who take credit to themselves after the flesh, I will do the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:10; 1 Corinthians 10:15; 1 Corinthians 8:1"> For you put up with the foolish gladly, being wise yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Galatians 2:4; Galatians 4:3; Galatians 5:1"> You put up with a man if he makes servants of you, if he makes profit out of you, if he makes you prisoners, if he puts himself in a high place, if he gives you blows on the face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:10; 2 Corinthians 11:17; 2 Corinthians 10:1–2"> I say this by way of shaming ourselves, as if we had been feeble. But if anyone puts himself forward (I am talking like a foolish person), I will do the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Philippians 3:5; Romans 11:1; Romans 9:4"> Are they Hebrews? so am I. Are they of Israel? so am I. Are they the seed of Abraham? so am I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:4–5; 2 Corinthians 1:9–10; 2 Corinthians 6:9"> Are they servants of Christ? (I am talking foolishly) I am more so; I have had more experience of hard work, of prisons, of blows more than measure, of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Mark 13:9; Deuteronomy 25:2–3; Matthew 10:17"> Five times the Jews gave me forty blows but one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 14:19; Acts 16:37; Hebrews 11:37"> Three times I was whipped with rods, once I was stoned, three times the ship I was in came to destruction at sea, a night and a day I have been in the water;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 14:5; Acts 17:5; Acts 13:50"> In frequent travels, in dangers on rivers, in dangers from outlaws, in dangers from my countrymen, in dangers from the Gentiles, in dangers in the town, in dangers in the waste land, in dangers at sea, in dangers among false brothers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Philippians 4:12; 2 Corinthians 6:5; 1 Thessalonians 2:9"> In hard work and weariness, in frequent watchings, going without food and drink, cold and in need of clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:17; Romans 15:16; Acts 20:18–35"> In addition to all the other things, there is that which comes on me every day, the care of all the churches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:13; 1 Corinthians 9:22; Galatians 4:8–20"> Who is feeble and I am not feeble? who is in danger of falling, and I am not angry?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:5–11; Proverbs 25:27; 1 Corinthians 2:3"> If I have to take credit to myself, I will do so in the things in which I am feeble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 9:5; Ephesians 1:3; John 10:30"> The God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, to whom be praise for ever, is witness that the things which I say are true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 9:24–25; 2 Corinthians 11:26"> In Damascus, the ruler under Aretas the king kept watch over the town of the people of Damascus, in order to take me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 9:25; 1 Samuel 19:12; Joshua 2:18"> And being let down in a basket from the wall through a window, I got free from his hands.</span>
