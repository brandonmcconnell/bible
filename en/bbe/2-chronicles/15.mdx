<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:20; 2 Chronicles 20:14; Numbers 24:2"> And the spirit of God came on Azariah, the son of Oded;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 4:8; 2 Chronicles 24:20; 2 Chronicles 15:4"> And he came face to face with Asa and said to him, Give ear to me, Asa and all Judah and Benjamin: the Lord is with you while you are with him; if your heart's desire is for him, he will be near you, but if you give him up, he will give you up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:9; Leviticus 10:11; 2 Chronicles 17:8–9"> Now for a long time Israel has been without the true God, and without a teaching priest and without the law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 55:6; Deuteronomy 4:29–30; 2 Chronicles 15:15"> But when in their trouble they were turned to the Lord, the God of Israel, searching after him, he let their search be rewarded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 5:6; Psalms 121:8; 1 Samuel 13:6"> In those times there was no peace for him who went out or for him who came in, but great trouble was on all the people of the lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 21:22–24; Matthew 24:7; 2 Chronicles 33:11"> And they were broken by divisions, nation against nation and town against town, because God sent all sorts of trouble on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; Joshua 1:7; Joshua 1:9"> But be you strong and let not your hands be feeble, for your work will be rewarded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:12; 2 Chronicles 13:19; 2 Chronicles 4:1"> And Asa, hearing these words of Azariah, the son of Oded the prophet, took heart and put away all the disgusting things out of all the land of Judah and Benjamin, and out of the towns which he had taken from the hill-country of Ephraim; and he made new again the altar of the Lord in front of the covered way of the Lord's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:16; Acts 9:31; Zechariah 8:21–23"> And he got together all Judah and Benjamin and those of Ephraim and Manasseh and Simeon who were living with them; for numbers of them came to him out of Israel when they saw that the Lord his God was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esther 8:9"> So they came together at Jerusalem in the third month, in the fifteenth year of the rule of Asa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:13–15; Numbers 31:28–29; Numbers 31:50"> And that day they made offerings to the Lord of the things they had taken in war, seven hundred oxen and seven thousand sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 24:14; 2 Chronicles 23:16; Deuteronomy 10:12"> And they made an agreement to be true to the Lord, the God of their fathers, with all their heart and all their soul;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 22:20; Psalms 115:13; 1 Kings 18:40"> And that anyone, small or great, man or woman, who was not true to the Lord, the God of Israel, would be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:13; Psalms 81:1–4; Nehemiah 10:29"> And they made an oath to the Lord, with a loud voice, sounding wind-instruments and horns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:7; 2 Chronicles 15:12; Isaiah 26:8"> And all Judah was glad because of the oath, for they had taken it with all their heart, turning to the Lord with all their desire; and he was with them and gave them rest on every side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 15:13–24; 1 Kings 15:10; 2 Kings 23:15"> And Asa would not let Maacah, his mother, be queen, because she had made a disgusting image for Asherah; and Asa had her image cut down and broken up and burned by the stream Kidron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 14:4; 2 Kings 12:3; 1 Kings 11:4"> But the high places were not taken away out of Israel; but still the heart of Asa was true to the Lord all his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 7:51; 1 Chronicles 26:20–26; 1 Kings 15:14–15"> He took into the house of God all the things which his father had made holy and those which he himself had made holy, silver and gold and vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 15:31; 1 Kings 15:33; 1 Kings 15:16–17"> And there was no more war till the thirty-fifth year of the rule of Asa.</span>
