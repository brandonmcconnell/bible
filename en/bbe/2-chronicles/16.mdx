<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:9; 2 Chronicles 15:5; 1 Kings 12:27"> In the thirty-sixth year of the rule of Asa, Baasha, king of Israel, went up against Judah, building Ramah so that no one was able to go out or in to Asa, king of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 12:18; 2 Chronicles 28:21; 2 Kings 18:15"> Then Asa took silver and gold out of the stores of the Lord's house and of the king's store-house, and sent to Ben-hadad, king of Aram, at Damascus, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 15:4; 2 Chronicles 18:3; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> Let there be an agreement between me and you as there was between my father and your father: see, I have sent you silver and gold; go and put an end to your agreement with Baasha, king of Israel, so that he may give up attacking me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 20:1; 2 Chronicles 17:12; 1 Kings 15:20"> And Ben-hadad did as King Asa said, and sent the captains of his armies against the towns of Israel, attacking Ijon and Dan and Abel-maim, and all the store-towns of Naphtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Then Baasha, hearing of it, put a stop to the building of Ramah, and let his work come to an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:17; Joshua 15:38; Isaiah 10:29"> Then King Asa, with all Judah, took away the stones and wood with which Baasha was building Ramah, and he made use of them for building Geba and Mizpah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:2; 1 Kings 16:1; Psalms 146:3–6"> At that time Hanani the seer came to Asa, king of Judah, and said to him, Because you have put your faith in the king of Aram and not in the Lord your God, the army of the king of Aram has got away out of your hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:3; 2 Chronicles 13:16; 2 Chronicles 16:7"> Were not the Ethiopians and the Lubim a very great army, with war-carriages and horsemen more than might be numbered? but because your faith was in the Lord, he gave them up into your hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 3:12; Proverbs 15:3; Jeremiah 16:17"> For the eyes of the Lord go this way and that, through all the earth, letting it be seen that he is the strong support of those whose hearts are true to him. In this you have done foolishly, for from now you will have wars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 18:26; Acts 16:23–24; 2 Samuel 12:13"> Then Asa was angry with the seer, and put him in prison, burning with wrath against him because of this thing. And at the same time Asa was cruel to some of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:15; 2 Chronicles 9:29; 2 Chronicles 35:27"> Now the acts of Asa, first and last, are recorded in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5; 2 Chronicles 28:22; 1 Chronicles 10:14"> In the thirty-ninth year of his rule, Asa had a very bad disease of the feet; but he did not go to the Lord for help in his disease, but to medical men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 15:24"> So Asa went to rest with his fathers, and death came to him in the forty-first year of his rule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 50:2; 2 Chronicles 21:19; Jeremiah 34:5"> And they put him into the resting-place which he had made for himself in the town of David, in a bed full of sweet perfumes of all sorts of spices, made by the perfumer's art, and they made a great burning for him.</span>
