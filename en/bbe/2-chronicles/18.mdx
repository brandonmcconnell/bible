<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:5; 2 Chronicles 21:6; 2 Chronicles 19:2"> Now Jehoshaphat had great wealth and honour, and his son was married to Ahab's daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 9:1; 2 Chronicles 19:2; 1 Kings 1:9"> And after some years he went down to Samaria to see Ahab. And Ahab made a feast for him and the people who were with him, putting to death great numbers of sheep and oxen; and he got Jehoshaphat to go with him to Ramoth-gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 22:4; 2 Kings 3:7; 2 John 1:10–11"> For Ahab, king of Israel, said to Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, Will you go with me to Ramoth-gilead? And he said, I am as you are, and my people as your people; we will be with you in the war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:2; 2 Samuel 5:19; 2 Samuel 2:1"> Then Jehoshaphat said to the king of Israel, Let us now get directions from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3; Micah 3:11; Jeremiah 28:1–17"> So the king of Israel got together all the prophets, four hundred men, and said to them, Am I to go to Ramoth-gilead to make war or not? And they said, Go up: for God will give it into the hands of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 22:7–9; 2 Kings 3:11–13"> But Jehoshaphat said, Is there no other prophet of the Lord here from whom we may get directions?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 5:10; Micah 2:7; 1 Kings 21:20"> And the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, There is still one man by whom we may get directions from the Lord, but I have no love for him, because he has never been a prophet of good to me, but only of evil: he is Micaiah, the son of Imla. And Jehoshaphat said, Let not the king say so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 22:9; Isaiah 39:7; 2 Chronicles 18:25–26"> Then the king of Israel sent for one of his unsexed servants and said, Go quickly and come back with Micaiah, the son of Imla.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 14:9; Daniel 7:9; Matthew 19:28"> Now the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat, the king of Judah, were seated on their seats of authority, dressed in their robes, by the doorway into Samaria; and all the prophets were acting as prophets before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:17; Zechariah 1:18–21; Jeremiah 28:2–3"> And Zedekiah, the son of Chenaanah, made himself iron horns and said, The Lord says, Pushing back the Aramaeans with these, you will put an end to them completely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 24:24–25; 2 Chronicles 18:33–34; Jude 1:16"> And all the prophets said the same thing, saying, Go up to Ramoth-gilead, and it will go well for you, for the Lord will give it into the hands of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 2:6; Psalms 10:11; Micah 2:11"> Now the servant who had gone to get Micaiah said to him, See now, all the prophets with one voice are saying good things to the king; so let your words be like theirs, and say good things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 22:35; Numbers 24:13; Numbers 22:18–20"> And Micaiah said, By the living Lord, whatever the Lord says to me I will say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 22:15; Lamentations 4:21; Amos 4:4–5"> When he came to the king, the king said to him, Micaiah, are we to go to Ramoth-gilead to make war or not? And he said, Go up, and it will go well for you; and they will be given up into your hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 26:63; 1 Samuel 14:24; 1 Kings 22:16"> And the king said to him, Have I not, again and again, put you on your oath to say nothing to me but what is true in the name of the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; Ezekiel 34:5–6; Numbers 27:17"> Then he said, I saw all Israel wandering on the mountains like sheep without a keeper; and the Lord said, These have no master: let them go back, every man to his house in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 18:7; 1 Kings 22:18; Proverbs 29:1"> And the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, Did I not say that he would not be a prophet of good to me, but of evil?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 7:9; Isaiah 6:1–5; 1 Kings 22:19–23"> Then he said, Give ear now to the word of the Lord: I saw the Lord seated on his seat of power, and all the army of heaven in their places, at his right hand and at his left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:9; 2 Chronicles 25:19; Proverbs 11:5"> And the Lord said, How may Ahab, king of Israel, be tricked into going up to Ramoth-gilead to his death? And one said one thing and one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 1:6; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> Then a spirit came forward and took his place before the Lord and said, I will get him to do it by a trick. And the Lord said to him, How?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 8:44; 1 Kings 22:21–22; 1 John 4:6"> And he said, I will go out and be a spirit of deceit in the mouth of all his prophets. And the Lord said, Your trick will have its effect on him: go out and do so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 19:14; Ezekiel 14:9; Job 12:16"> And now, see, the Lord has put a spirit of deceit in the mouth of these prophets of yours; and the Lord has said evil against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 14:65; Jeremiah 20:2; 2 Chronicles 18:10"> Then Zedekiah, the son of Chenaanah, came near and gave Micaiah a blow on the side of his face, saying, Where is the spirit of the Lord whose word is in you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:21–22; Isaiah 26:20; Jeremiah 28:16–17"> And Micaiah said, Truly, you will see on that day when you go into an inner room to keep yourself safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 18:8; 2 Chronicles 34:8; Jeremiah 38:6–7"> And the king of Israel said, Take Micaiah and send him back to Amon, the ruler of the town, and to Joash, the king's son;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:10; Psalms 80:5; Luke 23:2"> And say, By the king's order this man is to be put in prison, and given prison food till I come back in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 20:45–46; Amos 9:10; Micah 1:2"> And Micaiah said, If you come back at all in peace, the Lord has not sent his word by me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Kings 22:29–33"> So the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat, the king of Judah, went up to Ramoth-gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:8; Jeremiah 23:24; Proverbs 26:25"> And the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, I will make a change in my clothing, so that I do not seem to be the king, and will go into the fight; but do you put on your robes. So the king of Israel made a change in his dress, and they went to the fight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 19:11; Deuteronomy 1:17; 1 Kings 20:42"> Now the king of Aram had given orders to the captains of his war-carriages, saying, Make no attack on small or great, but only on the king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:14; Psalms 118:13; 2 Corinthians 1:9–10"> So when the captains of the war-carriages saw Jehoshaphat, they said, It is the king of Israel. And turning about, they came round him, but Jehoshaphat gave a cry, and the Lord came to his help, and God sent them away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 22:33"> Now when the captains of the war-carriages saw that he was not the king of Israel, they went back from going after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:23; 2 Samuel 15:11; 1 Kings 22:34–35"> And a certain man sent an arrow from his bow without thought of its direction, and gave the king of Israel a wound where his breastplate was joined to his clothing; so he said to the driver of his war-carriage, Go to one side and take me away out of the army, for I am badly wounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 13:21; 2 Chronicles 18:27; Proverbs 28:17"> But the fight became more violent while the day went on; and the king of Israel was supported in his war-carriage facing the Aramaeans till the evening; and by sundown he was dead.</span>
