<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 21:1–18; 1 Chronicles 3:13; Isaiah 3:12"> Manasseh was twelve years old when he became king, and he was ruling for fifty-five years in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:3; Deuteronomy 18:9; 2 Kings 17:11"> He did evil in the eyes of the Lord, copying the disgusting ways of the nations whom the Lord had sent out of the land before the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:21; Deuteronomy 17:3; 2 Chronicles 31:1"> For he put up again the high places which had been pulled down by his father Hezekiah; and he made altars for the Baals, and pillars of wood, and was a worshipper and servant of all the stars of heaven;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:16; 1 Kings 8:29; Deuteronomy 12:11"> And he made altars in the house of the Lord, of which the Lord had said, In Jerusalem will my name be for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:9; Jeremiah 32:34–35; Ezekiel 8:7–18"> And he made altars for all the stars of heaven in the two outer squares of the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 18:21; Leviticus 20:6; 2 Chronicles 28:3"> More than this, he made his children go through the fire in the valley of the son of Hinnom; and he made use of secret arts, and signs for reading the future, and unnatural powers, and gave positions to those who had control of spirits and to wonder-workers: he did much evil in the eyes of the Lord, moving him to wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:4; 2 Chronicles 7:16; 1 Kings 11:13"> And he put the image he had made in the house of God, the house of which God had said to David and to Solomon his son, In this house, and in Jerusalem, the town which I have made mine out of all the tribes of Israel, will I put my name for ever:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:10; Deuteronomy 5:31–1; Leviticus 8:36"> And never again will I let the feet of Israel be moved out of the land which I have given to their fathers; if only they will take care to do all my orders, even all the law and the orders and the rules given to them by Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 24:3–4; Proverbs 29:12; 2 Chronicles 33:2"> And Manasseh made Judah and the people of Jerusalem go out of the true way, so that they did more evil than those nations whom the Lord gave up to destruction before the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Jeremiah 44:4–5; Zechariah 1:4"> And the word of the Lord came to Manasseh and his people, but they gave no attention.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:36; 2 Chronicles 36:6; Nehemiah 9:32"> So the Lord sent against them the captains of the army of Assyria, who made Manasseh a prisoner and took him away in chains to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 10:3; Psalms 50:15; 2 Chronicles 32:26"> And crying out to the Lord his God in his trouble, he made himself low before the God of his fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:20; Daniel 4:25; Matthew 7:7–8"> And made prayer to him; and in answer to his prayer God let him come back to Jerusalem and to his kingdom. Then Manasseh was certain that the Lord was God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 27:3; 1 Kings 1:33; Nehemiah 3:3"> After this he made an outer wall for the town of David, on the west side of Gihon in the valley, as far as the way into the town by the fish doorway; and he put a very high wall round the Ophel; and he put captains of the army in all the walled towns of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:3–7; Hosea 14:1–3; 2 Kings 21:7"> He took away the strange gods and the image out of the house of the Lord, and all the altars he had put up on the hill of the Lord's house and in Jerusalem, and put them out of the town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:4; 1 Kings 18:30; 2 Chronicles 33:9"> And he put the altar of the Lord in order, offering peace-offerings and praise-offerings on it, and said that all Judah were to be servants of the Lord, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:12; 1 Kings 22:43; 2 Kings 15:4"> However, the people still made offerings in the high places, but only to the Lord their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:19; 2 Chronicles 33:12–13; Amos 7:12"> Now the rest of the acts of Manasseh, and his prayer to his God, and the words which the seers said to him in the name of the Lord, the God of Israel, are recorded among the acts of the kings of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 9:11; 1 John 1:9; Daniel 5:22"> And the prayer which he made to God, and how God gave him an answer, and all his sin and his wrongdoing, and the places where he made high places and put up pillars of wood and images, before he put away his pride, are recorded in the history of the seers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 1:10; 2 Chronicles 32:33; 2 Kings 21:18–25"> So Manasseh went to rest with his fathers, and they put his body to rest in his house, and Amon his son became king in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="James 4:13–15; Luke 12:19–20; 2 Chronicles 33:1"> Amon was twenty-two years old when he became king; and he was ruling for two years in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Kings 21:20; Ezekiel 20:18; 2 Kings 21:1–11"> He did evil in the eyes of the Lord, as Manasseh his father had done; and Amon made offerings to all the images which his father Manasseh had made, and was their servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:12; 2 Chronicles 33:19; Jeremiah 7:26"> He did not make himself low before the Lord, as his father Manasseh had done, but went on sinning more and more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 11:22; 2 Chronicles 25:27–28; 2 Chronicles 24:25–26"> And his servants made a secret design against him, and put him to death in his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 35:31; 2 Chronicles 34:1; 2 Chronicles 36:1"> But the people of the land put to death all those who had taken part in the design against King Amon, and made his son Josiah king in his place.</span>
