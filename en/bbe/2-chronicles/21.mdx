<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 22:50; 2 Kings 8:16–17; 2 Chronicles 12:16"> And Jehoshaphat went to rest with his fathers, and his body was put into the earth in the town of David. And Jehoram his son became king in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:6; 2 Chronicles 23:2; 2 Chronicles 24:5"> And he had brothers, sons of Jehoshaphat, Azariah, Jehiel, Zechariah, Azariah, Michael, and Shephatiah; all these were sons of Jehoshaphat, king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:23; Deuteronomy 21:15–17; 2 Chronicles 11:5"> And their father gave them much silver and gold and things of great value, as well as walled towns in Judah; but the kingdom he gave to Jehoram, because he was the oldest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 9:5; 2 Chronicles 21:17; Genesis 4:8"> Now when Jehoram had taken his place over his father's kingdom, and had made his position safe, he put all his brothers to death with the sword, as well as some of the princes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 8:17–22"> Jehoram was thirty-two years old when he became king; and he was ruling in Jerusalem for eight years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 18:1; 1 Kings 12:28–30; 2 Kings 8:18"> He went in the ways of the kings of Israel, and did as the family of Ahab did, for the daughter of Ahab was his wife; and he did evil in the eyes of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 11:36; Psalms 132:17–18; 2 Samuel 7:12–17"> But it was not the Lord's purpose to send destruction on the family of David, because of the agreement he had made with David, when he said he would give to him and to his sons a light for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:10; 2 Chronicles 20:22–23; Genesis 27:40"> In his time Edom made themselves free from the rule of Judah, and took a king for themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Then Jehoram went over with his captains and all his war-carriages ... made an attack by night on the Edomites, whose forces were all round him ... on the captains of the war-carriages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; Jeremiah 2:13; 2 Chronicles 13:10"> So Edom made themselves free from the rule of Judah, to this day: and at the same time Libnah made itself free from his rule; because he was turned away from the Lord, the God of his fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 20:5; 1 Kings 11:7; Leviticus 17:7"> And more than this, he made high places in the mountains of Judah, teaching the people of Jerusalem to go after false gods, and guiding Judah away from the true way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:2–5; 2 Chronicles 17:3–4; 2 Kings 2:1"> And a letter came to him from Elijah the prophet, saying, The Lord, the God of your father David, says, Because you have not kept to the ways of your father Jehoshaphat or the ways of Asa, king of Judah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:4; 2 Chronicles 21:11; 1 Kings 16:30–33"> But have gone in the way of the kings of Israel, and have made Judah and the people of Jerusalem go after false gods, as the family of Ahab did: and because you have put to death your father's sons, your brothers, who were better than yourself:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 26:21; Exodus 20:5; Hosea 5:11"> Now, truly, the Lord will send a great destruction on your people and your children and your wives and everything which is yours:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:18–19; Acts 1:18; Deuteronomy 28:67"> And you yourself will undergo the cruel pains of a disease in your stomach, so that day by day your inside will be falling out because of the disease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:11; 2 Chronicles 33:11; 1 Kings 11:14"> Then the Philistines and the Arabians, who are by Ethiopia, were moved by the Lord to make war on Jehoram;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:1; 2 Chronicles 22:6; 2 Chronicles 25:23"> And they came up against Judah, forcing a way into it, and took away all the goods in the king's house, as well as his sons and his wives; so that he had no son but only Jehoahaz, the youngest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:15; Acts 12:23; 2 Kings 9:29"> And after all this the Lord sent on him a disease of the stomach from which it was impossible for him to be made well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:14; Jeremiah 34:5"> And time went on, and after two years, his inside falling out because of the disease, he came to his death in cruel pain. And his people made no burning for him like the burning made for his fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:18; 2 Chronicles 28:27; Jeremiah 22:28"> He was thirty-two years old when he became king, and he was ruling in Jerusalem for eight years: and at his death he was not regretted; they put his body into the earth in the town of David, but not in the resting-place of the kings.</span>
