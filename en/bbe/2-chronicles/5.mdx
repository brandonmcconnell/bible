<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 7:51; 1 Chronicles 22:14; 2 Samuel 8:11"> So all the work which Solomon did for the house of the Lord was complete. And Solomon took the holy things which David his father had given, the silver and the gold and all the vessels, and put them in the store-houses of the house of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:12; 2 Chronicles 1:4; Numbers 10:33"> Then Solomon sent for all the responsible men of Israel, all the chiefs of the tribes and the heads of families of the children of Israel, to come to Jerusalem and take the ark of the Lord's agreement up out of the town of David, which is Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 8:2; 2 Chronicles 7:8–10; Leviticus 23:34–36"> And all the men of Israel came together to the king at the feast in the seventh month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 8:3; Joshua 3:6; Joshua 6:6"> All the responsible men of Israel came, and the Levites took up the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 8:6; 2 Chronicles 1:3; 1 Kings 8:4"> They took up the ark and the Tent of meeting and all the holy vessels which were in the Tent; all these the priests, the Levites, took up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:21; 2 Samuel 6:13; 1 Chronicles 16:1–2"> And King Solomon and all the men of Israel who had come together there with him, were before the ark, making offerings of sheep and oxen more than might be numbered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 132:8; 1 Kings 6:23–28; 1 Kings 8:6–7"> And the priests took the ark of the Lord's agreement and put it in its place, in the inner room of the house, in the most holy place, under the wings of the winged ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 25:12–15; Numbers 4:6; Exodus 37:3–5"> For their wings were outstretched over the place where the ark was, covering the ark and its rods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 8:8–9"> The rods were so long that their ends were seen from the holy place before the inmost room; but they were not seen from outside; and there they are to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 9:4; 2 Chronicles 6:11; Deuteronomy 10:2–5"> Nothing was in the ark but the two flat stones which Moses put there at Horeb, where the Lord made an agreement with the children of Israel when they came out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:4; 2 Chronicles 29:5; Job 1:5"> Now when the priests had come out of the holy place, (for all the priests who were present had made themselves holy, not keeping to their divisions;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:24; Psalms 68:25; 1 Chronicles 6:39"> And the Levites who made the music, all of them, Asaph, Heman, Jeduthun, and their sons and brothers, robed in fair linen, were in their places with their brass and corded instruments at the east side of the altar, and with them a hundred and twenty priests blowing horns;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra 3:11; Jeremiah 33:11; 2 Chronicles 7:3"> And when the players on horns, and those who made melody in song, with one voice were sounding the praise and glory of the Lord; with loud voices and with wind instruments, and brass and corded instruments of music, praising the Lord and saying, He is good; his mercy is unchanging for ever: then the house was full of the cloud of the glory of the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 40:35; 1 Kings 8:11; Isaiah 6:1–4"> So that the priests were not able to keep their places to do their work because of the cloud; for the house of God was full of the glory of the Lord.</span>
