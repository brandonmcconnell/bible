<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 22:16; Revelation 22:6; Galatians 1:12"> The Revelation of Jesus Christ which God gave him so that his servants might have knowledge of the things which will quickly take place: and he sent and made it clear by his angel to his servant John;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:6; John 19:35; 1 John 1:1"> Who gave witness of the word of God, and of the witness of Jesus Christ, even of all the things which he saw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 11:28; Revelation 22:10; Romans 13:11"> A blessing be on the reader, and on those who give ear to the prophet's words, and keep the things which he has put in the book: for the time is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 11:2; John 1:1; Hebrews 13:8"> John to the seven churches which are in Asia: Grace to you and peace, from him who is and was and is to come; and from the seven Spirits which are before his high seat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 89:27; Hebrews 9:14; 1 John 1:7"> And from Jesus Christ, the true witness, the first to come back from the dead, and the ruler of the kings of the earth. To him who had love for us and has made us clean from our sins by his blood;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; Revelation 20:6; Exodus 19:6"> And has made us to be a kingdom and priests to his God and Father; to him let glory and power be given for ever and ever. So be it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 24:30; Zechariah 12:10; Daniel 7:13"> See, he comes with the clouds, and every eye will see him, and those by whom he was wounded; and all the tribes of the earth will be sorrowing because of him. Yes, so be it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; Revelation 22:13; Isaiah 43:10"> I am the First and the Last, says the Lord God who is and was and is to come, the Ruler of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 14:22; Revelation 3:10; John 16:33"> I, John, your brother, who have a part with you in the trouble and the kingdom and the quiet strength of Jesus, was in the island which is named Patmos, for the word of God and the witness of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 21:10; 2 Corinthians 12:2–4; Revelation 4:1–2"> I was in the Spirit on the Lord's day, and a great voice at my back, as of a horn, came to my ears,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 3:1; Jeremiah 30:2; Habakkuk 2:2"> Saying, What you see, put in a book, and send it to the seven churches; to Ephesus and to Smyrna and to Pergamos and to Thyatira and to Sardis and to Philadelphia and to Laodicea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zechariah 4:2; Exodus 25:37; Revelation 1:20–1"> And turning to see the voice which said these words to me, I saw seven gold vessels with lights burning in them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 7:13; Daniel 10:5–6; Daniel 10:16"> And in the middle of them one like a son of man, clothed with a robe down to his feet, and with a band of gold round his breasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 10:6; Daniel 7:9; Revelation 19:12"> And his head and his hair were white like wool, as white as snow; and his eyes were as a flame of fire;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:2; Daniel 10:6; Revelation 14:2"> And his feet like polished brass, as if it had been burned in a fire; and his voice was as the sound of great waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Revelation 19:15; Revelation 2:12"> And he had in his right hand seven stars: and out of his mouth came a sharp two-edged sword: and his face was like the sun shining in its strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; Revelation 22:13; Daniel 10:8–10"> And when I saw him, I went down on my face at his feet as one dead. And he put his right hand on me, saying, Have no fear; I am the first and the last and the Living one;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 6:9; Hebrews 7:25; Romans 14:8–9"> And I was dead, and see, I am living for ever, and I have the keys of death and of Hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 4:1–11; Revelation 1:11–3"> Put in writing, then, the things which you have seen, and the things which are, and the things which will be after these;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 2:1; Revelation 3:1; Revelation 1:16"> The secret of the seven stars which you saw in my right hand, and of the seven gold vessels with burning lights. The seven stars are the angels of the seven churches: and the seven lights are the seven churches.</span>
