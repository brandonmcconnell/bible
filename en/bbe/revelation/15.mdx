<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 111:2; Exodus 15:1–18; Psalms 139:14"> And they give the song of Moses, the servant of God, and the song of the Lamb, saying, Great and full of wonder are your works, O Lord God, Ruler of all; true and full of righteousness are your ways, eternal King.</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 10:6; Revelation 5:8; Revelation 16:2–1"> And one of the four beasts gave to the seven angels seven gold vessels full of the wrath of God, who is living for ever and ever.</span>
