<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 24:23; James 2:9; Leviticus 19:15"> My brothers, if you have the faith of our Lord Jesus Christ of glory, do not take a man's position into account.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 15:22; Esther 3:10; Esther 8:2"> For if a man comes into your Synagogue in fair clothing and with a gold ring, and a poor man comes in with dirty clothing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:9; James 2:6; Luke 7:44–46"> And you do honour to the man in fair clothing and say, Come here and take this good place; and you say to the poor man, Take up your position there, or be seated at my feet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 7:24; Job 34:19; Matthew 7:1–5"> Is there not a division in your minds? have you not become judges with evil thoughts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 5:3; Luke 6:20; Revelation 2:9"> Give ear, my dear brothers; are not those who are poor in the things of this world marked out by God to have faith as their wealth, and for their heritage the kingdom which he has said he will give to those who have love for him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 17:5; 1 Corinthians 11:22; Proverbs 14:31"> But you have put the poor man to shame. Are not the men of wealth rulers over you? do they not take you by force before their judges?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Philippians 2:9–11; Revelation 13:5–6; 1 Timothy 1:13"> Do they not say evil of the holy name which was given to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 19:18; Galatians 5:14; Matthew 22:39"> But if you keep the greatest law of all, as it is given in the holy Writings, Have love for your neighbour as for yourself, you do well:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 19:15; James 2:1–4; 1 John 3:4"> But if you take a man's position into account, you do evil, and are judged as evil-doers by the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Matthew 5:18–19; Deuteronomy 27:26"> For anyone who keeps all the law, but makes a slip in one point, is judged to have gone against it all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 20:13–14; Matthew 5:21–28; Deuteronomy 5:17–18"> For he who said, Do not be untrue in married life, is the same who said, Put no man to death. Now if you are not untrue in married life, but you put a man to death, the law is broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 1:25; James 2:8; 2 Peter 1:4–8"> Let your words and your acts be those of men who are to be judged by the law which makes free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; Matthew 6:15; Proverbs 21:13"> For the man who has had no mercy will be judged without mercy, but mercy takes pride in overcoming judging.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 7:26–27; Matthew 7:21–23; James 1:22–25"> What use is it, my brothers, for a man to say that he has faith, if he does nothing? will such a faith give him salvation?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 3:11; Matthew 25:35–40; Isaiah 58:7"> If a brother or a sister is without clothing and in need of the day's food,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 3:27–28; 1 John 3:16–18; Matthew 25:42–45"> And one of you says to them, Go in peace, be warm and full of food; but you do not give them the things of which their bodies have need, what profit is there in this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 2:26; 2 Peter 1:5–9; James 2:19–20"> Even so faith without works is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 3:13; James 2:14; Galatians 5:6"> But a man may say, You have faith and I have works; let me see your faith without your works, and I will make my faith clear to you by my works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 4:34; Mark 1:24; Matthew 8:29"> You have the belief that God is one, and you do well: the evil spirits have the same belief, shaking with fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 2:17; James 2:26; Proverbs 12:11"> Do you not see, O foolish man, that faith without works is of no use?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 22:16–18; Genesis 22:9–12; John 8:39"> Was not the righteousness of Abraham our father judged by his works, when he made an offering of Isaac his son on the altar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:3; Hebrews 11:17–19; Galatians 5:6"> You see that his faith was helping his works and was made complete by them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 15:6; Isaiah 41:8; 2 Chronicles 20:7"> And the holy Writings were put into effect which said, And Abraham had faith in God and it was put to his account as righteousness; and he was named the friend of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="James 2:21–22; James 2:15–18; Psalms 60:12"> You see that a man's righteousness is judged by his works and not by his faith only.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 11:31; Joshua 6:22–25; Joshua 2:19–21"> And in the same way, was not the righteousness of Rahab, the loose woman, judged by her works, when she took into her house those who were sent and let them go out by another way?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 2:20; James 2:17; James 2:14"> For as the body without the spirit is dead even so faith without works is dead.</span>
