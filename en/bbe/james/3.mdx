<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 12:47–48; 1 Timothy 1:7; 1 Peter 5:3"> Do not all be teachers, my brothers, because we teachers will be judged more hardly than others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 1:26; Proverbs 13:3; 1 Peter 3:10"> For we all go wrong in a number of things. If a man never makes a slip in his talk, then he is a complete man and able to keep all his body in control.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 32:9; Psalms 39:1; James 1:26"> Now if we put bits of iron into horses' mouths so that they may be guided by us, we have complete control of their bodies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 107:25–27; Matthew 8:24; Acts 27:14–38"> And again ships, though they are so great and are moved by violent winds, are turned by a very small guiding-blade, at the impulse of the man who is using it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 18:21; Jeremiah 9:3–8; Proverbs 12:18"> Even so the tongue is a small part of the body, but it takes credit for great things. How much wood may be lighted by a very little fire!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 120:2–3; Proverbs 16:27; Mark 7:15"> And the tongue is a fire; it is the power of evil placed in our bodies, making all the body unclean, putting the wheel of life on fire, and getting its fire from hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 5:4"> For every sort of beast and bird and every living thing on earth and in the sea has been controlled by man and is under his authority;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:13; Psalms 140:3; Psalms 55:21"> But the tongue may not be controlled by man; it is an unresting evil, it is full of the poison of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Genesis 1:26–27; Psalms 62:4"> With it we give praise to our Lord and Father; and with it we put a curse on men who are made in God's image.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:14; 1 Peter 3:9; 1 Corinthians 3:3"> Out of the same mouth comes blessing and cursing. My brothers, it is not right for these things to be so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Does the fountain send from the same outlet sweet and bitter water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 6:43–44; Matthew 12:33; Matthew 7:16–20"> Is a fig-tree able to give us olives, my brothers, or do we get figs from a vine, or sweet water from the salt sea?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 5:23; James 3:17; Matthew 5:5"> Who has wisdom and good sense among you? let him make his works clear by a life of gentle wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 3:16; 1 Corinthians 13:4; Philippians 2:3"> But if you have bitter envy in your heart and the desire to get the better of others, have no pride in this, talking falsely against what is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 3:17; James 1:17; 1 Corinthians 3:19"> This wisdom is not from heaven, but is of the earth and the flesh and the Evil One.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 3:14; 1 Corinthians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 14:33"> For where envy is, and the desire to get the better of others, there is no order, but every sort of evil-doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 5:22–23; Proverbs 2:6; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7"> But the wisdom which is from heaven is first holy, then gentle, readily giving way in argument, full of peace and mercy and good works, not doubting, not seeming other than it is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 5:9; Hosea 10:12; Philippians 1:11"> And the fruit of righteousness is planted in peace for those who make peace.</span>
