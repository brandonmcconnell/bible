<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 27:1–2; Matthew 27:11–14; Luke 22:66"> And they all went and took him before Pilate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 17:6–7; John 19:12; Luke 23:14"> And they made statements against him, saying, This man has to our knowledge been teaching our nation to do wrong, and not to make payment of taxes to Caesar, even saying that he himself is Christ, a king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 1:49; 1 Timothy 6:13; Luke 22:70"> And Pilate said to him, Are you the King of the Jews? And he said in answer, You say so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 18:38; Luke 23:14–15; Luke 23:22"> And Pilate said to the chief priests and the people, In my opinion this man has done no wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 1:14; John 1:43; John 2:11"> But they became more violent than before, saying, He has made trouble among the people, teaching through all Judaea from Galilee to this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 5:37; Luke 13:1"> But at these words Pilate said, Is the man a Galilaean?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Luke 13:31; Matthew 14:1"> And when he saw that he was under the authority of Herod, he sent him to Herod, who was in Jerusalem himself at that time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 9:7–9; Matthew 14:1; Mark 6:14"> Now when Herod saw Jesus he was very glad, having for a long time had a desire to see him, for he had had accounts of him, and was hoping to see some wonders done by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 27:14; Matthew 7:6; 1 Peter 2:23"> And he put a great number of questions to him, but he said nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 11:53; Luke 23:14–15; Luke 23:5"> And the chief priests and the scribes were there, making statements against him violently.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 15:16–20; Matthew 27:27–30; Psalms 69:19–20"> And Herod, with the men of his army, put shame on him and made sport of him, and dressing him in shining robes, he sent him back to Pilate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 4:27; Revelation 17:13–14; Psalms 83:4–6"> And that day Herod and Pilate became friends with one another, for before they had been against one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 15:14; Matthew 27:21–23; John 18:38"> And Pilate sent for the chief priests and the rulers and the people, and said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 6:4; Hebrews 7:26; Luke 23:1–2"> You say that this man has been teaching the people evil things: now I, after going into the question before you, see nothing wrong in this man in connection with the things which you have said against him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Herod is of the same opinion, for he has sent him back to us; for, you see, he has done nothing for which I might put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:26; Luke 23:22; Mark 15:15"> And so I will give him punishment and let him go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> &#91;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 21:36; Acts 3:14; Acts 22:22"> But with loud voices they said all together, Put this man to death, and make Barabbas free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 3:14; Luke 23:5; Luke 23:2"> Now this man was in prison because of an attack against the government in the town, in which there had been loss of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 15:15; Matthew 14:8–9; Matthew 27:19"> And Pilate again said to them that it was his desire to let Jesus go free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 27:22–25; Mark 15:13; John 19:15"> But crying out they said, To the cross with him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 23:14; Luke 23:20; 1 Peter 3:18"> And he said to them a third time, Why, what evil has he done? I see no reason for putting him to death: I will give him punishment and let him go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zechariah 11:8; Psalms 57:4; Psalms 22:12–13"> But they went on crying out loudly, Let him be put to death on the cross. And they had their way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 27:26; Exodus 23:2; Proverbs 17:15"> And Pilate gave his decision for their desire to be put into effect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 3:14; Mark 15:6–7; Mark 15:15"> And in answer to their request, he let that man go free who had been in prison for acting against the government and causing death, and Jesus he gave up to their pleasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 27:32–44; Mark 15:21–32; Acts 6:9"> And while they were taking him away, they put their hands on Simon of Cyrene, who was coming from the country, and made him take the cross after Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 8:52; Luke 8:2; Mark 15:40"> And a great band of people went after him, and of women making signs of grief and weeping for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Song of Solomon 8:4; Song of Solomon 5:8; Song of Solomon 1:5"> But Jesus, turning to them, said, Daughters of Jerusalem, let not your weeping be for me, but for yourselves and for your children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 24:19; Luke 21:23–24; Mark 13:17–19"> For the days are coming in which they will say, Happy are those who have had no children, whose bodies have never given birth, whose breasts have never given milk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 6:16; Hosea 10:8; Isaiah 2:19"> And they will say to the mountains, Come down on us, and to the hills, Be a cover over us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17–18; John 15:6; Proverbs 11:31"> For if they do these things when the tree is green, what will they do when it is dry?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 15:27; Isaiah 53:12; Matthew 27:38"> And two others, evil-doers, were taken with him to be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 15:22–23; John 19:17–18; John 18:32"> And when they came to the place which is named Golgotha, they put him on the cross, and the evil-doers, one on the right side, and the other on the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; Acts 7:60; 1 Peter 3:9"> And Jesus said, Father, let them have forgiveness, for they have no knowledge of what they are doing. And they made division of his clothing among them by the decision of chance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 22:17; Isaiah 42:1; 1 Peter 2:4"> And the people were looking on. And the rulers made sport of him, saying, He was a saviour of others; let him do something for himself, if he is the Christ, the man of God's selection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Matthew 27:48; Matthew 27:34"> And the men of the army made sport of him, coming to him and giving him bitter wine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And saying, If you are the King of the Jews, get yourself free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Mark 15:26; Matthew 27:37; John 19:19–22"> And these words were put in writing over him, THIS IS THE KING OF THE JEWS.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 27:44; Mark 15:32; Luke 23:35"> And one of the evil-doers on the cross, with bitter feeling, said to him, Are you not the Christ? Get yourself and us out of this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; Luke 12:5; Revelation 15:4"> But the other, protesting, said, Have you no fear of God? for you have a part in the same punishment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Leviticus 26:40–41; Ezra 9:13; 1 Peter 1:19"> And with reason; for we have the right reward of our acts, but this man has done nothing wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Romans 10:9–14; Acts 16:31; 1 John 5:11–13"> And he said, Jesus, keep me in mind when you come in your kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Revelation 2:7; Psalms 50:15; 1 Timothy 1:15–16"> And he said to him, Truly I say to you, Today you will be with me in Paradise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 27:45; Mark 15:33–34; Amos 8:9"> And it was now about the sixth hour; and all the land was dark till the ninth hour;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; Matthew 27:51; Hebrews 9:3–8"> The light of the sun went out, and the curtain in the Temple was parted in two.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 31:5; Acts 7:59; John 19:30"> And Jesus gave a loud cry and said, Father, into your hands I give my spirit: and when he had said this, he gave up his spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 27:54; Mark 15:39; Luke 23:41"> And when the captain saw what was done, he gave praise to God, saying, Without doubt this was an upright man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 18:13; Acts 2:37; Jeremiah 31:19"> And all the people who had come together to see it, when they saw the things which were done, went back again making signs of grief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 8:2; Psalms 38:11; Luke 23:55"> And all his friends and the women who came with him from Galilee, were waiting at a distance, watching these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 19:38–42; Acts 11:24; Acts 10:22"> Now there was a man named Joseph, a man of authority and a good and upright man</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 2:25; Luke 2:38; Isaiah 8:12"> (He had not given his approval to their decision or their acts), of Arimathaea, a town of the Jews, who was waiting for the kingdom of God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 19:38–42"> This man went to Pilate and made a request for the body of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Matthew 27:59–60; Isaiah 53:9; Mark 15:46"> And he took it down, and folding it in a linen cloth, he put it in a place cut in the rock for a dead body; and no one had ever been put in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 27:62; John 19:31; Mark 15:42"> Now it was the day of making ready and the Sabbath was coming on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Luke 23:49; Matthew 27:61; Mark 15:47"> And the women who had come with him from Galilee went after him and saw the place and how his body had been put to rest;</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Luke 24:1; Mark 16:1; Deuteronomy 5:14"> And they went back and got ready spices and perfumes; and on the Sabbath they took their rest, in agreement with the law.</span>
