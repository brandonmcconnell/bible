<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 8:5–13; Luke 7:1–10; Matthew 7:28–29"> After he had come to the end of all his words in the hearing of the people, he went into Capernaum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 5:2–3; Genesis 24:27; John 4:46–47"> And a certain captain had a servant who was very dear to him; this servant was ill and near to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 8:5; John 4:47; Luke 9:38"> And when news of Jesus came to his ears, he sent to him rulers of the Jews, requesting that he would come and make his servant well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 3:4; Matthew 10:11; Luke 7:6–7"> And they, when they came to Jesus, made their request warmly, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:11–12; 1 Chronicles 29:3–9; 1 Kings 5:1"> It is right for you to do this for him, because he is a friend to our nation, and himself has put up a Synagogue for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 5:24; Genesis 32:10; James 4:6"> And Jesus went with them. And when he was not far from the house, the man sent friends to him, saying, Lord, do not give yourself trouble: for I am not important enough for you to come into my house:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 107:20; Psalms 33:9; Luke 4:36"> And I had the feeling that I was not even good enough to come to you: but say the word only, and my servant will be well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Colossians 3:22; Acts 23:17; Acts 22:25–26"> For I, myself, am a man under authority, having men under me; and I say to this one, Go, and he goes; and to another, Come, and he comes; and to my servant, Do this, and he does it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 15:28; Romans 3:1–3; Matthew 8:10"> And when these things were said to Jesus, he was surprised, and, turning to the mass of people coming after him, said, I have not seen such great faith, no, not in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 4:50–53; Matthew 8:13; Matthew 15:28"> And when those who were sent came back to the house they saw that the servant was well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 10:38"> And it came about, after a little time, that he went to a town named Nain; and his disciples went with him, and a great number of people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 8:42; Luke 8:52; 1 Kings 17:9"> Now when he came near the door of the town, a dead man was being taken out, the only son of his mother, who was a widow: and a great number of people from the town were with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 103:13; Isaiah 63:9; Psalms 86:15"> And when the Lord saw her, he had pity on her and said to her, Be not sad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 5:25; John 11:43–44; Acts 9:40–41"> And he came near, and put his hand on the stretcher where the dead man was: and those who were moving it came to a stop. And he said, Young man, I say to you, Get up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 13:21; 2 Kings 4:32–37; 1 Kings 17:23–24"> And the dead man got up, and words came from his lips. And he gave him to his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Luke 7:39; Luke 1:68"> And fear came on all, and they gave praise to God, saying, A great prophet is among us: and, God has given thought to his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Mark 6:14; Matthew 9:26"> And this story about him went through all Judaea and the places round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 3:26; Matthew 11:2–19"> And the disciples of John gave him an account of all these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6–7; John 4:25; Genesis 22:18"> Then John sent two of his disciples to the Lord, saying, Are you he who is to come, or are we waiting for another?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 7:19"> And when the men came to him they said, John the Baptist has sent us to you, saying, Are you he who is to come, or are we waiting for another?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 3:10; Psalms 90:7–9; Hebrews 12:6"> At that time, he made a number of people free from their diseases and their pains, and from evil spirits; and to others who were blind he gave back the use of their eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5–6; Isaiah 61:1–3; Luke 4:18"> And answering them he said, Go back and give news to John of what you have seen, and the things which have come to your ears; the blind now see, those who had no power in their legs are walking, lepers are made clean, those who had no hearing now have their ears open, dead men come to life again, and the poor have the good news given to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 11:6; 1 Peter 2:7–8; Romans 9:32–33"> And a blessing will be on him who has no doubts about me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 4:14; John 1:23; Luke 1:80"> And when the men who were sent by John had gone away, he said to the people, about John, What did you go out into the waste land to see? a tall stem moving in the wind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 59:17; Esther 8:15; Esther 1:3"> But what did you go out to see? a man in soft clothing? See now, those who have beautiful clothing and delicate food are in kings' houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 1:76; John 5:35; Luke 20:6"> But what did you go out to see? a prophet? Yes, I say to you, and more than a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Luke 1:76; Mark 1:2"> This is he of whom it has been said, See, I send my servant before your face, who will make ready your way before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 13:16–17; Matthew 11:11; Colossians 1:25–27"> I say to you, Among all the sons of women, not one is greater than John: but he who is least in the kingdom of God is greater than he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 3:12; Luke 7:35; Acts 18:25"> (And all the people, and the tax-farmers, to whom John had given baptism, when they had knowledge of these things, gave glory to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 13:34; 2 Corinthians 6:1; Matthew 22:35"> But the Pharisees and the teachers of the law were against the purpose of God for themselves, not having had his baptism.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 11:16–19; Lamentations 2:13; Mark 4:30"> What comparison am I to make of the men of this generation? what are they like?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 11:16–19; Jeremiah 5:3–5; Proverbs 17:16"> They are like children who are seated in the market-place, crying out to one another, and saying, We made music for you, but you did not take part in the dance; we gave cries of sorrow, but you were not sad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 1:15; Mark 1:6; John 8:52"> For John the Baptist came, taking no food or drink, and you say, He has an evil spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 15:2; Luke 7:36; John 12:2"> The Son of man came feasting, and you say, Here is a lover of food and wine, a friend of tax-farmers and sinners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 8:32–36; Matthew 11:19; Hosea 14:9"> But wisdom is judged to be right by all her children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; Matthew 26:5–6; Luke 14:1"> And one of the Pharisees made a request that he would take a meal with him. And he went into the Pharisee's house and took his seat at the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; Matthew 26:6–13; Matthew 21:31"> And there was a woman in the town who was a sinner; and when she had news that he was a guest in the Pharisee's house, she took a bottle of perfume,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Psalms 126:5–6; Psalms 51:17; Genesis 18:4"> And went in and took her place at the back of him, near his feet, weeping, so that his feet were washed with the drops from her eyes, and with her hair she made them dry, and kissing his feet she put the perfume on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 7:16; Luke 15:2; Mark 7:21"> Now when the Pharisee in whose house he was saw it, he said to himself, This man, if he was a prophet, would be conscious what sort of woman this is who has put her hands on him, that she is a sinner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 16:19; Luke 5:31; Ezekiel 33:31"> And Jesus, answering, said, Simon, I have something to say to you. And he said, Master, say on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 7:47; Romans 5:20; Matthew 18:28"> And he said, Two men were in debt to a certain man of business: one had a debt of five hundred pence, and the other of fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Daniel 9:18–19; Colossians 3:13; Romans 3:24"> When they were unable to make payment, he made the two of them free of their debts. Which of them, now, will have the greater love for him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalms 116:16–18; 1 Timothy 1:13–16; Luke 7:47"> Simon, in answer, said, It seems he whose debt was greater. And he said, Your decision is right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:10; Judges 19:21; Genesis 18:4"> And turning to the woman he said to Simon, You see this woman? I came into your house; you did not give me water for my feet: but she has been washing my feet with the drops from her eyes, and drying them with her hair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:5; 2 Samuel 19:39; Romans 16:16"> You did not give me a kiss: but she, from the time when I came in, has gone on kissing my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 23:5; Ecclesiastes 9:8; Matthew 6:17"> You put no oil on my head: but she has put perfume on my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 John 4:19; 1 John 3:18; Isaiah 1:18"> And so I say to you, She will have forgiveness for her sins which are great in number, because of her great love: but he who has small need of forgiveness gives little love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Matthew 9:2; Mark 2:5; Luke 5:20"> And he said to her, You have forgiveness for your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 5:20–21; Matthew 9:3; Mark 2:7"> And those who were seated at table with him said to themselves, Who is this who even gives forgiveness of sins?</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Mark 5:34; Luke 8:48; Luke 18:42"> And he said to the woman, By your faith you have salvation; go in peace.</span>
