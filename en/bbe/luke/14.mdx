<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 3:2; Luke 20:20; Psalms 62:4"> And it came about that when he went into the house of one of the chief Pharisees on the Sabbath, to have a meal, they were watching him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And a certain man was there who had a disease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 12:10; Mark 3:4; Luke 13:14–16"> And Jesus, answering, said to the scribes and Pharisees, Is it right to make people well on the Sabbath or not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 22:46; Matthew 21:25–27"> But they said nothing. And he made him well and sent him away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 13:15; Exodus 23:4–5; Matthew 12:11–12"> And he said to them, Which of you, whose ox or ass has got into a water-hole, will not straight away get him out on the Sabbath?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 20:40; Luke 21:15; Acts 6:10"> And they had no answer to that question.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Philippians 2:3; Luke 11:43; Matthew 23:6"> And he gave teaching in the form of a story to the guests who came to the feast, when he saw how they took the best seats; saying to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 25:6–7"> When you get a request to come to a feast, do not take the best seat, for a more important man than you may be coming,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 16:18; Proverbs 11:2; Proverbs 3:35"> And then the giver of the feast will come to you and say, Give your place to this man; and you, with shame, will have to take the lowest seat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 25:6–7; Proverbs 15:33; 1 Samuel 15:17"> But when you come, go and take the lowest seat, so that when the giver of the feast comes, he may say to you, Friend, come up higher; and then you will have honour in the eyes of all the others who are there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 18:14; Matthew 23:12; Proverbs 29:23"> For every man who gives himself a high place will be put down, but he who takes a low place will be lifted up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 5:46; James 2:1–6; Luke 6:32–36"> And he said to the master of the house, When you give a feast, do not send for your friends and your brothers and your family or your neighbours who have wealth, for they may give a feast for you, and so you will get a reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 14:21; Isaiah 58:10; Proverbs 14:31"> But when you give a feast, send for the poor and the blind and those who are broken in body:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Acts 24:15; John 5:29"> And you will have a blessing, because they will not be able to give you any payment, and you will get your reward when the upright come back from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 19:9; Luke 22:30; Luke 13:29"> And, hearing these words, one of those who were at table with him said to him, Happy is the man who will be a guest in the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 22:2–14; Revelation 22:17; Isaiah 55:1–7"> And he said to them, A certain man gave a great feast, and sent word of it to a number of people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 11:27–29; Matthew 10:1–4; Matthew 22:3–4"> And when the time had come, he sent his servants to say to them, Come, for all things are now ready.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Luke 17:26–31; Luke 18:24"> And they all gave reasons why they were not able to come. The first said to him, I have got a new field, and it is necessary for me to go and see it: I am full of regret that I am unable to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And another said, I have got some cattle, and I am going to make a test of them: I am full of regret that I am unable to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:5; 1 Corinthians 7:33; Luke 14:26–28"> And another said, I have been married, and so I am not able to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 14:13; Matthew 11:5; Luke 14:24"> And the servant came back and gave his master an account of these things. Then the master of the house was angry and said to the servant, Go out quickly into the streets of the town and get the poor, the blind, and those who are broken in body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 14:2; Ephesians 3:8; 1 Timothy 2:5–6"> And the servant said, Lord, your orders have been done, and still there is room.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 22:9–10; Colossians 1:28; Acts 13:47–48"> And the lord said to the servant, Go out into the roads and the fields, and make them come in, so that my house may be full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 21:43; Acts 13:46; John 8:24"> For I say to you that not one of those who were requested to come will have a taste of my feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 6:24–27; Luke 12:1"> Now a great number of people went with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; John 12:25; Philippians 3:8"> And turning round, he said to them, If any man comes to me, and has not hate for his father and mother and wife and children and brothers and sisters, and even for his life, he may not be my disciple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 10:38; Luke 9:23–25; Mark 8:34–37"> Whoever does not take up his cross and come after me may not be my disciple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 24:27; Matthew 10:22; Joshua 24:19–24"> For which of you, desiring to put up a tower, does not first give much thought to the price, if he will have enough to make it complete?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> For fear that if he makes a start and is not able to go on with it to the end, all who see it will be laughing at him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 6:11; 2 John 1:8; 1 Corinthians 3:11–14"> And saying, This man made a start at building and is not able to make it complete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 20:18; Proverbs 25:8; 2 Kings 18:20–22"> Or what king, going to war with another king, will not first take thought if he will be strong enough, with ten thousand men, to keep off him who comes against him with twenty thousand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 12:20; James 4:6–10; Luke 12:58"> Or while the other is still a great distance away, he sends representatives requesting conditions of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Philippians 3:7–8; 1 John 2:15–16; Luke 5:28"> And so whoever is not ready to give up all he has may not be my disciple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 5:13; Colossians 4:6; Mark 9:49–50"> For salt is good, but if the taste goes from it, of what use is it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 11:15; Luke 9:44; Revelation 2:11"> It is no good for the land or for the place of waste; no one has a use for it. He who has ears, let him give ear.</span>
