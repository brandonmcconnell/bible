<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Matthew 24:14; Acts 25:21"> Now it came about in those days that an order went out from Caesar Augustus that there was to be a numbering of all the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Matthew 4:24; Acts 26:30"> This was the first numbering, which was made when Quirinius was ruler of Syria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And all men went to be numbered, everyone to his town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 5:2; John 7:42; 1 Samuel 16:1"> And Joseph went up from Galilee, out of the town of Nazareth, into Judaea, to Beth-lehem, the town of David, because he was of the house and family of David,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:22–27; Matthew 1:18–19"> To be put on the list with Mary, his future wife, who was about to become a mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 33:11; Revelation 12:1–5; Micah 5:2"> And while they were there, the time came for her to give birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Galatians 4:4; 2 Corinthians 8:9; Isaiah 53:2–3"> And she had her first son; and folding him in linen, she put him to rest in the place where the cattle had their food, because there was no room for them in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 31:39–40; Psalms 78:70–71; 1 Samuel 17:34–35"> And in the same country there were keepers of sheep in the fields, watching over their flock by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1; 2 Corinthians 3:18; 1 Kings 8:11"> And an angel of the Lord came to them, and the glory of the Lord was shining round about them: and fear came on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 40:9; Isaiah 61:1; Isaiah 52:7"> And the angel said, Have no fear; for truly, I give you good news of great joy which will be for all the people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6; Matthew 1:21; Acts 2:36"> For on this day, in the town of David, a Saviour has come to birth, who is Christ the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Isaiah 53:1–2; Isaiah 7:11"> And this is the sign to you: you will see a young child folded in linen, in the place where the cattle have their food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 103:20–21; Revelation 5:11; Psalms 148:2"> And suddenly there was with the angel a great band of spirits from heaven, giving praise to God, and saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 5:1; Luke 19:38; Colossians 1:20"> Glory to God in the highest, and on the earth peace among men with whom he is well pleased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:22; Matthew 2:1–2; Matthew 2:9–11"> And when the angels had gone away from them into heaven, the keepers of the sheep said to one another, Let us go now to Beth-lehem, and see this thing which has come about, which the Lord has made clear to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:32; Luke 2:12; Luke 2:7"> And they came quickly, and saw Mary and Joseph, and the child in the place where the cattle had their food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 66:16; Luke 8:39; Psalms 71:17–18"> And when they saw it, they gave them an account of the things which had been said to them about the child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 2:47; Luke 4:36; Luke 1:65–66"> And all those to whose ears it came were full of wonder at the things said by the keepers of the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 2:51; Luke 1:66; Proverbs 4:4"> But Mary kept all these words in her heart, and gave much thought to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 106:48; Isaiah 29:19; Acts 11:18"> Then the keepers of the sheep went back, giving glory and praise to God for all the things which had come to their ears and which they had seen, as it had been said to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:31; Genesis 17:12; Galatians 4:4–5"> And when, after eight days, the time came for his circumcision, he was named Jesus, the name which the angel had given to him before his birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 12:1–8"> And when the necessary days for making them clean by the law of Moses had come to an end, they took him to Jerusalem to give him to the Lord</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Exodus 22:29; Numbers 3:13"> (As it says in the law of the Lord, Every mother's first male child is to be holy to the Lord),</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 12:6–8; Leviticus 12:2; 2 Corinthians 8:9"> And to make an offering, as it is ordered in the law of the Lord, of two doves or other young birds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 25:9; Luke 2:38; Luke 1:6"> And there was then in Jerusalem a man whose name was Simeon; and he was an upright man, fearing God and waiting for the comfort of Israel: and the Holy Spirit was on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 8:51; Hebrews 11:5; Luke 9:27"> And he had knowledge, through the Holy Spirit, that he would not see death till he had seen the Lord's Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 4:1; Luke 2:41; Luke 2:51"> And full of the Spirit he came into the Temple; and when the father and mother came in with the child Jesus, to do with him what was ordered by the law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 1:64; Luke 2:20; Luke 2:13–14"> Then he took him in his arms and gave praise to God and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Genesis 15:15; Philippians 1:23"> Now you are letting your servant go in peace, O Lord, as you have said;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 49:6; Luke 2:10–11; Luke 3:6"> For my eyes have seen your salvation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 62:1–2; Isaiah 42:1–4; Psalms 98:2–3"> Which you have made ready before the face of all nations;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1–3; Isaiah 49:6; Acts 26:23"> A light of revelation to the Gentiles, and the glory of your people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 1:65–66; Isaiah 8:18; Luke 2:48"> And his father and mother were full of wonder at the things which were said about him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:23; 1 Peter 2:7–8; Isaiah 8:14–15"> And Simeon gave them his blessing and said to Mary, his mother, See, this child will be the cause of the downfall and the lifting up of great numbers of people in Israel, and he will be a sign against which hard words will be said;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:2; 1 Corinthians 11:19; Matthew 12:24–35"> (And a sword will go through your heart;) so that the secret thoughts of men may come to light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 15:20; Genesis 30:13; Acts 21:9"> And there was one, Anna, a woman prophet, the daughter of Phanuel, of the family of Asher (she was very old, and after seven years of married life</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:5; Psalms 23:6; Acts 14:23"> She had been a widow for eighty-four years); she was in the Temple at all times, worshipping with prayers and going without food, night and day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 2:25; 2 Corinthians 9:15; Mark 15:43"> And coming up at that time, she gave praise to God, talking of him to all those who were waiting for the freeing of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 2:4; Galatians 4:4–5; Deuteronomy 12:32"> And when they had done all the things which were ordered by the law of the Lord, they went back to Galilee, to Nazareth, the town where they were living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 2:52; Luke 1:80; Isaiah 11:1–5"> And the child became tall and strong and full of wisdom, and the grace of God was on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 12:14; Numbers 28:16; Deuteronomy 12:18"> And every year his father and mother went to Jerusalem at the feast of the Passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And when he was twelve years old, they went up, as their way was, to the feast;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:21–23; Exodus 12:15; 2 Chronicles 25:17"> And when the days of the feast came to an end and they were going back, the boy Jesus was still in Jerusalem, but they had no knowledge of it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 122:1–4; Isaiah 2:3; Psalms 42:4"> And in the belief that he was with some of their number, they went a day's journey; and after looking for him among their relations and friends,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> And seeing that he was not there, they went back to Jerusalem, to make search for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 5:17; Isaiah 49:1–2; Isaiah 50:4"> And after three days they came across him in the Temple, seated among the wise men, giving ear to their words and putting questions to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; Luke 4:32; Psalms 119:99"> And all to whose ears it came were full of wonder at his knowledge and the answers which he gave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 2:49; Matthew 12:46; Luke 3:23"> And when they saw him they were surprised, and his mother said to him, Son, why have you done this to us? see, your father and I have been looking for you with sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 6:38; John 8:29; Malachi 3:1"> And he said to them, Why were you looking for me? was it not clear to you that my right place was in my Father's house?</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Luke 18:34; Luke 9:45; Mark 9:32"> And his words seemed strange to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 2:19; Daniel 7:28; Genesis 37:11"> And he went down with them and came to Nazareth; and did as he was ordered: and his mother kept all these words in her heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Luke 2:40; Proverbs 3:3–4; 1 Samuel 2:26"> And Jesus was increasing in wisdom and in years, and in grace before God and men.</span>
</div>
