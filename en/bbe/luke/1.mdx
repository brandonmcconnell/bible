<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 1:1–3; 2 Peter 1:16–19; John 20:31"> As a number of attempts have been made to put together in order an account of those events which took place among us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 15:27; Acts 26:16; Hebrews 2:3"> As they were handed down to us by those who saw them from the first and were preachers of the word,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 1:1; Acts 26:25; Acts 11:4"> It seemed good to me, having made observation, with great care, of the direction of events in their order, to put the facts in writing for you, most noble Theophilus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 20:31; Acts 18:25; 1 Corinthians 14:19"> So that you might have certain knowledge of those things about which you were given teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:10; Matthew 2:1; Nehemiah 12:4"> In the days of Herod, king of Judaea, there was a certain priest, by name Zacharias, of the order of Abijah; and he had a wife of the family of Aaron, and her name was Elisabeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 7:1; Genesis 17:1; Philippians 2:15"> They were upright in the eyes of God, keeping all the rules and orders of God, and doing no wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 11:11; Genesis 18:11; 1 Samuel 1:2"> And they were without children, because Elisabeth had never given birth, and they were at that time very old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 24:19; 2 Chronicles 31:2"> Now it came about that in his turn he was acting as priest before God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:11; Exodus 30:7–8; 1 Chronicles 23:13"> And as was the way of the priests, he had to go into the Temple to see to the burning of perfumes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 16:17; Revelation 8:3; Hebrews 4:14"> And all the people were offering prayers outside, at the time of the burning of perfumes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 40:26–27; Acts 10:3–4; Judges 13:3"> And he saw an angel of the Lord in his place on the right side of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 13:22; Judges 6:22; Luke 2:9–10"> And Zacharias was troubled when he saw him, and fear came on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:21; Acts 10:31; Genesis 25:21"> But the angel said, Have no fear, Zacharias, for your prayer has come to the ears of God, and your wife Elisabeth will have a son, and his name will be John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 1:58; Proverbs 23:24; Genesis 21:6"> And you will be glad and have great delight; and numbers of people will have joy at his birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Numbers 6:2–4; Luke 7:33"> For he will be great in the eyes of the Lord; he will not take wine or strong drink; and he will be full of the Spirit of God from his birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–5; Matthew 21:32; Malachi 3:1"> And through him great numbers of the children of Israel will be turned to the Lord their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Malachi 4:5–6; Matthew 11:14; Luke 1:76"> And he will go before his face in the spirit and power of Elijah, turning the hearts of fathers to their children, and wrongdoers to the way of righteousness; to make ready a people whose hearts have been turned to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 38:22; Genesis 17:17; Luke 1:7"> And Zacharias said to the angel, How may I be certain of this? For I am an old man, and my wife is far on in years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 1:26; Matthew 18:10; Daniel 9:21–23"> And the angel, answering, said, I am Gabriel, whose place is before God; I have been sent to say these words to you and to give you this good news.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:26; Ezekiel 24:27; Luke 1:22"> Now, see, you will be without voice or language till the day when these things come about, because you had not faith in my words, which will have effect at the right time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 6:23–27"> And the people were waiting for Zacharias and were surprised because he was in the Temple for such a long time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 1:62; John 13:24; Acts 21:40"> And when he came out he was not able to say anything, and they saw that he had seen a vision in the Temple; and he was making signs to them without words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:25; 2 Kings 11:5–7"> And when the days of his work in the Temple were ended, he went back to his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> After that time, Elisabeth, being certain that she was to become a mother, kept herself from men's eyes for five months, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 30:22–23; Isaiah 54:1–4; Genesis 21:1–2"> The Lord has done this to me, for his eyes were on me, to take away my shame in the eyes of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 2:23; Luke 1:19; John 7:41"> Now in the sixth month the angel Gabriel was sent from God to a town in Galilee, named Nazareth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 1:18; Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:16"> To a virgin who was to be married to a man named Joseph, of the family of David; and the name of the virgin was Mary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 6:12; Daniel 10:19; Luke 1:30"> And the angel came in to her and said, Peace be with you, to whom special grace has been given; the Lord is with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 10:17; Luke 1:12; Acts 10:4"> But she was greatly troubled at his words, and said to herself, What may be the purpose of these words?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 1:13; Romans 8:31; Luke 12:32"> And the angel said to her, Have no fear, Mary, for you have God's approval.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:21; Matthew 1:25"> And see, you will give birth to a son, and his name will be Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6–7; Isaiah 16:5; 2 Samuel 7:11–13"> He will be great, and will be named the Son of the Most High: and the Lord God will give him the kingdom of David, his father:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Daniel 2:44; Hebrews 1:8; Daniel 7:13–14"> He will have rule over the house of Jacob for ever, and of his kingdom there will be no end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Judges 13:8–12; Acts 9:6"> And Mary said to the angel, How may this be, because I have had no knowledge of a man?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 1:18; John 1:49; Matthew 1:20"> And the angel in answer said to her, The Holy Spirit will come on you, and the power of the Most High will come to rest on you, and so that which will come to birth will be named holy, Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Even now Elisabeth, who is of your family, is to be a mother, though she is old: and this is the sixth month with her who was without children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 19:26; Jeremiah 32:27; Mark 10:27"> For there is nothing which God is not able to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Romans 4:20–21; Psalms 119:38; Psalms 116:16"> And Mary said: I am the servant of the Lord; may it be to me as you say. And the angel went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 1:65; Joshua 15:48–59; Joshua 10:40"> Then Mary got up and went quickly into the high lands, to a town of Judah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And went into the house of Zacharias and took Elisabeth in her arms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 1:67; Acts 4:8; Luke 1:44"> And when the voice of Mary came to the ears of Elisabeth, the baby made a sudden move inside her; then Elisabeth was full of the Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 1:28; Judges 5:24; Genesis 22:18"> And she said with a loud voice: May blessing be on you among women, and a blessing on the child of your body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 20:28; John 13:13; Luke 2:11"> How is it that the mother of my Lord comes to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> For, truly, when the sound of your voice came to my ears, the baby in my body made a sudden move for joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 20:29; John 11:40; Luke 11:27–28"> Happy will she be who had faith that the things which the Lord has said to her will be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:1–10; Psalms 34:2–3; Psalms 35:9"> And Mary said: My soul gives glory to God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 35:9; 1 Timothy 2:3; Habakkuk 3:18"> My spirit is glad in God my Saviour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 138:6; Malachi 3:12; 1 Corinthians 1:26–28"> For he has had pity on his servant, though she is poor and lowly placed: and from this hour will all generations give witness to the blessing which has come to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Psalms 126:2–3; Psalms 111:9; Ephesians 3:20"> For he who is strong has done great things for me; and holy is his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Psalms 103:17–18; Exodus 20:6; Genesis 17:7"> His mercy is for all generations in whom is the fear of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Psalms 98:1; Isaiah 52:10; 1 Samuel 2:3–4"> With his arm he has done acts of power; he has put to flight those who have pride in their hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:24; James 4:10; Psalms 107:40–41"> He has put down kings from their seats, lifting up on high the men of low degree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Psalms 107:8–9; Luke 6:21; Psalms 34:10"> Those who had no food he made full of good things; the men of wealth he sent away with nothing in their hands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Psalms 98:3; Micah 7:20; Isaiah 44:21"> His help he has given to Israel, his servant, so that he might keep in mind his mercy to Abraham and his seed for ever,</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Psalms 105:6–10; Genesis 17:19; Psalms 132:11–17"> As he gave his word to our fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> And Mary was with her for about three months and then went back to her house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Luke 1:13; Genesis 21:2–3; Luke 2:6–7"> Now it was time for Elisabeth to give birth, and she had a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Luke 1:25; Luke 1:14; Romans 12:15"> And it came to the ears of her neighbours and relations that the Lord had been very good to her, and they took part in her joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Genesis 17:12; Leviticus 12:3; Philippians 3:5"> And on the eighth day they came to see to the circumcision of the child, and they would have given him the name of Zacharias, his father's name;</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Luke 1:13; 2 Samuel 12:25; Luke 1:63"> But his mother made answer and said, No, his name is John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61"> And they said, Not one of your relations has that name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Luke 1:22"> And they made signs to his father, to say what name was to be given to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Luke 1:60; Isaiah 30:8; Luke 1:13"> And he sent for writing materials and put down: His name is John; and they were all surprised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Luke 1:20; Exodus 4:15–16; Ezekiel 33:22"> And straight away his mouth was open and his tongue was free and he gave praise to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Luke 1:39; Acts 5:11; Luke 7:16"> And fear came on all those who were living round about them: and there was much talk about all these things in all the hill-country of Judaea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Acts 11:21; Luke 2:19; Luke 2:51"> And all who had word of them kept them in their minds and said, What will this child be? For the hand of the Lord was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Joel 2:28; Luke 1:41; Luke 1:15"> And his father, Zacharias, was full of the Holy Spirit, and with the voice of a prophet said these words:</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Psalms 106:48; Luke 7:16; Psalms 41:13"> Praise be to the Lord, the God of Israel, for he has come to his people and made them free,</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="Psalms 18:2; Ezekiel 29:21; 2 Samuel 22:3"> Lifting up a horn of salvation for us in the house of his servant David,</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="Acts 3:21–24; Romans 1:2; 2 Peter 1:21"> (As he said, by the mouth of his holy prophets, from the earliest times,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Psalms 106:10; Jeremiah 23:6; Zephaniah 3:15–20"> Salvation from those who are against us, and from the hands of those who have hate for us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72" data-xref="Hebrews 6:13–18; Micah 7:20; Psalms 106:45"> To do acts of mercy to our fathers and to keep in mind his holy word,</span>
<span className="v" id="v73" data-v="73" data-xref="Genesis 22:16–18; Psalms 105:9; Deuteronomy 7:8"> The oath which he made to Abraham, our father,</span>
<span className="v" id="v74" data-v="74" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7; Zephaniah 3:15–17; Isaiah 54:13–14"> That we, being made free from the fear of those who are against us, might give him worship,</span>
<span className="v" id="v75" data-v="75" data-xref="Ephesians 4:24; Ephesians 2:10; Titus 2:11–14"> In righteousness and holy living before him all our days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v76" data-v="76" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Isaiah 40:3–5; Mark 1:2–3"> And you, child, will be named the prophet of the Most High: you will go before the face of the Lord, to make ready his ways;</span>
<span className="v" id="v77" data-v="77" data-xref="Acts 5:31; Acts 2:38; Acts 10:43"> To give knowledge of salvation to his people, through the forgiveness of sins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v78" data-v="78" data-xref="Malachi 4:2; 2 Peter 1:19; Revelation 22:16"> Because of the loving mercies of our God, by which the dawn from heaven has come to us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v79" data-v="79" data-xref="Isaiah 9:2; Matthew 4:16; Acts 26:18"> To give light to those in dark places, and in the shade of death, so that our feet may be guided into the way of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v80" data-v="80" data-xref="Luke 2:40; Mark 1:3–4; Luke 2:52"> And the child became tall, and strong in spirit; and he was living in the waste land till the day when he came before the eyes of Israel.</span>
