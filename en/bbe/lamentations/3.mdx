<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 88:7; Job 19:21; Psalms 88:15–16"> I am the man who has seen trouble by the rod of his wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 30:26; Isaiah 59:9; Amos 5:18–20"> By him I have been made to go in the dark where there is no light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:20; Job 31:21; Isaiah 1:25"> Truly against me his hand has been turned again and again all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 38:13; Psalms 51:8; Psalms 31:9–10"> My flesh and my skin have been used up by him and my bones broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Lamentations 3:19; Jeremiah 23:15; Job 19:8"> He has put up a wall against me, shutting me in with bitter sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 88:5–6; Psalms 143:3; Psalms 143:7"> He has kept me in dark places, like those who have been long dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 19:8; Job 3:23; Jeremiah 40:4"> He has put a wall round me, so that I am not able to go out; he has made great the weight of my chain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 30:20; Psalms 22:2; Job 19:7"> Even when I send up a cry for help, he keeps my prayer shut out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 63:17; Lamentations 3:11; Isaiah 30:28"> He has put up a wall of cut stones about my ways, he has made my roads twisted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 10:9; Amos 5:18–20; Hosea 13:7–8"> He is like a bear waiting for me, like a lion in secret places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 6:1; Job 16:12–13; Lamentations 1:13"> By him my ways have been turned on one side and I have been pulled in bits; he has made me waste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 38:2; Job 7:20; Job 6:4"> With his bow bent, he has made me the mark for his arrows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 6:4; Job 41:28; Deuteronomy 32:23"> He has let loose his arrows into the inmost parts of my body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:7; Lamentations 3:63; Psalms 22:6–7"> I have become the sport of all the peoples; I am their song all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:15; Jeremiah 23:15; Ruth 1:20"> He has made my life nothing but pain, he has given me the bitter root in full measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 20:17; Jeremiah 6:26; Psalms 3:7"> By him my teeth have been broken with crushed stones, and I am bent low in the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 59:11; Zechariah 8:10; Jeremiah 8:15"> My soul is sent far away from peace, I have no more memory of good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 17:15; 1 Samuel 27:1; Psalms 116:11"> And I said, My strength is cut off, and my hope from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Lamentations 3:15; Lamentations 3:5; Psalms 89:50"> Keep in mind my trouble and my wandering, the bitter root and the poison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 42:11; Psalms 42:5–6; Job 21:6"> My soul still keeps the memory of them; and is bent down in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:3; Psalms 130:7; Psalms 77:7–11"> This I keep in mind, and because of this I have hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 86:15; Luke 1:50; Nehemiah 9:31"> It is through the Lord's love that we have not come to destruction, because his mercies have no limit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 10:23; Psalms 30:5; Isaiah 33:2"> They are new every morning; great is your good faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 73:26; Psalms 16:5; Lamentations 3:21"> I said to myself, The Lord is my heritage; and because of this I will have hope in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Micah 7:7–8; Isaiah 40:31; Psalms 130:5–6"> The Lord is good to those who are waiting for him, to the soul which is looking for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Psalms 37:34; Psalms 130:5"> It is good to go on hoping and quietly waiting for the salvation of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:1; Matthew 11:29–30; Psalms 119:71"> It is good for a man to undergo the yoke when he is young.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:17; Lamentations 2:10; Psalms 102:7"> Let him be seated by himself, saying nothing, because he has put it on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 40:4; Luke 15:18–19; Romans 3:19"> Let him put his mouth in the dust, if by chance there may be hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 16:10; Isaiah 50:6; Matthew 26:67"> Let his face be turned to him who gives him blows; let him be full of shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 94:14; Psalms 103:8–10; Psalms 77:7"> For the Lord does not give a man up for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 78:38; Psalms 30:5; Psalms 103:11"> For though he sends grief, still he will have pity in the full measure of his love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:11; Ezekiel 18:32; Hebrews 12:9–10"> For he has no pleasure in troubling and causing grief to the children of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Zechariah 9:11–12; Isaiah 51:22–23; Psalms 102:20"> In a man's crushing under his feet all the prisoners of the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> In his turning away the right of a man before the face of the Most High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 59:15; Habakkuk 1:13; Jeremiah 22:3"> In his doing wrong to a man in his cause, the Lord has no pleasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Psalms 33:9–11; Isaiah 46:10; Proverbs 16:9"> Who is able to say a thing, and give effect to it, if it has not been ordered by the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Amos 3:6; Jeremiah 32:42"> Do not evil and good come from the mouth of the Most High?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Proverbs 19:3; Micah 7:9; Genesis 4:5–7"> What protest may a living man make, even a man about the punishment of his sin?</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:5; Psalms 139:23–24; Joel 2:12–13"> Let us make search and put our ways to the test, turning again to the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Psalms 25:1; Psalms 28:2; Psalms 86:4"> Lifting up our hearts with our hands to God in the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 15:18–19; Nehemiah 9:26; 2 Kings 24:4"> We have done wrong and gone against your law; we have not had your forgiveness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Lamentations 2:17; Lamentations 2:21; Lamentations 3:66"> Covering yourself with wrath you have gone after us, cutting us off without pity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Lamentations 3:8; Zechariah 7:13; Psalms 97:2"> Covering yourself with a cloud, so that prayer may not get through.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:13; Lamentations 2:15; Lamentations 3:14"> You have made us like waste and that for which there is no use, among the peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 22:6–8; Lamentations 2:16; Psalms 44:13–14"> The mouths of all our haters are open wide against us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Isaiah 24:17–18; Isaiah 51:19; Jeremiah 48:43–44"> Fear and deep waters have come on us, wasting and destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Lamentations 2:11; Jeremiah 9:18; Jeremiah 9:1"> Rivers of water are running down from my eyes, for the destruction of the daughter of my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:17; Psalms 77:2; Lamentations 1:16"> My eyes are streaming without stopping, they have no rest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Isaiah 63:15; Lamentations 5:1; Daniel 9:16–19"> Till the Lord's eye is turned on me, till he sees my trouble from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:9; Luke 19:41–44; Lamentations 1:18"> The Lord is unkind to my soul, more than all the daughters of my town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; Psalms 35:7; Jeremiah 37:15–16"> They who are against me without cause have gone hard after me as if I was a bird;</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Daniel 6:17; Jeremiah 38:9; Jeremiah 38:6"> They have put an end to my life in the prison, stoning me with stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Jonah 2:3–5; Ezekiel 37:11; Lamentations 3:18"> Waters were flowing over my head; I said, I am cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Acts 16:24–28; Psalms 142:3–7; Jonah 2:2–4"> I was making prayer to your name, O Lord, out of the lowest prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Isaiah 38:5; Job 34:28; Psalms 6:8–9"> My voice came to you; let not your ear be shut to my breathing, to my cry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="James 4:8; Isaiah 41:10; Isaiah 58:9"> You came near in the day when I made my prayer to you: you said, Have no fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Psalms 34:22–1; Jeremiah 51:36; Psalms 103:4"> O Lord, you have taken up the cause of my soul, you have made my life safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Psalms 43:1; Psalms 9:4; Genesis 31:42"> O Lord, you have seen my wrong; be judge in my cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:19–20; Psalms 10:14; Lamentations 3:59"> You have seen all the evil rewards they have sent on me, and all their designs against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Lamentations 5:1; Psalms 89:50; Zephaniah 2:8"> Their bitter words have come to your ears, O Lord, and all their designs against me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:3; Psalms 59:7; Psalms 140:3"> The lips of those who came up against me, and their thoughts against me all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Psalms 139:2; Lamentations 3:14; Job 30:9"> Take note of them when they are seated, and when they get up; I am their song.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Psalms 28:4; Jeremiah 11:20; 2 Timothy 4:14"> You will give them their reward, O Lord, answering to the work of their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10; 1 Corinthians 16:22; Deuteronomy 2:30"> You will let their hearts be covered over with your curse on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Psalms 8:3; Lamentations 3:43; Psalms 115:16"> You will go after them in wrath, and put an end to them from under the heavens of the Lord.</span>
