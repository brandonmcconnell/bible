<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 1:7; Zephaniah 1:13; Psalms 79:1–2"> Our heritage is given up to men of strange lands, our houses to those who are not our countrymen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:21; Exodus 22:24; Jeremiah 15:8"> We are children without fathers, our mothers are like widows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 3:1; Ezekiel 4:9–17; Deuteronomy 28:48"> We give money for a drink of water, we get our wood for a price.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:36–37; Lamentations 1:14; Jeremiah 28:14"> Our attackers are on our necks: overcome with weariness, we have no rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 12:1; Hosea 9:3; Hosea 7:11"> We have given our hands to the Egyptians and to the Assyrians so that we might have enough bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:29; Jeremiah 16:12; Ezekiel 18:2"> Our fathers were sinners and are dead; and the weight of their evil-doing is on us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:15; Zechariah 11:6; Proverbs 30:22"> Servants are ruling over us, and there is no one to make us free from their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:16–17; Jeremiah 40:9–12; Ezekiel 12:18–19"> We put our lives in danger to get our bread, because of the sword of the waste land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Lamentations 4:8; Job 30:30; Psalms 119:83"> Our skin is heated like an oven because of our burning heat from need of food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 13:16; Zechariah 14:2; Deuteronomy 28:30"> They took by force the women in Zion, the virgins in the towns of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Lamentations 4:16; Isaiah 47:6; Lamentations 2:20"> Their hands put princes to death by hanging: the faces of old men were not honoured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 16:21; Exodus 11:5; Matthew 23:4"> The young men were crushing the grain, and the boys were falling under the wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:34; Isaiah 24:7–11; Ezekiel 26:13"> The old men are no longer seated in the doorway, and the music of the young men has come to an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Amos 8:10; Jeremiah 25:10; James 4:9–10"> The joy of our hearts is ended; our dancing is changed into sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 89:39; Jeremiah 13:18; Isaiah 3:9–11"> The crown has been taken from our head: sorrow is ours, for we are sinners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 1:5; Job 17:7; Lamentations 2:11"> Because of this our hearts are feeble; for these things our eyes are dark;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Micah 3:12; Jeremiah 9:11; Psalms 74:2–3"> Because of the mountain of Zion which is a waste; jackals go over it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 102:12; Hebrews 1:8–12; Psalms 45:6"> You, O Lord, are seated as King for ever; the seat of your power is eternal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 44:24; Psalms 13:1; Jeremiah 14:19–21"> Why have we gone from your memory for ever? why have you been turned away from us for so long?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 80:3; Psalms 80:7; Psalms 85:4"> Make us come back to you, O Lord, and let us be turned; make our days new again as in the past.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 60:1–2; Ezekiel 37:11; Psalms 44:9"> But you have quite given us up; you are full of wrath against us.</span>
