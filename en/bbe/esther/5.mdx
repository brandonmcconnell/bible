<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esther 6:4; Esther 4:16; Revelation 3:21"> Now on the third day, Esther put on her queen's robes, and took her place in the inner room of the king's house, facing the king's house: and the king was seated on his high seat in the king's house, facing the doorway of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esther 8:4; Esther 4:11; Proverbs 21:1"> And when the king saw Esther the queen waiting in the inner room, looking kindly on her he put out the rod of gold in his hand to her. So Esther came near and put her fingers on the top of the rod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 6:23; Esther 7:2; Esther 5:6"> Then the king said, What is your desire, Queen Esther, and what is your request? I will give it to you, even to the half of my kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esther 5:8; Proverbs 29:11; Esther 3:15"> And Esther in answer said, If it seems good to the king, let the king and Haman come today to the feast which I have made ready for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esther 6:14"> Then the king said, Let Haman come quickly, so that what Esther has said may be done. So the king and Haman came to the feast which Esther had made ready.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esther 7:2; Esther 5:3; Esther 9:12"> And while they were drinking wine the king said to Esther, What is your prayer? for it will be given to you and what is your request? for it will be done, even to the half of my kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Then Esther said in answer, My prayer and my request is this:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esther 7:3; Proverbs 16:9; Esther 8:5"> If I have the king's approval, and if it is the king's pleasure to give me my prayer and do my request, let the king and Haman come to the feast which I will make ready for them, and tomorrow I will do as the king has said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esther 3:5; Matthew 2:16; Psalms 27:3"> Then on that day Haman went out full of joy and glad in heart; but when he saw Mordecai in the king's doorway, and he did not get to his feet or give any sign of fear before him, Haman was full of wrath against Mordecai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esther 6:13; Ecclesiastes 7:9; 2 Samuel 13:22–23"> But controlling himself, he went to his house; and he sent for his friends and Zeresh, his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esther 9:7–10; Esther 3:1; Luke 12:19–20"> And he gave them an account of the glories of his wealth, and the number of children he had, and the ways in which he had been honoured by the king, and how he had put him over the captains and servants of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; Psalms 37:35–36; Job 20:5–8"> And Haman said further, Truly, Esther the queen let no man but myself come in to the feast which she had made ready for the king; and tomorrow again I am to be her guest with the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 15:20; Job 18:4; Philippians 4:11–12"> But all this is nothing to me while I see Mordecai the Jew seated by the king's doorway.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esther 6:4; Psalms 37:14; 1 Kings 21:7"> Then his wife Zeresh and all his friends said to him, Let a pillar, fifty cubits high, be made ready for hanging him, and in the morning get the king to give orders for the hanging of Mordecai: then you will be able to go to the feast with the king with a glad heart. And Haman was pleased with the suggestion, and he had the pillar made.</span>
