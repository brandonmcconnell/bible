<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Genesis 23:4; Genesis 36:7"> Now Jacob was living in the land where his father had made a place for himself, in the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 35:25–26; Genesis 35:22; 1 Samuel 2:22–24"> These are the generations of Jacob: Joseph, a boy seventeen years old, was looking after the flock, together with his brothers, the sons of Bilhah and Zilpah, his father's wives; and Joseph gave their father a bad account of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 3:35; Genesis 37:32; 2 Samuel 13:18"> Now the love which Israel had for Joseph was greater than his love for all his other children, because he got him when he was an old man: and he had a long coat made for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 27:41; 1 John 3:12; 1 John 4:20"> And because his brothers saw that Joseph was dearer to his father than all the others, they were full of hate for him, and would not say a kind word to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 28:12; Genesis 42:9; John 17:14"> Now Joseph had a dream, and he gave his brothers an account of it, which made their hate greater than ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 44:18; Judges 9:7"> And he said to them, Let me give you the story of my dream.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 44:14; Genesis 42:6; Genesis 43:26"> We were in the field, getting the grain stems together, and my grain kept upright, and yours came round and went down on the earth before mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:27; Acts 7:35; Hebrews 10:29"> And his brothers said to him, Are you to be our king? will you have authority over us? And because of his dream and his words, their hate for him became greater than ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 7:9–14; Genesis 37:7; Daniel 8:10"> Then he had another dream, and gave his brothers an account of it, saying, I have had another dream: the sun and the moon and eleven stars gave honour to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 27:29; Philippians 2:10–11; Isaiah 60:14"> And he gave word of it to his father and his brothers; but his father protesting said, What sort of a dream is this? am I and your mother and your brothers to go down on our faces to the earth before you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 7:9; Luke 2:19; Luke 2:51"> And his brothers were full of envy; but his father kept his words in mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 37:1; Genesis 33:18; Genesis 34:25–31"> Now his brothers went to keep watch over their father's flock in Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1–3; 1 Samuel 3:8; 1 Samuel 3:16"> And Israel said to Joseph, Are not your brothers with the flock in Shechem? come, I will send you to them. And he said to him, Here am I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 35:27; Luke 19:42; Psalms 125:5"> And he said to him, Go now, and see if your brothers are well and how the flock is; then come back and give me word. So he sent him out of the valley of Hebron, and he came to Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 4:27; Judges 4:22; Genesis 21:14"> And a man saw him wandering in the country, and said to him, What are you looking for?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:10; Song of Solomon 1:7"> And he said, I am looking for my brothers; please give me word of where they are keeping their flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 6:13"> And the man said, They have gone away from here, for they said in my hearing, Let us go to Dothan. So Joseph went after them and came up with them at Dothan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 23:12; Psalms 37:12; Psalms 37:32"> But they saw him when he was a long way off, and before he came near them they made a secret design against him to put him to death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 28:12; Genesis 37:11; Genesis 37:5"> Saying to one another, See, here comes this dreamer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Titus 3:3; Proverbs 27:4; Matthew 2:2–16"> Let us now put him to death and put his body into one of these holes, and we will say, An evil beast has put him to death: then we will see what becomes of his dreams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 42:22; Genesis 35:22; Joshua 10:28"> But Reuben, hearing these words, got him out of their hands, saying, Let us not take his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 22:12; Deuteronomy 13:9; Genesis 42:22"> Do not put him to a violent death, but let him be placed in one of the holes; this he said to keep him safe from their hands, with the purpose of taking him back to his father again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 37:3; Matthew 27:28; Genesis 42:21"> So when Joseph came to his brothers, they took off his long coat which he had on;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:6; Psalms 130:1–2; Zechariah 9:11"> And they took him and put him in the hole: now the hole had no water in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 37:28; Genesis 43:11; Jeremiah 8:22"> Then seating themselves, they took their meal: and looking up, they saw a travelling band of Ishmaelites, coming from Gilead on their way to Egypt, with spices and perfumes on their camels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 37:20; Genesis 4:10; Genesis 25:32"> And Judah said to his brothers, What profit is there in putting our brother to death and covering up his blood?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; Genesis 42:21; 1 Samuel 18:17"> Let us give him to these Ishmaelites for a price, and let us not put violent hands on him, for he is our brother, our flesh. And his brothers gave ear to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 7:9; Psalms 105:17; Genesis 45:4–5"> And some traders from Midian went by; so pulling Joseph up out of the hole, they gave him to the Ishmaelites for twenty bits of silver, and they took him to Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 1:20; Genesis 37:34; Genesis 44:13"> Now when Reuben came back to the hole, Joseph was not there; and giving signs of grief,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 42:13; Jeremiah 31:15; Genesis 42:32"> He went back to his brothers, and said, The child is gone; what am I to do?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 37:23; Proverbs 28:13; Genesis 37:3"> Then they took Joseph's coat, and put on it some of the blood from a young goat which they had put to death,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 15:30; Genesis 37:3; Genesis 44:20–23"> And they took the coat to their father, and said, We came across this; is it your son's coat or not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 37:20; Genesis 44:28; John 13:7"> And he saw that it was, and said, It is my son's coat; an evil beast has put him to death; without doubt Joseph has come to a cruel end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 37:29; 2 Samuel 3:31; Psalms 69:11"> Then Jacob, giving signs of grief, put on haircloth, and went on weeping for his son day after day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 42:38; Job 2:11; 2 Samuel 12:17"> And all his sons and all his daughters came to give him comfort, but he would not be comforted, saying with weeping, I will go down to the underworld to my son. So great was his father's sorrow for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 40:4; Genesis 37:28; 2 Kings 25:8"> And in Egypt the men of Midian gave him for a price to Potiphar, a captain of high position in Pharaoh's house.</span>
