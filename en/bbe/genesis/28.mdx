<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 28:3–4; Genesis 24:3; Genesis 27:46"> Then Isaac sent for Jacob, and blessing him, said, Do not take a wife from among the women of Canaan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 25:20; Hosea 12:12; Genesis 24:50"> But go to Paddan-aram, to the house of Bethuel, your mother's father, and there get yourself a wife from the daughters of Laban, your mother's brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 48:3; Genesis 35:11; Genesis 1:28"> And may God, the Ruler of all, give you his blessing, giving you fruit and increase, so that you may become an army of peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 3:14; Genesis 12:7; Ephesians 1:3"> And may God give you the blessing of Abraham, to you and to your seed, so that the land of your wanderings, which God gave to Abraham, may be your heritage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> So Isaac sent Jacob away: and he went to Paddan-aram, to Laban, son of Bethuel the Aramaean, the brother of Rebekah, the mother of Jacob and Esau.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 27:33; Genesis 28:1"> So when Esau saw that Isaac had given Jacob his blessing, and sent him away to Paddan-aram to get a wife for himself there, blessing him and saying to him, Do not take a wife from among the women of Canaan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1; Ephesians 6:3; Colossians 3:20"> And that Jacob had done as his father and mother said and had gone to Paddan-aram;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 24:3; Genesis 26:34–35; 1 Samuel 8:6"> It was clear to Esau that his father had no love for the women of Canaan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 36:3; Genesis 36:18; Genesis 36:13"> So Esau went to Ishmael and took Mahalath, the daughter of Abraham's son Ishmael, the sister of Nebaioth, to be his wife in addition to the wives he had.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 7:2; Genesis 11:31; Genesis 32:10"> So Jacob went out from Beer-sheba to go to Haran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 8:20; Genesis 28:18; Genesis 31:46"> And coming to a certain place, he made it his resting-place for the night, for the sun had gone down; and he took one of the stones which were there, and putting it under his head he went to sleep in that place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 1:51; Numbers 12:6; Genesis 32:1–2"> And he had a dream, and in his dream he saw steps stretching from earth to heaven, and the angels of God were going up and down on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 13:15; Genesis 48:3; Genesis 35:12"> And he saw the Lord by his side, saying, I am the Lord, the God of Abraham your father, and the God of Isaac: I will give to you and to your seed this land on which you are sleeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Acts 3:25; Genesis 18:18"> Your seed will be like the dust of the earth, covering all the land to the west and to the east, to the north and to the south: you and your seed will be a name of blessing to all the families of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Isaiah 41:10; Jeremiah 1:19"> And truly, I will be with you, and will keep you wherever you go, guiding you back again to this land; and I will not give you up till I have done what I have said to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 5:15; Exodus 3:5; Exodus 15:11"> And Jacob, awaking from his sleep, said, Truly, the Lord is in this place and I was not conscious of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:15; Genesis 35:1–13; Ecclesiastes 5:1"> And fear came on him, and he said, This is a holy place; this is nothing less than the house of God and the doorway of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 35:14; Genesis 31:45; Genesis 31:13"> And early in the morning Jacob took the stone which had been under his head, and put it up as a pillar and put oil on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hosea 12:4–5; Genesis 35:1; Genesis 48:3"> And he gave that place the name of Beth-el, but before that time the town was named Luz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:8; 2 Samuel 15:8; Genesis 31:13"> Then Jacob took an oath, and said, If God will be with me, and keep me safe on my journey, and give me food and clothing to put on,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 11:31; Deuteronomy 26:17; 2 Samuel 19:24"> So that I come again to my father's house in peace, then I will take the Lord to be my God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 35:7; Genesis 14:20; Deuteronomy 14:22–23"> And this stone which I have put up for a pillar will be God's house: and of all you give me, I will give a tenth part to you.</span>
