<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 17:17"> Now the years of Sarah's life were a hundred and twenty-seven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 23:19; Genesis 13:18; 1 Chronicles 6:57"> And Sarah's death took place in Kiriath-arba, that is, Hebron, in the land of Canaan: and Abraham went into his house, weeping and sorrowing for Sarah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 10:15; Genesis 23:7; 1 Samuel 26:6"> And Abraham came from his dead and said to the children of Heth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:15; Hebrews 11:9; Genesis 17:8"> I am living among you as one from a strange country: give me some land here as my property, so that I may put my dead to rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And in answer the children of Heth said to Abraham,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 24:35; Genesis 14:14; Exodus 32:22"> My lord, truly you are a great chief among us; take the best of our resting-places for your dead; not one of us will keep back from you a place where you may put your dead to rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 12:14; Genesis 19:1; Proverbs 18:24"> And Abraham got up and gave honour to the children of Heth, the people of that land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 25:9; 1 John 2:1–2; 1 Kings 2:17"> And he said to them, If you will let me put my dead to rest here, make a request for me to Ephron, the son of Zohar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 13:8; Romans 12:17"> That he will give me the hollow in the rock named Machpelah, which is his property at the end of his field; let him give it to me for its full price as a resting-place for my dead among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 34:20; Genesis 34:24; Genesis 23:18"> Now Ephron was seated among the children of Heth: and Ephron the Hittite gave Abraham his answer in the hearing of the children of Heth, and of all those who came into his town, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:24; Isaiah 32:8; Deuteronomy 19:15"> No, my lord, I will give you the field with the hollow in the rock; before all the children of my people will I give it to you for a resting-place for your dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 23:7; Genesis 18:2; Genesis 19:1"> And Abraham went down on his face before the people of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 13:8; 2 Samuel 24:24; Genesis 14:22–23"> And Abraham said to Ephron, in the hearing of the people of the land, If only you will give ear to me, I will give you the price of the field; take it, and let me put my dead to rest there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> So Ephron said to Abraham,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:12; Exodus 30:13; Exodus 30:15"> My lord, give ear to me: the value of the land is four hundred shekels; what is that between me and you? so put your dead to rest there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 11:12; Jeremiah 32:9; Exodus 30:13"> And Abraham took note of the price fixed by Ephron in the hearing of the children of Heth, and gave him four hundred shekels in current money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 25:9; Genesis 50:13; Genesis 23:20"> So Ephron's field at Machpelah near Mamre, with the hollow in the rock and all the trees in the field and round it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ruth 4:1; Jeremiah 32:12; Genesis 34:20"> Became the property of Abraham before the eyes of the children of Heth and of all who came into the town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 50:13; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Ecclesiastes 12:5"> Then Abraham put Sarah his wife to rest in the hollow rock in the field of Machpelah near Mamre, that is, Hebron in the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:10–14; Ruth 4:7–10; Genesis 49:31–32"> And the field and the hollow rock were handed over to Abraham as his property by the children of Heth.</span>
