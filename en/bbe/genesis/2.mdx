<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 31:17; Exodus 20:11; Psalms 33:6"> And the heaven and the earth and all things in them were complete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 4:4; Exodus 31:17; Deuteronomy 5:14"> And on the seventh day God came to the end of all his work; and on the seventh day he took his rest from all the work which he had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:12; Mark 2:27; Leviticus 23:3"> And God gave his blessing to the seventh day and made it holy: because on that day he took his rest from all the work which he had made and done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 5:1; Revelation 1:8; Genesis 1:4"> These are the generations of the heaven and the earth when they were made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 3:23; Hebrews 6:7; Job 5:10"> In the day when the Lord God made earth and heaven there were no plants of the field on the earth, and no grass had come up: for the Lord God had not sent rain on the earth and there was no man to do work on the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> But a mist went up from the earth, watering all the face of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:7; Job 33:4; 1 Corinthians 15:45"> And the Lord God made man from the dust of the earth, breathing into him the breath of life: and man became a living soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13; Genesis 3:24; Genesis 13:10"> And the Lord God made a garden in the east, in Eden; and there he put the man whom he had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 3:22; Revelation 2:7; Revelation 22:14"> And out of the earth the Lord made every tree to come, delighting the eye and good for food; and in the middle of the garden, the tree of life and the tree of the knowledge of good and evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; Psalms 46:4"> And a river went out of Eden giving water to the garden; and from there it was parted and became four streams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 25:18; Genesis 10:29; 1 Samuel 15:7"> The name of the first is Pishon, which goes round about all the land of Havilah where there is gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 28:20; Numbers 11:7; Ezekiel 28:13"> And the gold of that land is good: there is bdellium and the onyx stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 10:6; Isaiah 11:11"> And the name of the second river is Gihon: this river goes round all the land of Cush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 10:4; Genesis 15:18; Deuteronomy 1:7"> And the name of the third river is Tigris, which goes to the east of Assyria. And the fourth river is Euphrates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 128:2; Genesis 2:8; Ephesians 4:28"> And the Lord God took the man and put him in the garden of Eden to do work in it and take care of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:22; 1 Timothy 4:4; 1 Timothy 6:17"> And the Lord God gave the man orders, saying, You may freely take of the fruit of every tree of the garden:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 6:23; James 1:15; Genesis 3:19"> But of the fruit of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil you may not take; for on the day when you take of it, death will certainly come to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:9–12; 1 Corinthians 11:7–12; Proverbs 18:22"> And the Lord God said, It is not good for the man to be by himself: I will make one like himself as a help to him</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 8:4–8; Genesis 1:28; Genesis 9:2"> And from the earth the Lord God made every beast of the field and every bird of the air, and took them to the man to see what names he would give them: and whatever name he gave to any living thing, that was its name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 2:18"> And the man gave names to all cattle and to the birds of the air and to every beast of the field; but Adam had no one like himself as a help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 15:12; Job 4:13; 1 Samuel 26:12"> And the Lord God sent a deep sleep on the man, and took one of the bones from his side while he was sleeping, joining up the flesh again in its place:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:8–9; 1 Timothy 2:13; Hebrews 13:4"> And the bone which the Lord God had taken from the man he made into a woman, and took her to the man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ephesians 5:28–30; 1 Corinthians 11:8–9; 2 Samuel 5:1"> And the man said, This is now bone of my bone and flesh of my flesh: let her name be Woman because she was taken out of Man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 19:3–9; Ephesians 5:28–31; Mark 10:6–12"> For this cause will a man go away from his father and his mother and be joined to his wife; and they will be one flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 3:7; Genesis 3:10–11; Romans 10:11"> And the man and his wife were without clothing, and they had no sense of shame.</span>
