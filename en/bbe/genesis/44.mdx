<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 30:27; Leviticus 19:26; 2 Kings 21:6"> Is not this the cup from which my lord takes wine and by which he gets knowledge of the future? Truly, you have done evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> So he overtook them and said these words to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 38:16–18; Genesis 37:18–32; 2 Kings 8:13"> And they said to him, Why does my lord say such words as these? far be it from your servants to do such a thing:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 43:12; Genesis 42:35; Romans 13:9"> See, the money which was in the mouth of our bags we gave back to you when we came again from Canaan: how then might we take silver or gold from your lord's house?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 31:32; Psalms 7:3–5; Job 31:38–40"> If it comes to light that any of your servants has done this, let him be put to death, and we will be your lord's servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 22:3; Genesis 44:17; Genesis 44:33"> And he said, Let it be as you say: he in whose bag it is seen will become my servant; and you will not be responsible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Then every man quickly got his bag down and undid it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 44:26–32; Genesis 42:36–38; Genesis 43:14"> And he made a search, starting with the oldest and ending with the youngest; and the cup was in Benjamin's bag.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:11; Genesis 37:29–34; 2 Samuel 1:2"> Then in bitter grief they put the bags on the asses again and went back to the town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 50:18; Genesis 43:25; Genesis 43:16"> So Judah and his brothers came to Joseph's house; and he was still there: and they went down on their faces before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 4:10; Genesis 21:26; Genesis 3:13"> And Joseph said, What is this thing which you have done? had you no thought that such a man as I would have power to see what is secret?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 44:9; Daniel 9:7; Numbers 32:23"> And Judah said, What are we to say to my lord? how may we put ourselves right in his eyes? God has made clear the sin of your servants: now we are in your hands, we and the man in whose bag your cup was seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 17:15; 2 Samuel 23:3; Genesis 18:25"> Then he said, Far be it from me to do so: but the man who had my cup will be my servant; and you may go back to your father in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 32:22; Genesis 41:40; Genesis 18:30"> Then Judah came near him, and said, Let your servant say a word in my lord's ears, and let not your wrath be burning against your servant: for you are in the place of Pharaoh to us.</span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 37:3; Genesis 42:38; Luke 7:12"> And we said to my lord, We have an old father and a young child, whom he had when he was old; his brother is dead and he is the only son of his mother, and is very dear to his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 42:15; Genesis 42:20; Amos 9:4"> And you said to your servants, Let him come down to me with you, so that I may see him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 44:30; Genesis 42:38"> And we said to my lord, His father will not let him go; for if he went away his father would come to his death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 43:5; Genesis 43:3; Genesis 42:15–20"> But you said to your servants, If your youngest brother does not come with you, you will not see my face again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 42:29–34"> And when we went back to your servant, our father, we gave him an account of my lord's words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 43:2; Genesis 43:5"> And our father said, Go again and get us a little food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 11:7; Genesis 43:4–5"> And we said, Only if our youngest brother goes with us will we go down; for we may not see the man's face again if our youngest brother is not with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 46:19; Genesis 35:16–18; Genesis 30:22–25"> And our father said to us, You have knowledge that my wife gave me two sons;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 37:33; Genesis 37:13–14; Genesis 42:36"> The one went away from me, and I said, Truly he has come to a violent death; and from that time I have not seen him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 42:38; Genesis 43:14; Deuteronomy 31:17"> If now you take this one from me, and some evil comes to him, you will make my grey head go down in sorrow to the underworld.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:1; 2 Samuel 18:33; Genesis 44:31"> If then I go back to your servant, my father, without the boy, because his life and the boy's life are one,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 44:29; Genesis 37:35; 2 Corinthians 7:10"> When he sees that the boy is not with us, he will come to his death, and our father's grey head will go down in sorrow to the underworld.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 43:8–9; Genesis 43:16"> For I made myself responsible for the boy to my father, saying, If I do not give him safely back to you, let mine be the sin for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 John 3:16; Exodus 32:32; Hebrews 7:22"> So now let me be my lord's servant here in place of the boy, and let him go back with his brothers.</span>
