<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 91:11; Psalms 34:7; Hebrews 1:4"> And on his way Jacob came face to face with the angels of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:8; Joshua 5:14; Joshua 21:38"> And when he saw them he said, This is the army of God: so he gave that place the name of Mahanaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 25:30; Joshua 24:4; Deuteronomy 2:5"> Now Jacob sent servants before him to Esau, his brother, in the land of Seir, the country of Edom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 3:6; Luke 14:11; Genesis 33:8"> And he gave them orders to say these words to Esau: Your servant Jacob says, Till now I have been living with Laban:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 33:8; Genesis 33:15; Genesis 33:11"> And I have oxen and asses and flocks and men-servants and women-servants: and I have sent to give my lord news of these things so that I may have grace in his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 33:1; Genesis 32:8; Genesis 32:11"> When the servants came back they said, We have seen your brother Esau and he is coming out to you, and four hundred men with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 35:3; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10; Exodus 14:10"> Then Jacob was in great fear and trouble of mind: and he put all the people and the flocks and the herds and the camels into two groups;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 10:16; Genesis 33:1–3"> And said, If Esau, meeting one group, makes an attack on them, the others will get away safely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 31:42; Psalms 34:4–6; Genesis 31:3"> Then Jacob said, O God of my father Abraham, the God of my father Isaac, the Lord who said to me, Go back to your country and your family and I will be good to you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 18:35; Genesis 24:27; 2 Samuel 7:18"> I am less than nothing in comparison with all your mercies and your faith to me your servant; for with only my stick in my hand I went across Jordan, and now I have become two armies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 59:1–2; Proverbs 18:19; Matthew 6:13"> Be my saviour from the hand of Esau, my brother: for my fear is that he will make an attack on me, putting to death mother and child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 28:13–15; Hebrews 6:17; Titus 1:2"> And you said, Truly, I will be good to you, and make your seed like the sand of the sea which may not be numbered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 18:16; Genesis 43:11; Proverbs 19:6"> Then he put up his tent there for the night; and from among his goods he took, as an offering for his brother Esau,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 1:3; Deuteronomy 8:18; Job 42:12"> Two hundred she-goats and twenty he-goats, two hundred females and twenty males from the sheep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Thirty camels with their young ones, forty cows, ten oxen, twenty asses, and ten young asses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 33:8–9; Genesis 32:20; Proverbs 2:11"> These he gave to his servants, every herd by itself, and he said to his servants, Go on before me, and let there be a space between one herd and another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 33:3"> And he gave orders to the first, saying, When my brother Esau comes to you and says, Whose servant are you, and where are you going, and whose are these herds?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 32:4–5"> Then say to him, These are your servant Jacob's; they are an offering for my lord, for Esau; and he himself is coming after us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And he gave the same orders to the second and the third and to all those who were with the herds, saying, This is what you are to say to Esau when you see him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 42:8–9; Proverbs 21:14; Genesis 43:11"> And you are to say further, Jacob, your servant, is coming after us. For he said to himself, I will take away his wrath by the offering which I have sent on, and then I will come before him: it may be that I will have grace in his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> So the servants with the offerings went on in front, and he himself took his rest that night in the tents with his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:16; Deuteronomy 2:37; 1 Timothy 5:8"> And in the night he got up, and taking with him his two wives and the two servant-women and his eleven children, he went over the river Jabbok.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> He took them and sent them over the stream with all he had.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hosea 12:3–5; Ephesians 6:12; Luke 13:24"> Then Jacob was by himself; and a man was fighting with him till dawn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:7–9; Hosea 12:3–4; Matthew 26:41"> But when the man saw that he was not able to overcome Jacob, he gave him a blow in the hollow part of his leg, so that his leg was damaged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hosea 12:4; Psalms 115:12–13; 2 Corinthians 12:8–9"> And he said to him, Let me go now, for the dawn is near. But Jacob said, I will not let you go till you have given me your blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Then he said, What is your name? And he said, Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 2:17; Genesis 35:10; Isaiah 62:2–4"> And he said, Your name will no longer be Jacob, but Israel: for in your fight with God and with men you have overcome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6; Judges 13:16–18; Job 11:7"> Then Jacob said, What is your name? And he said, What is my name to you? Then he gave him a blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 24:10–11; Numbers 12:8; Isaiah 6:5"> And Jacob gave that place the name of Peniel, saying, I have seen God face to face, and still I am living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:7; Genesis 19:15; Judges 8:8"> And while he was going past Peniel, the sun came up. And he went with unequal steps because of his damaged leg.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:5"> For this reason the children of Israel, even today, never take that muscle in the hollow of the leg as food, because the hollow of Jacob's leg was touched.</span>
