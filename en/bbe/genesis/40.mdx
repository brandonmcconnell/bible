<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 40:13; Genesis 39:20–23; Esther 6:1"> Now after these things the chief servant who had the care of the wine, and the chief bread-maker in Pharaoh's house, did something against Pharaoh's orders;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 16:14; Proverbs 19:12; Psalms 76:10"> And Pharaoh was angry with his two servants, with the chief wine-servant and the chief bread-maker;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 39:20; Genesis 39:23"> And he put them in prison under the care of the captain of the army, in the same prison where Joseph himself was shut up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 39:1; Psalms 37:5; Genesis 37:36"> And the captain put them in Joseph's care, and he did what was needed for them; and they were kept in prison for some time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 41:11; Genesis 40:8; Genesis 37:5–10"> And these two had a dream on the same night; the chief wine-servant and the chief bread-maker of the king of Egypt, who were in prison, the two of them had dreams with a special sense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 41:8; Daniel 2:1–3; Genesis 40:8"> And in the morning when Joseph came to them he saw that they were looking sad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:2; 1 Samuel 1:8; Luke 24:17"> And he said to the servants of Pharaoh who were in prison with him, Why are you looking so sad?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 2:47; Genesis 41:15–16; Daniel 2:28"> Then they said to him, We have had a dream, and no one is able to give us the sense. And Joseph said, Does not the sense of dreams come from God? what was your dream?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 7:13–15; Genesis 37:5–10; Daniel 4:8"> Then the chief wine-servant gave Joseph an account of his dream, and said, In my dream I saw a vine before me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And on the vine were three branches; and it seemed as if it put out buds and flowers, and from them came grapes ready for cutting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:11–1; 1 Kings 10:5; 2 Chronicles 9:4"> And Pharaoh's cup was in my hand, and I took the grapes and crushing them into Pharaoh's cup, gave the cup into Pharaoh's hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 41:12; Judges 7:14; Genesis 41:25–26"> Then Joseph said, This is the sense of your dream: the three branches are three days;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 3:3; Jeremiah 52:31; 2 Kings 25:27"> After three days Pharaoh will give you honour, and put you back into your place, and you will give him his cup as you did before, when you were his wine-servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 2:12; Luke 23:42; 1 Samuel 20:14–15"> But keep me in mind when things go well for you, and be good to me and say a good word for me to Pharaoh and get me out of this prison:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:17–18; Genesis 39:20; 1 Timothy 1:10"> For truly I was taken by force from the land of the Hebrews; and I have done nothing for which I might be put in prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 40:1–2"> Now when the chief bread-maker saw that the first dream had a good sense, he said to Joseph, I had a dream; and in my dream there were three baskets of white bread on my head;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 49:20; 1 Chronicles 12:20"> And in the top basket were all sorts of cooked meats for Pharaoh; and the birds were taking them out of the baskets on my head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 40:12; Genesis 41:26; 1 Corinthians 11:24"> Then Joseph said, This is the sense of your dream: the three baskets are three days;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 40:22; Acts 20:27; 2 Samuel 21:6"> After three days Pharaoh will take you out of prison, hanging you on a tree, so that your flesh will be food for birds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 40:19; Genesis 40:13; Matthew 14:6"> Now the third day was Pharaoh's birthday, and he gave a feast for all his servants; and he gave honour to the chief wine-servant and the chief bread-maker among the others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 40:13; Nehemiah 2:1"> And he put the chief wine-servant back in his old place; and he gave the cup into Pharaoh's hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 40:19; Genesis 41:16; Daniel 2:30"> But the chief bread-maker was put to death by hanging, as Joseph had said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 31:12; Job 19:14; Psalms 105:19"> But the wine-servant did not keep Joseph in mind or give a thought to him.</span>
