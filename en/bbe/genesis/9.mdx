<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 9:7; Genesis 1:28; Genesis 8:17"> And God gave his blessing to Noah and his sons, and said, Be fertile, and have increase, and make the earth full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 8:4–8; Genesis 1:28; James 3:7"> And the fear of you will be strong in every beast of the earth and every bird of the air; everything which goes on the land, and all the fishes of the sea, are given into your hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 14:14; Colossians 2:21–22; 1 Corinthians 10:31"> Every living and moving thing will be food for you; I give them all to you as before I gave you all green things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:23; Deuteronomy 15:23; Deuteronomy 12:16"> But flesh with the life-blood in it you may not take for food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 21:28–29; Exodus 21:12; Genesis 4:9–10"> And for your blood, which is your life, will I take payment; from every beast I will take it, and from every man will I take payment for the blood of his brother-man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 24:17; Matthew 26:52; Revelation 13:10"> Whoever takes a man's life, by man will his life be taken; because God made man in his image.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 1:28; Genesis 9:1; Genesis 9:19"> And now, be fertile and have increase; have offspring on the earth and become great in number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And God said to Noah and to his sons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 6:18; Isaiah 54:9–10; Romans 1:3"> Truly, I will make my agreement with you and with your seed after you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 8:1; Genesis 9:15–16; Psalms 36:5–6"> And with every living thing with you, all birds and cattle and every beast of the earth which comes out of the ark with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 54:9; Genesis 8:21–22; Genesis 7:21–23"> And I will make my agreement with you; never again will all flesh be cut off by the waters; never again will the waters come over all the earth for its destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 17:11; Matthew 26:26–28; Exodus 12:13"> And God said, This is the sign of the agreement which I make between me and you and every living thing with you, for all future generations:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:28; Revelation 4:3; Revelation 10:1"> I will put my bow in the cloud and it will be for a sign of the agreement between me and the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And whenever I make a cloud come over the earth, the bow will be seen in the cloud,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:9; Ezekiel 16:60; Isaiah 54:8–10"> And I will keep in mind the agreement between me and you and every living thing; and never again will there be a great flow of waters causing destruction to all flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 54:8–10; Jeremiah 32:40; 2 Samuel 23:5"> And the bow will be in the cloud, and looking on it, I will keep in mind the eternal agreement between God and every living thing on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And God said to Noah, This is the sign of the agreement which I have made between me and all flesh on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 10:1; Genesis 10:6; Genesis 9:25–27"> And the sons of Noah who went out of the ark were Shem, Ham, and Japheth; and Ham is the father of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:4–28; Genesis 8:17; Genesis 5:32"> These three were the sons of Noah and from them all the earth was peopled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:6; Proverbs 10:11; Isaiah 28:24–26"> In those days Noah became a farmer, and he made a vine-garden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Habakkuk 2:15–16; Proverbs 23:31–32"> And he took of the wine of it and was overcome by drink; and he was uncovered in his tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 30:17; Galatians 6:1; Matthew 18:15"> And Ham, the father of Canaan, saw his father unclothed, and gave news of it to his two brothers outside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 6:1; Exodus 20:12; 1 Timothy 5:17"> And Shem and Japheth took a robe, and putting it on their backs went in with their faces turned away, and put it over their father so that they might not see him unclothed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And, awaking from his wine, Noah saw what his youngest son had done to him, and he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 9:23; Deuteronomy 27:16; Judges 1:28–30"> Cursed be Canaan; let him be a servant of servants to his brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 144:15; Genesis 12:1–3; Luke 3:23–36"> And he said, Praise to the Lord, the God of Shem; let Canaan be his servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 11:12; Romans 15:12; Hebrews 11:9–10"> May God make Japheth great, and let his living-place be in the tents of Shem, and let Canaan be his servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Noah went on living three hundred and fifty years after the great flow of waters;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 5:27; Psalms 90:10; Genesis 5:32"> all the years of his life were nine hundred and fifty: and he came to his end.</span>
