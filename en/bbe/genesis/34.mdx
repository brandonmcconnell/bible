<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 30:21; Jeremiah 2:36; Genesis 30:13"> Now Dinah, the daughter whom Leah had by Jacob, went out to see the women of that country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 5:28; Job 31:1; Job 31:9"> And when Shechem, the son of Hamor the Hivite who was the chief of that land, saw her, he took her by force and had connection with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 2:14; Isaiah 40:2; 2 Chronicles 30:22"> Then his heart went out in love to Dinah, the daughter of Jacob, and he said comforting words to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 14:2; 2 Samuel 13:13; Genesis 21:21"> And Shechem said to Hamor, his father, Get me this girl for my wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:22; Leviticus 10:3; 1 Samuel 10:27"> Now Jacob had word of what Shechem had done to his daughter; but his sons were in the fields with the cattle, and Jacob said nothing till they came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Then Hamor, the father of Shechem, came out to have a talk with Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 7:15; Judges 20:6; Deuteronomy 22:21"> Now the sons of Jacob came in from the fields when they had news of it, and they were wounded and very angry because of the shame he had done in Israel by having connection with Jacob's daughter; and they said, Such a thing is not to be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 11:2; Psalms 119:20; Psalms 84:2"> But Hamor said to them, Shechem, my son, is full of desire for your daughter: will you then give her to him for a wife?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:3; Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 19:14"> And let our two peoples be joined together; give your daughters to us, and take our daughters for yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 20:15; Genesis 47:27; Genesis 42:34"> Go on living with us, and the country will be open to you; do trade and get property there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 33:15; Genesis 18:3"> And Shechem said to her father and her brothers, If you will give ear to my request, whatever you say I will give to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 22:16–17; Deuteronomy 22:28–29; 1 Samuel 18:25–27"> However great you make the bride-price and payment, I will give it; only let me have the girl for my wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 25:27–34; Isaiah 59:13; Job 13:4"> But the sons of Jacob gave a false answer to Shechem and Hamor his father, because of what had been done to Dinah their sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 23:1–39; 2 Samuel 15:7; Genesis 17:11"> And they said, It is not possible for us to give our sister to one who is without circumcision, for that would be a cause of shame to us:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 4:12"> But on this condition only will we come to an agreement with you: if every male among you becomes like us and undergoes circumcision;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Then we will give our daughters to you and take your daughters to us and go on living with you as one people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> But if you will not undergo circumcision as we say, then we will take our daughter and go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And their words were pleasing to Hamor and his son Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 17:12; Acts 13:50; 1 Samuel 22:14"> And without loss of time the young man did as they said, because he had delight in Jacob's daughter, and he was the noblest of his father's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ruth 4:1; Proverbs 31:23; Amos 5:12"> Then Hamor and Shechem, his son, went to the meeting-place of their town, and said to the men of the town,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> It is the desire of these men to be at peace with us; let them then go on living in this country and doing trade here, for the country is wide open before them; let us take their daughters as wives and let us give them our daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> But these men will make an agreement with us to go on living with us and to become one people, only on the condition that every male among us undergoes circumcision as they have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 1:12–13; John 6:26–27; John 2:16"> Then will not their cattle and their goods and all their beasts be ours? so let us come to an agreement with them so that they may go on living with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 23:10; 1 Corinthians 7:19; Isaiah 1:10–16"> Then all the men of the town gave ear to the words of Hamor and Shechem his son; and every male in the town underwent circumcision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 49:5–7; Genesis 29:33–34; Numbers 31:7"> But on the third day after, before the wounds were well, two of the sons of Jacob, Simeon and Levi, Dinah's brothers, took their swords and came into the town by surprise and put all the males to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:26; Deuteronomy 32:42; Isaiah 31:8"> And Hamor and his son they put to death with the sword, and they took Dinah from Shechem's house and went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 7:13; Joshua 7:21; Exodus 2:14"> And the sons of Jacob came on them when they were wounded and made waste the town because of what had been done to their sister;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 1:15–16; Deuteronomy 8:17–18; Job 20:5"> They took their flocks and their herds and their asses and everything in their town and in their fields,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And all their wealth and all their little ones and their wives; everything in their houses they took and made them waste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 105:12; Joshua 7:25; Exodus 5:21"> And Jacob said to Simeon and Levi, You have made trouble for me and given me a bad name among the people of this country, among the Canaanites and the Perizzites: and because we are small in number they will come together against me and make war on me; and it will be the end of me and all my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 6:34; Genesis 34:13; Genesis 49:7"> But they said, Were we to let him make use of our sister as a loose woman?</span>
