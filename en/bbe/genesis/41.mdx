<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 20:3; Genesis 29:14; Exodus 4:9"> Now after two years had gone by, Pharaoh had a dream; and in his dream he was by the side of the Nile;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 19:6"> And out of the Nile came seven cows, good-looking and fat, and their food was the river-grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 41:20–21; Genesis 41:4"> And after them seven other cows came out of the Nile, poor-looking and thin; and they were by the side of the other cows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 3:15"> And the seven thin cows made a meal of the seven fat cows. Then Pharaoh came out of his sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:14"> But he went to sleep again and had a second dream, in which he saw seven heads of grain, full and good, all on one stem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:10; Hosea 13:15; Ezekiel 19:12"> And after them came up seven other heads, thin and wasted by the east wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 37:5; Genesis 20:3"> And the seven thin heads made a meal of the good heads. And when Pharaoh was awake he saw it was a dream.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 4:7; Exodus 7:22; Matthew 2:1"> And in the morning his spirit was troubled; and he sent for all the wise men of Egypt and all the holy men, and put his dream before them, but no one was able to give him the sense of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 40:14; Genesis 40:23; Genesis 40:1–3"> Then the chief wine-servant said to Pharaoh, The memory of my sin comes back to me now;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 39:20; Genesis 40:2–3; Genesis 37:36"> Pharaoh had been angry with his servants, and had put me in prison in the house of the captain of the army, together with the chief bread-maker;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 40:5–8"> And we had a dream on the same night, the two of us, and the dreams had a special sense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 40:12–19; Genesis 37:36; Genesis 39:1"> And there was with us a young Hebrew, the captain's servant, and when we put our dreams before him, he gave us the sense of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:3; Genesis 40:20–22; Genesis 40:12"> And it came about as he said: I was put back in my place, and the bread-maker was put to death by hanging.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 2:25; Psalms 113:7–8; Psalms 105:19–22"> Then Pharaoh sent for Joseph, and they took him quickly out of prison; and when his hair had been cut and his dress changed, he came before Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 5:16; Daniel 5:12; Psalms 25:14"> And Pharaoh said to Joseph, I have had a dream, and no one is able to give me the sense of it; now it has come to my ears that you are able to give the sense of a dream when it is put before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:5; Genesis 40:8; Daniel 2:47"> Then Joseph said, Without God there will be no answer of peace for Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then Pharaoh said, In my dream I was by the side of the Nile:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:8; Jeremiah 24:1–3; Jeremiah 24:5"> And out of the Nile came seven cows, fat and good-looking, and their food was the river-grass;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Then after them came seven other cows, very thin and poor-looking, worse than any I ever saw in the land of Egypt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And the thin cows made a meal of the seven fat cows who came up first;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:3; Psalms 37:19; Revelation 10:9–10"> And even with the fat cows inside them they seemed as bad as before. And so I came out of my sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And again in a dream I saw seven heads of grain, full and good, coming up on one stem:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 129:6–7; Hosea 13:15; 2 Kings 19:26"> And then I saw seven other heads, dry, thin, and wasted by the east wind, coming up after them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 4:7; Genesis 41:8; Exodus 8:19"> And the seven thin heads made a meal of the seven good heads; and I put this dream before the wise men, but not one of them was able to give me the sense of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 2:45; Revelation 4:1; Ephesians 1:17"> Then Joseph said, These two dreams have the same sense: God has made clear to Pharaoh what he is about to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 41:47; Genesis 41:2; Genesis 41:53"> The seven fat cows are seven years, and the seven good heads of grain are seven years: the two have the same sense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 8:1; 2 Samuel 24:19"> The seven thin and poor-looking cows who came up after them are seven years; and the seven heads of grain, dry and wasted by the east wind, are seven years when there will be no food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 41:25; Genesis 41:16"> As I said to Pharaoh before, God has made clear to him what he is about to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 41:26; Genesis 41:46–47; Genesis 41:49"> Seven years are coming in which there will be great wealth of grain in Egypt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 47:13; Genesis 41:54; James 5:17"> And after that will come seven years when there will not be enough food; and the memory of the good years will go from men's minds; and the land will be made waste by the bad years;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 24:20; 1 Samuel 5:6"> And men will have no memory of the good time because of the need which will come after, for it will be very bitter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Isaiah 46:10–11; Isaiah 14:24–27"> And this dream came to Pharaoh twice, because this thing is certain, and God will quickly make it come about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 41:39; Acts 6:3; Deuteronomy 1:13"> And now let Pharaoh make search for a man of wisdom and good sense, and put him in authority over the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 22:3; Luke 16:5; Proverbs 6:6–8"> Let Pharaoh do this, and let him put overseers over the land of Egypt to put in store a fifth part of the produce of the land in the good years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 45:6–7; Genesis 41:56; Exodus 4:13"> And let them get together all the food in those good years and make a store of grain under Pharaoh's control for the use of the towns, and let them keep it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 47:13–25"> And let that food be kept in store for the land till the seven bad years which are to come in Egypt; so that the land may not come to destruction through need of food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 7:10; 1 Kings 21:2; 2 Samuel 3:36"> And this seemed good to Pharaoh and to all his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Job 32:8; Daniel 5:14; Numbers 27:18"> Then Pharaoh said to his servants, Where may we get such a man as this, a man in whom is the spirit of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 41:33; Genesis 41:28; Genesis 41:25"> And Pharaoh said to Joseph, Seeing that God has made all this clear to you, there is no other man of such wisdom and good sense as you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Proverbs 22:29; Acts 7:10; Psalms 105:21–22"> You, then, are to be over my house, and all my people will be ruled by your word: only as king will I be greater than you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 28:18; Philippians 2:9–11; Genesis 42:6"> And Pharaoh said to Joseph, See, I have put you over all the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Daniel 5:29; Daniel 5:7; Esther 3:10"> Then Pharaoh took off his ring from his hand and put it on Joseph's hand, and he had him clothed with the best linen, and put a chain of gold round his neck;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Acts 7:10; Genesis 45:8; Genesis 45:26"> And he made him take his seat in the second of his carriages; and they went before him crying, Make way! So he made him ruler over all the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 105:22; Exodus 11:7"> Then Pharaoh said to Joseph, I am Pharaoh; and without your order no man may do anything in all the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Genesis 46:20; Ezekiel 30:17; Exodus 2:16"> And Pharaoh gave Joseph the name of Zaphnath-paaneah; and he gave him Asenath, the daughter of Poti-phera, the priest of On, to be his wife. So Joseph went through all the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Daniel 1:19; 1 Kings 12:6; 1 Samuel 16:21"> Now Joseph was thirty years old when he came before Pharaoh, king of Egypt. And Joseph went out from before the face of Pharaoh and went through all the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Genesis 26:12; Psalms 72:16"> Now in the seven good years the earth gave fruit in masses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Genesis 41:34–36; Genesis 47:21"> And Joseph got together all the food of those seven years, and made a store of food in the towns: the produce of the fields round every town was stored up in the town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Psalms 78:27; Judges 6:5; Genesis 22:17"> So he got together a store of grain like the sand of the sea; so great a store that after a time he gave up measuring it, for it might not be measured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Genesis 46:20; Genesis 48:5; 2 Samuel 8:18"> And before the time of need, Joseph had two sons, to whom Asenath, the daughter of Poti-phera, priest of On, gave birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Psalms 30:5; Proverbs 31:7; Deuteronomy 33:17"> And to the first he gave the name Manasseh, for he said, God has taken away from me all memory of my hard life and of my father's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Genesis 49:22; Genesis 50:23; Genesis 17:6"> And to the second he gave the name Ephraim, for he said, God has given me fruit in the land of my sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Genesis 41:29–31; Psalms 73:20; Luke 16:25"> And so the seven good years in Egypt came to an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Genesis 45:11; Acts 7:11; Psalms 105:16"> Then came the first of the seven years of need as Joseph had said: and in every other land they were short of food; but in the land of Egypt there was bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Matthew 17:5; Matthew 3:17; Jeremiah 14:1–6"> And when all the land of Egypt was in need of food, the people came crying to Pharaoh for bread; and Pharaoh said to the people, Go to Joseph, and whatever he says to you, do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Genesis 42:6; Zechariah 5:3; Luke 21:35"> And everywhere on the earth they were short of food; then Joseph, opening all his store-houses, gave the people of Egypt grain for money; so great was the need of food in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Genesis 41:54; Genesis 42:5; Genesis 41:56"> And all lands sent to Egypt, to Joseph, to get grain, for the need was great over all the earth.</span>
