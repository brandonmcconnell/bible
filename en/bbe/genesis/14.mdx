<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 10:10; Genesis 10:22; Isaiah 37:12"> Now in the days of Amraphel, king of Shinar, Arioch, king of Ellasar, Chedorlaomer, king of Elam, and Tidal, king of Goiim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 13:10; Deuteronomy 29:23; Genesis 10:19"> They made war with Bera, king of Sodom, and with Birsha, king of Gomorrah, Shinab, king of Admah, and Shemeber, king of Zeboiim, and the king of Bela (which is Zoar).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 3:16; Deuteronomy 3:17; Numbers 34:12"> All these came together in the valley of Siddim (which is the Salt Sea).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 9:25–26; Ezekiel 17:15"> For twelve years they were under the rule of Chedorlaomer, but in the thirteenth year they put off his control.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:11; Genesis 15:20; Deuteronomy 1:4"> And in the fourteenth year, Chedorlaomer and the kings who were on his side, overcame the Rephaim in Ashteroth-karnaim, and the Zuzim in Ham, and the Emim in Shaveh-kiriathaim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:22; Deuteronomy 2:12; Genesis 21:21"> And the Horites in their mountain Seir, driving them as far as El-paran, which is near the waste land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:2; Genesis 16:14; Genesis 20:1"> Then they came back to En-mishpat (which is Kadesh), making waste all the country of the Amalekites and of the Amorites living in Hazazon-tamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 19:22; Genesis 13:10; Genesis 19:20"> And the king of Sodom with the king of Gomorrah and the king of Admah and the king of Zeboiim and the king of Bela (that is Zoar), went out, and put their forces in position in the valley of Siddim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Against Chedorlaomer, king of Elam, and Tidal, king of Goiim, and Amraphel, king of Shinar, and Arioch, king of Ellasar: four kings against the five.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 19:17; Genesis 19:30; Isaiah 24:18"> Now the valley of Siddim was full of holes of sticky earth; and the kings of Sodom and Gomorrah were put to flight and came to their end there, but the rest got away to the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 14:21; Genesis 14:16; Deuteronomy 28:31"> And the four kings took all the goods and food from Sodom and Gomorrah and went on their way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 12:5; Genesis 11:27; Revelation 3:19"> And in addition they took Lot, Abram's brother's son, who was living in Sodom, and all his goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 13:18; Genesis 14:24; Genesis 40:15"> And one who had got away from the fight came and gave word of it to Abram the Hebrew, who was living by the holy tree of Mamre, the Amorite, the brother of Eshcol and Aner, who were friends of Abram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 18:29; Genesis 15:3; Genesis 17:27"> And Abram, hearing that his brother's son had been made a prisoner, took a band of his trained men, three hundred and eighteen of them, sons of his house, and went after them as far as Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 9:2; Psalms 112:5; 1 Kings 15:18"> And separating his forces by night, he overcame them, putting them to flight and going after them as far as Hobah, which is on the north side of Damascus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:18–19; 1 Samuel 30:8; Genesis 12:2"> And he got back all the goods, and Lot, his brother's son, with his goods and the women and the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:18; Proverbs 19:4; Hebrews 7:1"> And when he was coming back after putting to flight Chedorlaomer and the other kings, he had a meeting with the king of Sodom in the valley of Shaveh, that is, the King's Valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 6:20–3; Psalms 110:4; Hebrews 5:6"> And Melchizedek, king of Salem, the priest of the Most High God, took bread and wine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 7:6–7; Genesis 14:22; Psalms 24:1"> And blessing him, said, May the blessing of the Most High God, maker of heaven and earth, be on Abram:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 28:22; Hebrews 7:4–9; Malachi 3:10"> And let the Most High God be praised, who has given into your hands those who were against you. Then Abram gave him a tenth of all the goods he had taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And the king of Sodom said to Abram, Give me the prisoners and take the goods for yourself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 12:7; Daniel 4:34; Deuteronomy 32:40"> But Abram said to the king of Sodom, I have taken an oath to the Lord, the Most High God, maker of heaven and earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 5:16; 2 Corinthians 11:9–12; Esther 9:15–16"> That I will not take so much as a thread or the cord of a shoe of yours; so that you may not say, I have given wealth to Abram:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 14:13; Proverbs 3:27; Matthew 7:12"> Give me nothing but the food which the fighting-men who went with me have had; but let Aner and Eshcol and Mamre have their part of the goods.</span>
