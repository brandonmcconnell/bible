<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 46:4; Deuteronomy 6:7–8; Acts 8:2"> And Joseph put his head down on his father's face, weeping and kissing him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 50:26; 2 Chronicles 16:14; John 19:39–40"> And Joseph gave orders to his servants who had the necessary knowledge, to make his father's body ready, folding it in linen with spices, and they did so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 20:29; Deuteronomy 34:8; Genesis 50:10"> And the forty days needed for making the body ready went by: and there was weeping for him among the Egyptians for seventy days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 18:3; Esther 4:2"> And when the days of weeping for him were past, Joseph said to the servants of Pharaoh, If now you have love for me, say these words to Pharaoh:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:14; Isaiah 22:16; Matthew 27:60"> My father made me take an oath, saying, When I am dead, put me to rest in the place I have made ready for myself in the land of Canaan. So now let me go and put my father in his last resting-place, and I will come back again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 48:21"> And Pharaoh said, Go up and put your father to rest, as you gave your oath to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 14:16"> So Joseph went up to put his father in his last resting-place; and with him went all the servants of Pharaoh, and the chief men of his house and all the chiefs of the land of Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 32:24–27; Exodus 10:26; Exodus 10:8–9"> And all the family of Joseph, and his brothers and his father's people: only their little ones and their flocks and herds they did not take with them from the land of Goshen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 14:28; 2 Kings 18:24; Song of Solomon 1:9"> And carriages went up with him and horsemen, a great army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 2:13; Acts 8:2; 2 Samuel 1:17"> And they came to the grain-floor of Atad on the other side of Jordan, and there they gave the last honours to Jacob, with great and bitter sorrow, weeping for their father for seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 10:15–19; Genesis 24:6; Deuteronomy 11:30"> And when the people of the land, the people of Canaan, at the grain-floor of Atad, saw their grief, they said, Great is the grief of the Egyptians: so the place was named Abel-mizraim, on the other side of Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 7:16; Genesis 49:29–32; Ephesians 6:1"> So his sons did as he had given them orders to do:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 49:29–31; Genesis 23:16–18; Acts 7:16"> For they took him into the land of Canaan and put him to rest in the hollow rock in the field of Machpelah, which Abraham got with the field, for a resting-place, from Ephron the Hittite at Mamre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And when his father had been put to rest, Joseph and his brothers and all who had gone with him, went back to Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 28:1; Genesis 42:21–22; Psalms 53:5"> Now after the death of their father, Joseph's brothers said to themselves, It may be that Joseph's heart will be turned against us, and he will give us punishment for all the evil which we did to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 29:25"> So they sent word to Joseph, saying, Your father, before his death, gave us orders, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Matthew 6:14–15; Genesis 45:8"> You are to say to Joseph, Let the wrongdoing of your brothers be overlooked, and the evil they did to you: now, if it is your pleasure, let the sin of the servants of your father's God have forgiveness. And at these words, Joseph was overcome with weeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 37:7–11; Genesis 42:6; Genesis 45:3"> Then his brothers went, and falling at his feet, said, Truly, we are your servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 12:19; Genesis 45:5; Genesis 30:2"> And Joseph said, Have no fear: am I in the place of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 8:28; Psalms 119:71; Genesis 45:5–8"> As for you, it was in your mind to do me evil, but God has given a happy outcome, the salvation of numbers of people, as you see today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 47:12; 1 Peter 3:9; Genesis 45:10–11"> So now, have no fear: for I will take care of you and your little ones. So he gave them comfort with kind words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Now Joseph and all his father's family went on living in Egypt: and the years of Joseph's life were a hundred and ten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 32:39; Genesis 30:3; Job 42:16"> And Joseph saw Ephraim's children of the third generation: and the children of Machir, the son of Manasseh, came to birth on Joseph's knees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 3:16–17; Genesis 35:12; Genesis 26:3"> Then Joseph said to his brothers, The time of my death has come; but God will keep you in mind and take you out of this land into the land which he gave by his oath to Abraham and Isaac and Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 13:19; Joshua 24:32; Genesis 47:29–31"> Then Joseph made the children of Israel take an oath, saying, God will certainly give effect to his word, and you are to take my bones away from here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 50:2–3; Genesis 47:9; Joshua 24:29"> So Joseph came to his death, being a hundred and ten years old: and they made his body ready, and he was put in a chest in Egypt.</span>
