<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 5:2; Genesis 29:31; Genesis 37:11"> Now Rachel, because she had no children, was full of envy of her sister; and she said to Jacob, If you do not give me children I will not go on living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 16:2; Psalms 127:3; 2 Kings 5:7"> But Jacob was angry with Rachel, and said, Am I in the place of God, who has kept your body from having fruit?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 50:23; Job 3:12; Ruth 4:11"> Then she said, Here is my servant Bilhah, go in to her, so that she may have a child on my knees, and I may have a family by her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 35:22; Genesis 21:10; 2 Samuel 12:11"> So she gave him her servant Bilhah as a wife, and Jacob went in to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And Bilhah became with child, and gave birth to a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Lamentations 3:59; Psalms 35:24; Psalms 43:1"> Then Rachel said, God has been my judge, and has given ear to my voice, and has given me a son; so he was named Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And again Bilhah, Rachel's servant, was with child, and gave birth to a second son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 32:24–25; Genesis 23:6; Genesis 49:21"> And Rachel said, I have had a great fight with my sister, and I have overcome her: and she gave the child the name Naphtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 30:4; Genesis 30:17; Genesis 29:35"> When it was clear to Leah that she would have no more children for a time, she gave Zilpah, her servant, to Jacob as a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Zilpah, Leah's servant, gave birth to a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 49:19; Isaiah 65:11; Genesis 35:26"> And Leah said, It has gone well for me: and she gave him the name Gad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And Zilpah, Leah's servant, gave birth to a second son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 1:48; Proverbs 31:28; Song of Solomon 6:9"> And Leah said, Happy am I! and all women will give witness to my joy: and she gave him the name Asher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Song of Solomon 7:13; Genesis 25:30"> Now at the time of the grain-cutting, Reuben saw some love-fruits in the field, and took them to his mother Leah. And Rachel said to her, Let me have some of your son's love-fruits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 16:13; 1 Corinthians 4:3; Numbers 16:9–10"> But Leah said to her, Is it a small thing that you have taken my husband from me? and now would you take my son's love-fruits? Then Rachel said, You may have him tonight in exchange for your son's love-fruits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> In the evening, when Jacob came in from the field, Leah went out to him and said, Tonight you are to come to me, for I have given my son's love-fruits as a price for you. And he went in to her that night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 30:6; Genesis 30:22; Exodus 3:7"> And God gave ear to her and she became with child, and gave Jacob a fifth son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 49:14–15; Deuteronomy 33:18; Genesis 46:13"> Then Leah said, God has made payment to me for giving my servant-girl to my husband: so she gave her son the name Issachar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And again Leah became with child, and she gave Jacob a sixth son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 35:23; Matthew 4:13; Genesis 49:13"> And she said, God has given me a good bride-price; now at last will I have my husband living with me, for I have given him six sons: and she gave him the name Zebulun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 34:1–3; Genesis 34:26; Genesis 46:15"> After that she had a daughter, to whom she gave the name Dinah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 29:31; Psalms 113:9; Psalms 127:3"> Then God gave thought to Rachel, and hearing her prayer he made her fertile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 1:25; Genesis 29:31; Luke 1:27"> And she was with child, and gave birth to a son: and she said, God has taken away my shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 49:22–26; Genesis 35:24; Genesis 37:2"> And she gave him the name Joseph, saying, May the Lord give me another son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 24:54; Genesis 24:56; Genesis 28:15"> Now after the birth of Joseph, Jacob said to Laban, Let me go away to my place and my country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 29:30; Hosea 12:12; Genesis 31:26"> Give me my wives and my children, for whom I have been your servant, and let me go: for you have knowledge of all the work I have done for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ruth 2:13; Acts 7:10; Isaiah 61:9"> And Laban said, If you will let me say so, do not go away; for I have seen by the signs that the Lord has been good to me because of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 29:15; Genesis 29:19"> Say then what your payment is to be and I will give it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 31:6; Genesis 31:38–40; 1 Peter 2:18"> Then Jacob said, You have seen what I have done for you, and how your cattle have done well under my care.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:8; 2 Corinthians 12:14; Deuteronomy 11:10"> For before I came you had little, and it has been greatly increased; and the Lord has given you a blessing in everything I have done; but when am I to do something for my family?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:4–6; Hebrews 13:5; Psalms 118:8"> And Laban said, What am I to give you? And Jacob said, Do not give me anything; but I will again take up the care of your flock if you will only do this for me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 31:8; Genesis 31:10; Genesis 30:35"> Let me go through all your flock today, taking out from among them all the sheep which are marked or coloured or black, and all the marked or coloured goats: these will be my payment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 13:14; 2 Samuel 22:21; 1 Samuel 26:23"> And so you will be able to put my honour to the test in time to come; if you see among my flocks any goats which are not marked or coloured, or any sheep which is not black, you may take me for a thief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:7; Numbers 22:29; 1 Corinthians 14:5"> And Laban said, Let it be as you say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 31:9"> So that day he took all the he-goats which were banded or coloured, and all the she-goats which were marked or coloured or had white marks, and all the black sheep, and gave them into the care of his sons;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And sent them three days' journey away: and Jacob took care of the rest of Laban's flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Genesis 31:9–13; Ezekiel 31:8"> Then Jacob took young branches of trees, cutting off the skin so that the white wood was seen in bands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And he put the banded sticks in the drinking-places where the flock came to get water; and they became with young when they came to the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 12:35–36; Genesis 31:9–12; Genesis 31:40"> And because of this, the flock gave birth to young which were marked with bands of colour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> These lambs Jacob kept separate; and he put his flock in a place by themselves and not with Laban's flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> And whenever the stronger ones of the flock became with young, Jacob put the sticks in front of them in the drinking-places, so that they might become with young when they saw the sticks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> But when the flocks were feeble, he did not put the sticks before them; so that the feebler flocks were Laban's and the stronger were Jacob's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Genesis 24:35; Genesis 30:30; Genesis 26:13–14"> So Jacob's wealth was greatly increased; he had great flocks and women-servants and men-servants and camels and asses.</span>
