<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 7:24; Genesis 7:11"> And the waters went slowly back from the earth, and at the end of a hundred and fifty days the waters were lower.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 19:37; Jeremiah 51:27; Isaiah 37:38"> And on the seventeenth day of the seventh month the ark came to rest on the mountains of Ararat.</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 6:16; Daniel 6:10"> Then, after forty days, through the open window of the ark which he had made,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 147:9; 1 Kings 17:6; 1 Kings 17:4"> Noah sent out a raven, which went this way and that till the waters were gone from the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:14; Song of Solomon 1:15; Matthew 10:16"> And he sent out a dove, to see if the waters had gone from the face of the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 11:28; Psalms 116:7; Deuteronomy 28:65"> But the dove saw no resting-place for her foot, and came back to the ark, for the waters were still over all the earth; and he put out his hand, and took her into the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; Genesis 7:10; Genesis 8:12"> And after waiting another seven days, he sent the dove out again;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:15; Zechariah 4:12–14; Romans 10:15"> And the dove came back at evening, and in her mouth was an olive-leaf broken off: so Noah was certain that the waters had gone down on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 25:9; Isaiah 30:18; Genesis 8:10"> And after seven days more, he sent the dove out again, but she did not come back to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 7:11"> And in the six hundred and first year, on the first day of the first month, the waters were dry on the earth: and Noah took the cover off the ark and saw that the face of the earth was dry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 7:13–14; Genesis 7:11"> And on the twenty-seventh day of the second month the earth was dry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And God said to Noah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 7:13; Psalms 121:8; Psalms 91:11"> Go out of the ark, you and your wife and your sons and your sons' wives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 1:22; Genesis 9:1; Jeremiah 31:27–28"> Take out with you every living thing which is with you, birds and cattle and everything which goes on the earth, so that they may have offspring and be fertile and be increased on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 121:8"> And Noah went out with his sons and his wife and his sons' wives;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And every beast and bird and every living thing of every sort which goes on the earth, went out of the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15–16; Romans 12:1; Genesis 22:9"> And Noah made an altar to the Lord, and from every clean beast and bird he made burned offerings on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:15; Ephesians 5:2; Isaiah 54:9–10"> And when the sweet smell came up to the Lord, he said in his heart, I will not again put a curse on the earth because of man, for the thoughts of man's heart are evil from his earliest days; never again will I send destruction on all living things as I have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 5:7; Psalms 74:16–17; Jeremiah 5:24"> While the earth goes on, seed time and the getting in of the grain, cold and heat, summer and winter, day and night, will not come to an end.</span>
