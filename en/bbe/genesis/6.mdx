<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 1:28"> And after a time, when men were increasing on the earth, and had daughters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 82:6–7; 1 John 2:16; 2 Peter 2:14"> The sons of God saw that the daughters of men were fair; and they took wives for themselves from those who were pleasing to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:30; Isaiah 63:10; Jeremiah 11:11"> And the Lord said, My spirit will not be in man for ever, for he is only flesh; so the days of his life will be a hundred and twenty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 13:33; 2 Samuel 21:15–22; 1 Samuel 17:4"> There were men of great strength and size on the earth in those days; and after that, when the sons of God had connection with the daughters of men, they gave birth to children: these were the great men of old days, the men of great name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 15:19; Romans 1:28–31; Mark 7:21–23"> And the Lord saw that the sin of man was great on the earth, and that all the thoughts of his heart were evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 63:10; 1 Samuel 15:11; Ephesians 4:30"> And the Lord had sorrow because he had made man on the earth, and grief was in his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:3; Psalms 37:20; Jeremiah 12:3–4"> And the Lord said, I will take away man, whom I have made, from the face of the earth, even man and beast and that which goes on the earth and every bird of the air; for I have sorrow for having made them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 19:19; Hebrews 4:16; Luke 1:30"> But Noah had grace in the eyes of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 1:6; Hebrews 11:7; 2 Peter 2:5"> These are the generations of Noah. Noah was an upright man and without sin in his generation: he went in the ways of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 5:32"> And Noah had three sons, Shem, Ham, and Japheth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:17; Hosea 4:1–2; Habakkuk 2:8"> And the earth was evil in God's eyes and full of violent ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 53:2–3; Proverbs 15:3; Psalms 14:1–3"> And God, looking on the earth, saw that it was evil: for the way of all flesh had become evil on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 4:1–2; 1 Peter 4:7; Ezekiel 7:2–6"> And God said to Noah, The end of all flesh has come; the earth is full of their violent doings, and now I will put an end to them with the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 3:20; Luke 17:27; Matthew 24:38"> Make for yourself an ark of gopher wood with rooms in it, and make it safe from the water inside and out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:11; Genesis 7:20"> And this is the way you are to make it: it is to be three hundred cubits long, fifty cubits wide, and thirty cubits high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 8:6; Genesis 7:16; Ezekiel 41:16"> You are to put a window in the ark, a cubit from the roof, and a door in the side of it, and you are to make it with a lower and second and third floors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; 2 Peter 2:5; Genesis 7:21–23"> For truly, I will send a great flow of waters over the earth, for the destruction from under the heaven of all flesh in which is the breath of life; everything on the earth will come to an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 11:7; Genesis 7:7; Genesis 17:7"> But with you I will make an agreement; and you will come into the ark, you and your sons and your wife and your sons' wives with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 7:15–16; Genesis 7:8–9; Genesis 8:17"> And you will take with you into the ark two of every sort of living thing, and keep them safe with you; they will be male and female.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 10:11–12; Genesis 2:19; Genesis 7:8–16"> Two of every sort of bird and cattle and of every sort of living thing which goes on the earth will you take with you to keep them from destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 1:29–30; Psalms 145:16; Psalms 136:25"> And make a store of every sort of food for yourself and them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 40:16; John 15:14; Matthew 7:24–27"> And all these things Noah did; as God said, so he did.</span>
