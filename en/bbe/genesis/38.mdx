<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:1; 2 Kings 4:8; Proverbs 13:20"> Now at that time, Judah went away from his brothers and became the friend of a man of Adullam named Hirah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:3; Genesis 24:3; Genesis 6:2"> And there he saw the daughter of a certain man of Canaan named Shua, and took her as his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Numbers 26:19"> And she gave birth to a son, and he gave him the name Er.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Numbers 26:19"> And again she gave birth to a son, and he gave him the name Onan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 26:20; Genesis 38:26; Genesis 46:12"> Then she had another son, to whom she gave the name Shelah; she was at Chezib when the birth took place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 24:3; Genesis 21:21; Matthew 1:3"> And Judah took a wife for his first son Er, and her name was Tamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:3; Genesis 46:12; Numbers 26:19"> Now Er, Judah's first son, did evil in the eyes of the Lord, so that he put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Matthew 22:23–27; Ruth 4:5–11"> Then Judah said to Onan, Go in to your brother's wife and do what it is right for a husband's brother to do; make her your wife and get offspring for your brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Titus 3:3; Deuteronomy 25:6; James 3:16"> But Onan, seeing that the offspring would not be his, went in to his brother's wife, but let his seed go on to the earth, so that he might not get offspring for his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Numbers 11:1; 1 Chronicles 21:7"> And what he did was evil in the eyes of the Lord, so that he put him to death, like his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 22:13; Ruth 1:11–13"> Then Judah said to Tamar, his daughter-in-law, Go back to your father's house and keep yourself as a widow till my son Shelah becomes a man: for he had in his mind the thought that death might come to him as it had come to his brothers. So Tamar went back to her father's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 15:10; 2 Samuel 13:39; Judges 14:1"> And after a time, Bath-shua, Judah's wife, came to her end; and after Judah was comforted for her loss, he went to Timnah, where they were cutting the wool of his sheep, and his friend Hirah of Adullam went with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And when Tamar had news that her father-in-law was going up to Timnah to the wool-cutting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 38:26; Genesis 38:11–13; Jeremiah 3:2"> She took off her widow's clothing, and covering herself with her veil, she took her seat near Enaim on the road to Timnah; for she saw that Shelah was now a man, but she had not been made his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> When Judah saw her he took her to be a loose woman of the town, because her face was covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:10; Matthew 26:15; 2 Samuel 13:11"> And turning to her by the roadside, he said to her, Let me come in to you; for he had no idea that she was his daughter-in-law. And she said, What will you give me as my price?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:33; Genesis 38:20; Proverbs 20:16"> And he said, I will give you a young goat from the flock. And she said, What will you give me as a sign till you send it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 38:25–26; Hosea 4:11; Jeremiah 22:24"> And he said, What would you have? And she said, Your ring and its cord and the stick in your hand. So he gave them to her and went in to her, and she became with child by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 38:14; 2 Samuel 14:5; 2 Samuel 14:2"> Then she got up and went away and took off her veil and put on her widow's clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 20:9; Leviticus 19:17; 2 Samuel 13:3"> Then Judah sent his friend Hirah with the young goat, to get back the things which he had given as a sign to the woman: but she was not there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And he put questions to the men of the place, saying, Where is the loose woman who was in Enaim by the wayside? And they said, There was no such woman there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> So he went back to Judah, and said, I have not seen her, and the men of the place say that there is no such woman there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ephesians 5:12; Proverbs 6:33; Revelation 16:15"> And Judah said, Let her keep the things, so that we may not be shamed; I sent the young goat, but you did not see the woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 21:9; Ezekiel 23:19; Judges 19:2"> Now about three months after this, word came to Judah that Tamar, his daughter-in-law, had been acting like a loose woman and was with child. And Judah said, Take her out and let her be burned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 37:32; Genesis 38:18; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> And while she was being taken out, she sent word to her father-in-law, saying, The man whose property these things are, is the father of my child: say then, whose are this ring and this cord and this stick?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:17; John 8:9; Titus 2:11–12"> Then Judah said openly that they were his, and said, She is more upright than I am, for I did not give her to Shelah my son. And he had no more connection with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 25:24"> And when the time came for her to give birth, it was clear that there were two children in her body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And while she was in the act of giving birth, one of them put out his hand; and the woman who was with her put a red thread round his hand, saying, This one came out first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:4; Matthew 1:3; Genesis 46:12"> But then he took his hand back again, and his brother came first to birth: and the woman said, What an opening you have made for yourself! So he was named Perez.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:6; 1 Chronicles 2:4; Matthew 1:3"> And then his brother came out, with the red thread round his hand, and he was named Zerah.</span>
