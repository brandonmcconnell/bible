<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 7:1–17; Psalms 86:1–17; Psalms 90:1–17"> A prayer of Habakkuk the prophet, put to Shigionoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 85:6; Psalms 90:13–17; Hosea 6:2–3"> O Lord, word of you has come to my ears; I have seen your work, O Lord; when the years come near make it clear; in wrath keep mercy in mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:2; Psalms 4:4; Obadiah 1:9"> God came from Teman, and the Holy One from Mount Paran. Selah. The heavens were covered with his glory, and the earth was full of his praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19–20; Matthew 17:2; Job 26:14"> He was shining like the light; he had rays coming out from his hand: there his power was kept secret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 12:29–30; Numbers 16:46–49; Psalms 78:50–51"> Before him went disease, and flames went out at his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 49:26; Deuteronomy 33:15; Nahum 1:5"> From his high place he sent shaking on the earth; he saw and nations were suddenly moved: and the eternal mountains were broken, the unchanging hills were bent down; his ways are eternal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 15:14–16; Genesis 10:6–7; Genesis 25:1–4"> The curtains of Cushan were troubled, and the tents of Midian were shaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 68:17; Deuteronomy 33:26–27; Exodus 14:21–22"> Was your wrath burning against the rivers? were you angry with the sea, that you went on your horses, on your war-carriages of salvation?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 7:12–13; Psalms 105:41; Psalms 78:15–16"> Your bow was quite uncovered. Selah. By you the earth was cut through with rivers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:24; Joshua 4:23–24; Psalms 93:3"> The mountains saw you and were moved with fear; the clouds were streaming with water: the voice of the deep was sounding; the sun did not come up, and the moon kept still in her place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 144:5–6; Psalms 18:12–14; Joshua 10:11–13"> At the light of your arrows they went away, at the shining of your polished spear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:33; Micah 4:12–13; Numbers 21:23–35"> You went stepping through the land in wrath, crushing the nations in your passion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 110:6; Psalms 105:15; Psalms 68:19–23"> You went out for the salvation of your people, for the salvation of the one on whom your holy oil was put; wounding the head of the family of the evil-doer, uncovering the base even to the neck. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Zechariah 9:14; Daniel 11:40; Psalms 118:10–12"> You have put your spears through his head, his horsemen were sent in flight like dry stems; they had joy in driving away the poor, in making a meal of them secretly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 77:19; Habakkuk 3:8; Exodus 15:8"> The feet of your horses were on the sea, on the mass of great waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:9; Habakkuk 1:5–11; Habakkuk 3:2"> Hearing it, my inner parts were moved, and my lips were shaking at the sound; my bones became feeble, and my steps were uncertain under me: I gave sounds of grief in the day of trouble, when his forces came up against the people in bands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:17; Joel 1:16–18; Deuteronomy 28:15–18"> For though the fig-tree has no flowers, and there is no fruit on the vine, and work on the olive comes to nothing, and the fields give no food; and the flock is cut off from its resting-place, and there is no herd in the cattle-house:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 13:15; Philippians 4:4; Micah 7:7"> Still, I will be glad in the Lord, my joy will be in the God of my salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:34; Psalms 18:33; Philippians 4:13"> The Lord God is my strength, and he makes my feet like roes' feet, guiding me on my high places. For the chief music-maker on corded instruments.</span>
