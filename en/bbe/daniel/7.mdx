<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 1:17; Amos 3:7; Daniel 5:1"> In the first year of Belshazzar, king of Babylon, Daniel saw a dream, and visions came into his head on his bed: then he put the dream in writing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 7:1; Revelation 17:15"> I had a vision by night, and saw the four winds of heaven violently moving the great sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 13:1; Daniel 7:17; Daniel 2:32–33"> And four great beasts came up from the sea, different one from another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:7; Isaiah 14:13–17; Ezekiel 17:3"> The first was like a lion and had eagle's wings; while I was watching its wings were pulled off, and it was lifted up from the earth and placed on two feet like a man, and a man's heart was given to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 2:39; Isaiah 13:17–18; Proverbs 17:12"> And I saw another beast, like a bear, and it was lifted up on one side, and three side-bones were in its mouth, between its teeth: and they said to it, Up! take much flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 13:2; Daniel 8:20–22; Daniel 8:5–8"> After this I saw another beast, like a leopard, which had on its back four wings like those of a bird; and the beast had four heads, and the power of a ruler was given to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 13:1; Revelation 12:3; Revelation 17:12"> After this, in my vision of the night, I saw a fourth beast, a thing causing fear and very troubling, full of power and very strong; and it had great iron teeth: it took its food, crushing some of it to bits and stamping down the rest with its feet: it was different from all the beasts before it; and it had ten horns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 13:5–6; Revelation 13:1; Psalms 12:3"> I was watching the horns with care, and I saw another coming up among them, a little one, before which three of the first horns were pulled up by the roots: and there were eyes like a man's eyes in this horn, and a mouth saying great things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 1:14; Mark 9:3; Daniel 7:13"> I went on looking till the seats of kings were placed, and one like a very old man took his seat: his clothing was white as snow, and the hair of his head was like clean wool; his seat was flames of fire and its wheels burning fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 30:27; Deuteronomy 33:2; Revelation 20:11–15"> A stream of fire was flowing and coming out from before him: a thousand thousands were his servants, and ten thousand times ten thousand were in their places before him: the judge was seated and the books were open.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 19:20; Revelation 20:10; 2 Thessalonians 2:8"> Then I saw — because of the voice of the great words which the horn said — I saw till the beast was put to death, and its body was given to destruction, and the beast was given to the burning of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 8:7; Daniel 7:4–6"> As for the rest of the beasts, their authority was taken away: but they let them go on living for a measure of time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 26:64; Revelation 1:7; Luke 21:27"> I saw in visions of the night, and there was coming with the clouds of heaven one like a man, and he came to the one who was very old, and they took him near before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 11:15; Daniel 7:27; John 3:35"> And to him was given authority and glory and a kingdom; and all peoples, nations, and languages were his servants: his authority is an eternal authority which will not come to an end, and his kingdom is one which will not come to destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 7:28; Jeremiah 17:16; Genesis 40:7–8"> As for me, Daniel, my spirit was pained because of this, and the visions of my head were troubling me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 7:13–14; Zechariah 2:3; Revelation 5:5"> I came near to one of those who were waiting there, questioning him about what all this was. And he said to me that he would make clear to me the sense of these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Daniel 8:19–22; Daniel 2:37–40; John 18:36"> These great beasts are four kings who will be cut off from the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 7:27; Isaiah 60:12–14; Revelation 5:10"> But the saints of the Most High will take the kingdom, and it will be theirs for ever, even for ever and ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 7:7; Daniel 2:40–43"> Then it was my desire to have certain knowledge about the fourth beast, which was different from all the others, a cause of great fear, whose teeth were of iron and his nails of brass; who took his food, crushing some of it to bits and stamping on the rest with his feet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 7:23; Daniel 7:8; Daniel 7:11"> And about the ten horns on his head and the other which came up, causing the fall of three; that horn which had eyes, and a mouth saying great things, which seemed to be greater than the other horns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 12:7; Revelation 17:14; Daniel 8:24"> And I saw how that horn made war on the saints and overcame them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 7:18; Revelation 20:4; Revelation 5:10"> Till he came, who was very old, and the decision was made and the authority was given to the saints of the Most High; and the time came when the saints took the kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 2:40; Daniel 7:7; Luke 2:1"> This is what he said: The fourth beast is a fourth kingdom which will come on earth, different from all the kingdoms, and it will overcome all the earth, crushing it down and smashing it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 7:20; Daniel 11:36; Revelation 13:1"> And as for the ten horns, out of this kingdom ten kings will come to power; and after them another will come up: he will be different from the first ones and will put down three kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 12:7; Revelation 12:14; 2 Thessalonians 2:4"> And he will say words against the Most High, attempting to put an end to the saints of the Most High; and he will have the idea of changing times and law; and the saints will be given into his hands for a time and times and half a time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 20:10–11; 2 Thessalonians 2:8; Revelation 11:13"> But the judge will be seated, and they will put an end to his authority, to overcome it and send complete destruction on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 72:11; Luke 1:33; Revelation 11:15"> And the kingdom and the authority and the power of the kingdoms under all the heaven will be given to the people of the saints of the Most High: his kingdom is an eternal kingdom, and all powers will be his servants and do his pleasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 2:19; Luke 2:51; Daniel 10:8"> Here is the end of the account. As for me, Daniel, I was greatly troubled by my thoughts, and the colour went from my face: but I kept the thing in my heart.</span>
</div>
