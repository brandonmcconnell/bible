<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:7; Matthew 24:21; Mark 13:19"> And at that time Michael will take up his place, the great angel, who is the supporter of the children of your people: and there will be a time of trouble, such as there never was from the time there was a nation even till that same time: and at that time your people will be kept safe, everyone who is recorded in the book.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 5:28–29; Isaiah 26:19; Matthew 25:46"> And a number of those who are sleeping in the dust of the earth will come out of their sleep, some to eternal life and some to eternal shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 13:43; Proverbs 4:18; James 5:19–20"> And those who are wise will be shining like the light of the outstretched sky; and those by whom numbers have been turned to righteousness will be like the stars for ever and ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 12:9; Revelation 22:10; Daniel 8:26"> But as for you, O Daniel, let the words be kept secret and the book rolled up and kept shut till the time of the end: numbers will be going out of the way and troubles will be increased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 10:4–6; Daniel 10:16; Daniel 10:10"> Then I, Daniel, looking, saw two others, one at the edge of the river on this side and one at the edge of the river on that side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 8:13; Ezekiel 9:2; Zechariah 1:12–13"> And I said to the man clothed in linen, who was over the waters of the river, How long will it be to the end of these wonders?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 7:25; Revelation 12:14; Daniel 8:24"> Then in my hearing the man clothed in linen, who was over the river, lifting up his right hand and his left hand to heaven, took an oath by him who is living for ever that it would be a time, times, and a half; and when the power of the crusher of the holy people comes to an end, all these things will be ended.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:7; 1 Peter 1:11; Daniel 10:14"> And the words came to my ears, but the sense of them was not clear to me: then I said, O my lord, what is the sense of these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 12:4; Daniel 8:26; Revelation 10:4"> And he said, Go on your way, Daniel: for the words are secret and shut up till the time of the end;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 22:11; Daniel 11:35; Hosea 14:9"> Till a number are tested and make themselves clean; and the evil-doers will do evil; for not one of the evil-doers will have knowledge; but all will be made clear to those who are wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 11:31; Matthew 24:15; Mark 13:14"> And from the time when the regular burned offering is taken away, and an unclean thing causing fear is put up, there will be a thousand, two hundred and ninety days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 30:18; Daniel 8:14; Romans 11:15"> A blessing will be on the man who goes on waiting, and comes to the thousand, three hundred and thirty-five days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; 2 Timothy 4:7–8; Zechariah 3:7"> But you, go on your way and take your rest: for you will be in your place at the end of the days.</span>
