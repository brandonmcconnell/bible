<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 12:14; 1 Corinthians 1:10; Mark 9:50"> I make request to Euodias and Syntyche to be of the same mind in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 3:5; Revelation 13:8; Luke 10:20"> And I make request to you, true helper in my work, to see to the needs of those women who took part with me in the good news, with Clement and the rest of my brother-workers whose names are in the book of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:16–18; Romans 12:12; James 1:2–4"> Be glad in the Lord at all times: again I say, Be glad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 5:8–9; Matthew 6:25; Titus 3:2"> Let your gentle behaviour be clear to all men. The Lord is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; 1 Peter 5:7; Matthew 6:25–33"> Have no cares; but in everything with prayer and praise put your requests before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 14:27; Isaiah 26:3; 2 Thessalonians 3:16"> And the peace of God, which is deeper than all knowledge, will keep your hearts and minds in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 3:17; Titus 2:7; Romans 12:9–21"> For the rest, my brothers, whatever things are true, whatever things have honour, whatever things are upright, whatever things are holy, whatever things are beautiful, whatever things are of value, if there is any virtue and if there is any praise, give thought to these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 1:22; Philippians 3:17; 1 Thessalonians 4:1–8"> The things which came to you by my teaching and preaching, and which you saw in me, these things do, and the God of peace will be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:9; Galatians 6:6; 2 Corinthians 7:6–7"> But I am very glad in the Lord that your care for me has come to life again; though you did in fact take thought for me, but you were not able to give effect to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:6–9; Hebrews 13:5–6; Matthew 6:31–34"> But I will not say anything about my needs, for I am able, wherever I am, to be dependent on myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 11:29; 2 Corinthians 12:7–10; 2 Corinthians 11:27"> It is the same to me if I am looked down on or honoured; everywhere and in all things I have the secret of how to be full and how to go without food; how to have wealth and how to be in need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Colossians 1:11"> I am able to do all things through him who gives me strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Philippians 1:7; Hebrews 10:34"> But you did well to have care for me in my need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philippians 1:5; 2 Corinthians 11:8–12; Acts 16:40–5"> And you have knowledge, Philippians, that when the good news first came to you, when I went away from Macedonia, no church took part with me in the business of giving to the saints, but you only;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:9; Acts 17:1; 1 Thessalonians 2:18"> Because even in Thessalonica you sent once and again to me in my need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Titus 3:14; Hebrews 6:10; 2 Corinthians 9:9–13"> Not that I am looking for an offering, but for fruit which may be put to your credit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Philippians 4:12; 2 Corinthians 9:12"> I have all things and more than enough: I am made full, having had from Epaphroditus the things which came from you, a perfume of a sweet smell, an offering well pleasing to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:8–11; Psalms 84:11; 1 Peter 5:10"> And my God will give you all you have need of from the wealth of his glory in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Psalms 115:1; Revelation 14:7"> Now to God our Father be glory for ever and ever. So be it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Galatians 1:2; Philemon 1:23–24; Colossians 4:10–14"> Give words of love to every saint in Christ Jesus. The brothers who are with me send you their love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Philippians 1:13; 2 Corinthians 13:13; Romans 16:16"> All the saints send their love to you, specially those who are of Caesar's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:14; Romans 16:20; Romans 16:23"> The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit.</span>
