<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 5:11; Philippians 4:4; James 1:2"> For the rest, my brothers, be glad in the Lord. Writing the same things to you is no trouble to me, and for you it is safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:13; Titus 1:16; Revelation 22:15"> Be on the watch against dogs, against the workers of evil, against those of the circumcision:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 2:11; Deuteronomy 30:6; Romans 7:6"> For we are the circumcision, who give worship to God and have glory in Jesus Christ, and have no faith in the flesh:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:18–22"> Even though I myself might have faith in the flesh: if any other man has reason to have faith in the flesh, I have more:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:22; Romans 11:1; Acts 23:6"> Being given circumcision on the eighth day, of the nation of Israel, of the tribe of Benjamin, a Hebrew of Hebrews; in relation to the law, a Pharisee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 1:13–14; Acts 8:3; Acts 26:9–10"> In bitter hate I was cruel to the church; I kept all the righteousness of the law to the last detail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 14:33; Matthew 16:26; Matthew 13:44–46"> But those things which were profit to me, I gave up for Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23–24; Romans 8:18; Philippians 3:7"> Yes truly, and I am ready to give up all things for the knowledge of Christ Jesus my Lord, which is more than all: for whom I have undergone the loss of all things, and to me they are less than nothing, so that I may have Christ as my reward,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:21; 1 Corinthians 1:30; Galatians 2:16"> And be seen in him, not having my righteousness which is of the law, but that which is through faith in Christ, the righteousness which is of God by faith:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 2:20; Romans 8:17; 1 Peter 4:1–2"> That I may have knowledge of him, and of the power of his coming back from the dead, and a part with him in his pains, becoming like him in his death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 20:35–36; Revelation 20:5; Acts 23:6"> If in any way I may have the reward of life from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:11–12; 2 Peter 1:5–8; Psalms 138:8"> Not as if I had even now got the reward or been made complete: but I go on in the hope that I may come to the knowledge of that for which I was made the servant of Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:62; Hebrews 12:1–2; Philippians 3:12"> Brothers, it is clear to me that I have not come to that knowledge; but one thing I do, letting go those things which are past, and stretching out to the things which are before,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:24; 1 Peter 5:10; Revelation 3:21"> I go forward to the mark, even the reward of the high purpose of God in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 7:17; James 1:4–5; Proverbs 2:3–6"> Then let us all, who have come to full growth, be of this mind: and if in anything you are of a different mind, even this will God make clear to you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 2:6; Revelation 3:3; Revelation 2:4–5"> Only, as far as we have got, let us be guided by the same rule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Philippians 4:9; 1 Corinthians 4:16; 1 Peter 5:3"> Brothers, take me as your example, and take note of those who are walking after the example we have given.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 4:17; Isaiah 8:11; Galatians 6:12"> For there are those, of whom I have given you word before, and do so now with sorrow, who are haters of the cross of Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 16:18; Romans 8:5–7; 2 Peter 2:3"> Whose end is destruction, whose god is the stomach, and whose glory is in their shame, whose minds are fixed on the things of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 2:19; Colossians 3:1–3; 2 Corinthians 4:18–1"> For our country is in heaven; from where the Saviour for whom we are waiting will come, even the Lord Jesus Christ:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 3:2; Colossians 3:4; 1 Corinthians 15:42–56"> By whom this poor body of ours will be changed into the image of the body of his glory, in the measure of the working by which he is able to put all things under himself.</span>
