<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 38:1–7; Exodus 20:24–26; Exodus 40:10"> And make an altar of hard wood, a square altar, five cubits long, five cubits wide and three cubits high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 118:27; Exodus 29:12; Leviticus 4:7"> Put horns at the four angles of it, made of the same, plating it all with brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 7:45; Numbers 4:14; 1 Chronicles 28:17"> And make all its vessels, the baskets for taking away the dust of the fire, the spades and basins and meat-hooks and fire-trays, of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 35:16; Exodus 25:12; Exodus 38:4–5"> And make a network of brass, with four brass rings at its four angles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 38:4"> And put the network under the shelf round the altar so that the net comes half-way up the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 35:13–15; Numbers 4:44; Exodus 30:4"> And make rods for the altar, of hard wood, plated with brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 25:28; Exodus 30:4; Numbers 4:13–14"> And put the rods through the rings at the two opposite sides of the altar, for lifting it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Exodus 25:9; Hebrews 8:5"> The altar is to be hollow, boarded in with wood; make it from the design which you saw on the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 38:9–20; Ezekiel 40:32; Ezekiel 42:3"> And let there be an open space round the House, with hangings for its south side of the best linen, a hundred cubits long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 26:19–21; Jeremiah 52:21; Exodus 36:38"> Their twenty pillars and their twenty bases are to be of brass; the hooks of the pillars and their bands are to be of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And on the north side in the same way, hangings a hundred cubits long, with twenty pillars of brass on bases of brass; their hooks and their bands are to be of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And for the open space on the west side, the hangings are to be fifty cubits wide, with ten pillars and ten bases;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And on the east side the space is to be fifty cubits wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:9; Exodus 26:36"> On the one side of the doorway will be hangings fifteen cubits long, with three pillars and three bases;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And on the other side, hangings fifteen cubits long, with three pillars and three bases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 26:36; Judges 5:30; Exodus 28:39"> And across the doorway, a veil of twenty cubits of the best linen, made of needlework of blue and purple and red, with four pillars and four bases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> All the pillars round the open space are to have silver bands, with hooks of silver and bases of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The open space is to be a hundred cubits long, fifty cubits wide, with sides five cubits high, curtained with the best linen, with bases of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 35:18; Isaiah 22:23–25; Numbers 4:32"> All the instruments for the work of the House, and all its nails, and the nails of the open space are to be of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 24:1–4; Zechariah 4:11–14; Psalms 23:5"> Give orders to the children of Israel to give you clear olive oil for the lights, so that a light may be burning there at all times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 19:21; Numbers 18:23; 1 Samuel 3:3"> Let Aaron and his sons put this in order, evening and morning, before the Lord, inside the Tent of meeting, outside the veil which is before the ark; this is to be an order for ever, from generation to generation, to be kept by the children of Israel.</span>
