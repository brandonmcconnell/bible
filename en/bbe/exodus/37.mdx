<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 25:10–20; Exodus 26:33; Numbers 10:33–36"> And Bezalel made the ark of hard wood, two and a half cubits long, a cubit and a half wide and a cubit and a half high;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 30:3"> Plating it inside and out with the best gold, and putting an edge of gold all round it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And he made four gold rings for its four angles, two on one side and two on the other,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Peter 1:7; Numbers 4:14–15"> And rods of the same wood plated with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; 2 Samuel 6:3–7; Numbers 1:50"> These rods he put in the rings at the sides of the ark, for lifting it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 9:5; Titus 2:14; Romans 3:25"> And he made the cover all of gold, two and a half cubits long and a cubit and a half wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:2; Psalms 104:4; Psalms 80:1"> And he made two winged ones, hammered out of one bit of gold, for the two ends of the cover;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Placing one at one end and one at the other; the winged ones were part of the cover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 1:51; Hebrews 1:14; Genesis 28:12"> And their wings were stretched out over the cover; the faces of the winged ones were opposite one another and facing the cover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 1:14; Ezekiel 40:39–42; Malachi 1:12"> And he made the table of hard wood, two cubits long, a cubit wide and a cubit and a half high;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Plating it with the best gold and putting a gold edge all round it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And he made a frame all round it about as wide as a man's hand, edged with gold all round.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And he made four gold rings, and put the rings at the angles of its four feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The rings were fixed under the frame to take the rods with which the table was to be lifted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The rods for lifting the table he made of hard wood plated with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 25:29; 1 Kings 7:50; 2 Kings 12:13"> And all the table-vessels, the plates and spoons and basins and the cups for liquids, he made of the best gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–39; Hebrews 9:2; 2 Chronicles 13:11"> Then he made the support for the lights, all of the best gold; its base and its pillar were of hammered gold; its cups and buds and flowers were all made out of the same metal:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> It had six branches coming out from its sides, three from one side and three from the other;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Every branch having three cups made like almond flowers, every cup with a bud and a flower on all the branches;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:5; Jeremiah 1:11; Exodus 25:33"> And on its pillar, four cups like almond flowers, every one with its bud and its flower;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 25:35"> And under every two branches a bud, made with the branch, for all six branches of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:5; Exodus 25:31; Psalms 51:17"> The buds and the branches were made of the same metal, all together one complete work of the best hammered gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 1:12; Exodus 25:37; Revelation 1:20–1"> And he made the seven vessels for the lights, and all the necessary instruments for it, of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> A talent of the best gold was used for the making of it and its vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 30:1–5; Hebrews 7:25; Revelation 8:3–4"> And he made the altar for the burning of spices, using the same hard wood; it was square, a cubit long and a cubit wide and two cubits high; the horns made of the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> The top and the sides and the horns were all plated with the best gold; and he put an edge of gold all round it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And he made two gold rings, placing them on the two opposite sides under the edge, to take the rods for lifting it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> The rods he made of the same hard wood, plating them with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 141:2; Hebrews 7:25; 1 John 2:20"> And he made the holy oil and the perfume of sweet spices for burning, after the art of the perfume-maker.</span>
