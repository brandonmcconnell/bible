<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 26:30; Exodus 13:4; Numbers 7:1"> On the first day of the first month you are to put up the House of the Tent of meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:12; Numbers 4:5; Revelation 11:19"> And inside it put the ark of the law, hanging the veil before it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 24:8; Exodus 40:22–25; Exodus 37:10–24"> And put the table inside, placing all the things on it in order; and put in the support for the lights, and let its lights be burning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; Hebrews 9:24; 1 John 2:1–2"> And put the gold altar for burning perfumes in front of the ark of the law, hanging the curtain over the doorway of the House.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 40:29; 1 John 4:9–10; Hebrews 13:10"> And put the altar of burned offerings before the doorway of the House of the Tent of meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 13:1; 1 John 1:7; Psalms 26:6"> And let the washing-vessel, with water in it, be put between the Tent of meeting and the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 40:33; Ephesians 4:11–12; Matthew 16:18"> And put up the hangings forming the open space all round it, with the curtain over its doorway.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 8:10; Numbers 7:1; Matthew 3:16"> And take the holy oil and put it on the House and everything in it, and make it and everything in it holy:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 29:36–37; Luke 1:35; Isaiah 61:1"> And put oil on the altar of burned offering, and make it and all its vessels holy; this altar is to be most holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And put oil on the washing-vessel and its base, and make them holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 8:1–13; Leviticus 9:1–24; Exodus 29:1–35"> Then let Aaron and his sons come to the door of the Tent of meeting; and after washing them with water,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 28:41; 1 John 2:20; 1 John 2:27"> You are to put on Aaron the holy robes; and you are to put oil on him, and make him holy, so that he may be my priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; Romans 8:30; 1 Corinthians 1:30"> And take his sons with him and put coats on them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 25:13; Hebrews 5:1–14; Hebrews 8:1–10"> And put oil on them as you did on their father, so that they may be my priests: the putting on of oil will make them priests for ever, from generation to generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:1; Exodus 40:17–32; Deuteronomy 12:32"> And Moses did this; as the Lord gave him orders, so he did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 7:1; Exodus 40:1–2; Numbers 9:1"> So on the first day of the first month in the second year the House was put up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:27–28; 1 Peter 1:5; Leviticus 26:11"> Moses put up the House; placing its bases in position and lifting up its uprights, putting in the rods and planting the pillars in their places;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 26:1–14; Exodus 36:8–19"> Stretching the outer tent over it, and covering it, as the Lord had given him orders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 16:34; Deuteronomy 10:5; Hebrews 10:19–21"> And he took the law and put it inside the ark, and put the rods at its side and the cover over it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 26:33; Exodus 40:3; Exodus 35:12"> And he took the ark into the House, hanging up the veil before it as the Lord had given him orders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 26:35; John 6:53–57; Ephesians 3:8"> And he put the table in the Tent of meeting, on the north side outside the veil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 25:30; Exodus 40:4; Hebrews 9:2"> And he put the bread on it in order before the Lord, as the Lord had said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 26:35; Revelation 2:5; John 1:1"> The support for the lights he put in the Tent of meeting, opposite the table, on the south side:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 40:4; Exodus 25:37; Revelation 4:5"> Lighting the lights before the Lord, as the Lord had given him orders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 40:5; Hebrews 7:25; John 11:42"> And he put the gold altar in the Tent of meeting, in front of the veil:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 30:7"> Burning sweet perfumes on it, as the Lord had given him orders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 40:5; Hebrews 10:19–20; Ephesians 2:18"> And he put up the curtain at the doorway of the House.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 40:6; Hebrews 13:5–6; Hebrews 13:10"> And at the door of the House of the Tent of meeting, he put the altar of burned offerings, offering on it the burned offering and the meal offering, as the Lord had given him orders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 40:7; Exodus 38:8; Ezekiel 36:25"> And between the altar and the Tent of meeting he put the vessel with water in it for washing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 30:19–20; Psalms 26:6; John 13:10"> In it the hands and feet of Moses and Aaron and his sons were washed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 73:19; Exodus 30:19–20; Exodus 40:19"> Whenever they went into the Tent of meeting, and when they came near the altar, as the Lord had given orders to Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 40:8; Exodus 27:9–16; John 17:4"> And he put up the hangings forming the open space round the House and the altar, and put the curtain over the doorway. So Moses made the work complete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Revelation 15:8; Numbers 9:15–23; 2 Chronicles 7:2"> Then the cloud came down covering the Tent of meeting, and the House was full of the glory of the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 8:11; Leviticus 16:2; 2 Chronicles 7:2"> So that Moses was not able to go into the Tent of meeting, because the cloud was resting on it, and the House was full of the glory of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:19; Numbers 9:17; Exodus 13:21–22"> And whenever the cloud was taken up from the House, the children of Israel went forward on their journey:</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 9:19–22; Psalms 31:15"> But while the cloud was there, they made no move till it was taken up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Psalms 78:14; Numbers 9:15; Exodus 13:21"> For the cloud of the Lord was resting on the House by day, and at night there was fire in the cloud, before the eyes of all the people of Israel, and so it was through all their journeys.</span>
