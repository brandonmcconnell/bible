<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Nehemiah 11:2; Ezra 2:68"> Say to the children of Israel that they are to make me an offering; from every man who has the impulse in his heart take an offering for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:9; Job 28:2"> And this is the offering you are to take from them: gold and silver and brass;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 19:8; Genesis 41:42; Ezekiel 16:10"> And blue and purple and red, and the best linen, and goats' hair;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 26:37–1; Exodus 26:14–15; Exodus 36:20"> And sheepskins coloured red, and leather, and hard wood;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 27:20; Exodus 25:37; Exodus 40:24–25"> Oil for the light, spices for the sweet-smelling oil, sweet perfumes for burning;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Exodus 28:6–30"> Beryls and stones of value to be put on the ephod and on the priest's bag.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Kings 6:13; Revelation 21:3"> And let them make me a holy place, so that I may be ever present among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Hebrews 8:5; Acts 7:44"> Make the House and everything in it from the designs which I will give you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 9:4; Exodus 37:1–3; Deuteronomy 10:1–3"> And they are to make an ark of hard wood; two and a half cubits long, and a cubit and a half wide and high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 25:24; Exodus 30:3; 2 Chronicles 3:4"> It is to be plated inside and out with the best gold, with an edge of gold all round it</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 27:7; Exodus 25:26; Exodus 25:15"> And make four rings of gold for it, to be fixed on its four feet, two rings on one side of it and two on the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:15; Numbers 4:11; Exodus 27:6"> And make rods of the same wood, plating them with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And put the rods through the rings at the sides of the ark, for lifting it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 8:8; 2 Chronicles 5:9"> The rods are to be kept in the rings, and never taken out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:26; 1 Kings 8:9; Hebrews 9:4"> Inside the ark you are to put the record which I will give you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 9:5; Exodus 37:6; Hebrews 4:16"> And you are to make a cover of the best gold, two and a half cubits long and a cubit and a half wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:20; Hebrews 9:5; 1 Kings 8:6–7"> And at the two ends of the cover you are to make two winged ones of hammered gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> One at one end and one at the other; the winged ones are to be part of the cover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:18; 1 Kings 8:7; Hebrews 9:5"> And their wings are to be outstretched over the cover, and the winged ones are to be opposite one another, facing the cover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 26:34; Romans 10:4; Exodus 25:16–17"> And put the cover over the ark, and in the ark the record which I will give you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 29:42–43; Numbers 7:89; Isaiah 37:16"> And there, between the two winged ones on the cover of the ark, I will come to you, face to face, and make clear to you all the orders I have to give you for the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 9:2; Exodus 37:10–16; 1 Kings 7:48"> And you are to make a table of the same wood, two cubits long, a cubit wide and a cubit and a half high,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 25:11; 1 Kings 6:20–22"> Plated with the best gold, with a gold edge all round it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 37:2; Exodus 30:3"> And make a frame all round it, as wide as a man's hand, with a gold edge to the frame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And make four gold rings and put them at the four angles, on the four feet of the table;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 25:14; Exodus 25:28"> The rings are to be fixed under the frame to take the rods with which the table is to be lifted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 25:14; Acts 9:15; Numbers 10:17"> Make rods of the same wood, plated with gold, for lifting the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 4:7; Exodus 37:16; Revelation 3:20"> And make the table-vessels, the spoons and the cups and the basins for liquids, all of the best gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 24:5–9; Matthew 12:4; Exodus 39:36"> And on the table at all times you are to keep my holy bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 7:49; Zechariah 4:2; Exodus 37:17–24"> And you are to make a support for lights, of the best gold; its base and its pillar are to be of hammered gold; its cups, its buds, and its flowers are to be made of the same metal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> It is to have six branches coming out from its sides; three branches from one side and three from the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 17:4–8; Jeremiah 1:11–12; Zechariah 4:3"> Every branch having three cups made like almond flowers, every cup with a bud and a flower, on all the branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And on the pillar, four cups like almond flowers, every one with its bud and its flower:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And under every two branches a bud, made with the branch, for all the six branches of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 25:18; 1 Kings 10:16–17; 2 Chronicles 9:15"> The buds and the branches are to be made of the same metal; all together one complete work of hammered gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 8:2; Revelation 1:20–1; Leviticus 24:2–4"> Then you are to make its seven vessels for the lights, putting them in their place so that they give light in front of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Kings 12:13; Jeremiah 52:18; 2 Kings 25:14"> And the instruments and trays for use with it are all to be of the best gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> A talent of gold will be needed for it, with all these vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Hebrews 8:5; Exodus 26:30; Acts 7:44"> And see that you make them from the design which you saw on the mountain.</span>
