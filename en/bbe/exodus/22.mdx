<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 19:8; 2 Samuel 12:6; Proverbs 6:31"> If a man takes without right another man's ox or his sheep, and puts it to death or gets a price for it, he is to give five oxen for an ox, or four sheep for a sheep, in payment: the thief will have to make payment for what he has taken; if he has no money, he himself will have to be exchanged for money, so that payment may be made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 24:43; Matthew 6:19–20; Numbers 35:27"> If a thief is taken in the act of forcing his way into a house, and his death is caused by a blow, the owner of the house is not responsible for his blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 21:2; Isaiah 50:1; Matthew 18:25"> But if it is after dawn, he will be responsible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 22:7; Exodus 22:1; Exodus 22:9"> If he still has what he had taken, whatever it is, ox or ass or sheep, he is to give twice its value.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 22:3; Exodus 22:12; Job 20:18"> If a man makes a fire in a field or a vine-garden, and lets the fire do damage to another man's field, he is to give of the best produce of his field or his vine-garden to make up for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 22:9; Exodus 21:33–34; 2 Samuel 14:30–31"> If there is a fire and the flames get to the thorns at the edge of the field, causing destruction of the cut grain or of the living grain, or of the field, he who made the fire will have to make up for the damage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 22:4; Proverbs 6:30–31; Jeremiah 2:26"> If a man puts money or goods in the care of his neighbour to keep for him, and it is taken from the man's house, if they get the thief, he will have to make payment of twice the value.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 22:28; Deuteronomy 16:18; Exodus 21:6"> If they do not get the thief, let the master of the house come before the judges and take an oath that he has not put his hand on his neighbour's goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:1; Exodus 22:4; Numbers 5:6–7"> In any question about an ox or an ass or a sheep or clothing, or about the loss of any property which anyone says is his, let the two sides put their cause before God; and he who is judged to be in the wrong is to make payment to his neighbour of twice the value.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 12:48; 2 Timothy 1:12; Luke 16:11"> If a man puts an ass or an ox or a sheep or any beast into the keeping of his neighbour, and it comes to death or is damaged or is taken away, without any person seeing it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 6:16; Leviticus 5:1; 1 Kings 2:42–43"> If he takes his oath before the Lord that he has not put his hand to his neighbour's goods, the owner is to take his word for it and he will not have to make payment for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 31:39; Exodus 22:7"> But if it is taken from him by a thief, he is to make up for the loss of it to its owner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Nahum 2:12; Micah 5:8; Ezekiel 4:14"> But if it has been damaged by a beast, and he is able to make this clear, he will not have to make payment for what was damaged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:19–20; Exodus 21:34; Deuteronomy 15:2"> If a man gets from his neighbour the use of one of his beasts, and it is damaged or put to death when the owner is not with it, he will certainly have to make payment for the loss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 8:10"> If the owner is with it, he will not have to make payment: if he gave money for the use of it, the loss is covered by the payment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:28–29; Genesis 34:2–4"> If a man takes a virgin, who has not given her word to another man, and has connection with her, he will have to give a bride-price for her to be his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 34:12; 1 Samuel 18:25; Deuteronomy 22:29"> If her father will not give her to him on any account, he will have to give the regular payment for virgins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 20:27; Deuteronomy 18:10–11; 1 Samuel 28:3"> Any woman using unnatural powers or secret arts is to be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 18:23; Deuteronomy 27:21; Leviticus 20:15–16"> Any man who has sex connection with a beast is to be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:2–5; Numbers 25:7–8; Deuteronomy 18:20"> Complete destruction will come on any man who makes offerings to any other god but the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 23:9; Deuteronomy 10:19; Leviticus 19:33"> Do no wrong to a man from a strange country, and do not be hard on him; for you yourselves were living in a strange country, in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:17; Isaiah 1:17; Isaiah 1:23"> Do no wrong to a widow, or to a child whose father is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 18:6; Psalms 10:17–18; Luke 18:7"> If you are cruel to them in any way, and their cry comes up to me, I will certainly give ear;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 109:9; Jeremiah 15:8; Psalms 76:7"> And in the heat of my wrath I will put you to death with the sword, so that your wives will be widows and your children without fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 15:5; Leviticus 25:35–37; Deuteronomy 23:19–20"> If you let any of the poor among my people have the use of your money, do not be a hard creditor to him, and do not take interest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 20:16; Amos 2:8; Deuteronomy 24:6"> If ever you take your neighbour's clothing in exchange for the use of your money, let him have it back before the sun goes down:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 34:6; 2 Chronicles 30:9; Psalms 136:10–11"> For it is the only thing he has for covering his skin; what is he to go to sleep in? and when his cry comes up to me, I will give ear, for my mercy is great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 23:5; Ecclesiastes 10:20; Exodus 22:8–9"> You may not say evil of the judges, or put a curse on the ruler of your people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 23:16; Exodus 13:2; James 1:18"> Do not keep back your offerings from the wealth of your grain and your vines. The first of your sons you are to give to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 22:27; Deuteronomy 15:19; Exodus 13:12"> In the same way with your oxen and your sheep: for seven days let the young one be with its mother; on the eighth day give it to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:14; Leviticus 19:2; Deuteronomy 14:21"> You are to be holy men to me: the flesh of no animal whose death has been caused by the beasts of the field may be used for your food; it is to be given to the dogs.</span>
