<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 27:1–8; Ezekiel 43:13–17; Exodus 40:6"> The altar of burned offerings he made of hard wood; a square altar, five cubits long, five cubits wide and three cubits high,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 27:2; Job 6:12"> And he put horns at its four angles made of the same, plating it all with brass;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 27:3; 1 Samuel 2:13"> And brass was used for all the vessels of the altar, the baskets and the spades, the basins and the meat-hooks and the fire-trays; all the vessels he made of brass</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And he made a network of brass for the altar, under the frame round it, stretching half-way up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 27:4"> And four rings for the four angles of this network, to take the rods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:3; Exodus 25:6"> The rods he made of hard wood plated with brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Corinthians 2:2; 1 Corinthians 1:24"> He put the rods through the rings at the opposite sides of the altar for lifting it; he made the altar hollow, boarded in with wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:22; Luke 2:37; Exodus 40:7"> And he made the washing-vessel of brass on a brass base, using the polished brass looking-glasses given by the women who did work at the doors of the Tent of meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 27:9–19; Psalms 89:7; 1 Kings 6:36"> To make the open space, he put hangings on the south side, of the best linen, a hundred cubits long:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Their twenty pillars and their twenty bases were brass; and the hooks of the pillars and their bands were of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And for the north side. hangings a hundred cubits long, on twenty brass pillars in brass bases, with silver hooks and bands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And on the west side, hangings fifty cubits long, on ten pillars in ten bases, with silver bands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And on the east side, the open space was fifty cubits long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:14"> The hangings on one side of the doorway were fifteen cubits long, on three pillars with their three bases;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the same on the other side of the doorway; on this side and on that the hangings were fifteen cubits long, on three pillars with their three bases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> All the hangings were of the best linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the bases of the pillars were of brass; their hooks and the bands round the tops of them were of silver; all the pillars were ringed with silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:14"> And the curtain for the doorway of the open space was of the best linen, with designs of blue and purple and red in needlework; it was twenty cubits long and five cubits high, to go with the hangings round the sides.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> There were four pillars with their bases, all of brass, the hooks being of silver, and their tops and their bands being covered with silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:9; Exodus 27:19; Ephesians 2:21–22"> All the nails used for the House and the open space round it were of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 7:44; Numbers 9:15; 2 Chronicles 24:6"> This is the price of the making of the House, even the House of witness, as it was valued by the word of Moses, for the work of the Levites under the direction of Ithamar, the son of Aaron the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 35:30–3; Exodus 31:1–5; Psalms 119:6"> Bezalel, the son of Uri, the son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah, made everything as the Lord had given orders to Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 35:34; Exodus 31:6"> And with him was Oholiab, the son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan; a designer and a trained workman, expert in needlework of blue and purple and red and the best linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 18:16; Numbers 3:47; Leviticus 27:25"> The gold used for all the different work done for the holy place, the gold which was given, was twenty-nine talents, and seven hundred and thirty shekels in weight, by the scale of the holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 30:12"> And the silver given by those who were numbered of the people was a hundred talents, and a thousand, seven hundred and seventy-five shekels in weight, by the scale of the holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 1:46; Exodus 30:13; Exodus 12:37"> A beka, that is, half a shekel by the holy scale, for everyone who was numbered; there were six hundred and three thousand, five hundred and fifty men of twenty years old and over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 26:19; Exodus 26:25; Exodus 26:21"> Of this silver, a hundred talents was used for making the bases of the pillars of the holy place and of the veil; a talent for every base.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 27:17"> And a thousand, seven hundred and seventy-five shekels of silver was used to make the hooks for the pillars, and for plating the tops of the pillars and for making their bands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> The brass which was given was seventy talents, two thousand four hundred shekels;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 26:37; Exodus 27:17; Exodus 27:10"> From it he made the bases of the doorway of the Tent of meeting and the brass altar and the network for it and all the vessels for the altar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And the bases for the open space all round and for its doorway, and all the nails for the House and for the open space.</span>
