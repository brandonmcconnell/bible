<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 2:16; Psalms 77:14–15; Exodus 3:1"> Now news came to Jethro, the priest of Midian, Moses' father-in-law, of all God had done for Moses and for Israel his people, and how the Lord had taken Israel out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 2:21; Exodus 4:25–26"> And Jethro, Moses' father-in-law, took Zipporah, Moses' wife, after he had sent her away,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 2:22; Acts 7:29; Psalms 39:12"> And her two sons, one of whom was named Gershom, for he said, I have been living in a strange land:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 13:6; 2 Timothy 4:17; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> And the name of the other was Eliezer, for he said, The God of my father was my help, and kept me safe from the sword of Pharaoh:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; Exodus 3:12; Exodus 24:16–17"> And Jethro, Moses' father-in-law, came with his sons and his wife to where Moses had put up his tent in the waste land, by the mountain of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And he said to Moses, I, your father-in-law, have come to you, with your wife and your two sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 29:13; Genesis 14:17; 2 Samuel 11:7"> And Moses went out to his father-in-law, and went down on his face before him and gave him a kiss; and they said to one another, Are you well? and they came into the tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 145:4–12; Nehemiah 9:9–15; Psalms 107:2"> And Moses gave his father-in-law an account of all the Lord had done to Pharaoh and to the Egyptians because of Israel, and of all the troubles which had come on them by the way, and how the Lord had given them salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:26; Romans 12:15; Isaiah 66:10"> And Jethro was glad because the Lord had been good to Israel, freeing them from the power of the Egyptians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 14:20; 2 Samuel 18:28; Luke 1:68"> And Jethro said, Praise be to the Lord, who has taken you out of the hand of Pharaoh and out of the hand of the Egyptians; freeing the people from the yoke of the Egyptians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 95:3; Exodus 15:11; 2 Chronicles 2:5"> Now I am certain that the Lord is greater than all gods, for he has overcome them in their pride.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 31:54; Deuteronomy 12:7; Exodus 24:11"> Then Jethro, Moses' father-in-law, made a burned offering to God: and Aaron came, with the chiefs of Israel, and had a meal with Moses' father-in-law, before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 16:5; Job 29:7; Judges 5:10"> Now on the day after, Moses took his seat to give decisions for the people: and the people were waiting before Moses from morning till evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And when Moses' father-in-law saw all he was doing, he said, What is this you are doing for the people? why are you seated here by yourself, with all the people waiting before you from morning till evening?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 15:34; Numbers 27:5; Numbers 9:8"> And Moses said to his father-in-law, Because the people come to me to get directions from God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 24:14; 1 Corinthians 6:1; 2 Samuel 15:3"> And if they have any question between themselves, they come to me, and I am judge between a man and his neighbour, and I give them the orders and laws of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Moses' father-in-law said to him, What you are doing is not good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 11:14–17; Acts 6:1–4; 2 Corinthians 12:15"> Your strength and that of the people will be completely used up: this work is more than you are able to do by yourself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 27:5; Deuteronomy 5:5; Exodus 20:19"> Give ear now to my suggestion, and may God be with you: you are to be the people's representative before God, taking their causes to him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:1; Psalms 143:8; Deuteronomy 1:18"> Teaching them his rules and his laws, guiding them in the way they have to go, and making clear to them the work they have to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 6:3; Deuteronomy 1:13–17; 2 Chronicles 19:5–10"> But for the rest, take from among the people able men, such as have the fear of God, true men hating profits wrongly made; and put such men over them, to be captains of thousands, captains of hundreds and of fifties and of tens;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 11:17; Numbers 27:2; Numbers 15:33"> And let them be judges in the causes of the people at all times: and let them put before you all important questions, but in small things let them give decisions themselves: in this way, it will be less hard for you, and they will take the weight off you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:6–7; Exodus 18:18; 1 Samuel 8:22"> If you do this, and God gives approval, then you will be able to go on without weariness, and all this people will go to their tents in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezra 10:2; Exodus 18:19; Exodus 18:2–5"> So Moses took note of the words of his father-in-law, and did as he had said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:15; Exodus 18:21; Acts 6:5"> And he made selection of able men out of all Israel, and made them heads over the people, captains of thousands, captains of hundreds and of fifties and of tens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 18:22; Exodus 18:14–15; Deuteronomy 17:8"> And they were judges in the causes of the people at all times: the hard questions they put before Moses; but on every small point they gave decisions themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 10:29–30; Judges 19:9; Genesis 31:55"> And Moses let his father-in-law go away, and he went back to his land.</span>
