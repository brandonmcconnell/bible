<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 10:9; Acts 4:29; Matthew 10:28"> And after that, Moses and Aaron came to Pharaoh, and said, The Lord, the God of Israel, says, Let my people go so that they may keep a feast to me in the waste land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 3:19; Job 21:15; 2 Kings 18:35"> And Pharaoh said, Who is the Lord, to whose voice I am to give ear and let Israel go? I have no knowledge of the Lord and I will not let Israel go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 3:18; Zechariah 14:16–19; Ezekiel 6:11"> And they said, The God of the Hebrews has come to us: let us then go three days' journey into the waste land to make an offering to the Lord our God, so that he may not send death on us by disease or the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 1:11; Acts 24:5; Amos 7:10"> And the king of Egypt said to them, Why do you, Moses and Aaron, take the people away from their work? get back to your work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 1:7–11; Proverbs 14:28"> And Pharaoh said, Truly, the people of the land are increasing in number, and you are keeping them back from their work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 5:10; Exodus 5:19; Deuteronomy 16:18"> The same day Pharaoh gave orders to the overseers and those who were responsible for the work, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 19:19; Genesis 24:25"> Give these men no more dry stems for their brick-making as you have been doing; let them go and get the material for themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 106:41"> But see that they make the same number of bricks as before, and no less: for they have no love for work; and so they are crying out and saying, Let us go and make an offering to our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 18:20; Jeremiah 43:2; Job 16:3"> Give the men harder work, and see that they do it; let them not give attention to false words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 1:11; Exodus 3:7; Proverbs 29:12"> And the overseers of the people and their responsible men went out and said to the people, Pharaoh says, I will give you no more dry stems.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 5:13–14"> Go yourselves and get dry stems wherever you are able; for your work is not to be any less.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 15:7; Isaiah 5:24; Isaiah 47:14"> So the people were sent in all directions through the land of Egypt to get dry grass for stems.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the overseers went on driving them and saying, Do your full day's work as before when there were dry stems for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 10:24"> And the responsible men of the children of Israel, whom Pharaoh's overseers had put over them, were given blows, and they said to them, Why have you not done your regular work, in making bricks as before?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then the responsible men of the children of Israel came to Pharaoh, protesting and saying, Why are you acting in this way to your servants?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> They give us no dry stems and they say to us, Make bricks: and they give your servants blows; but it is your people who are in the wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 26:8; John 6:27; Exodus 5:8"> But he said, You have no love for work: that is why you say, Let us go and make an offering to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:18; Daniel 2:9–13"> Go now, get back to your work; no dry stems will be given to you, but you are to make the full number of bricks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:8; Ecclesiastes 4:1; Deuteronomy 32:36"> Then the responsible men of the children of Israel saw that they were purposing evil when they said, The number of bricks which you have to make every day will be no less than before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And they came face to face with Moses and Aaron, who were in their way when they came out from Pharaoh:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; 1 Samuel 13:4; 1 Chronicles 19:6"> And they said to them, May the Lord take note of you and be your judge; for you have given Pharaoh and his servants a bad opinion of us, putting a sword in their hands for our destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:7; 1 Kings 19:10; Numbers 11:14–15"> And Moses went back to the Lord and said, Lord, why have you done evil to this people? why have you sent me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:21; Hebrews 10:36–37; Isaiah 28:16"> For from the time when I came to Pharaoh to put your words before him, he has done evil to this people, and you have given them no help.</span>
