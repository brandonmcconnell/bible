<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 12:1; Exodus 13:1"> And the Lord said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 33:7–8; Jeremiah 44:1; Exodus 13:17–18"> Give orders to the children of Israel to go back and put up their tents before Pi-hahiroth, between Migdol and the sea, in front of Baal-zephon, opposite to which you are to put up your tents by the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 4:28; 1 Samuel 23:23; Psalms 71:11"> And Pharaoh will say of the children of Israel, They are wandering without direction, they are shut in by the waste land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 9:22–23; Exodus 7:5; Romans 9:17"> And I will make Pharaoh's heart hard, and he will come after them and I will be honoured over Pharaoh and all his army, so that the Egyptians may see that I am the Lord. And they did so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 105:25; Exodus 12:33; Luke 11:24–26"> And word came to Pharaoh of the flight of the people: and the feeling of Pharaoh and of his servants about the people was changed, and they said, Why have we let Israel go, so that they will do no more work for us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> So he had his war-carriage made ready and took his people with him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 15:4; Judges 4:3; Psalms 68:17"> And he took six hundred carriages, all the carriages of Egypt, and captains over all of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 13:17; Numbers 33:3; Deuteronomy 26:8"> And the Lord made the heart of Pharaoh hard, and he went after the children of Israel: for the children of Israel had gone out without fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 15:9; Joshua 24:6; Exodus 14:2"> But the Egyptians went after them, all the horses and carriages of Pharaoh, and his horsemen, and his army, and overtook them in their tents by the sea, by Pihahiroth, before Baal-zephon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 24:7; Psalms 34:17; Nehemiah 9:9"> And when Pharaoh came near, the children of Israel, lifting up their eyes, saw the Egyptians coming after them, and were full of fear; and their cry went up to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 106:7–8; Numbers 14:1–4; Exodus 15:23–24"> And they said to Moses, Was there no resting-place for the dead in Egypt, that you have taken us away to come to our death in the waste land? why have you taken us out of Egypt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jonah 4:3; Exodus 6:9; Jonah 4:8"> Did we not say to you in Egypt, Let us be as we are, working for the Egyptians? for it is better to be the servants of the Egyptians than to come to our death in the waste land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:17; 2 Chronicles 20:15; Exodus 14:30"> But Moses said, Keep where you are and have no fear; now you will see the salvation of the Lord which he will give you today; for the Egyptians whom you see today you will never see again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:22; Deuteronomy 20:4; 2 Chronicles 20:17"> The Lord will make war for you, you have only to keep quiet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 7:10; Ezra 10:4–5; Nehemiah 9:9"> And the Lord said to Moses, Why are you crying out to me? give the children of Israel the order to go forward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 7:19; Exodus 4:20; Exodus 4:2"> And let your rod be lifted up and your hand stretched out over the sea, and it will be parted in two; and the children of Israel will go through on dry land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 14:8; Exodus 14:4; Ezekiel 34:11"> And I will make the heart of the Egyptians hard, and they will go in after them: and I will be honoured over Pharaoh and over his army, his war-carriages, and his horsemen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 7:5; Exodus 14:4; Exodus 7:17"> And the Egyptians will see that I am the Lord, when I get honour over Pharaoh and his war-carriages and his horsemen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 63:9; Exodus 32:34; Exodus 23:20–21"> Then the angel of God, who had been before the tents of Israel, took his place at their back; and the pillar of cloud, moving from before them, came to rest at their back:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14; 2 Corinthians 2:15–16; Proverbs 4:18–19"> And it came between the army of Egypt and the army of Israel; and there was a dark cloud between them, and they went on through the night; but the one army came no nearer to the other all the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 63:12; Exodus 15:8; Psalms 66:6"> And when Moses' hand was stretched out over the sea, the Lord with a strong east wind made the sea go back all night, and the waters were parted in two and the sea became dry land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 11:29; Psalms 66:6; Exodus 15:19"> And the children of Israel went through the sea on dry land: and the waters were a wall on their right side and on their left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 15:19; Exodus 14:17; Exodus 15:9"> Then the Egyptians went after them into the middle of the sea, all Pharaoh's horses and his war-carriages and his horsemen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 13:21; Psalms 77:16–19; Exodus 14:25"> And in the morning watch, the Lord, looking out on the armies of the Egyptians from the pillar of fire and cloud, sent trouble on the army of the Egyptians;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 14:14; Deuteronomy 3:22; Job 20:24"> And made the wheels of their war-carriages stiff, so that they had hard work driving them: so the Egyptians said, Let us go in flight from before the face of Israel, for the Lord is fighting for them against the Egyptians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 14:16; Exodus 8:5; James 2:13"> And the Lord said to Moses, Let your hand be stretched out over the sea, and the waters will come back again on the Egyptians, and on their war-carriages and on their horsemen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 4:18; Psalms 78:53; Hebrews 11:29"> And when Moses' hand was stretched out over the sea, at dawn the sea came flowing back, meeting the Egyptians in their flight, and the Lord sent destruction on the Egyptians in the middle of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 78:53; Psalms 106:9–11; Habakkuk 3:13"> And the waters came back, covering the war-carriages and the horsemen and all the army of Pharaoh which went after them into the middle of the sea; not one of them was to be seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 14:22; Psalms 77:19–20; Isaiah 43:2"> But the children of Israel went through the sea walking on dry land, and the waters were a wall on their right side and on their left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 106:8; Psalms 106:10; Psalms 92:9–11"> So that day the Lord gave Israel salvation from the hands of the Egyptians; and Israel saw the Egyptians dead on the sea's edge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 2:11; John 11:45; John 2:23–25"> And Israel saw the great work which the Lord had done against the Egyptians, and the fear of the Lord came on the people and they had faith in the Lord and in his servant Moses.</span>
