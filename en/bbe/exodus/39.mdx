<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 31:10; Exodus 35:23; Exodus 35:19"> And from the needlework of blue and purple and red they made the robes used for the work of the holy place, and the holy robes for Aaron, as the Lord had given orders to Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 28:6–12; Leviticus 8:7; Exodus 25:7"> The ephod he made of gold and blue and purple and red and the best linen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 36:8; Exodus 26:1"> Hammering the gold into thin plates and cutting it into wires to be worked into the blue and the purple and the red and the linen by the designer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And they made two bands for joining its edges together at the top of the arms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 1:13; Matthew 28:20; Isaiah 11:5"> And the beautifully worked band which went on it was of the same design and the same material, worked in gold and blue and purple and red and twisted linen-work, as the Lord gave orders to Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 28:9; Ezekiel 28:13; Job 28:16"> Then they made the beryl stones, fixed in twisted frames of gold and cut like the cutting of a stamp, with the names of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 4:7; Exodus 28:12; Exodus 28:29"> These he put on the ephod, over the arm-holes, to be stones of memory for the children of Israel, as the Lord had said to Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Leviticus 8:8–9; Isaiah 59:17"> The priest's bag was designed like the ephod, of the best linen worked with gold and blue and purple and red.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> It was square and folded in two, as long and as wide as the stretch of a man's hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 28:21; Exodus 28:16–17; Revelation 21:19–21"> And on it they put four lines of stones: in the first line was a carnelian, a chrysolite, and an emerald;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 28:18; Ezekiel 28:13"> In the second, a ruby, a sapphire, and an onyx;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> In the third, a jacinth, an agate, and an amethyst;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> In the fourth, a topaz, a beryl, and a jasper; they were fixed in twisted frames of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 21:12"> There were twelve stones for the twelve tribes of Israel; on every one the name of one of the tribes of Israel was cut, like the cutting of a stamp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 10:28; John 17:12; 1 Peter 1:5"> And on the bag they put gold chains, twisted like cords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 25:12"> And they made two gold frames and two gold rings, the rings being fixed to the ends of the priest's bag;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And they put the two twisted chains on the two rings at the ends of the priest's bag;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 28:14; Song of Solomon 1:10"> And the other two ends of the chains were joined to the two frames and fixed to the front of the ephod over the arm-holes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And they made two rings of gold and put them on the two lower ends of the bag, on the inner side nearest to the ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 26:3"> And two other gold rings were put on the front of the ephod, over the arm-holes, at the join, and over the worked band.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 16:24; 1 Corinthians 1:25; 1 Corinthians 1:27"> And the rings on the bag were fixed to the rings of the ephod by a blue cord, keeping it in place over the band, so that the bag might not get loose, as the Lord gave orders to Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 28:31–35"> The robe which went with the ephod was made all of blue;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> With a hole at the top in the middle, like the hole in the coat of a fighting-man, edged with a band to make it strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 28:33; Galatians 5:22"> The skirts of the robe were worked all round with fruits in blue and purple and red made of twisted linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 28:33–34; Song of Solomon 4:13; Psalms 89:15"> And between the fruits all round the skirt they put gold bells, as the Lord gave orders to Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 28:34; Song of Solomon 4:13; Song of Solomon 6:7"> All round the skirt of the robe were bells and fruits in turn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:18; 1 Peter 1:13; Isaiah 61:10"> The coats for Aaron and his sons they made of the best linen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Ezekiel 44:18; Exodus 28:39"> And the twisted head-dress for Aaron, and beautiful head-dresses of linen, and linen trousers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 28:39"> And a linen band worked with a design of blue and purple and red, as the Lord had said to Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; Hebrews 7:26; Hebrews 1:3"> The plate for the holy crown was made of the best gold, and on it were cut these words, HOLY TO THE LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> It was fixed to the head-dress by a blue cord, as the Lord had given orders to Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 39:42–43; Matthew 28:20; Exodus 25:40"> So all the work on the House of the Tent of meeting was done; as the Lord had given orders to Moses, so the children of Israel did it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 35:11–19; Exodus 31:7–11; Exodus 36:1–38"> Then they took the House to Moses, the tent with all the things for it; its hooks, its boards, its rods, its pillars, and its bases;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> The outer cover of sheepskins coloured red, and the cover of leather, and the veil for the doorway;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Hebrews 9:5; Exodus 25:17; Hebrews 9:8"> The ark of the law, with its rods and its cover;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 25:30; 1 Kings 7:48"> The table, with all its vessels and the holy bread;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Philippians 2:15; Exodus 27:21; Matthew 5:14–16"> The support for the lights, with the vessels for the lights to be put in their places on it, and all its vessels, and the oil for the lights;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 30:7; Exodus 37:29; Exodus 30:3"> And the gold altar, and the holy oil, and the sweet perfume for burning, and the curtain for the doorway of the tent;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 8:64; Exodus 38:30"> And the brass altar, with its network of brass, and its rods and all its vessels, and the washing-vessel and its base;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Exodus 27:9–16"> The hangings for the open space, with the pillars and their bases, and the curtain for the doorway, and the cords and nails, and all the instruments necessary for the work of the House of the Tent of meeting;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 28:2; Exodus 31:10; Exodus 39:1"> The robes for use in the holy place, and the holy robes for Aaron and his sons when acting as priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Exodus 35:10; Exodus 23:21–22; 2 Timothy 2:15"> The children of Israel did everything as the Lord had given orders to Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 6:23–27; Leviticus 9:22–23; 2 Samuel 6:18"> Then Moses, when he saw all their work and saw that they had done everything as the Lord had said, gave them his blessing.</span>
