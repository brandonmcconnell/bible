<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 8:3; Exodus 37:25–28; Exodus 40:5"> And you are to make an altar for the burning of perfume; of hard wood let it be made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 27:2"> The altar is to be square, a cubit long and a cubit wide, and two cubits high, and its horns are to be made of the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 25:11; Exodus 25:24"> It is to be plated with the best gold, the top of it and the sides and the horns, with an edging of gold all round it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 25:27; Exodus 26:29; Exodus 27:7"> Under the edge on the two opposite sides, you are to make two gold rings, to take the rods for lifting it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 25:13; Exodus 25:27"> And make these rods of the same wood, plating them with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 25:21–22; Exodus 30:36; Leviticus 16:13"> And let it be placed in front of the veil before the ark of the law, before the cover which is over the law, where I will come face to face with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:28; 1 Chronicles 23:13; Exodus 27:20–21"> And on this altar sweet spices are to be burned by Aaron every morning when he sees to the lights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; 1 Thessalonians 5:17; Hebrews 7:25"> And every evening, when he puts the lights up in their places, the spices are to be burned, a sweet-smelling smoke going up before the Lord from generation to generation for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1"> No strange perfume, no burned offering or meal offering, and no drink offering is to be offered on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 16:18; Hebrews 9:22–23; Leviticus 16:29–30"> And once every year Aaron is to make its horns clean: with the blood of the sin-offering he is to make it clean once every year from generation to generation: it is most holy to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the Lord said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 31:50; 1 Chronicles 27:24; Exodus 38:25–26"> When you are taking the number of the children of Israel, let every man who is numbered give to the Lord a price for his life, so that no disease may come on them when they are numbered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 27:25; Numbers 3:47; Ezekiel 45:12"> And this is what they are to give; let every man who is numbered give half a shekel, by the scale of the holy place: (the shekel being valued at twenty gerahs:) this money is an offering to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 1:20; Numbers 1:18; Numbers 1:3"> Everyone who is numbered, from twenty years old and over, is to give an offering to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 31:50; Leviticus 17:11; Exodus 30:12"> The man of wealth is to give no more and the poor man no less than the half-shekel of silver, when the offering is made to the Lord as the price for your lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:32–33; Numbers 16:40; Exodus 38:25–31"> And you are to take this money from the children of Israel to be used for the work of the Tent of meeting, to keep the memory of the children of Israel before the Lord and to be the price of your lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the Lord said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 40:7; Exodus 38:8; 1 Kings 7:23"> You are to make a brass washing-vessel, with a brass base; and put it between the Tent of meeting and the altar, with water in it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; Exodus 40:31–32; Psalms 26:6"> That it may be used by Aaron and his sons for washing their hands and feet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1–3; Hebrews 12:28–29; 1 Chronicles 13:10"> Whenever they go into the Tent of meeting they are to be washed with water, to keep them from death; and whenever they come near to do the work of the altar, or to make an offering by fire to the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 28:43; Exodus 27:21"> Their hands and feet are to be washed. so that they may be safe from death: this is an order to them for ever; to him and his seed from generation to generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And the Lord said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:19; Song of Solomon 4:14; Ezekiel 27:22"> Take the best spices, five hundred shekels' weight of liquid myrrh, and of sweet cinnamon half as much, that is, two hundred and fifty shekels, and two hundred and fifty shekels of sweet calamus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 45:8; Exodus 29:40; Numbers 3:47"> And of cassia, five hundred shekels' weight measured by the scale of the holy place, and of olive oil a hin:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; 1 Chronicles 9:30; Exodus 40:9"> And make these into a holy oil, a perfume made by the art of the perfume-maker; it is to be a holy oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 7:1; 1 John 2:20; Isaiah 61:1"> This oil is to be put on the Tent of meeting, and on the ark of the law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And on the table and all its vessels, and on the support for the lights, with its vessels, and on the altar for burning spices,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And on the altar of burned offerings with its vessels, and on the washing-vessel and its base.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 23:19; Exodus 29:37; Leviticus 6:18"> And you are to make them most holy; anything touching them will become holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 8:12; Exodus 28:3; Leviticus 8:30"> And put the oil on Aaron and his sons, making them holy to do the work of priests to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; Leviticus 21:10; Psalms 89:20"> And say to the children of Israel, This is to be the Lord's holy oil, from generation to generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 30:25; Exodus 30:37–38; Leviticus 21:10"> It is not to be used for man's flesh, and no other is to be made like it: holy it is, and you are to keep it holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 30:38; Genesis 17:14; Exodus 12:15"> Whoever makes any like it, or puts it on one who is not a priest, will be cut off from his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; Exodus 25:6; Exodus 30:23"> And the Lord said to Moses, Take sweet spices, stacte and onycha and galbanum, with the best frankincense, in equal weights;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 2:13; Exodus 30:25; John 12:3"> And make from them a perfume, such as is made by the art of the perfume-maker, mixed with salt, and clean and holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 25:22; Exodus 30:6; Exodus 16:34"> And put some of it, crushed very small, in front of the ark in the Tent of meeting, where I will come face to face with you; it is to be most holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3; Exodus 29:37; Exodus 30:32–33"> You are not to make any perfume like it for yourselves: it is to be kept holy to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 30:33"> Whoever makes any like it, for its sweet smell, will be cut off from his people.</span>
