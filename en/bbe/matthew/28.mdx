<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 16:1–8; John 20:1–10; Matthew 27:56"> Now late on the Sabbath, when the dawn of the first day of the week was near, Mary Magdalene and the other Mary came to see the place where his body was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 16:26; John 20:12–13; Mark 16:3–5"> And there was a great earth-shock; for an angel of the Lord came down from heaven and, rolling back the stone, took his seat on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 9:3; Acts 1:10; Mark 16:5"> His form was shining like the light, and his clothing was white as snow:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Daniel 10:7; Matthew 27:65–66"> And for fear of him the watchmen were shaking, and became as dead men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 1:17–18; Hebrews 1:14; Isaiah 41:10"> And the angel said to the women, Have no fear: for I see that you are searching for Jesus, who was put to death on the cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 16:6; Matthew 12:40; Luke 24:6–8"> He is not here, for he has come to life again, even as he said. Come, see the Lord's resting-place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 26:32; Luke 24:22–24; Matthew 28:10"> And go quickly and give his disciples the news that he has come back from the dead, and is going before you into Galilee; there you will see him, as I have said to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 2:11; Mark 16:8; Luke 24:36–41"> And they went away quickly, with fear and great joy, to give his disciples the news.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 28:17; John 20:14–17; Matthew 14:33"> And on the way, Jesus came to them, saying, Be glad. And they came and put their hands on his feet, and gave him worship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 8:29; John 20:17; Matthew 28:7"> Then said Jesus to them, Have no fear: go and give word to my brothers to go into Galilee, and there they will see me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 27:65–66; Matthew 28:4"> Now, while they were going, some of the watchmen came into the town and gave news to the chief priests of all the things which had taken place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 26:3–4; Acts 4:5–22; Matthew 27:1–2"> And when they had come together with those in authority, and had made their decision, they gave much money to the watchmen, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 26:64"> Say, His disciples came by night and took him away secretly while we were sleeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 27:2; Acts 12:19"> And if this comes to the ruler's ears, we will see that he does not make you responsible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:10; Matthew 27:8; Matthew 26:15"> So they took the money, and did as they had been ordered: and this account has been current among the Jews till the present time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 26:32; Matthew 28:10; Matthew 28:7"> But the eleven disciples went into Galilee, to the mountain where Jesus had given them orders to go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 45:11; Psalms 2:12; John 5:23"> And when they saw him they gave him worship: but some were in doubt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 3:35; 1 Peter 3:22; Matthew 11:27"> And Jesus came to them and said, All authority has been given to me in heaven and on earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 16:15–16; Acts 2:38–39; Acts 1:8"> Go then, and make disciples of all the nations, giving them baptism in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Joshua 1:5; Acts 18:9–10"> Teaching them to keep all the rules which I have given you: and see, I am ever with you, even to the end of the world.</span>
