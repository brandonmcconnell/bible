<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 20:1–16; Matthew 12:43–45; Luke 14:16"> And Jesus, talking to them again in stories, said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 19:7–9; Matthew 13:24; Ephesians 5:24–32"> The kingdom of heaven is like a certain king, who made a feast when his son was married,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 22:17; John 5:40; Luke 14:15–17"> And sent out his servants to get in the guests to the feast: and they would not come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 13:46; Matthew 21:36; John 6:50–57"> Again he sent out other servants, with orders to say to the guests, See, I have made ready my feast: my oxen and my fat beasts have been put to death, and all things are ready: come to the feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 2:3; Luke 14:18–20; 1 John 2:15–16"> But they gave no attention, and went about their business, one to his farm, another to his trade:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 15:19–20; John 16:2–3; Acts 7:51–57"> And the rest put violent hands on his servants, and did evil to them, and put them to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 19:27; 1 Peter 4:17–18; Luke 21:24"> But the king was angry; and he sent his armies, and those who had put his servants to death he gave to destruction, burning down their town with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:5; Acts 13:46; Revelation 3:4"> Then he said to his servants, The feast is ready but the guests were not good enough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 14:21–24; Proverbs 1:20–23; Mark 16:15–16"> Go then to the cross-roads, and get all those whom you see to come to the bride-feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–11; Revelation 19:6–9; 2 Corinthians 12:21"> And those servants went out into the streets, and got together all those whom they came across, bad and good: and the feast was full of guests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 3:4–5; Revelation 19:8; Hebrews 4:12–13"> But when the king came in to see the guests, he saw there a man who had not on a guest's robe;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:9; Job 5:16; Matthew 20:13"> And he says to him, Friend, how came you in here not having a guest's robe? And he had nothing to say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Matthew 25:30; Matthew 24:51"> Then the king said to the servants, Put cords round his hands and feet and put him out into the dark; there will be weeping and cries of sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Peter 1:10; Luke 13:23–24; Matthew 7:13–14"> For out of all to whom the good news has come, only a small number will get salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 20:20–26; Mark 12:13–17; Psalms 2:2"> Then the Pharisees went and had a meeting to see how they might make use of his words to take him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 3:6; Mark 8:15; Mark 12:14"> And they sent to him their disciples, with the Herodians, saying, Master, we see that you are true, and that you are teaching the true way of God, and have no fear of anyone, because you have no respect for a man's position.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 2:1; Romans 13:6–7; Matthew 17:25"> Give us, then, your opinion of this: Is it right to give tax to Caesar, or not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 8:6; Luke 5:22; John 2:25"> But Jesus saw their trick and said, Oh false ones, why are you attempting to put me in the wrong?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 20:2; Revelation 6:6; Matthew 18:28"> Let me see the tax money. And they gave him a penny.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 20:24"> And he said to them, Whose is this image and name on it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 13:7; Proverbs 24:21; Acts 4:19"> They say to him, Caesar's. Then he said to them, Give to Caesar the things which are Caesar's, and to God the things which are God's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 12:12; Matthew 22:46; Luke 20:25–26"> And hearing it, they were full of wonder, and went away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 4:1; Mark 12:18–27; Acts 23:6–8"> On the same day there came to him the Sadducees, who say that there is no coming back from the dead: and they put a question to him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Luke 20:28; Ruth 1:11"> Master, Moses said, If a man, at the time of his death, has no children, let his brother take his wife, and get a family for his brother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 20:29–33; Mark 12:19–23; Hebrews 9:27"> Now there were among us seven brothers; and the first was married and at his death, having no seed, gave his wife to his brother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> In the same way the second and the third, up to the seventh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And last of all the woman came to her end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> When they come back from the dead, then, whose wife will she be of the seven? because they all had her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 20:9; Romans 15:4; Luke 1:37"> But Jesus said to them in answer, You are in error, not having knowledge of the Writings, or of the power of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 20:34–36; Mark 12:24–25; Matthew 24:38"> For when they come back from the dead there are no husbands and wives, but they are as the angels in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 12:3; Matthew 21:16; Matthew 21:42"> But about the dead coming back to life, have you no knowledge of what was said to you by God in the Writings:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 3:6; Mark 12:26–27; Luke 20:37–38"> I am the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob? God is not the God of the dead but of the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 12:17; Mark 6:2; John 7:46"> And the people hearing it were surprised at his teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 12:28–31; Matthew 25:3–5; Matthew 12:14"> But the Pharisees, hearing how the mouths of the Sadducees had been stopped, came together;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 7:30; Luke 10:25–37; Luke 11:52"> And one of them, a teacher of the law, put a question to him, testing him, and saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 12:28–33; Matthew 5:19–20; Luke 11:42"> Master, which is the chief rule in the law?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 12:29–30; Luke 10:27; Deuteronomy 6:5"> And he said to him, Have love for the Lord your God with all your heart, and with all your soul, and with all your mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> This is the first and greatest rule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Mark 12:31; Luke 10:27–37; Leviticus 19:18"> And a second like it is this, Have love for your neighbour as for yourself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Matthew 7:12; 1 John 4:19–21"> On these two rules all the law and the prophets are based.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 20:41–44; Mark 12:35–37; Matthew 22:34"> Now while the Pharisees were together, Jesus put a question to them, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5–6; John 6:68–69; Matthew 16:13–17"> What is your opinion of the Christ? whose son is he? They say to him, The Son of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:2; Revelation 4:2; Mark 12:36"> He says to them, How then does David in the Spirit give him the name of Lord, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Hebrews 1:13; Acts 2:34–35"> The Lord said to my Lord, Be seated at my right hand, till I put under your feet all those who are against you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Romans 1:3–4; Revelation 22:16; John 8:58"> If David then gives him the name of Lord, how is he his son?</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Mark 12:34; Luke 20:40; Luke 14:6"> And no one was able to give him an answer, and so great was their fear of him, that from that day no one put any more questions to him.</span>
