<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 23:5; John 12:43; Hebrews 6:10"> Take care not to do your good works before men, to be seen by them; or you will have no reward from your Father in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 6:5; Matthew 6:16; 2 Corinthians 9:6–15"> When then you give money to the poor, do not make a noise about it, as the false-hearted men do in the Synagogues and in the streets, so that they may have glory from men. Truly, I say to you, They have their reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 7:4; Matthew 9:30; Mark 1:44"> But when you give money, let not your left hand see what your right hand does:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 6:18; Matthew 6:6; Jeremiah 17:10"> So that your giving may be in secret; and your Father, who sees in secret, will give you your reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 18:10–11; Matthew 6:2; Luke 18:13"> And when you make your prayers, be not like the false-hearted men, who take pleasure in getting up and saying their prayers in the Synagogues and at the street turnings so that they may be seen by men. Truly I say to you, They have their reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 4:33; Matthew 14:23; Isaiah 65:24"> But when you make your prayer, go into your private room, and, shutting the door, say a prayer to your Father in secret, and your Father, who sees in secret, will give you your reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:2–3; Ecclesiastes 5:7; 1 Kings 18:26–29"> And in your prayer do not make use of the same words again and again, as the Gentiles do: for they have the idea that God will give attention to them because of the number of their words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 6:32; Luke 12:30; Philippians 4:6"> So be not like them; because your Father has knowledge of your needs even before you make your requests to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 11:1–4; Revelation 4:11; Romans 8:15"> Let this then be your prayer: Our Father in heaven, may your name be kept holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Matthew 26:42; Matthew 12:50"> Let your kingdom come. Let your pleasure be done, as in heaven, so on earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 30:8; Luke 11:3; Matthew 4:4"> Give us this day bread for our needs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Mark 11:25; Ephesians 1:7"> And make us free of our debts, as we have made those free who are in debt to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; Revelation 7:14–17; Matthew 26:41"> And let us not be put to the test, but keep us safe from the Evil One.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Mark 11:25; Colossians 3:13"> For if you let men have forgiveness for their sins, you will have forgiveness from your Father in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:35"> But if you do not let men have forgiveness for their sins, you will not have forgiveness from your Father for your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 58:3–5; Matthew 9:14–15; Daniel 9:3"> And when you go without food, be not sad-faced as the false-hearted are. For they go about with changed looks, so that men may see that they are going without food. Truly I say to you, They have their reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ruth 3:3; Ecclesiastes 9:8; 2 Samuel 12:20"> But when you go without food, put oil on your head and make your face clean;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 6:6; Matthew 6:4; 2 Corinthians 10:18"> So that no one may see that you are going without food, but your Father in secret; and your Father, who sees in secret, will give you your reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5; 1 John 2:15–16; Luke 12:21"> Make no store of wealth for yourselves on earth, where it may be turned to dust by worms and weather, and where thieves may come in by force and take it away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 12:33; Matthew 19:21; James 2:5"> But make a store for yourselves in heaven, where it will not be turned to dust and where thieves do not come in to take it away:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Colossians 3:1–3; Luke 12:34; 2 Corinthians 4:18"> For where your wealth is, there will your heart be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 11:34–36; 2 Corinthians 11:3; Colossians 3:22"> The light of the body is the eye; if then your eye is true, all your body will be full of light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 John 2:11; Matthew 6:22; Isaiah 5:20–21"> But if your eye is evil, all your body will be dark. If then the light which is in you is dark, how dark it will be!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; Luke 16:13; James 4:4"> No man is able to be a servant to two masters: for he will have hate for the one and love for the other, or he will keep to one and have no respect for the other. You may not be servants of God and of wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Philippians 4:6; 1 Peter 5:7; Romans 8:32"> So I say to you, Take no thought for your life, about food or drink, or about clothing for your body. Is not life more than food, and the body more than its clothing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 10:29–31; Psalms 147:9; Luke 12:24–32"> See the birds of heaven; they do not put seeds in the earth, they do not get in grain, or put it in store-houses; and your Father in heaven gives them food. Are you not of much more value than they?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 12:25–26; Ecclesiastes 3:14; Psalms 39:5–6"> And which of you by taking thought is able to make himself a cubit taller?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 6:25; Philippians 4:6; Luke 12:27"> And why are you troubled about clothing? See the flowers of the field, how they come up; they do no work, they make no thread:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:20–22; 2 Chronicles 9:4–6; 1 Peter 3:2–5"> But I say to you that even Solomon in all his glory was not clothed like one of these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 14:31; Luke 12:28; 1 Peter 1:24"> But if God gives such clothing to the grass of the field, which is here today and tomorrow is put into the oven, will he not much more give you clothing, O you of little faith?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Peter 5:7; Luke 12:29; Matthew 4:4"> Then do not be full of care, saying, What are we to have for food or drink? or, With what may we be clothed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 6:8; Luke 12:30; Ephesians 4:17"> Because the Gentiles go in search of all these things: for your Father in heaven has knowledge that you have need of all these things:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 12:31; Matthew 5:6; John 6:27"> But let your first care be for his kingdom and his righteousness; and all these other things will be given to you in addition.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 6:25; John 14:27; Hebrews 13:5–6"> Then have no care for tomorrow: tomorrow will take care of itself. Take the trouble of the day as it comes.</span>
