<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 12:35–36; Philippians 2:15–16; Matthew 24:42–51"> Then the kingdom of heaven will be like ten virgins, the friends of the bride, who took their lights, and went out with the purpose of meeting the husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:2; 1 Corinthians 10:1–5; Matthew 13:19–23"> And five of them were foolish, and five were wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 3:15–16; 2 Timothy 3:5; Isaiah 48:1–2"> For the foolish, when they took their lights, took no oil with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 8:9; 1 John 2:27; 1 John 2:20"> But the wise took oil in their vessels with their lights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:6–8; 2 Peter 3:4–9"> Now the husband was a long time in coming, and they all went to sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Matthew 24:31; Matthew 24:44"> But in the middle of the night there is a cry, The husband comes! Go out to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 12:35; Revelation 3:19–20; 2 Peter 3:14"> Then all those virgins got up, and made ready their lights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 13:9; Luke 12:35; Hebrews 4:1"> And the foolish said to the wise, Give us of your oil; for our lights are going out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 49:7–9; Revelation 3:17–18; Isaiah 55:6–7"> But the wise made answer, saying, There may not be enough for us and you; it would be better for you to go to the traders and get oil for yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 13:25; Matthew 24:44; Luke 12:36–37"> And while they went to get oil, the master came; and those who were ready went in with him to the feast: and the door was shut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 7:21–23; Hebrews 12:16–17"> After that the other virgins came, saying, Lord, Lord, let us in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:3; 2 Timothy 2:19; John 10:27"> But he made answer and said, Truly I say to you, I have no knowledge of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 24:42–44; Revelation 16:15; 1 Thessalonians 5:6"> Keep watch, then, because you are not certain of the day or of the hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 13:34; Luke 19:12–27; 1 Peter 4:9–11"> For it is as when a man, about to take a journey, got his servants together, and gave them his property.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:24; Luke 19:13–14; Luke 12:48"> And to one he gave five talents, to another two, to another one; to everyone as he was able; and he went on his journey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="3 John 1:5–8; 1 Timothy 6:17–18; 2 Timothy 2:6"> Straight away he who had been given the five talents went and did trade with them, and made five more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 3:9–10; Ephesians 5:16; 1 Peter 4:10"> In the same way he who had been given the two got two more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Peter 1:8; Haggai 1:2–4; Malachi 1:10"> But he who was given the one went away and put it in a hole in the earth, and kept his lord's money in a secret place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:12–15; 2 Corinthians 5:10; Romans 14:7–12"> Now after a long time the lord of those servants comes, and makes up his account with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 20:24; Luke 19:16–17; Colossians 1:29"> And he who had the five talents came with his other five talents, saying, Lord, you gave into my care five talents: see, I have got five more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 25:23; Luke 16:10; John 12:26"> His lord said to him, Well done, good and true servant: you have been true in a small thing, I will give you control over great things: take your part in the joy of your lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; 2 Corinthians 8:7–8; 2 Corinthians 8:1–3"> And he who had the two talents came and said, Lord, you gave into my care two talents: see, I have got two more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Mark 14:8–9; Mark 12:41–44"> His lord said to him, Well done, good and true servant: you have been true in a small thing, I will give you control over great things: take your part in the joy of your lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 19:20–22; Luke 15:29; Malachi 3:14–15"> And he who had had the one talent came and said, Lord, I had knowledge that you are a hard man, getting in grain where you have not put seed, and making profits for which you have done no work:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:6–7; Romans 8:15; Revelation 21:8"> And I was in fear, and went away, and put your talent in the earth: here is what is yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 18:32; Job 15:5–6"> But his lord in answer said to him, You are a bad and unready servant; if you had knowledge that I get in grain where I did not put seed, and make profits for which I have done no work,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:19–20; Romans 3:19; Jude 1:15"> Why, then, did you not put my money in the bank, and at my coming I would have got back what is mine with interest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 19:24; Luke 10:42"> Take away, then, his talent and give it to him who has the ten talents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 8:18; Mark 4:25; Matthew 13:12"> For to everyone who has will be given, and he will have more: but from him who has not, even what he has will be taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Revelation 3:15–16; Matthew 13:50"> And put out the servant who is of no profit into the outer dark: there will be weeping and cries of sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:7–8; Matthew 19:28"> But when the Son of man comes in his glory, and all the angels with him, then will he be seated in his glory:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Malachi 3:18; Matthew 13:49; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> And before him all the nations will come together; and they will be parted one from another, as the sheep are parted from the goats by the keeper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 100:3; John 10:26–28; John 21:15–17"> And he will put the sheep on his right, but the goats on the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Peter 1:19–20; James 2:5; Revelation 21:7"> Then will the King say to those on his right, Come, you who have the blessing of my Father, into the kingdom made ready for you before the world was:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 3:16–19; James 2:15–16; Deuteronomy 15:7–11"> For I was in need of food, and you gave it to me: I was in need of drink, and you gave it to me: I was wandering, and you took me in;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="James 1:27; 2 Timothy 1:16–18; James 5:14–15"> I had no clothing, and you gave it to me: when I was ill, or in prison, you came to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 6:3; 1 Chronicles 29:14; 1 Peter 5:5–6"> Then will the upright make answer to him, saying, Lord, when did we see you in need of food, and give it to you? or in need of drink, and give it to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And when did we see you wandering, and take you in? or without clothing, and give it to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> And when did we see you ill, or in prison, and come to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Proverbs 14:31; Matthew 10:42"> And the King will make answer and say to them, Truly I say to you, Because you did it to the least of these my brothers, you did it to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Jude 1:6–7; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> Then will he say to those on the left, Go from me, you cursed ones, into the eternal fire which is ready for the Evil One and his angels:</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="James 2:15–24; John 5:23; Matthew 12:30"> For I was in need of food, and you gave it not to me; I was in need of drink, and you gave it not to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> I was wandering, and you took me not in; without clothing, and you gave me no clothing; ill, and in prison, and you came not to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 7:22; Luke 10:29; Matthew 25:24–27"> Then will they make answer, saying, Lord, when did we see you in need of food or drink, or wandering, or without clothing, or ill, or in prison, and did not take care of you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Proverbs 21:13; Proverbs 17:5; Proverbs 14:31"> Then will he make answer to them, saying, Truly I say to you, Because you did it not to the least of these, you did it not to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; John 3:36; John 5:29"> And these will go away into eternal punishment; but the upright into eternal life.</span>
