<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 4:13; Luke 8:37; Matthew 8:18"> And he got into a boat and went across and came to his town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 2:1–12; Luke 5:18–26; Jeremiah 31:33–34"> And they took to him a man stretched on a bed who had no power of moving; and Jesus, seeing their faith, said to the man who was ill, Son, take heart; you have forgiveness for your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 26:65; Luke 5:21; Leviticus 24:16"> And some of the scribes said among themselves, This man has no respect for God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 6:8; Matthew 12:25; Revelation 2:23"> And Jesus, having knowledge of what was in their minds, said, Why are your thoughts evil?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 5:8–14; Mark 2:9–12; Acts 3:16"> For which is the simpler, to say, You have forgiveness for your sins; or to say, Get up and go?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Matthew 9:5; John 20:21–23"> But so that you may see that on earth the Son of man has authority for the forgiveness of sins, (then said he to the man who was ill,) Get up, and take up your bed, and go to your house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And he got up and went away to his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 7:16; Matthew 15:31; Acts 4:21"> But when the people saw it they were full of fear, and gave glory to God who had given such authority to men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 5:27–38; Mark 2:14–22; Luke 19:2–10"> And when Jesus was going from there, he saw a man whose name was Matthew, seated at the place where taxes were taken; and he said to him, Come after me. And he got up and went after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13–16; Luke 5:29–32; Mark 2:15–17"> And it came about, when he was in the house taking food, that a number of tax-farmers and sinners came and took their places with Jesus and his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 11:19; Luke 5:30; Mark 2:16"> And when the Pharisees saw it, they said to his disciples, Why does your Master take food with tax-farmers and sinners?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 5:31; Revelation 3:17–18; Mark 2:17"> But on hearing this he said, Those who are well have no need of a medical man, but those who are ill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 2:17; Luke 19:10; Hosea 6:6"> But go and take to heart the sense of these words, My desire is for mercy, not offerings: for I have come not to get the upright, but sinners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 5:33–39; Mark 2:18–22; Matthew 6:16"> Then the disciples of John came to him, saying, Why do we and the Pharisees frequently go without food, but your disciples do not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 19:9; John 3:29; Revelation 21:2"> And Jesus said to them, Will the friends of the newly-married man be sad as long as he is with them? But the days will come when he will be taken away from them, and then will they go without food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:1–2; John 16:12; Psalms 125:3"> And no man puts a bit of new cloth on an old coat, for by pulling away from the old, it makes a worse hole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 9:4; Job 32:19; Psalms 119:83"> And men do not put new wine into old wine-skins; or the skins will be burst and the wine will come out, and the skins are of no more use: but they put new wine into new wine-skins, and so the two will be safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 8:41–56; Mark 5:22–43; Matthew 8:2"> While he was saying these things to them, there came a ruler and gave him worship, saying, My daughter is even now dead; but come and put your hand on her, and she will come back to life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 10:38; Galatians 6:9–10; Matthew 8:7"> And Jesus got up and went after him, and so did his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 8:43–56; Matthew 14:36; Mark 6:56"> And a woman, who for twelve years had had a flow of blood, came after him, and put her hand on the edge of his robe:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 5:26–33; Luke 8:45–47; Acts 19:12"> Because, she said to herself, if I may but put my hand on his robe, I will be made well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 7:50; Matthew 15:28; Matthew 9:2"> But Jesus, turning and seeing her, said, Daughter, take heart; your faith has made you well. And the woman was made well from that hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:25; Mark 5:35–40; Luke 8:49–51"> And when Jesus came into the ruler's house and saw the players with their instruments and the people making a noise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 20:10; John 11:11–13; Matthew 27:39–43"> He said, Make room; for the girl is not dead, but sleeping. And they were laughing at him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 9:27; Mark 5:41; Luke 8:54"> But when the people were sent out, he went in and took her by the hand; and the girl got up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Matthew 14:1–2; Mark 1:45"> And the news of it went out into all that land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Matthew 20:30–31; Mark 10:46–48"> And when Jesus went on from there, two blind men came after him, crying out, Have mercy on us, you Son of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 9:23–24; Matthew 13:58; Matthew 8:2"> And when he had come into the house, the blind men came to him; and Jesus said to them, Have you faith that I am able to do this? They said to him, Yes, Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 15:28; Mark 10:52; Matthew 8:13"> Then he put his hand on their eyes, saying, As your faith is, let it be done to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Luke 5:14; Matthew 17:9"> And their eyes were made open. And Jesus said to them sharply, Let no man have knowledge of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 7:36; Matthew 9:26; Mark 1:44–45"> But they went out and gave news of him in all that land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Matthew 12:22–24; Luke 11:14"> And while they were going away, there came to him a man without the power of talking, and with an evil spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 35:6; Mark 7:32–37; Exodus 4:11–12"> And when the evil spirit had been sent out, the man had the power of talking: and they were all surprised, saying, Such a thing has never been seen in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 11:15; Mark 3:22; Matthew 12:23–24"> But the Pharisees said, By the ruler of evil spirits, he sends evil spirits out of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 4:23–24; Acts 10:38; Mark 6:56"> And Jesus went about all the towns and small places, teaching in their Synagogues and preaching the good news of the kingdom and making well all sorts of disease and pain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 6:34; Numbers 27:17; Jeremiah 50:6"> But when he saw all the people he was moved with pity for them, because they were troubled and wandering like sheep without a keeper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 10:2; Mark 16:15; 1 Corinthians 3:9"> Then he said to his disciples, There is much grain but not enough men to get it in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 10:1–2; Jeremiah 3:15; 2 Thessalonians 3:1"> Make prayer, then, to the Lord of the grain-fields, that he may send out workers to get in his grain.</span>
