<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 13:1–37; Luke 21:5–36; Ezekiel 8:6"> And Jesus went out of the Temple, and on the way his disciples came to him, pointing out the buildings of the Temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 19:44; Jeremiah 26:18; Micah 3:12"> But he, answering, said to them, See you not all these things? truly I say to you that here there will not be one stone resting on another, which will not be pulled down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 13:3–4; Acts 1:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:1–11"> And while he was seated on the Mountain of Olives, the disciples came to him privately, saying, Make clear to us, when will these things be? and what will be the sign of your coming and of the end of the world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 4:1; Jeremiah 29:8; 2 Thessalonians 2:3"> And Jesus said to them in answer, Take care that you are not tricked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 24:11; Matthew 24:24; Jeremiah 14:14"> For people will come in my name, saying, I am the Christ; and a number will be turned from the true way through them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 13:7–8; Luke 21:9; 1 Peter 3:14–15"> And news will come to you of wars and talk of wars: do not be troubled, for these things have to be; but it is still not the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 21:11; Revelation 6:8; Isaiah 19:2"> For nation will be moved against nation, and kingdom against kingdom, and men will be without food, and the earth will be shaking in different places;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; 1 Peter 4:17–18; Deuteronomy 28:59"> But all these things are the first of the troubles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 16:2; John 15:19–20; Luke 21:16–17"> Then they will be cruel to you, and will put you to death: and you will be hated by all nations because of my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 7:5–6; Luke 21:16; Matthew 11:6"> And numbers of people will be turned from the right way, and will give one another up and have hate for one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 24:24; Matthew 7:15; Matthew 24:5"> And a number of false prophets will come, causing error.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 2:4–5; James 4:1–4; Revelation 3:15"> And because wrongdoing will be increased, the love of most people will become cold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 10:22; Mark 13:13; Revelation 2:10"> But he who goes through to the end will get salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 14:6; Romans 10:18; Romans 16:25–26"> And this good news of the kingdom will be given through all the world for a witness to all nations; and then the end will come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 9:27; Daniel 12:11; Mark 13:14"> When, then, you see in the holy place the unclean thing which makes destruction, of which word was given by Daniel the prophet (let this be clear to the reader),</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 19:15–17; Luke 21:21–22; Hebrews 11:7"> Then let those who are in Judaea go in flight to the mountains:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 17:31–33; Mark 13:15–16; Matthew 10:27"> Let not him who is on the house-top go down to take anything out of his house:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And let not him who is in the field go back to get his coat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 23:29–30; Deuteronomy 28:53–56; Luke 21:23"> But it will be hard for women who are with child and for those with babies at the breast in those days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 16:29; Acts 1:12"> And say a prayer that your flight may not be in the winter, or on a Sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 12:1; Joel 2:2; Malachi 4:1"> Because in those days there will be great sorrow, such as there has not been from the start of the world till now, or ever will be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 13:20; 2 Timothy 2:10; Matthew 24:31"> And if those days had not been made short there would have been no salvation for any, but because of the saints those days will be made short.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 21:8; Luke 17:23–24; Deuteronomy 13:1–3"> Then if any man says to you, See, here is the Christ, or, Here; do not put faith in him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:9–11; Mark 13:22; Revelation 13:13–14"> For there will come up false Christs, and false prophets, who will do great signs and wonders; so that if possible even the saints might be tricked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 16:1; Luke 21:13; Isaiah 48:5–6"> See, I have made it clear to you before it comes about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 3:2–3; Isaiah 40:3; Matthew 3:1"> If, then, they say to you, See, he is in the waste land; go not out: See, he is in the inner rooms; put no faith in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 17:24–37; Zechariah 9:14; Job 37:3"> Because as in a thunderstorm the bright light coming from the east is seen even in the west; so will be the coming of the Son of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 17:37; Job 39:27–30; Deuteronomy 28:49"> Wherever the dead body is, there will the eagles come together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Amos 8:9; Joel 3:15; Isaiah 13:10"> But straight away, after the trouble of those days, the sun will be made dark and the moon will not give her light and the stars will come down from heaven and the powers of heaven will be moved:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; Daniel 7:13; Luke 21:27"> And then the sign of the Son of man will be seen in heaven: and then all the nations of the earth will have sorrow, and they will see the Son of man coming on the clouds of heaven with power and great glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:16; Mark 13:27; Isaiah 27:13"> And he will send out his angels with a great sound of a horn, and they will get his saints together from the four winds, from one end of heaven to the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 13:28–29; Luke 21:29–30"> Now take an example from the fig-tree: when her branch has become soft and puts out its leaves, you are certain that the summer is near;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="James 5:9; Hebrews 10:37; Ezekiel 7:2–14"> Even so, when you see all these things, you may be certain that he is near, even at the doors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 13:30–31; Luke 21:32–33; Matthew 16:28"> Truly I say to you, This generation will not come to an end till all these things are complete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 40:8; 1 Peter 1:25; Numbers 23:19"> Heaven and earth will come to an end, but my words will not come to an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 13:32; Acts 1:7; Matthew 24:44"> But of that day and hour no one has knowledge, not even the angels in heaven, or the Son, but the Father only.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 17:26–27; Genesis 6:1–7; 2 Peter 2:5"> And as were the days of Noah, so will be the coming of the Son of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 17:26–28; Romans 13:13–14; Luke 14:18–20"> Because as in those days before the overflowing of the waters, they were feasting and taking wives and getting married, till the day when Noah went into the ark,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 3:20; Matthew 24:37; Acts 13:41"> And they had no care till the waters came and took them all away; so will be the coming of the Son of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 17:34–37; 2 Peter 2:7–9; 2 Peter 2:5"> Then two men will be in the field; one is taken, and one let go;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 11:5; Isaiah 47:2; Luke 17:35"> Two women will be crushing grain; one is taken, and one let go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 21:36; 1 Thessalonians 5:6; Matthew 25:13"> Be watching, then! for you have no knowledge on what day your Lord will come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 12:39; Matthew 24:44; 1 Thessalonians 5:2–6"> But be certain of this, that if the master of the house had had knowledge of the time when the thief was coming, he would have been watching, and would not have let his house be broken into.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 25:10; Matthew 24:42; Matthew 25:13"> Be ready then; for at a time which you have no thought of the Son of man will come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Matthew 25:23; 1 Corinthians 4:1–2"> Who is the true and wise servant, whom his lord has put over those in his house, to give them their food at the right time?</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Revelation 16:15; Luke 12:43; Luke 12:37"> A blessing on that servant, who will be doing so when his lord comes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 25:23; Matthew 25:21; Luke 12:37"> Truly, I say to you, he will put him over all he has.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Matthew 18:32; Isaiah 32:6; Luke 12:45"> But if that evil servant says in his heart, My lord is a long time in coming;</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Revelation 17:6; Revelation 16:6; Micah 3:5"> And is cruel to the other servants, taking his pleasure with those who are overcome with wine;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2–3; Revelation 3:3; Matthew 24:42–44"> The lord of that servant will come in a day when he is not looking for him, and in an hour of which he has no knowledge,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Matthew 22:13; Luke 12:46"> And will have him cut in two, and will give him a part in the fate of the false ones: there will be weeping and cries of sorrow.</span>
