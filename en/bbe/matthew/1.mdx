<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 22:18; Romans 1:3; Galatians 3:16"> The book of the generations of Jesus Christ, the son of David, the son of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 7:8; Hebrews 11:17–18; Genesis 46:8–27"> The son of Abraham was Isaac; and the son of Isaac was Jacob; and the sons of Jacob were Judah and his brothers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Genesis 38:29–30; 1 Chronicles 4:1"> And the sons of Judah were Perez and Zerah by Tamar; and the son of Perez was Hezron; and the son of Hezron was Ram;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:10–12; Numbers 7:12; Numbers 2:3"> And the son of Ram was Amminadab; and the son of Amminadab was Nahshon; and the son of Nahshon was Salmon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:31; Ruth 1:16–17; James 2:25"> And the son of Salmon by Rahab was Boaz; and the son of Boaz by Ruth was Obed; and the son of Obed was Jesse;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 15:5; 1 Samuel 17:12; 2 Samuel 12:24–25"> And the son of Jesse was David the king; and the son of David was Solomon by her who had been the wife of Uriah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:1–15; 1 Kings 14:31; 1 Kings 11:43–24"> And the son of Solomon was Rehoboam; and the son of Rehoboam was Abijah; and the son of Abijah was Asa;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:11; 2 Chronicles 26:1–23; 2 Kings 8:16"> And the son of Asa was Jehoshaphat; and the son of Jehoshaphat was Joram; and the son of Joram was Uzziah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 7:1–13; 2 Chronicles 26:21; 2 Chronicles 27:1–32"> And the son of Uzziah was Jotham; and the son of Jotham was Ahaz; and the son of Ahaz was Hezekiah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:33–27; 1 Kings 13:2; 1 Chronicles 3:13–15"> And the son of Hezekiah was Manasseh; and the son of Manasseh was Amon; and the son of Amon was Josiah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:20; Jeremiah 52:28–30; Daniel 1:2"> And the sons of Josiah were Jechoniah and his brothers, at the time of the taking away to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:17; Ezra 3:2; Luke 3:27"> And after the taking away to Babylon, Jechoniah had a son Shealtiel; and Shealtiel had Zerubbabel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And Zerubbabel had Abiud; and Abiud had Eliakim; and Eliakim had Azor;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And Azor had Zadok; and Zadok had Achim; and Achim had Eliud;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Eliud had Eleazar; and Eleazar had Matthan; and Matthan had Jacob;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:17; John 4:25; Luke 3:23"> And the son of Jacob was Joseph the husband of Mary, who gave birth to Jesus, whose name is Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 1:11–12; 2 Kings 24:14; Matthew 11:2"> So all the generations from Abraham to David are fourteen generations; and from David to the taking away to Babylon, fourteen generations; and from the taking away to Babylon to the coming of Christ, fourteen generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 4:4–5; Luke 1:27–38; Job 14:4"> Now the birth of Jesus Christ was in this way: when his mother Mary was going to be married to Joseph, before they came together the discovery was made that she was with child by the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:21–24; Deuteronomy 24:1–4; John 8:4–5"> And Joseph, her husband, being an upright man, and not desiring to make her a public example, had a mind to put her away privately.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Luke 1:19; Matthew 1:18"> But when he was giving thought to these things, an angel of the Lord came to him in a dream, saying, Joseph, son of David, have no fear of taking Mary as your wife; because that which is in her body is of the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 13:23; Acts 4:12; John 1:29"> And she will give birth to a son; and you will give him the name Jesus; for he will give his people salvation from their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 24:44; Matthew 2:23; 1 Kings 8:15"> Now all this took place so that the word of the Lord by the prophet might come true,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Isaiah 9:6–7; Psalms 46:11"> See, the virgin will be with child, and will give birth to a son, and they will give him the name Immanuel, that is, God with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 15:14; James 2:21–26; John 2:5–8"> And Joseph did as the angel of the Lord had said to him, and took her as his wife;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 2:21; Exodus 13:2; Matthew 1:21"> And he had no connection with her till she had given birth to a son; and he gave him the name Jesus.</span>
