<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Mark 8:15; Luke 9:7–9"> At that time news of Jesus came to Herod the king;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 16:14; John 10:41; Mark 8:28"> And he said to his servants, This is John the Baptist; he has come back from the dead, and so these powers are working in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 6:17; Luke 3:19–20; Matthew 4:12"> For Herod had taken John and put him in prison because of Herodias, his brother Philip's wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 20:21; Leviticus 18:16; Mark 6:18"> Because John had said to him, It is not right for you to have her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 21:26; Matthew 11:9; Mark 6:19–20"> And he would have put him to death, but for his fear of the people, because in their eyes John was a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 6:21–23; Genesis 40:20; Hosea 1:5–6"> But when Herod's birthday came, the daughter of Herodias was dancing before them, and Herod was pleased with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esther 5:3; Esther 5:6; Esther 7:2"> So he gave her his word with an oath to let her have whatever she might make request for.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 6:24; Proverbs 29:10; Proverbs 1:16"> And she, at her mother's suggestion, said, Give me here on a plate the head of John the Baptist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 19:12–16; 1 Samuel 14:28; Mark 6:26"> And the king was sad; but because of his oaths and because of his guests, he gave the order for it to be given to her;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 17:12; Matthew 21:35–36; Luke 9:9"> And he sent and had John's head cut off in the prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 16:6; Genesis 49:7; Ezekiel 16:3–4"> And his head was put on a plate and given to the girl; and she took it to her mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 8:2; Matthew 27:58–61"> And his disciples came, and took up his body and put it in the earth; and they went and gave Jesus news of what had taken place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:10–17; Matthew 14:1–2; Mark 6:30–44"> Now when it came to the ears of Jesus, he went away from there in a boat, to a waste place by himself: and the people hearing of it, went after him on foot from the towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; Matthew 9:36; Hebrews 2:17"> And he came out and saw a great number of people and he had pity on them, and made well those of them who were ill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 15:23; Mark 8:3; Mark 6:35–36"> And when evening had come, the disciples came to him, saying, This place is waste land, and the time is now past; send the people away so that they may go into the towns and get themselves food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42–44; 2 Corinthians 8:2–3; Ecclesiastes 11:2"> But Jesus said to them, There is no need for them to go away; give them food yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 15:33–34; Mark 6:37–38; Luke 9:13"> And they say to him, We have here but five cakes of bread and two fishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And he said, Give them to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 8:6–7; Luke 24:30; 1 Samuel 9:13"> And he gave orders for the people to be seated on the grass; and he took the five cakes of bread and the two fishes and, looking up to heaven, he said words of blessing, and made division of the food, and gave it to the disciples, and the disciples gave it to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 16:12; Mark 6:42–44; Matthew 16:8–10"> And they all took of the food and had enough: and they took up twelve baskets full of broken bits which were not used.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 4:19; 2 Corinthians 9:8–11; Acts 4:4"> And those who had food were about five thousand men, in addition to women and children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 6:45–51; John 6:15–21; Matthew 15:39"> And straight away he made the disciples get into the boat and go before him to the other side, till he had sent the people away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Mark 6:46; Matthew 26:36"> And after he had sent the people away, he went up into the mountain by himself for prayer: and when evening was come, he was there by himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:18; Mark 6:48; Matthew 8:24"> But the boat was now in the middle of the sea, and was troubled by the waves: for the wind was against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 9:8; John 6:19; Mark 6:48"> And in the fourth watch of the night he came to them, walking on the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 6:49–50; Revelation 1:17; Luke 1:11–12"> And when they saw him walking on the sea, they were troubled, saying, It is a spirit; and they gave cries of fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 23:11; Revelation 1:17–18; John 16:33"> But straight away Jesus said to them, Take heart; it is I, have no fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 6:68; Romans 12:3; Matthew 26:33–35"> And Peter, answering, said to him, Lord, if it is you, give me the order to come to you on the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Mark 9:23; Acts 3:16"> And he said, Come. And Peter got out of the boat, and walking on the water, went to Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Lamentations 3:54–57; 2 Timothy 4:16–17; Mark 14:38"> But when he saw the wind he was in fear and, starting to go down, he gave a cry, saying, Help, Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 1:6–8; Psalms 138:7; Genesis 22:14"> And straight away Jesus put out his hand and took a grip of him, and said to him, O man of little faith, why were you in doubt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 107:29–30; Mark 4:41; John 6:21"> And when they had got into the boat, the wind went down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 11:27; Psalms 2:7; Romans 1:4"> And those who were in the boat gave him worship, saying, Truly you are the Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 6:53–56; Luke 5:1; John 6:24–25"> And when they had gone across, they came to land at Gennesaret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 4:24–25; Mark 6:55; Mark 1:28–34"> And when the men of that place had news of him, they sent into all the country round about, and took to him all who were ill,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 6:19; Mark 3:10; Exodus 28:33–43"> With the request that they might only put their hands on the edge of his robe: and all those who did so were made well.</span>
