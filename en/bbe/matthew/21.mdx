<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zechariah 14:4; Mark 11:1–10; Luke 21:37"> And when they were near Jerusalem, and had come to Beth-phage, to the Mountain of Olives, Jesus sent two disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 19:30–32; Mark 11:2–3; John 2:5–8"> Saying to them, Go into the little town in front of you, and straight away you will see an ass with a cord round her neck, and a young one with her; let them loose and come with them to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:1–2; Acts 17:25; 2 Corinthians 8:9"> And if anyone says anything to you, you will say, The Lord has need of them; and straight away he will send them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; John 12:15; Matthew 1:22"> Now this took place so that these words of the prophet might come true,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9–10; Isaiah 62:11; Isaiah 12:6"> Say to the daughter of Zion, See, your King comes to you, gentle and seated on an ass, and on a young ass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 39:43; John 15:14; Exodus 40:16"> And the disciples went and did as Jesus had given them orders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 9:13; Mark 11:4–8; Luke 19:32–35"> And got the ass and the young one, and put their clothing on them, and he took his seat on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 9:13; John 12:13; Leviticus 23:40"> And all the people put their clothing down in the way; and others got branches from the trees, and put them down in the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 118:24–26; Luke 2:14; John 12:13–15"> And those who went before him, and those who came after, gave loud cries, saying, Glory to the Son of David: A blessing on him who comes in the name of the Lord: Glory in the highest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 9:5; Ruth 1:19; John 12:16–19"> And when he came into Jerusalem, all the town was moved, saying, Who is this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 6:14; Luke 7:16; John 7:40"> And the people said, This is the prophet Jesus, from Nazareth of Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 2:14–17; Luke 19:45–47; Mark 11:15–18"> And Jesus went into the Temple and sent out all who were trading there, overturning the tables of the money-changers and the seats of those trading in doves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:11; Isaiah 56:7; Luke 19:46"> And he said to them, It is in the Writings, My house is to be named a house of prayer, but you are making it a hole of thieves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 3:1–9; Acts 10:38; Matthew 11:4–5"> And the blind and the broken in body came to him in the Temple, and he made them well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 21:9; Luke 19:39–40; Isaiah 26:11"> But when the chief priests and the scribes saw the works of power which he did, and the children crying out in the Temple, Glory to the son of David, they were angry and said to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 8:2; Matthew 11:25; Luke 19:39–40"> Have you any idea what these are saying? And Jesus said to them, Yes: have you not seen in the Writings, From the lips of children and babies at the breast you have made your praise complete?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 11:18; Mark 11:11; Luke 24:50"> And he went away from them, and went out of the town to Bethany, and was there for the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 4:2; Hebrews 4:15; Matthew 12:1"> Now in the morning when he was coming back to the town, he had a desire for food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 15:6; Mark 11:14; Titus 1:16"> And seeing a fig-tree by the wayside, he came to it, and saw nothing on it but leaves only; and he said to it, Let there be no fruit from you from this time forward for ever. And straight away the fig-tree became dry and dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 40:6–8; Mark 11:20–21; James 1:10–11"> And when the disciples saw it they were surprised, saying, How did the fig-tree become dry in so short a time?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="James 1:6; Matthew 17:20; Mark 11:22–23"> And Jesus in answer said to them, Truly I say to you, If you have faith, without doubting, not only may you do what has been done to the fig-tree, but even if you say to this mountain, Be taken up and put into the sea, it will be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 7:7; 1 John 5:14–15; Mark 11:24"> And all things, whatever you make request for in prayer, having faith, you will get.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 4:7; Mark 11:27–33; 1 Chronicles 24:1–19"> And when he had come into the Temple, the chief priests and those in authority over the people came to him while he was teaching, and said, By what authority do you do these things? and who gave you this authority?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 6:9; Colossians 4:6; Matthew 10:16"> And Jesus said to them in answer, I will put one question to you, and if you give me the answer, I will say by what authority I do these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 John 3:20; Mark 1:1–11; John 3:18"> The baptism of John, where did it come from? from heaven or from men? And they were reasoning among themselves, saying, If we say, From heaven; he will say to us, Why then did you not have faith in him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 6:20; Matthew 14:5; John 5:35"> But if we say, From men; we are in fear of the people, because all take John to be a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 1:18–22; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–10; Luke 20:7–8"> And they made answer and said, We have no idea. Then he said to them, And I will not say to you by what authority I do these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; Matthew 20:1; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> But how does it seem to you? A man had two sons; and he came to the first, and said, Son, go and do work today in the vine-garden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:28–32; Matthew 3:2–8; Isaiah 55:6–7"> And he said in answer, I will not: but later, changing his decision, he went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Titus 1:16; Ezekiel 33:31; Matthew 23:3"> And he came to the second and said the same. And he made answer and said, I go, sir: and went not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 7:29; Luke 7:37–50; Matthew 7:21"> Which of the two did his father's pleasure? They say, The first. Jesus said to them, Truly I say to you, that tax-farmers and loose women are going into the kingdom of God before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 7:37–50; Zechariah 7:11–12; Luke 7:29–30"> For John came to you in the way of righteousness, and you had no faith in him, but the tax-farmers and the loose women had faith in him: and you, when you saw it, did not even have regret for your sins, so as to have faith in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 5:1–4; Mark 12:1–12; Luke 20:9–19"> Give ear to another story. A master of a house made a vine garden, and put a wall round it, and made a place for crushing out the wine, and made a tower, and let it out to field-workers, and went into another country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 20:10–19; Isaiah 5:4; Zechariah 1:3–6"> And when the time for the fruit came near, he sent his servants to the workmen, to get the fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Matthew 5:12; Hebrews 11:36–37"> And the workmen made an attack on his servants, giving blows to one, putting another to death, and stoning another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 22:4"> Again, he sent other servants more in number than the first: and they did the same to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 3:16; John 3:35–36; Zephaniah 3:7"> But after that he sent his son to them, saying, They will have respect for my son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 20:14; Genesis 37:18–20; Mark 12:7–8"> But when the workmen saw the son, they said among themselves, This is he who will one day be the owner of the property; come, let us put him to death and take his heritage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 2:23; James 5:6; Luke 22:52–54"> And they took him and, driving him out of the vine-garden, put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Hebrews 10:29; Mark 12:9; Luke 20:15–16"> When, then, the lord of the vine-garden comes, what will he do to those workmen?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Acts 18:6; Matthew 21:43; Acts 28:28"> They say to him, He will put those cruel men to a cruel death, and will let out the vine-garden to other workmen, who will give him the fruit when it is ready.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; Isaiah 28:16; Acts 4:11"> Jesus says to them, Did you never see in the Writings, The stone which the builders put on one side, the same has been made the chief stone of the building: this was the Lord's doing, and it is a wonder in our eyes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 21:41; Matthew 8:11–12; 1 Peter 2:9"> For this reason I say to you, The kingdom of God will be taken away from you, and will be given to a nation producing the fruits of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14–15; 1 Peter 2:8; Daniel 2:34–35"> Any man falling on this stone will be broken, but he on whom it comes down will be crushed to dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Luke 20:19; Luke 11:45; Matthew 12:12"> And when his stories came to the ears of the chief priests and the Pharisees, they saw that he was talking of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Matthew 21:11; Matthew 21:26; Luke 7:16"> And though they had a desire to take him, they were in fear of the people, because in their eyes he was a prophet.</span>
