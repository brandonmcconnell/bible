<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 6:37; Romans 2:1–2; James 4:11–12"> Be not judges of others, and you will not be judged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 2:13; Mark 4:24; Luke 6:38"> For as you have been judging, so you will be judged, and with your measure will it be measured to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 8:7–9; Galatians 6:1; Luke 6:41–42"> And why do you take note of the grain of dust in your brother's eye, but take no note of the bit of wood which is in your eye?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Or how will you say to your brother, Let me take out the grain of dust from your eye, when you yourself have a bit of wood in your eye?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 6:42; Psalms 51:9–13; Matthew 23:13–28"> You false one, first take out the bit of wood from your eye, then will you see clearly to take out the grain of dust from your brother's eye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 9:7–8; Proverbs 23:9; Matthew 10:14–15"> Do not give that which is holy to the dogs, or put your jewels before pigs, for fear that they will be crushed under foot by the pigs whose attack will then be made against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; 1 John 3:22; John 15:7"> Make a request, and it will be answered; what you are searching for you will get; give the sign, and the door will be open to you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 81:10; Psalms 81:16; Luke 23:42–43"> Because to everyone who makes a request, it will be given; and he who is searching will get his desire, and to him who gives the sign, the door will be open.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 11:11–13"> Or which of you, if his son makes a request for bread, will give him a stone?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Or if he makes a request for a fish, will give him a snake?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 11:11–13; James 1:17; Romans 8:32"> If you, then, being evil, are able to give good things to your children, how much more will your Father in heaven give good things to those who make requests to him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 6:31; Romans 13:8–10; Galatians 5:13–14"> All those things, then, which you would have men do to you, even so do you to them: because this is the law and the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 13:24–25; Proverbs 16:25; John 14:6"> Go in by the narrow door; for wide is the door and open is the way which goes to destruction, and great numbers go in by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 13:23–30; Matthew 22:14; Matthew 16:24–25"> For narrow is the door and hard the road to life, and only a small number make discovery of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 4:1; 2 Peter 2:1–3; Matthew 24:11"> Be on the watch for false prophets, who come to you in sheep's clothing, but inside they are cruel wolves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 6:43–45; Matthew 12:33; James 3:12"> By their fruits you will get knowledge of them. Do men get grapes from thorns or figs from thistles?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 12:33–35; Galatians 5:22–24; Ephesians 5:9"> Even so, every good tree gives good fruit; but the bad tree gives evil fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 5:17; 1 John 3:9–10"> It is not possible for a good tree to give bad fruit, and a bad tree will not give good fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 3:10; Hebrews 6:8; John 15:2–6"> Every tree which does not give good fruit is cut down and put in the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 7:16; Acts 5:38"> So by their fruits you will get knowledge of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 2:13; Luke 6:46; James 1:22"> Not everyone who says to me, Lord, Lord, will go into the kingdom of heaven; but he who does the pleasure of my Father in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 13:25–27; 1 Corinthians 13:1–2; Malachi 3:17–18"> A great number will say to me on that day, Lord, Lord, were we not prophets in your name, and did we not by your name send out evil spirits, and by your name do works of power?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 25:41; Psalms 6:8; Luke 13:25"> And then will I say to them, I never had knowledge of you: go from me, you workers of evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 6:47–49; John 13:17; 1 Corinthians 3:10–11"> Everyone, then, to whom my words come and who does them, will be like a wise man who made his house on a rock;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 1:12; Psalms 125:1–2; 1 Peter 1:7"> And the rain came down and there was a rush of waters and the winds were driving against that house, but it was not moved; because it was based on the rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:9; Luke 6:49; James 2:20"> And everyone to whom my words come and who does them not, will be like a foolish man who made his house on sand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 13:19–22; 2 Peter 2:20–22; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> And the rain came down and there was a rush of waters and the winds were driving against that house; and it came down and great was its fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 4:32; Mark 1:22; John 7:46"> And it came about, when Jesus had come to the end of these words, that the people were surprised at his teaching,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12–13; Luke 20:8; Deuteronomy 18:18–19"> for he was teaching as one having authority, and not as their scribes.</span>
