<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:8; Jeremiah 51:63; Jeremiah 42:22"> And it came about that when Jeremiah had come to the end of giving all the people the words of the Lord their God, which the Lord their God had sent him to say to them, even all these words,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:13; Isaiah 7:9; Jeremiah 40:8"> Then Azariah, the son of Hoshaiah, and Johanan, the son of Kareah, and all the men of pride, said to Jeremiah, You have said what is false: the Lord our God has not sent you to say, You are not to go into the land of Egypt and make your living-place there:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 45:1–3; Jeremiah 36:4; Jeremiah 36:26"> But Baruch, the son of Neriah, is moving you against us, to give us up into the hands of the Chaldaeans so that they may put us to death, and take us away prisoners into Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:5–6; Psalms 37:3; 2 Chronicles 25:16"> So Johanan, the son of Kareah, and all the captains of the forces, and all the people, did not give ear to the order of the Lord that they were to go on living in the land of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:11–12; Jeremiah 41:15–16; 1 Samuel 26:19"> But Johanan, the son of Kareah, and all the captains of the forces took all the rest of Judah who had come back into the land of Judah from all the nations where they had been forced to go;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:10; Jeremiah 40:7; Jeremiah 41:10"> The men and the women and the children and the king's daughters, and every person whom Nebuzaradan, the captain of the armed men, had put under the care of Gedaliah, the son of Ahikam, the son of Shaphan, and Jeremiah the prophet and Baruch, the son of Neriah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:16; Jeremiah 44:1; Isaiah 30:4"> And they came into the land of Egypt; for they did not give ear to the voice of the Lord: and they came to Tahpanhes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:16; Psalms 139:7; 2 Timothy 2:9"> Then the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah in Tahpanhes, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:2–12; Jeremiah 19:1–15; Jeremiah 51:63–64"> Take in your hand some great stones, and put them in a safe place in the paste in the brickwork which is at the way into Pharaoh's house in Tahpanhes, before the eyes of the men of Judah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 44:28–1; Jeremiah 1:15; Psalms 31:20"> And say to them, This is what the Lord of armies, the God of Israel, has said: See, I will send and take Nebuchadrezzar, the king of Babylon, my servant, and he will put the seat of his kingdom on these stones which have been put in a safe place here by you; and his tent will be stretched over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:2; Jeremiah 44:13; Ezekiel 29:19–20"> And he will come and overcome the land of Egypt; those who are for death will be put to death, those who are to be prisoners will be made prisoners, and those who are for the sword will be given to the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:13; Psalms 109:18–19; Isaiah 19:1"> And he will put a fire in the houses of the gods of Egypt; and they will be burned by him: and he will make Egypt clean as a keeper of sheep makes clean his clothing; and he will go out from there in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 19:18"> And the stone pillars of Beth-shemesh in the land of Egypt will be broken by him, and the houses of the gods of Egypt burned with fire.</span>
