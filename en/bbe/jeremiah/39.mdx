<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:1–2; Jeremiah 52:4–7; 2 Kings 25:1–12"> And it came about, that when Jerusalem was taken, (in the ninth year of Zedekiah, king of Judah, in the tenth month, Nebuchadrezzar, king of Babylon, with all his army, came against Jerusalem, shutting it in on every side;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:6–7; Micah 2:12–13; 2 Kings 25:3–4"> In the eleventh year of Zedekiah, in the fourth month, on the ninth day of the month, the town was broken into:)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:4; Jeremiah 38:17; Jeremiah 39:13"> All the captains of the king of Babylon came in and took their places in the middle doorway of the town, Nergal-shar-ezer, ruler of Sin-magir, the Rabmag, and Nebushazban, the Rab-saris, and all the captains of the king of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 2:14; 2 Chronicles 32:5; 2 Kings 25:4–7"> And when Zedekiah, king of Judah, and all the men of war saw it, they went in flight from the town by night, by the way of the king's garden, through the doorway between the two walls: and they went out by the Arabah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 23:33; Joshua 5:10; Jeremiah 52:26–27"> But the Chaldaean army went after them and overtook Zedekiah in the lowlands of Jericho: and they made him a prisoner and took him up to Nebuchadrezzar, king of Babylon, to Riblah in the land of Hamath, to be judged by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:10; Jeremiah 34:19–21; Jeremiah 24:8–10"> Then the king of Babylon put the sons of Zedekiah to death before his eyes in Riblah: and the king of Babylon put to death all the great men of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; 2 Kings 25:7; Jeremiah 52:11"> And more than this, he put out Zedekiah's eyes, and had him put in chains to take him away to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:3; Jeremiah 38:18; Jeremiah 21:10"> And the Chaldaeans put the king's house on fire, as well as the houses of the people, and had the walls of Jerusalem broken down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:1; Genesis 37:36; Jeremiah 10:18"> Then Nebuzaradan, the captain of the armed men, took away to Babylon as prisoners, all the rest of the workmen who were still in the town, as well as those who had given themselves up to him, and all the rest of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 25:12; Jeremiah 40:7; Jeremiah 52:16"> But Nebuzaradan, the captain of the armed men, let the poorest of the people, who had nothing whatever, go on living in the land of Judah, and gave them vine-gardens and fields at the same time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 24:23; Jeremiah 15:21; Job 5:19"> Now Nebuchadrezzar, king of Babylon, gave orders about Jeremiah to Nebuzaradan, the captain of the armed men, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Jeremiah 40:4; Proverbs 21:1"> Take him and keep an eye on him and see that no evil comes to him; but do with him whatever he says to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:3; Jeremiah 39:9"> So Nebuzaradan, the captain of the armed men, sent Nebushazban, the Rab-saris, and Nergal-shar-ezer, the Rabmag, and all the chief captains of the king of Babylon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:28; 2 Kings 22:12; Jeremiah 26:24"> And they sent and took Jeremiah out of the place of the watchmen, and gave him into the care of Gedaliah, the son of Ahikam, the son of Shaphan, to take him to his house: so he was living among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:9; Jeremiah 32:1–2; Jeremiah 36:1–5"> Now the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah while he was shut up in the place of the armed watchmen, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 9:12; Zechariah 1:6; Joshua 23:14–15"> Go and say to Ebed-melech the Ethiopian, This is what the Lord of armies, the God of Israel, has said: See, my words will come true for this town, for evil and not for good: they will come about before your eyes on that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 41:1–2; Psalms 91:14–15; Psalms 50:15"> But I will keep you safe on that day, says the Lord: you will not be given into the hands of the men you are fearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:2; Psalms 34:22; Jeremiah 21:9"> For I will certainly let you go free, and you will not be put to the sword, but your life will be given to you out of the hands of your attackers: because you have put your faith in me, says the Lord.</span>
