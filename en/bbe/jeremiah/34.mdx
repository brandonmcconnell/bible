<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:15; Daniel 2:37–38; 2 Kings 25:1–9"> The word which came to Jeremiah from the Lord, when Nebuchadrezzar, king of Babylon, and all his army, and all the kingdoms of the earth which were under his rule, and all the peoples, were fighting against Jerusalem and all its towns, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:10; Jeremiah 34:22; Jeremiah 37:8–10"> The Lord, the God of Israel, has said, Go and say to Zedekiah, king of Judah, This is what the Lord has said: See, I will give this town into the hands of the king of Babylon, and he will have it burned with fire:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:4; Jeremiah 21:7; 2 Kings 25:4–7"> And you will not get away from him, but will certainly be taken and given up into his hands; and you will see the king of Babylon, eye to eye, and he will have talk with you, mouth to mouth, and you will go to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> But give ear to the word of the Lord, O Zedekiah, king of Judah; this is what the Lord has said about you: Death will not come to you by the sword:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:18; 2 Chronicles 16:14; 2 Kings 22:20"> You will come to your end in peace; and such burnings as they made for your fathers, the earlier kings before you, will be made for you; and they will be weeping for you and saying, Ah lord! for I have said the word, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:16–24; 1 Samuel 3:18; 1 Kings 21:19"> Then Jeremiah the prophet said all these things to Zedekiah, king of Judah, in Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 10:3; Jeremiah 4:5; 2 Kings 18:13–14"> When the army of the king of Babylon was fighting against Jerusalem and against all the towns of Judah which had not been taken, against Lachish and against Azekah; for these were the last of the walled towns of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 25:10; 2 Kings 23:2–3; 2 Kings 11:17"> The word which came to Jeremiah from the Lord, after King Zedekiah had made an agreement with all the people in Jerusalem, to give news in public that servants were to be made free;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:12; Leviticus 25:39–46; Exodus 3:18"> That every man was to let his Hebrew man-servant and his Hebrew servant-girl go free; so that no one might make use of a Jew, his countryman, as a servant:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:16; Jeremiah 26:10; Jeremiah 38:4"> And this was done by all the rulers and the people who had taken part in the agreement, and every one let his man-servant and his servant-girl go free, not to be used as servants any longer; they did so, and let them go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 2:20–22; Proverbs 26:11; Matthew 12:43–45"> But later, they took back again the servants and the servant-girls whom they had let go free, and put them again under the yoke as servants and servant-girls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> For this reason the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah from the Lord, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 6:8; Joshua 24:17; Deuteronomy 5:27"> The Lord, the God of Israel, has said, I made an agreement with your fathers on the day when I took them out of Egypt, out of the prison-house, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:12; 1 Kings 9:22; 2 Kings 17:13–14"> At the end of seven years every man is to let go his countryman who is a Hebrew, who has become yours for a price and has been your servant for six years; you are to let him go free: but your fathers gave no attention and did not give ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:8; 2 Kings 23:3; Nehemiah 10:29"> And now, turning away from evil, you had done what is right in my eyes, giving a public undertaking for every man to make his neighbour free; and you had made an agreement before me in the house which is named by my name:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 19:12; Exodus 20:7; Jeremiah 34:11"> But again you have put shame on my name, and you have taken back, every one his man-servant and his servant-girl, whom you had sent away free, and you have put them under the yoke again to be your servants and servant-girls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Deuteronomy 28:25; Galatians 6:7"> And so the Lord has said, You have not given ear to me and undertaken publicly, every man to let loose his countryman and his neighbour: see, I undertake to let loose against you the sword and disease and need of food; and I will send you wandering among all the kingdoms of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 15:10; Genesis 15:17–18; Hosea 6:7"> And I will give the men who have gone against my agreement and have not given effect to the words of the agreement which they made before me, when the ox was cut in two and they went between the parts of it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:10; Zephaniah 3:3–4; Daniel 9:6"> The rulers of Judah and the rulers of Jerusalem, the unsexed servants and the priests and all the people of the land who went between the parts of the ox,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:33; Jeremiah 16:4; Jeremiah 19:7"> Even these I will give up into the hands of their haters and into the hands of those who have designs against their lives: and their dead bodies will become food for the birds of heaven and the beasts of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:24–27; Jeremiah 39:6; Jeremiah 52:10"> And Zedekiah, king of Judah, and his rulers I will give into the hands of their haters and into the hands of those who have designs against their lives, and into the hands of the king of Babylon's army which has gone away from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:11; Jeremiah 39:8; Jeremiah 39:1–2"> See, I will give orders, says the Lord, and make them come back to this town; and they will make war on it and take it and have it burned with fire: and I will make the towns of Judah waste and unpeopled.</span>
