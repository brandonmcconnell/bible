<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:11–12; Hosea 13:15; Amos 3:6"> The Lord has said: See, I will make a wind of destruction come up against Babylon and against those who are living in Chaldaea;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:7; Matthew 3:12; Isaiah 41:16"> And I will send men to Babylon to make her clean and get her land cleared: for in the day of trouble they will put up their tents against her on every side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:14; Jeremiah 46:4; Jeremiah 50:21"> Against her the bow of the archer is bent, and he puts on his coat of metal: have no mercy on her young men, give all her army up to the curse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:26; Jeremiah 50:30; Isaiah 13:15"> And the dead will be stretched out in the land of the Chaldaeans, and the wounded in her streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 11:1–2; Zechariah 12:6; Zechariah 12:8"> For Israel has not been given up, or Judah, by his God, by the Lord of armies; for their land is full of sin against the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:8; Jeremiah 25:14; Jeremiah 50:28"> Go in flight out of Babylon, so that every man may keep his life; do not be cut off in her evil-doing: for it is the time of the Lord's punishment; he will give her her reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 14:8; Revelation 17:4; Revelation 17:2"> Babylon has been a gold cup in the hand of the Lord, which has made all the earth overcome with wine: the nations have taken of her wine, and for this cause the nations have gone off their heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:11; Isaiah 21:9; Revelation 14:8"> Sudden is the downfall of Babylon and her destruction: make cries of grief for her; take sweet oil for her pain, if it is possible for her to be made well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:16; Revelation 18:5; Isaiah 13:14"> We would have made Babylon well, but she is not made well: give her up, and let us go everyone to his country: for her punishment is stretching up to heaven, and lifted up even to the skies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:28; Psalms 37:6; Isaiah 51:11"> The Lord has made clear our righteousness: come, and let us give an account in Zion of the work of the Lord our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:4; Jeremiah 50:45; Jeremiah 46:9"> Make bright the arrows; take up the body-covers: the Lord has been moving the spirit of the king of the Medes; because his design against Babylon is its destruction: for it is the punishment from the Lord, the payment for his Temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 13:2; Jeremiah 51:11; Jeremiah 51:29"> Let the flag be lifted up against the walls of Babylon, make the watch strong, put the watchmen in their places, make ready a surprise attack: for it is the Lord's purpose, and he has done what he said about the people of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 17:1; Isaiah 45:3; Revelation 17:15"> O you whose living-place is by the wide waters, whose stores are great, your end is come, your evil profit is ended.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joel 2:25; Jeremiah 50:15; Jeremiah 49:13"> The Lord of armies has taken an oath by himself, saying, Truly, I will make you full with men as with locusts, and their voices will be loud against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 4:11; Jeremiah 10:12–16; Jeremiah 32:17"> He has made the earth by his power, he has made the world strong in its place by his wisdom, and by his wise design the heavens have been stretched out:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 135:7; Psalms 18:13; Jonah 1:4"> At the sound of his voice there is a massing of the waters in the heavens, and he makes the mists go up from the ends of the earth; he makes the thunder-flames for the rain and sends out the wind from his store-houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:18–19; Jeremiah 10:14; Isaiah 44:18–20"> Then every man becomes like a beast without knowledge; every gold-worker is put to shame by the image he has made: for his metal image is deceit, and there is no breath in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:8; Isaiah 19:1; Zephaniah 2:11"> They are nothing, a work of error: in the time of their punishment, destruction will overtake them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 73:26; Jeremiah 10:16; Psalms 33:12"> The heritage of Jacob is not like these; for the maker of all things is his heritage: the Lord of armies is his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 41:15–16; Micah 4:13; Isaiah 37:26"> You are my fighting axe and my instrument of war: with you the nations will be broken; with you kingdoms will be broken;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 15:1; Zechariah 10:5; Haggai 2:22"> With you the horse and the horseman will be broken; with you the war-carriage and he who goes in it will be broken;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:17; Isaiah 13:18; 1 Samuel 15:3"> With you man and woman will be broken; with you the old man and the boy will be broken; with you the young man and the virgin will be broken;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:57"> With you the keeper of sheep with his flock will be broken, and with you the farmer and his oxen will be broken, and with you captains and rulers will be broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:15; Revelation 18:24; Isaiah 63:1–4"> And I will give to Babylon, and to all the people of Chaldaea, their reward for all the evil they have done in Zion before your eyes, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Revelation 8:8; Zechariah 4:7; Jeremiah 50:31"> See, I am against you, says the Lord, O mountain of destruction, causing the destruction of all the earth: and my hand will be stretched out on you, rolling you down from the rocks, and making you a burned mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 13:19–22; Jeremiah 51:43; Revelation 18:20–24"> And they will not take from you a stone for the angle of a wall or the base of a building; but you will be a waste place for ever, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 10:3; Genesis 8:4; 1 Chronicles 1:6"> Let a flag be lifted up in the land, let the horn be sounded among the nations, make the nations ready against her; get the kingdoms of Ararat, Minni, and Ashkenaz together against her, make ready a scribe against her; let the horses come up against her like massed locusts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:11; Jeremiah 25:25; Esther 1:3"> Make the nations ready for war against her, the king of the Medes and his rulers and all his captains, and all the land under his rule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:11–12; Jeremiah 51:43; Jeremiah 8:16"> And the land is shaking and in pain: for the purposes of the Lord are fixed, to make the land of Babylon an unpeopled waste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Nahum 3:13; Jeremiah 50:36–37; Lamentations 2:9"> Babylon's men of war have kept back from the fight, waiting in their strong places; their strength has given way, they have become like women: her houses have been put on fire, her locks are broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:6; 2 Samuel 18:19–31; Esther 8:14"> One man, running, will give word to another, and one who goes with news will be handing it on to another, to give word to the king of Babylon that his town has been taken from every quarter:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:37–38; Isaiah 44:27; Jeremiah 51:30"> And the ways across the river have been taken, and the water-holes ... burned with fire, and the men of war are in the grip of fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 21:10; Hosea 6:11; Joel 3:13"> For these are the words of the Lord of armies, the God of Israel: The daughter of Babylon is like a grain-floor when it is stamped down; before long, the time of her grain-cutting will come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:17; Jeremiah 51:44; Matthew 23:13"> Nebuchadrezzar, king of Babylon, has made a meal of me, violently crushing me, he has made me a vessel with nothing in it, he has taken me in his mouth like a dragon, he has made his stomach full with my delicate flesh, crushing me with his teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Revelation 18:20; Jeremiah 50:29; Isaiah 26:20–21"> May the violent things done to me, and my downfall, come on Babylon, the daughter of Zion will say; and, May my blood be on the people of Chaldaea, Jerusalem will say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:38; Romans 12:19; Psalms 140:12"> For this reason the Lord has said: See, I will give support to your cause, and take payment for what you have undergone; I will make her sea dry, and her fountain without water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Revelation 18:2; Revelation 18:21–23; Jeremiah 18:16"> And Babylon will become a mass of broken walls, a hole for jackals, a cause of wonder and surprise, without a living man in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:15; Psalms 58:6; Judges 16:20"> They will be crying out together like lions, their voices will be like the voices of young lions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:57; Jeremiah 25:27; Nahum 3:11"> When they are heated, I will make a feast for them, and overcome them with wine, so that they may become unconscious, sleeping an eternal sleep without awaking, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:27; Isaiah 34:6; Psalms 44:22"> I will make them go down to death like lambs, like he-goats together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:26; Isaiah 13:19; Jeremiah 49:25"> How is Babylon taken! and the praise of all the earth surprised! how has Babylon become a cause of wonder among the nations!</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–8; Daniel 9:26; Psalms 18:4"> The sea has come up over Babylon; she is covered with the mass of its waves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Isaiah 13:20; Jeremiah 2:6; Jeremiah 51:29"> Her towns have become a waste, a dry and unwatered land, where no man has his living-place and no son of man goes by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:34; Jeremiah 51:58; Isaiah 2:2"> And I will send punishment on Bel in Babylon, and take out of his mouth what went into it; no longer will the nations be flowing together to him: truly, the wall of Babylon will come down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:6; Revelation 18:4; Isaiah 48:20"> My people, go out from her, and let every man get away safe from the burning wrath of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 13:3–5; Isaiah 19:2; Isaiah 21:2–3"> So that your hearts may not become feeble and full of fear because of the news which will go about in the land; for a story will go about one year, and after that in another year another story, and violent acts in the land, ruler against ruler.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:2; Isaiah 46:1–2; Jeremiah 51:52"> For this cause, truly, the days are coming when I will send punishment on the images of Babylon, and all her land will be shamed, and her dead will be falling down in her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Revelation 18:20; Isaiah 44:23; Jeremiah 50:3"> And the heaven and the earth and everything in them, will make a song of joy over Babylon: for those who make her waste will come from the north, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:29; Jeremiah 51:24; Psalms 137:8–9"> As Babylon had the dead of Israel put to the sword, so in Babylon the dead of all the land will be stretched out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:28; Psalms 122:6; Revelation 18:4"> You who have got away safe from the sword, go, waiting for nothing; have the Lord in memory when you are far away, and keep Jerusalem in mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Lamentations 1:10; Psalms 44:13–16; Psalms 74:3–7"> We are shamed because bitter words have come to our ears; our faces are covered with shame: for men from strange lands have come into the holy places of the Lord's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:47; Jeremiah 50:38; Daniel 5:30–31"> For this reason, see, the days are coming, says the Lord, when I will send punishment on her images; and through all her land the wounded will be crying out in pain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Genesis 11:4; Jeremiah 49:16; Psalms 139:8–10"> Even if Babylon was lifted up to heaven, even if she had the high places of her strength shut in with walls, still I would send against her those who will make her waste, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:22; Jeremiah 48:3–5; Jeremiah 50:46"> There is the sound of a cry from Babylon, and of a great destruction from the land of the Chaldaeans:</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:10; Isaiah 47:5; Isaiah 15:1"> For the Lord is making Babylon waste, and putting an end to the great voice coming out of her; and her waves are thundering like great waters, their voice is sounding loud:</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Psalms 46:9; Jeremiah 51:48; Psalms 94:1–2"> For the waster has come on her, even on Babylon, and her men of war are taken, their bows are broken: for the Lord is a rewarding God, and he will certainly give payment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:18; Jeremiah 25:27; Jeremiah 48:15"> And I will make her chiefs and her wise men, her rulers and her captains and her men of war, overcome with wine; their sleep will be an eternal sleep without awaking, says the King; the Lord of armies is his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:13; Jeremiah 51:64; Jeremiah 51:44"> The Lord of armies has said: The wide walls of Babylon will be completely uncovered and her high doorways will be burned with fire; so peoples keep on working for nothing, and the weariness of nations comes to an end in the smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:12; Jeremiah 36:4; Jeremiah 45:1"> The order which Jeremiah the prophet gave to Seraiah, the son of Neriah, the son of Mahseiah, when he went with Zedekiah, the king of Judah, to Babylon in the fourth year of his rule. Now Seraiah was the chief controller of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:2–3; Jeremiah 36:32; Isaiah 30:8"> And Jeremiah put in a book all the evil which was to come on Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Mark 13:1; 1 Thessalonians 5:27; 1 Thessalonians 4:18"> And Jeremiah said to Seraiah, When you come to Babylon, see that you give them all these words;</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:13; Jeremiah 50:3; Ezekiel 35:9"> And after reading them, say, O Lord, you have said about this place that it is to be cut off, so that no one will be living in it, not a man or a beast, but it will be unpeopled for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Revelation 18:21; Jeremiah 19:10–11"> And it will be that, when you have come to an end of reading this book, you are to have a stone fixed to it, and have it dropped into the Euphrates:</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:58; Job 31:40; Nahum 1:8–9"> And you are to say, So Babylon will go down, never to be lifted up again, because of the evil which I will send on her: and weariness will overcome them. So far, these are the words of Jeremiah.</span>
