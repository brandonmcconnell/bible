<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:22; Jeremiah 30:24; Zechariah 13:9"> At that time, says the Lord, I will be the God of all the families of Israel, and they will be my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 10:33; Psalms 95:11; Matthew 11:28"> The Lord has said, Grace came in the waste land to a people kept safe from the sword, even to Israel on the way to his resting-place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 103:17; Isaiah 54:8–9; Hosea 11:4"> From far away he saw the Lord: my love for you is an eternal love: so with mercy I have made you come with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:7; Jeremiah 31:13; Amos 5:2"> I will again make new your buildings, O virgin of Israel, and you will take up your place: again you will take up your instruments of music, and go out in the dances of those who are glad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 9:14; Leviticus 19:23–25; Deuteronomy 28:30"> Again will your vine-gardens be planted on the hill of Samaria: the planters will be planting and using the fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:4–5; Micah 4:1–3; Jeremiah 50:19"> For there will be a day when those who get in the grapes on the hills of Ephraim will be crying, Up! let us go up to Zion to the Lord our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 14:7; Psalms 28:9; Isaiah 37:31"> For the Lord has said, Make a glad song for Jacob and give a cry on the top of the mountains: give the news, give praise, and say, The Lord has given salvation to his people, even to the rest of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Isaiah 42:16; Jeremiah 23:8"> See, I will take them from the north country, and get them from the inmost parts of the earth, and with them the blind and the feeble-footed, the woman with child and her who is in birth-pains together: a very great army, they will come back here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 5:4; Jeremiah 3:4; Exodus 4:22"> They will come with weeping, and going before them I will be their guide: guiding them by streams of water in a straight way where there is no falling: for I am a father to Israel, and Ephraim is the first of my sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; Ezekiel 34:12; Deuteronomy 30:4"> Give ear to the word of the Lord, O you nations, and give news of it in the sea-lands far away, and say, He who has sent Israel wandering will get him together and will keep him as a keeper does his flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 48:20; Titus 2:14; Isaiah 44:23"> For the Lord has given a price for Jacob, and made him free from the hands of him who was stronger than he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 35:10; Isaiah 58:11; Joel 3:18"> So they will come with songs on the high places, flowing together to the good things of the Lord, to the grain and the wine and the oil, to the young ones of the flock and of the herd: their souls will be like a watered garden, and they will have no more sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 30:11; Isaiah 61:3; Isaiah 51:11"> Then the virgin will have joy in the dance, and the young men and the old will be glad: for I will have their weeping turned into joy, I will give them comfort and make them glad after their sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:25; 1 Peter 2:9; Jeremiah 33:9"> I will give the priests their desired fat things, and my people will have a full measure of my good things, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 2:16–18; Jeremiah 40:1; Psalms 77:2"> So has the Lord said: In Ramah there is a sound of crying, weeping and bitter sorrow; Rachel weeping for her children; she will not be comforted for their loss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ruth 2:12; Jeremiah 30:3; Hebrews 6:10"> The Lord has said this: Keep your voice from sorrow and your eyes from weeping: for your work will be rewarded, says the Lord; and they will come back from the land of their hater.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Lamentations 3:21; Jeremiah 29:11–16; Lamentations 3:18"> And there is hope for the future, says the Lord; and your children will come back to the land which is theirs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:9; Psalms 80:3; Job 5:17"> Certainly Ephraim's words of grief have come to my ears, You have given me training and I have undergone it like a young cow unused to the yoke: let me be turned and come back, for you are the Lord my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:31; Jeremiah 3:25; Ezekiel 21:12"> Truly, after I had been turned, I had regret for my ways; and after I had got knowledge, I made signs of sorrow: I was put to shame, truly, I was covered with shame, because I had to undergo the shame of my early years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 55:7; Micah 7:18–19; Isaiah 63:15"> Is Ephraim my dear son? is he the child of my delight? for whenever I say things against him, I still keep him in my memory: so my heart is troubled for him; I will certainly have mercy on him, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:5; Isaiah 62:10; Psalms 84:5"> Put up guiding pillars, make road signs for yourself: give attention to the highway, even the way in which you went: be turned again, O virgin of Israel, be turned to these your towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:4; Jeremiah 7:24; Matthew 1:21"> How long will you go on turning this way and that, O wandering daughter? for the Lord has made a new thing on the earth, a woman changed into a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zechariah 8:3; Isaiah 1:26; Jeremiah 50:7"> So the Lord of armies, the God of Israel, has said, Again will these words be used in the land of Judah and in its towns, when I have let their fate be changed: May the blessing of the Lord be on you, O resting-place of righteousness, O holy mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 8:4–8; Ezekiel 36:10; Zechariah 2:4"> And Judah and all its towns will be living there together; the farmers and those who go about with flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 5:6; Matthew 11:28; John 4:14"> For I have given new strength to the tired soul and to every sorrowing soul in full measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 127:2; Zechariah 4:1–2"> At this, awaking from my sleep, I saw; and my sleep was sweet to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hosea 2:23; Zechariah 10:9; Ezekiel 36:9"> See, the days are coming, says the Lord, when I will have Israel and Judah planted with the seed of man and with the seed of beast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:6; Jeremiah 1:10; Jeremiah 18:7–9"> And it will come about that, as I have been watching over them for the purpose of uprooting and smashing down and overturning and sending destruction and causing trouble; so I will be watching over them for the purpose of building up and planting, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Lamentations 5:7; Ezekiel 18:2–3; Jeremiah 31:30"> In those days they will no longer say, The fathers have been tasting bitter grapes and the children's teeth are put on edge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:20; Isaiah 3:11; Deuteronomy 24:16"> But everyone will be put to death for the evil which he himself has done: whoever has taken bitter grapes will himself have his teeth put on edge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Hebrews 10:16–17; Ezekiel 37:26; Jeremiah 31:31–34"> See, the days are coming, says the Lord, when I will make a new agreement with the people of Israel and with the people of Judah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 24:6–8; Deuteronomy 5:3; Exodus 19:5"> Not like the agreement which I made with their fathers, on the day when I took them by the hand to be their guide out of the land of Egypt; which agreement was broken by them, and I gave them up, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:3; Hebrews 10:16; Hebrews 8:10"> But this is the agreement which I will make with the people of Israel after those days, says the Lord; I will put my law in their inner parts, writing it in their hearts; and I will be their God, and they will be my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Hebrews 8:12; 1 John 2:27"> And no longer will they be teaching every man his neighbour and every man his brother, saying, Get knowledge of the Lord: for they will all have knowledge of me, from the least of them to the greatest of them, says the Lord: for they will have my forgiveness for their evil-doing, and their sin will go from my memory for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:16; Isaiah 51:15; Psalms 136:7–9"> These are the words of the Lord, who has given the sun for a light by day, ordering the moon and stars for a light by night, who puts the sea in motion, causing the thunder of its waves; the Lord of armies is his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 54:9–10; Jeremiah 33:20–26; Psalms 89:36–37"> If the order of these things before me is ever broken, says the Lord, then will the seed of Israel come to an end as a nation before me for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:24–26; Isaiah 40:12; Jeremiah 33:22"> This is what the Lord has said: If the heavens on high may be measured, and the bases of the earth searched out, then I will give up the seed of Israel, because of all they have done, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Zechariah 14:10; 2 Kings 14:13; Jeremiah 30:18"> See, the days are coming, says the Lord, for the building of the Lord's town, from the tower of Hananel to the doorway of the angle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Zechariah 2:1–2; Ezekiel 40:8"> And the measuring-line will go out in front of it as far as the hill Gareb, going round to Goah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:23; Joel 3:17; 2 Kings 11:16"> And all the valley of the dead bodies, and all the field of death as far as the stream Kidron, up to the angle of the horses' doorway to the east, will be holy to the Lord; it will not again be uprooted or overturned for ever.</span>
