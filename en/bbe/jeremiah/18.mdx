<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The word which came to Jeremiah from the Lord, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 1:1; Acts 9:6; Jeremiah 19:1–2"> Up! go down to the potter's house, and there I will let my words come to your ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 15:14; Jonah 1:3; Acts 26:19"> Then I went down to the potter's house, and he was doing his work on the stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 9:20–23; Jeremiah 18:6; Isaiah 45:9"> And when the vessel, which he was forming out of earth, got damaged in the hand of the potter, he made it again into another vessel, as it seemed good to the potter to make it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Then the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; Romans 9:20–21; Jeremiah 18:4"> O Israel, am I not able to do with you as this potter does? says the Lord. See, like earth in the potter's hand are you in my hands, O Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:10; Jeremiah 12:14–17; Jeremiah 45:4"> Whenever I say anything about uprooting a nation or a kingdom, and smashing it and sending destruction on it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:21; Jeremiah 26:13; Jonah 3:9–10"> If, in that very minute, that nation of which I was talking is turned away from its evil, my purpose of doing evil to them will be changed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:28; Amos 9:11–15; Jeremiah 1:10"> And whenever I say anything about building up a nation or a kingdom, and planting it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:18; 1 Samuel 2:30; Ezekiel 18:24"> If, in that very minute, it does evil in my eyes, going against my orders, then my good purpose, which I said I would do for them, will be changed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 17:13; Isaiah 55:6–7; Jeremiah 35:15"> Now, then, say to the men of Judah and to the people of Jerusalem, This is what the Lord has said: See, I am forming an evil thing against you, and designing a design against you: let every man come back now from his evil way, and let your ways and your doings be changed for the better.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:25; Jeremiah 7:24; Deuteronomy 29:19"> But they will say, There is no hope: we will go on in our designs, and every one of us will do what he is moved by the pride of his evil heart to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:30; Hosea 6:10; Jeremiah 23:14"> So this is what the Lord has said: Make search among the nations and see who has had word of such things; the virgin of Israel has done a very shocking thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 6:68"> Will the white snow go away from the top of Sirion? will the cold waters flowing from the mountains become dry?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:16; Jeremiah 2:32; Hosea 11:2"> For my people have put me out of their memory, burning perfumes to that which is nothing; and because of this, I will put a cause of falling in their ways, even in the old roads, and will make them go on side-roads, in a way not lifted up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 22:7; Jeremiah 25:9; Jeremiah 19:8"> Making their land a thing of wonder, causing sounds of surprise for ever; everyone who goes by will be overcome with wonder, shaking his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:24; Jeremiah 2:27; Job 27:21"> I will send them in flight, as from an east wind, before the attacker; I will let them see my back and not my face on the day of their downfall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 52:2; Jeremiah 11:19; Malachi 2:7"> Then they said, Come, let us make a design against Jeremiah; for teaching will never be cut off from the priest, or wisdom from the wise, or the word from the prophet. Come, let us make use of his words for an attack on him, and let us give attention with care to what he says.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Nehemiah 6:9; Jeremiah 20:12; Psalms 55:16–17"> Give thought to me, O Lord, and give ear to the voice of those who put forward a cause against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 35:7; Psalms 57:6; Psalms 106:23"> Is evil to be the reward of good? for they have made a deep hole for my soul. Keep in mind how I took my place before you, to say a good word for them so that your wrath might be turned away from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 109:9–20; Lamentations 5:3; Amos 4:10"> For this cause, let their children be without food, and give them over to the power of the sword; and let their wives be without children and become widows; let their men be overtaken by death, and their young men be put to the sword in the fight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:26; Psalms 140:5; Zephaniah 1:10–11"> Let a cry for help go up from their houses, when you send an armed band on them suddenly: for they have made a hole in which to take me, and have put nets for my feet secretly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 35:4; Psalms 69:22–28; Nehemiah 4:4–5"> But you, Lord, have knowledge of all the designs which they have made against my life; let not their evil-doing be covered or their sin be washed away from before your eyes: but let it be a cause of falling before you: so do to them in the time of your wrath.</span>
