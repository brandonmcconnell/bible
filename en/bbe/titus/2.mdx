<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:13; 1 Timothy 6:3; Titus 2:11–14"> But let your words be in agreement with true and right teaching:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 5:23; 1 Peter 4:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:8"> That old men are to be simple in their tastes, serious, wise, true in faith, in love, and of a quiet mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Titus 2:4; 1 Timothy 3:11; 1 Peter 3:3–5"> That old women are to be self-respecting in behaviour, not saying evil of others, not given to taking much wine, teachers of that which is good,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:14; 1 Timothy 5:2; 1 Timothy 5:11"> Training the younger women to have love for their husbands and children,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 31:10–31; Colossians 3:18; 1 Timothy 5:10"> To be wise in mind, clean in heart, kind; working in their houses, living under the authority of their husbands; so that no evil may be said of the word of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:1; 1 Peter 5:5; Joel 2:28"> To the young men give orders to be wise and serious-minded:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 5:3; 1 Timothy 4:12; 2 Corinthians 4:2"> In all things see that you are an example of good works; holy in your teaching, serious in behaviour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; 1 Peter 2:15; 1 Peter 3:16"> Saying true and right words, against which no protest may be made, so that he who is not on our side may be put to shame, unable to say any evil of us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 2:18–25; 1 Timothy 6:1–2; Colossians 3:22–25"> Servants are to be under the authority of their masters, pleasing them in all things, without argument;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 5:16; 1 Peter 2:12; 2 John 1:9"> Not taking what is not theirs, but giving clear signs of their good faith, in all things doing credit to the teaching of God our Saviour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 2:8; 1 Timothy 2:4; Isaiah 45:22"> For the grace of God has come, giving salvation to all men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:27; Matthew 16:24; Romans 6:19"> Training us so that, turning away from evil and the desires of this world, we may be living wisely and uprightly in the knowledge of God in this present life;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:8; 1 Corinthians 1:7; Matthew 16:27"> Looking for the glad hope, the revelation of the glory of our great God and Saviour Jesus Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 9:14; Ephesians 2:10; 1 Peter 2:9"> Who gave himself for us, so that he might make us free from all wrongdoing, and make for himself a people clean in heart and on fire with good works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:2; 1 Timothy 4:12–13; Titus 1:13"> On all these points give teaching and help, and make clear what is right with all authority. Let all men give you honour.</span>
