<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 14:1; Joshua 19:51; Joshua 17:4"> Then the heads of the families of the Levites came to Eleazar the priest and Joshua, the son of Nun, and to the heads of families of the tribes of the children of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; Numbers 35:2–8; Matthew 10:10"> And said to them in Shiloh in the land of Canaan, The Lord gave orders by Moses that we were to have towns for living in, with their grass-lands for our cattle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 49:7; Deuteronomy 33:8–10; 1 Chronicles 6:54–81"> And the children of Israel out of their heritage gave to the Levites these towns with their grass-lands, by the order of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:54–60; Joshua 24:33; Joshua 21:8–19"> And the heritage came out for the families of the Kohathites: the children of Aaron the priest, who were of the Levites, were given thirteen towns from the tribes of Judah, Simeon, and Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 21:20–26; Exodus 6:16–25; 1 Chronicles 6:66–70"> The rest of the children of Kohath by their families were given ten towns from the tribes of Ephraim and Dan and the half-tribe of Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 21:27–33; 1 Chronicles 6:71–76; Exodus 6:16–17"> The children of Gershon by their families were given thirteen towns from the tribes of Issachar and Asher and Naphtali and the half-tribe of Manasseh which was in Bashan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 21:34–40; 1 Chronicles 6:77–81; Exodus 6:19"> The children of Merari by their families were given twelve towns from the tribes of Reuben and Gad and Zebulun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 21:3; Joshua 18:6; Numbers 33:54"> All these towns with their grass-lands the children of Israel gave by the decision of the Lord to the Levites, as the Lord had given orders by Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:65; Joshua 21:13–18"> From the tribes of the children of Judah and the children of Simeon they gave these towns, listed here by name:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 21:4; Numbers 3:27; Exodus 6:20–26"> These were for the children of Aaron among the families of the Kohathites, of the children of Levi: for they came first in the distribution.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:55; Luke 1:39; 2 Samuel 5:1–5"> They gave them Kiriath-arba, the town of Arba, the father of Anak, (which is Hebron) in the hill-country of Judah, with its grass-lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 14:13–15; 1 Chronicles 6:55–57"> But the open country round the town, and its unwalled places, they gave to Caleb, the son of Jephunneh, as his property.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 15:42; Joshua 10:29; Joshua 15:54"> And to the children of Aaron the priest they gave Hebron with its grass-lands, the town where the taker of life might be safe, and Libnah with its grass-lands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 15:48; Joshua 15:50; 1 Samuel 30:27–28"> And Jattir with its grass-lands, and Eshtemoa with its grass-lands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 15:49; Joshua 15:51; Joshua 12:13"> And Holon with its grass-lands, and Debir with its grass-lands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 15:10; 1 Chronicles 6:59; Joshua 15:55"> And Ain, and Juttah, and Beth-shemesh, with their grass-lands; nine towns from those two tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 18:24–25; Joshua 9:3; 1 Chronicles 6:60"> And from the tribe of Benjamin they gave Gibeon and Geba with their grass-lands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 10:30; 1 Chronicles 6:60; Jeremiah 1:1"> Anathoth and Almon with their grass-lands, four towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Thirteen towns with their grass-lands were given to the children of Aaron, the priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:66; Joshua 21:5"> The rest of the families of the children of Kohath, the Levites, were given towns from the tribe of Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 20:7; Judges 9:1; 1 Kings 12:1"> And they gave them Shechem with its grass-lands in the hill-country of Ephraim, the town where the taker of life might be safe, and Gezer with its grass-lands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:68; Joshua 16:3; Joshua 18:13–14"> And Kibzaim and Beth-horon with their grass-lands, four towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 19:44–45"> And from the tribe of Dan, Elteke and Gibbethon with their grass-lands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 19:42; Joshua 10:12; 1 Chronicles 6:69"> Aijalon and Gath-rimmon with their grass-lands, four towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 17:11; Judges 5:19"> And from the half-tribe of Manasseh, Taanach and Gath-rimmon with their grass-lands, two towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> All the towns of the rest of the families of the children of Kohath were ten with their grass-lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:71; Deuteronomy 1:4; Joshua 20:8"> And to the children of Gershon, of the families of the Levites, they gave from the half-tribe of Manasseh, Golan in Bashan with its grass-lands, the town where the taker of life might be safe, and Ashtaroth with its grass-lands, two towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 19:12; 1 Chronicles 6:72–73"> And from the tribe of Issachar, Kishion and Daberath with their grass-lands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 10:23; Joshua 12:11; Joshua 10:3"> Jarmuth and En-gannim with their grass-lands, four towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:74–75; Joshua 19:25–28"> And from the tribe of Asher, Mishal and Abdon, with their grass-lands:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 18:21; 1 Chronicles 6:75; Judges 1:31"> Helkath and Rehob with their grass-lands, four towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:76; Joshua 20:7; Joshua 19:37"> And from the tribe of Naphtali, Kedesh in Galilee with its grass-lands, the town where the taker of life might be safe, and Hammoth-dor and Kartan with their grass-lands, three towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> All the towns of the Gershonites with their families were thirteen with their grass-lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:77; Joshua 19:11; Joshua 21:7"> And to the rest of the Levites, that is, the families of the children of Merari, they gave from the tribe of Zebulun, Jokneam and Kartah with their grass-lands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Dimnah and Nahalal with their grass-lands, four towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Joshua 20:8; Deuteronomy 4:43; Numbers 21:23"> And from the tribe of Reuben, Bezer and Jahaz with their grass-lands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Kedemoth and Mephaath with their grass-lands, four towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 32:2; 1 Kings 22:3; 1 Chronicles 6:80"> And from the tribe of Gad, Ramoth in Gilead, the town where the taker of life might be safe, and Mahanaim with their grass-lands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 16:8–9; Numbers 32:35; Numbers 32:1"> Heshbon and Jazer with their grass-lands, four towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> All these towns were given to the children of Merari by their families, that is, the rest of the families of the Levites; and their heritage was twelve towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 35:1–8; Deuteronomy 33:10; Genesis 49:7"> All the towns of the Levites, among the heritage of the children of Israel, were forty-eight towns with their grass-lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Every one of these towns had grass-lands round it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Genesis 13:15; Psalms 106:42–45; Exodus 23:27–31"> So the Lord gave to Israel all the land which he gave by oath to their fathers; so it became their heritage and their living-place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Joshua 11:23; Hebrews 4:9; Deuteronomy 31:3–5"> And the Lord gave them peace on every side, as he had said to their fathers: all those who were against them gave way before them, for the Lord gave them all up into their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Kings 8:56; Joshua 23:14–15; Numbers 23:19"> The Lord kept faith with the house of Israel about all the good which he said he would do for them, and all his words came true.</span>
