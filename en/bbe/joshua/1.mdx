<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:9; Deuteronomy 1:38; Deuteronomy 31:23"> Now after the death of Moses, the servant of the Lord, the word of the Lord came to Joshua, the son of Nun, Moses' helper, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:28; Deuteronomy 31:7; Numbers 27:16–21"> Moses my servant is dead; so now get up! Go over Jordan, you and all this people, into the land which I am giving to them, to the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:24; Joshua 14:9; Titus 1:2"> Every place on which you put your foot I have given to you, as I said to Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 23:31; Genesis 15:18–21; Deuteronomy 3:25"> From the waste land and this mountain Lebanon, as far as the great river, the river Euphrates, and all the land of the Hittites to the Great Sea, in the west, will be your country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Deuteronomy 31:6–8; Matthew 28:20"> While you are living, all will give way before you: as I was with Moses, so I will be with you; I will not take away my help from you or give you up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 10:19; Ephesians 6:10; Joshua 1:9"> Take heart and be strong; for you will give to this people for their heritage the land which I gave by an oath to their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:13; Deuteronomy 5:32; Joshua 1:8"> Only take heart and be very strong; take care to do all the law which Moses my servant gave you, not turning from it to the right hand or to the left, so that you may do well in all your undertakings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 119:15; Psalms 19:14; Colossians 3:16"> Let this book of the law be ever on your lips and in your thoughts day and night, so that you may keep with care everything in it; then a blessing will be on all your way, and you will do well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 28:15; Jeremiah 1:7–8; Joshua 1:6–7"> Have I not given you your orders? Take heart and be strong; have no fear and do not be troubled; for the Lord your God is with you wherever you go,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then Joshua gave their orders to those who were in authority over the people, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:1; Hosea 6:2; 2 Kings 20:5"> Go through the tents and give orders to the people, saying, Get ready a store of food; for in three days you are to go over this river Jordan and take for your heritage the land which the Lord your God is giving you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 32:20–22"> And to the Reubenites and the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh, Joshua said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 32:20–28; Deuteronomy 3:18–20; Joshua 22:1–4"> Keep in mind what Moses, the servant of the Lord, said to you, The Lord your God is sending you rest and will give you this land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:8; Exodus 13:18; Revelation 17:4"> Your wives, your little ones, and your cattle will be kept here in the land which Moses gave you on this side of Jordan; but you, the fighting-men, are to go over before your brothers, armed, to give them help;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philippians 2:4; Numbers 32:17–22; 1 Corinthians 12:26"> Till the Lord has given your brothers rest, as he has given it to you, and they have taken their heritage in the land which the Lord your God is giving them: then you will go back to the land of your heritage which Moses, the servant of the Lord, gave you on the east side of Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Titus 3:1; Deuteronomy 5:27; 1 Peter 2:13–15"> Then they said to Joshua in answer, Whatever you say to us we will do, and wherever you send us we will go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; 1 Kings 1:37; Joshua 1:9"> As we gave attention to Moses in all things, so we will give attention to you: and may the Lord your God be with you as he was with Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 6:10; Joshua 1:9; Ezra 10:4"> Whoever goes against your orders, and does not give attention to all your words, will be put to death: only take heart and be strong.</span>
