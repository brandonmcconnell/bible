<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 4:2; Mark 3:14–19"> So Joshua sent for the twelve men, whom he had ready, one man out of every tribe of the children of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And he said to them, Go over before the ark of the Lord your God into the middle of Jordan, and let every one of you take up a stone on his back, one for every tribe of the children of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 13:14; Joshua 4:21; Psalms 44:1"> So that this may be a sign among you; when your children say to you in time to come, What is the reason for these stones?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 12:14; Numbers 16:40; Exodus 28:12"> Then you will say to them, Because the waters of Jordan were cut off before the ark of the Lord's agreement; when it went over Jordan the waters of Jordan were cut off: and these stones will be a sign for the children of Israel, keeping it in their memory for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 4:2–5; Joshua 1:16–18; Joshua 4:20"> So the children of Israel did as Joshua gave them orders, and took twelve stones from the middle of Jordan, as the Lord had said to Joshua, one for every tribe of the children of Israel; these they took across with them to their night's resting-place and put them down there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:12; Judges 1:26; 2 Chronicles 5:9"> And Joshua put up twelve stones in the middle of Jordan, where the feet of the priests who took up the ark of the agreement had been placed: and there they are to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 3:13; Psalms 119:60; Proverbs 27:1"> For the priests who took up the ark kept there in the middle of Jordan till all the orders given to Joshua by Moses from the Lord had been done: then the people went over quickly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 3:8; Joshua 4:18; Joshua 3:17"> And when all the people had come to the other side, the ark of the Lord went over, and the priests, before the eyes of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 32:20–32; Joshua 1:14"> And the children of Reuben and the children of Gad and the half-tribe of Manasseh went over armed before the children of Israel as Moses had said to them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 6:11; Jeremiah 39:5; Jeremiah 52:8"> About forty thousand armed for war went over before the Lord to the fight, to the lowlands of Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 3:7; 2 Chronicles 30:12; 1 Kings 3:28"> That day the Lord made Joshua great in the eyes of all Israel; and all the days of his life they went in fear of him, as they had gone in fear of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then the Lord said to Joshua,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 11:19; Exodus 25:16–22; Joshua 3:3–6"> Give orders to the priests who take up the ark of witness, to come up out of Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 8:16–18; Acts 16:35–39; Acts 16:23"> So Joshua gave orders to the priests, saying, Come up now out of Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 3:15; 1 Chronicles 12:15; Joshua 3:13"> And when the priests who took up the ark of the Lord's agreement came up out of Jordan and their feet came out on to dry land, the waters of Jordan went back to their place, overflowing its edges as before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 5:9; Micah 6:5; Exodus 12:2–3"> So on the tenth day of the first month the people came up out of Jordan, and put up their tents in Gilgal, on the east side of Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 4:3; Joshua 4:8"> And the twelve stones which they took out of Jordan, Joshua put up in Gilgal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 4:6; Psalms 145:4–7; Psalms 105:2–5"> And he said to the children of Israel, When your children say to their fathers in time to come, What is the reason for these stones?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 3:17; Isaiah 51:10; Isaiah 44:27"> Then give your children the story, and say, Israel came over this river Jordan on dry land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Isaiah 43:16; Isaiah 63:12–14"> For the Lord your God made the waters of Jordan dry before you till you had gone across, as he did to the Red Sea, drying it up before us till we had gone across:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 106:8; Psalms 89:13; 1 Kings 8:42–43"> So that all the peoples of the earth may see that the hand of the Lord is strong; and that they may go in fear of the Lord your God for ever.</span>
