<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 11:31; James 2:25; Matthew 1:5"> Then Joshua, the son of Nun, sent two men from Shittim secretly, with the purpose of searching out the land, and Jericho. So they went and came to the house of a loose woman of the town, named Rahab, where they took their rest for the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 43:13; Daniel 4:35; Proverbs 21:30"> And it was said to the king of Jericho, See, some men have come here tonight from the children of Israel with the purpose of searching out the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:3; Genesis 42:9–12; Acts 12:6"> Then the king of Jericho sent to Rahab, saying, Send out the men who have come to you and are in your house; for they have come with the purpose of searching out all the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 6:19; 2 Samuel 17:19–20; 2 Samuel 16:18–19"> And the woman took the two men and put them in a secret place; then she said, Yes, the men came to me, but I had no idea where they came from;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:20; Ezekiel 47:12; Revelation 21:25"> And when it was the time for shutting the doors at dark, they went out; I have no idea where the men went: but if you go after them quickly, you will overtake them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 11:23; James 2:25; Colossians 3:3"> But she had taken them up to the roof, covering them with the stems of flax which she had put out in order there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 3:28; Joshua 2:5; Judges 12:5"> So the men went after them on the road to Jordan as far as the river-crossing: and when they had gone out after them, the door into the town was shut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And before the men went to rest, she came up to them on the roof,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 23:27; Deuteronomy 2:25; Joshua 2:11"> And said to them, It is clear to me that the Lord has given you the land, and that the fear of you has come on us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 21:21–35; Exodus 15:14–16; Exodus 14:21–31"> For we have had news of how the Lord made the Red Sea dry before you when you came out of Egypt; and what you did to the two kings of the Amorites, on the other side of Jordan, to Sihon and Og, whom you gave up to the curse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:39; Joshua 5:1; Joshua 7:5"> And because of this news, our hearts became like water, and there was no more spirit in any of us because of you; for the Lord your God is God in heaven on high and here on earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 2:18; Joshua 9:18–20; 2 Timothy 1:16–18"> So now, will you give me your oath by the Lord, that, because I have been kind to you, you will be kind to my father's house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And that you will keep safe my father and mother and my brothers and sisters and all they have, so that death may not come on us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; Judges 1:24–25; Genesis 24:49"> And the men said to her, Our life for yours if you keep our business secret; and when the Lord has given us the land, we will keep faith and be kind to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 9:25; 2 Corinthians 11:33; Joshua 6:20"> Then she let them down from the window by a cord, for the house where she was living was on the town wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 2:25; 1 Samuel 23:14; Psalms 11:1"> And she said to them, Get away into the hill-country, or the men who have gone after you will overtake you; keep yourselves safe there for three days, till the searchers have come back, and then go on your way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 30:2; 2 Samuel 21:7; Genesis 24:3–8"> And the men said to her, We will only be responsible for this oath which you have made us take,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 6:23; Joshua 2:21; Joshua 2:12–13"> If, when we come into the land, you put this cord of bright red thread in the window from which you let us down; and get your father and mother and your brothers and all your family into the house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 27:31; 1 Kings 2:36–42; Exodus 12:13"> Then if anyone goes out of your house into the street, his blood will be on his head, we will not be responsible; but if any damage comes to anyone in the house, his blood will be on our heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 11:13"> But if you say anything about our business here, then we will be free from the oath you have made us take.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 2:5; Joshua 2:18; Matthew 7:24"> And she said, Let it be as you say. Then she sent them away, and they went; and she put the bright red cord in the window.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:10–12; 2 Samuel 17:20; Psalms 32:6–7"> And they went into the hill-country and were there three days, till the men who had gone after them had come back; and those who went after them were searching for them everywhere without coming across them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Then the two men came down from the hill-country and went over and came back to Joshua, the son of Nun; and they gave him a complete account of what had taken place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 23:31; Joshua 1:8; Joshua 2:9–11"> And they said to Joshua, Truly, the Lord has given all the land into our hands; and all the people of the land have become like water because of us.</span>
