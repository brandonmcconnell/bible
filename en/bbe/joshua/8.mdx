<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Deuteronomy 7:18; Deuteronomy 1:21"> Then the Lord said to Joshua, Have no fear and do not be troubled: take with you all the fighting-men and go up against Ai: for I have given into your hands the king of Ai and his people and his town and his land:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Joshua 6:21; 2 Chronicles 20:22"> And you are to do to Ai and its king as you did to Jericho and its king: but their goods and their cattle you may take for yourselves: let a secret force be stationed to make a surprise attack on the town from the back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 24:39; Matthew 25:6; 1 Thessalonians 5:2"> So Joshua and the fighting-men got ready to go up against Ai; and Joshua took thirty thousand men of war, and sent them out by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 20:29; Joshua 8:16; Judges 20:36"> And he gave them their orders, saying, Go and take up your position secretly at the back of the town: do not go very far away, and let all of you be ready:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 7:5; Matthew 10:16; Judges 20:31–33"> And I and all the people with me will come near the town, and when they come out against us as they did before, we will go in flight from them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 20:32; Exodus 15:9; Ecclesiastes 8:11"> And they will come out after us, till we have got them away from the town; for they will say, They have gone in flight from us as before; so we will go in flight before them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 21:30–31; 2 Kings 5:1; Joshua 8:1"> Then you will get up from your secret position and take the town, for the Lord your God will give it up into your hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:28; Joshua 1:16; Joshua 1:9"> And when you have taken the town, put fire to it, as the Lord has said: see, I have given you your orders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 7:2; Genesis 12:8; Ezra 2:28"> So Joshua sent them out: and they took up a secret position between Beth-el and Ai, on the west side of Ai: but Joshua kept with the people that night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 6:12; Psalms 119:60; Genesis 22:3"> And early in the morning Joshua got up, and put the people in order, and he and the chiefs of Israel went up before the people to Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And all the fighting-men who were with him went up and came near the town, and took up a position on the north side of Ai facing the town, with a valley between him and the town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 8:2–3"> And taking about five thousand men, he put them in position for a surprise attack on the west side of Ai, between Beth-el and Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 8:12; Joshua 8:8"> So all the people were in their places, the army on the north side of the town and the secret force on the west; and that night Joshua went down into the valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 24:39; Judges 20:34–36; Deuteronomy 1:1"> Now when the king of Ai saw it, he got up quickly and went out to war against Israel, he and all his people, to the slope going down to the valley; but he had no idea that a secret force was waiting at the back of the town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 18:12; Joshua 16:1; Judges 20:36"> Then Joshua and all Israel, acting as if they were overcome before them, went in flight by way of the waste land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 20:31; Judges 20:36–39; Joshua 8:5–6"> And all the people in Ai came together to go after them; and they went after Joshua, moving away from the town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 5:13; Joshua 8:3; Isaiah 19:11–13"> There was not a man in Ai and Beth-el who did not go out after Israel; and the town was open and unwatched while they went after Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 8:26; Job 15:25; 1 Samuel 17:41"> And the Lord said to Joshua, Let your spear be stretched out against Ai; for I will give it into your hands. So Joshua took up his spear, stretching it out in the direction of the town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Then the secret force came quickly from their place, and running forward when they saw his hand stretched out, went into the town and took it, and put fire to it straight away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 76:5; Job 11:20; Genesis 19:28"> Then the men of Ai, looking back, saw the smoke of the town going up to heaven, and were unable to go this way or that: and the people who had gone in flight to the waste land were turned back on those who were coming after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And when Joshua and all Israel saw that the town had been taken by the surprise attack, and that the smoke of the town had gone up, turning round they overcame the men of Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Joshua 10:28; Joshua 6:21"> Then the other force came out of the town against them, so that they were being attacked on this side and on that: and Israel overcame them and let not one of them get away with his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:8; Joshua 10:17; Revelation 19:20"> But the king of Ai they made prisoner, and took him to Joshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 11:10–14; Joshua 10:30–41; Numbers 21:24"> Then, after the destruction of all the people of Ai in the field and in the waste land where they went after them, and when all the people had been put to death without mercy, all Israel went back to Ai, and put to death all who were in it without mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:16–18"> On that day twelve thousand were put to death, men and women, all the people of Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 17:11–12; Joshua 8:18"> For Joshua did not take back his hand with the outstretched spear till the destruction of the people of Ai was complete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 8:2; Numbers 31:26; Psalms 50:10"> But the cattle and the goods from that town, Israel took for themselves, as the Lord had given orders to Joshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:16; Jeremiah 49:2; 2 Kings 19:25"> So Joshua gave Ai to the flames, and made it a waste mass of stones for ever, as it is to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 7:26; 2 Samuel 18:17; Deuteronomy 21:22–23"> And he put the king of Ai to death, hanging him on a tree till evening: and when the sun went down, Joshua gave them orders to take his body down from the tree, and put it in the public place of the town, covering it with a great mass of stones, which is there to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:4–6; Exodus 20:24; Genesis 12:7–8"> Then Joshua put up an altar to the Lord, the God of Israel, in Mount Ebal,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:5–7; Exodus 20:24–25; Joshua 8:34–35"> In the way ordered by Moses, the servant of the Lord, as it is recorded in the book of the law of Moses, an altar of uncut stones, untouched by any iron instrument: and on it they made burned offerings and peace-offerings to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:2–3; Deuteronomy 27:8"> And he made there on the stones a copy of the law of Moses, writing it before the eyes of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:29; Deuteronomy 31:12; Deuteronomy 31:9"> And all Israel, those who were Israelites by birth, as well as the men from other lands living with them, and their responsible men and their overseers and judges, took their places round the ark, in front of the priests, the Levites, whose work it was to take up the ark of the Lord's agreement; half of them were stationed in front of Mount Gerizim and half in front of Mount Ebal, in agreement with the orders for the blessing of the children of Israel which Moses, the servant of the Lord, had given.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:2–3; Nehemiah 13:1; Deuteronomy 31:10–12"> And after, he gave them all the words of the law, the blessing and the curse, as it is all recorded in the book of the law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:12; Nehemiah 8:2; Jeremiah 26:2"> Reading to all the meeting of Israel, with the women and the children and the men from other lands who were living among them, every word of the orders which Moses had given.</span>
