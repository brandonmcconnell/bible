<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 20:18; Numbers 27:21; Judges 20:28"> Now after the death of Joshua, the children of Israel made request to the Lord, saying, Who is to go up first to make war for us against the Canaanites?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 5:5; Hebrews 7:14; Numbers 2:3"> And the Lord said, Judah is to go up: see, I have given the land into his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:11; Joshua 19:1; Judges 1:17"> Then Judah said to Simeon his brother, Come up with me into my heritage, so that we may make war against the Canaanites; and I will then go with you into your heritage. So Simeon went with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:8; Exodus 23:28–29; Joshua 11:6–8"> And Judah went up; and the Lord gave the Canaanites and the Perizzites into their hands; and they overcame ten thousand of them in Bezek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And they came across Adoni-zedek, and made war on him; and they overcame the Canaanites and the Perizzites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> But Adoni-zedek went in flight; and they went after him and overtook him, and had his thumbs and his great toes cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:33; Revelation 13:10; Luke 6:37–38"> And Adoni-zedek said, Seventy kings, whose thumbs and great toes had been cut off, got broken meat under my table: as I have done, so has God done to me in full. And they took him to Jerusalem, and he came to his end there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 15:63; Judges 1:21"> Then the children of Judah made an attack on Jerusalem, and took it, burning down the town after they had put its people to the sword without mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 15:13–20; Joshua 11:21; Joshua 10:36"> After that the children of Judah went down to make war on the Canaanites living in the hill-country and in the south and in the lowlands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 13:22; Joshua 14:15; Jeremiah 9:23"> And Caleb went against the Canaanites of Hebron: (now in earlier times Hebron was named Kiriath-arba:) and he put Sheshai and Ahiman and Talmai to the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 15:15; Joshua 10:38–39"> And from there he went up against the people of Debir. (Now the name of Debir in earlier times was Kiriath-sepher.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 15:16–17; 1 Samuel 17:25; 1 Samuel 18:23"> And Caleb said, I will give Achsah, my daughter, as wife to the man who overcomes Kiriath-sepher and takes it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 3:9"> And Othniel, the son of Kenaz, Caleb's younger brother, took it; so he gave him his daughter Achsah for his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 15:18–19"> Now when she came to him, he put into her mind the idea of requesting a field from her father: and she got down from her ass; and Caleb said to her, What is it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:18; 2 Corinthians 9:5; 1 Peter 3:9"> And she said to him, Give me a blessing; because you have put me in a dry south-land, now give me springs of water. So Caleb gave her the higher spring and the lower spring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 10:29–32; Judges 4:11; 1 Samuel 15:6"> Now Hobab the Kenite, Moses' father-in-law, had come up out of the town of palm-trees, with the children of Judah, into the waste land of Arad; and he went and was living among the Amalekites;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 21:3; Judges 1:3; Joshua 19:4"> And Judah went with Simeon, his brother, and overcame the Canaanites living in Zephath, and put it under the curse; and he gave the town the name of Hormah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 11:22; Judges 3:3; Joshua 15:45–47"> Then Judah took Gaza and its limit, and Ashkelon and its limit, and Ekron and its limit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Judges 1:2; Psalms 60:12"> And the Lord was with Judah; and he took the hill-country for his heritage; but he was unable to make the people of the valley go out, for they had war-carriages of iron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 1:10; Joshua 15:13–14; Numbers 14:24"> And they gave Hebron to Caleb, as Moses had said; and he took the land of the three sons of Anak, driving them out from there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 15:63; Judges 19:10–12; Judges 1:8"> And the children of Judah did not make the Jebusites who were living in Jerusalem go out; the Jebusites are still living with the children of Benjamin in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 1:19; 2 Kings 18:7; Numbers 1:32"> And the family of Joseph went up against Beth-el, and the Lord was with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 28:19; Genesis 35:6; Genesis 48:3"> So they sent men to make a search round Beth-el. (Now the name of the town in earlier times was Luz.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 2:12–14; 1 Samuel 30:15"> And the watchers saw a man coming out of the town, and said to him, If you will make clear to us the way into the town, we will be kind to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 6:22–25"> So he made clear to them the way into the town, and they put it to the sword; but they let the man and all his family get away safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 7:6; 2 Chronicles 1:17"> And he went into the land of the Hittites, building a town there and naming it Luz: which is its name to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 17:11–13; Jeremiah 48:10; Psalms 106:34–35"> And Manasseh did not take away the land of the people of Beth-shean and its daughter-towns, or of Taanach and its daughter-towns, or of the people of Dor and its daughter-towns, or of the people of Ibleam and its daughter-towns, or of the people of Megiddo and its daughter-towns, driving them out; but the Canaanites would go on living in that land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And whenever Israel became strong, they put the Canaanites to forced work, without driving them out completely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 16:10; 1 Kings 9:16"> And Ephraim did not make the Canaanites who were living in Gezer go out; but the Canaanites went on living in Gezer among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 19:15"> Zebulun did not make the people of Kitron or the people of Nahalol go out; but the Canaanites went on living among them and were put to forced work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 19:24–30"> And Asher did not take the land of the people of Acco, or Zidon, or Ahlab, or Achzib, or Helbah, or Aphik, or Rehob, driving them out;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 106:34–35"> But the Asherites went on living among the Canaanites, the people of the land, without driving them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 19:32–38; Judges 1:30; Psalms 18:24"> Naphtali did not take the land of the people of Beth-shemesh or of Beth-anath, driving them out; but he was living among the Canaanites in the land; however, the people of Beth-shemesh and Beth-anath were put to forced work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Joshua 19:47; Judges 18:1"> And the children of Dan were forced into the hill-country by the Amorites, who would not let them come down into the valley;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Joshua 19:42; Judges 12:12; Joshua 10:12"> For the Amorites would go on living in Mount Heres, in Aijalon, and in Shaalbim; but the children of Joseph became stronger than they, and put them to forced work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Numbers 34:4; Joshua 15:2–3"> And the limit of the Edomites went from the slope of Akrabbim from Sela and up.</span>
