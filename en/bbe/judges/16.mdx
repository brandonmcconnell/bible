<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Psalms 107:16; Isaiah 63:1–5"> And Samson was there till the middle of the night; then he got up, and took a grip on the doors of the town, pulling them up, together with their two supports and their locks, and put them on his back and took them up to the top of the hill in front of Hebron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:6; Proverbs 22:14; Nehemiah 13:26"> Now after this, he was in love with a woman in the valley of Sorek, named Delilah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 13:3; Judges 14:15; 1 Timothy 6:9–10"> And the chiefs of the Philistines came up to her, and said to her, Make use of your power over him and see what is the secret of his great strength, and how we may get the better of him, and put bands on him, so that we may make him feeble; and every one of us will give you eleven hundred shekels of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 7:21; Proverbs 6:26; Proverbs 22:14"> So Delilah said to Samson, Make clear to me now what is the secret of your great strength, and how you may be put in bands and made feeble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:2–3; Romans 3:8; 1 Samuel 27:10"> And Samson said to her, If seven new bow-cords which have never been made dry are knotted round me, I will become feeble and will be like any other man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26"> So the chiefs of the Philistines gave her seven new bow-cords which had never been made dry, and she had them tightly knotted round him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 58:9"> Now she had men waiting secretly in the inner room; and she said to him, The Philistines are on you, Samson. And the cords were broken by him as a twist of thread is broken when touched by a flame. So the secret of his strength did not come to light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 16:13; Luke 22:48; Judges 16:7"> Then Delilah said to Samson, See, you have been making sport of me with false words; now, say truly how may you be put in bands?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 13:5; Ephesians 4:25; Proverbs 13:3"> And he said to her, If they only put round me new thick cords which have never been used, then I will become feeble and will be like any other man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> So Delilah took new thick cords, knotting them tightly round him, and said to him, The Philistines are on you, Samson. And men were waiting secretly in the inner room. And the cords were broken off his arms like threads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Then Delilah said to Samson, Up to now you have made sport of me with false words; now say truly, how may you be put in bands? And he said to her, If you get the seven twists of my hair worked into the cloth you are making and fixed with the pin, I will become feeble and will be like any other man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 106:43; Ezra 9:13–14"> So while he was sleeping she got the seven twists of his hair worked into her cloth and fixed with the pin, and said to him, The Philistines are on you, Samson. Then awaking from his sleep, he got up quickly, pulling up cloth and machine together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 14:16; 1 John 2:15–16; John 14:21–24"> And she said to him, Why do you say you are my lover when your heart is not mine? Three times you have made sport of me, and have not made clear to me the secret of your great strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 7:21–23; Luke 18:5; Mark 14:24"> So day after day she gave him no peace, for ever questioning him till his soul was troubled to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Micah 7:5; Judges 13:5; Proverbs 12:23"> And opening all his heart to her, he said to her, My head has never been touched by a blade, for I have been separate to God from the day of my birth: if my hair is cut off, then my strength will go from me and I will become feeble, and will be like any other man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 26:15; Judges 16:5; 1 Timothy 6:10"> And when Delilah saw that he had let her see into his heart, she sent word to the chiefs of the Philistines saying, Come up this time, for he has let out all his heart to me. Then the chiefs of the Philistines came to her, with the money in their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 7:26–27; Proverbs 7:21–23; Ecclesiastes 7:26"> And she made him go to sleep on her knees; and she sent for a man and had his seven twists of hair cut off; and while it was being done he became feeble and his strength went from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 14:42–43; 1 Samuel 16:14; Joshua 7:12"> Then she said, The Philistines are on you, Samson. And awaking from his sleep, he said, I will go out as at other times, shaking myself free. But he was not conscious that the Lord had gone from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 2:19; Proverbs 14:14; Isaiah 47:2"> So the Philistines took him and put out his eyes; then they took him down to Gaza, and, chaining him with bands of brass, put him to work crushing grain in the prison-house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 107:13–14; Psalms 106:44–45; Leviticus 26:44"> But the growth of his hair was starting again after it had been cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:2–5; Proverbs 24:17; Psalms 35:15–16"> And the chiefs of the Philistines came together to make a great offering to Dagon their god, and to be glad; for they said, Our god has given into our hands Samson our hater.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 5:4; Daniel 5:23; Judges 15:16"> And when the people saw him, they gave praise to their god; for they said, Our god has given into our hands the one who was fighting against us, who made our country waste, and who put great numbers of us to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:28; Judges 19:6; Judges 9:27"> Now when their hearts were full of joy, they said, Send for Samson to make sport for us. And they sent for Samson out of the prison-house, and he made sport before them; and they put him between the pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And Samson said to the boy who took him by the hand, Let me put my hand on the pillars supporting the house, so that I may put my back against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:8; 2 Samuel 11:2; Joshua 2:8"> Now the house was full of men and women; and all the lords of the Philistines were there; and about three thousand men and women were on the roof, looking on while Samson made sport.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:15; Lamentations 3:31–32; Psalms 143:12"> And Samson, crying out to the Lord, said, O Lord God, do have me now in mind, and do make me strong only this once, O God, so that I may take one last payment from the Philistines for my two eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Then Samson put his arms round the two middle pillars supporting the house, putting his weight on them, on one with his right hand and on the other with his left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Philippians 2:8; Matthew 16:25; Matthew 24:38–39"> And Samson said, Let death overtake me with the Philistines. And he put out all his strength, and the house came down on the chiefs and on all the people who were in it. So the dead whom he sent to destruction by his death were more than all those on whom he had sent destruction in his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 15:20; Judges 13:25; Judges 13:2"> Then his brothers and his father's people came down and took him up and put his body to rest in the earth between Zorah and Eshtaol in the resting-place of Manoah his father. And he had been judge of Israel for twenty years.</span>
