<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 18:2; Joshua 17:14–18; Judges 10:1"> Now there was a man of the hill-country of Ephraim named Micah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:25; Ruth 3:10; 1 Samuel 23:21"> And he said to his mother, The eleven hundred shekels of silver which were taken from you, about which you took an oath and said in my hearing, I have given this silver to the Lord from my hand for myself, to make a pictured image and a metal image: see, I have the silver, for I took it: so now I will give it back to you. And his mother said, May the blessing of the Lord be on my son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 20:4; Exodus 20:23; Leviticus 19:4"> And he gave back the eleven hundred shekels of silver to his mother, and his mother said, I have made the silver holy to the Lord from me for my son, to make a pictured image and a metal image.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 46:6–7; Jeremiah 10:9–10"> So he gave the silver back to his mother. Then his mother took two hundred shekels of silver and gave them to a metal-worker who made a pictured image and a metal image from them: and it was in the house of Micah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 18:14; Judges 8:27; Genesis 31:19"> And the man Micah had a house of gods; and he made an ephod and family gods and put one of his sons in the position of priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:8; Proverbs 14:12; Judges 21:25"> In those days there was no king in Israel: every man did as seemed right to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Matthew 2:1; Ruth 1:1–2"> Now there was a young man living in Beth-lehem-judah, of the family of Judah and a Levite, who was not a townsman of the place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:10–11; Judges 17:11; Joshua 24:33"> And he went away from the town of Beth-lehem-judah, looking for somewhere to make his living-place; and on his journey he came to the hill-country of Ephraim, to the house of Micah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Micah said to him, Where do you come from? And he said to him, I am a Levite from Beth-lehem-judah, and I am looking for a living-place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:19; Judges 18:19–20; 1 Timothy 6:10"> Then Micah said to him, Make your living-place with me, and be a father and a priest to me, and I will give you ten shekels of silver a year and your clothing and food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the Levite said he would make his living-place with the man, and he became to him as one of his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 18:30; Numbers 16:8–10; Numbers 16:5"> And Micah gave the position to the Levite, and the young man became his priest, and was in the house of Micah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 10:2–3; Proverbs 14:12; John 16:2"> Then Micah said, Now I am certain that the Lord will do me good, seeing that the Levite has become my priest.</span>
