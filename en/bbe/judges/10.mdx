<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 2:16; Judges 3:9; Joshua 15:48"> Now after Abimelech, Tola, the son of Puah, the son of Dodo, a man of Issachar, became the saviour of Israel; he was living in Shamir in the hill-country of Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> He was judge over Israel for twenty-three years; and at his death his body was put to rest in the earth in Shamir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 32:29; Genesis 31:48"> And after him came Jair the Gileadite, who was judge over Israel for twenty-two years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 32:41; Judges 5:10; Deuteronomy 3:14"> And he had thirty sons, who went on thirty young asses; and they had thirty towns in the land of Gilead, which are named Havvoth-Jair to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And at the death of Jair his body was put to rest in the earth in Kamon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 11:33; 1 Kings 11:7; 2 Kings 23:13"> And again the children of Israel did evil in the eyes of the Lord, worshipping the Baals and Astartes, and the gods of Aram and the gods of Zidon and the gods of Moab and the gods of the children of Ammon and the gods of the Philistines; they gave up the Lord and were servants to him no longer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Deuteronomy 32:16–22; 1 Samuel 12:9–10"> And the wrath of the Lord was burning against Israel, and he gave them up into the hands of the Philistines and into the hands of the children of Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Isaiah 30:13; Judges 10:5"> And that year the children of Israel were crushed under their yoke; for eighteen years all the children of Israel on the other side of Jordan, in the land of the Amorites which is in Gilead, were cruelly crushed down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:1–2; Deuteronomy 28:65; 2 Chronicles 15:5"> And the children of Ammon went over Jordan, to make war against Judah and Benjamin and the house of Ephraim; and Israel was in great trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:10; Psalms 107:28; Psalms 107:13"> Then the children of Israel, crying out to the Lord, said, Great is our sin against you, for we have given up our God and have been servants to the Baals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 3:31; Numbers 21:21–25; Exodus 14:30"> And the Lord said to the children of Israel, Were not the Egyptians and the Amorites and the children of Ammon and the Philistines</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 6:3; 2 Chronicles 26:6–7; Psalms 106:42–43"> And the Zidonians and Amalek and Midian crushing you down, and in answer to your cry did I not give you salvation from their hands?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:13; 1 Chronicles 28:9; Judges 2:12"> But, for all this, you have given me up and have been servants to other gods: so I will be your saviour no longer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Deuteronomy 32:37–38; 1 Kings 18:27–28"> Go, send up your cry for help to the gods of your selection; let them be your saviours in the time of your trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:18; 2 Samuel 15:26; Proverbs 28:13"> And the children of Israel said to the Lord, We are sinners; do to us whatever seems good to you: only give us salvation this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 63:9; Psalms 106:44–45; Ezekiel 18:30–32"> So they put away the strange gods from among them, and became the Lord's servants; and his soul was angry because of the sorrows of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 11:29; Genesis 31:49; Judges 11:11"> Then the children of Ammon came together and put their army in position in Gilead. And the children of Israel came together and put their army in position in Mizpah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 11:11; Judges 12:7; Judges 1:1"> And the people of Israel said to one another, Who will be the first to make an attack on the children of Ammon? We will make him head over all Gilead.</span>
