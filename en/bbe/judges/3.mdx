<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 2:21–22; Deuteronomy 7:22; Proverbs 17:3"> Now these are the nations which the Lord kept in the land for the purpose of testing Israel by them, all those who had had no experience of all the wars of Canaan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 3:5; Genesis 3:7; 1 Corinthians 9:26–27"> Only because of the generations of the children of Israel, for the purpose of teaching them war — only those who up till then had no experience of it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 13:3; Joshua 13:5; Judges 10:7"> The five chiefs of the Philistines, and all the Canaanites and the Zidonians and the Hivites living in Mount Lebanon, from the mountain Baal-hermon as far as Hamath:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 3:1; Judges 2:22; Exodus 15:25"> For the purpose of testing Israel by them, to see if they would give ear to the orders of the Lord, which he had given to their fathers by the hand of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 106:34–38; Genesis 10:15–18; Exodus 3:8"> Now the children of Israel were living among the Canaanites, the Hittites, and the Amorites, and the Perizzites, and the Hivites, and the Jebusites:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:3–4; Exodus 34:16; 1 Kings 11:1–5"> And they took as wives the daughters of these nations and gave their daughters to their sons, and became servants to their gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 34:13; 2 Kings 23:6; 1 Kings 18:19"> And the children of Israel did evil in the eyes of the Lord, and put out of their minds the Lord their God, and became servants to the Baals and the Astartes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Habakkuk 3:7; Judges 4:9"> So the wrath of the Lord was burning against Israel, and he gave them up into the hands of Cushan-rishathaim, king of Mesopotamia; and the children of Israel were his servants for eight years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 3:15; Judges 1:13; Psalms 22:5"> And when the children of Israel made prayer to the Lord, he gave them a saviour, Othniel, the son of Kenaz, Caleb's younger brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 13:25; 1 Samuel 11:6; Judges 14:19"> And the spirit of the Lord came on him and he became judge of Israel, and went out to war, and the Lord gave up Cushan-rishathaim, king of Mesopotamia, into his hands and he overcame him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 5:31; Judges 8:28; Joshua 11:23"> Then for forty years the land had peace, till the death of Othniel, the son of Kenaz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:9; Judges 2:19; Judges 2:14"> Then the children of Israel again did evil in the eyes of the Lord; and the Lord made Eglon, king of Moab, strong against Israel, because they had done evil in the Lord's eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 1:16; Deuteronomy 34:3; Judges 5:14"> And Eglon got together the people of Ammon and Amalek, and they went and overcame Israel and took the town of palm-trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:47–48; Deuteronomy 28:40; Leviticus 26:23–25"> And the children of Israel were servants to Eglon, king of Moab, for eighteen years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 3:9; Psalms 78:34; Isaiah 36:16"> Then when the children of Israel made prayer to the Lord, he gave them a saviour, Ehud, the son of Gera, the Benjamite, a left-handed man; and the children of Israel sent an offering by him to Eglon, king of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Song of Solomon 3:8; Revelation 2:12; Psalms 149:6"> So Ehud made himself a two-edged sword, a cubit long, which he put on at his right side under his robe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:20; Job 15:27; Jeremiah 50:11"> And he took the offering to Eglon, king of Moab, who was a very fat man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And after giving the offering, he sent away the people who had come with the offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 4:20; Judges 3:20; Acts 23:18–19"> But he himself, turning back from the stone images at Gilgal, said, I have something to say to you in secret, O king. And he said, Let there be quiet. Then all those who were waiting before him went out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:7; Amos 3:15; Psalms 29:1"> Then Ehud came in to him while he was seated by himself in his summer-house. And Ehud said, I have a word from God for you. And he got up from his seat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zechariah 13:3; Job 20:25; 1 Samuel 15:33"> And Ehud put out his left hand, and took the sword from his right side, and sent it into his stomach;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And the hand-part went in after the blade, and the fat was joined up over the blade; for he did not take the sword out of his stomach. And he went out into the ...</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Then Ehud went out into the covered way, shutting the doors of the summer-house on him and locking them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:3"> Now when he had gone, the king's servants came, and saw that the doors of the summer-house were locked; and they said, It may be that he is in his summer-house for a private purpose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And they went on waiting till they were shamed, but the doors were still shut; so they took the key, and, opening them, saw their lord stretched out dead on the floor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> But Ehud had got away while they were waiting and had gone past the stone images and got away to Seirah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 6:34; 1 Samuel 13:3; Judges 5:14"> And when he came there, he had a horn sounded in the hill-country of Ephraim, and all the children of Israel went down with him from the hill-country, and he at their head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 12:5; Judges 7:9; Judges 7:15"> And he said to them, Come after me; for the Lord has given the Moabites, your haters, into your hands. So they went down after him and took the crossing-places of Jordan against Moab, and let no one go across.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:15; Judges 3:17; Psalms 17:10"> At that time they put about ten thousand men of Moab to the sword, every strong man and every man of war; not a man got away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 3:11; Judges 5:31"> So Moab was broken that day under the hand of Israel. And for eighty years the land had peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 5:6; 1 Corinthians 1:17; 1 Samuel 17:50"> And after him came Shamgar, the son of Anath, who put to death six hundred Philistines with an ox-stick; and he was another saviour of Israel.</span>
