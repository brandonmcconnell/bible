Now we will put your house on fire over you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 11:12–33"> And Jephthah said to them, I and my people were in danger, and the children of Ammon were very cruel to us, and when I sent for you, you gave me no help against them.</span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:10; Deuteronomy 3:12–17; Proverbs 15:1"> Then Jephthah got together all the men of Gilead and made war on Ephraim; and the men of Gilead overcame Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 3:28; Joshua 22:11; Joshua 2:7"> And the Gileadites took the crossing-places of Jordan against the Ephraimites; and when any of the men of Ephraim who had gone in flight said, let me go over; the men of Gilead said to him, Are you an Ephraimite? And if he said, No;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 69:2; Psalms 69:15; Mark 14:70"> Then they said to him, Now say Shibboleth; and he said Sibboleth, and was not able to say it in the right way; then they took him and put him to death at the crossing-places of Jordan; and at that time forty-two thousand Ephraimites were put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Now Jephthah was judge of Israel for six years. And Jephthah the Gileadite came to his death, and his body was put to rest in his town, Mizpeh of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Micah 5:2; 1 Samuel 16:1; Matthew 2:1"> And after him, Ibzan of Beth-lehem was judge of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 10:4; Judges 12:14"> He had thirty sons, and thirty daughters whom he sent to other places, and he got thirty wives from other places for his sons. And he was judge of Israel for seven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Ibzan came to his death and his body was put to rest at Beth-lehem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And after him, Elon the Zebulonite was judge of Israel; and he was judge of Israel for ten years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 8:13; 1 Chronicles 6:69; Joshua 19:42"> And Elon the Zebulonite came to his death, and his body was put to rest in Aijalon in the land of Zebulun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And after him, Abdon, the son of Hillel, the Pirathonite, was judge of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 5:10; Judges 10:4"> He had forty sons and thirty sons' sons who went on seventy young asses; and he was judge of Israel for eight years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 5:14; Judges 3:13; Judges 3:27"> And Abdon, the son of Hillel, came to his death, and his body was put to rest in Pirathon in the land of Ephraim, in the hill-country of the Amalekites.</span>
