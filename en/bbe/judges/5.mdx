<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 15:1; Revelation 19:1–3; Psalms 18:1"> At that time Deborah and Barak, the son of Abinoam, made this song, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 5:9; 2 Chronicles 17:16; Psalms 110:3"> Because of the flowing hair of the fighters in Israel, because the people gave themselves freely, give praise to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 138:4–5; Psalms 119:46; Psalms 49:1–2"> Give attention, O kings; give ear, O rulers; I, even I, will make a song to the Lord; I will make melody to the Lord, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:2; Psalms 68:7–8; Psalms 77:17"> Lord, when you went out from Seir, moving like an army from the field of Edom, the earth was shaking and the heavens were troubled, and the clouds were dropping water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 19:18; Psalms 97:5; Isaiah 64:1–3"> The mountains were shaking before the Lord, before the Lord, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 33:8; Judges 3:31; Lamentations 1:4"> In the days of Shamgar, the son of Anath, in the days of Jael, the highways were not used, and travellers went by side roads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; 2 Samuel 20:19; Esther 9:19"> Country towns were no more in Israel, &#42;&#42;&#42;were no more, till you, Deborah, came up, till you came up as a mother in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:16–17; Judges 2:12; Judges 2:17"> They had no one to make arms, there were no more armed men in the towns; was there a body-cover or a spear to be seen among forty thousand in Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 5:2; 2 Corinthians 8:3–4; 2 Corinthians 9:5"> Come, you rulers of Israel, you who gave yourselves freely among the people: give praise to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 12:14; Judges 10:4; Psalms 105:2"> Let them give thought to it, who go on white asses, and those who are walking on the road.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Micah 6:5; Judges 5:8; 1 Samuel 12:7"> Give ear to the women laughing by the water-springs; there they will give again the story of the upright acts of the Lord, all the upright acts of his arm in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 4:8; Psalms 68:18; Psalms 57:8"> Awake! awake! Deborah: awake! awake! give a song: Up! Barak, and take prisoner those who took you prisoner, O son of Abinoam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 2:26–27; Romans 8:37; Psalms 75:7"> Then the chiefs went down to the doors; the Lord's people went down among the strong ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 12:15; Numbers 32:39–40; Judges 3:27"> Out of Ephraim they came down into the valley; after you, Benjamin, among your tribesmen; from Machir came down the captains, and from Zebulun those in whose hand is the ruler's rod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 4:14; Proverbs 22:13; Acts 20:13"> Your chiefs, Issachar, were with Deborah; and Naphtali was true to Barak; into the valley they went rushing out at his feet. In Reuben there were divisions, and great searchings of heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 32:24; Numbers 32:1–5; Judges 5:15"> Why did you keep quiet among the sheep, hearing nothing but the watchers piping to the flocks?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 13:25; Joshua 19:24–31; Joshua 13:31"> Gilead was living over Jordan; and Dan was waiting in his ships; Asher kept in his place by the sea's edge, living by his inlets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 4:10; Judges 4:6; Esther 4:16"> It was the people of Zebulun who put their lives in danger, even to death, with Naphtali on the high places of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Judges 5:30; Judges 1:27; 1 Kings 4:12"> The kings came on to the fight, the kings of Canaan were warring; in Taanach by the waters of Megiddo: they took no profit in money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 10:11; 1 Samuel 7:10; Psalms 77:17–18"> The stars from heaven were fighting; from their highways they were fighting against Sisera.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 4:7; Micah 7:10; Psalms 44:5"> The river Kishon took them violently away, stopping their flight, the river Kishon. Give praise, O my soul, to the strength of the Lord!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 20:7; Psalms 147:10–11; Jeremiah 47:4"> Then loudly the feet of the horses were sounding with the stamping, the stamping of their war-horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:10; Judges 21:9–10; 1 Corinthians 16:22"> A curse, a curse on Meroz! said the angel of the Lord. A bitter curse on her townspeople! Because they came not to the help of the Lord, to the help of the Lord among the strong ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 1:42; Luke 1:28; Judges 4:17"> Blessings be on Jael, more than on all women! Blessings greater than on any in the tents!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Judges 4:19–21"> His request was for water, she gave him milk; she put butter before him on a fair plate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:49–51; Judges 4:21; 2 Samuel 20:22"> She put out her hand to the tent-pin, and her right hand to the workman's hammer; and she gave Sisera a blow, crushing his head, wounding and driving through his brow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 52:7; James 2:13; Matthew 7:2"> Bent at her feet he went down, he was stretched out; bent at her feet he went down; where he was bent down, there he went down in death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 4:15; James 5:7; Song of Solomon 8:14"> Looking out from the window she gave a cry, the mother of Sisera was crying out through the window, Why is his carriage so long in coming? When will the noise of his wheels be sounding?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Her wise women gave answer to her, yes, she made answer again to herself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 15:9; 2 Samuel 13:18; Genesis 37:3"> Are they not getting, are they not parting the goods among them: a young girl or two to every man; and to Sisera robes of coloured needlework, worked in fair colours on this side and on that, for the neck of the queen?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 37:6; 2 Samuel 23:4; Psalms 19:4–5"> So may destruction come on all your haters, O Lord; but let your lovers be like the sun going out in his strength. And for forty years the land had peace.</span>
