<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:9; Judges 6:1; Romans 2:6"> And the children of Israel again did evil in the eyes of the Lord; and the Lord gave them into the hands of the Philistines for forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 19:41; Luke 1:7; Joshua 15:33"> Now there was a certain man of Zorah of the family of the Danites, and his name was Manoah; and his wife had never given birth to a child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 1:13; Luke 1:11; Judges 6:11–12"> And the angel of the Lord came to the woman, and said to her, See now! though you have never given birth to children, you will be with child and give birth to a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 1:15; Judges 13:14; Numbers 6:2–3"> Now then take care to have no wine or strong drink and to take no unclean thing for food;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 6:5; 1 Samuel 1:11; 1 Chronicles 18:1"> For you are with child and will give birth to a son; his hair is never to be cut, for the child is to be separate to God from his birth; and he will take up the work of freeing Israel from the hands of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 13:17–18; 1 Samuel 9:6; Matthew 28:3–4"> Then the woman came in, and said to her husband, A man came to me, and his form was like the form of a god, causing great fear; I put no question to him about where he came from, and he did not give me his name;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 13:4"> But he said to me, You are with child and will give birth to a son; and now do not take any wine or strong drink or let anything unclean be your food; for the child will be separate to God from his birth to the day of his death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 9:6; Job 34:32; Proverbs 3:5–6"> Then Manoah made prayer to the Lord, and said, O Lord, let the man of God whom you sent come to us again and make clear to us what we are to do for the child who is to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 65:2; Matthew 7:7–11"> And God gave ear to the voice of Manoah; and the angel of God came to the woman again when she was seated in the field; but her husband Manoah was not with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 4:28–29; John 1:41–42"> So the woman, running quickly, gave her husband the news, saying, I have seen the man who came to me the other day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Manoah got up and went after his wife, and came up to the man and said to him, Are you the man who was talking to this woman? And he said, I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 22:6; Ephesians 6:4; Genesis 18:19"> And Manoah said, Now when your words come true, what is to be the rule for the child and what will be his work?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the angel of the Lord said to Manoah, Let the woman take note of what I have said to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 2:5; Judges 13:4; 2 Thessalonians 3:4"> She is to have nothing which comes from the vine for her food, and let her take no wine or strong drink or anything which is unclean; let her take care to do all I have given her orders to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 6:18–19; Genesis 18:3–5; Judges 13:3"> And Manoah said to the angel of the Lord, Now let us keep you while we make ready a young goat for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 13:23; Judges 6:20; Judges 6:26"> And the angel of the Lord said to Manoah, Though you keep me I will not take of your food; but if you will make a burned offering, let it be offered to the Lord. For it had not come into Manoah's mind that he was the angel of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 32:29"> Then Manoah said to the angel of the Lord, What is your name, so that when your words come true we may give you honour?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 32:29; Isaiah 9:6; Judges 13:6"> But the angel of the Lord said to him, Why are you questioning me about my name, seeing that it is a wonder?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Judges 6:19–21; 1 Kings 18:30–38"> So Manoah took the young goat with its meal offering, offering it on the rock to the Lord, who did strange things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 9:24; 1 Chronicles 21:16; 2 Kings 2:11"> And when the flame went up to heaven from the altar, the angel of the Lord went up in the flame of the altar, while Manoah and his wife were looking on; and they went down on their faces to the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 6:22; Hosea 12:4–5; Judges 13:16"> But the angel of the Lord was seen no more by Manoah and his wife. Then it was clear to Manoah that he was the angel of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 32:30; Deuteronomy 5:26; John 1:18"> And Manoah said to his wife, Death will certainly be our fate, for it is a god whom we have seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 25:14; 1 Corinthians 12:21; John 15:15"> But his wife said to him, If the Lord was purposing our death, he would not have taken our burned offering and our meal offering, or have given us such orders about the child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 1:80; 1 Samuel 3:19; Hebrews 11:32"> So the woman gave birth to a son, and gave him the name Samson; and he became a man and the blessing of the Lord was on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Judges 3:10; Joshua 15:33; John 3:34"> And the spirit of the Lord first came on him in Mahaneh-dan, between Zorah and Eshtaol.</span>
