<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:25; 1 Samuel 8:7–8; Deuteronomy 9:24"> And he said to me, Son of man, I am sending you to the children of Israel, to an uncontrolled nation which has gone against me: they and their fathers have been sinners against me even to this very day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:7; Isaiah 48:4; Jeremiah 5:3"> And the children are hard and stiff-hearted; I am sending you to them: and you are to say to them, These are the words of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:33; Ezekiel 3:27; Matthew 10:12–15"> And they, if they give ear to you or if they do not give ear (for they are an uncontrolled people), will see that there has been a prophet among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:8; Isaiah 51:12; 1 Peter 3:14"> And you, son of man, have no fear of them or of their words, even if sharp thorns are round you and you are living among scorpions: have no fear of their words and do not be overcome by their looks, for they are an uncontrolled people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:28; Ezekiel 3:17; Jeremiah 1:7"> And you are to give them my words, if they give ear to you or if they do not: for they are uncontrolled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 10:9; Jeremiah 15:16; Isaiah 50:5"> But you, son of man, give ear to what I say to you, and do not be uncontrolled like that uncontrolled people: let your mouth be open and take what I give you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:3; Revelation 5:1–5; Daniel 10:10"> And looking, I saw a hand stretched out to me, and I saw the roll of a book in it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 8:13; Isaiah 3:11; Revelation 9:12"> And he put it open before me, and it had writing on the front and on the back; words of grief and sorrow and trouble were recorded in it.</span>
