<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:4; Ezekiel 11:22–23; Ezekiel 43:2–4"> And the glory of the God of Israel had gone up from the winged ones on which it was resting, to the doorstep of the house. <span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 9:4; 2 Corinthians 1:22; Revelation 14:1"> The Lord said to him, Go through the town, through the middle of Jerusalem, and put a mark on the brows of the men who are sorrowing and crying for all the disgusting things which are done in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:11; Ezekiel 8:18; Ezekiel 7:9"> And to these he said in my hearing, Go through the town after him using your axes: do not let your eyes have mercy, and have no pity:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 9:4; Exodus 12:23; Jeremiah 25:29"> Give up to destruction old men and young men and virgins, little children and women: but do not come near any man who has the mark on him: and make a start at my holy place. So they made a start with the old men who were before the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:17; Ezekiel 7:20–22; Lamentations 2:4–7"> And he said to them, Make the house unclean, make the open places full of dead: go forward and send destruction on the town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:13; Numbers 16:45; Joshua 7:6"> Now while they were doing so, and I was untouched, I went down on my face, and crying out, I said, Ah, Lord! will you give all the rest of Israel to destruction in letting loose your wrath on Jerusalem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 22:13; Ezekiel 8:12; Ezekiel 7:23"> Then he said to me, The sin of the children of Israel and Judah is very, very great, and the land is full of blood and the town full of evil ways: for they say, The Lord has gone away from the land, and the Lord does not see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:4; Ezekiel 8:18; Ezekiel 11:21"> And as for me, my eye will not have mercy, and I will have no pity, but I will send the punishment of their ways on their heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zechariah 6:7–8; Revelation 16:17; Revelation 16:2"> Then the man clothed in linen, who had the inkpot at his side, came back and said, I have done what you gave me orders to do.</span>
