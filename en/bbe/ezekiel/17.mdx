<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:49; Matthew 13:35; 1 Corinthians 13:12"> Son of man, give out a dark saying, and make a comparison for the children of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 8:1; Jeremiah 48:40; Ezekiel 17:7"> And say, This is what the Lord has said: A great eagle with great wings, full of long feathers of different colours, came to Lebanon, and took the top of the cedar:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 43:14; Isaiah 47:15; Revelation 18:11–19"> Biting off the highest of its young branches, he took it to the land of Canaan, and put it in a town of traders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 44:4; Deuteronomy 8:7–9; Jeremiah 37:1"> And he took some of the seed of the land, planting it in fertile earth, placing it by great waters; he put it in like a willow-tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:14; Proverbs 16:18–19"> And its growth went on and it became a vine, low and widely stretching, whose branches were turned to him and its roots were under him: so it became a vine, putting out branches and young leaves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:4; Ezekiel 17:15; 2 Kings 24:20"> And there was another eagle with great wings and thick feathers: and now this vine, pushing out its roots to him, sent out its branches in his direction from the bed where it was planted, so that he might give it water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:5–6"> He had it planted in a good field by great waters so that it might put out branches and have fruit and be a strong vine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:10; Jeremiah 32:5; Isaiah 30:1–7"> Say, This is what the Lord has said: Will it do well? will he not have its roots pulled up and its branches cut off, so that all its young leaves may become dry and it may be pulled up by its roots?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 13:15; John 15:6; Matthew 21:19"> And if it is planted will it do well? will it not become quite dry at the touch of the east wind, drying up in the bed where it was planted?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Then the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:19; Ezekiel 17:3; Ezekiel 1:2"> Say now to this uncontrolled people, Are these things not clear to you? Say to them, See, the king of Babylon came to Jerusalem and took its king and its rulers away with him to Babylon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:13; 2 Kings 24:15–17; Ezekiel 17:5"> And he took one of the sons of the king and made an agreement with him; and he put him under an oath, and took away the great men of the land:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:14; Deuteronomy 28:43; 1 Samuel 2:30"> So that the kingdom might be made low with no power of lifting itself up, but might keep his agreement to be his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:3; Jeremiah 38:18; Ezekiel 17:7"> But he went against his authority in sending representatives to Egypt to get from them horses and a great army. Will he do well? will he be safe who does such things? if the agreement is broken will he be safe?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Jeremiah 52:11; Ezekiel 17:18–19"> By my life, says the Lord, truly in the place of the king who made him king, whose oath he put on one side and let his agreement with him be broken, even in Babylon he will come to his death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:7; Ezekiel 4:2; Ezekiel 29:6–7"> And Pharaoh with his strong army and great forces will be no help to him in the war, when they put up earthworks and make strong walls for the cutting off of lives:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:24; 2 Chronicles 30:8; Lamentations 5:6"> For he put his oath on one side in letting the agreement be broken; and though he had given his hand to it, he did all these things; he will not get away safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:11; Jeremiah 5:9; Jeremiah 5:2"> And so the Lord has said, By my life, truly, for my oath which he put on one side, and my agreement which has been broken, I will send punishment on his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Ezekiel 32:3; Jeremiah 2:35"> My net will be stretched out over him, and he will be taken in my cords, and I will send him to Babylon, and there I will be his judge for the wrong which he has done against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 25:5; 2 Kings 25:11; Ezekiel 12:14"> All his best fighting-men will be put to the sword, and the rest will be sent away to every wind: and you will be certain that I the Lord have said it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2; Zechariah 6:12–13; Isaiah 11:1–5"> This is what the Lord has said: Further, I will take the highest top of the cedar and put it in the earth; cutting off from the highest of his young branches a soft one, I will have it planted on a high and great mountain;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 13:32; Ezekiel 31:6; Isaiah 2:2"> It will be planted on the high mountain of Israel: it will put out branches and have fruit and be a fair cedar: under it all birds of every sort will make their living-place, resting in the shade of its branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Amos 9:11; 1 Corinthians 1:27–28; Ezekiel 24:14"> And it will be clear to all the trees of the field that I the Lord have made low the high tree and made high the low tree, drying up the green tree and making the dry tree full of growth; I the Lord have said it and have done it.</span>
