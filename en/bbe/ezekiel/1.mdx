<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 3:16; Ezekiel 11:24; Acts 7:56"> Now it came about in the thirtieth year, in the fourth month, on the fifth day of the month, while I was by the river Chebar among those who had been made prisoners, that the heavens were made open and I saw visions of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 24:12–15; Ezekiel 8:1; Ezekiel 20:1"> On the fifth day of the month, in the fifth year after King Jehoiachin had been made a prisoner,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:14; Ezekiel 3:22; 2 Kings 3:15"> The word of the Lord came to me, Ezekiel the priest, the son of Buzi, in the land of the Chaldaeans by the river Chebar; and the hand of the Lord was on me there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:27; Ezekiel 8:2; Jeremiah 23:19"> And, looking, I saw a storm-wind coming out of the north, a great cloud with flames of fire coming after one another, and a bright light shining round about it and in the heart of it was something coloured like electrum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 4:6–8; Ezekiel 1:26; Revelation 6:6"> And in the heart of it were the forms of four living beings. And this was what they were like; they had the form of a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:14; Revelation 4:7–8; Ezekiel 1:15"> And every one had four faces, and every one of them had four wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 1:15; Daniel 10:6; Psalms 104:4"> And their feet were straight feet; and the under sides of their feet were like the feet of oxen; and they were shining like polished brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:21; Ezekiel 10:11; Isaiah 6:6"> And they had the hands of a man under their wings; the four of them had faces on their four sides.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:22; Ezekiel 10:11; Ezekiel 1:11–12"> They went without turning, every one went straight forward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:14; Revelation 4:7; 1 Corinthians 9:9–10"> As for the form of their faces, they had the face of a man, and the four of them had the face of a lion on the right side, and the four of them had the face of an ox on the left side, and the four of them had the face of an eagle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 6:2; Ezekiel 1:23; Ezekiel 10:19"> And their wings were separate at the top; two of the wings of every one were joined one to another, and two were covering their bodies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:9; Ezekiel 10:22; Hebrews 1:14"> Every one of them went straight forward; wherever the spirit was to go they went; they went on without turning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 4:5; Psalms 104:4; Matthew 28:3"> And between the living beings it was like burning coals of fire, as if flames were going one after the other between the living beings; and the fire was bright, and out of the fire went thunder-flames.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 24:27; Zechariah 4:10; Mark 13:27"> And the living beings went out and came back as quickly as a thunder-flame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 7:9; Ezekiel 10:9; Ezekiel 1:19–21"> Now while I was looking at the four living beings, I saw one wheel on the earth, by the side of the living beings, for the four of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 10:6; Ezekiel 10:9–11; Exodus 39:13"> The form of the wheels and their work was like a beryl; the four of them had the same form and design, and they were like a wheel inside a wheel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:9; Ezekiel 1:12; Isaiah 55:11"> The four of them went straight forward without turning to one side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:12; Revelation 4:6; Revelation 4:8"> And I saw that they had edges, and their edges, even of the four, were full of eyes round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:16; Ezekiel 10:19; Psalms 103:20"> And when the living beings went on, the wheels went by their side; and when the living beings were lifted up from the earth, the wheels were lifted up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:12; Ezekiel 10:17; 1 Corinthians 14:32"> Wherever the spirit was to go they went; and the wheels were lifted up by their side: for the spirit of the living beings was in the wheels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:17; Ezekiel 1:19–20; Romans 8:2"> When these went on, the others went; and when these came to rest, the others came to rest; and when these were lifted up from the earth, the wheels were lifted up by their side: for the spirit of the living beings was in the wheels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:1; Ezekiel 1:26; Revelation 4:6"> And over the heads of the living beings there was the form of an arch, looking like ice, stretched out over their heads on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 4:18; Ezekiel 1:11–12; Psalms 89:7"> Under the arch their wings were straight, one stretched out to another: every one had two wings covering their bodies on this side and two covering their bodies on that side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:2; Daniel 10:6; Revelation 1:15"> And when they went, the sound of their wings was like the sound of great waters to my ears, like the voice of the Ruler of all, a sound like the rushing of an army: when they came to rest they let down their wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:22"> And there was a voice from the top of the arch which was over their heads: when they came to rest they let down their wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:1; Exodus 24:10; Revelation 1:13"> And on the top of the arch which was over their heads was the form of a king's seat, like a sapphire stone; and on the form of the seat was the form of a man seated on it on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:4; Ezekiel 8:2; Hebrews 12:29"> And I saw it coloured like electrum, with the look of fire in it and round it, going up from what seemed to be the middle of his body; and going down from what seemed to be the middle of his body I saw what was like fire, and there was a bright light shining round him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 10:1; Revelation 4:3; Genesis 17:3"> Like the bow in the cloud on a day of rain, so was the light shining round him. And this is what the glory of the Lord was like. And when I saw it I went down on my face, and the voice of one talking came to my ears.</span>
