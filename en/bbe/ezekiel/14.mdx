<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:1; Ezekiel 20:1; 2 Kings 6:32"> Then certain of the responsible men of Israel came to me and took their seats before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Amos 3:7; 1 Kings 14:4"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:7; Ezekiel 7:19; Ezekiel 20:3"> Son of man, these men have taken their false gods into their hearts and put before their faces the sin which is the cause of their fall: am I to give ear when they come to me for directions?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 66:4; Ezekiel 14:7; Isaiah 3:11"> For this cause say to them, These are the words of the Lord: Every man of Israel who has taken his false god into his heart, and put before his face the sin which is the cause of his fall, and comes to the prophet; I the Lord will give him an answer by myself in agreement with the number of his false gods;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 1:4; Zechariah 11:8; Romans 1:28"> So as to take the children of Israel in the thoughts of their hearts, because they have become strange to me through their false gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:30; Isaiah 30:22; Ezekiel 36:31–32"> For this cause say to the children of Israel, These are the words of the Lord: Come back and give up your false gods and let your faces be turned from your disgusting things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 12:48; Isaiah 58:1–2; Leviticus 24:22"> When any one of the men of Israel, or of those from other lands who are living in Israel, who has become strange to me, and takes his false gods into his heart, and puts before his face the sin which is the cause of his fall, comes to the prophet to get directions from me; I the Lord will give him an answer by myself:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 15:7; Jeremiah 44:11; Ezekiel 5:15"> And my face will be turned against that man, and I will make him a sign and a common saying, cutting him off from among my people; and you will be certain that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:25; Jeremiah 4:10; Psalms 81:11–12"> And if the prophet, tricked by deceit, says anything, it is I the Lord by whom he has been tricked, and I will put out my hand against him, and he will be cut off from among my people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:49; Ezekiel 14:4; Jeremiah 8:11–12"> And the punishment of their sin will be on them: the sin of the prophet will be the same as the sin of him who goes to him for directions;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:23; Ezekiel 44:10; Revelation 21:7"> So that the children of Israel may no longer go wandering away from me, or make themselves unclean with all their wrongdoing; but they will be my people, and I will be their God, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 26:26; Ezekiel 4:16; Isaiah 24:20"> Son of man, when a land, sinning against me, does wrong, and my hand is stretched out against it, and the support of its bread is broken, and I make it short of food, cutting off man and beast from it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:20; Jeremiah 15:1; Ezekiel 28:3"> Even if these three men, Noah, Daniel, and Job, were in it, only themselves would they keep safe by their righteousness, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:17; Leviticus 26:22; Jeremiah 15:3"> Or if I send evil beasts through the land causing destruction and making it waste, so that no man may go through because of the beasts:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 19:29; James 5:16; Ezekiel 33:11"> Even if these three men were in it, by my life, says the Lord, they would not keep safe their sons or daughters, but only themselves, and the land would be made waste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 26:25; Ezekiel 21:3–4; Zephaniah 1:3"> Or if I send a sword against that land, and say, Sword, go through the land, cutting off from it man and beast:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Even if these three men were in it, by my life, says the Lord, they would not keep safe their sons or daughters, but only themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:22; Ezekiel 5:12; Jeremiah 14:12"> Or if I send disease into that land, letting loose my wrath on it in blood, cutting off from it man and beast:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:14; 1 John 2:29; Isaiah 3:10"> Even if Noah, Daniel, and Job were in it, by my life, says the Lord, they would not keep son or daughter safe; only themselves would they keep safe through their righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:27; Ezekiel 5:17; Ezekiel 14:13"> For this is what the Lord has said: How much more when I send my four bitter punishments on Jerusalem, the sword and need of food and evil beasts and disease, cutting off from it man and beast?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:16; Ezekiel 20:43; Hebrews 12:6–11"> But truly, there will still be a small band who will be safe, even sons and daughters: and they will come out to you, and you will see their ways and their doings: and you will be comforted about the evil which I have sent on Jerusalem, even about everything I have sent on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:8–9; Revelation 15:4; Deuteronomy 8:2"> They will give you comfort when you see their ways and their doings: and you will be certain that not for nothing have I done all the things I have done in it, says the Lord.</span>
