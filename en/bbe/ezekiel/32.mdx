<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:20; Ezekiel 32:17; Ezekiel 31:1"> And it came about in the twelfth year, in the twelfth month, on the first day of the month, that the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:3; Nahum 2:11–13; Ezekiel 27:2"> Son of man, make a song of grief for Pharaoh, king of Egypt, and say to him, Young lion of the nations, destruction has come on you; and you were like a sea-beast in the seas, sending out bursts of water, troubling the waters with your feet, making their streams dirty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Jeremiah 16:16; Ecclesiastes 9:12"> This is what the Lord has said: My net will be stretched out over you, and I will take you up in my fishing-net.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 18:6; Ezekiel 29:5; Ezekiel 31:12–13"> And I will let you be stretched on the land; I will send you out violently into the open field; I will let all the birds of heaven come to rest on you and will make the beasts of all the earth full of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:12"> And I will put your flesh on the mountains, and make the valleys full of your blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 34:3; Isaiah 34:7; Revelation 14:20"> And the land will be watered with your blood, and the waterways will be full of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 24:29; Joel 2:31; Isaiah 13:10"> And when I put out your life, the heaven will be covered and its stars made dark; I will let the sun be covered with a cloud and the moon will not give her light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 1:14; Proverbs 20:20"> All the bright lights of heaven I will make dark over you, and put dark night on your land, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 18:10–15; Ezekiel 30:26; Jeremiah 25:15–25"> And the hearts of numbers of peoples will be troubled, when I send your prisoners among the nations, into a country which is strange to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:16; Ezekiel 27:35; 1 Kings 9:8"> And I will make a number of peoples overcome with wonder at you, and their kings will be full of fear because of you, when my sword is waved before them: they will be shaking every minute, every man fearing for his life, in the day of your fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:10; Ezekiel 26:7; Ezekiel 30:22–25"> For this is what the Lord has said: The sword of the king of Babylon will come on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:7; Ezekiel 29:19; Ezekiel 31:11"> I will let the swords of the strong be the cause of the fall of your people; all of them men to be feared among the nations: and they will make waste the pride of Egypt, and all its people will come to destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:11; Ezekiel 29:8; Ezekiel 30:12"> And I will put an end to all her beasts which are by the great waters, and they will never again be troubled by the foot of man or by the feet of beasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Then I will make their waters clear and their rivers will be flowing like oil, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:7; Exodus 7:5; Exodus 14:4"> When I make Egypt an unpeopled waste, cutting off from the land all the things in it; when I send punishment on all those living in it, then it will be clear to them that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:17; 2 Chronicles 35:25; Ezekiel 26:17"> It is a song of grief, and people will give voice to it, the daughters of the nations will give voice to it, even for Egypt and all her people, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:1; Ezekiel 33:21; Ezekiel 1:2"> And in the twelfth year, on the fifteenth day of the month, the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:14; Ezekiel 32:2; Jeremiah 1:10"> Son of man, let your voice be loud in sorrow for the people of Egypt and send them down, even you and the daughters of the nations; I will send them down into the lowest parts of the earth, with those who go down into the underworld.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:18; Ezekiel 32:29–30; Jeremiah 9:25–26"> Are you more beautiful than any? go down, and take your rest among those without circumcision,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 28:3; Ezekiel 32:23–26; Ezekiel 32:29–30"> Among those who have been put to the sword: they will give a resting-place with them to all their people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 14:9–10; Ezekiel 32:27; Ezekiel 32:19"> The strong among the great ones will say to him from the underworld, Are you more beautiful than any? go down, you and your helpers, and take your rest among those without circumcision, and those who have been put to the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:26; Ezekiel 32:29–30; Ezekiel 32:24"> There is Asshur and all her army, round about her last resting-place: all of them put to death by the sword:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:20; Ezekiel 32:24–27; Psalms 27:13"> Whose resting-places are in the inmost parts of the underworld, who were a cause of fear in the land of the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:18; Ezekiel 26:20; Jeremiah 49:34–39"> There is Elam and all her people, round about her last resting-place: all of them put to death by the sword, who have gone down without circumcision into the lowest parts of the earth, who were a cause of fear in the land of the living, and are put to shame with those who go down to the underworld:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 139:8; 1 Chronicles 10:4; 2 Samuel 1:20"> They have made a bed for her among the dead, and all her people are round about her resting-place: all of them without circumcision, put to death with the sword; for they were a cause of fear in the land of the living, and are put to shame with those who go down to the underworld: they have been given a place among those who have been put to the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:13; Genesis 10:2; Ezekiel 38:2–3"> There is Meshech, Tubal, and all her people, round about her last resting-place: all of them without circumcision, put to death by the sword; for they were a cause of fear in the land of the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 14:18–19; Job 3:13–15; Ezekiel 32:21"> And they have been put to rest with the fighting men who came to their end in days long past, who went down to the underworld with their instruments of war, placing their swords under their heads, and their body-covers are over their bones; for their strength was a cause of fear in the land of the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Daniel 2:34–35"> But you will have your bed among those without circumcision, and will be put to rest with those who have been put to death with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:7–22; Malachi 1:3–4; Obadiah 1:1–9"> There is Edom, her kings and all her princes, who have been given a resting-place with those who were put to the sword: they will be resting among those without circumcision, even with those who go down to the underworld.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:6; Ezekiel 39:2; Ezekiel 28:21"> There are the chiefs of the north, all of them, and all the Zidonians, who have gone down with those who have been put to the sword: they are shamed on account of all the fear caused by their strength; they are resting there without circumcision, among those who have been put to the sword, and are put to shame with those who go down to the underworld.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:16; Ezekiel 14:22; Lamentations 2:13"> Pharaoh will see them and be comforted on account of all his people: even Pharaoh and all his army, put to death by the sword, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:27; Genesis 35:5; 2 Corinthians 5:11"> For he put his fear in the land of the living: and he will be put to rest among those without circumcision, with those who have been put to death with the sword, even Pharaoh and all his people, says the Lord.</span>
