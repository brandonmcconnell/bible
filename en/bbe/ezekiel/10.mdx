<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 24:10; Revelation 4:2–3; Revelation 1:13"> Then looking, I saw that on the arch which was over the head of the winged ones there was seen over them what seemed like a sapphire stone, having the form of a king's seat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 8:5; Ezekiel 10:16; Ezekiel 1:13"> And he said to the man clothed in linen, Go in between the wheels, under the winged ones, and get your two hands full of burning coals from between the winged ones and send them in a shower over the town. And he went in before my eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:4; Ezekiel 9:3; Ezekiel 8:16"> Now the winged ones were stationed on the right side of the house when the man went in; and the inner square was full of the cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:28; Ezekiel 9:3; Exodus 40:35"> And the glory of the Lord went up from the winged ones and came to rest over the doorstep of the house; and the house was full of the cloud and the open square was full of the shining of the Lord's glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:24; Job 40:9; Exodus 19:16"> And the sound of the wings of the winged ones was clear even in the outer square, like the voice of the Ruler of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:2; Psalms 99:1; Psalms 80:1"> And when he gave orders to the man clothed in linen, saying, Take fire from between the wheels, from between the winged ones, then he went in and took his place at the side of a wheel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 13:41–42; Ezekiel 1:13; Ezekiel 41:23–26"> And stretching out his hand to the fire which was between the winged ones, he took some of it and went out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:8; Ezekiel 10:21; Isaiah 6:6"> And I saw the form of a man's hands among the winged ones under their wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:15–17; Daniel 10:6; Revelation 21:20"> And looking, I saw four wheels by the side of the winged ones, one wheel by the side of a winged one and another wheel by the side of another: and the wheels were like the colour of a beryl stone to the eye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:16; Psalms 36:6; Psalms 104:24"> In form the four of them were all the same, they seemed like a wheel inside a wheel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:17; Ezekiel 10:22; Ezekiel 1:20"> When they were moving, they went on their four sides without turning; they went after the head in the direction in which it was looking; they went without turning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:18; Revelation 4:8; Revelation 4:6"> And the edges of the four wheels were full of eyes round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> As for the wheels, they were named in my hearing, the circling wheels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 4:7; Ezekiel 10:21; 1 Kings 7:36"> And every one had four faces: the first face was the face of a winged one, and the second was the face of a man, and the third the face of a lion, and the fourth the face of an eagle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:5; Hosea 9:12; Ezekiel 11:22"> And the winged ones went up on high: this is the living being which I saw by the river Chebar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:19–21"> And when the winged ones went, the wheels went by their side: and when their wings were lifted to take them up from the earth, the wheels were not turned from their side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:20–21; Romans 8:2; Ezekiel 1:12"> When they were at rest in their place, these were at rest; when they were lifted up, these went up with them: for the spirit of life was in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 18:10; Matthew 23:37–39; Psalms 78:60–61"> Then the glory of the Lord went out from the doorstep of the house, and came to rest over the winged ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:16; Ezekiel 1:17–21; Ezekiel 1:26–28"> And the winged ones, lifting up their wings, went up from the earth before my eyes, with the wheels by their side: and they came to rest at the east doorway of the Lord's house; and the glory of the God of Israel was over them on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:1; Ezekiel 10:15; 1 Kings 7:36"> This is the living being which I saw under the God of Israel by the river Chebar; and it was clear to me that they were the winged ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:6; Ezekiel 41:18–19; Ezekiel 10:14"> Every one had four faces and every one had four wings; and hands like a man's hands were under their wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:11; Ezekiel 1:10; Ezekiel 1:12"> As for the form of their faces, they were the faces whose form I saw by the river Chebar; when they went, every one of them went straight forward.</span>
