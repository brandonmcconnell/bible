<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 6:7; Leviticus 11:44; Ezekiel 20:5"> Say to the children of Israel, I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 20:23; Exodus 23:24; 1 Peter 4:2–4"> You may not do those things which were done in the land of Egypt where you were living; and you may not do those things which are done in the land of Canaan where I am taking you, or be guided in your behaviour by their rules.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 18:2; Leviticus 18:26; Ezekiel 20:19"> But you are to be guided by my decisions and keep my rules, and be guided by them: I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 10:5; Ezekiel 20:11; Galatians 3:12"> So keep my rules and my decisions, which, if a man does them, will be life to him: I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17–21; Leviticus 18:7–19; Leviticus 20:11–12"> You may not have sex connection with anyone who is a near relation: I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 20:11; Ezekiel 22:10; Leviticus 18:8–16"> You may not have sex relations with your father or your mother: she is your mother, you may not take her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 20:11; 1 Corinthians 5:1; Deuteronomy 27:20"> And you may not have sex relations with your father's wife: she is your father's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17; Deuteronomy 27:22; Ezekiel 22:11"> You may not take your sister, the daughter of your father or of your mother, wherever her birth took place, among you or in another country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> You may not have sex relations with your son's daughter or your daughter's daughter, for they are part of yourself;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Or your father's wife's daughter, the child of your father, for she is your sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 20:19; Exodus 6:20"> You may not have sex connection with your father's sister, for she is your father's near relation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> You may not have sex connection with your mother's sister, for she is your mother's near relation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 20:20"> You may not have sex relations with the wife of your father's brother, for she is of your family;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 20:12; Genesis 38:26; Ezekiel 22:11"> Or with your daughter-in-law, for she is your son's wife, and you may not take her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5; Leviticus 20:21; Matthew 22:24"> You may not have sex relations with your brother's wife, for she is your brother's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 20:14; Amos 2:7; Deuteronomy 27:23"> You may not take as wife a woman and her daughter, or her son's daughter or her daughter's daughter, for they are of one family: it is an act of shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Malachi 2:15; Genesis 30:15; Genesis 29:28"> And you may not take as wife a woman and at the same time her sister, to be in competition with her in her life-time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 20:18; Leviticus 15:24; Ezekiel 22:10"> And you may not go near a woman or have sex relations with her when she is unclean, at her regular time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:14; 1 Corinthians 6:9; Hebrews 13:4"> And you may not have sex relations with your neighbour's wife, making yourself unclean with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:31; Leviticus 20:2–5; Deuteronomy 18:10"> And you may not make any of your children go through the fire as an offering to Molech, and you may not put shame on the name of your God: I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 20:13; Romans 1:26–27; 1 Corinthians 6:9"> You may not have sex relations with men, as you do with women: it is a disgusting thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 22:19; Leviticus 20:15–16; Leviticus 20:12"> And you may not have sex relations with a beast, making yourself unclean with it; and a woman may not give herself to a beast: it is an unnatural act.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:12; Leviticus 18:30; Leviticus 20:22–23"> Do not make yourself unclean in any of these ways; for so have those nations whom I am driving out from before you made themselves unclean:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 18:28; Leviticus 20:22–23; Jeremiah 2:7"> And the land itself has become unclean; so that I have sent on it the reward of its wrongdoing, and the land itself puts out those who are living in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Leviticus 17:10; Leviticus 18:30"> So then keep my rules and my decisions, and do not do any of these disgusting things, those of you who are Israelites by birth, or any others who are living with you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:14; Leviticus 18:24; Deuteronomy 23:18"> (For all these disgusting things were done by the men of this country who were there before you, and the land has been made unclean by them;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 3:16; Leviticus 20:22; Leviticus 18:25"> So that the land may not put you out from it, when you make it unclean, as it put out the nations which were there before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 20:6; Exodus 12:15; Leviticus 17:10"> For all those who do any of these disgusting things will be cut off from among their people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 20:23; Deuteronomy 18:9–12; Leviticus 22:9"> So then, keep my orders, so that you may not do any of these disgusting things which were done before you, or make yourselves unclean through them: I am the Lord your God.</span>
