<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:6; Ephesians 5:8–14; Exodus 27:20–21"> Give orders to the children of Israel to give you clean olive oil for the light, so that a light may be burning at all times,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Outside the veil of the ark in the Tent of meeting; let Aaron see that it is burning from evening till morning at all times before the Lord: it is a rule for ever through all your generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 31:8; Exodus 37:17–24; Exodus 39:37"> Let Aaron put the lights in order on the support before the Lord at all times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 25:30; 1 Kings 18:31; Exodus 40:23"> And take the best meal and make twelve cakes of it, a fifth part of an ephah in every cake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 7:48; Exodus 25:23–24; 2 Chronicles 4:19"> And put them in two lines, six in a line, on the holy table before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 2:2; Revelation 8:3–4; Genesis 9:16"> And on the lines of cakes put clean sweet-smelling spices, for a sign on the bread, an offering made by fire to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:4; Numbers 4:7; 1 Chronicles 9:32"> Every Sabbath day regularly, the priest is to put it in order before the Lord: it is offered for the children of Israel, an agreement made for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 8:31; Luke 6:4; Mark 2:26"> And it will be for Aaron and his sons; they are to take it for food in a holy place: it is the most holy of all the offerings made by fire to the Lord, a rule for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 11:4; Exodus 12:38"> And a son of an Israelite woman, whose father was an Egyptian, went out among the children of Israel and had a fight with a man of Israel by the tents;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 20:7; Leviticus 24:15–16; Exodus 3:15"> And the son of the Israelite woman said evil against the holy Name, with curses; and they took him to Moses. His mother's name was Shelomith, the daughter of Dibri, of the tribe of Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 18:15–16; Numbers 15:34; Numbers 27:5"> And they kept him shut up, till a decision might be given by the mouth of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the Lord said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:7; Leviticus 20:27; Deuteronomy 21:21"> Take the curser outside the tent-circle; and let all in whose hearing the words were said put their hands on his head, and let him be stoned by all the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 9:13; Exodus 22:28; Leviticus 5:1"> And say to the children of Israel, As for any man cursing God, his sin will be on his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 12:31; James 2:7; John 10:33–36"> And he who says evil against the name of the Lord will certainly be put to death; he will be stoned by all the people; the man who is not of your nation and one who is an Israelite by birth, whoever says evil against the holy Name is to be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 9:5–6; Deuteronomy 19:11–12; Deuteronomy 27:24"> And anyone who takes another's life is certainly to be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 24:21; Exodus 21:34–36"> And anyone wounding a beast and causing its death, will have to make payment for it: a life for a life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 5:38; Matthew 7:2; Deuteronomy 19:21"> And if a man does damage to his neighbour, as he has done, so let it be done to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:21; Matthew 5:38; Exodus 21:23–25"> Wound for wound, eye for eye, tooth for tooth; whatever damage he has done, so let it be done to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 24:17–18; Exodus 21:33"> He who puts a beast to death will have to make payment for it; he who puts a man to death will himself be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 12:49; Numbers 15:15–16; Numbers 9:14"> You are to have the same law for a man of another nation living among you as for an Israelite; for I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 10:28–29; Hebrews 2:2–3; Numbers 15:35–36"> And Moses said these words to the children of Israel, and they took the man who had been cursing outside the tent-circle and had him stoned. The children of Israel did as the Lord gave orders to Moses.</span>
