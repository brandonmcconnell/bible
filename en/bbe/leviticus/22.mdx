<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 18:21; Deuteronomy 15:19; Leviticus 22:32"> Give orders to Aaron and to his sons to keep themselves separate from the holy things of the children of Israel which they give to me, and not to make my holy name common: I am the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 7:20–21; Numbers 19:13; Psalms 16:11"> Say to them, If any man of all your seed through all your generations, being unclean, comes near the holy things which the children of Israel make holy to the Lord, he will be cut off from before me: I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 11:39; Leviticus 15:2–3; Leviticus 15:13–16"> No man of the seed of Aaron who is a leper, or who has a flow from his body, may take of the holy food till he is clean. And any man touching anything which is unclean because of the dead, or any man whose seed goes from him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 11:43–44; Leviticus 15:7; Leviticus 15:19"> Or anyone touching any unclean thing which goes flat on the earth, or someone by whom he may be made unclean in any way whatever;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 15:5; Hebrews 10:22; Haggai 2:13"> Any person touching any such unclean thing will be unclean till evening, and may not take of the holy food till his flesh has been bathed in water;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 21:22; Deuteronomy 18:3–4; 1 Corinthians 9:13–14"> And when the sun has gone down he will be clean; and after that he may take part in the holy food, because it is his bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 17:15; Exodus 22:31; Ezekiel 44:31"> That which comes to a natural death, or is attacked by beasts, he may not take as food, for it will make him unclean: I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 28:43; Numbers 18:22; Numbers 18:32"> So then, let them keep what I have put into their care, for fear that sin may come on them because of it, so causing their death because they have made it common: I am the Lord, who make them holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 12:4; 1 Samuel 21:6; Exodus 29:33"> No outside person may take of the holy food, or one living as a guest in the priest's house, or a servant working for payment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 17:13; Exodus 12:44; Numbers 18:11–13"> But any person for whom the priest has given money, to make him his, may take of it with him; and those who come to birth in his house may take of his bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 40:13; Leviticus 21:3"> And if the daughter of a priest is married to an outside person she may not take of the holy things which are lifted up as offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 10:14; Genesis 38:11; Numbers 18:11–19"> But if a priest's daughter is a widow, or parted from her husband, and has no child, and has come back to her father's house as when she was a girl, she may take of her father's bread; but no outside person may do so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 27:15; Leviticus 27:13; Leviticus 5:15–19"> And if a man takes the holy food in error, he will have to give the holy thing back to the priest, with the addition of a fifth part.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 18:32; Leviticus 19:8; Ezekiel 22:26"> And they may not make common the holy things which the children of Israel give to the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 22:9; 1 Peter 2:24; Isaiah 53:11–12"> So causing sin to come on them when they take their holy things for food: I am the Lord who make them holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the Lord said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 1:2; Deuteronomy 12:6; Numbers 15:3"> Say to Aaron and to his sons and to all the children of Israel, If any man of the children of Israel, or of another nation living in Israel, makes an offering, given because of an oath or freely given to the Lord for a burned offering;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 1:10; Leviticus 1:3; Ephesians 5:27"> So that it may be pleasing to the Lord, let him give a male, without any mark, from among the oxen or the sheep or the goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:1; Malachi 1:8; Deuteronomy 15:21"> But anything which has a mark you may not give; it will not make you pleasing to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 15:8; Numbers 15:3; Leviticus 3:6"> And whoever makes a peace-offering to the Lord, in payment of an oath or as a free offering, from the herd or the flock, if it is to be pleasing to the Lord, let it be free from any mark or damage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 22:20; Malachi 1:8; Leviticus 1:9"> Anything blind or broken or damaged or having any disease or any mark on it may not be offered to the Lord; you may not make an offering of it by fire on the altar to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 21:18"> An ox or a lamb which has more or less than its natural parts, may be given as a free offering; but it will not be taken in payment of an oath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 22:20; Deuteronomy 23:1; Leviticus 21:20"> An animal which has its sex parts damaged or crushed or broken or cut, may not be offered to the Lord; such a thing may not be done anywhere in your land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 21:8; Ezra 6:8–10; Numbers 15:14–16"> And from one who is not an Israelite you may not take any of these for an offering to the Lord; for they are unclean, there is a mark on them, and the Lord will not be pleased with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And the Lord said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 22:30; Leviticus 19:23–24; Leviticus 22:25"> When an ox or a sheep or a goat is given birth, let it be with its mother for seven days; and after the eighth day it may be taken as an offering made by fire to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:6–7; Deuteronomy 14:21; Exodus 34:26"> A cow or a sheep may not be put to death with its young on the same day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 116:17; Psalms 107:22; Amos 4:5"> And when you make an offering of praise to the Lord, make it in a way which is pleasing to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 7:15–18; Exodus 16:19–20; Leviticus 19:7"> Let it be used for food on the same day; do not keep any part of it till the morning: I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Leviticus 19:37; Numbers 15:40"> So then, keep my orders and do them: I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 18:21; Luke 11:2; Matthew 6:9"> And do not make my holy name common; so that it may be kept holy by the children of Israel: I am the Lord who make you holy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 11:45; Exodus 6:7; Leviticus 25:38"> Who took you out of the land of Egypt that I might be your God: I am the Lord.</span>
