<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 6:11; Exodus 29:6; Job 29:14"> And let them put a clean head-dress on his head. So they put a clean head-dress on his head, clothing him with clean robes: and to him he said, See, I have taken your sin away from you.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5; Isaiah 11:1; Zechariah 6:12"> Give ear now, O Joshua, the high priest, you and your friends who are seated before you; for these are men who are a sign: for see, I will let my servant the Branch be seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 4:10; Jeremiah 50:20; Isaiah 28:16"> For see, the stone which I have put before Joshua; on one stone are seven eyes: see, the design cut on it will be my work, says the Lord of armies, and I will take away the sin of that land in one day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 4:25; Micah 4:4; Zechariah 2:11"> In that day, says the Lord of armies, you will be one another's guests under the vine and under the fig-tree.</span>
