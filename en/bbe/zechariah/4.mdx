<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 11:4; Zechariah 4:14; Romans 11:17"> And two olive-trees by it, one on the right side of the cup and one on the left.</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:7–8; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; 1 Corinthians 2:4–5"> This is the word of the Lord to Zerubbabel, saying, Not by force or by power, but by my spirit, says the Lord of armies.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Then the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 8:7; 1 Corinthians 1:28–29; 2 Chronicles 16:9"> For who has had a poor opinion of the day of small things? for they will be glad when they see the weighted measuring-line in the hand of Zerubbabel. Then he said in answer to me, These seven lights are the eyes of the Lord which go quickly up and down through all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zechariah 4:3; Revelation 11:4"> And I made answer and said to him, What are these two olive-trees on the right side of the light-support and on the left?</span>
