<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:1; Ezra 6:14–15; Zechariah 1:1"> And it came about in the fourth year of King Darius, that the word of the Lord came to Zechariah on the fourth day of the ninth month, the month Chislev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 8:21; Jeremiah 26:19; 1 Samuel 13:12"> Now they of Beth-el had sent Sharezer and Regem-melech to make a request for grace from the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 8:19; James 4:8–10; Malachi 2:7"> And to say to the priests of the house of the Lord of armies and to the prophets, Am I to go on weeping in the fifth month, separating myself as I have done in past years?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 10:16"> Then the word of the Lord of armies came to me, saying</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 1:12; Isaiah 58:4–6; 1 Corinthians 10:31"> Say to all the people of the land and to the priests, When you went without food and gave yourselves to grief in the fifth and the seventh months for these seventy years, did you ever do it because of me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:20–21; 1 Corinthians 11:26–29; Hosea 8:13"> And when you are feasting and drinking, are you not doing it only for yourselves?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:16–20; Jeremiah 17:26; Jeremiah 7:5"> Are not these the words which the Lord said to you by the earlier prophets, when Jerusalem was full of people and wealth, and the towns round about her and the South and the Lowland were peopled?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the word of the Lord came to Zechariah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Micah 6:8; Psalms 82:2–4; Proverbs 21:3"> This is what the Lord of armies has said: Let your judging be upright and done in good faith, let every man have mercy and pity for his brother:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:28; Isaiah 1:23; James 5:4"> Do not be hard on the widow, or the child without a father, on the man from a strange country, or on the poor; let there be no evil thought in your heart against your brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:29; Jeremiah 8:5; Hosea 4:16"> But they would not give attention, turning their backs and stopping their ears from hearing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19; Nehemiah 9:29–30; 2 Chronicles 36:16"> And they made their hearts like the hardest stone, so that they might not give ear to the law and the words which the Lord of armies had said by the earlier prophets: and there came great wrath from the Lord of armies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24–28; Jeremiah 14:12; Isaiah 1:15"> And it came about that as they would not give ear to his voice, so I would not give ear to their voice, says the Lord of armies:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:33; Deuteronomy 28:64; Jeremiah 23:19"> But with a storm-wind I sent them in flight among all the nations of whom they had no knowledge. So the land was waste after them, so that no man went through or came back: for they had made waste the desired land.</span>
