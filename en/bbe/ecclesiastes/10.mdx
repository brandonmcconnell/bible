<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 5:13–16; Galatians 2:12–14; Nehemiah 13:26"> Dead flies make the oil of the perfumer give out an evil smell; more valued is a little wisdom than the great glory of the foolish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 12:18–20; Ecclesiastes 10:14; Ecclesiastes 10:10"> The heart of the wise man goes in the right direction; but the heart of a foolish man in the wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 18:2; Proverbs 13:16; Proverbs 18:6"> And when the foolish man is walking in the way, he has no sense and lets everyone see that he is foolish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 25:15; Ecclesiastes 8:3; 1 Samuel 25:24–44"> If the wrath of the ruler is against you, keep in your place; in him who keeps quiet even great sins may be overlooked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:13; Ecclesiastes 4:7; Ecclesiastes 9:3"> There is an evil which I have seen under the sun, like an error which comes by chance from a ruler:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 28:12; Proverbs 29:2; Esther 3:1"> The foolish are placed in high positions, but men of wealth are kept low.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 19:10; Esther 6:8; Proverbs 30:22"> I have seen servants on horses, and rulers walking on the earth as servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 26:27; Psalms 7:15–16; Esther 7:10"> He who makes a hole for others will himself go into it, and for him who makes a hole through a wall the bite of a snake will be a punishment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> He who gets out stones from the earth will be damaged by them, and in the cutting of wood there is danger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 1:5; Colossians 4:5; Ecclesiastes 10:15"> If the iron has no edge, and he does not make it sharp, then he has to put out more strength; but wisdom makes things go well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:17; Psalms 58:4–5; Psalms 64:3"> If a snake gives a bite before the word of power is said, then there is no longer any use in the word of power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Luke 4:22; Proverbs 22:11"> The words of a wise man's mouth are sweet to all, but the lips of a foolish man are his destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:10–11; Proverbs 29:9; Acts 6:9–11"> The first words of his mouth are foolish, and the end of his talk is evil crime.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 15:2; Ecclesiastes 6:12; Ecclesiastes 5:3"> The foolish are full of words; man has no knowledge of what will be; and who is able to say what will be after him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:4–5; Isaiah 57:1; Isaiah 35:8–10"> The work of the foolish will be a weariness to him, because he has no knowledge of the way to the town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 3:12; Isaiah 5:11–12; Isaiah 3:4–5"> Unhappy is the land whose king is a boy, and whose rulers are feasting in the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 31:4–5; Proverbs 28:2–3; Isaiah 5:11"> Happy is the land whose ruler is of noble birth, and whose chiefs take food at the right time, for strength and not for feasting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 14:1; Proverbs 24:30–34; Proverbs 20:4"> When no work is done the roof goes in, and when the hands do nothing water comes into the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 104:15; Ecclesiastes 2:1–2; 1 Peter 4:3"> A feast is for laughing, and wine makes glad the heart; but by the one and the other money is wasted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 12:2–3; Acts 23:5; Ecclesiastes 7:21–22"> Say not a curse against the king, even in your thoughts; and even secretly say not a curse against the man of wealth; because a bird of the air will take the voice, and that which has wings will give news of it.</span>
