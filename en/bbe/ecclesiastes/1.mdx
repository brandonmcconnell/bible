<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:8–10; Ecclesiastes 1:12; Ecclesiastes 7:27"> The words of the Preacher, the son of David, king in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 144:4; Romans 8:20; Ecclesiastes 5:10"> All is to no purpose, said the Preacher, all the ways of man are to no purpose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:9; Ecclesiastes 5:16; Ecclesiastes 2:22"> What is a man profited by all his work which he does under the sun?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 104:5; Zechariah 1:5; Ecclesiastes 6:12"> One generation goes and another comes; but the earth is for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 19:4–6; Habakkuk 3:11; Psalms 42:1"> The sun comes up and the sun goes down, and goes quickly back to the place where he came up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 3:8; Ecclesiastes 11:5; Psalms 107:25"> The wind goes to the south, turning back again to the north; circling round for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 38:10–11; Psalms 104:6–9"> All the rivers go down to the sea, but the sea is not full; to the place where the rivers go, there they go again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 27:20; Matthew 11:28; Ecclesiastes 4:8"> All things are full of weariness; man may not give their story: the eye has never enough of its seeing, or the ear of its hearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:15; Ecclesiastes 6:10; Ecclesiastes 2:12"> That which has been, is that which is to be, and that which has been done, is that which will be done, and there is no new thing under the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Luke 17:26–30; Matthew 23:30–32"> Is there anything of which men say, See, this is new? It has been in the old time which was before us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:16; Psalms 9:6; Ecclesiastes 9:5"> There is no memory of those who have gone before, and of those who come after there will be no memory for those who are still to come after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:1; 1 Kings 4:1–19"> I, the Preacher, was king over Israel in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:10; Genesis 3:19; 1 Timothy 4:15"> And I gave my heart to searching out in wisdom all things which are done under heaven: it is a hard thing which God has put on the sons of men to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:11; Ecclesiastes 2:17; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> I have seen all the works which are done under the sun; all is to no purpose, and desire for wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:12–13; Isaiah 40:4; Ecclesiastes 3:14"> That which is bent may not be made straight, and that which is not there may not be numbered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:9; 1 Kings 4:30; 1 Kings 10:7"> I said to my heart, See, I have become great and am increased in wisdom more than any who were before me in Jerusalem — yes, my heart has seen much wisdom and knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:21; Ecclesiastes 1:13–14"> And I gave my heart to getting knowledge of wisdom, and of the ways of the foolish. And I saw that this again was desire for wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 28:28; Ecclesiastes 12:12–13; 1 Corinthians 3:18–20"> Because in much wisdom is much grief, and increase of knowledge is increase of sorrow.</span>
