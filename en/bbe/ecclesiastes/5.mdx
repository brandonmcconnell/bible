<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="James 1:19; Hebrews 12:28–29; Proverbs 15:8"> Put your feet down with care when you go to the house of God, for it is better to give ear than to make the burned offerings of the foolish, whose knowledge is only of doing evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 6:7; Proverbs 10:19; Isaiah 55:9"> Be not unwise with your mouth, and let not your heart be quick to say anything before God, because God is in heaven and you are on the earth — so let not the number of your words be great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 15:2; Proverbs 10:19; Ecclesiastes 10:12–14"> As a dream comes from much business, so the voice of a foolish man comes with words in great number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 30:2; Deuteronomy 23:21–23; Psalms 76:11"> When you take an oath before God, put it quickly into effect, because he has no pleasure in the foolish; keep the oath you have taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 5:4; Deuteronomy 23:22; Proverbs 20:25"> It is better not to take an oath than to take an oath and not keep it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 1:26; 2 John 1:8; James 3:2"> Let not your mouth make your flesh do evil. And say not before the angel, It was an error. So that God may not be angry with your words and put an end to the work of your hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:13; Matthew 12:36; Ecclesiastes 8:12"> Because much talk comes from dreams and things of no purpose. But let the fear of God be in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 12:5; Ecclesiastes 4:1; Ecclesiastes 3:16"> If you see the poor under a cruel yoke, and law and right being violently overturned in a country, be not surprised, because one authority is keeping watch on another and there are higher than they.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 4:7–23; Psalms 115:16; Genesis 1:29–30"> It is good generally for a country where the land is worked to have a king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:10; Matthew 6:24; Luke 12:15"> He who has a love for silver never has enough silver, or he who has love for wealth, enough profit. This again is to no purpose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 John 2:16; Habakkuk 2:13; Psalms 119:36–37"> When goods are increased, the number of those who take of them is increased; and what profit has the owner but to see them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 3:24; Psalms 4:8; Jeremiah 31:26"> The sleep of a working man is sweet, if he has little food or much; but to him who is full, sleep will not come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 6:1–2; Luke 18:22–23; Genesis 19:14"> There is a great evil which I have seen under the sun — wealth kept by the owner to be his downfall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 6:19–20; Haggai 2:16–17; Proverbs 23:5"> And I saw the destruction of his wealth by an evil chance; and when he became the father of a son he had nothing in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 49:17; Job 1:21; 1 Timothy 6:7"> As he came from his mother at birth, so does he go again; he gets from his work no reward which he may take away in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 11:29; Ecclesiastes 1:3; John 6:27"> And this again is a great evil, that in all points as he came so will he go; and what profit has he in working for the wind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 127:2; Ezekiel 4:16–17; Psalms 90:7–11"> All his days are in the dark, and he has much sorrow, pain, disease, and trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:24; Ecclesiastes 3:12–13; Ecclesiastes 3:22"> This is what I have seen: it is good and fair for a man to take meat and drink and to have joy in all his work under the sun, all the days of his life which God has given him; that is his reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:18; Ecclesiastes 3:13; Ecclesiastes 6:2"> Every man to whom God has given money and wealth and the power to have pleasure in it and to do his part and have joy in his work: this is given by God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 65:21–24; Isaiah 65:13–14; Psalms 37:16"> He will not give much thought to the days of his life; because God lets him be taken up with the joy of his heart.</span>
