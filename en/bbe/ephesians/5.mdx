<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 4:11; 1 Peter 1:15–16; Ephesians 4:32"> Let it then be your desire to be like God, as well-loved children;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 13:34; Galatians 2:20; Colossians 3:14"> And be living in love, even as Christ had love for you, and gave himself up for us, an offering to God for a perfume of a sweet smell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 3:5; Ephesians 5:5; Galatians 5:19–21"> But evil acts of the flesh and all unclean things, or desire for others' property, let it not even be named among you, as is right for saints;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 3:8; Ephesians 4:29; 1 Thessalonians 5:18"> And let there be no low behaviour, or foolish talk, or words said in sport, which are not right, but in place of them the giving of praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 5:21; 1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Ephesians 5:3"> Being certain of this, that no man who gives way to the passions of the flesh, no unclean person, or one who has desire for the property of others, or who gives worship to images, has any heritage in the kingdom of Christ and God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 1:18; Colossians 2:8; Matthew 24:4"> Do not be turned from the right way by foolish words; for because of these things the punishment of God comes on those who do not put themselves under him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 18:4; Ephesians 5:11; 1 Timothy 5:22"> Have no part with such men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 8:12; 1 John 1:7; Acts 26:18"> For you at one time were dark, but now are light in the Lord: let your behaviour be that of children of light</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 3:9–10; Galatians 5:22–23; 3 John 1:11"> (Because the fruit of the light is in all righteousness and in everything which is good and true),</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:1–2; Hebrews 12:28; Psalms 19:14"> Testing by experience what is well-pleasing to the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 13:12; 1 Timothy 5:20; 2 Thessalonians 3:6"> And have no company with the works of the dark, which give no fruit, but make their true quality clear;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 5:3; Jeremiah 23:24; Ecclesiastes 12:14"> For the things which are done by them in secret it is shame even to put into words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 3:20–21; 1 Corinthians 4:5; Hosea 2:10"> But all things, when their true quality is seen, are made clear by the light: because everything which is made clear is light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1; Romans 13:11–12; Isaiah 26:19"> For this reason he says, Be awake, you who are sleeping, and come up from among the dead, and Christ will be your light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 4:5; James 3:13; Philippians 1:27"> Take care then how you are living, not as unwise, but as wise;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 4:5; Amos 5:13; John 12:35"> Making good use of the time, because the days are evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 12:2; 1 Thessalonians 5:18; Colossians 1:9"> For this reason, then, do not be foolish, but be conscious of the Lord's pleasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Luke 1:15; Romans 13:13"> And do not take overmuch wine by which one may be overcome, but be full of the Spirit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; 1 Corinthians 14:26; Psalms 95:2"> Joining with one another in holy songs of praise and of the Spirit, using your voice in songs and making melody in your heart to the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Colossians 3:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:18; Hebrews 13:15"> Giving praise at all times for all things in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, to God, even the Father;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 2:3; Ephesians 5:24; Ephesians 5:22"> Letting yourselves be ruled by one another in the fear of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:18–1; 1 Peter 3:1–6; Titus 2:5"> Wives, be under the authority of your husbands, as of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:3–10; Colossians 1:18; Ephesians 1:22–23"> For the husband is the head of the wife, as Christ is the head of the church, being himself the saviour of the body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 5:33; Colossians 3:20; Titus 2:7"> And as the church is under Christ's authority, so let wives be under the rule of their husbands in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Peter 3:7; Colossians 3:19; Ephesians 5:28"> Husbands, have love for your wives, even as Christ had love for the church, and gave himself for it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; John 15:3; Ezekiel 36:25"> So that he might make it holy, having made it clean with the washing of water by the word,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 1:22; Hebrews 9:14; 1 Peter 1:19"> And might take it for himself, a church full of glory, not having one mark or fold or any such thing; but that it might be holy and complete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ephesians 5:25; Matthew 19:5; Genesis 2:21–24"> Even so it is right for husbands to have love for their wives as for their bodies. He who has love for his wife has love for himself:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:14–15; Ephesians 5:31; Ecclesiastes 4:5"> For no man ever had hate for his flesh; but he gives it food and takes care of it, even as Christ does for the church;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:15; 1 Corinthians 12:12–27; Romans 12:5"> Because we are parts of his body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 2:24; Matthew 19:5; Mark 10:7–8"> For this cause will a man go away from his father and mother and be joined to his wife, and the two will become one flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Revelation 21:2; 2 Corinthians 11:2; Isaiah 54:5"> This is a great secret: but my words are about Christ and the church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Peter 3:2–7; Esther 1:20; Ephesians 5:25"> But do you, everyone, have love for his wife, even as for himself; and let the wife see that she has respect for her husband.</span>
