<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Philippians 3:14; Hebrews 4:14–15"> For this reason, holy brothers, marked out to have a part in heaven, give thought to Jesus the representative and high priest of our faith;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 12:7; 1 Timothy 3:15; Ephesians 2:22"> Who kept faith with God who gave him his place, even as Moses did in all his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:9; 1 Peter 2:5–7; Zechariah 6:12–13"> And it was right for this man to have more honour than Moses, even as the builder of a house has more honour than the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 3:3; Hebrews 1:2; Esther 2:10"> For every house has a builder; but the builder of all things is God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 12:7; Hebrews 3:2; Exodus 14:31"> And Moses certainly kept faith as a servant, in all his house, and as a witness of those things which were to be said later;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Corinthians 3:16; Hebrews 10:35"> But Christ as a son, over his house; whose house are we, if we keep our hearts fixed in the glad and certain hope till the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 95:7–11; Hebrews 3:15; Isaiah 55:3"> And so, as the Holy Spirit says, Today if you let his voice come to your ears,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 28:14; Exodus 17:7; Zechariah 7:11–12"> Be not hard of heart, as when you made me angry, on the day of testing in the waste land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 7:36; Deuteronomy 4:9; Exodus 19:4"> When your fathers put me to the test, and saw my works for forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 95:10; 2 Thessalonians 2:10–12; Psalms 67:2"> So that I was angry with this generation, and I said, Their hearts are in error at all times, and they have no knowledge of my ways;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:34–35; Hebrews 4:3; Numbers 32:10–13"> And being angry I made an oath, saying, They may not come into my rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5; Jeremiah 7:24; Jeremiah 17:9"> My brothers, take care that there is not by chance in any one of you an evil heart without belief, turning away from the living God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; Hebrews 10:24–25; 2 Timothy 4:2"> But give comfort to one another every day as long as it is still Today; so that no one among you may be made hard by the deceit of sin:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 3:6; Hebrews 6:11; Hebrews 12:10"> For if we keep the substance of the faith which we had at the start, even till the end, we have a part with Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 3:7–8; Psalms 95:7; Hebrews 10:38"> As it is said, Today if you will let his voice come to your ears, be not hard of heart, as when you made him angry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 14:2; Numbers 14:30; Numbers 26:65"> Who made him angry when his voice came to them? was it not all those who came out of Egypt with Moses?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 14:29; Numbers 26:64–65; Numbers 14:32–33"> And with whom was he angry for forty years? was it not with those who did evil, who came to their deaths in the waste land?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 14:30; Hebrews 4:6; Deuteronomy 1:34–35"> And to whom did he make an oath that they might not come into his rest? was it not to those who went against his orders?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jude 1:5; John 3:36; Mark 16:16"> So we see that they were not able to go in because they had no belief.</span>
