<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 25:8; Hebrews 9:10–11; Hebrews 8:2"> Now the first agreement had its rules of worship, and a holy order.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 40:4; Leviticus 24:5–8; Exodus 26:35"> For the first Tent was made ready, having in it the vessels for the lights and the table and the ordering of the bread; and this is named the holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 26:31–33; Exodus 40:3; Matthew 27:51"> And inside the second veil was the place which is named the Holy of holies;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 17:10; 2 Chronicles 5:10; Exodus 40:3"> Having a vessel of gold in it for burning perfumes, and the ark of the agreement, which was covered with gold and which had in it a pot made of gold for the manna, and Aaron's rod which put out buds, and the stones with the writing of the agreement;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 16:2; Numbers 7:89; Exodus 25:17–22"> And over it were the winged ones of glory with their wings covering the mercy-seat; about which it is not possible now to say anything in detail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 28:3; Exodus 27:21; Luke 1:8–11"> Now while these things were in existence, the priests went into the first Tent at all times, for prayer and the making of offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 16:34; Leviticus 5:18; Hebrews 10:19–20"> But only the high priest went into the second, once a year, not without making an offering of blood for himself and for the errors of the people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 14:6; Hebrews 3:7; Hebrews 10:19–22"> The Holy Spirit witnessing by this that the way into the holy place had not at that time been made open, while the first Tent was still in being;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 3:21; Hebrews 5:1; Romans 5:14"> And this is an image of the present time; when the offerings which are given are not able to make the heart of the worshipper completely clean,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 2:15; Colossians 2:16; Leviticus 11:2–47"> Because they are only rules of the flesh, of meats and drinks and washings, which have their place till the time comes when things will be put right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 9:23–24; 2 Corinthians 5:1; Hebrews 8:1–2"> But now Christ has come as the high priest of the good things of the future, through this greater and better Tent, not made with hands, that is to say, not of this world,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 10:4; 1 Peter 1:18–19; Hebrews 9:15"> And has gone once and for ever into the holy place, having got eternal salvation, not through the blood of goats and young oxen, but through his blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 19:2–21; Leviticus 16:14–16; Acts 15:9"> For if the blood of goats and oxen, and the dust from the burning of a young cow, being put on the unclean, make the flesh clean:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; 1 Peter 1:19; 1 John 1:7"> How much more will the blood of Christ, who, being without sin, made an offering of himself to God through the Holy Spirit, make your hearts clean from dead works to be servants of the living God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 3:24–26; Romans 8:30; Romans 8:28"> And for this cause it is through him that a new agreement has come into being, so that after the errors under the first agreement had been taken away by his death, the word of God might have effect for those who were marked out for an eternal heritage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 2:10; 1 Corinthians 15:22; Romans 5:12"> Because where there is a testament, there has to be the death of the man who made it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 48:21; John 14:27; Galatians 3:15"> For a testament has effect after death; for what power has it while the man who made it is living?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 24:3–8; Hebrews 9:14; Hebrews 9:22"> So that even the first agreement was not made without blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 24:5–11; Numbers 19:6; Hebrews 10:4"> For when Moses had given all the rules of the law to the people, he took the blood of goats and young oxen, with water and red wool and hyssop, and put it on the book itself and on all the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 24:8; Matthew 26:28; Hebrews 13:20"> Saying, This blood is the sign of the agreement which God has made with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 8:15; Leviticus 8:19; Exodus 29:36"> And the blood was put on the Tent and all the holy vessels in the same way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 17:11; Leviticus 6:7; Leviticus 4:35"> And by the law almost all things are made clean with blood, and without blood there is no forgiveness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Peter 1:19–21; Hebrews 10:4; Hebrews 9:14"> For this cause it was necessary to make the copies of the things in heaven clean with these offerings; but the things themselves are made clean with better offerings than these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Hebrews 9:11–12; Hebrews 9:23"> For Christ did not go into a holy place which had been made by men's hands as the copy of the true one; but he went into heaven itself, and now takes his place before the face of God for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 9:12; Hebrews 9:7; Hebrews 9:14"> And he did not have to make an offering of himself again and again, as the high priest goes into the holy place every year with blood which is not his;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 9:12; 1 Peter 1:20; Hebrews 1:2"> For then he would have undergone a number of deaths from the time of the making of the world: but now he has come to us at the end of the old order, to put away sin by the offering of himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Ecclesiastes 12:14"> And because by God's law death comes to men once, and after that they are judged;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Titus 2:13; 1 Peter 2:24; Romans 6:10"> So Christ, having at his first coming taken on himself the sins of men, will be seen a second time, without sin, by those who are waiting for him, for their salvation.</span>
