<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 8:15; 2 Peter 1:12–13; Proverbs 7:1–2"> For this reason there is the more need for us to give attention to the things which have come to our ears, for fear that by chance we might be slipping away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 7:53; Galatians 3:19; Hebrews 10:28"> Because if the word which came through the angels was fixed, and in the past every evil act against God's orders was given its full punishment;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 10:28–29; Hebrews 12:25; Hebrews 5:9"> What will come on us, if we do not give our minds to such a great salvation? a salvation of which our fathers first had knowledge through the words of the Lord, and which was made certain to us by those to whom his words came;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 14:3; Ephesians 1:5; Mark 16:20"> And God was a witness with them, by signs and wonders, and by more than natural powers, and by his distribution of the Holy Spirit at his pleasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 11:15; 2 Peter 3:13; Hebrews 6:5"> For he did not make the angels rulers over the world to come, of which I am writing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 8:4–8; Psalms 144:3; Job 7:17–18"> But a certain writer has given his witness, saying, What is man, that you keep him in mind? what is the son of man, that you take him into account?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 8:5; Philippians 2:6–7"> You made him a little lower than the angels; you gave him a crown of glory and honour, and made him ruler over all the works of your hands:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 13:3; 1 Corinthians 15:27; Psalms 8:6"> You put all things under his feet. For in making man the ruler over all things, God did not put anything outside his authority; though we do not see everything under him now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philippians 2:7–9; Revelation 5:9; 1 John 4:9–10"> But we see him who was made a little lower than the angels, even Jesus, crowned with glory and honour, because he let himself be put to death so that by the grace of God he might undergo death for all men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Acts 5:31; Luke 24:46"> Because it was right for him, for whom and through whom all things have being, in guiding his sons to glory, to make the captain of their salvation complete through pain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:10; Hebrews 13:12; Hebrews 10:14"> For he who makes holy and those who are made holy are all of one family; and for this reason it is no shame for him to give them the name of brothers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 22:22; Psalms 40:10; John 18:20"> Saying, I will give the knowledge of your name to my brothers, I will make a song of praise to you before the church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 8:17–18; Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 91:2"> And again he says, I will put my faith in him. And again, See, I am here, and the children which God has given to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:10; Romans 8:3; Colossians 2:15"> And because the children are flesh and blood, he took a body himself and became like them; so that by his death he might put an end to him who had the power of death, that is to say, the Evil One;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 8:15; 2 Timothy 1:7; Luke 1:74–75"> And let those who all their lives were in chains because of their fear of death, go free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 3:29; Galatians 3:16; Romans 4:16–25"> For, truly, he does not take on the life of angels, but that of the seed of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 4:14–2; Philippians 2:7–8; Hebrews 2:14"> Because of this it was necessary for him to be made like his brothers in every way, so that he might be a high priest full of mercy and keeping faith in everything to do with God, making offerings for the sins of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; Hebrews 4:15–16; Jude 1:24"> For having been put to the test himself, he is able to give help to others when they are tested.</span>
