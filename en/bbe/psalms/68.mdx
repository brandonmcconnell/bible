<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 15:20; Psalms 40:3; Exodus 17:9–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord gives the word; great is the number of the women who make it public.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 10:16; Judges 5:19; 1 Samuel 30:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Kings of armies quickly go in flight: and the women in the houses make a division of their goods.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 9:48; Psalms 51:7; Isaiah 1:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">When the Most High put the kings to flight, it was as white as snow in Salmon.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 87:1–2; Psalms 78:68–69; Deuteronomy 3:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">A hill of God is the hill of Bashan; a hill with high tops is the hill of Bashan.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5; Psalms 132:13–14; Revelation 21:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why are you looking with envy, you high hills, on the hill desired by God as his resting-place? truly, God will make it his house for ever.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:2; Daniel 7:10; Revelation 5:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">The war-carriage of God is among Israel's thousands; the Lord has come from Sinai to the holy place.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:13; Psalms 7:12; Psalms 110:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">The heads of the haters of God will be crushed; even the head of him who still goes on in his evil ways.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 21:33; Ezekiel 36:24; Amos 9:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord said, I will make them come back from Bashan, and from the deep parts of the sea;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 21:19; Psalms 58:10; 1 Kings 22:38"> <span className="pl pl-1">So that your foot may be red with blood, and the tongues of your dogs with the same.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 63:2; 2 Samuel 6:12–17; 1 Chronicles 15:16–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">We see your going, O God: even the going of my God, my King, into the holy place.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:8; Judges 11:34; Exodus 15:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">The makers of songs go before, the players of music come after, among the young girls playing on brass instruments.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 48:1; Deuteronomy 33:28; Psalms 26:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Give praise to God in the great meeting; even the Lord, you who come from the fountain of Israel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:21; Judges 20:35; Judges 21:6–25"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is little Benjamin ruling them, the chiefs of Judah and their army, the rulers of Zebulun and the rulers of Naphtali.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 138:8; Psalms 71:3; Psalms 42:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">O God, send out your strength; the strength, O God, with which you have done great things for us,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 10:10; Psalms 76:11; Isaiah 60:6–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Out of your Temple in Jerusalem.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 89:10; Psalms 22:12–13; Jeremiah 50:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Say sharp words to the beast among the water-plants, the band of strong ones, with the lords of the peoples, put an end to the people whose delight is in war.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 45:14; Zephaniah 3:10; Psalms 44:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Kings will give you offerings, they will come out of Egypt; from Pathros will come offerings of silver; Ethiopia will be stretching out her hands to God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 67:2–5; Romans 15:10–11; Psalms 117:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Make songs to God, you kingdoms of the earth; O make songs of praise to the Lord; (Selah.)</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 104:3; Psalms 18:10; Psalms 68:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">To him who goes or the clouds of heaven, the heaven which was from earliest times; he sends out his voice of power.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Revelation 19:6; 2 Peter 1:17; 1 Chronicles 16:28–29"> <span className="pl pl-1">Make clear that strength is God's: he is lifted up over Israel, and his power is in the clouds.</span><br /></span>
