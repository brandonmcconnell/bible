<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 15:34; Matthew 27:46; Hebrews 5:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">My God, my God, why are you turned away from me? why are you so far from helping me, and from the words of my crying?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 42:3; Luke 18:7; Luke 22:41–46"> <span className="pl pl-1">O my God, I make my cry in the day, and you give no answer; and in the night, and have no rest.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 50:23; Deuteronomy 10:21; Isaiah 6:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">But you are holy, O you who are seated among the praises of Israel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 14:31; Exodus 14:13–14; Hebrews 11:8–32"> <span className="pl pl-1">Our fathers had faith in you: they had faith and you were their saviour.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 10:11; Isaiah 49:23; Romans 9:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">They sent up their cry to you and were made free: they put their faith in you and were not put to shame.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 53:3; Job 25:6; Isaiah 49:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I am a worm and not a man; cursed by men, and looked down on by the people.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 15:29; Psalms 109:25; Matthew 27:39–40"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am laughed at by all those who see me: pushing out their lips and shaking their heads they say,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 27:42–43; Mark 15:30–32; Psalms 91:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">He put his faith in the Lord; let the Lord be his saviour now: let the Lord be his saviour, because he had delight in him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 71:6; Isaiah 49:1–2; Psalms 71:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">But it was you who took care of me from the day of my birth: you gave me faith even from my mother's breasts.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 1:15; Jeremiah 1:5; Isaiah 49:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">I was in your hands even before my birth; you are my God from the time when I was in my mother's body.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 71:12; Psalms 72:12; Psalms 10:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Be not far from me, for trouble is near; there is no one to give help.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 4:1–3; Psalms 68:30; Acts 4:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">A great herd of oxen is round me: I am shut in by the strong oxen of Bashan.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Psalms 35:21; Lamentations 3:46"> <span className="pl pl-1">I saw their mouths wide open, like lions crying after food.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 31:10; Daniel 5:6; Job 30:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am flowing away like water, and all my bones are out of place: my heart is like wax, it has become soft in my body.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 19:28; Psalms 69:3; Psalms 69:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">My throat is dry like a broken vessel; my tongue is fixed to the roof of my mouth, and the dust of death is on my lips.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 19:37; Zechariah 12:10; Matthew 27:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">Dogs have come round me: I am shut in by the band of evil-doers; they made wounds in my hands and feet.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 23:35; Job 33:21; Matthew 27:39–41"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am able to see all my bones; their looks are fixed on me:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 27:35; Luke 23:34; Mark 15:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">They make a division of my robes among them, by the decision of chance they take my clothing.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 22:11; Psalms 40:13; Psalms 40:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not be far from me, O Lord: O my strength, come quickly to my help.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 35:17; Zechariah 13:7; Psalms 17:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Make my soul safe from the sword, my life from the power of the dog.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:17; Numbers 23:22; 1 Peter 5:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Be my saviour from the lion's mouth; let me go free from the horns of the cruel oxen.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 2:11–12; Romans 8:29; Psalms 40:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will give the knowledge of your name to my brothers: I will give you praise among the people.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 135:19–20; Revelation 15:4; Psalms 107:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">You who have fear of the Lord, give him praise; all you seed of Jacob, give him glory; go in fear of him, all you seed of Israel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Isaiah 50:6–9; Psalms 116:3–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">For he has not been unmoved by the pain of him who is troubled; or kept his face covered from him; but he has given an answer to his cry.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 40:9–10; Psalms 35:18; Psalms 111:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">My praise will be of you in the great meeting: I will make my offerings before his worshippers.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 69:32; Psalms 40:16; Isaiah 65:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">The poor will have a feast of good things: those who make search for the Lord will give him praise: your heart will have life for ever.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 86:9; Isaiah 45:22; Psalms 2:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">All the ends of the earth will keep it in mind and be turned to the Lord: all the families of the nations will give him worship.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Zechariah 14:9; Psalms 47:7–8; Revelation 11:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the kingdom is the Lord's; he is the ruler among the nations.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Philippians 2:10; Isaiah 26:19; Psalms 45:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">All the fat ones of the earth will give him worship; all those who go down to the dust will make themselves low before him, even he who has not enough for the life of his soul.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 2:13; Psalms 14:5; 1 Peter 2:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">A seed will be his servant; the doings of the Lord will be made clear to the generation which comes after.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 78:6; Psalms 86:9; Romans 1:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will come and make his righteousness clear to a people of the future because he has done this.</span></span>
