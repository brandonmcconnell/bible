<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 11:1; Revelation 12:14; Psalms 139:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I said, If only I had wings like a dove! for then I would go in flight from here and be at rest.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 4:6; Matthew 7:25–27; Isaiah 17:12–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">I would quickly take cover from the driving storm and from the violent wind.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:7; Genesis 11:7–9; Jeremiah 23:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Send destruction on them, O Lord, make a division of tongues among them: for I have seen fighting and violent acts in the town.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 59:14–15; Isaiah 59:6–15; Ezekiel 9:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">By day and night they go round the town, on the walls; trouble and sorrow are in the heart of it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:26–27; Psalms 10:7; Psalms 5:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Evil is there; cruel rule and deceit are ever in the streets.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:12; Micah 7:5; Jeremiah 9:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">But it was you, my equal, my guide, my well-loved friend.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 42:4; Isaiah 2:3; Ezekiel 33:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">We had loving talk together, and went to the house of God in company.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 59:13; Psalms 64:7; Psalms 9:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let the hand of death come on them suddenly, and let them go down living into the underworld; because evil is in their houses and in their hearts.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Psalms 109:4; Psalms 73:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">As for me, I will make my prayer to God, and he will be my saviour.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; 1 Thessalonians 5:17; Psalms 141:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">In the evening and in the morning and in the middle of the day I will make my prayer with sounds of grief; and my voice will come to his ears.</span><br /></span>

(Selah.) Because they are unchanged, they have no fear of God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 12:1; Psalms 89:34; Psalms 7:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">He has put out his hand against those who were at peace with him; he has not kept his agreement.</span><br /></span>
