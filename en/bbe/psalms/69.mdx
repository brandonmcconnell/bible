<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 79:6; Hosea 5:10; Exodus 15:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let your curse come on them; let the heat of your wrath overtake them.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Philippians 4:3; Exodus 32:32–33; Luke 10:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let their names be taken from the book of the living, let them not be numbered with the upright.</span><br /></span>
