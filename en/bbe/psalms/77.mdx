<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Genesis 37:35; Isaiah 26:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">In the day of my trouble, my heart was turned to the Lord: my hand was stretched out in the night without resting; my soul would not be comforted.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 61:2; Psalms 142:2–3; Job 31:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will keep God in memory, with sounds of grief; my thoughts are troubled, and my spirit is overcome. (Selah.)</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 7:13–15; Psalms 6:6; Job 2:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">You keep my eyes from sleep; I am so troubled that no words come.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:7; Isaiah 51:9; Psalms 143:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">My thoughts go back to the days of the past, to the years which are gone.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 42:8; Psalms 4:4; Psalms 139:23–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">The memory of my song comes back to me in the night; my thoughts are moving in my heart; my spirit is searching with care.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 85:1; Lamentations 3:31–32; Psalms 85:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Will the Lord put me away for ever? will he be kind no longer?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Romans 9:6; 2 Peter 3:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Is his mercy quite gone for ever? has his word come to nothing?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 40:11; Isaiah 49:14–15; Isaiah 63:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Has God put away the memory of his pity? are his mercies shut up by his wrath? (Selah.)</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 42:3; Mark 9:24; Exodus 15:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I said, It is a weight on my spirit; but I will keep in mind the years of the right hand of the Most High.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 143:5; Psalms 105:5; 1 Chronicles 16:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will keep in mind the works of Jah: I will keep the memory of your wonders in the past.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 71:24; Psalms 145:11; Deuteronomy 6:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will give thought to all your work, while my mind goes over your acts of power.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 73:17; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 89:6–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your way, O God, is holy: what god is so great as our God?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 6:27; Psalms 136:4; Exodus 15:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">You are the God who does works of power: you have made your strength clear to the nations.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 6:6; Deuteronomy 9:29; Deuteronomy 9:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">With your arm you have made your people free, the sons of Jacob and Joseph. (Selah.)</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Habakkuk 3:15; Joshua 3:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">The waters saw you, O God; the waters saw you, they were in fear: even the deep was troubled.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 18:14; 2 Samuel 22:15; Habakkuk 3:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">The clouds sent out water; the skies gave out a sound; truly, your arrows went far and wide.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 97:4; 2 Samuel 22:14; Revelation 11:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">The voice of your thunder went rolling on; the world was flaming with the light of the storm; the earth was shaking.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 14:28; Psalms 29:10; Nahum 1:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your way was in the sea, and your road in the great waters; there was no knowledge of your footsteps.</span><br /></span>
