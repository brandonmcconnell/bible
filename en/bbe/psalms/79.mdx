<div className="hs">A Psalm. Of Asaph.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Micah 3:12; 2 Chronicles 36:19; Psalms 74:1–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">O God, the nations have come into your heritage; they have made your holy Temple unclean; they have made Jerusalem a mass of broken walls.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:33; Jeremiah 34:20; Jeremiah 16:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">They have given the bodies of your servants as food to the birds of the air, and the flesh of your saints to the beasts of the earth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:4; Jeremiah 14:16; Revelation 11:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their blood has been flowing like water round about Jerusalem; there was no one to put them in their last resting-place.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 80:6; Lamentations 5:1; Psalms 89:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">We are looked down on by our neighbours, we are laughed at and made sport of by those who are round us.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 89:46; Psalms 74:1; Psalms 85:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">How long, O Lord? will you be angry for ever? will your wrath go on burning like fire?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:25; 2 Thessalonians 1:8; Psalms 14:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let your wrath be on the nations who have no knowledge of you, and on the kingdoms who have not made prayer to your name.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:34–35; Zechariah 1:15; Isaiah 64:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">For they have taken Jacob for their meat, and made waste his house.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 64:9; Psalms 142:6; Psalms 116:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not keep in mind against us the sins of our fathers; let your mercy come to us quickly, for we have been made very low.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:7; Psalms 25:11; 2 Chronicles 14:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Give us help, O God of our salvation, for the glory of your name; take us out of danger and give us forgiveness for our sins, because of your name.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 42:10; Exodus 7:5; Psalms 115:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why may the nations say, Where is their God? Let payment for the blood of your servants be made openly among the nations before our eyes.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 102:20; Psalms 146:6–7; Isaiah 33:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let the cry of the prisoner come before you; with your strong arm make free the children of death;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:18; Genesis 4:15; Leviticus 26:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">And give punishment seven times over into the breast of our neighbours for the bitter words which they have said against you, O Lord.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 95:7; Isaiah 43:21; Psalms 74:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">So we your people, and the sheep of your flock, will give you glory for ever: we will go on praising you through all generations.</span></span>
