<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 5:8; Psalms 51:10; Luke 12:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">Truly, God is good to Israel, even to such as are clean in heart.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 94:18; 1 Samuel 2:9; Psalms 116:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">But as for me, my feet had almost gone from under me; I was near to slipping;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 21:7; Psalms 37:7; Proverbs 23:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because of my envy of the men of pride, when I saw the well-being of the wrongdoers.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 21:23–24; Ecclesiastes 2:16; Luke 16:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">For they have no pain; their bodies are fat and strong.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 21:9; Psalms 73:12; Revelation 3:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">They are not in trouble as others are; they have no part in the unhappy fate of men.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 109:18; 1 Peter 5:5; Daniel 4:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">For this reason pride is round them like a chain; they are clothed with violent behaviour as with a robe.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:28; Psalms 17:10; Job 15:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their eyes are bursting with fat; they have more than their heart's desire.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jude 1:16; Psalms 17:10; Psalms 10:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their thoughts are deep with evil designs; their talk from their seats of power is of cruel acts.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 13:6; James 3:6; Exodus 5:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their mouth goes up to heaven; their tongues go walking through the earth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 75:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">For this reason they are full of bread; and water is ever flowing for them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 7:2; Zephaniah 1:12; Job 22:13–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">And they say, How will the Lord see this? is there knowledge in the Most High?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 52:7; Psalms 49:6; Hosea 12:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Truly, such are the sinners; they do well at all times, and their wealth is increased.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 21:15; Psalms 26:6; Job 34:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">As for me, I have made my heart clean to no purpose, washing my hands in righteousness;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 12:5; Psalms 94:12; Job 7:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I have been troubled all the day; every morning have I undergone punishment.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:6–7; Malachi 2:8; 1 Peter 2:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">If I would make clear what it is like, I would say, You are false to the generation of your children.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:17; John 16:18–19; Psalms 36:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">When my thoughts were turned to see the reason of this, it was a weariness in my eyes;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 12:20; Psalms 77:13; Ecclesiastes 8:12–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Till I went into God's holy place, and saw the end of the evil-doers.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 35:6; Jeremiah 23:12; Deuteronomy 32:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">You put their feet where there was danger of slipping, so that they go down into destruction.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 28:1; Isaiah 47:11; Daniel 5:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">How suddenly are they wasted! fears are the cause of their destruction.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 20:8; Psalms 78:65; Psalms 90:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">As a dream when one is awake, they are ended; they are like an image gone out of mind when sleep is over.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Psalms 73:3; Job 16:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">My heart was made bitter, and I was pained by the bite of grief:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:18; Psalms 92:6; Psalms 49:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">As for me, I was foolish, and without knowledge; I was like a beast before you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 41:13; Psalms 63:8; Matthew 28:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">But still I am ever with you; you have taken me by my right hand.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 32:8; Isaiah 58:11; Proverbs 3:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your wisdom will be my guide, and later you will put me in a place of honour.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Philippians 3:8; Psalms 16:2; Psalms 63:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Whom have I in heaven but you? and having you I have no desire for anything on earth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Isaiah 40:29–31; Psalms 138:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">My flesh and my heart are wasting away: but God is the Rock of my heart and my eternal heritage.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 4:4; Psalms 119:155; Exodus 34:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">For those who are far away from you will come to destruction: you will put an end to all those who have not kept faith with you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="James 4:8; Psalms 40:5; Hebrews 10:19–22"> <span className="pl pl-1">But it is good for me to come near to God: I have put my faith in the Lord God, so that I may make clear all his works.</span></span>
