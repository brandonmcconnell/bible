<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 22:2; Luke 18:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">O Lord, God of my salvation, I have been crying to you for help by day and by night:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 31:2; Psalms 141:1–2; Lamentations 3:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let my prayer come before you; give ear to my cry:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 107:18; Lamentations 3:15–19; Mark 14:33–34"> <span className="pl pl-1">For my soul is full of evils, and my life has come near to the underworld.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 28:1; 2 Corinthians 1:9; Psalms 143:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am numbered among those who go down into the earth; I have become like a man for whom there is no help:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 53:8; Psalms 31:22; Isaiah 14:9–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">My soul is among the dead, like those in the underworld, to whom you give no more thought; for they are cut off from your care.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 69:15; Psalms 86:13; Psalms 143:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have put me in the lowest deep, even in dark places.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 42:7; Job 6:4; Romans 2:5–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">The weight of your wrath is crushing me, all your waves have overcome me. (Selah.)</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 31:11; Isaiah 49:7; Jeremiah 32:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have sent my friends far away from me; you have made me a disgusting thing in their eyes: I am shut up, and not able to come out.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 11:13; Psalms 38:10; Psalms 86:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">My eyes are wasting away because of my trouble: Lord, my cry has gone up to you every day, my hands are stretched out to you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 6:5; Isaiah 26:19; Psalms 30:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Will you do works of wonder for the dead? will the shades come back to give you praise? (Selah.)</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 26:6; Proverbs 15:11; Psalms 73:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Will the story of your mercy be given in the house of the dead? will news of your faith come to the place of destruction?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:5; Ecclesiastes 2:16; Isaiah 8:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">May there be knowledge of your wonders in the dark? or of your righteousness where memory is dead?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 5:3; Psalms 30:2; Mark 1:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">But to you did I send up my cry, O Lord; in the morning my prayer came before you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 13:24; Psalms 13:1; Psalms 44:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lord, why have you sent away my soul? why is your face covered from me?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 6:4; Luke 22:44; Job 7:11–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have been troubled and in fear of death from the time when I was young; your wrath is hard on me, and I have no strength.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 90:11; Isaiah 53:4–6; Romans 8:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">The heat of your wrath has gone over me; I am broken by your cruel punishments.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 22:16; Psalms 116:3; Psalms 69:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">They are round me all the day like water; they have made a circle about me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 88:8; Psalms 38:11; Job 19:12–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have sent my friends and lovers far from me; I am gone from the memory of those who are dear to me.</span></span>
