<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 48:2; Psalms 80:1; Revelation 21:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">From Zion, most beautiful of places, God has sent out his light.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 7:10; Numbers 16:35; Leviticus 10:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Our God will come, and will not keep quiet; with fire burning before him, and storm-winds round him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Isaiah 1:2; Deuteronomy 31:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">His voice will go out to the heavens and to the earth, for the judging of his people:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 26:28; Exodus 24:3–8; Hebrews 13:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let my saints come together to me; those who have made an agreement with me by offerings.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 97:6; Psalms 75:7; Psalms 89:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">And let the heavens make clear his righteousness; for God himself is the judge. (Selah.)</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 20:2; Psalms 81:8; Jeremiah 2:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Give ear, O my people, to my words; O Israel, I will be a witness against you; I am God, even your God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 1:11–31; Hebrews 10:4–10; Hosea 6:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will not take up a cause against you because of your offerings, or because of your burned offerings, which are ever before me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Micah 6:6–8; Hebrews 10:4–6; Psalms 69:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will take no ox out of your house, or he-goats from your flocks;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:5–6; Psalms 8:6–8; Psalms 104:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">For every beast of the woodland is mine, and the cattle on a thousand hills.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 1:20–22; Matthew 6:26; Luke 12:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">I see all the birds of the mountains, and the beasts of the field are mine.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 19:5; Deuteronomy 10:14; Psalms 24:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">If I had need of food, I would not give you word of it; for the earth is mine and all its wealth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Am I to take the flesh of the ox for my food, or the blood of goats for my drink?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15; Psalms 56:12; Deuteronomy 23:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Make an offering of praise to God; keep the agreements which you have made with the Most High;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 107:28; Psalms 91:15; Psalms 34:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let your voice come up to me in the day of trouble; I will be your saviour, so that you may give glory to me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Psalms 78:36–38; Romans 2:17–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">But to the sinner, God says, What are you doing, talking of my laws, or taking the words of my agreement in your mouth?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:26; Proverbs 12:1; 2 Timothy 4:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Seeing that you have no desire for my teaching, turning your back on my words.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 1:32; 1 Timothy 5:22; Hebrews 13:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">When you saw a thief, you were in agreement with him, and you were joined with those who took other men's wives.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Psalms 52:2–4; Psalms 55:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have given your mouth to evil, your tongue to words of deceit.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 10:21; Proverbs 10:18; Leviticus 19:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">You say evil of your brother; you make false statements against your mother's son.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 2:4–5; Proverbs 29:1; Isaiah 57:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">These things have you done, and I said nothing; it seemed to you that I was such a one as yourself; but I will make a protest against you, and put them in order before your eyes.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 8:13; Psalms 9:17; Hosea 4:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Now keep this in mind, you who have no memory of God, for fear that you may be crushed under my hand, with no one to give you help:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 91:16; James 3:13; Psalms 24:4–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Whoever makes an offering of praise gives glory to me; and to him who is upright in his ways I will make clear the salvation of God.</span></span>
