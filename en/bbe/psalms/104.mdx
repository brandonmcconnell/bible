<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 107:23; Isaiah 27:1; Psalms 74:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">There go the ships; there is that great beast, which you have made as a plaything.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 136:25; Psalms 147:9; Job 38:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">All of them are waiting for you, to give them their food in its time.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 145:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">They take what you give them; they are full of the good things which come from your open hand.</span><br /></span>
