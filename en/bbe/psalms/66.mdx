<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 18:44; Psalms 65:5; Psalms 81:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Say to God, How greatly to be feared are your works! because of your great power your haters are forced to put themselves under your feet.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 22:27; Psalms 117:1; Psalms 96:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let all the earth give you worship, and make songs to you; let them make songs to your name. (Selah.)</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 126:1–3; Numbers 23:23; Psalms 66:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Come and see the works of God: he is to be feared in all he does to the children of men.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 3:16; Exodus 15:1–21; Joshua 3:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">The sea was turned into dry land: they went through the river on foot: there did we have joy in him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 11:4; Matthew 28:18; Psalms 140:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">He is ruling in power for ever; his eyes are watching the nations: may his haters have no strength against him. (Selah.)</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 98:4; Revelation 19:1; Jeremiah 33:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Give blessings to our God, O you peoples, let the voice of his praise be loud;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 121:3; Psalms 112:6; Psalms 94:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because he gives us life, and has not let our feet be moved.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 48:10; Psalms 17:3; 1 Peter 1:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">For you, O God, have put us to the test: testing us by fire like silver.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Lamentations 1:13; Ezekiel 12:13; Lamentations 3:2–66"> <span className="pl pl-1">You let us be put in prison; chains were put on our legs.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 43:1–2; Isaiah 51:23; Psalms 40:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">You let men go driving over our heads; we went through fire and through water; but you took us out into a wide place.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 100:4; Psalms 56:12; Ecclesiastes 5:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will come into your house with burned offerings, I will make payment of my debt to you,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:11; Judges 11:35–36; Psalms 18:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Keeping the word which came from my lips, and which my mouth said, when I was in trouble.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 51:19; 2 Samuel 6:17–19; 1 Chronicles 16:1–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will give you burned offerings of fat beasts, and the smoke of sheep; I will make offerings of oxen and goats. (Selah.)</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 71:15; Psalms 34:11; Psalms 71:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Come, give ear to me, all you God-fearing men, so that I may make clear to you what he has done for my soul.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 34:6; Psalms 145:1; Psalms 116:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">My voice went up to him, and I was lifted up from the underworld.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 9:31; Proverbs 28:9; Proverbs 15:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">I said in my heart, The Lord will not give ear to me:</span><br /></span>
