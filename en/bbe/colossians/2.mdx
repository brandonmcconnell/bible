<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:29; Philippians 1:30; Revelation 3:14–22"> For it is my desire to give you news of the great fight I am making for you and for those at Laodicea, and for all who have not seen my face in the flesh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 1:27; Colossians 4:8; Ephesians 6:22"> So that their hearts may be comforted, and that being joined together in love, they may come to the full wealth of the certain knowledge of the secret of God, even Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:24; 1 Corinthians 1:30; Romans 11:33"> In whom are all the secret stores of wisdom and knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 4:1; Ephesians 4:14; Romans 16:18–19"> I say this so that you may not be turned away by any deceit of words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:3–4; 1 Thessalonians 2:17; 1 Corinthians 16:13"> For though I am not present in the flesh, still I am with you in the spirit, seeing with joy your order, and your unchanging faith in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; 1 John 2:6; 1 Thessalonians 4:1"> As, then, you took Christ Jesus the Lord, so go on in him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 3:17; Jeremiah 17:8; Ephesians 2:20–22"> Rooted and based together in him, strong in the faith which the teaching gave you, giving praise to God at all times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:20; Ephesians 5:6; Matthew 7:15"> Take care that no one takes you away by force, through man's wisdom and deceit, going after the beliefs of men and the theories of the world, and not after Christ:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 1:19; John 10:30; John 1:14"> For in him all the wealth of God's being has a living form,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 3:19; Colossians 1:16–18; Ephesians 1:20–23"> And you are complete in him, who is the head of all rule and authority:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 2:29; Philippians 3:3; Romans 6:6"> In whom you had a circumcision not made with hands, in the putting off of the body of the flesh, in the circumcision of Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 6:3–5; 1 Peter 3:21; Galatians 3:27"> Having been put to death with him in baptism, by which you came to life again with him, through faith in the working of God, who made him come back from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 2:5–6; Romans 8:11; Ephesians 2:1"> And you, being dead through your sins and the evil condition of your flesh, to you, I say, he gave life together with him, and forgiveness of all our sins;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 44:22; Ephesians 2:14–16; Isaiah 43:25"> Having put an end to the handwriting of the law which was against us, taking it out of the way by nailing it to his cross;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 2:14; Ephesians 4:8; Matthew 12:29"> Having made himself free from the rule of authorities and powers, he put them openly to shame, glorying over them in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 14:5–6; Romans 14:10; Galatians 4:10"> For this reason let no man be your judge in any question of food or drink or feast days or new moons or Sabbaths:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 10:1; Hebrews 8:5; John 1:17"> For these are an image of the things which are to come; but the body is Christ's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; 1 John 4:1–2; Colossians 2:23"> Let no man take your reward from you by consciously making little of himself and giving worship to angels; having his thoughts fixed on the things which he has seen, being foolishly lifted up in his natural mind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 4:15–16; John 15:4–6; 2 Peter 3:18"> And not joined to the Head, from whom all the body, being given strength and kept together through its joins and bands, has its growth with the increase of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; Colossians 2:16; Galatians 4:3"> If you were made free, by your death with Christ, from the rules of the world, why do you put yourselves under the authority of orders</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:3; Genesis 3:3; Isaiah 52:11"> Which say there may be no touching, tasting, or taking in your hands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Titus 1:14; 1 Corinthians 6:13"> (Rules which are all to come to an end with their use) after the orders and teaching of men?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:8; Colossians 2:18; Colossians 2:8"> These things seem to have a sort of wisdom in self-ordered worship and making little of oneself, and being cruel to the body, not honouring it by giving it its natural use.</span>
