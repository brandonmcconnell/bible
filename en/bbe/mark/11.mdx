<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 21:1–11; John 12:14–19; Luke 19:29–40"> And when they came near to Jerusalem, to Beth-phage and Bethany, at the Mountain of Olives, he sent two of his disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 21:2–3; Luke 19:30–31"> And said to them, Go into the little town opposite: and when you come to it, you will see a young ass with a cord round his neck, on which no man has ever been seated; let him loose, and come back with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 10:36; Mark 14:15; Psalms 110:3"> And if anyone says to you, Why are you doing this? say, The Lord has need of him and will send him back straight away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 26:19; Luke 19:32–34; Matthew 21:6–7"> And they went away and saw a young ass by the door outside in the open street; and they were getting him loose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And some of those who were there said to them, What are you doing, taking the ass?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And they said to them the words which Jesus had said; and they let them go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Luke 19:35–36; Matthew 21:7–8"> And they took the young ass to Jesus, and put their clothing on him, and he got on his back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 23:40"> And a great number put down their clothing in the way; and others put down branches which they had taken from the fields.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 118:25–26; Matthew 21:9; Matthew 23:39"> And those who went in front, and those who came after, were crying, Glory: A blessing on him who comes in the name of the Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:24–25; Luke 2:14; Luke 19:38–40"> A blessing on the coming kingdom of our father David: Glory in the highest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 8:1–2; Luke 19:41–45; Luke 21:37–38"> And he went into Jerusalem into the Temple; and after looking round about on all things, it being now evening, he went out to Bethany with the twelve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 21:18–22; Matthew 4:2; John 19:28"> And on the day after, when they had come out from Bethany, he was in need of food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 21:19; Luke 13:6–9; Isaiah 5:7"> And seeing a fig-tree in the distance with leaves, he went to see if by chance it had anything on it: and when he came to it, he saw nothing but leaves, for it was not the time for the fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 21:19; Matthew 7:19; Matthew 3:10"> And he said to it, Let no man take fruit from you for ever. And his disciples took note of his words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 2:13–17; Matthew 21:12–16; Luke 19:45–47"> And they came to Jerusalem; and he went into the Temple, and sent out those who were trading there, overturning the tables of the money-changers and the seats of those who were offering doves for money;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And he would not let any man take a vessel through the Temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; Jeremiah 7:11; Luke 19:46"> And he gave them teaching, and said to them, Is it not in the Writings, My house is to be named a house of prayer for all the nations? but you have made it a hole of thieves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; Mark 12:12; Mark 14:1–2"> And it came to the ears of the chief priests and scribes, and they took thought how they might put him to death; being in fear of him, because all the people were full of wonder at his teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 21:37; Mark 11:11; John 12:36"> And every evening he went out of the town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 15:13; Mark 11:14; John 15:6"> And when they were going by in the morning, they saw the fig-tree dead from the roots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 3:33; Matthew 23:7; Matthew 25:41"> And Peter, having a memory of it, said to him, Master, see, the tree which was cursed by you is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 62:8; John 14:1; Mark 9:23"> And Jesus, answering, said to them, Have God's faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 17:20; John 15:7; Matthew 21:21"> Truly I say to you, Whoever says to this mountain, Be taken up and be put into the sea; and has no doubt in his heart, but has faith that what he says will come about, he will have his desire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; 1 John 5:14–15; 1 John 3:22"> For this reason I say to you, Whatever you make a request for in prayer, have faith that it has been given to you, and you will have it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 6:14–15; Colossians 3:13; Ephesians 4:32"> And whenever you make a prayer, let there be forgiveness in your hearts, if you have anything against anyone; so that you may have forgiveness for your sins from your Father who is in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> &#91;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 20:1–8; Matthew 21:23–27; Psalms 2:1–5"> And they came again to Jerusalem: and while he was walking in the Temple, there came to him the chief priests and the scribes and those in authority:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 2:14; Acts 7:38–39; Numbers 16:3"> And they said to him, By what authority do you do these things? or who gave you authority to do these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 20:3–8; Matthew 21:24; Isaiah 52:13"> And Jesus said to them, I will put to you one question; give me an answer, and I will say by what authority I do these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 3:1–20; John 3:25–36; Mark 1:1–11"> The baptism of John, was it from heaven or from men? give me an answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 1:29; John 1:34; Matthew 21:31–32"> And they gave thought to it among themselves, saying, If we say, From heaven; he will say, Why then did you not have faith in him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 21:46; Mark 12:12; Matthew 14:5"> But if we say, From men — they were in fear of the people, because all took John to be truly a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4; 2 Thessalonians 2:10–12"> And they said in answer to Jesus, We have no idea. And Jesus said to them, And I will not say to you by what authority I do these things.</span>
