<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 15:1–20; Mark 3:22; Luke 5:17"> And there came together to him the Pharisees and certain of the scribes who had come from Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 10:28; Matthew 7:3–5; Romans 14:14"> And had seen that some of his disciples took their bread with unclean, that is, unwashed, hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 1:14; Mark 7:13; Colossians 2:8"> Now the Pharisees, and all the Jews, do not take food without washing their hands with care, keeping the old rule which has been handed down to them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 9:10; Matthew 23:25; Luke 11:38–39"> And when they come from the market-place, they take no food till their hands are washed; and a number of other orders there are, which have been handed down to them to keep — washings of cups and pots and brass vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:6; Acts 21:21; Matthew 15:2"> And the Pharisees and the scribes put the question to him, Why do your disciples not keep the rules of the fathers, but take their bread with unwashed hands?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Titus 1:16; Matthew 15:7–9"> And he said, Well did Isaiah say of you, you false ones: These people give me honour with their lips, but their heart is far from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Matthew 15:9; James 1:26"> But their worship is to no purpose, while they give as their teaching the rules of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 7:3–4; Isaiah 1:12"> For, turning away from the law of God, you keep the rules of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:16–17; Galatians 2:21; Isaiah 29:13"> And he said to them, Truly you put on one side the law of God, so that you may keep the rules which have been handed down to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 21:17; Exodus 20:12; Deuteronomy 5:16"> For Moses said, Give honour to your father and mother, and, He who says evil of father or mother, let him have the punishment of death:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 23:18; 1 Timothy 5:4–8; Matthew 15:5"> But you say, If a man says to his father or his mother, That by which you might have had profit from me is Corban, that is to say, Given to God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> You no longer let him do anything for his father or his mother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 7:9; Titus 1:14; Matthew 5:17–20"> Making the word of God of no effect by your rule, which you have given: and a number of other such things you do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 15:10; Isaiah 6:9; Proverbs 8:5"> And turning to the people again, he said to them, Give ear to me all of you, and let my words be clear to you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 4:23; Leviticus 11:42–47; Titus 1:15"> There is nothing outside the man which, going into him, is able to make him unclean: but the things which come out of the man are those which make the man unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> &#91;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 15:15; Matthew 13:36; Mark 9:28"> And when he had gone into the house away from all the people, his disciples put questions to him about the saying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 15:16–17; Hebrews 5:11; Isaiah 28:9–10"> And he said to them, Have even you so little wisdom? Do you not see that whatever goes into a man from outside is not able to make him unclean,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 15:17; 1 Corinthians 6:13; Colossians 2:21–22"> Because it goes not into the heart but into the stomach, and goes out with the waste? He said this, making all food clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 3:6; James 1:14–15; James 4:1"> And he said, That which comes out of the man, that makes the man unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:9; Romans 8:7–8; James 4:1–3"> Because from inside, from the heart of men, come evil thoughts and unclean pleasures,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 20:15; Matthew 6:23; 1 Peter 2:15"> The taking of goods and of life, broken faith between husband and wife, the desire of wealth, wrongdoing, deceit, sins of the flesh, an evil eye, angry words, pride, foolish acts:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 7:15; Titus 1:15; 1 Corinthians 3:17"> All these evil things come from inside, and make the man unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 15:21–28; Matthew 11:21; Ezekiel 28:2"> And he went away from there to the country of Tyre and Sidon. And he went into a house, desiring that no man might have knowledge of it: and he was not able to keep it secret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Mark 9:17–23; Acts 10:25–26"> But a woman, whose little daughter had an unclean spirit, having had news of him, came straight away and went down at his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Isaiah 49:12; Galatians 3:28"> Now the woman was a Greek, a Syro-phoenician by birth: and she made a request to him that he would send the evil spirit out of her daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 15:23–28; Matthew 7:6; Acts 22:21"> And he said to her, Let the children first have their food: for it is not right to take the children's bread and give it to the dogs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 10:12; Romans 3:29; Acts 11:17–18"> But she said to him in answer, Yes, Lord: even the dogs under the table take the bits dropped by the children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 66:2; Matthew 5:3; 1 John 3:8"> And he said to her, For this saying go your way; the evil spirit has gone out of your daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 John 3:8; John 4:50–52"> And she went away to her house, and saw the child on the bed, and the evil spirit gone out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 5:20; Matthew 15:29–31; Matthew 4:25"> And again he went out from Tyre, and came through Sidon to the sea of Galilee, through the country of Decapolis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 11:14; Mark 5:23; Matthew 9:32–33"> And they came to him with one who had no power of hearing and had trouble in talking; and they made a request to him to put his hands on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 8:23; John 9:6–7; Mark 5:40"> And he took him on one side from the people privately, and put his fingers into his ears, and he put water from his mouth on the man's tongue with his finger;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 11:41; Mark 5:41; Mark 6:41"> And looking up to heaven, he took a deep breath, and said to him, Ephphatha, that is, Be open.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5–6; Psalms 33:9; Mark 2:12"> And his ears became open, and the band of his tongue was made loose, and his words became clear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Mark 8:26; Mark 3:12"> And he gave them orders not to give news of it to anyone; but the more he made this request, so much the more they made it public.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 6:51; Mark 5:42; Exodus 4:10–11"> And they were overcome with wonder, saying, He has done all things well: he even gives back the power of hearing and the power of talking to those who have been without them.</span>
