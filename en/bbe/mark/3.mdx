<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 12:9–14; Luke 6:6–11; Mark 1:21"> And he went again into the Synagogue; and there was a man there whose hand was dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 37:32; Luke 14:1; Luke 6:7"> And they were watching him to see if he would make him well on the Sabbath day, so that they might have something against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 1:28–30; Isaiah 42:4; Luke 6:8"> And he said to the man, Get up and come forward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 6:9; Matthew 12:10–12; Hosea 6:6"> And he said to them, Is it right to do good on the Sabbath or to do evil? to give life or to put to death? But they said nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 6:10; Ephesians 4:18; Romans 11:25"> And looking round on them he was angry, being sad because of their hard hearts; and he said to the man, Put out your hand. And he put it out, and his hand was made well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 22:16; Mark 12:13; Matthew 12:14"> And the Pharisees went out, and straight away made designs with the Herodians about how they might put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 4:25; Luke 6:17; Matthew 12:15"> And Jesus went away with his disciples to the sea, and a great number from Galilee came after him: and from Judaea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:15; Isaiah 34:5; Matthew 11:21"> And from Jerusalem, and from Idumaea, and the other side of Jordan, and the country about Tyre and Sidon, a great number, hearing what great things he did, came to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 5:30; John 6:15"> And he made a request to his disciples to have a little boat ready for him, so that he might not be crushed by the people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 14:36; Mark 6:56; Matthew 4:23"> For he had made such a great number well that all those who were diseased were falling down before him for the purpose of touching him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Mark 1:23–24; Luke 8:28"> And the unclean spirits, whenever they saw him, went down before him, crying out, and saying, You are the Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:16; Mark 1:25; Mark 1:34"> And he gave them special orders not to say who he was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:1; Luke 6:12–16; Matthew 10:1–4"> And he went up into the mountain, and sent for those whom it was his pleasure to have with him: and they went to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Luke 9:1–6; John 15:16"> And he took twelve to be with him, so that he might send them out as preachers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And give them the power of driving out evil spirits:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 1:42; Acts 1:13; Matthew 16:16–18"> To Simon he gave the second name of Peter;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Mark 14:33; Jeremiah 23:29"> And to James, the son of Zebedee, and John, the brother of James, he gave the second name of Boanerges, which is, Sons of thunder:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Mark 2:14; Matthew 9:9"> And Andrew, and Philip, and Bartholomew, and Matthew, and Thomas, and James, the son of Alphaeus, and Thaddaeus, and Simon the Zealot;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 6:71; John 13:2; Acts 1:16–25"> And Judas Iscariot, who was false to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 6:31; John 4:31–34; Mark 9:28"> And he went into a house. And the people came together again, so that they were not even able to take bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 26:24; John 10:20; Mark 3:31"> And when his friends had news of it, they went out to get him, saying, He is off his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 15:1; Matthew 10:25; Matthew 9:34"> And the scribes who came down from Jerusalem, said, He has Beelzebub, and, By the ruler of evil spirits he sends evil spirits out of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 11:17–23; Matthew 12:25–30; Matthew 13:34"> And turning to them, he said to them in the form of a story, How is it possible for Satan to put out Satan?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 11:14; Isaiah 19:2–3; 2 Samuel 20:6"> If there is division in a kingdom, that kingdom will come to destruction;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 3:16; Psalms 133:1; Galatians 5:15"> And if there is division in a house, that house will come to destruction;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And if Satan is at war with himself, and there is division in him, he will not keep his place but will come to an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 49:24–26; Matthew 12:29; Luke 11:21–23"> But no one is able to go into the house of the strong man and take his goods, without first putting cords round the strong man, and then he will take his goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 12:10; Matthew 12:31–32; 1 John 5:16"> Truly, I say to you, The sons of men will have forgiveness for all their sins and for all the evil words they say:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 12:31–32; Luke 12:10; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> But whoever says evil things against the Holy Spirit will never have forgiveness, but the evil he has done will be with him for ever:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 10:20; Mark 3:22"> Because they said, He has an unclean spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 8:19–21; Matthew 12:46–50"> And his mother and brothers came and were outside, and sent for him, requesting to see him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And a great number were seated round him; and they said to him, See, your mother and your brothers are outside looking for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 3:21; Deuteronomy 33:9; Mark 6:3"> And he said in answer, Who are my mother and my brothers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 12:49–50; Luke 11:27–28; Romans 8:29"> And looking round at those who were seated about him, he said, See, my mother and my brothers!</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 2:17; Matthew 7:21; 1 John 3:22–23"> Whoever does God's pleasure, the same is my brother, and sister, and mother.</span>
