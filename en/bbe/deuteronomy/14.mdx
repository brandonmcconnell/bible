<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 21:5; Romans 9:8; Galatians 3:26"> You are the children of the Lord your God: you are not to make cuts on your bodies or take off the hair on your brows in honour of the dead;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6; Leviticus 20:26; Exodus 19:5–6"> For you are a holy people to the Lord your God, and the Lord has taken you to be his special people out of all the nations on the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:14; Romans 14:14; Leviticus 20:25"> No disgusting thing may be your food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 11:2–45; Acts 10:14; 1 Kings 4:23"> These are the beasts which you may have for food: the ox, the sheep, and the goat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The hart, the gazelle, and the roe, the mountain goat and the pygarg and the antelope and the mountain sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 18:1; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Psalms 1:1–2"> Any beast which has a division in the horn of its foot and whose food comes back into its mouth to be crushed again, may be used for food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:22–23; Titus 1:16; 2 Timothy 3:5"> But even among these, there are some which may not be used for food: such as the camel, the hare, and the coney, which are unclean to you, because, though their food comes back, the horn of their feet is not parted in two.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 11:26–27; Isaiah 66:3; 2 Peter 2:22"> And the pig is unclean to you, because though it has a division in the horn of its foot, its food does not come back; their flesh may not be used for food or their dead bodies touched by you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 11:9–12"> And of the things living in the waters, you may take all those who have wings for swimming with and skins formed of thin plates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> But any which have no skin-plates or wings for swimming, you may not take; they are unclean for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> All clean birds may be used for food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 11:13–19"> But these birds you may not take: the eagle and the gier-eagle and the ospray;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The falcon and the kite, and birds of that sort;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Every raven, and all birds of that sort;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 30:29"> And the ostrich and the night-hawk and the sea-hawk and birds of that sort;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The little owl and the great owl and the water-hen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the pelican and the vulture and the cormorant;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The stork and the heron and birds of that sort, and the hoopoe and the bat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philippians 3:19; Leviticus 11:20–23"> Every winged thing which goes flat on the earth is unclean to you and may not be used as food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> But all clean birds you may take.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 23:19; Exodus 34:26; Leviticus 22:8"> You may not have as food anything which has come to a natural death; the man from another country who is living with you may take it for food, or you may get a price for it from one of another nation; for you are a holy people to the Lord your God. The young goat is not to be cooked in its mother's milk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:37; Deuteronomy 12:17; Deuteronomy 26:12–15"> Put on one side a tenth of all the increase of your seed, produced year by year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5–7; Deuteronomy 4:10; Deuteronomy 12:17–18"> And make a feast before the Lord your God, in the place which is to be marked out, where his name will be for ever, of the tenth part of your grain and your wine and your oil, and the first births of your herds and your flocks; so that you may have the fear of the Lord your God in your hearts at all times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:21; Deuteronomy 12:5; Deuteronomy 11:24"> And if the way is so long that you are not able to take these things to the place marked out by the Lord your God for his name, when he has given you his blessing, because it is far away from you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Then let these things be exchanged for money, and, taking the money in your hand, go to the place marked out by the Lord your God for himself;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:7; Deuteronomy 12:7; Matthew 21:12"> And with the money get whatever you have a desire for, oxen or sheep or wine or strong drink, whatever your soul's desire may be: and make a feast there before the Lord your God, and be glad, you and all your house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 18:20; Deuteronomy 14:29; Deuteronomy 12:12"> And give a thought to the Levite who is living among you, for he has no part or heritage in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:22; Amos 4:4; Deuteronomy 26:12–15"> At the end of every three years take a tenth part of all your increase for that year, and put it in store inside your walls:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:10; Deuteronomy 14:27; Proverbs 19:17"> And the Levite, because he has no part or heritage in the land, and the man from a strange country, and the child who has no father, and the widow, who are living among you, will come and take food and have enough; and so the blessing of the Lord your God will be on you in everything you do.</span>
