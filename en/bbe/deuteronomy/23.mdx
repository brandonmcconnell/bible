<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; Isaiah 56:3–4; Leviticus 21:17–21"> No man whose private parts have been wounded or cut off may come into the meeting of the Lord's people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 9:6; Isaiah 57:3; Hebrews 12:8"> One whose father and mother are not married may not come into the meeting of the Lord's people, or any of his family to the tenth generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:1–2; Nehemiah 13:23; Ruth 4:6"> No Ammonite or Moabite or any of their people to the tenth generation may come into the meeting of the Lord's people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:2; 2 Peter 2:15; 1 Kings 18:4"> Because they gave you no bread or water on your way, when you came out of Egypt: and they got Balaam, the son of Peor, from Pethor in Aram-naharaim to put curses on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Romans 8:31; Ephesians 2:4–5"> But the Lord your God would not give ear to Balaam, but let the curse be changed into a blessing to you, because of his love for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra 9:12; 2 Samuel 12:31; Nehemiah 13:23–25"> Do nothing for their peace or well-being for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:19; Exodus 22:21; Exodus 23:9"> But have no hate for an Edomite, because he is your brother, or for an Egyptian, for you were living in his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 20:5–6; Ephesians 2:12–13; Deuteronomy 23:1–3"> Their children in the third generation may come into the meeting of the Lord's people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 6:18; Judges 20:26; Revelation 19:11–14"> When you go out to war and put your tents in position, keep from every evil thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 15:16; Numbers 5:2–3; 1 Corinthians 5:11–13"> If any man among you becomes unclean through anything which has taken place in the night, he is to go out from the tent-circle and keep outside it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; Ephesians 5:26–27; Leviticus 14:9"> But when evening comes near, let him take a bath: and after sundown he may come back to the tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Let there be a place outside the tent-circle to which you may go;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:6–8"> And have among your arms a spade; and when you have been to that place, let that which comes from you be covered up with earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 17:1; Leviticus 26:12; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> For the Lord your God is walking among your tents, to keep you safe and to give up into your hands those who are fighting against you; then let your tents be holy, so that he may see no unclean thing among you, and be turned away from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:15; Obadiah 1:14"> Do not give back to his master a servant who has gone in flight from his master and come to you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 22:21; Zechariah 7:10; Jeremiah 7:6"> Let him go on living among you in whatever place is most pleasing to him: do not be hard on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 23:7; 1 Kings 14:24; Leviticus 19:29"> No daughter of Israel is to let herself be used as a loose woman for a strange god, and no son of Israel is to give himself to a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philippians 3:2; Matthew 7:6; Revelation 22:15"> Do not take into the house of the Lord your God, as an offering for an oath, the price of a loose woman or the money given to one used for sex purposes in the worship of the gods: for these two things are disgusting to the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 22:25; Leviticus 25:35–37; Psalms 15:5"> Do not take interest from an Israelite on anything, money or food or any other goods, which you let him have:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:10; Deuteronomy 15:3; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> From men of other nations you may take interest, but not from an Israelite: so that the blessing of the Lord your God may be on everything to which you put your hand, in the land which you are about to take as your heritage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:4–5; Psalms 66:13–14; Psalms 116:18"> When you take an oath to the Lord, do not be slow to give effect to it: for without doubt the Lord your God will make you responsible, and will put it to your account as sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> But if you take no oath, there will be no sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 20:25; Judges 11:30–31; 1 Samuel 1:11"> Whatever your lips have said, see that you do it; for you gave your word freely to the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:26; Romans 12:13; Hebrews 13:5"> When you go into your neighbour's vine-garden, you may take of his grapes at your pleasure, but you may not take them away in your vessel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 12:1–2; Mark 2:23; Luke 6:1–2"> When you go into your neighbour's field, you may take the heads of grain with your hand; but you may not put your blade to his grain.</span>
