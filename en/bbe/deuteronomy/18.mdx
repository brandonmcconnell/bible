<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 18:8–9; Deuteronomy 10:9; 1 Corinthians 9:13–14"> The priests, the Levites, that is, all the tribe of Levi, will have no part or heritage with Israel: their food and their heritage will be the offerings of the Lord made by fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 1:5–6; Psalms 84:11; 1 Peter 2:5"> And they will have no heritage among their countrymen: the Lord is their heritage, as he has said to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 7:30–34; Deuteronomy 12:27"> And this is to be the priests' right: those who make an offering of a sheep or an ox are to give to the priest the top part of the leg and the two sides of the head and the stomach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:44–47; Numbers 18:12–24; 2 Chronicles 31:4–10"> And in addition you are to give him the first of your grain and wine and oil, and the first wool cut from your sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:8; Numbers 3:10; Exodus 28:1–14"> For he, and his sons after him for ever, have been marked out by the Lord your God from all your tribes, to do the work of priests in the name of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 35:2–3; Deuteronomy 12:5; Deuteronomy 16:2"> And if a Levite, moved by a strong desire, comes from any town in all Israel where he is living to the place marked out by the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:2–4"> Then he will do the work of a priest in the name of the Lord his God, with all his brothers the Levites who are there before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:44; Nehemiah 12:47; 2 Chronicles 31:4"> His food will be the same as theirs, in addition to what has come to him as the price of his property.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:29–31; Leviticus 18:26–27; Leviticus 18:30"> When you have come into the land which the Lord your God is giving you, do not take as your example the disgusting ways of those nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; Exodus 22:18; Leviticus 19:26"> Let there not be seen among you anyone who makes his son or his daughter go through the fire, or anyone using secret arts, or a maker of strange sounds, or a reader of signs, or any wonder-worker,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; 1 Samuel 28:11–14"> Or anyone using secret force on people, or putting questions to a spirit, or having secret knowledge, or going to the dead for directions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 18:24; Deuteronomy 9:4; Leviticus 18:27"> For all who do such things are disgusting to the Lord; and because of these disgusting things the Lord your God is driving them out before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 5:48; Genesis 17:1; Genesis 6:9"> You are to be upright in heart before the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 14:16; Deuteronomy 18:10; Genesis 20:6"> For these nations, whose land you are taking, give attention to readers of signs and to those using secret arts: but the Lord your God will not let you do so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 3:22–23; Acts 7:37; John 1:45"> The Lord your God will give you a prophet from among your people, like me; you will give ear to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 20:19; Deuteronomy 5:23–28; Deuteronomy 9:10"> In answer to the request you made to the Lord your God in Horeb on the day of the great meeting, when you said, Let not the voice of the Lord my God come to my ears again, and let me not see this great fire any more, or death will overtake me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:28"> Then the Lord said to me, What they have said is well said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15; John 8:28; John 12:49–50"> I will give them a prophet from among themselves, like you, and I will put my words in his mouth, and he will say to them whatever I give him orders to say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 3:22–23; Hebrews 2:3; Hebrews 10:26"> And whoever does not give ear to my words which he will say in my name, will be responsible to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:1–5; Jeremiah 27:15; Jeremiah 23:13–15"> But the prophet who takes it on himself to say words in my name which I have not given him orders to say, or who says anything in the name of other gods, will come to his death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 4:1–3; 1 Thessalonians 5:24; Revelation 2:2"> And if you say in your hearts, How are we to be certain that the word does not come from the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:20; 2 Kings 20:1; Jeremiah 28:1–17"> When a prophet makes a statement in the name of the Lord, if what he says does not take place and his words do not come true, then his word is not the word of the Lord: the words of the prophet were said in the pride of his heart, and you are to have no fear of him.</span>
