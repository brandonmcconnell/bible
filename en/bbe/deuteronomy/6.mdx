<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:1; Deuteronomy 12:1; Deuteronomy 4:45"> Now these are the orders and the laws and the decisions which the Lord your God gave me for your teaching, so that you might do them in the land of your heritage to which you are going:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Deuteronomy 13:4; Exodus 20:20"> So that living in the fear of the Lord your God, you may keep all his laws and his orders, which I give you: you and your son and your son's son, all the days of your life; and so that your life may be long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Exodus 3:8; Genesis 26:4"> So give ear, O Israel, and take care to do this; so that it may be well for you, and you may be greatly increased, as the Lord the God of your fathers has given you his word, in a land flowing with milk and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 12:29–32; Isaiah 44:6; 1 Timothy 2:5"> Give ear, O Israel: the Lord our God is one Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 12:30; Matthew 22:37; Luke 10:27"> And the Lord your God is to be loved with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; Proverbs 7:3; Jeremiah 31:33"> Keep these words, which I say to you this day, deep in your hearts;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:19; Ephesians 6:4; Psalms 78:4–6"> Teaching them to your children with all care, talking of them when you are at rest in your house or walking by the way, when you go to sleep and when you get up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 6:21; Proverbs 3:3; Deuteronomy 11:18"> Let them be fixed as a sign on your hand, and marked on your brow;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:20; Isaiah 30:8; Isaiah 57:8"> Have them lettered on the pillars of your houses and over the doors of your towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 24:13; Psalms 105:44; Nehemiah 9:25"> And when the Lord your God has taken you into the land which he gave his oath to your fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, that he would give you; with great and fair towns which were not of your building;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:12–18; Deuteronomy 8:10–20; Ezekiel 16:10–20"> And houses full of good things not stored up by you, and places for storing water which you did not make, and vine-gardens and olive-trees not of your planting; and you have taken food and are full;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Then take care that you keep your hearts true to the Lord, who took you out of the land of Egypt, out of the prison-house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 4:8; Deuteronomy 10:20; Matthew 4:10"> Let the fear of the Lord your God be in your hearts, and be his servants, taking your oaths by his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:6; 1 John 5:21; Deuteronomy 13:7"> Do not go after other gods, the gods of the peoples round about you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:24; Exodus 20:5; Deuteronomy 7:4"> For the Lord your God who is with you is a God who will not let his honour be given to another; or the wrath of the Lord will be burning against you, causing your destruction from the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 17:7; Luke 4:12; Matthew 4:7"> Do not put the Lord your God to the test as you did in Massah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:22; Psalms 119:4; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> Keep with care the orders of the Lord your God, and his rules and his laws which he has given you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Deuteronomy 12:25; Romans 12:2"> And do what is upright and good in the eyes of the Lord your God, so that it may be well for you and you may go in and take for your heritage that good land from which the Lord undertook by an oath to your fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 33:52–53; Exodus 23:28–30; Judges 3:1–4"> To send out from before you all those who are against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 13:14; Exodus 12:26; Joshua 4:6–7"> And when your son says to you in time to come, What is the reason for these rules and laws and decisions which the Lord our God has given you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 6:17–18; Ephesians 2:11–12; Deuteronomy 15:15"> Then you will say to your son, We were servants under Pharaoh's yoke in Egypt; and the Lord took us out of Egypt with a strong hand:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:34; Psalms 58:10–11; Deuteronomy 7:19"> And the Lord did great signs and wonders against Egypt, and against Pharaoh and all his house, before our eyes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:18; Deuteronomy 6:10; Exodus 13:5"> And he took us out from that place, guiding us here to give us this land, as he said in his oath to our fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:39; Psalms 41:2; Deuteronomy 10:12–13"> And the Lord gave us orders to keep all these laws, in the fear of the Lord our God, so that it might be well for us for ever, and that he might keep us from death, as he has done to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:13; Romans 10:3; Leviticus 18:5"> And it will be our righteousness if we take care to keep all this order before the Lord our God as he has given it to us.</span>
