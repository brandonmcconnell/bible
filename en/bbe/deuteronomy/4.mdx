<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 10:5; Ezekiel 20:11; Leviticus 18:5"> And now give ear, O Israel, to the laws and the decisions which I am teaching you, and do them; so that life may be yours, and you may go in and take for yourselves the land which the Lord, the God of your fathers, is giving you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; Proverbs 30:6; Revelation 22:18–19"> Make no addition to the orders which I give you, and take nothing from them, but keep the orders of the Lord your God which I give you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 25:1–9; Psalms 106:28–29; Joshua 22:17"> Your eyes have seen what the Lord did because of Baal-peor: for destruction came from the Lord on all those among you who went after Baal-peor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:20; Joshua 23:8; John 6:67–69"> But you who kept faith with the Lord are living, every one of you, today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 28:20; 1 Thessalonians 4:1–2; Hebrews 3:5"> I have been teaching you laws and decisions, as I was ordered to do by the Lord my God, so that you might keep them in the land to which you are going to take it for your heritage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 28:28; Psalms 111:10; 2 Timothy 3:15"> So keep these laws and do them; for so will your wisdom and good sense be clear in the eyes of the peoples, who hearing all these laws will say, Truly, this great nation is a wise and far-seeing people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 145:18; 2 Samuel 7:23; Psalms 34:18"> For what great nation has a god so near to them as the Lord our God is, whenever we are turned to him in prayer?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 147:19–20; Romans 7:12–14; Psalms 119:127–128"> And what great nation has laws and decisions so right as all this law which I put before you today?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Deuteronomy 11:19; Psalms 78:3–8"> Only take care, and keep watch on your soul, for fear that the things which your eyes have seen go from your memory and from your heart all the days of your life; but let the knowledge of them be given to your children and to your children's children;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 19:9; Hebrews 12:18–19; Exodus 19:16"> That day when you were waiting before the Lord your God in Horeb, and the Lord said to me, Make all the people come together, so that hearing my words they may go in fear of me all the days of their life on earth and give this teaching to their children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:23; Exodus 19:16–18; Hebrews 12:18–19"> And you came near, waiting at the foot of the mountain; and flames of fire went up from the mountain to the heart of heaven, with dark clouds, and all was black as night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:15; Deuteronomy 5:4; Deuteronomy 5:22"> And the voice of the Lord came to you out of the fire: the sound of his words came to your ears but you saw no form; there was nothing but a voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 34:28; Exodus 31:18; Deuteronomy 9:9–11"> And he gave you his agreement with you, the ten rules which you were to keep, which he put in writing on the two stones of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 105:44–45; Ezekiel 21:1–23"> And the Lord gave me orders at that time to make clear to you these laws and decisions, so that you might do them in the land to which you are going, and which is to be your heritage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:12; Isaiah 40:18; 2 Corinthians 4:4–6"> So keep watch on yourselves with care; for you saw no form of any sort on the day when the voice of the Lord came to you in Horeb out of the heart of the fire:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 17:29; Deuteronomy 5:8; Exodus 20:4–5"> So that you may not be turned to evil ways and make for yourselves an image in the form of any living thing, male or female,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 1:23"> Or any beast of the earth, or winged bird of the air,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Or of anything which goes flat on the earth, or any fish in the water under the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 1:25; Deuteronomy 17:3; 2 Kings 21:3"> And when your eyes are lifted up to heaven, and you see the sun and the moon and the stars, all the army of heaven, do not let yourselves be moved to give them worship, or become the servants of what the Lord has given equally to all peoples under heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 8:51; Jeremiah 11:4; Exodus 19:5–6"> But the Lord has taken you out of the flaming fire, out of Egypt, to be to him the people of his heritage, as you are today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:37; Numbers 20:12; Psalms 106:32–33"> And the Lord was angry with me because of you, and made an oath that I was not to go over Jordan into the good land which the Lord is giving you for your heritage:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:25; Deuteronomy 3:27; 2 Peter 1:13–15"> But death is to come to me in this land, I may not go over Jordan: but you will go over and take that good land for your heritage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:9; Hebrews 3:12; Joshua 23:16"> Take care that you do not let the agreement of the Lord your God, which he has made with you, go out of your mind, or make for yourselves images of any sort, against the orders which the Lord your God has given you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Nahum 1:6; Deuteronomy 9:3"> For the Lord your God is an all-burning fire, and he will not let the honour which is his be given to any other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:16; Hosea 9:9; 1 Corinthians 10:22"> If, when you have had children and children's children, and have been living a long time in the land, you are turned to evil ways, and make an image of any sort, and do evil in the eyes of the Lord your God, moving him to wrath:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Micah 6:2; Jeremiah 22:29"> May heaven and earth be my witnesses against you today, that destruction will quickly overtake you, cutting you off from that land which you are going over Jordan to take; your days will not be long in that land, but you will come to a complete end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:62–64; Leviticus 26:33; Ezekiel 12:15"> And the Lord will send you wandering among the peoples; only a small band of you will be kept from death among the nations where the Lord will send you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:64; Jeremiah 16:13; Deuteronomy 28:36"> There you will be the servants of gods, made by men's hands, of wood and stone, having no power of seeing or hearing or taking food or smelling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 119:2; Jeremiah 29:12–14; 2 Chronicles 15:4"> But if in those lands you are turned again to the Lord your God, searching for him with all your heart and soul, he will not keep himself from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joel 2:12–13; Jeremiah 23:20; Hosea 3:5"> When you are in trouble and all these things have come on you, if, in the future, you are turned again to the Lord your God, and give ear to his voice:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:8; Psalms 116:5; Nehemiah 9:31"> Because the Lord your God is a God of mercy, he will not take away his help from you or let destruction overtake you, or be false to the agreement which he made by an oath with your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 8:8; Matthew 24:31; Psalms 44:1"> Give thought now to the days which are past, before your time, from the day when God first gave life to man on the earth, and searching from one end of heaven to the other, see if such a great thing as this has ever been, or if anything like it has been talked of in story.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Judges 6:22; Deuteronomy 5:24; Exodus 33:20"> Has any people ever gone on living after hearing the voice of God out of the heart of the fire as you did?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:8; Deuteronomy 5:15; Exodus 6:6"> Has God ever before taken a nation for himself from out of another nation, by punishments and signs and wonders, by war and by a strong hand and a stretched-out arm and great acts of wonder and fear, as the Lord your God did for you in Egypt, before your very eyes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 45:18; 1 Samuel 2:2; Isaiah 45:5"> All this he let you see, so that you might be certain that the Lord is God and there is no other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 19:9; Exodus 19:19; Deuteronomy 4:33"> Out of heaven itself his voice came to you, teaching you; and on earth he let you see his great fire; and his words came to your ears out of the heart of the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:15; Isaiah 63:9; Exodus 13:9"> And because of his love for your fathers, he took their seed and made it his, and he himself, present among you, took you out of Egypt by his great power;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:1; Deuteronomy 9:1–5; Exodus 23:27–28"> Driving out before you nations greater and stronger than you, to take you into their land and give it to you for your heritage, as at this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Joshua 2:11; Deuteronomy 4:35; 1 Chronicles 29:11"> So today be certain, and keep the knowledge deep in your hearts, that the Lord is God, in heaven on high and here on earth; there is no other God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:3; Ephesians 6:3; Deuteronomy 12:28"> Then keep his laws and his orders which I give you today, so that it may be well for you and for your children after you, and that your lives may be long in the land which the Lord your God is giving you for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 35:6; Numbers 35:14–15; Joshua 20:2–9"> Then Moses had three towns marked out on the far side of Jordan looking to the east;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Hebrews 6:18; Deuteronomy 19:1–10; Numbers 35:15–28"> To which anyone causing the death of his neighbour in error and not through hate, might go in flight; so that in one of these towns he might be kept from death:</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Joshua 20:8; 1 Kings 4:13; Joshua 21:27"> The names of the towns were Bezer in the waste land, in the table-land, for the Reubenites; and Ramoth in Gilead for the Gadites; and Golan in Bashan for Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:3; Leviticus 27:34; Malachi 4:4"> This is the law which Moses put before the children of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Kings 2:3; Deuteronomy 4:1; Psalms 119:7"> These are the rules and the laws and the decisions which Moses gave to the children of Israel after they came out of Egypt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:29; Deuteronomy 4:47; Deuteronomy 1:4–5"> On the far side of Jordan, in the valley facing Beth-peor, in the land of Sihon, king of the Amorites, who was ruling in Heshbon, whom Moses and the children of Israel overcame after they had come out of Egypt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:1–14; Numbers 21:33–35; Deuteronomy 29:7–8"> And they took his land for a heritage, and the land of Og, king of Bashan, the two kings of the Amorites, whose lands were on the other side of Jordan to the east;</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:9; Deuteronomy 2:36; Deuteronomy 3:12"> From Aroer on the edge of the valley of the Arnon as far as Mount Sion, which is Hermon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:17; Joshua 13:20; Deuteronomy 34:1"> And all the Arabah on the far side of Jordan to the east, as far as the sea of the Arabah under the slopes of Pisgah.</span>
