<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:10; Exodus 23:10–11; Leviticus 25:2–4"> At the end of every seven years there is to be a general forgiveness of debt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 7:42; James 2:13; Luke 6:34–38"> This is how it is to be done: every creditor is to give up his right to whatever he has let his neighbour have; he is not to make his neighbour, his countryman, give it back; because a general forgiveness has been ordered by the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:20; Matthew 17:25–26; 1 Corinthians 6:6–7"> A man of another nation may be forced to make payment of his debt, but if your brother has anything of yours, let it go;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 58:10–11; Proverbs 28:27; Proverbs 11:24–25"> But there will be no poor among you; for the Lord will certainly give you his blessing in the land which the Lord your God is giving you for your heritage;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:1–15; Isaiah 1:19–20; Joshua 1:7"> If only you give ear to the voice of the Lord your God, and take care to keep all these orders which I give you today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:12–13; Luke 6:35; Nehemiah 9:27"> For the Lord your God will give you his blessing as he has said: you will let other nations have the use of your money, but you will not make use of theirs; you will be rulers over a number of nations, but they will not be your rulers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 3:16–17; Deuteronomy 15:9; Proverbs 21:13"> If in any of your towns in the land which the Lord your God is giving you, there is a poor man, one of your countrymen, do not let your heart be hard or your hand shut to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 6:34; Matthew 5:42"> But let your hand be open to give him the use of whatever he is in need of.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:15; Matthew 20:15; Jeremiah 17:10"> And see that there is no evil thought in your heart, moving you to say to yourself, The seventh year, the year of forgiveness is near; and so looking coldly on your poor countryman you give him nothing; and he will make an outcry to the Lord against you, and it will be judged as sin in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 22:9; Hebrews 13:16; Isaiah 58:10"> But it is right for you to give to him, without grief of heart: for because of this, the blessing of the Lord your God will be on all your work and on everything to which you put your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 14:7; Matthew 26:11; John 12:8"> For there will never be a time when there are no poor in the land; and so I give orders to you, Let your hand be open to your countrymen, to those who are poor and in need in your land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:14; Leviticus 25:39–41; Exodus 21:2–6"> If one of your countrymen, a Hebrew man or woman, becomes your servant for a price and does work for you six years, in the seventh year let him go free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Malachi 3:5; Jeremiah 22:13; Proverbs 3:27–28"> And when you make him free, do not let him go away with nothing in his hands:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:2; Proverbs 10:22; Psalms 68:10"> But give him freely from your flock and from your grain and your wine: in the measure of the wealth which the Lord your God has given you, you are to give to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:12; Matthew 18:32–33; Deuteronomy 5:14–15"> And keep in mind that you yourself were a servant in the land of Egypt, and the Lord your God made you free: so I give you this order today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 21:5–6; Psalms 40:8; Psalms 40:6"> But if he says to you, I have no desire to go away from you; because you and your family are dear to him and he is happy with you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:22; Leviticus 25:39–42"> Then take a sharp-pointed instrument, driving it through his ear into the door, and he will be your servant for ever. And you may do the same for your servant-girl.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 17:7–8; Isaiah 16:14; Isaiah 21:16"> Let it not seem hard to you that you have to send him away free; for he has been working for you for six years, which is twice the regular time for a servant: and the blessing of the Lord your God will be on you in everything you do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Exodus 13:12; Leviticus 27:26"> All the first males to come to birth in your herd and your flock are to be holy to the Lord your God: the first birth of your ox is not to be used for work, the wool of your first lamb is not to be cut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:17; Deuteronomy 14:23; Deuteronomy 12:5–7"> But year by year you and all your house are to take a meal of it before the Lord, in the place of his selection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:1; Malachi 1:7–8; Leviticus 22:19–25"> But if it has any mark on it, if it is blind or has damaged legs, or if there is anything wrong with it, it may not be offered to the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:21–22; Deuteronomy 12:15–16"> It may be used for food in your houses: the unclean and the clean may take of it, as of the gazelle and the roe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:16; Leviticus 7:26; Deuteronomy 12:23"> Only do not take its blood for food, but let it be drained out on the earth like water.</span>
