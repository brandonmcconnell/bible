<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:21; 2 Peter 2:1; 1 Timothy 4:1"> Beloved, don’t believe every spirit, but test the spirits to see whether they are from God; because many false prophets have gone out into the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:14; 1 Corinthians 12:3; 1 Timothy 3:16"> This is how you know God’s Spirit: every spirit that acknowledges that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh is from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 2:22; 2 John 1:7; 1 John 2:18"> Every spirit that fails to acknowledge Jesus isn’t from God. This is the spirit of the antichrist, which you have heard is coming, and now it is already in the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 8:31; 1 John 5:4; Romans 8:37"> You are from God, little children, and have overcome them, because He who is in you is greater than he who is in the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 8:23; 2 Peter 2:2–3; John 17:14"> They are from the world, therefore they talk like they are from the world, and the world listens to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 14:17; John 10:27; 1 Corinthians 2:12–14"> We are from God. Whoever knows God listens to us. Whoever is not from God does not listen to us. By this we know the spirit of truth and the spirit of error.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 4:20–1; 1 John 4:8; 1 Peter 1:22"> Beloved, let us sincerely love one another, for sincere love is from God, and everyone who sincerely loves is born of God and knows God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 4:16; Psalms 86:15; 2 Corinthians 13:11"> The one who doesn’t sincerely love doesn’t know God, for God is sincere love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 3:16; 1 John 5:11; Romans 8:32"> The sincere love of God was manifested in us by this, that God has sent His only born Son into the world so that we might live through Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4–5; Romans 5:8–10; 1 John 4:19"> In this is sincere love: not that we sincerely loved God, but that He sincerely loved us and sent his Son as an atoning sacrifice for our sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 15:12–13; Colossians 3:13; John 13:34"> Beloved, if God sincerely loved us like this, we ought to sincerely love one another, too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 1:18; 1 John 2:5; 1 John 4:17–18"> No one has ever seen God, but if we sincerely love one another, God lives in us and His sincere love is made complete in us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 2:20–22; 1 John 3:24; 1 Corinthians 3:16–17"> This is how we know that we live in Him and He in us, because He has given us of His Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 John 4:10; John 3:16–17; John 4:42"> We have seen and testify that the Father has sent the Son as Savior of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 10:9; 1 John 5:5; Matthew 10:32"> God lives in whoever confesses that Jesus is the Son of God, and that person lives in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 3:24; Psalms 36:7–9; 1 John 3:1"> We have come to know and have believed the sincere love which God has for us.<br />God is sincere love. One who lives in sincere love lives in God, and God lives in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 John 4:12; 1 John 2:28; Romans 8:29"> Sincere love has been perfected among us in this: that we may have confident boldness on the judgment day, because as He is, so are we in this world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7; Romans 8:15; 1 John 4:12"> There is no fear in sincere love, but perfect, sincere love casts out fear, because fear involves punishment. But whoever fears has not been made perfect in sincere love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 4:10; John 3:16; John 15:16"> We sincerely love, because He first sincerely loved us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 John 3:17; 1 John 2:9; 1 John 4:12"> If someone says, “I sincerely love God,” and hates his brother or sister, he is a liar. If someone doesn’t sincerely love a brother or sister he has seen, how can he sincerely love God whom he hasn’t seen?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 22:37–39; Leviticus 19:18; 1 John 3:11"> This commandment we have from Him: that whoever sincerely loves God must sincerely love his brothers and sisters, too.</span>
</div>
