<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:4; 1 John 5:20; 1 Timothy 3:16"> The life was revealed, and we have seen, and testify, and declare to you this eternal life which was with the Father and was revealed to us.</span>

Truly our fellowship is with the Father and with His Son, Jesus Christ.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Christ” (Greek) and “Messiah” (Hebrew) both mean “Anointed One.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 15:11; John 16:24; 2 John 1:12"> We write these things to you so that your joy may be full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 8:12; James 1:17; Isaiah 60:19"> This is the message which we have heard from Him and declare to you: that God is light and in Him is no darkness at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 John 4:20; 1 John 2:4; John 3:19–21"> If we say that we have fellowship with Him, but walk in darkness, we lie and don’t do what is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Romans 13:12; Revelation 1:5"> But if we walk in the light, as He is in the light, we have fellowship with one another, and the blood of Jesus Christ, His Son, cleanses us from all sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:23; Ecclesiastes 7:20; James 3:2"> If we say that we don’t sin, we are deceiving ourselves, and the truth isn’t in us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 32:5; Proverbs 28:13; Jeremiah 33:8"> If we admit to our sins, He is faithful and with righteous justice forgives us our sins and cleanses us from all unrighteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 1:8; 1 John 5:10; 1 John 2:4"> If we claim that we haven’t sinned, we call Him a liar, and His word isn’t in us.</span>
