<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 1:12–13; John 8:42; 1 John 2:29"> Whoever believes that Jesus is the Christ is born of God, and whoever loves the Father loves the child born of Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 13:34–35; 1 John 2:5; 1 John 3:22–24"> This is how we know that we sincerely love the children of God: by sincerely loving God and keeping His commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 14:15; John 15:10; John 14:21–24"> For this is God’s sincere love, that we keep His commandments. His commandments aren’t burdensome,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:57; 1 John 4:4; John 16:33"> for whatever is born of God overcomes the world. This is the victory that has overcome the world - our faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 John 4:15; 1 John 5:1"> Who is the one who overcomes the world, but one who believes that Jesus is God’s Son?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 15:26; Hebrews 9:14; John 16:13"> This is He who came by water and blood - Jesus Christ; not only by water, but by water and blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 28:19; 2 Corinthians 13:14; Acts 5:32"> It is the Spirit who testifies, because the Spirit is truth. For there are three that testify,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 5:6–7; John 15:26; 1 Peter 3:21"> the Spirit, the water, and the blood; and these three are in agreement as one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 3:16–17; Matthew 17:5; Acts 17:31"> If we receive the witness of men, the witness of God is greater; because this is God’s testimony which He has testified about His Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 8:16; Galatians 4:6; John 3:33"> One who believes in the Son of God has the witness in himself. One who doesn’t believe God has accused Him of being a liar, because he hasn’t believed in the testimony that God has given concerning His Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 6:23; 1 John 2:25; John 3:36"> This is the witness, that God has given us eternal life, and this life is in His Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 3:36; John 5:24; John 1:12"> Whoever has the Son has life. Whoever doesn’t have God’s Son doesn’t have life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 20:31; John 1:12; 2 Peter 1:10–11"> I have written these things to you who believe in the name of God’s Son, so that you may know that you have eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 14:13; Jeremiah 33:3; Jeremiah 29:12–13"> This is the confidence that we have in Him: that if we ask anything according to His will, He hears us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 11:9–10; Mark 11:24; Proverbs 15:29"> If we know that He hears us in whatever we ask, then we know that we have the requests which we have asked from Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 6:4–6; 1 Samuel 2:25; Jeremiah 11:14"> If you see your brother or sister committing a sin not leading to death, you should ask, and God will, for you, give life to those who commit sin not leading to death. There is a kind of sin leading to death. I don’t say that you should make requests for this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 John 3:4; 1 John 5:16; James 4:7–10"> All unrighteousness is sin, and there is a kind of sin that doesn’t lead to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 John 3:9; 1 John 5:4; 1 John 5:1"> We know that anyone who is born of God doesn’t keep sinning, but He who was born of God keeps him. The wicked one doesn’t harm or even touch him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4; 1 John 4:4–6; Ephesians 2:2"> We know that we belong to God, and the whole world lies in the power of the evil one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 17:3; Luke 24:45; John 14:9"> We know that God’s Son has come, and has given us understanding, so that we might know Him who is true. We are in Him who is true, in His Son Jesus Christ. This is the true God and eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:14; 1 Corinthians 10:7; Exodus 20:3–4"> Little children, keep yourselves from idols.</span>
</div>
</div>
