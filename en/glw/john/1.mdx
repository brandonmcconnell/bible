<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 1:1; John 17:5; Revelation 19:13"> In the beginning was the Word,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The|strong=&quot;G3588&quot; Greek word translated “word” (Logos) means more than just a collection of a few letters. It means an entire message and the thought behind it.">a</a></sup>and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> He was in the beginning with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 1:16–17; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Revelation 4:11"> Everything was made through Him, and without Him nothing was made that was made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 8:12; John 12:46; John 5:26"> In Him was life, and that life was the light of mankind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 3:19–20; John 12:36–40; 1 Corinthians 2:14"> The light shines in the darkness, but the darkness hasn’t overcome<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The|strong=&quot;G3588&quot; Greek word translated “overcome” (katelaben) also means overcome, comprehend, and overtake (to grasp and defeat an enemy or to grasp and understand an idea).">b</a></sup>it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; John 3:28; Isaiah 40:3–5"> There was a man sent from God, whose name was John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 19:4; John 1:36; John 5:33–35"> This man came as a witness, to testify about the light, that all through him might believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:20; John 3:28; Acts 19:4"> He wasn’t that light, but was sent as a witness of that light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 12:46; John 1:4; 1 John 2:8"> The true light that enlightens everyone was coming into the world.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 17:25; 1 John 3:1; 1 Corinthians 2:8"> The Word was in the world, and though the world was made through Him, the world didn’t recognize Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:14; Isaiah 53:2–3; John 3:32"> He came to His own, and those who were His own didn’t receive Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 3:26; Romans 8:14; 1 John 3:1"> But as many as received Him, to them He gave the right to become God’s children, to those who believe in His name:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 John 3:9; James 1:18; 1 Peter 1:23"> who were born, not of blood, nor of the will of the flesh, nor of the will of man, but of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 John 4:9; 1 Timothy 3:16; Isaiah 40:5"> The Word became flesh and lived among us, and we saw His glory, the glory as of the only Son of the Father, full of grace and truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 3:11; Colossians 1:17; Luke 3:16"> John testified about Him. He cried out, saying, “This was He who I told you about when I said, ‘He who comes after me has surpassed me because He was before me.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 1:19; Romans 5:2; Ephesians 2:5–10"> From His fullness we have all received grace upon grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 6:14; John 14:6; Romans 3:19–26"> The Law was given through Moses, but grace and truth came through Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 1:15; John 6:46; Matthew 11:27"> No one has ever seen God, but God’s only born Son, who is near to the heart of His Father, has made Him known.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 3:15–18; John 10:24; Acts 19:4"> Now this is the testimony of John, when the Jews sent priests and Levites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Levites|strong=&quot;G3019&quot; are descendants of Levi, who were commissioned to help the priests in their duties. (Numbers 18:23; Deuteronomy 18:1).">c</a></sup>from Jerusalem to ask him, “Who are you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 1:7–8; Matthew 3:11–12; John 3:28–36"> He confessed, and didn’t deny, but confessed, “I’m not the Christ.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Deuteronomy 18:15–18; Matthew 11:14"> They asked him, “Then who are you? Are you Elijah?”<br />He said, “I am not.”<br />“Are you the Prophet?”<br />He answered, “No.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:13"> Then they asked him, “Who are you? Give us an answer to take back to those who sent us. What do you say about yourself?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–5; Mark 1:3; Matthew 3:3"> John replied in the words of Isaiah the prophet, “I am the voice of one calling in the desert, ‘Make straight the way for the Lord.’”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Isaiah|strong=&quot;G2268&quot; 40:3">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 11:53; Matthew 23:13–15; Luke 11:39–44"> Now those who were sent were Pharisees.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Pharisees|strong=&quot;G5330&quot; were one of two main religious parties at this time, the other being the Sadducees. The Pharisees emphasized strict compliance with the Jewish Law and the traditions of the elders. Acts 23:8.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:18; Deuteronomy 18:15; John 1:20–22"> They asked him, saying, “Why then do you baptize if you aren’t the Christ, nor Elijah, nor the Prophet?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 1:10–11; Luke 3:16; Acts 1:5"> John answered them, saying, “I baptize with water, but One stands among you that you don’t know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 1:30; Mark 1:7; Matthew 3:11"> It is He who, coming after me, is preferred before me, whose sandal strap I’m not worthy to untie.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 10:40; John 3:23; Judges 7:24"> This all happened at Bethany on the other side of the Jordan, where John was baptizing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; 1 John 2:2; 1 John 3:5"> The next day, John saw Jesus coming toward him, and said, “Look! God’s Lamb who takes away the sin of the world!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 1:27; John 1:15; Luke 3:16"> This is the One I meant when I said, ‘A man who comes after me is preferred before me because He existed before me.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Luke 1:17; Malachi 4:2–5"> I didn’t know Him, but the reason I came baptizing with water was that He might be revealed to Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 1:10; Luke 3:22; Matthew 3:16"> John testified, saying, “I saw the Spirit descending from Heaven like a dove, and He remained upon Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 1:5; Matthew 3:11; Luke 3:16"> I wouldn’t have known Him, but He who sent me to baptize with water told me, ‘Whoever you see the Spirit descending upon, and remaining on Him, this is He who baptizes with the Holy Spirit.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 2:7; John 1:49; Psalms 89:26–27"> I have seen and I testify that this is God’s Son.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 3:25–26; Malachi 3:16"> The next day, John again stood with two of His disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 1:29; 1 Peter 1:19–20; Isaiah 65:1–2"> When he saw Jesus passing by, he said, “Look, God’s Lamb!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Romans 10:17; John 1:43; Zechariah 8:21"> The two disciples heard him say this, and they followed Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 18:7; John 3:2; John 1:49"> Jesus turned, and seeing them following, asked them,<span className="woj">“What are you looking for?”</span>They said to Him, “Rabbi” (which, when translated, means Teacher), “Where are You staying?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 24:29; Matthew 11:28–30; John 6:37"> He told them,<span className="woj">“Come and see.”</span>They came and saw where He was staying, and spent that day with Him. It was about the tenth hour.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The|strong=&quot;G3588&quot; tenth hour since sunrise, or about 4:00 p.m.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 1:16–20; Matthew 4:18–22; John 1:40–42"> Andrew, Simon Peter’s brother, was one of the two who heard what John had said and who had followed Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 4:25; John 1:45; Psalms 89:20"> The first thing Andrew did was to find his brother, Simon, and tell him, “We have found the Messiah” (that is, translated, the Christ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="“Messiah” (Hebrew) and “Christ” (Greek) both mean “Anointed One.”">g</a></sup>).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 21:15–17; 1 Corinthians 3:22; 1 Corinthians 15:5"> Andrew brought Simon to Jesus. Jesus looked at him, and said,<span className="woj">“You are Simon the son of Jonah. You will be called Cephas”</span>(which is translated Peter<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="“Cephas” (Aramaic) and “Peter” (Greek) both mean “Rock.”">h</a></sup>).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 9:9; John 14:8; Isaiah 65:1"> The next day Jesus decided to leave for Galilee. Finding Philip, he told him,<span className="woj">“Follow me.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 12:21; Matthew 11:21; Mark 8:22"> Philip was from Bethsaida, the city of Andrew and Peter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:18–22; Luke 24:27; Micah 5:2"> Philip found Nathanael and told him, “We have found Him who Moses in the Law, and also the prophets, wrote about — Jesus of Nazareth, the son of Joseph.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 7:52; John 7:41–42; John 4:29"> Nathanael asked Philip, “Can anything good come out of Nazareth?”<br />Philip told Nathanael, “Come and see.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Revelation 14:5; Psalms 32:2; Psalms 73:1"> Jesus saw Nathanael coming toward Him, and said of Nathanael,<span className="woj">“Look! An Israelite indeed, who has no deceit in him!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Isaiah 65:24; John 2:25; Psalms 139:1–2"> Nathanael asked Him, “How do You know me?”<br />Jesus answered,<span className="woj">“Before Philip called you, when you were under the fig tree, I saw you.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:15; Zechariah 9:9; Matthew 27:42"> Nathanael answered, “Rabbi,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Rabbi|strong=&quot;G4461&quot; is Hebrew for “teacher.”">i</a></sup>You are God’s Son. You are the King of Israel.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 11:40; John 20:29; Luke 1:45"> Jesus said to him,<span className="woj">“Because I told you that I saw you under the fig tree, do you believe? You will see greater things than these.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Genesis 28:12; Acts 7:56; Luke 22:43"> And He told him,<span className="woj">“Most assuredly, I tell you, you will see Heaven opened, and God’s angels ascending and descending on the Son of Man.”</span></span>
</div>
