<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 12:23; 1 John 4:19; John 13:34"> Before the Feast of the Passover, Jesus knew that His time had come that He should depart out of this world to the Father. Having sincerely loved His own who were in the world, He sincerely loved them to the end.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 1:13–17; Acts 5:3; John 13:26–27"> Supper was finished, and the Devil had already put it into the heart of Judas Iscariot, Simon’s son, to betray Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 3:35; Matthew 11:27; Hebrews 1:2"> Jesus, knowing that the Father had given everything into His hands, and that He had come from God and was going to God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 22:27; 2 Corinthians 8:9; Luke 12:37"> got up from supper, laid aside His outer garment, and wrapped a towel around His waist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 7:44; Psalms 51:2; Genesis 18:4"> Then He poured water into a basin and began to wash the disciples’ feet, and to wipe them with the towel that was wrapped around Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 3:11–14; John 1:27; Luke 5:8"> Then He came to Simon Peter, and Peter asked Him, “Lord, are You washing my feet?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 12:8; John 12:16; John 14:26"> Jesus answered,<span className="woj">“You don’t understand what I am doing now, but you will know after this.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:25; Acts 22:16; Titus 3:5"> Peter told Him, “You will never wash my feet!”<br />Jesus answered,<span className="woj">“If I don’t wash you, you have no part with me.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 51:7; Psalms 51:2; 1 Peter 3:21"> Simon Peter said to Him, “Lord, not just my feet, but also my hands and my head!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 15:3; 2 Corinthians 7:1; 2 Corinthians 5:17"> Jesus said to him,<span className="woj">“Someone who has bathed only needs to wash their feet, but is completely clean; and most of you are clean, but not all of you.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 13:26; John 13:2; John 13:21"> For He knew who would betray Him, therefore He said,<span className="woj">“You aren’t all clean.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 13:7; John 13:4; Ezekiel 24:24"> When He had washed their feet, He put on His outer garment and sat down again. He asked them,<span className="woj">“Do you know what I have done to you?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 6:46; 1 Corinthians 12:3; John 11:28"> <span className="woj">You call me Teacher and Lord, and you are right in saying so, for so I am.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; Acts 20:35; Romans 12:16"> <span className="woj">If I, your Lord and Teacher, have washed your feet, you also ought to wash one another’s feet.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 2:6; Matthew 11:29; 1 Peter 3:17–18"> <span className="woj">I have given you an example, that you should also do as I have done to you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 6:40; John 15:20; Matthew 10:24–25"> <span className="woj">Most assuredly, I tell you, a servant isn’t greater than his master, nor is he who is sent greater than he who sent him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 11:28; James 4:17; James 1:25"> <span className="woj">If you know these things, you are blessed if you do them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; Matthew 26:23; John 17:12"> <span className="woj">I’m not talking about all of you. I know who I have chosen; but this is to fulfill the Scripture, ‘He who eats bread with me has lifted up his heel against me.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Psalm 41:9">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 43:10; John 14:29; John 16:4"> <span className="woj">Now I tell you before it happens, that when it does happen, you may believe that I AM.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Exodus 3:14">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:16; Luke 9:48; Matthew 25:40"> <span className="woj">Most assuredly, I tell you, whoever receives anyone I send receives me; and he who receives me receives Him who sent me.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 13:18; Matthew 26:21; Mark 14:18"> When Jesus had said these things, He was troubled in spirit, and testified and said,<span className="woj">“Most assuredly, I tell you, one of you will betray me.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 22:23; Matthew 26:22; Mark 14:19"> The disciples began looking at one another, perplexed about which one of them He was talking about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 19:26; John 21:7; John 21:20"> One of Jesus’ disciples, who Jesus sincerely loved, was leaning against His breast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 12:17; Luke 1:22; Acts 13:16"> Simon Peter therefore nodded to him to ask who He spoke about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 21:20; Genesis 44:4–12; Esther 7:5"> Then, leaning back on Jesus’ breast, he asked Him, “Lord, who is it?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 26:23; John 13:30; Mark 14:19–20"> Jesus answered,<span className="woj">“It is he who I will give a morsel to when I have dipped it.”</span>Having dipped the morsel, He gave it to Judas Iscariot, Simon’s son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 22:3; John 13:2; Ecclesiastes 9:3"> After the morsel, Satan<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Satan” means “Adversary” or “Accuser.”">c</a></sup>entered into him. Jesus therefore told him,<span className="woj">“What you do, do quickly.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> None of those reclining at the table knew why He had said this to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 12:5–6; John 13:1; Ephesians 4:28"> Some were supposing, because Judas had the money box, that Jesus was saying to him, “Buy the things we need for the feast”; or else, that he should give something to the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Job 24:13–15; Proverbs 4:16"> Having received the morsel, he went out immediately. It was night.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 14:13; 1 Peter 4:11; John 7:39"> When he had gone out, Jesus said,<span className="woj">“Now the Son of Man is glorified, and God is glorified in Him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; Isaiah 53:10–12; John 17:4–6"> <span className="woj">If God is glorified in Him, God will also glorify Him in Himself, and will glorify Him immediately.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 7:33–34; John 8:21–24; John 16:16–22"> <span className="woj">Little children, I am with you a little while longer. You will look for me, and as I told the Jews, I now tell you, also, ‘Where I am going, you can’t come.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="John|strong=&quot;G3004&quot; 7:34">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Leviticus 19:18; John 15:17"> <span className="woj">I give to you a new commandment, that you sincerely love one another, even as I have sincerely loved you, so you should also sincerely love one another.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 4:20–21; 1 John 3:10–14; 1 John 2:10"> <span className="woj">By this everyone will know that you are my disciples, if you have sincere love for one another.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Peter 1:14; John 13:33; John 21:21–22"> Simon Peter asked Him, “Lord, where are You going?”<br />Jesus answered,<span className="woj">“Where I go, you aren’t able to follow me now, but you will follow later.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 21:13; Acts 20:24; Mark 14:27–31"> Peter asked Him, “Lord, why can’t I follow You right now? I will lay down my life for You.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 22:34; Matthew 26:34; Mark 14:30"> Jesus answered,<span className="woj">“Will you lay down your life for my sake? Most assuredly, I tell you, the rooster won’t crow until you have denied me three times.</span></span>
</div>
