<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 23:14–17; Exodus 34:23"> After this, there was a feast of the Jews, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:39; Nehemiah 3:1; Nehemiah 3:32"> Near the Sheep Gate in Jerusalem, there is a pool, which is called Bethesda in Hebrew. It’s surrounded by five covered colonnades.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 3:1–4; Luke 7:22; Matthew 15:30"> In these lay a great multitude of sick people, blind, lame, or paralyzed, waiting for the moving of the water,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> for an angel went down from time to time into the pool and stirred up the water. Whoever stepped in first, after the stirring of the water, was made well of whatever disease that person had.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 3:2; Luke 8:43; Mark 9:21"> A certain man was there who had an infirmity thirty-eight years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 18:41; Hebrews 4:15; Hebrews 4:13"> When Jesus saw him lying there, and knew that he had already been sick for a long time, He asked him,<span className="woj">“Do you want to be made well?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 72:12; Deuteronomy 32:36; 1 Corinthians 9:24"> The sick man answered, “Sir, I have no one to put me into the pool when the water is stirred up, but while I’m coming, another steps down before me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 5:24; Matthew 9:6; Mark 2:11"> Jesus told him,<span className="woj">“Stand up, pick up your mat, and walk.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 3:7–8; John 9:14; Mark 3:2–4"> Immediately, the man was made well, took up his mat, and walked. That was on the Sabbath day.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The|strong=&quot;G3588&quot; Sabbath day is the seventh day of the week, which was set aside by God for rest. Genesis 2:2-3; Exodus 20:8-11.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:21; Luke 6:2; John 9:16"> Therefore the Jews were telling him who was cured, “It’s the Sabbath, and it isn’t legal for you to carry your mat.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 2:9–11; John 9:16"> He answered them, “He who made me well told me,<span className="woj">‘Take up your mat and walk.’”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Judges 6:29; Romans 10:2"> Then they asked him, “Who told you to<span className="woj">‘Take up your mat and walk’</span>?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 4:30; Luke 24:31; John 8:59"> The man who had been healed didn’t know who it was, because Jesus had disappeared into the crowd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 8:11; Leviticus 26:23–24; Psalms 118:18"> Later, Jesus found him in the temple and told him,<span className="woj">“See, you have been made well! Don’t sin any more, so that nothing worse happens to you.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 9:11–12; John 4:29; John 9:25"> The man went away, and told the Jews that it was Jesus who had made him well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 10:39; John 7:19–20; Mark 3:6"> For this reason, the Jews persecuted Jesus, and sought to kill Him, because He was doing these things on the Sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 9:4; John 14:10; Colossians 1:16"> Jesus answered them,<span className="woj">“My Father is still working, so I am working, too.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 10:30; John 10:33; John 19:7"> Because of this, the Jews tried all the harder to kill Him. Not only was He breaking the Sabbath, but He was even calling God His own Father, making Himself equal with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 12:49; John 8:28; John 14:10"> Then Jesus answered,<span className="woj">“Most assuredly, I tell you, the Son can do nothing of Himself, unless He sees the Father do it, for whatever He does, the Son does likewise.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 3:35; Matthew 3:17; John 17:26"> <span className="woj">The Father loves the Son, and shows Him everything that He Himself does. He will show Him even greater works than these, that you may marvel.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 8:11; Deuteronomy 32:39; John 11:25"> <span className="woj">As the Father raises the dead and gives life to them, even so the Son gives life to whoever He wishes.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 5:27; Acts 17:31; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> <span className="woj">The Father judges no one, but has committed all judgment to the Son,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 John 2:23; Luke 10:16; Matthew 11:27"> <span className="woj">that all may honor the Son just as they honor the Father. He who doesn’t honor the Son doesn’t honor the Father who sent Him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 3:36; John 6:40; 1 John 5:11–13"> “Most assuredly, I tell you, the person who hears my words and believes in Him who sent me has everlasting life, and won’t come into judgment, but has passed from death into life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 5:28; Ephesians 5:14; Ephesians 2:5"> <span className="woj">Most assuredly, I tell you, the time is coming, and now is, when the dead will hear the voice of God’s Son, and those who hear will live.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 11:26; John 1:4; Exodus 3:14"> <span className="woj">For just as the Father has life in Himself, so He has granted the Son to have life in Himself,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 10:42; John 5:22; Acts 17:31"> <span className="woj">and has given Him authority to execute judgment, because He is the Son of Man.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 20:12; 1 Thessalonians 4:14–17; Isaiah 26:19"> <span className="woj">Don’t marvel at this, for the time is coming in which all who are in the graves will hear His voice</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Daniel 12:2–3; Romans 2:6–10; Acts 24:15"> <span className="woj">and come out — those who have done good, to the resurrection of life, and those who have done evil, to the resurrection of condemnation.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 6:38; John 5:19; John 4:34"> <span className="woj">I can do nothing by myself. As I hear, I judge, and my judgment is righteous, because I don’t seek my own will, but the will of the Father who sent me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 27:2; John 8:54; Revelation 3:14"> <span className="woj">“If I testify about myself, my testimony isn’t valid.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 1:33; John 12:28–30; John 5:36–37"> <span className="woj">There is another who testifies on my behalf, and I know that his testimony about me is valid.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 1:29–34; John 3:26–36; John 1:15–27"> <span className="woj">You have sent to John, and he has testified to the truth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 19:41–42; John 20:31; 1 John 5:9"> <span className="woj">Not that I accept human testimony, but I tell you this so that you may be saved.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Peter 1:19; John 1:7–8; Matthew 11:11"> <span className="woj">John was the burning and shining lamp, and you were willing for a time to rejoice in his light.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 10:25; John 10:37–38; John 15:24"> <span className="woj">I have a greater testimony than John’s. The works that the Father has given me to finish — the very works that I do — testify about me, that the Father has sent me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 8:18; John 1:18; Matthew 3:17"> <span className="woj">The Father Himself, who sent me, has testified about me. You have neither heard His voice at any time, nor seen His form.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 John 2:14; Deuteronomy 6:6–9; John 12:44–48"> <span className="woj">You don’t have His words dwelling in you, because you don’t believe the One who He sent.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 24:27; Luke 24:44; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> <span className="woj">You search the Scriptures, for in them you think you have eternal life, and these testify about me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 3:19; John 1:11; Psalms 81:11"> <span className="woj">Yet you refuse to come to me to have life.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 7:18; John 5:34; 1 Thessalonians 2:6"> <span className="woj">“I don’t accept glory from men,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 John 4:20; 1 John 3:17; John 2:25"> <span className="woj">but I know you. I know that you don’t have God’s sincere love in you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 10:25; Matthew 24:5; John 17:4–6"> <span className="woj">I have come in my Father’s name, and you don’t accept me, but if someone else comes in his own name, you will accept him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 12:43; Romans 2:29; Philippians 2:3"> <span className="woj">How can you believe when you accept glory from one another, and don’t seek the glory that comes from the only God?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Romans 3:19–20; Galatians 3:10; 2 Corinthians 3:7–11"> <span className="woj">“Don’t think that I will accuse you before the Father. Your accuser is Moses, who you have set your hope on.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15; Deuteronomy 18:18–19; John 1:45"> <span className="woj">If you believed Moses, you would believe me, for he wrote about me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Luke 16:31; Luke 16:29"> <span className="woj">If you don’t believe what he wrote, how will you believe what I say?”</span></span>
