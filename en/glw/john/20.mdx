<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 16:22; John 16:20; John 20:27"> When He had said this, He showed them His hands and His side. Then the disciples rejoiced when they saw the Lord.</span>

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 6:30; Mark 16:11; Hebrews 3:12"> Therefore the other disciples were telling him, “We have seen the Lord!”<br />But he told them, “Unless I see the imprint of the nails in His hands, put my finger into the nail prints, and put my hand into His side, I won’t believe.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 20:19; Isaiah 54:10; John 20:21"> After eight days, His disciples were again inside, and Thomas with them. Jesus came, the doors having been locked, and stood in their midst, and said,<span className="woj">“Peace be with you.”</span></span>
