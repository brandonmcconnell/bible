<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 10:38–42; John 12:17; 2 Kings 20:1–12"> Now a certain man was sick, Lazarus from Bethany, the village of Mary and her sister Martha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 12:3; Mark 14:3; Matthew 26:6–7"> It was the Mary who anointed the Lord with ointment, and wiped His feet with her hair, whose brother Lazarus was sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:5; Revelation 3:19; 2 Timothy 4:20"> These sisters therefore sent to Him, saying, “Lord, one You love is sick!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 11:40; John 9:3; 1 Peter 4:14"> When Jesus heard this, He said,<span className="woj">“This sickness won’t end in death, but for God’s glory, so that God’s Son may be glorified by it.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:9–13; John 16:27; John 17:26"> Now Jesus sincerely loved Martha, and her sister, and Lazarus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 30:18; Isaiah 55:8–9; Genesis 45:1–5"> Yet, when He heard that Lazarus was sick, Jesus stayed where He was for two more days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 10:40–42; Luke 9:51; Acts 20:22–24"> After this, He said to the disciples,<span className="woj">“Let’s go back to Judea.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:31; John 8:59; Acts 21:12–13"> The disciples said to Him, “Rabbi, the Jews were just recently trying to stone You! Are You really going back there?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 9:4; John 12:35; Jeremiah 31:9"> Jesus answered,<span className="woj">“Aren’t there twelve hours of daylight? Anyone who walks by day doesn’t stumble, because he sees the light of this world.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 2:10–11; Proverbs 4:18–19; Jeremiah 13:16"> <span className="woj">But anyone who walks by night stumbles, because the light isn’t in him.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; John 11:13; John 11:43–44"> After He had said this, He told them,<span className="woj">“Our friend Lazarus has fallen asleep, but I am going there to wake him up.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The disciples therefore said to Him, “Lord, if he is sleeping, he will recover.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 9:24"> Now Jesus had spoken of his death, but they thought that He was talking about literal sleep.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 16:25; John 16:29; John 10:24"> So Jesus told them plainly,<span className="woj">“Lazarus is dead,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:10; John 14:10–11; Isaiah 54:15"> <span className="woj">and I am glad for your sakes that I wasn’t there, so that you may believe — but let’s go to him.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 13:37; Matthew 10:3; John 21:2"> Therefore Thomas, who is called the Twin,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Greek: Didymus">a</a></sup>said to his fellow disciples, “Let’s go, too, that we may die with Him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 11:39; Hosea 6:2; Acts 2:27–31"> When Jesus arrived, he found that Lazarus had already been in the tomb four days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 14:20; Revelation 21:16; John 6:19"> Bethany was near Jerusalem, about fifteen stadia<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="15 stadia is about 1.7 miles or about 2.8 kilometers.">b</a></sup>away,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 2:11; John 11:31; 1 Thessalonians 5:11"> and many of the Jews had come to Martha and Mary, to comfort them concerning their brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:38–42; 1 Thessalonians 4:17; Matthew 25:1"> When Martha heard that Jesus was coming, she went out to meet him, but Mary stayed home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 11:32; 1 Kings 17:18; Matthew 9:18"> Then Martha said to Jesus, “Lord, if You had been here, my brother wouldn’t have died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 9:31; John 11:41–42; Matthew 28:18"> Even now I know that God will give you whatever You ask of Him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 11:43–44"> Jesus told her,<span className="woj">“Your brother will rise again.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 12:2–3; Acts 24:15; John 5:28–29"> Martha replied, “I know that he will rise again in the resurrection on the last day.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:14; John 3:36; 1 John 5:11–12"> Jesus told her,<span className="woj">“I am the resurrection and the life. Whoever believes in me will live even if they die,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 10:28; John 5:24; Romans 8:13"> <span className="woj">and everyone who lives and believes in me will never die. Do you believe this?”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 16:16; John 6:69; John 6:14"> “Yes, Lord,” she answered, “I have believed that You are the Christ, God’s Son, who was to come into the world.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 11:20; John 13:13; Mark 10:49"> When she had said this, she went back and called Mary, her sister, saying secretly, “The Teacher is here, and is asking for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 15:23; Psalms 27:8; Song of Solomon 3:1–4"> When she heard this, she arose quickly, and went to Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 11:20"> Jesus had not yet entered the village, but was still at the place where Martha met Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 11:19; Genesis 37:35; 2 Samuel 12:16–18"> The Jews who were with her in the house, consoling her, saw Mary get up quickly and go out. They followed her, saying, “She is going to the tomb to weep there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 11:21; Luke 17:16; Luke 5:8"> When Mary came to where Jesus was, she saw Him, and fell at His feet, saying to Him, “Lord, if You had been here, my brother wouldn’t have died.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 11:38; John 12:27; Mark 3:5"> When Jesus saw her weeping, and the Jews who came with her weeping, He groaned in the spirit and was troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 15:47; Matthew 28:6; John 1:39"> He asked,<span className="woj">“Where have you laid him?”</span><br />They replied, “Lord, come and see.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 19:41; Hebrews 4:15; John 11:33"> Jesus wept.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 11:3; 1 John 3:1; 1 John 4:9–10"> So the Jews were saying, “See how He loved him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 9:6–7; John 11:32; Luke 23:39"> But some of them said, “Couldn’t this man, who opened the eyes of the blind man, have kept this man from dying?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 11:33; Luke 24:2; Matthew 27:60"> Then Jesus, again groaning in Himself, came to the tomb. It was a cave, and a boulder was placed against the entrance.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 11:17; Mark 16:3; Psalms 49:7"> Jesus said,<span className="woj">“Take away the boulder.”</span><br />Martha, the dead man’s sister, said to Him, “Lord, by this time there is a stench, because he has been dead four days.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 11:4; John 11:23–26; Romans 4:17–25"> Jesus told her,<span className="woj">“Didn’t I tell you that if you would believe you would see God’s glory?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 11:25; John 17:1; Psalms 123:1"> Then they took the boulder away from the place where the dead man was lying. Jesus lifted up His eyes and said,<span className="woj">“Father, I thank You that You have heard me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 3:17; John 17:21; 1 John 4:14"> <span className="woj">I know that You always hear me, but because of the people who are standing here I said this, that they may believe that You sent me.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Kings 4:33–36; Acts 9:40; Acts 9:34"> Now when He had said these things, He called loudly,<span className="woj">“Lazarus, come out!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 5:25; John 20:7; John 11:25–26"> He who had died came out, bound hand and foot with wrappings, and his face was wrapped around with a cloth. Jesus told them,<span className="woj">“Unbind him, and let him go.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 2:23; John 11:19; John 10:41"> Then many of the Jews who had come to Mary, and had seen the things Jesus did, believed in Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 9:13; Acts 5:25; John 12:37"> But some of them went away to the Pharisees and told them what Jesus did.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; John 12:19; Acts 4:5–6"> Then the chief priests and the Pharisees gathered a council and said, “What are we doing? For this Man performs many signs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:15–16; Matthew 23:35–38; Matthew 27:25"> If we let Him go on like this, everyone will believe in Him, and the Romans will come and take away both our place and our nation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; John 11:51; John 18:13–14"> Then a certain one of them, Caiaphas, who was high priest that year, told them, “You don’t know anything,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 18:14; Luke 24:46; Romans 3:8"> nor do you take into account that it’s better for you that one man should die for the people, than for the whole nation to perish.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Daniel 9:26; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Exodus 28:30"> He didn’t say this on his own initiative; but being high priest that year, he prophesied that Jesus was going to die for the nation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 10:16; Isaiah 49:6; 1 John 2:2"> and not for the nation only, but that Jesus might also gather together into one God’s children who are scattered abroad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Acts 5:33; Acts 9:23; Mark 14:1"> So from that day on, they planned together to kill Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 7:1; John 7:4; 2 Chronicles 13:19"> Therefore Jesus stopped walking publicly among the Jews, but went away from there to the region near the wilderness, into a city called Ephraim, and there He stayed with the disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:17–20; Hebrews 9:13–14; John 6:4"> Now the Passover of the Jews was near, and many went from the country up to Jerusalem before the Passover, to purify themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="John 7:11; John 11:7–8"> Then they were looking for Jesus, and spoke among themselves as they stood in the temple, “What do you think? Isn’t He coming to the feast at all?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="John 9:22; John 8:59; John 5:16–18"> Now the chief priests and the Pharisees had given a command, that anyone who knew where Jesus was should report it, that they might arrest Him.</span>
</div>
