<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 14:32; Matthew 26:36; 2 Samuel 15:23"> When Jesus had said these things, He went with His disciples across the Kidron ravine, where there was a garden, into which He and His disciples entered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 21:37; Luke 22:39; Mark 11:11–12"> Now Judas, who was betraying Him, also knew the place, because Jesus had often met there with His disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 18:12; Luke 22:47–53; Psalms 22:12"> Then Judas, leading a detachment<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The|strong=&quot;G3588&quot; Greek word used here indicates a unit size that normally consisted of about 600 Roman soldiers.">a</a></sup>of soldiers and officers from the chief priests and Pharisees, came there with lanterns, torches, and weapons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 13:1; John 18:7; Psalms 27:3"> Therefore Jesus, knowing everything that would happen to Him, went forward and asked them,<span className="woj">“Who are you looking for?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 21:11; John 19:19; Matthew 2:23"> They answered, “Jesus of Nazareth.” Jesus told them,<span className="woj">“I AM.”</span>Judas, who betrayed Him, also stood with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 27:2; Psalms 70:2–3; 2 Kings 1:9–15"> Now when He told them,<span className="woj">“I AM,”</span>they drew back and fell to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 18:4"> Then He asked them again,<span className="woj">“Who are you looking for?”</span>They said, “Jesus of Nazareth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:28; Matthew 26:56; 2 Corinthians 12:9"> Jesus answered,<span className="woj">“I told you that I AM. So, if you are looking for me, let these go their way,”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 17:12"> that the saying might be fulfilled which He spoke,<span className="woj">“I have lost none of those who You gave me.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 14:47; Matthew 26:51–54; Luke 22:49–51"> Then Simon Peter, having a sword, drew it and struck the high priest’s servant, and cut off his right ear. The servant’s name was Malchus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 20:22; Matthew 26:39; John 20:17"> So Jesus told Peter,<span className="woj">“Put your sword into its sheath! Shouldn’t I drink the cup that my Father has given me?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 26:57; John 18:3; Acts 21:37"> Then the detachment of soldiers and their commander and the officers of the Jews arrested Jesus and tied Him up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 18:24; John 11:51; Matthew 26:3"> They led Him away to Annas first, because he was the father-in-law of Caiaphas, who was high priest that year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 11:49–52"> It was Caiaphas who advised the Jews that it was expedient that one man should die for the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 22:54; Mark 14:54; Matthew 26:58–68"> Simon Peter was following Jesus, and so was another disciple. Since the high priest knew that disciple, he entered with Jesus into the high priest’s court,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 26:69; Mark 14:66–68; Luke 22:55–57"> but Peter was standing just outside the door. So the other disciple, who the high priest knew, went out and spoke to the woman who guarded the door, and brought Peter in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 26:69–70; John 21:15; Luke 22:56–57"> Then the maidservant who kept the door asked Peter, “You aren’t one of this man’s disciples, too, are you?” He said, “I am not.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 14:54; John 18:25; Mark 14:67"> Because it was cold, the servants and the officers were standing there warming themselves, having made a charcoal fire. Peter was also with them, standing and warming himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 14:55–65; Luke 20:20; Luke 22:63–71"> Then the high priest asked Jesus about His disciples and His doctrine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:26; Isaiah 48:16; Matthew 4:23"> Jesus answered him,<span className="woj">“I have spoken openly to the world. I always taught in synagogues, and in the temple, where all the Jews come together. I said nothing in secret.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 26:59–60; Acts 24:12–13; Luke 22:67"> <span className="woj">Why do you ask me? Ask those who have heard me to tell you what I told them. They certainly know what I said.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 19:3; Mark 14:65; Luke 22:63–64"> When He had said these things, one of the officers who stood by hit Jesus with the palm of his hand, saying, “Is that how you answer the high priest?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Peter 2:20–23; 2 Corinthians 10:1; Matthew 5:39"> Jesus answered him,<span className="woj">“If I have spoken evil, testify about the evil, but if I have spoken honestly, why do you hit me?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 18:13; Matthew 26:57"> Then Annas sent Him, still bound, to Caiaphas, the high priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 14:67–72; John 18:17–18; Genesis 18:15"> Now Simon Peter stood and warmed himself. Therefore they asked him, “You aren’t one of His disciples, too, are you?” He denied it, saying, “I am not!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 18:1; John 18:10; Matthew 26:73"> One of the servants of the high priest, a relative of the man whose ear Peter cut off, said, “Didn’t I see you in the garden with Him?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 13:38; Mark 14:71–72; Luke 22:60–62"> Peter then denied it again, and immediately a rooster crowed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 18:33; John 19:9; Matthew 27:27"> Then they led Jesus from Caiaphas to the Praetorium.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The|strong=&quot;G3588&quot; Praetorium was the Roman Governor’s official residence.">b</a></sup>It was early morning. They themselves didn’t go into the Praetorium, so that they wouldn’t be defiled, and so that they might eat the Passover supper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 27:23; Mark 15:2–5; Matthew 27:11–14"> So Pilate came out to them and asked, “What charges do you bring against this man?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 3:13; Luke 20:19–26; Mark 10:33"> They answered, “If this man wasn’t a criminal, we wouldn’t have handed him over to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 19:6–7; John 19:15; Acts 25:18–20"> Then Pilate told them, “You take Him and judge Him according to your Law.” Therefore the Jews said to him, “It’s illegal for us to put anyone to death.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 3:14; Matthew 20:19; Matthew 26:2"> This happened so that the words would be fulfilled which Jesus had spoken indicating the kind of death he was going to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 23:3–4; John 18:37; John 19:3"> Then Pilate went back into the Praetorium, summoned Jesus, and asked Him, “Are You the King of the Jews?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Jesus answered,<span className="woj">“Are you speaking for yourself about this, or did others tell you this about me?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 18:14–16; Nehemiah 4:2; Romans 3:1–2"> Pilate answered, “I’m not a Jew, am I? Your own nation and the chief priests have delivered You to me. What have You done?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 17:20–21; Colossians 1:12–14; Romans 14:17"> Jesus answered,<span className="woj">“My kingdom doesn’t belong to this world. If my kingdom did belong to this world, then my servants would be fighting to keep me from being delivered up to the Jews; but as it is, my kingdom isn’t from here.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 John 4:6; John 8:47; John 8:14"> Pilate therefore asked Him, “So You are a king?” Jesus answered,<span className="woj">“You are right in saying that I am a king. For this I have been born, and for this I have come into the world, to testify about the truth. Everyone who belongs to the truth listens to my voice.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 19:4; Luke 23:4; John 19:6"> “What is truth?” Pilate asked. After asking this, he went out again to the Jews and said, “I find no basis for a charge against Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 27:15–18; Luke 23:16–23; Mark 15:6–14"> but you have a custom that I should release someone to you at the Passover. Therefore you decide: do you want me to release the King of the Jews to you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 23:25; Mark 15:15; Matthew 27:16"> Then they all shouted back, saying, “Not this man, but Barabbas!” Now Barabbas was a robber.</span>
