<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 12:46; John 7:5; Luke 8:19"> Therefore His brothers told Him, “Leave here and go to Judea, so that Your disciples will see the works that You are doing, too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 18:20; Proverbs 18:1–2; Matthew 23:5"> Nobody does anything in secret while he seeks to be known openly. If You do these things, show Yourself to the world.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 3:21; John 1:11–13; Micah 7:5–6"> For even His brothers didn’t believe in Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 7:8; John 2:4; John 7:30"> Then Jesus told them,<span className="woj">“My time hasn’t yet come, but your time is always right.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:18–19; John 3:19; Galatians 4:16"> <span className="woj">The world can’t hate you, but it hates me because I testify that its works are evil.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 7:6; John 8:20; John 7:30"> <span className="woj">Go up to this feast yourselves. I’m not going up to this feast, yet, for my time hasn’t yet fully come.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Having said these things to them, He stayed in Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 5:13; Matthew 10:16; Isaiah 42:2–3"> When His brothers had gone up to the feast, however, Jesus also went up, not publicly, but in secret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 11:56"> The Jews were looking for Him at the feast, and saying, “Where is He?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 7:40–43; John 9:16; John 7:32"> There was much complaining among the people about Him. Some said, “He is good,” but others said, “No, He deceives the people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 9:22; John 19:38; John 20:19"> No one spoke openly of Him for fear of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 7:28; Luke 19:47; John 7:37"> About the middle of the feast, Jesus went up into the temple and began to teach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 4:22; Matthew 7:28–29; Matthew 13:54"> The Jews marveled, saying, “How does this man know letters, having never been educated?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 14:10; John 14:24; John 17:8"> Jesus answered,<span className="woj">“My teaching isn’t mine, but His who sent me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 8:31–32; John 8:43; Jeremiah 31:33–34"> <span className="woj">Anyone who wants to do His will shall know if the teaching is from God or if I speak on my own.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 5:41; 1 Corinthians 10:31–33; John 13:31–32"> <span className="woj">The one who speaks on his own seeks his own glory; but He who seeks the glory of the One who sent Him is true, and no unrighteousness is in Him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 1:17; John 11:53; Matthew 12:14"> <span className="woj">Didn’t Moses give you the Law, yet none of you keeps the Law? Why are you trying to kill me?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 10:20; John 8:48; John 8:52"> The people answered, “You have a demon. Who is trying to kill You?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 7:23; John 5:2–11"> Jesus answered,<span className="woj">“I did one work, and you all marvel.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 12:3; Genesis 17:10–14; Romans 4:9–11"> <span className="woj">Moses gave you circumcision (not that it’s from Moses, but from the forefathers), and you circumcise a boy on the Sabbath.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 12:5; John 5:14–16; Matthew 12:2"> <span className="woj">If a boy gets circumcised on the Sabbath, so that the Law of Moses should not be broken, are you angry with me because I made a man completely well on the Sabbath?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 11:3–4; James 2:9; John 8:15"> <span className="woj">Don’t judge by appearance, but judge with righteous judgment.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 7:10–11"> Some of the people of Jerusalem said, “Isn’t this the man they are trying to kill?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7–8; John 7:48; John 12:42"> Look! He is speaking boldly, and they say nothing to Him. The rulers haven’t really concluded that this is the Christ, have they?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Micah 5:2; John 7:41–42; John 6:42"> Still, we know where this man is from, but when the Christ comes, no one will know where He is from.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 8:42; John 8:19; John 8:26"> Then Jesus cried out, as He taught in the temple,<span className="woj">“You know me, and you know where I’m from. I haven’t come of my own accord, but He who sent me is true, who you don’t know.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:55; Matthew 11:27; John 17:18"> <span className="woj">I know Him, because I am from Him, and He sent me.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 8:20; John 10:39; John 7:6"> Because of this, they were seeking to seize Him, but no one laid hands on Him, because His time had not yet come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 12:42; Matthew 12:23; John 10:41–42"> Many of the people believed in Him, saying, “When the Christ comes, will He do more signs than this man has done?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 18:3; Luke 22:52–53; John 11:47–48"> The Pharisees heard the crowd murmuring these things about Jesus, and the Pharisees and the chief priests sent officers to seize Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 16:5; John 13:33; Mark 16:19"> Then Jesus told them,<span className="woj">“I will be with you a little while longer, and then I am going to Him who sent me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24–31; John 13:33–36; John 14:6"> <span className="woj">You will look for me, and not find me, and where I am, you can’t come.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="James 1:1; Isaiah 11:12; Zephaniah 3:10"> Then the Jews said among themselves, “Where does He intend to go that we won’t be able to find Him? He doesn’t intend to go to the dispersion among the Greeks and teach the Greeks, does He?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 3:9; John 7:34; John 12:34"> What does He mean by saying,<span className="woj">‘You will look for me, and not find me, and where I am, you can’t come’</span>?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 55:1; John 4:14; John 6:35"> On the last and greatest day of the feast, Jesus stood and declared loudly,<span className="woj">“If anyone is thirsty, let that person come to me and drink.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 4:14; Isaiah 44:3; Isaiah 58:11"> <span className="woj">Rivers of living water will flow from within whoever believes in me, as the Scripture has said.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 2:17; Joel 2:28; John 14:16–17"> He said this about the Spirit, who those believing in Him would receive. The Holy Spirit wasn’t yet given, because Jesus wasn’t yet glorified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 21:11; John 1:21; John 6:14"> When they heard these words, many in the crowd were saying, “This is really the prophet.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 7:52; John 1:46; John 1:49"> Others were saying, “This is the Christ,” but some were saying, “Surely the Christ isn’t going to come from Galilee, is He?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Matthew 2:5; Luke 2:4"> Hasn’t the Scripture said that the Christ comes from the seed of David and from the town of Bethlehem, where David was?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 9:16; John 10:19; John 7:12"> So there was a division among the crowd because of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 7:30; Acts 23:11; John 8:20"> Some of them wanted to seize Him, but no one laid hands on Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 7:32; Acts 5:21–27"> When the officers went back to the chief priests and Pharisees, they asked them, “Why didn’t you bring Him in?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Matthew 7:28–29; Luke 4:22; John 7:26"> The officers answered, “No man ever spoke like this man!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 7:12; 2 Corinthians 6:8; 2 Kings 18:32"> Then the Pharisees answered, “You haven’t been deceived, too, have you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 12:42; 1 Corinthians 1:20; 1 Corinthians 2:8"> Have any of the rulers or Pharisees believed in Him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:20–21; John 9:40; John 9:34"> But this accursed crowd doesn’t know the Law.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 19:39; John 3:1–2"> Nicodemus (who came to Jesus before, being one of them) asked them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Proverbs 18:13; Deuteronomy 1:17; Deuteronomy 19:15–19"> “Does our Law permit us to judge a man before hearing him and finding out what he is doing?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 7:41; John 1:46; Isaiah 9:1–2"> They answered him, “You aren’t from Galilee, too, are you? Search and you will see that no prophet comes out of Galilee.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Psalms 33:10; Job 5:12–13; Psalms 76:10"> Then everyone went home,</span>
