<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 13:31–32; John 17:4–5; Philippians 2:9–11"> After Jesus said these things, He looked toward Heaven, and said:<span className="woj">“Father, the time has come. Glorify Your Son, so that Your Son may also glorify You,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 10:28–29; John 6:39; Daniel 7:14"> <span className="woj">as You have given Him authority over all flesh, that He should give eternal life to as many as You have given Him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 5:20; 1 John 5:11–12; Jeremiah 9:23–24"> <span className="woj">This is eternal life, that they may know You, the only true God, and Jesus Christ who You have sent.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 4:34; Acts 20:24; 2 Timothy 4:7"> <span className="woj">I have glorified You on the Earth. I have finished the work which You have given me to do.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 1:1–3; Colossians 1:15–17; John 10:30"> <span className="woj">Now, O Father, glorify me together with Yourself, with the glory which I had with You before the world existed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 17:26; John 14:21–24; 1 John 5:20"> <span className="woj">“I have revealed Your name to the people You’ve given me out of the world. They were Yours. You gave them to me, and they have kept Your word.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 7:16–17; John 16:15; John 14:7–10"> <span className="woj">Now they know that everything You’ve given me comes from You,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 17:14; John 15:15; John 16:30"> <span className="woj">for I have spoken to them the words You’ve given me. They have received Your words, and have come to know for certain that I came forth from You. They have believed that You sent me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; John 17:2; John 17:6"> <span className="woj">It is for them I pray. I’m not praying for the world, but for those who You’ve given me, because they belong to You.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 10:30; 1 Peter 2:9; Revelation 5:8–14"> <span className="woj">All of mine are Yours, and Yours are mine. I am glorified in them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 6:9; Proverbs 18:10; John 17:12–18"> <span className="woj">Now I am no longer in the world, but these are in the world. I am coming to You. Holy Father, keep through Your name those who You’ve given me, that they may be one just as we are one.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 109:6–19; John 10:27–28; John 18:9"> <span className="woj">I kept them in Your name while I was with them in the world. I have kept the people You gave me; and none of them is lost except the son of perdition, so that the Scripture might be fulfilled.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Psalms 41:9; John 6:70-71">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Galatians 5:22; John 15:11"> <span className="woj">Now I am coming to You, and I speak these things in the world, so that they may have my joy fulfilled in themselves.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 17:16; John 15:18–21; 1 John 5:19–20"> <span className="woj">I have given them Your word. The world has hated them because they don’t belong to the world, just as I don’t belong to the world.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 121:7; 1 John 5:18; Matthew 6:13"> <span className="woj">I don’t pray that You should take them out of the world, but that You should keep them from the evil one.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 17:14"> <span className="woj">They don’t belong to the world, just as I don’t belong to the world.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 15:3; Psalms 119:160; Ephesians 5:26"> <span className="woj">Sanctify them by Your truth. Your word is truth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:20; John 20:21; John 17:3"> <span className="woj">As You sent me into the world, I have sent them into the world.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Titus 2:14; Hebrews 2:11; John 17:17"> <span className="woj">I sanctify myself for their sakes, so that they may also be sanctified by the truth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 17:6–11; Acts 2:41; Acts 4:4"> <span className="woj">“I don’t pray for these alone, but also for those who will believe in me through their message,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:12; Colossians 3:11–14; John 17:11"> <span className="woj">that they all may be one, as You, Father, are in me, and I in You. I pray that they also may be one in Us, so that the world may believe that You sent me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:18; John 14:20; Mark 16:17–20"> <span className="woj">I have given them the glory which You gave me, that they may be one just as we are one:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 17:21; John 16:27; 1 John 3:1"> <span className="woj">I in them and You in me. May they be brought to complete unity to let the world know that You sent me and have sincerely loved them even as You have sincerely loved me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 17:5; John 12:26; John 14:3"> <span className="woj">Father, I desire that those You gave me may also be with me where I am, that they may see my glory which You’ve given me; because You sincerely loved me before the foundation of the world.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 17:11; John 7:29; John 17:8"> <span className="woj">O righteous Father! The world hasn’t known You, but I have always known You, and these have come to know that You sent me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 17:23; John 17:6; John 15:9"> <span className="woj">I have declared to them Your name, and will continue to declare it, so that the sincere love with which You sincerely loved me may be in them, and I may be in them.”</span></span>
