<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 14:3–8; John 11:1; Luke 24:50"> Six days before the Passover, Jesus came to Bethany, where Lazarus was who had been dead, who He had raised from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 22:27; Luke 10:38–42; Revelation 3:20"> There they made Him a supper, and Martha served, but Lazarus was one of those reclining at the table with Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; John 11:2; Luke 10:38–39"> Then Mary took a pound of very costly perfume of pure nard,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Nard|strong=&quot;G3487&quot;, also called spikenard, is an East Indian plant with pleasantly scented roots.">a</a></sup>and anointed the feet of Jesus, and wiped His feet with her hair. The house was filled with the fragrance of the perfume.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:4; 1 Samuel 17:28–29; John 13:2"> Then one of Jesus’ disciples, Judas Iscariot, Simon’s son, who was about to betray Him, said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 18:22; Luke 12:33; Mark 14:4–5"> “Why wasn’t this fragrant oil sold for three hundred denarii<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="300 denarii was about a year’s wages for a laborer.">b</a></sup>and given to the poor?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 13:29; Psalms 50:16–20; James 2:6"> Now he said this, not because he was concerned about the poor, but because he was a thief, and since he had the money box, he used to steal from what was put into it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 26:12; Mark 14:6; Matthew 27:57–60"> Jesus therefore said,<span className="woj">“Leave her alone. She has kept this for the day of my burial.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:11; Mark 14:7; Matthew 26:11"> <span className="woj">You will always have the poor among you, but you won’t always have me.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 4:14; John 11:43–45; Acts 3:10–11"> Then a great multitude of the Jews learned that He was there; and they came, not just for Jesus’ sake, but also so that they could see Lazarus, who He had raised from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 16:31; Matthew 2:16; John 11:47–53"> So the chief priests plotted to kill Lazarus too,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 11:45; John 12:18; Acts 13:45"> because on account of him many of the Jews were going away, and were believing in Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 11:7–10; Matthew 21:4–9; Luke 19:35–38"> The next day, when a great multitude that had come to the feast heard that Jesus was coming to Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 118:25–26; Revelation 15:3; Matthew 21:9–11"> they took branches of palm trees and went out to meet Him, and shouted:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Hosanna!</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Hosanna” is derived from a Hebrew word for “Save us” and is used as an expression of praise. Psalm 118:25-26.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘Blessed is He who comes in the name of the LORD!’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">The King of Israel!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Mark 11:1–7; Matthew 21:1–7"> Then Jesus, finding a young donkey, sat on it, as it is written:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Isaiah 62:11; Zephaniah 3:16–17"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Don’t be afraid, Daughter of Zion;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">look, your king is coming,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">sitting on a donkey’s colt.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Zechariah 9:9">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 2:22; John 14:26; John 7:39"> His disciples didn’t understand these things at first, but when Jesus was glorified, then they remembered that these things were written about Him and that they had done these things to Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 1:2; 1 John 5:9–12; John 19:35"> So the crowd that had been with Him when He called Lazarus out of the tomb and raised him from the dead continued to testify.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 12:11"> The crowd also met Him because they heard that He had done this sign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 17:6; Psalms 22:27; 1 John 2:2"> Then the Pharisees said among themselves, “You see that you are accomplishing nothing. Look, the whole world has gone after Him!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:35; Isaiah 11:10; Acts 17:4"> Now there were certain Greeks among those who came up to worship at the feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 2:2; Luke 19:2–4; John 6:40"> Then they came to Philip, who was from Bethsaida of Galilee, and told him, “Sir, we would like to see Jesus.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 10:13–14; Luke 9:49–50; John 1:40–41"> Philip came and told Andrew, then Andrew and Philip came and told Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 13:31–32; John 17:1–5; Isaiah 60:9"> Jesus answered them, saying,<span className="woj">“The time has come for the Son of Man to be glorified.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:36–38; Isaiah 53:10–12; Hebrews 2:9–10"> <span className="woj">Most assuredly, I tell you, unless a grain of wheat falls into the ground and dies, it remains alone, but if it dies, it produces many grains.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 10:39; Luke 9:23–24; Mark 8:35"> <span className="woj">Anyone who loves their life will lose it, and anyone who hates their life in this world will keep it for eternal life.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 3:24; Galatians 1:10; 1 Samuel 2:30"> <span className="woj">If anyone serves me, let them follow me; and where I am, there my servant will be also. If anyone serves me, my Father will honor them.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 12:23; Hebrews 5:7; Matthew 26:42"> <span className="woj">“Now my soul has become troubled; and what will I say? ‘Father, save me from this time’? No, for this purpose I came to this time.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 13:31–32; Matthew 26:42; Revelation 5:9–14"> <span className="woj">Father, glorify Your name.”</span><br />Then a voice came from Heaven, saying,<span className="woj">“I have both glorified it and will glorify it again.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 37:2–5; Revelation 6:1; Revelation 14:2"> Therefore the people who stood by and heard it said that it had thundered. Others said, “An angel has spoken to Him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 11:42; John 11:15; John 5:34"> Jesus answered,<span className="woj">“This voice hasn’t come for my sake, but for your sakes.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4; 1 John 5:19; 1 John 3:8"> <span className="woj">Now judgment is upon this world. Now the ruler of this world will be cast out.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 3:14; John 8:28; 1 Peter 2:24"> <span className="woj">And I, if I am lifted up from the Earth, will draw everyone to myself.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 18:32; John 21:19"> He was saying this to indicate the kind of death He was about to die.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:24–25; Isaiah 9:7; Daniel 7:14"> Therefore the crowd answered Him, “We have heard from the Law that the Christ is to remain forever, so how can You say,<span className="woj">‘The Son of Man must be lifted up’</span>? Who is this Son of Man?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 12:46; John 16:16; Ephesians 5:8"> Jesus told them,<span className="woj">“The light is with you for a little longer. Walk while you have the light, so that the darkness may not overtake you. If you walk in the darkness, you don’t know where you are going.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; John 8:59; Acts 13:47–48"> <span className="woj">Believe in the light while you have the light, so that you may become children of light.”</span>After Jesus had said this, he departed and hid from them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 16:31; John 1:11; John 15:24"> Although He had performed so many signs in front of them, they still didn’t believe in Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1; Romans 10:16; Romans 10:20"> so that the word of Isaiah the prophet might be fulfilled, which he spoke:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Lord, who has believed our report?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And who has the arm of the LORD been revealed to?”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Isaiah|strong=&quot;G2268&quot; 53:1">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 10:38; John 5:44; 2 Peter 2:14"> Therefore they couldn’t believe, because Isaiah said again:</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10; Matthew 13:13–15; Luke 8:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">“He has blinded their eyes</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and hardened their hearts,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">lest they see with their eyes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and understand with their hearts and turn —</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I would heal them.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Isaiah 6:10">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 6:1–5; Isaiah 6:9–10; John 1:14"> Isaiah said this because he saw Jesus’ glory and spoke about Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 9:22; John 7:13; Isaiah 51:7"> Nevertheless, even many of the rulers believed in Him, but because of the Pharisees they weren’t confessing Him, for fear that they might be put out of the synagogue;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 5:44; Matthew 23:5–7; John 5:41"> because they sincerely loved men’s approval more than God’s approval.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 10:40; John 13:20; John 5:24"> Jesus cried out and said,<span className="woj">“Whoever believes in me doesn’t just believe in me, but in Him who sent me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 14:9–10; 2 Corinthians 4:6; Colossians 1:15"> <span className="woj">Whoever sees me sees Him who sent me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 8:12; Psalms 36:9; John 9:5"> <span className="woj">I have come into the world as light, that everyone who believes in me should not stay in darkness.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 3:17; John 8:15–16; John 12:48"> <span className="woj">If anyone hears the words I speak, and doesn’t keep them, I don’t judge them; because I didn’t come to judge the world, but to save the world.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 10:16; Hebrews 9:27–28; Romans 2:16"> <span className="woj">Whoever rejects me, and doesn’t receive my sayings, has one who judges them. The words that I spoke are what will judge him at the last day.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 14:10; John 8:26; John 5:30"> <span className="woj">For I haven’t spoken on my own initiative, but the Father Himself who sent me has given me commandment, what to say, and what to speak.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 17:3; John 6:68; 1 John 5:20"> <span className="woj">I know that His commandment is eternal life, so the things I speak, I speak just as the Father has told me.”</span></span>
</div>
