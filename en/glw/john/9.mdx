<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Passing by, Jesus saw a man who was blind from birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 13:2; John 9:34; Acts 28:4"> His disciples asked Him, “Rabbi, who sinned, this man or his parents, that he was born blind?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:4; John 11:40; Ecclesiastes 9:1–2"> Jesus answered,<span className="woj">“Neither this man nor his parents sinned, but this happened so that God’s works might be revealed in this man.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 12:35; Ephesians 5:16; John 4:34"> <span className="woj">We must work the works of Him who sent me, as long as it’s day. Night is coming, when nobody can work.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 8:12; John 12:46; Acts 13:47"> <span className="woj">As long as I am in the world, I am the world’s light.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 8:23; Mark 7:33; Revelation 3:18"> After Jesus said this, He spat on the ground and made mud with the saliva, put it on the blind man’s eyes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5; John 9:11; John 11:37"> and told him,<span className="woj">“Go, wash in the pool of Siloam”</span>(which means Sent). So he went away and washed, and came back seeing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:8; 1 Samuel 21:11; Luke 18:35"> Then the neighbors and those who used to see him as a beggar began to ask, “Isn’t this the man who used to sit and beg?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Some were saying, “Yes, it is he,” others were saying, “No, but he is like him.” He kept saying, “I’m the one.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 9:26; John 9:21; Ecclesiastes 11:5"> So they asked him, “How were your eyes opened?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 9:6–7; John 9:27; Jeremiah 36:17"> He answered, “The man called Jesus made mud, put it on my eyes, and told me,<span className="woj">‘Go to Siloam, and wash’</span>; so I went and washed, and I received sight.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 5:11–13; Exodus 2:18–20; John 7:11"> “Where is this man?” they asked him.<br />“I don’t know,” he said.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 11:57; John 11:46–47; John 12:42"> They brought the man who used to be blind to the Pharisees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 5:9; Luke 13:10–17; Matthew 12:1–14"> Now it was a sabbath day when Jesus made the mud and opened the man’s eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 9:26–27; John 9:10–11"> So the Pharisees were also asking him how he received his sight. He told them, “He applied mud to my eyes, I washed, and I see.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 7:43; John 10:19; Matthew 12:2"> Therefore some of the Pharisees said, “This Man isn’t from God, because He doesn’t keep the Sabbath.” Others said, “How can a sinner do such signs?” So there was a division among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 4:19; John 6:14; Acts 3:22–26"> So they turned again to the blind man and asked, “What do you say about him? It was your eyes he opened.”<br />He said, “He is a prophet.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 3:15–19; John 5:44; Isaiah 26:11"> The Jews didn’t believe that he had been blind, and had received sight, until they called the parents of the one who had received his sight,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 9:8–9; Acts 4:14; Acts 3:10"> and questioned them, saying, “Is this your son, who you say was born blind? Then how does he now see?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> His parents answered them, “We know that this is our son, and that he was born blind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> but how he now sees, we don’t know. Who opened his eyes, we don’t know. Ask him. He is of age. He will speak for himself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 7:13; John 16:2; Luke 6:22"> His parents said this because they were afraid of the Jews, for the Jews had already agreed that if anyone would confess that Jesus was the Christ, they would be put out of the synagogue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 9:21"> For this reason, his parents said, “Ask him. He is of age.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 7:19; John 9:16; John 8:49"> So they called the man who had been blind a second time, and told him, “Give glory to God. We know that this man is a sinner.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 John 5:10; John 9:30; John 5:11"> He answered, “Whether He is a sinner or not I don’t know. One thing I do know: that though I was blind, now I see.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Then they asked him again, “What did He do to you? How did He open your eyes?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 22:67; John 9:10–15"> He answered, “I told you already, and you didn’t listen. Why do you want to hear it again? You don’t want to become His disciples, too, do you?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 2:17; John 7:19; Matthew 27:39"> Then they became abusive towards him and said, “You are that man’s disciple, but we are Moses’ disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:14; Numbers 12:2–7; Acts 7:35"> We know that God spoke to Moses, but as for this man, we don’t know where He is from.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 12:37; Psalms 119:18; Isaiah 29:18"> The man answered, “How amazing! You don’t know where He comes from, yet He opened my eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 15:29; Isaiah 1:15; Proverbs 28:9"> We know that God doesn’t hear sinners, but if anyone is God-fearing, and does His will, He hears him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 64:4; Revelation 16:18; Job 20:4"> Since the beginning of time it has never been heard of that anyone opened the eyes of a person born blind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 9:16; John 3:2; Acts 5:38–39"> If this man weren’t from God, He could do nothing.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 9:22; John 9:2; Isaiah 66:5"> To this they replied, “Who are you to teach us? You were born entirely in sin!” Then they threw him out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 20:31; 1 John 5:13; John 10:36"> Jesus heard that they had thrown him out, and finding him, He said,<span className="woj">“Do you believe in the Son of Man?”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Romans 10:14; Song of Solomon 5:9; John 1:38"> He answered, “Who is He, sir, that I may believe in Him?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 4:26; Psalms 25:8–9; John 14:21–23"> Jesus told him,<span className="woj">“You have seen Him, and it is He who is talking with you.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 14:33; Matthew 28:9; Luke 24:52"> Then he said, “Lord, I believe!” and he worshipped Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 3:19; John 12:46; Acts 26:18"> Jesus said,<span className="woj">“I came into this world for judgment, so that those who don’t see may see, and those who do see may become blind.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Revelation 3:17; Matthew 15:12–14; John 7:47–52"> Some of the Pharisees who were with Him heard these things, and asked Him, “We aren’t blind too, are we?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 5:21; John 15:22–24; 1 John 1:8–10"> Jesus told them,<span className="woj">“If you were blind, you would have no sin, but since you say, ‘We see,’ your sin remains.</span></span>
</div>
</div>
