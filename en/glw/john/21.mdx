<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 6:1; John 6:23; Matthew 28:16"> After these things, Jesus showed Himself to the disciples again, at the Sea of Tiberias. He showed Himself in the following way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 4:21–22; John 1:45–1; John 4:46"> Simon Peter, Thomas called the Twin,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Greek: Didymus">a</a></sup>Nathanael of Cana in Galilee, the sons of Zebedee, and two others of His disciples were together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 5:5; Matthew 4:18–20; 2 Thessalonians 3:7–9"> Simon Peter told them, “I’m going fishing.” They said to him, “We’ll go with you, too.” They went out immediately and got into the boat, and that night they caught nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 20:14; Luke 24:15–16; Luke 24:31"> Now when the morning had come, Jesus stood on the shore, but the disciples didn’t know that it was Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 4:19; Philippians 4:11–13; 1 John 2:13"> Therefore Jesus asked them,<span className="woj">“Children, you don’t have any fish, do you?”</span>“No,” they answered Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 5:4–7; Acts 2:41; John 2:5"> He told them,<span className="woj">“Cast the net on the right-hand side of the boat, and you’ll find some.”</span>So, they cast the net, and then they were unable to haul it in because of the great number of fish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 13:23; John 20:2; Matthew 14:28–29"> Therefore that disciple who Jesus sincerely loved told Peter, “It’s the Lord!” So when Simon Peter heard that it was the Lord, he put his outer garment on (for he had taken it off), and threw himself into the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:11"> The other disciples came in the little boat, for they weren’t far from land, but about two hundred cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="200 cubits equals about 100 yards or about 91 meters">b</a></sup>away, dragging the net full of fish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 21:13; 1 Kings 19:5–6; John 21:10"> So when they came ashore, they saw a charcoal fire already burning, with fish and bread placed on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Jesus told them,<span className="woj">“Bring some of the fish that you just caught.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 5:6–8; Acts 2:41"> Simon Peter went up and dragged the net to land, full of one hundred fifty-three large fish. Although there were so many, the net wasn’t torn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 10:41; Luke 9:45; John 16:19"> Jesus said to them,<span className="woj">“Come and eat breakfast.”</span>Yet none of the disciples dared ask Him, “Who are You?” They knew that it was the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 21:9; Acts 10:41; Luke 24:42–43"> Jesus then came and took the bread and gave it to them, and likewise the fish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 20:26; John 20:19"> This is now the third time that Jesus showed Himself to His disciples after He was raised from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; 1 John 4:19; Romans 15:1"> When they had eaten breakfast, Jesus asked Simon Peter,<span className="woj">“Simon, son of Jonah, do you sincerely love me more than these?”</span>He answered, “Yes, Lord. You know that I love You.”<span className="woj">“Feed my lambs,”</span>Jesus replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 20:28; 1 Peter 5:2; 1 Peter 2:25"> He asked him a second time,<span className="woj">“Simon, son of Jonah, do you sincerely love me?”</span>He answered, “Yes, Lord. You know that I love You.”<span className="woj">“Shepherd my sheep,”</span>He replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 13:38; John 16:30; Mark 14:72"> He asked the third time,<span className="woj">“Simon, son of Jonah, do you love me?”</span>Peter was grieved because He asked him the third time,<span className="woj">“Do you love me?”</span>He answered, “Lord, You know everything. You know that I love You.”<span className="woj">“Feed My sheep,”</span>Jesus replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 13:36; Acts 12:3–4; Acts 21:11"> <span className="woj">“Most assuredly, I tell you, when you were younger, you dressed yourself and walked where you wanted to; but when you are old, you will stretch out your hands, and another will dress you and carry you where you don’t want to go.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Peter 1:14; John 21:22; Matthew 10:38"> He said this to indicate what kind of death he would glorify God with. When He had said this, He told Peter,<span className="woj">“Follow me!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 21:7; John 20:2; John 21:24"> Then Peter, turning around, saw a disciple following. This was the disciple who Jesus sincerely loved, the one who had also leaned on Jesus’ breast at the supper and asked, “Lord, who is going to betray You?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 13:23–24; Acts 1:6–7; Matthew 24:3–4"> Peter therefore, seeing him, asked Jesus, “Lord, what about this man?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 2:25; Revelation 3:11; Mark 9:1"> Jesus answered,<span className="woj">“If I want him to remain until I come, what is that to you? You follow me!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:29; Job 33:13; Daniel 4:35"> Because of this answer, the rumor spread among the brothers that this disciple wouldn’t die, but Jesus didn’t tell him that he wouldn’t die, but only,<span className="woj">“If I want him to remain until I come, what is that to you?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 19:35; John 15:27; 1 John 5:6"> This is the disciple who testifies about these things, and who wrote these things. We know that his testimony is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 40:5; John 20:30–31; Psalms 71:15"> There are also many other things that Jesus did, which if they were written one by one, I suppose that even the world itself couldn’t contain the books that would be written. Amen.</span>
