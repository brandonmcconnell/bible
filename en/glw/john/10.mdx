<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 7:15; John 10:8–10; Romans 16:18"> <span className="woj">“Most assuredly, I tell you, whoever doesn’t enter the sheepfold by the door, but climbs in some other way, is a thief and a robber.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 10:14; Acts 20:28; 1 Peter 5:4"> <span className="woj">The one who enters by the gate is the sheep’s shepherd.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:11–16; 2 Timothy 2:19; John 10:4"> <span className="woj">The gatekeeper opens the gate for him, and the sheep hear his voice. He calls his own sheep by name and leads them out.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:30; John 10:16; John 12:26"> <span className="woj">When he brings out his own sheep, he goes before them, and the sheep follow him, because they know his voice.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 John 4:5–6; Colossians 2:6–10; Mark 4:24"> <span className="woj">There is no way that they will follow a stranger, but will run away from the stranger, because they don’t know the voice of strangers.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 56:11; Psalms 82:5; Proverbs 28:5"> Jesus used this illustration, but the Pharisees didn’t understand what He was telling them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 14:6; Ephesians 2:18; John 10:1"> Then Jesus told them again,<span className="woj">“Most assuredly, I tell you, I am the sheep’s door.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2; Jeremiah 23:1; John 10:1"> <span className="woj">All who ever came before me are thieves and robbers, but the sheep didn’t listen to them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 14:6; Ephesians 2:18; John 10:7"> <span className="woj">I am the door. Anyone who enters by me will be saved, and will go in and out and find pasture.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 19:10; John 6:51; John 12:47"> <span className="woj">The thief comes only to steal, and kill, and destroy. I came that they might have life, and that they might have it in all its fullness.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 10:14–15; Psalms 23:1; John 15:13"> <span className="woj">I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd lays down His life for the sheep.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 20:29; Zechariah 11:16–17; Ezekiel 34:2–6"> <span className="woj">The hired hand, who isn’t the shepherd and doesn’t own the sheep, sees the wolf coming and leaves the sheep and runs away. So, the wolf snatches them and scatters them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 2:20; John 12:6; Acts 18:17"> <span className="woj">The hired hand is concerned about the wages, and not the sheep, so the hired hand runs away.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 10:27; John 10:11; 2 Timothy 2:19"> <span className="woj">I am the good shepherd. I know my own and my own know me,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 1:4; John 10:11; Isaiah 53:10"> <span className="woj">just as the Father knows me and I know the Father. I lay down my life for the sheep.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 56:8; John 11:52; 1 Peter 2:25"> <span className="woj">I have other sheep that don’t belong to this fold. I must bring them also, and they will listen to my voice. So, there will be one flock, with one shepherd.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 10:18; John 10:11; Hebrews 2:9"> <span className="woj">For this reason the Father sincerely loves me, because I lay down my life, so that I may take it up again.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 2:9; Titus 2:14; John 2:19–21"> <span className="woj">No one takes it away from me, but I lay it down of my own free will. I have the authority to lay it down, and I have the authority to take it up again. I received this commandment from my Father.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 9:16; 1 Corinthians 3:3; John 7:40–43"> The Jews were divided again, because of these words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:20; Mark 3:21; John 8:47–48"> Many of them were saying, “He has a demon and is insane. Why do you listen to Him?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 4:11; Isaiah 35:5–6; Psalms 94:9"> Others were saying, “These aren’t the sayings of a demon-possessed person. Can a demon open the eyes of the blind?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> At that time the Feast of Dedication<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Chanukkah — celebration of recovery and cleansing of the Temple by Judas Maccabeaus in 164 BC.">a</a></sup>took place in Jerusalem. It was winter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 5:12; Acts 3:11"> and Jesus was walking in the temple, in Solomon’s porch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 22:67–70; John 1:19; John 8:25"> Then the Jews gathered around Him, and asked Him, “How long will You keep us in suspense? If You are the Christ, tell us plainly.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 10:38; John 8:58; John 14:11"> Jesus answered,<span className="woj">“I told you, but you don’t believe. The works that I do in my Father’s name testify about me,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 8:47; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4; John 12:37–40"> <span className="woj">but you don’t believe, because you aren’t of my sheep.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 10:16; John 10:14; Revelation 3:20"> <span className="woj">My sheep listen to my voice. I know them, and they follow me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 6:39–40; John 6:37; John 3:15–16"> <span className="woj">I give them eternal life, and they will never perish. No one is able to snatch them out of my hand.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 6:37; John 17:2; John 17:9"> <span className="woj">My Father, who has given them to me, is greater than all; and no one is able to snatch them out of my Father’s hand.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 1:1–2; John 14:9; John 17:21–23"> <span className="woj">I and my Father are one.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 5:18; John 8:59; 1 Samuel 30:6"> Therefore the Jews took up stones again to stone Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 John 3:12; John 5:36; Psalms 35:12"> Jesus answered,<span className="woj">“I showed you many good works from the Father. For which of them are you stoning me?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 5:18; Leviticus 24:16; John 10:30"> The Jews answered Him, “We aren’t stoning you for a good work, but for blasphemy, because You, being a man, claim to be God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 82:6–7; Exodus 7:1; Psalms 82:1"> Jesus answered,<span className="woj">“Isn’t it written in your Law, ‘I said, “You are gods”’?</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Psalm 82:6">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Romans 13:1; Luke 16:17"> <span className="woj">If he, who God’s word came to, called them gods, (and the Scripture can’t be broken),</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 5:17–18; John 3:17; Jeremiah 1:5"> <span className="woj">do you say of Him who the Father set apart and sent into the world, ‘You are blaspheming,’ because I said, ‘I am God’s Son’?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 15:24; John 10:25; John 12:37–40"> <span className="woj">If I don’t do my Father’s works, then don’t believe me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 14:20; John 10:25; John 10:30"> <span className="woj">But if I do them, even if you don’t believe me, believe the works, so that you may know and understand that the Father is in me, and I am in the Father.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 7:30; John 8:59; John 7:44"> Therefore they were trying again to seize Him, and He escaped from their grasp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 1:28; John 11:54; John 7:1"> Jesus went away again across the Jordan to the place where John was first baptizing, and He was staying there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 1:33–34; John 1:29–30; Mark 1:37"> Many came to Him and were saying, “John performed no miraculous sign, but everything that John said about this man was true.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 4:41; John 2:23; John 7:31"> Many believed in Jesus there.</span>
