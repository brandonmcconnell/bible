<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 53:5; Isaiah 50:6; Matthew 20:19"> Then Pilate took Jesus and had him flogged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 15:16–20; Luke 23:11; Matthew 27:27–31"> The soldiers twisted thorns into a crown and put it on His head, and they put a purple robe on Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 27:29; Matthew 26:49; Luke 1:28"> They kept coming up to Him, saying, “Hail, King of the Jews!” and slapping Him on the face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 18:38; John 19:6; Luke 23:4"> Pilate then went out again, and told the Jews, “Look, I’m bringing Him out to you, that you may know that I find no basis for a charge against Him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 19:2; Hebrews 12:2; Isaiah 7:14"> Then Jesus came out, wearing the crown of thorns and the purple robe, and Pilate said to them, “Look! Here is the man!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:31; John 19:4; Acts 2:23"> When the chief priests and officers saw Him, they shouted “Crucify Him! Crucify Him!” Pilate told them, “You take Him and crucify Him, because I find no basis for a charge against Him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 24:16; John 5:18; Matthew 26:63–66"> The Jews answered him, “We have a law, and according to our law He ought to die, because He claimed to be God’s Son.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 19:13; Acts 14:11–19"> When Pilate heard this, he was even more afraid,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 18:33; Psalms 38:13–15; Philippians 1:28"> and went back into the Praetorium, and asked Jesus, “Where are You from?” But Jesus didn’t answer him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 5:19; John 18:39; Daniel 3:14–15"> Pilate therefore asked him, “Do you refuse to speak to me? Don’t you know that I have authority to release you, and authority to crucify you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 13:1; John 3:27; Acts 2:23"> Jesus answered,<span className="woj">“You would have no authority at all against me unless it had been given you from above. Therefore the one who delivered me to you has the greater sin.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 17:6–7; John 18:33–36; Mark 6:16–26"> From then on, Pilate tried to release Him, but the Jews shouted, “If you let this man go, you aren’t Caesar’s friend. Whoever makes himself a king speaks against Caesar.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 82:5–7; Matthew 27:19; Amos 4:7"> Therefore when Pilate heard these words, he brought Jesus out, and sat down on the judgment seat at a place called “The Pavement,” but in Hebrew, “Gabbatha.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 27:62; John 19:5; Mark 15:25"> Now it was the day of preparation for the Passover. It was about the sixth hour.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The|strong=&quot;G3588&quot; sixth hour since sunrise, about noon.">a</a></sup>Pilate told the Jews, “Look! Here is your King!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 23:18; Acts 21:36; John 19:6"> They therefore shouted, “Away with Him! Away with Him! Crucify Him!” Pilate asked them, “Shall I crucify your King?” The chief priests answered, “We have no king but Caesar!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 23:24–25; Matthew 27:26–31; Mark 15:15–20"> Then Pilate delivered Jesus to them to be crucified. So they took Jesus and led Him away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 23:33; Luke 14:27; Luke 23:26"> And He, bearing His cross, went out to a place called the Place of a Skull, which is called in Hebrew, Golgotha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Mark 15:24–27; Galatians 3:13"> There they crucified Him, and two others with Him, one on either side, with Jesus in the center.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 27:37; Luke 23:38; Mark 15:26"> Now Pilate wrote a notice, and put it on the cross. The notice said:<br />“JESUS OF NAZARETH, THE KING OF THE JEWS.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 26:14; Revelation 16:16; Acts 22:2"> Then many of the Jews read this notice, because the place where Jesus was crucified was near the city, and it was written in Hebrew, Greek, and Latin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 19:14; John 19:19"> Therefore the chief priests of the Jews told Pilate, “Don’t write, ‘The King of the Jews,’ but, ‘He said, “I am the King of the Jews.”’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 76:10; Psalms 65:7; Proverbs 8:29"> Pilate answered, “What I have written, I have written.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 23:34; Mark 15:24; Matthew 27:35"> When the soldiers had crucified Jesus, they split His garments into four parts, to each soldier a part, including the tunic. Now the tunic was without seam, woven from the top in one piece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 22:18; John 19:28; John 19:36–37"> Therefore they said to one another, “Let’s not tear it, but cast lots to see whose it will be,” that the Scripture might be fulfilled which says: “They divided my garments among them, And for my clothing they cast lots.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Psalm 22:18">b</a></sup>So the soldiers did these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 23:49; Matthew 27:55–56; John 20:1"> Now Jesus’ mother, His mother’s sister, Mary the wife of Clopas, and Mary Magdalene stood by Jesus’ cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 21:24; John 20:2; John 2:4"> Therefore when Jesus saw His mother, and the disciple who He sincerely loved standing by, He told His mother,<span className="woj">“Woman, here is your son!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 12:48–50; John 16:32; 1 Timothy 5:2–4"> Then He told the disciple,<span className="woj">“Here is your mother!”</span>From that hour, that disciple took her into his own home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Psalms 22:15; John 19:30"> After this, Jesus, knowing that everything was now accomplished, so that the Scripture might be fulfilled, said,<span className="woj">“I’m thirsty!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 19:18; Luke 23:36; Mark 15:36"> A jar full of vinegar<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A|strong=&quot;G4060&quot; sour wine.">c</a></sup>was standing there, so they put a sponge full of the vinegar on a hyssop stalk, and lifted it up to His mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 17:4; Philippians 2:8; Colossians 2:14–17"> When Jesus had received the vinegar, He said,<span className="woj">“It is finished!”</span>Then, He bowed His head, and gave up His spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 19:14; John 19:42; Mark 15:42"> Because it was the Preparation Day,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The|strong=&quot;G3588&quot; Preparation Day is the day before the Sabbath. Preparations for meals and activities are made in advance so that no work has to be done on the Sabbath.">d</a></sup>the Jews asked Pilate for the prisoners’ legs to be broken, and that they might be taken away, so that the bodies wouldn’t stay on the cross on the Sabbath (because that Sabbath was a special one).</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 19:18; Luke 23:39–43"> Then the soldiers came and broke the legs of the first man and of the other man who was crucified with Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> but when they came to Jesus and saw that He was already dead, they didn’t break His legs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 John 5:6; Zechariah 12:10; Zechariah 13:1"> Instead, one of the soldiers pierced His side with a spear, and immediately blood and water came out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 21:24; Hebrews 2:3–4; 1 John 1:1–3"> He who saw it has testified, and his testimony is true; and he knows that he is telling the truth, so that you may believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 34:20; Numbers 9:12; Exodus 12:46"> These things were done so that the Scripture should be fulfilled, “Not one of His bones shall be broken.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Exodus 12:46; Numbers 9:12; Psalm 34:19-20">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Zechariah 12:10; Revelation 1:7; Psalms 22:16–17"> Another Scripture says, “They will look on Him who they pierced.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Zechariah 12:10">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Mark 15:42–47; Matthew 27:57–61; Proverbs 29:25"> After this, Joseph of Arimathea, being a disciple of Jesus, but secretly, for fear of the Jews, asked Pilate if he could take the body of Jesus away; and Pilate gave him permission. So he came and took the body of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 7:50–52; Song of Solomon 4:14; John 3:1–21"> Nicodemus, who at first came to Jesus by night, also came, bringing about a hundred Roman pounds<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="100 Roman pounds equals about 72 pounds weight or about 33 Kilograms. A Roman pound is 12 ounces.">g</a></sup>of a mixture of myrrh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Myrrh|strong=&quot;G4666&quot; is an aromatic resin formed from the gum that drips from the stems and branches of a low, shrubby tree (Commiphora myrhha and closely related trees) native to Arabian deserts and parts of Africa.">h</a></sup>and aloes.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Aloes|strong=&quot;G0250&quot; probably refers to an aromatic derivative from eaglewood (Aquilaria agallochum) trees found today in Malaya, East Bengal, and China.">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 11:44; John 20:5–7; Acts 5:6"> So they took the body of Jesus, and wrapped it in strips of linen with the spices, as is the burial custom of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 23:53; Matthew 27:60; John 20:15"> In the place where He was crucified, there was a garden, and in the garden a new tomb, in which no one had yet been laid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 19:31; John 19:14; Isaiah 53:9"> Because of the Jewish Preparation Day, and because the tomb was nearby, they laid Jesus there.</span>
</div>
