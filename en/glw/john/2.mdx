<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:14; Genesis 6:22; Hebrews 11:8"> His mother told the servants, “Do whatever He tells you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 3:25; Hebrews 9:10; Ephesians 5:26"> Nearby stood six stone water jars, the kind used by the Jews for ceremonial washing, each holding two to three metretes.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Two|strong=&quot;G1417&quot; to three metretes equals about 20 to 30 U. <span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 4:2–6; John 2:5; John 2:3"> Jesus told the servants,<span className="woj">“Fill the jars with water,”</span>so they filled them to the brim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 2:9; Proverbs 3:5–6; Romans 13:7"> Then He told them,<span className="woj">“Now draw some out and take it to the master of the banquet.”</span>They did so,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 4:46; John 7:17; Psalms 119:100"> and the master of the banquet tasted the water that had been turned into wine. He didn’t realize where it had come from, though the servants who had drawn the water knew. Then he called the bridegroom aside</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 7:16–17; Proverbs 9:1–6; Luke 16:25"> and said, “Everyone brings out the choice wine first, and then the cheaper wine after the guests have drunk freely, but you have saved the best till now!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 20:30–31; John 1:14; John 3:2"> Jesus did this beginning of signs in Cana of Galilee. He revealed His glory, and His disciples believed in Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:46; Matthew 13:55–56; Mark 6:3"> After this, He went down to Capernaum with His mother, His brothers, and His disciples, and they stayed there only a few days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 2:41; John 11:55; John 2:23"> The time for the Passover<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The|strong=&quot;G3588&quot; Passover Feast is also called the Feast of Unleavened Bread. <span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 19:45–46; Mark 11:15; Mark 11:17"> He found in the temple those who sold oxen and sheep and doves, and the money changers doing business.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 18:6; Zechariah 4:6; 2 Corinthians 10:4"> When He had made a whip of cords, He drove them all out of the temple, with the sheep and the oxen, and poured out the changers’ money and overturned the tables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 21:13; Jeremiah 7:11; Mark 11:17"> He told those who sold doves,<span className="woj">“Take these things away! Don’t make my Father’s house a market place!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 69:9; Psalms 119:139"> Then His disciples remembered that it was written, “Zeal for Your house will consume me.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Psalm 69:9">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Mark 8:11; Mark 11:27–28"> So the Jews answered and asked Him, “What sign do You show to us, since You do these things?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 14:58; Mark 15:29; Matthew 27:40"> Jesus answered them,<span className="woj">“Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Then the Jews said, “It has taken forty-six years to build this temple, and will You raise it up in three days?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:19; 1 Corinthians 3:16; Ephesians 2:20–22"> But the temple He had spoken of was His body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 12:16; John 16:4; Luke 24:7–8"> After He was raised from the dead, His disciples recalled what He had said. Then they believed the Scripture and the words that Jesus had spoken.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 7:31; Luke 8:13; John 6:14"> Now while He was in Jerusalem at the Passover Feast, many people saw the miraculous signs He was doing and believed in His name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:64; Hebrews 4:13; Acts 1:24"> But Jesus didn’t commit Himself to them, because He knew all people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 13:11; Matthew 9:4; John 6:64"> and had no need for anyone to testify about people, for He knew what was in people.</span>
