<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 3:18; Romans 16:23; 1 Corinthians 1:14"> The Elder, To the beloved Gaius, whom I sincerely love<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“sincere love” is used to translate “agape” (the strongest kind of love).">a</a></sup>in truth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Peter 1:3–9; 2 Peter 3:18; Colossians 1:4–6"> Beloved, I pray that in all ways you may prosper and be in good health, just as I know that your soul prospers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 John 1:4; 2 John 1:2; Psalms 119:11"> For I rejoiced greatly when some brothers came and testified about the truth that is in you, as indeed you do walk in the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 4:19; 2 Timothy 1:2; Proverbs 23:24"> I have no greater joy than to hear that my children are walking in the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 6:10; Luke 12:42; Luke 16:10–12"> Beloved, you are acting faithfully in whatever you accomplish for the brothers and sisters, and especially when they are strangers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:12; Titus 3:13; Acts 15:3"> They have testified to your sincere love before the church. You will do well to send them on their way in a manner worthy of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:5; Revelation 2:3; 2 Kings 5:15–16"> for they went out for the sake of the Name, accepting nothing from the heathen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:5–9; 1 Corinthians 16:10–11; 2 Corinthians 7:2–3"> Therefore we ought to support such people, that we may be fellow workers with the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Titus 1:7–16; Luke 22:24–27; Matthew 23:4–8"> I wrote to the church, but Diotrephes, who loves to be first among them, doesn’t accept what we say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 66:5; 2 Corinthians 13:2; Luke 6:22"> For this reason, if I come, I will call attention to his deeds which he does, unjustly accusing us with wicked words. Not satisfied with this, he himself doesn’t receive the brothers and sisters, either, and he forbids those who desire to do so and puts them out of the church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 1:16–17; Psalms 37:27; Ephesians 5:1"> Beloved, don’t imitate what is evil, but what is good. The one who does good belongs to God; the one who does evil hasn’t seen God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:7; John 21:24; Acts 22:12"> Demetrius has received a good testimony from everyone, and from the truth itself. We add our testimony, and you know that our testimony is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 John 1:12"> I had many things to write to you, but I would rather not write them to you with pen and ink;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 43:23; Ephesians 6:23; Romans 16:1–16"> but I hope to see you soon, and we will speak face to face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 10:3"> Peace be to you. The friends greet you. Greet the friends by name.</span>
