<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:13; 1 Timothy 6:3; Titus 2:11–14"> But say the things which fit sound doctrine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 5:23; 1 Peter 4:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:8"> that older men should be temperate, sensible, sober minded, sound in faith, in love, and in perseverance,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Titus 2:4; 1 Timothy 3:11; 1 Peter 3:3–5"> and that older women likewise be reverent in behavior, not slanderers nor enslaved to much wine, teachers of that which is good,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:14; 1 Timothy 5:2; 1 Timothy 5:11"> that they may train the young wives to love their husbands, to love their children,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 31:10–31; Colossians 3:18; 1 Timothy 5:10"> to be sober minded, chaste, workers at home, kind, being in subjection to their own husbands, that God’s word may not be blasphemed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:1; 1 Peter 5:5; Joel 2:28"> Likewise, exhort the younger men to be sober minded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 5:3; 1 Timothy 4:12; 2 Corinthians 4:2"> In all things show yourself an example of good works. In your teaching, show integrity, seriousness, incorruptibility,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; 1 Peter 2:15; 1 Peter 3:16"> and soundness of speech that can’t be condemned, that he who opposes you may be ashamed, having no evil thing to say about us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 2:18–25; 1 Timothy 6:1–2; Colossians 3:22–25"> Exhort servants to be in subjection to their own masters and to be well-pleasing in all things, not contradicting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 5:16; 1 Peter 2:12; 2 Yochanan 1:9"> not stealing, but showing all good fidelity, that they may adorn the doctrine of God, our Savior, in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 2:8; 1 Timothy 2:4; Isaiah 45:22"> For the grace of God has appeared, bringing salvation to all men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:27; Matthew 16:24; Romans 6:19"> instructing us to the intent that, denying ungodliness and worldly lusts, we would live soberly, righteously, and godly in this present age;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:8; 1 Corinthians 1:7; Matthew 16:27"> looking for the blessed hope and appearing of the glory of our great God and Savior, Yeshua the Messiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 9:14; Ephesians 2:10; 1 Peter 2:9"> who gave himself for us, that he might redeem us from all iniquity and purify for himself a people for his own possession, zealous for good works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:2; 1 Timothy 4:12–13; Titus 1:13"> Say these things and exhort and reprove with all authority. Let no one despise you.</span>
