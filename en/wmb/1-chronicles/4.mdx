<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Naarah bore him Ahuzzam, Hepher, Temeni, and Haahashtari. These were the sons of Naarah.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Hakkoz became the father of Anub, Zobebah, and the families of Aharhel the son of Harum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:21; 1 Chronicles 7:23; Genesis 35:18"> Jabez was more honorable than his brothers. His mother named him Jabez,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Jabez” sounds similar to the Hebrew word for “pain”.">a</a></sup>saying, “Because I bore him with sorrow.”</span>

May your hand be with me, and may you keep me from evil, that I may not cause pain!” God granted him that which he requested.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Chelub the brother of Shuhah became the father of Mehir, who was the father of Eshton.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Eshton became the father of Beth Rapha, Paseah, and Tehinnah the father of Ir Nahash. These are the men of Recah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 15:17; Judges 1:13; Judges 3:9–11"> The sons of Kenaz: Othniel and Seraiah. The sons of Othniel: Hathath.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Greek and Vulgate add “and Meonothai”">c</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 13:6; Numbers 14:6–10; Joshua 15:13–20"> The sons of Caleb the son of Jephunneh: Iru, Elah, and Naam. The son of Elah: Kenaz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The sons of Jehallelel: Ziph, Ziphah, Tiria, and Asarel.</span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:42; 1 Chronicles 4:39; Joshua 15:58"> His wife Yehudiyah bore Jered the father of Gedor, Heber the father of Soco, and Jekuthiel the father of Zanoah. These are the sons of Bithiah the daughter of Pharaoh, whom Mered took.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 15:44; 1 Samuel 23:1–13"> The sons of the wife of Hodiah, the sister of Naham, were the fathers of Keilah the Garmite and Eshtemoa the Maacathite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The sons of Shimon: Amnon, Rinnah, Ben Hanan, and Tilon. The sons of Ishi: Zoheth, and Ben Zoheth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 38:5; 1 Chronicles 2:3; Numbers 26:20"> The sons of Shelah the son of Judah: Er the father of Lecah, Laadah the father of Mareshah, and the families of the house of those who worked fine linen, of the house of Ashbea;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> and Jokim, and the men of Cozeba, and Joash, and Saraph, who had dominion in Moab, and Jashubilehem. These records are ancient.</span>

<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> The sons of Mishma: Hammuel his son, Zaccur his son, Shimei his son.</span>

<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Joel, Jehu the son of Joshibiah, the son of Seraiah, the son of Asiel,</span>

<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Judges 18:7–10; Psalms 105:23; Psalms 78:51"> They found rich, good pasture, and the land was wide, and quiet, and peaceful, for those who lived there before were descended from Ham.</span>
