<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Yochanan 3:18–19; 2 Corinthians 5:17; Galatians 3:13"> There is therefore now no condemnation to those who are in Messiah Yeshua, who don’t walk according to the flesh, but according to the Spirit.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="NU omits “who don’t walk according to the flesh, but according to the Spirit”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 6:14; 2 Corinthians 3:17; Romans 6:18"> For the law of the Spirit of life in Messiah Yeshua made me free from the law of sin and of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 13:39; Galatians 4:4–5; Hebrews 7:18–19"> For what the law couldn’t do, in that it was weak through the flesh, God did, sending his own Son in the likeness of sinful flesh and for sin, he condemned sin in the flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 5:16; Galatians 5:22–25; Colossians 1:22"> that the ordinance of the law might be fulfilled in us who don’t walk according to the flesh, but according to the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; Galatians 5:19–25; Yochanan 3:6"> For those who live according to the flesh set their minds on the things of the flesh, but those who live according to the Spirit, the things of the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 6:8; Romans 8:7; Romans 8:13"> For the mind of the flesh is death, but the mind of the Spirit is life and peace;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; 1 Yochanan 2:15–16; Jacob 4:4"> because the mind of the flesh is hostile toward God, for it is not subject to God’s Torah, neither indeed can it be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Yochanan 3:5–6; Hebrews 11:5–6; Romans 8:9"> Those who are in the flesh can’t please God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 4:6; 1 Corinthians 3:16; 2 Timothy 1:14"> But you are not in the flesh but in the Spirit, if it is so that the Spirit of God dwells in you. But if any man doesn’t have the Spirit of Messiah, he is not his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 3:17; Galatians 2:20; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> If Messiah is in you, the body is dead because of sin, but the spirit is alive because of righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:14; Romans 6:4–5; 2 Corinthians 4:11"> But if the Spirit of him who raised up Yeshua from the dead dwells in you, he who raised up Messiah Yeshua from the dead will also give life to your mortal bodies through his Spirit who dwells in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 6:2–15; 1 Corinthians 6:19–20; 1 Peter 4:2–3"> So then, brothers, we are debtors, not to the flesh, to live after the flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 6:8; 1 Peter 2:11; 1 Corinthians 9:27"> For if you live after the flesh, you must die; but if by the Spirit you put to death the deeds of the body, you will live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 5:18; Yochanan 1:12; Galatians 3:26"> For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, these are children of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 4:5–7; 2 Timothy 1:7; 1 Corinthians 2:12"> For you didn’t receive the spirit of bondage again to fear, but you received the Spirit of adoption, by whom we cry, “Abba!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Abba is an Aramaic word for “Father” or “Daddy”, which can be used affectionately and respectfully in prayer to our Father in heaven.">b</a></sup>Father!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Yochanan 4:13; 2 Corinthians 1:22; 2 Corinthians 5:5"> The Spirit himself testifies with our spirit that we are children of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 4:7; Galatians 3:29; Ephesians 3:6"> and if children, then heirs—heirs of God and joint heirs with Messiah, if indeed we suffer with him, that we may also be glorified with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:17–18; 1 Peter 4:13; 1 Peter 1:5–7"> For I consider that the sufferings of this present time are not worthy to be compared with the glory which will be revealed toward us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Malachi 3:17–18; Romans 8:23; 1 Yochanan 3:2"> For the creation waits with eager expectation for the children of God to be revealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 3:17–19; Genesis 5:29; Romans 8:22"> For the creation was subjected to vanity, not of its own will, but because of him who subjected it, in hope</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 22:3–5; Acts 3:21; 2 Peter 3:13"> that the creation itself also will be delivered from the bondage of decay into the liberty of the glory of the children of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:4; Jeremiah 12:11; Yochanan 16:21"> For we know that the whole creation groans and travails in pain together until now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 3:20–21; 2 Corinthians 5:2–5; Hebrews 9:28"> Not only so, but ourselves also, who have the first fruits of the Spirit, even we ourselves groan within ourselves, waiting for adoption, the redemption of our body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:7; Hebrews 11:1; 2 Corinthians 4:18"> For we were saved in hope, but hope that is seen is not hope. For who hopes for that which he sees?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 12:12; Hebrews 10:36; Psalms 27:14"> But if we hope for that which we don’t see, we wait for it with patience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 10:20; Ephesians 6:18; Galatians 4:6"> In the same way, the Spirit also helps our weaknesses, for we don’t know how to pray as we ought. But the Spirit himself makes intercession for us with groanings which can’t be uttered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:10; 1 Yochanan 5:14–15; Ephesians 2:18"> He who searches the hearts knows what is on the Spirit’s mind, because he makes intercession for the holy ones according to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; Jacob 1:12; Genesis 50:20"> We know that all things work together for good for those who love God, for those who are called according to his purpose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 1:4–5; Jeremiah 1:5; Ephesians 1:11"> For whom he foreknew, he also predestined to be conformed to the image of his Son, that he might be the firstborn among many brothers.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The word for “brothers” here and where context allows may also be correctly translated “brothers and sisters” or “siblings.”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 17:14; Romans 8:28; Ephesians 1:11"> Whom he predestined, those he also called. Whom he called, those he also justified. Whom he justified, those he also glorified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 118:6; Jeremiah 1:19; Psalms 56:11"> What then shall we say about these things? If God is for us, who can be against us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Yochanan 3:16; Romans 8:28; Psalms 84:11"> He who didn’t spare his own Son, but delivered him up for us all, how would he not also with him freely give us all things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Revelation 12:10–11; Isaiah 50:8–9; Isaiah 54:17"> Who could bring a charge against God’s chosen ones? It is God who justifies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hebrews 7:25; Romans 8:27; Hebrews 9:24"> Who is he who condemns? It is Messiah who died, yes rather, who was raised from the dead, who is at the right hand of God, who also makes intercession for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Yochanan 10:28; Psalms 103:17; Yochanan 16:33"> Who shall separate us from the love of Messiah? Could oppression, or anguish, or persecution, or famine, or nakedness, or peril, or sword?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 44:22; Isaiah 53:7; 1 Corinthians 15:30"> Even as it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“For your sake we are killed all day long.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">We were accounted as sheep for the slaughter.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:57; 1 Yochanan 4:4; 2 Corinthians 2:14"> No, in all these things we are more than conquerors through him who loved us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Yochanan 10:28; Romans 14:8; 2 Timothy 1:12"> For I am persuaded that neither death, nor life, nor angels, nor principalities, nor things present, nor things to come, nor powers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Yochanan 10:28–30; Romans 8:35; Romans 5:8"> nor height, nor depth, nor any other created thing will be able to separate us from God’s love which is in Messiah Yeshua our Lord.</span>
