<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 2:5; Romans 6:13; Hebrews 13:15–16"> Therefore I urge you, brothers, by the mercies of God, to present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable to God, which is your spiritual service.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 1:14; Ephesians 4:22–24; 2 Corinthians 5:17"> Don’t be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind, so that you may prove what is the good, well-pleasing, and perfect will of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 6:3; Philippians 2:3–8; Proverbs 26:12"> For I say through the grace that was given me, to everyone who is among you, not to think of yourself more highly than you ought to think; but to think reasonably, as God has apportioned to each person a measure of faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:27; 1 Corinthians 12:12–14; Ephesians 4:15–16"> For even as we have many members in one body, and all the members don’t have the same function,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:20; 1 Corinthians 10:17; 1 Corinthians 12:27–28"> so we, who are many, are one body in Messiah, and individually members of one another,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10–11; 1 Corinthians 7:7; 1 Corinthians 12:28–31"> having gifts differing according to the grace that was given to us: if prophecy, let’s prophesy according to the proportion of our faith;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 4:11; 2 Timothy 4:2; Acts 13:1"> or service, let’s give ourselves to service; or he who teaches, to his teaching;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; 1 Timothy 5:17; 1 Corinthians 12:28"> or he who exhorts, to his exhorting; he who gives, let him do it with generosity; he who rules, with diligence; he who shows mercy, with cheerfulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 34:14; 1 Peter 4:8; 1 Timothy 1:5"> Let love be without hypocrisy. Abhor that which is evil. Cling to that which is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 2:3; Yochanan 13:34–35; Hebrews 13:1"> In love of the brothers be tenderly affectionate to one another; in honor prefer one another,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 6:10–11; Acts 18:25; Revelation 3:15–16"> not lagging in diligence, fervent in spirit, serving the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 15:13; Hebrews 10:36; Psalms 62:8"> rejoicing in hope, enduring in troubles, continuing steadfastly in prayer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; 2 Corinthians 9:12; 1 Yochanan 3:17"> contributing to the needs of the holy ones, and given to hospitality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Matthew 5:44; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> Bless those who persecute you; bless, and don’t curse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:26; Hebrews 13:3; Psalms 35:13–14"> Rejoice with those who rejoice. Weep with those who weep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 3:8; Proverbs 3:7; Jacob 3:13–17"> Be of the same mind one toward another. Don’t set your mind on high things, but associate with the humble. Don’t be wise in your own conceits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 20:22; 1 Thessalonians 5:15; Matthew 5:39"> Repay no one evil for evil. Respect what is honorable in the sight of all men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 12:14; Matthew 5:9; Romans 14:19"> If it is possible, as much as it is up to you, be at peace with all men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:35; Hebrews 10:30; Proverbs 24:29"> Don’t seek revenge yourselves, beloved, but give place to God’s wrath. For it is written, “Vengeance belongs to me; I will repay, says the Lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 25:21–22; Matthew 5:44; Luke 6:27"> Therefore<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“If your enemy is hungry, feed him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">If he is thirsty, give him a drink;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for in doing so, you will heap coals of fire on his head.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Luke 6:27–30; Proverbs 16:32"> Don’t be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good.</span>
