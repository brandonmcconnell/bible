<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:9–10; 1 Peter 4:5; Revelation 20:12"> <span className="woj">He called him, and said to him, ‘What is this that I hear about you? Give an accounting of your management, for you can no longer be manager.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:31; Isaiah 10:3; Proverbs 18:9"> <span className="woj">“The manager said within himself, ‘What will I do, seeing that my lord is taking away the management position from me? I don’t have strength to dig. I am ashamed to beg.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 30:9; Jeremiah 4:22; Jacob 3:15"> <span className="woj">I know what I will do, so that when I am removed from management, they may receive me into their houses.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 7:41–42; Matthew 18:24"> <span className="woj">Calling each one of his lord’s debtors to him, he said to the first, ‘How much do you owe to my lord?’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 2:10; Luke 16:12; Luke 16:9"> <span className="woj">He said, ‘A hundred batos</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="100 batos is about 395 liters or 104 U. S. gallons.">a</a></sup><span className="woj">of oil.’ He said to him, ‘Take your bill, and sit down quickly and write fifty.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Song of Solomon 8:11–12; Luke 20:9; Luke 20:12"> <span className="woj">Then he said to another, ‘How much do you owe?’ He said, ‘A hundred cors</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="100 cors = about 2,110 liters or 600 bushels.">b</a></sup><span className="woj">of wheat.’ He said to him, ‘Take your bill, and write eighty.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:5; Ephesians 5:8; Proverbs 6:6–8"> <span className="woj">“His lord commended the dishonest manager because he had done wisely, for the children of this world are, in their own generation, wiser than the children of the light.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 19:21; 1 Timothy 6:17–19; Daniel 4:27"> <span className="woj">I tell you, make for yourselves friends by means of unrighteous mammon, so that when you fail, they may receive you into the eternal tents.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 16:11–12; Matthew 25:21; Luke 19:17"> <span className="woj">He who is faithful in a very little is faithful also in much. He who is dishonest in a very little is also dishonest in much.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 12:33; Jacob 2:5; Proverbs 8:18–19"> <span className="woj">If therefore you have not been faithful in the unrighteous mammon, who will commit to your trust the true riches?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 19:13–26; Matthew 25:14–29; Job 1:21"> <span className="woj">If you have not been faithful in that which is another’s, who will give you that which is your own?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Yochanan 2:15–16; Matthew 6:24; Jacob 4:4"> <span className="woj">No servant can serve two masters, for either he will hate the one and love the other; or else he will hold to one and despise the other. You aren’t able to serve God and Mammon.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Mammon” refers to riches or a false god of wealth.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 23:35; Luke 20:47; Jeremiah 6:13"> The Pharisees, who were lovers of money, also heard all these things, and they scoffed at him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:7; Proverbs 21:2; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“You are those who justify yourselves in the sight of men, but God knows your hearts. For that which is exalted among men is an abomination in the sight of God.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 11:9–14; Matthew 21:32; Acts 3:24–25"> <span className="woj">“The Torah and the Prophets were until Yochanan. From that time the Good News of God’s Kingdom is preached, and everyone is forcing his way into it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 5:18; Luke 21:33; 1 Peter 1:25"> <span className="woj">But it is easier for heaven and earth to pass away than for one tiny stroke of a pen in the Torah to become void.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 19:9; Mark 10:11–12; Matthew 5:31–32"> <span className="woj">“Everyone who divorces his wife and marries another commits adultery. He who marries one who is divorced from a husband commits adultery.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:49; Amos 6:4–6; Luke 18:24–25"> <span className="woj">“Now there was a certain rich man, and he was clothed in purple and fine linen, living in luxury every day.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 3:2; 1 Samuel 2:8; Jacob 2:5"> <span className="woj">A certain beggar, named Lazarus, was taken to his gate, full of sores,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 15:27; Mark 7:28; Yochanan 6:12"> <span className="woj">and desiring to be fed with the crumbs that fell from the rich man’s table. Yes, even the dogs came and licked his sores.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Psalms 49:16–19; Jacob 1:11"> <span className="woj">The beggar died, and he was carried away by the angels to Abraham’s bosom. The rich man also died and was buried.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Luke 16:28; Matthew 18:9"> <span className="woj">In Sheol,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="or, Hell">d</a></sup><span className="woj">he lifted up his eyes, being in torment, and saw Abraham far off, and Lazarus at his bosom.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Yochanan 4:10; Revelation 22:1; Revelation 7:16–17"> <span className="woj">He cried and said, ‘Father Abraham, have mercy on me, and send Lazarus, that he may dip the tip of his finger in water and cool my tongue! For I am in anguish in this flame.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 6:24; 1 Yochanan 2:15; Psalms 17:14"> <span className="woj">“But Abraham said, ‘Son, remember that you, in your lifetime, received your good things, and Lazarus, in the same way, bad things. But here he is now comforted and you are in anguish.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Yochanan 3:36; Psalms 49:14; Psalms 50:22"> <span className="woj">Besides all this, between us and you there is a great gulf fixed, that those who want to pass from here to you are not able, and that no one may cross over from there to us.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> <span className="woj">“He said, ‘I ask you therefore, father, that you would send him to my father’s house—</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 2:40; Psalms 49:12–13; 1 Thessalonians 4:6"> <span className="woj">for I have five brothers—that he may testify to them, so they won’t also come into this place of torment.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 15:21; Luke 24:27; Luke 16:16"> <span className="woj">“But Abraham said to him, ‘They have Moses and the Prophets. Let them listen to them.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 16:9–11; Luke 16:24; Luke 13:5"> <span className="woj">“He said, ‘No, father Abraham, but if one goes to them from the dead, they will repent.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:3; Acts 19:8; Yochanan 11:43–53"> <span className="woj">“He said to him, ‘If they don’t listen to Moses and the Prophets, neither will they be persuaded if one rises from the dead.’”</span></span>
