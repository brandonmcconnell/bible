<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 6:6–15; Ecclesiastes 5:2; Psalms 72:18–19"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“When you pray, say,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1 woj">‘Our Father in heaven,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2 woj">may your name be kept holy.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1 woj">May your Kingdom come.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2 woj">May your will be done on earth, as it is in heaven.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 6:11; Matthew 6:34; Proverbs 30:8"> <span className="pl pl-1 woj">Give us day by day our daily bread.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 6:12–15; Matthew 26:41; Isaiah 43:25–26"> <span className="pl pl-1 woj">Forgive us our sins,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2 woj">for we ourselves also forgive everyone who is indebted to us.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1 woj">Bring us not into temptation,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2 woj">but deliver us from the evil one.’”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 18:1–8"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“Which of you, if you go to a friend at midnight and tell him, ‘Friend, lend me three loaves of bread,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> <span className="woj">for a friend of mine has come to me from a journey, and I have nothing to set before him,’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 13:25; Galatians 6:17; Luke 7:6"> <span className="woj">and he from within will answer and say, ‘Don’t bother me. The door is now shut, and my children are with me in bed. I can’t get up and give it to you’?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 32:26; Colossians 4:12; Romans 15:30"> <span className="woj">I tell you, although he will not rise and give it to him because he is his friend, yet because of his persistence, he will get up and give him as many as he needs.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 11:24; Yochanan 16:23–24; Matthew 21:22"> <span className="woj">“I tell you, keep asking, and it will be given you. Keep seeking, and you will find. Keep knocking, and it will be opened to you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jacob 4:3; Lamentations 3:54–58; Psalms 31:22"> <span className="woj">For everyone who asks receives. He who seeks finds. To him who knocks it will be opened.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 7:9; Isaiah 49:15"> <span className="woj">“Which of you fathers, if your son asks for bread, will give him a stone? Or if he asks for a fish, he won’t give him a snake instead of a fish, will he?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 9:10; Luke 10:19; Ezekiel 2:6"> <span className="woj">Or if he asks for an egg, he won’t give him a scorpion, will he?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 7:11; Isaiah 44:3–4; Romans 8:32"> <span className="woj">If you then, being evil, know how to give good gifts to your children, how much more will your heavenly Father give the Holy Spirit to those who ask him?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 12:22–24; Matthew 9:32–34; Mark 7:32–37"> He was casting out a demon, and it was mute. When the demon had gone out, the mute man spoke; and the multitudes marveled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 9:34; Matthew 12:24–30; Yochanan 8:48"> But some of them said, “He casts out demons by Beelzebul, the prince of the demons.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Yochanan 6:30; 1 Corinthians 1:22; Mark 8:11–12"> Others, testing him, sought from him a sign from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 3:23–27; Matthew 12:25–29; Isaiah 19:2–3"> But he, knowing their thoughts, said to them,<span className="woj">“Every kingdom divided against itself is brought to desolation. A house divided against itself falls.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 12:26; Matthew 12:31–34; Luke 11:15"> <span className="woj">If Satan also is divided against himself, how will his kingdom stand? For you say that I cast out demons by Beelzebul.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 9:49; Job 15:6; Romans 3:19"> <span className="woj">But if I cast out demons by Beelzebul, by whom do your children cast them out? Therefore they will be your judges.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 8:19; Matthew 12:28; Luke 10:9"> <span className="woj">But if I by God’s finger cast out demons, then God’s Kingdom has come to you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 3:27; Matthew 12:29"> <span className="woj">“When the strong man, fully armed, guards his own dwelling, his goods are safe.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 49:24–25; Isaiah 53:12; 1 Yochanan 3:8"> <span className="woj">But when someone stronger attacks him and overcomes him, he takes from him his whole armor in which he trusted, and divides his plunder.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 12:30; Luke 9:50; Mark 9:40"> <span className="woj">“He who is not with me is against me. He who doesn’t gather with me scatters.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 12:43–45; 1 Peter 5:8; Job 2:2"> <span className="woj">The unclean spirit, when he has gone out of the man, passes through dry places, seeking rest; and finding none, he says, ‘I will turn back to my house from which I came out.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:9–12; 2 Peter 2:10–19; Judah 1:8–13"> <span className="woj">When he returns, he finds it swept and put in order.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Yochanan 5:14; Matthew 12:45; 2 Peter 2:20–22"> <span className="woj">Then he goes and takes seven other spirits more evil than himself, and they enter in and dwell there. The last state of that man becomes worse than the first.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 1:42; Luke 1:48; Luke 23:29"> It came to pass, as he said these things, a certain woman out of the multitude lifted up her voice and said to him, “Blessed is the womb that bore you, and the breasts which nursed you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 6:47–48; Luke 8:21; Psalms 112:1"> But he said,<span className="woj">“On the contrary, blessed are those who hear the word of God, and keep it.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 11:16; Mark 8:11–12; 1 Corinthians 1:22"> When the multitudes were gathering together to him, he began to say,<span className="woj">“This is an evil generation. It seeks after a sign. No sign will be given to it but the sign of Jonah the prophet.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 12:40–42; Jonah 1:17; Jonah 3:2–10"> <span className="woj">For even as Jonah became a sign to the Ninevites, so the Son of Man will also be to this generation.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:1; 1 Kings 10:1–13; Romans 2:27"> <span className="woj">The Queen of the South will rise up in the judgment with the men of this generation and will condemn them, for she came from the ends of the earth to hear the wisdom of Solomon; and behold, one greater than Solomon is here.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 7:26; Luke 11:31; Jonah 3:5–4"> <span className="woj">The men of Nineveh will stand up in the judgment with this generation, and will condemn it, for they repented at the proclaiming of Jonah; and behold, one greater than Jonah is here.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 8:16–17; Mark 4:21–22; Matthew 5:15–16"> <span className="woj">“No one, when he has lit a lamp, puts it in a cellar or under a basket, but on a stand, that those who come in may see the light.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 6:22–23; Acts 26:18; Jeremiah 5:21"> <span className="woj">The lamp of the body is the eye. Therefore when your eye is good, your whole body is also full of light; but when it is evil, your body also is full of darkness.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 16:25; 2 Peter 1:9; Jacob 3:13–17"> <span className="woj">Therefore see whether the light that is in you isn’t darkness.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 20:27; Proverbs 6:23; Proverbs 4:18–19"> <span className="woj">If therefore your whole body is full of light, having no part dark, it will be wholly full of light, as when the lamp with its bright shining gives you light.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 7:36; 1 Corinthians 9:19–23; Luke 14:1"> Now as he spoke, a certain Pharisee asked him to dine with him. He went in and sat at the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 15:2–3; Mark 7:2–5; Yochanan 3:25"> When the Pharisee saw it, he marveled that he had not first washed himself before dinner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Proverbs 30:12; Genesis 6:5"> The Lord said to him,<span className="woj">“Now you Pharisees cleanse the outside of the cup and of the platter, but your inward part is full of extortion and wickedness.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 12:20; 1 Corinthians 15:36; Matthew 23:26"> <span className="woj">You foolish ones, didn’t he who made the outside make the inside also?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 12:33; Jacob 1:27; Titus 1:15"> <span className="woj">But give for gifts to the needy those things which are within, and behold, all things will be clean to you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 23:23; Luke 18:12; Proverbs 21:3"> <span className="woj">But woe to you Pharisees! For you tithe mint and rue and every herb, but you bypass justice and God’s love. You ought to have done these, and not to have left the other undone.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 20:46; Mark 12:38–39; Luke 14:7–11"> <span className="woj">Woe to you Pharisees! For you love the best seats in the synagogues and the greetings in the marketplaces.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 23:27–28; Psalms 5:9; Acts 23:3"> <span className="woj">Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you are like hidden graves, and the men who walk over them don’t know it.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Yochanan 9:40; Matthew 22:35; Jeremiah 6:10"> One of the Torah scholars answered him, “Rabbi, in saying this you insult us also.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 11:52; Isaiah 10:1; Matthew 23:2–4"> He said,<span className="woj">“Woe to you Torah scholars also! For you load men with burdens that are difficult to carry, and you yourselves won’t even lift one finger to help carry those burdens.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:15; Acts 7:51; Matthew 23:29–33"> <span className="woj">Woe to you! For you build the tombs of the prophets, and your fathers killed them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Jacob 5:10; Matthew 23:31; 2 Chronicles 36:16"> <span className="woj">So you testify and consent to the works of your fathers. For they killed them, and you build their tombs.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:30; Colossians 2:3; Ephesians 4:11"> <span className="woj">Therefore also the wisdom of God said, ‘I will send to them prophets and emissaries; and some of them they will kill and persecute,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Genesis 9:5–6; Revelation 18:20–24; 2 Kings 24:4"> <span className="woj">that the blood of all the prophets, which was shed from the foundation of the world, may be required of this generation,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:20–22; Matthew 23:35; Hebrews 11:4"> <span className="woj">from the blood of Abel to the blood of Zechariah, who perished between the altar and the sanctuary.’ Yes, I tell you, it will be required of this generation.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 23:13; Malachi 2:7; Luke 11:45–46"> <span className="woj">Woe to you Torah scholars! For you took away the key of knowledge. You didn’t enter in yourselves, and those who were entering in, you hindered.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:5; Jeremiah 20:10; Luke 20:20"> As he said these things to them, the scribes and the Pharisees began to be terribly angry, and to draw many things out of him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Mark 12:13; Matthew 22:15; Matthew 22:35"> lying in wait for him, and seeking to catch him in something he might say, that they might accuse him.</span>
