<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 3:2; Luke 20:20; Psalms 62:4"> When he went into the house of one of the rulers of the Pharisees on a Sabbath to eat bread, they were watching him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Behold, a certain man who had dropsy was in front of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 12:10; Mark 3:4; Luke 13:14–16"> Yeshua, answering, spoke to the Torah scholars and Pharisees, saying,<span className="woj">“Is it lawful to heal on the Sabbath?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 22:46; Matthew 21:25–27"> But they were silent. He took him, and healed him, and let him go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 13:15; Exodus 23:4–5; Matthew 12:11–12"> He answered them,<span className="woj">“Which of you, if your son</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="TR reads “donkey” instead of “son”">a</a></sup><span className="woj">or an ox fell into a well, wouldn’t immediately pull him out on a Sabbath day?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 20:40; Luke 21:15; Acts 6:10"> They couldn’t answer him regarding these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Philippians 2:3; Luke 11:43; Matthew 23:6"> He spoke a parable to those who were invited, when he noticed how they chose the best seats, and said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 25:6–7"> <span className="woj">“When you are invited by anyone to a wedding feast, don’t sit in the best seat, since perhaps someone more honorable than you might be invited by him,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 16:18; Proverbs 11:2; Proverbs 3:35"> <span className="woj">and he who invited both of you would come and tell you, ‘Make room for this person.’ Then you would begin, with shame, to take the lowest place.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 25:6–7; Proverbs 15:33; 1 Samuel 15:17"> <span className="woj">But when you are invited, go and sit in the lowest place, so that when he who invited you comes, he may tell you, ‘Friend, move up higher.’ Then you will be honored in the presence of all who sit at the table with you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 18:14; Matthew 23:12; Proverbs 29:23"> <span className="woj">For everyone who exalts himself will be humbled, and whoever humbles himself will be exalted.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 5:46; Jacob 2:1–6; Luke 6:32–36"> He also said to the one who had invited him,<span className="woj">“When you make a dinner or a supper, don’t call your friends, nor your brothers, nor your kinsmen, nor rich neighbors, or perhaps they might also return the favor, and pay you back.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 14:21; Isaiah 58:10; Proverbs 14:31"> <span className="woj">But when you make a feast, ask the poor, the maimed, the lame, or the blind;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Acts 24:15; Yochanan 5:29"> <span className="woj">and you will be blessed, because they don’t have the resources to repay you. For you will be repaid in the resurrection of the righteous.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 19:9; Luke 22:30; Luke 13:29"> When one of those who sat at the table with him heard these things, he said to him, “Blessed is he who will feast in God’s Kingdom!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 22:2–14; Revelation 22:17; Isaiah 55:1–7"> But he said to him,<span className="woj">“A certain man made a great supper, and he invited many people.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 11:27–29; Matthew 10:1–4; Matthew 22:3–4"> <span className="woj">He sent out his servant at supper time to tell those who were invited, ‘Come, for everything is ready now.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Luke 17:26–31; Luke 18:24"> <span className="woj">They all as one began to make excuses.</span><span className="woj">“The first said to him, ‘I have bought a field, and I must go and see it. Please have me excused.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> <span className="woj">“Another said, ‘I have bought five yoke of oxen, and I must go try them out. Please have me excused.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:5; 1 Corinthians 7:33; Luke 14:26–28"> <span className="woj">“Another said, ‘I have married a wife, and therefore I can’t come.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 14:13; Matthew 11:5; Luke 14:24"> <span className="woj">“That servant came, and told his lord these things. Then the master of the house, being angry, said to his servant, ‘Go out quickly into the streets and lanes of the city, and bring in the poor, maimed, blind, and lame.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Yochanan 14:2; Ephesians 3:8; 1 Timothy 2:5–6"> <span className="woj">“The servant said, ‘Lord, it is done as you commanded, and there is still room.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 22:9–10; Colossians 1:28; Acts 13:47–48"> <span className="woj">“The lord said to the servant, ‘Go out into the highways and hedges, and compel them to come in, that my house may be filled.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 21:43; Acts 13:46; Yochanan 8:24"> <span className="woj">For I tell you that none of those men who were invited will taste of my supper. For many are called, but few are chosen.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="RP MT includes the last sentence. TR, NU, and FH MT omit: For many are called, but few are chosen.">b</a></sup><span className="woj">’”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Yochanan 6:24–27; Luke 12:1"> Now great multitudes were going with him. He turned and said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; Yochanan 12:25; Philippians 3:8"> <span className="woj">“If anyone comes to me, and doesn’t disregard</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="or, hate">c</a></sup><span className="woj">his own father, mother, wife, children, brothers, and sisters, yes, and his own life also, he can’t be my disciple.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 10:38; Luke 9:23–25; Mark 8:34–37"> <span className="woj">Whoever doesn’t bear his own cross and come after me, can’t be my disciple.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 24:27; Matthew 10:22; Joshua 24:19–24"> <span className="woj">For which of you, desiring to build a tower, doesn’t first sit down and count the cost, to see if he has enough to complete it?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> <span className="woj">Or perhaps, when he has laid a foundation and isn’t able to finish, everyone who sees begins to mock him,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 6:11; 2 Yochanan 1:8; 1 Corinthians 3:11–14"> <span className="woj">saying, ‘This man began to build and wasn’t able to finish.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 20:18; Proverbs 25:8; 2 Kings 18:20–22"> <span className="woj">Or what king, as he goes to encounter another king in war, will not sit down first and consider whether he is able with ten thousand to meet him who comes against him with twenty thousand?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 12:20; Jacob 4:6–10; Luke 12:58"> <span className="woj">Or else, while the other is yet a great way off, he sends an envoy and asks for conditions of peace.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Philippians 3:7–8; 1 Yochanan 2:15–16; Luke 5:28"> <span className="woj">So therefore, whoever of you who doesn’t renounce all that he has, he can’t be my disciple.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 5:13; Colossians 4:6; Mark 9:49–50"> <span className="woj">“Salt is good, but if the salt becomes flat and tasteless, with what do you season it?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 11:15; Luke 9:44; Revelation 2:11"> <span className="woj">It is fit neither for the soil nor for the manure pile. It is thrown out. He who has ears to hear, let him hear.”</span></span>
