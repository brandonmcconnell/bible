<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:4–11; 1 Chronicles 6:15; Jeremiah 34:1–6"> In the ninth year of his reign, in the tenth month, in the tenth day of the month, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came, he and all his army, against Jerusalem, and encamped against it; and they built forts against it around it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> So the city was besieged until the eleventh year of King Zedekiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:6; Ezekiel 5:12; Jeremiah 38:2"> On the ninth day of the fourth month, the famine was severe in the city, so that there was no bread for the people of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:21; 2 Kings 25:5; Jeremiah 39:2–7"> Then a breach was made in the city, and all the men of war fled by night by the way of the gate between the two walls, which was by the king’s garden (now the Kasdim were against the city around it); and the king went by the way of the Arabah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 30:16; Jeremiah 39:5; Amos 2:14–16"> But the Chaldean army pursued the king, and overtook him in the plains of Jericho; and all his army was scattered from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 23:33; Jeremiah 34:21–22; Jeremiah 21:7"> Then they captured the king and carried him up to the king of Babylon to Riblah; and they passed judgment on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:4–5; Judges 16:21; Jeremiah 39:6–7"> They killed Zedekiah’s sons before his eyes, then put out Zedekiah’s eyes, bound him in fetters, and carried him to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:12–16; Jeremiah 40:1–4; Lamentations 4:12"> Now in the fifth month, on the seventh day of the month, which was the nineteenth year of King Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard, a servant of the king of Babylon, came to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 2:5; Psalms 74:3–7; 1 Kings 9:8"> He burned the LORD’s house, the king’s house, and all the houses of Jerusalem. He burned every great house with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:3; Jeremiah 39:8; Jeremiah 52:14–23"> All the army of the Kasdim, who were with the captain of the guard, broke down the walls around Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:2; Jeremiah 15:1–2; Jeremiah 39:9"> Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard carried away captive the rest of the people who were left in the city and those who had deserted to the king of Babylon—all the rest of the multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:7; 2 Kings 24:14; Ezekiel 33:24"> But the captain of the guard left some of the poorest of the land to work the vineyards and fields.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:18; Lamentations 1:10; 2 Kings 20:17"> The Kasdim broke up the pillars of bronze that were in the LORD’s house and the bases and the bronze sea that were in the LORD’s house, and carried the bronze pieces to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:3; 1 Kings 7:47–50; Exodus 38:3"> They took away the pots, the shovels, the snuffers, the spoons, and all the vessels of bronze with which they ministered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 5:2–3; Numbers 7:13–14; 1 Kings 7:48–51"> The captain of the guard took away the fire pans, the basins, that which was of gold, for gold, and that which was of silver, for silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 7:47"> The two pillars, the one sea, and the bases, which Solomon had made for the LORD’s house, the bronze of all these vessels was not weighed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 7:15–22; Jeremiah 52:21–23"> The height of the one pillar was eighteen cubits,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A cubit is the length from the tip of the middle finger to the elbow on a man’s arm, or about 18 inches or 46 centimeters.">a</a></sup>and a capital of bronze was on it. The height of the capital was three cubits, with network and pomegranates on the capital around it, all of bronze; and the second pillar with its network was like these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:25; Ezra 7:1; Jeremiah 21:1"> The captain of the guard took Seraiah the chief priest, Zephaniah the second priest, and the three keepers of the threshold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esther 1:14"> and out of the city he took an officer who was set over the men of war; and five men of those who saw the king’s face, who were found in the city; and the scribe, the captain of the army, who mustered the people of the land, and sixty men of the people of the land who were found in the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Lamentations 4:16; Jeremiah 52:26–27; 2 Kings 23:33"> Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard took them, and brought them to the king of Babylon to Riblah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:64; 2 Kings 23:27; Deuteronomy 4:26"> The king of Babylon attacked them and put them to death at Riblah in the land of Hamath. So Judah was carried away captive out of his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:14; 2 Kings 22:12; Jeremiah 41:2"> As for the people who were left in the land of Judah whom Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon had left, even over them he made Gedaliah the son of Ahikam, the son of Shaphan, governor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:7–9; Jeremiah 40:11–12; Joshua 18:26"> Now when all the captains of the forces, they and their men, heard that the king of Babylon had made Gedaliah governor, they came to Gedaliah to Mizpah, even Ishmael the son of Nethaniah, Yochanan the son of Kareah, Seraiah the son of Tanhumeth the Netophathite, and Jaazaniah the son of the Maacathite, they and their men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:9–10; Jeremiah 43:6; 2 Samuel 19:23"> Gedaliah swore to them and to their men, and said to them, “Don’t be afraid because of the servants of the Kasdim. Dwell in the land and serve the king of Babylon, and it will be well with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Zechariah 7:5; Zechariah 8:19; Jeremiah 40:15"> But in the seventh month, Ishmael the son of Nethaniah, the son of Elishama, of the royal offspring came, and ten men with him, and struck Gedaliah so that he died, with the Jews and the Kasdim that were with him at Mizpah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:4–7; Jeremiah 41:16–18; Jeremiah 42:14–22"> All the people, both small and great, and the captains of the forces arose and came to Egypt; for they were afraid of the Kasdim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:31–34; Genesis 40:20; Genesis 40:13"> In the thirty-seventh year of the captivity of Jehoiachin king of Judah, in the twelfth month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, Evilmerodach king of Babylon, in the year that he began to reign, released Jehoiachin king of Judah out of prison,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Daniel 2:37; Jeremiah 27:6–11; Daniel 5:18–19"> and he spoke kindly to him and set his throne above the throne of the kings who were with him in Babylon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:7; Isaiah 61:3; Genesis 41:42"> and changed his prison garments. Jehoiachin ate bread before him continually all the days of his life;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 11:3; Matthew 6:11; Nehemiah 11:23"> and for his allowance, there was a continual allowance given him from the king, every day a portion, all the days of his life.</span>
