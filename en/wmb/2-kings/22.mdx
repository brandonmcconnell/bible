<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 15:39; 2 Chronicles 34:1–33; Jeremiah 1:2"> Josiah was eight years old when he began to reign, and he reigned thirty-one years in Jerusalem. His mother’s name was Jedidah the daughter of Adaiah of Bozkath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:32; Joshua 1:7; 2 Kings 18:3"> He did that which was right in the LORD’s eyes, and walked in all the ways of David his father, and didn’t turn away to the right hand or to the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:3–33"> In the eighteenth year of King Josiah, the king sent Shaphan, the son of Azaliah the son of Meshullam, the scribe, to the LORD’s house, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 12:4; Mark 12:41–42; 2 Chronicles 34:9–18"> “Go up to Hilkiah the high priest, that he may count the money which is brought into the LORD’s house, which the keepers of the threshold have gathered of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 12:11–14; 2 Kings 12:5; 2 Chronicles 24:12–13"> Let them deliver it into the hand of the workers who have the oversight of the LORD’s house; and let them give it to the workers who are in the LORD’s house, to repair the damage to the house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> to the carpenters, and to the builders, and to the masons, and for buying timber and cut stone to repair the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 12:15; 1 Corinthians 4:2; Nehemiah 7:2"> However, no accounting shall be asked of them for the money delivered into their hand, for they deal faithfully.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:24–26; 2 Chronicles 34:14–28"> Hilkiah the high priest said to Shaphan the scribe, “I have found the scroll of the Torah in the LORD’s house.” Hilkiah delivered the book to Shaphan, and he read it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:11; 2 Kings 25:22; 2 Kings 18:18"> Shaphan the scribe came to the king, and brought the king word again, and said, “Your servants have emptied out the money that was found in the house, and have delivered it into the hands of the workmen who have the oversight of the LORD’s house.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:21; Jeremiah 13:18; Deuteronomy 17:18–20"> Shaphan the scribe told the king, saying, “Hilkiah the priest has delivered a book to me.” Then Shaphan read it before the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:19; Joel 2:13; 2 Kings 22:19"> When the king had heard the words of the scroll of the Torah, he tore his clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:24; 2 Kings 25:22; 2 Kings 19:2–3"> The king commanded Hilkiah the priest, Ahikam the son of Shaphan, Achbor the son of Micaiah, Shaphan the scribe, and Asaiah the king’s servant, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:23–28; Deuteronomy 31:17–18; Jeremiah 37:17"> “Go inquire of the LORD for me, and for the people, and for all Judah, concerning the words of this book that is found; for great is the LORD’s wrath that is kindled against us, because our fathers have not listened to the words of this book, to do according to all that which is written concerning us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 4:4; 2 Chronicles 34:22; Micah 6:4"> So Hilkiah the priest, Ahikam, Achbor, Shaphan, and Asaiah went to Huldah the prophetess, the wife of Shallum the son of Tikvah, the son of Harhas, keeper of the wardrobe (now she lived in Jerusalem in the second quarter); and they talked with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 1:6; 2 Kings 1:16; Jeremiah 23:28"> She said to them, “The LORD the God of Israel says, ‘Tell the man who sent you to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 9:11–14; Joshua 23:15; Deuteronomy 29:27"> “The LORD says, ‘Behold, I will bring evil on this place and on its inhabitants, even all the words of the book which the king of Judah has read.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 21:22; Jeremiah 7:20; Deuteronomy 29:24–28"> Because they have forsaken me and have burned incense to other gods, that they might provoke me to anger with all the work of their hands, therefore my wrath shall be kindled against this place, and it will not be quenched.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:26–28; Malachi 3:16–17; Isaiah 3:10"> But to the king of Judah, who sent you to inquire of the LORD, tell him, “The LORD the God of Israel says, ‘Concerning the words which you have heard,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 51:17; 1 Kings 21:29; Isaiah 57:15"> because your heart was tender, and you humbled yourself before the LORD when you heard what I spoke against this place and against its inhabitants, that they should become a desolation and a curse, and have torn your clothes and wept before me, I also have heard you,’ says the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 37:37; Isaiah 57:1–2; 1 Chronicles 17:11"> ‘Therefore behold, I will gather you to your fathers, and you will be gathered to your grave in peace. Your eyes will not see all the evil which I will bring on this place.’”’” So they brought this message back to the king.</span>
