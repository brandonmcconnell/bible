<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 4:27; Romans 15:18; Proverbs 21:31"> Now Naaman, captain of the army of the king of Syria, was a great man with his master, and honorable, because by him the LORD had given victory to Syria; he was also a mighty man of valor, but he was a leper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 6:23; 2 Kings 13:20; 1 Samuel 13:17–18"> The Syrians had gone out in bands, and had brought away captive out of Eretz-Israel a little girl, and she waited on Naaman’s wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 26:29; Luke 17:12–14; Numbers 11:29"> She said to her mistress, “I wish that my lord were with the prophet who is in Samaria! Then he would heal him of his leprosy.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:26–27; Yochanan 4:28–29; Yochanan 1:42–46"> Someone went in and told his lord, saying, “The girl who is from Eretz-Israel said this.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 14:12; 2 Kings 8:8–9; Numbers 22:17–18"> The king of Syria said, “Go now, and I will send a letter to the king of Israel.”<br />He departed, and took with him ten talents<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A talent is about 30 kilograms or 66 pounds">a</a></sup>of silver, six thousand pieces of gold, and ten changes of clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> He brought the letter to the king of Israel, saying, “Now when this letter has come to you, behold, I have sent Naaman my servant to you, that you may heal him of his leprosy.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:6; 1 Kings 20:7; Genesis 30:2"> When the king of Israel had read the letter, he tore his clothes and said, “Am I God, to kill and to make alive, that this man sends to me to heal a man of his leprosy? But please consider and see how he seeks a quarrel against me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 18:36–37; Hosea 12:13; Ezekiel 2:5"> It was so, when Elisha the man of God heard that the king of Israel had torn his clothes, that he sent to the king, saying, “Why have you torn your clothes? Let him come now to me, and he shall know that there is a prophet in Israel.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 16:37–39; 2 Kings 6:32; Isaiah 60:14"> So Naaman came with his horses and with his chariots, and stood at the door of the house of Elisha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Yochanan 9:7; 1 Corinthians 6:11; Leviticus 14:7"> Elisha sent a messenger to him, saying, “Go and wash in the Jordan seven times, and your flesh shall come again to you, and you shall be clean.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 15:27; Luke 14:11; Matthew 8:8"> But Naaman was angry, and went away and said, “Behold, I thought, ‘He will surely come out to me, and stand, and call on the name of the LORD his God, and wave his hand over the place, and heal the leper.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 1:9; 2 Kings 5:17; 2 Kings 2:14"> Aren’t Abanah and Pharpar, the rivers of Damascus, better than all the waters of Israel? Couldn’t I wash in them and be clean?” So he turned and went away in a rage.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 6:21; 2 Kings 13:14; 2 Kings 2:12"> His servants came near and spoke to him, and said, “My father, if the prophet had asked you do some great thing, wouldn’t you have done it? How much rather then, when he says to you, ‘Wash, and be clean’?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 33:25; Luke 4:27; Luke 5:13"> Then went he down and dipped himself seven times in the Jordan, according to the saying of the man of God; and his flesh was restored like the flesh of a little child, and he was clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 2:47; Daniel 6:26–27; Joshua 2:9–11"> He returned to the man of God, he and all his company, and came, and stood before him; and he said, “See now, I know that there is no God in all the earth, but in Israel. Now therefore, please take a gift from your servant.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 5:26; 2 Kings 5:20; 2 Kings 3:14"> But he said, “As the LORD lives, before whom I stand, I will receive none.”<br />He urged him to take it; but he refused.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 26:18; 2 Kings 5:12; Romans 14:1"> Naaman said, “If not, then, please let two mules’ load of earth be given to your servant; for your servant will from now on offer neither burnt offering nor sacrifice to other gods, but to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 7:2; 2 Kings 7:17; 2 Kings 17:35"> In this thing may the LORD pardon your servant: when my master goes into the house of Rimmon to worship there, and he leans on my hand, and I bow myself in the house of Rimmon. When I bow myself in the house of Rimmon, may the LORD pardon your servant in this thing.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:17; Mark 5:34; Exodus 4:18"> He said to him, “Go in peace.”<br />So he departed from him a little way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 4:12; 2 Kings 4:31; 2 Kings 6:31"> But Gehazi the servant of Elisha the man of God, said, “Behold, my master has spared this Naaman the Syrian, in not receiving at his hands that which he brought. As the LORD lives, I will run after him, and take something from him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 7:6–7; 2 Kings 9:17–22; 2 Kings 4:26"> So Gehazi followed after Naaman. When Naaman saw one running after him, he came down from the chariot to meet him, and said, “Is all well?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Kings 5:5; Isaiah 59:3; Acts 5:3–4"> He said, “All is well. My master has sent me, saying, ‘Behold, even now two young men of the sons of the prophets have come to me from the hill country of Ephraim. Please give them a talent<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A talent is about 30 kilograms or 66 pounds">b</a></sup>of silver and two changes of clothing.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 12:10; 1 Kings 20:7; 2 Kings 2:17"> Naaman said, “Be pleased to take two talents.” He urged him, and bound two talents of silver in two bags, with two changes of clothing, and laid them on two of his servants; and they carried them before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 21:16; Joshua 7:11–12; Joshua 7:1"> When he came to the hill, he took them from their hand, and stored them in the house. Then he let the men go, and they departed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 5:22; Genesis 16:8; Proverbs 30:20"> But he went in, and stood before his master. Elisha said to him, “Where did you come from, Gehazi?”<br />He said, “Your servant went nowhere.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 2:5; 1 Corinthians 5:3; 2 Kings 6:12"> He said to him, “Didn’t my heart go with you when the man turned from his chariot to meet you? Is it a time to receive money, and to receive garments, and olive groves and vineyards, and sheep and cattle, and male servants and female servants?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 4:6; Numbers 12:10; 2 Kings 15:5"> Therefore the leprosy of Naaman will cling to you and to your offspring<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="or, seed">c</a></sup>forever.”<br />He went out from his presence a leper, as white as snow.</span>
</div>
