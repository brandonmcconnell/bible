<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 12:32–33; 2 Kings 23:17; 1 Thessalonians 4:15"> Behold, a man of God came out of Judah by the LORD’s word to Bethel; and Jeroboam was standing by the altar to burn incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:1; 2 Kings 23:15–18; 2 Kings 22:1–2"> He cried against the altar by the LORD’s word, and said, “Altar! Altar! The LORD says: ‘Behold, a son will be born to David’s house, Josiah by name. On you he will sacrifice the priests of the high places who burn incense on you, and they will burn men’s bones on you.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:22; Exodus 4:3–5; Judges 6:17"> He gave a sign the same day, saying, “This is the sign which the LORD has spoken: Behold, the altar will be split apart, and the ashes that are on it will be poured out.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 11:5; 2 Chronicles 18:25–27; Luke 6:10"> When the king heard the saying of the man of God, which he cried against the altar in Bethel, Jeroboam put out his hand from the altar, saying, “Seize him!” His hand, which he put out against him, dried up, so that he could not draw it back again to himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 13:3; Jeremiah 28:16–17; Deuteronomy 18:22"> The altar was also split apart, and the ashes poured out from the altar, according to the sign which the man of God had given by the LORD’s word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 9:28; Acts 8:24; Exodus 10:17"> The king answered the man of God, “Now intercede for the favor of the LORD your God, and pray for me, that my hand may be restored me again.” The man of God interceded with the LORD, and the king’s hand was restored to him again, and became as it was before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 5:15; 1 Samuel 9:7–8; Judges 13:15"> The king said to the man of God, “Come home with me and refresh yourself, and I will give you a reward.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 24:13; Numbers 22:18; Mark 6:23"> The man of God said to the king, “Even if you gave me half of your house, I would not go in with you, neither would I eat bread nor drink water in this place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Yochanan 13:17; Deuteronomy 13:13–18; Romans 16:17"> for so was it commanded me by the LORD’s word, saying, ‘You shall eat no bread, drink no water, and don’t return by the way that you came.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> So he went another way, and didn’t return by the way that he came to Bethel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 23:18; 1 Kings 13:20–21; Numbers 24:2"> Now an old prophet lived in Bethel, and one of his sons came and told him all the works that the man of God had done that day in Bethel. They also told their father the words which he had spoken to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Their father said to them, “Which way did he go?” Now his sons had seen which way the man of God went, who came from Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 10:4; 2 Samuel 19:26; Judges 5:10"> He said to his sons, “Saddle the donkey for me.” So they saddled the donkey for him; and he rode on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Philippians 4:12–13; 2 Corinthians 11:27; 1 Corinthians 4:11–12"> He went after the man of God, and found him sitting under an oak. He said to him, “Are you the man of God who came from Judah?” He said, “I am.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then he said to him, “Come home with me and eat bread.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 13:8–9; Matthew 16:23; Numbers 22:19"> He said, “I may not return with you, nor go in with you. I will not eat bread or drink water with you in this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 20:35; 1 Thessalonians 4:15; 1 Kings 13:1"> For it was said to me by the LORD’s word, ‘You shall eat no bread or drink water there, and don’t turn again to go by the way that you came.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Yochanan 4:1; Matthew 7:15; Numbers 22:35"> He said to him, “I also am a prophet as you are; and an angel spoke to me by the LORD’s word, saying, ‘Bring him back with you into your house, that he may eat bread and drink water.’” He lied to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:1; Genesis 3:6; Acts 4:19"> So he went back with him, ate bread in his house, and drank water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 7:22; Numbers 24:4; Numbers 24:16–24"> As they sat at the table, the LORD’s word came to the prophet who brought him back;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 13:17; 1 Samuel 13:13–14; 1 Samuel 4:18"> and he cried out to the man of God who came from Judah, saying, “The LORD says, ‘Because you have been disobedient to the LORD’s word, and have not kept the commandment which the LORD your God commanded you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 14:18–20; 1 Kings 14:13; Jeremiah 22:18–19"> but came back, and have eaten bread and drank water in the place of which he said to you, “Eat no bread, and drink no water,” your body will not come to the tomb of your fathers.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> After he had eaten bread and after he drank, he saddled the donkey for the prophet whom he had brought back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 20:36; 2 Kings 2:24; Proverbs 26:13"> When he had gone, a lion met him by the way and killed him. His body was thrown on the path, and the donkey stood by it. The lion also stood by the body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 13:11"> Behold, men passed by and saw the body thrown on the path, and the lion standing by the body; and they came and told it in the city where the old prophet lived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:6; Proverbs 11:31; Psalms 119:120"> When the prophet who brought him back from the way heard of it, he said, “It is the man of God who was disobedient to the LORD’s word. Therefore the LORD has delivered him to the lion, which has mauled him and slain him, according to the LORD’s word which he spoke to him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> He said to his sons, saying, “Saddle the donkey for me,” and they saddled it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Kings 17:6; Psalms 148:7–8; Daniel 6:22–24"> He went and found his body thrown on the path, and the donkey and the lion standing by the body. The lion had not eaten the body nor mauled the donkey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> The prophet took up the body of the man of God, and laid it on the donkey, and brought it back. He came to the city of the old prophet to mourn, and to bury him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:18; Acts 8:2; 1 Kings 14:13"> He laid his body in his own grave; and they mourned over him, saying, “Alas, my brother!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Kings 23:17–18; Numbers 23:10; Luke 16:22–23"> After he had buried him, he spoke to his sons, saying, “When I am dead, bury me in the tomb in which the man of God is buried. Lay my bones beside his bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 16:24; 1 Kings 13:2; 1 Kings 12:31"> For the saying which he cried by the LORD’s word against the altar in Bethel, and against all the houses of the high places which are in the cities of Samaria, will surely happen.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:15; 2 Chronicles 13:9; Numbers 17:12–13"> After this thing, Jeroboam didn’t turn from his evil way, but again made priests of the high places from among all the people. Whoever wanted to, he consecrated him, that there might be priests of the high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 14:10; 1 Kings 15:29–30; 1 Kings 12:30"> This thing became sin to the house of Jeroboam, even to cut it off and to destroy it from off the surface of the earth.</span>
