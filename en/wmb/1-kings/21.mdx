<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 6:33; 2 Chronicles 28:22; Joshua 19:18"> After these things, Naboth the Jezreelite had a vineyard which was in Jezreel, next to the palace of Ahab king of Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:14; Jacob 1:14–15; 1 Timothy 6:9"> Ahab spoke to Naboth, saying, “Give me your vineyard, that I may have it for a garden of herbs, because it is near my house; and I will give you for it a better vineyard than it. Or, if it seems good to you, I will give you its worth in money.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 36:7; Leviticus 25:23; Ezekiel 46:18"> Naboth said to Ahab, “May the LORD forbid me, that I should give the inheritance of my fathers to you!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 20:43; Jacob 1:14; Jonah 4:9"> Ahab came into his house sullen and angry because of the word which Naboth the Jezreelite had spoken to him, for he had said, “I will not give you the inheritance of my fathers.” He laid himself down on his bed, and turned away his face, and would eat no bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 16:31; Genesis 3:6; 1 Kings 19:2"> But Jezebel his wife came to him, and said to him, “Why is your spirit so sad that you eat no bread?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esther 5:9–14; Jacob 4:2–7; 1 Timothy 6:9–10"> He said to her, “Because I spoke to Naboth the Jezreelite, and said to him, ‘Give me your vineyard for money; or else, if it pleases you, I will give you another vineyard for it.’ He answered, ‘I will not give you my vineyard.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:14; Micah 7:3; 2 Samuel 13:4"> Jezebel his wife said to him, “Do you now govern the kingdom of Israel? Arise, and eat bread, and let your heart be merry. I will give you the vineyard of Naboth the Jezreelite.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 6:5; 2 Kings 10:11; Ezra 4:7–8"> So she wrote letters in Ahab’s name and sealed them with his seal, and sent the letters to the elders and to the nobles who were in his city, who lived with Naboth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 58:4; Matthew 23:13; Luke 20:47"> She wrote in the letters, saying, “Proclaim a fast, and set Naboth on high among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 22:28; Acts 6:11; Leviticus 24:15–16"> Set two men, wicked fellows, before him, and let them testify against him, saying, ‘You cursed God and the king!’ Then carry him out, and stone him to death.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 4:19; Leviticus 19:15; 2 Kings 10:6–7"> The men of his city, even the elders and the nobles who lived in his city, did as Jezebel had instructed them in the letters which she had written and sent to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 58:4; 1 Kings 21:8–10"> They proclaimed a fast, and set Naboth on high among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 9:26; Deuteronomy 22:24; Ecclesiastes 10:20"> The two men, the wicked fellows, came in and sat before him. The wicked fellows testified against him, even against Naboth, in the presence of the people, saying, “Naboth cursed God and the king!” Then they carried him out of the city and stoned him to death with stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:14; 2 Samuel 11:14–24; Ecclesiastes 5:8"> Then they sent to Jezebel, saying, “Naboth has been stoned and is dead.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 1:10–16; Proverbs 4:17; 1 Kings 21:7"> When Jezebel heard that Naboth had been stoned and was dead, Jezebel said to Ahab, “Arise, take possession of the vineyard of Naboth the Jezreelite, which he refused to give you for money; for Naboth is not alive, but dead.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Obadiah 1:12–14; Romans 1:32; 2 Samuel 11:25–27"> When Ahab heard that Naboth was dead, Ahab rose up to go down to the vineyard of Naboth the Jezreelite, to take possession of it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 9:12; Isaiah 26:21; 2 Kings 5:26"> The LORD’s word came to Elijah the Tishbite, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 13:32; 2 Chronicles 22:9"> “Arise, go down to meet Ahab king of Israel, who dwells in Samaria. Behold, he is in the vineyard of Naboth, where he has gone down to take possession of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 22:38; 2 Samuel 12:9; Habakkuk 2:9"> You shall speak to him, saying, ‘The LORD says, “Have you killed and also taken possession?”’ You shall speak to him, saying, ‘The LORD says, “In the place where dogs licked the blood of Naboth, dogs will lick your blood, even yours.”’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 17:17; Romans 7:14; 1 Kings 21:25"> Ahab said to Elijah, “Have you found me, my enemy?”<br />He answered, “I have found you, because you have sold yourself to do that which is evil in the LORD’s sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 14:10; 1 Samuel 25:34; 2 Kings 10:1–7"> Behold, I will bring evil on you, and will utterly sweep you away and will cut off from Ahab everyone who urinates against a wall,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or, male">a</a></sup>and him who is shut up and him who is left at large in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Kings 16:3; 1 Kings 14:16; 1 Kings 16:11"> I will make your house like the house of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, and like the house of Baasha the son of Ahijah, for the provocation with which you have provoked me to anger, and have made Israel to sin.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 9:10; 2 Kings 9:30–37; 1 Kings 21:25"> The LORD also spoke of Jezebel, saying, “The dogs will eat Jezebel by the rampart of Jezreel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 16:4; 1 Kings 14:11; Jeremiah 15:3"> The dogs will eat whoever dies of Ahab in the city; and the birds of the sky will eat whoever dies in the field.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 22:14; 1 Kings 21:20; 1 Kings 16:30–33"> But there was no one like Ahab, who sold himself to do that which was evil in the LORD’s sight, whom Jezebel his wife stirred up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 21:11; Genesis 15:16; Leviticus 18:25–30"> He did very abominably in following idols, according to all that the Amorites did, whom the LORD cast out before the children of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 6:30; Genesis 37:34; 2 Samuel 3:31"> When Ahab heard those words, he tore his clothes, put sackcloth on his body, fasted, lay in sackcloth, and went about despondently.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> The LORD’s word came to Elijah the Tishbite, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Peter 3:9; Micah 7:18; Psalms 86:15"> “See how Ahab humbles himself before me? Because he humbles himself before me, I will not bring the evil in his days; but I will bring the evil on his house in his son’s day.”</span>
</div>
