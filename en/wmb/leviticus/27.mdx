<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 6:2; 1 Samuel 1:28; Judges 11:30–31"> “Speak to the children of Israel, and say to them, ‘When a man consecrates a person to the LORD in a vow, according to your valuation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Leviticus 27:25; Numbers 18:16"> your valuation of a male from twenty years old to sixty years old shall be fifty shekels of silver, according to the shekel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A shekel is about 10 grams or about 0.35 ounces.">a</a></sup>of the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 27:9–10; Zechariah 11:12–13; Matthew 26:15"> If she is a female, then your valuation shall be thirty shekels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> If the person is from five years old to twenty years old, then your valuation shall be for a male twenty shekels, and for a female ten shekels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 3:40–43; Numbers 18:14–16"> If the person is from a month old to five years old, then your valuation shall be for a male five shekels of silver, and for a female your valuation shall be three shekels of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 90:10"> If the person is from sixty years old and upward; if he is a male, then your valuation shall be fifteen shekels, and for a female ten shekels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 14:7; Leviticus 14:21–22; Luke 21:1–4"> But if he is poorer than your valuation, then he shall be set before the priest, and the priest shall assign a value to him. The priest shall assign a value according to his ability to pay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> “‘If it is an animal of which men offer an offering to the LORD, all that any man gives of such to the LORD becomes holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 27:15–33; Jacob 1:8"> He shall not alter it, nor exchange it, a good for a bad, or a bad for a good. If he shall at all exchange animal for animal, then both it and that for which it is exchanged shall be holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:18; Malachi 1:14"> If it is any unclean animal, of which they do not offer as an offering to the LORD, then he shall set the animal before the priest;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and the priest shall evaluate it, whether it is good or bad. As the priest evaluates it, so it shall be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 27:19; Leviticus 27:15; Leviticus 22:14"> But if he will indeed redeem it, then he shall add the fifth part of it to its valuation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 18:14; Leviticus 25:29–31; Leviticus 27:21"> “‘When a man dedicates his house to be holy to the LORD, then the priest shall evaluate it, whether it is good or bad. As the priest evaluates it, so it shall stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 27:13"> If he who dedicates it will redeem his house, then he shall add the fifth part of the money of your valuation to it, and it shall be his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:11–14; Hosea 3:2; Acts 4:34–37"> “‘If a man dedicates to the LORD part of the field of his possession, then your valuation shall be according to the seed for it. The sowing of a homer<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1 homer is about 220 liters or 6 bushels">b</a></sup>of barley shall be valued at fifty shekels<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A shekel is about 10 grams or about 0.35 ounces.">c</a></sup>of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> If he dedicates his field from the Year of Jubilee, according to your valuation it shall stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 25:15–16; Leviticus 25:51–52; Leviticus 25:27"> But if he dedicates his field after the Jubilee, then the priest shall reckon to him the money according to the years that remain to the Year of Jubilee; and an abatement shall be made from your valuation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 27:13"> If he who dedicated the field will indeed redeem it, then he shall add the fifth part of the money of your valuation to it, and it shall remain his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> If he will not redeem the field, or if he has sold the field to another man, it shall not be redeemed any more;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:29; Numbers 18:14; Leviticus 25:31"> but the field, when it goes out in the Jubilee, shall be holy to the LORD, as a devoted field. It shall be owned by the priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 25:10; Leviticus 25:25"> “‘If he dedicates a field to the LORD which he has bought, which is not of the field of his possession,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 27:18; Leviticus 27:12"> then the priest shall reckon to him the worth of your valuation up to the Year of Jubilee; and he shall give your valuation on that day, as a holy thing to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 25:28; Leviticus 27:20"> In the Year of Jubilee the field shall return to him from whom it was bought, even to him to whom the possession of the land belongs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Numbers 18:16; Numbers 3:47"> All your valuations shall be according to the shekel of the sanctuary: twenty gerahs<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A gerah is about 0.5 grams or about 7.7 grains.">d</a></sup>to the shekel.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A shekel is about 10 grams or about 0.35 ounces.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Exodus 13:12–13; Numbers 18:17"> “‘However the firstborn among animals, which belongs to the LORD as a firstborn, no man may dedicate, whether an ox or a sheep. It is the LORD’s.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 27:11"> If it is an unclean animal, then he shall buy it back according to your valuation, and shall add to it the fifth part of it; or if it isn’t redeemed, then it shall be sold according to your valuation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 6:17–19; Leviticus 27:21; Exodus 22:20"> “‘Notwithstanding, no devoted thing that a man devotes to the LORD of all that he has, whether of man or animal, or of the field of his possession, shall be sold or redeemed. Everything that is permanently devoted is most holy to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:18–23; Numbers 21:2–3"> “‘No one devoted to destruction, who shall be devoted from among men, shall be ransomed. He shall surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 23:23; Nehemiah 13:12; Genesis 28:22"> “‘All the tithe of the land, whether of the seed of the land or of the fruit of the trees, is the LORD’s. It is holy to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> If a man redeems anything of his tithe, he shall add a fifth part to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:13; Ezekiel 20:37; Micah 7:14"> All the tithe of the herds or the flocks, whatever passes under the rod, the tenth shall be holy to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 27:10"> He shall not examine whether it is good or bad, neither shall he exchange it. If he exchanges it at all, then both it and that for which it is exchanged shall be holy. It shall not be redeemed.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Leviticus 26:46; Numbers 1:1; Hebrews 12:18–25"> These are the commandments which the LORD commanded Moses for the children of Israel on Mount Sinai.</span>
