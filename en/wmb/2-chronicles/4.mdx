<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 3:5; Zechariah 13:1; Exodus 30:18–21"> Also he made the molten sea<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="or, pool, or, reservoir">b</a></sup>of ten cubits from brim to brim. It was round, five cubits high, and thirty cubits in circumference.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:10; Ezekiel 10:14; 1 Corinthians 9:9–10"> Under it was the likeness of oxen, which encircled it, for ten cubits, encircling the sea. The oxen were in two rows, cast when it was cast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 28:19–20; Revelation 21:14; Mark 16:15"> It stood on twelve oxen, three looking toward the north, three looking toward the west, three looking toward the south, and three looking toward the east; and the sea was set on them above, and all their hindquarters were inward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 7:26"> It was a handbreadth thick. Its brim was made like the brim of a cup, like the flower of a lily. It received and held three thousand baths.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A bath is about 5.6 U. S. gallons or 21.1 liters, so 3,000 baths is about 16,800 gallons or 63.3 kiloliters.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 7:38; Exodus 30:18–21; 1 Kings 7:40"> He also made ten basins, and put five on the right hand and five on the left, to wash in them. The things that belonged to the burnt offering were washed in them, but the sea was for the priests to wash in.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–40; 1 Kings 7:49; Yochanan 8:12"> He made the ten lamp stands of gold according to the ordinance concerning them; and he set them in the temple, five on the right hand and five on the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 7:48; Malachi 1:12; Exodus 25:23–30"> He made also ten tables, and placed them in the temple, five on the right side and five on the left. He made one hundred basins of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 6:36; 2 Kings 21:5; 1 Kings 7:12"> Furthermore he made the court of the priests, the great court, and doors for the court, and overlaid their doors with bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 7:39"> He set the sea on the right side of the house eastward, toward the south.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 7:41; 2 Chronicles 3:15–17"> the two pillars, the bowls, the two capitals which were on the top of the pillars, the two networks to cover the two bowls of the capitals that were on the top of the pillars,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 7:20; 1 Kings 7:42; Jeremiah 52:23"> and the four hundred pomegranates for the two networks—two rows of pomegranates for each network, to cover the two bowls of the capitals that were on the pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 7:27–43"> He also made the bases, and he made the basins on the bases—</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> one sea, and the twelve oxen under it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:13; 1 Kings 7:13–14; 2 Chronicles 4:11"> Huram-abi<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“abi” means “his father”">d</a></sup>also made the pots, the shovels, the forks, and all its vessels for King Solomon, for the LORD’s house, of bright bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 7:46"> The king cast them in the plain of the Jordan, in the clay ground between Succoth and Zeredah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 7:47; Jeremiah 52:20; 1 Chronicles 22:14"> Thus Solomon made all these vessels in great abundance, so that the weight of the bronze could not be determined.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:7; Exodus 25:31–37; 1 Kings 6:5"> and the lamp stands with their lamps to burn according to the ordinance before the inner sanctuary, of pure gold;</span>
