<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:12; Isaiah 1:1; Matthew 1:9"> Jotham was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem. His mother’s name was Jerushah the daughter of Zadok.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 15:34–35; Acts 5:13; 2 Chronicles 26:4"> He did that which was right in the LORD’s eyes, according to all that his father Uzziah had done. However he didn’t enter into the LORD’s temple. The people still acted corruptly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:14; Nehemiah 3:26–27; Jeremiah 20:2"> He built the upper gate of the LORD’s house, and he built much on the wall of Ophel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:5–10; 2 Chronicles 26:9–10; Joshua 14:12–13"> Moreover he built cities in the hill country of Judah, and in the forests he built fortresses and towers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 11:4–33; 2 Samuel 10:1–14; Jeremiah 49:1–6"> He also fought with the king of the children of Ammon, and prevailed against them. The children of Ammon gave him the same year one hundred talents<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A talent is about 30 kilograms or 66 pounds">a</a></sup>of silver, ten thousand cors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1 cor is the same as a homer, or about 55.9 U. S. gallons (liquid) or 211 liters or 6 bushels. 10,000 cors of wheat would weigh about 1,640 metric tons.">b</a></sup>of wheat, and ten thousand cors of barley.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="10,000 cors of barley would weigh about 1,310 metric tons.">c</a></sup>The children of Ammon also gave that much to him in the second year, and in the third.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:5; 2 Chronicles 19:3"> So Jotham became mighty, because he ordered his ways before the LORD his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:22–23; 2 Kings 15:36; 2 Chronicles 32:32–33"> Now the rest of the acts of Jotham, and all his wars and his ways, behold, they are written in the book of the kings of Israel and Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 27:1"> He was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 15:38"> Jotham slept with his fathers, and they buried him in David’s city; and Ahaz his son reigned in his place.</span>
