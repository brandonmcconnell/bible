<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:11; 2 Corinthians 10:12; 2 Corinthians 5:12"> Are we beginning again to commend ourselves? Or do we need, as do some, letters of commendation to you or from you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:1–2; 1 Thessalonians 1:8; Romans 1:8"> You are our letter, written in our hearts, known and read by all men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19; Jeremiah 31:33; Hebrews 8:10"> being revealed that you are a letter of Messiah, served by us, written not with ink, but with the Spirit of the living God; not in tablets of stone, but in tablets that are hearts of flesh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Philippians 1:6; Ephesians 3:12; 2 Corinthians 2:14"> Such confidence we have through Messiah toward God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:9; Philippians 4:13; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> not that we are sufficient of ourselves to account anything as from ourselves; but our sufficiency is from God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Yochanan 6:63; Romans 7:6; Jeremiah 31:31"> who also made us sufficient as servants of a new covenant, not of the letter but of the Spirit. For the letter kills, but the Spirit gives life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:9–11; Romans 10:4; Exodus 31:18"> But if the service of death, written engraved on stones, came with glory, so that the children of Israel could not look steadfastly on the face of Moses for the glory of his face, which was passing away,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:16; Ephesians 2:18; Galatians 3:14"> won’t service of the Spirit be with much more glory?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 3:21–22; Hebrews 12:18–21; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> For if the service of condemnation has glory, the service of righteousness exceeds much more in glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Peter 1:17; Haggai 2:7–9; Acts 26:13"> For most certainly that which has been made glorious has not been made glorious in this respect, by reason of the glory that surpasses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 5:20–21; Hebrews 8:13; Hebrews 7:21–25"> For if that which passes away was with glory, much more that which remains is in glory.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:4; Acts 4:13; Philippians 1:20"> Having therefore such a hope, we use great boldness of speech,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 34:33–35; Hebrews 10:1–9; Ephesians 2:14–15"> and not as Moses, who put a veil on his face so that the children of Israel wouldn’t look steadfastly on the end of that which was passing away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:3–4; Acts 26:18; Romans 11:25"> But their minds were hardened, for until this very day at the reading of the old covenant the same veil remains, because in Messiah it passes away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 13:27–29"> But to this day, when Moses is read, a veil lies on their heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 34:34; Isaiah 29:18; Isaiah 25:7"> But whenever someone turns to the Lord, the veil is taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 8:2; Galatians 5:13; Yochanan 8:32"> Now the Lord is the Spirit; and where the Spirit of the Lord is, there is liberty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:6; Ephesians 4:22–24; Romans 12:2"> But we all, with unveiled face seeing the glory of the Lord as in a mirror, are transformed into the same image from glory to glory, even as from the Lord, the Spirit.</span>
</div>
