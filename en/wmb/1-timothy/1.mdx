<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 1:3; Colossians 1:27; Acts 9:15"> Paul, an emissary of Yeshua the Messiah according to the commandment of God our Savior and the Lord Yeshua the Messiah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="NU reads Messiah Yeshua and omits the Lord.">a</a></sup>our hope,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:4; 2 Timothy 1:2; 1 Corinthians 4:14–17"> to Timothy, my true child in faith: Grace, mercy, and peace from God our Father and Messiah Yeshua our Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:3; Galatians 1:6–7; 2 Yochanan 1:9–10"> As I urged you when I was going into Macedonia, stay at Ephesus that you might command certain men not to teach a different doctrine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:7; Titus 1:14; Titus 3:9"> and not to pay attention to myths and endless genealogies, which cause disputes rather than God’s stewardship, which is in faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:19; Galatians 5:22; 2 Timothy 2:22"> But the goal of this command is love from a pure heart, a good conscience, and sincere faith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 1:10; 2 Timothy 2:23–24; Titus 3:9"> from which things some, having missed the mark, have turned away to vain talking,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 1:22; 1 Timothy 6:4; 2 Peter 2:12"> desiring to be teachers of the law, though they understand neither what they say nor about what they strongly affirm.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 7:12–13; Psalms 19:7–10; Romans 7:16"> But we know that the law is good if a person uses it lawfully,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:19; 1 Peter 4:18; Romans 5:20"> as knowing this, that law is not made for a righteous person, but for the lawless and insubordinate, for the ungodly and sinners, for the unholy and profane, for murderers of fathers and murderers of mothers, for manslayers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Leviticus 18:22; 2 Timothy 4:3"> for the sexually immoral, for homosexuals, for slave-traders, for liars, for perjurers, and for any other thing contrary to the sound doctrine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 2:7; Titus 1:3; 1 Timothy 6:15"> according to the Good News of the glory of the blessed God, which was committed to my trust.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; 2 Timothy 4:17; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> I thank him who enabled me, Messiah Yeshua our Lord, because he counted me faithful, appointing me to service,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 8:3; 1 Peter 2:10; Hebrews 6:4–8"> although I used to be a blasphemer, a persecutor, and insolent. However, I obtained mercy because I did it ignorantly in unbelief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:13; Ephesians 1:7–8; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> The grace of our Lord abounded exceedingly with faith and love which is in Messiah Yeshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Mark 2:17; Luke 19:10; Yochanan 3:16–17"> The saying is faithful and worthy of all acceptance, that Messiah Yeshua came into the world to save sinners, of whom I am chief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13; Ephesians 2:7; Luke 7:47"> However, for this cause I obtained mercy, that in me first, Yeshua the Messiah might display all his patience for an example of those who were going to believe in him for eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 3:20–21; Judah 1:25; 1 Timothy 6:15–16"> Now to the King eternal, immortal, invisible, to God who alone is wise, be honor and glory forever and ever. Amen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:14; 1 Timothy 6:20; 2 Timothy 4:1–3"> I commit this instruction to you, my child Timothy, according to the prophecies which were given to you before, that by them you may wage the good warfare,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:9; 1 Peter 3:15–16; Revelation 3:10"> holding faith and a good conscience, which some having thrust away made a shipwreck concerning the faith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:4–5; 2 Timothy 2:17; 1 Corinthians 11:32"> of whom are Hymenaeus and Alexander, whom I delivered to Satan that they might be taught not to blaspheme.</span>
</div>
</div>
