<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:1–4; Ezekiel 16:26; Deuteronomy 4:29–31"> “They say, ‘If a man puts away his wife, and she goes from him, and becomes another man’s, should he return to her again?’ Wouldn’t that land be greatly polluted? But you have played the prostitute with many lovers; yet return again to me,” says the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:7; Genesis 38:14; Ezekiel 8:4–6"> “Lift up your eyes to the bare heights, and see! Where have you not been lain with? You have sat waiting for them by the road, as an Arabian in the wilderness. You have polluted the land with your prostitution and with your wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 26:19; Jeremiah 14:4; Deuteronomy 28:23"> Therefore the showers have been withheld and there has been no latter rain; yet you have had a prostitute’s forehead and you refused to be ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:2; Jeremiah 3:19; Psalms 71:17"> Will you not from this time cry to me, ‘My Father, you are the guide of my youth!’?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 7:3; Isaiah 57:16; Ezekiel 22:6"> “‘Will he retain his anger forever? Will he keep it to the end?’ Behold, you have spoken and have done evil things, and have had your way.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 17:7–17; Ezekiel 16:31; Jeremiah 7:24"> Moreover, the LORD said to me in the days of Josiah the king, “Have you seen that which backsliding Israel has done? She has gone up on every high mountain and under every green tree, and has played the prostitute there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:46; Hosea 6:1–4; Hosea 14:1"> I said after she had done all these things, ‘She will return to me;’ but she didn’t return, and her treacherous sister Judah saw it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:1; Hosea 4:15–17; Isaiah 50:1"> I saw when, for this very cause, that backsliding Israel had committed adultery, I had put her away and given her a certificate of divorce, yet treacherous Judah, her sister, had no fear, but she also went and played the prostitute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:27; Isaiah 57:6; Jeremiah 3:2"> Because she took her prostitution lightly, the land was polluted, and she committed adultery with stones and with wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 7:14; Jeremiah 12:2; 2 Chronicles 34:33–18"> Yet for all this her treacherous sister, Judah, has not returned to me with her whole heart, but only in pretense,” says the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:11; Ezekiel 16:51–52; Hosea 11:7"> The LORD said to me, “Backsliding Israel has shown herself more righteous than treacherous Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 86:15; Ezekiel 33:11; Deuteronomy 4:29–31"> Go, and proclaim these words toward the north, and say, ‘Return, you backsliding Israel,’ says the LORD; ‘I will not look in anger on you, for I am merciful,’ says the LORD. ‘I will not keep anger forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:25; Jeremiah 3:6; Deuteronomy 12:2"> Only acknowledge your iniquity, that you have transgressed against the LORD your God, and have scattered your ways to the strangers under every green tree, and you have not obeyed my voice,’” says the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hosea 2:19–20; Jeremiah 2:19; Jeremiah 31:32"> “Return, backsliding children,” says the LORD, “for I am a husband to you. I will take one of you from a city, and two from a family, and I will bring you to Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:4; Acts 20:28; Ephesians 4:11–12"> I will give you shepherds according to my heart, who will feed you with knowledge and understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 65:17; Amos 9:14–15; Ezekiel 37:26"> It will come to pass, when you are multiplied and increased in the land in those days,” says the LORD, “they will no longer say, ‘the ark of the LORD’s covenant!’ It will not come to mind. They won’t remember it. They won’t miss it, nor will another be made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:7; Micah 4:1–5; Jeremiah 17:12"> At that time they will call Jerusalem ‘The LORD’s Throne;’ and all the nations will be gathered to it, to the LORD’s name, to Jerusalem. They will no longer walk after the stubbornness of their evil heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:8; Hosea 1:11; Amos 9:15"> In those days the house of Judah will walk with the house of Israel, and they will come together out of the land of the north to the land that I gave for an inheritance to your fathers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:4; Jeremiah 31:9; Isaiah 63:16"> “But I said, ‘How I desire to put you among the children, and give you a pleasant land, a goodly heritage of the armies of the nations!’ and I said, ‘You shall call me “My Father”, and shall not turn away from following me.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 48:8; Jeremiah 5:11; Hosea 5:7"> “Surely as a wife treacherously departs from her husband, so you have dealt treacherously with me, house of Israel,” says the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10; Jeremiah 31:9; Jeremiah 2:32"> A voice is heard on the bare heights, the weeping and the petitions of the children of Israel; because they have perverted their way, they have forgotten the LORD their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 14:1; Hosea 14:4; Zechariah 13:9"> Return, you backsliding children, and I will heal your backsliding.<br />“Behold, we have come to you; for you are the LORD our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 121:1–2; Psalms 3:8; Isaiah 12:2"> Truly help from the hills, the tumult on the mountains, is in vain. Truly the salvation of Israel is in the LORD our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hosea 9:10; Ezekiel 16:63; Ezekiel 16:61"> But the shameful thing has devoured the labor of our fathers from our youth, their flocks and their herds, their sons and their daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:21; Proverbs 5:13; Isaiah 50:11"> Let us lie down in our shame, and let our confusion cover us; for we have sinned against the LORD our God, we and our fathers, from our youth even to this day. We have not obeyed the LORD our God’s voice.”</span>
</div>
