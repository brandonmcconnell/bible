<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:6; Exodus 19:5; 2 Chronicles 29:10"> “Hear the words of this covenant, and speak to the men of Judah, and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:26; Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Deuteronomy 29:19–20"> and say to them, the LORD, the God of Israel says: ‘Cursed is the man who doesn’t hear the words of this covenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:12; Jeremiah 7:22–23; Deuteronomy 4:20"> which I commanded your fathers in the day that I brought them out of the land of Egypt, out of the iron furnace,’ saying, ‘Obey my voice and do them, according to all which I command you; so you shall be my people, and I will be your God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:12–13; Psalms 105:9–11; Jeremiah 28:6"> that I may establish the oath which I swore to your fathers, to give them a land flowing with milk and honey,’ as it is today.”<br />Then I answered, and said, “Amen, LORD.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jacob 1:22; Romans 2:13; Jeremiah 7:2"> The LORD said to me, “Proclaim all these words in the cities of Judah, and in the streets of Jerusalem, saying, ‘Hear the words of this covenant, and do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:15; Ephesians 4:17; 1 Samuel 8:9"> For I earnestly protested to your fathers in the day that I brought them up out of the land of Egypt, even to this day, rising early and protesting, saying, “Obey my voice.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:24; Jeremiah 7:26; Jeremiah 35:15"> Yet they didn’t obey, nor turn their ear, but everyone walked in the stubbornness of their evil heart. Therefore I brought on them all the words of this covenant, which I commanded them to do, but they didn’t do them.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 6:9; Matthew 26:3–4; Micah 3:11"> The LORD said to me, “A conspiracy is found among the men of Judah, and among the inhabitants of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:16; Ezekiel 16:59; Psalms 78:8–10"> They have turned back to the iniquities of their forefathers, who refused to hear my words. They have gone after other gods to serve them. The house of Israel and the house of Judah have broken my covenant which I made with their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:18; Proverbs 1:28; Isaiah 1:15"> Therefore the LORD says, ‘Behold, I will bring evil on them which they will not be able to escape; and they will cry to me, but I will not listen to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Deuteronomy 32:37; 2 Chronicles 28:22"> Then the cities of Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem will go and cry to the gods to which they offer incense, but they will not save them at all in the time of their trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; 2 Kings 23:13; Isaiah 2:8"> For according to the number of your cities are your gods, Judah; and according to the number of the streets of Jerusalem you have set up altars to the shameful thing, even altars to burn incense to Baal.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:16; Jeremiah 11:11; 1 Yochanan 5:16"> “Therefore don’t pray for this people. Don’t lift up cry or prayer for them; for I will not hear them in the time that they cry to me because of their trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 2:14; Titus 1:15; Jeremiah 3:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">What has my beloved to do in my house,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">since she has behaved lewdly with many,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the holy flesh has passed from you?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">When you do evil,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">then you rejoice.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 11:17–24; Isaiah 27:11; Psalms 52:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">The LORD called your name, “A green olive tree,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">beautiful with goodly fruit.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">With the noise of a great roar he has kindled fire on it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and its branches are broken.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 5:2; Jeremiah 2:21; Jeremiah 12:2"> For the LORD of Hosts, who planted you, has pronounced evil against you, because of the evil of the house of Israel and of the house of Judah, which they have done to themselves in provoking me to anger by offering incense to Baal.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:11–12; 2 Kings 6:9–10; Ezekiel 8:6–18"> The LORD gave me knowledge of it, and I knew it. Then you showed me their doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7–8; Jeremiah 20:10; Psalms 83:4"> But I was like a gentle lamb that is led to the slaughter. I didn’t know that they had devised plans against me, saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Let’s destroy the tree with its fruit,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and let’s cut him off from the land of the living,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that his name may be no more remembered.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:12; Jeremiah 17:10; 1 Samuel 16:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">But, LORD of Hosts, who judges righteously,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who tests the heart and the mind,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will see your vengeance on them;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for to you I have revealed my cause.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:10; Amos 2:12; Luke 13:33–34"> “Therefore the LORD says concerning the men of Anathoth, who seek your life, saying, ‘You shall not prophesy in the LORD’s name, that you not die by our hand’—</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:21; 2 Chronicles 36:17; Jeremiah 9:21"> therefore the LORD of Hosts says, ‘Behold, I will punish them. The young men will die by the sword. Their sons and their daughters will die by famine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:12; Micah 7:4; Hosea 9:7"> There will be no remnant to them, for I will bring evil on the men of Anathoth, even the year of their visitation.’”</span>
</div>
</div>
