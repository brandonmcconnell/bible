</div>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 36:2; Genesis 5:21"> Irad was born to Enoch. Irad became the father of Mehujael. Mehujael became the father of Methushael. Methushael became the father of Lamech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 2:24; Matthew 19:8; Genesis 2:18"> Lamech took two wives: the name of the first one was Adah, and the name of the second one was Zillah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 4:2; Jeremiah 35:9–10; Yochanan 8:44"> Adah gave birth to Jabal, who was the father of those who dwell in tents and have livestock.</span>
