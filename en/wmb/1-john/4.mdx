<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:21; 2 Peter 2:1; 1 Timothy 4:1"> Beloved, don’t believe every spirit, but test the spirits, whether they are of God, because many false prophets have gone out into the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Yochanan 1:14; 1 Corinthians 12:3; 1 Timothy 3:16"> By this you know the Spirit of God: every spirit who confesses that Yeshua the Messiah has come in the flesh is of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Yochanan 2:22; 2 Yochanan 1:7; 1 Yochanan 2:18"> and every spirit who doesn’t confess that Yeshua the Messiah has come in the flesh is not of God; and this is the spirit of the Anti-messiah, of whom you have heard that it comes. Now it is in the world already.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 8:31; 1 Yochanan 5:4; Romans 8:37"> You are of God, little children, and have overcome them, because greater is he who is in you than he who is in the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Yochanan 8:23; 2 Peter 2:2–3; Yochanan 17:14"> They are of the world. Therefore they speak of the world, and the world hears them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Yochanan 14:17; Yochanan 10:27; 1 Corinthians 2:12–14"> We are of God. He who knows God listens to us. He who is not of God doesn’t listen to us. By this we know the spirit of truth, and the spirit of error.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Yochanan 4:20–1; 1 Yochanan 4:8; 1 Peter 1:22"> Beloved, let’s love one another, for love is of God; and everyone who loves has been born of God and knows God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Yochanan 4:16; Psalms 86:15; 2 Corinthians 13:11"> He who doesn’t love doesn’t know God, for God is love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Yochanan 3:16; 1 Yochanan 5:11; Romans 8:32"> By this God’s love was revealed in us, that God has sent his only born<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The phrase “only born” is from the Greek word “μονογενη”, which is sometimes translated “only begotten” or “one and only”.">a</a></sup>Son into the world that we might live through him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4–5; Romans 5:8–10; 1 Yochanan 4:19"> In this is love, not that we loved God, but that he loved us, and sent his Son as the atoning sacrifice<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“atoning sacrifice” is from the Greek “ιλασμος”, an appeasing, propitiating, or the means of appeasement or propitiation—the sacrifice that turns away God’s wrath because of our sin.">b</a></sup>for our sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Yochanan 15:12–13; Colossians 3:13; Yochanan 13:34"> Beloved, if God loved us in this way, we also ought to love one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Yochanan 1:18; 1 Yochanan 2:5; 1 Yochanan 4:17–18"> No one has seen God at any time. If we love one another, God remains in us, and his love has been perfected in us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 2:20–22; 1 Yochanan 3:24; 1 Corinthians 3:16–17"> By this we know that we remain in him and he in us, because he has given us of his Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Yochanan 4:10; Yochanan 3:16–17; Yochanan 4:42"> We have seen and testify that the Father has sent the Son as the Savior of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 10:9; 1 Yochanan 5:5; Matthew 10:32"> Whoever confesses that Yeshua is the Son of God, God remains in him, and he in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Yochanan 3:24; Psalms 36:7–9; 1 Yochanan 3:1"> We know and have believed the love which God has for us. God is love, and he who remains in love remains in God, and God remains in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Yochanan 4:12; 1 Yochanan 2:28; Romans 8:29"> In this, love has been made perfect among us, that we may have boldness in the day of judgment, because as he is, even so we are in this world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7; Romans 8:15; 1 Yochanan 4:12"> There is no fear in love; but perfect love casts out fear, because fear has punishment. He who fears is not made perfect in love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Yochanan 4:10; Yochanan 3:16; Yochanan 15:16"> We love him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="NU omits “him”.">c</a></sup>because he first loved us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Yochanan 3:17; 1 Yochanan 2:9; 1 Yochanan 4:12"> If a man says, “I love God,” and hates his brother, he is a liar; for he who doesn’t love his brother whom he has seen, how can he love God whom he has not seen?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 22:37–39; Leviticus 19:18; 1 Yochanan 3:11"> This commandment we have from him, that he who loves God should also love his brother.</span>
</div>
