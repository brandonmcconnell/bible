<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Yochanan 1:12–13; Yochanan 8:42; 1 Yochanan 2:29"> Whoever believes that Yeshua is the Messiah has been born of God. Whoever loves the Father also loves the child who is born of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Yochanan 13:34–35; 1 Yochanan 2:5; 1 Yochanan 3:22–24"> By this we know that we love the children of God, when we love God and keep his commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Yochanan 14:15; Yochanan 15:10; Yochanan 14:21–24"> For this is loving God, that we keep his commandments. His commandments are not grievous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:57; 1 Yochanan 4:4; Yochanan 16:33"> For whatever is born of God overcomes the world. This is the victory that has overcome the world: your faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Yochanan 4:15; 1 Yochanan 5:1"> Who is he who overcomes the world, but he who believes that Yeshua is the Son of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Yochanan 15:26; Hebrews 9:14; Yochanan 16:13"> This is he who came by water and blood, Yeshua the Messiah; not with the water only, but with the water and the blood. It is the Spirit who testifies, because the Spirit is the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 28:19; 2 Corinthians 13:14; Acts 5:32"> For there are three who testify:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Only a few recent manuscripts add “in heaven: the Father, the Word, and the Holy Spirit; and these three are one. And there are three that testify on earth:”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Yochanan 5:6–7; Yochanan 15:26; 1 Peter 3:21"> the Spirit, the water, and the blood; and the three agree as one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 3:16–17; Matthew 17:5; Acts 17:31"> If we receive the witness of men, the witness of God is greater; for this is God’s testimony which he has testified concerning his Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 8:16; Galatians 4:6; Yochanan 3:33"> He who believes in the Son of God has the testimony in himself. He who doesn’t believe God has made him a liar, because he has not believed in the testimony that God has given concerning his Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 6:23; 1 Yochanan 2:25; Yochanan 3:36"> The testimony is this: that God gave to us eternal life, and this life is in his Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Yochanan 3:36; Yochanan 5:24; Yochanan 1:12"> He who has the Son has the life. He who doesn’t have God’s Son doesn’t have the life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Yochanan 20:31; Yochanan 1:12; 2 Peter 1:10–11"> These things I have written to you who believe in the name of the Son of God, that you may know that you have eternal life, and that you may continue to believe in the name of the Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Yochanan 14:13; Jeremiah 33:3; Jeremiah 29:12–13"> This is the boldness which we have toward him, that if we ask anything according to his will, he listens to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 11:9–10; Mark 11:24; Proverbs 15:29"> And if we know that he listens to us, whatever we ask, we know that we have the petitions which we have asked of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 6:4–6; 1 Samuel 2:25; Jeremiah 11:14"> If anyone sees his brother sinning a sin not leading to death, he shall ask, and God will give him life for those who sin not leading to death. There is sin leading to death. I don’t say that he should make a request concerning this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Yochanan 3:4; 1 Yochanan 5:16; Jacob 4:7–10"> All unrighteousness is sin, and there is sin not leading to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Yochanan 3:9; 1 Yochanan 5:4; 1 Yochanan 5:1"> We know that whoever is born of God doesn’t sin, but he who was born of God keeps himself, and the evil one doesn’t touch him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4; 1 Yochanan 4:4–6; Ephesians 2:2"> We know that we are of God, and the whole world lies in the power of the evil one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Yochanan 17:3; Luke 24:45; Yochanan 14:9"> We know that the Son of God has come and has given us an understanding, that we know him who is true; and we are in him who is true, in his Son Yeshua the Messiah. This is the true God and eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:14; 1 Corinthians 10:7; Exodus 20:3–4"> Little children, keep yourselves from idols.</span>
