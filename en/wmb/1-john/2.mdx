<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:5; Romans 8:34; Yochanan 14:6"> My little children, I write these things to you so that you may not sin. If anyone sins, we have a Counselor<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Greek παρακλητον: Counselor, Helper, Intercessor, Advocate, and Comforter.">a</a></sup>with the Father, Yeshua the Messiah, the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Yochanan 4:14; 1 Peter 3:18; 1 Yochanan 4:10"> And he is the atoning sacrifice<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“atoning sacrifice” is from the Greek “ιλασμος”, an appeasing, propitiating, or the means of appeasement or propitiation—the sacrifice that turns away God’s wrath because of our sin.">b</a></sup>for our sins, and not for ours only, but also for the whole world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Yochanan 14:15; Yochanan 15:10; Luke 6:46"> This is how we know that we know him: if we keep his commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Yochanan 1:6; Titus 1:16; Jacob 2:14–16"> One who says, “I know him,” and doesn’t keep his commandments, is a liar, and the truth isn’t in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Yochanan 14:23; Yochanan 14:21; 1 Yochanan 4:12–13"> But God’s love has most certainly been perfected in whoever keeps his word. This is how we know that we are in him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 2:21; Yochanan 13:15; 1 Corinthians 11:1"> he who says he remains in him ought himself also to walk just like he walked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Yochanan 3:11; Leviticus 19:18; Mark 12:29–34"> Brothers, I write no new commandment to you, but an old commandment which you had from the beginning. The old commandment is the word which you heard from the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Romans 13:12; Yochanan 13:34"> Again, I write a new commandment to you, which is true in him and in you, because the darkness is passing away and the true light already shines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Yochanan 4:20; 1 Yochanan 2:11; 1 Yochanan 3:13–17"> He who says he is in the light and hates his brother is in the darkness even until now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Yochanan 3:14; Romans 9:32–33; Romans 14:13"> He who loves his brother remains in the light, and there is no occasion for stumbling in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Yochanan 12:35; Titus 3:3; 1 Yochanan 1:6"> But he who hates his brother is in the darkness, and walks in the darkness, and doesn’t know where he is going, because the darkness has blinded his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 10:43; Acts 13:38; Ephesians 1:7"> I write to you, little children, because your sins are forgiven you for his name’s sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Yochanan 14:7; 1 Yochanan 2:14; Luke 10:22"> I write to you, fathers, because you know him who is from the beginning. I write to you, young men, because you have overcome the evil one. I write to you, little children, because you know the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; 1 Yochanan 2:13; Colossians 3:16"> I have written to you, fathers, because you know him who is from the beginning. I have written to you, young men, because you are strong, and the word of God remains in you, and you have overcome the evil one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jacob 4:4; Romans 12:2; Matthew 6:24"> Don’t love the world or the things that are in the world. If anyone loves the world, the Father’s love isn’t in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 13:14; Galatians 5:17; Psalms 119:36–37"> For all that is in the world—the lust of the flesh, the lust of the eyes, and the pride of life—isn’t the Father’s, but is the world’s.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Romans 12:2; 1 Corinthians 7:31"> The world is passing away with its lusts, but he who does God’s will remains forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Yochanan 4:3; 2 Yochanan 1:7; Matthew 24:24"> Little children, these are the end times, and as you heard that the Anti-messiah is coming, even now many anti-messiahs have arisen. By this we know that it is the final hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 20:30; 1 Corinthians 11:19; Luke 8:13"> They went out from us, but they didn’t belong to us; for if they had belonged to us, they would have continued with us. But they left, that they might be revealed that none of them belong to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Yochanan 14:26; 1 Yochanan 2:27; 2 Corinthians 1:21–22"> You have an anointing from the Holy One, and you all have knowledge.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, “know what is true”, or, “know all things”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 1:12; Judah 1:5; Proverbs 1:5"> I have not written to you because you don’t know the truth, but because you know it, and because no lie is of the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Yochanan 1:7; 1 Yochanan 4:3; 1 Yochanan 2:18"> Who is the liar but he who denies that Yeshua is the Messiah? This is the Anti-messiah, he who denies the Father and the Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Yochanan 4:15; Yochanan 8:19; Yochanan 5:23"> Whoever denies the Son doesn’t have the Father. He who confesses the Son has the Father also.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="MT omits: He who confesses the Son has the Father also.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Yochanan 14:23; Yochanan 15:9–10; 1 Yochanan 4:13"> Therefore, as for you, let that remain in you which you heard from the beginning. If that which you heard from the beginning remains in you, you also will remain in the Son, and in the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Yochanan 10:28; Titus 3:7; Judah 1:21"> This is the promise which he promised us, the eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Peter 2:1–3; 2 Yochanan 1:7; Acts 20:29–30"> These things I have written to you concerning those who would lead you astray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Yochanan 14:26; 1 Yochanan 2:20–21; 1 Corinthians 2:13"> As for you, the anointing which you received from him remains in you, and you don’t need for anyone to teach you. But as his anointing teaches you concerning all things, and is true, and is no lie, and even as it taught you, you will remain in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Yochanan 4:17; Mark 8:38; 1 Yochanan 3:2"> Now, little children, remain in him, that when he appears, we may have boldness and not be ashamed before him at his coming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Yochanan 3:3–5; 1 Yochanan 3:7; 1 Yochanan 5:1"> If you know that he is righteous, you know that everyone who practices righteousness has been born of him.</span>
