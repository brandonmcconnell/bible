<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jacob 1:19; Hebrews 12:28–29; Proverbs 15:8"> Guard your steps when you go to God’s house; for to draw near to listen is better than to give the sacrifice of fools, for they don’t know that they do evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 6:7; Proverbs 10:19; Isaiah 55:9"> Don’t be rash with your mouth, and don’t let your heart be hasty to utter anything before God; for God is in heaven, and you on earth. Therefore let your words be few.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 15:2; Proverbs 10:19; Ecclesiastes 10:12–14"> For as a dream comes with a multitude of cares, so a fool’s speech with a multitude of words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 30:2; Deuteronomy 23:21–23; Psalms 76:11"> When you vow a vow to God, don’t defer to pay it; for he has no pleasure in fools. Pay that which you vow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 5:4; Deuteronomy 23:22; Proverbs 20:25"> It is better that you should not vow, than that you should vow and not pay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jacob 1:26; 2 Yochanan 1:8; Jacob 3:2"> Don’t allow your mouth to lead you into sin. Don’t protest before the messenger that this was a mistake. Why should God be angry at your voice, and destroy the work of your hands?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:13; Matthew 12:36; Ecclesiastes 8:12"> For in the multitude of dreams there are vanities, as well as in many words; but you must fear God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 12:5; Ecclesiastes 4:1; Ecclesiastes 3:16"> If you see the oppression of the poor, and the violent taking away of justice and righteousness in a district, don’t marvel at the matter, for one official is eyed by a higher one, and there are officials over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 4:7–23; Psalms 115:16; Genesis 1:29–30"> Moreover the profit of the earth is for all. The king profits from the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:10; Matthew 6:24; Luke 12:15"> He who loves silver shall not be satisfied with silver, nor he who loves abundance, with increase. This also is vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Yochanan 2:16; Habakkuk 2:13; Psalms 119:36–37"> When goods increase, those who eat them are increased; and what advantage is there to its owner, except to feast on them with his eyes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 3:24; Psalms 4:8; Jeremiah 31:26"> The sleep of a laboring man is sweet, whether he eats little or much; but the abundance of the rich will not allow him to sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 6:1–2; Luke 18:22–23; Genesis 19:14"> There is a grievous evil which I have seen under the sun: wealth kept by its owner to his harm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 6:19–20; Haggai 2:16–17; Proverbs 23:5"> Those riches perish by misfortune, and if he has fathered a son, there is nothing in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 49:17; Job 1:21; 1 Timothy 6:7"> As he came out of his mother’s womb, naked shall he go again as he came, and shall take nothing for his labor, which he may carry away in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 11:29; Ecclesiastes 1:3; Yochanan 6:27"> This also is a grievous evil, that in all points as he came, so shall he go. And what profit does he have who labors for the wind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 127:2; Ezekiel 4:16–17; Psalms 90:7–11"> All his days he also eats in darkness, he is frustrated, and has sickness and wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:24; Ecclesiastes 3:12–13; Ecclesiastes 3:22"> Behold, that which I have seen to be good and proper is for one to eat and to drink, and to enjoy good in all his labor, in which he labors under the sun, all the days of his life which God has given him; for this is his portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:18; Ecclesiastes 3:13; Ecclesiastes 6:2"> Every man also to whom God has given riches and wealth, and has given him power to eat of it, and to take his portion, and to rejoice in his labor—this is the gift of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 65:21–24; Isaiah 65:13–14; Psalms 37:16"> For he shall not often reflect on the days of his life, because God occupies him with the joy of his heart.</span>
