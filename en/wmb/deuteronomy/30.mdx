<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joel 2:12–13; Nehemiah 1:9; Deuteronomy 4:28–31"> and return to the LORD your God and obey his voice according to all that I command you today, you and your children, with all your heart and with all your soul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:14; Ezekiel 36:24; Psalms 147:2"> that then the LORD your God will release you from captivity, have compassion on you, and will return and gather you from all the peoples where the LORD your God has scattered you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:9; Ezekiel 39:25–29; Deuteronomy 28:64"> If your outcasts are in the uttermost parts of the heavens, from there the LORD your God will gather you, and from there he will bring you back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:14"> The LORD your God will bring you into the land which your fathers possessed, and you will possess it. He will do you good, and increase your numbers more than your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:16; Ezekiel 36:26–27; Jeremiah 32:39"> The LORD your God will circumcise your heart, and the heart of your offspring, to love the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul, that you may live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:15; Lamentations 4:21–22; Jeremiah 51:24–26"> The LORD your God will put all these curses on your enemies and on those who hate you, who persecuted you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 2:16; Ezekiel 36:27; Ezekiel 11:19–20"> You shall return and obey the LORD’s voice, and do all his commandments which I command you today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:11–14; Jeremiah 32:41; Jeremiah 33:9"> The LORD your God will make you prosperous in all the work of your hand, in the fruit of your body, in the fruit of your livestock, and in the fruit of your ground, for good; for the LORD will again rejoice over you for good, as he rejoiced over your fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40–41; Deuteronomy 4:29; Nehemiah 1:9"> if you will obey the LORD your God’s voice, to keep his commandments and his statutes which are written in this scroll of the Torah, if you turn to the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 45:19; Psalms 147:19–20; Romans 16:25–26"> For this commandment which I command you today is not too hard for you or too distant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 10:6–8; Proverbs 30:4; Yochanan 3:13"> It is not in heaven, that you should say, “Who will go up for us to heaven, bring it to us, and proclaim it to us, that we may do it?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 10:33; Matthew 12:42; Proverbs 2:1–5"> Neither is it beyond the sea, that you should say, “Who will go over the sea for us, bring it to us, and proclaim it to us, that we may do it?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 10:8–10; Jeremiah 12:2; Matthew 7:21"> But the word is very near to you, in your mouth and in your heart, that you may do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:26; Deuteronomy 30:19; 1 Yochanan 5:11–12"> Behold, I have set before you today life and prosperity, and death and evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:6; Matthew 22:37–38; 1 Yochanan 5:2–3"> For I command you today to love the LORD your God, to walk in his ways and to keep his commandments, his statutes, and his ordinances, that you may live and multiply, and that the LORD your God may bless you in the land where you go in to possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:4; Hebrews 3:12; Proverbs 1:32"> But if your heart turns away, and you will not hear, but are drawn away and worship other gods, and serve them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:19–20; Isaiah 63:17–18; Deuteronomy 4:26"> I declare to you today that you will surely perish. You will not prolong your days in the land where you pass over the Jordan to go in to possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:15; Deuteronomy 11:26; Psalms 119:30"> I call heaven and earth to witness against you today that I have set before you life and death, the blessing and the curse. Therefore choose life, that you may live, you and your descendants,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Yochanan 11:25–26; Deuteronomy 10:20; Yochanan 17:3"> to love the LORD your God, to obey his voice, and to cling to him; for he is your life, and the length of your days, that you may dwell in the land which the LORD swore to your fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, to give them.</span>
