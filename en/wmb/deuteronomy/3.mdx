<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 21:33–35; Deuteronomy 1:4; Deuteronomy 29:7"> Then we turned, and went up the way to Bashan. Og the king of Bashan came out against us, he and all his people, to battle at Edrei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 2:10; 2 Chronicles 20:17; Numbers 21:23–25"> The LORD said to me, “Don’t fear him; for I have delivered him, with all his people and his land, into your hand. You shall do to him as you did to Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived at Heshbon.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 21:35; Joshua 13:12; Joshua 13:30"> So the LORD our God also delivered into our hand Og, the king of Bashan, and all his people. We struck him until no one was left to him remaining.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 4:13; Joshua 12:4; Joshua 13:30–31"> We took all his cities at that time. There was not a city which we didn’t take from them: sixty cities, all the region of Argob, the kingdom of Og in Bashan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; Numbers 13:28; Hebrews 11:30"> All these were cities fortified with high walls, gates, and bars, in addition to a great many villages without walls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:34; Psalms 135:10–12; Deuteronomy 2:24"> We utterly destroyed them, as we did to Sihon king of Heshbon, utterly destroying every inhabited city, with the women and the little ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:35; Joshua 8:27; Joshua 11:11–14"> But all the livestock, and the plunder of the cities, we took for plunder for ourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 13:9–12; Joshua 12:2–6; Numbers 32:33–42"> We took the land at that time out of the hand of the two kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan, from the valley of the Arnon to Mount Hermon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:23; Psalms 29:6; Ezekiel 27:5"> (The Sidonians call Hermon Sirion, and the Amorites call it Senir.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 13:31; Joshua 13:11–12; Deuteronomy 4:49"> We took all the cities of the plain, and all Gilead, and all Bashan, to Salecah and Edrei, cities of the kingdom of Og in Bashan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 14:5; Jeremiah 49:2; 2 Samuel 12:26"> (For only Og king of Bashan remained of the remnant of the Rephaim. Behold, his bedstead was a bedstead of iron. Isn’t it in Rabbah of the children of Ammon? Nine cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A cubit is the length from the tip of the middle finger to the elbow on a man’s arm, or about 18 inches or 46 centimeters.">a</a></sup>was its length, and four cubits its width, after the cubit of a man.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:36; Numbers 32:32–38; Joshua 13:8–28"> This land we took in possession at that time: from Aroer, which is by the valley of the Arnon, and half the hill country of Gilead with its cities, I gave to the Reubenites and to the Gadites;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 32:39–42; Joshua 13:29–32; 1 Chronicles 5:23–26"> and the rest of Gilead, and all Bashan, the kingdom of Og, I gave to the half-tribe of Manasseh—all the region of Argob, even all Bashan. (The same is called the land of Rephaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 32:41; Joshua 13:13; 2 Samuel 3:3"> Jair the son of Manasseh took all the region of Argob, to the border of the Geshurites and the Maacathites, and called them, even Bashan, after his own name, Havvoth Jair, to this day.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 32:39–40; Joshua 17:1–3; Joshua 22:7"> I gave Gilead to Machir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 21:24; Deuteronomy 2:37; 2 Samuel 24:5"> To the Reubenites and to the Gadites I gave from Gilead even to the valley of the Arnon, the middle of the valley, and its border, even to the river Jabbok, which is the border of the children of Ammon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 12:3; Genesis 14:3; Joshua 3:16"> the Arabah also, and the Jordan and its border, from Chinnereth even to the sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea, under the slopes of Pisgah eastward.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 32:20–24; Joshua 4:12–13; Joshua 1:12–15"> I commanded you at that time, saying, “The LORD your God has given you this land to possess it. All of you men of valor shall pass over armed before your brothers, the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> But your wives, and your little ones, and your livestock, (I know that you have much livestock), shall live in your cities which I have given you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 22:4; Joshua 22:8"> until the LORD gives rest to your brothers, as to you, and they also possess the land which the LORD your God gives them beyond the Jordan. Then you shall each return to his own possession, which I have given you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 27:18–23; 2 Corinthians 12:10; Psalms 9:10"> I commanded Joshua at that time, saying, “Your eyes have seen all that the LORD your God has done to these two kings. So shall the LORD do to all the kingdoms where you go over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 14:14; Deuteronomy 20:4; Deuteronomy 1:30"> You shall not fear them; for the LORD your God himself fights for you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:8–9"> I begged GOD at that time, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 15:11; Deuteronomy 11:2; Psalms 71:19"> “Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The word translated “Lord” (mixed case) is “Adonai.”">b</a></sup>GOD, you have begun to show your servant your greatness, and your strong hand. For what god is there in heaven or in earth that can do works like yours, and mighty acts like yours?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:21–22; Ezekiel 20:6; Exodus 3:8"> Please let me go over and see the good land that is beyond the Jordan, that fine mountain, and Lebanon.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:37; Deuteronomy 31:2; 1 Chronicles 22:7–9"> But the LORD was angry with me because of you, and didn’t listen to me. The LORD said to me, “That is enough! Speak no more to me of this matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 27:12; Deuteronomy 34:1–4; Genesis 13:14–15"> Go up to the top of Pisgah, and lift up your eyes westward, and northward, and southward, and eastward, and see with your eyes; for you shall not go over this Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:3; Deuteronomy 31:7; Deuteronomy 1:38"> But commission Joshua, and encourage him, and strengthen him; for he shall go over before this people, and he shall cause them to inherit the land which you shall see.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:6; Deuteronomy 4:46; Numbers 25:3"> So we stayed in the valley near Beth Peor.</span>
</div>
