<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Deuteronomy 13:4; Exodus 20:20"> that you might fear the LORD your God, to keep all his statutes and his commandments, which I command you—you, your son, and your son’s son, all the days of your life; and that your days may be prolonged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Exodus 3:8; Genesis 26:4"> Hear therefore, Israel, and observe to do it, that it may be well with you, and that you may increase mightily, as the LORD, the God of your fathers, has promised to you, in a land flowing with milk and honey.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 12:29–32; Isaiah 44:6; 1 Timothy 2:5"> Hear, Israel: The LORD is our God. The LORD is one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 12:30; Matthew 22:37; Luke 10:27"> You shall love the LORD your God with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; Proverbs 7:3; Jeremiah 31:33"> These words, which I command you today, shall be on your heart;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:19; Ephesians 6:4; Psalms 78:4–6"> and you shall teach them diligently to your children, and shall talk of them when you sit in your house, and when you walk by the way, and when you lie down, and when you rise up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 6:21; Proverbs 3:3; Deuteronomy 11:18"> You shall bind them for a sign on your hand, and they shall be for frontlets between your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:20; Isaiah 30:8; Isaiah 57:8"> You shall write them on the door posts of your house and on your gates.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 24:13; Psalms 105:44; Nehemiah 9:25"> It shall be, when the LORD your God brings you into the land which he swore to your fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, to give you–a land with great and good cities which you didn’t build,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:12–18; Deuteronomy 8:10–20; Ezekiel 16:10–20"> and houses full of all good things which you didn’t fill, and cisterns dug out which you didn’t dig, vineyards and olive trees which you didn’t plant, and you shall eat and be full;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> then beware lest you forget the LORD, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 4:8; Deuteronomy 10:20; Matthew 4:10"> You shall fear the LORD your God; and you shall serve him, and shall swear by his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:6; 1 Yochanan 5:21; Deuteronomy 13:7"> You shall not go after other gods, of the gods of the peoples who are around you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:24; Exodus 20:5; Deuteronomy 7:4"> for the LORD your God among you is a jealous God, lest the anger of the LORD your God be kindled against you, and he destroy you from off the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 17:7; Luke 4:12; Matthew 4:7"> You shall not tempt the LORD your God, as you tempted him in Massah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:22; Psalms 119:4; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> You shall diligently keep the commandments of the LORD your God, and his testimonies, and his statutes, which he has commanded you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Deuteronomy 12:25; Romans 12:2"> You shall do that which is right and good in the LORD’s sight, that it may be well with you and that you may go in and possess the good land which the LORD swore to your fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 33:52–53; Exodus 23:28–30; Judges 3:1–4"> to thrust out all your enemies from before you, as the LORD has spoken.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 13:14; Exodus 12:26; Joshua 4:6–7"> When your son asks you in time to come, saying, “What do the testimonies, the statutes, and the ordinances, which the LORD our God has commanded you mean?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 6:17–18; Ephesians 2:11–12; Deuteronomy 15:15"> then you shall tell your son, “We were Pharaoh’s slaves in Egypt. The LORD brought us out of Egypt with a mighty hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:34; Psalms 58:10–11; Deuteronomy 7:19"> and the LORD showed great and awesome signs and wonders on Egypt, on Pharaoh, and on all his house, before our eyes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:18; Deuteronomy 6:10; Exodus 13:5"> and he brought us out from there, that he might bring us in, to give us the land which he swore to our fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:39; Psalms 41:2; Deuteronomy 10:12–13"> The LORD commanded us to do all these statutes, to fear the LORD our God, for our good always, that he might preserve us alive, as we are today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:13; Romans 10:3; Leviticus 18:5"> It shall be righteousness to us, if we observe to do all these commandments before the LORD our God, as he has commanded us.”</span>
