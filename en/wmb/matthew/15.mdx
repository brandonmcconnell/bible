<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Leviticus 20:9; Exodus 21:17"> <span className="woj">For God commanded, ‘Honor your father and your mother,’</span><span className="woj">and, ‘He who speaks evil of father or mother, let him be put to death.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 7:10–13; Acts 5:29; Matthew 23:16–18"> <span className="woj">But you say, ‘Whoever may tell his father or his mother, “Whatever help you might otherwise have gotten from me is a gift devoted to God,”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:8; Jeremiah 8:8; Mark 7:13"> <span className="woj">he shall not honor his father or mother.’ You have made the commandment of God void because of your tradition.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 7:6; Matthew 23:23–29; Acts 28:25–27"> <span className="woj">You hypocrites! Well did Isaiah prophesy of you, saying,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Ezekiel 33:31; Acts 8:21"> <span className="pl pl-1 woj">‘These people draw near to me with their mouth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2 woj">and honor me with their lips;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2 woj">but their heart is far from me.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 7:14–15; Luke 20:45–47; Luke 24:45"> He summoned the multitude, and said to them,<span className="woj">“Hear, and understand.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 7:15; Matthew 15:18–20; Jacob 3:5–8"> <span className="woj">That which enters into the mouth doesn’t defile the man; but that which proceeds out of the mouth, this defiles the man.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 22:13–14; 1 Corinthians 10:32–33; Galatians 2:5"> Then the disciples came and said to him, “Do you know that the Pharisees were offended when they heard this saying?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Yochanan 15:2; Isaiah 60:21; Yochanan 15:6"> But he answered,<span className="woj">“Every plant which my heavenly Father didn’t plant will be uprooted.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 6:39; Isaiah 56:10; Matthew 23:16–24"> <span className="woj">Leave them alone. They are blind guides of the blind. If the blind guide the blind, both will fall into a pit.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 13:36; Mark 4:34; Yochanan 16:29"> Peter answered him, “Explain the parable to us.”</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 16:9; Mark 7:18; Mark 9:32"> So Yeshua said,<span className="woj">“Do you also still not understand?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 6:45; 1 Corinthians 6:13; Matthew 7:19–20"> <span className="woj">Don’t you understand that whatever goes into the mouth passes into the belly and then out of the body?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 15:28; Matthew 12:34; Jacob 3:6–10"> <span className="woj">But the things which proceed out of the mouth come out of the heart, and they defile the man.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 7:21–23; Galatians 5:19–21; Jeremiah 17:9"> <span className="woj">For out of the heart come evil thoughts, murders, adulteries, sexual sins, thefts, false testimony, and blasphemies.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 7:3–4; Ephesians 5:3–6; 1 Corinthians 6:18–20"> <span className="woj">These are the things which defile the man; but to eat with unwashed hands doesn’t defile the man.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 7:24–30; Matthew 11:21–23; Judges 1:31"> Yeshua went out from there and withdrew into the region of Tyre and Sidon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 9:27; Mark 7:25–26; Matthew 17:15"> Behold, a Canaanite woman came out from those borders and cried, saying, “Have mercy on me, Lord, you son of David! My daughter is severely possessed by a demon!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 14:15; Mark 10:47–48; Lamentations 3:8"> But he answered her not a word.<br />His disciples came and begged him, saying, “Send her away; for she cries after us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:5–6; Romans 15:8; Jeremiah 50:6–7"> But he answered,<span className="woj">“I wasn’t sent to anyone but the lost sheep of the house of Israel.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 32:26; Matthew 20:31; Matthew 14:33"> But she came and worshiped him, saying, “Lord, help me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 7:27–28; Matthew 7:6; Philippians 3:2"> But he answered,<span className="woj">“It is not appropriate to take the children’s bread and throw it to the dogs.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ephesians 3:8; Luke 16:21; Job 40:4–5"> But she said, “Yes, Lord, but even the dogs eat the crumbs which fall from their masters’ table.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 9:23–24; Matthew 8:13; Psalms 145:19"> Then Yeshua answered her,<span className="woj">“Woman, great is your faith! Be it done to you even as you desire.”</span>And her daughter was healed from that hour.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 4:18; Yochanan 6:1; Matthew 5:1"> Yeshua departed from there and came near to the sea of Galilee; and he went up on the mountain and sat there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 6:17–19; Acts 5:15–16; Matthew 14:35–36"> Great multitudes came to him, having with them the lame, blind, mute, maimed, and many others, and they put them down at his feet. He healed them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Luke 18:43; Luke 17:15–18"> so that the multitude wondered when they saw the mute speaking, the injured healed, the lame walking, and the blind seeing—and they glorified the God of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 27:33; Luke 12:29–30; Matthew 9:36"> Yeshua summoned his disciples and said,<span className="woj">“I have compassion on the multitude, because they have continued with me now three days and have nothing to eat. I don’t want to send them away fasting, or they might faint on the way.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 6:37; Matthew 14:15; Mark 8:4–5"> The disciples said to him, “Where could we get so many loaves in a deserted place as to satisfy so great a multitude?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 16:9–10; Luke 24:41–42; Yochanan 21:9–10"> Yeshua said to them,<span className="woj">“How many loaves do you have?”</span><br />They said, “Seven, and a few small fish.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 9:14–16; Mark 6:39–40; Yochanan 6:10"> He commanded the multitude to sit down on the ground;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 22:19; Romans 14:6; Yochanan 6:11"> and he took the seven loaves and the fish. He gave thanks and broke them, and gave to the disciples, and the disciples to the multitudes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 8:19–21; Matthew 16:9–10; Matthew 15:33"> They all ate and were filled. They took up seven baskets full of the broken pieces that were left over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Those who ate were four thousand men, in addition to women and children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Mark 8:10; Matthew 14:22"> Then he sent away the multitudes, got into the boat, and came into the borders of Magdala.</span>
