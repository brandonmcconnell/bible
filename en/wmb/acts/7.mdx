<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 12:1; Hebrews 11:8; Joshua 24:3"> and said to him, ‘Get out of your land and away from your relatives, and come into a land which I will show you.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 12:4–5; Genesis 11:31–32; Isaiah 41:9"> Then he came out of the land of the Kasdim and lived in Haran. From there, when his father was dead, God moved him into this land where you are now living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Genesis 12:7; Genesis 13:15"> He gave him no inheritance in it, no, not so much as to set his foot on. He promised that he would give it to him for a possession, and to his offspring after him, when he still had no child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 15:13; Exodus 12:40–41; Galatians 3:17"> God spoke in this way: that his offspring would live as aliens in a strange land, and that they would be enslaved and mistreated for four hundred years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 3:12; Psalms 105:27–36; Nehemiah 9:9–11"> ‘I will judge the nation to which they will be in bondage,’ said God, ‘and after that they will come out and serve me in this place.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 35:23–26; Genesis 17:9–14; Genesis 21:1–4"> He gave him the covenant of circumcision. So Abraham became the father of Isaac, and circumcised him the eighth day. Isaac became the father of Jacob, and Jacob became the father of the twelve patriarchs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 39:2; Psalms 105:17; Isaiah 43:2"> “The patriarchs, moved with jealousy against Joseph, sold him into Egypt. God was with him</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 3:4; 2 Timothy 4:18; Proverbs 2:6"> and delivered him out of all his afflictions, and gave him favor and wisdom before Pharaoh, king of Egypt. He made him governor over Egypt and all his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 41:54–57; Genesis 45:11; Genesis 42:5"> Now a famine came over all the land of Egypt and Canaan, and great affliction. Our fathers found no food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 42:1–24; Genesis 43:2"> But when Jacob heard that there was grain in Egypt, he sent out our fathers the first time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 45:1–18; Genesis 46:31–10"> On the second time Joseph was made known to his brothers, and Joseph’s family was revealed to Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 46:26–27; Deuteronomy 10:22; Exodus 1:5"> Joseph sent and summoned Jacob his father and all his relatives, seventy-five souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 49:33; Exodus 1:6; Genesis 46:3–7"> Jacob went down into Egypt and he died, himself and our fathers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 24:32; Genesis 23:16; Exodus 13:19"> and they were brought back to Shechem and laid in the tomb that Abraham bought for a price in silver from the children of Hamor of Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 1:7–12; Acts 7:6; Acts 13:17"> “But as the time of the promise came close which God had sworn to Abraham, the people grew and multiplied in Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 1:8"> until there arose a different king who didn’t know Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 105:25; Exodus 1:9–22; Revelation 12:4–5"> The same took advantage of our race and mistreated our fathers, and forced them to abandon their babies, so that they wouldn’t stay alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 11:23; Exodus 2:2–10; 1 Samuel 16:12"> At that time Moses was born, and was exceedingly handsome to God. He was nourished three months in his father’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 11:24; Exodus 2:2–10; Deuteronomy 32:26"> When he was abandoned, Pharaoh’s daughter took him up and reared him as her own son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 19:11; Daniel 1:17–20; 2 Chronicles 9:22"> Moses was instructed in all the wisdom of the Egyptians. He was mighty in his words and works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 2:11–12; Proverbs 21:1; Hebrews 11:24–26"> But when he was forty years old, it came into his heart to visit his brothers,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The word for “brothers” here and where the context allows may be also correctly translated “brothers and sisters” or “siblings.”">a</a></sup>the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Yochanan 18:10–11; Acts 7:28; Yochanan 18:25–27"> Seeing one of them suffer wrong, he defended him and avenged him who was oppressed, striking the Egyptian.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 15:7; Mark 9:32; 2 Kings 5:1"> He supposed that his brothers understood that God, by his hand, was giving them deliverance; but they didn’t understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 2:13–15; Proverbs 18:19; 1 Yochanan 3:11–15"> “The day following, he appeared to them as they fought, and urged them to be at peace again, saying, ‘Sirs, you are brothers. Why do you wrong one another?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 7:35; Luke 12:14; Acts 7:54"> But he who did his neighbor wrong pushed him away, saying, ‘Who made you a ruler and a judge over us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Do you want to kill me as you killed the Egyptian yesterday?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 18:2–4; Exodus 2:14–22; Exodus 4:19–20"> Moses fled at this saying, and became a stranger in the land of Midian, where he became the father of two sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 3:1–2; Acts 7:35; Isaiah 43:2"> “When forty years were fulfilled, an angel of the Lord appeared to him in the wilderness of Mount Sinai, in a flame of fire in a bush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 3:3–4"> When Moses saw it, he wondered at the sight. As he came close to see, the voice of the Lord came to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 3:6; Matthew 22:32; Job 4:14"> ‘I am the God of your fathers: the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob.’ Moses trembled and dared not look.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 3:5; Joshua 5:15; Ecclesiastes 5:1"> The Lord said to him, ‘Take off your sandals, for the place where you stand is holy ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 3:7–10; Exodus 4:31; Psalms 106:44"> I have surely seen the affliction of my people who are in Egypt, and have heard their groaning. I have come down to deliver them. Now come, I will send you into Egypt.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; Luke 19:14; Psalms 113:7–8"> “This Moses whom they refused, saying, ‘Who made you a ruler and a judge?’—God has sent him as both a ruler and a deliverer by the hand of the angel who appeared to him in the bush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 12:41; Exodus 14:21; Exodus 33:1"> This man led them out, having worked wonders and signs in Egypt, in the Sea of Suf, and in the wilderness for forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15–19; Acts 3:22–23; Yochanan 18:37"> This is that Moses who said to the children of Israel, ‘The Lord our God will raise up a prophet for you from among your brothers, like me.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="TR adds “You shall listen to him.”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Romans 3:2; Hebrews 5:12; 1 Peter 4:11"> This is he who was in the assembly in the wilderness with the angel that spoke to him on Mount Sinai, and with our fathers, who received living revelations to give to us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Numbers 14:3–4; Numbers 11:5; Nehemiah 9:16–17"> to whom our fathers wouldn’t be obedient, but rejected him and turned back in their hearts to Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Exodus 32:1; Exodus 32:23"> saying to Aaron, ‘Make us gods that will go before us, for as for this Moses who led us out of the land of Egypt, we don’t know what has become of him.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Psalms 106:19–21; Nehemiah 9:18; Revelation 9:20"> They made a calf in those days, and brought a sacrifice to the idol, and rejoiced in the works of their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Amos 5:25–26; Ezekiel 20:39; Jeremiah 19:13"> But God turned away and gave them up to serve the army of the sky,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="This idiom could also be translated “host of heaven”, or “angelic beings”, or “heavenly bodies.”">c</a></sup>as it is written in the book of the prophets,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Did you offer to me slain animals and sacrifices</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">forty years in the wilderness, O house of Israel?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Amos 5:26–27; Exodus 20:4–5; Deuteronomy 5:8–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">You took up the tabernacle of Moloch,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the star of your god Rephan,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the figures which you made to worship,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so I will carry you away beyond Babylon.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Exodus 38:21; Hebrews 8:5"> “Our fathers had the tabernacle of the testimony in the wilderness, even as he who spoke to Moses commanded him to make it according to the pattern that he had seen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Psalms 44:2; Joshua 18:1; Joshua 23:9"> which also our fathers, in their turn, brought in with Joshua when they entered into the possession of the nations whom God drove out before the face of our fathers to the days of David,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 132:1–5; Acts 13:22; 2 Samuel 7:1–5"> who found favor in the sight of God, and asked to find a habitation for the God of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 Kings 8:20; 1 Kings 6:37–38; 2 Samuel 7:13"> But Solomon built him a house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Acts 17:24–25; Isaiah 66:1–2; 1 Kings 8:27"> However, the Most High doesn’t dwell in temples made with hands, as the prophet says,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Isaiah 66:1; Psalms 11:4; Yochanan 4:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘heaven is my throne,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the earth a footstool for my feet.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">What kind of house will you build me?’ says the Lord.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘Or what is the place of my rest?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:17; Psalms 33:6–9; Acts 14:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Didn’t my hand make all these things?’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:16; Ephesians 4:30; Isaiah 63:10"> “You stiff-necked and uncircumcised in heart and ears, you always resist the Holy Spirit! As your fathers did, so you do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 5:12; 2 Chronicles 36:16; 1 Kings 19:10"> Which of the prophets didn’t your fathers persecute? They killed those who foretold the coming of the Righteous One, of whom you have now become betrayers and murderers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Hebrews 2:2; Galatians 3:19; Acts 7:38"> You received the Torah as it was ordained by angels, and didn’t keep it!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Acts 5:33; Psalms 35:16; Matthew 25:30"> Now when they heard these things, they were cut to the heart, and they gnashed at him with their teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Mark 16:19; Hebrews 1:3; 2 Corinthians 4:6"> But he, being full of the Holy Spirit, looked up steadfastly into heaven and saw the glory of God, and Yeshua standing on the right hand of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Matthew 26:64–65; Acts 10:11; Revelation 11:19"> and said, “Behold, I see the heavens opened and the Son of Man standing at the right hand of God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Zechariah 7:11; Psalms 58:4; Acts 23:27"> But they cried out with a loud voice and stopped their ears, then rushed at him with one accord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Acts 22:20; Acts 8:1; Acts 6:13"> They threw him out of the city and stoned him. The witnesses placed their garments at the feet of a young man named Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Luke 23:46; Psalms 31:5; Acts 22:16"> They stoned Stephen as he called out, saying, “Lord Yeshua, receive my spirit!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; Luke 23:34; 1 Corinthians 15:20"> He kneeled down and cried with a loud voice, “Lord, don’t hold this sin against them!” When he had said this, he fell asleep.</span>
