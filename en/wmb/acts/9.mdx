<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 8:3; 1 Timothy 1:13; Galatians 1:13"> But Saul, still breathing threats and slaughter against the disciples of the Lord, went to the high priest</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 19:9; Acts 19:23; Acts 24:14"> and asked for letters from him to the synagogues of Damascus, that if he found any who were of the Way, whether men or women, he might bring them bound to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 26:12–13; 1 Corinthians 15:8; Acts 22:6"> As he traveled, he got close to Damascus, and suddenly a light from the sky shone around him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 2:8; Genesis 22:11; Romans 11:22"> He fell on the earth, and heard a voice saying to him,<span className="woj">“Saul, Saul, why do you persecute me?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13; 1 Samuel 3:4–10; Job 9:4"> He said, “Who are you, Lord?” The Lord said,<span className="woj">“I am Yeshua, whom you are persecuting.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="TR adds “It’s hard for you to kick against the cattle prods.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 22:10; Acts 26:16; 1 Timothy 1:14–16"> <span className="woj">But</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="TR omits “But”">b</a></sup><span className="woj">rise up and enter into the city, then you will be told what you must do.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 22:9; Daniel 10:7; Acts 26:13–14"> The men who traveled with him stood speechless, hearing the sound, but seeing no one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 22:11; Acts 9:18; 2 Kings 6:17–20"> Saul arose from the ground, and when his eyes were opened, he saw no one. They led him by the hand and brought him into Damascus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jonah 3:6–8; Esther 4:16; 2 Chronicles 33:12–13"> He was without sight for three days, and neither ate nor drank.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 22:1; Acts 10:3; Acts 22:12"> Now there was a certain disciple at Damascus named Hananiah. The Lord said to him in a vision,<span className="woj">“Hananiah!”</span>He said, “Behold, it’s me, Lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 21:39; Acts 22:3; Acts 9:30"> The Lord said to him,<span className="woj">“Arise and go to the street which is called Straight, and inquire in the house of Judah</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="or, Judah">c</a></sup><span className="woj">for one named Saul, a man of Tarsus. For behold, he is praying,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 5:23; Acts 9:10; Acts 9:17–18"> <span className="woj">and in a vision he has seen a man named Hananiah coming in and laying his hands on him, that he might receive his sight.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13–15; Acts 8:3; Acts 22:19–20"> But Hananiah answered, “Lord, I have heard from many about this man, how much evil he did to your holy ones at Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 9:21; Acts 7:59; 2 Timothy 2:22"> Here he has authority from the chief priests to bind all who call on your name.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 1:15–16; Ephesians 3:7–8; 2 Timothy 4:16–17"> But the Lord said to him,<span className="woj">“Go your way, for he is my chosen vessel to bear my name before the nations and kings, and the children of Israel.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:3; Acts 21:11; 2 Corinthians 6:4"> <span className="woj">For I will show him how many things he must suffer for my name’s sake.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 8:17; Acts 6:6; Acts 2:4"> Hananiah departed and entered into the house. Laying his hands on him, he said, “Brother Saul, the Lord, who appeared to you on the road by which you came, has sent me that you may receive your sight and be filled with the Holy Spirit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:14; Acts 22:16; Acts 2:38"> Immediately something like scales fell from his eyes, and he received his sight. He arose and was immersed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 26:20; 1 Samuel 30:12; Galatians 1:17"> He took food and was strengthened. Saul stayed several days with the disciples who were at Damascus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Yochanan 20:28; Galatians 2:20; 1 Yochanan 4:14–15"> Immediately in the synagogues he proclaimed the Messiah, that he is the Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 9:13–14; Acts 8:3; 2 Thessalonians 1:10"> All who heard him were amazed, and said, “Isn’t this he who in Jerusalem made havoc of those who called on this name? And he had come here intending to bring them bound before the chief priests!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 18:5; Isaiah 40:29; Luke 21:15"> But Saul increased more in strength, and confounded the Jews who lived at Damascus, proving that this is the Messiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 1:17–18; 1 Thessalonians 2:15–16; Acts 14:19"> When many days were fulfilled, the Jews conspired together to kill him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:32; Acts 25:3; Acts 20:3"> but their plot became known to Saul. They watched the gates both day and night that they might kill him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 2:15; 2 Corinthians 11:33; 1 Samuel 19:11–12"> but his disciples took him by night and let him down through the wall, lowering him in a basket.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 26:20; Galatians 1:17–19; Acts 9:19"> When Saul had come to Jerusalem, he tried to join himself to the disciples; but they were all afraid of him, not believing that he was a disciple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 4:36; Acts 4:29; Acts 4:13"> But Barnabas took him and brought him to the emissaries, and declared to them how he had seen the Lord on the way, and that he had spoken to him, and how at Damascus he had preached boldly in the name of Yeshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 1:21; Numbers 27:16–17; Psalms 121:8"> He was with them entering into<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="TR and NU add “and going out”">d</a></sup>Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 6:1; Judah 1:9; Acts 11:20"> proclaiming boldly in the name of the Lord Yeshua.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="TR and NU omit “Yeshua” and reverse the order of verses 28 &amp; 29.">e</a></sup>He spoke and disputed against the Hellenists,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The Hellenists were Hebrews who used Greek language and culture.">f</a></sup>but they were seeking to kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 11:25; Acts 8:40; Acts 9:11"> When the brothers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="The word for “brothers” here and where the context allows may also be correctly translated “brothers and sisters” or “siblings.”">g</a></sup>knew it, they brought him down to Caesarea and sent him off to Tarsus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 111:10; Yochanan 14:16–18; Isaiah 33:6"> So the assemblies throughout all Judea, Galilee, and Samaria had peace and were built up. They were multiplied, walking in the fear of the Lord and in the comfort of the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 9:13; Acts 8:25; Proverbs 2:8"> As Peter went throughout all those parts, he came down also to the holy ones who lived at Lydda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 13:16; Yochanan 5:5; Mark 2:3–11"> There he found a certain man named Aeneas, who had been bedridden for eight years because he was paralyzed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 9:6; Acts 3:12; Acts 3:6"> Peter said to him, “Aeneas, Yeshua the Messiah heals you. Get up and make your bed!” Immediately he arose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 11:21; Acts 9:42; 1 Chronicles 5:16"> All who lived at Lydda and in Sharon saw him, and they turned to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Titus 3:8; Titus 2:7; 2 Chronicles 2:16"> Now there was at Joppa a certain disciple named Tavita, which when translated means Dorcas.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="“Dorcas” is Greek for “Gazelle.”">h</a></sup>This woman was full of good works and acts of mercy which she did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Acts 20:8; Yochanan 11:36–37"> In those days, she became sick and died. When they had washed her, they laid her in an upper room.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 9:36; Acts 11:26; Acts 9:32"> As Lydda was near Joppa, the disciples, hearing that Peter was there, sent two men<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Reading from NU, TR; MT omits “two men”">i</a></sup>to him, imploring him not to delay in coming to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; Proverbs 31:30–31; 1 Thessalonians 1:3"> Peter got up and went with them. When he had come, they brought him into the upper room. All the widows stood by him weeping, and showing the tunics and other garments which Dorcas had made while she was with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 9:25; Mark 5:40–42; Luke 8:54"> Peter sent them all out, and knelt down and prayed. Turning to the body, he said, “Tavita, get up!” She opened her eyes, and when she saw Peter, she sat up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 1:31; Acts 6:1; Genesis 45:26"> He gave her his hand and raised her up. Calling the holy ones and widows, he presented her alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Yochanan 12:44; Yochanan 12:11; Yochanan 11:45"> This became known throughout all Joppa, and many believed in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Acts 10:6; Acts 10:32"> He stayed many days in Joppa with a tanner named Simon.</span>
