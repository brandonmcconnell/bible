<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 2:24; Joshua 2:9–14; Psalms 127:1"> Now Jericho was tightly shut up because of the children of Israel. No one went out, and no one came in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:24; Joshua 8:1; Joshua 2:24"> The LORD said to Joshua, “Behold, I have given Jericho into your hand, with its king and the mighty men of valor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:7; Numbers 14:9; Joshua 6:7"> All of your men of war shall march around the city, going around the city once. You shall do this six days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:17; Revelation 16:1; 1 Kings 18:43"> Seven priests shall bear seven shofars<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or, trumpets">a</a></sup>of rams’ horns before the ark. On the seventh day, you shall march around the city seven times, and the priests shall blow the shofars<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="or, trumpets">b</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Hebrews 11:30; Exodus 19:19"> It shall be that when they make a long blast with the ram’s horn, and when you hear the sound of the shofar, all the people shall shout with a great shout; then the city wall will fall down flat, and the people shall go up, every man straight in front of him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 9:1; Deuteronomy 20:2–4; Joshua 3:3"> Joshua the son of Nun called the priests, and said to them, “Take up the ark of the covenant, and let seven priests bear seven shofars<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="or, trumpets">c</a></sup>of rams’ horns before the LORD’s ark.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 4:13; Joshua 6:3; Joshua 1:14"> They said to the people, “Advance! March around the city, and let the armed men pass on before the LORD’s ark.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 32:20; Joshua 6:3–4"> It was so, that when Joshua had spoken to the people, the seven priests bearing the seven shofars<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="or, trumpets">d</a></sup>of rams’ horns before the LORD advanced and blew the shofars<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="or, trumpets">e</a></sup>, and the ark of the LORD’s covenant followed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 6:13; Numbers 10:25; Isaiah 58:8"> The armed men went before the priests who blew the shofars<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="or, trumpets">f</a></sup>, and the ark went after them. The shofars<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="or, trumpets">g</a></sup>sounded as they went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 24:49; Matthew 12:19; Isaiah 42:2"> Joshua commanded the people, saying, “You shall not shout nor let your voice be heard, neither shall any word proceed out of your mouth until the day I tell you to shout. Then you shall shout.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> So he caused the LORD’s ark to go around the city, circling it once. Then they came into the camp, and stayed in the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 3:1; Hebrews 11:7–8; Genesis 22:3"> Joshua rose early in the morning, and the priests took up the LORD’s ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; Matthew 24:13; 1 Chronicles 15:26"> The seven priests bearing the seven shofars<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="or, trumpets">h</a></sup>of rams’ horns in front of the LORD’s ark went on continually, and blew the shofars<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="or, trumpets">i</a></sup>. The armed men went in front of them. The rear guard came after the LORD’s ark. The shofars<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="or, trumpets">j</a></sup>sounded as they went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 6:15; Joshua 6:11; Joshua 6:3"> The second day they marched around the city once, and returned into the camp. They did this six days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:147; 2 Peter 1:19; Matthew 28:1"> On the seventh day, they rose early at the dawning of the day, and marched around the city in the same way seven times. On this day only they marched around the city seven times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:15; Joshua 6:5; 2 Chronicles 20:22–23"> At the seventh time, when the priests blew the shofars<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="or, trumpets">k</a></sup>, Joshua said to the people, “Shout, for the LORD has given you the city!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:17; Jacob 2:25; Micah 4:13"> The city shall be devoted, even it and all that is in it, to the LORD. Only Rahab the prostitute shall live, she and all who are with her in the house, because she hid the messengers that we sent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Yochanan 5:21; Ephesians 5:11; Joshua 7:1"> But as for you, only keep yourselves from what is devoted to destruction, lest when you have devoted it, you take of the devoted thing; so you would make the camp of Israel accursed and trouble it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:20; Matthew 27:6; Mark 12:41"> But all the silver, gold, and vessels of bronze and iron are holy to the LORD. They shall come into the LORD’s treasury.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 11:30; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Joshua 6:5"> So the people shouted and the priests blew the shofars<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="or, trumpets">l</a></sup>. When the people heard the sound of the shofar, the people shouted with a great shout, and the wall fell down flat, so that the people went up into the city, every man straight in front of him, and they took the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 137:8–9; Jeremiah 48:18; Joshua 11:14"> They utterly destroyed all that was in the city, both man and woman, both young and old, and ox, sheep, and donkey, with the edge of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 11:31; Joshua 6:17; Ezekiel 17:18–19"> Joshua said to the two men who had spied out the land, “Go into the prostitute’s house, and bring the woman and all that she has out from there, as you swore to her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 11:7; Genesis 18:24; Acts 27:24"> The young men who were spies went in, and brought out Rahab with her father, her mother, her brothers, and all that she had. They also brought out all of her relatives, and they set them outside of the camp of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 8:28; Revelation 17:16; 2 Kings 25:9"> They burned the city with fire, and all that was in it. Only they put the silver, the gold, and the vessels of bronze and of iron into the treasury of the LORD’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 11:31; Matthew 1:5; Judges 1:24–25"> But Rahab the prostitute, her father’s household, and all that she had, Joshua saved alive. She lives in the middle of Israel to this day, because she hid the messengers whom Joshua sent to spy out Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 16:34; Acts 19:13; Numbers 5:19–21"> Joshua commanded them with an oath at that time, saying, “Cursed is the man before the LORD who rises up and builds this city Jericho. With the loss of his firstborn he will lay its foundation, and with the loss of his youngest son he will set up its gates.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 9:3; Joshua 1:5; Matthew 28:20"> So the LORD was with Joshua; and his fame was in all the land.</span>
