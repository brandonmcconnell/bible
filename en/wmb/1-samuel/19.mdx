<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:1; 1 Samuel 18:9"> Saul spoke to Jonathan his son and to all his servants, that they should kill David. But Jonathan, Saul’s son, greatly delighted in David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 17:17; Acts 9:24; 1 Samuel 18:1–3"> Jonathan told David, saying, “Saul my father seeks to kill you. Now therefore, please take care of yourself in the morning, live in a secret place, and hide yourself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:9; 1 Samuel 20:13"> I will go out and stand beside my father in the field where you are, and I will talk with my father about you; and if I see anything, I will tell you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:20; Proverbs 31:8–9; Genesis 42:22"> Jonathan spoke good of David to Saul his father, and said to him, “Don’t let the king sin against his servant, against David; because he has not sinned against you, and because his works have been very good toward you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:13; 1 Chronicles 11:14; Judges 9:17"> for he put his life in his hand and struck the Philistine, and the LORD worked a great victory for all Israel. You saw it and rejoiced. Why then will you sin against innocent blood, to kill David without a cause?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 26:24–25; Jeremiah 5:2; Psalms 15:4"> Saul listened to the voice of Jonathan; and Saul swore, “As the LORD lives, he shall not be put to death.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:2; 1 Samuel 16:21; 1 Samuel 18:13"> Jonathan called David, and Jonathan showed him all those things. Then Jonathan brought David to Saul, and he was in his presence as before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 27:3; Psalms 18:32–50"> There was war again. David went out and fought with the Philistines, and killed them with a great slaughter; and they fled before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:14; 1 Samuel 18:10–11; 1 Samuel 16:16"> An evil spirit from the LORD was on Saul as he sat in his house with his spear in his hand; and David was playing music with his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:33; 1 Samuel 18:11; Proverbs 21:30"> Saul sought to pin David to the wall with the spear, but he slipped away out of Saul’s presence; and he stuck the spear into the wall. David fled and escaped that night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 59:1–17; Judges 16:2"> Saul sent messengers to David’s house to watch him and to kill him in the morning. Michal, David’s wife, told him, saying, “If you don’t save your life tonight, tomorrow you will be killed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 2:15; Acts 9:24–25; Psalms 34:19"> So Michal let David down through the window. He went away, fled, and escaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 31:19; Judges 18:17; Judges 18:14"> Michal took the teraphim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="teraphim were household idols that may have been associated with inheritance rights to the household property.">a</a></sup>and laid it in the bed, and put a pillow of goats’ hair at its head and covered it with clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 2:5; 2 Samuel 16:17–19; 2 Samuel 17:20"> When Saul sent messengers to take David, she said, “He is sick.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 27:3–4; Psalms 37:12; Job 31:31"> Saul sent the messengers to see David, saying, “Bring him up to me in the bed, that I may kill him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> When the messengers came in, behold, the teraphim was in the bed, with the pillow of goats’ hair at its head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:22; 1 Samuel 22:17; Exodus 1:17–19"> Saul said to Michal, “Why have you deceived me like this and let my enemy go, so that he has escaped?” Michal answered Saul, “He said to me, ‘Let me go! Why should I kill you?’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:17; Psalms 116:11; 1 Samuel 15:34"> Now David fled and escaped, and came to Samuel at Ramah, and told him all that Saul had done to him. He and Samuel went and lived in Naioth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:19; 1 Samuel 22:9–10; 1 Samuel 26:1"> Saul was told, saying, “Behold, David is at Naioth in Ramah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joel 2:28; Yochanan 7:32; 1 Samuel 10:5–6"> Saul sent messengers to seize David; and when they saw the company of the prophets prophesying, and Samuel standing as head over them, God’s Spirit came on Saul’s messengers, and they also prophesied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 27:22; Jeremiah 13:23; Joel 2:28"> When Saul was told, he sent other messengers, and they also prophesied. Saul sent messengers again the third time, and they also prophesied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Then he also went to Ramah, and came to the great well that is in Secu: and he asked, “Where are Samuel and David?” One said, “Behold, they are at Naioth in Ramah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 16:9; 1 Samuel 10:10; 1 Samuel 19:20"> He went there to Naioth in Ramah. Then God’s Spirit came on him also, and he went on, and prophesied, until he came to Naioth in Ramah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:20; Isaiah 20:2; Micah 1:8"> He also stripped off his clothes. He also prophesied before Samuel and lay down naked all that day and all that night. Therefore they say, “Is Saul also among the prophets?”</span>
