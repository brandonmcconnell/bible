<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Peter 1:20–21; Joel 2:28; Numbers 12:6–8"> God, having in the past spoken to the fathers through the prophets at many times and in various ways,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Yochanan 1:3; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Colossians 1:16–18"> has at the end of these days spoken to us by his Son, whom he appointed heir of all things, through whom also he made the worlds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 1:15–17; Revelation 4:11; Yochanan 14:9–10"> His Son is the radiance of his glory, the very image of his substance, and upholding all things by the word of his power, who, when he had by himself purified us of our sins, sat down on the right hand of the Majesty on high,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 1:21; Philippians 2:9–11; 1 Peter 3:22"> having become as much better than the angels as the more excellent name he has inherited is better than theirs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 2:7; Acts 13:33; Hebrews 5:5"> For to which of the angels did he say at any time,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“You are my Son.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Today I have become your father?”</span>and again,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will be to him a Father,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he will be to me a Son?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 97:7; Colossians 1:18; Deuteronomy 32:43"> When he again brings in the firstborn into the world he says, “Let all the angels of God worship him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 104:4; Hebrews 1:14; 2 Kings 6:17"> Of the angels he says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“He makes his angels winds,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and his servants a flame of fire.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 45:6–7; Isaiah 9:6–7; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> But of the Son he says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Your throne, O God, is forever and ever.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">The scepter of uprightness is the scepter of your Kingdom.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 45:7; Psalms 23:5; Isaiah 61:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have loved righteousness and hated iniquity;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">therefore God, your God, has anointed you with the oil of gladness above your fellows.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 102:25–27; Genesis 1:1; Yochanan 1:1–3"> And,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“You, Lord, in the beginning, laid the foundation of the earth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">The heavens are the works of your hands.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 51:6; Mark 13:31; Luke 21:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will perish, but you continue.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They all will grow old like a garment does.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 102:26–27; Hebrews 13:8; Yochanan 8:58"> <span className="pl pl-1">You will roll them up like a mantle,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will be changed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but you are the same.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Your years won’t fail.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Mark 12:36; Matthew 22:44"> But which of the angels has he told at any time,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Sit at my right hand,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">until I make your enemies the footstool of your feet?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 34:7; Psalms 103:20–21; Psalms 91:11–12"> Aren’t they all serving spirits, sent out to do service for the sake of those who will inherit salvation?</span>
