<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 20:15; Isaiah 43:28; Isaiah 24:6"> Then he said to me, “This is the curse that goes out over the surface of the whole land, for everyone who steals shall be cut off according to it on the one side; and everyone who swears falsely shall be cut off according to it on the other side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:9–11; Proverbs 3:33; Malachi 3:5"> I will cause it to go out,” says the LORD of Hosts, “and it will enter into the house of the thief, and into the house of him who swears falsely by my name; and it will remain in the middle of his house, and will destroy it with its timber and its stones.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 1:9; Zechariah 1:14; Zechariah 2:3"> Then the angel who talked with me came forward and said to me, “Lift up now your eyes and see what this is that is appearing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 8:5; Ezekiel 44:10–11"> I said, “What is it?” He said, “This is the efah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="An efah is a measure of volume of about 22 liters, 5.8 U. S. gallons, or about 2/3 of a bushel.">b</a></sup>basket that is appearing.” He said moreover, “This is their appearance in all the land—</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:1–49; Revelation 17:1–18; Jeremiah 3:1–2"> and behold, a lead cover weighing one talent<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A talent is about 30 kilograms or 66 pounds.">c</a></sup>was lifted up—and there was a woman sitting in the middle of the efah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1 efah is about 22 liters or about 2/3 of a bushel">d</a></sup>basket.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 15:16; Amos 9:1–4; Proverbs 5:22"> He said, “This is Wickedness;” and he threw her down into the middle of the efah basket; and he threw the lead weight on its mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:49; Hosea 8:1; Daniel 9:26–27"> Then I lifted up my eyes and saw, and behold, there were two women; and the wind was in their wings. Now they had wings like the wings of a stork, and they lifted up the efah basket between earth and the sky.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then I said to the angel who talked with me, “Where are these carrying the efah basket?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 11:2; Genesis 10:10; Daniel 1:2"> He said to me, “To build her a house in the land of Shinar. When it is prepared, she will be set there in her own place.”</span>
