<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 20:18; Numbers 27:21; Judges 20:28"> After the death of Joshua, the children of Israel asked of the LORD,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="When rendered in ALL CAPITAL LETTERS, “LORD” or “GOD” is the translation of God’s Proper Name.">a</a></sup>saying, “Who should go up for us first against the Canaanites, to fight against them?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 5:5; Hebrews 7:14; Numbers 2:3"> The LORD said, “Judah shall go up. Behold,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Behold”, from “הִנֵּה”, means look at, take notice, observe, see, or gaze at. It is often used as an interjection.">b</a></sup>I have delivered the land into his hand.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:11; Joshua 19:1; Judges 1:17"> Judah said to Simeon his brother, “Come up with me into my lot, that we may fight against the Canaanites; and I likewise will go with you into your lot.” So Simeon went with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:8; Exodus 23:28–29; Joshua 11:6–8"> Judah went up, and the LORD delivered the Canaanites and the Perizzites into their hand. They struck ten thousand men in Bezek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> They found Adoni-Bezek in Bezek, and they fought against him. They struck the Canaanites and the Perizzites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> But Adoni-Bezek fled. They pursued him, caught him, and cut off his thumbs and his big toes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:33; Revelation 13:10; Luke 6:37–38"> Adoni-Bezek said, “Seventy kings, having their thumbs and their big toes cut off, scavenged under my table. As I have done, so God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The Hebrew word rendered “God” is “אֱלֹהִ֑ים” (Elohim).">c</a></sup>has done to me.” They brought him to Jerusalem, and he died there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 15:63; Judges 1:21"> The children of Judah fought against Jerusalem, took it, struck it with the edge of the sword, and set the city on fire.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 15:13–20; Joshua 11:21; Joshua 10:36"> After that, the children of Judah went down to fight against the Canaanites who lived in the hill country, and in the South, and in the lowland.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 13:22; Joshua 14:15; Jeremiah 9:23"> Judah went against the Canaanites who lived in Hebron. (The name of Hebron before that was Kiriath Arba.) They struck Sheshai, Ahiman, and Talmai.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 15:15; Joshua 10:38–39"> From there he went against the inhabitants of Debir. (The name of Debir before that was Kiriath Sepher.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 15:16–17; 1 Samuel 17:25; 1 Samuel 18:23"> Caleb said, “I will give Achsah my daughter as wife to the man who strikes Kiriath Sepher, and takes it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 3:9"> Othniel the son of Kenaz, Caleb’s younger brother, took it, so he gave him Achsah his daughter as his wife.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 15:18–19"> When she came, she got him to ask her father for a field. She got off her donkey; and Caleb said to her, “What would you like?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:18; 2 Corinthians 9:5; 1 Peter 3:9"> She said to him, “Give me a blessing; because you have set me in the land of the South, give me also springs of water.” Then Caleb gave her the upper springs and the lower springs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 10:29–32; Judges 4:11; 1 Samuel 15:6"> The children of the Kenite, Moses’ brother-in-law, went up out of the city of palm trees with the children of Judah into the wilderness of Judah, which is in the south of Arad; and they went and lived with the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 21:3; Judges 1:3; Joshua 19:4"> Judah went with Simeon his brother, and they struck the Canaanites who inhabited Zephath, and utterly destroyed it. The name of the city was called Hormah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 11:22; Judges 3:3; Joshua 15:45–47"> Also Judah took Gaza with its border, and Ashkelon with its border, and Ekron with its border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Judges 1:2; Psalms 60:12"> The LORD was with Judah, and drove out the inhabitants of the hill country; for he could not drive out the inhabitants of the valley, because they had chariots of iron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 1:10; Joshua 15:13–14; Numbers 14:24"> They gave Hebron to Caleb, as Moses had said, and he drove the three sons of Anak out of there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 15:63; Judges 19:10–12; Judges 1:8"> The children of Benjamin didn’t drive out the Jebusites who inhabited Jerusalem, but the Jebusites dwell with the children of Benjamin in Jerusalem to this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 1:19; 2 Kings 18:7; Numbers 1:32"> The house of Joseph also went up against Bethel, and the LORD was with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 28:19; Genesis 35:6; Genesis 48:3"> The house of Joseph sent to spy out Bethel. (The name of the city before that was Luz.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 2:12–14; 1 Samuel 30:15"> The watchers saw a man come out of the city, and they said to him, “Please show us the entrance into the city, and we will deal kindly with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 6:22–25"> He showed them the entrance into the city, and they struck the city with the edge of the sword; but they let the man and all his family go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 7:6; 2 Chronicles 1:17"> The man went into the land of the Hittites, built a city, and called its name Luz, which is its name to this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 17:11–13; Jeremiah 48:10; Psalms 106:34–35"> Manasseh didn’t drive out the inhabitants of Beth Shean and its towns, nor Taanach and its towns, nor the inhabitants of Dor and its towns, nor the inhabitants of Ibleam and its towns, nor the inhabitants of Megiddo and its towns; but the Canaanites would dwell in that land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> When Israel had grown strong, they put the Canaanites to forced labor, and didn’t utterly drive them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 16:10; 1 Kings 9:16"> Ephraim didn’t drive out the Canaanites who lived in Gezer, but the Canaanites lived in Gezer among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 19:15"> Zebulun didn’t drive out the inhabitants of Kitron, nor the inhabitants of Nahalol; but the Canaanites lived among them, and became subject to forced labor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 19:24–30"> Asher didn’t drive out the inhabitants of Acco, nor the inhabitants of Sidon, nor of Ahlab, nor of Achzib, nor of Helbah, nor of Aphik, nor of Rehob;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 106:34–35"> but the Asherites lived among the Canaanites, the inhabitants of the land, for they didn’t drive them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 19:32–38; Judges 1:30; Psalms 18:24"> Naphtali didn’t drive out the inhabitants of Beth Shemesh, nor the inhabitants of Beth Anath; but he lived among the Canaanites, the inhabitants of the land. Nevertheless the inhabitants of Beth Shemesh and of Beth Anath became subject to forced labor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Joshua 19:47; Judges 18:1"> The Amorites forced the children of Dan into the hill country, for they would not allow them to come down to the valley;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Joshua 19:42; Judges 12:12; Joshua 10:12"> but the Amorites would dwell in Mount Heres, in Aijalon, and in Shaalbim. Yet the hand of the house of Joseph prevailed, so that they became subject to forced labor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Numbers 34:4; Joshua 15:2–3"> The border of the Amorites was from the ascent of Akrabbim, from the rock, and upward.</span>
</div>
