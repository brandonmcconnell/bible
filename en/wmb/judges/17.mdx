<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 18:2; Joshua 17:14–18; Judges 10:1"> There was a man of the hill country of Ephraim, whose name was Micah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:25; Ruth 3:10; 1 Samuel 23:21"> He said to his mother, “The eleven hundred pieces of silver that were taken from you, about which you uttered a curse, and also spoke it in my ears—behold, the silver is with me. I took it.” His mother said, “May the LORD bless my son!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 20:4; Exodus 20:23; Leviticus 19:4"> He restored the eleven hundred pieces of silver to his mother, then his mother said, “I most certainly dedicate the silver to the LORD from my hand for my son, to make a carved image and a molten image. Now therefore I will restore it to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 46:6–7; Jeremiah 10:9–10"> When he restored the money to his mother, his mother took two hundred pieces of silver, and gave them to a silversmith, who made a carved image and a molten image out of it. It was in the house of Micah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 18:14; Judges 8:27; Genesis 31:19"> The man Micah had a house of gods, and he made an ephod, and teraphim,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="teraphim were household idols that may have been associated with inheritance rights to the household property.">a</a></sup>and consecrated one of his sons, who became his priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:8; Proverbs 14:12; Judges 21:25"> In those days there was no king in Israel. Everyone did that which was right in his own eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Matthew 2:1; Ruth 1:1–2"> There was a young man out of Bethlehem Judah, of the family of Judah, who was a Levite; and he lived there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:10–11; Judges 17:11; Joshua 24:33"> The man departed out of the city, out of Bethlehem Judah, to live where he could find a place, and he came to the hill country of Ephraim, to the house of Micah, as he traveled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Micah said to him, “Where did you come from?” He said to him, “I am a Levite of Bethlehem Judah, and I am looking for a place to live.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:19; Judges 18:19–20; 1 Timothy 6:10"> Micah said to him, “Dwell with me, and be to me a father and a priest, and I will give you ten pieces of silver per year, a suit of clothing, and your food.” So the Levite went in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The Levite was content to dwell with the man; and the young man was to him as one of his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 18:30; Numbers 16:8–10; Numbers 16:5"> Micah consecrated the Levite, and the young man became his priest, and was in the house of Micah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 10:2–3; Proverbs 14:12; Yochanan 16:2"> Then Micah said, “Now I know that the LORD will do good to me, since I have a Levite as my priest.”</span>
