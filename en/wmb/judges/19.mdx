<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 18:1; Judges 17:8; Malachi 2:15"> In those days, when there was no king in Israel, there was a certain Levite living on the farther side of the hill country of Ephraim, who took for himself a concubine out of Bethlehem Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:28; Deuteronomy 22:21; Leviticus 21:9"> His concubine played the prostitute against him, and went away from him to her father’s house to Bethlehem Judah, and was there for four months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 34:3; Genesis 50:21; Leviticus 19:17"> Her husband arose and went after her to speak kindly to her, to bring her again, having his servant with him and a couple of donkeys. She brought him into her father’s house; and when the father of the young lady saw him, he rejoiced to meet him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> His father-in-law, the young lady’s father, kept him there; and he stayed with him three days. So they ate and drank, and stayed there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 19:8; Genesis 18:5; Psalms 104:15"> On the fourth day, they got up early in the morning, and he rose up to depart. The young lady’s father said to his son-in-law, “Strengthen your heart with a morsel of bread, and afterward you shall go your way.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 19:9; Judges 16:25; Ruth 3:7"> So they sat down, ate, and drank, both of them together. Then the young lady’s father said to the man, “Please be pleased to stay all night, and let your heart be merry.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> The man rose up to depart; but his father-in-law urged him, and he stayed there again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> He arose early in the morning on the fifth day to depart; and the young lady’s father said, “Please strengthen your heart and stay until the day declines;” and they both ate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; Luke 24:29; Jacob 4:13–14"> When the man rose up to depart, he, and his concubine, and his servant, his father-in-law, the young lady’s father, said to him, “Behold, now the day draws toward evening, please stay all night. Behold, the day is ending. Stay here, that your heart may be merry; and tomorrow go on your way early, that you may go home.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 15:63; Joshua 15:8; Judges 1:8"> But the man wouldn’t stay that night, but he rose up and went near Jebus (also called Jerusalem). With him were a couple of saddled donkeys. His concubine also was with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:6; Judges 1:21; Judges 19:10"> When they were by Jebus, the day was far spent; and the servant said to his master, “Please come and let’s enter into this city of the Jebusites, and stay in it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> His master said to him, “We won’t enter into the city of a foreigner that is not of the children of Israel; but we will pass over to Gibeah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:26; Hosea 5:8; Isaiah 10:29"> He said to his servant, “Come and let’s draw near to one of these places; and we will lodge in Gibeah, or in Ramah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> So they passed on and went their way; and the sun went down on them near Gibeah, which belongs to Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 19:18; Matthew 25:35; Matthew 25:43"> They went over there, to go in to stay in Gibeah. He went in, and sat down in the street of the city; for there was no one who took them into his house to stay.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 19:1; Psalms 104:23; Genesis 3:19"> Behold, an old man came from his work out of the field at evening. Now the man was from the hill country of Ephraim, and he lived in Gibeah; but the men of the place were Benjamites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 32:17; Genesis 16:8"> He lifted up his eyes, and saw the wayfaring man in the street of the city; and the old man said, “Where are you going? Where did you come from?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 18:31; Yochanan 15:6; 1 Samuel 1:7"> He said to him, “We are passing from Bethlehem Judah to the farther side of the hill country of Ephraim. I am from there, and I went to Bethlehem Judah. I am going to the LORD’s house; and there is no one who has taken me into his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Yet there is both straw and feed for our donkeys; and there is bread and wine also for me, and for your servant, and for the young man who is with your servants. There is no lack of anything.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 6:23; Jacob 2:15–16; Hebrews 13:2"> The old man said, “Peace be to you! Just let me supply all your needs, but don’t sleep in the street.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 43:24; Genesis 18:4; Luke 7:44"> So he brought him into his house, and gave the donkeys fodder. Then they washed their feet, and ate and drank.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 19:4–5; 1 Corinthians 6:9; Judges 20:5"> As they were making their hearts merry, behold, the men of the city, certain wicked fellows, surrounded the house, beating at the door; and they spoke to the master of the house, the old man, saying, “Bring out the man who came into your house, that we can have sex with him!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 34:7; 2 Samuel 13:12; Judges 20:6"> The man, the master of the house, went out to them, and said to them, “No, my brothers, please don’t act so wickedly; since this man has come into my house, don’t do this folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 19:8; Deuteronomy 21:14; Genesis 34:2"> Behold, here is my virgin daughter and his concubine. I will bring them out now. Humble them, and do with them what seems good to you; but to this man don’t do any such folly.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hosea 10:9; Hosea 9:9; Hosea 7:4–7"> But the men wouldn’t listen to him; so the man grabbed his concubine, and brought her out to them; and they had sex with her, and abused her all night until the morning. When the day began to dawn, they let her go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Judges 19:3; Genesis 18:12; 1 Peter 3:6"> Then the woman came in the dawning of the day, and fell down at the door of the man’s house where her lord was, until it was light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Her lord rose up in the morning and opened the doors of the house, and went out to go his way; and behold, the woman his concubine had fallen down at the door of the house, with her hands on the threshold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 20:5; 1 Kings 18:29"> He said to her, “Get up, and let’s get going!” but no one answered. Then he took her up on the donkey; and the man rose up, and went to his place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 20:6–7; 1 Samuel 11:7; Romans 10:2"> When he had come into his house, he took a knife and cut up his concubine, and divided her, limb by limb, into twelve pieces, and sent her throughout all the borders of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 20:7; Proverbs 13:10; Hosea 9:9"> It was so, that all who saw it said, “Such a deed has not been done or seen from the day that the children of Israel came up out of the land of Egypt to this day! Consider it, take counsel, and speak.”</span>
</div>
