<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:8; Numbers 33:49; Joshua 2:1"> Israel stayed in Shittim; and the people began to play the prostitute with the daughters of Moab;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:20; Exodus 20:5; Exodus 34:15–16"> for they called the people to the sacrifices of their gods. The people ate and bowed down to their gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 22:17; Psalms 106:28–29; Hosea 9:10"> Israel joined himself to Baal Peor, and the LORD’s anger burned against Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:17; Deuteronomy 4:3; Deuteronomy 21:23"> The LORD said to Moses, “Take all the chiefs of the people, and hang them up to the LORD before the sun, that the fierce anger of the LORD may turn away from Israel.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; 1 Kings 18:40; Deuteronomy 13:9"> Moses said to the judges of Israel, “Everyone kill his men who have joined themselves to Baal Peor.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 2:17; Numbers 22:4; Jeremiah 36:23"> Behold, one of the children of Israel came and brought to his brothers a Midianite woman in the sight of Moses, and in the sight of all the congregation of the children of Israel, while they were weeping at the door of the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 6:25; Psalms 106:30; Joshua 22:30–31"> When Phinehas, the son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron the priest, saw it, he rose up from the middle of the congregation, and took a spear in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 16:46–48; Psalms 106:29–31; 1 Chronicles 21:22"> He went after the man of Israel into the pavilion, and thrust both of them through, the man of Israel, and the woman through her body. So the plague was stopped among the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:8; Deuteronomy 4:3–4; Numbers 25:4–5"> Those who died by the plague were twenty-four thousand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Psalms 78:58; Deuteronomy 32:16"> “Phinehas, the son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron the priest, has turned my wrath away from the children of Israel, in that he was jealous with my jealousy among them, so that I didn’t consume the children of Israel in my jealousy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Malachi 2:4–5; Malachi 3:1; Isaiah 54:10"> Therefore say, ‘Behold, I give to him my covenant of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17; Romans 10:2–4; Jeremiah 33:22"> It shall be to him, and to his offspring after him, the covenant of an everlasting priesthood, because he was jealous for his God, and made atonement for the children of Israel.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 1:23; Numbers 26:14; 2 Chronicles 19:7"> Now the name of the man of Israel that was slain, who was slain with the Midianite woman, was Zimri, the son of Salu, a prince of a fathers’ house among the Simeonites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 31:8; Joshua 13:21; Numbers 25:18"> The name of the Midianite woman who was slain was Cozbi, the daughter of Zur. He was head of the people of a fathers’ house in Midian.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 31:1–3; Revelation 18:6"> “Harass the Midianites, and strike them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 2:14; Exodus 32:35; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> for they harassed you with their wiles, wherein they have deceived you in the matter of Peor, and in the incident regarding Cozbi, the daughter of the prince of Midian, their sister, who was slain on the day of the plague in the matter of Peor.”</span>
</div>
