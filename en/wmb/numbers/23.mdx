<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:28; Deuteronomy 32:8; Exodus 33:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">For from the top of the rocks I see him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">From the hills I see him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Behold, it is a people that dwells alone,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and shall not be listed among the nations.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 37:37; Genesis 13:16; Psalms 116:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Who can count the dust of Jacob,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or count the fourth part of Israel?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let me die the death of the righteous!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Let my last end be like his!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:2; Numbers 24:10; Numbers 22:17"> Balak said to Balaam, “What have you done to me? I took you to curse my enemies, and behold, you have blessed them altogether.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 22:38; Numbers 23:20; Numbers 23:26"> He answered and said, “Must I not take heed to speak that which the LORD puts in my mouth?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 109:17; Jacob 3:9–10; Numbers 22:41"> Balak said to him, “Please come with me to another place, where you may see them. You shall see just part of them, and shall not see them all. Curse them from there for me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 46:6; Numbers 23:29; Isaiah 1:10–11"> He took him into the field of Zophim, to the top of Pisgah, and built seven altars, and offered up a bull and a ram on every altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 23:3; Numbers 22:8"> He said to Balak, “Stand here by your burnt offering, while I meet God over there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 23:5; Numbers 24:1; Numbers 22:35"> The LORD met Balaam, and put a word in his mouth, and said, “Return to Balak, and say this.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 23:26; 1 Samuel 3:17; Jeremiah 37:17"> He came to him, and behold, he was standing by his burnt offering, and the princes of Moab with him. Balak said to him, “What has the LORD spoken?”</span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 24:8; Deuteronomy 33:17; Job 39:9–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">God brings them out of Egypt.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He has as it were the strength of the wild ox.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 10:18–19; Joshua 13:22; Galatians 1:23–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Surely there is no enchantment with Jacob;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">neither is there any divination with Israel.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Now it shall be said of Jacob and of Israel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘What has God done!’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 49:27; Genesis 49:9; Numbers 24:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Behold, a people rises up as a lioness.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">As a lion he lifts himself up.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He shall not lie down until he eats of the prey,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and drinks the blood of the slain.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 2:1–3"> Balak said to Balaam, “Neither curse them at all, nor bless them at all.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Numbers 22:18; Acts 4:19–20"> But Balaam answered Balak, “Didn’t I tell you, saying, ‘All that the LORD speaks, that I must do’?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 11:29; Proverbs 21:30; Numbers 23:19–20"> Balak said to Balaam, “Come now, I will take you to another place; perhaps it will please God that you may curse them for me from there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 21:20; Psalms 106:28"> Balak took Balaam to the top of Peor, that looks down on the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 23:1–2"> Balaam said to Balak, “Build seven altars for me here, and prepare seven bulls and seven rams for me here.”</span>
