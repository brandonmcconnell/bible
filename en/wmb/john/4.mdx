<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Yochanan 4:6; Yochanan 8:53; Matthew 12:42"> Are you greater than our father Jacob, who gave us the well and drank from it himself, as did his children and his livestock?”</span>

<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">

<div className="bp">

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:8; Malachi 1:11; Yochanan 4:23"> Yeshua said to her,<span className="woj">“Woman, believe me, the hour is coming when neither in this mountain nor in Jerusalem will you worship the Father.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 3:3; Yochanan 5:25; Matthew 15:7–9"> <span className="woj">But the hour comes, and now is, when the true worshipers will worship the Father in spirit and truth, for the Father seeks such to be his worshipers.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:17; Matthew 15:8–9; Philippians 3:3"> <span className="woj">God is spirit, and those who worship him must worship in spirit and truth.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Yochanan 1:49; Daniel 9:24–26; Yochanan 1:41–42"> The woman said to him, “I know that Messiah is coming, he who is called Messiah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Messiah” (Hebrew) and “Christ” (Greek) both mean “Anointed One”.">b</a></sup>When he has come, he will declare to us all things.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Yochanan 8:24; Mark 14:61–62; Romans 10:20–21"> Yeshua said to her,<span className="woj">“I am he, the one who speaks to you.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 7:39; Yochanan 4:8–9"> Just then, his disciples came. They marveled that he was speaking with a woman; yet no one said, “What are you looking for?” or, “Why do you speak with her?”</span>

<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Micah 6:15; Job 31:8; Luke 19:21"> <span className="woj">For in this the saying is true, ‘One sows, and another reaps.’</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Yochanan 4:29; Yochanan 4:5; Yochanan 10:41–42"> From that city many of the Samaritans believed in him because of the word of the woman, who testified, “He told me everything that I have done.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Revelation 3:20; Acts 16:15; Luke 24:29"> So when the Samaritans came to him, they begged him to stay with them. He stayed there two days.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 4:13; Romans 15:8; Mark 7:27–28"> After the two days he went out from there and went into Galilee.</span>

<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Yochanan 2:23; Yochanan 2:13–16; Deuteronomy 16:16"> So when he came into Galilee, the Galileans received him, having seen all the things that he did in Jerusalem at the feast, for they also went to the feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Yochanan 2:1–11; Luke 7:2; Hosea 5:15"> Yeshua came therefore again to Cana of Galilee, where he made the water into wine. There was a certain nobleman whose son was sick at Capernaum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Yochanan 4:3; Yochanan 11:21; Yochanan 11:32"> When he heard that Yeshua had come out of Judea into Galilee, he went to him and begged him that he would come down and heal his son, for he was at the point of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Yochanan 20:29; Hebrews 2:4; 1 Corinthians 1:22"> Yeshua therefore said to him,<span className="woj">“Unless you people see signs and wonders, you will in no way believe.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Mark 5:23; Psalms 88:10–12; Psalms 40:17"> The nobleman said to him, “Sir, come down before my child dies.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 8:13; Mark 7:29–30; Romans 4:20–21"> Yeshua said to him,<span className="woj">“Go your way. Your son lives.”</span>The man believed the word that Yeshua spoke to him, and he went his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="1 Kings 17:23; Yochanan 4:50; Yochanan 4:53"> As he was going down, his servants met him and reported, saying “Your child lives!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> So he inquired of them the hour when he began to get better. They said therefore to him, “Yesterday at the seventh hour,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1:00 p.m.">c</a></sup>the fever left him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Psalms 107:20; Luke 19:9; Acts 16:34"> So the father knew that it was at that hour in which Yeshua said to him,<span className="woj">“Your son lives.”</span>He believed, as did his whole house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Yochanan 4:45; Yochanan 2:1–11"> This is again the second sign that Yeshua did, having come out of Judea into Galilee.</span>
