<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 26:30; Exodus 13:4; Numbers 7:1"> “On the first day of the first month you shall raise up the tabernacle of the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:12; Numbers 4:5; Revelation 11:19"> You shall put the ark of the covenant in it, and you shall screen the ark with the veil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 24:8; Exodus 40:22–25; Exodus 37:10–24"> You shall bring in the table, and set in order the things that are on it. You shall bring in the lamp stand, and light its lamps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; Hebrews 9:24; 1 Yochanan 2:1–2"> You shall set the golden altar for incense before the ark of the covenant, and put the screen of the door to the tabernacle.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 40:29; 1 Yochanan 4:9–10; Hebrews 13:10"> “You shall set the altar of burnt offering before the door of the tabernacle of the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 13:1; 1 Yochanan 1:7; Psalms 26:6"> You shall set the basin between the Tent of Meeting and the altar, and shall put water therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 40:33; Ephesians 4:11–12; Matthew 16:18"> You shall set up the court around it, and hang up the screen of the gate of the court.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 8:10; Numbers 7:1; Matthew 3:16"> “You shall take the anointing oil, and anoint the tabernacle and all that is in it, and shall make it holy, and all its furniture, and it will be holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 29:36–37; Luke 1:35; Isaiah 61:1"> You shall anoint the altar of burnt offering, with all its vessels, and sanctify the altar, and the altar will be most holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> You shall anoint the basin and its base, and sanctify it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 8:1–13; Leviticus 9:1–24; Exodus 29:1–35"> “You shall bring Aaron and his sons to the door of the Tent of Meeting, and shall wash them with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 28:41; 1 Yochanan 2:20; 1 Yochanan 2:27"> You shall put on Aaron the holy garments; and you shall anoint him, and sanctify him, that he may minister to me in the priest’s office.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; Romans 8:30; 1 Corinthians 1:30"> You shall bring his sons, and put tunics on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 25:13; Hebrews 5:1–14; Hebrews 8:1–10"> You shall anoint them, as you anointed their father, that they may minister to me in the priest’s office. Their anointing shall be to them for an everlasting priesthood throughout their generations.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:1; Exodus 40:17–32; Deuteronomy 12:32"> Moses did so. According to all that the LORD commanded him, so he did.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 7:1; Exodus 40:1–2; Numbers 9:1"> In the first month in the second year, on the first day of the month, the tabernacle was raised up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:27–28; 1 Peter 1:5; Leviticus 26:11"> Moses raised up the tabernacle, and laid its sockets, and set up its boards, and put in its bars, and raised up its pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 26:1–14; Exodus 36:8–19"> He spread the covering over the tent, and put the roof of the tabernacle above on it, as the LORD commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 16:34; Deuteronomy 10:5; Hebrews 10:19–21"> He took and put the covenant into the ark, and set the poles on the ark, and put the mercy seat above on the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 26:33; Exodus 40:3; Exodus 35:12"> He brought the ark into the tabernacle, and set up the veil of the screen, and screened the ark of the covenant, as the LORD commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 26:35; Yochanan 6:53–57; Ephesians 3:8"> He put the table in the Tent of Meeting, on the north side of the tabernacle, outside of the veil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 25:30; Exodus 40:4; Hebrews 9:2"> He set the bread in order on it before the LORD, as the LORD commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 26:35; Revelation 2:5; Yochanan 1:1"> He put the lamp stand in the Tent of Meeting, opposite the table, on the south side of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 40:4; Exodus 25:37; Revelation 4:5"> He lit the lamps before the LORD, as the LORD commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 40:5; Hebrews 7:25; Yochanan 11:42"> He put the golden altar in the Tent of Meeting before the veil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 30:7"> and he burned incense of sweet spices on it, as the LORD commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 40:5; Hebrews 10:19–20; Ephesians 2:18"> He put up the screen of the door to the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 40:6; Hebrews 13:5–6; Hebrews 13:10"> He set the altar of burnt offering at the door of the tabernacle of the Tent of Meeting, and offered on it the burnt offering and the meal offering, as the LORD commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 40:7; Exodus 38:8; Ezekiel 36:25"> He set the basin between the Tent of Meeting and the altar, and put water therein, with which to wash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 30:19–20; Psalms 26:6; Yochanan 13:10"> Moses, Aaron, and his sons washed their hands and their feet there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 73:19; Exodus 30:19–20; Exodus 40:19"> When they went into the Tent of Meeting, and when they came near to the altar, they washed, as the LORD commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 40:8; Exodus 27:9–16; Yochanan 17:4"> He raised up the court around the tabernacle and the altar, and set up the screen of the gate of the court. So Moses finished the work.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Revelation 15:8; Numbers 9:15–23; 2 Chronicles 7:2"> Then the cloud covered the Tent of Meeting, and the LORD’s glory filled the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 8:11; Leviticus 16:2; 2 Chronicles 7:2"> Moses wasn’t able to enter into the Tent of Meeting, because the cloud stayed on it, and the LORD’s glory filled the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:19; Numbers 9:17; Exodus 13:21–22"> When the cloud was taken up from over the tabernacle, the children of Israel went onward, throughout all their journeys;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 9:19–22; Psalms 31:15"> but if the cloud wasn’t taken up, then they didn’t travel until the day that it was taken up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Psalms 78:14; Numbers 9:15; Exodus 13:21"> For the cloud of the LORD was on the tabernacle by day, and there was fire in the cloud by night, in the sight of all the house of Israel, throughout all their journeys.</span>
</div>
