<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 24:1"> Then Moses, Aaron, Nadab, Abihu, and seventy of the elders of Israel went up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Yochanan 1:18; Ezekiel 10:1; Genesis 32:30"> They saw the God of Israel. Under his feet was like a paved work of sapphire<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or, lapis lazuli">a</a></sup>stone, like the skies for clearness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 15:23–24; Exodus 18:12; Genesis 31:54"> He didn’t lay his hand on the nobles of the children of Israel. They saw God, and ate and drank.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:22; Exodus 31:18; Exodus 32:15–16"> The LORD said to Moses, “Come up to me on the mountain, and stay here, and I will give you the stone tablets with the law and the commands that I have written, that you may teach them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 33:11; Numbers 11:28; Exodus 3:1"> Moses rose up with Joshua, his servant, and Moses went up onto God’s Mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 17:10; Exodus 17:12; Genesis 22:5"> He said to the elders, “Wait here for us, until we come again to you. Behold, Aaron and Hur are with you. Whoever is involved in a dispute can go to them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 19:9; Matthew 17:5; Exodus 19:16"> Moses went up on the mountain, and the cloud covered the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 16:10; Numbers 16:42; Ezekiel 1:28"> The LORD’s glory settled on Mount Sinai, and the cloud covered it six days. The seventh day he called to Moses out of the middle of the cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Deuteronomy 4:24; Exodus 3:2"> The appearance of the LORD’s glory was like devouring fire on the top of the mountain in the eyes of the children of Israel.</span>
