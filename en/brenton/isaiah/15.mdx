<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 2:1–3; Jeremias 48:1–47; Esaias 11:14"> <span className="desc">The Word against the Land of Moab.</span><br />By night the land of Moab shall be destroyed; for by night the wall of the land of Moab shall be destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 48:1; Leviticus 21:5; Esaias 3:24"> Grieve for yourselves; for even Debon, where your altar is, shall be destroyed: thither shall ye go up to weep, over Nabau of the land of Moab: howl ye: baldness shall be on every head, and all arms shall be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. cut to pieces.">a</a></sup>wounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jonas 3:6–8; Jeremias 19:13; Deuteronomy 22:8"> Gird yourselves with sackcloth in her streets: and lament upon her roofs, and in her streets, and in her ways; howl all of you with weeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremias 48:34; Kings III 19:4; Jonas 4:8"> For Esebon and Eleale have cried: their voice was heard to Jassa: therefore the loins of the region of Moab cry aloud; her soul shall know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 48:5; Jeremias 4:20; Luke 19:41–44"> The heart of the region of Moab cries within her to Segor; for it is as a heifer of three years old: and on the ascent of Luith they shall go up to thee weeping by the way of Aroniim: she cries, Destruction, and trembling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 1:10–12; Esaias 19:5–7; Numbers 32:36"> The water of Nemerim shall be desolate, and the grass thereof shall fail: for there shall be no green grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremias 48:36; Naum 2:12–13; Esaias 10:6"> Shall Moab even thus be delivered? for I will bring the Arabians upon the valley, and they shall take it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jezekiel 47:10; Esaias 15:2–5; Jeremias 48:31–34"> For the cry has reached the border of the region of Moab, even of Agalim; and her howling has gone as far as the well of Ælim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings IV 17:25; Leviticus 26:28; Leviticus 26:24"> And the water of Dimon shall be filled with blood: for I will bring Arabians upon Dimon, and I will take away the seed of Moab, and Ariel, and the remnant of Adama.</span>
