<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 102:13; Zacharias 1:17; Zacharias 8:22–23"> And the Lord will have mercy on Jacob, and will yet choose Israel, and they shall rest on their land: and the stranger shall be added to them, yea, shall be added to the house of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esaias 61:5; Esaias 60:14; Jeremias 30:16"> And the Gentiles shall take them, and bring them into their place: and they shall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i. e. the Israelites.">a</a></sup>inherit them, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i. e. the Gentiles.">b</a></sup>they shall be multiplied upon the land for servants and handmaidens: and they that took them captives shall become captives to them; and they that had lordship over them shall be under their rule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 30:10; Esaias 32:18; Ezra and Nehemiah 9:8–9"> And it shall come to pass in that day, that the Lord shall give thee rest from thy sorrow and vexation, and from thy hard servitude wherein thou didst serve them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esaias 49:26; Ambacum 1:2–10; Jeremias 51:34–35"> And thou shalt take up this lamentation against the king of Babylon,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Alex. + 'and thou shalt say in that day'.">c</a></sup>How has the extortioner ceased, and the taskmaster ceased!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 125:3; Esaias 9:4; Jeremias 48:15–17"> The Lord has broken the yoke of sinners, the yoke of princes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 7:19–21; James 2:13; Revelation 17:16–17"> Having smitten a nation in wrath, with an incurable plague, smiting a nation with a wrathful plague, which spared them not, he rested in quiet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 126:1–3; Psalms 98:7–9; Psalms 96:11–13"> All the earth cries aloud with joy:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jezekiel 31:16; Esaias 55:12–13; Zacharias 11:2"> the trees also of Libanus rejoice against thee, and the cedar of Libanus, saying, From the time that thou hast been laid low, no one has come up to cut us down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jezekiel 32:21–32; Jeremias 50:8; Proverbs 15:24"> Hell from beneath is provoked to meet thee: all the great ones that have ruled over the earth have risen up together against thee, they that have raised up from their thrones all the kings of the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 49:6–14; Ecclesiastes 2:16; Psalms 82:6–7"> All shall answer and say to thee, Thou also hast been taken, even as we; and thou art numbered amongst us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 5:25–30; Job 21:11–15; Esaias 66:24"> Thy glory has come down to Hades, and thy great mirth: under thee they shall spread corruption, and the worm shall be thy covering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jezekiel 28:13–17; Revelation 12:7–10; Luke 10:18"> How has Lucifer, that rose in the morning, fallen from heaven! He that sent orders to all the nations is crushed to the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 8:10–12; Jezekiel 28:2; Jezekiel 28:12–16"> But thou saidst in thine heart, I will go up to heaven, I will set my throne above the stars of heaven: I will sit on a lofty mount, on the lofty mountains toward the north:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esaias 47:8; Genesis 3:5; 2 Thessalonians 2:4"> I will go up above the clouds; I will be like the Most High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 11:23; Jezekiel 32:23; Acts 12:22–23"> But now thou shalt go down to hell, even to the foundations of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremias 50:23; Psalms 58:10–11; Jeremias 51:20–23"> They that see thee shall wonder at thee, and say,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See chap 5. 25.">d</a></sup>This is the man that troubled the earth, that made kings to shake;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joel 2:3; Esaias 13:19–22; Esaias 45:13"> that made the whole world desolate, and destroyed its cities; he loosed not those who were in captivity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Chronicles II 24:25; Esaias 22:16; Jezekiel 32:18–32"> All the kings of the nations lie in honour, every man in his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremias 41:9; Jeremias 41:7; Kings III 21:24"> But thou shalt be cast forth on the mountains, as a loathed carcase, with many dead who have been pierced with swords, going down to the grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 21:10; Job 18:19; Psalms 37:28"> As a garment defiled with blood shall not be pure, so neither shalt thou be pure; because thou hast destroyed my land, and hast slain my people: thou shalt not endure for ever,—thou an evil seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 23:35; Exodus 20:5; Ambacum 2:8–12"> Prepare thy children to be slain for the sins of their father; that they arise not, and inherit the earth, nor fill the earth with wars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremias 50:29–35; Proverbs 10:7; Kings III 14:10"> And I will rise up against them, saith the Lord of hosts, and I will destroy their name, and remnant, and seed: thus saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Sophonias 2:14; Esaias 13:21–22; Kings III 14:10"> And I will make the region of Babylon desert, so that hedgehogs shall dwell there, and it shall come to nothing: and I will make it a pit of clay for destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Job 23:13; Esaias 46:10–11"> Thus saith the Lord of hosts, As I have said, so it shall be: and as I have purposed, so the matter shall remain:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Esaias 9:4; Naum 1:13; Esaias 37:36–38"> even to destroy the Assyrians upon my land, and upon my mountains: and they shall be for trampling; and their yoke shall be taken away from them, and their glory shall be taken away from their shoulders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Esaias 23:9; Sophonias 3:6–8; Exodus 15:12"> This is the purpose which the Lord has purposed upon the whole earth: and this the hand that is uplifted against all the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Esaias 43:13; Job 23:13; Chronicles II 20:6"> For what the Holy God has purposed, who shall frustrate? and who shall turn back his uplifted hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Kings IV 16:20; Esaias 13:1; Chronicles II 28:27"> In the year in which king Achaz died this word came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Esaias 30:6; Esaias 11:8; Chronicles II 26:6"> Rejoice not, all ye Philistines, because the yoke of him that smote you is broken: for out of the seed of the serpent shall come forth the young of asps, and their young shall come forth flying serpents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Esaias 7:21–22; Sophonias 2:4–7; Esaias 5:17"> And the poor shall be fed by him, and poor men shall rest in peace: but he shall destroy thy seed with hunger, and shall destroy thy remnant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremias 1:14; Esaias 20:1; Esaias 14:29"> Howl, ye gates of cities; let the cities be troubled and cry, even all the Philistines: for smoke is coming from the north, and there is no possibility<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. of being.">e</a></sup>of living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 102:16; Psalms 87:5; James 2:5"> And what shall the kings of the nations answer? That the Lord has founded Sion, and by him the poor of the people shall be saved.</span>
