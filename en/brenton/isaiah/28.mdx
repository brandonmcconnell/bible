<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Osee 7:5; Esaias 28:7; Kings IV 14:25–27"> Woe to the crown of pride, the hirelings of Ephraim, the flower that has fallen from the glory of the top of the fertile mountain, they that are drunken without wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esaias 30:30; Naum 1:8; Jezekiel 13:11"> Behold, the anger of the Lord is strong and severe, as descending hail where there is no shelter, violently descending; as a great body of water sweeping away the soil, he shall make rest for the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Esaias 26:6; Hebrews 10:29; Revelation 11:2"> The crown of pride, the hirelings of Ephraim, shall be beaten down with the hands and with the feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Naum 3:12; Michæas 7:1; Esaias 28:1"> And the fading flower of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. hope of glory.">a</a></sup>glorious hope on the top of the high mountain shall be as the early fig; he that sees it, before he takes it into his hand, will desire to swallow it down.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 5:4; Esaias 62:3; Esaias 60:19"> In that day the Lord of hosts shall be the crown of hope, the woven crown of glory, to the remnant of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 18:32–34; John 5:30; Joshua 1:9"> They shall be left in the spirit of judgment for judgment, and for the strength of them that hinder slaying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esaias 56:10–12; Luke 21:34; Esaias 19:14"> For these have trespassed through wine; they have erred through strong drink: the priest and the prophet are mad through strong drink, they are swallowed up by reason of wine, they have staggered<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Lit. from.">b</a></sup>through drunkenness; they have erred: this is their vision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremias 48:26; Proverbs 26:11; Ambacum 2:15–16"> A curse shall devour this counsel, for this is their counsel for the sake of covetousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 131:2; Jeremias 6:10; John 12:38"> To whom have we reported evils? and to whom have we reported a message? even to those that are weaned from the milk, who are drawn from the breast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esaias 28:13; 2 Timothy 3:7; Jeremias 11:7"> Expect thou affliction on affliction, hope upon hope: yet a little, and yet a little,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:21; Deuteronomy 28:49; Esaias 33:19"> by reason of the contemptuous words of the lips, by means of another language: for they shall speak to this people, saying to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 11:28–29; Esaias 30:15; Jeremias 6:16"> This is the rest to him that is hungry, and this is the calamity: but they would not hear.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 13:14; Esaias 6:9–10; 2 Peter 3:16"> Therefore the oracle of God shall be to them affliction on affliction, hope on hope, yet a little, and yet a little, that they may go and fall backward; and they shall be crushed and shall be in danger, and shall be taken.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esaias 28:22; Esaias 29:20; Esaias 1:10"> Therefore hear ye the word of the Lord, ye afflicted men, and ye princes of this people that is in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esaias 8:7–8; Jezekiel 13:22; Amos 2:4"> Because ye have said, We have made a covenant with Hades, and agreements with death; if the rushing storm should pass, it shall not come upon us: we have made falsehood our hope, and by falsehood shall we be protected:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 118:22; Acts 4:11–12; 1 Peter 2:6–8"> therefore thus saith the Lord, even the Lord,<br />Behold, I lay for the foundations of Sion a costly stone, a choice, a corner-stone, a precious stone, for its foundations; and he that believes on him shall by no means be ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings IV 21:13; Amos 7:7–9; Revelation 16:21"> And I will cause judgment to be for hope, and my compassion shall be for just measures, and ye that trust vainly in falsehood shall fall: for the storm shall by no means pass by you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Esaias 7:7; Esaias 8:10; Esaias 28:15"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, shall it not also? etc.">c</a></sup>except it also take away your covenant of death, and your trust in Hades shall by no means stand: if the rushing storm should come upon you, ye shall be beaten down by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ambacum 3:16; Esaias 36:22; Kings IV 24:2"> Whenever it shall pass by, it shall take you; morning by morning it shall pass by in the day, and in the night there shall be an evil hope.<br />Learn to hear,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Esaias 64:6; 1 Corinthians 1:18–31; Romans 9:30–32"> ye that are distressed; we cannot fight, but we are ourselves too weak for you to be gathered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Chronicles I 14:11; Joshua 10:12; Kings II 5:20"> The Lord shall rise up as a mountain of ungodly men, and shall be in the valley of Gabaon; he shall perform his works with wrath, even a work of bitterness, and his wrath shall deal strangely, and his destruction shall be strange.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Esaias 10:22–23; Esaias 24:1–23; Matthew 27:44"> Therefore do not ye rejoice, neither let your bands be made strong; for I have heard of works finished and cut short by the Lord of hosts, which he will execute upon all the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Esaias 1:2; Jeremias 22:29; Revelation 2:11"> Hearken, and hear my voice; attend, and hear my words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremias 4:3; Osee 10:11–12"> Will the ploughman plough all the day? or will he prepare the seed beforehand, before he tills the ground?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jezekiel 4:9; Matthew 23:23; Exodus 9:31–32"> Does he not, when he has levelled the surface thereof, then sow the small black poppy, or cumin, and afterward sow wheat, and barley, and millet, and bread-corn in thy borders?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 31:3–6; Exodus 36:2; Job 39:17"> So thou shalt be chastened by the judgment of thy God, and shalt rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Esaias 41:15; Esaias 27:7–8; Jeremias 46:28"> For the black poppy is not cleansed with harsh treatment, nor will a waggon-wheel pass over the cumin; but the black poppy is threshed with a rod, and the cumin shall be eaten with bread;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:9–10; Matthew 3:12; John 12:24"> for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. I am.">d</a></sup>I will not be wroth with you for ever, neither shall the voice of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. bitterness trample you.">e</a></sup>my anger crush you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Jeremias 32:19; Job 5:9"> And these signs came forth from the Lord of hosts. Take counsel, exalt vain comfort.</span>
</div>
</div>
