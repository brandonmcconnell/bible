<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esaias 1:18; Ambacum 2:20; Zacharias 2:13"> Hold a feast to me, ye islands: for the princes shall renew their strength: let them draw nigh and speak together: then let them declare judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esaias 45:1; Kings II 22:43; Ezra and Nehemiah 1:2"> Who raised up righteousness from the east, and called it to his feet, so that it should go? shall appoint it an adversary of Gentiles, and shall dismay kings, and bury their swords in the earth, and cast forth their bows and arrows as sticks?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 5:24; Esaias 57:2"> And he shall pursue them; the way of his feet shall proceed in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esaias 48:12; Revelation 22:13; Revelation 1:17"> Who has wrought and done these things? he has called it who called it from the generations of old; I God, the first and to all futurity, I AM.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jezekiel 26:15–16; Psalms 65:8; Psalms 66:3"> The nations saw, and feared; the ends of the earth drew nigh, and came together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esaias 44:12; Esaias 40:19; Daniel 3:1–7"> every one judging for his neighbour and that to assist his brother: and one will say,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esaias 40:19–20; Daniel 3:1–7; Judges 18:17–18"> The artificer has become strong, and the coppersmith that smites with the hammer, and forges also: sometimes he will say, It is a piece well joined: they have fastened them with nails; they will fix them, and they shall not be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 2:23; Chronicles II 20:7; Deuteronomy 14:2"> But thou, Israel, art my servant Jacob, and he whom I have chosen, the seed of Abraam, whom I have loved:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings I 12:22; Psalms 94:14; Deuteronomy 7:6–7"> whom I have taken hold of from the ends of the earth, and from the high places of it I have called thee, and said to thee, Thou art my servant; I have chosen thee, and I have not forsaken thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Esaias 43:1–2; Romans 8:31"> Fear not; for I am with thee: wander not; for I am thy God, who have strengthened thee; and I have helped thee, and have established thee with my just right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 23:22; Esaias 45:24; Revelation 3:9"> Behold, all thine adversaries shall be ashamed and confounded; for they shall be as if they were not: and all thine opponents shall perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 37:35–36; Job 20:7–9; Esaias 17:14"> Thou shalt seek them, and thou shalt not find the men who shall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Lit. transgress by wine against.">a</a></sup>insolently rage against thee: for they shall be as if they were not, and they that war against thee shall not be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Esaias 41:10; Psalms 73:23; Psalms 63:8"> For I am thy God, who holdeth thy right hand, who saith to thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esaias 43:14; Psalms 22:6; Esaias 54:5"> Fear not, Jacob, and thou Israel few in number; I have helped thee, saith thy God, he that redeems thee, O Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Michæas 4:13; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Zacharias 4:7"> Behold, I have made thee as new saw-shaped threshing wheels of a waggon; and thou shalt thresh the mountains, and beat the hills to powder, and make them as chaff:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremias 51:2; Esaias 61:10–11; Esaias 45:24–25"> and thou shalt winnow them, and the wind shall carry them away, and a tempest shall scatter them: but thou shalt rejoice in the holy ones of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Esaias 55:1; Esaias 30:19; Esaias 44:3"> And the poor and the needy shall exult; for when they shall seek water, and there shall be none, and their tongue is parched with thirst, I the Lord God, I the God of Israel will hear, and will not forsake them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 107:35; Esaias 35:6–7; Esaias 58:11"> but I will open rivers on the mountains, and fountains in the midst of plains: I will make the desert pools of water, and a thirsty land watercourses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esaias 55:13; Esaias 37:31–32; Esaias 35:1"> I will plant in the dry land the cedar and box, the myrtle and cypress, and white poplar:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 12:9; Esaias 44:23; Esaias 43:21"> that they may see, and know, and perceive, and understand together, that the hand of the Lord has wrought these works, and the Holy One of Israel has displayed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 23:3–4; Job 38:3; Michæas 6:1–2"> Your judgment draws nigh, saith the Lord God; your counsels have drawn nigh, saith the King of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Esaias 45:21; Esaias 48:14; Esaias 44:7"> Let them draw nigh, and declare to you what things shall come to pass; or tell us what things were of old, and we will apply our understanding, and we shall know what are the last and the future things:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 13:19; Esaias 42:9; Jeremias 10:5"> tell us, declare ye to us the things that are coming on at the last time, and we shall know that ye are gods: do good, and do evil, and we shall wonder, and see at the same time</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 115:8; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Esaias 41:29"> whence ye are, and whence is your work: they have chosen you an abomination out of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Esaias 41:2; Kings II 22:43; Esaias 10:6"> But I have raised up him that comes from the north, and him that comes from the rising of the sun: they shall be called by my name: let the princes come, and as potter's clay, and as a potter treading clay, so shall ye be trodden down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Esaias 44:7; Esaias 41:22; Esaias 45:21"> For who will declare the things from the beginning, that we may know also the former things,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, and say.">b</a></sup>and we will say that they are true? there is no one that speaks beforehand, nor any one that hears your words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Esaias 40:9; Esaias 52:7; Esaias 44:28"> I will give dominion to Sion, and will comfort Jerusalem by the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Esaias 63:5; Daniel 2:10–11; Esaias 40:13–14"> For from among the nations, behold, there was no one; and of their idols there was none to declare anything: and if I should ask them, Whence are ye? they could not answer me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremias 5:13; Esaias 41:24; Jeremias 10:2–16"> For these are your makers, as ye think, and they that cause you to err in vain.</span>
