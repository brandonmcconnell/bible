<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esaias 9:6–7; Jeremias 33:15; Hebrews 1:8–9"> For, behold, a righteous king shall reign, and princes shall govern with judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esaias 25:4; Esaias 44:3; Esaias 9:6"> And a man shall hide his words, and be hidden, as from rushing water, and shall appear in Sion as a rushing river, glorious in a thirsty land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Esaias 29:18; Acts 26:18; 2 Corinthians 4:6"> And they shall no more trust in men, but they shall incline their ears to hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esaias 29:24; Luke 21:14–15; Matthew 16:17"> And the heart of the weak ones shall attend to hear, and the stammering tongues shall soon learn to speak peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esaias 5:20; Psalms 15:4; Malachias 3:18"> And they shall no more at all tell a fool to rule, and thy servants shall no more at all say, Be silent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 15:19; Matthew 23:13; Esaias 3:15"> For the fool shall speak foolish words, and his heart shall meditate vanities, and to perform lawless deeds and to speak error against the Lord, to scatter hungry souls, and he will cause the thirsty souls to be empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremias 5:26–28; Matthew 26:59–60; Matthew 26:14–16"> For the counsel of the wicked will devise iniquity, to destroy the poor with unjust words, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. disperse the words.">a</a></sup>ruin the cause of the poor in judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:2; Acts 11:29–30; Luke 6:33–35"> But the godly have devised wise measures, and this counsel shall stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 13:9; Esaias 3:16; Esaias 28:23"> Rise up, ye rich women, and hear my voice;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. daughters in hope.">b</a></sup>ye confident daughters, hearken to my words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esaias 7:23; Jeremias 8:13; Sophonias 1:13"> Remember for a full year in pain, yet with hope: the vintage has been cut off, it has ceased, it shall by no means come again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esaias 3:24; Esaias 33:14; Esaias 20:4"> Be amazed, be pained, ye confident ones: strip you, bare yourselves, gird your loins;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jezekiel 20:15; Jezekiel 20:6; Naum 2:7"> and beat on your breasts, because of the pleasant field, and the fruit of the vine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Esaias 22:2; Osee 9:6; Esaias 7:23"> As for the land of my people, the thorn and grass shall come upon it, and joy shall be removed from every house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esaias 24:12; Esaias 24:10; Esaias 25:2"> As for the rich city, the houses are deserted; they shall abandon the wealth of the city, and the pleasant houses: and the villages shall be caves for ever, the joy of wild asses, shepherds' pastures;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esaias 29:17; Jezekiel 39:29; Esaias 44:3"> until the Spirit shall come upon you from on high, and Chermel shall be desert, and Chermel shall be counted for a forest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 94:14–15; 1 Peter 2:9–12; Esaias 60:21"> Then judgment shall abide in the wilderness, and righteousness shall dwell in Carmel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Esaias 26:3; Psalms 119:165; Philippians 4:6–9"> And the works of righteousness shall be peace; and righteousness shall ensure rest, and the righteous shall be confident for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zacharias 2:5; Osee 2:18–23; Jeremias 33:16"> And his people shall inhabit a city of peace, and dwell in it in confidence, and they shall rest with wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esaias 28:17; Esaias 30:30; Esaias 28:2"> And if the hail should come down, it shall not come upon you; and they that dwell in the forests shall be in confidence, as those in the plain country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:1; Esaias 30:23–24; Esaias 55:10–11"> Blessed are they that sow by every water, where the ox and ass tread.</span>
