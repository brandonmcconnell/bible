<div className="bible-chapter" lang="en" dir="ltr" data-lang="en" data-translation="brenton" data-translation-id="eng_bre" data-translation-label="Brenton LXX" data-book="ISA" data-book-slug="isaiah" data-book-name="Esaias" data-chapter="24" data-verses="23">

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Naum 2:10; Esaias 2:19; Esaias 42:15"> Behold, the Lord is about to lay waste the world, and will make it desolate, and will lay bare the surface of it, and scatter them that dwell therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Osee 4:9; Lamentations 5:12–14; Jezekiel 7:12–13"> And the people shall be as the priest, and the servant as the lord, and the maid as the mistress; the buyer shall be as the seller, the lender as the borrower, and the debtor as his creditor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Esaias 24:1; Esaias 6:11; Deuteronomy 29:23"> The earth shall be completely laid waste, and the earth shall be utterly spoiled: for the mouth of the Lord has spoken these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Osee 4:3; Esaias 33:9; Esaias 3:26"> The earth mourns, and the world is ruined, the lofty ones of the earth are mourning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 13:20; Romans 8:20–21; Daniel 7:25"> And she has sinned by reason of her inhabitants; because they have transgressed the law, and changed the ordinances, even the everlasting covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachias 4:1; Matthew 7:14; Zacharias 5:3–4"> Therefore a curse shall consume the earth, because the inhabitants thereof have sinned: therefore the dwellers in the earth shall be poor, and few men shall be left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esaias 16:10; Joel 1:10–12; Esaias 16:8"> The wine shall mourn, the vine shall mourn, all the merry-hearted shall sigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Osee 2:11; Revelation 18:22; Jezekiel 26:13"> The mirth of timbrels has ceased, the sound of the harp has ceased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esaias 5:22; Ecclesiastes 9:7; Esaias 5:20"> They are ashamed, they have not drunk wine; strong drink has become bitter to them that drink it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 18:2; Revelation 17:5–6; Esaias 24:12"> All the city has become desolate: one shall shut his house so that none shall enter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Osee 7:14; Esaias 24:7–9; Jeremias 48:33"> There is a howling for the wine everywhere; all the mirth of the land has ceased, all the mirth of the land has departed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 9:11; Michæas 1:12; Lamentations 5:18"> And cities shall be left desolate, and houses being left shall fall to ruin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Esaias 1:9; Jezekiel 6:8–11; Jezekiel 11:16–20"> All this shall be in the land in the midst of the nations, as if one should strip an olive tree, so shall they strip them; but when the vintage is done,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esaias 40:9; Jeremias 31:12; Esaias 54:1"> these shall cry aloud; and they that are left on the land shall rejoice together in the glory of the Lord: the water of the sea shall be troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Malachias 1:11; Esaias 42:4; Esaias 60:9"> Therefore shall the glory of the Lord be in the isles of the sea; the name of the Lord shall be glorious.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esaias 21:2; Jeremias 5:11; Esaias 33:1"> O Lord God of Israel, from the ends of the earth we have heard wonderful things, and there is hope to the godly: but they shall say, Woe to the despisers, that despise the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremias 48:43–44; Kings III 19:17; Leviticus 26:21–22"> Fear, and a pit, and a snare, are upon you that dwell on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 7:11; Psalms 18:7; Joshua 10:10–11"> And it shall come to pass, that he that flees from the fear shall fall into the pit; and he that comes up out of the pit shall be caught by the snare: for windows have been opened in heaven, and the foundations of the earth shall be shaken,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esaias 34:4–10; Deuteronomy 11:6; Revelation 20:11"> the earth shall be utterly confounded, and the earth shall be completely perplexed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Esaias 19:14; Osee 4:1–5; Daniel 11:19"> It reels as a drunkard and one oppressed with wine, and the earth shall be shaken as a storehouse of fruits; for iniquity has prevailed upon it, and it shall fall, and shall not be able to rise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 76:12; Esaias 34:2–17; Zacharias 14:12–19"> And God shall bring his hand upon the host of heaven, and upon the kings of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zacharias 9:11; Esaias 42:22; Jeremias 38:6–13"> And they shall gather the multitude thereof into prisons, and they shall shut them into a strong hold: after many generations they shall be visited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Esaias 60:19; Revelation 21:23; Esaias 13:10"> And the brick shall decay, and the wall shall fall; for the Lord shall reign<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Heb. and Alex. ‘in’.">a</a></sup>from out of Sion, and out of Jerusalem, and shall be glorified before his elders.</span>
</div>
</div>
