<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 13:11–14; Psalms 85:9; Matthew 3:2"> Thus saith the Lord, Keep ye judgment, and do justice: for my salvation is near to come, and my mercy to be revealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 31:13–16; Esaias 58:13; Psalms 112:1"> Blessed is the man that does these things, and the man that holds by them, and keeps the sabbaths from profaning them, and keeps his hands from doing unrighteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:17; Acts 10:34; Matthew 19:12–30"> Let not the stranger who attaches himself to the Lord, say, Surely the Lord will separate me from his people: and let not the eunuch say, I am a dry tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 119:111; Luke 10:42; Esaias 27:5"> Thus saith the Lord to the eunuchs, as many as shall keep my sabbaths, and choose the things which I take pleasure in, and take hold of my covenant;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 3:12; 1 John 3:1; Revelation 3:5"> I will give to them in my house and within my walls an honourable place, better than sons and daughters: I will give them an everlasting name, and it shall not fail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremias 50:5; Esaias 56:2–4; Esaias 58:13"> And I will give it to the strangers that attach themselves to the Lord, to serve him, and to love the name of the Lord, to be to him servants and handmaids; and as for all that keep my sabbaths from profaning them, and that take hold of my covenant;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 11:17; Esaias 2:2–3; Malachias 1:11"> I will bring them to my holy mountain, and gladden them in my house of prayer: their whole-burnt-offerings and their sacrifices shall be acceptable upon mine altar; for my house shall be called a house of prayer for all nations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:16; Esaias 54:7; Esaias 60:3–11"> saith the Lord that gathers the dispersed of Israel; for I will gather to him a congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremias 12:9; Revelation 19:17–18; Esaias 18:6"> All ye beasts of the field, come, devour, all ye beasts of the forest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esaias 29:10; Philippians 3:2; Esaias 58:1"> See how they are all blinded: they have not known; they are dumb dogs that will not bark; dreaming of rest, loving to slumber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jezekiel 34:2–3; Jezekiel 13:19; Michæas 3:11"> Yea, they are insatiable dogs, that know not what it is to be filled, and they are wicked, having no understanding: all have followed their own ways, each according to his own will.</span>
