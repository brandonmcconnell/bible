<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esaias 41:8; Romans 11:5–6; Jeremias 30:10"> But now hear, Jacob my servant; and Israel, whom I have chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 1:5; Esaias 43:1; Esaias 46:3–4"> Thus saith the Lord God that made thee, and he that formed thee from the womb; Thou shalt yet be helped: fear not, my servant Jacob; and beloved Israel, whom I have chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joel 2:28; Acts 2:17; Esaias 59:21"> For I will give water to the thirsty that walk in a dry land: I will put my Spirit upon thy seed, and my blessings upon thy children:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esaias 58:11; Psalms 1:3; Psalms 92:13–15"> and they shall spring up as grass between brooks, and as willows on the banks of running water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:5; Zacharias 13:9; 1 Peter 2:9"> One shall say, I am God's; and another shall call himself by the name of Jacob; and another shall write with his hand, I am God's, and shall call himself by the name of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esaias 41:4; Esaias 43:10–11; Revelation 1:8"> Thus saith God the King of Israel, and the God of hosts that delivered him; I am the first, and I am hereafter: beside me there is no God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esaias 41:26; Esaias 41:22; Esaias 46:9–10"> Who is like me? let him stand, and call, and declare, and prepare for me from the time that I made man for ever; and let them tell you the things that are coming before they arrive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esaias 43:10; Kings I 2:2; Deuteronomy 4:35"> Hide not yourselves, nor go astray: have ye not heard from the beginning, and have not I told you? ye are witnesses if there is a God beside me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esaias 41:24; Jeremias 10:14–15; Psalms 97:7"> But they that framed false gods did not then hearken; and they that graved images are all vain, performing their own desires, which shall not profit them, but they shall be ashamed</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 10:5; Ambacum 2:18; Acts 19:26"> that form a god, and all that grave worthless things:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esaias 42:17; Esaias 45:16; Psalms 97:7"> and all by whom they were made are withered: yea, let all the deaf be gathered from among men, and let them stand together; and let them be ashamed and confounded together:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esaias 41:6–7; Esaias 40:19; Esaias 46:6–7"> For the artificer sharpens the iron; he fashions the idol with an axe, and fixes it with an awl, and fashions it with the strength of his arm: and he will be hungry and weak, and will drink no water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 17:4–5; Psalms 115:5–7; Acts 17:29"> The artificer having chosen a piece of wood, marks it out with a rule, and fits it with glue, and makes it as the form of a man, and as the beauty of a man, to set it up in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esaias 40:20; Jeremias 10:3–8; Osee 4:12"> He cuts wood out of the forest, which the Lord planted, even a pine tree, and the rain made it grow,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Chronicles II 25:14; Revelation 9:20; Judges 2:19"> that it might be for men to burn: and having taken part of it he warms himself; yea, they burn part of it, and bake loaves thereon; and of the rest they make for themselves gods, and they worship them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Half thereof he burns in the fire, and with half of it he bakes loaves on the coals; and having roasted flesh on it he eats, and is satisfied, and having warmed himself he says, I am comfortable, for I have warmed myself, and have seen the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Esaias 45:20; Kings III 18:26; Daniel 6:27"> And the rest he makes a graven god, and worships, and prays, saying, Deliver me; for thou art my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Esaias 6:9–10; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4; John 12:39–40"> They have no understanding to perceive; for they have been blinded so that they should not see with their eyes, nor perceive with their heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:15; Kings III 11:7; Kings III 11:5"> And one has not considered in his mind, nor known in his understanding, that he has burnt up half of it in the fire, and baked loaves on the coals thereof and has roasted and eaten flesh, and of the rest of it he has made an abomination, and they worship it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 1:28; Job 15:31; Osee 12:1"> Know thou that their heart is ashes, and they err, and no one is able to deliver his soul: see, ye will not say, There is a lie in my right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esaias 44:1–2; Esaias 49:15–16; Esaias 43:15"> Remember these things, O Jacob and Israel; for thou art my servant; I have formed thee to be my servant: and do thou, Israel, not forget me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Esaias 43:25; 1 Corinthians 6:20; Acts 3:18–19"> For behold, I have blotted out as a cloud thy transgressions, and thy sin as darkness: turn to me, and I will redeem thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ephesians 1:6–7; Revelation 5:8–14; Psalms 69:34"> Rejoice, ye heavens; for God has had mercy upon Israel: sound the trumpet, ye foundations of the earth: ye mountains, shout with joy, ye hills, and all the trees therein: for God has redeemed Jacob, and Israel shall be glorified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Esaias 45:12; Esaias 42:5; Esaias 54:5"> Thus saith the Lord that redeems thee, and who formed thee from the womb, I am the Lord that performs all things: I stretched out the heaven alone, and established the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 33:10; Job 5:12–14; Jeremias 50:36"> Who else will frustrate the tokens of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. ventriloquists.">a</a></sup>those that have divining spirits, and prophecies<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Heb.">b</a></sup>from the heart of man? turning the wise back, and making their counsel foolishness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Kings III 18:36–38; Esaias 42:9; Nehemiah 1:3"> and confirming the word of his servant, and verifying the counsel of his messengers: who says to Jerusalem, Thou shalt be inhabited; and to the cities of Idumea, Ye shall be built, and her desert places shall spring forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Esaias 42:15; Jeremias 51:36; Jeremias 50:38"> Who says to the deep, Thou shalt be dried up, and I will dry up the rivers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:22–3; Esaias 45:1; Esaias 45:13"> Who bids Cyrus be wise, and he shall perform all my will: who says to Jerusalem, Thou shalt be built, and I will lay the foundation of my holy house.</span>
