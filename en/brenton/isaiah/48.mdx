<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremias 4:2; Psalms 68:26; Esaias 65:16"> Hear these words, ye house of Jacob, who are called by the name of Israel, and have come forth out of Juda, who swear by the name of the Lord God of Israel, making mention of it, but not with truth, nor with righteousness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Michæas 3:11; Romans 2:17; Esaias 52:1"> maintaining also the name of the holy city, and staying themselves on the God of Israel: the Lord of hosts is his name. The former things I have already declared;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Esaias 42:9; Joshua 21:45; Esaias 41:22"> and they have proceeded out of my mouth, and it became well known; I wrought suddenly, and the events came to pass.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Exodus 32:9; Zacharias 7:11–12"> I know that thou art stubborn, and thy neck is an iron sinew, and thy forehead brazen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esaias 48:3; Luke 1:70; Acts 15:18"> And I told thee<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Alex. the ancient things before they came.">a</a></sup>of old what should be before it came upon thee; I made it known to thee, lest thou shouldest say, My idols have done it for me; and shouldest say, My graven and molten images have commanded me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 4:1; Esaias 42:9; Romans 16:25–26"> Ye have heard all this, but ye have not known: yet I have made<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, audible.">b</a></sup>known to thee the new things from henceforth, which are coming to pass, and thou saidst not,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Now they come to pass, and not formerly: and thou heardest not of them in former days: say not thou, Yea, I know them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 58:3; Osee 5:7; Jeremias 6:10"> Thou hast neither known, nor understood, neither from the beginning have I opened thine ears: for I knew that thou wouldest surely deal treacherously, and wouldest be called a transgressor even from the womb.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 79:9; Psalms 106:8; Psalms 143:11"> For mine own sake will I shew thee my wrath, and will bring before thee my glorious acts, that I may not utterly destroy thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 4:12; Proverbs 17:3; Job 23:10"> Behold, I have sold thee, but not for silver; but I have rescued thee from the furnace of affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esaias 42:8; Jezekiel 20:9; Esaias 37:35"> For mine own sake I will do this for thee, because my name is profaned; and I will not give my glory to another.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esaias 41:4; Revelation 22:13; Revelation 1:8"> Hear me, O Jacob, and Israel whom I call; I am the first, and I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. am.">c</a></sup>endure for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 1:10–12; Psalms 102:25; Esaias 40:26"> My hand also has founded the earth, and my right hand has fixed the sky: I will call them, and they shall stand together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esaias 43:9; Jeremias 50:21–29; Esaias 13:17–18"> And all shall be gathered, and shall hear: who has told them these things? Out of love to thee I have fulfilled thy desire on Babylon, to abolish the seed of the Chaldeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 1:8; Esaias 45:1–2; Psalms 45:4"> I have spoken, I have called, I have brought him, and made his way prosperous.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 18:20; John 20:21–22; John 3:34"> Draw nigh to me, and hear ye these words; I have not spoken in secret from the beginning: when it took place, there was I, and now the Lord, even the Lord, and his Spirit, hath sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 32:8; Psalms 25:12; Job 22:21–22"> Thus saith the Lord that delivered thee, the Holy One of Israel; I am thy God, I have shewn thee how thou shouldest find the way wherein thou shouldest walk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:29; Esaias 66:12; Psalms 119:165"> And if thou hadst hearkened to my commandments, then would thy peace have been like a river, and thy righteousness as a wave of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esaias 10:22; Genesis 22:17; Romans 9:27"> Thy seed also would have been as the sand, and the offspring of thy belly as the dust of the ground: neither now shalt thou by any means be utterly destroyed, neither shall thy name perish before me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremias 51:45; Jeremias 50:8; Esaias 52:9"> Go forth of Babylon, thou that fleest from the Chaldeans: utter aloud a voice of joy, and let this be made known, proclaim it to the end of the earth; say ye, The Lord hath delivered his servant Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 17:6; Esaias 43:19–20; Psalms 105:41"> And if they shall thirst, he shall lead them through the desert; he shall bring forth water to them out of the rock: the rock shall be cloven, and the water shall flow forth, and my people shall drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Esaias 57:21; Job 15:20–24; Luke 19:42"> There is no joy, saith the Lord, to the ungodly.</span>
</div>
