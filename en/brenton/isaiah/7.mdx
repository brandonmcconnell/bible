<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings IV 15:37; Kings IV 16:1; Esaias 1:1"> And it came to pass in the days of Achaz the son of Joatham, the son of Ozias, king of Juda, there came up Rasin king of Aram, and Phakee son of Romelias, king of Israel, against Jerusalem to war against it, but they could not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Lit. besiege See Heb.">a</a></sup>take it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esaias 8:12; Esaias 7:13; Esaias 9:9"> And a message was brought to the house of David, saying, Aram has conspired with Ephraim. And his soul was amazed, and the soul of his people, as in a wood a tree is moved by the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings IV 18:17; Esaias 36:2; Romans 9:27"> And the Lord said to Esaias, Go forth to meet Achaz, thou, and thy son Jasub who is left, to the pool of the upper way of the fuller's field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esaias 35:4; Lamentations 3:26; Deuteronomy 20:3"> And thou shalt say to him, Take care to be quiet, and fear not, neither let thy soul be disheartened because of these two smoking firebrands: for when my fierce anger is over, I will heal again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 83:3–4; Zacharias 1:15; Psalms 2:2"> And as for the son of Aram, and the son of Romelias, forasmuch as they have devised an evil counsel, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> We will go up against Judea, and having conferred with them we will turn them away to our side, and we will make the son of Tabeel king of it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esaias 8:10; Lamentations 3:37; Esaias 46:10–11"> thus saith the Lord of hosts, This counsel shall not abide, nor come to pass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esaias 17:1–3; Genesis 14:15; Kings II 8:6"> But the head of Aram is Damascus, and the head of Damascus, Rasim; and yet within sixty and five years the kingdom of Ephraim shall cease from being a people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 11:6; Romans 11:20; Chronicles II 20:20"> And the head of Ephraim is Somoron, and the head of Somoron the son of Romelias: but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Comp. Heb.">b</a></sup>if ye believe not, neither will ye at all understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esaias 8:5; Esaias 1:5; Esaias 10:20"> And the Lord again spoke to Achaz, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esaias 37:30; Esaias 38:7–8; Esaias 38:22"> Ask for thyself a sign of the Lord thy God, in the depth or in the height.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jezekiel 33:31; Chronicles II 28:22; Malachias 3:15"> And Achaz said, I will not ask, neither will I tempt the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Esaias 43:24; Esaias 63:10; Jezekiel 34:18"> And he said, Hear ye now, O house of David; is it a little thing for you to contend with men? and how do ye contend against the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 1:23; Esaias 9:6; Luke 1:31"> Therefore the Lord himself shall give you a sign; behold, a virgin shall conceive in the womb, and shall bring forth a son, and thou shalt call his name Emmanuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esaias 7:22; Amos 5:15; Matthew 3:4"> Butter and honey shall he eat, before he knows either to prefer evil, or choose the good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esaias 8:4; Deuteronomy 1:39; Esaias 9:11"> For before the child shall know good or evil, he refuses evil, to choose the good; and the land shall be forsaken which thou art afraid of because of the two kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Esaias 8:7–8; Esaias 10:5–6; Chronicles II 28:19–21"> But God shall bring upon thee, and upon thy people, and upon the house of thy father, days which have never come, from the day that Ephraim took away from Juda the king of the Assyrians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Esaias 5:26; Joshua 24:12; Deuteronomy 1:44"> And it shall come to pass in that day that the Lord shall hiss for the flies,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, which part of the enemy, etc. shall rule over the river of Egypt; but according to Alex. the reading in the text is the right one.">c</a></sup>which insect shall rule over a part of the river of Egypt, and for the bee which is in the land of the Assyrians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esaias 2:19; Jeremias 16:16; Esaias 2:21"> And they all shall enter into the clefts of the land, and into the holes of the rocks, and into the caves, and into every ravine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Esaias 8:7; Jezekiel 29:18; Esaias 10:15"> In that day the Lord shall shave with the hired razor of the king of Assyria beyond the river the head, and the hairs of the feet, and will remove the beard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esaias 5:17; Jeremias 39:10; Esaias 37:30"> And it shall come to pass in that day, that a man shall rear a heifer, and two sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Esaias 7:15; Matthew 3:4; Kings II 17:29"> And it shall come to pass from their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Alex. ποιεῖν, 'giving.'">d</a></sup>drinking an abundance of milk, that every one that is left on the land shall eat butter and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Esaias 5:6; Song of Solomon 8:11–12; Matthew 21:33"> And it shall come to pass in that day, for every place where there shall be a thousand vines at a thousand shekels, they shall become<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. for land and for a thorn.">e</a></sup>barren land and thorns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 27:3"> Men shall enter thither with arrow and bow; for all the land shall be barren ground and thorns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Esaias 7:21–22; Esaias 13:20–22; Esaias 5:17"> And every mountain shall be certainly ploughed: there shall no fear come thither: for there shall be from among the barren ground and thorns that whereon cattle shall feed and oxen shall tread.</span>
