<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 4:27; Esaias 62:4; Kings I 2:5"> Rejoice, thou barren that bearest not; break forth and cry, thou that dost not travail: for more are the children of the desolate than of her that has a husband: for the Lord has said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esaias 33:20; Jeremias 10:20; Esaias 49:19–20"> Enlarge the place of thy tent, and of thy curtains: fix the pins, spare not, lengthen thy cords, and strengthen thy pins;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jezekiel 36:35–36; Genesis 28:14; Esaias 43:5–6"> spread forth thy tent yet to the right and the left: for thy seed shall inherit the Gentiles, and thou shalt make the desolate cities to be inhabited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esaias 41:10; 1 Peter 2:6; Jeremias 31:19"> Fear not, because thou hast been put to shame, neither be confounded, because thou wast reproached: for thou shalt forget thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. ancient Or, everlasting.">a</a></sup>former shame, and shalt no more at all remember the reproach of thy widowhood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 5:25–27; Osee 2:19–20; 2 Corinthians 11:2–3"> For it is the Lord that made thee; the Lord of hosts is his name: and he that delivered thee, he is the God of Israel, and shall be called so by the whole earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esaias 62:4; Ecclesiastes 9:9; Osee 2:14–15"> The Lord has not called thee as a deserted and faint-hearted woman, nor as a woman hated from her youth, saith thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 30:5; 2 Peter 3:8; Michæas 4:6"> For a little while I left thee: but with great mercy will I have compassion upon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremias 31:3; Esaias 60:10; Jezekiel 39:23–24"> In a little wrath I turned away my face from thee; but with everlasting mercy will I have compassion upon thee, saith the Lord that delivers thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esaias 12:1; Genesis 8:21; Hebrews 6:16–18"> From the time of the water of Noe this is my purpose: as I sware to him at that time, saying of the earth, I will no more be wroth with thee, neither when thou art threatened,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 89:33–34; Psalms 46:2; Ephesians 2:4–5"> shall the mountains depart, nor shall thy hills be removed: so neither shall my mercy fail thee, nor shall the covenant of thy peace be at all removed: for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Alex. κύριος for κύριε adopted here. Compare Mat. 16. 22, with this passage.">b</a></sup>the Lord who is gracious to thee has spoken it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4–6; Acts 14:22; Jeremias 30:17"> Afflicted and outcast thou hast not been comforted: behold, I will prepare carbuncle for thy stones, and sapphire for thy foundations;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and I will make thy buttresses jasper, and thy gates crystal, and thy border precious stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 6:45; Philippians 4:7; Psalms 119:165"> And I will cause all thy sons to be taught of God, and thy children to be in great peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 3:25–26; Jeremias 30:10; Zacharias 9:8"> And thou shalt be built in righteousness: abstain from injustice, and thou shalt not fear; and trembling shall not come nigh thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esaias 41:11–16; Psalms 37:12–13; Zacharias 12:9"> Behold, strangers shall come to thee by me, and shall sojourn with thee, and shall run to thee for refuge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 9:16; Proverbs 16:4; Esaias 46:11"> Behold, I have created thee, not as the coppersmith blowing coals, and bringing out a vessel fit for work; but I have created thee, not for ruin, that I should destroy thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 8:28–39; Esaias 54:15; John 10:28–30"> I will not suffer any<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. instrument.">c</a></sup>weapon formed against thee to prosper; and every voice that shall rise up against thee for judgment, thou shalt vanquish them all; and thine adversaries shall be condemned thereby. There is an inheritance to them that serve the Lord, and ye shall be righteous before me, saith the Lord.</span>
