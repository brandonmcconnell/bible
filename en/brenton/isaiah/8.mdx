<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esaias 30:8; Job 19:23–24; Ambacum 2:2–3"> And the Lord said to me, Take to thyself a volume of a great new<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Alex. paper, or, parchment.">a</a></sup>book, and write in it with a man's pen concerning the making a rapid plunder of spoils; for it is near at hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings IV 16:10–11; Kings IV 18:2; 2 Corinthians 13:1"> And make me witnesses of faithful men, Urias, and Zacharias the son of Barachias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Osee 1:3–9; Judges 4:4; Esaias 7:13–14"> And I went in to the prophetess; and she conceived, and bore a son. And the Lord said to me, Call his name, Spoil quickly, plunder speedily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 9:11; Kings IV 16:9; Deuteronomy 1:39"> For before the child shall know how to call his father or his mother, one shall take the power of Damascus and the spoils of Samaria before the king of the Assyrians.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esaias 7:10"> And the Lord spoke to me yet again, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 9:7; Nehemiah 3:15; Esaias 7:6"> Because this people chooses not the water of Siloam that goes softly, but wills to have Rassin, and the son of Romelias to be king over you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esaias 17:12–13; Genesis 6:17; Esaias 7:17"> therefore, behold, the Lord brings up upon you the water of the river, strong and abundant, even the king of the Assyrians, and his glory: and he shall come up over every valley of yours, and shall walk over every wall of yours:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esaias 7:14; Esaias 30:28; Matthew 1:23"> and he shall take away from Juda every man who shall be able to lift up his head, and every one able to accomplish anything; and his camp shall fill the breadth of thy land, O<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Heb. Immanuel.">b</a></sup>God with us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esaias 14:5–6; Proverbs 11:21; Psalms 37:14–15"> Know, ye Gentiles, and be conquered; hearken ye, even to the extremity of the earth: be conquered, after ye have strengthened yourselves; for even if ye should again strengthen yourselves, ye shall again be conquered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Lamentations 3:37; Romans 8:31; Job 5:12"> And whatsoever counsel ye shall take, the Lord shall bring it to nought; and whatsoever word ye shall speak, it shall not stand among you: for God is with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jezekiel 3:14; Proverbs 1:15; Jeremias 15:19"> Thus saith the Lord, With a strong hand they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. disobey.">c</a></sup>revolt from the course of the way of this people, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:14–15; Luke 21:9; Psalms 53:5"> Let them not say, It is hard, for whatsoever this people says, is hard: but fear not ye their fear, neither be dismayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 12:5; Esaias 26:3–4; Revelation 15:4"> Sanctify ye the Lord himself; and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or, let him be.">d</a></sup>he shall be thy fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 2:8; Romans 9:32–33; Esaias 28:16"> And if thou shalt trust in him, he shall be to thee for a sanctuary; and ye shall not come against him as against a stumbling-stone, neither as against the falling of a rock: but the houses of Jacob are in a snare, and the dwellers in Jerusalem in a pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 21:44; Luke 20:17–18; Esaias 28:13"> Therefore many among them shall be weak, and fall, and be crushed; and they shall draw nigh, and men shall be taken securely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 12:4; Hebrews 3:5; Mark 4:10–11"> Then shall those who seal themselves that they may<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Alex. — 'not.'">e</a></sup>not learn the law be made manifest.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36–39; Ambacum 2:3; Lamentations 3:25–26"> And one shall say, I will wait for God, who has turned away his face from the house of Jacob, and I will trust in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 2:13–14; Luke 2:34; Zacharias 8:3"> Behold I and the children which God has given me: and they shall be for signs and wonders in the house of Israel from the Lord of hosts, who dwells in mount Sion.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Chronicles I 10:13; Leviticus 20:6; Chronicles II 33:6"> And if they should say to you, Seek<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. ventriloquists.">f</a></sup>those who have in them a divining spirit, and them that speak out of the earth, them that speak vain words, who speak out of their belly: shall not a nation diligently seek to their God? why do they seek to the dead concerning the living?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 1:19; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; Esaias 30:8–11"> For he has given the law for a help, that they should not speak according to this word, concerning which there are no<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="See Heb.">g</a></sup>gifts to give for it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esaias 9:20; Proverbs 19:3; Lamentations 4:4–5"> And famine shall come sorely upon you, and it shall come to pass, that when ye shall be hungry, ye shall be grieved, and ye shall speak ill of the prince and your fathers' ordinances: and they shall look up to heaven above,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Esaias 5:30; Matthew 22:13; Jeremias 13:16"> and they shall look on the earth below, and behold severe distress, and darkness, affliction, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or, a strait.">h</a></sup>anguish, and darkness so that one cannot see; and he that is in anguish shall not be distressed only for a time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Drink this first.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or, do it quickly, i. e. ‘drink’; See Hebrew.">i</a></sup>Act quickly, O land of Zabulon, land of Nephthalim, and the rest inhabiting the sea-coast, and the land beyond Jordan, Galilee of the Gentiles.</span>
</div>
</div>
