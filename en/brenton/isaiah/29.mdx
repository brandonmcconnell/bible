<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 4:4–5; Kings II 5:9; Michæas 6:6–7"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, woe to.">a</a></sup>Alas for the city Ariel, which David<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Heb.">b</a></sup>besieged. Gather ye fruits year by year; eat ye, for ye shall eat with Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esaias 5:25–30; Esaias 3:26; Esaias 10:32"> For I will grievously afflict Ariel: and her strength and her wealth shall be mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 19:43–44; Jezekiel 21:22; Kings IV 25:1–4"> And I will compass thee about like David, and will raise a mound about thee, and set up towers round thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esaias 8:19; Psalms 44:25; Esaias 3:8"> And thy words shall be brought down to the earth, and thy words shall sink down to the earth, and thy voice shall be as they that speak out of the earth, and thy voice shall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. become weak.">c</a></sup>be lowered to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Esaias 17:13–14; Esaias 37:36"> But the wealth of the ungodly shall be as dust from a wheel, and the multitude of them that oppress thee as flying chaff, and it shall be suddenly as a moment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 13:8; Kings I 2:10; Matthew 24:7"> from the Lord of hosts: for there shall be a visitation with thunder, and earthquake, and a loud<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. voice.">d</a></sup>noise, a rushing tempest, and devouring flame of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 20:8; Michæas 4:11–12; Zacharias 12:9"> And the wealth of all the nations together, as many as have fought against Ariel, and all they that war against Jerusalem, and all who are gathered against her, and they that distress her, shall be as one that dreams in sleep by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esaias 44:12; Psalms 73:20; Esaias 10:7–16"> And as men drink and eat in sleep, and when they have arisen, the dream is vain: and as a thirsty man dreams as if he drank, and having arisen is still thirsty, and his soul has desired in vain: so shall be the wealth of all the nations, as many as have fought against the mount Sion.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esaias 51:21–22; Esaias 19:14; Revelation 17:6"> Faint ye, and be amazed, and be overpowered, not with strong drink nor with wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 11:8; Esaias 44:18; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–12"> For the Lord has made you to drink a spirit of deep sleep; and he shall close their eyes, and the eyes of their prophets and of their rulers, who see secret things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 13:11; Daniel 12:4; Esaias 8:16"> And all these things shall be to you as the words of this sealed book, which if they shall give to a learned man, saying, Read this, he shall then say, I cannot read it, for it is sealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 7:15–16; Esaias 29:18; Jeremias 5:4"> And this book shall be given into the hands of a man that is unlearned, and one shall say to him, Read this; and he shall say, I am not learned.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremias 12:2; Jezekiel 33:31–33; Mark 7:2–13"> And the Lord has said, This people draw nigh to me with their mouth, and they honour me with their lips, but their heart is far from me: but in vain do they worship me, teaching the commandments and doctrines of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ambacum 1:5; 1 Corinthians 1:19–24; Esaias 28:21"> Therefore behold I will proceed to remove this people, and I will remove them: and I will destroy the wisdom of the wise, and will hide the understanding of the prudent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jezekiel 8:12; Esaias 47:10; Esaias 30:1"> Woe to them that deepen their counsel, and not by the Lord. Woe to them that take secret counsel, and whose works<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. shall be.">e</a></sup>are in darkness, and they say, Who has seen us? and who shall know us, or what we do?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 9:19–21; Esaias 64:8; Esaias 45:9–11"> Shall ye not be counted as clay of the potter? Shall the thing formed say to him that formed it, Thou didst not form me? or the work to the maker, Thou hast not made me wisely?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Esaias 32:15; Matthew 19:30; Hebrews 10:37"> Is it not yet a little while, and Libanus shall be changed as the mountain of Chermel, and Chermel shall be reckoned as a forest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Esaias 35:5; Matthew 11:5; Mark 7:37"> And in that day the deaf shall hear the words of the book, and they that are in darkness, and they that are in mist: the eyes of the blind shall see,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="James 2:5; Esaias 61:1; Matthew 11:29"> and the poor shall rejoice with joy because of the Lord, and they that had no hope among men shall be filled with joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Michæas 2:1; Esaias 28:14–22; Luke 16:14"> The lawless man has come to nought, and the proud man has perished, and they that transgress mischievously have been utterly destroyed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Amos 5:10–12; James 5:6; Acts 3:14"> and they that cause men to sin by a word: and men shall make all that reprove in the gates an offence, because they have unjustly turned aside the righteous.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Esaias 45:17; Esaias 51:2; Jeremias 30:10"> Therefore thus saith the Lord concerning the house of Jacob, whom he set apart from Abraam, Jacob shall not now be ashamed, neither shall he now change countenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; Esaias 19:25; Esaias 45:11"> But when their children shall have seen my works, they shall sanctify my name for my sake, and they shall sanctify the Holy One of Jacob, and shall fear the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 15:17–19; Hebrews 5:2; Esaias 28:7"> And they that erred in spirit shall know understanding, and the murmurers shall learn obedience, and the stammering tongues shall learn to speak peace.</span>
</div>
</div>
