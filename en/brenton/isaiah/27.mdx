<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esaias 51:9; Jezekiel 29:3; Esaias 66:16"> In that day God shall bring his holy and great and strong sword upon the dragon, even the serpent that flees, upon the dragon, the crooked serpent: he shall destroy the dragon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 2:21; Luke 20:9–18; Esaias 5:1–7"> In that day there shall be a fair vineyard, and a desire to commence a song concerning it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Esaias 58:11; John 15:1–2; Kings I 2:9"> I am a strong city, a city in a siege: in vain shall I water it; for it shall be taken by night, and by day the wall shall fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esaias 10:17; Hebrews 6:8; Matthew 3:12"> There is no woman that has not taken hold of it; who will set me to watch stubble in the field? because of this enemy I have set her aside; therefore on this account the Lord has done all that he appointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esaias 25:4; Job 22:21; Ephesians 2:16–17"> I am burnt up; they that dwell in her shall cry, Let us make peace with him, let us make peace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esaias 37:31; Osee 14:5–6; Romans 11:16–26"> they that are coming are the children of Jacob. Israel shall bud and blossom, and the world shall be filled with his fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esaias 37:36–38; Jeremias 51:24; Esaias 10:20–25"> Shall he himself be thus smitten, even as he smote? and as he slew, shall he be thus slain?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremias 10:24; Jeremias 4:11; Jezekiel 19:12"> Fighting and reproaching he will dismiss them; didst thou not meditate with a harsh spirit, to slay them with a wrathful spirit?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 11:27; Esaias 48:10; Esaias 17:8"> Therefore shall the iniquity of Jacob be taken away; and this is his blessing, when I shall have taken away his sin; when they shall have broken to pieces all the stones of the altars as fine dust, and their trees shall not remain, and their idols shall be cut off, as a thicket afar off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esaias 17:2; Jeremias 26:18; Esaias 32:13–14"> The flock that dwelt there shall be left, as a deserted flock; and the ground shall be for a long time for pasture, and there shall flocks lie down to rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremias 8:7; Esaias 1:3; Esaias 43:1"> And after a time there shall be in it no green thing because of the grass being parched. Come hither, ye women that come<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Heb.">a</a></sup>from a sight; for it is a people of no understanding; therefore he that made them shall have no pity upon them, and he that formed them shall have no mercy upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; Deuteronomy 30:3–4; Esaias 56:8"> And it shall come to pass in that day that God shall fence men off from the channel of the river as far as Rhinocorura; but do ye gather one by one the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 24:31; Leviticus 25:9; Hebrews 12:22"> And it shall come to pass in that day, that they shall blow the great trumpet, and the lost ones in the land of the Assyrians shall come, and the lost ones in Egypt, and shall worship the Lord on the holy mountain in Jerusalem.</span>
