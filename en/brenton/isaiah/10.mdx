<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 11:52; Michæas 3:1–4; Matthew 23:23"> Woe to them that write wickedness; for when they write they do write wickedness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Malachias 3:5; Esaias 3:14; Esaias 29:21"> perverting the cause of the poor, violently wresting the judgment of the needy ones of my people, that the widow may be a prey to them, and the orphan a spoil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Osee 9:7; Luke 19:44; Job 31:14"> And what will they do in the day of visitation? for affliction shall come to you from afar: and to whom will ye flee for help? and where will ye leave your glory,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esaias 5:25; Esaias 9:12; Jeremias 37:10"> that ye may not fall into<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Heb. and Alex. + ‘and they shall fall under the slain’.">a</a></sup>captivity?<br />For all this his wrath is not turned away, but his hand is yet exalted.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 51:20–24; Esaias 14:25; Esaias 30:30"> Woe to the Assyrians; the rod of my wrath, and anger are in their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esaias 9:17; Jeremias 25:9; Zacharias 10:5"> I will send my wrath against a sinful nation, and I will charge my people to take plunder and spoil, and to trample the cities, and to make them dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 50:20; Michæas 4:11–12; Acts 2:23"> But he meant not thus, neither did he devise thus in his soul: but his mind shall change, and that to destroy nations not a few.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings IV 18:24; Kings IV 19:10; Jezekiel 26:7"> And if they should say to him, Thou alone art ruler;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Chronicles II 35:20; Kings IV 16:9; Genesis 10:10"> then shall he say, Have I not taken the country above Babylon and Chalanes, where the tower was built? and have I not taken Arabia, and Damascus, and Samaria?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esaias 10:14; Kings IV 19:17–19; Kings IV 18:33–35"> As I have taken them, I will also take all the kingdoms: howl, ye idols in Jerusalem, and in Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esaias 37:10–13; Esaias 2:8; Esaias 36:19–20"> For as I did to Samaria and her idols, so will I do also to Jerusalem and her idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings IV 19:31; Esaias 65:7; Jeremias 50:18"> And it shall come to pass, when the Lord shall have finished doing all things on mount Sion and Jerusalem, that I will visit upon the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. great mind.">b</a></sup>proud heart, even upon the ruler of the Assyrians, and upon the boastful haughtiness of his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:17; Daniel 4:30; Jezekiel 26:2"> For he said, I will act in strength, and in the wisdom of my understanding I will remove the boundaries of nations, and will spoil their strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 31:25; Proverbs 21:6–7; Naum 2:9–1"> And I will shake the inhabited cities: and I will take with my hand all the world as a nest: and I will even take them as eggs that have been left; and there is none that shall escape me, or contradict me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 9:20–21; Esaias 45:9; Esaias 10:5"> Shall the axe glorify itself without him that hews with it? or shall the saw lift up itself without him that uses it, as if one should lift a rod or staff? but it shall not be so;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Chronicles II 32:21; Acts 12:23; Psalms 106:15"> but the Lord of hosts shall send dishonour upon thine honour, and burning fire shall be kindled upon thy glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Esaias 27:4; Numbers 11:1–3; Esaias 37:23"> And the light of Israel shall be for a fire, and he shall sanctify him with burning fire, and it shall devour the wood as grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings IV 19:23; Esaias 10:33–34; Esaias 9:18"> In that day the mountains shall be consumed, and the hills, and the forests, and fire shall devour both soul and body: and he that flees shall be as one fleeing from burning flame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esaias 21:17; Esaias 37:36"> And they that are left of them shall be a small number, and a child shall write them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Esaias 37:31–32; Esaias 50:10; Esaias 1:9"> And it shall come to pass in that day that the remnant of Israel shall no more<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, repeat their offence.">c</a></sup>join themselves with, and the saved of Jacob shall no more trust in, them that injured them; but they shall trust in the Holy God of Israel, in truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esaias 9:6; 2 Corinthians 3:14–16; Osee 7:10"> And the remnant of Jacob shall trust on the mighty God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 9:27–28; Esaias 6:13; Revelation 20:8"> And though the people of Israel be as the sand of the sea, a remnant of them shall be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Esaias 28:22; Esaias 24:1–23; Romans 9:28"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. finishing — cutting.">d</a></sup>He will finish the work, and cut it short in righteousness: because the Lord will make a short work in all the world.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 12:22–24; Esaias 37:6; Esaias 61:3"> Therefore thus saith the Lord of hosts, Be not afraid, my people who dwell in Sion, of the Assyrians, because he shall smite thee with a rod: for I am bringing a stroke upon thee, that thou mayest see the way of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 11:36; Kings IV 19:35; Hebrews 10:37"> For yet a little while, and the indignation shall cease: but my wrath shall be against their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="sc. of their enemies.">e</a></sup>council.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Esaias 9:4; Judges 7:25; Kings IV 19:35"> And God will stir up enemies against them, according to the stroke of Madiam in the place of affliction: and his wrath shall be by the way of the sea, even to the way that leads to Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Esaias 9:4; Luke 4:18; Esaias 14:25"> And it shall come to pass in that day, that his yoke shall be taken away from thy shoulder, and his fear from thee, and the yoke shall be destroyed from off your shoulders.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Kings I 14:2; Kings I 13:2; Kings I 13:5"> For he shall arrive at the city of Angai, and shall pass on to Maggedo, and shall lay up his stores in Machmas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Kings I 13:23; Kings I 7:17; Joshua 21:17"> And he shall pass by the valley, and shall arrive at Angai: fear shall seize upon Rama, the city of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Kings I 25:44; Jeremias 1:1; Joshua 21:18"> The daughter of Gallim shall flee; Laisa shall hear; one shall hear in Anathoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 15:31"> Madebena also is amazed, and the inhabitants of Gibbir.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Kings I 21:1; Esaias 37:22; Esaias 19:16"> Exhort ye them to-day to remain in the way: exhort ye beckoning with the hand the mountain, the daughter of Sion, even ye hills that are in Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Amos 2:9; Daniel 4:37; Esaias 37:38"> Behold, the Lord, the Lord of hosts, will mightily confound the glorious ones; and the haughty in pride shall be crushed, and the lofty shall be brought low:</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Esaias 37:24; Daniel 4:23; Jeremias 48:2"> and the lofty ones shall fall by the sword, and Libanus shall fall with his lofty ones.</span>
</div>
