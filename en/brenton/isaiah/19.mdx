<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 12:12; Revelation 1:7; Joshua 2:11"> <span className="desc">The Vision of Egypt.</span><br />Behold, the Lord sits on a swift cloud, and shall come to Egypt: and the idols of Egypt shall be moved at his presence, and their heart shall faint within them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 7:22; Kings I 14:20; Matthew 10:36"> And the Egyptians shall be stirred up against the Egyptians: and a man shall fight against his brother, and a man against his neighbour, city against city, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Alex. ‘district,’ the accent being different.">a</a></sup>law against<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Alex. ‘district,’ the accent being different.">b</a></sup>law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Esaias 8:19; Daniel 2:2; Chronicles I 10:13"> And the spirit of the Egyptians shall be troubled within them; and I will frustrate their counsel: and they shall enquire of their gods and their images, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. the ventriloquists.">c</a></sup>them that speak out of the earth, and them that have in them a divining spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esaias 20:4; Jeremias 46:26; Jezekiel 29:19"> And I will deliver Egypt into the hands of men, of cruel lords; and cruel kings shall rule over them: thus saith the Lord of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 51:36; Jezekiel 30:12; Zacharias 14:18"> And the Egyptians shall drink the water that is by the sea, but the river shall fail, and be dried up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esaias 37:25; Exodus 7:18; Kings IV 19:24"> And the streams shall fail, and the canals of the river; and every<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Lit. gathering; See Gen. 1. 9. also Jer. 28. 32.">d</a></sup>reservoir of water shall be dried up, in every marsh also of reed and papyrus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esaias 32:20; Joel 1:17–18; Esaias 23:3"> And all the green herbage round about the river, and everything sown by the side of the river, shall be blasted with the wind and dried up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jezekiel 47:10; Ambacum 1:15; Numbers 11:5"> And the fishermen shall groan, and all that cast a hook into the river shall groan; they also that cast nets, and the anglers shall mourn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jezekiel 27:7; Proverbs 7:16; Kings III 10:28"> And shame shall come upon them that work fine flax, and them that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. work at.">e</a></sup>make fine linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:10; Exodus 7:19; Exodus 8:5"> And they that work at them shall be in pain, and all that make beer shall be grieved, and be pained in their souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 13:22; Acts 7:22; Kings III 4:30"> And the princes of Tanis shall be fools: as for the king's wise counsellors, their counsel shall be turned into folly: how will ye say to the king, We are sons of wise men, sons of ancient kings?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:20; Esaias 14:24; Judges 9:38"> Where are now thy wise men? and let them declare to thee, and say, What has the Lord of hosts purposed upon Egypt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jezekiel 30:13; Jeremias 2:16; Jeremias 46:14"> The princes of Tanis have failed, and the princes of Memphis are lifted up with pride, and they shall cause Egypt to wander by tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:11; Kings III 22:20–23; Jezekiel 14:7–9"> For the Lord has prepared for them a spirit of error, and they have caused Egypt to err in all their works, as one staggers who is drunken and vomits also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esaias 9:14–15; 1 Thessalonians 4:11–12; Proverbs 14:23"> And there shall be no work to the Egyptians, which shall make head or tail, or beginning or end.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremias 51:30; Naum 3:13; Esaias 11:15"> But in that day the Egyptians shall be as women, in fear and in trembling because of the hand of the Lord of hosts, which he shall bring upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Esaias 14:24; Daniel 4:35; Jezekiel 29:6–7"> And the land of the Jews shall be for a terror to the Egyptians: whosoever shall name it to them, they shall fear, because of the counsel which the Lord of hosts has purposed concerning it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Sophonias 3:9; Zacharias 2:11; Esaias 19:21"> In that day there shall be five cities in Egypt speaking the language of Chanaan, and swearing by the name of the Lord of hosts; one city shall be called the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Heb. city of destruction.">f</a></sup>city of Asedec.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 28:18; Joshua 22:10; Joshua 22:26"> In that day there shall be an altar to the Lord in the land of the Egyptians, and a pillar to the Lord by its border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Titus 2:13; Esaias 45:21–22"> And it shall be for a sign to the Lord for ever in the land of Egypt: for they shall presently cry to the Lord by reason of them that afflict them, and he shall send them a man who shall save them; he shall judge and save them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esaias 11:9; Malachias 1:11; John 17:3"> And the Lord shall be known to the Egyptians, and the Egyptians shall know the Lord in that day; and they shall offer sacrifices, and shall vow vows to the Lord, and pay them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Hebrews 12:11; Osee 14:1"> And the Lord shall smite the Egyptians with a stroke, and shall completely heal them: and they shall return to the Lord, and he shall hear them, and thoroughly heal them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Esaias 11:16; Ephesians 2:18–22; Esaias 35:8–10"> In that day there shall be a way from Egypt to the Assyrians, and the Assyrians shall enter into Egypt, and the Egyptians shall go to the Assyrians, and the Egyptians shall serve the Assyrians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Esaias 65:8; Genesis 12:2; Galatians 3:14"> In that day shall Israel be third with the Egyptians and the Assyrians, blessed in the land which the Lord of hosts has blessed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Esaias 29:23; Osee 2:23; Romans 9:24–25"> saying, Blessed be my people that is in Egypt, and that is among the Assyrians, and Israel mine inheritance.</span>
</div>
