<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 4:8; Jezekiel 1:11; Psalms 104:4"> And seraphs stood round about him: each one had six wings: and with two they covered their face, and with two they covered their feet, and with two they flew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 4:8–9; Psalms 72:19; Numbers 14:21"> And one cried to the other, and they said, Holy, holy, holy, is the Lord of hosts: the whole earth is full of his glory.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 15:8; Exodus 40:34; Chronicles II 5:13–1"> And the lintel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. was lifted.">a</a></sup>shook at the voice they uttered, and the house was filled with smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 5:8–9; James 3:6–10; Jeremias 9:3–8"> And I said, Woe is me, for I am pricked to the heart; for being a man, and having unclean lips, I dwell in the midst of a people having unclean lips; and I have seen with mine eyes the King, the Lord of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 8:3–5; Hebrews 9:22–26; Jezekiel 10:2"> And there was sent to me one of the seraphs, and he had in his hand a coal, which he had taken off the altar with the tongs:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremias 1:9; 1 John 1:7; Esaias 43:25"> and he touched my mouth, and said, Behold, this has touched thy lips, and will take away thine iniquities, and will purge off thy sins.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 4:10–13; Acts 26:16–17; Ephesians 3:8"> And I heard the voice of the Lord, saying, Whom<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, should I send?">b</a></sup>shall I send, and who will go to this people? And I said, Behold, I am here, send me. And he said, Go, and say to this people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 13:14–15; Luke 8:10; Mark 4:12"> Ye shall hear indeed, but ye shall not understand; and ye shall see indeed, but ye shall not perceive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 13:15; Acts 28:27; Jeremias 5:21"> For the heart of this people has become gross, and their ears are dull of hearing, and their eyes have they closed; lest they should see with their eyes, and hear with their ears, and understand with their heart, and be converted, and I should heal them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esaias 24:1–12; Psalms 94:3; Leviticus 26:31"> And I said, How long, O Lord? And he said, Until the cities be deserted<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Compare use of παρὰ, Jer. 40. 10,12;also 1 Cor. 12. 15,16.">c</a></sup>by reason of their not being inhabited, and the houses by reason of there being no men, and the land shall be left desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 4:29; Deuteronomy 28:64; Kings IV 25:11"> And after this God shall remove the men far off, and they that are left upon the land shall be multiplied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 14:7–9; Romans 11:5–6; Genesis 22:18"> And yet there<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. is.">d</a></sup>shall be a tenth upon it, and again it shall be for a spoil, as a turpentine tree, and as an acorn when it falls out of its husk.</span>
