<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremias 51:44; Esaias 21:9; Jeremias 50:2"> Bel has fallen, Nabo is broken to pieces, their graven images are gone to the wild beasts and the cattle: ye take them packed up as a burden to the weary, exhausted, hungry, and at the same time helpless man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 48:7; Judges 18:17–18; Kings II 5:21"> who will not be able to save themselves from war, but they themselves are led away captive.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:31; Esaias 51:7; Esaias 51:1"> Hear me, O house of Jacob, and all the remnant of Israel, who are borne by me from the womb, and taught by me from infancy, even to old age:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 71:18; Psalms 48:14; Psalms 92:14"> I am he; and until ye shall have grown old, I am he: I bear you, I have made, and I will<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, put up with.">a</a></sup>relieve, I will take up and save you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esaias 40:18; Esaias 40:25; Psalms 89:6"> To whom have ye compared me? see, consider, ye that go astray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 17:29; Esaias 40:19–20; Ambacum 2:18–20"> They that furnish gold out of a purse, and silver by weight, will weigh it in a scale, and they hire a goldsmith and make<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. things made with hands.">b</a></sup>idols, and bow down, and worship them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esaias 45:20; Jeremias 10:5; Esaias 46:1"> They bear it upon the shoulder, and go; and if they put it upon its place, it remains, it cannot move: and whosoever shall cry to it, it cannot hear; it cannot save him from trouble.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esaias 44:18–21; Psalms 115:8; Jezekiel 18:28"> Remember ye these things, and groan: repent, ye that have gone astray, return in your heart;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 111:4; Esaias 42:9; Esaias 45:21–22"> and remember the former things that were of old: for I am God, and there is none other beside me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Psalms 33:11; Ephesians 1:9–11"> telling beforehand the latter events before they come to pass, and they are accomplished together: and I said, All my counsel shall stand, and I will do all things that I have planned:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Esaias 14:24–27; Job 23:13"> calling a bird from the east, and from a land afar off, for the things which I have planned: I have spoken, and brought him; I have created and made him; I have brought him, and prospered his way.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 2:5; Esaias 46:3; Psalms 76:5"> Hearken to me, ye senseless ones, that are far from righteousness:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Esaias 62:11; Esaias 12:2; Esaias 61:3"> I have brought near my righteousness, and I will not be slow with the salvation that is from me: I have given salvation in Sion to Israel for glory.</span>
</div>
