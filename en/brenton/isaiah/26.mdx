<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esaias 60:18; Esaias 62:11; Ephesians 5:19–20"> In that day they shall sing this song in the land of Judea; Behold a strong city; and he shall make salvation its wall and bulwark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 5:9; Esaias 60:11; 1 Peter 2:9"> Open ye the gates, let the nation enter that keeps righteousness, and keeps truth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 4:7; John 16:33; John 14:27"> supporting truth, and keeping peace: for on thee, O Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esaias 12:2; Psalms 62:8; Psalms 55:22"> they have trusted with confidence for ever, the great, the eternal God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 51:37; Revelation 18:2; Job 40:11–13"> who hast humbled and brought down them that dwell on high, thou shalt cast down strong cities, and bring them to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 16:20; Luke 10:19; Revelation 2:26"> And the feet of the meek and lowly shall trample them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; Esaias 42:16; 1 John 3:7"> The way of the godly is made straight: the way of the godly is also prepared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 37:3–7; James 5:7–11; Esaias 33:2"> For the way of the Lord is judgment: we have hoped in thy name, and on the remembrance of thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Psalms 63:1; Psalms 119:62"> which our soul longs for: my spirit seeks thee very early in the morning, O God, for thy commandments are a light on the earth: learn righteousness, ye that dwell upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Osee 11:7; John 5:37–38; Romans 2:4–5"> For the ungodly one is put down: no one who will not learn righteousness on the earth, shall be able to do the truth: let the ungodly be taken away, that he see not the glory of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:27; Michæas 5:9; Esaias 5:24"> O Lord, thine arm is exalted, yet they knew it not: but when they know they shall be ashamed: jealousy shall seize upon an untaught nation, and now fire shall devour the adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; Psalms 57:2; Psalms 29:11"> O Lord our God, give us peace: for thou hast rendered to us all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Esaias 63:7; Esaias 2:8; Esaias 10:11"> O Lord our God, take possession of us: O Lord, we know not any other beside thee: we name thy name.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 18:2–3; Proverbs 10:7; Esaias 51:12–13"> But the dead shall not see life, neither shall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Job 26. 5.">a</a></sup>physicians by any means raise them up: therefore thou hast brought wrath upon them, and slain them, and hast taken away every male of them. Bring more evils upon them, O Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esaias 9:3; Esaias 33:17; Nehemiah 9:23"> bring more evils on the glorious ones of the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Osee 5:15; Psalms 50:15; Psalms 77:1–2"> Lord, in affliction I remembered thee; thy chastening was to us with small affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Esaias 21:3; Esaias 13:8; John 16:21"> And as a woman in travail draws nigh to be delivered, and cries out in her pain; so have we been to thy beloved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Osee 13:13; Psalms 17:14; Kings IV 19:3"> We have conceived, O Lord, because of thy fear, and have been in pain, and have brought forth the breath of thy salvation, which we have wrought upon the earth: we shall not fall, but all that dwell upon the land shall fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; Jezekiel 37:1–14; Matthew 27:52"> The dead shall rise, and they that are in the tombs shall be raised, and they that are in the earth shall rejoice: for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Ps. 110.">b</a></sup>the dew from thee is healing to them: but the land of the ungodly shall perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 30:5; Psalms 27:5; Esaias 54:7–8"> Go, my people, enter into thy closets, shut thy door, hide thyself for a little season, until the anger of the Lord have passed away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 6:9–11; Esaias 13:11; Jude 1:14–15"> For, behold, the Lord is bringing wrath from his holy place upon the dwellers on the earth: the earth also shall disclose her blood, and shall not cover her slain.</span>
</div>
