<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:30; Jeremias 3:8; Matthew 18:25"> Thus saith the Lord, Of what kind is your mother's bill of divorcement, by which I put her away? or to which debtor have I sold you? Behold, ye are sold for your sins, and for your iniquities have I put your mother away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esaias 59:1; Esaias 66:4; Numbers 11:23"> Why did I come, and there was no man? why did I call, and there was none to hearken? Is not my hand strong to redeem? or can I not deliver? behold, by my rebuke I will dry up the sea, and make rivers a wilderness; and their fish shall be dried up because there is no water, and shall die for thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 6:12; Psalms 18:11–12; Exodus 10:21"> I will clothe the sky with darkness, and will make its covering as sackcloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Jeremias 1:9; John 7:15–17"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. the Lord the Lord. See on 3 Kings 8. 53.">a</a></sup>The Lord even God gives me the tongue of instruction, to know when it is fit to speak a word: he has appointed for me early, he has given me an ear to hear:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 14:31; Hebrews 5:8; Esaias 35:5"> and the instruction of the Lord, even the Lord, opens mine ears, and I do not disobey, nor dispute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 26:67; Mark 14:65; Mark 15:19"> I gave my back to scourges, and my cheeks to blows; and I turned not away my face from the shame of spitting:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 13:6; Jezekiel 3:8–9; John 16:33"> but the Lord God became my helper; therefore I was not ashamed, but I set my face as a solid rock; and I know that I shall never be ashamed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 8:32–34; Revelation 12:10; Esaias 41:1"> for he that has justified me draws near; who is he that pleads with me? let him stand up against me at the same time: yea, who is he that pleads with me? let him draw nigh to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 13:28; Esaias 41:10; Hebrews 1:11–12"> Behold, the Lord, the Lord, will help me; who will hurt me? behold, all ye shall wax old as a garment, and a moth shall devour you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 12:46; Michæas 7:7–9; Esaias 26:3–4"> Who is among you that fears the Lord? let him hearken to the voice of his servant: ye that walk in darkness, and have no light, trust in the name of the Lord, and stay upon God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremias 17:5–7; Romans 1:21–22; Romans 10:3"> Behold, ye all kindle a fire, and feed a flame: walk in the light of your fire, and in the flame which ye have kindled. This has happened to you for my sake; ye shall lie down in sorrow.</span>
