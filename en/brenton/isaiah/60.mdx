<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ephesians 5:14; Ephesians 5:8; John 8:12"> Be enlightened, be enlightened, O Jerusalem, for thy light is come, and the glory of the Lord is risen upon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 1:13; 1 Peter 2:9; Acts 17:30–31"> Behold, darkness shall cover the earth, and there shall be gross darkness on the nations: but the Lord shall appear upon thee, and his glory shall be seen upon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 13:47; Esaias 49:23; Revelation 21:24"> And kings shall walk in thy light, and nations in thy brightness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esaias 42:6; Esaias 49:20–22; Esaias 49:18"> Lift up thine eyes round about, and behold thy children gathered: all thy sons have come from far, and thy daughters shall be borne on men's shoulders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esaias 61:6; Esaias 23:18; Esaias 60:11"> Then shalt thou see, and fear, and be amazed in thine heart; for the wealth of the sea shall come round to thee, and of nations and peoples; and herds of camels shall come to thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 2:11; Psalms 72:10; Psalms 72:15"> and the camels of Madiam and Gæpha shall cover thee: all from Saba shall come bearing gold, and shall bring frankincense, and they shall publish the salvation of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esaias 56:7; Genesis 25:13; Hebrews 13:15–16"> And all the flocks of Kedar shall be gathered, and the rams of Nabæoth shall come; and acceptable sacrifices shall be offered on my altar, and my house of prayer shall be glorified.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 7:9; Luke 13:29; Esaias 60:4"> Who are these that fly as clouds, and as doves with young ones to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esaias 55:5; Esaias 2:16; Esaias 51:5"> The isles have waited for me, and the ships of Tharsis among the first, to bring thy children from afar, and their silver and their gold with them, and that for the sake of the holy name of the Lord, and because the Holy One of Israel is glorified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esaias 61:5; Esaias 49:23; Revelation 21:26"> And strangers shall build thy walls, and their kings shall wait upon thee: for by reason of my wrath I smote thee, and by reason of mercy I loved thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 21:25–26; Esaias 60:5; Esaias 60:18"> And thy gates shall be opened continually; they shall not be shut day nor night; to bring in to thee the power of the Gentiles, and their kings as captives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zacharias 14:12–19; Esaias 54:15; Psalms 2:12"> For the nations and the kings which will not serve thee shall perish; and those nations shall be made utterly desolate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Chronicles I 28:2; Esaias 35:2; Psalms 132:7"> And the glory of Libanus shall come to thee, with the cypress, and pine, and cedar together, to glorify my holy place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 3:9; Esaias 49:23; Hebrews 12:22"> And the sons of them that afflicted thee, and of them that provoked thee, shall come to thee<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Lit. having feared.">a</a></sup>in fear; and thou shalt be called Sion, the city of the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esaias 61:7; Jeremias 30:17; Esaias 49:14–23"> Because thou hast become desolate and hated, and there was no helper, therefore I will make thee a perpetual gladness, a joy of many generations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esaias 49:23; Esaias 66:11–12; Esaias 66:14"> And thou shalt suck the milk of the Gentiles, and shalt eat the wealth of kings: and shalt know that I am the Lord that saves thee and delivers thee, the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 11:40; Esaias 30:26; Esaias 1:26"> And for brass I will bring thee gold, and for iron I will bring thee silver, and instead of wood I will bring thee brass, and instead of stones, iron; and I will make thy princes peaceable, and thine overseers righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Esaias 26:1; Esaias 11:9; Zacharias 9:8"> And injustice shall no more be heard in thy land, nor destruction nor misery in thy coasts; but thy walls shall be called Salvation, and thy gates Sculptured Work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 21:23; Revelation 22:5; Zacharias 2:5"> And thou shalt no more have the sun for a light by day, nor shall the rising of the moon lighten thy night; but the Lord shall be thine everlasting light, and God thy glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; Psalms 84:11; Esaias 35:10"> For the sun shall no more set, nor shall the moon be eclipsed; for the Lord shall be thine everlasting light, and the days of thy mourning shall be completed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 15:13; Psalms 37:22; Psalms 37:11"> Thy people also shall be all righteous; they shall inherit the land for ever, preserving that which they have planted, even the works of their hands, for glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ambacum 2:3; Hebrews 10:36; Luke 18:7"> The<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, people few in number.">b</a></sup>little one shall become thousands, and the least a great nation; I the Lord will gather them in due time.</span>
</div>
