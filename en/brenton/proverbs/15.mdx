<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 10:12; Proverbs 25:15; Proverbs 15:18"> Anger slays even wise men; yet a submissive answer turns away wrath: but a grievous word stirs up anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 12:23; Proverbs 15:28; Proverbs 13:16"> The tongue of the wise knows what is good: but the mouth of the foolish tells out evil things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Jeremias 16:17; Jeremias 23:24"> The eyes of the Lord behold both the evil and the good in every place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 12:18; Proverbs 16:24; Proverbs 18:8"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. the healing of the tongue.">a</a></sup>The wholesome tongue is a tree of life, and he that keeps it shall be filled with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. spirit.">b</a></sup>understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 15:31–32; Proverbs 13:1; Proverbs 13:18"> A fool scorns his father's instruction: but he that keeps his commandments is more prudent. In abounding righteousness is great strength: but the ungodly shall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. with the roots wholly torn up.">c</a></sup>utterly perish from the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 8:21; Proverbs 21:20; Proverbs 10:22"> In the houses of the righteous is much strength: but the fruits of the ungodly shall perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 12:34; James 3:6; Ephesians 4:29"> The lips of the wise are bound by discretion: but the hearts of the foolish are not safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 15:29; Proverbs 21:27; Chronicles I 29:17"> The sacrifices of the ungodly are an abomination to the Lord; but the prayers of them that walk honestly are acceptable with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:11; Proverbs 21:21; Matthew 7:13"> The ways of an ungodly man are an abomination to the Lord; but he loves those that follow after righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 12:1; John 3:20; Proverbs 13:1"> The instruction of the simple is known by them that pass by; but they that hate reproofs die disgracefully.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 44:21; Hebrews 4:13; Psalms 139:8"> Hell and destruction are manifest to the Lord; how shall not also be the hearts of men?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 5:10; Job 21:14; Proverbs 9:7–8"> An uninstructed person will not love those that reprove him; neither will he associate with the wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 17:22; John 14:1; Proverbs 18:14"> When the heart rejoices the countenance is cheerful; but when it is in sorrow, the countenance is sad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 9:9; Esaias 30:10; Proverbs 18:15"> An upright heart seeks discretion; but the mouth of the uninstructed will experience evils.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:10; Romans 12:12; Proverbs 15:13"> The eyes of the wicked are always looking for evil things; but the good are always quiet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 16:8; Psalms 37:16; Ecclesiastes 5:10–12"> Better is a small portion with the fear of the Lord, than great treasures without the fear of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 17:1; Proverbs 21:19; 1 John 4:16"> Better is an entertainment of herbs with friendliness and kindness, than a feast of calves, with enmity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; Proverbs 26:21; Proverbs 29:22"> A passionate man stirs up strife; but he that is slow to anger appeases even a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. future.">d</a></sup>rising one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; Proverbs 26:21; Proverbs 29:22"> A man slow to anger will extinguish quarrels; but an ungodly man rather stirs them up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esaias 35:8; Proverbs 22:5; Esaias 30:21"> The ways of sluggards are strewn with thorns; but those of the diligent are made smooth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 29:3; Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 23:15–16"> A wise son gladdens his father; but a foolish son sneers at his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 5:15; Proverbs 10:23; Proverbs 14:16"> The ways of a foolish man are void of sense; but a wise man proceeds on his way aright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 11:14; Proverbs 20:18; Ecclesiastes 8:6"> They that honour not councils put off deliberation; but counsel abides in the hearts of counsellors.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Esaias 50:4; Proverbs 25:11–12; Ephesians 4:29"> A bad man will by no means<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or, obey.">e</a></sup>attend to counsel; neither will he say anything seasonable, or good for the common weal.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Colossians 3:1–2; Psalms 16:11; Matthew 7:14"> The thoughts of the wise are ways of life, that he may turn aside and escape from hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 146:9; Psalms 68:5–6; Proverbs 14:11"> The Lord pulls down the houses of scorners; but he establishes the border of the widow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 6:16–19; Jeremias 4:14; Proverbs 15:23"> An unrighteous thought is abomination to the Lord; but the sayings of the pure are held in honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 23:8; Proverbs 1:19; Jeremias 17:11"> A receiver of bribes destroys himself; but he that hates the receiving of bribes is safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 23:8; Proverbs 1:19; Jeremias 17:11"> &#91;By alms and by faithful dealings<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="a Observe, this is not in the Heb. nor any such doctrine.">f</a></sup>sins are purged away;&#93; but by the fear of the Lord every one departs from evil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Peter 3:15; Proverbs 29:11; Ecclesiastes 5:2"> The hearts of the righteous meditate faithfulness; but the mouth of the ungodly answers evil things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Peter 3:15; Proverbs 29:11; Ecclesiastes 5:2"> The ways of righteous men are acceptable with the Lord; and through them even enemies become friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 9:31; Psalms 145:18–19; Proverbs 15:8"> God is far from the ungodly; but he hearkens to the prayers of the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 9:31; Psalms 145:18–19; Proverbs 15:8"> Better are small receipts with righteousness, than abundant fruits with unrighteousness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 9:31; Psalms 145:18–19; Proverbs 15:8"> Let the heart of a man think justly, that his steps may be rightly ordered of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Esaias 58:11; Revelation 22:5; Revelation 21:23"> The eye that sees rightly rejoices the heart; and a good report fattens the bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24–33; Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 1:7"> He that rejects instruction hates himself; but he that minds reproofs loves his soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; Proverbs 18:12; Proverbs 1:7"> The fear of the Lord is instruction and wisdom; and the highest honour will correspond therewith.</span>
</div>
