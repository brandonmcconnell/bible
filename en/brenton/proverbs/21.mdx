<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 16:9; Proverbs 20:24; Ezra and Nehemiah 6:22"> As a rush of water, so is the king's heart in God's hand: he turns it whithersoever he may desire to point out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 16:2; Proverbs 16:25; Luke 16:15"> Every man seems to himself righteous; but the Lord directs the hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings I 15:22; Osee 6:6; Michæas 6:6–8"> To do justly and to speak truth, are more pleasing to God than the blood of sacrifices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 6:17; 1 Peter 5:5; Esaias 2:17"> A high-minded man is stout-hearted in his pride; and the lamp of the wicked is sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 13:11; Proverbs 10:2; Proverbs 20:21"> He that gathers treasures with a lying tongue pursues vanity on to the snares of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 21:21; Ephesians 5:6; Proverbs 1:18–19"> Destruction shall lodge with the ungodly; for they refuse to do justly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 2:15; 1 John 2:29; Psalms 14:2–3"> To the froward God sends froward ways; for his works are pure and right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 19:13; Proverbs 21:19; Proverbs 27:15–16"> It is better to dwell in a corner<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. in the open air.">a</a></sup>on the house-top, than in plastered rooms with unrighteousness, and in an open house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Michæas 3:2–3; Proverbs 3:29; Psalms 36:4"> The soul of the ungodly shall not be pitied by any man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 19:25; Proverbs 18:15; Proverbs 9:9"> When an intemperate man is punished the simple becomes wiser: and a wise man understanding will receive knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 14:11; Psalms 37:35–36; 2 Peter 3:6–7"> A righteous man understands the hearts of the ungodly: and despises the ungodly for their wickedness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 28:27; Matthew 6:14; Proverbs 1:28"> He that stops his ears from hearing the poor, himself also shall cry, and there shall be none to hear him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 18:16; Proverbs 19:6; Proverbs 17:8"> A secret gift calms anger: but he that forbears to give stirs up strong wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 10:29; Matthew 7:23; Matthew 13:41–42"> It is the joy of the righteous to do judgment: but a holy man is abominable with evil-doers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 10:38; Psalms 125:5; Psalms 49:14"> A man that wanders out of the way of righteousness, shall rest in the congregation of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Heb. Rephaim. see Appendix. Note on 2 Kings 5. 18.">b</a></sup>giants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 23:21; 1 Timothy 5:6; 2 Timothy 3:4"> A poor man loves mirth, loving wine and oil in abundance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 11:8; Esaias 43:3–4; Esaias 53:4–5"> and a transgressor is the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. `off-scouring;' perhaps `ransom,' q. d. that which cleans.">c</a></sup>abomination of a righteous man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 21:9; Psalms 120:5–6; Psalms 55:6–7"> It is better to dwell in a wilderness than with a quarrelsome and talkative and passionate woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 25:3–4; Psalms 112:3; Ecclesiastes 7:11"> A desirable treasure will rest on the mouth of the wise; but foolish men will swallow it up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 5:6; 1 Corinthians 15:58; Proverbs 22:4"> The way of righteousness and mercy will find life and glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:19; Proverbs 24:5; Ecclesiastes 9:13–18"> A wise man assaults strong cities, and demolishes the fortress in which the ungodly trusted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 13:3; Proverbs 18:21; James 1:26"> He that keeps his mouth and his tongue keeps his soul from trouble.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 1:1; Proverbs 18:12; Proverbs 1:22"> A bold and self-willed and insolent man is called a pest: and he that remembers injuries is a transgressor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 12:24; Matthew 25:26"> Desires kill the sluggard; for his hands do not choose to do anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 37:26; Acts 20:33–35; Ephesians 4:28"> An ungodly man entertains evil desires all the day: but the righteous is unsparingly merciful and compassionate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Esaias 66:3; Proverbs 15:8; Jeremias 6:20"> The sacrifices of the ungodly are abomination to the Lord, for they offer them wickedly.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or, unlawfully.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:13; Proverbs 19:5; Proverbs 19:9"> A false witness shall perish; but an obedient man will speak cautiously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:11; Proverbs 11:5; Aggæus 2:18–19"> An ungodly man<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See Alex. ungodly.">e</a></sup>impudently withstands with his face; but the upright man himself understands his ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Acts 5:39; Jeremias 9:23"> There is no wisdom, there is no courage, there is no counsel against the ungodly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 20:7; Esaias 31:1; Psalms 33:17–18"> A horse is prepared for the day of battle; but help is of the Lord.</span>
</div>
