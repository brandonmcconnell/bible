<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:13–16; Proverbs 16:11; Proverbs 20:23"> False balances are an abomination before the Lord: but a just weight is acceptable unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 29:23; Proverbs 15:33; Proverbs 16:18–19"> Wherever pride enters, there will be also disgrace: but the mouth of the lowly meditates wisdom.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Appendix.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 13:6; Proverbs 28:18; Psalms 26:1"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="the Alexandrine text reads: “The integrity of the upright shall guide them, but the overthrow of the rebellious shall spoil them.”">b</a></sup>When a just man dies he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Comp. Heb.">c</a></sup>leaves regret: but the destruction of the ungodly is speedy, and causes joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 5:22; Psalms 9:15–16; Proverbs 1:31–32"> Righteousness<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. right divides.">d</a></sup>traces out blameless paths: but ungodliness encounters unjust dealing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:8; Kings III 2:44; Psalms 7:15–16"> The righteousness of upright men delivers them: but transgressors are caught in their own destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 10:28; Job 8:13–14; Job 11:20"> At the death of a just man his hope does not perish: but the boast of the ungodly perishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 6:23–24; Proverbs 21:18; Esther 7:9–10"> A righteous man escapes from a snare, and the ungodly man is delivered up in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 2:10–16; Proverbs 4:5–6; 1 John 2:27"> In the mouth of ungodly men is a snare to citizens: but the understanding of righteous men is prosperous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 28:12; Esther 8:15–16; Psalms 58:10–11"> In the prosperity of righteous men a city prospers:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See Appendix which gives the Alexandrine text as: “[note - Part of verse 10.]— but at the destruction of the wicked there is exultation.”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 14:34; Proverbs 29:8; James 3:6"> but by the mouths of ungodly men it is overthrown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 10:19; 1 Peter 2:23; Luke 18:9"> A man void of understanding sneers at his fellow citizens: but a sensible man is quiet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 20:19; Proverbs 25:9; Leviticus 19:16"> A double-tongued man discloses the secret counsels of an assembly: but he that is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="See chap 20. 27.">f</a></sup>faithful in spirit conceals matters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 24:6; Proverbs 15:22; Proverbs 20:18"> They that have no<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or, governance.">g</a></sup>guidance fall like leaves: but in much counsel there is safety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 17:18; Proverbs 6:1–5; Proverbs 22:26–27"> A bad man does harm wherever he meets a just man: and he hates the sound of safety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 31:30–31; Luke 10:42; Acts 16:14–15"> A gracious wife<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. raises.">h</a></sup>brings glory to her husband: but a woman hating righteousness is a theme of dishonour. The slothful come to want: but the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. manly.">i</a></sup>diligent support themselves with wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; Matthew 25:34–40; James 2:13"> A merciful man does good to his own soul: but the merciless destroys his own body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 6:8–9; Ecclesiastes 10:8; Osee 10:12–13"> An ungodly man performs unrighteous works: but the seed of the righteous is a reward of truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 10:16; Romans 2:8–9; Proverbs 12:28"> A righteous son is born for life: but the persecution of the ungodly ends in death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 119:1; Proverbs 21:29; Chronicles I 29:17"> Perverse ways are an abomination to the Lord: but all they that are blameless in their ways are acceptable to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 112:1–2; Acts 2:39; Jeremias 32:39"> He that unjustly strikes hands shall not be unpunished: but he that sows righteousness shall receive a faithful reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 3:3–4; Proverbs 31:30; Naum 3:4–6"> As an ornament in a swine's snout, so is beauty to an ill-minded woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; Romans 2:8–9; Matthew 5:6"> All the desire of the righteous is good: but the hope of the ungodly shall perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Luke 6:38; Deuteronomy 15:10"> There are some who scatter their own, and make it more: and there are some also who gather, yet have less.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gr. diminished.">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; 2 Corinthians 9:6–9; Esaias 58:7–11"> Every sincere soul is blessed: but a passionate man is not graceful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 29:13; Genesis 42:6; Amos 8:4–6"> May he that hoards corn leave it to the nation: but blessing be on the head of him that gives it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 7:15–16; Proverbs 17:11; Psalms 9:15–16"> He that devises good counsels seeks good favour: but as for him that seeks after evil, evil shall overtake him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:17; Jeremias 17:8; Psalms 52:7–8"> He that trusts in wealth shall fall; but he that helps righteous men shall rise.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="See 1 Tim. 5. 8.">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:16; Proverbs 14:19; Proverbs 15:27"> He that deals not graciously with his own house shall inherit the wind; and the fool shall be servant to the wise man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Daniel 12:3; James 5:20; 1 Corinthians 9:19–23"> Out of the fruit of righteousness grows a tree of life; but the souls of transgressors are cut off before their time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17–18; Jeremias 25:29; 1 Corinthians 11:30–32"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="See 1 Pet.">l</a></sup>If the righteous scarcely be saved, where shall the ungodly and the sinner appear?</span>
</div>
