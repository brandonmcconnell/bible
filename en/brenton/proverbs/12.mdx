<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 10:25; Ephesians 3:17; Proverbs 12:12"> A man shall not prosper by wickedness; but the roots of the righteous shall not be taken up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 14:30; Proverbs 31:10–25; Proverbs 14:1"> A virtuous woman is a crown to her husband; but as a worm in wood, so a bad woman destroys her husband.</span>

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:24–27; Proverbs 11:21; Proverbs 10:25"> When the ungodly is overthrown, he vanishes away; but the houses of the just remain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:18; 1 Corinthians 4:5; 1 Corinthians 3:10–15"> The mouth of an understanding man is praised by a man; but he that is dull of heart is had in derision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 14:11; Proverbs 13:7"> Better is a man in dishonour serving himself, than one honouring himself and wanting bread.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:4; Genesis 33:13–14; Numbers 22:28–32"> A righteous man has pity for the lives of his cattle; but the bowels of the ungodly are unmerciful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 28:19; Proverbs 14:23; 1 Thessalonians 4:11–12"> He that tills his own land shall be satisfied with bread; but they that pursue vanities are void of understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 28:19; Proverbs 14:23; 1 Thessalonians 4:11–12"> He that enjoys himself in banquets of wine, shall leave dishonour in his own strong holds.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 17:7–8; John 15:5; Luke 8:13–15"> The desires of the ungodly are evil; but the roots of the godly are firmly set.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 21:23; Proverbs 11:8; Psalms 64:8"> For the sin of his lips a sinner falls into snares; but a righteous man escapes from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 21:23; Proverbs 11:8; Psalms 64:8"> He whose looks are gentle shall be pitied, but he that contends in the gates will afflict souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 18:20–21; Esaias 3:10–11; Proverbs 13:2"> The soul of a man shall be filled with good from the fruits of his mouth; and the recompence of his lips shall be given to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 3:7; Proverbs 26:12; Proverbs 14:16"> The ways of fools are right in their own eyes; but a wise man hearkens to counsels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 29:11; James 1:19; Proverbs 10:12"> A fool declares his wrath the same day; but a prudent man hides his own disgrace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 14:5; Proverbs 14:25; Proverbs 24:28"> A righteous man declares the open truth; but an unjust witness is deceitful.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 16:24; Proverbs 15:4; Psalms 57:4"> Some wound as they speak, like swords; but the tongues of the wise heal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 19:9; Psalms 52:4–5; Job 20:5"> True lips establish testimony; but a hasty witness has an unjust tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 5:9; 1 Peter 3:8–13; Proverbs 12:12"> There is deceit in the heart of him that imagines evil; but they that love peace shall rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 3:13; Psalms 91:10; Romans 8:28"> No injustice will please a just man; but the ungodly will be filled with mischief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 5:6; Proverbs 11:20; Revelation 22:15"> Lying lips are an abomination to the Lord; but he that deals faithfully is accepted with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 13:16; Proverbs 11:13; Proverbs 10:19"> An understanding man is a throne of wisdom; but the heart of fools shall meet with curses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 12:27; Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 21:25–26"> The hand of chosen men shall easily obtain rule; but the deceitful shall be for a prey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 16:24; Proverbs 15:13; Proverbs 17:22"> A terrible word troubles the heart of a righteous man; but a good message rejoices him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 John 2:26; Matthew 5:46–48; Proverbs 17:27"> A just arbitrator shall be his own friend; but mischief shall pursue sinners; and the way of ungodly men shall lead them astray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 26:15; Proverbs 15:16"> A deceitful man shall catch no game; but a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. pure.">b</a></sup>blameless man is a precious possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="3 John 1:11; Romans 5:21; Jezekiel 18:20–24"> In the ways of righteousness is life; but the ways of those that remember injuries lead to death.</span>
