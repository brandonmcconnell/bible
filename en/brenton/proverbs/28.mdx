<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:7; Psalms 53:5; Esaias 26:3–4"> The ungodly man flees when no one pursues: but the righteous is confident as a lion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings III 15:28; Kings III 16:8–29; Kings IV 15:8–31"> By reason of the sins of ungodly men quarrels arise; but a wise man will<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. quench.">a</a></sup>quell them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 18:28–30"> A bold man oppresses the poor by ungodly deeds. As an impetuous and unprofitable rain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:32; Ephesians 5:11; Matthew 14:4"> so they that forsake the law praise ungodliness; but they that love the law fortify themselves with a wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 92:6; James 1:5; 1 John 2:27"> Evil men will not understand judgment: but they that seek the Lord will understand<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. in everything.">b</a></sup>everything.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 19:1; Proverbs 28:18; Luke 16:19–23"> A poor man walking in truth is better than a rich liar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:3–4; Proverbs 29:3; Proverbs 3:1–35"> A wise son keeps the law: but he that keeps up debauchery dishonours his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 27:16–17; Proverbs 13:22; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> He that increases his wealth by usuries and unjust gains, gathers it for him that pities the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 66:18; 2 Timothy 4:3–4; Zacharias 7:11–13"> He that turns away his ear from hearing the law, even he has<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, abhorred his prayer.">c</a></sup>made his prayer abominable.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 26:27; Psalms 37:25–26; Galatians 2:4"> He that causes upright men to err in an evil way, himself shall fall into destruction: transgressors also shall pass by prosperity, but shall not enter into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 18:17; Ecclesiastes 9:15–17; Proverbs 26:16"> A rich man is wise in his own conceit; but an intelligent poor man will condemn him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 29:2; Proverbs 11:10; Proverbs 28:28"> By reason of the help of righteous men great glory arises: but in the places of the ungodly men are caught.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 John 1:8–10; Psalms 32:3–5; Job 31:33"> He that covers his own ungodliness shall not prosper: but he that blames himself shall be loved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 23:17; Psalms 2:11; Esaias 66:2"> Blessed is the man who religiously fears<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. all things.">d</a></sup>always: but the hard of heart shall fall into mischiefs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Matthew 2:16; Proverbs 19:12"> A hungry lion and a thirsty wolf is he, who, being poor, rules over a poor nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; Jeremias 22:15–17; Ecclesiastes 4:1"> A king in need of revenues is a great oppressor: but he that hates injustice shall live a long time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 9:6; Kings III 21:19; Exodus 21:14"> He that becomes surety for a man charged with murder shall be an exile, and not in safety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 9:6; Kings III 21:19; Exodus 21:14"> Chasten thy son, and he shall love thee, and give honour to thy soul: he shall not obey a sinful nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 10:9; Proverbs 28:6; Revelation 3:3"> He that walks justly is assisted: but he that walks in crooked ways shall be entangled therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Judges 9:4; Proverbs 27:23–27"> He that tills his own land shall be satisfied with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. plural.">e</a></sup>bread: but he that follows idleness shall have plenty of poverty.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 16:10–12; Proverbs 13:11; Revelation 2:10"> A man worthy of credit shall be much blessed: but the wicked shall not be unpunished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 24:23; Proverbs 18:5; Jezekiel 13:19"> He that reverences not the persons of the just is not good: such a one will sell a man for a morsel of bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 23:6; 1 Timothy 6:9; Proverbs 28:20"> An envious man makes haste to be rich, and knows not that the merciful man will have the mastery over him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 27:5–6; Matthew 18:15; Psalms 141:5"> He that reproves a man's ways shall have more favour than he that flatters with the tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 15:4–6; Proverbs 18:9; Proverbs 19:26"> He that casts off father or mother, and thinks he sins not; the same is partaker with an ungodly man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 11:25; 1 Timothy 6:6; Proverbs 13:10"> An unbelieving man judges rashly: but he that trusts in the Lord will act carefully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5; Jeremias 17:9; Job 28:28"> He that trusts to a bold heart, such an one is a fool: but he that walks in wisdom shall be safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Deuteronomy 15:7; Psalms 41:1–3"> He that gives to the poor shall not be in want: but he that turns away his eye from him shall be in great distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 29:2; Job 24:4; Acts 12:23–24"> In the places of ungodly men the righteous<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. groan.">f</a></sup>mourn: but in their destruction the righteous shall be multiplied.</span>
</div>
