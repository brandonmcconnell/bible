<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 22:26; Proverbs 17:18; Proverbs 20:16"> My son, if thou become surety for thy friend, thou shalt deliver thine hand to an enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 18:7; Proverbs 12:13"> For a man's own lips become a strong snare to him, and he is caught with the lips of his own mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 4:10; Psalms 31:8; Kings II 24:14"> My son, do what I command thee, and deliver thyself; for on thy friend's account thou art come into the power of evil men: faint not, but stir up even thy friend for whom thou art become surety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 132:4; Proverbs 6:10–11; Mark 13:35–36"> Give not sleep to thine eyes, nor slumber with thine eyelids;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 91:3; Psalms 124:7; Proverbs 1:17"> that thou mayest deliver thyself as a doe out of the toils, and as a bird out of a snare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 20:4; Proverbs 18:9; Proverbs 6:9"> Go to the ant, O sluggard; and see, and emulate his ways, and become wiser than he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 38:39–12; Proverbs 30:27; Job 39:26–30"> For whereas he has no husbandry, nor any one to compel him, and is under no master,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 10:5; 1 Timothy 6:19; Proverbs 30:25"> he prepares food for himself in the summer, and lays by abundant store in harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 10:5; 1 Timothy 6:19; Proverbs 30:25"> Or go to the bee, and learn how diligent she is, and how earnestly she is engaged in her work;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 10:5; 1 Timothy 6:19; Proverbs 30:25"> whose labours kings and private men use for health, and she is desired and respected by all:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 10:5; 1 Timothy 6:19; Proverbs 30:25"> though weak in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="c Gr. strength.">a</a></sup>body, she is advanced by honouring wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 24:33–34; Jeremias 4:14; Ephesians 5:14"> How long wilt thou lie, O sluggard? and when wilt thou awake out of sleep?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 24:33–34; Proverbs 6:6; Proverbs 23:33–34"> Thou sleepest a little, and thou restest a little, and thou slumberest a short time, and thou foldest thine arms over thy breast a little.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 24:34"> Then poverty comes upon thee as an evil traveller, and want as a swift courier:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 24:34"> but if thou be diligent, thine harvest shall arrive as a fountain, and poverty shall flee away as a bad courier.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:34; 1 Timothy 5:13; Proverbs 16:27"> A foolish man and a transgressor goes in ways that are not good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; Proverbs 10:10; Job 15:12"> And the same winks with the eye, and makes a sign with his foot, and teaches with the beckonings of his fingers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 16:17; Michæas 2:1; Osee 8:7"> His perverse heart devises evils: at all times such a one causes troubles to a city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:16; Jeremias 19:11; 1 Thessalonians 5:3"> Therefore his destruction shall come suddenly; overthrow and irretrievable ruin.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Comp. Heb.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 11:5; Proverbs 8:13; Proverbs 11:20"> For he rejoices in all things which God hates, and he is ruined by reason of impurity of soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 120:2–3; Proverbs 12:22; Psalms 101:5"> The eye of the haughty, a tongue unjust, hands shedding the blood of the just;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 6:5; Proverbs 1:16; Romans 3:15"> and a heart devising evil thoughts, and feet hastening to do evil,—are hateful to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 19:5; Proverbs 6:14; James 3:18"> An unjust witness kindles falsehoods, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr sends forth judgments.">c</a></sup>brings on quarrels between brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1; Deuteronomy 27:16; Proverbs 7:1–4"> My son, keep the laws of thy father, and reject not the ordinances of thy mother:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 3:3; Proverbs 7:3–4; 2 Corinthians 3:3"> but bind them upon thy soul continually, and hang them as a chain about thy neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; Proverbs 3:23–24; Psalms 119:24"> Whensoever thou walkest, lead this along and let it be with thee; and when thou sleepest let it keep thee; that it may talk with thee when thou wakest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 119:105; 2 Peter 1:19; Psalms 19:8"> For the commandment of the law is a lamp and a light; a way of life; reproof also and correction:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 2:16; Proverbs 7:5; Ecclesiastes 7:26"> to keep thee continually from a married woman, and from the calumny of a strange tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 1:14–15; Matthew 5:28; Kings IV 9:30"> Let not the desire of beauty overcome thee, neither be thou caught by thine eyes, neither be captivated with her eyelids.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 15:30; Proverbs 29:3; Kings I 2:36"> For the value of a harlot is as much as of one loaf; and a woman hunts for the precious souls of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 31:9–12; Osee 7:4–7; James 3:5"> Shall any one bind fire in his bosom, and not burn his garments?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> or will any one walk on coals of fire, and not burn his feet?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:1; Malachias 3:5; Kings II 12:9–10"> So is he that goes in to a married woman; he shall not be held guiltless, neither any one that touches her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 38:39"> It is not to be wondered at if one should be taken stealing, for he steals that when hungry he may satisfy his soul:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 19:8; Job 20:18; Kings II 12:6"> but if he should be taken, he shall repay sevenfold, and shall deliver himself by giving all his goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 7:7; Hebrews 13:4; Romans 1:22–24"> But the adulterer through want of sense procures destruction to his soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 5:9–11; Psalms 51:8; Judges 16:19–21"> He endures both pain and disgrace, and his reproach shall never be wiped off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 27:4; Numbers 5:14; Song of Solomon 8:6"> For the soul of her husband is full of jealousy: he will not spare in the day of vengeance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 8:25; Malachias 2:9; Proverbs 7:13"> He will not forego his enmity for any ransom: neither will he be reconciled for many gifts.</span>
