<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 16:18; Revelation 3:12; Kings III 7:2–3"> Wisdom has built a house for herself, and set up seven pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 22:3–14; Esaias 25:6; Luke 14:16–17"> She has killed her beasts; she has mingled her wine in a bowl, and prepared her table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 9:14; Proverbs 8:1–3; Matthew 22:3–4"> She has sent forth her servants, calling with a loud proclamation to the feast, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 6:32; Proverbs 9:16; Proverbs 8:5"> Whoso is foolish, let him turn aside to me: and to them that want understanding she says,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 6:27; Song of Solomon 5:1; Proverbs 9:17"> Come, eat of my bread, and drink wine which I have mingled for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; Proverbs 4:11; Luke 13:24"> Leave folly, that ye may reign for ever; and seek<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Alex. + 'that ye may live.'">a</a></sup>wisdom, and improve understanding by knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 15:12; Proverbs 23:9; Chronicles II 36:16"> He that reproves evil men shall get dishonour to himself; and he that rebukes an ungodly man shall disgrace himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 141:5; Proverbs 13:18; Proverbs 23:9"> Rebuke not evil men, lest they should hate thee: rebuke a wise man, and he will love thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 1:5; Matthew 13:11–12; 1 John 5:13"> Give an opportunity to a wise man, and he will be wiser: instruct a just man, and he will receive more instruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 111:10; Job 28:28; Proverbs 1:7"> The fear of the Lord is the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, summit.">b</a></sup>beginning of wisdom, and the counsel of saints is understanding:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 111:10; Job 28:28; Proverbs 1:7"> for to know the law is the character of a sound mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 10:27; Proverbs 3:16; Proverbs 3:2"> For in this way thou shalt live long, and years of thy life shall be added to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 22:2–3; 2 Peter 3:3–4; Jezekiel 18:20"> Son, if thou be wise for thyself, thou shalt also be wise for thy neighbours; and if thou shouldest prove wicked, thou alone wilt bear the evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 22:2–3; 2 Peter 3:3–4; Jezekiel 18:20"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="a Heb. — to beginning of verse 13.">c</a></sup>He that stays himself upon falsehoods, attempts to rule the winds, and the same will pursue birds in their flight:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 22:2–3; 2 Peter 3:3–4; Jezekiel 18:20"> for he has forsaken the ways of his own vineyard, and he has caused the axles of his own husbandry to go astray;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 22:2–3; 2 Peter 3:3–4; Jezekiel 18:20"> and he goes through a dry desert, and a land appointed to drought, and he gathers barrenness with his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 7:11; Proverbs 5:6; 1 Timothy 6:4"> A foolish and bold woman, who knows not modesty, comes to want a morsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 9:3; Proverbs 7:10–12"> She sits at the doors of her house, on a seat openly in the streets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 7:25–27; Proverbs 23:27–28; Proverbs 7:13–15"> calling to passers by, and to those that are going right on their ways;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 9:4"> saying, Whoso is most senseless of you, let him turn aside to me; and I exhort those that want prudence, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 20:17; Proverbs 23:31–32; James 1:14–15"> Take and enjoy secret bread, and the sweet water of theft.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 7:27; Proverbs 2:18–19; Proverbs 5:5"> But he knows that mighty men die by her, and he falls in with a snare of hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 7:27; Proverbs 2:18–19; Proverbs 5:5"> But hasten away, delay not in the place, neither fix thine eye upon her:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 7:27; Proverbs 2:18–19; Proverbs 5:5"> for thus shalt thou go through strange water;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 7:27; Proverbs 2:18–19; Proverbs 5:5"> but do thou abstain from strange water, and drink not of a strange fountain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 7:27; Proverbs 2:18–19; Proverbs 5:5"> that thou mayest live long, and years of life may be added to thee.</span>
