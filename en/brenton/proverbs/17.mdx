<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 15:17; Psalms 37:16; Proverbs 21:19"> Better is a morsel with pleasure in peace, than a house full of many good things and unjust sacrifices, with strife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 10:5; Ecclesiastes 4:13; Proverbs 14:35"> A wise servant shall have rule over foolish masters, and shall divide portions among brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Esaias 48:10; Jeremias 17:10; Psalms 26:2"> As silver and gold are tried in a furnace, so are choice hearts with the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3–4; 1 John 4:5; Proverbs 28:4"> A bad man hearkens to the tongue of transgressors: but a righteous man attends not to false lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 14:31; Job 31:29; 1 John 3:17"> He that laughs at the poor provokes him that made him; and he that rejoices at the destruction of another shall not be held guiltless: but he that has compassion shall find mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 127:3–5; Proverbs 13:22; Proverbs 16:31"> Children's children are the crown of old men; and their fathers are the glory of children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 127:3–5; Proverbs 13:22; Proverbs 16:31"> The faithful has the whole world full of wealth; but the faithless not even a farthing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 29:12; Proverbs 12:19; Psalms 50:16–17"> Faithful lips will not suit a fool; nor lying lips a just man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 23:8; Michæas 7:3; Kings I 25:35"> Instruction is to them that use it a gracious reward: and whithersoever it may turn, it shall prosper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 10:12; 1 Peter 4:8; Proverbs 16:28"> He that conceals injuries seeks love; but he that hates to hide them separates friends and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Comp. Heb.">a</a></sup>kindred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 9:8–9; Proverbs 15:5"> A threat breaks down the heart of a wise man; but a fool, though scourged, understands not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings III 2:46; Kings II 16:5–9; Kings II 18:15"> Every bad man stirs up strifes: but the Lord will send out against him an unmerciful messenger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Osee 13:8; Kings II 17:8; Proverbs 28:15"> Care may befall a man of understanding; but fools will meditate evils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 109:4–13; 1 Peter 3:9; Romans 12:17"> Whoso rewards evil for good, evil shall not be removed from his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 20:3; Proverbs 25:8; 2 Timothy 2:23–24"> Rightful rule gives power to words; but sedition and strife precede poverty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 23:7; Proverbs 18:5; Esaias 5:23"> He that pronounces the unjust just, and the just unjust, is unclean and abominable with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 21:25–26; Proverbs 23:23; Proverbs 18:15"> Why has the fool wealth? for a senseless man will not be able to purchase wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 21:25–26; Proverbs 23:23; Proverbs 18:15"> He that exalts his own house seeks ruin; and he that turns aside from instruction shall fall into mischiefs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 18:24; John 15:13–14; Kings I 20:17"> Have thou a friend for every time, and let brethren be useful in distress; for on this account are they born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 11:15; Proverbs 6:1–5; Proverbs 22:26–27"> A foolish man applauds and rejoices over himself, as he also that becomes surety would make himself responsible for his own friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 16:18; Proverbs 18:12; Kings I 25:36–38"> A lover of sin rejoices in strifes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 3:6–8; Proverbs 10:31; Proverbs 6:12–15"> and the hard-hearted man<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, meets not with good men.">b</a></sup>comes not in for good. A man of a changeful tongue will fall into mischiefs;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 19:13; Proverbs 17:25"> and the heart of a fool is grief to its possessor. A father rejoices not over an uninstructed son; but a wise son gladdens his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 15:13; Proverbs 12:25; Proverbs 18:14"> A glad heart promotes health; but the bones of a sorrowful man dry up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 17:8; Deuteronomy 16:19; Exodus 23:8"> The ways of a man who unjustly receives gifts in his bosom do not prosper; and an ungodly man perverts the ways of righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:14; Ecclesiastes 8:1; Proverbs 14:6"> The countenance of a wise man is sensible; but the eyes of a fool go to the ends of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; Kings II 13:1–22; Proverbs 19:13"> A foolish son is a cause of anger to his father, and grief to her that bore him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 17:15; Proverbs 18:5; John 18:22"> It is not right to punish a righteous man, nor is it holy to plot against righteous princes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 3:18; James 1:19; Proverbs 10:19"> He that forbears to utter a hard word is discreet, and a patient man is wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 13:5; Proverbs 15:2; Ecclesiastes 10:14"> Wisdom shall be imputed to a fool who asks after wisdom: and he who holds his peace shall seem to be sensible.</span>
