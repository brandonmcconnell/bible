<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 15:5; Proverbs 9:7–8; Proverbs 15:20"> A wise son is obedient to his father: but a disobedient son will be destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 12:14; Proverbs 18:20; Proverbs 10:11"> A good man shall eat of the fruits of righteousness: but the lives of transgressors shall perish before their time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 21:23; Proverbs 18:21; Proverbs 18:7"> He that keeps his own mouth keeps his own life: but he that is hasty with his lips shall bring terror upon himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 12:24; Proverbs 12:11; 2 Peter 1:5–11"> Every slothful man desires, but the hands of the active are diligent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 3:9; Ephesians 4:25; Psalms 119:163"> A righteous man hates an unjust word: but an ungodly man is ashamed, and will have no confidence.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Appendix for Alexandrine text shown as: “Righteousness preserves the simple in the way, but sin makes worthless the ungodly.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 18:11–14; Revelation 3:17; Proverbs 11:24"> There are some who, having nothing, enrich themselves: and there are some who bring themselves down in the midst of much wealth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; 1 Peter 1:18–19; Psalms 49:6–10"> A man's own wealth is the ransom of his life: but the poor<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, comes not in for.">b</a></sup>endures not threatening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 4:18; Proverbs 24:20; Proverbs 20:20"> The righteous always have light: but the light of the ungodly is quenched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 4:18; Proverbs 24:20; Proverbs 20:20"> Crafty souls go astray in sins: but just men pity, and are merciful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 19:20; Proverbs 12:15–16; Proverbs 17:14"> A bad man does evil with insolence: but they that are judges of themselves are wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 20:21; Proverbs 10:2; James 5:1–5"> Wealth gotten hastily with iniquity is diminished: but he that gathers for himself with godliness shall be increased. The righteous is merciful, and lends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 16:22; Proverbs 13:19; Psalms 119:81–83"> Better is he that begins to help heartily, than he that promises and leads another to hope: for a good desire is a tree of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 15:31; Chronicles II 36:16; 2 John 1:8"> He that slights a matter shall be slighted of it: but he that fears the commandment has health of soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 15:31; Chronicles II 36:16; 2 John 1:8"> To a crafty son there shall be nothing good: but a wise servant shall have prosperous doings, and his way shall be directed aright.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 18:5; Proverbs 10:11; Proverbs 14:27"> The law of the wise is a fountain of life: but the man void of understanding shall die by a snare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremias 2:19; Proverbs 4:19; Acts 7:10"> Sound discretion gives favour, and to know the law is the part of a sound understanding: but the ways of scorners tend to destruction.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 15:2; Proverbs 12:22–23; Ephesians 5:17"> Every prudent man acts with knowledge: but the fool displays his own mischief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 25:13; 1 Corinthians 4:2; 2 Corinthians 5:20"> A rash king shall fall into mischief: but a wise messenger shall deliver him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 15:5; Proverbs 12:1; Proverbs 15:31–32"> Instruction removes poverty and disgrace: but he that attends to reproofs shall be honoured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 7:14–17; Proverbs 13:12; Proverbs 29:27"> The desires of the godly gladden the soul, but the works of the ungodly are far from knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33–34; Proverbs 15:31; Proverbs 9:6"> If thou walkest with wise men thou shalt be wise: but he that walks with fools shall be known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 32:10; Esaias 3:10–11; Romans 2:7–10"> Evil shall pursue sinners; but good shall overtake the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:26; Job 27:16–17; Proverbs 28:8"> A good man shall inherit children's children; and the wealth of ungodly men is laid up for the just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Proverbs 28:19; Ecclesiastes 5:9"> The righteous shall spend many years in wealth: but the unrighteous shall perish suddenly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 29:17; Proverbs 23:13–14; Proverbs 29:15"> He that spares the rod hates his son: but he that loves, carefully chastens him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 34:10; Proverbs 10:3; Hebrews 13:5"> A just man eats and satisfies his soul: but the souls of the ungodly are in want.</span>
</div>
