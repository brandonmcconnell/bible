<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 16:9–11; Psalms 37:7; Numbers 16:19–41"> The folly of a man spoils his ways: and he blames God in his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 14:20; Proverbs 19:6–7; Luke 15:13–15"> Wealth acquires many friends; but the poor is deserted even of the friend he has.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 21:28; Proverbs 19:9; Proverbs 6:19"> A false witness shall not be unpunished, and he that accuses unjustly shall not escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 29:26; Proverbs 18:16; Proverbs 17:8"> Many court the favour of kings; but every bad man becomes a reproach to another man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 19:4; 1 John 3:17–18; James 2:15–16"> Every one who hates his poor brother shall also be far from friendship. Good understanding will draw near to them that know it, and a sensible man will find it. He that does much harm perfects mischief; and he that uses provoking words shall not escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 16:20; Proverbs 8:35–36; Proverbs 3:18"> He that procures wisdom loves himself; and he that keeps wisdom shall find good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 22:15; Proverbs 19:5; Revelation 19:20"> A false witness shall not be unpunished; and whosoever shall kindle mischief shall perish by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 30:21–22; Proverbs 26:1; Luke 16:19"> Delight does not suit a fool, nor is it seemly if a servant should begin to rule with haughtiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 16:32; Proverbs 14:29; James 1:19"> A merciful man is long-suffering; and his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. boasting comes upon.">a</a></sup>triumph overtakes transgressors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Osee 14:5; Proverbs 20:2; Proverbs 16:14–15"> The threatening of a king is like the roaring of a lion; but as dew on the grass, so is his favour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 27:15; Proverbs 21:9; Proverbs 10:1"> A foolish son is a disgrace to his father: vows paid out of the hire of a harlot are not pure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 18:22; Proverbs 31:10–31; Proverbs 13:22"> Fathers divide house and substance to their children: but a wife is suited to a man by the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 23:21; Proverbs 20:13; 2 Thessalonians 3:10"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, keeps down.">b</a></sup>Cowardice possesses the effeminate man; and the soul of the sluggard shall hunger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 10:28; Proverbs 13:13; Luke 11:28"> He that keeps the commandment keeps his own soul; but he that despises his ways shall perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 28:27; Hebrews 6:10; Luke 6:38"> He that has pity on the poor lends to the Lord; and he will recompense to him according to his gift.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 13:24; Proverbs 29:15; Proverbs 22:15"> Chasten thy son, for so he shall be hopeful; and be not exalted in thy soul to haughtiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 25:28; Kings II 16:5–6; Kings I 24:17–22"> A malicious man shall be severely punished, and if he commit injury, he shall also lose his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 12:15; Proverbs 8:34–35; Proverbs 2:1–9"> Hear, son, the instruction of thy father, that thou mayest be wise at thy latter end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 16:9; Esaias 14:24; Proverbs 16:1"> There are many thoughts in a man's heart; but the counsel of the Lord abides for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 14:6–8; 2 Corinthians 8:12; Chronicles II 6:8"> Mercy is a fruit to a man: and a poor man is better than a rich liar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 5:6; Proverbs 14:26–27; Proverbs 12:21"> The fear of the Lord is life to a man:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, 'but he that is without fear (sc. of the Lord) shall dwell' etc.">c</a></sup>and he shall lodge without fear in places where knowledge is not seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 15:19; Proverbs 6:9–10; Proverbs 24:30–34"> He that unjustly hides his hands in his bosom, will not even bring them up to his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 21:11; Proverbs 17:10; Proverbs 15:5"> When a pestilent character is scourged, a simple man is made wiser: and if thou reprove a wise man, he will understand discretion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 10:5; Proverbs 17:2; Proverbs 28:24"> He that dishonours his father, and drives away his mother, shall be disgraced and shall be exposed to reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 4:24; 1 Timothy 6:3–5; Matthew 7:15"> A son who ceases to attend to the instruction of a father will cherish evil designs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 15:16; Job 34:7; Luke 18:2–4"> He that becomes surety for a foolish child will despise the ordinance: and the mouth of ungodly men shall drink down judgments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 26:3; Proverbs 10:13; Proverbs 9:12"> Scourges are preparing for the intemperate, and punishments likewise for fools.</span>
