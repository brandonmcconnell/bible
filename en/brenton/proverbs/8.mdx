<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 1:20–21; Matthew 3:3; John 7:37"> Thou shalt proclaim wisdom, that understanding may be obedient to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 9:14; Proverbs 9:3"> For she is on lofty eminences, and stands in the midst of the ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 18:20; Acts 5:20; Job 29:7"> For she sits by the gates of princes, and sings in the entrances, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 11:15; John 3:16; Psalms 50:1"> You, O men, I exhort; and utter my voice to the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 1:22; Acts 26:18; Revelation 3:17–18"> O ye simple,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="For the use of ἄκατος and πανοῦργος in this book, see Appendix.">a</a></sup>understand subtlety, and ye that are untaught, imbibe knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 1:26; Matthew 7:28–29; Proverbs 23:16"> Hearken to me; for I will speak solemn truths; and will produce right sayings from my lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 37:30; John 8:14; John 17:17"> For my throat shall meditate truth; and false lips are an abomination before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 7:46; Psalms 12:6; Proverbs 8:13"> All the words of my mouth are in righteousness; there is nothing in them wrong or perverse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 14:6; James 1:5; John 7:17"> They are all evident to those that understand, and right to those that find knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 2:4–5; Psalms 119:127; Psalms 119:72"> Receive instruction, and not silver; and knowledge rather than tried gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 3:14–15; Proverbs 4:5–7; Proverbs 16:16"> For wisdom is better than precious stones; and no valuable substance is of equal worth with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esaias 28:26; Exodus 35:30–4; Romans 11:33"> I wisdom have dwelt with counsel and knowledge, and I have called upon understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 97:10; Proverbs 16:6; Kings I 2:3"> The fear of the Lord hates unrighteousness, and insolence, and pride, and the ways of wicked men; and I hate the perverse ways of bad men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:19; Esaias 9:6; Esaias 40:14"> Counsel and safety are mine: prudence is mine, and strength is mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 13:1; Daniel 2:21; Matthew 28:18"> By me kings reign, and princes decree justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> By me nobles become great, and monarchs by me rule over the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 14:23; John 14:21; Psalms 91:14"> I love those that love me; and they that seek me shall find me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:16; Matthew 6:33; Luke 16:11–12"> Wealth and glory belong to me; yea, abundant possessions and righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 3:14; Proverbs 10:20; Ecclesiastes 7:12"> It is better to have my fruit than to have gold and precious stones; and my produce is better than choice silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:32; John 10:27–28; Psalms 32:8"> I walk in ways of righteousness, and am conversant with the paths of judgment;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 1:4; Proverbs 24:4; Matthew 25:46"> that I may divide substance to them that love me, and may fill their treasures with good things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 1:4; Proverbs 24:4; Matthew 25:46"> If I declare to you the things that daily happen, I will remember also to recount the things of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 1:17; John 1:1–2; Proverbs 3:19"> The Lord made me the beginning of his ways for his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 17:24; Psalms 2:6; Genesis 1:26"> He established me<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. before the age.">b</a></sup>before time was in the beginning, before he made the earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 3:20; Genesis 1:2; 1 John 4:9"> even before he made the depths; before the fountains of water came forth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 90:2; Psalms 102:25–28; Job 38:4–11"> before the mountains were settled, and before all hills, he begets me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 1:1–31"> The Lord made countries and uninhabited tracts, and the highest inhabited parts of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Esaias 40:22; Job 26:10; Proverbs 3:19"> When he prepared the heaven, I was present with him; and when he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, marked out.">c</a></sup>prepared his throne upon the winds:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and when he strengthened the clouds above; and when he secured the fountains of the earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 104:9; Jeremias 5:22; Genesis 1:9–10"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See Appendix.">d</a></sup>and when he strengthened the foundations of the earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 1:1–3; John 1:18; Esaias 42:1"> I was by him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or, arranging all things.">e</a></sup>suiting myself to him, I was that wherein he took delight; and daily I rejoiced in his presence continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 16:3; John 13:1; Psalms 40:6–8"> For he rejoiced when he had completed the world, and rejoiced among the children of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 11:28; Psalms 119:1–2; Psalms 1:1–4"> Now then, my son, hear me: blessed is the man who shall hearken to me, and the mortal who shall keep my ways;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="appendix has Alexandrine text as: “And blessed are they that keep my ways.”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 11:28; Matthew 7:24; Proverbs 3:13"> watching daily at my doors, waiting at the posts of my entrances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 3:36; 1 John 5:11–12; Proverbs 12:2"> For my outgoings are the outgoings of life, and in them is prepared favour from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 15:32; Proverbs 1:31; 1 Corinthians 16:22"> But they that sin against me act wickedly against their own souls: and they that hate me love death.</span>
