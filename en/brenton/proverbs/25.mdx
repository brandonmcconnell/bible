<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 1:1; Kings III 4:32; Michæas 1:1"> These are the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Possibly genuine, q. d. beyond doubt.">a</a></sup>miscellaneous instructions of Solomon, which the friends of Ezekias king of Judea copied out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:29; Romans 11:33–34; Job 11:7–8"> The glory of God conceals a matter: but the glory of a king honours business.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Esaias 55:9; Psalms 103:11; Kings III 4:29"> Heaven is high, and earth is deep, and a king's heart is unsearchable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:20–21; Jezekiel 22:18; Proverbs 17:3"> Beat the drossy silver, and it shall be made entirely pure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 16:12; Proverbs 20:8; Psalms 101:7–8"> Slay the ungodly from before the king, and his throne shall prosper in righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 27:2; Proverbs 25:27; Proverbs 16:19"> Be not boastful in the presence of the king, and remain not in the places of princes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 14:7–11; Luke 18:14; 1 Peter 5:5"> for it is better for thee that it should be said, Come up to me, than that one should humble thee in the presence of the prince; speak of that which thine eyes have seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 5:25; Proverbs 17:14; Proverbs 18:6"> Get not suddenly into a quarrel, lest thou repent at last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 18:5–17; Proverbs 11:13; Proverbs 20:19"> Whenever thy friend shall reproach thee, retreat backward, despise him not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 119:39"> lest thy friend continue to reproach thee, so thy quarrel and enmity shall not depart, but shall be to thee like death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 119:39"> Favour and friendship set a man free, which do thou keep for thyself, lest thou be made liable to reproach; but take heed to thy ways peaceably.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 15:23; Esaias 50:4; Ecclesiastes 12:10"> As a golden apple in a necklace of sardius, so is it to speak a wise word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 141:5; Proverbs 15:31–32; Proverbs 15:5"> In an ear-ring of gold a precious sardius is also set; so is a wise word to an obedient ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 13:17; Proverbs 25:25; Philippians 2:25–30"> As a fall of snow in the time of harvest is good against heat, so a faithful messenger refreshes those that send him; for he helps the souls of his employers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 20:6; Luke 14:11; 2 Peter 2:15–19"> As winds and clouds and rains are most evident objects, so is he that boasts of a false gift.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; Ecclesiastes 10:4; Proverbs 16:14"> In long-suffering is prosperity to kings, and a soft tongue breaks the bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 25:27; Ephesians 5:18; Proverbs 24:13–14"> Having found honey, eat only what is enough, lest haply thou be filled, and vomit it up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 19:2–3; Judges 19:18–21; Romans 15:24"> Enter sparingly into thy friend's house, lest he be satiated with thy company, and hate thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Proverbs 12:18; James 3:6"> As a club, and a dagger, and a pointed arrow, so also is a man who bears false witness against his friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esaias 36:6; Jezekiel 29:6–7; Job 6:14–20"> The way of the wicked and the foot of the transgressor shall perish in an evil day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Ecclesiastes 3:4; Esaias 58:7"> As vinegar is bad for a sore, so trouble befalling the body afflicts the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Ecclesiastes 3:4; Esaias 58:7"> As a moth in a garment, and a worm in wood, so the grief of a man hurts the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 12:20–21; Matthew 5:44; Exodus 23:4–5"> If thine enemy hunger, feed him; if he thirst, give him drink;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings II 16:12; Matthew 10:13; 1 Corinthians 15:18"> for so doing thou shalt heap coals of fire upon his head, and the Lord shall reward thee with good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 37:22; Psalms 15:3; Romans 1:30"> The north wind raises clouds; so an impudent face provokes the tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 21:19; Proverbs 21:9; Proverbs 27:15–16"> It is better to dwell on a corner of the roof, than with a railing woman in an open house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 15:30; Romans 10:15; Psalms 63:1"> As cold water is agreeable to a thirsting soul, so is a good message from a land far off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jezekiel 34:18–19; Kings I 22:14–18; 1 Thessalonians 2:15"> As if one should stop a well, and corrupt a spring of water, so is it unseemly for a righteous man to fall before an ungodly man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 5:44; Proverbs 27:2; Philippians 2:3"> It is not good to eat much honey; but it is right to honour venerable sayings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 16:32; Proverbs 22:24; Chronicles II 32:5"> As a city whose walls are broken down, and which is unfortified, so is a man who does anything without counsel.</span>
