<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 31:10–31; Proverbs 24:3–4; Proverbs 21:9"> Wise women build houses: but a foolish one digs hers down with her hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:2–3; Acts 10:35; Proverbs 28:6"> He that walks uprightly fears the Lord; but he that is perverse in his ways shall be dishonoured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 12:6; James 3:5–6; Revelation 12:11"> Out of the mouth of fools comes a rod of pride; but the lips of the wise preserve them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:9–11; Proverbs 13:23; Amos 4:6"> Where no oxen are, the cribs are clean; but where there is abundant produce, the strength of the ox is apparent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 6:19; Proverbs 19:5; Exodus 23:1"> A faithful witness does not lie; but an unjust witness kindles falsehoods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 1:5; Matthew 13:11–12; Matthew 11:25–27"> Thou shalt seek wisdom with bad men, and shalt not find it; but discretion is easily available with the prudent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; Ephesians 5:11; 1 Corinthians 5:11"> All things are adverse to a foolish man; but wise lips are the weapons of discretion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 5:17; 2 Timothy 3:13; James 3:13"> The wisdom of the prudent will understand their ways; but the folly of fools leads astray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 26:18–19; Proverbs 10:23; Romans 14:17–18"> The houses of transgressors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. shall owe. See Job 6. 21.">a</a></sup>will need purification; but the houses of the just are acceptable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 4:7; Proverbs 18:14; Proverbs 15:13"> If a man's<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, heart be sensitive.">b</a></sup>mind is intelligent, his soul is sorrowful; and when he rejoices, he has no fellowship with pride.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 3:33; Proverbs 12:7; Matthew 7:26–27"> The houses of ungodly men shall be utterly destroyed; but the tabernacles of them that walk uprightly shall stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 16:25; Matthew 7:13–14; Proverbs 12:15"> There is a way which seems to be right with men, but the ends of it<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. come.">c</a></sup>reach to the depth of hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:2; James 4:9; Ecclesiastes 2:10–11"> Grief mingles not with mirth; and joy in the end comes to grief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 6:8; Proverbs 12:14; Hebrews 3:12"> A<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Lit. bold-hearted.">d</a></sup>stout-hearted man shall be filled with his own ways; and a good man with his own thoughts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 5:17; Proverbs 14:8; 1 John 4:1"> The simple believes every word: but the prudent man betakes himself to after-thought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 22:3; Proverbs 3:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:22"> A wise man fears, and departs from evil; but the fool trusts in himself, and joins himself with the transgressor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 14:29; James 1:19; Ecclesiastes 7:9"> A passionate man acts inconsiderately; but a sensible man bears up under many things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Peter 1:18; 1 Peter 5:4; Jeremias 44:17"> Fools shall have mischief for their portion; but the prudent shall take fast hold of understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 42:6; Malachias 4:3; Exodus 8:8"> Evil men shall fall before the good; and the ungodly shall attend at the gates of the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 19:4; Proverbs 10:15; Proverbs 19:6–7"> Friends will hate poor friends; but the friends of the rich are many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Proverbs 28:27; Proverbs 11:12"> He that dishonours the needy sins: but he that has pity on the poor is most blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 12:2; John 1:17; Psalms 61:7"> They that go astray devise evils: but the good devise mercy and truth. The framers of evil do not understand mercy and truth: but compassion and faithfulness are with the framers of good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 28:19; Proverbs 12:24; 2 Thessalonians 3:10–12"> With every one who is careful there is abundance: but the pleasure-taking and indolent shall be in want.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:11–12; Luke 12:19–20; Luke 16:19–25"> A prudent man is the crown of the wise: but the occupation of fools is evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 14:5; Acts 26:16–20; 2 Peter 3:3"> A faithful witness shall deliver a soul from evil: but a deceitful man kindles falsehoods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 19:23; Jeremias 32:39–40; Psalms 115:13–14"> In the fear of the Lord is strong confidence: and he leaves his children a support.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Revelation 21:6; Proverbs 13:14; Proverbs 2:10–18"> The commandment of the Lord is a fountain of life; and it causes men to turn aside from the snare of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Kings III 4:20–21; Kings IV 13:7; Exodus 1:22"> In a populous nation is the glory of a king: but in the failure of people is the ruin of a prince.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="James 1:19; Ecclesiastes 7:9; Proverbs 14:17"> A man slow to wrath abounds in wisdom: but a man of impatient spirit is very foolish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 17:22; Proverbs 4:23; Proverbs 3:8"> A meek-spirited man is a healer of the heart: but a sensitive heart is a corruption of the bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 22:2; Proverbs 17:5; Proverbs 22:16"> He that oppresses the needy provokes his Maker: but he that honours him has pity upon the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:18; 2 Corinthians 5:8; Psalms 23:4"> The ungodly shall be driven away in his wickedness: but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Comp. Heb.">e</a></sup>he who is secure in his own holiness is just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 12:16; Proverbs 13:16; Proverbs 12:23"> There is wisdom in the good heart of a man: but in the heart of fools it is not discerned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 107:34; Deuteronomy 28:1–68; Deuteronomy 29:18–28"> Righteousness exalts a nation: but sins diminish tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 24:45–51; Luke 12:42–48; Matthew 25:23"> An understanding servant is acceptable to a king; and by his good behaviour he removes disgrace.</span>
