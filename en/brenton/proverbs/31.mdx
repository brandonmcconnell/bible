<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings I 25:18–22; Kings I 25:26–27"> For she employs all her living for her husband's good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Titus 2:5; 1 Timothy 5:10; 2 Thessalonians 3:10–12"> Gathering wool and flax, she makes it serviceable with her hands.</span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 12:11; Mark 1:35; Luke 12:42"> And she rises by night, and gives food to her household, and appointed tasks to her maidens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 13:44; Song of Solomon 8:12; Joshua 15:18"> She views a farm, and buys it: and with the fruit of her hands she plants a possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 38:3; 1 Peter 1:13; Ephesians 6:10"> She strongly girds her loins, and strengthens her arms for work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:9; Matthew 25:3–10; Psalms 127:2"> And she finds by experience that working is good; and her candle goes not out all night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 35:25–26"> She reaches forth her arms to needful works, and applies her hands to the spindle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 4:28; Hebrews 13:16; Proverbs 22:9"> And she opens her hands to the needy, and reaches out fruit to the poor.</span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 45:22; Kings II 1:24; Proverbs 25:20"> Her husband is not anxious about those at home when he tarries anywhere abroad: for all<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. those with her.">a</a></sup>her household are clothed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 19:8; Proverbs 7:16; Genesis 41:42"> She makes for her husband<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Comp. <span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 12:4; Ruth 4:1; Deuteronomy 16:18"> And her husband becomes a distinguished person in the gates, when he sits in council with the old inhabitants of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jezekiel 27:16; Luke 16:19; Proverbs 31:19"> She makes fine linens, and sells girdles to the Chananites: she opens her mouth heedfully and with propriety, and controls her tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 40:10; Romans 13:14; Job 29:14"> She puts on strength and honour; and rejoices in the last days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Proverbs 16:24; Proverbs 10:31"> The ways of her household are careful, and she eats not the bread of idleness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:10; Titus 2:4; 2 Thessalonians 3:6"> But she opens her mouth wisely, and according to law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:15–17; 2 Timothy 1:5; Kings III 2:19"> And her kindness to them sets up her children for them, and they grow rich, and her husband praises her.</span>
