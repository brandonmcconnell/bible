<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> She prospered their works in the hand of the holy prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> They went through the wilderness that was not inhabited, and pitched tents in places where there lay no way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> They stood against their enemies, and were avenged of their adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> When they were thirsty, they called upon thee, and water was given them out of the flinty rock, and their thirst was quenched out of the hard stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> For by what things their enemies were punished, by the same they in their need were benefited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> For instead of a fountain of a perpetual running river troubled with foul blood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> for a manifest reproof of that commandment, whereby the infants were slain, thou gavest unto them abundance of water by a means which they hoped not for:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> declaring by that thirst then how thou hadst punished their adversaries.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> For when they were tried, albeit but in mercy chastised, they knew how the ungodly were judged in wrath and tormented, thirsting in another manner than the just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> For these thou didst admonish and try, as a father: but the others, as a severe king, thou didst condemn and punish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Whether they were absent or present, they were vexed alike.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> For a double grief came upon them, and a groaning for the remembrance of things past.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> For when they heard by their own punishments the other to be benefited, they had some feeling of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For whom they rejected with scorn, when he was long before thrown out at the casting forth of the infants, him in the end, when they saw what came to pass, they admired.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> But for the foolish devices of their wickedness, wherewith being deceived they worshipped serpents void of reason, and vile beasts, thou didst send a multitude of unreasonable beasts upon them for vengeance:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> that they might know, that wherewithal a man sinneth, by the same shall he be punished.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> For thy Almighty hand, that made the world of matter without form, wanted not means to send among them a multitude of bears, or fierce lions,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> or unknown wild beasts, full of rage, newly created, breathing out either a fiery vapour, or filthy scents of scattered smoke, or shooting horrible sparkles out of their eyes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> whereof not only the harm might dispatch them at once, but also the terrible sight utterly destroy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Yea, and without these might they have fallen down with one blast, being persecuted of vengeance, and scattered abroad through the breath of thy power: but thou hast ordered all things in measure and number and weight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> For thou canst shew thy great strength at all times when thou wilt; and who may withstand the power of thine arm?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> For the whole world before thee is as a little grain of the balance, yea, as a drop of the morning dew that falleth down upon the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> But thou hast mercy upon all; for thou canst do all things, and winkest at the sins of men, because they should amend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> For thou lovest all the things that are, and abhorrest nothing which thou hast made: for never wouldest thou have made any thing, if thou hadst hated it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And how could any thing have endured, if it had not been thy will? or been preserved, if not called by thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> But thou sparest all: for they are thine, O Lord, thou lover of souls.</span>
</div>
</div>
