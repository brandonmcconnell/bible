<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> For the ungodly said, reasoning with themselves, but not aright, Our life is short and tedious, and in the death of a man there is no remedy: neither was there any man known to have returned from the grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> For we are born at all adventure: and we shall be hereafter as though we had never been: for the breath in our nostrils is as smoke, and a little spark in the moving of our heart:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> which being extinguished, our body shall be turned to ashes, and our spirit shall vanish as the soft air,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> and our name shall be forgotten in time, and no man shall have our works in remembrance, and our life shall pass away as the trace of a cloud, and shall be dispersed as a mist, that is driven away with the beams of the sun, and overcome with the heat thereof.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> For our time is a very shadow that passeth away; and after our end there is no returning: for it is fast sealed, so that no man cometh again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Come on therefore, let us enjoy the good things that are present: and let us speedily use the creatures like as in youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Let us fill ourselves with costly wine and ointments: and let no flower of the spring pass by us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Let us crown ourselves with rosebuds, before they be withered:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> let none of us go without his part of our voluptuousness: let us leave tokens of our joyfulness in every place: for this is our portion, and our lot is this.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Let us oppress the poor righteous man, let us not spare the widow, nor reverence the ancient grey hairs of the aged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Let our strength be the law of justice: for that which is feeble is found to be nothing worth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Therefore let us lie in wait for the righteous; because he is not for our turn, and he is clean contrary to our doings: he upbraideth us with our offending the law, and objecteth to our infamy the transgressings of our education.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> He professeth to have the knowledge of God: and he calleth himself the child of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> He was made to reprove our thoughts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> He is grievous unto us even to behold: for his life is not like other men's, his ways are of another fashion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> We are esteemed of him as counterfeits: he abstaineth from our ways as from filthiness: he pronounceth the end of the just to be blessed, and maketh his boast that God is his father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Let us see if his words be true: and let us prove what shall happen in the end of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> For if the just man be the son of God, he will help him, and deliver him from the hand of his enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Let us examine him with despitefulness and torture, that we may know his meekness, and prove his patience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Let us condemn him with a shameful death: for by his own saying he shall be respected.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Such things they did imagine, and were deceived: for their own wickedness hath blinded them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> As for the mysteries of God, they knew them not: neither hoped they for the wages of righteousness, nor discerned a reward for blameless souls.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> For God created man to be immortal, and made him to be an image of his own eternity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Nevertheless through envy of the devil came death into the world: and they that are of his side do find it.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
