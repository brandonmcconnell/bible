<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings III 21:27; Kings IV 18:37; Chronicles II 32:20–22"> And it came to pass when king Ezekias heard it, that he rent his clothes, and put on sackcloth, and went into the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esaias 1:1; Esaias 2:1; Matthew 4:14"> And he sent Heliakim the steward, and Somnas the scribe, and the elders of the priests, clothed with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. sackclothes.">a</a></sup>sackcloth, to Esaias the prophet the son of Amos.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 30:5–7; Esaias 66:9; Osee 13:13"> And they said to him, Thus says Ezekias, This day is a day of tribulation, and rebuke, and provocation: for the children are come to the travail-pangs, but the mother has no strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings II 16:12; Romans 9:27; Joshua 14:12"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. if by any means.">b</a></sup>Peradventure the Lord thy God will hear all the words of Rapsakes, whom the king of Assyria his master has sent to reproach the living God and to revile him with the words which the Lord thy God has heard: and thou shalt<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. take.">c</a></sup>offer thy prayer for the remnant that is found.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> So the servants of king Ezekias came to Esaias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings IV 18:35; Kings IV 18:17; Esaias 37:6–38"> And Esaias said to them, Thus shall ye say to your master, Thus saith the Lord, Be not afraid of the words which thou hast heard, wherewith the servants of the king of the Assyrians have blasphemed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Obdias 1:1; Kings IV 7:6; Psalms 18:14–15"> Behold, I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. give.">d</a></sup>send a blast upon him, and he shall hear a report, and shall return to his own land; and I will overthrow him with the sword in his own land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings IV 18:14; Joshua 10:29; Joshua 12:11"> So Rapsakes returned, and found the king of Assyria warring against Lobna: for he heard that he had departed from Lachis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings I 23:27; Esaias 37:9; Kings IV 18:17"> And he heard concerning Tharaca king of the Ethiopians, saying, Behold, he is come forth to fight with thee: and he returned, and sent messengers to Ezekias, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings IV 18:5; Kings IV 18:29–30; Chronicles II 32:15–19"> Let not thy God on whom thou trustest encourage thee, saying, Jerusalem shall not be delivered into the hands of the king of the Assyrians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esaias 10:8–11; Chronicles II 32:13–14; Kings IV 19:17–18"> Behold, thou hast heard all that the kings of the Assyrians have done in all the lands, to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. curse them. q. d. devote to destruction.">e</a></sup>waste them utterly: and shalt thou be delivered?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings IV 17:6; Genesis 11:31; Jezekiel 27:23"> Have the gods of the nations at all delivered them, whom my fathers destroyed; both Gozan, and Charran, and Raphis, and the sons of Edem who were in Thaesthen?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings IV 18:34; Kings IV 17:24; Esaias 11:11"> Where is the king of Hæmath, and the king of Arphad? and where is the king of the city of Seppharvaim, of Ana, and Aba?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esaias 37:14; Psalms 91:1–2; Psalms 123:1–4"> And Ezekias took<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. the books.">f</a></sup>the letter from the hand of the messengers, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. them.">g</a></sup>read it: and he went up to the house of the Lord, and Ezekias spread it before the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esaias 44:6; Kings IV 5:15; Daniel 4:34–35"> and said, O Lord God of Israel, that dwellest over the cherubs, thou art the only God in all the kingdoms of the earth; thou hast made heaven and earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Chronicles II 6:40; Psalms 31:2; Kings IV 19:4"> Incline thine ear, O Lord, and hear: open, Lord, thine eyes, and see: and hear the words of Sennacherim, which he has sent to reproach the living God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Chronicles I 5:26; Job 9:2; 1 Corinthians 14:25"> For truly, Lord, the kings of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. the Assyrians.">h</a></sup>Assyria have wasted the nations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 17:29; Esaias 44:9–20; Jeremias 10:3–9"> and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. gave.">i</a></sup>have cast their gods into the fire: because they are no gods, but the works of men's hands, wood and stone; and they have destroyed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 83:18; Kings III 8:43; Psalms 67:1–2"> And now, O Lord our God, deliver us out of his hand, and all the kingdoms of the earth shall know that thou alone art the Lord God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings IV 20:5; Chronicles II 32:20–21; Jeremias 33:3"> And Esaias the son of Amos sent to Ezekias, saying, Thus saith the Lord God of hosts, the God of Israel, I have heard<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Lit. what things thou hast prayed.">j</a></sup>thy prayer to me concerning Sennacherim king of the Assyrians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Lamentations 2:13; Job 16:4; Jeremias 14:17"> This is the word which the Lord has spoken against him; The virgin daughter of Sion has made light of thee, and mocked thee; the daughter of Jerusalem has shaken her head at thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Esaias 5:24; Psalms 71:22; Esaias 30:11–12"> Whom hast thou reproached, and whom hast thou reviled? and against whom hast thou lifted up thy voice, and raised thine eyes on high? Is it against the Holy One of Israel?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings IV 18:17; Psalms 20:7; Esaias 10:18"> By<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gr. by the hand of.">k</a></sup>thy messengers thou hast reproached the Lord, and hast said, I will go up with the multitude of my chariots, to the height of the mountains, to the sides of Libanus, and I have cut down the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Gr. bulk or size.">l</a></sup>height of his cedar, and his choice cypresses; and I have come into the midst of the forest and of Carmel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Esaias 19:6; Exodus 15:9; Kings II 17:13"> I have<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Alex. reads ἐφύλαξα.">m</a></sup>refreshed myself, and have drunk strange waters, and I have dried up with the sole of my foot all the rivers of fortified places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Esaias 45:7; Esaias 10:5–6; Esaias 37:26–27"> I have brought about the matter, I have brought it to a conclusion; and it is come to the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Gr. captivities.">n</a></sup>destruction of the bands of warlike prisoners, even of strong cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremias 51:30; Psalms 102:11; Jeremias 51:32"> And they that dwelt in them were weak in hand, they quaked and were confounded, they became as grass of the field, or as the green herb, the grass growing on houses, and that which is trodden down<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or, before it stands up.">o</a></sup>by him that stands upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Esaias 37:28–29; Jeremias 23:23–24; Psalms 139:1–11"> But I know thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Gr. seat.">p</a></sup>down-sitting, and thy going forth, and thy rage against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jezekiel 29:4; Jezekiel 38:4; Kings IV 19:33"> Because thou wast angry against me, and thy fierceness is come up into my ears, therefore will I put my hooks in thy nostrils, and my bridle in thy lips, and I will turn thee back by the way by which thou camest.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 25:20–22; Kings IV 20:8–9; Exodus 3:12"> And this shall be a sign to thee; eat this year the things that grow of themselves, and in the second year the things which spring up: and in the third year let there be sowing, and reaping, and planting of vineyards, and eat ye the fruit of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Kings IV 19:4; Esaias 27:6; Chronicles II 32:22–23"> And he shall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Gr. add.">q</a></sup>increase<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Gr. τὸ διασες. οἴκον.">r</a></sup>him that has escaped of the house of Juda: and the remnant shall strike root beneath, and it shall produce fruit above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Esaias 9:7; Romans 11:5; Romans 9:27"> For from Jerusalem shall go forth a remnant, and he that escapes from the mountain of Sion: the zeal of the Lord of hosts shall do this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Esaias 8:7–10; Kings II 20:15; Esaias 37:33–35"> Is it not so?<br />Thus saith the Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or, of. Heb.">s</a></sup>concerning the king of the Assyrians, He shall not enter into this city, and he shall not shoot<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Gr. a weapon.">t</a></sup>an arrow there, neither shall a shield<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Gr. come against it beforehand, etc.">u</a></sup>come against it, neither shall he heap a mound against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Kings IV 19:28; Kings IV 19:36"> By the way by which he comes, by it shall he return, and he shall not enter into this city, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Kings III 11:12–13; Kings IV 20:6; Kings III 15:4"> And I will defend this city as with a shield, for my own sake, and for my servant David's sake.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Chronicles II 32:21–22; Kings II 24:16; Exodus 12:29–30"> And it came to pass at night that the angel of the Lord went forth, and smote in the camp of the Assyrians a hundred and eighty-five thousand: and they rose early in the morning, and, behold, these were all dead corpses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jonas 1:2; Kings IV 19:7; Kings IV 19:33"> And Sennacherim king of the Assyrians departed, and went and returned, and dwelt in Nineve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 4:2; Genesis 8:4; Kings IV 19:7"> And it came to pass, while he was worshipping in the house of Meserach his god, that Adramelech and Sarasar his sons smote him with the sword: and they escaped into the land of Ararath; and Asordan his son reigned in his stead.</span>
</div>
