<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles I 3:11; Kings IV 11:21; Kings IV 11:1–4"> Joas was<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. a son of seven years in his reigning.">a</a></sup>seven years old when he began to reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Chronicles II 24:17–22; Chronicles II 26:4; Chronicles II 25:2"> Joas<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. reigned.">b</a></sup>began to reign in the seventh year of Ju, and he reigned forty years in Jerusalem: and his mother's name was Sabia of Bersabee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings IV 14:4; Kings III 22:43; Kings III 15:14"> And Joas did that which was right in the sight of the Lord all the days that Jodae the priest instructed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings IV 22:4; Exodus 35:5; Chronicles I 29:3–9"> Only there were not any of the high places removed, and the people still sacrificed there, and burned incense on the high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esaias 58:12; Kings III 11:27; Kings IV 22:5–6"> And Joas said to the priests, As for all the money of the holy things that is brought into the house of the Lord, the money of valuation, as each man brings the money of valuation, all the money which<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. it may come into the heart, etc.">c</a></sup>any man may feel disposed to bring into the house of the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Chronicles II 29:34; 1 Peter 5:2; Esaias 56:10–12"> let the priests take it to themselves, every man from the proceeds of his sale: and they shall repair the breaches of the house in all places wheresoever a breach shall be found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Chronicles II 24:16; Chronicles II 24:5–14; Kings IV 11:4"> And it came to pass in the twenty-third year of king Joas the priests had not repaired the breaches of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And king Joas called Jodae the priest, and the other priests, and said to them, Why have ye not repaired the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. breach.">d</a></sup>breaches of the house? now then receive no more money from your sales, for ye shall give it to repair the breaches of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 12:41; Psalms 84:10; Jeremias 35:4"> And the priests consented to receive no more money of the people, and not to repair the breaches of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings II 8:17; Kings IV 22:12; Kings IV 19:2"> And Jodae the priest took a chest, and bored a hole in the lid of it, and set it by the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The Gr. is a Hebrew word in Greek letters.">e</a></sup>altar in the house of a man belonging to the house of the Lord, and the priests that kept the door put therein all the money that was found in the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings IV 22:5–6; Chronicles II 24:11–12; Chronicles II 34:9–11"> And it came to pass, when they saw that there was much money in the chest, that the king's scribe and the high priest went up, and they tied up and counted the money that was found in the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings IV 22:5–6; Kings III 5:17–18; Ezra and Nehemiah 3:7"> And they gave the money that had been<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. prepared.">f</a></sup>collected into the hands of them that wrought the works, the overseers of the house of the Lord; and they gave it out to the carpenters and to the builders that wrought in the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Chronicles II 24:14; Kings III 7:48–50; Ezra and Nehemiah 1:9–11"> And to the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. wall-builders.">g</a></sup>masons, and to the hewers of stone, to purchase timber and hewn stone to repair the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. breach.">h</a></sup>breaches of the house of the Lord, for all that was spent on the house of the Lord to repair it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Only there<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. shall not be made. This change of future and past is frequent.">i</a></sup>were not to be made for the house of the Lord silver<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gr. doors.">j</a></sup>plates, studs, bowls, or trumpets, any vessel of gold or vessel of silver, of the money that was brought<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gr. in.">k</a></sup>into the house of the Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings IV 22:7; 3 John 1:5; 1 Corinthians 4:2–3"> for they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Gr. will give, vide v. 13.">l</a></sup>were to give it to the workmen, and they repaired therewith the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 7:7; Leviticus 5:15–18; Leviticus 4:24"> Also they took no account of the men into whose hands they gave the money to give to the workmen, for they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Heb. באמכה הס.">m</a></sup>acted faithfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Chronicles II 24:23–24; Luke 9:53; Jeremias 42:15"> Money for a sin-offering, and money for a trespass-offering, whatever happened to be brought into the house of the Lord, went to the priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings III 15:18; Kings IV 18:15–16; Kings IV 12:4"> Then went up Azael king of Syria, and fought against Geth, and took it: and Azael set his face to go against Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings III 14:29; Kings III 14:19; Kings III 11:41"> And Joas king of Juda took all the holy things which Josaphat, and Joram, Ochozias, his fathers, and kings of Juda had consecrated, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Gr. his own holy things.">n</a></sup>what he had himself dedicated, and all the gold that was found in the treasures of the Lord's house and the king's house, and he sent them to Azael king of Syria; and he went up from Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Chronicles II 24:24–27; Kings IV 14:5; Kings II 5:9"> And the rest of the acts of Joas, and all that he did, behold, are not these things written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Juda?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings IV 14:1; Chronicles II 24:26–27"> And his servants rose up and made a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Complut. πάντα.">o</a></sup>conspiracy, and smote Joas in the house of Mallo that is in Sela.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And Jezirchar the son of Jemuath, and Jezabuth Somer's son, his servants, smote him, and he died; and they buried him with his fathers in the city of David: and Amessias his son reigned in his stead.</span>
