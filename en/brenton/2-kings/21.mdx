<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esaias 62:4; Chronicles II 32:33–9; Matthew 1:10"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. a son of 12 years in his reigning.">a</a></sup>Manasses was twelve years old when he began to reign, and he reigned fifty-five years in Jerusalem: and his mother's name was Apsiba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:31; Chronicles II 36:14; Chronicles II 33:2–4"> And he did that which was evil in the eyes of the Lord, according to the abominations of the nations which the Lord cast out from before the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings IV 18:4; Kings IV 17:16; Deuteronomy 17:3"> And he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. returned and built.">b</a></sup>built again the high places, which Ezekias his father had demolished; and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, built.">c</a></sup>set up an altar to Baal, and made groves as Achaab king of Israel made them; and worshipped all the host of heaven, and served them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings III 8:29; Kings II 7:13; Jeremias 32:34"> And he built an altar in the house of the Lord, whereas he had said, In Jerusalem I will place my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings III 7:12; Kings IV 23:4; Kings IV 23:12"> And he built an altar to all the host of heaven in the two courts of the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings IV 17:17; Leviticus 19:31; Leviticus 18:21"> And he caused his sons to pass through the fire, and used divination and auspices, and made<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Lit. peculiar places cut off.">d</a></sup>groves, and multiplied<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Alex. θελητὴν.">e</a></sup>wizards, so as to do that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, to provoke him to anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Chronicles II 7:16; Kings III 9:3; Kings II 7:13"> And he set up the graven image of the grove in the house of which the Lord said to David, and to Solomon his son, In this house, and in Jerusalem which I have chosen out of all the tribes of Israel, will I even place my name for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings II 7:10; Deuteronomy 5:28–29; Psalms 81:11–16"> And I will not again remove the foot of Israel from the land which I gave to their fathers, even of those who shall keep all that I commanded, according to all the commandments which my servant Moses commanded them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 29:12; Nehemiah 9:26; Kings III 14:16"> But they hearkened not; and Manasses led them astray to do evil in the sight of the Lord, beyond the nations whom the Lord utterly destroyed from before the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 23:34–37; Chronicles II 33:10; Chronicles II 36:15"> And the Lord spoke by his servants the prophets, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings IV 24:3–4; Kings III 21:26; Kings IV 21:9"> Forasmuch as Manasses the king of Juda has wrought all these evil abominations, beyond all that the Amorite did, who lived before him, and has led Juda also into sin by their idols,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 19:3; Kings I 3:11; Daniel 9:12"> it shall not be so. Thus saith the Lord God of Israel, Behold, I bring calamities upon Jerusalem and Juda, so that both the ears of every one that hears shall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. sound.">f</a></sup>tingle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Amos 7:7–8; Lamentations 2:8; Esaias 34:11"> And I will stretch out over Jerusalem the measure of Samaria, and the plummet of the house of Achaab: and I will wipe Jerusalem as a jar is wiped, and turned upside down in the wiping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremias 23:33; Kings IV 19:4; Psalms 89:38–45"> And I will reject the remnant of my inheritance, and will deliver them into the hands of their enemies; and they shall be for a plunder and for a spoil to all their enemies:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jezekiel 20:30; Deuteronomy 31:27; Jezekiel 20:21"> forasmuch as they have done wickedly in my sight, and have provoked me from the day that I brought out their fathers out of Egypt, even until this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 35:33; Chronicles II 33:9; Kings IV 21:11"> Moreover Manasses shed very much innocent blood, until he filled Jerusalem with it<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. mouth to mouth.">g</a></sup>from one end to the other, beside his sins with which he caused Juda to sin, in doing evil in the eyes of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings IV 20:20–21; Chronicles II 33:1–20"> And the rest of the acts of Manasses, and all that he did, and his sin which he sinned, are not these things written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Juda?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Chronicles II 33:20; Kings IV 21:26; Chronicles II 32:33"> And Manasses slept with his fathers, and was buried in the garden of his house, even in the garden of Oza: and Amos his son reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Chronicles II 33:21–23; Kings III 22:51; Chronicles I 3:14"> Twenty and two years old was Amos when he began to reign, and he reigned two years in Jerusalem: and his mother's name was Mesollam, daughter of Arus of Jeteba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 23:32; Kings IV 21:11; Acts 7:51"> And he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, as Manasses his father did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And he walked in all the way in which his father walked, and served the idols which his father served, and worshipped them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings IV 22:17; Kings III 11:33; Chronicles I 28:9"> And he forsook the Lord God of his fathers, and walked not in the way of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings IV 12:20; Kings IV 14:19; Chronicles II 33:24–25"> And the servants of Amos conspired against him, and slew the king in his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Kings IV 14:5; Kings IV 11:17; Kings IV 14:21"> And the people of the land<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. smote.">h</a></sup>slew all that had conspired against king Amos; and the people of the land made Josias his son king in his room.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings IV 20:20; Kings IV 21:17"> And the rest of the acts of Amos, even all that he did, behold, are not these written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Juda?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Kings IV 21:18; Matthew 1:10; Kings III 13:5"> And they buried him in his tomb in the garden of Oza: and Josias his son reigned in his stead.</span>
