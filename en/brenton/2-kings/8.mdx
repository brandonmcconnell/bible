<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Aggæus 1:11; Kings IV 4:31–35; Ruth 1:1"> And Elisaie spoke to the woman, whose son he had<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The Gr. signifies, to rekindle a fire.">a</a></sup>restored to life, saying, Arise, and go thou and thy house, and sojourn wherever thou mayest sojourn: for the Lord has called for a famine upon the land; indeed it is come upon the land for seven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:8; Judges 3:3; Kings I 27:1–3"> And the woman arose, and did according to the word of Elisaie, both she and her house; and they sojourned in the land of the Philistines seven years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 22:16; Luke 18:3–5; Kings IV 4:13"> And it came to pass after the expiration of the seven years, that the woman returned out of the land of the Philistines to the city; and came to cry to the king for her house and for her lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings IV 5:20–27; Kings IV 2:14; Kings IV 6:32"> And the king spoke to Giezi the servant of Elisaie the man of God, saying, Tell me, I pray thee, all the great things which Elisaie has done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings IV 4:35; Matthew 10:29–30; Kings IV 6:26"> And it came to pass, as he was telling the king how he had restored to life the dead son, behold, the woman whose son Elisaie restored to life came crying to the king for her house and for her lands. And Giezi said, My lord, O king, this is the woman, and this is her son, whom Elisaie restored to life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Proverbs 21:1; Kings II 9:7"> And the king asked the woman, and she told him: and the king appointed her a eunuch, saying, Restore all that was hers, and all the fruits of the field from the day that she left the land until now.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings III 11:24; Kings IV 6:24; Kings III 20:1"> And Elisaie came to Damascus; and the king of Syria the son of Ader was ill, and they brought him word, saying, The man of God is come hither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings III 19:15; Kings IV 1:2; Kings I 9:7"> And the king said to Azael, Take in thine hand<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The Gr. retains the Heb. word.">b</a></sup>a present, and go to meet the man of God, and enquire of the Lord by him, saying, Shall I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. live?">c</a></sup>recover of this my disease?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings IV 5:13; Kings IV 13:14; Kings III 19:15"> And Azael went to meet him, and he took a present in his hand, and all the good things of Damascus, forty camels' load, and came and stood before him, and said to Elisaie, Thy son the son of Ader, the king of Syria, has sent me to thee to enquire, saying, Shall I recover of this my disease?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 7:7; Genesis 2:17; Amos 7:1"> And Elisaie said, Go, say, Thou shalt certainly live; yet the Lord has shewed me that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Complut. and Ald. ἀποθανεῖται.">d</a></sup>thou shalt surely die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:41; Romans 9:2; Jeremias 9:18"> And he stood before him, and fixed his countenance till he was ashamed: and the man of God wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Osee 13:16; Naum 3:10; Kings IV 15:16"> And Azael said, Why does my lord weep? And he said, Because I know all the evil that thou wilt do to the children of Israel: thou wilt<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. send away.">e</a></sup>utterly destroy their strong holds with fire, and thou wilt slay their choice men with the sword, and thou wilt dash their infants against the ground, and their women with child thou wilt rip up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings I 17:43; Kings III 19:15; Kings II 9:8"> And Azael said, Who is thy servant? a dead dog, that he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. shall.">f</a></sup>should do this thing? And Elisaie said, The Lord has shewn me thee ruling over Syria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings IV 8:10; Matthew 26:16; Kings IV 5:25"> And he departed from Elisaie, and went in to his lord; and he said to him, What said Elisaie to thee? and he said, He said to me, Thou shalt surely live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings IV 8:13; Kings III 15:28; Kings III 11:26–37"> And it came to pass on the next day that he took a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="The Gr. is from the Heb. rug or quilt, etc.">g</a></sup>thick cloth, and dipped it in water, and put it on his face, and he died: and Azael reigned in his stead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings IV 1:17; Kings IV 3:1; Chronicles II 21:1–20"> In the fifth year<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. to.">h</a></sup>of Joram son of Achaab king of Israel, and while Josaphat was king of Juda, Joram the son of Josaphat king of Juda<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. reigned.">i</a></sup>began to reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Chronicles II 21:5–10"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gr. a son of 32 years in his reigning.">j</a></sup>Thirty and two years old was he when he began to reign, and he reigned eight years in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings IV 8:26; Kings III 11:1–5; Chronicles II 21:13"> And he walked in the way of the kings of Israel, as did the house of Achaab; for the daughter of Achaab was his wife: and he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings III 11:36; Kings II 7:12–13; Luke 1:32–33"> But the Lord would not destroy Juda for David his servant's sake, as he said he would give a light to him and to his sons continually.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings III 22:47; Kings IV 3:9; Kings IV 8:22"> In his days Edom revolted from under the hand of Juda, and they made a king over<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gr. himself.">k</a></sup>themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings II 18:17"> And Joram went up to Sior, and all the chariots that were with him: and it came to pass after he had arisen, that he smote Edom who compassed him about, and the captains of the chariots; and the people fled to their tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings IV 19:8; Joshua 21:13; Genesis 27:40"> Yet Edom revolted from under the hand of Juda till this day. Then Lobna revolted at that time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings IV 15:36; Kings III 11:41; Chronicles II 21:11–20"> And the rest of the acts of Joram, and all that he did, behold, are not these written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Juda?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Chronicles II 22:6; Chronicles II 25:23; Chronicles II 21:1"> So Joram slept with his fathers, and was buried with his fathers in the city of his father David: and Ochozias his son reigned in his stead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings IV 9:29; Kings IV 8:16–17; Chronicles II 21:20–6"> In the twelfth year<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Gr. to.">l</a></sup>of Joram son of Achaab king of Israel, Ochozias son of Joram<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Gr. reigned.">m</a></sup>began to reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Chronicles II 22:2; Kings IV 9:21–27; Kings IV 11:1"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Gr. a son of 22 years in his reigning.">n</a></sup>Twenty and two years old was Ochozias when he began to reign, and he reigned one year in Jerusalem: and the name of his mother was Gotholia, daughter of Ambri king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Chronicles II 22:3–4; 2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Ecclesiastes 7:26"> And he walked in the way of the house of Achaab, and did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, as did the house of Achaab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Chronicles II 22:5; Kings III 4:13; Chronicles II 19:2"> And he went with Joram the son of Achaab to war<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Gr. with.">o</a></sup>against Azael king of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="The Gr. word ἀλλοφύλοι is almost always applied elsewhere to the Philistines.">p</a></sup>the Syrians in Remmoth Galaad; and the Syrians wounded Joram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Kings IV 9:15–16; Kings IV 8:28; Chronicles II 22:5–7"> And king Joram returned to be healed in Jezrael of the wounds with which they wounded him in Remmoth, when he fought with Azael king of Syria. And Ochozias son of Joram went down to see Joram the son of Achaab in Jezrael, because he was sick.</span>
</div>
