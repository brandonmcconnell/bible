<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles II 28:27–1; Matthew 1:9–10; Chronicles I 3:13"> And it came to pass in the third year of Osee son of Ela king of Israel that Ezekias son of Achaz king of Juda<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. reigned.">a</a></sup>began to reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Chronicles II 29:1–2"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. a son of 25 years in his reigning.">b</a></sup>Five and twenty years old was he when he began to reign, and he reigned twenty and nine years in Jerusalem: and his mother's name was Abu, daughter of Zacharias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings IV 20:3; Kings III 11:4; Chronicles II 31:20–21"> And he did that which was right in the sight of the Lord, according to all that his father David did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Chronicles II 31:1; Numbers 21:8–9; Kings IV 18:22"> He<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, destroyed.">c</a></sup>removed the high places, and broke in pieces the pillars, and utterly destroyed the groves, and the brazen serpent which Moses made: because until those days the children of Israel burnt incense to it: and he called it Neesthan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings IV 23:25; Kings IV 19:10; Matthew 27:43"> He<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. hoped.">d</a></sup>trusted in the Lord God of Israel; and after him there was not any like him among the kings of Juda,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. and.">e</a></sup>nor among those that were before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 23:8; Deuteronomy 10:20; John 14:15"> And he clave to the Lord, he departed not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. from behind him.">f</a></sup>from following him; and he kept his commandments, as many as he commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Genesis 39:2–3; Kings IV 16:7"> And the Lord was with him; and he was wise in all that he undertook: and he revolted from the king of the Assyrians, and served him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esaias 14:29; Kings IV 17:9; Chronicles II 28:18"> He smote the Philistines even to Gaza, and to the border of it, from the tower of the watchmen even to the strong city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Osee 10:14; Kings IV 17:3–23; Kings IV 18:1"> And it came to pass in the fourth year of king Ezekias (this is the seventh year of Osee son of Ela king of Israel,) that Salamanassar king of the Assyrians came up against Samaria, and besieged it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 6:7; Amos 9:1–4; Michæas 1:6–9"> And he took it<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. from.">g</a></sup>at the end of three years, in the sixth year of Ezekias, (this is the ninth year of Osee king of Israel, when Samaria was taken.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Chronicles I 5:26; Kings IV 17:6; Esaias 37:12"> And the king of the Assyrians carried away the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. Samaria to the Assyrians.">h</a></sup>Samaritans to Assyria, and put them in Alaë and in Abor, by the river Gozan, and in the mountains of the Medes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17; 1 Peter 2:8; Michæas 3:4"> because they hearkened not to the voice of the Lord their God, and transgressed his covenant, even in all things that Moses the servant of the Lord commanded, and hearkened not to them, nor did them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Esaias 36:1–22; Chronicles II 32:1–23; Esaias 7:17–25"> And in the fourteenth year of king Ezekias came up Sennacherim king of the Assyrians against the strong cities of Juda, and took them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings IV 18:7; Luke 14:32; Kings III 20:4"> And Ezekias king of Juda sent messengers to the king of the Assyrians to Lachis, saying, I have offended; depart from me: whatsoever thou shalt lay upon me, I will bear. And the king of Assyria laid upon Ezekias king of Juda a tribute of three hundred talents of silver, and thirty talents of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings IV 16:8; Kings III 15:18–19; Kings IV 12:18"> And Ezekias gave all the silver that was found in the house of the Lord, and in the treasures of the king's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Chronicles II 29:3; Kings III 6:31–35"> At that time Ezekias cut off the gold from the doors of the temple, and from the pillars which Ezekias king of Juda had overlaid with gold, and gave<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. them.">i</a></sup>it to the king of the Assyrians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Esaias 20:1; Kings IV 20:20; Esaias 7:3"> And the king of the Assyrians sent Tharthan and Raphis and Rapsakes from Lachis to king Ezekias with a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gr. heavy.">j</a></sup>strong force against Jerusalem. And they went up and came to Jerusalem, and stood by the aqueduct of the upper pool, which is by the way of the fuller's field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings IV 19:2; Esaias 36:3; Kings II 8:16–17"> And they cried to Ezekias: and there came to him Heliakim the son of Chelcias the steward, and Somnas the scribe, and Joas the son of Saphat the recorder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esaias 36:4; Kings IV 19:10; Esaias 10:8–14"> And Rapsakes said to them, Say now to Ezekias, Thus says the king, the great king of the Assyrians, What is this confidence wherein thou trustest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 21:30–31"> Thou hast said, (but they are<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gr. words of lips.">k</a></sup>mere words,) I have counsel and strength for war. Now then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Gr. in whom trusting, etc.">l</a></sup>in whom dost thou trust, that thou hast revolted from me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esaias 30:7; Jezekiel 29:6–7; Esaias 30:2–3"> See now, art thou trusting for thyself on this broken staff of reed, even upon Egypt? whosoever shall stay himself upon it, it shall even go into his hand, and pierce it: so is Pharao king of Egypt to all that trust on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Chronicles II 31:1; Kings IV 18:4–5; Matthew 27:43"> And whereas thou hast said to me, We trust on the Lord God: is not this he,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Gr. his.">m</a></sup>whose high places and altars Ezekias has removed, and has said to Juda and Jerusalem, Ye shall worship before this altar in Jerusalem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings I 17:42; Kings III 20:10; Nehemiah 4:2–5"> And now, I pray you, make an agreement with my lord the king of the Assyrians, and I will give thee two thousand horses, if thou shalt be able on thy part to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Gr. give.">n</a></sup>set riders upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Esaias 10:8; Deuteronomy 17:16; Esaias 36:6"> How then wilt thou turn away the face of one<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Gr. local ruler.">o</a></sup>petty governor from among the least of my lord's servants? whereas thou trustest for thyself on Egypt for chariots and horsemen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings IV 19:6; Esaias 10:5–6; Kings IV 19:22–37"> And now have we come up without the Lord against this place to destroy it? The Lord said to me, Go up against this land, and destroy it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 4:7; Daniel 2:4; Esaias 36:11–12"> And Heliakim the son of Chelkias, and Somnas, and Joas, said to Rapsakes, Speak now to thy servants in the Syrian language, for we understand it; and speak not with us in the Jewish language: and why dost thou speak in the ears of the people that are on the wall?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 73:8; Lamentations 4:5; Jezekiel 4:15"> And Rapsakes said to them, Has my master sent me to thy master, and to thee, to speak these words? has he not sent me to the men who sit on the wall, that they may eat their own dung, and drink their own water together with you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Esaias 36:13–18; Esaias 10:8–13; Jezekiel 29:3"> And Rapsakes stood, and cried with a loud voice in the Jewish language, and spoke, and said, Hear the words of the great king of the Assyrians:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Chronicles II 32:15; 2 Thessalonians 2:8; Chronicles II 32:11"> thus says the king, Let not Ezekias encourage you with words: for he shall not be able to deliver you out of his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 125:1–2; Luke 23:35; Kings IV 19:32–34"> And let not Ezekias cause you to trust on the Lord, saying, The Lord will certainly deliver us; this city shall not be delivered<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Gr. in.">p</a></sup>into the hand of the king of the Assyrians: hearken not to Ezekias:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Kings III 4:25; Kings III 4:20; Zacharias 3:10"> for thus says the king of the Assyrians,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Lit. make a blessing with me.">q</a></sup>Gain my favour, and come forth to me, and every man shall drink of the wine of his own vine, and every man shall eat of his own fig-tree, and shall drink water out of his own cistern;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:7–9; Exodus 3:8; Deuteronomy 11:12"> until I come and remove you to a land like your own land, a land of corn and wine, and bread and vineyards, a land of olive oil, and honey, and ye shall live and not die: and do not ye hearken to Ezekias, for he deceives you, saying, The Lord shall deliver you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Esaias 10:10–11; Kings IV 19:12–13; Chronicles II 32:19"> Have the gods of the nations at all delivered each their own land out of the hand of the king of the Assyrians?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Esaias 10:9; Kings IV 17:23–33; Jeremias 49:23"> Where is the god of Hæmath, and of Arphad? where is the god of Seppharvaim, Ana, and Aba? for have they delivered Samaria out of my hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Daniel 3:15; Esaias 10:15; Exodus 5:2"> Who is there among all the gods of the countries, who have delivered their countries out of my hand, that the Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Gr. shall deliver.">r</a></sup>should deliver Jerusalem out of my hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Amos 5:13; Proverbs 9:7; Matthew 7:6"> But the men were silent, and answered him not a word: for there was a commandment of the king, saying, Ye shall not answer him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Kings IV 22:11; Genesis 37:29; Kings IV 5:7"> And Heliakim the son of Chelcias, the steward, and Somnas the scribe, and Joas the son of Saphat the recorder came in to Ezekias, having rent their garments; and they reported to him the words of Rapsakes.</span>
