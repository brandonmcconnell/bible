<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 1:1; Jeremias 25:1; Jeremias 25:9"> In his days went up Nabuchodonosor king of Babylon, and Joakim became his servant three years; and then he turned and revolted from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 35:11; Kings IV 23:27; Kings IV 21:12–14"> And the Lord sent against him the bands of the Chaldeans, and the bands of Syria, and the bands of Moab, and the bands of the children of Ammon, and sent them into the land of Juda to prevail against it, according to the word of the Lord, which he spoke by his servants the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings IV 18:25; Kings IV 21:2–11; Kings IV 23:26–27"> Moreover it was<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. on the mind.">a</a></sup>the purpose of the Lord concerning Juda, to remove<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. him.">b</a></sup>them from his presence, because of the sins of Manasses, according to all that he did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings IV 21:16; Psalms 106:38; Numbers 35:33"> Moreover he shed innocent blood, and filled Jerusalem with innocent blood, and the Lord would not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. be propitiated.">c</a></sup>pardon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:8; Jeremias 26:1–24; Jeremias 22:13–17"> And the rest of the acts of Joakim, and all that he did, behold, are not these written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Juda?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremias 22:18–19; Chronicles II 36:6; Jeremias 36:30"> And Joakim slept with his fathers: and Joachim his son reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremias 37:5–7; Jeremias 46:2; Genesis 15:18"> And the king of Egypt came no more out of his land: for the king of Babylon took away all that belonged to the king of Egypt from the river of Egypt as far as the river Euphrates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:9; Chronicles I 3:16; Jeremias 37:1"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. Joachim a son of 18 years in his reigning.">d</a></sup>Eighteen years old was Joachim when he began to reign, and he reigned three months in Jerusalem: and his mother's name was Nestha, daughter of Ellanastham, of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, according to all that his father did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings IV 25:2; Daniel 1:1–2"> At that time went up Nabuchodonosor king of Babylon to Jerusalem, and the city<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. came into seige.">e</a></sup>was besieged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Nabuchodonosor king of Babylon came against the city, and his servants besieged it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 24:1; Jeremias 52:28; Kings IV 25:27"> And Joachim king of Juda came forth to the king of Babylon, he and his servants, and his mother, and his princes, and his eunuchs; and the king of Babylon took him in the eighth year of his reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Esaias 39:6; Kings IV 20:17; Jeremias 20:5"> And he brought forth thence all the treasures of the house of the Lord, and the treasures of the king's house, and he cut up all the golden vessels which Solomon the king of Israel had made in the temple of the Lord, according to the word of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremias 52:28; Kings IV 25:12; Kings IV 24:16"> And he carried away the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and all the captains, and the mighty men, taking captive ten thousand<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Lit. captivities.">f</a></sup>prisoners, and every artificer and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Lit. shutter-up.">g</a></sup>smith: and only the poor of the land were left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:10; Jeremias 22:24–28; Esther 2:6"> And he carried Joachim away to Babylon, and the king's mother, and the king's wives, and his eunuchs: and he carried away the mighty men of the land into<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. emigration.">h</a></sup>captivity from Jerusalem to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremias 52:28; Kings IV 24:14; Jeremias 29:2"> And all the men of might, even seven thousand, and one thousand artificers and smiths: all were mighty men fit for war; and the king of Babylon carried them captive to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremias 37:1; Chronicles II 36:4; Kings IV 23:34"> And the king of Babylon made<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="A. V. Mattanaiah.">i</a></sup>Batthanias his son king in his stead, and called his name Sedekias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings IV 23:31; Jeremias 52:1–11; Jeremias 37:1"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gr. a son of 21 years, Sedekias.">j</a></sup>Twenty and one years old was Sedekias when he began to reign, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem: and his mother's name was Amital, daughter of Jeremias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings IV 23:37; Jeremias 24:8; Jezekiel 21:25"> And he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, according to all that Joachim did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:13; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11; Jeremias 38:17–21"> For it was<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Lit. it was in the Lord's mind to bring evil upon.">k</a></sup>according to the Lord's anger against Jerusalem and on Juda, until he cast them out of his presence, that Sedekias revolted against the king of Babylon.</span>
