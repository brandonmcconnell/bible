<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings II 8:2; Numbers 24:7; Kings IV 3:4–5"> And Moab<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. despised.">a</a></sup>rebelled against Israel after the death of Achaab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings IV 1:16; Matthew 10:25; Mark 3:22"> And Ochozias fell through the lattice that was in his upper chamber in Samaria, and was sick; and he sent messengers, and said to them, Go and enquire of Baal fly, the god of Accaron, whether I shall recover of this my sickness. And they went to enquire of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings III 17:1; Jonas 2:8; Acts 12:7–11"> And an angel of the Lord called Eliu the Thesbite, saying, Arise, and go to meet the messengers of Ochozias king of Samaria, and thou shalt say to them, Is it because there is no God in Israel, that ye go to enquire of Baal fly, the god of Accaron? but it shall not be so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 3:4; Genesis 2:17; Jezekiel 18:4"> For thus saith the Lord, The bed on which thou art gone up, thou shalt not come down from it, for thou shalt surely die. And Eliu went, and said so to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the messengers returned to him, and he said to them, Why have ye returned?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 16:4; Chronicles I 10:13–14; Esaias 41:22–23"> And they said to him, A man came up to meet us, and said to us, Go, return to the king that sent you, and say to him, Thus saith the Lord, Is it because there is no God in Israel, that thou goest to enquire of Baal fly, the god of Accaron? it shall not be so: the bed on which thou art gone up, thou shalt not come down from it, for thou shalt surely die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings I 28:14; Judges 8:18"> So they returned and reported to the king as Eliu said: and he said to them, What was the manner of the man who went up to meet you, and spoke to you these words?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 3:4; Zacharias 13:4; Mark 1:6"> And they said to him, He was a hairy man, and girt with a leathern girdle about his loins. And he said, This is Eliu the Thesbite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings IV 6:13–14; Luke 6:11–12; Hebrews 11:36"> And he sent to him a captain of fifty and his fifty; and he went up to him: and, behold, Eliu sat on the top of a mountain. And the captain of fifty spoke to him, and said, O man of God, the king has called thee, come down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 9:54; Daniel 3:25; Kings III 18:36–38"> And Eliu answered and said to the captain of fifty, And if I am a man of God, fire shall come down out of heaven, and devour thee and thy fifty. And fire came down out of heaven, and devoured him and his fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 2:16; Luke 22:63–64; Jeremias 5:3"> And the king<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. added and sent.">b</a></sup>sent a second time to him another captain of fifty, and his fifty. And the captain of fifty spoke to him, and said, O man of God, thus says the king, Come down quickly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings IV 1:9–10"> And Eliu answered and spoke to him, and said, If I am a man of God, fire shall come down out of heaven, and devour thee and thy fifty. And fire came down out of heaven, and devoured him and his fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Esaias 1:5; Kings I 26:21; Ecclesiastes 9:3"> And the king<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. added yet to send.">c</a></sup>sent yet again a captain and his fifty. And the third captain of fifty came, and knelt on his knees before Eliu, and entreated him, and spoke to him and said, O man of God, let my life, and the life of these fifty thy servants, be precious in thine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 20:24; Psalms 49:8; Proverbs 6:26"> Behold, fire came down from heaven, and devoured the two first captains of fifty: and now, I pray, let my life be precious in thine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esaias 51:12; Jezekiel 2:6; Jeremias 1:17"> And the angel of the Lord spoke to Eliu, and said, Go down with him, be not afraid of them. And Eliu rose up, and went down with him to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings IV 1:2–4; Kings III 22:28; Exodus 4:22–23"> And Eliu spoke to him, and said, Thus saith the Lord, Why hast thou sent messengers to enquire of Baal fly, the god of Accaron? it shall not be so: the bed on which thou art gone up, thou shalt not come down from it, for thou shalt surely die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings IV 3:1; Kings III 22:51; Kings IV 8:16–17"> So he died according to the word of the Lord which Eliu had spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings III 22:39; Kings III 14:19"> And the rest of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. words.">d</a></sup>acts of Ochozias which he did, behold, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings III 22:39; Kings III 14:19"> And Joram son of Achaab reigns over Israel in Samaria twelve years beginning in the eighteenth year of Josaphat king of Juda:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings III 22:39; Kings III 14:19"> and he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, only not as his brethren, nor as his mother:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings III 22:39; Kings III 14:19"> and he removed the pillars of Baal which his father made, and broke them in pieces: only he was joined to the sins of the house of Jeroboam, who led Israel to sin; he departed not from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings III 22:39; Kings III 14:19"> And the Lord was very angry with the house of Achaab.</span>
