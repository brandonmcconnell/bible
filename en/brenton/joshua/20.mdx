<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:2–13; Deuteronomy 4:41–43; Numbers 35:11–24"> Speak to the children of Israel, saying,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. give.">a</a></sup>Assign the cities<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. refugees.">b</a></sup>of refuge, of which I spoke to you by Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Even a refuge to the slayer who has smitten a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. life or soul.">c</a></sup>man unintentionally; and the cities shall be to you a refuge, and the slayer shall not be put to death by the avenger of blood, until he have stood before the congregation for judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 21:11; Chronicles I 6:76; Luke 1:39"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. he.">d</a></sup>Joshua separated Cades in Galilee in the mount<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or, of N.">e</a></sup>Nephthali, and Sychem in the mount Ephraim, and the city of Arboc; this is Chebron, in the mountain of Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Chronicles I 6:78; Joshua 21:36; Joshua 21:27"> And beyond Jordan he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. gave.">f</a></sup>appointed Bosor in the wilderness in the plain out of the tribe of Ruben, and Aremoth in Galaad out of the tribe of Gad, and Gaulon in the country of Basan out of the tribe of Manasse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 35:15; Joshua 20:4; Joshua 20:6"> These were the cities selected for the sons of Israel, and for the stranger abiding among them, that every one who smites a soul unintentionally should flee thither, that he should not die by the hand of the avenger of blood, until he should stand before the congregation for judgment.</span>
