<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 19:51; Kings I 1:3; Joshua 21:2"> And all the congregation of the children of Israel were assembled at Selo, and there they pitched the tabernacle of witness; and the land was subdued by them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And the sons of Israel remained, even those who had not received their inheritance, seven tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 18:9; Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 10:4"> And Joshua said to the sons of Israel, How long will ye be slack to inherit the land, which the Lord our God has given you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 18:6; Joshua 4:2; Joshua 18:3"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. give.">a</a></sup>Appoint of yourselves three men of each tribe, and let them rise up and go through the land, and let them describe it before me, as it will be proper to divide it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 16:1–10; Joshua 19:1–9; Joshua 15:1–12"> And they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. went through.">b</a></sup>came to him: and he divided to them seven portions, saying, Juda shall stand to them a border southward, and the sons of Joseph shall stand to them northward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 14:2; Joshua 18:10; Acts 13:19"> And do ye divide the land into seven parts, and bring the description hither to me, and I will<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. bring out.">c</a></sup>give you a lot before the Lord our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 13:33; Joshua 13:8–31; Numbers 18:20"> For the sons of Levi have no part among you; for the priesthood of the Lord is his portion; and Gad, and Ruben, and the half tribe of Manasse, have received their inheritance beyond Jordan eastward, which Moses the servant of the Lord gave to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 18:10; Romans 14:19; Kings I 14:41"> And the men rose up and went; and Joshua charged the men who went to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or, walk through.">d</a></sup>explore the land, saying, Go and explore the land, and come to me, and I will bring you forth a lot here before the Lord in Selo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 13:19"> And they went, and explored the land: and they viewed it, and described it according to the cities, seven parts in a book, and brought the book to Joshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 1:12; Acts 13:19; Joshua 18:6"> And Joshua cast the lot for them in Selo before the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 16:1–10; Joshua 15:1–8; Deuteronomy 10:1–22"> And the lot of the tribe of Benjamin came forth first according to their families: and the borders of their lot came forth between the children of Juda and the children of Joseph.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 16:1; Joshua 7:2; Joshua 2:1"> And their borders were northward: the borders shall go up from Jordan behind Jericho northward, and shall go up to the mountain westward, and the issue of it shall be Bæthon of Mabdara.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 28:19; Joshua 16:2–3; Joshua 10:11"> And the borders will go forth thence to Luz, behind Luz, from the south of it; this is Bæthel: and the borders shall go down to Maatarob Orech, to the hill country, which is southward of Bæthoron the lower.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 15:9; Chronicles I 13:5–6; Kings I 7:1–2"> And the borders shall pass through and proceed to the part that looks toward the sea, on the south, from the mountain in front of Bæthoron southward, and its termination shall be at Cariath-Baal, this is Cariath-Jarin, a city of the children of Juda; this is the part toward the west.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 15:9"> And the south side on the part of Cariath-Baal; and the borders shall go across to Gasin, to the fountain of the water of Naphtho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings IV 23:10; Joshua 15:7–8; Esaias 30:33"> And the borders shall extend down on one side, this is in front of the forest of Sonnam, which is on the side of Emec Raphain northward, and it shall come down to Gæenna behind Jebusai southward: it shall come down to the fountain of Rogel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 15:6"> And the borders shall go across to the fountain of Bæthsamys:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 15:6; Joshua 15:61"> and shall proceed to Galiloth, which is in front by the going up of Æthamin; and they shall come down to the stone of Bæon of the sons of Ruben; and shall pass over behind Bætharaba northward, and shall go down to the borders behind the sea northward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 14:3; Joshua 15:2; Numbers 34:3"> And the termination of the borders shall be at the creek of the salt sea northward to the side of Jordan southward: these are their southern borders.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And Jordan shall be their boundary on the east: this is the inheritance of the children of Benjamin, these are their borders round about according to their families.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 18:12; Joshua 6:1; Joshua 18:19"> And the cities of the children of Benjamin according to their families were Jericho, and Bethegæo, and Amecasis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 15:6; Kings III 12:29–32; Joshua 18:18"> and Bæthabara, and Sara, and Besana,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings I 13:17"> and Æein, and Phara, and Ephratha,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 2:26; Joshua 21:17; Esaias 10:29"> and Carapha, and Cephira, and Moni, and Gabaa, twelve cities and their villages:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 9:17; Kings III 3:4–5; Joshua 10:2"> Gabaon, and Rama, and Beerotha;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Judges 10:17; Ezra and Nehemiah 2:25; Joshua 9:17"> and Massema, and Miron, and Amoke;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> and Phira, and Caphan, and Nacan, and Selecan, and Thareela,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Kings II 21:14; Joshua 15:8; Judges 20:4–5"> and Jebus (this is Jerusalem); and Gabaoth, Jarim, thirteen cities, and their villages; this is the inheritance of the sons of Benjamin according to their families.</span>
</div>
