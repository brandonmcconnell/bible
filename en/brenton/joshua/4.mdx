<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> said to them, Advance before me in the presence of the Lord into the midst of Jordan, and each having taken up a stone from thence, let him carry it on his shoulders, according to the number of the twelve tribes of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 13:14; Joshua 4:21; Psalms 44:1"> that these may be to you continually for an appointed sign, that when thy son asks thee<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. to-morrow.">c</a></sup>in future, saying, What are these stones to us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 12:14; Numbers 16:40; Exodus 28:12"> then thou mayest explain to thy son, saying, The river Jordan was<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. failed.">d</a></sup>dried up from before the ark of the covenant of the Lord of the whole earth, when it passed it: and these stones shall be for a memorial for you for the children of Israel for ever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 4:2–5; Joshua 1:16–18; Joshua 4:20"> And the children of Israel did so, as the Lord commanded Joshua; and they took up twelve stones out of the midst of Jordan, (as the Lord commanded Joshua, when the children of Israel had completely passed over,) and carried these stones with them into the camp, and laid them down there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings I 7:12; Judges 1:26; Chronicles II 5:9"> And Joshua set also other twelve stones in Jordan itself, in the place that was under the feet of the priests that bore the ark of the covenant of the Lord; and there they are to this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 3:13; Psalms 119:60; Proverbs 27:1"> And the priests that bore the ark of the covenant stood in Jordan, until Joshua had finished all that the Lord commanded him to report to the people; and the people hasted and passed over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 3:8; Joshua 4:18; Joshua 3:17"> And it came to pass when all the people had passed over, that the ark of the covenant of the Lord passed over, and the stones before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 32:20–32; Joshua 1:14"> And the sons of Ruben, and the sons of Gad, and the half tribe of Manasse passed over<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or, equipped.">e</a></sup>armed before the children of Israel, as Moses commanded them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 6:11; Jeremias 39:5; Jeremias 52:8"> Forty thousand<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. in good order.">f</a></sup>armed for battle went over before the Lord to war, to the city of Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 3:7; Chronicles II 30:12; Kings III 3:28"> In that day the Lord magnified Joshua before all the people of Israel; and they feared him, as they did Moses, as long as he lived.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the Lord spoke to Joshua, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 11:19; Exodus 25:16–22; Joshua 3:3–6"> Charge the priests that bear the ark of the covenant of the testimony of the Lord, to go up out of Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 8:16–18; Acts 16:35–39; Acts 16:23"> And Joshua charged the priests, saying, Go up out of Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 3:15; Chronicles I 12:15; Joshua 3:13"> And it came to pass when the priests who bore the ark of the covenant of the Lord were gone up out of Jordan, and set their feet upon the land, that the water of Jordan returned impetuously to its place, and went as before over all its banks.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 5:9; Michæas 6:5; Exodus 12:2–3"> And the people went up out of Jordan on the tenth day of the first month; and the children of Israel encamped in Galgala in the region eastward from Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 4:3; Joshua 4:8"> And Joshua set these twelve stones which he took out of Jordan, in Galgala,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 4:6; Psalms 145:4–7; Psalms 105:2–5"> saying, When your sons ask you, saying, What are these stones?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 3:17; Esaias 51:10; Esaias 44:27"> Tell your sons, that Israel went over this Jordan on dry land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Esaias 43:16; Esaias 63:12–14"> when the Lord our God had dried up the water of Jordan from before them, until they had passed over; as the Lord our God did to the Red Sea, which the Lord our God dried up from before us, until we passed over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 106:8; Psalms 89:13; Kings III 8:42–43"> That all the nations of the earth might know, that the power of the Lord is mighty, and that ye might worship the Lord our God in every work.</span>
