<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the chiefs of the people dwelt in Jerusalem: and the rest of the people cast lots, to bring one of every ten to dwell in Jerusalem the holy city, and nine parts in the other cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And the people blessed all the men that volunteered to dwell in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Now these are the chiefs of the province who dwelt in Jerusalem, and in the cities of Juda; every man dwelt in his possession in their cities: Israel, the priests, and the Levites, and the Nathinim, and the children of the servants of Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And there dwelt in Jerusalem some of the children of Juda, and of the children of Benjamin. Of the children of Juda; Athaia son of Azia, the son of Zacharia, the son of Samaria, the son of Saphatia, the son of Maleleel, and some of the sons of Phares;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> and Maasia son of Baruch, son of Chalaza, son of Ozia, son of Adaia, son of Joarib, son of Zacharias, son of Seloni.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> All the sons of Phares who dwelt in Jerusalem were four hundred and sixty-eight men of might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And these were the children of Benjamin; Selo son of Mesulam, son of Joad, son of Phadaia, son of Coleia, son of Maasias, son of Ethiel, son of Jesia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And after him Gebe, Seli, nine hundred and twenty-eight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Joel son of Zechri was overseer over them: and Juda son of Asana was second in the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Of the priests: both Jadia son of Joarib, and Jachin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Saraia, son of Elchia, son of Mesulam, son of Sadduc, son of Marioth, son of Ætoth, was over the house of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And their brethren doing the work of the house were eight hundred and twenty-two: and Adaia son of Jeroam, son of Phalalia, son of Amasi, son of Zacharia, son of Phassur, son of Melchia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and his brethren, chiefs of families, two hundred and forty-two: and Amasia son of Esdriel, son of Mesarimith, son of Emmer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and his brethren, mighty men of war, a hundred and twenty-eight: and their overseer was Badiel son of one of the great men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And of the Levites; Samaia, son of Esricam,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Matthanias son of Micha, and Jobeb son of Samui,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> two hundred and eighty-four.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And the porters; Acub, Telamin, and their brethren, a hundred and seventy-two.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And the overseer of the Levites was the son of Bani, son of Ozi, the son of Asabia, the son of Micha. Of the sons of Asaph the singers some were over the house of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> for so was the king's commandment concerning them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And Phathaia son of Baseza was in attendance on the king in every matter for the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> and with regard to villages in their country district: and some of the children of Juda dwelt in Cariatharboc,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and in Jesu,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> and in Bersabee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> and their villages were Lachis and her lands: and they pitched their tents in Beersabee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And the children of Benjamin dwelt from Gabaa to Machmas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And of the Levites there were divisions to Juda and to Benjamin.</span>
