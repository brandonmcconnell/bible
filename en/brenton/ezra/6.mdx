<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 5:17; Proverbs 25:2; Ezra and Nehemiah 4:15"> Then Darius the king made a decree, and caused a search to be made in the record-offices, where the treasure is stored in Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings IV 17:6"> And there was found in the city, in the palace, a volume, and this was the record written in it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:22–4; Revelation 21:16; Jezekiel 41:13–15"> In the first year of king Cyrus, Cyrus the king made a decree concerning the holy house of God that was in Jerusalem, saying, Let the house be built, and the place where they sacrifice the sacrifices. (Also he appointed its elevation, in height sixty cubits; its breadth was of sixty cubits.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings III 6:36; Psalms 68:29; Esaias 49:23"> And let there be three strong layers of stone, and one layer of timber; and the expense shall be paid out of the house of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 5:2; Ezra and Nehemiah 5:14; Chronicles II 36:6–7"> And the silver and the gold vessels of the house of God, which Nabuchodonosor carried off from the house that was in Jerusalem, and carried to Babylon, let them even be given, and be carried to the temple that is in Jerusalem, and put in the place where they were set in the house of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 5:3; Romans 8:31; Proverbs 21:30"> Now, ye rulers beyond the river, Satharbuzanai, and their fellow-servants the Apharsachæans, who are on the other side of the river, give these things, keeping far from that place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 5:38–39"> Now let alone the work of the house of God: let the rulers of the Jews and the elders of the Jews build that house of God on its place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 6:4; Aggæus 2:8; Ezra and Nehemiah 7:15–22"> Also a decree has been made by me, if haply ye may do somewhat in concert with the elders of the Jews for the building of that house of God: to wit, out of the king's property, even the tributes beyond the river, let there be money to defray the expenses carefully granted to those men, so that they be not hindered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 1:10; Psalms 50:9–13; Mark 9:49"> And whatever need there may be, ye shall give both the young of bulls and rams, and lambs for whole-burnt-offerings to the God of heaven, wheat, salt, wine, oil:—let it be given them according to the word of the priests that are in Jerusalem, day by day whatsoever they shall ask;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 29:7; Ezra and Nehemiah 7:23; 1 Timothy 2:1–2"> that they may offer sweet savours to the God of heaven, and that they may pray for the life of the king and his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 3:29; Ezra and Nehemiah 7:26; Daniel 2:5"> And a decree has been made by me, that every man who shall alter this word, timber shall be pulled down from his house, and let him be lifted up and slain upon it, and his house shall be confiscated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings III 9:3; Deuteronomy 12:5; Deuteronomy 12:11"> And may the God whose name dwells there, overthrow every king and people who shall stretch out his hand to alter or destroy the house of God which is in Jerusalem. I Darius have made a decree; let it be diligently attended to.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 4:9; Ezra and Nehemiah 6:6; Job 5:12–13"> Then Thanthanai the governor on this side beyond the river, Satharbuzanai, and his fellow-servants, according to that which king Darius sent, so they did diligently.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 7:1; Ezra and Nehemiah 5:13; Ezra and Nehemiah 4:24–2"> And the elders of the Jews and the Levites built, at the prophecy of Aggæus the prophet, and Zacharias the son of Addo: and they built up, and finished it, by the decree of the God of Israel, and by the decree of Cyrus, and Darius, and Arthasastha, kings of the Persians.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esther 3:7; Esther 9:21; Esther 9:1"> And they finished this house by the third day of the month Adar, which is the sixth year of the reign of Darius the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Chronicles II 7:5; Kings III 8:63; John 10:22"> And the children of Israel, the priests, and the Levites, and the rest of the children of the captivity, kept the dedication of the house of God with gladness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 8:35; Revelation 7:4–8; Chronicles II 29:21–23"> And they offered for the dedication of the house of God a hundred calves, two hundred rams, four hundred lambs, twelve kids of the goats for a sin-offering for all Israel, according to the number of the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 3:6; Chronicles I 24:1; Chronicles II 35:4–5"> And they set the priests in their divisions, and the Levites in their separate orders, for the services of God in Jerusalem, according to the writing of the book of Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 12:6–36; Chronicles II 30:1–27; Joshua 5:10"> And the children of the captivity kept the passover on the fourteenth day of the first month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Chronicles II 35:11; Hebrews 7:27; Chronicles II 29:34"> For the priests and Levites were purified, all were clean to a man, and they slew the passover for all the children of the captivity, and for their brethren the priests, and for themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 9:11; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Esaias 52:11"> And the children of Israel ate the passover, even they that were of the captivity, and every one who separated himself to them from the uncleanness of the nations of the land, to seek the Lord God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 1:1; Chronicles II 30:21; Proverbs 21:1"> And they kept the feast of unleavened bread seven days with gladness, because the Lord made them glad, and he turned the heart of the king of Assyria to them, to strengthen their hands in the works of the house of the God of Israel.</span>
</div>
</div>
