<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Now these are the priests and the Levites that went up with Zorobabel the son of Salathiel and Jesus: Saraia, Jeremia, Esdra,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Amaria, Maluch,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Sechenia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> These were the chiefs of the priests, and their brethren in the days of Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the Levites were, Jesus, Banui, Cadmiel, Sarabia, Jodae, Matthania: he was over the bands, and his brethren</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> were appointed to the daily courses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Jesus begot Joakim, and Joakim begot Eliasib, and Eliasib begot Jodae,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and Jodae begot Jonathan, and Jonathan begot Jadu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And in the days of Joakim, his brethren the priests and the heads of families were, belonging to Saraia, Amaria; to Jeremia, Anania;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> to Esdra, Mesulam; to Amaria, Joanan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> to Amaluch, Jonathan; to Sechenia, Joseph;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> to Are, Mannas; to Marioth, Elcaï;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> to Adadai, Zacharia; to Ganathoth, Mesolam;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> to Abia, Zechri; to Miamin, Maadai; to Pheleti, one;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> to Balgas, Samue; to Semia, Jonathan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> to Joarib, Matthanai; to Edio, Ozi;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> to Salai, Callai; to Amec, Abed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> to Elkia, Asabias; to Jedeiu, Nathanael.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The Levites in the days of Eliasib, Joada, and Joa, and Joanan, and Idua, were recorded heads of families: also the priests, in the reign of Darius the Persian.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And the sons of Levi, heads of families, were written in the book of the chronicles, even to the days of Joanan son of Elisue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And the heads of the Levites were Asabia, and Sarabia, and Jesu: and the sons of Cadmiel, and their brethren over against them, were to sing hymns of praise, according to the commandment of David the man of God, course by course.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> When I gathered the porters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> it was in the days of Joakim son of Jesus, son of Josedec, and in the days of Neemia: and Esdras the priest was scribe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And at the dedication of the wall of Jerusalem they sought the Levites in their places, to bring them to Jerusalem, to keep a feast of dedication and gladness with thanksgiving, and they sounded cymbals with songs, and had psalteries and harps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And the sons of the singers were assembled both from the neighbourhood round about to Jerusalem, and from the villages,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> and from the country: for the singers built themselves villages by Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And the priests and the Levites purified themselves, and they purified the people, and the porters, and the wall.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And they brought up the princes of Juda on the wall, and they appointed two great companies for thanksgiving, and they passed on the right hand on the wall of the dung-gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And after them went Osaia, and half the princes of Juda,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> and Azarias, and Esdras, and Mesollam,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> and Juda, and Benjamin, and Samaias and Jeremia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And some of the sons of the priests with trumpets, Zacharias son of Jonathan, son of Samaias, son of Matthania, son of Michaia, son of Zacchur, son of Asaph:</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> and his brethren, Samaia, and Oziel, Gelol, Jama, Aia, Nathanael, and Juda, Anani, to praise with the hymns of David the man of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> and Esdras the scribe was before them, at the gate, to praise before them, and they went up by the steps of the city of David, in the ascent of the wall, above the house of David, even to the water-gate</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> of Ephraim, and to the fish-gate, and by the tower of Anameel, and as far as the sheep-gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And the singers were heard, and were numbered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> And in that day they offered great sacrifices, and rejoiced; for God had made them very joyful: and their wives and their children rejoiced: and the joy in Jerusalem was heard from afar off.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And in that day they appointed men over the treasuries, for the treasures, the first-fruits, and the tithes, and for the chiefs of the cities who were assembled among them, to furnish portions for the priests and Levites: for there was joy in Juda over the priests and over the Levites that waited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> And they kept the charges of their God, and the charges of the purification, and ordered the singers and the porters, according to the commandments of David and his son Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> For in the days of David Asaph was originally first of the singers, and they sang hymns and praise to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> And all Israel in the days of Zorobabel, and in the days of Neemias, gave the portions of the singers and the porters, a daily rate: and consecrated them to the Levites: and the Levites consecrated them to the sons of Aaron.</span>
</div>
