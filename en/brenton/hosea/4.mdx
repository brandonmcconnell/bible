<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremias 4:22; Michæas 6:2; Esaias 3:13–14"> Hear the word of the Lord, ye children of Israel: for the Lord has a controversy with the inhabitants of the land, because there is no truth, nor mercy, nor knowledge of God in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Osee 6:9; Jeremias 6:7; Osee 7:1"> Cursing, and lying, and murder, and theft, and adultery abound in the land, and they mingle blood with blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Sophonias 1:3; Esaias 24:4–12; Naum 1:4"> Therefore shall the land mourn, and shall be diminished with all that dwell in it, with the wild beasts of the field, and the reptiles of the earth, and with the birds of the sky, and the fish of the sea shall fail:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:12; Osee 4:17; Amos 5:13"> that neither any one may plead, nor any one reprove another: but my people are as a priest spoken against.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 15:8; Osee 2:2; Galatians 4:26"> Therefore they shall fall by day, and the prophet with thee shall fall: I have compared thy mother unto night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esaias 5:13; Proverbs 19:2; Osee 6:6"> My people are like as if they had no knowledge: because thou hast rejected knowledge, I will also reject thee, that thou shalt not minister as priest to me: and as thou hast forgotten the law of thy God, I also will forget thy children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Osee 13:6; Philippians 3:19; Kings I 2:30"> According to their multitude, so they sinned against me: I will turn their glory into shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Michæas 3:11; Esaias 56:11; Titus 1:11"> They will devour the sins of my people, and will set their hearts on their iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esaias 24:2; Jeremias 5:31; Matthew 15:14"> And the priest shall be as the people: and I will avenge on them their ways, and I will recompense to them their counsels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Michæas 6:14; Leviticus 26:26; Aggæus 1:6"> And they shall eat, and shall not be satisfied: they have gone a-whoring, and shall by no means prosper: because they have left off to take heed to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Luke 21:34; Esaias 28:7"> The heart of my people has gladly engaged in fornication and wine and strong drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 2:27; Osee 5:4; Ambacum 2:19"> They asked counsel by means of signs, and they reported answer to them by their staves: they have gone astray in a spirit of whoredom, and gone grievously a-whoring from their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Esaias 1:29; Jeremias 3:6; Jezekiel 6:13"> They have sacrificed on the tops of the mountains, and on the hills they have sacrificed under the oak and poplar, and under the shady tree, because the shade was good: therefore your daughters shall go a-whoring, and your daughters-in-law shall commit adultery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Osee 4:1; John 8:43; Ephesians 4:18"> And I will not visit upon your daughters when they shall commit fornication, nor your daughters-in-law when they shall commit adultery: for they themselves mingled themselves with harlots, and sacrificed with polluted ones, and the people that understood not entangled itself with a harlot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Osee 12:11; Osee 9:15; Amos 4:4"> But thou, Israel, be not ignorant, and go ye not, men of Juda, to Galgala; and go not up to the house of On, and swear not by the living Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremias 7:24; Jeremias 8:5; Jeremias 3:11"> For Israel was maddened like a mad heifer: now the Lord will feed them as a lamb in a wide place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 81:12; Revelation 22:11; Osee 13:2"> Ephraim, joined with idols, has laid stumbling-blocks in his own way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Michæas 3:11; Amos 5:12; Kings IV 17:7–17"> He has chosen the Chananites: they have grievously gone a-whoring: they have loved dishonour through her insolence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esaias 1:29; Jeremias 4:11–12; Osee 13:15"> Thou art a blast of wind in her wings, and they shall be ashamed because of their altars.</span>
