<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Michæas 7:18–19; Hebrews 13:15; Job 34:31–32"> Return, O Israel, to the Lord thy God; for the people have fallen through thine iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 68:5; Psalms 10:14; Esaias 31:1"> Take with you words, and turn to the Lord your God: speak to him, that ye may not receive the reward of unrighteousness, but that ye may receive good things: and we will render in return the fruit of our lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esaias 57:18; Ephesians 2:4–9; Jeremias 3:22"> Assur shall never save us; we will not mount on horseback; we will no longer say to the works of our hands, Our gods. He who is in thee shall pity the orphan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esaias 35:2; Esaias 27:6; Ephesians 3:17"> I will restore their dwellings, I will love them truly: for he has turned away my wrath from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 52:8; John 15:1; 2 Corinthians 2:14–15"> I will be as dew to Israel: he shall bloom as the lily, and cast forth his roots as Libanus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 91:1; Psalms 85:6; 1 Corinthians 15:36–38"> His branches shall spread, and he shall be as a fruitful olive, and his smell shall be as the smell of Libanus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 34:32; Esaias 55:13; James 1:17"> They shall return, and dwell under his shadow: they shall live and be satisfied with corn, and he shall flower as a vine: his memorial shall be to Ephraim as the wine of Libanus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 10:29; Psalms 107:43; John 8:47"> What has he to do any more with idols? I have afflicted him, and I will strengthen him: I am as a leafy juniper tree. From me is thy fruit found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Who is wise, and will understand these things? or prudent, and will know them? for the ways of the Lord are straight, and the righteous shall walk in them: but the ungodly shall fall therein.</span>
