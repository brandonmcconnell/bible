<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 11:12–33"> And Jephthae said to them,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. <span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings I 19:5; Kings I 28:21; Job 13:14"> And I saw that thou<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. art.">c</a></sup>wert no<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. saviour or deliverer.">d</a></sup>helper, and I put my life in my hand, and passed on to the sons of Ammon; and the Lord delivered them into my hand: and wherefore are ye come up against me this day to fight with me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings I 25:10; Deuteronomy 3:12–17; Proverbs 15:1"> And Jephthae gathered all the men of Galaad, and fought with Ephraim; and the men of Galaad smote Ephraim, because they that were escaped of Ephraim said, Ye are of Galaad in the midst of Ephraim and in the midst of Manasse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 3:28; Joshua 22:11; Joshua 2:7"> And Galaad took the fords of Jordan before Ephraim; and they that escaped of Ephraim said to them, Let us go over: and the men of Galaad said, Art thou an Ephrathite? and he said, No.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 69:2; Psalms 69:15; Mark 14:70"> Then they said to him, Say now<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Heb. שבלה, “Shibboleth,” ear of corn. <span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And Jephthae judged Israel six years; and Jephthae the Galaadite died, and was buried in his city Galaad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Michæas 5:2; Kings I 16:1; Matthew 2:1"> And after him Abaissan of Bethleem judged Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 10:4; Judges 12:14"> And he had thirty sons, and thirty daughters, whom he sent forth; and he brought in thirty daughters for his sons from without; and he judged Israel seven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Abaissan died, and was buried in Bethleem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And after him Ælom of Zabulon judged Israel ten years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Chronicles I 8:13; Chronicles I 6:69; Joshua 19:42"> And Ælom of Zabulon died, and was buried in Ælom in the land of Zabulon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And after him Abdon the son of Ellel, the Pharathonite, judged Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 5:10; Judges 10:4"> And he had forty sons, and thirty grandsons, that rode upon seventy colts: and he judged Israel eight years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 5:14; Judges 3:13; Judges 3:27"> And Abdon the son of Ellel, the Pharathonite, died, and was buried in Pharathon in the land of Ephraim in the mount of Amalec.</span>
