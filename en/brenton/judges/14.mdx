<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 15:10; Joshua 19:43; 1 John 2:16"> And Sampson went down to Thamnatha, and saw a woman in Thamnatha of the daughters of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Observe, ἀλλόφυλοι here and elsewhere is rendered Philistines.">a</a></sup>Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 34:4; Genesis 21:21; Genesis 24:2–3"> And he went up and told his father and his mother, and said, I have seen a woman in Thamnatha of the daughters of the Phylistines; and now take her to me for a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 15:18; Kings I 17:36; Kings I 14:6"> And his father and his mother said to him, Are there no daughters of thy brethren, and is there not a woman of all my people, that thou goest to take a wife of the uncircumcised Philistines? And Sampson said to his father, Take her for me, for she is right in my eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 11:20; Psalms 115:3; Judges 13:1"> And his father and his mother knew not that it<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. is.">b</a></sup>was of the Lord, that he sought to be revenged on the Philistines: and at that time the Philistines lorded it over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And Sampson and his father and his mother went down to Thamnatha, and he came to the vineyard of Thamnatha; and behold, a young lion roared<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, against him.">c</a></sup>in meeting him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 3:10; Judges 13:25; Kings I 11:6"> And the Spirit of the Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. leapt. Heb. חעלח.">d</a></sup>came powerfully upon him, and he crushed him as he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. will crush.">e</a></sup>would have crushed a kid of the goats, and there was nothing in his hands: and he told not his father and his mother what he had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And they went down and spoke to the woman,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or, the thing was right.">f</a></sup>and she was pleasing in the eyes of Sampson.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 1:20; Genesis 29:21"> And after<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. days.">g</a></sup>some time he returned to take her, and he turned aside to see the carcase of the lion; and behold, a swarm of bees, and honey were in the mouth of the lion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings I 14:25–30; Proverbs 25:15"> And he took it into his hands, and went on eating, and he went to his father and his mother, and gave to them, and they did eat; but he told them not that he took the honey out of the mouth of the lion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 29:22; Revelation 19:9; Esther 1:7–22"> And his father went down to the woman, and Sampson made there a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="i. e. in the original sense of the word, a drinking party.">h</a></sup>banquet for seven days, for so the young men are used to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 9:15; Kings I 16:6; Kings I 10:23"> And it came to pass when they saw him, that they took thirty guests, and they were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings III 10:1; Jezekiel 17:2; Genesis 45:22"> And Sampson said to them, I propound you a riddle: if ye will indeed tell it me, and discover it within the seven days of the feast, I will give you thirty sheets and thirty changes of raiment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And if ye cannot tell it me, ye shall give me thirty napkins and thirty<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. changeable or changing robes.">i</a></sup>changes of apparel: and they said to him, Propound thy riddle, and we will hear it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 1:2–4; Kings III 17:6; Romans 8:37"> And he said to them, Meat came forth of the eater, and sweetness out of the strong: and they could not tell the riddle for three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 16:5; Judges 15:6; Proverbs 6:26"> And it came to pass on the fourth day, that they said to the wife of Sampson, Deceive now thy husband, and let him tell thee the riddle, lest we burn thee and thy father's house with fire: did ye invite us to do us violence?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 16:15; Genesis 2:24"> And Sampson's wife wept before him, and said, Thou dost but hate me, and lovest me not; for the riddle which thou hast propounded to the children of my people, thou hast not told me: and Sampson said to her, If I have not told it to my father and my mother, shall I tell it to thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 16:16; Job 2:9; Judges 16:13"> And she wept before him the seven days, during which their banquet lasted: and it came to pass on the seventh day, that he told her, because she troubled him; and she told it to the children of her people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And the men of the city said to him on the seventh day, before sunrise, What is sweeter than honey? and what is stronger than a lion? and Sampson said to them, If ye had not ploughed with my heifer, ye would not have known my riddle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings I 11:6; Judges 3:10; Judges 13:25"> And the Spirit of the Lord came upon him powerfully, and he went down to Ascalon, and destroyed of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gr. them.">j</a></sup>the inhabitants thirty men, and took their garments, and gave the changes of raiment to them that told the riddle; and Sampson was very angry, and went up to the house of his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 15:2; John 3:29; Psalms 55:12–13"> And the wife of Sampson was given to one of his friends, with whom he was on terms of friendship.</span>
