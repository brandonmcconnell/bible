<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 2:16; Judges 3:9; Joshua 15:48"> And after Abimelech Thola the son of Phua rose up to save Israel, being the son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i. e. Abimelech's.">a</a></sup>his father's brother, a man of Issachar; and he dwelt in Samir in mount Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And he judged Israel twenty-three years, and died, and was buried in Samir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 32:29; Genesis 31:48"> And after him arose Jair of Galaad, and he judged Israel twenty-two years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 32:41; Judges 5:10; Deuteronomy 3:14"> And he had thirty-two sons riding on thirty-two colts, and they had thirty-two cities; and they called them Jair's towns until this day in the land of Galaad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And Jair died, and was buried in Rhamnon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings III 11:33; Kings III 11:7; Kings IV 23:13"> And the children of Israel did evil again in the sight of the Lord, and served Baalim, and Astaroth, and the gods of Aram, and the gods of Sidon, and the gods of Moab, and the gods of the children of Ammon, and the gods of the Phylistines; and they forsook the Lord, and did not serve him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Deuteronomy 32:16–22; Kings I 12:9–10"> And the Lord was very angry with Israel, and sold them into the hand of the Phylistines, and into the hand of the children of Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Esaias 30:13; Judges 10:5"> And they afflicted and bruised the children of Israel at that time eighteen years, all the children of Israel beyond Jordan in the land of the Amorite in Galaad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Chronicles II 20:1–2; Deuteronomy 28:65; Chronicles II 15:5"> And the children of Ammon went over Jordan to fight with Juda, and Benjamin, and with Ephraim; and the children of Israel were greatly afflicted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings I 12:10; Psalms 107:28; Psalms 107:13"> And the children of Israel cried to the Lord, saying, We have sinned against thee, because we have forsaken God, and served Baalim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 3:31; Numbers 21:21–25; Exodus 14:30"> And the Lord said to the children of Israel, Did I not save you from Egypt and from the Amorite, and from the children of Ammon, and from the Phylistines,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 6:3; Chronicles II 26:6–7; Psalms 106:42–43"> and from the Sidonians, and Amalec, and Madiam, who afflicted you? and ye cried to me, and I saved you out of their hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremias 2:13; Chronicles I 28:9; Judges 2:12"> Yet ye forsook me and served other gods; therefore I will not save you any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremias 2:28; Deuteronomy 32:37–38; Kings III 18:27–28"> Go, and cry to the gods whom ye have chosen to yourselves, and let them save you in the time of your affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings I 3:18; Kings II 15:26; Proverbs 28:13"> And the children of Israel said to the Lord, We have sinned: do thou to us according to all that is good in thine eyes; only deliver us this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esaias 63:9; Psalms 106:44–45; Jezekiel 18:30–32"> And they put away the strange gods from the midst of them, and served the Lord only, and his soul was pained for the trouble of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 11:29; Genesis 31:49; Judges 11:11"> And the children of Ammon went up, and encamped in Galaad; and the children of Israel were gathered together and encamped<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, near the watch-tower. Heb. מעפה, name of a town.">b</a></sup>on the hill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 11:11; Judges 12:7; Judges 1:1"> And the people the princes of Galaad said every man to his neighbour, Who is he that shall begin to fight against the children of Ammon? he shall even be head over all that dwell in Galaad.</span>
