<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 20:1; Mark 6:23; Acts 23:12"> Now the children of Israel swore in Massephath, saying, No man of us shall give his daughter to Benjamin for a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 20:26; Judges 20:18; Kings I 30:4"> And the people came to Bæthel, and sat there until evening before God: and they lifted up their voice and wept with a great weeping;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 74:1; Joshua 7:7–9; Esaias 63:17"> and said, Wherefore, O Lord God of Israel, has this come to pass, that to-day one tribe should be counted as missing from Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings II 24:25; Kings III 8:64; Psalms 78:34–35"> And it came to pass on the morrow that the people rose up early, and built there an altar, and offered up whole-burnt-offerings and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See chap 20. 26.">a</a></sup>peace-offerings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 5:23; Judges 21:18; Judges 21:1"> And the children of Israel said, Who of all the tribes of Israel, went not up in the congregation to the Lord? for there was a great oath concerning those who went not up to the Lord to Massephath, saying, He shall surely be put to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 21:15; Judges 20:23; Luke 19:41–42"> And the children of Israel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, comforted themselves, or were comforted.">b</a></sup>relented toward Benjamin their brother, and said, To-day one tribe is cut off from Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 21:1; Judges 21:18; Kings I 14:28–29"> What shall we do for wives for the rest that remain? whereas we have sworn by the Lord, not to give them of our daughters for wives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings I 31:11–13; Kings I 11:1–3; Kings II 2:5–6"> And they said, What one man is there of the tribes of Israel, who went not up to the Lord to Massephath? and, behold, no man came to the camp from Jabis Galaad to the assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And the people were numbered, and there was not there a man from the inhabitants of Jabis Galaad.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 5:23; Judges 21:5; Kings I 11:7"> And the congregation sent thither twelve thousand men of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. sons of strength.">c</a></sup>strongest, and they charged them, saying, Go ye and smite the inhabitants of Jabis Galaad with the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. mouth.">d</a></sup>edge of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 31:17–18; Deuteronomy 2:34"> And this shall ye do: every male and every woman that has known the lying with man ye shall devote to destruction, but the virgins ye shall save alive: and they did so.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; Jeremias 7:12; Psalms 78:60"> And they found<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. from, out of.">e</a></sup>among the inhabitants of Jabis Galaad four hundred young virgins, who had not known man by lying with him; and they brought them to Selom in the land of Chanaan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 20:47; Deuteronomy 20:10; Ephesians 2:17"> And all the congregation sent and spoke to the children of Benjamin in the rock Remmon, and invited them to make peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 21:12; 1 Corinthians 7:2; Judges 20:47"> And Benjamin returned to the children of Israel at that time, and the children of Israel gave them the women whom they had saved alive of the daughters of Jabis Galaad; and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. it pleased them thus.">f</a></sup>they were content.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 21:6; Chronicles I 15:13; Esaias 58:12"> And the people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. See ver. 6.">g</a></sup>relented for Benjamin, because the Lord had made a breach in the tribes of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the elders of the congregation said, What shall we do for wives for them that remain? for the women have been destroyed out of Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 26:55; Numbers 36:7"> And they said, There must be an inheritance of them that are escaped of Benjamin; and so a tribe shall not be destroyed out of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 21:1; Judges 11:35"> For we shall not be able to give them wives of our daughters, because we swore among the children of Israel, saying, Cursed is he that gives a wife to Benjamin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:10; Deuteronomy 16:1; Deuteronomy 16:13"> And they said, Lo! now there is a feast of the Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. from days to days. Hebraism.">h</a></sup>from year to year in Selom, which is on the north of Bæthel, eastward on the way that goes up from Bæthel to Sychem, and from the south of Lebona.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And they charged the children of Benjamin, saying, Go and lie in wait in the vineyards;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 11:34; Exodus 15:20; Ecclesiastes 3:4"> and ye shall see; and lo! if there come out the daughters of the inhabitants of Selom to dance in dances, then shall ye go out of the vineyards and seize for yourselves every man a wife of the daughters of Selom, and go ye into the land of Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 21:1; Judges 21:18; Judges 21:14"> And it shall come to pass, when their fathers or their brethren come to dispute with us, that we will say to them, Grant them freely to us, for we have not taken every man his wife in the battle: because ye did not give to them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Translated from Alex.">i</a></sup>according to the occasion, ye transgressed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 20:48"> And the children of Benjamin did so; and they took wives according to their number from the dancers whom they seized: and they went and returned to their inheritance, and built the cities, and dwelt in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And the children of Israel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gr. walked.">j</a></sup>went thence at that time every man to his tribe and his kindred; and they went thence every man to his inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:8; Judges 17:6; Proverbs 3:5"> And in those days there was no king in Israel; every man did that which was right in his own sight.</span>
</div>
