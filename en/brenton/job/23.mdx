<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Job answered and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 10:1; Psalms 32:4; Job 7:11"> Yea, I know that pleading is out of my reach; and his hand has been made heavy upon my groaning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 16:21; Jeremias 14:7; Job 13:3"> Who would then know that I might find him, and come to an end of the matter?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 13:18; Daniel 9:18–19; Exodus 32:12–13"> And I would plead my own cause, and he would fill my mouth with arguments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 13:22–23; Job 10:2; 1 Corinthians 4:3–4"> And I would know the remedies which he would speak to me, and I would perceive what he would tell me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 13:21; Job 9:33–34; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> Though he should come on me in his great strength, then he would not threaten me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 13:3; Romans 8:1; Esaias 1:18"> for truth and reproof are from him; and he would bring forth my judgment to an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 10:1; Job 9:11; Job 35:14"> For if I shall go first, and exist no longer, still what do I know concerning the latter end?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:46; Esaias 8:17"> When he wrought on the left hand, then I observed it not: his right hand shall encompass me but I shall not see it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 1:12; James 1:2–4; 1 Peter 1:7"> For he knows already my way; and he has tried me as gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 44:18; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Job 17:9"> And I will go forth according to his commandments, for I have kept his ways; and I shall not turn aside from his commandments,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 15:16; Psalms 119:103; Psalms 119:11"> neither shall I transgress; but I have hid his words in my bosom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 115:3; Proverbs 19:21; Esaias 46:10"> And if too he has thus judged, who is he that has contradicted, for he has both willed a thing and done it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; Psalms 77:3; Job 31:23"> Therefore am I troubled at him; and when I was reproved, I thought of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; Psalms 77:3; Job 31:23"> Therefore let me take good heed before him: I will consider, and be afraid of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 22:14; Job 27:2; Psalms 88:16"> But the Lord has softened my heart, and the Almighty has troubled me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 19:8; Esaias 57:1; Kings IV 22:20"> For I knew not that darkness would come upon me, and thick darkness has covered me before my face.</span>
