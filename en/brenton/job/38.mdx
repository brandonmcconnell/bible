<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 40:6; Job 37:1–2; Kings IV 2:11"> And after Elius had ceased from speaking, the Lord spoke to Job through the whirlwind and clouds, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 42:3; 1 Timothy 1:7; Job 35:16"> Who is this that hides counsel from me, and confines words in his heart, and thinks to conceal them from me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 40:7; Exodus 12:11; Jeremias 1:17"> Gird thy loins like a man; and I will ask thee, and do thou answer me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 104:5; Hebrews 1:10; Proverbs 30:4"> Where wast thou when I founded the earth? tell me now, if thou hast knowledge,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esaias 40:12; Zacharias 2:1–2; Job 11:9"> who set the measures of it, if thou knowest? or who stretched a line upon it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 26:7; Psalms 104:5; Psalms 93:1"> On what are its rings fastened? and who is he that laid the corner-stone upon it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 5:11; Job 1:6; Ezra and Nehemiah 3:11–12"> When the stars were made, all my angels praised me with a loud voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 1:9; Proverbs 8:29; Jeremias 5:22"> And I shut up the sea with gates, when it rushed out, coming forth out of its mother's womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 1:2"> And I made a cloud its clothing, and swathed it in mist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 5:22; Psalms 104:9; Psalms 33:7"> And I set bounds to it, surrounding it with bars and gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 89:9; Proverbs 8:29; Psalms 65:6–7"> And I said to it, Hitherto shalt thou come, but thou shalt not go beyond, but thy waves shall be confined within thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 74:16; Psalms 148:3–5; Genesis 1:5"> Or did I order the morning light in thy time; and did the morning star then first see his appointed place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 104:35; Job 37:3; Exodus 14:27"> to lay hold of the extremities of the earth, to cast out the ungodly out of it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 104:6; Psalms 104:2"> Or didst thou take clay of the ground, and form a living creature, and set it with the power of speech upon the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 10:15; Psalms 37:17; Job 18:5"> And hast thou removed light from the ungodly, and crushed the arm of the proud?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 8:24; Job 26:5–6; Psalms 77:19"> Or hast thou gone to the source of the sea, and walked in the tracks of the deep?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 9:13; Psalms 107:18; Job 3:5"> And do the gates of death open to thee for fear; and did the porters of hell quake when they saw thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 89:11–12; Revelation 20:9; Jeremias 31:37"> And hast thou been instructed in the breadth of the whole earth under heaven? tell me now, what is the extent of it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jezekiel 32:8; Genesis 1:14–18; Genesis 1:3–4"> And in what kind of a land does the light dwell? and of what kind is the place of darkness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 10:19; Job 26:10; Genesis 23:17"> If thou couldest bring me to their utmost boundaries, and if also thou knowest their paths;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 15:7; Job 38:12; Job 38:4"> I know then that thou wert born at that time, and the number of thy years is great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 37:6; Psalms 135:7; Psalms 33:7"> But hast thou gone to the treasures of snow? and hast thou seen the treasures of hail?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 10:11; Esaias 30:30; Exodus 9:18"> And is there a store of them, for thee against the time of thine enemies, for the day of wars and battle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 38:12–13; Jonas 4:8; Matthew 24:27"> And whence proceeds the frost? or whence is the south wind dispersed over the whole world under heaven?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 28:26; Psalms 29:3–10; Job 37:3–6"> And who prepared a course for the violent rain, and a way for the thunders;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremias 14:22; Hebrews 6:7–8; Psalms 107:35"> to rain upon the land where there is no man, the wilderness, where there is not a man in it; so as to feed the untrodden and uninhabited land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 107:35; Psalms 104:14"> and cause it to send forth a crop of green herbs?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremias 14:22; Psalms 147:8; Jeremias 10:13"> Who is the rain's father? and who has generated the drops of dew?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 147:16–17; Job 37:10; Job 38:8"> And out of whose womb comes the ice? and who has produced the frost in the sky,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 37:10"> which descends like flowing water? who has terrified the face of the ungodly?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Amos 5:8; Job 9:9"> And dost thou understand the band of Pleias, and hast thou opened the barrier of Orion?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 38:31; Job 9:9; Kings IV 23:5"> Or wilt thou reveal Mazuroth in his season, and the evening star with his rays? Wilt thou guide them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremias 31:35–36; Genesis 8:22; Job 38:12–13"> And knowest thou the changes of heaven, or the events which take place together under heaven?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 22:11; Job 36:27–28; Amos 5:8"> And wilt thou call a cloud with thy voice, and will it obey thee with a violent shower of much rain?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 36:32; Job 37:3; Esaias 65:1"> And wilt thou send lightnings, and they shall go? and shall they say to thee, What is thy pleasure?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Job 32:8; Psalms 51:6; Proverbs 2:6"> And who has given to women skill in weaving, or knowledge of embroidery?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Psalms 147:4; Genesis 15:5; Genesis 8:1"> And who is he that numbers the clouds in wisdom, and has bowed the heaven down to the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> For it is spread out as dusty earth, and I have cemented it as one hewn stone to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 104:21; Job 4:10–11; Psalms 34:10"> And wilt thou hunt a prey for the lions? and satisfy the desires of the serpents?</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Job 37:8; Genesis 49:9; Numbers 24:9"> For they fear in their lairs, and lying in wait couch in the woods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 12:24; Matthew 6:26; Psalms 147:9"> And who has prepared food for the raven? for its young ones wander and cry to the Lord, in search of food.</span>
