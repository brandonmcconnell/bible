<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 12:4; Psalms 35:15–16; Psalms 69:12"> But now the youngest have laughed me to scorn, now they reprove me in their turn, whose fathers I set at nought; whom I did not deem worthy to be with my shepherd dogs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Yea, why had I the strength of their hands? for them the full term of life was lost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 24:5; Hebrews 11:38; Job 24:13–16"> One is childless in want and famine, such as they that fled but lately the distress and misery of drought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 15:16; Kings IV 4:38–39; Amos 7:14"> Who compass the salt places on the sounding shore, who had salt herbs for their food, and were dishonourable and of no repute, in want of every good thing; who also ate roots of trees by reason of great hunger.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 4:25; Daniel 4:32–33; Psalms 109:10"> Thieves have risen up against me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings I 22:1–2; Judges 6:2; Esaias 2:19"> whose houses were the caves of the rocks, who lived under the wild shrubs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 6:5; Job 11:12; Genesis 16:12"> They will cry out among the rustling bushes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 16:22; Job 40:4; Esaias 32:6"> They are sons of fools and vile men, whose name and glory are quenched from off the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Lamentations 3:63; Job 17:6; Lamentations 3:14"> But now I am their music, and they have me for a by-word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 26:67; Esaias 50:6; Numbers 12:14"> And they stood aloof and abhorred me, and spared not to spit in my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 12:21; Ruth 1:21; Job 12:18"> For he has opened his quiver and afflicted me: they also have cast off the restraint of my presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 19:12; Esaias 3:5; Job 19:18"> They have risen up against me on the right hand of their offspring; they have stretched out their foot, and directed against me the ways of their destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Esaias 3:12; Zacharias 1:15; Psalms 69:26"> My paths are ruined; for they have stripped off my raiment: he has shot at me with his weapons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:16; Psalms 18:4; Psalms 69:14–15"> And he has pleaded against me as he will: I am overwhelmed with pains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Osee 13:3; Job 6:4; Esaias 44:22"> My pains return upon me; my hope is gone like the wind, and my safety as a cloud.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 42:4; Psalms 22:14; Esaias 53:12"> Even now my life shall be poured forth upon me; and days of anguish seize me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 7:4; Job 33:19–21; Esaias 38:13"> And by night my bones are confounded; and my sinews are relaxed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 7:5; Job 19:20; Job 2:7"> With great force my disease has taken hold of my garment: it has compassed me as the collar of my coat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 9:31; Job 42:6; Genesis 18:27"> And thou hast counted me as clay; my portion is in dust and ashes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 19:7; Psalms 80:4–5; Psalms 22:2"> And I have cried to thee, but thou hearest me not: but they stood still, and observed me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 10:14–17; Job 16:9–14; Job 19:6–9"> They attacked me also without mercy: thou hast scourged me with a strong hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 27:21; Psalms 1:4; Osee 4:19"> And thou hast put me to grief, and hast cast me away from safety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:5–7; Hebrews 9:27; Ecclesiastes 9:5"> For I know that death will destroy me: for the earth is the house appointed for every mortal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Judges 5:31; Matthew 27:39–44; Job 19:7"> Oh then that I might lay hands upon myself, or at least ask another, and he should do this for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Psalms 35:13–14; Jeremias 13:17"> Yet I wept over every helpless man; I groaned when I saw a man in distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremias 8:15; Job 3:25–26; Jeremias 14:19"> But I, when I waited for good things, behold, days of evils came the more upon me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Lamentations 2:11; Jeremias 31:20; Jeremias 4:19"> My belly boiled, and would not cease: the days of poverty prevented me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 42:9; Psalms 38:6; Psalms 43:2"> I went mourning without restraint: and I have stood and cried out in the assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Michæas 1:8; Psalms 102:6; Psalms 44:19"> I am become a brother of monsters, and a companion of ostriches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Lamentations 4:8; Psalms 102:3; Psalms 119:83"> And my skin has been greatly blackened, and my bones are burned with heat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Lamentations 5:15; Esaias 21:4; Esaias 22:12"> My harp also has been turned into mourning, and my song into my weeping.</span>
</div>
