<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 22:21; Job 40:8; Job 27:2"> Then Elius the son of Barachiel, the Buzite, of the kindred of Ram, of the country of Ausis, was angered: and he was very angry with Job, because he justified himself before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 8:6; Job 24:25; Job 22:5–30"> And he was also very angry with his three friends, because they were not able to return answers to Job, yet set him down for an ungodly man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 32:11–12; Proverbs 18:13"> But Elius had forborne to give an answer to Job, because they were older than he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 32:2; Exodus 32:19"> And Elius saw that there was no answer in the mouth of the three men; and he was angered in his wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 15:10; 1 Timothy 5:1; Titus 2:6"> And Elius the Buzite the son of Barachiel answered and said, I am younger in age, and ye are elder; wherefore I kept silence, fearing to declare to you my own knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 16:31; Job 12:12; Hebrews 5:12"> And I said, It is not time that speaks, though in many years men know wisdom:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 38:36; Job 33:4; 2 Timothy 3:16"> but there is a spirit in mortals; and the inspiration of the Almighty is that which teaches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 11:25; Job 12:20; Ecclesiastes 4:13"> The long-lived are not wise as such; neither do the aged know judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:25; 1 Corinthians 7:40"> Wherefore I said, Hear me, and I will tell you what I know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 29:21; Job 5:27; Proverbs 28:11"> Hearken to my words; for I will speak in your hearing, until ye shall have tried the matter with words:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 32:3; 1 Timothy 1:7"> and I shall understand as far as you; and, behold, there was no one of you that answered Job his words in argument,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremias 9:23; 1 Corinthians 1:27–29; 1 Corinthians 3:18"> lest ye should say, We have found that we have added wisdom to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And ye have commissioned a man to speak such words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 6:24–25; Matthew 22:46; Job 29:22"> They were afraid, they answered no longer; they gave up their speaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 1:19; Job 13:5; Amos 5:13"> I waited, (for I had not spoken,) because they stood still, they answered not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 35:3–4; Job 33:12; Job 32:10"> And Elius continued, and said, I will again speak,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 39:3; Acts 4:20; 2 Corinthians 5:13–14"> for I am full of words, for the spirit of my belly destroys me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 9:17"> And my belly is as a skin of sweet wine bound up and ready to burst; or as a brazier's labouring bellows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 8:6–7; Job 21:3; Job 13:13"> I will speak, that I may open my lips and relieve myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 19:15; Job 13:8; Matthew 22:16"> For truly I will not be awed because of man, nor indeed will I be confounded before a mortal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 12:2–3; 1 Thessalonians 2:5; Proverbs 29:5"> For I know not how to respect persons: and if otherwise, even the moths would eat me.</span>
