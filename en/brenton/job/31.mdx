<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="James 1:14–15; 1 John 2:16; Matthew 5:28–29"> I made a covenant with mine eyes, and I will not think upon a virgin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 20:29; Job 27:13; Hebrews 13:4"> Now what portion has God given from above? and is there an inheritance given of the Mighty One from the highest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 21:30; Job 34:22; Matthew 7:13"> Alas! destruction to the unrighteous, and rejection to them that do iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 34:21; Proverbs 5:21; Chronicles II 16:9"> Will he not see my way, and number all my steps?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Psalms 44:20–21; Psalms 12:2"> But if I had gone with scorners, and if too my foot has hasted to deceit:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 7:8–9; Daniel 5:27; Esaias 26:7"> (for I am weighed in a just balance, and the Lord knows my innocence:)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 15:39; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Matthew 5:29"> if my foot has turned aside out of the way, or if mine heart has followed mine eye, and if too I have touched gifts with my hands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Michæas 6:15; Leviticus 26:16; Deuteronomy 28:38"> then let me sow, and let others eat; and let me be uprooted on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings III 11:4; Ecclesiastes 7:26; Judges 16:5"> If my heart has gone forth after another man's wife, and if I laid wait at her doors;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 8:10; Kings II 12:11; Esaias 47:2"> then let my wife also please another, and let my children be brought low.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 20:10; Deuteronomy 22:22–24; Job 31:28"> For the rage of anger is not to be controlled, in the case of defiling another man's wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 6:27; Jeremias 5:7–9; Job 15:30"> For it is a fire burning on every side, and whomsoever it attacks, it utterly destroys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:14–15; Exodus 21:26–27; Leviticus 25:46"> And if too I despised the judgment of my servant or my handmaid, when they pleaded with me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 2:13; Psalms 9:12; Zacharias 2:13"> what then shall I do if the Lord should try me? and if also he should at all visit me, can I make an answer?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 14:31; Malachias 2:10; Job 34:19"> Were not they too formed as I also was formed in the womb? yea, we were formed in the same womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esaias 38:14; Deuteronomy 15:7–10; Deuteronomy 28:32"> But the helpless missed not whatever need they had, and I did not cause the eye of the widow to fail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 29:12–16; John 13:29; Job 22:7"> And if too I ate my morsel alone, and did not impart of it to the orphan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> (for I nourished them as a father from my youth, and guided them from my mother's womb.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 22:6; Luke 3:11; Job 24:4"> And if too I overlooked the naked as he was perishing, and did not clothe him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:13; Job 29:11"> and if the poor did not bless me, and their shoulders were not warmed with the fleece of my lambs;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 22:9; Job 29:12; Michæas 7:3"> if I lifted my hand against an orphan, trusting that my strength was far superior to his:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 137:6; Job 38:15; Job 31:40"> let then my shoulder start from the blade-bone, and my arm be crushed off from the elbow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 13:11; Psalms 119:120; 2 Corinthians 5:11"> For the fear of the Lord constrained me, and I cannot bear up by reason of his burden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 49:6–7; Deuteronomy 8:12–14; Proverbs 11:28"> If I made gold my treasure, and if too I trusted the precious stone;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 62:10; Ambacum 1:16; Esaias 10:13–14"> and if too I rejoiced when my wealth was abundant, and if too I laid my hand on innumerable treasures:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jezekiel 8:16; Deuteronomy 4:19; Deuteronomy 17:3"> (do we not see the shining sun eclipsed, and the moon waning? for they have not power to continue:)</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:6; Deuteronomy 11:16; Psalms 2:12"> and if my heart was secretly deceived, and if I have laid my hand upon my mouth and kissed it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:2–7; Job 31:11; Psalms 50:6"> let this also then be reckoned to me as the greatest iniquity: for I should have lied against the Lord Most High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 24:17–18; Proverbs 17:5; Psalms 35:13–14"> And if too I was glad at the fall of mine enemies, and mine heart said, Aha!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Romans 12:14; Exodus 23:4–5"> let then mine ear hear my curse, and let me be a by-word among my people in my affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 1:18; Proverbs 1:11–12; Michæas 3:2–3"> And if too my handmaids have often said, Oh that we might be satisfied with his flesh; (whereas I was very kind:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 13:2; Genesis 19:2–3; Judges 19:20–21"> for the stranger did not lodge without, and my door was opened to every one that came:)</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; Osee 6:7; Acts 5:8"> or if too having sinned unintentionally, I hid my sin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 23:2; Proverbs 29:25; Esther 4:11"> (for I did not stand in awe of a great multitude, so as not to declare boldly before them:) and if too I permitted a poor man to go out of my door with an empty bosom:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 13:21–22; Job 40:4–5; Job 17:3"> (Oh that I had a hearer,) and if I had not feared the hand of the Lord; and as to the written charge which I had against any one,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Esaias 22:22; Job 29:14; Esaias 62:3"> I would place it as a chaplet on my shoulders, and read it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Job 13:15; Ephesians 3:12; Hebrews 4:15–16"> And if I did not read it and return it, having taken nothing from the debtor:</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Ambacum 2:11; James 5:4; Psalms 65:13"> If at any time the land groaned against me, and if its furrows mourned together;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Kings III 21:19; James 5:4; Esaias 26:21"> and if I ate its strength alone without price, and if too I grieved the heart of the owner of the soil, by taking aught from him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Genesis 3:17–18; Malachias 1:3; Psalms 72:20"> then let the nettle come up to me instead of wheat, and a bramble instead of barley. And Job ceased speaking.</span>
