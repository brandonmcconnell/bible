<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 144:3; Psalms 143:2; Psalms 8:4"> Hast thou not taken account even of him, and caused him to enter into judgment before thee?</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esaias 27:6; Esaias 11:1; Job 14:14"> For there is hope for a tree, even if it should be cut down, that it shall blossom again, and its branch shall not fail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:36; Esaias 26:19; John 12:24"> For though its root should grow old in the earth, and its stem die in the rock;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 11:17–24; Jezekiel 17:3–10; Jezekiel 17:22–24"> it will blossom from the scent of water, and will produce a crop, as one newly planted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 19:26; Job 7:7–10; Job 14:12"> But a man that has died is utterly gone; and when a mortal has fallen, he is no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esaias 19:5; Jeremias 15:18; Job 6:15–18"> For the sea wastes in length of time, and a river fails and is dried up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 10:21–22; Job 3:13; Ecclesiastes 3:19–21"> And man that has lain down in death shall certainly not rise again till the heaven be dissolved, and they shall not awake from their sleep.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 13:32; Esaias 57:1–2; Esaias 26:20–21"> For oh that thou hadst kept me in the grave, and hadst hidden me until thy wrath should cease, and thou shouldest set me a time in which thou wouldest remember me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:51–52; Job 7:1; Acts 26:8"> For if a man should die, shall he live again, having accomplished the days of his life? I will wait till I exist again?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 13:22; Psalms 138:8; Job 10:8"> Then shalt thou call, and I will hearken to thee: but do not thou reject the work of thine hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 10:6; Job 31:4; Psalms 139:1–4"> But thou hast numbered my devices: and not one of my sins shall escape thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:34; Osee 13:12; Job 21:19"> And thou hast sealed up my transgressions in a bag, and marked if I have been guilty of any transgression unawares.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 6:14; Job 18:4; Jeremias 4:24"> And verily a mountain falling will utterly be destroyed, and a rock shall be worn out of its place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 7:6; Job 27:8; Job 19:10"> The waters wear the stones, and waters falling headlong overflow a heap of the earth: and thou destroyest the hope of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 14:14; Job 2:12; Ecclesiastes 8:8"> Thou drivest him to an end, and he is gone: thou settest thy face against him, and sendest him away;</span>
