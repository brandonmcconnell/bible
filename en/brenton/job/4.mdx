<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 8:1; Job 22:1; Job 15:1"> Then Eliphaz the Thæmanite answered and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 32:18–20; Acts 4:20; Jeremias 6:11"> Hast thou been often spoken to in distress? but who shall endure the force of thy words?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Esaias 35:3; Hebrews 12:12; Proverbs 10:21"> For whereas thou hast instructed many, and hast strengthened the hands of the weak one,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 12:18; Psalms 145:14; Hebrews 12:12"> and hast supported the failing with words, and hast imparted courage to feeble knees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 19:21; 2 Corinthians 4:16; Job 2:5"> Yet now that pain has come upon thee, and touched thee, thou art troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 3:26; Proverbs 14:26; Job 1:1"> Is not thy fear founded in folly, thy hope also, and the mischief of thy way?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 37:25; 2 Peter 2:9; Job 36:7"> Remember then who has perished, being pure? or when were the true-hearted utterly destroyed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 6:7–8; Proverbs 22:8; Osee 8:7"> Accordingly as I have seen men ploughing barren places, and they that sow them will reap sorrows for themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 15:30; Esaias 11:4; Esaias 30:33"> They shall perish by the command of the Lord, and shall be utterly consumed by the breath of his wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 58:6; Proverbs 30:14; Psalms 3:7"> The strength of the lion, and the voice of the lioness, and the exulting cry of serpents are quenched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 34:10; Job 8:3–4; Job 1:19"> The old lion has perished for want of food, and the lions' whelps have forsaken one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 26:14; Psalms 62:11; 1 Corinthians 13:12"> But if there had been any truth in thy words, none of these evils would have befallen thee. Shall not mine ear receive excellent revelations from him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 33:14–16; Genesis 2:21; Genesis 31:24"> But as when terror falls upon men, with dread and a sound in the night,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Job 33:19; Luke 1:12"> horror and trembling seized me, and caused all my bones greatly to shake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 1:7; Daniel 5:6; Psalms 104:4"> And a spirit came before my face; and my hair and flesh quivered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings III 19:12"> I arose and perceived it not: I looked, and there was no form before my eyes: but I only heard a breath and a voice, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 9:2; Job 25:4; Ecclesiastes 7:20"> What, shall a mortal be pure before the Lord? or a man be blameless in regard to his works?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 25:5–6; 2 Peter 2:4; Jude 1:6"> Whereas he trusts not in his servants, and perceives perverseness in his angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 10:9; Genesis 2:7; Genesis 3:19"> But as for them that dwell in houses of clay, of whom we also are formed of the same clay, he smites them like a moth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 20:7; Job 14:14; Job 16:22"> And from morning to evening they no longer exist: they have perished, because they cannot help themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 36:12; Psalms 39:11; Job 8:22"> For he blows upon them, and they are withered: they have perished for lack of wisdom.</span>
