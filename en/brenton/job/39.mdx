<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 29:9; Kings I 24:2; Psalms 104:18"> Say if thou knowest the time of the bringing forth of the wild goats of the rock, and if thou hast marked the calving of the hinds:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 2:24"> and if thou hast numbered the full months of their being with young, and if thou hast relieved their pangs:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and hast reared their young without fear; and wilt thou loosen their pangs?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Their young will break forth; they will be multiplied with offspring: their young will go forth, and will not return to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 11:12; Job 24:5; Job 6:5"> And who is he that sent forth the wild ass free? and who loosed his bands?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 107:34; Job 24:5; Deuteronomy 29:23"> whereas I made his habitation the wilderness, and the salt land his coverts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 5:18; Job 3:18; Exodus 5:13–16"> He laughs to scorn the multitude of the city, and hears not the chiding of the tax-gatherer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 104:27–28; Psalms 145:15–16; Job 40:15"> He will survey the mountains as his pasture, and he seeks after every green thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 23:22; Deuteronomy 33:17; Psalms 92:10"> And will the unicorn be willing to serve thee, or to lie down at thy manger?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Osee 10:10–11; Psalms 129:3; Job 39:7"> And wilt thou bind his yoke with thongs, or will he plough furrows for thee in the plain?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 1:26; Psalms 144:14; Genesis 42:26"> And dost thou trust him, because his strength is great? and wilt thou commit thy works to him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:15; Proverbs 3:16; Amos 2:13"> And wilt thou believe that he will return to thee thy seed, and bring it in to thy threshing-floor?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremias 8:7; Zacharias 5:9; Chronicles II 9:21"> The peacock has a beautiful wing: if the stork and the ostrich conceive, it is worthy of notice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> for the ostrich will leave her eggs in the ground, and warm them on the dust,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and has forgotten that the foot will scatter them, and the wild beasts of the field trample them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Lamentations 4:3; Deuteronomy 28:56–57; Kings III 3:26–27"> She has hardened herself against her young ones, as though she bereaved not herself: she labours in vain without fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 35:11; Job 17:4; Esaias 57:17"> For God has withholden wisdom from her, and not given her a portion in understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 41:29; Job 39:22; Job 5:22"> In her season she will lift herself on high; she will scorn the horse and his rider.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 147:10; Psalms 93:1; Psalms 104:1"> Hast thou invested the horse with strength, and clothed his neck with terror?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremias 8:16; Job 41:20–21; Joel 2:5"> And hast thou clad him in perfect armour, and made his breast glorious with courage?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremias 8:6; Psalms 19:5; Kings I 17:4–10"> He paws exulting in the plain, and goes forth in strength into the plain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 41:33; Job 39:16; Job 39:18"> He laughs to scorn a king as he meets him, and will by no means turn back from the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 41:26–29"> The bow and sword resound against him; and his rage will swallow up the ground:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Amos 3:6; Luke 24:41; Job 37:20"> and he will not believe until the trumpet sounds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 70:3; Jezekiel 26:2; Jezekiel 36:2"> And when the trumpet sounds, he says, Aha! and afar off he smells the war with prancing and neighing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:15; Song of Solomon 2:12; Leviticus 16:11"> And does the hawk remain steady by thy wisdom, having spread out her wings unmoved, looking toward the region of the south?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Obdias 1:4; Jeremias 49:16; Exodus 19:4"> And does the eagle rise at thy command, and the vulture remain sitting over his nest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Kings I 14:4"> on a crag of a rock, and in a secret place?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 9:26"> Thence he seeks food, his eyes observe from far.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 24:28; Luke 17:37; Jezekiel 39:17–19"> And his young ones roll themselves in blood, and wherever the carcases may be, immediately they are found.</span>
