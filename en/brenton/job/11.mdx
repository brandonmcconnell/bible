<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 20:1"> Then Sophar the Minæan answered and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 10:19; James 1:19; Job 18:2"> He that speaks much, should also hear on the other side: or does the fluent speaker think himself to be righteous? blessed is the short-lived offspring of woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 17:2; Job 24:25; Titus 2:8"> Be not a speaker of many words; for is there none to answer thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 10:7; Job 6:10; Job 35:2"> For say not, I am pure in my works, and blameless before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 38:1–2; Job 31:35; Job 33:6–18"> But oh that the Lord would speak to thee, and open his lips with thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 9:13; Lamentations 3:22; Daniel 2:28"> Then shall he declare to thee the power of wisdom; for it shall be double of that which is with thee: and then shalt thou know, that a just recompence of thy sins has come to thee from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Ecclesiastes 3:11; Psalms 145:3"> Wilt thou find out the traces of the Lord? or hast thou come to the end of that which the Almighty has made?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 22:12; Psalms 139:6–8; Esaias 55:9"> Heaven is high; and what wilt thou do? and there are deeper things than those in hell; what dost thou know?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 139:9–10; Job 28:24–25; Psalms 65:5–8"> Or longer than the measure of the earth, or the breadth of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:12–13; Job 12:14; Revelation 3:7"> And if he should overthrow all things, who will say to him, What hast thou done?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 10:14; Hebrews 4:13; Revelation 2:23"> For he knows the works of transgressors; and when he sees wickedness, he will not overlook it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 2:20; Ecclesiastes 3:18; James 3:13–17"> But man vainly buoys himself up with words; and a mortal born of woman is like an ass of the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 143:6; Psalms 88:9; Kings I 7:3"> For if thou hast made thine heart pure, and liftest up thine hands towards him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:23; James 4:8; Job 4:7"> if there is any iniquity in thy hands, put it far from thee, and let not unrighteousness lodge in thy habitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 22:26; 1 John 2:28; 1 John 3:19–22"> For thus shall thy countenance shine again, as pure water; and thou shalt divest thyself of uncleanness, and shalt not fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esaias 65:16; Ecclesiastes 5:20; John 16:21"> And thou shalt forget trouble, as a wave that has passed by; and thou shalt not be scared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Esaias 58:8–10; Psalms 37:6; Proverbs 4:18"> And thy prayer shall be as the morning star, and life shall arise to thee as from the noon-day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:24–26; Psalms 43:5; Psalms 3:5"> And thou shalt be confident, because thou hast hope; and peace shall dawn to thee from out of anxiety and care.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 45:12; Esaias 60:14; Job 42:8–9"> For thou shalt be at ease, and there shall be no one to fight against thee; and many shall charge, and make supplication to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:65; Job 31:16; Job 17:5"> But safety shall fail them; for their hope is destruction, and the eyes of the ungodly shall waste away.</span>
