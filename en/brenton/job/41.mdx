<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 16:25–30; Job 28:11"> Who will open the face of his garment? and who can enter within the fold of his breast-plate?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 14:11"> Who will open the doors of his face? terror is round about his teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> His inwards are as brazen plates, and the texture of his skin as a smyrite stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings III 20:11; Luke 14:31–32; Kings IV 10:4"> One part cleaves fast to another, and the air cannot come between them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esaias 28:19; Kings I 3:11; Luke 21:11"> They will remain united each to the other: they are closely joined, and cannot be separated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:4; Psalms 2:11–12; 1 Corinthians 10:22"> At his sneezing a light shines, and his eyes are as the appearance of the morning star.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 11:35; Psalms 24:1; Job 35:7"> Out of his mouth proceed as it were burning lamps, and as it were hearths of fire are cast abroad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 1:25"> Out of his nostrils proceeds smoke of a furnace burning with fire of coals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings IV 19:28; Psalms 32:9; James 3:3"> His breath is as live coals, and a flame goes out of his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:4; Psalms 57:4; Psalms 58:6"> And power is lodged in his neck, before him destruction runs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 5:5; Revelation 5:2–3; Jeremias 9:23"> The flesh also of his body is joined together: if one pours violence upon him, he shall not be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> His heart is firm as a stone, and it stands like an unyielding anvil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And when he turns, he is a terror to the four-footed wild beasts which leap upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 3:9; Revelation 1:14"> If spears should come against him, men will effect nothing, either with the spear or the breast-plate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 18:8"> For he considers iron as chaff, and brass as rotten wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremias 1:13–14"> The bow of brass shall not wound him, he deems a slinger as grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 18:8; Esaias 30:33; Ambacum 3:5"> Mauls are counted as stubble; and he laughs to scorn the waving of the firebrand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Osee 13:14; Job 40:16; 1 Corinthians 15:55–57"> His lair is formed of sharp points; and all the gold of the sea under him is as an immense quantity of clay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> He makes the deep boil like a brazen caldron; and he regards the sea as a pot of ointment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremias 5:3; Esaias 48:4; Zacharias 7:12"> and the lowest part of the deep as a captive: he reckons the deep as his range.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jonas 1:4–6; Psalms 107:28"> There is nothing upon the earth like to him, formed to be sported with by my angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 39:21–24"> He beholds every high thing: and he is king of all that are in the waters.</span>
