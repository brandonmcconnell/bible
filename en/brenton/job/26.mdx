<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> But Job answered and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esaias 35:3–4; Job 4:3–4; Esaias 41:5–7"> To whom dost thou attach thyself, or whom art thou going to assist? is it not he that has much strength, and he who has a strong arm?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 71:15–18; Job 32:11–13; Job 33:3"> To whom hast thou given counsel? is it not to him who has all wisdom? whom wilt thou follow? is it not one who has the greatest power?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings III 22:23–24; Job 32:18; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> To whom hast thou uttered words? and whose breath is it that has come forth from thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 88:10; Genesis 6:4; Job 41:1–34"> Shall giants be born from under the water and the inhabitants thereof?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 139:8; Proverbs 15:11; Hebrews 4:13"> Hell is naked before him, and destruction has no covering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 9:8; Psalms 104:2–5; Esaias 40:22"> He stretches out the north wind upon nothing, and he upon nothing hangs the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 30:4; Jeremias 10:13; Esaias 5:6"> binding water in his clouds, and the cloud is not rent under it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; Job 22:14; Ambacum 3:3–5"> He keeps back the face of his throne, stretching out his cloud upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 8:29; Job 38:8–11; Jeremias 5:22"> He has encompassed the face of the water by an appointed ordinance, until the end of light and darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings I 2:8; Psalms 18:7; Job 15:15"> The pillars of heaven are prostrate and astonished at his rebuke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esaias 51:15; Jeremias 31:35; Psalms 89:9–10"> He has calmed the sea with his might, and by his wisdom the whale has been overthrown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Esaias 27:1; Psalms 104:30; Psalms 74:13–14"> And the barriers of heaven fear him, and by a command he has slain the apostate dragon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esaias 40:26–29; Psalms 145:3; Romans 11:33"> Behold, these are parts of his way; and we will hearken to him at the least intimation of his word: but the strength of his thunder who knows, when he shall employ it?</span>
