<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 1:7; Ecclesiastes 3:17; 2 Peter 3:7–8"> But why have the seasons been hidden from the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:14; Deuteronomy 27:17; Proverbs 23:10"> while the ungodly have passed over the bound, carrying off the flock with the shepherd?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:6; Deuteronomy 24:10–13; Kings I 12:3"> They have led away the ass of the fatherless, and taken the widow's ox for a pledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 28:28; Job 24:14; Jezekiel 22:29"> They have turned aside the weak from the right way: and the meek of the earth have hidden themselves together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Michæas 2:1; Job 39:5–7; Psalms 104:23"> And they have departed like asses in the field, having gone forth on my account according to their own order: his bread is sweet to his little ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Michæas 6:15; Deuteronomy 28:51; Deuteronomy 28:33"> They have reaped a field that was not their own before the time: the poor have laboured in the vineyards of the ungodly without pay and without food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 22:26–27; Job 22:6; Deuteronomy 24:11–13"> They have caused many naked to sleep without clothes, and they have taken away the covering of their body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 4:5; Song of Solomon 5:2; Hebrews 11:38"> They are wet with the drops of the mountains: they have embraced the rock, because they had no shelter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:5; Kings IV 4:1"> They have snatched the fatherless from the breast, and have afflicted the outcast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 2:7–8; Deuteronomy 24:19; Amos 5:11–12"> And they have wrongfully caused others to sleep without clothing, and taken away the morsel of the hungry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="James 5:4; Jeremias 22:13; Deuteronomy 25:4"> They have unrighteously laid wait in narrow places, and have not known the righteous way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:1; Malachias 2:17; Romans 2:4–5"> Who have cast forth the poor from the city and their own houses, and the soul of the children has groaned aloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 3:19–20; Proverbs 4:19; Jude 1:6"> Why then has he not visited these? forasmuch as they were upon the earth, and took no notice, and they knew not the way of righteousness, neither have they walked in their appointed paths?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Michæas 2:1–2; Psalms 10:8–10; Revelation 3:3"> But having known their works, he delivered them into darkness: and in the night one will be as a thief:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 10:11; Proverbs 7:9–10; Psalms 94:7"> and the eye of the adulterer has watched for the darkness, saying, Eye shall not perceive me, and he puts a covering on his face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 3:20; Ephesians 5:11–13; Matthew 24:43"> In darkness he digs through houses: by day they conceal themselves securely: they know not the light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 3:5; Jeremias 2:26; Psalms 73:18–19"> For the morning is to them all as the shadow of death, for each will be conscious of the terror of the shadow of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 69:22; Psalms 58:7; Job 9:26"> He is swift on the face of the water: let his portion be cursed on the earth; and let their plants be laid bare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 49:14; Luke 16:22; Proverbs 14:32"> Let them be withered upon the earth; for they have plundered the sheaves of the fatherless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; Daniel 4:14; Job 18:16–17"> Then is his sin brought to remembrance, and he vanishes like a vapour of dew: but let what he has done be recompensed to him, and let every unrighteous one be crushed like rotten wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings I 1:6–7; Job 22:9; Job 29:13"> For he has not treated the barren woman well, and has had no pity on a feeble woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 6:4–9; Esther 3:8–10; John 19:12–16"> And in wrath he has overthrown the helpless: therefore when he has arisen, a man will not feel secure of his own life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 15:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Revelation 2:23"> When he has fallen sick, let him not hope to recover: but let him perish by disease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 37:10; Psalms 37:35–36; James 5:1–3"> For his exaltation has hurt many; but he has withered as mallows in the heat, or as an ear of corn falling off of itself from the stalk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 9:24; Job 11:2–3; Job 6:28"> But if not, who is he that says I speak falsely, and will make my words of no account?</span>
