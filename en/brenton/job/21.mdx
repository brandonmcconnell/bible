<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> But Job answered and said,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 2:1; Job 34:2; Job 15:11"> Hear ye, hear ye my words, that I may not have this consolation from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 17:2; Job 16:10; Job 16:20"> Raise me, and I will speak; then ye shall not laugh me to scorn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 102:1; Psalms 42:11; Psalms 142:2–3"> What! is my reproof of man? and why should I not be angry?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 29:9; Job 40:4; Judges 18:19"> Look upon me, and wonder, laying your hand upon your cheek.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ambacum 3:16; Psalms 119:120; Psalms 88:15"> For even when I remember, I am alarmed, and pains seize my flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 73:3–12; Job 12:6; Jeremias 12:1–3"> Wherefore do the ungodly live, and grow old even in wealth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 17:6; Job 5:3–4; Job 20:10"> Their seed is according to their desire, and their children are in their sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 9:34; Psalms 73:5; Psalms 73:19"> Their houses are prosperous, neither have they any where cause for fear, neither is there a scourge from the Lord upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 23:26; Luke 16:19; Deuteronomy 7:13–14"> Their cow does not cast her calf, and their beast with young is safe, and does not miscarry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 107:41; Psalms 127:3–5"> And they remain as an unfailing flock, and their children play before them, taking up the psaltery and harp;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 6:4–6; Esaias 5:12; Genesis 4:21"> and they rejoice at the voice of a song.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 36:11; Luke 12:19–20; Psalms 73:4"> And they spend their days in wealth, and fall asleep in the rest of the grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:17; Romans 8:7; 2 Thessalonians 2:10–12"> Yet such a man says to the Lord, Depart from me; I desire not to know thy ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 5:2; Job 34:9; Malachias 1:13–14"> What is the Mighty One, that we should serve him? and what profit is there that we should approach him?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 1:1; Job 22:18; Genesis 49:6"> For their good things were in their hands, but he regards not the works of the ungodly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 18:5–6; Proverbs 20:20; Proverbs 13:9"> Nevertheless, the lamp of the ungodly also shall be put out, and destruction shall come upon them, and pangs of vengeance shall seize them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 1:4; Psalms 83:13; Job 13:25"> And they shall be as chaff before the wind, or as dust which the storm has taken up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; Jezekiel 18:19–20; Jezekiel 18:2"> Let his substance fail to supply his children: God shall recompense him, and he shall know it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 14:10; Esaias 51:17; Psalms 75:8"> Let his eyes see his own destruction, and let him not be saved by the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 14:5; Ecclesiastes 2:18–19; Psalms 55:23"> For his desire is in his house with him, and the number of his months has been suddenly cut off.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 11:34; 1 Corinthians 2:16; Esaias 40:13–14"> Is it not the Lord who teaches understanding and knowledge? and does not he judge murders?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 20:22–23; Luke 12:19–21; Psalms 73:4–5"> One shall die in his perfect strength, and wholly at ease and prosperous;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 3:8; Psalms 17:10; Job 15:27"> and his inwards are full of fat, and his marrow is diffused throughout him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jezekiel 4:16–17; Job 7:11; Proverbs 14:10"> And another dies in bitterness of soul, not eating any good thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:2; Esaias 14:11; Job 20:11"> But they lie down in the earth together, and corruption covers them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 5:22; Psalms 119:86; Psalms 59:4"> So I know you, that ye presumptuously attack me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 8:22; Job 20:7; Ambacum 2:9–11"> so that ye will say, Where is the house of the prince? and where is the covering of the tabernacles of the ungodly?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 129:8"> Ask those that go by the way, and do not disown their tokens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 16:4; 2 Peter 2:9–17; 2 Peter 3:7"> For the wicked hastens to the day of destruction: they shall be led away for the day of his vengeance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Galatians 2:11; Deuteronomy 7:10; Romans 12:19"> Who will tell him his way to his face, whereas he has done it? who shall recompense him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 16:22; Psalms 49:14; Jezekiel 32:21–32"> And he has been led away to the tombs, and he has watched over the heaps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 9:27; Job 24:24; Job 30:23"> The stones of the valley have been sweet to him, and every man shall depart after him, and there are innumerable ones before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 16:2; Job 32:3; Job 13:4"> How then do ye comfort me in vain? whereas I have no rest from your molestation.</span>
</div>
</div>
