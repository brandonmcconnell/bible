<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> But Job answered and said,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 13:4–5; Psalms 69:26; Job 11:2–3"> I have heard many such things: poor comforters are ye all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 6:26; Job 15:2; Titus 2:8"> What! is there any reason in vain words? or what will hinder thee from answering?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 109:25; Psalms 22:7; Lamentations 2:15"> I also will speak as ye do: if indeed your soul were in my soul's stead, then would I insult you with words, and I would shake my head at you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 27:9; Galatians 6:1; Esaias 35:3–4"> And would there were strength in my mouth, and I would not spare the movement of my lips.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 10:1; Psalms 88:15–18; Psalms 77:1–9"> For if I should speak, I shall not feel the pain of my wound: and if I should be silent, how shall I be wounded the less?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 7:3; Job 1:15–19; Psalms 6:6–7"> But now he has made me weary, and a worn-out fool; and thou hast laid hold of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 10:17; Job 19:20; Ruth 1:21"> My falsehood has become a testimony, and has risen up against me: it has confronted me to my face.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 35:16; Job 13:24; Lamentations 2:16"> In his anger he has cast me down; he has gnashed his teeth upon me: the weapons of his robbers have fallen upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 35:15; Psalms 22:13; Esaias 50:6"> He has attacked me with the keen glances of his eyes; with his sharp spear he has smitten me down upon my knees; and they have run upon me with one accord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 27:12; Psalms 7:14; Psalms 31:8"> For the Lord has delivered me into the hands of unrighteous men, and thrown me upon the ungodly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 7:20; Lamentations 3:11–12; Lamentations 3:4"> When I was at peace he distracted me: he took me by the hair of the head, and plucked it out: he set me up as a mark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 20:25; Job 6:4; Lamentations 2:11"> They surrounded me with spears, aiming at my reins: without sparing me they poured out my gall upon the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 15:8; Lamentations 3:3–5; Joel 2:7"> They overthrew me with fall upon fall: they ran upon me in their might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 37:34; Psalms 7:5; Psalms 75:5"> They sewed sackcloth upon my skin, and my strength has been spent on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 14:34; Psalms 102:3–5; Lamentations 1:16"> My belly has been parched with wailing, and darkness is on my eyelids.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 15:8; Psalms 66:18–19; Job 27:6–7"> Yet there was no injustice in my hands, and my prayer is pure.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Esaias 26:21; Jezekiel 24:7; Genesis 4:11"> Earth, cover not over the blood of my flesh, and let my cry have no place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 1:9; 1 Thessalonians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 11:31"> And now, behold, my witness is in heaven, and my advocate is on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Psalms 142:2; Job 16:4"> Let my supplication come to the Lord, and let mine eye weep before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 6:10; Esaias 45:9; Romans 9:20"> Oh that a man might plead before the Lord, even as the son of man with his neighbour!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 14:10; Job 14:14; Job 14:5"> But my years are numbered and their end come, and I shall go by the way by which I shall not return.</span>
</div>
