<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Job answered and said,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 64:3; Job 18:2; Job 27:2"> How long will ye vex my soul, and destroy me with words? only know that the Lord has dealt with me thus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 31:7; Nehemiah 4:12; Job 18:4–21"> Ye speak against me; ye do not feel for me, but bear hard upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Proverbs 9:12; Jezekiel 18:4"> Yea verily, I have erred in truth, (but the error abides with myself)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Proverbs 9:12; Jezekiel 18:4"> in having spoken words which it was not right to speak; and my words err, and are unseasonable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 35:26; Psalms 38:16; Psalms 55:12"> But alas! for ye magnify yourselves against me, and insult me with reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 18:8–10; Job 27:2; Job 16:11–14"> Know then that it is the Lord that has troubled me, and has raised his bulwark against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ambacum 1:2–3; Lamentations 3:8; Job 31:35–36"> Behold, I laugh at reproach; I will not speak: or I will cry out, but there is nowhere judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 3:7; Job 3:23; Proverbs 4:19"> I am fenced round about, and can by no means escape: he has set darkness before my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:44; Job 29:7–14; Psalms 89:39"> And he has stripped me of my glory, and has taken the crown from my head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 24:20; Psalms 37:35–36; 2 Corinthians 4:8–9"> He has torn me round about, and I am gone: and he has cut off my hope like a tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:24; Job 16:9; Job 33:10"> And he has dreadfully handled me in anger, and has counted me for an enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 30:12; Job 16:13; Esaias 51:23"> His troops also came upon me with one accord, liers in wait compassed my ways.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 88:8; Psalms 88:18; Psalms 69:8"> My brethren have stood aloof from me; they have recognised strangers rather than me: and my friends have become pitiless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 38:11; Jeremias 20:10; Kings II 16:23"> My nearest of kin have not acknowledged me, and they that knew my name, have forgotten me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 31:31–32; Psalms 123:3"> As for my household, and my maid-servants, I was a stranger before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 1:15–17; Job 1:19"> I called my servant, and he hearkened not; and my mouth intreated him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 2:9–10; Job 17:1"> And I besought my wife, and earnestly intreated the sons of my concubines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings IV 2:23; Job 30:12; Job 30:1"> But they rejected me for ever; whenever I rise up, they speak against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; Psalms 109:4–5; Psalms 55:12–14"> They that saw me abhorred me: the very persons whom I had loved, rose up against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 102:5; Lamentations 4:8; Job 33:19–22"> My flesh is corrupt under my skin, and my bones are held in my teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 1:11; Psalms 38:2; Job 2:5"> Pity me, pity me, O friends; for it is the hand of the Lord that has touched me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 69:26; Job 31:31; Job 16:13–14"> Wherefore do ye persecute me as also the Lord does, and are not satisfied with my flesh?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Esaias 30:8; Job 31:35; Esaias 8:1"> For oh that my words were written, and that they were recorded in a book for ever,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremias 17:1; Exodus 28:21; Exodus 32:16"> with an iron pen and lead, or graven in the rocks!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ephesians 1:7; Esaias 54:5; Psalms 78:35"> For I know that he is eternal who is about to deliver me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 5:8; 1 John 3:2; Psalms 17:15"> and to raise up upon the earth my skin that endures these sufferings: for these things have been accomplished to me of the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 73:26; Esaias 26:19; Numbers 24:17"> which I am conscious of in myself, which mine eye has seen, and not another, but all have been fulfilled to me in my bosom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 19:22; Psalms 69:26; Kings III 14:13"> But if ye shall also say, What shall we say before him, and so find the root of the matter in him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:14; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Psalms 58:10–11"> Do ye also beware of deceit: for wrath will come upon transgressors; and then shall they know where their substance is.</span>
</div>
</div>
