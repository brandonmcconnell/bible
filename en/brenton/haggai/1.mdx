<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 2:2; Ezra and Nehemiah 3:8; Ezra and Nehemiah 3:2"> In the second year of Darius the king, in the sixth month, on the first day of the month, the word of the Lord came by the hand of the prophet Aggæus, saying, Speak to Zorobabel the son of Salathiel, of the tribe of Juda, and to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, Joshua.">a</a></sup>Jesus the son of Josedec, the high priest, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 4:23–2; Nehemiah 4:10; Ecclesiastes 11:4"> Thus saith the Lord Almighty, saying, This people say, The time is not come to build the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 5:1; Zacharias 1:1"> And the word of the Lord came by the hand of the prophet Aggæus, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings II 7:2; Psalms 132:3–5; Philippians 2:21"> Is it time for you to dwell in your ceiled houses, whereas our house is desolate?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40; 2 Corinthians 13:5; Aggæus 1:7"> And now thus saith the Lord Almighty; Consider your ways, I pray you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Aggæus 1:9; Deuteronomy 28:38–40; Osee 4:10"> Ye have sown much, but brought in little; ye have eaten, and are not satisfied; ye have drunk, and are not satisfied with drink, ye have clothed yourselves, and have not become warm<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. in them.">b</a></sup>thereby: and he that earns wages has gathered them into a bag full of holes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 119:59–60; Aggæus 1:5; Philippians 3:1"> Thus saith the Lord Almighty; Consider your ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 132:13–14; Chronicles II 7:16; Aggæus 2:7"> Go up to the mountain, and cut timber; build the house, and I will take pleasure in it, and be glorified, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Aggæus 1:4; Aggæus 2:16–17; Aggæus 1:6"> Ye looked for much, and there came little; and it was brought into the house, and I blew it away. Therefore thus saith the Lord Almighty, Because my house is desolate, and ye run every one into his own house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings III 8:35; Leviticus 26:19; Deuteronomy 28:23–24"> therefore shall the sky withhold dew, and the earth shall keep back her produce.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Aggæus 2:17; Deuteronomy 28:22; Kings IV 8:1"> And I will bring a sword upon the land, and upon the mountains, and upon the corn, and upon the wine, and upon the oil, and all that the earth produces, and upon the men, and upon the cattle, and upon all the labours of their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esaias 50:10; Ezra and Nehemiah 5:2; Aggæus 1:14"> And Zorobabel the son of Salathiel, of the tribe of Juda, and Jesus the son of Josedec, the high priest, and all the remnant of the people, hearkened to the voice of the Lord their God, and the words of the prophet Aggæus, according as the Lord their God had sent him to them, and the people feared before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Esaias 43:2; Matthew 28:20; Malachias 3:1"> And Aggæus the Lord's messenger spoke among the messengers of the Lord to the people, saying, I am with you, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:22; Hebrews 13:21; Aggæus 2:21"> And the Lord stirred up the spirit of Zorobabel the son of Salathiel, of the tribe of Juda, and the spirit of Jesus the son of Josedec, the high priest, and the spirit of the remnant of all the people; and they went in, and wrought in the house of the Lord Almighty their God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Aggæus 1:1; Aggæus 2:20; Aggæus 2:1"> on the four and twentieth day of the sixth month, in the second year of Darius the king.</span>
