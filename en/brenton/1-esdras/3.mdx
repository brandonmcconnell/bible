<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Now when Darius reigned, he made a great feast unto all his subjects, and unto all his household, and unto all the princes of Media and Persia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> and to all the governors and captains and lieutenants that were under him, from India unto Ethiopia, in the hundred twenty and seven provinces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And when they had eaten and drunken, and being satisfied were gone home, then Darius the king went into his bedchamber, and slept, and soon after awaked.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Then three young men, that were of the guard that kept the king's body, spake one to another;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Let every one of us speak a sentence: he that shall overcome, and whose sentence shall seem wiser than the others, unto him shall the king Darius give great gifts, and great things in token of victory:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> as to be clothed in purple, to drink in gold, and to sleep upon gold, and a chariot with bridles of gold, and an headtire of fine linen, and a chain about his neck:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and he shall sit next to Darius because of his wisdom, and shall be called Darius's cousin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And then every one wrote his sentence, sealed it, and laid it under the pillow of king Darius;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> and said that, when the king is risen, some will give him the writing; and of whose side the king and the three princes of Persia shall judge that his sentence is the wisest, to him shall the victory be given, as was appointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> The first wrote, Wine is the strongest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The second wrote, The king is strongest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The third wrote, Women are strongest: but above all things Truth beareth away the victory.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Now when the king was risen up, they took their writings, and delivered them unto him, and so he read them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and sending forth he called all the princes of Persia and Media, and the governors, and the captains, and the lieutenants, and the chief officers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and sat him down in the royal seat of judgment; and the writing was read before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And he said, Call the young men, and they shall declare their own sentences. So they were called, and came in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And he said unto them, Declare unto us your mind concerning the writings.<br />Then began the first, who had spoken of the strength of wine;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and he said thus, O ye men, how exceeding strong is wine! it causeth all men to err that drink it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> it maketh the mind of the king and of the fatherless child to be all one; of the bondman and of the freeman, of the poor man and of the rich:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> it turneth also every thought into jollity and mirth, so that a man remembereth neither sorrow nor debt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and it maketh every heart rich, so that a man remembereth neither king nor governor; and it maketh to speak all things by talents:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> and when they are in their cups, they forget their love both to friends and brethren, and a little after draw out swords:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> but when they are from the wine, they remember not what they have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> O ye men, is not wine the strongest, that enforceth to do thus? And when he had so spoken, he held his peace.</span>
</div>
