<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings III 19:8; Exodus 17:6; Psalms 78:70–72"> And Moses was feeding the flock of Jothor his father-in-law, the priest of Madiam; and he brought the sheep nigh to the wilderness, and came to the mount of Choreb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 7:30–35; Esaias 43:2; Mark 12:26"> And an angel of the Lord appeared to him in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. fire of flame.">a</a></sup>flaming fire out of the bush, and he sees that the bush burns with fire,—but the bush was not consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 7:31; Psalms 107:8; Job 37:14"> And Moses said, I will go near and see this great sight, why the bush is not consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:16; Kings I 3:6; Acts 9:4"> And when the Lord saw that he drew nigh to see, the Lord called him out of the bush, saying, Moses, Moses; and he said, What is it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 5:15; Acts 7:33; Exodus 19:12"> And he said, Draw not nigh hither: loose thy sandals from off thy feet, for the place whereon thou standest is holy ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 20:37; Matthew 22:32; Exodus 4:5"> And he said, I am the God of thy father, the God of Abraam, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob; and Moses turned away his face, for he was afraid to gaze at God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 145:19; Psalms 106:44; Exodus 2:23–25"> And the Lord said to Moses, I have surely seen the affliction of my people that is in Egypt, and I have heard their cry caused by their task-masters; for I know their affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 50:24; Genesis 15:18–21; Exodus 3:17"> And I have come down to deliver them out of the hand of the Egyptians, and to bring them out of that land, and to bring them into a good and wide land, into a land flowing with milk and honey, into the place of the Chananites, and the Chettites, and Amorites, and Pherezites, and Gergesites, and Evites, and Jebusites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 22:22–23; Exodus 2:23; Exodus 1:13–14"> And now, behold, the cry of the children of Israel is come to me, and I have seen the affliction with which the Egyptians afflict them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Michæas 6:4; Kings I 12:6; Psalms 105:26"> And now come, I will send thee to Pharao king of Egypt, and thou shalt bring out my people the children of Israel from the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings I 18:18; 2 Corinthians 3:5; Exodus 6:12"> And Moses said to God, Who am I, that I should go to Pharao king of Egypt, and that I should bring out the children of Israel from the land of Egypt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Romans 8:31; Esaias 41:10"> And God spoke to Moses, saying, I will be with thee, and this shall be the sign to thee that I shall send thee forth,—when thou bringest out my people out of Egypt, then ye shall serve God in this mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 30:4; Esaias 9:6; Jeremias 23:6"> And Moses said to God, Behold, I shall go forth to the children of Israel, and shall say to them, The God of our fathers has sent me to you; and they will ask me, What is his name? What shall I say to them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 8:58; Hebrews 13:8; Revelation 1:8"> And God spoke to Moses, saying, I am THE BEING; and he said, Thus shall ye say to the children of Israel, THE BEING has sent me to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 17:7–8; Hebrews 13:8; Malachias 3:6"> And God said again to Moses, Thus shalt thou say to the sons of Israel, The Lord God of our fathers, the God of Abraam, and God of Isaac, and God of Jacob, has sent me to you: this is my name for ever, and my memorial to generations of generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 8:4; Exodus 4:31; Exodus 4:29"> Go then and gather the elders of the children of Israel, and thou shalt say to them, The Lord God of our fathers has appeared to me, the God of Abraam, and God of Isaac, and God of Jacob, saying, I have surely looked upon you, and upon all the things which have happened to you in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 15:13–21; Genesis 50:24; Genesis 46:4"> And he said, I will bring you up out of the affliction of the Egyptians to the land of the Chananites and the Chettites, and Amorites and Pherezites, and Gergesites, and Evites, and Jebusites, to a land flowing with milk and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 9:1; Exodus 7:16; Exodus 10:24–26"> And they shall hearken to thy voice, and thou and the elders of Israel shall go in to Pharao king of Egypt, and thou shalt say to him, The God of the Hebrews has called us; we will go then a journey of three days into the wilderness, that we may sacrifice to our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 5:2; Exodus 6:1; Exodus 7:1–14"> But I know that Pharao king of Egypt will not let you go, save with a mighty hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 7:36; Nehemiah 9:10; Exodus 9:15"> and I will stretch out my hand, and smite the Egyptians with all my wonders, which I shall work among them, and after that he will send you forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 12:36; Psalms 105:37; Exodus 11:3"> And I will give this people favour in the sight of the Egyptians, and whenever ye shall escape, ye shall not depart empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jezekiel 39:10; Exodus 11:2; Exodus 12:35–36"> But every woman shall ask of her neighbour and fellow lodger, articles of gold and silver, and apparel; and ye shall put them upon your sons and upon your daughters,—and spoil ye the Egyptians.</span>
