<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 19:8; Kings II 12:6; Proverbs 6:31"> And if the thief be found in the breach made by himself and be smitten and die, there shall not be blood shed for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 24:43; Matthew 6:19–20; Numbers 35:27"> But if the sun be risen upon him, he is guilty, he shall die instead; and if<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. he.">a</a></sup>a thief have nothing, let him be sold in compensation for what he has stolen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 21:2; Esaias 50:1; Matthew 18:25"> And if the thing stolen be left and be in his hand alive, whether ox or sheep, he shall restore them two-fold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 22:7; Exodus 22:1; Exodus 22:9"> And if any one should feed down a field or a vineyard, and should send in his beast to feed down another field, he shall make compensation of his own field according to his produce; and if he shall have fed down the whole field, he shall pay for compensation the best of his own field and the best of his vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 22:3; Exodus 22:12; Job 20:18"> And if fire have gone forth and caught thorns, and should also set on fire threshing-floors or ears of corn or a field, he that kindled the fire shall make compensation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 22:9; Exodus 21:33–34; Kings II 14:30–31"> And if any one give to his neighbour money or goods to keep, and they be stolen out of the man's house, if the thief be found, he shall repay double.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 22:4; Proverbs 6:30–31; Jeremias 2:26"> But if the thief be not found, the master of the house shall come forward before God, and shall swear that surely he has not wrought wickedly in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. over.">b</a></sup>regard of any part of his neighbour's deposit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 22:28; Deuteronomy 16:18; Exodus 21:6"> according to every injury alleged, both concerning a calf, and an ass, and a sheep, and a garment, and every alleged loss, whatsoever in fact it may be,—the judgment of both shall proceed before God, and he that is convicted by God shall repay to his neighbour double.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:1; Exodus 22:4; Numbers 5:6–7"> And if any one give to his neighbour to keep a calf or sheep or any beast, and it be wounded or die or be taken, and no one know,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 12:48; 2 Timothy 1:12; Luke 16:11"> an oath of God shall be between both, each swearing that he has surely not at all been guilty in the matter of his neighbour's deposit; and so his master shall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. accept him.">c</a></sup>hold him guiltless, and he shall not make compensation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 6:16; Leviticus 5:1; Kings III 2:42–43"> And if it be stolen from him, he shall make compensation to the owner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 31:39; Exodus 22:7"> And if it be seized of beasts, he shall bring him to witness the prey, and he shall not make compensation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Naum 2:12; Michæas 5:8; Jezekiel 4:14"> And if any one borrow ought of his neighbour, and it be wounded or die or be carried away, and the owner of it be not with it, he shall make compensation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:19–20; Exodus 21:34; Deuteronomy 15:2"> But if the owner be with it, he shall not make compensation: but if it be a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Qu. If the borrower be a hireling, he shall have the ruined beast instead of his hire?">d</a></sup>hired thing, there shall be a compensation to him instead of his hire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zacharias 8:10"> And if any one deceive a virgin that is not betrothed, and lie with her, he shall surely endow her for a wife to himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:28–29; Genesis 34:2–4"> And if her father positively refuse, and will not consent to give her to him for a wife, he shall pay<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. money.">e</a></sup>compensation to her father according to the amount of the dowry of virgins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 34:12; Kings I 18:25; Deuteronomy 22:29"> Ye shall not save the lives of sorcerers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 20:27; Deuteronomy 18:10–11; Kings I 28:3"> Every one that lies with a beast ye shall surely put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 18:23; Deuteronomy 27:21; Leviticus 20:15–16"> He that sacrifices to any gods but to the Lord alone, shall be destroyed by death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:2–5; Numbers 25:7–8; Deuteronomy 18:20"> And ye shall not hurt a stranger, nor afflict him; for ye were strangers in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 23:9; Deuteronomy 10:19; Leviticus 19:33"> Ye shall hurt no widow or orphan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:17; Esaias 1:17; Esaias 1:23"> And if ye should afflict them by ill-treatment, and they should cry aloud to me, I will surely hear their voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 18:6; Psalms 10:17–18; Luke 18:7"> And I will be very angry, and will slay you with the sword, and your wives shall be widows and your children orphans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 109:9; Jeremias 15:8; Psalms 76:7"> And if thou shouldest lend money to thy poor brother who is by thee, thou shalt not be hard upon him, thou shalt not exact usury of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 15:5; Leviticus 25:35–37; Deuteronomy 23:19–20"> And if thou take thy neighbour's garment for a pledge, thou shalt restore it to him before sunset.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 20:16; Amos 2:8; Deuteronomy 24:6"> For this is his clothing, this is the only covering of his nakedness; wherein shall he sleep? If then he shall cry to me, I will hearken to him, for I am merciful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 34:6; Chronicles II 30:9; Psalms 136:10–11"> Thou shalt not revile the gods, nor speak ill of the ruler of thy people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 23:5; Ecclesiastes 10:20; Exodus 22:8–9"> Thou shalt not keep back the first-fruits of thy threshing floor and press. The first-born of thy sons thou shalt give to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 23:16; Exodus 13:2; James 1:18"> So shalt thou do with thy calf and thy sheep and thine ass; seven days shall it be under the mother, and the eighth day thou shalt give it to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 22:27; Deuteronomy 15:19; Exodus 13:12"> And ye shall be holy men to me; and ye shall not eat flesh taken of beasts, ye shall cast it to the dog.</span>
