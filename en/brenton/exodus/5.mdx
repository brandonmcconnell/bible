<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 10:9; Acts 4:29; Matthew 10:28"> And after this went in Moses and Aaron to Pharao, and they said to him, These things says the Lord God of Israel, Send my people away, that they may keep a feast to me in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 3:19; Job 21:15; Kings IV 18:35"> And Pharao said, Who is he that I should hearken to his voice, so that I should send away the children of Israel? I do not know the Lord, and I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. do not let, etc.">a</a></sup>will not let Israel go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 3:18; Zacharias 14:16–19; Jezekiel 6:11"> And they say to him, The God of the Hebrews has called us to him: we will go therefore a three days' journey into the wilderness, that we may sacrifice to the Lord our God, lest at any time death or slaughter happen to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 1:11; Acts 24:5; Amos 7:10"> And the king of Egypt said to them, Why do ye, Moses and Aaron, turn the people from their works? depart each of you to your works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 1:7–11; Proverbs 14:28"> And Pharao said, Behold now, the people is very numerous; let us not then give them rest from their work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 5:10; Exodus 5:19; Deuteronomy 16:18"> And Pharao gave orders to the task-masters of the people and the accountants, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 19:19; Genesis 24:25"> Ye shall no longer give straw to the people for brick-making as yesterday and the third day; but let them go themselves, and collect straw for themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 106:41"> And thou shalt impose on them daily the rate of brick-making which they perform: thou shalt not abate anything, for they are idle; therefore have they cried, saying, Let us arise and do sacrifice to our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings IV 18:20; Jeremias 43:2; Job 16:3"> Let the works of these men be made grievous, and let them care for these things, and not care for vain words.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 1:11; Exodus 3:7; Proverbs 29:12"> And the taskmasters and the accountants hastened them, and they spoke to the people, saying, Thus says Pharao, I will give you straw no longer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 5:13–14"> Go ye, yourselves, get for yourselves straw whencesoever ye can find it, for nothing is diminished from your rate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 15:7; Esaias 5:24; Esaias 47:14"> So the people were dispersed in all the land of Egypt, to gather stubble for straw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the taskmasters hastened them, saying, Fulfil your regular daily tasks, even as when straw was given you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esaias 10:24"> And the accountants of the race of the children of Israel, who were set over them by the masters of Pharao, were scourged, &#91;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="supplied from the Hebrew.">b</a></sup>and questioned,&#93; men saying, Why have ye not fulfilled your rates of brick-work as yesterday and the third day, to-day also?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the accountants of the children of Israel went in and cried to Pharao, saying, Why dost thou act thus to thy servants?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Straw is not given to thy servants, and they tell us to make brick; and behold thy servants have been scourged: thou wilt therefore injure thy people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 26:8; John 6:27; Exodus 5:8"> And he said to them, Ye are idle, ye are idlers: therefore ye say, Let us go and do sacrifice to our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jezekiel 18:18; Daniel 2:9–13"> Now then go and work, for straw shall not be given to you, yet ye shall return the rate of bricks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:8; Ecclesiastes 4:1; Deuteronomy 32:36"> And the accountants of the children of Israel saw themselves in an evil plight, men saying, Ye shall not fail<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. from the brick-making to deliver that which belongs to each day.">c</a></sup>to deliver the daily rate of the brick-making.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And they met Moses and Aaron coming forth to meet them, as they came forth from Pharao.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; Kings I 13:4; Chronicles I 19:6"> And they said to them, The Lord look upon you and judge you, for ye have made our savour abominable before Pharao, and before his servants, to put a sword into his hands to slay us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremias 20:7; Kings III 19:10; Numbers 11:14–15"> And Moses turned to the Lord, and said, I pray, Lord, why hast thou afflicted this people? and wherefore hast thou sent me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremias 11:21; Hebrews 10:36–37; Esaias 28:16"> For from the time that I went to Pharao to speak in thy name, he has afflicted this people, and thou hast not delivered thy people.</span>
