<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 12:1; Exodus 13:1"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 33:7–8; Jeremias 44:1; Exodus 13:17–18"> Speak to the children of Israel, and let them turn and encamp before the village, between Magdol and the sea, opposite Beel-sepphon: before them shalt thou encamp by the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 4:28; Kings I 23:23; Psalms 71:11"> And Pharao will say to his people, As for these children of Israel, they are wandering in the land, for the wilderness has shut them in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 9:22–23; Exodus 7:5; Romans 9:17"> And I will harden the heart of Pharao, and he shall pursue after them; and I will be glorified in Pharao, and in all his host, and all the Egyptians shall know that I am the Lord. And they did so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 105:25; Exodus 12:33; Luke 11:24–26"> And it was reported to the king of the Egyptians that the people had fled: and the heart of Pharao was turned, and that of his servants against the people; and they said, What is this that we have done, to let the children of Israel go, so that they should not serve us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> So Pharao yoked his chariots, and led off all his people with himself:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 15:4; Judges 4:3; Psalms 68:17"> having also taken six hundred chosen chariots, and all the cavalry of the Egyptians, and rulers over all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 13:17; Numbers 33:3; Deuteronomy 26:8"> And the Lord hardened the heart of Pharao king of Egypt, and of his servants, and he pursued after the children of Israel; and the children of Israel went forth with a high hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 15:9; Joshua 24:6; Exodus 14:2"> And the Egyptians pursued after them, and found them encamped by the sea; and all the cavalry and the chariots of Pharao, and the horsemen, and his host were before the village, over against Beel-sepphon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 24:7; Psalms 34:17; Nehemiah 9:9"> And Pharao approached, and the children of Israel having<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. having, etc. with their eyes.">a</a></sup>looked up, beheld, and the Egyptians encamped behind them: and they were very greatly terrified, and the children of Israel cried to the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 106:7–8; Numbers 14:1–4; Exodus 15:23–24"> and said to Moses, Because there were no graves in the land of Egypt, hast thou brought us forth to slay us in the wilderness? What is this that thou hast done to us, having brought us out of Egypt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jonas 4:3; Exodus 6:9; Jonas 4:8"> Is not this the word which we spoke to thee in Egypt, saying, Let us alone that we may serve the Egyptians? for it is better for us to serve the Egyptians than to die in this wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Chronicles II 20:17; Chronicles II 20:15; Exodus 14:30"> And Moses said to the people, Be of good courage: stand and see the salvation which is from the Lord, which he will work for us this day; for as ye have seen the Egyptians to-day, ye shall see them again no more for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:22; Deuteronomy 20:4; Chronicles II 20:17"> The Lord shall fight for you, and ye shall hold your peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 7:10; Ezra and Nehemiah 10:4–5; Nehemiah 9:9"> And the Lord said to Moses, Why criest thou to me? speak to the children of Israel, and let them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. harness or yoke the horses again.">b</a></sup>proceed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 7:19; Exodus 4:20; Exodus 4:2"> And do thou lift up<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, thy hand with thy rod; but another reading is τὴν ῥάβδον.">c</a></sup>thy rod, and stretch forth thy hand over the sea, and divide it, and let the children of Israel enter into the midst of the sea on the dry land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 14:8; Exodus 14:4; Jezekiel 34:11"> And lo! I will harden the heart of Pharao and of all the Egyptians, and they shall go in after them; and I will be glorified upon Pharao, and on all his host, and on his chariots and his horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 7:5; Exodus 14:4; Exodus 7:17"> And all the Egyptians shall know that I am the Lord, when I am glorified upon Pharao and upon his chariots and his horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esaias 63:9; Exodus 32:34; Exodus 23:20–21"> And the angel of God that went before the camp of the children of Israel removed and went behind, and the pillar of the cloud also removed from before them and stood behind them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Esaias 8:14; 2 Corinthians 2:15–16; Proverbs 4:18–19"> And it went between the camp of the Egyptians and the camp of Israel, and stood; and there was darkness and blackness; and the night passed, and they came not near to one another during the whole night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esaias 63:12; Exodus 15:8; Psalms 66:6"> And Moses stretched forth his hand over the sea, and the Lord carried back the sea with a strong south wind all the night, and made the sea dry, and the water was divided.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 11:29; Psalms 66:6; Exodus 15:19"> And the children of Israel went into the midst of the sea on the dry land, and the water of it was a wall on the right hand and a wall on the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 15:19; Exodus 14:17; Exodus 15:9"> And the Egyptians pursued them and went in after them, and every horse of Pharao, and his chariots, and his horsemen, into the midst of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 13:21; Psalms 77:16–19; Exodus 14:25"> And it came to pass in the morning watch that the Lord looked forth on the camp of the Egyptians through the pillar of fire and cloud, and troubled the camp of the Egyptians,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 14:14; Deuteronomy 3:22; Job 20:24"> and bound the axle-trees of their chariots, and caused them to go with difficulty; and the Egyptians said, Let us flee from the face of Israel, for the Lord fights for them against the Egyptians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 14:16; Exodus 8:5; James 2:13"> And the Lord said to Moses, Stretch forth thine hand over the sea, and let the water be turned back to its place, and let it cover the Egyptians coming both upon the chariots and the riders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 4:18; Psalms 78:53; Hebrews 11:29"> And Moses stretched forth his hand over the sea, and the water returned to its place toward day; and the Egyptians fled<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Lit. under.">d</a></sup>from the water, and the Lord shook off the Egyptians in the midst of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 78:53; Psalms 106:9–11; Ambacum 3:13"> And the water returned and covered the chariots and the riders, and all the forces of Pharao, who entered after them into the sea: and there was not left of them even one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 14:22; Psalms 77:19–20; Esaias 43:2"> But the children of Israel went along dry land in the midst of the sea, and the water was to them a wall on the right hand, and a wall on the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 106:8; Psalms 106:10; Psalms 92:9–11"> So the Lord delivered Israel in that day from the hand of the Egyptians, and Israel saw the Egyptians dead by the shore of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 2:11; John 11:45; John 2:23–25"> And Israel saw the mighty hand, the things which the Lord did to the Egyptians; and the people feared the Lord, and they believed God and Moses his servant.</span>
