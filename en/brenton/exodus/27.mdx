<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 38:1–7; Exodus 20:24–26; Exodus 40:10"> And thou shalt make an altar of incorruptible wood, of five cubits in the length, and five cubits in the breadth; the altar shall be square, and the height of it shall be of three cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 118:27; Exodus 29:12; Leviticus 4:7"> And thou shalt make the horns on the four corners; the horns shall be of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. of it.">a</a></sup>same piece, and thou shalt overlay them with brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings III 7:45; Numbers 4:14; Chronicles I 28:17"> And thou shalt make a rim for the altar; and its covering and its cups, and its flesh-hooks, and its fire-pan, and all its vessels shalt thou make of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 35:16; Exodus 25:12; Exodus 38:4–5"> And thou shalt make for it a brazen grate with net-work; and thou shalt make for the grate four brazen rings under the four sides.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 38:4"> And thou shalt put them below under the grate of the altar, and the grate shall extend to the middle of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 35:13–15; Numbers 4:44; Exodus 30:4"> And thou shalt make for the altar staves of incorruptible wood, and thou shalt overlay them with brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 25:28; Exodus 30:4; Numbers 4:13–14"> And thou shalt put the staves into the rings; and let the staves be on the sides of the altar to carry it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Exodus 25:9; Hebrews 8:5"> Thou shalt make it hollow with boards: according to what was shewed thee in the mount, so thou shalt make it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 38:9–20; Jezekiel 40:32; Jezekiel 42:3"> And thou shalt make a court for the tabernacle, curtains of the court of fine linen spun on the south side, the length of a hundred cubits for one side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 26:19–21; Jeremias 52:21; Exodus 36:38"> And their pillars twenty, and twenty brazen sockets<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. of.">b</a></sup>for them, and their rings and their clasps of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Thus shall there be to the side toward the north curtains of a hundred cubits in length; and their pillars twenty, and their sockets twenty of brass, and the rings and the clasps of the pillars, and their sockets overlaid with silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And in the breadth of the tabernacle toward the west curtains of fifty cubits, their pillars ten and their sockets ten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And in the breadth of the tabernacle toward the south, curtains of fifty cubits; their pillars ten, and their sockets ten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:9; Exodus 26:36"> And the height of the curtains shall be of fifteen cubits for the one side of the gate; their pillars three, and their sockets three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And for the second side the height of the curtains shall be of fifteen cubits; their pillars three, and their sockets three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 26:36; Judges 5:30; Exodus 28:39"> And a veil for the door of the court, the height of it of twenty cubits of blue linen, and of purple, and spun scarlet, and of fine linen spun with the art of the embroiderer; their pillars four, and their sockets four.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> All the pillars of the court round about overlaid with silver, and their chapiters silver and their brass sockets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And the length of the court shall be a hundred cubits on each side, and the breadth fifty on each side, and the height five cubits of fine linen spun, and their sockets of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 35:18; Esaias 22:23–25; Numbers 4:32"> And all the furniture and all the instruments and the pins of the court shall be of brass.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 24:1–4; Zacharias 4:11–14; Psalms 23:5"> And do thou charge the children of Israel, and let them take for thee refined pure olive-oil beaten to burn for light, that a lamp may burn continually</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 19:21; Numbers 18:23; Kings I 3:3"> in the tabernacle of the testimony, without the veil that is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. over.">c</a></sup>before the ark of the covenant, shall Aaron and his sons burn it from evening until morning, before the Lord: it is a perpetual ordinance<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. to.">d</a></sup>throughout your generations of the children of Israel.</span>
