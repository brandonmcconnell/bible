<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And he made the staves of the ark and of the table, and gilded them with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And he made the furniture of the table, both the dishes, and the censers, and the cups, and the bowls with which he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. will.">d</a></sup>should offer drink-offerings, of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And he made the candlestick which gives light, of gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:14"> the stem solid, and the branches from both its sides;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and blossoms proceeding from its branches, three on this side, and three on the other, made equal to each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And as to their lamps, which are on the ends,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. knops like walnuts.">e</a></sup>knops proceeded from them; and sockets proceeding from them, that the lamps might be upon them; and the seventh socket, on the top of the candlestick, on the summit above, entirely of solid gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And on<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. it.">f</a></sup>the candlestick seven golden lamps, and its snuffers gold, and its<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or, snuff-dishes; but the word seems to mean the instruments with which oil was poured into the lamp.">g</a></sup>funnels gold.</span>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 35:34; Exodus 31:6"> He made all the vessels of the altar and its<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or, firepan.">i</a></sup>grate, and its base, and its bowls, and the brazen flesh-hooks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 18:16; Numbers 3:47; Leviticus 27:25"> He made an appendage for the altar of network under the grate, beneath it as far as the middle of it; and he fastened to it four brazen rings on the four parts of the appendage of the altar, wide enough for the bars, so as to bear the altar with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 30:12"> He made the holy anointing oil and the composition of the incense, the pure work of the perfumer.</span>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 26:19; Exodus 26:25; Exodus 26:21"> And he made the laver, that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gr. of it.">j</a></sup>at it Moses and Aaron and his sons might wash their hands and their feet: when they went into the tabernacle of witness, or whensoever they should advance to the altar to do service, they washed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gr. of it.">k</a></sup>at it, as the Lord commanded Moses.</span>
