<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 28:3; Exodus 35:30–35; Exodus 31:1–6"> And Beseleel wrought, and Eliab and every one wise in understanding, to whom was given wisdom and knowledge, to understand to do all the works according to the holy offices, according to all things which the Lord appointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 4:17; Hebrews 5:4; Exodus 31:6"> And Moses called Beseleel and Eliab, and all that had wisdom, to whom God gave knowledge in their heart, and all who were freely willing to come forward to the works, to perform them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:29; Exodus 35:27; Exodus 35:5–21"> And they received from Moses all the offerings, which the children of Israel brought for all the works of the sanctuary to do them; and they continued to receive the gifts brought, from those who brought them in the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:10; Luke 12:42; Matthew 24:45"> And there came all the wise men who wrought the works of the sanctuary, each according to his own work, which they wrought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Chronicles II 31:6–10; 2 Corinthians 8:2–3; Chronicles II 24:14"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Another reading is εἶπαν, but there is occasionally confusion of number in LXX.; the singular being several times used for the plural.">a</a></sup>one said to Moses, The people bring an abundance too great in proportion to all the works which the Lord has appointed them to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And Moses commanded, and proclaimed in the camp, saying, Let neither man nor woman any longer labour for the offerings of the sanctuary; and the people were restrained from bringing any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Chronicles II 31:10"> And they had<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. works.">b</a></sup>materials sufficient for making the furniture, and they left some besides.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings III 6:23; Exodus 26:1–37; Jezekiel 1:5–28"> And every wise one among those that wrought made the robes of the holy places, which belong to Aaron the priest, as the Lord commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, they.">c</a></sup>he made the ephod of gold, and blue, and purple, and spun scarlet, and fine linen twined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:27; Exodus 26:3; Psalms 122:3"> And the plates were divided, the threads of gold, so as to interweave with the blue and purple, and with the spun scarlet, and the fine linen twined, they made it a woven work;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 26:4"> shoulder-pieces joined from both sides, a work woven by mutual twisting of the parts into<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. itself.">d</a></sup>one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 26:5; Exodus 26:10"> They made it of the same material according to the making of it, of gold, and blue, and purple, and spun scarlet, and fine linen twined, as the Lord commanded Moses;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4–5; Ephesians 2:20–22; 1 Corinthians 12:20"> and they made the two emerald stones clasped together and set in gold, graven and cut after the cutting of a seal with the names of the children of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 26:7–13"> and he put them on the shoulder-pieces of the ephod, as stones of memorial of the children of Israel, as the Lord appointed Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And they made the oracle, a work woven with embroidery, according to the work of the ephod, of gold, and blue, and purple, and spun scarlet, and fine linen twined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> They made the oracle square and double, the length of a span, and the breadth of a span,—double.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And there was interwoven with it a woven work of four rows of stones, a series of stones, the first row, a sardius and topaz and emerald;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and the second row, a carbuncle and sapphire and jasper;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 26:14"> and the third row, a ligure and agate and amethyst;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 25:5; Numbers 25:1; Exodus 40:18–19"> and the fourth row a chrysolite and beryl and onyx set round about with gold, and fastened with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And the stones were twelve according to the names of the children of Israel, graven according to their names<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. for seals.">e</a></sup>like seals, each according to his own name for the twelve tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And they made on the oracle turned wreaths, wreathen work, of pure gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and they made two golden circlets and two golden rings. And they put the two golden rings on both the upper corners of the oracle;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> and they put the golden wreaths on the rings on both sides of the oracle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> and the two wreaths into the two couplings. And they put them on the two circlets, and they put them on the shoulders of the ephod opposite each other in front.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And they made two golden rings, and put them on the two projections on the top of the oracle, and on the top of the hinder part of the ephod within.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 26:22; Exodus 26:27"> And they made two golden rings, and put them on both the shoulders of the ephod under it, in front by the coupling above the connexion of the ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And he fastened the oracle by the rings that were on it to the rings of the ephod, which were fastened with a string of blue, joined together with the woven work of the ephod; that the oracle should not be loosed from the ephod, as the Lord commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 4:32; Ephesians 3:18–19; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> And they made the tunic under the ephod, woven work, all of blue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 26:25"> And the opening of the tunic in the midst woven closely together, the opening having a fringe round about, that it might not be rent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 26:26–29; Exodus 25:28; Exodus 30:5"> And they made on the border of the tunic below pomegranates as of a flowering pomegranate tree, of blue, and purple, and spun scarlet, and fine linen twined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 26:26"> And they made golden bells, and put the bells on the border of the tunic round about between the pomegranates:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> a golden bell and a pomegranate on the border of the tunic round about, for the ministration, as the Lord commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And they made vestments of fine linen, a woven work, for Aaron and his sons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; Hebrews 10:20; Exodus 40:21"> and the tires of fine linen, and the mitre of fine linen, and the drawers of fine linen twined;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremias 1:18"> and their girdles of fine linen, and blue, and purple, and scarlet spun, the work of an embroiderer, according as the Lord commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 26:36–37; Exodus 40:28"> And they made the golden plate, a dedicated thing of the sanctuary, of pure gold; and he wrote upon it graven letters as of a seal, Holiness to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 27:10"> And they put it on the border of blue, so that it should be on the mitre above, as the Lord commanded Moses.</span>
