<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:22; Deuteronomy 5:4; Deuteronomy 4:36"> And the Lord spoke all these words, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:6; Osee 13:4; Leviticus 26:13"> I am the Lord thy God, who brought thee out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:7; Matthew 4:10; Psalms 81:9"> Thou shalt have no other gods beside me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:8; Leviticus 26:1; Deuteronomy 4:15–19"> Thou shalt not make to thyself an idol, nor likeness of anything, whatever things are in the heaven above, and whatever are in the earth beneath, and whatever are in the waters under the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:9; Numbers 14:18; Deuteronomy 4:24"> Thou shalt not bow down to them, nor serve them; for I am the Lord thy God, a jealous God, recompensing the sins of the fathers upon the children, to the third and fourth generation to them that hate me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:9; John 14:21; 1 John 5:3"> and bestowing mercy on them that love me to thousands of them, and on them that keep my commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:11; Leviticus 19:12; James 5:12"> Thou shalt not take the name of the Lord thy God in vain; for the Lord thy God will not acquit him that takes his name in vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 26:2; Leviticus 19:3; Exodus 31:13–16"> Remember the sabbath day to keep it holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 13:14; Exodus 34:21; Exodus 23:12"> Six days thou shalt labour, and shalt perform all thy work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:14–15; Exodus 34:21; Numbers 15:32–36"> But on the seventh day is the sabbath of the Lord thy God; on it thou shalt do no work, thou, nor thy son, nor thy daughter, thy servant nor thy maidservant, thine ox nor thine ass, nor any cattle of thine, nor the stranger that sojourns with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 1:1–2; Genesis 2:2–3; Exodus 31:17"> For in six days the Lord made the heaven and the earth, and the sea and all things in them, and rested on the seventh day; therefore the Lord blessed the seventh day, and hallowed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1–3; Colossians 3:20; Deuteronomy 5:16"> Honour thy father and thy mother, that it may be well with thee, and that thou mayest live long on the good land, which the Lord thy God gives to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Deuteronomy 5:17; Genesis 9:5–6"> Thou shalt not commit adultery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:4; Deuteronomy 5:18; Romans 7:2–3"> Thou shalt not steal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Ephesians 4:28; Leviticus 19:11"> Thou shalt not kill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 19:5; Matthew 19:18; Psalms 15:3"> Thou shalt not bear false witness against thy neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Luke 12:15; Hebrews 13:5"> Thou shalt not covet thy neighbour's wife; thou shalt not covet thy neighbour's house; nor his field, nor his servant, nor his maid, nor his ox, nor his ass, nor any of his cattle, nor whatever belongs to thy neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 19:16–18; Jeremias 23:23; Psalms 139:7–8"> And all the people perceived the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, saw the lightening, lit. saw the voice.">a</a></sup>thundering, and the flashes, and the voice of the trumpet, and the mountain smoking; and all the people feared and stood afar off,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:16; Acts 7:38; Galatians 3:19"> and said to Moses, Speak thou to us, and let not God speak to us, lest we die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:3; Proverbs 3:7; Kings I 12:20"> And Moses says to them, Be of good courage, for God is come to you to try you, that his fear may be among you, that ye sin not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings III 8:12; Psalms 97:2; Psalms 18:9"> And the people stood afar off, and Moses went into the darkness where God was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:13; Deuteronomy 4:36; Hebrews 12:25–26"> And the Lord said to Moses, Thus shalt thou say to the house of Jacob, and thou shalt report it to the children of Israel, Ye have seen that I have spoken to you from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jezekiel 20:39; Sophonias 1:5; Kings IV 17:33"> Ye shall not make to yourselves gods of silver, and gods of gold ye shall not make to yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5; Chronicles II 6:6; Psalms 132:13–14"> Ye shall make to me an altar of earth; and upon it ye shall sacrifice your whole burnt-offerings, and your peace-offerings, and your sheep and your calves in every place, where I shall record my name; and I will come to thee and bless thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 8:31; Deuteronomy 27:5–6"> And if thou wilt make to me an altar of stones, thou shalt not build them hewn stones; for thou hast lifted up thy tool upon them, and they are defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 1:16; Ecclesiastes 5:1; Leviticus 10:3"> Thou shalt not go up to my altar by steps, that thou mayest not uncover thy nakedness upon it.</span>
