<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 36:1; Exodus 35:30; John 3:27"> Behold, I have called by name Beseleel the son of Urias the son of Or, of the tribe of Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:31; Esaias 28:26; Kings III 7:14"> And I have filled him with a divine spirit of wisdom, and understanding, and knowledge, to invent in every work,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 26:1; Exodus 25:32–35; Chronicles II 2:13–14"> and to frame works, to labour in gold, and silver, and brass, and blue, and purple, and spun scarlet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 28:9–21"> and works in stone, and for artificers' work in wood, to work at all works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 35:10; Exodus 35:34–1; Exodus 28:3"> And I have<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. given.">a</a></sup>appointed him and Eliab the son of Achisamach of the tribe of Dan, and to every one understanding in heart I have given understanding; and they shall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, work in or at. One reading is ποιήσουσι.">b</a></sup>make all things as many as I have appointed thee,—</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 36:8–9; Exodus 25:10–22; Exodus 26:1–37"> the tabernacle of witness, and the ark of the covenant, and the propitiatory that is upon it, and the furniture of the tabernacle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 37:10–28; Exodus 30:1–10; Exodus 25:23–40"> and the altars, and the table and all its furniture,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 38:1–8; Exodus 27:1–8; Exodus 40:11"> and the pure candlestick and all its furniture, and the laver and its base,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 4:5–14; Exodus 28:1–43; Exodus 39:1–43"> and Aaron's robes of ministry, and the robes of his sons to minister to me as priests,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; Exodus 30:23–38"> and the anointing oil and the compound incense of the sanctuary; according to all that I have commanded thee shall they make them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jezekiel 20:12; Jezekiel 20:20; Leviticus 19:30"> Do thou also charge the children of Israel, saying, Take heed and keep my sabbaths; for they are a sign with me and among you throughout your generations, that ye may know that I am the Lord that sanctifies you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 35:2–3; Numbers 15:32–36; Jezekiel 20:21"> And ye shall keep the sabbaths, because this is holy to the Lord for you; he that profanes it shall surely be put to death: every one who shall do a work on it, that soul shall be destroyed from the midst of his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 2:2; Exodus 16:23; Leviticus 23:3"> Six days thou shalt do works, but the seventh day is the sabbath, a holy rest to the Lord; every one who shall do a work on the seventh day shall be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremias 50:5; Genesis 9:13; Genesis 17:11"> And the children of Israel shall keep the sabbaths, to observe them throughout their generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 1:31; Exodus 31:13; Genesis 2:2–3"> It is a perpetual covenant with me and the children of Israel, it is a perpetual sign with me; for in six days the Lord made the heaven and the earth, and on the seventh day he ceased, and rested.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:13; Exodus 32:15–16; Deuteronomy 5:22"> And he gave to Moses when he left off speaking to him in mount Sina the two tables of testimony, tables of stone written upon with the finger of God.</span>
</div>
