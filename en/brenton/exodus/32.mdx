<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 7:40; Deuteronomy 9:9; Exodus 24:18"> And when the people saw that Moses delayed to come down from the mountain, the people combined against Aaron, and said to him, Arise and make us gods who shall go before us; for this Moses, the man who brought us forth out of the land of Egypt—we do not know what is become of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 8:24–27; Exodus 12:35–36; Jezekiel 16:17"> And Aaron says to them, Take off the golden ear-rings which are in the ears of your wives and daughters, and bring them to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Esaias 46:6; Esaias 40:19–20; Jeremias 10:9"> And all the people took off the golden ear-rings that were in their ears, and brought them to Aaron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:18; Deuteronomy 9:16; Kings III 12:28"> And he received them at their hands, and formed them with a graving tool; and he made them a molten calf, and said, These are thy gods, O Israel, which have brought thee up out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings IV 10:20; Leviticus 23:2; Leviticus 23:37"> And Aaron having seen it built an altar before it, and Aaron made proclamation saying, To-morrow is a feast of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:7; Numbers 25:2; Acts 7:41–42"> And having risen early on the morrow, he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. Set upon the altar.">a</a></sup>offered whole burnt-offerings, and offered a peace-offering; and the people sat down to eat and drink, and rose up to play.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 32:11; Osee 9:9; Judges 2:19"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying, Go quickly, descend hence, for thy people whom thou broughtest out of the land of Egypt have transgressed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings III 12:28; Exodus 20:23; Exodus 20:3–4"> they have quickly gone out of the way which thou commandedst; they have made for themselves a calf, and worshipped it, and sacrificed to it, and said, These are thy gods, O Israel, who brought thee up out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:14; Numbers 14:12; Deuteronomy 9:19"> And now let me alone, and I will be very angry with them and consume them, and I will make thee a great nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:18–20; Deuteronomy 9:26–29; Psalms 106:23"> And Moses prayed before the Lord God, and said, Wherefore, O Lord, art thou very angry with thy people, whom thou broughtest out of the land of Egypt with great strength, and with thy high arm?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 14:13–16; Deuteronomy 9:28; Joshua 7:9"> Take heed lest at any time the Egyptians speak, saying, With evil intent he brought them out to slay them in the mountains, and to consume them from off the earth; cease from thy wrathful anger, and be merciful to the sin of thy people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 6:13; Genesis 12:7; Genesis 22:16"> remembering Abraam and Isaac and Jacob thy servants, to whom thou hast sworn by thyself, and hast spoken to them, saying, I will greatly multiply your seed as the stars of heaven for multitude, and all this land which thou spokest of to give to them, so that they shall possess it for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jonas 3:10; Psalms 106:45; Jeremias 26:13"> And the Lord was<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. dost.">b</a></sup>prevailed upon to preserve his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:15; Exodus 40:20; Deuteronomy 5:22"> And Moses turned and went down from the mountain, and the two tables of testimony were in his hands, tables of stone written on both their sides: they were written within and without.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 31:18; Hebrews 8:10; Deuteronomy 10:1"> And the tables were the work of God, and the writing the writing of God written on the tables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 24:13; Exodus 17:9; Joshua 6:20"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. Jesus.">c</a></sup>Joshua having heard the voice of the people crying, says to Moses, There is a noise of war in the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 15:1–18; Daniel 5:4; Daniel 5:23"> And Moses says, It is not the voice of them that begin the battle, nor the voice of them that begin the cry of defeat, but the voice of them that begin the banquet of wine do I hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:16–17; Jeremias 31:32; Lamentations 5:15"> And when he drew nigh to the camp, he sees the calf and the dances; and Moses being very angry cast the two tables out of his hands, and broke them to pieces under the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:21; Kings IV 23:6; Kings IV 23:15"> And having taken the calf which they made, he consumed it with fire, and ground it very small, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. sowed it under the water, but another reading is ἐπὶ.">d</a></sup>scattered it on the water, and made the children of Israel to drink it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 20:9; Joshua 7:19–26; Kings III 21:22"> And Moses said to Aaron, What has this people done to thee, that thou hast brought upon them a great sin?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:24; Exodus 15:24; Exodus 16:20"> And Aaron said to Moses, Be not angry, my lord, for thou knowest the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. impulse.">e</a></sup>impetuosity of this people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 32:1–4; Exodus 32:8"> For they say to me, Make us gods, which shall go before us; for as for this man Moses, who brought us out of Egypt, we do not know what is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Lit. done to him.">f</a></sup>become of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 32:4; Luke 10:29; Romans 3:10"> And I said to them, If any one has golden ornaments, take them off; and they gave them me, and I cast them into the fire, and there came out this calf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Esaias 47:3; Michæas 1:11; Revelation 16:15"> And when Moses saw that the people was scattered,—for Aaron had scattered them so as to be a rejoicing to their enemies,—</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Kings IV 9:32; Matthew 12:30; Kings II 20:11"> then stood Moses at the gate of the camp, and said, Who is on the Lord's side? let him come to me. Then all the sons of Levi came to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 25:5; Luke 14:26; Deuteronomy 33:8–9"> And he says to them, Thus saith the Lord God of Israel, Put every one his sword on his thigh, and go through and return from gate to gate through the camp, and slay every one his brother, and every one his neighbour, and every one him that is nearest to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:9; Malachias 2:4–6; 1 Corinthians 10:8"> And the sons of Levi did as Moses spoke to them, and there fell of the people in that day to the number of three thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 25:11–13; Matthew 10:37; Zacharias 13:3"> And Moses said to them, Ye have filled your hands this day to the Lord each one on his son or on his brother, so that blessing should be given to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Kings I 12:20; Numbers 25:13; Kings IV 17:21"> And it came to pass after the morrow had begun, that Moses said to the people, Ye have sinned a great sin; and now I will go up to God, that I may make atonement for your sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 20:23; Daniel 9:11; Daniel 9:8"> And Moses returned to the Lord and said, I pray, O Lord, this people has sinned a great sin, and they have made for themselves golden gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 69:28; Romans 9:3; Revelation 21:27"> And now if thou<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. dost.">g</a></sup>wilt forgive their sin, forgive it; and if not, blot me out of thy book, which thou hast written.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:20; Jezekiel 18:4; Revelation 20:12"> And the Lord said to Moses, If any one has sinned against me, I will blot them out of my book.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 23:20; Deuteronomy 32:35; Matthew 23:35"> And now go, descend, and lead this people into the place of which I spoke to thee: behold, my angel shall go before thy face; and in the day when I shall visit I will bring upon them their sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 7:41; Exodus 32:25; Kings II 12:9–10"> And the Lord smote the people for the making of the calf, which Aaron made.</span>
