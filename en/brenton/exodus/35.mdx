<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 34:32; Exodus 31:1–11; Romans 2:13"> And Moses gathered all the congregation of the children of Israel together, and said, These are the words which the Lord has spoken for you to do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 23:3; Luke 13:14–15; Exodus 20:9–10"> Six days shalt thou perform works, but on the seventh day shall be rest—a holy sabbath—a rest for the Lord: every one that does work on it, let him die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 16:23; Numbers 15:32–36; Exodus 12:16"> Ye shall not burn a fire in any of your dwellings on the sabbath-day; I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 25:1–2"> And Moses spoke to all the congregation of the children of Israel, saying, This is the thing which the Lord has appointed you, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Psalms 110:3; Exodus 25:2–7"> Take of yourselves an offering for the Lord: every one that engages in his heart<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. they shall.">a</a></sup>shall bring the first-fruits to the Lord; gold, silver, brass,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 26:7–14; Exodus 26:31; Exodus 28:15"> blue, purple, double scarlet spun, and fine linen spun, and goats' hair,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and rams' skins dyed red, and skins dyed blue, and incorruptible wood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 39:6–14; Exodus 28:9; Exodus 25:5"> and sardine stones, and stones for engraving for the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, ephod.">b</a></sup>shoulder-piece and full-length robe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 31:1–6; Exodus 36:1–4"> And every man that is wise in heart among you, let him come and work all things whatsoever the Lord has commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 26:1–37; Exodus 36:8–34; Exodus 31:7–9"> The tabernacle, and the cords, and the coverings, and the rings, and the bars, and the posts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 25:10–22; Exodus 26:31–33; Exodus 37:1–9"> and the ark of the testimony, and its staves, and its propitiatory, and the veil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 25:10–22; Exodus 26:31–33; Exodus 37:1–9"> and the curtains of the court, and its posts, and the emerald stones, and the incense, and the anointing oil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 25:23–30; Leviticus 24:5–6; Exodus 37:10–16"> and the table and all its furniture,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–39; Matthew 5:14–15; Exodus 37:17–24"> and the candlestick for the light and all its furniture,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 27:1–8; Exodus 30:18–21; Exodus 38:1–8"> and the altar and all its furniture;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 31:10; Numbers 4:5–15; Exodus 28:1–43"> and the holy garments of Aaron the priest, and the garments in which they shall do service; and the garments of priesthood for the sons of Aaron and the anointing oil, and the compound incense.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And all the congregation of the children of Israel went out from Moses. And they brought, they whose heart prompted them, and they to whomsoever it seemed good in their mind, each an offering:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 25:2; Exodus 35:26; Chronicles I 29:14"> and they brought an offering to the Lord for all the works of the tabernacle of witness, and all its services, and for all the robes of the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:70–72; Matthew 2:11; Ezra and Nehemiah 2:68–69"> And the men, even every one to whom it seemed good in his heart, brought from the women, even brought seals and ear-rings, and finger-rings, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, chains.">c</a></sup>necklaces, and bracelets, every article of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Chronicles I 29:8; Exodus 25:2–7; Exodus 35:6–10"> And all as many as brought ornaments of gold to the Lord, and with whomsoever fine linen was found; and they brought skins dyed blue, and rams' skins dyed red.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12"> And every one that offered an offering<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. they brought.">d</a></sup>brought silver and brass, the offerings to the Lord; and they with whom was found incorruptible wood; and they brought offerings for all the works of the preparation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 16:12; Luke 8:2–3; Proverbs 14:1"> And every woman skilled in her heart to spin with her hands,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. they brought.">e</a></sup>brought spun articles, the blue, and purple, and scarlet and fine linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 35:21; Exodus 36:8; Exodus 35:29"> And all the women to whom it seemed good in their heart in their wisdom, spun the goats' hair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 2:68; Chronicles I 29:6; Exodus 35:9"> And the rulers brought the emerald stones, and the stones for setting in the ephod, and the oracle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 30:23–38; Exodus 35:8"> and the compounds both for the anointing oil, and the composition of the incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Exodus 35:21–22; Exodus 35:4"> And every man and woman whose mind inclined them to come in and do all the works as many as the Lord appointed them to do by Moses—they the children of Israel brought an offering to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 31:1–6; James 1:17; 1 Corinthians 12:11"> And Moses said to the children of Israel, Behold, God has called by name Beseleel the son of Urias the son of Or, of the tribe of Juda,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 1:17; Esaias 11:2–5; Esaias 61:1–3"> and has filled him with a divine spirit of wisdom and understanding, and knowledge of all things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> to labour<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="i. e. as a master workman.">f</a></sup>skilfully in all works of cunning workmanship, to form the gold and the silver and the brass,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> and to work in stone, and to fashion the wood, and to work in every work of wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 31:6; Chronicles II 2:14; Ezra and Nehemiah 7:27"> And God gave improvement in understanding both to him, and to Eliab the son of Achisamach of the tribe of Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Exodus 35:31; Kings III 7:14; Esaias 28:26"> And God filled them with wisdom, understanding and perception, to understand to work all the works of the sanctuary, and to weave the woven and embroidered work with scarlet and fine linen, to do all work of curious workmanship and embroidery.</span>
</div>
