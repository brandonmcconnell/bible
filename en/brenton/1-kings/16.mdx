<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles II 20:34; Chronicles II 19:2; Kings III 16:7"> And the word of the Lord came by the hand of Ju son of Anani to Baasa, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings III 15:34; Kings III 14:7; Kings I 2:8"> Forasmuch as I lifted thee up from the earth, and made thee ruler over my people Israel; and thou hast walked in the way of Jeroboam, and hast caused my people Israel to sin, to provoke me with their vanities;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings III 14:10; Kings III 15:29–30; Esaias 66:24"> Behold, I raise up enemies after Baasa, and after his house; and I will<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. give.">a</a></sup>make thy house as the house of Jeroboam son of Nabat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings III 14:11; Kings III 21:24"> Him that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. has died.">b</a></sup>dies of Baasa in the city the dogs shall devour, and him that dies of his in the field the birds of the sky shall devour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings III 14:19; Kings III 15:31; Chronicles II 16:1–6"> Now the rest of the history of Baasa, and all that he did, and his mighty acts, behold, are not these written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings III 14:17; Kings III 15:21; Kings III 16:13–14"> And Baasa slept with his fathers, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. he is buried.">c</a></sup>they bury him in Thersa; and Ela his son reigns in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings III 14:14; Osee 1:4; Psalms 115:4"> And the Lord spoke by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. the hand of Ju.">d</a></sup>Ju the son of Anani against Baasa, and against his house, even all the evil which he wrought before the Lord to provoke him to anger by the works of his hands, in being like the house of Jeroboam; and because he smote him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Heb. and Alex. 'in the 26th year of Asa king of Juda, Ela,' etc.">e</a></sup>And Ela son of Baasa reigned over Israel two years in Thersa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings III 18:3; Genesis 39:4; Daniel 5:1–4"> And Zambri, captain of half his cavalry, conspired against him, while he was in Thersa, drinking himself drunk in the house of Osa the steward at Thersa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings IV 9:31"> And Zambri went in and smote him and slew him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Heb. and Alex. insert 'in the 27th year of Asa king of Juda.'">f</a></sup>and reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings I 25:22; Kings III 16:3; Kings III 15:29"> And it came to pass when he reigned, when he sat upon his throne,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings III 16:1; Kings III 16:7; Chronicles II 10:15"> that he smote all the house of Baasa, according to the word which the Lord spoke against the house of Baasa, and to Ju the prophet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Kings III 15:30; Esaias 41:29"> for all the sins of Baasa and Ela his son, as he led Israel astray to sin, to provoke the Lord God of Israel with their vanities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And the rest of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. words.">g</a></sup>deeds of Ela which he did, behold, are not these written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings III 15:27; Joshua 19:44; Job 20:5"> And Zambri reigned in Thersa seven days: and the army of Israel was encamped against Gabathon of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Chronicles II 22:2; Kings IV 8:26; Michæas 6:16"> And the people heard in the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or, camp.">h</a></sup>army, saying, Zambri has conspired and smitten the king: and the people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. in Israel.">i</a></sup>of Israel made Ambri the captain of the host king in that day in the camp over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 9:50; Luke 19:43–44; Kings IV 18:9–12"> And Ambri went up, and all Israel with him, out of Gabathon; and they besieged Thersa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings II 17:23; Kings I 31:4–5; Job 2:9–10"> And it came to pass when Zambri saw that his city was<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or, surprised.">j</a></sup>taken, that he goes into the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gr. the cave of the house.">k</a></sup>inner chamber of the house of the king, and burnt the king's house over him, and died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings III 15:26; Kings III 14:16; Kings III 12:28"> Because of his sins which he committed, doing that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, so as to walk in the way of Jeroboam the son of Nabat, and in his sins wherein he caused Israel to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings III 16:14; Kings III 16:5; Kings III 16:27"> And the rest of the history of Zambri, and his conspiracies wherein he conspired, behold, are not these written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 12:25; Ephesians 4:3–5; Proverbs 28:2"> Then the people of Israel divides; half the people goes after Thamni the son of Gonath to make him king; and half the people goes after Ambri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The people that followed Ambri overpowered the people that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Gr. was after.">l</a></sup>followed Thamni son of Gonath: and Thamni died and Joram his brother at that time, and Ambri reigned after Thamni.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings III 16:8; Kings III 16:29; Chronicles II 22:2"> In the thirty-first year of king Asa, Ambri<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Gr. reigns.">m</a></sup>begins to reign over Israel twelve years: he reigns six years in Thersa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Kings III 13:32; Kings III 16:32; Acts 8:5–8"> And Ambri bought the mount Semeron of Semer the lord of the mountain for two talents of silver; and he built upon the mountain, and they called the name of the mountain on which he built, after the name of Semer the lord of the mount, Semeron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Michæas 6:16; Kings III 16:30–31; Kings III 14:9"> And Ambri did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, and wrought wickedly beyond all that were before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Kings III 16:19; Kings III 16:13; Kings III 12:26–33"> And he walked in all the way of Jeroboam the son of Nabat, and in his sins wherewith he caused Israel to sin, to provoke the Lord God of Israel by their vanities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Kings III 16:20; Kings III 16:14; Kings III 16:5"> And the rest of the acts of Ambri, and all that he did, and all his might, behold, are not these things written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Ambri slept with his fathers, and is buried in Samaria; and Achaab his son reigns in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And in the eleventh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="a The word ἔτει is redundant.">n</a></sup>year of Ambri Josaphat the son of Asa reigns, being thirty-five years old<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="a Gr. in his kingdom.">o</a></sup>in the beginning of his reign, and he reigned twenty-five years in Jerusalem: and his mother's name was Gazuba, daughter of Seli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And he walked in the way of Asa his father, and turned not from it, even from doing right in the eyes of the Lord: only they removed not any of the high places; they sacrificed and burnt incense on the high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Now the engagements which Josaphat made with the king of Israel, and all his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="c Gr. might.">p</a></sup>mighty deeds which he performed, and the enemies whom he fought against, behold, are not these written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Juda?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and the remains of the prostitution<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="d See 1 Kings 22. 46-50. A. V.">q</a></sup>which they practised in the days of Asa his father, he removed out of the land:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and there was no king in Syria, but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="e Heb. כצב praefectus.">r</a></sup>a deputy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And king Josaphat made a ship<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="f Or, for.">s</a></sup>at Tharsis to go to Sophir for gold; but it went not, for the ship was broken at Gasion Gaber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Then the king of Israel said to Josaphat,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="g Or, let me.">t</a></sup>I will send forth thy servants and my servants in the ship: but Josaphat would not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Josaphat slept with his fathers, and is buried with his fathers in the city of David: and Joram his son reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> In the second year of Josaphat king of Juda, Achaab son of Ambri reigned over Israel in Samaria twenty-two years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Kings III 16:25; Kings III 21:25; Kings III 14:9"> And Achaab did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, and did more wickedly than all that were before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Kings IV 17:16; Kings IV 10:18; Judges 18:7"> And it was not enough for him to walk in the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nabat, but he took to wife Jezabel the daughter of Jethebaal king of the Sidonians; and he went and served Baal, and worshipped him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Kings IV 10:21; Kings IV 10:26–27"> And he set up an altar to Baal in the house of his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or, provocations, etc.">u</a></sup>abominations, which he built in Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Kings IV 13:6; Kings III 21:25; Kings IV 21:3"> And Achaab made a grove; and Achaab did yet more abominably, to provoke the Lord God of Israel, and to sin against his own life so that he should be destroyed: he did evil above all the kings of Israel that were before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Joshua 6:26; Joshua 23:14–15; Matthew 24:35"> And in his days Achiel the Bæthelite built Jericho: he laid the foundation of it in Abiron his first-born, and he set up the doors of it in Segub his younger son, according to the word of the Lord which he spoke by Joshua the son of Naue.</span>
