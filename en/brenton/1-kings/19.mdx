<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings III 18:40; Kings III 21:25; Kings III 21:5–7"> And Achaab told Jezabel his wife all that Eliu had done, and how he had slain the prophets with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ruth 1:17; Kings IV 6:31; Kings III 20:10"> And Jezabel sent to Eliu, and said, If thou art Eliu and I am Jezabel, God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Lit. do these things to me, and add these things.">a</a></sup>do so to me, and more also, if I do not make thy life by this time to-morrow as the life of one of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 21:31; Matthew 26:70–74; Matthew 26:56"> And Eliu feared, and rose, and departed for his life: and he comes to Bersabee to the land of Juda, and he left his servant there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jonas 4:3; Numbers 11:15; Jonas 4:8"> And he himself went a day's journey in the wilderness, and came and sat under a juniper tree; and asked concerning his life that he might die, and said, Let it be enough now, O Lord, take, I pray thee, my life from me; for I am no better than my fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 34:7; Acts 12:7; Hebrews 13:5"> And he lay down and slept there under a tree; and behold, some one touched him, and said to him, Arise and eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings III 17:6; Matthew 4:11; Matthew 6:32"> And Eliu looked, and, behold, at his head there was a cake of meal and a cruse of water; and he arose, and ate and drank, and returned and lay down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 103:13–14; Deuteronomy 33:25"> And the angel of the Lord returned again, and touched him, and said to him, Arise, and eat, for the journey is far from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 34:28; Exodus 24:18; Exodus 3:1"> And he arose, and ate and drank, and went in the strength of that meat forty days and forty nights to mount Choreb.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings III 19:13; Exodus 33:21–22; Jeremias 2:18"> And he entered there into a cave, and rested there; and, behold, the word of the Lord came to him, and he said, What doest thou here, Eliu?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings III 18:4; Kings III 18:22; Exodus 34:14"> And Eliu said, I have been very jealous for the Lord Almighty, because the children of Israel have forsaken thee: they have digged down thine altars, and have slain thy prophets with the sword; and I only am left alone, and they seek my life to take it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jezekiel 1:4; Psalms 50:3; Job 38:1"> And he said, Thou shalt go forth to-morrow, and shalt stand before the Lord in the mount; behold, the Lord will pass by. And, behold, a great and strong wind rending the mountains, and crushing the rocks before the Lord; but the Lord was not in the wind; and after the wind an earthquake; but the Lord was not in the earthquake:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 4:16; Zacharias 4:6; Deuteronomy 4:33"> and after the earthquake a fire; but the Lord was not in the fire: and after the fire the voice of a gentle breeze.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings III 19:9; Esaias 6:2; Genesis 16:8"> And it came to pass when Eliu heard, that he wrapt his face in his mantle, and went forth and stood in the cave: and, behold, a voice came to him and said, What doest thou here, Eliu?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings III 19:9–10; Jeremias 22:9; Esaias 62:1"> And Eliu said, I have been very jealous for the Lord Almighty; for the children of Israel have forsaken thy covenant, and they have overthrown thine altars, and have slain thy prophets with the sword! and I am left entirely alone, and they seek my life to take it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esaias 45:1; Jeremias 1:10; Kings IV 8:7–15"> And the Lord said to him, Go, return, and thou shalt come into the way of the wilderness of Damascus: and thou shalt go and anoint Azael to be king over Syria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings IV 9:1–14; Kings III 19:19–21; Kings IV 2:9"> And Ju the son of Namessi shalt thou anoint to be king over Israel; and Elisaie the son of Saphat shalt thou anoint to be prophet in thy room.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings IV 13:22; Kings IV 8:12; Kings IV 13:3"> And it shall come to pass, that him that escapes from the sword of Azael, Ju shall slay; and him that escapes from the sword of Ju, Elisaie shall slay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 11:4–5; Esaias 1:9; Esaias 10:20–22"> And thou shalt leave in Israel seven thousand men, all the knees which had not bowed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. the knee.">b</a></sup>themselves to Baal, and every mouth which had not worshipped him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings IV 2:8; Kings IV 2:13–14; Kings I 28:14"> And he departed thence, and finds Elisaie the son of Saphat, and he was ploughing with oxen; there were twelve yoke before him, and he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. in.">c</a></sup>with the twelve, and he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. departed.">d</a></sup>passed by to him, and cast his mantle upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 8:21–22; Luke 9:61–62; Acts 20:37"> And Elisaie left the cattle, and ran after Eliu and said, I will kiss my father, and follow after thee. And Eliu said, Return, for I have done a work for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings II 24:22; 2 Timothy 4:11; Philemon 1:13"> And he returned<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. from behind him.">e</a></sup>from following him, and took<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. the yokes.">f</a></sup>a yoke of oxen, and slew them, and boiled them with the instruments of the oxen, and gave to the people, and they ate: and he arose, and went after Eliu, and ministered to him.</span>
</div>
