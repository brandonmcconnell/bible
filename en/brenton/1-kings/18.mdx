<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 4:25; James 5:17; Deuteronomy 28:12"> And it came to pass after many days, that the word of the Lord came to Eliu in the third year, saying, Go, and appear before Achaab, and I will bring rain upon the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings IV 6:25; Proverbs 28:1; Leviticus 26:26"> And Eliu went to appear before Achaab: and the famine was severe in Samaria.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:2; Kings III 16:9; Matthew 10:28"> And Achaab called Abdiu the steward. Now Abdiu feared the Lord greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings III 18:13; Matthew 10:40–42; Revelation 17:4–6"> And it came to pass when Jezabel smote the prophets of the Lord, that Abdiu took a hundred prophets, and hid them by fifty in a cave, and fed them with bread and water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 14:5–6; Joel 1:18; Joel 2:22"> And Achaab said to Abdiu, Come, and let us go through the land, and to the fountains of water, and to the brooks, if by any means we may find grass, and may save the horses and mules, and so they will not perish from the tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremias 14:3"> And they made a division of the way between them to pass through it: Achaab went one way, and Abdiu went by another way alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings IV 1:6–8; Genesis 44:20; Esaias 60:14"> And Abdiu was alone in the way; and Eliu came alone to meet him: and Abdiu hasted, and fell upon his face, and said, My lord Eliu,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. if thou art, etc.">a</a></sup>art thou indeed he?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 13:7; Kings III 18:3; 1 Peter 2:17–18"> And Eliu said to him, I am: go, say to thy master, Behold, Eliu is here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 5:21; Kings III 18:12; Kings III 17:18"> And Abdiu said, What sin have I committed, that thou givest thy servant into the hand of Achaab to slay me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings III 17:1; Kings III 1:29; Kings III 18:15"> As the Lord thy God lives, there is not a nation or kingdom, whither my lord has not sent to seek thee; and if they said, He is not here, then has he set fire to the kingdom and its territories, because he has not found thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings III 18:8; Kings III 18:14"> And now thou sayest, Go, tell thy lord, Behold, Eliu is here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings IV 2:16; Acts 8:39; Jezekiel 8:3"> And it shall come to pass when I shall have departed from thee, that the Spirit of the Lord shall carry thee to a land which I know not, and I shall go in to tell the matter to Achaab, and he will not find thee and will slay me: yet thy servant fears the Lord from his youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings III 18:4; Matthew 25:35; Psalms 18:21–24"> Has it not been told to thee my lord, what I did when Jezabel slew the prophets of the Lord, that I hid a hundred men of the prophets of the Lord, by fifty in a cave, and fed them with bread and water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 10:28"> And now thou sayest to me, Go, say to thy master, Behold, Eliu is here: and he shall slay me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings III 17:1; Kings III 18:10; Luke 2:13–14"> And Eliu said, As the Lord of Hosts before whom I stand lives, to-day I will appear before him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Abdiu went to meet Achaab, and told him: and Achaab<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. ran forth.">b</a></sup>hasted forth, and went to meet Eliu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 7:25; Kings III 21:20; Acts 16:20"> And it came to pass when Achaab saw Eliu, that Achaab said to Eliu, Art thou he that perverts Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Chronicles II 15:2; Kings III 9:9; Jeremias 2:19"> And Eliu said, I do not pervert Israel; but it is thou and thy father's house, in that ye forsake the Lord your God, and thou hast gone after Baalim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 19:26; Kings III 16:33; 2 Peter 2:1"> And now send, gather to me all Israel to mount Carmel, and the prophets of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Heb. Baal. See Jer. 11. 13; Hos. 9. 10.">c</a></sup>shame four hundred and fifty, and the prophets of the groves four hundred, that eat at Jezabel's table.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings III 22:9"> And Achaab sent to all Israel, and gathered all the prophets to mount Carmel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 6:24; Joshua 24:15; 1 Corinthians 10:21–22"> And Eliu drew near to them all: and Eliu said to them, How long wilt ye halt on both<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. the hams, from γόνν, the knee.">d</a></sup>feet? if the Lord be God, follow him; but if Baal, follow him. And the people answered not a word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings III 19:10; Kings III 19:14; 2 Timothy 4:3–4"> And Eliu said to the people, I am left, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or, quite alone.">e</a></sup>only one prophet of the Lord; and the prophets of Baal are four hundred and fifty men, and the prophets of the groves four hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Let them give us two oxen, and let them choose one for themselves, and cut it in pieces, and lay it on the wood, and put no fire on the wood: and I will dress the other bullock, and put on no fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Kings III 18:38; Chronicles I 21:26; Chronicles II 7:1"> And do ye call loudly on the name of your gods, and I will call on the name of the Lord my God, and it shall come to pass that the God who shall answer by fire, he is God. And all the people answered and said, The word which thou hast spoken is good.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And Eliu said to the prophets of shame, Choose to yourselves one calf, and dress it first, for ye are many; and call ye on the name of your god; but apply no fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremias 10:5; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Matthew 6:7"> And they took the calf and drest it, and called on the name of Baal from morning till noon, and said, Hear us, O Baal, hear us. And there was no voice, neither was there hearing, and they ran up and down on the altar which they had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 78:65–66; Esaias 41:23; Ecclesiastes 11:9"> And it was noon, and Eliu the Thesbite mocked them, and said, Call with a loud voice, for he is a god; for he is meditating, or else perhaps he is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or, preparing an answer.">f</a></sup>engaged in business, or perhaps he is asleep, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. will awake or arise.">g</a></sup>is to be awaked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 19:28; Deuteronomy 14:1; Mark 5:5"> And they cried with a loud voice, and cut themselves according to their custom with knives and lancets until the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. shedding of blood upon them.">h</a></sup>blood gushed out upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Kings III 18:26; Kings I 18:10; Exodus 29:39"> And they prophesied until the evening came; and it came to pass as it was the time of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. the sacrifice going up.">i</a></sup>offering of the sacrifice, that Eliu the Thesbite spoke to the prophets of the abominations, saying, Stand by for the present, and I will offer my sacrifice. And they stood aside and departed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Chronicles II 33:16; Kings III 19:14; Kings III 19:10"> And Eliu said to the people, Come near to me. And all the people came near to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Kings IV 17:34; Genesis 35:10; Genesis 32:28"> And Eliu took twelve stones, according to the number of the tribes of Israel, as the Lord spoke to him, saying, Israel shall be thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Colossians 3:17; Exodus 20:24–25; Judges 21:4"> And he built up the stones in the name of the Lord, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gr. healed.">j</a></sup>repaired the altar that had been broken down; and he made a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gr. sea.">k</a></sup>trench that would hold two measures of seed round about the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 22:9; Judges 6:20; Leviticus 1:6–8"> And he piled the cleft wood on the altar which he had made, and divided the whole-burnt-offering, and laid it on the wood, and laid it in order on the altar, and said, Fetch me four pitchers of water, and pour it on the whole-burnt-offering, and on the wood. And they did so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:21; 2 Corinthians 4:2"> And he said, Do it the second time. And they did it the second time. And he said, Do it the third time. And they did it the third time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Kings III 18:32; Kings III 18:38"> And the water ran round about the altar, and they filled the trench with water.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Kings III 18:29; Kings IV 19:19; Exodus 3:6"> And Eliu cried aloud to heaven, and said, Lord God of Abraam, and Isaac, and Israel, answer me, O Lord,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or, hearken to me.">l</a></sup>answer me this day by fire, and let all this people know that thou art the Lord, the God of Israel, and I am thy servant, and for thy sake I have wrought these works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Daniel 9:17–19; Luke 1:16–17; Jezekiel 36:25–27"> Hear me, O Lord, hear me, and let this people know that thou art the Lord God, and thou hast turned back the heart of this people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Chronicles I 21:26; Leviticus 9:24; Chronicles II 7:1"> Then fire fell from the Lord out of heaven, and devoured the whole-burnt-offerings, and the wood and the water that was in the trench, and the fire licked up the stones and the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Kings III 18:24; Chronicles II 7:3; Kings III 18:21"> And all the people fell upon their faces, and said, Truly the Lord is God; he is God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:20; Judges 4:7; Deuteronomy 13:5"> And Eliu said to the people, Take the prophets of Baal; let not one of them escape. And they took them; and Eliu brings them down to the brook Kisson, and he slew them there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Kings III 18:1; Ecclesiastes 9:7; Kings III 17:1"> And Eliu said to Achaab, Go up, and eat and drink, for there is a sound of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Gr. feet of rain.">m</a></sup>coming of rain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Daniel 9:7; Joshua 7:6"> And Achaab went up to eat and to drink; and Eliu went up to Carmel, and stooped to the ground, and put his face between his knees,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Ambacum 2:3; Luke 18:7; Luke 18:1"> and said to his servant, Go up, and look toward the sea. And the servant looked, and said, There is nothing: and Eliu said, Do thou then go again seven times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Luke 12:54; Job 8:7; Zacharias 4:10"> And the servant went again seven times: and it came to pass at the seventh time, that, behold, a little cloud like the sole of a man's foot<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Gr. bringing.">n</a></sup>brought water; and he said, Go up, and say to Achaab, Make ready thy chariot, and go down, lest the rain overtake thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Kings III 18:39–40; Joshua 17:16; Kings III 21:1"> And it came to pass in the meanwhile, that the heaven grew black with clouds and wind, and there was a great rain. And Achaab wept, and went to Jezrael.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Jezekiel 3:14; Jezekiel 1:3; Kings IV 9:1"> And the hand of the Lord was upon Eliu, and he girt up his loins, and ran before Achaab to Jezrael.</span>
</div>
