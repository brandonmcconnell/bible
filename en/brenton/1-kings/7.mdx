<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings III 9:10; Chronicles II 8:1; Ecclesiastes 2:4–5"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Verse 1, etc. See the first 12 verses of this chapter placed at the end of it in the Vatican copy. See below.">a</a></sup>And king Solomon sent, and took Chiram out of Tyre,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings III 10:17; Chronicles II 9:16; Kings III 10:21"> the son of a widow woman; and he was of the tribe of Nephthalim, and his father was a Tyrian; a worker in brass, and accomplished in art and skill and knowledge to work every work in brass: and he was brought in to king Solomon, and he wrought all the works.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings III 6:5"> And he cast the two pillars for the porch of the house: eighteen cubits was the height of each pillar, and a circumference of fourteen cubits encompassed it, even the thickness of the pillar: the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, embossed or hollow work.">b</a></sup>flutings were four fingers wide, and thus was the other pillar formed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jezekiel 40:22; Jezekiel 40:16; Kings III 6:4"> And he made two molten chapiters to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. give.">c</a></sup>put on the heads of the pillars: five cubits was the height of one chapiter, and five cubits was the height of the other chapiter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And he made two<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. nets.">d</a></sup>ornaments of net-work to cover the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. chapiter.">e</a></sup>chapiters of the pillars; even a net for one chapiter, and a net for the other chapiter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings III 7:12; Jezekiel 41:25–26"> And hanging work, two rows of brazen pomegranates,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="q. d. netted.">f</a></sup>formed with net-work, hanging work, row upon row: and thus he framed the ornaments for the second chapiter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 20:8; Psalms 122:5; Esaias 9:7"> And he set up the pillars of the porch of the temple: and he set up the one pillar, and called its name Jachum: and he set up the second pillar, and called its name Boloz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Chronicles II 8:11; Kings III 3:1; Kings III 9:24"> And on the heads of the pillars he made lily-work against the porch, of four cubits,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings III 7:10–11; Kings III 5:17"> and a chamber over both the pillars, and above the sides an addition equal to the chamber in width.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 21:19–20; 1 Corinthians 3:10–11; Esaias 54:11"> And he made the sea, ten cubits from one rim to the other, the same was<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. round in a circle.">g</a></sup>completely circular round about: its height was five cubits, and its circumference thirty-three cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 2:20–22; 1 Peter 2:5"> And stays underneath its rim round about compassed it ten cubits round;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings III 6:36; Kings III 7:6; John 10:23"> and its rim was as the work of the rim of a cup, a lily-flower, and the thickness of it was a span.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Chronicles II 4:11; Chronicles II 2:13–14; Kings III 7:40"> And there were twelve oxen under the sea; three looking to the north, and three looking to the west, and three looking to the south, and three looking to the east: and all their hinder parts were<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Lit. to or into the house.">h</a></sup>inward, and the sea was above upon them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Chronicles II 2:14; Chronicles II 4:16; Exodus 31:2–6"> And he made ten brazen bases: five cubits was the length of one base, and four cubits the breadth of it, and its height was six cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremias 52:21; Chronicles II 3:15–17; Kings III 7:21"> And this work of the bases was<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. shut up.">i</a></sup>formed with a border to them, and there was a border between the ledges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 38:19; Chronicles II 4:12–13; Exodus 38:17"> And upon their borders between the projections were lions, and oxen, and cherubs: and on the projections, even so above, and also below were the places of lions and oxen, hanging work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 28:14; Exodus 28:24–25; Exodus 28:22"> And there were four brazen wheels to one base; and there were brazen bases, and their four sides answering to them, side<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or, bearings, or shoulders; A. V. undersetters.">j</a></sup>pieces under the bases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 28:14; Exodus 39:15–18; Exodus 28:22"> And there were<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gr. hands.">k</a></sup>axles in the wheels<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Gr. in.">l</a></sup>under the base. And the height of one wheel was a cubit and a half.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings III 7:22; Kings III 6:32–35; Kings III 6:18"> And the work of the wheels was as the work of chariot wheels: their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Gr. their hands and their backs.">m</a></sup>axles, and their felloes, and the rest of their work, were all molten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Chronicles II 3:16; Chronicles II 4:13; Jeremias 52:22–23"> The four side-pieces were at the four corners of each base; its shoulders were formed of the base.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Chronicles II 3:17; Kings III 6:3; Kings III 7:12"> And on the top of the base half a cubit was the size of it, there was a circle on the top of the base, and there was the top of its spaces and its borders: and it<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Gr. was opened.">n</a></sup>was open at the top of its spaces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And its borders were cherubs, and lions, and palm-trees, upright, each was joined in front and within and round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Chronicles II 4:2; Kings IV 25:13; Jeremias 52:17"> According to the same form he made all the ten bases, even one order and one measure to all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Kings III 6:18; Chronicles II 4:3; Exodus 37:17–22"> And he made ten brazen lavers, each laver containing forty<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Gr. gallons. The Gr. seems to have this meaning, but the Heb. word means more.">o</a></sup>baths, and measuring four cubits, each laver placed on a several base throughout the ten bases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremias 52:20; Chronicles II 4:4–5; Matthew 28:19"> And he put five bases on the right side of the house, and five on the left side of the house: and the sea was placed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Gr. on the shoulder-piece of the house on the right.">p</a></sup>on the right side of the house eastward in the direction of the south.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Chronicles II 4:5; Kings III 7:38; Kings III 6:18"> And Chiram made the caldrons, and the pans, and the bowls; and Chiram finished making all the works that he wrought for king Solomon in the house of the Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Chronicles II 4:14; Kings IV 25:13; Jeremias 52:17"> two pillars and the wreathen works of the pillars on the heads of the two pillars; and the two<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Gr. nets.">q</a></sup>net-works to cover both the wreathen works of the flutings that were upon the pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> The four hundred pomegranates for both the net-works, two rows of pomegranates for one net-work, to cover both the wreathen works of the bases belonging to both pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Osee 5:14; Jezekiel 10:14; Exodus 37:7"> And the ten bases, and the ten lavers upon the bases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jezekiel 10:10–13; Jezekiel 3:13; Jezekiel 1:15–21"> And one sea, and the twelve oxen under the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And the caldrons, and pans, and bowls, and all the furniture, which Chiram made for king Solomon for the house of the Lord: and there were eight and forty pillars of the house of the king and of the house of the Lord: all the works of the king which Chiram made were entirely of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> There was no<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Gr. weight.">r</a></sup>reckoning of the brass of which he made all these works, from the very great abundance, there was no end of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Gr. weights.">s</a></sup>weight of the brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jezekiel 1:18; Jezekiel 1:16"> In the country round about Jordan did he cast them, in the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="thick part of the land.">t</a></sup>clay land between Socchoth and Sira.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And king Solomon took the furniture which Chiram made for the house of the Lord, the golden altar, and the golden table of shewbread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And he put the five candlesticks on the left, and five on the right in front of the oracle, being of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Lit. golden, shut up.">u</a></sup>pure gold, and the lamp-stands, and the lamps, and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Perhaps small vessels with lips for pouring oil.">v</a></sup>snuffers of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Kings III 7:29; Kings III 6:29; Jezekiel 41:18–20"> And there were made the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or, rather work about the door.">w</a></sup>porches, and the nails, and the bowls, and the spoons, and the golden censers,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Gr. shut up.">x</a></sup>of pure gold: and the panels of the doors of the innermost part of the house, even the holy of holies, and the golden doors of the temple.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> So the work of the house of the Lord which Solomon wrought was finished; and Solomon brought in the holy things of David his father, and all the holy things of Solomon; he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Gr. gave.">y</a></sup>put the silver, and the gold, and the furniture, into the treasures of the house of the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 40:11–12; Zacharias 13:1; 1 John 1:7"> And Solomon built a house for himself in thirteen years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Chronicles II 4:10; Chronicles II 4:6"> And he built the house with the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Lit. thicket.">z</a></sup>wood of Libanus; its length was a hundred cubits, and its breadth was fifty cubits, and its height was of thirty cubits, and it was made<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Gr. of.">aa</a></sup>with three rows of cedar pillars, and the pillars had<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Or, shoulders, or shoulder pieces.">ab</a></sup>side-pieces of cedar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Chronicles II 4:11–16; Exodus 24:6; Exodus 39:32–43"> And he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Gr. ceiled or wainscoted the house.">ac</a></sup>formed the house with chambers above on the sides of the pillars, and the number of the pillars was each row forty and five,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Kings III 7:15–22; Chronicles II 4:12"> and there were three chambers, and space against space in three rows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Kings III 7:20"> And all the doors and spaces formed like chambers were square, and from door to door was a correspondence in three rows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> And he made the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Or, portico.">ad</a></sup>porch of the pillars, they were fifty cubits long and fifty broad, the porch joining them in front; and the other pillars and the thick beam were in front of the house by the porches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Kings III 7:23; Kings III 7:25"> And there was the Porch of seats where he would judge, the porch of judgment.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Exodus 27:3; Exodus 38:3; Chronicles II 4:16"> And their house where he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Gr. will.">ae</a></sup>would dwell, had one court communicating with these according to this work; and he built the house for the daughter of Pharao whom Solomon had taken, according to this porch.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Joshua 3:16; Chronicles II 4:17; Genesis 33:17"> All these were of costly stones, sculptured at intervals within even from the foundation even to the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Gr. chapiters, mouldings, etc.">af</a></sup>top, and outward to the great court,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Chronicles I 22:14; Chronicles I 22:3; Chronicles II 4:18"> founded with large costly stones, stones of ten cubits and eight cubits long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Exodus 37:10–16; Chronicles II 4:8; Exodus 39:36"> And above with costly stones, according to the measure of hewn stones, and with cedars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Chronicles II 4:7; Chronicles II 4:20; Zacharias 4:11–14"> There were three rows of hewn stones round about the great hall, and a row of sculptured cedar:</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Exodus 25:29; Chronicles II 4:21–22; Exodus 27:3"> and Solomon finished all his house.</span>
</div>
