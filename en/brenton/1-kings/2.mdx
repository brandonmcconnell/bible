<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 47:29; Deuteronomy 31:14; 1 Timothy 1:18"> And the days of David drew near that he should die: and he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. answered.">a</a></sup>addressed his son Solomon, saying, I go the way of all the earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 1:6–7; Joshua 23:14; Ephesians 6:10"> but be thou strong, and shew thyself a man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:9; Deuteronomy 17:18–20; Proverbs 3:1–4"> and keep the charge of the Lord thy God, to walk in his ways, to keep the commandments and the ordinances and the judgments which are written in the law of Moses; that thou mayest understand what thou shalt do in all things that I command thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings IV 20:3; Kings II 7:25; Kings III 8:25"> that the Lord may confirm his word which he spoke, saying, If thy children shall take heed to their way to walk before me in truth with all their heart, I promise thee, saying, there shall not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. be destroyed to thee.">b</a></sup>fail thee a man on the throne of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings II 3:27; Kings II 20:10; Kings II 18:5"> Moreover thou knowest all that Joab the son of Saruia did to me, what he did to the two captains of the forces of Israel, to Abenner the son of Ner, and to Amessai the son of Jether, that he slew them, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. ordered.">c</a></sup>shed the blood of war in peace, and put innocent blood on his girdle that was about his loins, and on his sandal that was on his foot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings III 2:9; Esaias 57:21; Psalms 37:37"> Therefore thou shalt deal with him according to thy wisdom, and thou shalt not bring down his grey hairs in peace to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. Hades.">d</a></sup>the grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings II 17:27–29; Kings II 9:7; Kings II 19:31–40"> But thou shalt deal kindly with the sons of Berzelli the Galaadite, and they shall be among those that eat at thy table; for thus they drew nigh to me when I fled from the face of thy brother Abessalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings II 16:5–8; Kings III 2:36–46; Kings II 19:16–23"> And, behold, there is with thee Semei the son of Gera, a Benjamite of Baurim: and he cursed me with a grievous curse in the day when I went into the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. camps.">e</a></sup>camp; and he came down to Jordan to meet me, and I swore to him by the Lord, saying, I will not put thee to death with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings III 2:6; Genesis 42:38; Genesis 44:31"> But thou shalt by no means hold him guiltless, for thou art a wise man, and wilt know what thou shalt do to him, and shalt bring down his grey hairs with blood to the grave.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 2:29; Acts 13:36; Kings III 1:21"> And David slept with his fathers, and was buried in the city of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings II 5:4–5; Chronicles I 29:26–27"> And the days which David reigned over Israel were forty years; he reigned seven years in Chebron, and thirty-three years in Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Chronicles II 1:1; Chronicles I 29:23–25; Psalms 132:12"> And Solomon sat on the throne of his father David, and his kingdom was<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. prepared.">f</a></sup>established greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings I 16:4–5; Kings III 1:5–10; Luke 10:5–6"> And Adonias the son of Aggith came in to Bersabee the mother of Solomon, and did obeisance to her: and she said,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. Is thine entrance peace?">g</a></sup>Dost thou enter peaceably? and he said, Peaceably:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings II 14:12; Luke 7:40"> I have business with thee. And she said to him, Say on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Chronicles I 22:9–10; Chronicles I 28:5–7; Kings III 1:25"> And he said to her, Thou knowest that the kingdom was mine, and all Israel turned their face toward me for a king; but the kingdom was turned from me and became my brother's: for it was appointed to him from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 30:7; Psalms 132:10"> And now I make one request of thee, do not turn away thy face. And Bersabee said to him, Speak on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings II 12:8; Kings III 1:2–4; Kings II 3:7"> And he said to her, Speak, I pray thee, to king Solomon, for he will not turn away his face from thee, and let him give me Abisag the Somanite for a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 14:15"> And Bersabee said, Well; I will speak for thee to the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 45:9; Kings III 15:13; Leviticus 19:32"> And Bersabee went in to king Solomon to speak to him concerning Adonias; and the king rose up to meet her, and kissed her, and sat on the throne, and a throne was set for the mother of the king, and she sat on his right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–11; Matthew 11:24; John 2:3–4"> And she said to him, I ask of thee one little request; turn not away my face from thee. And the king said to her, Ask, my mother, and I will not reject thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings III 1:3; Kings II 16:21–22"> And she said, Let, I pray thee, Abisag the Somanite be given to Adonias thy brother to wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Chronicles I 3:2; Chronicles I 3:5; Matthew 20:22"> And king Solomon answered and said to his mother, And why hast thou asked Abisag for Adonias? ask for him the kingdom also; for he is my elder brother, and he has for his companion Abiathar the priest, and Joab the son of Saruia the commander-in-chief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ruth 1:17; Psalms 140:9; Psalms 64:8"> And king Solomon swore by the Lord, saying, God do so to me, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. add these things.">h</a></sup>more also, if it be not that Adonias has spoken this word against his own life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Chronicles I 22:10; Chronicles I 17:10; Chronicles I 17:17"> And now as the Lord lives who has established me, and set me on the throne of my father David, and he has made me a house, as the Lord spoke, this day shall Adonias be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings II 8:18; Kings III 2:46; Judges 8:20–21"> So king Solomon sent by the hand of Banæas the son of Jodae, and he slew him, and Adonias died in that day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Joshua 21:18; Kings I 22:20–23; Jeremias 1:1"> And the king said to Abiathar the priest, Depart thou quickly to Anathoth to thy farm, for thou art<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. a man of death.">i</a></sup>worthy of death this day; but I will not slay thee, because thou hast borne the ark of the covenant of the Lord before my father, and because thou wast afflicted in all things wherein my father was afflicted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Kings I 2:27–36; Kings I 3:12–14; Jeremias 7:12–14"> And Solomon removed Abiathar from being a priest of the Lord, that the word of the Lord might be fulfilled, which he spoke<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or, against.">j</a></sup>concerning the house of Heli in Selom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Kings III 1:50; Kings III 1:7; Kings II 18:2"> And the report came to Joab son of Saruia; for Joab had turned after Adonias, and he went not after Solomon: and Joab fled to the tabernacle of the Lord, and caught hold of the horns of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Kings III 2:25; Exodus 21:14; Kings III 2:46"> And it was told Solomon, saying, Joab has fled to the tabernacle of the Lord, and lo! he has hold of the horns of the altar. And king Solomon sent to Joab, saying,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gr. What has happened to thee?">k</a></sup>What ails thee, that thou hast fled to the altar? and Joab said, Because I was afraid of thee, and fled for refuge to the Lord. And Solomon sent Banæas son of Jodae, saying, Go and slay him, and bury him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And Banæas son of Jodae came to Joab to the tabernacle of the Lord, and said to him, Thus says the king, Come forth. And Joab said, I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Gr. do not.">l</a></sup>will not come forth, for I will die here. And Banæas son of Jodae returned and spoke to the king, saying, Thus has Joab spoken, and thus has he answered me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:8–9; Numbers 35:33; Exodus 21:14"> And the king said to him, Go, and do to him as he has spoken, and kill him: and thou shalt bury him, and thou shalt remove this day the blood which he shed without cause, from me and from the house of my father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Judges 9:24; Judges 9:57; Psalms 7:16"> And the Lord has returned upon his own head the blood of his unrighteousness, inasmuch as he attacked two men more righteous and better than himself, and slew them with the sword, and my father David knew not of their blood, even Abenner the son of Ner the commander-in-chief of Israel, and Amessa the son of Jether the commander-in-chief of Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; Kings III 2:32; Luke 2:14"> And their blood is returned upon his head, and upon the head of his seed for ever: but to David, and his seed, and his house, and his throne, may there be peace for ever from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 3:1; Joshua 15:61; Kings III 2:25"> So Banæas son of Jodae went up, and attacked him, and slew him, and buried him in his house in the wilderness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Kings III 2:27; Chronicles I 24:3; Chronicles I 29:22"> And the king<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Gr. gave.">m</a></sup>appointed Banæas son of Jodae in his place over the host; and the kingdom was established in Jerusalem; and as for Sadoc the priest, the king appointed him to be high priest in the room of Abiathar. And Solomon son of David reigned over Israel and Juda in Jerusalem:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Kings III 2:27; Chronicles I 24:3; Chronicles I 29:22"> and the Lord gave understanding to Solomon, and very much wisdom, and largeness of heart, as the sand by the sea-shore.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="a See Appendix.">n</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Kings III 2:27; Chronicles I 24:3; Chronicles I 29:22"> And the wisdom of Solomon abounded exceedingly beyond the wisdom of all the ancients, and beyond all the wise men of Egypt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Kings III 2:27; Chronicles I 24:3; Chronicles I 29:22"> and he took the daughter of Pharao, and brought her into the city of David, until he had finished building his own house, and the house of the Lord first, and the wall of Jerusalem round about. In seven years he made and finished them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Kings III 2:27; Chronicles I 24:3; Chronicles I 29:22"> And Solomon had seventy thousand bearers of burdens, and eighty thousand hewers of stone in the mountain:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Kings III 2:27; Chronicles I 24:3; Chronicles I 29:22"> and Solomon made the sea, and the bases, and the great lavers, and the pillars, and the fountain of the court, and the brazen sea—<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="e Probably some hiatus in the text.">o</a></sup>and he built the citadel as a defence above it, he made a breach in the wall of the city of David;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Kings III 2:27; Chronicles I 24:3; Chronicles I 29:22"> thus the daughter of Pharao went up out of the city of David to her house which he built for her. Then he built the citadel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Kings III 2:27; Chronicles I 24:3; Chronicles I 29:22"> and Solomon offered up three whole-burnt-offerings in the year, and peace-offerings on the altar which he built to the Lord, and he burnt incense before the Lord, and finished the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Kings III 2:27; Chronicles I 24:3; Chronicles I 29:22"> And these are the chief persons who presided over the works of Solomon; three thousand and six hundred masters of the people that wrought the works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Kings III 2:27; Chronicles I 24:3; Chronicles I 29:22"> And he built Assur, and Magdo, and Gazer, and upper Bæthoron, and Ballath:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Kings III 2:27; Chronicles I 24:3; Chronicles I 29:22"> only after he had built the house of the Lord, and the wall of Jerusalem round about, afterwards he built these cities.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Kings III 2:27; Chronicles I 24:3; Chronicles I 29:22"> And when David was yet living, he charged Solomon, saying, Behold, there is with thee Semei the son of Gera, of the seed of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="l Gr. Jemini.">p</a></sup>Benjamin out of Chebron:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Kings III 2:27; Chronicles I 24:3; Chronicles I 29:22"> he cursed me with a grievous curse in the day when I went into the camp;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Kings III 2:27; Chronicles I 24:3; Chronicles I 29:22"> and he came down to meet me at Jordan, and I swore to him by the Lord, saying,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="n Gr. if he shall be slain.">q</a></sup>He shall not be slain with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Kings III 2:27; Chronicles I 24:3; Chronicles I 29:22"> But now do not thou hold him guiltless, for thou art a man of understanding, and thou wilt know what thou shalt do to him, and thou shalt bring down his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="o Gr. hoary head.">r</a></sup>grey hairs with blood to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="o Gr. Hades.">s</a></sup>the grave.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Kings II 16:5–9; Kings III 2:8–9; Proverbs 20:26"> And the king called Semei, and said to him, Build thee a house in Jerusalem, and dwell there, and thou shalt not go out thence any whither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Kings II 1:16; Kings II 15:23; Joshua 2:19"> And it shall come to pass in the day<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Gr. of thy going forth.">t</a></sup>that thou shalt go forth and cross over the brook Kedron,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Gr. knowing thou shalt know.">u</a></sup>know assuredly that thou shalt certainly die: thy blood shall be upon thine head. And the king caused him to swear in that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Kings III 20:4; Kings IV 20:19"> And Semei said to the king, Good is the word that thou hast spoken, my lord O king: thus will thy servant do. And Semei dwelt in Jerusalem three years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Kings I 21:10; Kings I 27:2–3"> And it came to pass after the three years, that two servants of Semei ran away to Anchus son of Maacha king of Geth: and it was told Semei, saying, Behold, thy servants are in Geth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 12:15; Proverbs 15:27; 1 Timothy 6:10"> And Semei rose up, and saddled his ass, and went to Geth to Anchus to seek out his servants: and Semei went, and brought his servants out of Geth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> And it was told Solomon, saying, Semei is gone out of Jerusalem to Geth, and has brought back his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 15:4; Kings III 2:36–38; Luke 15:22"> And the king sent and called Semei, and said to him, Did I not adjure thee by the Lord, and testify to thee, saying, In whatsoever day thou shalt go out of Jerusalem, and go to the right or left, know certainly that thou shalt assuredly die?</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Romans 13:5; Chronicles II 30:12; Ecclesiastes 8:2"> And why hast thou not kept the oath of the Lord, and the commandment which I commanded<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Gr. against thee.">v</a></sup>thee?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Kings II 16:5–13; Psalms 7:16; Jezekiel 17:19"> And the king said to Semei, Thou knowest all thy mischief which thy heart knows, which thou didst to David my father: and the Lord has recompensed thy mischief on thine own head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Proverbs 25:5; Kings III 2:24; Esaias 9:6–7"> And king Solomon is blessed, and the throne of David shall be established before the Lord for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Chronicles II 1:1; Kings III 2:12; Kings III 2:45"> And Solomon commanded Banæas the son of Jodae, and he went forth and slew him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Chronicles II 1:1; Kings III 2:12; Kings III 2:45"> And king Solomon was very prudent and wise: and Juda and Israel were very many, as the sand which is by the sea for multitude, eating, and drinking, and rejoicing:</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Chronicles II 1:1; Kings III 2:12; Kings III 2:45"> and Solomon was chief in all the kingdoms, and they brought gifts, and served Solomon all the days of his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Chronicles II 1:1; Kings III 2:12; Kings III 2:45"> And Solomon began to open the domains of Libanus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Chronicles II 1:1; Kings III 2:12; Kings III 2:45"> and he built Thermæ in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Chronicles II 1:1; Kings III 2:12; Kings III 2:45"> And this was the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="e Gr. dinner.">w</a></sup>daily provision of Solomon, thirty measures of fine flour, and sixty measures of ground meal, ten choice calves, and twenty oxen from the pastures, and a hundred sheep, besides stags, and does, and choice fed birds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Chronicles II 1:1; Kings III 2:12; Kings III 2:45"> For he ruled in all the country on this side the river, from Raphi unto Gaza, over all the kings<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="f Gr. beyond.">x</a></sup>on this side the river:</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Chronicles II 1:1; Kings III 2:12; Kings III 2:45"> and he was at peace on all sides round about; and Juda and Israel dwelt<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="g Gr. trusting in confidence. See Heb.">y</a></sup>safely, every one under his vine and under his fig tree, eating and drinking, and feasting, from Dan even to Bersabee, all the days of Solomon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Chronicles II 1:1; Kings III 2:12; Kings III 2:45"> And these were the princes of Solomon; Azariu son of Sadoc the priest, and Orniu son of Nathan chief of the officers, and he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="h Gr. ran.">z</a></sup>went to his house; and Suba the scribe, and Basa son of Achithalam recorder, and Abi son of Joab commander-in-chief, and Achire son of Edrai was over the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="h Or, tributes.">aa</a></sup>levies, and Banæas son of Jodae over the household and over the brickwork, and Cachur the son of Nathan was counsellor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Chronicles II 1:1; Kings III 2:12; Kings III 2:45"> And Solomon had forty thousand brood mares for his chariots, and twelve thousand horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Chronicles II 1:1; Kings III 2:12; Kings III 2:45"> And he reigned over all the kings from the river and to the land of the Philistines, and to the borders of Egypt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Chronicles II 1:1; Kings III 2:12; Kings III 2:45"> so Solomon the son of David reigned over Israel and Juda in Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
