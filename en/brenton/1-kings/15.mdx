<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Chronicles II 13:2; Chronicles II 11:20–22; Kings III 15:13"> And he reigned<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Alex. 16 years.">a</a></sup>three years over Jerusalem: and his mother's name was Maacha, daughter of Abessalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings III 11:4; Psalms 119:80; Kings III 3:14"> And he walked in the sins of his father which he wrought in his presence, and his heart was not perfect with the Lord his God, as was the heart of his father David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings III 11:36; Chronicles II 21:7; Esaias 37:35"> Howbeit for David's sake the Lord gave him a remnant, that he might establish his children after him, and might establish Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings III 14:8; Kings II 11:15–17; Kings III 15:3"> Forasmuch as David did that which was right in the sight of the Lord: he turned not from any thing that he commanded him all the days of his life.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Chronicles II 13:2–22; Kings III 14:29"> And the rest of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. words.">b</a></sup>history of Abiu, and all that he did, behold, are not these written in the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Lit. the words of the days.">c</a></sup>book of the chronicles of the kings of Juda? And there was war between Abiu and Jeroboam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Chronicles II 14:1; Kings III 14:31; Matthew 1:7–8"> And Abiu slept with his fathers in the twenty-fourth year of Jeroboam; and he is buried with his fathers in the city of David: and Asa his son reigns in his stead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Heb. and Alex. In the twentieth year.">d</a></sup>In the four and twentieth year of Jeroboam king of Israel, Asa begins to reign over Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings III 15:2; Kings III 15:13; Chronicles II 13:2"> And he reigned forty-one<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. year. Hebraism.">e</a></sup>years in Jerusalem: and his mother's name was Ana, daughter of Abessalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Chronicles II 14:2; Chronicles II 15:17; Kings III 15:3"> And Asa did that which was right in the sight of the Lord, as David his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings III 22:46; Kings III 14:23–24; Chronicles II 14:2–5"> And he removed the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Lit. sacrifices.">f</a></sup>sodomites out of the land, and abolished all the practices which his fathers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. did, or wrought.">g</a></sup>had kept up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 32:20; Deuteronomy 7:5; Zacharias 13:3"> And he removed Ana his mother from being queen, forasmuch as she<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. made.">h</a></sup>gathered a meeting in her grove: and Asa cut down her retreats, and burnt them with fire in the brook of Kedron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings III 22:43; Kings III 8:61; Kings III 15:3"> But he removed not the high places; nevertheless the heart of Asa was perfect with the Lord all his days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings III 7:51; Chronicles II 14:13; Chronicles II 15:18"> And he brought in the pillars of his father, he even brought in his gold and silver pillars into the house of the Lord, and his vessels.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings III 15:32; Kings III 14:30; Chronicles II 16:1–6"> And there was war between Asa and Baasa king of Israel all their days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings III 12:27; Chronicles II 16:1–6; Joshua 18:25"> And Baasa king of Israel went up against Juda, and built Rama, so that no one should go out or come in for Asa king of Juda.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings III 14:26; Kings IV 12:18; Kings III 11:23–24"> And Asa took all the silver and the gold that was found in the treasures of the house of the Lord, and in the treasures of the king's house, and gave them into the hands of his servants; and king Asa sent them out to the son of Ader, the son of Taberema son of Azin king of Syria, who dwelt in Damascus, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 1:31; Chronicles II 16:7; Romans 3:8"> Make a covenant between me and thee, and between my father and thy father: lo! I have sent forth to thee gold and silver for gifts: come, break thy league with Baasa king of Israel,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. and he shall.">i</a></sup>that he may go up from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings IV 15:29; Judges 18:29; Joshua 11:2"> And the son of Ader hearkened to king Asa, and sent the chiefs of his forces to the cities of Israel; and they smote Ain, Dan, and Abel of the house of Maacha, and all Chennereth, as far as the whole land of Nephthali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings III 14:17; Kings III 16:15–18; Chronicles II 16:5"> And it came to pass when Baasa heard it, that he left off building Rama, and returned to Thersa.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 21:17; Joshua 18:24; Joshua 18:26"> And king Asa charged all Juda without exception: and they take up the stones of Rama and its timbers with which Baasa was building; and king Asa built with them upon the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Heb. Geba and Mizpeh.">j</a></sup>whole hill of Benjamin, and the watch-tower.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Chronicles II 16:11–14; Kings III 14:29–31; Kings III 15:7–8"> And the rest of the history of Asa, and all his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gr. might.">k</a></sup>mighty deeds which he wrought, and the cities which he built, behold, are not these written in the book of the chronicles<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Gr. for.">l</a></sup>of the kings of Juda? Nevertheless in the time of his old age he was diseased in his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 1:8; Kings III 22:41–43; Chronicles II 17:1–9"> And Asa slept with his fathers, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Gr. is.">m</a></sup>was buried with his fathers in the city of David his father: and Josaphat his son reigns in his stead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings III 14:20; Kings III 14:12"> And Nabat son of Jeroboam reigns over Israel in the second year of Asa king of Juda, and he reigned two years in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Kings III 14:16; Kings III 15:34; Kings III 15:30"> And he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, and walked in the way of his father, and in his sins wherein he caused Israel to sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 19:44; Joshua 21:23; Kings III 14:14"> And Baasa son of Achia, who was over the house of Belaan son of Achia, conspired against him, and smote him in Gabathon of the Philistines; for Nabat and all Israel were besieging Gabathon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:35"> And Baasa slew him in the third year of Asa son of Asa king of Juda; and reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Kings III 14:9–16; Kings IV 19:25; Kings IV 10:10–11"> And it came to pass when he reigned, that he smote the whole house of Jeroboam, and left<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Gr. no breath.">n</a></sup>none that breathed of Jeroboam, until he had destroyed him utterly, according to the word of the Lord which he spoke by his servant Achia the Selonite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Kings III 15:26; Kings III 14:22; Kings III 14:9–16"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="For a similar use of περὶ, see Jer. 7. 22.">o</a></sup>for the sins of Jeroboam, who led Israel into sin, even by his provocation wherewith he provoked the Lord God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Kings III 14:19; Kings III 16:20; Kings III 16:14"> And the rest of the history of Nabat, and all that he did, behold, are not these written in the book of the chronicles<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Gr. for.">p</a></sup>of the kings of Israel?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Verse 32 is not in Vat., it is here read from Alex.">q</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Kings III 16:8"> And in the third year of Asa king of Juda, Baasa the son of Achia begins to reign over Israel in Thersa, twenty and four years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Kings III 15:26; Kings III 14:16; Kings III 12:28–29"> And he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, and walked in the way of Jeroboam the son of Nabat, and in his sins, as he caused Israel to sin.</span>
</div>
