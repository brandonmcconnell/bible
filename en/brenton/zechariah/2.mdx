<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zacharias 1:19; Zacharias 1:8–11; Zacharias 4:5"> And the Lord shewed me four artificers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zacharias 1:17; Jeremias 33:22; Jezekiel 38:11"> And I said, What are these coming to do? </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esaias 4:5; Zacharias 9:8; Psalms 3:3"> And I lifted up mine eyes, and looked, and behold a man, and in his hand a measuring line.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esaias 48:20; Jezekiel 17:21; Esaias 52:11–12"> And I said to him, Whither goest thou? And he said to me, To measure Jerusalem, to see what is the breadth of it, and what is the length of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esaias 48:20; Genesis 19:17; Esaias 52:11"> And, behold, the angel that spoke with me stood by, and another angel went forth to meet him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 17:8; Deuteronomy 32:10; John 15:21–23"> and spoke to him, saying, Run and speak to that young man, saying,<br />Jerusalem shall be fully inhabited by reason of the abundance of men and cattle in the midst of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esaias 19:16; Esaias 14:2; Zacharias 4:9"> And I will be to her, saith the Lord, a wall of fire round about, and I will be for a glory in the midst of her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 28:20; John 1:14; Sophonias 3:14–15"> Ho, ho, flee from the land of the north, saith the Lord: for I will gather you from the four winds of heaven, saith the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 11:15; 1 Peter 2:9–10; Acts 28:28"> even to Sion: deliver yourselves, ye that dwell with the daughter of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zacharias 1:17; Deuteronomy 32:9; Jeremias 10:16"> For thus saith the Lord Almighty; After the glory has he sent me to the nations that spoiled you: for he that touches you is as one that touches the apple of his eye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ambacum 2:20; Psalms 68:5; Psalms 78:65"> For, behold, I bring my hand upon them, and they shall be a spoil to them that serve them: and ye shall know that the Lord Almighty has sent me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Rejoice and be glad, O daughter of Sion: for, behold, I come, and will dwell in the midst of thee, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And many nations shall flee for refuge to the Lord in that day, and they shall be for a people to him, and they shall dwell in the midst of thee: and thou shalt know that the Lord Almighty has sent me to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the Lord shall inherit Juda his portion in the holy land, and he will yet choose Jerusalem.</span>
