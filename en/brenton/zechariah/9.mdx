<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 1:3–5; Jeremias 49:23–27; Amos 3:12"> The burden of the word of the Lord in the land of Sedrach, and his sacrifice shall be in Damascus; for the Lord looks upon men, and upon all the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 49:23; Jezekiel 28:12; Jezekiel 28:2–5"> And in Emath, even in her coasts, are Tyre and Sidon, because they were very wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jezekiel 27:33; Job 27:16; Jezekiel 28:4–5"> And Tyrus built strong-holds for herself, and heaped up silver as dust, and gathered gold as the mire of the ways.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jezekiel 28:18; Jezekiel 26:17; Esaias 23:1–7"> And therefore the Lord will take them for a possession, and will smite her power in the sea; and she shall be consumed with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 8:26; Revelation 18:9–17; Jeremias 47:1"> Ascalon shall see, and fear; Gaza also, and shall be greatly pained, and Accaron; for she is ashamed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Alex. of her hope.">a</a></sup>at her trespass; and the king shall perish from Gaza, and Ascalon shall not be inhabited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 1:8; Sophonias 2:10; 1 Peter 5:5"> And aliens shall dwell in Azotus, and I will bring down the pride of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 3:12; Jezekiel 16:57–61; Esaias 60:14–16"> And I will take their blood out of their mouth, and their abominations from between their teeth; and these also shall be left to our God, and they shall be as a captain of a thousand in Juda, and Accaron as a Jebusite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esaias 54:14; Esaias 60:18; Esaias 52:1"> And I will set up a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, bulwark. See Zeph. 2. 14.">b</a></sup>defence for my house, that they may not pass through, nor turn back, neither shall there any more come upon them one to drive them away: for now have I seen with mine eyes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 21:4–7; Sophonias 3:14–15; John 12:13–16"> Rejoice greatly, O daughter of Sion; proclaim it aloud, O daughter of Jerusalem; behold, the King is coming to thee, just, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. saving.">c</a></sup>a Saviour; he is meek and riding on an ass, and a young foal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Osee 1:7; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Osee 2:18"> And he shall destroy the chariots out of Ephraim, and the horse out of Jerusalem, and the bow of war shall be utterly destroyed; and there shall be abundance and peace out of the nations; and he shall rule over the waters as far as the sea, and the rivers to the ends of the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esaias 51:14; Exodus 24:8; Psalms 102:19–21"> And thou by the blood of thy covenant hast sent forth thy prisoners out of the pit that has no water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 42:10; Esaias 61:7; Lamentations 3:21–22"> Ye shall dwell in strongholds, ye prisoners of the congregation: and for one day of thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or, sojourning.">d</a></sup>captivity I will recompense thee double.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 17:14; Zacharias 10:3–7; Esaias 49:2"> For I have bent thee, O Juda, for myself as a bow, I have filled<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or, it with Ephraim.">e</a></sup>Ephraim; and I will raise up thy children, O Sion, against the children of the Greeks, and I will handle thee as the sword of a warrior.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 18:14; Esaias 66:15; Psalms 144:5–6"> And the Lord shall be over them, and his arrow shall go forth as lightning: and the Lord Almighty shall blow with the trumpet; and shall proceed with the tumult of his threatening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zacharias 12:6; Zacharias 10:7; Zacharias 10:5"> The Lord Almighty shall protect them, and they shall destroy them, and overwhelm them with sling-stones; and they shall swallow them down as wine, and fill<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Alex. the altar as bowls.">f</a></sup>the bowls as the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esaias 62:3; Luke 12:32; Aggæus 2:23"> And the Lord their God shall save them in that day, even his people as a flock; for holy stones are rolled upon his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Esaias 33:17; Esaias 62:8–9; 1 John 4:8–11"> For if he has anything good, and if he has anything fair, the young men shall have corn, and there shall be fragrant wine to the virgins.</span>
</div>
