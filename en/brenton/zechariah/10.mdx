<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="James 5:16–18; Matthew 7:7–8; Esaias 44:3"> Ask ye of the Lord rain in season, the early and the latter: the Lord has given bright signs, and will give them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. stormy.">a</a></sup>abundant rain, to every one grass in the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; Jeremias 27:9; Jezekiel 34:5"> For the speakers have uttered grievous things, and the diviners have<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, related.">b</a></sup>seen false visions, and they have spoken false dreams, they have given vain comfort: therefore have they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. been dried up.">c</a></sup>fallen away like sheep, and been afflicted, because there was no healing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jezekiel 34:2; Jezekiel 34:7–10; Jeremias 23:1–2"> Mine anger was kindled against the shepherds, and I will<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or, oversee. or, look upon.">d</a></sup>visit the lambs; and the Lord God Almighty shall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or, oversee. or, look upon.">e</a></sup>visit his flock, the house of Juda, and he shall make them as his goodly horse in war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zacharias 9:10; Zacharias 9:8; Numbers 24:17"> And from him he looked, and from him he set the battle in order, and from him came the bow in anger, and from him shall come forth every<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Lit. he that expels.">f</a></sup>oppressor together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Aggæus 2:22; Zacharias 9:13; Psalms 20:7"> And they shall be as warriors treading clay in the ways in war; and they shall set the battle in array, because the Lord is with them, and the riders on horses shall be put to shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zacharias 13:9; Zacharias 10:12; Jeremias 3:18"> And I will strengthen the house of Juda, and save the house of Joseph, and I will settle them; because I have loved them: and they shall be as if I had not cast them off: for I am the Lord their God, and I will hear them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zacharias 9:15; Zacharias 9:17; Kings I 2:1"> And they shall be as the warriors of Ephraim, and their heart shall rejoice as with wine: and their children also shall see it, and be glad; and their heart shall rejoice in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esaias 5:26; Esaias 7:18; Jeremias 33:22"> I will make a sign to them, and gather them in; for I will redeem them, and they shall be multiplied according to their number before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jezekiel 6:9; Osee 2:23; Esther 8:17"> And I will sow them among the people; and they that are afar off shall remember me: they shall nourish their children, and they shall return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Osee 11:11; Michæas 7:14; Zacharias 8:7"> And I will bring them again from the land of Egypt, and I will gather them in from among the Assyrians; and I will bring them into the land of Galaad and to Libanus; and there shall not even one of them be left behind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jezekiel 30:13; Michæas 5:5–6; Esaias 43:2"> And they shall pass through a narrow sea, they shall smite the waves in the sea, and all the deep places of the rivers shall be dried up: and all the pride of the Assyrians shall be taken away, and the sceptre of Egypt shall be removed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esaias 41:10; Esaias 2:5; Zacharias 10:6"> And I will strengthen them in the Lord their God; and they shall boast in his name, saith the Lord.</span>
