<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Now when Simon heard that Tryphon had gathered together a great host to invade the land of Judea, and destroy it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> and saw that the people was in great trembling and fear, he went up to Jerusalem, and gathered the people together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and gave them exhortation, saying, Ye yourselves know what great things I, and my brethren, and my father's house, have done for the laws and the sanctuary, the battles also and troubles which we have seen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> by reason whereof all my brethren are slain for Israel's sake, and I am left alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Now therefore be it far from me, that I should spare mine own life in any time of trouble: for I am no better than my brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Doubtless I will avenge my nation, and the sanctuary, and our wives, and our children: for all the heathen are gathered to destroy us of very malice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Now as soon as the people heard these words, their spirit revived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And they answered with a loud voice, saying, Thou shalt be our leader instead of Judas and Jonathan thy brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Fight thou our battles, and whatsoever thou commandest us, that will we do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> So then he gathered together all the men of war, and made haste to finish the walls of Jerusalem, and he fortified it round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Also he sent Jonathan the son of Absalom, and with him a great power, to Joppe: who casting out them that were therein remained there in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> So Tryphon removed from Ptolemais with a great power to invade the land of Judea, and Jonathan was with him in ward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> But Simon pitched his tents at Adida, over against the plain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Now when Tryphon knew that Simon was risen up instead of his brother Jonathan, and meant to join battle with him, he sent messengers unto him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Whereas we have Jonathan thy brother in hold, it is for money that he is owing unto the king's treasure, concerning the business that was committed unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Wherefore now send an hundred talents of silver, and two of his sons for hostages, that when he is at liberty he may not revolt from us, and we will let him go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Hereupon Simon, albeit he perceived that they spake deceitfully unto him, yet sent he the money and the children, lest peradventure he should procure to himself great hatred of the people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> who might have said, Because I sent him not the money and the children, therefore is Jonathan dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> So he sent them the children and the hundred talents: howbeit Tryphon dissembled, neither would he let Jonathan go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And after this came Tryphon to invade the land, and destroy it, going round about by the way that leadeth unto Adora: but Simon and his host marched against him in every place, wheresoever he went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Now they that were in the tower sent messengers unto Tryphon, to the end that he should hasten his coming unto them by the wilderness, and send them victuals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Wherefore Tryphon made ready all his horsemen to come that night: but there fell a very great snow, by reason whereof he came not. So he departed, and came into the country of Galaad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And when he came near to Bascama, he slew Jonathan, who was buried there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Afterward Tryphon returned and went into his own land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Then sent Simon, and took the bones of Jonathan his brother, and buried them in Modin, the city of his fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And all Israel made great lamentation for him, and bewailed him many days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Simon also built a monument upon the sepulchre of his father and his brethren, and raised it aloft to the sight, with hewn stone behind and before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Moreover he set up seven pyramids, one against another, for his father, and his mother, and his four brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And in these he made cunning devices, about the which he set great pillars, and upon the pillars he made all their armour for a perpetual memory, and by the armour ships carved, that they might be seen of all that sail on the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> This is the sepulchre which he made at Modin, and it standeth yet unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Now Tryphon dealt deceitfully with the young king Antiochus, and slew him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And he reigned in his stead, and crowned himself king of Asia, and brought a great calamity upon the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Then Simon built up the strong holds in Judea, and fenced them about with high towers, and great walls, and gates, and bars, and laid up victuals therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Moreover Simon chose men, and sent to king Demetrius, to the end he should give the land an immunity, because all that Tryphon did was to spoil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Unto whom king Demetrius answered and wrote after this manner:</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> King Demetrius unto Simon the high priest, and friend of kings, as also unto the elders and nation of the Jews, sendeth greeting:</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> The golden crown, and the scarlet robe, which ye sent unto us, we have received: and we are ready to make a stedfast peace with you, yea, and to write unto our officers, to confirm the immunities which we have granted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And whatsoever covenants we have made with you shall stand; and the strong holds, which ye have builded, shall be your own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> As for any oversight or fault committed unto this day, we forgive it, and the crown tax also, which ye owe us: and if there were any other tribute paid in Jerusalem, it shall no more be paid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And look who are meet among you to be in our court, let them be enrolled, and let there be peace betwixt us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> Thus the yoke of the heathen was taken away from Israel in the hundred and seventieth year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Then the people of Israel began to write in their instruments and contracts, In the first year of Simon the high priest, the governor and leader of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> In those days Simon camped against Gaza, and besieged it round about; he made also an engine of war, and set it by the city, and battered a certain tower, and took it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And they that were in the engine leaped into the city; whereupon there was a great uproar in the city:</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> insomuch as the people of the city rent their clothes, and climbed upon the walls with their wives and children, and cried with a loud voice, beseeching Simon to grant them peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> And they said, Deal not with us according to our wickedness, but according to thy mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> So Simon was appeased toward them, and fought no more against them, but put them out of the city, and cleansed the houses wherein the idols were, and so entered into it with songs and thanksgiving.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> Yea, he put all uncleanness out of it, and placed such men there as would keep the law, and made it stronger than it was before, and built therein a dwelling place for himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> They also of the tower in Jerusalem were kept so strait, that they could neither come forth, nor go into the country, nor buy, nor sell: wherefore they were in great distress for want of victuals, and a great number of them perished through famine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> Then cried they to Simon, beseeching him to be at one with them: which thing he granted them; and when he had put them out from thence, he cleansed the tower from pollutions;</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> and entered into it the three and twentieth day of the second month, in the hundred seventy and first year, with thanksgiving, and branches of palm trees, and with harps, and cymbals, and with viols, and hymns, and songs: because there was destroyed a great enemy out of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> He ordained also that that day should be kept every year with gladness. Moreover the hill of the temple that was by the tower he made stronger than it was, and there he dwelt himself with his company.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53"> And when Simon saw that John his son was a valiant man, he made him captain of all the hosts; and he dwelt in Gazara.</span>
