<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 7:12; Joshua 7:10; Kings IV 8:3–4"> And Jacob having seen that there was a sale of corn in Egypt, said to his sons, Why are ye indolent?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 43:8; Genesis 45:9; Genesis 43:2"> Behold, I have heard that there is corn in Egypt; go down thither, and buy for us a little food, that we may live, and not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 42:5; Genesis 42:13"> And the ten brethren of Joseph went down to buy corn out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 42:38; Genesis 43:29; Genesis 11:4"> But Jacob sent not Benjamin, the brother of Joseph, with his brethren; for he said, Lest, haply, disease befal him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 41:57; Acts 7:11; Genesis 12:10"> And the sons of Israel came to buy with those that came, for the famine was in the land of Chanaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 3:9; Genesis 37:7–10; Genesis 41:40–41"> And Joseph was ruler of the land; he sold to all the people of the land. And the brethren of Joseph, having come, did reverence to him, bowing with the face to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 42:30; Genesis 42:14–17; Matthew 15:23–26"> And when Joseph saw his brethren, he knew them, and estranged himself from them, and spoke hard words to them; and said to them, Whence are ye come? And they said, Out of the land of Chanaan, to buy food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 37:2; John 20:14; Luke 24:16"> And Joseph knew his brethren, but they knew not him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 37:5–9; Exodus 32:35; Joshua 2:1"> And Joseph remembered his dream, which he saw; and he said to them, Ye are spies; to observe the marks of the land are ye come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 37:8; Kings I 26:17; Genesis 27:37"> But they said, Nay, Sir, we thy servants are come to buy food;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 42:19; 2 Corinthians 6:4; John 7:18"> we are all sons of one man; we are peaceable, thy servants are not spies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And he said to them, Nay, but ye are come to observe the marks of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 37:30; Genesis 42:32; Genesis 44:20"> And they said, We thy servants are twelve brethren, in the land of Chanaan; and, behold, the youngest is with our father to-day, but the other one is not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 15:21–28; Job 13:24; Genesis 42:9–11"> And Joseph said to them, This is it that I spoke to you, saying, ye are spies;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings I 17:55; Jeremias 5:7; Genesis 42:16"> herein shall ye be manifested; by the health of Pharao, ye shall not depart hence, unless your younger brother come hither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 42:11"> Send one of you, and take your brother; and go ye to prison, till your words be clear, whether ye speak the truth or not; but, if not, by the health of Pharao, verily ye are spies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 40:4; Hebrews 12:10; Genesis 40:7"> And he put them in prison three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 25:43; Nehemiah 5:15; Luke 18:2"> And he said to them on the third day, This do, and ye shall live, for I fear God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 45:23; Esaias 42:7; Genesis 40:3"> If ye be peaceable, let one of your brethren be detained in prison; but go ye, and carry back the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. the purchase of your gift of corn.">a</a></sup>corn ye have purchased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 44:23; Genesis 43:5; Genesis 42:34"> And bring your younger brother to me, and your words shall be believed; but, if not, ye shall die. And they did so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Osee 5:15; Mark 9:43–45; Genesis 37:23–28"> And each said to his brother, Yes, indeed, for we are in fault concerning our brother, when we disregarded the anguish of his soul, when he besought us, and we hearkened not to him; and therefore has this affliction come upon us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Chronicles II 24:22; Psalms 9:12; Kings III 2:32"> And Ruben answered them, saying, Did I not speak to you, saying, Hurt not the boy, and ye heard me not? and, behold, his blood is required.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:20; John 16:13–14"> But they knew not that Joseph<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. heard them.">b</a></sup>understood them; for there was an interpreter between them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 43:30; Jude 1:22–23; Hebrews 4:15"> And Joseph turned away from them, and wept; and again he came to them, and spoke to them; and he took Symeon from them, and bound him before their eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Romans 12:17–21; Genesis 45:21"> And Joseph gave orders to fill their vessels with corn, and to return their money to each into his sack, and to give them provision for the way; and it was so done to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And having put the corn on the asses, they departed thence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 4:24; Luke 2:7; Luke 10:34"> And one having opened his sack to give his asses fodder, at the place where they rested, saw also his bundle of money, for it was on the mouth of his sack.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Esaias 45:7; Lamentations 3:37; Genesis 42:36"> And he said to his brethren, My money has been restored to me, and behold this is in my sack. And their heart was wonder-struck, and they were troubled, saying one to another, What is this that God has done to us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And they came to their father, Jacob, into the land of Chanaan, and reported to him all that had happened to them, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 42:7"> The man, the lord of the land, spoke harsh words to us, and put us in prison as spies of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 42:11"> And we said to him, We are men of peace, we are not spies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> We are twelve brethren, sons of our father; one is not, and the youngest is with his father to-day in the land of Chanaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 42:19–20; Genesis 42:15"> And the man, the lord of the land, said to us, Herein shall I know that ye are peaceable; leave one brother here with me, and having taken the corn ye have purchased for your family, depart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 34:10; Genesis 34:21; Kings III 10:15"> And bring to me your younger brother; then I shall know that ye are not spies, but that ye are men of peace: and I will restore you your brother, and ye shall trade in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 43:21; Genesis 43:12; Genesis 43:15"> And it came to pass as they were emptying their sacks, there was each man's bundle of money in his sack; and they and their father saw their bundles of money, and they were afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 43:14; Romans 8:31; Esaias 27:9"> And their father Jacob said to them, Ye have bereaved me. Joseph is not, Symeon is not, and will ye take Benjamin? all these things have come upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Genesis 46:9; Michæas 6:7; Genesis 43:9"> And Ruben spoke to his father, saying, Slay my two sons, if I bring him not to thee; give him into my hand, and I will bring him back to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 37:35; Genesis 42:4; Genesis 37:33"> But he said, My son shall not go down with you, because his brother is dead, and he only has been left; and suppose it shall come to pass that he is afflicted by the way by which ye go, then ye shall bring down my old age with sorrow to Hades.</span>
