<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 24:37–39; 2 Peter 2:5–9; Genesis 6:9"> And the Lord God said to Noe, Enter thou and all thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. house.">a</a></sup>family into the ark, for thee have I seen righteous before me in this generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 8:20; Leviticus 10:10; Leviticus 11:1–47"> And of the clean cattle take in to thee sevens, male and female, and of the unclean cattle pairs male and female.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And of clean flying creatures of the sky sevens, male and female, and of all unclean flying creatures pairs, male and female, to maintain seed on all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 6:13; Genesis 7:12; Genesis 7:17"> For yet seven days having passed I bring rain upon the earth forty days and forty nights, and I will blot out every offspring which I have made from the face of all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 6:22; Luke 8:21; Exodus 39:32"> And Noe did all things whatever the Lord God commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 5:32; Genesis 8:13"> And Noe was six hundred years old when the flood of water was upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 17:27; Genesis 6:18; Matthew 24:38"> And then went in Noe and his sons and his wife, and his sons' wives with him into the ark, because of the water of the flood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And of clean flying creatures and of unclean flying creatures, and of clean cattle and of unclean cattle, and of all things that creep upon the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 7:16; Genesis 2:19; Jeremias 8:7"> pairs went in to Noe into the ark, male and female, as God commanded Noe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 6:17; Genesis 7:4; Luke 17:27"> And it came to pass after the seven days that the water of the flood came upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 8:2; Malachias 3:10; Proverbs 8:28–29"> In the six hundredth year of the life of Noe, in the second month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, on this day all the fountains of the abyss were broken up, and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, bars, or, cataracts.">b</a></sup>flood-gates of heaven were opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; Matthew 4:2; Genesis 7:17"> And the rain was upon the earth forty days and forty nights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 6:18; Genesis 7:1; Genesis 6:10"> On that very day entered Noe, Sem, Cham, Japheth, the sons of Noe, and the wife of Noe, and the three wives of his sons with him into the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 7:8–9; Genesis 7:2–3"> And all the wild beasts after their kind, and all cattle after their kind, and every reptile moving itself on the earth after its kind, and every flying bird after its kind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 6:19–20; Esaias 11:6"> went in to Noe into the ark, pairs, male and female of all flesh in which is the breath of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 25:10; Luke 13:25; Psalms 91:1–10"> And they that entered went in male and female of all flesh, as God commanded Noe, and the Lord God shut the ark outside of him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; Genesis 7:12"> And the flood was upon the earth forty days and forty nights, and the water abounded greatly and bore up the ark, and it was lifted on high from off the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 14:28; Psalms 69:15; Job 22:16"> And the water prevailed and abounded exceedingly upon the earth, and the ark was borne upon the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 104:6–9; Psalms 46:2–3; 2 Peter 3:6"> And the water prevailed exceedingly upon the earth, and covered all the high mountains which were under heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremias 3:23; Psalms 104:6"> Fifteen cubits upwards was the water raised, and it covered all the high mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 6:13; Genesis 7:4; Genesis 6:17"> And there died all flesh that moved upon the earth, of flying creatures and cattle, and of wild beasts, and every reptile moving upon the earth, and every man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 2:7; Genesis 6:17"> And all things which have the breath of life, and whatever was on the dry land, died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Peter 2:5; 1 Peter 3:20; Hebrews 11:7"> And God blotted out every offspring which was upon the face of the earth, both man and beast, and reptiles, and birds of the sky, and they were blotted out from the earth, and Noe was left alone, and those with him in the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 8:3–4"> And the water was raised over the earth an hundred and fifty days.</span>
