<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 12:10; Acts 7:11–13; Lamentations 5:10"> But the famine prevailed in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:6–8; Proverbs 16:18; Proverbs 15:16"> And it came to pass, when they had finished eating the corn which they had brought out of Egypt, that their father said to them, Go again; buy us a little food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 44:23; Genesis 43:5; Kings II 3:13"> And Judas spoke to him, saying, The man, the lord of the country, positively testified to us, saying, Ye shall not see my face, unless your younger brother be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> If, then, thou send our brother with us, we will go down, and buy thee food;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Genesis 44:26; Genesis 42:38"> but if thou send not our brother with us, we will not go: for the man spoke to us, saying, Ye shall not see my face, unless your younger brother be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And Israel said, Why did ye harm me, inasmuch as ye told the man that ye had a brother?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 43:3; Genesis 42:13; Genesis 43:27"> And they said, The man closely questioned us about our family also, saying, Does your father yet live, and have ye a brother? and we answered him according to this question: did we know that he would say to us, Bring your brother?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 42:2; Deuteronomy 33:6; Genesis 45:19"> And Judas said to his father Israel, Send the boy with me, and we will arise and go, that we may live and not die, both we and thou, and our store.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 42:37; Philemon 1:18–19; Hebrews 7:22"> And I engage for him; at my hand do thou require him; if I bring him not to thee, and place him before thee, I shall be guilty toward thee for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 19:16"> For if we had not tarried, we should now have returned twice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 37:25; Jeremias 8:22; Jezekiel 27:17"> And Israel, their father, said to them, If it be so, do this; take of the fruits of the earth in your vessels, and carry down to the man presents of gum and honey, and frankincense, and stacte, and turpentine, and walnuts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 42:25; Genesis 42:35; 1 Thessalonians 4:6"> And take double money in your hands, and the money that was returned in your sacks, carry back with you, lest peradventure it is a mistake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 42:38"> And take your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. one.">a</a></sup>brother; and arise, go down to the man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esther 4:16; Genesis 17:1; Acts 21:14"> And my God give you favour in the sight of the man, and send away your other brother, and Benjamin, for I accordingly as I have been bereaved, am bereaved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the men having taken these presents, and the double money, took in their hands also Benjamin; and they rose up and went down to Egypt, and stood before Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 44:1; Genesis 43:19; Genesis 24:2–10"> And Joseph saw them and his brother Benjamin, born of the same mother; and he said to the steward of his household, Bring the men into the house, and slay beasts and make ready, for the men are to eat bread with me at noon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the man did as Joseph said; and he brought the men into the house of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 42:35; Romans 7:8; Genesis 42:21"> And the men, when they perceived that they were brought into the house of Joseph, said, We are brought in because of the money that was returned in our sacks at the first; even in order to inform against us, and lay it to our charge; to take us for servants, and our asses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And having approached the man who was over the house of Joseph, they spoke to him in the porch of the house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 42:3; Genesis 42:10; Genesis 42:27"> saying, We pray thee, Sir; we came down at first to buy food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 43:12; Genesis 42:27–35; Romans 13:8"> And it came to pass, when we came to unlade, and opened our sacks, there was also this money of each in his sack; we have now brought back our money by weight in our hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And we have brought other money with us to buy food; we know not who put the money into our sacks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 20:26; Genesis 42:24; John 14:27"> And he said to them, God deal mercifully with you; be not afraid; your God, and the God of your fathers, has given you treasures in your sacks, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="q. d. I am satisfied with the money you have given me, both as to quality and quantity.">b</a></sup>I have enough of your good money. And he brought Symeon out to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 18:4; Genesis 24:32; Luke 7:44"> And he brought water to wash their feet; and gave provender to their asses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 43:11; Genesis 43:16"> And they prepared their gifts, until Joseph came at noon, for they heard that he was going to dine there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Philippians 2:10–11; Genesis 27:29; Genesis 42:6"> And Joseph entered into the house, and they brought him the gifts which they had in their hands, into the house; and they did him reverence with their face to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 42:13; Kings I 25:5; Kings I 17:22"> And he asked them, How are ye? and he said to them, Is your father, the old man of whom ye spoke, well? Does he yet live?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 37:7; Genesis 43:26; Kings II 14:4"> And they said, Thy servant our father is well; he is yet alive. And he said, Blessed be that man by God;—and they bowed, and did him reverence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 42:13; Numbers 6:25; Genesis 30:22–24"> And Joseph<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. having looked up with, etc.">c</a></sup>lifted up his eyes, and saw his brother Benjamin, born of the same mother; and he said, Is this your younger brother, whom ye spoke of bringing to me? and he said, God have mercy on thee, my son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 42:24; Jeremias 31:20; Kings III 3:26"> And Joseph was troubled, for his bowels yearned over his brother, and he sought to weep; and he went into his chamber, and wept there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 45:1; 1 Peter 3:10; Esaias 42:14"> And he washed his face and came out, and refrained himself, and said, Set on bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 46:34; Exodus 8:26; Genesis 43:16"> And they set on bread for him alone, and for them by themselves, and for the Egyptians feasting with him by themselves, for the Egyptians could not eat bread with the Hebrews, for it is an abomination to the Egyptians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And they sat before him, the first-born according to his seniority, and the younger according to his youth; and the men<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. were amazed.">d</a></sup>looked with amazement every one at his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 45:22; Kings II 11:8; Kings I 1:5"> And they took their portions from him to themselves; but Benjamin's portion was<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. was magnified beyond the portions of all five times in comparison of theirs.">e</a></sup>five times as much as the portions of the others. And they drank and were filled with drink with him.</span>
