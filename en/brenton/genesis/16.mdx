<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 16:5; Genesis 12:4–5; Kings II 5:13"> So Sara the wife of Abram having taken Agar the Egyptian her handmaid, after Abram had dwelt ten years in the land of Chanaan, gave her to Abram her husband as a wife to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 30:23; Kings II 6:16; 1 Corinthians 13:4–5"> And he went in to Agar, and she conceived, and saw that she was with child, and her mistress was dishonoured before her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 31:53; Exodus 5:21; Psalms 7:8"> And Sara said to Abram, I am injured by thee; I gave my handmaid into thy bosom, and when I saw that she was with child, I was dishonoured before her. The Lord judge between me and thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremias 38:5; Proverbs 14:29; 1 Peter 3:7"> And Abram said to Sara, Behold thy handmaid is in thy hands, use her as it may seem good to thee. And Sara afflicted her, and she fled from her face.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 25:18; Exodus 15:22; Genesis 21:17"> And an angel of the Lord found her by the fountain of water in the wilderness, by the fountain in the way to Sur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 3:9; Genesis 4:10; Ecclesiastes 10:4"> And the angel of the Lord said to her, Agar, Sara's maid, whence comest thou, and whither goest thou? and she said, I am fleeing from the face of my mistress Sara.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5–6; Ecclesiastes 10:4; Titus 2:9"> And the angel of the Lord said to her, Return to thy mistress, and submit thyself under her hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 17:20; Psalms 83:6–7; Genesis 25:12–18"> And the angel of the Lord said to her, I will surely multiply thy seed, and it shall not be numbered for multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 2:23–24; Exodus 3:7; Esaias 7:14"> And the angel of the Lord said to her, Behold, thou art with child, and shalt bear a son, and shalt call his name Ismael, for the Lord hath hearkened to thy humiliation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 25:18; Genesis 21:20; Job 39:5–8"> He shall be a wild man, his hands against all, and the hands of all against him, and he shall dwell in the presence of all his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 139:1–12; Genesis 32:30; Proverbs 5:21"> And she called the name of the Lord God who spoke to her, Thou art God who seest me; for she said, For I have openly seen him that appeared to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 24:62; Genesis 25:11; Genesis 21:31"> Therefore she called the well, The well of him whom I have openly seen; behold it is between Cades and Barad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 25:12; Genesis 21:9–21; Genesis 17:25–26"> And Agar bore a son to Abram; and Abram called the name of his son which Agar bore to him, Ismael.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Abram was eighty-six years old, when Agar bore Ismael to Abram.</span>
