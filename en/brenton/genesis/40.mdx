<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 40:13; Genesis 39:20–23; Esther 6:1"> And it came to pass after these things, that the chief cupbearer of the king of Egypt and the chief baker trespassed against their lord the king of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 16:14; Proverbs 19:12; Psalms 76:10"> And Pharao was wroth with his two eunuchs, with his chief cupbearer, and with his chief baker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 39:20; Genesis 39:23"> And he put them in ward, into the prison, into the place whereinto Joseph had been led.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 39:1; Psalms 37:5; Genesis 37:36"> And the chief keeper of the prison committed them to Joseph, and he stood by them; and they were some days in the prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 41:11; Genesis 40:8; Genesis 37:5–10"> And they both<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. saw.">a</a></sup>had a dream in one night; and the vision of the dream of the chief cupbearer and chief baker, who belonged to the king of Egypt, who were in the prison, was this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 41:8; Daniel 2:1–3; Genesis 40:8"> Joseph went in to them in the morning, and saw them, and they had been troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:2; Kings I 1:8; Luke 24:17"> And he asked the eunuchs of Pharao who were with him in the prison with his master, saying, Why is it that your countenances are sad to-day?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 2:47; Genesis 41:15–16; Daniel 2:28"> And they said to him, We have seen a dream, and there is no interpreter of it. And Joseph said to them, Is not the interpretation of them through God? tell them then to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 7:13–15; Genesis 37:5–10; Daniel 4:8"> And the chief cupbearer related his dream to Joseph, and said, In my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. sleep.">b</a></sup>dream a vine was before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And in the vine were three stems; and it budding shot forth blossoms; the clusters of grapes were ripe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:11–1; Kings III 10:5; Chronicles II 9:4"> And the cup of Pharao was in my hand; and I took the bunch of grapes, and squeezed it into the cup, and gave the cup into Pharao's hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 41:12; Judges 7:14; Genesis 41:25–26"> And Joseph said to him, This is the interpretation of it. The three stems are three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 3:3; Jeremias 52:31; Kings IV 25:27"> Yet three days and Pharao shall remember thy office, and he shall restore thee to thy place of chief cupbearer, and thou shalt give the cup of Pharao into his hand, according to thy former high place, as thou wast wont to be cupbearer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 2:12; Luke 23:42; Kings I 20:14–15"> But remember me of thyself, when it shall be well with thee, and thou shalt deal mercifully with me, and thou shalt make mention of me to Pharao, and thou shalt bring me forth out of this dungeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:17–18; Genesis 39:20; 1 Timothy 1:10"> For surely I was stolen away out of the land of the Hebrews, and here I have done nothing, but they have cast me into this pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 40:1–2"> And the chief baker saw that he interpreted aright; and he said to Joseph, I also saw a dream, and methought I took up on my head three baskets of mealy food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 49:20; Chronicles I 12:20"> And in the upper basket there was the work of the baker of every kind which Pharao eats; and the fowls of the air ate them out of the basket that was on my head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 40:12; Genesis 41:26; 1 Corinthians 11:24"> And Joseph answered and said to him, This is the interpretation of it; The three baskets are three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 40:22; Acts 20:27; Kings II 21:6"> Yet three days, and Pharao shall take away thy head from off thee, and shall hang thee on a tree, and the birds of the sky shall eat thy flesh from off thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 40:19; Genesis 40:13; Matthew 14:6"> And it came to pass on the third day that it was Pharao's birth-day, and he made a banquet for all his servants, and he remembered the office of the cupbearer and the office of the baker in the midst of his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 40:13; Nehemiah 2:1"> And he restored the chief cupbearer to his office, and he gave the cup into Pharao's hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 40:19; Genesis 41:16; Daniel 2:30"> And he hanged the chief baker, as Joseph interpreted to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 31:12; Job 19:14; Psalms 105:19"> Yet did not the chief cupbearer remember Joseph, but forgot him.</span>
