<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Ephesians 3:20; Luke 1:6"> And Abram was ninety-nine years old, and the Lord appeared to Abram and said to him, I am thy God, be well-pleasing before me, and be blameless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 17:4–6; Genesis 12:2; Psalms 105:8–11"> And I will establish my covenant between me and thee, and I will multiply thee exceedingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 13:20; Genesis 17:17; Numbers 16:45"> And Abram fell upon his face, and God spoke to him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 35:11; Galatians 3:28–29; Genesis 12:2"> And I, behold! my covenant is with thee, and thou shalt be a father of a multitude of nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 1:21–23; Nehemiah 9:7; Genesis 17:15"> And thy name shall no more be called Abram, but thy name shall be Abraam, for I have made thee a father of many nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 17:4; Matthew 1:6–17; Genesis 35:11"> And I will increase thee very exceedingly, and I will make nations of thee, and kings shall come out of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 9:7–9; Acts 2:39; Genesis 26:24"> And I will establish my covenant between thee and thy seed after thee, to their generations, for an everlasting covenant, to be thy God, and the God of thy seed after thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 26:12; Psalms 105:11; Exodus 6:7"> And I will give to thee and to thy seed after thee the land wherein thou sojournest, even all the land of Chanaan for an everlasting possession, and I will be to them a God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esaias 56:4–5; Psalms 25:10; Psalms 103:18"> And God said to Abraam, Thou also shalt fully keep my covenant, thou and thy seed after thee for their generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 2:11–12; Romans 4:9–11; Acts 7:8"> And this is the covenant which thou shalt fully keep between me and you, and between thy seed after thee for their generations; every male of you shall be circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 4:11; Acts 7:8; Deuteronomy 10:16"> And ye shall be circumcised in the flesh of your foreskin, and it shall be for a sign of a covenant between me and you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 12:3; Luke 2:21; Exodus 12:48–49"> And the child of eight days old shall be circumcised by you, every male throughout your generations, and the servant born in the house and he that is bought with money, of every son of a stranger, who is not of thy seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 12:44; Genesis 37:36; Nehemiah 5:8"> He that is born in thy house, and he that is bought with money shall be surely circumcised, and my covenant shall be on your flesh for an everlasting covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 4:24–26; Numbers 15:30–31; Exodus 12:19"> And the uncircumcised male, who shall not be circumcised in the flesh of his foreskin on the eighth day, that soul shall be utterly destroyed from its family, for he has broken my covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings II 12:25; Genesis 32:28; Genesis 17:5"> And God said to Abraam, Sara thy wife—her name shall not be called Sara, Sarrha shall be her name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 35:11; Genesis 17:6; Esaias 49:23"> And I will bless her, and give thee a son of her, and I will bless him, and he shall become nations, and kings of nations shall be of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 11:16; Genesis 21:6; Genesis 18:12"> And Abraam fell upon his face, and laughed; and spoke in his heart, saying, Shall there be a child to one who is a hundred years old, and shall Sarrha who is ninety years old, bear?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 2:39; Psalms 41:12; Psalms 4:6"> And Abraam said to God, Let this Ismael live before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 1:13–20; Genesis 21:2–3; Kings IV 4:16–17"> And God said to Abraam, Yea, behold, Sarrha thy wife shall bear thee a son, and thou shalt call his name Isaac; and I will establish my covenant with him, for an everlasting covenant, to be a God to him and to his seed after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 21:18; Genesis 21:13; Genesis 25:12–18"> And concerning Ismael, behold, I have heard thee, and, behold, I have blessed him, and will increase him and multiply him exceedingly; twelve nations shall he beget, and I will make him a great nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 21:2–3; Genesis 26:2–5; Galatians 3:29"> But I will establish my covenant with Isaac, whom Sarrha shall bear to thee at this time, in the next year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 18:33; Genesis 35:9–15; Judges 6:21"> And he left off speaking with him, and God went up from Abraam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 119:60; Genesis 18:19; Genesis 17:10–14"> And Abraam took Ismael his son, and all his home-born servants, and all those bought with money, and every male of the men in the house of Abraam, and he circumcised their foreskins in the time of that day, according as God spoke to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 4:11; Genesis 17:17; Romans 4:19–20"> And Abraam was ninety-nine years old, when he was circumcised in the flesh of his foreskin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And Ismael his son was thirteen years old when he was circumcised in the flesh of his foreskin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 119:60; Genesis 22:3–4; Genesis 12:4"> And at the period of that day, Abraam was circumcised, and Ismael his son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 18:19"> and all the men of his house, both those born in the house, and those bought with money of foreign nations.</span>
