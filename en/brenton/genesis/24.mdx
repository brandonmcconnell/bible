<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 112:1–3; Galatians 3:9; Matthew 6:33"> And Abraam was old, advanced in days, and the Lord blessed Abraam in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 47:29; Genesis 39:4–6; Genesis 15:2"> And Abraam said to his servant the elder of his house, who had rule over all his possessions, Put thy hand under my thigh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 28:8"> and I will adjure thee by the Lord the God of heaven, and the God of the earth, that thou take not a wife for my son Isaac from the daughters of the Chananites, with whom I dwell, in the midst of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 28:2; Genesis 22:20–23; Genesis 12:7"> But thou shalt go instead to my country, where I was born, and to my tribe, and thou shalt take from thence a wife for my son Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 4:2; Exodus 9:2; Exodus 20:7"> And the servant said to him, Shall I carry back thy son to the land whence thou camest forth,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See the use of μή ποτε in a somewhat similar case, 2 Tim. 2. 25.">a</a></sup>if haply the woman should not be willing to return with me to this land?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 5:1; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Hebrews 10:39"> And Abraam said to him, Take heed to thyself that thou carry not my son back thither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 33:2; Genesis 13:15; Hebrews 1:14"> The Lord the God of heaven, and the God of the earth, who took me out of my father's house, and out of the land whence I sprang, who spoke to me, and who swore to me, saying, I will give this land to thee and to thy seed, he shall send his angel before thee, and thou shalt take a wife to my son from thence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 2:17–20; Numbers 30:5; Numbers 30:8"> And if the woman should not be willing to come with thee into this land, thou shalt be clear from my oath, only carry not my son thither again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 24:2"> And the servant put his hand under the thigh of his master Abraam, and swore to him concerning this matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 11:31; Deuteronomy 23:4; Genesis 27:43"> And the servant took ten camels of his master's camels, and he took of all the goods of his master with him, and he arose and went into Mesopotamia to the city of Nachor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings I 9:11; Exodus 2:16; John 4:7"> And he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Heb. caused to kneel down. Gr. caused to sleep.">b</a></sup>rested his camels without the city by the well of water towards evening, when damsels go forth to draw water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 24:27; Exodus 3:6; Genesis 26:24"> And he said, O Lord God of my master Abraam, prosper my way before me to day, and deal mercifully with my master Abraam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 24:43; Psalms 37:5; Exodus 2:16"> Lo! I stand by the well of water, and the daughters of them that inhabit the city come forth to draw water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 6:37; Judges 6:17; Genesis 15:8"> And it shall be, the virgin to whomsoever I shall say, Incline thy water-pot, that I may drink, and she shall say, Drink thou, and I will give thy camels drink, until they shall have done drinking—even this one thou hast prepared for thy servant Isaac, and hereby shall I know that thou hast dealt mercifully with my master Abraam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 11:29; Genesis 24:45; Esaias 65:24"> And it came to pass before he had done speaking in his mind, that behold, Rebecca the daughter of Bathuel, the son of Melcha, the wife of Nachor, and the same<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="i. e. Nachor.">c</a></sup>the brother of Abraam, came forth, having a water-pot on her shoulders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 26:7; Numbers 31:17–18; Genesis 39:6"> And the virgin was very beautiful in appearance, she was a virgin, a man had not known her; and she went down to the well, and filled her water-pot, and came up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 4:7; Esaias 21:14; Genesis 26:1–35"> And the servant ran up to meet her, and said, Give me a little water to drink out of thy pitcher;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 31:26; 1 Peter 3:8; Genesis 24:14"> and she said, Drink, Sir; and she hasted, and let down the pitcher upon her arm, and gave him to drink, till he ceased drinking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 24:14; 1 Peter 4:9; Genesis 24:45–46"> And she said, I will also draw water for thy camels, till they shall all have drunk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And she hasted, and emptied the water-pot into the trough, and ran to the well to draw again, and drew water for all the camels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 107:43; Genesis 24:12; Kings II 7:18–20"> And the man took great notice of her, and remained silent to know whether the Lord had made his way prosperous or not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jezekiel 16:11–12; Exodus 32:2–3; Genesis 23:15–16"> And it came to pass when all the camels ceased drinking, that the man took golden ear-rings, each of a drachm weight, and he put two bracelets on her hands, their weight was ten pieces of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And he asked her, and said, Whose daughter art thou? Tell me if there is room for us to lodge with thy father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 24:15; Genesis 22:23; Genesis 22:20"> And she said to him, I am the daughter of Bathuel the son of Melcha, whom she bore to Nachor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Esaias 32:8; 1 Peter 4:9; Judges 19:19–21"> And she said to him, We have both straw and much provender, and a place for resting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 24:48; Exodus 4:31; Genesis 24:52"> And the man being well pleased, worshipped the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 24:48; Genesis 24:12; Psalms 98:3"> and said, Blessed be the Lord the God of my master Abraam, who has not suffered his righteousness to fail, nor his truth from my master, and the Lord has brought me prosperously to the house of the brother of my lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 24:67; Genesis 24:55; Genesis 31:33"> And the damsel ran and reported to the house of her mother according to these words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 29:5; Genesis 29:13; Genesis 24:55"> And Rebecca had a brother whose name was Laban; and Laban ran out to meet the man, to the well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And it came to pass when he saw the ear-rings and the bracelets on the hands of his sister, and when he heard the words of Rebecca his sister, saying, Thus the man spoke to me, that he went to the man, as he stood by the camels at the well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ruth 3:10; Genesis 26:29; Psalms 115:15"> And he said to him, Come in hither, thou blessed of the Lord, why standest thou without, whereas I have prepared the house and a place for the camels?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Judges 19:21; Genesis 43:24; Genesis 18:4"> And the man entered into the house, and unloaded the camels, and gave the camels straw and provender, and water to wash his feet, and the feet of the men that were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 132:3–5; 1 Timothy 6:2; Ephesians 6:5–8"> And he set before them loaves to eat; but he said, I will not eat, until I have<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. spoken my words.">d</a></sup>told my errand. And he said, Speak on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And he said, I am a servant of Abraam;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 24:1; Genesis 13:2; Proverbs 10:22"> and the Lord has blessed my master greatly, and he is exalted, and he has given him sheep, and calves, and silver, and gold, servants and servant-maids, camels, and asses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 25:5; Genesis 21:10; Genesis 21:1–7"> And Sarrha my master's wife bore one son to my master after he had grown old; and he gave him whatever he had.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Genesis 27:46; Ezra and Nehemiah 9:1–3; Genesis 24:2–9"> And my master caused me to swear, saying, Thou shalt not take a wife to my son of the daughters of the Chananites, among whom I sojourn in their land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 24:4; Genesis 31:19; Genesis 12:1"> But thou shalt go to the house of my father, and to my tribe, and thou shalt take thence a wife for my son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 24:5"> And I said to my master, Haply the woman will not go with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Exodus 23:20; Psalms 16:8; Genesis 5:24"> And he said to me, The Lord God to whom I have been acceptable in his presence, himself shall send out his angel with thee, and shall prosper thy journey, and thou shalt take a wife for my son of my tribe, and of the house of my father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Genesis 24:8; Deuteronomy 29:12"> Then shalt thou be clear from my curse, for whensoever thou shalt have come to my tribe, and they shall not give her to thee, then shalt thou be clear from my oath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:11; Genesis 24:12–14; Ezra and Nehemiah 8:21"> And having come this day to the well, I said, Lord God of my master Abraam, if thou prosperest my journey on which I am now going,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Genesis 24:13–14"> behold, I stand by the well of water, and the daughters of the men of the city come forth to draw water, and it shall be that the damsel to whom I shall say, Give me a little water to drink out of thy pitcher,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Genesis 24:14; 1 Peter 3:8; Genesis 2:22"> and she shall say to me, Both drink thou, and I will draw water for thy camels, this shall be the wife whom the Lord has prepared for his own servant Isaac; and hereby shall I know that thou hast wrought mercy with my master Abraam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 7:7; Acts 12:12–17; Genesis 24:15–20"> And it came to pass before I had done speaking in my mind, straightway Rebecca came forth, having her pitcher on her shoulders; and she went down to the well, and drew water; and I said to her, Give me to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Genesis 24:18–19"> And she hasted and let down her pitcher on her arm<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. from herself.">e</a></sup>from her head, and said, Drink thou, and I will give thy camels drink; and I drank, and she gave the camels drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 45:13–14; Genesis 24:22–24; Esaias 62:3–5"> And I asked her, and said, Whose daughter art thou? tell me; and she said, I am daughter of Bathuel the son of Nachor, whom Melcha bore to him; and I put on her the ear-rings, and the bracelets on her hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Genesis 24:26–27; Psalms 48:14"> And being well-pleased I worshipped the Lord, and I blessed the Lord the God of my master Abraam, who has prospered me in a true way, so that I should take the daughter of my master's brother for his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Genesis 47:29; Joshua 2:14; Proverbs 3:3"> If then ye will deal mercifully and justly with my lord, tell me, and if not, tell me, that I may turn to the right hand or to the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Psalms 118:23; Genesis 31:24; Genesis 31:29"> And Laban and Bathuel answered and said, This matter has come forth from the Lord, we shall not be able to answer thee bad or good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Genesis 20:15; Genesis 24:15; Kings II 16:10"> Behold, Rebecca is before thee, take her and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. run away.">f</a></sup>go away, and let her be wife to the son of thy master, as the Lord has said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Genesis 24:26; Genesis 24:48; Psalms 116:1–2"> And it came to pass when the servant of Abraam heard these words, he bowed himself to the Lord down to the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Genesis 24:22; Genesis 24:10; Exodus 12:35"> And the servant having brought forth jewels of silver and gold and raiment, gave them to Rebecca, and gave gifts to her brother, and to her mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Genesis 24:56; Genesis 24:59; Genesis 28:5–6"> And both he and the men with him ate and drank and went to sleep. And he arose in the morning and said, Send me away, that I may go to my master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Genesis 4:3; Leviticus 25:29; Judges 14:8"> And her brethren and her mother said, Let the virgin remain with us about ten days, and after that she shall depart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Proverbs 25:25; Joshua 1:8; Esaias 48:15"> But he said to them, Hinder me not, for the Lord has prospered my journey for me; send me away, that I may depart to my master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> And they said, Let us call the damsel, and enquire at her mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Luke 1:38; Psalms 45:10–11"> And they called Rebecca, and said to her, Wilt thou go with this man? and she said, I will go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Genesis 35:8; Numbers 11:12; Genesis 24:50"> So they sent forth Rebecca their sister, and her goods, and the servant of Abraam, and his attendants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Genesis 17:16; Ruth 4:11–12"> And they blessed Rebecca, and said to her, Thou art our sister; become thou thousands of myriads, and let thy seed possess the cities of their enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Psalms 45:10; Genesis 2:24; Esther 8:14"> And Rebecca rose up and her maidens, and they mounted the camels and went with the man; and the servant having taken up Rebecca, departed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Genesis 25:11; Genesis 16:14; Genesis 12:9"> And Isaac went through the wilderness to the well of the vision, and he dwelt in the land toward the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Psalms 119:15; Psalms 1:2; Psalms 104:34"> And Isaac went forth into the plain toward evening to meditate; and having lifted up his eyes, he saw camels coming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Judges 1:14; Joshua 15:18"> And Rebecca lifted up her eyes, and saw Isaac; and she alighted briskly from the camel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:9; 1 Corinthians 11:5–6; 1 Corinthians 11:10"> and said to the servant, Who is that man that walks in the plain to meet us? And the servant said, This is my master; and she took her veil and covered herself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Mark 6:30"> And the servant told Isaac all<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Lit. all the words which. Hebraism.">g</a></sup>that he had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Song of Solomon 8:2; Ephesians 5:22–33; Genesis 23:1–2"> And Isaac went into the house of his mother, and took Rebecca, and she became his wife, and he loved her; and Isaac was comforted for Sarrha his mother.</span>
