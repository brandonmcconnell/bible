<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 9:7; Genesis 1:28; Genesis 8:17"> And God blessed Noe and his sons, and said to them, Increase and multiply, and fill the earth and have dominion over it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 8:4–8; Genesis 1:28; James 3:7"> And the dread and the fear of you shall be upon all the wild beasts of the earth, on all the birds of the sky, and on all things moving upon the earth, and upon all the fishes of the sea, I have placed them under your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. hands.">a</a></sup>power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 14:14; Colossians 2:21–22; 1 Corinthians 10:31"> And every reptile which is living shall be to you for meat, I have given all things to you as the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. herbs of grass.">b</a></sup>green herbs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:23; Deuteronomy 15:23; Deuteronomy 12:16"> But flesh with blood of life ye shall not eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 21:28–29; Exodus 21:12; Genesis 4:9–10"> For your blood of your lives will I require at the hand of all wild beasts, and I will require the life of man at the hand of his brother man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 24:17; Matthew 26:52; Revelation 13:10"> He that sheds man's blood, instead of that blood shall his own be shed, for in the image of God I made man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 1:28; Genesis 9:1; Genesis 9:19"> But do ye increase and multiply, and fill the earth, and have dominion over it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And God spoke to Noe, and to his sons with him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 6:18; Esaias 54:9–10; Romans 1:3"> And behold I establish my covenant with you, and with your seed after you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 8:1; Genesis 9:15–16; Psalms 36:5–6"> and with every<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. living soul.">c</a></sup>living creature with you, of birds and of beasts, and with all the wild beasts of the earth, as many as are with you, of all that come out of the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esaias 54:9; Genesis 8:21–22; Genesis 7:21–23"> And I will establish my covenant with you and all flesh shall not any more die by the water of the flood, and there shall no more be a flood of water to destroy all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 17:11; Matthew 26:26–28; Exodus 12:13"> And the Lord God said to Noe, This is the sign of the covenant which I set between me and you, and between every living creature which is with you for perpetual generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jezekiel 1:28; Revelation 4:3; Revelation 10:1"> I set my bow in the cloud, and it shall be for a sign of covenant between me and the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And it shall be when I gather clouds upon the earth, that my bow shall be seen in the cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:9; Jezekiel 16:60; Esaias 54:8–10"> And I will remember my covenant, which is between me and you, and between every living soul in all flesh, and there shall no longer be water for a deluge, so as to blot out all flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esaias 54:8–10; Jeremias 32:40; Kings II 23:5"> And my bow shall be in the cloud, and I will look to remember the everlasting covenant between me and the earth, and between every living soul in all flesh, which is upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And God said to Noe, This is the sign of the covenant, which I have made between me<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. and between.">d</a></sup>and all flesh, which is upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 10:1; Genesis 10:6; Genesis 9:25–27"> Now the sons of Noe which came out of the ark, were Sem, Cham, Japheth. And Cham was father of Chanaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Chronicles I 1:4–28; Genesis 8:17; Genesis 5:32"> These three are the sons of Noe, of these were men scattered over all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:6; Proverbs 10:11; Esaias 28:24–26"> And Noe began to be a husbandman, and he planted a vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Ambacum 2:15–16; Proverbs 23:31–32"> And he drank of the wine, and was drunk, and was naked in his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 30:17; Galatians 6:1; Matthew 18:15"> And Cham the father of Chanaan saw the nakedness of his father, and he went out and told his two brothers without.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 6:1; Exodus 20:12; 1 Timothy 5:17"> And Sem and Japheth having taken a garment, put it on both their backs and went backwards, and covered the nakedness of their father; and their face was backward, and they saw not the nakedness of their father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And Noe recovered from the wine, and knew all that his younger son had done to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 9:23; Deuteronomy 27:16; Judges 1:28–30"> And he said, Cursed be the servant Chanaan, a slave shall he be to his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 144:15; Genesis 12:1–3; Luke 3:23–36"> And he said, Blessed be the Lord God of Sem, and Chanaan shall be his bond-servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 11:12; Romans 15:12; Hebrews 11:9–10"> May God make room for Japheth, and let him dwell in the habitations of Sem, and let Chanaan be his servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Noe lived after the flood three hundred and fifty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 5:27; Psalms 90:10; Genesis 5:32"> And all the days of Noe were nine hundred and fifty years, and he died.</span>
