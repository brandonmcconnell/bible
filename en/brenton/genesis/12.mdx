<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings II 7:9; Genesis 18:18; Galatians 3:14"> And I will make thee a great nation, and I will bless thee and magnify thy name, and thou shalt be blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 3:8; Genesis 22:18; Genesis 27:29"> And I will bless those that bless thee, and curse those that curse thee, and in thee shall all the tribes of the earth be blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 11:8; Genesis 11:31; Genesis 11:27"> And Abram went as the Lord spoke to him, and Lot departed with him, and Abram was seventy-five years old, when he went out of Charrhan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 14:14; Genesis 11:31; Hebrews 11:8–9"> And Abram took Sara his wife, and Lot the son of his brother, and all their possessions, as many as they had got, and every soul which they had got in Charrhan, and they went forth to go into the land of Chanaan.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Alex. + and came into the land of Chanaan. <span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 11:9; Deuteronomy 11:30; Genesis 35:4"> And Abram traversed the land lengthwise as far as the place Sychem, to the high oak, and the Chananites then inhabited the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Genesis 18:1; Genesis 13:15"> And the Lord appeared to Abram, and said to him, I will give this land to thy seed. And Abram built an altar there to the Lord, who appeared to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:31; Genesis 4:26; Genesis 21:33"> And he departed thence to the mountain eastward of Bæthel, and there he pitched his tent in Bæthel near the sea, and Aggai toward the east, and there he built an altar to the Lord, and called on the name of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 13:3; Genesis 24:62; Genesis 13:1"> And Abram departed and went and encamped in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 43:1; Genesis 26:1–3; Kings III 17:1–18"> And there was a famine in the land, and Abram went down to Egypt to sojourn there, because the famine prevailed in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 26:7; Genesis 29:17; Genesis 12:14"> And it came to pass when Abram drew nigh to enter into Egypt, Abram said to Sara his wife, I know that thou art a fair woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 20:11; Genesis 26:7; 1 John 1:8–10"> It shall come to pass then that when the Egyptians shall see thee, they shall say, This is his wife, and they shall slay me, but they shall save thee alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 26:7; Genesis 20:12–13; Genesis 20:2"> Say, therefore, I am his sister, that it may be well with me on account of thee, and my soul shall live because of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 6:2; Matthew 5:28; Genesis 39:7"> And it came to pass when Abram entered into Egypt—the Egyptians having seen his wife that she was very beautiful—</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 20:2; Genesis 41:1; Kings IV 18:21"> that the princes of Pharao saw her, and praised her to Pharao and brought her into the house of Pharao.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 20:14; Genesis 13:2; Genesis 24:35"> And they treated Abram well on her account, and he had sheep, and calves, and asses, and men-servants, and women-servants, and mules, and camels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Chronicles I 16:21; Genesis 20:18; Psalms 105:14–15"> And God afflicted Pharao with great and severe afflictions, and his house, because of Sara, Abram's wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 20:9–10; Genesis 26:9–11; Kings I 14:43"> And Pharao having called Abram, said, What is this thou hast done to me, that thou didst not tell me that she was thy wife?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Wherefore didst thou say, She is my sister? and I took her for a wife to myself; and now, behold, thy wife is before thee, take her and go quickly away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 105:14–15; Proverbs 21:1; Exodus 18:27"> And Pharao gave charge to men concerning Abram, to join in sending him forward, and his wife, and all that he had.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Alex. + and Lot with him.">b</a></sup></span>
