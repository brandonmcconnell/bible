<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 6:10; Kings I 23:16; Deuteronomy 3:28"> And it was reported to Jacob, saying, Behold, thy son Joseph cometh to thee; and Israel having strengthened himself, sat upon the bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 35:9–12; Exodus 6:3; Genesis 28:3"> And Jacob said to Joseph, My God appeared to me in Luza, in the land of Chanaan, and blessed me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Genesis 35:11; Genesis 26:4"> and said to me, Behold, I will increase thee, and multiply thee, and will make of thee multitudes of nations; and I will give this land to thee, and to thy seed after thee, for an everlasting possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 41:50–52; Joshua 14:4; Genesis 46:20"> Now then thy two sons, who were born to thee in the land of Egypt, before I came to thee into Egypt, are mine; Ephraim and Manasse, as Ruben and Symeon they shall be mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 14:4"> And the children which thou shalt beget hereafter, shall be in the name of their brethren; they shall be named after their inheritances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 35:16–19; Matthew 2:18; Genesis 35:9"> And as for me, when I came out of Mesopotamia of Syria, Rachel, thy mother, died in the land of Chanaan, as I drew nigh to the horse-course of Chabratha of the land of Chanaan, so as to come to Ephratha; and I buried her in the road of the course; this is Bethlehem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And when Israel saw the sons of Joseph, he said, Who are these to thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 27:4; Genesis 33:5; Hebrews 11:21"> And Joseph said to his father, They are my sons, whom God gave me here; and Jacob said, Bring me them, that I may bless them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 27:1; Genesis 27:27; Kings I 3:2"> Now the eyes of Israel were dim through age, and he could not see; and he brought them near to him, and he kissed them, and embraced them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 3:20; Genesis 45:26; Genesis 42:36"> And Israel said to Joseph, Behold, I have not been deprived of seeing thy face, and lo! God has showed me thy seed also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 42:6; Leviticus 19:32; Genesis 19:1"> And Joseph brought them out from between his knees, and they did reverence to him, with their face to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And Joseph took his two sons, both Ephraim in his right hand, but on the left of Israel, and Manasse on his left hand, but on the right of Israel, and brought them near to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 41:51; Psalms 118:16; Matthew 19:15"> But Israel having stretched out his right hand, laid it on the head of Ephraim, and he was the younger; and his left hand on the head of Manasse, guiding his hands crosswise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 17:1; Colossians 2:6; Esaias 33:16"> And he blessed them and said, The God in whose sight my fathers were well pleasing, even Abraam and Isaac, the God who continues to feed me from my youth until this day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 34:22; Genesis 49:22; Genesis 48:5"> the angel who delivers me from all evils, bless these boys, and my name shall be called upon them, and the name of my fathers, Abraam and Isaac; and let them be increased to a great multitude on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 48:14; Romans 9:7–8; Chronicles I 21:7"> And Joseph having seen that his father put his right hand on the head of Ephraim—it seemed grievous to him; and Joseph took hold of the hand of his father, to remove it from the head of Ephraim to the head of Manasse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 19:18; Genesis 29:26; Genesis 43:33"> And Joseph said to his father, Not so, father; for this is the first-born; lay thy right-hand upon his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:17; Numbers 1:33–35; Genesis 25:28"> And he would not, but said, I know it, son, I know it; he also shall be a people, and he shall be exalted, but his younger brother shall be greater than he, and his seed shall become a multitude of nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 24:60; Ruth 4:11–12; Genesis 28:3"> And he blessed them in that day, saying, In you shall Israel be blessed, saying, God make thee as Ephraim and Manasse; and he set Ephraim before Manasse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 50:24; Genesis 46:4; Genesis 28:15"> And Israel said to Joseph, Behold, I die; and God shall be with you, and restore you to the land of your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 4:5; Joshua 24:32; Chronicles I 5:2"> And I give to thee Sicima, a select portion above thy brethren, which I took out of the hand of the Amorites with my sword and bow.</span>
