<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 18:22; Genesis 18:1–5; Hebrews 13:2"> And the two angels came to Sodom at evening. And Lot sat by the gate of Sodom, and Lot having seen them, rose up to meet them, and he worshipped with his face to the ground, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 18:4; Luke 24:28–29; Hebrews 13:2"> Lo! my lords, turn aside to the house of your servant, and rest from your journey, and wash your feet, and having risen early in the morning ye shall depart on your journey. And they said, Nay, but we will lodge in the street.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 18:6–8; Judges 6:19; Kings I 28:24"> And he constrained them, and they turned aside to him, and they entered into his house, and he made a feast for them, and baked unleavened cakes for them, and they did eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 13:13; Proverbs 4:16; Exodus 23:2"> But before they went to sleep, the men of the city, the Sodomites, compassed the house, both young and old, all the people together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 19:22; Esaias 3:9; Jude 1:7"> And they called out Lot, and said to him, Where are the men that went in to thee this night? bring them out to us that we may be with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 19:23"> And Lot went out to them to the porch, and he shut the door after him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–11; Judges 19:23; Leviticus 20:13"> and said to them, By no means, brethren, do not act villanously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 19:24; Genesis 19:31–38; Judges 9:15"> But I have two daughters, who have not known a man. I will bring them out to you, and do ye use them as it may please you, only do not injury to these men, to avoid which they came under the shelter of my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Lit. beams.">a</a></sup>roof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 2:14; 2 Peter 2:7–8; Genesis 13:12"> And they said to him, Stand back there, thou camest in to sojourn, was it also to judge? Now then we would harm thee more than them. And they pressed hard on the man, even Lot, and they drew nigh to break the door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And the men stretched forth their hands and drew Lot in to them into the house, and shut the door of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings IV 6:18; Acts 13:11; Deuteronomy 28:28–29"> And they smote the men that were at the door of the house with blindness, both small and great, and they were wearied with seeking the door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 7:1; 2 Peter 2:7; Revelation 18:4"> And the men said to Lot, Hast thou here sons-in-law, or sons or daughters, or if thou hast any other friend in the city, bring them out of this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 18:20; Matthew 13:49–50; Jude 1:7"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Note, Lam. 3. 21.">b</a></sup>For we are going to destroy this place; for their cry has been raised up before the Lord, and the Lord has sent us to destroy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="αὐτήν. Scil. πόλιν.">c</a></sup>it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 16:21; Jeremias 51:6; Numbers 16:45"> And Lot went out, and spoke to his sons-in-law who had married his daughters, and said, Rise up, and depart out of this place, for the Lord is about to destroy the city; but he seemed to be speaking absurdly before his sons-in-law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 16:24–27; Revelation 18:4; Genesis 19:22"> But when it was morning, the angels hastened Lot, saying, Arise and take thy wife, and thy two daughters whom thou hast, and go forth; lest thou also be destroyed with the iniquities of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 9:15–16; Titus 3:5; John 6:44"> And they were troubled, and the angels laid hold on his hand, and the hand of his wife, and the hands of his two daughters, in that the Lord spared him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 24:16–18; Luke 9:62; Philippians 3:13–14"> And it came to pass when they brought them out, that they said, Save thine own life by all means; look not round to that which is behind, nor stay in all the country round about, escape to the mountain, lest perhaps thou be overtaken together with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 13:6–8; Acts 10:14; Genesis 32:26"> And Lot said to them, I pray, Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings I 27:1; Psalms 103:1–22; 1 Timothy 1:14–16"> since thy servant has found mercy before thee, and thou hast magnified thy righteousness, in what thou doest towards me that my soul may live,—but I shall not be able to escape to the mountain, lest perhaps the calamity overtake me and I die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 119:175; Proverbs 3:5–7; Genesis 19:30"> Behold this city is near for me to escape thither, which is a small one, and there shall I be preserved, is it not little? and my soul shall live because of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 145:19; Psalms 34:15; Psalms 102:17"> And he said to him, Behold, I have had respect to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. thy countenance.">d</a></sup>thee also about this thing, that I should not overthrow the city about which thou hast spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 13:10; Genesis 14:2; Genesis 32:25–28"> Hasten therefore to escape thither, for I shall not be able to do anything until thou art come thither; therefore he called the name of that city, Segor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> The sun was risen upon the earth, when Lot entered into Segor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:23; Esaias 13:19; Amos 4:11"> And the Lord rained on Sodom and Gomorrha brimstone and fire from the Lord out of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 107:34; Genesis 14:3; Genesis 13:10"> And he overthrew these cities, and all the country round about, and all that dwelt in the cities, and the plants springing out of the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 17:31–32; Genesis 19:17; Proverbs 14:14"> And his wife looked back, and she became a pillar of salt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ambacum 2:1; Psalms 5:3; Hebrews 2:1"> And Abraam rose up early to go to the place, where he had stood before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 18:9; Revelation 9:2; Revelation 21:8"> And he looked towards Sodom and Gomorrha, and towards the surrounding country, and saw, and behold a flame went up from the earth, as the smoke of a furnace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 8:1; Deuteronomy 9:5; Genesis 18:23–33"> And it came to pass that when God destroyed all the cities of the region round about, God remembered Abraam, and sent Lot out of the midst of the overthrow, when the Lord overthrew those cities in which Lot dwelt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 19:19; Esaias 15:5; James 1:8"> And Lot went up out of Segor, and dwelt in the mountain, he and his two daughters with him, for he feared to dwell in Segor; and he dwelt in a cave, he and his two daughters with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5; Esaias 4:1; Genesis 16:2"> And the elder said to the younger, Our father is old, and there is no one on the earth who shall come in to us, as it is fit in all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 18:6–7; Genesis 11:3; Genesis 9:21"> Come and let us make our father drink wine, and let us sleep with him, and let us raise up seed from our father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ambacum 2:15–16; Proverbs 23:29–35; Proverbs 20:1"> So they made their father drink wine in that night, and the elder went in and lay with her father that night, and he knew not when he slept and when he rose up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jeremias 8:12; Jeremias 6:15; Esaias 3:9"> And it came to pass on the morrow, that the elder said to the younger, Behold, I slept yesternight with our father, let us make him drink wine in this night also, and do thou go in and sleep with him, and let us raise up seed of our father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 24:16; Psalms 8:4; 1 Peter 4:7"> So they made their father drink wine in that night also, and the younger went in and slept with her father, and he knew not when he slept, nor when he arose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ambacum 2:15; Judges 1:7; Matthew 7:2"> And the two daughters of Lot conceived by their father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:9; Kings IV 3:1–27; Numbers 21:29"> And the elder bore a son, and called his name Moab, saying, He is of my father. This is the father of the Moabites to this present day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:19; Judges 10:6–40; Psalms 83:4–8"> And the younger also bore a son, and called his name Amman, saying, The son of my family. This is the father of the Ammanites to this present day.</span>
