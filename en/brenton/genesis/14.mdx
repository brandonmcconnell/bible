<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:22; Deuteronomy 2:12; Genesis 21:21"> And the Chorrhæans in the mountains of Seir, to the turpentine tree of Pharan, which is in the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Chronicles II 20:2; Genesis 16:14; Genesis 20:1"> And having turned back they came to the well of judgment; this is Cades, and they cut in pieces all the princes of Amalec, and the Amorites dwelling in Asasonthamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 19:22; Genesis 13:10; Genesis 19:20"> And the king of Sodom went out, and the king of Gomorrha, and king of Adama, and king of Seboim, and king of Balac, this is Segor, and they set themselves in array against them for war in the salt valley,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> against Chodollogomor king of Elam, and Thargal king of nations, and Amarphal king of Sennaar, and Arioch king of Ellasar, the four kings against the five.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 19:17; Genesis 19:30; Esaias 24:18"> Now the salt valley consists of slime-pits. And the king of Sodom fled and the king of Gomorrha, and they fell in there: and they that were left fled to the mountain country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 14:21; Genesis 14:16; Deuteronomy 28:31"> And they took all the cavalry of Sodom and Gomorrha, and all their provisions, and departed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 12:5; Genesis 11:27; Revelation 3:19"> And they took also Lot the son of Abram's brother, and his baggage, and departed, for he dwelt in Sodom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 13:18; Genesis 14:24; Genesis 40:15"> And one of them that had been rescued came and told Abram the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. passer. Heb. עֹבֹר.">b</a></sup>Hebrew; and he dwelt by the oak of Mamre the Amorite the brother of Eschol, and the brother of Aunan, who were confederates with Abram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 18:29; Genesis 15:3; Genesis 17:27"> And Abram having heard that Lot his nephew had been taken captive, numbered his own home-born servants three hundred and eighteen, and pursued after them to Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 9:2; Psalms 112:5; Kings III 15:18"> And he came upon them by night, he and his servants, and he smote them and pursued them as far as Choba, which is on the left of Damascus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings I 30:18–19; Kings I 30:8; Genesis 12:2"> And he recovered all the cavalry of Sodom, and he recovered Lot his nephew, and all his possessions, and the women and the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings II 18:18; Proverbs 19:4; Hebrews 7:1"> And the king of Sodom went out to meet him, after he returned from the slaughter of Chodollogomor, and the kings with him, to the valley of Saby; this was the plain of the kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 6:20–3; Psalms 110:4; Hebrews 5:6"> And Melchisedec king of Salem brought forth loaves and wine, and he was the priest of the most high God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 7:6–7; Genesis 14:22; Psalms 24:1"> And he blessed Abram, and said, Blessed be Abram of the most high God, who made heaven and earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 28:22; Hebrews 7:4–9; Malachias 3:10"> and blessed be the most high God who delivered thine enemies into thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. under hand to thee.">c</a></sup>power. And Abram gave him the tithe of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And the king of Sodom said to Abram, Give me the men, and take the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or, cavalry. Heb. הרכש.">d</a></sup>horses to thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 12:7; Daniel 4:34; Deuteronomy 32:40"> And Abram said to the king of Sodom, I will stretch out my hand to the Lord the most high God, who made the heaven and the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings IV 5:16; 2 Corinthians 11:9–12; Esther 9:15–16"> that I will not take from all thy goods from a string to a shoe-latchet, lest thou shouldest say, I have made Abram rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 14:13; Proverbs 3:27; Matthew 7:12"> Except what things the young men have eaten, and the portion of the men that went with me, Eschol, Aunan, Mambre, these shall take a portion.</span>
