<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jezekiel 19:1; Jeremias 9:10; Jeremias 7:29"> Hear ye this word of the Lord, even a lamentation, which I take up against you. The house of Israel is fallen; it shall no more rise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 14:17; Lamentations 2:13; Jeremias 2:27"> The virgin of Israel has fallen upon his land; there is none that shall raise her up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Esaias 10:22; Jezekiel 12:16; Esaias 6:13"> Therefore thus saith the Lord God; The city out of which there went forth a thousand, in it there shall be left a hundred, and in that out of which there went forth a hundred, there shall be left ten to the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Chronicles II 15:2; Sophonias 2:3; Jeremias 29:12–13"> Wherefore thus saith the Lord to the house of Israel, Seek ye me, and ye shall live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Osee 4:15; Amos 4:4; Amos 8:14"> But seek not Bæthel, and go not in to Galgala, and cross not over to the Well of the Oath: for Galgala shall surely go into captivity, and Bæthel shall be as that which is not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jezekiel 33:11; Esaias 55:6; Jezekiel 20:47–48"> Seek ye the Lord, and ye shall live; lest the house of Joseph blaze as fire, and it devour him, and there shall be none to quench it for the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 6:12; Amos 5:11–12; Sophonias 1:6"> It is he that executes judgment in the height above, and he has established justice on the earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 4:13; Job 9:9; Amos 9:6"> who makes all things, and changes them, and turns darkness into the morning, and darkens the day into night: who calls for the water of the sea, and pours it out on the face of the earth: the Lord is his name:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings IV 13:17; Jeremias 37:10; Michæas 5:11"> who dispenses ruin to strength, and brings distress upon the fortress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esaias 29:21; Kings III 22:8; John 8:45–47"> They hated him that reproved in the gates, and abhorred holy speech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:30; Michæas 6:15; Sophonias 1:13"> Therefore because they have smitten the poor with their fists, and ye have received of them choice gifts; ye have built polished houses, but ye shall not dwell in them; ye have planted desirable vineyards, but ye shall not drink the wine of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 2:6–7; Amos 5:10; Esaias 1:23"> For I know your many transgressions, and your sins are great, trampling on the just, taking bribes, and turning aside the judgment of the poor in the gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Osee 4:4; 2 Timothy 3:1; Ecclesiastes 3:7"> Therefore the prudent shall be silent at that time; for it is a time of evils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Matthew 6:33; Michæas 6:8"> Seek good, and not evil, that ye may live: and so the Lord God Almighty shall be with you, as ye have said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 12:9; 3 John 1:11; Psalms 97:10"> We have hated evil, and loved good: and restore ye judgment in the gates; that the Lord God Almighty may have mercy on the remnant of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joel 1:11; Jeremias 9:17–20; Jeremias 9:10"> Therefore thus saith the Lord God Almighty; In all the streets shall be lamentations; and in all the ways shall it be said, Woe, woe! the husbandman shall be called to mourning and lamentation, and to them that are skilled in complaining.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 12:12; Jeremias 48:33; Esaias 16:10"> And there shall be lamentation in all the ways; because I will pass through the midst of thee, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joel 1:15; Joel 2:1–2; Joel 2:31"> Woe to you that desire the day of the Lord! what is this day of the Lord to you? whereas it is darkness, and not light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esaias 24:17–18; Jeremias 15:2–3; Acts 28:4"> As if a man should flee from the face of a lion, and a bear should meet him; and he should spring into his house, and lean his hands upon the wall, and a serpent should bite him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Esaias 13:10; Jezekiel 34:12; Sophonias 1:15"> Is not the day of the Lord darkness, and not light? and is not this day gloom without brightness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esaias 1:11–16; Proverbs 28:9; Leviticus 26:31"> I hate, I reject your feasts, and I will not smell your meat-offerings in your general assemblies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Michæas 6:6–7; Amos 4:4–5; Esaias 66:3"> Wherefore if ye should bring me your whole-burnt-sacrifices and meat-offerings, I will not accept them: neither will I have respect to your grand peace-offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Amos 6:5; Amos 8:10; Amos 8:3"> Remove from me the sound of thy songs, and I will not hear the music of thine instruments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Michæas 6:8; Jeremias 22:3; Osee 6:6"> But let judgment roll down as water, and righteousness as an impassable torrent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 7:42–43; Jezekiel 20:8; Nehemiah 9:18"> Have ye offered to me victims and sacrifices, O house of Israel, forty years in the wilderness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Kings IV 23:12–13; Kings III 11:33; Leviticus 20:2–5"> Yea, ye took up the tabernacle of Moloch, and the star of your god Ræphan, the images of them which ye made for yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 7:43; Kings IV 17:6; Amos 4:13"> And I will carry you away beyond Damascus, saith the Lord, the Almighty God is his name.</span>
