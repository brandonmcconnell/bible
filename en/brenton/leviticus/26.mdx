<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 19:4; Deuteronomy 27:15; Numbers 33:52"> I am the Lord your God: ye shall not make to yourselves gods made with hands, or graven; neither shall ye rear up a pillar for yourselves, neither shall ye set up a stone for an object in your land to worship it: I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 19:30"> Ye shall keep my sabbaths, and reverence my sanctuaries: I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Esaias 1:19; Deuteronomy 11:13–15; Deuteronomy 28:1–14"> If ye will walk in my ordinances, and keep my commandments, and do them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zacharias 8:12; Psalms 67:6; Leviticus 25:21"> then will I give you the rain in its season, and the land shall produce its fruits, and the trees of the field shall yield their fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 9:13; Joel 2:26; Joel 2:19"> And your threshing time shall overtake the vintage, and your vintage shall overtake your seed time; and ye shall eat your bread to the full; and ye shall dwell safely upon your land, and war shall not go through your land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esaias 35:9; Sophonias 3:13; Psalms 147:14"> And I will give peace in your land, and ye shall sleep, and none shall make you afraid; and I will destroy the evil beasts out of your land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and ye shall pursue your enemies, and they shall fall before you with slaughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 23:10; Deuteronomy 28:7; Deuteronomy 32:30"> And five of you shall chase a hundred, and a hundred of you shall chase tens of thousands; and your enemies shall fall before you by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 17:6–7; Nehemiah 9:23; Jezekiel 16:62"> And I will look upon you, and increase you, and multiply you, and establish my covenant with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 25:22; Kings IV 19:29; Luke 12:17"> And ye shall eat that which is old and very old, and bring forth the old to make way for the new.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 25:8; Revelation 21:3; Exodus 29:45"> And I will set my tabernacle among you, and my soul shall not abhor you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; Jeremias 30:22; Jeremias 7:23"> and I will walk among you, and be your God, and ye shall be my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jezekiel 34:27; Leviticus 25:38; Psalms 116:16"> I am the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, where ye were slaves; and I broke the band of your yoke, and brought you forth openly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Leviticus 26:18; Jeremias 17:27"> But if ye will not hearken to me, nor obey these my ordinances,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings IV 17:15; Deuteronomy 31:16; Zacharias 7:11–13"> but disobey them, and your soul should loathe my judgments, so that ye should not keep all my commands, so as to break my covenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 31:8; Jeremias 5:17; Michæas 6:15"> then will I do thus to you: I will even bring upon you perplexity and the itch, and the fever that causes your eyes to waste away, and disease that consumes your life; and ye shall sow your seeds in vain, and your enemies shall eat them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 17:10; Proverbs 28:1; Psalms 53:5"> And I will set my face against you, and ye shall fall before your enemies, and they that hate you shall pursue you; and ye shall flee, no one pursuing you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 26:21; Leviticus 26:28; Leviticus 26:24"> And if ye still refuse to hearken to me, then will I chasten you yet more even seven times for your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:23; Kings III 17:1; Daniel 4:37"> And I will break down the haughtiness of your pride; and I will make your heaven iron, and your earth as it were brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 127:1; Deuteronomy 11:17; Esaias 49:4"> And your strength shall be in vain; and your land shall not yield its seed, and the tree of your field shall not yield its fruit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 26:18; Leviticus 26:27"> And if after this ye should walk perversely, and not be willing to obey me, I will further bring upon you seven plagues according to your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jezekiel 14:15; Deuteronomy 32:24; Judges 5:6"> And I will send upon you the wild beasts of the land, and they shall devour you, and shall consume your cattle: and I will make you few in number, and your ways shall be desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremias 5:3; Jeremias 2:30; Amos 4:6–12"> And if hereupon ye are not corrected, but walk perversely towards me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 18:26; Kings II 22:27; Job 9:4"> I also will walk with you with a perverse spirit, and I also will smite you seven times for your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 14:12; Jezekiel 5:17; Jeremias 29:17–18"> And I will bring upon you a sword avenging the cause of my covenant, and ye shall flee for refuge to your cities; and I will send out death against you, and ye shall be delivered into the hands of your enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Michæas 6:14; Esaias 3:1; Psalms 105:16"> When I afflict you with famine of bread, then ten women shall bake your loaves in one oven, and they shall render your loaves by weight; and ye shall eat, and not be satisfied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 26:24; Leviticus 26:21"> And if hereupon ye will not obey me, but walk perversely towards me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Esaias 59:18; Jeremias 21:5; Esaias 66:15"> then will I walk with you with a froward mind, and I will chasten you sevenfold according to your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jezekiel 5:10; Lamentations 4:10; Matthew 24:19"> And ye shall eat the flesh of your sons, and the flesh of your daughters shall ye eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Kings IV 23:20; Esaias 27:9; Jezekiel 6:13"> And I will render your pillars desolate, and will utterly destroy your wooden images made with hands; and I will lay your carcases on the carcases of your idols, and my soul shall loathe you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:3; Lamentations 1:10; Hebrews 10:26"> And I will lay your cities waste, and I will make your sanctuaries desolate, and I will not smell the savour of your sacrifices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremias 9:11; Jeremias 19:8; Jeremias 18:16"> And I will lay your land desolate, and your enemies who dwell in it shall wonder at it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Zacharias 7:14; Jezekiel 20:23; Deuteronomy 28:64–66"> And I will scatter you among the nations, and the sword shall come upon you and consume you; and your land shall be desolate, and your cities shall be desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:21; Leviticus 26:43; Leviticus 25:2–4"> Then the land shall enjoy its sabbaths all the days of its desolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Esaias 24:5–6; Romans 8:22"> And ye shall be in the land of your enemies; then the land shall keep its sabbaths, and the land shall enjoy its sabbaths all the days of its desolation: it shall keep sabbaths which it kept not among your sabbaths, when ye dwelt in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jezekiel 21:7; Esaias 30:17; Leviticus 26:17"> And to those who are left of you I will bring bondage into their heart in the land of their enemies; and the sound of a shaken leaf shall chase them, and they shall flee as fleeing from war, and shall fall when none pursues them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Joshua 7:12–13; Judges 2:14; Jeremias 37:10"> And brother shall disregard brother as in war, when none pursues; and ye shall not be able to withstand your enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:48; Esaias 27:13; Jeremias 42:17–18"> And ye shall perish among the Gentiles, and the land of your enemies shall devour you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Jezekiel 4:17; Jezekiel 24:23; Jezekiel 33:10"> And those who are left of you shall perish, because of their sins, and because of the sins of their fathers: in the land of their enemies shall they consume away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; Nehemiah 9:2–5; 1 John 1:8–10"> And they shall confess their sins, and the sins of their fathers, that they have transgressed and neglected me, and that they have walked perversely before me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Jezekiel 44:7; Chronicles II 32:26; Chronicles II 12:6–7"> and I walked with them with a perverse mind; and I will destroy them in the land of their enemies: then shall their uncircumcised heart be ashamed, and then shall they acquiesce in the punishment of their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 106:45; Exodus 6:5; Jezekiel 16:60"> And I will remember the covenant of Jacob, and the covenant of Isaac, and the covenant of Abraam will I remember.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Romans 8:7; Leviticus 26:15; John 7:7"> And I will remember the land, and the land shall be left of them; then the land shall enjoy her sabbaths, when it is deserted through them: and they shall accept the punishment of their iniquities, because they neglected my judgments, and in their soul loathed my ordinances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Romans 11:2; Nehemiah 9:31; Deuteronomy 4:29–31"> And yet not even thus, while they were in the land of their enemies, did I overlook them, nor did I loathe them so as to consume them, to break my covenant made with them; for I am the Lord their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Leviticus 25:38; Jezekiel 20:22; Jezekiel 20:9"> And I will remember their former covenant, when I brought them out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage before the nations, to be their God; I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Leviticus 27:34; Deuteronomy 12:1; Deuteronomy 6:1"> These are my judgments and my ordinances, and the law which the Lord gave between himself and the children of Israel, in the mount Sina, by the hand of Moses.</span>
</div>
