<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 6:7; Leviticus 11:44; Jezekiel 20:5"> Speak to the children of Israel, and thou shalt say to them, I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 20:23; Exodus 23:24; 1 Peter 4:2–4"> Ye shall not do according to the devices of Egypt, in which ye dwelt: and according to the devices of the land of Chanaan, into which I bring you, ye shall not do; and ye shall not walk in their ordinances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 18:2; Leviticus 18:26; Jezekiel 20:19"> Ye shall observe my judgments, and shall keep my ordinances, and shall walk in them: I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 10:5; Jezekiel 20:11; Galatians 3:12"> So ye shall keep all my ordinances, and all my judgments, and do them; which if a man do, he shall live in them: I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17–21; Leviticus 18:7–19; Leviticus 20:11–12"> No man shall draw nigh to any of his near kindred to uncover their nakedness; I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 20:11; Jezekiel 22:10; Leviticus 18:8–16"> Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy father, or the nakedness of thy mother, for she is thy mother; thou shalt not uncover her nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 20:11; 1 Corinthians 5:1; Deuteronomy 27:20"> Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy father's wife; it is thy father's nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17; Deuteronomy 27:22; Jezekiel 22:11"> The nakedness of thy sister by thy father or by thy mother, born at home or abroad, their nakedness thou shalt not uncover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> The nakedness of thy son's daughter, or thy daughter's daughter, their nakedness thou shalt not uncover; because it is thy nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of the daughter of thy father's wife; she is thy sister by the same father: thou shalt not uncover her nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 20:19; Exodus 6:20"> Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy father's sister, for she is near akin to thy father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy mother's sister, for she is near akin to thy mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 20:20"> Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy father's brother, and thou shalt not go in to his wife; for she is thy relation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 20:12; Genesis 38:26; Jezekiel 22:11"> Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy daughter-in-law, for she is thy son's wife, thou shalt not uncover her nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5; Leviticus 20:21; Matthew 22:24"> Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy brother's wife: it is thy brother's nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 20:14; Amos 2:7; Deuteronomy 27:23"> The nakedness of a woman and her daughter shalt thou not uncover; her son's daughter, and her daughter's daughter, shalt thou not take, to uncover their nakedness, for they are thy kinswomen: it is impiety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Malachias 2:15; Genesis 30:15; Genesis 29:28"> Thou shalt not take a wife in addition to her sister, as a rival, to uncover her nakedness in opposition to her, while she is yet living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 20:18; Leviticus 15:24; Jezekiel 22:10"> And thou shalt not go in to a woman under separation for her uncleanness, to uncover her nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:14; 1 Corinthians 6:9; Hebrews 13:4"> And thou shalt not lie with thy neighbour's wife, to defile thyself with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:31; Leviticus 20:2–5; Deuteronomy 18:10"> And thou shalt not give of thy seed to serve a ruler; and thou shalt not profane my holy name; I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 20:13; Romans 1:26–27; 1 Corinthians 6:9"> And thou shalt not lie with a man as with a woman, for it is an abomination.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 22:19; Leviticus 20:15–16; Leviticus 20:12"> Neither shalt thou lie with any quadruped for copulation, to be polluted with it: neither shall a woman present herself before any quadruped to have connexion with it; for it is an abomination.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:12; Leviticus 18:30; Leviticus 20:22–23"> Do not defile yourselves with any of these things; for in all these things the nations are defiled, which I drive out before you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 18:28; Leviticus 20:22–23; Jeremias 2:7"> and the land is polluted; and I have recompensed their iniquity to them because of it, and the land is aggrieved with them that dwell upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Leviticus 17:10; Leviticus 18:30"> And ye shall keep all my statutes and all my ordinances, and ye shall do none of these abominations; neither the native, nor the stranger that joins himself with you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:14; Leviticus 18:24; Deuteronomy 23:18"> (for all these abominations the men of the land did who were before you, and the land was defiled,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 3:16; Leviticus 20:22; Leviticus 18:25"> and lest the land be aggrieved with you in your polluting it, as it was aggrieved with the nations before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 20:6; Exodus 12:15; Leviticus 17:10"> For whosoever shall do any of these abominations, the souls that do them shall be destroyed from among their people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 20:23; Deuteronomy 18:9–12; Leviticus 22:9"> And ye shall keep mine ordinances, that ye may not do any of the abominable practices, which have taken place before your time: and ye shall not be polluted in them; for I am the Lord your God.</span>
