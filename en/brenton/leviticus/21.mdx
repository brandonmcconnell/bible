<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jezekiel 44:25; Leviticus 21:11; Leviticus 10:6–7"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying, Speak to the priests the sons of Aaron, and thou shalt tell them that they shall not defile themselves in their nation for the dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:13; Leviticus 18:6"> but they may mourn for a relative who is very near to them, for a father and mother, and sons and daughters, for a brother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and for a virgin sister that is near to one, that is not espoused to a man; for these one shall defile himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jezekiel 24:16–17"> He shall not defile himself suddenly among his people to profane himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:1; Jezekiel 44:20; Leviticus 19:27–28"> And ye shall not shave your head for the dead with a baldness on the top; and they shall not shave their beard, neither shall they make gashes on their flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 3:11; Leviticus 18:21; 1 Peter 2:9"> They shall be holy to their God, and they shall not profane the name of their God; for they offer the sacrifices of the Lord as the gifts of their God, and they shall be holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 21:13–14; Jezekiel 44:22; Leviticus 21:8"> They shall not take a woman who is a harlot and profaned, or a woman put away from her husband; for he is holy to the Lord his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 20:7–8; Leviticus 19:2; Leviticus 11:44–45"> And thou shalt hallow him; he offers the gifts of the Lord your God: he shall be holy, for I the Lord that sanctify them am holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 38:24; Leviticus 19:29; Jezekiel 9:6"> And if the daughter of a priest should be profaned to go a whoring, she profanes the name of her father: she shall be burnt with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 10:6–7; Leviticus 16:32; Matthew 26:65"> And the priest that is chief among his brethren, the oil having been poured upon the head of the anointed one, and he having been consecrated to put on the garments, shall not take the mitre off his head, and shall not rend his garments:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 19:14; Leviticus 21:1–2; Numbers 6:7"> neither shall he go in to any dead body, neither shall he defile himself for his father or his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 10:7; Exodus 29:6–7; Leviticus 8:9–12"> And he shall not go forth out of the sanctuary, and he shall not profane the sanctuary of his God, because the holy anointing oil of God is upon him: I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 21:7; 2 Corinthians 11:2; Revelation 14:4"> He shall take for a wife a virgin of his own tribe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 21:7; Jezekiel 44:22"> But a widow, or one that is put away, or profaned, or a harlot, these he shall not take; but he shall take for a wife a virgin of his own people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 11:16; 1 Corinthians 7:14; Nehemiah 13:23–29"> And he shall not profane his seed among his people: I am the Lord that sanctifies him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 65:4; Numbers 16:5; Leviticus 10:3"> Say to Aaron, A man of thy tribe throughout your generations, who shall have a blemish on him, shall not draw nigh to offer the gifts of his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2–3; Leviticus 22:19–25; Titus 1:7"> No man who has a blemish on him shall draw nigh; a man lame, blind, with his nose disfigured, or his ears cut,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> a man who has a broken hand or a broken foot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:1; Esaias 56:3"> or hump-backed, or blear-eyed, or that has lost his eye-lashes, or a man who has a malignant ulcer, or tetter, or one that has lost a testicle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 21:6; Leviticus 21:8; Leviticus 21:17"> Whoever of the seed of Aaron the priest has a blemish on him, shall not draw nigh to offer sacrifices to thy God, because he has a blemish on him; he shall not draw nigh to offer the gifts of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3; Numbers 18:19; Leviticus 24:8–9"> The gifts of God are most holy, and he shall eat of the holy things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 21:12; Jezekiel 44:9–14; Exodus 30:6–8"> Only he shall not approach the veil, and he shall not draw nigh to the altar, because he has a blemish; and he shall not profane the sanctuary of his God, for I am the Lord that sanctifies them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:18; Malachias 2:1–7; 2 Timothy 2:2"> And Moses spoke to Aaron and his sons, and to all the children of Israel.</span>
