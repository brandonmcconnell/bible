<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Peter 2:9; 2 Corinthians 1:12; 1 John 3:21"> And David fled from Navath in Rama, and comes into the presence of Jonathan; and he said, What have I done, and what is my fault, and wherein have I sinned before thy father, that he seeks my life?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings I 9:15; John 17:8; John 15:15"> And Jonathan said to him, Far be it from thee: thou shalt not die: behold, my father will not do any thing great or small<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. and will not uncover my ear.">a</a></sup>without discovering it to me; and why should my father hide this matter from me? this thing is not so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings I 25:26; Deuteronomy 6:13; Kings IV 2:6"> And David answered Jonathan, and said, Thy father knows surely that I have found grace in thy sight, and he said, Let not Jonathan know this, lest he refuse his consent: but as the Lord lives and thy soul lives, as I said, the space is filled up between me and death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And Jonathan said to David, What does thy soul desire, and what shall I do for thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 10:10; Kings I 19:2; Numbers 28:11"> And David said to Jonathan, Behold, to-morrow is the new moon, and I shall not on any account sit down to eat, but thou shalt let me go, and I will hide in the plain till the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings I 17:58; Kings I 9:12; Deuteronomy 12:5"> And if thy father do in anywise<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, notice me as present or absent.">b</a></sup>enquire for me, then shalt thou say, David earnestly asked leave of me to run to Bethleem his city, for there is there a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. sacrifice of days. Hebraism.">c</a></sup>yearly sacrifice for all the family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings I 25:17; Esther 7:7; Deuteronomy 1:23"> If he shall say thus, Well,—<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. peace to thy servant.">d</a></sup>all is safe for thy servant: but if he shall answer harshly to thee, know that evil is determined by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings II 14:32; Kings I 18:3; Kings I 23:18"> And thou shalt deal mercifully with thy servant; for thou hast brought thy servant into a covenant of the Lord with thyself: and if there is iniquity in thy servant, slay me thyself; but why dost thou thus bring me to thy father?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings I 20:42; Kings I 19:2; Kings I 20:38"> And Jonathan said, That be far from thee: for if I surely know that evil is determined by my father to come upon thee, although it should not be against thy cities, I will tell thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings I 25:14; Kings I 25:17; Genesis 42:30"> And David said to Jonathan, Who can tell me if thy father should answer roughly?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Jonathan said to David, Go, and abide in the field. And they went out both into the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 22:22; Job 31:4; Proverbs 25:2–3"> And Jonathan said to David, The Lord God of Israel knows that I will sound my father as I have an opportunity,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Heb. השליח the third day.">e</a></sup>three several times, and, behold, if good should be determined concerning David, and I do not send to thee to the field,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ruth 1:17; Chronicles I 22:11; Joshua 1:5"> God do so to Jonathan and more also: as I shall also report the evil to thee, and make it known to thee, and I will let thee go; and thou shalt depart in peace, and the Lord shall be with thee, as he was with my father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings II 9:3; Ephesians 5:1–2"> And if indeed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. I yet being alive.">f</a></sup>I continue to live, then shalt thou deal mercifully with me; and if I indeed die,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings II 21:7; Kings I 24:21; Kings II 9:1–7"> thou shalt not withdraw thy mercy from my house for ever: and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. The meaning of the Heb. is here greatly obscured.">g</a></sup>if thou doest not, when the Lord cuts off the enemies of David each from the face of the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings I 25:22; Kings I 18:3; Kings II 21:8"> should it happen that the name of Jonathan be discovered by the house of David, then let the Lord seek out the enemies of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 18:24; Kings I 18:1; Kings I 18:3"> And Jonathan swore yet again to David, because he loved the soul of him that loved him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings I 20:25; Kings I 20:5"> And Jonathan said, To-morrow is the new moon, and thou wilt be enquired for, because thy seat will be observed as vacant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings I 19:2; Kings I 20:5"> And thou shalt<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. act thrice.">h</a></sup>stay three days, and watch an opportunity, and shalt come to thy place where thou mayest hide thyself in the day of thy business, and thou shalt wait by that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="A corruption of the Heb. ezel.">i</a></sup>ergab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And I will shoot<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gr. with.">j</a></sup>three arrows, aiming them at<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="The Heb. has been turned into a proper name.">k</a></sup>a mark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremias 5:2; Amos 8:14; Jeremias 12:16"> And behold, I will send a lad, saying, Go find me the arrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings I 20:37"> If I should expressly say to the lad, The arrow is here, and on this side of thee, take it; then come, for it is well with thee, and there is no reason for fear, as the Lord lives: but if I should say thus to the young man, The arrow is on that side of thee, and beyond; go, for the Lord hath sent thee away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings I 20:14–15; Kings I 20:42; Genesis 31:50"> And as for the word which thou and I have spoken, behold, the Lord is witness between me and thee for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 4:17; Esaias 1:11–15; Proverbs 21:3"> So David hides himself in the field, and the new<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or, moon.">l</a></sup>month arrives, and the king comes to the table to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings I 20:18; Judges 16:20"> And he sat upon his seat as<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Gr. once and once.">m</a></sup>in former times, even on his seat by the wall, and he went before Jonathan; and Abenner sat on one side of Saul, and the place of David was empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 15:5; Leviticus 11:24; Leviticus 11:27"> And Saul said nothing on that day, for he said, It seems to have fallen out that he is not clean, because he has not purified himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4; Kings I 19:15; Kings I 18:11"> And it came to pass on the morrow, on the second day of the month, that the place of David was empty; and Saul said to Jonathan his son, Why has not the son of Jessæ attended both yesterday and to-day at the table?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Kings I 20:6"> And Jonathan answered Saul, and said to him, David asked leave of me to go as far as Bethleem his city;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Kings I 17:28"> and he said, Let me go, I pray thee, for we have a family sacrifice in the city, and my brethren have<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Gr. given a charge for me.">n</a></sup>sent for me; and now, if I have found grace in thine eyes, I will even go over and see my brethren: therefore he is not present at the table of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 21:24; James 1:19–20; Proverbs 25:28"> And Saul was exceedingly angry with Jonathan, and said to him, Thou son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Lit. deserting in a military sense.">o</a></sup>traitorous damsels! for do I not know that thou art an accomplice with the son of Jessæ to thy shame, and to the shame of thy mother's nakedness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Kings I 19:6; Kings I 20:8; Kings I 19:11–15"> For<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Gr. all the days that.">p</a></sup>so long as the son of Jessæ lives upon the earth, thy kingdom shall not be established: now then send and take the young man, for he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Gr. is a son of death.">q</a></sup>shall surely die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 27:23; Kings I 19:5; Luke 23:22"> And Jonathan answered Saul, Why<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Gr. does he die.">r</a></sup>is he to die? what has he done?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Kings I 20:7; Kings I 18:11; Kings I 19:10"> And Saul lifted up his spear against Jonathan to slay him: so Jonathan knew that this evil was determined on by his father to slay David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ephesians 4:26; Mark 3:5; Ecclesiastes 7:20"> And Jonathan sprang up from the table in great anger, and did not eat bread on the second day of the month, for he grieved bitterly for David, because his father determined on mischief against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Kings II 20:5; Kings I 20:19"> And morning came, and Jonathan went out to the field, as he appointed to do for a signal to David, and a little boy was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Kings I 20:20–22"> And he said to the boy, Run, find me the arrows which I shoot: and the boy ran, and Jonathan shot an arrow, and sent it beyond him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Kings I 20:22"> And the boy came to the place where the arrow was which Jonathan shot; and Jonathan cried out after the lad, and said, The arrow is on that side of thee and beyond thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Psalms 55:6–9; Proverbs 6:4–5; Mark 13:14–16"> And Jonathan cried out after his boy, saying, Make all speed, and stay not. And Jonathan's boy gathered up the arrows, and brought the arrows to his master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> And the boy knew nothing, only Jonathan and David knew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And Jonathan gave his weapons to his boy, and said to his boy, Go, enter into the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Genesis 29:11; Kings I 18:3; Kings II 9:6"> And when the lad went in, then David arose from the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Gr. See v. 19.">s</a></sup>argab, and fell upon his face, and did obeisance to him three times, and they kissed each other, and wept for each other, for a great while.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Kings I 1:17; Kings I 23:18; Kings I 20:22–23"> And Jonathan said to David, Go in peace, and as we have both sworn in the name of the Lord, saying, The Lord shall be witness between me and thee, and between my seed and thy seed for ever—even so let it be.</span>
