<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings I 4:1; Joshua 19:30; Kings III 20:30"> And the Philistines gather all their armies to Aphec, and Israel encamped in Aendor, which is in Jezrael.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings I 28:1–2; Joshua 13:3; Kings I 29:6–7"> And the lords of the Philistines went on<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. to or at.">a</a></sup>by hundreds and thousands, and David and his men went on in the rear with Anchus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 6:5; Kings I 27:7; John 19:6"> And the lords of the Philistines said, Who are these<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The word עכר is here translated; as in Gen. 14. 13.">b</a></sup>that pass by? And Anchus said to the captains of the Philistines, Is not this David the servant of Saul king of Israel? he has been with us<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. to or days.">c</a></sup>some time, even this second year, and I have not found any fault in him from the day that he attached himself to me even until this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings I 14:21; Chronicles I 12:19; Luke 16:8"> And the captains of the Philistines were displeased at him, and they say to him, Send the man away, and let him return to his place, where thou didst set him; and let him not come with us to the war, and let him not be a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or, to or a plotter against the camp.">d</a></sup>traitor in the camp: and wherewith will he be reconciled to his master? will it not be with the heads of those men?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings I 21:11; Kings I 18:6–7; Proverbs 27:14"> Is not this David whom they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. to or began to celebrate or gave the precedence.">e</a></sup>celebrated in dances, saying, Saul has smitten his thousands, and David his ten thousands?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings II 3:25; Kings IV 19:27; Kings I 20:3"> And Anchus called David, and said to him, As the Lord lives, thou art right and approved in my eyes, and so is thy going out and thy coming in with me in the army, and I have not found any evil to charge against thee from the day that thou camest to me until this day: but thou art not approved in the eyes of the lords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 22:34"> Now then return and go in peace, thus thou shalt not do evil in the sight of the lords of the Philistines.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 6:13; Psalms 34:13–14; Kings I 28:2"> And David said to Anchus, What have I done to thee? and what hast thou found in thy servant from the first day that I was before thee even until this day, that I should not come<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. to or warring, or having warred.">f</a></sup>and war against the enemies of the lord my king?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings II 19:27; Kings II 14:17; Kings II 14:20"> And Anchus answered David, I know that thou art good in my eyes,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Heb. and Alex. + an angel of God.">g</a></sup>but the lords of the Philistines say, He shall not come with us to the war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Chronicles I 12:19; Kings I 30:1–2; Chronicles I 12:22"> Now then rise up early in the morning, thou and the servants of thy lord that are come with thee, and go to the place where I appointed you, and entertain no evil thought in thy heart, for thou art good in my sight: and rise early for your journey<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. and let there be light upon you.">h</a></sup>when it is light, and depart.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings I 29:1; Kings II 4:4; Joshua 19:18"> So David arose early, he and his men, to depart and guard the land of the Philistines: and the Philistines went up to Jezrael to battle.</span>
</div>
