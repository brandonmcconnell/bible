<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings I 23:28–29; Jezekiel 22:9; Kings I 23:19"> And David rose up from thence, and dwelt in the narrow passes of Engaddi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings I 26:2; Psalms 104:18; Psalms 37:32"> And it came to pass when Saul returned from pursuing after the Philistines, that it was reported to him, saying, David is in the wilderness of Engaddi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 3:24; Psalms 57:1–11; Psalms 142:1–7"> And he took with him three thousand men, chosen out of all Israel, and went to seek David and his men in front of Saddæem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings I 26:8–11; Kings I 24:10; Kings I 26:23"> And he came to the flocks of sheep that were by the way, and there was a cave there; and Saul went in to make preparation, and David and his men were sitting in the inner part of the cave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings II 24:10; Kings IV 22:19; 1 John 3:20–21"> And the men of David said to him, Behold, this is the day of which the Lord spoke to thee, that he would deliver thine enemy into thy hands; and thou shalt do to him as it is good in thy sight. So David arose and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. took away.">a</a></sup>cut off the skirt of Saul's garment secretly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings II 1:14; Job 31:29–30; Kings I 26:9–11"> And it came to pass after this that David's heart smote him, because he had cut off the skirt of his garment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; Psalms 7:4; Romans 12:17–21"> And David said to his men, The Lord forbid it me, that I should do this thing to my lord the anointed of the Lord, to lift my hand against him; for he is the anointed of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings I 25:23–24; Romans 13:7; Exodus 20:12"> So David persuaded his men by his words, and did not suffer them to arise and slay Saul: and Saul arose and went his way.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:21–22; Proverbs 29:12; Proverbs 16:28"> And David rose up and went after him out of the cave: and David cried after Saul, saying, My lord, O king! and Saul looked behind him, and David<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. stooped.">b</a></sup>bowed with his face to the ground, and did obeisance to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 105:15; Kings I 24:4; Kings I 26:8–9"> And David said to Saul, Why dost thou hearken to the words of the people, saying, Behold, David seeks thy life?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings I 26:20; Kings I 23:14; Kings I 23:23"> Behold, thine eyes have seen this day how that the Lord has delivered thee this day into my hands in the cave; and I would not slay thee, but spared thee, and said, I will not lift up my hand against my lord, for he is the Lord's anointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 43:1; Judges 11:27; Psalms 35:1"> And behold, the skirt of thy mantle is in my hand, I cut off the skirt, and did not slay thee: know then and see to-day, there is no evil in my hand, nor impiety, nor rebellion; and I have not sinned against thee, yet thou<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. bindest my soul.">c</a></sup>layest snares for my soul to take it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 7:20; Matthew 7:16–18; Matthew 12:33–34"> The Lord judge between me and thee, and the Lord requite thee on thyself: but my hand shall not be upon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings I 26:20; Kings I 17:43; Kings II 9:8"> As the old proverb<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. is said.">d</a></sup>says, Transgression will proceed from the wicked ones: but my hand shall not be upon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 43:1; Psalms 35:1; Psalms 119:154"> And now after whom dost thou come forth, O king of Israel? after whom dost thou pursue? after a dead dog, and after a flea?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings I 26:17; Genesis 33:4; Luke 21:15"> The Lord be judge and umpire between me and thee, the Lord look upon and judge my cause, and rescue me out of thy hand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; Kings I 26:21; Genesis 38:26"> And it came to pass when David had finished speaking these words to Saul, that Saul said, Is this thy voice, son David? and Saul lifted up his voice, and wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings I 26:23; Psalms 31:8; Kings I 23:12"> And Saul said to David, Thou art more righteous than I, for thou hast recompensed me good, but I have recompensed thee evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 18:20; Kings I 23:21; Proverbs 25:21–22"> And thou hast told me to-day what good thou hast done me, how the Lord shut me up into thy hands to-day, and thou didst not slay me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings I 23:17; Matthew 2:3–6; Kings II 3:17–18"> And if any one should find his enemy in distress, and should send him forth in a good way, then the Lord will reward him good, as thou hast done this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 21:23; Kings II 21:6–8; Kings I 20:14–17"> And now, behold, I know that thou shalt surely reign, and the kingdom of Israel shall be established in thy hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings I 23:29; John 2:24; Matthew 10:16–17"> Now then swear to me by the Lord, that thou wilt not destroy my seed after me, that thou wilt not blot out my name from the house of my father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> So David swore to Saul: and Saul departed to his place, and David and his men went up to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. narrow Messera.">e</a></sup>the strong-hold of Messera.</span>
</div>
