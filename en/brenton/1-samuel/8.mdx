<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:18–19; 1 Timothy 5:21; Nehemiah 7:2"> And it came to pass when Samuel was old, that he made his sons judges over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Chronicles I 6:38; Genesis 22:19; Chronicles I 6:28"> And these are the names of his sons; Joel the first-born, and the name of the second Abia, judges in Bersabee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Psalms 15:5; 1 Timothy 6:10"> And his sons did not walk in his way; and they turned aside after gain, and took gifts, and perverted judgments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings I 7:17; Kings II 5:3; Exodus 3:16"> And the men of Israel gather themselves together, and come to Armathaim to Samuel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:14–15; Osee 13:10–11; Kings I 8:6–8"> and they said to him, Behold, thou art grown old, and thy sons walk not in thy way; and now set over us a king to judge us, as also the other nations have.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings I 15:11; James 1:5; Kings I 12:17"> And the thing was evil in the eyes of Samuel, when they said, Give us a king to judge us: and Samuel prayed to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 10:16; Kings I 10:19; Exodus 16:8"> And the Lord said to Samuel, Hear the voice of the people, in whatever they shall say to thee; for they have not rejected thee, but they have rejected me from reigning over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 106:34–40; Exodus 32:1; Judges 13:1"> According to all their doings which they have done to me, from the day that I brought them out of Egypt until this day, even as they have deserted me, and served other gods, so they do also to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings I 10:25; Kings I 8:11–18; Jezekiel 46:18"> And now hearken to their voice; only thou shalt solemnly testify to them, and thou shalt<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. report.">a</a></sup>describe to them the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. judgment.">b</a></sup>manner of the king who shall reign over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Samuel spoke every word of the Lord to the people who asked of him a king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings I 14:52; Deuteronomy 17:14–20; Kings I 10:25"> And he said, This shall be the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. judgment.">c</a></sup>manner of the king that shall rule over you: he shall take your sons, and put them in his chariots, and among his horsemen, and running before his chariots,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings I 22:7; Kings III 4:7; Chronicles I 27:1–22"> and his manner shall be to make them to himself captains of hundreds and captains of thousands; and to reap his harvest, and gather his vintage, and prepare his instruments of war, and the implements of his chariots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And he will take your daughters to be perfumers, and cooks, and bakers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jezekiel 46:18; Kings III 21:7; Kings III 21:19"> And he will take your fields, and your vineyards, and your good oliveyards, and give them to his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 1:3; Esaias 39:7; Genesis 37:36"> And he will take the tithe of your seeds and your vineyards, and give it to his eunuchs, and to his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And he will take your servants, and your handmaids, and your good herds and your asses, and will take the tenth of them for his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And he will tithe your flocks; and ye shall be his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Michæas 3:4; Esaias 1:15; Proverbs 1:25–28"> And ye shall cry out in that day because of your king whom ye have chosen to yourselves, and the Lord shall not hear you in those days, because ye have chosen to yourselves a king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremias 44:16; Jeremias 7:13; Esaias 66:4"> But the people would not hearken to Samuel; and they said to him, Nay, but there shall be a king over us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings I 8:5; Psalms 106:35; Deuteronomy 7:6"> And we also will be like all the nations; and our king shall judge us, and shall go out before us, and fight our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. war.">d</a></sup>battles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 11:11"> And Samuel heard all the words of the people, and spoke them in the ears of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings I 8:7; Osee 13:11"> And the Lord said to Samuel, Hearken to their voice, and appoint them a king. And Samuel said to the men of Israel, Let each man depart to his city.</span>
