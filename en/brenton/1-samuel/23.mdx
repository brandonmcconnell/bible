<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 15:44; Michæas 6:15; Nehemiah 3:17–18"> And it was told David, saying, Behold, the Philistines war in Keila, and they rob, they trample on the threshing-floors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings I 23:4; Kings II 5:19; Kings II 5:23"> And David enquired of the Lord, saying, Shall I go and smite these Philistines? And the Lord said, Go, and thou shalt smite these Philistines, and shalt save Keila.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 12:5; Psalms 11:1; Kings I 23:15"> And the men of David said to him, Behold, we are afraid here in Judea; and how shall it be if we go to Keila? shall we go after the spoils of the Philistines?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 7:7; Joshua 8:7; Kings IV 3:18"> And David enquired yet again of the Lord; and the Lord answered him, and said to him, Arise and go down to Keila, for I will deliver the Philistines into thy hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> So David and his men with him went to Keila, and fought with the Philistines; and they fled from before him, and he carried off their cattle, and smote them with a great slaughter, and David rescued the inhabitants of Keila.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings I 22:20; Kings I 14:3; Kings I 14:18"> And it came to pass when Abiathar the son of Achimelech fled to David, that he went down with David to Keila, having an ephod in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings I 24:4–6; Job 20:5; Kings I 26:8–9"> And it was told Saul that David was come to Keila: and Saul said, God has sold him into my hands, for he is shut up, having entered into a city that has gates and bars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And Saul charged all the people to go down to war to Keila, to besiege David and his men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings I 30:7; Kings I 23:6; Numbers 27:21"> And David knew that Saul<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. is not silent concerning.">a</a></sup>spoke openly of mischief against him: and David said to Abiathar the priest, Bring the ephod of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 3:15–16; Kings I 23:8; Genesis 18:24"> And David said, Lord God of Israel, thy servant has indeed heard, that Saul seeks to come against Keila to destroy the city on my account.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremias 33:3; Psalms 50:15; Matthew 7:7–8"> Will the place<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i. e. besieged.">b</a></sup>be shut up? and now will Saul come down, as thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Verse 12 is here supplied by Alex.">c</a></sup>servant has heard? Lord God of Israel, tell thy servant. And the Lord said, It will be shut up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings I 22:2; Kings I 25:13; Kings I 30:9–10"> And David arose, and the men with him, in number about four hundred, and they went forth from Keila, and went whithersoever they could go: and it was told Saul that David had escaped from Keila, and he forbore to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 54:3–4; Psalms 32:7; Joshua 15:24"> And he dwelt in Maserem in the wilderness, in the narrow passes; and dwelt in the wilderness in mount Ziph, in the dry country. And Saul sought him continually, but the Lord delivered him not into his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And David perceived that Saul went forth to seek David; and David was in the dry mountain in the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The Hebrew הוש has here been read as if חוש.">d</a></sup>New Ziph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esaias 35:3–4; Hebrews 12:12–13; Job 4:3–4"> And Jonathan son of Saul rose, and went to David to Cæne, and strengthened his hands in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings I 20:31; Kings I 24:20; Acts 28:16"> And he said to him, Fear not, for the hand of Saul my father shall not find thee; and thou shalt be king over Israel, and I shall be second to thee; and Saul my father knows it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings II 21:7; Kings I 20:42; Kings I 18:3"> So they both made a covenant before the Lord; and David dwelt in Cæne, and Jonathan went to his home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings I 26:1; Kings I 26:3; Psalms 54:1–7"> And the Ziphites came up out of the dry country to Saul to the hill, saying, Behold, is not David hidden with us in Messara, in the narrows in Cæne in the hill of Echela, which is on the right of Jessæmon?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings I 23:12; Proverbs 29:26; Proverbs 11:23"> And now according to all the king's desire to come down, let him come down to us; they have shut him up into the hands of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings I 22:8; Esaias 66:5; Michæas 3:11"> And Saul said to them, Blessed be ye of the Lord, for ye have been grieved on my account.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 5:13"> Go, I pray you, and make preparations yet, and notice his place where his foot shall be, quickly, in that place which ye spoke of, lest by any means he should deal craftily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 3:15–16; Mark 14:1; Numbers 10:36"> Take notice, then, and learn, and I will go with you; and it shall come to pass that if he is in the land, I will search him out among all the thousands of Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Kings I 25:2; Joshua 15:55"> And the Ziphites arose, and went before Saul: and David and his men were in the wilderness of Maon, westward, to the right of Jessæmon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Judges 15:8; Kings I 23:28"> And Saul and his men went to seek him: and they brought word to David, and he went down to the rock that was in the wilderness of Maon: and Saul heard, and followed after David to the wilderness of Maon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 17:9; 2 Corinthians 1:8; Psalms 140:1–9"> And Saul and his men go on one side of the mountain, and David and his men are on the other side of the mountain: and David was hiding himself to escape from Saul: and Saul and his men encamped against David and his men, in order to take them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Kings IV 19:9; Psalms 116:3; Deuteronomy 32:36"> And there came a messenger to Saul, saying, Haste thee, and come hither, for the Philistines have invaded the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> So Saul returned from following after David, and went to meet the Philistines: therefore that place was called The divided Rock.</span>
