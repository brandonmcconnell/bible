<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings III 14:30; Proverbs 4:18–19; Job 8:7"> And there was war for a long time between the house of Saul and the house of David; and the house of David grew continually stronger; but the house of Saul grew continually weaker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Chronicles I 3:1–4; Genesis 49:3–4; Kings II 13:1–29"> And sons were born to David in Chebron: and his first-born was Amnon the son of Achinoom the Jezraelitess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings I 27:8; Chronicles I 3:1; Deuteronomy 3:14"> And his second son was Daluia, the son of Abigaia the Carmelitess; and the third, Abessalom the son of Maacha the daughter of Tholmi the king of Gessir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings III 2:13–25; Kings III 1:5–18"> And the fourth was Ornia, the son of Aggith, and the fifth was Saphatia, the son of Abital.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the sixth was Jetheraam, the son of Ægal the wife of David. These were born to David in Chebron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings II 2:8–9; Chronicles II 25:8; Esaias 8:9–10"> And it came to pass while there was war between the house of Saul and the house of David, that Abenner was governing the house of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings II 21:8–11; Kings III 2:21–22; Kings II 12:8"> And Saul had a concubine, Respha, the daughter of Jol; and Jebosthe the son of Saul said to Abenner, Why hast thou gone in to my father's concubine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings II 9:8; Kings II 16:9; Mark 6:18–19"> And Abenner was very angry with Jebosthe for this saying; and Abenner said to him, Am I a dog's head? I have this day wrought kindness with the house of Saul thy father, and with his brethren and friends, and have not gone over to the house of David, and dost thou this day seek a charge against me concerning injury to a woman?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings I 15:28; Kings III 19:2; Ruth 1:17"> God do thus and more also to Abenner, if as the Lord swore to David, so do I not to him this day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 20:1; Kings I 3:20; Kings III 4:25"> to take away the kingdom from the house of Saul, and to raise up the throne of David over Israel and over Juda from Dan to Bersabee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Jebosthe could not any longer answer Abenner a word, because he feared him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Chronicles I 12:38–40; Kings II 3:21; Luke 16:5–8"> And Abenner sent messengers to David to Thælam where he was, immediately, saying, Make thy covenant with me, and, behold, my hand is with thee to bring back to thee all the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 43:3; Kings I 19:11–17; Genesis 44:23"> And David said, With a good will I will make with thee a covenant: only I demand one condition of thee, saying, Thou shalt not see my face, unless thou bring Melchol the daughter of Saul, when thou comest to see my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings I 18:27; Kings I 18:25; Kings II 2:10"> And David sent messengers to Jebosthe the son of Saul, saying, Restore me my wife Melchol, whom I took for a hundred foreskins of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings I 25:44"> And Jebosthe sent, and took her from her husband, even from Phaltiel the son of Selle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings II 16:5; Kings II 19:16; Kings III 2:8"> And her husband went with her weeping behind her as far as Barakim. And Abenner said to him, Go, return; and he returned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Abenner spoke to the elders of Israel, saying, In former days ye sought David to reign over you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings I 15:28; Psalms 89:19–23; Kings I 9:16"> and now perform it: for the Lord has spoken concerning David, saying, By the hand of my servant David I will save Israel out of the hand of all their enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Chronicles I 12:29; Kings I 10:20–21; Psalms 68:27"> And Abenner spoke in the ears of Benjamin: and Abenner went to speak in the ears of David at Chebron, all that seemed good in the eyes of Israel and in the eyes of the house of Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 31:54; Genesis 26:30; Esther 1:3"> And Abenner came to David to Chebron, and with him twenty men: and David made for Abenner and his men with him a banquet of wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings II 3:12; Kings III 11:37; Kings II 3:10"> And Abenner said to David, I will arise now, and go, and gather to my lord the king all Israel; and I will make with him a covenant, and thou shalt reign over all whom thy soul desires. And David sent away Abenner, and he departed in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And, behold, the servants of David and Joab arrived from their expedition, and they brought much spoil with them: and Abenner was not with David in Chebron, because he had sent him away, and he had departed in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And Joab and all his army came, and it was reported to Joab, saying, Abenner the son of Ner is come to David, and David has let him go, and he has departed in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Kings II 3:8; Kings II 19:5–7; John 18:35"> And Joab went in to the king, and said, What is this that thou hast done? behold, Abenner came to thee; and why hast thou let him go, and he has departed in peace?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:6; Esaias 37:28; Numbers 27:17"> Knowest thou not the mischief of Abenner the son of Ner, that he came to deceive thee, and to know thy going out and thy coming in, and to know all things that thou doest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 26:23–26; Proverbs 27:4–6"> And Joab returned from David, and sent messengers to Abenner after him; and they bring him back from the well of Seïram: but David knew it not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Kings III 2:5; Kings II 20:9–10; Kings III 2:32"> And he brought back Abenner to Chebron, and Joab caused him to turn aside from the gate to speak to him, laying wait for him: and he smote him there in the loins, and he died for the blood of Asael the brother of Joab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 35:33; Exodus 21:12; Genesis 9:6"> And David heard of it afterwards, and said, I and my kingdom are guiltless before the Lord even for ever of the blood of Abenner the son of Ner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Kings II 1:16; Psalms 109:8–19; Leviticus 13:44–46"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. Lit. them. (sc. bloods) meet.">a</a></sup>Let it fall upon the head of Joab, and upon all the house of his father; and let there not be wanting of the house of Joab one that has an issue, or a leper, or that leans on a staff, or that falls by the sword, or that wants bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 28:17; Acts 28:4; Kings II 2:19–23"> For Joab and Abessa his brother laid wait continually for Abenner, because he slew Asael their brother at Gabaon in the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 37:34; Kings II 1:11; Kings II 1:2"> And David said to Joab and to all the people with him, Rend your garments, and gird yourselves with sackcloth, and lament before Abenner. And king David followed the bier.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 24:17; Job 31:28; Kings I 30:4"> And they bury Abenner in Chebron: and the king lifted up his voice, and wept at his tomb, and all the people wept for Abenner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Kings II 1:17; Chronicles II 35:25; Luke 12:19–20"> And the king mourned over Abenner, and said, Shall Abenner die according to the death of Nabal?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Judges 16:21; Kings II 1:12; Job 24:14"> Thy hands were not bound, and thy feet were not put in fetters: one brought thee not near as Nabal; thou didst fall before children of iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Kings II 12:17; Kings II 1:12; Ruth 1:17"> And all the people assembled to weep for him. And all the people came to cause David to eat bread while it was yet day: and David swore, saying, God do so to me, and more also, if I eat bread or any thing else before the sun goes down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 7:37; Psalms 62:9; Mark 15:11–13"> And all the people took notice, and all things that the king did before the people were pleasing in their sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> So all the people and all Israel perceived in that day, that it was not of the king to slay Abenner the son of Ner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Job 32:9; Kings II 2:8; Kings II 3:12"> And the king said to his servants, Know ye not that a great prince is this day fallen in Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Kings III 2:33–34; Psalms 101:8; Psalms 28:4"> And that I am this day a mere kinsman of his, and as it were<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. appointed by a king.">b</a></sup>a subject; but these men the sons of Saruia are too hard for me: the Lord reward the evil-doer according to his wickedness.</span>
