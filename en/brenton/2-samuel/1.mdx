<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings I 27:6; Kings I 31:6; Kings I 30:17–26"> And it came to pass after Saul was dead, that David returned from smiting Amalec, and David abode two days in Sekelac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings I 4:12; Kings II 4:10; Joshua 7:6"> And it came to pass on the third day, that, behold, a man came from the camp, from the people of Saul, and his garments were rent, and earth was upon his head: and it came to pass when he went in to David, that he fell upon the earth, and did obeisance to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 1:15–19; Kings IV 5:25"> And David said to him, Whence comest thou? and he said to him, I have escaped out of the camp of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings I 4:16; Kings I 31:1–6; Chronicles I 10:1–6"> And David said to him, What is the matter? tell me. And he said, The people fled out of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. war.">a</a></sup>battle, and many of the people have fallen and are dead, and Saul and Jonathan his son are dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 25:2; Proverbs 14:15"> And David said to the young man who brought him the tidings, How knowest thou that Saul and Jonathan his son are dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings I 28:4; Kings II 1:21; Chronicles I 10:4–6"> And the young man that brought the tidings, said to him, I happened accidentally to be upon mount Gelbue; and, behold, Saul was leaning upon his spear, and, behold, the chariots and captains of horse pressed hard upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings I 22:12; Kings II 9:6; Judges 9:54"> And he looked behind him, and saw me, and called me; and I said, Behold, here am I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings I 30:17; Kings I 30:13; Kings I 30:1"> And he said to me, Who art thou? and I said, I am an Amalekite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And he said to me, Stand, I pray thee, over me, and slay me, for a dreadful darkness has come upon me, for all my life is in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Judges 9:54; Kings IV 11:12"> So I stood over him and slew him, because I knew he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. will.">b</a></sup>would not live after he was fallen; and I took the crown that was upon his head, and the bracelet that was upon his arm, and I have brought them hither to my lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings II 13:31; Genesis 37:29; Kings II 3:31"> And David laid hold of his garments, and rent them; and all the men who were with him rent their garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 24:17; Psalms 35:13–14; Matthew 5:44"> And they lamented, and wept, and fasted till evening, for Saul and for Jonathan his son, and for the people of Juda, and for the house of Israel, because they were smitten with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings II 1:8"> And David said to the young man who brought the tidings to him, Whence art thou? and he said, I am the son of an Amalekite sojourner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings I 26:9; Kings I 31:4; 2 Peter 2:10"> And David said to him, How was it thou wast not afraid to lift thy hand to destroy the anointed of the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings III 2:34; Judges 8:20; Kings II 4:10–12"> And David called one of his young men, and said, Go and fall upon him: and he smote him, and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; Luke 19:22; Leviticus 20:9"> And David said to him, Thy blood be upon thine own head; for thy mouth has<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. answered.">c</a></sup>testified against thee, saying, I have slain the anointed of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Chronicles II 35:25; Kings II 1:19; Genesis 50:11"> And David lamented with this lamentation over Saul and over Jonathan his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 10:13; Genesis 49:8; Deuteronomy 4:10"> And he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. told.">d</a></sup>gave orders to teach it the sons of Juda: behold, it is written in the book of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. straight, or right, sometimes upright, as of a man. Heb. Jasher.">e</a></sup>Right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings II 1:27; Kings II 1:25; Deuteronomy 4:7–8"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The LXX. take הצבי as from כיצב, the A. V. from צבי.">f</a></sup>Set up a pillar, O Israel, for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Two words here are used, as is often the case in the LXX. to express one. viz. τεθηκότων and τραυματτιῶν to express חלל.">g</a></sup>the slain that died upon thy high places: how are the mighty fallen!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Michæas 1:10; Kings I 18:6; Judges 11:34"> Tell it not in Geth, and tell it not as glad tidings in the streets of Ascalon, lest the daughters of the Philistines rejoice, lest the daughters of the uncircumcised triumph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings I 31:1; Esaias 21:5; Kings I 10:1"> Ye mountains of Gelbue, let not dew nor rain descend upon you, nor fields of first-fruits be upon you, for there the shield of the mighty ones has been grievously assailed; the shield of Saul was not anointed with oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings I 18:4; Esaias 34:6–7; Kings I 14:6–14"> From the blood of the slain, and from the fat of the mighty, the bow of Jonathan returned not empty; and the sword of Saul turned not back empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremias 4:13; Judges 14:18; Lamentations 4:19"> Saul and Jonathan, the beloved and the beautiful, were not divided: comely were they in their life, and in their death they were not divided: they were swifter than eagles, and they were stronger than lions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Peter 3:3–5; Jeremias 2:32; Judges 5:30"> Daughters of Israel, weep for Saul, who clothed you with scarlet together with your adorning, who added golden ornaments to your apparel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings II 1:19; Kings II 1:27; Judges 5:18"> How are the mighty fallen in the midst of the battle! O Jonathan, even the slain ones upon thy high places!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Kings I 20:17; Kings I 23:16; Kings I 20:41"> I am grieved for thee, my brother Jonathan; thou wast very lovely to me; thy love to me was wonderful beyond the love of women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Kings II 1:25; Kings II 1:19; Jezekiel 39:9–10"> How are the mighty fallen, and the weapons of war perished!</span>
