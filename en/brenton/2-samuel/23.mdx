<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Chronicles I 27:4; Chronicles I 11:12–14; Kings I 17:26"> And after him Eleanan the son of his uncle, son of Dudi who was among the three mighty men with David; and when<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, they defied him among the Philistines.">a</a></sup>he defied the Philistines they were gathered there to war, and the men of Israel went up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings I 19:5; Kings I 11:13; Judges 15:14"> He arose and smote the Philistines, until his hand was weary, and his hand clave to the sword: and the Lord wrought a great salvation in that day, and the people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Heb. returned.">b</a></sup>rested behind him only to strip the slain.</span>

These things did these three mighty men.</span>
</div>
