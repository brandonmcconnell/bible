<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings I 30:31; Kings I 23:4; Genesis 32:2"> And it came to pass after this that David enquired of the Lord, saying, Shall I go up into one of the cities of Juda? and the Lord said to him, Go up. And David said, Whither shall I go up? and he said, To Chebron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings I 25:42–43; Kings I 30:5; Luke 22:28–29"> And David went up thither to Chebron, he and both his wives, Achinaam the Jezraelitess, and Abigaia the wife of Nabal the Carmelite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings I 30:1; Chronicles I 12:1–7; Kings I 27:2–3"> and the men that were with him, every one and his family; and they dwelt in the cities of Chebron.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings II 5:5; Kings I 31:11–13; Kings II 5:3"> And the men of Judea come, and anoint David there to reign over the house of Juda; and they reported to David, saying, The men of Jabis of the country of Galaad have buried Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings I 23:21; Psalms 115:15; Ruth 2:20"> And David sent messengers to the rulers of Jabis of the country of Galaad, and David said to them, Blessed be ye of the Lord, because ye have wrought this mercy toward your lord, even toward Saul the anointed of the Lord, and ye have buried him and Jonathan his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 14:22; Exodus 34:6; Matthew 5:44"> And now may the Lord deal in mercy and truth towards you: and I also will requite towards you this good deed, because ye have done this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 6:10; Kings II 10:12; Genesis 15:1"> And now let your hands be made strong, and be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. mighty sons.">a</a></sup>valiant; for your master Saul is dead, and moreover the house of Juda have anointed me to be king over them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings I 14:50; Genesis 32:2; Kings II 3:7–8"> But Abenner, the son of Ner, the commander-in-chief of Saul's army, took Jebosthe son of Saul, and brought him up from the camp to Manaem</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 19:18; Joshua 13:8–11; Numbers 1:40"> and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Observe the active use of ἐβασίλευσεν, common in LXX.">b</a></sup>made him king over the land of Galaad, and over Thasiri, and over Jezrael, and over Ephraim, and over Benjamin, and over all Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Jebosthe, Saul's son was forty years old, when he reigned over Israel; and he reigned two years, but not over the house of Juda, who followed David.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings III 2:11; Kings II 5:4–5; Chronicles I 3:4"> And the days which David reigned in Chebron over the house of Juda were seven years and six months.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 18:25; Joshua 10:2; Joshua 9:3"> And Abenner the son of Ner went forth, and the servants of Jebosthe the son of Saul, from Manaem to Gabaon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings II 8:16; Chronicles I 2:16; Kings II 20:23"> And Joab the son of Saruia, and the servants of David, went forth from Chebron, and met them at the fountain of Gabaon, at the same place: and these sat down by the fountain on this side, and those by the fountain on that side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings II 2:17; Proverbs 10:23; Proverbs 17:14"> And Abenner said to Joab, Let now the young men arise, and play before us. And Joab said, Let them arise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And there arose and passed over by number twelve of the children of Benjamin, belonging to Jebosthe the son of Saul, and twelve of the servants of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And they seized every one the head of his neighbour with his hand, and his sword was thrust into the side of his neighbour, and they fall down together: and the name of that place was called The portion of the treacherous ones, which is in Gabaon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings II 3:1"> And the battle was very severe on that day; and Abenner and the men of Israel were worsted before the servants of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Chronicles I 12:8; Ambacum 3:19; Song of Solomon 8:14"> And there were there the three sons of Saruia, Joab, and Abessa, and Asael: and Asael was swift in his feet as a roe in the field.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings II 2:21; Joshua 1:7; Proverbs 4:27"> And Asael followed after Abenner, and turned not to go to the right hand or to the left from following Abenner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And Abenner looked behind him, and said, Art thou Asael himself? and he said, I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 14:19"> And Abenner said to him, Turn thou to the right hand or to the left, and lay hold for thyself on one of the young men, and take to thyself his armour: but Asael would not turn back from following him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings II 3:27; Proverbs 29:1; Kings IV 14:10–12"> And Abenner said yet again to Asael, Stand aloof from me, lest I smite thee to the ground? and how should I lift up my face to Joab?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings II 3:27; Kings II 4:6; Kings II 5:6"> And what does this mean? return to Joab thy brother. But he would not stand aloof; and Abenner smites him with the hinder end of the spear on the loins, and the spear went out behind him, and he falls there and dies<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A literal version of the Hebrew חחחו q. d. sur le champ.">c</a></sup>on the spot: and it came to pass that every one that came to the place where Asael fell and died, stood still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And Joab and Abessa pursued after Abenner, and the sun went down: and they went as far as the hill of Amman, which is in the front of Gai, by the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See Acts 8. 26.">d</a></sup>desert way of Gabaon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And the children of Benjamin who followed Abenner gather themselves together, and they formed themselves into one body, and stood on the top of a hill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremias 46:14; Jeremias 46:10; Acts 7:26"> And Abenner called Joab, and said, Shall the sword devour perpetually? knowest thou not that it will be bitter at last? How long then wilt thou refuse to tell the people to turn from following our brethren?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 17:14; Kings II 2:14; Proverbs 15:1"> And Joab said, As the Lord lives, if thou hadst not spoken, even from the morning the people had gone up every one from following his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Joab sounded the trumpet, and all the people departed, and did not pursue after Israel, and did not fight any longer.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Kings II 2:8; Song of Solomon 2:17"> And Abenner and his men departed at evening, and went all that night, and crossed over Jordan, and went along the whole adjacent country, and they come to the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And Joab returned from following Abenner, and he assembled all the people, and there were missing of the people of David, nineteen men, and Asael.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Kings II 3:1; Kings III 20:11"> And the servants of David smote of the children of Benjamin, of the men of Abenner, three hundred and sixty men belonging to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Chronicles I 2:13–16; Kings I 17:58; Chronicles II 21:1"> And they take up Asael, and bury him in the tomb of his father in Bethleem. And Joab and the men with him went all the night, and the morning rose upon them in Chebron.</span>
</div>
