<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings I 25:18; Kings II 15:32; Kings II 9:2–13"> And David passed on a little way from Ros; and, behold, Siba the servant of Memphibosthe came to meet him; and he had a couple of asses laden, and upon them two hundred loaves, and a hundred bunches of raisins, and a hundred cakes of dates, and a bottle of wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings II 17:29; Judges 10:4; Judges 5:10"> And the king said to Siba, What meanest thou by these? and Siba said, The asses are for the household of the king to sit upon, and the loaves and the dates are for the young men to eat, and the wine is for them that are faint in the wilderness to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings II 9:9–10; Psalms 15:3; Kings II 19:24–30"> And the king said, And where is the son of thy master? and Siba said to the king, Behold, he remains in Jerusalem; for he said, To-day shall the house of Israel restore to me the kingdom of my father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 18:13; Proverbs 18:17; Proverbs 19:2"> And the king said to Siba, Behold, all Memphibosthe's property is thine. And Siba did obeisance and said, My lord, O king, let me find grace in thine eyes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings II 3:16; Kings I 17:43; Exodus 22:28"> And king David came to Baurim; and, behold, there came out from thence a man of the family of the house of Saul, and his name was Semei the son of Gera. He came forth and cursed as he went,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> and cast stones at David, and at all the servants of king David: and all the people and all the mighty men were on the right and left hand of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:13; Kings I 25:17; Kings III 21:10"> And thus Semei said when he cursed him, Go out, go out, thou bloody man, and man of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings II 1:16; Kings II 3:28–29; Kings III 2:32–33"> The Lord has returned upon thee all the blood of the house of Saul, because thou hast reigned in his stead; and the Lord has given the kingdom into the hand of Abessalom thy son: and, behold, thou art taken in thy mischief, because thou art a bloody man.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 22:28; Kings II 9:8; Kings I 24:14"> And Abessa the son of Saruia said to the king, Why does this dead dog curse my lord the king? let me go over now and take off his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings II 19:22; Romans 9:20; John 18:11"> And the king said, What have I to do with you, ye sons of Saruia? even let him alone, and so let him curse, for the Lord has told him to curse David: and who shall say, Why hast thou done thus?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings II 17:1–4; Genesis 45:5; Kings II 12:11–12"> And David said to Abessa and to all his servants, Behold, my son who came forth out of my bowels seeks my life; still more now may the son of Benjamin: let him curse, because the Lord has told him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:5; Hebrews 12:10; Psalms 25:18"> If by any means the Lord may look on my affliction, thus shall he return me good for his cursing this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 23:23; Kings II 16:5–6"> And David and all the men with him went on the way: and Semei went by the side of the hill next to him, cursing as he went, and casting stones<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. obliquely, etc.">a</a></sup>at him, and sprinkling him with dirt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And the king, and all the people with him, came away and refreshed themselves there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings II 15:37; Kings II 15:12"> And Abessalom and all the men of Israel went into Jerusalem, and Achitophel with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings IV 11:12; Kings I 10:24; Kings III 1:25"> And it came to pass when Chusi the chief friend of David came to Abessalom, that Chusi said to Abessalom, Let the king live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings II 19:25; Kings II 15:32–37; Deuteronomy 32:6"> And Abessalom said to Chusi, Is this thy kindness to thy friend? why wentest thou not forth with thy friend?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings I 16:13; Kings II 5:1–3"> And Chusi said to Abessalom, Nay, but following whom the Lord, and this people, and all Israel have chosen,—his will I be, and with him will I dwell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings II 15:34; Psalms 55:21; Kings I 28:2"> And again, whom shall I serve? should I not in the presence of his son? As I served in the sight of thy father, so will I be in thy presence.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Esaias 8:10; Psalms 37:12–13; Esaias 29:15"> And Abessalom said to Achitophel, Deliberate among yourselves concerning what we should do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings II 20:3; Kings II 15:16; Zacharias 8:13"> And Achitophel said to Abessalom, Go in to thy father's concubines, whom he left to keep his house; and all Israel shall hear that thou hast dishonoured thy father; and the hands of all that are with thee shall be strengthened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings II 12:11–12; Kings II 15:16; Kings II 20:3"> And they pitched a tent for Abessalom on the roof, and Abessalom went in to his father's concubines in the sight of all Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings II 15:12; Kings II 17:14; Kings II 17:23"> And the counsel of Achitophel, which he counselled in former days, was as if one should enquire of the word of God: so was all the counsel of Achitophel both to David and also to Abessalom.</span>
</div>
