<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 21:4; Deuteronomy 1:40; Numbers 14:25"> And we turned and departed into the wilderness, by the way of the Red Sea, as the Lord spoke to me, and we compassed mount Seir many days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And the Lord said to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:6; Deuteronomy 2:14; Deuteronomy 2:7"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, Let it suffice you to compass.">a</a></sup>Ye have compassed this mount long enough; turn therefore toward the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 20:14–21; Exodus 15:15; Deuteronomy 23:7"> And charge the people, saying, Ye are going through the borders of your brethren the children of Esau, who dwell in Seir; and they shall fear you, and dread you greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 36:8; Joshua 24:4; Jeremias 27:5"> Do not engage in war against them, for I will not give you of their land even enough to set your foot upon, for I have given mount Seir to the children of Esau as an inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 7:12; Numbers 20:19; Romans 12:17"> Buy food of them for money and eat, and ye shall receive water of them by measure for money, and drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 22:35; Nehemiah 9:21; Deuteronomy 8:2–4"> For the Lord our God has blessed thee in every work of thy hands. Consider how thou wentest through that great and terrible wilderness: behold, the Lord thy God has been with thee forty years; thou didst not lack any thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings III 9:26; Judges 11:18; Kings IV 16:6"> And we passed by our brethren the children of Esau, who dwelt in Seir, by the way of Araba from Ælon and from Gesion Gaber; and we turned and passed by the way of the desert of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 19:36–37; Numbers 21:15; Numbers 21:28"> And the Lord said to me, Do not ye quarrel with the Moabites, and do not engage in war with them; for I will not give you of their land for an inheritance, for I have given Aroer to the children of Lot to inherit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 14:5; Deuteronomy 2:11; Numbers 13:22"> Formerly the Ommin dwelt in it, a great and numerous nation and powerful, like the Enakim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; Numbers 13:28; Genesis 14:5"> These also shall be accounted<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Heb. giants.">b</a></sup>Raphain like the Enakim; and the Moabites call them Ommin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:22; Genesis 14:6; Deuteronomy 2:32–11"> And the Chorrhite dwelt in Seir before, and the sons of Esau destroyed them, and utterly consumed them from before them; and they dwelt in their place, as Israel did to the land of his inheritance, which the Lord gave to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 21:12; Numbers 13:23"> Now then, arise ye, said I, and depart, and cross the valley of Zaret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 26:64–65; Numbers 14:28–35; Deuteronomy 1:34–35"> And the days in which we travelled from Cades Barne till we crossed the valley of Zaret, were thirty and eight years, until the whole generation of the men of war failed, dying out of the camp, as the Lord God sware to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 106:26; Esaias 66:14; Psalms 32:4"> And the hand of the Lord was upon them to destroy them out of the midst of the camp, until they were consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And it came to pass when all the men of war dying out of the midst of the people had fallen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> that the Lord spoke to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 21:23; Esaias 15:1; Numbers 21:15"> Thou shalt pass over this day the borders of Moab<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, even Aroer.">c</a></sup>to Aroer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:9; Judges 11:13–27; Chronicles II 20:10"> and ye shall draw nigh to the children of Amman: do not quarrel with them, nor wage war with them; for I will not give thee of the land of the children of Amman for an inheritance, because I have given it to the children of Lot for an inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 14:5"> It shall be accounted a land of Raphain, for the Raphain dwelt there before, and the Ammanites call them Zochommin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremias 27:7–8; Deuteronomy 1:28; Deuteronomy 2:22"> A great nation and populous, and mightier than you, as also the Enakim: yet the Lord destroyed them from before them, and they inherited their land, and they dwelt there instead of them until this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 36:8; Genesis 36:20–30; Deuteronomy 2:12"> As they did to the children of Esau that dwell in Seir, even as they destroyed the Chorrhite from before them, and inherited<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. them.">d</a></sup>their country, and dwelt therein instead of them until this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 10:14; Joshua 13:3; Amos 9:7"> And the Evites who dwell in Asedoth to Gaza, and the Cappadocians who came out of Cappadocia, destroyed them, and dwelt in their room.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremias 27:5; Daniel 4:17; Judges 11:18–21"> Now then arise and depart, and pass over the valley of Arnon: behold, I have delivered into thy hands Seon the king of Esebon the Amorite, and his land: begin to inherit it: engage in war with him this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:25; Exodus 15:14–16; Deuteronomy 28:10"> Begin to put thy terror and thy fear on the face of all the nations under heaven, who shall be troubled when they have heard thy name, and shall be in anguish<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or, for fear of thee. Hebraism.">e</a></sup>before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 10:5–6; Luke 10:10–12; Joshua 13:18"> And I sent ambassadors from the wilderness of Kedamoth to Seon king of Esebon with peaceable words, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 11:19; Deuteronomy 2:6; Numbers 21:21–23"> I will pass through thy land: I will go by the road, I will not turn aside to the right hand or to the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 20:19"> Thou shalt give me food for money, and I will eat; and thou shalt give me water for money, and I will drink; I will only go through on my feet:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 20:18; Judges 11:17–18; Deuteronomy 23:3–4"> as the sons of Esau did to me, who dwelt in Seir, and the Moabites who dwelt in Aroer, until I shall have passed Jordan into the land which the Lord our God gives us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 21:23; Exodus 4:21; Joshua 11:19–20"> And Seon king of Esebon would not that we should pass by him, because the Lord our God hardened his spirit, and made his heart stubborn, that he might be delivered into thy hands, as on this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:8; Deuteronomy 2:24"> And the Lord said to me, Behold, I have begun to deliver before thee Seon the king of Esebon the Amorite, and his land, and do thou begin to inherit his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 21:23–30; Judges 11:20–23; Psalms 136:19"> And Seon the king of Esebon came forth to meet us, he and all his people to war at Jassa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Joshua 21:44; Deuteronomy 3:2–3"> And the Lord our God delivered him before our face, and we smote him, and his sons, and all his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Kings I 15:3; Deuteronomy 3:6"> And we took possession of all his cities at that time, and we utterly destroyed every city in succession, and their wives, and their children; we left no living prey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Joshua 8:27; Deuteronomy 20:14; Numbers 31:9–11"> Only we took the cattle captive, and took the spoil of the cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Joshua 13:9; Deuteronomy 4:48; Deuteronomy 3:12"> From Aroer, which is by the brink of the brook of Arnon, and the city which is in the valley, and as far as the mount of Galaad; there was not a city which escaped us: the Lord our God delivered all of them into our hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 21:24; Deuteronomy 3:16; Genesis 32:22"> Only we did not draw near to the children of Amman, even all the parts bordering on the brook Jaboc, and the cities in the mountain country, as the Lord our God charged us.</span>
