<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:1; Deuteronomy 12:1; Deuteronomy 4:45"> And these are the commands, and the ordinances, and the judgments, as many as the Lord our God gave commandment to teach you to do so in the land on which ye enter to inherit it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Deuteronomy 13:4; Exodus 20:20"> That ye may fear the Lord your God, keep ye all his ordinances, and his commandments, which I command thee to-day, thou, and thy sons, and thy sons' sons, all the days of thy life, that ye may live many days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Exodus 3:8; Genesis 26:4"> Hear, therefore, O Israel, and observe to do them, that it may be well with thee, and that ye may be greatly multiplied, as the Lord God of thy fathers said that he would give thee a land flowing with milk and honey: and these are the ordinances, and the judgments, which the Lord commanded the children of Israel in the wilderness, when they had gone forth from the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 12:29–32; Esaias 44:6; 1 Timothy 2:5"> Hear, O Israel, The Lord our God is one Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 12:30; Matthew 22:37; Luke 10:27"> And thou shalt love the Lord thy God with all thy mind, and with all thy soul, and all thy strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; Proverbs 7:3; Jeremias 31:33"> And these words, all that I command thee this day, shall be in thy heart and in thy soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:19; Ephesians 6:4; Psalms 78:4–6"> And thou shalt teach them to thy children, and thou shalt speak of them sitting in the house, and walking by the way, and lying down, and rising up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 6:21; Proverbs 3:3; Deuteronomy 11:18"> And thou shalt fasten them for a sign upon thy hand, and it shall be immoveable before thine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:20; Esaias 30:8; Esaias 57:8"> And ye shall write them on the lintels of your houses and of your gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 24:13; Psalms 105:44; Nehemiah 9:25"> And it shall come to pass when the Lord thy God shall have brought thee into the land which he sware to thy fathers, to Abraam, and to Isaac, and to Jacob, to give thee great and beautiful cities which thou didst not build,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:12–18; Deuteronomy 8:10–20; Jezekiel 16:10–20"> houses full of all good things which thou didst not fill,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, pits or pools.">a</a></sup>wells dug in the rock which thou didst not dig, vineyards and oliveyards which thou didst not plant, then having eaten and been filled,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> beware lest thou forget the Lord thy God that brought thee forth out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 4:8; Deuteronomy 10:20; Matthew 4:10"> Thou shalt fear the Lord thy God, and him only shalt thou serve; and thou shalt cleave to him, and by his name thou shalt swear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremias 25:6; 1 John 5:21; Deuteronomy 13:7"> Go ye not after other gods of the gods of the nations round about you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:24; Exodus 20:5; Deuteronomy 7:4"> for the Lord thy God in the midst of thee is a jealous God, lest the Lord thy God be very angry with thee, and destroy thee from off the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 17:7; Luke 4:12; Matthew 4:7"> Thou shalt not tempt the Lord thy God, as ye tempted him in the temptation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:22; Psalms 119:4; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> Thou shalt by all means keep the commands of the Lord thy God, the testimonies, and the ordinances, which he commanded thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Deuteronomy 12:25; Romans 12:2"> And thou shalt do that which is pleasing and good before the Lord thy God, that it may be well with thee, and that thou mayest go in and inherit the good land, which the Lord sware to your fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 33:52–53; Exodus 23:28–30; Judges 3:1–4"> to chase all thine enemies from before thy face, as the Lord said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 13:14; Exodus 12:26; Joshua 4:6–7"> And it shall come to pass when thy son shall ask thee<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. to-morrow.">b</a></sup>at a future time, saying, What are the testimonies, and the ordinances, and the judgments, which the Lord our God has commanded us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 6:17–18; Ephesians 2:11–12; Deuteronomy 15:15"> Then shalt thou say to thy son, We were slaves to Pharao in the land of Egypt, and the Lord brought us forth thence with a mighty hand, and with a high arm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:34; Psalms 58:10–11; Deuteronomy 7:19"> And the Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. gave.">c</a></sup>wrought signs and great and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. evil.">d</a></sup>grievous wonders in Egypt, on Pharao and on his house before us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:18; Deuteronomy 6:10; Exodus 13:5"> And he brought us out thence to give us this land, which he sware to give to our fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremias 32:39; Psalms 41:2; Deuteronomy 10:12–13"> And the Lord charged us to observe all these ordinances; to fear the Lord our God, that it may be well with us for ever, that we may live, as even to-day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:13; Romans 10:3; Leviticus 18:5"> And there shall be mercy to us, if we take heed to keep all these commands before the Lord our God, as he has commanded us.</span>
