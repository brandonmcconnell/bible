<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:2–3; Hebrews 8:9; Exodus 24:2–8"> These are the words of the covenant, which the Lord commanded Moses to make with the children of Israel in the land of Moab, besides the covenant which he made with them in Choreb.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 19:4; Exodus 8:12; Joshua 24:5–6"> And Moses called all the sons of Israel and said to them, Ye have seen all things that the Lord did in the land of Egypt before you to Pharao and his servants, and all his land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:32–35; Nehemiah 9:9–11; Deuteronomy 7:18–19"> the great temptations which thine eyes have seen, the signs, and those great wonders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 4:18; Esaias 6:9–10; Proverbs 20:12"> Yet the Lord God has not given you a heart to know, and eyes to see, and ears to hear, until this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:4; Nehemiah 9:21; Deuteronomy 8:2"> And he led you forty years in the wilderness; your garments did not grow old, and your sandals were not worn away off your feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:3; Ephesians 5:18; 1 Corinthians 9:25"> Ye did not eat bread, ye did not drink wine or strong drink, that ye might know that I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:24–17; Numbers 32:33–42; Psalms 135:10–12"> And ye came as far as this place; and there came forth Seon king of Esebon, and Og king of Basan, to meet us in war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:12–13; Numbers 32:33"> And we smote them and took their land, and I gave it for an inheritance to Ruben and Gad, and to the half-tribe of Manasse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings III 2:3; Deuteronomy 4:6; Luke 11:28"> And ye shall take heed to do all the words of this covenant, that ye may understand all things that ye shall do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joel 2:16–17; Revelation 20:12; Deuteronomy 31:12–13"> Ye all stand to-day before the Lord your God, the heads of your tribes, and your elders, and your judges, and your officers, every man of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 12:38; Joshua 9:21–27; Exodus 12:48–49"> your wives, and your children, and the stranger who is in the midst of your camp, from your hewer of wood even to your drawer of water,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Chronicles II 15:12–15; Kings IV 11:17; Deuteronomy 5:2–3"> that thou shouldest enter into the covenant of the Lord thy God and into his oaths, as many as the Lord thy God appoints thee this day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 6:7; Genesis 17:7; Deuteronomy 28:9"> that he may appoint thee to himself for a people, and he shall be thy God, as he said to thee, and as he sware to thy fathers, Abraam, and Isaac, and Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremias 31:31–34; Hebrews 8:7–12"> And I do not appoint to you alone this covenant and this oath;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 2:39; 1 Corinthians 7:14; Jeremias 50:5"> but to those also who are here with you to-day before the Lord your God, and to those who are not here with you to-day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:19; Deuteronomy 2:9; Deuteronomy 2:4"> For ye know how we dwelt in the land of Egypt, how we came through the midst of the nations through whom ye came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:36"> And ye beheld their abominations, and their idols, wood and stone, silver and gold, which are among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 12:15; Deuteronomy 11:16–17; Deuteronomy 13:1–15"> Lest there be among you man, or woman, or family, or tribe, whose heart has turned aside from the Lord your God, having gone to serve the gods of these nations; lest there be in you a root springing up with gall and bitterness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremias 7:24; Numbers 15:30; Numbers 15:39"> And it shall be if one shall hear the words of this curse, and shall flatter himself in his heart, saying,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Lit. May holy things happen to me. See the use of ὅσια in Is. 55. 3; Acts 13. 34.">a</a></sup>Let good happen to me, for I will walk in the error of my heart, lest the sinner destroy the guiltless with him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 74:1; Psalms 79:5; Deuteronomy 9:14"> God shall by no means be willing to pardon him, but then the wrath of the Lord and his jealousy shall flame out against that man; and all the curses of this covenant shall attach themselves to him, which are written in this book, and the Lord shall blot out his name from under heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 25:41; Joshua 7:1–26; Jezekiel 13:9"> And the Lord shall separate that man for evil of all the children of Israel, according to all the curses of the covenant that are written in the book of this law.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremias 19:8"> And another generation shall say—even your sons who shall rise up after you, and the stranger who shall come from a land afar off, and shall see the plagues of that land and their diseases, which the Lord has sent upon it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Sophonias 2:9; Jeremias 17:6; Esaias 34:9"> brimstone and burning salt, (the whole land shall not be sown, neither shall any green thing spring, nor rise upon it, as Sodom and Gomorrha were overthrown, Adama and Seboim, which the Lord overthrew in his wrath and anger:)—</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremias 22:8–9; Kings III 9:8–9; Romans 2:5"> and all the nations shall say, Why has the Lord done thus to this land? what is this great fierceness of anger?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 8:9; Esaias 47:6; Jeremias 50:7"> And men shall say, Because they forsook the covenant of the Lord God of their fathers, the things which he appointed to their fathers, when he brought them out of the land of Egypt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:64; Judges 2:12–13; Judges 5:8"> and they went and served other gods, which they knew not, neither did he assign them to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Daniel 9:11–14; Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Deuteronomy 27:15–26"> And the Lord was exceedingly angry with that land to bring upon it according to all the curses which are written in the book of this law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Chronicles II 7:20; Kings III 14:15; Psalms 52:5"> And the Lord removed them from their land in anger, and wrath, and very great indignation, and cast them out into another land as at present.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Daniel 2:22; Acts 1:7; Romans 11:33–34"> The secret things belong to the Lord our God, but the things that are revealed belong to us and to our children for ever, to do all the words of this law.</span>
</div>
</div>
