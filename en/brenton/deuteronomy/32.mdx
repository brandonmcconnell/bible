<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esaias 1:2; Deuteronomy 4:26; Jeremias 22:29"> Attend, O heaven, and I will speak; and let the earth hear the words out of my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esaias 55:10–11; Psalms 72:6; Michæas 5:7"> Let my speech be looked for as the rain, and my words come down as dew, as the shower upon the herbage, and as snow upon the grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 10:6; Psalms 150:2; Ephesians 1:19"> For I have called on the name of the Lord: assign ye greatness to our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 18:30–31; Psalms 92:15; Esaias 26:4"> As for God, his works are true, and all his ways are<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. judgments.">a</a></sup>judgment: God is faithful, and there is no unrighteousness in him; just and holy is the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esaias 1:4; Philippians 2:15; Matthew 17:17"> They have sinned, not pleasing him; spotted children, a froward and perverse generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esaias 63:16; 1 John 3:1; Esaias 44:2"> Do ye thus recompense the Lord? is the people thus foolish and unwise? did not he himself thy father purchase thee, and make thee, and form thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 78:3–4; Job 8:8–10; Psalms 44:1"> Remember the days of old, consider the years<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. in ages of ages.">b</a></sup>for past ages: ask thy father, and he shall relate to thee, thine elders, and they shall tell thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 17:26; Genesis 11:8–9; Daniel 4:17"> When the Most High divided the nations, when he separated the sons of Adam, he set the bounds of the nations according to the number of the angels of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremias 10:16; 1 Peter 2:9–10; Exodus 19:5–6"> And his people Jacob became the portion of the Lord, Israel was the line of his inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zacharias 2:8; Psalms 17:8; Osee 13:5"> He maintained him in the wilderness, in burning thirst and a dry land: he led him about and instructed him, and kept him as the apple of an eye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 19:4; Esaias 46:4; Esaias 63:9"> As an eagle would watch over his brood, and yearns over his young, receives them having spread his wings, and takes them up on his back:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 78:52–53; Esaias 46:4; Esaias 44:7–8"> the Lord alone led them, there was no strange god with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Esaias 58:14; Job 29:6; Psalms 81:16"> He brought them up on the strength of the land; he fed them with the fruits of the fields; they sucked honey out of the rock, and oil out of the solid rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 49:11; Psalms 81:16; Psalms 147:14"> Butter of cows, and milk of sheep, with the fat of lambs and rams, of calves and kids, with fat of kidneys of wheat; and he drank wine, the blood of the grape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:20; Osee 13:6; Deuteronomy 33:5"> So Jacob ate and was filled, and the beloved one kicked; he grew fat, he became thick and broad: then he forsook the God that made him, and departed from God his Saviour.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 78:58; 1 Corinthians 10:22; Naum 1:1–2"> They provoked me to anger with strange gods; with their abominations they bitterly angered me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 5:8; Deuteronomy 28:64; Leviticus 17:7"> They sacrificed to devils, and not to God; to gods whom they knew not: new and fresh gods came in, whom their fathers knew not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Esaias 17:10; Jeremias 2:32; Psalms 106:21"> Thou hast forsaken God that begot thee, and forgotten God who feeds thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 106:40; Psalms 5:4; Esaias 1:2"> And the Lord saw, and was jealous; and was provoked by the anger of his sons and daughters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:17–18; Luke 7:31–32; Deuteronomy 32:5"> and said, I will turn away my face from them, and will show what shall happen to them in the last days; for it is a perverse generation, sons in whom is no faith.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 10:19; Romans 9:25; Romans 11:11–14"> They have provoked me to jealousy with that which is not God, they have exasperated me with their idols; and I will provoke them to jealousy with them that are no nation, I will anger them with a nation void of understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Lamentations 4:11; Jeremias 15:14; Michæas 1:4"> For a fire has been kindled out of my wrath, it shall burn to hell below; it shall devour the land, and the fruits of it; it shall set on fire the foundations of the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jezekiel 5:16; Psalms 7:12–13; Esaias 24:17–18"> I will gather evils upon them, and will<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. cause my weapons to war together against them.">c</a></sup>fight with my weapons against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 26:22; Jezekiel 5:17; Esaias 65:25"> They shall be consumed with hunger and the devouring of birds, and there shall be irremediable<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. downfall, or falling away.">d</a></sup>destruction: I will send forth against them the teeth of wild beasts, with the rage of serpents creeping on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jezekiel 7:15; Lamentations 1:20; Esaias 30:16"> Without, the sword shall bereave them of children, and terror shall issue out of the secret chambers; the young man shall perish with the virgin, the suckling with him who has grown old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:64; Leviticus 26:33; Deuteronomy 28:37"> I said, I will scatter them, and I will cause their memorial to cease from among men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 14:15–16; Exodus 32:12; Zacharias 1:14–15"> Were it not for the wrath of the enemy, lest they should live long, lest their enemies should combine against them; lest they should say, Our own high arm, and not the Lord, has done all these things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremias 4:22; Esaias 27:11; Job 28:28"> It is a nation that has lost counsel, neither is there understanding in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 81:13; Deuteronomy 5:29; Osee 14:9"> They had not sense to understand: let them reserve these things against the time to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 26:8; Joshua 23:10; Esaias 30:17"> How<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. shall.">e</a></sup>should one pursue a thousand, and two rout tens of thousands, if God had not sold them, and the Lord delivered them up?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 14:25; Kings I 2:2; Daniel 2:47"> For their gods are not as our God, but our enemies are void of understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:18; Jezekiel 16:45–51; Esaias 1:10"> For their vine is of the vine of Sodom, and their vine-branch of Gomorrha: their grape is a grape of gall, their cluster is one of bitterness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 58:4; Romans 3:13; Psalms 140:3"> Their wine is the rage of serpents, and the incurable rage of asps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Osee 13:12; Job 14:17; Jeremias 2:22"> Lo! are not these things stored up by me, and sealed among my treasures?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Romans 12:19; Hebrews 10:30; Naum 1:2"> In the day of vengeance I will recompense, whensoever their foot shall be tripped up; for the day of their destruction is near to them, and the judgments at hand are close upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 135:14; Psalms 106:45; Judges 2:18"> For the Lord shall judge his people, and shall be comforted over his servants; for he saw that they were<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. paralysed.">f</a></sup>utterly weakened, and failed in the hostile invasion, and were become feeble:</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremias 2:28; Judges 10:14; Kings IV 3:13"> and the Lord said, Where are their gods on whom they trusted?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jezekiel 16:18–19; Psalms 50:13; Judges 10:14"> the fat of whose sacrifices ye ate, and ye drank the wine of their drink-offerings? let them arise and help you, and be your protectors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Kings I 2:6; Osee 6:1; Job 5:18"> Behold, behold that I am he, and there is no god beside me: I kill, and I will make to live: I will smite, and I will heal; and there is none who shall deliver out of my hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Hebrews 6:17–18; Genesis 14:22; Revelation 10:5–6"> For I will lift up my hand to heaven, and swear by my right hand, and I will say, I live for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Esaias 66:16; Esaias 34:5–6; Esaias 27:1"> For I will sharpen my sword like lightning, and my hand shall take hold of judgment; and I will render judgment to my enemies, and will recompense them that hate me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:23; Jeremias 46:10; Jezekiel 38:21–22"> I will make my weapons drunk with blood, and my sword shall devour flesh, it shall glut itself with the blood of the wounded, and from the captivity of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Alex. ἐθνῶν, Gentiles.">g</a></sup>heads of their enemies that rule over them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Revelation 19:2; Romans 12:19; Revelation 6:10"> Rejoice, ye heavens, with him, and let all the angels of God worship him; rejoice ye Gentiles, with his people, and let all the sons of God strengthen themselves in him; for he will avenge the blood of his sons, and he will render vengeance, and recompense justice to his enemies, and will reward them that hate him; and the Lord shall purge the land of his people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Numbers 13:8; Numbers 13:16; Deuteronomy 31:30"> And Moses wrote this song in that day, and taught it to the children of Israel; and Moses went in and spoke all the words of this law in the ears of the people, he and Joshua the son of Naue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> And Moses finished speaking to all Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Chronicles I 22:19; Jezekiel 40:4; Proverbs 3:1–4"> And he said to them, Take heed with your heart to all these words, which I testify to you this day, which ye shall command your sons, to observe and do all the words of this law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Proverbs 4:22; Leviticus 18:5; 2 Peter 1:16"> For this is no vain word to you; for it is your life, and because of this word ye shall live long upon the land, into which ye go over Jordan to inherit it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Numbers 27:12–13"> And the Lord spoke to Moses in this day, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Numbers 27:12; Esaias 33:17; Deuteronomy 34:1–5"> Go up to the mount Abarim, this mountain Nabau which is in the land of Moab over against Jericho, and behold the land of Chanaan, which I give to the sons of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Genesis 25:8; Numbers 33:38; Numbers 20:24–29"> and die in the mount whither thou goest up, and be added to thy people; as Aaron thy brother died in mount Or, and was added to his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 27:14; Numbers 20:11–14; Deuteronomy 3:23–27"> Because ye disobeyed my word among the children of Israel, at the waters of strife of Cades in the wilderness of Sin; because ye sanctified me not among the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:1–4; Deuteronomy 3:27; Hebrews 11:39"> Thou shalt see the land before thee, but thou shalt not enter into it.</span>
</div>
