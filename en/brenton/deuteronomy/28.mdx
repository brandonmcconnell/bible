<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esaias 1:19; Exodus 23:22; Leviticus 26:3–13"> And it shall come to pass, if thou wilt indeed hear the voice of the Lord thy God, to observe and do all these commands, which I charge thee this day, that the Lord thy God shall set thee on high above all the nations of the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zacharias 1:6; Deuteronomy 28:15; 1 Timothy 4:8"> and all these blessings shall come upon thee, and shall find thee. If thou wilt indeed hear the voice of the Lord thy God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 144:12–15; Genesis 39:5; Aggæus 2:19"> blessed shalt thou be in the city, and blessed shalt thou be in the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 10:22; Genesis 49:25; Deuteronomy 7:13"> Blessed shall be the offspring of thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. belly.">a</a></sup>body, and the fruits of thy land, and the herds of thy oxen, and the flocks of thy sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Blessed shall be thy barns, and thy stores.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 121:8; Numbers 27:17; Chronicles II 1:10"> Blessed shalt thou be in thy coming in, and blessed shalt thou be in thy going out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings II 22:38–41; Leviticus 26:7–8; Psalms 89:23"> The Lord deliver thine enemies that withstand thee utterly broken before thy face: they shall come out against thee one way, and they shall flee seven ways from before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:10; Leviticus 26:10; Proverbs 3:9–10"> The Lord send upon thee his blessing in thy barns, and on all on which thou shalt put thine hand, in the land which the Lord thy God gives thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 19:5–6; Deuteronomy 7:6; Deuteronomy 26:18–19"> The Lord raise thee up for himself a holy people, as he sware to thy fathers; if thou wilt hear the voice of the Lord thy God, and walk in all his ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Chronicles II 7:14; Deuteronomy 11:25; Jeremias 33:9"> And all the nations of the earth shall see<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, see that the name, etc.">b</a></sup>thee, that the name of the Lord is called upon thee, and they shall stand in awe of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:9; Proverbs 10:22; Leviticus 26:9"> And the Lord thy God shall multiply thee for good in the offspring of thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. belly.">c</a></sup>body, and in the offspring of thy cattle, and in the fruits of thy land, on thy land which the Lord sware to thy fathers to give to thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:6; Joel 2:23–24; Deuteronomy 15:10"> May the Lord open to thee his good treasure, the heaven, to give rain to thy land in season: may he bless all the works of thy hands: so shalt thou lend to many nations, but thou shalt not borrow; and thou shalt rule over many nations, but they shall not rule over thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:1; Deuteronomy 4:6–9; Philippians 1:27"> The Lord thy God make thee the head, and not the tail; and thou shalt then be above and thou shalt not be below, if thou wilt hearken to the voice of the Lord thy God, in all things that I charge thee this day to observe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esaias 30:21; Deuteronomy 5:32; Deuteronomy 11:16"> Thou shalt not turn aside from any of the commandments, which I charge thee this day, to the right hand or to the left, to go after other gods to serve them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Malachias 2:2; Deuteronomy 28:2; Deuteronomy 29:20"> But it shall come to pass, if thou wilt not hearken to the voice of the Lord thy God, to observe all his commandments, as many as I charge thee this day, then all these curses shall come on thee, and overtake thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Malachias 3:9–12; Proverbs 3:33; Joel 1:4"> Cursed shalt thou be in the city, and cursed shalt thou be in the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Aggæus 1:6; Deuteronomy 28:5; Psalms 69:22"> Cursed shall be thy barns and thy stores.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 26:26; Leviticus 26:19–20; Job 18:16–19"> Cursed shall be the offspring of thy body, and the fruits of thy land, the herds of thine oxen, and the flocks of thy sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:6; Judges 5:6–7; Chronicles II 15:5"> Cursed shalt thou be in thy coming in, and cursed shalt thou be in thy going out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Esaias 66:15; Malachias 2:2; Deuteronomy 4:26"> The Lord send upon thee want, and famine, and consumption on all things on which thou shalt put thy hand, until he shall have utterly destroyed thee, and until he shall have consumed thee quickly because of thine evil devices, because thou hast forsaken me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 26:25; Jeremias 24:10; Jeremias 16:4"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. May the Lord.">d</a></sup>The Lord cause the pestilence to cleave to thee, until he shall have consumed thee off the land into which thou goest to inherit it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Amos 4:9; Leviticus 26:16; Aggæus 2:17"> The Lord smite thee with distress, and fever, and cold, and inflammation, and blighting, and paleness, and they shall pursue thee until they have destroyed thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 26:19; Jeremias 14:1–6; Kings III 18:2"> And thou shalt have over thine head a sky of brass, and the earth under thee shall be iron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:12; Esaias 5:24; Amos 4:11"> The Lord thy God make the rain of thy land dust; and dust shall come down from heaven, until it shall have destroyed thee, and until it shall have quickly consumed thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Esaias 30:17; Jezekiel 23:46; Jeremias 24:9"> The Lord give thee up for slaughter before thine enemies: thou shalt go out against them one way, and flee from their face seven ways; and thou shalt be a dispersion in all the kingdoms of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremias 34:20; Jeremias 16:4; Jeremias 7:33"> And your dead men shall be food to the birds of the sky, and to the beasts of the earth; and there shall be none to scare them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Kings I 5:6; Deuteronomy 28:35; Leviticus 21:20"> The Lord smite thee with the botch of Egypt in the seat, and with a malignant scab, and itch, so that thou canst not be healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:9–11; Esaias 19:11–17; Esaias 6:9–10"> The Lord smite thee with insanity, and blindness, and astonishment of mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Esaias 59:10; Job 5:14; Judges 6:1–6"> And thou shalt grope at mid-day, as a blind man would grope in the darkness, and thou shalt not prosper in thy ways; and then thou shalt be unjustly treated, and plundered continually, and there shall be no helper.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Amos 5:11; Jeremias 8:10; Job 31:10"> Thou shalt take a wife, and another man shall have her; thou shalt build a house, and thou shalt not dwell in it; thou shalt plant a vineyard, and shalt not gather the grapes of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Job 1:14–15; Judges 6:1"> Thy calf shall be slain before thee, and thou shalt not eat of it; thine ass shall be violently taken away from thee, and shall not be restored to thee: thy sheep shall be given to thine enemies, and thou shalt have no helper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Joel 3:6; Deuteronomy 28:41; Job 17:5"> Thy sons and thy daughters shall be given to another nation, and thine eyes wasting away shall look for them: thine hand shall have no strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremias 5:17; Deuteronomy 28:29–30; Esaias 1:7"> A nation which thou knowest not shall eat the produce of thy land, and all thy labours; and thou shalt be injured and crushed always.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Revelation 16:10–11; Deuteronomy 28:68; Esaias 33:14"> And thou shalt be distracted, because of the sights of thine eyes which thou shalt see.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:27; Esaias 3:24; Esaias 3:17"> The Lord smite thee with an evil sore, on the knees and the legs, so that thou shalt not be able to be healed from the sole of thy foot to the crown of thy head.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremias 16:13; Deuteronomy 4:28; Kings IV 25:11"> The Lord carry away thee and thy princes, whom thou shalt set over thee, to a nation which neither thou nor thy fathers know; and thou shalt there serve other gods, wood and stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremias 25:9; Kings III 9:7–8; Jeremias 24:9"> And thou shalt be there for a wonder, and a parable, and a tale, among all the nations, to which the Lord thy God shall carry thee away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Aggæus 1:6; Michæas 6:15; Joel 1:4"> Thou shalt carry forth much seed into the field, and thou shalt bring in little, because the locust shall devour it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Esaias 17:10–11; Joel 2:2–4; Jonas 4:7"> Thou shalt plant a vineyard, and dress it, and shalt not drink the wine, neither shalt thou delight thyself with it, because the worm shall devour<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. them. i. e. the fruits of it.">e</a></sup>it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Michæas 6:15; Psalms 23:5; Psalms 104:15"> Thou shalt have olive trees in all thy borders, and thou shalt not anoint thee with oil, because thine olive shall utterly<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Lit. flow, or fall down.">f</a></sup>cast its fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:32; Lamentations 1:5; Kings IV 24:14"> Thou shalt beget sons and daughters, and they shall not be thine, for they shall depart into captivity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Amos 7:1–2; Deuteronomy 28:38–39"> All thy trees and the fruits of thy land shall the blight consume.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:13; Kings I 13:19–23; Kings IV 17:20"> The stranger that is within thee shall get up very high, and thou shalt come down very low.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:12–13; Lamentations 1:5"> He shall lend to thee, and thou shalt not lend to him: he shall be the head, and thou shalt be the tail.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:15; Esaias 1:20; Jeremias 24:9–10"> And all these curses shall come upon thee, and shall pursue thee, and shall overtake thee, until he shall have consumed thee, and until he shall have destroyed thee; because thou didst not hearken to the voice of the Lord thy God, to keep his commands, and his ordinances which he has commanded thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Esaias 8:18; Deuteronomy 28:37; Jezekiel 14:8"> And these things shall be signs in thee, and wonders among thy seed for ever;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:11; Deuteronomy 12:7–12; Nehemiah 9:35–37"> because thou didst not serve the Lord thy God with gladness and a good heart, because of the abundance of all things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Jeremias 28:13–14; Lamentations 5:2–6; Jezekiel 4:16–17"> And thou shalt serve thine enemies, which the Lord will send forth against thee, in hunger, and in thirst, and in nakedness, and in the want of all things; and thou shalt wear upon thy neck a yoke of iron until he shall have destroyed thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Lamentations 4:19; Jeremias 49:22; Jeremias 48:40"> The Lord shall bring upon thee a nation from the extremity of the earth, like the swift flying of an eagle, a nation whose voice thou shalt not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. bear.">g</a></sup>understand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Esaias 47:6; Chronicles II 36:17; Luke 21:23–24"> a nation bold in countenance, which shall not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. wonder at.">h</a></sup>respect the person of the aged and shall not pity the young.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:33; Esaias 62:8; Leviticus 26:26"> And it shall eat up the young of thy cattle, and the fruits of thy land, so as not to leave to thee corn, wine, oil, the herds of thine oxen, and the flocks of thy sheep, until it shall have destroyed thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Jeremias 52:4–7; Jezekiel 4:1–8; Matthew 24:15–16"> and have utterly crushed thee in thy cities, until the high and strong walls be destroyed, in which thou trustest, in all thy land; and it shall afflict thee in thy cities, which he has given to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Lamentations 4:10; Jeremias 19:9; Leviticus 26:29"> And thou shalt eat the fruit of thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. belly.">i</a></sup>body, the flesh of thy sons and of thy daughters, all that he has given thee, in thy straitness and thy affliction, with which thine enemy shall afflict thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:9; Matthew 20:15; Deuteronomy 13:6"> He that is tender and very delicate within thee shall look with an evil eye upon his brother, and the wife in his bosom, and the children that are left, which may have been left to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Jeremias 34:2; Jeremias 52:6; Jeremias 5:10"> so as not to give to one of them of the flesh of his children, whom he shall eat, because of his having nothing left him in thy straitness, and in thy affliction, with which thine enemies shall afflict thee in all thy cities.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:54; Esaias 3:16; Lamentations 4:3–6"> And she that is tender and delicate among you, whose foot has not assayed to go upon the earth for delicacy and tenderness, shall look with an evil eye on her husband in her bosom, and her son and her daughter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Esaias 49:15; Genesis 49:10"> and her<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Lit. afterbirth.">j</a></sup>offspring that comes out between her feet, and the child which she shall bear; for she shall eat them because of the want of all things, secretly in thy straitness, and in thy affliction, with which thine enemy shall afflict thee in thy cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Esaias 42:8; Jeremias 7:9–10; Malachias 1:14"> If thou wilt not hearken to do all the words of this law, which have been written in this book, to fear this glorious and wonderful name, the Lord thy God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Mark 13:19; Deuteronomy 28:46; Daniel 9:12"> then the Lord shall magnify thy plagues, and the plagues of thy seed, great and wonderful plagues, and evil and abiding diseases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:15; Exodus 15:26; Deuteronomy 28:27"> And he shall bring upon thee all the evil pain of Egypt,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gr. which thou feardst before their face. Hebraism.">k</a></sup>of which thou wast afraid, and they shall cleave to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:25–26"> And the Lord shall bring upon thee every sickness, and every plague that is not written, and every one that is written in the book of this law, until he shall have destroyed thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:22; Nehemiah 9:23; Deuteronomy 4:27"> And ye shall be left few in number, whereas ye were as the stars of the sky in multitude; because thou didst not hearken to the voice of the Lord thy God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Proverbs 1:26; Jeremias 32:41; Deuteronomy 30:9"> And it shall come to pass that as the Lord rejoiced over you to do you good, and to multiply you, so the Lord will rejoice over you to destroy you; and ye shall be quickly removed from the land, into which ye go to inherit it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:27–28; Leviticus 26:33; Jeremias 16:13"> And the Lord thy God shall scatter thee among all nations, from one end of the earth to the other; and thou shalt there serve other gods, wood and stone, which thou hast not known, nor thy fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Leviticus 26:36; Romans 11:10; Amos 9:4"> Moreover among those nations he will not give thee quiet, neither by any means shall the sole of thy foot have rest; and the Lord shall give thee there another and a misgiving heart, and failing eyes, and a wasting soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Lamentations 1:13; Hebrews 10:27; Revelation 6:15–17"> And thy life shall be in suspense before thine eyes; and thou shalt be afraid by day and by night, and thou shalt have no assurance of thy life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:34; Job 7:3–4; Revelation 9:6"> In the morning thou shalt say, Would it were evening! and in the evening thou shalt say, Would it were morning! for the fear of thine heart with which thou shalt fear, and for the sights of thine eyes which thou shalt see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Jeremias 44:12; Jeremias 43:7; Osee 8:13"> And the Lord shall bring thee back to Egypt in ships, by the way of which I said, Thou shalt not see it again; and ye shall be sold there to your enemies for bondmen and bondwomen, and none shall buy you.</span>
