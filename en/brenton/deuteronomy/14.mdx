<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 21:5; Romans 9:8; Galatians 3:26"> Ye are the children of the Lord your God: ye shall not make any baldness between your eyes for the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6; Leviticus 20:26; Exodus 19:5–6"> For thou art a holy people to the Lord thy God, and the Lord thy God has chosen thee to be a peculiar people to himself of all the nations on the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jezekiel 4:14; Romans 14:14; Leviticus 20:25"> Ye shall not eat any abominable thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 11:2–45; Acts 10:14; Kings III 4:23"> These are the beasts which ye shall eat; the calf of the herd, and lamb of the sheep, and kid of the goats;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> the stag, and doe, and pygarg, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, buffalo.">a</a></sup>wild goat, and camelopard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 18:1; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Psalms 1:1–2"> Every beast that divides the hoofs, and makes claws of two divisions, and that chews the cud among beasts, these ye shall eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:22–23; Titus 1:16; 2 Timothy 3:5"> And these ye shall not eat of them that chew the cud, and of those that divide the hoofs, and make distinct claws; the camel, and the hare, and the rabbit; because they chew the cud, and do not divide the hoof, these are unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 11:26–27; Esaias 66:3; 2 Peter 2:22"> And as for the swine, because he divides the hoof, and makes claws of the hoof, yet he chews not the cud, he is unclean to you; ye shall not eat of their flesh, ye shall not touch their dead bodies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 11:9–12"> And these ye shall eat of all that are in the water, ye shall eat all that have fins and scales.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And all that have not fins and scales ye shall not eat; they are unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Ye shall eat every clean bird.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 11:13–19"> And these of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i. e. birds.">b</a></sup>them ye shall not eat; the eagle, and the ossifrage, and the sea-eagle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and the vulture, and the kite and the like to it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 30:29"> and the sparrow, and the owl, and the sea-mew,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> and the heron, and the swan, and the stork,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> and the cormorant, and the hawk, and its like, and the hoopoe, and the raven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and the pelican, and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, heron.">c</a></sup>diver and the like to it, and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or, flamingo.">d</a></sup>red-bill and the bat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philippians 3:19; Leviticus 11:20–23"> All winged animals that creep are unclean to you; ye shall not eat of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Ye shall eat every clean bird.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 23:19; Exodus 34:26; Leviticus 22:8"> Ye shall eat nothing that dies of itself; it shall be given to the sojourner in thy cities and he shall eat it, or thou shalt sell it to a stranger, because thou art a holy people to the Lord thy God. Thou shalt not boil a lamb in his mother's milk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:37; Deuteronomy 12:17; Deuteronomy 26:12–15"> Thou shalt tithe a tenth of all the produce of thy seed, the fruit of thy field year by year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5–7; Deuteronomy 4:10; Deuteronomy 12:17–18"> And thou shalt eat it in the place which the Lord thy God shall choose to have his name called there; ye shall bring the tithe of thy corn and of thy wine, and of thine oil, the first-born of thy herd and of thy flock, that thou mayest learn to fear the Lord thy God always.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:21; Deuteronomy 12:5; Deuteronomy 11:24"> And if the journey be too far for thee, and thou art not able to bring them, because the place is far from thee which the Lord thy God shall choose to have his name called there, because the Lord thy God will bless thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> then thou shalt sell them for money, and thou shalt take the money in thy hands, and thou shalt go to the place which the Lord thy God shall choose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:7; Deuteronomy 12:7; Matthew 21:12"> And thou shalt give the money for whatsoever thy soul shall desire, for oxen or for sheep, or for wine, or thou shalt lay it out on strong drink, or on whatsoever thy soul may desire, and thou shalt eat there before the Lord thy God, and thou shalt rejoice and thy house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 18:20; Deuteronomy 14:29; Deuteronomy 12:12"> and the Levite that is in thy cities, because he has not a portion or inheritance with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:22; Amos 4:4; Deuteronomy 26:12–15"> After three years thou shalt bring out all the tithes of thy fruits, in that year thou shalt lay it up in thy cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:10; Deuteronomy 14:27; Proverbs 19:17"> And the Levite shall come, because he has no part or lot with thee, and the stranger, and the orphan, and the widow which is in thy cities; and they shall eat and be filled, that the Lord thy God may bless thee in all the works which thou shalt do.</span>
