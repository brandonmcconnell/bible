<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings I 25:1; Joshua 22:7; Numbers 33:17–18"> These are the words which Moses spoke to all Israel on this side Jordan in the desert towards the west near the Red Sea, between Pharan Tophol, and Lobon, and Aulon, and the gold works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 32:8; Numbers 13:26; Deuteronomy 9:23"> It is a journey of eleven days from Choreb to mount Seir as far as Cades Barne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 33:38; Deuteronomy 4:1–2; Numbers 20:1"> And it came to pass in the fortieth year, in the eleventh month, on the first day of the month, Moses spoke to all the children of Israel, according to all things which the Lord commanded him for them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 21:21–35; Joshua 12:2–6; Joshua 13:10–12"> after he had smitten Seon king of the Amorites who dwelt in Esebon, and Og the king of Basan who dwelt in Astaroth and in Edrain;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:8; Deuteronomy 32:46; Deuteronomy 31:9"> beyond Jordan in the land of Moab, Moses began to declare this law, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; Numbers 10:11–13; Exodus 19:1–2"> The Lord your God spoke to us in Choreb, saying, Let it suffice you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. to dwell.">a</a></sup>to have dwelt so long in this mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:24; Joshua 10:40; Amos 2:9"> Turn ye and depart and enter into the mountain of the Amorites, and go to all that dwell near about Araba, to the mountain and the plain and to the south, and the land of the Chananites near the sea, and Antilibanus, as far as the great river, the river Euphrates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; Genesis 12:7; Genesis 26:3–4"> Behold, God has delivered the land before you; go in and inherit the land, which I sware to your fathers, Abraam, and Isaac, and Jacob, to give it to them and to their seed after them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 18:18; Numbers 11:17; Numbers 11:11–14"> And I spoke to you at that time, saying, I shall not be able by myself to bear you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:22; Exodus 32:13; Genesis 22:17"> The Lord your God has multiplied you, and, behold, ye are to-day as the stars of heaven for multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 115:14; Genesis 22:17; Numbers 6:27"> The Lord God of your fathers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, increase you.">b</a></sup>add to you a thousand-fold more than you are, and bless you as he has spoken to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:5; Kings III 3:7–9; 2 Corinthians 2:16"> How shall I alone be able to bear your labour, and your burden, and your gainsayings?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; Acts 1:21–23; Numbers 11:16–17"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. give.">c</a></sup>Take to yourselves wise and understanding and prudent men for your tribes, and I will set your leaders over you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And ye answered me and said, The thing which thou hast told us is good to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:18; Exodus 18:25–26; Ephesians 4:11"> So I took of you wise and understanding and prudent men, and I set them to rule over you as rulers of thousands, and rulers of hundreds, and rulers of fifties, and rulers of tens, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Perhaps, recorders: more lit. instructors in reading and writing.">d</a></sup>officers to your judges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 7:24; Exodus 22:21; Chronicles II 19:6–10"> And I charged your judges at that time, saying, Hear causes between your brethren, and judge rightly between a man and his brother, and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. his stranger.">e</a></sup>stranger that is with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Exodus 18:26; James 2:9"> Thou shalt not have respect to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. a face.">f</a></sup>persons in judgment, thou shalt judge<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. according to small and great.">g</a></sup>small and great equally; thou shalt not shrink from before the person of a man, for the judgment is God's; and whatsoever matter shall be too hard for you, ye shall bring it to me, and I will hear it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; Deuteronomy 4:5; Acts 20:20"> And I charged upon you at that time all the commands which ye shall perform.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:2; Deuteronomy 8:15; Jeremias 2:6"> And we departed from Choreb, and went through all that great wilderness and terrible, which ye saw, by the way of the mountain of the Amorite, as the Lord our God charged us, and we came as far as Cades Barne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:7–8"> And I said to you, Ye have come as far as the mountain of the Amorite, which the Lord our God gives to you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Psalms 46:1; Numbers 14:8–9"> behold, the Lord your God has delivered to us the land before you: go up and inherit it as the Lord God of your fathers said to you; fear not, neither be afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 13:1–20"> And ye all came to me, and said, Let us send men before us, and let them go up to the land for us; and let them bring back to us a report of the way by which we shall go up, and of the cities into which we shall enter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 13:3–33"> And the saying pleased me: and I took of you twelve men, one man of a tribe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 13:21–27; Joshua 2:1–2"> And they turned and went up to the mountain, and they came as far as the valley of the cluster, and surveyed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And they took in their hands of the fruit of the land, and brought it to you, and said, The land is good which the Lord our God gives us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 14:1–4; Acts 7:51; Psalms 106:24–25"> Yet ye would not go up, but rebelled against the words of the Lord our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:28; Psalms 106:25; Numbers 14:3"> And ye murmured in your tents, and said, Because the Lord hated us, he has brought us out of the land of Egypt to deliver us into the hands of the Amorites, to destroy us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 13:28–33; Deuteronomy 9:1–2; Joshua 15:14"> Whither do we go up? and your brethren drew away your heart, saying, It is a great nation and populous, and mightier than we; and there are cities great and walled up to heaven: moreover we saw there the sons of the giants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And I said to you, Fear not, neither be ye afraid of them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 14:14; Nehemiah 4:20; Romans 8:31"> the Lord your God who goes before your face, he shall fight against them together with you effectually, according to all that he wrought for you in the land of Egypt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Esaias 46:3–4; Acts 13:18; Osee 11:3–4"> and in this wilderness which ye saw, by the way of the mountain of the Amorite;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="See Acts 13. 18, and note in the margin of English Bible on ἐτροφόρησεν.">h</a></sup>how the Lord thy God will bear thee as a nursling, as if any man should nurse his child, through all the way which ye have gone until ye came to this place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 106:24; Jude 1:5; Hebrews 3:18–19"> And in this matter ye believed not the Lord our God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 10:33; Psalms 78:14; Nehemiah 9:12"> who goes before you in the way to choose you a place, guiding you in fire by night, shewing you the way by which ye go, and a cloud by day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 14:22–30; Psalms 95:11; Jezekiel 20:15"> And the Lord heard the voice of your words, and being greatly provoked he sware, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 95:11"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. If one.">i</a></sup>Not one of these men shall see this good land, which I sware to their fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Numbers 14:24; Joshua 14:6–14; Numbers 32:12"> except Chaleb the son of Jephonne, he shall see it; and to him I will give the land on which he went up, and to his sons, because he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or, followed closely after the Lord.">j</a></sup>attended to the things of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 20:12; Deuteronomy 4:21; Deuteronomy 34:4"> And the Lord was angry with me for your sake, saying, Neither shalt thou by any means enter therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:28; Numbers 14:30; Deuteronomy 31:7–8"> Joshua the son of Naue, who stands by thee, he shall enter in there; do thou strengthen him, for he shall cause Israel to inherit it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Esaias 7:15–16; Numbers 14:3; Numbers 14:31"> And every young child who this day knows not good or evil,—they shall enter therein, and to them I will give it, and they shall inherit it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 14:25"> And ye turned and marched into the wilderness, in the way by the Red Sea.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 14:39–45; Proverbs 19:3; Numbers 22:34"> And ye answered and said, We have sinned before the Lord our God; we will go up and fight according to all that the Lord our God has commanded us: and having taken every one his weapons of war, and being gathered together, ye<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gr. go up.">k</a></sup>went up to the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Numbers 14:41–43; Joshua 7:8–13; Esaias 59:1–2"> And the Lord said to me, Tell them, Ye shall not go up, neither shall ye fight, for I am not with you; thus shall ye not be destroyed before your enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 14:44; Esaias 63:10; Acts 7:51"> And I spoke to you, and ye did not hearken to me; and ye transgressed the commandment of the Lord; and ye forced your way and went up into the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 118:12; Numbers 14:45; Numbers 21:3"> And the Amorite who dwelt in that mountain came out to meet you, and pursued you as bees do, and wounded you from Seir to Herma.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Hebrews 12:17; Psalms 78:34"> And ye sat down and wept before the Lord our God, and the Lord hearkened not to your voice, neither did he take heed to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Numbers 20:1; Numbers 20:22; Judges 11:16–17"> And ye dwelt in Cades many days, as many days as ye dwelt there.</span>
</div>
