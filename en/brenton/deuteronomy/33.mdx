<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 14:6; Psalms 90:1; Genesis 27:4"> And this is the blessing with which Moses the man of God blessed the children of Israel before his death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jude 1:14; Ambacum 3:3; Revelation 5:11"> And he said, The Lord is come from Sina, and has appeared from Seir to us, and has hasted out of the mount of Pharan, with the ten thousands of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Heb. קרש, saints.">a</a></sup>Cades; on his right hand were his angels with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 10:39; Malachias 1:2; Deuteronomy 7:6–8"> And he spared his people, and all his sanctified ones are under thy hands; and they are under thee; and he received of his words</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 1:17; Psalms 119:111; John 7:19"> the law which Moses charged us, an inheritance to the assemblies of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:15; Judges 17:6; Numbers 23:21"> And he shall be prince with the beloved one, when the princes of the people are gathered together with the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 49:8; Numbers 32:31–32; Joshua 22:1–9"> Let Ruben live, and not die; and let him be many in number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 49:8–12; Psalms 20:2; Kings II 7:9–12"> And this is the blessing of Juda; Hear, Lord, the voice of Juda, and do thou visit his people: his hands shall contend for him, and thou shalt be a help from his enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 17:7; Exodus 28:30; Numbers 20:13"> And to Levi he said, Give to Levi his manifestations, and his truth to the holy man, whom they tempted in the temptation; they reviled him at the water of strife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; Exodus 32:25–29; Matthew 12:48"> Who says to his father and mother, I have not seen thee; and he knew not his brethren, and he refused to know his sons: he kept thine oracles, and observed thy covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 10:11; Psalms 51:19; Deuteronomy 17:9–11"> They shall declare thine ordinances to Jacob, and thy law to Israel: they shall place incense in the time of thy wrath continually upon thine altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings II 24:23; Jezekiel 43:27; Jezekiel 20:40–41"> Bless, Lord, his strength, and accept the works of his hands; break the loins of his enemies that have risen up against him, and let not them that hate him rise up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Chronicles II 15:2; Psalms 91:4; Matthew 23:37"> And to Benjamin he said, The beloved of the Lord shall dwell in confidence, and God overshadows him always, and he rested between his shoulders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 49:22–26; Michæas 5:7; Esaias 18:4"> And to Joseph he said, His land is of the blessing of the Lord, of the seasons of sky and dew, and of the deeps of wells below,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 104:19; Matthew 5:45; Psalms 8:3"> and of the fruits of the changes of the sun in season, and of the produce of the months,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ambacum 3:6; Genesis 49:26; James 5:7"> from the top of the ancient mountains, and from the top of the everlasting hills,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 7:35; Psalms 24:1; Exodus 3:2–4"> and of the fulness of the land in season: and let the things pleasing to him that dwelt in the bush come on the head of Joseph, and on the crown of him who was glorified above his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings III 22:11; Numbers 23:22; Psalms 44:5"> His beauty is as the firstling of his bull, his horns are the horns of a unicorn; with them he shall thrust the nations at once, even from the end of the earth: these are the ten thousands of Ephraim, and these are the thousands of Manasse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 49:13–15; Joshua 19:11; Judges 5:14"> And to Zabulon he said, Rejoice, Zabulon, in thy going out, and Issachar in his tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esaias 60:5; Esaias 2:3; Psalms 4:5"> They shall utterly destroy the nations, and ye shall call men there, and there offer the sacrifice of righteousness; for the wealth of the sea shall suckle thee, and so shall the marts of them that dwell by the sea-coast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 49:19; Genesis 9:26–27; Chronicles I 5:18–21"> And to Gad he said, Blessed be he that enlarges Gad: as a lion he rested, having broken the arm and the ruler.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 32:16–42; Judges 5:11; Judges 5:2"> And he saw his first-fruits, that there the land of the princes gathered with the chiefs of the people was divided; the Lord wrought righteousness, and his judgment with Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 18:27; Joshua 19:47; Judges 15:8"> And to Dan he said, Dan is a lion's whelp, and shall leap out of Basan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 49:21; Psalms 36:8; Matthew 4:16"> And to Nephthali he said, Nephthali has the fulness of good things; and let him be filled with blessing from the Lord: he shall inherit the west and the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 49:20; Job 29:6; Romans 14:18"> And to Aser he said, Aser is blessed with children; and he shall be acceptable to his brethren: he shall dip his foot in oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Esaias 41:10; Psalms 138:3; Philippians 4:13"> His sandal shall be iron and brass; as thy days, so shall be thy strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 15:11; Esaias 43:11–13; Jeremias 10:6"> There is not any such as the God of the beloved; he who rides upon the heaven is thy helper, and the magnificent One of the firmament.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 18:2; Psalms 90:1–2; Psalms 46:1"> And the rule of God shall protect thee, and that under the strength of the everlasting arms; and he shall cast forth the enemy from before thy face, saying, Perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 23:9; Genesis 27:28; Exodus 33:16"> And Israel shall dwell in confidence alone on the land of Jacob, with corn and wine; and the sky shall be misty with dew upon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Esaias 45:17; Esaias 12:2; Psalms 115:9–11"> Blessed art thou, O Israel; who is like to thee, O people saved by the Lord? thy helper shall hold his shield over thee, and his sword is thy boast; and thine enemies shall speak falsely to thee, and thou shalt tread upon their neck.</span>
