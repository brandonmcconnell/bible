<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 18:3; James 2:2–3; Luke 15:30"> And they shall scourge him with forty stripes in number, they shall not inflict more; for if thou shouldest scourge him with more stripes beyond these stripes, thy brother will be disgraced before thee.</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:19; Psalms 9:5; Psalms 109:13"> And it shall come to pass that the child which she shall bear, shall be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. constituted.">a</a></sup>named by the name of the deceased, and his name shall not be blotted out of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ruth 4:5–6; Ruth 4:1–2"> And if the man should not be willing to take his brother's wife, then shall the woman go up to the gate to the elders, and she shall say, My husband's brother will not raise up the name of his brother in Israel, my husband's brother<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. has not been willing.">b</a></sup>has refused.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ruth 4:6"> And the elders of his city shall call him, and speak to him; and if he stand and say, I will not take her:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ruth 4:7–8; Numbers 12:14; Esaias 50:6"> then his brother's wife shall come forward before the elders, and shall loose one shoe from off his foot, and shall spit in his face, and shall answer and say, Thus shall they do to the man who will not build his brother's house in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And his name shall be called in Israel, The house of him that has had his shoe loosed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:9; Romans 3:8"> And if men should strive together, a man with his brother, and the wife of one of them should advance to rescue her husband out of the hand of him that smites him, and she should stretch forth her hand, and take hold of his private parts;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:13; Deuteronomy 19:21; Deuteronomy 7:2"> thou shalt cut off her hand; thine eye shall not spare her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 11:1; Proverbs 16:11; Leviticus 19:35–37"> Thou shalt not have in thy bag divers weights, a great<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. or.">c</a></sup>and a small.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Thou shalt not have in thine house divers measures, a great<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. or.">d</a></sup>and a small.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Deuteronomy 4:40; Ephesians 6:3"> Thou shalt have a true and just weight, and a true and just measure, that thou mayest live long upon the land which the Lord thy God gives thee for an inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 11:1; Revelation 21:27; Deuteronomy 22:5"> For every one that does this is an abomination to the Lord thy God, even every one that does injustice.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 17:8–16; Numbers 25:17–18; Numbers 24:20"> Remember what things Amalec did to thee by the way, when thou wentest forth out of the land of Egypt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 3:18; Psalms 36:1; Proverbs 16:6"> how he withstood thee in the way, and harassed thy rear, even those that were weary behind thee, and thou didst hunger and wast weary; and he did not fear God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 17:14; Kings I 14:48; Exodus 17:16"> And it shall come to pass whenever the Lord thy God shall have given thee rest from all thine enemies round about thee, in the land which the Lord thy God gives thee to inherit, thou shalt blot out the name of Amalec from under heaven, and shalt not forget to do it.</span>
