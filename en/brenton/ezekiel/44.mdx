<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jezekiel 43:1; Jezekiel 40:17; Jezekiel 42:14"> Then he brought me back by the way of the outer gate of the sanctuary that looks eastward; and it was shut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jezekiel 43:2–4; Exodus 24:10; Esaias 6:1–5"> And the Lord said to me, This gate shall be shut, it shall not be opened, and no one shall pass through it; for the Lord God of Israel shall enter by it, and it shall be shut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jezekiel 46:2; Jezekiel 34:24; Jezekiel 37:25"> For the prince, he shall sit in it, to eat bread before the Lord; he shall go in by the way of the porch of the gate, and shall go forth by the way of the same.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jezekiel 1:28; Revelation 1:17; Jezekiel 3:23"> And he brought me in by the way of the gate that looks northward, in front of the house: and I looked, and, behold, the house was full of the glory of the Lord: and I fell upon my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jezekiel 40:4; Jezekiel 43:10–11; Deuteronomy 12:32"> And the Lord said to me, Son of man, attend with thine heart, and see with thine eyes, and hear with thine ears all that I say to thee, according to all the ordinances of the house of the Lord, and all the regulations thereof; and thou shalt attend well to the entrance of the house, according to all its outlets, in all the holy things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jezekiel 3:9; Jezekiel 45:9; 1 Peter 4:3"> And thou shalt say to the provoking house, even to the house of Israel, Thus saith the Lord God; Let it suffice you to have committed all your iniquities, O house of Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 22:25; Jeremias 9:26; Jezekiel 44:9"> that ye have brought in aliens, uncircumcised in heart, and uncircumcised in flesh, to be in my sanctuary, and to profane it, when ye offered bread, flesh, and blood; and ye transgressed my covenant by all your iniquities;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jezekiel 44:14; Numbers 18:3–5; Jezekiel 40:45–46"> and ye appointed others to keep the charges in my sanctuary.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joel 3:17; Jezekiel 44:7; Zacharias 14:21"> Therefore thus saith the Lord God; No alien, uncircumcised in heart or uncircumcised in flesh, shall enter into my sanctuary, of all the children of strangers that are in the midst of the house of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jezekiel 22:26; Jezekiel 48:11; Kings IV 23:8–9"> But as for the Levites who departed far from me when Israel went astray from me after their imaginations, they shall even bear their iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 16:9; Chronicles II 29:34; Chronicles II 30:17"> Yet they shall minister in my sanctuary, being porters at the gates of the house, and serving the house: they shall slay the victims and the whole-burnt-offerings for the people, and they shall stand before the people to minister to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings IV 16:10–16; Psalms 106:26; Jezekiel 14:3–4"> Because they ministered to them before their idols, and it became to the house of Israel a punishment of iniquity; therefore have I lifted up my hand against them, saith the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 18:3; Kings IV 23:9; Jezekiel 16:61"> And they shall not draw nigh to me to minister to me in the priests' office, nor to approach the holy things of the children of Israel, nor to approach my holy of holies: but they shall bear their reproach for the error wherein they erred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Chronicles I 23:28–32; Numbers 18:4"> They shall bring them to keep the charges of the house, for all the service of it, and for all that they shall do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:8; Jezekiel 48:11; Jezekiel 44:7"> The priests the Levites, the sons of Sadduc, who kept the charges of my sanctuary when the house of Israel went astray from me, these shall draw nigh to me to minister to me, and shall stand before my face, to offer sacrifice to me, the fat and the blood, saith the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jezekiel 41:22; Malachias 1:12; Numbers 18:7–8"> These shall enter into my sanctuary, and these shall approach my table, to minister to me, and they shall keep my charges.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 19:8; Exodus 28:43; Exodus 39:27–29"> And it shall come to pass when they enter the gates of the inner court, that they shall put on linen robes; and they shall not put on woollen garments when they minister at the gate of the inner court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 39:28; 1 Corinthians 11:4–10; Esaias 3:20"> And they shall have linen mitres upon their heads, and shall have linen drawers upon their loins; and they shall not tightly gird themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jezekiel 46:20; Leviticus 6:27; Leviticus 6:10–11"> And when they go out into the outer court to the people, they shall put off their robes, in which they minister; and they shall lay them up in the chambers of the sanctuary, and shall put on other robes, and they shall not sanctify the people with their robes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:14; Numbers 6:5; Leviticus 21:5–24"> And they shall not shave their heads, nor shall they pluck off their hair; they shall carefully cover their heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 10:9; Titus 1:7–8; 1 Timothy 5:23"> And no priest shall drink any wine, when they go into the inner court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 21:7; Leviticus 21:13–14; 1 Timothy 3:2"> Neither shall they take to themselves to wife a widow, or one that is put away, but a virgin of the seed of Israel: but if there should happen to be a priest's widow, they shall take her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jezekiel 22:26; Leviticus 10:10–11; 2 Timothy 2:24–25"> And they shall teach my people to distinguish between holy and profane, and they shall make known to them the difference between unclean and clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Chronicles I 23:4; Jezekiel 22:26; Chronicles II 19:8–10"> And these shall attend at a judgment of blood to decide it: they shall rightly observe my ordinances, and judge my judgments, and keep my statutes and my commandments in all my feasts; and they shall hallow my sabbaths.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:16; Leviticus 22:4; Matthew 8:21–22"> And they shall not go in to the dead body of a man to defile themselves: only a priest may defile himself for a father, or for a mother, or for a son, or for a daughter, or for a brother, or for his sister, who has not been married.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 9:13–14; Numbers 19:11–14; Numbers 6:10–21"> And after he has been cleansed, let him number to himself seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 6:9–11; Jezekiel 44:17; Leviticus 4:3–35"> And on whatsoever day they shall enter into the inner court to minister in the holy place, they shall bring a propitiation, saith the Lord God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 13:33; Numbers 18:20; Deuteronomy 18:1–2"> And it shall be to them for an inheritance: I am their inheritance: and no possession shall be given them among the children of Israel; for I am their possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 18:14; Leviticus 27:21; Leviticus 27:28"> And these shall eat the meat-offerings, and the sin-offerings, and the trespass-offerings; and every special offering in Israel shall be theirs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:35–37; Chronicles II 31:10; Deuteronomy 18:4"> And the first-fruits of all things, and the first-born of all animals and all offerings, of all your first-fruits there shall be a share for the priests; and ye shall give your earliest produce to the priest, to bring your blessings upon your houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 22:8; Exodus 22:31; Deuteronomy 14:21"> And the priests shall eat no bird or beast that dies of itself, or is taken of wild beasts.</span>
</div>
