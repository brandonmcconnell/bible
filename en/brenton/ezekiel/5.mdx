<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jezekiel 15:7; Jeremias 24:9; Jezekiel 21:3"> Behold, I am against thee, and I will execute judgment in the midst of thee in the sight of the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 24:21; Daniel 9:12; Amos 3:2"> And I will do in thee things which I have not done, and the like of which I will not do again, for all thine abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zacharias 2:6; Deuteronomy 28:64; Jezekiel 12:14"> Therefore the fathers shall eat their children in the midst of thee, and children shall eat their fathers; and I will execute judgments in thee, and I will scatter all that are left of thee to every wind.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jezekiel 5:2; Jeremias 15:2; Jeremias 43:10–11"> A fourth part of thee shall be cut off by pestilence, and a fourth part of thee shall be consumed in the midst of thee with famine: and as for another fourth part of thee, I will scatter them to every wind; and a fourth part of thee shall fall by the sword round about thee, and I will draw out a sword after them.</span>
</div>
