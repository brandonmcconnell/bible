<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Osee 1:2–9; Amos 3:2; Jezekiel 12:3–16"> And thou, son of man, take thee a brick, and thou shalt set it before thy face, and shalt portray on it the city, even Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jezekiel 21:22; Jeremias 6:6; Luke 19:42–44"> And thou shalt besiege it, and build works against it, and throw up a mound round about it, and pitch camps against it, and set up engines round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jezekiel 12:6; Esaias 8:18; Esaias 20:3"> And take thou to thyself an iron pan, and thou shalt set it for an iron wall between thee and the city: and thou shalt set thy face against it, and it shall be in a siege, and thou shalt besiege it. This is a sign to the children of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 16:22; Leviticus 10:17; Numbers 18:1"> And thou shalt lie upon thy left side, and lay the iniquities of the house of Israel upon it, according to the number of the hundred and fifty days during which thou shalt lie upon it: and thou shalt bear their iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 14:34; Esaias 53:6"> For I have appointed thee their iniquities for a number of days, for a hundred and ninety days: so thou shalt bear the iniquities of the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:34; Daniel 12:11–12; Daniel 9:24–26"> And thou shalt accomplish this, and then shalt lie on thy right side, and shalt bear the iniquities of the house of Juda forty days: I have appointed thee a day for a year.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esaias 52:10; Jezekiel 21:2; Jezekiel 6:2"> So thou shalt set thy face to the siege of Jerusalem, and shalt strengthen thine arm, and shalt prophesy against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jezekiel 3:25"> And, behold, I have prepared bonds for thee, and thou mayest not turn from thy one side to the other, until the days of thy siege shall be accomplished.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jezekiel 4:13; Esaias 28:25; Jezekiel 4:16"> Take thou also to thee wheat, and barley, and beans, and lentiles, and millet, and bread-corn; and thou shalt cast them into one earthen vessel, and shalt make them into loaves for thyself; and thou shalt eat them a hundred and ninety days, according to the number of the days during which thou sleepest on thy side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 26:26; Jezekiel 45:12; Deuteronomy 28:51–68"> And thou shalt eat thy food by weight, twenty shekels a day: from time to time shalt thou eat them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esaias 5:13; John 3:34; Jezekiel 4:16"> And thou shalt drink water by measure, even from time to time thou shalt drink the sixth part of a hin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esaias 36:12; Genesis 18:6"> And thou shalt eat them as a barley cake: thou shalt bake them before their eyes in man's dung.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 1:8; Osee 9:3–4"> And thou shalt say, Thus saith the Lord God of Israel; Thus shall the children of Israel eat unclean things among the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 10:14; Jezekiel 20:49; Jezekiel 9:8"> Then I said, Not so, Lord God of Israel: surely my soul has not been defiled with uncleanness; nor have I eaten that which died of itself or was torn of beasts from my birth until now; neither has any corrupt flesh entered into my mouth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And he said to me, Behold, I have given thee dung of oxen instead of man's dung, and thou shalt prepare thy loaves upon it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jezekiel 5:16; Esaias 3:1; Leviticus 26:26"> And he said to me, Son of man, behold, I break the support of bread in Jerusalem: and they shall eat bread by weight and in want; and shall drink water by measure, and in a state of ruin:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jezekiel 24:23; Leviticus 26:39; Jezekiel 33:10"> that they may want bread and water; and a man and his brother shall be brought to ruin, and they shall pine away in their iniquities.</span>
</div>
