<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jezekiel 33:21; Jezekiel 1:2–3; Jezekiel 3:14"> And it came to pass in the twenty-fifth year of our captivity, in the first month, on the tenth day of the month, in the fourteenth year after the taking of the city, in that day the hand of the Lord was upon me, and brought me</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jezekiel 1:1; Daniel 7:1; Daniel 7:7"> in a vision of God into the land of Israel, and set me on a very high mountain, and upon it there was as it were the frame of a city before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jezekiel 47:3; Revelation 11:1; Daniel 10:5–6"> And he brought me in thither, and, behold, there was a man, and the appearance of him was as the appearance of shining brass, and in his hand was a builder's line, and a measuring reed; and he stood at the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jezekiel 44:5; Matthew 10:27; Jezekiel 2:7–8"> And the man said to me, Look with thine eyes at him whom thou hast seen, son of man, and hear with thine ears, and lay up in thine heart all things that I show thee; for thou hast come in hither that I might show thee, and thou shalt show all things that thou seest to the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jezekiel 42:20; Esaias 26:1; Revelation 21:12"> And behold a wall round about the house without, and in the man's hand a reed, the measure of it was six cubits by the cubit, and a span: and he measured across the front wall; the breadth was equal to the reed, and the length of it equal to the reed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jezekiel 43:1; Jezekiel 8:16; Jezekiel 11:1"> And he entered by seven steps into the gate that looks eastward, and he measured across the porch of the gate equal to the reed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jezekiel 40:36; Jezekiel 40:33; Chronicles I 23:28"> And the chamber was equal in length to the reed, and equal in breadth to the reed; and the porch between the chambers six cubits; and the second chamber equal in breadth to the reed, and equal in length to the reed, and the porch five cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the third chamber equal in length to the reed, and equal in breadth to the reed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jezekiel 45:19"> And the porch of the gateway (near the porch of the gate) eight cubits; and the posts thereof two cubits; and the porch of the gate was inward:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> and the chambers of the gate of the chamber in front were three on one side and three on the other, and there was one measure to the three: there was one measure to the porches on this side and on that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And he measured the breadth of the door of the gateway, ten cubits; and the breadth of the gateway thirteen cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And the space before the chambers was narrowed to a cubit in front of the chambers on this side and on that side: and the chamber was six cubits this way, and six cubits that way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And he measured the gate from the wall of one chamber to the wall of the other chamber: the breadth was twenty-five cubits, the one gate over against the other gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:9; Esaias 62:9; Jezekiel 42:1"> And the open space of the porch of the gate without, was twenty cubits to the chambers round about the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the open space of the gate without to the open space of the porch of the gate within was fifty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Chronicles II 3:5; Kings III 6:4; Jezekiel 41:26"> And there were secret windows to the chambers, and to the porches within the gate of the court round about, and in the same manner windows to the porches round about within: and on the porch there were palm-trees on this side and on that side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 11:2; Jezekiel 42:1; Chronicles I 23:28"> And he brought me into the inner court, and, behold, there were chambers, and peristyles round about the court; thirty chambers within the ranges of columns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And the porticos were behind the gates; according to the length of the gates, was the lower peristyle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jezekiel 40:23; Jezekiel 40:27; Jezekiel 46:1–2"> And he measured the breadth of the court, from the open space of the outer gate inwards to the open space of the gate looking outwards: a hundred cubits was the distance to the place of the gate looking eastward: and he brought me to the north;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jezekiel 40:6"> and behold a gate looking northwards belonging to the outer court, and he measured it, both the length of it and the breadth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jezekiel 40:29–30; Jezekiel 40:25–26; Jezekiel 40:10–16"> and the chambers, three on this side and three on that; and the posts, and the porches, and the palm-trees thereof: and they were according to the measures of the gate that looks eastward: the length thereof was fifty cubits, and the breadth thereof was twenty-five cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jezekiel 40:31; Jezekiel 40:37; Jezekiel 40:6"> And its windows, and its porches, and its palm-trees, were according to the dimensions of the gate looking eastward; and they went up to it by seven steps; and the porches were within.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jezekiel 40:19; Jezekiel 40:27–28; Exodus 38:9–12"> And there was a gate to the inner court looking toward the north gate, after the manner of the gate looking toward the east; and he measured the court from gate to gate, a hundred cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jezekiel 46:9; Jezekiel 40:6; Jezekiel 40:20–21"> And he brought me to the south side, and behold a gate looking southwards: and he measured it, and its chambers, and its posts, and its porches, according to these dimensions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jezekiel 40:33; Jezekiel 40:16; Jezekiel 40:21–22"> And its windows and its porches round about were according to the windows of the porch: the length thereof was fifty cubits, and the breadth thereof was five and twenty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jezekiel 40:22; Jezekiel 40:16; Jezekiel 40:6"> And it had seven steps, and porches within: and it had palm-trees on the posts, one on one side, and one on the other side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jezekiel 40:23; Jezekiel 40:32; Jezekiel 40:19"> And there was a gate opposite the gate of the inner court southward: and he measured the court from gate to gate, a hundred cubits in breadth southward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jezekiel 40:35; Jezekiel 40:32"> And he brought me into the inner court of the south gate: and he measured the gate according to these measures;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jezekiel 40:25; Jezekiel 40:16; Jezekiel 40:7"> and the chambers, and the posts, and the porches, according to these measures: and there were windows to it and to the porches round about: its length was fifty cubits, and its breadth twenty-five cubits,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jezekiel 40:26; Jezekiel 40:34; Jezekiel 40:22"> from the porch to the outer court: and there were palm-trees to the post thereof, and eight steps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jezekiel 40:35; Jezekiel 40:28–31"> And he brought me in at the gate that looks eastward: and he measured it according to these measures:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jezekiel 40:21; Jezekiel 40:36; Jezekiel 40:25"> and the chambers, and the posts, and the porches according to these measures: and there were windows to it, and porches round about: the length of it was fifty cubits, and the breadth of it twenty-five cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jezekiel 40:22; Jezekiel 40:31; Jezekiel 40:37"> And there were porches opening into the inner court, and palm-trees on the posts on this side and on that side: and it had eight steps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Jezekiel 47:2; Jezekiel 40:32; Jezekiel 44:4"> And he brought me in at the northern gate, and measured it according to these measures;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jezekiel 40:29; Jezekiel 40:21; Jezekiel 40:16"> and the chambers, and the posts, and the porches: and it had windows round about, and it had its porches: the length of it was fifty cubits, and the breadth twenty-five cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jezekiel 40:34; Jezekiel 40:31"> And its porches were toward the inner court; and there were palm-trees to the posts on this side and on that side: and it had eight steps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Chronicles II 4:6; Jezekiel 40:17; Leviticus 8:21"> Its chambers and its door-ways, and its porches at the second gate served as a drain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Jezekiel 46:2; Leviticus 1:3–17; Leviticus 4:2–3"> that they might slay in it the sin-offerings, and the trespass-offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And behind the drain for the whole-burnt-offerings at the north gate, two tables eastward behind the second gate; and behind the porch of the gate two tables eastward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Jezekiel 40:39–40"> Four on one side and four on the other side behind the gate; upon them they kill the victims, in front of the eight tables of sacrifices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Jezekiel 40:39; Exodus 20:25"> And there were four tables of hewn stone for whole-burnt-offerings, the breadth of them was a cubit and a half, and the length of them two cubits and a half, and their height was a cubit: on them they shall place the instruments with which they slay there the whole-burnt-offerings and the victims.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Leviticus 1:8; Leviticus 1:6; Leviticus 8:20"> And they shall have within a border of hewn stone round about of a span broad, and over the tables above screens for covering them from the wet and from the heat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Chronicles I 6:31–32; Chronicles I 16:41–43; Jezekiel 40:27"> And he brought me into the inner court, and behold there were two chambers in the inner court, one behind the gate looking to the north, turning southward, and one behind the southern gate, but which looks to the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Chronicles I 9:23; Psalms 134:1; Leviticus 8:35"> And he said to me, This chamber that looks to the south, is for the priests that keep the charge of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Jezekiel 44:15; Jezekiel 43:19; Kings III 2:35"> And the chamber that looks to the north is for the priests that keep the charge of the altar: they are the sons of Sadduc, those of the tribe of Levi who draw near to the Lord to serve him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Jezekiel 40:19; Jezekiel 40:23; Jezekiel 40:27"> And he measured the court, the length whereof was a hundred cubits, and the breadth a hundred cubits, on its four sides; and the altar in front of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Chronicles II 3:4; Kings III 6:3"> And he brought me into the porch of the house; and he measured the post of the porch, the breadth was five cubits on one side and five cubits on the other side; and the breadth of the door was fourteen cubits, and the side-pieces of the door of the porch were three cubits on one side, and three cubits on the other side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Kings III 7:15–21; Jezekiel 40:34; Chronicles II 3:17"> And the length of the porch was twenty cubits, and the breadth twelve cubits; and they went up to it by ten steps; and there were pillars to the porch, one on this side and one on that side.</span>
