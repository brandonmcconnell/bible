<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jezekiel 27:2; Jezekiel 26:17; Kings IV 24:6"> Moreover do thou take up a lamentation for the prince of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zacharias 11:3; Esaias 5:29; Job 4:11"> and say, Why is thy mother become a whelp in the midst of lions? in the midst of lions she has multiplied her whelps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jezekiel 22:25; Kings IV 23:31–32; Chronicles II 36:1–2"> And one of her whelps sprang forth; he became a lion, and learned to take prey, he devoured men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:4; Kings IV 23:33–34; Chronicles II 36:6"> And the nations heard a report of him; he was caught in their pit, and they brought him into the land of Egypt in chains.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jezekiel 19:3; Kings IV 23:34–37"> And she saw that he was driven away from her, and her hope of him perished, and she took another of her whelps; she made him a lion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:5; Chronicles II 36:9; Jeremias 26:1–24"> And he went up and down in the midst of lions, he became a lion, and learned to take prey, he devoured men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jezekiel 30:12; Jezekiel 12:19; Michæas 1:2"> And he prowled in his boldness and laid waste their cities, and made the land desolate, and the fulness of it, by the voice of his roaring.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 4:20; Jezekiel 19:4; Jezekiel 12:13"> Then the nations set upon him from the countries round about, and they spread their nets upon him: he was taken in their pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:6; Jezekiel 6:2; Jeremias 36:30–31"> And they put him in chains and in a cage, and he came to the king of Babylon; and he cast him into prison, that his voice should not be heard on the mountains of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 80:8–11; Deuteronomy 8:7; Numbers 24:6–7"> Thy mother was as a vine and as a blossom on a pomegranate tree, planted by water: her fruit and her shoots abounded by reason of much water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jezekiel 31:3; Psalms 80:15; Daniel 4:11"> And she became a rod for a tribe of princes, and was elevated in her bulk in the midst of other trees, and she saw her bulk in the multitude of her branches.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jezekiel 17:10; Osee 13:15; Esaias 27:11"> But she was broken down in wrath, she was cast upon the ground, and the east wind dried up her choice branches: vengeance came upon them, and the rod of her strength was withered; fire consumed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Osee 2:3; Jezekiel 19:10; Psalms 63:1"> And now they have planted her in the wilderness, in a dry land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 80:15–16; Jezekiel 19:11; Kings IV 24:20"> And fire is gone out of a rod of her choice boughs, and has devoured her; and there was no rod of strength in her. Her race is become a parable of lamentation, and it shall be for a lamentation.</span>
</div>
