<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jezekiel 30:20; Jezekiel 32:17; Jezekiel 31:1"> And it came to pass in the twelfth year, in the tenth month, on the first day of the month, that the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jezekiel 29:3; Naum 2:11–13; Jezekiel 27:2"> Son of man, take up a lamentation for Pharao king of Egypt, and say to him,<br />Thou art become like a lion of the nations, and as the serpent that is in the sea: and thou didst make assaults with thy rivers, and didst disturb the water with thy feet, and didst trample thy rivers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jezekiel 12:13; Jeremias 16:16; Ecclesiastes 9:12"> Thus saith the Lord; I will also cast over thee the nets of many nations, and will bring thee up with my hook:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esaias 18:6; Jezekiel 29:5; Jezekiel 31:12–13"> and I will stretch thee upon the earth: the fields shall be covered with thee, and I will cause all the birds of the sky to settle upon thee, and I will fill with thee all the wild beasts of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jezekiel 31:12"> And I will cast thy flesh upon the mountains, and will saturate them with thy blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esaias 34:3; Esaias 34:7; Revelation 14:20"> And the land shall be drenched with thy dung, because of thy multitude upon the mountains: I will fill the valleys with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 24:29; Joel 2:31; Esaias 13:10"> And I will veil the heavens when thou art extinguished, and will darken the stars thereof; I will cover the sun with a cloud, and the moon shall not give her light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 1:14; Proverbs 20:20"> All the bodies that give light in the sky, shall be darkened over thee, and I will bring darkness upon the earth, saith the Lord God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 18:10–15; Jezekiel 30:26; Jeremias 25:15–25"> And I will provoke to anger the heart of many people, when I shall lead thee captive among the nations, to a land which thou hast not known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jezekiel 26:16; Jezekiel 27:35; Kings III 9:8"> And many nations shall mourn over thee, and their kings shall be utterly amazed, when my sword flies in their faces, as they wait for their own fall from the day of thy fall.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremias 43:10; Jezekiel 26:7; Jezekiel 30:22–25"> For thus saith the Lord God; The sword of the king of Babylon shall come upon thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jezekiel 28:7; Jezekiel 29:19; Jezekiel 31:11"> with the swords of mighty men; and I will cast down thy strength: they are all destroying ones from the nations, and they shall destroy the pride of Egypt, and all her strength shall be crushed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jezekiel 29:11; Jezekiel 29:8; Jezekiel 30:12"> And I will destroy all her cattle from beside the great water; and the foot of man shall not trouble it any more, and the step of cattle shall no more trample it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Thus shall their waters then be at rest, and their rivers shall flow like oil, saith the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jezekiel 6:7; Exodus 7:5; Exodus 14:4"> when I shall give up Egypt to destruction, and the land shall be made desolate with the fulness thereof; when I shall scatter all that dwell in it, and they shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings II 1:17; Chronicles II 35:25; Jezekiel 26:17"> There is a lamentation, and thou shalt utter it; and the daughters of the nations shall utter it, even for Egypt, and they shall mourn for it over all the strength thereof, saith the Lord God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jezekiel 32:1; Jezekiel 33:21; Jezekiel 1:2"> And it came to pass in the twelfth year, in the first month, on the fifteenth day of the month, the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jezekiel 31:14; Jezekiel 32:2; Jeremias 1:10"> Son of man, lament over the strength of Egypt, for the nations shall bring down her daughters dead to the depth of the earth, to them that go down to the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 28:3; Jezekiel 32:23–26; Jezekiel 32:29–30"> They shall fall with him in the midst of them that are slain with the sword, and all his strength shall perish: the giants also shall say to thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esaias 14:9–10; Jezekiel 32:27; Jezekiel 32:19"> Be thou in the depth of the pit: to whom art thou superior? yea, go down, and lie with the uncircumcised, in the midst of them that are slain with the sword.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jezekiel 32:26; Jezekiel 32:29–30; Jezekiel 32:24"> There are Assur and all his company: all his slain have been laid there:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jezekiel 26:20; Jezekiel 32:24–27; Psalms 27:13"> and their burial is in the depth of the pit, and his company are set round about his tomb: all the slain that fell by the sword, who had caused the fear of them to be upon the land of the living.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jezekiel 32:18; Jezekiel 26:20; Jeremias 49:34–39"> There is Ælam and all his host round about his tomb: all the slain that fell by the sword, and the uncircumcised that go down to the deep of the earth, who caused their fear to be upon the land of the living: and they have received their punishment with them that go down to the pit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 139:8; Chronicles I 10:4; Kings II 1:20"> in the midst of the slain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jezekiel 27:13; Genesis 10:2; Jezekiel 38:2–3"> There were laid Mosoch, and Thobel, and all his strength round about his tomb: all his slain men, all the uncircumcised, slain with the sword, who caused their fear to be in the land of the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Esaias 14:18–19; Job 3:13–15; Jezekiel 32:21"> And they are laid with the giants that fell of old, who went down to Hades with their weapons of war: and they laid their swords under their heads, but their iniquities were upon their bones, because they terrified all men during their life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Daniel 2:34–35"> And thou shalt lie in the midst of the uncircumcised, with them that have been slain by the sword.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremias 49:7–22; Malachias 1:3–4; Obdias 1:1–9"> There are laid the princes of Assur, who yielded their strength to a wound of the sword: these are laid with the slain, with them that go down to the pit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jezekiel 38:6; Jezekiel 39:2; Jezekiel 28:21"> There are the princes of the north, even all the captains of Assur, who go down slain to Hades: they lie uncircumcised among the slain with the sword together with their terror and their strength, and they have received their punishment with them that go down to the pit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jezekiel 31:16; Jezekiel 14:22; Lamentations 2:13"> King Pharao shall see them, and shall be comforted over all their force, saith the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jezekiel 32:27; Genesis 35:5; 2 Corinthians 5:11"> For I have caused his fear to be upon the land of the living: yet he shall lie in the midst of the uncircumcised with them that are slain with the sword, even Pharao, and all his multitude with him, saith the Lord God.</span>
</div>
</div>
