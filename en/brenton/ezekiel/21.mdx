<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jezekiel 20:46; Jezekiel 4:7; Jezekiel 25:2"> Son of man, set thy face against Thæman, and look toward Darom, and prophesy against the chief forest of Nageb,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 9:22; Jeremias 21:13; Jezekiel 5:8"> and thou shalt say to the forest of Nageb, Hear the word of the Lord; thus saith the Lord, even the Lord; Behold, I will kindle a fire in thee, and it shall devour in thee every green tree, and every dry tree: the flame that is kindled shall not be quenched, and every face shall be scorched with it from the south to the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jezekiel 20:47; Jezekiel 7:2; Jezekiel 6:11–14"> And all flesh shall know that I the Lord have kindled it: it shall not be quenched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jezekiel 21:30; Naum 1:9; Kings I 3:12"> And I said, Not so, O Lord God! they say to me, Is not this that is spoken a parable?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esaias 22:4; Jezekiel 6:11; Jeremias 19:10"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jezekiel 7:17; Jezekiel 7:26; Esaias 13:7"> Therefore prophesy, son of man, set thy face steadfastly toward Jerusalem, and look toward their holy places, and thou shalt prophesy against the land of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> and thou shalt say to the land of Israel, Thus saith the Lord; Behold, I am against thee, and I will draw forth my sword out of its sheath, and I will destroy out of thee the transgressor and unrighteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esaias 34:5–6; Jezekiel 21:28; Jezekiel 21:3"> Because I will destroy out of thee the unrighteous and the transgressor, therefore so shall my sword come forth out of its sheath against all flesh from the south to the north:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 110:5–6; Jezekiel 20:47; Jezekiel 21:25–27"> and all flesh shall know that I the Lord have drawn forth my sword out of its sheath: it shall not return any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jezekiel 21:19; Jeremias 25:33; Jeremias 51:20–23"> And thou, son of man, groan with the breaking of thy loins; thou shalt even groan heavily in their sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jezekiel 21:6; Joel 1:13; Jezekiel 21:14"> And it shall come to pass, if they shall say to thee, Wherefore dost thou groan? that thou shalt say, For the report; because it comes: and every heart shall break, and all hands shall become feeble, and all flesh and every spirit shall faint, and all thighs shall be defiled with moisture: behold, it comes, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jezekiel 21:10; 2 Corinthians 8:2; Jezekiel 21:25"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings IV 24:1; Numbers 24:10; Leviticus 26:24"> Son of man, prophesy, and thou shalt say, Thus saith the Lord; Say, Sword, sword, be sharpened and rage,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jezekiel 21:10; Jeremias 17:27; Jezekiel 21:22"> that thou mayest slay victims; be sharpened that thou mayest be bright, ready for slaughter, slay, set at nought, despise every tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jezekiel 21:20; Jezekiel 21:4; Jezekiel 14:17"> And he made it ready for his hand to hold: the sword is sharpened, it is ready to put into the hand of the slayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jezekiel 5:13; Jezekiel 21:14; Zacharias 6:8"> Cry out and howl, son of man: for this sword is come upon my people, this sword is come upon all the princes of Israel: they shall be as strangers: judgment with the sword is come upon my people: therefore clap thine hands, for sentence has been passed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and what if even the tribe be rejected? it shall not be, saith the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jezekiel 4:1–3; Jeremias 1:10; Jezekiel 5:1–17"> And thou, son of man, prophesy, and clap thine hands, and take a second sword: the third sword is the sword of the slain, the great sword of the slain: and thou shalt strike them with amazement, lest the heart should faint</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Amos 1:14; Jeremias 49:2; Jezekiel 25:5"> and the weak ones be multiplied at every gate—they are given up to the slaughter of the sword: it is well fitted for slaughter, it is well fitted for glittering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 18:20; Proverbs 16:33; Judges 17:5"> And do thou go on, sharpen thyself on the right and on the left whithersoever thy face may set itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jezekiel 4:2; Jezekiel 26:9; Jeremias 51:14"> And I also will clap my hands, and let loose my fury: I the Lord have spoken it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 5:15; Jezekiel 21:24; Jezekiel 29:16"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Osee 4:2; Michæas 3:10–12; Jeremias 3:2"> And thou, son of man, appoint thee two ways, that the sword of the king of Babylon may enter in: the two shall go forth of one country; and there shall be a force at the top of the way of the city, thou shalt set it at the top of the way,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jezekiel 35:5; Jezekiel 21:29; Jezekiel 17:19"> that the sword may enter in upon Rabbath of the children of Ammon, and upon Judea, and upon Jerusalem in the midst thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jezekiel 17:24; Psalms 75:7; Jeremias 13:18"> For the king of Babylon shall stand on the old way, at the head of the two ways, to use divination, to make bright the arrow, and to enquire of the graven images, and to examine the victims.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Michæas 5:2; Jeremias 23:5–6; Zacharias 9:9"> On his right was the divination against Jerusalem, to cast a mound, to open the mouth in shouting, to lift up the voice with crying, to cast a mound against her gates, to cast up a heap, and to build forts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jezekiel 21:20; Jezekiel 21:9–10; Sophonias 2:8–10"> And he was to them as one using divination before them, and he himself recounting his iniquities, that they might be borne in mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jezekiel 21:25; Jezekiel 22:28; Psalms 37:13"> Therefore thus saith the Lord, Because ye have caused your iniquities to be remembered, in the discovery of your wickedness, so that your sins should be seen, in all your wickedness and in your evil practices; because ye have caused remembrance of them, in these shall ye be taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jezekiel 16:38; Genesis 15:14; Jeremias 47:6–7"> And thou profane wicked prince of Israel, whose day, even an end, is come in a season of iniquity, thus saith the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Naum 1:6; Jeremias 6:22–23; Jezekiel 14:19"> Thou hast taken off the mitre and put on the crown, it shall not have such another after it: thou hast abased that which was high, and exalted that which was low.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jezekiel 25:10; Malachias 4:1; Jezekiel 20:47–48"> Injustice, injustice, injustice, will I make it: woe to it: such shall it be until he comes to whom it belongs; and I will deliver it to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And thou, son of man, prophesy, and thou shalt say, Thus saith the Lord, concerning the children of Ammon, and concerning their reproach; and thou shalt say, O sword, sword, drawn for slaughter, and drawn for destruction, awake, that thou mayest gleam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> While thou art seeing vain visions, and while thou art prophesying falsehoods, to bring thyself upon the necks of ungodly transgressors, the day is come, even an end, in a season of iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Turn, rest not in this place wherein thou wert born: in thine own land will I judge thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And I will pour out my wrath upon thee, I will blow upon thee with the fire of my wrath, and I will deliver thee into the hands of barbarians skilled in working destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Thou shalt be fuel for fire; thy blood shall be in the midst of thy land; there shall be no remembrance at all of thee: for I the Lord have spoken it.</span>
