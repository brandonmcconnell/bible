<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jezekiel 8:1; Jezekiel 30:20; Jezekiel 24:1"> And it came to pass in the seventh year, on the fifteenth day of the month, there came men of the elders of the house of Israel to enquire of the Lord, and they sat before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 28:9; Matthew 15:8–9; Psalms 50:15–21"> Son of man, speak to the elders of the house of Israel, and thou shalt say to them, Thus saith the Lord; Are ye come to enquire of me? As I live, I will not be enquired of by you, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jezekiel 22:2; Jezekiel 23:36; Jezekiel 14:20"> Shall I utterly take vengeance on them, son of man? testify to them of the iniquities of their fathers:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6; Jezekiel 20:15; Exodus 3:8"> and thou shalt say to them, Thus saith the Lord;<br />From the day that I chose the house of Israel, and became known to the seed of the house of Jacob, and was known to them in the land of Egypt, and helped them with my hand, saying, I am the Lord your God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Jeremias 32:22; Jezekiel 20:15"> in that day I helped them with my hand, to bring them out of the land of Egypt into the land which I prepared for them, a land flowing with milk and honey, it is abundant beyond every land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 18:3; Jezekiel 20:8; Leviticus 11:44"> And I said to them, Let every one cast away the abominations of his eyes, and defile not yourselves with the devices of Egypt: I am the Lord your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jezekiel 20:7; Esaias 63:10; Jezekiel 20:21"> But they revolted from me, and would not hearken to me: they cast not away the abominations of their eyes, and forsook not the devices of Egypt: then I said that I would pour out my wrath upon them, to accomplish my wrath upon them in the midst of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jezekiel 39:7; Jezekiel 20:22; Exodus 32:12"> But I wrought so that my name should not be at all profaned in the sight of the Gentiles, in the midst of whom they are, among whom I was made known to them in their sight, to bring them out of the land of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 15:22; Exodus 13:17–18; Exodus 14:17–22"> And I brought them into the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 10:5; Leviticus 18:5; Deuteronomy 4:8"> And I gave them my commandments, and made known to them mine ordinances, all which if a man shall do, he shall even live in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jezekiel 20:20; Jezekiel 37:28; Leviticus 21:23"> And I gave them my sabbaths, that they should be for a sign between me and them, that they should know that I am the Lord that sanctify them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jezekiel 20:21; Jezekiel 20:8; Esaias 56:6"> And I said to the house of Israel in the wilderness, Walk ye in my commandments: but they walked not in them, and they rejected mine ordinances, which if a man shall do, he shall even live in them; and they grievously profaned my sabbaths: and I said that I would pour out my wrath upon them in the wilderness, to consume them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jezekiel 20:9; Jezekiel 36:22–23; Jezekiel 20:22"> But I wrought so that my name should not be at all profaned before the Gentiles, before whose eyes I brought them out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 95:11; Psalms 106:26; Hebrews 3:18"> But I lifted up my hand against them in the wilderness once for all, that I would not bring them into the land which I gave them, a land flowing with milk and honey, it is sweeter than all lands:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 15:39; Jezekiel 20:8; Jezekiel 14:3–4"> because they rejected mine ordinances, and walked not in my commandments, but profaned my sabbaths, and went after the imaginations of their hearts.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jezekiel 11:13; Jeremias 4:27; Jeremias 5:18"> Yet mine eyes spared them, so as not to destroy them utterly, and I did not make an end of them in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zacharias 1:2–4; Psalms 78:6–8; 1 Peter 1:18"> And I said to their children in the wilderness, Walk not ye in the customs of your fathers, and keep not their ordinances, and have no fellowship with their practices, nor defile yourselves with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jezekiel 36:27; Deuteronomy 5:32–8; Jezekiel 37:24"> I am the Lord your God; walk in my commandments, and keep mine ordinances, and do them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Esaias 58:13; Jeremias 17:22; Exodus 31:13–17"> and hallow my sabbaths, and let them be a sign between me and you, that ye may know that I am the Lord your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jezekiel 20:13; Numbers 21:5; Jezekiel 20:8"> But they provoked me, and their children walked not in my commandments, and they took no heed to mine ordinances to do them, which if a man shall do, he shall even live in them, and they profaned my sabbaths: then I said that I would pour out my wrath upon them in the wilderness, to accomplish mine anger upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jezekiel 20:14; Jezekiel 20:9; Jeremias 14:21"> But I wrought so that my name might not be at all profaned before the Gentiles; and I brought them out in their sight.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 26:33; Deuteronomy 28:64–68; Deuteronomy 32:26–27"> I lifted up my hand against them in the wilderness, that I would scatter them among the Gentiles, and disperse them in the countries;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jezekiel 20:16; Jezekiel 6:9; Amos 2:4"> because they kept not mine ordinances, and rejected my commandments, and profaned my sabbaths, and their eyes went after the imaginations of their fathers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 81:12; Esaias 66:4; Jezekiel 20:39"> So I gave them commandments that were not good, and ordinances in which they should not live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jezekiel 6:7; Leviticus 18:21; Jezekiel 20:31"> And I will defile them by their own decrees, when I pass through upon every one that opens the womb, that I may destroy them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 2:24; Jezekiel 2:7; Jezekiel 3:11"> Therefore, son of man, speak to the house of Israel, and thou shalt say to them, Thus saith the Lord: Hitherto have your fathers provoked me in their trespasses in which they transgressed against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jezekiel 6:13; Nehemiah 9:22–26; Jeremias 3:6"> Whereas I brought them into the land concerning which I lifted up mine hand to give it them; and they looked upon every high hill, and every shady tree, and they sacrificed there to their gods, and offered there a sweet-smelling savour, and there they poured out their drink-offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jezekiel 16:24–25; Jezekiel 16:31"> And I said to them, What is Abama, that ye go in thither? and they called its name Abama, until this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremias 16:12; Jeremias 7:26; Judges 2:19"> Therefore say to the house of Israel, Thus saith the Lord, Do ye pollute yourselves with the iniquities of your fathers, and do ye go a-whoring after their abominations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jezekiel 20:26; Psalms 106:37–39; Jeremias 7:31"> and do ye pollute yourselves with the first-fruits of your gifts, in the offerings wherewith ye pollute yourselves in all your imaginations, until this day; and shall I answer you, O house of Israel? As I live, saith the Lord, I will not answer you, neither shall this thing come upon your spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jezekiel 11:5; Jeremias 44:17; Psalms 139:2"> And it shall not be as ye say, We will be as the nations, and as the tribes of the earth, to worship stocks and stones.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremias 21:5; Jezekiel 8:18; Jeremias 44:6"> Therefore, as I live, saith the Lord, I will reign over you with a strong hand, and with a high arm, and with outpoured wrath:</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jezekiel 20:38; Amos 9:9–10; Jezekiel 34:16"> I will bring you out from the nations, and will take you out of the lands wherein ye were dispersed, with a strong hand, and with a high arm, and with outpoured wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Osee 2:14; Jeremias 2:35; Jezekiel 17:20"> And I will bring you into the wilderness of the nations, and will plead with you there face to face.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:5–10; Numbers 11:1–35; Jezekiel 20:13"> As I pleaded with your fathers in the wilderness of the land of Egypt, so will I judge you, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremias 33:13; Leviticus 27:32; Matthew 25:32–33"> And I will cause you to pass under my rod, and I will bring you in by number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jezekiel 13:9; Amos 9:9–10; Psalms 95:11"> And I will separate from among you the ungodly and the revolters; for I will lead them forth out of their place of sojourning, and they shall not enter into the land of Israel: and ye shall know that I am the Lord, even the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Osee 4:17; Esaias 1:13–15; Jezekiel 43:7"> And as to you, O house of Israel, thus saith the Lord, even the Lord; Put away each one his evil practices, and hereafter if ye hearken to me, then shall ye no more profane my holy name by your gifts and by devices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Esaias 56:7; Malachias 3:4; Esaias 60:7"> For upon my holy mountain, on the high mountain, saith the Lord, even the Lord, there shall all the house of Israel serve me for ever: and there will I accept you, and there will I have respect to your first-fruits, and the first-fruits of your offerings, in all your holy things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Jezekiel 28:25; Jezekiel 11:17; Jezekiel 38:23"> I will accept you with a sweet-smelling savour, when I bring you out from the nations, and take you out of the countries wherein ye have been dispersed; and I will be sanctified among you in the sight of the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Jezekiel 34:13; Jezekiel 38:23; Jezekiel 36:23–24"> And ye shall know that I am the Lord, when I have brought you into the land of Israel, into the land concerning which I lifted up my hand to give it to your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Jezekiel 6:9; Osee 5:15; Jeremias 31:18"> And ye shall there remember your ways, and your devices wherewith ye defiled yourselves; and ye shall bewail yourselves for all your wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Jezekiel 24:24; Psalms 115:1; Jezekiel 20:9"> And ye shall know that I am the Lord, when I have done thus to you, that my name may not be profaned in your evil ways, and in your corrupt devices, saith the Lord.</span>
</div>
