<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jezekiel 19:1; Jeremias 9:10; Jezekiel 28:12"> And thou, son of man, take up a lamentation against Sor;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jezekiel 27:4; Esaias 23:11; Esaias 23:2–3"> and thou shalt say to Sor that dwells at the entrance of the sea, to the mart of the nations coming from many islands, Thus saith the Lord to Sor; Thou hast said, I have clothed myself with my beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jezekiel 26:5"> In the heart of the sea thy sons have put beauty upon thee for Beelim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:9; Esaias 14:8; Song of Solomon 4:8"> Cedar in Senir was employed for thee in building: boards of cypress timber were taken out of Libanus, and wood to make thee masts of fir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 10:4; Esaias 2:13; Zacharias 11:2"> They made thine oars of wood out of the land of Basan; thy sacred utensils they made of ivory, thy shady houses of wood from the isles of Chetiim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremias 10:9; Exodus 25:4; Genesis 10:4"> Fine linen with embroidery from Egypt supplied thy couch, to put honour upon thee, and to clothe thee with blue and purple from the isles of Elisai; and they became thy coverings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 10:18; Kings III 9:27; Jezekiel 27:11"> And thy princes were the dwellers in Sidon, and the Aradians were thy rowers: thy wise men, O Sor, who were in thee, these were thy pilots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings III 5:18; Joshua 13:5; Psalms 83:7"> The elders of the Biblians, and their wise men, who were in thee, these helped thy counsel: and all the ships of the sea and their rowers traded for thee to the utmost west.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jezekiel 38:5; Jezekiel 30:5; Esaias 66:19"> Persians and Lydians and Libyans were in thine army: thy warriors hung in thee shields and helmets; these gave thee thy glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jezekiel 27:3–4"> The sons of the Aradians and thine army were upon thy walls; there were guards in thy towers: they hung their quivers on thy battlements round about; these completed thy beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 10:4; Esaias 23:10; Jezekiel 38:13"> The Carthaginians were thy merchants because of the abundance of all thy strength; they furnished thy market with silver, and gold, and iron, and tin, and lead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 10:2; Esaias 66:19; Jezekiel 39:1"> Greece, both the whole world, and the adjacent coasts, these traded with thee in the persons of men, and they gave as thy merchandise vessels of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 10:3; Jezekiel 38:6; Chronicles I 1:6"> Out of the house of Thogarma horses and horsemen furnished thy market.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 10:7; Revelation 18:12; Kings III 10:22"> The sons of the Rhodians were thy merchants; from the islands they multiplied thy merchandise, even elephants' teeth: and to them that came in thou didst return thy prices,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 10:6; Jezekiel 28:13; Kings II 10:6"> even men as thy merchandise, from the multitude of thy trading population, myrrh and embroidered works from Tharsis: Ramoth also and Chorchor furnished thy market.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 11:33; Kings III 5:9; Jeremias 8:22"> Juda and the children of Israel, these were thy merchants; in the sale of corn and ointments and cassia: and they gave the best honey, and oil, and resin, to thy trading population.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Esaias 7:8; Genesis 15:2; Jezekiel 47:16–18"> The people of Damascus were thy merchants by reason of the abundance of all thy power; wine out of Chelbon, and wool from Miletus; and they brought wine into thy market.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 30:23–24; Psalms 45:8; Song of Solomon 4:13–14"> Out of Asel came wrought iron, and there is the sound of wheels among thy trading population.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 25:3; Jezekiel 27:15"> The people of Dædan were thy merchants, with choice cattle for chariots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esaias 60:7; Genesis 25:13; Kings III 10:15"> Arabia and all the princes of Kedar, these were thy traders with thee, bringing camels, and lambs, and rams, in which they trade with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 10:7; Esaias 60:6; Psalms 72:10"> The merchants of Sabba and Ramma, these were thy merchants, with choice spices, and precious stones: and they brought gold to thy market.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings IV 19:12; Esaias 37:12; Amos 1:5"> Charra, and Chanaa, these were thy merchants: Assur, and Charman, were thy merchants:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Kings IV 2:8"> bringing for merchandise blue, and choice stores bound with cords, and cypress wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Esaias 2:16; Psalms 48:7; Esaias 23:14"> Ships were thy merchants, in abundance, with thy trading population: and thou wast filled and very heavily loaded in the heart of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 48:7; Jezekiel 26:19; Acts 27:14"> Thy rowers have brought thee into great waters: the south wind has broken thee in the heart of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 11:4; Jezekiel 27:24; Jezekiel 27:12"> Thy forces, and thy gain, and that of thy traders, and thy rowers, and thy pilots, and thy counsellors, and they that traffic with thee, and all thy warriors that are in thee: and all thy company in the midst of thee shall perish in the heart of the sea, in the day of thy fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jezekiel 26:10; Exodus 15:14; Jezekiel 31:16"> At the cry of thy voice thy pilots shall be greatly terrified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jezekiel 26:16; Jezekiel 32:10; Revelation 18:17–24"> And all the rowers and the mariners shall come down from the ships, and the pilots of the sea shall stand on the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremias 6:26; Kings II 1:2; Lamentations 2:10"> And they shall wail over thee with their voice, and cry bitterly, and put earth on their heads, and spread ashes under them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jezekiel 26:17; Revelation 18:18; Jezekiel 27:2"> And their sons shall take up a lament for thee, even a lamentation for Sor, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Revelation 18:19; Jezekiel 27:3; Jezekiel 28:16"> How large a reward hast thou gained from the sea? thou hast filled nations out of thine abundance; and out of thy mixed merchandise thou hast enriched all the kings of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jezekiel 27:26–27; Zacharias 9:3–4; Jezekiel 26:19–21"> Now art thou broken in the sea, thy traders are in the deep water, and all thy company in the midst of thee: all thy rowers have fallen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Jezekiel 32:10; Esaias 23:6; Jezekiel 26:15–18"> All the dwellers in the islands have mourned over thee, and their kings have been utterly amazed, and their countenance has wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremias 18:16; Psalms 37:10; Sophonias 2:15"> Merchants from the nations have hissed at thee; thou art utterly destroyed, and shalt not be any more for ever.</span>
