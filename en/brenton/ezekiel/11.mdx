<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jezekiel 3:14; Jezekiel 11:24; Jezekiel 11:13"> Moreover the Spirit took me up, and brought me to the front gate of the house of the Lord, that looks eastward: and behold at the entrance of the gate were about five and twenty men; and I saw in the midst of them Jechonias the son of Ezer, and Phaltias the son of Banæas, the leaders of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esaias 30:1; Jeremias 5:5; Psalms 2:1–2"> And the Lord said to me, Son of man, these are the men that devise vanities, and take evil counsel in this city:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jezekiel 12:27; Jezekiel 12:22; Jezekiel 11:7–11"> who say, Have not the houses been newly built? This is the caldron, and we are the flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jezekiel 20:46–47; Jezekiel 21:2; Jezekiel 3:2–15"> Therefore prophesy against them, prophesy, son of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 17:10; Jeremias 16:17; Revelation 2:23"> And the Spirit of the Lord fell upon me, and said to me, say; Thus saith the Lord; Thus have ye said, O house of Israel: and I know the devices of your spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jezekiel 7:23; Jezekiel 22:27; Jezekiel 22:12"> Ye have multiplied your dead in this city, and ye have filled your ways with slain men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jezekiel 24:3–13; Michæas 3:2–3; Kings IV 25:18–22"> Therefore thus saith the Lord; Your dead whom ye have smitten in the midst of it, these are the flesh, and thiscity is the caldron: but I will bring you forth out of the midst of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 10:24; Esaias 24:17–18; Job 3:25"> Ye fear the sword; and I will bring a sword upon you, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jezekiel 5:8; Psalms 106:41; Deuteronomy 28:36"> And I will bring you forth out of the midst of it, and will deliver you into the hands of strangers, and will execute judgments among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings IV 14:25; Jeremias 52:9–10; Kings IV 25:19–21"> Ye shall fall by the sword; I will judge you on the mountains of Israel; and ye shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jezekiel 11:1; Jezekiel 9:8; Acts 5:5"> And it came to pass, while I was prophesying, that Phaltias the son of Banæas died. And I fell upon my face, and cried with a loud voice, and said, Alas, alas, O Lord! wilt thou utterly destroy the remnant of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jezekiel 33:24; Esaias 65:5; John 16:2"> Son of man, thy brethren, and the men of thy captivity, and all the house of Israel are come to the full, to whom the inhabitants of Jerusalem said, Keep ye far away from the Lord: the land is given to us for an inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esaias 8:14; Deuteronomy 30:3–4; Psalms 90:1"> Therefore say thou, Thus saith the Lord; I will cast them off among the nations, and will disperse them into every land, yet will I be to them for a little sanctuary in the countries which they shall enter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jezekiel 28:25; Jeremias 3:18; Jezekiel 34:13"> Therefore say thou, Thus saith the Lord; I will also take them from the heathen, and gather them out of the lands wherein I have scattered them, and will give them the land of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jezekiel 37:23; Jezekiel 5:11; Jezekiel 42:7–8"> And they shall enter in there, and shall remove all the abominations of it, and all its iniquities from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jezekiel 36:26–27; Jeremias 31:33; Psalms 51:10"> And I will give them another heart, and will put a new spirit within them; and will extract the heart of stone from their flesh, and give them a heart of flesh:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jezekiel 14:11; Psalms 105:45; Jeremias 30:22"> that they may walk in my commandments, and keep mine ordinances, and do them: and they shall be to me a people, and I will be to them a God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jezekiel 9:10; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Jeremias 1:16"> And as for the heart set upon their abominations and their iniquities, as their heart went after them, I have recompensed their ways on their heads, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jezekiel 10:19; Jezekiel 1:19–20"> Then the cherubs lifted up their wings, and the wheels beside them; and the glory of the God of Israel was over them above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zacharias 14:4; Jezekiel 8:4; Jezekiel 10:4"> And the glory of the Lord went up from the midst of the city, and stood on the mountain which was in front of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jezekiel 3:12; Jezekiel 8:3; Jezekiel 11:1"> And the Spirit took me up, and brought me to the land of the Chaldeans, to the captivity, in a vision by the Spirit of God: and I went up after the vision which I saw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jezekiel 3:4; Jezekiel 3:27; Jezekiel 3:17"> And I spoke to the captivity all the words of the Lord which he had shewed me.</span>
