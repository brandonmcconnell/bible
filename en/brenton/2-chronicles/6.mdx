<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 18:8–11; Leviticus 16:2; Exodus 20:21"> Then said Solomon, The Lord said that he would dwell in thick darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Chronicles II 2:4–6; Psalms 135:21; Chronicles I 28:6"> But I have built a house to thy name, holy to thee, and prepared for thee to dwell in for ever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings III 8:14; Chronicles II 29:29; Kings III 8:55–61"> And the king turned his face, and blessed all the congregation of Israel: and all the congregation of Israel stood by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 72:18–19; Matthew 24:35; Psalms 68:32–35"> And he said, Blessed be the Lord God of Israel: he has even fulfilled with his hands as he spoke with his mouth to my father David, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 20:24; Kings III 8:16; Kings I 13:13–14"> From the day when I brought up my people out of the land of Egypt, I chose no city of all the tribes of Israel, to build a house that my name should be there; neither did I choose a man to be a leader over my people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Chronicles I 28:4; Psalms 132:13; Chronicles II 12:13"> But I chose Jerusalem that my name should be there; and I chose David to be over my people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Chronicles I 17:1; Kings III 5:3; Chronicles I 22:7"> And it came into the heart of David my father, to build a house for the name of the Lord God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; Mark 14:8; Kings III 8:18–21"> But the Lord said to my father David, Whereas it came into thy heart to build a house for my name, thou didst well that it came into thy heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings II 7:12–13; Chronicles I 17:4; Chronicles I 17:11–12"> Nevertheless thou shalt not build the house; for thy son who shall come forth out of thy loins, he shall build the house for my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:4; Chronicles I 28:5; Chronicles I 17:11"> And the Lord has confirmed this word, which he spoke; and I am raised up in the room of my father David, and I sit upon the throne of Israel as the Lord said, and I have built the house for the name of the Lord God of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Chronicles II 5:10; Chronicles II 5:7; Kings III 8:21"> and I have set there the ark in which is the covenant of the Lord, which he made with Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 63:4; Esaias 50:10; Job 11:13"> And he stood before the altar of the Lord in the presence of all the congregation of Israel, and spread out his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings III 8:54; Nehemiah 8:4; Psalms 95:6"> For Solomon had made a brazen scaffold, and set it in the midst of the court of the sanctuary; the length of it was five cubits, and the breadth of it five cubits, and the height of it three cubits: and he stood upon it, and fell upon his knees before the whole congregation of Israel, and spread abroad his hands to heaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 15:11; Deuteronomy 7:9; Nehemiah 1:5"> and said,<br />Lord God of Israel, there is no God like thee in heaven, or on the earth; keeping covenant and mercy with thy servants that walk before thee with their whole heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Chronicles I 22:9–10; Kings II 7:12; Kings III 8:24"> Even as thou hast kept them with thy servant David my father, as thou hast spoken to him in words:—thou hast both spoken with thy mouth, and hast fulfilled it with thy hands, as it is this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings III 2:4; John 15:14–15; Chronicles II 7:18"> And now, Lord God of Israel, keep with thy servant David my father the things which thou spokest to him, saying, There shall not fail thee a man before me sitting on the throne of Israel, if only thy sons will take heed to their way to walk in my law, as thou didst walk before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremias 11:5; Esaias 41:17; Chronicles II 6:4"> And now, Lord God of Israel, let, I pray thee, thy word be confirmed, which thou hast spoken to thy servant David.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Chronicles II 2:6; Esaias 66:1; Psalms 139:7–10"> For will God indeed dwell with men upon the earth? if the heaven and the heaven of heavens will not suffice thee, what then is this house which I have built?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings III 8:28; Luke 18:1–7; John 17:20"> Yet thou shalt have respect to the prayer of thy servant, and to my petition, O Lord God, so as to hearken to the petition and the prayer which thy servant prays before thee this day:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 34:15; Daniel 6:10; Psalms 121:5"> so that thine eyes should be open over this house by day and by night, towards this place, whereon thou saidst thy name should be called, so as to hear the prayer which thy servant prays towards this house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Michæas 7:18; Daniel 9:19; Esaias 43:25"> And thou shalt hear the supplication of thy servant, and of thy people Israel, whatsoever prayers they shall make towards this place: and thou shalt hearken in thy dwelling-place out of heaven, yea thou shalt hear, and be merciful.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 22:11; Leviticus 5:1; Numbers 5:19–22"> If a man sin against his neighbour, and he bring an oath upon him so as to make him swear, and he come and swear before the altar in this house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Esaias 3:10–11; Proverbs 17:15; Jeremias 51:56"> then shalt thou hearken out of heaven, and do, and judge thy servants, to recompense the transgressor, and to return his ways upon his head: and to justify the righteous, to recompense him according to his righteousness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 26:17; Kings IV 17:7–18; Joshua 7:8"> And if thy people Israel should be put to the worse before the enemy, if they should sin against thee, and then turn and confess to thy name, and pray and make supplication before thee in this house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremias 33:6–13; Ezra and Nehemiah 1:1–6; Genesis 13:15"> then shalt thou hearken out of heaven and shalt be merciful to the sins of thy people Israel, and thou shalt restore them to the land which thou gavest to them and to their fathers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:23; Deuteronomy 11:17; Leviticus 26:19"> When heaven is restrained, and there is no rain, because they shall have sinned against thee, and when they shall pray towards this place, and praise thy name, and shall turn from their sins, because thou shalt afflict them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Kings III 8:35–36; Jeremias 5:24; Zacharias 10:1"> then shalt thou hearken from heaven, and thou shalt be merciful to the sins of thy servants, and of thy people Israel; for thou shalt shew them the good way in which they shall walk; and thou shalt send rain upon thy land, which thou gavest to thy people for an inheritance.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 9:3–11; Joel 2:25; Ruth 1:1"> If there should be famine upon the land, if there should be death, a pestilent wind and blight; if there should be locust and caterpillar, and if the enemy should harass them before their cities: in whatever plague and whatever distress they may be;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Psalms 32:2–6; Chronicles II 6:12–13"> then whatever prayer and whatever supplication shall be made by any man and all thy people Israel, if a man should know his own plague and his own sickness, and should spread forth his hands toward this house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Chronicles I 28:9; Kings I 16:7; Revelation 2:23"> then shalt thou hear from heaven, out of thy prepared dwelling-place, and shalt be merciful, and shalt recompense to the man according to his ways, as thou shalt know his heart to be; for thou alone knowest the heart of the children of men:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 9:31; Job 28:28; Psalms 128:1"> that they may reverence all thy ways all the days which they live upon the face of the land, which thou gavest to our fathers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Esaias 56:3–7; Kings III 8:41–43; Matthew 8:10–11"> And every stranger who is not himself of thy people Israel, and who shall have come from a distant land because of thy great name, and thy mighty hand, and thy high arm; when they shall come and worship toward this place;—</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Chronicles II 7:14; Kings III 8:16; Revelation 11:15"> then shalt thou hearken out of heaven, out of thy prepared dwelling-place, and shalt do according to all that the stranger shall call upon thee for; that all the nations of the earth may know thy name, and that they may fear thee, as thy people Israel do, and that they may know that thy name is called upon this house which I have built.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Chronicles II 20:6–13; Chronicles II 18:31; Chronicles II 20:4"> And if thy people shall go forth to war against their enemies by the way by which thou shalt send them, and shall pray to thee toward this city which thou hast chosen, and toward the house which I have built to thy name;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Daniel 9:17–19; Psalms 9:3–4; Jeremias 5:28"> then shalt thou hear out of heaven their prayer and their supplication, and maintain their cause.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; James 3:2; 1 John 1:8–10"> Whereas if they shall sin against thee, (for there is no man who will not sin,) and thou shalt smite them, and deliver them up before their enemies, and they that take them captive shall carry them away into a land of enemies, to a land far off or near;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremias 31:18–20; Daniel 9:5–11; Esaias 64:6–12"> and if they shall repent in their land whither they were carried captive, and shall also turn and make supplication to thee in their captivity, saying, We have sinned, we have transgressed, we have wrought unrighteously;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Chronicles II 33:11–13; Daniel 6:10; Daniel 9:3–4"> and if they shall turn to thee with all their heart and all their soul in the land of them that carried them captives, whither they carried them captives, and shall pray toward their land which thou gavest to their fathers, and the city which thou didst choose, and the house which I built to thy name:—</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Michæas 7:18–20; Chronicles II 6:35; Psalms 25:18"> then shalt thou hear out of heaven, out of thy prepared dwelling-place, their prayer and their supplication, and thou shalt execute justice, and shalt be merciful to thy people that sin against thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Chronicles II 7:15; Psalms 116:2; Nehemiah 1:6"> And now, Lord, let, I pray thee, thine eyes be opened, and thine ears be attentive to the petition made in this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Esaias 61:10; Chronicles I 28:2; Psalms 132:8–10"> And now, O Lord God, arise into thy resting-place, thou, and the ark of thy strength: let thy priests, O Lord God, clothe themselves with salvation, and thy sons rejoice in prosperity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Esaias 55:3; Psalms 132:1; Esaias 61:1"> O Lord God, turn not away the face of thine anointed: remember the mercies of thy servant David.</span>
</div>
</div>
