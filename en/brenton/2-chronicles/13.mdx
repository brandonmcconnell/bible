<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles II 12:16; Kings III 15:1–8"> In the eighteenth year of the reign of Jeroboam Abia began to reign over Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Chronicles II 11:20; Kings III 15:6–7; Judges 19:16"> He reigned three years in Jerusalem. And his mother's name was Maacha, daughter of Uriel of Gabaon.<br />And there was war between Abia and Jeroboam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Chronicles II 26:12–13; Chronicles I 21:5; Kings I 17:1–3"> And Abia set the battle in array with an army, with mighty men of war, even four hundred thousand mighty men: and Jeroboam set the battle in array against him with eight hundred thousand, they were mighty warriors of the host.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 18:22; Chronicles II 15:2; Genesis 10:18"> And Abia rose up from the mount Somoron, which is in mount Ephraim, and said, Hear ye, Jeroboam, and all Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 18:19; Leviticus 2:13; Mark 9:49–50"> Is it not for you to know that the Lord God of Israel has given a king over Israel for ever to David, and to his sons, by a covenant of salt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings III 11:26; Kings III 12:20; Chronicles II 10:19"> But Jeroboam the son of Nabat, the servant of Solomon the son of David, is risen up, and has revolted from his master:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 9:4; Chronicles II 12:13; Acts 17:5"> and there are gathered to him pestilent men, transgressors, and he has risen up against Roboam the son of Solomon, while Roboam was young and fearful in heart, and he withstood him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings III 12:28; Chronicles II 11:15; Luke 19:14"> And now ye profess to resist the kingdom of the Lord in the hand of the sons of David; and ye are a great multitude, and with you are golden calves, which Jeroboam made you for gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Chronicles II 11:14–15; Jeremias 2:11; Exodus 29:35"> Did ye not cast out the priests of the Lord, the sons of Aaron, and the Levites, and make to yourselves priests of the people of any other land? whoever came to consecrate himself with a calf of the herd and seven rams, he forthwith became a priest to that which is no god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 19:5–6; Chronicles II 11:16–17; Zacharias 13:9"> But we have not forsaken the Lord our God, and his priests, the sons of Aaron, and the Levites, minister to the Lord; and in their daily courses</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Chronicles II 2:4; Leviticus 24:2–9; Exodus 29:38"> they sacrifice to the Lord whole-burnt-offering, morning and evening, and compound incense, and set the shewbread on the pure table; and there is the golden candlestick, and the lamps for burning, to light in the evening: for we keep the charge of the Lord God of our fathers; but ye have forsaken him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 5:39; Romans 8:31; Numbers 10:8–9"> And, behold, the Lord and his priests are with us at our head, and the signal trumpets to sound an alarm over us. Children of Israel, fight not against the Lord God of our fathers; for ye shall not prosper.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 8:4; Joshua 8:9; Proverbs 21:30"> Now Jeroboam had caused an ambush to come round upon him from behind: and he himself was before Juda, and the ambush behind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Chronicles II 14:11; Exodus 14:10; Joshua 8:20"> And Juda looked back, and, behold, the battle was against them before and behind: and they cried to the Lord, and the priests sounded with the trumpets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Chronicles II 14:12; Joshua 11:8; Psalms 118:4–7"> And the men of Juda shouted: and it came to pass, when the men of Juda shouted, that the Lord smote Jeroboam and Israel before Abia and Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Chronicles II 16:8; Judges 11:21; Joshua 10:12"> And the children of Israel fled from before Juda; and the Lord delivered them into their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Chronicles II 28:6; Chronicles II 13:3; Chronicles II 13:12"> And Abia and his people smote them with a great slaughter: and there fell slain of Israel five hundred thousand mighty men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Chronicles I 5:20; Chronicles II 14:11; Naum 1:7"> So the children of Israel were brought low in that day, and the children of Juda prevailed, because they trusted on the Lord God of their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Chronicles II 15:8; Joshua 15:9; Joshua 10:39"> And Abia pursued after Jeroboam, and he took from him the cities, Bæthel and her towns, and Jesyna and her towns, and Ephron and her towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings I 25:38; Kings III 14:20; Acts 12:23"> And Jeroboam did not recover strength again all the days of Abia: and the Lord smote him, and he died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 9:5; Kings II 5:12–13; Judges 10:4"> But Abia strengthened himself, and took to himself fourteen wives, and he begot twenty-two sons, and sixteen daughters.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Chronicles II 9:29; Chronicles II 12:15; Chronicles II 24:27"> And the rest of the acts of Abia, and his deeds, and his sayings, are written in the book of the prophet Addo.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And Abia died with his fathers, and they buried him in the city of David; and Asa his son reigned in his stead. In the days of Asa the land of Juda had rest ten years.</span>
</div>
</div>
