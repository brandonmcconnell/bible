<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings III 5:5; Chronicles I 22:10; Kings III 7:1"> And Solomon gathered seventy thousand men that bore burdens, and eighty thousand hewers of stone in the mountain, and there were three thousand six hundred superintendents over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Chronicles II 2:18; Kings III 5:15–16"> And Solomon sent to Chiram king of Tyre, saying, Whereas thou didst deal favourably with David my father, and didst send him cedars to build for himself a house to dwell in,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Chronicles I 14:1; Kings II 5:11; Kings III 5:1–11"> behold, I also his son am building a house to the name of the Lord my God, to consecrate it to him, to burn incense before him, and to offer shewbread continually, and to offer up whole-burnt-offerings continually morning and evening, and on the sabbaths, and at the new moons, and at the feasts of the Lord our God: this is a perpetual statute for Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 30:7; Exodus 25:30; Chronicles II 2:1"> And the house which I am building is to be great: for the Lord our God is great beyond all gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Chronicles I 16:25; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 135:5"> And who will be able to build him a house? for the heaven and heaven of heavens do not bear his glory: and who am I, that I should build him a house, save only to burn incense before him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esaias 66:1; Chronicles II 6:18; Kings III 8:27"> And now send me a man wise and skilled to work in gold, and in silver, and in brass, and in iron, and in purple, and in scarlet, and in blue, and one that knows how to grave together with the craftsmen who are with me in Juda and in Jerusalem, which materials my father David prepared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Chronicles II 2:13–14; Chronicles I 22:15–16; Exodus 31:3–5"> And send me from Libanus cedar wood, and wood of juniper, and pine; for I know that thy servants are skilled in cutting timber in Libanus: and, behold, thy servants shall go with my servants,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings III 5:6; Kings III 10:11; Chronicles II 9:10–11"> to prepare timber for me in abundance: for the house which I am building must be great and glorious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings III 9:8; Chronicles II 2:5; Chronicles II 7:21"> And, behold, I have given freely to thy servants that work and cut the wood, corn for food, even twenty thousand measures of wheat, and twenty thousand measures of barley, and twenty thousand measures of wine, and twenty thousand measures of oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings III 5:11; Ezra and Nehemiah 7:22; Romans 13:7–8"> And Chiram king of Tyre answered in writing, and sent to Solomon, saying, Because the Lord loved his people, he made thee king over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings III 10:9; Chronicles II 9:8; Psalms 72:17"> And Chiram said, Blessed be the Lord God of Israel, who made heaven and earth, who has given to king David a wise son, and one endowed with knowledge and understanding, who shall build a house for the Lord, and a house for his kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 33:6; 1 Peter 1:3; Revelation 4:11"> And now I have sent thee a wise and understanding man who belonged to Chiram my father</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Chronicles II 4:16"> (his mother was of the daughters of Dan, and his father was a Tyrian), skilled to work in gold, and in silver, and in brass, and in iron, and in stones and wood; and to weave with purple, and blue, and fine linen, and scarlet; and to engrave, and to understand every device, whatsoever thou shalt give him to do with thy craftsmen, and the craftsmen of my lord David thy father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Chronicles II 2:7; Exodus 31:3–4; Kings III 7:13–14"> And now, the wheat, and the barley, and the oil, and the wine which my lord mentioned, let him send to his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Chronicles II 2:10; Kings III 5:11"> And we will cut timber out of Libanus according to all thy need, and we will bring it on rafts to the sea of Joppa, and thou shalt bring it to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 19:46; Kings III 5:8–9; Ezra and Nehemiah 3:7"> And Solomon gathered all the foreigners that were in the land of Israel, after the numbering with which David his father numbered them; and there were found a hundred and fifty-three thousand six hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Chronicles I 22:2; Kings III 5:13–16; Chronicles II 8:7–8"> And he made of them seventy thousand burden-bearers, and eighty thousand hewers of stone, and three thousand six hundred taskmasters over the people.</span>
