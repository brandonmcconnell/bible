<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles I 3:12; Esaias 1:1; Matthew 1:9"> Joatham was twenty and five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem: and his mother's name was Jerusa, daughter of Sadoc.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings IV 15:34–35; Acts 5:13; Chronicles II 26:4"> And he did that which was right in the sight of the Lord, according to all that his father Ozias did: but he went not into the temple of the Lord. And still the people corrupted themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Chronicles II 33:14; Nehemiah 3:26–27; Jeremias 20:2"> He built the high gate of the house of the Lord, and he built much in the wall of Opel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Chronicles II 11:5–10; Chronicles II 26:9–10; Joshua 14:12–13"> In the mountain of Juda, and in the woods, he built both dwelling-places and towers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 11:4–33; Kings II 10:1–14; Jeremias 49:1–6"> He fought against the king of the children of Ammon, and prevailed against him: and the children of Ammon gave him even annually a hundred talents of silver, and ten thousand measures of wheat, and ten thousand of barley. These the king of the children of Ammon brought to him annually in the first and second and third years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Chronicles II 26:5; Chronicles II 19:3"> Joatham grew strong, because he prepared his ways before the Lord his God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Chronicles II 26:22–23; Kings IV 15:36; Chronicles II 32:32–33"> And the rest of the acts of Joatham, and his war, and his deeds, behold, they are written in the book of the kings of Juda and Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings IV 15:38"> And Joatham slept with his fathers, and was buried in the city of David: and Achaz his son reigned in his stead.</span>
</div>
