<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles II 15:12; Kings IV 11:4–20; Nehemiah 9:38"> And in the eighth year Jodae strengthened himself, and took the captains of hundreds, Azarias the son of Joram, and Ismael the son of Joanan, and Azarias the son of Obed, and Maasæas the son of Adia, and Elisaphan the son of Zacharias, with him into the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Chronicles II 11:13–17; Chronicles II 21:2; Chronicles I 15:12"> And they went round about Juda, and gathered the Levites out of all the cities of Juda, and heads of the families of Israel, and they came to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Chronicles II 21:7; Kings II 7:12; Chronicles II 6:16"> And all the congregation of Juda made a covenant with the king in the house of God. And he shewed them the king's son, and said to them, Lo, let the king's son reign, as the Lord said concerning the house of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Chronicles I 23:3–6; Chronicles I 26:13–16; Luke 1:8–9"> Now this is the thing which ye shall do. Let a third part of you, even of the priests and of the Levites, enter in on the sabbath, even into the gates of the entrances;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jezekiel 46:2–3; Acts 3:2; Kings IV 11:5–6"> and let a third part be in the house of the king; and another third at the middle gate: and all the people in the courts of the Lord's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Chronicles I 23:28–32; Kings IV 11:6–7"> And let not any one enter into the house of the Lord, except the priests and the Levites, and the servants of the Levites; they shall enter in, because they are holy: and let all the people keep the watch of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 3:38; Numbers 3:10; Kings IV 11:8–9"> And the Levites shall compass the king round about, every man's weapon in his hand; and whoever else goes into the house shall die: but they shall be with the king when he goes out, and when he comes in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings IV 11:9; Chronicles I 24:1–26"> And the Levites and all Juda did according to all that the priest Jodae commanded them, and they took each his men from the beginning of the sabbath to the end of the sabbath, for Jodae the priest did not dismiss the courses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings I 21:9; Kings II 8:7"> And Jodae gave to the men the swords, and the shields, and the arms, which had belonged to king David, in the house of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 40:6; Chronicles II 6:12; Kings IV 11:11"> And he set the whole people, every man with his arms, from the right side of the house to the left side of the altar and the house, over against the king round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings I 10:24; Exodus 25:16; Kings IV 11:12"> And he brought out the king's son, and put on him the crown and the testimony, and Jodae the priest and his sons proclaimed him king, and anointed him, and said, Long live the king!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings IV 11:13–16; Kings IV 9:32–37"> And Gotholia heard the sound of the people running, and acknowledging and praising the king: and she went in to the king into the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 29:2; Chronicles II 34:31; Kings IV 9:23"> And she looked, and, behold, the king stood in his place, and the princes and trumpets were at the entrance, and the princes were round the king: and all the people of the land rejoiced, and sounded the trumpets, and there were the singers singing with instruments, and singing hymns of praise. And Gotholia rent her robe, and cried, Ye surely are plotting against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jezekiel 9:7; Kings IV 11:8; Kings IV 11:15"> And Jodae the priest went forth, and Jodae the priest charged the captains of hundreds, even the captains of the host, and said to them, Thrust her forth outside the house, and follow her, and let her be slain with the sword. For the priest said, Let her not be slain in the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:28; Jeremias 31:40; Chronicles II 22:10"> So they let her go out; and she went through the horsemen's gate of the house of the king, and they slew her there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Chronicles II 29:10; Nehemiah 9:38; Kings IV 11:17"> And Jodae made a covenant between himself, and the people, and the king, that the people should be the Lord's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings III 18:40; Kings IV 10:25–28; Kings IV 18:4"> And all the people of the land went into the house of Baal, and tore down it and its altars, and they ground his images to powder, and they slew Matthan the priest of Baal before his altars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Chronicles II 5:5; Numbers 28:1–31; Chronicles II 29:25"> And Jodae the priest committed the works of the house of the Lord into the hand of the priests and Levites, and he re-established the courses of the priests and Levites which David appointed over the house of the Lord, and he appointed them to offer whole-burnt-offerings to the Lord, as it is written in the law of Moses, with gladness, and with songs by the hand of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Chronicles I 26:1–32; Chronicles I 9:22–24"> And the porters stood at the gates of the house of the Lord, that no one unclean in any respect should enter in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings IV 11:19; Kings IV 15:35; Kings IV 11:9–10"> And he took the heads of families, and the mighty men, and the chiefs of the people, and all the people of the land, and they conducted the king into the house of the Lord; and he went through the inner gate into the king's house, and they seated the king on the throne of the kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings IV 11:20; Revelation 18:20; Revelation 19:1–4"> And all the people of the land rejoiced; and the city was quiet: and they slew Gotholia.</span>
