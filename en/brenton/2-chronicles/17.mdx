<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings IV 22:2; Esaias 41:10; 2 Timothy 4:22"> And the Lord was with Josaphat, for he walked in the first ways of his father, and did not seek to idols;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings III 12:28; Luke 1:6; 1 Thessalonians 2:12"> but he sought to the Lord God of his father, and walked in the commandments of his father, and not according to the works of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Chronicles II 18:1; Matthew 6:33; Psalms 127:1"> And the Lord prospered the kingdom in his hand; and all Juda gave gifts to Josaphat; and he had great wealth and glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Osee 14:9; Chronicles II 15:17; Chronicles II 20:33"> And his heart was exalted in the way of the Lord; and he removed the high places and the groves from the land of Juda.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Chronicles II 35:3; Nehemiah 8:7–8; Ecclesiastes 12:9–10"> And in the third year of his reign, he sent his chief men, and his mighty men, Abdias and Zacharias, and Nathanael, and Michaias, to teach in the cities of Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 7:1–6; Chronicles II 19:8; Malachias 2:7"> And with them were the Levites, Samaias, and Nathanias, and Zabdias, and Asiel, and Semiramoth, and Jonathan, and Adonias, and Tobias, and Tobadonias, Levites, and with them Elisama and Joram, the priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 5:46; Romans 3:2; Acts 8:40"> And they taught in Juda, and there was with them the book of the law of the Lord, and they passed through the cities of Juda, and taught the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Chronicles II 14:14; Exodus 15:14–16; Proverbs 16:7"> And a terror of the Lord was upon all the kingdoms of the land round about Juda, and they made no war against Josaphat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Chronicles II 26:8; Chronicles II 9:14; Kings II 8:2"> And some of the Philistines brought to Josaphat gifts, and silver, and presents; and the Arabians brought him seven thousand seven hundred rams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Chronicles II 8:2–6; Chronicles II 14:6–7; Chronicles II 32:5"> And Josaphat increased in greatness exceedingly, and built in Judea places of abode, and strong cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Chronicles I 27:25–31; Chronicles II 26:10–15"> And he had many works in Judea: and the mighty men of war, the men of strength, were in Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Chronicles II 11:1; Genesis 15:5; Numbers 1:18"> And this is their number according to the houses of their fathers; even the captains of thousands in Juda were, Ednas the chief, and with him mighty men of strength three hundred thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And after him, Joanan the captain, and with him two hundred and eighty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 5:9; Judges 5:2; Chronicles I 29:9"> And after him Amasias the son of Zari, who was zealous for the Lord; and with him two hundred thousand mighty men of strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Chronicles II 14:8; Kings II 1:21–22"> And out of Benjamin there was a mighty man of strength, even Eliada, and with him two hundred thousand archers and targeteers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And after him Jozabad, and with him a hundred and eighty thousand mighty men of war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Chronicles II 17:2; Chronicles II 11:12; Chronicles II 11:23"> These were the king's servants, besides those whom the king put in the strong cities in all Judea.</span>
