<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings IV 18:13–37; Kings IV 17:6; Chronicles II 20:1–2"> And after these things and this faithful dealing, came Sennacherim king of the Assyrians, and he came to Juda, and encamped against the fortified cities, and intended to take them for himself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 9:53; Luke 9:51; Kings IV 12:17"> And Ezekias saw that Sennacherim was come, and that his face was set to fight against Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 24:6; Proverbs 20:18; Proverbs 15:22"> And he took counsel with his elders and his mighty men to stop the wells of water which were without the city: and they helped him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Chronicles II 32:30; Kings IV 18:9; Kings III 19:21"> And he collected many people, and stopped the wells of water, and the river that flowed through the city, saying, Lest the king of Assyria come, and find much water, and strengthen himself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Chronicles II 25:23; Kings III 9:24; Kings IV 25:4"> And Ezekias strengthened himself, and built all the wall that had been pulled down, and the towers, and another wall in front without, and fortified the strong place of the city of David, and prepared arms in abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esaias 40:2; Chronicles II 30:22; Nehemiah 8:16"> And he appointed captains of war over the people, and they were gathered to meet him to the open place of the gate of the valley, and he encouraged them, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings IV 6:16; Romans 8:31; Ephesians 6:10"> Be strong and courageous, and fear not, neither be dismayed before the king of Assyria, and before all the nation that is with him: for there are more with us than with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremias 17:5; Chronicles II 20:17; Esaias 41:10"> With him are arms of flesh; but with us is the Lord our God to save us, and to fight our battle. And the people were encouraged at the words of Ezekias king of Juda.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings IV 18:17; Joshua 10:31; Esaias 36:2"> And afterward Sennacherim king of the Assyrians sent his servants to Jerusalem; and he went himself against Lachis, and all his army with him, and sent to Ezekias king of Juda, and to all Juda that was in Jerusalem, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings IV 18:19; Esaias 36:4"> Thus says Sennacherim king of the Assyrians, On what do ye trust, that ye will remain in the siege in Jerusalem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 27:43; Psalms 11:1–3; Esaias 36:18"> Does not Ezekias deceive you, to deliver you to death and famine and thirst, saying, The Lord our God will deliver us out of the hand of the king of Assyria?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Chronicles II 31:1; Deuteronomy 12:13–14; Deuteronomy 12:26–27"> Is not this Ezekias who has taken down his altars and his high places, and has spoken to Juda and the dwellers in Jerusalem, saying, Ye shall worship before this altar, and burn incense upon it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings IV 18:33–35; Esaias 44:8–10; Daniel 4:30"> Know ye not what I and my fathers have done to all the nations of the countries? Could the gods of the nations of all the earth at all rescue their people out of my hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esaias 10:11–12; Exodus 15:9–11; Esaias 42:8"> Who is there among all the gods of those nations whom my fathers utterly destroyed, worthy of trust? Could they deliver their people out of my hand, that your God should deliver you out of my hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 5:2; Daniel 3:15; Esaias 36:18"> Now then, let not Ezekias deceive you, and let him not make you thus confident, and believe him not: for no god of any kingdom or nation is at all able to deliver his people out of my hand, or the hand of my fathers: therefore your God shall not deliver you out of my hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 73:9; John 15:21; Job 15:25–26"> And his servants continued to speak against the Lord God, and against his servant Ezekias.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Esaias 37:14; Kings IV 19:12; Esaias 37:28–29"> And he wrote a letter to reproach the Lord God of Israel, and spoke concerning him, saying, As the gods of the nations of the earth have not delivered their people out of my hand, so the God of Ezekias shall by no means deliver his people out of my hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings I 17:10; Kings IV 18:26–28; Kings I 17:26"> And he cried with a loud voice in the Jews' language to the people of Jerusalem on the wall, calling them to assist them, and pull down the walls, that they might take the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings IV 19:18; Esaias 2:8; Jeremias 32:30"> And he spoke against the God of Jerusalem, even as against the gods of the nations of the earth, the works of the hands of men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 91:14–15; Esaias 37:14–20; Chronicles II 14:11"> And king Ezekias and Esaias the prophet the son of Amos prayed concerning these things, and they cried to heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esaias 37:36–38; Psalms 76:5; Psalms 18:50"> And the Lord sent an angel, and he destroyed every mighty man and warrior, and leader and captain in the camp of the king of Assyria: and he returned with shame of face to his own land and came into the house of his god: and some of them that came out of his bowels slew him with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Esaias 31:4–5; Psalms 18:48–50; 2 Thessalonians 3:5"> So the Lord delivered Ezekias and the dwellers in Jerusalem out of the hand of Sennacherim king of Assyria, and out of the hand of all his enemies, and gave them rest round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Chronicles II 17:5; Matthew 2:11; Chronicles II 1:1"> And many brought gifts to the Lord to Jerusalem, and presents to Ezekias king of Juda; and he was exalted in the eyes of all the nations after these things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Esaias 38:1–8; Kings IV 20:1–11; Esaias 38:21–22"> In those days Ezekias was sick even to death, and prayed to the Lord: and he hearkened to him, and gave him a sign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Chronicles II 24:18; Chronicles II 26:16; Chronicles II 32:31"> But Ezekias did not recompense the Lord according to the return which he made him, but his heart was lifted up: and wrath came upon him, and upon Juda and Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremias 26:18–19; Chronicles II 34:27–28; Chronicles II 33:12"> And Ezekias humbled himself after the exaltation of his heart, he and the dwellers in Jerusalem; and the wrath of the Lord did not come upon them in the days of Ezekias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Chronicles I 27:25–31; Chronicles II 1:12; Proverbs 10:22"> And Ezekias had wealth and very great glory: and he made for himself treasuries of gold, and silver, and precious stones, also for spices, and stores for arms, and for precious vessels;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Kings II 7:8; Chronicles II 26:10; Kings III 4:26"> and cities for the produce of corn, and wine, and oil; and stalls and mangers for every kind of cattle, and folds for flocks;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Chronicles I 29:12; Proverbs 10:22; Kings I 2:7"> and cities which he built for himself, and store of sheep and oxen in abundance, for the Lord gave him a very great store.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Kings III 1:33; Esaias 22:9–11; Psalms 1:1–3"> The same Ezekias stopped up the course of the water of Gion above, and brought the water down straight south of the city of David. And Ezekias prospered in all his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:2; Proverbs 17:3; Genesis 22:1"> Notwithstanding, in regard to the ambassadors of the princes of Babylon, who were sent to him to enquire of him concerning the prodigy which came upon the land, the Lord left him, to try him, to know what was in his heart.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Esaias 36:1–22; Kings IV 18:1–20; Chronicles II 31:20–21"> And the rest of the acts of Ezekias, and his kindness, behold, they are written in the prophecy of Esaias the son of Amos the prophet, and in the book of the kings of Juda and Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; Kings III 1:21; Chronicles II 16:14"> And Ezekias slept with his fathers, and they buried him in a high place among the sepulchres of the sons of David: and all Juda and the dwellers in Jerusalem gave him glory and honour at his death. And Manasses his son reigned in his stead.</span>
</div>
