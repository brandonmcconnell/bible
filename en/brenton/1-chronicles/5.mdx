<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 21:9; Chronicles I 5:18–21; Psalms 83:6"> And in the days of Saul they made war upon the sojourners in the land; and they fell into their hands, all of them dwelling in their tents eastward of Galaad.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 13:11; Joshua 13:24–28; Deuteronomy 3:10–17"> The sons of Gad dwelt over against them in the land of Basan even to Sela.</span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And their brethren according to the houses of their fathers; Michael, Mosollam, and Sebee, and Joree, and Joachan, and Zue, and Obed, seven.</span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> who was the brother of the son of Abdiel, the son of Guni, he was chief of the house of their families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Chronicles I 27:29; Song of Solomon 2:1; Esaias 35:2"> They dwelt in Galaad, in Basan, and in their villages, and in all the country round about Saron to the border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings IV 14:16; Kings IV 14:28; Kings IV 15:32"> The enumeration of them all took place in the days of Joatham king of Juda, and in the days of Jeroboam king of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 1:3; Joshua 4:12–13"> The sons of Ruben and Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasse, of mighty men, bearing shields and sword, and bending the bow, and skilled in war, were forty and four thousand and seven hundred and sixty, going forth to battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 25:15; Chronicles I 1:31; Chronicles I 5:10"> And they made war with the Agarenes, and Itureans, and Naphiseans, and Nadabeans,</span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 18:13; Numbers 31:35"> And they took captive their store; five thousand camels, and two hundred and fifty thousand sheep, two thousand asses, and a hundred thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Chronicles II 32:8; Romans 8:31; Joshua 23:10"> For many fell slain, because the war was of God. And they dwelt in their place until the captivity.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:8–9; Song of Solomon 4:8; Psalms 133:3"> And the half-tribe of Manasse dwelt from Basan to Baal, Ermon, and Sanir, and to the mount Aërmon: and they increased in Libanus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Chronicles I 4:38"> And these were the heads of the houses of their families; Opher, and Sei, and Eliel, and Jeremia, and Oduia, and Jediel, mighty men of valour, men of renown, heads of the houses of their families.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Revelation 17:5; Psalms 106:34–39; Exodus 34:15"> But they rebelled against the God of their fathers, and went a-whoring after the gods of the nations of the land, whom God cast out from before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Kings IV 15:19; Kings IV 17:6; Kings IV 15:29"> And the God of Israel stirred up the spirit of Phaloch king of Assyria, and the spirit of Thagla-phallasar king of Assyria, and carried away Ruben and Gaddi, and the half-tribe of Manasse, and brought them to Chaach, and Chabor, and to the river Gozan, until this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> The sons of Levi: Gedson, Caath, and Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And the sons of Caath; Ambram, and Issaar, Chebron, and Oziel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And the sons of Ambram; Aaron, and Moses, and Mariam: and the sons of Aaron; Nadab, and Abiud, Eleazar, and Ithamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Eleazar begot Phinees, Phinees begot Abisu;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Abisu begot Bokki, and Bokki begot Ozi;</span>

<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> and Mariel begot Amaria, and Amaria begot Achitob;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> and Achitob begot Sadoc, and Sadoc begot Achimaas;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> and Achimaas begot Azarias, and Azarias begot Joanan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> and Joanan begot Azarias: he ministered as priest in the house which Solomon built in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And Azarias begot Amaria, and Amaria begot Achitob;</span>
