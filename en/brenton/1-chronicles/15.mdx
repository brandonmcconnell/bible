<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles I 16:1; Chronicles I 17:1–5; Psalms 132:5"> And David made for himself houses in the city of David, and he prepared a place for the ark of God, and made a tent for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:8; Deuteronomy 31:9; Numbers 18:1–8"> Then said David, It is not lawful for any to bear the ark of God, but the Levites; for the Lord has chosen them to bear the ark of the Lord, and to minister to him for ever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings III 8:1; Chronicles I 13:5; Chronicles I 15:1"> And David assembled all Israel at Jerusalem, to bring up the ark of the Lord to the place which he had prepared for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 6:16–22; Chronicles I 12:26–28; Chronicles I 6:16–30"> And David gathered together the sons of Aaron the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Chronicles I 6:22–24"> Of the sons of Caath; there was Uriel the chief, and his brethren, a hundred and twenty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Chronicles I 6:29–30"> Of the sons of Merari; Asaia the chief, and his brethren, two hundred and twenty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Chronicles I 15:11; Chronicles I 23:8"> Of the sons of Gedson; Joel the chief, and his brethren, a hundred and thirty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 6:22"> Of the sons of Elisaphat; Semei the chief, and his brethren, two hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 6:18; Chronicles I 23:19; Chronicles I 26:30–31"> Of the sons of Chebrom; Eliel the chief, and his brethren eighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 6:18; Chronicles I 6:18; Chronicles I 23:12"> Of the sons of Oziel; Aminadab the chief, and his brethren a hundred and twelve.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Chronicles I 12:28; Kings III 2:35; Kings I 22:20–23"> And David called Sadoc and Abiathar the priests, and the Levites, Uriel, Asaia, and Joel, and Semaia, and Eliel, and Aminadab,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Chronicles II 35:6; Exodus 19:14–15; Chronicles I 15:14"> and said to them, Ye are the heads of the families of the Levites: sanctify yourselves, you and your brethren, and ye shall carry up the ark of the God of Israel, to the place which I have prepared for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings II 6:3; 1 Corinthians 14:40; 1 John 1:8–10"> For because ye were not ready at the first, our God made a breach upon us, because we sought him not according to the ordinance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Chronicles II 29:15; Joel 2:16–17; Chronicles II 29:34"> So the priests and the Levites sanctified themselves, to bring up the ark of the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; Numbers 7:9; Chronicles II 5:9"> And the sons of the Levites took the ark of God, (as Moses commanded by the word of God according to the scripture) upon their shoulders with staves.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Chronicles I 13:8; Chronicles II 5:13; Chronicles I 16:42"> And David said to the chiefs of the Levites, Set your brethren the singers with musical instruments, lutes, harps, and cymbals, to sound aloud with a voice of joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Chronicles I 6:39; Chronicles I 6:44; Chronicles I 6:33"> So the Levites appointed Æman the son of Joel; Asaph the son of Barachias was one of his brethren; and Æthan the son of Kisæus was of the sons of Merari their brethren;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Chronicles I 26:4; Chronicles I 26:8; Chronicles I 13:14"> and with them their brethren of the second rank, Zacharias, and Oziel, and Semiramoth, and Jeiel, and Elioel, and Eliab, and Banaia, and Maasaia, and Matthathia, and Eliphena, and Makellia, and Abdedom, and Jeiel, and Ozias, the porters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Chronicles I 25:6; Chronicles I 16:5; Chronicles I 13:8"> And the singers, Æman, Asaph, and Æthan, with brazen cymbals to make a sound to be heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 46:1–11"> Zacharias, and Oziel, Semiramoth, Jeiel, Oni, Eliab, Maasæas, Banæas, with lutes, on alæmoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings I 10:5; Chronicles I 25:6–7; Psalms 81:1–2"> And Mattathias, and Eliphalu, and Makenia, and Abdedom, and Jeiel, and Ozias, with harps of Amasenith, to make a loud noise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Chronicles I 15:27; Chronicles I 15:16; Chronicles I 25:7–8"> And Chonenia chief of the Levites was master of the bands, because he was skilful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 84:10; Kings IV 22:4; Chronicles I 9:21–23"> And Barachia and Elcana were door-keepers of the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Chronicles I 16:6; Numbers 10:8; Chronicles I 15:28"> And Somnia, and Josaphat, and Nathanael, and Amasai, and Zacharia, and Banæa, and Eliezer, the priests, were sounding with trumpets before the ark of God: and Abdedom and Jeia were door-keepers of the ark of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings II 6:12–23; Michæas 5:2; Philippians 3:3"> So David, and the elders of Israel, and the captains of thousands, went to bring up the ark of the covenant from the house of Abdedom with gladness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 23:29; Chronicles I 29:14; Psalms 66:13–15"> And it came to pass when God strengthened the Levites bearing the ark of the covenant of the Lord, that they sacrificed seven calves and seven rams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Kings I 2:18; Kings II 6:14"> And David was girt with a fine linen robe, and all the Levites who were bearing the ark of the covenant of the Lord, and the singers, and Chonenias the master of the band of singers; also upon David there was a robe of fine linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Chronicles I 13:8; Chronicles I 15:16; Chronicles II 5:12–13"> And all Israel brought up the ark of the covenant of the Lord with shouting, and with the sound of a horn, and with trumpets, and with cymbals, playing loudly on lutes and harps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Kings II 6:20–23; Kings II 6:16; Deuteronomy 31:26"> And the ark of the covenant of the Lord arrived, and came to the city of David; and Melchol the daughter of Saul looked down through the window, and saw king David dancing and playing: and she despised him in her heart.</span>
</div>
