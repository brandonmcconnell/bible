<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles II 6:7–9; Chronicles I 15:1; Chronicles I 17:5"> And it came to pass as David dwelt in his house, that David said to Nathan the prophet, Behold, I dwell in a house of cedar, but the ark of the covenant of the Lord is under curtains of skins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Chronicles I 28:2; 1 Corinthians 13:9; Zacharias 8:23"> And Nathan said to David, Do all that is in thy heart; for God is with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 3:7; Numbers 12:6; Esaias 30:21"> And it came to pass in that night, that the word of the Lord came to Nathan, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Chronicles I 28:2–3; Esaias 55:8–9; Chronicles II 6:8–9"> Go and say to David my servant, Thus said the Lord, Thou shalt not build me a house for me to dwell in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings II 7:6; Exodus 40:2–3; Kings III 8:16"> For I have not dwelt in a house from the day that I brought up Israel until this day, but I have been in a tabernacle and a tent,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings II 7:7; Numbers 10:33–36; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> in all places through which I have gone with all Israel: did I ever speak to any one tribe of Israel whom I commanded to feed my people, saying, Why is it that ye have not built me a house of cedar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings II 6:21; Exodus 3:1–10; Kings I 17:15"> And now thus shalt thou say to my servant David, Thus saith the Lord Almighty, I took thee from the sheepfold, from following the flocks, to be a ruler over my people Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings I 18:14; Chronicles I 17:2; Psalms 113:7–8"> and I was with thee in all places whither thou wentest, and I destroyed all thine enemies from before thee, and I made for thee a name according to the name of the great ones that are upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 2:23; Jezekiel 28:4; Ephesians 5:6"> And I will appoint a place for my people Israel, and I will plant him, and he shall dwell by himself, and shall no longer be anxious; and the son of iniquity shall no longer afflict him, as at the beginning,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings II 7:11; Judges 2:14–18; Exodus 1:21"> and from the days when I appointed judges over my people Israel. Also I have humbled all thine enemies, and I will increase thee, and the Lord will build thee a house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 132:11; Kings III 1:21; Chronicles I 28:5"> And it shall come to pass when thy days shall be fulfilled, and thou shalt sleep with thy fathers, that I will raise up thy seed after thee, which shall be of thy bowels, and I will establish his kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings III 5:5; Daniel 2:44; Esaias 9:7"> He shall build me a house, and I will set up his throne for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 1:5; Esaias 55:3; Chronicles I 17:12"> I will be to him a father, and he shall be to me a son: and my mercy will I not withdraw from him, as I withdrew it from them that were before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 89:36; Luke 1:32–33; Psalms 72:17"> And I will establish him in my house and in his kingdom for ever; and his throne shall be set up for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremias 23:28; Acts 20:27; Kings II 7:17"> According to all these words, and according to all this vision, so spoke Nathan to David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings II 7:18; Genesis 32:10; Ephesians 3:8"> And king David came and sat before the Lord, and said, Who am I, O Lord God? and what is my house, that thou hast loved me for ever?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Chronicles I 17:11–15; Philippians 2:8–11; Esaias 49:6"> And these things were little in thy sight, O God: thou hast also spoken concerning the house of thy servant for a long time to come, and thou hast looked upon me as a man looks upon his fellow, and hast exalted me, O Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 21:17; Kings I 2:30; Kings II 7:20–24"> What shall David do more toward thee to glorify thee? and thou knowest thy servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esaias 37:35; Kings II 7:21; Daniel 9:17"> And thou hast wrought all this greatness according to thine heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Esaias 44:6; Jeremias 10:6–7; Ephesians 3:20"> O Lord, there is none like thee, and there is no God beside thee, according to all things which we have heard with our ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jezekiel 20:9–10; Esaias 63:9; Esaias 48:9"> Neither is there another nation upon the earth such as thy people Israel, whereas God led him in the way, to redeem a people for himself, to make for himself a great and glorious name, to cast out nations from before thy people, whom thou redeemedst out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 19:5–6; 1 Peter 2:9; Romans 11:1–12"> And thou hast appointed thy people Israel as a people to thyself for ever; and thou, Lord, didst become a God to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 119:49; Jeremias 11:5; Luke 1:38"> And now, Lord, let the word which thou spokest to thy servant, and concerning his house, be confirmed for ever, and do thou as thou hast spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 6:13; Jeremias 31:1; Revelation 21:3"> And let thy name be established and magnified for ever, men saying, Lord, Lord, Almighty God of Israel: and let the house of thy servant David be established before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings I 9:15; Jezekiel 36:37; Psalms 10:17"> For thou, O Lord my God, hast revealed to the ear of thy servant that thou wilt build him a house; therefore thy servant has found a willingness to pray before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 34:6–7; Hebrews 6:18; Titus 1:2"> And now, Lord, thou thyself art God, and thou hast spoken these good things concerning thy servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ephesians 1:3; Romans 11:29; Psalms 72:17"> And now thou hast begun to bless the house of thy servant, so that it should continue for ever before thee: for thou, Lord, hast blessed it, and do thou bless it for ever.</span>
