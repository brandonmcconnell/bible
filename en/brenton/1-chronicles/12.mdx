<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Aza the chief, Abdia the second, Eliab the third,</span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 6:34; Judges 3:10; Kings II 17:25"> And David went out to meet them, and said to them, If ye are come peaceably to me, let my heart be at peace with you: but if ye are come to betray me to my enemies unfaithfully, the God of your fathers look upon it, and reprove it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings I 29:2–9"> And the Spirit came upon Amasai, a captain of the thirty, and he said, Go, David, son of Jesse, thou and thy people, peace, peace be to thee, and peace to thy helpers, for thy God has helped thee. And David received them, and made them captains of the forces.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:17; Deuteronomy 1:15; Kings I 29:11"> And some came to David from Manasse, when the Philistines came against Saul to war: and he helped them not, because the captains of the Philistines took counsel, saying, With the heads of those men will he return to his master Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings I 30:1–17; Chronicles I 12:20; Chronicles I 5:24"> When David was going to Sikelag, there came to him of Manasse, Edna and Jozabath, and Rodiel, and Michael, and Josabaith, and Elimuth, and Semathi: these are the captains of thousands of Manasse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings II 3:1; Job 17:9; Genesis 32:2"> And they fought on the side of David against a troop, for they were all men of might; and they were commanders in the army, because of their might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings II 2:3–4; Chronicles I 11:10; Kings I 16:1"> For daily men came to David, till they amounted to a great force, as the force of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> The sons of Juda, bearing shields and spears, six thousand and eight hundred mighty in war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Of the sons of Symeon mighty for battle, seven thousand and a hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Kings IV 11:9; Kings IV 25:18; Chronicles I 6:49–57"> Of the sons of Levi, four thousand and six hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Kings II 8:17; Chronicles I 6:8; Chronicles I 6:53"> And Joadas the chief of the family of Aaron, and with him three thousand and seven hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Kings II 2:8–9; Chronicles I 12:2; Genesis 31:23"> And Sadoc, a young man mighty in strength, and there were twenty-two leaders of his father's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 6:4"> And of the sons of Benjamin, the brethren of Saul, three thousand: and still the greater part of them kept the guard of the house of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 17:1–18"> And of the sons of Ephraim, twenty thousand and eight hundred mighty men, famous in the houses of their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ephesians 5:17; Luke 12:56–57; Esther 1:13"> And of the half-tribe of Manasse, eighteen thousand, even those who were named by name, to make David king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 12:2; John 1:47"> And of the sons of Issachar having wisdom with regard to the times, knowing what Israel should do, two hundred; and all their brethren with them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And of Zabulon they that went out to battle, with all weapons of war, were fifty thousand to help David, not weak-handed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And of Nephthali a thousand captains, and with them men with shields and spears, thirty-seven thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Chronicles I 12:33; Joel 2:7"> And of the Danites men ready for war twenty-eight thousand and eight hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Joshua 14:3; Deuteronomy 3:12–16; Joshua 13:7–32"> And of Aser, they that went out to give aid in war, forty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 49:8–10; Kings III 8:61; Psalms 101:2"> And from the country beyond Jordan, from Ruben, and the Gadites, and from the half-tribe of Manasse, a hundred and twenty thousand, with all weapons of war.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 31:54; Kings II 19:42; Genesis 26:30"> All these were men of war, setting the army in battle array, with a peaceful mind towards him, and they came to Chebron to make David king over all Israel: and the rest of Israel were of one mind to make David king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Kings I 25:18; Kings II 16:1; Jeremias 23:5–6"> And they were there three days eating and drinking, for their brethren had made preparations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> And their neighbours, as far as Issachar and Zabulon and Nephthali, brought to them upon camels, and asses, and mules, and upon calves, victuals, meal, cakes of figs, raisins, wine, and oil, calves and sheep abundantly: for there was joy in Israel.</span>
</div>
