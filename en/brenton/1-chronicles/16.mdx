<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles I 15:1; Ezra and Nehemiah 6:16–18; Chronicles II 5:6–7"> So they brought in the ark of God, and set it in the midst of the tabernacle which David pitched for it; and they offered whole-burnt-offerings and peace-offerings before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 47:10; Genesis 14:19; Leviticus 1:3"> And David finished offering up whole-burnt-offerings and peace-offerings, and blessed the people in the name of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:10; Jezekiel 45:17; Chronicles II 35:7–8"> And he divided to every man of Israel (both men and women), to every man one baker's loaf, and a cake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Chronicles I 23:27–32; Psalms 72:18; Psalms 103:2"> And he appointed before the ark of the covenant of the Lord, Levites to minister and lift up the voice, and to give thanks and praise the Lord God of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Chronicles I 6:39; Chronicles II 29:25; Chronicles I 15:16–24"> Asaph was the chief, and next to him Zacharias, Jeiel, Semiramoth, and Jeiel, Mattathias, Eliab, and Banæas, and Abdedom: and Jeiel sounding with musical instruments, lutes and harps, and Asaph with cymbals:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Chronicles II 13:12; Chronicles II 29:26–28; Chronicles II 5:12–13"> and Banæas and Oziel the priests sounding continually with trumpets before the ark of the covenant of God in that day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings II 22:1; Psalms 18:1; Kings II 23:1–2"> Then David first gave orders to praise the Lord by the hand of Asaph and his brethren.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 105:1–15; Esaias 12:4; Kings IV 19:19"> Song. Give thanks to the Lord, call upon him by his name, make known his designs among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; Psalms 96:1–3; Psalms 98:1–4"> Sing songs to him, and sing hymns to him, relate to all people his wonderful deeds, which the Lord has wrought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–8; Jeremias 29:13; Psalms 34:2"> Praise his holy name, the heart that seeks his pleasure shall rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 27:8–9; Sophonias 2:2–3; Psalms 68:35"> Seek the Lord and be strong, seek his face continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 111:4; Psalms 119:20; Psalms 103:2"> Remember his wonderful works which he has wrought, his wonders, and the judgments of his mouth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Exodus 19:5–6; Psalms 135:4"> ye seed of Israel his servants, ye seed of Jacob his chosen ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 48:10–11; Esaias 26:9; Exodus 15:2"> He is the Lord our God; his judgments are in all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 105:8; Malachias 4:4; Deuteronomy 7:9"> Let us remember his covenant for ever, his word which he commanded to a thousand generations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 26:3; Genesis 17:2; Genesis 35:11"> which he covenanted with Abraham, and his oath sworn to Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 17:7–8; Genesis 35:11–12; Joshua 24:11–13"> He confirmed it to Jacob for an ordinance, to Israel as an everlasting covenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 13:15; Michæas 2:5; Genesis 17:8"> saying, To thee will I give the land of Chanaan, the line of your inheritance:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; Deuteronomy 7:7; Acts 7:5"> when they were few in number, when they were but little, and dwelt as strangers in it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 46:6; Genesis 20:1; Genesis 46:3"> and went from nation to nation, and from one kingdom to another people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 12:17; Genesis 20:3; Exodus 7:15–18"> He suffered not a man to oppress them, and he reproved kings for their sakes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 20:7; Psalms 105:15; Genesis 49:8–10"> saying, Touch not my anointed ones, and deal not wrongfully with my prophets.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 96:1–13; Psalms 71:15; Esaias 51:6–8"> Sing ye to the Lord, all the earth; proclaim his salvation from day to day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 96:1–13; Psalms 71:15; Esaias 51:6–8"> Declare among the nations his glory, his wondrous deeds among all peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 89:7; Psalms 76:7; Psalms 66:3–5"> For the Lord is great, and greatly to be praised: he is to be feared above all gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 102:25; Leviticus 19:4; Esaias 42:5"> For all the gods of the nations are idols; but our God made the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 16:11; Psalms 96:6; Psalms 28:7–8"> Glory and praise are in his presence; strength and rejoicing are in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:10; Philippians 4:13; Psalms 100:1–2"> Give to the Lord, ye families of the nations, give to the Lord glory and strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 100:4; Psalms 96:9; Esaias 6:3"> Give to the Lord the glory belonging to his name: take gifts and offer them before him; and worship the Lord in his holy courts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 33:9; Psalms 148:5–6; Hebrews 1:3"> Let the whole earth fear before him; let the earth be established, and not be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 96:10; Revelation 19:6; Psalms 97:1"> Let the heavens rejoice, and let the earth exult; and let them say among the nations, The Lord reigns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 148:9–10; Esaias 44:23; Psalms 98:7–8"> The sea with its fulness shall resound and the tree of the field, and all things in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 98:9; Psalms 96:12–13; Revelation 11:17–18"> Then shall the trees of the wood rejoice before the Lord, for he is come to judge the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 106:1; Chronicles II 7:3; Psalms 107:1"> Give thanks to the Lord, for it is good, for his mercy is for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 106:47–48; 1 Peter 2:9; Psalms 14:7"> And say ye, Save us, O God of our salvation, and gather us, and rescue us from among the heathen, that we may praise thy holy name, and glory in thy praises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Kings III 8:15; Psalms 72:18–19; Kings III 8:56"> Blessed be the Lord God of Israel from everlasting and to everlasting:<br />And all the people shall say, Amen. So they praised the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Chronicles II 8:14; Ezra and Nehemiah 3:4; Chronicles I 16:4–6"> And they left there Asaph and his brethren before the ark of the covenant of the Lord, to minister before the ark continually, according to the service of each day: from day to day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Chronicles I 26:10; Chronicles I 13:14; Chronicles I 25:3"> And Abdedom and his brethren were sixty and eight; and Abdedom the son of Idithun, and Osa, were to be door-keepers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Kings III 3:4; Chronicles I 15:11; Chronicles II 1:3–4"> And they appointed Sadoc the priest, and his brethren the priests, before the tabernacle of the Lord in the high place in Gabaon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 28:3–8; Exodus 29:38–42; Chronicles II 31:3"> to offer up whole-burnt-offerings to the Lord on the altar of whole-burnt-offerings continually morning and evening, and according to all things written in the law of the Lord, which he commanded the children of Israel by Moses the servant of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Chronicles II 5:13; Chronicles I 25:1–6; Chronicles II 20:21"> And with him were Æman and Idithun, and the rest chosen out by name to praise the Lord, for his mercy endures for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 150:3–6; Chronicles I 25:6–7; Chronicles II 29:25–28"> And with them there were trumpets and cymbals to sound aloud, and musical instruments for the songs of God: and the sons of Idithun were at the gate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Kings II 6:19–20; Genesis 18:19; Psalms 101:2"> And all the people went every one to his home: and David returned to bless his house.</span>
</div>
