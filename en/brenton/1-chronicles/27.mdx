<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings III 4:27; Chronicles II 26:11–13; Deuteronomy 1:15"> Now the sons of Israel according to their number, heads of families, captains of thousands and captains of hundreds, and scribes ministering to the king, and for every affair of the king according to their divisions, for every ordinance of coming in and going out monthly, for all the months of the year, one division of them was twenty-four thousand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Chronicles I 11:11; Kings II 23:8"> And over the first division of the first month was Isboaz the son of Zabdiel: in his division were twenty-four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 26:20; Numbers 10:14; Genesis 49:8–10"> Of the sons of Tharez one was chief of all the captains of the host for the first month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings II 23:9; Chronicles I 11:12"> And over the division of the second month was Dodia the son of Ecchoc, and over his division was Makelloth also chief: and in his division were twenty and four thousand, chief men of the host.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Chronicles I 18:17; Kings III 4:4–5"> The third for the third month was Banaias the son of Jodae the chief priest: and in his division were twenty and four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings II 23:20–23; Chronicles I 11:22–25; Kings II 22:20–23"> This Banæas was more mighty than the thirty, and over the thirty: and Zabad his son was over his division.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Chronicles I 11:26; Kings II 23:24; Kings II 2:18–23"> The fourth for the fourth month was Asael the brother of Joab, and Zabadias his son, and his brethren: and in his division were twenty and four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Chronicles I 11:27; Kings II 23:25; Chronicles I 26:29"> The fifth chief for the fifth month was Samaoth the Jezraite: and in his division were twenty and four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Chronicles I 11:28; Kings II 23:26"> The sixth for the sixth month was Hoduias the son of Ekkes the Thecoite: and in his division were twenty and four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Chronicles I 11:27; Kings II 23:26"> The seventh for the seventh month was Chelles of Phallus of the children of Ephraim: and in his division were twenty and four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings II 21:18; Chronicles I 11:29; Numbers 26:20"> The eighth for the eighth month was Sobochai the Usathite, belonging to Zarai: and in his division were twenty and four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Chronicles I 11:28; Kings II 23:27"> The ninth for the ninth month was Abiezer of Anathoth, of the land of Benjamin: and in his division were twenty and four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Chronicles I 11:30; Kings II 23:28"> The tenth for the tenth month was Meera the Netophathite, belonging to Zarai: and in his division were twenty and four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Chronicles I 11:31; Kings II 23:30"> The eleventh for the eleventh month was Banaias of Pharathon, of the sons of Ephraim: and in his division were twenty and four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings II 23:29; Chronicles I 4:13; Judges 3:9"> The twelfth for the twelfth month was Choldia the Netophathite, belonging to Gothoniel: and in his division were twenty and four thousand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And over the tribes of Israel, the chief for Ruben was Eliezer the son of Zechri: for Symeon, Saphatias the son of Maacha:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Chronicles I 26:30; Chronicles I 24:31; Chronicles I 12:27–28"> for Levi, Asabias the son of Camuel: for Aaron, Sadoc:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings I 16:6; Kings I 17:29; Kings I 17:13"> for Juda, Eliab of the brethren of David: for Issachar, Ambri the son of Michael:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> for Zabulon, Samæas the son of Abdiu: for Nephthali, Jerimoth the son of Oziel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> for Ephraim, Ose the son of Ozia: for the half-tribe of Manasse, Joel the son of Phadæa:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings II 3:27; Kings I 14:50–51; Kings II 3:37"> for the half-tribe of Manasse in the land of Galaad, Jadai the son of Zadæas, for the sons of Benjamin, Jasiel the son of Abenner:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Chronicles I 28:1"> for Dan, Azariel the son of Iroab: these are the chiefs of the tribes of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 15:5; Chronicles I 21:2–5; Hebrews 11:12"> But David took not their number from twenty years old and under: because the Lord said that he would multiply Israel as the stars of the heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Chronicles I 21:1–17; Kings II 24:1–15"> And Joab the son of Saruia began to number the people, and did not finish the work, for there was hereupon wrath on Israel; and the number was not recorded in the book of the chronicles of king David.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Chronicles II 16:2; Exodus 1:11; Chronicles II 26:10"> And over the king's treasures was Asmoth the son of Odiel; and over the treasures in the country, and in the towns, and in the villages, and in the towers, was Jonathan the son of Ozia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And over the husbandmen who tilled the ground was Esdri the son of Chelub.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And over the fields was Semei of Rael; and over the treasures of wine in the fields was Zabdi the son of Sephni.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Kings III 10:27; Chronicles II 1:15; Kings III 4:7"> And over the oliveyards, and over the sycamores in the plain country was Ballanan the Gedorite; and over the stores of oil was Joas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Chronicles I 5:16; Esaias 65:10"> And over the oxen pasturing in Saron was Satrai the Saronite; and over the oxen in the valleys was Sophat the son of Adli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 47:6; Job 1:3"> And over the camels was Abias the Ismaelite; and over the asses was Jadias of Merathon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Chronicles I 5:10"> And over the sheep was Jaziz the Agarite. All these were superintendents of the substance of king David.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Kings II 21:21; Kings II 13:3; Chronicles I 11:11"> And Jonathan, David's uncle by the father's side, was a counsellor, a wise man: and Jeel the son of Achami was with the king's sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Kings II 15:12; Kings II 15:37; Kings II 15:32"> Achitophel was the king's counsellor: and Chusi the chief friend of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Chronicles I 11:6; Kings III 1:7; Chronicles I 27:5"> And after this Achitophel Jodae the son of Banæas came next, and Abiathar: and Joab was the king's commander-in-chief.</span>
</div>
</div>
