<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 7:1–17; Psalms 86:1–17; Psalms 90:1–17"> <span className="desc">A Prayer of the Prophet Ambacum, with a Song.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 85:6; Psalms 90:13–17; Osee 6:2–3"> O Lord, I have heard thy report, and was afraid: I considered thy works, and was amazed: thou shalt be known between the two living creatures, thou shalt be acknowledged when the years draw nigh; thou shalt be manifested when the time is come; when my soul is troubled, thou wilt in wrath remember mercy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:2; Psalms 4:4; Obdias 1:9"> God shall come from Thæman, and the Holy One from the dark shady mount Pharan. Pause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esaias 60:19–20; Matthew 17:2; Job 26:14"> His excellence covered the heavens, and the earth was full of his praise. And his brightness shall be as light; there were horns in his hands, and he caused a mighty love of his strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 12:29–30; Numbers 16:46–49; Psalms 78:50–51"> Before his face shall go a report, and it shall go forth into the plains,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 49:26; Deuteronomy 33:15; Naum 1:5"> the earth stood at his feet and trembled: he beheld, and the nations melted away: the mountains were violently burst through, the everlasting hills melted at his everlasting going forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 15:14–16; Genesis 10:6–7; Genesis 25:1–4"> Because of troubles I looked upon the tents of the Ethiopians: the tabernacles also of the land of Madiam shall be dismayed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 68:17; Deuteronomy 33:26–27; Exodus 14:21–22"> Wast thou angry, O Lord, with the rivers? or was thy wrath against the rivers, or thine anger against the sea? for thou wilt mount on thine horses, and thy chariots are salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 7:12–13; Psalms 105:41; Psalms 78:15–16"> Surely thou didst bend thy bow at sceptres, saith the Lord. Pause. The land of rivers shall be torn asunder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 4:24; Joshua 4:23–24; Psalms 93:3"> The nations shall see thee and be in pain, as thou dost divide the moving waters: the deep uttered her voice, and raised her form on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 144:5–6; Psalms 18:12–14; Joshua 10:11–13"> The sun was exalted, and the moon stood still in her course: thy darts shall go forth at the light, at the brightness of the gleaming of thine arms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 51:33; Michæas 4:12–13; Numbers 21:23–35"> Thou wilt bring low the land with threatening, and in wrath thou wilt break down the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 110:6; Psalms 105:15; Psalms 68:19–23"> Thou wentest forth for the salvation of thy people, to save thine anointed: thou shalt bring death on the heads of transgressors; thou hast brought bands upon their neck. Pause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Zacharias 9:14; Daniel 11:40; Psalms 118:10–12"> Thou didst cut asunder the heads of princes with amazement, they shall tremble in it; they shall burst their bridles, they shall be as a poor man devouring in secret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 77:19; Ambacum 3:8; Exodus 15:8"> And thou dost cause thine horses to enter the sea, disturbing much water.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremias 23:9; Ambacum 1:5–11; Ambacum 3:2"> I watched, and my belly trembled at the sound of the prayer of my lips, and trembling entered into my bones, and my frame was troubled within me; I will rest in the day of affliction, from going up to the people of my sojourning.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremias 5:17; Joel 1:16–18; Deuteronomy 28:15–18"> For though the fig-tree shall bear no fruit, and there shall be no produce on the vines; the labour of the olive shall fail, and the fields shall produce no food: the sheep have failed from the pasture, and there are no oxen at the cribs;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 13:15; Philippians 4:4; Michæas 7:7"> yet I will exult in the Lord, I will joy in God my Saviour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings II 22:34; Psalms 18:33; Philippians 4:13"> The Lord God is my strength, and he will perfectly strengthen my feet; he mounts me upon high places, that I may conquer by his song.</span>
</div>
</div>
