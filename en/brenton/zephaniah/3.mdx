<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremias 6:6; Esaias 30:12; Esaias 59:13"> Alas the glorious and ransomed city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 5:3; Jeremias 22:21; Jeremias 7:23–28"> The dove hearkened not to the voice; she received not correction; she trusted not in the Lord, and she drew not near to her God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ambacum 1:8; Jezekiel 22:25–27; Jeremias 5:6"> Her princes within her were as roaring lions, her judges as the wolves of Arabia; they remained not till the morrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jezekiel 22:26; Osee 9:7; Malachias 2:8"> Her prophets are light and scornful men: her priests profane the holy things, and sinfully transgress the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Psalms 145:17; Lamentations 3:23"> But the just Lord is in the midst of her, and he will never do an unjust thing: morning by morning he will bring out his judgment to the light, and it is not hidden, and he knows not injustice by extortion, nor injustice in strife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:6; Sophonias 2:5; 1 Corinthians 10:11"> I have brought down the proud with destruction; their corners are destroyed: I will make their ways completely waste, so that none shall go through: their cities are come to an end, by reason of no man living or dwelling in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Osee 9:9; Jeremias 36:3; Michæas 2:1–2"> I said, But do ye fear me, and receive instruction, and ye shall not be cut off from the face of the land for all the vengeance I have brought upon her: prepare thou, rise early: all their produce is spoilt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joel 3:2; Sophonias 1:18; Michæas 7:7"> Therefore wait upon me, saith the Lord, until the day when I rise up for a witness: because my judgment shall be on the gatherings of the nations, to draw to me kings, to pour out upon them all my fierce anger: for the whole earth shall be consumed with the fire of my jealousy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 11:1; Psalms 22:27; Esaias 19:18"> For then will I turn to the peoples a tongue for her generation, that all may call on the name of the Lord, to serve him under one yoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esaias 11:11; Psalms 68:31; Esaias 18:1"> From the boundaries of the rivers of Ethiopia will I receive my dispersed ones; they shall offer sacrifices to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esaias 54:4; Joel 2:26–27; Esaias 11:9"> In that day thou shalt not be ashamed of all thy practices, wherein thou hast transgressed against me: for then will I take away from thee thy disdainful pride, and thou shalt no more magnify thyself upon my holy mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esaias 14:32; Naum 1:7; Zacharias 13:8–9"> And I will leave in thee a meek and lowly people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 14:5; Sophonias 2:7; Michæas 4:7"> and the remnant of Israel shall fear the name of the Lord, and shall do no iniquity, neither shall they speak vanity; neither shall a deceitful tongue be found in their mouth: for they shall feed, and lie down, and there shall be none to terrify them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esaias 12:6; Zacharias 2:10–11; Michæas 4:8"> Rejoice, O daughter of Sion; cry aloud, O daughter of Jerusalem; rejoice and delight thyself with all thine heart, O daughter of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esaias 51:22; Sophonias 3:5; Esaias 33:22"> The Lord has taken away thine iniquities, he has ransomed thee from the hand of thine enemies: the Lord, the King of Israel, is in the midst of thee: thou shalt not see evil any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 12:12; Esaias 35:3–4; Esaias 41:10"> At that time the Lord shall say to Jerusalem, Be of good courage, Sion; let not thine hands be slack.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 149:4; Psalms 147:11; Jeremias 32:41"> The Lord thy God is in thee; the Mighty One shall save thee: he shall bring joy upon thee, and shall refresh thee with his love; and he shall rejoice over thee with delight as in a day of feasting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 42:2–4; Jezekiel 36:24; Lamentations 2:6–7"> And I will gather thine afflicted ones. Alas! who has taken up a reproach against her?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esaias 49:25–26; Esaias 60:14; Jeremias 30:16"> Behold, I will work in thee for thy sake at that time, saith the Lord: and I will save her that was oppressed, and receive her that was rejected; and I will make them a praise, and honoured in all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremias 29:14; Malachias 3:12; Esaias 56:5"> And their enemies shall be ashamed at that time, when I shall deal well with you, and at the time when I shall receive you: for I will make you honoured and a praise among all the nations of the earth, when I turn back your captivity before you, saith the Lord.</span>
