<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 1:14; Chronicles II 20:4; Esther 4:16"> Be ye gathered and closely joined together, O unchastened nation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esaias 17:13; Osee 13:3; Naum 1:6"> before ye become as the flower that passes away, before the anger of the Lord come upon you, before the day of the wrath of the Lord come upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 5:14–15; Esaias 26:20–21; Psalms 76:9"> Seek ye the Lord, all ye meek of the earth; do judgment, and seek justice, and answer accordingly; that ye may be hid in the day of the wrath of the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zacharias 9:5–7; Amos 1:6–8; Jeremias 47:1–7"> For Gaza shall be utterly spoiled, and Ascalon shall be destroyed; and Azotus shall be cast forth at noon-day, and Accaron shall be rooted up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jezekiel 25:16; Jeremias 47:7; Joshua 13:3"> Woe to them that dwell on the border of the sea, neighbours of the Cretans! the word of the Lord is against you, O Chanaan, land of the Philistines, and I will destroy you out of your dwelling-place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esaias 17:2; Jezekiel 25:5; Esaias 5:17"> And Crete shall be a pasture of flocks, and a fold of sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 4:31; Jezekiel 39:25; Sophonias 3:20"> And the sea coast shall be for the remnant of the house of Juda; they shall pasture upon them in the houses of Ascalon; they shall rest in the evening because of the children of Juda; for the Lord their God has visited them, and he will turn away their captivity.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 1:13; Psalms 83:4–7; Jeremias 48:27–29"> I have heard the revilings of Moab, and the insults of the children of Ammon, wherewith they have reviled my people, and magnified themselves against my coasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esaias 15:1–9; Esaias 11:14; Deuteronomy 29:23"> Therefore, as I live, saith the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, Moab shall be as Sodoma, and the children of Ammon as Gomorrha; and Damascus shall be left as a heap of the threshing-floor, and desolate for ever: and the remnant of my people shall plunder them, and the remnant of my nation shall inherit them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esaias 16:6; Jeremias 48:29; Sophonias 2:8"> This is their punishment in return for their haughtiness, because they have reproached and magnified themselves against the Lord Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Michæas 4:1–3; Zacharias 13:2; Psalms 138:4"> The Lord shall appear against them, and shall utterly destroy all the gods of the nations of the earth; and they shall worship him every one from his place, even all the islands of the nations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jezekiel 30:4–9; Esaias 43:3; Jeremias 51:20–23"> Ye Ethiopians also are the slain of my sword.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Naum 3:7; Naum 1:1; Esaias 10:12"> And he shall stretch forth his hand against the north and destroy the Assyrian, and make Nineve a dry wilderness, even as a desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esaias 13:19–22; Esaias 34:11–17; Amos 9:1"> And flocks, and all the wild beasts of the land, and chameleons shall feed in the midst thereof: and hedgehogs shall lodge in the ceilings thereof; and wild beasts shall cry in the breaches thereof, and ravens in her porches, whereas her loftiness was as a cedar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jezekiel 28:2; Esaias 22:2; Esaias 47:7–8"> This is the scornful city that dwells securely, that says in her heart, I am, and there is no longer any to be after me: how is she become desolate, a habitation of wild beasts! every one that passes through her shall hiss, and shake his hands.</span>
</div>
</div>
