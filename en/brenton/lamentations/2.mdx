<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles I 28:2; Psalms 132:7; Matthew 11:23"> Aleph. How has the Lord darkened in his wrath the daughter of Sion! he has cast down the glory of Israel from heaven to earth, and has not remembered his footstool.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Lamentations 2:17; Psalms 89:39–40; Lamentations 3:43"> Beth. In the day of his wrath the Lord has overwhelmed her as in the sea, and not spared: he has brought down in his fury all the beautiful things of Jacob; he has brought down to the ground the strong-holds of the daughter of Juda: he has profaned her king and her princes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 74:11; Psalms 75:10; Psalms 75:5"> Gimel. He has broken in his fierce anger all the horn of Israel: he has turned back his right hand from the face of the enemy, and has kindled a flame in Jacob as fire, and it has devoured all things round about.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremias 7:20; Jeremias 21:5; Lamentations 3:12–13"> Daleth. He has bent his bow as an opposing enemy: he has strengthened his right hand as an adversary, and has destroyed all the desirable things of my eyes in the tabernacle of the daughter of Sion: he has poured forth his anger as fire.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Lamentations 2:2; Jeremias 30:14; Jeremias 52:13"> He. The Lord is become as an enemy: he has overwhelmed Israel as in the sea, he has overwhelmed her palaces: he has destroyed his strong-holds, and has multiplied the afflicted and humbled ones to the daughter of Juda.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Lamentations 1:4; Sophonias 3:18; Lamentations 4:16"> Vau. And he has scattered his tabernacle as a vine, he has marred his feast: the Lord has forgotten the feast and the sabbath which he appointed in Sion, and in the fury of his wrath has vexed the king, and priest, and prince.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jezekiel 24:21; Amos 2:5; Psalms 74:3–8"> Zain. The Lord has rejected his altar, he has cast off his sanctuary, he has broken by the hand of the enemy the wall of her palaces; they have uttered their voice in the house of the Lord as on a feast day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings IV 21:13; Esaias 34:11; Esaias 3:26"> Heth. And he has turned to destroy the wall of the daughter of Zion: he has stretched out the measuring line, he has not turned back his hand from afflicting her: therefore the bulwark mourned, and the wall was weakened with it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jezekiel 7:26; Osee 3:4; Chronicles II 15:3"> Teth. Her gates are sunk into the ground: he has destroyed and broken to pieces her bars, and her king and her prince among the Gentiles: there is no law, nay, her prophets have seen no vision from the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esaias 3:26; Esaias 15:3; Job 2:12–13"> Jod. The elders of the daughter of Sion have sat upon the ground, they have kept silence: they have cast up dust upon their heads; they have girded themselves with sackcloths: they have brought down to the ground the chief virgins in Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Lamentations 1:20; Job 16:13; Lamentations 1:16"> Chaph. Mine eyes have failed with tears, my heart is troubled, my glory is cast down to the ground, for the destruction of the daughter of my people; while the infant and suckling swoon in the streets of the city.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esaias 53:12; Jezekiel 30:24"> Lamed. They said to their mothers, Where is corn and wine? while they fainted like wounded men in the streets of the city, while their souls were poured out into their mother's bosom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Lamentations 1:12; Jeremias 8:22; Kings II 5:20"> Mem. What shall I testify to thee, or what shall I compare to thee, O daughter of Jerusalem? who shall save and comfort thee, O virgin daughter of Sion? for the cup of thy destruction is enlarged: who shall heal thee?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jezekiel 22:28; Esaias 58:1; Jeremias 5:31"> Nun. Thy prophets have seen for thee vanities and folly: and they have not discovered thine iniquity, to turn back thy captivity; but they have seen for thee vain burdens, and worthless visions.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremias 18:16; Psalms 48:2; Psalms 50:2"> Samech. All that go by the way have clapped their hands at thee; they have hissed and shaken their head at the daughter of Jerusalem. Is this the city, they say, the crown of joy of all the earth?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 35:21; Lamentations 3:46; Job 16:9–10"> Ain. All thine enemies have opened their mouth against thee: they have hissed and gnashed their teeth, and said, We have swallowed her up: moreover this is the day which we looked for; we have found it, we have seen it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 89:42; Jezekiel 5:11; Deuteronomy 31:16–17"> Phe. The Lord has done that which he purposed; he has accomplished his word, even the things which he commanded from the ancient days: he has thrown down, and has not spared: and he has caused the enemy to rejoice over thee, he has exalted the horn of him that afflicted thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 119:145; Jeremias 9:1; Lamentations 2:8"> Tsade. Their heart cried to the Lord, Ye walls of Sion, pour down tears like torrents day and night: give thyself no rest; let not the apple of thine eyes cease.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 62:8; Psalms 119:147–148; Esaias 26:9"> Koph. Arise, rejoice in the night at the beginning of thy watch: pour out thy heart as water before the face of the Lord: lift up thy hands to him for the life of thine infants, who faint for hunger at the top of all the streets.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremias 19:9; Lamentations 4:10; Psalms 78:64"> Rhechs. Behold, O Lord, and see for whom thou hast gathered thus. Shall the women eat the fruit of their womb? the cook has made a gathering: shall the infants sucking at the breasts be slain? wilt thou slay the priest and prophet in the sanctuary of the Lord?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:17; Lamentations 3:43; Zacharias 11:6"> Chsen. The child and old man have lain down in the street: my virgins and my young men are gone into captivity: thou hast slain them with the sword and with famine; in the day of thy wrath thou hast mangled them, thou hast not spared.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremias 6:25; Psalms 31:13; Jeremias 16:2–4"> Thau. He has called my sojourners round about to a solemn day, and there was not in the day of the wrath of the Lord any one that escaped or was left; whereas I have strengthened and multiplied all mine enemies.</span>
</div>
</div>
