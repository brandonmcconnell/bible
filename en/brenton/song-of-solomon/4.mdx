<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Song of Solomon 6:7; Song of Solomon 5:16; Joshua 2:18"> Thy lips are as a thread of scarlet, and thy speech is comely: like the rind of a pomegranate is thy cheek without thy veil.</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:16; Song of Solomon 6:3; Song of Solomon 7:3"> Thy two breasts are as two twin fawns, that feed among the lilies.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Song of Solomon 5:1–2; Song of Solomon 4:12; Proverbs 5:19–20"> My sister, my spouse, thou hast ravished my heart; thou hast ravished my heart with one of thine eyes, with one chain of thy neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:2–4; Song of Solomon 7:6; Galatians 5:22"> How beautiful are thy breasts, my sister, my spouse! how much more beautiful are thy breasts than wine, and the smell of thy garments than all spices!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Song of Solomon 5:1; Song of Solomon 7:9; Genesis 27:27"> Thy lips drop honeycomb, my spouse: honey and milk are under thy tongue; and the smell of thy garments is as the smell of Libanus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 5:15–18; Revelation 21:27; 1 Corinthians 6:13"> My sister, my spouse is a garden enclosed; a garden enclosed, a fountain sealed.</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 30:23; Song of Solomon 4:6; Song of Solomon 6:2"> spikenard and saffron, calamus and cinnamon; with all woods of Libanus, myrrh, aloes, with all chief spices:</span>
