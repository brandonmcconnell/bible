<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:9; Ezra and Nehemiah 2:3; Acts 4:6–10"> At that time, saith the Lord, I will be a God to the family of Israel, and they shall be to me a people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 39:18; Jeremias 34:17; Jeremias 45:5"> Thus saith the Lord, I found him warm in the wilderness with them that were slain with the sword: go ye and destroy not Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 21:10; Jeremias 32:3–5"> The Lord appeared to him from afar, saying, I have loved thee with an everlasting love: therefore have I drawn thee in compassion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremias 26:11; Acts 16:20; Nehemiah 6:9"> For I will build thee, and thou shalt be built, O virgin of Israel: thou shalt yet take thy timbrel, and go forth with the party of them that make merry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 19:12–16; Kings II 3:39; Kings II 19:22"> For ye have planted vineyards on the mountains of Samaria: plant ye, and praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremias 37:21; Jeremias 38:22; Jeremias 37:16"> For it is a day when those that plead on the mountains of Ephraim shall call, saying, Arise ye, and go up to Sion to the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:19; Amos 5:10; Jeremias 29:2"> For thus saith the Lord to Jacob; Rejoice ye, and exult over the head of the nations: make proclamation, and praise ye: say, The Lord has delivered his people, the remnant of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Behold, I bring them from the north, and will gather them from the end of the earth to the feast of the passover: and the people shall beget a great multitude, and they shall return hither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremias 37:21–6; Esther 7:4–6; Proverbs 31:8–9"> They went forth with weeping, and I will bring them back with consolation, causing them to lodge by the channels of waters in a straight way, and they shall not err in it: for I am become a father to Israel, and Ephraim is my first-born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 21:1; Esther 8:7; Esther 5:2"> Hear the words of the Lord, ye nations, and proclaim them to the islands afar off; say, He that scattered Israel will also gather him, and keep him as one that feeds his flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> For the Lord has ransomed Jacob, he has rescued him out of the hand of them that were stronger than he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 12:10; Ephesians 4:32; Romans 12:15"> And they shall come, and shall rejoice in the mount of Sion, and shall come to the good things of the Lord, even to a land of corn, and wine, and fruits, and cattle, and sheep: and their soul shall be as a fruitful tree; and they shall hunger no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremias 37:21; Acts 28:16; Acts 23:35"> Then shall the virgins rejoice in the assembly of youth, and the old men shall rejoice; and I will turn their mourning into joy, and will make them merry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremias 37:17; Kings III 22:16; Jeremias 21:1–2"> I will expand and cheer with wine the soul of the priests the sons of Levi, and my people shall be satisfied with my good things: thus saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 22:67–68"> A voice was heard in Rama, of lamentation, and of weeping, and wailing; Rachel would not cease weeping for her children, because they are not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremias 37:17; Jeremias 38:1–6; Esaias 57:16"> Thus saith the Lord; Let thy voice cease from weeping, and thine eyes from thy tears: for there is a reward for thy works; and they shall return from the land of thine enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremias 27:12; Jeremias 27:17; Jeremias 38:2"> There shall be an abiding home for thy children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremias 38:3; Jeremias 24:8–10; Kings IV 25:4–10"> I have heard the sound of Ephraim lamenting, and saying, Thou hast chastened me, and I was chastened; I as a calf was not willingly taught: turn thou me, and I shall turn; for thou art the Lord my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 12:42; Jeremias 38:22; John 19:12–13"> For after my captivity I repented; and after I knew, I groaned for the day of shame, and shewed thee that I bore reproach from my youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 4:27; Esaias 55:3; Jeremias 26:13"> Ephraim is a beloved son, a pleasing child to me: for because my words are in him, I will surely remember him: therefore I made haste to help him; I will surely have mercy upon him, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jezekiel 2:7; Jezekiel 2:4–5; Jeremias 26:15"> Prepare thyself, O Sion; execute vengeance; look to thy ways: return, O virgin of Israel, by the way by which thou wentest, return mourning to thy cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremias 43:6; Lamentations 1:2; Lamentations 5:11"> How long, O disgraced daughter, wilt thou turn away? for the Lord has created safety for a new plantation: men shall go about in safety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremias 41:10; Jeremias 39:6; Jeremias 38:18"> For thus saith the Lord; They shall yet speak this word in the land of Juda, and in the cities thereof, when I shall turn his captivity; blessed be the Lord on his righteous holy mountain!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And there shall be dwellers in the cities of Juda, and in all his land, together with the husbandman, and the shepherd shall go forth with the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremias 38:27; Jeremias 38:4–6"> For I have saturated every thirsting soul, and filled every hungry soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremias 37:20; Jeremias 37:15; Esther 4:8"> Therefore I awaked, and beheld; and my sleep was sweet to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Kings I 10:15–16; Kings IV 6:19; Acts 23:6"> Therefore, behold, the days come, saith the Lord, when I will sow the house of Israel and the house of Juda with the seed of man, and the seed of beast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremias 37:21; Jeremias 39:14; Psalms 23:4"> And it shall come to pass, that as I watched over them, to pull down, and to afflict, so will I watch over them, to build, and to plant, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> In those days they shall certainly not say, The fathers ate a sour grape, and the children's teeth were set on edge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> But every one shall die in his own sin; and the teeth of him that eats the sour grape shall be set on edge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Behold, the days come, saith the Lord, when I will make a new covenant with the house of Israel, and with the house of Juda:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> not according to the covenant which I made with their fathers in the day when I took hold of their hand to bring them out of the land of Egypt; for they abode not in my covenant, and I disregarded them, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> For this is my covenant which I will make with the house of Israel; after those days, saith the Lord, I will surely put my laws into their mind, and write them on their hearts; and I will be to them a God, and they shall be to me a people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And they shall not at all teach every one his fellow citizen, and every one his brother, saying, Know the Lord: for all shall know me, from the least of them to the greatest of them: for I will be merciful to their iniquities, and their sins I will remember no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Though the sky should be raised to a greater height, saith the Lord, and though the ground of the earth should be sunk lower beneath, yet I will not cast off the family of Israel, saith the Lord, for all that they have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Thus saith the Lord, who gives the sun for a light by day, the moon and the stars for a light by night, and makes a roaring in the sea, so that the waves thereof roar; the Lord Almighty is his name:</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> if these ordinances cease from before me, saith the Lord, then shall the family of Israel cease to be a nation before me for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Behold, the days come, saith the Lord, when the city shall be built to the Lord from the tower of Anameel to the gate of the corner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> And the measurement of it shall proceed in front of them as far as the hills of Gareb, and it shall be compassed with a circular wall of choice stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And all the Asaremoth even to Nachal Kedron, as far as the corner of the horse-gate eastward, shall be holiness to the Lord; and it shall not fail any more, and shall not be destroyed for ever.</span>
