<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jezekiel 24:1–2; Jeremias 52:4–7; Kings IV 25:1–12"> The word that came from the Lord to Jeremias in the tenth year of king Sedekias, this is the eighteenth year of king Nabuchodonosor king of Babylon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 52:6–7; Michæas 2:12–13; Kings IV 25:3–4"> And the host of the king of Babylon had made a rampart against Jerusalem: and Jeremias was kept in the court of the prison, which is in the king's house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 21:4; Jeremias 38:17; Jeremias 39:13"> in which king Sedekias had shut him up, saying, Wherefore dost thou prophesy, saying, Thus saith the Lord, Behold, I will give this city into the hands of the king of Babylon, and he shall take it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 2:14; Chronicles II 32:5; Kings IV 25:4–7"> and Sedekias shall by no means be delivered out of the hand of the Chaldeans, for he shall certainly be given up into the hands of the king of Babylon, and his mouth shall speak to his mouth, and his eyes shall look upon his eyes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings IV 23:33; Joshua 5:10; Jeremias 52:26–27"> and Sedekias shall go into Babylon, and dwell there?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremias 52:10; Jeremias 34:19–21; Jeremias 24:8–10"> And the Word of the Lord came to Jeremias, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jezekiel 12:13; Kings IV 25:7; Jeremias 52:11"> Behold, Anameel the son of Salom thy father's brother is coming to thee, saying, Buy thee my field that is in Anathoth: for thou hast the right to take it as a purchase.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:3; Jeremias 38:18; Jeremias 21:10"> So Anameel the son of Salom my father's brother came to me into the court of the prison, and said, Buy thee my field that is in the land of Benjamin, in Anathoth: for thou hast a right to buy it, and thou art the elder. So I knew that it was the word of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremias 40:1; Genesis 37:36; Jeremias 10:18"> And I bought the field of Anameel the son of my father's brother, and I weighed him seventeen shekels of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings IV 25:12; Jeremias 40:7; Jeremias 52:16"> And I wrote it in a book, and sealed it, and took the testimony of witnesses, and weighed the money in the balance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 24:23; Jeremias 15:21; Job 5:19"> And I took the book of the purchase that was sealed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Jeremias 40:4; Proverbs 21:1"> and I gave it to Baruch son of Nerias, son of Maasæas, in the sight of Anameel my father's brother's son, and in the sight of the men that stood by and wrote in the book of the purchase, and in the sight of the Jews that were in the court of the prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremias 39:3; Jeremias 39:9"> And I charged Baruch in their presence, saying, Thus saith the Lord Almighty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremias 38:28; Kings IV 22:12; Jeremias 26:24"> Take this book of the purchase, and the book that has been read; and thou shalt put it into an earthen vessel, that it may remain many days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:9; Jeremias 32:1–2; Jeremias 36:1–5"> For thus saith the Lord; There shall yet be bought fields and houses and vineyards in this land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 9:12; Zacharias 1:6; Joshua 23:14–15"> And I prayed to the Lord after I had given the book of the purchase to Baruch the son of Nerias, saying,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 41:1–2; Psalms 91:14–15; Psalms 50:15"> O ever living Lord! thou hast made the heaven and the earth by thy great power, and with thy high and lofty arm: nothing can be hidden from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremias 38:2; Psalms 34:22; Jeremias 21:9"> Granting mercy to thousands, and recompensing the sins of the fathers into the bosoms of their children after them: the great, the strong God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> the Lord of great counsel, and mighty in deeds, the great Almighty God, and Lord of great name: thine eyes are upon the ways of the children of men, to give to every one according to his way:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> who hast wrought signs and wonders in the land of Egypt even to this day, and in Israel, and among the inhabitants of the earth; and thou didst make for thyself a name, as at this day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and thou didst bring out thy people Israel out of the land of Egypt with signs, and with wonders, with a mighty hand, and with a high arm, and with great sights;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> and thou gavest them this land, which thou didst swear to give to their fathers, a land flowing with milk and honey;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and they went in, and took it; but they hearkened not to thy voice, and walked not in thine ordinances; they did none of the things which thou didst command them, and they caused all these calamities to happen to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Behold, a multitude is come against the city to take it; and the city is given into the hands of the Chaldeans that fight against it, by the power of the sword, and the famine: as thou hast spoken, so has it happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And thou sayest to me, Buy thee the field for money; and I wrote a book, and sealed it, and took the testimony of witnesses: and the city is given into the hands of the Chaldeans.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> I am the Lord, the God of all flesh: shall anything be hidden from me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Therefore thus saith the Lord God of Israel; This city shall certainly be delivered into the hands of the king of Babylon, and he shall take it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> and the Chaldeans shall come to war against this city, and they shall burn this city with fire, and shall burn down the houses wherein they burnt incense on the roofs thereof to Baal, and poured drink-offerings to other gods, to provoke me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> For the children of Israel and the children of Juda alone did evil in my sight from their youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> For this city was obnoxious to my anger and my wrath, from the day that they built it even to this day; that I should remove it from my presence,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> because of all the wickedness of the children of Israel and Juda, which they wrought to provoke me, they and their kings, and their princes, and their priests, and their prophets, the men of Juda, and the dwellers in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And they turned the back to me, and not the face: whereas I taught them early in the morning, but they hearkened no more to receive instruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And they set their pollutions in the house, on which my name was called, by their uncleannesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And they built to Baal the altars that are in the valley of the son of Ennom, to offer their sons and their daughters to king Moloch; which things I commanded them not, neither came it into my mind that they should do this abomination, to cause Juda to sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And now thus has the Lord God of Israel said concerning this city, of which thou sayest, It shall be delivered into the hands of the king of Babylon by the sword, and by famine, and banishment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Behold, I will gather them out of every land, where I have scattered them in my anger, and my wrath, and great fury; and I will bring them back into this place, and will cause them to dwell safely:</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> and they shall be to me a people, and I will be to them a God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> And I will give them another way, and another heart, to fear me continually, and that for good to them and their children after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And I will make with them an everlasting covenant, which I will by no means turn away from them, and I will put my fear into their heart, that they may not depart from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> And I will visit them to do them good, and I will plant them in this land in faithfulness, and with all my heart, and with all my soul.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> For thus saith the Lord; As I have brought upon this people all these great evils, so will I bring upon them all the good things which I pronounced upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> And there shall yet be fields bought in the land, of which thou sayest, It shall be destitute of man and beast; and they are delivered into the hands of the Chaldeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And they shall buy fields for money, and thou shalt write a book, and seal it, and shalt take the testimony of witnesses in the land of Benjamin, and round about Jerusalem, and in the cities of Juda, and in the cities of the mountain, and in the cities of the plain, and in the cities of the south: for I will turn their captivity.</span>
</div>
</div>
