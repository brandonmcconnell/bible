<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles I 6:60; Jeremias 11:21; Chronicles II 36:21"> The word of God which came to Jeremias the son of Chelcias, of the priests, who dwelt in Anathoth in the land of Benjamin:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings III 13:20; Jonas 1:1; Jeremias 1:11"> accordingly as the word of God came to him in the days of Josias son of Amos king of Juda, in the thirteenth year of his reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 39:2; Chronicles II 36:5–8; Kings IV 23:34"> And it was in the days of Joakim son of Josias king of Juda, until the eleventh year of Sedekias king of Juda, even until the captivity of Jerusalem in the fifth month.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jezekiel 3:16; Jezekiel 1:3; Jeremias 1:2"> And the word of the Lord came to him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 139:16; Esaias 49:1; Galatians 1:15–16"> Before I formed thee in the belly, I knew thee; and before thou camest forth from the womb, I sanctified thee; I appointed thee a prophet to the nations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 6:12; Exodus 4:10–16; Kings III 3:7–9"> And I said, O Lord, thou that art supreme Lord, behold, I know not how to speak, for I am a child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 16:15–16; Jezekiel 3:27; Numbers 22:20"> And the Lord said to me, Say not, I am a child: for thou shalt go to all to whomsoever I shall send thee, and according to all the words that I shall command thee, thou shalt speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Joshua 1:9; Esaias 51:7"> Be not afraid before them: for I am with thee to deliver thee, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Exodus 4:11–12; Luke 12:12"> And the Lord stretched forth his hand to me, and touched my mouth: and the Lord said to me, Behold, I have put my words into thy mouth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 31:28; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Esaias 44:26–28"> Behold, I have appointed thee this day over nations and over kingdoms, to root out, and to pull down, and to destroy, and to rebuild, and to plant.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Amos 7:8; Amos 8:2; Zacharias 5:2"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying, What seest thou? And I said, A rod of an almond tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jezekiel 12:28; Jezekiel 12:25; Jezekiel 12:22–23"> And the Lord said to me, Thou hast well seen: for I have watched over my words to perform them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jezekiel 11:3; Jezekiel 11:7; Zacharias 4:2"> And the word of the Lord came to me a second time, saying, What seest thou? And I said, A caldron on the fire; and the face of it is toward the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esaias 41:25; Jeremias 4:6; Jeremias 10:22"> And the Lord said to me, From the north shall flame forth evils upon all the inhabitants of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremias 43:10; Jeremias 25:9; Jeremias 9:11"> For, behold, I call together all the kingdoms of the earth from the north, saith the Lord; and they shall come, and shall set each one his throne at the entrance of the gates of Jerusalem, and against all the walls round about her, and against all the cities of Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremias 7:9; Jeremias 19:4; Acts 7:41"> And I will speak to them in judgment, concerning all their iniquity, forasmuch as they have forsaken me, and sacrificed to strange gods, and worshipped the works of their own hands.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremias 1:7–8; Kings III 18:46; 1 Peter 1:13"> And do thou gird up thy loins, and stand up, and speak all the words that I shall command thee: be not afraid of their face, neither be thou alarmed before them; for I am with thee to deliver thee, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Esaias 50:7; Jezekiel 3:8–9; Jeremias 15:20"> Behold, I have made thee this day as a strong city, and as a brazen wall, strong against all the kings of Juda, and the princes thereof, and the people of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Jeremias 1:8; Jeremias 20:11"> And they shall fight against thee; but they shall by no means prevail against thee; because I am with thee, to deliver thee, saith the Lord.</span>
</div>
</div>
