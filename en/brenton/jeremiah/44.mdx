<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esaias 6:11; Jeremias 9:11; Jeremias 34:22"> And he and his servants and the people of the land hearkened not to the words of the Lord, which he spoke by Jeremias.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:26; Deuteronomy 13:6; Deuteronomy 32:17"> And king Sedekias sent Joachal son of Selemias and Sophonias the priest son of Maasæas to Jeremias, saying, Pray now for us to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremias 26:5; Jeremias 7:25; Jeremias 7:13"> Now Jeremias came and went through the midst of the city: for they had not put him into the house of the prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 11:10; Jeremias 19:13; Chronicles II 36:16"> And the host of Pharao was come forth out of Egypt; and the Chaldeans heard the report of them, and they went up from Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremias 7:20; Jeremias 21:5; Esaias 51:17"> And the word of the Lord came to Jeremias, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ambacum 2:10; Jeremias 51:22; Jezekiel 33:11"> Thus said the Lord; Thus shalt thou say to the king of Juda who sent to thee, to seek me; Behold, the army of Pharao which is come forth to help you: they shall return to the land of Egypt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremias 42:18; Jeremias 25:6–7; Jeremias 44:3"> and the Chaldeans themselves shall turn again, and fight against this city, and take it, and burn it with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremias 7:17–18; Joshua 22:17–20; Jeremias 44:15–19"> For thus saith the Lord; Suppose not in your hearts, saying, The Chaldeans will certainly depart from us: for they shall not depart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 28:14; 1 Peter 5:6; Revelation 15:4"> And though ye should smite the whole host of the Chaldeans that fight against you, and there should be left a few wounded men, these should rise up each in his place, and burn this city with fire.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremias 21:10; Leviticus 26:17; Leviticus 20:5–6"> And it came to pass, when the host of the Chaldeans had gone up from Jerusalem for fear of the host of Pharao,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 42:15–18; Jeremias 42:22; Esaias 65:15"> that Jeremias went forth from Jerusalem to go into the land of Benjamin, to buy thence a property in the midst of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremias 11:22; Jeremias 43:11; Jeremias 44:27–28"> And he was in the gate of Benjamin, and there was there a man with whom he lodged, Saruia the son of Selemias, the son of Ananias; and he caught Jeremias, saying, Thou art fleeing to the Chaldeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 9:27; Jeremias 22:26–27; Jeremias 42:17"> And he said, It is false; I do not flee to the Chaldeans. But he hearkened not to him; and Saruia caught Jeremias, and brought him to the princes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 11:21; Jeremias 5:1–5; Genesis 19:4"> And the princes were very angry with Jeremias, and smote him, and sent him into the house of Jonathan the scribe: for they had made this a prison.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 21:14–15; Luke 19:27; Jeremias 16:15–17"> So Jeremias came into the dungeon, and into the cells, and he remained there many days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremias 7:18; Nehemiah 9:34; Jeremias 44:21"> Then Sedekias sent, and called him; and the king asked him secretly, saying, Is there a word from the Lord? and he said, There is: thou shalt be delivered into the hands of the king of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Malachias 3:13–15; Numbers 11:5–6; Job 21:14–15"> And Jeremias said to the king, Wherein have I wronged thee, or thy servants, or this people, that thou puttest me in prison?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremias 7:18; Jeremias 44:15; Kings III 21:25"> And where are your prophets who prophesied to you saying, The king of Babylon shall not come against this land?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Now therefore, my lord the king, let my supplication come before thy face: and why dost thou send me back to the house of Jonathan the scribe? and let me not on any account die there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremias 44:17; Jeremias 44:9; Jeremias 11:13"> Then the king commanded, and they cast him into the prison, and gave him a loaf a day out of the place where they bake, until the bread failed out of the city. So Jeremias continued in the court of the prison.</span>
</div>
