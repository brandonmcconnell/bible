<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 2:37–38; Chronicles I 24:14; Kings IV 25:18"> Now Paschor the son of Emmer, the priest, who also had been appointed chief of the house of the Lord, heard Jeremias prophesying these words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 37:13; Zacharias 14:10; Jeremias 1:19"> And he smote him, and cast him into the dungeon which was by the gate of the upper house that was set apart, which was by the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 20:10; Jeremias 46:5; Lamentations 2:22"> And Paschor brought Jeremias out of the dungeon: and Jeremias said to him, The Lord has not called thy name Paschor, but Exile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremias 29:21; Jeremias 25:9; Jeremias 21:4–10"> For thus saith the Lord, Behold, I will give thee up to captivity with all thy friends: and they shall fall by the sword of their enemies, and thine eyes shall see it: and I will give thee and all Juda into the hands of the king of Babylon, and they shall carry them captives, and cut them in pieces with swords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 15:13; Jezekiel 22:25; Lamentations 1:10"> And I will give all the strength of this city, and all the labours of it, and all the treasures of the king of Juda, into the hands of his enemies, and they shall bring them to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremias 14:14–15; Lamentations 2:14; Jezekiel 22:28"> And thou and all the dwellers in thine house shall go into captivity: and thou shalt die in Babylon, and there thou and all thy friends shall be buried, to whom thou hast prophesied lies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Osee 9:7; Jeremias 20:9; Lamentations 3:14"> Thou hast deceived me, O Lord, and I have been deceived: thou hast been strong, and hast prevailed: I am become a laughing-stock, I am continually mocked every day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:16; Jeremias 6:10; Jeremias 5:15–17"> For I will laugh with my bitter speech, I will call upon rebellion and misery: for the word of the Lord is become a reproach to me and a mockery all my days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 39:3; Job 32:18–20; Acts 4:20"> Then I said, I will by no means name the name of the Lord, and I will no more at all speak in his name. But it was as burning fire flaming in my bones, and I am utterly weakened on all sides, and cannot bear up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; Psalms 31:13; Esaias 29:21"> For I have heard the reproach of many gathering round, saying, Conspire ye, and let us conspire together against him, even all his friends: watch his intentions, if perhaps he shall be deceived, and we shall prevail against him, and we shall be avenged on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremias 1:19; Jeremias 1:8; Jeremias 15:20"> But the Lord was with me as a mighty man of war: therefore they persecuted me, but could not perceive anything against me; they were greatly confounded, for they perceived not their disgrace, which shall never be forgotten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 62:8; Jeremias 11:20; Psalms 54:7"> O Lord, that provest just deeds, understanding the reins and hearts, let me see thy vengeance upon them: for to thee I have revealed my cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Esaias 25:4; Psalms 35:9–11; Psalms 69:33"> Sing ye to the Lord, sing praise to him: for he has rescued the soul of the poor from the hand of evil-doers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremias 15:10; Job 3:3–16"> Cursed be the day wherein I was born: the day wherein my mother brought me forth, let it not be blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 21:5–6; Luke 1:14; Jeremias 1:5"> Cursed be the man who brought the glad tidings to my father, saying, A male child is born to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremias 18:22; Jeremias 48:3–4; Deuteronomy 29:23"> Let that man rejoice as the cities which the Lord overthrew in wrath, and repented not: let him hear crying in the morning, and loud lamentation at noon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 10:18–19; Job 3:10–11; Ecclesiastes 6:3"> because he slew me not in the womb, and my mother became not my tomb, and her womb always great with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 3:20; Lamentations 3:1; 1 Peter 4:14–16"> Why is it that I came forth of the womb to see troubles and distresses, and my days are spent in shame?</span>
