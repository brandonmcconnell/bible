<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremias 25:26–27; Jeremias 51:1–14; Esaias 13:1–3"> And it came to pass, when Jeremias ceased speaking to the people all the words of the Lord, for which the Lord had sent him to them, even all these words,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esaias 46:1; Esaias 21:9; Jeremias 51:44"> that Azarias son of Maasæas spoke, and Joanan, the son of Careë, and all the men who had spoken to Jeremias, saying, It is false: the Lord has not sent thee to us, saying, Enter not into Egypt to dwell there:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Sophonias 1:3; Jeremias 51:11; Jeremias 51:62"> but Baruch the son of Nerias sets thee against us, that thou mayest deliver us into the hands of the Chaldeans, to kill us, and that we should be carried away captives to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremias 31:31; Osee 1:11; Osee 3:5"> So Joanan, and all the leaders of the host, and all the people, refused to hearken to the voice of the Lord, to dwell in the land of Juda.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 32:40; Hebrews 8:6–10; Esaias 55:3"> And Joanan, and all the leaders of the host, took all the remnant of Juda, who had returned to dwell in the land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esaias 53:6; Matthew 9:36; Jezekiel 34:14"> the mighty men, and the women, and the children that were left, and the daughters of the king, and the souls which Nabuzardan had left with Godolias the son of Achicam, and Jeremias the prophet, and Baruch the son of Nerias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremias 14:8; Jeremias 2:3; Zacharias 11:5"> And they came into Egypt: for they hearkened not to the voice of the Lord: and they entered into Taphnas.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esaias 48:20; Jeremias 51:6; Revelation 18:4"> And the word of the Lord came to Jeremias in Taphnas, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremias 51:11; Jeremias 15:14; Kings II 1:22"> Take thee great stones, and hide them in the entrance, at the gate of the house of Pharao in Taphnas, in the sight of the men of Juda:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 25:12; Esaias 33:23; Esaias 33:4"> and thou shalt say, Thus has the Lord said; Behold, I will send, and will bring Nabuchodonosor king of Babylon, and he shall place his throne upon these stones which thou hast hidden, and he shall lift up weapons against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esaias 47:6; Jezekiel 25:15–17; Jezekiel 26:2–3"> And he shall enter in, and smite the land of Egypt, delivering some for death to death; and some for captivity to captivity; and some for the sword to the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 51:43; Jeremias 49:2; Galatians 4:26"> And he shall kindle a fire in the houses of their gods, and shall burn them, and shall carry them away captives: and shall search the land of Egypt, as a shepherd searches his garment; and he shall go forth in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremias 18:16; Jeremias 49:17; Lamentations 2:15–16"> And he shall break to pieces the pillars of Heliopolis that are in On, and shall burn their houses with fire.</span>
