<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremias 17:8"> <span className="desc">And the Word of the Lord came to Jeremias concerning the Drought.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esaias 3:26; Jeremias 8:21; Kings I 5:12"> Judea has mourned, and her gates are emptied, and are darkened upon the earth; and the cry of Jerusalem is gone up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings II 15:30; Psalms 40:14; Kings IV 18:31"> And her nobles have sent their little ones to the water: they came to the wells, and found no water: and brought back their vessels empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joel 1:19–20; Joel 1:11; Jeremias 3:3"> And the labours of the land failed, because there was no rain: the husbandmen were ashamed, they covered their heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 29:9; Job 39:1–4; Esaias 15:6"> And hinds calved in the field, and forsook it, because there was no grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremias 2:24; Job 39:5–6; Joel 1:18"> The wild asses stood by the forests, and snuffed up the wind; their eyes failed, because there was no grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 25:11; Osee 5:5; Jeremias 14:20–21"> Our sins have risen up against us: O Lord, do thou for us for thine own sake; for our sins are many before thee; for we have sinned against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremias 17:13; Jeremias 50:7; Esaias 43:3"> O Lord, thou art the hope of Israel, and deliverest us in time of troubles; why art thou become as a sojourner upon the land, or as one born in the land, yet turning aside for a resting-place?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esaias 63:19; Esaias 59:1; Jeremias 15:16"> Wilt thou be as a man asleep, or as a strong man that cannot save? yet thou art among us, O Lord, and thy name is called upon us; forget us not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Osee 9:9; Osee 8:13; Psalms 119:101"> Thus saith the Lord to this people, They have loved to wander, and they have not spared, therefore God has not prospered them; now will he remember their iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremias 7:16; Jeremias 11:14; Exodus 32:32–34"> And the Lord said to me, Pray not for this people for their good:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 11:11; Jezekiel 8:18; Proverbs 1:28"> for though they fast, I will not hear their supplication; and though they offer whole-burnt-offerings and sacrifices, I will take no pleasure in them: for I will consume them with sword, and with famine, and with pestilence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremias 6:14; Jeremias 4:10; 2 Peter 2:1"> And I said, O ever living Lord! behold, their prophets prophesy, and say, Ye shall not see a sword, nor shall famine be among you; for I will give truth and peace on the land, and in this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jezekiel 13:6–7; Jezekiel 12:24; Jeremias 29:8–9"> Then the Lord said to me, The prophets prophesy lies in my name: I sent them not, and I commanded them not, and I spoke not to them: for they prophesy to you false visions, and divinations, and auguries, and devices of their own heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremias 5:12–13; Jezekiel 14:10; Jeremias 23:14–15"> Therefore thus saith the Lord concerning the prophets that prophesy lies in my name, and I sent them not, who say, Sword and famine shall not be upon this land; they shall die by a grievous death, and the prophets shall be consumed by famine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremias 7:33; Proverbs 1:31; Jeremias 15:2–3"> And the people to whom they prophesy, they also shall be cast out in the streets of Jerusalem, because of the sword and famine; and there shall be none to bury them: their wives also, and their sons, and their daughters shall die thus; and I will pour out their wickedness upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremias 8:21; Jeremias 9:1; Jeremias 13:17"> And thou shalt speak this word to them; Let your eyes shed tears day and night, and let them not cease: for the daughter of my people has been sorely bruised, and her plague is very grievous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jezekiel 7:15; Lamentations 1:20; Jeremias 8:10"> If I go forth into the plain, then behold the slain by the sword! and if I enter into the city, then behold the distress of famine! for priest and prophet have gone to a land which they knew not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Lamentations 5:22; Jeremias 8:15; Jeremias 6:30"> Hast thou utterly rejected Juda? and has thy soul departed from Sion? wherefore hast thou smitten us, and there is no healing for us? we waited for peace, but there was no prosperity; for a time of healing, and behold trouble!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremias 3:25; Psalms 32:5; 1 John 1:7–9"> We know, O Lord, our sins, and the iniquities of our fathers: for we have sinned before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremias 14:7; Psalms 106:45; Jeremias 3:17"> Refrain for thy name's sake, destroy not the throne of thy glory: remember, break not thy covenant with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joel 2:23; Jeremias 5:24; Psalms 135:7"> Is there any one among the idols of the Gentiles that can give rain? and will the sky yield his fulness at their bidding? Art not thou he? we will even wait on thee, O Lord: for thou hast made all these things.</span>
