<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremias 25:1; Chronicles II 36:5; Jeremias 45:1"> And these are the words of the book which Jeremias sent from Jerusalem to the elders of the captivity, and to the priests, and to the false prophets, even an epistle to Babylon for the captivity, and to all the people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 30:2; Jeremias 25:3; Jeremias 1:10"> (after the departure of Jechonias the king and the queen, and the eunuchs, and every freeman, and bondman, and artificer, out of Jerusalem;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 18:8; Acts 3:19; Jeremias 26:3"> by the hand of Eleasan son of Saphan, and Gamarias son of Chelcias, (whom Sedekias king of Juda sent to the king of Babylon to Babylon) saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremias 32:12; Jeremias 36:32; Jezekiel 2:9"> Thus said the Lord God of Israel concerning the captivity which I caused to be carried away from Jerusalem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 32:2; Jeremias 33:1; 2 Corinthians 11:23"> Build ye houses, and inhabit them; and plant gardens, and eat the fruits thereof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremias 36:8–9; Jeremias 26:2; Jeremias 22:2"> and take ye wives, and beget sons and daughters; and take wives for your sons, and give your daughters to husbands, and be multiplied, and be not diminished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremias 36:3; Jeremias 21:5; Chronicles II 33:12–13"> And seek the peace of the land into which I have carried you captive, and ye shall pray to the Lord for the people: for in its peace ye shall have peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremias 1:17; 1 Corinthians 16:10; Luke 4:16–30"> For thus saith the Lord; Let not the false prophets that are among you persuade you, and let not your diviners persuade you, and hearken not to your dreams which ye dream.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Chronicles II 20:3; Jonas 3:5; Esther 4:16"> For they prophesy to you unrighteous words in my name; and I sent them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 36:11; Jeremias 26:10; Jeremias 52:25"> For thus said the Lord; When seventy years shall be on the point of being accomplished at Babylon, I will visit you, and will confirm my words to you, to bring back your people to this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremias 36:10; Chronicles II 34:20; Kings IV 25:22"> And I will devise for you a device of peace, and not evil, to bestow upon you these good things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 26:22; Kings IV 22:12; Jeremias 36:25"> And do ye pray to me, and I will hearken to you: and do ye earnestly seek me, and ye shall find me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings IV 22:10; Chronicles II 34:24; Chronicles II 34:16–18"> for ye shall seek me with your whole heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremias 36:2; Jeremias 41:18; Jeremias 41:1–2"> And I will appear to you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> whereas ye said, The Lord has appointed for us prophets in Babylon:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings IV 22:10; Chronicles II 34:18; Jezekiel 2:4–5"> Thus saith the Lord concerning Achiab, and concerning Sedekias; Behold, I will deliver them into the hands of the king of Babylon; and he shall smite them in your sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Amos 3:15; Jeremias 22:14–16; Jeremias 36:9"> And they shall make of them a curse in all the captivity of Juda in Babylon, saying, The Lord do to thee as he did to Sedekias, and as he did to Achiab, whom the king of Babylon fried in the fire;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings III 22:8; Proverbs 21:30; Kings III 22:27"> because of the iniquity which they wrought in Israel, and because they committed adultery with the wives of their fellow-citizens, and spoke a word in my name, which I did not command them to speak, and I am witness, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremias 36:16; Psalms 36:1; Jonas 3:6"> And to Samæas the Ælamite thou shalt say,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 37:22; Jeremias 36:12; Acts 5:34–39"> I sent thee not in my name: and to Sophonias the priest the son of Maasæas say thou,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Kings III 17:3; Kings III 19:1–3; Kings III 19:14"> The Lord has made thee priest in the place of Jodae the priest, to be ruler in the house of the Lord over every prophet, and to every madman, and thou shalt put them in prison, and into the dungeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremias 36:4"> And now wherefore have ye reviled together Jeremias of Anathoth, who prophesied to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Zacharias 1:5–6; Job 23:13; Jeremias 44:28"> Did he not send for this purpose? for in the course of this month he sent to you to Babylon, saying, It is far off: build ye houses, and inhabit them; and plant gardens, and eat the fruit of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Esaias 30:10; Jeremias 26:9; Deuteronomy 29:19"> And Sophonias read the book in the ears of Jeremias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremias 22:30; Kings IV 24:12–15; Genesis 31:40"> Then the word of the Lord came to Jeremias, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 29:1; Jeremias 19:15; Jeremias 35:17"> Send to the captivity, saying, Thus saith the Lord concerning Samæas the Ælamite, Since Samæas has prophesied to you, and I sent him not, and he has made you to trust in iniquity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremias 36:4; Jeremias 36:18; Exodus 4:15–16"> therefore thus saith the Lord; Behold, I will visit Samæas, and his family: and there shall not be a man of them in the midst of you to see the good which I will do to you: they shall not see it.</span>
