<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremias 38:28; Jeremias 37:21; Jeremias 32:2–3"> In the beginning of the Reign of King Joakim Son of Josias there came this Word from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 6:3; Exodus 15:3; Esaias 37:26"> Thus said the Lord; Stand in the court of the Lord's house, and thou shalt declare to all the Jews, and to all that come to worship in the house of the Lord, all the words which I commanded thee to speak to them; abate not one word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 29:12; Esaias 65:24; Ephesians 3:20"> Peradventure they will hear, and turn every one from his evil way: then I will cease from the evils which I purpose to do to them, because of their evil practices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremias 32:24; Ambacum 1:10; Jezekiel 4:2"> And thou shalt say, Thus said the Lord; If ye will not hearken to me, to walk in my statutes which I set before you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esaias 8:17; Jeremias 21:10; Jeremias 21:4–7"> to hearken to the words of my servants the prophets, whom I send to you early in the morning; yea, I sent them, but ye hearkened not to me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 10:10; Esaias 30:26; Titus 3:5–6"> then will I make this house as Selo, and I will make this city a curse to all the nations of all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esaias 1:26; Jeremias 30:3; Amos 9:14–15"> And the priests, and the false prophets, and all the people heard Jeremias speaking these words in the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Zacharias 13:1; Michæas 7:18–19; Psalms 51:2"> And it came to pass, when Jeremias had ceased speaking all that the Lord had ordered him to speak to all the people, that the priests and the false prophets and all the people took him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 126:2–3; Esaias 60:5; Sophonias 3:17–20"> Thou shalt surely die, because thou hast prophesied in the name of the Lord, saying, This house shall be as Selo, and this city shall be made quite destitute of inhabitants. And all the people assembled against Jeremias in the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 32:43; Jeremias 32:36; Jezekiel 37:11"> And the princes of Juda heard this word, and they went up out of the house of the king to the house of the Lord, and sat in the entrance of the new gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Chronicles I 16:34; Psalms 106:1; Psalms 107:22"> Then the priests and the false prophets said to the princes and to all the people, The judgment of death is due to this man; because he has prophesied against this city, as ye have heard with your ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esaias 65:10; Jezekiel 34:12–15; Jeremias 36:29"> Then Jeremias spoke to the princes, and to all the people, saying, The Lord sent me to prophesy against this house and against this city, all the words which ye have heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 27:32; Luke 15:4; Jeremias 17:26"> And now amend your ways and your works, and hearken to the voice of the Lord; and the Lord shall cease from the evils which he has pronounced against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Aggæus 2:6–9; Jeremias 23:5; Chronicles I 17:13–14"> And behold, I am in your hands; do to me as is expedient, and as it is best for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esaias 11:1–5; Jeremias 23:5–6; Esaias 4:2"> But know for a certainty, that if ye slay me, ye bring innocent blood upon yourselves, and upon this city, and upon them that dwell in it; for in truth the Lord has sent me to you to speak in your ears all these words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremias 23:6; 1 Corinthians 1:30; Esaias 45:17"> Then the princes and all the people said to the priests and to the false prophets; Judgment of death is not due to this man; for he has spoken to us in the name of the Lord our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings III 2:4; Psalms 89:29–37; Luke 1:32–33"> And there rose up men of the elders of the land, and said to all the assembly of the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Esaias 56:7; Revelation 1:6; Deuteronomy 18:1"> Michæas the Morathite lived in the days of Ezekias king of Juda, and said to all the people of Juda, Thus saith the Lord; Sion shall be ploughed as a field, and Jerusalem shall become a desolation, and the mountain of the house shall be a thicket of trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Did Ezekias and all Juda in any way slay him? Was it not that they feared the Lord, and they made supplication before the Lord, and the Lord ceased from the evils which he had pronounced against them? whereas we have wrought great evil against our own souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 8:22; Psalms 89:37; Esaias 54:9–10"> And there was another man prophesying in the name of the Lord, Urias the son of Samæas of Cariathiarim; and he prophesied concerning this land according to all the words of Jeremias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 89:34; Chronicles II 7:18; Kings II 23:5"> And king Joakim and all the princes heard all his words, and sought to slay him; and Urias heard it and went into Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 15:5; Genesis 22:17; Hebrews 11:12"> And the king sent men into Egypt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and they brought him thence, and brought him in to the king; and he smote him with the sword, and cast him into the sepulchre of the children of his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:2–4; Psalms 83:4; Psalms 44:13–14"> Nevertheless the hand of Achicam son of Saphan was with Jeremias, to prevent his being delivered into the hands of the people, or being killed.</span>
