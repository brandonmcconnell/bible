<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Osee 4:1; Jeremias 42:15; Amos 7:16"> Hear ye the word of the Lord, which he has spoken to you, O house of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 20:23; Leviticus 18:3; Luke 21:25–28"> Thus saith the Lord, Learn ye not the ways of the heathen, and be not alarmed at the signs of the sky; for they are alarmed at them, falling on their faces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Esaias 44:9–20; Leviticus 18:30; 1 Peter 1:18"> For the customs of the nations are vain; it is a tree cut out of the forest, the work of the carpenter, or a molten image.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esaias 46:7; Esaias 40:19–20; Psalms 115:4"> They are beautified with silver and gold, they fix them with hammers and nails;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esaias 46:7; 1 Corinthians 12:2; Esaias 41:23–24"> they will set them up that they may not move; it is wrought silver, they will not walk,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 115:4; Daniel 10:5; Kings III 10:22"> it is forged silver brought from Tharsis, gold will come from Mophaz, and the work of goldsmiths: they are all the works of craftsmen, they will clothe themselves with blue and scarlet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 115:4; Daniel 10:5; Kings III 10:22"> They must certainly be borne, for they cannot ride of themselves. Fear them not; for they cannot do any evil, and there is no good in them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 96:5; Esaias 2:18; Lamentations 3:66"> Thus shall ye say to them, Let the gods which have not made heaven and earth perish from off the earth, and from under this sky.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 1:1; Job 9:8; Proverbs 3:19"> It is the Lord that made the earth by his strength, who set up the world by his wisdom, and by his understanding stretched out the sky,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 135:7; Job 38:22; Job 38:34–35"> and set abundance of waters in the sky, and brought up clouds from the ends of the earth; he made lightnings for the rain, and brought forth light out of his treasures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 135:16–18; Psalms 115:4–8; Esaias 46:7–8"> Every man is deprived of knowledge, every goldsmith is confounded because of his graven images; for he has cast false gods, there is no breath in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremias 14:22; Esaias 41:24; Jeremias 51:18"> They are vain works, wrought in mockery; in the time of their visitation they shall perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:9; Jeremias 32:18; Jeremias 31:35"> Such is not the portion of Jacob; for he that formed all things, he is his inheritance; the Lord is his name.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jezekiel 12:3–12; Jeremias 6:1; Matthew 24:15"> He has gathered thy substance from without that lodged in choice vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings I 25:29; Zacharias 1:6; Deuteronomy 28:63–64"> For thus saith the Lord, Behold, I will overthrow the inhabitants of this land with affliction, that thy plague may be discovered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Michæas 7:9; Jeremias 4:31; Jeremias 14:17"> Alas for thy ruin! thy plague is grievous: and I said, Surely this is thy wound, and it has overtaken thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremias 4:20; Jeremias 31:15; Esaias 54:2"> Thy tabernacle is in a ruinous state, it has perished; and all thy curtains have been torn asunder: my children and my cattle are no more: there is no more any place for my tabernacle, nor place for my curtains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jezekiel 34:2–10; Jeremias 12:10; Jeremias 23:1–2"> For the shepherds have become foolish, and have not sought the Lord; therefore the whole pasture has failed, and the sheep have been scattered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremias 9:11; Ambacum 1:6–9; Jeremias 6:22"> Behold, there comes a sound of a noise, and a great earthquake from the land of the north, to make the cities of Juda a desolation, and a resting-place for ostriches.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 20:24; Proverbs 16:1; Psalms 37:23"> I know, O Lord, that man's way is not his own; neither shall a man go, and direct his going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 38:1; Psalms 6:1; Jeremias 30:11"> Chasten us, O Lord, but with judgment; and not in wrath, lest thou make us few.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 79:6–7; Jeremias 8:16; Job 18:21"> Pour out thy wrath upon the nations that have not known thee, and upon the families that have not called upon thy name: for they have devoured Jacob, and consumed him, and have made his pasture desolate.</span>
</div>
</div>
