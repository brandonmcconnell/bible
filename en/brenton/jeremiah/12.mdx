<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Malachias 3:15; Psalms 92:7; Psalms 37:1"> Righteous art thou, O Lord, that I may make my defence to thee, yea, I will speak to thee of judgments. Why is it that the way of ungodly men prospers? that all that deal very treacherously are flourishing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esaias 29:13; Titus 1:16; Matthew 15:8"> Thou hast planted them, and they have taken root; they have begotten children, and become fruitful; thou art near to their mouth, and far from their reins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 17:18; Kings IV 20:3; Psalms 17:3"> But thou, Lord, knowest me; thou hast proved my heart before thee; purify them for the day of their slaughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremias 9:10; Psalms 107:34; Jeremias 4:25"> How long shall the land mourn, and the grass of the field wither, for the wickedness of them that dwell in it? the beasts and birds are utterly destroyed; because the people said, God shall not see our ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 4:12; Proverbs 24:10; Hebrews 12:3–4"> Thy feet run, and they cause thee to faint; how wilt thou prepare to ride upon horses? and thou hast been confident in the land of thy peace? how wilt thou do in the roaring of Jordan?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 26:25; Jeremias 9:4; Michæas 7:5–6"> For even thy brethren and the house of thy father, even these have dealt treacherously with thee; and they have cried out, they are gathered together in pursuit of thee; trust not thou in them, though they shall speak fair words to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esaias 2:6; Jeremias 11:15; Psalms 78:59–60"> I have forsaken mine house, I have left mine heritage; I have given my beloved one into the hands of her enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 6:8; Osee 9:15; Jeremias 2:15"> My inheritance has become to me as a lion in a forest; she has uttered her voice against me; therefore have I hated her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esaias 56:9; Jeremias 7:33; Kings IV 24:2"> Is not my inheritance to me a hyæna's cave, or a cave round about her? Go ye, gather together all the wild beasts of the field, and let them come to devour her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esaias 5:1–7; Jeremias 23:1; Esaias 63:18"> Many shepherds have destroyed my vineyard, they have defiled my portion, they have made my desirable portion a trackless wilderness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremias 23:10; Esaias 42:25; Jeremias 14:2"> it is made a complete ruin: for my sake the whole land has been utterly ruined, because there is none that lays the matter to heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 47:6; Amos 9:4; Jezekiel 14:17"> The ravagers are come to every passage in the wilderness: for the sword of the Lord will devour from one end of the land to the other: no flesh has any peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Michæas 6:15; Deuteronomy 28:38; Aggæus 1:6"> Sow wheat, and reap thorns; their portions shall not profit them: be ashamed of your boasting, because of reproach before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremias 3:18; Zacharias 2:8; Zacharias 10:6–12"> For thus saith the Lord concerning all the evil neighbours that touch mine inheritance, which I have divided to my people Israel; Behold, I will draw them away from their land, and I will cast out Juda from the midst of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremias 49:6; Jeremias 48:47; Jeremias 49:39"> And it shall come to pass, after I have cast them out, that I will return, and have mercy upon them, and will cause them to dwell every one in his inheritance, and every one in his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremias 4:2; Joshua 23:7; Sophonias 1:5"> And it shall be, if they will indeed learn the way of my people, to swear by my name, saying, The Lord lives; as they taught my people to swear by Baal; then shall that nation be built in the midst of my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Esaias 60:12; Psalms 2:8–12; Daniel 11:4"> But if they will not return, then will I cut off that nation with utter ruin and destruction.</span>
