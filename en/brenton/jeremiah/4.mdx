<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 2:12; Jeremias 3:22; Jeremias 3:12"> If Israel will return to me, saith the Lord, he shall return: and if he will remove his abominations out of his mouth, and fear before me, and swear,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esaias 65:16; Jeremias 9:24; Genesis 22:18"> The Lord lives, with truth, in judgment and righteousness, then shall nations bless by him, and by him they shall praise God in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Osee 10:12; Luke 8:7; Luke 8:14"> For thus saith the Lord to the men of Juda, and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem, Break up fresh ground for yourselves, and sow not among thorns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:16; Deuteronomy 30:6; Romans 2:28–29"> Circumcise yourselves to your God, and circumcise your hardness of heart, ye men of Juda, and inhabitants of Jerusalem: lest my wrath go forth as fire, and burn, and there be none to quench it, because of the evil of your devices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 8:14; Jeremias 6:1; Joshua 10:20"> Declare ye in Juda, and let it be heard in Jerusalem: say ye, Sound the trumpet in the land; cry ye aloud: say ye, Gather yourselves together, and let us enter into the fortified cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremias 50:2; Jeremias 1:13–15; Jeremias 51:27"> Gather up your wares and flee to Sion: hasten, stay not: for I will bring evils from the north, and great destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esaias 1:7; Jeremias 5:6; Esaias 6:11"> The lion is gone up from his lair, he has roused himself to the destruction of the nations, and has gone forth out of his place, to make the land desolate; and the cities shall be destroyed, so as to be without inhabitant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esaias 22:12; Jeremias 6:26; Esaias 10:4"> For these things gird yourselves with sackclothes, and lament, and howl: for the anger of the Lord is not turned away from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jezekiel 13:9–16; Esaias 29:9–10; Esaias 22:3–5"> And it shall come to pass in that day, saith the Lord, that the heart of the king shall perish, and the heart of the princes; and the priests shall be amazed, and the prophets shall wonder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 5:12; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–12; Esaias 37:35"> And I said, O sovereign Lord, verily thou hast greatly deceived this people and Jerusalem, saying, There shall be peace; whereas behold, the sword has reached even to their soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Osee 13:15; Jezekiel 17:10; Jeremias 23:19"> At that time they shall say to this people and to Jerusalem, There is a spirit of error in the wilderness: the way of the daughter of my people is not to purity, nor to holiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 1:16; Jezekiel 5:8; Jezekiel 6:11–13"> But a spirit of full vengeance shall come upon me; and now I declare my judgments against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Esaias 19:1; Esaias 66:15; Ambacum 1:8"> Behold, he shall come up as a cloud, and his chariots as a tempest: his horses are swifter than eagles. Woe unto us! for we are in misery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 4:8; Esaias 55:7; Acts 8:22"> Cleanse thine heart from wickedness, O Jerusalem, that thou mayest be saved: how long will thy grievous thoughts be within thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremias 8:16; Joshua 17:15; Judges 18:29"> For a voice of one publishing from Dan shall come, and trouble out of mount Ephraim shall be heard of.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremias 5:15; Jezekiel 21:22; Esaias 39:3"> Remind ye the nations; behold, they are come: proclaim it in Jerusalem, that bands are approaching from a land afar off, and have uttered their voice against the cities of Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremias 5:23; Kings IV 25:1–4; Nehemiah 9:30"> As keepers of a field, they have surrounded her; because thou, saith the Lord, hast neglected me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremias 2:19; Psalms 107:17; Proverbs 1:31"> Thy ways and thy devices have brought these things upon thee; this is thy wickedness, for it is bitter, for it has reached to thy heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ambacum 3:16; Esaias 16:11; Esaias 22:4"> I am pained in my bowels, my bowels, and the sensitive powers of my heart; my soul is in great commotion, my heart is torn: I will not be silent, for my soul has heard the sound of a trumpet, the cry of war, and of distress: it calls on destruction;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 42:7; Matthew 10:28; Joel 1:15"> for all the land is distressed: suddenly my tabernacle is distressed, my curtains have been rent asunder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:10; Chronicles II 36:3; Chronicles II 36:6–7"> How long shall I see fugitives, and hear the sound of the trumpet?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:20; Romans 16:19; Jeremias 5:21"> For the princes of my people have not known me, they are foolish and unwise children: they are wise to do evil, but how to do good they have not known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 24:29; Mark 13:24–25; Esaias 13:10"> I looked upon the earth, and, behold, it was not; and to the sky, and there was no light in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jezekiel 38:20; Esaias 5:25; Naum 1:5–6"> I beheld the mountains, and they trembled, and I saw all the hills in commotion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Sophonias 1:2–3; Jeremias 12:4; Jeremias 9:10"> I looked, and, behold, there was no man, and all the birds of the sky were scared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 107:34; Jeremias 12:4; Jeremias 14:2–6"> I saw, and, behold, Carmel was desert, and all the cities were burnt with fire at the presence of the Lord, and at the presence of his fierce anger they were utterly destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremias 46:28; Jeremias 5:10; Jeremias 5:18"> Thus saith the Lord, The whole land shall be desolate; but I will not make a full end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Esaias 50:3; Osee 4:3"> For these things let the earth mourn, and let the sky be dark above: for I have spoken, and I will not repent; I have purposed, and I will not turn back from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Esaias 2:19–21; Jeremias 4:7; Jeremias 52:7"> The whole land has recoiled from the noise of the horseman and the bent bow; they have gone into the caves, and have hidden themselves in the groves, and have gone up upon the rocks: every city was abandoned, no man dwelt in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Kings IV 9:30; Lamentations 1:2; Lamentations 1:19"> And what wilt thou do? Though thou clothe thyself with scarlet, and adorn thyself with golden ornaments; though thou adorn thine eyes with stibium, thy beauty will be in vain: thy lovers have rejected thee, they seek thy life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Lamentations 1:17; Esaias 1:15; Jeremias 13:21"> For I have heard thy groaning as the voice of a woman in travail, as of her that brings forth her first child; the voice of the daughter of Zion shall fail through weakness, and she shall lose the strength of her hands, saying, Woe is me! for my soul faints because of the slain.</span>
