<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 42:7; Lamentations 3:54; Psalms 69:1–2"> and said,<br />I cried in my affliction to the Lord my God, and he hearkened to me, even to my cry out of the belly of hell: thou heardest my voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 31:22; Psalms 5:7; Chronicles II 6:38"> Thou didst cast me into the depths of the heart of the sea, and the floods compassed me: all thy billows and thy waves have passed upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 69:1–2; Lamentations 3:54; Psalms 40:2"> And I said, I am cast out of thy presence: shall I indeed look again toward thy holy temple?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 30:3; Psalms 16:10; Esaias 38:17"> Water was poured around me to the soul: the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. last.">a</a></sup>lowest deep compassed me, my head went down</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 18:6; Chronicles II 30:27; Psalms 11:4"> to the clefts of the mountains; I went down into the earth, whose bars are the everlasting barriers: yet, O Lord my God, let my ruined life be restored.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 31:6; Kings IV 17:15; Jeremias 2:13"> When my soul was failing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. from me.">b</a></sup>me, I remembered the Lord; and may my prayer come to thee into thy holy temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 3:8; Psalms 50:14; Ecclesiastes 5:4–5"> They that observe<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. vain and false things.">c</a></sup>vanities and lies have forsaken their own mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 8:26–27; Psalms 33:9; Genesis 1:3"> But I will sacrifice to thee with the voice of praise and thanksgiving: all that I have vowed I will pay to thee,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or, for a thank-offering to the Lord.">d</a></sup>the Lord of my salvation.</span>
