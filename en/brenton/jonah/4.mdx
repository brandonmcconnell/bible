<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 15:28; Jonas 4:9; Jonas 4:4"> But Jonas was very deeply grieved, and he was confounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 86:15; Exodus 34:6–7; Numbers 14:18–19"> And he prayed to the Lord, and said, O Lord, were not these my words when I was yet in my land? therefore I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. anticipate.">a</a></sup>made haste to flee to Tharsis; because I knew that thou art merciful and compassionate, long-suffering, and abundant in kindness, and repentest of evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings III 19:4; Ecclesiastes 7:1; Numbers 11:15"> And now,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. sovereign Lord.">b</a></sup>Lord God, take my life from me; for it is better for me to die than to live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 20:15; Jonas 4:9; Michæas 6:3"> And the Lord said to Jonas, Art thou very much grieved?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings III 19:9; Kings III 19:13; Jeremias 17:15–16"> And Jonas went out from the city, and sat over against the city; and he made for himself there a booth, and he sat under it, until he should perceive what would become of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 103:10–14; Esaias 39:2; Esther 5:9"> And the Lord God commanded a gourd, and it came up over the head of Jonas, to be a shadow over his head, to shade him from his calamities: and Jonas rejoiced with great joy for the gourd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joel 1:12; Job 1:21; Psalms 102:10"> And God commanded a worm the next morning, and it smote the gourd, and it withered away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jonas 4:3; Psalms 121:6; Jezekiel 19:12"> And it came to pass at the rising of the sun, that God commanded a burning east wind; and the sun smote on the head of Jonas, and he fainted, and despaired of his life, and said, It is better for me to die than to live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 5:2; Job 18:4; Judges 16:16"> And God said to Jonas, Art thou very much grieved for the gourd? And he said, I am very much grieved, even to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings I 20:31; Genesis 17:12"> And the Lord said, Thou hadst pity on the gourd, for which thou hast not suffered, neither didst thou rear it; which came up<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, little before.">c</a></sup>before night, and perished before another night:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 18:33; Jonas 1:2; Luke 15:28–32"> and shall not I spare Nineve, the great city, in which dwell more than twelve myriads of human beings, who do not know their right hand or their left hand; and also much cattle?</span>
