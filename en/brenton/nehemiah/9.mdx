<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 13:2–3; Ezra and Nehemiah 8:23; Nehemiah 8:2"> Now on the twenty-fourth day of this month the children of Israel assembled with fasting, and in sackcloths, and with ashes on their head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Daniel 9:3–10; Nehemiah 13:3; Ezra and Nehemiah 10:11"> And the children of Israel separated themselves from every stranger, and stood and confessed their sins, and the iniquities of their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:7–8; Nehemiah 8:3–4"> And they stood in their place, and read in the book of the law of the Lord their god: and they confessed their sins to the Lord, and worshipped the Lord their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:7; Nehemiah 12:8; Nehemiah 9:5"> And there stood upon the stairs, of the Levites, Jesus, and the sons of Cadmiel, Sechenia the son of Sarabia, sons of Choneni; and they cried with a loud voice to the Lord their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 103:1–2; 1 Peter 1:3; 2 Corinthians 4:6"> And the Levites, Jesus and Cadmiel, said, Rise up, bless the Lord our God forever and ever: and let them bless thy glorious name, and exalt it with all blessing and praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esaias 37:16; Genesis 1:1; Revelation 4:11"> And Esdras said, Thou art the only true Lord; thou madest the heaven, and the heaven of heavens, and all their array, the earth, and all things that are in it, the seas, and all things in them; and thou quickenest all things, and the hosts of heaven worship thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 11:31; Genesis 17:5; Esaias 51:2"> Thou art the Lord God, thou didst choose Abram, and broughtest him out of the land of the Chaldeans, and gavest him the name of Abraam:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 15:6; Genesis 15:18–21; Hebrews 6:18"> and thou foundest his heart faithful before thee, and didst make a covenant with him to give to him and to his seed the land of the Chananites, and the Chettites, and Amorites, and Pherezites, and Jebusites, and Gergesites; and thou hast confirmed thy words, for thou art righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 3:7–9; Acts 7:34; Exodus 3:16"> And thou sawest the affliction of our fathers in Egypt, and thou heardest their cry at the Red Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 32:20; Daniel 9:15; Exodus 18:11"> And thou shewedst signs and wonders in Egypt, on Pharao and all his servants, and on all the people of his land: for thou knowest that they behaved insolently against them: and thou madest thyself a name, as at this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 78:13; Exodus 14:21–22; Exodus 14:27–28"> And thou didst cleave the sea before them, and they passed through the midst of the sea on dry land; and thou didst cast into the deep them that were about to pursue them, as a stone in the mighty water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 13:21–22; Nehemiah 9:19; Psalms 107:7"> And thou guidedst them by day by a pillar of cloud, and by night by a pillar of fire, to enlighten for them the way wherein they should walk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 119:160; Exodus 20:1; Psalms 119:127–128"> Also thou camest down upon mount Sina, and thou spakest to them out of heaven, and gavest them right judgments, and laws of truth, ordinances, and good commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 20:8–11; Genesis 2:3; Jezekiel 20:12"> And thou didst make known to them thy holy sabbath; thou didst enjoin upon them commandments, and ordinances, and a law, by the hand of thy servant Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:3–4; Deuteronomy 1:8; Exodus 17:6"> And thou gavest them bread from heaven for their food, and thou broughtest them forth water from a rock for their thirst; and thou badest them go in to inherit the land over which thou stretchedst out thy hand to give it them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:29; Psalms 81:11–14; Hebrews 3:15"> But they and our fathers behaved proudly, and hardened their neck, and did not hearken to thy commandments,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 86:5; Joel 2:13; Psalms 145:8–9"> and refused to listen, and remembered not thy wonders which thou wroughtest with them; and they hardened their neck, and appointed a leader to return to their slavery in Egypt: but thou, O God, art merciful and compassionate, long-suffering, and abundant in mercy, and thou didst not forsake them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 106:19–23; Exodus 32:4–8; Jezekiel 20:7–44"> And still farther they even made to themselves a molten calf, and said, These are the gods that brought us up out of Egypt: and they wrought great provocations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 106:45; Nehemiah 9:27; Malachias 3:6"> Yet thou in thy great compassions didst not forsake them in the wilderness: thou didst not turn away from them the pillar of the cloud by day, to guide them in the way, nor the pillar of fire by night, to enlighten for them the way wherein they should walk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 143:10; Numbers 11:17; Esaias 63:11–14"> And thou gavest thy good Spirit to instruct them, and thou didst not withhold thy manna from their mouth, and gavest them water in their thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:7; Deuteronomy 8:4; Psalms 34:10"> And thou didst sustain them forty years in the wilderness; thou didst not allow anything to fail them: their garments did not wax old, and their feet were not bruised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 21:21–35; Deuteronomy 3:1–17; Deuteronomy 2:26–36"> Moreover, thou gavest them kingdoms, and didst divide nations to them: and they inherited the land of Seon king of Esebon, and the land of Og king of Basan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Genesis 15:5; Chronicles I 27:23"> And thou didst multiply their children as the stars of heaven, and broughtest them into the land of which thou spokest to their fathers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 21:43; Joshua 11:23; Psalms 44:2–3"> And they inherited it: and thou didst destroy from before them the dwellers in the land of the Chananites, and thou gavest into their hands them and their kings, and the nations of the land, to do unto them as it pleased them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:15; Nehemiah 9:35; Deuteronomy 3:5"> And they took lofty cities, and inherited houses full of all good things, wells dug, vineyards, and oliveyards, and every fruit tree in abundance: so they ate, and were filled, and grew fat, and rioted in thy great goodness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Kings III 14:9; Judges 2:11–12; Nehemiah 9:18"> But they turned, and revolted from thee, and cast thy law behind their backs; and they slew thy prophets, who testified against them to turn them back to thee, and they wrought great provocations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 106:41–45; Kings IV 14:27; Kings IV 13:5"> Then thou gavest them into the hand of them that afflicted them, and they did afflict them: and they cried to thee in the time of their affliction, and thou didst hear them from thy heaven, and in thy great compassions gavest them deliverers, and didst save them from the hand of them that afflicted them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 5:31–1; Judges 4:1; Kings III 8:39"> But when they rested, they did evil again before thee: so thou leftest them in the hands of their enemies, and they ruled over them: and they cried again to thee, and thou heardest them from heaven, and didst deliver them in thy great compassions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:16; Leviticus 18:5; Nehemiah 9:26"> And thou didst testify against them, to bring them back to thy law: but they hearkened not, but sinned against thy commandments and thy judgments, which if a man do, he shall live in them; and they turned their back, and hardened their neck, and heard not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Romans 2:4; Chronicles II 36:15–16; Esaias 42:24"> Yet thou didst bear long with them many years, and didst testify to them by thy Spirit by the hand of thy prophets: but they hearkened not; so thou gavest them into the hand of the nations of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Lamentations 3:22; Jeremias 4:27; Nehemiah 9:17"> But thou in thy many mercies didst not appoint them to destruction, and didst not forsake them; for thou art strong, and merciful, and pitiful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:9; Nehemiah 1:5; Daniel 9:4"> And now, O our God, the powerful, the great, the mighty, and the terrible, keeping thy covenant and thy mercy, let not all the trouble seem little in thy sight which has come upon us, and our kings, and our princes, and our priests, and our prophets, and our fathers, and upon all thy people, from the days of the kings of Assur even to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 18:25; Psalms 106:6; Job 34:23"> But thou art righteous in all the things that come upon us; for thou hast wrought faithfully, but we have greatly sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jeremias 29:19; Kings IV 17:13; Deuteronomy 31:21"> And our kings, and our princes, and our priests, and our fathers, have not performed thy law, and have not given heed to thy commandments, and have not kept thy testimonies which thou didst testify to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:47; Deuteronomy 32:12–15; Jeremias 5:19"> And they did not serve thee in thy kingdom, and in thy great goodness which thou gavest to them, and in the large and fat land which thou didst furnish before them, and they turned not from their evil devices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 9:9; Deuteronomy 28:48; Chronicles II 12:8"> Behold, we are servants this day, and as for the land which thou gavest to our fathers to eat the fruit of it and the good things of it, behold, we are servants upon it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:33; Deuteronomy 28:48; Ezra and Nehemiah 7:24"> and its produce is abundant for the kings whom thou didst appoint over us because of our sins; and they have dominion over our bodies, and over our cattle, as it pleases them, and we are in great affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:1; Chronicles II 34:31; Ezra and Nehemiah 10:3"> And in regard to all these circumstances we make a covenant, and write it, and our princes, our Levites, and our priests, set their seal to it.</span>
</div>
