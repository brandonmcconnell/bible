<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> He that liveth for ever created all things in general.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> The Lord only is righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> To none hath he given power to declare his works; and who shall find out his noble acts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Who shall number the strength of his majesty? and who shall also tell out his mercies?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> As for the wondrous works of the Lord, there may nothing be taken from them, neither may any thing be put unto them, neither can the ground of them be found out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> When a man hath done, then he beginneth; and when he leaveth off, then he shall be doubtful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> What is man, and whereto serveth he; what is his good, and what is his evil?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The number of a man's days at the most is an hundred years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> As a drop of water unto the sea, and a gravel-stone in comparison of the sand; so are a thousand years to the days of eternity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Therefore is God patient with them, and poureth forth his mercy upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> He saw and perceived their end to be evil; therefore he multiplied his compassion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The mercy of man is toward his neighbour; but the mercy of the Lord is upon all flesh: he reproveth, and nurtureth, and teacheth, and bringeth again, as a shepherd his flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> He hath mercy on them that receive discipline, and that diligently seek after his judgments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> My son, blemish not thy good deeds, neither use uncomfortable words when thou givest any thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Shall not the dew assuage the heat? so is a word better than a gift.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Lo, is not a word better than a gift? but both are with a gracious man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> A fool will upbraid churlishly, and a gift of the envious consumeth the eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Learn before thou speak, and use physic or ever thou be sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Before judgment examine thyself, and in the day of visitation thou shalt find mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Humble thyself before thou be sick, and in the time of sins shew repentance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Let nothing hinder thee to pay thy vow in due time, and defer not until death to be justified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Before thou prayest, prepare thyself; and be not as one that tempteth the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Think upon the wrath that shall be at the end, and the time of vengeance, when he shall turn away his face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> When thou hast enough, remember the time of hunger: and when thou art rich, think upon poverty and need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> From the morning until the evening the time is changed, and all things are soon done before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> A wise man will fear in every thing, and in the day of sinning he will beware of offence: but a fool will not observe time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Every man of understanding knoweth wisdom, and will give praise unto him that found her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> They that had understanding in sayings became also wise themselves, and poured forth exquisite parables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Go not after thy lusts, but refrain thyself from thine appetites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> If thou givest thy soul the desires that please her, she will make thee a laughingstock to thine enemies that malign thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Take not pleasure in much good cheer, neither be tied to the expence thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Be not made a beggar by banqueting upon borrowing, when thou hast nothing in thy purse.</span>
