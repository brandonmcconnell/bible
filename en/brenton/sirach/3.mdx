<div className="bible-chapter" lang="en" dir="ltr" data-lang="en" data-translation="brenton" data-translation-id="eng_bre" data-translation-label="Brenton LXX" data-book="SIR" data-book-slug="sirach" data-book-name="Sirach" data-chapter="3" data-verses="29">

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Hear me your father, O children, and do thereafter, that ye may be safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> For the Lord hath given the father honour over the children, and hath confirmed the authority of the mother over the sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Whoso honoureth his father maketh an atonement for his sins:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> and he that honoureth his mother is as one that layeth up treasure.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Whoso honoureth his father shall have joy of his own children; and when he maketh his prayer, he shall be heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> He that honoureth his father shall have a long life; and he that is obedient unto the Lord shall be a comfort to his mother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and will do service unto his parents, as to his masters.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Honour thy father and mother both in word and deed, that a blessing may come upon thee from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> For the blessing of the father establisheth the houses of children; but the curse of the mother rooteth out foundations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Glory not in the dishonour of thy father; for thy father's dishonour is no glory unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> For the glory of a man is from the honour of his father; and a mother in dishonour is a reproach to the children.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> My son, help thy father in his age, and grieve him not as long as he liveth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And if his understanding fail, have patience with him; and despise him not when thou art in thy full strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For the relieving of thy father shall not be forgotten: and instead of sins it shall be added to build thee up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> In the day of thine affliction it shall be remembered; thy sins also shall melt away, as the ice in the fair warm weather.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> He that forsaketh his father is as a blasphemer; and he that angereth his mother is cursed of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> My son, go on with thy business in meekness; so shalt thou be beloved of him that is approved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The greater thou art, the more humble thyself, and thou shalt find favour before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> For the power of the Lord is great, and he is honoured of the lowly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Seek not out the things that are too hard for thee, neither search the things that are above thy strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> But what is commanded thee, think thereupon with reverence; for it is not needful for thee to see with thine eyes the things that are in secret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Be not curious in unnecessary matters; for more things are shewed unto thee than men understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> For many are deceived by their own vain opinion; and an evil suspicion hath overthrown their judgment.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> A stubborn heart shall fare evil at the last; and he that loveth danger shall perish therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> An obstinate heart shall be laden with sorrows; and the wicked man shall heap sin upon sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> In the punishment of the proud there is no remedy; for the plant of wickedness hath taken root in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> The heart of the prudent will understand a parable; and an attentive ear is the desire of a wise man.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Water will quench a flaming fire; and alms maketh an atonement for sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And he that requiteth good turns is mindful of that which may come hereafter; and when he falleth, he shall find a stay.</span>
</div>
</div>
