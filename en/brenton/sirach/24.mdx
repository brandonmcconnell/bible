<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Wisdom shall praise herself, and shall glory in the midst of her people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> In the congregation of the most High shall she open her mouth, and triumph before his power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> I came out of the mouth of the most High, and covered the earth as a cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> I dwelt in high places, and my throne is in a cloudy pillar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> I alone compassed the circuit of heaven, and walked in the bottom of the deep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> In the waves of the sea, and in all the earth, and in every people and nation, I got a possession.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> With all these I sought rest: and in whose inheritance shall I abide?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> So the Creator of all things gave me a commandment, and he that made me caused my tabernacle to rest, and said, Let thy dwelling be in Jacob, and thine inheritance in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> He created me from the beginning before the world, and I shall never fail.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> In the holy tabernacle I served before him; and so was I established in Sion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Likewise in the beloved city he gave me rest, and in Jerusalem was my power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And I took root in an honourable people, even in the portion of the Lord's inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> I was exalted like a cedar in Libanus, and as a cypress tree upon the mountains of Hermon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> I was exalted like a palm tree on the sea shore, and as a rose plant in Jericho, as a fair olive tree in a plain, and grew up as a plane tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> I gave a sweet smell like cinnamon and aspalathus, and I yielded a pleasant odour like the best myrrh, as galbanum, and onyx, and sweet storax, and as the fume of frankincense in the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> As the turpentine tree I stretched out my branches, and my branches are the branches of honour and grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> As the vine brought I forth pleasant savour, and my flowers are the fruit of honour and riches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Come unto me, all ye that be desirous of me, and fill yourselves with my fruits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> For my memorial is sweeter than honey, and mine inheritance than the honeycomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> They that eat me shall yet be hungry, and they that drink me shall yet be thirsty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> He that obeyeth me shall never be confounded, and they that work by me shall not do amiss.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> All these things are the book of the covenant of the most high God, even the law which Moses commanded for an heritage unto the congregations of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> He filleth all things with his wisdom, as Phison and as Tigris in the time of the new fruits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> He maketh the understanding to abound like Euphrates, and as Jordan in the time of the harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> He maketh the doctrine of knowledge appear as the light, and as Geon in the time of vintage.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> The first man knew her not perfectly: no more shall the last find her out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> For her thoughts are more than the sea, and her counsels profounder than the great deep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> I also came out as a brook from a river, and as a conduit into a garden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> I said, I will water my best garden, and will water abundantly my garden bed: and, lo, my brook became a river, and my river became a sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> I will yet make doctrine to shine as the morning, and will send forth her light afar off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> I will yet pour out doctrine as prophecy, and leave it to all ages for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Behold that I have not laboured for myself only, but for all them that seek wisdom.</span>
</div>
</div>
