<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The pride of the height, the clear firmament, the beauty of heaven, with his glorious shew;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> the sun when it appeareth, declaring at his rising a marvellous instrument, the work of the most High:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> at noon it parcheth the country, and who can abide the burning heat thereof?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> A man blowing a furnace is in works of heat, but the sun burneth the mountains three times more; breathing out fiery vapours, and sending forth bright beams, it dimmeth the eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Great is the Lord that made it; and at his commandment it runneth hastily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> He made the moon also to serve in her season for a declaration of times, and a sign of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> From the moon is the sign of feasts, a light that decreaseth in her perfection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The month is called after her name, increasing wonderfully in her changing, being an instrument of the armies above, shining in the firmament of heaven;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> the beauty of heaven, the glory of the stars, an ornament giving light in the highest places of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> At the commandment of the Holy One they will stand in their order, and never faint in their watches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Look upon the rainbow, and praise him that made it; very beautiful it is in the brightness thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> It compasseth the heaven about with a glorious circle, and the hands of the most High have bended it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> By his commandment he maketh the snow to fall apace, and sendeth swiftly the lightnings of his judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Through this the treasures are opened: and clouds fly forth as fowls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> By his great power he maketh the clouds firm, and the hailstones are broken small.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> At his sight the mountains are shaken, and at his will the south wind bloweth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The noise of the thunder maketh the earth to tremble: so doth the northern storm and the whirlwind: as birds flying he scattereth the snow, and the falling down thereof is as the lighting of grasshoppers:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> the eye marvelleth at the beauty of the whiteness thereof, and the heart is astonished at the raining of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> The hoarfrost also as salt he poureth on the earth, and being congealed, it becometh sharp peaks.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> When the cold north wind bloweth, and the water is congealed into ice, it abideth upon every gathering together of water, and clotheth the water as with a breastplate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> It devoureth the mountains, and burneth the wilderness, and consumeth the grass as fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> A present remedy of all is a mist coming speedily: a dew coming after heat refresheth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> By his counsel he appeaseth the deep, and planteth islands therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> They that sail on the sea tell of the danger thereof; and when we hear it with our ears, we marvel thereat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> For therein be strange and wondrous works, variety of all kinds of beasts, the race of whales.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> By him the end of them hath prosperous success, and by his word all things consist.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> We may speak much, and yet come short; wherefore in sum, he is all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> How shall we be able to magnify him? for he is great above all his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> The Lord is terrible and very great, and marvellous is his power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> When ye glorify the Lord, exalt him as much as ye can; for even yet will he far exceed: and when ye exalt him, put forth all your strength, and be not weary; for ye can never go far enough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Who hath seen him, that he might tell us? and who can magnify him as he is?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> There are yet hid greater things than these be, for we have seen but a few of his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> For the Lord hath made all things; and to the godly hath he given wisdom.</span>
</div>
</div>
