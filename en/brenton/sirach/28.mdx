<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> He that revengeth shall find vengeance from the Lord, and he will surely keep his sins &#91;in remembrance&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Forgive thy neighbour the hurt that he hath done unto thee, so shall thy sins also be forgiven when thou prayest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> One man beareth hatred against another, and doth he seek pardon from the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> He sheweth no mercy to a man, which is like himself: and doth he ask forgiveness of his own sins?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> If he that is but flesh nourish hatred, who will intreat for pardon of his sins?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Remember thy end, and let thy enmity cease; &#91;remember&#93; corruption and death, and abide in the commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Remember the commandments, and bear no malice to thy neighbour: &#91;remember&#93; the covenant of the Highest, and wink at ignorance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Abstain from strife, and thou shalt diminish thy sins: for a furious man will kindle strife.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> A sinful man disquieteth friends, and maketh debate among them that be at peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> As the matter of the fire is, so it burneth: and as a man's strength is, so is his wrath; and according to his riches his anger riseth; and the stronger they are which contend, the more they will be inflamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> An hasty contention kindleth a fire: and an hasty fighting sheddeth blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> If thou blow the spark, it shall burn: if thou spit upon it, it shall be quenched: and both these come out of thy mouth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Curse the whisperer and double-tongued: for such have destroyed many that were at peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> A backbiting tongue hath disquieted many, and driven them from nation to nation: strong cities hath it pulled down, and overthrown the houses of great men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> A backbiting tongue hath cast out virtuous women, and deprived them of their labours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Whoso hearkeneth unto it shall never find rest, and never dwell quietly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The stroke of the whip maketh marks in the flesh: but the stroke of the tongue breaketh the bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Many have fallen by the edge of the sword: but not so many as have fallen by the tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Well is he that is defended from it, and hath not passed through the venom thereof, who hath not drawn the yoke thereof, nor hath been bound in her bands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> For the yoke thereof is a yoke of iron, and the bands thereof are bands of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The death thereof is an evil death, the grave were better than it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> It shall not have rule over them that fear God, neither shall they be burned with the flame thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Such as forsake the Lord shall fall into it; and it shall burn in them, and not be quenched; it shall be sent upon them as a lion, and devour them as a leopard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Look that thou hedge thy possession about with thorns, and bind up thy silver and gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> and weigh thy words in a balance, and make a door and bar for thy mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Beware thou slide not by it, lest thou fall before him that lieth in wait.</span>
</div>
