<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Blessed is the man that hath not slipped with his mouth, and is not pricked with the multitude of sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Blessed is he whose conscience hath not condemned him, and who is not fallen from his hope in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Riches are not comely for a niggard: and what should an envious man do with money?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> He that gathereth by defrauding his own soul gathereth for others that shall spend his goods riotously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> He that is evil to himself, to whom will he be good? he shall not take pleasure in his goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> There is none worse than he that envieth himself; and this is a recompence of his wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And if he doeth good, he doeth it unwillingly; and at the last he will declare his wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The envious man hath a wicked eye; he turneth away his face, and despiseth men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> A covetous man's eye is not satisfied with his portion; and the iniquity of the wicked drieth up his soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> A wicked eye envieth &#91;his&#93; bread, and he is a niggard at his table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> My son, according to thy ability do good to thyself, and give the Lord his due offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Remember that death will not be long in coming, and that the covenant of the grave is not shewed unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Do good unto thy friend before thou die, and according to thy ability stretch out thy hand and give to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Defraud not thyself of the good day, and let not the part of a good desire overpass thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Shalt thou not leave thy travails unto another? and thy labours to be divided by lot?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Give, and take, and sanctify thy soul; for there is no seeking of dainties in the grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> All flesh waxeth old as a garment: for the covenant from the beginning is, Thou shalt die the death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> As of the green leaves on a thick tree, some fall, and some grow: so is the generation of flesh and blood, one cometh to an end, and another is born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Every work rotteth and consumeth away, and the worker thereof shall go withal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Blessed is the man that doth meditate good things in wisdom, and that reasoneth of holy things by his understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> He that considereth her ways in his heart shall also have understanding in her secrets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Go after her as one that traceth, and lie in wait in her ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> He that prieth in at her windows shall also hearken at her doors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> He that doth lodge near her house shall also fasten a pin in her walls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> He shall pitch his tent nigh unto her, and shall lodge in a lodging where good things are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> He shall set his children under her shelter, and shall lodge under her branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> By her he shall be covered from heat, and in her glory shall he dwell.</span>
