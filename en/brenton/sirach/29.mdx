<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> He that is merciful will lend unto his neighbour; and he that strengtheneth his hand keepeth the commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Lend to thy neighbour in time of his need, and pay thou thy neighbour again in due season.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Keep thy word, and deal faithfully with him, and thou shalt always find the thing that is necessary for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Many, when a thing was lent them, reckoned it to be found, and put them to trouble that helped them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Till he hath received, he will kiss a man's hand; and for his neighbour's money he will speak submissively: but when he should repay, he will prolong the time, and return words of grief, and complain of the time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> If he prevail, he shall hardly receive the half, and he will count as if he had found it: if not, he hath deprived him of his money, and he hath gotten him an enemy without cause: he payeth him with cursings and railings; and for honour he will pay him disgrace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Many therefore have refused to lend for other men's ill dealing, fearing to be defrauded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Yet have thou patience with a man in poor estate, and delay not to shew him mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Help the poor for the commandment's sake, and turn him not away because of his poverty.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Lose thy money for thy brother and thy friend, and let it not rust under a stone to be lost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Lay up thy treasure according to the commandments of the most High, and it shall bring thee more profit than gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Shut up alms in thy storehouses: and it shall deliver thee from all affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> It shall fight for thee against thine enemies better than a mighty shield and strong spear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> An honest man is surety for his neighbour: but he that is impudent will forsake him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Forget not the friendship of thy surety, for he hath given his life for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> A sinner will overthrow the good estate of his surety:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> and he that is of an unthankful mind will leave him &#91;in danger&#93; that delivered him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Suretyship hath undone many of good estate, and shaken them as a wave of the sea: mighty men hath it driven from their houses, so that they wandered among strange nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> A wicked man transgressing the commandments of the Lord shall fall into suretyship: and he that undertaketh and followeth other men's business for gain shall fall into suits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Help thy neighbour according to thy power, and beware that thou thyself fall not into the same.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The chief thing for life is water, and bread, and clothing, and a house to cover shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Better is the life of a poor man in a mean cottage, than delicate fare in another man's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Be it little or much, hold thee contented,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> for it is a miserable life to go from house to house: for where thou art a stranger, thou darest not open thy mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Thou shalt entertain, and feast, and have no thanks: moreover thou shalt hear bitter words:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Come, thou stranger, and furnish a table, and feed me of that thou hast ready.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Give place, thou stranger, to an honourable man; my brother cometh to be lodged, and I have need of mine house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> These things are grievous to a man of understanding; the upbraiding of houseroom, and reproaching of the lender.</span>
</div>
</div>
