<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Of these things be not thou ashamed, and accept no person to sin thereby:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> of the law of the most High, and his covenant; and of judgment to justify the ungodly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> of reckoning with thy partners and travellers; or of the gift of the heritage of friends;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> of exactness of balance and weights; or of getting much or little;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> and of merchants' indifferent selling; of much correction of children; and to make the side of an evil servant to bleed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Sure keeping is good, where an evil wife is; and locked up, where many hands are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Deliver all things in number and weight; and put all in writing that thou givest out, or receivest in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Be not ashamed to inform the unwise and foolish, and the extreme aged that contendeth with those that are young: thus shalt thou be truly learned, and approved of all men living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> A daughter is a wakeful care to a father; and the care for her taketh away sleep: when she is young, lest she pass away the flower of her age; and being married, lest she should be hated:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> in her virginity, lest she should be defiled and gotten with child in her father's house; and having an husband, lest she should misbehave herself; and when she is married, lest she should be barren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Keep a sure watch over a shameless daughter, lest she make thee a laughingstock to thine enemies, and a byword in the city, and a reproach among the people, and make thee ashamed before the multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Behold not everybody's beauty, and sit not in the midst of women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> For from garments cometh a moth, and from women wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Better is the churlishness of a man than a courteous woman, a woman, I say, which bringeth shame and reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> I will now remember the works of the Lord, and declare the things that I have seen: In the words of the Lord are his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The sun that giveth light looketh upon all things, and the work thereof is full of the glory of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The Lord hath not given power to the saints to declare all his marvellous works, which the Almighty Lord firmly settled, that whatsoever is might be established for his glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> He seeketh out the deep, and the heart, and considereth their crafty devices: for the Lord knoweth all that can be known, and he beholdeth the signs of the world,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> declaring the things that are past, and for to come, and revealing the steps of hidden things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> No thought escapeth him, neither any word is hidden from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> He hath garnished the excellent works of his wisdom, and he is from everlasting to everlasting: unto him may nothing be added, neither can he be diminished, and he hath no need of any counsellor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Oh how desirable are all his works! and that a man may see even to a spark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> All these things live and remain for ever for all uses, and they are all obedient.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> All things are double one against another: and he hath made nothing imperfect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> One thing establisheth the good of another: and who shall be filled with beholding his glory?</span>
