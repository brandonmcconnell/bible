<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> My son, defraud not the poor of his living, and make not the needy eyes to wait long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Make not a hungry soul sorrowful; neither provoke a man in his distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Add not more trouble to a heart that is vexed; and defer not to give to him that is in need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Reject not the supplication of the afflicted; neither turn away thy face from a poor man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Turn not away thine eye from the needy, and give him none occasion to curse thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> for if he curse thee in the bitterness of his soul, his prayer shall be heard of him that made him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Get thyself the love of the congregation, and bow thy head to a great man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Let it not grieve thee to bow down thine ear to the poor, and give him a friendly answer with meekness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Deliver him that suffereth wrong from the hand of the oppressor; and be not fainthearted when thou sittest in judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Be as a father unto the fatherless, and instead of a husband unto their mother: so shalt thou be as the son of the most High, and he shall love thee more than thy mother doth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Wisdom exalteth her children, and layeth hold of them that seek her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> He that loveth her loveth life; and they that seek to her early shall be filled with joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> He that holdeth her fast shall inherit glory; and wheresoever she entereth, the Lord will bless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> They that serve her shall minister to the Holy One: and them that love her the Lord doth love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Whoso giveth ear unto her shall judge the nations: and he that attendeth unto her shall dwell securely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> If a man commit himself unto her, he shall inherit her; and his generation shall hold her in possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> For at the first she will walk with him by crooked ways, and bring fear and dread upon him, and torment him with her discipline, until she may trust his soul, and try him by her laws.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Then will she return the straight way unto him, and comfort him, and shew him her secrets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> But if he go wrong, she will forsake him, and give him over to his own ruin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Observe the opportunity, and beware of evil; and be not ashamed when it concerneth thy soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> For there is a shame that bringeth sin; and there is a shame which is glory and grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Accept no person against thy soul, and let not the reverence of any man cause thee to fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And refrain not to speak, when there is occasion to do good, and hide not thy wisdom in her beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> For by speech wisdom shall be known: and learning by the word of the tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> In no wise speak against the truth; but be abashed of the error of thine ignorance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Be not ashamed to confess thy sins; and force not the course of the river.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Make not thyself an underling to a foolish man; neither accept the person of the mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Strive for the truth unto death, and the Lord shall fight for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Be not hasty in thy tongue, and in thy deeds slack and remiss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Be not as a lion in thy house, nor frantic among thy servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Let not thine hand be stretched out to receive, and shut when thou shouldest repay.</span>
