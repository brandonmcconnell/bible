<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> He that loveth his son causeth him oft to feel the rod, that he may have joy of him in the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> He that chastiseth his son shall have joy in him, and shall rejoice of him among his acquaintance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> He that teacheth his son grieveth the enemy: and before his friends he shall rejoice of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Though his father die, yet he is as though he were not dead: for he hath left one behind him that is like himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> While he lived, he saw and rejoiced in him: and when he died, he was not sorrowful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> He left behind him an avenger against his enemies, and one that shall requite kindness to his friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> He that maketh too much of his son shall bind up his wounds; and his bowels will be troubled at every cry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> An horse not broken becometh headstrong: and a child left to himself will be wilful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Cocker thy child, and he shall make thee afraid: play with him, and he will bring thee to heaviness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Laugh not with him, lest thou have sorrow with him, and lest thou gnash thy teeth in the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Give him not liberty in youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Beat his sides while he is still young, lest becoming stubborn, he disobey thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Train up thy son, and exercise him with work, lest by thy looseness thou stumble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And overlook not his ignorance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Bow down his neck in his youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Better is the poor, being sound and strong of constitution, than a rich man that is afflicted in his body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Health and good estate of body are above all gold, and a strong body above infinite wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> There is no riches above a sound body, and no joy above the joy of the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Death is better than a bitter life or continual sickness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Delicacies poured upon a mouth shut up are as messes of meat set upon a grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> What good doeth the offering unto an idol? for neither can it eat nor smell: so is he that is persecuted of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> He seeth with his eyes and groaneth, as an eunuch that embraceth a virgin and sigheth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Give not over thy mind to heaviness, and afflict not thyself in thine own counsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The gladness of the heart is the life of man, and the joyfulness of a man prolongeth his days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Love thine own soul, and comfort thy heart, remove sorrow far from thee: for sorrow hath killed many, and there is no profit therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Envy and wrath shorten the life, and carefulness bringeth age before the time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> I awaked up last of all, as one that gathereth after the grape-gatherers: by the blessing of the Lord I profited,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and filled my winepress like a gatherer of grapes. Consider that I laboured not for myself only, but for all them that seek learning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Hear me, O ye great men of the people, and hearken with your ears, ye rulers of the congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Give not thy son and wife, thy brother and friend, power over thee while thou livest, and give not thy goods to another, lest it repent thee, and thou intreat for the same again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> As long as thou livest and hast breath in thee, give not thyself over to any.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> For better it is that thy children should seek to thee, than that thou shouldest stand to their courtesy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> In all thy works keep to thyself the preeminence; leave not a stain in thine honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> At the time when thou shalt end thy days, and finish thy life, distribute thine inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Fodder, a stick, and burdens, are for the ass; and bread, correction, and work, for a servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> If thou set thy servant to labour, thou shalt find rest: but if thou let him go idle, he will seek liberty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> A yoke and a collar do bow the neck: so are tortures and torments for an evil servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Send him to labour, that he be not idle; for idleness teacheth much evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Set him to work, as is fit for him: if he be not obedient, put on more heavy fetters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> But be not excessive toward any; and without discretion do nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> If thou have a servant, let him be unto thee as thyself, because thou hast bought him with a price.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> If thou have a servant, entreat him as a brother: for thou hast need of him, as of thine own soul: if thou entreat him evil, and he run from thee, which way wilt thou go to seek him?</span>
