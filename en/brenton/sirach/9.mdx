<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Be not jealous over the wife of thy bosom, and teach her not an evil lesson against thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Give not thy soul unto a woman to set her foot upon thy substance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Meet not with an harlot, lest thou fall into her snares.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Use not much the company of a woman that is a singer, lest thou be taken with her attempts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Gaze not on a maid, that thou fall not by those things that are precious in her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Give not thy soul unto harlots, that thou lose not thine inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Look not round about thee in the streets of the city, neither wander thou in the solitary places thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Turn away thine eye from a beautiful woman, and look not upon another's beauty; for many have been deceived by the beauty of a woman; for herewith love is kindled as a fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Sit not at all with another man's wife, nor sit down with her in thine arms, and spend not thy money with her at the wine; lest thine heart incline unto her, and so through thy desire thou fall into destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Forsake not an old friend; for the new is not comparable to him: a new friend is as new wine; when it is old, thou shalt drink it with pleasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Envy not the glory of a sinner: for thou knowest not what shall be his end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Delight not in the thing that the ungodly have pleasure in; but remember they shall not go unpunished unto their grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Keep thee far from the man that hath power to kill: so shalt thou not doubt the fear of death: and if thou come unto him, make no fault, lest he take away thy life presently: remember that thou goest in the midst of snares, and that thou walkest upon the battlements of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> As near as thou canst, guess at thy neighbour, and consult with the wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Let thy talk be with the wise, and all thy communication in the law of the most High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And let just men eat and drink with thee; and let thy glorying be in the fear of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> For the hand of the artificer the work shall be commended; and the wise ruler of the people for his speech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> A man of an ill tongue is dangerous in his city; and he that is rash in his talk shall be hated.</span>
