<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> When thou wilt do good, know to whom thou doest it; so shalt thou be thanked for thy benefits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Do good to the godly man, and thou shalt find a recompence; and if not from him, yet from the most High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> There can no good come to him that is always occupied in evil, nor to him that giveth no alms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Give to the godly man, and help not a sinner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Do well unto him that is lowly, but give not to the ungodly: hold back thy bread, and give it not unto him, lest he overmaster thee thereby: for &#91;else&#93; thou shalt receive twice as much evil for all the good thou shalt have done unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> For the most High hateth sinners, and will repay vengeance unto the ungodly, and keepeth them against the mighty day of their punishment.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Give unto the good, and help not the sinner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> A friend cannot be known in prosperity: and an enemy cannot be hidden in adversity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> In the prosperity of a man enemies will be grieved: but in his adversity even a friend will depart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Never trust thine enemy: for like as iron rusteth, so is his wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Though he humble himself, and go crouching, yet take good heed and beware of him, and thou shalt be unto him as if thou hadst wiped a looking-glass, and thou shalt know that his rust hath not been altogether wiped away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Set him not by thee, lest, when he hath overthrown thee, he stand up in thy place; neither let him sit at thy right hand, lest he seek to take thy seat, and thou at the last remember my words, and be pricked therewith.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Who will pity a charmer that is bitten with a serpent, or any such as come nigh wild beasts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> So one that goeth to a sinner, and is defiled with him in his sins, who will pity?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> For a while he will abide with thee, but if thou begin to fall, he will not tarry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> An enemy speaketh sweetly with his lips, but in his heart he imagineth how to throw thee into a pit: he will weep with his eyes, but if he find opportunity, he will not be satisfied with blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> If adversity come upon thee, thou shalt find him there first; and though he pretend to help thee, yet shall he undermine thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> He will shake his head, and clap his hands, and whisper much, and change his countenance.</span>
</div>
