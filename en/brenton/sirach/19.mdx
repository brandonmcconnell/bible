<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> A labouring man that is given to drunkenness shall not be rich: and he that contemneth small things shall fall by little and little.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Wine and women will make men of understanding to fall away: and he that cleaveth to harlots will become impudent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Moths and worms shall have him to heritage, and a bold man shall be taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> He that is hasty to give credit is light-minded; and he that sinneth shall offend against his own soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Whoso taketh pleasure in wickedness shall be condemned:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> but he that hateth babbling hath less of evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Rehearse not unto another that which is told unto thee, and thou shalt fare never the worse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Whether it be to friend or foe, talk not of other men's lives; and if thou canst not without offence, reveal them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> For he heard and observed thee, and when time cometh he will hate thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> If thou hast heard a word, let it die with thee; and be bold, it will not burst thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> A fool travaileth with a word, as a woman in labour of a child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> As an arrow that sticketh in a man's thigh, so is a word within a fool's belly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Admonish a friend, it may be he hath not done it: and if he have done it, that he do it no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Admonish thy friend, it may be he hath not said it: and if he have, that he speak it not again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Admonish a friend: for many times it is a slander, and believe not every tale.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> There is one that slippeth in his speech, but not from his heart; and who is he that hath not offended with his tongue?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Admonish thy neighbour before thou threaten him; and give place to the law of the most High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The fear of the Lord is all wisdom; and in all wisdom is the performance of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The knowledge of wickedness is not wisdom, neither at any time the counsel of sinners prudence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> There is a wickedness, and the same an abomination; and there is a fool wanting in wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> He that hath small understanding, and feareth God, is better than one that hath much wisdom, and transgresseth the law of the most High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> There is an exquisite subtilty, and the same is unjust; and there is one that turneth aside to make judgment appear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> There is a wicked man that hangeth down his head sadly; but inwardly he is full of deceit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> casting down his countenance, and making as if he heard not: where he is not known, he will do thee a mischief before thou be aware.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And if for want of power he be hindered from sinning, yet when he findeth opportunity he will do evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> A man may be known by his look, and one that hath understanding by his countenance, when thou meetest him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> A man's attire, and excessive laughter, and gait, shew what he is.</span>
