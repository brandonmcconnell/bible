<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The Lord created man of the earth, and turned him into it again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> He gave them few days, and a short time, and power also over the things therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> He endued them with strength by themselves, and made them according to his image,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> and put the fear of man upon all flesh, and gave him dominion over beasts and fowls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Counsel, and a tongue, and eyes, ears, and a heart, gave he them to understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Withal he filled them with the knowledge of understanding, and shewed them good and evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> He set his eye upon their hearts, that he might shew them the greatness of his works;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> and they shall praise his holy name, that they may search out his marvellous works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Beside this he gave them knowledge, and the law of life for an heritage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> He made an everlasting covenant with them, and shewed them his judgments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Their eyes saw the majesty of his glory, and their ears heard his glorious voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And he said unto them, Beware of all unrighteousness; and he gave every man commandment concerning his neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Their ways are ever before him, and shall not be hid from his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> For in the division of the nations of the whole earth he set a ruler over every people; but Israel is the Lord's portion:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Therefore all their works are as the sun before him, and his eyes are continually upon their ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> None of their unrighteous deeds are hid from him, but all their sins are before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The alms of a man is as a signet with him, and he will keep the good deeds of man as the apple of the eye, and give repentance to his sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Afterwards he will rise up and reward them, and render their recompence upon their heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> But unto them that repent, he granted them return, and comforted those that failed in patience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Return unto the Lord, and forsake thy sins, make thy prayer before his face, and offend less.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Turn again to the most High, and turn away from iniquity: for he will lead thee out of darkness into the light of health, and hate thou abomination vehemently.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Who shall praise the most High in the grave, instead of them which live and give thanks?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Thanksgiving perisheth from the dead, as from one that is not: the living and sound in heart shall praise the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> How great is the lovingkindness of the Lord our God, and his compassion unto such as turn unto him in holiness!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> For all things cannot be in men, because the son of man is not immortal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> What is brighter than the sun? yet the light thereof faileth: and flesh and blood will imagine evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> He vieweth the power of the height of heaven; and all men are but earth and ashes.</span>
