<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> For, behold, in those days and at that time, when I shall have turned the captivity of Juda and Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> I will also gather all the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, nations. See Mat. 25. 31.">a</a></sup>Gentiles, and bring them down to the valley of Josaphat, and will plead with them there for my people and my heritage Israel, who have been dispersed among the Gentiles; and these Gentiles have divided my land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and cast lots over my people, and have given their boys to harlots, and sold their girls for wine, and have drunk.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And what have ye to do with me, O Tyre, and Sidon, and all Galilee of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="ἀλλοφυλων, usually Philistines.">b</a></sup>Gentiles? do ye render me a recompense? or do ye bear malice against me? quickly and speedily will I return your recompense on your own heads:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> because ye have taken my silver and my gold, and ye have brought my choice ornaments into your temples;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> and ye have sold the children of Juda and the children of Jerusalem to the children of the Greeks, that ye might expel them from their coasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Therefore, behold, I will raise them up out of the place whither ye have sold them, and I will return your recompense on your own heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And I will sell your sons and your daughters into the hands of the children of Juda, and they shall sell them into captivity to a far distant nation: for the Lord has spoken it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Proclaim these things among the Gentiles;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. sanctify.">c</a></sup>declare war, arouse the warriors, draw near and go up, all ye men of war.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Beat your ploughshares into swords, and your sickles into<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or, daggers.">d</a></sup>spears: let the weak say, I am strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Gather yourselves together, and go in, all ye nations round about, and gather yourselves there: let the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. meek.">e</a></sup>timid become a warrior.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Let them be aroused, let all the nations go up to the valley of Josaphat: for there will I sit to judge all the Gentiles round about.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Bring forth the sickles, for the vintage is come: go in, tread the grapes, for the press is full: cause the vats to overflow; for their wickedness is multiplied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Noises have resounded in the valley of judgment: for the day of the Lord is near in the valley of judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The sun and the moon shall be darkened, and the stars shall withdraw their light.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the Lord shall cry out of Sion, and shall utter his voice from Jerusalem; and the heaven and the earth shall be shaken, but the Lord shall spare his people, and shall strengthen the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And ye shall know that I am the Lord your God, who dwell in Sion my holy mountain: and Jerusalem shall be holy, and strangers shall not pass through her any more.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And it shall come to pass in that day that the mountains shall drop sweet wine, and the hills shall flow with milk, and all the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="See chap 1. 20.">f</a></sup>fountains of Juda shall flow with water, and a fountain shall go forth of the house of the Lord, and water the valley of flags.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Egypt shall be a desolation, and Idumea shall be a desolate plain, because of the wrongs of the children of Juda, because they have shed righteous blood in their land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> But Judea shall be inhabited for ever, and Jerusalem to all generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And I will make inquisition for their blood, and will by no means leave it unavenged: and the Lord shall dwell in Sion.</span>
</div>
</div>
