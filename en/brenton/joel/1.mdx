<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Exodus 13:14; Psalms 145:4"> Tell your children concerning them, and let your children tell their children, and their children another generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joel 2:25; Amos 4:9; Esaias 33:4"> The leavings of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="It is difficult to assign the exact meaning of the Greek.">b</a></sup>caterpillar has the locust eaten, and the leavings of the locust has the palmerworm eaten, and the leavings of the palmerworm has the cankerworm eaten.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 5:1; Esaias 32:10–12; Luke 21:34–36"> Awake, ye drunkards, from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. their.">c</a></sup>your wine, and weep: mourn, all ye that drink wine to drunkenness: for joy and gladness are removed from your mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 2:25; Revelation 9:7–10; Proverbs 30:14"> For a strong and innumerable nation is come up against my land, their teeth are lion's teeth, and their back teeth those of a lion's whelp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esaias 5:6; Amos 4:9; Joel 1:12"> He has ruined my vine, and utterly broken my fig-trees: he has utterly searched my vine, and cast it down; he has<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. whitened, Gen. 30. 37.">d</a></sup>peeled its branches.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esaias 22:12; Malachias 2:15; Jeremias 3:4"> Lament to me more than a virgin girded with sackcloth for the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="i. e. one who married her as a virgin.">e</a></sup>husband of her youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joel 2:17; Joel 2:14; Osee 9:4"> The meat-offering and drink-offering are removed from the house of the Lord: mourn, ye priests that serve at the altar of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Osee 4:3; Osee 9:2; Jeremias 12:11"> For the plains languish: let the land mourn, for the corn languishes; the wine is dried up, the oil becomes scarce;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremias 14:3–4; Esaias 17:11; Jeremias 9:12"> the husbandmen are consumed: mourn your property on account of the wheat and barley; for the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or, vintage.">f</a></sup>harvest has perished from off the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esaias 16:10; Esaias 24:11; Song of Solomon 2:3"> The vine is dried up, and the fig-trees are become few; the pomegranate, and palm-tree, and apple, and all the trees of the field are dried up: for the sons of men<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. disfigured, or, disgraced.">g</a></sup>have abolished joy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joel 1:8–9; Jeremias 4:8; Joel 2:17"> Gird yourselves with sackcloth, and lament, ye priests: mourn, ye that serve at the altar: go in, sleep in sackcloths, ye that minister to God: for the meat-offering and drink-offering are withheld from the house of your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joel 2:15–16; Deuteronomy 29:10–11; Chronicles II 20:13"> Sanctify a fast, proclaim a solemn service, gather the elders and all the inhabitants of the land into the house of your God, and cry earnestly to the Lord,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esaias 13:6–9; Sophonias 1:14–18; Jeremias 30:7"> Alas, alas, alas for the day! for the day of the Lord is nigh, and it will come as trouble upon trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:6–7; Psalms 43:4; Esaias 62:8–9"> Your meat has been destroyed before your eyes, joy and gladness from out of the house of your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Esaias 17:10–11; Genesis 23:16"> The heifers have started at their mangers, the treasures are abolished, the wine-presses are broken down; for the corn is withered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremias 12:4; Osee 4:3; Jeremias 14:5–6"> What shall we store up for ourselves? the herds of cattle have mourned, because they had no pasture; and the flocks of sheep have been utterly destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Jeremias 9:10; Amos 7:4"> To thee, O Lord, will I cry: for fire has devoured the fair places of the wilderness, and a flame has burnt up all the trees of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 104:21; Kings III 17:7; Job 38:41"> And the cattle of the field have looked up to thee: for the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. issues. See 2 Kings 22. 16; Ezek. 47. 4.">h</a></sup>fountains of waters have been dried up, and fire has devoured the fair places of the wilderness.</span>
</div>
