<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:13"> There is an evil which I have seen under the sun, and it is abundant with man:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:19; Psalms 17:14; Psalms 73:7"> a man to whom God shall give wealth, and substance, and honour, and he wants nothing for his soul of all things that he shall desire, yet God shall not give him power to eat of it, for a stranger shall devour it: this is vanity, and an evil infirmity.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 3:16; Jeremias 22:19; Ecclesiastes 4:3"> If a man beget a hundred children, and live many years, yea, however abundant the days of his years shall be, yet if his soul shall not be satisfied with good, and also he have no burial; I said, An untimely birth is better than he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 109:13"> For he came in vanity, and departs in darkness, and his name shall be covered in darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 58:8; Job 14:1; Job 3:10–13"> Moreover he has not seen the sun, nor known rest: there is no more rest to this one than another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 9:27; Ecclesiastes 3:20; Job 30:23"> Though he has lived to the return of a thousand years, yet he has seen no good: do not all go to one place?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 6:27; Proverbs 16:26; 1 Timothy 6:6–8"> All the labour of a man is for his mouth, and yet the appetite shall not be satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 19:1; Ecclesiastes 2:14–16; Psalms 101:2"> For what advantage has the wise man over the fool, since even the poor knows how to walk in the direction of life?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:14; Job 31:7; Ecclesiastes 1:2"> The sight of the eyes is better than that which wanders in soul: this is also vanity, and waywardness of spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:32; Esaias 45:9–10; Job 40:2"> If anything has been, its name has already been called: and it is known what man is; neither can he contend with him who is stronger than he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:16; Ecclesiastes 5:7; Ecclesiastes 2:3–11"> For there are many things which increase vanity.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 4:14; Job 14:2; Ecclesiastes 3:22"> What advantage has a man? for who knows what is good for a man in his life, during the number of the life of the days of his vanity? and he has spent them as a shadow; for who shall tell a man what shall be after him under the sun?</span>
</div>
