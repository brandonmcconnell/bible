<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:13; Ecclesiastes 4:7; Ecclesiastes 9:3"> There is an evil which I have seen under the sun, wherein an error has proceeded from the ruler.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 28:12; Proverbs 29:2; Esther 3:1"> The fool has been set in very high places, while rich men would sit in a low one.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 26:27; Psalms 7:15–16; Esther 7:10"> He that digs a pit shall fall into it; and him that breaks down a hedge a serpent shall bite.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremias 8:17; Psalms 58:4–5; Psalms 64:3"> If a serpent bite when there is no charmer's whisper, then there is no advantage to the charmer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Luke 4:22; Proverbs 22:11"> The words of a wise mouth are gracious: but the lips of a fool will swallow him up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings I 25:10–11; Proverbs 29:9; Acts 6:9–11"> The beginning of the words of his mouth is folly: and the end of his talk mischievous madness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 15:2; Ecclesiastes 6:12; Ecclesiastes 5:3"> A fool moreover multiplies words: man knows not what has been, nor what will be: who shall tell him what will come after him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremias 50:4–5; Esaias 57:1; Esaias 35:8–10"> The labour of fools will afflict them, as that of one who knows not to go to the city.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esaias 3:12; Esaias 5:11–12; Esaias 3:4–5"> Woe to thee, O city, whose king is young, and thy princes eat in the morning!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 31:4–5; Proverbs 28:2–3; Esaias 5:11"> Blessed art thou, O land, whose king is a son of nobles, and whose princes shall eat seasonably, for strength, and shall not be ashamed.</span>
