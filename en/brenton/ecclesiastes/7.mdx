<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Proverbs 22:1; Ecclesiastes 4:2"> A good name is better than good oil; and the day of death than the day of birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 90:12; Deuteronomy 32:29; Hebrews 9:27"> It is better to go to the house of mourning, than to go to the banquet house: since this is the end of every man; and the living man will apply good warning to his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:9–11; Romans 5:3–4; James 4:8–10"> Sorrow is better than laughter: for by the sadness of the countenance the heart will be made better.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 8:14–16; Kings I 25:36; Luke 7:12–13"> The heart of the wise is in the house of mourning; but the heart of fools is in the house of mirth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 15:31–32; Psalms 141:5; Revelation 3:19"> It is better to hear a reproof of a wise man, than for a man to hear the song of fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 58:9; Psalms 118:12; Ecclesiastes 2:2"> As the sound of thorns under a caldron, so is the laughter of fools: this is also vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Exodus 23:8; Proverbs 17:23"> For oppression makes a wise man mad, and destroys his noble heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Proverbs 14:29; Psalms 126:5–6"> The end of a matter is better than the beginning thereof: the patient is better than the high-minded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 1:19; Proverbs 14:17; Ephesians 4:26–27"> Be not hasty in thy spirit to be angry: for anger will rest in the bosom of fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 6:13; Psalms 14:2–3; Genesis 6:11–12"> Say not, What has happened, that the former days were better than these? for thou dost not enquire in wisdom concerning this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:7; 1 Timothy 6:17–19; Ecclesiastes 2:13"> Wisdom is good with an inheritance: and there is an advantage by it to them that see the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 8:35; Proverbs 2:7; Proverbs 3:18"> For wisdom in its shadow is as the shadow of silver: and the excellence of the knowledge of wisdom will give life to him that has it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:15; Job 12:14; Psalms 107:43"> Behold the works of God: for who shall be able to straighten him whom God has made crooked?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:4; Ecclesiastes 3:22; Ecclesiastes 12:13"> In the day of prosperity live joyfully, and consider in the day of adversity: consider, I say, God also has caused the one to agree with the other for this reason, that man should find nothing after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:12–14; Ecclesiastes 6:12; Psalms 73:3–13"> I have seen all things in the days of my vanity: there is a just man perishing in his justice, and there is an ungodly man remaining in his wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:3; Proverbs 25:16; Colossians 2:18"> Be not very just; neither be very wise: lest thou be confounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 10:27; Psalms 55:23; Job 22:16"> Be not very wicked; and be not stubborn: lest thou shouldest die before thy time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:13; Malachias 4:2; Ecclesiastes 11:6"> It is well for thee to hold fast by this; also by this defile not thine hand: for to them that fear God all things shall come forth well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 21:22; Colossians 1:9–11; Proverbs 24:5"> Wisdom will help the wise man more than ten mighty men which are in the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 3:23; Esaias 53:6; 1 John 1:8–10"> For there is not a righteous man in the earth, who will do good, and not sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 30:10; Esaias 29:21; Kings II 16:10"> Also take no heed to all the words which ungodly men shall speak; lest thou hear thy servant cursing thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 3:9; Matthew 18:32–35; John 8:7–9"> For many times he shall trespass against thee, and repeatedly shall he afflict thine heart; for thus also hast thou cursed others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 1:22; Genesis 3:5; 1 Corinthians 1:20"> All these things have I proved in wisdom: I said, I will be wise; but it was far from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 11:33; 1 Timothy 6:16; Esaias 55:8–9"> That which is far beyond what was, and a great depth, who shall find it out?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:13; Joshua 7:13; Ecclesiastes 2:20"> I and my heart went round about to know, and to examine, and to seek wisdom, and the account of things, and to know the folly and trouble and madness of the ungodly man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 22:14; Proverbs 5:3–5; Proverbs 2:18–19"> And I find her to be, and I will pronounce to be more bitter than death the woman which is a snare, and her heart nets, who has a band in her hands: he that is good in the sight of God shall be delivered from her; but the sinner shall be caught by her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:8–10; Ecclesiastes 1:1–2"> Behold, this have I found, said the Preacher, seeking by one at a time to find out the account,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 33:23; Kings III 11:1–3; Psalms 12:1"> which my soul sought after, but I found not: for I have found one man of a thousand; but a woman in all these I have not found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremias 4:22; Mark 7:8–9; Titus 3:3"> But, behold, this have I found, that God made man upright; but they have sought out many devices. Who knows the wise? and who knows the interpretation of a saying?</span>
