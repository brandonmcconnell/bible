<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:17; Matthew 16:3; Ecclesiastes 8:5–6"> To all things there is a time, and a season for every matter under heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 9:27; Job 14:5; Matthew 15:13"> A time of birth, and a time to die; a time to plant, and a time to pluck up what has been planted;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Osee 6:1–2; Kings I 2:6; Jeremias 45:4"> a time to kill, and a time to heal; a time to pull down, and a time to build up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Psalms 30:5; James 4:9"> a time to weep, and a time to laugh; a time to lament, and a time to dance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings IV 3:25; Joshua 10:27; Joshua 4:3–9"> a time to throw stones, and a time to gather stones together; a time to embrace, and a time to abstain from embracing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Philippians 3:7–8; Luke 9:24–25; Mark 8:35–37"> a time to seek, and a time to lose; a time to keep, and a time to cast away;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 5:13; Michæas 7:5; Lamentations 3:28"> a time to rend, and a time to sew; a time to be silent, and a time to speak;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 5:25; Ephesians 5:28–29; Luke 14:26"> a time to love, and a time to hate; a time of war, and a time of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:3; Ecclesiastes 5:16; Proverbs 14:23"> What advantage has he that works in those things wherein he labours?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:13–14; Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> I have seen all the trouble, which God has given to the sons of men to be troubled with.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Romans 1:19–20; Job 11:7"> All the things which he has made are beautiful in his time: he has also set the whole world in their heart, that man might not find out the work which God has wrought from the beginning even to the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 37:3; Ecclesiastes 3:22; 1 Timothy 6:18"> I know that there is no good in them, except for a man to rejoice, and to do good in his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 128:2; Ecclesiastes 9:7; Ecclesiastes 2:24"> Also in the case of every man who shall eat and drink, and see good in all his labour, this is a gift of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 1:17; Psalms 33:11; Romans 11:36"> I know that whatsoever things God has done, they shall be for ever: it is impossible to add to it, and it is impossible to take away from it: and God has done it, that men may fear before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:9–10; Ecclesiastes 6:10"> That which has been is now; and whatever things are appointed to be have already been; and God will seek out that which is past.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:1; Ecclesiastes 5:8; Psalms 94:21–22"> And moreover I saw under the sun the place of judgment, there was the ungodly one; and the place of righteousness, there was the godly one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Ecclesiastes 12:14; Matthew 16:27"> And I said in my heart, God will judge the righteous and the ungodly: for there is a time there for every action and for every work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 73:22; Psalms 49:12; Hebrews 9:27"> I said in my heart, concerning the speech of the sons of man, God will judge them, and that to shew that they are beasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 49:12; Psalms 49:20; Psalms 89:47–48"> Also to them is the event of the sons of man, and the event of the brute; one event befalls them: as is the death of the one, so also the death of the other; and there is one breath to all: and what has the man more than the brute? nothing; for all is vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Job 34:15; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> All go to one place; all were formed of the dust, and all will return to dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:7; John 14:3; 2 Corinthians 5:1"> And who has seen the spirit of the sons of man, whether it goes upward? and the spirit of the beast, whether it goes downward to the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:24; Ecclesiastes 3:11–12; Ecclesiastes 10:14"> And I saw that there was no good, but that wherein a man shall rejoice in his works, for it is his portion, for who shall bring him to see any thing of that which shall be after him?</span>
