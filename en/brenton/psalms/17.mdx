<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 142:6; Psalms 61:1; 1 John 3:21"> <span className="desc">For the end, a Psalm of David the servant of the Lord; the words which he spoke to the Lord, even the words of this Song, in the day in which the Lord delivered him out of the hand of all his enemies, and out of the hand of Saul: and he said:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 37:6; Jezekiel 18:25; 2 Thessalonians 1:6–9"> I will love thee, O Lord, my strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 11:23; Job 23:10; Psalms 26:2"> The Lord is my firm support, and my refuge, and my deliverer; my God is my helper, I will hope in him; he is my defender, and the horn of my salvation, and my helper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Psalms 119:9–11; 1 Corinthians 3:3"> I will call upon the Lord with praises, and I shall be saved from mine enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 44:18; Psalms 119:133; Kings I 2:9"> The pangs of death compassed me, and the torrents of ungodliness troubled me exceedingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 116:2; Psalms 86:7; Psalms 88:2"> The pangs of hell came round about me: the snares of death prevented me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 60:5; Psalms 5:11–12; Psalms 31:21"> And when I was afflicted I called upon the Lord, and cried to my God: he heard my voice out of his holy temple, and my cry shall enter before him, even into his ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:10; Psalms 91:4; Psalms 91:1"> Then the earth shook and quaked, and the foundations of the mountains were disturbed, and were shaken, because God was angry with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 35:4; Psalms 35:7; Psalms 7:5"> There went up a smoke in his wrath, and fire burst into a flame at his presence: coals were kindled at it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings I 2:3; Psalms 123:4; Psalms 31:18"> And he bowed the heaven, and came down: and thick darkness was under his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 37:14; Proverbs 6:13–14; Psalms 10:8–10"> And he mounted on cherubs and flew: he flew on the wings of winds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 7:2; 1 Peter 5:8; Psalms 10:9"> And he made darkness his secret place: round about him was his tabernacle, even dark water in the clouds of the air.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 44:26; Psalms 44:23; Psalms 7:6"> At the brightness before him the clouds passed, hail and coals of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:18; John 17:14; Luke 16:25"> The Lord also thundered from heaven, and the Highest uttered his voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:18; Matthew 5:6; Psalms 11:7"> And he sent forth his weapons, and scattered them; and multiplied lightnings, and routed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the springs of waters appeared, and the foundations of the world were exposed, at thy rebuke, O Lord, at the blasting of the breath of thy wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> He sent from on high and took me, he drew me to himself out of many waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> He will deliver me from my mighty enemies, and from them that hate me; for they are stronger than I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> They prevented me in the day of mine affliction: but the Lord was my stay against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And he brought me out into a wide place: he will deliver me, because he has pleasure in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And the Lord will recompense me according to my righteousness; even according to the purity of my hands will he recompense me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> For I have kept the ways of the Lord and have not wickedly departed from my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> For all his judgments were before me, and his ordinances departed not from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And I shall be blameless with him, and shall keep myself from mine iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And the Lord shall recompense me according to my righteousness, and according to the purity of my hands before his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> With the holy thou wilt be holy; and with the innocent man thou wilt be innocent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And with the excellent man thou wilt be excellent; and with the perverse thou wilt shew frowardness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> For thou wilt save the lowly people, and wilt humble the eyes of the proud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> For thou, O Lord, wilt light my lamp: my God, thou wilt lighten my darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> For by thee shall I be delivered from a troop; and by my God I will pass over a wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> As for my God, his way is perfect: the oracles of the Lord are tried in the fire; he is a protector of all them that hope in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> For who is God but the Lord? and who is a God except our God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> It is God that girds me with strength, and has made my way blameless:</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> who strengthens my feet as hart's feet, and sets me upon high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> He instructs my hands for war; and thou hast made my arms as a brazen bow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And thou hast made me<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. the protection of.">a</a></sup>secure in my salvation: and thy right hand has helped me, and thy correction has upheld me to the end; yea, thy correction itself shall instruct me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Thou hast made room for my goings under me, and my footsteps did not fail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> I will pursue mine enemies, and overtake them; and I will not turn back until they are consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> I will dash them to pieces and they shall not be able to stand: they shall fall under my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> For thou hast girded me with strength for war: thou hast beaten down under me all that rose up against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> And thou hast made mine enemies turn their backs before me; and thou hast destroyed them that hated me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> They cried, but there was no deliverer: even to the Lord, but he hearkened not to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> I will grind them as the mud of the streets: and I will beat them small as dust before the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> Deliver me from the gainsayings of the people: thou shalt make me head of the Gentiles: a people whom I knew not served me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> at the hearing of the ear they obeyed me: the strange children<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, feigned obedience.">b</a></sup>lied to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> The strange children waxed old, and fell away from their paths through lameness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> The Lord lives; and blessed be my God; and let the God of my salvation be exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> It is God that avenges me, and has subdued the nations under me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> my deliverer from angry enemies: thou shalt set me on high<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, out of the way of.">c</a></sup>above them that rise up against me: thou shalt deliver me from the unrighteous man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> Therefore will I confess to thee, O Lord, among the Gentiles, and sing to thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> God magnifies the deliverances of his king; and deals mercifully with David his anointed, and his seed, for ever.</span>
