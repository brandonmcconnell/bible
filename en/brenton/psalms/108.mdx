<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 57:7–11; Psalms 145:21–2; Psalms 71:15"> <span className="desc">For the end, a Psalm of David.</span>O God, pass not over my praise in silence;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 69:30; Psalms 81:2; Psalms 33:2"> for the mouth of the sinner and the mouth of the crafty man have been opened against me: they have spoken against me with a crafty tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 22:27; Psalms 22:22; Psalms 117:1"> And they have compassed me with words of hatred; and fought against me without a cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 36:5; Ephesians 2:4–7; Psalms 103:11"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, in return for my love.">a</a></sup>Instead of loving me, they falsely accused me: but I continued to pray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 57:5; Matthew 6:9–10; Psalms 8:1"> And they rewarded me evil for good, and hatred for my love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 60:5–12; Chronicles II 32:20–22; Colossians 3:12"> Set thou a sinner against him; and let<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, the accuser.">b</a></sup>the devil stand at his right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 1:8; Joshua 20:7; 2 Peter 1:3–4"> When he is judged, let him go forth condemned: and let his prayer become sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 49:10; Psalms 122:5; Kings II 2:8"> Let his days be few: and let another take his office of overseer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esaias 14:29–32; Ruth 4:7–8; Kings II 21:15–22"> Let his children be orphans, and his wife a widow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Obdias 1:3–4; Esaias 63:1–6; Psalms 20:6–8"> Let his children wander without a dwelling-place, and beg: let them be cast out of their habitations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 44:9; Chronicles II 13:12; Kings I 17:36"> Let his creditor exact all that belongs to him: and let strangers spoil his labours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 17:5–8; Psalms 146:3–5; Esaias 31:3"> Let him have no helper; neither let there be any one to have compassion on his fatherless children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 118:6–13; Psalms 60:12; Esaias 63:3"> Let his children be given up to utter destruction: in one generation let his name be blotted out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Let the iniquity of his fathers be remembered before the Lord; and let not the sin of his mother be blotted out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Let them be before the Lord continually; and let their memorial be blotted out from the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Because he remembered not to shew mercy, but persecuted the needy and poor man, and that to slay him that was pricked in the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> He loved cursing also, and it shall come upon him; and he took not pleasure in blessing, so it shall be removed far from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Yea, he put on cursing as a garment, and it is come as water into his bowels, and as oil into his bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Let it be to him as a garment which he puts on, and as a girdle with which he girds himself continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> This is the dealing of the Lord with those who falsely accuse me, and of them that speak evil against my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> But thou, O Lord, Lord, deal mercifully with me, for thy name's sake: for thy mercy is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Deliver me, for I am poor and needy; and my heart is troubled within me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> I am removed as a shadow in its going down: I am tossed up and down like locusts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> My knees are weakened through fasting, and my flesh is changed by reason of the want of oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> I became also a reproach to them: when they saw me they shook their heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Help me, O Lord my God; and save me according to thy mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And let them know that this is thy hand; and that thou, Lord, hast wrought it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Let them curse, but thou shalt bless: let them that rise up against me be ashamed, but let thy servant rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Let those that falsely accuse me be clothed with shame, and let them cover themselves with their shame as with a mantle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> I will give thanks to the Lord abundantly with my mouth; and in the midst of many I will praise him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> For he stood on the right hand of the poor, to save me from them that persecute my soul.</span>
