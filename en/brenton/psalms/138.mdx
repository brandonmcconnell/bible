<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 111:1; Psalms 95:3; 1 Corinthians 14:15"> <span className="desc">For the end, a Psalm of David.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Alex. + of Zacharias in the dispersion.">a</a></sup>O Lord, thou hast proved me, and known me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 5:18; Psalms 56:10; Matthew 24:35"> Thou knowest my down-sitting and mine up-rising: thou understandest my thoughts<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. from afar.">b</a></sup>long before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; Psalms 34:4–6; Psalms 18:6"> Thou hast traced my path and my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="q. d. of rushes, lit. rush.">c</a></sup>bed, and hast foreseen all my ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 102:15; Psalms 72:11; Psalms 22:27"> For there is no unrighteous word in my tongue: behold, O Lord, thou hast known all things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 21:5; Revelation 4:11; Revelation 7:12"> the last and the first: thou hast fashioned me, and laid thine hand upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 113:5–6; Kings I 2:7–8; Esaias 57:15"> The knowledge of thee is too wonderful for me; it is very difficult, I cannot attain to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esaias 41:10; Psalms 23:3–4; Psalms 71:20–21"> Whither shall I go from thy Spirit? and whither shall I flee from thy presence?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 1:6; Psalms 57:2; 1 Thessalonians 5:24"> If I should go up to heaven, thou art there: if I should go down to hell, thou art present.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> If I should spread my wings to fly<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Alex. κατ᾿ ὄρθον, toward the dawn.">d</a></sup>straight forward, and sojourn at the extremity of the sea, it would be vain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> for even there thy hand would guide me, and thy right hand would hold me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> When I said, Surely the darkness will cover me; even the night was light in my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Comp. Heb.">e</a></sup>luxury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> For darkness will not be darkness with thee; but night will be light as day: as its darkness, so shall its light be to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> For thou, O Lord, hast possessed my reins; thou hast helped me from my mother's womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> I will give thee thanks; for thou art fearfully wondrous; wondrous are thy works; and my soul knows it well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> My bones which thou madest in secret were not hidden from thee, nor my substance, in the lowest parts of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Thine eyes saw my unwrought substance, and all men shall be written in thy book; they shall be formed by day, though there should for a time be no one among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> But thy friends, O God, have been greatly honoured by me; their rule has been greatly strengthened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> I will number them, and they shall be multiplied beyond the sand; I awake, and am still with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Oh that thou wouldest slay the wicked, O God; depart from me, ye men of blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> For thou wilt say concerning their thought, that they shall take thy cities in vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Have I not hated them, O Lord, that hate thee? and wasted away because of thine enemies?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> I have hated them with perfect hatred; they were counted my enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Prove me, O God, and know my heart; examine me, and know my paths;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> and see if there is any way of iniquity in me, and lead me in an everlasting way.</span>
