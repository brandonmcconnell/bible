<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 141:9; Psalms 140:5; Jeremias 18:22"> For the enemy has persecuted my soul; he has brought my life down to the ground; he has made me to dwell in a dark place, as those that have been long dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremias 30:17; Psalms 88:18; Psalms 31:11"> Therefore my spirit was grieved in me; my heart was troubled within me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 16:5; Psalms 119:57; Lamentations 3:24"> I remembered the days of old; and I meditated on all thy doings: yea, I meditated on the works of thine hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 79:8; Psalms 116:6; Psalms 136:23"> I spread forth my hands to thee; my soul thirsts for thee, as a dry land. Pause.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 143:11; Psalms 146:7; Psalms 7:6–7"> Hear me speedily, O Lord; my spirit has failed; turn not away thy face from me, else I shall be like to them that go down to the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Cause me to hear thy mercy in the morning; for I have hoped in thee; make known to me, O Lord, the way wherein I should walk; for I have lifted up my soul to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Deliver me from mine enemies, O Lord; for I have fled to thee for refuge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Teach me to do thy will; for thou art my God; thy good Spirit shall guide me in the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Alex. as Heb. 'land of uprightness.'">a</a></sup>straight way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Thou shalt quicken me, O Lord, for thy name's sake; in thy righteousness thou shalt bring my soul out of affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And in thy mercy thou wilt destroy mine enemies, and wilt destroy all those that afflict my soul; for I am thy servant.</span>
