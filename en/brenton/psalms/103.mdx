<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 146:1–2; Psalms 57:7–11; John 4:24"> <span className="desc">A Psalm of David.</span>Bless the Lord, O my soul. O Lord my God, thou art very great; thou hast clothed thyself with praise and honour:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esaias 63:7; Psalms 116:12; Psalms 105:5"> who dost robe thyself with light as with a garment; spreading out the heaven as a curtain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 15:26; Esaias 53:5; Jeremias 17:14"> Who covers his chambers with waters; who makes the clouds his chariot; who walks on the wings of the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 56:13; Psalms 71:23; Psalms 103:12"> Who makes his angels spirits, and his ministers a flaming fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esaias 40:31; Psalms 107:9; 1 Timothy 6:17"> Who establishes the earth on her sure foundation: it shall not be moved for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Psalms 12:5; Psalms 72:12"> The deep, as it were a garment, is his covering: the waters shall stand<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, above.">a</a></sup>on the hills.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 33:13; Psalms 147:19; Psalms 78:11"> At thy rebuke they shall flee; at the voice of thy thunder they shall be alarmed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 86:15; Joel 2:13; Exodus 34:6–7"> They go up to the mountains, and down to the plains, to the place which thou hast founded for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esaias 57:16; Psalms 30:5; Michæas 7:18–19"> Thou hast set a bound which they shall not pass, neither shall they turn again to cover the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 9:13; Lamentations 3:22; Nehemiah 9:31"> He sends forth his fountains among the valleys: the waters shall run between the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 57:10; Luke 1:50; Psalms 36:5"> They shall give drink to all the wild beasts of the field: the wild asses shall take of them to quench their thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esaias 43:25; 1 John 1:7; Michæas 7:18"> By them shall the birds of the sky lodge: they shall utter a voice out of the midst of the rocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 11:11–12; Hebrews 12:5–11; Luke 15:21–22"> He waters the mountains from his chambers: the earth shall be satisfied with the fruit of thy works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:7; Job 10:9; Psalms 78:38–39"> He makes grass to grow for the cattle, and green herb for the service of men, to bring bread out of the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 1:24; James 1:10–11; Esaias 51:12"> and wine makes glad the heart of man, to make his face cheerful with oil: and bread strengthens man's heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esaias 40:7; Job 20:9; Job 14:10"> The trees of the plain shall be full of sap; even the cedars of Libanus which he has planted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 100:5; Exodus 20:6; Ephesians 2:4–7"> There the sparrows will build their nests; and the house of the heron takes the lead among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:9; Psalms 132:12; Luke 1:6"> The high mountains are a refuge for the stags, and the rock for the rabbits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 47:2; Philippians 2:9–10; Psalms 9:7"> He appointed the moon for seasons: the sun knows his going down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 1:14; Psalms 148:2; Luke 1:19"> Thou didst make darkness, and it was night; in it all the wild beasts of the forest will be abroad:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings III 22:19; Nehemiah 9:6; Luke 2:13"> even young lions roaring for prey, and to seek meat for themselves from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Esaias 49:13; Psalms 145:10; Esaias 42:10–12"> The sun arises, and they shall be gathered together, and shall lie down in their dens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Man shall go forth to his work, and to his labour till evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> How great are thy works, O Lord! in wisdom hast thou wrought them all: the earth is filled with thy creation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> So is this great and wide sea: there are things creeping innumerable, small animals and great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> There go the ships; and this dragon whom thou hast made to play in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> All wait upon thee, to give them their food in due season.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> When thou hast given it them, they will gather it; and when thou hast opened thine hand, they shall all be filled with good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> But when thou hast turned away thy face, they shall be troubled: thou wilt take away their breath, and they shall fail, and return to their dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Thou shalt send forth thy Spirit, and they shall be created; and thou shalt renew the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Let the glory of the Lord be for ever: the Lord shall rejoice in his works;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> who looks upon the earth, and makes it tremble; who touches the mountains, and they smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> I will sing to the Lord while I live; I will sing praise to my God while I exist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Let my meditation be sweet to him: and I will rejoice in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Let the sinners fail from off the earth, and transgressors, so that they shall be no more. Bless the Lord, O my soul.</span>
</div>
