<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jezekiel 1:1; Jezekiel 3:15; Lamentations 3:48"> <span className="desc">A Psalm for David, of Aggæus and Zacharias.</span>I will give thee thanks, O Lord, with my whole heart; and I will sing psalms to thee before the angels; for thou hast heard all the words of my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esaias 24:8; Jezekiel 26:13; Psalms 33:2"> I will worship toward thy holy temple, and give thanks to thy name, on account of thy mercy and thy truth; for thou hast magnified thy holy name above every thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 21:6; Psalms 9:14; Michæas 3:12"> In whatsoever day I shall call upon thee, hear me speedily; thou shalt abundantly provide me with thy power in my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 8:3; Osee 9:4; Esaias 22:12"> Let all the kings of the earth, O Lord, give thanks unto thee; for they have heard all the words of thy mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 51:50; Zacharias 11:17; Daniel 6:10–11"> And let them sing in the ways of the Lord; for great is the glory of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jezekiel 3:26; Psalms 22:15; Job 29:10"> For the Lord is high, and yet regards the lowly; and he knows high things from afar off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Lamentations 4:21–22; Obdias 1:10–14; Jezekiel 25:12–14"> Though I should walk in the midst of affliction, thou wilt quicken me; thou hast stretched forth thine hands against the wrath of mine enemies, and thy right hand has saved me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 18:6; Jeremias 25:12–14; Esaias 14:4–24"> O Lord, thou shalt recompense them on my behalf: thy mercy, O Lord, endures for ever: overlook not the works of thine hands.</span>
