<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Michæas 7:18; Psalms 32:1; Colossians 2:13"> Preserve my soul, for I am holy; save thy servant, O God, who hopes in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 106:23; Deuteronomy 13:17; Jonas 3:9"> Pity me, O Lord: for to thee will I cry all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 80:3; Lamentations 5:21; Psalms 80:7"> Rejoice the soul of thy servant: for to thee, O Lord, have I lifted up my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 79:5; Psalms 80:4; Psalms 74:1"> For thou, O Lord, art kind, and gentle; and plenteous in mercy to all that call upon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ambacum 3:2; Psalms 80:18; Esaias 57:15"> Give ear to my prayer, O Lord; and attend to the voice of my supplication.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esaias 46:13; Zacharias 2:5; John 7:17"> All nations whom thou hast made shall come, and shall worship before thee, O Lord; and shall glorify thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 3:3; Psalms 89:14; Psalms 100:5"> For thou art great, and doest wonders: thou art the only and the great God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esaias 45:8; John 14:6; 1 John 5:20–21"> Guide me, O Lord, in thy way, and I will walk in thy truth: let my heart rejoice, that I may fear thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 1:17; Psalms 84:11; Psalms 67:6"> I will give thee thanks, O Lord my God, with all my heart; and I will glorify thy name for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 89:14; John 13:34; 2 Corinthians 3:18"> For thy mercy is great toward me; and thou hast delivered my soul from the lowest hell.</span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Look thou upon me, and have mercy upon me: give thy strength to thy servant, and save the son of thine handmaid.</span>
