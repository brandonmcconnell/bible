<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 18:6; Psalms 130:1–2; Psalms 142:2–3"> <span className="desc">A Psalm of David.</span>Bless the Lord, O my soul; and all that is within me, bless his holy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 69:17; Psalms 27:9; Esaias 8:17"> Bless the Lord, O my soul, and forget not all his praises:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 4:14; Psalms 37:20; Job 30:30"> who forgives all thy transgressions, who heals all thy diseases;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esaias 40:7; Psalms 37:2; Psalms 77:3"> who redeems thy life from corruption; who crowns thee with mercy and compassion;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 19:20; Lamentations 4:8; Psalms 6:8"> who satisfies thy desire with good things: so that thy youth shall be renewed like that of the eagle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Sophonias 2:14; Esaias 38:14; Esaias 34:11–15"> The Lord executes mercy and judgment for all that are injured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 77:4; Psalms 38:11; Deuteronomy 28:66–67"> He made known his ways to Moses, his will to the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 26:11; Psalms 69:9–10; Esaias 65:15"> The Lord is compassionate and pitiful, long-suffering, and full of mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 42:3; Psalms 80:5; Michæas 1:10"> He will not be always angry; neither will he be wrathful for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 38:3; Psalms 90:7–9; Psalms 147:6"> He has not dealt with us according to our sins, nor recompensed us according to our iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 14:2; Psalms 109:23; Psalms 144:4"> For as the heaven is high above the earth, the Lord has so increased his mercy toward them that fear him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 135:13; Lamentations 5:19; Exodus 3:15"> As far as the east is from the west, so far has he removed our transgressions from us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Esaias 60:1; Galatians 4:4; Esaias 40:2"> As a father pities his children, the Lord pities them that fear him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:2; Ezra and Nehemiah 7:27; Ezra and Nehemiah 3:1–3"> For he knows our frame: remember that we are dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings III 8:43; Psalms 138:4; Esaias 60:3–22"> As for man, his days are as grass; as a flower of the field, so shall he flourish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esaias 60:1–2; Psalms 147:2; Jeremias 33:7"> For the wind passes over it, and it shall not be; and it shall know its place no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:6; Psalms 22:24; Jeremias 29:11–14"> But the mercy of the Lord is from generation to generation upon them that fear him, and his righteousness to children's children;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 22:30–31; Romans 15:4; Esaias 43:21"> to them that keep his covenant, and remember his commandments to do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:15; Psalms 14:2; Kings III 8:43"> The Lord has prepared his throne in the heaven; and his kingdom rules over all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 79:11; Psalms 146:7; Exodus 3:7"> Bless the Lord, all ye his angels, mighty in strength, who perform his bidding, ready to hearken to the voice of his words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 22:22; Esaias 51:11; Psalms 79:13"> Bless the Lord, all ye his hosts; ye ministers of his that do his will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zacharias 8:20–23; Matthew 24:14; Psalms 72:8–11"> Bless the Lord, all his works, in every place of his dominion: bless the Lord, O my soul.</span>
