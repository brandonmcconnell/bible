<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 82:5; 2 Timothy 2:19; Esaias 58:12"> Every one has spoken vanity to his neighbour: their lips are deceitful, they have spoken with a double heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 103:19; Psalms 18:6; Psalms 2:4"> Let the Lord destroy all the deceitful lips, and the tongue that speaks great words:</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 22:4; Psalms 17:15; 1 Peter 3:12"> The oracles of the Lord are pure oracles; as silver tried in the fire, proved in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. the earth.">b</a></sup>a furnace of earth, purified seven times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Thou, O Lord, shalt keep us, and shalt preserve us, from this generation, and for ever.</span>
