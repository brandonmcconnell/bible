<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esaias 51:4; Esaias 55:3; Psalms 74:1"> <span className="desc">A Psalm for Asaph.</span><br />O God, the heathen are come into thine inheritance; they have polluted thy holy temple; they have made Jerusalem a storehouse of fruits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 13:34–35; Mark 4:34; Matthew 13:11–13"> They have given the dead bodies of thy servants to be food for the birds of the sky, the flesh of thy holy ones for the wild beasts of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 44:1; Exodus 13:14–15; Exodus 12:26–27"> They have shed their blood as water, round about Jerusalem; and there was none to bury them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:19; Psalms 145:4–6; Deuteronomy 4:9"> We are become a reproach to our neighbours, a scorn and derision to them that are round about us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Ephesians 6:4; Deuteronomy 4:45"> How long, O Lord? wilt thou be angry for ever? shall thy jealousy burn like fire?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 102:18; Joel 1:3; Esther 9:28"> Pour out thy wrath upon the heathen that have not known thee, and upon the kingdoms which have not called upon thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:29; Revelation 14:12; 1 John 5:3"> For they have devoured Jacob, and laid his place waste.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 78:37; Exodus 32:9; Kings IV 17:14"> Remember not our old transgressions; let thy tender mercies speedily prevent us; for we are greatly impoverished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:41–44; Kings I 31:1; Psalms 78:57"> Help us, O God our Saviour; for the glory of thy name, O Lord, deliver us; and be merciful to our sins, for thy name's sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:20; Deuteronomy 31:16; Nehemiah 9:26–29"> Lest haply they should say among the heathen, Where is their God? and let the avenging of thy servants' blood that has been shed be known among the heathen before our eyes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 106:13; Deuteronomy 32:18; Psalms 106:21–22"> Let the groaning of the prisoners come in before thee; according to the greatness of thine arm preserve the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i. e. men appointed to die.">a</a></sup>sons of the slain ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 13:22; Jezekiel 30:14; Esaias 19:11"> Repay to our neighbours sevenfold into their bosom their reproach, with which they have reproached thee, O Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Exodus 15:8; Psalms 66:6"> For we are thy people and the sheep of thy pasture; we will give thee thanks for ever; we will declare thy praise throughout all generations.</span>
</div>
