<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 62:1; Psalms 76:11; Psalms 116:17–18"> <span className="desc">For the end, a Song of a Psalm of resurrection.</span><br />Shout unto God, all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esaias 65:24; Jeremias 29:12–13; Psalms 86:9"> O sing praises to his name; give glory to his praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 38:4; Psalms 79:9; Hebrews 9:14"> Say unto God, How awful are thy works! through the greatness of thy power thine enemies shall lie to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 1:4; Psalms 84:4; Psalms 23:6"> Let all the earth worship thee, and sing to thee; let them sing to thy name. Pause.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:21; Psalms 66:3; Revelation 15:3–4"> Come and behold the works of God; he is terrible in his counsels beyond the children of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 93:1; Michæas 6:2; Ambacum 3:6"> Who turns the sea into dry land; they shall go through the river on foot; there shall we rejoice in him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 89:9; Esaias 17:12–13; Psalms 93:3–4"> who by his power<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, has dominion for ever.">a</a></sup>is Lord over the age, his eyes look upon the nations; let not them that provoke him be exalted in themselves. Pause.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 148:3; Psalms 136:8; Psalms 104:20–23"> Bless our God, ye Gentiles, and make the voice of his praise to be heard;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 46:4; Psalms 104:13–15; Psalms 68:9–10"> who quickens my soul in life, and does not suffer my feet to be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 147:8; 1 Corinthians 3:6–7; Deuteronomy 32:2"> For thou, O God, hast proved us; thou hast tried us with fire as silver is tried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joel 2:21–26; Psalms 103:4; Psalms 5:12"> Thou broughtest us into the snare; thou laidest afflictions on our back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 38:26–27; Psalms 104:10–13; Joel 2:22"> Thou didst mount men upon our heads; we went through fire and water; but thou broughtest us out into a place of refreshment.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Esaias 55:12; Acts 14:17; Esaias 35:1–2"> I will go into thine house with whole-burnt-offerings; I will pay thee my vows,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> which my lips framed, and my mouth uttered in my affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> I will offer to thee whole-burnt-sacrifices full of marrow, with incense and rams; I will sacrifice to thee oxen with goats. Pause.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Come, hear, and I will tell, all ye that fear God, how great things he has done for my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> I cried to him with my mouth, and exalted him with my tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> If I have regarded iniquity in my heart, let not the Lord hearken to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Therefore God has hearkened to me; he has attended to the voice of my prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Blessed be God, who has not turned away my prayer, nor his mercy from me.</span>
</div>
