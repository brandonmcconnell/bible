<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 40:13–17; Psalms 71:12; Psalms 143:7"> <span className="desc">By David, a Psalm sung by the sons of Jonadab, and the first that were taken captive.</span><br />O Lord, I have hoped in thee: let me never be put to shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 35:4; Psalms 35:26; John 18:6"> In thy righteousness deliver me and rescue me: incline thine ear to me, and save me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 40:15; Acts 1:18; Jezekiel 26:2"> Be to me a protecting God, and a strong hold to save me: for thou art my fortress and my refuge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25; Psalms 40:16; Psalms 35:27"> Deliver me, O my God, from the hand of the sinner, from the hand of the transgressor and unjust man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 40:17; Psalms 141:1; Psalms 69:29"> For thou art my support, O Lord; O Lord, thou art my hope from my youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> On thee have I been stayed from the womb: from the belly of my mother thou art my protector: of thee is my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. hymn singing.">a</a></sup>praise continually.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> I am become as it were a wonder to many; but thou art my strong helper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Let my mouth be filled with praise, that I may hymn thy glory, and thy majesty all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Cast me not off at the time of old age; forsake me not when my strength fails.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> For mine enemies have spoken against me; and they that lay wait for my soul have taken counsel together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> saying, God has forsaken him: persecute ye and take him; for there is none to deliver him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> O God, go not far from me, O my God, draw nigh to my help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Let those that plot against my soul be ashamed and utterly fail: let those that seek my hurt be clothed with shame and dishonour.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> But I will hope continually, and will praise thee more and more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> My mouth shall declare thy righteousness openly, and thy salvation all the day; for I am not acquainted with the affairs of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> I will go on in the might of the Lord: O Lord, I will make mention of thy righteousness only.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> O God, thou hast taught me from my youth, and until now will I declare thy wonders;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> even until I am old and advanced in years. O God, forsake me not; until I shall have declared thine arm to all the generation that is to come:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> even thy power and thy righteousness, O God, up to the highest heavens, even the mighty works which thou hast done: O God, who is like to thee?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> What afflictions many and sore hast thou shewed me! yet thou didst turn and quicken me, and broughtest me again from the depths of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Thou didst multiply thy righteousness, and didst turn and comfort me, and broughtest me again out of the depths of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> I will also therefore give thanks to thee, O God, because of thy truth, on an instrument of psalmody: I will sing psalms to thee on the harp, O Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> My lips shall rejoice when I sing to thee; and my soul, which thou hast redeemed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Moreover also my tongue shall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Lit. meditate.">b</a></sup>dwell all the day upon thy righteousness; when they shall be ashamed and confounded that seek my hurt.</span>
</div>
</div>
