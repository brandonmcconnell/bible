<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 5:8; Psalms 51:10; Luke 12:32"> <span className="desc">A Psalm of</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. understanding.">a</a></sup><span className="desc">instruction for Asaph.</span>Wherefore hast thou rejected us, O God, for ever? wherefore is thy wrath kindled against the sheep of thy pasture?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 94:18; Kings I 2:9; Psalms 116:8"> Remember thy congregation which thou hast purchased from the beginning; thou didst ransom the rod of thine inheritance; this mount Sion wherein thou hast dwelt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 21:7; Psalms 37:7; Proverbs 23:17"> Lift up thine hands against their pride continually; because of all that the enemy has done wickedly in thy holy places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 21:23–24; Ecclesiastes 2:16; Luke 16:22"> And they that hate thee have boasted in the midst of thy feast; they have set up their standards for signs,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 21:9; Psalms 73:12; Revelation 3:19"> ignorantly as it were in the entrance above;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 109:18; 1 Peter 5:5; Daniel 4:30"> they cut down its doors at once with axes as in a wood of trees; they have broken it down with hatchet and stone cutter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremias 5:28; Psalms 17:10; Job 15:27"> They have burnt thy sanctuary with fire to the ground; they have profaned the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. tabernacle.">b</a></sup>habitation of thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jude 1:16; Psalms 17:10; Psalms 10:2"> They have said in their heart, even all their kindred together, Come, let us abolish the feasts of the Lord from the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 13:6; James 3:6; Exodus 5:2"> We have not seen our signs; there is no longer a prophet; and God will not know us any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 75:8"> How long, O God, shall the enemy reproach? shall the enemy provoke thy name for ever?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Osee 7:2; Sophonias 1:12; Job 22:13–14"> Wherefore turnest thou away thine hand, and thy right hand from the midst of thy bosom for ever?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 52:7; Psalms 49:6; Osee 12:7–8"> But God is our King<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. before the age.">c</a></sup>of old; he has wrought salvation in the midst of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 21:15; Psalms 26:6; Job 34:9"> Thou didst establish the sea, in thy might, thou didst break to pieces the heads of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or, serpents.">d</a></sup>dragons in the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 12:5; Psalms 94:12; Job 7:3–4"> Thou didst break to pieces the heads of the dragon; thou didst give him for meat to the Ethiopian nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:6–7; Malachias 2:8; 1 Peter 2:9"> Thou didst cleave fountains and torrents; thou driedst up<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Heb. rivers of Etham.">e</a></sup>mighty rivers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:17; John 16:18–19; Psalms 36:6"> The day is thine, and the night is thine; thou hast prepared the sun and the moon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 12:20; Psalms 77:13; Ecclesiastes 8:12–13"> Thou hast made all the borders of the earth; thou hast made summer and spring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 35:6; Jeremias 23:12; Deuteronomy 32:35"> Remember this thy creation: an enemy has reproached the Lord, and a foolish people has provoked thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 28:1; Esaias 47:11; Daniel 5:6"> Deliver not to the wild beasts a soul that gives praise to thee: forget not for ever the souls of thy poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 20:8; Psalms 78:65; Psalms 90:5"> Look upon thy covenant: for the dark places of the earth are filled with the habitations of iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Psalms 73:3; Job 16:13"> Let not the afflicted and shamed one be rejected: the poor and needy shall praise thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:18; Psalms 92:6; Psalms 49:10"> Arise, O God, plead thy cause: remember thy reproaches that come from the foolish one all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Esaias 41:13; Psalms 63:8; Matthew 28:20"> Forget not the voice of thy suppliants: let the pride of them that hate thee continually ascend before thee.</span>
