<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ephesians 5:19–20; Psalms 135:1–3; Psalms 33:1–2"> <span className="desc">Alleluia.</span><br />At the going forth of Israel from Egypt, of the house of Jacob from a barbarous people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Daniel 2:20; Revelation 5:13; Psalms 41:13"> Judea became his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, consecrated thing.">a</a></sup>sanctuary, and Israel his dominion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Malachias 1:11; Psalms 18:3; Esaias 49:13"> The sea saw and fled: Jordan was turned back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 8:1; Psalms 99:2; Psalms 97:9"> The mountains skipped like rams, and the hills like lambs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 89:6; Jeremias 10:6; Esaias 40:25"> What ailed thee, O sea, that thou fleddest? and thou Jordan, that thou wast turned back?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 138:6; Esaias 57:15; Psalms 11:4"> Ye mountains, that ye skipped like rams, and ye hills, like lambs?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings I 2:7–8; Luke 1:52–53; Psalms 107:41"> The earth trembled at the presence of the Lord, at the presence of the God of Jacob;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 36:7; Philippians 2:8–11; Psalms 45:16"> who turned the rock into pools of water, and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. sharp rock.">b</a></sup>flint into fountains of water.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 68:6; Genesis 21:5–7; Genesis 30:22–23"> Not to us, O Lord, not to us, but to thy name give glory, because of thy mercy and thy truth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> lest at any time the nations should say, Where is their God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> But our God has done in heaven and on earth, whatsoever he has pleased.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The idols of the nations are silver and gold, the works of men's hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> They have a mouth, but they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. will not.">c</a></sup>cannot speak; they have eyes, but they cannot see:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> they have ears, but they cannot hear; they have noses, but they cannot smell;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> they have hands, but they cannot handle; they have feet, but they cannot walk: they cannot speak through their throat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Let those that make them become like to them, and all who trust in them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The house of Israel trusts in the Lord: he is their helper and defender.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The house of Aaron trusts in the Lord: he is their helper and defender.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> They that fear the Lord trust in the Lord: he is their helper and defender.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The Lord has remembered us, and blessed us: he has blessed the house of Israel, he has blessed the house of Aaron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> He has blessed them that fear the Lord, both small and great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or, increase you.">d</a></sup>add blessings to you and to your children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Blessed are ye of the Lord, who made the heaven and the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> The heaven of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. sing.">e</a></sup>heavens belongs to the Lord: but he has given the earth to the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> The dead shall not praise thee, O Lord, nor any that go down to Hades.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> But we, the living, will bless the Lord, from henceforth and for ever.</span>
</div>
</div>
