<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 103:22; Psalms 145:3; Psalms 96:6"> <span className="desc">Alleluia.</span><br />Give thanks to the Lord, and call upon his name; declare his works among the heathen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esaias 40:22; Daniel 7:9; Esaias 45:12"> Sing to him, yea, sing praises to him: tell forth all his wonderful works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 9:6; Kings II 22:11; Esaias 19:1"> Glory in his holy name: let the heart of them that seek the Lord rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 1:7; Kings IV 2:11; Kings IV 6:17"> Seek ye the Lord, and be strengthened; seek his face continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 26:7; Psalms 24:2; Psalms 33:9"> Remember his wonderful works that he has done; his wonders, and the judgments of his mouth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 7:19; 2 Peter 3:5; Genesis 1:2–10"> ye seed of Abraam, his servants, ye children of Jacob, his chosen ones.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 18:15; Genesis 8:1; Mark 4:39"> He is the Lord our God; his judgments are in all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 8:5; Psalms 33:7"> He has remembered his covenant for ever, the word which he commanded for a thousand generations:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 38:10–11; Genesis 9:11–15; Jeremias 5:22"> which he established as a covenant to Abraam, and he remembered his oath to Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esaias 41:18; Psalms 107:35; Esaias 35:7"> And he established it to Jacob for an ordinance, and to Israel for an everlasting covenant;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 145:16; Job 39:5–8; Psalms 104:13"> saying, To thee will I give the land of Chanaan, the line of your inheritance:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 50:11; Psalms 148:10; Matthew 8:20"> when they were few in number, very few, and sojourners in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 147:8; Jeremias 10:13; Jeremias 14:22"> And they went from nation to nation, and from one kingdom to another people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 136:25; Job 28:5; Psalms 147:8–9"> He suffered no man to wrong them; and he rebuked kings for their sakes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 23:5; Ecclesiastes 10:19; Ecclesiastes 9:7"> saying, Touch not my anointed ones; and do my prophets no harm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 24:6; Psalms 92:2; Psalms 29:5"> Moreover he called for a famine upon the land; he broke the whole support of bread.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 104:12; Leviticus 11:19; Daniel 4:21"> He sent a man before them; Joseph was sold for a slave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 30:26; Job 39:1; Leviticus 11:5"> They<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. humbled.">a</a></sup>hurt his feet with fetters;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, his body, see Heb.">b</a></sup>his soul passed into iron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremias 31:35; Genesis 1:14–18; Psalms 136:7–9"> until the time that his cause came on; the word of the Lord tried him as fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Esaias 45:7; Psalms 74:16; Amos 1:13"> The king sent and loosed him; even the prince of the people, and let him go free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 38:39; Joel 1:20; Psalms 34:10"> He made him lord over his house, and ruler of all his substance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 37:8; Naum 3:17; John 3:20"> to chastise his rulers at his pleasure, and to teach his elders wisdom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 5:12; Ephesians 4:28"> Israel also came into Egypt, and Jacob sojourned in the land of Cham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremias 10:12; Psalms 40:5; Psalms 50:10–12"> And he increased his people greatly, and made them stronger than their enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 69:34; Psalms 95:4–5; Genesis 1:28"> And he turned their heart to hate his people, to deal craftily with his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 107:23; Esaias 27:1; Psalms 74:14"> He sent forth Moses his servant, and Aaron whom he had chosen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 136:25; Psalms 147:9; Job 38:41"> He established among them his signs, and his wonders in the land of Cham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 145:16"> He sent forth darkness, and made it dark; yet they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. embittered.">c</a></sup>rebelled against his words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 34:14–15; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Psalms 146:4"> He turned their waters into blood, and slew their fish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 33:4; Jezekiel 37:9; Revelation 21:5"> Their land produced frogs abundantly, in the chambers of their kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 1:31; 2 Timothy 4:18; Ephesians 3:21"> He spoke, and the dog-fly came, and lice in all their coasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 144:5; Psalms 97:4–5; Exodus 19:18"> He turned their rain into hail, and sent flaming fire in their land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 63:4; Psalms 146:2; Psalms 145:1–2"> And he smote their vines and their fig-trees; and broke every tree of their coast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ambacum 3:17–18; Philippians 4:4; Psalms 1:2"> He spoke, and the locust came, and caterpillars innumerable,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 103:22–1; Psalms 37:38; Psalms 106:48"> and devoured all the grass in their land, and devoured the fruit of their ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> He smote also every first-born of their land, the first-fruits of all their labour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And he brought them out with silver and gold; and there was not a feeble one among their tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Egypt rejoiced at their departing; for the fear of them fell upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> He spread out a cloud for a covering to them, and fire to give them light by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> They asked, and the quail came, and he satisfied them with the bread of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> He clave the rock, and the waters flowed; rivers ran in dry places.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> For he remembered his holy word, which he promised to Abraam his servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> And he brought out his people with exultation, and his chosen with joy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> and gave them the lands of the heathen; and they inherited the labours of the people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> that they might keep his ordinances, and diligently seek his law.</span>
</div>
</div>
