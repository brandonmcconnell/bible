<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 4:6; 2 Corinthians 4:6; Psalms 4:1"> <span className="desc">For the end, a Psalm of a Song by David.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 2:11; Psalms 98:2–3; Acts 18:25"> Let God arise, and let his enemies be scattered; and let them that hate him flee from before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 67:5; Psalms 74:21; Psalms 45:17"> As smoke vanishes, let them vanish: as wax melts before the fire, so let the sinners perish from before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 98:9; Romans 15:10–11; Psalms 9:8"> But let the righteous rejoice; let them exult before God: let them be delighted with joy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 67:3; Matthew 6:9–10"> Sing to God, sing praises to his name: make a way for him that rides upon the west (the Lord is his name) and exult before him. They shall be troubled before the face of him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 26:4; Exodus 3:15; Osee 2:21–22"> who is the father of the orphans, and judge of the widows: such is God in his holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 22:27; Acts 13:26; Esaias 45:22"> God settles the solitary in a house; leading forth prisoners mightily, also them that act provokingly, even them that dwell in tombs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> O God, when thou wentest forth before thy people, when thou wentest through the wilderness; Pause:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> the earth quaked, yea, the heavens dropped water at the presence of the God of Sina, at the presence of the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> O God, thou wilt grant to thine inheritance a gracious rain; for it was weary, but thou didst refresh it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See the Hebrew.">a</a></sup>creatures dwell in it: thou hast in thy goodness prepared for the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The Lord God will give a word to them that preach it in a great company.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The king of the forces of the beloved, of the beloved, will even grant them for the beauty of the house to divide the spoils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Even if ye should lie among the lots, ye shall have the wings of a dove covered with silver, and her breast with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Lit. greenness of gold.">b</a></sup>yellow gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> When the heavenly One scatters kings upon it, they shall be made snow-white in Selmon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The mountain of God is a rich mountain; a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. curdled like cheese.">c</a></sup>swelling mountain, a rich mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Wherefore do ye conceive evil, ye swelling mountains? this is the mountain which God has delighted to dwell in; yea, the Lord will dwell in it for ever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The chariots of God are ten thousand fold, thousands of rejoicing ones: the Lord is among them, in Sina, in the holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. having gone up.">d</a></sup>Thou art gone up on high, thou hast led captivity captive, thou hast received gifts for man, yea, for they were rebellious, that thou mightest dwell among them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Blessed be the Lord God, blessed be the Lord daily; and the God of our salvation shall prosper us. Pause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Our God is the God of salvation; and to the Lord belong the issues from death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> But God shall crush the heads of his enemies; the hairy crown of them that go on in their trespasses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> The Lord said, I will bring again from Basan, I will bring my people again through the depths of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> That thy foot may be dipped in blood, and the tongue of thy dogs be stained with that of thine enemies.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Thy goings, O God, have been seen; the goings of my God, the king, in the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> The princes went first, next before the players on instruments, in the midst of damsels playing on timbrels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Praise God in the congregations, the Lord from the fountains of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> There is Benjamin the younger one in ecstasy, the princes of Juda their rulers, the princes of Zabulon, the princes of Nephthali.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> O God, command thou thy strength: strengthen, O God, this which thou hast wrought in us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Because of thy temple at Jerusalem shall kings bring presents to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Rebuke the wild beasts of the reed: let the crowd of bulls with the heifers of the nations be rebuked, so that they who have been proved with silver may not be shut out: scatter thou the nations that wish for wars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Ambassadors shall arrive out of Egypt; Ethiopia shall hasten to stretch out her hand readily to God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Sing to God, ye kingdoms of the earth; sing psalms to the Lord. Pause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Sing to God that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. mounts, or, has mounted.">e</a></sup>rides on the heaven of heaven, eastward: lo, he will utter a mighty sound with his voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Give ye glory to God: his excellency is over Israel, and his power is in the clouds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> God is wonderful<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or, among his holy ones.">f</a></sup>in his holy places, the God of Israel: he will give power and strength to his people: blessed be God.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
