<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 98:4; Luke 19:37; Psalms 32:11"> <span className="desc">A Psalm of David.</span>I will sing to thee, O Lord, of mercy and judgment; I will sing a psalm,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 4:4; Psalms 71:23; Psalms 95:2"> and I will be wise in a blameless way. When wilt thou come to me? I walked in the innocence of my heart, in the midst of my house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 95:6–7; Psalms 46:10; Psalms 119:73"> I have not set before mine eyes any unlawful thing; I have hated transgressors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 3:16–17; Hebrews 13:15; Psalms 103:1–2"> A perverse heart has not cleaved to me; I have not known an evil man, forasmuch as he turns away from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 106:1; Psalms 107:1; Psalms 86:5"> Him that privily speaks against his neighbour, him have I driven from me: he that is proud in look and insatiable in heart,—with him I have not eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Mine eyes shall be upon the faithful of the land, that they may dwell with me: he that walked in a perfect way, the same ministered to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> The proud doer dwelt not in the midst of my house; the unjust speaker prospered not in my sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Early did I slay all the sinners of the land, that I might destroy out of the city of the Lord all that work iniquity.</span>
