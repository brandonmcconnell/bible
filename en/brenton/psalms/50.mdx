<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 113:3; Joshua 22:22; Matthew 25:32"> <span className="desc">For the end, a Psalm of David,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 48:2; Psalms 80:1; Revelation 21:23"> <span className="desc">when Nathan the prophet came to him, when he had gone in to Bersabee.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 7:10; Numbers 16:35; Leviticus 10:2"> Have mercy upon me, O God, according to thy great mercy; and according to the multitude of thy compassions blot out my transgression.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Esaias 1:2; Deuteronomy 31:28"> Wash me thoroughly from mine iniquity, and cleanse me from my sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 26:28; Exodus 24:3–8; Hebrews 13:20"> For I am conscious of mine iniquity; and my sin is continually before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 97:6; Psalms 75:7; Psalms 89:5"> Against thee only have I sinned, and done evil before thee: that thou mightest be justified in thy sayings, and mightest overcome when thou art judged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 20:2; Psalms 81:8; Jeremias 2:9"> For, behold, I was conceived in iniquities, and in sins did my mother conceive me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esaias 1:11–31; Hebrews 10:4–10; Osee 6:6"> For, behold, thou lovest truth: thou hast manifested to me the secret and hidden things of thy wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Michæas 6:6–8; Hebrews 10:4–6; Psalms 69:31"> Thou shalt sprinkle me with hyssop, and I shall be purified: thou shalt wash me, and I shall be made whiter than snow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 27:5–6; Psalms 8:6–8; Psalms 104:14"> Thou shalt cause me to hear gladness and joy: the afflicted bones shall rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 1:20–22; Matthew 6:26; Luke 12:24"> Turn away thy face from my sins, and blot out all mine iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 19:5; Deuteronomy 10:14; Psalms 24:1–2"> Create in me a clean heart, O God; and renew a right spirit in my inward parts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Cast me not away from thy presence; and remove not thy Holy Spirit from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15; Psalms 56:12; Deuteronomy 23:21"> Restore to me the joy of thy salvation: establish me with thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. governing.">a</a></sup>directing Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 107:28; Psalms 91:15; Psalms 34:3–4"> Then will I teach transgressors thy ways; and ungodly men shall turn to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esaias 29:13; Psalms 78:36–38; Romans 2:17–24"> Deliver me from blood-guiltiness, O God, the God of my salvation: and my tongue shall joyfully declare thy righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:26; Proverbs 12:1; 2 Timothy 4:3–4"> O Lord, thou shalt open my lips; and my mouth shall declare thy praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 1:32; 1 Timothy 5:22; Hebrews 13:4"> For if thou desiredst sacrifice, I would have given it: thou wilt not take pleasure in whole-burnt-offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Psalms 52:2–4; Psalms 55:21"> Sacrifice to God is a broken spirit: a broken and humbled heart God will not despise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 10:21; Proverbs 10:18; Leviticus 19:16"> Do good, O Lord, to Sion in thy good pleasure; and let the walls of Jerusalem be built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 2:4–5; Proverbs 29:1; Esaias 57:11"> Then shalt thou be pleased with a sacrifice of righteousness,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Properly, a heave or wave-offfering.">b</a></sup>offering, and whole-burnt-sacrifices: then shall they offer calves upon thine altar.</span>
