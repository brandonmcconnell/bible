<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 10:11; Psalms 25:2; Psalms 71:1–3"> <span className="desc">A Psalm of</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. for or, of.">a</a></sup><span className="desc">instruction by David.</span><br />Blessed are they whose transgressions are forgiven, and whose sins are covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 71:2–3; Psalms 102:2; Psalms 86:1"> Blessed is the man to whom the Lord will not impute sin, and in whose mouth there is no guile.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 143:10–11; Luke 1:79; Psalms 79:9"> Because I kept silence, my bones waxed old, from my crying all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 25:15; Psalms 140:5; Psalms 19:14"> For day and night thy hand was heavy upon me: I became thoroughly miserable while a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Heb.">b</a></sup>thorn was fastened in me. Pause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 23:46; Acts 7:59; Deuteronomy 32:4"> I acknowledged my sin, and hid not mine iniquity: I said, I will confess mine iniquity to the Lord against myself; and thou forgavest the ungodliness of my heart. Pause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jonas 2:8; Psalms 24:4; Jeremias 10:15"> Therefore shall every holy one pray to thee in a fit time: only in the deluge of many waters they shall not come nigh to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esaias 49:13; Esaias 63:9; Psalms 119:153"> Thou art my refuge from the affliction that encompasses me; my joy, to deliver me from them that have compassed me. Pause.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 36:16; Deuteronomy 32:30; Psalms 18:19"> I will instruct thee and guide thee in this way wherein thou shalt go: I will fix mine eyes upon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 6:7; Psalms 88:9; Psalms 73:26"> Be ye not as horse and mule, which have no understanding:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Alex. ἄγξεις.">c</a></sup>but thou must constrain their jaws with bit and curb, lest they should come nigh to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 88:15; Psalms 32:3–4; Psalms 38:3"> Many are the scourges of the sinner: but him that hopes in the Lord mercy shall compass about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 38:11; Psalms 88:8; Psalms 88:18"> Be glad in the Lord, and exult, ye righteous: and glory, all ye that are upright in heart.</span>
