<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 28:7; Psalms 7:8; Proverbs 20:7"> <span className="desc">A Psalm of David, before he was anointed.</span>The Lord is my light and my Saviour; whom shall I fear? the Lord is the defender of my life; of whom shall I be afraid?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 139:23–24; Psalms 7:9; Psalms 17:3"> When evil-doers drew nigh against me to eat up my flesh, my persecutors and mine enemies, they fainted and fell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 1:7; Kings IV 20:3; Ephesians 4:32–2"> Though an army should set itself in array against me, my heart shall not be afraid: though war should rise up against me, in this am I confident.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremias 15:17; Psalms 1:1; Psalms 119:115"> One thing have I asked of the Lord, this will I earnestly seek: that I should dwell in the house of the Lord all the days of my life, that I should behold the fair beauty of the Lord, and survey his temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 31:6; Psalms 101:3–8; Psalms 139:21–22"> For in the day of mine afflictions he hid me in his tabernacle: he sheltered me in the secret of his tabernacle; he set me up on a rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 73:13; Esaias 1:16–18; Exodus 30:19–20"> And now, behold, he has lifted up mine head over mine enemies: I went round and offered in his tabernacle the sacrifice of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. shouting.">a</a></sup>joy; I will sing,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, and play on a psaltery.">b</a></sup>even sing psalms to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 9:1; Psalms 71:17–19; Psalms 145:5"> Hear, O Lord, my voice which I have uttered aloud: pity me, and hearken to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 2:14–17; Psalms 27:4–6; Psalms 84:1–2"> My heart said to thee, I have diligently sought thy face: thy face, O Lord, I will seek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 22:14–15; Psalms 139:19; Matthew 25:44"> Turn not thy face away from me, turn not thou away from thy servant in anger: be thou my helper, forsake me not; and, O God my Saviour, overlook me not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Exodus 23:8; Psalms 52:2"> For my father and my mother have forsaken me, but the Lord has taken me to himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 26:1; Psalms 69:18; 1 Peter 1:18–19"> Teach me, O Lord, in thy way, and guide me in a right path, because of mine enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 27:11; Psalms 111:1; Psalms 40:2"> Deliver me not over to the desire of them that afflict me; for unjust witnesses have risen up against me, and injustice has lied within herself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> I believe that I shall see the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. good things.">c</a></sup>goodness of the Lord in the land of the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Wait on the Lord: be of good courage, and let thy heart be strengthened: yea, wait on the Lord.</span>
