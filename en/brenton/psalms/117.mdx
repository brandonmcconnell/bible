<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 15:4; Romans 15:11; Psalms 67:3"> <span className="desc">Alleluia.</span><br />Give thanks to the Lord; for he is good: for his mercy endures for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esaias 25:1; Psalms 103:11; Psalms 89:1"> Let now the house of Israel say, that he is good: for his mercy endures for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Let now the house of Aaron say, that he is good: for his mercy endures for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Let now all that fear the Lord say, that he is good: for his mercy endures for ever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> I called on the Lord out of affliction: and he hearkened to me, so as to bring me into a wide place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> The Lord is my helper; and I will not fear what man shall do to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> The Lord is my helper; and I shall see my desire upon mine enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> It is better to trust in the Lord than to trust in man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> It is better to hope in the Lord, than to hope in princes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> All nations compassed me about: but in the name of the Lord I repulsed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> They completely compassed me about: but in the name of the Lord I repulsed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> They compassed me about as bees do a honeycomb, and they burst into flame as fire among thorns: but in the name of the Lord I repulsed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> I was thrust, and sorely shaken, that I might fall: but the Lord helped me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The Lord is my strength and my song, and is become my salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The voice of exultation and salvation is in the tabernacles of the righteous: the right hand of the Lord has wrought mightily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The right hand of the Lord has exalted me: the right hand of the Lord has wrought powerfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> I shall not die, but live, and recount the works of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The Lord has chastened me sore: but he has not given me up to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Open to me the gates of righteousness: I will go into them, and give praise to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> This is the gate of the Lord: the righteous shall enter by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> I will give thanks to thee; because thou hast heard me, and art become my salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The stone which the builders rejected, the same is become the head of the corner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> This has been done of the Lord; and it is wonderful in our eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> This is the day which the Lord has made: let us exult and rejoice in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> O Lord, save now: O Lord, send now prosperity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Blessed is he that comes in the name of the Lord: we have blessed you out of the house of the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> God is the Lord, and he has shined upon us: celebrate the feast with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Possibly, the multitude, q. d. cœtu frequenti.">a</a></sup>thick branches, binding the victims even to the horns of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Thou art my God, and I will give thee thanks: thou art my God, and I will exalt thee. I will give thanks to thee, for thou hast heard me, and art become my salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Give thanks to the Lord; for he is good: for his mercy endures for ever.</span>
</div>
</div>
