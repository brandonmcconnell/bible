<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 31:1–3; Psalms 25:2–3; 1 Peter 2:6"> <span className="desc">For Solomon.</span>O God, give thy judgment to the king, and thy righteousness to the king's son;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 31:1; Psalms 17:6; Psalms 116:1–2"> that he may judge thy people with righteousness, and thy poor with judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 18:2; Psalms 91:9; Proverbs 18:10"> Let the mountains and the hills raise peace to thy people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 140:1–4; Psalms 59:1–2; Kings II 17:1–2"> he shall judge the poor of the people in righteousness, and save the children of the needy; and shall bring low the false accuser.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 17:7; Psalms 39:7; Psalms 119:81"> And he shall continue as long as the sun, and before the moon for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 22:9–10; Jeremias 1:5; Psalms 139:15–16"> He shall come down as rain upon a fleece; and as drops falling upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esaias 8:18; 1 Corinthians 4:9; Acts 4:13"> In his days shall righteousness spring up; and abundance of peace till the moon be removed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 35:28; Psalms 146:2; Psalms 104:1"> And he shall have dominion from sea to sea, and from the river to the ends of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esaias 46:4; 2 Timothy 1:12; Psalms 71:18"> The Ethiopians shall fall down before him; and his enemies shall lick the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 83:3; Psalms 56:6; Jeremias 20:10"> The kings of Tharsis, and the isles, shall bring presents: the kings of the Arabians and Saba shall offer gifts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 7:2; Psalms 3:2; Matthew 27:46"> And all kings shall worship him; all the Gentiles shall serve him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 35:22; Psalms 38:21–22; Psalms 70:5"> For he has delivered the poor from the oppressor; and the needy who had no helper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremias 20:11; Psalms 71:24; Psalms 35:26"> He shall spare the poor and needy, and shall deliver the souls of the needy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 130:7; 1 John 3:3; Psalms 71:6"> He shall redeem their souls from usury and injustice: and their name shall be precious before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 40:5; Psalms 35:28; Psalms 71:8"> And he shall live, and there shall be given him of the gold of Arabia: and men shall pray for him continually; and all the day shall they praise him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Psalms 51:14; Philippians 3:9"> There shall be an establishment on the earth on the tops of the mountains: the fruit thereof shall be exalted above Libanus, and they of the city shall flourish as grass of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 71:5; Psalms 119:9; Psalms 119:102"> Let his name be blessed for ever: his name shall endure<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. before.">a</a></sup>longer than the sun: and all the tribes of the earth shall be blessed in him: all nations shall call him blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Esaias 46:4; Psalms 71:9; Psalms 78:4"> Blessed is the Lord God of Israel, who alone does wonders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 35:10; Psalms 36:5–6; Psalms 57:10"> And blessed is his glorious name for ever, even for ever and ever: and all the earth shall be filled with his glory. So be it, so be it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 138:7; Psalms 60:3; Psalms 86:13"> <span className="desc">The hymns of David the son of Jessæ are ended.</span></span>
