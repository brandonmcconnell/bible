<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 98:4; Jeremias 31:7; Psalms 106:47"> <span className="desc">A Psalm of</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A song.">a</a></sup><span className="desc">praise for the sons of Core on the second day of the week.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Malachias 1:14; Psalms 99:3; Psalms 95:3"> Great is the Lord, and greatly to be praised in the city of our God, in his holy mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 18:47; 1 Corinthians 15:25; Psalms 81:14"> The city of the great King is well planted on the mountains of Sion, with the joy of the whole earth, on the sides of the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 1:4; Amos 6:8; Naum 2:2"> God is known in her palaces, when he undertakes to help her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 68:33; Psalms 24:7–10; Numbers 23:21"> For, behold, the kings of the earth were assembled, they came together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 25:34; Psalms 68:4; Esaias 33:22"> They saw, and so they wondered: they were troubled, they were moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zacharias 14:9; Colossians 3:16; Psalms 47:8"> Trembling took hold on them: there were the pangs as of a woman in travail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Chronicles I 16:31; Revelation 19:6; Psalms 99:1"> Thou wilt break the ships of Tharsis with a vehement wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:18; Genesis 49:10; Psalms 72:7–9"> As we have heard, so have we also seen, in the city of the Lord of hosts, in the city of our God: God has founded it for ever. Pause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> We have thought of thy mercy, O God, in the midst of thy people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> According to thy name, O God, so is also thy praise to the ends of the earth: thy right hand is full of righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Let mount Sion rejoice, let the daughters of Judæa exult, because of thy judgments, O Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Go round about Sion, and encompass her: tell ye her towers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Mark ye well her strength, and observe her palaces; that ye may tell the next generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> For this is our God for ever and ever: he will be our guide for evermore.</span>
