<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 26:4–5; Proverbs 4:14–15; Proverbs 13:20"> Blessed is the man who has not walked in the counsel of the ungodly, and has not stood in the way of sinners, and has not sat in the seat of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. pestilent.">a</a></sup>evil men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 1:8; Psalms 119:92; Psalms 119:11"> But his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. will.">b</a></sup>pleasure is in the law of the Lord; and in his law will he meditate day and night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 17:8; Jezekiel 47:12; Psalms 92:14"> And he shall be as a tree planted by the brooks of waters, which shall yield its fruit in its season, and its leaf shall not fall off; and whatsoever he shall do shall be prospered.</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 5:5; Matthew 25:46; Malachias 3:18"> Therefore the ungodly shall not rise in judgment, nor sinners in the counsel of the just.</span>
