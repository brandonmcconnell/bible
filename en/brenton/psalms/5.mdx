<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 119:147; Psalms 88:13; Psalms 130:6"> Attend to the voice of my supplication, my King, and my God: for to thee, O Lord, will I pray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 11:5; Hebrews 12:14; Malachias 2:17"> In the morning thou shalt hear my voice: in the morning will I wait upon thee, and will look up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 11:5; Psalms 1:5; Osee 9:15"> For thou art not a God that desires iniquity; neither shall the worker of wickedness dwell with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 55:23; Revelation 21:8; Revelation 22:15"> Neither shall the transgressors continue in thy sight: thou hatest, O Lord, all them that work iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 132:7; Psalms 138:2; Hebrews 4:16"> Thou wilt destroy all that speak falsehood: the Lord abhors the bloody and deceitful man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 27:11; Psalms 25:4–5; Proverbs 3:5–6"> But I will enter into thine house in the multitude of thy mercy: I will worship in thy fear toward thy holy temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 3:13; Jeremias 9:3–6; Psalms 62:4"> Lead me, O Lord, in thy righteousness because of mine enemies; make my way plain before thy face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esaias 63:10; Psalms 140:9–10; Kings II 17:14"> For there is no truth in their mouth; their heart is vain; their throat is an open sepulchre; with their tongues they have used deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 8:28; Revelation 19:1–7; Psalms 68:3"> Judge them, O God; let them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. fall from, or, by reason of.">b</a></sup>fail of their counsels: cast them out according to the abundance of their ungodliness; for they have provoked thee, O Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 84:11; Psalms 32:10; Psalms 115:13"> But let all that trust on thee be glad in thee: they shall exult for ever, and thou shalt dwell among them; and all that love thy name shall rejoice in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> For thou, Lord, shalt bless the righteous: thou hast<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Lit. crowned.">c</a></sup>compassed us as with a shield of favour.</span>
