<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 103:22–1; Psalms 103:1; Psalms 104:35"> <span className="desc">Alleluia, a Psalm of Aggæus and Zacharias.</span><br />Praise ye the Lord: for psalmody is a good thing; let praise be sweetly sung to our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 63:4; Psalms 104:33; Psalms 71:14–15"> The Lord builds up Jerusalem; and he will gather together the dispersed of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 118:8–9; Jeremias 17:5–6; Esaias 2:22"> He heals the broken in heart, and binds up their wounds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:7; Psalms 104:29; Genesis 2:7"> He numbers the multitudes of stars; and calls them all by names.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 17:7–8; Psalms 144:15; Psalms 71:5"> Great is our Lord, and great is his strength; and his understanding is infinite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 20:11; Revelation 14:7; Psalms 136:5–6"> The Lord lifts up the meek; but brings sinners down to the ground.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 103:6; Psalms 68:6; Esaias 61:1"> Begin the song with thanksgiving to the Lord; sing praises on the harp to our God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 11:7; Psalms 145:14; Matthew 9:30"> who covers the heaven with clouds, who prepares rain for the earth, who causes grass to spring up on the mountains, &#91;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Ps 103. 14.">a</a></sup>and green herb for the service of men;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 68:5; Psalms 147:6; Deuteronomy 10:18–19"> and gives to cattle their food, and to the young ravens that call upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 10:16; Exodus 15:18; Revelation 11:15"> He will not take pleasure in the strength of a horse; neither is he well-pleased with the legs of a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The Lord takes pleasure in them that fear him, and in all that hope in his mercy.</span>
