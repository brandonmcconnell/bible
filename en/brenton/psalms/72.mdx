<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremias 23:5–6; Chronicles I 22:12–13; Esaias 11:2"> <span className="desc">A Psalm for Asaph.</span>How good is God to Israel, to the upright in heart!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esaias 32:1; Psalms 72:12–14; Revelation 19:11"> But my feet were almost overthrown; my goings very nearly slipped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Esaias 52:7; Psalms 96:11–13; Psalms 72:16"> For I was jealous of the transgressors, beholding the tranquillity of sinners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esaias 11:4; Jezekiel 34:15–16; Psalms 109:31"> For there is no sign of reluctance in their death: and they have firmness under their affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 89:36–37; Psalms 72:17; Daniel 7:14"> They are not in the troubles of other men; and they shall not be scourged with other men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Osee 6:3; Deuteronomy 32:2; Kings II 23:4"> Therefore pride has possessed them; they have clothed themselves with their injustice and ungodliness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esaias 2:4; Malachias 4:2; Psalms 92:12"> Their injustice shall go forth as out of fatness: they have fulfilled their intention.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 23:31; Zacharias 9:10; Psalms 80:11"> They have taken counsel and spoken in wickedness: they have uttered unrighteousness loftily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esaias 49:23; Michæas 7:17; Luke 19:27"> They have set their mouth against heaven, and their tongue has gone through upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esaias 60:6; Psalms 68:29; Esaias 49:7"> Therefore shall my people return hither: and full days shall be found<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. in.">a</a></sup>with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 86:9; Esaias 49:22–23; Revelation 11:15"> And they said, How does God know? and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. if there is.">b</a></sup>is there knowledge in the Most High?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 29:12; Revelation 3:17–18; Psalms 102:17"> Behold, these are the sinners, and they that prosper always: they have possessed wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jezekiel 34:16; Psalms 109:31; Matthew 5:3"> And I said, Verily in vain have I justified my heart, and washed my hands in innocency.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 116:15; Revelation 19:2; Psalms 69:18"> For I was plagued all the day, and my reproof was every morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings III 10:14; Revelation 5:8–14; Jude 1:25"> If I said, I will speak thus; behold, I should have broken covenant with the generation of thy children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 7:9; Psalms 92:12–14; Esaias 30:23"> And I undertook to understand this, but it is too hard for me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Esaias 7:14; Matthew 1:23; Psalms 89:36"> until I go into the sanctuary of God; and so understand the latter end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 41:13; Psalms 106:48; Chronicles I 29:10"> Surely thou hast appointed judgments to them because of their crafty dealings: thou hast cast them down when they were lifted up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 14:21; Matthew 6:10; Psalms 41:13"> How have they become desolate! suddenly they have failed: they have perished because of their iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings II 23:1; Job 31:40; Jeremias 51:64"> As the dream of one awakening, O Lord, in thy city thou wilt despise their image.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> For my heart has rejoiced, and my reins have been<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. changed.">c</a></sup>gladdened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> But I was vile and knew not: I became brutish before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Yet I am continually with thee: thou hast holden my right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Thou hast guided me by thy counsel, and thou hast taken me to thyself with glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> For what have I in heaven but thee? and what have I desired upon the earth beside thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> My heart and my flesh have failed: but God is the strength of my heart, and God is my portion for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> For, behold, they that remove themselves far from thee shall perish: thou hast destroyed every one that goes a whoring from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> But it is good for me to cleave close to God, to put my trust in the Lord; that I may proclaim all thy praises in the gates of the daughter of Sion.</span>
