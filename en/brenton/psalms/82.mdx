<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 11:3; Michæas 3:1; Romans 1:28"> They have said, Come, and let us utterly destroy them out of the nation; and let the name of Israel be remembered no more at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 10:34–36; Psalms 82:1; Exodus 22:28"> For they have taken counsel together with one consent: they have made a confederacy against thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jezekiel 31:14; Psalms 49:12; Job 21:32"> even the tents of the Idumeans, and the Ismaelites; Moab, and the Agarenes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 11:15; Psalms 2:8; Psalms 96:13"> Gebal, and Ammon, and Amalec; the Philistines also, with them that dwell at Tyre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Yea, Assur too is come with them: they have become a help to the children of Lot. Pause.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Do thou to them as to Madiam, and to Sisera; as to Jabin at the brook of Kison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> They were utterly destroyed at Aendor: they became as dung for the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Make their princes as Oreb and Zeb, and Zebee and Salmana; even all their princes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> who said, Let us take to ourselves the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Alex. sanctuary.">a</a></sup>altar of God as an inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> O my God, make them as a wheel; as stubble before the face of the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> As fire which shall burn up a wood, as the flame may consume the mountains;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> so shalt thou persecute them with thy tempest, and trouble them in thine anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Fill their faces with dishonour; so shall they seek thy name, O Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Let them be ashamed and troubled for evermore; yea, let them be confounded and destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And let them know that thy name is Lord; that thou alone art Most High over all the earth.</span>
