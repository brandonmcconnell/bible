<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esaias 26:3–4; Psalms 56:1; Psalms 31:23"> <span className="desc">A Prayer of David.</span>Hearken, O Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, to my righteous plea, etc.">a</a></sup>of my righteousness, attend to my petition; give ear to my prayer not uttered with deceitful lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 73:25; Psalms 31:14; Psalms 89:26"> Let my judgment come forth from thy presence; let mine eyes behold righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 12:26; Hebrews 6:10; Proverbs 13:20"> Thou hast proved mine heart; thou hast visited me by night; thou hast tried me as with fire, and unrighteousness has not been found in me: I am purposed that my mouth shall not speak amiss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 23:13; Joshua 23:7; Psalms 32:10"> As for the works of men, by the words of thy lips I have<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Lit. I have observed hard ways.">b</a></sup>guarded myself from hard ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 73:26; Psalms 142:5; Lamentations 3:24"> Direct my steps in thy paths, that my steps slip not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremias 3:19; Ephesians 1:18; Psalms 21:1–3"> I have cried, for thou heardest me, O God: incline thine ear to me, and hearken to my words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 73:24; Esaias 50:4; Psalms 77:6"> Shew the marvels of thy mercies, thou that savest them that hope in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 62:6; Acts 2:25–28; Psalms 121:5"> Keep me as the apple of the eye from those that resist thy right hand: thou shalt screen me by the covering of thy wings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 2:26; Psalms 30:12; Psalms 57:8"> from the face of the ungodly that have afflicted me: mine enemies have compassed about my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 2:27–31; Acts 13:35–38; Psalms 49:15"> They have enclosed themselves with their own fat: their mouth has spoken pride.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 2:28; Jude 1:24; Psalms 36:7–8"> They have now cast me out and compassed me round about: they have set their eyes so as to bow them down to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> They laid wait for me as a lion ready for prey, and like a lion's whelp dwelling in secret places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Arise, O Lord, prevent them, and cast them down: deliver my soul from the ungodly: draw thy sword,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> because of the enemies of thine hand: O Lord, destroy them from the earth; scatter them in their life, though their belly has been filled with thy hidden treasures: they have been satisfied with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Alex. children.">c</a></sup>uncleanness, and have left the remnant of their possessions to their babes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> But I shall appear in righteousness before thy face: I shall be satisfied when thy glory appears.</span>
