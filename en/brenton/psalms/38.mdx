<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 6:2; Psalms 51:8; Psalms 31:9–10"> I was dumb, and humbled myself, and kept silence from good words; and my grief was renewed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 40:12; Ezra and Nehemiah 9:6; Matthew 11:28"> My heart grew hot within me, and a fire would kindle in my meditation: I spoke with my tongue,</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 30:28; Psalms 35:14; Psalms 42:9"> Behold, thou hast made my days<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Alex. a span long.">a</a></sup>old; and my existence is as nothing before thee: nay, every man living is altogether vanity. Pause.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 3:24; Psalms 32:3; Job 30:28"> And now what is my expectation? is it not the Lord? and my ground of hope is with thee. Pause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 10:17; Psalms 6:6; 2 Corinthians 5:2"> Deliver me from all my transgressions: thou hast made me a reproach to the foolish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 6:7; Psalms 88:9; Psalms 69:3"> I was<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. made dumb.">c</a></sup>dumb, and opened not my mouth; for thou art he that made me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 31:11; Job 19:13–17; Luke 23:49"> Remove thy scourges from me: I have fainted by reason of the strength of thine hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 35:20; Psalms 140:5; Kings II 17:1–3"> Thou chastenest man with rebukes for iniquity, and thou makest his life to consume away like a spider's web: nay, every man is disquieted in vain. Pause.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 5:13; John 8:6; Mark 15:3–5"> Spare me, that I may be refreshed, before I depart, and be no more.</span>
