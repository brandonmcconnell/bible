<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 138:1; Psalms 149:1; Psalms 106:1"> <span className="desc">Alleluia.</span>Blessed is the man that fears the Lord: he will delight greatly in his commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 104:24; Proverbs 24:14; Psalms 139:14"> His seed shall be mighty in the earth: the generation of the upright shall be blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 145:4–5; Psalms 145:10–12; Revelation 5:12–14"> Glory and riches shall be in his house; and his righteousness endures for evermore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 103:8; Esaias 63:7; Psalms 145:8"> To the upright light has sprung up in darkness: he is pitiful, and merciful, and righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 105:8; Luke 1:72; Matthew 6:26–33"> The good man is he that pities and lends: he will direct his affairs with judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 2:8; Psalms 80:8; Psalms 44:2"> For he shall not be moved for ever; the righteous shall be in everlasting remembrance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 19:7; Psalms 89:14; Psalms 119:160"> He shall not be afraid of any evil report: his heart is ready to trust in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 5:18; Psalms 19:9; Revelation 15:3"> His heart is established, he shall not fear, till he shall see his desire upon his enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 99:3; Titus 2:14; Luke 1:49"> He has dispersed abroad; he has given to the poor; his righteousness endures for evermore: his horn shall be exalted with honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 1:7; Proverbs 9:10; Job 28:28"> The sinner shall see and be angry, he shall gnash his teeth, and consume away: the desire of the sinner shall perish.</span>
