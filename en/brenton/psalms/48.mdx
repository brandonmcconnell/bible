<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 50:2; Lamentations 2:15; Matthew 5:35"> Hear these words, all ye nations, hearken, all ye that dwell upon the earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zacharias 2:4–5; Psalms 125:1; Psalms 46:7"> both the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. earth-born.">a</a></sup>sons of mean men, and sons of great men; the rich and poor man together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings II 10:6–19; Revelation 17:12–14; Revelation 20:8–9"> My mouth shall speak of wisdom; and the meditation of my heart shall bring forth understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 14:25; Kings IV 7:6–7; Kings IV 19:35–37"> I will incline mine ear to a parable: I will open my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. problem.">b</a></sup>riddle on the harp.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Osee 13:13; Esaias 13:6–8; Esaias 21:3"> Wherefore<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. shall.">c</a></sup>should I fear in the evil day? the iniquity of my heel shall compass me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremias 18:17; Jezekiel 27:25–26; Kings III 22:48"> They that trust in their strength, and boast themselves in the multitude of their wealth—</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 87:5; Psalms 78:3–6; Psalms 46:5"> A brother does not redeem, shall a man redeem? he shall not give to God a ransom for himself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 26:3; Psalms 40:10; Psalms 77:10–14"> or the price of the redemption of his soul, though he labour for ever,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Malachias 1:11; Deuteronomy 28:58; Psalms 113:3"> and live to the end, so that he should not see corruption.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 97:8; Revelation 16:5–7; Revelation 18:20"> When he shall see wise men dying, the fool and the senseless one shall perish together; and they shall leave their wealth to strangers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esaias 33:18–20; Nehemiah 12:31–40; Matthew 24:1–2"> And their sepulchres are their houses for ever, even their tabernacles to all generations: they have called their lands after their own names.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 71:18; Psalms 122:7; Deuteronomy 11:19"> And man being in honour, understands not: he is compared to the senseless cattle, and is like to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esaias 58:11; Psalms 23:3–4; Psalms 73:24"> This their way is an offence to them: yet afterwards men will commend their sayings. Pause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> They have laid them as sheep in Hades; death shall feed on them; and the upright shall have dominion over them in the morning, and their help shall fail in Hades from their glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> But God shall deliver my soul from the power of Hades, when he shall receive me. Pause.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Fear not when a man is enriched, and when the glory of his house is increased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> For he shall take nothing when he dies; neither shall his glory descend with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> For his soul shall be blessed in his life: he shall give thanks to thee when thou dost well to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Yet he shall go in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. as far as.">d</a></sup>to the generation of his fathers; he shall never see light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Man that is in honour, understands not: he is compared to the senseless cattle, and is like them.</span>
</div>
