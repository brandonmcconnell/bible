<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings I 29:4; Psalms 69:13–16; Psalms 59:1"> <span className="desc">For the end. Destroy not: by David, for a memorial, when he fled from the presence of Saul to the cave.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 118:10–12; Psalms 57:3; Psalms 92:8"> Have mercy, upon me, O God, have mercy upon me: for my soul has trusted in thee: and in the shadow of thy wings will I hope, until the iniquity have passed away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 34:4; Kings I 30:6; 2 Corinthians 7:5–6"> I will cry to God most high; the God who has benefited me. Pause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 13:6; Psalms 118:6; Romans 8:31–39"> He sent from heaven and saved me; he gave to reproach them that trampled on me: God has sent forth his mercy and his truth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 11:54; Matthew 22:15; Esaias 29:20–21"> and he has delivered my soul from the midst of lions' whelps: I lay down to sleep, though troubled. As for the sons of men, their teeth are arms and missile weapons, and their tongue a sharp sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 71:10; Psalms 59:3; Psalms 140:2"> Be thou exalted, O God, above the heavens; and thy glory above all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 55:23; Psalms 94:20–21; Esaias 28:15"> They have prepared snares for my feet, and have bowed down my soul: they have dug a pit before my face, and fallen into it themselves. Pause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings IV 20:5; Psalms 39:12; Psalms 126:5–6"> My heart, O God, is ready, my heart is ready: I will sing, yea will sing psalms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Psalms 46:11; Psalms 118:11–13"> Awake, my glory; awake, psaltery and harp: I will awake early.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; 2 Peter 1:4; Genesis 32:11"> O Lord, I will give thanks to thee among the nations: I will sing to thee among the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 27:1; Esaias 51:12–13; Esaias 51:7–8"> For thy mercy has been magnified even to the heavens, and thy truth to the clouds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 9:1–3; Kings I 1:24–28; Psalms 50:14"> Be thou exalted, O God, above the heavens; and thy glory above all the earth.</span>
