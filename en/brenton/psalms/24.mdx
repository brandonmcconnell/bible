<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 89:11; 1 Corinthians 10:26; Chronicles I 29:11"> <span className="desc">A Psalm of David.</span>To thee, O Lord, have I lifted up my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 5:22; Psalms 136:6; Psalms 104:5–6"> O my God, I have trusted in thee: let me not be confounded, neither let mine enemies laugh me to scorn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 15:1; Hebrews 12:28; Psalms 65:4"> For none of them that wait on thee shall in any wise be ashamed: let them be ashamed that transgress without cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 5:8; Psalms 51:10; James 4:8"> Shew me thy ways, O Lord; and teach me thy paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 3:9; Ephesians 1:3; 1 Peter 3:9"> Lead me in thy truth, and teach me: for thou art God my Saviour: and I have waited on thee all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 27:8; Psalms 105:4; Psalms 22:30"> Remember thy compassions, O Lord, and thy mercies, for they are from everlasting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esaias 26:2; Psalms 118:19–20; Psalms 97:6"> Remember not the sins of my youth, nor my sins of ignorance: remember me according to thy mercy, for thy goodness' sake, O Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 19:11–21; Exodus 15:3; Revelation 6:2"> Good and upright is the Lord: therefore will he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="sc. as a law-giver.">a</a></sup>instruct sinners in the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The meek will he guide in judgment: the meek will he teach his ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Titus 2:13; Matthew 25:34; Esaias 54:5"> All the ways of the Lord are mercy and truth to them that seek his covenant and his testimonies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> For thy name's sake, O Lord, do thou also be merciful to my sin; for it is great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Who is the man that fears the Lord? he shall instruct him in the way which he has chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> His soul shall dwell in prosperity; and his seed shall inherit the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The Lord is the strength of them that fear him; and his covenant is to manifest<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, 'it,' sc. what has just been stated.">b</a></sup>truth to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Mine eyes are continually to the Lord; for he shall draw my feet out of the snare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Look upon me, and have mercy upon me; for I am an only child and poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The afflictions of my heart have been multiplied; deliver me from my distresses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Look upon mine affliction and my trouble; and forgive all my sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Look upon mine enemies; for they have been multiplied; and they have hated me with unjust hatred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Keep my soul, and deliver me: let me not be ashamed; for I have hoped in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The harmless and upright joined themselves to me: for I waited for thee, O Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Deliver Israel, O God, out of all his afflictions.</span>
