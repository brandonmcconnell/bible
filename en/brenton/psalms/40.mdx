<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremias 17:7–8; Psalms 84:11–12; Psalms 34:8"> May the Lord help him upon the bed of his pain; thou hast made all his bed in his sickness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 29:11; Psalms 139:17–18; Exodus 15:11"> I said, O Lord, have mercy upon me; heal my soul; for I have sinned against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 10:5–12; Esaias 1:11; Kings I 15:22"> Mine enemies have spoken evil against me, saying, When shall he die, and his name perish?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 10:7–9; Luke 24:44; John 5:39"> And if he came to see me, his heart spoke vainly; he gathered iniquity to himself; he went forth and spoke in like manner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 7:22; John 4:34; Jeremias 15:16"> All my enemies whispered against me; against me they devised my hurt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 22:25; Mark 16:15–16; Psalms 22:22"> They denounced a wicked word against me, saying, Now that he lies, shall he not rise up again?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 1:16–17; Acts 20:20–21; Romans 10:9–10"> For even the man of my peace, in whom I trusted, who ate my bread, lifted up his heel against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 23:6; Psalms 43:3; Psalms 57:3"> But thou, O Lord, have compassion upon me, and raise me up, and I shall requite them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 38:4; Psalms 73:26; Psalms 69:4"> By this I know that thou hast delighted in me, because mine enemy shall not rejoice over me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 25:17–18; Psalms 70:1–5; Psalms 38:22"> But thou didst help me because of mine innocence, and hast established me before thee for ever.</span>
