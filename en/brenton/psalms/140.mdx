<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 18:48; Psalms 140:4; Psalms 71:4"> <span className="desc">A Psalm of David.</span>O Lord, I have cried to thee; hear me: attend to the voice of my supplication, when I cry to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 56:6; Psalms 36:4; Psalms 64:5–6"> Let my prayer be set forth before thee as incense; the lifting up of my hands as an evening sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 58:4; 2 Corinthians 11:3; Romans 3:13–14"> Set a watch, O Lord, on my mouth, and a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Lit. a door of fortification.">a</a></sup>strong door about my lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 36:11; Psalms 71:4; Psalms 17:5"> Incline not my heart to evil things, to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. pretend pretences.">b</a></sup>employ pretexts<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. in.">c</a></sup>for sins, with men who work iniquity: and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. I will not.">d</a></sup>let me not unite with their choice ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 142:3; Psalms 35:7; Jeremias 18:22"> The righteous shall chasten me with mercy, and reprove me: but let not the oil of the sinner anoint my head: for yet shall my prayer also be in their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="q. d. lest I be injured by them.">e</a></sup>pleasures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 16:2; Psalms 31:14; Psalms 119:57"> Their mighty ones have been swallowed up near the rock: they shall hear my words, for they are sweet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 62:7; Psalms 89:26; Psalms 18:1–2"> As a lump of earth is crushed upon the ground, our bones have been scattered by the mouth of the grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 10:2–3; Deuteronomy 32:27; Psalms 27:12"> For mine eyes are to thee, O Lord God: I have hoped in thee; take not away my life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 7:16; Proverbs 18:7; Proverbs 12:13"> Keep me from the snare which they have set for me, and from the stumbling-blocks of them that work iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 11:6; Psalms 21:9; Revelation 21:8"> Sinners shall fall by their own net: I am alone until I shall escape.</span>
