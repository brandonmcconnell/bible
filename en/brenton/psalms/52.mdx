<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 18:21; Psalms 57:4; Jeremias 9:3–4"> The fool has said in his heart, There is no God. They have corrupted themselves, and become abominable in iniquities: there is none that does good.</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 22:19; Psalms 40:3; Psalms 37:34"> for God has scattered the bones of the men-pleasers; they were ashamed, for God despised them.</span>
