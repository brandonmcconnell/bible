<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 22:12; 2 Timothy 4:14; Revelation 18:6"> The voice of the Lord is mighty; the voice of the Lord is full of majesty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esaias 5:12; Romans 1:28; John 12:37"> There is the voice of the Lord who breaks the cedars; the Lord will break the cedars of Libanus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 31:21–22; Psalms 107:19–22; Psalms 118:5"> And he will beat them small, even Libanus itself, like a calf; and the beloved one is as a young unicorn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 30:11–12; Psalms 56:3–4; Esaias 61:10"> There is a voice of the Lord who divides a flame of fire.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> The Lord will dwell on the waterflood: and the Lord will sit a king for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The Lord will give strength to his people; the Lord will bless his people with peace.</span>
