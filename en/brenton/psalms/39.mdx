<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 90:12; Psalms 103:14; Job 14:13"> And he put a new song into my mouth, even a hymn to our God: many shall see it, and fear, and shall hope in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 90:9–10; Psalms 144:4; Psalms 89:47"> Blessed is the man whose hope is the name of the Lord, and who has not regarded vanities and false frenzies.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 2:10; Kings II 16:10; Leviticus 10:3"> I desired to do thy will, O my God, and thy law in the midst of mine heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:34; Job 13:21; Psalms 25:16–17"> I have preached righteousness in the great congregation; lo! I will not refrain my lips; O Lord, thou knowest my righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:28; 1 Corinthians 11:30–32; Esaias 50:9"> I have not hid thy truth within my heart, and I have declared thy salvation; I have not hid thy mercy and thy truth from the great congregation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Hebrews 11:13; Kings IV 20:5"> But thou, Lord, remove not thy compassion far from me; thy mercy and thy truth have helped me continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 10:20–21; Job 14:10–12; Job 14:5–6"> For innumerable evils have encompassed me; my transgressions have taken hold of me, and I could not see; they are multiplied more than the hairs of my head; and my heart has failed me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Be pleased, O Lord, to deliver me; O Lord, draw nigh to help me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Let those that seek my soul, to destroy it, be ashamed and confounded together; let those that wish me evil be turned backward and put to shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Let those that say to me, Aha, aha, quickly receive shame for their reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Let all those that seek thee, O Lord, exult and rejoice in thee; and let them that love thy salvation say continually, The Lord be magnified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> But I am poor and needy; the Lord will take care of me; thou art my helper, and my defender, O my God, delay not.</span>
