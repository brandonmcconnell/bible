<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 143:6; Psalms 84:2; Psalms 42:1–2"> <span className="desc">For the end, a Psalm of David.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 27:4; Psalms 105:4; Psalms 84:2–11"> Hear my prayer, O God, when I make my petition to thee; deliver my soul from fear of the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 69:16; Psalms 51:15; Philippians 1:23"> Thou hast sheltered me from the conspiracy of them that do wickedly; from the multitude of them that work iniquity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 104:33; Psalms 134:2; Psalms 28:2"> who have sharpened their tongues as a sword; they have bent their bow maliciously;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 36:7–9; Esaias 25:6; Ezra and Nehemiah 3:11–13"> to shoot in secret at the blameless; they will shoot him suddenly, and will not fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 42:8; Psalms 119:147–148; Psalms 149:5"> They have set up for themselves an evil matter, they have given counsel to hide snares; they have said, Who shall see them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:10; Psalms 61:4; Psalms 27:9"> They have searched out iniquity; they have wearied themselves with searching diligently, a man shall approach and the heart is deep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esaias 41:10; Psalms 18:35; Psalms 73:23"> and God shall be exalted, their wounds were caused by the weapon of the foolish children,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 55:15; Psalms 9:17; Psalms 40:14"> and their tongues have set him at nought, all that saw them were troubled;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 18:21; Jezekiel 35:5; Jezekiel 39:4"> and every man was alarmed, and they related the works of God, and understood his deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:13; Psalms 21:1; Esaias 65:16"> The righteous shall rejoice in the Lord, and hope on him, and all the upright in heart shall be praised.</span>
