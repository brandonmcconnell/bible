<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esaias 12:2; Psalms 22:2; Luke 18:7"> <span className="desc">A Psalm of instruction for Ætham the Israelite.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 31:2; Psalms 141:1–2; Lamentations 3:8"> I will sing of thy mercies, O Lord, for ever: I will declare thy truth with my mouth to all generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 107:18; Lamentations 3:15–19; Mark 14:33–34"> For thou hast said, Mercy shall be built up for ever: thy truth shall be established in the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 28:1; 2 Corinthians 1:9; Psalms 143:7"> I made a covenant with my chosen ones, I sware unto David my servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esaias 53:8; Psalms 31:22; Esaias 14:9–12"> I will<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. prepare.">a</a></sup>establish thy seed for ever, and build up thy throne to all generations. Pause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 69:15; Psalms 86:13; Psalms 143:3"> The heavens shall declare thy wonders, O Lord; and thy truth in the assembly of the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 42:7; Job 6:4; Romans 2:5–9"> For who in the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. clouds.">b</a></sup>heavens shall be compared to the Lord? and who shall be likened to the Lord among the sons of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 31:11; Esaias 49:7; Jeremias 32:2"> God is glorified in the council of the saints; great and terrible toward all that are round about him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 11:13; Psalms 38:10; Psalms 86:3"> O Lord God of hosts, who is like to thee? thou art mighty, O Lord, and thy truth is round about thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 6:5; Esaias 26:19; Psalms 30:9"> Thou rulest the power of the sea; and thou calmest the tumult of its waves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 26:6; Proverbs 15:11; Psalms 73:18"> Thou hast brought down the proud as one that is slain; and with the arm of thy power thou hast scattered thine enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:5; Ecclesiastes 2:16; Esaias 8:22"> The heavens are thine, and the earth is thine: thou hast founded the world, and the fulness of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 5:3; Psalms 30:2; Mark 1:35"> Thou hast created the north and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. sea.">c</a></sup>west: Thabor and Hermon shall rejoice in thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 13:24; Psalms 13:1; Psalms 44:24"> Thine is the mighty arm: let thy hand be strengthened, let thy right hand be exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 6:4; Luke 22:44; Job 7:11–16"> Justice and judgment are the establishment of thy throne: mercy and truth shall go before thy face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 90:11; Esaias 53:4–6; Romans 8:32"> Blessed is the people that knows the joyful sound: they shall walk, O Lord, in the light of thy countenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 22:16; Psalms 116:3; Psalms 69:1–2"> And in thy name shall they rejoice all the day: and in thy righteousness shall they be exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 88:8; Psalms 38:11; Job 19:12–15"> For thou art the boast of their strength; and in thy good pleasure shall our horn be exalted,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> for our help is of the Lord; and of the Holy One of Israel, our king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Then thou spokest in vision to thy children, and saidst, I have laid help on a mighty one; I have exalted one chosen out of my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> I have found David my servant; I have anointed him by my holy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Heb. and Alex. oil, see Ps. 91. 10.">d</a></sup>mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> For my hand shall support him; and mine arm shall strengthen him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> The enemy shall have no advantage against him; and the son of transgression shall not hurt him again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And I will hew down his foes before him, and put to flight those that hate him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> But my truth and my mercy shall be with him; and in my name shall his horn be exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And I will set his hand in the sea, and his right hand in the rivers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> He shall call upon me, saying, Thou art my Father, my God, and the helper of my salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And I will make him my first-born, higher than the kings of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> I will keep my mercy for him for ever, and my covenant shall be firm with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And I will establish his seed for ever and ever, and his throne as the days of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> If his children should forsake my law, and walk not in my judgments;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> if they should profane my ordinances, and not keep my commandments;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> I will visit their transgressions with a rod, and their sins with scourges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> But my mercy I will not utterly remove from him, nor wrong my truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Neither will I by any means profane my covenant; and I will not make void the things that proceed out of my lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Once have I sworn by my holiness, that I will not lie to David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> His seed shall endure for ever, and his throne as the sun before me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> and as the moon that is established for ever, and as the faithful witness in heaven. Pause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> But thou hast cast off and set at nought, thou hast rejected thine anointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> Thou hast overthrown the covenant of thy servant; thou hast profaned his sanctuary, casting it to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> Thou hast broken down all his hedges; thou hast made his strong holds a terror.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> All that go by the way have spoiled him: he is become a reproach to his neighbours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> Thou hast exalted the right hand of his enemies; thou hast made all his enemies to rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> Thou hast turned back the help of his sword, and hast not helped him in the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> Thou hast deprived him of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. purification.">e</a></sup>glory: thou hast broken down his throne to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> Thou hast shortened the days of his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Alex. time.">f</a></sup>throne: thou hast poured shame upon him. Pause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> How long, O Lord, wilt thou turn away, for ever? shall thine anger flame out as fire?</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> Remember what my being is: for hast thou created all the sons of men in vain?</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> What man is there who shall live, and not see death? shall any one deliver his soul from the hand of Hades? Pause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> Where are thine ancient mercies, O Lord, which thou swarest to David in thy truth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> Remember, O Lord, the reproach of thy servants, which I have borne in my bosom, even the reproach of many nations;</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> wherewith thine enemies have reviled, O Lord: wherewith they have reviled the recompence of thine anointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53"> Blessed be the Lord for ever. So be it, so be it.</span>
</div>
