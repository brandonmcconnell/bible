<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 91:4; Psalms 36:7; Psalms 17:7–8"> <span className="desc">For the end. Destroy not: by David, for a memorial.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 40:11; Psalms 43:3; Acts 12:11"> For ye work iniquities in your hearts in the earth: your hands plot unrighteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 58:6; Proverbs 30:14; Psalms 35:17"> Sinners have gone astray from the womb: they go astray from the belly: they speak lies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ambacum 2:14; Esaias 6:3; Psalms 72:19"> Their venom is like that of a serpent; as that of a deaf asp, and that stops her ears;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 145:14; Psalms 7:15–16; Proverbs 28:10"> which will not hear the voice of charmers, nor heed the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. poison.">a</a></sup>charm prepared skilfully by the wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 112:7; Ephesians 5:20; Psalms 34:4"> God has crushed their teeth in their mouth: God has broken the cheek-teeth of the lions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 16:9; Psalms 108:1–3; Psalms 30:12"> They shall utterly pass away like water running through: he shall bend his bow till they shall fail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 96:3; Psalms 22:22–23; Psalms 18:49"> They shall be destroyed as melted wax: the fire has fallen and they have not seen the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 108:4; Psalms 103:11; Psalms 36:5"> Before your thorns feel the white thorn, he shall swallow you up as living, as in his wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 57:5; Psalms 8:1; Psalms 8:9"> The righteous shall rejoice when he sees the vengeance of the ungodly: he shall wash his hands in the blood of the sinner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And a man shall say, Verily then there is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. fruit.">b</a></sup>a reward for the righteous: verily there is a God that judges them in the earth.</span>
