</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 96:13; Romans 14:10–12; Psalms 43:1"> And the congregation of the nations shall compass thee: and for this cause do thou return on high.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 15:35; James 1:15; Esaias 33:11"> And on it he has fitted the instruments of death; he has completed his arrows for the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Heb. perhaps 'persecuting ones.'">c</a></sup>raging ones.</span>
