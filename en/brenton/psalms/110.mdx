<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 10:12–13; 1 Corinthians 15:25; Luke 20:42–43"> <span className="desc">Alleluia.</span><br />I will give thee thanks, O Lord, with my whole heart, in the council of the upright, and in the congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 48:17; Matthew 28:18–20; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5"> The works of the Lord are great, sought out<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Comp. Gr. with Heb.">a</a></sup>according to all his will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 5:2; Hebrews 13:21; 1 Thessalonians 4:7"> His work is worthy of thanksgiving and honour: and his righteousness endures for ever and ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 7:17; Hebrews 5:6; Hebrews 7:21"> He has caused his wonderful works to be remembered: the Lord is merciful and compassionate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 2:5; Psalms 16:8; Revelation 6:15–17"> He has given food to them that fear him: he will remember his covenant for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 68:21; Revelation 19:11; John 5:22"> He has declared to his people the power of his works, to give them the inheritance of the heathen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 27:6; Judges 7:5–6; Matthew 26:42"> The works of his hands are truth and judgment: all his commandments are sure:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> established for ever and ever, done in truth and uprightness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> He sent redemption to his people: he commanded his covenant for ever: holy and fearful is his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> The fear of the Lord is the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, sum.">b</a></sup>beginning of wisdom, and all that act accordingly have a good understanding; his praise endures for ever and ever.</span>
