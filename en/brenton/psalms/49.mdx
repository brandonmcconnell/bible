<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 78:1; Psalms 33:8; Matthew 11:15"> <span className="desc">A Psalm</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, of.">a</a></sup><span className="desc">for Asaph.</span><br />The God of gods, the Lord, has spoken, and called the earth from the rising of the sun to the going down thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 62:9; Proverbs 22:2; Jeremias 5:4–5"> Out of Sion comes the excellence of his beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 19:14; Proverbs 22:17; Matthew 12:35"> God, our God, shall come manifestly, and shall not keep silence: a fire shall be kindled before him, and round about him there shall be a very great tempest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 78:2; Matthew 13:35; Numbers 12:8"> He shall summon the heaven above, and the earth, that he may judge his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 27:24; Romans 8:33–34; Amos 5:13"> Assemble ye his saints to him, those that have engaged in a covenant with him upon sacrifices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:17; Psalms 52:7; Jeremias 9:23"> And the heavens shall declare his righteousness: for God is judge. Pause.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; 1 Timothy 2:6; 1 Peter 1:18"> Hear, my people, and I will speak to thee, O Israel: and I will testify to thee: I am God, thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; Job 36:18–19"> I will not reprove thee on account of thy sacrifices; for thy whole-burnt-offerings are before me continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:48; Psalms 16:10; Ecclesiastes 8:8"> I will take no bullocks out of thine house, nor he-goats out of thy flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 12:20; Psalms 39:6; Hebrews 9:27"> For all the wild beasts of the thicket are mine, the cattle on the mountains, and oxen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 4:17; Psalms 5:9; Psalms 64:6"> I know all the birds of the sky; and the beauty of the field is mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:18–21; Psalms 49:20; Psalms 82:7"> If I should be hungry, I will not tell thee: for the world is mine, and the fulness of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 12:20; 1 Corinthians 3:19; Luke 16:27–28"> Will I eat the flesh of bulls, or drink the blood of goats?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachias 4:3; 1 Corinthians 6:2; Daniel 7:22"> Offer to God the sacrifice of praise; and pay thy vows to the Most High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 73:24; Psalms 56:13; Psalms 86:13"> And call upon me in the day of affliction; and I will deliver thee, and thou shalt glorify me. Pause.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Psalms 37:1; Revelation 21:26"> But to the sinner God has said, Why dost thou declare my ordinances, and take up my covenant in thy mouth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:7; Job 27:19; Luke 16:24"> Whereas thou hast hated instruction, and hast cast my words behind thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 12:19; Deuteronomy 29:19; Psalms 10:3"> If thou sawest a thief, thou<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See 1 Pe 4. 4.">b</a></sup>rannest along with him, and hast cast in thy lot with adulterers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 33:30; Genesis 15:15; Psalms 56:13"> Thy mouth has multiplied wickedness, and thy tongue has framed deceit.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="From these words in Alex. to Ps. 79. 11, thirty psalms are wanting.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 49:12; Ecclesiastes 3:18–19; Job 4:21"> Thou didst sit and speak against thy brother, and didst scandalise thy mother's son.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> These things thou didst, and I kept silence: thou thoughtest wickedly that I should be like thee; but I will reprove thee, and set thine offences before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Now consider these things, ye that forget God, lest he rend you, and there be no deliverer.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> The sacrifice of praise will glorify me: and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. there.">d</a></sup>that is the way wherein I will shew to him the salvation of God.</span>
</div>
