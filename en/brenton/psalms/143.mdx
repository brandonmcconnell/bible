<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; Romans 3:20; Job 25:4"> My mercy, and my refuge; my helper, and my deliverer; my protector, in whom I have trusted; who subdues my people under me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 3:6; Psalms 7:1–2; Psalms 7:5"> Lord, what is man, that thou art made known to him? or the son of man, that thou takest account of him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 142:3; Psalms 77:3; Psalms 102:3–4"> Man is like to vanity: his days pass as a shadow.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 77:5–6; Psalms 77:10–12; Michæas 6:5"> O Lord, bow thy heavens, and come down: touch the mountains, and they shall smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 63:1; Job 11:13; Psalms 84:2"> Send lightning, and thou shalt scatter them: send forth thine arrows, and thou shalt discomfit them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 69:17; Psalms 28:1; Psalms 27:9"> Send forth thine hand from on high; rescue me, and deliver me out of great<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. many.">a</a></sup>waters, out of the hand of strange children;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 25:4–5; Proverbs 3:5–6; Esaias 48:17"> whose mouth has spoken vanity, and their right hand is a right hand of iniquity.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 18:10; Psalms 61:3–4; Psalms 59:1"> O God, I will sing a new song to thee: I will play to thee on a psaltery of ten strings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 25:4–5; John 14:26; Hebrews 13:21"> Even to him who gives<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, victory.">b</a></sup>salvation to kings: who redeems his servant David from the hurtful sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 31:1; Ephesians 2:4–5; Psalms 71:2"> Deliver me, and rescue me from the hand of strange children, whose mouth has spoken vanity, and their right hand is a right hand of iniquity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 54:5; Psalms 116:16; Psalms 119:94"> whose children are as plants, strengthened in their youth: their daughters are beautiful, sumptuously adorned after the similitude of a temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Their garners are full, and bursting with one kind of store after another; their sheep are prolific, multiplying in their streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Their oxen are fat: there is no falling down of a hedge, nor going out, nor cry in their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, habitations.">c</a></sup>folds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Men bless the people to whom this lot belongs, but blessed is the people whose God is the Lord.</span>
