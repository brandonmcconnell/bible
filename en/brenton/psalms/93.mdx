<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 96:10; Chronicles I 29:12; Psalms 99:1"> <span className="desc">A Psalm of David for the fourth day of the week.</span>The Lord is a God of vengeance; the God of vengeance<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. plural, bis.">a</a></sup>has declared himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 45:6; Psalms 90:2; Proverbs 8:22–23"> Be thou exalted, thou that judgest the earth: render a reward to the proud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 96:11; Psalms 69:1–2; Psalms 18:4"> How long shall sinners, O Lord, how long shall sinners boast?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 65:7; Psalms 89:9; Job 38:11"> They will utter and speak unrighteousness; all the workers of iniquity will speak so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 19:7–8; Psalms 29:2; Psalms 119:111"> They have afflicted thy people, O Lord, and hurt thine heritage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> They have slain the widow and fatherless, and murdered the stranger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And they said, The Lord shall not see, neither shall the God of Jacob understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Understand now, ye simple among the people; and ye fools, at length be wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> He that planted the ear, does he not hear? or he that formed the eye, does not he perceive?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> He that chastises the heathen, shall not he punish, even he that teaches man knowledge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The Lord knows the thoughts of men, that they are vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Blessed is the man whomsoever thou shalt chasten, O Lord, and shalt teach him out of thy law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> to give him rest from evil days, until a pit be digged for the sinful one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For the Lord will not cast off his people, neither will he forsake his inheritance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> until righteousness return to judgment, and all the upright in heart shall follow it. Pause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Who will rise up for me against the transgressors? or who will stand up with me against the workers of iniquity?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> If the Lord had not helped me, my soul had almost sojourned in Hades.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> If I said, My foot has been moved;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> thy mercy, O Lord, helped me. O Lord, according to the multitude of my griefs within my heart, thy consolations have<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Lit. 'have loved'. Alex. ἠὺφπαναν, 'have gladdened'.">b</a></sup>soothed my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Shall the throne of iniquity have fellowship with thee, which frames mischief by an ordinance?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> They will hunt for the soul of the righteous, and condemn innocent blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> But the Lord was my refuge; and my God the helper of my hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And he will recompense to them their iniquity and their wickedness: the Lord our God shall utterly destroy them.</span>
