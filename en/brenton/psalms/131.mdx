<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 12:16; Psalms 139:6; Job 42:3"> <span className="desc">A Song of Degrees.</span><br />Lord, remember David, and all his meekness:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:20; Mark 10:15; Psalms 43:5"> how he sware to the Lord, and vowed to the God of Jacob, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 130:7; Esaias 26:4; Psalms 146:5"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. if I shall, etc. Hebraism.">a</a></sup>I will not go into the tabernacle of my house; I will not go up to the couch of my bed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> I will not give sleep to mine eyes, nor slumber to mine eyelids, nor rest to my temples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> until I find a place for the Lord, a tabernacle for the God of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Behold, we heard of it in Ephratha; we found it in the fields of the wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Let us enter into his tabernacles: let us worship at the place where his feet stood.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Arise, O Lord, into thy rest; thou, and the ark of thine holiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Thy priests shall clothe themselves with righteousness; and thy saints shall exult.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> For the sake of thy servant David turn not away the face of thine anointed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The Lord sware in truth to David, and he will not annul it, saying, Of the fruit of thy body will I set a king upon thy throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> If thy children will keep my covenant, and these my testimonies which I shall teach them, their children also shall sit upon thy throne for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> For the Lord has elected Sion, he has chosen her for a habitation for himself, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> This is my rest for ever: here will I dwell; for I have chosen it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> I will surely bless her provision: I will satisfy her poor with bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> I will clothe her priests with salvation; and her saints shall greatly exult.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> There will I cause to spring up a horn to David: I have prepared a lamp for mine anointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> His enemies will I clothe with shame; but upon himself shall my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Ps. 88. 39.">b</a></sup>holiness flourish.</span>
</div>
