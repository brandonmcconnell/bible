<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esaias 56:7; Esaias 28:16; Ephesians 2:20–22"> <span className="desc">A song of a Psalm for the sons of Core for the end, upon Maeleth for responsive strains, of</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. understanding.">a</a></sup><span className="desc">instruction for Æman the Israelite.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 132:13–14; Psalms 78:67–69; Deuteronomy 12:5"> O Lord God of my salvation, I have cried by day and in the night before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jezekiel 37:27–28; Jezekiel 36:11–38; Psalms 48:2–3"> Let my prayer come in before thee; incline thine ear to my supplication, O Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 45:12; Psalms 68:31; Esaias 19:23–25"> For my soul is filled with troubles, and my life has drawn nigh to Hades.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 3:26–28; 1 Peter 1:23–24; Jezekiel 48:35"> I have been reckoned with them that go down to the pit; I became as a man without help;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esaias 4:3; Luke 10:20; Jezekiel 13:9"> free among the dead, as the slain ones<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Alex. om. ἐῤῥιμμένοι, cast out.">b</a></sup>cast out, who sleep in the tomb; whom thou rememberest no more; and they are rejected from thy hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 36:9; John 1:16; Revelation 22:17"> They laid me in the lowest pit, in dark places, and in the shadow of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Thy wrath has pressed heavily upon me, and thou hast brought upon me all thy billows. Pause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Thou hast removed my acquaintance far from me; they have made me an abomination to themselves; I have been delivered up, and have not gone forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Mine eyes are dimmed from poverty; but I cried to thee, O Lord, all the day; I spread forth my hands to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Wilt thou work wonders for the dead? or shall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Heb.">c</a></sup>physicians raise them up, that they shall praise thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Shall any one declare thy mercy in the tomb? and thy truth in destruction?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Shall thy wonders be known in darkness? and thy righteousness in a forgotten land?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> But I cried to thee, O Lord; and in the morning shall my prayer prevent thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Wherefore, O Lord, dost thou reject my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Heb. and Alex. soul.">d</a></sup>prayer, and turn thy face away from me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> I am poor and in troubles from my youth; and having been exalted, I was brought low and into<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See 2 Cor. 4. 8.">e</a></sup>despair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Thy wrath has passed over me; and thy terrors have greatly disquieted me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> They compassed me like water; all the day they beset me together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Thou hast put far from me<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Alex. friend and neighbour.">f</a></sup>every friend, and mine acquaintances because of my wretchedness.</span>
