<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 13:24; Psalms 89:46; Lamentations 5:20"> <span className="desc">For the end, a Psalm of David.</span>The fool has said in his heart, There is no God. They have corrupted themselves, and become abominable in their devices; there is none that does goodness, there is not even so much as one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Michæas 7:8–10; Jeremias 8:18; Jeremias 15:18"> The Lord looked down from heaven upon the sons of men, to see if there were any that understood, or sought after God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezra and Nehemiah 9:8; Psalms 18:28; Jeremias 51:39"> They are all gone out of the way, they are together become good for nothing, there is none that does good, no not one. Their throat is an open sepulchre; with their tongues they have used deceit; the poison of asps is under their lips: whose mouth is full of cursing and bitterness; their feet are swift to shed blood: destruction and misery are in their ways; and the way of peace they have not known: there is no fear of God before their eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 25:2; Jeremias 1:19; Proverbs 12:3"> Will not all the workers of iniquity know, who eat up my people as they would eat bread? they have not called upon the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esaias 12:2; Jude 1:21; Ambacum 3:18"> There were they alarmed with fear, where there was no fear; for God is in the righteous generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 116:7; Psalms 21:13; Psalms 119:7"> Ye have shamed the counsel of the poor, because the Lord is his hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Who will bring the salvation of Israel out of Sion? when the Lord brings back the captivity of his people, let Jacob exult, and Israel be glad.</span>
