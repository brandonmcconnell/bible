<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 101:1; Psalms 89:8; Psalms 119:90"> <span className="desc">A Prayer of Moses the man of God.</span><br />Lord, thou hast been our refuge in all generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 36:5; Nehemiah 9:31; Psalms 89:5"> Before the mountains existed, and before the earth and the world were formed, even from age to age, Thou art.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 132:11; Kings II 7:10–16; Psalms 89:34–35"> Turn not man back to his low place, whereas thou saidst, Return, ye sons of men?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings II 7:12–16; Luke 1:32–33; Psalms 132:12"> For a thousand years in thy sight are as the yesterday which is past, and as a watch in the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 97:6; Psalms 19:1; Esaias 44:23"> Years shall be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. their vanities or rejections.">a</a></sup>vanity to them: let the morning pass away as grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 86:8; Psalms 71:19; Psalms 113:5"> In the morning let it flower, and pass away: in the evening let it droop, let it be withered and dried up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 47:2; Psalms 76:7–11; Hebrews 12:28–29"> For we have perished in thine anger, and in thy wrath we have been troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 71:19; Kings I 2:2; Jeremias 32:17"> Thou hast set our transgressions before thee: our age is in the light of thy countenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 65:7; Psalms 107:25–29; Mark 4:41"> For all our days are gone, and we have passed away in thy wrath: our years have<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. mediated.">b</a></sup>spun out their tale as a spider.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 87:4; Psalms 68:30; Psalms 144:6"> As for the days of our years, in them are seventy years; and if men should be in strength, eighty years: and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, what is more than these.">c</a></sup>the greater part of them would be labour and trouble; for weakness overtakes us, and we shall be chastened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Chronicles I 29:11; Genesis 1:1; Psalms 115:16"> Who knows the power of thy wrath?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 12:1; Job 26:7; Joshua 19:22"> and who knows how to number his days because of the fear of thy wrath? So manifest thy right hand, and those that are instructed in wisdom in the heart.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 4:34–35; Psalms 89:10; Psalms 62:11"> Return, O Lord, how long? and be intreated concerning thy servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; Proverbs 16:12; Psalms 45:6–7"> We have been satisfied in the morning with thy mercy; and we did exult and rejoice:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 44:3; Job 29:3; Psalms 4:6"> let us rejoice in all our days, in return for the days wherein thou didst afflict us, the years wherein we saw evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Philippians 4:4; Philippians 3:9; Luke 1:47"> And look upon thy servants, and upon thy works; and guide their children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 75:10; Psalms 92:10; Psalms 148:14"> And let the brightness of the Lord our God be upon us: and do thou<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or, prosper.">d</a></sup>direct for us the works of our hands.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Alex. + yea, prosper thou the work of our hands.">e</a></sup></span>
