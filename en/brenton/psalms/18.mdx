<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings II 22:1–51; Colossians 1:11; 1 John 4:19"> <span className="desc">For the end, a Psalm of David.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 144:2; Psalms 62:7; Psalms 19:14"> The heavens declare the glory of God; and the firmament proclaims the work of his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 91:15; Psalms 145:3; Psalms 62:8"> Day to day<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. eructat.">a</a></sup>utters speech, and night to night proclaims knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 116:3; Matthew 27:24–25; Mark 14:33–34"> There are no speeches or words,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. of which.">b</a></sup>in which their voices are not heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 86:13; Psalms 88:3–8; Acts 2:24"> Their voice is gone out into all the earth, and their words to the ends of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Kings II 22:7; Psalms 34:15"> In the sun he has set his tabernacle; and he comes forth as a bridegroom out of his chamber: he will exult as a giant to run his course.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 4:31; Psalms 68:7–8; Acts 16:25–26"> His going forth is from the extremity of heaven, and his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. meeting.">c</a></sup>circuit to the other end of heaven: and no one shall be hidden from his heat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 21:9; Psalms 11:6; Naum 1:5–6"> The law of the Lord is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. spotless.">d</a></sup>perfect, converting souls: the testimony of the Lord is faithful, instructing babes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 144:5–15; Deuteronomy 33:26; Kings II 22:10"> The ordinances of the Lord are right, rejoicing the heart: the commandment of the Lord is bright, enlightening the eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 104:3; Kings II 22:11–12; Psalms 99:1"> The fear of the Lord is pure, enduring for ever and ever: the judgments of the Lord are true, and justified altogether.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; Deuteronomy 4:11; Psalms 91:1"> To be desired more than gold, and much precious stone: sweeter also than honey and the honey-comb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 10:11; Revelation 16:21; Matthew 17:2"> For thy servant keeps to them: in the keeping of them there is great reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 104:7; Kings I 7:10; Psalms 140:10"> Who will understand his transgressions? purge thou me from my secret sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 144:6; Ambacum 3:11; Numbers 24:8"> And spare thy servant the attack of strangers: if they do not gain the dominion over me, then shall I be blameless, and I shall be clear from great sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 106:9; Exodus 15:8; Michæas 6:2"> So shall the sayings of my mouth, and the meditation of my heart, be pleasing continually before thee, O Lord my helper, and my redeemer.</span>
