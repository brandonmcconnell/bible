<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 35:1; Psalms 26:1; Psalms 7:8"> <span className="desc">For the end, a Psalm for</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. understanding.">a</a></sup><span className="desc">instruction, for the sons of Core.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 42:9; Psalms 28:7; Ephesians 6:10"> O God, we have heard with our ears, our fathers have told us, the work which thou wroughtest in their days, in the days of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 36:9; Proverbs 3:5–6; John 1:4"> Thine hand utterly destroyed the heathen, and thou didst plant them: thou didst afflict the nations, and cast them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esaias 61:10; Ambacum 3:17–18; Psalms 66:13–15"> For they inherited not the land by their own sword, and their own arm did not deliver them; but thy right hand, and thine arm, and the light of thy countenance, because thou wert well pleased in them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 42:5; Psalms 42:11"> Thou art indeed my King and my God, who commandest deliverances for Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> In thee will we push down our enemies, and in thy name will we bring to nought them that rise up against us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> For I will not trust in my bow, and my sword shall not save me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> For thou hast saved us from them that afflicted us, and hast put to shame them that hated us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> In God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. will be praised etc. See Ps. 106.">b</a></sup>will we make our boast all the day, and to thy name will we give thanks for ever. Pause.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> But now thou hast cast off, and put us to shame; and thou wilt not go forth with our hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Thou hast turned us back before our enemies; and they that hated us spoiled for themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Thou madest us as sheep for meat; and thou scatteredst us among the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Thou hast sold thy people without price, and there was no profit by their exchange.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Thou hast made us a reproach to our neighbours, a scorn and derision to them that are round about us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Thou hast made us a proverb among the Gentiles, a shaking of the head among the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> All the day my shame is before me, and the confusion of my face has covered me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> because of the voice of the slanderer and reviler; because of the enemy and avenger.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> All these things are come upon us: but we have not forgotten thee, neither have we dealt unrighteously in thy covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And our heart has not gone back; but thou hast turned aside our paths from thy way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> For thou hast laid us low in a place of affliction, and the shadow of death has covered us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> If we have forgotten the name of our God, and if we have spread out our hands to a strange god; shall not God search these things out?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> for he knows the secrets of the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> For, for thy sake we are killed all the day long; we are counted as sheep for slaughter.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Awake, wherefore sleepest thou, O Lord? arise, and do not cast us off for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Wherefore turnest thou thy face away, and forgettest our poverty and our affliction?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> For our soul has been brought down to the dust; our belly has cleaved to the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Arise, O Lord, help us, and redeem us for thy name's sake.</span>
</div>
</div>
