<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:18; Psalms 105:1; Chronicles I 16:34"> <span className="desc">Alleluia.</span><br />Give thanks to the Lord, for he is good; for his mercy endures for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Ephesians 1:19; Psalms 145:3–12"> Let them say so who have been redeemed by the Lord, whom he has redeemed from the hand of the enemy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 15:2; James 1:25; John 15:14"> and gathered them out of the countries, from the east, and west, and north, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. sea.">a</a></sup>south.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 119:132; Luke 23:42; Nehemiah 13:14"> They wandered in the wilderness in a dry land; they found no way to a city of habitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:16; Psalms 105:43; Psalms 1:3"> Hungry and thirsty, their soul fainted in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:16; Psalms 78:8; Kings III 8:47"> Then they cried to the Lord in their affliction, and he delivered them out of their distresses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 14:11–12; Psalms 78:42; Psalms 78:11"> And he guided them into a straight path, that they might go to a city of habitation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 9:16; Jezekiel 20:9; Jezekiel 20:14"> Let them acknowledge to the Lord his mercies, and his wonderful works to the children of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 18:15; Esaias 51:10; Esaias 63:11–14"> For he satisfies the empty soul, and fills the hungry soul with good things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 14:30; Psalms 107:2; Michæas 6:4"> even them that sit in darkness and the shadow of death, fettered in poverty and iron;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 15:5; Psalms 78:53; Exodus 14:13"> because they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. embittered the oracles.">b</a></sup>rebelled against the words of God, and provoked the counsel of the Most High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 14:31–21; Luke 8:13; John 8:30–31"> So their heart was brought low with troubles; they were weak, and there was no helper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 78:11; Exodus 15:24; Esaias 48:17–18"> Then they cried to the Lord in their affliction, and he saved them out of their distresses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:9; Numbers 11:4; 1 Corinthians 10:6"> And he brought them out of darkness and the shadow of death, and broke their bonds asunder.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 11:31–34; Esaias 10:16; Psalms 78:29–31"> Let them acknowledge to the Lord his mercies, and his wonders to the children of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 16:1–50; Leviticus 21:10–12; Leviticus 21:6–8"> For he broke to pieces the brazen gates, and crushed the iron bars.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:6; Numbers 16:29–33; Numbers 26:10"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="see the Hebrew.">c</a></sup>He helped them out of the way of their iniquity; for they were brought low because of their iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Numbers 16:35–40; Numbers 16:46"> Their soul abhorred all meat; and they drew near to the gates of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 32:4–8; Exodus 32:35; Nehemiah 9:18"> Then they cried to the Lord in their affliction, and he saved them out of their distresses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremias 2:11; Exodus 20:4–5; Romans 1:22–23"> He sent his word, and healed them, and delivered them out of their destructions.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremias 2:32; Psalms 78:42–51; Psalms 106:13"> Let them acknowledge to the Lord his mercies, and his wonderful works to the children of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 105:23; Psalms 78:51; Exodus 14:25–28"> And let them offer to him the sacrifice of praise, and proclaim his works with exultation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:13–14; Deuteronomy 9:25; Jezekiel 22:30"> They that go down to the sea in ships, doing business in many waters;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:32; Jezekiel 20:6; Hebrews 3:18–19"> these men have seen the works of the Lord, and his wonders in the deep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 14:22; Deuteronomy 1:26–27; Hebrews 3:15"> He speaks, and the stormy wind arises, and its waves are lifted up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jezekiel 20:15; Numbers 14:28–35; Hebrews 3:11"> They go up to the heavens, and go down to the depths; their soul melts because of troubles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 44:11; Leviticus 26:33; Deuteronomy 28:64–65"> They are troubled, they stagger as a drunkard, and all their wisdom is swallowed up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Osee 9:10; Numbers 25:1–3; Numbers 25:5"> Then they cry to the Lord in their affliction, and he brings them out of their distresses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:16–21; 1 Corinthians 10:8; Romans 1:21–24"> And he commands the storm, and it is calmed into a gentle breeze, and its waves are still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 25:6–8; Joshua 7:12; Numbers 25:14–15"> And they are glad, because they are quiet; and he guides them to their desired haven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 25:11–13; Mark 14:3–9; Deuteronomy 24:13"> Let them acknowledge to the Lord his mercies, and his wonderful works to the children of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 20:2–13; Numbers 20:23–24; Deuteronomy 4:21"> Let them exalt him in the congregation of the people, and praise him in the seat of the elders.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 20:10–11; Psalms 107:11; James 3:2"> He turns rivers into a desert, and streams of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. waters.">d</a></sup>water into<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. thirst.">e</a></sup>a dry land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Deuteronomy 7:16; Judges 1:21"> a fruitful land into saltness, for the wickedness of them that dwell in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Judges 3:5–6; 1 Corinthians 15:33; 1 Corinthians 5:6"> He turns a wilderness into pools of water, and a dry land into streams of water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:16; Judges 2:3; Exodus 23:33"> And there he causes the hungry to dwell, and they establish for themselves cities of habitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Kings IV 17:17; Kings IV 16:3; Deuteronomy 32:17"> And they sow fields, and plant vineyards, and they yield fruit of increase.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 35:33; Deuteronomy 21:9; Esaias 26:21"> And he blesses them, and they multiply exceedingly, and he diminishes not the number of their cattle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Jezekiel 20:18; Numbers 15:39; Leviticus 17:7"> Again they become few, and are brought low, by the pressure of evils and pain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Deuteronomy 9:29; Judges 2:20"> Contempt is poured upon their princes, and he causes them to wander in a desert and trackless land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Nehemiah 9:27–38; Judges 3:8"> But he helps the poor out of poverty, and makes him families as a flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Judges 4:3; Judges 10:12"> The upright shall see and rejoice; and all iniquity shall stop her mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalms 81:12; Judges 2:16–18; Psalms 1:1"> Who is wise, and will observe these things, and understand the mercies of the Lord?</span>
</div>
