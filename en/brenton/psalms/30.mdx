<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 25:2; Daniel 4:37; Psalms 35:19"> <span className="desc">For the end, a Psalm of David, an utterance of</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. ecstasy. See title.">a</a></sup><span className="desc">extreme fear.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 103:3–4; Kings IV 20:5; Psalms 147:3"> O Lord, I have hoped in thee; let me never be ashamed: deliver me in thy righteousness and rescue me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 86:13; Psalms 16:10; Jonas 2:4–6"> Incline thine ear to me; make haste to rescue me: be thou to me for a protecting God, and for a house of refuge to save me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 97:12; Revelation 4:8; Chronicles I 16:4"> For thou art my strength and my refuge; and thou shalt guide me for thy name's sake, and maintain me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esaias 54:7–8; 2 Corinthians 4:17; Psalms 126:5–6"> Thou shalt bring me out of the snare which they have hidden for me; for thou, O Lord, art my defender.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 12:19; 2 Corinthians 12:7; Psalms 119:117"> Into thine hands I will commit my spirit: thou hast redeemed me, O Lord God of truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 104:29; Psalms 143:7; Psalms 30:5"> Thou hast hated them that idly persist in vanities: but I have hoped in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 4:6–7; Psalms 34:6; Psalms 130:1–2"> I will exult and be glad in thy mercy: for thou hast looked upon mine affliction; thou hast saved my soul from distresses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 6:5; Psalms 115:17–18; Esaias 38:18"> And thou hast not shut me up into the hands of the enemy: thou hast set my feet in a wide place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 54:4; Psalms 143:1; Psalms 143:7–9"> Pity me, O Lord, for I am afflicted: my eye is troubled with indignation, my soul and my belly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremias 31:4; Jeremias 31:13–14; Ecclesiastes 3:4"> For my life is spent with grief, and my years with groanings: my strength has been weakened through poverty, and my bones are troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 145:2; Psalms 146:1–2; Psalms 44:8"> I became a reproach among all mine enemies, but exceedingly so to my neighbours, and a fear to mine acquaintance: they that saw me without fled from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> I have been forgotten as a dead man out of mind: I am become as a broken vessel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For I heard the slander of many that dwelt round about: when they were gathered together against me, they took counsel to take my life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> But I hoped in thee, O Lord: I said, Thou art my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> My lots are in thy hands: deliver me from the hand of mine enemies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> and from them that persecute me. Make thy face to shine upon thy servant: save me in thy mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> O Lord, let me not be ashamed, for I have called upon thee: let the ungodly be ashamed, and brought down to Hades.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Let the deceitful lips become dumb, which speak iniquity against the righteous with pride and scorn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> How abundant is the multitude of thy goodness, O Lord, which thou hast laid up for them that fear thee! thou hast wrought it out for them that hope on thee, in the presence of the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Thou wilt hide them in the secret of thy presence from the vexation of man: thou wilt screen them in a tabernacle from the contradiction of tongues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Blessed be the Lord: for he has magnified his mercy in a fortified city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> But I said in my extreme fear, I am cast out from the sight of thine eyes: therefore thou didst hearken, O Lord, to the voice of my supplication when I cried to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Love the Lord, all ye his saints: for the Lord seeks for truth, and renders a reward to them that deal very proudly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Be of good courage, and let your heart be strengthened, all ye that hope in the Lord.</span>
