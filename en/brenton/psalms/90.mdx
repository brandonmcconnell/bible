<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:27; Psalms 71:3; 1 John 4:16"> <span className="desc">Praise of a Song, by David.</span>He that dwells in the help of the Highest, shall sojourn under the shelter of the God of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 13:8; Genesis 1:1; Psalms 102:24–27"> He shall say to the Lord, Thou art my helper and my refuge: my God; I will hope in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Psalms 146:4"> For he shall deliver thee from the snare of the hunters, from every troublesome matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Peter 3:8; Psalms 39:5; Matthew 24:43"> He shall overshadow thee with his shoulders, and thou shalt trust under his wings: his truth shall cover thee with a shield.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esaias 40:6; Psalms 73:20; Job 22:16"> Thou shalt not be afraid of terror by night; nor of the arrow flying by day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 14:2; Psalms 92:7; James 1:11"> nor of the evil<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See ver 3.">a</a></sup>thing that walks in darkness; nor of calamity, and the evil spirit at noon-day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 39:11; Psalms 59:13; Exodus 14:24"> A thousand shall fall at thy side, and ten thousand at thy right hand; but it shall not come nigh thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremias 16:17; Ecclesiastes 12:14; Hebrews 4:12–13"> Only with thine eyes shalt thou observe and see the reward of sinners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 78:33; Psalms 39:5; Psalms 90:4"> For thou, O Lord, art my hope: thou, my soul, hast made the Most High thy refuge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 4:14; Psalms 78:39; Kings II 19:35"> No evils shall come upon thee, and no scourge shall draw nigh to thy dwelling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 26:24; Esaias 33:14; Naum 1:6"> For he shall give his angels charge concerning thee, to keep thee in all thy ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 5:16–17; Psalms 39:4; John 9:4"> They shall bear thee up on their hands, lest at any time thou dash thy foot against a stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 106:45; Psalms 135:14; Deuteronomy 32:36"> Thou shalt tread on the asp and basilisk: and thou shalt trample on the lion and dragon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 23:6; Psalms 86:4; Psalms 31:7"> For he has hoped in me, and I will deliver him: I will protect him, because he has known my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 126:5–6; Psalms 30:5; John 16:20"> He shall call upon me, and I will hearken to him: I am with him in affliction; and I will deliver him, and glorify him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ambacum 3:2; Psalms 44:1; Deuteronomy 32:4"> I will satisfy him with length of days, and shew him my salvation.</span>
