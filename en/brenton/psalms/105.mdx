<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esaias 12:4; Psalms 145:11–12; Romans 10:13"> <span className="desc">Alleluia.</span><br />Give thanks to the Lord; for he is good: for his mercy endures for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 119:27; Psalms 98:5; Esaias 12:5–6"> Who shall tell the mighty acts of the Lord? who shall cause all his praises to be heard?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25; Galatians 6:14; 1 Corinthians 1:29"> Blessed are they that keep judgment, and do righteousness at all times.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 27:8; Sophonias 2:2–3; Chronicles II 6:41"> Remember us, O Lord, with the favour thou hast to thy people: visit us with thy salvation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 77:11; Psalms 119:13; Deuteronomy 32:7"> that we may behold the good of thine elect, that we may rejoice in the gladness of thy nation, that we may glory with thine inheritance.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esaias 44:1–2; John 15:16; 1 Peter 2:9"> We have sinned with our fathers, we have transgressed, we have done unrighteously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esaias 26:9; Deuteronomy 26:17–18; Exodus 20:2"> Our fathers in Egypt understood not thy wonders, and remembered not the multitude of thy mercy; but provoked him as they went up by the Red Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:9; Psalms 106:45; Psalms 111:5"> Yet he saved them for his name's sake, that he might cause his mighty power to be known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 17:2; Genesis 22:16–18; Genesis 26:3"> And he rebuked the Red Sea, and it was dried up: so he led them through the deep as through the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 17:7–8; Kings II 23:5; Genesis 28:13–15"> And he saved them out of the hand of them that hated them, and redeemed them out of the hand of the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 13:15; Genesis 15:18; Psalms 78:55"> The water covered those that oppressed them: there was not one of them left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; Deuteronomy 7:7; Hebrews 11:9"> Then they believed his words, and celebrated his praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> They made haste, they forgot his works; they waited not for his counsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 35:5; Exodus 7:16–17; Genesis 26:14–33"> And they lusted exceedingly in the wilderness, and tempted God in the dry land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zacharias 2:8; Genesis 26:11; Genesis 20:7"> And he gave them their request, and sent fulness into their souls.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jezekiel 4:16; Genesis 41:54; Esaias 3:1"> They provoked Moses also in the camp, and Aaron the holy one of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 7:9; Genesis 37:36; Genesis 45:4–5"> The earth opened and swallowed up Dathan, and closed upon the congregation of Abiron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 16:24; Genesis 39:20; Genesis 40:15"> And a fire was kindled in their congregation, and a flame burnt up the sinners.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 7:10; Proverbs 21:1; Genesis 40:20–21"> And they made a calf in Choreb, and worshipped the graven image,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 41:14"> and they changed their glory into the similitude of a calf that feeds on grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 41:40–44; Genesis 45:8; Genesis 41:55"> They forgot God that saved them, who had wrought great deeds in Egypt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Esaias 19:11; Genesis 41:33; Genesis 41:38"> wondrous works in the land of Cham, and terrible things at the Red Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 7:11–15; Acts 13:17; Psalms 78:51"> So he said that he would have destroyed them, had not Moses his chosen stood before him in the breach, to turn him away from the fierceness of his anger, so that he should not destroy them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 46:3; Exodus 1:7–9; Acts 7:17"> Moreover they set at nought the desirable land, and believed not his word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 7:19; Genesis 15:13; Exodus 9:16"> And they murmured in their tents: they hearkened not to the voice of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 3:10; Exodus 29:5–46; Exodus 28:12"> So he lifted up his hand against them, to cast them down in the wilderness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 78:43–51; Nehemiah 9:10–11; Psalms 105:23"> and to cast down their seed among the nations, and to scatter them in the countries.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 99:7; Joel 2:31; Joel 2:2"> They were joined also to Beelphegor, and ate the sacrifices of the dead. And they provoked him with their devices;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 7:20–21; Psalms 78:44; Esaias 50:2"> and destruction was multiplied among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 16:13–14; Exodus 8:3–14; Psalms 78:45"> Then Phinees stood up, and made atonement: and the plague ceased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 8:16–18; Exodus 8:21–24; Esaias 7:18"> And it was counted to him for righteousness, to all generations for ever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 78:47–48; Exodus 9:18–28; Revelation 8:7"> They provoked him also at the water of Strife, and Moses was hurt for their sakes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 78:47; Revelation 9:4"> for they provoked his spirit, and he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, gave commandment.">a</a></sup>spoke unadvisedly with his lips.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 10:12–15; Psalms 78:46; Joel 1:4–7"> They destroyed not the nations which the Lord told them to destroy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> but were mingled with the heathen, and learned their works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 49:3; Exodus 12:29–30; Psalms 78:51"> And they served their graven images; and it became an offence to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 12:35–36; Exodus 3:22; Acts 13:17"> And they sacrificed their sons and their daughters to devils,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 12:33; Exodus 15:16; Genesis 35:5"> and shed innocent blood, the blood of their sons and daughters, whom they sacrificed to the idols of Chanaan; and the land was<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, murderously defiled.">b</a></sup>defiled with blood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:12; Psalms 78:14; Esaias 4:5"> and was polluted with their works; and they went a whoring with their own devices.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Psalms 78:18; Numbers 11:31–33; Joshua 5:12"> So the Lord was very angry with his people, and he abhorred his inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 20:11; 1 Corinthians 10:4; Exodus 17:6"> And he delivered them into the hands of their enemies; and they that hated them ruled over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Exodus 2:24; Luke 1:72–73; Genesis 12:7"> And their enemies oppressed them, and they were brought down under their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Esaias 35:10; Acts 7:36; Jeremias 31:11–12"> Many a time he delivered them; but they provoked him by their counsel, and they were brought low by their iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:10–11; Joshua 24:13; Psalms 78:55"> Yet the Lord looked upon their affliction, when he heard their petition.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Titus 2:14; Ephesians 2:8–10"> And he remembered his covenant, and repented according to the multitude of his mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> And he caused them to be pitied in the sight of all who carried them captive.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> Save us, O Lord our God, and gather us from among the heathen, that we may give thanks to thy holy name, that we may glory in thy praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> Blessed be the Lord God of Israel from everlasting and to everlasting; and all the people shall say, Amen, Amen.</span>
</div>
