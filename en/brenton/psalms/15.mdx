<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 24:3–5; Psalms 84:4; Psalms 61:4"> <span className="desc">A writing of David.</span>Keep me, O Lord; for I have hoped in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 28:18; Ephesians 4:25; Colossians 3:9"> I said to the Lord, Thou art my Lord; for thou hast no need of my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. good deeds, or things.">a</a></sup>goodness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 13:10; Psalms 101:5–8; Matthew 7:12"> On behalf of the saints that are in his land, he has magnified all his pleasure in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 11:35; Matthew 5:33; Psalms 101:6"> Their weaknesses have been multiplied; afterward they hasted. I will by no means assemble their bloody meetings, neither will I make mention of their names with my lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 22:25; Deuteronomy 16:19; Jezekiel 22:12"> The Lord is the portion of mine inheritance and of my cup: thou art he that restores my inheritance to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> The lines have fallen to me in the best places, yea, I have a most excellent heritage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> I will bless the Lord who has instructed me; my reins too have chastened me even<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Possibly, during the night. q. d. as long as night lasted.">b</a></sup>till night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> I foresaw the Lord always before my face; for he is on my right hand, that I should not be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Therefore my heart rejoiced and my tongue exulted; moreover also my flesh shall rest in hope:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> because thou wilt not leave my soul in hell, neither wilt thou suffer thine Holy One to see corruption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Thou hast made known to me the ways of life; thou wilt fill me with joy with thy countenance: at thy right hand there are delights for ever.</span>
