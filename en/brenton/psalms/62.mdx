<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25; Psalms 62:2; Psalms 37:7"> <span className="desc">A Psalm of David, when he was in the wilderness of Idumea.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 18:2; 2 Corinthians 4:8–9; Psalms 62:6"> O God, my God, I cry to thee early; my soul has thirsted for thee: how often has my flesh longed after thee, in a barren and trackless and dry land!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 4:14; Psalms 140:2; Psalms 82:2"> Thus have I appeared before thee in the sanctuary, that I might see thy power and thy glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 55:21; Psalms 28:3; Psalms 5:9"> For thy mercy is better than<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. lives.">a</a></sup>life: my lips shall praise thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Michæas 7:7; Psalms 27:13–14; Lamentations 3:24–26"> Thus will I bless thee during my life: I will lift up my hands in thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 112:6; Psalms 62:2; Psalms 16:8"> Let my soul be filled as with marrow and fatness; and my joyful lips shall praise thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 3:3; Esaias 26:4; Psalms 94:22"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. if. See Acts 26. 23.">b</a></sup>Forasmuch as I have remembered thee on my bed: in the early seasons I have meditated on thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 4:6; Lamentations 2:19; Esaias 26:4"> For thou hast been my helper, and in the shelter of thy wings will I rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 39:5; Esaias 40:15; Esaias 40:17"> My soul has<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. been glued.">c</a></sup>kept very close behind thee: thy right hand has upheld me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:17; Psalms 52:7; Jeremias 17:11"> But they vainly sought after my soul; they shall go into the lowest parts of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 19:1; Job 33:14; Esaias 26:4"> They shall be delivered up to the power of the sword; they shall be portions for foxes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 16:27; Job 34:11; Psalms 103:8"> But the king shall rejoice in God; every one that swears by him shall be praised; for the mouth of them that speak unjust things has been stopped.</span>
