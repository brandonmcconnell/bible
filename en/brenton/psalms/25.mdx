<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esaias 43:25; Esaias 38:17; Job 13:26"> to hear the voice of praise, and to declare all thy wonderful works.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 79:9; 1 John 2:12; Esaias 43:25"> But I have walked in my innocence: redeem me, and have mercy upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 37:23; Psalms 32:8; John 7:17"> My foot stands in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. in evenness or uprightness.">b</a></sup>an even place: in the congregations will I bless thee, O Lord.</span>
