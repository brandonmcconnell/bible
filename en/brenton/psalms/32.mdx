<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 4:6–8; Esaias 44:22; Psalms 85:2"> <span className="desc">A Psalm of David.</span><br />Rejoice in the Lord, ye righteous; praise becomes the upright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:47; 2 Corinthians 5:19–21; Revelation 14:5"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Rather, 'confess' or give thanks to.'">a</a></sup>Praise the Lord on the harp; play to him on a psaltery of ten strings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; Psalms 38:8; Psalms 38:3"> Sing to him a new song; play skilfully with a loud noise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings I 5:11; Psalms 38:2–8; Psalms 39:10–11"> For the word of the Lord is right; and all his works are<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Rather, Gr. in or with faithfulness.">b</a></sup>faithful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; Kings II 12:13; Psalms 38:18"> He loves mercy and judgment; the earth is full of the mercy of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esaias 55:6; Esaias 43:2; 2 Corinthians 6:2"> By the word of the Lord the heavens were established; and all the host of them by the breath of his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 91:1; Psalms 119:114; Psalms 9:9"> Who gathers the waters of the sea as in a bottle; who lays up the deeps in treasuries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Psalms 33:18; Psalms 25:8–10"> Let all the earth fear the Lord; and let all that dwell in the world be moved because of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 26:3; Jeremias 4:22; James 3:3"> For he spoke, and they were made; he commanded, and they were created.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esaias 12:2–3; Proverbs 16:20; Proverbs 13:21"> The Lord frustrates the counsels of the nations; he brings to nought also the reasonings of the peoples, and brings to nought the counsels of princes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 64:10; Philippians 4:4; Psalms 97:12"> But the counsel of the Lord endures for ever, the thoughts of his heart from generation to generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Blessed is the nation whose God is the Lord; the people whom he has chosen for his own inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The Lord looks out of heaven; he beholds all the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> He looks from his prepared habitation on all the dwellers on the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> who fashioned their hearts<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Perhaps 'individually.' Vulg. sigillatim.">c</a></sup>alone; who understands all their works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> A king is not saved by reason of a great host; and a giant shall not be delivered by the greatness of his strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> A horse is vain for safety; neither shall he be delivered by the greatness of his power.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Behold, the eyes of the Lord are on them that fear him, those that hope in his mercy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> to deliver their souls from death, and to keep them alive in famine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Our soul waits on the Lord; for he is our helper and defender.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> For our heart shall rejoice in him, and we have hoped in his holy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Let thy mercy, O Lord, be upon us, according as we have hoped in thee.</span>
</div>
</div>
