<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 78:3–6; Exodus 13:14–15; Deuteronomy 6:20"> <span className="desc">For the end, for</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See also Ps. 68; 79, titles; q. d. repeated, see Hebrew.">a</a></sup><span className="desc">alternate strains by the sons of Core; for instruction, a Song concerning the beloved.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 78:55; Exodus 15:17; Joshua 11:23"> My heart<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Lit. eructavit.">b</a></sup>has uttered a good matter: I declare my works to the king: my tongue is the pen of a quick writer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Deuteronomy 8:17–18; Joshua 24:12"> Thou art more beautiful than the sons of men: grace has been shed forth on thy lips: therefore God has blessed thee for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 74:12; Psalms 42:8; Psalms 149:2"> Gird thy sword upon thy thigh, O Mighty One, in thy comeliness, and in thy beauty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 108:13; Psalms 60:12; Psalms 118:10–13"> and bend thy bow, and prosper, and reign, because of truth and meekness and righteousness; and thy right hand shall guide thee wonderfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 20:7; Osee 1:7; Kings I 17:47"> Thy weapons are sharpened, Mighty One, (the nations shall fall under thee) they are in the heart of the king's enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Psalms 132:18; Psalms 136:24"> Thy throne, O God, is for ever and ever: the sceptre of thy kingdom is a sceptre of righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 34:2; 1 Corinthians 1:29–31; Jeremias 9:24"> Thou hast loved righteousness, and hated iniquity: therefore God, thy God, has anointed thee with the oil of gladness beyond thy fellows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 74:1; Psalms 60:10; Psalms 60:1"> Myrrh, and stacte, and cassia are exhaled from thy garments, and out of the ivory palaces,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 7:8; Leviticus 26:17; Joshua 7:12"> with which kings' daughters have gladdened thee for thine honour: the queen stood by on thy right hand, clothed in vesture wrought with gold, and arrayed in divers colours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:27; Deuteronomy 28:64; Romans 8:36"> Hear, O daughter, and see, and incline thine ear; forget also thy people, and thy father's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 15:13; Deuteronomy 32:30; Esaias 52:3–4"> Because the king has desired thy beauty; for he is thy Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 79:4; Psalms 80:6; Psalms 89:41"> And the daughter of Tyre shall adore him with gifts; the rich of the people of the land shall supplicate thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. face.">c</a></sup>favour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremias 24:9; Kings IV 19:21; Job 16:4"> All her glory is that of the daughter of the king<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Alex. ἔσωθεν, within, so Heb.">d</a></sup>of Esebon, robed as she is in golden fringed garments,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 69:7; Jeremias 3:25; Psalms 89:45"> in embroidered clothing: virgins shall be brought to the king after her: her fellows shall be brought to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 8:2; Psalms 74:10; Psalms 74:18"> They shall be brought with gladness and exultation: they shall be led into the king's temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Daniel 9:13; Deuteronomy 8:14; Deuteronomy 6:12"> Instead of thy fathers children are born to thee: thou shalt make them princes over all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 119:51; Psalms 119:157; Psalms 78:57"> They shall make mention of thy name from generation to generation: therefore shall the nations give thanks to thee<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. to the age of the age.">e</a></sup>for ever, even for ever and ever.</span>
