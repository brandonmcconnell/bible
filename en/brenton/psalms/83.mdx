<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 2:2; Revelation 17:13; Esaias 8:9–10"> Blessed are they that dwell in thy house: they will praise thee evermore. Pause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Chronicles I 5:10; Chronicles II 20:1; Psalms 137:7"> Blessed is the man whose help is of thee, O Lord; in his heart he has purposed to go up</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 4:15–24; Esaias 9:4; Judges 5:21"> O Lord God of hosts, hear my prayer: hearken, O God of Jacob. Pause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Sophonias 1:17; Joshua 17:11; Kings I 28:7"> Behold, O God our defender, and look upon the face of thine anointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 7:25; Judges 8:12–21"> For one day in thy courts is better than thousands. I would rather be an abject in the house of God, than dwell in the tents of sinners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Chronicles II 20:11; Psalms 83:4; Psalms 74:7–8"> For the Lord loves mercy and truth: God will give grace and glory: the Lord will not withhold good things from them that walk in innocence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 21:18; Psalms 35:5; Jeremias 13:24"> O Lord of hosts, blessed is the man that trusts in thee.</span>
