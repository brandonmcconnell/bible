<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 14:18; Esaias 12:6; Psalms 132:13–14"> I cried to the Lord with my voice, yea, my voice was addressed to God; and he gave heed to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 46:9; Jezekiel 39:3–4; Jezekiel 39:9–10"> In the day of mine affliction I earnestly sought the Lord; even with my hands by night before him, and I was not deceived; my soul refused to be comforted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jezekiel 19:1–4; Daniel 7:17–28; Jezekiel 19:6"> I remembered God, and rejoiced; I poured out my complaint, and my soul fainted. Pause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Naum 3:18; Psalms 13:3; Esaias 46:12"> All mine enemies set a watch against me: I was troubled, and spoke not.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 15:1; Psalms 80:16; Exodus 15:21"> I considered the days of old, and remembered ancient years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Naum 1:6; Psalms 2:12; Psalms 89:7"> And I meditated; I communed with my heart by night, and diligently searched my spirit, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Chronicles II 20:29–30; Ambacum 2:20; Judges 5:20"> Will the Lord cast off for ever? and will he be well pleased no more?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 5:5; Sophonias 2:3; Esaias 11:4"> Will he cut off his mercy for ever, even for ever and ever?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 9:17; Matthew 24:22; Daniel 3:19–20"> Will God forget to pity? or will he shut up his compassions in his wrath? Pause.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:4–6; Psalms 50:14; Psalms 68:29"> And I said, Now I have begun; this is the change of the right hand of the Most High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Sophonias 3:6; Chronicles II 32:21; Psalms 68:12"> I remembered the works of the Lord; for I will remember thy wonders from the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And I will meditate on all thy works, and will consider thy doings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> O God, thy way is in the sanctuary; who is a great God as our God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Thou art the God that doest wonders; thou hast made known thy power among the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Thou hast with thine arm redeemed thy people, the sons of Jacob and Joseph. Pause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The waters saw thee, O God, the waters saw thee, and feared; and the depths were troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> There was an abundant sound of waters: the clouds uttered a voice; for thine arrows went abroad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> The voice of thy thunder was abroad, and around thy lightnings appeared to the world; the earth trembled and quaked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Thy way is in the sea, and thy paths in many waters, and thy footsteps cannot be known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Thou didst guide thy people as sheep by the hand of Moses and Aaron.</span>
