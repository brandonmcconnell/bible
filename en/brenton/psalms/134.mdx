<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 120:1; Chronicles I 9:33; Psalms 135:1–2"> <span className="desc">Alleluia.</span>Praise ye the name of the Lord; praise the Lord, ye his servants,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 28:2; 1 Timothy 2:8; Psalms 63:4"> who stand in the house of the Lord, in the courts of the house of our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 128:5; Psalms 20:2; Psalms 124:8"> Praise ye the Lord; for the Lord is good: sing praises to his name; for it is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> For the Lord has chosen Jacob for himself, and Israel for his peculiar treasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> For I know that the Lord is great, and our Lord is above all gods;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> all that the Lord willed, he did in heaven, and on the earth, in the sea, and in all deeps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Who brings up clouds from the extremity of the earth: he has made lightnings for the rain: he brings winds out of his treasures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Who smote the first-born of Egypt, both of man and beast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> He sent signs and wonders into the midst of thee, O Egypt, on Pharao, and on all his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Who smote many nations, and slew mighty kings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Seon king of the Amorites, and Og king of Basan, and all the kingdoms of Chanaan:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and gave their land for an inheritance, an inheritance to Israel his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> O Lord, thy name endures for ever, and thy memorial to all generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For the Lord shall judge his people, and comfort himself concerning his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The idols of the heathen are silver and gold, the works of men's hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> They have a mouth, but they cannot speak; they have eyes, but they cannot see;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> they have ears, but they cannot hear; for there is no breath in their mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Let those who make them be made like to them; and all those who trust in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> O house of Israel, bless ye the Lord: O house of Aaron, bless ye the Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> O house of Levi, bless ye the Lord: ye that fear the Lord, bless the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Blessed in Sion be the Lord, who dwells in Jerusalem.</span>
