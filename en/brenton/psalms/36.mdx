<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 3:18; Proverbs 16:6; Psalms 143:12"> <span className="desc">A Psalm of David.</span><br />Fret not thyself because of evil-doers, neither be envious of them that do iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:19; Psalms 10:3; Psalms 49:18"> For they shall soon be withered as the grass, and shall soon fall away as the green herbs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 55:21; Psalms 10:7; Jeremias 4:22"> Hope in the Lord, and do good; and dwell on the land, and thou shalt be fed with the wealth of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Michæas 2:1; Proverbs 4:16; Romans 12:9"> Delight thyself in the Lord; and he shall grant thee the requests of thine heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 100:5; Psalms 108:4; Psalms 103:11"> Disclose thy way to the Lord, and hope in him; and he shall bring it to pass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 77:19; Romans 11:33; Psalms 71:19"> And he shall bring forth thy righteousness as the light, and thy judgment as the noon-day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 86:5; Psalms 91:4; Ruth 2:12"> Submit thyself to the Lord, and supplicate him: fret not thyself because of him that prospers in his way, at the man that does unlawful deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 16:11; Psalms 65:4; Esaias 58:11"> Cease from anger, and forsake wrath: fret not thyself so as to do evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 8:12; John 4:14; 1 John 1:7"> For evil-doers shall be destroyed: but they that wait on the Lord, they shall inherit the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 24:7; Hebrews 8:11; Psalms 97:10–11"> And yet a little while, and the sinner shall not be, and thou shalt seek for his place, and shalt not find it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 16:8; Psalms 17:8–14; Psalms 119:51"> But the meek shall inherit the earth; and shall delight themselves in the abundance of peace.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 1:5; Psalms 58:10–11; Psalms 55:23"> The sinner will watch for the righteous, and gnash his teeth upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> But the Lord shall laugh at him: for he foresees that his day will come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Sinners have drawn their swords, they have bent their bow, to cast down the poor and needy one, and to slay the upright in heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Let their sword enter into their own heart, and their bows be broken.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> A little is better to the righteous than abundant wealth of sinners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> For the arms of sinners shall be broken; but the Lord supports the righteous.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The Lord knows the ways of the perfect; and their inheritance shall be for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> They shall not be ashamed in an evil time; and in days of famine they shall be satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> For the sinners shall perish; and the enemies of the Lord at the moment of their being honoured and exalted have utterly vanished like smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The sinner borrows, and will not pay again: but the righteous has compassion, and gives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> For they that bless him shall inherit the earth; and they that curse him shall be utterly destroyed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> The steps of a man are rightly ordered by the Lord: and he will take pleasure in his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> When he falls, he shall not be ruined: for the Lord supports his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> I was once young, indeed I am now old; yet I have not seen the righteous forsaken, nor his seed seeking bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> He is merciful, and lends continually; and his seed shall be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. for a blessing.">a</a></sup>blessed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Turn aside from evil, and do good; and dwell for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> For the Lord loves judgment, and will not forsake his saints; they shall be preserved for ever: the blameless shall be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, cleared in judgement.">b</a></sup>avenged, but the seed of the ungodly shall be utterly destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> But the righteous shall inherit the earth, and dwell upon it for ever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> The mouth of the righteous will meditate wisdom, and his tongue will speak of judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> The law of his God is in his heart; and his steps shall not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. be tripped up, or supplanted.">c</a></sup>slide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> The sinner watches the righteous, and seeks to slay him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> But the Lord will not leave him in his hands, nor by any means condemn him when he is judged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Wait on the Lord, and keep his way, and he shall exalt thee to inherit the land: when the wicked are destroyed, thou shalt see it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> I saw the ungodly very highly exalting himself, and lifting himself up like the cedars of Libanus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Yet I passed by, and lo! he was not: and I sought him, but his place was not found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Maintain innocence, and behold uprightness: for there is a remnant to the peaceable man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> But the transgressors shall be utterly destroyed together: the remnants of the ungodly shall be utterly destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> But the salvation of the righteous is of the Lord; and he is their defender in the time of affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And the Lord shall help them, and deliver them: and he shall rescue them from sinners, and save them, because they have hoped in him.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
