<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Michæas 3:12; Chronicles II 36:19; Psalms 74:1–4"> <span className="desc">For the</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Ps. 44, and 68, titles.">a</a></sup><span className="desc">end, for alternate strains, a testimony for Asaph, a Psalm concerning the Assyrian.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 7:33; Jeremias 34:20; Jeremias 16:4"> Attend, O Shepherd of Israel, who guidest Joseph like a flock; thou who sittest upon the cherubs, manifest thyself;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 16:4; Jeremias 14:16; Revelation 11:9"> before Ephraim and Benjamin and Manasse, stir up thy power, and come to deliver us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 80:6; Lamentations 5:1; Psalms 89:41"> Turn us, O God, and cause thy face to shine; and we shall be delivered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 89:46; Psalms 74:1; Psalms 85:5"> O Lord God of hosts, how long art thou angry with the prayer of thy servant?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremias 10:25; 2 Thessalonians 1:8; Psalms 14:4"> Thou wilt feed us with bread of tears; and wilt cause us to drink tears by measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremias 51:34–35; Zacharias 1:15; Esaias 64:10–11"> Thou hast made us a strife to our neighbours; and our enemies have mocked at us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esaias 64:9; Psalms 142:6; Psalms 116:6"> Turn us, O Lord God of hosts, and cause thy face to shine; and we shall be saved. Pause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremias 14:7; Psalms 25:11; Chronicles II 14:11"> Thou hast transplanted a vine out of Egypt: thou hast cast out the heathen, and planted it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 42:10; Exodus 7:5; Psalms 115:2"> Thou madest a way before it, and didst cause its roots to strike, and the land was filled with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 102:20; Psalms 146:6–7; Esaias 33:2"> Its shadow covered the mountains, and its shoots equalled the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. Cedars of God, see Gen. 30. 8; Jon. 3. 3; Acts 7. 20.">b</a></sup>goodly cedars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 32:18; Genesis 4:15; Leviticus 26:28"> It sent forth its branches to the sea, and its shoots to the river.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 95:7; Esaias 43:21; Psalms 74:1"> Wherefore hast thou broken down its hedge, while all that pass by the way pluck it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The boar out of the wood has laid it waste, and the wild beast has devoured it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> O God of hosts, turn, we pray thee: look on us from heaven, and behold and visit this vine;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> and restore that which thy right hand has planted: and look on the son of man whom thou didst strengthen for thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> It is burnt with fire and dug up: they shall perish at the rebuke of thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. See Ps. 20. 9.">c</a></sup>presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Let thy hand be upon the man of thy right hand, and upon the son of man whom thou didst strengthen for thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> So will we not depart from thee: thou shalt quicken us, and we will call upon thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Turn us, O Lord God of hosts, and make thy face to shine; and we shall be saved.</span>
