<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 89:1; Psalms 71:22–23; Psalms 145:7"> <span className="desc">A Prayer for the Poor; when he is deeply afflicted, and pours out his supplication before the Lord.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 119:106; Kings III 9:4; 1 Timothy 3:4–5"> Hear my prayer, O Lord, and let my cry come to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 119:37; Job 31:1; Kings II 11:2–3"> Turn not away thy face from me: in the day when I am afflicted, incline thine ear to me: in the day when I shall call upon thee, speedily hear me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 11:20; 2 Corinthians 6:14–16; Matthew 7:23"> For my days have vanished like smoke, and my bones have been parched like a stick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 15:3; Psalms 18:27; Proverbs 30:13"> I am blighted like grass, and my heart is dried up; for I have forgotten to eat my bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 119:63; Luke 12:43–44; Revelation 21:3"> By reason of the voice of my groaning, my bone has cleaved to my flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings II 4:10–12; Acts 5:1–10; Psalms 52:2"> I have become like a pelican of the wilderness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 75:10; Jeremias 21:12; Proverbs 16:12"> I have become like an owl in a ruined house. I have watched, and am become as a sparrow dwelling alone on a roof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> All the day long mine enemies have reproached me; and they that praised me have sworn against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> For I have eaten ashes as it were bread, and mingled my drink with weeping;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> because of thine anger and thy wrath: for thou hast lifted me up, and dashed me down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> My days have declined like a shadow; and I am withered like grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> But thou, Lord, endurest for ever, and thy memorial to generation and generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Thou shalt arise, and have mercy upon Sion: for it is time to have mercy upon her, for the set time is come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> For thy servants have taken pleasure in her stones, and they shall pity her dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> So the nations shall fear thy name, O Lord, and all kings thy glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> For the Lord shall build up Sion,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, then shall be.">a</a></sup>and shall appear in his glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> He has had regard to the prayer of the lowly, and has not despised their petition.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Let this be written for another generation; and the people that shall be created shall praise the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> For he has looked out from the height of his sanctuary; the Lord looked upon the earth from heaven;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> to hear the groaning of the fettered ones, to loosen the sons of the slain;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> to proclaim the name of the Lord in Sion, and his praise in Jerusalem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> when the people are gathered together, and the kings, to serve the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> He answered him in the way of his strength: tell me the fewness of my days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Take me not away in the midst of my days: thy years are through all generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> In the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. plural.">b</a></sup>beginning thou, O Lord, didst lay the foundation of the earth; and the heavens are the works of thine hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> They shall perish, but thou remainest: and they all shall wax old as a garment; and as a vesture shalt thou fold them, and they shall be changed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> But thou art the same, and thy years shall not fail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> The children of thy servants shall dwell securely, and their seed shall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, be directed aright.">c</a></sup>prosper for ever.</span>
