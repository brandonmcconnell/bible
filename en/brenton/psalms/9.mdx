<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 86:12; Chronicles I 16:24; Psalms 145:1–3"> <span className="desc">For the end, a Psalm of David, concerning the secrets of the Son.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 83:18; Psalms 5:11; Psalms 7:17"> I will give thanks to thee, O Lord, with my whole heart; I will recount all thy wonderful works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:9; Revelation 6:12–17; Psalms 80:16"> I will be glad and exult in thee: I will sing to thy name, O thou Most High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 140:12; 1 Peter 2:23; Psalms 98:9"> When mine enemies are turned back, they shall be feeble and perish at thy presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; Deuteronomy 9:14; Proverbs 13:9"> For thou hast maintained my cause and my right; thou satest on the throne, that judgest righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esaias 14:22–23; Exodus 14:13; Michæas 7:8"> Thou hast rebuked the nations, and the ungodly one has perished; thou hast blotted out their name for ever, even for ever and ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 103:19; Hebrews 13:8; Psalms 102:12"> The swords of the enemy have failed utterly; and thou hast destroyed cities: their memorial has been destroyed with a noise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 96:13; Psalms 98:9; Revelation 20:12–13"> but the Lord endures for ever: he has prepared his throne for judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 18:10; Psalms 91:1–2; Naum 1:7"> And he will judge the world in righteousness, he will judge the nations in uprightness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 91:14; Proverbs 18:10; Esaias 26:3–4"> The Lord also is become a refuge for the poor, a seasonable help, in affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 107:22; Psalms 96:1–2; Psalms 148:1–5"> And let them that know thy name hope in thee: for thou, O Lord, hast not failed them that diligently seek thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 34:6; Exodus 3:7; Revelation 16:6"> Sing praises to the Lord, who dwells in Sion: declare his dealings among the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 86:13; Psalms 30:3; Psalms 107:18"> For he remembered them, in making inquisition for blood: he has not forgotten the supplication of the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 13:5; Psalms 51:12; Psalms 106:2"> Have mercy upon me, O Lord; look upon my affliction which I suffer of mine enemies, thou that liftest me up from the gates of death:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 7:15–16; Psalms 57:6; Psalms 35:8"> that I may declare all thy praises in the gates of the daughter of Sion: I will exult in thy salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 7:5; Judges 1:7; Proverbs 12:13"> The heathen are caught in the destruction which they planned: in the very snare which they hid is their foot taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 50:22; Job 8:13; Romans 2:8–9"> The Lord is known as executing judgments: the sinner is taken in the works of his hands. A song of Pause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 12:5; Proverbs 23:18; Proverbs 24:14"> Let sinners be driven away into Hades, even all the nations that forget God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 3:7; Psalms 44:23; Psalms 7:6"> For the poor shall not be forgotten for ever: the patience of the needy ones shall not perish for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 62:9; Psalms 82:6–7; Esaias 31:3"> Arise, O Lord, let not man prevail: let the heathen be judged before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Appoint, O Lord, a lawgiver over them: let the heathen know that they are men. Pause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Note.—Here begins a change of numbers as compared with the Heb. which continues to Ps. 146, this psalm having no number, and the eleventh being called the tenth. The following verses 22-39 are numbered 1-18 in the original text.">a</a></sup>Why standest thou afar off, O Lord? why dost thou overlook us in times of need, in affliction?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> While the ungodly one acts proudly, the poor is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. inflamed, as in a fever.">b</a></sup>hotly pursued: the wicked are taken in the crafty counsels which they imagine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Because the sinner praises himself for the desires of his heart; and the unjust one blesses himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> The sinner has provoked the Lord: according to the abundance of his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, anger.">c</a></sup>pride he will not seek after him: God is not before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> His ways are<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. profaned.">d</a></sup>profane at all times; thy judgments are removed from before him: he will gain the mastery over all his enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> For he has said in his heart, I shall not be moved, continuing without evil from generation to generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Whose mouth is full of cursing, and bitterness, and fraud: under his tongue are trouble and pain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> He lies in wait with rich men in secret places, in order to slay the innocent: his eyes are<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. look.">e</a></sup>set against the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> He lies in wait in secret as a lion in his den: he lies in wait to ravish the poor, to ravish the poor when he draws him after him: he will bring him down in his snare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> He will bow down and fall when he has mastered the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> For he has said in his heart, God has forgotten: he has turned away his face so as never to look.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Arise, O Lord God; let thy hand be lifted up: forget not the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Wherefore has the wicked provoked God? for he has said in his heart, He will not require it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Thou seest it; for thou dost observe trouble and wrath, to deliver them into thy hands: the poor has been left to thee; thou wast a helper to the orphan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Break thou the arm of the sinner and wicked man: his sin shall be sought for, and shall not be found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> The Lord shall reign for ever, even for ever and ever: ye Gentiles shall perish out of his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> The Lord has heard the desire of the poor: thine ear has inclined to the preparation of their heart;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> to plead for the orphan and afflicted, that man may no more boast upon the earth.</span>
</div>
