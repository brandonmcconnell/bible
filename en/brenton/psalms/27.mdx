<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 118:12; Psalms 14:4; Psalms 9:3"> Hearken to the voice of my supplication, when I pray to thee, when I lift up my hands toward thy holy temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 3:6; 1 Peter 3:14; Romans 8:35–37"> Draw not away my soul with sinners, and destroy me not with the workers of iniquity, who speak peace with their neighbours, but evils are in their hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 26:8; Psalms 23:6; Psalms 65:4"> Give them according to their works, and according to the wickedness of their devices: give them according to the works of their hands; render their recompence unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 18:10; Psalms 31:20; Psalms 138:7"> Because they have not attended to the works of the Lord, even to the works of his hands, thou shalt pull them down, and shalt not build them up.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 130:2–4; Psalms 13:3; Psalms 5:2"> The Lord is my helper and my defender; my heart has hoped in him, and I am helped: my flesh has revived, and willingly will I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, confess to him.">a</a></sup>give praise to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 105:4; Jeremias 29:12–13; Esaias 55:6–7"> The Lord is the strength of his people, and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. defender of the salvation of.">b</a></sup>saving defender of his anointed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 69:17; Psalms 143:7; Psalms 102:2"> Save thy people, and bless thine inheritance: and take care of them, and lift them up for ever.</span>
