<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 3:27; Numbers 3:19"> Take the sum of the children of Caath from the midst of the sons of Levi, after their families, according to the houses of their fathers' households;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 4:23; Chronicles I 23:3–5; Kings IV 11:4–12"> from twenty-five years old and upward until fifty years, every one that goes in to minister, to do all the works in the tabernacle of witness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 4:19; Mark 13:34; Numbers 4:30"> And these are the works of the sons of Caath in the tabernacle of witness; it is most holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; Hebrews 9:3; Exodus 40:3"> And Aaron and his sons shall go in, when the camp is about to move, and shall take down the shadowing veil, and shall cover with it the ark of the testimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 25:13–15; Exodus 35:19; Numbers 4:7–8"> And they shall put on it a cover, even a blue skin, and put on it above a garment all of blue, and shall put the staves through the rings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 24:5–8; Exodus 37:10–16; Exodus 25:23–30"> And they shall put on the table set forth for shew-bread a cloth all of purple, and the dishes, and the censers, and the cups, and the vessels with which one offers drink-offerings; and the continual loaves shall be upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 4:6–7; Numbers 4:11–13; Numbers 4:9"> And they shall put upon it a scarlet cloth, and they shall cover it with a blue covering of skin, and they shall put the staves into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–39; Exodus 37:17–24; Revelation 1:20–1"> And they shall take a blue covering, and cover the candlestick that gives light, and its lamps, and its snuffers, and its funnels, and all the vessels of oil with which they minister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 4:12; Numbers 4:6"> And they shall put it, and all its vessels, into a blue skin cover; and they shall put it on bearers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 40:5; Exodus 30:1–19; Exodus 40:26–27"> And they shall put a blue cloth for a cover on the golden altar, and shall cover it with a blue skin cover, and put in its staves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Chronicles I 9:29; Exodus 25:9; Chronicles II 4:22"> And they shall take all the instruments of service, with which they minister in the sanctuary: and shall place them in a cloth of blue, and shall cover them with blue skin covering, and put them upon staves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 6:12–13; Exodus 39:1; Exodus 39:41"> And he shall put the covering on the altar, and they shall cover it with a cloth all of purple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Chronicles II 4:19; Exodus 38:1–7"> And they shall put upon it all the vessels with which they minister upon it, and the fire-pans, and the flesh-hooks, and the cups, and the cover, and all the vessels of the altar; and they shall put on it a blue cover of skins, and shall put in its staves; and they shall take a purple cloth, and cover the laver and its foot, and they shall put it into a blue cover of skin, and put it on bars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings II 6:6–7; Numbers 7:9; Deuteronomy 31:9"> And Aaron and his sons shall finish covering the holy things, and all the holy vessels, when the camp begins to move; and afterwards the sons of Caath shall go in to take up the furniture; but shall not touch the holy things, lest they die: these shall the sons of Caath bear in the tabernacle of witness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 25:6; Leviticus 24:2; Exodus 30:23–38"> Eleazar the son of Aaron the priest is overseer—the oil of the light, and the incense of composition, and the daily meat-offering and the anointing oil, are his charge; even the oversight of the whole tabernacle, and all things that are in it in the holy place, in all the works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings II 6:6–7; Numbers 17:10; Numbers 18:5"> Ye shall not destroy the family of Caath from the tribe out of the midst of the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 4:15"> This do ye to them, and they shall live and not die, when they approach the holy of holies: Let Aaron and his sons advance, and they shall place them each in his post for bearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings I 6:19; Exodus 19:21; Numbers 4:15"> And so they shall by no means go in to look suddenly upon the holy things, and die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 3:18; Numbers 3:21; Numbers 3:24"> Take the sum of the children of Gedson, and these according to the houses of their lineage, according to their families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 4:3; 1 Timothy 1:18; 1 Corinthians 9:7"> Take the number of them from five and twenty years old and upwards until the age of fifty, every one that goes in to minister, to do his business in the tabernacle of witness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 4:19; Numbers 4:31–32; Numbers 4:47"> This is the public service of the family of Gedson, to minister and to bear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 3:25–26; Exodus 26:14; Numbers 7:5–7"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. it, i. e. the family.">a</a></sup>they shall bear the skins of the tabernacle, and the tabernacle of witness, and its veil, and the blue cover that was on it above, and the cover of the door of the tabernacle of witness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 35:18; Exodus 27:9"> And all the curtains of the court which were upon the tabernacle of witness, and the appendages, and all the vessels of service that they minister with they shall attend to.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 1:70; 1 Corinthians 11:2"> According to the direction of Aaron and his sons shall be the ministry of the sons of Gedson, in all their ministries, and in all their works; and thou shalt take account of them by name in all things borne by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 4:33; 1 Corinthians 12:5–6"> This is the service of the sons of Gedson in the tabernacle of witness, and their charge by the hand of Ithamar the son of Aaron the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 3:33–35"> The sons of Merari according to their families, according to the houses of their lineage, take ye the number of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 4:3; Numbers 4:23; 2 Timothy 2:4"> Take the number of them from five and twenty years old and upwards until fifty years old, every one that goes in to perform the services of the tabernacle of witness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 3:36–37; Exodus 26:15; Numbers 7:8–9"> And these are the charges of the things borne by them according to all their works in the tabernacle of witness: they shall bear the chapiters of the tabernacle, and the bars, and its pillars, and its sockets, and the veil, and there shall be their sockets, and their pillars, and the curtain of the door of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 38:21; Chronicles I 9:29; Numbers 3:8"> And they shall bear the pillars of the court round about, and there shall be their sockets, and they shall bear the pillars of the veil of the door of the court, and their sockets and their pins, and their cords, and all their furniture, and all their instruments of service: take ye their number by name, and all the articles of the charge of the things borne by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 4:28; Joshua 3:6; Esaias 3:6"> This is the ministration of the family of the sons of Merari in all their works in the tabernacle of witness, by the hand of Ithamar the son of Aaron the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And Moses and Aaron and the rulers of Israel took the number of the sons of Caath according to their families, according to the houses of their lineage;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Chronicles I 23:24; Numbers 4:23; Chronicles I 23:3"> from five and twenty years old and upwards to the age of fifty years, every one that goes in to minister and do service in the tabernacle of witness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And the numbering of them according to their families was two thousand,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Alex. 350.">b</a></sup>seven hundred and fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> This is the numbering of the family of Caath, every one that ministers in the tabernacle of witness, as Moses and Aaron numbered them by the word of the Lord, by the hand of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And the sons of Gedson were numbered according to their families, according to the houses of their lineage,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> from five and twenty years old and upward till fifty years old, every one that goes in to minister and to do the services in the tabernacle of witness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 3:32"> And the numbering of them according to their families, according to the houses of their lineage, was two thousand six hundred and thirty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> This is the numbering of the family of the sons of Gedson, every one who ministers in the tabernacle of witness; whom Moses and Aaron numbered by the word of the Lord, by the hand of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And also the family of the sons of Merari were numbered according to their divisions, according to the house of their fathers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> from five and twenty years old and upward till fifty years old, every one that goes in to minister in the services of the tabernacle of witness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10; 1 Corinthians 12:8–12"> And the numbering of them according to their families, according to the houses of their lineage, was three thousand and two hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Numbers 4:29"> This is the numbering of the family of the sons of Merari, whom Moses and Aaron numbered by the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. voice.">c</a></sup>word of the Lord, by the hand of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> All that were numbered, whom Moses and Aaron and the rulers of Israel numbered, namely, the Levites, according to their families and according to the houses of their lineage,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:4–31; Romans 12:6–8; Numbers 4:3"> from five and twenty years old and upward till fifty years old, every one that goes in to the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. work.">d</a></sup>service of the works, and the charge of the things that are carried in the tabernacle of witness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Numbers 3:39; Matthew 20:16; Matthew 22:15"> And they that were numbered were eight thousand<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Alex. 450.">e</a></sup>five hundred and eighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Numbers 1:54; Numbers 4:31; Numbers 4:15"> He reviewed them by the word of the Lord by the hand of Moses, appointing each man severally over their respective work, and over their burdens; and they were numbered, as the Lord commanded Moses.</span>
