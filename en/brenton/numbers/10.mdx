<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esaias 1:13; Joel 1:14; Psalms 81:3"> Make to thyself two silver trumpets: thou shalt make them of beaten work; and they shall be to thee for the purpose of calling the assembly, and of removing the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i. e. the successive encampments.">a</a></sup>camps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 4:5; Joel 2:15–16"> And thou shalt sound with them, and all the congregation shall be gathered to the door of the tabernacle of witness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 7:2; Exodus 18:21; Numbers 1:4–16"> And if they shall sound with one, all the rulers even the princes of Israel shall come to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 2:3–9; Numbers 10:14; Esaias 58:1"> And ye shall sound an alarm, and the camps pitched eastward shall begin to move.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 10:18; Numbers 2:10–16"> And ye shall sound a second alarm, and the camps pitched southward shall move; and ye shall sound a third alarm, and the camps pitched westward shall move forward; and ye shall sound a fourth alarm, and they that encamp toward the north shall move forward: they shall sound an alarm at their departure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joel 2:1; Numbers 10:3–4"> And whenever ye shall gather the assembly, ye shall sound, but not an alarm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 6:4–16; Numbers 31:6; Chronicles I 15:24"> And the priests the sons of Aaron shall sound with the trumpets; and it shall be a perpetual ordinance for you throughout your generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings I 10:18; Judges 2:18; Genesis 8:1"> And if ye shall go forth to war in your land against your enemies that are opposed to you, then shall ye sound with the trumpets; and ye shall be had in remembrance before the Lord, and ye shall be saved from your enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 81:3; 1 Thessalonians 4:16; 1 Corinthians 11:24–26"> And in the days of your gladness, and in your feasts, and in your new moons, ye shall sound with the trumpets at your whole-burnt-offerings, and at the sacrifices of your peace-offerings; and there shall be a memorial for you before your God: I am the Lord your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 40:17; Numbers 1:1; Numbers 9:11"> And it came to pass in the second year, in the second month, on the twentieth day of the month, the cloud went up from the tabernacle of witness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 21:21; Numbers 12:16; Numbers 9:5"> And the children of Israel set forward with their baggage in the wilderness of Sina; and the cloud rested in the wilderness of Pharan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:6; Numbers 9:23"> And the first rank departed by the word of the Lord by the hand of Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 2:3–9; Numbers 1:7; Numbers 7:12"> And they first set in motion the order of the camp of the children of Juda with their host; and over their host was Naasson, son of Aminadab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 7:18; Numbers 1:8"> And over the host of the tribe of the sons of Issachar, was Nathanael son of Sogar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 7:24; Numbers 1:9"> And over the host of the tribe of the sons of Zabulon, was Eliab the son of Chælon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 7:6–8; Numbers 1:51; Hebrews 12:28"> And they shall take down the tabernacle, and the sons of Gedson shall set forward, and the sons of Merari, who bear the tabernacle.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 2:10–16; Numbers 1:5; Numbers 26:5–18"> And the order of the camp of Ruben set forward with their host; and over their host was Elisur the son of Sediur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 1:6; Numbers 7:36"> And over the host of the tribe of the sons of Symeon, was Salamiel son of Surisadai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 1:14; Numbers 2:14; Numbers 7:42"> And over the host of the tribe of the children of Gad, was Elisaph the son of Raguel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 10:17; Numbers 7:9; Numbers 4:4–16"> And the sons of Caath shall set forward bearing the holy things, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i. e. the Gershonites and the Merarites. A. V. margin.">b</a></sup>the others shall set up the tabernacle until they arrive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 2:18–24; Numbers 1:10; Numbers 26:23–41"> And the order of the camp of Ephraim shall set forward with their forces; and over their forces was Elisama the son of Semiud.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 1:10; Numbers 7:54"> And over the forces of the tribe of the sons of Manasse, was Gamaliel the son of Phadassur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 1:11; Numbers 7:60"> And over the forces of the tribe of the children of Benjamin, was Abidan the son of Gadeoni.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 6:9; Numbers 1:12; Genesis 49:16–17"> And the order of the camp of the sons of Dan shall set forward the last of all the camps, with their forces: and over their forces was Achiezer the son of Amisadai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 1:13; Numbers 7:72"> And over the forces of the tribe of the sons of Aser, was Phageel the son of Echran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 1:15; Numbers 7:78"> And over the forces of the tribe of the sons of Nephthali, was Achire the son of Ænan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 2:34; Numbers 24:4–5; 1 Corinthians 14:33"> These are the armies of the children of Israel; and they set forward with their forces.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 12:7; Judges 4:11; Exodus 3:1"> And Moses said to Obab the son of Raguel the Madianite, the father-in-law of Moses, We are going forward to the place concerning which the Lord said, This will I give to you: Come with us, and we will do thee good, for the Lord has spoken good concerning Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ruth 1:15–17; Psalms 45:10; Matthew 21:29"> And he said to him, I will not go, but I will go to my land and to my kindred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Job 29:15; Galatians 6:2; 1 Corinthians 12:14–21"> And he said, Leave us not, because thou hast been with us in the wilderness, and thou shalt be an elder among us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Judges 1:16; Deuteronomy 10:18; 1 John 1:3"> And it shall come to pass if thou wilt go with us, it shall even come to pass that in whatsoever things the Lord shall do us good, we will also do thee good.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:33; Exodus 3:1; Psalms 95:11"> And they departed from the mount of the Lord a three days' journey; and the ark of the covenant of the Lord went before them a three days' journey to provide rest for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:12; Nehemiah 9:19; Psalms 105:39"> And it came to pass when the ark set forward, that Moses said, Arise, O Lord, and let thine enemies be scattered: let all that hate thee flee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 68:1–2; Esaias 17:12–14; Psalms 132:8"> And in the resting he said, Turn again, O Lord, the thousands and tens of thousands in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:10; Psalms 90:13–17; Esaias 63:17"> And the cloud overshadowed them by day, when they departed from the camp.</span>
</div>
