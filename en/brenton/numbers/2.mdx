<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:40; Numbers 10:22; Numbers 1:50"> Let the children of Israel encamp fronting each other, every man keeping his own rank, according to their standards, according to the houses of their families; the children of Israel shall encamp round about the tabernacle of witness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Chronicles I 2:10; Ruth 4:20; Luke 3:32–33"> And they that encamp first toward the east shall be the order of the camp of Juda with their host, and the prince of the sons of Juda, Naasson the son of Aminadab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 26:22; Numbers 1:27"> His forces that were numbered, were seventy-four thousand and six hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 1:8; Numbers 7:18; Numbers 7:23"> And they that encamp next shall be of the tribe of Issachar, and the prince of the sons of Issachar shall be Nathanael the son of Sogar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 26:25; Numbers 1:29"> His forces that were numbered, were fifty-four thousand and four hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 1:9; Numbers 7:24; Numbers 10:16"> And they that encamp next shall be of the tribe of Zabulon, and the prince of the sons of Zabulon shall be Eliab the son of Chælon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 26:26–27; Numbers 1:31"> His forces that were numbered, were fifty-seven thousand and four hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 10:14"> All that were numbered of the camp of Juda were a hundred and eighty thousand and six thousand and four hundred: they shall move first with their forces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 1:5; Genesis 49:3–4; Numbers 7:35"> This is the order of the camp of Ruben; their forces shall be toward the south, and the prince of the children of Ruben shall be Elisur the son of Sediur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 26:7; Numbers 1:21"> His forces that were numbered, were forty-six thousand and five hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 1:6; Numbers 10:19; Numbers 7:41"> And they that encamp next to him shall be of the tribe of Symeon, and the prince of the sons of Symeon shall be Salamiel the son of Surisadai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 26:14; Numbers 1:23"> His forces that were numbered, were fifty-nine thousand and three hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 1:14; Numbers 7:42; Numbers 7:47"> And they that encamp next to them shall be the tribe of Gad; and the prince of the sons of Gad, Elisaph the son of Raguel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 26:18; Numbers 1:25"> His forces that were numbered, were forty-five thousand and six hundred and fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 10:18; Numbers 2:9; Numbers 2:24"> All who were numbered of the camp of Ruben, were a hundred and fifty-one thousand and four hundred and fifty: they with their forces shall proceed in the second place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 10:21; Numbers 10:17; Numbers 3:38"> And then the tabernacle of witness shall be set forward, and the camp of the Levites shall be between the camps; as they shall encamp, so also shall they commence their march, each one next in order to his fellow according to their companies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 1:10; Numbers 10:22; Genesis 48:14–20"> The station of the camp of Ephraim shall be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. by the sea.">a</a></sup>westward with their forces, and the head of the children of Ephraim shall be Elisama the son of Emiud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 1:33; Numbers 26:37"> His forces that were numbered, are forty thousand and five hundred.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 1:10; Numbers 7:54; Numbers 10:23"> And they that encamp next shall be of the tribe of Manasse, and the prince of the sons of Manasse, Gamaliel the son of Phadassur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 26:34; Numbers 1:35"> His forces that were numbered, were thirty-two thousand and two hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 1:11; Numbers 7:60; Numbers 10:24"> And they that encamp next shall be of the tribe of Benjamin, and the prince of the sons of Benjamin, Abidan the son of Gadeoni.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 1:37; Numbers 26:41"> His forces that were numbered, were thirty-five thousand and four hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 10:22; Numbers 2:31; Psalms 80:2"> All that were numbered of the camp of Ephraim, were one hundred and eight thousand and one hundred: they with their forces shall set out third.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 1:12; Numbers 7:66; Numbers 10:25"> The order of the camp of Dan shall be northward with their forces; and the prince of the sons of Dan, Achiezer the son of Amisadai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 26:43; Numbers 1:39"> His forces that were numbered, were sixty-two thousand and seven hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 1:13; Numbers 7:77; Numbers 7:72"> And they that encamp next to him shall be the tribe of Aser; and the prince of the sons of Aser, Phagiel the son of Echran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 1:41; Numbers 26:47"> His forces that were numbered, were forty-one thousand and five hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 1:15; Numbers 10:27; Numbers 26:48–50"> And they that encamp next shall be of the tribe of Nephthali; and the prince of the children of Nephthali, Achire son of Ænan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 1:42–43; Numbers 26:50"> His forces that were numbered were fifty-three thousand and four hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 10:25; Numbers 2:16; Numbers 2:9"> All that were numbered of the camp of Dan, were a hundred and fifty-seven thousand and six hundred: they shall set out last according to their order.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 1:46; Exodus 38:26; Numbers 2:9"> This is the numbering of the children of Israel according to the houses of their families: all the numbering of the camps with their forces, was six hundred and three thousand, five hundred and fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 26:57–62; Numbers 1:47–49"> But the Levites were not numbered with them, as the Lord commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 119:6; Numbers 24:2; Numbers 24:5–6"> And the children of Israel did all things that the Lord commanded Moses; thus they encamped in their order, and thus they began their march in succession each according to their divisions, according to the houses of their families.</span>
</div>
