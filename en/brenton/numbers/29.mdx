<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 28:27; Numbers 28:19; Numbers 29:36"> And ye shall offer whole-burnt-offerings for a sweet savour to the Lord, one calf of the herd, one ram, seven lambs of a year old without blemish.</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 5:4; Leviticus 16:29–31; Zacharias 12:10"> And on the tenth of this month there shall be to you a holy convocation; and ye shall afflict your souls, and ye shall do no work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 28:19; Numbers 29:13; Numbers 29:2"> And ye shall bring near whole-burnt-offerings for a sweet-smelling savour to the Lord; burnt-sacrifices to the Lord, one calf of the herd, one ram, seven lambs of a year old; they shall be to you without blemish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 15:3–12"> Their meat-offering shall be fine flour mingled with oil; three tenth deals for one calf, and two tenth deals for one ram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> A tenth deal for each several lamb, for the seven lambs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 16:3; Leviticus 16:5; Numbers 28:3–8"> And one kid of the goats for a sin-offering, to make atonement for you; beside the sin-offering for atonement, and the continual whole-burnt-offering, its meat-offering, and its drink-offering according to its ordinance for a smell of sweet savour, a burnt-sacrifice to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:13–14; Zacharias 14:16–19; Exodus 34:22"> And on the fifteenth day of this seventh month ye shall have a holy convocation; ye shall do no servile work; and ye shall keep it a feast to the Lord seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 28:11; Ezra and Nehemiah 3:4; Numbers 29:2"> And ye shall bring near whole-burnt-offerings, a sacrifice for a smell of sweet savour to the Lord, on the first day thirteen calves of the herd, two rams, fourteen lambs of a year old; they shall be without blemish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Their meat-offerings shall be fine flour mingled with oil; there shall be three tenth deals for one calf, for the thirteen calves; and two tenth deals for one ram, for the two rams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> A tenth deal for every lamb, for the fourteen lambs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And one kid of the goats for a sin-offering; beside the continual whole-burnt-offering: there shall be their meat-offerings and their drink-offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 8:13; Psalms 50:8–9; Psalms 69:31"> And on the second day twelve calves, two rams, fourteen lambs of a year old without blemish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 28:7; Numbers 15:4–12; Numbers 28:14"> Their meat-offering and their drink-offering shall be for the calves and the rams and the lambs according to their number, according to their ordinance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 29:11; Numbers 29:25; Numbers 29:22"> And one kid of the goats for a sin-offering; beside the perpetual whole-burnt-offering; their meat-offerings and their drink-offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> On the third day eleven calves, two rams, fourteen lambs of a year old without blemish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Their meat-offering and their drink-offering shall be to the calves and to the rams and to the lambs according to their number, according to their ordinance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joel 1:13; Psalms 16:4; Joel 2:14"> And one kid of the goats for a sin-offering; beside the continual whole-burnt-offering; there shall be their meat-offerings and their drink-offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> On the fourth day ten calves, two rams, fourteen lambs of a year old without spot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> There shall be their meat-offerings and their drink-offerings to the calves and the rams and the lambs according to their number, according to their ordinance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 13:43; Galatians 2:5; Galatians 6:9"> And one kid of the goats for a sin-offering; beside the continual whole-burnt-offering there shall be their meat-offerings and their drink-offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> On the fifth day nine calves, two rams, fourteen lambs of a year old without spot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Their meat-offerings and their drink-offerings shall be to the calves and the rams and the lambs according to their number, according to their ordinance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And one kid of the goats for a sin-offering; beside the perpetual whole-burnt-offering; there shall be their meat-offerings and their drink-offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> On the sixth day eight calves, two rams, fourteen lambs of a year old without blemish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> There shall be their meat-offerings and their drink-offerings to the calves and rams and lambs according to their number, according to their ordinance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And one kid of the goats for a sin-offering; beside the perpetual whole-burnt-offering; there shall be their meat-offerings and their drink-offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> On the seventh day seven calves, two rams, fourteen lambs of a year old without blemish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Their meat-offerings and their drink-offerings shall be to the calves and the rams and the lambs according to their number, according to their ordinance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And one kid of the goats for a sin-offering; beside the continual whole-burnt-offering; there shall be their meat-offerings and their drink-offerings.</span>

See Lev. 23. 36.">a</a></sup>a release: ye shall do no servile work in it.</span>
