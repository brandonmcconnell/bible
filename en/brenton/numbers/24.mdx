<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 23:3; Numbers 23:23; Numbers 23:28"> And when Balaam saw that it pleased God to bless Israel, he did not go according to his custom to meet the omens, but turned his face toward the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings I 19:20; Chronicles II 15:1; Kings I 19:23"> And Balaam lifted up his eyes, and sees Israel encamped by their tribes; and the Spirit of God came upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 22:31; Numbers 24:16; Numbers 23:7"> And he took up his parable and said, Balaam son of Beor says, the man who sees truly says,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Revelation 1:10; Numbers 12:6"> he says who hears the oracle of the Mighty One, who saw a vision of God in sleep; his eyes were opened:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> How goodly are thy habitations, Jacob, and thy tents, Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 1:3; Esaias 58:11; Psalms 104:16"> as shady groves, and as gardens by a river, and as tents which God pitched, and as cedars by the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings II 5:12; Chronicles I 14:2; Revelation 17:1"> There shall come a man out of his seed, and he shall rule over many nations; and the kingdom of Gog shall be exalted, and his kingdom shall be increased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 45:5; Psalms 2:9; Numbers 14:9"> God led him out of Egypt; he has as it were the glory of a unicorn: he shall consume the nations of his enemies, and he shall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. suck the marrow out of their fat bones.">a</a></sup>drain their marrow, and with his darts he shall shoot through the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Genesis 27:29; Genesis 49:9"> He lay down, he rested as a lion, and as a young lion; who shall stir him up? they that bless thee are blessed, and they that curse thee are cursed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 23:11; Jezekiel 21:14; Jezekiel 21:17"> And Balac was angry with Balaam, and clapped his hands together; and Balac said to Balaam, I called thee to curse my enemy, and behold thou hast decidedly blessed him this third time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 22:17; Numbers 22:37; Hebrews 11:24–26"> Now therefore flee to thy place: I said, I will honour thee, but now the Lord has deprived thee of glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 22:18; Numbers 22:38"> And Balaam said to Balac, Did I not speak to thy messengers also whom thou sentest to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 16:28; Numbers 22:18; Numbers 22:20"> If Balac should give me his house full of silver and gold, I shall not be able to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, go beyond.">b</a></sup>transgress the word of the Lord to make it good or bad by myself; whatsoever things God shall say, them will I speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Michæas 6:5; Revelation 2:14; Genesis 49:1"> And now, behold, I return to my place; come, I will advise thee of what this people shall do to thy people in the last days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 24:3–4; Numbers 23:7; Matthew 13:35"> And he took up his parable and said, Balaam the son of Beor says, the man who sees truly says,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 24:4; 1 Corinthians 8:1; Kings II 23:1–2"> hearing the oracles of God,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. knowing knowledge.">c</a></sup>receiving knowledge from the Most High, and having seen a vision of God in sleep; his eyes were<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or, unveiled.">d</a></sup>opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 22:16; Luke 1:32–33; Genesis 49:10"> I will point to him, but not now; I bless him, but he draws not near: a star shall rise out of Jacob, a man shall spring out of Israel; and shall crush the princes of Moab, and shall spoil all the sons of Seth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Amos 9:12; Genesis 27:29; Kings II 8:14"> And Edom shall be an inheritance, and Esau his enemy shall be an inheritance of Israel, and Israel wrought valiantly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 49:10; Michæas 5:2; Psalms 2:1–12"> And one shall arise out of Jacob, and destroy out of the city him that escapes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 17:14; Judges 6:3; Chronicles I 4:43"> And having seen Amalec, he took up his parable and said, Amalec is the first of the nations; yet his seed shall perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 15:19; Judges 1:16; Job 29:18"> And having seen the Kenite, he took up his parable and said, Thy dwelling-place is strong; yet though thou shouldest put thy nest in a rock,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 83:8; Genesis 10:22; Ezra and Nehemiah 4:2"> and though Beor should have a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. nest of cunning.">e</a></sup>skilfully contrived hiding-place, the Assyrians shall carry thee away captive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 23:23; Malachias 3:2; Kings IV 5:1"> And he looked upon Og, and took up his parable and said, Oh, oh, who shall live, when God shall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. put.">f</a></sup>do these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 10:4; Numbers 24:20; Daniel 10:20"> And one shall come forth from the hands of the Citians, and shall afflict Assur, and shall afflict the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or, men of Heber.">g</a></sup>Hebrews, and they shall perish together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 31:8; Joshua 13:22; Numbers 24:11"> And Balaam rose up and departed and returned to his place, and Balac went<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. q. d. chez lui.">h</a></sup>to his own home.</span>
