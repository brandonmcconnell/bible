<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 3:11; Genesis 8:21; Numbers 9:2–3"> Charge the children of Israel, and thou shalt speak to them, saying, Ye shall observe to offer to me in my feasts my gifts, my presents, my burnt-offerings for a sweet-smelling savour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 6:9; Daniel 11:31; John 1:29"> And thou shalt say to them, These are the burnt-offerings, all that ye shall bring to the Lord; two lambs of a year old without blemish daily, for a whole-burnt offering perpetually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 9:21; Kings III 18:36; Exodus 12:6"> Thou shalt offer one lamb in the morning, and thou shalt offer the second lamb towards evening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 2:1; Exodus 16:36; Exodus 29:38–42"> And thou shalt offer the tenth part of an ephah of fine flour for a meat-offering, mingled with oil, with the fourth part of a hin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 5:25; Leviticus 6:9; Psalms 50:8"> It is a perpetual whole-burnt-offering, a sacrifice offered in the mount of Sina for a sweet-smelling savour to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 29:42; Numbers 28:14; Esaias 57:6"> And its drink-offering, the fourth part of a hin to each lamb; in the holy place shalt thou pour strong drink as a drink-offering to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the second lamb thou shalt offer toward evening; thou shalt offer it according to its meat-offering and according to its drink-offering for a smell of sweet savour to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esaias 58:13; Psalms 92:1–4; Revelation 1:10"> And on the sabbath-day ye shall offer two lambs of a year old without blemish, and two tenth deals of fine flour mingled with oil for a meat-offering, and a drink-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jezekiel 46:4–5; Numbers 29:22; Numbers 29:31"> It is a whole-burnt-offering of the sabbaths on the sabbath days, besides the continued whole-burnt-offering, and its drink offering.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 10:10; Jezekiel 46:6; Ezra and Nehemiah 3:5"> And at the new moons ye shall bring a whole-burnt-offering to the Lord, two calves of the herd, and one ram, seven lambs of a year old without blemish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 15:4–12; Jezekiel 46:5–7; Numbers 29:10"> Three tenth deals of fine flour mingled with oil for one calf, and two tenth deals of fine flour mingled with oil for one ram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> A tenth deal of fine flour mingled with oil for each lamb, as a meat-offering, a sweet-smelling savour, a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="This seems to be the general meaning of κάρπωμα in LXX.">a</a></sup>burnt-offering to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Their drink-offering shall be the half of a hin for one calf; and the third of a hin for one ram; and the fourth part of a hin of wine for one lamb: this is the whole-burnt-offering monthly throughout the months of the year.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 28:3; Numbers 28:22; Numbers 15:24"> And he shall offer one kid of the goats for a sin-offering to the Lord; it shall be offered beside the continual whole-burnt-offering and its drink-offering.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:1–8; Exodus 12:18; Leviticus 23:5–8"> And in the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, is the passover to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 23:6; Exodus 23:15; Exodus 13:6"> And on the fifteenth day of this month is a feast; seven days ye shall eat unleavened bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 12:16; Leviticus 23:7–8"> And the first day shall be to you a holy convocation; ye shall do no servile work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:21; Jezekiel 45:21–25; Numbers 29:8"> And ye shall bring whole-burnt-offerings, a sacrifice to the Lord, two calves of the herd, one ram, seven lambs of a year old; they shall be to you without blemish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And their meat-offering shall be fine flour mingled with oil; three tenth deals for one calf, and two tenth deals for one ram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Thou shalt offer a tenth for each lamb, for the seven lambs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 8:3"> And thou shalt offer one kid of the goats for a sin-offering, to make atonement for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 28:3; Numbers 28:10"> Beside the perpetual whole-burnt-offering in the morning, which is a whole-burnt-sacrifice for a continuance,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> these shall ye thus offer daily for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Alex. two days.">b</a></sup>seven days, a gift, a sacrifice for a sweet-smelling savour to the Lord; beside the continual whole-burnt-offering, thou shalt offer its drink-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 23:8; Numbers 28:18; Exodus 13:6"> And the seventh day shall be to you a holy convocation; ye shall do no servile work in it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 23:16; Exodus 34:22; Leviticus 23:10"> And on the day of the new corn, when ye shall offer a new sacrifice at the festival of weeks to the Lord, there shall be to you a holy convocation; ye shall do no servile work,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 23:18–19; Numbers 28:11; Numbers 28:19"> and ye shall bring whole-burnt-offerings for a sweet-smelling savour to the Lord, two calves of the herd, one ram, seven lambs without blemish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Their meat-offering shall be fine flour mingled with oil; there shall be three tenth deals for one calf, and two tenth deals for one ram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> A tenth for each lamb separately, for the seven lambs; and a kid of the goats,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 28:15; Numbers 28:22; 1 Peter 2:24"> for a sin-offering, to make atonement for you; beside the perpetual whole-burnt-offering: and</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 28:19; Numbers 28:3; Malachias 1:13–14"> ye shall offer to me their meat-offering. They shall be to you unblemished, and ye shall offer their drink-offerings.</span>
</div>
