<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 23:29; Jude 1:11; Exodus 27:1–8"> And Balaam said to Balac, Build me here seven altars, and prepare me here seven calves, and seven rams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 23:14; Numbers 23:30"> And Balac did as Balaam told him; and he offered up a calf and a ram on every altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 23:15; Genesis 8:20; Genesis 22:2"> And Balaam said to Balac, Stand by thy sacrifice, and I will go and see if God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. appear to me in meeting.">a</a></sup>will appear to me and meet me, and the word which he shall shew me, I will report to thee. And Balac stood by his sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 23:16; Ephesians 2:9; Luke 18:12"> And Balaam went to enquire of God; and he went straight forward, and God appeared to Balaam; and Balaam said to him, I have prepared the seven altars, and have offered a calf and a ram on every altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:18; Jeremias 1:9; Esaias 59:21"> And God put a word into the mouth of Balaam, and said, Thou shalt return to Balac, and thus shalt thou speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And he returned to him, and moreover he stood over his whole-burnt-offerings, and all the princes of Moab with him; and the Spirit of God came upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:4; Numbers 24:3; Numbers 22:5–6"> And he took up his parable, and said, Balac king of Moab sent for me out of Mesopotamia, out of the mountains of the east, saying, Come, curse me Jacob, and Come, call for a curse for me upon Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:23; Esaias 44:25; Numbers 23:20"> How can I curse whom the Lord curses not? or how can I devote whom God devotes not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:28; Deuteronomy 32:8; Exodus 33:16"> For from the top of the mountains I shall see him, and from the hills I shall observe him: behold, the people shall dwell alone, and shall not be reckoned among the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 37:37; Genesis 13:16; Psalms 116:15"> Who has exactly calculated the seed of Jacob, and who shall number the families of Israel? let my soul die with the souls of the righteous, and let my seed be as their seed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:2; Numbers 24:10; Numbers 22:17"> And Balac said to Balaam, What hast thou done to me? I called thee to curse my enemies, and behold thou hast greatly blessed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 22:38; Numbers 23:20; Numbers 23:26"> And Balaam said to Balac, Whatsoever the Lord shall put into my mouth, shall I not take heed to speak this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 109:17; James 3:9–10; Numbers 22:41"> And Balac said to him, Come yet with me to another place where thou shalt not see<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. him or it.">b</a></sup>the people, but only thou shalt see a part of them, and shalt not see them all; and curse me them from thence.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esaias 46:6; Numbers 23:29; Esaias 1:10–11"> And he took him to a high place of the field to the top of the quarried rock, and he built there seven altars, and offered a calf and a ram on every altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 23:3; Numbers 22:8"> And Balaam said to Balac, Stand by thy sacrifice, and I will go to enquire of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 23:5; Numbers 24:1; Numbers 22:35"> And God met Balaam, and put a word into his mouth, and said, Return to Balac, and thus shalt thou speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 23:26; Kings I 3:17; Jeremias 37:17"> And he returned to him: and he also was standing by his whole-burnt-sacrifice, and all the princes of Moab with him; and Balac said to him, What has the Lord spoken?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 3:20"> And he took up his parable, and said, Rise up, Balac, and hear; hearken as a witness, thou son of Sepphor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings I 15:29; James 1:17; Malachias 3:6"> God is not as man to waver, nor as the son of man to be threatened; shall he say and not perform? shall he speak and not keep to his word?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 10:27–29; Numbers 22:12; Genesis 12:2"> Behold, I have received commandment to bless: I will bless, and not turn back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 23:4; Matthew 1:23; Jeremias 50:20"> There shall not be trouble in Jacob, neither shall sorrow be seen in Israel: the Lord his God is with him, the glories of rulers are in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 24:8; Deuteronomy 33:17; Job 39:9–11"> It was God who brought him out of Egypt; he has as it were the glory of a unicorn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 10:18–19; Joshua 13:22; Galatians 1:23–24"> For there is no divination<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, against.">c</a></sup>in Jacob, nor enchantment<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or, against.">d</a></sup>in Israel; in season it shall be told to Jacob and Israel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or, what shall God perform?">e</a></sup>what God shall perform.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 49:27; Genesis 49:9; Numbers 24:17"> Behold, the people shall rise up as a lion's whelp, and shall exalt himself as a lion; he shall not lie down till he have eaten the prey, and he shall drink the blood of the slain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 2:1–3"> And Balac said to Balaam, Neither curse<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. him.">f</a></sup>the people at all for me, nor bless them at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Numbers 22:18; Acts 4:19–20"> And Balaam answered and said to Balac, Spoke I not to thee, saying, Whatsoever thing God shall speak to me, that will I do?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 11:29; Proverbs 21:30; Numbers 23:19–20"> And Balac said to Balaam, Come and I will remove thee to another place, if it shall please God, and curse me them from thence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 21:20; Psalms 106:28"> And Balac took Balaam to the top of Phogor, which extends to the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 23:1–2"> And Balaam said to Balac, Build me here seven altars, and prepare me here seven calves, and seven rams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And Balac did as Balaam told him, and offered a calf and a ram on every altar.</span>
</div>
