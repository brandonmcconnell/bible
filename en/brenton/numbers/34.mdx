<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Psalms 105:11; Deuteronomy 1:7–8"> Charge the children of Israel, and thou shalt say to them, Ye are entering into the land of Chanaan: it shall be to you for an inheritance, the land of Chanaan with its boundaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 14:3; Jezekiel 47:13; Joshua 3:16"> And your southern side shall be from the wilderness of Sin to the border of Edom, and your border southward shall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. be.">a</a></sup>extend on the side of the salt sea eastward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 32:8; Joshua 15:3–4; Numbers 13:26"> And your border shall go round you from the south to the ascent of Acrabin, and shall proceed by Ennac, and the going forth of it shall be southward to Cades Barne, and it shall go forth to the village of Arad, and shall proceed by Asemona.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 15:4; Genesis 15:18; Kings III 8:65"> And the border shall compass from Asemona to the river of Egypt, and the sea shall be the termination.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 1:4; Jezekiel 47:20; Jezekiel 47:15"> And ye shall have your border on the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. sea.">b</a></sup>west, the great sea shall be the boundary: this shall be to you the border on the west.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 33:37; Numbers 34:6; Numbers 34:3"> And this shall be your northern border; from the great sea ye shall measure to yourselves, by the side of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. the mountain, the mountain. By this repetition is perhaps meant mount Hor.">c</a></sup>the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 13:21; Kings IV 14:25; Joshua 13:5–6"> And ye shall measure to yourselves the mountain from mount Hor at the entering in to Emath, and the termination of it shall be the coasts of Saradac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jezekiel 47:17"> And the border shall go out to Dephrona, and its termination shall be at Arsenain; this shall be your border from the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And ye shall measure to yourselves the eastern border from Arsenain to Sepphamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings IV 23:33; Deuteronomy 3:17; Joshua 11:2"> And the border shall go down from Sepphamar to Bela eastward to the fountains, and the border shall go down from Bela behind the sea Chenereth eastward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 34:3; Genesis 13:10; Genesis 19:24–26"> And the border shall go down to Jordan, and the termination shall be the salt sea; this shall be your land and its borders round about.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 34:1; Joshua 14:1–5"> And Moses charged the children of Israel, saying, This is the land which ye shall inherit by lot, even as the Lord commanded us to give it to the nine tribes and the half-tribe of Manasse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 32:33; Numbers 32:23; Joshua 14:2–3"> For the tribe of the children of Ruben, and the tribe of the children of Gad have received their inheritance according to their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. the houses of their families.">d</a></sup>families; and the half-tribe of Manasse have received their inheritances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 32:32"> Two tribes and half a tribe have received their inheritance beyond Jordan by Jericho from the south eastwards.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 14:1; Joshua 19:51; Numbers 13:8"> These are the names of the men who shall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. inherit the land for you.">e</a></sup>divide the land to you for an inheritance; Eleazar the priest and Joshua the son of Naue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 1:4–16"> And ye shall take one ruler from each tribe to divide the land to you by lot.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 26:65; Numbers 13:30; Numbers 14:24"> And these are the names of the men; of the tribe of Juda, Chaleb the son of Jephonne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 49:5"> Of the tribe of Symeon, Salamiel the son of Semiud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 49:27; Psalms 68:27"> Of the tribe of Benjamin, Eldad the son of Chaslon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Of the tribe of Dan the prince was Bacchir the son of Egli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Of the sons of Joseph of the tribe of the sons of Manasse, the prince was Aniel the son of Suphi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Of the tribe of the sons of Ephraim, the prince was Camuel the son of Sabathan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Of the tribe of Zabulon, the prince was Elisaphan the son of Pharnac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Of the tribe of the sons of Issachar, the prince was Phaltiel the son of Oza.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Of the tribe of the children of Aser, the prince was Achior the son of Selemi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Of the tribe of Nephthali, the prince was Phadael the son of Jamiud.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 19:51; Numbers 34:18"> These did the Lord command to distribute the inheritances to the children of Israel in the land of Chanaan.</span>
</div>
