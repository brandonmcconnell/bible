<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 27:1; Chronicles I 7:14–16; Joshua 17:2–3"> And the heads of the tribe of the sons of Galaad the son of Machir the son of Manasse, of the tribe of the sons of Joseph, drew near, and spoke before Moses, and before Eleazar the priest, and before the heads of the houses of the families of the children of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 17:3–6; Numbers 27:1–7; Numbers 33:54"> and they said, The Lord commanded our lord to render the land of inheritance by lot to the children of Israel; and the Lord appointed our lord to give the inheritance of Salpaad our brother to his daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And they will become wives in one of the tribes of the children of Israel; so their inheritance shall be taken away from the possession of our fathers, and shall be added to the inheritance of the tribe into which the women shall marry, and shall be taken away from the portion of our inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 25:10–18; Esaias 61:2; Leviticus 25:23"> And if there shall be a release of the children of Israel, then shall their inheritance be added to the inheritance of the tribe into which the women marry, and their inheritance shall be taken away from the inheritance of our family's tribe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 27:7; Deuteronomy 5:28"> And Moses charged the children of Israel by the commandment of the Lord, saying, Thus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. say.">a</a></sup>says the tribe of the children of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 36:12; 2 Corinthians 6:14; Genesis 24:3"> This is the thing which the Lord has appointed the daughters of Salpaad, saying, Let them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. be wives.">b</a></sup>marry where they please, only let them marry men of their father's tribe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings III 21:3; Numbers 36:9"> So shall not the inheritance of the children of Israel go about from tribe to tribe, for the children of Israel shall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. be cemented. See Mat. 19. 5; Acts 5. 26.">c</a></sup>steadfastly continue each in the inheritance of his family's tribe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Chronicles I 23:22"> And whatever daughter is heiress to a property of the tribes of the children of Israel, such women shall be married each to one of her father's tribe, that the sons of Israel may each inherit the property of his father's tribe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And the inheritance shall not go about from one tribe to another, but the children of Israel shall steadfastly continue each in his own inheritance.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Chronicles II 30:12; Matthew 28:20; Exodus 39:42–43"> As the Lord commanded Moses, so did they to the daughters of Salpaad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 27:1; Numbers 26:33"> So Thersa, and Egla, and Melcha, and Nua, and Malaa, the daughters of Salpaad, married their cousins;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> they were married to men of the tribe of Manasse of the sons of Joseph; and their inheritance was attached to the tribe of their father's family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 27:34; Leviticus 26:46; Numbers 22:1"> These are the commandments, and the ordinances, and the judgments, which the Lord commanded by the hand of Moses, at the west of Moab, at Jordan by Jericho.</span>
</div>
