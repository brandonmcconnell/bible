<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:45; Numbers 11:1–4"> And all the congregation lifted up their voice and cried; and the people wept all that night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 15:24; Exodus 17:3; Numbers 16:41"> And all the children of Israel murmured against Moses and Aaron; and all the congregation said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 14:31–32; Jeremias 9:3; Psalms 78:40"> Would we had died in the land of Egypt! or in this wilderness, would we had died! and why does the Lord bring us into this land to fall in war? our wives and our children shall be for a prey: now then it is better to return into Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 7:39; Nehemiah 9:16–17; Deuteronomy 17:16"> And they said one to another, Let us make a ruler, and return into Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 16:4; Numbers 16:22; Numbers 16:45"> And Moses and Aaron fell upon their face before all the congregation of the children of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:30; Numbers 13:30; Numbers 14:38"> But Joshua the son of Naue, and Chaleb the son of Jephonne, of the number of them that spied out the land, rent their garments,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 13:27; Deuteronomy 1:25; Deuteronomy 8:7–9"> and spoke to all the congregation of the children of Israel, saying, The land which we surveyed is indeed extremely good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings II 22:20; Esaias 62:4; Numbers 13:27"> If the Lord choose us, he will bring us into this land, and give it us; a land which flows with milk and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:18; Romans 8:31; Deuteronomy 31:6"> Only depart not from the Lord; and fear ye not the people of the land, for they are meat for us; for the season of prosperity is departed from them, but the Lord is among us: fear them not.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 17:4; Kings I 30:6; Leviticus 9:23"> And all the congregation bade stone them with stones; and the glory of the Lord appeared in the cloud on the tabernacle of witness to all the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 78:32; Psalms 106:24; Psalms 78:22"> And the Lord said to Moses, How long does this people provoke me? and how long do they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. not believe me.">a</a></sup>refuse to believe me for all the signs which I have wrought among them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 32:10; Numbers 25:9; Kings II 24:12–15"> I will smite them with death, and destroy them; and I will make of thee and of thy father's house a great nation, and much greater than this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 106:23; Jezekiel 20:9; Deuteronomy 32:27"> And Moses said to the Lord, So Egypt shall hear, for thou hast brought up this people from them by thy might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 15:14; Exodus 33:11; Deuteronomy 5:4"> Moreover all the dwellers upon this land have heard that thou art Lord in the midst of this people, who, O Lord, art seen by them face to face, and thy cloud rests upon them, and thou goest before them by day in a pillar of a cloud, and by night in a pillar of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 6:16"> And if thou shalt destroy this nation as one man; then all the nations that have heard thy name shall speak, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:28; Joshua 7:9; Joshua 7:7"> Because the Lord could not bring this people into the land which he sware to them, he has overthrown them in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 9:6; Matthew 9:8; Michæas 3:8"> And now, O Lord, let thy strength be exalted, as thou spakest, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 34:6–7; Psalms 145:8; Exodus 20:5"> The Lord is long-suffering and merciful, and true, removing transgressions and iniquities and sins, and he will by no means clear the guilty, visiting the sins of the fathers upon the children to the third and fourth generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 34:9; Psalms 78:38; Psalms 106:45"> Forgive this people their sin according to thy great mercy, as thou wast favourable to them from Egypt until now.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Michæas 7:18–20; James 5:16; Psalms 106:23"> And the Lord said to Moses, I am gracious to them according to thy word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 72:19; Ambacum 2:14; Esaias 6:3"> But as I live and my name is living, so the glory of the Lord shall fill all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 19:3; Genesis 31:7; Exodus 32:1"> For all the men who see my glory, and the signs which I wrought in Egypt, and in the wilderness, and have tempted me this tenth time, and have not hearkened to my voice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 32:11; Numbers 26:64; Jezekiel 20:15"> surely they shall not see the land, which I sware to their fathers; but their children which are with me here, as many as know not good or evil, every inexperienced youth, to them will I give the land; but none who have provoked me shall see it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 14:6–9; Joshua 14:6–14; Ephesians 6:6"> But my servant Chaleb, because there was another spirit in him, and he followed me, I will bring him into the land into which he entered, and his seed shall inherit it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:40; Numbers 13:29; Psalms 81:11–13"> But Amalec and the Chananite dwell in the valley: to-morrow turn and depart for the wilderness by the way of the Red Sea.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 16:12; 1 Corinthians 10:10; Exodus 16:28"> How long shall I endure this wicked congregation? I have heard their murmurings against me, even the murmuring of the children of Israel, which they have murmured concerning you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 14:21; Hebrews 3:17; Numbers 14:2"> Say to them, As I live, saith the Lord: surely as ye spoke into my ears, so will I do to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 1:45; 1 Corinthians 10:5; Hebrews 3:17"> Your carcases shall fall in this wilderness; and all those of you that were reviewed, and those of you that were numbered from twenty years old and upward, all that murmured against me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 26:65; Numbers 14:38; Deuteronomy 1:36–38"> ye shall not enter into the land for which I stretched out my hand to establish you upon it; except only Chaleb the son of Jephonne, and Joshua the son of Naue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:39; Psalms 106:24; Numbers 26:64"> And your little ones, who ye said should be a prey, them will I bring into the land; and they shall inherit the land,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. from which ye turned away.">b</a></sup>which ye rejected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:5; Numbers 14:29; Hebrews 3:17"> And your carcases shall fall in this wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jezekiel 23:35; Numbers 32:13; Deuteronomy 2:14"> And your sons shall be fed in the wilderness forty years, and they shall bear your fornication, until your carcases be consumed in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jezekiel 4:6; Numbers 13:25; Jeremias 18:9–10"> According to the number of the days during which ye spied the land, forty days, a day for a year, ye shall bear your sins forty years, and ye shall know my fierce anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; 1 Corinthians 10:5; Hebrews 3:19"> I the Lord have spoken, Surely will I do thus to this evil congregation<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See ἐπισύστασις, 2 Cor. 11. 28.">c</a></sup>that has risen up together against me: in this wilderness they shall be utterly consumed, and there they shall die.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Numbers 13:31–33; Numbers 13:4–16"> And the men whom Moses sent to spy out the land, and who came and murmured against it to the assembly so as to bring out evil words concerning the land,—</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:10; Numbers 16:49; Hebrews 3:17"> the men that spoke evil reports against the land, even died of the plague before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Joshua 14:6–10; Numbers 26:65"> And Joshua the son of Naue and Chaleb the son of Jephonne still lived of those men that went to spy out the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 33:4; Matthew 8:12; Hebrews 12:17"> And Moses spoke these words to all the children of Israel; and the people mourned exceedingly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:41; Matthew 7:21–23; Luke 13:25"> And they rose early in the morning and went up to the top of the mountain, saying, Behold, we<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. these men.">d</a></sup>that are here will go up to the place of which the Lord has spoken, because we have sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Chronicles II 24:20; Job 4:9; Jeremias 2:37"> And Moses said, Why do ye transgress the word of the Lord? ye shall not prosper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:42; Joshua 7:8; Joshua 7:12"> Go not up, for the Lord is not with you; so shall ye fall before the face of your enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 13:29; Numbers 14:25; Chronicles II 15:2"> For Amalec and the Chananite are there before you, and ye shall fall by the sword; because ye have disobeyed the Lord and turned aside, and the Lord will not be among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:43; Kings I 4:3–11; Numbers 31:6"> And having forced their passage, they went up to the top of the mountain; but the ark of the covenant of the Lord and Moses stirred not out of the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Numbers 21:3; Judges 1:17; Deuteronomy 1:44"> And Amalec and the Chananite that dwelt in that mountain came down, and routed them, and destroyed them unto Herman; and they returned to the camp.</span>
</div>
