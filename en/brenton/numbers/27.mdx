<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 26:33; Galatians 3:28; Chronicles I 7:15"> And the daughters of Salpaad the son of Opher, the son of Galaad, the son of Machir, of the tribe of Manasse, of the sons of Joseph, came near; and these were their names, Maala, and Nua, and Egla, and Melcha, and Thersa;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 15:33–34; Deuteronomy 17:8–10; Exodus 18:19–26"> and they stood before Moses, and before Eleazar the priest, and before the princes, and before all the congregation at the door of the tabernacle of witness, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 26:64–65; Jezekiel 18:4; Numbers 14:35"> Our father died in the wilderness, and he was not in the midst of the congregation that rebelled against the Lord in the gathering of Core; for he died for his own sin, and he had no sons. Let not the name of our father be blotted out of the midst of his people, because he has no son: give us an inheritance in the midst of our father's brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 17:4; Psalms 109:13; Exodus 32:11"> And Moses brought their case before the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 24:12–13; Job 23:4; Proverbs 3:5–6"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; Psalms 68:5–6"> The daughters of Salpaad have spoken rightly: thou shalt surely give them a possession of inheritance in the midst of their father's brethren, and thou shalt assign their father's inheritance to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; Jeremias 49:11; Joshua 17:4"> And thou shalt speak to the children of Israel, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> If a man die, and have no son, ye shall assign his inheritance to his daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And if he have no daughter, ye shall give his inheritance to his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And if he have no brethren, ye shall give his inheritance to his father's brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 35:29; Ruth 4:3–6; Jeremias 32:8"> And if there be no brethren of his father, ye shall give the inheritance to his nearest relation of his tribe, to inherit his possessions; and this shall be to the children of Israel an ordinance of judgment, as the Lord commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 33:47–48; Deuteronomy 34:1–4; Deuteronomy 3:23–27"> And the Lord said to Moses, Go up to the mountain that is in the country beyond Jordan, this mount Nabau, and behold the land Chanaan, which I give to the sons of Israel for a possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:6; Numbers 31:2; Numbers 20:24–28"> And thou shalt see it, and thou also shalt be added to thy people, as Aaron thy brother was added to them in mount Or:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:37; Numbers 20:8–13; Exodus 17:7"> because ye transgressed my commandment in the wilderness of Sin, when the congregation resisted and refused to sanctify me; ye sanctified me not at the water before them. This is the water of Strife in Cades in the wilderness of Sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Moses said to the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 16:22; Jeremias 3:15; Hebrews 12:9"> Let the Lord God of spirits and of all flesh look out for a man over this congregation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jezekiel 34:5; Mark 6:34; Kings III 22:17"> who shall go out before them, and who shall come in before them, and who shall lead them out, and who shall bring them in; so the congregation of the Lord shall not be as sheep without a shepherd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:9; Numbers 27:23; Genesis 41:38"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying, Take to thyself Joshua the son of Naue, a man who has the Spirit in him, and thou shalt lay thy hands upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:28; Deuteronomy 31:7–8; Deuteronomy 31:23"> And thou shalt set him before Eleazar the priest, and thou shalt give him a charge before all the congregation, and thou shalt give a charge concerning him before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 1:16–18; Kings I 10:9; Kings IV 2:9–10"> And thou shalt put of thy glory upon him, that the children of Israel may hearken to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 9:14; Kings I 28:6; Exodus 28:30"> And he shall stand before Eleazar the priest, and they shall ask of him before the Lord the judgment of the Urim: they shall go forth at his word, and at his word they shall come in, he and the children of Israel with one accord, and all the congregation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And Moses did as the Lord commanded him; and he took Joshua, and set him before Eleazar the priest, and before all the congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:28; Numbers 27:19; Deuteronomy 31:7–8"> And he laid his hands on him, and appointed him as the Lord ordered Moses.</span>
</div>
