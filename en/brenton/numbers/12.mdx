<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 2:21; Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 24:37"> And Mariam and Aaron spoke against Moses, because of the Ethiopian woman whom Moses took; for he had taken an Ethiopian woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jezekiel 35:12–13; Numbers 16:3; Michæas 6:4"> And they said, Has the Lord spoken to Moses only? has he not also spoken to us? and the Lord heard it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 11:29; James 3:13; 2 Corinthians 11:5"> And the man Moses was very meek beyond all the men that were upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 76:9; Numbers 16:16–21"> And the Lord said immediately to Moses and Aaron and Mariam, Come forth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. the three.">a</a></sup>all three of you to the tabernacle of witness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 11:25; Exodus 34:5; Psalms 99:7"> And the three came forth to the tabernacle of witness; and the Lord descended in a pillar of a cloud, and stood at the door of the tabernacle of witness; and Aaron and Mariam were called; and both came forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings III 3:5; Genesis 31:10–11; Genesis 46:2"> And he said to them, Hear my words: If there should be of you a prophet to the Lord, I will be made known to him in a vision, and in sleep will I speak to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 3:2–6; Joshua 1:1; Psalms 105:26"> My servant Moses is not so; he is faithful in all my house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:10; 1 Corinthians 13:12; Exodus 33:11"> I will speak to him mouth to mouth apparently, and not in dark speeches; and he has seen the glory of the Lord; and why were ye not afraid to speak against my servant Moses?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 17:22; Osee 5:15; Numbers 11:1"> And the great anger of the Lord was upon them, and he departed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:9; Kings IV 5:27; Exodus 4:6"> And the cloud departed from the tabernacle; and, behold, Mariam was leprous, white as snow; and Aaron looked upon Mariam, and, behold, she was leprous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings II 19:19; Kings II 24:10; Proverbs 30:32"> And Aaron said to Moses, I beseech thee, my lord, do not lay sin upon us, for we were ignorant wherein we sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 88:4–5; 1 Corinthians 15:8; 1 Timothy 5:6"> Let her not be as it were like death, as an abortion coming out of his mother's womb, when the disease devours the half of the flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 12:21; Luke 6:28; James 5:15"> And Moses cried to the Lord, saying, O God, I beseech thee, heal her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:9; Job 30:10; Esaias 50:6"> And the Lord said to Moses, If her father had only spit in her face,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. will.">b</a></sup>would she not be ashamed seven days? let her be set apart seven days without the camp, and afterwards she shall come in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:8–9; Michæas 7:8–9; Michæas 6:4"> And Mariam was separated without the camp seven days; and the people moved not forward till Mariam was cleansed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 11:35; Numbers 33:18; Numbers 10:12"> And afterwards the people set forth from Aseroth, and encamped in the wilderness of Pharan.</span>
