<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spak to Moyses, &#96;and seide, Lo!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 36:1; Exodus 35:30; John 3:27"> Y haue clepid Beseleel bi name, the sone of Hury, sone of Hur, of the lynage of Juda;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:31; Isaiah 28:26; 1 Kings 7:14"> and Y haue fillid hym with the spirit of God, with wisdom, and vndirstondyng, and kunnyng in al werk,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 26:1; Exodus 25:32–35; 2 Chronicles 2:13–14"> to fynde out what euer thing may be maad suteli, of gold, and siluer, and bras, and marbil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 28:9–21"> and gemmes, and dyuersite of trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 35:10; Exodus 35:34–1; Exodus 28:3"> And Y haue youe to hym a felowe, Ooliab, the sone of Achisameth, of the kynrede of Dan; and Y haue put in &#96;the herte of hem the wisdom of ech lerned man, that thei make alle thingis, whiche Y comaundide to thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 36:8–9; Exodus 25:10–22; Exodus 26:1–37"> the tabernacle of boond of pees, and the arke of witnessyng, and the propiciatorie, ether table, which is theronne, and alle the vessels of the tabernacle;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 37:10–28; Exodus 30:1–10; Exodus 25:23–40"> also the bord, and vessels therof, the clenneste candilstike with hise vessels, and the auteris of encence,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 38:1–8; Exodus 27:1–8; Exodus 40:11"> and of brent sacrifice, and alle the vessels of hem; the greet &#96;waischyng vessel with his foundement;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 4:5–14; Exodus 28:1–43; Exodus 39:1–43"> hooli clothis in seruyce to Aaron prest, and to hise sones, that thei be set in her office in hooli thingis;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; Exodus 30:23–38"> the oile of anoyntyng, and encence of swete smellynge spiceryes in the seyntuarie; thei schulen make alle thingis whiche Y comaundide to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And the Lord spak to Moises, &#96;and seide, Speke thou to the sones of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:12; Ezekiel 20:20; Leviticus 19:30"> and thou schalt seie to hem, Se ye that ye kepe my sabat, for it is a signe bytwixe me and you in youre generaciouns; that ye wite, that Y am the Lord, which halewe you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 35:2–3; Numbers 15:32–36; Ezekiel 20:21"> Kepe ye my sabat, for it is hooli to you; he that defoulith it, schal die bi deeth, the soule of hym, that doith werk in the sabat, schal perische fro the myddis of his puple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 2:2; Exodus 16:23; Leviticus 23:3"> Sixe daies ye schulen do werk; in the seuenthe dai is sabat, hooli reste to the Lord; ech man that doith werk in this dai schal die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:5; Genesis 9:13; Genesis 17:11"> The sones of Israel kepe sabat, and halewe it in her generaciouns;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 1:31; Exodus 31:13; Genesis 2:2–3"> it is a couenaunt euerlastinge bitwixe me and the sones of Israel, and it is &#96;a signe euerlastynge; for in sixe daies God made heuene and erthe, and in the seuenthe day he ceessid of werk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:13; Exodus 32:15–16; Deuteronomy 5:22"> And whanne siche wordis<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="In Ebru it is not, siche wordis, but oonly, whan he hadde fillid to speke to Moyses.">a</a></sup>weren fillid, the Lord yaf to Moises, in the hil of Synay, twei stonun tablis of witnessyng, writun with the fyngur of God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="that is, bi werk of God, not of man.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
