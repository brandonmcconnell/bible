<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spak to Moises, and seide, Speke thou to the sones of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Nehemiah 11:2; Ezra 2:68"> that thei take to me the firste fruytis; of ech man that offrith wilfuli, ye schulen take tho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:9; Job 28:2"> Forsothe these thingis it ben, whiche ye schulen take, gold, and siluer, and bras, iacynt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 19:8; Genesis 41:42; Ezekiel 16:10"> and purpur, and reed silk twies died, and bijs, heeris of geet, and &#96;skynnes of wetheris maad reed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 26:37–1; Exodus 26:14–15; Exodus 36:20"> and skynnes of iacynt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 27:20; Exodus 25:37; Exodus 40:24–25"> and trees of Sechym, and oile to liytis to be ordeyned, swete smellynge spiceries in to oynement, and encensis of good odour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Exodus 28:6–30"> onochym stoonys, and gemmes to ourne ephod, and the racional.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="that is, an ouche hanginge on the prestis breest, in whiche was writen dome and treuth.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Kings 6:13; Revelation 21:3"> And thei schulen make a seyntuarie to me, and Y schal dwelle in the myddis of hem, bi al the licnesse of the tabernacle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Hebrews 8:5; Acts 7:44"> which Y schal schewe to thee, and of alle the vessels of ournyng therof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 9:4; Exodus 37:1–3; Deuteronomy 10:1–3"> And thus ye schulen make it; ioyne ye to gidere an arke of the trees of Sechym, whos lengthe haue twey cubitis and an half, the broodnesse haue a cubit and half, the hiynesse haue &#96;in lijk maner a cubit and half.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 25:24; Exodus 30:3; 2 Chronicles 3:4"> And thou schalt ouergilde it with clenneste gold with ynne and with out forth; and thou schalt make a goldun crowne aboue &#96;bi cumpas,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 27:7; Exodus 25:26; Exodus 25:15"> and foure goldun cerclis, whiche thou schalt sette bi foure corneris of the arke; twei ceerclis be in o syde, and twei cerclis in the tother side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:15; Numbers 4:11; Exodus 27:6"> Also thou schalt make barris of the trees of Sechym, and thou schalt hile tho with gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and thou schalt brynge yn bi the cerclis that ben in the sidis of the arke,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 8:8; 2 Chronicles 5:9"> that it be borun in tho, whiche schulen euere be in the ceerclis, nether schulen ony tyme be drawun out of thoo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:26; 1 Kings 8:9; Hebrews 9:4"> And thou schalt putte in to the arke the witnessing,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="It was seid the arke of witnessing, for there yn was put a golden pott ful of manna, to witnesse that God fedde hem with bred fro heuene, and tables, to witnesse that the lawe of kynde flowyed in herte, he reiside the lawe writen, the yerde of Aaron, to witnesse that eche power is of the Lord God, and Deutronomye, to witnesse the couenaunt that he had seid.">b</a></sup>which Y schal yyue to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 9:5; Exodus 37:6; Hebrews 4:16"> And thou schalt make a propiciatorie<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="In the dai of merci the glorie of God descendide alwey there.">c</a></sup>of clenneste gold; &#96;that is a table hilinge the arke; the lengthe therof schal holde twei cubitis and an half, the broodnesse schal holde a cubit and half.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:20; Hebrews 9:5; 1 Kings 8:6–7"> Also thou schalt make on euer eithir side of &#96;Goddis answeryng place<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="that is the place that God answeride fro to men preiynge.">d</a></sup>twei cherubyns of gold, and betun out with hamer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> o cherub be in o syde of &#96;Goddis answeryng place, and the tother in the tother side;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:18; 1 Kings 8:7; Hebrews 9:5"> hele thei euer either side of the propiciatorie, and holde thei forth wyngis, and hile thei &#96;Goddis answeryng place; and biholde thei hem silf to gidere, while the faces ben turned in to the propiciatorie, with which the arke of the Lord schal be hilid,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 26:34; Romans 10:4; Exodus 25:16–17"> in which arke thou schalt putte the &#96;witnessyng, which Y schal yyue to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 29:42–43; Numbers 7:89; Isaiah 37:16"> Fro thennus Y schal comaunde, and schal speke to thee aboue the propiciatorie, that is, fro the myddis of twei cherubyns, that schulen be on the arke of witnessyng, alle thingis whiche Y schal comaunde &#96;bi thee to the sones of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 9:2; Exodus 37:10–16; 1 Kings 7:48"> Also thou schalt make a boord of the trees of Sechym, hauinge twei cubitis of lengthe, and a cubit in broodnesse, and a cubit and half in hiyenesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 25:11; 1 Kings 6:20–22"> And thou schalt ouergilde the bord with purest gold, and thou schalt make to it a goldun brynke &#96;bi cumpas;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 37:2; Exodus 30:3"> and &#96;thou schalt make to that brynke a coroun rasid bitwixe foure fyngris hiy, and &#96;thou schalt make on that another lytil goldun coroun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And thou schalt make redi foure goldun cerclis, and thou schalt put thoo in foure corners of the same boord, bi alle feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 25:14; Exodus 25:28"> Vndur the coroun schulen be goldun cerclis, that the barris be put thorou tho, and that the boord may be borun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 25:14; Acts 9:15; Numbers 10:17"> Thou schalt make tho barris of the trees of Sechym, and thou schalt cumpas with gold to bere the boord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 4:7; Exodus 37:16; Revelation 3:20"> And thou schalt make redi vessels of vynegre, and viols, cenceris, and cuppis of pureste gold, in whiche fletynge sacrifices schulen be offrid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 24:5–9; Matthew 12:4; Exodus 39:36"> And thou schalt sette on the boord looues of proposicioun, in my siyt euere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 7:49; Zechariah 4:2; Exodus 37:17–24"> And thou schalt make a candilstike &#96;betun forth with hamer, of clenneste gold, and thou schalt make the schaft therof, and yerdis, cuppis, and litle rundelis, and lilies comynge forth therof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Sixe yerdis schulen go out of the sidis, thre of o side, and thre of the tother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 17:4–8; Jeremiah 1:11–12; Zechariah 4:3"> Thre cuppis as in the maner of a note bi ech yerde, and litle rundelis to gidere, and a lilie, and in lijk maner thre cuppis at the licnesse of a note in the tother, and litle rundelis togidere, and a lilie; this schal be the werk of sixe yerdis, that schulen be brouyt forth of the schaft.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Forsothe in thilke candilstik e schulen be foure cuppis in the maner of a note, and litle rundels and lilies by ech cuppe;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> and litle rundelis schulen be vndir twey yerdis bi thre places, whiche yerdis to gidere ben maad sixe, comynge forth of o schaft; and therfor the litle rundelis and yerdis</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 25:18; 1 Kings 10:16–17; 2 Chronicles 9:15"> therof schulen be alle betun out with hamer, of clenneste gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 8:2; Revelation 1:20–1; Leviticus 24:2–4"> And thou schalt make seuene lanternes, and thou schalt sette tho on the candilstike, that tho schyne euene ayens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Kings 12:13; Jeremiah 52:18; 2 Kings 25:14"> Also tongis to &#96;do out the snottis, and where tho thingis, that ben snottid out, ben quenchid, be maad of clenneste gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Al the weiyt of the candilstike with alle hise vesselis schal haue a talent of clennest gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Hebrews 8:5; Exodus 26:30; Acts 7:44"> Biholde thou, and make bi the saumpler, which ys schewide to thee in the hil.</span>
