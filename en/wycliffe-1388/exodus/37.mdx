<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 25:10–20; Exodus 26:33; Numbers 10:33–36"> Forsothe Beseleel made also an arke of the trees of Sechym, hauynge twey cubitis and an half in lengthe, and a cubit and an half in breede; forsothe the hiynesse was of o cubit and an half; and he clothide the arke with purest gold, with ynne and without forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 30:3"> And he made to it a goldun coroun &#96;bi cumpas,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and yetide foure goldun ryngis, bi foure corneris therof, twey ryngis in o side, and twei ryngis in the tother side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Peter 1:7; Numbers 4:14–15"> And he made barris of the trees of Sechym, whiche barris he clothide with gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; 2 Samuel 6:3–7; Numbers 1:50"> and whiche barris he putte into the ryngis that weren in the sidis of the arke, to bere it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 9:5; Titus 2:14; Romans 3:25"> He made also a propiciatorie, that is, Goddis answeryng place, of pureste gold, of twei cubitis and an half in lengthe, and of o cubit and an half in breede.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:2; Psalms 104:4; Psalms 80:1"> Also he made twei cherubyns of gold, betun out with hamer, whiche he settide on euer eithir side of the propiciatorie,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> o cherub in the hiynesse of o part, and the tother cherub in the hiynesse of the tothir part; twei cherubyns, oon in ech hiynesse of the propiciatorie, stretchynge out the wengis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 1:51; Hebrews 1:14; Genesis 28:12"> and hilynge the propiciatorie, and biholdynge hem silf togidere and that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 1:14; Ezekiel 40:39–42; Malachi 1:12"> He made also a boord of &#96;the trees of Sechym, in the lengthe of twey cubitis, and in the breede of o cubit, whiche boord hadde &#96;a cubit and an half in heiythe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And he cumpaside the boord with clenneste gold, and made to it a goldun brynke bi cumpas;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and he made to that brynke a goldun coroun, rasid bitwixe of foure fyngris; and on the same coroun he made anothir goldun coroun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Also he yetide foure goldun serclis whiche he settide in foure corneris,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> bi alle the feet of the boord ayens the coroun, and he puttide barris in to the serclis, that the &#96;boord may be borun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And he made tho barris of the trees of Sechym, and cumpasside tho with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 25:29; 1 Kings 7:50; 2 Kings 12:13"> And he made vesselis to dyuerse vsis of the boord, vessels of vynegre, violis, and litle cuppis, and censeris of pure gold, in whiche the fletynge sacrifices schulen be offrid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–39; Hebrews 9:2; 2 Chronicles 13:11"> And he made a candilstike, betun out with hamer, of clenneste gold, of whos barre yerdis, cuppis, and litle rundelis and lilies camen forth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> sixe in euer eithir side, thre yerdis on o side, and thre on the tother side; thre cuppis in the maner of a note bi ech yerde, and litle rundels to gidere, and lilies;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and thre cuppis at the licnesse of a note in the tother yerde, and litle rundels to gidere, and lilies; forsothe the werk of sixe schaftis, that camen forth of the &#96;stok of the candilstike, was euene.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:5; Jeremiah 1:11; Exodus 25:33"> Sotheli in that barre weren foure cuppis, in the maner of a note, and litle rundels and lilies weren bi alle cuppis;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 25:35"> and litle rundels vndur twei schaftis, bi thre placis, whiche to gidre be maad sixe schaftis comynge forth of o barre;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:5; Exodus 25:31; Psalms 51:17"> therfor and the litle rundels, and schaftis therof, weren alle betun out with hamer, of pureste gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 1:12; Exodus 25:37; Revelation 1:20–1"> He made also seuene lanternes, with her &#96;snytyng tongis, and the vessels where &#96;tho thingis, that ben snytid out, ben quenchid, of clennest gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> The candilstike with alle his vessels weiyede a talent of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 30:1–5; Hebrews 7:25; Revelation 8:3–4"> He made also the auter of encense, of trees of Sechym, hauynge a cubit bi square, and twei cubitis in heiythe, of whos corneris camen forth hornes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And he clothide it with clenneste gold, and the gridele, and wallis, and hornes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> and he made to it a litil goldun coroun bi cumpas, and twei goldun ryngis vndur the coroun, bi ech syde, that barris be put in to tho, and the auter mow be borun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Forsothe he made tho barris of the trees of Sechym, and hilide with goldun platis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 141:2; Hebrews 7:25; 1 John 2:20"> He made also oile to the oynement of halewyng, and encense of swete smellynge spiceries, moost clene, bi the werk of &#96;a makere of oynement.</span>
