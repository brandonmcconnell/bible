<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 2:16; Psalms 77:14–15; Exodus 3:1"> And whanne Jetro, the prest of Madian, &#96;the alye of Moises,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="the alie of Moises, in Ebreu it is, fadir of the wiyf of Moises.">a</a></sup>hadde herd alle thingis which God hadde do to Moises, and to Israel his puple, for the Lord hadde led Israel out of the lond of Egipt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 2:21; Exodus 4:25–26"> he took Sefora, &#96;the wijf of Moises, whom he hadde sent ayen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 2:22; Acts 7:29; Psalms 39:12"> and hise twei sones, of which oon was clepid Gersan, for the fadir seide, Y was a comelyng in alien lond,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 13:6; 2 Timothy 4:17; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> forsothe the tother was clepid Eliezer, for Moises seide, God of my fadir is myn helpere, and he delyuerede me fro the swerd of Farao.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; Exodus 3:12; Exodus 24:16–17"> Therfor Jetro, &#96;alie of Moises, cam, and the sones of Moises and his wijf camen to Moises, in to deseert, where Jetro settide tentis bisidis the hil of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> and sente to Moises, and seide, Y Jetro, thin alie, come to thee, and thi wijf, and thi twei sones with hir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 29:13; Genesis 14:17; 2 Samuel 11:7"> And Moises yede out into the comyng of his alie, and worschipide, and kiste hym, and thei gretten hem silf to gidere with pesible wordis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 145:4–12; Nehemiah 9:9–15; Psalms 107:2"> And whanne he hadde entrid in to the tabernacle, Moises tolde to &#96;his alie alle thingis whiche God hadde do to Farao, and to Egipcians, for Israel, and he tolde al the trauel which bifelle to hem in the weie, of which the Lord delyuerede hem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:26; Romans 12:15; Isaiah 66:10"> And Jetro was glad on alle the goodis whiche the Lord hadde do to Israel, for he delyuerede Israel fro the hond of Egipcians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 14:20; 2 Samuel 18:28; Luke 1:68"> And Jetro seide, Blessid be &#96;the Lord, that delyuerede you fro the hond of Egipcians, and fro &#96;the hond of Farao, which Lord delyuered his puple fro the hond of Egipt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 95:3; Exodus 15:11; 2 Chronicles 2:5"> now Y knowe that the Lord is greet aboue alle goddis, for &#96;thei diden proudli ayens hem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 31:54; Deuteronomy 12:7; Exodus 24:11"> Therfor Jetro, &#96;alie of Moises, offride brent sacrifices and offryngis to God; and Aaron, and alle the eldere men of Israel, camen to ete breed with hym bifore God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 16:5; Job 29:7; Judges 5:10"> Forsothe in the tother dai Moises sat that he schulde deme the puple, that stood niy Moises, fro the morewtid til to euentid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And whanne &#96;his alie hadde seyn this, that is, alle thingis &#96;whiche he dide in the puple, he seide, What is this that thou doist in the puple? whi sittist thou aloone, and al the puple abidith fro the morewtid til to euentid?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 15:34; Numbers 27:5; Numbers 9:8"> To whom Moises answeride, The puple cometh to me, and axith the sentence of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 24:14; 1 Corinthians 6:1; 2 Samuel 15:3"> and whanne ony strijf bifallith to hem, thei comen to me, that Y deme bitwixe hem, and schewe &#96;the comaundementis of God, and hise lawis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Jetro seide, Thou doist a thing not good,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 11:14–17; Acts 6:1–4; 2 Corinthians 12:15"> thou art wastid with a fonned trauel, bothe thou and this puple which is with thee; the werk is a boue thi strengthis, thou aloone maist not suffre it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 27:5; Deuteronomy 5:5; Exodus 20:19"> But here thou my wordis and counseils, and the Lord schal be with thee; be thou to the puple in these thingis that perteynen to God, that thou telle the thingis that ben seid to the puple;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:1; Psalms 143:8; Deuteronomy 1:18"> and schewe to the puple the cerymonyes, and custom of worschipyng, and the weie bi which &#96;thei owen to go, and the werk which &#96;thei owen to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 6:3; Deuteronomy 1:13–17; 2 Chronicles 19:5–10"> Forsothe puruey thou of al the puple myyti men,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Another translacioun hath wise men, wher we han miyti men.">b</a></sup>and dredynge God, in whiche is treuthe, and whiche haten auarice; and ordeyne thou of hem tribunes,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="a tribune is he that is souereyn of a thousinde men, centurioun of an hundrid, quinquagenarie of fifty, a deen of ten.">c</a></sup>and centuriouns, and quinquagenaries, and deenys,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 11:17; Numbers 27:2; Numbers 15:33"> whiche schulen deme the puple in al tyme; sotheli what ever thing is grettere, telle thei to thee, and deme thei ooneli lesse thingis, and be it esiere to thee, whanne the burthun is departid in to othere men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:6–7; Exodus 18:18; 1 Samuel 8:22"> If thou schalt do this, thou schalt fille the comaundement of God, and thou schalt mowe bere hise comaundementis; and al this puple schal turne ayen with pees to her places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezra 10:2; Exodus 18:19; Exodus 18:2–5"> And whanne these thingis weren herd, Moises dide alle thingis whiche Jetro counselide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:15; Exodus 18:21; Acts 6:5"> And whanne noble men of al Israel weren chosun Moises ordeynede hem princis of the puple, tribunes, and centuriouns, and quinquagenaries, and denes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 18:22; Exodus 18:14–15; Deuteronomy 17:8"> whiche demeden the puple in al tyme; forsothe, whateuer thing was hardere, thei telden to Moises, and thei demeden esiere thingis oneli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 10:29–30; Judges 19:9; Genesis 31:55"> And Moises lefte &#96;his alie, which turnede ayen, and yede in to his lond.</span>
