<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 118:27; Exodus 29:12; Leviticus 4:7"> Forsothe hornes schulen be bi foure corneris therof; and thou schalt hile it with bras.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 7:45; Numbers 4:14; 1 Chronicles 28:17"> And thou schalt make in to the vsis of the auter pannes, to resseyue aischis, and tongis, and fleisch hookis, and resettis of fyris; thou schalt make alle vessilis of bras.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 35:16; Exodus 25:12; Exodus 38:4–5"> And thou schalt make a brasun gridele in the maner of a net, and bi four corneris therof schulen be foure brasun ryngis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 38:4"> whiche thou schalt putte vndur the yrun panne of the auter; and the gridele schal be til to the myddis of the auter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 35:13–15; Numbers 4:44; Exodus 30:4"> And thou schalt make twey barris of the auter, of the trees of Sechym, whiche barris thou schalt hile with platis of bras;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 25:28; Exodus 30:4; Numbers 4:13–14"> and thou schalt lede yn &#96;the barris bi the cerclis, and tho schulen be on euer eithir side of the auter, to bere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Exodus 25:9; Hebrews 8:5"> Thou schalt make that auter not massif, but voide, and holowe with ynne, as it was schewid to thee in the hil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 38:9–20; Ezekiel 40:32; Ezekiel 42:3"> Also thou schalt make a large street of the tabernacle, &#96;in the maner of a chirche yeerd, in whos mydday coost ayens the south schulen be tentis of bijs foldid ayen; o side schal holde an hundrid cubitis in lengthe,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 26:19–21; Jeremiah 52:21; Exodus 36:38"> and twenti pileris, with so many brasun foundementis, whiche pileris schulen haue silueren heedis with her grauyngis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> In lijk maner in the north side, bi the lengthe, schulen be tentis of an hundrid cubitis, twenti pileris, and brasun foundementis of the same noumbre; and the heedis of tho pileris with her grauyngis schulen be of siluer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Forsothe in the breede of the large street, that biholdith to the west, schulen be tentis bi fifti cubitis, and ten pileris schulen be, and so many foundementis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> In that breede of the large street, that biholdith to the eest, schulen be fifti cubitis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:9; Exodus 26:36"> in whiche the tentis of fiftene cubitis schulen be assigned to o side, and thre pileris, and so many foundementis;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and in the tother side schulen be tentis holdynge fiftene cubitis, and thre pileris, and so many foundementis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 26:36; Judges 5:30; Exodus 28:39"> Forsothe in the entryng of the &#96;greet strete schal be maad a tente of twenti cubitis, of iacynt, and purpur, and of reed selk twies died, and of bijs foldid ayen bi broideri werk; it schal haue four pileris, with so many foundementis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Alle the pileris of the grete street bi cumpas schulen be clothid with platis of siluer, with hedis of siluer, and with foundementis of bras.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The greet street schal ocupie an hundrid cubitis in lengthe, fifti in breede; the hiyenesse of the tente schal be of fiue cubitis; and it schal be maad of bijs foldid ayen; and it schal haue brasun foundementis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 35:18; Isaiah 22:23–25; Numbers 4:32"> Thou schalt make of bras alle the vesselis of the tabernacle, in to alle vsis and cerymonyes, as wel stakis therof, as of the greet street.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 24:1–4; Zechariah 4:11–14; Psalms 23:5"> Comaunde thou to the sones of Israel, that thei brynge to thee the clenneste oile of &#96;the trees of olyues, and powned with a pestel, that a lanterne</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 19:21; Numbers 18:23; 1 Samuel 3:3"> brenne euere in the tabernacle of witnessyng with out the veil, which is hangid in the tabernacle of witnessyng; and Aaron and hise sones schulen sette it, that it schyne bifore the Lord til the morewtid; it schal be euerlastynge worschiping bi her successiouns of the sones of Israel.</span>
