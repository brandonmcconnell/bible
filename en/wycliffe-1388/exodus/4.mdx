<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 4:17; Exodus 4:20; Isaiah 11:4"> Therfor the Lord seide to hym, What is this that thou holdist in thin hond? Moises answeride, A yerde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 7:10–15; Amos 5:19; Exodus 4:17"> And the Lord seide, Caste it forth into erthe; and he castide forth, and it was turned in to a serpent, so that Moises fledde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 28:3–6; Genesis 22:1–2; Psalms 91:13"> And the Lord seide, Holde forth thin hond, and take the tail therof; he stretchide forth, and helde, and it was turned in to a yerde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 19:9; Genesis 48:3; Exodus 3:15"> And the Lord seide, That thei bileue, that the Lord God of thi fadris apperide to thee, God of Abraham, and God of Isaac, and God of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 5:27; Numbers 12:10"> And the Lord seide eft, Putte thin hond in to thi bosum; and whanne he hadde put it in to the bosum, he brouyte forth it leprouse, at the licnesse of snow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 5:14; Deuteronomy 32:39; Matthew 8:3"> The Lord seide, Withdrawe thin hond in to thi bosum; he withdrow, and brouyte forth eft, and it was lijc the tother fleisch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 28:10; Deuteronomy 32:39; Exodus 4:30–31"> The Lord seide, If thei schulen not bileue to thee, nether schulen here the word of the formere signe, thei schulen bileue to the word of the signe suynge;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 7:19–25; Exodus 1:22; Matthew 7:2"> that if thei bileuen not sotheli to these twei signes, nether heren thi vois, take thou watir of the flood, and schedde out it on the drie lond, and what euer thing thou schalt drawe vp of the flood, it schal be turned in to blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:6; Exodus 6:12; Acts 7:22"> Moises seide, Lord, Y biseche, Y am &#96;not eloquent fro yistirdai and the thridde dai ago; and sithen thou hast spokun to thi seruaunt, Y am of more lettid and slowere tunge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Amos 3:6; Psalms 146:8; Ezekiel 3:26–27"> The Lord seide to hym, Who made the mouth of man, ether who made a doumb man and &#96;deef, seynge and blynd? whether not Y?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:9; Luke 12:11–12; Matthew 10:19–20"> Therfor go thou, and Y schal be in thi mouth, and Y schal teche thee what thou schalt speke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jonah 1:3; Exodus 4:1; Matthew 13:41"> And he seide, Lord, Y biseche, sende thou whom thou schalt sende.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 14:13–15; Exodus 4:27; 1 Chronicles 21:7"> And the Lord was wrooth ayens Moises, and seide, Y woot, that Aaron, thi brother, of the lynage of Leuy, is eloquent; lo! he schal go out in to thi comyng, and he schal se thee, and schal be glad in herte.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 51:16; Deuteronomy 18:18; Luke 21:15"> Speke thou to hym, and putte thou my wordis in his mouth, and Y schal be in thi mouth, and in the mouth of hym; and Y schal schewe to you what ye owen to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 7:1–2; Psalms 82:6; John 10:34–35"> He schal speke for thee to the puple, and he schal be thi mouth; forsothe thou schalt be to him in these thingis, that perteynen to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 4:2; 1 Corinthians 1:27; Exodus 7:9–20"> Also take thou this yerde in thin hond, in which thou schalt do myraclis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; 1 Samuel 1:17; 1 Timothy 6:1"> Moises yede, and turnede ayen to Jetro, his wyues fadir, and seide to hym, Y schal go, and turne ayen to my britheren in to Egipt, that Y se, whether thei lyuen yit. To whom Jetro seide, Go thou in pees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 2:20; Exodus 2:23; Exodus 2:15"> Therfor the Lord seide to Moyses in Madian, Go thou, and turne ayen into Egipt; for alle thei ben deed that souyten thi lijf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 17:9; Numbers 20:8–9; Exodus 18:3–4"> Moises took his wijf, and hise sones, and puttide hem on an asse, and he turnede ayen in to Egipt, and bar the yerde of God in his hond.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 9:18; Isaiah 63:17; Exodus 14:8"> And the Lord seide to hym turnynge ayen in to Egipt, Se, that thou do alle wondris, whiche Y haue put in thin hond, bifore Farao; Y schal make hard<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="I shal make harde, that is, I shal suffre that his herte be maad hard. <span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 11:1; Romans 9:4; Jeremiah 31:9"> The Lord seith these thingis, My firste gendrid sone is Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 11:5; Exodus 12:29; Psalms 135:8"> Y seide to thee, delyuere thou my sone, that he serue me, and thou noldist delyuere hym; lo! Y schal sle thi firste gendrid sone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 17:14; Numbers 22:22–23; Hosea 13:8"> And whanne Moises was in the weie, in an yn, the Lord cam to him, and wolde sle hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 5:2–3; Genesis 17:14; 2 Samuel 16:7"> Sefora took anoon a moost scharp stoon, and circumcidide the yerde of hir sone; and sche towchide &#96;the feet of Moises, and seide, Thou art an hosebonde of bloodis to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And he lefte hym, aftir that sche hadde seid, Thou art an hosebonde of bloodis to me for circumcisioun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; Exodus 4:14–16; 1 Kings 19:8"> Forsothe the Lord seide to Aaron, Go thou in to the comyng of Moises in to deseert; which yede ayens Moises in to the hil of God, and kisside him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 4:8–9; Exodus 4:15–16; Matthew 21:29"> And Moises telde to Aaron alle the wordis of the Lord, for whiche he hadde sent Moises; and &#96;he telde the myraclis, whiche the Lord hadde comaundid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 3:16; Exodus 24:11; Exodus 24:1"> And thei camen togidere, and gaderiden alle the eldere men of the sones of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 4:16"> And Aaron spak alle the wordis, whiche the Lord hadde seid to Moises, and he dide the signes bifore the puple;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 3:18; Exodus 2:25; Psalms 106:12–13"> and the puple bileuede; and thei herden, that the Lord hadde visitid the sones of Israel, and that he hadde biholde the turment of hem; and thei worschipiden lowe.</span>
