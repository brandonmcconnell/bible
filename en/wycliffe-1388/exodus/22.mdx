<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 19:8; 2 Samuel 12:6; Proverbs 6:31"> If ony man stelith a scheep, ether oxe, and sleeth, ether sillith, he schal restore fiue oxen for oon oxe, and foure scheep for o scheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 24:43; Matthew 6:19–20; Numbers 35:27"> And if a nyyt theef brekynge an hows, ether vndurmynynge, is foundun, and is deed bi a wounde takun, the smytere schal not be gilti of blood;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 21:2; Isaiah 50:1; Matthew 18:25"> that if he dide this whanne the sunne was rysun, he dide man sleyng, and he schal die. If a theef hath not that, that he schal yelde for thefte, he schal be seeld;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 22:7; Exodus 22:1; Exodus 22:9"> if that thing that he staal, is foundun quyk at hym, ether oxe, ether asse, ether scheep, he schal restore the double.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 22:3; Exodus 22:12; Job 20:18"> If a man harmeth a feeld, ethir vyner, and suffrith his beeste, that it waaste othere mennus thingis, he schal restore for the valu of harm, &#96;what euer beste thing he hath in his feeld, ethir vyner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 22:9; Exodus 21:33–34; 2 Samuel 14:30–31"> If fier goith out, and fyndith eeris of corn, and catchith heepis of corn, ethir cornes stondynge in feeldis, he that kyndlide the fier schal yeelde the harm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 22:4; Proverbs 6:30–31; Jeremiah 2:26"> If a man bitakith in to kepyng monei to a freend, ether a vessel &#96;in to keping, and it is takun awey bi thefte fro hym that resseyuede, if the theef is foundun, he schal restore the double.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 22:28; Deuteronomy 16:18; Exodus 21:6"> If the theef is hid, the lord of the hows schal be brouyt to goddis, &#96;that is, iugis, and he schal swere, that he helde not forth the hond in to &#96;the thing of his neiybore,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:1; Exodus 22:4; Numbers 5:6–7"> to &#96;do fraude; as wel in oxe, as in asse, and in scheep, and in clooth; and what euer thing may brynge in harm, the cause of euer eithir schal come to goddis, and if thei demen, he schal restore the double to his neiybore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 12:48; 2 Timothy 1:12; Luke 16:11"> If ony man bitakith to his neiybore oxe, asse, scheep, and al werk beeste to kepyng, and it is deed, ether is maad feble, ethir is takun of enemyes, and no man seeth this,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 6:16; Leviticus 5:1; 1 Kings 2:42–43"> an ooth schal be in the myddis,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="that is, he schal purge him openli bi ooth.">a</a></sup>that he helde not forth the hond to the &#96;thing of his neiybore; and the lord schal resseyue the ooth, and he schal not be compellid to yelde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 31:39; Exodus 22:7"> That if it is takun awei bi thefte, he schal restore the harm to the lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Nahum 2:12; Micah 5:8; Ezekiel 4:14"> if it is etun of a beeste, he schal brynge to the lord that that is slayn, and he schal not restore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:19–20; Exodus 21:34; Deuteronomy 15:2"> He that axith of his neiybore ony thing of these bi borewyng, and it is feblid, ether deed, while the lord is not present, he schal be constreyned to yelde; that if the lord is in presence,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 8:10"> he schal not restore, moost if it cam hirid, for the meede of his werk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:28–29; Genesis 34:2–4"> If a man disseyueth a virgyn not yit weddid, and slepith with hir, he schal yyue dower to hir, and schal haue hir wijf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 34:12; 1 Samuel 18:25; Deuteronomy 22:29"> If the fadir of the virgyn nyle yyue, he schal yelde money, bi the maner of dower, which virgyns weren wont to take.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 20:27; Deuteronomy 18:10–11; 1 Samuel 28:3"> Thou schalt not suffre witchis to lyue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 18:23; Deuteronomy 27:21; Leviticus 20:15–16"> He that doith letcherie with a beeste, die by deeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:2–5; Numbers 25:7–8; Deuteronomy 18:20"> He that offrith to goddis, out takun to the Lord aloone, be he slayn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 23:9; Deuteronomy 10:19; Leviticus 19:33"> Thou schalt not make sory a comelyng, nether thou schalt turmente hym; for also ye weren comelyngis in the lond of Egipt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:17; Isaiah 1:17; Isaiah 1:23"> Ye schulen not anoye a widewe, and a fadirles ethir modirles child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 18:6; Psalms 10:17–18; Luke 18:7"> If ye hirten hem, thei schulen crye to me, and Y schal here the cry of hem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 109:9; Jeremiah 15:8; Psalms 76:7"> and my greet veniaunce schal haue indignacioun, and Y schal smyte you with swerd, and youre wyues schulen be widewis, and youre sones schulen be fadirles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 15:5; Leviticus 25:35–37; Deuteronomy 23:19–20"> If thou yyuest money to loone to my pore puple, that dwellith with thee, thou schalt not constreyne hym, as an extorsioner doith, nether thou schalt oppresse hym by vsuris.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 20:16; Amos 2:8; Deuteronomy 24:6"> If thou takist of thi neiybore &#96;a wed a clooth, thou schalt yelde to hym bifore the goyng doun of the sunne;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 34:6; 2 Chronicles 30:9; Psalms 136:10–11"> for that aloone is the cloothing of his fleisch, with which he is hilid, nether he hath another, in which he slepith; if he crieth to me, Y schal here hym; for Y am mercyful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 23:5; Ecclesiastes 10:20; Exodus 22:8–9"> Thou schalt not bacbyte goddis, and thou schalt not curse the prince of thi puple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 23:16; Exodus 13:2; James 1:18"> Thou schalt not tarye to offre to the Lord thi tithis, and firste fruytis. Thou schalt yyue to me the firste gendrid of thi sones;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 22:27; Deuteronomy 15:19; Exodus 13:12"> also of oxen, and of scheep thou schalt do in lijk maner; seuene daies be he with his modir, in the eiytithe dai thou schalt yelde hym to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:14; Leviticus 19:2; Deuteronomy 14:21"> Ye schulen be holi men to me; ye schulen not ete fleisch which is bifore taastid of beestis, but ye schulen caste forth to houndis.</span>
