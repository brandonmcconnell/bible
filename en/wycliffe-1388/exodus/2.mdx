<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 26:59; 1 Chronicles 23:12–14; Exodus 6:16–20"> Aftir these thingis a man of &#96;the hows of Leuy yede out, and took a wijf<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="took a wiyf into fleisly coupling: for sche was his wiyf bifore, and hadde childid Aaron and Marie his sister.">a</a></sup>of his kyn,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 11:23; Acts 7:20; Psalms 112:5"> which conseyuede, and childide a sone. And sche seiy hym wel farynge, and hidde him bi thre monethis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 1:22; Acts 7:19; Genesis 6:14"> And whanne sche myyte not hele, thanne sche took a &#96;leep of segge, and bawmede it with tar and pitch, and puttide the yong child with ynne, and puttide hym forth in a &#96;place of spier of the brenke of the flood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 26:59; Exodus 15:20; Micah 6:4"> the while his sistir stood afer, and bihelde the bifalling of the thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 7:21; Psalms 12:5; Psalms 9:9"> Lo! forsothe the douytir of Farao cam doun to be waischun in the flood, and hir damysels walkiden bi the brenke of the flood. And whanne sche hadde seyn a leep in the &#96;place of spier, sche sente oon of hir seruauntessis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 8:50; 1 Peter 3:8; Acts 7:21"> and sche openyde the leep brouyt to hir, and seiy a litil child wepynge ther ynne. And sche hadde mercy on the child, and seide, It is of the yonge children of Ebrews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 2:4; Numbers 26:59; Numbers 12:1"> To whom the &#96;sister of the child seide, Wolt thou that Y go, and clepe to thee an Ebrew womman, that may nurische the yong child?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 46:3–4; Psalms 27:10; Exodus 6:20"> She answeride, Go thou. The damysel yede, and clepide the &#96;modir of the child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> To whom &#96;the douytir of Farao spak, and seide, Take thou this child, and nurische to me; Y schal yyue to thee thi mede. The womman took, and nurischide the child, and bitook hym woxun to &#96;the douytir of Farao,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 11:24; Genesis 16:11; 1 Samuel 1:20"> whom sche purchaside &#96;in to the place of sone; and sche clepide his name Moises, and seide, For Y took hym fro the watir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 11:24–26; Acts 7:22–24; Exodus 1:11"> In tho daies, aftir that Moises encreesside, he yede out to hise britheren, and seiy the turment of hem, and a man Egipcian smytynge &#96;oon of Ebrews, hise britheren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 7:24–26"> And whanne he hadde biholdun hidur and thidir, and hadde seyn, that no man was present, he killide the Egipcian, and hidde in soond.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 7:26–28; 1 Corinthians 6:7–8"> And he yede out in another dai, and seiy tweyne Ebrews chidynge, and he seide to hym that dide wrong, Whi smytist thou thi brother?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 12:14; Matthew 21:23; Genesis 19:9"> Which answeride, Who ordeynede thee prince, ether iuge on vs? Whether thou wolt sle me, as thou killidist yisterdai the Egipcian? Moises dredde, and seide, Hou is this word maad opun?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 7:29; Hebrews 11:27; Genesis 24:11"> And Farao herde this word, and souyte to sle Moyses, which fledde fro his siyt, and dwellide in the lond of Madian, and sat bisidis a pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 24:11; Exodus 3:1; 1 Samuel 9:11"> Forsothe seuene douytris weren to the preest of Madian, that camen to drawe watir; and whanne the trouyis weren fillid, thei coueitiden to watere &#96;the flockis of her fadir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 29:10; Genesis 21:25; Genesis 26:15–22"> Scheepherdis camen aboue, and dreuen hem awei; and Moises roos, and defendide the dameselis; and he watride &#96;the scheep of hem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; Numbers 10:29; Exodus 4:18"> And whanne thei hadden turned ayen to Jetro, her fadir, he seide to hem, Whi camen ye swiftliere than ye weren wont?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 50:11; Genesis 29:10"> Thei answeriden, A man of Egipt delyuerede vs fro the hond of scheepherdis; ferthermore and he drow watir with vs, and yaf drynk to the scheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 31:54; Genesis 43:25; Hebrews 13:2"> And he seide, Where is that man? whi leften ye the man? clepe ye hym, that he ete breed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 31:38–40; Hebrews 13:5; Hebrews 11:25"> Therfor Moises swoor, that he wolde dwelle with Jetro; and he took a wijf, Sefora, &#96;the douyter of Jetro.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 11:13–14; Acts 7:29; Exodus 22:21"> And sche childide a sone to hym, whom he clepide Gersan, and seide, Y was a comelyng in an alyen lond. Forsothe sche childide an othir sone, whom he clepide Eliezer, and seide, For God of my fadir is myn helpere, and delyuerede me fro the hond of Farao.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 18:20–21; James 5:4; Exodus 3:7–9"> Forsothe aftir myche tyme the kyng of Egipt diede, and the sones of Israel inwardli weiliden for werkis, and crieden, and the cry of hem for werkis stiede to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 26:3; Psalms 105:42; Exodus 6:5"> And he herde the weilyng of hem, and he hadde mynde of the boond of pees, which he hadde maad with Abraham, Ysaac, and Jacob; and he bihelde the sones of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 4:31; Psalms 55:22; Exodus 3:7–8"> and knewe hem.</span>
