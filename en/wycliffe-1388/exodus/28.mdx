<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 18:7; Hebrews 5:1–5; Numbers 17:2–9"> Also applie thou to thee Aaron, thi brother, with hise sones, fro the myddis of the sones of Israel, that Aaron, Nadab, and Abyu, Eleazar, and Ythamar, be set in preesthod to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 8:30; Exodus 31:10; Revelation 19:8"> And thou schalt make an hooli clooth to Aaron, thi brother, in to glorie and fairenesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ephesians 1:17; 1 Corinthians 12:7–11; Isaiah 11:2"> And thou schalt speke to alle wise men in herte, whiche Y haue fillid with the spirit of prudence, that thei make clothis to Aaron, in whiche he schal be halewid, and schal mynystre to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 6:14; Revelation 9:17; Isaiah 59:17"> Forsothe these schulen be the clothis, whiche thei schulen make; &#96;thei schulen make racional, and a clooth on the schuldris, a coote, and a streyt lynnun clooth, a mytre, and a girdil; hooli cloothis to Aaron, thi brother, and to hise sones, that thei be set in preesthod to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 25:3–4; Exodus 39:2–3"> And thei schulen take gold, and iacynt, and purpur, and &#96;reed selk twies died, and bijs;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 39:2–7; Exodus 26:1"> forsothe thei schulen make the clooth on the schuldris of gold, and of iacynt, and purpur, and of &#96;reed selk twies died, and of bijs foldid ayen, bi broyderi werk of dyuerse colours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 39:4"> It schal haue twey hemmes ioyned in euer either side of hiynessis, that tho go ayen in to oon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 28:27–28; Leviticus 8:7; Exodus 29:5"> Thilke weuyng, and al dyuersite of the werk schal be of gold, and iacynt, and purpur, and of &#96;reed selk twies died, and bijs foldis ayen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13; 2 Chronicles 2:7; Isaiah 49:16"> And thou schalt take twei stoonys of onychym, and thou schalt graue in tho the names of the sones of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 1:1–4; Genesis 43:33"> sixe names in o stoon, and sixe othere in the tother stoon, bi the ordre of her birthe;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:19; Revelation 7:2; Jeremiah 22:24"> bi the werk of a grauere, and bi the peyntyng of a man that ourneth with gemmes thou schalt graue tho stoonys, with the names of the sones of Israel; and thou schalt enclose and cumpasse in gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 28:29; Joshua 4:7; Exodus 39:6–7"> And thou schalt sette tho stoonus in euer either side of the cloth on the schuldris, a memorial to the sones of Israel; and Aaron schal bere the names of hem bifor the Lord on euer either schuldre, for remembryng.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And thou schalt make hookis of gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 39:15; Exodus 39:17–18; 1 Kings 7:17"> and twey litil chaynes of clenneste gold, cleuynge to hem silf togidere, whiche litil chaynes thou schalt sette in the hookis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 28:30; Exodus 26:1; Exodus 28:4"> Also thou schalt make the racional of doom by werk of dyuerse colours, bi the weuyng of the cloth on the schuldre, of gold, iacynt, and purpur, of &#96;reed silk twies died, and of bijs foldid ayen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> It schal be foure cornerid, and double; it schal haue the mesure of a pawme of the hond, as wel in lengthe, as in breede.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13; Exodus 39:10–21; Proverbs 8:11"> And thou schalt sette ther ynne foure ordris of stoonys; in the firste ordre schal be the stoon sardius, and topazyus, and smaragdus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:16; Job 28:6; Song of Solomon 5:14"> in the secunde ordre schal be charbuncle, safir, and iaspis; in the thridde ordre schal be ligurie,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 54:12; Exodus 39:12"> achates, and ametiste;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:16; Revelation 4:3; Daniel 10:6"> in the fourthe ordre schal be crisolitus, onochyn, and berille; tho schulen be closid in gold, bi her ordris,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 21:12; Revelation 7:4–8; 1 Kings 18:31"> and schulen haue the names of the sones of Israel: tho schulen be graven with twelue names; al stonus bi hem silf, with the names of the sones &#96;of Israel bi hem silf, bi twelue lynagis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Thou schalt make in the racional chaynes cleuynge to hem silf togidere of pureste gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 25:11–15"> and thou schalt make twei goldun ryngis, whiche thou schalt sette in euer either hiynesse of racional.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And thou schalt ioyne the goldun chaynes with the ryngis that ben in the brynkis therof,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 39:15; Exodus 39:4; Exodus 28:14"> and thou schalt couple the &#96;last thingis of tho chaynes to twey hookis in euer either side of the &#96;cloth on the schuldur, that biholdith the racional.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And thou schalt make twei goldun ryngis, whiche thou schalt sette in the hiynesses of the racional, and in the hemmes of the cloth on the schuldur, that ben euene ayens, and biholden the lattere thingis therof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Also and thou schalt make tweyne othere goldun ryngis, that schulen be set in euer either side of the clooth on the schuldur bynethe, that biholdith ayens the face of the lowere ioynyng, that it may be set couenabli with the &#96;cloth on the schuldre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 15:38; Exodus 28:31; Exodus 39:30–31"> And the racional be boundun bi hise ryngis with the ryngis of the &#96;cloth on the schuldre, with a lace of iacynt, that the ioyning maad craftili dwelle, and that the racional and &#96;cloth on the schuldre moun not be departid ech fro other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 28:12; Exodus 28:30; Jeremiah 30:21"> And Aaron schal bere the names of the sones of Israel in the racional of doom on his brest, whanne he entrith in to the seyntuarie, a memorial bifor the Lord with outen ende.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:65; Ezra 2:63; Numbers 27:21"> Forsothe thou schalt sette in the racional of doom, techyng, and treuthe, whiche schulen be in the brest of Aaron, whanne he entrith bifor the Lord, and he schal bere the doom of the sones of Israel in his brest in the siyt of the Lord euere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 8:7; Exodus 28:4; Exodus 39:22–26"> And thou schalt make the coote of the &#96;cloth on the schuldre al of iacynt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:16; John 19:23–24; Exodus 39:28"> in whos myddil aboue schal be an hood, and a wouun hemme &#96;bi cumpas therof, as it is wont to be don in the hemmes of clothis, lest it be brokun liytli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 39:24–26; Zechariah 14:20; 1 Kings 7:18"> Forsothe bynethe at the feet of the same coote, bi cumpas, thou schalt make as &#96;piyn applis, of iacynt, and purpur, of &#96;reed selk twies died, and of biis foldid ayen; while smale bellis ben medlid in the myddis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Song of Solomon 8:2; John 15:4–8; Colossians 1:10"> so that a litil &#96;belle of gold be and a &#96;piyn appil, and eft another litel belle of gold and a &#96;pyn appil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Hebrews 9:12; Leviticus 16:2"> And Aaron schal be clothid with that coote in the office of seruyce, that sown be herd, whanne he entrith in to the seyntuarie, and goith out, in the siyt of the Lord; and that he die not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Zechariah 14:20; Leviticus 8:9; Exodus 39:30"> And thou schalt make a plate of pureste gold, in which thou schalt graue bi the werk of a grauere, the holi<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="the hooli of the Lord, that is, the name of the Lord, Tetragramaton.">a</a></sup>to the Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="the tragramaton.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 29:6; Exodus 28:4; Zechariah 3:5"> And thou schalt bynde that plate with a lace of iacynt, and it schal be on the mytre,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Numbers 18:1; Leviticus 10:17"> and schal neiye the forheed of the bischop. And Aaron schal bere the wickidnessis of hem whiche the sones of Israel &#96;offeriden, and halewiden in alle her yiftis and fre yiftis; forsothe the plate schal euere be in &#96;his forhed, that the Lord be plesid to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 45:14"> And thou schalt bynde the coot of biis, and the myter of bijs, and thou schalt make also a girdil, &#96;bi werk of broiderye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Exodus 39:41; Exodus 28:4; Exodus 28:2"> Forsothe thou schalt make redi to &#96;the sones of Aaron linnun cootis, and girdlis, and mytris, in to glorie and fairnesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 29:7; Leviticus 8:1–36; Exodus 40:15"> And thou schalt clothe Aaron, thi brother, with alle these, and hise sones with hym. And thou schalt sacre the hondis of alle; and thou schalt halewe hem, that thei be set in preesthood to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:18; Leviticus 6:10; Leviticus 16:4"> Also thou schalt make lynnun brechis, that thei hile the fleisch of her filthe fro the reynes &#96;til to the hipis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Exodus 27:21; Exodus 20:26; Leviticus 5:17"> And Aaron and hise sones schulen vse tho, whanne thei schulen entre in to the tabernacle of witnessyng, ether whanne thei neiyen to the auter, that thei mynystren in the seyntuarie, lest thei ben gilti of wickidnesse, and dien; it schal be a lawful thing euerlastynge to Aaron, and to his seed after hym.</span>
