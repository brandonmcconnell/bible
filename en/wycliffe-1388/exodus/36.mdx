<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 28:3; Exodus 35:30–35; Exodus 31:1–6"> Therfor Beseleel, and Ooliab, and ech wijs man, to whiche the Lord yaf wisdom and vndurstondyng, that thei kouden worche crafteli, maden thingis that weren nedeful in to vsis of seyntuarie, and whiche the Lord comaundide to be maad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 4:17; Hebrews 5:4; Exodus 31:6"> And whanne Moises hadde clepid hem, and ech lerned man, to whom the Lord hadde youe wisdom and kunnyng, and whiche profriden hem bi her wille to make werk,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:29; Exodus 35:27; Exodus 35:5–21"> he bitook to hem alle the yiftis of the sones of Israel. And whanne thei weren bisi in the werk ech dai, the puple offride auowis eerli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:10; Luke 12:42; Matthew 24:45"> Wherfor the werkmen weren compellid to come,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:6–10; 2 Corinthians 8:2–3; 2 Chronicles 24:14"> and thei seiden to Moises, The puple offrith more than is nedeful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Therfor Moises comaundide to be cried bi the vois of a criere, Nether man nether womman offre more ony thing in the werk of seyntuarie; and so it was ceessid fro yiftis to be offrid, for the thingis offrid sufficiden,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:10"> and weren ouer abundant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 6:23; Exodus 26:1–37; Ezekiel 1:5–28"> And alle wise men in herte to fille the werk of the tabernacle maden ten curteyns of bijs foldid ayen, and of iacynct, and purpur, and of reed selk twies died, bi dyuerse werk, and bi the craft of many colouris.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Of whiche curteyns oon hadde in lengthe eiyte and twenti cubitis, and foure cubitis in breede; o mesure was of alle curteyns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:27; Exodus 26:3; Psalms 122:3"> And he ioynede fyue curteyns oon to anothir, and he couplide othere fyue to hem silf to gidere;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 26:4"> and he made handlis of iacynt in the hemme of o curteyn on euer either side,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 26:5; Exodus 26:10"> and in lijk maner in the hemme of the tother curteyn, that the handlis schulen comen to gidere ayens hem silf, and schulen be ioyned togider;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4–5; Ephesians 2:20–22; 1 Corinthians 12:20"> wherfor he yettide also fifti goldun serclis, that schulen &#96;bite the handlis of curteyns; and o tabernacle was maad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 26:7–13"> &#96;He made also enleuene saies of the heeris of geet, to hile the roof of the tabernacle;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> o saie hadde thretti cubitis in lengthe, foure cubitis in breede; alle the saies weren of o mesure;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> of whiche saies he ioynede fyue bi hem silf, and sixe othere bi hem silf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And he made fifti handlis in the hemme of o say, and fifti in the hemme of the tother say, that tho schulden be ioyned to hem silf to gidere; and he made fifti bokelis of bras bi whiche</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> the roof was fastned to gidere, that oon hilyng were maad of alle the saies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 26:14"> He made also an hilyng of the tabernacle of the skynnes of rammes maad reed, and another veil aboue of skynnes of iacynt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 25:5; Numbers 25:1; Exodus 40:18–19"> He made also stondynge tablis of the tabernacle of the trees of Sechym;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> the lengthe of o table was of ten cubitis, and the breede helde o cubit and an half.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Twey dentyngis weren bi ech table, that the oon schulde be ioyned to the tother; so he made in al the tablis of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Of whiche tablis twenti weren at the mydday coost ayens the south,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> with fourti foundementis of siluer; twey foundementis weren set vndur o table on euer either side of the corneris, where the dentyngis of the sidis weren endid in the corneris.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And at the coost of the tabernacle that biholdith to the north he made twenti tablis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> with fourti foundementis of siluer, twei foundementis bi ech table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 26:22; Exodus 26:27"> Forsothe ayens the west<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="This that sueth, that is, al that part of the tabernacle that biholdith the see, is not in Ebru.">a</a></sup>he made sixe tablis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and tweyne othere tablis bi ech corner of the tabernacle bihinde,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 4:32; Ephesians 3:18–19; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> whiche weren ioyned fro bynethe til to aboue, and weren borun in to o ioynyng to gidere; so he made on euer either part bi the corneris,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 26:25"> that tho weren eiyte tablis to gidere, and hadden sixtene foundementis of siluer, that is, twei foundementis vndur ech table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 26:26–29; Exodus 25:28; Exodus 30:5"> He made also barris of the trees of Sechym, fyue barris to holde to gidere the tablis of o side of the tabernacle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 26:26"> and fyue othere barris to schappe to gidere the tablis of the tother side; and without these, he made fyue othere barris at the west coost of the tabernacle ayens the see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> He made also another barre, that schulde come bi the myddil tables fro corner til to corner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Forsothe he ouergildide tho wallis of tablis, and yetide the siluerne foundementis &#96;of tho, and he made the goldun serclis &#96;of tho, bi whiche the barris myyten be brouyt in, and be hilide the same barris with goldun platis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; Hebrews 10:20; Exodus 40:21"> He made also a veil dyuerse and departid, of iacynt, and purpur, and reed selk, and bijs foldid ayen bi werk of broiderie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:18"> He made also foure pileris of &#96;the trees of Sechym, whyche pileris with the heedis he ouergildide, and yetide the siluerne foundementis &#96;of tho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 26:36–37; Exodus 40:28"> He made also in the entryng of the tabernacle a tent of iacynt, and purpur, and reed selk &#96;and bijs foldid ayen bi the werk of a broydreie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 27:10"> And he made fyue pileris with her heedis, whiche he hilide with gold, and he yetide the brasun foundementis &#96;of tho, whiche he hilide with gold.</span>
