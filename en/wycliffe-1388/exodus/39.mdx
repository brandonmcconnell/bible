<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 31:10; Exodus 35:23; Exodus 35:19"> Forsothe of iacynt, and purpur, vermyloun, and bijs, he made clothis, in whiche Aaron was clothid, whanne he mynystride in hooli thingis, as the Lord comaundide to Moises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 28:6–12; Leviticus 8:7; Exodus 25:7"> Therfor he made the &#96;cloth on the schuldris of gold, iacynt, and purpur, and of reed selk twies died,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 36:8; Exodus 26:1"> and of bijs foldid ayen, bi werk of broiderie; also he kittide thinne goldun platis, and made thinne in to threedis, that tho moun be foldid ayen, with the warp of the formere colouris;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> and he made tweyne hemmes couplid to hem silf to gidere, in euer either side of the endis; and &#96;he made a girdil of the same colouris,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 1:13; Matthew 28:20; Isaiah 11:5"> as the Lord comaundide to Moises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 28:9; Ezekiel 28:13; Job 28:16"> And he made redi twei &#96;stonys of onychyn, boundun and closid in gold, and grauun bi the craft of worchere in iemmys, with the names of the sones of Israel; sixe names in o stoon, and sixe in the tother stoon, bi the ordre of her birthe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 4:7; Exodus 28:12; Exodus 28:29"> And he settide tho stoonus in the sidis of the &#96;clooth on the schuldris, in to a memorial of the sones of Israel, as the Lord comaundide to Moises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Leviticus 8:8–9; Isaiah 59:17"> He made also the racional, &#96;by werk of broiderie, bi the werk of the &#96;cloth on the schuldris, of gold, iacynt, purpur, and reed selk twies died, and of biis foldid ayen; he made the racional foure cornerid,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> double, of the mesure of foure fyngris.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 28:21; Exodus 28:16–17; Revelation 21:19–21"> And settide thereynne foure ordris of iemmes; in the firste ordre was sardius, topazius, smaragdus; in the secounde was carbuncle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 28:18; Ezekiel 28:13"> safir, iaspis;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> in the thridde ordre was ligurie, achates, ametiste;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> in the fourthe ordre was crisolite, onochyn, and berille, cumpassid and enclosid with gold, bi her ordris.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 21:12"> And tho twelue stonys weren grauyn with twelue names, of the lynage of Israel, alle stonys bi hem silf, bi the names of alle lynagis bi hem silf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 10:28; John 17:12; 1 Peter 1:5"> Thei maden also in the racional litle chaynes, cleuynge to hem silf togidre,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 25:12"> of pureste gold, and tweyne hokys, and so many ryngis of gold. Forsothe thei settiden the ryngis on euer either side of the racional,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> of whiche ryngis twei goldun chaynes hangiden, whiche thei settiden in the hokis, that stonden forth in the corneris of the &#96;cloth on the schuldris.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 28:14; Song of Solomon 1:10"> These acordiden so to hem silf, bothe bifore and bihynde, that the &#96;cloth on the schuldris, and the racional,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> weren knyt togidere, fastned to the girdil, and couplid ful strongli with ryngis, whiche ryngis a lace of iacynt ioynede togidere, lest tho weren loose, and &#96;fletiden doun, and weren moued ech from other, as the Lord comaundide to Moises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 26:3"> Thei maden also &#96;a coote on the schuldris, al of iacynt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 16:24; 1 Corinthians 1:25; 1 Corinthians 1:27"> and the hood in the hiyere part, aboute the myddis, and a wouun hemme, bi the cumpas of the hood;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 28:31–35"> forsothe bynethe at the feet piyn applis of iacynt, and purpur, and vermyloun, and biys foldid ayen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and litle bellis of pureste gold, whiche thei settiden bitwixe pum garnadis, in the &#96;lowest part of the coote, bi cumpas;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 28:33; Galatians 5:22"> a goldun litle belle, and a piyn apple; with whiche the bischop yede ourned, whanne he &#96;was set in seruyce, as the Lord comaundide to Moises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 28:33–34; Song of Solomon 4:13; Psalms 89:15"> Thei maden also cootis of bijs, bi wouun werk, to Aaron and to hise sones,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 28:34; Song of Solomon 4:13; Song of Solomon 6:7"> and mytres with smale corouns of biys,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:18; 1 Peter 1:13; Isaiah 61:10"> and lynnun clothis of bijs;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Ezekiel 44:18; Exodus 28:39"> forsothe a girdil of bijs foldid ayen, of iacynt, purpur, and vermyloun, departid bi craft of broyderie, as the Lord comaundide to Moises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 28:39"> Thei maden also a plate of hooli worschipyng, of pureste gold, and thei writeden therynne bi werk of a worchere in iemmes, The hooli of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; Hebrews 7:26; Hebrews 1:3"> And thei bounden it with the mytre bi a lace of iacynt, as the Lord comaundide to Moises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Therfor al the werk of the tabernacle, and the hilyng of the witnessyng, was parformed; and the sones of Israel diden alle thingis whiche the Lord comaundide to Moises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 39:42–43; Matthew 28:20; Exodus 25:40"> And thei offeriden the tabernacle, and the roof, and al the purtenaunce, ryngis, tablis, barris, pileris, and foundementis;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 35:11–19; Exodus 31:7–11; Exodus 36:1–38"> the hilyng of &#96;skynnes of rammes, maad reed, and another hilyng of skynnys of iacynt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> the veil, the arke, barris, propiciatorie;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Hebrews 9:5; Exodus 25:17; Hebrews 9:8"> the boord with vessels, and with the looues of settyng forth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 25:30; 1 Kings 7:48"> the candilstike, lanternes, and the purtenauncis of tho, with oile;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Philippians 2:15; Exodus 27:21; Matthew 5:14–16"> the goldun auter, and oynement, and encense of swete smellynge spiceries;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 30:7; Exodus 37:29; Exodus 30:3"> and the tente in the entryng of the tabernacle;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 8:64; Exodus 38:30"> the brasun auter, gridile, barris, and alle vessels therof; the &#96;greet waischyng vessel, with his foundement; the tentis of the greet street, and the pilers with her foundementis;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Exodus 27:9–16"> the tente in the entring of the greet street, and the coordis, and stakis therof. No thing of the vessels failide, that weren comaundid to be maad in to the seruyce of the tabernacle, and in to the roof of the boond of pees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 28:2; Exodus 31:10; Exodus 39:1"> Also the sones of Israel offriden the clothis whiche the prestis, that is, Aaron and hise sones, vsen in the seyntuarie,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Exodus 35:10; Exodus 23:21–22; 2 Timothy 2:15"> as the Lord comaundide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 6:23–27; Leviticus 9:22–23; 2 Samuel 6:18"> And aftir that Moises siy alle tho thingis fillid, he blesside hem.</span>
