<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:5; Exodus 21:2–3"> If ony man sillith his douyter in to seruauntesse, sche schal not go out as handmaidis weren wont to go out;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 28:8; Deuteronomy 20:7; Malachi 2:11–15"> if sche displesith in the iyen of hir lord, to whom sche was bitakun, he schal delyuere hir; sotheli he schal not haue power to sille hir to an alien puple, if he forsakith hir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Forsothe if he weddith hir to his sonne, he schal do to hir &#96;bi the custom of douytris;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:1–6"> that if he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="In Ebru it is thus, that if he take to him another womman, he shal not decreese exspencis and hilynge and hir our, that is, if he takith to him, that is, to his sone, another wyf with the handmayde takun bifore into wyf, he shal decreese spensis, that is, sufficient lyflode in hir hylynge, that is, couenable clothinge, and hir our, that is, tyme to yelde dette, he shal not decreesse this in withdrawynge fro hir for the tother wyf weddid aboue, for thanne thei miyten haue manye wyues togidere.">b</a></sup>takith another womman to hym, he schal puruey to the damysele weddingis, and clothis, and he schal not denye the prijs of chastite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> If he doith not these thre, sche schal go out freli without money.</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 2:28–34; Hebrews 10:26; Numbers 15:30–31"> If ony man sleeth his neiybore bi biforecastyng, and bi aspies, drawe thou hym awey fro myn auter, that he die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:18–21; Proverbs 30:17; 1 Timothy 1:9"> He that smytith his fadir, ether modir, die by deeth.</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 20:20; Mark 7:10–11; Deuteronomy 27:16"> He that stelith a man, and sillith hym, if he is conuyt of the gilt, die bi deeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 21:20; Deuteronomy 25:11; 2 Samuel 14:6"> If men chiden, and the tother smyte his neiybore with a stoon, ether with the fist, and he is not deed, but liggith in the bed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:29; Zechariah 8:4"> if he risith, and goith forth on his staf, he that smoot schal be innocent; so netheles that he restore hise trauelis, and costis in lechis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 29:19; Deuteronomy 19:21; Numbers 35:19"> He that smytith his seruaunt, ether handmayde, with a yerde, and thei ben deed in hise hondis, schal be gilti of cryme.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 25:44–46"> Sotheli if the seruaunt ouerlyueth o dai, ether tweyne, he schal not be suget to peyne, &#96;that is of deeth, for the seruaunt is his catel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:18–19; Exodus 21:30; Deuteronomy 16:18"> If men chiden, and a man smytith a womman with childe, and sotheli makith the child deed borun, but the womman ouerlyueth, he schal be suget to the harm, as myche as the &#96;hosebonde of the womman axith, and the iugis demen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:21; Numbers 35:31; Leviticus 24:19"> But if the deeth of hir sueth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:21; Matthew 5:38–40; Matthew 7:2"> he schal yelde lijf for lijf, iye for iye, tooth for tooth, hond for hond, foot for foot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> brennyng for brennyng, wounde &#96;with schedyng of blood for wounde &#96;with schedyng of blood, &#96;a wan wounde for a wan wounde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 21:20; Job 31:13–15; Psalms 72:12–14"> If a man smytith the iye of his seruaunt, ethir of handmaide, and makith hem oon iyed, he schal delyuere hem fre for &#96;the iye which he puttide out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Also if he smytith out a tooth fro his seruaunt, ethir handmaide, in lijk maner he schal delyuere hem fre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 21:32; Leviticus 20:15–16; Genesis 9:5–6"> If an oxe smytith with horn a man, ether a womman, and thei ben deed, the oxe schal be oppressid with stoonus, and hise fleischis schulen not be etun, and the lord of the oxe schal be innocent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:1–9"> That if the oxe was &#96;a pultere with horn fro yisterdai and the thridde dai ago, and men warneden &#96;the lord of hym, nether the lord closide hym, and he sleeth a man, ethir womman, bothe the oxe schal be oppressid with stoonus, and thei schulen sle &#96;the lord of hym;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 21:22; Exodus 30:12; Numbers 35:31–33"> that if prijs is put to the lord, he schal yyue for his lijf what euer he is axide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And if he smytith with horn a son, and a douytir, he schal be suget to lijk sentence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 26:15; Zechariah 11:12–13; Matthew 27:3–9"> If the oxe asailith a seruaunt, and handmaide, the lord of the oxe schal yyue thretti siclis<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A sicle is xx. halpens.">c</a></sup>of siluer to &#96;his lord; forsothe the oxe schal be oppressid with stoonus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 28:10; Jeremiah 18:20; Ecclesiastes 10:8"> If ony man openeth a cisterne, and diggith, and hilith it not, and an oxe ether asse fallith in to it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 22:6; Exodus 21:29–30; Exodus 22:14"> the lord of the cisterne schal yelde the prijs of the werk beestis; forsothe that that is deed schal be his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> If another mannus oxe woundith the oxe of another man, and he is deed, thei schulen sille the quyke oxe, and thei schulen departe the prijs; forsothe thei schulen departe bitwixe hem the karkeis of the deed oxe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Forsothe if his lord wiste, that the oxe was a puttere fro yistirdai and the thridde dai ago, and kepte not him, he schal yelde oxe for oxe, and he schal take the hool carkeys.</span>
