<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 78:18; Deuteronomy 6:16; 1 Corinthians 10:9"> Whiche puple chidde ayens Moises, and seide, Yyue thou water to vs, that we drynke. To whiche Moises answeride, What chiden ye ayens me, and whi tempten ye the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 16:2–3; Exodus 15:24"> Therfor the puple thristide there for the scarsnesse of watir, and grutchiden ayens Moises, and seide, Whi madist thou vs to go out of Egipt, to sle vs, and oure fre children, and beestis, for thrist?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:6; Numbers 14:10; John 8:59"> Forsothe Moises criede to the Lord, and seide, What schal Y do to this puple? yit a litil, also it schal stone me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 7:19–20; Numbers 20:8–11; Acts 20:23–24"> The Lord seide to Moises, Go thou bifore the puple, and take with thee of the eldre men of Israel, and take in thin hond the yerde, &#96;bi which thou hast smyte the flood, and go; lo!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:4; Numbers 20:9–11; Nehemiah 9:15"> Y schal stonde there before thee, aboue the stoon of Oreb, and thou schalt smyte the stoon, and water schal go out therof, that the puple drynke. Moises dide so byfore the eldere men of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 81:7; Psalms 95:8; Numbers 20:13"> and he clepide the name of that place Temptacioun, for the chidyng of the sones of Israel, and for thei temptiden the Lord, and seiden, Whether the Lord is in vs, ether nay?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 36:12; Deuteronomy 25:17–19; Numbers 24:20"> Forsothe Amalech cam, and fauyt ayens Israel in Rafidym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 24:13; Deuteronomy 32:44; Exodus 4:20"> And Moises seide to Josue, Chese thou men, and go out, and fiyte to morewe ayens men of Amalech; lo! Y schal stonde in the cop of the hil, and Y schal haue &#96;the yerde of God in myn hond.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 24:14; Exodus 31:2; Exodus 17:12"> Josue dide as Moises spak, and fauyt ayens Amalech. Forsothe Moises, and Aaron, and Hur stieden on the cop of the hil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:8; James 5:16; Psalms 56:9"> and whanne Moises reiside the hondis, Israel ouercam; forsothe if he let down a litil, Amalech ouercam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3; Philippians 1:19; Hebrews 12:12"> Sotheli &#96;the hondis of Moises weren heuy, therfor thei token a stoon, and puttide vndir hym, in which stoon he sat. Forsothe Aaron and Hur susteyneden hise hondis, on euer eithir side; and it was don, that hise hondis weren not maad weri, til to the goyng down of the sunne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 10:42; Joshua 10:37; Joshua 10:32"> And Josue<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Josue drof awey, in Ebreu it is, made feble, for as Ebreus seien, he killide alle the stronge men of Amalech in that batel.">a</a></sup>droof a wey Amalech and his puple, in &#96;the mouth of swerd, that is, bi the scharpnesse of the swerd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 34:27; Numbers 24:20; 1 Samuel 30:1"> Forsothe the Lord seide to Moises, Wryte thou this in a book, for mynde, and take in the eeris of Josue; for Y schal do a wei the mynde of Amalech fro vndur heuene.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 22:14; Judges 6:24; Psalms 60:4"> And Moises bildide an auter, and clepide the name therof The Lord myn enhaunsere,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 21:8–11; Isaiah 66:1; Acts 7:49"> and seide, For the hond of the Lord aloone, and the bateil of God schal be ayens Amalech, fro generacioun in to generacioun.</span>
