<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 4:15–16; Psalms 82:6; John 10:35–36"> And the Lord seide to Moises, Lo! Y haue maad thee the god of Farao; and Aaron, thi brother, schal be thi prophete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 4:15; Exodus 6:29; Deuteronomy 4:2"> Thou schalt speke to Aaron alle thingis whiche Y comaunde to thee, and he schal speke to Farao, that he delyuere the sones of Israel fro his hond.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 4:21; Exodus 11:9; Psalms 105:27–36"> But Y schal make hard his herte, and Y schal multiplie my signes and merueils in the lond of Egipt, and he schal not here you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 11:9; Exodus 6:6; Exodus 12:51"> and Y schal sende myn hond on Egipt, and Y schal lede out myn oost, and my puple, the sones of Israel, fro the lond of Egipt bi mooste domes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 14:18; Exodus 7:17; Exodus 8:22"> and Egipcians schulen wite, that Y am the Lord, which haue holde forth myn hond on Egipt, and haue led out of the myddis of hem the sones of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 7:2; Genesis 6:22; Exodus 7:10"> And so Moises dide and Aaron; as the Lord comaundide, so thei diden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:7; Acts 7:30; Deuteronomy 31:2"> Forsothe Moyses was of fourescoor yeer, and Aaron was of fourescoor yeer and thre, whanne thei spaken to Farao.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the Lord seide to Moises and to Aaron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 4:2; John 2:18; Isaiah 7:11"> Whanne Farao schal seie to you, Schewe ye signes to vs, thou schalt seie to Aaron, Take thi yerde, and caste forth it before Farao, and be it turned into a serpent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 4:3; Luke 10:19; Amos 9:3"> And so Moises and Aaron entriden to Farao, and diden as the Lord comaundide; and Aaron took the yeerde, and castide forth bifore Farao and hise seruauntis, which yerde was turned in to a serpent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 7:22; 2 Timothy 3:8; Exodus 8:18"> Forsothe Farao clepide wise men, and witchis, and thei also diden bi enchauntementis of Egipt, and bi summe priuy thingis in lijk maner;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 4:4; Exodus 9:11; Exodus 8:18–19"> and alle castiden forth her yerdis, whiche weren turned in to dragouns; but the yerde of Aaron deuouride &#96;the yerdis of hem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 4:21; Exodus 8:15; Zechariah 7:11–12"> And the herte of Farao was maad hard, and he herde not hem, as the Lord comaundide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 10:20; Exodus 8:15; Exodus 10:1"> Forsothe the Lord seide to Moyses, The herte of Farao is maad greuouse, he nyle delyuere the puple;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 8:20; Exodus 2:5; Exodus 7:10"> go thou to hym eerli; lo! he schal go out to the watris, and thou schalt stonde in the comyng of hym on the brynke of the flood; and thou schalt take in thin honde the yerde, that was turned into a dragoun,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 3:18; Exodus 9:1; Exodus 9:13"> and thou schalt seie to hym, The Lord God of Ebrews sente me to thee, and seide, Delyuere thou my puple, that it make sacrifice to me in desert; til to present time thou noldist here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 4:9; Exodus 5:2; Exodus 7:5"> Therfor the Lord seith these thingis, In this thou schalt wite, that Y am the Lord; lo! Y schal smyte with the yerde, which is in myn hond, the watir of the flood, and it schal be turned in to blood;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 7:24; Exodus 7:21; Numbers 11:20"> and the fischis that ben in the flood schulen die; and the watris schulen wexe rotun, and Egipcians drynkynge the watir of the flood schulen be turmentid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Exodus 10:12; Exodus 8:5–6"> Also the Lord seide to Moises, Seie thou to Aaron, Take thi yerde, and holde forth thin hond on the watris of Egipt, and on the flodis of hem, and on the stremys &#96;of hem, and on the mareis, and alle lakis of watris, that tho be turned in to blood; and blood be in al the lond of Egipt, as wel in vessils of tree as of stoon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 78:44; Psalms 105:29; Exodus 7:17–18"> And Moises and Aaron diden so, as the Lord comaundide; and Aaron reiside the yerde, and smoot the watir of the flood bifore Farao and hise seruauntis, which watir was turned in to blood;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 7:18; Revelation 8:9"> and fischis, that weren in the flood, dieden; and the flood was rotun, and Egipcians myyten not drynke the water of the flood; and blood was in al the lond of Egipt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 7:11; 2 Timothy 3:8; Jeremiah 27:18"> And the witchis of Egipcians diden in lijk maner by her enchauntementis; and the herte of Farao was maad hard, and he herde not hem, as the Lord comaundide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:24; Ezekiel 40:4; Malachi 2:2"> And he turnede awei hym silf, and entride in to his hows, nethir he took it to herte, yhe, in this tyme.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Forsothe alle Egipcians diggiden watir &#96;bi the cumpas of the flood, to drinke; for thei myyten not drynke of the &#96;watir of the flood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 10:23; Exodus 8:9–10; 2 Samuel 24:13"> And seuene daies weren fillid, aftir that the Lord smoot the flood.</span>
