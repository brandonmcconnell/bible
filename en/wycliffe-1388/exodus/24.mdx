<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 24:15; Exodus 24:13; Hebrews 10:21–22"> and Moises aloone stie to the Lord, and thei schulen not neiye, nether the puple schal stie with hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 19:8; Exodus 24:7; Joshua 24:22"> Therfore Moises cam, and telde to the puple alle the wordis and domes of the Lord; and al the puple answeride with o vois, We schulen do alle the wordis of the Lord, whiche he spak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 28:18; Deuteronomy 31:9; Joshua 4:8–9"> Forsothe Moises wroot alle the wordis of the Lord; and he roos eerli, and bildide an auter to the Lord at the rootis of the hil, and he bildide twelue titlis bi twelue lynagis of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 18:12; Leviticus 3:1–17; Exodus 19:22"> And he sente yonge men of the sones of Israel, and thei offriden brent sacrifices, and &#96;thei offriden pesible sacrifices &#96;to the Lord, twelue<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="twelue is not in Ebreu, and therfor it semeth that ij. caluys onely weren there.">a</a></sup>calues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 1:19; Exodus 24:8; Hebrews 9:18"> And so Moises took half the part of the blood, and sente in to grete cuppis; forsothe he schedde the residue part on the auter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:11–13; 1 Thessalonians 5:27; Exodus 24:3–4"> And he took the book of the boond of pees, and redde, while the puple herde; whiche seiden, We schulen do alle thingis which the Lord spak, and we schulen be obedient.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:4–5; Exodus 24:6; Ezekiel 36:25"> Forsothe he took, and sprengide &#96;the blood on the puple, and seide, This is the blood of the boond of pees, which the Lord couenauntide with yow on alle these wordis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 24:1"> And Moises, and Aaron, and Nadab, and Abyu, and seuenti of the eldere men of Israel stieden,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 1:18; Ezekiel 10:1; Genesis 32:30"> and seiyen God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="and sien God, not in his beyng, but in mannus licnesse representinge him.">b</a></sup>of Israel, vndur hise feet, as the werk of safire stoon, and as heuene whanne it is cleer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 15:23–24; Exodus 18:12; Genesis 31:54"> And he sente not his hond on hem<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ebrus seyn, that this is the very text, and he sente not his hond on the lordis of the sones of Israel. that is, on lordis, the gloos seith.">c</a></sup>of the sones of Israel, that hadden go fer awei; and thei sien God, and eeten and drunkun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:22; Exodus 31:18; Exodus 32:15–16"> Forsothe the Lord seide to Moises, &#96;Stie thou to me in to the hil, and be thou there, and Y schal yyue to thee tablis of stoon, and the lawe, and comaundementis, whiche Y haue write, that thou teche the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 33:11; Numbers 11:28; Exodus 3:1"> Moises and Josue his mynystre risen, and Moises stiede in to the hil of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 17:10; Exodus 17:12; Genesis 22:5"> and seide to the eldere men, Abide ye here, til we turnen ayen to you; ye han Aaron and Hur with you, if ony thing of questioun is maad, ye schulen telle to hem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 19:9; Matthew 17:5; Exodus 19:16"> And whanne Moises hadde stied,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 16:10; Numbers 16:42; Ezekiel 1:28"> a cloude hilide the hil, and the glorie of the Lord dwellide on Synai, and kyueride it with a cloude sixe daies; forsothe in the seuenthe dai the Lord clepide hym fro the myddis of the cloude; forsothe the licnesse of glorie of the Lord</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Deuteronomy 4:24; Exodus 3:2"> was as fier brennynge on the cop of the hil in the siyt of the sones of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:9; Exodus 34:28; Deuteronomy 10:10"> And Moises entride into the myddis of the cloude, and stiede in to the hil, and he was there fourti daies and fourti nyytis.</span>
