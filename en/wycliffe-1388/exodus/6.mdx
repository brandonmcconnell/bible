<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 28:4; Genesis 15:18; Genesis 17:7–8"> and Y made couenaunt with hem, that Y schulde yyue to hem the lond of Canaan, the lond of her pilgrymage, in which thei weren comelyngis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 2:24; Psalms 106:44–45; Isaiah 63:9"> Y herde the weilyng of the sones of Israel, in which the Egipcians oppresseden hem, and Y hadde mynde of my couenaunt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:8; 1 Chronicles 17:21; Exodus 7:4"> Therfor seie thou to the sones of Israel, Y am the Lord, that schal lede out you of the prisoun of Egipcians; and Y schal delyuere fro seruage; and Y schal ayen bie in &#96;an hiy arm, and in grete domes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6; Deuteronomy 29:13; Deuteronomy 4:20"> and Y schal take you to me in to a puple, and Y schal be youre God; and ye schulen wite, for Y am youre Lord God, &#96;which haue led you out of the prisoun of Egipcians,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 26:3; Genesis 15:18; Ezekiel 20:42"> and haue led you in to the lond, on which Y reiside myn hond, that Y schulde yyue it to Abraham, and to Ysaac, and to Jacob; and Y schal yyue to you that lond to be weldid; I the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 5:21; Job 21:4; Exodus 14:12"> Therfor Moises telde alle thingis to the sones of Irael, whiche assentide not to hym for the angwisch of spirit, and for the hardest werk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And the Lord spak to Moises,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 6:29; Exodus 5:23; Exodus 3:10"> and seide, Entre thou, and speke to Farao, kyng of Egipt, that he delyuere the children of Israel fro his lond.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 4:10; Jeremiah 1:6; Exodus 6:30"> Moises answeride bifore the Lord, Lo! the children of Israel here not me, and hou schal Farao here, moost sithen Y am vncircumcidid in lippis?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 27:23; 1 Timothy 6:13; Psalms 91:11"> And the Lord spak to Moises and to Aaron, and yaf comaundementis to the sones of Israel, and to Farao, kyng of Egipt, that thei schulden lede out the sones of Israel fro the lond of Egipt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 46:9; 1 Chronicles 5:3; Numbers 26:5–7"> These ben the princis of housis bi her meynees. The sones of Ruben, the firste gendrid of Israel, Enoch, and Fallu, Esrom, and Charmy; these ben the kynredis of Ruben.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 4:24; Genesis 46:10; Numbers 26:12–13"> The sones of Symeon, Jamuel, and Jamyn, and Aod, and Jachym, and Soer, and Saul, the sone of a womman of Canaan; these ben the kynretis of Symeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 3:17; Genesis 46:11; 1 Chronicles 6:1"> And these ben the names of the sones of Leuy by her kynredis, Gerson, and Caath, and Merary. Forsothe the yeeris of lijf of Leuy weren an hundrid and seuene and thretti.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:17; Numbers 3:18; 1 Chronicles 23:7"> The sones of Gerson, Lobny and Semei, bi her kynredis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:18; 1 Chronicles 6:2; Numbers 3:19"> The sones of Caath, Amram, and Isuar, and Hebron, and Oziel; and the yeeris of lijf of Caath weren an hundrid and thre and thretti.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 3:20; 1 Chronicles 23:21; 1 Chronicles 6:19"> The sones of Merari weren Mooli and Musi. These weren the kynredis of Leuy bi her meynees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 26:59; Exodus 2:1–2; Exodus 6:18"> Forsothe Amram took a wijf, Jocabed, douytir of his fadris brother, and sche childide to hym Aaron, and Moises, and Marie; and the yeeris of lijf of Amram weren an hundred and seuene and thretti.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 16:1; 1 Chronicles 6:37–38; Numbers 16:32"> Also the sones of Isuar weren Chore, and Nafeg, and Zechry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 10:4; Numbers 3:30; Nehemiah 3:20"> Also the sones of Oziel weren Misael, and Elisaphan, and Sechery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ruth 4:19–20; Numbers 2:3; Numbers 1:7"> Sotheli Aaron took a wijf, Elizabeth, the douytir of Amynadab, the sistir of Naason, and sche childide to hym Nadab, and Abyu, and Eleazar, and Ythamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:22–23; Numbers 16:1; 1 Samuel 1:1"> Also the sones of Chore weren Aser, and Elcana, and Abiasab; thes weren the kinredis of Chore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 24:33; Numbers 25:7–13; Joshua 22:31–32"> And sotheli Eleazar, sone of Aaron, took a wijf of the douytris of Phatiel, and sche childide Fynees to hym. These ben the princis of the meynees of Leuy bi her kynredis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 12:51; Exodus 12:17; Exodus 7:4"> This is Aaron and Moises, to whiche the Lord comaundide, that thei schulden lede out of the lond of Egipt the sones of Israel by her cumpanyes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 6:26; Exodus 7:10; Psalms 77:20"> these it ben, that speken to Pharao king of Egipt, that thei lede the sones of Israel out of Egipt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> this is Moises and Aaron, in the dai in which the Lord spak to Moises in the lond of Egipt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 6:11; Exodus 7:2; Exodus 6:2"> And the Lord spak to Moises, and seide, Y am the Lord; spek thou to Farao, kyng of Egipt, alle thingis whiche Y speke to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 6:12; Exodus 4:10; 1 Corinthians 9:16–17"> And Moises seide bifore the Lord, Lo! Y am vncircumcidid in lippis; hou schal Farao here me?</span>
