<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 8:3; Exodus 37:25–28; Exodus 40:5"> Also thou schalt make an auter of the trees of Sechym, to brenne encense;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 27:2"> and the auter schal haue a cubit of lengthe, and another cubit of brede, that is foure cornerid, and twei cubitis in heiythe; corneris schulen come forth of the auter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 25:11; Exodus 25:24"> And thou schalt clothe it with clennest gold, as wel the gridil<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="the gridil therof, in Ebreu it is, the roof therof, that is, the hiyere part.">a</a></sup>therof, as the wallis and corneris bi cumpas therof; and thou schalt make to the auter a litil goldun coroun,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 25:27; Exodus 26:29; Exodus 27:7"> &#96;bi cumpas, and twei goldun serclis vndur the coroun by alle sidis, that barris be put in to the serclis, and the auter be borun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 25:13; Exodus 25:27"> Also thou schalt make tho barris of the trees of Sechym, and thou schalt ouergilde;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 25:21–22; Exodus 30:36; Leviticus 16:13"> and thou schalt sette the auter ayens the veil, which veil hangith bifor the ark of witnessyng bifor the propiciatorie, bi which the witnessyng is hilid, where Y schal speke to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:28; 1 Chronicles 23:13; Exodus 27:20–21"> And Aaron schal brenne theronne encense smellynge swetly eerli; whanne he schal araye the lanternes, he schal brenne it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; 1 Thessalonians 5:17; Hebrews 7:25"> and whanne he settith the lanternes at euentid, he schal brenne euerlastynge encense bifor the Lord, in to youre generaciouns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1"> Ye schulen not offre theronne encense of other makyng, nethir offryng, and slayn sacrifice, nether ye schulen offre fletynge offryngis thereonne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 16:18; Hebrews 9:22–23; Leviticus 16:29–30"> And Aaron schal preie on the corneres therof onis bi the yeer, in the blood which is offrid for synne, and he schal plese theronne in youre generaciouns; it schal be the hooli of hooli thingis to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the Lord spak to Moises,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 31:50; 1 Chronicles 27:24; Exodus 38:25–26"> and seide, Whanne thou schalt take the summe of the sones of Israel, alle bi hem silf schulen yyue &#96;bi the noumbre prijs for her soulis to the Lord, and veniaunce schal not be in hem, whanne thei ben noumbrid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 27:25; Numbers 3:47; Ezekiel 45:12"> Sotheli ech that passith to the name, schal yyue this, half a sicle bi the mesure of the temple; a sicle hath twenti halpens; the myddil part of a cicle schal be offrid to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 1:20; Numbers 1:18; Numbers 1:3"> He that is hadde in noumbre, fro twenti yeer and aboue,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 31:50; Leviticus 17:11; Exodus 30:12"> schal yyue prijs; a riche man schal not adde to the myddil of cicle, and a pore man schal no thing abate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:32–33; Numbers 16:40; Exodus 38:25–31"> And thou schalt bitake in to vsis of the tabernacle of witnessyng the money takun, which is gaderid of the sones of Israel, that it be the mynde of hem bifor the Lord, and he schal be merciful to &#96;the soulis of hem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the Lord spak to Moises,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 40:7; Exodus 38:8; 1 Kings 7:23"> and seide, Also thou schalt make a greet vessil of bras with his foundement<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="In Ebru it is with his feete.">b</a></sup>to waische, and thou schalt sette it bitwixe the tabernacle of witnessyng and the auter &#96;of brent sacrifices; and whanne watir is put therynne,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; Exodus 40:31–32; Psalms 26:6"> Aaron and hise sones schulen waische therynne her hondis and feet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1–3; Hebrews 12:28–29; 1 Chronicles 13:10"> whanne thei schulen entre in to the tabernacle of witnessyng, and whanne thei schulen neiye to the auter that thei offre therynne encense to the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 28:43; Exodus 27:21"> lest perauenture thei dien; it schal be a lawful thing euerlastinge to hym and to his seed bi successiouns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And the Lord spak to Moises,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:19; Song of Solomon 4:14; Ezekiel 27:22"> and seide, Take to thee swete smellynge spiceries, of the firste and chosun myrre, fyue hundrid siclis; and of canel the half, that is, twei hundrid and fifti siclis;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 45:8; Exodus 29:40; Numbers 3:47"> in lijk maner of calamy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="that is, a swete smellynge tre, smal and ful of knottis.">c</a></sup>twei hundrid and fifti siclis; also of casia fyue hundrid siclis, in the weiyte of seyntuarie; oile of olyue trees, the mesure hyn<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="that is, ij pound.">d</a></sup>;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; 1 Chronicles 9:30; Exodus 40:9"> and thou schalt make the hooly oile of anoyntyng, an oynement maad bi the werk of a &#96;makere of oynement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 7:1; 1 John 2:20; Isaiah 61:1"> And thou schal anoynte therof the tabernacle of witnessyng, and the ark of testament, and the boord with hise vessels,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> the candilstike, and the purtenaunces therof, the auteris of encense,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and of brent sacrifice, and al the purtenaunce, that perteyneth to the ournyng of tho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 23:19; Exodus 29:37; Leviticus 6:18"> And thou schalt halewe alle thingis, and tho schulen be the hooli of holi thingis; he that schal touche tho, schal be halewid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 8:12; Exodus 28:3; Leviticus 8:30"> Thou schalt anoynte Aaron, and hise sones, and thou schalt halewe hem, that thei be set in presthod to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; Leviticus 21:10; Psalms 89:20"> And thou schalt seie to the sones of Israel, This oile of anoyntyng schal be hooli to me in to youre generaciouns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 30:25; Exodus 30:37–38; Leviticus 21:10"> The fleisch of man schal not be anoyntid therof, and bi the makyng therof ye schulen not make another, for it is halewid, and it schal be hooli to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 30:38; Genesis 17:14; Exodus 12:15"> What euer man makith sich oile, and yyueth therof to an alien, he schal be &#96;destried fro his puple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; Exodus 25:6; Exodus 30:23"> Forsothe the Lord seide to Moises, Take to thee swete smellynge spyceries, stacten,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="that is, the beste mirre.">e</a></sup>and onyca,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="that is, a litil oystre smellynge sweetely.">f</a></sup>galban of good odour, and pureste encense, alle schulen be of euene weiyte.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 2:13; Exodus 30:25; John 12:3"> And thou schal make encence, maad by werk of oynement makere, meddlid diligentli, and pure, and moost worthi of halewyng.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 25:22; Exodus 30:6; Exodus 16:34"> And whanne thou hast powned alle thingis in to smalleste poudre, thou schalt putte therof bifor the tabernacle of witnessyng, in which place Y schal appere to thee; encense schal be to you the hooli of hooli thingis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3; Exodus 29:37; Exodus 30:32–33"> Ye schulen not make siche a makyng in to youre vsis, for it is hooli to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 30:33"> What euer man makith a lijk thing, that he vse the odour therof, he schal perische fro his puple.</span>
