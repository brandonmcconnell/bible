<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 12:10; Genesis 25:11; Genesis 21:22–32"> Forsothe for hungur roos on the lond, aftir thilke bareynesse that bifelde in the daies of Abraham, Isaac yede forth to Abymelech, kyng of Palestyns, in Gerara.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 12:7; Psalms 37:3; Genesis 17:1"> And the Lord apperide to hym, and seide, Go not doun in to Egipt, but reste thou in the lond which Y schal seie to thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 28:15; Psalms 105:9; Genesis 17:8"> and be thou a pilgrym ther ynne; and Y schal be with thee, and Y schal blesse thee; for Y schal yyue alle these cuntrees to thee and to thi seed, and Y schal fille the ooth which Y bihiyte to Abraham, thi fadir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 3:25; Genesis 15:5; Genesis 22:17–18"> And Y schal multiplie thi seed as the sterris of heuene, and Y schal yyue alle these thingis to thin eyris, and alle folkis of erthe schulen be blessid in thi seed, for Abraham obeide to my vois,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 7:24; Genesis 22:18; Genesis 22:16"> and kepte &#96;my preceptis and comaundementis, and kepte cerymonyes and lawis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 20:1"> And so Ysaac dwellide in Geraris.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 29:25; Genesis 12:13; Genesis 20:2"> And whanne he was axid of men of that place of his wijf, he answarde, Sche is my sistir; for he dredde to knowleche that sche was felouschipid to hym in matrymonye, and gesside lest peraduenture thei wolden sle him for the fairnesse of hir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 62:5; Proverbs 5:18–19; Proverbs 7:6"> And whanne ful many daies weren passid, and he dwellide there, Abymelech, kyng of Palestyns, bihelde bi a wyndow, and seiy hym pleiynge with Rebecca, his wijf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And whanne Isaac was clepid, the kyng seide, It is opyn, that sche is thi wijf; whi liedist thou, that sche was thi sistir? Isaac answerde, Y dredde, lest Y schulde die for hir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 20:9–10; Genesis 12:18–19"> And Abymelech seide, Whi hast thou disseyued vs? Sum man of the puple myyte do letcherie with thi wijf, and thou haddist brouyt in greuous synne on vs. And the kyng comaundide to al the puple,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 105:15; Genesis 20:6; Proverbs 6:29"> and seide, He that touchith the wijf of this man schal die bi deeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 4:8; Genesis 24:1; Genesis 26:3"> Forsothe Isaac sowide in that lond, and he foond an hundrid fold in that yeer; and the Lord blesside hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 112:3; Proverbs 10:22; Genesis 24:35"> And the man was maad riche, and he yede profitynge and encreessynge til he was maad ful greet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 37:11; Genesis 13:2; Proverbs 27:4"> Also he hadde possessioun of scheep and grete beestis, and ful myche of meyne. For this thing Palestyns hadden enuye to hym,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 21:30; Genesis 21:25"> and thei stoppiden in that tyme and filliden with erthe alle the pittis whiche the seruauntis of Abraham his fadir hadden diggid,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 1:9"> in so myche that Abymelech him silf seide to Ysaac, Go thou awei fro vs, for thou art maad greetly myytier than we.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And he yede awei, that he schulde come to the stronde of Gerare, and dwelle there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 13:2; Hosea 2:17; Numbers 32:38"> And he diggide eft other pittis, whiche the seruauntis of Abraham his fadir hadden diggid, and whiche the Filisteis hadden stoppid sumtyme, whanne Abraham was deed; and he clepide tho pittis bi the same names, bi whiche his fadir hadde clepid bifore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 4:10–11; John 7:38; Song of Solomon 4:15"> Thei diggiden in the stronde, and thei founden wellynge watir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 21:25"> But also strijf of scheepherdis of Gerare was there ayens the scheepherdis of Isaac, and thei seiden, The watir is oure; wherfor of that that bifelde he clepide the name of the pit fals chaleng.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezra 4:6"> And thei diggiden anothir, and thei stryueden also for that, and Ysaac clepide that pit enemytes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 17:6; Exodus 1:7; Psalms 118:5"> And he yede forth fro thennus, and diggide another pit, for which thei stryueden not, therfor he clepid the name of that pit largenesse; and seide, Now God hath alargid vs, and hath maad to encreesse on erthe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 20:1; Genesis 21:31; Genesis 46:1"> Forsothe he stiede fro that place in to Bersabee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 15:1; Exodus 3:6; Isaiah 43:1–2"> where the Lord God apperide to him in that nyyt; and seide, Y am God of Abraham, thi fadir; nyle thou drede, for Y am with thee, and Y schal blesse thee, and Y schal multiplie thi seed for my seruaunt Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 13:18; Psalms 116:17; Genesis 12:7–8"> And so Ysaac bildide ther an auter to the Lord; and whanne the name of the Lord was inwardli clepid, he stretchide forth a tabernacle; and he comaundide hise seruauntis that thei schulden digge pittis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 21:22–32; Genesis 20:3"> And whanne Abymelech, and Ochosat, hise frendis, and Ficol, duk of knyytis, hadden come fro Geraris to that place,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 26:16; Judges 11:7; Acts 7:14"> Isaac spak to hem, What camen ye to me, a man whom ye hatiden, and puttiden awei fro you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 21:22–23; Isaiah 45:14; Isaiah 61:9"> Whiche answeriden, We seiyen that God is with thee, and therfor we seiden now, An ooth be bitwixe vs, and make we a couenaunt of pees,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 24:31; Genesis 21:22; Genesis 22:17"> that thou do not ony yuel to vs, as we touchiden &#96;not ony thing of thine, nethir diden that that hirtide thee, but with pees we leften thee encressid bi the blessyng of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 19:3; Romans 12:18; Hebrews 12:14"> Therfor Isaac made a feeste to hem; and after mete and drynk thei risen eerli,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:16–17; Hebrews 6:16; Genesis 25:33"> and sworen ech to other; and Isaac lefte hem peisibli in to her place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 2:4–5"> Lo! forsothe in that dai the seruauntis of Ysaac camen, tellynge to him of the pit which thei hadden diggid, and seiden, We han foundun watir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 21:31; Genesis 26:28"> Wherfor Ysaac clepide that pit abundaunce; and the name of the citee was set Bersabee til in to present dai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 36:2; Genesis 36:13; Exodus 34:16"> Esau forsothe fourti yeer eld weddide twei wyues, Judith, the douytir of Beeri Ethei, and Bethsamath, the douyter of Elon, of the same place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 27:46–2; Genesis 28:8; Genesis 6:2"> whiche bothe offendiden the soule of Isaac and of Rebecca.</span>
