<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 18:15; Acts 10:41; Genesis 43:30–31"> Joseph myyte no lengere absteyne hym silf, while many men stoden bifore; wherfor he comaundide that alle men schulden go out, and that noon alien were present in the knowyng of Joseph and hise britheren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 46:29; Acts 20:37; Numbers 14:1"> And Joseph reiside the vois with wepyng, which Egipcians herden, and al the hows of Farao.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 7:13; Acts 9:5; Zechariah 12:10"> And he seide to hise britheren, Y am Joseph; lyueth my fadir yit? The brithren myyten not answere, and weren agast bi ful myche drede.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 37:28; Matthew 14:27; Genesis 50:18"> To whiche he seide mekeli, Neiye ye to me. And whanne thei hadden neiyed nyy, he seide, Y am Joseph youre brother, whom ye selden in to Egipt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 50:20; Acts 7:9–15; Genesis 45:7–8"> nyle ye drede, nether seme it to be hard to you, that ye seelden me in to these cuntreis; for God hath sent me bifore you in to Egipt for youre helthe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 47:23; Genesis 41:54; Genesis 41:56"> For it is twei yeer that hungur bigan &#96;to be in the lond, yit fyue yeer suen, in whiche me schal not mow ere, nether repe;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 44:4; Acts 7:35; Psalms 18:50"> and God bifor sente me, that ye be reserued on erthe, and moun haue metis to lyue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 15:16; Romans 9:16; John 19:11"> Y was sent hidur not bi youre counsel, but bi Goddis wille, which made me as the fadir of Farao, and the lord of al his hows, and prince in al the lond of Egipt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 45:19–20; Genesis 45:13"> Haste ye, and &#96;stie ye to my fadir, and ye schulen seie to hym, Thi sone Joseph sendith these thingis; God hath maad me lord of al the lond of Egipt; come doun to me, and tarie not, and dwelle in the lond of Gessen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 9:26; Exodus 8:22; Genesis 46:34–6"> and thou schalt be bisidis me, thou, and thi sones, and the sones of thi sones, thi scheep, and thi grete beestis, and alle thingis whiche thou weldist,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 47:12; Genesis 47:6; Matthew 15:5–6"> and there Y schal fede thee; for yit fyue yeer of hungur ben residue, lest bothe thou perische, and thin hows, and alle thingis whiche thou weldist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 42:23; Luke 24:39; John 20:27"> Lo! youre iyen, and the iyen of my brother Beniamyn seen, that my mouth spekith to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 7:14; Revelation 21:23; 1 Peter 1:10–12"> telle ye to my fadir al my glorie, and alle thingis whiche ye sien in Egipt; haste ye, and brynge ye hym to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 33:4; Genesis 29:11; Genesis 46:29"> And whanne he hadde biclippid, and hadde feld in to the necke of Beniamyn, his brother, he wepte, the while also Benjamin wepte in lijk maner on the necke of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 15:20; 1 Samuel 10:1; Psalms 77:4"> And Joseph kisside alle hise britheren, and wepte on alle; aftir whiche thingis thei weren hardi to speke to hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 41:33; Esther 1:21; Genesis 20:15"> And it was herd, and pupplischid bi famouse word in the halle of the kyng, The britheren of Joseph ben comun. And Farao ioiede, and al his meynee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 42:25–26; Genesis 44:1–2"> and Farao seide to Joseph, that he schulde comaunde hise britheren, and &#96;seie, Charge youre beestis, and go ye in to the lond of Canaan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 27:28; Numbers 18:12; Genesis 47:6"> and take ye fro thennus youre fadir, and kynrede, and come ye to me; and Y schal yyue to you alle the goodis of Egipt, that ye ete the merow of the lond.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 46:5; Genesis 45:27; Genesis 31:17–18"> Comaunde thou also, that thei take waynes of the lond of Egipt to the cariage of her litle children, and wyues, and seie thou, &#96;Take ye youre fadir, and haste ye comynge soone,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 1:19; Luke 17:31; Deuteronomy 7:16"> nether leeue ye ony thing of the purtenaunce of youre hows, for alle the richessis of Egipt schulen be youre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 45:19; Ezekiel 23:24; Lamentations 1:18"> The sones of Israel diden, as it was comaundid to hem; to whiche Joseph yaf waynes, bi the comaundement of Farao, and metis in the weie;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 43:34; 2 Kings 5:5; Judges 14:12"> and he comaundide twei stoolis to be brouyt forth to ech; forsothe he yaf to Beniamyn thre hundrid platis of siluer, with fyue the beste stoolis;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 24:10; Exodus 16:3; Genesis 43:11"> and sente to his fadir so myche of siluer, and of cloothis, and he addide to hem ten male assis, that schulden bere of alle richessis of Egipt, and so many femal assis, berynge wheete and looues in the weie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:13; Psalms 133:1–3; Genesis 42:21–22"> Therfor he lefte hise britheren, and seide to hem goynge forth, Be ye not wrooth in the weie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Whiche stieden fro Egipt, and camen in to the lond of Canaan, to her fadir Jacob;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 44:28; Genesis 37:35; Luke 24:34"> and telden to hym, and seiden, Joseph, thi sone, lyueth, and he is lord in al the lond of Egipt. And whanne this was herd, Jacob wakide as of a greuouse sleep; netheles he bileuyde not to hem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 85:6; Isaiah 57:15; Genesis 45:19"> Thei telden ayenward al the ordre of the thing; and whanne Jacob hadde seyn the waynes, and alle thingis whiche Joseph hadde sent, his spirit lyuede ayen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 2:28–30; John 16:21–22; Genesis 46:30"> and he seide, It suffisith to me, if Joseph my sone lyueth yit, Y schal go and &#96;Y schal se hym bifore that Y die.</span>
