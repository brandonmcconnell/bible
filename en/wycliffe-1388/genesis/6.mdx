<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 82:6–7; 1 John 2:16; 2 Peter 2:14"> the sones of God seiyen the douytris of men that thei weren faire, and token wyues to hem of alle whiche thei hadden chose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:30; Isaiah 63:10; Jeremiah 11:11"> And God seide, My spirit schal not dwelle in man with outen ende, for he is fleisch; and the daies of hym schulen be an hundrid and twenti yeer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 13:33; 2 Samuel 21:15–22; 1 Samuel 17:4"> Sotheli giauntis weren on erthe in tho daies, forsothe aftir that the sones of God entriden to the douytris of men, and tho douytris gendriden; these weren myyti of the world and famouse men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 15:19; Romans 1:28–31; Mark 7:21–23"> Sotheli God seiy that myche malice of men was in erthe, and that al the thouyt of herte was ententif to yuel in al tyme,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 63:10; 1 Samuel 15:11; Ephesians 4:30"> and repentide him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="repentide hym. Penaunce, sorowe, and othere passyouns, ethir disesis, ben not in ony maner in God by truthe, but by liknesse; and therfore it is seid that he repentide, for he hadde hym silf at the maner of a man repentinge, and wolde distrye that that he made bifore.">a</a></sup>that he hadde maad man in erthe; and God was war bifore ayens tyme to comyng, and was touchid with sorewe of herte<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="sorowe of herte. For he hadde him silf at the maner of a man sorowinge, in doynge awey man, whom he hadde formed to his ymage. Penaunce, either sorewe of herte, fallith not in God, to whom the sentence of alle thingis is as stidefast as the biforeknowynge is certeyn, sith hooly scripture vsith comoun wordis,">b</a></sup>with ynne;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:3; Psalms 37:20; Jeremiah 12:3–4"> and seide, Y schal do awei man, whom Y made of nouyt, fro the face of the erthe, fro man til to lyuynge thingis, fro crepynge beeste til to the briddis of heuene; for it repentith me that Y made hem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 19:19; Hebrews 4:16; Luke 1:30"> Forsothe Noe foond grace bifore the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 1:6; Hebrews 11:7; 2 Peter 2:5"> These ben the generaciouns of Noe. Noe was a iust man and perfit in hise generaciouns; Noe yede with God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 5:32"> and gendride thre sones, Sem, Cam, and Jafeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:17; Hosea 4:1–2; Habakkuk 2:8"> Forsothe the erthe was corrupt bifore God, and was fillid with wickidnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 53:2–3; Proverbs 15:3; Psalms 14:1–3"> And whanne God seiy, that the erthe was corrupt, for ech fleisch ether man hadde corrupt his weie on erthe,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 4:1–2; 1 Peter 4:7; Ezekiel 7:2–6"> he seide to Noe, The ende of al fleisch is comen bifore me; the erthe is fillid with wickidnesse of the face of hem, and Y schal distrye hem with the erthe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 3:20; Luke 17:27; Matthew 24:38"> Make thou to thee a schip of trees hewun and planed; thou schalt make dwellynge placis in the schip, and thou schalt anoynte it with pitche with ynne and with outforth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:11; Genesis 7:20"> And so thou schalt make it. The lengthe of the schip schal be of thre hundrid cubitis, the brede schal be of fifti cubitis, and the hiynesse therof schal be of thretti cubitis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 8:6; Genesis 7:16; Ezekiel 41:16"> Thou schalt make a wyndow in the schip, and thou schalt ende the hiynesse therof in a cubite; sotheli thou schalt sette the dore of the schip in the side binethe; thou shalt make soleris and placis of thre chaumbris in the schip.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; 2 Peter 2:5; Genesis 7:21–23"> Lo! Y schal brynge &#96;watris of diluuye ether greet flood on erthe, and Y schal sle ech fleisch in which is the spirit of lijf vndir heuene, and alle thingis that ben in erthe, schulen be wastid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 11:7; Genesis 7:7; Genesis 17:7"> And Y schal sette my couenaunt of pees with thee; and thou schalt entre in to the schip, and thy sones, and thi wijf, and the wiues of thi sones schulen entre with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 7:15–16; Genesis 7:8–9; Genesis 8:17"> And of alle lyuynge beestis of al fleisch thou schalt brynge in to the schip tweyne and tweyne, of male kynde and female, that thei lyue with thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 10:11–12; Genesis 2:19; Genesis 7:8–16"> of briddis bi her kynde, and of werk beestis in her kynde, and of ech crepynge beeste of erthe, by her kynde; tweyne and tweyne of alle schulen entre with thee, that thei moun lyue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 1:29–30; Psalms 145:16; Psalms 136:25"> Therfore thou schalt take with thee of alle metis that moun be etun, and thou schalt bere to gidre at thee, and tho schulen be as well to thee as to the beestis in to mete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 40:16; John 15:14; Matthew 7:24–27"> Therfor Noe dide alle thingis whiche God comaundide to hym.</span>
