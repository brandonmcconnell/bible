<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; Psalms 26:9; Psalms 16:9"> my soule come not in to the councel of hem, and my glorie be not in the congregacioun of hem; for in her woodnesse thei killiden a man, and in her wille thei myneden the wal;</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 63:1–3; Joel 3:18; Revelation 7:14"> and he schal tye his colt at the vyner, and his femal asse at the vyne; A! my sone, he schal waische his stoole in wyn, and his mentil in the blood of grape;</span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 81:6; Matthew 23:4; Joshua 14:15"> liggynge bitwixe termes, seiy reste, that it was good and seiy the lond that it was best, and he vndirsettide his schuldre to bere, and he was maad seruynge to tributis.</span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 119:174; Psalms 119:166; Micah 7:7"> Y schal abide thin helthe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 30:11; 1 Chronicles 5:11–22; Deuteronomy 33:20–21"> Gad schal be gird, and schal fiyte bifor hym, and he schal be gird bihynde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:24–25; Genesis 30:13; Genesis 46:17"> Aser his breed schal be plenteuouse, and he schal yyue delicis to kyngis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:23; Genesis 30:8; Joshua 19:32–39"> Neptalym schal be an hert sent out, and yyuynge spechis of fairenesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 41:52; Psalms 128:3; Psalms 1:1–3"> Joseph, a sone encreessynge, &#96;a sone encresinge, and fair in biholdyng; douytris runnen aboute on the wal,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 37:24; Psalms 64:3; Psalms 118:13"> but hise brithren wraththeden hym, and chidden, and thei hadden dartis, and hadden enuye to hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 132:2; Isaiah 28:16; Psalms 132:5"> His bowe sat in the stronge, and the boondis of his armes, and hondis weren vnboundun bi the hond of the myyti of Jacob; of hym a scheepherd yede out, the stoon of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 28:13; Genesis 35:11; Philippians 4:19"> God of thi fadir schal be thin helpere, and Almyyti God schal blesse thee with blessyngis of heuene fro aboue, and with blessyngis of the see liggynge binethe, with blessyngis of tetis, and of wombe;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:15–16; Habakkuk 3:6; Isaiah 66:5"> the blessyngis of thi fadir ben coumfortid, the blessyngis of his fadris, til the desire of euerlastynge hillis cam; blessyngis ben maad in the heed of Joseph, and in the nol of Nazarei among his britheren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 20:25; Genesis 35:18; Ezekiel 22:27"> Beniamyn, a rauyschynge wolf, schal ete prey eerly, and in the euentid he schal departe spuylis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 7:4; Esther 8:11; 1 Kings 18:31"> Alle these weren in twelue kynredis of Israel; her fadir spak these thingys to hem, and blesside hem alle by propre blessyngis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 47:30; Genesis 50:13; Genesis 15:15"> and comaundide hem, and seide, Y am gaderid to my puple, birie ye me with my fadris in the double denne, which is in the lond of Efron Ethei, ayens Manbre,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 23:20; Genesis 23:16–18; Genesis 23:8–9"> in the lond of Canaan, which denne Abraham bouyte with the feeld of Efron Ethei, in to possessioun of sepulcre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 25:9; Genesis 35:29; Genesis 50:13"> There thei birieden hym, and Sare his wijf, also Ysaac was biried there with Rebecca his wijf; there also Lia liggith biried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 23:17–20"> And whanne the comaundementis weren endid, bi whiche he tauyte the sones, he gaderide hise feet on the bed, and diede, and he was put to his puple.</span>
