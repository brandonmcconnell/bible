<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 41:50–52; Genesis 46:20; Psalms 128:6"> And so whanne these thingis weren don, it was teld to Joseph, that his fadir was sijk. And he took hise twei sones, Manasses and Effraym, and he disposide to go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 6:10; 1 Samuel 23:16; Deuteronomy 3:28"> And it was seid to the elde man, Lo! thi sone Joseph cometh to thee; which was coumfortid, and sat in the bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 35:9–12; Exodus 6:3; Genesis 28:3"> And whanne Joseph entride to hym, he seide, Almyyti God apperide to me in Luza, which is in the lond of Canaan, and blesside me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Genesis 35:11; Genesis 26:4"> and seide, Y schal encreesse and multiplie thee, and Y schal make thee in to cumpanyes of puplis, and Y schal yyue to thee this lond, and to thi seed aftir thee, in to euerlastinge possessioun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 41:50–52; Joshua 14:4; Genesis 46:20"> Therfor thi twei sones, that ben borun to thee in the lond of Egipt bifore that Y cam hidir to thee, schulen be myne, Effraym and Manasses as Ruben and Symeon schulen be arettid to me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 14:4"> forsothe the othere whiche thou schalt gendre aftir hem schulen be thine; and thei schulen be clepid bi the name of her britheren in her possessiouns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 35:16–19; Matthew 2:18; Genesis 35:9"> Forsothe whanne Y cam fro Mesopotamye, Rachel was deed to me in the lond of Canaan, in thilke weie; and it was the bigynnyng of somer; and Y entride in to Effrata, and beriede hir bisidis the weie of Effrata, which bi anothir name is clepid Bethleem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Forsothe Jacob seiy the sones of Joseph, and seide to him, Who ben these?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 27:4; Genesis 33:5; Hebrews 11:21"> He answeride, Thei ben my sones, whiche God yaf to me in this place. Jacob seide, Brynge hem to me that Y blesse hem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 27:1; Genesis 27:27; 1 Samuel 3:2"> For &#96;the iyen of Israel, dasewiden for greet eelde, and he myyte not se clereli; and he kisside and collide tho children ioyned to hym, and seide to his sone,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 3:20; Genesis 45:26; Genesis 42:36"> Y am not defraudid of thi siyt; ferthermore God schewide to me thi seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 42:6; Leviticus 19:32; Genesis 19:1"> And whanne Joseph hadde take hem fro &#96;the fadris lappe, he worschipide lowe to erthe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And he sette Effraym on his riyt side, that is, on the lift side of Israel; forsothe he settide Manasses in his lift side, that is, on the riyt side of the fadir; and he ioynede bothe to hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 41:51; Psalms 118:16; Matthew 19:15"> Which helde forth the riyt hond, and settide on &#96;the heed of Effraym, the lesse brothir; sotheli he settide the left hond on &#96;the heed of Manasses, that was the more thury birthe. Jacob chaungide &#96;the hondes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 17:1; Colossians 2:6; Isaiah 33:16"> and blesside his sone Joseph, and seide, God, in whos siyt my fadris Abraham and Isaac yeden; God, that fedith me fro my yong wexynge age til in to present day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 34:22; Genesis 49:22; Genesis 48:5"> the aungel that delyuerede me fro alle yuelis, blesse thes children, and my name be clepid on hem, and the names of my fadris Abraham and Ysaac; and wexe thei in multitude on erthe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 48:14; Romans 9:7–8; 1 Chronicles 21:7"> Forsothe Joseph seiy that his fadir hadde set the riyt hond on the heed of Effraym, and took heuyli, and he enforside to reise the fadris hond takun fro the heed of Effraym, and to bere &#96;ouer on &#96;the heed of Manasses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 19:18; Genesis 29:26; Genesis 43:33"> And he seide to the fadir, Fadir, it acordith not so; for this is the firste gendrid; sette thi riyt hond on the heed &#96;of hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:17; Numbers 1:33–35; Genesis 25:28"> Which forsook and seide, Y woot, my sone, Y woot; and sotheli this child schal be in to puplis, and he schal be multiplied; but his yonger brother schal be more than he, and &#96;his seed schal encreesse in to folkis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 24:60; Ruth 4:11–12; Genesis 28:3"> And he blesside hem in that tyme, and seide, Israel schal be blessid in thee, Joseph, and it schal be seid, God do to thee as to Effraym and as to Manasses. And he settide Effraym bifore Manasses;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 50:24; Genesis 46:4; Genesis 28:15"> and seide to Joseph, his sone, Lo! Y die, and God schal be with you, and schal lede you ayen to the lond of youre fadris;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 4:5; Joshua 24:32; 1 Chronicles 5:2"> Y yyue to thee o part ouer thi britheren which Y took fro the hand of Amorei, in my swerd and bowe.</span>
