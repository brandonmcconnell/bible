<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 2:14; Isaiah 40:2; 2 Chronicles 30:22"> And his soule was boundun faste with hir, and he pleside hir sory with flateringis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 14:2; 2 Samuel 13:13; Genesis 21:21"> And he yede to Emor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:22; Leviticus 10:3; 1 Samuel 10:27"> his fadir, and seide, Take to me this damysel a wijf. And whanne Jacob hadde herd this thing, while the sones weren absent, and ocupied in the fedyng of scheep, he was stille, til thei camen ayen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Sotheli whanne Emor, the fadir of Sichem, was gon out, &#96;that he schulde speke to Jacob, lo!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 7:15; Judges 20:6; Deuteronomy 22:21"> hise sones camen fro the feeld. And whanne this thing that bifelde was herd, thei weren wroothe greetli; for he wrouyte a foul thing in Israel, and he hadde do a thing vnleueful in the defoulyng of the douyter of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 11:2; Psalms 119:20; Psalms 84:2"> And so Emor spak to hem, The soule of my sone Sichem cleuyde to youre douytir, yeue ye hir a wijf to hym,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:3; Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 19:14"> and ioyne we weddyngis to gidere; yyue ye youre douytris to vs,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 20:15; Genesis 47:27; Genesis 42:34"> and take ye oure douytris, and dwelle ye with vs; the lond is in youre power, tile ye, make ye marchaundise, and welde ye it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 33:15; Genesis 18:3"> But also Sichem seide to the fadir and britheren of hir, Fynde Y grace bifor you, and what euer thingis ye ordeynen Y schal yyue;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 22:16–17; Deuteronomy 22:28–29; 1 Samuel 18:25–27"> encreesse ye the dower, and axe ye yiftis, Y schal yyue wilfull that that ye axen; oonli yyue ye this damysele a wijf to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 25:27–34; Isaiah 59:13; Job 13:4"> The sones of Jacob answeriden in gile to Sichem and his fadir, and weren feerse for the defoulyng of maidenhod of the sistir,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 23:1–39; 2 Samuel 15:7; Genesis 17:11"> We moun not do this that ye axen, nether we moun yyue oure sistir to a man vncircumcidid, which thing is vnleueful and abhomynable anentis vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 4:12"> But in this we schulen mowe be boundun in pees, if ye wole be lijk vs, and ech of male kynde be circumcidid in you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> thanne we schulen yyue and take togidre oure douytris and youre; and we schulen dwelle with you, and we schulen be o puple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Forsothe if ye nylen be circumcidid, we schulen take oure douytir, and schulen go a wei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The profryng of hem pleside Emor and Sichem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 17:12; Acts 13:50; 1 Samuel 22:14"> his sone, and the yong wexynge man dilaiede not, that ne he fillide anoon that that was axid, for he louede the damysele greetli, and he was noble in al &#96;the hous of his fadir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ruth 4:1; Proverbs 31:23; Amos 5:12"> And thei entriden in to the yate of the citee, and spaken to the puple,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> These men ben pesible, and wolen dwelle with vs; make thei marchaundie in the loond, and tile thei it, which is large and brood, and hath nede to tileris; we schulen take her douytris to wyues, and we schulen yyue oure douytris to hem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> O thing is, for which so greet good is dilaied; if we circumciden oure malis, and suen the custom of the folc,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 1:12–13; John 6:26–27; John 2:16"> bothe her substaunce, and scheep, and alle thingis which thei welden, schulen be oure; oneli assente we in this, that we dwelle to gidere, and make o puple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 23:10; 1 Corinthians 7:19; Isaiah 1:10–16"> And alle men assentiden, and alle malis weren circumcidid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 49:5–7; Genesis 29:33–34; Numbers 31:7"> And lo! in the thridde day, whanne the sorewe of woundis was moost greuous, twei sones of acob, Symeon and Leuy, britheren of Dyna, token swerdis, and entriden in to the citee booldeli; and whanne alle malis weren slayn,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:26; Deuteronomy 32:42; Isaiah 31:8"> thei killiden Emor and Sichem togidere, and token Dyna, her sistir, fro the hous of Sichem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 7:13; Joshua 7:21; Exodus 2:14"> And whanne thei weren goon out, othere sones of Jacob felden in on the slayn men, and rifeliden the citee for the veniaunce of defoulyng of a virgyn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 1:15–16; Deuteronomy 8:17–18; Job 20:5"> And thei wastiden the scheep of tho men, and droues of oxun, and assis, and alle thingis that weren in howsis and feeldis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> and ledden prisoneris the litle children, and wyues of tho men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 105:12; Joshua 7:25; Exodus 5:21"> And whanne these thingis weren don hardili, Jacob seide to Symeon and Leuy, Ye han troblid me, and han maad me hateful to Cananeis and Fereseis, dwellers of this lond; we ben fewe, thei schulen be gaderid to gidere and schulen sle me, and Y schal be don a wey and myn hous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 6:34; Genesis 34:13; Genesis 49:7"> Symeon and Leuy answeriden, Whether thei ouyten mysuse oure sistir as an hoore?</span>
