<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 5:2; Genesis 29:31; Genesis 37:11"> Forsothe Rachel seiy, that sche was vnfruytful, and hadde enuye<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="enuye, that is, sorewide of hir owne defaute in comparisoun of hir sister, that hadde no sich defaute.">a</a></sup>to the sister, and seide to hir hosebonde, Yyue thou fre children to me, ellis Y schal die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 16:2; Psalms 127:3; 2 Kings 5:7"> To whom Jacob was wrooth, and answerde, Wher Y am for God, which haue priued thee fro the fruyt of thi wombe?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 50:23; Job 3:12; Ruth 4:11"> And sche seide, Y haue &#96;a seruauntesse Bala, entre thou to hir that she childe on my knees, and that Y haue sones of hir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 35:22; Genesis 21:10; 2 Samuel 12:11"> And sche yaf to hym Bala in to matrimony;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> and whanne the hosebonde hadde entrid to hir, sche conseyuede, and childide a sone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Lamentations 3:59; Psalms 35:24; Psalms 43:1"> And Rachel seide, the Lord demede to me, and herde my preier, and yaf a sone to me; and therfor sche clepide his name Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And eft Bala conseyuede, and childide anothir sone,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 32:24–25; Genesis 23:6; Genesis 49:21"> for whom Rachel seide, The Lord hath maad me lijk to my sistir, and Y wexide strong; and sche clepide hym Neptalym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 30:4; Genesis 30:17; Genesis 29:35"> Lya feelide that sche ceesside to bere child, and sche yaf Selfa, hir handmayde, to the hosebonde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And whanne Selfa aftir conseyuyng childide a sone, Lya seide, Blessidly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 49:19; Isaiah 65:11; Genesis 35:26"> and therfor sche clepide his name Gad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Also Selfa childide anothir sone,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 1:48; Proverbs 31:28; Song of Solomon 6:9"> and Lia seide, This is for my blis, for alle wymmen schulen seie me blessid; therfor sche clepide hym Aser.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Song of Solomon 7:13; Genesis 25:30"> Forsothe Ruben yede out in to the feeld in the tyme of wheete heruest, and foond mandragis, whiche he brouyte to Lya, his modir. And Rachel seide, Yyue thou to me a part of the mandragis of thi sone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 16:13; 1 Corinthians 4:3; Numbers 16:9–10"> Lya answeride, Whether it semeth litil to thee, that thou hast rauyschid the hosebonde fro me, no but thou take also the mandragis of my sone? Rachel seide, The hosebonde sleepe with thee in this nyyt for the mandragis of thi sone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And whanne Jacob cam ayen fro the feeld at euentid, Lya yede out in to his comyng, and seide, Thou shalt entre to me, for Y haue hired thee with hire for the mandragis of my sone. He slepte with hir in that nyyt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 30:6; Genesis 30:22; Exodus 3:7"> and God herde hir preiers, and sche conseyuede, and childide the fyuethe sone;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 49:14–15; Deuteronomy 33:18; Genesis 46:13"> and seide, God yaf meede to me, for Y yaf myn handmayde to myn hosebond; and sche clepide his name Isacar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Eft Lia conseyuede, and childide the sixte sone,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 35:23; Matthew 4:13; Genesis 49:13"> and seide, The Lord hath maad me riche with a good dower, also in this tyme myn hosebonde schal be with me, for Y childide sixe sones to hym; and therfore sche clepide his name Sabulon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 34:1–3; Genesis 34:26; Genesis 46:15"> Aftir whom sche childide a douyter, Dyna bi name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 29:31; Psalms 113:9; Psalms 127:3"> Also the Lord hadde mynde on Rachel, and herde hir, and openyde hir wombe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 1:25; Genesis 29:31; Luke 1:27"> And sche conseyuede, and childide a sone, and seide, God hath take a wey my schenschipe; and sche clepid his name Joseph,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 49:22–26; Genesis 35:24; Genesis 37:2"> and seide, The Lord yyue to me another sone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 24:54; Genesis 24:56; Genesis 28:15"> Sotheli whanne Joseph was borun, Jacob seide to his wyues fadir, Delyuere thou me, that Y turne ayen in to my cuntrey and to my lond.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 29:30; Hosea 12:12; Genesis 31:26"> Yyue thou to me my wyues and fre children for whiche Y seruede thee, that Y go; forsothe thou knowist the seruyce bi which Y seruede thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ruth 2:13; Acts 7:10; Isaiah 61:9"> Laban seide to hym, Fynde Y grace in thi siyt, Y haue lerned bi experience that God blesside me for thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 29:15; Genesis 29:19"> ordeyne thou the meede which Y schal yyue to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 31:6; Genesis 31:38–40; 1 Peter 2:18"> And he answeride, Thou woost hou Y seruede thee, and hou greet thi possessioun was in myn hondis;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:8; 2 Corinthians 12:14; Deuteronomy 11:10"> thou haddist litil bifore that Y cam to thee, and now thou art maad riche, and the Lord blesside thee at myn entryng; therfor it is iust that Y purueye sum tyme also to myn hows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:4–6; Hebrews 13:5; Psalms 118:8"> And Laban seide, What schal Y yyue to thee? And Jacob seide, Y wole no thing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="no thyng, that is, of thi yifte.">b</a></sup>but if thou doist that that Y axe, eft Y schal fede and kepe thi scheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 31:8; Genesis 31:10; Genesis 30:35"> Cumpasse thou alle thi flockis, and departe thou alle diuerse scheep and of spottid flees, and what euer thing schal be dun, and spottid, and dyuerse, as wel in scheep as in geet, it schal be my mede.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 13:14; 2 Samuel 22:21; 1 Samuel 26:23"> And my riytfulnesse schal answere to me to morewe, whanne the tyme of couenaunt schal come bifor thee; and alle that ben not dyuerse and spottid and dunne, as well in sheep as in geet, schulen repreue me of thefte.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:7; Numbers 22:29; 1 Corinthians 14:5"> And Laban seide, Y haue acceptable that that thou axist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 31:9"> And he departide in that dai the geet, and scheep, geet buckis, and rammes, dyuerse and spottid. Sothely he bitook al the flok of o coloure, that is, of white and of blak flees in the hond of hise sones;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> and he settide the space of weie of thre daies bitwixe hise sones and the hosebonde of hise douytris, that fedde othere flockis' of hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Genesis 31:9–13; Ezekiel 31:8"> Therfor Jacob took greene yerdis of popeleris, and of almoundis, and of planes, and in parti dide awei the rynde of tho, and whanne the ryndis weren &#96;drawun a wei, whitnesse apperide in these that weren maad bare; sothely tho that weren hoole dwelliden grene, and bi this maner the coloure was maad dyuerse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And Jacob puttide tho yerdis in the trowis, where the watir was held out, that whanne the flockis schulden come to drynke, thei schulden haue the yerdis bifor the iyen, and schulden conseyue in the siyt of the yerdis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 12:35–36; Genesis 31:9–12; Genesis 31:40"> And it was doon that in thilke heete of riding the sheep schulde biholde the yerdis, and that thei schulden brynge forth spotti beestis, and dyuerse, and bispreynt with dyuerse colour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And Jacob departide<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="the floc, that is, departide the rammes fro the ewes til to the tyme of wateryng.">c</a></sup>the floc, and puttide the yerdis in the trowis bifor the iyen of the rammys. Sotheli alle the white and blake weren Labans; sotheli the othere weren Jacobis; for the flockis weren departid bytwixe hem silf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> Therfor whanne the scheep weren ridun in the firste tyme, Jacob puttide the yerdis in the &#96;trouyis of watir bifor the iyen of rammys and of scheep, that thei schulden conseyue in the siyt of tho yerdis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Forsothe whanne the late medlyng and the laste conseyuyng weren, Jacob puttide not tho yerdis; and tho that weren late, weren maad Labans, and tho that weren of the firste tyme weren Jacobis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Genesis 24:35; Genesis 30:30; Genesis 26:13–14"> And he was maad ful riche, and hadde many flockis, handmaydis, and seruauntis, camels, and assis.</span>
