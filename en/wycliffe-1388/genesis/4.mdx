<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 3:12; Genesis 3:15; Genesis 4:25"> Forsothe Adam knewe Eue his wijf, which conseyuede, and childide Cayn, and seide, Y haue gete a man bi God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 3:15; 1 John 3:12; Luke 11:51"> And efte sche childide his brother Abel. Forsothe Abel was a kepere of scheep, and Cayn was an erthe tilyere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 18:12; Leviticus 2:1–11; 1 Kings 17:7"> Sotheli it was don after many daies, that Cayn offride yiftis to the Lord of the fruytis of erthe;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 11:4; Proverbs 3:9; Exodus 13:12"> and Abel offride of the first gendrid of his floc, and of the fatnesse of tho. And the Lord bihelde to Abel and to the yiftis of hym;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:4; Job 5:2; Luke 15:28–30"> sotheli he bihelde not to Cayn and to hise yiftis. And Cayn was wrooth greetli, and his cheer felde doun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 1:18; Job 5:2; Luke 15:31–32"> And the Lord seide to hym, Whi art thou wrooth, and whi felde doun thi face?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:15; Romans 2:6–10; Isaiah 3:10–11"> Whether not if thou schalt do wel, thou schalt resseyue; but if thou doist yuele, thi synne schal be present anoon in the yatis? but the desir therof schal be vndur thee, and thou schalt be lord therof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 23:35; 1 John 3:12–15; Jude 1:11"> And Cayn seide to Abel his brother, Go we out. And whanne thei weren in the feeld, Cayn roos ayens his brother Abel, and killide him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; John 8:44; Psalms 9:12"> And the Lord seide to Cayn, Where is Abel thi brother? Which answerde, Y woot not; whether Y am the kepere of my brothir?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 12:24; Psalms 72:14; Hebrews 11:4"> And God seide to Cayn, What hast thou do? the vois of the blood of thi brother crieth to me fro erthe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Job 16:18; Genesis 3:14"> Now therfor thou schalt be cursid on erthe, that openyde his mouth, and resseyuede of thin hond the blood of thi brothir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 9:17; Leviticus 26:36; Leviticus 26:20"> Whanne thou schalt worche the erthe, it schal not yyue his fruytis to thee; thou schalt be vnstable of dwellyng and fleynge aboute on erthe in alle the daies of thi lijf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 15:22; Revelation 16:9; Revelation 16:11"> And Cayn seide to the Lord, My wickidnesse is more than that Y disserue foryyuenesse; lo!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 35:19; Psalms 143:7; Job 15:20–24"> to dai thou castist me out fro the face of the erthe; and Y schal be hid fro thi face, and Y schal be vnstable of dwellyng and fleynge aboute in erthe; therfore ech man that schal fynde me schal slee me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:4; Psalms 79:12; Genesis 4:24"> And the Lord seide to hym, It schal not be don so, but ech man that schal slee Cayn shal be punyschid seuenfold. And the Lord settide a signe in Cayn, that ech man that schulde fynde hym schulde not slee hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:39; Job 2:7; Genesis 4:14"> And Cayn yede out fro the face of the Lord, and dwellide fleynge aboute in erthe, at the eest coost of Eden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 49:11; Ecclesiastes 2:4–11; Daniel 4:30"> Forsothe Cayn knewe his wiif, which conseyuede, and childide Enoth; and Cayn bildide a citee, and clepide the name therof of the name of hise sone Enoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 36:2; Genesis 5:21"> Forsothe Enoth gendride Irad, and Irad gendride Manyael, and Manyael gendride Matusael, and Matusael gendride Lameth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 2:24; Matthew 19:8; Genesis 2:18"> that took twei wyues, the name to o wijf was Ada, and the name to the tother was Sella.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 4:2; Jeremiah 35:9–10; John 8:44"> And Ada gendride Jabel, that was the fadir of dwellers in tentis and of shepherdis;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 21:12; Isaiah 5:12; Genesis 31:27"> and the name of his brother was Tubal, he was the fadir of syngeris in harpe and orgun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:25; Deuteronomy 8:9; 2 Chronicles 2:7"> And Sella gendride Tubalcayn, that was an hamerbetere, and smyyt on alle werkis of bras and of yrun; forsothe the sistir of Tubalcayn was Neoma.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 23:18; Exodus 20:13; Leviticus 19:18"> And Lameth seide to his wyues Ada and Sella, Ye wyues of Lameth, here my vois, and herkne my word; for Y haue slayn a man bi my wounde, and a yong wexynge man bi my &#96;violent betyng;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 18:22; Genesis 4:15"> veniaunce schal be youun seuenfold of Cayn, forsothe of Lameth seuentisithis seuensithis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 4:8; Genesis 4:10–11; 1 Chronicles 1:1"> Also yit Adam knewe his wijf, and sche childide a sone, and clepide his name Seth, and seide, God hath put to me another seed for Abel, whom Cayn killide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 116:17; 1 Kings 18:24; Joel 2:32"> But also a sone was borun to Seth, which sone he clepide Enos; this Enos bigan to clepe inwardli the name of the Lord.</span>
