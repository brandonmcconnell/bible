<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 19:22; Genesis 13:10; Genesis 19:20"> And the kyng of Sodom, and the king of Gomorre, and the kyng of Adama, and the kyng of Seboym, also and the kyng of Bale, which is Segor, yeden out, and dressiden scheltrun ayens hem in the valei of wode,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> that is, ayens Chodorlaomor, kyng of Elamytis, and Thadal, kyng of folkis, and Amrafel, kyng of Sennaar, and Ariok, kyng of Ponte; foure kyngis ayens fyue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 19:17; Genesis 19:30; Isaiah 24:18"> Forsothe the valey of the wode hadde many pittis of pitche; and so the kyng of Sodom and the kyng of Gomorre turneden the backis, and felden doun there; and thei that leften fledden to the hil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 14:21; Genesis 14:16; Deuteronomy 28:31"> Sotheli thei token awei al the catel of Sodom and Gomorre, and alle thingis that perteynen to mete, and yeden awei;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 12:5; Genesis 11:27; Revelation 3:19"> also and thei token awey Loth and his catel, the sone of the brother of Abram, which Loth dwellide in Sodom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 13:18; Genesis 14:24; Genesis 40:15"> And, lo! oon that ascapide, telde to Abram Ebrew, that dwellide in the valei of Mambre of Amorrei, brother of Escol, and brother of Aner; for these maden couenaunt of pees with Abram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 18:29; Genesis 15:3; Genesis 17:27"> And whanne Abram hadde herd this thing, that is, Loth his brothir takun, he noumbride his borun seruauntis maad redy thre hundrid and eiytene, and pursuede hem &#96;til to Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 9:2; Psalms 112:5; 1 Kings 15:18"> And whanne his felowis weren departid, he felde on hem in the niyt, and he smoot hem, and pursuede hem &#96;til to Hoba, and Fenyce, which is at the left side of Damask.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:18–19; 1 Samuel 30:8; Genesis 12:2"> And he brouyte ayen al the catel, and Loth his brother with his catel, also wymmen and the puple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:18; Proverbs 19:4; Hebrews 7:1"> Sotheli the kyng of Sodom yede out in to the metyng of him, after that he turnede ayen fro sleyng of Chodorlaomor, and of kyngis that weren with him, in the valei of Sabe, which is the valey of the kyng.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 6:20–3; Psalms 110:4; Hebrews 5:6"> And sotheli Melchisedech, kyng of Salem, brouyte forth breed and wyn, for he was the preest of hiyeste God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 7:6–7; Genesis 14:22; Psalms 24:1"> and he blesside Abram, and seide, Blessid be Abram of hiy God, that made heuene and erthe of nouyt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 28:22; Hebrews 7:4–9; Malachi 3:10"> and blessid be hiy God, bi whom defendynge, enemyes ben bitakun in thin hondis. And Abram yaf tithis of alle thingis to hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Forsothe the kyng of Sodom seide to Abram, Yyue thou the men to me; take thou othir thingis to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 12:7; Daniel 4:34; Deuteronomy 32:40"> And Abram answerde to hym, Y reyse myn hondis to the hiy Lord God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 5:16; 2 Corinthians 11:9–12; Esther 9:15–16"> Lord of heuene and of erthe, that fro the threde of oof til to the layner of the hose I schal not take of alle thingis that ben thine, lest thou seie, I made Abram riche;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 14:13; Proverbs 3:27; Matthew 7:12"> out takun these thingis whiche the yonge men eeten, and the partis of men that camen with me, Aner, Escol, and Mambre; these men schulen take her partis.</span>
