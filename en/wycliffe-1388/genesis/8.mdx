<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 7:11; Matthew 8:26–27; Proverbs 8:28"> And watris weren decreessid, and the wellis of the see weren closid, and the wyndowis of heuene weren closid, and reynes of heuene weren ceessid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 7:24; Genesis 7:11"> And watrys turneden ayen fro erthe, and yeden ayen, and bigunnen to be decreessid aftir an hundrid and fifti daies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 19:37; Jeremiah 51:27; Isaiah 37:38"> And the schip restide in the seuenthe monthe, in the seuene and twentithe dai of the monthe, on the hillis of Armenye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 7:11"> And sotheli the watrys yeden and decresiden til to the tenthe monethe, for in the tenthe monethe, in the firste dai of the monethe, the coppis of hillis apperiden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 6:16; Daniel 6:10"> And whanne fourti daies weren passid, Noe openyde the wyndow of the schip which he hadde maad, and sente out a crowe,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 147:9; 1 Kings 17:6; 1 Kings 17:4"> which yede out, and turnede not ayen til the watris weren dried on erthe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:14; Song of Solomon 1:15; Matthew 10:16"> Also Noe sente out a culuer aftir hym, to se if the watris hadden ceessid thanne on the face of erthe;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 11:28; Psalms 116:7; Deuteronomy 28:65"> and whanne the culuer foond not where hir foot schulde reste, sche turnede ayen to hym in to the schip, for the watris weren on al erthe; and Noe helde forth his hoond, and brouyte the culuer takun in to the schip.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; Genesis 7:10; Genesis 8:12"> Sotheli whanne othere seuene daies weren abedun aftirward, eft he leet out a culuer fro the schip;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:15; Zechariah 4:12–14; Romans 10:15"> and sche cam to hym at euentid, and bare in hir mouth a braunche of olyue tre with greene leeuys. Therfor Noe vndirstood that the watris hadden ceessid on erthe;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 25:9; Isaiah 30:18; Genesis 8:10"> and neuerthelesse he abood seuene othere daies, and sente out a culuer, which turnede &#96;no more ayen to hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 7:11"> Therfor in the sixe hundrid and o yeer of the lijf of Noe, in the firste monethe, in the firste day of the monethe, watris weren decreessid on erthe; and Noe openede the roof of the schip, and bihelde and seiy that the face of the erthe was dried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 7:13–14; Genesis 7:11"> In the secunde monethe, in the seuene and twentithe dai of the monethe, the erthe was maad drie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Sotheli the Lord spak to Noe;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 7:13; Psalms 121:8; Psalms 91:11"> and seide, Go out of the schip, thou, and thi wijf, thi sones, and the wyues of thi sones with thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 1:22; Genesis 9:1; Jeremiah 31:27–28"> and lede out with thee alle lyuynge beestis that ben at thee of ech fleisch, as wel in volatilis as in vnresonable beestis, and alle &#96;reptils that crepen on erthe; and entre ye on the erthe, encreesse ye, and be ye multiplied on erthe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 121:8"> Therfor Noe yede out, and hise sones, and his wijf, and the wyues of hise sones with hym;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> but also alle lyuynge beestis, and werk beestis, and &#96;reptils that crepen on erthe, bi her kynde, yeden out of the schip.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15–16; Romans 12:1; Genesis 22:9"> Forsothe Noe bildide an auter to the Lord, and he took of alle clene beestis and briddis, and offride brent sacrifices on the auter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:15; Ephesians 5:2; Isaiah 54:9–10"> And the Lord sauerede the odour of swetnesse, and seide to hym, Y schal no more curse the erthe for men, for the wit and thouyt of mannus herte ben redi in to yuel fro yong wexynge age; therfor Y schal no more smyte ech lyuynge soule as Y dide;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 5:7; Psalms 74:16–17; Jeremiah 5:24"> in alle the daies of erthe, seed and ripe corn, coold and heete, somer and wyntir, nyyt and dai, shulen not reste.</span>
