<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 37:28; Psalms 105:17; Genesis 37:36"> Therfor Joseph was led in to Egipt, and Putifar, &#96;chast and onest seruaunt of Farao, prince of the oost, a man of Egipt, bouyte hym of the hondis of Ismaelitis, of which he was brouyt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:14; Psalms 91:15; Jeremiah 15:20"> And the Lord was with hym, and he was a man doynge with prosperite in alle thingis. And Joseph dwellide in &#96;the hows of his lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 1:3; Genesis 30:27; Matthew 5:16"> which knew best that the Lord was with Joseph, and that alle thingis whiche he dide, weren dressid of the Lord in &#96;the hond of hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 39:21–22; Proverbs 27:18; Genesis 19:19"> And Joseph foond grace bifor his lord, and &#96;mynystride to hym, of whom Joseph was maad souereyn of alle thingis, and gouernede the hows bitaken to hym, and alle thingis that weren bitakun to hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 30:27; 2 Samuel 6:11–12; Acts 27:24"> And the Lord blesside the &#96;hows of Egipcian for Joseph, and multipliede al his catel, as wel in howsis as in feeldis;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:12; Luke 19:17; Proverbs 31:11"> nether he knew ony other thing no but &#96;breed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Bi breed in holi scripture is vndirstonden al manere of mete, and bi watir al manere of drynk.">a</a></sup>which he eet. Forsothe Joseph was fair in face, and schapli in siyt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:11; Ezekiel 16:34; 2 Peter 2:14"> And so aftir many daies the ladi castide hir iyen in to Joseph, and seide, Slepe thou with me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 23:26–28; Proverbs 22:14; Proverbs 9:13–18"> which assentide not to the vnleueful werk, and seide to hir, Lo! while alle thingis ben bitakun to me, my lord woot not what he hath in his hows,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:13; Genesis 20:6; Genesis 42:18"> nether ony thing is, which is not in my power, ether which &#96;he hath not bitake to me, outakun thee, which art his wijf; how therfor may Y do this yuel, and do synne ayens my lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Genesis 39:8; 1 Thessalonians 5:22"> Thei spaken siche wordis &#96;bi alle daies, and the womman was diseseful to the yong waxynge man, and he forsook auoutrie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 5:12; Ephesians 5:3; Malachi 3:5"> Forsothe it bifelde in a dai, that Joseph entride in to the hows, and dide sum werk with out witnessis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:22; Proverbs 7:13–27; Ecclesiastes 7:26"> And sche took &#96;the hem of his clooth, and sche seide, Slepe thou with me; and he lefte the mentil in hir hoond, and he fledde, and yede out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And whanne the womman hadde seyn the clooth in hir hondis, and that sche was dispisid,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 10:21; 1 Peter 2:20; Psalms 35:11"> sche clepide to hir the men of hir hows, and seide to hem, Lo! my lord hath brouyt in an Ebrew man, that he schulde scorn vs; he entride to me to do leccherie with me, and whanne Y criede, and he herde my vois,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> he lefte the mentil which Y helde, and he fledde out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 37:32; Jeremiah 4:22; Jeremiah 9:3–5"> Therfor in to the preuyng of trouthe, sche schewide the mantil, holdun to the hosebonde turnynge ayen hoom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 23:1; 1 Kings 18:17; Psalms 37:14"> And she seide, The Ebrew seruaunt, whom thou brouytist, entride to me to scorne me; and whanne he siy me crye,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> he lefte the mentil which Y helde, and he fledde out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 4:5–6; Proverbs 18:17; Proverbs 6:34–35"> And whanne these thingis weren herd, the lord bileuyde ouer myche to the wordis of the wijf, and was ful wrooth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 40:15; 2 Timothy 2:9; Psalms 105:18–19"> and he bitook Joseph in to prisoun, where the bounden men of the kyng weren kept, and he was closid there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 3:21; Isaiah 41:10; 1 Peter 3:17"> Forsothe the Lord was with Joseph, and hadde mercy on hym, and yaf grace to hym in the siyt of the prince of the prisoun,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 40:3–4; Genesis 39:4; 1 Samuel 2:30"> which bitook in the hond of Joseph alle prisoneris that weren holdun in kepyng, and what euer thing was doon, it was vndur Joseph, nethir the prince knewe ony thing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Genesis 39:2–3; Psalms 1:3"> for alle thingis weren bitakun to Joseph; for the Lord was with hym, and dresside alle his werkis.</span>
