<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 24:35; Psalms 112:1–3; Genesis 26:12–13"> Forsothe he was ful riche in possessyoun of siluer and of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 12:8–9; Genesis 12:6"> And he turnede ayen bi the weye in which he cam fro the south in to Bethel, &#96;til to the place, in which bifore he hadde sett tabernacle, bitwixe Bethel and Hay,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:12; Genesis 12:7–8; Isaiah 58:9"> in the place of the auter which he made bifore, and inwardli clepide there the name of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:29; Genesis 25:27; Genesis 4:20"> But also flockis of scheep, and droues of oxun, and tabernaclis weren to Loth, that was with Abram;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 36:6–7; Ecclesiastes 5:10–11; 1 Timothy 6:9"> and the lond miyte not take hem, that thei schulden dwelle togidre, for the catel of hem was myche, and thei miyten not dwelle in comyn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 26:20; Genesis 12:6; James 3:16"> Wherfor also strijf was maad bitwixe the keperis of flockis of Abram and of Loth. Forsothe Chananei and Feresei dwelliden in that lond in that tyme.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:6–7; Acts 7:26; Psalms 133:1"> Therfor Abram seide to Loth, Y biseche, that no strijf be bitwixe me and thee, and bitwixe my scheepherdis and thi scheepherdis; for we ben britheren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 20:15; Genesis 34:10; 1 Peter 3:8–12"> Lo! al the lond is bifore thee, Y biseche, departe thou fro me; if thou go to the left side, Y schal holde the riyt side; if thou chese the riyt side, Y schal go to the left side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:3; Isaiah 51:3; Genesis 14:8"> And so Loth reiside hise iyen, and seiy aboute al the cuntrei of Jordan, which was al moistid, bifor that the Lord distriede Sodom and Gomorre, as paradis of the Lord, and as Egipt, as men comen in to Segor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 13:9; 1 Peter 2:17; Hebrews 10:25"> And Loth chees to him the cuntre aboute Jordan, and departide fro the eest; and thei weren departid ech fro his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 19:29; Genesis 14:12; Genesis 19:1"> Abram dwellide in the lond of Chanaan; sotheli Loth dwellide in townes aboute Jordan, and wonide in Sodom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Peter 2:10; 2 Peter 2:6–8; Jude 1:7"> Forsothe men of Sodom weren ful wickid, and synneris greetly bifore the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 49:18; Deuteronomy 3:27; Genesis 28:14"> And the Lord seide to Abram, aftir that Loth was departid fro him, Reise thin iyen forth riyt, and se fro the place in which thou art now, to the north and south, to the eest and west;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:7; Genesis 35:12; Genesis 15:18"> Y schal yyue al the lond which thou seest to thee and to thi seed, til in to with outen ende.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 28:14; Numbers 23:10; Genesis 32:12"> And Y schal make thi seed as the dust of erthe; if ony man may noumbre the dust of erthe, also he schal mowe noumbre thi seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 13:15; Numbers 13:17–24"> Therfor rise thou, and passe thorou the lond in his lengthe and breede, for Y schal yyue it to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 18:1; Genesis 8:20; Genesis 35:27"> Therfor Abram, mouynge his tabernacle, cam and dwellide bisidis the valei of Mambre, which is in Ebron; and he bildide there an auter to the Lord.</span>
