<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:32–33; Genesis 23:1–2; Genesis 28:1"> Forsothe Abraham weddide another wijf, Ceture bi name,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:32–33; Numbers 25:17–18; Job 2:11"> which childide to him Samram, and Jexan, and Madan, and Madian, and Jesboth, and Sue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:13; Jeremiah 49:8; Ezekiel 27:20"> Also Jexan gendride Saba and Dadan. Forsothe the sones of Dadan weren Asurym, and Lathusym, and Laomym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 60:6"> And sotheli of Madian was borun Efa, and Ofer, and Enoth, and Abida, and Heldaa; alle these weren the sones of Cethure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 24:36; Hebrews 1:2; Galatians 3:29"> And Abraham yaf alle thingis whiche he hadde in possessioun to Isaac;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 21:14; Judges 6:3; Acts 14:17"> sotheli he yaf yiftis to the sones of concubyns; and Abraham, while he lyuede yit, departide hem fro Ysaac, his sone, to the eest coost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 12:4"> Forsothe the daies of lijf of Abraham weren an hundrid and &#96;fyue and seuenti yeer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 35:28–29; Genesis 15:15; 1 Chronicles 29:28"> and he failide, and diede in good eelde, and of greet age, and ful of daies, and he was gaderid to his puple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 50:13; Genesis 35:29; Genesis 49:29–30"> And Ysaac and Ismael, his sones, birieden him in the double denne, which is set in the feeld of Efron, sone of Seor Ethei,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 23:16; Genesis 49:31"> euene ayens Mambre, which denne he bouyte of the sones of Heth; and he was biried there, and Sare his wijf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Genesis 16:14; Genesis 24:62"> And aftir the deeth of Abraham God blesside Isaac his sone, which dwellide bisidis the pit bi name of hym that lyueth and seeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 83:6; Genesis 17:20; Genesis 21:13"> These ben the generaciouns of Ismael, sone of Abraham, whom Agar Egipcian, seruauntesse of Sare, childide to Abraham;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:29–31; Isaiah 60:7; Song of Solomon 1:5"> and these ben the names of the sones of Ismael, in her names and generaciouns. The firste gendride of Ismael was Nabaioth, aftirward Cedar, and Abdeel, and Mabsan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 21:11; Isaiah 21:16"> and Masma, and Duma, and Massa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:19; Job 2:11"> and Adad, and Thema, and Ithur, and Nafir, and Cedma.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 17:20; Genesis 17:23"> These weren the sones of Ismael, and these weren names by castels and townes of hem, twelue princes of her lynagis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 15:15; Genesis 25:7–8"> And the yeeris of lijf of Ismael weren maad an hundrid and seuene and thretti, and he failide, and diede, and was put to his puple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 16:12; Genesis 20:1; 2 Kings 23:29"> Forsothe he enhabitide fro Euila til to Sur, that biholdith Egipt, as me entrith in to Assiriens; he diede bifore alle his britheren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 1:2; Luke 3:34; Acts 7:8"> Also these ben the generaciouns of Ysaac sone of Abraham. Abraham gendride Isaac,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 24:29; Genesis 24:67; Genesis 22:23"> and whanne Isaac was of fourti yeer, he weddide a wijf, Rebecca, douyter of Batuel, of Sirie of Mesopotanye, the sistir of Laban.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:13; Isaiah 58:9; Ezra 8:23"> And Isaac bisouyte the Lord for his wijf, for sche was bareyn; and the Lord herde him, and yaf conseiuyng to Rebecca.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:9; 1 Samuel 28:6; 1 Samuel 30:8"> But the litle children weren hurtlid togidre in hir wombe; and sche seide, If it was so to comynge to me, what nede was it to conseyue? And sche yede and axide counsel of the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 9:10–13; Genesis 27:40; Genesis 27:29"> which answerde, and seide, Twei folkis ben in thi wombe, and twei puplis schulen be departid fro thi wombe, and a puple schal ouercome a puple, and the more schal serue the lesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Thanne the tyme of childberyng cam, and lo! twei children weren foundun in hir wombe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 27:11; Genesis 27:16; Genesis 27:23"> He that yede out first was reed, and al rouy in the manere of a skyn; and his name was clepid Esau.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 27:36; Hosea 12:3; Genesis 38:28–30"> Anoon the tothir yede out, and helde with the hond the heele of the brother; and therfore he clepide him Jacob. Isaac was sixti yeer eeld, whanne the litle children weren borun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 11:9; Genesis 21:20; Genesis 10:9"> And whanne thei weren woxun, Esau was maad a man kunnynge of huntyng, and a man erthe tilier; forsothe Jacob was a symple man, and dwellide in tabernaclis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 27:19; Genesis 27:31; Genesis 27:4"> Isaac louyde Esau, for he eet of the huntyng of Esau; and Rebecca louyde Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 13:25; Isaiah 40:30–31; 1 Samuel 14:28"> Sotheli Jacob sethide potage; and whanne Esau cam weri fro the feld,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 36:1; Exodus 15:15; Genesis 36:9"> he seide to Jacob, Yyue thou to me of this reed sething, for Y am ful weri; for which cause his name was clepid Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And Jacob seide to him, Sille to me the riyt of the first gendrid childe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Malachi 3:14; Job 21:15; Job 22:17"> He answerde, Lo! Y die, what schulen the firste gendrid thingis profite to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 12:16; Genesis 27:36; Hebrews 6:16"> Jacob seide, therfor swere thou to me. Therfor Esau swoor, and selde the firste gendrid thingis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Philippians 3:18–19; Hebrews 12:16–17; 1 Corinthians 15:32"> And so whanne he hadde take breed and potage, Esau eet and drank, and yede forth, and chargide litil that he hadde seld the riyt of the firste gendrid child.</span>
