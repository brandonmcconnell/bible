<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:14"> Farao wakide, and slepte eft, and seiy another dreem; seuen eeris of corn ful and faire camen forth in o stalke,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:10; Hosea 13:15; Ezekiel 19:12"> and othere as many eeris of corn, thinne and smytun with corrupcioun of brennynge wynd,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 37:5; Genesis 20:3"> camen forth, deuourynge al the fairenesse of the firste. Farao wakide aftir reste,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 4:7; Exodus 7:22; Matthew 2:1"> and whanne morewtid was maad, he was aferd bi inward drede, and he sente to alle the expowneris of Egipt, and to alle wise men; and whanne thei weren clepid, he telde the dreem, and noon was that expownede.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 40:14; Genesis 40:23; Genesis 40:1–3"> Thanne at the laste the maistir &#96;of boteleris bithouyte, and seide, Y knowleche my synne;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 39:20; Genesis 40:2–3; Genesis 37:36"> the kyng was wrooth to hise seruauntis, and comaundide me and the maister &#96;of bakeris to be cast doun in to the prisoun of the prince of knyytis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 40:5–8"> where we bothe saien a dreem in o nyyt, biforeschewynge of thingis to comynge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 40:12–19; Genesis 37:36; Genesis 39:1"> An Ebrew child, seruaunt of the same duk of knyytis was there, to whom we telden the dremes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:3; Genesis 40:20–22; Genesis 40:12"> and herden what euer thing the bifallyng of thing preuede afterward; for Y am restorid to myn office, and he was hangid in a cros.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 2:25; Psalms 113:7–8; Psalms 105:19–22"> Anoon at the comaundement of the kyng thei polliden Joseph led out of prisoun, and whanne &#96;the clooth was chaungid, thei brouyten Joseph to the kyng.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 5:16; Daniel 5:12; Psalms 25:14"> To whom the kyng seide, Y seiye dremes, and noon is that expowneth tho thingis that Y seiy, I haue herd that thou expownest moost prudentli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:5; Genesis 40:8; Daniel 2:47"> Joseph answerde, With out me, God schal answere prosperitees to Farao.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Therfor Farao telde that that he seiy; Y gesside that Y stood on the brenke of the flood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:8; Jeremiah 24:1–3; Jeremiah 24:5"> and seuene kiyn, ful faire and with fleischis able to etyng, stieden fro the watir, whiche kiyn gaderiden grene seggis in the pasture of the marreis;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and lo! seuene othere kiyn, so foule and leene, sueden these, that Y seiy neuere siche in the lond of Egipt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> and whanne the formere kien weren deuourid and wastid, tho secounde yauen no steppe of fulnesse,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:3; Psalms 37:19; Revelation 10:9–10"> but weren slowe bi lijk leenesse and palenesse. I wakide, and eft Y was oppressid bi sleep, and Y seiy a dreem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> seuene eeris of corn, ful and faireste, camen forth in o stalke,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 129:6–7; Hosea 13:15; 2 Kings 19:26"> and othere seuene, thinne and smytun with &#96;corrupcioun of brennynge wynd, camen forth of the stobil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 4:7; Genesis 41:8; Exodus 8:19"> whiche deuouriden the fairenesse of the formere;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 2:45; Revelation 4:1; Ephesians 1:17"> Y telde the dreem to expowneris, and no man is that expowneth. Joseph answerde, The dreem of the king is oon; God schewide to Farao what thingis he schal do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 41:47; Genesis 41:2; Genesis 41:53"> Seuene faire kiyn, and seuene ful eeris of corn, ben seuene yeeris of plentee, and tho comprehenden the same strengthe of dreem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 8:1; 2 Samuel 24:19"> and seuene kiyn thinne and leene, that stieden aftir tho, and seuene thinne eeris of corn and smytun with brennynge wynd, ben seuene yeer of hungur to comynge,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 41:25; Genesis 41:16"> whiche schulen be fillid bi this ordre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 41:26; Genesis 41:46–47; Genesis 41:49"> Lo! seuene yeer of greet plentee in al the lond of Egipt schulen come,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 47:13; Genesis 41:54; James 5:17"> and seuene othre yeer of so greet bareynesse schulen sue tho, that al the abundaunce bifore be youun to foryetyng; for the hungur schal waste al the lond,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 24:20; 1 Samuel 5:6"> and the greetnesse of pouert schal leese the greetnesse of plentee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Isaiah 46:10–11; Isaiah 14:24–27"> Forsothe this that thou siyest the secunde tyme a dreem, perteynynge to the same thing, is a &#96;schewyng of sadnesse, for the word of God schal be doon, and schal be fillid ful swiftli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 41:39; Acts 6:3; Deuteronomy 1:13"> Now therfor puruey the kyng a wijs man and a redi, and make the kyng hym souereyn to the lond of Egipt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 22:3; Luke 16:5; Proverbs 6:6–8"> which man ordeyne gouernouris bi alle cuntreis, and gadere he in to bernys the fyuethe part of fruytis bi seuene yeer of plentee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 45:6–7; Genesis 41:56; Exodus 4:13"> that schulen come now; and al the wheete be kept vndur the power of Farao,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="that is, not bi violence nether bi fre yifte, but bi biyng and paiyng of priys.">a</a></sup>and be it kept in citees,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 47:13–25"> and be it maad redi to the hungur to comynge of seuene yeer that schal oppresse Egipt, and the lond be not wastid bi pouert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 7:10; 1 Kings 21:2; 2 Samuel 3:36"> The counsel pleside Farao,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Job 32:8; Daniel 5:14; Numbers 27:18"> and alle his mynystris, and he spak to hem, Wher we moun fynde sich a man which is ful of Goddis spirit?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 41:33; Genesis 41:28; Genesis 41:25"> Therfor Farao seide to Joseph, For God hath schewid to thee alle thingis whiche thou hast spoke, wher Y mai fynde a wisere man and lijk thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Proverbs 22:29; Acts 7:10; Psalms 105:21–22"> Therfor thou schalt be ouer myn hous, and al the puple schal obeie to the comaundement of thi mouth; Y schal passe thee onely by o trone of the rewme.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 28:18; Philippians 2:9–11; Genesis 42:6"> And eft Farao seide to Joseph, Lo! Y haue ordeyned thee on al the lond of Egipt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Daniel 5:29; Daniel 5:7; Esther 3:10"> And Farao took the ryng fro his hond, and yaf it in the hond of Joseph, and he clothide Joseph with a stoole of bijs, and puttide a goldun wrethe aboute the necke;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Acts 7:10; Genesis 45:8; Genesis 45:26"> and Farao made Joseph to &#96;stie on his secounde chare, while a bidele criede, that alle men schulden knele bifore hym, and schulden knowe that he was souereyn of al the lond of Egipt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 105:22; Exodus 11:7"> And the kyng seide to Joseph, Y am Farao, without thi comaundement no man shal stire hond ether foot in al the lond of Egipt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Genesis 46:20; Ezekiel 30:17; Exodus 2:16"> And he turnede the name of Joseph, and clepide him bi Egipcian langage, the sauyour<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="In Ebrew it is schewinge priuitees, as Jerom and Lire here seyen.">b</a></sup>of the world; and he yaf to Joseph a wijf, Asenech, the douyter of Potifar, preest of Heliopoleos. And so Joseph yede out to the lond of Egipt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Daniel 1:19; 1 Kings 12:6; 1 Samuel 16:21"> Forsothe Joseph was of thretti yeer, whanne he stood in the siyt of kyng Farao, and cumpasside alle the cuntreis of Egipt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Genesis 26:12; Psalms 72:16"> And the plente of seuene yeer cam, and ripe corn weren bounden into handfuls, and weren gaderid into the bernys of Egipt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Genesis 41:34–36; Genesis 47:21"> also al the aboundaunce of cornes weren kept in alle citeis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Psalms 78:27; Judges 6:5; Genesis 22:17"> and so greet aboundaunce was of wheete, that it was maad euene to the grauel of the see, and the plente passide mesure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Genesis 46:20; Genesis 48:5; 2 Samuel 8:18"> Sotheli twei sones were born to Joseph bifor that the hungur came, whiche Asenech, douytir of Putifar, preest of Heliopoleos, childide to hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Psalms 30:5; Proverbs 31:7; Deuteronomy 33:17"> And he clepide the name of the firste gendrid sone, Manasses, and seide, God hath maad me to foryete alle my traueilis, and the hous of my fadir;</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Genesis 49:22; Genesis 50:23; Genesis 17:6"> and he clepide the name of the secunde sone Effraym, and seide, God hath maad me to encreesse in the lond of my pouert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Genesis 41:29–31; Psalms 73:20; Luke 16:25"> Therfor whanne seuene yeer of plentee that weren in Egipt weren passid,</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Genesis 45:11; Acts 7:11; Psalms 105:16"> seuene yeer of pouert bigunnen to come, whiche Joseph bifore seide, and hungur hadde the maistri in al the world; also hungur was in al the lond of Egipt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Matthew 17:5; Matthew 3:17; Jeremiah 14:1–6"> and whanne that lond hungride, the puple criede to Farao, and axide metis; to whiche he answeride, Go ye to Joseph, and do ye what euer thing he seith to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Genesis 42:6; Zechariah 5:3; Luke 21:35"> Forsothe hungur encreesside ech dai in al the lond, and Joseph openyde alle the bernys, and seelde to Egipcians, for also hungur oppresside hem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Genesis 41:54; Genesis 42:5; Genesis 41:56"> and alle prouynces camen in to Egipt to bie metis, and to abate the yuel of nedynesse.</span>
