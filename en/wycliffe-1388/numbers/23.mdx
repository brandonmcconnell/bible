<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 23:14; Numbers 23:30"> And whanne he hadde do bi the word of Balaam, thei puttiden a calf and a ram to gidere on the auter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 23:15; Genesis 8:20; Genesis 22:2"> And Balaam seide to Balaach, Stond thou a litil while bisidis thi brent sacrifice, while Y go, if in hap the Lord meete me; and Y schal &#96;speke to thee what euer thing he schal comaunde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 23:16; Ephesians 2:9; Luke 18:12"> And whanne he hadde go swiftli, God cam to hym; and Balaam spak to hym, and seide, Y reiside seuene auteris, and Y puttide a calf and a ram aboue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:18; Jeremiah 1:9; Isaiah 59:21"> Forsothe the Lord &#96;puttide a word in his mouth, and seide, Turne ayen to Balaach, and thou schalt speke these thingis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> He turnede ayen, and fond Balach stondynge bisidis his brent sacrifice, and alle the princes of Moabitis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:4; Numbers 24:3; Numbers 22:5–6"> And whanne his parable &#96;was takun, he seide, Balaach, the kyng of Moabitis, brouyte me fro Aran, fro the &#96;hillis of the eest; and he seide, Come thou and curse Jacob; haaste thou, and greetli curse thou Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:23; Isaiah 44:25; Numbers 23:20"> How schal Y curse whom God cursid not? bi what resoun schal Y &#96;haue abhomynable whom God &#96;hath not abhomynable?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:28; Deuteronomy 32:8; Exodus 33:16"> Fro the hiyeste flyntis Y schal se hym, and fro litle hillis Y schal biholde hym; the puple schal dwelle aloone, and it schal not be arettid among hethene men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 37:37; Genesis 13:16; Psalms 116:15"> Who may noumbre the dust, that is, kynrede, of Jacob, and knowe the noumbre of the generacioun of Israel? My lijf die in the deeth of iust men, and my laste thingis be maad lijk hem!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:2; Numbers 24:10; Numbers 22:17"> And Balaach seide to Balaam, What is this that thou doist? Y clepide thee, that thou schuldist curse myn enemyes, and ayenward thou blessist hem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 22:38; Numbers 23:20; Numbers 23:26"> To whom Balaam answeride, Whether Y may speke othir thing no but that that the Lord comaundith?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 109:17; James 3:9–10; Numbers 22:41"> Therfor Balaach seide to Balaam, Come with me in to anothir place, fro whennus thou se a part of Israel, and mayst not se al; fro thennus curse thou hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 46:6; Numbers 23:29; Isaiah 1:10–11"> And whanne he hadde led Balaam in to an hiy place, on the cop of the hil of Phasga, he bildide seuene auteris to Balaam, and whanne calues and rammes weren put aboue,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 23:3; Numbers 22:8"> he seide to Balaach, Stonde here bisidis thi brent sacrifice, while Y go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 23:5; Numbers 24:1; Numbers 22:35"> And whanne the Lord hadde &#96;come to him, and hadde put &#96;a word in his mouth, he seide, Turne ayen to Balach, and thou schalt seie these thingis to hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 23:26; 1 Samuel 3:17; Jeremiah 37:17"> He turnyde ayen, and foond Balach stondynge bisidis his brent sacrifice, and the princis of Moabitis with hym. To whom Balach seide, What spak the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 3:20"> And whanne his parable &#96;was takun, he seide, Stonde, Balach, and herkene; here, thou sone of Sephor. God is not &#96;as a man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:29; James 1:17; Malachi 3:6"> that he lye, nethir he is as the sone of a man, that he be chaungid; therfor he seide, and schal he not do? he spak, and schal he not fulfille?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 10:27–29; Numbers 22:12; Genesis 12:2"> Y am brouyt to blesse, Y may not forbede blessyng.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 23:4; Matthew 1:23; Jeremiah 50:20"> Noon idol is in Jacob, nethir symylacre is seyn in Israel; his Lord God is with hym, and the sown of victorie of kyng is in hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 24:8; Deuteronomy 33:17; Job 39:9–11"> The Lord God ledde hym out of Egipt, whos strengthe is lijk an vnicorn;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 10:18–19; Joshua 13:22; Galatians 1:23–24"> fals tellyng bi chiteryng of bryddis, &#96;ethir idolatrie, is not in Jacob, nethir fals dyuynyng is in Israel. In his tymes it schal be seide to Jacob and Israel, What the Lord hath wrought!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 49:27; Genesis 49:9; Numbers 24:17"> Lo! the puple schal rise to gidere as a lionesse, and schal be reisid as a lioun; the lioun schal not reste, til he deuoure prey, and drynke the blood of hem that ben slayn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 2:1–3"> And Balach seide to Balaam, Nether curse thou, nether blesse thou hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Numbers 22:18; Acts 4:19–20"> And he seide, Whether Y seide not to thee, that what euer thing that God comaundide to me, Y wolde do this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 11:29; Proverbs 21:30; Numbers 23:19–20"> And Balach seide to hym, Come, and Y schal lede thee to an other place, if in hap it plesith God that fro thennus thou curse hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 21:20; Psalms 106:28"> And whanne Balaach hadde led hym out on the &#96;cop of the hil of Phegor, that biholdith the wildirnesse,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 23:1–2"> Balaam seide to hym, Bilde here seuene auteris to me, and make redi so many caluys, and rammes of the same noumbre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Balaach dide as Balaam seide, and he puttide caluys and rammes, bi alle auteris.</span>
