<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spak to Moises,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Psalms 105:11; Deuteronomy 1:7–8"> and seide, Comaunde thou to the sones of Israel, and thou schalt seie to hem, Whanne ye han entrid in to the lond of Canaan, and it bifelde in to possessioun &#96;to you bi lot, it schal be endid bi these endis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 14:3; Ezekiel 47:13; Joshua 3:16"> The south part schal bigynne at the wildirnesse of Syn, which is bisidis Edom, and it schal haue termes ayens the eest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 32:8; Joshua 15:3–4; Numbers 13:26"> the saltiste see, whiche termes schulen cumpasse the south coost bi the &#96;stiynge of Scorpioun, &#96;that is, of an hil clepid Scorpioun, so that tho passe in to Senna, and come to the south, &#96;til to Cades Barne; fro whennus the coostis schulen go out to the town, Abdar bi name, and schulen strecche forth &#96;til to Asemona;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 15:4; Genesis 15:18; 1 Kings 8:65"> and the terme schal go bi cumpas fro Assemona &#96;til to the stronde of Egipt, and it schal be endid bi the brynke of the grete see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 1:4; Ezekiel 47:20; Ezekiel 47:15"> Forsothe the west coost schal bigynne at the greet see, and schal be closid bi that ende.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 33:37; Numbers 34:6; Numbers 34:3"> Sotheli at the north coost, the termes schulen bigynne at the greet see, and schulen come &#96;til to the hiyeste hil,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="the hiyeste hil. In Ebreu it is, til to the hil of hil.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 13:21; 2 Kings 14:25; Joshua 13:5–6"> fro which tho schulen come in to Emath, &#96;til to the termes of Sedada;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:17"> and the coostis schulen go &#96;til to Ephrona, and the town of Enan. These schulen be the termes in the north part.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Fro thennus thei schulen mete coostis ayens the eest coost, fro the town Henan &#96;til to Sephama;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 23:33; Deuteronomy 3:17; Joshua 11:2"> and fro Sephama termes schulen go doun in to Reblatha, ayens the welle &#96;of Daphnyn; fro thennus tho schulen come ayens the eest to the se of Cenereth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 34:3; Genesis 13:10; Genesis 19:24–26"> and tho schulen strecche forth &#96;til to Jordan, and at the laste tho schulen be closid with the salteste see. Ye schulen haue this lond bi hise coostis &#96;in cumpas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 34:1; Joshua 14:1–5"> And Moises comaundide to the sones of Israel, and seide, This schal be the lond which ye schulen welde bi lot, and which the Lord comaundide to be youun to nyne lynagis and to the half lynage;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 32:33; Numbers 32:23; Joshua 14:2–3"> for the lynage of the sones of Ruben, bi her meynees, and the lynage of the sones of Gad, bi kynrede and noumbre, and half the lynage of Manasses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 32:32"> that is, twey lynagis and an half, han take her part ouer Jordan, ayens Jerico, at the eest coost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the Lord seide to Moises,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 14:1; Joshua 19:51; Numbers 13:8"> These ben the &#96;names of men that schulen departe the lond to you, Eleazar, preest, and Josue, the sone of Nun, and of each lynage, o prynce;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 1:4–16"> of whiche these ben the names, of the lynage of Juda,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 26:65; Numbers 13:30; Numbers 14:24"> Caleph, the sone of Jephone;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 49:5"> of the lynage of Symeon, Samuhel, the sone of Amyud;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 49:27; Psalms 68:27"> of the lynage of Beniamyn, Heliad, sone of Casselon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> of the lynage of the sones of Dan, Bochi, sone of Jogli; of the sones of Joseph,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> of the lynage of Manasses, Hamyel, sone of Ephoth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> of the lynage of Effraym, Camuhel, sone of Septhan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> of the lynage of Zabulon, Elisaphan, sone of Pharnat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> of the lynage of Isacar, duyk Phaltiel, the sone of Ozan; of the lynage of Azer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Abyud, the sone of Salomy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> of the lynage of Neptalym, Fedahel, the sone of Amyud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 19:51; Numbers 34:18"> These men it ben, to whiche the Lord comaundide, that thei schulden departe to the sones of Israel the lond of Chanaan.</span>
