<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 23:3; Numbers 23:23; Numbers 23:28"> And whanne Balaam siy that it pleside the Lord that he schulde blesse Israel, he yede not as he &#96;hadde go bifore, &#96;that he schulde seke fals dyuynyng &#96;bi chiteryng of briddis, but he dresside his face ayens the desert,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:20; 2 Chronicles 15:1; 1 Samuel 19:23"> and reiside iyen, and siy Israel dwellynge in tentis bi hise lynagis. And whanne the Spirit of God felde on hym, and whanne a parable was takun,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 22:31; Numbers 24:16; Numbers 23:7"> he seide, Balaam, the sone of Beor, seide, a man whois iye is stoppid seide,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Revelation 1:10; Numbers 12:6"> the herere of Goddis wordis seide, which bihelde the reuelacioun of almyyti God, which fallith doun, and hise iyen ben openyd so, Hou faire ben thi tabernaclis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Jacob, and thi tentis, Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 1:3; Isaiah 58:11; Psalms 104:16"> as valeys ful of woodis, and moiste gardyns bisidis floodis, as tabernaclis whiche the Lord hath set, as cedris bisidis watris;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:12; 1 Chronicles 14:2; Revelation 17:1"> watir schal flowe of his bokat, and his seed schal be in to many watris, &#96;that is, puplis. The kyng of hym schal be takun a wei for Agag, and the rewme of hym schal be doon awai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 45:5; Psalms 2:9; Numbers 14:9"> God ledde hym out of Egipt, whos strengthe is lijk an vnicorn; thei schulen deuoure<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="thei schulen deuoure etc. In Ebreu it is thus, Israel schal deuoure hethen men hise enemyes, and schal gnawe the bonys of hem, that is, schal distrie the strengthe of hem.">a</a></sup>hethene men, enemyes &#96;of hym, that is, of Israel; and thei schulen breke the boonus of hem, and schulen perse with arowis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Genesis 27:29; Genesis 49:9"> He restide and slepte as a lyoun, and as a lionesse, whom no man schal dore reise. He that blessith thee, schal be blessid; he that cursith, schal</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 23:11; Ezekiel 21:14; Ezekiel 21:17"> be arettid in to cursyng And Balaach was wrooth ayens Balaam, and seide, whanne the hondis weren wrungun to gidere, I clepide thee to curse myn enemyes, whiche ayenward thou hast blessid thries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 22:17; Numbers 22:37; Hebrews 11:24–26"> Turne ayen to thi place; forsothe Y demede to onoure thee greetli, but the Lord priuyde thee fro onour disposid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 22:18; Numbers 22:38"> Balaam answeride to Balaach, Whethir Y seide not to thi messangeris, whiche thou sentist to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 16:28; Numbers 22:18; Numbers 22:20"> Thouy Balaach schal yyue to me his hows ful of siluer and of gold, Y schal not mow passe the word of my Lord God, that Y brynge forth of myn herte ony thing, ethir of good ethir of yuel, but what euer thing the Lord schal seie, Y schal speke this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Micah 6:5; Revelation 2:14; Genesis 49:1"> Netheles Y schal go to my puple, and Y schal yyue counsel to thee, what thi puple schal do in the laste tyme to this puple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 24:3–4; Numbers 23:7; Matthew 13:35"> Therfor whanne a parable was takun, he seide eft, Balaam, the sone of Beor seide, a man whos iye is stoppid,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 24:4; 1 Corinthians 8:1; 2 Samuel 23:1–2"> seide, the herere of Goddis wordis seide, which knowith the doctrine of the hiyeste, and seeth the reuelacioun of almiyti God, which fallith doun and hath opyn iyen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 22:16; Luke 1:32–33; Genesis 49:10"> Y schal se hym, but not now; Y schal biholde hym, but not nyy; a sterre schal be borun of Jacob, and a yerde schal rise of Israel; and he schal smyte the duykis of Moab, and he schal waste alle the sones of Seth; and Ydumye schal be hys possessioun,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Amos 9:12; Genesis 27:29; 2 Samuel 8:14"> the eritage of Seir schal bifalle to his enemyes; forsothe Israel schal do strongli, of Jacob schal be he that schal be lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 49:10; Micah 5:2; Psalms 2:1–12"> and schal leese the relikis of the citee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 17:14; Judges 6:3; 1 Chronicles 4:43"> And whanne he hadde seyn Amalech, he took a parable, and seide, Amalech is the bigynning<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Amalech is the bigynnyng, that is, he roos first ayenus Israel aftir the goyng out of Egipt.">b</a></sup>of hethene men, whos laste thingis schulen be lost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 15:19; Judges 1:16; Job 29:18"> Also &#96;he siy Cyney, and whanne a parable was takun, he seide, Forsothe thi dwellyng place is strong, but if thou schalt sette thi nest in a stoon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 83:8; Genesis 10:22; Ezra 4:2"> and schalt be chosun of the generacioun of Cyn, hou longe schalt thou mow dwelle? forsothe Assur schal take thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 23:23; Malachi 3:2; 2 Kings 5:1"> And whanne a parable was takun, he spak eft, Alas! who schal lyue, whanne the Lord schal make thes thingis?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 10:4; Numbers 24:20; Daniel 10:20"> Thei schulen come in grete schippis fro Ytalie, thei schulen ouercome Assiries, and thei schulen distrie Ebrews, and at the last also thei hem silf schulen perische.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 31:8; Joshua 13:22; Numbers 24:11"> And Balaam roos, and turnide ayen in to his place; and Balaach yede ayen bi the weye in which he cam.</span>
