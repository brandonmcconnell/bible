<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 28:41; Leviticus 8:30; Leviticus 8:12"> preestis, that weren anoyntid, and whos hondis weren fillid and halewid, that thei schulden &#96;be set in preesthod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1–2; Numbers 26:61"> Nadab and Abyu, whanne thei offeriden alien fier in the &#96;siyt of the Lord, in the deseert of Synay, weren deed without fre children; and Eleazar and Ythamar &#96;weren set in preesthod bifor Aaron hir fadir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the Lord spak to Moises,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:8; Numbers 2:33; Numbers 1:49–53"> &#96;and seide, &#96;Presente thou the lynage of Leuy, and make to stonde in the siyt of Aaron, preest, that thei mynystre to hym;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 1:50; Numbers 8:15; Numbers 8:11"> and wake, and that thei kepe what euer thing perteyneth to the religioun of multitude, bifor the tabernacle of witnessyng;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 4:33; Numbers 10:17; Isaiah 52:11"> and that thei kepe the vessels of the tabernacle, and serue in the seruyce therof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 8:19; Numbers 18:6–7; Ephesians 4:8"> And thou schalt yyue bi fre yifte the Leuytis<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="that is, the sones of the lynage of Leuy.">a</a></sup>to Aaron and hise sones, to whiche thei ben youun of the sones of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 1:51; Numbers 3:38; Romans 12:7"> Sotheli thou schalt ordeyne Aaron and hise sones on the religioun of preesthod; a straungere,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="a straunger, that is, which is not of the lynage of Leuy.">b</a></sup>that neiyeth for to mynystre, and schal die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the Lord spak to Moyses, &#96;and seide,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 3:41; Numbers 8:16; Numbers 18:6"> Y haue take the Leuytis of the sones of Israel for ech firste gendrid thing that openeth the womb in the sones of Israel; and the Leuytis schulen be myne,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 2:23; Exodus 13:12; Exodus 13:2"> for ech firste gendrid thing is myn; sithen Y smoot the firste gendrid in the lond of Egipt, Y halewide to me what euer thing is borun first in Israel; fro man &#96;til to beest thei ben myne; Y am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And the Lord spak to Moises in the deseert</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 26:62; Mark 10:14; 2 Timothy 3:15"> of Synay, and seide, Noumbre thou the sones of Leuy bi &#96;the housis of her fadris, and bi meynees, ech male fro o monethe and aboue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 3:39; Numbers 4:37; Numbers 4:49"> Moises noumbride, as the Lord comaundide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 46:11; Exodus 6:16–19; Numbers 15:5–23"> And the sones of Leuy weren foundun, bi her names, Gerson, and Caath, and Merary;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:17; 1 Chronicles 26:1–32; Exodus 6:17–19"> the sones of Gerson weren Lebny, and Semey;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 6:18; 1 Chronicles 6:18; 1 Chronicles 15:17–21"> the sones of Caath weren Amram, and Jessaar, Hebron, and Oziel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 6:19; 1 Chronicles 6:19; Numbers 3:33"> and the sones of Merari weren Mooly, and Musi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Of Gerson weren twei meynees, of Lebny, and of Semei;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 4:38–40"> of whiche the puple of male kynde was noumbrid, fro o monethe and aboue, seuene thousynde and fyue hundrid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 2:17"> These schulen sette tentis aftir the tabernacle at the west,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> vndur the prince Eliasaph, the sone of Jahel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 25:9; 1 Chronicles 23:32; 1 Chronicles 9:14–33"> And thei schulen haue kepyngis in the tabernacle of boond of pees, the tabernacle it silf, and the hilyng therof, the tente<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="In Ebru it is, the veyl of the dore of the grete strete, whiche is on the tabernacle and auteer bi cumpas.">c</a></sup>which is drawun bifor the yatis of the hilyng of the witnessyng of boond of pees;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 35:18; Exodus 38:9–16; Exodus 27:9–16"> and the curteyns of the greet street, also the tente which is hangid in the entryng of the greet street of the tabernacle, and what euer thing perteyneth to the custom of the auter, the cordis of the tabernacle, and al the purtenaunce therof.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="In Ebreu it is, to al the seruice therof.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:23; Numbers 3:19; 1 Chronicles 23:12"> The kynrede of Caath schal haue the puplis of Amram, and of Jessaar, and of Ebron, and of Oziel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 3:31; Numbers 4:35–36; Numbers 3:7"> these ben the meynees of Caathitis, noumbrid bi her names, alle of male kynde, fro o monethe and aboue, eiyte thousynde and sixe hundrid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 3:23; Numbers 2:10"> Thei schulen haue kepyngis of the seyntuarie, and schulen sette tentis at the south coost;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> and &#96;the prince of hem schal be Elisaphan, the sone of Oziel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 36:35–36; Exodus 27:1–8; Exodus 26:31–33"> And thei schulen kepe the arke, and the boord, and the candilstike, the auters, and vesselis of the seyntuarie in whiche it is mynystrid, and the veil, and al sich purtenaunce.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 4:27; Numbers 4:16; Numbers 20:25–28"> Sotheli the prince of princis of Leuytis schal be Eleazar, the sone of Aaron, preest; and he schal be on the keperis of the kepyng of the seyntuarie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 3:20; 1 Chronicles 6:19; 1 Chronicles 23:21"> And sotheli of Merary schulen be the puplis of Mooli, and of Musi,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 2:9; Numbers 2:11; Numbers 1:21"> noumbrid bi her names, alle of male kynde fro o monethe and aboue, sixe thousynde and two hundrid;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 2:25; Numbers 3:28–29"> &#96;the prince of hem schal be Suriel, the sone of Abiahiel; thei schulen sette tentis in the north coost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 26:32; Exodus 26:37; Exodus 26:15–29"> And vndur &#96;the kepyng of hem schulen be the tablis of the tabernacle, and the barris, and the pileris, and &#96;the foundementis of tho, and alle thingis that perteynen to sich ournyng,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> and the pileris of the greet street bi cumpas, with her foundementis, and the stakis with coordis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 3:10; Numbers 1:53; Numbers 2:3"> Forsothe Moises and Aaron with hise sones schulen sette tentis bifor the tabernacle of boond of pees, that is, at the eest coost, and schulen haue the keping of the seyntuarie, in the myddis of the sones of Israel; what euer alien neiyeth,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="neiyith, that is, without licence.">e</a></sup>he schal die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Numbers 26:62; Matthew 7:14; Numbers 4:47–48"> Alle the Leuytis, whiche Moises and Aaron noumbriden, bi comaundement of the Lord, bi her meynees, in male kynde, fro o monethe and aboue, were two and twenti thousynd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 3:15; Luke 10:20; Psalms 87:6"> And the Lord seide to Moises, Noumbre thou the firste gendrid children of male kynde of the sones of Israel, fro o monethe and aboue; and thou schalt haue the summe of hem; and</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 3:12; Numbers 3:45; Numbers 8:16"> thou schalt take Leuytis to me for alle the firste gendrid of the sones of Israel; Y am the Lord; and thou schalt take &#96;the beestis of hem for alle the firste gendrid of the sones of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And as the Lord comaundide, Moises noumbride the firste gendrid children of the sones of Israel; and the males weren bi her names,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 3:39"> fro o monethe and aboue, two and twenti thousynde two hundrid and seuenti and thre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And the Lord spak to Moises, and seide,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Numbers 3:12; Numbers 3:40–41"> Take thou Leuytis for the firste gendrid children of the sones of Israel, and the beestis of Leuytis for the beestis of hem, and the Leuytis schulen be myne; Y am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Exodus 13:13; Numbers 18:15–16"> Forsothe in the prijs of two hundrid seuenti and thre, that passen the noumbre of &#96;Leuytis, of the firste gendrid children of the sones of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Leviticus 27:6; Numbers 18:16"> thou schalt take fyue ciclis bi ech heed, at the mesure of seyntuarie; a sicle hath xx. halpens;</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> and thou schalt yyue the money to Aaron and to hise sones, the prijs of hem that ben aboue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> Therfor Moises took the money of hem that weren aboue, and whiche thei hadden ayenbouyt of the Leuytis, for the firste gendrid of the sones of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Numbers 3:46–47; 1 Peter 3:18; Matthew 20:28"> a thousand thre hundrid sixti and fyue of siclis, bi the weiyte of seyntuarie;</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 3:48; Numbers 16:15; 1 Samuel 12:3–4"> and he yaf that money to Aaron and to hise sones, bi the word which the Lord comaundide to hym.</span>
