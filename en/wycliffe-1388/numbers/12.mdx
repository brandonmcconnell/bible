<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 2:21; Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 24:37"> And Marie spak and Aaron ayens Moises, for his wijf a womman of Ethiope,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:12–13; Numbers 16:3; Micah 6:4"> and seiden, Whethir God spak oneli by Moises? whethir he spak not also to vs in lijk maner? And whanne the Lord hadde herd this, he was wrooth greetli;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 11:29; James 3:13; 2 Corinthians 11:5"> for Moises was the myldest man, ouer alle men that dwelliden in erthe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 76:9; Numbers 16:16–21"> And anoon<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="In Ebru it is, sodeynly.">a</a></sup>the Lord spak to Moises and to Aaron and to Marye, Go out ye thre aloone to the tabernacle of boond of pees. And whanne thei weren gon yn,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 11:25; Exodus 34:5; Psalms 99:7"> the Lord cam doun in a piler of cloude, and he stood in the entryng of the tabernacle, and clepide Aaron and Marie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 3:5; Genesis 31:10–11; Genesis 46:2"> And whanne thei hadden go, he seide to hem, Here ye my wordis; if ony among you is a profete of the Lord, Y schal appere to hym in reuelacioun, ethir Y schal speke to hym bi &#96;a dreem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 3:2–6; Joshua 1:1; Psalms 105:26"> And he seide, And my seruaunt Moises is not siche,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="or so straunge to me.">b</a></sup>which is moost feithful in al myn hows;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:10; 1 Corinthians 13:12; Exodus 33:11"> for Y speke to hym mouth to mouth, and he seeth God opynli,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="or clerli passinge other.">c</a></sup>and not bi derke spechis and figuris. Why therfor dredden ye not to bacbite &#96;ether depraue my seruaunt Moises?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 17:22; Hosea 5:15; Numbers 11:1"> And the Lord was wrooth ayens hem, and he wente a wei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:9; 2 Kings 5:27; Exodus 4:6"> And the cloude yede awei, that was on the tabernacle and lo! Marie apperide whijt with lepre as snow. And whanne Aaron biheelde hir, and siy hir bispreynd with lepre,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:19; 2 Samuel 24:10; Proverbs 30:32"> he seide to Moises, My lord, Y beseche, putte thou not this synne on vs,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 88:4–5; 1 Corinthians 15:8; 1 Timothy 5:6"> which we diden folili, that this womman be not maad as deed, and as a deed borun thing which is cast out of the &#96;wombe of his modir; lo! now the half of hir fleisch is deuourid with lepre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 12:21; Luke 6:28; James 5:15"> And Moises criede to the Lord, and seide, Lord, Y biseche, heele thou hir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:9; Job 30:10; Isaiah 50:6"> To whom the Lord answerid, If hir fadir hadde spet in to hir face, where sche ouyte not to be fillid with schame, nameli in seuene daies? Therfor be sche departid out of the tentis bi seuen daies, and aftirward sche schal be clepid ayen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:8–9; Micah 7:8–9; Micah 6:4"> And so Marie was excludid out of the tentis bi seuene daies; and the puple was not mouyd fro that place, til Marie was clepid ayen.</span>
