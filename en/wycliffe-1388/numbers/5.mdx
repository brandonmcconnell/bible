<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spak to Moises, and seide, Comaunde thou to the sones of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 21:1; Leviticus 13:46; Numbers 12:14"> that thei caste out of the castels ech leprouse man, and that fletith out seed, and is defoulid on a deed bodi; caste ye out of the castels,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:14; Leviticus 26:11–12; Psalms 68:18"> as wel a male as a female, lest thei defoulen tho, whanne thei dwellen with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And the sones of Israel diden so; and thei castiden hem out of the castels, as the Lord spak to Moises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the Lord spak to Moises,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 5:1–4; Leviticus 5:14–7"> and seide, Speke thou to the sones of Israel, Whanne a man ethir a womman han do of alle synnes that ben wont to falle to men, and han broke bi necgligence the &#96;comaundement of the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 5:5; Leviticus 26:40; Joshua 7:19"> and han trespassid, thei schulen knowleche her synne, and thei schulen yelde thilke heed, and the fyuethe part aboue, to hym ayens whom thei synneden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 6:6–7; Leviticus 7:7; Leviticus 25:25–26"> But if noon is that schal resseyue thei schulen yyue to the Lord, and it schal be the preestis part, outakun the ram which is offrid for clensyng, that it be a quemeful sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:3–4; Leviticus 7:6–14; Leviticus 6:17–18"> Also alle the firste fruytis, whiche the sones of Israel offren, perteynen to the preest;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 10:13"> and what euer thing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="thing that is offrid, this is soth of the firste fruytis, not of othere sacrifices.">a</a></sup>is offrid of ech man in the seyntuarie, and is youun to the &#96;hondis of the preest,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="and is youun to the hondis of the prest, in Ebreu it is, whiche a man halewid, and yaf to the hondis of the prest.">b</a></sup>it schal be the preestis part.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the Lord spak to Moises,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 5:19–20; Proverbs 2:16–17"> and seide, Speke thou to the sones of Israel, and thou schalt seie to hem, If &#96;the wijf of a man hath errid, and hath dispisid the hosebonde,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 18:20; Leviticus 20:10; Proverbs 7:18–19"> and hath slept with another man, and the hosebonde may not take ether preue this, but the auowtrye is hid, and may not be preuyd bi witnessis, for sche is not foundun in leccherie;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Song of Solomon 8:6; Proverbs 6:34; Zephaniah 3:8"> if the spirit of gelousie stirith the housebonde ayens his wijf, which is ether defoulid, ethir is apechid bi fals suspecioun,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:16; 1 Kings 17:18; Leviticus 5:11"> the man schal brynge hir to the preest, and he schal offre an offryng for hir &#96;the tenthe part of a mesure clepid satum of barli meele; he schal not schede oyle ther onne, nethir he schal putte encense, for it is the sacrifice of gelousie, and an offryng enquerynge auowtrye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 2:22–23; Jeremiah 17:10; Hebrews 13:4"> Therfor the preest schal offre hir, and schal sette bifore the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 30:18; John 8:6; Numbers 19:2–9"> and he schal take holi watir in &#96;a vessel of erthe, and he schal putte in to it a litil of the erthe of the pawment of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 5:4; Numbers 5:22; Revelation 10:9–10"> And whanne the womman stondith in the siyt of the Lord, he schal diskyuere hir heed, and he schal putte &#96;on the hondis of hir the sacrifice of remembryng, and the offryng of gelousie. Sotheli he schal holde moost bittir watris, in whiche he gaderide togidere cursis with cursyng.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 7:2; Numbers 5:12; Matthew 26:63"> And he schal conioure hir, and schal seie, If an alien man slepte not with thee, and if thou art not defoulid in the forsakyng the bed of the hosebonde, these bittereste watris schulen not anoye thee, in to whiche Y haue gaderid togidere cursis;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 5:12"> ellis if thou bowidst awei fro thin hosebonde, and art defoulid, and hast leyn with another man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 6:26; Nehemiah 10:29; 1 Samuel 14:24"> thou schalt be suget to these cursyngys; the Lord yyue thee in to cursyng, and in to ensaumple of alle men in his puple; &#96;the Lord make thin hipe to wexe rotun, and thi wombe swelle, and be brokun;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 109:18; Psalms 89:52; Job 31:39–40"> cursid watris entre in to thi wombe, and while the wombe swellith, thin hipe wexe rotun. And the womman schal answere, Amen! amen!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 3:19; Psalms 51:1; Psalms 51:9"> And the preest schal write thes cursis in a litil book, and he schal do awey tho cursis with bittereste watris, in to whiche he gaderide cursis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 5:3–4; Malachi 3:5"> and he schal yyue to hir to drynke. And whanne sche hath drunke tho watris,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 8:27; Numbers 5:18; Numbers 5:15"> the preest schal take of hir hond the sacrifice of gelousie, and he schal reise it bifor the Lord, and he schal putte on the auter;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 2:2; Leviticus 2:9; Leviticus 5:12"> so oneli that he take bifore an handful of sacrifice &#96;of that that is offrid, and brenne on the auter, and so yyue drynke to the womman the moost bittere watris.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:18; Zechariah 8:13; Jeremiah 29:18"> And whanne sche hath drunke tho watris, if sche is defoulid, and is gilti of auowtrie, for the hosebonde is dispisid, the watris of cursyng schulen passe thorouy hir, and while the wombe is bolnyd, the hipe schal wexe rotun, and the womman schal be in to cursyng and in to ensaumple to al the puple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Micah 7:7–10; 2 Corinthians 4:17; Psalms 113:9"> That if sche is not pollutid, sche schal be harmeles, and schal brynge forth fre children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 5:19; Numbers 5:12; Numbers 5:15"> This is the lawe of gelousie, if a womman bowith awei fro hir hosebonde, and is defoulid,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> and the hosebonde is stirid with the spirit of gelousye, and bryngith hir in to the &#96;siyt of the Lord, and the preest doith to hir bi alle thingis that ben writun, the hosebonde schal be with out synne,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 20:10; Romans 2:8–9; Ezekiel 18:4"> and sche schal resseyue hir wickidnesse.</span>
