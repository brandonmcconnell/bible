<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:22–25; Numbers 32:8; Numbers 34:18"> And there the Lord spak to Moises,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 12:16; Numbers 32:8; Deuteronomy 1:19"> and seide, Sende thou men that schulen biholde the lond of Canaan, which Y schal yyue to the sones of Israel, of ech lynage o man of the princes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Moises dide that that the Lord comaundide,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="In Ebru it is, seid, for it was not propirly a comaundement but a suffraunce.">a</a></sup>and sente fro the deseert of Pharan princes, men of whiche these ben the names.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Of the lynage of Ruben, Semmya, the sone of Zectur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 34:19; Numbers 14:6; Numbers 13:30"> Of the lynage of Symeon, Saphat, the sone of Hury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Of the lynage of Juda, Caleph, the sone of Jephone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 24:13; Numbers 11:28; Numbers 13:16"> Of the lynage of Isachar, Igal, the sone of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Of the lynage of Effraym, Osee, the sone of Nun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Of the lynage of Beniamyn, Phalti, the sone of Raphu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Of the lynage of Zabulon, Gediel, the sone of Sodi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Of the lynage of Joseph, of the gouernaunce of Manasses, Gaddi, the sone of Susy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Of the lynage of Dan, Amyel, the sone of Gemalli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Of the lynage of Aser, Sur, the sone of Mychael.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Of the lynage of Neptalym, Nabdi, the sone of Napsi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 13:8; Exodus 17:9; Numbers 14:6"> Of the lynage of Gad, Guel, the sone of Machi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 1:9; Genesis 12:9; Judges 1:19"> These ben the names of men, which Moises sente to biholde the lond of Canaan; and he clepide Osee, the sone of Nun, Josue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:14; Exodus 3:8"> Therfor Moises sente hem to biholde the lond of Canaan, and seide to hem, &#96;Stie ye bi the south coost; and whanne ye comen to the hillis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> biholde ye the lond, what maner lond it is; and biholde ye the puple which is the dwellere therof, whether it is strong, ethir feble, &#96;whether thei ben fewe in noumbre, ether manye;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:23; Deuteronomy 31:6–8; Ezekiel 34:14"> whether that lond is good, ethir yuel; what maner citees ben, wallid, ether without wallis;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 20:1; Numbers 33:36; Numbers 27:14"> whether the lond is fat, ether bareyn, &#96;whether it is ful of woodis, ethir without trees. Be ye coumfortid, and &#96;brynge ye to vs of the fruytis of that lond. Sotheli the tyme was, whanne grapis first ripe myyten be etun thanne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 78:12; Psalms 78:43; Isaiah 19:11"> And whanne thei hadden stied, thei aspieden the lond, fro the deseert of Syn &#96;til to Rohob, as men entryth to Emath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 13:24; Numbers 32:9; Deuteronomy 1:24–25"> And thei stieden to the south, and camen in to Ebron,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="In Ebru it is, and he cam in to Ebron, for Caleph aloone yede thidir, as Rabi Salomon seith.">b</a></sup>where Achyman, and Sisai, and Tholmai, the sones of Enach, weren; for Hebron was maad bi seuen yeer bifor Thamnys, the citee of Egipt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And thei yeden til to the stronde of clustre, and kittiden doun a sioun with his grape, which twei men baren in a barre; also thei token of pumgarnadis, and of the figis of that place which is clepid Nehelescol,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 34:28; Exodus 24:18; Numbers 14:33–34"> that is, the stronde of grape, for the sones of Israel baren a clustre fro thennus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 32:8; Numbers 20:1; Deuteronomy 1:19"> And the aspieris of the lond, whanne thei hadden cumpassid al the cuntrey, after fourti daies camen to Moises and Aaron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Exodus 3:17; Exodus 13:5"> and to al the cumpany of the sones of Israel, in to the deseert of Pharan which is in Cades. And &#96;the aspieris spaken to hem, and schewiden the fruytis of the lond to al the multitude, and telden,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:1–2; Deuteronomy 1:28; Numbers 13:33"> and seiden, We camen to the lond, to which thou sentest vs, which lond treuli flowith with mylk and hony, as it may be knowun bi these fruytis;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 14:43; Judges 6:3; Genesis 15:19–21"> but it hath strongeste inhabiteris, and grete cytees, and wallid; we sien there the kynrede of Anachym; Amalech dwellith in the south;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 14:24; Romans 8:31; Philippians 4:13"> Ethei, and Jebusei, and Amorey dwellen in the hilli placis; forsothe Cananey dwellith bisidis the see, and bisidis the floodis of Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; Joshua 14:8; Hebrews 3:19"> Among thes thingis Caleph peeside the grutchyng of the puple, that was maad ayens Moises, and seide, &#96;Stie we, and welde we the lond, for we moun gete it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Amos 2:9; Numbers 14:36–37; Ezekiel 36:13–14"> Forsothe other aspieris, that weren with hym, seiden, We moun not stie to this puple, for it is strongere than we.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; Isaiah 40:22; Genesis 6:4"> And thei deprauyden the lond which thei hadden biholde, anentis the sones of Israel, and seiden, The lond which we cumpassiden deuourith hise dwelleris; the puple which we bihelden is of large stature; there we syen summe wondris ayens kynde, of the sones of Enach, of the kynde of geauntis, to whiche we weren comparisound, and weren seien as locustis.</span>
