<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 14:3–4; Ezekiel 48:8; Joshua 21:2–42"> ayens Jericho, Comaunde thou to the sones of Israel, that thei yyue to dekenes of her possessiouns,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:14; Joshua 21:11; Ezekiel 45:2"> citees to dwelle,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="citees to dwelle, he seith not to be lord, ether to resseyue rentis there, for the citees in whiche the dekenes dwelliden, weren so the kyngis, ether of othere lordis; wherfor and Ebron was oon of tho citees, as it is ha in xx. co. of Josue; and ne theles Caleph was lord therof for it was youun to him in to possessioun, as it is had in xv. co. of Josue.">a</a></sup>and the suburbabis of tho bi cumpas, that thei dwelle in &#96;the citees, and the suburbabis be to beestis, and &#96;werk beestis;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> whiche suburbabis schulen be strecchid forth fro the wallis of citees with outforth &#96;bi cumpas, in the space of a thousynde paacis;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> ayens the eest schulen be two thousynde cubitis, and ayens the south in lijk manere schulen be two thousynde cubitis, and at the see that biholdith to the west schal be the same mesure, and the north coost schal be endid bi euene terme. And the citees schulen be in the myddis, and the suburbabis with outforth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 21:13; Joshua 21:3; Psalms 9:9"> Forsothe of tho citees whiche ye schulen yyue to dekenes, sixe schulen be departid in to helpis of fugityues, &#96;ether of fleynge men, that he that schedde blood, fle to tho; and outakun these sixe, ye schulen yyue to dekenes othere two and fourti citees,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 21:3–42; 1 Chronicles 6:54–81"> that is, togidere eiyte and fourti, with her surburbabis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 26:54; Numbers 33:54; Joshua 21:1–42"> And tho citees that schulen be youun of the possessiouns of sones of Israel, schulen be takun awey, mo fro hem that han more, and fewere &#96;schulen be takun awey fro hem that han lesse, alle bi hem silf schulen yyue bi the mesure of her eritage, citees to dekenes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The Lord seide to Moises,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:9; Deuteronomy 19:1–2; Leviticus 14:34"> Spek thou to the sones of Israel, and thou schalt seie to hem, Whanne ye han passid Jordan, in the lond of Canaan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 21:13; Joshua 20:2; Numbers 35:6"> deme ye whiche citees owen to be in to the helpis of fugityues, whiche not wilfuli han sched blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 20:3–6; Deuteronomy 19:6; Joshua 20:9"> In whiche whanne the fleere hath fled, the kynesman of hym that is slayn, schal not mow sle hym, til he stonde in the siyt of the multitude, and the cause of hym be demed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Forsothe of tho citees that ben departid to the helpis of fugityues,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:8–10; Deuteronomy 4:41–43; Joshua 20:7–9"> thre schulen be ouer Jordan, and thre in the lond of Canaan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 15:16; Galatians 3:28; Romans 3:29"> as wel to the sones of Israel as to comelyngis and pilgryms; that he fle to tho citees, that schedde blood not wilfuli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 24:17; Genesis 9:5–6; Deuteronomy 19:11–13"> If ony man smytith a man with yrun, and he that is smytun is deed, &#96;the smyter schal be gilti of mansleyng, and he schal die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 21:18"> If he castith a stoon, and a man is deed bi the strook, he schal be punyschid in lijk maner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> If a man smytun with a staf dieth, he schal be vengid bi &#96;the blood of the smytere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 20:3; Deuteronomy 19:6; Deuteronomy 19:12"> The niy kynesman of hym that is slayn schal sle the mansleere; anoon as he takith hym, &#96;that is, the manquellere, he schal sle hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 21:14; Deuteronomy 19:11; 2 Samuel 3:27"> If bi haterede a man hurtlith, &#96;ethir schoufith, &#96;a man, ethir castith ony thing in to hym bi aspiyngis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> ether whanne he was enemy, smoot with hond, and he is deed, the smytere schal be gilti of mansleyng. The kynesman &#96;of him that is slayn, anoon as he findith him, &#96;that is, the sleere, schal sle hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 35:11; Exodus 21:13; Joshua 20:3"> That if bi sudeyn caas, and without hatrede and enemytees,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> he doith ony thing of these;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 20:6; Numbers 35:12"> and this is preued in heryng &#96;of the puple, and the questioun of blood is discussid bitwixe the smytere and the kynesman,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 29:7; Leviticus 4:3; Romans 3:24–26"> the innocent schal be delyuered fro the hond of the vengere, and bi sentence of iugis he schal be led ayen in to the citee, to which he fledde, and he schal dwelle there, til the grete preest die, which is anoyntid with oile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> If the sleere is foundun with out the coostis &#96;of the citees that ben asigned to exilid men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 22:2; Deuteronomy 19:10; Deuteronomy 19:6"> and is slayn of him that is vengere, he that sleeth him, &#96;that is, the exilid man, schal be with out gilt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 3:14; Hebrews 10:26–30; Hebrews 6:4–8"> for the exilid man ouyte sitte in the citee &#96;til to the &#96;deth of the bischop; forsothe aftir that thilke bischop is deed, the mansleere schal turne ayen in to his lond.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 27:11; Numbers 27:1"> These schulen be euerlastynge and lawful thingis in alle youre dwellyngis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 10:28; Deuteronomy 19:15; Matthew 18:16"> A mansleere schal be punyschid vndur witnessis; no man schal &#96;be dampned at the witnessyng of o man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:11–13; Genesis 9:5–6; Psalms 51:14"> Ye schulen not take prijs of him which is gilti of blood, anoon and he schal die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 4:12; Galatians 3:10–13; Galatians 2:21"> Men exilid, and fugityues, schulen not mow turne ayen in ony maner in to her citees, bifore the deeth of the bischop, lest ye defoulen the lond of youre abitacioun,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 11:50–51; Psalms 106:38; Hosea 4:2–3"> which is defoulid bi the blood of innocent men; and it may not be clensid in other maner, no but bi the blood of hym, that schedde the blood of anothir man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 5:3; Psalms 135:21; Revelation 21:27"> And so youre possessioun schal be clensid, for Y schal dwelle with you; for Y am the Lord, that dwelle among the sones of Israel.</span>
