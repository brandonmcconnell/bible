<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spak to Moises and to Aaron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 22:20–25; Deuteronomy 21:3; 1 Samuel 6:7"> and seide, This is the religioun of sacrifice, which the Lord ordeynede. Comaunde thou to the sones of Israel, that thei brynge to thee a reed cow of hool age,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="In Ebru it is, not of age, but reed hoollich, that is, withoute diuersite of ony colour.">a</a></sup>in which is no wem, nether sche hath bore yok.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 4:12; Leviticus 4:21; Leviticus 16:27"> And ye schulen bitake hir to Eleazar, preest, which schal offre &#96;the cow, led out of the tentis, in the siyt of alle men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 4:17; Leviticus 4:6; Leviticus 16:14"> And he schal dippe his fyngur in the blood therof, and schal sprynge seuene sithis ayens the yatis of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 29:14; Leviticus 4:11–12; Psalms 22:14"> And he schal brenne that cow, while alle men sien; and he schal yyue as wel the skyn and fleischis therof as the blood and dung to flawme.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 14:4; Leviticus 14:49; Psalms 51:7"> Also the preest schal &#96;sende a tre of cedre, and ysope, and reed threed died twies, into the flawme that deuourith the cow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 11:25; Leviticus 11:40; Numbers 19:8"> And thanne at the laste, whanne hise clothis &#96;and bodi ben waischun, he schal entre in to the tentis, and he schal be defoulid &#96;til to euentid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> But also he that brente the cow, schal waische hise clothis, and bodi, and he schal be vncleene &#96;til to euentid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 9:13; Numbers 19:13; Numbers 19:20–21"> Forsothe a cleene man schal gadere the aischis of the cow, and schal schede out tho with out the tentis, in a place moost cleene, that tho be to the multitude of the sones of Israel in to keping, and in to watir of spryngyng; for the cow is brent for synne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 12:49; Numbers 19:7–8; Colossians 3:11"> And whanne he that bar the aischis of the cow, hath waische hise clothis, he schal be vncleene &#96;til to euentid. And the sones of Israel, and comelyngis that dwellen among hem, schulen haue this hooli bi euerlastynge lawe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 5:2; Numbers 31:19; Leviticus 21:1"> He that touchith a deed bodi of man, and is vncleene for this bi seuene daies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 31:19; Revelation 7:14; Leviticus 7:17"> schal be spreynt of this watir in the thridde, and in the seuenthe dai; and so he schal be clensid. If he is not spreynt in the thridde dai, he schal not mow be clensid in the seuenthe dai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 15:31; Leviticus 22:3; Leviticus 7:20"> Ech that touchith the deed bodi bi it silf of mannus soule, and is not spreynt with this medlyng, defoulith the &#96;tabernacle of the Lord, and he schal perische fro Israel; for he is not spreynt with the wateris of clensyng, he schal be vncleene, and his filthe schal dwelle on hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> This is the lawe of a man that dieth in the tabernacle; alle that entren in to his tente, and alle vessels that ben there, schulen be defoulid bi seuene daies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 14:36; Leviticus 11:32; Numbers 31:20"> A vessel that hath not an hilyng, nethir a byndyng aboue, schal be vncleene.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 31:19; Matthew 23:27; Luke 11:44"> If ony man touchith the deed bodi of man slayn in the feeld, ether deed bi hym silf, ether a boon, ether the sepulcre &#96;of hym, he schal be vncleene bi seuene daies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 7:38; Genesis 26:19; Revelation 7:17"> And thei schulen take &#96;of the aischis of the brennyng, and of the synne, and thei schulen sende quyk watris in to a vessel on tho aischis;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:25–27; Hebrews 9:14; Numbers 19:9"> in whiche whanne &#96;a cleene man hath dippid ysope, he schal spreynge therof the tente, and al the purtenaunce of howshold, and men defoulid bi sich defoulyng.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 1:7; Hebrews 10:22; Ezekiel 36:25"> And in this maner a cleene man schal clense an vncleene, in the thridde and in the seuenthe dai; and he schal be clensid in the seuenthe dai. And he schal waische hym silf, and hise clothis, and he schal be vncleene &#96;til to euentid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 16:16; Genesis 17:14; Numbers 19:13"> If ony man is not clensid bi this custom, the soule of hym schal perische fro the myddis of the chirche; for he defoulith the &#96;seyntuarie of the Lord, and is not spreynt with the watir of clensyng.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 9:13–14; Hebrews 10:4; Hebrews 9:10"> This comaundement schal be a lawful thing euerlastynge. Also he that schal sprenge the watris schal waische his clothis; ech man that touchith the watris of clensyng, schal be vncleene &#96;til to euentid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Haggai 2:13; Leviticus 7:19; Matthew 15:19–20"> What euer thing an vncleene man touchith, he schal make it vncleene; and a soule that touchith ony of these thingis &#96;defoulid so, schal be vncleene &#96;til to euentid.</span>
