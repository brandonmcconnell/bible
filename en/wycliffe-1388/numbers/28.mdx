<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 3:11; Genesis 8:21; Numbers 9:2–3"> Offre ye bi her tymes myn offryng, and looues, and encense of swettist odour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 6:9; Daniel 11:31; John 1:29"> These ben the sacrificis whiche ye owen to offre; twey lambren of o yeer, with out wem, ech dai in to euerlastynge brent sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 9:21; 1 Kings 18:36; Exodus 12:6"> Ye schulen offre oon eerli, and the tother at euentid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 2:1; Exodus 16:36; Exodus 29:38–42"> &#96;Ye schulen offre the tenthe part of ephi &#96;of floure, &#96;which be spreynt with pureste oile, and haue the fourthe part of hyn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 5:25; Leviticus 6:9; Psalms 50:8"> It is continuel brent sacrifice, which ye offriden in the hil of Synai, in to &#96;odour of swettiste encense to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 29:42; Numbers 28:14; Isaiah 57:6"> And ye schulen offre the fourthe part of hyn of wyn, bi ech lomb, in the seyntuarie of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And ye schulen offre in lijk maner the tother lomb at euentid, bi al the custom of the morewe sacrifice, and of moist sacrifices therof, an offryng of swettist odour to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 58:13; Psalms 92:1–4; Revelation 1:10"> Forsothe in the &#96;dai of sabat ye schulen offre twey lambren of o yeer, without wem, and twei tenthe partis of flour spreynt togidere with oile, in sacrifice, &#96;and ye schulen offre moiste sacrificis that ben sched bi custom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:4–5; Numbers 29:22; Numbers 29:31"> bi alle sabatis, in to euerlastynge<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="into euerlastynge etc. In Ebreu it is, ouer the contynuel brent sacrifice.">a</a></sup>brent sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 10:10; Ezekiel 46:6; Ezra 3:5"> Forsothe in calendis, that is, in the bigynnyngis of monethis, ye schulen offre brent sacrifice to the Lord, tweyne calues of the droue, o ram, seuene lambren of o yeer, without wem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 15:4–12; Ezekiel 46:5–7; Numbers 29:10"> and thre tenthe partis of flour spreynt to gidere with oile, in sacrifice, bi ech calf, and twey tenthe partis of flour spreynt to gidere with oile, bi ech ram;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and the tenthe part of &#96;a dyme of flour of oile in sacrifice, bi ech lomb; it is brent sacrifice of &#96;swetist odour, and of encense to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Forsothe the moiste sacrifices of wyn, that schulen be sched bi alle slayn sacrificis, schulen be these; the half part of hyn bi ech calf, the thridde part bi a ram, the fourthe part bi a lomb; this schal be brent sacrifices bi ech monethe, that comen oon aftir anothir while the yeer turneth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 28:3; Numbers 28:22; Numbers 15:24"> Also a &#96;buc of geet schal be offrid to the Lord for synnes,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="for synnes. <span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 23:6; Exodus 23:15; Exodus 13:6"> and in the fiftenthe day schal be the solempnyte of the therf looues. Bi seuene daies ye schulen ete therf looues;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 12:16; Leviticus 23:7–8"> of whiche the firste dai schal be worschipful and hooli; ye schulen not do ony seruyle werk therynne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:21; Ezekiel 45:21–25; Numbers 29:8"> And ye schulen offre brent sacrifice to the Lord, twey calues, o ram, seuene lambren of o yeer, without wem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> and the sacrifices of ech bi itsilf of flour, which be spreynt to gidere with oile, thre tenthe partis bi ech calf,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and twey tenthe partis bi a ram, and the tenthe part of &#96;a dyme bi ech lomb, that is, bi seuene lambren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 8:3"> &#96;And ye schulen offre o &#96;buc of geet for synne, that clensyng be maad for you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 28:3; Numbers 28:10"> outakun the brent sacrifice of the morewtid, which ye schulen offre euere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> So ye schulen do bi ech dai of seuene daies, into the nurschyng of fier, and in to swettist odour to the Lord, that schal rise of the brent sacrifice, and of moiste sacrifices of ech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 23:8; Numbers 28:18; Exodus 13:6"> Also the seuenthe day schal be moost solempne and hooli to you; ye schulen not do ony seruyle werk ther ynne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 23:16; Exodus 34:22; Leviticus 23:10"> Also the dai of the firste fruytis, whanne ye schulen offre newe fruitis to the Lord, whanne the wokis schulen be fillyd, schal be worschipful and hooli; ye schulen not do ony seruyle werk ther ynne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 23:18–19; Numbers 28:11; Numbers 28:19"> And ye schulen offre brent sacrifice to the Lord, in to &#96;swettiste odour; twey calues of the droue, o ram, and seuene lambren of o yeer, with out wem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and in the sacrifices of tho ye schulen offre thre tenthe partis of flour spreynt togidere with oile, bi ech calf, twei tenthe partis bi rammes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> the tenthe parte of &#96;a dyme bi the lambren, whiche ben to gidere, seuene lambren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 28:15; Numbers 28:22; 1 Peter 2:24"> &#96;And ye schulen offre a &#96;buc of geet, which is offrid for clensyng, outakun brent sacrifice euerlastynge, and the moiste sacrifices therof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 28:19; Numbers 28:3; Malachi 1:13–14"> ye schulen offre alle thingis with out wem, with her moyste sacrifices.</span>
