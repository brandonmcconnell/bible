<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 13:21; Exodus 15:20; Numbers 33:36"> And the sones of Israel and al the multitude camen in to the deseert of Syn, in the firste monethe.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="in the firste monethe of the xl. yeer fro the going out of Egipt.">a</a></sup>And the puple dwellide in Cades; and Marie was deed there, and biried in the same place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 16:19; Numbers 16:42; Exodus 15:23–24"> And whanne the puple hadde nede to watir, thei yeden togidere ayens Moises and Aaron; and thei weren turned in to dissensioun,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="in to dissencioun, that is, rebelte and stryuyng.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 17:2; Numbers 14:1–2; Numbers 16:31–35"> and seiden, We wolden that we hadden perischid among oure britheren bifor the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 17:3; Psalms 106:21; Numbers 16:13–14"> Whi han ye led out the chirche of the Lord in to wildirnesse, that bothe we and oure beestis die?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 16:14; Jeremiah 2:2; Jeremiah 2:6"> Whi han ye maad vs to stie from Egipt, and han brouyt vs in to this werste place, which may not be sowun, which nether bryngith forth fige tre, nether vineris, nether pumgranatis, ferthermore and hath not watir to drynke?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:5; Numbers 16:4; Numbers 16:19"> And whanne the multitude was left, Moises and Aaron entriden in to the tabernacle of boond of pees, and felden lowe to erthe, and crieden to God, and seiden, Lord God, here the cry of this puple, and opene to hem thi tresour, a welle of quyk watir, that whanne thei ben fillid, the grutchyng of hem ceesse. And the glorie of the Lord apperide on hem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and the Lord spak to Moises,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 4:17; Acts 2:1–4; Revelation 22:17"> and seide, Take the yerde,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="the yerde, this was the yerde of Aaron.">c</a></sup>and gadere the puple, thou, and Aaron thi brother; and speke ye to the stoon bifore hem, and it schal yyue watris. And whanne thou hast led watir out of the stoon, al the multitude schal drynke, and the beestis therof &#96;schulden drynke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 17:10"> Therfor Moises took the yerde that was in the &#96;siyt of the Lord, as the Lord comaundide to hym,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 106:32–33; Acts 14:9–15; Luke 9:54–55"> whanne the multitude was gaderid bifor the stoon; and he seide to hem, Here ye, rebel and vnbileueful; whether we moun brynge out of this stoon watir to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:4; Exodus 17:6; Deuteronomy 8:15"> And whanne Moises hadde reisid the hond, and hadde smyte the flynt twies with the yerde, largeste watris yeden out, so that the puple drank, and the beestis drunken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:37; Numbers 27:14; Ezekiel 36:23"> And the Lord seide to Moises and to Aaron, For ye bileueden not to me, that ye schulden halewe me<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="that ye schulden halewe me, that is, in bileuynge with out doute myn hoolynesse thorou filling of my biheest, and in pronounsinge youre feith with out doute bifor the puple, but ye diden the contrarie, therfor ye schulen not lede, and so forth.">d</a></sup>bifor the sones of Israel, ye schulen not lede these puples in to the lond which Y schal yyue to hem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 17:7; Psalms 95:8; Deuteronomy 33:8"> This is the watir of ayenseiyng; there the sones of Israel stryueden ayens the Lord, and he was halewid in hem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 11:16–17; Deuteronomy 23:7; Obadiah 1:10–12"> In the meene tyme Moises sente messangeres fro Cades to the kyng of Edom, whiche seiden, Israel thi brother sendith these thinges. Thou knowist al the trauel that took vs,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 12:40; Deuteronomy 26:6; Genesis 46:6"> hou oure fadris yeden doun in to Egipt, and we dwelliden there myche tyme, and Egipcians turmentiden vs and oure fadris; and hou we crieden to the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 14:19; Exodus 23:20; Exodus 33:2"> and he herde vs, and sente an aungel that ledde vs out of Egipt. And lo! we ben set in the citee of Cades, which is in thi laste coostis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:27; Deuteronomy 2:29; Numbers 21:22–24"> and we bisechen that it be leueful to vs to passe thorou thi lond; we schulen not go bi feeldis, nether bi vyneris, nether we schulen drynke watris of thi pittis; but we schulen go in the comyn weie, and we schulen not bowe to the riyt side, nether to the left side, til we passen thi termes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> To whom Edom answeride, Ye schulen not passe bi me, ellis Y schal be armed, and come ayens thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:28; Deuteronomy 2:6; Exodus 12:38"> And the sones of Israel seiden, We schulen go bi the weie comynli vsid, and if we and oure beestis drynken thi watris, we schulen yyue that that is iust; noon hardnesse schal be in prijs, onely passe we swiftli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 11:17; Amos 1:11; Genesis 32:6"> And he answeride, Ye schulen not passe. And anoon he yede out ayens Israel, with a multitude without noumbre, and &#96;strong hond,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 11:18; Deuteronomy 2:29; Deuteronomy 2:4–8"> nether he wolde assente to Israel bisechynge, that he schulde graunte passage bi hise coostis. Wherfor Israel turnede awey fro hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 20:1; Numbers 20:14; Numbers 21:4"> And whanne thei hadden moued tentis fro Cades, thei camen in to the hil of Hor, which is in the endis of the lond of Edom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 33:37"> where the Lord spak to Moyses and seide, Aaron go to his puples;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 25:8; Deuteronomy 32:50; Numbers 31:2"> for he schal not entre in to the lond which Y yaf to the sones of Israel, for he was vnbileueful to my mouth, at the watris of ayenseiyng.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 33:38–39"> Take thou Aaron, and his sone with hym, and thou schalt lede hem in to the hil of Hor;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 20:24; Isaiah 22:21–22; Hebrews 7:11"> and whanne thou hast maad nakid the fadir of his cloth,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="cloth, that is, the bischops coote.">e</a></sup>thou schalt clothe &#96;with it Eleazar, his sone, and Aaron schal be gederid, and schal die there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Moises dide as the Lord comaundide; and thei stieden in to the hil of Hor, bifor al the multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:6; Exodus 29:29–30; Deuteronomy 34:9"> And whanne he hadde maad nakid Aaron of hise clothis, he clothide with tho Eleazar, his sone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:8; 2 Chronicles 35:24–25; Genesis 1:10"> Sotheli whanne Aaron was deed in the &#96;cop of the hil, Moises cam doun with Eleazar. Sotheli al the multitude siy that Aaron was deed, and wepte on hym thretti daies, bi alle her meyness.</span>
