<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 1:16; Numbers 33:40; Joshua 12:14"> And whanne Chananei, the kyng of Arad, that dwellide at the south, hadde herd this, that is, that Israel cam bi the weye of aspieris, he fauyt ayens hem; and Chananei was ouercomere and ledde pray of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 11:30; Genesis 28:20; 1 Samuel 1:11"> And Israel bounde hym sylf bi avow to the Lord, and seide, If thou schalt bitake this puple in myn hond, Y schal do awei &#96;the citees therof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 14:45; Deuteronomy 1:44; Psalms 10:17"> And the Lord herde the preieris of Israel, and bitook the Chananey; and Israel killid hym, and distruyede hise citees; and clepide the name of that place Horma, that is, cursyng, &#96;ethir hangyng up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 6:9; Judges 11:18; Numbers 32:7"> &#96;Forsothe thei yeden forth also fro the hil of Hor, bi the weie that ledith to the reed see, that thei schulden cumpasse the lond of Edom; and it bigan to anoye the puple, of the weie and trauel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 78:19; Numbers 16:13–14; Exodus 17:2–3"> And the puple spak ayens the Lord and Moises, and seide, Whi leddist thou vs out of Egipt, that we schulden die in wildirnesse? breed failith, watris ben not; oure soule wlatith now on this &#96;meete moost liyt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:9; Jeremiah 8:17; Deuteronomy 8:15"> Wherfor the Lord sente &#96;firid serpentis in to the puple; at the woundis of whiche serpentis, and the dethis of ful many men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 8:24; Numbers 11:2; Psalms 78:34"> thei camen to Moyses, and seiden, We synneden, for we spaken ayens the Lord and thee; preie thou, that he take awey fro vs the serpentis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 3:14; Psalms 106:43–45; Psalms 145:8"> And Moises preiede for the puple; and the Lord seide to hym, Make thou a serpent of bras,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="of bras is not here in Ebreu, but aftirward.">a</a></sup>and sette thou it for a signe<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="a signe, in Ebreu it is, in a perche.">b</a></sup>; he that is smytun and biholdith it, schal lyue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 3:14–15; Hebrews 12:2; John 6:40"> Therfor Moyses made a serpent of bras, and settide for a signe; and men smytun and biholdynge it, weren heelid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 33:43–45"> And the sones of Israel yeden forth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and settiden tentis in Oboth; fro whennus thei yeden forth, and settiden tentis in Neabarym,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="that is, in the wildirnessis of Abarym.">c</a></sup>in the wildirnesse, that biholdith Moab, ayens the eest coost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:13–14"> And thei moueden fro thennus, and camen to the stronde of Zareth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 11:18; Numbers 22:36; Isaiah 16:2"> which thei leften, and settiden tentis ayens Arnon, which is in the deseert, and apperith in the coostis of Amorrei. Forsothe Arnon is the terme of Moab, and departith Moabitis and Ammoreis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 10:13; 2 Samuel 1:18"> Wherfor it is seid in the book<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="book, ether in the telling, as Ebrews seyen, for the Ebreu word here signefieth bothe book and telling.">d</a></sup>of batels of the Lord, As he dide in the reed see, so he schal do in the strondis of Arnon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 21:28; Deuteronomy 2:18; Deuteronomy 2:9"> the harde rochis of the strondis weren bowid, that tho schulen reste in Arnon, and schulden ligge in the coostis of Moabitis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 20:8; Isaiah 12:3; Isaiah 41:17–18"> Fro that place<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="fro that place, in Ebreu it is thus, fro this place to the pit, and they camen to the place, where the pit was youun.">e</a></sup>the pit apperide, of which the Lord spak to Moyses, Gadere thou the puple, and Y schal yyue watir to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 5:13; Exodus 15:1–2; Psalms 106:12"> Thanne Israel soong this song, The pit<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="that is, water of the pit.">f</a></sup>stie;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 33:22; 1 Timothy 6:17–18; James 4:12"> thei sungen togidere, The pit which the princes diggiden, and the duykis of the multitude maden redi, in the yyuere of the lawe, and in her stauys. And thei yeden forth fro the wildirnesse to Mathana,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> fro Mathana to Naaliel, fro Naaliel in to Bamoth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 23:28; Numbers 23:14; Deuteronomy 1:5"> Bamoth is a valey in the cuntrey of Moab, in the cop of Phasga, that biholdith ayens the deseert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:26–28; Numbers 20:14–19; Judges 11:19–21"> Forsothe Israel sente messangeris to Seon, kyng of Ammorreis, and seide,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 20:17"> Y biseche that it be leueful to me to passe thorou thi loond; we schulen not bowe in to the feeldis and vyneris; we schulen not drynke watris of pittis; we schulen go in the kyngis weie, til we passen thi termes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 11:20; Deuteronomy 29:7–8; Isaiah 15:4"> Which nolde graunte that Israel schulde passe thury hise coostis, but rather, whanne the oost was gaderid, he yede out ayens Israel, in to deseert. And he cam in to Yasa, and fauyt ayens Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Amos 2:9; Joshua 24:8; Genesis 32:22"> of whom he was smytun in the scharpnesse of swerd, and his lond was weldid fro Arnon &#96;til to Jeboth and &#96;the sones of Amon; for the termes of Amonytis weren holdun bi strong help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 32:33–42; Isaiah 16:8–9; Jeremiah 48:2"> Therfor Israel took alle &#96;the citees of hym, and dwelliden in the citees of Amorrei, that is, in Esebon, and hise townes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> The citee of Esebon was Seons, kyng of Ammorei, which Seon fauyt ayens the kyng of Moab, and took al the lond that was of his lordschip, &#96;til to Arnon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 14:4; Numbers 21:14; Habakkuk 2:6"> Therfor it is seid in prouerbe,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="that is, in a comown song maad of the victorye of Seon.">g</a></sup>Come ye in to Esebon, be it bildid, and maad the citee of Seon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 21:15; Jeremiah 48:45–46; Isaiah 15:1–2"> fier yede out of Esebon, flawme yede out of the citee &#96;ethir greet castel of Seon, and deuouryde Ar of Moabitis, and the dwelleris of the &#96;hiye places of Arnon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 11:24; 2 Kings 23:13; Jeremiah 48:46"> Moab, wo to thee! thou, puple of Chamos, perischidist; it yaf the sones therof in to fliyt, and the douytris in to caitifte to Seon, kyng of Ammoreis;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:18; Isaiah 15:2; Jeremiah 48:22"> the yok of hem<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="that is, the lordschip of Moabitis.">h</a></sup>perischide, fro Esebon &#96;til to Dibon; the wery men camen in to Jophe, and &#96;til to Medaba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 13:8–12; Numbers 32:33–42; Deuteronomy 3:16–17"> And so Israel dwellide in the lond of Ammorrey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:32; Numbers 32:1; Numbers 32:35"> And Moises sente men that schulden aspie Jaser, whos &#96;townes thei token, and weldiden the dwelleris.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 13:12; Deuteronomy 1:4; Deuteronomy 29:7"> And thei turniden hem silf, and stieden bi the weie of Basan. And Og, the kyng of Basan, with al his puple cam ayens hem, to fiyte in Edray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 135:10–11; Joshua 8:7; Deuteronomy 3:2–3"> And the Lord seide to Moises, Drede thou not hym, for Y haue bitake hym, and al his loond, and puple, in thin hoond; and thou schalt do to hym as thou didist to Seon, kyng of Ammorreis, the dwellere of Esebon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:7–8; Joshua 13:12; Deuteronomy 3:3–17"> Therfor thei smytiden &#96;bothe hym with hise sones and al his puple, &#96;til to deeth; and thei weldiden &#96;the lond of hym.</span>
