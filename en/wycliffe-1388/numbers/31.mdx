<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spak to Moyses, and seide,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 27:13; Numbers 25:14–18; Revelation 6:10"> Venge thou firste the sones of Israel of Madianytis, and so thou schalt be gaderid to thi puple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 26:25; Numbers 25:11; 2 Kings 10:30"> And anoon Moises seide, Arme ye men of you to batel, that moun take of Madianytis the veniaunce of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:8; 1 Samuel 14:6; Judges 7:2"> Of ech lynage be chosun a thousynde men of Israel, that schulen be sent to batel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And of ech lynage thei yauen a thousynde, that is twelue thousynde of men, redi to batel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:44; Numbers 10:8–9; 2 Chronicles 13:12–15"> whiche Moises sente with Fynees, the sone of Eleazar, preest. And he bitook to hem hooli vesselis, and trumpis to make sown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 21:11; 1 Kings 11:15–16; 1 Samuel 27:9"> And whanne thei hadden fouyt ayens Madianytis, and hadden ouercome, thei killiden<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="thei killiden, etc. In Ebreu it is, and thei killiden the kyngis of hem on slayn men, that is, on men cast doun bi Goddis vertu, as Ebrews expownen.">a</a></sup>alle the malis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 13:21–22; Numbers 25:15; Revelation 2:14"> and &#96;the kyngis of hem, Euy, and Reem, and Sur, and Hur, and Rebe, fyue princes of &#96;the folc of hem. Also thei killiden bi swerd Balaam, the sone of Beor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; 2 Chronicles 28:8–10; 2 Chronicles 28:5"> And thei token the wymmen of hem, and the litle children, and alle beestis, and al purtenaunce of howshold; what euer thei myyten haue, thei spuyleden;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 1:7; 1 Samuel 30:1; Joshua 6:24"> flawme brente as wel citees, as litle townes and castels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Joshua 8:2"> And they token pray, and alle thingis whiche thei hadden take, as wel of men as of beestis, and thei brouyten to Moyses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 22:1"> and to Eleazar, preest, and to al the multitude of the sones of Israel. Forsothe thei baren othere &#96;thingis perteynynge to vss, to the castels in the feldi places of Moab bisidis Jordan, ayens Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 5:2; 1 Samuel 30:21; Numbers 31:12"> Moises and Eleazar, preest, and alle the princes of the synagoge, yeden out in to the comyng of hem, with out the castels, &#96;that is, of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 13:19; 1 Samuel 15:13–14; Numbers 31:48"> And Moises was wrooth to the princes of the oost, to tribunes, and centuriouns, that camen fro batel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:3; Jeremiah 48:10; Deuteronomy 2:34"> and he seide, Whi reserueden ye wymmen?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 25:1–9; 2 Peter 2:15; Revelation 2:14"> whether it be not these that disseyueden the sones of Israel, at the suggestioun of Balaam, and maden you to do trespas ayens the Lord, on the synne of Phegor, wherfor also the puple was slayn?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 21:11–12; Deuteronomy 20:16–18; Deuteronomy 7:2"> And therfor sle ye alle men, what euer thing is of male kynde, and litle children; and strangle ye the wymmen that knew men fleischli;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:10–14; 2 Chronicles 28:8–10; Deuteronomy 20:14"> forsothe reserue ye to you damesels, and alle wymmen virgyns,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 19:11–22; Numbers 5:2; 1 Chronicles 22:8"> and dwelle ye with out the castels in seuene daies. He that sleeth a man, ether touchith a slayn man, schal be clensid in the thridde and the seuenthe dai;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 19:22; Genesis 35:2; Exodus 19:10"> and of al the pray, whether it is clooth, ether vessel, and ony thing maad redi in to thingis perteynynge to vss, of the skynnys and heeris of geet, and &#96;of tre, it schal be clensid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 30:16"> And Eleazar, preest, spak thus to the men of the oost that fouyten, This is the comaundement of the lawe, which the Lord comaundide to Moises,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The gold, and siluer, and bras, and yrun, and tiyn, and leed, and al thing that may passe by flawme, schal be purgid bi fier;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 19:9; Numbers 19:17; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> sotheli what euer thing may not suffre fier, schal be halewid bi the watir of clensyng.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 11:25; Leviticus 15:13; Leviticus 14:9"> And ye schulen waische youre clothis in the seuenthe dai, and ye schulen be clensid; and aftirward ye schulen entre in to the castels &#96;of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And the Lord seide to Moises, Take ye the summe of tho thingis that ben takun, fro man &#96;til to beeste,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> thou, and Eleazar, preest, and alle the princes of the comyn puple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 22:8; Psalms 68:12; 1 Samuel 30:24–25"> And thou schalt departe euenli the prey bytwixe hem that fouyten and yeden out to batel, and bitwixe al the multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 18:26; Numbers 31:30; Numbers 31:47"> And thou schalt departe a part to the Lord, of hem that fouyten, and weren in batel, &#96;o soule of fiue hundrid, as wel of men, as of oxun, and of assis, and of scheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 29:27; Deuteronomy 12:19; Deuteronomy 12:12"> And thou schalt yyue &#96;that part to Eleazar, preest, for tho ben the firste fruytis of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 3:31; Numbers 3:7–8; Numbers 3:25"> Also of the myddil part of the sones of Israel, thou schalt take the fiftithe heed of men, and of oxun, and of assis, and of scheep, and of alle lyuynge beestis; and thou schalt yyue tho to the dekenes, that waken in the kepyngis of the tabernacle of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And Moyses and Eleazar diden, as the Lord comaundide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Forsothe the prey which the oost hadde take, was sixe hundrid fyue and seuenti thousynde of scheep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> of oxun two and seuenti thousynde,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> of assis sixti thousynde and a thousynde;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> the soules of persones of femal kynde, that knewen not fleischli men, two and thretti thousynde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And the myddil part was youun to hem that weren in the batel, of scheep thre hundrid seuene and thretti thousynde and fyue hundrid;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> of whiche sixe hundrid fyue and seuenti scheep weren noumbrid in to the part of the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> and of sixe and thretti thousynde oxun,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> two and seuenti oxun, and of thretti thousynde assis and fyue hundryd, oon and sixti assis;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> of sixtene thousynde persoones of men, twei and thretti persoones bifelden in to the &#96;part of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 18:8; Numbers 18:19; Hebrews 7:9–12"> And Moises bitook the noumbre of the firste fruytis of the Lord to Eleazar, preest, as it was comaundid to hym,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> of the myddil part of the sones of Israel, which he departide to hem that weren in batel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> And of the myddil part that bifelde to the tother multitude, that is, of thre hundrid seuene and thretti thousynde scheep and fyue hundrid,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> and of sixe and thretti thousynde oxun,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> and of thretti thousynde assis and fyue hundrid, and of sixtene thousynde wymmen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> Moyses took the fyftithe heed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Numbers 31:30; Luke 10:1–8; Isaiah 56:10–11"> and yaf to the dekenes, that wakiden in the tabernacle of the Lord, as the Lord comaundide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> And whanne the princes of the oost, and the tribunes and centuriouns hadden neiyed to Moises,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 18:9; 1 Samuel 30:18–19; Psalms 72:14"> thei seiden, We thi seruauntis han teld the noumbre of fiyters, whiche we hadden vndur oure hoond, and sotheli not oon failide;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Exodus 30:12; Exodus 30:15–16; Psalms 116:12"> for which cause we offren &#96;in the fre yiftis of the Lord, alle bi vs silf, that that we myyten fynde of gold in the pray, girdelis for &#96;the myddil of wymmen, and bies of the armes, and ryngis, and ournementis of the arm nyy the hond, and bies of the neckis of wymmen, that thou preye the Lord for vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 7:2–6"> And Moises and Eleazar, preest, token al the gold in dyuerse spices,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> &#96;ether kyndis, bi the weiyte of the seyntuarye, sixtene thousynde seuene hundrid and fifti siclis, of the tribunes, and centuriouns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Numbers 31:32"> For that that ech man rauyschide in the prey, was his owne;</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Exodus 30:16; Zechariah 6:14; Acts 10:4"> and thei baren the gold taken in to the tabernacle of witnessyng, in to the mynde of the sones of Israel, bifor the Lord.</span>
