<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spak to Moises, and seide, Speke thou to the sones of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 23:10; Numbers 15:18; Leviticus 25:2"> and thou schalt seie to hem, Whanne ye han entrid in to the lond of youre abitacioun which Y schal yyue to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 29:18; Genesis 8:21; Philippians 4:18"> and ye make an offryng to the Lord in to brent sacrifice, ether a pesible sacrifice, and ye payen auowis, ethir offren yiftis bi fre wille, ethir in youre solempnytees ye brennen odour of swetnesse to the Lord, of oxun, ether of scheep;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 29:40; Leviticus 23:13; Leviticus 6:14"> who euer offrith the slayn sacrifice, schal offre a sacrifice of flour, the tenthe part of ephi, spreynt togidere with oile, which oil schal haue a mesure the fourthe part of hyn;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 28:7; Numbers 28:14; Judges 9:13"> and he schal yyue wyn to fletynge sacrifices to be sched, of the same mesure, in to brent sacrifice, and slayn sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 28:12–14; Numbers 15:4"> Bi ech loomb and ram schal be the sacrifice of flour, of twey tenthe partis, which schal be spreynt togidere with oile, of the thridde part of hyn;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and he schal offre wyn to the fletynge sacrifice, of the thridde part of the same mesure, in to odour of swetnesse to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 3:1; Leviticus 7:11–18; Leviticus 1:3"> Forsothe whanne thou makist a brent sacrifice, ethir an offryng of oxun, that thou fille avow, ethir pesible sacrifice, thou schalt yyue,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 28:14; Numbers 28:12; Leviticus 6:14"> bi ech oxe, thre tenthe partis of flour, spreynt togidere with oile, which schal haue the half of mesure of hyn;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 15:5; Numbers 6:15"> and thou schalt yyue wyn to fletynge sacrifices to be sched, of the same mesure, in to offryng of the swettest odour to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> So ye schulen do bi ech oxe, and ram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and lomb, and kide;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> as wel men borun in the lond,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> as pilgrymys,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="that is, men conuerted to the lawe of Jewis.">a</a></sup>schulen offre sacrifices bi the same custom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 9:14; Numbers 15:29; Exodus 12:49"> o comaundement and doom schal be, as wel to you as to comelyngis of the lond.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the Lord spak to Moises,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> and seide, Speke thou to the sones of Israel, and thou schalt seie to hem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 15:2; Deuteronomy 26:1–15"> Whanne ye comen in to the lond which Y schal yyue to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 5:11–12"> and &#96;ye eten of the looues of that cuntrey, ye</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 2:14; Exodus 34:26; Deuteronomy 26:2–10"> schulen departe the firste fruytis<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="the firste fruytis: in Ebreu it is a litil cake of youre pastis.">b</a></sup>of youre metis to the Lord; as ye schulen departe the firste fruytis of corn flooris,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 29:28; Numbers 18:26"> so ye schulen yyue the firste fruytis also of sewis to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 4:2; Leviticus 4:27; Leviticus 5:15–17"> That if bi ignoraunce ye passen ony of tho thingis whiche the Lord spak to Moyses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and comaundide bi hym to you, fro the dai in which he bigan to comaunde,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 4:23; Leviticus 4:13–21; Leviticus 5:15"> and ouer, and the multitude hath foryete to do, it schal offre a calf of the drooue, brent sacrifice in to swettist odour to the Lord, and the sacrificis therof, and fletynge offryngis, as the cerymonyes therof axen; and it schal offre a &#96;buc of geet for synne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 4:20; Romans 3:25; Hebrews 2:17"> And the preest schal preie for al the multitude of the sones of Israel, and it schal be foryouun to hem, for thei synneden not wilfuli. And neuerthelesse thei schulen offre encense to the Lord for hemsilf, and for her synne and errour;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 15:24"> and it schal be foryouun to al the puple of the sones of Israel, and to comelyngis that ben pilgryms among hem, for it is the synne of al the multitude bi ignoraunce.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 4:27–28; Acts 17:30; Acts 3:17"> That if a soule synneth vnwityngli, it schal offre a geet of o yeer for his synne; and the preest schal preye for that soule, for it synnede vnwityngli bifor the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 4:35"> and the preest schal gete foryyuenesse to it, and synne schal be foryouun to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 15:15; Leviticus 16:29; Leviticus 17:15"> As wel to men borun in the lond as to comelyngis, o lawe schal be of alle that synnen vnwityngli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26; Psalms 19:13; Deuteronomy 1:43"> Forsothe a man that doith ony synne bi pride, schal perische fro his puple, whether he be a citeseyn, ethir a pilgrym, for he was rebel ayens the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:9; Proverbs 13:13; Ezekiel 18:20"> for he dispiside the word of the Lord, and made voide his comaundement; therfor he schal be doon awei, and schal bere his owne wickidnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 35:2–3; Exodus 31:14–15; Exodus 20:8–10"> Forsothe it was doon, whanne the sones of Israel weren in wildirnesse, and hadde founde a man gaderynge woode in the &#96;day of sabat,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 8:3–20"> thei brouyten hym to Moises, and to Aaron, and to al the multitude; whiche closiden hym in to prisoun,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Leviticus 24:12; Numbers 9:8"> and wisten<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="and wisten not, etc. they wisten not bi what deth he schulde die, ether wher he dide this synue witingly, til God certifiede to hem.">c</a></sup>not what thei schulden do of hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 7:58; Exodus 31:14–15; 1 Kings 21:13"> And the Lord seide to Moises, This man die bi deeth; al the cumpeny oppresse hym with stoonus with out the tentis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Joshua 7:25"> And whanne thei hadden led hym with out forth, thei oppressiden him with stoonus, and he was deed, as the Lord comaundide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Also the Lord seide to Moises,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 23:5; Deuteronomy 22:12; Matthew 9:20"> Speke thou to the sones of Israel, and thou schalt seye to hem, that thei make to hem hemmes bi foure corneris of mentils, and sette laces of iacynct &#96;in tho;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 106:39; Proverbs 28:26; Psalms 73:27"> and whanne thei seen thoo, haue thei mynde of alle comaundementis of the Lord, lest thei suen her thouytis and iyen, doynge fornycacioun bi dyuerse thingis;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Romans 12:1; 1 Thessalonians 4:7; Leviticus 11:44–45"> but more be thei myndeful of the &#96;Lordis heestis, and do thei tho, and be thei hooli to her God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9–10; Ezekiel 36:25–27; Hebrews 11:16"> Y am youre Lord God, which ledde you out of the lond of Egipt, that Y schulde be youre God.</span>
