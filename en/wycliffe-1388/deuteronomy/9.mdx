<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:31; Deuteronomy 4:38; Deuteronomy 1:28"> Here thou, Israel; thou schalt passe Jordan to dai, that thou welde mooste naciouns, and strengere than thou; grete citees, and wallid &#96;til to heuene;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 13:22; Nahum 1:6; Deuteronomy 7:24"> a greet puple, and hiy; the sones of Enachym, whiche thi silf &#96;siest, and herdist, whiche no man may ayenstonde in the contrarie part.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:24; Hebrews 12:29; Romans 8:31"> Therfor thou schalt wite to dai, that thi Lord God hym silf schal passe bifor thee; he is a fier deuourynge and wastynge, that schal al to breke hem, and schal do awei, and destrie bifor thi face swiftli, as he spak to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:17; Deuteronomy 9:5; Deuteronomy 18:9–14"> Seie thou not in thin herte, whanne thi Lord God hath do hem awey in thi siyt, For my riytfulnesse the Lord brouyte me yn, that Y schulde welde this lond; sithen these naciouns ben doon awey for her wickidnessis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Titus 3:5; Genesis 17:8; Genesis 26:4"> For not for thi riytfulnessis, and equyte of thin herte thou schalt entre that thou welde the lond &#96;of hem; but for thei diden wickidli, thei weren doon awey, whanne thou entridist, and that the Lord schulde fille his word which he bihiyte vndur an ooth to thi fadris, to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:16; Acts 7:51; Deuteronomy 31:27"> Therfor wite thou that not for thi riytfulnesses thi Lord God yaf to thee this beste lond in to possessioun, sithen thou art a puple of hardeste nol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 16:2; Exodus 14:11; Numbers 21:5"> Haue thou mynde, and foryete not, hou in the wildirnesse thou terridist thi Lord God to greet wraththe; fro that dai in which thou yedist out of Egipt &#96;til to this place, thou striuedist euere ayens the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 106:19–22; Exodus 32:16; Exodus 32:7–10"> For whi also in Oreb thou terridist hym, and he was wrooth, and wolde do thee awei, whanne Y stiede in to the hil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 34:28; Exodus 24:18; 1 Kings 19:8"> that Y schulde take two tablis of stoon, the tablis of couenaunt which the Lord made with you, and Y continuede in the hil fourti daies and nyytis, and Y eet not breed, and Y drank not watir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 31:18; Hebrews 8:10; Deuteronomy 10:4"> And the Lord yaf to me, twey tablis of stoon, euer either wrytun with Goddis fyngur, and conteynynge alle the wordis whiche he spak to you in the hil, fro the myddis of the fier, whanne the cumpany of puple was gaderid togidere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:9; Numbers 10:33; Hebrews 8:6–10"> And whanne fourti daies and so many nyytis hadden passid, the Lord yaf to me twei tablis of stoon, tablis of boond of pees;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 32:7–8; Deuteronomy 31:29; Judges 2:17"> and he seide to me, Rise thou, and go doun for hennys soone, for thi puple, which thou leddist out of Egipt, han forsake swiftli the weie which thou schewidist to hem, and thei han maad to hem a yotun calf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:6; 2 Kings 17:14; Deuteronomy 10:16"> And eft the Lord seide to me, &#96;Y se that this puple is of hard nol;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:20; Exodus 32:10–13; Psalms 9:5"> suffre thou me, that I alto breke hym, and do awey the name &#96;of hym fro vndur heuene; and Y schal ordeyne thee on a folk which is grettere and strongere than this folk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 19:18; Deuteronomy 5:23; Deuteronomy 4:11"> And whanne Y cam doun fro the hil brennynge, and helde with euer either hond twei tablis of boond of pees, and Y seiy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 32:19; Acts 7:40–41"> that ye hadde synned to youre Lord God, and hadden maad to you a yotun calf, and hadden forsake swiftli the weie of God which he schewide to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Y castide doun the tablis fro myn hondis, and brak tho tablis in youre siyt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 34:28; Deuteronomy 9:9; Psalms 106:23"> And Y felde doun bifor the Lord as &#96;biforto, in fourti daies and fourti nyytis, and Y eet not breed, &#96;and drank not watir, for alle youre synnes whiche ye diden ayens the Lord, and terriden hym to &#96;greet wraththe;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:10; Exodus 32:14; James 5:16–17"> for Y dredde the indignacioun and yre of hym, by which he was stirid ayens you, and wolde do you awey. And the Lord herde me also in this tyme.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 32:35; Exodus 32:21; Hebrews 7:26–28"> Also the Lord was wrooth greteli ayens Aaron, and wolde alto breke hym, and Y preiede in lijk maner for hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 32:20; Isaiah 31:7; Isaiah 2:18–21"> &#96;Forsothe Y took youre synne which ye maden, that is, the calf, and brente it in fier, and Y alto brak in gobetis, and droof outerli in to dust, and castide forth in to the stronde, that cam doun fro the hil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 11:34; Exodus 17:7; Numbers 11:1–5"> Also in the brennyng, and in the temptacioun at the watris of ayenseiyng, and in the Sepulcris of Coueytise, ye terriden the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 106:24–25; Hebrews 4:2; Psalms 78:22"> and whanne Y sente you fro Cades Barne, and seide, &#96;Stye ye, and welde the lond which Y yaf to you, and ye dispisiden the comaundement of youre Lord God, and ye bileueden not to him, nether ye wolden here his vois;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:27; Acts 7:51; Deuteronomy 9:6–7"> but euere ye weren rebel, fro the day in which Y bigan to knowe you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:18; Deuteronomy 9:16"> And Y lay byfore the Lord fourti daies and fourti nyytis, in whiche Y bisouyte hym mekeli, that he schulde not &#96;do awey you, as he manaasside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 32:11–13; Titus 2:14; Psalms 107:2"> And Y preiede, and seide, Lord God, distrye not thi puple, and thin eritage, which thou &#96;ayen bouytist in thi greetnesse, which thou leddist out of Egipt in strong hond.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:21; Micah 7:18–19; 1 Samuel 25:25"> Haue thou mynde of thi seruauntis, of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob; biholde thou not the hardnesse of this puple, and the wickidnesse, and the synne therof,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 14:16; Exodus 32:12"> lest perauenture the dwelleris of the lond, out of which thou leddist vs, seien, The Lord myyte not bryng hem in to the lond which he bihiyte to hem, and he hatide hem; therfor he ledde hem out that he schulde sle hem in wildirnesse;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:10; 1 Kings 8:51; Deuteronomy 9:26"> and thei ben thi puple and thin eritage, which thou leddist out in thi greet strengthe, and in thin arm holdun forth.</span>
