<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 21:5; Romans 9:8; Galatians 3:26"> Be ye the sones of youre Lord God; ye schulen not kitte you,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="kitte you, in Ebreu it is, ye schulen not to-reende you.">a</a></sup>nether ye schulen make ballidnesse,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6; Leviticus 20:26; Exodus 19:5–6"> on a deed man, for thou art an hooli puple to thi Lord God, and he chees thee that thou be to hym in to a special puple, of alle folkis that ben on erthe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:14; Romans 14:14; Leviticus 20:25"> Ete ye not tho thingis that ben vncleene.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 11:2–45; Acts 10:14; 1 Kings 4:23"> This is a beeste which ye schulen ete; an oxe, and a scheep, and a goet, an hert,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> a capret, a &#96;wielde oxe, tregelafun, &#96;that is, a beeste in parti lijk &#96;a buk of geet, and in parti liik an hert, a figarde, an ostrich, a camelioun, &#96;that is, a beeste lijk in the heed to a camel, and hath white spottis in the bodi as a parde, and &#96;is lijk an hors in the necke, and in the feet is lijc a &#96;wilde oxe, and a parde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 18:1; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Psalms 1:1–2"> Ye schulen ete ech beeste that departith the clee &#96;in to twei partis, and chewith code.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:22–23; Titus 1:16; 2 Timothy 3:5"> Sotheli ye schulen not ete these beestis, of these that chewen code, and departen not the clee; a camel, an hare, and a cirogrille, &#96;that is, a beeste ful of prickis, and is more than an irchoun; for tho chewen code, and departen not the clee, tho schulen be vncleene to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 11:26–27; Isaiah 66:3; 2 Peter 2:22"> also a swyn, for it departith the clee, and chewith not code, schal be vncleene; ye schulen not ete the fleischis of tho, and ye schulen not touche the deed bodies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 11:9–12"> Ye schulen ete these thingis, of alle that dwellen in watris; ete ye tho thingis that han fynnes and scalis;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> ete ye not tho thingis that ben with out fynnes and scalis, for tho ben vncleene.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Ete ye alle clene briddis;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 11:13–19"> ete ye not vncleene briddis, that is, an egle, and a gripe,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and an aliete, ixon, &#96;that is, a whijt brid lesse than a vultur, and is of the &#96;kynde of vultris, and a vultur, and a kite bi his kynde,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and al thing of rauenys kynde,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 30:29"> and a strucioun, and a nyyt crowe, and a lare,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> and an hauk bi his kynde, a fawcun,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> and a swan, and a siconye, and a dippere, a pursirioun, and a reremous, a cormeraunt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and a caladrie, alle in her kynde; also a lapwynke and a backe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philippians 3:19; Leviticus 11:20–23"> And al thing that crepith, and hath fynnes, schal be vncleene, and schal not be etun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Ete ye al thing that is cleene; sotheli what euer thing is deed bi it silf, ete ye not therof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 23:19; Exodus 34:26; Leviticus 22:8"> Yyue thou to the pilgrym which is with ynne thi yatis, that he ete, ether sille thou to hym, for thou art the hooli puple of thi Lord God. Thou schalt not sethe a kyde in &#96;the mylk of his modir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:37; Deuteronomy 12:17; Deuteronomy 26:12–15"> Thou schalt departe the tenthe part of alle thi fruytis that comen forth in the lond bi ech yeer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5–7; Deuteronomy 4:10; Deuteronomy 12:17–18"> and thou schal ete in the siyt of thi Lord God, in the place which he chees, that his name be clepid therynne; thou schalt offre the tithe of thi wheete, wyn, and oile, and the firste gendryd thingis of thi droues, and scheep, that thou lerne to drede thi Lord God in al tyme.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:21; Deuteronomy 12:5; Deuteronomy 11:24"> Sotheli whanne the wei is lengere, and the place which thi Lord God chees is fer, and he hath blessid thee, and thou maist not bere alle these thingis to that place,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> thou schalt sille alle thingis, and schalt turne in to prijs, and thou schalt bere in thin hond, and thou schalt go to the place which thi Lord God chees;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:7; Deuteronomy 12:7; Matthew 21:12"> and thou schalt bie of the same money what euer thing plesith to thee, ethir of droues, ether of scheep; also thou schalt bie wyn, and sidur, and al thing that thi soule desirith; and thou schalt ete bifor thi Lord God, and thou schalt make feeste,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 18:20; Deuteronomy 14:29; Deuteronomy 12:12"> thou, and thin hows, and the dekene which is withynne thi yatis; be thou war lest thou forsake hym, for he hath not other part in possessioun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:22; Amos 4:4; Deuteronomy 26:12–15"> In the thridde yeer thou schalt departe another dyme of alle thingis that growen to thee in that yeer, and thou schalt kepe withynne thi yatis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:10; Deuteronomy 14:27; Proverbs 19:17"> And the dekene schal come, whych hath noon other part nether possessioun with thee, and the pilgrym, and the fadirles, ether modirles child, and widue, that ben withynne thi yatis, &#96;schulen come, and schulen ete, and be fillid, that thi Lord God blesse thee, in alle werkis of thin hondis whiche thou schalt do.</span>
