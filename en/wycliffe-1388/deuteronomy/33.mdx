<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 10:39; Malachi 1:2; Deuteronomy 7:6–8"> He louede puplis; alle seyntis ben in his hond, and thei that neiyen to hise feet schulen take of his doctryn.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 17:7; Exodus 28:30; Numbers 20:13"> Also he seide to Leuy, Thi perfeccioun and thi techyng is of an hooly man, whom thou preuedist in temptacioun, and demedist at the Watris of Ayenseiynge;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; Exodus 32:25–29; Matthew 12:48"> which Leuy seide to his fadir and to his modir, Y knowe not you, and to hise britheren, Y knowe not hem; and knewen not her sones. These kepten thi speche, and these kepten thi couenaunt; A!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 10:11; Psalms 51:19; Deuteronomy 17:9–11"> Jacob, thei kepten thi domes, and &#96;thou, Israel, thei kepten thi lawe; thei schulen putte encense in thi strong veniaunce,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="putte encense in thi strong veniaunce, that is, to turn awey thi strong veniaunce fro thi peple.">b</a></sup>and brent sacrifice on thin auter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:23; Ezekiel 43:27; Ezekiel 20:40–41"> Lord, blesse thou the strengthe of hym, and resseyue thou the werkis of his hondis; smyte thou the backis of hise enemyes, and thei that haten hym, rise not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; Psalms 91:4; Matthew 23:37"> And he seide to Benjamyn, The moost loued of the Lord schal dwelle tristili in hym, &#96;that is, in the Lord; he schal dwelle al day as in a chaumbur, and he schal reste bitwixe the schuldris of hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 49:22–26; Micah 5:7; Isaiah 18:4"> Also he seide to Joseph, &#96;His lond is of the Lordis blessyng; of the applis of heuene, and of the dewe, and of watir liggynge bynethe;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 104:19; Matthew 5:45; Psalms 8:3"> of the applis of fruytis of the sunne and moone; of the coppe of elde munteyns,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:6; Genesis 49:26; James 5:7"> and of the applis of euerlastynge litle hillis;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 7:35; Psalms 24:1; Exodus 3:2–4"> and of the fruytis of the lond, and of the fulnesse therof. The blessyng of hym that apperide in the busch come on the heed of Joseph, and on the cop of Nazarey, &#96;that is, hooli, among hise britheren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 22:11; Numbers 23:22; Psalms 44:5"> As the first gendrid of a bole is the feirnesse of hym; the hornes of an vnicorn ben the hornes of hym; in tho he schal wyndewe folkis, &#96;til to the termes of erthe. These ben the multitudis of Effraym, and these ben the thousyndis of Manasses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 49:13–15; Joshua 19:11; Judges 5:14"> And he seide to Zabulon, Zabulon, be thou glad in thi goyng out, and, Ysacar, in thi tabernaclis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 60:5; Isaiah 2:3; Psalms 4:5"> Thei schulen clepe puplis to the hil, there thei schulen offre sacrifices of riytfulnesse; whiche schulen souke the flowing of the see as mylk, and hid tresours of grauel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 49:19; Genesis 9:26–27; 1 Chronicles 5:18–21"> And he seide to Gad, Gad is blessid in broodnesse; he restide as a lioun, and he took the arm and the nol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 32:16–42; Judges 5:11; Judges 5:2"> And he siy his prinshed, that &#96;the techere was kept in his part; which Gad was with the princes of the puple, and dide the riytfulnesses of the Lord, and his doom with Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 18:27; Joshua 19:47; Judges 15:8"> Also he seide to Dan, Dan, a whelp of a lioun, schal flowe largeli fro Basan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 49:21; Psalms 36:8; Matthew 4:16"> And he seide to Neptalym, Neptalym schal vse abundaunce, and he schal be ful with blessyngis of the Lord; and he schal welde the see<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="he schal welde the see, etc.; for the see of Galilee, as to the south part therof, felde in the lot of Neptalym.">c</a></sup>and the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 49:20; Job 29:6; Romans 14:18"> Also he seide to Aser, Aser, be blessid in sones, and plese he hise britheren; dippe he his foot in oile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Psalms 138:3; Philippians 4:13"> Yrun and bras the scho of hym; as the dai of thi youthe so and thin eelde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 15:11; Isaiah 43:11–13; Jeremiah 10:6"> Noon other god is as the God of the moost riytful, that is, &#96;as the God &#96;of the puple of Israel, gouerned bi moost riytful lawe; the stiere of heuene is thin helpere; cloudis<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="cloudis. In Ebrew it is, heuenes and armes of the world.">d</a></sup>rennen aboute bi the glorie of hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 18:2; Psalms 90:1–2; Psalms 46:1"> His dwellynge place is aboue, and armes euerlastynge ben bynethe; he schal caste out fro thi face the enemy, and he schal seie, Be thou al to-brokun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 23:9; Genesis 27:28; Exodus 33:16"> Israel schal dwelle trustili and aloone; the iye of Jacob in the lond of whete, and of wyn; and heuenes schulen be derk with dew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 45:17; Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 115:9–11"> Blessed art thou, Israel; thou puple that art saued in the Lord, who is lijk thee?
