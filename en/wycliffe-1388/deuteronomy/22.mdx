<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> hir, and thei schulen bere with hem the signes of her virgynyte to the eldre men of the citee, that ben in the yate;</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> that he seye, Y foond not thi douytir virgyn; and lo! these ben the signes of virgynyte of my douytir; thei schulen sprede forth a cloth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="sprede forth clooth; that is, as Ebrews seyen, thei schulen declare the virgynete of her douyter bi the wordis of witnessis.">c</a></sup>bifor the eldre men of the citee. <span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> That if it is soth, that he puttith ayens hir, and virgynyte is not foundun in the damysel, thei schulen caste hir &#96;out of &#96;the yatis of</span>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 1:18–19; Deuteronomy 20:7"> If a man spousith a damysel virgyn, and a man fyndith hir in the citee, and doith letcherie with hir,</span>

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:14"> Forsothe if a man fyndith in the feeld a &#96;damysel, which is spousid, and he takith, and doith letcherie with hir, he aloone schal die;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:22"> the damysel schal suffre no thing of yuel, nethir is gilti of deeth; for as a theef risith ayens his brothir, and sleeth &#96;his lijf, so and the damysel suffride; sche was aloone in the feeld,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:7"> sche criede, and noon was present, that schulde delyuer hir.</span>
