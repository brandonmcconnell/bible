<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:5; Deuteronomy 10:12; Luke 1:74–75"> Therfor loue thi Lord God, and kepe thou hise comaundementis and cerymonyes, domes and heestis, in al tyme.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:2–5; Deuteronomy 29:10; Acts 26:22"> Knowe ye to day tho thingis whiche youre sones knowen not, &#96;whiche sones sien not the doctryn of youre Lord God, hise grete dedis, and strong hond, and &#96;arm holdun forth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:19; Deuteronomy 4:34; Psalms 105:27–45"> myraclis and werkis, whiche he dide &#96;in the myddis of Egipt to Farao, kyng, and to al &#96;the lond of hym, and to al the oost of Egipcians,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 106:11; Exodus 14:23–31; Exodus 15:19"> and to horsis, and carris; hou the watris of the reed see hiliden hem, whanne thei pursueden you, and the Lord &#96;dide awei hem &#96;til in to &#96;present dai;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 77:20; Psalms 106:12–48; Psalms 78:14–72"> and whiche thingis the Lord dide to you in wildernesse, til ye camen to this place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 26:9–10; Psalms 106:17; Numbers 16:1–35"> and to Dathan and Abiron, &#96;the sones of Heliab, that was &#96;the sone of Ruben, whiche the erthe swolewide, whanne his mouth was openyd, with &#96;the housis and tabernaclis, and al the catel &#96;of hem which thei hadden, in the myddis of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 145:4–6; Psalms 145:12; Psalms 150:2"> Youre iyen sien alle the grete werkis of the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 6:10; Joshua 1:6–7; Ephesians 3:16"> whiche he dide, that ye kepe alle hise heestis whiche Y comaunde to dai to you, and that ye moun entre, and welde the lond,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Deuteronomy 4:40; Deuteronomy 6:2"> to which ye schulen entre, and ye lyue therynne in myche time; which lond, flowynge with mylk and hony, the Lord bihiyte vndur an ooth to youre fadris and to &#96;the seed of hem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 14:18"> For the lond, to which thou schalt entre to welde, is not as the lond of Egipt, &#96;out of which thou yedist, where whanne the seed is cast in the maner of gardyns, moist waters ben led;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 6:7; Deuteronomy 8:7–9; Psalms 65:12–13"> but it is hilli, and feldi, and abidith reynes fro heuene,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:6; Psalms 33:18; 1 Kings 9:3"> which lond thi Lord God biholdith, and hise iyen ben therynne, fro the bigynnyng of the yeer &#96;til to the ende therof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:12; Deuteronomy 4:29; Deuteronomy 6:17"> Therfor if ye schulen obeie to myn heestis whiche Y comaunde to dai to you, that ye loue youre Lord God, and serue hym in al youre herte, and in al youre soule;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 5:7; Leviticus 26:4; Ezekiel 34:26"> he schal yyue to youre lond reyn tymeful and late, that ye gadere wheete, and wyn, and oile,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 104:14; Deuteronomy 6:11; Joel 2:19"> hey of the feeldis to feede beestis, that ye bothe ete and be fillid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 31:27; Hebrews 3:12; Hebrews 2:1"> Be ye war, lest perauenture youre herte be disseyued, and ye go awei fro the Lord, and serue alien goddis, and worschipe hem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 8:35; Deuteronomy 6:15; 2 Chronicles 6:26"> and the Lord be wrooth, and close heuene, and reynes come not doun, nether the erthe yyue his fruyt, and ye perische swiftli fro the beste lond which the Lord schal yyue to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; Deuteronomy 6:6–9; Proverbs 3:1"> Putte ye thes wordis in youre hertes and soules, and honge ye &#96;tho wordis for a signe in the hondis, and sette ye bitwixe youre iyen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Isaiah 38:19; Deuteronomy 4:9–10"> Teche youre sones, that thei thenke on tho wordis, whanne thou sittist in thin hows, and goist in the weie, and lyggist doun, and risist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:9"> Thou schalt write tho wordis on the postis, and yatis of thin hous,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 4:10; Proverbs 3:2; Proverbs 9:11"> that the daies of thee and of thi sones be multiplied in the lond which the Lord swoor to thi fadris, that he schulde yyue to hem, as long as heuene is aboue erthe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:17; Deuteronomy 11:13; Deuteronomy 10:20"> For if ye kepen the heestis whiche Y comaunde to you, and ye do tho, that ye loue youre Lord God, and go in alle hise weies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:38; Deuteronomy 9:1; Deuteronomy 7:1–2"> and cleue to hym, the Lord schal destrie alle these hethen men bifor youre face, and ye schulen welde tho folkis that ben grettere and strongere than ye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 14:9; Exodus 23:31; Genesis 15:18–21"> Ech place which youre foot schal trede, schal be youre; fro the deseert, and fro the Liban, and fro the greet flood Eufrates &#96;til to the west see, schulen be youre termes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:24; Exodus 23:27; Joshua 1:5"> Noon schal stonde ayens you; youre Lord God schal yiue youre outward drede and inward drede on ech lond which ye schulen trede, as he spak to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:1; Deuteronomy 30:15–20; Galatians 3:10"> Lo! Y sette forth in youre siyt to day blissyng and cursyng;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 1:19; Deuteronomy 28:1–14; Isaiah 3:10"> blessyng, if ye obeien to the heestis of youre Lord God, whiche Y comaunde to you to dai;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Romans 2:8–9; Galatians 3:10"> cursyng, if ye heren not the heestis of youre Lord God, but goen awei fro the weie which Y schewe now to you, and goen after alien goddis whiche ye knowen not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:12–26; Joshua 8:30–35"> Sotheli whanne thi Lord God hath brouyt thee in to the lond, to which to enhabite thou goist, thou schalt sette blessyng on the hil Garisym, cursyng on the hil Hebal, whiche hillis ben biyende Jordan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 12:6; Judges 7:1; Joshua 4:19"> aftir the weie that goith to the goyng doun of the sunne, in the lond of Cananey, that dwellith in the feeldi places ayens Galgala, which is bisidis the valey goynge and entrynge fer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 1:11; Deuteronomy 9:1; Joshua 3:13–17"> For ye schulen passe Jordan, that ye welde the lond which youre Lord God schal yyue to you, and that ye haue and welde that lond.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 28:20; Deuteronomy 12:32; 1 Thessalonians 4:1–2"> Therfor se ye, &#96;that ye fille the cerymonyes and domes, whiche I schal sette to dai in youre siyt.</span>
