<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 18:8–9; Deuteronomy 10:9; 1 Corinthians 9:13–14"> Preestis and dekenes, and alle men that ben of the same lynage, schulen &#96;not haue part and eritage with the tother puple of Israel, for thei schulen ete the sacrifices of the Lord, and the offryngis of hym;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 1:5–6; Psalms 84:11; 1 Peter 2:5"> and thei schulen not take ony othir thing of the possessioun of her britheren; for the Lord hym silf is the &#96;eritage of hem, as he spak to hem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 7:30–34; Deuteronomy 12:27"> This schal be the doom<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="that is, the thing iustly youun ether grauntid.">a</a></sup>of preestis of the puple, and of hem that offren sacrifices; whether &#96;thei offren an oxe, ether a scheep, thei schulen yyue to the preest the schuldre, and the paunche, the firste fruytis of wheete,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:44–47; Numbers 18:12–24; 2 Chronicles 31:4–10"> and of wyn, and of oile, and a part of wollis of the scheryng of scheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:8; Numbers 3:10; Exodus 28:1–14"> For thi Lord God chees hym of alle thi lynagis, that he stonde and mynystre to &#96;the name of the Lord, he and hise sones, with outen ende.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 35:2–3; Deuteronomy 12:5; Deuteronomy 16:2"> If a dekene goith out of oon of thi citees of al Israel, in which he dwellith, &#96;and wole come and desirith the place which the Lord chees,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:2–4"> he schal mynystre in the name of his Lord God as alle hise britheren dekenes, that schulen stonde in that tyme byfore the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:44; Nehemiah 12:47; 2 Chronicles 31:4"> He schal take the same part of meetis, &#96;which and othere dekenes schulen take; outakun that that is due to hym in his citee, bi &#96;successioun ethir eritage &#96;of fadir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:29–31; Leviticus 18:26–27; Leviticus 18:30"> Whanne thou hast entrid in to the lond which thi Lord God schal yyue to thee, be thou war lest thou wole sue abhomynaciouns of tho folkis;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; Exodus 22:18; Leviticus 19:26"> noon be foundun in thee that clensith his sone, ether his douytir, &#96;and ledith bi the fier, ethir that axith questiouns of dyuynouris &#96;that dyuynen aboute the auteris, and that taketh hede to dremes and chiteryng of bryddis; nethir ony wicche be,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; 1 Samuel 28:11–14"> nethir an enchauntere, &#96;that is, that disseyueth mennus iyen that a thing seme that is not; nether a man take counsel at hem that han a feend spekynge &#96;in the wombe, nether take counsel at false dyuynouris nethir seke of deed men the treuthe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 18:24; Deuteronomy 9:4; Leviticus 18:27"> For the Lord hath abhomynacioun of alle these thingis, and for siche wickidnessis he schal do awei hem in thin entryng.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 5:48; Genesis 17:1; Genesis 6:9"> Thou schalt be perfit and without filthe, with thi Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 14:16; Deuteronomy 18:10; Genesis 20:6"> These hethen men, &#96;the lond of whiche thou schalt welde, heren hem that worchen bi chiteryng of briddis, and false dyuynouris; forsothe thou art tauyt in other maner of thi Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 3:22–23; Acts 7:37; John 1:45"> Thi Lord God schal reise a prophete of thi folk and of thi britheren as me, thou schalt here hym;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 20:19; Deuteronomy 5:23–28; Deuteronomy 9:10"> as thou axidist of thi Lord God in Oreb, whanne the cumpany was gaderid, and thou seidist, Y schal no more here the vois of my Lord God, and Y schal no more se &#96;this grettiste fier, lest Y die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:28"> And the Lord seide to me, Thei spaken wel alle thingis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15; John 8:28; John 12:49–50"> Y schal reise to hem a prophete, lijk thee, of the myddis of her britheren, and Y schal putte my wordis in his mouth, and he schal speke to hem alle thingis, whiche I schal comaunde to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 3:22–23; Hebrews 2:3; Hebrews 10:26"> Forsothe Y schal be vengere of &#96;that man, that nyle here the wordis &#96;of hym, whiche he schal speke in my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:1–5; Jeremiah 27:15; Jeremiah 23:13–15"> &#96;Sotheli a prophete &#96;schal be slayn, which is bischrewid with pride, and wole speke in my name tho thingis, whiche Y comaundide not to hym, that he schulde seie, ethir bi the name of alien goddis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 4:1–3; 1 Thessalonians 5:24; Revelation 2:2"> That if thou answerist bi pryuy thouyt, Hou may Y vndirstonde the word, which the Lord spak not? thou schalt haue this signe,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="to knowe a false prophete fro a trewe prophete.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:20; 2 Kings 20:1; Jeremiah 28:1–17"> &#96;The Lord spak not this thing which thilke prophete biforseid in the name of the Lord, &#96;and it bifallith not, but &#96;the prophete feynede bi the pride of his soule, and therfor thou schalt not drede hym.</span>
