<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 21:4; Deuteronomy 1:40; Numbers 14:25"> And we yeden forth fro thennus, and camen in to the wildirnesse that ledith to the Reed See, as the Lord seide to me; and we cumpassiden the hil of Seir in long tyme.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And the Lord seide to me, It sufficith to you to cumpasse this hil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:6; Deuteronomy 2:14; Deuteronomy 2:7"> go ye ayens the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 20:14–21; Exodus 15:15; Deuteronomy 23:7"> And comaunde thou to the puple, and seie, Ye schulen passe bi the termes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="bi the termes, that is, nyy the termes.">a</a></sup>of youre britheren, the sones of Esau, that dwellen in Seir, and thei schulen drede you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 36:8; Joshua 24:4; Jeremiah 27:5"> Therfor se ye diligentli, that ye be not moued ayens hem; for Y schal not yyue to you of the land &#96;of hem as myche as the steppe of o foot may trede, for Y yaf the hil of Seir in to the possessioun of Esau.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 7:12; Numbers 20:19; Romans 12:17"> Ye schulden bie of hem metis for money, and ye schulen ete; ye schulden drawe, and drynke watir bouyt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 22:35; Nehemiah 9:21; Deuteronomy 8:2–4"> Thi Lord God blesside thee in al the werk of thin hondis; he knewe thi weye, hou thou passidist this moste wildirnesse, bi fourti yeer; and thi Lord God dwellide with thee, and no thing failide to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 9:26; Judges 11:18; 2 Kings 16:6"> And whanne we hadden passid bi oure britheren, the sones of Esau, that dwelliden in Seir, bi the weie of the feeld of Elath, and of Asiongaber, we camen to the weie that ledith in to deseert of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 19:36–37; Numbers 21:15; Numbers 21:28"> And the Lord seide to me, Fiyte thou not ayens Moabitis, nether bigyn thou batel ayens hem, for Y schal not yyue to thee ony thing of the lond &#96;of hem, for Y yaf Ar in to possessioun to &#96;the sones of Loth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 14:5; Deuteronomy 2:11; Numbers 13:22"> Emyn, &#96;that is, griseful men, weren first dwelleris therof, a greet puple, and strong, and so hiy, that thei weren bileued as giantis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; Numbers 13:28; Genesis 14:5"> of the generacioun of Enachym, and thei weren lijk the sones of Enachym; forsothe Moabitis clepen hem Emyn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:22; Genesis 14:6; Deuteronomy 2:32–11"> Forsothe Horreis dwelliden bifore in Seir, and whanne thei weren put out, and weren doon awey, &#96;the sones of Esau dwelliden there, as Israel dide in the lond of his possessioun, which the Lord yaf to hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 21:12; Numbers 13:23"> Therfor we riseden, that we schulden passe the stronde of Zared, and camen to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 26:64–65; Numbers 14:28–35; Deuteronomy 1:34–35"> Sotheli the tyme in whiche we yeden fro Cades Barne &#96;til to the passynge of the stronde of Zared, was of eiyte and thretti yeer, til al the generacioun of &#96;men fiyteris was wastid fro &#96;the castels, as the Lord hadde swore; whos hond was ayens hem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 106:26; Isaiah 66:14; Psalms 32:4"> that thei schulden perische fro the myddis of &#96;the castels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Forsothe after that alle the fiyteris felden doun,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> the Lord spak to me, and seide,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 21:23; Isaiah 15:1; Numbers 21:15"> Thou schalt passe to dai the termes of Moab,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:9; Judges 11:13–27; 2 Chronicles 20:10"> the cytee, Ar bi name, and thou schalt neiy in the nyy coost of the sones of Amon; be thou war that thou fiyte not ayens hem, nether be moued to batel; for Y schal not yyue to thee of the lond of the sones of Amon, for Y yaf it to the &#96;sones of Loth in to possessioun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 14:5"> It is arettid the lond of giauntis, and giauntis enhabitiden therynne sumtyme, whiche giauntis Amonytis clepen Zonym;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:7–8; Deuteronomy 1:28; Deuteronomy 2:22"> a myche puple and greet, and of noble lengthe, as Enachym, whiche the Lord dide awey fro the face of hem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 36:8; Genesis 36:20–30; Deuteronomy 2:12"> and made hem to dwelle for &#96;tho giauntis, as he dide to the sones of Esau, that dwellen in Seire, &#96;and dide awai Horreis, and yaf to hem the lond &#96;of Horreis, which &#96;the sones of Esau welden &#96;til in to present tyme.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 10:14; Joshua 13:3; Amos 9:7"> Also men of Capadocie puttiden out Eueys, that dwelliden in Asseryn, &#96;til to Gaza; which yeden out fro Capadocie, and diden awey Eueis, and dwelliden for hem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:5; Daniel 4:17; Judges 11:18–21"> Rise ye, and &#96;passe ye the stronde of Arnon; lo! Y haue bitake in &#96;thin hond Seon, king of Esebon, of Amorreis; and his lond bigynne thou &#96;to welde, and smyte thou batel ayens him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:25; Exodus 15:14–16; Deuteronomy 28:10"> To dai Y schal bigynne to sende thi drede and strengthe in to puplis that dwellen vndir al heuene, that whanne thi name is herd, thei drede, and tremble bi the maner of wymmen trauelynge of child, and &#96;be holdun with sorewe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 10:5–6; Luke 10:10–12; Joshua 13:18"> Therfor Y sente messangeris fro the wildirnesse of Cademoch to Seon, kyng of Esebon; and Y seide with pesible wordis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 11:19; Deuteronomy 2:6; Numbers 21:21–23"> We schulen passe thorou thi lond, we schulen go in the comyn weie; we schulen not bowe nether to the riyt side, nether to the left side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 20:19"> Sille thow metis &#96;to vs for prijs, that we ete; yif thow watir for money, and so we schulen drynke. Oneli it is that thou graunte passage to vs,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 20:18; Judges 11:17–18; Deuteronomy 23:3–4"> as the sones of Esau diden, that dwellen in Seir, and as Moabitis diden, that dwellen in Ar, til we comen to Jordan, and passen to the lond which oure Lord God schal yyue to vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 21:23; Exodus 4:21; Joshua 11:19–20"> And Seon, kyng of Esebon, nolde yyue passage &#96;to vs; for thi Lord God made hard his spirit, and made sad in yuel &#96;the herte of hym, that he schulde be bitakun in to thin hondis, as thou seest now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:8; Deuteronomy 2:24"> And the Lord seide to me, Lo, Y bigan to bitake to thee Seon, and his lond; bigynne thou to welde it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 21:23–30; Judges 11:20–23; Psalms 136:19"> And Seon yede out ayens vs with al his puple to batel in Jasa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Joshua 21:44; Deuteronomy 3:2–3"> And oure Lord God bitook hym to vs, and we han smyte hym with hise sones, and al his puple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; 1 Samuel 15:3; Deuteronomy 3:6"> And we token in that tyme alle the citees, whanne the dwelleris of tho citees, men, and wymmen, and children weren slayn; we leften not in hem ony thing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Joshua 8:27; Deuteronomy 20:14; Numbers 31:9–11"> outakun beestis that camen in to the part of men takynge prey, and outakun spuylis of the cytees whiche we tokun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Joshua 13:9; Deuteronomy 4:48; Deuteronomy 3:12"> Fro Aroer, which is on the brenke of the stronde of Arnon, fro the toun which is set in the valey, &#96;til to Galaad, no town was ether citee, that ascapide oure hondis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 21:24; Deuteronomy 3:16; Genesis 32:22"> Oure Lord God bitook alle to vs; outakun the lond of the sones of Amon, to which lond we neiyiden not, and outakun alle thingis that liggen to the stronde of Jeboth, and outakun the citees of the munteyns, and alle places fro whiche oure Lord God forbeed vs.</span>
