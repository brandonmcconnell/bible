<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:1; Joshua 22:7; Numbers 33:17–18"> These ben the wordis whiche Moyses spak to al Israel ouer Jordan, in the wildirnesse of the feeld, ayens the reed see, bitwix Pharan and Tophel and Laban and Asseroth, where is ful myche gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 32:8; Numbers 13:26; Deuteronomy 9:23"> by enleuene daies fro Oreb bi the weie of the hil of Seir, til to Cades Barne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 33:38; Deuteronomy 4:1–2; Numbers 20:1"> In the fortithe yeer, in the enleuenth monethe, in the firste dai of the monethe, Moises spak to the sones of Israel alle thingis whiche the Lord commandide to hym that he schulde seie to hem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 21:21–35; Joshua 12:2–6; Joshua 13:10–12"> after that he smoot Seon, kyng of Ammorreis, that dwellide in Esebon, and Og, the kyng of Basan, that dwelide in Asseroth and in Edray, ouer Jordan, in the lond of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:8; Deuteronomy 32:46; Deuteronomy 31:9"> And Moyses bigan to declare the lawe, and to seie,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; Numbers 10:11–13; Exodus 19:1–2"> Oure Lord God spak to vs in Oreb, and seide, It suffisith to you that ye han dwellid in this hil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:24; Joshua 10:40; Amos 2:9"> turne ye ayen, and come ye to the hil of Amorreis, and to othere placis that ben next it; to places of feeldis, and of hillis, and to lowere places ayens the south, and bisidis the brenke of the see, to the lond of Cananeys, and of Liban, &#96;til to the greet flood Eufrates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; Genesis 12:7; Genesis 26:3–4"> Lo, &#96;he seith, Y haue youe to you; entre ye, and &#96;welde ye &#96;that lond on which the Lord swoor to youre fadrys, Abraham, Ysaac, and Jacob, that he schulde yyue it to hem, and to her seed after hem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="that is, he bihiyt stidefastli">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 18:18; Numbers 11:17; Numbers 11:11–14"> And Y seide to you in that time, Y may not aloone susteyne you, for youre Lord God hath multiplied you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:22; Exodus 32:13; Genesis 22:17"> and ye ben ful many to dai, as the sterris of heuene;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 115:14; Genesis 22:17; Numbers 6:27"> the Lord God of youre fadris adde to this noumbre many thousyndis, and blesse you, as he spak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:5; 1 Kings 3:7–9; 2 Corinthians 2:16"> Y may not aloone susteyne youre causis, and birthun, and stryues; yyue ye of you men wise &#96;in dyuyn thingis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; Acts 1:21–23; Numbers 11:16–17"> and witti &#96;in mennus thingis worthi to be don, whose conuersacioun is preued in youre lynagis, that Y sette hem princes to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Thanne ye answeriden to me, The thing is good which thou wolt do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:18; Exodus 18:25–26; Ephesians 4:11"> And Y took of youre lynagis men wise and noble, &#96;in vertues and kyn; and Y ordeynede hem princis, tribunes, and centuryouns, and quynquagenaries, and denys, whiche schulden teche you all thingis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 7:24; Exodus 22:21; 2 Chronicles 19:6–10"> And Y comaundide to hem, and seide, Here ye hem, and deme ye that that is iust, whether he be a citeseyn, whether a pilgrym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Exodus 18:26; James 2:9"> No difference schal be of persones; ye schulen here so a litil man, &#96;that is, pore, as a greet man, nether ye schulen take the persoone of ony man, for it is the doom of God. That if ony thing semeth hard to you, telle ye to me, and Y schal here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; Deuteronomy 4:5; Acts 20:20"> And Y comaundide alle thingis whiche ye ouyten to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:2; Deuteronomy 8:15; Jeremiah 2:6"> Forsothe we yeden forth fro Oreb, and passiden bi a feerdful deseert, and grettiste wildirnesse, which ye sien, bi the weye of the hil of Ammorrey, as oure Lord God comaundide to vs. And whanne we hadden come in to Cades Barne,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:7–8"> Y seide to you, Ye ben comen to the hil of Ammorrey, which youre Lord God schal yyue to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Psalms 46:1; Numbers 14:8–9"> se thou the lond which thi Lord God schal yyue to thee; &#96;stie thou, and welde it, as oure Lord God spak to thi fadris; &#96;nyle thou drede, nether &#96;drede thou in herte ony thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 13:1–20"> And alle ye neiyiden to me, and ye seiden, Sende we men, that schulen biholde the lond, and telle to vs bi what weye we owen stie, and to whiche citees we owen to go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 13:3–33"> And whanne the word pleside to me,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="pleside to me, that is, Y consentide for youre vnrestefulnesse.">b</a></sup>Y sente of you twelue men, of ech lynage oon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 13:21–27; Joshua 2:1–2"> And whanne thei hadden go, and hadden stied in to the hilli places, thei camen &#96;til to the valei of Clustre; and whanne thei hadden biholde the lond,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> thei token of the fruytis therof, to schewe the plentee, and brouyten &#96;to vs, and seiden, The lond is good which oure Lord God schal yyue to vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 14:1–4; Acts 7:51; Psalms 106:24–25"> And ye &#96;nolden stie, but ye weren vnbileueful to the word of oure Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:28; Psalms 106:25; Numbers 14:3"> And ye grutchiden in youre tabernaclis, and ye seiden, The Lord hatith vs, and herfor he ledde vs out of the lond of Egipt, that he schulde bitake vs in the hond of Ammorey, and schulde do awei vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 13:28–33; Deuteronomy 9:1–2; Joshua 15:14"> Whidur schulen we stie? the messangeris maden aferd oure herte, and seiden, A grettiste multitude is, and largere in stature than we; the citees ben greete, and wallid &#96;til to the heuene; we sien there the sones of Enachym, that is, giauntis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And Y seide to you, &#96;Nyle ye drede &#96;with ynne, nether &#96;drede withoutforth; the Lord God hym silf,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 14:14; Nehemiah 4:20; Romans 8:31"> which is youre ledere, schal fiyte for you, as he dide in Egipt, while alle men sien.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 46:3–4; Acts 13:18; Hosea 11:3–4"> And ye sien in the wildirnesse, thi Lord God bar thee, as a man is wont to bere his litil sone, in al the weie bi which ye yeden til ye camen to this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 106:24; Jude 1:5; Hebrews 3:18–19"> And sotheli nether so ye bileueden to youre Lord God, that yede bifor you in the weie,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 10:33; Psalms 78:14; Nehemiah 9:12"> and mesuride the place in which ye ouyten to sette tentis, and schewide in nyyt the weie to you bi fier, and in dai bi a piler of cloude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 14:22–30; Psalms 95:11; Ezekiel 20:15"> And whanne the Lord hadde herd the vois of youre wordis, he was wrooth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 95:11"> and swoor, and seide, Noon of the men of this werste generacioun schal se the good lond, which Y bihiyte vndur an ooth to youre fadris,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Numbers 14:24; Joshua 14:6–14; Numbers 32:12"> outakun Caleph,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="outakun Caleph and outakun Josue and Leuytis and children not of xx. yeer.">c</a></sup>the sone of Jephone; for he schal se it, and Y schal yyue to hym the lond on which he trad, and to hise sones, for he suede the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 20:12; Deuteronomy 4:21; Deuteronomy 34:4"> Nether the indignacioun ayens the puple is wondirful, sithen the Lord was wrooth also to me for you, and seide,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:28; Numbers 14:30; Deuteronomy 31:7–8"> Nether thou schalt entre thidur, but Josue, the sone of Nun, thi mynystre, he schal entre for thee; excyte and strengthe thou him, and he schal departe the lond bi lot to Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 7:15–16; Numbers 14:3; Numbers 14:31"> Youre litle children, of whiche ye seiden, that thei schulden be led prisoneris, and the sones that kunnen not to dai the diuersite of good and of yuel, thei schulen entre; and Y schal yyue to hem the lond, and thei schulen welde it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 14:25"> Sotheli turne ye ayen, and go ye in to the wildirnesse, bi the weie of the Reed See.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 14:39–45; Proverbs 19:3; Numbers 22:34"> And ye answeriden to me, We synneden to the Lord; we schulen stie, and we schulen fiyte, as oure Lord God comaundide. And whanne ye weren arayed with armeris, and yeden &#96;into the hil, the Lord seide to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Numbers 14:41–43; Joshua 7:8–13; Isaiah 59:1–2"> Seie thou to hem, &#96;Nyle ye stye, nether fiyte ye, for Y am not with you, lest ye fallen bifor youre enemyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 14:44; Isaiah 63:10; Acts 7:51"> Y spak, and ye herden not; but ye &#96;weren aduersaries to the comaundement of the Lord, and bolnden with prijde, and stieden in to the hil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 118:12; Numbers 14:45; Numbers 21:3"> Therfor Ammorrey yede out, that dwellide in the hillis, and he cam ayens you, and pursuede you, as bees ben wont to pursue, and killide fro Seir til Horma. And whanne ye turneden ayen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Hebrews 12:17; Psalms 78:34"> and wepten bifor the Lord, he herde not you, nether wolde asente to youre vois;</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Numbers 20:1; Numbers 20:22; Judges 11:16–17"> therfor ye saten<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="In Ebreu it is thus, and ye saten in Cades bi many daies, bi the daies in whiche ye saten in othere dwellingis.">d</a></sup>in Cades Barne bi myche tyme.</span>
