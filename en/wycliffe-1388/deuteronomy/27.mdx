<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 15:14; Luke 11:28; James 2:10"> Forsothe Moyses comaundide, and the eldre men, to the puple of Israel, and seiden, Kepe ye ech &#96;comaundement which Y comaunde to you to dai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 8:31; Ezekiel 36:26; Ezekiel 11:19"> And whanne ye han passid Jordan, in to the lond which thi Lord God schal yyue to thee, thou schalt reyse grete stoonus, and thou schalt make tho pleyn with chalk,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 8:32; Deuteronomy 26:9; 2 Corinthians 3:2–3"> that thou mow write in tho alle the wordis of this lawe, whanne Jordan is passid, that thou entre in to the lond which thi Lord God schal yyue to thee, the lond flowynge with mylke and hony, as he swoor to thi fadris.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:29–30; Joshua 8:30–33"> Therfor whanne thou hast passid Jordan, reise thou the stonus whiche Y comaunde to dai to thee, in the hil of Hebal; and thou schalt make tho pleyn with chalk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 20:25; Exodus 24:4; Joshua 8:30–31"> And there thou schalt bilde an auter to thi Lord God, of stoonys whiche yrun touchide not,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Leviticus 1:1–17"> and of stonys vnformed and vnpolischid; and thou schalt offre theron brent sacrifices to thi Lord God; and thou schalt offre pesible sacrifices,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 3:1–17; Isaiah 12:3; Psalms 100:1–2"> and thou schalt ete there, and thou schalt make feeste bifor thi Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:2; John 16:25; 2 Corinthians 3:12"> And thou schalt write pleynli and clereli on the stoonys alle the wordis of this lawe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 2:10–11; Deuteronomy 26:16–18; Ephesians 5:8–9"> And Moises and the preestis of the kynde of Leuy seiden to al Israel, Israel, perseyue thou, and here; to day thou art maad the puple of thi Lord God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:1; 1 Peter 1:14–16; Matthew 5:48"> thou schalt here his vois, and thou schalt do &#96;the comaundementis, and riytfulnessis, whiche Y comaunde to thee to dai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Moises comaundide to the puple in that day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 9:7; Genesis 30:24; Genesis 35:18"> and seide, These men schulen stonde on the hil of Garizym to blesse the Lord, whanne Jordan &#96;is passid; Symeon, Leuy, Judas, Isachar, Joseph, and Benjamyn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:4; Genesis 29:32; Genesis 30:20"> And euene ayens these men schulen stonde in the hil of Hebal to curse, Ruben, Gad, and Aser, Zabulon, Dan, and Neptalym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:11; Deuteronomy 33:9–10; Joshua 8:33"> And the dekenes schulen pronounce, and schulen seie &#96;with hiy vois to alle the men of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 34:17; Exodus 20:4; Deuteronomy 5:8"> Cursid is the man that makith a grauun ymage and yotun togidere, abhomynacioun of the Lord, the werk of &#96;hondis of crafti men, and schal sette it in priuey place; and al the puple schal answere, and schal seie, Amen!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 19:3; Exodus 21:17; Deuteronomy 21:18–21"> He is cursid that onoureth not his fadir and modir<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="that onourith not his fadir, etc. In Ebreu it is, that dispisith his fadir.">a</a></sup>; and al the puple schal seie, Amen!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:14; Proverbs 22:28; Proverbs 23:10–11"> Cursid is he that &#96;berith ouer the termes of his neiybore<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="he fordoth his neiybour termes, that doth other wise to his neiybore, than he wolde he dide to him">b</a></sup>; and al the puple schal seie, Amen!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 19:14; Matthew 15:14; Proverbs 28:10"> Cursid is he that makith a blynde man to erre in the weie; and al the puple schal seie, Amen!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:18; Deuteronomy 24:17; Proverbs 17:23"> He is cursid that peruertith the doom of a comelyng, of a fadirles, ethir modirles child, and of a widewe; and al the puple schal seie, Amen!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:30; Leviticus 18:8; 1 Corinthians 5:1"> Cursid is he that slepith with &#96;the wijf of his fadir, and schewith the hiling of his bed; and al the puple schal seie, Amen!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 18:23; Exodus 22:19; Leviticus 20:15"> Cursid is he that slepith with ony beeste; and al the puple schal seie, Amen!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17; Leviticus 18:9; Ezekiel 22:11"> Cursid is he that slepith with his sistir, the douytir of his fadir, ethir of his modir; and al the puple schal seie, Amen!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 20:14; Leviticus 18:17"> Cursid is he that slepith with his wyues modir; and al the puple schal seye, Amen!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 35:31; Leviticus 24:17; 2 Samuel 11:15–17"> Cursid is he that sleeth pryueli his neiybore; and al the puple schal seie, Amen!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:17; Exodus 23:7–8; Psalms 15:5"> Cursid is he that slepith with &#96;the wijf of his neiybore; and al the puple schal seie, Amen!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Romans 10:5"> Cursid is he that takith yiftis, that he smyte the lijf of innocent blood; and al the puple schal seie, Amen! Cursid is he that dwellith not in the wordis of this lawe, nethir &#96;parfourmeth tho in werk; and al the puple schal seie, Amen!</span>
