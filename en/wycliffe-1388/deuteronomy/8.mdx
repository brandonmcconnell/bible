<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:1; Deuteronomy 5:32–3; 1 Thessalonians 4:1–2"> Be thou war diligentli, that thou do ech comaundement which Y comaunde to thee to dai, that ye moun lyue, and be multiplied, and that ye entre, and welde the lond, for which the Lord swoor to youre fadris.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 1:3; 1 Peter 1:7; Deuteronomy 8:16"> And thou schalt haue mynde of al the weie, bi which thi Lord God ledde thee by fourti yeer, bi deseert, that he schulde turmente, and schulde tempte<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="that he schulde turmente thee, and schulde tempte, that thi pacience were knowun to othere men, to ensaumple of hem, and to merit to thee; and the Scripture spekith here, as to the goode men of that puple, for bi the rule of Isidore, ofte a thing is arettid to al the puple, which thyng acordith to it bi resoun of o part.">a</a></sup>thee; and that tho thingis that weren tretid in &#96;thi soule schulden be knowun, whether thou woldist kepe hise comaundementis, ethir nay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 4:4; Luke 4:4; Hebrews 13:5–6"> And he turmentide thee with nedynesse, and he yaf to thee meete, manna which thou knewist not, and thi fadris &#96;knewen not, that he schulde schewe to thee, that a man lyueth not in breed aloone, but in ech word<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="word is not in Ebreu.">b</a></sup>that cometh &#96;out of the Lordis mouth, &#96;that is, bi manna, that cam down &#96;at the heest of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:21; Deuteronomy 29:5; Matthew 26:25–30"> Thi cloth, bi which thou were hilid, failide not for eldnesse, and thi foot was not brokun undernethe, lo!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 3:12; 2 Samuel 7:14"> the fourtith yeer is; that thou thenke in thin herte, for as a man techith his sone,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:31; Deuteronomy 5:33; 1 Samuel 12:24"> so thi Lord God tauyte thee, that thou kepe the comaundementis of thi Lord God, and go in hise weies, and drede hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:9–12; Exodus 3:8; Ezekiel 20:6"> For thi Lord God schal lede thee in to a good lond, in to the lond of ryueris, and of &#96;stondynge watris, and of wellis, in whos feeldis and mounteyns the depthis of floodis breken out;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 6:13; Psalms 147:14; Deuteronomy 32:14"> in to the lond of wheete, of barli, and of vyneris, in which lond fige trees, and pumgranadis, and &#96;olyue trees comen forth; in to the lond of oile, and of hony;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:14; Deuteronomy 33:25; Joshua 22:8"> where thow schalt ete thi breed with out nedynesse, and schalt vse the aboundaunce of alle thingis; of which lond the stonys ben yrun, and metals of tyn ben diggid of the hillis therof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 103:2; Romans 14:6; Proverbs 3:9"> that whanne thou hast ete, and art fillid, thou blesse thi Lord God for the beste lond which he yaf to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 106:21; Hosea 2:8–9; Proverbs 1:32"> Therfor kepe thou, and be war, lest ony tyme thou foryete thi Lord God, and dispise hise comaundementis, and domes, and cerymonyes, whiche Y comaunde to thee to dai;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 30:9; Deuteronomy 28:47; Deuteronomy 32:15"> lest aftir that thou hast ete, and art fillid, hast bildid faire housis, and hast dwellid in tho,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 1:3; Genesis 13:1–5; Luke 12:13–21"> and hast droues of oxun, and flockis of scheep, and plente of siluer, and of gold, and of alle thingis, thine herte be reisid,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 106:21; 1 Corinthians 4:7–8; Deuteronomy 8:11"> and thenke not on thi Lord God, that ledde thee out of the lond of Egipt, and fro the hous of seruage,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 21:6; Numbers 20:11; Jeremiah 2:6"> and was thi ledere in the greet wildirnesse and ferdful, in which was a serpent<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="in which was a serpent and so forth, fro the hirting of whiche the Lord defendide thee; and the synguler is takun here for the plurel, a serpent, that is, serpentis, and so of scorpioun and dipsas, as in viij co. of Exodi, a greuouseste flie cam, that is, the multitude of flies; also the glos seith, that dipsas is a kynde of the leeste serpent, so that it is unnethis perseyued, and he perischith soone, whom euere dipsas bitith.">c</a></sup>brenninge with blast, and scorpioun,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="that is, an eddre that feyneth swetnesse with the heed, but venemeth with the tayl.">d</a></sup>and dipsas,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="that is, an eddre. dipsas is an eddre, that makith hem whom he stinggith to die for thirst.">e</a></sup>and outirli no &#96;watris; which Lord ledde out stremes of the hardeste stoon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:3; James 1:12; Exodus 16:15"> and fedde thee with manna in the wildirnesse, which manna thi fadris knewen not. And after that the Lord turmentid thee, and preuede, at the last he hadd mersi on thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:4; 1 Corinthians 4:7; Daniel 4:30"> lest thou woldist seie in thin herte, My strengthe, and the myyt of myn hond yaf alle these thingis to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 10:22; Hosea 2:8; Deuteronomy 7:8"> But thenke thou on thi Lord God, that he yaf strengthis to thee, that he schulde fille his couenaunt, of whiche he swoor to thi fadris, as present dai schewith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:26; 1 Samuel 12:25; Zephaniah 1:18"> Forsothe if thou foryetist thi Lord God, and suest aliene goddis, and worschipist hem &#96;in herte, and onourist &#96;with outforth, lo! now Y biforseie to thee, that thou schalt perische outerli;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 9:11–12; 2 Chronicles 36:16–17"> as hethen men perischiden, whiche the Lord dide awei in thin entryng, so and ye schulen perische, if ye schulen be vnobedient to the vois of youre Lord God.</span>
