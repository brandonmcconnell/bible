<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 11:2; Daniel 6:3; Acts 6:6"> Forsothe Josue, the sone of Nun, was fillid with &#96;the spyrit of wisdom, for Moises settide hise hondis on hym; and the sones of Israel obeieden to Josue, and diden as the Lord comaundide to Moises.</span>
