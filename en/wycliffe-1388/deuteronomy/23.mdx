<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; Isaiah 56:3–4; Leviticus 21:17–21"> A geldyng whanne hise stoonys ben brokun, ethir kit awey, and his yerde is kit awei, schal not entre in to the chirche of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 9:6; Isaiah 57:3; Hebrews 12:8"> A child borun of hordom schal not entre<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="a child born of hordum schal not entre etc. that is, to the hiy gouernaunce of the peple, but ech man that wolde, was resseyued to feith, and vertuouse liyf nedeful to saluacioun.">a</a></sup>in to the chirche of the Lord, &#96;til to the tenthe generacioun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:1–2; Nehemiah 13:23; Ruth 4:6"> Ammonytis and Moabitis, yhe aftir the tenthe generacioun, schulen not entre into the &#96;chirche of the Lord with outen ende;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:2; 2 Peter 2:15; 1 Kings 18:4"> for thei nolden come to you with breed and watir in the weie, whanne ye yeden out of Egipt; and for thei hireden ayens thee Balaam, the sone of Beor, fro Mesopotanye of Sirye, that he schulde curse thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Romans 8:31; Ephesians 2:4–5"> and thi Lord God nolde here Balaam, and God turnede &#96;the cursyng of Balaam in to thi blessyng, for he louyde thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra 9:12; 2 Samuel 12:31; Nehemiah 13:23–25"> Thou schalt not make pees with hem, nethir thou schalt seke goodis to hem, in alle the daies of thi lijf in to with outen ende.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:19; Exodus 22:21; Exodus 23:9"> Thou schalt not &#96;haue abhomynacioun of a man of Ydumye, for he is thi brothir, nethir of a man of Egipt, for thou were a comelyng in the lond of hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 20:5–6; Ephesians 2:12–13; Deuteronomy 23:1–3"> Thei that ben borun of hem, schulen entre in the thridde generacioun in to the &#96;chirche of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 6:18; Judges 20:26; Revelation 19:11–14"> Whanne thou schalt go out &#96;in to batel ayens thin enemyes, thou schalt kepe thee fro al yuel thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 15:16; Numbers 5:2–3; 1 Corinthians 5:11–13"> If a man is among you, which is defoulid in &#96;sleep of nyyt, he schal go out of &#96;the castels<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="he schal go out of the castels of the Godhed and of Leuytis, not out of the castels of the comyn peeple.">b</a></sup>;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; Ephesians 5:26–27; Leviticus 14:9"> and he schal not turne ayen bifore that he be waischun in watir at euentid, and aftir the goyng doun of the sunne he schal go ayen in to the castels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Thou schalt haue a place without the castels, to which thou schalt go out to nedeful thingis of kynde;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:6–8"> and thou schalt bere a litil stake in the girdil; and whanne thou hast sete, thou schalt digge &#96;bi cumpas, and &#96;thou schalt hile with erthe thingis &#96;defied out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 17:1; Leviticus 26:12; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> where thou art releuyd. For thi Lord God goeth in the myddis of castels, that he diliuere thee, and bitake thin enemyes to thee, that thi castels be hooli, and no thing of filthe appere in tho, lest he forsake thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:15; Obadiah 1:14"> Thou schalt not bitake a seruaunt to his lord,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="thou schalt not bitake a seruaunt to his lord, that is, whanne the seruaunt is souyt of his lord to be slayn, ether to be meymed, ether to be turmentid in other maner vnduly: he schal dwelle with thee, and so forth; til the ire of his lord ceese, and thanne he schal be yoldun to his lord, sithen he is the thing ether catel of his lord, as also other thingis schulen be restorid.">c</a></sup>which seruaunt fleeth to thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 22:21; Zechariah 7:10; Jeremiah 7:6"> he schal dwelle with thee in the place that plesith hym, and he schal reste in oon of thi citees; and make thou not hym sori.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 23:7; 1 Kings 14:24; Leviticus 19:29"> Noon hoore schal be of the douytris of Israel, nether a letchour of the sones of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philippians 3:2; Matthew 7:6; Revelation 22:15"> Thou schalt not offre the hire of &#96;an hoore hows, nether the prijs of a dogge, in the hows of thi Lord God, what euer thing it is that thou hast avowid; for euer eithir is abhomynacioun bifor thi Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 22:25; Leviticus 25:35–37; Psalms 15:5"> Thou schalt not leene to thi brothir to vsure money, neither fruytis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:10; Deuteronomy 15:3; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> nethir ony othir thing, but to an alien.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="but to an alien, this is graunting of a thing not leueful, but of a thing lesse yuel to eschewe a more yuel, that is, lest they wolden greue her britheren Iewis with vsuris.">d</a></sup>Forsothe thou schalt leene to thi brothir without vsure that that he nedith, that thi Lord God blesse thee in al thi werk, in the lond to which thou schalt entre to welde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:4–5; Psalms 66:13–14; Psalms 116:18"> Whanne thou makist auow to thi Lord God, thou schalt not tarie to yelde, for thi Lord God schal &#96;requyre, ether axe, that; and if thou tariest, it schal be arretid to thee in to synne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> If thou &#96;nylt bihete, thou schalt be with out synne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 20:25; Judges 11:30–31; 1 Samuel 1:11"> Forsothe<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Forthi kepe the heestis for that thou hast auowid, in comparisoun of him that hootith, and fulfillith it not.">e</a></sup>thou schalt kepe, and &#96;do that that yede out onys of thi lippis, as thou bihiytist to thi Lord God, and hast spoke with thin owne wille and thi mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:26; Romans 12:13; Hebrews 13:5"> If thou entrist in to the vynere of thi neiybore, ete thou grapis, as myche as plesith thee; but bere thou not out with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 12:1–2; Mark 2:23; Luke 6:1–2"> If thou entrist in to &#96;the corn of thi freend, thou schalt breke &#96;eeris of corn, and frote togidere with &#96;the hond; but thou schalt not repe with a sikil.</span>
