<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Colossians 1:10; John 15:16"> Y am a vyne, ye the braunchis. Who that dwellith in me, and Y in hym, this berith myche fruyt, for with outen me ye moun no thing do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 7:19; Hebrews 6:7–8; Matthew 13:41"> If ony man dwellith not in me, he schal be caste out as a braunche, and schal wexe drie; and thei schulen gadere hym, and thei schulen caste hym in to the fier, and he brenneth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; 1 John 5:14; John 14:13"> If ye dwellen in me, and my wordis dwelle in you, what euer thing ye wolen, ye schulen axe, and it schal be don to you.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 17:23–24; John 17:26; 1 John 2:28"> As my fadir louede me, Y haue loued you; dwelle ye in my loue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 14:15; 1 John 5:3; 1 John 3:21–24"> If ye kepen my comaundementis, ye schulen dwelle in my loue; as Y haue kept the comaundementis of my fadir, and Y dwelle in his loue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 17:13; Romans 15:13; John 16:24"> These thingis Y spak to you, that my ioye be in you, and youre ioye be fulfillid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 4:21; 1 Peter 4:8; John 13:34"> This is my comaundement, that ye loue togidere, as Y louede you.</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 12:50; 1 John 5:3; James 2:23"> Ye ben my freendis if ye doen tho thingis, that Y comaunde to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 25:14; 1 Corinthians 2:9–12; Matthew 13:11"> Now Y schal not clepe you seruauntis, for the seruaunt woot not, what his lord schal do; but Y haue clepid you freendis, for alle thingis what euere Y herde of my fadir, Y haue maad knowun to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 15:19; Jeremiah 1:5–7; Ephesians 2:10"> Ye han not chosun me, but Y chees you; and Y haue put you, that ye go, and brynge forth fruyt, and youre fruyt dwelle; that what euere thing ye axen the fadir in my name, he yyue to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 15:12; 1 John 3:14–17; 1 Peter 2:17"> These thingis Y comaunde to you, that ye loue togidere.</span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:12; John 13:16; Matthew 10:24"> Haue ye mynde of my word, which Y seide to you, The seruaunt is not grettere than his lord. If thei han pursued me, thei schulen pursue you also; if thei han kept my word, thei schulen kepe youre also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 10:22; John 16:3; Matthew 10:18"> But thei schulen do to you alle these thingis for my name, for thei knowen not hym that sente me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 9:41; James 4:17; Acts 17:30"> If Y hadde not comun, and hadde not spokun to hem, thei schulden not haue synne; but now thei haue noon excusacioun of her synne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 8:40–42; 2 John 1:9; 1 John 2:23"> He that hatith me, hatith also my fadir.</span>

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 109:3; Psalms 69:4; Psalms 35:19"> But that the word be fulfillid, that is writun in her lawe, For thei hadden me in hate with outen cause.</span>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 John 4:14; John 21:24; Acts 10:39–42"> and ye schulen bere witnessyng, for ye ben with me fro the bigynnyng.</span>
