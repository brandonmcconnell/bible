<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 5:16–18; John 4:3; Matthew 21:38"> Aftir these thingis Jhesus walkide in to Galilee, for he wolde not walke in to Judee, for the Jewis souyten to sle hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 14:16–19; Deuteronomy 16:13–16; Exodus 23:16–17"> And ther was neiy a feeste dai of the Jewis, Senofegia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 12:46; John 7:5; Luke 8:19"> And hise britheren seiden to hym, Passe fro hennus, and go in to Judee, that also thi disciplis seen thi werkis that thou doist;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 18:20; Proverbs 18:1–2; Matthew 23:5"> for no man doith ony thing in hiddlis, and hym silf sekith to be opyn. If thou doist these thingis, schewe thi silf to the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 3:21; John 1:11–13; Micah 7:5–6"> For nether hise britheren bileueden in hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 7:8; John 2:4; John 7:30"> Therfor Jhesus seith to hem, My tyme cam not yit, but youre tyme is euermore redi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:18–19; John 3:19; Galatians 4:16"> The world may not hate you, sothely it hatith me; for Y bere witnessyng therof, that the werkis of it ben yuele.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 7:6; John 8:20; John 7:30"> Go ye vp to this feeste dai, but Y schal not go vp to this feeste dai, for my tyme is not yit fulfillid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Whanne he hadde seid these thingis, he dwelte in Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 5:13; Matthew 10:16; Isaiah 42:2–3"> And aftir that hise britheren weren gon vp, thanne he yede vp to the feeste dai, not opynli, but as in priuyte.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 11:56"> Therfor the Jewis souyten hym in the feeste dai, and seiden, Where is he?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 7:40–43; John 9:16; John 7:32"> And myche grutchyng was of hym among the puple. For summe seiden, That he is good; and othere seiden, Nai, but he disceyueth the puple;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 9:22; John 19:38; John 20:19"> netheles no man spak opynli of hym, for drede of the Jewis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 7:28; Luke 19:47; John 7:37"> But whanne the myddil feeste dai cam, Jhesus wente vp in to the temple, and tauyte.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 4:22; Matthew 7:28–29; Matthew 13:54"> And the Jewis wondriden, and seiden, Hou can this man lettris, sithen he hath not lerned?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 14:10; John 14:24; John 17:8"> Jhesus answerde to hem, and seide, My doctryne is not myn, but his that sente me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 8:31–32; John 8:43; Jeremiah 31:33–34"> If ony man wole do his wille, he schal knowe of the techyng, whethir it be of God, or Y speke of my silf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 5:41; 1 Corinthians 10:31–33; John 13:31–32"> He that spekith of hym silf, sekith his owne glorie; but he that sekith the glorie of hym that sente hym, is sothefast, and vnriytwisnesse is not in hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 1:17; John 11:53; Matthew 12:14"> Whether Moises yaf not to you a lawe, and noon of you doith the lawe?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 10:20; John 8:48; John 8:52"> What seken ye to sle me? And the puple answerde, and seide, Thou hast a deuel; who sekith to sle thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 7:23; John 5:2–11"> Jhesus answerde, and seide to hem, Y haue don o werk, and alle ye wondren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 12:3; Genesis 17:10–14; Romans 4:9–11"> Therfor Moises yaf to you circumcisioun; not for it is of Moyses, but of the fadris; and in the sabat ye circumciden a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 12:5; John 5:14–16; Matthew 12:2"> If a man take circumcicioun in the sabat, that the lawe of Moises be not brokun, han ye indignacioun to me, for Y made al a man hool in the sabat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 11:3–4; James 2:9; John 8:15"> Nile ye deme aftir the face, but deme ye a riytful doom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 7:10–11"> Therfor summe of Jerusalem seiden, Whethir this is not he, whom the Jewis seken to sle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7–8; John 7:48; John 12:42"> and lo! he spekith opynli, and thei seien no thing to hym. Whether the princes knewen verili, that this is Crist?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Micah 5:2; John 7:41–42; John 6:42"> But we knowun this man, of whennus he is; but whanne Crist schal come, no man woot of whennus he is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 8:42; John 8:19; John 8:26"> Therfor Jhesus criede in the temple &#96;techynge, and seide, Ye knowen me, and &#96;ye knowen of whennus Y am; and Y cam not of my silf, but he is trewe that sente me, whom ye knowen not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:55; Matthew 11:27; John 17:18"> Y knowe hym, and if Y seie that Y knowe hym not, Y schal be lijk to you, a liere; but Y knowe hym, for of hym Y am, and he sente me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 8:20; John 10:39; John 7:6"> Therfor thei souyten to take hym, and no man sette on hym hoondis, for his our cam not yit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 12:42; Matthew 12:23; John 10:41–42"> And many of the puple bileueden in hym, and seiden, Whanne Crist schal come, whether he schal do mo tokenes, than tho that this doith?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 18:3; Luke 22:52–53; John 11:47–48"> Farisees herden the puple musinge of hym these thingis; and the princis and Farisees senten mynystris, to take hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 16:5; John 13:33; Mark 16:19"> Therfor Jhesus seide to hem, Yit a litil tyme Y am with you, and Y go to the fadir, that sente me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24–31; John 13:33–36; John 14:6"> Ye schulen seke me, and ye schulen not fynde; and where Y am, ye may not come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="James 1:1; Isaiah 11:12; Zephaniah 3:10"> Therfor the Jewis seiden to hem silf, Whidur schal this gon, for we schulen not fynde hym? whether he wole go in to the scateryng of hethene men, and wole teche the hethene?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 3:9; John 7:34; John 12:34"> What is this word, which he seide, Ye schulen seke me, and ye schulen not fynde; and where Y am, ye moun not come?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 55:1; John 4:14; John 6:35"> But in the laste dai of the greet feeste, Jhesus stood, and criede, and seide, If ony man thirstith, come he to me, and drynke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 4:14; Isaiah 44:3; Isaiah 58:11"> He that bileueth in me, as the scripture seith, Floodis of quyk watir schulen flowe fro his wombe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 2:17; Joel 2:28; John 14:16–17"> But he seide this thing of the Spirit, whom men that bileueden in hym schulden take; for the Spirit was not yit youun, for Jhesus was not yit glorified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 21:11; John 1:21; John 6:14"> Therfor of that cumpanye, whanne thei hadden herd these wordis of hym, thei seiden, This is verili a prophete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 7:52; John 1:46; John 1:49"> Othere seiden, This is Crist. &#96;But summe seiden, Whether Crist cometh fro Galilee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Matthew 2:5; Luke 2:4"> Whether the scripture seith not, that of the seed of Dauid, and of the castel of Bethleem, where Dauid was, Crist cometh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 9:16; John 10:19; John 7:12"> Therfor discencioun was maad among the puple for hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 7:30; Acts 23:11; John 8:20"> For summe of hem wolden haue take hym, but no man sette hondis on hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 7:32; Acts 5:21–27"> Therfor the mynystris camen to bischopis and Farisees, and thei seiden to hem, Whi brouyten ye not hym?</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Matthew 7:28–29; Luke 4:22; John 7:26"> The mynystris answeriden, Neuere man spak so, as this man spekith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 7:12; 2 Corinthians 6:8; 2 Kings 18:32"> Therfor the Farisees answeriden to hem, Whether ye ben disseyued also?</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 12:42; 1 Corinthians 1:20; 1 Corinthians 2:8"> whether ony of the pryncis or of the Farisees bileueden in hym?</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:20–21; John 9:40; John 9:34"> But this puple, that knowith not the lawe, ben cursid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 19:39; John 3:1–2"> Nychodeme seith to hem, he that cam to hym bi nyyt, that was oon of hem, Whethir oure lawe demith a man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Proverbs 18:13; Deuteronomy 1:17; Deuteronomy 19:15–19"> but it haue first herde of hym, and knowe what he doith?</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 7:41; John 1:46; Isaiah 9:1–2"> Thei answeriden, and seiden to hym, Whether thou art a man of Galilee also? Seke thou scripturis, and se thou, that a prophete risith not of Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Psalms 33:10; Job 5:12–13; Psalms 76:10"> And thei turneden ayen, ech in to his hous.</span>
