<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 21:1; Mark 11:1; Luke 19:37"> But Jhesus wente in to the mount of Olyuete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 26:55; Luke 4:20; Luke 21:37"> And eerli eft he cam in to the temple; and al the puple cam to hym; and he sat, and tauyte hem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And scribis and Fariseis bryngen a womman takun in auoutrye, and thei settiden hir in the myddil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> and seiden to hym, Maystir, this womman is now takun in auoutrie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 20:10; Deuteronomy 22:21–24; Ezekiel 16:38–40"> And in the lawe Moises comaundide vs to stoone suche; therfor what seist thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:13; Luke 10:25; Ecclesiastes 3:7"> And thei seiden this thing temptynge hym, that thei myyten accuse hym. And Jhesus bowide hym silf doun, and wroot with his fyngur in the erthe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:1–5; Romans 2:21–25; Romans 2:1–3"> And whanne thei abiden axynge hym, he reiside hym silf, and seide to hem, He of you that is without synne, first caste a stoon in to hir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And eft he bowide hym silf, and wroot in the erthe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:22; Romans 2:15; 1 John 3:20"> And thei herynge these thingis, wenten awei oon aftir anothir, and thei bigunnen fro the eldre men; and Jhesus dwelte aloone, and the womman stondynge in the myddil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 41:11–12"> And Jhesus reiside hym silf, and seide to hir, Womman, where ben thei that accusiden thee? no man hath dampned thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 3:17; John 5:14; Isaiah 1:16–18"> Sche seide, No man, Lord. Jhesus seide &#96;to hir, Nethir Y schal dampne thee; go thou, and now aftirward nyle thou synne more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 12:46; John 9:5; John 12:35"> Therfor eft Jhesus spak to hem, and seide, Y am the liyt of the world; he that sueth me, walkith not in derknessis, but schal haue the liyt of lijf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 5:31–47"> Therfor the Fariseis seiden, Thou berist witnessyng of thi silf; thi witnessyng is not trewe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 16:28; John 13:3; John 8:42"> Jhesus answerde, and seide to hem, And if Y bere witnessyng of my silf, my witnessyng is trewe; for Y woot fro whennus Y cam, and whidur Y go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:7; John 7:24; John 12:47"> But ye witen not fro whennus Y cam, ne whidur Y go. For ye demen aftir the fleisch, but Y deme no man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 17:31; John 8:29; John 16:32"> and if Y deme, my doom is trewe, for Y am not aloone, but Y and the fadir that sente me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:15; Deuteronomy 17:6; Hebrews 10:28"> And in youre lawe it is writun, that the witnessyng of twei men is trewe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 8:38; John 8:58; John 8:25"> Y am, that bere witnessyng of my silf, and the fadir that sente me, berith witnessyng of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 16:3; John 8:54–55; John 14:6–9"> Therfor thei seiden to hym, Where is thi fadir? Jhesus answeride, Nether ye knowen me, nethir ye knowen my fadir; if ye knewen me, perauenture ye schulden knowe also my fadir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:30; John 7:8; Mark 12:41"> Jhesus spak these wordis in the tresorie, techynge in the temple; and no man took hym, for his our cam not yit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 7:34; John 8:24; 1 Corinthians 15:17–18"> Therfor eft Jhesus seide to hem, Lo! Y go, and ye schulen seke me, and ye schulen die in youre synne; whidur Y go, ye moun not come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 8:52; Psalms 22:6; John 8:48"> Therfor the Jewis seiden, Whether he schal sle hym silf, for he seith, Whidur Y go, ye moun not come?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 3:31; John 17:14; John 17:16"> And he seide to hem, Ye ben of bynethe, Y am of aboue; ye ben of this world, Y am not of this world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 3:36; Mark 16:16; John 3:18"> Therfor Y seide to you, that ye schulen die in youre synnes; for if ye bileuen not that Y am, ye schulen die in youre synne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 5:17–29; Luke 22:67; John 1:22"> Therfor thei seiden to hym, Who art thou? Jhesus seide to hem, The bigynnyng, which also speke to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 15:15; John 12:47–50; John 8:40"> Y haue many thingis to speke, and deme of you, but he that sente me is sothefast; and Y speke in the world these thingis, that Y herde of hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9; John 8:43; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4"> And thei knewen not, that he clepide his fadir God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 5:19; John 5:30; John 6:38"> Therfor Jhesus seith to hem, Whanne ye han areisid mannus sone, thanne ye schulen knowe, that Y am, and of my silf Y do no thing; but as my fadir tauyte me, Y speke these thingis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 6:38; John 16:32; John 4:34"> And he that sente me is with me, and lefte me not aloone; for Y do euermore tho thingis, that ben plesynge to hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 7:31; John 2:23; John 11:45"> Whanne he spak these thingis, manye bileueden in hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 John 1:9; John 15:4–9; 2 Timothy 3:14"> Therfor Jhesus seide to the Jewis, that bileueden in hym, If ye dwellen in my word, verili ye schulen be my disciplis;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 8:2; John 8:36; John 17:17"> and ye schulen knowe the treuthe, and the treuthe schal make you fre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 3:9; John 8:39; Genesis 15:13"> Therfor the Jewis answeriden to hym, We ben the seed of Abraham, and we serueden neuere to man; hou seist thou, That ye schulen be fre?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Romans 6:16; 2 Peter 2:19; Proverbs 5:22"> Jhesus answeride to hem, Treuli, treuli, Y seie to you, ech man that doith synne, is seruaunt of synne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Galatians 4:4–7; Luke 15:31; Romans 8:15–17"> And the seruaunt dwellith not in the hows with outen ende, but the sone dwellith with outen ende.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:17; Galatians 5:1; Romans 8:2"> Therfor if the sone make you fre, verili ye schulen be fre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; John 8:45–47; John 7:1"> Y woot that ye ben Abrahams sones, but ye seken to sle me, for my word takith not in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 5:19; John 8:44; John 8:41"> Y speke tho thingis, that Y say at my fadir; and ye doen tho thingis, that ye sayn at youre fadir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Galatians 3:7; John 8:37; Romans 9:7"> Thei answerden, and seiden to hym, Abraham is oure fadir. Jhesus seith to hem, If ye ben the sones of Abraham, do ye the werkis of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 8:26; Galatians 4:29; Galatians 4:16"> But now ye seken to sle &#96;me, a man that haue spoken to you treuthe, that Y herde of God; Abraham dide not this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; John 8:38; Isaiah 63:16"> Ye doen the werkis of youre fadir. Therfor thei seiden to hym, We ben not borun of fornycacioun; we han o fadir, God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 12:49; John 14:10; John 17:8"> But Jhesus seith to hem, If God were youre fadir, sotheli ye schulden loue me; for Y passide forth of God, and cam; for nether Y cam of my silf, but he sente me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 12:39–40; Romans 8:7–8; John 7:17"> Whi knowen ye not my speche? for ye moun not here my word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Acts 13:10; 1 John 3:12; 1 John 3:8–10"> Ye ben of the fadir, the deuel, and ye wolen do the desyris of youre fadir. He was a mansleere fro the bigynnyng, and he stood not in treuthe; for treuthe is not in hym. Whanne he spekith lesyng, he spekith of his owne; for he is a liere, and fadir of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3–4; Galatians 4:16; John 18:37"> But for Y seie treuthe, ye bileuen not to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; John 16:8; 1 Peter 2:22"> Who of you schal repreue me of synne? if Y sey treuthe, whi bileuen ye not to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 10:26–27; 2 John 1:9; John 18:37"> He that is of God, herith the wordis of God; therfor ye heren not, for ye ben not of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 7:20; John 8:52; Matthew 10:5"> Therfor the Jewis answeriden, and seiden, Whether we seien not wel, that thou art a Samaritan, and hast a deuel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Peter 2:23; Isaiah 49:3; John 13:31–32"> Jhesus answerde, and seide, Y haue not a deuel, but Y onoure my fadir, and ye han vnhonourid me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 5:41; John 8:54; John 7:18"> For Y seke not my glorye; there is he, that sekith, and demeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="John 11:25–26; John 5:24; John 3:15–16"> Treuli, treuli, Y seie to you, if ony man kepe my word, he schal not taste deth with outen ende.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 9:24; John 8:48; Hebrews 2:9"> Therfor the Jewis seiden, Now we han knowun, that thou hast a deuel. Abraham is deed, and the prophetis, and thou seist, If ony man kepe my word, he schal not taste deth withouten ende.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="John 4:12; Romans 9:5; Hebrews 7:1–7"> Whether thou art grettere than oure fader Abraham, that is deed, and the prophetis ben deed; whom makist thou thi silf?</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 8:50; Proverbs 25:27; 2 Peter 1:17"> Jhesus answeride, If Y glorifie my silf, my glorie is nouyt; my fadir, is that glorifieth me, whom ye seien, that he is youre God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="John 8:19; John 8:44; John 7:28–29"> And ye han not knowun hym, but Y haue knowun hym; and if Y seie that Y knowe hym not, Y schal be a liere lich to you; but Y knowe hym, and Y kepe his word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Hebrews 11:13; Genesis 22:18; Galatians 3:7–9"> Abraham, youre fadir, gladide to se my dai; and he saiy, and ioyede.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> Thanne the Jewis seiden to hym, Thou hast not yit fifti yeer, and hast thou seien Abraham?</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Exodus 3:14; Colossians 1:17; John 17:5"> Therfor Jhesus seide to hem, Treuli, treuli, Y seie to you, bifor that Abraham schulde be, Y am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="John 11:8; John 10:30–33; John 10:39–40"> Therfor thei token stonys, to caste to hym; but Jhesus hidde hym, and wente out of the temple.</span>
