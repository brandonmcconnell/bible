<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:16–17; John 12:26; 2 Timothy 2:12"> And if Y go, and make redi to you a place, eftsoones Y come, and Y schal take you to my silf, that where Y am, ye be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 10:9; John 16:28; John 13:3"> And whidur Y go, ye witen, and ye witen the weie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 11:16; Mark 9:19; John 20:25–28"> Thomas seith to hym, Lord, we witen not whidur thou goist, and hou moun we wite the weie?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 4:12; John 10:9; Ephesians 2:18"> Jhesus seith to hym, Y am weie, treuthe, and lijf; no man cometh to the fadir, but bi me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 1:15–17; John 8:19; Luke 10:22"> If ye hadden knowe me, sotheli ye hadden knowe also my fadir; and aftirward ye schulen knowe hym, and ye han seyn hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 33:18–23; Revelation 22:3–5; John 1:43–46"> Filip seith to hym, Lord, schewe to vs the fadir, and it suffisith to vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 1:15; John 12:45; John 14:7"> Jhesus seith to hym, So long tyme Y am with you, and &#96;han ye not knowun me? Filip, he that seeth me, seeth also the fadir. Hou seist thou, schewe to vs the fadir?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 5:19; John 10:38; John 10:30"> Bileuest thou not, that Y am in the fadir, and the fadir is in me? The wordis that Y speke to you, Y speke not of my silf; but the fadir hym silf dwellynge in me, doith the werkis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 10:38; John 10:25; John 5:36"> Bileue ye not, that Y am in the fadir, and the fadir is in me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 21:21; Mark 16:17; Luke 10:17–19"> Ellis bileue ye for thilke werkis. Treuli, treuli, Y seie to you, if a man bileueth in me, also he schal do the werkis that Y do; and he schal do grettere werkis than these, for Y go to the fadir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 15:7; Mark 11:24; Matthew 7:7"> And what euere thing ye axen the fadir in my name, Y schal do this thing, that the fadir be glorified in the sone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> If ye axen ony thing in my name, Y schal do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 2:3–5; 1 John 5:2–3; John 14:21–24"> If ye louen me, kepe ye my comaundementis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 14:26; John 15:26; John 16:7–15"> And Y schal preye the fadir, and he schal yyue to you another coumfortour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:27; John 16:13; 1 John 2:27"> the spirit of treuthe, to dwelle with you with outen ende; which spirit the world may not take, for it seeth hym not, nether knowith hym. But ye schulen knowe hym, for he schal dwelle with you, and he schal be in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 14:3; Psalms 23:4; Matthew 18:20"> Y schal not leeue you fadirles, Y schal come to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 16:16; John 7:33; Hebrews 7:25"> Yit a litil, and the world seeth not now me; but ye schulen se me, for Y lyue, and ye schulen lyue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 15:5–7; Galatians 2:20; John 17:21–23"> In that dai ye schulen knowe, that Y am in my fadir, and ye in me, and Y in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 14:15; 1 John 5:3; 1 John 2:5"> He that hath my comaundementis, and kepith hem, he it is that loueth me; and he that loueth me, schal be loued of my fadir, and Y schal loue hym, and Y schal schewe to hym my silf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Luke 6:16; Matthew 10:3"> Judas seith to hym, not he of Scarioth, Lord, what is don, that thou schalt schewe thi silf to vs, and not to the world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 14:21; John 14:15; Revelation 3:20–21"> Jhesus answerde, and seide &#96;to hym, If ony man loueth me, he schal kepe my word; and my fadir schal loue hym, and we schulen come to hym, and we schulen dwelle with hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 7:16; John 14:10; John 8:42"> He that loueth me not, kepith not my wordis; and the word which ye han herd, is not myn, but the fadris, that sente me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 17:6–8; John 13:19; John 16:12"> These thingis Y haue spokun to you, dwellynge among you; but thilke Hooli Goost,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 15:26; John 14:16; 1 John 2:27"> the coumfortour, whom the fadir schal sende in my name, he schal teche you alle thingis, &#96;and schal schewe to you alle thingis, what euere thingis Y schal seie to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Philippians 4:7; John 16:33; Isaiah 41:10"> Pees Y leeue to you, my pees Y yyue to you; not as the world yyueth, Y yiue to you; be not youre herte affrayed, ne drede it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 10:29–30; 1 Corinthians 15:24–28; 1 Corinthians 11:3"> Ye han herd, that Y seide to you, Y go, and come to you. If ye loueden me, forsothe ye schulden haue ioye, for Y go to the fadir, for the fadir is grettere than Y.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 13:19; John 16:4–31; Matthew 24:24–25"> And now Y haue seid to you, bifor that it be don, that whanne it is don, ye bileuen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 John 5:19; 1 John 4:4; Revelation 12:9"> Now Y schal not speke many thingis with you; for the prince of this world cometh, and hath not in me ony thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 10:18; Philippians 2:8; John 12:49"> But that the world knowe, that Y loue the fadir; and as the fadir yaf a comaundement to me, so Y do. &#96;Rise ye, go we hennus.</span>
