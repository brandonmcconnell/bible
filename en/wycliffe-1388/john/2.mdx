<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:40–49; Matthew 10:40–42; 1 Corinthians 7:39"> And Jhesus was clepid, and hise disciplis, to the weddyngis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 104:15; Philippians 4:6; Isaiah 24:11"> And whanne wijn failide, the modir of Jhesu seide to hym, Thei han not wijn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 7:6; John 13:1; John 7:30"> And Jhesus seith to hir, What to me and to thee, womman? myn our cam not yit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:14; Genesis 6:22; Hebrews 11:8"> His modir seith to the mynystris, What euere thing he seie to you, do ye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 3:25; Hebrews 9:10; Ephesians 5:26"> And there weren set &#96;sixe stonun cannes, aftir the clensyng of the Jewis, holdynge ech tweyne ether thre metretis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 4:2–6; John 2:5; John 2:3"> And Jhesus seith to hem, Fille ye the pottis with watir. And thei filliden hem, vp to the mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 2:9; Proverbs 3:5–6; Romans 13:7"> And Jhesus seide to hem, Drawe ye now, and bere ye to the architriclyn. And thei baren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 4:46; John 7:17; Psalms 119:100"> And whanne the architriclyn hadde tastid the watir maad wiyn, and wiste not wherof it was, but the mynystris wisten that drowen the watir, the architriclyn clepith the spouse,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 7:16–17; Proverbs 9:1–6; Luke 16:25"> and seith to hym, Ech man settith first good wiyn, and whanne men ben fulfillid, thanne that that is worse; but thou hast kept the good wiyn &#96;in to this tyme.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 20:30–31; John 1:14; John 3:2"> Jhesus dide this the bigynnyng of signes in the Cane of Galilee, and schewide his glorie; and hise disciplis bileueden in hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:46; Matthew 13:55–56; Mark 6:3"> Aftir these thingis he cam doun to Cafarnaum, and his modir, and hise britheren, and hise disciplis; and thei dwelliden &#96;there not many daies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 2:41; John 11:55; John 2:23"> And the pask of Jewis was nyy, and Jhesus wente vp to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 19:45–46; Mark 11:15; Mark 11:17"> And he foond in the temple men sillynge oxun, and scheep, and culueris, and chaungeris sittynge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 18:6; Zechariah 4:6; 2 Corinthians 10:4"> And whanne he hadde maad as it were a scourge of smale cordis, he droof out alle of the temple, and oxun, and scheep; and he schedde the money of chaungeris, and turnede vpsedoun the boordis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 21:13; Jeremiah 7:11; Mark 11:17"> And he seide to hem that selden culueris, Take awei fro hennus these thingis, and nyle ye make the hous of my fadir an hous of marchaundise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 69:9; Psalms 119:139"> And hise disciplis hadden mynde, for it was writun, The feruent loue of thin hous hath etun me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Mark 8:11; Mark 11:27–28"> Therfor the Jewis answeriden, and seiden to hym, What token schewist thou to vs, that thou doist these thingis?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 14:58; Mark 15:29; Matthew 27:40"> Jhesus answerde, and seide to hem, Vndo ye this temple, and in thre daies Y schal reise it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Therfor the Jewis seiden to hym, In fourti and sixe yeer this temple was bildid, and schalt thou in thre daies reise it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:19; 1 Corinthians 3:16; Ephesians 2:20–22"> But he seide of the temple of his bodi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 12:16; John 16:4; Luke 24:7–8"> Therfor whanne he was risun fro deeth, hise disciplis hadden mynde, that he seide these thingis of his bodi; and thei bileueden to the scripture, and to the word that Jhesus seide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 7:31; Luke 8:13; John 6:14"> And whanne Jhesus was at Jerusalem in pask, in the feeste dai, many bileueden in his name, seynge his signes that he dide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:64; Hebrews 4:13; Acts 1:24"> But Jhesus trowide not hym silf to hem, for he knewe alle men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 13:11; Matthew 9:4; John 6:64"> and for it was not nede to hym, that ony man schulde bere witnessyng, for he wiste, what was in man.</span>
