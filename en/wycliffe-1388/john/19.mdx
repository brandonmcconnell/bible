<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 53:5; Isaiah 50:6; Matthew 20:19"> Therfor Pilat took thanne Jhesu, and scourgide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 15:16–20; Luke 23:11; Matthew 27:27–31"> And kniytis writhen a coroun of thornes, and setten on his heed, and diden aboute hym a cloth of purpur,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 27:29; Matthew 26:49; Luke 1:28"> and camen to him, and seiden, Heil, kyng of Jewis. And thei yauen to him buffatis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 18:38; John 19:6; Luke 23:4"> Eftsoone Pilat wente out, and seide to hem, Lo! Y brynge hym out to you, that ye knowe, that Y fynde no cause in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 19:2; Hebrews 12:2; Isaiah 7:14"> And so Jhesus wente out, berynge a coroun of thornes, and a cloth of purpur. And he seith to hem, Lo! the man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:31; John 19:4; Acts 2:23"> But whanne the bischopis and mynystris hadden seyn hym, thei crieden, and seiden, Crucifie, crucifie hym. Pilat seith to hem, Take ye hym, and crucifie ye, for Y fynde no cause in hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 24:16; John 5:18; Matthew 26:63–66"> The Jewis answeriden to hym. We han a lawe, and bi the lawe he owith to die, for he made hym Goddis sone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 19:13; Acts 14:11–19"> Therfor whanne Pilat hadde herd this word, he dredde the more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 18:33; Psalms 38:13–15; Philippians 1:28"> And he wente in to the moot halle eftsoone, and seide to Jhesu, Of whennus art thou? But Jhesus yaf noon answere to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 5:19; John 18:39; Daniel 3:14–15"> Pilat seith to him, Spekist thou not to me? Woost thou not, that Y haue power to crucifie thee, and Y haue power to delyuere thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 13:1; John 3:27; Acts 2:23"> Jhesus answeride, Thou schuldist not &#96;haue ony power ayens me, but it were youun to thee from aboue; therfor he that bitook me to thee, hath the more synne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 17:6–7; John 18:33–36; Mark 6:16–26"> Fro that tyme Pilat souyte to delyuere hym; but the Jewis crieden, and seiden, If thou delyuerist this man, thou art not the emperouris freend; for ech man that makith hym silf king, ayen seith the emperoure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 82:5–7; Matthew 27:19; Amos 4:7"> And Pilat, whanne he hadde herd these wordis, ledde Jhesu forth, and sat for domesman in a place, that is seid Licostratos, but in Ebrew Golgatha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 27:62; John 19:5; Mark 15:25"> And it was pask eue, as it were the sixte our. And he seith to the Jewis, Lo! youre king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 23:18; Acts 21:36; John 19:6"> But thei crieden, and seiden, Take awei, take awei; crucifie him. Pilat seith to hem, Schal I crucifie youre king? The bischops answeriden, We han no king but the emperour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 23:24–25; Matthew 27:26–31; Mark 15:15–20"> And thanne Pilat bitook him to hem, that he schulde be crucified. And thei token Jhesu, and ledden him out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 23:33; Luke 14:27; Luke 23:26"> And he bar to hym silf a cros, and wente out in to that place, that is seid of Caluarie, in Ebreu Golgatha;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Mark 15:24–27; Galatians 3:13"> where thei crucifieden him, and othere tweyne with him, oon on this side and oon on that side, and Jhesus in the myddil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 27:37; Luke 23:38; Mark 15:26"> And Pilat wroot a title, and sette on the cros; and it was writun, Jhesu of Nazareth, king of Jewis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 26:14; Revelation 16:16; Acts 22:2"> Therfor manye of the Jewis redden this title, for the place where Jhesus was crucified, was niy the citee, and it was writun in Ebreu, Greek, and Latyn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 19:14; John 19:19"> Therfor the bischops of the Jewis seiden to Pilat, Nyle thou write kyng of Jewis, but for he seide, Y am king of Jewis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 76:10; Psalms 65:7; Proverbs 8:29"> Pilat answeride, That that Y haue writun, Y haue writun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 23:34; Mark 15:24; Matthew 27:35"> Therfor the knyytis whanne thei hadden crucified hym, token hise clothis, and maden foure partis, to ech knyyt a part, and a coot. And the coot was without seem, and wouun al aboute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 22:18; John 19:28; John 19:36–37"> Therfor thei seiden togidere, Kitte we not it, but caste we lot, whos it is; that the scripture be fulfillid, seiynge, Thei partiden my clothis to hem, and on my cloth thei casten lot. And the kniytis diden these thingis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 23:49; Matthew 27:55–56; John 20:1"> But bisidis the cros of Jhesu stoden his modir, and the sistir of his modir, Marie Cleofe, and Marie Maudeleyne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 21:24; John 20:2; John 2:4"> Therfor whanne Jhesu hadde seyn his modir, and the disciple stondynge, whom he louyde, he seith to hise modir, Womman, lo thi sone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 12:48–50; John 16:32; 1 Timothy 5:2–4"> Aftyrward he seith to the disciple, Lo! thi modir. And fro that our the disciple took hir in to his modir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Psalms 22:15; John 19:30"> Aftirward Jhesus witynge, that now alle thingis ben endid, that the scripture were fulfillid, he seith, Y thirste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 19:18; Luke 23:36; Mark 15:36"> And a vessel was set ful of vynegre. And thei &#96;leiden in isope aboute the spounge ful of vynegre, and putten to his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 17:4; Philippians 2:8; Colossians 2:14–17"> Therfor whanne Jhesus hadde &#96;takun the vynegre, he seid, It is endid. And &#96;whanne his heed was bowid doun, &#96;he yaf vp the goost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 19:14; John 19:42; Mark 15:42"> Therfor for it was the pask eue, that the bodies schulden not abide on the cros in the sabat, for that was a greet sabat dai, the Jewis preiden Pilat, that the hipis of hem schulden be brokun, and thei takun awei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 19:18; Luke 23:39–43"> Therfor knyytis camen, and thei braken the thies of the firste, and of the tothere, that was crucified with hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> But whanne thei weren comun to Jhesu, as thei sayn him deed thanne, thei braken not hise thies;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 John 5:6; Zechariah 12:10; Zechariah 13:1"> but oon of the knyytis openyde his side with a spere, and anoon blood and watir wenten out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 21:24; Hebrews 2:3–4; 1 John 1:1–3"> And he that saiy, bare witnessyng, and his witnessing is trewe; and he woot that he seith trewe thingis, that ye bileue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 34:20; Numbers 9:12; Exodus 12:46"> And these thingis weren don, that the scripture schulde be fulfillid, Ye schulen not breke a boon of hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Zechariah 12:10; Revelation 1:7; Psalms 22:16–17"> And eftsoone another scripture seith, Thei schulen se in whom thei piyten thorow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Mark 15:42–47; Matthew 27:57–61; Proverbs 29:25"> But after these thingis Joseph of Armathi preyede Pilat, that he schulde take awei the bodi of Jhesu, for that he was a disciple of Jhesu, but priui for drede of the Jewis. And Pilat suffride. And so he cam, and took awei the bodi of Jhesu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 7:50–52; Song of Solomon 4:14; John 3:1–21"> And Nychodeme cam also, that hadde come to hym first bi nyyt, and brouyte a meddlynge of myrre and aloes, as it were an hundrid pound.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 11:44; John 20:5–7; Acts 5:6"> And thei token the bodi of Jhesu, and boundun it in lynun clothis with swete smellynge oynementis, as it is custom to Jewis for to birie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 23:53; Matthew 27:60; John 20:15"> And in the place where he was crucified, was a yerd, and in the yerd a newe graue, in which yit no man was leid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 19:31; John 19:14; Isaiah 53:9"> Therfor there thei putten Jhesu, for the vigilie of Jewis feeste, for the sepulcre was niy.</span>
