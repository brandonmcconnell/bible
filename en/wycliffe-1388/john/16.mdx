<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 2:8; Matthew 13:21; Matthew 26:31–33"> These thingis Y haue spokun to you, that ye be not sclaundrid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 9:22; Acts 26:9–11; Romans 10:2–3"> Thei schulen make you with outen the synagogis, but the our cometh, that ech man that sleeth you, deme that he doith seruyce to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 15:21; John 17:25; John 8:55"> And thei schulen do to you these thingis, for thei han not knowun the fadir, nether me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 14:29; Mark 13:23; John 17:12–13"> But these thingis Y spak to you, that whanne the our &#96;of hem schal come, ye haue mynde, that Y seide to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 13:36; John 7:33; John 16:28"> Y seide not to you these thingis fro the bigynnyng, for Y was with you. And now Y go to hym that sente me, and no man of you axith me, Whidur &#96;thou goist?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 14:1; John 16:20–22; Luke 24:17"> but for Y haue spokun to you these thingis, heuynesse hath fulfillid youre herte.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 14:26; John 14:16–17; John 15:26"> But Y seie to you treuthe, it spedith to you, that Y go; for if Y go not forth, the coumfortour schal not come to you; but if Y go forth, Y schal sende hym to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 2:37; Jude 1:15; Zechariah 12:10"> And whanne he cometh, he schal repreue the world of synne, and of riytwisnesse, and of doom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 15:22–25; John 8:23–24; John 12:47–48"> Of synne, for thei han not bileued in me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:21; 1 Peter 3:18; Romans 10:3–4"> and of riytwisnesse, for Y go to the fadir, and now ye schulen not se me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 12:31; 1 John 3:8; Hebrews 2:14"> but of doom, for the prince of this world is now demed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 4:33; John 15:15; 1 Corinthians 3:1–2"> Yit Y haue many thingis for to seie to you, but ye moun not bere hem now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 14:26; 1 Corinthians 2:10–13; John 14:17"> But whanne thilke spirit of treuthe cometh, he schal teche you al trewthe; for he schal not speke of hym silf, but what euer thinges he schal here, he schal speke; and he schal telle to you tho thingis that ben to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 John 4:13–14; 2 Corinthians 3:14–18; John 15:26"> He schal clarifie me, for of myne he schal take, and schal telle to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 17:10; Matthew 11:27; John 3:35"> Alle thingis &#96;whiche euer the fadir hath, ben myne; therfor Y seide to you, for of myne he schal take, and schal telle to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 7:33; Acts 1:3; John 14:18–24"> A litil, and thanne ye schulen not se me; and eftsoone a litil, and ye schulen se me, for Y go to the fadir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 16:16; Mark 9:32; John 16:5"> Therfor summe of hise disciplis seiden togidere, What is this thing that he seith to vs, A litil, and ye schulen not se me; and eftsoone a litil, and ye schulen se me, for Y go to the fadir?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 24:25; Matthew 16:9–11; Hebrews 5:12"> Therfor thei seiden, What is this that he seith to vs, A litil? we witen not what he spekith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 16:30; John 16:16; John 14:19"> And Jhesus knew, that thei wolden axe hym, and he seide to hem, Of this thing ye seken among you, for Y seide, A litil, and ye schulen not se me; and eftsoone a litil, and ye schulen se me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 1:2; Matthew 5:4; Psalms 126:5–6"> Treuli, treuli, Y seie to you, that ye schulen mourne and wepe, but the world schal haue ioye; and ye schulen be soreuful, but youre sorewe schal turne in to ioye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 113:9; Isaiah 13:8; 1 Thessalonians 5:3"> A womman whanne sche berith child, hath heuynesse, for hir tyme is comun; but whanne sche hath borun a sone, now sche thenkith not on the peyne, for ioye, for a man is borun in to the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 1:8; Acts 13:52; John 16:6"> And therfor ye han now sorew, but eftsoone Y schal se you, and youre herte schal haue ioie, and no man schal take fro you youre ioie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 15:7; John 14:13–14; Matthew 21:22"> And in that day ye schulen not axe me ony thing; treuli, treuli, &#96;Y seie to you, if ye axen the fadir ony thing in my name, he schal yyue to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–8; James 4:2–3; John 15:11"> &#96;Til now ye axiden no thing in my name; &#96;axe ye, &#96;and ye schulen take, that youre ioie be ful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 16:12; Mark 4:13; Matthew 13:34–35"> Y haue spokun to you these thingis in prouerbis; the our cometh, whanne now Y schal not speke to you in prouerbis, but opynli of my fadir Y schal telle to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 8:34; John 17:24; John 17:19"> In that dai ye schulen axe in my name; and Y seie not to you, that Y schal preye the fadir of you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 14:21; John 14:23; John 17:23"> for the fadir hym silf loueth you, for ye han loued me, and han bileued, that Y wente out fro God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 13:1; John 13:3; John 8:14"> Y wente out fro the fadir, and Y cam in to the world; eftsoone Y leeue the world, and Y go to the fadir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 16:25"> Hise disciplis seiden to hym, Lo! now thou spekist opynli, and thou seist no prouerbe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 21:17; John 17:8; John 16:17"> Now we witen, that thou wost alle thingis; and it is not nede to thee, that ony man axe thee. In this thing we bileuen, that thou wentist out fro God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 9:44–45; John 13:38"> Jhesus answeride to hem, Now ye bileuen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; Matthew 26:31; John 8:29"> Lo! the our cometh, and now it cometh, that ye be disparplid, ech in to hise owne thingis, and that ye leeue me aloone; and Y am not aloone, for the fadir is with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 14:27; 1 John 5:4; 2 Thessalonians 3:16"> These thingis Y haue spokun to you, that ye haue pees in me; in the world ye schulen haue disese, but trust ye, Y haue ouercomun the world.</span>
