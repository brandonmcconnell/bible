<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 19:39; John 7:47–50; John 3:10"> And there was a man of the Farisees, Nychodeme bi name, a prince of the Jewis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 5:36; Acts 10:38; Acts 2:22"> And he cam to Jhesu bi niyt, and seide to hym, Rabi, we witen, that thou art comun fro God maister; for no man may do these signes, that thou doist, but God be with hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:17; John 3:5–6; 1 Peter 1:23–25"> Jhesus answerde, and seide to hym, Treuli, treuli, Y seie to thee, but a man be borun ayen, he may not se the kyngdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; 1 Corinthians 1:18; John 3:3"> Nychodeme seide to hym, Hou may a man be borun, whanne he is eeld? whether he may entre ayen in to his modris wombe, and be borun ayen?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:25–27; Ephesians 5:26; Acts 2:38"> Jhesus answeride, Treuli, treuli, Y seie to thee, but a man be borun ayen of watir, and of the Hooli Goost, he may not entre in to the kyngdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 5:16–21; Ezekiel 36:26–27; Romans 8:4–9"> &#96;That that is borun of the fleisch, is fleisch; and &#96;that that is borun of spirit, is spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 3:3; Ephesians 4:22–24; Romans 12:1–2"> Wondre thou not, for Y seide to thee, It bihoueth you to be borun ayen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:4–5; 1 Corinthians 2:11; Acts 2:2"> The spirit brethith where he wole, and thou herist his vois, but thou wost not, fro whennus he cometh, ne whidir he goith; so is ech man that is borun of the spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 6:60; Luke 1:34; Isaiah 42:16"> Nychodeme answeride, and seide to hym, Hou moun these thingis be don?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 22:29; Jeremiah 8:8–9; Ezekiel 11:19"> Jhesus answeride, and seide to hym, Thou art a maister in Israel, and knowist not these thingis?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 8:38; 1 John 5:6–12; John 12:49"> Treuli, treuli, Y seie to thee, for we speken that that we witen, and we witnessen that that we han seyn, and ye taken not oure witnessyng.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:1–2; 1 Corinthians 2:7–9; Hebrews 5:11"> If Y haue seid to you ertheli thingis, and ye bileuen not, hou if Y seie to you heueneli thingis, schulen ye bileue?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 30:4; John 6:38; Acts 2:34"> And no man stieth in to heuene, but he that cam doun fro heuene, mannys sone that is in heuene.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 21:7–9; John 8:28; John 12:32–34"> And as Moises areride a serpent in desert, so it bihoueth mannys sone to be reisid,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 3:16; John 6:40; John 12:44–46"> that ech man that bileueth in hym, perische not, but haue euerlastynge lijf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 5:8; 1 John 4:9–10; John 3:15"> For God louede so the world, that he yaf his &#96;oon bigetun sone, that ech man that bileueth in him perische not, but haue euerlastynge lijf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 John 4:14; Luke 19:10; John 12:47–48"> For God sente not his sone in to the world, that he iuge the world, but that the world be saued bi him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 16:16; 1 John 5:10; Romans 8:1"> He that bileueth in hym, is not demed; but he that bileueth not, is now demed, for he bileueth not in the name of the &#96;oon bigetun sone of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 8:12; 2 Thessalonians 2:12; John 9:39–41"> And this is the dom, for liyt cam in to the world, and men loueden more derknessis than liyt; for her werkes weren yuele.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11–13; John 7:7; Job 24:13–17"> For ech man that doith yuele, hatith the liyt; and he cometh not to the liyt, that hise werkis be not repreued.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 1:6; Psalms 139:23–24; 3 John 1:11"> But he that doith treuthe, cometh to the liyt, that hise werkis be schewid, that thei ben don in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 4:1–3; John 3:26; John 2:13"> Aftir these thingis Jhesus cam, and hise disciplis, in to the loond of Judee, and there he dwellide with hem, and baptiside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 1:4–5; Ezekiel 43:2; Matthew 3:5–6"> And Joon was baptisinge in Ennon, bisidis Salym, for many watris weren there; and thei camen, and weren baptisid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 4:12; Matthew 14:3; Mark 6:17"> And Joon was not yit sent in to prisoun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 2:6; Matthew 3:11; Hebrews 6:2"> Therfor a questioun was maad of Jonys disciplis with the Jewis, of the purificacioun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 1:7; John 1:15; John 12:19"> And thei camen to Joon, and seiden &#96;to hym, Maister, he that was with thee biyonde Jordan, to whom thou hast borun witnessyng, lo! he baptisith, and alle men comen to hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 1:17; 1 Corinthians 4:7; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> Joon answerde, and seide, A man may not take ony thing, but it be youun to hym fro heuene.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 1:20; John 1:23; Matthew 3:11–12"> Ye you silf beren witnessyng to me, that Y seide, Y am not Crist, but that Y am sent bifore hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 9:15; Hosea 2:19; Isaiah 54:5"> He that hath a wijf, is the hosebonde; but the freend of the spouse that stondith, and herith hym, ioieth with ioye, for the vois of the spouse. Therfor in this thing my ioye is fulfillid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 1:18; Psalms 72:17–19; Hebrews 3:2–6"> It bihoueth hym to wexe, but me to be maad lesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 8:23; Matthew 28:18; Romans 9:5"> He that cam from aboue, is aboue alle; he that is of the erthe, spekith of the erthe; he that cometh from heuene, is aboue alle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 3:11; John 15:15; John 1:11"> And he witnessith that thing that he hath seie, and herde, and no man takith his witnessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 6:17; Ephesians 1:13; Titus 1:1–2"> But he that takith his witnessyng, hath confermyd that God is sothefast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 10:38; Matthew 12:18; John 15:26"> But he whom God hath sent, spekith the wordis of God; for not to mesure God yyueth the spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 28:18; John 5:22; John 5:20"> The fadir loueth the sone, and he hath youun alle thingis in his hoond.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 5:24; John 1:12; 1 Thessalonians 5:9"> He that bileueth in the sone, hath euerlastynge lijf; but he that is vnbileueful to the sone, schal not se euerlastynge lijf, but the wraththe of God dwellith on hym.</span>
