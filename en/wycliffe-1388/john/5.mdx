<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 23:14–17; Exodus 34:23"> Aftir these thingis ther was a feeste dai of Jewis, and Jhesus wente vp to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:39; Nehemiah 3:1; Nehemiah 3:32"> And in Jerusalem is a waissynge place, that in Ebrew is named Bethsaida, and hath fyue porchis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 3:1–4; Luke 7:22; Matthew 15:30"> In these lay a greet multitude of sike men, blynde, crokid, and drie, abidynge the mouyng of the watir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> For the aungel &#96;of the Lord cam doun certeyne tymes in to the watir, and the watir was moued; and he that first cam doun in to the sisterne,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="that is, a watir gederid togidere, hauinge no fiyss.">a</a></sup>aftir the mouynge of the watir, was maad hool of what euer sijknesse he was holdun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 3:2; Luke 8:43; Mark 9:21"> And a man was there, hauynge eiyte and thritti yeer in his sikenesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 18:41; Hebrews 4:15; Hebrews 4:13"> And whanne Jhesus hadde seyn hym liggynge, and hadde knowun, that he hadde myche tyme, he seith to hym, Wolt thou be maad hool?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 72:12; Deuteronomy 32:36; 1 Corinthians 9:24"> The sijk man answerde to hym, Lord, Y haue no man, that whanne the watir is moued, to putte me &#96;in to the cisterne; for the while Y come, anothir goith doun bifor me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 5:24; Matthew 9:6; Mark 2:11"> Jhesus seith to hym, Rise vp, take thi bed, and go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 3:7–8; John 9:14; Mark 3:2–4"> And anoon the man was maad hool, and took vp his bed, and wente forth. And it was sabat in that dai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:21; Luke 6:2; John 9:16"> Therfor the Jewis seiden to him that was maad hool, It is sabat, it is not leueful to thee, to take awei thi bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 2:9–11; John 9:16"> He answeride to hem, He that made me hool, seide to me, Take thi bed, and go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Judges 6:29; Romans 10:2"> Therfor thei axiden him, What man &#96;is that, that seide to thee, Take vp thi bed, and go?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 4:30; Luke 24:31; John 8:59"> But he that was maad hool, wiste not who it was. And Jhesus bowide awei fro the puple, that was set in the place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 8:11; Leviticus 26:23–24; Psalms 118:18"> Aftirward Jhesus foond hym in the temple, and seide to hym, Lo! thou art maad hool; now nyle thou do synne, lest any worse thing bifalle to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 9:11–12; John 4:29; John 9:25"> Thilke man wente, and telde to the Jewis, that it was Jhesu that made hym hool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 10:39; John 7:19–20; Mark 3:6"> Therfor the Jewis pursueden Jhesu, for he dide this thing in the sabat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 9:4; John 14:10; Colossians 1:16"> And Jhesus answeride to hem, My fadir worchith til now, and Y worche.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 10:30; John 10:33; John 19:7"> Therfor the Jewis souyten more to sle hym, for not oneli he brak the sabat, but he seide that God was his fadir, and made hym euene to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 12:49; John 8:28; John 14:10"> Therfor Jhesus answerde, and seide to hem, Treuli, treuli, Y seye to you, the sone may not of hym silf do ony thing, but that thing that he seeth the fadir doynge; for what euere thingis he doith, the sone doith in lijk maner tho thingis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 3:35; Matthew 3:17; John 17:26"> For the fadir loueth the sone, and schewith to hym alle thingis that he doith; and he schal schewe to hym grettere werkis than these, that ye wondren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 8:11; Deuteronomy 32:39; John 11:25"> For as the fadir reisith deed men, and quykeneth, so the sone quykeneth whom he wole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 5:27; Acts 17:31; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> For nethir the fadir iugith ony man, but hath youun ech doom to the sone,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 John 2:23; Luke 10:16; Matthew 11:27"> that alle men onoure the sone, as thei onouren the fadir. He that onourith not the sone, onourith not the fadir that sente hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 3:36; John 6:40; 1 John 5:11–13"> Treuli, treuli, Y seie to you, that he that herith my word, and bileueth to hym that sente me, hath euerlastynge lijf, and he cometh not in to doom, but passith fro deeth in to lijf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 5:28; Ephesians 5:14; Ephesians 2:5"> Treuli, treuli Y seie to you, for the our cometh, and now it is, whanne deed men schulen here the vois of &#96;Goddis sone, and thei that heren, schulen lyue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 11:26; John 1:4; Exodus 3:14"> For as the fadir hath lijf in hym silf, so he yaf to the sone, to haue lijf in him silf;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 10:42; John 5:22; Acts 17:31"> and he yaf to hym power to make doom, for he is mannys sone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 20:12; 1 Thessalonians 4:14–17; Isaiah 26:19"> Nyle ye wondre this, for the our cometh, in which alle men that ben in birielis, schulen here the voice of Goddis sone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Daniel 12:2–3; Romans 2:6–10; Acts 24:15"> And thei that han do goode thingis, schulen go in to ayenrisyng of lijf; but thei that han done yuele thingis, in to ayenrisyng of doom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 6:38; John 5:19; John 4:34"> Y may no thing do of my silf, but as Y here, Y deme, and my doom is iust, for Y seke not my wille, but the wille of the fadir that sente me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 27:2; John 8:54; Revelation 3:14"> If Y bere witnessing of my silf, my witnessyng is not trewe;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 1:33; John 12:28–30; John 5:36–37"> another is that berith witnessyng of me, and Y woot that his witnessyng is trewe, that he berith of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 1:29–34; John 3:26–36; John 1:15–27"> Ye senten to Joon, and he bar witnessyng to treuthe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 19:41–42; John 20:31; 1 John 5:9"> But Y take not witnessyng of man; but Y seie these thingis, that ye be saaf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Peter 1:19; John 1:7–8; Matthew 11:11"> He was a lanterne brennynge and schynynge; but ye wolden glade at an our in his liyt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 10:25; John 10:37–38; John 15:24"> But Y haue more witnessyng than Joon, for the werkis that my fadir yaf to me to perfourme hem, thilke werkis that Y do beren witnessyng of me, that the fadir sente me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 8:18; John 1:18; Matthew 3:17"> And the fadir that sente me, he bar witnessyng of me. Nether ye herden euere his vois, nether ye seien his licnesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 John 2:14; Deuteronomy 6:6–9; John 12:44–48"> And ye han not his word dwellynge in you; for ye byleuen not to hym, whom he sente.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 24:27; Luke 24:44; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> Seke ye scripturis, in which ye gessen to haue euerlastynge lijf; and tho it ben, that beren witnessyng of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 3:19; John 1:11; Psalms 81:11"> And ye wolen not come to me, that ye haue lijf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 7:18; John 5:34; 1 Thessalonians 2:6"> Y take not clerenesse of men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 John 4:20; 1 John 3:17; John 2:25"> but Y haue knowun you, that ye han not the loue of God in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 10:25; Matthew 24:5; John 17:4–6"> Y cam in the name of my fadir, and ye token not me. If another come in his owne name, ye schulen resseyue hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 12:43; Romans 2:29; Philippians 2:3"> Hou moun ye bileue, that resseyuen glorie ech of othere, and ye seken not the glorie &#96;that is of God aloone?</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Romans 3:19–20; Galatians 3:10; 2 Corinthians 3:7–11"> Nyle ye gesse, that Y am to accuse you anentis the fadir; it is Moises that accusith you, in whom ye hopen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15; Deuteronomy 18:18–19; John 1:45"> For if ye bileueden to Moises, perauenture ye schulden bileue also to me; for he wroot of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Luke 16:31; Luke 16:29"> But if ye bileuen not to hise lettris, hou schulen ye bileue to my wordis?</span>
