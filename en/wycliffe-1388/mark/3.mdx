<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 1:28–30; Isaiah 42:4; Luke 6:8"> And he seide to the man that hadde a drie hoond, Rise in to the myddil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 6:9; Matthew 12:10–12; Hosea 6:6"> And he seith to hem, Is it leeueful to do wel in the sabatis, ether yuel? to make a soul saaf, ether to leese? And thei weren stille.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 6:10; Ephesians 4:18; Romans 11:25"> And he biheeld hem aboute with wraththe, and hadde sorewe on the blyndnesse of her herte, and seith to the man, Hold forth thin hoond. And he helde forth, and his hoond was restorid to hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 22:16; Mark 12:13; Matthew 12:14"> Sotheli Farisees yeden out anoon, and maden a counsel with Erodians ayens hym, hou thei schulden lese hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 4:25; Luke 6:17; Matthew 12:15"> But Jhesus with hise disciplis wente to the see; and myche puple fro Galilee and Judee suede hym,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:15; Isaiah 34:5; Matthew 11:21"> and fro Jerusalem, and fro Ydume, and fro biyondis Jordan, and thei that weren aboute Tire and Sidon, a greet multitude, heringe the thingis that he dide, and cam to hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 5:30; John 6:15"> And Jhesus seide to hise disciplis, that the boot schulde serue hym, for the puple, lest thei thristen hym;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 14:36; Mark 6:56; Matthew 4:23"> for he heelide many, so that thei felden fast to hym, to touche hym. And hou many euer hadde syknessis, and vnclene spirits,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Mark 1:23–24; Luke 8:28"> whanne thei seyen hym, felden doun to hym, and crieden, seiynge, Thou art the sone of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:16; Mark 1:25; Mark 1:34"> And greetli he manasside hem, that thei schulden not make hym knowun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:1; Luke 6:12–16; Matthew 10:1–4"> And he wente in to an hille, and clepide to hym whom he wolde; and thei camen to hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Luke 9:1–6; John 15:16"> And he made, that there weren twelue with hym, to sende hem to preche.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And he yaf to hem pouwer to heele sijknessis, and to caste out feendis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 1:42; Acts 1:13; Matthew 16:16–18"> And to Symount he yaf a name Petre, and he clepide James of Zebede and Joon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Mark 14:33; Jeremiah 23:29"> the brother of James, and he yaf to hem names Boenarges, that is, sones of thundryng.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Mark 2:14; Matthew 9:9"> And he clepide Andrew and Filip, and Bartholomew and Matheu, and Thomas and James Alfey, and Thadee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 6:71; John 13:2; Acts 1:16–25"> and Symount Cananee, and Judas Scarioth, that bitraiede hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 6:31; John 4:31–34; Mark 9:28"> And thei camen to an hous, and the puple cam togidere eftsoone, so that thei miyten not ete breed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 26:24; John 10:20; Mark 3:31"> And whanne his kynnysmen hadden herd, thei wenten out &#96;to holde him; for thei seiden, that he is turned in to woodnesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 15:1; Matthew 10:25; Matthew 9:34"> And the scribis that camen doun fro Jerusalem, seiden, That he hath Belsabub, and that in the prince of deuelis he castith out fendis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 11:17–23; Matthew 12:25–30; Matthew 13:34"> And he clepide hem togidir, and he seide to hem in parablis, Hou may Sathanas caste out Sathanas?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 11:14; Isaiah 19:2–3; 2 Samuel 20:6"> And if a rewme be departid ayens it silf, thilke rewme may not stonde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 3:16; Psalms 133:1; Galatians 5:15"> And if an hous be disparpoilid on it silf, thilke hous may not stonde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And if Sathanas hath risun ayens hym silf, he is departid, and he schal not mowe stonde, but hath an ende.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 49:24–26; Matthew 12:29; Luke 11:21–23"> No man may go in to a stronge mannus hous, and take awey hise vessels, but he bynde first the stronge man, and thanne he schal spoile his hous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 12:10; Matthew 12:31–32; 1 John 5:16"> Treuli Y seie to you, that alle synnes and blasfemyes, bi whiche thei han blasfemed, schulen be foryouun to the sones of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 12:31–32; Luke 12:10; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> But he that blasfemeth ayens the Hooli Goost, hath not remissioun in to with outen ende, but he schal be gilty of euerlastynge trespas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 10:20; Mark 3:22"> For thei seiden, He hath an vnclene spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 8:19–21; Matthew 12:46–50"> And his modir and britheren camen, and thei stoden withoutforth, and senten to hym, and clepiden hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And the puple sat aboute hym; and thei seien to hym, Lo! thi modir and thi britheren with outforth seken thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 3:21; Deuteronomy 33:9; Mark 6:3"> And he answeride to hem, and seide, Who is my modir and my britheren?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 12:49–50; Luke 11:27–28; Romans 8:29"> And he bihelde thilke that saten aboute hym, and seide, Lo! my modir and my britheren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 2:17; Matthew 7:21; 1 John 3:22–23"> For who that doith the wille of God, he is my brothir, and my sistir, and modir.</span>
