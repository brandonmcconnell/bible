<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 5:1–3; Mark 2:13; Luke 8:4–10"> And eft Jhesus bigan to teche at the see; and myche puple was gaderid to hym, so that he wente in to a boot, and sat in the see, and al the puple was aboute the see on the loond.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 13:34–35; Mark 3:23; Mark 4:11"> And he tauyte hem in parablis many thingis. And he seide to hem in his techyng,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 2:5; Matthew 13:26; Mark 4:26–29"> Here ye. Lo! a man sowynge goith out to sowe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 13:19; Mark 4:15; Matthew 13:4"> And the while he sowith, summe seed felde aboute the weie, and briddis of heuene camen, and eeten it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:26; Luke 8:6; Luke 8:13"> Othere felde doun on stony places, where it had not myche erthe; and anoon it spronge vp, for it had not depnesse of erthe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 1:11; Jeremiah 17:5–8; Jonah 4:8"> And whanne the sunne roos vp, it welewide for heete, and it driede vp, for it hadde no roote.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:3; Luke 8:14; Genesis 3:17–18"> And othere felde doun in to thornes, and thornes sprongen vp, and strangliden it, and it yaf not fruyt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 15:5; Colossians 1:6; Mark 4:20"> And other felde doun in to good loond, and yaf fruyt, springynge vp, and wexynge; and oon brouyte thretti foold, and oon sixti fold, and oon an hundrid fold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 11:15; Mark 4:23–24; Revelation 3:22"> And he seide, He that hath eeris of heryng, here he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 13:10–17; Mark 4:34; Proverbs 13:20"> And whanne he was bi hym silf, tho twelue that weren with hym axiden hym to expowne the parable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 11:25; Luke 8:10; 1 John 5:20"> And he seide to hem, To you it is youun to knowe the priuete of the kyngdom of God. But to hem that ben with outforth, alle thingis be maad in parablis, that thei seynge se,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; Isaiah 44:18; Matthew 13:14–15"> and se not, and thei herynge here and vnderstonde not; lest sum tyme thei be conuertid, and synnes be foryouun to hem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Matthew 13:51–52; Mark 7:17–18"> And he seide to hem, Knowe not ye this parable? and hou ye schulen knowe alle parablis?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 8:4; Matthew 13:19; Matthew 13:37"> He that sowith, sowith a word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Matthew 13:19; 2 Corinthians 2:11"> But these it ben that ben aboute the weie, where the word is sowun; and whanne thei han herd, anoon cometh Satanas, and takith awei the word that is sowun in her hertis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 6:20; Luke 8:13; Matthew 13:20–21"> And in lijk maner ben these that ben sowun on stony placis, whiche whanne thei han herd the word, anoon thei taken it with ioye;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 11:6; 1 Thessalonians 3:3–5; Matthew 12:31"> and thei han not roote in hem silf, but thei ben lastynge a litil tyme; aftirward whanne tribulacioun risith, and persecucioun for the word, anoon thei ben sclaundrid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 8:14; Jeremiah 4:3; Matthew 13:22"> And ther ben othir that ben sowun in thornes; these it ben that heren the word,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; 1 Timothy 6:9–10; 1 Timothy 6:17"> and disese of the world, and disseit of ritchessis, and othir charge of coueytise entrith, and stranglith the word, and it is maad with out fruyt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; Luke 8:15; John 15:4–5"> And these it ben that ben sowun on good lond, whiche heren the word, and taken, and maken fruyt, oon thritti fold, oon sixti fold, and oon an hundrid fold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 5:15; Luke 11:33; Luke 8:16"> And he seide to hem, Wher a lanterne cometh, that it be put vndur a buschel, or vndur a bed? nay, but that it be put on a candilstike?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 8:17; Ecclesiastes 12:14; Matthew 10:26–27"> Ther is no thing hid, that schal not be maad opyn; nethir ony thing is pryuey, that schal not come in to opyn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 4:9; Matthew 11:15; Revelation 2:29"> If ony man haue eeris of heryng, here he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 2:1; Luke 8:18; Matthew 7:2"> And he seide to hem, Se ye what ye heren. In what mesure ye meten, it schal be metun to you ayen, and be cast to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 13:12; Luke 8:18; Matthew 25:28–29"> For it schal be youun to hym that hath, and it schal be takun awei fro him that hath not, also that that he hath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 13:24; 1 Corinthians 3:6–9; Luke 8:11"> And he seide, So the kingdom of God is, as if a man caste seede in to the erthe,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:5; 2 Thessalonians 1:3; 1 Corinthians 15:37–38"> and he sleepe, and it rise up niyt and dai, and brynge forth seede, and wexe faste, while he woot not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 4:31–32; Hosea 6:3; Philippians 1:9–11"> For the erthe makith fruyt, first the gras, aftirward the ere, and aftir ful fruyt in the ere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joel 3:13; Matthew 13:30; Matthew 13:40–43"> And whanne of it silf it hath brouyt forth fruyt, anoon he sendith a sikil, for repyng tyme is come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 13:31–32; Luke 13:18–21; Lamentations 2:13"> And he seide, To what thing schulen we likne the kyngdom of God? or to what parable schulen we comparisoun it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 19:20; Luke 13:18–19; Daniel 2:44–45"> As a corne of seneuei, which whanne it is sowun in the erthe, is lesse than alle seedis that ben in the erthe;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Daniel 4:20–22; Daniel 4:10–14; Ezekiel 31:3–10"> and whanne it is sprongun up, it waxith in to a tre, and is maad gretter than alle erbis; and it makith grete braunchis, so that briddis of heuene moun dwelle vndur the schadewe therof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 13:34–35; John 16:12; Hebrews 5:11–14"> And in many suche parablis he spak to hem the word, as thei myyten here;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 16:25; Mark 4:10; Matthew 15:15–20"> and he spak not to hem with out parable. But he expownede to hise disciplis alle thingis bi hemsilf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 8:18; Luke 8:22; Luke 8:25"> And he seide to hem in that dai, whanne euenyng was come, Passe we ayenward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 3:9; Mark 4:1; Mark 5:2"> And thei leften the puple, and token hym, so that he was in a boot; and othere bootys weren with hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 8:23–24; Job 1:19; Luke 8:22–23"> And a greet storm of wynde was maad, and keste wawis in to the boot, so that the boot was ful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Peter 5:7; Hebrews 4:15; Isaiah 54:6–8"> And he was in the hyndir part of the boot, and slepte on a pilewe. And thei reisen hym, and seien to hym, Maistir, perteyneth it not to thee, that we perischen?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 107:29; Psalms 89:9; Psalms 65:7"> And he roos vp, and manasside the wynde, and seide to the see, Be stille, wexe doumbe. And the wynde ceesside, and greet pesiblenesse was maad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 8:25; Matthew 8:26; Matthew 14:31"> And he seide to hem, What dreden ye? &#96;Ye han no feith yit?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 8:25; Job 38:11; Psalms 89:7"> And thei dredden with greet drede, and seiden &#96;ech to other, Who, gessist thou, is this? for the wynde and the see obeschen to hym.</span>
