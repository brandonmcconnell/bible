<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 23:56–12; John 20:1–10; Matthew 28:1–10"> And whanne the sabat was passid, Marie Maudeleyne, and Marie of James, and Salomee bouyten swete smellynge oynementis, to come and to anoynte Jhesu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 20:1; Luke 24:1; Matthew 28:1"> And ful eerli in oon of the woke daies, thei camen to the sepulcre, whanne the sunne was risun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 15:46–47; Matthew 27:60–66"> And thei seiden togidere, Who schal meue awey to vs the stoon fro the dore of the sepulcre?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 28:2–4; Luke 24:2; John 20:1"> And thei bihelden, and seien the stoon walewid awei, for it was ful greet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 20:11–12; Luke 24:3–5; John 20:8"> And thei yeden in to the sepulcre, and sayn a yonglyng, hilide with a white stole, sittynge &#96;at the riythalf; and thei weren afeerd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 1:17–18; 1 Corinthians 15:3–7; Luke 24:46"> Which seith to hem, Nyle ye drede; ye seken Jhesu of Nazareth crucified; he is risun, he is not here; lo! the place where thei leiden hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 14:28; Matthew 26:32; Matthew 28:10"> But go ye, and seie ye to hise disciplis, and to Petir, that he schal go bifor you in to Galilee; there ye schulen se hym, as he seide to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 24:22–24; Luke 24:9–11; Mark 16:5–6"> And thei yeden out, and fledden fro the sepulcre; for drede and quakyng had assailed hem, and to no man thei seiden ony thing, for thei dredden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 20:14–19; Luke 8:2; John 20:12"> And Jhesus roos eerli the firste dai of the woke, and apperid firste to Marie Maudeleyne, fro whom he had caste out seuene deuelis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 20:18; Luke 24:17; John 16:6"> And sche yede, and tolde to hem that hadden ben with hym, whiche weren weilynge and wepynge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 24:11; Mark 16:13–14; Mark 9:19"> And thei herynge that he lyuyde, and was seyn of hir, bileueden not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 24:13–32; John 21:14; John 21:1"> But after these thingis whanne tweyne of hem wandriden, he was schewid in anothir liknesse to hem goynge in to a toun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 20:25; Luke 24:33–35; John 20:8"> And thei yeden, and telden to the othir, and nether thei bileueden to hem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:5; Luke 24:36–43; Hebrews 3:7–8"> But &#96;at the laste, whanne the enleuene disciplis saten at the mete, Jhesus apperide to hem, and repreuede the vnbileue of hem, and the hardnesse of herte, for thei bileueden not to hem, that hadden seyn that he was risun fro deeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 28:19; Mark 13:10; Acts 1:8"> And he seide to hem, Go ye in to al the world, and preche the gospel to eche creature.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 11:25–26; John 3:36; Romans 10:9–14"> Who that bileueth, and is baptisid, schal be saaf; but he that bileueth not, schal be dampned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 5:16; 1 Corinthians 12:28; Acts 8:7"> And these tokenes schulen sue hem, that bileuen. In my name thei schulen caste out feendis; thei schulen speke with newe tungis;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 10:19; James 5:14–15; Acts 28:3–6"> thei schulen do awei serpentis; and if thei drynke ony venym, it schal not noye hem. Thei schulen sette her hondis on sijk men, and thei schulen wexe hoole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Peter 3:22; Hebrews 1:3; Hebrews 12:2"> And the Lord Jhesu, aftir he hadde spokun to hem, was takun vp in to heuene, and he sittith on the riythalf of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:4–5; Acts 14:3; Romans 15:19"> And thei yeden forth, and prechiden euery where, for the Lord wrouyte with hem, and confermyde the word with signes folewynge.</span>
