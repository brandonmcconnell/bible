<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 21:1–11; John 12:14–19; Luke 19:29–40"> And whanne Jhesus cam nyy to Jerusalem and to Betanye, to the mount of Olyues, he sendith tweyne of hise disciplis, and seith to hem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 21:2–3; Luke 19:30–31"> Go ye in to the castel that is ayens you; and anoon as ye entren there ye schulen fynde a colt tied, on which no man hath sete yit; vntie ye, and brynge hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 10:36; Mark 14:15; Psalms 110:3"> And if ony man seye ony thing to you, What doen ye? seie ye, that he is nedeful to the Lord, and anoon he schal leeue hym hidir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 26:19; Luke 19:32–34; Matthew 21:6–7"> And thei yeden forth, and founden a colt tied bifor the yate with out forth, in the metyng of twei weies; and thei vntieden hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And summe of hem that stoden there seiden to hem, What doen ye, vntiynge the colt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And thei seiden to hem, as Jhesus comaundide hem; and thei leften it to hem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Luke 19:35–36; Matthew 21:7–8"> And thei brouyten the colt to Jhesu, and thei leiden on hym her clothis, and Jhesus sat on hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 23:40"> And many strewiden her clothis in the weie, othere men kittiden braunchis fro trees, and strewiden in the weie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 118:25–26; Matthew 21:9; Matthew 23:39"> And thei that wenten bifor, and that sueden, crieden, and seiden, Osanna,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:24–25; Luke 2:14; Luke 19:38–40"> blissid is he that cometh in the name of the Lord; blessid be the kyngdom of oure fadir Dauid that is come; Osanna in hiyest thingis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 8:1–2; Luke 19:41–45; Luke 21:37–38"> And he entride in to Jerusalem, in to the temple; and whanne he &#96;hadde seyn al thing aboute, whanne it was eue, he wente out in to Betanye, with the twelue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 21:18–22; Matthew 4:2; John 19:28"> And anothir daye, whanne he wente out of Betanye, he hungride.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 21:19; Luke 13:6–9; Isaiah 5:7"> And whanne he hadde seyn a fige tree afer hauynge leeues, he cam, if happili he schulde fynde ony thing theron; and whanne he cam to it, he foonde no thing, out takun leeues; for it was not tyme of figis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 21:19; Matthew 7:19; Matthew 3:10"> And Jhesus answeride and seide to it, Now neuer ete ony man fruyt of thee more. And hise disciplis herden;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 2:13–17; Matthew 21:12–16; Luke 19:45–47"> and thei camen to Jerusalem. And whanne he was entrid in to the temple, he bigan to caste out silleris and biggeris in the temple; and he turnede vpsodoun the bordis of chaungeris, and the chayeris of men that selden culueris;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> and he suffride not, that ony man schulde bere a vessel thorou the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; Jeremiah 7:11; Luke 19:46"> And he tauyte hem, and seide, Whether it is not writun, That myn hous schal be clepid the hous of preyng to alle folkis? but ye han maad it a denne of theues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; Mark 12:12; Mark 14:1–2"> And whanne this thing was herd, the princis of prestis and scribis souyten hou thei schulden leese hym; for thei dredden hym, for al the puple wondride on his techyng.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 21:37; Mark 11:11; John 12:36"> And whanne euenyng was come, he wente out of the citee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 15:13; Mark 11:14; John 15:6"> And as thei passiden forth eerli, thei sayn the fige tree maad drye fro the rootis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 3:33; Matthew 23:7; Matthew 25:41"> And Petir bithouyte hym, and seide to hym, Maister, lo! the fige tree, whom thou cursidist, is dried vp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 62:8; John 14:1; Mark 9:23"> And Jhesus answeride and seide to hem, Haue ye the feith of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 17:20; John 15:7; Matthew 21:21"> treuli Y seie to you, that who euer seith to this hil, Be thou takun, and cast in to the see; and doute not in his herte, but bileueth, that what euer he seie, schal be don, it schal be don to hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; 1 John 5:14–15; 1 John 3:22"> Therfor Y seie to you, alle thingis what euer thingis ye preynge schulen axe, bileue ye that ye schulen take, and thei schulen come to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 6:14–15; Colossians 3:13; Ephesians 4:32"> And whanne ye schulen stonde to preye, foryyue ye, if ye han ony thing ayens ony man, that youre fadir that is in heuenes, foryyue to you youre synnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And if ye foryyuen not, nether youre fadir that is in heuenes, schal foryyue to you youre synnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 20:1–8; Matthew 21:23–27; Psalms 2:1–5"> And eftsoone thei camen to Jerusalem. And whanne he walkide in the temple, the hiyeste prestis, and scribis, and the elder men camen to hym,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 2:14; Acts 7:38–39; Numbers 16:3"> and seyn to hym, In what power doist thou these thingis? or who yaf to thee this power, that thou do these thingis?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 20:3–8; Matthew 21:24; Isaiah 52:13"> Jhesus answeride and seide to hem, And Y schal axe you o word, and answere ye to me, and Y schal seie to you in what power Y do these thingis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 3:1–20; John 3:25–36; Mark 1:1–11"> Whether was the baptym of Joon of heuene, or of men? answere ye to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 1:29; John 1:34; Matthew 21:31–32"> And thei thouyten with ynne hem silf, seiynge, If we seien of heuene, he schal seie to vs, Whi thanne bileuen ye not to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 21:46; Mark 12:12; Matthew 14:5"> if we seien of men, we dreden the puple; for alle men hadden Joon, that he was verili a prophete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4; 2 Thessalonians 2:10–12"> And thei answeryden, and seien to Jhesu, We witen neuer. And Jhesu answerde, and seide to hem, Nether Y seie to you, in what power Y do these thingis.</span>
