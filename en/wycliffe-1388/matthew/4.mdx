<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 2:18; Luke 4:1–13; Mark 1:12–15"> Thanne Jhesus was led of a spirit in to desert, to be temptid of the feend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:18; Exodus 34:28; 1 Kings 19:8"> And whanne he hadde fastid fourti daies and fourti nyytis, aftirward he hungride.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 3:1–5; 1 Thessalonians 3:5; Numbers 11:4–6"> And the tempter cam nyy, and seide to hym, If thou art Goddis sone, seie that thes stoones be maad looues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:3; Luke 4:4; John 6:63"> Which answeride, and seide to hym, It is writun, Not oonli in breed luyeth man, but in ech word that cometh of Goddis mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 4:9; Matthew 27:53; Revelation 11:2"> Thanne the feend took hym in to the hooli citee, and settide hym on the pynacle of the temple,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 91:11–12; Hebrews 1:14; Psalms 34:7"> and seide to hym, If thou art Goddis sone, sende thee adoun; for it is writun, That to hise aungels he comaundide of thee, and thei schulen take thee in hondis, lest perauenture thou hirte thi foot at a stoon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:16; 1 Corinthians 10:9; Psalms 78:18"> Eftsoone Jhesus seide to hym, It is writun, Thou shalt not tempte thi Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; Revelation 11:15; Psalms 49:16–17"> Eftsoone the feend took hym in to a ful hiy hil, and schewide to hym alle the rewmes of the world, and the ioye of hem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4; Daniel 5:18–19; John 16:11"> and seide to hym, Alle these &#96;Y schal yyue to thee, if thou falle doun and worschipe me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:13–14; Deuteronomy 10:20; Joshua 24:14"> Thanne Jhesus seide to hym, Goo, Sathanas; for it is writun, Thou schalt worschipe thi Lord God, and to hym aloone thou shalt serue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 26:53; Hebrews 1:14; Luke 22:43"> Thanne the feend lafte hym; and lo! aungels camen nyy, and serueden to hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 1:14; Luke 3:20; Matthew 14:3"> But whanne Jhesus hadde herd that Joon was takun, he wente in to Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 4:46; Mark 1:21; Matthew 11:23"> And he lefte the citee of Nazareth, and cam, and dwelte in the citee of Cafarnaum, biside the see, in the coostis of Zabulon and Neptalym,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 9:1–2; Matthew 2:23; Luke 24:44"> that it shulde be fulfillid, that was seid by Ysaie, the profete, seiynge,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 9:1; 2 Kings 15:29; Joshua 21:32"> The lond of Sabulon and the lond of Neptalym, the weie of the see ouer Jordan, of Galilee of hethen men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 9:2; Isaiah 42:6–7; Isaiah 60:1–3"> the puple that walkide in derknessis saye greet liyt, and while men satten in the cuntre of shadewe of deth, liyt aroos to hem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 3:2; Acts 2:38; Mark 1:14–15"> Fro that tyme Jhesus bigan to preche, and seie, Do ye penaunce, for the kyngdom of heuenes schal come niy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 1:40–42; Matthew 10:2; Luke 6:14"> And Jhesus walkide bisidis the see of Galilee, and saye twei britheren, Symount, that is clepid Petre, and Andrewe, his brothir, castynge nettis in to the see; for thei weren fischeris.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 1:17–18; John 12:26; Matthew 16:24"> And he seide to hem, Come ye aftir me, and Y shal make you to be maad fisscheris of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 10:28–31; Matthew 19:27; Luke 18:28–30"> And anoon thei leften the nettis, and sueden hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 1:19–20; Matthew 10:2; Mark 3:17"> And he yede forth fro that place, and saie tweyne othere britheren, James of Zebede, and Joon, his brother, in a schip with Zebede, her fadir, amendynge her nettis, and he clepide hem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; Luke 9:59–60; Deuteronomy 33:9–10"> And anoon thei leften the nettis and the fadir, and sueden hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 9:35; Mark 1:39; Acts 10:38"> And Jhesus yede aboute al Galilee, techynge in the synagogis of hem, and prechynge the gospel of the kyngdom, and heelynge euery languor and eche sekenesse among the puple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 8:6; Matthew 17:15; Luke 5:15"> And his fame wente in to al Sirie; and thei brouyten to hym alle that weren at male ese, and that weren take with dyuerse languores and turmentis, and hem that hadden feendis, and lunatike men, and men in palesy, and he heelide hem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 6:17; Mark 5:20; Mark 3:7–8"> And ther sueden hym myche puple of Galile, and of Decapoli, and of Jerusalem, and of Judee, and of biyende Jordan.</span>
