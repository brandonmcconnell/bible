<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 16:1–8; John 20:1–10; Matthew 27:56"> But in the euentid of the sabat, that bigynneth to schyne in the firste dai of the woke, Marie Mawdelene cam, and another Marie, to se the sepulcre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 16:26; John 20:12–13; Mark 16:3–5"> And lo! ther was maad a greet ertheschakyng; for the aungel of the Lord cam doun fro heuene, and neiyede, and turnede awei the stoon, and sat theron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 9:3; Acts 1:10; Mark 16:5"> And his lokyng was as leit, and hise clothis as snowe;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Daniel 10:7; Matthew 27:65–66"> and for drede of hym the keperis weren afeerd, and thei weren maad as deede men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 1:17–18; Hebrews 1:14; Isaiah 41:10"> But the aungel answeride, and seide to the wymmen, Nyle ye drede, for Y woot that ye seken Jhesu, that was crucified;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 16:6; Matthew 12:40; Luke 24:6–8"> he is not here, for he is risun, as he seide; come ye, and se ye the place, where the Lord was leid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 26:32; Luke 24:22–24; Matthew 28:10"> And go ye soone, and seie ye to his disciplis, that he is risun. And lo! he schal go bifore you in to Galilee; there ye schulen se hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 2:11; Mark 16:8; Luke 24:36–41"> Lo! Y haue biforseid to you. And thei wenten out soone fro the biriels, with drede and greet ioye, rennynge to telle to hise disciplis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 28:17; John 20:14–17; Matthew 14:33"> And lo! Jhesus mette hem, and seide, Heile ye. And thei neiyeden, and heelden his feet, and worschipiden him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 8:29; John 20:17; Matthew 28:7"> Thanne Jhesus seide to hem, Nyle ye drede; go ye, &#96;telle ye to my britheren, that thei go in to Galile; there thei schulen se me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 27:65–66; Matthew 28:4"> And whanne thei weren goon, lo! summe of the keperis camen in to the citee, and telden to the princis of prestis alle thingis that weren doon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 26:3–4; Acts 4:5–22; Matthew 27:1–2"> And whanne thei weren gaderid togidere with the elder men, and hadden take her counseil, thei yauen to the kniytis miche monei, and seiden, Seie ye,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 26:64"> that hise disciplis camen bi nyyt, and han stolen hym, while ye slepten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 27:2; Acts 12:19"> And if this be herd of the iustice, we schulen counseile hym, and make you sikir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:10; Matthew 27:8; Matthew 26:15"> And whanne the monei was takun, thei diden, as thei weren tauyt. And this word is pupplischid among the Jewis, til in to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 26:32; Matthew 28:10; Matthew 28:7"> And the enleuen disciplis wenten in to Galilee, in to an hille, where Jhesus hadde ordeyned to hem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 45:11; Psalms 2:12; John 5:23"> And thei sayn hym, and worschipiden; but summe of hem doutiden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 3:35; 1 Peter 3:22; Matthew 11:27"> And Jhesus cam nyy, and spak to hem, and seide, Al power in heuene and in erthe is youun to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 16:15–16; Acts 2:38–39; Acts 1:8"> Therfor go ye, and teche alle folkis, baptisynge hem in the name of the Fadir, and of the Sone, and of the Hooli Goost;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Joshua 1:5; Acts 18:9–10"> techynge hem to kepe alle thingis, what euer thingis Y haue comaundid to you; and lo! Y am with you in alle daies, in to the ende of the world.</span>
