<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; Luke 7:37–39; John 12:1–8"> And whanne Jhesus was in Betanye, in the hous of Symount leprous,</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 22:3–6; Matthew 10:4; Mark 14:10–11"> Thanne oon of the twelue, that was clepid Judas Scarioth, wente forth to the princis of prestis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 11:12–13; Genesis 37:26–28; Exodus 21:32"> and seide to hem, What wolen ye yyue to me, and Y schal bitake hym to you? And thei ordeyneden to hym thretti pans of siluer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 14:11; Luke 22:6"> And fro that tyme he souyte oportunyte, to bitraye hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 22:7–13; Mark 14:12–16; Exodus 12:18–20"> And in the firste dai of therf looues the disciplis camen to Jhesu, and seiden, Where wolt thou we make redi to thee, to ete paske?</span>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 25:6; 1 Corinthians 10:16; Psalms 116:13"> And he took the cuppe, and dide thankyngis, and yaf to hem,</span>

<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 21:1; Ephesians 5:19–20; Luke 22:39"> And whanne the ympne was seid, thei wenten out in to the mount of Olyuete.</span>

<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 28:10; Luke 18:33–34; Matthew 28:16"> But aftir that Y schal rise ayen, Y schal go bifore you in to Galilee.</span>

<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 13:38; Luke 22:34; Matthew 26:75"> Jhesus seide to him, Treuli Y seie to thee, for in this nyyt bifor the cok crowe, thries thou schalt denye me.</span>

<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 9:32; Romans 13:1; Proverbs 23:34"> And eftsoone he cam, and foond hem slepynge; for her iyen weren heuyed.</span>

<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; Matthew 26:24; Acts 1:16"> Hou thanne schulen the scriptures be fulfilled? for so it bihoueth to be doon.</span>

<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Acts 6:11–13; Deuteronomy 19:16–21; Mark 14:55–56"> And the prince of prestis, and al the counsel souyten fals witnessing ayens Jhesu, that thei schulden take hym to deeth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:15; Psalms 35:11; Psalms 27:12"> and thei founden not, whanne manye false witnessis weren comun. But at the laste, twei false witnessis camen,</span>

<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:24; John 10:33; Matthew 9:3"> Thanne the prince of prestis to-rente his clothis, and seide, He hath blasfemed; what yit han we nede to witnessis? lo! now ye han herd blasfemye; what semeth to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="John 19:7; Acts 7:52; Leviticus 24:11–16"> And thei answeriden, and seiden, He is gilti of deeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Isaiah 50:6; Isaiah 53:3; Matthew 27:30"> Thanne thei speten &#96;in to his face, and smyten hym with buffatis; and othere yauen strokis with the pawme of her hondis in his face,</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Luke 22:63–65; Mark 14:65; 1 Peter 2:4–8"> and seide, Thou Crist, arede to vs, who is he that smoot thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="1 Kings 19:13; Matthew 26:71; Acts 5:37"> And Petir sat with outen in the halle; and a damysel cam to hym, and seide, Thou were with Jhesu of Galilee.</span>

<span className="v" id="v73" data-v="73" data-xref="Judges 12:6; John 18:26–27; Nehemiah 13:24"> And a litil aftir, thei that stooden camen, and seiden to Petir, Treuli thou art of hem; for thi speche makith thee knowun.</span>
