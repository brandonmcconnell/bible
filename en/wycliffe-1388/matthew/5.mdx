<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 3:13; Matthew 15:29; Mark 4:1"> And Jhesus, seynge the puple, wente vp in to an hil; and whanne he was set, hise disciplis camen to hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 8:35; Luke 6:20–26; Acts 10:34"> And he openyde his mouth, and tauyte hem, and seide,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 66:2; Proverbs 29:23; Psalms 34:18"> Blessed ben pore men in spirit, for the kyngdom of heuenes is herne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; 2 Corinthians 7:9–10; John 16:20–22"> Blessid ben mylde men, for thei schulen welde the erthe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 37:11; Isaiah 29:19; James 3:13"> Blessid ben thei that mornen, for thei schulen be coumfortid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 4:14; Psalms 107:9; John 6:27"> Blessid ben thei that hungren and thristen riytwisnesse, for thei schulen be fulfillid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32–1; Matthew 6:14–15; 2 Samuel 22:26"> Blessid ben merciful men, for thei schulen gete merci.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 24:4; Psalms 51:10; Hebrews 10:22"> Blessid ben thei that ben of clene herte, for thei schulen se God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 12:14; James 3:16–18; Romans 12:18"> Blessid ben pesible men, for thei schulen be clepid Goddis children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 1:12; 1 Peter 3:13–14; 2 Corinthians 4:17"> Blessid ben thei that suffren persecusioun for riytfulnesse, for the kingdam of heuenes is herne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 4:14; Luke 21:17; Mark 13:13"> &#96;Ye schulen be blessid, whanne men schulen curse you, and schulen pursue you, and shulen seie al yuel ayens you liynge, for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 6:23; James 1:2; Colossians 3:24"> Ioie ye, and be ye glad, for youre meede is plenteuouse in heuenes; for so thei han pursued &#96;also profetis that weren bifor you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Luke 14:34–35; Mark 9:49–50"> Ye ben salt of the erthe; that if the salt vanysche awey, whereynne schal it be saltid? To no thing it is worth ouere, no but that it be cast out, and be defoulid of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Philippians 2:15; John 8:12; Ephesians 5:8–14"> Ye ben liyt of the world; a citee set on an hil may not be hid;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 11:33; Mark 4:21; Luke 8:16"> ne me teendith not a lanterne, and puttith it vndur a busschel, but on a candilstike, that it yyue liyt to alle that ben in the hous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; Titus 2:7; John 15:8"> So schyne youre liyt befor men, that thei se youre goode werkis, and glorifie youre fadir that is in heuenes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 10:4; Luke 16:17; Galatians 4:4–5"> Nil ye deme, that Y cam to vndo the lawe, or the profetis; Y cam not to vndo the lawe, but to fulfille.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 16:17; Isaiah 40:8; 1 Peter 1:25"> Forsothe Y seie to you, til heuene and erthe passe, o lettir or o titel shal not passe fro the lawe, til alle thingis be doon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; James 2:10–11; Matthew 28:20"> Therfor he that brekith oon of these leeste maundementis, and techith thus men, schal be clepid the leste in the rewme of heuenes; &#96;but he that doith, and techith, schal be clepid greet in the kyngdom of heuenes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 10:2–3; Philippians 3:9; Romans 9:30–32"> And Y seie to you, that but your riytfulnesse be more plenteuouse than of scribis and of Farisees, ye schulen not entre into the kyngdom of heuenes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:17; Exodus 20:13; Numbers 35:30–34"> Ye han herd that it was seid to elde men, Thou schalt not slee; and he that sleeth, schal be gilti to doom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 John 3:14–15; 1 John 4:20–21; 1 John 2:9"> But Y seie to you, that ech man that is wrooth to his brothir, schal be gilti to doom; and he that seith to his brother, Fy! schal be gilti to the counseil; but he that seith, Fool, schal be gilti to the fier of helle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 11:25; Leviticus 6:2–6; Matthew 5:24"> Therfor if thou offrist thi yifte &#96;at the auter, and ther thou bithenkist, that thi brothir hath sum what ayens thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="James 5:16; Romans 12:17–18; Matthew 18:15–17"> leeue there thi yifte bifor the auter, and go first to be recounselid to thi brothir, and thanne thou schalt come, and schalt offre thi yifte.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 12:58–59; Proverbs 25:8; Hebrews 3:13"> Be thou consentynge to thin aduersarie soone, while thou art in the weie with hym, lest perauenture thin aduersarie take thee to the domesman, and the domesman take thee to the mynystre, and thou be sent in to prisoun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 18:34; Luke 12:59; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> Treuli Y seie to thee, thou shalt not go out fro thennus, til thou yelde the last ferthing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:18; Proverbs 6:32; Exodus 20:14"> Ye han herd that it was seid to elde men, Thou schalt &#96;do no letcherie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 John 2:16; James 1:14–15; Proverbs 6:25"> But Y seie to you, that euery man that seeth a womman for to coueite hir, hath now do letcherie bi hir in his herte.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Romans 8:13; Mark 9:43–48; Colossians 3:5"> That if thi riyt iye sclaundre thee, pulle hym out, and caste fro thee; for it spedith to thee, that oon of thi membris perische, than that al thi bodi go in to helle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Mark 9:43; Matthew 5:29; 1 Peter 2:8"> And if thi riyt hond sclaundre thee, kitte hym aweye, and caste fro thee; for it spedith to thee that oon of thi membris perische, than that al thi bodi go in to helle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:1; Deuteronomy 24:1–4; Matthew 19:7"> And it hath be seyd, Who euere leeueth his wijf, yyue he to hir a libel of forsakyng.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 7:3; 1 Corinthians 7:10–11; Luke 16:18"> But Y seie to you, that euery man that leeueth his wijf, outtakun cause of fornycacioun, makith hir to do letcherie, and he that weddith the forsakun wijf, doith auowtrye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:21; Leviticus 19:12; Ecclesiastes 5:4–6"> Eftsoone ye han herd, that it was seid to elde men, Thou schalt not forswere, but thou schalt yelde thin othis to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="James 5:12; Isaiah 66:1; Deuteronomy 23:21–23"> But Y seie to you, that ye swere not &#96;for ony thing; nethir bi heuene, for it is the trone of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 48:2; Malachi 1:14; 2 Chronicles 6:6"> nether bi the erthe, for it is the stole of his feet; nether bi Jerusalem, for it is the citee of a greet kyng; nether thou shalt not swere bi thin heed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 12:25; Matthew 6:27; Matthew 23:16–21"> for thou maist not make oon heere white, ne blacke;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="James 5:12; Colossians 4:6; Colossians 3:9"> but be youre word, Yhe, yhe; Nay, nay; and that that is more than these, is of yuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Leviticus 24:19–20; Deuteronomy 19:21; Exodus 21:22–27"> Ye han herd that it hath be seid, Iye for iye, and tothe for tothe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:15; 1 Peter 3:9; Romans 12:17–19"> But Y seie to you, that ye ayenstonde not an yuel man; but if ony smyte thee in the riyt cheke, schewe to him also the tothir;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 6:29; 1 Corinthians 6:7"> and to hym that wole stryue with thee in doom, and take awey thi coote, leeue thou &#96;to him also thi mantil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 23:26; Matthew 27:32; Mark 15:21"> and who euer constreyneth thee a thousynde pacis, go thou with hym othir tweyne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Hebrews 13:16; Luke 6:30–36"> Yyue thou to hym that axith of thee, and turne not awey fro hym that wole borewe of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Leviticus 19:18; Psalms 139:21–22; Galatians 5:13–14"> Ye han herd that it was seid, Thou shalt loue thi neiybore, and hate thin enemye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Romans 12:14; 1 Peter 3:9; Luke 6:27–28"> But Y seie to you, loue ye youre enemyes, do ye wel to hem that hatiden you, and preye ye for hem that pursuen, and sclaundren you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Psalms 145:9; Luke 6:35; Acts 14:17"> that ye be the sones of your fadir that is in heuenes, that makith his sunne to rise vpon goode &#96;and yuele men, and reyneth on iust men and vniuste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 6:32–35; 1 Peter 2:20–23; Matthew 9:10–11"> For if ye louen hem that louen you, what mede schulen ye han? whether pupplicans doon not this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 5:20; Luke 6:32; 1 Peter 2:20"> And if ye greten youre britheren oonli, what schulen ye do more? ne doon not hethene men this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Peter 1:15–16; James 1:4; Leviticus 19:2"> Therfore be ye parfit, as youre heuenli fadir is parfit.</span>
