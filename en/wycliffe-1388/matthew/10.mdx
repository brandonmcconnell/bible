<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 9:1–6; Mark 3:13–15; Mark 6:7–13"> And whanne his twelue disciplis weren clepid togidere, he yaf to hem powere of vnclene spiritis, to caste hem out of men, and to heele eueri langour, and sijknesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 4:21; Acts 1:13; Luke 5:10"> And these ben the names of the twelue apostlis; the firste, Symount, that is clepid Petre, and Andrew, his brothir; James of Zebede, and Joon, his brothir; Filip, and Bartholomeu;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 3:18; Acts 1:13; Mark 2:14"> Thomas, and Matheu, pupplican; and James Alfey, and Tadee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 26:14; Matthew 26:47; John 13:2"> Symount Chananee, and Judas Scarioth, that bitrayede Crist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 4:9; Acts 22:21–23; 2 Kings 17:24–41"> Jhesus sente these twelue, and comaundide hem, and seide, Go ye not &#96;in to the weie of hethene men, and entre ye not in to the citees of Samaritans;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:6; Ezekiel 34:6; Isaiah 53:6"> but rather go ye to the scheep of the hous of Israel, that han perischid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 28:31; Matthew 3:2; Luke 9:60"> And go ye, and preche ye, and seie, that the kyngdam of heuenes shal neiye;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 3:6; Luke 10:9; Acts 4:30"> heele ye sike men, reise ye deede men, clense ye mesels, caste ye out deuelis; freeli ye han takun, freli yyue ye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 22:35; Luke 9:3–5; Luke 10:4–12"> Nyle ye welde gold, nether siluer, ne money in youre girdlis, not a scrippe in the weie,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 10:7–12; 1 Timothy 5:17–18; 1 Corinthians 9:4–14"> nether twei cootis, nethir shoon, nether a yerde; for a werkman is worthi his mete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 10:7–8; Job 31:32; Judges 19:16–21"> In to what euere citee or castel ye schulen entre, axe ye who therynne is worthi, and there dwelle ye, til ye go out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:6; Luke 10:5–6; Acts 10:36"> And whanne ye goon in to an hous, &#96;grete ye it, and seyn, Pees to this hous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 10:6; Psalms 35:13; 2 Corinthians 2:16"> And if thilk hous be worthi, youre pees schal come on it; but if that hous be not worthi, youre pees schal turne ayen to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 10:10–11; Acts 18:6; Nehemiah 5:13"> And who euere resseyueth not you, nethir herith youre wordis, go ye fro that hous or citee, and sprenge of the dust of youre feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Peter 2:6; 1 John 4:17; John 15:22–24"> Treuly Y seie to you, it shal be more suffrable to the loond of men of Sodom and of Gommor in the dai of iugement, than to thilke citee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 10:3; Philippians 2:15; 1 Corinthians 14:20"> Lo! Y sende you as scheep in the myddil of wolues; therfor be ye sliy as serpentis, and symple as dowues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 13:9; Matthew 23:34; Acts 26:11"> But be ye war of men, for thei schulen take you in counseilis, and thei schulen bete you in her synagogis;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 5:25–27; Matthew 8:4; Psalms 2:1–6"> and to meyris, or presidentis, and to kyngis, ye schulen be lad for me, in witnessyng to hem, and to the hethen men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 4:12; Mark 13:11–13; 2 Timothy 4:17"> But whanne thei take you, nyle ye thenke, hou or what thing ye schulen speke, for it shal be youun &#96;to you in that our, what ye schulen speke;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Acts 6:10; Luke 12:12"> for it ben not ye that speken, but the spirit of youre fadir, that spekith in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 21:16–17; Micah 7:5–6; Mark 13:12–13"> &#96;And the brother shal take the brother in to deeth, and the fader the sone, and sones schulen rise ayens fadir and modir, and schulen turmente hem bi deeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 24:13; James 1:12; Revelation 2:10"> And ye schulen be in hate to alle men for my name; but he that shall dwelle stille in to the ende, shal be saaf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 16:28; Acts 8:1; Mark 13:26"> And whanne thei pursuen you in this citee, fle ye in to anothir. Treuli Y seie to you, ye schulen not ende the citees of Israel, to for that mannus sone come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 15:20; Luke 6:40; John 13:16"> The disciple is not aboue the maistir, ne the seruaunt aboue hys lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 11:15; Matthew 12:24; Matthew 9:34"> it is ynowy to the disciple, that he be as his maistir, and to the seruaunt as his lord. If thei han clepid the hosebonde man Belsabub, hou myche more his houshold meyne?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 8:17; Mark 4:22; Jeremiah 1:8"> Therfor drede ye not hem; for no thing is hid, that schal not be shewid; and no thing is priuey, that schal not be wist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 5:20; John 16:13; John 16:1"> That thing that Y seie to you in derknessis, seie ye in the liyt; and preche ye on housis, that thing that ye heeren in the ere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 12:4–5; Isaiah 8:12–13; Revelation 2:10"> And nyle ye drede hem that sleen the bodi; for thei moun not sle the soule; but rather drede ye hym, that mai lese bothe soule and bodi in to helle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 12:6–7; Psalms 104:27–30"> Whether twei sparewis ben not seeld for an halpeny? and oon of hem shal not falle on the erthe with outen youre fadir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 12:7; 1 Samuel 14:45; Acts 27:34"> &#96;And alle the heeris of youre heed ben noumbrid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 12:24; Psalms 8:5; Matthew 6:26"> Therfor nyle ye drede; ye ben betere than many sparewis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Revelation 3:5; Romans 10:9–10; Luke 12:8–9"> Therfor euery man that schal knouleche me bifore men, Y schal knouleche hym bifor my fadir that is in heuenes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:12; Luke 9:26; Luke 12:9"> But he that shal denye me bifor men, and I shal denye him bifor my fadir that is in heuenes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 12:49–53; Acts 14:4; Acts 13:45–50"> Nile ye deme, that Y cam to sende pees in to erthe; Y cam not to sende pees, but swerd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Micah 7:5–6; Matthew 10:21; Luke 21:16"> For Y cam to departe a man ayens his fadir, and the douytir ayens hir modir, and the sones wijf ayens the housbondis modir;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Micah 7:6; Jeremiah 12:6; Psalms 41:9"> and the enemyes of a man ben &#96;thei, that ben homeli with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 14:26; Matthew 22:37; Philippians 3:7–9"> He that loueth fadir or modir more than me, is not worthi to me. And he that loueth sone or douyter ouer me, is not worthi to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 14:27; Mark 8:34; Matthew 16:24"> And he that takith not his croos, and sueth me, is not worthi to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 12:25; Mark 8:35–36; Luke 17:33"> He that fyndith his lijf, shal lose it; and he that lesith his lijf for me, shal fynde it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 13:20; Luke 9:48; Luke 10:16"> He that resseyueth you, resseyueth me; and he that resseyueth me, resseyueth hym that sente me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="3 John 1:5–8; Hebrews 6:10; 2 Kings 4:16–17"> He that resseyueth a prophete in the name of a prophete, shal take the mede of a prophete. And he that resseyueth a iust man in the name of a iust man, schal take the mede of a iust man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Hebrews 6:10; Matthew 25:40; Mark 9:41–42"> And who euer yyueth drynke to oon of these leeste a cuppe of coolde watir oonli in the name of a disciple, treuli Y seie to you, he shal not leese his mede.</span>
