<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 13:9; Luke 12:35; Hebrews 4:1"> And the foolis seiden to the wise, Yyue ye to vs of youre oile, for oure laumpis ben quenchid.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 13:25; Matthew 24:44; Luke 12:36–37"> And while thei wenten for to bie, the spouse cam; and tho that weren redi, entreden with him to the weddyngis; and the yate was schit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 7:21–23; Hebrews 12:16–17"> And at the last the othere virgyns camen, and seiden, Lord, lord, opene to vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:3; 2 Timothy 2:19; John 10:27"> And he answeride, and seide, Treuli Y seie to you, Y knowe you not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 24:42–44; Revelation 16:15; 1 Thessalonians 5:6"> Therfor wake ye, for ye witen not the dai ne the our.</span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:24; Luke 19:13–14; Luke 12:48"> and to oon he yaf fyue talentis, and to another tweyne, and to another oon, to ech after his owne vertu; and wente forth anoon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="3 John 1:5–8; 1 Timothy 6:17–18; 2 Timothy 2:6"> And he that hadde fyue besauntis, wente forth, and wrouyte in hem, and wan othere fyue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 3:9–10; Ephesians 5:16; 1 Peter 4:10"> Also and he that hadde takun tweyne, wan othere tweyne.</span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 20:24; Luke 19:16–17; Colossians 1:29"> And he that hadde takun fyue besauntis, cam, and brouyte othere fyue, and seide, Lord, thou bytokist to me fyue besauntis, loo! Y haue getun aboue fyue othere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 25:23; Luke 16:10; John 12:26"> His lord seide to hym, Wel be thou, good seruaunt and feithful; for on fewe thingis thou hast be trewe, Y schal ordeyne thee on manye thingis; entre thou in to the ioye of thi lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; 2 Corinthians 8:7–8; 2 Corinthians 8:1–3"> And he that hadde takun twey talentis, cam, and seide, Lord, thou bitokist to me twey besauntis; loo!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Mark 14:8–9; Mark 12:41–44"> Y haue wonnen ouer othir tweyne. His lord seide to him, Wel be thou, good seruaunt and trewe; for on fewe thingis thou hast be trewe, Y schal ordeyne thee on many thingis; entre thou in to the ioie of thi lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 19:20–22; Luke 15:29; Malachi 3:14–15"> But he that hadde takun o besaunt, cam, and seide, Lord, Y woot that thou art an hard man; thou repist where thou hast not sowe, and thou gederist togidere where thou hast not spred abrood;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:6–7; Romans 8:15; Revelation 21:8"> and Y dredynge wente, and hidde thi besaunt in the erthe; lo! thou hast that that is thin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 18:32; Job 15:5–6"> His lord answeride, and seide to hym, Yuel seruaunt and slowe, wistist thou that Y repe where Y sewe not, and gadir to gidere where Y spredde not abrood?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:19–20; Romans 3:19; Jude 1:15"> Therfor it bihofte thee to bitake my money to chaungeris, that whanne Y cam, Y schulde resseyue that that is myn with vsuris.</span>

<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 8:18; Mark 4:25; Matthew 13:12"> For to euery man that hath me schal yyue, and he schal encreese; but fro hym that hath not, also that that hym semeth to haue, schal be taken awey fro him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Revelation 3:15–16; Matthew 13:50"> And caste ye out the vnprofitable seruaunt in to vtmer derknessis; ther schal be wepyng, and gryntyng of teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:7–8; Matthew 19:28"> Whanne mannus sone schal come in his maieste, and alle hise aungels with hym, thanne he schal sitte on the sege of his maieste;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Malachi 3:18; Matthew 13:49; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> and alle folkis schulen be gaderid bifor hym,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 100:3; John 10:26–28; John 21:15–17"> and he schal departe hem atwynne, as a scheeperde departith scheep from kidis; and he schal sette the scheep on his riythalf, and the kidis on the lefthalf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Peter 1:19–20; James 2:5; Revelation 21:7"> Thanne the kyng schal seie to hem, that schulen be on his riythalf, Come ye, the blessid of my fadir, take ye in possessioun the kyngdoom maad redi to you fro the makyng of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 3:16–19; James 2:15–16; Deuteronomy 15:7–11"> For Y hungride, and ye yauen me to ete; Y thristide, and ye yauen me to drynke; Y was herboreles, and ye herboriden me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="James 1:27; 2 Timothy 1:16–18; James 5:14–15"> nakid, and ye hiliden me; sijk, and ye visitiden me; Y was in prisoun, and ye camen to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 6:3; 1 Chronicles 29:14; 1 Peter 5:5–6"> Thanne iust men schulen answere to hym, and seie, Lord, whanne siyen we thee hungry, and we fedden thee; thristi, and we yauen to thee drynk?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> and whanne sayn we thee herborles, and we herboreden thee; or nakid, and we hiliden thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> or whanne sayn we thee sijk, or in prisoun, and we camen to thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Proverbs 14:31; Matthew 10:42"> And the kyng answerynge schal seie to hem, Treuli Y seie to you, as longe as ye diden to oon of these my leeste britheren, ye diden to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Jude 1:6–7; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> Thanne the kyng schal seie also to hem, that schulen be on his lefthalf, Departe fro me, ye cursid, in to euerlastynge fijr, that is maad redi to the deuel and hise aungels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="James 2:15–24; John 5:23; Matthew 12:30"> For Y hungride, and ye yauen not me to ete; Y thristide, and ye yauen not me to drynke;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> Y was herborles, and ye herberden not me; nakid, and ye keuerden not me; sijk, and in prisoun, and ye visitiden not me.</span>
