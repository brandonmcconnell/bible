<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 20:1–16; Matthew 12:43–45; Luke 14:16"> And Jhesus answeride, and spak eftsoone in parablis to hem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 19:7–9; Matthew 13:24; Ephesians 5:24–32"> and seide, The kyngdom of heuenes is maad lijk to a kyng that made weddyngis to his sone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 22:17; John 5:40; Luke 14:15–17"> And he sente hise seruauntis for to clepe men that weren bode to the weddyngis, and thei wolden not come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 13:46; Matthew 21:36; John 6:50–57"> Eftsoone he sente othere seruauntis, and seide, Seie ye to the men that ben bode to the feeste, Lo! Y haue maad redi my meete, my bolis and my volatilis ben slayn, and alle thingis ben redy; come ye to the weddyngis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 2:3; Luke 14:18–20; 1 John 2:15–16"> But thei dispisiden, and wenten forth, oon in to his toun, anothir to his marchaundise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 15:19–20; John 16:2–3; Acts 7:51–57"> But othere helden his seruauntis, and turmentiden hem, and slowen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 19:27; 1 Peter 4:17–18; Luke 21:24"> But the kyng, whanne he hadde herd, was wroth; and he sente hise oostis, and he distruyede tho manquelleris, and brente her citee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:5; Acts 13:46; Revelation 3:4"> Thanne he seide to hise seruauntis, The weddyngis ben redi, but thei that weren clepid to the feeste, weren not worthi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 14:21–24; Proverbs 1:20–23; Mark 16:15–16"> Therfor go ye to the endis of weies, and whom euere ye fynden, clepe ye to the weddyngis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–11; Revelation 19:6–9; 2 Corinthians 12:21"> And hise seruauntis yeden out in to weies, and gadriden togider alle that thei founden, good and yuele; and the bridale was fulfillid with men sittynge at the mete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 3:4–5; Revelation 19:8; Hebrews 4:12–13"> And the kyng entride, to se men sittynge at the mete; and he siye there a man not clothid with bride cloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:9; Job 5:16; Matthew 20:13"> And he seide to hym, Freend, hou entridist thou hidir with out bride clothis? And he was doumbe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Matthew 25:30; Matthew 24:51"> Thanne the kyng bad hise mynystris, Bynde hym bothe hondis and feet, and sende ye him in to vtmer derknessis; there schal be wepyng and grentyng of teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Peter 1:10; Luke 13:23–24; Matthew 7:13–14"> For many ben clepid, but fewe ben chosun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 20:20–26; Mark 12:13–17; Psalms 2:2"> Thanne Farisees yeden awei, and token a counsel to take Jhesu in word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 3:6; Mark 8:15; Mark 12:14"> And thei senden to hym her disciplis, with Erodians, and seien, Maister, we witen, that thou art sothefast, and thou techist in treuthe the weie of God, and thou chargist not of ony man, for thou biholdist not the persoone of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 2:1; Romans 13:6–7; Matthew 17:25"> Therfor seie to vs, what it seemeth to thee. Is it leueful that tribute be youun to the emperoure, ether nay?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 8:6; Luke 5:22; John 2:25"> And whanne Jhesus hadde knowe the wickidnesse of hem, he seide, Ypocritis, what tempten ye me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 20:2; Revelation 6:6; Matthew 18:28"> Schewe ye to me the prynte of the money. And thei brouyten to hym a peny.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 20:24"> And Jhesus seide to hem, Whos is this ymage, and the writyng aboue?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 13:7; Proverbs 24:21; Acts 4:19"> Thei seien to hym, The emperouris. Thanne he seide to hem, Therfor yelde ye to the emperoure tho thingis that ben the emperouris, and to God tho thingis that ben of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 12:12; Matthew 22:46; Luke 20:25–26"> And thei herden, and wondriden; and thei leften hym, and wenten awey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 4:1; Mark 12:18–27; Acts 23:6–8"> In that dai Saduceis, that seien there is no risyng ayen to lijf, camen to hym, and axiden him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Luke 20:28; Ruth 1:11"> and seiden, Mayster, Moises seide, if ony man is deed, not hauynge a sone, that his brother wedde his wijf, and reise seed to his brothir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 20:29–33; Mark 12:19–23; Hebrews 9:27"> And seuen britheren weren at vs; and the firste weddide a wijf, and is deed. And he hadde no seed, and lefte his wijf to his brother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> also the secounde, and the thridde, til to the seuenthe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> But the laste of alle, the woman is deed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Also in the risyng ayen to lijf, whos wijf of the seuene schal sche be? for alle hadden hir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 20:9; Romans 15:4; Luke 1:37"> Jhesus answeride, and seide to hem, Ye erren, &#96;and ye knowen not scripturis, ne the vertu of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 20:34–36; Mark 12:24–25; Matthew 24:38"> For in the rysyng ayen to lijf, nether thei schulen wedde, nethir schulen be weddid; but thei ben as the aungels of God in heuene.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 12:3; Matthew 21:16; Matthew 21:42"> And of the risyng ayen of deed men, &#96;han ye not red, that is seid of the Lord, that seith to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 3:6; Mark 12:26–27; Luke 20:37–38"> Y am God of Abraham, and God of Ysaac, and God of Jacob? he is not God of deede men, but of lyuynge men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 12:17; Mark 6:2; John 7:46"> And the puple herynge, wondriden in his techynge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 12:28–31; Matthew 25:3–5; Matthew 12:14"> And Fariseis herden that he hadde put silence to Saduceis, and camen togidere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 7:30; Luke 10:25–37; Luke 11:52"> And oon of hem, a techere of the lawe, axide Jhesu, and temptide him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 12:28–33; Matthew 5:19–20; Luke 11:42"> Maistir, which is a greet maundement in the lawe?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 12:29–30; Luke 10:27; Deuteronomy 6:5"> Jhesus seide to him, Thou schalt loue thi Lord God, of al thin herte, and in al thi soule, and in al thi mynde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> This is the firste and the moste maundement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Mark 12:31; Luke 10:27–37; Leviticus 19:18"> And the secounde is lijk to this; Thou schalt loue thi neiyebore as thi silf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Matthew 7:12; 1 John 4:19–21"> In these twey maundementis hangith al the lawe and the profetis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 20:41–44; Mark 12:35–37; Matthew 22:34"> And whanne the Farisees weren gederid togidere, Jhesus axide hem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5–6; John 6:68–69; Matthew 16:13–17"> and seide, What semeth to you of Crist, whos sone is he? Thei seien to hym, Of Dauid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:2; Revelation 4:2; Mark 12:36"> He seith to hem, Hou thanne Dauid in spirit clepith hym Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Hebrews 1:13; Acts 2:34–35"> and seith, The Lord seide to my Lord, Sitte on my riythalf, til Y putte thin enemyes a stool of thi feet?</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Romans 1:3–4; Revelation 22:16; John 8:58"> Thanne if Dauid clepith hym Lord, hou is he his sone?</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Mark 12:34; Luke 20:40; Luke 14:6"> And no man miyte answere a word to hym, nethir ony man was hardi fro that day, to axe hym more.</span>
