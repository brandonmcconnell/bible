<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Mark 8:15; Luke 9:7–9"> In that tyme Eroude tetrarke, prynce of the fourthe part, herde the fame of Jhesu;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 16:14; John 10:41; Mark 8:28"> and seide to hise children, This is Joon Baptist, he is rysun fro deeth, and therfor vertues worchen in hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 6:17; Luke 3:19–20; Matthew 4:12"> For Heroude hadde holde Joon, and bounde hym, and puttide hym &#96;in to prisoun for Herodias, the wijf of his brothir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 20:21; Leviticus 18:16; Mark 6:18"> For Joon seide to him, It is not leueful to thee to haue hir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 21:26; Matthew 11:9; Mark 6:19–20"> And he willynge to sle hym, dredde the puple; for thei hadden hym as a prophete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 6:21–23; Genesis 40:20; Hosea 1:5–6"> But in the dai of Heroudis birthe, the douytir of Herodias daunside in the myddil, and pleside Heroude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esther 5:3; Esther 5:6; Esther 7:2"> Wherfor with an ooth he bihiyte to yyue to hir, what euere thing she hadde axid of hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 6:24; Proverbs 29:10; Proverbs 1:16"> And she bifor warned of hir modir, seide, Yif thou to me here the heed of Joon Baptist in a disch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 19:12–16; 1 Samuel 14:28; Mark 6:26"> And the kyng was sorewful, but for the ooth, and for hem that saten to gidere at the mete, he comaundide to be youun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 17:12; Matthew 21:35–36; Luke 9:9"> And he sente, and bihedide Joon in the prisoun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 16:6; Genesis 49:7; Ezekiel 16:3–4"> And his heed was brouyt in a dische, and it was youun to the damysel, and she bar it to hir modir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 8:2; Matthew 27:58–61"> And hise disciplis camen, and token his bodi, and birieden it; and thei camen, and tolden to Jhesu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:10–17; Matthew 14:1–2; Mark 6:30–44"> And whanne Jhesus hadde herd this thing, he wente fro thennus in a boot, in to desert place bisides. And whanne the puple hadde herd, thei folewiden hym on her feet fro citees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; Matthew 9:36; Hebrews 2:17"> And Jhesus yede out, and sai a greet puple, and hadde reuthe on hem, and heelide the sike men of hem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 15:23; Mark 8:3; Mark 6:35–36"> But whanne the euentid was com, hise disciplis camen to him, and seiden, The place is desert, and the tyme is now passid; lat the puple go in to townes, to bye hem mete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42–44; 2 Corinthians 8:2–3; Ecclesiastes 11:2"> Jhesus seide to hem, Thei han not nede to go; yyue ye hem sumwhat to ete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 15:33–34; Mark 6:37–38; Luke 9:13"> Thei answeriden, We han not heere, but fyue looues and twei fischis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And he seide to hem, Brynge ye hem hidur to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 8:6–7; Luke 24:30; 1 Samuel 9:13"> And whanne he hadde comaundid the puple to sitte to meete on the heye, he took fyue looues and twei fischis, and he bihelde in to heuene, and blesside, and brak, and yaf to hise disciplis; and the disciplis yauen to the puple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 16:12; Mark 6:42–44; Matthew 16:8–10"> And alle eten, and weren fulfillid. And thei tooken the relifs of brokun gobetis, twelue cofynes ful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 4:19; 2 Corinthians 9:8–11; Acts 4:4"> And the noumbre of men that eten was fyue thousynde of men, outakun wymmen and lytle children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 6:45–51; John 6:15–21; Matthew 15:39"> And anoon Jhesus compellide the disciplis to go vp in to a boot, and go bifor hym ouer the see, while he lefte the puple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Mark 6:46; Matthew 26:36"> And whanne the puple was left, he stiede aloone in to an hil for to preie. But whanne the euenyng was come, he was there aloone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:18; Mark 6:48; Matthew 8:24"> And the boot in the myddel of the see was schoggid with wawis, for the wynd was contrarie to hem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 9:8; John 6:19; Mark 6:48"> But in the fourthe wakyng of the niyt, he cam to hem walkynge aboue the see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 6:49–50; Revelation 1:17; Luke 1:11–12"> And thei, seynge hym walking on the see, weren disturblid, and seiden, That it is a fantum; and for drede thei crieden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 23:11; Revelation 1:17–18; John 16:33"> And anoon Jhesus spac to hem, and seide, Haue ye trust, Y am; nyle ye drede.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 6:68; Romans 12:3; Matthew 26:33–35"> And Petre answeride, and seide, Lord, if thou art, comaunde me to come to thee on the watris.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Mark 9:23; Acts 3:16"> And he seide, Come thou. And Petre yede doun fro the boot, and walkide on the watris to come to Jhesu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Lamentations 3:54–57; 2 Timothy 4:16–17; Mark 14:38"> But he siy the wynd strong, and was aferde; and whanne he bigan to drenche, he criede, and seide, Lord, make me saaf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 1:6–8; Psalms 138:7; Genesis 22:14"> And anoon Jhesus helde forth his hoond, and took Petre, and seide to hym, Thou of litil feith, whi hast thou doutid?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 107:29–30; Mark 4:41; John 6:21"> And whanne he hadde stied in to the boot, the wynd ceessid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 11:27; Psalms 2:7; Romans 1:4"> And thei, that weren in the boot, camen, and worschipiden hym, and seiden, Verili, thou art Goddis sone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 6:53–56; Luke 5:1; John 6:24–25"> And whanne thei hadden passid ouer the see, thei camen in to the loond of Genesar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 4:24–25; Mark 6:55; Mark 1:28–34"> And whanne men of that place hadden knowe hym, thei senten in to al that cuntre; and thei brouyten to hym alle that hadden siknesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 6:19; Mark 3:10; Exodus 28:33–43"> And thei preieden hym, that thei schulden touche the hemme of his clothing; and who euere touchiden weren maad saaf.</span>
