<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spak to Moises, and seide, Comaunde thou to the sones of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:6; Ephesians 5:8–14; Exodus 27:20–21"> that thei brynge to thee oile of olyues, pureste oile, and briyt, to the lanternes to be ordeyned contynueli with out the veil of witnessyng,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> in the tabernacle of boond of pees; and Aaron schal araye tho lanternes fro euentid &#96;til to euentid bifor the Lord, bi religioun and custom euerlastynge in youre generaciouns;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 31:8; Exodus 37:17–24; Exodus 39:37"> tho schulen be set euere on a clenneste candilstike in the siyt of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 25:30; 1 Kings 18:31; Exodus 40:23"> Also thou schalt take wheete flour, and thou schalt bake therof twelue looues, which schulen haue ech bi hem silf twei tenthe partis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 7:48; Exodus 25:23–24; 2 Chronicles 4:19"> of whiche thou schalt sette sexe on euer eithir side, on a clenneste boord bifor the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 2:2; Revelation 8:3–4; Genesis 9:16"> and thou schalt sette clereste encense on tho looues, that the looues be in to mynde of offryng of the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:4; Numbers 4:7; 1 Chronicles 9:32"> bi ech sabat tho schulen be chaungid bifor the Lord, and schulen be takun of the sones of Israel bi euerlastynge boond of pees;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 8:31; Luke 6:4; Mark 2:26"> and tho schulen be Aarons and hise sones, that thei ete tho in the hooli place, for it is hooli of the noumbre of hooli thingis, of the sacrifices of the Lord, bi euerlastynge lawe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 11:4; Exodus 12:38"> Lo! forsothe the sone of a womman of Israel, whom sche childide of a man Egipcian, yede out among the sones of Israel, and chidde in the castels with a man of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 20:7; Leviticus 24:15–16; Exodus 3:15"> and whanne he hadde blasfemyd the name of the Lord, and hadde cursid the Lord, he was brouyt to Moises; forsothe his modir was clepid Salumyth, the douytir of Dabry, of the lynage of Dan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 18:15–16; Numbers 15:34; Numbers 27:5"> and thei senten hym to prisoun, til thei wisten what the Lord comaundide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the Lord spak to Moises and seide,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:7; Leviticus 20:27; Deuteronomy 21:21"> Lede out the blasfemere without the castels, and alle men that herden, sette her hondis on his heed, and al the puple stone hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 9:13; Exodus 22:28; Leviticus 5:1"> And thou schalt speke to the sones of Israel, A man that cursith his God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 12:31; James 2:7; John 10:33–36"> schal bere his synne, and he that blasfemeth the name of the Lord, die bi deeth; al the multitude of the puple schal oppresse hym with stoonus, whether he that blasfemede the name of the Lord is a citeseyn, whether a pilgrym, die he bi deeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 9:5–6; Deuteronomy 19:11–12; Deuteronomy 27:24"> He that smytith and sleeth a man, die bi deeth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 24:21; Exodus 21:34–36"> he that smytith a beeste, yelde oon in his stide, that is, lijf for lijf.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="liyf for liyf, that is, a beeste of the same kynde and valu, ether the priys bi the doom of iugis.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 5:38; Matthew 7:2; Deuteronomy 19:21"> If a man yyueth a wem<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="that is, meymyng ether orrible hirting.">b</a></sup>to ony of hise citeseyns, as he dide, so be it don to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:21; Matthew 5:38; Exodus 21:23–25"> he schal restore brekyng for brekyng, iye for iye, tooth for tooth; what maner wem he yaf, he schal be compellid to suffre sich a wem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 24:17–18; Exodus 21:33"> He that smytith werk beeste, yeelde another; he that smytith a man, schal be punyschid.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="be punyschid, in Ebreu it is, die.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 12:49; Numbers 15:15–16; Numbers 9:14"> Euene doom be among you, whether a pilgrym ethir a citeseyn synneth, for Y am youre Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 10:28–29; Hebrews 2:2–3; Numbers 15:35–36"> And Moyses spak to the sones of Israel, and thei brouyten forth out of the castels hym that blasfemede, and oppressiden with stoonus. And the sones of Israel diden, as the Lord comaundide to Moyses.</span>
