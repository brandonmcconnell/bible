<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The Lord spak to Moises, and seide,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44–45; Leviticus 20:7; 1 Peter 1:15–16"> Speke thou to al the cumpenye of the sones of Israel, and thou schalt seie to hem, Be ye hooli, for Y am hooli, youre Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Exodus 20:8; Hebrews 12:9"> Ech man drede his fadir and his modir. Kepe ye my sabatis; Y am youre Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:1; Exodus 20:23; Psalms 115:4–7"> Nyle ye be turned to ydols, nether ye schulen make to you yotun goddis; Y am youre Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 22:19; Leviticus 22:29; Leviticus 7:16"> If ye offren a sacrifice of pesible thingis to the Lord, that it be quemeful,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 7:11–17"> ye schulen ete it in that day, in which it is offrid, and in the tother dai; sotheli what euer thing is residue in to the thridde dai, ye schulen brenne in fier.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 7:18–21; Leviticus 22:25; Jeremiah 16:18"> If ony man etith therof aftir twei daes, he schal be vnhooli, and gilti of vnfeithfulnes &#96;ether wickidnesse; and he schal bere his wickidnesse,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 22:15; Leviticus 5:1"> for he defoulide the hooli thing of the Lord, and his soule schal perische fro his puple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 23:22; Deuteronomy 24:19–22; Ruth 2:2"> Whanne thou schalt repe the fruytis of thi lond, thou schalt not kitte &#96;til to the ground the corn of the lond, nether thou schalt gadere the eeris of corn that ben left;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 17:6; Leviticus 25:6; Isaiah 24:13"> nethir in thi vyner thou schalt gadere reysyns and greynes fallynge doun, but thou schalt leeue to be gaderid of pore men and pilgryms; Y am youre Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 101:7; Ephesians 4:25; Colossians 3:9"> Ye schulen not do thefte. Ye schulen not lye, and no man disseyue his neiybour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 20:7; Deuteronomy 5:11; Leviticus 18:21"> Thou schalt not forswere in my name, nethir thou schalt defoule the name of thi God; Y am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 5:4; Malachi 3:5; Deuteronomy 24:14–15"> Thou schalt not make fals chalenge to thi neiybore, nethir thou schalt oppresse hym bi violence. The werk of thin hirid man schal not dwelle at thee til the morewtid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:18; Leviticus 19:32; Leviticus 25:17"> Thou schalt not curse a deef man, nether thou schalt sette an hurtyng bifor a blynd man; but thou schalt drede thi Lord God, for Y am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:17; Proverbs 24:23; Deuteronomy 27:19"> Thou schalt not do that, that is wickid, nether thou schalt deme vniustli; biholde thou not the persoone of a pore man, nethir onoure thou the face of a myyti man; deme thou iustli to thi neiybore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 23:1; Exodus 23:7; Proverbs 20:19"> Thou schalt not be a sclaunderere, nether a priuey bacbitere in the puplis; thou schalt not stonde ayens the blood of thi neiybore<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Thou schalt not stonde ayenus the blood of thi neiybore, ioynyng thee with hem that ymagynen the deth of the innocent.">a</a></sup>; Y am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 John 2:9; 1 John 2:11; Luke 17:3"> Thou schalt not hate thi brothir in thin herte, but repreue hym opynly, lest thou haue synne on hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Matthew 22:39–40; Mark 12:31–34"> Thou schalt not seke veniaunce, nether thou schalt be myndeful of the wrong of thi cyteseyns; thou schalt loue thi freend as thi silf; Y am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Deuteronomy 22:9–11; Matthew 9:16–17"> Kepe ye my lawis. Thou schalt not make thi beestis to gendre with the lyuynge beestis of another kynde. Thou schalt not sowe the feeld with dyuerse sede. Thou schalt not be clothid in a cloth, which is wouun of twei thingis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:23–24; Exodus 21:20–21"> If a man slepith with a womman by fleischly knowyng of seed, which womman is an &#96;hand maide, ye, a noble womman of kyn, and netheles is not ayenbouyt bi prijs, nethir rewardid with fredom, bothe<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="bothe etc. in Ebrue it is, sche schal be betun.">b</a></sup>schulen be betun, and thei schulen not die, for sche was not fre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 6:6–7; Leviticus 5:1–6; Leviticus 5:15"> Sotheli the man for his trespas schal offre a ram to the Lord, at the dore of the tabernacle of witnessyng;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 4:26; Leviticus 4:20"> and the preest schal preye for hym, and for his trespas, bifor the Lord; and the Lord schal be merciful to hym, and the synne schal be foryouun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 22:27; Acts 7:51; Jeremiah 9:25–26"> Whanne ye han entrid in to the lond of biheest, and han plauntid therynne appil trees, ye schulen do awei the firste flouris; the applis whiche tho trees bryngen forth, schulen be vncleene to you, nethir ye schulen ete of tho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 3:9; Deuteronomy 18:4; Numbers 18:12–13"> Forsothe in the fourthe yeer al the fruyt of tho trees schal be &#96;halewid preiseful<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="preiseful, in Ebreu it is, to hem that preysen the Lord.">c</a></sup>to the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Haggai 1:9–11; Malachi 3:8–10; Leviticus 26:3–4"> forsothe in the fifthe yeer ye schulen ete fruytis, and schulen gadere applis, whiche tho trees bryngen forth; Y am youre Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:10–14; 2 Kings 17:17; 2 Chronicles 33:6"> Ye schulen not ete fleisch with blood. Ye schulen not make veyn diuynyng, nether ye schulen kepe dremes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 21:5; Deuteronomy 14:1; Ezekiel 44:20"> nether ye schulen clippe the heer in round, nether ye schulen schaue the beerd;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:1; Leviticus 21:5; 1 Kings 18:28"> and on deed men ye schulen not kitte youre fleischis, nether ye schulen make to you ony fyguris, ether markis in youre fleisch; Y am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:17–18; Leviticus 21:7; Hosea 4:12–14"> Sette thou not thi douytir to do leccherie for hire, and the lond be defoulid, and be fillid with synne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 26:2; Leviticus 19:3; Ecclesiastes 5:1"> Kepe ye my sabatis, and drede ye my seyntuarie; Y &#96;am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 8:19; Leviticus 20:27; Leviticus 20:6–7"> Bowe ye not to astronomyers, nether axe ye ony thing of fals dyuynours, that ye be defoulid bi hem; Y am youre Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:1; Lamentations 5:12; Proverbs 16:31"> Rise thou bifor an hoor heed, and onoure thou the persoone of an eld man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="an eeld man vndurstonde of him that is eeld in vertues and wisdom.">d</a></sup>and drede thou thi Lord God; Y am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 23:9; Exodus 22:21; Malachi 3:5"> If a comelyng enhabitith in youre lond, and dwellith among you, dispise ye not hym,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:19; Leviticus 19:18; Exodus 12:48–49"> but be he among you as a man borun in the lond; and ye schulen loue hym as you silf, for also ye weren comelyngis in the lond of Egipt; Y am youre Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:15; Deuteronomy 25:13; Proverbs 16:11"> Nyle ye do ony wickid thing in doom, in reule, in weiyte, and in mesure; the balance be iust,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 11:1; Exodus 20:2; Deuteronomy 25:13–15"> and the weiytis be euene, the buschel be iust, and the sextarie be euene; Y am youre Lord God, that ladde you out of the lond of Egipt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:1; Leviticus 18:4–5; Deuteronomy 5:1"> Kepe ye alle myn heestis, and alle domes, and do ye tho; Y am the Lord.</span>
