<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 3:7; Leviticus 13:1; Leviticus 11:1"> And the Lord spak to Moises and Aaron, &#96;and seide,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 5:2; 2 Samuel 3:29; Leviticus 22:4"> Speke ye to the sones of Israel, and seie ye to hem, A man that suffrith the rennyng out of seed, schal be vncleene;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:20; Ezekiel 16:26; Leviticus 12:3"> and thanne he schal be demed to be suget to this vice, whanne bi alle momentis foul vmour &#96;ethir moysture cleueth to his fleisch, and growith togidere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Ephesians 5:11; 1 Corinthians 15:33"> Ech bed in which he slepith schal be vncleene, and where euer he sittith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 11:25; Leviticus 16:26; Leviticus 17:15"> If ony man touchith his bed, he schal waische his clothis, and he schal be waischun in watir, and schal be vncleene &#96;til to euentid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 4:8; Isaiah 1:16"> If a man sittith where he satt, also thilke man schal waische hise clothis, and he schal be waischun in watir, and schal be vnclene &#96;til to euentid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> He that touchith hise fleischis, schal waische hise clothis, and he schal be waischun in watir, and schal be vncleene &#96;til to euentid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jude 1:4; 2 Peter 2:1–3; Isaiah 1:16"> If sich a man castith out spetyng on hym that is cleene, he schal waische his clothis, and he schal be waischun in watir, and schal be vncleene &#96;til to euentid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 31:34"> The sadil on which he sittith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 4:8; Psalms 26:6; Leviticus 15:8"> schal be vncleene; and ech man that touchith what euer thing is vndur hym that suffrith the fletyng out of seed, schal be defoulid &#96;til to euentid. He that berith ony of these thingis, schal waische hise clothis, and he schal be waischun in watir, and schal be vncleene &#96;til to euentid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Ech man, whom he that is such touchith with hondis not waischun bifore, schal waische hise clothis, and he schal be waischun in watir, and schal be vncleene &#96;til to euentid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 6:28; Leviticus 11:32–33; 2 Corinthians 5:1"> &#96;A vessel of erthe which he touchith, schal be brokun; but a &#96;vessel of tre schal be waischun in watir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 14:8; Leviticus 8:33; Leviticus 15:28"> If he that suffrith sich a passioun, is heelid, he schal noumbre seuene daies aftir his clensyng, and whanne the clothis and al &#96;the bodi ben waischun in lyuynge watris, he schal be clene.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 12:8; Leviticus 12:6; Hebrews 10:10"> Forsothe in the eiytthe dai he schal take twei turtlis, ethir twei &#96;briddis of a culuer, and he schal come in the &#96;siyt of the Lord at the dore of tabernacle of witnessyng, and schal yyue tho to the preest;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 14:30–31; Leviticus 4:26; Matthew 3:17"> and the preest schal make oon for synne, and the tother in to brent sacrifice; and the preest schal preye for hym bifor the Lord, that he be clensid fro the fletyng out of his seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:10–11; Leviticus 22:4; Leviticus 15:5"> A man fro whom the seed of letcherie, &#96;ethir of fleischli couplyng, goith out, schal waische in watir al his bodi, and he schal be vncleene &#96;til to euentid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> He schal waische in watir the cloth &#96;and skyn which he hath, and it schal be unclene &#96;til to euentid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 15:5; 1 Thessalonians 4:3–5; 1 Corinthians 6:12"> The womman with which he &#96;is couplid fleischli, schal be waischun in watir, and schal be vncleene &#96;til to euentid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 12:2; Matthew 15:19; Mark 5:25"> A womman that suffrith the fletyng out of blood, whanne the moneth cometh ayen, schal be departid bi seuene daies; ech man that touchith hir schal be vncleene &#96;til to euentid,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 15:4–9; Proverbs 2:16–19; Proverbs 6:35"> and the place in which sche slepith ether sittith in the daies of hir departyng, schal be defoulid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 7:14; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Isaiah 22:14"> He that touchith her bed, schal waische hise clothis, and he schal be waischun in watir, and schal be vncleene &#96;til to euentid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Who euer touchith ony vessel on which sche sittith, he schal waische hise clothis, and he schal be waischun in watir, and schal be defoulid &#96;til to euentid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 20:18; 1 Peter 2:11; Leviticus 18:19"> If a man is couplid fleischli with hir in the tyme of blood that renneth bi monethis, he schal be vncleene bi seuene daies, and ech bed in which he slepith schal be defoulid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 5:25; Matthew 9:20; Luke 8:43"> A womman that suffrith in many daies the &#96;fletyng out of blood, not in the tyme of monethis, ethir which womman ceessith not to flete out blood aftir the blood of monethis, schal be vncleene as longe as sche &#96;schal be suget to this passioun, as if sche is in the tyme of monethis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Ech bed in which sche slepith, and &#96;vessel in which sche sittith, schal be defoulid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 15:21; Hebrews 10:22; Leviticus 17:15–16"> Who euer touchith hir schal waische his clothis, and he schal be waischun in watir, and schal be vncleene &#96;til to euentid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 15:13–15; Galatians 3:13; Ephesians 1:6–7"> If the blood stondith, and ceessith to flete out, sche schal noumbre seuene daies of hir clensyng,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> and in the eiytthe dai sche schal offre for hir silf to the preest twei turtlis, ethir twei &#96;briddis of culueris, at the dore of the tabernacle of witnessyng;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> and the preest schal make oon for synne, and the tothir in to brent sacrifice; and the preest schal preye for hir bifor the Lord, and for the fletyng out of hir vnclennesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 19:20; Numbers 19:13; Ezekiel 5:11"> Therfor ye schulen teche the sones of Israel, that thei eschewe vnclennessis, and that thei die not for her filthis, whanne thei defoulen my tabernacle which is among hem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 11:46; Leviticus 13:59; Numbers 6:13"> This is the lawe of hym that suffrith fletyng out of seed, and which is defoulid with fleischly couplyng,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 15:19; Leviticus 20:18; Leviticus 15:24–25"> and of a womman which is departid in the tymes of monethis, ethir which flowith out in contynuel blood, and of the man that slepith with hir.</span>
