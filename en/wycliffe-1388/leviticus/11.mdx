<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spak to Moises and Aaron, and seide,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 15:11; Leviticus 11:11; Hebrews 9:10"> Seie ye to the sones of Israel, Kepe ye alle thingis whiche Y wroot to you, that Y be youre God. These ben the beestis, whiche ye schulen ete, of alle lyuynge beestis of erthe;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 2:10; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Deuteronomy 6:6–7"> ye schulen ete &#96;al thing among beestis that hath a clee departid, and chewith code;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> sotheli what euer thing chewith code, and hath a clee, but departith not it, as a camel and othere beestis doon, ye schulen not ete it, and ye schulen arette among vnclene thingis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 104:18; Proverbs 30:26; Matthew 7:26"> A cirogrille,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A cirogrille, that is, a beeste ful of thornes, and more than an irchoun. In Ebru it is a cony.">a</a></sup>which chewith code, and departith not the clee, is vnclene; and an hare,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:7"> for also he chewith code, but departith not the clee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 66:17; Isaiah 65:4; Isaiah 66:3"> and a swiyn, that chewith not code, thouy he departith the clee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; Colossians 2:16; Hebrews 9:10"> Ye schulen not ete the fleischis of these, nether ye schulen touche the deed bodies, for tho ben vnclene to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 5:6; James 2:18; Acts 20:21"> Also these thingis ben that ben gendrid in watris, and is leueful to ete;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 29:27; Leviticus 7:18; Psalms 139:21–22"> ye schulen ete al thing that hath fynnes and scalis, as wel in the see, as in floodis and stondynge watris; sotheli what euer thing of tho that ben moued and lyuen in watris, hath not fynnes and scalis, schal be abhominable, and wlatsum to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> ye schulen not ete the fleischis of tho, and ye schulen eschewe the bodies deed bi hem silf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Alle thingis in watris that han not fynnes and scalis, schulen be pollutid,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 39:27–30; Jeremiah 4:22; Titus 3:3"> These thingis ben of foulis whiche ye schulen not ete, and schulen be eschewid of you; an egle, and a grippe, aliete,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="aliete, that is, a kynde of egle.">b</a></sup>and a kyte, and a vultur by his kynde;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and al of &#96;rauyns kynde bi his licnesse;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 12:24; 1 Kings 17:6; Proverbs 30:17"> a strucioun,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 34:11–15; Ephesians 5:7–11; Ephesians 2:2–3"> and nyyt crowe, a lare, and an hauke bi his kinde;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> an owle, and dippere, and ibis<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="ibis, that is, a ciconye, that etith paddokis and serpentis. ibis, that is, an vnclene watir foule, that with his bille puttynge water into his ers, purgith himsilf.">c</a></sup>;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> a swan and cormoraunt, and a pellican;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> a fawcun, a iay bi his kynde; a leepwynke, and a reremows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:10; Philippians 3:18–19; Leviticus 11:23"> Al thing of foulis that goith on foure feet, schal be abhomynable to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> sotheli what euer thing goith on foure feet, but hath lengere hipis bihynde, bi whiche it skippith on the erthe, ye schulen ete;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 3:4; Mark 1:6; Exodus 10:4–5"> as is a bruke<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="a bruke is the fruyt of locuste, bifor that it hath wyngis.">d</a></sup>in his kynde, and acatus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="acatus, that is, fruyt of locust, whanne it bigynneth to haue wyngis.">e</a></sup>and opymacus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="opymacus, that is, a beeste that fiytith with serpentis. opymacus, that is, a foule that is enemye to serpentis.">f</a></sup>and a locuste, alle bi her kynde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Forsothe what euer thing of briddis hath foure feet oneli, it schal be abhomynable to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; Ephesians 2:1–3; 2 Corinthians 6:17"> and who euer touchith her bodies deed bi hem silf, schal be defoulid, and &#96;schal be vnclene &#96;til to euentid;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 15:5; Leviticus 14:8; Numbers 31:24"> and if it is nede, that he bere ony deed thing of these, he schal waische his clothis, and he schal be vnclene til to the goyng doun of the sunne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Sotheli ech beeste that hath a clee, but departith not it, nether chewith code, schal be vnclene; and what euer thing touchith it, schal be defoulid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 11:23; Leviticus 11:20"> That that goith on hondis, of alle beestis that gon on foure feet, schal be vnclene; he, that touchith her bodies deed bi hem silf, schal be defoulid &#96;til to euentid;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 11:24–25"> and he, that berith siche deed bodies, schal waische hise clothis, and he schal be vnclene &#96;til to euentid; for alle these thingis ben vnclene to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 11:41–42; 2 Timothy 3:2–5; Psalms 10:3"> Also these thingis schulen be arettid among defoulid thingis, of these that ben moued on erthe; a wesele, and mows, and a cocodrille,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="a cocodrille, that is, a beeste of iiij. feet, that hath nailes and teeth, and [hauynge the nether cheke vnmouable, and he s.] restith on the lond bi day, and in water bi nyyt. In Ebru it is an urchun.">g</a></sup>&#96;alle bi her kynde;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> mygal,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="mygal, ether a glotenouse mows. an amygal, that is, a trecherous born beest, and a gileful and most gloterouse mous.">h</a></sup>camelion,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="camelion, that is, a beeste dyuersid in dyuerse colours. a camelion, that is, a beest diuersid in to dyuerse colours after dyuerse lokyngis.">i</a></sup>and stellio,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="stellio, that is, a worm that hath many briyt iemmes in the bak. stellio, that is, a worme peyntid as with sterris.">j</a></sup>and lacerta,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="lacerta, that is, a serpent that hath many schuldris as hondis.">k</a></sup>and a maldewerp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 11:24–25; Leviticus 11:8"> Alle these ben vnclene; he that touchith her bodies deed bi hem silf, schal be vnclene &#96;til to euentid;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 15:12; Titus 3:5; Titus 2:14"> and that thing schal be defoulid, on which ony thing of her bodies deed bi hem silf fallith, as wel a vessel of tree, and a cloth, as skynnes &#96;and heiris; and in what euer thing werk is maad, it schal be dippid in watir, and tho thingis schulen be defoulid &#96;til to euentid, and so aftirward tho schulen be clensid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 6:28; Leviticus 15:12; Leviticus 14:45"> Sotheli a vessel of erthe, in which ony thing of these fallith with ynne, schal be defoulid, and therfor it schal be brokun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Proverbs 21:4; Proverbs 21:27"> Ech mete, which ye schulen ete, schal be vnclene, if water<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="watir, that is, vnclene watir. watir, that is, conteyned in a vessel, wherynne vnclene thing biforseid felde.">l</a></sup>is sched thereon; and ech fletynge thing, which is drunkun of ech vessel, &#96;where ynne vnclene thingis bifelden, schal be vnclene;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 15:12; Leviticus 6:28; Leviticus 11:33"> and what euer thing of siche deed bodies bi hem silf felde theronne, it schal be vnclene, whether furneisis, ethir vessels of thre feet, tho schulen be destried, and schulen be vnclene.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Zechariah 13:1; John 4:14"> Sotheli wellis and cisternes, and al the congregacioun of watris, schal be clene. He that touchith her bodi deed bi it silf, schal be defoulid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:37; 1 Peter 1:23; 1 John 5:18"> If it fallith on seed, it schal not defoule the seed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> sotheli if ony man schedith seed with watir, and aftirward the watir is touchid with deed bodies bi hem silf, it schal be defoulid anoon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Leviticus 11:40; Leviticus 11:24; Leviticus 15:7"> If a beeste is deed, which it is leueful to you to ete, he that touchith the deed bodi therof schal be vnclene &#96;til to euentid; and he that etith therof ony thing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Leviticus 22:8; Ezekiel 44:31; Deuteronomy 14:21"> ethir berith, schal waische his clothis, and schal be vnclene &#96;til to euentid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Leviticus 11:29; Leviticus 11:20; Leviticus 11:23"> Al thing that crepith on erthe, schal be abhomynable, nether schal be takun in to mete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Titus 1:12; Isaiah 65:25; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> &#96;What euer thing goith on the brest and foure feet, and hath many feet, ethir drawun bi the erthe, ye schulen not ete, for it is abhomynable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Leviticus 20:25; Leviticus 11:41–42"> Nyle ye defoule youre soulis, nether touche ye ony thing of tho, lest ye ben vnclene;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Exodus 20:2; 1 Peter 1:15–16; Leviticus 19:2"> for Y am youre Lord God; be ye hooli, for Y am hooli. Defoule ye not youre soulis in ech crepynge &#96;beeste which is moued on erthe; for Y am the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44; Exodus 20:2; Exodus 6:7"> that ladde you out of the lond of Egipt, that Y schulde be to you in to God; ye schulen be hooli, for Y am hooli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Leviticus 14:54; Leviticus 15:32; Ezekiel 43:12"> This is the lawe of lyuynge beestes, and of foulis, and of ech lyuynge soule which is moued in watir, and crepith in erthe;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Leviticus 10:10; Ezekiel 44:23; Romans 14:13–23"> that ye knowe differences of clene thing and vnclene, and that ye wite what ye schulen ete, and what ye owen forsake.</span>
