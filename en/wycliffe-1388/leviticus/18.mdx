<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spak to Moises, and seide, Speke thou to the sones of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 6:7; Leviticus 11:44; Ezekiel 20:5"> and thou schalt seie to hem, Y am youre Lord God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 20:23; Exodus 23:24; 1 Peter 4:2–4"> ye schulen not do by the custom of the lond of Egipt, in which ye dwelliden; ye schulen not do bi the custom of the cuntrei of Canaan, &#96;to which Y schal brynge you yn, nethir ye schulen go in the lawful thingis of hem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 18:2; Leviticus 18:26; Ezekiel 20:19"> Ye schulen do my domes, and ye schulen kepe myn heestis, and ye schulen go in tho; Y am youre Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 10:5; Ezekiel 20:11; Galatians 3:12"> Kepe ye my lawis and domes, whiche a man &#96;schal do, and schal lyue in tho; Y am youre Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17–21; Leviticus 18:7–19; Leviticus 20:11–12"> Ech man schal not neiy to the nyy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="nyy, onely degrees of consanguinyte expressid in this co. ben forbedun in mariage.">a</a></sup>womman of his blood, that he schewe &#96;the filthe of hir; Y am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 20:11; Ezekiel 22:10; Leviticus 18:8–16"> Thou schalt not diskyuere the filthe of thi fadir and the filthe of thi modir; sche is thi modir, thou schalt not schewe hir filthe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 20:11; 1 Corinthians 5:1; Deuteronomy 27:20"> Thou schalt not vnhile the filthe of the wijf of thi fadir, for it is the filthe of thi fadir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17; Deuteronomy 27:22; Ezekiel 22:11"> Thou schalt not schewe the filthe of thi sistir, of fadir &#96;ether of modir, which sister is gendrid at hoome ether without forth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="at hoome ether withoute, that is, in wedloc, ether out therof.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Thou schalt not schewe the filthe of the douyter of thi sone, ether of neece<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="neece, that is, the douytir of thi douytir.">c</a></sup>of thi douyter, for it is thi filthe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Thou schalt not schewe the filthe of the douyter of the wijf of thi fadir, which sche childide to thi fadir, and is thi sistir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 20:19; Exodus 6:20"> Thou schalt not opene the filthe of the &#96;sister of thi fadir, for sche is the fleisch of thi fadir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Thou schalt not schewe the filthe of the sistir of thi modir, for sche is the fleisch of thi modir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 20:20"> Thou schalt not shewe the filthe of the brothir of thi fadir, nethir thou schalt neiye to his wijf, which is ioyned to thee bi affinyte.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 20:12; Genesis 38:26; Ezekiel 22:11"> Thou schalt not schewe the filthe of thi sones wijf, for sche is the wijf of thi sone, nether thou schalt diskiuere hir schenschip; and no man take his brotheris wijf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5; Leviticus 20:21; Matthew 22:24"> Thou schalt not schewe the filthe of &#96;the wijf of thi brother, for it is the filthe of thi brothir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 20:14; Amos 2:7; Deuteronomy 27:23"> Thou schalt not schewe the filthe of thi wijf,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="of thi wijf, etc., in Ebrew it is, the filthe of a womman, and of hir douytir.">d</a></sup>and of hir douyter; thou schalt not take the douytir of hir sone, and the douytir of hir douyter, that thou schewe hir schenschip; thei ben the fleisch of hir, and siche letcherie is incest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Malachi 2:15; Genesis 30:15; Genesis 29:28"> Thou schalt not take &#96;the sister of thi wijf, in to concubynage<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="In Ebru it is, to anguish of hire.">e</a></sup>of hir, nethir thou schalt schewe &#96;the filthe of hir, while thi wijf lyueth yit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 20:18; Leviticus 15:24; Ezekiel 22:10"> Thou schalt not neiye to a womman that suffrith rennyng of blood of monethe, nethir thou schalt schewe hir filthe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:14; 1 Corinthians 6:9; Hebrews 13:4"> Thou schalt not do letcherie with &#96;the wijf of thi neiybore, nether thou schalt be defoulid with medlyng of seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:31; Leviticus 20:2–5; Deuteronomy 18:10"> Thou schalt not yyue of thi seed, that it be offrid to the idol Moloch, nether thou schalt defoule the name of thi God; Y am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 20:13; Romans 1:26–27; 1 Corinthians 6:9"> Thou schalt not be medlid with a man bi letcherie of womman, for it is abhomynacioun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 22:19; Leviticus 20:15–16; Leviticus 20:12"> Thou schalt not do letcherie with ony beeste, nethir thou schalt be defoulid with it. A womman schal not ligge vnder a beeste, nether schal be medlid therwith,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="nether schal be medlid therwith, that is, defoulid bi fleisly knowing therof.">f</a></sup>for it is greet synne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:12; Leviticus 18:30; Leviticus 20:22–23"> Be ye not defoulid in alle these thingis, in whiche alle &#96;folkis, ether hethen men, ben defoulid, whiche folkis Y schal caste out bifor youre siyt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 18:28; Leviticus 20:22–23; Jeremiah 2:7"> of whiche the lond is defoulid, of which lond Y schal vysyte the grete synnes, that it spewe out hise dwellers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Leviticus 17:10; Leviticus 18:30"> Kepe ye my lawful thingis and domes, that ye do not of alle these abhomynaciouns, as wel a man borun in the lond as a comelyng which is a pilgrym at you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:14; Leviticus 18:24; Deuteronomy 23:18"> For the dwellers of the lond, that weren bifor you, diden alle these abhomynaciouns, and defouliden that lond.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 3:16; Leviticus 20:22; Leviticus 18:25"> Therfor be ye war, lest it caste out viliche also you in lijk manere, whanne ye han do lijk synnes, as it castide out vileche the folk, that was bifor you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 20:6; Exodus 12:15; Leviticus 17:10"> Ech man that doith ony thing of these abhomynaciouns, schal perische fro the myddis of his puple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 20:23; Deuteronomy 18:9–12; Leviticus 22:9"> Kepe ye myn heestis; nyle ye do tho thingis, whiche thei that weren bifor you diden, and be ye not defoulid in tho; Y am youre Lord God.</span>
