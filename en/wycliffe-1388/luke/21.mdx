<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 12:41–44; John 8:20; Matthew 27:6"> And he biheeld, and saye tho riche men, that casten her yiftis in to the treserie;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 12:42"> but he saye also a litil pore widewe castynge twei ferthingis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; Acts 10:34; 2 Corinthians 8:2–3"> And he seide, Treuli Y seie to you, that this pore widewe keste more than alle men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 2:44–45; Luke 15:12; Acts 4:34"> For whi alle these of thing that was plenteuouse to hem casten in to the yiftis of God; but this widewe of that thing that failide to hir, caste al hir liflode, that sche hadde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 2:20; Matthew 24:1–51; Mark 13:1–37"> And whanne sum men seiden of the temple, that it was apparailid with gode stoonus and yiftis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 24:2; Mark 13:2; Lamentations 5:18"> he seide, These thingis that ye seen, daies schulen come, in whiche a stoon schal not be left on a stoon, which schal not be destried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 1:6–7; Mark 13:14; Mark 13:3–4"> And thei axiden hym, and seiden, Comaundour, whanne schulen these thingis be? and what tokne schal be, whanne thei schulen bigynne to be don?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 4:1; Mark 13:5–6; Ephesians 5:6"> And he seide, Se ye, that ye be not disseyued; for many schulen come in my name, seiynge, For Y am, and the tyme schal neiye; therfor nyle ye go aftir hem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 21:28; Matthew 24:6–8; Mark 13:7–8"> And whanne ye schulen here batailis and stryues with ynne, nyle ye be aferd; it bihoueth first these thingis to be don, but not yit anoon is an ende.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 2:19–20; Mark 13:8; 2 Chronicles 15:5–6"> Thanne he seide to hem, Folk schal rise ayens folk, and rewme ayens rewme;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 24:29–30; Luke 21:25–27"> grete mouyngis of erthe schulen be bi placis, and pestilencis, and hungris, and dredis fro heuene, and grete tokenes schulen be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 4:12–14; Acts 5:40; Acts 22:30"> But bifore alle these thingis thei schulen sette her hoondis on you, and schulen pursue, bitakynge in to synagogis and kepyngis, drawynge to kyngis and to iusticis, for my name;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 1:12; Philippians 1:28; 2 Thessalonians 1:5"> but it schal falle to you in to witnessyng.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 12:11–12; Mark 13:11; Matthew 10:19–20"> Therfor putte ye in youre hertis, not to thenke bifore, hou ye schulen answere; for Y schal yyue to you mouth and wisdom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:9; Ephesians 6:19; Luke 12:12"> to whiche alle youre aduersaries schulen not mowe ayenstonde, and ayenseie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 12:53; Revelation 6:9; Micah 7:5–6"> And ye schulen be takun of fadir, and modir, and britheren, and cosyns, and freendis, and bi deeth thei schulen turmente of you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 15:19; Luke 6:22; John 15:21"> and ye schulen be in haate to alle men for my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 10:30; 1 Samuel 14:45; Luke 12:7"> And an heere of youre heed schal not perische;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Matthew 10:22; James 1:3"> in youre pacience ye schulen welde youre soulis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 19:43; Daniel 9:27; Mark 13:14"> But whanne ye schulen se Jerusalem ben enuyround with an oost, thanne wite ye, that the desolacioun of it schal neiye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:1; Genesis 19:17; Luke 17:31–33"> Thanne thei that ben in Judee, fle to the mountans; and thei that ben in the mydil of it, gon awei; and thei that ben in the cuntreis, entre not in to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 9:7; Isaiah 65:12–16; Isaiah 63:4"> For these ben daies of veniaunce, that alle thingis that ben writun, be fulfillid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 13:17; Matthew 24:19; Luke 23:29"> And wo to hem, that ben with child, and norischen in tho daies; for a greet diseese schal be on the erthe, and wraththe to this puple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 11:25; Revelation 11:2; Isaiah 63:18"> And thei schulen falle bi the scharpnesse of swerd, and thei schulen be led prisoneris in to alle folkis; and Jerusalem schal be defoulid of hethene men, til the tymes of naciouns be fulfillid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Joel 2:30–31; Revelation 6:12–14"> And tokenes schulen be in the sunne, and the mone, and in the sterris; and in the erthe ouerleiyng of folkis, for confusioun of sown of the see and of floodis;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 24:29; Deuteronomy 28:65–67; 2 Peter 3:10–12"> for men schulen wexe drye for drede and abidyng that schulen come to al the world; for vertues of heuenes schulen be mouyd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 24:30; Revelation 1:7; Mark 13:26"> And thanne thei schulen se mannys sone comynge in a cloude, with greet power and maieste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 8:23; Isaiah 60:1–2; Ephesians 4:30"> And whanne these thingis bigynnen to be maad, biholde ye, and reise ye youre heedis, for youre redempcioun neiyeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 24:32–35; Mark 13:28–30"> And he seide to hem a liknesse, Se ye the fige tre, and alle trees,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 12:57"> whanne thei bryngen forth now of hem silf fruyt, ye witen that somer is nyy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 5:9; Hebrews 10:37; 1 Peter 4:7"> so ye, whanne ye seen these thingis to be don, wite ye, that the kyngdom of God is nyy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 13:30; Matthew 24:34; Matthew 23:36"> Treuli Y seie to you, that this generacioun schal not passe, til alle thingis be don.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 40:8; Mark 13:31; Matthew 24:35"> Heuene and erthe schulen passe, but my wordis schulen not passe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 4:19; Luke 12:45–46; Luke 12:40"> But take ye heede to you silf, lest perauenture youre hertis be greuyd with glotony, and drunkenesse, and bisynessis of this lijf, and thilke dai come sodein on you; for as a snare it schal come on alle men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Revelation 16:15; Psalms 11:6; Jeremiah 48:43–44"> that sitten on the face of al erthe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 John 2:28; Matthew 25:13; 1 Peter 5:8"> Therfor wake ye, preiynge in ech tyme, that ye be hadde worthi to fle alle these thingis that ben to come, and to stonde bifor mannus sone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 22:39; Mark 11:19; Matthew 21:17"> And in daies he was techynge in the temple, but in nyytis he yede out, and dwellide in the mount, that is clepid of Olyuet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 8:1–2"> And al the puple roos eerli, to come to hym in the temple, and to here hym.</span>
