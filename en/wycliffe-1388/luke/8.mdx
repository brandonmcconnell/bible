<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Romans 10:15; Matthew 9:35"> And it was don aftirward, and Jhesus made iourney bi citees and castels, prechynge and euangelisynge the rewme of God, and twelue with hym;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 16:9; Matthew 27:55–56; Mark 15:40–41"> and sum wymmen that weren heelid of wickid spiritis and sijknessis, Marie, that is clepid Maudeleyn, of whom seuene deuelis wenten out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 4:46–53; 2 Corinthians 8:9; Acts 9:36–39"> and Joone, the wijf of Chuse, the procuratoure of Eroude, and Susanne, and many othir, that mynystriden to hym of her ritchesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 4:1–9; Matthew 13:2–9"> And whanne myche puple was come togidir, and men hiyeden to hym fro the citees, he seide bi a symylitude,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 2:1; James 1:23–24; Mark 4:15"> He that sowith, yede out to sowe his seed. And while he sowith, sum fel bisidis the weie, and was defoulid, and briddis of the eir eten it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 3:7–8; Luke 8:13; Mark 4:5–6"> And othir fel on a stoon, and it sprunge vp, and driede, for it hadde not moysture.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:3; Luke 8:14; Luke 21:34"> And othir fel among thornes, and the thornes sprongen vp togider, and strangliden it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 11:15; Revelation 2:11; Revelation 2:7"> And othir fel in to good erthe, and it sprungun made an hundrid foold fruyt. He seide these thingis, and criede, He that hath eeris of heryng, here he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 4:10; Matthew 13:10; Matthew 13:18"> But hise disciplis axiden him, what this parable was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9; Mark 4:11; Matthew 11:25"> And he seide to hem, To you it is grauntid to knowe the pryuete of the kyngdom of God; but to othir men in parablis, that thei seynge se not, and thei herynge vndurstonde not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 13:19; Mark 4:14–20; James 1:21"> And this is the parable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:9–14; Revelation 12:9; James 1:23–24"> The seed is Goddis word; and thei that ben bisidis the weie, ben these that heren; and aftirward the feend cometh, and takith awei the word fro her herte, lest thei bileuynge be maad saaf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 6:4; John 5:35; Proverbs 12:12"> But thei that fel on a stoon, ben these that whanne thei han herd, resseyuen the word with ioye. And these han not rootis; for at a tyme thei bileuen, and in tyme of temptacioun thei goen awei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; 1 Timothy 6:9–10; 1 Timothy 6:17"> But that that fel among thornes, ben these that herden, and of bisynessis, and ritchessis, and lustis of lijf thei gon forth, and ben stranglid, and bryngen forth no fruyt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Galatians 5:22–26; Colossians 1:10"> But that that fel in to good erthe, ben these that, in a good herte, and best heren the word, and holdun, and brengen forth fruyt in pacience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 11:33; Matthew 5:15–16; Mark 4:21–22"> No man lityneth a lanterne, and hilith it with a vessel, or puttith it vndur a bed, but on a candilstike, that men that entren seen liyt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 12:2–3; Matthew 10:26; Mark 4:22"> For ther is no priuei thing, which schal not be openyd, nether hid thing, which schal not be knowun, and come in to open.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 13:12; Luke 19:26; Mark 4:23–25"> Therfor se ye, hou ye heren; for it schal be youun to hym that hath, and who euer hath not, also that that he weneth that he haue, schal be takun awei fro hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 12:46–50; Mark 3:31–35; Mark 3:21"> And his modir and britheren camen to hym; and thei myyten not come to hym for the puple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 1:14; Mark 6:3; Matthew 13:55–56"> And it was teeld to hym, Thi modir and thi britheren stonden with outforth, willynge to se thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 11:27–28; James 1:22; John 13:17"> And he answeride, and seide to hem, My modir and my britheren ben these, that heren the word of God, and doon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 8:23–27; Mark 4:35–41; Mark 6:45"> And it was don in oon of daies, he wente vp in to a boot, and hise disciplis. And he seide to hem, Passe we ouer the see. And thei wenten vp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 93:3–4; Psalms 124:2–4; Psalms 148:8"> And while thei rowiden, he slepte. And a tempest of wynde cam doun in to the watir, and thei weren dryuun hidur and thidur with wawis, and weren in perel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 107:25–29; Luke 4:39; Psalms 104:6–9"> And thei camen nyy, and reisiden hym, and seiden, Comaundoure, we perischen. And he roos, and blamyde the wynde, and the tempest of the watir; and it ceesside, and pesibilte was maad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 8:26; Proverbs 30:4; Mark 4:40–41"> And he seide to hem, Where is youre feith? Which dredynge wondriden, and seiden togidir, Who, gessist thou, is this? for he comaundith to wyndis and to the see, and thei obeien to hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 5:1–20; Matthew 8:28–34"> And thei rowiden to the cuntree of Gerasenus, that is ayens Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 5:2–5; 1 Samuel 19:24; Numbers 19:16"> And whanne he wente out to the loond, a man ran to hym, that hadde a deuel long tyme, and he was not clothid with cloth, nether dwellide in hous, but in sepulcris.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 8:29; 2 Peter 2:4; Mark 5:6–8"> This, whanne he saiy Jhesu, fel doun bifor hym, and he criynge with a greet vois seide, What to me and to thee, Jhesu, the sone of the hiyest God? Y biseche thee, that thou turmente &#96;not me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 5:3–5; Luke 9:39; 2 Timothy 2:25–26"> For he comaundide the vncleene spirit, that he schulde go out fro the man. For he took hym ofte tymes, and he was boundun with cheynes, and kept in stockis, and, whanne the boondis weren brokun, he was lad of deuelis in to desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 26:53; Luke 8:2; Matthew 8:29"> And Jhesus axide hym, and seide, What name is to thee? And he seide, A legioun<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A legioun is a thousand, sixte hundrid, sixti and sixe.">a</a></sup>; for many deuelis weren entrid in to hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 25:41; Philippians 2:10–11; Job 2:5"> And thei preyden hym, that he schulde not comaunde hem, that thei schulden go in to helle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 65:4; Isaiah 66:3; Revelation 20:7"> And there was a flok of many swyne lesewynge in an hil, and thei preieden hym, that he schulde suffre hem to entre in to hem. And he suffride hem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; John 8:44; Luke 8:22–23"> And so the deuelis wenten out fro the man, and entriden in to the swyne; and with a birre the flok wente heedlyng in to the pool, and was drenchid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 5:14; Matthew 8:33; Matthew 28:11"> And whanne the hirdis sayn this thing don, thei flowen, and tolden in to the cite, and in to the townes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 10:39; Acts 22:3; 1 John 3:8"> And thei yeden out to se that thing that was don. And thei camen to Jhesu, and thei founden the man sittynge clothid, fro whom the deuelis wenten out, and in hool mynde at hise feet; and thei dredden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 4:24"> And thei that sayn tolden to hem, hou he was maad hool of the legioun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 16:39; Luke 8:28; Luke 10:10–11"> And al the multitude of the cuntre of Gerasenus preiede hym, that he schulde go fro hem, for thei werun holdun with greet drede. He wente vp in to a boot, and turnede ayen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 9:13–16; Mark 5:18–20; Psalms 145:3–12"> And the man of whom the deuelis weren gon out, preide hym, that he schulde be with hym. Jhesus lefte hym,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 17:15–18; 1 Timothy 5:8; Deuteronomy 10:21"> and seide, Go ayen in to thin hous, and telle hou grete thingis God hath don to thee. And he wente thorow al the cite, and prechide, hou grete thingis Jhesus hadde don to hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 9:1; Mark 5:21; Mark 6:20"> And it was don, whanne Jhesus was gon ayen, the puple resseyuede hym; for alle weren abidynge hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 5:22–43; Luke 13:14; Acts 18:8"> And lo! a man, to whom the name was Jayrus, and he was prynce of a synagoge; and he fel doun at the feet of Jhesu, and preiede hym, that he schulde entre in to his hous,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 7:12; Luke 8:45; Job 1:18–19"> for he hadde but o douyter &#96;almost of twelue yeer eelde, and sche was deed. And it bifel, the while he wente, he was thrungun of the puple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Mark 5:25–26; Matthew 9:20–22; Leviticus 15:25–33"> And a womman that hadde a flux of blood twelue yeer, and hadde spendid al hir catel in leechis, and sche miyte not be curid of ony,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Mark 5:27–28; Deuteronomy 22:12; Acts 19:12"> and sche cam nyy bihynde, and touchide the hem of his cloth, and anoon the fluxe of hir blood ceesside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Mark 5:30–32; Luke 9:13; Luke 5:5"> And Jhesus seide, Who is that touchide me? And whanne alle men denyeden, Petre seide, and thei that weren with hym, Comaundour, the puple thristen, and disesen thee, and thou seist, Who touchide me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 6:19; Luke 5:17; 1 Peter 2:9"> And Jhesus seide, Summan hath touchid me, for that vertu yede out of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 5:33; Luke 17:15–16; 1 Corinthians 2:3"> And the womman seynge, that it was not hid fro hym, cam tremblynge, and fel doun at hise feet, and for what cause sche hadde touchid hym sche schewide bifor al the puple, and hou anoon sche was helid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 7:50; Matthew 9:22; Hebrews 4:2"> And he seide to hir, Douytir, thi feith hath maad thee saaf; go thou in pees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Mark 5:35–43; Isaiah 7:12; Luke 7:6"> And yit while he spak, a man cam fro the prince of the synagoge, and seide to hym, Thi douytir is deed, nyle thou trauel the maister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 11:40; John 11:25; Mark 5:36"> And whanne this word was herd, Jhesus answeride to the fadir of the damysel, Nyle thou drede, but bileue thou oonli, and sche schal be saaf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Mark 14:33; Matthew 6:5–6; 1 Kings 17:19–23"> And whanne he cam to the hous, he suffride no man to entre with hym, but Petir and Joon and James, and the fadir and the modir of the damysel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Luke 23:27; John 11:4; Zechariah 12:10"> And alle wepten, and biweileden hir. And he seide, Nyle ye wepe, for the damysel is not deed, but slepith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Luke 16:14; Isaiah 53:3; John 11:39"> And thei scorneden hym, and wisten that sche was deed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:32; John 11:43; Mark 1:31"> But he helde hir hoond, and criede, and seide, Damysel, rise vp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="John 11:44; 1 Kings 17:21–23; Mark 5:43"> And hir spirit turnede ayen, and sche roos anoon. And he comaundide to yyue to hir to ete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Matthew 9:30; Luke 5:14"> And hir fadir and modir wondriden greetli; and he comaundide hem, that thei schulden not seie to ony that thing that was don.</span>
