<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 6:1–27; Luke 18:35; 2 Kings 2:18–22"> And Jhesus &#96;goynge yn, walkide thorou Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 18:24–27; 2 Chronicles 17:5–6"> And lo! a man, Sache bi name, and this was a prince of pupplicans, and he was riche.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 23:8; Luke 9:7–9; John 12:21"> And he souyte to se Jhesu, who he was, and he myyte not, for the puple, for he was litil in stature.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 9:10; Luke 5:19; 1 Kings 10:27"> And he ran bifore, and stiyede in to a sicomoure tree, to se hym; for he was to passe fro thennus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 3:20; John 14:23; Hebrews 13:2"> And Jhesus biheld vp, whanne he cam to the place, and saiy hym, and seide to hym, Sache, haste thee, and come doun, for to dai Y mot dwelle in thin hous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 5:29; Acts 16:34; Acts 16:15"> And he hiyynge cam doun, and ioiynge resseyuede hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 15:2; Luke 7:39; Luke 7:34"> And whanne alle men sayn, thei grutchiden seiynge, For he hadde turned to a synful man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:6; Numbers 5:7; Luke 18:22–23"> But Sache stood, and seide to the Lord, Lo! Lord, Y yyue the half of my good to pore men; and if Y haue ony thing defraudid ony man, Y yelde foure so myche.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:7; Luke 13:16; Romans 4:16"> Jhesus seith to hym, For to dai heelthe is maad to this hous, for that he is Abrahams sone;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Matthew 1:21; 1 Timothy 1:13–16"> for mannus sone cam to seke, and make saaf that thing that perischide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 17:20; Acts 1:6; 2 Thessalonians 2:1–3"> Whanne thei herden these thingis, he addide, and seide a parable, for that he was nyy Jerusalem, &#96;and for thei gessiden, that anoon the kyngdom of God schulde be schewid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 25:14–30; Luke 19:12–27; Luke 20:9"> Therfor he seide, A worthi man wente in to a fer cuntre, to take to hym a kyngdom, and to turne ayen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 25:14–15; John 12:26; 1 Corinthians 12:7–11"> And whanne hise ten seruauntis weren clepid, he yaf to hem ten besauntis; and seide to hem, Chaffare ye, til Y come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:7; Acts 7:51–52; Acts 3:14–15"> But hise citeseyns hatiden hym, and senten a messanger aftir hym, and seiden, We wolen not, that he regne on vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:1–5; Romans 14:10–12; Psalms 2:4–6"> And it was don, that he turnede ayen, whan he hadde take the kyngdom; and he comaundide hise seruauntis to be clepid, to whiche he hadde yyue monei, to wite, hou myche ech hadde wonne bi chaffaryng.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 2:18–26; 2 Timothy 4:7–8; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> And the firste cam, and seide, Lord, thi besaunt hath wonne ten besauntis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 16:10; Matthew 25:21; Revelation 2:26–29"> He seide to hym, Wel be, thou good seruaunt; for in litil thing thou hast be trewe, thou schalt be hauynge power on ten citees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 4:20; Matthew 13:23; 2 Corinthians 8:12"> And the tother cam, and seide, Lord, thi besaunt hath maad fyue besauntis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; 1 Corinthians 15:41–42; 1 Corinthians 3:8"> And to this he seide, And be thou on fyue citees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 25:24; Proverbs 26:13–16; James 4:17"> And the thridde cam, and seide, Lord, lo! thi besaunt, that Y hadde, put vp in a sudarie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 25:24–25; Romans 8:7; 1 John 4:18"> For Y dredde thee, for thou art &#96;a sterne man; thou takist awey that that thou settidist not, and thou repist that that thou hast not sowun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:16; Matthew 12:37; Job 15:5–6"> He seith to hym, Wickid seruaunt, of thi mouth Y deme thee. Wistist thou, that Y am &#96;a sterne man, takynge awei that thing that Y settide not, and repyng ethat thing that Y sewe not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 22:25–27; Romans 2:4–5; Deuteronomy 23:19–20"> and whi hast thou not youun my money to the bord, and Y comynge schulde haue axid it with vsuris?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 16:2; Luke 12:20"> And he seide to men stondynge nyy, Take ye awei fro hym the besaunt, and yyue ye to hym that hath ten beyauntis:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 16:2; 2 Samuel 7:19; Isaiah 55:8–9"> And thei seiden to hym, Lord, he hath ten besauntis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 John 1:8; Luke 8:18; Matthew 21:43"> And Y seie to you, to ech man that hath, it schal be youun, and he schal encreese; but fro him that hath not, also that thing that he hath, schal be takun of hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 19:14; Psalms 21:8–9; Matthew 22:7"> Netheles brynge ye hidur tho myn enemyes, that wolden not that Y regnede on hem, and sle ye bifor me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 9:51; Hebrews 12:2; 1 Peter 4:1"> And whanne these thingis weren seid, he wente bifore, and yede vp to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 1:12; Matthew 21:17; Matthew 21:1–11"> And it was don, whanne Jhesus cam nyy to Bethfage and Betanye, at the mount, that is clepid of Olyuete, he sente hise twei disciplis, and seide,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 22:8–13; Luke 19:32; 1 Samuel 10:2–9"> Go ye in to the castel, that is ayens you; in to which as ye entren, ye schulen fynde a colt of an asse tied, on which neuer man sat; vntye ye hym, and brynge ye to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 24:1; Psalms 50:10–12; Mark 11:3–6"> And if ony man axe you, whi ye vntien, thus ye schulen seie to hym, For the Lord desirith his werk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 22:13"> And thei that weren sent, wenten forth, and fonden as he seide to hem, a colt stondynge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And whanne thei vntieden the colt, the lordis of hym seiden to hem, What vntien ye the colt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; John 10:35; 2 Corinthians 8:9"> And thei seiden, For the Lord hath nede to hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 11:7–8; Matthew 21:7; John 12:14"> And thei ledden hym to Jhesu; and thei castynge her clothis on the colt, setten Jhesu on hym.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Kings 9:13; Matthew 21:8"> And whanne he wente, thei strowiden her clothis in the weie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 12:12–13; Ezra 3:10–13; Matthew 21:1"> And whanne he cam nyy to the comyng doun of the mount of Olyuete, al the puple that cam doun bygunnen to ioye, and to herie God with greet vois on alle the vertues, that thei hadden sayn,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Luke 13:35; Psalms 118:22–26"> and seiden, Blessid be the king, that cometh in the name of the Lord; pees in heuene, and glorie in hiye thingis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 4:1–2; Matthew 23:13; Acts 4:16–18"> And sum of the Farisees of the puple seiden to hym, Maister, blame thi disciplis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:11; Isaiah 55:12; Matthew 3:9"> And he seide to hem, Y seie to you, for if these ben stille, stoonus schulen crye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 13:34–35; John 11:35; Psalms 119:136"> And whanne he neiyede, &#96;he seiy the citee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:29; Hebrews 10:26–29; Isaiah 48:18"> and wepte on it, and seide, For if thou haddist knowun, thou schuldist wepe also; for in this dai the thingis ben in pees to thee, but now thei ben hid fro thin iyen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 21:20–24; Isaiah 29:1–4; Ezekiel 4:2"> But daies schulen come in thee, and thin enemyes schulen enuyroun thee with a pale, and thei schulen go aboute thee, and make thee streit on alle sidis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Luke 21:6; Luke 13:34–35; John 3:18–21"> and caste thee doun to the erthe, and thi sones that ben in thee; and thei schulen not leeue in thee a stoon on a stoon, for thou hast not knowun the tyme of thi visitacioun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 21:12–13; Mark 11:15–17; John 2:13–17"> And he entride in to the temple, and bigan to caste out men sellynge ther inne and biynge,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; Jeremiah 7:11; Psalms 93:5"> and seide to hem, It is writun, That myn hous is an hous of preyer, but ye han maad it a den of theues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 11:18; Matthew 26:55; Mark 11:27–33"> And he was techynge euerydai in the temple. And the princis of prestis, and the scribis, and the princis of the puple souyten to lese hym;</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:3; Acts 16:14; John 7:46–49"> and thei founden not, what thei schulden do to hym, for al the puple was ocupied, and herde hym.</span>
