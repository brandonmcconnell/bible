<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 1:12; Galatians 3:26; 2 Corinthians 6:18"> Behold what manner of charity the Father hath bestowed upon us, that we should be called, and should be the sons of God. Therefore the world knoweth not us, because it knew not him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 3:21; Colossians 3:4; 1 Corinthians 13:12"> Dearly beloved, we are now the sons of God; and it hath not yet appeared what we shall be. We know, that, when he shall appear, we shall be like to him: because we shall see him as he is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:1; 1 John 2:6; 2 Peter 3:14"> And every one that hath this hope in him, sanctifieth himself, as he also is holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 5:17; Romans 3:20; Romans 4:15"> Whosoever committeth sin committeth also iniquity; and sin is iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:21; Hebrews 9:28; 1 Peter 3:18"> And you know that he appeared to take away our sins, and in him there is no sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="3 John 1:11; 1 John 2:4; 1 John 3:9"> Whosoever abideth in him, sinneth not; and whosoever sinneth, hath not seen him, nor known him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 2:29; 1 Peter 1:15–16; Romans 2:13"> Little children, let no man deceive you. He that doth justice is just, even as he is just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 2:14; John 8:44; Colossians 2:15"> He that committeth sin is of the devil: for the devil sinneth from the beginning. For this purpose, the Son of God appeared, that he might destroy the works of the devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 5:18; 1 Peter 1:23; John 1:13"> Whosoever is born of God, committeth not sin: for his seed abideth in him, and he can not sin, because he is born of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 4:8; 3 John 1:11; Luke 6:35"> In this the children of God are manifest, and the children of the devil. Whosoever is not just, is not of God, nor he that loveth not his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 15:12; 2 John 1:5; 1 John 4:7"> For this is the declaration, which you have heard from the beginning, that you should love one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 11:4; Genesis 4:4–15; Proverbs 29:27"> Not as Cain, who was of the wicked one, and killed his brother. And wherefore did he kill him? Because his own works were wicked: and his brother’s just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 15:18–19; John 17:14; Luke 21:17"> Wonder not, brethren, if the world hate you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 13:35; 1 John 5:2; 1 John 4:20–21"> We know that we have passed from death to life, because we love the brethren. He that loveth not, abideth in death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:21–22; Proverbs 26:24–26; Revelation 21:8"> Whosoever hateth his brother is a murderer. And you know that no murderer hath eternal life abiding in himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 4:9–11; Ephesians 5:2; John 13:34"> In this we have known the charity of God, because he hath laid down his life for us: and we ought to lay down our lives for the brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Isaiah 58:7–10; Deuteronomy 15:7–11"> He that hath the substance of this world, and shall see his brother in need, and shall shut up his bowels from him: how doth the charity of God abide in him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 2:15–16; Romans 12:9; 1 Peter 1:22"> My little children, let us not love in word, nor in tongue, but in deed, and in truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; 1 John 3:21; Hebrews 6:10–11"> In this we know that we are of the truth: and in his sight shall persuade our hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 139:1–4; Jeremiah 17:10; Hebrews 4:13"> For if our heart reprehend us, God is greater than our heart, and knoweth all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 2:28; Hebrews 4:16; 2 Corinthians 1:12"> Dearly beloved, if our heart do not reprehend us, we have confidence towards God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 15:7; John 9:31; 1 John 5:14"> And whatsoever we shall ask, we shall receive of him: because we keep his commandments, and do those things which are pleasing in his sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 6:29; John 13:34; 1 Peter 1:22"> And this is his commandment, that we should believe in the name of his Son Jesus Christ: and love one another, as he hath given commandment unto us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:16; 1 John 4:15–16; 1 John 4:12–13"> And he that keepeth his commandments, abideth in him, and he in him. And in this we know that he abideth in us, by the Spirit which he hath given us.</span>
