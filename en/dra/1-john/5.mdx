<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 1:12–13; John 8:42; 1 John 2:29"> Whosoever believeth that Jesus is the Christ, is born of God. And every one that loveth him who begot, loveth him also who is born of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 13:34–35; 1 John 2:5; 1 John 3:22–24"> In this we know that we love the children of God: when we love God, and keep his commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 14:15; John 15:10; John 14:21–24"> For this is the charity of God, that we keep his commandments: and his commandments are not heavy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:57; 1 John 4:4; John 16:33"> For whatsoever is born of God, overcometh the world: and this is the victory which overcometh the world, our faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 John 4:15; 1 John 5:1"> Who is he that overcometh the world, but he that believeth that Jesus is the Son of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 15:26; Hebrews 9:14; John 16:13"> This is he that came by water and blood, Jesus Christ: not by water only, but by water and blood. And it is the Spirit which testifieth, that Christ is the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 28:19; 2 Corinthians 13:14; Acts 5:32"> And there are three who give testimony in heaven, the Father, the Word, and the Holy Ghost. And these three are one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 5:6–7; John 15:26; 1 Peter 3:21"> And there are three that give testimony on earth: the spirit, and the water, and the blood: and these three are one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 3:16–17; Matthew 17:5; Acts 17:31"> If we receive the testimony of men, the testimony of God is greater. For this is the testimony of God, which is greater, because he hath testified of his Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 8:16; Galatians 4:6; John 3:33"> He that believeth in the Son of God, hath the testimony of God in himself. He that believeth not the Son, maketh him a liar: because he believeth not in the testimony which God hath testified of his Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 6:23; 1 John 2:25; John 3:36"> And this is the testimony, that God hath given to us eternal life. And this life is in his Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 3:36; John 5:24; John 1:12"> He that hath the Son, hath life. He that hath not the Son, hath not life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 20:31; John 1:12; 2 Peter 1:10–11"> These things I write to you, that you may know that you have eternal life, you who believe in the name of the Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 14:13; Jeremiah 33:3; Jeremiah 29:12–13"> And this is the confidence which we have towards him: That, whatsoever we shall ask according to his will, he heareth us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 11:9–10; Mark 11:24; Proverbs 15:29"> And we know that he heareth us whatsoever we ask: we know that we have the petitions which we request of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 6:4–6; 1 Samuel 2:25; Jeremiah 11:14"> He that knoweth his brother to sin a sin which is not to death, let him ask, and life shall be given to him, who sinneth not to death. There is a sin unto death: for that I say not that any man ask.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 John 3:4; 1 John 5:16; James 4:7–10"> All iniquity is sin. And there is a sin unto death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 John 3:9; 1 John 5:4; 1 John 5:1"> We know that whosoever is born of God, sinneth not: but the generation of God preserveth him, and the wicked one toucheth him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4; 1 John 4:4–6; Ephesians 2:2"> We know that we are of God, and the whole world is seated in wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 17:3; Luke 24:45; John 14:9"> And we know that the Son of God is come: and he hath given us understanding that we may know the true God, and may be in his true Son. This is the true God and life eternal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:14; 1 Corinthians 10:7; Exodus 20:3–4"> Little children, keep yourselves from idols. Amen.</span>
