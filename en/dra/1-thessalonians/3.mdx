<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:2; Isaiah 52:7; Proverbs 25:25"> But now when Timothy came to us from you, and related to us your faith and charity, and that you have a good remembrance of us always, desiring to see us as we also to see you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:4; 2 John 1:4; 1 Thessalonians 3:8–9"> Therefore we were comforted, brethren, in you, in all our necessity and tribulation, by your faith,</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 71:14–15; Psalms 96:12–13; 2 Corinthians 9:15"> For what thanks can we return to God for you, in all the joy wherewith we rejoice for you before our God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:9; 1 Thessalonians 3:11; Philippians 1:25"> Night and day more abundantly praying that we may see your face, and may accomplish those things that are wanting to your faith?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Matthew 6:32; 2 Thessalonians 2:16"> Now God himself and our Father, and our Lord Jesus Christ, direct our way unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Philippians 1:9; 1 John 4:7–16; Matthew 22:39"> And may the Lord multiply you, and make you abound in charity towards one another, and towards all men: as we do also towards you,</span>
