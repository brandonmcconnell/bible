<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 1:7; 1 Thessalonians 4:9; Matthew 24:36"> But of the times and moments, brethren, you need not, that we should write to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 16:15; 2 Peter 3:10; Matthew 24:42–44"> For yourselves know perfectly, that the day of the Lord shall so come, as a thief in the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 21:34–35; Matthew 24:37–39; Luke 17:26–30"> For when they shall say, peace and security; then shall sudden destruction come upon them, as the pains upon her that is with child, and they shall not escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 2:8; 2 Peter 3:10; Deuteronomy 19:6"> But you, brethren, are not in darkness, that that day should overtake you as a thief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Acts 26:18; John 12:36"> For all you are the children of light, and children of the day: we are not of the night, nor of darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 1:13; Matthew 24:42; Luke 22:46"> Therefore, let us not sleep, as others do; but let us watch, and be sober.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 13:13; 1 Corinthians 15:34; 2 Peter 2:13"> For they that sleep, sleep in the night; and they that are drunk, are drunk in the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 13:12–13; Ephesians 6:11; 1 Peter 1:13"> But let us, who are of the day, be sober, having on the breastplate of faith and charity, and for a helmet the hope of salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:10; 1 Peter 2:10; 2 Timothy 2:10"> For God hath not appointed us unto wrath, but unto the purchasing of salvation by our Lord Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 14:8–9; 1 Peter 3:18; Romans 8:34"> Who died for us; that, whether we watch or sleep, we may live together with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Hebrews 10:25; Romans 15:2"> For which cause comfort one another; and edify one another, as you also do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 13:17; Hebrews 13:7; 1 Timothy 5:17–18"> And we beseech you, brethren, to know them who labour among you, and are over you in the Lord, and admonish you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 9:50; Hebrews 12:14; Galatians 6:6"> That you esteem them more abundantly in charity, for their work’s sake. Have peace with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:2; Romans 14:1; Acts 20:35"> And we beseech you, brethren, rebuke the unquiet, comfort the feeble minded, support the weak, be patient towards all men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Galatians 6:10; Leviticus 19:18"> See that none render evil for evil to any man; but ever follow that which is good towards each other, and towards all men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:12; Philippians 4:4; Matthew 5:12"> Always rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 12:12; Colossians 4:2; Ephesians 6:18"> Pray without ceasing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 5:20; Philippians 4:6; Colossians 3:17"> In all things give thanks; for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus concerning you all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 4:30; Acts 7:51; 2 Timothy 1:6"> Extinguish not the spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:3–6; 1 Corinthians 14:1; Numbers 11:25–29"> Despise not prophecies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 4:1; Romans 12:2; Ephesians 5:10"> But prove all things; hold fast that which is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:3; Philippians 4:8; 1 Corinthians 10:31–33"> From all appearance of evil refrain yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jude 1:24; Hebrews 4:12; 1 Corinthians 1:8–9"> And may the God of peace himself sanctify you in all things; that your whole spirit, and soul, and body, may be preserved blameless in the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:3; Numbers 23:19; 1 Corinthians 1:9"> He is faithful who hath called you, who also will do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 13:18–19; Philippians 1:19; Philemon 1:22"> Brethren, pray for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 16:16; 1 Corinthians 16:20"> Salute all the brethren with a holy kiss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 4:16; Acts 19:13; 1 Timothy 1:3"> I charge you by the Lord, that this epistle be read to all the holy brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 16:20; 2 Thessalonians 3:18; Romans 16:23"> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you. Amen.</span>
