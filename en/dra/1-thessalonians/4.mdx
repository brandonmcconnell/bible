<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; 2 Thessalonians 1:3; 2 Peter 3:18"> For the rest therefore, brethren, we pray and beseech you in the Lord Jesus, that as you have received from us, how you ought to walk, and to please God, so also you would walk, that you may abound the more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 28:20; Ezekiel 3:17; 2 Thessalonians 3:10"> For you know what precepts I have given to you by the Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 4:2; Romans 12:2; 1 Thessalonians 5:23"> For this is the will of God, your sanctification; that you should abstain from fornication;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:9; Romans 12:1; Hebrews 13:4"> That every one of you should know how to possess his vessel in sanctification and honour:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 4:8; Acts 17:30–31; Ephesians 4:17–19"> Not in the passion of lust, like the Gentiles that know not God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 13:4; Exodus 20:17; Romans 12:19"> And that no man overreach, nor circumvent his brother in business: because the Lord is the avenger of all these things, as we have told you before, and have testified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44; Ephesians 1:4; 1 Peter 2:9–12"> For God hath not called us unto uncleanness, but unto sanctification.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 3:24; Luke 10:16; John 12:48"> Therefore, he that despiseth these things, despiseth not man, but God, who also hath given his holy Spirit in us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 3:11; Romans 12:10; 1 John 3:23"> But as touching the charity of brotherhood, we have no need to write to you: for yourselves have learned of God to love one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 1:9; 1 Thessalonians 3:12; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> For indeed you do it towards all the brethren in all Macedonia. But we entreat you, brethren, that you abound more:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:6; Ephesians 4:28; 1 Peter 3:4"> And that you use your endeavour to be quiet, and that you do your own business, and work with your own hands, as we commanded you: and that you walk honestly towards them that are without; and that you want nothing of any man’s.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Colossians 4:5; 1 Peter 2:12; Romans 12:17"> And we will not have you ignorant, brethren, concerning them that are asleep, that you be not sorrowful, even as others who have no hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; 1 Thessalonians 4:15; 1 Thessalonians 5:10"> For if we believe that Jesus died, and rose again; even so them who have slept through Jesus, will God bring with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 8:11; Philippians 3:20–21; Isaiah 26:19"> For this we say unto you in the word of the Lord, that we who are alive, who remain unto the coming of the Lord, shall not prevent them who have slept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:51–53; 2 Corinthians 4:14; 1 Thessalonians 2:19"> For the Lord himself shall come down from heaven with commandment, and with the voice of an archangel, and with the trumpet of God: and the dead who are in Christ, shall rise first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:51–52; Revelation 1:7; Matthew 24:30–31"> Then we who are alive, who are left, shall be taken up together with them in the clouds to meet Christ, into the air, and so shall we be always with the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:52; Revelation 11:12; John 14:3"> Wherefore, comfort ye one another with these words.</span>
