<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 26:12; 1 Corinthians 8:2; 1 Corinthians 3:18"> For if any man think himself to be some thing, whereas he is nothing, he deceiveth himself.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; 2 Thessalonians 3:13; Isaiah 40:30–31"> And in doing good, let us not fail. For in due time we shall reap, not failing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Proverbs 3:27; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> Therefore, whilst we have time, let us work good to all men, but especially to those who are of the household of the faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:21–23; Romans 16:22"> See what a letter I have written to you with my own hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 5:11; Colossians 2:23; Acts 15:1"> For as many as desire to please in the flesh, they constrain you to be circumcised, only that they may not suffer the persecution of the cross of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 5:6; Romans 2:17–25"> For neither they themselves who are circumcised, keep the law; but they will have you to be circumcised, that they may glory in your flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 6:6; Jeremiah 9:23–24; Galatians 2:20"> But God forbid that I should glory, save in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ; by whom the world is crucified to me, and I to the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:17; Colossians 3:10–11; Ephesians 2:10"> For in Christ Jesus neither circumcision availeth any thing, nor uncircumcision, but a new creature.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 3:29; Romans 9:6–8; Philippians 3:3"> And whosoever shall follow this rule, peace on them, and mercy, and upon the Israel of God.</span>
