<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:7; 1 Peter 2:11; Proverbs 8:13"> Having therefore these promises, dearly beloved, let us cleanse ourselves from all defilement of the flesh and of the spirit, perfecting sactification in the fear of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 20:33; 2 Corinthians 6:12–13; 2 Corinthians 11:9"> Receive us. We have injured no man, we have corrupted no man, we have overreached no man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:11–12; 1 Thessalonians 2:8; 2 Corinthians 11:11"> I speak not this to your condemnation. For we have said before, that you are in our hearts, to die together, and to live together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:4; 2 Corinthians 3:12; James 1:2"> Great is my confidence for you, great is my glorying for you. I am filled with comfort: I exceedingly abound with joy in all our tribulation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:13; Deuteronomy 32:25; Acts 20:1"> For also when we were come into Macedonia, our flesh had no rest, but we suffered all tribulation; combats without, fears within.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:3–4; 2 Thessalonians 2:16–17; 2 Corinthians 7:13"> But God, who comforteth the humble, comforted us by the coming of Titus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:2; Jude 1:3; 2 Corinthians 8:16"> And not by his coming only, but also by the consolation, wherewith he was comforted in you, relating to us your desire, your mourning, your zeal for me, so that I rejoiced the more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Jeremiah 20:7–9; Matthew 26:21–22"> For although I made you sorrowful by my epistle, I do not repent; and if I did repent, seeing that the same epistle (although but for a time) did make you sorrowful;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 15:7; Jeremiah 31:18–20; 2 Corinthians 7:10–11"> Now I am glad: not because you were made sorrowful; but because you were made sorrowful unto penance. For you were made sorrowful according to God, that you might suffer damage by us in nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 3:19; Acts 11:18; 2 Timothy 2:25–26"> For the sorrow that is according to God worketh penance, steadfast unto salvation; but the sorrow of the world worketh death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 14:18; 1 Timothy 5:21–22; 2 Timothy 2:15"> For behold this selfsame thing, that you were made sorrowful according to God, how great carefulness it worketh in you; yea defence, yea indignation, yea fear, yea desire, yea zeal, yea revenge: in all things you have shewed yourselves to be undefiled in the matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:9; 1 Timothy 3:5; 2 Corinthians 2:17"> Wherefore although I wrote to you, it was not for his sake that I did the wrong, nor for him that suffered it; but to manifest our carefulness that we have for you</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:6; 1 Corinthians 12:26; 1 Corinthians 16:13"> Before God: therefore we were comforted. But in our consolation, we did the more abundantly rejoice for the joy of Titus, because his spirit was refreshed by you all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:4; 2 Thessalonians 1:4; 2 Corinthians 1:18–20"> And if I have boasted any thing to him of you, I have not been put to shame; but as we have spoken all things to you in truth, so also our boasting that was made to Titus is found a truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philippians 2:12; 2 Corinthians 2:9; 1 John 3:17"> And his bowels are more abundantly towards you; remembering the obedience of you all, how with fear and trembling you have received him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:3; Philemon 1:8; Philemon 1:21"> I rejoice that in all things I have confidence in you.</span>
