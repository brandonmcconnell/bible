<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 14:13; 1 Corinthians 9:12; Matthew 17:27"> Giving no offence to any man, that our ministry be not blamed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:10; 2 Corinthians 4:8; 2 Corinthians 11:27"> But in all things let us exhibit ourselves as the ministers of God, in much patience, in tribulation, in necessities, in distresses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:23–25; 2 Corinthians 11:27; Acts 19:23–34"> In stripes, in prisons, in seditions, in labours, in watchings, in fastings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Colossians 1:27; Galatians 5:22"> In chastity, in knowledge, in longsuffering, in sweetness, in the Holy Ghost, in charity unfeigned,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:2; 2 Timothy 2:15; Ephesians 6:11–20"> In the word of truth, in the power of God; by the armour of justice on the right hand and on the left;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 27:63; 1 Peter 4:14; 1 Timothy 4:10"> By honour and dishonour, by evil report and good report; as deceivers, and yet true; as unknown, and yet known;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:10–11; Romans 8:36; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> As dying, and behold we live; as chastised, and not killed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 16:22; 2 Corinthians 8:9; Revelation 2:9"> As sorrowful, yet always rejoicing; as needy, yet enriching many; as having nothing, and possessing all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:3–4; Philippians 1:8; Psalms 119:32"> Our mouth is open to you, O ye Corinthians, our heart is enlarged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:2; Micah 2:7; Philippians 1:8"> You are not straitened in us, but in your own bowels you are straitened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="3 John 1:4; 1 John 3:18; 1 Thessalonians 2:11"> But having the same recompense, (I speak as to my children,) be you also enlarged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; 1 Corinthians 10:21; James 4:4"> Bear not the yoke with unbelievers. For what participation hath justice with injustice? Or what fellowship hath light with darkness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 18:21; 1 Corinthians 10:20–21; 1 John 5:11–13"> And what concord hath Christ with Belial? Or what part hath the faithful with the unbeliever?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 21:3; Exodus 29:45; Hebrews 8:10"> And what agreement hath the temple of God with idols? For you are the temple of the living God; as God saith: I will dwell in them, and walk among them; and I will be their God, and they shall be my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; Revelation 18:4; 2 Corinthians 7:1"> Wherefore, Go out from among them, and be ye separate, saith the Lord, and touch not the unclean thing:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 21:7; John 1:12; 2 Samuel 7:14"> And I will receive you; and I will be a Father to you; and you shall be my sons and daughters, saith the Lord Almighty.</span>
