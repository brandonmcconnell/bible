<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:4; 1 Corinthians 1:21; 2 Corinthians 11:19"> Would to God you could bear with some little of my folly: but do bear with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 2:19–20; Ephesians 5:26–27; Isaiah 54:5"> For I am jealous of you with the jealousy of God. For I have espoused you to one husband that I may present you as a chaste virgin to Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; Galatians 1:6; Ephesians 4:14"> But I fear lest, as the serpent seduced Eve by his subtilty, so your minds should be corrupted, and fall from the simplicity that is in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 1:6–8; 1 Corinthians 3:11; 1 Timothy 2:5"> For if he that cometh preacheth another Christ, whom we have not preached; or if you receive another Spirit, whom you have not received; or another gospel which you have not received; you might well bear with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:11–12; 1 Corinthians 15:10; Galatians 2:6–9"> For I suppose that I have done nothing less than the great apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 3:4; 1 Corinthians 1:17; 2 Corinthians 4:2"> For although I be rude in speech, yet not in knowledge; but in all things we have been made manifest to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:13; 1 Thessalonians 2:9; 2 Corinthians 10:1"> Or did I commit a fault, humbling myself, that you might be exalted? Because I preached unto you the gospel of God freely?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 4:18; 2 Corinthians 11:9; Philippians 4:14–16"> I have taken from other churches, receiving wages of them for your ministry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:8–9; 1 Thessalonians 2:9; 1 Thessalonians 2:6"> And, when I was present with you, and wanted, I was chargeable to no man: for that which was wanting to me, the brethren supplied who came from Macedonia; and in all things I have kept myself from being burthensome to you, and so I will keep myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 9:1; Romans 1:9; Acts 18:12"> The truth of Christ is in me, that this glorying shall not be broken off in me in the regions of Achaia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:15; 2 Corinthians 6:11–12; 2 Corinthians 7:3"> Wherefore? Because I love you not? God knoweth it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:12; 1 Corinthians 5:6; 2 Corinthians 5:12"> But what I do, that I will do, that I may cut off the occasion from them that desire occasion, that wherein they glory, they may be found even as we.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 1:7; Revelation 2:2; 1 John 4:1"> For such false apostles are deceitful workmen, transforming themselves into the apostles of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 1:8; 2 Corinthians 2:11; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> And no wonder: for Satan himself transformeth himself into an angel of light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philippians 3:19; Revelation 9:11; Isaiah 9:14–15"> Therefore it is no great thing if his ministers be transformed as the ministers of justice, whose end shall be according to their works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:1; 2 Corinthians 12:6; 2 Corinthians 12:11"> I say again, (let no man think me to be foolish, otherwise take me as one foolish, that I also may glory a little.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:12; Philippians 3:3–6; 2 Corinthians 9:4"> That which I speak, I speak not according to God, but as it were in foolishness, in this matter of glorying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:10; 2 Corinthians 11:21–23; 2 Corinthians 10:12–18"> Seeing that many glory according to the flesh, I will glory also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:10; 1 Corinthians 10:15; 1 Corinthians 8:1"> For you gladly suffer the foolish; whereas yourselves are wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Galatians 2:4; Galatians 4:3; Galatians 5:1"> For you suffer if a man bring you into bondage, if a man devour you, if a man take from you, if a man be lifted up, if a man strike you on the face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:10; 2 Corinthians 11:17; 2 Corinthians 10:1–2"> I speak according to dishonour, as if we had been weak in this part. Wherein if any man dare (I speak foolishly), I dare also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Philippians 3:5; Romans 11:1; Romans 9:4"> They are Hebrews: so am I. They are Israelites: so am I. They are the seed of Abraham: so am I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:4–5; 2 Corinthians 1:9–10; 2 Corinthians 6:9"> They are the ministers of Christ (I speak as one less wise). I am more; in many more labours, in prisons more frequently, in stripes above measure, in deaths often.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Mark 13:9; Deuteronomy 25:2–3; Matthew 10:17"> Of the Jews five times did I receive forty stripes, save one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 14:19; Acts 16:37; Hebrews 11:37"> Thrice was I beaten with rods, once I was stoned, thrice I suffered shipwreck, a night and a day I was in the depth of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 14:5; Acts 17:5; Acts 13:50"> In journeying often, in perils of waters, in perils of robbers, in perils from my own nation, in perils from the Gentiles, in perils in the city, in perils in the wilderness, in perils in the sea, in perils from false brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Philippians 4:12; 2 Corinthians 6:5; 1 Thessalonians 2:9"> In labour and painfulness, in much watchings, in hunger and thirst, in fastings often, in cold and nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:17; Romans 15:16; Acts 20:18–35"> Besides those things which are without: my daily instance, the solicitude for all the churches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:13; 1 Corinthians 9:22; Galatians 4:8–20"> Who is weak, and I am not weak? Who is scandalized, and I am not on fire?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:5–11; Proverbs 25:27; 1 Corinthians 2:3"> If I must needs glory, I will glory of the things that concern my infirmity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 9:5; Ephesians 1:3; John 10:30"> The God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who is blessed for ever, knoweth that I lie not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 9:24–25; 2 Corinthians 11:26"> At Damascus, the governor of the nation under Aretas the king, guarded the city of the Damascenes, to apprehend me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 9:25; 1 Samuel 19:12; Joshua 2:18"> And through a window in a basket was I let down by the wall, and so escaped his hands.</span>
