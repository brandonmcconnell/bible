<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:23; 2 Corinthians 12:20–21; 2 Corinthians 13:10"> But I determined this with myself, not to come to you again in sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:8; 2 Corinthians 1:14; 1 Corinthians 12:26"> For if I make you sorrowful, who is he then that can make me glad, but the same who is made sorrowful by me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 5:10; 2 Corinthians 8:22; Philemon 1:21"> And I wrote this same to you; that I may not, when I come, have sorrow upon sorrow, from them of whom I ought to rejoice: having confidence in you all, that my joy is the joy of you all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:12; Proverbs 27:5–6; Leviticus 19:17–18"> For out of much affliction and anguish of heart, I wrote to you with many tears: not that you should be made sorrowful: but that you might know the charity I have more abundantly towards you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:12–13; Galatians 4:12; Proverbs 17:25"> And if any one have caused grief, he hath not grieved me; but in part, that I may not burden you all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:4–5; 1 Timothy 5:20; 2 Corinthians 7:11"> To him who is such a one, this rebuke is sufficient, which is given by many:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Galatians 6:1–2; Colossians 3:13"> So that on the contrary, you should rather forgive him and comfort him, lest perhaps such a one be swallowed up with overmuch sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jude 1:22–23; Galatians 6:1–2; Galatians 6:10"> Wherefore, I beseech you, that you would confirm your charity towards him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:6; Philemon 1:21; Philippians 2:22"> For to this end also did I write, that I may know the experiment of you, whether you be obedient in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 20:23; Matthew 18:18; 2 Corinthians 5:20"> And to whom you have pardoned any thing, I also. For, what I have pardoned, if I have pardoned any thing, for your sakes have I done it in the person of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Ephesians 6:11–12; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> That we be not overreached by Satan. For we are not ignorant of his devices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 16:8; Acts 14:27; Colossians 4:3"> And when I was come to Troas for the gospel of Christ, and a door was opened unto me in the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:5–6; 2 Corinthians 12:18; Titus 1:4"> I had no rest in my spirit, because I found not Titus my brother; but bidding them farewell, I went into Macedonia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Romans 8:37; 2 Corinthians 2:15–16"> Now thanks be to God, who always maketh us to triumph in Christ Jesus, and manifesteth the odour of his knowledge by us in every place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; 1 Corinthians 1:18; Ezekiel 20:41"> For we are the good odour of Christ unto God, in them that are saved, and in them that perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:5–6; John 9:39; 1 Peter 2:7–8"> To the one indeed the odour of death unto death: but to the others the odour of life unto life. And for these things who is so sufficient?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:2; 2 Corinthians 1:12; 2 Peter 2:1–3"> For we are not as many, adulterating the word of God; but with sincerity, but as from God, before God, in Christ we speak.</span>
