<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 16:9; 2 Corinthians 9:2; 2 Corinthians 9:4"> Now we make known unto you, brethren, the grace of God, that hath been given in the churches of Macedonia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 2:5; Proverbs 11:25; 2 Corinthians 6:10"> That in much experience of tribulation, they have had abundance of joy; and their very deep poverty hath abounded unto the riches of their simplicity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:29; Exodus 35:5; Exodus 35:21–22"> For according to their power (I bear them witness), and beyond their power, they were willing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 24:17; Galatians 6:10; 2 Corinthians 9:1"> With much entreaty begging of us the grace and communication of the ministry that is done toward the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 12:1; Romans 14:7–9; 2 Chronicles 30:12"> And not as we hoped, but they gave their own selves first to the Lord, then to us by the will of God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:18; 2 Corinthians 8:19; 2 Corinthians 8:4"> Insomuch, that we desired Titus, that as he had begun, so also he would finish among you this same grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:8; 2 Peter 1:5–8; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> That as in all things you abound in faith, and word, and knowledge, and all carefulness; moreover also in your charity towards us, so in this grace also you may abound.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:6; 1 John 3:17–19; James 2:14–16"> I speak not as commanding; but by the carefulness of others, approving also the good disposition of your charity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 8:32; Philippians 2:6–8; Matthew 20:28"> For you know the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, that being rich he became poor, for your sakes; that through his poverty you might be rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:18–19; Hebrews 13:16; 2 Corinthians 9:2"> And herein I give my advice; for this is profitable for you, who have begun not only to do, but also to be willing, a year ago.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:2"> Now therefore perform ye it also in deed; that as your mind is forward to be willing, so it may be also to perform, out of that which you have.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 12:42–44; 2 Corinthians 9:7; Luke 21:1–4"> For if the will be forward, it is accepted according to that which a man hath, not according to that which he hath not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 4:34; Romans 15:26–27"> For I mean not that others should be eased, and you burthened, but by an equality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:12; Acts 4:34"> In this present time let your abundance supply their want, that their abundance also may supply your want, that there may be an equality,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 16:18; Luke 22:35"> As it is written: He that had much, had nothing over; and he that had little, had no want.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 17:17; Jeremiah 32:40; 2 Corinthians 2:13–14"> And thanks be to God, who hath given the same carefulness for you in the heart of Titus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:6; Hebrews 13:22; 2 Corinthians 8:10"> For indeed he accepted the exhortation; but being more careful, of his own will he went unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:18; 2 Corinthians 8:19; 1 Corinthians 7:17"> We have sent also with him the brother, whose praise is in the gospel through all the churches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:3–4; 2 Corinthians 4:15; 2 Corinthians 9:8"> And not that only, but he was also ordained by the churches companion of our travels, for this grace, which is administered by us, to the glory of the Lord, and our determined will:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:22; Romans 14:16; Ephesians 5:15"> Avoiding this, lest any man should blame us in this abundance which is administered by us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 12:17; 1 Peter 2:12; Philippians 4:8"> For we forecast what may be good not only before God, but also before men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Philippians 2:20–22"> And we have sent with them our brother also, whom we have often proved diligent in many things; but now much more diligent, with much confidence in you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 2:25; Philemon 1:17; 2 Corinthians 8:6"> Either for Titus, who is my companion and fellow labourer towards you, or our brethren, the apostles of the churches, the glory of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:14; 2 Corinthians 7:4; 2 Corinthians 9:2–4"> Wherefore shew ye to them, in the sight of the churches, the evidence of your charity, and of our boasting on your behalf.</span>
