<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 12:32–33; 2 Kings 23:17; 1 Thessalonians 4:15"> And behold there came a man of God out of Juda, by the word of the Lord to Bethel, when Jeroboam was standing upon the altar, and burning incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:1; 2 Kings 23:15–18; 2 Kings 22:1–2"> And he cried out against the altar in the word of the Lord, and said: O altar, altar, thus saith the Lord: Behold a child shall be born to the house of David, Josias by name, and he shall immolate upon thee the priests of the high places, who now burn incense upon thee, and he shall burn men’s bones upon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:22; Exodus 4:3–5; Judges 6:17"> And he gave a sign the same day, saying: This shall be the sign, that the Lord hath spoken: Behold the altar shall be rent, and the ashes that are upon it shall be poured out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 11:5; 2 Chronicles 18:25–27; Luke 6:10"> And when the king had heard the word of the man of God, which he had cried out against the altar in Bethel, he stretched forth his hand from the altar, saying: Lay hold on him. And his hand which he stretched forth against him withered: and he was not able to draw it back again to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 13:3; Jeremiah 28:16–17; Deuteronomy 18:22"> The altar also was rent, and the ashes were poured out from the altar, according to the sign which the man of God had given before in the word of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 9:28; Acts 8:24; Exodus 10:17"> And the king said to the man of God: Entreat the face of the Lord thy God, and pray for me, that my hand may be restored to me. And the man of God besought the face of the Lord, and the king’s hand was restored to him, and it became as it was before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 5:15; 1 Samuel 9:7–8; Judges 13:15"> And the king said to the man of God: Come home with me to dine, and I will make thee presents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 24:13; Numbers 22:18; Mark 6:23"> And the man of God answered the king: If thou wouldst give me half thy house I will not go with thee, nor eat bread, nor drink water in this place:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 13:17; Deuteronomy 13:13–18; Romans 16:17"> For so it was enjoined me by the word of the Lord commanding me: Thou shalt not eat bread nor drink water, nor return by the same way that thou camest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> So he departed by another way, and returned not by the way that he came into Bethel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 23:18; 1 Kings 13:20–21; Numbers 24:2"> Now a certain old prophet dwelt in Bethel, and his sons came to him and told him all the works that the man of God had done that day in Bethel: and they told their father the words which he had spoken to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And their father said to them: What way went he? His sons shewed him the way by which the man of God went, who came out of Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 10:4; 2 Samuel 19:26; Judges 5:10"> And he said to his sons: Saddle me the ass. And when they had saddled him, he got up,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Philippians 4:12–13; 2 Corinthians 11:27; 1 Corinthians 4:11–12"> And went after the man of God, and found him sitting under a turpentine tree: and he said to him: Art thou the man of God that camest from Juda? He answered: I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And he said to him: Come home with me, to eat bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 13:8–9; Matthew 16:23; Numbers 22:19"> But he said: I must not return, nor go with thee, neither will I eat bread, nor drink water in this place:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 20:35; 1 Thessalonians 4:15; 1 Kings 13:1"> Because the Lord spoke to me in the word of the Lord, saying: Thou shalt not eat bread, and thou shalt not drink water there, nor return by the way thou wentest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 John 4:1; Matthew 7:15; Numbers 22:35"> He said to him: I also am a prophet like unto thee: and an angel spoke to me in the word of the Lord, saying: Bring him back with thee into thy house, that he may eat bread, and drink water. He deceived him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:1; Genesis 3:6; Acts 4:19"> And brought him back with him: so he ate bread and drank water in his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 7:22; Numbers 24:4; Numbers 24:16–24"> And as they sat at table, the word of the Lord came to the prophet that brought him back:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 13:17; 1 Samuel 13:13–14; 1 Samuel 4:18"> And he cried out to the man of God who came out of Juda, saying: Thus saith the Lord: Because thou hast not been obedient to the Lord, and hast not kept the commandment which the Lord thy God commanded thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 14:18–20; 1 Kings 14:13; Jeremiah 22:18–19"> And hast returned and eaten bread, and drunk water in the place wherein he commanded thee that thou shouldst not eat bread, nor drink water, thy dead body shall not be brought into the sepulchre of thy fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And when he had eaten and drunk, he saddled his ass for the prophet, whom he had brought back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 20:36; 2 Kings 2:24; Proverbs 26:13"> And when he was gone, a lion found him in the way, and killed him, and his body was cast in the way: and the ass stood by him, and the lion stood by the dead body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 13:11"> And behold, men passing by saw the dead body cast in the way, and the lion standing by the body. And they came and told it in the city, wherein that old prophet dwelt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:6; Proverbs 11:31; Psalms 119:120"> And when that prophet, who had brought him back out of the way, heard of it, he said: It is the man of God, that was disobedient to the mouth of the Lord, and the Lord hath delivered him to the lion, and he hath torn him, and killed him according to the word of the Lord, which he spoke to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And he said to his sons: Saddle me an ass. And when they had saddled it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Kings 17:6; Psalms 148:7–8; Daniel 6:22–24"> And he was gone, he found the dead body cast in the way, and the ass and the lion standing by the carcass: the lion had not eaten of the dead body, nor hurt the ass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And the prophet took up the body of the man of God, and laid it upon the ass, and going back brought it into the city of the old prophet, to mourn for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:18; Acts 8:2; 1 Kings 14:13"> And he laid his dead body in his own sepulchre: and they mourned over him, saying: Alas! alas! my brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Kings 23:17–18; Numbers 23:10; Luke 16:22–23"> And when they had mourned over him, he said to his sons: When I am dead, bury me in the sepulchre wherein the man of God is buried: lay my bones beside his bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 16:24; 1 Kings 13:2; 1 Kings 12:31"> For assuredly the word shall come to pass which he hath foretold in the word of the Lord against the altar that is in Bethel: and against all the temples of the high places, that are in the cities of Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:15; 2 Chronicles 13:9; Numbers 17:12–13"> After these words Jeroboam came not back from his wicked way: but on the contrary he made of the meanest of the people priests of the high places: whosoever would, he filled his hand, and he was made a priest of the high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 14:10; 1 Kings 15:29–30; 1 Kings 12:30"> And for this cause did the house of Jeroboam sin, and was cut off and destroyed from the face of the earth.</span>
