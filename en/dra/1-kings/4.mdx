<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; 1 Corinthians 12:28; 2 Samuel 8:15–18"> And these were the princes which he had: Azarias the son of Sadoc the priest:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:16; Isaiah 62:6; 1 Chronicles 18:15"> Elihoreph, and Ahia, the sons of Sisa, scribes: Josaphat the son of Ahilud, recorder:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 2:35; 1 Kings 2:26–27"> Banaias the son of Joiada, over the army: and Sadoc and Abiathar priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:33; 2 Samuel 15:37; 2 Samuel 16:16"> Azarias the son of Nathan, over them that were about the king: Zabud, the son of Nathan the priest, the king’s friend:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 9:15; 2 Samuel 20:24; 1 Kings 12:18"> And Ahisar governor of the house: and Adoniram the son of Abda over the tribute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:1–15"> And Solomon had twelve governors over all Israel, who provided victuals for the king and for his household: for every one provided necessaries, each man his month in the year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 24:33; Judges 19:1; Judges 17:1"> And these are their names: Benhur, in mount Ephraim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 21:16; 1 Samuel 6:20; 1 Samuel 6:12"> Bendecar, in Macces, and in Salebim, and in Bethsames, and in Elon, and in Bethanan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 15:35; Joshua 12:17; Joshua 17:2"> Benhesed in Aruboth: his was Socho, and all the land of Epher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 11:2; Joshua 12:23; Joshua 17:11"> Benabinadab, to whom belonged all Nephath-Dor, he had Tapheth the daughter of Solomon to wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 17:11; Judges 5:19; Joshua 3:16"> Bana the son of Ahilud, who governed Thanac and Mageddo, and all Bethsan, which is by Sarthana beneath Jezrael, from Bethsan unto Abelmehula over against Jecmaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:4; Numbers 32:41; 1 Kings 22:3"> Bengaber in Ramoth Galaad: he had the towns of Jair the son of Manasses in Galaad, he was chief in all the country of Argob, which is in Basan, threescore great cities with walls, and brazen bolts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 13:26; 2 Samuel 17:24; 2 Samuel 2:8"> Abinadab the son of Addo was chief in Manaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 19:32–39; 2 Samuel 15:27; 1 Samuel 18:18"> Achimaas in Nephtali: he also had Basemath the daughter of Solomon to wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 19:24–31; 2 Samuel 15:32"> Baana the son of Husi, in Aser and in Baloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 19:17–23"> Josaphat the son of Pharue, in Issachar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 1:8; Joshua 18:20–28; Zechariah 12:13"> Semei the son of Ela in Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:26–17; Joshua 13:9–12; Numbers 21:21–35"> Gaber the son of Uri, in the land of Galaad, in the land of Sehon the king of the Amorrhites and of Og the king of Basan, over all that were in that land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; 1 Kings 3:8; Genesis 32:12"> Juda and Israel were innumerable, as the sand of the sea in multitude: eating and drinking, and rejoicing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; Joshua 1:4; Psalms 68:29"> And Solomon had under him all the kingdoms from the river to the land of the Philistines, even to the border of Egypt: and they brought him presents, and served him, all the days of his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And the provision of Solomon for each day was thirty measures of fine flour, and threescore measures of meal,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:17–18"> Ten fat oxen and twenty out of the pastures, and a hundred rams, besides venison of harts, roes, and buffles, and fatted fowls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:9; Psalms 72:11; Psalms 72:3"> For he had all the country which was beyond the river, from Thaphsa to Gazan, and all the kings of those countries: and he had peace on every side round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Micah 4:4; Zechariah 3:10; Judges 20:1"> And Juda and Israel dwelt without any fear, every one under his vine, and under his fig tree, from Dan to Bersabee, all the days of Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:14; 2 Chronicles 9:25; 2 Samuel 8:4"> And Solomon had forty thousand stalls of chariot horses, and twelve thousand for the saddle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And the foresaid governors of the king fed them: and they furnished the necessaries also for king Solomon’s table, with great care in their time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Esther 8:10; Esther 8:14; Micah 1:13"> They brought barley also and straw for the horses, and beasts, to the place where the king was, according as it was appointed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 3:12; James 3:17; 1 Kings 4:20"> And God gave to Solomon wisdom and understanding exceeding much, and largeness of heart as the sand that is on the sea shore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Daniel 5:11–12; Genesis 25:6; Daniel 1:20"> And the wisdom of Solomon surpassed the wisdom of all the Orientals, and of the Egyptians,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:6; 1 Chronicles 15:19; 1 Kings 3:12"> And he was wiser than all men: wiser than Ethan the Ezrahite, and Heman, and Chalcol, and Dorda the sons of Mahol, and he was renowned in all nations round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:1–17; Matthew 13:35; Proverbs 1:1–31"> Solomon also spoke three thousand parables: and his poems were a thousand and five.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Kings 19:23; Psalms 51:7; Psalms 92:12"> And he treated about trees from the cedar that is in Libanus, unto the hyssop that cometh out of the wall: and he discoursed of beasts, and of fowls, and of creeping things, and of fishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:23; 1 Kings 10:1; 2 Chronicles 9:1"> And they came from all nations to hear the wisdom of Solomon, and from all the kings of the earth, who heard of his wisdom.</span>
