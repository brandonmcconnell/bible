<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 23:1–2; 1 Chronicles 29:27–28; 1 Chronicles 23:1"> Now king David was old, and advanced in years: and when he was covered with clothes, he was not warm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:11; Genesis 16:5; Micah 7:5"> His servants therefore said to him: Let us seek for our lord the king, a young virgin, and let her stand before the king, and cherish him, and sleep in his bosom, and warm our lord the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 19:18; Esther 2:4; Esther 2:2"> So they sought a beautiful young woman in all the coasts of Israel, and they found Abisag a Sunamitess, and brought her to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 1:25"> And the damsel was exceeding beautiful, and she slept with the king: and served him, but the king did not know her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:1; 2 Samuel 3:4; Deuteronomy 17:15–16"> And Adonias the son of Haggith exalted himself, saying: I will be king. And he made himself chariots and horsemen, and fifty men to run before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:3–4; 1 Samuel 3:13; Hebrews 12:5–6"> Neither did his father rebuke him at any time, saying: Why hast thou done this? And he also was very beautiful, the next in birth after Absalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:25; 1 Kings 2:22; 1 Samuel 22:20–23"> And he conferred with Joab the son of Sarvia, and with Abiathar the priest, who furthered Adonias’s side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 4:18; 2 Samuel 23:8–39; 2 Samuel 20:25"> But Sadoc the priest, and Banaias the son of Joiada, and Nathan the prophet, and Semei, and Rei, and the strength of David’s army was not with Adonias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:17; 2 Samuel 13:23–27; 2 Samuel 15:11–12"> And Adonias having slain rams and calves, and all fat cattle by the stone of Zoheleth, which was near the fountain Rogel, invited all his brethren the king’s sons, and all the men of Juda, the king’s servants:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:24; 1 Kings 1:19; 2 Samuel 12:1–15"> But Nathan the prophet, and Banaias, and all the valiant men, and Solomon his brother, he invited not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:4; 2 Samuel 7:12–17; 2 Samuel 12:24–25"> And Nathan said to Bethsabee the mother of Solomon: Hast thou not heard that Adonias the son of Haggith reigneth, and our lord David knoweth it not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 11:14; Acts 27:31; Genesis 19:17"> Now then come, take my counsel and save thy life, and the life of thy son Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 1:30; 1 Kings 1:17; Psalms 132:11–12"> Go, and get thee in to king David, and say to him: Didst not thou, my lord O king, swear to me thy handmaid, saying: Solomon thy son shall reign after me, and he shall sit on my throne? why then doth Adonias reign?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:1; 1 Kings 1:17–27"> And while thou art yet speaking there with the king, I will come in after thee, and will fill up thy words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 1:1"> So Bethsabee went in to the king into the chamber: now the king was very old, and Abisag the Sunamitess ministered to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:23; 1 Samuel 24:8; 1 Samuel 20:41"> Bethsabee bowed herself, and worshipped the king. And the king said to her: What is thy will?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 1:13; 1 Kings 1:30; 1 Peter 3:6"> She answered and said: My lord, thou didst swear to thy handmaid by the Lord thy God, saying: Solomon thy son shall reign after me, and he shall sit on my throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 1:24; 1 Kings 1:11; Acts 3:17"> And behold now Adonias reigneth, and thou, my lord the king, knowest nothing of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 1:25; 1 Kings 1:7–10"> He hath killed oxen, and all fat cattle, and many rams, and invited all the king’s sons, and Abiathar the priest, and Joab the general of the army: but Solomon thy servant he invited not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:1; Psalms 123:2; 1 Chronicles 22:8–10"> And now, my lord O king, the eyes of all Israel are upon thee, that thou shouldst tell them, who shall sit on thy throne, my lord the king, after thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 2:10; Deuteronomy 31:16; Genesis 15:15"> Otherwise it shall come to pass, when my lord the king sleepeth with his fathers, that I and my son Solomon shall be counted offenders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 1:16–18; Genesis 24:15; Daniel 9:20"> As she was yet speaking with the king, Nathan the prophet came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 1:16; 1 Peter 2:17; Romans 13:7"> And they told the king, saying: Nathan the prophet is here. And when he was come in before the king, and had worshipped, bowing down to the ground,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 1:17–18; 1 Kings 1:5; 1 Kings 1:13–14"> Nathan said: My lord O king, hast thou said: Let Adonias reign after me, and let him sit upon my throne?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:24; 1 Kings 1:19; 1 Kings 1:9"> Because he is gone down today, and hath killed oxen, and fatlings, and many rams, and invited all the king’s sons, and the captains of the army, and Abiathar the priest: and they are eating and drinking before him, and saying: God save king Adonias:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 1:8; 1 Kings 1:10; 2 Samuel 7:2"> But me thy servant, and Sadoc the priest, and Banaias the son of Joiada, and Solomon thy servant he hath not invited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 15:15; 1 Kings 1:24; 2 Kings 4:27"> Is this word come out from my lord the king, and hast thou not told me thy servant who should sit on the throne of my lord the king after him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And king David answered and said: Call to me Bethsabee. And when she was come in to the king, and stood before him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 4:9; Psalms 136:24; 1 Samuel 14:45"> The king swore and said: As the Lord liveth, who hath delivered my soul out of all distress,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Kings 1:13; 1 Kings 1:17"> Even as I swore to thee by the Lord the God of Israel, saying: Solomon thy son shall reign after me, and he shall sit upon my throne in my stead, so will I do this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Daniel 2:4; Daniel 5:10; Daniel 3:9"> And Bethsabee bowing with her face to the earth worshipped the king, saying: May my lord David live for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 1:8; 1 Kings 1:38; 1 Kings 1:26"> King David also said: Call me Sadoc the priest, and Nathan the prophet, and Banaias the son of Joiada. And when they were come in before the king,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:30; 1 Kings 1:38; 2 Chronicles 33:14"> He said to them: Take with you the servants of your lord, and set my son Solomon upon my mule: and bring him to Gihon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 1:25; 2 Samuel 15:10; 1 Samuel 10:1"> And let Sadoc the priest, and Nathan the prophet anoint him there king over Israel: and you shall sound the trumpet, and shall say: God save king Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 1:17; Psalms 72:1–2; 1 Kings 1:13"> And you shall come up after him, and he shall come, and shall sit upon my throne, and he shall reign in my stead: and I will appoint him to be ruler over Israel, and over Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 28:6; 1 Corinthians 14:16; Psalms 72:19"> And Banaias the son of Joiada answered the king, saying: Amen: so say the Lord the God of my lord the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:13; 1 Kings 1:47; Joshua 1:17"> As the Lord hath been with my lord the king, so be he with Solomon, and make his throne higher than the throne of my lord king David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:18; 1 Kings 1:8; 1 Kings 1:33"> So Sadoc the priest, and Nathan the prophet went down, and Banaias the son of Joiada, and the Cerethi, and Phelethi: and they set Solomon upon the mule of king David, and brought him to Gihon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 89:20; 1 Kings 1:34; 1 Chronicles 29:22"> And Sadoc the priest took a horn of oil out of the tabernacle, and anointed Solomon: and they sounded the trumpet, and all the people said: God save king Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Kings 11:14; Zechariah 9:9; 2 Kings 11:20"> And all the multitude went up after him, and the people played with pipes, and rejoiced with a great joy, and the earth rang with the noise of their cry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Job 15:21–22; Luke 17:26–29; Acts 21:31"> And Adonias, and all that were invited by him, heard it, and now the feast was at an end: Joab also hearing the sound of the trumpet, said: What meaneth this noise of the city in an uproar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:36; 2 Samuel 17:17; 2 Samuel 18:27"> While he yet spoke, Jonathan the son of Abiathar the priest came: and Adonias said to him: Come in, because thou art a valiant man, and bringest good news.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> And Jonathan answered Adonias: Not so: for our lord king David hath appointed Solomon king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And hath sent with him Sadoc the priest, and Nathan the prophet, and Banaias the son of Joiada, and the Cerethi, and Phelethi, and they have set him upon the king’s mule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Kings 1:40; Daniel 5:26–28; 1 Samuel 4:5"> And Sadoc the priest, and Nathan the prophet have anointed him king in Gihon: and they are gone up from thence rejoicing, so that the city rang again: this is the noise that you have heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:23; Psalms 132:11; 1 Kings 1:13"> Moreover Solomon sitteth upon the throne of the kingdom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Genesis 47:31; 1 Kings 1:37; Ezra 6:10"> And the king’s servants going in have blessed our lord king David, saying: May God make the name of Solomon greater than thy name, and make his throne greater than thy throne. And the king adored in his bed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Kings 3:6; Psalms 132:11–12; Psalms 145:2"> And he said: Blessed be the Lord the God of Israel, who hath given this day one to sit on my throne, my eyes seeing it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Isaiah 21:4–5; Proverbs 28:1; Daniel 5:4–6"> Then all the guests of Adonias were afraid, and they all arose and every man went his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="1 Kings 2:28; Exodus 21:14; Exodus 27:2"> And Adonias fearing Solomon, arose, and went, and took hold on the horn of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> And they told Solomon, saying: Behold Adonias, fearing king Solomon, hath taken hold of the horn of the altar, saying: Let king Solomon swear to me this day, that he will not kill his servant with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:45; 2 Samuel 14:11; Proverbs 13:6"> And Solomon said: If he be a good man, there shall not so much as one hair of his head fall to the ground: but if evil be found in him, he shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="1 Kings 1:16; 2 Samuel 14:24; 2 Samuel 1:2"> Then king Solomon sent, and brought him out from the altar: and going in he worshipped king Solomon: and Solomon said to him: Go to thy house.</span>
