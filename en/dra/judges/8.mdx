<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:41; Judges 12:1–6; Job 5:2"> And the men of Ephraim said to him: What is this that thou meanest to do, that thou wouldst not call us when thou wentest to fight against Madian? and they chid him sharply and almost offered violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:4–7; Philippians 2:2–3; James 3:13–18"> And he answered them: What could I have done like to that which you have done? Is not one bunch of grapes of Ephraim better than the vintages of Abiezer?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; Judges 7:24–25; John 4:37"> The Lord hath delivered into your bands the princes of Madian, Oreb and Zeb: what could I have done like to what you have done? And when he had said this, their spirit was appeased, with which they swelled against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; 2 Corinthians 4:16; 1 Samuel 14:28–29"> And when Gedeon was come to the Jordan, he passed over it with the three hundred men, that were with him: who were so weary that they could not pursue after them that fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 33:17; Psalms 60:6; 1 Samuel 25:18"> And he said to the men of Soccoth: Give, I beseech you, bread to the people that is with me, for they are faint: that we may pursue Zebee, and Salmana the kings of Madian.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 20:11; Philippians 2:21; Proverbs 18:23"> The princes of Soccoth answered: Peradventure the palms of the hands of Zebee and Salmana are in thy hand, and therefore thou demandest that we should give bread to thy army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 7:15"> And he said to them: When the Lord therefore shall have delivered Zebee and Salmana into my hands, I will thresh your flesh with the thorns and briers of the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 32:30–31; 1 Kings 12:25"> And going up from thence, he came to Phanuel: and he spoke the like things to the men of that place. And they also answered him, as the men of Soccoth had answered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 8:17; 1 Kings 22:27–28"> He said therefore to them also: When I shall return a conqueror in peace, I will destroy this tower.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 7:12; Judges 20:25; Judges 20:2"> But Zebee and Salmana were resting with all their army. For fifteen thousand men were left of all the troops of the eastern people, and one hundred and twenty thousand warriors that drew the sword, were slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 32:35; Numbers 32:42; Judges 18:27"> And Gedeon went up by the way of them that dwelt in tents, on the east of Nobe and Jegbaa, and smote the camp of the enemies, who were secure, and suspected no hurt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 83:11; Amos 2:14; Job 12:16–21"> And Zebee and Salmana fled, and Gedeon pursued and took them, all their host being put in confusion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And returning from the battle before the sun rising,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 1:24–25; 1 Samuel 30:11–15"> He took a boy of the men of Soccoth: and he asked him the names of the princes and ancients of Soccoth, and he described unto him seventy-seven men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 8:6–7"> And he came to Soccoth and said to them: Behold Zebee and Salmana, concerning whom you upbraided me, saying: Peradventure the hands of Zebee and Salmana, are in thy hands, and therefore thou demandest that we should give bread to the men that are weary and faint.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 8:7; Ezra 2:6; Proverbs 10:13"> So he took the ancients of the city and thorns and briers of the desert, and tore them with the same, and cut in pieces the men of Soccoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 8:9; 1 Kings 12:25"> And he demolished the tower of Phanuel, and slew the men of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 4:6; Jude 1:16; Psalms 89:12"> And he said to Zebee and Salmana: What manner of men were they whom you slew in Thabor? They answered: They were like thee, and one of them as the son of a king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> He answered them: They were my brethren, the sons of my mother. As the Lord liveth, if you had saved them, I would not kill you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 149:9; 1 Samuel 15:33; Joshua 10:24"> And he said to Jether his eldest son: Arise, and slay them. But he drew not his sword: for he was afraid, being but yet a boy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 83:11; Judges 8:26; Isaiah 3:18"> And Zebee and Salmana said: Do thou rise, and run upon us: because the strength of a man is according to his age: Gedeon rose up and slew Zebee and Salmana: and he took the ornaments and bosses, with which the necks of the camels of kings are wont to be adorned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 6:15; Judges 9:8–15; 1 Samuel 12:12"> And all the men of Israel said to Gedeon: Rule thou over us and thy son, and thy son’s son: because thou hast delivered us from the hand of Madian.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 33:22; 1 Samuel 12:12; 1 Samuel 10:19"> And he said to them: I will not rule over you, neither shall my son rule over you, but the Lord shall rule over you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 25:13; Genesis 37:25; Genesis 37:28"> And he said to them: I desire one request of you: Give me the earlets of your spoils. For the Ismaelites were accustomed to wear golden earlets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> They answered: We will give them most willingly. And spreading a mantle on the ground, they cast upon it the earlets of the spoils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 18:16; John 19:2; Revelation 18:12"> And the weight of the earlets that he requested, was a thousand seven hundred sicles of gold, besides the ornaments, and jewels, and purple raiment which the kings of Madian were went to use, and besides the golden chains that were about the camels’ necks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 17:5; Judges 18:14; Psalms 106:39"> And Gedeon made an ephod thereof, and put it in his city Ephra. And all Israel committed fornication with it, and it became a ruin to Gedeon and to all his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 5:31; Judges 3:11; Judges 3:30"> But Madian was humbled before the children of Israel, neither could they any more lift up their beads: but the land rested for forty years, while Gedeon presided.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 6:32; Judges 7:1; 1 Samuel 12:11"> So Jerobaal the son of Joas went, and dwelt in his own house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 9:5; Judges 9:2; 1 Kings 11:3"> And he had seventy sons, who came out of his thigh, for he had many wives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 9:18; Genesis 22:24; Genesis 16:15"> And his concubine, that he had in Sichem, bore him a son, whose name was Abimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 25:8; Judges 6:24; Job 5:26"> And Gedeon the son of Joas died in a good old age, and was buried in the sepulchre of his father in Ephra of the family of Ezri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Judges 9:46; Judges 2:19; Judges 9:4"> But after Gedeon was dead, the children of Israel turned again, and committed fornication with Baalim. And they made a covenant with Baal, that he should be their god:</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 78:42; Psalms 106:21; Psalms 78:11"> And they remembered not the Lord their God, who delivered them out of the hands of all their enemies round about:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:14–15; Judges 9:5; Judges 9:16–19"> Neither did they shew mercy to the house of Jerobaal Gedeon, according to all the good things he had done to Israel.</span>
