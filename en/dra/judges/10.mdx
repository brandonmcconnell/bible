<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 2:16; Judges 3:9; Joshua 15:48"> After Abimelech there arose a ruler in Israel, Thola son of Phua the uncle of Abimelech, a man of Issachar, who dwelt in Samir of mount Ephraim:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And he judged Israel three and twenty years, and he died and was buried in Samir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 32:29; Genesis 31:48"> To him succeeded Jair the Galaadite, who judged Israel for two and twenty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 32:41; Judges 5:10; Deuteronomy 3:14"> Having thirty sons that rode on thirty ass colts, and were princes of thirty cities, which from his name were called Havoth Jair, that is, the towns of Jair, until this present day in the land of Galaad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And Jair died: and was buried in the place which was called Camon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 11:33; 1 Kings 11:7; 2 Kings 23:13"> But the children of Israel, adding new sins to their old ones, did evil in the sight of the Lord, and served idols, Baalim and Astaroth, and the gods of Syria and of Sidon and of Moab and of the children of Ammon and of the Philistines: and they left the Lord, and did not serve him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Deuteronomy 32:16–22; 1 Samuel 12:9–10"> And the Lord being angry with them, delivered them into the hands of the Philistines and of the children of Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Isaiah 30:13; Judges 10:5"> And they were afflicted, and grievously oppressed for eighteen years, all they that dwelt beyond the Jordan in the land of the Amorrhite, who is in Galaad:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:1–2; Deuteronomy 28:65; 2 Chronicles 15:5"> Insomuch that the children of Ammon passing over the Jordan, wasted Juda and Benjamin and Ephraim: and Israel was distressed exceedingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:10; Psalms 107:28; Psalms 107:13"> And they cried to the Lord, and said: We have sinned against thee, because we have forsaken the Lord our God, and have served Baalim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 3:31; Numbers 21:21–25; Exodus 14:30"> And the Lord said to them: Did not the Egyptians and the Amorrhites, and the children of Ammon and the Philistines,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 6:3; 2 Chronicles 26:6–7; Psalms 106:42–43"> The Sidonians also and Amalec and Chanaan oppress you, and you cried to me, and I delivered you out of their hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:13; 1 Chronicles 28:9; Judges 2:12"> And yet you have forsaken me, and have worshipped strange gods: therefore I will deliver you no more:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Deuteronomy 32:37–38; 1 Kings 18:27–28"> Go and call upon the gods which you have chosen: let them deliver you in the time of distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:18; 2 Samuel 15:26; Proverbs 28:13"> And the children of Israel said to the Lord: We have sinned, do thou unto us whatsoever pleaseth thee: only deliver us this time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 63:9; Psalms 106:44–45; Ezekiel 18:30–32"> And saying these things, they cast away out of their coasts all the idols of strange gods and served the Lord their God: and he was touched with their miseries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 11:29; Genesis 31:49; Judges 11:11"> And the children of Ammon shouting together, pitched their tents in Galaad: against whom the children of Israel assembled themselves together and camped in Maspha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 11:11; Judges 12:7; Judges 1:1"> And the princes of Galaad said one to another: Whosoever of us shall first begin to fight against the children of Ammon, he shall be the leader of the people of Galaad.</span>
