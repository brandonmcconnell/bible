<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 15:1; Revelation 19:1–3; Psalms 18:1"> In that day Debbora and Barac son of Abinoem sung, and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 5:9; 2 Chronicles 17:16; Psalms 110:3"> O you of Israel, that have willingly offered your lives to danger, bless the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 138:4–5; Psalms 119:46; Psalms 49:1–2"> Hear, O ye kings, give ear, ye princes: It is I, it is I, that will sing to the Lord, I will sing to the Lord the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:2; Psalms 68:7–8; Psalms 77:17"> O Lord, when thou wentest out of Seir, and passedst by the regions of Edom, the earth trembled, and the heavens dropped water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 19:18; Psalms 97:5; Isaiah 64:1–3"> The mountains melted before the face of the Lord, and Sinai before the face of the Lord the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 33:8; Judges 3:31; Lamentations 1:4"> In the days of Samgar the son of Anath, in the days of Jahel the paths rested: and they that went by them, walked through by-ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; 2 Samuel 20:19; Esther 9:19"> The valiant men ceased, and rested in Israel: until Debbora arose, a mother arose in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:16–17; Judges 2:12; Judges 2:17"> The Lord chose new wars, and he himself overthrew the gates of the enemies: a shield and spear was not seen among forty thousand of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 5:2; 2 Corinthians 8:3–4; 2 Corinthians 9:5"> My heart loveth the princes of Israel: O you that of your own good will offered yourselves to danger, bless the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 12:14; Judges 10:4; Psalms 105:2"> Speak, you that ride upon fair asses, and you that sit in judgment, and walk in the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Micah 6:5; Judges 5:8; 1 Samuel 12:7"> Where the chariots were dashed together, and the army of the enemies was choked, there let the justices of the Lord be rehearsed, and his clemency towards the brave men of Israel: then the people of the Lord went down to the gates, and obtained the sovereignty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 4:8; Psalms 68:18; Psalms 57:8"> Arise, arise, O Debbora, arise, arise, and utter a canticle. Arise, Barac, and take hold of thy captives, O son of Abinoem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 2:26–27; Romans 8:37; Psalms 75:7"> The remnants of the people are saved, the Lord hath fought among the valiant ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 12:15; Numbers 32:39–40; Judges 3:27"> Out of Ephraim he destroyed them into Amalec, and after him out of Benjamin into thy people, O Amalec: Out of Machir there came down princes, and out of Zabulon they that led the army to fight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 4:14; Proverbs 22:13; Acts 20:13"> The captains of Issachar were with Debbora, and followed the steps of Barac, who exposed himself to danger, as one going headlong, and into a pit. Ruben being divided against himself, there was found a strife of courageous men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 32:24; Numbers 32:1–5; Judges 5:15"> Why dwellest thou between two borders, that thou mayest hear the bleatings of the flocks? Ruben being divided against himself, there was found a strife of courageous men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 13:25; Joshua 19:24–31; Joshua 13:31"> Galaad rested beyond the Jordan, and Dan applied himself to ships: Aser dwelt on the sea shore, and abode in the havens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 4:10; Judges 4:6; Esther 4:16"> But Zabulon and Nephtali offered their lives to death in the region of Merome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Judges 5:30; Judges 1:27; 1 Kings 4:12"> The kings came and fought, the kings of Chanaan fought in Thanach by the waters of Mageddo, and yet they took no spoils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 10:11; 1 Samuel 7:10; Psalms 77:17–18"> War from heaven was made against them, the stars remaining in their order and courses fought against Sisara.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 4:7; Micah 7:10; Psalms 44:5"> The torrent of Cison dragged their carcasses, the torrent of Cadumim, the torrent of Cisoii: tread thou, my soul, upon the strong ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 20:7; Psalms 147:10–11; Jeremiah 47:4"> The hoofs of the horses were broken whilst the stoutest of the enemies fled amain, and fell headlong down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:10; Judges 21:9–10; 1 Corinthians 16:22"> Curse ye the land of Meroz, said the angel of the Lord: curse the inhabitants thereof, because they came not to the help of the Lord, to help his most valiant men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 1:42; Luke 1:28; Judges 4:17"> Blessed among women be Jahel the wife of Haber the Cinite, and blessed be she in her tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Judges 4:19–21"> He asked her water and she gave him milk, and offered him butter in a dish fit for princes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:49–51; Judges 4:21; 2 Samuel 20:22"> She put her left hand to the nail, and her right hand to the workman’s hammer, and she struck Sisara, seeking in his head a place for the wound, and strongly piercing through his temples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 52:7; James 2:13; Matthew 7:2"> At her feet he fell: he fainted, and he died: he rolled before her feet, and he lay lifeless and wretched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 4:15; James 5:7; Song of Solomon 8:14"> His mother looked out at a window, and howled: and she spoke from the dining room: Why is his chariot so long in coming back? Why are the feet of his horses so slow?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> One that was wiser than the rest of his wives, returned this answer to her mother in law:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 15:9; 2 Samuel 13:18; Genesis 37:3"> Perhaps he is now dividing the spoils, and the fairest of the women is chosen out for him: garments of divers colours are given to Sisara for his prey, and furniture of different kinds is heaped together to adorn the necks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 37:6; 2 Samuel 23:4; Psalms 19:4–5"> So let all thy enemies perish, O Lord: but let them that love thee shine, as the sun shineth in his rising.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And the land rested for forty years.</span>
