<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 34:32; Exodus 31:1–11; Romans 2:13"> And all the multitude of the children of Israel being gathered together, he said to them: These are the things which the Lord hath commanded to be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 23:3; Luke 13:14–15; Exodus 20:9–10"> Six days you shall do work: the seventh day shall be holy unto you, the sabbath, and the rest of the Lord: he that shall do any work on it, shall be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 16:23; Numbers 15:32–36; Exodus 12:16"> You shall kindle no fire in any of your habitations on the sabbath day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 25:1–2"> And Moses said to all the assembly of the children of Israel: This is the word the Lord hath commanded, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Psalms 110:3; Exodus 25:2–7"> Set aside with you firstfruits to the Lord. Let every one that is willing and hath a ready heart, offer them to the Lord: gold, and silver, and brass,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 26:7–14; Exodus 26:31; Exodus 28:15"> Violet and purple, and scarlet twice dyed, and fine linen, goats’ hair,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And rams’ skins dyed red, and violet coloured skins, setim wood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 27:20; Exodus 25:1–40; Exodus 30:23"> And oil to maintain lights, and to make ointment, and most sweet incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 39:6–14; Exodus 28:9; Exodus 25:5"> Onyx stones, and precious stones, for the adorning of the ephod and the rational.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 31:1–6; Exodus 36:1–4"> Whosoever of you is wise, let him come, and make that which the Lord hath commanded:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 26:1–37; Exodus 36:8–34; Exodus 31:7–9"> To wit, the tabernacle and the roof thereof, and the cover, the rings, and the board work with the oars, the pillars, and the sockets:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 25:10–22; Exodus 26:31–33; Exodus 37:1–9"> The ark and the staves, the propitiatory, and the veil that is drawn before it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 25:23–30; Leviticus 24:5–6; Exodus 37:10–16"> The table with the bars and the vessels, and the loaves of proposition:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–39; Matthew 5:14–15; Exodus 37:17–24"> The candlestick to bear up the lights, the vessels thereof and the lamps, and the oil for the nourishing of fires:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 30:1–10; Exodus 30:22–38; Exodus 37:25–28"> The altar of incense, and the bars, and the oil of unction and the incense of spices: the hanging at the door of the tabernacle:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 27:1–8; Exodus 30:18–21; Exodus 38:1–8"> The altar of holocaust, and its grate of brass, with the bars and vessels thereof: the laver and its foot:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:2; Exodus 38:9–20; Exodus 27:9–19"> The curtains of the court with the pillars and the sockets, the hanging in the doors of the entry,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 27:19"> The pins of the tabernacle and of the court with their little cords:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 31:10; Numbers 4:5–15; Exodus 28:1–43"> The vestments that are to be used in the ministry of the sanctuary, the vesture of Aaron the high priest, and of his sons, to do the office of priesthood to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And all the multitude of the children of Israel going out from the presence of Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 25:2; Exodus 35:26; 1 Chronicles 29:14"> Offered firstfruits to the Lord with a most ready and devout mind, to make the work of the tabernacle of the testimony. Whatsoever was necessary to the service, and to the holy vestments,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:70–72; Matthew 2:11; Ezra 2:68–69"> Both men and women gave bracelets and earrings, rings and tablets: every vessel of gold was set aside to be offered to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:8; Exodus 25:2–7; Exodus 35:6–10"> If any man had violet, and purple, and scarlet twice dyed, fine linen and goats’ hair, rams’ skins dyed red, and violet coloured skins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12"> Metal of silver and brass, they offered it to the Lord, and setim wood for divers uses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 16:12; Luke 8:2–3; Proverbs 14:1"> The skillful women also gave such things as they had spun, violet, purple, and scarlet, and fine linen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 35:21; Exodus 36:8; Exodus 35:29"> And goats’ hair, giving all of their own accord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezra 2:68; 1 Chronicles 29:6; Exodus 35:9"> But the princes offered onyx stone, and precious stones, for the ephod and the rational,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 30:23–38; Exodus 35:8"> And spices and oil for the lights, and for the preparing of ointment, and to make the incense of most sweet savour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Exodus 35:21–22; Exodus 35:4"> All both men and women with devout mind offered gifts, that the works might be done which the Lord had commanded by the hand of Moses. All the children of Israel dedicated voluntary offerings to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 31:1–6; James 1:17; 1 Corinthians 12:11"> And Moses said to the children of Israel: Behold the Lord hath called by name Beseleel the son of Uri the son of Hur of the tribe of Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 1:17; Isaiah 11:2–5; Isaiah 61:1–3"> And hath filled him with the spirit of God, with wisdom and understanding and knowledge and all learning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> To devise and to work in gold and silver and brass,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And in engraving stones, and in carpenters’ work. Whatsoever can be devised artificially,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 31:6; 2 Chronicles 2:14; Ezra 7:27"> He hath given in his heart: Ooliab also the son of Achisamech of the tribe of Dan:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Exodus 35:31; 1 Kings 7:14; Isaiah 28:26"> Both of them hath he instructed with wisdom, to do carpenters’ work and tapestry, and embroidery in blue and purple, and scarlet twice dyed, and fine linen, and to weave all things, and to invent all new things.</span>
