<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 10:9; Acts 4:29; Matthew 10:28"> After these things Moses and Aaron went in, and said to Pharao: Thus saith the Lord God of Israel: Let my people go that they may sacrifice to me in the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 3:19; Job 21:15; 2 Kings 18:35"> But he answered: Who is the Lord, that I should hear his voice, and let Israel go? I know not the Lord, neither will I let Israel go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 3:18; Zechariah 14:16–19; Ezekiel 6:11"> And they said: The God of the Hebrews hath called us, to go three days’ journey into the wilderness and to sacrifice to the Lord our God: lest a pestilence or the sword fall upon us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 1:11; Acts 24:5; Amos 7:10"> The king of Egypt said to them: Why do you Moses and Aaron draw off the people from their works? Get you gone to your burdens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 1:7–11; Proverbs 14:28"> And Pharao said: The people of the land is numerous: you see that the multitude is increased: how much more if you give them rest from their works?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 5:10; Exodus 5:19; Deuteronomy 16:18"> Therefore he commanded the same day the overseers of the works, and the taskmasters of the people, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 19:19; Genesis 24:25"> You shall give straw no more to the people to make brick, as before: but let them go and gather straw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 106:41"> And you shall lay upon them the task of bricks, which they did before, neither shall you diminish any thing thereof: for they are idle, and therefore they cry, saying: Let us go and sacrifice to our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 18:20; Jeremiah 43:2; Job 16:3"> Let them be oppressed, with works, and let them fulfill them: that they may not regard lying words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 1:11; Exodus 3:7; Proverbs 29:12"> And the overseers of the works and the taskmasters went out and said to the people: Thus saith Pharao, I allow you no straw:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 5:13–14"> Go, and gather it where you can find it: neither shall any thing of your work be diminished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 15:7; Isaiah 5:24; Isaiah 47:14"> And the people was scattered through all the land of Egypt to gather straw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the overseers of the works pressed them, saying: Fulfill your work every day as before you were wont to do when straw was given you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 10:24"> And they that were over the works of the children of Israel were scourged by Pharao’s taskmasters, saying: Why have you not made up the task of bricks both yesterday and today as before?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the officers of the children of Israel came, and cried out to Pharao, saying: Why dealest thou so with thy servants?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Straw is not given us, and bricks are required of us as before: behold we thy servants are beaten with whips, and thy people is unjustly dealt withal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 26:8; John 6:27; Exodus 5:8"> And he said: You are idle, and therefore you say: Let us go and sacrifice to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:18; Daniel 2:9–13"> Go therefore, and work: straw shall not be given you, and you shall deliver the accustomed number of bricks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:8; Ecclesiastes 4:1; Deuteronomy 32:36"> And the officers of the children of Israel saw that they were in evil case, because it was said to them: There shall not a whit be diminished of the bricks for every day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And they met Moses and Aaron, who stood over against them as they came out from Pharao:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; 1 Samuel 13:4; 1 Chronicles 19:6"> And they said to them: The Lord see and judge, because you have made our savour to stink before Pharao and his servants, and you have given him a sword to kill us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:7; 1 Kings 19:10; Numbers 11:14–15"> And Moses returned to the Lord, and said: Lord, why hast thou afflicted this people? wherefore hast thou sent me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:21; Hebrews 10:36–37; Isaiah 28:16"> For since the time that I went in to Pharao to speak in thy name, he hath afflicted thy people: and thou hast not delivered them.</span>
