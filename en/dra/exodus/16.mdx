<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 33:10–12; Exodus 15:27; Exodus 17:1"> And they set forward from Elim, and all the multitude of the children of Israel came into the desert of Sin, which is between Elim and Sinai: the fifteenth day of the second month, after they came out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:10; Exodus 15:24; Psalms 106:25"> And all the congregation of the children of Israel murmured against Moses and Aaron in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 11:4–5; Numbers 20:3–5; Exodus 17:3"> And the children of Israel said to them: Would to God we had died by the hand of the Lord in the land of Egypt, when we sat over the flesh pots, and ate bread to the full. Why have you brought us into this desert, that you might destroy all the multitude with famine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:3; Psalms 78:24–25; Deuteronomy 8:2"> And the Lord said to Moses: Behold I will rain bread from heaven for you: let the people go forth, and gather what is sufficient for every day: that I may prove them whether they will walk in my law, or not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 16:22–23; Exodus 35:2–3; Leviticus 25:21–22"> But the sixth day let them provide for to bring in: and let it be double to that they were wont to gather every day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 6:7; Numbers 16:28; Exodus 16:12–13"> And Moses and Aaron said to the children of Israel: In the evening you shall know that the Lord hath brought you forth out of the land of Egypt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 40:5; Numbers 16:11; John 11:40"> And in the morning you shall see the glory of the Lord: for he hath heard your murmuring against the Lord: but as for us, what are we, that you mutter against us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 13:2; 1 Samuel 8:7; 1 Thessalonians 4:8"> And Moses said: In the evening the Lord will give you flesh to eat, and in the morning bread to the full: for he hath heard your murmurings, with which you have murmured against him, for what are we? your murmuring is not against us, but against the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 16:16; Exodus 16:2; Exodus 16:8"> Moses also said to Aaron: Say to the whole congregation of the children of Israel: Come before the Lord: for he hath heard your murmuring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 16:7; Numbers 16:19; Numbers 16:42"> And when Aaron spoke to all the assembly of the children of Israel, they looked towards the wilderness: and behold the glory of the Lord appeared in a cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joel 3:17; Ezekiel 39:22; Exodus 6:7"> I have heard the murmuring of the children of Israel: say to them: In the evening you shall eat flesh, and in the morning you shall have your fill of bread: and you shall know that I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 11:9; Psalms 105:40; Psalms 78:27–28"> So it came to pass in the evening, that quails coming up, covered the camp: and in the morning, a dew lay round about the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 11:7–9; Psalms 105:40; Psalms 78:24"> And when it had covered the face of the earth, it appeared in the wilderness small, and as it were beaten with a pestle, like unto the hoar frost on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 16:4; John 6:58; Exodus 16:31"> And when the children of Israel saw it, they said one to another: Manhu! which signifieth: What is this! for they knew not what it was. And Moses said to them: This is the bread, which the Lord hath given you to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 16:36; Exodus 16:18; Exodus 16:32–33"> This is the word, that the Lord hath commanded: Let every one gather of it as much as is enough to eat: a gomor for every man, according to the number of your souls that dwell in a tent, so shall you take of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the children of Israel did so: and they gathered, one more, another less.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:14–15"> And they measured by the measure of a gomor: neither had he more that had gathered more: nor did he find less that had provided less: but every one had gathered, according to what they were able to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 12:10; Exodus 23:18; Matthew 6:34"> And Moses said to them: Let no man leave thereof till the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 10:14; Numbers 16:15; Ephesians 4:26"> And they hearkened not to him, but some of them left until the morning, and it began to be full of worms, an it putrified, and Moses was angry with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 6:6–11; Ecclesiastes 12:1; 2 Corinthians 6:2"> Now every one of them gathered in the morning, as much as might suffice to eat: and after the sun grew hot, it melted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 16:5; Leviticus 25:22; Exodus 16:16"> But on the sixth day they gathered twice as much, that is, two gomors every man: and all the rulers of the multitude came, and told Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 23:3; Exodus 31:15; Exodus 20:8–11"> And he said to them: This is what the Lord hath spoken: Tomorrow is the rest of the sabbath sanctified to the Lord. Whatsoever work is to be done, do it: and the meats that are to be dressed, dress them: and whatsoever shall remain, lay it up until the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 16:20; Exodus 16:33"> And they did so as Moses had commanded, and it did not putrify, neither was there worm found in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 16:23; Exodus 16:29; Nehemiah 9:14"> And Moses said: Eat it today, because it is the sabbath of the Lord: today it shall not be found in the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:13; Exodus 20:9–11; Ezekiel 46:1"> Gather it six days: but on the seventh day is the sabbath of the Lord, therefore it shall not be found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 20:4"> And the seventh day came: and some of the people going forth to gather, found none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 78:10; Psalms 106:13; Numbers 14:11"> And the Lord said to Moses: How long will you refuse to keep my commandments, and my law?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 31:13; Isaiah 58:13–14; Ezekiel 20:12"> See that the Lord hath given you the sabbath, and for this reason on the sixth day he giveth you a double provision: let each man stay at home, and let none go forth out of his place the seventh day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:12–14; Leviticus 23:3; Hebrews 4:9"> And the people kept the sabbath on the seventh day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:3; Numbers 11:6–9; Exodus 16:15"> And the house of Israel called the name thereof Manna: and it was like coriander seed white, and the taste thereof like to flour with honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 105:5; Luke 22:19; Psalms 111:4–5"> And Moses said: This is the word, which the Lord hath commanded: Fill a gomor of it, and let it be kept unto generations to come hereafter, that they may know the bread, wherewith I fed you in the wilderness, when you were brought forth out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 9:4"> And Moses said to Aaron: Take a vessel, and put manna into it, as much as a gomor can hold: and lay it up before the Lord to keep unto your generations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 27:21; Exodus 25:21; Exodus 25:16"> As the Lord commanded Moses. And Aaron put it in the tabernacle to be kept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:20–21; Joshua 5:12; Deuteronomy 8:2–3"> And the children of Israel ate manna forty years, till they came to a habitable land: with this meat were they fed, until they reached the borders of the land of Chanaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 16:16; Exodus 16:32–33"> Now a gomor is the tenth part of an ephi.</span>
