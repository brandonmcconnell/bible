<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 36:8; Exodus 26:1"> With embroidered work: and he cut thin plates of gold, and drew them small into threads, that they might be twisted with the woof of the aforesaid colours,</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 1:13; Matthew 28:20; Isaiah 11:5"> And a girdle of the same colours, as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 28:9; Ezekiel 28:13; Job 28:16"> He prepared also two onyx stones, fast set and closed in gold, and graven by the art of a lapidary, with the names of the children of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 4:7; Exodus 28:12; Exodus 28:29"> And he set them in the sides of the ephod for a memorial of the children of Israel, as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Leviticus 8:8–9; Isaiah 59:17"> He made also a rational with embroidered work, according to the work of the ephod, of gold, violet, purple, and scarlet twice dyed, and fine twisted linen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Foursquare, double, of the measure of a span.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 28:21; Exodus 28:16–17; Revelation 21:19–21"> And he set four rows of precious stones in it. In the first row was a sardius, a topaz, and emerald.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 28:18; Ezekiel 28:13"> In the second, a carbuncle, a sapphire, and a jasper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> In the third, a ligurius, an agate, and an amethyst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> In the fourth, a chrysolite, an onyx, and a beryl, set and enclosed in gold by their rows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 21:12"> And the twelve stones were engraved with the names of the twelve tribes of Israel, each one with its several name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 10:28; John 17:12; 1 Peter 1:5"> They made also in the rational little chains linked one to another of the purest gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 25:12"> And two hooks, and as many rings of gold. And they set the rings on either side of the rational,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> On which rings the two golden chains should hang, which they put into the hooks that stood out in the corners of the ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 28:14; Song of Solomon 1:10"> These both before and behind so answered one another, that the ephod and the rational were bound together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Being fastened to the girdle and strongly coupled with rings, which a violet fillet joined, lest they should flag loose, and be moved one from the other, as the Lord commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 26:3"> They made also the tunick of the ephod all of violet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 16:24; 1 Corinthians 1:25; 1 Corinthians 1:27"> And a hole for the head in the upper part at the middle, and a woven border round about the hole:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 28:31–35"> And beneath at the feet pomegranates of violet, purple, scarlet, and fine twisted linen:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And little bells of the purest gold, which they put between the pomegranates at the bottom of the tunick round about:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 28:33; Galatians 5:22"> To wit, a bell of gold, and a pomegranate, wherewith the high priest went adorned, when he discharged his ministry, as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 28:33–34; Song of Solomon 4:13; Psalms 89:15"> They made also fine linen tunicks with woven work for Aaron and his sons:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 28:34; Song of Solomon 4:13; Song of Solomon 6:7"> And mitres with their little crowns of fine linen:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:18; 1 Peter 1:13; Isaiah 61:10"> And linen breeches of fine linen:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Ezekiel 44:18; Exodus 28:39"> And a girdle of fine twisted linen, violet, purple, and scarlet twice dyed, of embroidery work, as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 28:39"> They made also the plate of sacred veneration of the purest gold, and they wrote on it with the engraving of a lapidary, The Holy of the Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; Hebrews 7:26; Hebrews 1:3"> And they fastened it to the mitre with a violet fillet, as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> So all the work of the tabernacle and of the roof of the testimony was finished: and the children of Israel did all things which the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 39:42–43; Matthew 28:20; Exodus 25:40"> And they offered the tabernacle and the roof and the whole furniture, the rings, the boards, the bars, the pillars, and their sockets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 35:11–19; Exodus 31:7–11; Exodus 36:1–38"> The cover of rams’ skins dyed red, and the other cover of violet skins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> The veil, the ark, the bars, the propitiatory,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Hebrews 9:5; Exodus 25:17; Hebrews 9:8"> The table, with the vessels thereof, and the loaves of proposition:</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 25:30; 1 Kings 7:48"> The candlestick, the lamps, and the furniture of them with the oil:</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Philippians 2:15; Exodus 27:21; Matthew 5:14–16"> The altar of gold, and the ointment, and the incense of spices:</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 30:7; Exodus 37:29; Exodus 30:3"> And the hanging in the entry of the tabernacle:</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 8:64; Exodus 38:30"> The altar of brass, the grate, the bars, and all the vessels thereof: the laver with the foot thereof: the hangings of the court, and the pillars with their sockets:</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Exodus 27:9–16"> The hanging in the entry of the court, and the little cords, and the pins thereof. Nothing was wanting of the vessels, that were commanded to be made for the ministry of the tabernacle, and for the roof of the covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 28:2; Exodus 31:10; Exodus 39:1"> The vestments also, which the priests, to wit, Aaron and his sons, used in the sanctuary,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Exodus 35:10; Exodus 23:21–22; 2 Timothy 2:15"> The children of Israel offered as the Lord had commanded.</span>
