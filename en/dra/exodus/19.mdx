<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 16:1; Exodus 12:6; Leviticus 23:16–18"> In the third month of the departure of Israel out of the land of Egypt, on this day they came into the wilderness of Sinai:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; Exodus 17:1; Exodus 3:12"> For departing out of Raphidim, and coming to the desert of Sinai, they camped in the same place, and there Israel pitched their tents over against the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 3:4; Acts 7:38; Exodus 20:21"> And Moses went up to God: and the Lord called unto him from the mountain, and said: Thus shalt thou say to the house of Jacob, and tell the children of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:11–12; Isaiah 63:9; Isaiah 40:31"> You have seen what I have done to the Egyptians, how I have carried you upon the wings of eagles, and have taken you to myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:2; Deuteronomy 7:6; Deuteronomy 26:18"> If therefore you will hear my voice, and keep my covenant, you shall be my peculiar possession above all people: for all the earth is mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; 1 Peter 2:9; 1 Peter 2:5"> And you shall be to me a priestly kingdom, and a holy nation. Those are the words thou shalt speak to the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 4:29–30; Exodus 3:16; 1 Corinthians 15:1"> Moses came, and calling together the elders of the people, he declared all the words which the Lord had commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 24:7; Exodus 24:3; Joshua 24:24"> And all the people answered together: All that the Lord hath spoken, we will do. And when Moses had related the people’s words to the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:36; Exodus 19:16; Exodus 24:15–16"> The Lord said to him: Lo, now will I come to thee in the darkness of a cloud, that the people may hear me speaking to thee, and may believe thee for ever. And Moses told the words of the people to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; Genesis 35:2; Leviticus 11:44–45"> And he said to him: Go to the people, and sanctify them today, and tomorrow, and let them wash their garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 19:16; Exodus 34:5; Deuteronomy 33:2"> And let them be ready against the third day: for on the third day the Lord will come down in the sight of all the people upon mount Sinai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 12:20–21; Joshua 3:4; Exodus 19:21"> And thou shalt appoint certain limits to the people round about, and thou shalt say to them: Take heed you go not up into the mount, and that ye touch not the borders thereof: every one that toucheth the mount dying he shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:16; Hebrews 12:20; Exodus 19:16"> No hands shall touch him, but he shall be stoned to death, or shall be shot through with arrows: whether it be beast, or man, he shall not live. When the trumpet shall begin to sound, then let them go up into the mount.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 19:10"> And Moses came down from the mount to the people, and sanctified them. And when they had washed their garments,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:5; 1 Samuel 21:4–5; Exodus 19:11"> He said to them: Be ready against the third day, and come not near your wives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 12:18–19; Psalms 18:11–14; Hebrews 12:21"> And now the third day was come, and the morning appeared: and behold thunders began to be heard, and lightning to flash, and a very thick cloud to cover the mount, and the noise of the trumpet sounded exceeding loud, and the people that was in the camp, feared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:10; Deuteronomy 5:5"> And when Moses had brought them forth to meet God from the place of the camp, they stood at the bottom of the mount.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 5:5; Exodus 3:2; Psalms 144:5"> And all mount Sinai was on a smoke: because the Lord was come down upon it in fire, and the smoke arose from it as out of a furnace: and all the mount was terrible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 81:7; Exodus 19:16; Nehemiah 9:13"> And the sound of the trumpet grew by degrees louder and louder, and was drawn out to a greater length: Moses spoke, and God answered him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:13; Exodus 34:2; Exodus 19:11"> And the Lord came down upon mount Sinai, in the very top of the mount, and he called Moses unto the top thereof. And when he was gone up thither,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:19; Exodus 3:5; Hebrews 12:28–29"> He said unto him: Go down, and charge the people: lest they should have a mind to pass the limits to see the Lord, and a very great multitude of them should perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 24:5; Acts 5:10; 1 Chronicles 13:9–11"> The priests also that come to the Lord, let them be sanctified, lest he strike them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 19:12; Joshua 3:4–5"> And Moses said to the Lord: The people cannot come up to mount Sinai: for thou did charge, and command, saying: Set limits about the mount, and sanctify it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 4:16; Hebrews 12:18–25; Hebrews 12:29"> And the Lord said to him: Go, get thee down: and thou shalt come up, thou and Aaron with thee: but let not the priests and the people pass the limits, nor come up to the Lord, lest he kill them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And Moses went down to the people and told them all.</span>
