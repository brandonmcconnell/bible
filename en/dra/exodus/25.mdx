<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Nehemiah 11:2; Ezra 2:68"> Speak to the children of Israel, that they bring firstfruits to me: of every man that offereth of his own accord, you shall take them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:9; Job 28:2"> And these are the things you must take: gold, and silver, and brass,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 19:8; Genesis 41:42; Ezekiel 16:10"> Violet and purple, and scarlet twice dyed, and fine linen, and goats’ hair,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 26:37–1; Exodus 26:14–15; Exodus 36:20"> And rams’ skins dyed red, and violet skins, and setim wood:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 27:20; Exodus 25:37; Exodus 40:24–25"> Oil to make lights: spices for ointment, and for sweetsmelling incense:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Exodus 28:6–30"> Onyx stones, and precious stones to adorn the ephod and the rational.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Kings 6:13; Revelation 21:3"> And they shall make me a sanctuary, and I will dwell in the midst of them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Hebrews 8:5; Acts 7:44"> According to all the likeness of the tabernacle which I will shew thee, and of all the vessels for the service thereof: and thus you shall make it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 9:4; Exodus 37:1–3; Deuteronomy 10:1–3"> Frame an ark of setim wood, the length whereof shall be of two cubits and a half: the breadth, a cubit and a half: the height, likewise, a cubit and a half.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 25:24; Exodus 30:3; 2 Chronicles 3:4"> And thou shalt overlay it with the purest gold within and without: and over it thou shalt make a golden crown round about:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 27:7; Exodus 25:26; Exodus 25:15"> And four golden rings, which thou shall put at the four corners of the ark: let two rings be on the one side, and two on the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:15; Numbers 4:11; Exodus 27:6"> Thou shalt make bars also of setim wood, and shalt overlay them with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And thou shalt put them in through the rings that are in the sides of the ark, that it may be carried on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 8:8; 2 Chronicles 5:9"> And they shall be always in the rings, neither shall they at any time be drawn out of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:26; 1 Kings 8:9; Hebrews 9:4"> And thou shalt put in the ark the testimony which I will give thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 9:5; Exodus 37:6; Hebrews 4:16"> Thou shalt make also a propitiatory of the purest gold: the length thereof shall be two cubits and a half, and the breadth a cubit and a half.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:20; Hebrews 9:5; 1 Kings 8:6–7"> Thou shalt make also two cherubims of beaten gold, on the two sides of the oracle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Let one cherub be on the one side, and the other on the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:18; 1 Kings 8:7; Hebrews 9:5"> Let them cover both sides of the propitiatory, spreading their wings, and covering the oracle, and let them look one towards the other, their faces being turned towards the propitiatory wherewith the ark is to be covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 26:34; Romans 10:4; Exodus 25:16–17"> In which thou shalt put the testimony that I will give thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 29:42–43; Numbers 7:89; Isaiah 37:16"> Thence will I give orders, and will speak to thee over the propitiatory, and from the midst of the two cherubims, which shall be upon the ark of the testimony, all things which I will command the children of Israel by thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 9:2; Exodus 37:10–16; 1 Kings 7:48"> Thou shalt make a table also of setim wood, of two cubits in length, and a cubit in breadth, and a cubit and half in height.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 25:11; 1 Kings 6:20–22"> And thou shalt overlay it with the purest gold: and thou shalt make to it a golden ledge round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 37:2; Exodus 30:3"> And to the ledge itself a polished crown, four inches high: and over the same another little golden crown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Thou shalt prepare also four golden rings, and shalt put them in the four corners of the same table over each foot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 25:14; Exodus 25:28"> Under the crown shall the golden rings be, that the bars may be put through them, and the table may be carried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 25:14; Acts 9:15; Numbers 10:17"> The bars also themselves thou shalt make of setim wood, and shalt overlay them with gold to bear up the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 4:7; Exodus 37:16; Revelation 3:20"> Thou shalt prepare also dishes, and bowls, censers, and cups, wherein the libations are to be offered of the purest gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 24:5–9; Matthew 12:4; Exodus 39:36"> And thou shalt set upon the table loaves of proposition in my sight always.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 7:49; Zechariah 4:2; Exodus 37:17–24"> Thou shalt make also a candlestick of beaten work of the finest gold, the shaft thereof, and the branches, the cups, and the bowls, and the lilies going forth from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Six branches shall come out of the sides, three out of the one side, and three out of the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 17:4–8; Jeremiah 1:11–12; Zechariah 4:3"> Three cups as it were nuts to every branch, and a bowl withal, and a lily; and three cups, likewise of the fashion of nuts in the other branch, and a bowl withal, and a lily. Such shall be the work of the six branches, that are to come out from the shaft:</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And in the candlestick itself shall be four cups in the manner of a nut, and at every one, bowls and lilies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Bowls under two branches in three places, which together make six coming forth out of one shaft.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 25:18; 1 Kings 10:16–17; 2 Chronicles 9:15"> And both the bowls and the branches shall be of the same beaten work of the purest gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 8:2; Revelation 1:20–1; Leviticus 24:2–4"> Thou shalt make also seven lamps, and shalt set them upon the candlestick, to give light over against.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Kings 12:13; Jeremiah 52:18; 2 Kings 25:14"> The snuffers also and where the snuffings shall be put out, shall be made of the purest gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> The whole weight of the candlestick with all the furniture thereof shall be a talent of the purest gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Hebrews 8:5; Exodus 26:30; Acts 7:44"> Look and make it according to the pattern, that was shewn thee in the mount.</span>
