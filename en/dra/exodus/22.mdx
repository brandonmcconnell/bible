<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 19:8; 2 Samuel 12:6; Proverbs 6:31"> If any man steal an ox or a sheep, and kill or sell it: he shall restore five oxen for one ox, and four sheep for one sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 24:43; Matthew 6:19–20; Numbers 35:27"> If a thief be found breaking open a house or undermining it, and be wounded so as to die: he that slew him shall not be guilty of blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 21:2; Isaiah 50:1; Matthew 18:25"> But if he did this when the sun is risen, he hath committed murder, and he shall die. If he have not wherewith to make restitution for the theft, he shall be sold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 22:7; Exodus 22:1; Exodus 22:9"> If that which he stole be found with him, alive, either ox, or ass, or sheep: he shall restore double.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 22:3; Exodus 22:12; Job 20:18"> If any man hurt a field or a vineyard, and put in his beast to feed upon that which is other men’s: he shall restore the best of whatsoever he hath in his own field, or in his vineyard, according to the estimation of the damage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 22:9; Exodus 21:33–34; 2 Samuel 14:30–31"> If a fire breaking out light upon thorns, and catch stacks of corn, or corn standing in the fields, he that kindled the fire shall make good the loss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 22:4; Proverbs 6:30–31; Jeremiah 2:26"> If a man deliver money, or any vessel unto his friend to keep, and they be stolen away from him that received them: if the thief be found he shall restore double:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 22:28; Deuteronomy 16:18; Exodus 21:6"> If the thief be not known, the master of the house shall be brought to the gods, and shall swear that he did not lay his hand upon his neighbour’s goods,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:1; Exodus 22:4; Numbers 5:6–7"> To do any fraud, either in ox, or in ass, or sheep, or raiment, or any thing that may bring damage: the cause of both parties shall come to the gods: and if they give judgment, he shall restore double to his neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 12:48; 2 Timothy 1:12; Luke 16:11"> If a man deliver ass, ox, sheep, or any beast, to his neighbour’s custody, and it die, or be hurt, or be taken by enemies, and no man saw it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 6:16; Leviticus 5:1; 1 Kings 2:42–43"> There shall be an oath between them, that he did not put forth his hand to his neighbour’s goods: and the owner shall accept of the oath; and he shall not be compelled to make restitution.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 31:39; Exodus 22:7"> But if it were taken away by stealth, he shall make the loss good to the owner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Nahum 2:12; Micah 5:8; Ezekiel 4:14"> If it were eaten by a beast, let him bring to him that which was slain, and he shall not make restitution.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:19–20; Exodus 21:34; Deuteronomy 15:2"> If a man borrow of his neighbour any of these things, and it be hurt or die, the owner not being present, he shall be obliged to make restitution.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 8:10"> But if the owner be present, he shall not make restitution, especially if it were hired and came for the hire of his work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:28–29; Genesis 34:2–4"> If a man seduce a virgin not yet espoused, and lie with her: he shall endow her, and have her to wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 34:12; 1 Samuel 18:25; Deuteronomy 22:29"> If the maid’s father will not give her to him, he shall give money according to the dowry, which virgins are wont to receive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 20:27; Deuteronomy 18:10–11; 1 Samuel 28:3"> Wizards thou shalt not suffer to live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 18:23; Deuteronomy 27:21; Leviticus 20:15–16"> Whosoever copulateth with a beast shall be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:2–5; Numbers 25:7–8; Deuteronomy 18:20"> He that sacrificeth to gods, shall be put to death, save only to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 23:9; Deuteronomy 10:19; Leviticus 19:33"> Thou shalt not molest a stranger, nor afflict him: for yourselves also were strangers in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:17; Isaiah 1:17; Isaiah 1:23"> You shall not hurt a widow or an orphan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 18:6; Psalms 10:17–18; Luke 18:7"> If you hurt them they will cry out to me, and I will hear their cry:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 109:9; Jeremiah 15:8; Psalms 76:7"> And my rage shall be enkindled, and I will strike you with the sword, and your wives shall be widows, and your children fatherless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 15:5; Leviticus 25:35–37; Deuteronomy 23:19–20"> If thou lend money to any of my people that is poor, that dwelleth with thee, thou shalt not be hard upon them as an extortioner, nor oppress them with usuries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 20:16; Amos 2:8; Deuteronomy 24:6"> If thou take of thy neighbour a garment in pledge, thou shalt give it him again before sunset.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 34:6; 2 Chronicles 30:9; Psalms 136:10–11"> For that same is the only thing wherewith he is covered, the clothing of his body, neither hath he any other to sleep in: if he cry to me, I will hear him, because I am compassionate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 23:5; Ecclesiastes 10:20; Exodus 22:8–9"> Thou shalt not speak ill of the gods, and the prince of thy people thou shalt not curse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 23:16; Exodus 13:2; James 1:18"> Thou shalt not delay to pay thy tithes and thy firstfruits: thou shalt give the firstborn of thy sons to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 22:27; Deuteronomy 15:19; Exodus 13:12"> Thou shalt do the same with the firstborn of thy oxen also and sheep: seven days let it be with its dam, the eighth day thou shalt give it to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:14; Leviticus 19:2; Deuteronomy 14:21"> You shall be holy men to me: the flesh that beasts have tasted of before, you shall not eat, but shall cast it to the dogs.</span>
