<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 25:10–20; Exodus 26:33; Numbers 10:33–36"> And Beseleel made also the ark of setim wood: it was two cubits and a half in length, and a cubit and a half in breadth, and the height was of one cubit and a half: and he overlaid it with the purest gold within and without.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 30:3"> And he made to it a crown of gold round about,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Casting four rings of gold at the four corners thereof: two rings in one side, and two in the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Peter 1:7; Numbers 4:14–15"> And he made bars of setim wood, which he overlaid with gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; 2 Samuel 6:3–7; Numbers 1:50"> And he put them into the rings that were at the sides of the ark to carry it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 9:5; Titus 2:14; Romans 3:25"> He made also the propitiatory, that is, the oracle, of the purest gold, two cubits and a half in length, and a cubit and a half in breadth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:2; Psalms 104:4; Psalms 80:1"> Two cherubims also of beaten gold, which he set on the two sides of the propitiatory:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> One cherub in the top of one side, and the other cherub in the top of the other side: two cherubims at the two ends of the propitiatory,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 1:51; Hebrews 1:14; Genesis 28:12"> Spreading their wings, and covering the propitiatory, and looking one towards the other, and towards it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 1:14; Ezekiel 40:39–42; Malachi 1:12"> He made also the table of setim wood, in length two cubits, and in breadth one cubit, and in height it was a cubit and a half.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And he overlaid it with the finest gold, and he made to it a golden ledge round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And to the ledge itself he made a polished crown of gold, of four fingers’ breadth, and upon the same another golden crown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And he cast four rings of gold, which he put in the four corners at each foot of the table,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Over against the crown: and he put the bars into them, that the table might be carried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the bars also themselves he made of setim wood, and overlaid them with gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 25:29; 1 Kings 7:50; 2 Kings 12:13"> And the vessels for the divers uses of the table, dishes, bowls, and cups, and censers of pure gold, wherein the libations are to be offered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–39; Hebrews 9:2; 2 Chronicles 13:11"> He made also the candlestick of beaten work of the finest gold. From the shaft whereof its branches, its cups, and bowls, and lilies came out:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Six on the two sides: three branches on one side, and three on the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Three cups in manner of a nut on each branch, and bowls withal and lilies; and three cups of the fashion of a nut in another branch, and bowls withal and lilies. The work of the six branches that went out from the shaft of the candlestick was equal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:5; Jeremiah 1:11; Exodus 25:33"> And in the shaft itself were four cups after the manner of a nut, and bowls withal at every one, and lilies:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 25:35"> And bowls under two branches in three places, which together make six branches going out from one shaft.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:5; Exodus 25:31; Psalms 51:17"> So both the bowls, and the branches were of the same, all beaten work of the purest gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 1:12; Exodus 25:37; Revelation 1:20–1"> He made also the seven lamps with their snuffers, and the vessels where the snuffings were to be put out, of the purest gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> The candlestick with all the vessels thereof weighed a talent of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 30:1–5; Hebrews 7:25; Revelation 8:3–4"> He made also the altar of incense of setim wood, being a cubit on every side foursquare, and in height two cubits: from the corners of which went out horns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And he overlaid it with the purest gold, with its grate and the sides, and the horns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And he made to it a crown of gold round about, and two golden rings under the crown at each side, that the bars might be put into them, and the altar be carried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And the bars themselves he made also of setim wood, and overlaid them with plates of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 141:2; Hebrews 7:25; 1 John 2:20"> He compounded also the oil for the ointment of sanctification, and incense of the purest spices, according to the work of a perfumer.</span>
