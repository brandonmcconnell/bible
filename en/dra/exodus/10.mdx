<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 4:21; Exodus 7:13–14; Exodus 3:20"> And the Lord said to Moses: Go in to Pharao; for I have hardened his heart, and the heart of his servants: that I may work these my signs in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joel 1:3; Deuteronomy 4:9; Deuteronomy 6:20–22"> And thou mayest tell in the ears of thy sons, and of they grandsons, how often I have plagued the Egyptians, and wrought my signs amongst them: and you may know that I am the Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 5:6; 1 Kings 21:29; James 4:10"> Therefore Moses and Aaron went in to Pharao, and said to him: Thus saith the Lord God of the Hebrews: How long refusest thou to submit to me? let my people go, to sacrifice to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 9:3; Joel 2:25; Exodus 11:4–5"> But if thou resist, and wilt not let them go, behold I will bring in tomorrow the locust into thy coasts:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joel 1:4; Exodus 9:32; Joel 2:25"> To cover the face of the earth that nothing thereof may appear, but that which the hail hath left may be eaten: for they shall feed upon all the trees that spring in the fields.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 8:21; Exodus 8:3; Exodus 11:6"> And they shall fill thy houses, and the houses of thy servants, and of all the Egyptians: such a number as thy fathers have not seen, nor thy grandfathers, from the time they were first upon the earth, until this present day. And he turned himself away, and went forth from Pharao.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:21; Exodus 23:33; Joshua 23:13"> And Pharao’s servants said to him: How long shall we endure this scandal? let the men go to sacrifice to the Lord their God. Dost thou not see that Egypt is undone?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 10:24; Exodus 12:31; Exodus 8:8"> And they called back Moses and Aaron to Pharao: and he said to them: Go, sacrifice to the Lord your God: who are they that shall go?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 5:1; 1 Corinthians 5:7–8; Deuteronomy 31:12–13"> Moses said: We will go with our young and old, with our sons and daughters, with our sheep and herds: for it is the solemnity of the Lord our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 13:21; 2 Chronicles 32:15; Exodus 12:30–31"> And Pharao answered: So be the Lord with you, as I shall let you and your children go: who can doubt but that you intend some great evil?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 10:28; Psalms 119:69; Exodus 5:4"> It shall not be so: but go ye men only, and sacrifice to the Lord: for this yourselves also desired. And immediately they were cast out from Pharao’s presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 7:19; Exodus 10:4–5"> And the Lord said to Moses: Stretch forth thy hand upon the land of Egypt unto the locust, that it may come upon it, and devour every herb that is left after the hail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jonah 1:4; Psalms 78:26; Psalms 148:8"> And Moses stretched forth his rod upon the land of Egypt: and the Lord brought a burning wind all that day, and night: and when it was morning, the burning wind raised the locusts:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 78:46; Joel 1:2–4; Psalms 105:34–35"> And they came up over the whole land of Egypt: and rested in all the coasts of the Egyptians innumerable, the like as had not been before that time, nor shall be hereafter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 10:5; Psalms 78:46; Joel 1:6–7"> And they covered the whole face of the earth, wasting all things. And the grass of the earth was devoured, and what fruits soever were on the trees, which the hail had left: and there remained not any thing that was green on the trees, or in the herbs of the earth in all Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 9:27; 2 Samuel 19:20; 1 Samuel 15:30"> Wherefore Pharao in haste called Moses and Aaron, and said to them: I have sinned against the Lord your God, and against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 8:8; Exodus 9:28; 1 Kings 13:6"> But now forgive me my sin this time also, and pray to the Lord your God, that he take away from me this death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 6:28; Exodus 8:28–30; Exodus 8:9"> And Moses going forth from the presence of Pharao, prayed to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joel 2:20; Exodus 13:18; Exodus 15:4"> And he made a very strong wind to blow from the west, and it took the locusts and cast them into the Red Sea: there remained not so much as one in all the coasts of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 4:21; Exodus 11:10; Deuteronomy 2:30"> And the Lord hardened Pharao’s heart, neither did he let the children of Israel go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 16:10–11; Jude 1:6; Luke 23:44"> And the Lord said to Moses: Stretch out they hand towards heaven: and may there be darkness upon the land of Egypt, so thick that it may be felt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 105:28; Revelation 16:10; Exodus 20:21"> And Moses stretch forth his hand towards heaven: and there came horrible darkness in all the land of Egypt for three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 8:22; Isaiah 42:16; 1 Peter 2:9"> No man saw his brother, nor moved himself out of the place where he was: but wheresoever the children of Israel dwelt there was light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 10:8–10; Exodus 8:28; Genesis 34:23"> And Pharao called Moses and Aaron, and said to them: Go sacrifice to the Lord: let your sheep only, and herds remain; let your children go with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 9:22; Leviticus 16:9; Exodus 29:1–46"> Moses said: Thou shalt give us also sacrifices and burnt offerings, to the Lord our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 23:18; Acts 2:44–45; Proverbs 3:9"> All the flocks shall go with us: there shall not a hoof remain of them: for they are necessary for the service of the Lord our God: especially as we know not what must be offered, till we come to the very place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 10:20; Exodus 4:21; Exodus 14:8"> And the Lord hardened Pharao’s heart, and he would not let them go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:16; 2 Chronicles 16:10; Amos 7:13"> And Pharao said to Moses: Get thee from me, and beware thou see not my face any more: in what day soever thou shalt come in my sight, thou shalt die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 11:27; Exodus 12:30–31; Exodus 11:4–8"> Moses answered: So shall it be as thou hast spoken, I will not see thy face any more.</span>
