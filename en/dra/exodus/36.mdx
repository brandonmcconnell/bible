<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 28:3; Exodus 35:30–35; Exodus 31:1–6"> Beseleel, therefore, and Ooliab, and every wise man, to whom the Lord gave wisdom and understanding, to know how to work artificially, made the things that are necessary for the uses of the sanctuary, and which the Lord commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 4:17; Hebrews 5:4; Exodus 31:6"> And when Moses had called them, and every skillful man, to whom the Lord had given wisdom, and such as of their own accord had offered themselves to the making of the work,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:29; Exodus 35:27; Exodus 35:5–21"> He delivered all the offerings of the children of Israel unto them. And while they were earnest about the work, the people daily in the morning offered their vows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:10; Luke 12:42; Matthew 24:45"> Whereupon the workmen being constrained to come,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:6–10; 2 Corinthians 8:2–3; 2 Chronicles 24:14"> Said to Moses: The people offereth more than is necessary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Moses therefore commanded proclamation to be made by the crier’s voice: Let neither man nor woman offer any more for the work of the sanctuary. And so they ceased from offering gifts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:10"> Because the things that were offered did suffice, and were too much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 6:23; Exodus 26:1–37; Ezekiel 1:5–28"> And all the men that were wise of heart, to accomplish the work of the tabernacle, made ten curtains of twisted fine linen, and violet, and purple, and scarlet twice dyed, with varied work, and the art of embroidering:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The length of one curtain was twenty-eight cubits, and the breadth four: all the curtains were of the same size.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:27; Exodus 26:3; Psalms 122:3"> And he joined five curtains, one to another, and the other five he coupled one to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 26:4"> He made also loops of violet in the edge of the curtain on both sides, and in the edge of the other curtain in like manner,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 26:5; Exodus 26:10"> That the loops might meet on against another, and might be joined each with the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4–5; Ephesians 2:20–22; 1 Corinthians 12:20"> Whereupon also he cast fifty rings of gold, that might catch the loops of the curtains, and they might be made one tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 26:7–13"> He made also eleven curtains of goats’ hair, to cover the roof of the tabernacle:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> One curtain was thirty cubits long and four cubits broad: all the curtains were of one measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Five of which he joined apart, and the other six apart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And he made fifty loops in the edge of one curtain, and fifty in the edge of another curtain, that they might be joined one to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And fifty buckles of brass wherewith the roof might be knit together, that of all the curtains there might be made one covering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 26:14"> He made also a cover for the tabernacle of rams’ skins dyed red: and another cover over that of violet skins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 25:5; Numbers 25:1; Exodus 40:18–19"> He made also the boards of the tabernacle of setim wood standing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The length of one board was ten cubits: and the breadth was one cubit and a half.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> There were two mortises throughout every board, that one might be joined to the other. And in this manner he made for all the boards of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Of which twenty were at the south side southward,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> With forty sockets of silver, two sockets were put under one board on the two sides of the corners, where the mortises of the sides end in the corners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> At that side also of the tabernacle, that looketh toward the north, he made twenty boards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> With forty sockets of silver, two sockets for every board.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 26:22; Exodus 26:27"> But against the west, to wit, at that side of the tabernacle, which looketh to the sea, he made six boards,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And two others at each corner of the tabernacle behind:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 4:32; Ephesians 3:18–19; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> Which were also joined from beneath unto the top, and went together into one joint. Thus he did on both sides at the corners:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 26:25"> So there were in all eight boards and they had sixteen sockets of silver, to wit, two sockets under every board.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 26:26–29; Exodus 25:28; Exodus 30:5"> He made also bars of setim wood, five to hold together the boards of one side of the tabernacle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 26:26"> And five others to join together the boards of the other side: and besides these, five other bars at the west side of the tabernacle towards the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> He made also another bar, that might come by the midst of the boards from corner to corner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And the board works themselves he overlaid with gold, casting for them sockets of silver. And their rings he made of gold, through which the bars might be drawn: and he covered the bars themselves with plates of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; Hebrews 10:20; Exodus 40:21"> He made also a veil of violet, and purple, scarlet, and fine twisted linen, varied and distinguished with embroidery:</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:18"> And four pillars of setim wood, which with their heads be overlaid with gold, casting for them sockets of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 26:36–37; Exodus 40:28"> He made also a hanging in the entry of the tabernacle of violet, purple, scarlet, and fine twisted linen, with the work of an embroiderer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 27:10"> And five pillars with their heads, which he covered with gold, and their sockets he cast of brass.</span>
