<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 11:16; Exodus 6:23; Leviticus 10:1–2"> And he said to Moses: Come up to the Lord, thou, and Aaron, Nadab, and Abiu, and seventy of the ancients of Israel, and you shall adore afar off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 24:15; Exodus 24:13; Hebrews 10:21–22"> And Moses alone shall come up to the Lord, but they shall not come nigh; neither shall the people come up with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 19:8; Exodus 24:7; Joshua 24:22"> So Moses came and told the people all the words of the Lord, and all the judgments: and all the people answered with one voice: We will do all the words of the Lord, which he hath spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 28:18; Deuteronomy 31:9; Joshua 4:8–9"> And Moses wrote all the words of the Lord: and rising in the morning he built an altar at the foot of the mount, and twelve titles according to the twelve tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 18:12; Leviticus 3:1–17; Exodus 19:22"> And he sent young men of the children of Israel, and they offered holocausts, and sacrificed pacific victims of calves to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 1:19; Exodus 24:8; Hebrews 9:18"> Then Moses took half of the blood, and put it into bowls: and the rest he poured upon the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:11–13; 1 Thessalonians 5:27; Exodus 24:3–4"> And taking the book of the covenant, he read it in the hearing of the people: and they said: All things that the Lord hath spoken we will do, we will be obedient.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:4–5; Exodus 24:6; Ezekiel 36:25"> And he took the blood and sprinkled it upon the people, and he said: This is the blood of the covenant which the Lord hath made with you concerning all these words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 24:1"> Then Moses and Aaron, Nadab and Abiu, and seventy of the ancients of Israel went up:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 1:18; Ezekiel 10:1; Genesis 32:30"> And they saw the God of Israel: and under his feet as it were a work of sapphire stone, and as the heaven, when clear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 15:23–24; Exodus 18:12; Genesis 31:54"> Neither did he lay his hand upon those of the children of Israel, that retired afar off, and they saw God, and they did eat and drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:22; Exodus 31:18; Exodus 32:15–16"> And the Lord said to Moses: Come up to me into the mount, and be there: and I will give thee tables of stone, and the law, and the commandments which I have written: that thou mayst teach them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 33:11; Numbers 11:28; Exodus 3:1"> Moses rose up, and his minister Josue: and Moses going up into the mount of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 17:10; Exodus 17:12; Genesis 22:5"> Said to the ancients: Wait ye here till we return to you. You have Aaron and Hur with you: if any question shall arise, you shall refer it to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 19:9; Matthew 17:5; Exodus 19:16"> And when Moses was gone up, a cloud covered the mount.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 16:10; Numbers 16:42; Ezekiel 1:28"> And the glory of the Lord dwelt upon Sinai, covering it with a cloud six days: and the seventh day he called him out of the midst of the cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Deuteronomy 4:24; Exodus 3:2"> And the sight of the glory of the Lord was like a burning fire upon the top of the mount, in the eyes of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:9; Exodus 34:28; Deuteronomy 10:10"> And Moses, entering into the midst of the cloud, went up into the mountain: and he was there forty days, and forty nights.</span>
