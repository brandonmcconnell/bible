<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:22; Deuteronomy 5:4; Deuteronomy 4:36"> And the Lord spoke all these words:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:6; Hosea 13:4; Leviticus 26:13"> I am the Lord thy God, who brought thee out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:7; Matthew 4:10; Psalms 81:9"> Thou shalt not have strange gods before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:8; Leviticus 26:1; Deuteronomy 4:15–19"> Thou shalt not make to thyself a graven thing, nor the likeness of any thing that is in heaven above, or in the earth beneath, nor of those things that are in the waters under the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:9; Numbers 14:18; Deuteronomy 4:24"> Thou shalt not adore them, nor serve them: I am the Lord thy God, mighty, jealous, visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children, unto the third and fourth generation of them that hate me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:9; John 14:21; 1 John 5:3"> And shewing mercy unto thousands to them that love me, and keep my commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:11; Leviticus 19:12; James 5:12"> Thou shalt not take the name of the Lord thy God in vain: for the Lord will not hold him guiltless that shall take the name of the Lord his God in vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 26:2; Leviticus 19:3; Exodus 31:13–16"> Remember that thou keep holy the sabbath day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 13:14; Exodus 34:21; Exodus 23:12"> Six days shalt thou labour, and shalt do all thy works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:14–15; Exodus 34:21; Numbers 15:32–36"> But on the seventh day is the sabbath of the Lord thy God: thou shalt do no work on it, thou nor thy son, nor thy daughter, nor thy manservant, nor thy maidservant, nor thy beast, nor the stranger that is within thy gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 1:1–2; Genesis 2:2–3; Exodus 31:17"> For in six days the Lord made heaven and earth, and the sea, and all things that are in them, and rested on the seventh day: therefore the Lord blessed the seventh day, and sanctified it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1–3; Colossians 3:20; Deuteronomy 5:16"> Honour thy father and thy mother, that thou mayest be longlived upon the land which the Lord thy God will give thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Deuteronomy 5:17; Genesis 9:5–6"> Thou shalt not kill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:4; Deuteronomy 5:18; Romans 7:2–3"> Thou shalt not commit adultery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Ephesians 4:28; Leviticus 19:11"> Thou shalt not steal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 19:5; Matthew 19:18; Psalms 15:3"> Thou shalt not bear false witness against thy neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Luke 12:15; Hebrews 13:5"> Thou shalt not covet thy neighbour’s house: neither shalt thou desire his wife, nor his servant, nor his handmaid, nor his ox, nor his ass, nor any thing that is his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 19:16–18; Jeremiah 23:23; Psalms 139:7–8"> And all the people saw the voices and the flames, and the sound of the trumpet, and the mount smoking: and being terrified and struck with fear, they stood afar off,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:16; Acts 7:38; Galatians 3:19"> Saying to Moses: Speak thou to us, and we will hear: let not the Lord speak to us, lest we die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:3; Proverbs 3:7; 1 Samuel 12:20"> And Moses said to the people: Fear not: for God is come to prove you, and that the dread of him might be in you, and you should not sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 8:12; Psalms 97:2; Psalms 18:9"> And the people stood afar off. But Moses went to the dark cloud wherein God was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:13; Deuteronomy 4:36; Hebrews 12:25–26"> And the Lord said to Moses: Thus shalt thou say to the children of Israel: You have seen that I have spoken to you from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:39; Zephaniah 1:5; 2 Kings 17:33"> You shall not make gods of silver, nor shall you make to yourselves gods of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5; 2 Chronicles 6:6; Psalms 132:13–14"> You shall make an altar of earth unto me, and you shall offer upon it your holocausts and peace offerings, your sheep and oxen, in every place where the memory of my name shall be: I will come to thee, and will bless thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 8:31; Deuteronomy 27:5–6"> And if thou make an altar of stone unto me, thou shalt not build it of hewn stones: for if thou lift up a tool upon it, it shall be defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 1:16; Ecclesiastes 5:1; Leviticus 10:3"> Thou shalt not go up by steps unto my altar, lest thy nakedness be discovered.</span>
