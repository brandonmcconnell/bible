<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:4"> If thou see the ass of him that hateth thee lie underneath his burden, thou shalt not pass by, but shalt lift him up with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 3:5; Jeremiah 5:28; Leviticus 19:15"> Thou shalt not go aside in the poor man’s judgment.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Proverbs 17:23; Isaiah 5:23"> Neither shalt thou take bribes, which even blind the wise, and pervert the words of the just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:19; Exodus 22:21; Deuteronomy 24:14–18"> Thou shalt not molest a stranger, for you know the hearts of strangers: for you also were strangers in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 25:3–4"> Six years thou shalt sow thy ground, and shalt gather the corn thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 26:34–35; Leviticus 25:2–7; Leviticus 25:11–12"> But the seventh year thou shalt let it alone, and suffer it to rest, that the poor of thy people may eat, and whatsoever shall be left, let the beasts of the field eat it: so shalt thou do with thy vineyard and thy oliveyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 13:14; Exodus 34:21; Exodus 35:3"> Six days thou shalt work: the seventh day thou shalt cease, that thy ox and thy ass may rest: and the son of thy handmaid and the stranger may be refreshed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 23:7; Zechariah 13:2; 1 Timothy 4:16"> Keep all things that I have said to you. And by the name of strange gods you shall not swear, neither shall it be heard out of your mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 34:22–24; Leviticus 23:34"> Three times every year you shall celebrate feasts to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 22:7; Exodus 34:20; Numbers 28:16–25"> Thou shalt keep the feast of unleavened bread. Seven days shalt thou eat unleavened bread, as I commanded thee, in the time of the month of new corn, when thou didst come forth out of Egypt: thou shalt not appear empty before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 34:22; Exodus 22:29; Leviticus 23:9–21"> And the feast of the harvest of the firstfruits of thy work, whatsoever thou hast sown in the field. The feast also in the end of the year, when thou hast gathered in all thy corn out of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 34:23; Exodus 23:14"> Thrice a year shall all thy males appear before the Lord thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 34:25; Leviticus 2:11; Deuteronomy 16:4"> Thou shalt not sacrifice the blood of my victim upon leaven, neither shall the fat of my solemnity remain until the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 34:26; Deuteronomy 14:21; Deuteronomy 26:10"> Thou shalt carry the firstfruits of the corn of thy ground to the house of the Lord thy God. Thou shalt not boil a kid in the milk of his dam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 33:14; Psalms 91:11; Exodus 32:34"> Behold I will send my angel, who shall go before thee, and keep thee in thy journey, and bring thee into the place that I have prepared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 78:56; Psalms 78:40; Hebrews 12:25"> Take notice of him, and hear his voice, and do not think him one to be contemned: for he will not forgive when thou hast sinned, and my name is in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Deuteronomy 30:7; Zechariah 2:8"> But if thou wilt hear his voice, and do all that I speak, I will be an enemy to thy enemies, and will afflict them that afflict thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 23:20; Isaiah 5:13; Genesis 15:19–21"> And my angel shall go before thee, and shall bring thee in unto the Amorrhite, and the Hethite, and the Pherezite, and the Chanaanite, and the Hevite, and the Jebusite, whom I will destroy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; Deuteronomy 12:30–31; Numbers 33:52"> Thou shalt not adore their gods, nor serve them. Thou shalt not do their works, but shalt destroy them, and break their statues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:15; Exodus 15:26; Joshua 22:5"> And you shall serve the Lord your God, that I may bless your bread and your waters, and may take away sickness from the midst of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:14; Job 5:26; Deuteronomy 28:4"> There shall not be one fruitless nor barren in thy land: I will fill the number of thy days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:25; Genesis 35:5; Deuteronomy 11:25"> I will send my fear before thee, and will destroy all the people to whom thou shalt come: and will turn the backs of all thy enemies before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:20; Joshua 24:11–12"> Sending out hornets before, that shall drive away the Hevite, and the Chanaanite, and the Hethite, before thou come in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:22; Joshua 17:12–13; Judges 3:1–4"> I will not cast them out from thy face in one year: lest the land be brought into a wilderness, and the beasts multiply against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> By little and little I will drive them out from before thee, till thou be increased, and dost possess the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; Deuteronomy 11:24; Joshua 21:44"> And I will set thy bounds from the Red Sea to the sea of the Palestines, and from the desert to the river: I will deliver the inhabitants of the land into your hands, and will drive them out from before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Exodus 34:12; Exodus 34:15"> Thou shalt not enter into league with them, nor with their gods.</span>
