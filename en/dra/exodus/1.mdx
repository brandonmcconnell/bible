<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Dan, and Nephtali, Gad and Aser.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:22; Genesis 46:26–27; Judges 8:30"> And all the souls that came out of Jacob’s thigh, were seventy: but Joseph was in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 50:26; Genesis 50:24; Acts 7:14–16"> After he was dead, and all his brethren, and all that generation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 46:3; Genesis 1:28; Deuteronomy 26:5"> The children of Israel increased, and sprung up into multitudes, and growing exceedingly strong they filled the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 7:18; Ecclesiastes 2:18–19; Ecclesiastes 9:15"> In the mean time there arose a new king over Egypt, that knew not Joseph:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 105:24–25; Ecclesiastes 4:4; James 3:14–16"> And he said to his people: Behold the people of the children of Israel are numerous and stronger than we.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 7:19; Psalms 83:3–4; Proverbs 1:11"> Come, let us wisely oppress them, lest they multiply: and if any war shall rise against us, join with our enemies, and having overcome us, depart out of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 15:13; Genesis 47:11; Exodus 3:7"> Therefore he set over them masters of the works, to afflict them with burdens, and they built for Pharao cities of tabernacles, Phithom and Ramesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 105:24; Exodus 1:9; Romans 8:28"> But the more they oppressed them, the more they were multiplied, and increased:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:20"> And the Egyptians hated the children of Israel, and afflicted them and mocked them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 7:19; Numbers 20:15; Exodus 2:23"> And they made their life bitter with hard works in clay, and brick, and with all manner of service, wherewith they were overcharged in the works of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the king of Egypt spoke to the midwives of the Hebrews: of whom one was called Sephora, the other Phua,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 1:22; Matthew 21:38; Revelation 12:4"> Commanding them: When you shall do the office of midwives to the Hebrew women, and the time of delivery is come: if it be a man child, kill it: if a woman, keep it alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Exodus 1:21; Matthew 10:28"> But the midwives feared God, and did not do as the king of Egypt had commanded, but saved the men children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:4; 2 Samuel 13:28"> And the king called for them and said: What is that you meant to do, that you would save the men children?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 2:4–24; 2 Samuel 17:19–20; 1 Samuel 21:2"> They answered: The Hebrew women are not as the Egyptian women: for they themselves are skillful in the office of a midwife; and they are delivered before we come to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 3:10; Ecclesiastes 8:12; Proverbs 11:18"> Therefore God dealt well with the midwives: and the people multiplied and grew exceedingly strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 11:38; 1 Samuel 2:35; Psalms 37:3"> And because the midwives feared God, he built them houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 7:19; Proverbs 1:16; Exodus 1:16"> Pharao therefore charged all his people, saying: Whatsoever shall be born of the male sex, ye shall cast into the river: whatsoever of the female, ye shall save alive.</span>
