<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 78:18; Deuteronomy 6:16; 1 Corinthians 10:9"> And they chode with Moses, and said: Give us water, that we may drink. And Moses answered them: Why chide you with me? Wherefore do you tempt the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 16:2–3; Exodus 15:24"> So the people were thirsty there for want of water, and murmured against Moses, saying: Why didst thou make us go forth out of Egypt, to kill us and our children, and our beasts with thirst?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:6; Numbers 14:10; John 8:59"> And Moses cried to the Lord, saying: What shall I do to this people? Yet a little more and they will stone me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 7:19–20; Numbers 20:8–11; Acts 20:23–24"> And the Lord said to Moses: God before the people, and take with thee of the ancients of Israel: and take in thy hand the rod wherewith thou didst strike the river, and go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:4; Numbers 20:9–11; Nehemiah 9:15"> Behold I will stand there before thee, upon the rock Horeb: and thou shalt strike the rock, and water shall come out of it that the people may drink. Moses did so before the ancients of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 81:7; Psalms 95:8; Numbers 20:13"> And he called the name of that place Temptation, because the chiding of the children of Israel, and for that they tempted the Lord, saying: Is the Lord amongst us or not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 36:12; Deuteronomy 25:17–19; Numbers 24:20"> And Amalec came, and fought against Israel in Raphidim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 24:13; Deuteronomy 32:44; Exodus 4:20"> And Moses said to Josue: Choose out men: and go out and fight against Amalec: tomorrow I will stand on the top of the hill having the rod of God in my hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 24:14; Exodus 31:2; Exodus 17:12"> Josue did as Moses had spoken, and he fought against Amalec; but Moses, and Aaron, and Hur went up upon the top of the hill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:8; James 5:16; Psalms 56:9"> And when Moses lifted up his hands, Israel overcame: but if he let them down a little, Amalec overcame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3; Philippians 1:19; Hebrews 12:12"> And Moses’ hands were heavy: so they took a stone, and put under him, and he sat on it: and Aaron and Hur stayed up his hands on both sides. And it came to pass that his hands were not weary until sunset.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 10:42; Joshua 10:37; Joshua 10:32"> And Josue put Amalec and his people to flight, by the edge of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 34:27; Numbers 24:20; 1 Samuel 30:1"> And the Lord said to Moses: Write this for a memorial in a book, and deliver it to the ears of Josue: for I will destroy the memory of Amalec from under heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 22:14; Judges 6:24; Psalms 60:4"> And Moses built an altar: and called the name thereof, The Lord my exaltation, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 21:8–11; Isaiah 66:1; Acts 7:49"> Because the hand of the throne of the Lord, and the war of the Lord shall be against Amalec, from generation to generation.</span>
