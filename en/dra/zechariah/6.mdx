<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zechariah 1:18–19; Zechariah 6:5; Daniel 8:22"> And I turned, and lifted up my eyes, and saw: and behold four chariots came out from the midst of two mountains: and the mountains were mountains of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 1:8; Revelation 6:2–6; Zechariah 6:6"> In the first chariot were red horses, and in the second chariot black horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 6:2; Revelation 6:8; Zechariah 1:8"> And in the third chariot white horses, and in the fourth chariot grisled horses, and strong ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 1:9; Zechariah 5:10; Zechariah 1:19–21"> And I answered, and said to the angel that spoke in me: What are these, my lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 1:14; Hebrews 1:7; Psalms 104:3–4"> And the angel answered, and said to me: These are the four winds of the heaven, which go forth to stand before the Lord of all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:48; Jeremiah 4:6; Jeremiah 6:1"> That in which were the black horses went forth into the land of the north, and the white went forth after them: and the grisled went forth to the land of the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:9; Zechariah 1:10; Job 1:6–7"> And they that were most strong, went out, and sought to go, and to run to and fro through all the earth. And he said: Go, walk throughout the earth: and they walked throughout the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:13; Zechariah 1:15; Isaiah 18:3–4"> And he called me, and spoke to me, saying: Behold they that go forth into the land of the north, have quieted my spirit in the land of the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 8:1; Zechariah 7:1; Zechariah 1:1"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezra 7:14–16; Ezra 8:26–30; Isaiah 66:20"> Take of them of the captivity, of Holdai, and of Tobias, and of Idaias; thou shalt come in that day, and shalt go into the house of Josias, the son of Sophonias, who came out of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zechariah 3:1; Psalms 21:3; Song of Solomon 3:11"> And thou shalt take gold and silver: and shalt make crowns, and thou shalt set them on the head of Jesus the son of Josedec, the high priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zechariah 3:8; Isaiah 4:2; Jeremiah 33:15"> And thou shalt speak to him, saying: Thus saith the Lord of hosts, saying: BEHOLD A MAN, THE ORIENT IS HIS NAME: and under him shall he spring up, and shall build a temple to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 110:4; Isaiah 49:5–6; Hebrews 3:1"> Yea, he shall build a temple to the Lord: and he shall bear the glory, and shall sit, and rule upon his throne: and he shall be a priest upon his throne, and the counsel of peace shall be between them both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 26:13; Exodus 12:14; Mark 14:9"> And the crowns shall be to Helem, and Tobias, and Idaias, and to Hem, the son of Sophonias, a memorial in the temple of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 60:10; Zechariah 3:7; Isaiah 58:10–14"> And they that are far off, shall come and shall build in the temple of the Lord: and you shall know that the Lord of hosts sent me to you. But this shall come to pass, if hearing you will hear the voice of the Lord your God.</span>
