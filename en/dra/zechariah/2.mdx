<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:3; Zechariah 1:16; Ezekiel 47:4"> And I lifted up my eyes, and saw, and behold a man, with a measuring line in his hand.</span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 1:19; Zechariah 1:8–11; Zechariah 4:5"> And behold the angel that spoke in me went forth, and another angel went out to meet him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 1:17; Jeremiah 33:22; Ezekiel 38:11"> And he said to him: Run, speak to this young man, saying: Jerusalem shall be inhabited without walls, by reason of the multitude of men, and of the beasts in the midst thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 4:5; Zechariah 9:8; Psalms 3:3"> And I will be to it, saith the Lord, a wall of fire round about: and I will be in glory in the midst thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 48:20; Ezekiel 17:21; Isaiah 52:11–12"> O, O flee ye out of the land of the north, saith the Lord, for I have scattered you into the four winds of heaven, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 48:20; Genesis 19:17; Isaiah 52:11"> O Sion, flee, thou that dwellest with the daughter of Babylon:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 17:8; Deuteronomy 32:10; John 15:21–23"> For thus saith the Lord of hosts: After the glory he hath sent me to the nations that have robbed you: for he that toucheth you, toucheth the apple of my eye:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 19:16; Isaiah 14:2; Zechariah 4:9"> For behold I lift up my hand upon them, and they shall be a prey to those that served them: and you shall know that the Lord of hosts sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 28:20; John 1:14; Zephaniah 3:14–15"> Sing praise, and rejoice, O daughter of Sion: for behold I come, and I will dwell in the midst of thee: saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 11:15; 1 Peter 2:9–10; Acts 28:28"> And many nations shall be joined to the Lord in that day, and they shall be my people, and I will dwell in the midst of thee: and thou shalt know that the Lord of hosts hath sent me to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zechariah 1:17; Deuteronomy 32:9; Jeremiah 10:16"> And the Lord shall possess Juda his portion in the sanctified land: and he shall yet choose Jerusalem.</span>
