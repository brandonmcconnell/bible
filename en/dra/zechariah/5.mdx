<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zechariah 5:2; Isaiah 8:1; Ezekiel 2:9–10"> And I turned and lifted up my eyes: and I saw, and behold a volume flying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:14; Jeremiah 1:11–14; Amos 7:8"> And he said to me: What seest thou? And I said: I see a volume flying: the length thereof is twenty cubits, and the breadth thereof ten cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 20:15; Isaiah 43:28; Isaiah 24:6"> And he said to me: This is the curse that goeth forth over the face of the earth: for every thief shall be judged as is there written: and every one that sweareth in like manner shall be judged by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:9–11; Proverbs 3:33; Malachi 3:5"> I will bring it forth, saith the Lord of hosts: and it shall come to the house of the thief, and to the house of him that sweareth falsely by my name: and it shall remain in the midst of his house, and shall consume it, with the timber thereof, and the stones thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 1:9; Zechariah 1:14; Zechariah 2:3"> And the angel went forth that spoke in me, and he said to me: Lift up thy eyes, and see what this is, that goeth forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 8:5; Ezekiel 44:10–11"> And I said: What is it? And he said: This is a vessel going forth. And he said: This is their eye in all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:1–49; Revelation 17:1–18; Jeremiah 3:1–2"> And behold a talent of lead was carried, and behold a woman sitting in the midst of the vessel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 15:16; Amos 9:1–4; Proverbs 5:22"> And he said: This is wickedness. And he cast her into the midst of the vessel, and cast the weight of lead upon the mouth thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:49; Hosea 8:1; Daniel 9:26–27"> And I lifted up my eyes and looked: and behold there came out two women, and wind was in their wings, and they had wings like the wings of a kite: and they lifted up the vessel between the earth and the heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And I said to the angel that spoke in me: Whither do these carry the vessel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 11:2; Genesis 10:10; Daniel 1:2"> And he said to me: That a house may be built for it in the land of Sennaar, and that it may be established, and set there upon its own basis.</span>
