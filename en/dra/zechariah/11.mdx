<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:6–7; Jeremiah 22:23; Luke 21:23–24"> Open thy gates, O Libanus, and let fire devour thy cedars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 2:12–17; Ezekiel 31:17; Isaiah 10:33–34"> Howl, thou fir tree, for the cedar is fallen, for the mighty are laid waste: howl, ye oaks of Basan, because the fenced forest is cut down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:44; Jeremiah 25:34–36; Zechariah 11:8"> The voice of the howling of the shepherds, because their glory is laid waste: the voice of the roaring of the lions, because the pride of the Jordan is spoiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 11:7; Matthew 23:37; Romans 15:8"> Thus saith the Lord my God: Feed the flock of the slaughter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:7; Hosea 12:8; 1 Timothy 6:5–10"> Which they that possessed, slew, and repented not, and they sold them, saying: Blessed be the Lord, we are become rich: and their shepherds spared them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:14; Matthew 22:7; Isaiah 9:19–21"> And I will no more spare the inhabitants of the land, saith the Lord: behold I will deliver the men, every one into his neighbour’s hand, and into the hand of his king: and they shall destroy the land, and I will not deliver it out of their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 11:14; Zechariah 11:4; Isaiah 11:4"> And I will feed the hock of slaughter for this, O ye poor of the dock. And I took unto me two rods, one I called Beauty, and the other I called a Cord, and I fed the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 49:7; Matthew 23:34–36; John 15:18"> And I cut off three shepherds in one month, and my soul was straitened in their regard: for their soul also varied in my regard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:11; Matthew 13:10–11; Matthew 21:19"> And I said: I will not feed you: that which dieth, let it die: and that which is cut off, let it be cut off: and let the rest devour every one the flesh of his neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 11:7; Psalms 89:39; Hebrews 8:8–13"> And I took my rod that was called Beauty, and I cut it asunder to make void my covenant, which I had made with all people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:12; Luke 2:38; Deuteronomy 31:21"> And it was made void in that day: and so the poor of the flock that keep for me, understood that it is the word of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 26:15; Exodus 21:32; Luke 22:3–6"> And I said to them: If it be good in your eyes, bring hither my wages: and if not, be quiet. And they weighed for my wages thirty pieces of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 27:3–10; Acts 1:18–19; Isaiah 53:2–3"> And the Lord said to me: Cast it to the statuary, a handsome price, that I was prized at by them. And I took the thirty pieces of silver, and I cast them into the house of the Lord to the statuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 9:21; Isaiah 11:13; Acts 23:7–10"> And I cut off my second rod that was called a Cord, that I might break the brotherhood between Juda and Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10–12; Luke 11:40; Jeremiah 2:26–27"> And the Lord said to me: Take to thee yet the instruments of a foolish shepherd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2–6; Jeremiah 23:2; Matthew 23:2–4"> For behold I will raise up a shepherd in the land, who shall not visit what is forsaken, nor seek what is scattered, nor heal what is broken, nor nourish that which standeth, and he shall eat the flesh of the fat ones, and break their hoofs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:1; Ezekiel 13:3; 1 Corinthians 8:4"> O shepherd, and idol, that forsaketh the flock: the sword upon his arm and upon his right eye: his arm shall quite wither away, and his right eye shall be utterly darkened.</span>
