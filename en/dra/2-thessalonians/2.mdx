<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:14–17; Mark 13:27; Matthew 24:31"> And we beseech you, brethren, by the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ, and of our gathering together unto him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 4:1–2; Mark 13:7; Matthew 24:4–6"> That you be not easily moved from your sense, nor be terrified, neither by spirit, nor by word, nor by epistle, as sent from us, as if the day of the Lord were at hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 24:4–6; 1 John 2:18; Ephesians 5:6"> Let no man deceive you by any means, for unless there come a revolt first, and the man of sin be revealed, the son of perdition,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:2; Daniel 7:25; Revelation 13:6–7"> Who opposeth, and is lifted up above all that is called God, or that is worshipped, so that he sitteth in the temple of God, shewing himself as if he were God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Peter 1:15; Galatians 5:21; Matthew 16:9"> Remember you not, that when I was yet with you, I told you these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:3; 2 Thessalonians 2:8"> And now you know what withholdeth, that he may be revealed in his time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 4:3; Revelation 17:7; 2 Timothy 2:17–18"> For the mystery of iniquity already worketh; only that he who now holdeth, do hold, until he be taken out of the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:3; Revelation 19:20–21; Isaiah 11:4"> And then that wicked one shall be revealed whom the Lord Jesus shall kill with the spirit of his mouth; and shall destroy with the brightness of his coming, him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 24:24; Revelation 19:20; Ephesians 2:2"> Whose coming is according to the working of Satan, in all power, and signs, and lying wonders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:18; Proverbs 1:7; 2 Corinthians 4:2–3"> And in all seduction of iniquity to them that perish; because they receive not the love of the truth, that they might be saved. Therefore God shall send them the operation of error, to believe lying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 1:28; John 12:39–43; 1 Timothy 4:1"> That all may be judged who have not believed the truth, but have consented to iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 1:32; Romans 8:7–8; Mark 16:16"> But we ought to give thanks to God always for you, brethren, beloved of God, for that God hath chosen you firstfruits unto salvation, in sanctification of the spirit, and faith of the truth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Ephesians 1:4–5; 1 Thessalonians 1:4"> Whereunto also he hath called you by our gospel, unto the purchasing of the glory of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:12; 1 Peter 5:10; 1 Thessalonians 1:5"> Therefore, brethren, stand fast; and hold the traditions which you have learned, whether by word, or by our epistle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:2; 2 Thessalonians 3:6; 2 Thessalonians 2:2"> Now our Lord Jesus Christ himself, and God and our Father, who hath loved us, and hath given us everlasting consolation, and good hope in grace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 5:2–5; John 3:16; 1 Peter 1:3–8"> Exhort your hearts, and confirm you in every good work and word.</span>
