<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 1:13–15; Zephaniah 2:8–11; Ezekiel 25:2–10"> Against the children of Ammon. Thus saith the Lord: Hath Israel no sons? or hath he no heir? Why then hath Melchom inherited Gad: and his people dwelt in his cities?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:19; Ezekiel 21:20; Deuteronomy 3:11"> Therefore behold the days come, saith the Lord, and I will cause the noise of war to be heard in Rabbath of the children of Ammon, and it shall be destroyed into a heap, and her daughters shall be burnt with fire, and Israel shall possess them that have possessed him, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:7; Jeremiah 48:37; Isaiah 15:2"> Howl, O Hesebon, for Hai is wasted. Cry, ye daughters of Rabbath, gird yourselves with haircloth: mourn and go about by the hedges: for Melchom shall be carried into captivity, his priests, and his princes together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:17; Jeremiah 9:23; Jeremiah 21:13"> Why gloriest thou in the valleys? thy valley hath flowed away, O delicate daughter, that hast trusted in thy treasures, and hast said: Who shall come to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:5; Jeremiah 49:29; Jeremiah 15:8"> Behold I will bring a fear upon thee, saith the Lord God of hosts, from all that are round about thee: and you shall be scattered every one out of one another’s sight, neither shall there be any to gather together them that flee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:39; Jeremiah 48:47; Ezekiel 16:53"> And afterwards I will cause the captives of the children of Ammon to return, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 1:11–12; Jeremiah 25:21; Genesis 36:15"> Against Edom. Thus saith the Lord of hosts: Is wisdom no more in Theman? counsel is perished from her children: their wisdom is become unprofitable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:23; Jeremiah 46:21; Jeremiah 49:30"> Flee and turn your backs, go down into the deep hole, ye inhabitants of Dedan: for I have brought the destruction of Esau upon him, the time of his visitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Obadiah 1:5–6; Isaiah 17:6"> If grapegatherers had come to thee, would they not have left a bunch? if thieves in the night, they would have taken what was enough for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 17:14; Malachi 1:3–4; Isaiah 45:3"> But I have made Esau bare, I have revealed his secrets, and he cannot be hid: his seed is laid waste, and his brethren, and his neighbours, and he shall not be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 68:5; Hosea 14:3; James 1:27"> Leave thy fatherless children: I will make them live: and thy widows shall hope in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:28–29; Obadiah 1:16; Jeremiah 25:15"> For thus saith the Lord: Behold they whose judgment was not to drink of the cup, shall certainly drink: and shalt thou come off as innocent? thou shalt not come off as innocent, but drinking thou shalt drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 22:16; Isaiah 34:6; Genesis 36:33"> For I have sworn by myself, saith the Lord, that Bosra shall become a desolation, and a reproach, and a desert, and a curse: and all her cities shall be everlasting wastes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 30:4; Isaiah 37:7; Ezekiel 7:25–26"> I have heard a rumour from the Lord, and an ambassador is sent to the nations: Gather yourselves together, and come against her, and let us rise up to battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 1:51; Obadiah 1:2; Micah 7:10"> For behold I have made thee a little one among the nations, despicable among men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 49:25; Amos 9:2; Job 39:27"> Thy arrogancy hath deceived thee, and the pride of thy heart: O thou that dwellest in the clefts of the rock, and endeavourest to lay hold on the height of the hill: but though thou shouldst make thy nest as high as an eagle, I will bring thee down from thence, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:7; Jeremiah 50:13; Jeremiah 49:13"> And Edom shall be desolate: every one that shall pass by it, shall be astonished, and shall hiss at all its plagues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:33; Deuteronomy 29:23; Jeremiah 50:40"> As Sodom was overthrown and Gomorrha, and the neighbours thereof, saith the Lord: there shall not a man dwell there, and there shall no son of man inhabit it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:5; Jeremiah 30:21; Jeremiah 50:44–46"> Behold one shall come up as a lion from the swelling of the Jordan, against the strong and beautiful: for I will make him run suddenly upon her: and who shall be the chosen one whom I may appoint over her? for who is like to me? and who shall abide me? and who is that shepherd that can withstand my countenance?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:45; Malachi 1:3–4; Jeremiah 49:7"> Therefore hear ye the counsel of the Lord, which he hath taken concerning Edom: and his thoughts which he hath thought concerning the inhabitants of Theman: surely the little ones of the flock shall cast them down, of a truth they shall destroy them with their habitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:46; Ezekiel 32:10; Isaiah 14:4–15"> The earth is moved at the noise of their fall: the cry of their voice is heard in the Red Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:40–41; Hosea 8:1; Isaiah 13:8"> Behold he shall come up as an eagle, and fly: and he shall spread his wings over Bosra: and in that day the heart of the valiant ones of Edom shall be as the heart of a woman in labour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 10:9; 2 Kings 18:34; Genesis 15:2"> Against Damascus. Emath is confounded and Arphad: for they have heard very bad tidings, they are troubled as in the sea: through care they could not rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Damascus is undone, she is put to flight, trembling hath seized on her: anguish and sorrows have taken her as a woman in labour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:9; Jeremiah 51:41; Isaiah 1:26"> How have they forsaken the city of renown, the city of joy!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:30; Amos 4:10; Jeremiah 11:22"> Therefore her young men shall fall in her streets: and all the men of war shall be silent in that day, saith the Lord of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Amos 1:3–5; 1 Kings 15:18–20; Jeremiah 43:12"> And I will kindle a fire in the wall of Damascus, rind it shall devour the strong holds of Benadad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 6:3; Genesis 25:13; Isaiah 11:14"> Against Cedar and against the kingdoms of Asor, which Nabuchodonosor king of Babylon destroyed. Thus saith the Lord: Arise, and go ye up to Cedar, and waste the children of the east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:5; Jeremiah 6:25; Habakkuk 3:7"> They shall take their tents, and their flocks: and shall carry off for themselves their curtains, and all their vessels, and their camels: and they shall call fear upon them round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:9; Jeremiah 27:6; Isaiah 10:7"> Flee ye, get away speedily, sit in deep holes, you that inhabit Asur, saith the Lord: for Nabuchodonosor king of Babylon hath taken counsel against you, and hath conceived designs against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:11; Numbers 23:9; Deuteronomy 33:28"> Arise, and so up to a nation that is at ease, and that dwelleth securely, saith the Lord: they have neither gates, nor bars: they dwell alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:26; Jeremiah 25:23; Jeremiah 49:36"> And their camels shall be for a spoil, and the multitude of their cattle for a booty, and I will scatter into every wind them that have their hair cut round, and I will bring destruction upon them from I all their confines, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:22; Jeremiah 9:11; Jeremiah 51:37"> And Asor shall be a habitation for dragons, desolate for ever: no man shall abide there, nor son of man inhabit it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 10:22; Daniel 8:2; Jeremiah 25:25"> The word of the Lord that came to Jeremias the prophet against Elam, in the beginning of the reign of Sedecias king of Juda, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 22:6; Jeremiah 51:56; Psalms 46:9"> Thus saith the Lord of hosts: Behold I will break the bow of Elam, and their chief strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:32; Revelation 7:1; Ezekiel 5:10"> And I will bring upon Elam the four winds from the fear quarters of heaven: and I will scatter them into all these winds: and there shall be no nation, to which the fugitives of Elam shall not come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:16; Jeremiah 48:2; Jeremiah 49:22"> And I will cause Elam to be afraid before their enemies, and in the sight of them that seek their life: and I will bring evil upon them, my fierce wrath, saith the Lord: and will send the sword after them, till I consume them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:10; Daniel 7:9–14"> And I will set my throne in Elam, and destroy kings and princes from thence, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:47; Hosea 3:5; Micah 4:1"> But in the latter days I will cause the captives of Elam, to return, saith the Lord.</span>
