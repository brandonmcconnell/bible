<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The word that came from Jeremias to the Lord, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 1:1; Acts 9:6; Jeremiah 19:1–2"> Arise, and go down to the potter’s house, and there thou shalt hear my words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 15:14; Jonah 1:3; Acts 26:19"> And I went down into the potter’s house, and behold he was doing a work on the wheel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 9:20–23; Jeremiah 18:6; Isaiah 45:9"> And the vessel was broken which he was making with clay with his hands: and turning he made another vessel, as it seemed good in his eyes to make it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Then the word of the Lord came to me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; Romans 9:20–21; Jeremiah 18:4"> Cannot I do with you as this potter, saith the Lord? behold as clay is in the hand of the potter, so are you in my hand, O house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:10; Jeremiah 12:14–17; Jeremiah 45:4"> I will suddenly speak against a nation, and against a kingdom, to root out, and to pull down, and to destroy it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:21; Jeremiah 26:13; Jonah 3:9–10"> If that nation against which I have spoken, shall repent of their evil, I also will repent of the evil that I have thought to do to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:28; Amos 9:11–15; Jeremiah 1:10"> And I will suddenly speak of a nation and of a kingdom, to build up and plant it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:18; 1 Samuel 2:30; Ezekiel 18:24"> If it shall do evil in my sight, that it obey not my voice: I will repent of the good that I have spoken to do unto it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 17:13; Isaiah 55:6–7; Jeremiah 35:15"> Now therefore tell the men of Juda, and the inhabitants of Jerusalem, saying: Thus saith the Lord: Behold I frame evil against you, and devise a device against you: let every man of you return from his evil way, and make ye your ways and your doings good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:25; Jeremiah 7:24; Deuteronomy 29:19"> And they said: We have no hopes: for we will go after our own thoughts, and we will do every one according to the perverseness of his evil heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:30; Hosea 6:10; Jeremiah 23:14"> Therefore thus saith the Lord: Ask among the nations: Who hath heard such horrible things, as the virgin of Israel hath done to excess?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 6:68"> Shall now the snow of Libanus fail from the rock of the field? or can the cold waters that gush out and run down, be taken away?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:16; Jeremiah 2:32; Hosea 11:2"> Because my people have forgotten me, sacrificing in vain, and stumbling in their ways, in ancient paths, to walk by them in a way not trodden:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 22:7; Jeremiah 25:9; Jeremiah 19:8"> That their land might be given up to desolation, and to a perpetual hissing: every one that shall pass by it, shall be astonished, and wag his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:24; Jeremiah 2:27; Job 27:21"> As a burning will I scatter them before the enemy: I will shew them the back, and not the face, in the day of their destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 52:2; Jeremiah 11:19; Malachi 2:7"> And they said: Come, and let us invent devices against Jeremias: for the law shall not perish from the priest, nor counsel from the wise, nor the word from the prophet: come, and let us strike him with the tongue, and let us give no heed to all his words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Nehemiah 6:9; Jeremiah 20:12; Psalms 55:16–17"> Give heed to me, O Lord, and hear the voice of my adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 35:7; Psalms 57:6; Psalms 106:23"> Shall evil be rendered for good, because they have digged a pit for my soul? Remember that I have stood in thy sight, so speak good for them, and turn away thy indignation from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 109:9–20; Lamentations 5:3; Amos 4:10"> Therefore deliver up their children to famine, and bring them into the hands of the sword: let their wives be bereaved of children and widows: and let their husbands be slain by death: let their young men be stabbed with the sword in battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:26; Psalms 140:5; Zephaniah 1:10–11"> Let a cry be heard out of their houses: for thou shalt bring the robber upon them suddenly: because they have digged a pit to take me, and have hid snares for my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 35:4; Psalms 69:22–28; Nehemiah 4:4–5"> But thou, O Lord, knowest all their counsel against me unto death: forgive not their iniquity, and let not their sin be blotted out from thy sight: let them be overthrown before thy eyes, in the time of thy wrath do thou destroy them.</span>
