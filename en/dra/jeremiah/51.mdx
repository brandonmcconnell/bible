<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:11–12; Hosea 13:15; Amos 3:6"> Thus saith the Lord: Behold I will raise up as it were a pestilential wind against Babylon and against the inhabitants thereof, who have lifted up their heart against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:7; Matthew 3:12; Isaiah 41:16"> And I will send to Babylon fanners, and they shall fan her, and shall destroy her land: for they are come upon her on every side in the day of her affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:14; Jeremiah 46:4; Jeremiah 50:21"> Let not him that bendeth, bend his bow, and let not, him go up that is armed with a coat of mail: spare not her young men, destroy all her army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:26; Jeremiah 50:30; Isaiah 13:15"> And the slain shall fall in the land of the Chaldeans, and the wounded in the regions thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 11:1–2; Zechariah 12:6; Zechariah 12:8"> For Israel and Juda have not been forsaken by their God the Lord of hosts: but their land hath been filled with sin against the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:8; Jeremiah 25:14; Jeremiah 50:28"> Flee ye from the midst of Babylon, and let every one save his own life: be not silent upon her iniquity: for it is the time of revenge from the Lord, he will I render unto her what she hath deserved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 14:8; Revelation 17:4; Revelation 17:2"> Babylon hath been a golden cup in the hand of the Lord, that made all the earth drunk: the nations have drunk of her wine, and therefore they have staggered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:11; Isaiah 21:9; Revelation 14:8"> Babylon is suddenly fallen, and destroyed: howl for her, take balm for her pain, if so she may be healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:16; Revelation 18:5; Isaiah 13:14"> We would have cured Babylon, but she is not healed: let us forsake her, and let us go every man to his own land: because her judgment hath reached even to the heavens, and is lifted up to the clouds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:28; Psalms 37:6; Isaiah 51:11"> The Lord hath brought forth our justices: Come, and let us declare in Sion the work of the Lord our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:4; Jeremiah 50:45; Jeremiah 46:9"> Sharpen the arrows, fill the quivers, the Lord hath raised up the spirit of the kings of the Medes: and his mind is against Babylon to destroy it, because it is the vengeance of the Lord, the vengeance of his temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 13:2; Jeremiah 51:11; Jeremiah 51:29"> Upon the walls of Babylon set up the standard, strengthen the watch: set up the watchmen, prepare the ambushes: for the Lord hath both purposed, and done all that he spoke against the inhabitants of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 17:1; Isaiah 45:3; Revelation 17:15"> O thou that dwellest upon many waters, rich in treasures, thy end is come for thy entire destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joel 2:25; Jeremiah 50:15; Jeremiah 49:13"> The Lord of hosts hath sworn by himself, saying: I will fill thee with men as with locusts, and they shall lift up a joyful shout against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 4:11; Jeremiah 10:12–16; Jeremiah 32:17"> He that made the earth by his power, that hath prepared the world by his wisdom, and stretched out the heavens by his understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 135:7; Psalms 18:13; Jonah 1:4"> When he uttereth his voice the waters are multiplied in heaven: he lifteth up the clouds from the ends of the earth, he hath turned lightning into rain: and hath brought forth the wind out of his treasures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:18–19; Jeremiah 10:14; Isaiah 44:18–20"> Every man is become foolish by his knowledge: every founder is confounded by his idol, for what he hath cast is a lie, and there is no breath in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:8; Isaiah 19:1; Zephaniah 2:11"> They are vain works, and worthy to be laughed at, in the time of their visitation they shall perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 73:26; Jeremiah 10:16; Psalms 33:12"> The portion of Jacob is not like them: for he that made all things he it is, and Israel is the sceptre of his inheritance: the Lord of hosts is his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 41:15–16; Micah 4:13; Isaiah 37:26"> Thou dashest together for me the weapons of war, and with thee I will dash nations together, and with thee I will destroy kingdoms:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 15:1; Zechariah 10:5; Haggai 2:22"> And with thee I will break in pieces the horse, and his rider, and with thee I will break in pieces the chariot, and him that getteth up into it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:17; Isaiah 13:18; 1 Samuel 15:3"> And with thee I will break in pieces man and woman, and with thee I will break in pieces the old man and the child, and with thee I will break in pieces the young man and the virgin:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:57"> And with thee I will break in pieces the shepherd and his dock, and with thee I will break in pieces the husbandman and his yoke of oxen, and with thee I will break in pieces captains and rulers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:15; Revelation 18:24; Isaiah 63:1–4"> And I will render to Babylon, and to all the inhabitants of Chaldea all their evil, that they have done in Sion, before your eyes, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Revelation 8:8; Zechariah 4:7; Jeremiah 50:31"> Behold I come against thee, thou destroying mountain, saith the Lord, which corruptest the whole earth: and I will stretch out my hand upon thee, and will roll thee down from the rocks, and will make thee a burnt mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 13:19–22; Jeremiah 51:43; Revelation 18:20–24"> And they shall not take of thee a stone for the corner, nor a stone for foundations, but thou shalt be destroyed for ever, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 10:3; Genesis 8:4; 1 Chronicles 1:6"> Set ye up a standard in the land: sound with the trumpet among the nations: prepare the nations against her: call together against her the kings of Ararat, Menni, and Ascenez: number Taphsar against her, bring the horse as the stinging locust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:11; Jeremiah 25:25; Esther 1:3"> Prepare the nations against her, the kings of Media, their captains, and all their rulers, and all the land of their dominion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:11–12; Jeremiah 51:43; Jeremiah 8:16"> And the land shall be in a commotion, and shall be troubled: for the design of the Lord against Babylon shall awake, to make the land of Babylon desert and uninhabitable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Nahum 3:13; Jeremiah 50:36–37; Lamentations 2:9"> The valiant men of Babylon have forborne to fight, they have dwelt in holds: their strength hath failed, and they are become as women: her dwelling places are burnt, her bars are broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:6; 2 Samuel 18:19–31; Esther 8:14"> One running post shall meet another, and messenger shall meet messenger: to tell the king of Babylon that his city is taken from one end to the other:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:37–38; Isaiah 44:27; Jeremiah 51:30"> And that the fords are taken, and the marshes are burnt with fire, and the men of war are affrighted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 21:10; Hosea 6:11; Joel 3:13"> For thus saith the Lord of hosts the God of Israel: The daughter of Babylon is like a thrashingfloor, this is the time of her thrashing: yet a little while, and the time of her harvest shall come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:17; Jeremiah 51:44; Matthew 23:13"> Nabuchodonosor king of Babylon hath eaten me up, he hath devoured me: he hath made me as an empty vessel: he hath swallowed me up like a dragon, he hath filled his belly with my delicate meats, and he hath cast me out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Revelation 18:20; Jeremiah 50:29; Isaiah 26:20–21"> The wrong done to me, and my flesh be upon Babylon, saith the habitation of Sion: and my blood upon the inhabitants of Chaldea, saith Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:38; Romans 12:19; Psalms 140:12"> Therefore thus saith the Lord: Behold I will judge thy cause, and will take vengeance for thee, and I will make her sea desolate, and will dry up her spring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Revelation 18:2; Revelation 18:21–23; Jeremiah 18:16"> And Babylon shall be reduced to heaps, a dwelling place for dragons, an astonishment and a hissing, because there is no inhabitant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:15; Psalms 58:6; Judges 16:20"> They shall roar together like lions, they shall shake their manes like young lions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:57; Jeremiah 25:27; Nahum 3:11"> In their heat I will set them drink: and I will make them drunk, that they may slumber, and sleep an everlasting sleep, and awake no more, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:27; Isaiah 34:6; Psalms 44:22"> I will bring them down like lambs to the slaughter, and like rams with kids.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:26; Isaiah 13:19; Jeremiah 49:25"> How is Sesach taken, and the renowned one of all the earth surprised? How is Babylon become an astonishment among the nations?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–8; Daniel 9:26; Psalms 18:4"> The sea is come up over Babylon: she is covered with the multitude of the waves thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Isaiah 13:20; Jeremiah 2:6; Jeremiah 51:29"> Her cities are become an astonishment, a land uninhabited and desolate, a land wherein none can dwell, nor son of man pass through it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:34; Jeremiah 51:58; Isaiah 2:2"> And I will visit against Bel in Babylon, and I will bring forth out of his mouth that which he had swallowed down: and the rations shall no more flow together to him, for the wall also of Babylon shall fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:6; Revelation 18:4; Isaiah 48:20"> Go out of the midst of her, my people: that every man may save his life from the fierce wrath of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 13:3–5; Isaiah 19:2; Isaiah 21:2–3"> And lest your hearts faint, and ye fear for the rumour that shall be heard in the land: and a rumour shall come in one year, and after this year another rumour: and iniquity in the land, and ruler upon ruler.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:2; Isaiah 46:1–2; Jeremiah 51:52"> Therefore behold the days come, and I will visit the idols of Babylon: and her whole land shall be confounded, and all her slain shall fall in the midst of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Revelation 18:20; Isaiah 44:23; Jeremiah 50:3"> And the heavens and the earth, and all things that are in them shall give praise for Babylon: for spoilers shall come to her from the north, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:29; Jeremiah 51:24; Psalms 137:8–9"> And as Babylon caused that there should fall slain in Israel: so of Babylon there shall fall slain in all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:28; Psalms 122:6; Revelation 18:4"> You that have escaped the sword, come away, stand not still: remember the Lord afar off, and let Jerusalem come into your mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Lamentations 1:10; Psalms 44:13–16; Psalms 74:3–7"> We are confounded, because we have heard reproach: shame hath covered our faces: because strangers are come upon the sanctuaries of the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:47; Jeremiah 50:38; Daniel 5:30–31"> Therefore behold the days come, saith the Lord, and I will visit her graven things, and in all her land the wounded shall groan:</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Genesis 11:4; Jeremiah 49:16; Psalms 139:8–10"> If Babylon should mount up to heaven, and establish her strength on high: from me there should come spoilers upon her, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:22; Jeremiah 48:3–5; Jeremiah 50:46"> The noise of a cry from Babylon, and great destruction from the land of the Chaldeans:</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:10; Isaiah 47:5; Isaiah 15:1"> Because the Lord hath laid Babylon waste, and destroyed out of her the great voice: and their wave shall roar like many waters: their voice hath made a noise:</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Psalms 46:9; Jeremiah 51:48; Psalms 94:1–2"> Because the spoiler is come upon her, that is, upon Babylon, and her valiant men are taken, and their bow is weakened, because the Lord, who is a strong revenger, will surely repay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:18; Jeremiah 25:27; Jeremiah 48:15"> And I will make her princes drunk. and her wise men, and her captains, and her rulers, and her valiant men: and they shall sleep an everlasting sleep, and shall awake no more, saith the whose name is Lord of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:13; Jeremiah 51:64; Jeremiah 51:44"> Thus saith the Lord of hosts: That broad wall of Babylon shall be utterly broken down, and her high gates shall be burnt with fire, and the labours of the people shall come to nothing, and of the nations shall go to the fire, and shall perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:12; Jeremiah 36:4; Jeremiah 45:1"> The word that Jeremias the prophet commanded Saraias the son of Nerias, the son of Maasias, when he went with king Sedecias to Babylon, in the fourth year of his reign: now Saraias was chief over the prophecy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:2–3; Jeremiah 36:32; Isaiah 30:8"> And Jeremias wrote in one book all the evil that was to come upon Babylon: all these words that are written against Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Mark 13:1; 1 Thessalonians 5:27; 1 Thessalonians 4:18"> And Jeremias said to Saraias: When thou shalt come into Babylon, and shalt see, and shalt read all these words,</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:13; Jeremiah 50:3; Ezekiel 35:9"> Thou shalt say: O Lord, thou hast spoken against this place to destroy it: so that there should be neither man nor beast to dwell therein, and that it should be desolate for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Revelation 18:21; Jeremiah 19:10–11"> And when thou shalt have made an end of reading this book, thou shalt tie a stone to it, and shalt throw it into the midst of the Euphrates:</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:58; Job 31:40; Nahum 1:8–9"> And thou shalt say: Thus shall Babylon sink, and she shall not rise up from the affliction that I will bring upon her, and she shall be utterly destroyed. Thus far are the words of Jeremias.</span>
