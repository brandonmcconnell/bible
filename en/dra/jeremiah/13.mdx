<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:11; Hebrews 1:1; Jeremiah 27:2"> Thus saith the Lord to me: Go, and get thee a linen girdle, and thou shalt put it about thy loins, and shalt not put it into water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:8; John 15:14; Proverbs 3:5"> And I got a girdle accoding to the word of the Lord, and put it about my loins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:8"> And the word of the Lord came to me the second time, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 4:10; Psalms 137:1; Jeremiah 51:63–64"> Take the girdle which thou hast got, which is about thy loins, and arise, and go to the Euphrates, and hide it there in a hole of the rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 40:16; Exodus 39:42–43; 2 Timothy 2:3"> And I went, and hid it by the Euphrates, as the Lord had commanded me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And it came to pass after many days, that the Lord said to me: Arise, go to the Euphrates, and take from thence the girdle, which I commanded thee to hide there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 3:12; Isaiah 64:6; Jeremiah 13:10"> And I went to the Euphrates, and digged, and took the girdle out of the place where I had hid it: and behold the girdle was rotten, so that it was fit for no use.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 26:19; Jeremiah 13:15–17; Zephaniah 3:11"> Thus saith the Lord: after this manner will I make the pride of Juda, and the great pride of Jerusalem to rot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:12; Jeremiah 9:14; 2 Chronicles 36:15–16"> This wicked people, that will not hear my words, and that walk in the perverseness of their heart, and have gone after strange gods to serve them, and to adore them: and they shall be as this girdle which is fit for no use.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 19:5–6; Jeremiah 33:9; Psalms 81:11"> For as the girdle sticketh close to the loins of a man, so have I brought close to me all of the house of Israel, and all the house of Juda, saith the Lord: that they might be my people, and for a name, and for a praise, and for a glory: but they would not hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:19"> Thou shalt speak therefore to them this word: Thus saith the Lord God of Israel: Every bottle shall be filled with wine, and they shall say to thee: Do we not know that every bottle shall be filled with wine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 63:6; Psalms 75:8; Psalms 60:3"> And thou shalt say to them: Thus saith the Lord: Behold I will fill all the inhabitants of this land, and the kings of the race of David that sit upon his throne, and the priests, and the prophets, and all the inhabitants of Jerusalem. with drunkenness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:20; Psalms 2:9; Jeremiah 19:9–11"> And I will scatter them every man from his brother, and fathers and sons in like manner, saith the Lord: I will not spare, and I will not pardon: nor will I have mercy, but to destroy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 28:14–22; Revelation 2:29; Acts 4:19–20"> Hear ye, and give ear: Be not proud, for the Lord hath spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:9; Isaiah 8:22; Isaiah 5:30"> Give ye glory to the Lord your God, before it be dark, and before your feet stumble upon the dark mountains: you shall look for light, and he will turn it into the shadow of death, and into darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Malachi 2:2; Jeremiah 9:1; Jeremiah 14:17"> But if you will not hear this, my soul shall weep in secret for your pride: weeping it shall weep, and my eyes shall run down the tears, because the flock of the Lord is carried away captive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:26; 2 Kings 24:12; 2 Kings 24:15"> Say to the king, and to the queen: Humble yourselves, sit down: for the crown of your glory is come down from your head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:27; Jeremiah 39:9; Deuteronomy 28:52"> The cities of the south are shut up, and there is none to open them: all Juda is carried away captive with an entire captivity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:22; Jeremiah 1:14; Habakkuk 1:6"> Lift up your eyes, and see, you that come from the north: where is the flock that is given thee, thy beautiful cattle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 13:8; Jeremiah 4:31; Jeremiah 5:31"> What wilt thou say when he shall visit thee? for thou hast taught them against thee, and instructed them against thy own head: shall not sorrows lay hold on thee, as a woman in labour?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nahum 3:5; Jeremiah 16:10–11; Jeremiah 5:19"> And if thou shalt say in thy heart: Why are these things come upon me? For the greatness of thy iniquity, thy nakedness is discovered, the soles of thy feet are defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:22; Jeremiah 9:5; Matthew 19:24–28"> If the Ethiopian can change his skin, or the leopard his spots: you may also do well, when you have learned evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 1:4; Leviticus 26:33; Ezekiel 5:12"> And I will scatter them as stubble, which is carried away by the wind in the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 20:29; Jeremiah 2:32; Psalms 9:17"> This is thy lot, and the portion of thy measure from me, saith the Lord, because thou hast forgotten me, and hast trusted in falsehood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Lamentations 1:8; Hosea 2:10; Ezekiel 23:29"> Wherefore I have also bared my thighs against thy face, and thy shame hath appeared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hosea 8:5; Ezekiel 6:13; Ezekiel 24:13"> I have seen thy adulteries, and thy neighing, the wickedness of thy fornication: and thy abominations, upon the hills in the field. Woe to thee, Jerusalem, wilt thou not be made clean after me: how long yet?</span>
