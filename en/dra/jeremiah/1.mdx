<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:60; Jeremiah 11:21; 2 Chronicles 36:21"> The words of Jeremias the son of Helcias, of the priests that were in Anathoth, in the land of Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 13:20; Jonah 1:1; Jeremiah 1:11"> The word of the Lord which came to him in the days of Josias the son of Amon king of Juda, in the thirteenth year of his reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:2; 2 Chronicles 36:5–8; 2 Kings 23:34"> And which came to him in the days of Joakim the son of Josias king of Juda, unto the end of the eleventh year of Sedecias the son of Josias king of Juda, even unto the carrying away of Jerusalem captive, in the fifth month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:16; Ezekiel 1:3; Jeremiah 1:2"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 139:16; Isaiah 49:1; Galatians 1:15–16"> Before I formed thee in the bowels of thy mother, I knew thee: and before thou camest forth out of the womb, I sanctified thee, and made thee a prophet unto the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 6:12; Exodus 4:10–16; 1 Kings 3:7–9"> And I said: Ah, ah, ah, Lord God: behold, I cannot speak, for I am a child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 16:15–16; Ezekiel 3:27; Numbers 22:20"> And the Lord said to me: Say not: I am a child: for thou shalt go to all that I shall send thee: and whatsoever I shall command thee, thou shalt speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Joshua 1:9; Isaiah 51:7"> Be not afraid at their presence: for I am with thee to deliver thee, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Exodus 4:11–12; Luke 12:12"> And the Lord put forth his hand, and touched my mouth: and the Lord said to me: Behold I have given my words in thy mouth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:28; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Isaiah 44:26–28"> Lo, I have set thee this day over the nations, and over the kingdoms, to root up, and pull down, and to waste, and to destroy, and to build, and to plant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Amos 7:8; Amos 8:2; Zechariah 5:2"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying: What seest thou, Jeremias? And I said: I see a rod watching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:28; Ezekiel 12:25; Ezekiel 12:22–23"> And the Lord said to me: Thou hast seen well: for I will watch over my word to perform it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:3; Ezekiel 11:7; Zechariah 4:2"> And the word of the Lord came to me a second time, saying: What seest thou? I see a boiling caldron, and the face thereof from the face of the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 41:25; Jeremiah 4:6; Jeremiah 10:22"> And the Lord said to me: from the north shall an evil break forth upon all the inhabitants of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:10; Jeremiah 25:9; Jeremiah 9:11"> For behold I will call together all the families of the kingdoms of the north: saith the Lord: and they shall come, and shall set every one his throne in the entrance of the gates of Jerusalem, and upon all the walls thereof round about, and upon all the cities of Juda,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:9; Jeremiah 19:4; Acts 7:41"> And I will pronounce my judgements against them, touching all their wickedness, who have forsaken me, and have sacrificed to strange gods, and have adored the work of their own hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:7–8; 1 Kings 18:46; 1 Peter 1:13"> Thou therefore gird up thy loins, and arise, and speak to them all that I command thee. Be not afraid at their presence: for I will make thee not to fear their countenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 50:7; Ezekiel 3:8–9; Jeremiah 15:20"> For behold I have made thee this day a fortified city, and a pillar of iron, and a wall of brass, over all the land, to the kings of Juda, to the princes thereof, and to the priests, and to the people of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Jeremiah 1:8; Jeremiah 20:11"> And they shall fight against thee, and shall not prevail: for I am with thee, saith the Lord, to deliver thee.</span>
