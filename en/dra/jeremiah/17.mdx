<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 3:3; 2 Corinthians 3:3; Proverbs 7:3"> The sin of Juda is written with a pen of iron, with the point of a diamond, it is graven upon the table of their heart, upon the horns of their altars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:18; Jeremiah 2:20; Judges 3:7"> When their children shall remember their altars, and their groves, and their green trees upon high mountains,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:13; Jeremiah 26:18; 2 Kings 24:13"> Sacrificing in the field: I will give thy strength, and all thy treasures to the spoil, and thy high places for sin in all thy borders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:14; Lamentations 5:2; Isaiah 5:25"> And thou shalt be left stripped of thy inheritance, which I gave thee: and I will make thee serve thy enemies in a land which thou knowest not: because thou hast kindled a fire in my wrath, it shall burn for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 118:8–9; Psalms 146:3–4; Isaiah 2:22"> Thus saith the Lord: Cursed be the man that trusteth in man, and maketh flesh his arm, and whose heart departeth from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 20:17; Deuteronomy 29:23; Psalms 1:4"> For he shall be like tamaric in the desert, and he shall not see when good shall come: but he shall dwell in dryness in the desert in a salt land, and not inhabited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 26:3–4; Psalms 34:8; Psalms 40:4"> Blessed be the man that trusteth in the Lord, and the Lord shall be his confidence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 1:3; Isaiah 58:11; Ezekiel 47:12"> And he shall be as a tree that is planted by the waters, that spreadeth out its roots towards moisture: and it shall not fear when the heat cometh. And the leaf thereof shall be green, and in the time of drought it shall not be solicitous, neither shall it cease at any time to bring forth fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 7:21–22; Matthew 15:19; Ecclesiastes 9:3"> The heart is perverse above all things, and unsearchable, who can know it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:7; Romans 8:27; Jeremiah 11:20"> I am the Lord who search the heart and prove the reins: who give to every one according to his way, and according to the fruit of his devices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 28:20; Proverbs 21:6; Jeremiah 22:17"> As the partridge hath hatched eggs which she did not lay: so is he that hath gathered riches, and not by right: in the midst of his days he shall leave them, and in his latter end he shall be a fool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 96:6; Jeremiah 3:17; Ezekiel 43:7"> A high and glorious throne from the beginning is the place of our sanctification:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 7:17; John 4:14; John 7:37–38"> O Lord the hope of Israel: all that forsake thee shall be confounded: they that depart from thee, shall be written in the earth: because they have forsaken the Lord, the vein of living waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 6:2; Deuteronomy 32:39; Luke 4:18"> Heal me, O Lord, and I shall be healed: save me, and I shall be saved, for thou art my praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 5:19; Jeremiah 20:7–8; Ezekiel 12:22"> Behold they say to me: Where is the word of the Lord? let it come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 20:20; Jeremiah 4:19–20; Acts 20:27"> And I am not troubled, following thee for my pastor, and I have not desired the day of man, thou knowest. That which went out of my lips, hath been right in thy sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Nahum 1:7; Psalms 59:16; Jeremiah 16:19"> Be not thou a terror unto me, thou art my hope in the day of affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 35:4; Psalms 40:14; Psalms 83:17–18"> Let them be confounded that persecute me, and let not me be confounded: let them be afraid, and let not me be afraid: bring upon them the day of affliction, and with a double destruction, destroy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:2; Jeremiah 26:2; Acts 5:20"> Thus saith the Lord to me: Go, and stand in the gate of the children of the people, by which the kings of Juda come in, and go out, and in all the gates of Jerusalem:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:3; Jeremiah 22:2; Psalms 49:1–2"> And thou shalt say to them: Hear the word of the Lord, ye kings of Juda, and all Juda, and all the inhabitant of Jerusalem, that enter in by these gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:15–21; Numbers 15:32–36; John 5:9–12"> Thus saith the Lord: Take heed to your souls and carry no burdens on the Sabbath day: and bring them not in by the gates of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:12; Exodus 20:8–10; Isaiah 58:13"> And do not bring burdens out of your houses on the sabbath day, neither do ye any work: sanctify the sabbath day, as I commanded your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 29:1; Jeremiah 11:10; Acts 7:51"> But they did not hear, nor incline their ear: but hardened their neck, that they might not hear me, and might not receive instruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:21–22; Deuteronomy 11:13; 2 Peter 1:5–10"> And it shall come to pass: if you will hearken to me, saith the Lord, to bring in no burdens by the gates of this city on the sabbath day: and if you will sanctify the sabbath day, to do no work therein:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:4; Isaiah 9:7; Hebrews 12:22"> Then shall there enter in by the gates of this city kings and princes, sitting upon the throne of David, and riding in chariots and on horses, they and their princes, the men of Juda, and the inhabitants of Jerusalem: and this city shall be inhabited forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:13; Jeremiah 32:44; Zechariah 7:7"> And they shall come from the cities of Juda, and from the places round about Jerusalem, and from the land of Benjamin, and from the plains, and from the mountains, and from the south, bringing holocausts, and victims, and sacrifices, and frankincense, and they shall bring in an offering into the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 25:9; Jeremiah 7:20; Jeremiah 21:14"> But if you will not hearken to me, to sanctify the sabbath day, and not to carry burdens, and not to bring them in by the gates of Jerusalem on the sabbath day: I will kindle a fire in the gates thereof, and it shall devour the houses of Jerusalem, and it shall not be quenched.</span>
