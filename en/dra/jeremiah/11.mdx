<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The word that came from the Lord to Jeremias, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:6; Exodus 19:5; 2 Chronicles 29:10"> Hear ye the words of this covenant, and speak to the men of Juda, and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:26; Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Deuteronomy 29:19–20"> And thou shalt say to them: Thus saith the Lord the God of Israel: Cursed is the man that shall not hearken to the words of this covenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:12; Jeremiah 7:22–23; Deuteronomy 4:20"> Which I commanded your fathers in the day that I brought them out of the land of Egypt, from the iron furnace, saying: Hear ye my voice, and do all things that I command you: and you shall be my people, and I will be your God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:12–13; Psalms 105:9–11; Jeremiah 28:6"> That I may accomplish the oath which I swore to your fathers, to give them a land flowing with milk and honey, as it is this day. And I answered and said: Amen, O Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 1:22; Romans 2:13; Jeremiah 7:2"> And the Lord said to me: Proclaim aloud all these words in the cities of Juda, and in the streets of Jerusalem, saying: Hear ye the words of the covenant, and do them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:15; Ephesians 4:17; 1 Samuel 8:9"> For protesting I conjured your fathers in the day that I brought them out of the land of Egypt even to this day: rising early I conjured them, and said: Hearken ye to my voice:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:24; Jeremiah 7:26; Jeremiah 35:15"> And they obeyed not, nor inclined their ear: but walked every one in the perverseness of his own wicked heart: and I brought upon them all the words of this covenant, which I commanded them to do, but they did them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 6:9; Matthew 26:3–4; Micah 3:11"> And the Lord said to me: A conspiracy is found among the men of Juda, and among the inhabitants of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:16; Ezekiel 16:59; Psalms 78:8–10"> They are returned to the former iniquities of their fathers, who refused to hear my words: so these likewise have gone after strange gods, to serve them: the house of Israel, and the house of Juda have made void my covenant, which I made with their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:18; Proverbs 1:28; Isaiah 1:15"> Wherefore thus saith the Lord: Behold I will bring in evils upon them, which they shall not be able to escape: and they shall cry to me, and I will not hearken to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Deuteronomy 32:37; 2 Chronicles 28:22"> And the cities of Juda, and the inhabitants of Jerusalem shall go, and cry to the gods to whom they offer sacrifice, and they shall not save them in the time of their affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; 2 Kings 23:13; Isaiah 2:8"> For according to the number of thy cities were thy gods, O Juda: and according to the number of the streets of Jerusalem thou hast set up altars of confusion, altars to offer sacrifice to Baalim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:16; Jeremiah 11:11; 1 John 5:16"> Therefore, do not thou pray for this people, and do not take up praise and prayer for them: for I will not hear them in the time of their cry to me, in the time of their affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 2:14; Titus 1:15; Jeremiah 3:1–2"> What is the meaning that my beloved hath wrought muck wickedness in my house? shall the holy flesh take away from thee thy crimes, in which thou hast boasted?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 11:17–24; Isaiah 27:11; Psalms 52:8"> The Lord called thy name, a plentiful olive tree, fair, fruitful, and beautiful: at the noise of a word, a great fire was kindled in it and the branches thereof are burnt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 5:2; Jeremiah 2:21; Jeremiah 12:2"> And the Lord of hosts that planted thee, hath pronounced evil against thee: for the evils of the house of Israel, and the house of Juda, which they have done to themselves, to provoke me, offering sacrifice to Baalim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:11–12; 2 Kings 6:9–10; Ezekiel 8:6–18"> But thou, O Lord, hast shewn me, and I have known: then thou shewedst me their doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7–8; Jeremiah 20:10; Psalms 83:4"> And I was as a meek lamb, that is carried to be a victim: and I knew not that they had devised counsels against me, saying: Let us put wood on his bread, and cut him off from the land of the living, and let his name be remembered no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:12; Jeremiah 17:10; 1 Samuel 16:7"> But thou, O Lord of Sabaoth, who judgest justly, and triest the reins and hearts, let me see thy revenge on them: for to thee I have revealed my cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:10; Amos 2:12; Luke 13:33–34"> Therefore thus saith the Lord to the men of Anathoth, who seek thy life, and say: Thou shalt not prophesy in the name of the Lord, and thou shalt not die in our hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:21; 2 Chronicles 36:17; Jeremiah 9:21"> Therefore thus saith the Lord of hosts: Behold I will visit upon them: and their young men shall die by the sword, their sons and their daughters shall die by famine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:12; Micah 7:4; Hosea 9:7"> And there shall be no remains of them: for I will bring in evil upon the men of Anathoth, the year of their visitation.</span>
