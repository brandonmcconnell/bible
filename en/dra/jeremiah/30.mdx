<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:15; Jeremiah 1:1–2"> This is the word that came to Jeremias from the Lord, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:32; Isaiah 30:8; Habakkuk 2:2–3"> Thus saith the Lord, the God of Israel, saying: Write thee all the words that I have spoken to thee, in a book.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:14; Jeremiah 16:15; Jeremiah 30:18"> For behold the days come, saith the Lord, and I will bring again the captivity of my people Israel and Juda, saith the Lord: and I will cause them to return to the land which I gave to their fathers, and they shall possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And these are the words that the Lord hath spoken to Israel and to Juda:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:10–11; Amos 8:10; Isaiah 5:30"> For thus saith the Lord: We have heard a voice of terror: there is fear and no peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nahum 2:10; Joel 2:6; Jeremiah 4:31"> Ask ye, and see if a man bear children? why then have I seen every man with his hands on his loins, like a woman in labour, and all faces are turned yellow?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 12:1; Zechariah 14:1–2; Malachi 4:1"> Alas, for that day is great, neither is there the like to it; and it Is the time of tribulation to Jacob, but he shall be saved out of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nahum 1:13; Ezekiel 34:27; Isaiah 9:4"> And it shall come to pass in that day, saith the Lord of hosts, that I will break his yoke from off thy neck, and will burst his bands: and strangers shall no more rule over him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 3:5; Isaiah 55:3–5; Luke 1:69"> But they shall serve the Lord their God, and David their king, whom I will raise up to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:27–28; Isaiah 43:5; Jeremiah 29:14"> Therefore fear thou not, my servant Jacob, saith the Lord, neither be dismayed, O Israel: for behold, I will save thee from a country afar off, and thy seed from the land of their captivity: and Jacob shall return, and be at rest, and abound with all good things, and there shall be none whom he may fear:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:24; Jeremiah 1:19; Isaiah 8:10"> For I am with thee, saith the Lord, to save thee: for I will utterly consume all the nations, among which I have scattered thee: but I will not utterly consume thee: but I will chastise thee in judgment, that thou mayst not seem to thyself innocent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:18; Jeremiah 30:15; Jeremiah 14:17"> For thus saith the Lord: Thy bruise is incurable, thy wound is very grievous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:11; Jeremiah 8:22; Jeremiah 30:17"> There is none to judge thy judgment to bind it up: thou hast no healing medicines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:6; Job 30:21; Lamentations 1:2"> All thy lovers have forgotten thee, and will not seek after thee: for I have wounded thee with the wound of an enemy, with a cruel chastisement: by reason of the multitude of thy iniquities, thy sins are hardened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:14; Jeremiah 30:12; Jeremiah 6:6–7"> Why criest thou for thy affliction? thy sorrow is incurable: for the multitude of thy iniquity, and for thy hardened sins I have done these things to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:25; Isaiah 33:1; Exodus 23:22"> Therefore all they that devour thee shall be devoured: and all thy enemies shall be carried into captivity: and they that waste thee shall be wasted, and all that prey upon thee will I give for a prey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 107:20; Jeremiah 33:6; 1 Peter 2:24"> For I will close up thy scar, and will heal thee of thy wounds, saith the Lord. Because they have called thee, O Sion, an outcast: This is she that hath none to seek after her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Haggai 2:7–9; Psalms 102:13; Jeremiah 30:3"> Thus saith the Lord: Behold I bring back the captivity of the pavilions of Jacob, and will have pity on his houses, and the city shall be built in her place, and the temple shall be found according to the order thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:22; Isaiah 51:11; Isaiah 60:22"> And out of them shall come forth praise, and the voice of them that play: and I will multiply them, and they shall not be made few: and I will glorify them, and they shall not be lessened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:39; Jeremiah 2:3; Jeremiah 50:33–34"> And their children shall be as from the beginning, and their assembly be permanent before me: and I will against all that afflict them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 16:5; Ezekiel 34:23–24; Acts 5:31"> And their leader shall be of themselves: and their prince shall come forth from the midst of them: and I will bring him near, and he shall come to me: for who is this that setteth his heart to approach to me, saith the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 21:3; Ezekiel 36:28; Jeremiah 31:33"> And you shall be my people: and I will be your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:19–20; Zechariah 9:14; Jeremiah 25:32"> Behold the whirlwind of the Lord, his fury going forth, a violent storm, it shall rest upon the head of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:20; Hosea 3:5; Micah 4:1"> The Lord will not turn away the wrath of his indignation, till he have executed and performed the thought o his heart: in the latter days you shall understand these things.</span>
