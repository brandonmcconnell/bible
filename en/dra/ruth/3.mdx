<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ruth 1:9; 1 Timothy 5:8; 1 Timothy 5:14"> After she was returned to her mother in law, Noemi said to her: My daughter, I will seek rest for thee, and will provide that it may be well with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Ruth 2:20–23; Ruth 2:8"> This Booz, with whose maids thou wast joined in the field, is our near kinsman, and behold this night he winnoweth barley in the threshingfloor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:2; 1 Timothy 2:9–10; Esther 5:1"> Wash thyself therefore and anoint thee, and put on thy best garments, and go down to the barnfloor: but let not the man see thee, till he shall have done eating and drinking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:22"> And when he shall go to sleep, mark the place wherein he sleepeth: and thou shalt go in, and lift up the clothes wherewith he is covered towards his feet, and shalt lay thyself down there: and he will tell thee what thou must do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 3:20; Ephesians 6:1"> She answered: Whatsoever thou shalt command, I will do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 1:8; John 2:5; Exodus 20:12"> And she went down to the barnfloor, and did all that her mother in law had bid her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 19:6; Esther 1:10; 2 Samuel 13:28"> And when Booz had eaten, and drunk, and was merry, he went to sleep by the heap of sheaves, and she came softly and uncovering his feet, laid herself down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And behold, when it was now midnight the man was afraid, and troubled: and he saw a woman lying at his feet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:8; Ruth 2:20; Ruth 3:12"> And he said to her: Who art thou? And she answered: I am Ruth thy handmaid: spread thy coverlet over thy servant, for thou art a near kinsman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ruth 2:20; 1 Corinthians 13:4–5; Ruth 1:8"> And he said: Blessed art thou of the Lord, my daughter, and thy latter kindness has surpassed the former: because thou hast not followed young men either poor or rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 31:29–31; Proverbs 31:10; Proverbs 12:4"> Fear not therefore, but whatsoever thou shalt say to me I will do to thee. For all the people that dwell within the gates of my city, know that thou art a virtuous woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ruth 4:1; 1 Thessalonians 4:6; Matthew 7:12"> Neither do I deny myself to be near of kin, but there is another nearer than I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ruth 4:5; Jeremiah 4:2; Ruth 2:20"> Rest thou this night: and when morning is come, if he will take thee by the right of kindred, all is well: but if he will not, I will undoubtedly take thee, as the Lord liveth: sleep till the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:21; Romans 14:16; 1 Corinthians 10:32"> So she slept at his feet till the night was going off. And she arose before men could know one another, and Booz said: Beware lest any man know that thou camest hither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 32:8; Galatians 6:10"> And again he said: Spread thy mantle, wherewith thou art covered, and hold it with both hands. And when she spread it and held it, he measured six measures of barley, and laid it upon her. And she carried it and went into the city,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And came to her mother in law; who said to her: What hast thou done, daughter? And she told her all that the man had done to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And she said: Behold he hath given me six measures of barley: for he said: I will not have thee return empty to thy mother in law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 37:3–5; Isaiah 28:16; Isaiah 30:7"> And Noemi said: Wait my daughter, till we see what end the thing will have. For the man will not rest until he have accomplished what he hath said.</span>
