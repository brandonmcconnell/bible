<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:16–17; John 12:26; 2 Timothy 2:12"> And if I shall go, and prepare a place for you, I will come again, and will take you to myself; that where I am, you also may be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 10:9; John 16:28; John 13:3"> And whither I go you know, and the way you know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 11:16; Mark 9:19; John 20:25–28"> Thomas saith to him: Lord, we know not whither thou goest; and how can we know the way?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 4:12; John 10:9; Ephesians 2:18"> Jesus saith to him: I am the way, and the truth, and the life. No man cometh to the Father, but by me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 1:15–17; John 8:19; Luke 10:22"> If you had known me, you would without doubt have known my Father also: and from henceforth you shall know him, and you have seen him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 33:18–23; Revelation 22:3–5; John 1:43–46"> Philip saith to him: Lord, shew us the Father, and it is enough for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 1:15; John 12:45; John 14:7"> Jesus saith to him: Have I been so long a time with you; and have you not known me? Philip, he that seeth me seeth the Father also. How sayest thou, Shew us the Father?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 5:19; John 10:38; John 10:30"> Do you not believe, that I am in the Father, and the Father in me? The words that I speak to you, I speak not of myself. But the Father who abideth in me, he doth the works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 10:38; John 10:25; John 5:36"> Believe you not that I am in the Father, and the Father in me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 21:21; Mark 16:17; Luke 10:17–19"> Otherwise believe for the very works’ sake. Amen, amen I say to you, he that believeth in me, the works that I do, he also shall do; and greater than these shall he do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 15:7; Mark 11:24; Matthew 7:7"> Because I go to the Father: and whatsoever you shall ask the Father in my name, that will I do: that the Father may be glorified in the Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> If you shall ask me any thing in my name, that I will do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 2:3–5; 1 John 5:2–3; John 14:21–24"> If you love me, keep my commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 14:26; John 15:26; John 16:7–15"> And I will ask the Father, and he shall give you another Paraclete, that he may abide with you for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:27; John 16:13; 1 John 2:27"> The spirit of truth, whom the world cannot receive, because it seeth him not, nor knoweth him: but you shall know him; because he shall abide with you, and shall be in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 14:3; Psalms 23:4; Matthew 18:20"> I will not leave you orphans, I will come to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 16:16; John 7:33; Hebrews 7:25"> Yet a little while: and the world seeth me no more. But you see me: because I live, and you shall live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 15:5–7; Galatians 2:20; John 17:21–23"> In that day you shall know, that I am in my Father, and you in me, and I in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 14:15; 1 John 5:3; 1 John 2:5"> He that hath my commandments, and keepeth them; he it is that loveth me. And he that loveth me, shall be loved of my Father: and I will love him, and will manifest myself to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Luke 6:16; Matthew 10:3"> Judas saith to him, not the Iscariot: Lord, how is it, that thou wilt manifest thyself to us, and not to the world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 14:21; John 14:15; Revelation 3:20–21"> Jesus answered, and said to him: If any one love me, he will keep my word, and my Father will love him, and we will come to him, and will make our abode with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 7:16; John 14:10; John 8:42"> He that loveth me not, keepeth not my words. And the word which you have heard, is not mine; but the Father’s who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 17:6–8; John 13:19; John 16:12"> These things have I spoken to you, abiding with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 15:26; John 14:16; 1 John 2:27"> But the Paraclete, the Holy Ghost, whom the Father will send in my name, he will teach you all things, and bring all things to your mind, whatsoever I shall have said to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Philippians 4:7; John 16:33; Isaiah 41:10"> Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you: not as the world giveth, do I give unto you. Let not your heart be troubled, nor let it be afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 10:29–30; 1 Corinthians 15:24–28; 1 Corinthians 11:3"> You have heard that I said to you: I go away, and I come unto you. If you loved me, you would indeed be glad, because I go to the Father: for the Father is greater than I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 13:19; John 16:4–31; Matthew 24:24–25"> And now I have told you before it comes to pass: that when it shall come to pass, you may believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 John 5:19; 1 John 4:4; Revelation 12:9"> I will not now speak many things with you. For the prince of this world cometh, and in me he hath not any thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 10:18; Philippians 2:8; John 12:49"> But that the world may know, that I love the Father: and as the Father hath given me commandment, so do I: Arise, let us go hence.</span>
