<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 21:1; Mark 11:1; Luke 19:37"> And Jesus went unto mount Olivet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 26:55; Luke 4:20; Luke 21:37"> And early in the morning he came again into the temple, and all the people came to him, and sitting down he taught them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And the scribes and the Pharisees bring unto him a woman taken in adultery: and they set her in the midst,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And said to him: Master, this woman was even now taken in adultery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 20:10; Deuteronomy 22:21–24; Ezekiel 16:38–40"> Now Moses in the law commanded us to stone such a one. But what sayest thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:13; Luke 10:25; Ecclesiastes 3:7"> And this they said tempting him, that they might accuse him. But Jesus bowing himself down, wrote with his finger on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:1–5; Romans 2:21–25; Romans 2:1–3"> When therefore they continued asking him, he lifted up himself, and said to them: He that is without sin among you, let him first cast a stone at her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And again stooping down, he wrote on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:22; Romans 2:15; 1 John 3:20"> But they hearing this, went out one by one, beginning at the eldest. And Jesus alone remained, and the woman standing in the midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 41:11–12"> Then Jesus lifting up himself, said to her: Woman, where are they that accused thee? Hath no man condemned thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 3:17; John 5:14; Isaiah 1:16–18"> Who said: No man, Lord. And Jesus said: Neither will I condemn thee. Go, and now sin no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 12:46; John 9:5; John 12:35"> Again therefore, Jesus spoke to them, saying: I am the light of the world: he that followeth me, walketh not in darkness, but shall have the light of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 5:31–47"> The Pharisees therefore said to him: Thou givest testimony of thyself: thy testimony is not true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 16:28; John 13:3; John 8:42"> Jesus answered, and said to them: Although I give testimony of myself, my testimony is true: for I know whence I came, and whither I go: but you know not whence I come, or whither I go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:7; John 7:24; John 12:47"> You judge according to the flesh: I judge not any man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 17:31; John 8:29; John 16:32"> And if I do judge, my judgment is true: because I am not alone, but I and the Father that sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:15; Deuteronomy 17:6; Hebrews 10:28"> And in your law it is written, that the testimony of two men is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 8:38; John 8:58; John 8:25"> I am one that give testimony of myself: and the Father that sent me giveth testimony of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 16:3; John 8:54–55; John 14:6–9"> They said therefore to him: Where is thy Father? Jesus answered: Neither me do you know, nor my Father: if you did know me, perhaps you would know my Father also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:30; John 7:8; Mark 12:41"> These words Jesus spoke in the treasury, teaching in the temple: and no man laid hands on him, because his hour was not yet come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 7:34; John 8:24; 1 Corinthians 15:17–18"> Again therefore Jesus said to them: I go, and you shall seek me, and you shall die in your sin. Whither I go, you cannot come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 8:52; Psalms 22:6; John 8:48"> The Jews therefore said: Will he kill himself, because he said: Whither I go, you cannot come?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 3:31; John 17:14; John 17:16"> And he said to them: You are from beneath, I am from above. You are of this world, I am not of this world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 3:36; Mark 16:16; John 3:18"> Therefore I said to you, that you shall die in your sins. For if you believe not that I am he, you shall die in your sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 5:17–29; Luke 22:67; John 1:22"> They said therefore to him: Who art thou? Jesus said to them: The beginning, who also speak unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 15:15; John 12:47–50; John 8:40"> Many things I have to speak and to judge of you. But he that sent me, is true: and the things I have heard of him, these same I speak in the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9; John 8:43; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4"> And they understood not, that he called God his Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 5:19; John 5:30; John 6:38"> Jesus therefore said to them: When you shall have lifted up the Son of man, then shall you know, that I am he, and that I do nothing of myself, but as the Father hath taught me, these things I speak:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 6:38; John 16:32; John 4:34"> And he that sent me, is with me, and he hath not left me alone: for I do always the things that please him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 7:31; John 2:23; John 11:45"> When he spoke these things, many believed in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 John 1:9; John 15:4–9; 2 Timothy 3:14"> Then Jesus said to those Jews, who believed him: If you continue in my word, you shall be my disciples indeed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 8:2; John 8:36; John 17:17"> And you shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 3:9; John 8:39; Genesis 15:13"> They answered him: We are the seed of Abraham, and we have never been slaves to any man: how sayest thou: you shall be free?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Romans 6:16; 2 Peter 2:19; Proverbs 5:22"> Jesus answered them: Amen, amen I say unto you: that whosoever committeth sin, is the servant of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Galatians 4:4–7; Luke 15:31; Romans 8:15–17"> Now the servant abideth not in the house for ever; but the son abideth for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:17; Galatians 5:1; Romans 8:2"> If therefore the son shall make you free, you shall be free indeed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; John 8:45–47; John 7:1"> I know that you are the children of Abraham: but you seek to kill me, because my word hath no place in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 5:19; John 8:44; John 8:41"> I speak that which I have seen with my Father: and you do the things that you have seen with your father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Galatians 3:7; John 8:37; Romans 9:7"> They answered, and said to him: Abraham is our father. Jesus saith to them: If you be the children of Abraham, do the works of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 8:26; Galatians 4:29; Galatians 4:16"> But now you seek to kill me, a man who have spoken the truth to you, which I have heard of God. This Abraham did not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; John 8:38; Isaiah 63:16"> You do the works of your father. They said therefore to him: We are not born of fornication: we have one Father, even God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 12:49; John 14:10; John 17:8"> Jesus therefore said to them: If God were your Father, you would indeed love me. For from God I proceeded, and came; for I came not of myself, but he sent me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 12:39–40; Romans 8:7–8; John 7:17"> Why do you not know my speech? Because you cannot hear my word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Acts 13:10; 1 John 3:12; 1 John 3:8–10"> You are of your father the devil, and the desires of your father you will do. He was a murderer from the beginning, and he stood not in the truth; because truth is not in him. When he speaketh a lie, he speaketh of his own: for he is a liar, and the father thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3–4; Galatians 4:16; John 18:37"> But if I say the truth, you believe me not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; John 16:8; 1 Peter 2:22"> Which of you shall convince me of sin? If I say the truth to you, why do you not believe me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 10:26–27; 2 John 1:9; John 18:37"> He that is of God, heareth the words of God. Therefore you hear them not, because you are not of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 7:20; John 8:52; Matthew 10:5"> The Jews therefore answered, and said to him: Do not we say well that thou art a Samaritan, and hast a devil?</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Peter 2:23; Isaiah 49:3; John 13:31–32"> Jesus answered: I have not a devil: but I honour my Father, and you have dishonoured me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 5:41; John 8:54; John 7:18"> But I seek not my own glory: there is one that seeketh and judgeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="John 11:25–26; John 5:24; John 3:15–16"> Amen, amen I say to you: If any man keep my word, he shall not see death for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 9:24; John 8:48; Hebrews 2:9"> The Jews therefore said: Now we know that thou hast a devil. Abraham is dead, and the prophets; and thou sayest: If any man keep my word, he shall not taste death for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="John 4:12; Romans 9:5; Hebrews 7:1–7"> Art thou greater than our father Abraham, who is dead? and the prophets are dead. Whom dost thou make thyself?</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 8:50; Proverbs 25:27; 2 Peter 1:17"> Jesus answered: If I glorify myself, my glory is nothing. It is my Father that glorifieth me, of whom you say that he is your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="John 8:19; John 8:44; John 7:28–29"> And you have not known him, but I know him. And if I shall say that I know him not, I shall be like to you, a liar. But I do know him, and do keep his word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Hebrews 11:13; Genesis 22:18; Galatians 3:7–9"> Abraham your father rejoiced that he might see my day: he saw it, and was glad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> The Jews therefore said to him: Thou art not yet fifty years old, and hast thou seen Abraham?</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Exodus 3:14; Colossians 1:17; John 17:5"> Jesus said to them: Amen, amen I say to you, before Abraham was made, I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="John 11:8; John 10:30–33; John 10:39–40"> They took up stones therefore to cast at him. But Jesus hid himself, and went out of the temple.</span>
