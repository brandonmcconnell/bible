<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 12:23; 1 John 4:19; John 13:34"> Before the festival day of the pasch, Jesus knowing that his hour was come, that he should pass out of this world to the Father: having loved his own who were in the world, he loved them unto the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 1:13–17; Acts 5:3; John 13:26–27"> And when supper was done, (the devil having now put into the heart of Judas Iscariot, the son of Simon, to betray him,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 3:35; Matthew 11:27; Hebrews 1:2"> Knowing that the Father had given him all things into his hands, and that he came from God, and goeth to God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 22:27; 2 Corinthians 8:9; Luke 12:37"> He riseth from supper, and layeth aside his garments, and having taken a towel, girded himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 7:44; Psalms 51:2; Genesis 18:4"> After that, he putteth water into a basin, and began to wash the feet of the disciples, and to wipe them with the towel wherewith he was girded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 3:11–14; John 1:27; Luke 5:8"> He cometh therefore to Simon Peter. And Peter saith to him: Lord, dost thou wash my feet?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 12:8; John 12:16; John 14:26"> Jesus answered, and said to him: What I do thou knowest not now; but thou shalt know hereafter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:25; Acts 22:16; Titus 3:5"> Peter saith to him: Thou shalt never wash my feet. Jesus answered him: If I wash thee not, thou shalt have no part with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 51:7; Psalms 51:2; 1 Peter 3:21"> Simon Peter saith to him: Lord, not only my feet, but also my hands and my head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 15:3; 2 Corinthians 7:1; 2 Corinthians 5:17"> Jesus saith to him: He that is washed, needeth not but to wash his feet, but is clean wholly. And you are clean, but not all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 13:26; John 13:2; John 13:21"> For he knew who he was that would betray him; therefore he said: You are not all clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 13:7; John 13:4; Ezekiel 24:24"> Then after he had washed their feet, and taken his garments, being set down again, he said to them: Know you what I have done to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 6:46; 1 Corinthians 12:3; John 11:28"> You call me Master, and Lord; and you say well, for so I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; Acts 20:35; Romans 12:16"> If then I being your Lord and Master, have washed your feet; you also ought to wash one another’s feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 2:6; Matthew 11:29; 1 Peter 3:17–18"> For I have given you an example, that as I have done to you, so you do also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 6:40; John 15:20; Matthew 10:24–25"> Amen, amen I say to you: The servant is not greater than his lord; neither is the apostle greater than he that sent him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 11:28; James 4:17; James 1:25"> If you know these things, you shall be blessed if you do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; Matthew 26:23; John 17:12"> I speak not of you all: I know whom I have chosen. But that the scripture may be fulfilled: He that eateth bread with me, shall lift up his heel against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 43:10; John 14:29; John 16:4"> At present I tell you, before it come to pass: that when it shall come to pass, you may believe that I am he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:16; Luke 9:48; Matthew 25:40"> Amen, amen I say to you, he that receiveth whomsoever I send, receiveth me; and he that receiveth me, receiveth him that sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 13:18; Matthew 26:21; Mark 14:18"> When Jesus had said these things, he was troubled in spirit; and he testified, and said: Amen, amen I say to you, one of you shall betray me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 22:23; Matthew 26:22; Mark 14:19"> The disciples therefore looked one upon another, doubting of whom he spoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 19:26; John 21:7; John 21:20"> Now there was leaning on Jesus’ bosom one of his disciples, whom Jesus loved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 12:17; Luke 1:22; Acts 13:16"> Simon Peter therefore beckoned to him, and said to him: Who is it of whom he speaketh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 21:20; Genesis 44:4–12; Esther 7:5"> He therefore, leaning on the breast of Jesus, saith to him: Lord, who is it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 26:23; John 13:30; Mark 14:19–20"> Jesus answered: He it is to whom I shall reach bread dipped. And when he had dipped the bread, he gave it to Judas Iscariot, the son of Simon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 22:3; John 13:2; Ecclesiastes 9:3"> And after the morsel, Satan entered into him. And Jesus said to him: That which thou dost, do quickly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Now no man at the table knew to what purpose he said this unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 12:5–6; John 13:1; Ephesians 4:28"> For some thought, because Judas had the purse, that Jesus had said to him: Buy those things which we have need of for the festival day: or that he should give something to the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Job 24:13–15; Proverbs 4:16"> He therefore having received the morsel, went out immediately. And it was night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 14:13; 1 Peter 4:11; John 7:39"> When he therefore was gone out, Jesus said: Now is the Son of man glorified, and God is glorified in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; Isaiah 53:10–12; John 17:4–6"> If God be glorified in him, God also will glorify him in himself; and immediately will he glorify him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 7:33–34; John 8:21–24; John 16:16–22"> Little children, yet a little while I am with you. You shall seek me; and as I said to the Jews: Whither I go you cannot come; so I say to you now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Leviticus 19:18; John 15:17"> A new commandment I give unto you: That you love one another, as I have loved you, that you also love one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 4:20–21; 1 John 3:10–14; 1 John 2:10"> By this shall all men know that you are my disciples, if you have love one for another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Peter 1:14; John 13:33; John 21:21–22"> Simon Peter saith to him: Lord, whither goest thou? Jesus answered: Whither I go, thou canst not follow me now; but thou shalt follow hereafter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 21:13; Acts 20:24; Mark 14:27–31"> Peter saith to him: Why cannot I follow thee now? I will lay down my life for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 22:34; Matthew 26:34; Mark 14:30"> Jesus answered him: Wilt thou lay down thy life for me? Amen, amen I say to thee, the cock shall not crow, till thou deny me thrice.</span>
