<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 10:38–42; John 12:17; 2 Kings 20:1–12"> Now there was a certain man sick, named Lazarus, of Bethania, of the town of Mary and Martha her sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 12:3; Mark 14:3; Matthew 26:6–7"> (And Mary was she that anointed the Lord with ointment, and wiped his feet with her hair: whose brother Lazarus was sick.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:5; Revelation 3:19; 2 Timothy 4:20"> His sisters therefore sent to him, saying: Lord, behold, he whom thou lovest is sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 11:40; John 9:3; 1 Peter 4:14"> And Jesus hearing it, said to them: This sickness is not unto death, but for the glory of God: that the Son of God may be glorified by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:9–13; John 16:27; John 17:26"> Now Jesus loved Martha, and her sister Mary, and Lazarus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 30:18; Isaiah 55:8–9; Genesis 45:1–5"> When he had heard therefore that he was sick, he still remained in the same place two days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 10:40–42; Luke 9:51; Acts 20:22–24"> Then after that, he said to his disciples: Let us go into Judea again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:31; John 8:59; Acts 21:12–13"> The disciples say to him: Rabbi, the Jews but now sought to stone thee: and goest thou thither again?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 9:4; John 12:35; Jeremiah 31:9"> Jesus answered: Are there not twelve hours of the day? If a man walk in the day, he stumbleth not, because he seeth the light of this world:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 2:10–11; Proverbs 4:18–19; Jeremiah 13:16"> But if he walk in the night, he stumbleth, because the light is not in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; John 11:13; John 11:43–44"> These things he said; and after that he said to them: Lazarus our friend sleepeth; but I go that I may awake him out of sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> His disciples therefore said: Lord, if he sleep, he shall do well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 9:24"> But Jesus spoke of his death; and they thought that he spoke of the repose of sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 16:25; John 16:29; John 10:24"> Then therefore Jesus said to them plainly: Lazarus is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:10; John 14:10–11; Isaiah 54:15"> And I am glad, for your sakes, that I was not there, that you may believe: but let us go to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 13:37; Matthew 10:3; John 21:2"> Thomas therefore, who is called Didymus, said to his fellow disciples: Let us also go, that we may die with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 11:39; Hosea 6:2; Acts 2:27–31"> Jesus therefore came, and found that he had been four days already in the grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 14:20; Revelation 21:16; John 6:19"> (Now Bethania was near Jerusalem, about fifteen furlongs off.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 2:11; John 11:31; 1 Thessalonians 5:11"> And many of the Jews were come to Martha and Mary, to comfort them concerning their brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:38–42; 1 Thessalonians 4:17; Matthew 25:1"> Martha therefore, as soon as she heard that Jesus had come, went to meet him: but Mary sat at home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 11:32; 1 Kings 17:18; Matthew 9:18"> Martha therefore said to Jesus: Lord, if thou hadst been here, my brother had not died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 9:31; John 11:41–42; Matthew 28:18"> But now also I know that whatsoever thou wilt ask of God, God will give it thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 11:43–44"> Jesus saith to her: Thy brother shall rise again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 12:2–3; Acts 24:15; John 5:28–29"> Martha saith to him: I know that he shall rise again, in the resurrection at the last day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:14; John 3:36; 1 John 5:11–12"> Jesus said to her: I am the resurrection and the life: he that believeth in me, although he be dead, shall live:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 10:28; John 5:24; Romans 8:13"> And every one that liveth, and believeth in me, shall not die for ever. Believest thou this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 16:16; John 6:69; John 6:14"> She saith to him: Yea, Lord, I have believed that thou art Christ the Son of the living God, who art come into this world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 11:20; John 13:13; Mark 10:49"> And when she had said these things, she went, and called her sister Mary secretly, saying: The master is come, and calleth for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 15:23; Psalms 27:8; Song of Solomon 3:1–4"> She, as soon as she heard this, riseth quickly, and cometh to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 11:20"> For Jesus was not yet come into the town: but he was still in that place where Martha had met him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 11:19; Genesis 37:35; 2 Samuel 12:16–18"> The Jews therefore, who were with her in the house, and comforted her, when they saw Mary that she rose up speedily and went out, followed her, saying: She goeth to the grave to weep there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 11:21; Luke 17:16; Luke 5:8"> When Mary therefore was come where Jesus was, seeing him, she fell down at his feet, and saith to him: Lord, if thou hadst been here, my brother had not died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 11:38; John 12:27; Mark 3:5"> Jesus, therefore, when he saw her weeping, and the Jews that were come with her, weeping, groaned in the spirit, and troubled himself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 15:47; Matthew 28:6; John 1:39"> And said: Where have you laid him? They say to him: Lord, come and see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 19:41; Hebrews 4:15; John 11:33"> And Jesus wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 11:3; 1 John 3:1; 1 John 4:9–10"> The Jews therefore said: Behold how he loved him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 9:6–7; John 11:32; Luke 23:39"> But some of them said: Could not he that opened the eyes of the man born blind, have caused that this man should not die?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 11:33; Luke 24:2; Matthew 27:60"> Jesus therefore again groaning in himself, cometh to the sepulchre. Now it was a cave; and a stone was laid over it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 11:17; Mark 16:3; Psalms 49:7"> Jesus saith: Take away the stone. Martha, the sister of him that was dead, saith to him: Lord, by this time he stinketh, for he is now of four days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 11:4; John 11:23–26; Romans 4:17–25"> Jesus saith to her: Did not I say to thee, that if thou believe, thou shalt see the glory of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 11:25; John 17:1; Psalms 123:1"> They took therefore the stone away. And Jesus lifting up his eyes said: Father, I give thee thanks that thou hast heard me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 3:17; John 17:21; 1 John 4:14"> And I knew that thou hearest me always; but because of the people who stand about have I said it, that they may believe that thou hast sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Kings 4:33–36; Acts 9:40; Acts 9:34"> When he had said these things, he cried with a loud voice: Lazarus, come forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 5:25; John 20:7; John 11:25–26"> And presently he that had been dead came forth, bound feet and hands with winding bands; and his face was bound about with a napkin. Jesus said to them: Loose him, and let him go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 2:23; John 11:19; John 10:41"> Many therefore of the Jews, who were come to Mary and Martha, and had seen the things that Jesus did, believed in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 9:13; Acts 5:25; John 12:37"> But some of them went to the Pharisees, and told them the things that Jesus had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; John 12:19; Acts 4:5–6"> The chief priests therefore, and the Pharisees, gathered a council, and said: What do we, for this man doth many miracles?</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:15–16; Matthew 23:35–38; Matthew 27:25"> If we let him alone so, all will believe in him; and the Romans will come, and take away our place and nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; John 11:51; John 18:13–14"> But one of them, named Caiphas, being the high priest that year, said to them: You know nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 18:14; Luke 24:46; Romans 3:8"> Neither do you consider that it is expedient for you that one man should die for the people, and that the whole nation perish not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Daniel 9:26; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Exodus 28:30"> And this he spoke not of himself: but being the high priest of that year, he prophesied that Jesus should die for the nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 10:16; Isaiah 49:6; 1 John 2:2"> And not only for the nation, but to gather together in one the children of God, that were dispersed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Acts 5:33; Acts 9:23; Mark 14:1"> From that day therefore they devised to put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 7:1; John 7:4; 2 Chronicles 13:19"> Wherefore Jesus walked no more openly among the Jews; but he went into a country near the desert, unto a city that is called Ephrem, and there he abode with his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:17–20; Hebrews 9:13–14; John 6:4"> And the pasch of the Jews was at hand; and many from the country went up to Jerusalem, before the pasch to purify themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="John 7:11; John 11:7–8"> They sought therefore for Jesus; and they discoursed one with another, standing in the temple: What think you that he is not come to the festival day?</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="John 9:22; John 8:59; John 5:16–18"> And the chief priests and Pharisees had given a commandment, that if any man knew where he was, he should tell, that they might apprehend him.</span>
