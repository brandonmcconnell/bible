<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 5:16–18; John 4:3; Matthew 21:38"> After these things Jesus walked in Galilee; for he would not walk in Judea, because the Jews sought to kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 14:16–19; Deuteronomy 16:13–16; Exodus 23:16–17"> Now the Jews’ feast of tabernacles was at hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 12:46; John 7:5; Luke 8:19"> And his brethren said to him: Pass from hence, and go into Judea; that thy disciples also may see thy works which thou dost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 18:20; Proverbs 18:1–2; Matthew 23:5"> For there is no man that doth any thing in secret, and he himself seeketh to be known openly. If thou do these things, manifest thyself to the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 3:21; John 1:11–13; Micah 7:5–6"> For neither did his brethren believe in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 7:8; John 2:4; John 7:30"> Then Jesus said to them: My time is not yet come; but your time is always ready.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:18–19; John 3:19; Galatians 4:16"> The world cannot hate you; but me it hateth: because I give testimony of it, that the works thereof are evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 7:6; John 8:20; John 7:30"> Go you up to this festival day, but I go not up to this festival day: because my time is not accomplished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> When he had said these things, he himself stayed in Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 5:13; Matthew 10:16; Isaiah 42:2–3"> But after his brethren were gone up, then he also went up to the feast, not openly, but, as it were, in secret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 11:56"> The Jews therefore sought him on the festival day, and said: Where is he?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 7:40–43; John 9:16; John 7:32"> And there was much murmuring among the multitude concerning him. For some said: He is a good man. And others said: No, but he seduceth the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 9:22; John 19:38; John 20:19"> Yet no man spoke openly of him, for fear of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 7:28; Luke 19:47; John 7:37"> Now about the midst of the feast, Jesus went up into the temple, and taught.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 4:22; Matthew 7:28–29; Matthew 13:54"> And the Jews wondered, saying: How doth this man know letters, having never learned?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 14:10; John 14:24; John 17:8"> Jesus answered them, and said: My doctrine is not mine, but his that sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 8:31–32; John 8:43; Jeremiah 31:33–34"> If any man do the will of him; he shall know of the doctrine, whether it be of God, or whether I speak of myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 5:41; 1 Corinthians 10:31–33; John 13:31–32"> He that speaketh of himself, seeketh his own glory: but he that seeketh the glory of him that sent him, he is true, and there is no injustice in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 1:17; John 11:53; Matthew 12:14"> Did Moses not give you the law, and yet none of you keepeth the law?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 10:20; John 8:48; John 8:52"> Why seek you to kill me? The multitude answered, and said: Thou hast a devil; who seeketh to kill thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 7:23; John 5:2–11"> Jesus answered, and said to them: One work I have done; and you all wonder:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 12:3; Genesis 17:10–14; Romans 4:9–11"> Therefore, Moses gave you circumcision (not because it is of Moses, but of the fathers;) and on the sabbath day you circumcise a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 12:5; John 5:14–16; Matthew 12:2"> If a man receive circumcision on the sabbath day, that the law of Moses may not be broken; are you angry at me because I have healed the whole man on the sabbath day?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 11:3–4; James 2:9; John 8:15"> Judge not according to the appearance, but judge just judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 7:10–11"> Some therefore of Jerusalem said: Is not this he whom they seek to kill?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7–8; John 7:48; John 12:42"> And behold, he speaketh openly, and they say nothing to him. Have the rulers known for a truth, that this is the Christ?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Micah 5:2; John 7:41–42; John 6:42"> But we know this man, whence he is: but when the Christ cometh, no man knoweth whence he is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 8:42; John 8:19; John 8:26"> Jesus therefore cried out in the temple, teaching, and saying: You both know me, and you know whence I am: and I am not come of myself; but he that sent me, is true, whom you know not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:55; Matthew 11:27; John 17:18"> I know him, because I am from him, and he hath sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 8:20; John 10:39; John 7:6"> They sought therefore to apprehend him: and no man laid hands on him, because his hour was not yet come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 12:42; Matthew 12:23; John 10:41–42"> But of the people many believed in him, and said: When the Christ cometh, shall he do more miracles, than these which this man doth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 18:3; Luke 22:52–53; John 11:47–48"> The Pharisees heard the people murmuring these things concerning him: and the rulers and Pharisees sent ministers to apprehend him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 16:5; John 13:33; Mark 16:19"> Jesus therefore said to them: Yet a little while I am with you: and then I go to him that sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24–31; John 13:33–36; John 14:6"> You shall seek me, and shall not find me: and where I am, thither you cannot come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="James 1:1; Isaiah 11:12; Zephaniah 3:10"> The Jews therefore said among themselves: Whither will he go, that we shall not find him? will he go unto the dispersed among the Gentiles, and teach the Gentiles?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 3:9; John 7:34; John 12:34"> What is this saying that he hath said: You shall seek me, and shall not find me; and where I am, you cannot come?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 55:1; John 4:14; John 6:35"> And on the last, and great day of the festivity, Jesus stood and cried, saying: If any man thirst, let him come to me, and drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 4:14; Isaiah 44:3; Isaiah 58:11"> He that believeth in me, as the scripture saith, Out of his belly shall flow rivers of living water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 2:17; Joel 2:28; John 14:16–17"> Now this he said of the Spirit which they should receive, who believed in him: for as yet the Spirit was not given, because Jesus was not yet glorified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 21:11; John 1:21; John 6:14"> Of that multitude therefore, when they had heard these words of his, some said: This is the prophet indeed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 7:52; John 1:46; John 1:49"> Others said: This is the Christ. But some said: Doth the Christ come out of Galilee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Matthew 2:5; Luke 2:4"> Doth not the scripture say: That Christ cometh of the seed of David, and from Bethlehem the town where David was?</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 9:16; John 10:19; John 7:12"> So there arose a dissension among the people because of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 7:30; Acts 23:11; John 8:20"> And some of them would have apprehended him: but no man laid hands on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 7:32; Acts 5:21–27"> The ministers therefore came to the chief priests and the Pharisees. And they said to them: Why have you not brought him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Matthew 7:28–29; Luke 4:22; John 7:26"> The ministers answered: Never did man speak like this man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 7:12; 2 Corinthians 6:8; 2 Kings 18:32"> The Pharisees therefore answered them: Are you also seduced?</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 12:42; 1 Corinthians 1:20; 1 Corinthians 2:8"> Hath any one of the rulers believed in him, or of the Pharisees?</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:20–21; John 9:40; John 9:34"> But this multitude, that knoweth not the law, are accursed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 19:39; John 3:1–2"> Nicodemus said to them, (he that came to him by night, who was one of them:)</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Proverbs 18:13; Deuteronomy 1:17; Deuteronomy 19:15–19"> Doth our law judge any man, unless it first hear him, and know what he doth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 7:41; John 1:46; Isaiah 9:1–2"> They answered, and said to him: Art thou also a Galilean? Search the scriptures, and see, that out of Galilee a prophet riseth not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Psalms 33:10; Job 5:12–13; Psalms 76:10"> And every man returned to his own house.</span>
