<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 14:32; Matthew 26:36; 2 Samuel 15:23"> When Jesus had said these things, he went forth with his disciples over the brook Cedron, where there was a garden, into which he entered with his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 21:37; Luke 22:39; Mark 11:11–12"> And Judas also, who betrayed him, knew the place; because Jesus had often resorted thither together with his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 18:12; Luke 22:47–53; Psalms 22:12"> Judas therefore having received a band of soldiers and servants from the chief priests and the Pharisees, cometh thither with lanterns and torches and weapons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 13:1; John 18:7; Psalms 27:3"> Jesus therefore, knowing all things that should come upon him, went forth, and said to them: Whom seek ye?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 21:11; John 19:19; Matthew 2:23"> They answered him: Jesus of Nazareth. Jesus saith to them: I am he. And Judas also, who betrayed him, stood with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 27:2; Psalms 70:2–3; 2 Kings 1:9–15"> As soon therefore as he had said to them: I am he; they went backward, and fell to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 18:4"> Again therefore he asked them: Whom seek ye? And they said, Jesus of Nazareth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:28; Matthew 26:56; 2 Corinthians 12:9"> Jesus answered, I have told you that I am he. If therefore you seek me, let these go their way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 17:12"> That the word might be fulfilled which he said: Of them whom thou hast given me, I have not lost any one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 14:47; Matthew 26:51–54; Luke 22:49–51"> Then Simon Peter, having a sword, drew it, and struck the servant of the high priest, and cut off his right ear. And the name of the servant was Malchus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 20:22; Matthew 26:39; John 20:17"> Jesus therefore said to Peter: Put up thy sword into the scabbard. The chalice which my Father hath given me, shall I not drink it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 26:57; John 18:3; Acts 21:37"> Then the band and the tribune, and the servants of the Jews, took Jesus, and bound him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 18:24; John 11:51; Matthew 26:3"> And they led him away to Annas first, for he was father in law to Caiphas, who was the high priest of that year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 11:49–52"> Now Caiphas was he who had given the counsel to the Jews: That it was expedient that one man should die for the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 22:54; Mark 14:54; Matthew 26:58–68"> And Simon Peter followed Jesus, and so did another disciple. And that disciple was known to the high priest, and went in with Jesus into the court of the high priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 26:69; Mark 14:66–68; Luke 22:55–57"> But Peter stood at the door without. The other disciple therefore, who was known to the high priest, went out, and spoke to the portress, and brought in Peter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 26:69–70; John 21:15; Luke 22:56–57"> The maid therefore that was portress, saith to Peter: Art not thou also one of this man’s disciples? He saith: I am not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 14:54; John 18:25; Mark 14:67"> Now the servants and ministers stood at a fire of coals, because it was cold, and warmed themselves. And with them was Peter also, standing, and warming himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 14:55–65; Luke 20:20; Luke 22:63–71"> The high priest therefore asked Jesus of his disciples, and of his doctrine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:26; Isaiah 48:16; Matthew 4:23"> Jesus answered him: I have spoken openly to the world: I have always taught in the synagogue, and in the temple, whither all the Jews resort; and in secret I have spoken nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 26:59–60; Acts 24:12–13; Luke 22:67"> Why asketh thou me? ask them who have heard what I have spoken unto them: behold they know what things I have said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 19:3; Mark 14:65; Luke 22:63–64"> And when he had said these things, one of the servants standing by, gave Jesus a blow, saying: Answerest thou the high priest so?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Peter 2:20–23; 2 Corinthians 10:1; Matthew 5:39"> Jesus answered him: If I have spoken evil, give testimony of the evil; but if well, why strikest thou me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 18:13; Matthew 26:57"> And Annas sent him bound to Caiphas the high priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 14:67–72; John 18:17–18; Genesis 18:15"> And Simon Peter was standing, and warming himself. They said therefore to him: Art not thou also one of his disciples? He denied it, and said: I am not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 18:1; John 18:10; Matthew 26:73"> One of the servants of the high priest (a kinsman to him whose ear Peter cut off) saith to him: Did I not see thee in the garden with him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 13:38; Mark 14:71–72; Luke 22:60–62"> Again therefore Peter denied; and immediately the cock crew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 18:33; John 19:9; Matthew 27:27"> Then they led Jesus from Caiphas to the governor’s hall. And it was morning; and they went not into the hall, that they might not be defiled, but that they might eat the pasch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 27:23; Mark 15:2–5; Matthew 27:11–14"> Pilate therefore went out to them, and said: What accusation bring you against this man?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 3:13; Luke 20:19–26; Mark 10:33"> They answered, and said to him: If he were not a malefactor, we would not have delivered him up to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 19:6–7; John 19:15; Acts 25:18–20"> Pilate therefore said to them: Take him you, and judge him according to your law. The Jews therefore said to him: It is not lawful for us to put any man to death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 3:14; Matthew 20:19; Matthew 26:2"> That the word of Jesus might be fulfilled, which he said, signifying what death he should die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 23:3–4; John 18:37; John 19:3"> Pilate therefore went into the hall again, and called Jesus, and said to him: Art thou the king of the Jews?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Jesus answered: Sayest thou this thing of thyself, or have others told it thee of me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 18:14–16; Nehemiah 4:2; Romans 3:1–2"> Pilate answered: Am I a Jew? Thy own nation, and the chief priests, have delivered thee up to me: what hast thou done?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 17:20–21; Colossians 1:12–14; Romans 14:17"> Jesus answered: My kingdom is not of this world. If my kingdom were of this world, my servants would certainly strive that I should not be delivered to the Jews: but now my kingdom is not from hence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 John 4:6; John 8:47; John 8:14"> Pilate therefore said to him: Art thou a king then? Jesus answered: Thou sayest that I am a king. For this was I born, and for this came I into the world; that I should give testimony to the truth. Every one that is of the truth, heareth my voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 19:4; Luke 23:4; John 19:6"> Pilate saith to him: What is truth? And when he said this, he went out again to the Jews, and saith to them: I find no cause in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 27:15–18; Luke 23:16–23; Mark 15:6–14"> But you have a custom that I should release one unto you at the pasch: will you, therefore, that I release unto you the king of the Jews?</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 23:25; Mark 15:15; Matthew 27:16"> Then cried they all again, saying: Not this man, but Barabbas. Now Barabbas was a robber.</span>
