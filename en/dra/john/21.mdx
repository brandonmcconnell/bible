<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 6:1; John 6:23; Matthew 28:16"> After this, Jesus shewed himself again to the disciples at the sea of Tiberias. And he shewed himself after this manner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 4:21–22; John 1:45–1; John 4:46"> There were together Simon Peter, and Thomas, who is called Didymus, and Nathanael, who was of Cana of Galilee, and the sons of Zebedee, and two others of his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 5:5; Matthew 4:18–20; 2 Thessalonians 3:7–9"> Simon Peter saith to them: I go a fishing. They say to him: We also come with thee. And they went forth, and entered into the ship: and that night they caught nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 20:14; Luke 24:15–16; Luke 24:31"> But when the morning was come, Jesus stood on the shore: yet the disciples knew not that it was Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 4:19; Philippians 4:11–13; 1 John 2:13"> Jesus therefore said to them: Children, have you any meat? They answered him: No.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 5:4–7; Acts 2:41; John 2:5"> He saith to them: Cast the net on the right side of the ship, and you shall find. They cast therefore; and now they were not able to draw it, for the multitude of fishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 13:23; John 20:2; Matthew 14:28–29"> That disciple therefore whom Jesus loved, said to Peter: It is the Lord. Simon Peter, when he heard that it was the Lord, girt his coat about him, (for he was naked,) and cast himself into the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:11"> But the other disciples came in the ship, (for they were not far from the land, but as it were two hundred cubits,) dragging the net with fishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 21:13; 1 Kings 19:5–6; John 21:10"> As soon then as they came to land, they saw hot coals lying, and a fish laid thereon, and bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Jesus saith to them: Bring hither of the fishes which you have now caught.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 5:6–8; Acts 2:41"> Simon Peter went up, and drew the net to land, full of great fishes, one hundred and fifty-three. And although there were so many, the net was not broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 10:41; Luke 9:45; John 16:19"> Jesus saith to them: Come, and dine. And none of them who were at meat, durst ask him: Who art thou? knowing that it was the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 21:9; Acts 10:41; Luke 24:42–43"> And Jesus cometh and taketh bread, and giveth them, and fish in like manner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 20:26; John 20:19"> This is now the third time that Jesus was manifested to his disciples, after he was risen from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; 1 John 4:19; Romans 15:1"> When therefore they had dined, Jesus saith to Simon Peter: Simon son of John, lovest thou me more than these? He saith to him: Yea, Lord, thou knowest that I love thee. He saith to him: Feed my lambs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 20:28; 1 Peter 5:2; 1 Peter 2:25"> He saith to him again: Simon, son of John, lovest thou me? He saith to him: Yea, Lord, thou knowest that I love thee. He saith to him: Feed my lambs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 13:38; John 16:30; Mark 14:72"> He said to him the third time: Simon, son of John, lovest thou me? Peter was grieved, because he had said to him the third time: Lovest thou me? And he said to him: Lord, thou knowest all things: thou knowest that I love thee. He said to him: Feed my sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 13:36; Acts 12:3–4; Acts 21:11"> Amen, amen I say to thee, when thou wast younger, thou didst gird thyself, and didst walk where thou wouldst. But when thou shalt be old, thou shalt stretch forth thy hands, and another shall gird thee, and lead thee whither thou wouldst not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Peter 1:14; John 21:22; Matthew 10:38"> And this he said, signifying by what death he should glorify God. And when he had said this, he saith to him: Follow me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 21:7; John 20:2; John 21:24"> Peter turning about, saw that disciple whom Jesus loved following, who also leaned on his breast at supper, and said: Lord, who is he that shall betray thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 13:23–24; Acts 1:6–7; Matthew 24:3–4"> Him therefore when Peter had seen, he saith to Jesus: Lord, and what shall this man do?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 2:25; Revelation 3:11; Mark 9:1"> Jesus saith to him: So I will have him to remain till I come, what is it to thee? follow thou me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:29; Job 33:13; Daniel 4:35"> This saying therefore went abroad among the brethren, that that disciple should not die. And Jesus did not say to him: He should not die; but, So I will have him to remain till I come, what is it to thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 19:35; John 15:27; 1 John 5:6"> This is that disciple who giveth testimony of these things, and hath written these things; and we know that his testimony is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 40:5; John 20:30–31; Psalms 71:15"> But there are also many other things which Jesus did; which, if they were written every one, the world itself, I think, would not be able to contain the books that should be written.</span>
