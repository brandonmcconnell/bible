<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 3:26; John 3:22; 2 Corinthians 4:5"> When Jesus therefore understood that the Pharisees had heard that Jesus maketh more disciples, and baptizeth more than John,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 10:48; 1 Corinthians 1:13–17"> (Though Jesus himself did not baptize, but his disciples,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 10:23; John 10:40; John 2:11"> He left Judea, and went again into Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 17:11; Luke 2:49; Luke 9:51–52"> And he was of necessity to pass through Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 24:32; Genesis 33:19; Genesis 48:22"> He cometh therefore to a city of Samaria, which is called Sichar, near the land which Jacob gave to his son Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; Hebrews 2:17; Matthew 8:24"> Now Jacob’s well was there. Jesus therefore being wearied with his journey, sat thus on the well. It was about the sixth hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:42; 1 Kings 17:10; Genesis 24:43"> There cometh a woman of Samaria, to draw water. Jesus saith to her: Give me to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 9:13; John 4:39; John 4:5"> For his disciples were gone into the city to buy meats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 10:28; Matthew 10:5; 2 Kings 17:24–41"> Then that Samaritan woman saith to him: How dost thou, being a Jew, ask of me to drink, who am a Samaritan woman? For the Jews do not communicate with the Samaritans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 4:14; Revelation 7:17; John 7:37–39"> Jesus answered, and said to her: If thou didst know the gift of God, and who he is that saith to thee, Give me to drink; thou perhaps wouldst have asked of him, and he would have given thee living water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; John 3:4"> The woman saith to him: Sir, thou hast nothing wherein to draw, and the well is deep; from whence then hast thou living water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 4:6; John 8:53; Matthew 12:42"> Art thou greater than our father Jacob, who gave us the well, and drank thereof himself, and his children, and his cattle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 6:27; Isaiah 65:13–14; Luke 16:24"> Jesus answered, and said to her: Whosoever drinketh of this water, shall thirst again; but he that shall drink of the water that I will give him, shall not thirst for ever:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 7:38–39; John 6:35; Isaiah 49:10"> But the water that I will give him, shall become in him a fountain of water, springing up into life everlasting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 6:34; Romans 8:5; Psalms 4:6"> The woman saith to him: Sir, give me this water, that I may not thirst, nor come hither to draw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Revelation 2:23; John 4:18"> Jesus saith to her: Go, call thy husband, and come hither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The woman answered, and said: I have no husband. Jesus said to her: Thou hast said well, I have no husband:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:20; 1 Corinthians 7:10–11; Romans 7:3"> For thou hast had five husbands: and he whom thou now hast, is not thy husband. This thou hast said truly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 7:39; John 6:14; John 9:17"> The woman saith to him: Sir, I perceive that thou art a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 12:6–7; Deuteronomy 27:12; Deuteronomy 11:29"> Our fathers adored on this mountain, and you say, that at Jerusalem is the place where men must adore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:8; Malachi 1:11; John 4:23"> Jesus saith to her: Woman, believe me, that the hour cometh, when you shall neither on this mountain, not in Jerusalem, adore the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 17:23; Isaiah 2:3; Romans 9:4–5"> You adore that which you know not: we adore that which we know; for salvation is of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 3:3; John 5:25; Matthew 15:7–9"> But the hour cometh, and now is, when the true adorers shall adore the Father in spirit and in truth. For the Father also seeketh such to adore him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:17; Matthew 15:8–9; Philippians 3:3"> God is a spirit; and they that adore him, must adore him in spirit and in truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 1:49; Daniel 9:24–26; John 1:41–42"> The woman saith to him: I know that the Messias cometh (who is called Christ). Therefore, when he is come, he will tell us all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 8:24; Mark 14:61–62; Romans 10:20–21"> Jesus saith to her: I am he, who am speaking with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 7:39; John 4:8–9"> And immediately his disciples came; and they wondered that he talked with the woman. Yet no man said: What seekest thou? or, why talkest thou with her?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 4:7; Luke 24:33; Mark 16:8–10"> The woman therefore left her waterpot, and went her way into the city, and saith to the men there:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Revelation 22:17; 1 Corinthians 14:24–25; John 4:25"> Come, and see a man who has told me all things whatsoever I have done. Is not he the Christ?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Romans 5:20; Acts 8:5–8; Luke 17:16–18"> They went therefore out of the city, and came unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 1:38; Genesis 24:33; Acts 16:30–34"> In the mean time the disciples prayed him, saying: Rabbi, eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 23:12; Psalms 119:103; John 4:34"> But he said to them: I have meat to eat, which you know not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 16:6–11; Luke 9:45"> The disciples therefore said one to another: Hath any man brought him to eat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 6:38; John 5:30; Job 23:12"> Jesus saith to them: My meat is to do the will of him that sent me, that I may perfect his work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 10:2–3; Matthew 9:37–38; John 4:30"> Do you not say, There are yet four months, and then the harvest cometh? Behold, I say to you, lift up your eyes, and see the countries; for they are white already to harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="James 5:19–20; Romans 1:13; 1 Corinthians 3:5–9"> And he that reapeth receiveth wages, and gathereth fruit unto life everlasting: that both he that soweth, and he that reapeth, may rejoice together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Micah 6:15; Job 31:8; Luke 19:21"> For in this is the saying true: That it is one man that soweth, and it is another that reapeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 1:7; 2 Chronicles 36:15; Acts 10:42–43"> I have sent you to reap that in which you did not labour: others have laboured, and you have entered into their labours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 4:29; John 4:5; John 10:41–42"> Now of that city many of the Samaritans believed in him, for the word of the woman giving testimony: He told me all things whatsoever I have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Revelation 3:20; Acts 16:15; Luke 24:29"> So when the Samaritans were come to him, they desired that he would tarry there. And he abode there two days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:4–5; Acts 1:8; Acts 8:12"> And many more believed in him because of his own word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 John 4:14; John 1:29; 1 John 5:20"> And they said to the woman: We now believe, not for thy saying: for we ourselves have heard him, and know that this is indeed the Saviour of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 4:13; Romans 15:8; Mark 7:27–28"> Now after two days, he departed thence, and went into Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 13:57; Luke 4:24; Mark 6:4"> For Jesus himself gave testimony that a prophet hath no honour in his own country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 2:23; John 2:13–16; Deuteronomy 16:16"> And when he was come into Galilee, the Galileans received him, having seen all the things he had done at Jerusalem on the festival day; for they also went to the festival day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 2:1–11; Luke 7:2; Hosea 5:15"> He came again therefore into Cana of Galilee, where he made the water wine. And there was a certain ruler, whose son was sick at Capharnaum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 4:3; John 11:21; John 11:32"> He having heard that Jesus was come from Judea into Galilee, went to him, and prayed him to come down, and heal his son; for he was at the point of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 20:29; Hebrews 2:4; 1 Corinthians 1:22"> Jesus therefore said to him: Unless you see signs and wonders, you believe not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Mark 5:23; Psalms 88:10–12; Psalms 40:17"> The ruler saith to him: Lord, come down before that my son die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 8:13; Mark 7:29–30; Romans 4:20–21"> Jesus saith to him: Go thy way; thy son liveth. The man believed the word which Jesus said to him, and went his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="1 Kings 17:23; John 4:50; John 4:53"> And as he was going down, his servants met him; and they brought word, saying, that his son lived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> He asked therefore of them the hour wherein he grew better. And they said to him: Yesterday, at the seventh hour, the fever left him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Psalms 107:20; Luke 19:9; Acts 16:34"> The father therefore knew, that it was at the same hour that Jesus said to him, Thy son liveth; and himself believed, and his whole house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 4:45; John 2:1–11"> This is again the second miracle that Jesus did, when he was come out of Judea into Galilee.</span>
