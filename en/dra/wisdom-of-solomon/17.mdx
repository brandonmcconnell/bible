<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> For thy judgments, O Lord, are great, and thy words cannot be expressed: therefore undisciplined souls have erred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> For while the wicked thought to be able to have dominion over the holy nation, they themselves being fettered with the bonds of darkness, and a long night, shut up in their houses, lay there exiled from the eternal providence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And while they thought to lie hid in their obscure sins, they were scattered under a dark veil of forgetfulness, being horribly afraid and troubled with exceeding great astonishment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> For neither did the den that held them, keep them from fear: for noises coming down troubled them, and sad visions appearing to them, affrighted them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And no power of fire could give them light, neither could the bright flames of the stars enlighten that horrible night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> But there appeared to them a sudden fire, very dreadful: and being struck with the fear of that face, which was not seen, they thought the things which they saw to be worse:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And the delusions of their magic art were put down, and their boasting of wisdom was reproachfully rebuked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> For they who promised to drive away fears and troubles from a sick soul, were sick themselves of a fear worthy to be laughed at.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> For though no terrible thing disturbed them: yet being scared with the passing by of beasts, and hissing of serpents, they died for fear: and denying that they saw the air, which could by no means be avoided.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> For whereas wickedness is fearful, it beareth witness of its condemnation: for a troubled conscience always forecasteth grievous things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> For fear is nothing else but a yielding up of the succours from thought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And while there is less expectation from within, the greater doth it count the ignorance of that cause which bringeth the torment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> But they that during that night, in which nothing could be done, and which came upon them from the lowest and deepest hell, slept the same sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Were sometimes molested with the fear of monsters, sometimes fainted away, their soul failing them: for a sudden and unlooked for fear was come upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Moreover if any of them had fallen down, he was kept shut up in prison without irons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> For if any one were a husbandman, or a shepherd, or a labourer in the field, and was suddenly overtaken, he endured a necessity from which he could not fly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> For they were all bound together with one chain of darkness. Whether it were a whistling wind, or the melodious voice of birds, among the spreading branches of trees, or a fall of water running down with violence,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Or the mighty noise of stones tumbling down, or the running that could not be seen of beasts playing together, or the roaring voice of wild beasts, or a rebounding echo from the highest mountains: these things made them to swoon for fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> For the whole world was enlightened with a clear light, and none were hindered in their labours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> But over them only was spread a heavy night, an image of that darkness which was to come upon them. But they were to themselves more grievous than the darkness.</span>
