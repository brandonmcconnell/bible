<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> But the souls of the just are in the hand of God, and the torment of death shall not touch them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> In the sight of the unwise they seemed to die: and their departure was taken for misery:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And their going away from us, for utter destruction: but they are in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And though in the sight of men they suffered torments, their hope is full of immortality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Afflicted in few things, in many they shall be well rewarded: because God hath tried them, and found them worthy of himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> As gold in the furnace he hath proved them, and as a victim of a holocaust he hath received them, and in time there shall be respect had to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> The just shall shine, and shall run to and fro like sparks among the reeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> They shall judge nations, and rule over people, and their Lord shall reign for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> They that trust in him, shall understand the truth: and they that are faithful in love shall rest in him: for grace and peace is to his elect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> But the wicked shall be punished according to their own devices: who have neglected the just, and have revolted from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> For he that rejecteth wisdom, and discipline, is unhappy: and their hope is vain, and their labours without fruit, and their works unprofitable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Their wives are foolish, and their children wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Their offspring is cursed: for happy is the barren: and the undefiled, that hath not known bed in sin: she shall have fruit in the visitation of holy souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And the eunuch, that hath not wrought iniquity with his hands, nor thought wicked things against God: for the precious gift of faith shall be given to him, and a most acceptable lot in the temple of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> For the fruit of good labours is glorious, and the root of wisdom never faileth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> But the children of adulterers shall not come to perfection, and the seed of the unlawful bed shall be rooted out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And if they live long, they shall be nothing regarded, and their last old age shall be without honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And if they die quickly, they shall have no hope, nor speech of comfort in the day of trial.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> For dreadful are the ends of a wicked race.</span>
