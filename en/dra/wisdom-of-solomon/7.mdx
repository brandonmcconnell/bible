<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> I myself also am a mortal man, like all others, and of the race of him, that was first made of the earth, and in the womb of my mother I was fashioned to be flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> In the time of ten months I was compacted in blood, of the seed of man, and the pleasure of sleep concurring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And being born I drew in the common air, and fell upon the earth, that is made alike, and the first voice which I uttered was crying, as all others do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> I was nursed in swaddling clothes, and with great cares.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> For none of the kings had any other beginning of birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> For all men have one entrance into life, and the like going out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Wherefore I wished, and understanding was given me: and I called upon God, and the spirit of wisdom came upon me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And I preferred her before kingdoms and thrones, and esteemed riches nothing in comparison of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Neither did I compare unto her any precious stone: for all gold in comparison of her, is as a little sand, and silver in respect to her shall be counted as clay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> I loved her above health and beauty, and chose to have her instead of light: for her light cannot be put out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Now all good things came to me together with her, and innumerable riches through her hands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And I rejoiced in all these: for this wisdom went before me, and I knew not that she was the mother of them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Which I have learned without guile, and communicate without envy, and her riches I hide not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For she is an infinite treasure to men! which they that use, become the friends of God, being commended for the gift of discipline.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And God hath given to me to speak as I would, and to conceive thoughts worthy of those things that are given me: because he is the guide of wisdom, and the director of the wise:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> For in his hand are both we, and our words, and all wisdom, and the knowledge and skill of works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> For he hath given me the true knowledge of the things that are: to know the disposition of the whole world, and the virtues of the elements,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The beginning, and ending, and midst of the times, the alterations of their courses, and the changes of seasons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> The revolutions of the year, and the dispositions of the stars,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The natures of living creatures, and rage of wild beasts, the force of winds, and reasonings of men, the diversities of plants, and the virtues of roots,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And all such things as are hid and not foreseen, I have learned: for wisdom, which is the worker of all things, taught me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> For in her is the spirit of understanding: holy, one, manifold, subtile, eloquent, active, undefiled, sure, sweet, loving that which is good, quick, which nothing hindereth, beneficent,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Gentle, kind, steadfast, assured, secure, having all power, overseeing all things, and containing all spirits, intelligible, pure, subtile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> For wisdom is more active than all active things: and reacheth everywhere by reason of her purity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> For she is a vapour of the power of God, and a certain pure emanation of the glory of the almighty God: and therefore no defiled thing cometh into her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> For she is the brightness of eternal light, and the unspotted mirror of God’s majesty, and the image of his goodness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And being but one, she can do all things: and remaining in herself the same, she reneweth all things, and through nations conveyeth herself into holy souls, she maketh the friends of God and prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> For God loveth none but him that dwelleth with wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> For she is more beautiful than the sun, and above all the order of the stars: being compared with the light, she is found before it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> For after this cometh night, but no evil can overcome wisdom.</span>
