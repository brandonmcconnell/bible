<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> O how beautiful is the chaste generation with glory: for the memory thereof is immortal: because it is known both with God and with men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> When it is present, they imitate it: and they desire it when it hath withdrawn itself, and it triumpheth crowned for ever, winning the reward of undefiled conflicts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> But the multiplied brood of the wicked shall not thrive, and bastard slips shall not take deep root, nor any fast foundation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And if they flourish in branches for a time, yet standing not fast, they shall be shaken with the wind, and through the force of winds they shall be rooted out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> For the branches not being perfect, shall be broken, and their fruits shall be unprofitable, and sour to eat, and fit for nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> For the children that are born of unlawful beds, are witnesses of wickedness against their parents in their trial.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> But the just man, if he be prevented with death, shall be in rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> For venerable old age is not that of long time, nor counted by the number of years: but the understanding of a man is grey hairs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And a spotless life is old age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> He pleased God and was beloved, and living among sinners he was translated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> He was taken away lest wickedness should alter his understanding, or deceit beguile his soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> For the bewitching of vanity obscureth good things, and the wandering of concupiscence overturneth the innocent mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Being made perfect in a short space, he fulfilled a long time:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For his soul pleased God: therefore he hastened to bring him out of the midst of iniquities: but the people see this, and understand not, nor lay up such things in their hearts:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> That the grace of God, and his mercy is with his saints, and that he hath respect to his chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> But the just that is dead, condemneth the wicked that are living, and youth soon ended, the long life of the unjust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> For they shall see the end of the wise man, and shall not understand what God hath designed for him, and why the Lord hath set him in safety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> They shall see him, and shall despise him: but the Lord shall laugh them to scorn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And they shall fall after this without honour, and be a reproach among the dead for ever: for he shall burst them puffed up and speechless, and shall shake them from the foundations, and they shall be utterly laid waste: they shall be in sorrow, and their memory shall perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> They shall come with fear at the thought of their sins, and their iniquities shall stand against them to convict them.</span>
