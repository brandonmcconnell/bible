<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Love justice, you that are the judges of the earth. Think of the Lord in goodness, and seek him in simplicity of heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> For he is found by them that tempt him not: and he sheweth himself to them that have faith in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> For perverse thoughts separate from God: and his power, when it is tried, reproveth the unwise:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> For wisdom will not enter into a malicious soul, nor dwell in a body subject to sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> For the Holy Spirit of discipline will flee from the deceitful, and will withdraw himself from thoughts that are without understanding, and he shall not abide when iniquity cometh in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> For the spirit of wisdom is benevolent, and will not acquit the evil speaker from his lips: for God is witness of his reins, and he is a true searcher of his heart, and a hearer of his tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> For the spirit of the Lord hath filled the whole world: and that, which containeth all things, hath knowledge of the voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Therefore he that speaketh unjust things cannot be hid, neither shall the chastising judgment pass him by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> For inquisition shall be made into the thoughts of the ungodly: and the hearing of his words shall come to God, to the chastising of his iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> For the ear of jealousy heareth all things, and the tumult of murmuring shall not be hid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Keep yourselves therefore from murmuring, which profiteth nothing, and refrain your tongue from detraction, for an obscure speech shall not go for nought: and the mouth that belieth, killeth the soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Seek not death in the error of your life, neither procure ye destruction by the works of your hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> For God made not death, neither hath he pleasure in the destruction of the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For he created all things that they might be: and he made the nations of the earth for health: and there is no poison of destruction in them, nor kingdom of hell upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> For justice is perpetual and immortal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> But the wicked with works and words have called it to them: and esteeming it a friend have fallen away, and have made a covenant with it: because they are worthy to be of the part thereof.</span>
