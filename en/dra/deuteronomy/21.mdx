<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 26:21; Psalms 5:6; Proverbs 28:17"> Then there shall be found in the land, which the Lord thy God will give thee, the corpse of a man slain, and it is not known who is guilty of the murder,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 13:3–4; Deuteronomy 16:18–19"> Thy ancients and judges shall go out, and shall measure from the place where the body lieth the distance of every city round about:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 19:2; Matthew 11:28–30; Jeremiah 31:18"> And the ancients of that city which they shall perceive to be nearer than the rest, shall take a heifer of the herd, that hath not drawn in the yoke, nor ploughed the ground,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 2:21–24; 1 Peter 3:18"> And they shall bring her into a rough and stony valley, that never was ploughed, nor sown: and there they shall strike off the head of the heifer:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:13; Malachi 2:7; Deuteronomy 10:8"> And the priests the sons of Levi shall come, whom the Lord thy God hath chosen to minister to him, and to bless in his name, and that by their word every matter should be decided, and whatsoever is clean or unclean should be judged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 73:13; Psalms 26:6; Psalms 51:7"> And the ancients of that city shall come to the person slain, and shall wash their hands over the heifer that was killed in the valley,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 21:21–23; Job 21:31–34; 2 Samuel 16:8"> And shall say: Our hands did not shed this blood, nor did our eyes see it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jonah 1:14; 2 Kings 24:4; Ezekiel 23:24–25"> Be merciful to thy people Israel, whom thou hast redeemed, O Lord, and lay not innocent blood to their charge, in the midst of thy people Israel. And the guilt of blood shall be taken from them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:12–13; 2 Kings 10:30–31; Deuteronomy 13:18"> And thou shalt be free from the innocent’s blood, that was shed, when thou shalt have done what the Lord hath commanded thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:10–16; Joshua 21:44"> If thou go out to fight against thy enemies, and the Lord thy God deliver them into thy hand, and thou lead them away captives,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 31:10; Genesis 34:3; Genesis 12:14–15"> And seest in the number of the captives a beautiful woman, and lovest her, and wilt have her to wife,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 14:9; Numbers 6:9; Ephesians 4:22"> Thou shalt bring her into thy house: and she shall shave her hair, and pare her nails,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 45:10–11; Luke 14:26–27"> And shall put off the raiment, wherein she was taken: and shall remain in thy house, and mourn for her father and mother one month: and after that thou shalt go in unto her, and shalt sleep with her, and she shall be thy wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:29; Genesis 34:2; Deuteronomy 22:19"> Rut if afterwards she please thee not, thou shalt let her go free, but thou mayst not sell her for money nor oppress her by might because thou hast humbled her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 29:33; Genesis 29:20; Genesis 29:18"> If a man have two wives, one beloved, and the other hated, and they have had children by him, and the son of the hated be the firstborn,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:10; 1 Chronicles 5:2; 2 Chronicles 21:3"> And he meaneth to divide his substance among his sons: he may not make the son of the beloved the firstborn, and prefer him before the son of the hated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 49:3; Genesis 25:31–34; Psalms 105:36"> But he shall acknowledge the son of the hated for the firstborn, and shall give him a double portion of all he hath: for this is the first of his children, and to him are due the first birthrights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 19:3; Exodus 20:12; Amos 4:11–12"> If a man have a stubborn and unruly son, who will not hear the commandments of his father or mother, and being corrected, slighteth obedience:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 13:3; Deuteronomy 21:2; Deuteronomy 16:18"> They shall take him and bring him to the ancients of his city, and to the gate of judgment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 23:29–35; Proverbs 20:1; Proverbs 23:19–21"> And shall say to them: This our son is rebellious and stubborn, he slighteth hearing our admonitions, he giveth himself to revelling, and to debauchery and banquetings:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:19–20; Deuteronomy 13:10–11; Leviticus 24:16"> The people of the city shall stone him: and he shall die, that you may take away the evil out of the midst of you, and all Israel hearing it may be afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 19:31–38; Acts 26:31; Luke 23:33"> When a man hath committed a crime for which he is to be punished with death, and being condemned to die is hanged on a gibbet:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 3:13; John 19:31; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> His body shall not remain upon the tree, but shall be buried the same day: for he is accursed of God that hangeth on a tree: and thou shalt not defile thy land, which the Lord thy God shall give thee in possession.</span>
