<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 34:4; Exodus 37:1–9; Exodus 25:10"> And I made an ark of setim wood And when I had hewn two tables of stone like the former, I went up into the mount, having them in my hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:10; Exodus 34:28; Hebrews 12:18–19"> And he wrote in the tables, according as he had written before, the ten words, which the Lord spoke to you in the mount from the midst of the fire, when the people were assembled: and he gave them to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 34:29; Exodus 40:20; Deuteronomy 10:2"> And returning from the mount, I came down, and put the tables into the ark, that I had made, and they are there till this present, as the Lord commanded me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 33:38; Numbers 20:23–28; Numbers 33:30–33"> And the children of Israel removed their camp from Beroth of the children of Jacan into Mosera, where Aaron died and was buried, and Eleazar his son succeeded him in the priestly office.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 33:32–34"> From thence they came to Gadgad, from which place they departed, and camped in Jetebatha, in a land of waters and torrents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:5; Deuteronomy 18:5; Numbers 6:23–26"> At that time he separated the tribe of Levi, to carry the ark of the covenant of the Lord, and to stand before him in the ministry, and to bless in his name until this present day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:28; Numbers 18:20–24; Deuteronomy 18:1–2"> Wherefore Levi hath no part nor possession with his brethren: because the Lord himself is his possession, as the Lord thy God promised him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:18–19; Deuteronomy 9:25; Exodus 34:28"> And I stood in the mount, as before, forty days and nights: and the Lord heard me this time also, and would not destroy thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 33:1; Exodus 32:34"> And he said to me: Go, and walk before the people, that they may enter, and possess the land, which I swore to their fathers that I would give them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 6:8; Deuteronomy 6:5; Luke 10:27"> And now, Israel, what doth the Lord thy God require of thee, but that thou fear the Lord thy God, and walk in his ways, and love him, and serve the Lord thy God, with all thy heart, and with all thy soul:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:39; Deuteronomy 6:24; James 1:25"> And keep the commandments of the Lord, and his ceremonies, which I command thee this day, that it may be well with thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 19:5; Psalms 24:1; Psalms 115:16"> Behold heaven is the Lord’s thy God, and the heaven of heaven, the earth and all things that are therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:37; Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Romans 9:13–23"> And yet the Lord hath been closely joined to thy fathers, and loved them and chose their seed after them, that is to say, you, out of all nations, as this day it is proved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:4; Deuteronomy 30:6; Leviticus 26:41"> Circumcise therefore the foreskin of your heart, and stiffen your neck no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 10:34; Romans 2:11; Daniel 2:47"> Because the Lord your God he is the God of gods, and the Lord of lords, a great God and mighty and terrible, a who accepteth no person nor taketh bribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 146:9; Psalms 68:5; Jeremiah 49:11"> He doth judgment to the fatherless and the widow, loveth the stranger, and giveth him food and raiment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 22:21; Leviticus 19:33–34; Luke 17:18"> And do you therefore love strangers, because you also were strangers in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:13; Deuteronomy 13:4; Luke 4:8"> Thou shalt fear the Lord thy God, and serve him only: to him thou shalt adhere, and shalt swear by his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:14; Psalms 106:21–22; Exodus 15:2"> He is thy praise, and thy God, that hath done for thee these great and terrible things, which thy eyes have seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 46:27; Exodus 1:5; Genesis 15:5"> In seventy souls thy fathers went down into Egypt: and behold now the Lord thy God hath multiplied thee as the stars of heaven.</span>
