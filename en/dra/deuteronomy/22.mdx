<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 23:4–5; Ezekiel 34:16; Isaiah 58:7"> Thou shalt not pass by if thou seest thy brother’s ox, or his sheep go astray: but thou shalt bring them back to thy brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 7:12; 1 Thessalonians 4:6"> And if thy brother be not nigh, or thou know him not: thou shalt bring them to thy house, and they shall be with thee until thy brother seek them, and receive them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Thou shalt do in like manner with his ass, and with his raiment, and with every thing that is thy brother’s, which is lost: if thou find it, neglect it not as pertaining to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 6:1–2; Hebrews 12:12–13; Matthew 5:44"> If thou see thy brother’s ass or his ox to be fallen down in the way, thou shalt not slight it, but shalt lift it up with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:4–15; Deuteronomy 18:12"> A woman shall not be clothed with man’s apparel, neither shall a man use woman’s apparel: for he that doeth these things is abominable before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 22:28; Proverbs 12:10; Genesis 8:17"> If thou find as thou walkest by the way, a bird’s nest in a tree, or on the ground, and the dam sitting upon the young or upon the eggs: thou shalt not take her with her young:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Proverbs 22:4"> But shalt let her go, keeping the young which thou hast caught: that it may be well with thee, and thou mayst live a long time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 11:2; Isaiah 22:1; Exodus 21:28–36"> When thou buildest a new house, thou shalt make a battlement to the roof round about: lest blood be shed in thy house, and thou be guilty, if any one slip, and fall down headlong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 19:19; 2 Corinthians 11:3; Matthew 9:16"> Thou shalt not sow thy vineyard with divers seeds: lest both the seed which thou hast sown, and the fruit of the vineyard, be sanctified together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–16"> Thou shalt not plough with an ox and an ass together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 19:19"> Thou shalt not wear a garment that is woven of woollen and linen together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 23:5; Numbers 15:37–41"> Thou shalt make strings in the hem at the four corners of thy cloak, wherewith thou shalt be covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 29:21; Genesis 29:31; Ephesians 5:28–29"> If a man marry a wife, and afterwards hate her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:19; Proverbs 18:21; Proverbs 18:8"> And seek occasions to put her away, laying to her charge a very ill name, and say: I took this woman to wife, and going in to her, I found her not a virgin:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Her father and mother shall take her, and shall bring with them the tokens of her virginity to the ancients of the city that are in the gate:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the father shall say: I gave my daughter unto this man to wife: and because he hateth her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> He layeth to her charge a very ill name, so as to say: I found not thy daughter a virgin: and behold these are the tokens of my daughter’s virginity. And they shall spread the cloth before the ancients of the city:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 18:21"> And the ancients of that city shall take that man, and beat him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:29; Matthew 19:8–9; Deuteronomy 24:1–4"> Condemning him besides in a hundred sides of silver, which he shall give to the damsel’s father, because he hath defamed by a very ill name a virgin of Israel: and he shall have her to wife, and may not put her away all the days of his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> But if what he charged her with be true, and virginity be not found in the damsel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:5; Genesis 34:7; Judges 20:6"> They shall cast her out of the doors of her father’s house, and the men of the city shall stone her to death, and she shall die: because she hath done a wicked thing in Israel, to play the whore in her father’s house: and thou shalt take away the evil out of the midst of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 20:10; Ezekiel 23:45–47; John 8:4–5"> If a man lie with another man’s wife, they shall both die, that is to say, the adulterer and the adulteress: and thou shalt take away the evil out of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 1:18–19; Deuteronomy 20:7"> If a man have espoused a damsel that is a virgin, and some one find her in the city, and lie with her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:21–22; Deuteronomy 21:14; 1 Corinthians 5:13"> Thou shalt bring them both out to the gate of that city, and they shall be stoned: the damsel, because she cried not out, being in the city: the man, because he hath humbled his neighbour’s wife. And thou shalt take away the evil from the midst of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:14"> But if a man find a damsel that is betrothed, in the field, and taking hold of her, lie with her, he alone shall die:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:22"> The damsel shall suffer nothing, neither is she guilty of death: for as a robber riseth against his brother, and taketh away his life, so also did the damsel suffer:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:7"> She was alone in the field: she cried, and there was no man to help her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 22:16–17"> If a man find a damsel that is a virgin, who is not espoused, and taking her, lie with her, and the matter come to judgment:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:19; Deuteronomy 22:24; Deuteronomy 21:14"> He that lay with her shall give to the father of the maid fifty sides of silver, and shall have her to wife, because he hath humbled her: he may not put her away all the days of his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:20; 1 Corinthians 5:1; Leviticus 18:8"> No man shall take his father’s wife, nor remove his covering.</span>
