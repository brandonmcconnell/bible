<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 1:2; Deuteronomy 4:26; Jeremiah 22:29"> Hear, O ye heavens, the things I speak, let the earth give ear to the words of my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 55:10–11; Psalms 72:6; Micah 5:7"> Let my doctrine gather as the rain, let my speech distil as the dew, as a shower upon the herb, and as drops upon the grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:6; Psalms 150:2; Ephesians 1:19"> Because I will invoke the name of the Lord: give ye magnificence to our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 18:30–31; Psalms 92:15; Isaiah 26:4"> The works of God are perfect, and all his ways are judgments: God is faithful and without any iniquity, he is just and right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 1:4; Philippians 2:15; Matthew 17:17"> They have sinned against him, and are nose of his children in their filth: they are a wicked and perverse generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 63:16; 1 John 3:1; Isaiah 44:2"> Is this the return thou makest to the Lord, O foolish and senseless people? Is not he thy father, that hath possessed thee, and made thee, and created thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 78:3–4; Job 8:8–10; Psalms 44:1"> Remember the days of old, think upon every generation: ask thy father, and he will declare to thee: thy elders and they will tell thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 17:26; Genesis 11:8–9; Daniel 4:17"> When the Most High divided the nations: when he separated the sons of Adam, he appointed the bounds of people according to the number of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:16; 1 Peter 2:9–10; Exodus 19:5–6"> But the Lord’s portion is his people: Jacob the lot of his inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 2:8; Psalms 17:8; Hosea 13:5"> He found him in a desert land, in a place of horror, and of vast wilderness: he led him about, and taught him: and he kept him as the apple of his eye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 19:4; Isaiah 46:4; Isaiah 63:9"> As the eagle enticing her young to fly, and hovering over them, he spread his wings, and hath taken him and carried him on his shoulders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 78:52–53; Isaiah 46:4; Isaiah 44:7–8"> The Lord alone was his leader: and there was no strange god with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 58:14; Job 29:6; Psalms 81:16"> He set him upon high land: that he might eat the fruits of the fields, that he might suck honey out of the rock, and oil out of the hardest stone,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 49:11; Psalms 81:16; Psalms 147:14"> Butter of the herd, and milk of the sheep with the fat of lambs, and of the rams of the breed of Basan: and goats with the marrow of wheat, and might drink the purest blood of the grape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:20; Hosea 13:6; Deuteronomy 33:5"> The beloved grew fat, and kicked: he grew fat, and thick and gross, he forsook God who made him, and departed from God his saviour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 78:58; 1 Corinthians 10:22; Nahum 1:1–2"> They provoked him by strange gods, and stirred him up to anger, with their abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 5:8; Deuteronomy 28:64; Leviticus 17:7"> They sacrificed to devils and not to God: to gods whom they knew not: that were newly come up, whom their fathers worshipped not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 17:10; Jeremiah 2:32; Psalms 106:21"> Thou hast forsaken the God that beget thee, and hast forgotten the Lord that created thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 106:40; Psalms 5:4; Isaiah 1:2"> The Lord saw, and was moved to wrath: because his own sons and daughters provoked him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:17–18; Luke 7:31–32; Deuteronomy 32:5"> And he said: I will hide my face from them, and will consider what their last end shall be: for it is a perverse generation, and unfaithful children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 10:19; Romans 9:25; Romans 11:11–14"> They have provoked me with that which was no god, and have angered me with their vanities: and I will provoke them with that which is no people, and will vex them with a foolish nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Lamentations 4:11; Jeremiah 15:14; Micah 1:4"> A fire is kindled in my wrath, and shall burn even to the lowest hell: and shall devour the earth with her increase, and shall burn the foundations of the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:16; Psalms 7:12–13; Isaiah 24:17–18"> I will heap evils upon them, and will spend my arrows among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 26:22; Ezekiel 5:17; Isaiah 65:25"> They shall be consumed with famine, and birds shall devour them with a most bitter bite: I will send the teeth of beasts upon them, with the fury of creatures that trail upon the ground, and of serpents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:15; Lamentations 1:20; Isaiah 30:16"> Without, the sword shall lay them waste, and terror within, both the young man and the virgin, the sucking child with the man in years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:64; Leviticus 26:33; Deuteronomy 28:37"> I said: Where are they? I will make the memory of them to cease from among men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 14:15–16; Exodus 32:12; Zechariah 1:14–15"> But for the wrath of the enemies I have deferred it: lest perhaps their enemies might be proud, and should say: Our mighty hand, and not the Lord, hath done all these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:22; Isaiah 27:11; Job 28:28"> They are a nation without counsel, and without wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 81:13; Deuteronomy 5:29; Hosea 14:9"> O that they would be wise and would understand, and would provide for their last end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 26:8; Joshua 23:10; Isaiah 30:17"> How should one pursue after a thousand, and two chase ten thousand? Was it not, because their God had sold them, and the Lord had shut them up?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 14:25; 1 Samuel 2:2; Daniel 2:47"> For our God is not as their gods: our enemies themselves are judges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:18; Ezekiel 16:45–51; Isaiah 1:10"> Their vines are of the vineyard of Sodom, and of the suburbs of Gomorrha: their grapes are grapes of gall, and their clusters most bitter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 58:4; Romans 3:13; Psalms 140:3"> Their wine is the gall of dragons, and the venom of asps, which is incurable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hosea 13:12; Job 14:17; Jeremiah 2:22"> Are not these things stored up with me, and sealed up in my treasures?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Romans 12:19; Hebrews 10:30; Nahum 1:2"> Revenge is mine, and I will repay them in due time, that their foot may slide: the day of destruction is at hand, and the time makes haste to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 135:14; Psalms 106:45; Judges 2:18"> The Lord will judge his people, and will have mercy on his servants: he shall see that their hand is weakened, and that they who were shut up have also failed, and they that remained are consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Judges 10:14; 2 Kings 3:13"> And he shall say: Where are their gods, in whom they trusted?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:18–19; Psalms 50:13; Judges 10:14"> Of whose victims they ate the fat, and drank the wine of their drink offerings: let them arise and help you, and protect you in your distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:6; Hosea 6:1; Job 5:18"> See ye that I alone am, and there is no other God besides me: I will kill and I will make to live: I will strike, and I will heal, and there is none that can deliver out of my hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Hebrews 6:17–18; Genesis 14:22; Revelation 10:5–6"> I will lift up my hand to heaven, and I will say: I live for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 66:16; Isaiah 34:5–6; Isaiah 27:1"> If I shall whet my sword as the lightning, and my hand take hold on judgment: I will render vengeance to my enemies, and repay them that hate me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:23; Jeremiah 46:10; Ezekiel 38:21–22"> I will make my arrows drunk with blood, and my sword shall devour flesh, of the blood of the slain and of the captivity, of the bare head of the enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Revelation 19:2; Romans 12:19; Revelation 6:10"> Praise his people, ye nations, for he will revenge the blood of his servants: and will render vengeance to their enemies, and he will be merciful to the land of his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Numbers 13:8; Numbers 13:16; Deuteronomy 31:30"> So Moses came and spoke all the words of this canticle in the ears of the people, and Josue the son of Nun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> And he ended all these words, speaking to all Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:19; Ezekiel 40:4; Proverbs 3:1–4"> And he said to them: Set your hearts on all the words, which I testify to you this day: which you shall command your children to observe and to do, and to fulfill all that is written in this law:</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Proverbs 4:22; Leviticus 18:5; 2 Peter 1:16"> For they are not commanded you in vain, but that every one should live in them, and that doing them you may continue a long time in the land whither you are going over the Jordan to possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Numbers 27:12–13"> And the Lord spoke to Moses the same day, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Numbers 27:12; Isaiah 33:17; Deuteronomy 34:1–5"> Go up into this mountain Abarim, (that is to say, of passages,) unto mount Nebo, which is in the land of Moab over against Jericho: and see the land of Chanaan, which I will deliver to the children of Israel to possess, and die thou in the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Genesis 25:8; Numbers 33:38; Numbers 20:24–29"> When thou art gone up into it thou shalt be gathered to thy people, as Aaron thy brother died in mount Her, and was gathered to his people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 27:14; Numbers 20:11–14; Deuteronomy 3:23–27"> Because you trespassed against me in the midst of the children of Israel, at the waters of contradiction in Cades of the desert of Sin: and you did not sanctify me among the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:1–4; Deuteronomy 3:27; Hebrews 11:39"> Thou shalt see the land before thee, which I will give to the children of Israel, but thou shalt not enter into it.</span>
