<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 15:14; Luke 11:28; James 2:10"> And Moses with the ancients of Israel commanded the people, saying: Keep every commandment that I command you this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 8:31; Ezekiel 36:26; Ezekiel 11:19"> And when you are passed over the Jordan into the land which the Lord thy God will give thee, thou shalt set up great stones, and shalt plaster them over with plaster,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 8:32; Deuteronomy 26:9; 2 Corinthians 3:2–3"> That thou mayst write on them all the words of this law, when thou art passed over the Jordan: that thou mayst enter into the land which the Lord thy God will give thee, a land flowing with milk and honey, as he swore to thy fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:29–30; Joshua 8:30–33"> Therefore when you are passed over the Jordan, set up the stones which I command you this day, in mount Hebal, and thou shalt plaster them with plaster:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 20:25; Exodus 24:4; Joshua 8:30–31"> And thou shalt build there an altar to the Lord thy God, of stones which iron hath not touched,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Leviticus 1:1–17"> And of stones not fashioned nor polished: and thou shalt offer upon it holocausts to the Lord thy God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 3:1–17; Isaiah 12:3; Psalms 100:1–2"> And shalt immolate peace victims, and eat there, and feast before the Lord thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:2; John 16:25; 2 Corinthians 3:12"> And thou shalt write upon the stones all the words of this law plainly and clearly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 2:10–11; Deuteronomy 26:16–18; Ephesians 5:8–9"> And Moses and the priests of the race of Levi said to all Israel: Attend, and hear, O Israel: This day thou art made the people of the Lord thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:1; 1 Peter 1:14–16; Matthew 5:48"> Thou shalt hear his voice, and do the commandments and justices which I command thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Moses commanded the people in that day, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 9:7; Genesis 30:24; Genesis 35:18"> These shall stand upon mount Garizim to bless the people, when you are passed the Jordan: Simeon, Levi, Juda, Issachar, Joseph, and Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:4; Genesis 29:32; Genesis 30:20"> And over against them shall stand on mount Hebal to curse: Ruben, Gad, and Aser, and Zabulon, Dan, and Nephtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:11; Deuteronomy 33:9–10; Joshua 8:33"> And the Levites shall pronounce, and say to all the men of Israel with a loud voice:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 34:17; Exodus 20:4; Deuteronomy 5:8"> Cursed be the man that maketh a graven and molten thing, the abomination of the Lord, the work of the hands of artificers, and shall put it in a secret place: and all the people shall answer and say: Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 19:3; Exodus 21:17; Deuteronomy 21:18–21"> Cursed be he that honoureth not his father and mother: and all the people shall say: Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:14; Proverbs 22:28; Proverbs 23:10–11"> Cursed be he that removeth his neighbour’s landmarks: and all the people shall say: Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 19:14; Matthew 15:14; Proverbs 28:10"> Cursed be he that maketh the blind to wander out of his way: and all the people shall say: Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:18; Deuteronomy 24:17; Proverbs 17:23"> Cursed be he that perverteth the judgment of the stranger, of the fatherless and the widow: and all the people shall say: Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:30; Leviticus 18:8; 1 Corinthians 5:1"> Cursed be he that lieth with his father’s wife, and uncovereth his bed: and all the people shall say: Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 18:23; Exodus 22:19; Leviticus 20:15"> Cursed be he that lieth with any beast: and all the people shall say: Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17; Leviticus 18:9; Ezekiel 22:11"> Cursed be he that lieth with his sister, the daughter of his father, or of his mother: and all the people shall say: Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 20:14; Leviticus 18:17"> Cursed be he that lieth with his mother in law: and all the people shall say: Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 35:31; Leviticus 24:17; 2 Samuel 11:15–17"> Cursed be he that secretly killeth his neighbour: and all the people shall say: Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:17; Exodus 23:7–8; Psalms 15:5"> Cursed be he that taketh gifts, to slay an innocent person: and all the people shall say: Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Romans 10:5"> Cursed be he that abideth not in the words of this law, and fulfilleth them not in work: and all the people shall say: Amen.</span>
