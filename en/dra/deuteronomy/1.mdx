<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:1; Joshua 22:7; Numbers 33:17–18"> These are the words, which Moses spoke to all Israel beyond the Jordan, in the plain wilderness, over against the Red Sea, between Pharan and Thophel and Laban and Haseroth, where there is very much gold:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 32:8; Numbers 13:26; Deuteronomy 9:23"> Eleven days’ journey from Horeb by the way of Mount Seir to Cadesbarne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 33:38; Deuteronomy 4:1–2; Numbers 20:1"> In the fortieth year, the eleventh month, the first day of the month, Moses spoke to the children of Israel all that the Lord had commanded him to say to them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 21:21–35; Joshua 12:2–6; Joshua 13:10–12"> After that he had slain Sehon king of the Amorrhites, who dwelt in Hesebon: and Og king of Basan who abode in Astaroth, and in Edrai,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:8; Deuteronomy 32:46; Deuteronomy 31:9"> Beyond the Jordan in the land of Moab. And Moses began to expound the law, and to say:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; Numbers 10:11–13; Exodus 19:1–2"> The Lord our God spoke to us in Horeb, saying: You have stayed long enough in this mountain:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:24; Joshua 10:40; Amos 2:9"> Turn you, and come to the mountain of the Amorrhites, and to the other places that are next to it, the plains and the hills and the vales towards the south, and by the sea shore, the land of the Chanaanites, and of Libanus, as far as the great river Euphrates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; Genesis 12:7; Genesis 26:3–4"> Behold, said he, I have delivered it to you: go in and possess it, concerning which the Lord swore to your fathers Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, that he would give it to them, and to their seed after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 18:18; Numbers 11:17; Numbers 11:11–14"> And I said to you at that time:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:22; Exodus 32:13; Genesis 22:17"> I alone am not able to bear you: for the Lord your God hath multiplied you, and you are this day as the stars of heaven, for multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 115:14; Genesis 22:17; Numbers 6:27"> (The Lord God of your fathers add to this number many thousands, and bless you as he hath spoken.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:5; 1 Kings 3:7–9; 2 Corinthians 2:16"> I alone am not able to bear your business, and the charge of you and your differences.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; Acts 1:21–23; Numbers 11:16–17"> Let me have from among you wise and understanding men, and such whose conversation is approved among your tribes, that I may appoint them your rulers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Then you answered me: The thing is good which thou meanest to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:18; Exodus 18:25–26; Ephesians 4:11"> And I took out of your tribes men wise and honourable, and appointed them rulers, tribunes, and centurions, and officers over fifties, and over tens, who might teach you all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 7:24; Exodus 22:21; 2 Chronicles 19:6–10"> And I commanded them, saying: Hear them, and judge that which is just: whether he be one of your country, or a stranger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Exodus 18:26; James 2:9"> There shall be no difference of persons, you shall hear the little as well as the great: neither shall you respect any man’s person, because it is the judgment of God. And if any thing seem hard to you, refer it to me, and I will hear it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; Deuteronomy 4:5; Acts 20:20"> And I commanded you all things that you were to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:2; Deuteronomy 8:15; Jeremiah 2:6"> And departing from Horeb, we passed through the terrible and vast wilderness, which you saw, by the way of the mountain of the Amorrhite, as the Lord our God had commanded us. And when we were come into Cadesbarne,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:7–8"> I said to you: You are come to the mountain of the Amorrhite, which the Lord our God will give to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Psalms 46:1; Numbers 14:8–9"> See the land which the Lord thy God giveth thee: go up and possess it, as the Lord our God hath spoken to thy fathers: fear not, nor be any way discouraged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 13:1–20"> And you came all to me, and said: Let us send men who may view the land, and bring us word what way we shall go up, and to what cities we shall go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 13:3–33"> And because the saying pleased me, I sent of you twelve men, one of every tribe:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 13:21–27; Joshua 2:1–2"> Who, when they had set forward and had gone up to the mountains, came as far as the valley of the cluster: and having viewed the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Taking of the fruits thereof, to shew its fertility, they brought them to us, and said: The land is good, which the Lord our God will give us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 14:1–4; Acts 7:51; Psalms 106:24–25"> And you would not go up, but being incredulous to the word of the Lord our God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:28; Psalms 106:25; Numbers 14:3"> You murmured in your tents, and said: The Lord hateth us, and therefore he hath brought us out of the land of Egypt, that he might deliver us into the hand of the Amorrhite, and destroy us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 13:28–33; Deuteronomy 9:1–2; Joshua 15:14"> Whither shall we go up? the messengers have terrified our hearts, saying: The multitude is very great, and taller than we: the cities are great, and walled up to the sky, we have seen the sons of the Enacims there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And I said to you: Fear not, neither be ye afraid of them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 14:14; Nehemiah 4:20; Romans 8:31"> The Lord God, who is your leader, himself will fight for you, as he did in Egypt in the sight of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 46:3–4; Acts 13:18; Hosea 11:3–4"> And in the wilderness (as thou hast seen) the Lord thy God hath carried thee, as a man is wont to carry his little son, all the way that you have come, until you came to this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 106:24; Jude 1:5; Hebrews 3:18–19"> And yet for all this you did not believe the Lord your God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 10:33; Psalms 78:14; Nehemiah 9:12"> Who went before you in the way, and marked out the place, wherein you should pitch your tents, in the night shewing you the way by fire, and in the day by the pillar of a cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 14:22–30; Psalms 95:11; Ezekiel 20:15"> And when the Lord had heard the voice of your words, he was angry and swore, and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 95:11"> Not one of the men of this wicked generation shall see the good land, which I promised with an oath to your fathers:</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Numbers 14:24; Joshua 14:6–14; Numbers 32:12"> Except Caleb the son of Jephone: for he shall see it, and to him I will give the land that he hath trodden upon, and to his children, because he hath followed the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 20:12; Deuteronomy 4:21; Deuteronomy 34:4"> Neither is his indignation against the people to be wondered at, since the Lord was angry with me also on your account, and said: Neither shalt thou go in thither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:28; Numbers 14:30; Deuteronomy 31:7–8"> But Josue the son of Nun, thy minister, he shall go in for thee: exhort and encourage him, and he shall divide the land by lot to Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 7:15–16; Numbers 14:3; Numbers 14:31"> Your children, of whom you said that they should be led away captives, and your sons who know not this day the difference of good and evil, they shall go in: and to them I will give the land, and they shall possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 14:25"> But return you and go into the wilderness by the way of the Red Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 14:39–45; Proverbs 19:3; Numbers 22:34"> And you answered me: We have sinned against the Lord: we will go up and fight, as the Lord our God hath commanded. And when you went ready armed unto the mountain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Numbers 14:41–43; Joshua 7:8–13; Isaiah 59:1–2"> The Lord said to me: Say to them: Go not up, and fight not, for I am not with you: lest you fall before your enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 14:44; Isaiah 63:10; Acts 7:51"> I spoke, and you hearkened not: but resisting the commandment of the Lord, and swelling with pride, you went up into the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 118:12; Numbers 14:45; Numbers 21:3"> And the Amorrhite that dwelt in the mountains coming out, and meeting you, chased you, as bees do: and made slaughter of you from Seir as far as Horma.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Hebrews 12:17; Psalms 78:34"> And when you returned and wept before the Lord, he heard you not, neither would he yield to; your voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Numbers 20:1; Numbers 20:22; Judges 11:16–17"> So you abode in Cadesbarne a long time.</span>
</div>
