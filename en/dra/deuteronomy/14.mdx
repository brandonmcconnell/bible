<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 21:5; Romans 9:8; Galatians 3:26"> Be ye children of the Lord your God: you shall not cut yourselves, no, make any baldness for the dead;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6; Leviticus 20:26; Exodus 19:5–6"> Because thou art a holy people to the Lord thy God: and he chose thee to be his peculiar people of all nations that are upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:14; Romans 14:14; Leviticus 20:25"> Eat not the things that are unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 11:2–45; Acts 10:14; 1 Kings 4:23"> These are the beasts that you shall eat, the ox, and the sheep, and the goat,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The hart and the roe, the buffle, the chamois, the pygarg, the wild goat, the camelopardalus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 18:1; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Psalms 1:1–2"> Every beast that divideth the hoof in two parts, and cheweth the cud, you shall eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:22–23; Titus 1:16; 2 Timothy 3:5"> But of them that chew the cud, but divide not the hoof, you shall not eat, such as the camel, the hare, and the cherogril: because they chew the cud, but divide not the hoof, they shall be unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 11:26–27; Isaiah 66:3; 2 Peter 2:22"> The swine also, because it divideth the hoof, but cheweth not the cud, shall be unclean, their flesh you shall not eat, and their carcasses you shall not touch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 11:9–12"> These shall you eat of all that abide in the waters: All that have fins and scales, you shall eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Such as are without fins and scales, you shall not eat, because they are unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> All birds that are clean you shall eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 11:13–19"> The unclean eat not: to wit, the eagle, and the grype, and the osprey,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The ringtail, and the vulture, and the kite according to their kind:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And all of the raven’s kind:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 30:29"> And the ostrich, and the owl, and the larus, and the hawk according to its kind:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The heron, and the swan, and the stork,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the cormorant, the porphyrion, and the night crow,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The bittern, and the charadrion, every one in their kind: the hoop also and the bat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philippians 3:19; Leviticus 11:20–23"> Every thing that creepeth, and hath little wings, shall be unclean, and shall not be eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> All that is clean, you shall eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 23:19; Exodus 34:26; Leviticus 22:8"> But whatsoever is dead of itself, eat not thereof. Give it to the stranger, that is within thy gates, to eat, or sell it to him: because thou art the holy people of the Lord thy God. Thou shalt not boil a kid in the milk of his dam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:37; Deuteronomy 12:17; Deuteronomy 26:12–15"> Every year thou shalt set aside the tithes of all thy fruits that the earth bringeth forth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5–7; Deuteronomy 4:10; Deuteronomy 12:17–18"> And thou shalt eat before the Lord thy God in the place which he shall choose, that his name may be called upon therein, the tithe of thy corn, and thy wine, and thy oil, and the firstborn of thy herds and thy sheep: that thou mayst learn to fear the Lord thy God at all times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:21; Deuteronomy 12:5; Deuteronomy 11:24"> But when the way and the place which the Lord thy God shall choose, are far off, and he hath blessed thee, and thou canst not carry all these things thither,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Thou shalt sell them all, and turn them into money, and shalt carry it in thy hand, and shalt go to the place which the Lord shall choose:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:7; Deuteronomy 12:7; Matthew 21:12"> And thou shalt buy with the same money whatsoever pleaseth thee, either of the herds or of sheep, wine also and strong drink, and all that thy soul desireth: and thou shalt eat before the Lord thy God, and shalt feast, thou and thy house:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 18:20; Deuteronomy 14:29; Deuteronomy 12:12"> And the Levite that is within thy gates, beware thou forsake him not, because he hath no other part in thy possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:22; Amos 4:4; Deuteronomy 26:12–15"> The third year thou shalt separate another tithe of all things that grow to thee at that time, and shalt lay it up within thy gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:10; Deuteronomy 14:27; Proverbs 19:17"> And the Levite that hath no other part nor possession with thee, and the stranger and the fatherless and the widow, that are within thy gates, shall come and shall eat and be filled: that the Lord thy God may bless thee in all the works of thy hands that thou shalt do.</span>
