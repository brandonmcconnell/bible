<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:2–3; Hebrews 8:9; Exodus 24:2–8"> These are the words of the covenant which the Lord commanded Moses to make with the children of Israel in the land of Moab: beside that covenant which he made with them in Horeb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 19:4; Exodus 8:12; Joshua 24:5–6"> And Moses called all Israel, and said to them: You have seen all the things that the Lord did before you in the land of Egypt to Pharao, and to all his servants, and to his whole land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:32–35; Nehemiah 9:9–11; Deuteronomy 7:18–19"> The great temptations, which thy eyes have seen, those mighty signs and wonders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 4:18; Isaiah 6:9–10; Proverbs 20:12"> And the Lord hath not given you a heart to understand, and eyes to see, and ears that may hear, unto this present day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:4; Nehemiah 9:21; Deuteronomy 8:2"> He hath brought you forty years through the desert: your garments are not worn out, neither are the shoes of your feet consumed with age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:3; Ephesians 5:18; 1 Corinthians 9:25"> You have not eaten bread, nor have you drunk wine or strong drink: that you might know that I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:24–17; Numbers 32:33–42; Psalms 135:10–12"> And you came to this place: sand Sehon king of Hesebon, and Og king of Basan, came out against us to fight. And we slew them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:12–13; Numbers 32:33"> And took their land, and delivered it for a possession to Ruben and Gad, and the half tribe of Manasses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 2:3; Deuteronomy 4:6; Luke 11:28"> Keep therefore the words of this covenant, and fulfill them: that you may understand all that you do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joel 2:16–17; Revelation 20:12; Deuteronomy 31:12–13"> You all stand this day before the Lord your God, your princes, and tribes, and ancients, and doctors, all the people of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 12:38; Joshua 9:21–27; Exodus 12:48–49"> Your children and your wives, and the stranger that abideth with thee in the camp, besides the hewers of wood, and them that bring water:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:12–15; 2 Kings 11:17; Deuteronomy 5:2–3"> That thou mayst pass in the covenant of the Lord thy God, and in the oath which this day the Lord thy God maketh with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 6:7; Genesis 17:7; Deuteronomy 28:9"> That he may raise thee up a people to himself, and he may be thy God as he hath spoken to thee, and as he swore to thy fathers Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Hebrews 8:7–12"> Neither with you only do I make this covenant, and confirm these oaths,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 2:39; 1 Corinthians 7:14; Jeremiah 50:5"> But with all that are present and that are absent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:19; Deuteronomy 2:9; Deuteronomy 2:4"> For you know how we dwelt in the land of Egypt, and how we have passed through the midst of nations, and passing through them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:36"> You have seen their abominations and filth, that is to say, their idols, wood and stone, silver and gold, which they worshipped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 12:15; Deuteronomy 11:16–17; Deuteronomy 13:1–15"> Lest perhaps there should be among you a man or a woman, a family or a tribe, whose heart is turned away this day from the Lord our God, to go and serve the gods of those nations: and there should be among you a root bringing forth gall and bitterness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:24; Numbers 15:30; Numbers 15:39"> And when he shall hear the words of this oath, he should bless himself in his heart saying: I shall have peace, and will walk on in the naughtiness of my heart: and the drunken may consume the thirsty,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 74:1; Psalms 79:5; Deuteronomy 9:14"> And the Lord should not forgive him: but his wrath and jealousy against that man should be exceedingly enkindled at that time, and all the curses that are written in this volume should light upon him: and the Lord should blot out his name from under heaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 25:41; Joshua 7:1–26; Ezekiel 13:9"> And utterly destroy him out of all the tribes of Israel, according to the curses that are contained in the book of this law and covenant:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:8"> And the following generation shall say, and the children that shall be born hereafter, and the strangers that shall come from afar, seeing the plagues of that land and the evils wherewith the Lord hath afflicted it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:9; Jeremiah 17:6; Isaiah 34:9"> Burning it with brimstone, and the heat of salt, so that it cannot be sown any more, nor any green thing grow therein, after the example of the destruction of Sodom and Gomorrha, Adama and Seboim, which the Lord destroyed in his wrath and indignation:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:8–9; 1 Kings 9:8–9; Romans 2:5"> And all the nations shall say: Why hath the Lord done thus to this land? what meaneth this exceeding great heat of his wrath?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 8:9; Isaiah 47:6; Jeremiah 50:7"> And they shall answer: Because they forsook the covenant of the Lord, which he made with their fathers, when he brought them out of the land of Egypt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:64; Judges 2:12–13; Judges 5:8"> And they have served strange gods, and adored them, whom they knew not, and for whom they had not been assigned:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Daniel 9:11–14; Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Deuteronomy 27:15–26"> Therefore the wrath of the Lord was kindled against this land, to bring upon it all the curses that are written in this volume:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:20; 1 Kings 14:15; Psalms 52:5"> And he hath cast them out of their land, in anger and in wrath, and in very great indignation, and hath thrown them into a strange land, as it is seen this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Daniel 2:22; Acts 1:7; Romans 11:33–34"> Secret things to the Lord our God: things that are manifest, to us and to our children for ever, that we may do all the words of this law.</span>
