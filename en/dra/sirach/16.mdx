<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Rejoice not in ungodly children, if they be multiplied: neither be delighted in them, if the fear of God be not with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Trust not to their life, and respect not their labours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> For better is one that feareth God, than a thousand ungodly children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And it is better to die without children, than to leave ungodly children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> By one that is wise a country shall be inhabited, the tribe of the ungodly shall become desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Many such things hath my eyes seen, and greater things than these my ear hath heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> In the congregation of sinners a fire shall be kindled, and in an unbelieving nation wrath shall dame out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The ancient giants did not obtain pardon for their sins, who were destroyed trusting to their own strength:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And he spared not the place where Lot sojourned, but abhorred them for the pride of their word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> He had not pity on them, destroying the whole nation that extolled themselves in their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> So did he with the six hundred thousand footmen, who were gathered together in the hardness of their heart: and if one had been stiffnecked, it is a wonder if he had escaped unpunished:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> For mercy and wrath are with him. He is mighty to forgive, and to pour out indignation:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> According as his mercy is, so his correction judgeth a man according to his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The sinner shall not escape in his rapines, and the patience of him that sheweth mercy shall not be put off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> All mercy shall make a place for every man according to the merit of his works, and according to the wisdom of his sojournment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Say not: I shall be hidden from God. and who shall remember me from on high?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> In such a multitude I shall not be known: for what is my soul in such an immense creation?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Behold the heaven, and the heavens of heavens, the deep, and all the earth, and the things that are in them, shall be moved in his sight,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> The mountains also, and the hills, and the foundations of the earth: when God shall look upon them, they shall be shaken with trembling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And in all these things the heart is senseless: and every heart is understood by him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And his ways who shall understand, and the storm, which no eye of man see?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> For many of his works are hidden: hut the works of his justice who shall declare? or who shall endure? for the testament is far from some, and the examination of all is in the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> He that wanteth understanding thinketh vain things: and the foolish, and erring man, thinketh foolish things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Hearken to me, my son, and learn the discipline of understanding, and attend to my words in thy heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And I will shew forth good doctrine in equity, and will seek to declare wisdom: and attend to my words in thy heart, whilst with equity of spirit I tell thee the virtues that God hath put upon his works from the beginning, and I shew forth in truth his knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> The works of God are done in judgment from the beginning, and from the making of them he distinguished their parts, and their beginnings in their generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> He beautified their works for ever, they have neither hungered, nor laboured, and they have not ceased from their works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Nor shall any of them straiten his neighbour at any time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Be not thou incredulous to his word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> After this God looked upon the earth, and filled it with his goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> The soul of every living thing hath shewn forth before the face thereof, and into it they return again.</span>
