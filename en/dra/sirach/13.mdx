<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> He that toucheth pitch, shall be defiled with it: and he that hath fellowship with the proud, shall put on pride.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> He shall take a burden upon him that hath fellowship with one more honourable than himself. And have no fellowship with one that is richer than thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> What agreement shall the earthen pot have with the kettle? for if they knock one against the other, it shall be broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> The rich man hath done wrong, and yet he will fume: but the poor is wronged and must hold his peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> If thou give, he will make use of thee: and if thou have nothing, he will forsake thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> If thou have any thing, he will live with thee, and will make thee bare, and he will not be sorry for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> If he have need of thee he will deceive thee, and smiling upon thee will put thee in hope; he will speak thee fair, and will say: What wantest thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And he will shame thee by his meats, till he have drawn thee dry twice or thrice, and at last he will laugh at thee: and afterward when he seeth thee, he will forsake thee, and shake his head at thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Humble thyself to God, and wait for his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Beware that thou be not deceived Into folly, and be humbled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Be not lowly in thy wisdom, lest being humbled thou be deceived into folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> If thou be invited by one that is mightier, withdraw thyself: for so he will invite thee the more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Be not troublesome to him, lest thou be put back: and keep not far from him, lest thou be forgotten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Affect not to speak with him as an equal: and believe not his many words: for by much talk he will sift thee, and smiling will examine thee concerning thy secrets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> His cruel mind will lay up thy words: and he will not spare to do thee hurt, and to cast thee into prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Take heed to thyself, and attend diligently to what thou hearest: for thou walkest in danger of thy ruin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> When thou hearest those things, see as it were in sleep, and thou shalt awake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Love God all thy life, and call upon him for thy salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Every beast loveth its like: so also every man him that is nearest to himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> All flesh shall consort with the like to itself, and every man shall associate himself to his like.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> If the wolf shall at any time have fellowship with the lamb, so the sinner with the just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> What fellowship hath a holy man with a dog, or what part hath the rich with the poor?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> The wild ass is the lion’s prey in the desert: so also the poor are devoured by the rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And as humility is an abomination to the proud: so also the rich man abhorreth the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> When a rich man is shaken, he is kept up by his friends: but when a poor man is fallen down, he is thrust away even by his acquaintance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> When a rich man hath been deceived, he hath many helpers: he hath spoken proud things, and they have justified him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> The poor man was deceived, and he is rebuked also: he hath spoken wisely, and could have no place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> The rich man spoke, and all held their peace, and what he said they extol even to the clouds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> The poor man spoke, and they say: Who is this? and if he stumble, they will overthrow him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Riches are good to him that hath no sin in his conscience: and poverty is very wicked in the mouth of the ungodly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> The heart of a man changeth his countenance, either for good, or for evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> The token of a good heart, and a good countenance thou shalt hardly find, and with labour.</span>
