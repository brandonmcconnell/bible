<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The hopes of a man that is void of understanding are vain and deceitful: and dreams lift up fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> The man that giveth heed to lying visions, is like to him that catcheth at a shadow, and followeth after the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> The vision of dreams is the resemblance of one thing to another: as when a man’s likeness is before the face of a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> What can be made clean by the unclean? and what truth can come from that which is false?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Deceitful divinations and lying omens and the dreams of evildoers, are vanity:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And the heart fancieth as that of a woman in travail: except it be a vision sent forth from the most High, set no thy heart upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> For dreams have deceived many, and they have failed that put their trust in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The word of the law shall be fulfilled without a lie, and wisdom shall be made plain in the mouth of the faithful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> What doth he know, that hath not been tried? A man that hath much experience, shall think of many things: and he that hath learned many things, shall shew forth understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> He that hath no experience, knoweth little: and he that hath been experienced in many things, multiplieth prudence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> He that hath not been tried, what manner of things doth he know? he that hath been surprised, shall abound with subtlety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> I have seen many things by travelling, and many customs of things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Sometimes I have been in danger of death for these things, and I have been delivered by the grace of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The spirit of those that fear God; is sought after, and by his regard shall be blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> For their hope is on him that saveth them, and the eyes of God are upon them that love him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> He that feareth the Lord shall tremble at nothing, and shall not be afraid for he is his hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The soul of him that feareth the Lord is blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> To whom doth he look, and who in his strength?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> The eyes of the Lord are upon them that fear him, he is their powerful protector, and strong stay, a defence from the heat, and a cover from the sun at noon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> A preservation from stumbling, and a help from falling; he raiseth up the soul, and enlighteneth the eyes, and giveth health, and life, and blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The offering of him that sacrificeth of a thing wrongfully gotten, is stained, and the mockeries of the unjust are not acceptable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The Lord is only for them that wait upon him in the way of truth and justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> The most High approveth not the gifts of the wicked: neither hath he respect to the oblations of the unjust, nor will he be pacified for sins by the multitude of their sacrifices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> He that offereth sacrifice of the goods of the poor, is as one that sacrificeth the son in the presence of his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> The bread of the needy, is the life of the poor: he that defraudeth them thereof, is a man of blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> He that taketh away the bread gotten by sweat, is like him that killeth his neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> He that sheddeth blood, and he that defraudeth the labourer of his hire, are brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> When one buildeth up, and another pulleth down: what profit have they but the labour?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> When one prayeth, and another curseth: whose voice will God hear?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> He that washeth himself after touching the dead, if he toucheth him again, what doth his washing avail?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> So a man that fasteth for his sins, and doth the same again, what doth his humbling himself profit him? who will hear his prayer?</span>
