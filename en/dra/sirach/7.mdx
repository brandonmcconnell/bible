<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Do no evils, and no evils shall lay hold of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Depart from the unjust, and evils shall depart from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> My son, sow not evils in the furrows of injustice, and thou shalt not reap them sevenfold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Seek not of the Lord a pre-eminence, nor of the king the seat of honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Justify not thyself before God, for he knoweth the heart: and desire not to appear wise before the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Seek not to be made a judge, unless thou have strength enough to extirpate iniquities: lest thou fear the person of the powerful, and lay a stumblingblock for thy integrity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Offend not against the multitude of a city, neither cast thyself in upon the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Nor bind sin to sin: for even in one thou shalt not be unpunished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Be not fainthearted in thy mind:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Neglect not to pray, and to give alms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Say not: God will have respect to the multitude of my gifts, and when I offer to the most high God, he will accept my offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Laugh no man to scorn in the bitterness of his soul: for there is one that humbleth and exalteth, God who seeth all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Devise not a lie against thy brother: neither do the like against thy friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Be not willing to make any manner of lie: for the custom thereof is not good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Be not full of words in a multitude of ancients, and repeat not the word in thy prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Hate not laborious works, nor husbandry ordained by the most High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Number not thyself among the multitude of the disorderly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Remember wrath, for it will not tarry long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Humble thy spirit very much: for the vengeance on the flesh of the ungodly is fire and worms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Do not transgress against thy friend deferring money, nor despise thy dear brother for the sake of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Depart not from a wise and good wife, whom thou best gotten in the fear of the Lord: for the grace of her modesty is above gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Hurt not the servant that worketh faithfully, nor the hired man that giveth thee his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Let a wise servant be dear to thee as thy own soul, defraud him not of liberty, nor leave him needy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Hast thou cattle? have an eye to them: and if they be for thy profit, keep them with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Hast thou children? instruct them, and bow down their neck from their childhood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Hast thou daughters? have a care of their body, and shew not thy countenance gay towards them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Marry thy daughter well, and then shalt do a great work, and give her to a wise man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> If thou hast a wife according to thy soul, cast her not off: and to her that is hateful, trust not thyself. With thy whole heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Honour thy father, and forget not the groanings of thy mother:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Remember that thou hadst not been born but through them: and make a return to them as they have done for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> With all thy soul fear the Lord, and reverence his priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> With all thy strength love him that made thee: and forsake not his ministers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Honour God with all thy soul, and give honour to the priests, and purify thyself with thy arms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Give them their portion, as it is commanded thee, of the firstfruits and of purifications: and for thy negligences purify thyself with a few.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Offer to the Lord the gift of thy shoulders, and the sacrifice of sanctification, and the firstfruits of the holy things:</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And stretch out thy hand to the poor, that thy expiation and thy blessing may be perfected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> A gift hath grace in the sight of all the living, and restrain not grace from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Be not wanting in comforting them that weep, and walk with them that mourn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Be not slow to visit the sick: for by these things thou shalt be confirmed in love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> In all thy works remember thy last end, and thou shalt never sin.</span>
