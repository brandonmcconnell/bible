<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Wisdom shall praise her own self, and shall be honoured in God, and shall glory in the midst of her people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And shall open her mouth in the churches of the most High, and shall glorify herself in the sight of his power,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And in the midst of her own people she shall be exalted, and shall be admired in the holy assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And in the multitude of the elect she shall have praise, and among the blessed she shall be blessed, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> I came out of the mouth of the most High, the firstborn before all creatures:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> I made that in the heavens there should rise light that never faileth, and as a cloud I covered all the earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> I dwelt in the highest places, and my throne is in a pillar of a cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> I alone have compassed the circuit of heaven, and have penetrated into the bottom of the deep, and have walked in the waves of the sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And have stood in all the earth: and in every people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And in every nation I have had the chief rule:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And by my power I have trodden under my feet the hearts of all the high and low: and in all these I sought rest, and I shall abide in the inheritance of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Then the creator of all things commanded, and said to me: and he that made me, rested in my tabernacle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And he said to me: Let thy dwelling be in Jacob, and thy inheritance in Israel, and take root in my elect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> From the beginning, and before the world, was I created, and unto the world to come I shall not cease to be, and in the holy dwelling place I have ministered before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And so was I established in Sion, and in the holy city likewise I rested, and my power was in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And I took root in an honourable people, and in the portion of my God his inheritance, and my abode is in the full assembly of saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> I was exalted like a cedar in Libanus, and as a cypress tree on mount Sion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> I was exalted like a palm tree in Cades, and as a rose plant in Jericho:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> As a fair olive tree in the plains, and as a plane tree by the water in the streets, was I exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> I gave a sweet smell like cinnamon. and aromatical balm: I yielded a sweet odour like the best myrrh:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And I perfumed my dwelling as storax, and galbanum, and onyx, and aloes, and as the frankincense not cut, and my odour is as the purest balm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> I have stretched out my branches as the turpentine tree, and my branches are of honour and grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> As the vine I have brought forth a pleasant odour: and my flowers are the fruit of honour and riches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> I am the mother of fair love, and of fear, and of knowledge, and of holy hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> In me is all grace of the way and of the truth, in me is all hope of life and of virtue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Come over to me, all ye that desire me, and be filled with my fruits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> For my spirit is sweet above honey, and my inheritance above honey and the honeycomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> My memory is unto everlasting generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> They that eat me, shall yet hunger: and they that drink me, shall yet thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> He that hearkeneth to me, shall not be confounded: and they that work by me, shall not sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> They that explain me shall have life everlasting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> All these things are the book of life, and the covenant of the most High, and the knowledge of truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Moses commanded a law in the precepts of justices, and an inheritance to the house of Jacob, and the promises to Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> He appointed to David his servant to raise up of him a most mighty king, and sitting on the throne of glory for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Who filleth up wisdom as the Phison, and as the Tigris in the days of the new fruits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Who maketh understanding to abound as the Euphrates, who multiplieth it as the Jordan in the time of harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Who sendeth knowledge as the light, and riseth up as Gehon in the time of the vintage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Who first hath perfect knowledge of her, and a weaker shall not search her out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> For her thoughts are more vast than the sea, and her counsels more deep than the great ocean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> I, wisdom, have poured out rivers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> I, like a brook out of a river of a mighty water; I, like a channel of a river. and like an aqueduct, came out of paradise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> I said: I will water my garden of plants, and I will water abundantly the fruits of my meadow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> And behold my brook became a great river, and my river came near to a sea:</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> For I make doctrine to shine forth to all as the morning light, and I will declare it afar off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> I will penetrate to all the lower parts of the earth, and will behold all that sleep, and will enlighten all that hope in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> I will yet pour out doctrine as prophecy, and will leave it to them that seek wisdom, and will not cease to instruct their offspring even to the holy age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> See ye that I have not laboured for myself only, but for all that seek out the truth.</span>
