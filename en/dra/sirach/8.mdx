<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Strive not with a powerful man, lest thou fall into his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Contend not with a rich man, lest he bring an action against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> For gold and silver hath destroyed many, and hath reached even to the heart of kings, and perverted them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Strive not with a man that is full of tongue, and heap not wood upon his fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Communicate not with an ignorant man, lest he speak ill of thy family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Despise not a man that turneth away from sin, nor reproach him therewith: remember that we are all worthy of reproof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Despise not a man in his old age; for we also shall become old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Rejoice not at the death of thy enemy; knowing that we all die, and are not willing that others should rejoice at our death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Despise not the discourse of them that are ancient and wise, but acquaint thyself with their proverbs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> For of them thou shalt learn wisdom, and instruction of understanding, and to serve great men without blame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Let not the discourse of the ancients escape thee, for they have learned of their fathers:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> For of them thou shalt learn understanding, and to give an answer in time of need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Kindle not the coals of sinners by rebuking them, lest thou be burnt with the flame of the fire of their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Stand not against the face of an injurious person, lest he sit as a spy to entrap thee in thy words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Lend not to a man that is mightier than thyself: and if thou lendest, count it as lost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Be not surety above thy power: and if thou be surety, think as if thou wert to pay it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Judge not against a judge: for he judgeth according to that which is just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Go not on the way with a bold man, lest he burden thee with his evils: for he goeth according to his own will, and thou shalt perish together with his folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Quarrel not with a passionate man, and go not into the desert with a bold man: for blood is as nothing in his sight, and where there is no help he will overthrow thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Advise not with fools, for they cannot love but such things as please them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Before a stranger do no matter of counsel: for thou knowest not what he will bring forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Open not thy heart to every man: lest he repay thee with an evil turn, and speak reproachfully to thee.</span>
