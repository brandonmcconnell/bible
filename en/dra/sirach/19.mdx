<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> A workman that is a drunkard shall not be rich: and he that contemneth small things, shall fall by little and little.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Wine and women make wise men fall off, and shall rebuke the prudent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And he that joineth himself to harlots, will be wicked. Rottenness and worms shall inherit him, and he shall be lifted up for a greater example, and his soul shall be taken away out of the number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> He that is hasty to give credit, is light of heart, and shall be lessened: and he that sinneth against his own soul, shall be despised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> He that rejoiceth in iniquity, shall be censured, and he that hateth chastisement, shall have less life: and he that hateth babbling, extinguisheth evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> He that sinneth against his own soul, shall repent: and he that is delighted with wickedness, shall be condemned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Rehearse not again a wicked and harsh word, and thou shalt not fare the worse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Tell not thy mind to friend or foe: and if there be a sin with thee, disclose it not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> For he will hearken to thee, and will watch thee, and as it were defending thy sin he will hate thee, and so will he be with thee always.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Hast thou heard a word against thy neighbour? let it die within thee, trusting that it will not burst thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> At the hearing of a word the fool is in travail, as a woman groaning. in the bringing forth a child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> As an arrow that sticketh in a man’s thigh: so is a word in the heart of a fool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Reprove a friend, lest he may not have understood, and say: I did it not: or if he did it, that he may do it no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Reprove thy neighbour, for it may be he hath not said it: and if he hath said it, that he may not say it again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Admonish thy friend: for there is often a fault committed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And believe not every word. There is one, that slippeth with the tongue, but not from his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> For who is there that hath not offended with his tongue? Admonish thy neighbour before thou threaten him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And give place to the fear of the most High: for the fear of God is all wisdom, and therein is to fear God, and the disposition of the law is in all wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> But the learning of wickedness is not wisdom: and the device of sinners is not prudence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> There is a subtle wickedness, and the same is detestable: and there is a man that is foolish, wanting in wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Better is a man that hath less wisdom, and wanteth understanding, with the fear of God, than he that aboundeth in understanding, and transgresseth the law of the most High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> There is an exquisite subtilty, and the same is unjust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And there is one that uttereth an exact word telling the truth. There is one that humbleth himself wickedly, and his interior is full of deceit:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And there is one that submitteth himself exceedingly with a great lowliness: and there is one that casteth down his countenance, and maketh as if he did not see that which is unknown:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And if he be hindered from sinning for want of power, if he shall find opportunity to do evil, he will do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> A man is known by his look, and a wise man, when thou meetest him, is known by his countenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> The attire of the body, and the laughter of the teeth, and the gait of the man, shew what he is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> There is a lying rebuke in the anger of an injurious man: and there is a judgment that is not allowed to be good: and there is one that holdeth his peace, he is wise.</span>
