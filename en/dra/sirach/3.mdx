<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The sons of wisdom are the church of the just: and their generation, obedience and love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Children, hear the judgment of your father, and so do that you may be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> For God hath made the father honourable to the children: and seeking the judgment of the mothers, hath confirmed it upon the children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> He that loveth God, shall obtain pardon for his sins by prayer, and shall refrain himself from them, and shall be heard in the prayer of days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And he that honoureth his mother is as one that layeth up a treasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> He that honoureth his father shall have joy in his own children, and in the day of his prayer he shall be heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> He that honoureth his father shall enjoy a long life: and he that obeyeth the father, shall be a comfort to his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> He that feareth the Lord, honoureth his parents, and will serve them as his masters that brought him into the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Honour thy father, in work and word, and all patience,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> That a blessing may come upon thee from him, and his blessing may remain in the latter end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The father’s blessing establisheth the houses of the children: but the mother’s curse rooteth up the foundation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Glory not in the dishonour of thy father: for his shame is no glory to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> For the glory of a man is from the honour of his father, and a father without honour is the disgrace of the son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Son, support the old age of thy father, and grieve him not in his life;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And if his understanding fail, have patience with him, and despise him not when thou art in thy strength: for the relieving of the father shall not be forgotten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> For good shall be repaid to thee for the sin of thy mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And in justice thou shalt be built up, and in the day of affliction thou shalt be remembered: and thy sins shall melt away as the ice in the fair warm weather.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Of what an evil fame is he that forsaketh his father: and he is cursed of God that angereth his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> My son, do thy works in meekness, and thou shalt be beloved above the glory of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The greater thou art, the more humble thyself in all things, and thou shalt find grace before God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> For great is the power of God alone, and he is honoured by the humble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Seek not the things that are too high for thee, and search not into things above thy ability: but the things that God hath commanded thee, think on them always, and in many of his works be not curious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> For it is not necessary for thee to see with thy eyes those things that are hid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> In unnecessary matters be not over curious, and in many of his works thou shalt not be inquisitive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> For many things are shewn to thee above the understanding of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And the suspicion of them hath deceived many, and hath detained their minds in vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> A hard heart shall fear evil at the last: and he that loveth danger shall perish in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> A heart that goeth two ways shall not have success, and the perverse of heart shall be scandalized therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> A wicked heart shall be laden with sorrows, and the sinner will add sin to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> The congregation of the proud shall not be healed: for the plant of wickedness shall take root in them, and it shall not be perceived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> The heart of the wise is understood in wisdom, and a good ear will hear wisdom with all desire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> A wise heart, and which hath understanding, will abstain from sins, and in the works of justice shall have success.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Water quencheth a flaming fire, and alms resisteth sins:</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And God provideth for him that sheweth favour: he remembereth him afterwards, and in the time of his fall he shall find a sure stay.</span>
