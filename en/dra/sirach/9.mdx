<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Be not jealous over the wife of thy bosom, lest she shew in thy regard the malice of a wicked lesson.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Give not the power of thy soul to a woman, lest she enter upon thy strength, and thou be confounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Look not upon a woman that hath a mind for many: lest thou fall into her snares.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Use not much the company of her that is a dancer, and hearken not to her, lest thou perish by the force of her charms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Gaze not upon a maiden, lest her beauty be a stumblingblock to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Give not thy soul to harlots in any point: lest thou destroy thyself and thy inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Look not round about thee in the of the city, nor wander up and down in the streets thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Turn away thy face from a woman dressed up, and gaze not about upon another’s beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> For many have perished by the beauty of a woman, and hereby lust is enkindled as a fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Every woman that is a harlot, shall be trodden upon as dung in the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Many by admiring the beauty of another man’s wife, have become reprobate, for her conversation burneth as fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Sit not at all with another man’s wife, nor repose upon the bed with her:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And strive not with her over wine, lest thy heart decline towards her, and by thy blood thou fall into destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Forsake not an old friend, for the new will not be like to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> A new friend is as new wine: it shall grow old, and thou shalt drink it with pleasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Envy not the glory and riches of a sinner: for thou knowest not what his ruin shall be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Be not pleased with the wrong done by the unjust, knowing that even to hell the wicked shall not please.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Keep thee far from the man that hath power to kill, so thou shalt not suspect the fear of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And if thou come to him, commit no fault, lest he take away thy life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Know it to be a communication with death: for thou art going in the midst of snares, and walking upon the arms of them that are grieved:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> According to thy power beware of thy neighbor, and treat with the wise and prudent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Let just men be thy guests, and let thy glory be in the fear of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And let the thought of God be in thy mind, and all thy discourse on the commandments of the Highest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Works shall be praised for the hand of the artificers, and the prince of the people for the wisdom of his speech, but word of the ancients for the sense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> A man full of tongue is terrible in his city, and he that is rash in his word shall be hateful.</span>
