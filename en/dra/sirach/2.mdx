<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Son, when thou comest to the service of God, stand in justice and in fear, and prepare thy soul for temptation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Humble thy heart, and endure: incline thy ear, and receive the words of understanding: and make not haste in the time of clouds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Wait on God with patience: join thyself to God, and endure, that thy life may be increased in the latter end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Take all that shall be brought upon thee: and in thy sorrow endure, and in thy humiliation keep patience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> For gold and silver are tried in the fire, but acceptable men in the furnace of humiliation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Believe God, and he will recover thee: and direct thy way, and trust in him. Keep his fear, and grow old therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Ye that fear the Lord, wait for his mercy: and go not aside from him, lest ye fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Ye that fear the Lord, believe him: and your reward shall not be made void.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Ye that fear the Lord, hope in him: and mercy shall come to you for your delight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Ye that fear the Lord, love him, and your hearts shall be enlightened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> My children behold the generations of men: and know ye that no one hath hoped in the Lord, and hath been confounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> For who hath continued in his commandment, and hath been forsaken? or who hath called upon him, and he despised him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> For God is compassionate and merciful, and will forgive sins in the day of tribulation: and he is a protector to all that seek him in truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Woe to them that are of a double heart and to wicked lips, and to the hands that do evil, and to the sinner that goeth on the earth two ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Woe to them that are fainthearted, who believe not God: and therefore they shall not be protected by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Woe to them that have lost patience, and that have forsaken the right ways, and have gone aside into crooked ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And what will they do, when the Lord shall begin to examine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> They that fear the Lord, will not be incredulous to his word: and they that love him, will keep his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> They that fear the Lord, will seek after the things that are well pleasing to him: and they that love him, shall be filled with his law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> They that fear the Lord, will prepare their hearts, and in his sight will sanctify their souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> They that fear the Lord, keep his Commandments, and will have patience even until his visitation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Saying: If we do not penance, we shall fall into the hands of the Lord, and not into the hands of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> For according to his greatness, so also is his mercy with him.</span>
