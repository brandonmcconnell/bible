<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Watching for riches consumeth the flesh, and the thought thereof driveth away sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> The thinking beforehand turneth away the understanding, and a grievous sickness maketh the soul sober.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> The rich man hath laboured in gathering riches together, and when he resteth he shall be filled with his goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> The poor man hath laboured in his low way of life, and in the end he is still poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> He that loveth gold, shall not be justified: and he that followeth after corruption, shall be filled with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Many have been brought to fall for gold, and the beauty thereof hath been their ruin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Gold is a stumblingblock to them that sacrifice to it: woe to them that eagerly follow after it, and every fool shall perish by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Blessed is the rich man that is found without blemish: and that hath not gone after gold, nor put his trust in money nor in treasures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Who is he, and we will praise him? for he hath done wonderful things in his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Who hath been tried thereby, and made perfect, he shall have glory everlasting. He that could have transgressed, and hath not transgressed: and could do evil things, and hath not done them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Therefore are his goods established in the Lord, and all the church of the saints shall declare his alms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Art thou set at a great table? be not the first to open thy mouth upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Say not: There are many things which are upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Remember that a wicked eye is evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> What is created more wicked than an eye? therefore shall it weep over all the face when it shall see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Stretch not out thy hand first, lest being disgraced with envy thou be put to confusion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Be not hasty in a feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Judge of the disposition of thy neighbour by thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Use as a frugal man the things that are set before thee: lest if thou eatest much, thou be hated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Leave off first, for manners’ sake: and exceed not, lest thou offend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And if thou sittest among many, reach not thy hand out first of all: and be not the first to ask for drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> How sufficient is a little wine for a man well taught, and in sleeping thou shalt not be uneasy with it, and thou shalt feel no pain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Watching, and choler, and gripes, are with an intemperate man:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Sound and wholesome sleep with a moderate man: he shall sleep till morning, and his soul shall be delighted with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And if thou hast been forced to eat much, arise, go out, and vomit: and it shall refresh thee, and thou shalt not bring sickness upon thy body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Hear me, my son, and despise me not: and in the end thou shalt find my words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> In all thy works be quick, and no infirmity shall come to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> The lips of many shall bless him that is liberal of his bread, and the testimony of his truth is faithful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Against him that is niggardly of his bread, the city will murmur, and the testimony of his niggardliness is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Challenge not them that love wine: for wine hath destroyed very many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Fire trieth hard iron: so wine drunk to excess shall rebuke the hearts of the proud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Wine taken with sobriety is equal lire to men: if thou drink it moderately, thou shalt be sober.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> What is his life, who is diminished with wine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> What taketh away life? death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Wine was created from the beginning to make men joyful, and not to make them drunk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Wine drunken with moderation is the joy of the soul and the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Sober drinking is health to soul and body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Wine drunken with excess raiseth quarrels; and wrath, and many ruins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Wine drunken with excess is bitterness of the soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> The heat of drunkenness is the stumblingblock of the fool, lessening strength and causing wounds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> Rebuke not thy neighbour in a banquet of wine: and despise him not in hip mirth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Speak not to him words of reproach: and press him not in demanding again.</span>
