<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Set not thy heart upon unjust possessions, and say not: I have enough to live on: for it shall be of no service in the time of vengeance and darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Follow not in thy strength the desires of thy heart:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And say not: How mighty am I? and who shall bring me under for my deeds? for God will surely take revenge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Say not: I have sinned, and whet harm hath befallen me? for the most High is a patient rewarder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Be not without fear about sin forgiven, and add not sin upon sin:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And say not: The mercy of the Lord is great, he will have mercy on the multitude of my sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> For mercy and wrath quickly come from him, and his wrath looketh upon sinners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Delay not to be converted to the Lord, and defer it not from day to day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> For his wrath shall come on a sudden, and in the time of vengeance he will destroy thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Be not anxious for goods unjustly gotten: for they shall not profit thee in the day of calamity and revenge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Winnow not with every wind, and go not into every way: for so is every sinner proved by a double tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Be steadfast in the way of the Lord, and in the truth of thy judgment, and in knowledge, and let the word of peace and justice keep with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Be meek to hear the word, that thou mayst understand: and return a true answer with wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> If thou have understanding, answer thy neighbour: but if not, let thy hand be upon thy mouth, lest thou be surprised in an unskillful word, and be confounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Honour and glory is in the word of the wise, but the tongue of the fool is his ruin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Be not called a whisperer, and be not taken in thy tongue, and confounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> For confusion and repentance is upon a thief, and an evil mark of disgrace upon the double tongued, but to the whisperer hatred, and enmity, and reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Justify alike the small and the great.</span>
