<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Great labour is created for all men, and a heavy yoke is upon the children of Adam, from the day of their coming out of their mother’s womb, until the day of their burial into the mother of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Their thoughts, and fears of the heart, their imagination of things to come, and the day of their end:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> From him that sitteth on a glorious throne, unto him that is humbled in earth and ashes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> From him that weareth purple, and beareth the crown, even to him that is covered with rough linen: wrath, envy, trouble, unquietness, and the fear of death, continual anger, and strife,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And in the time of rest upon his bed, the sleep of the night changeth his knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> A little and as nothing is his rest, and afterward in sleep, as in the day of keeping watch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> He is troubled in the vision of his heart, as if he had escaped in the day of battle. In the time of his safety he rose up, and wondereth that there is no fear:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Such things happen to all flesh, from man even to beast, and upon sinners are sevenfold mere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Moreover, death, and bloodshed, strife, and sword, oppressions, famine, and affliction, and scourges:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> All these things are created for the wicked, and for their sakes came the flood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> All things that are of the earth, shall return to the earth again, and all waters shall return to the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> All bribery, and injustice shall blotted out, and fidelity shall stand for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The riches of the unjust shall be dried up like a river, and shall pass sway a noise like a great thunder in rain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> While he openeth his hands he shall rejoice: but transgressors shall pine away in the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The offspring of the ungodly shall not bring forth many branches, and make a noise as unclean roots upon the top of a rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The weed growing over every water, and at the bank of the river, shall be pulled up before all grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Grace is like a paradise in blessings, and mercy remaineth for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The life of a labourer that is content with what he hath, shall be sweet, and in it thou shalt find a treasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Children, and the building of a city shall establish a name, but a blameless wife shall be counted above them both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Wine and music rejoice the heart, but the love of wisdom is above them both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The flute and the psaltery make a sweet melody, but a pleasant tongue is above them both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Thy eye desireth favour and beauty, but more than these green sown fields.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> A friend and companion meeting together in season, but above them both is a wife with her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Brethren are a help in the time of trouble, but mercy shall deliver more than they.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Gold and silver make the feet stand sure: but wise counsel is above them both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Riches and strength lift up the heart: but above these is the fear of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> There is no want in the fear of the Lord, and it needeth not to seek for help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> The fear of the Lord is like a paradise of blessing, and they have covered it above all glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> My son, in thy lifetime be not indigent: for it is better to die than to want.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> The life of him that looketh toward another man’s table is not to be counted a life: for he feedeth his soul with another man’s meat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> But a man, well instructed and taught, will look to himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Begging will be sweet in the mouth of the unwise, but in his belly there shall burn a fire.</span>
