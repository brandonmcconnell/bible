<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> He that liveth for ever created all things together. God only shall be justified, and he remaineth an invincible king for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Who is able to declare his works?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> For who shall search out his glorious acts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And who shall shew forth the power of his majesty? or who shall be able to declare his mercy?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Nothing may be taken away, nor added, neither is it possible to find out the glorious works of God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> When a man hath done, then shall he begin: and when he leaveth off, he shall be at a loss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> What is man, and what is his grace? and what is his good, or what is his evil?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The number of the days of men at the most are a hundred years: as a drop of water of the sea are they esteemed: and as a pebble of the sand, so are a few years compared to eternity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Therefore God is patient in them, and poureth forth his mercy upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> He hath seen the presumption of their heart that it is wicked, and hath known their end that it is evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Therefore bath he filled up his mercy in their favour, and hath shewn them the way of justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The compassion of man is toward his neighbour: but the mercy of God is upon all flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> He hath mercy, and teacheth, and correcteth, as a shepherd doth his hock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> He hath mercy on him that receiveth the discipline of mercy, and that maketh haste in his judgments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> My son, in thy good deeds, make no complaint, and when thou givest any thing, add not grief by an evil word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Shall not the dew assuage the heat? so also the good word is better than the gift.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Lo, is not a word better than a gift? but both are with a justified man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> A fool will upbraid bitterly: and a gift of one ill taught consumeth the eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Before judgment prepare thee justice, and learn before thou speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Before sickness take a medicine, and before judgment examine thyself, and thou shalt find mercy in the sight of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Humble thyself before thou art sick, and in the time of sickness shew thy conversation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Let nothing hinder thee from praying always, and be not afraid to be justified even to death: for the reward of God continueth for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Before prayer prepare thy soul: and be not as a man that tempteth God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Remember the wrath that shall be at the last day, and the time of repaying when he shall turn away his face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Remember poverty is the time of abundance, and the necessities of poverty in the day of riches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> From the morning until the evening the time shall be changed, and all these are swift in the eyes of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> A wise man will fear in every thing, and in the days of sins will beware of sloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Every man of understanding knoweth wisdom, and will give praise to him that findeth her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> They that were of good understanding in words, have also done wisely themselves: and have understood truth and justice, and have poured forth proverbs and judgments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Go not after thy lusts, but turn away from thy own will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> If thou give to thy soul her desires, she will make thee a joy to thy enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Take no pleasure in riotous assemblies, be they ever so small: for their concertation is continual.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Make not thyself poor by borrowing to contribute to feasts when thou hast nothing in thy purse: for thou shalt be an enemy to thy own life.</span>
