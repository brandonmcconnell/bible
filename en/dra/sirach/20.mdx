<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> How much better is it to reprove, than to be angry, and not to hinder him that confesseth in prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> The lust of an eunuch shall devour a young maiden:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> So is he that by violence executeth unjust judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> How good is it, when thou art reproved, to shew repentance! for so thou shalt escape wilful sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> There is one that holdeth his peace, that is found wise: and there is another that is hateful, that is bold in speech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> There is one that holdeth his peace, because he knoweth not what to say: and there is another that holdeth his peace, knowing the proper time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> A wise man will hold his peace till he see opportunity: but a babbler, and a fool, will regard no time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> He that useth many words shall hurt his own soul: and he that taketh authority to himself unjustly shall be hated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> There is success in evil things to a man without discipline, and there is a finding that turneth to loss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> There is a gift that is not profitable: and there is a gift, the recompense of which is double.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> There is an abasement because of glory: and there is one that shall lift up his head from a low estate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> There is that buyeth much for a small price, and restoreth the same sevenfold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> A man wise in words shall make himself beloved: but the graces of fools shall be poured out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The gift of the fool shall do thee no good: for his eyes are sevenfold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> He will give a few things, and upbraid much: and the opening of his mouth is the kindling of a fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Today a man lendeth, and tomorrow he asketh it again: such a man as this is hateful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> A fool shall have no friend, and there shall be no thanks for his good deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> For they that eat his bread, are of a false tongue. How often, and how many will laugh him to scorn!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> For he doth not distribute with right understanding that which was to be had: in like manner also that which was not to be had.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The slipping of a false tongue is as one that falleth on the pavement: so the fall of the wicked shall come speedily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> A man without grace is as a vain fable, it shall be continually in the mouth of the unwise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> A parable coming out, of a fool’s mouth shall be rejected: for he doth not speak it in due season.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> There is that is hindered from sinning through want, and in his rest he shall be pricked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> There is that will destroy his own soul through shamefacedness, and by occasion of an unwise person he will destroy it: and by respect of person he will destroy himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> There is that for bashfulness promiseth to his friend, and maketh him his enemy for nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> A lie is a foul blot in a man, and yet it will be continually in the mouth of men without discipline.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> A thief is better than a man that is always lying: but both of them shall inherit destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> The manners of lying men are without honour: and their confusion is with them without ceasing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> A wise man shall advance himself with his words, and a prudent man shall please the great ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> He that tilleth his land shall make a high heap of corn: and he that worketh justice shall be exalted: and he that pleaseth great men shall escape iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Presents and gifts blind the eyes of judges, and make them dumb in the mouth, so that they cannot correct.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Wisdom that is hid, and treasure that is not seen: what profit is there in them both?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Better is he that hideth his folly, than the man that hideth his wisdom.</span>
