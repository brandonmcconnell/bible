<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Blessed is the man that hath not slipped by a word out of his mouth, and is not pricked with the remorse of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Happy is he that hath had no sadness of his mind, and who is not fallen from his hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Riches are not comely for a covetous man and a niggard, and what should an envious man do with gold?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> He that gathereth together by wronging his own soul, gathereth for others, and another will squander away his goods in rioting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> He that is evil to himself, to whom will he be good? and he shall not take pleasure in his goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> There is none worse than he that envieth himself, and this is the reward of his wickedness:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And if he do good, he doth it ignorantly, and unwillingly: and at the last he discovereth his wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The eye of the envious is wicked: and he turneth away his face, and despiseth his own soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The eye of the covetous man is insatiable in his portion of iniquity: he will not be satisfied till he consume his own soul, drying it up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> An evil eye is towards evil things: and he shall not have his fill of bread, but shall be needy and pensive at his own table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> My son, if thou have any thing, do good to thyself, and offer to God worthy offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Remember that death is not slow, and that the covenant of hell hath been shewn to thee: for the covenant of this world shall surely die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Do good to thy friend before thou die, and according to thy ability, stretching out thy hand give to the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Defraud not thyself of the good day, and let not the part of a good gift overpass thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Shalt thou not leave to others to divide by lot thy sorrows and labours?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Give and take, and justify thy soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Before thy death work justice: for in hell there is no finding food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> All flesh shall fade as grass, and as the leaf that springeth out on a green tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Some grow, and some fall off: so is the generation of flesh and blood, one cometh to an end, and another is born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Every work that is corruptible shall fail in the end: and the worker thereof shall go with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And every excellent work shall be justified: and the worker thereof shall be honoured therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Blessed is the man that shall continue in wisdom, and that shall meditate in his justice, and in his mind shall think of the all seeing eye of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> He that considereth her ways in his heart, and hath understanding in her secrets, who goeth after her as one that traceth, and stayeth in her ways:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> He who looketh in at her windows, and hearkeneth at her door:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> He that lodgeth near her house, and fastening a pin in her walls shall set up his tent nigh unto her, where good things shall rest in his lodging for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> He shall set his children under her shelter, and shall lodge under her branches:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> He shall be protected under her covering from the heat, and shall rest in her glory.</span>
