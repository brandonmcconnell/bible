<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> God created man of the earth, and made him after his own image.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And he turned him into it again, and clothed him with strength according to himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> He gave him the number of his days and time, and gave him power over all things that are upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> He put the fear of him upon all flesh, and he had dominion over beasts and fowls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> He created of him a helpmate like to himself: he gave them counsel, and a tongue, and eyes, and ears, and a heart to devise: and he filled them with the knowledge of understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> He created in them the science of the spirit, he filled their heart with wisdom, and shewed them both good and evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> He set his eye upon their hearts to shew them the greatness of his works:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> That they might praise the name which he hath sanctified: and glory in his wondrous acts, that they might declare the glorious things of his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Moreover he gave them instructions, and the law of life for an inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> He made an everlasting covenant with them, and he shewed them his justice and judgments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And their eye saw the majesty of his glory. and their ears heard his glorious voice, and he said to them: Beware of all iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And he gave to every one of them commandment concerning his neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Their ways are always before him, they are not hidden from his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Over every nation he set a ruler.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Israel was made the manifest portion of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And all their works are as the sun in the sight of God: and his eyes are continually upon their ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Their covenants were not hid by their iniquity, and all their iniquities are in the sight of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The alms of a man is as a signet with him, and shall preserve the grace of a man as the apple of the eye:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And afterward he shall rise up, and shall render them their reward, to every one upon their own head, and shall turn them down into the bowels of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> But to the penitent he hath given the way of justice, and he hath strengthened them that were fainting in patience, and hath appointed to them the lot of truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Turn to the Lord, and forsake thy sins:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Make thy prayer before the face of the Lord, and offend less.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Return to the Lord, and turn away from thy injustice, and greatly hate abomination.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And know the justices and judgments of God, and stand firm in the lot set before thee, and in prayer to the most high God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Go to the side of the holy age, with them that live and give praise to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Tarry not in the error of the ungodly, give glory before death. Praise perisheth from the dead as nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Give thanks whilst thou art living, whilst thou art alive and in health thou shalt give thanks, and shalt praise God, and shalt glory in his mercies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> How great is the mercy of the Lord, and his forgiveness to them that turn to him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> For all things cannot be in men, because the son of man is not immortal, and they are delighted with the vanity of evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> What is brighter than the sun; yet it shall be eclipsed. Or what is more wicked than that which flesh and blood hath invented? and this shall be reproved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> He beholdeth the power of the height of heaven: and all men are earth and ashes.</span>
