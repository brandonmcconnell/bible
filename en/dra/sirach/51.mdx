<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> A prayer of Jesus the son of Sirach. I will give glory to thee, O Lord, O King, and I will praise thee, O God my Saviour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> I will give glory to thy name: for thou hast been a helper and protector to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And hast preserved my body from destruction, from the snare of an unjust tongue, and from the lips of them that forge lies, and in the sight of them that stood by, thou hast been my helper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And thou hast delivered me, according to the multitude of the mercy of thy name, from them that did roar, prepared to devour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Out of the hands of them that sought my life, and from the gates of afflictions, which compassed me about:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> From the oppression of the flame which surrounded me, and in the midst of the fire I was not burnt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> From the depth of the belly of hell, and from an unclean tongue, and from lying words, from an unjust king, and from a slanderous tongue:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> My soul shall praise the Lord even to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And my life was drawing near to hell beneath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> They compassed me on every side, and there was no one that would help me. I looked for the succour of men, and there was none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> I remembered thy mercy, O Lord, and thy works, which are from the beginning of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> How thou deliverest them that wait for thee, O Lord, and savest them out of the hands of the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Thou hast exalted my dwelling place upon the earth and I have prayed for death to pass away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> I called upon the Lord, the father of my Lord, that he would not leave me in the day of my trouble, and in the time of the proud without help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> I will praise thy name continually, and will praise it with thanksgiving, and my prayer was heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And thou hast saved me from destruction, and hast delivered me from the evil time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Therefore I will give thanks, and praise thee, and bless the name of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> When I was yet young, before I wandered about, I sought for wisdom openly in my prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> I prayed for her before the temple, and unto the very end I will seek after her, and she flourished as a grape soon ripe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> My heart delighted in her, my foot walked in the right way, from my youth up I sought after her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> I bowed down my ear a little, and received her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> I found much wisdom in myself, and I profited much therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> To him that giveth me wisdom, will I give glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> For I have determined to follow her: I have had a zeal for good, and shall not be confounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> My soul hath wrestled for her, and in doing it I have been confirmed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> I stretched forth my hands on high, and I bewailed my ignorance of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> I directed my soul to her, and in knowledge I found her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> I possessed my heart with her from the beginning: therefore I shall not be forsaken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> My entrails were troubled in seeking her: therefore shall I possess a good possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> The Lord hath given me a tongue for my reward: and with it I will praise him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Draw near to me, ye unlearned, and gather yourselves together into the house of discipline.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Why are ye slow? and what do you say of these things? your souls are exceeding thirsty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> I have opened my mouth, and have spoken: buy her for yourselves without silver,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And submit your neck to the yoke, and let your soul receive discipline: for she is near at hand to be found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Behold with your eyes how I have laboured a little, and have found much rest to myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Receive ye discipline as a great sum of money, and possess abundance of gold by her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Let your soul rejoice in his mercy, and you shall not be confounded in his praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Work your work before the time, and he will give you your reward in his time.</span>
