<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> My son, hast thou sinned? do so no more: but for thy former sins also pray that they may be forgiven thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Flee from sins as from the face of a serpent: for if thou comest near them, they will take hold of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> The teeth thereof are the teeth of a lion, killing the souls of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> All iniquity is like a two-edged sword, there is no remedy for the wound thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Injuries and wrongs will waste riches: and the house that is very rich shall be brought to nothing by pride: so the substance of the proud shall be rooted out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> The prayer out of the mouth of the poor shall reach the ears of God, and judgment shall come for him speedily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> He that hateth to be reproved walketh in the trace of a sinner: and he that feareth God will turn to his own heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> He that is mighty by a bold tongue is known afar off, but a wise man knoweth to slip by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> He that buildeth his house at other men’s charges, is as he that gathereth himself stones to build in the winter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> The congregation of sinners is like tow heaped together, and the end of them is a flame of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The way of sinners is made plain with stones, and in their end is hell, and darkness, and pains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> He that keepeth justice shall get the understanding thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The perfection of the fear of God is wisdom and understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> He that is not wise in good, will not be taught.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> But there is a wisdom that aboundeth in evil: and there is no understanding where there is bitterness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The knowledge of a wise man shall abound like a flood, and his counsel continueth like a fountain of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The heart of a fool is like a broken vessel, and no wisdom at all shall it hold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> A man of sense will praise every wise word he shall hear, and will apply it to himself: the luxurious man hath heard it, and it shall displease him, and he will cast it behind his back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> The talking of a fool is like a burden in the way: but in the lips of the wise, grace shall be found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The mouth of the prudent is sought after in the church, and they will think upon his words in their hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> As a house that is destroyed, so is wisdom to a fool: and the knowledge of the unwise is as words without sense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Doctrine to a fool is as fetters on the feet, and like manacles on the right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> A fool lifteth up his voice in laughter: but a wise man will scarce laugh low to himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Learning to the prudent is as an ornament of gold, and like a bracelet upon his right arm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> The foot of a fool is soon in his neighbour’s house: but a man of experience will be abashed at the person of the mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> A fool will peep through the window into the house: but he that is well taught will stand without.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> It is the folly of a man to hearken at the door: and a wise man will be grieved with the disgrace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> The lips of the unwise will be telling foolish things but the words of the wise shall be weighed in a balance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> The heart of fools is in their mouth: and the mouth of wise men is in their heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> While the ungodly curseth the devil, he curseth his own soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> The talebearer shall defile his own soul, and shall be hated by all: and he that shall abide with him shall be hateful: the silent and wise man shall be honoured.</span>
