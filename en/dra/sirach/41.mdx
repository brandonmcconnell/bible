<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> O death, how bitter is the remembrance of thee to a man that hath peace in his possessions!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> To a man that is at rest, and whose ways are prosperous in all things, and that is yet able to take meat!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> O death, thy sentence is welcome to the man that is in need, and to him whose strength faileth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Who is in a decrepit age, and that is in care about all things, and to the distrustful that loseth patience!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Fear not the sentence of death. Remember what things have been before thee, and what shall come after thee: this sentence is from the Lord upon all flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And what shall come upon thee by the good pleasure of the most High? Whether ten, or a hundred, or a thousand years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> For among the dead there is no accusing of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The children of sinners become children of abominations, and they that converse near the houses of the ungodly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The inheritance of the children of sinners shall perish, and with their posterity shall be a perpetual reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> The children will complain of an ungodly father, because for his sake they are in reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Woe to you, ungodly men, who have forsaken the law of the most high Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And if you be born, you shall be born in malediction: and if you die, in malediction shall be your portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> All things that are of the earth, shall return into the earth: so the ungodly shall from malediction to destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The mourning of men is about their body, but the name of the ungodly shall be blotted out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Take care of a good name: for this shall continue with thee, more than a thousand treasures precious and great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> A good life hath its number of days: but a good name shall continue for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> My children, keep discipline in peace: for wisdom that is hid, and a treasure that is not seen, what profit is there in them both?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Better is the man that hideth his folly, then the man that hideth his wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Wherefore have a shame of these things I am now going to speak of.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> For it is not good to keep all shamefacedness: and all things do not please all men in opinion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Be ashamed of fornication before father and mother: and of a lie before a governor and a man in power:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Of an offence before a prince, and a judge: of iniquity before a congregation and a people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Of injustice before a companion and friend: and in regard to the place where thou dwellest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Of theft, and of the truth of God, and the covenant: of leaning with thy elbow over meat, and of deceit in giving and taking:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Of silence before them that salute thee: of looking upon a harlot: and of turning away thy face from thy kinsman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Turn not sway thy face from thy neighbour, and of taking away a portion and not restoring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Gaze not upon another man’s wife, and be not inquisitive after his handmaid, and approach not her bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Be ashamed of upbraiding speeches before friends: and after thou hast given, upbraid not.</span>
