<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Elias the prophet stood up, as a fire, and his word burnt like a torch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> He brought a famine upon them, and they that provoked him in their envy, were reduced to a small number, for they could not endure the commandments of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> By the word of the Lord he shut up the heaven, and he brought down fire from heaven thrice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Thus was Elias magnified in his wondrous works. And who can glory like to thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Who raisedst up a dead man from below, from the lot of death, by the word of the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Who broughtest down kings to destruction, and brokest easily their power in pieces, and the glorious from their bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Who heardest judgment in Sina, and in Horeb the judgments of vengeance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Who anointedst kings to penance, and madest prophets successors after thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Who wast taken up in a whirlwind of fire, in a chariot of fiery horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Who art registered in the judgments of times to appease the wrath of the Lord, to reconcile the heart of the father to the son, and to restore the tribes of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Blessed are they that saw thee, and were honoured with thy friendship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> For we live only in our life, but after death our name shall not be such.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Elias was indeed covered with the whirlwind, and his spirit was filled up in Eliseus: in his days he feared not the prince, and no man was more powerful than he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> No word could overcome him, and after death his body prophesied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> In his life he did great wonders, and is death he wrought miracles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> For all this the people repented not, neither did they depart from their sins till they were cast out of their land, and were scattered through all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And there was left but a small people, and a prince in the house of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Some of these did that which pleased God: but others committed many sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Ezechias fortified his city, and brought in water into the midst thereof, and he digged a rock with iron, and made a well for water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> In his days Sennacherib came up, and sent Rabsaces, and lifted up his hand against them, and he stretched out his hand against Sion, and became proud through his power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Then their hearts and hands trembled, and they were in pain as women in travail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And they called upon the Lord who is merciful, and spreading their hands, they lifted them up to heaven: and the holy Lord God quickly heard their voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> He was not mindful of their sins, neither did he deliver them up to their enemies, but he purified them by the hand of Isaias, the holy prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> He overthrew the army of the Assyrians, and the angel of the Lord destroyed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> For Ezechias did that which pleased God, and walked valiantly in the way of David his father, which Isaias, the great prophet, and faithful in the sight of God, had commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> In his days the sun went backward, and he lengthened the king’s life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> With a great spirit he saw the things that are to come to pass at last, and comforted the mourners in Sion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> He shewed what should come to pass for ever, and secret things before they came.</span>
