<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> With three things my spirit is pleased, which are approved before God and men:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> The concord of brethren, and the love of neighbours, and mall and wife that agree well together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Three sorts my soul hateth, and I am greatly grieved at their life:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> A poor man that is proud: a rich man that is a liar: an old man that is a fool, and doting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The things that thou hast not gathered in thy youth, how shalt thou find them in thy old age?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> O how comely is judgment for a grey head, and for ancients to know counsel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> O how comely is wisdom for the aged, and understanding and counsel to men of honour!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Much experience is the crown of old men, and the fear of God is their glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Nine things that are not to be imagined by the heart have I magnified, and the tenth I will utter to men with my tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> A man that hath joy of his children: and he that liveth and seeth the fall of his enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Blessed is he. that dwelleth with a wise woman, and that hath not slipped with his tongue, and that hath not served such as are unworthy of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Blessed is he that findeth a true friend, and that declareth justice to an ear that heareth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> How great is he that findeth wisdom and knowledge! but there is none above him that feareth the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The fear of God hath set itself above all things:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Blessed is the man, to whom it is given to have the fear of God: he that holdeth it, to whom shall he be likened?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The fear of God is the beginning of his love: and the beginning of faith is to be fast joined unto it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The sadness of the heart is every plague: and the wickedness of a woman is all evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And a man will choose any plague, but the plague of the heart:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And ally wickedness, but the wickedness of a woman:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And any affliction, but the affliction from them that hate him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And ally revenge, but the revenge of enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> There is no head worse than the head of a serpent:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And there is no anger above the anger of a woman. It will be more agreeable to abide with a lion and a dragon, than to dwell with a wicked woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> The wickedness of a woman changeth her face: and she darkeneth her countenance as a bear: and sheweth it like sackcloth. In the midst of her neighbours,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Her husband groaned, and hearing he sighed a little.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> All malice is shore to the malice of a woman, let the lot of sinners fall upon her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> As the climbing of a sandy way is to the feet of the aged, so is a wife full of tongue to a quiet man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Look not upon a woman’s beauty, and desire not a woman for beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> A woman’s anger, and impudence, and confusion is great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> A woman, if she have superiority, is contrary to her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> A wicked woman abateth the courage, and maketh a heavy countenance, and a wounded heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Feeble hands, and disjointed knees, a woman that doth not make her husband happy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> From the woman came the beginning of sin, and by her we all die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Give no issue to thy water, no, not a little: nor to a wicked woman liberty to gad abroad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> If she walk not at thy hand, she will confound thee in the sight of thy enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Cut her off from thy flesh, lest she always abuse thee.</span>
