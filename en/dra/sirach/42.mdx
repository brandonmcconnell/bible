<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Repeat not the word which thou hast heard, and disclose not the thing that is secret; so shalt thou be truly without confusion, and shall find favour before all men: be not ashamed of any of these things, and accept no person to sin thereby:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Of the law of the most High, and of his covenant, and of judgment to justify the ungodly:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Of the affair of companions and travellers, and of the gift of the inheritance of friends:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Of exactness of balance and weights, of getting much or little:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Of the corruption of buying, and of merchants, and of much correction of children, and to make the side of a wicked slave to bleed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Sure keeping is good over a wicked wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Where there are many hands, shut up, and deliver all things in number, and weight: and put all in writing that thou givest out or receivest in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Be not ashamed to inform the unwise and foolish, and the aged, that are judged I by young men: and thou shalt be well instructed in all things, and well approved in the sight of all men living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The father waketh for the daughter when no man knoweth, and the care for her taketh away his sleep, when she is young, lest she pass away the flower of her age, and when she is married, lest she should be hateful:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> In her virginity, lest she should be corrupted, and be found with child in her father’s house: and having a husband, lest she should misbehave herself, or at the least become barren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Keep a sure watch over a shameless daughter: lest at any time she make thee become a laughingstock to thy enemies, and a byword in the city, and a reproach among the people, and she make thee ashamed before all the multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Behold not everybody’s beauty: and tarry not among women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> For from garments cometh a moth, and from a woman the iniquity of a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For better is the iniquity of a man, than a woman doing a good turn, and a woman bringing shame and reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> I will now remember the works of the Lord, and I will declare the things I have seen. By the words of the Lord are his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The sun giving light hath looked upon all things, and full of the glory of the Lord is his work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Hath not the Lord made the saints to declare all his wonderful works, which the Lord Almighty hath firmly settled to be established for his glory?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> He hath searched out the deep, and the heart of men: and considered their crafty devices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> For the Lord knoweth all knowledge, and hath beheld the signs of the world, he declareth the things that are past, and the things that are to come, and revealeth the traces of hidden things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> No thought escapeth him, and no word can hide itself from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> He hath beautified the glorious works of his wisdom: and he Is from eternity to eternity, and to him nothing may be added,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Nor can he be diminished, and he hath no need of any counsellor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> O how desirable are all his works, and what we can know is but as a spark!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> All these things live, and remain for ever, and for every use all things obey him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> All things are double, one against another, and he hath made nothing defective.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> He hath established the good things of every one. And who shall be filled with beholding his glory?</span>
