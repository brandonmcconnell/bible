<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Have mercy upon us, O God of all, and behold us, and shew us the light of thy mercies:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And send thy fear upon the nations, that have not sought after thee: that they may know that there is no God beside thee, and that they may shew forth thy wonders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Lift up thy hand over the strange nations, that they may see thy power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> For as thou hast been sanctified in us in their sight, so thou shalt be magnified among them in our presence,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> That they may know thee, as we also have known thee, that there is no God beside thee, O Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Renew thy signs, and work new miracles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Glorify thy hand, and thy right arm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Raise up indignation, and pour out wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Take away the adversary, and crush the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Hasten the time, and remember the end, that they may declare thy wonderful works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Let him that escapeth be consumed by the rage of the fire: and let them perish that oppress thy people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Crush the head of the princes of the enemies that say: There is no other beside us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Gather together all the tribes of Jacob: that they may know that there is no God besides thee, and may declare thy great works: and thou shalt inherit them as from the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Have mercy on thy people, upon whom thy name is invoked: and upon Israel, whom thou hast raised up to be thy firstborn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Have mercy on Jerusalem, the city which thou hast sanctified, the city of thy rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Fill Sion with thy unspeakable words, and thy people with thy glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Give testimony to them that are thy creatures from the beginning, and raise up the prophecies which the former prophets spoke in thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Reward them that patiently wait for thee, that thy prophets may be found faithful: and hear the prayers of thy servants,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> According to the blessing of Aaron over thy people, and direct us into the way of justice, and let all know that dwell upon the earth, that thou art God the beholder of all ages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The belly will devour all meat, yet one is better than another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The palate tasteth venison and the wise heart false speeches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> A perverse heart will cause grief, and a man of experience will resist it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> A woman will receive every man: yet one daughter is better than another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> The beauty of a woman cheereth the countenance of her husband, and a man desireth nothing more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> If she have a tongue that can cure, and likewise mitigate and shew mercy: her husband is not like other men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> He that possesseth a good wife, beginneth a possession: she is a help like to himself, and a pillar of rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Where there is no hedge, the possession shall be spoiled: and where there is no wife, he mourneth that is in want.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Who will trust him that hath no rest, and that lodgeth wheresoever the night taketh him, as a robber well appointed, that skippeth from city to city.</span>
