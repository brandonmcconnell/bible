<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> If thou do good, know to whom thou dost it, and there shall be much thanks for thy good deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Do good to the just, and thou shalt find great recompense: and if not of him, assuredly of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> For there is no good for him that is always occupied in evil, and that giveth no alms: for the Highest hateth sinners, and hath mercy on the penitent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Give to the merciful and uphold not the sinner: God will repay vengeance to the ungodly and to sinners, and keep them against the day of vengeance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Give to the good, and receive not a sinner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Do good to the humble, and give not to the ungodly: hold back thy bread, and give it not to him, lest thereby he overmaster thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> For thou shalt receive twice as much evil for all the good thou shalt have done to him: for the Highest also hateth sinners, and will repay vengeance to the ungodly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> A friend shall not be known in prosperity, and an enemy shall not be hidden in adversity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> In the prosperity of a man, his enemies are grieved: and a friend is known in his adversity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Never trust thy enemy: for as a brass pot his wickedness rusteth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Though he humble himself and go crouching, yet take good heed and beware of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Set him not by thee, neither let him sit on thy right hand, lest he turn into thy place, and seek to take thy seat: and at the last thou acknowledge my words, and be pricked with my sayings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Who will pity an enchanter struck by a serpent, or any that come near wild beasts? so is it with him that keepeth company with a wicked man, and is involved in his sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For an hour he will abide with thee: but if thou begin to decline, he will not endure it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> An enemy speaketh sweetly with his lips, but in his heart he lieth in wait, to throw thee into a pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> An enemy weepeth with his eyes: but if he find an opportunity he will not be satisfied with blood:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And if evils come upon thee, thou shalt find him there first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> An enemy hath tears in his eyes, and while he pretendeth to help thee, will undermine thy feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> He will shake his head, and clap his hands, and whisper much, and change his countenance.</span>
