<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Son, defraud not the poor of alms, and turn not away thy eyes from the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Despise not the hungry soul: and provoke not the Boor in his want.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Afflict not the heart of the needy, and defer not to give to him that is in distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Reject not the petition of the afflicted: and turn not away thy face from the needy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Turn not away thy eyes from the poor for fear of anger: and leave not to them that ask of thee to curse thee behind thy back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> For the prayer of him that curseth thee in the bitterness of his soul, shall be heard, for he that made him will hear him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Make thyself affable to the congregation of the poor, and humble thy soul to the ancient, and bow thy head to a great man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Bow down thy ear cheerfully to the poor, and pay what thou owest, and answer him peaceable words with mildness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Deliver him that suffereth wrong out of the hand of the proud: and be not fainthearted in thy soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> In judging be merciful to the fatherless as a father, and as a husband to their mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And thou shalt be as the obedient son of the most High, and he will have mercy on thee more than a mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Wisdom inspireth life into her children, and protecteth them that seek after her, and will go before them in the way of justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And he that loveth her, loveth life: and they that watch for her, shall embrace her sweetness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> They that hold her fast, shall inherit life: and whithersoever she entereth, God will give a blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> They that serve her, shall be servants to the holy one: and God loveth them that love her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> He that hearkeneth to her, shall judge nations: and he that looketh upon her, shall remain secure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> If he trust to her, he shall inherit her, and his generation shall be in assurance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> For she walketh with him in temptation, and at the first she chooseth him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> She will bring upon him fear and dread and trial: and she will scourge him with the affliction of her discipline, till she try him by her laws, and trust his soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Then she will strengthen him, and make a straight way to him, and give him joy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And will disclose her secrets to him, and will heap upon him treasures of knowledge and understanding of justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> But if he go astray, she will forsake him, and deliver him into the hands of his enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Son, observe the time, and fly from evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> For thy soul be not ashamed to say the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> For there is a shame that bringeth sin, and there is a shame that bringeth glory and grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Accept no person against thy own person, nor against thy soul a lie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Reverence not thy neighbour in his fall:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And refrain not to speak in the time of salvation. Hide not thy wisdom in her beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> For by the tongue wisdom is discerned: and understanding, and knowledge, and learning by the word of the wise, and steadfastness in the works of justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> In nowise speak against the truth, but be ashamed of the lie of thy ignorance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Be not ashamed to confess thy sins, but submit not thyself to every man for sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Resist not against the face of the mighty, and do not strive against the stream of the river.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Strive for justice for thy soul, and even unto death fight for justice, and God will overthrow thy enemies for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Be not hasty in thy tongue: and slack and remiss in thy works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Be not as a lion in thy house, terrifying them of thy household, and oppressing them that are under thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Let not thy hand be stretched out to receive, and shut when thou shouldst give.</span>
