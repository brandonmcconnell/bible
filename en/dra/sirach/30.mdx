<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> He that loveth his son, frequently chastiseth him, that he may rejoice in his latter end, and not grope after the doors of his neighbours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> He that instructeth his son shall be praised in him, and shall glory in him in the midst of them of his household.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> He that teacheth his son, maketh his enemy jealous, and in the midst of his friends he shall glory in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> His father is dead, and he is as if he were not dead: for he hath left one behind him that is like himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> While he lived he saw and rejoiced in him: and when he died he was not sorrowful, neither was he confounded before his enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> For he left behind him a defender of his house against his enemies, and one that will requite kindness to his friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> For the souls of his sons he shall bind up his wounds, and at every cry his bowels shall be troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> A horse not broken becometh stubborn, and a child left to himself will become headstrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Give thy son his way, and he shall make thee afraid: play with him, and he shall make thee sorrowful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Laugh not with him, lest thou have sorrow, and at the last thy teeth be set on edge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Give him not liberty in his youth, and wink not at his devices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Bow down his neck while he is young, and beat his sides while he is a child, lest he grow stubborn, and regard thee not, and so be a sorrow of heart to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Instruct thy son, and labour about him, lest his lewd behaviour be an offence to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Better is a poor man who is sound, and strong of constitution, than a rich man who is weak and afflicted with evils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Health of the soul in holiness of justice, is better then all gold and silver: and a sound body, than immense revenues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> There is no riches above the riches of the health of the body: and there is no pleasure above the joy of the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Better is death than a bitter life: and everlasting rest, than continual sickness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Good things that are hidden in a mouth that is shut, are as masses of meat set about a grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> What good shall an offering do to an idol? for it can neither eat, nor smell:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> So is he that is persecuted by the Lord, bearing the reward of his iniquity:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> He seeth with his eyes, and groaneth, as an eunuch embracing a virgin, and sighing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Give not up thy soul to sadness, and afflict not thyself in thy own counsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> The joyfulness of the heart, is the life of a man, and a never failing treasure of holiness: and the joy of a man is length of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Have pity on thy own soul, pleasing God, and contain thyself: gather up thy heart in his holiness: and drive away sadness far from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> For sadness hath killed many, and there is no profit in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Envy and anger shorten a man’s days, and pensiveness will bring old age before the time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> A Cheerful and good heart is always feasting: for his banquets are prepared with diligence.</span>
