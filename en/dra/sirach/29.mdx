<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> He that sheweth mercy, lendeth to his neighbour: and he that is stronger in hand, keepeth the commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Lend to thy neighbour in the time of his need, and pay thou thy neighbour again in due time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Reap thy word, and deal faithfully with him: and thou shalt always find that which is necessary for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Many have looked upon a thing lent as a thing found, and have given trouble to them that helped them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Till they receive, they kiss the hands of the lender, and in promises they humble their voice:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> But when they should repay, they will ask time, and will return tedious and murmuring words, and will complain of the time:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And if he be able to pay, he will stand off, he will scarce pay one half, and will count it as if he had found it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> But if not, he will defraud him of his money, and he shall get him for an enemy without cause:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And he will pay him with reproaches and curses, and instead of honour and good turn will repay him injuries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Many have refused to lend, not out of wickedness, but they were afraid to be defrauded without cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> But yet towards the poor be thou more hearty, and delay not to shew him mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Help the poor because of the commandment: and send him not away empty handed because of his poverty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Lose thy money for thy brother and thy friend: and hide it not under a stone to be lost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Place thy treasure in the commandments of the most High, and it shall bring thee more profit than gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Shut up alms in the heart of the poor, and it shall obtain help for thee against all evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Better than the shield of the mighty, and better than the spear:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> It shall fight for thee against thy enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> A good man is surety for his neighbour: and he that hath lost shame, will leave him to himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Forget not the kindness of thy surety: for he hath given his life for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The sinner and the unclean fleeth from his surety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> A sinner attributeth to himself the goods of his surety: and he that is of an unthankful mind will leave him that delivered him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> A man is surety for his neighbour: and when he hath lost all shame, he shall forsake him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Evil suretyship hath undone many of good estate, and hath tossed them as a wave of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> It hath made powerful men to go from place to place round about, and they have wandered in strange countries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> A sinner that transgresseth the commandment of the Lord, shall fall into an evil suretyship: and he that undertaketh many things, shall fall into judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Recover thy neighbour according to thy power, and take heed to thyself that thou fall not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> The chief thing for man’s life is water and bread, and clothing, and a house to cover shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Better is the poor man’s fare under a roof of boards, than sumptuous cheer abroad in another man’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Be contented with little instead of much, and thou shalt not hear the reproach of going abroad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> It is a miserable life to go as a guest from house to house: for where a man is a stranger, he shall not deal confidently, nor open his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> He shall entertain and feed, and give drink to the unthankful, and moreover he shall hear bitter words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Go, stranger, and furnish the table, and give others to eat what thou hast in thy hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Give place to the honourable presence of my friends: for I want my house, my brother being to be lodged with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> These things are grievous to a man of understanding: the upbraiding of houseroom, and the reproaching of the lender.</span>
