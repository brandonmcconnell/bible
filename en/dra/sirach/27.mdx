<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Through poverty many have sinned: and he that seeketh to be enriched, turneth away his eye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> As a stake sticketh fast in the midst of the joining of stones, so also in the midst of selling and buying, sin shall stick fast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Sin shall be destroyed with the sinner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Unless thou hold thyself diligently in the fear of the Lord, thy house shall quickly be overthrown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> As when one sifteth with a sieve, the dust will remain: so will the perplexity of a man in his thoughts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> The furnace trieth the potter’s vessels, and the trial of affliction just men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Be the dressing of a tree sheweth the fruit thereof, so a word out of the thought of the heart of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Praise not a man before he speaketh, for this is the trial of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> If thou followest justice, thou shalt obtain her: and shalt put her on as a long robe of honour, and thou shalt dwell with her: and she shall protect thee for ever, and in the day of acknowledgment thou shalt find a strong foundation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Birds resort unto their like: so truth will return to them that practise her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The lion always lieth in wait for prey: so do sins for them that work iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> A holy man continueth in wisdom as the sun: but a fool is changed as the moon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> In the midst of the unwise keep in the word till its time: but be continually among men that think.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The discourse of sinners is hateful, and their laughter is at the pleasures of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The speech that sweareth much shall make the hair of the head stand upright: and its irreverence shall make one stop his ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Is the quarrels of the proud is the shedding of blood: and their cursing is a grievous hearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> He that discloseth the secret of a friend loseth his credit, and shall never find a friend to his mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Love thy neighbour, and be joined to him with fidelity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> But if thou discover his secrets, follow no more after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> For as a man that destroyeth his friend, so also is he that destroyeth the friendship of his neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And as one that letteth a bird go out of his hand, so hast thou let thy neighbour go, and thou shalt not get him again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Follow after him no more, for he is gone afar off, he is fled, as a roe escaped out of the snare: because his soul is wounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Thou canst no more bind him up. And of a curse there is reconciliation:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> But to disclose the secrets of a friend, leaveth no hope to an unhappy soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> He that winketh with the eye forgeth wicked things, and no man will cast him off:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> In the sight of thy eyes he will sweeten his mouth, and will admire thy words: but at the last he will writhe his mouth, and on thy words he will lay a stumblingblock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> I have hated many things, but not like him, and the Lord will hate him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> If one cast a stone on high, it will fall upon his own head: and the deceitful stroke will wound the deceitful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> He that diggeth a pit, shall fall into it: and he that setteth a stone for his neighbour, shall stumble upon it: and he that layeth a snare for another, shall perish in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> A mischievous counsel shall be rolled back upon the author, and he shall not know from whence it cometh to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Mockery and reproach are of the proud, and vengeance as a lion shall lie in wait for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> They shall perish in a snare that are delighted with the fall of the just: and sorrow shall consume them before they die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Anger and fury are both of them abominable, and the sinful man shall be subject to them.</span>
