<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> He that feareth God, will do good: and he that possesseth justice, shall lay hold on her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And she will meet him as an honourable mother, and will receive him as a wife married of a virgin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> With the bread of life and understanding, she shall feed him, and give him the water of wholesome wisdom to drink: and she shall be made strong in him, and he shall not be moved:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And she shall hold him fast, and he shall not be confounded: and she shall exalt him among his neighbours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And in the midst of the church she shall open his mouth, and shall fill him with the spirit of wisdom and understanding, and shall clothe him with a robe of glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> She shall heap upon him a treasure of joy and gladness, and shall cause him to inherit an everlasting name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> But foolish men shall not obtain her, and wise men shall meet her, foolish men shall not see her: for she is far from pride and deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Lying men shall not be mindful of her: but men that speak truth shall be found with her, and shall advance, even till they come to the sight of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Praise is not seemly in the mouth of a sinner:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> For wisdom came forth from God: for praise shall be with the wisdom of God, and shall abound in a faithful mouth, and the sovereign Lord will give praise unto it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Say not: It is through God, that she is not with me: for do not thou the things that he hateth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Say not: He hath caused me to err: for he hath no need of wicked men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The Lord hateth all abomination of error, and they that fear him shall not love it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> God made man from the beginning, and left him in the hand of his own counsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> He added his commandments and precepts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> If thou wilt keep the commandments and perform acceptable fidelity for ever, they shall preserve thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> He hath set water and fire before thee: stretch forth thy hand to which thou wilt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Before man is life and death, good and evil, that which he shall choose shall be given him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> For the wisdom of God is great, and he is strong in power, seeing all men without ceasing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The eyes of the Lord are towards them that fear him, and he knoweth all the work of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> He hath commanded no man to do wickedly, and he hath given no man license to sin:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> For he desireth not a multitude of faithless and unprofitable children.</span>
