<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The wise men will seek out the wisdom of all the ancients, and will be occupied in the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> He will keep the sayings of renowned men, and will enter withal into the subtilties of parables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> He will search out the hidden meanings of proverbs, and will be conversant in the secrets of parables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> He shall serve among great men, and: appear before the governor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> He shall pass into strange countries: for he shall try good and evil among men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> He will give his heart to resort early to the Lord that made him, and he will pray in the sight of the most High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> He will open his mouth in prayer, and will make supplication for his sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> For if it shall please the great Lord, he will fill him with the spirit of understanding:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And he will pour forth the words of his wisdom as showers, and in his prayer he will confess to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And he shall direct his counsel, and his knowledge, and in his secrets shall he meditate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> He shall shew forth the discipline he hath learned, and shall glory in the law of the covenant of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Many shall praise his wisdom, and it shall never be forgotten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The memory of him shall not depart away, and his name shall be in request from generation to generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Nations shall declare his wisdom, and the church shall shew forth his praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> If he continue, he shall leave a name above a thousand: and if he rest, it shall be to his advantage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> I will yet meditate that I may declare: for I am filled as with a holy transport.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> By a voice he saith: Hear me, ye divine offspring, and bud forth as the rose planted by the brooks of waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Give ye a sweet odour as frankincense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Send forth flowers, as the lily, and yield a smell, and bring forth leaves in grace, and praise with canticles, and bless the Lord in his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Magnify his name, and give glory to him with the voice of your lips, and with the canticles of your mouths, and with harps, and in praising him, you shall say in this manner:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> All the works of the Lord are exceeding good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> At his word the waters stood as a heap: and at the words of his mouth the receptacles of waters:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> For at his commandment favour is shewn, and there is no diminishing of his salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> The works of all flesh are before him, and there is nothing hid from his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> He seeth from eternity to eternity, and there is nothing wonderful before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> There is no saying: What is this, or what is that? for all things shall be sought in their time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> His blessing hath overflowed like a river.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And as a flood hath watered the earth; so shall his wrath inherit the nations, that have not sought after him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Even as he turned the waters into a dry land, and the earth was made dry: and his ways were made plain for their journey: so to sinners they are stumblingblocks in his wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Good things were created for the good from the beginning, so for the wicked, good and evil things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> The principal things necessary for the life of men, are water, fire, and iron, salt, milk, and bread of flour, and honey, and the cluster of the grape, and oil, and clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> All these things shall be for good to the holy, so to the sinners and the ungodly they shall be turned into evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> There are spirits that are created for vengeance, and in their fury they lay on grievous torments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> In the time of destruction they shall pour out their force: and they shall appease the wrath of him that made them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Fire, hail, famine, and death, all these were created for vengeance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> The teeth of beasts, and scorpions, and serpents, and the sword taking vengeance upon the ungodly unto destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> In his commandments they shall feast, and they shall be ready upon earth when need is, and when their time is come they shall not transgress his word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Therefore from the beginning I was resolved, and I have meditated, and thought on these things and left them in writing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> All the works of the Lord are good, and he will furnish every work in due time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> It is not to be said: This is worse than that: for all shall be well approved in their time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> Now therefore with the whole heart and mouth praise ye him, and bless the name of the Lord.</span>
