<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Let us now praise men of renown, and our fathers in their generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> The Lord hath wrought great glory through his magnificence from the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Such as have borne rule in their dominions, men of great power, and endued with their wisdom, shewing forth in the prophets the dignity of prophets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And ruling over the present people, and by the strength of wisdom instructing the people in most holy words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Such as by their skill sought out musical tunes, and published canticles of the scriptures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Rich men in virtue, studying beautifulness: living at peace in their houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> All these have gained glory in their generations, and were praised in their days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> They that were born of them have left a name behind them, that their praises might be related:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And there are some, of whom there is no memorial: who are perished, as if they had never been: and are become as if they had never been born, and their children with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> But these were men of mercy, whose godly deeds have not failed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Good things continue with their seed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Their posterity are a holy inheritance, and their seed hath stood in the covenants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And their children for their sakes remain for ever: their seed and their glory shall not be forsaken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Their bodies are buried in peace, and their name liveth unto generation and generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Let the people shew forth their wisdom, and the church declare their praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Henoch pleased God, and was translated into paradise, that he may give repentance to the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Noe was found perfect, just, and in the time of wrath he was made a reconciliation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Therefore was there a remnant left to the earth, when the flood came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> The covenants of the world were made with him, that all flesh should no more be destroyed with the flood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Abraham was the great father of a multitude of nations, and there was not found the like to him in glory, who kept the law of the most High, and was in covenant with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> In his flesh he established the covenant, and in temptation he was found faithful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Therefore by an oath he gave him glory in his posterity, that he should increase as the dust of the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And that he would exalt his seed as the stars, and they should inherit from sea to sea, and from the river to the ends of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And he did in like manner with Isaac for the sake of Abraham his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> The Lord gave him the blessing of all nations, and confirmed his covenant upon the head of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> He acknowledged him in his blessings, and gave him an inheritance, and divided him his portion in twelve tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And he preserved for him men of mercy, that found grace in the eyes of all flesh.</span>
