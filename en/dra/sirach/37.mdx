<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Every friend will say: I also am his friend: but there is a friend, that is only a friend in name. Is not this a grief even to death?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> But a companion and a friend shall be turned to an enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> O wicked presumption, whence camest thou to cover the earth with thy malice, and deceitfulness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> There is a companion who rejoiceth with his friend in his joys, but in the time of trouble, he will be against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> There is a companion who condoleth with his friend for his belly’s sake, and he will take up a shield against enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Forget not thy friend in thy mind, and be not unmindful of him in thy riches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Consult not with him that layeth a snare for thee, and hide thy counsel from them that envy thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Every counsellor giveth out counsel, but there is one that is a counsellor for himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Beware of a counsellor. And know before what need he hath: for he will devise to his own mind:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Lest he thrust a stake into the ground, and say to thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Thy way is good; and then stand on the other side to see what shall befall thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Treat not with a man without religion concerning holiness, nor with an unjust man concerning justice, nor with a woman touching her of whom she is jealous, nor with a coward concerning war, nor with a merchant about traffic, nor with a buyer of selling, nor with an envious man of giving thanks,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Nor with the ungodly of piety, nor with the dishonest of honesty, nor with the held labourer of every work,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Nor with him that worketh by the year of the finishing of the year, nor with an idle servant of much business: give no heed to these in any matter of counsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> But be continually with a holy man, whomsoever thou shalt know to observe the fear of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Whose soul is according to thy own soul: and who, when thou shalt stumble in the dark, will be sorry for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And establish within thyself a heart of good counsel: for there is no other thing of more worth to thee than it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The soul of a holy man discovereth sometimes true things, more than seven watchmen that sit in a high piece to watch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> But above all these things pray to the most High, that he may direct thy way in truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> In all thy works let the true word go before thee, and steady counsel before every action.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> A wicked word shall change the beast: out of which four manner of things arise, good and evil, life and death: and the tongue is continually the ruler of them. There is a man that is subtle and a teacher of many, and yet is unprofitable to his own soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> A skillful man hath taught many, and is sweet to his own soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> He that speaketh sophistically, is hateful: he shall be destitute of every thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Grace is not given him from the Lord: for he is deprived of all wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> There is a wise man that is wise to his own soul: and the fruit of his understanding is commendable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> A wise man instructeth his own people, and the fruits of his understanding are faithful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> A wise man shall be filled with blessings, and they that see shall praise him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> The life of a man is in the number of his days: but the days of Israel are innumerable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> A wise man shall inherit honour among his people, and his name shall live for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> My son, prove thy soul in thy life: and if it be wicked, give it no power:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> For all things are not expedient for all, and every kind pleaseth not every soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Be not greedy in any feasting, and pour not out thyself upon any meat:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> For in many meats there will be sickness, and greediness will turn to choler.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> By surfeiting many have perished: but he that is temperate, shall prolong life.</span>
