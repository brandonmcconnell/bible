<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> He that seeketh to revenge himself, shall find vengeance from the Lord, and he will surely keep his sins in remembrance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Forgive thy neighbour if he hath hurl thee: and then shall thy sins be forgiven to thee when thou prayest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Man to man reserveth anger, and doth he seek remedy of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> He hath no mercy on a man like himself, and doth he entreat for his own sins?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> He that is but flesh, nourisheth anger, and doth he ask forgiveness of God? who shall obtain pardon for his sins?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Remember thy last things, and let enmity cease:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> For corruption and death hang over in his commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Remember the fear of God, and be not angry with thy neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Remember the covenant of the most High, and overlook the ignorance of thy neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Refrain from strife, and thou shalt diminish thy sins:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> For a passionate man kindleth strife, and a sinful man will trouble his friends, and bring in debate in the midst of them that are at peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> For as the wood of the forest is, so the fire burneth: and as a man’s strength is, so shall his anger be, and according to his riches he shall increase his anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> A hasty contention kindleth a fire: and a hasty quarrel sheddeth blood: and a tongue that beareth witness bringeth death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> If thou blow the spark, it shall burn as a fire: and if thou spit upon it, it shall be quenched: both come out of the mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The whisperer and the double tongued is accursed: for he hath troubled many that were at peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The tongue of a third person hath disquieted many, and scattered them from nation to nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> It hath destroyed the strong cities of the rich, and hath overthrown the houses of great men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> It hath cut in pieces the forces of people, and undone strong nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> The tongue of a third person hath cast out valiant women, and deprived them of their labours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> He that hearkeneth to it, shall never have rest, neither shall he have a friend in whom he may repose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The stroke of a whip maketh a blue mark: but the stroke of the tongue will break the bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Many have fallen by the edge of the sword, but not so many as have perished by their own tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Blessed is he that is defended from a wicked tongue, that hath not passed into the wrath thereof, and that hath not drawn the yoke thereof, and hath not been bound in its bands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> For its yoke is a yoke of iron: and its bands are bands of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> The death thereof is a most evil death: and hell is preferable to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Its continuance shall not be for a long time, but it shall possess the ways of the unjust: and the just shall not be burnt with its flame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> They that forsake God shall fall into it, and it shall burn in them, and shall not be quenched, and it shall be sent upon them as a lion, and as a leopard it shall tear them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Hedge in thy ears with thorns, hear not a wicked tongue, and make doors and bars to thy mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Melt down thy gold and silver, and make a balance for thy words, and a just bridle for thy mouth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And take heed lest thou slip with thy tongue, and fall in the sight of thy enemies who lie in wait for thee, and thy fall be incurable unto death.</span>
