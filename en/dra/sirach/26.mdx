<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Happy is the husband of a good wife: for the number of his years is double.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> A virtuous woman rejoiceth her husband: and shall fulfill the years of his life in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> A good wife is a good portion, she shall be given in the portion of them that fear God, to a man for his good deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Rich or poor, if his heart is good, his countenance shall be cheerful at all times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Of three things my heart hath been afraid, and at the fourth my face hath trembled:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> The accusation of a city, and the gathering together of the people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And a false calumny, all are more grievous than death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> A jealous woman is the grief and mourning of the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> With a jealous woman is a scourge of the tongue which communicateth with all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> As a yoke of oxen that is moved to and fro, so also is a wicked woman: he that hath hold of her, is as he that taketh hold of a scorpion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> A drunken woman is a great wrath: and her reproach and shame shall not be hid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The fornication of a woman shall be known by the haughtiness of her eyes, and by her eyelids.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> On a daughter that turneth not away herself, set a strict watch: lest finding an opportunity she abuse herself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Take heed of the impudence of her eyes, and wonder not if she slight thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> She will open her mouth as a thirsty traveller to the fountain, and will drink of every water near her, and will sit down by every hedge, and open her quiver against every arrow, until she fail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The grace of a diligent woman shall delight her husband, and shall fat his bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Her discipline is the gift of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Such is a wise and silent woman, and there is nothing so much worth as a well instructed soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> A holy and shamefaced woman is grace upon grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And no price is worthy of a continent soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> As the sun when it riseth to the world in the high places of God, so is the beauty of a good wife for the ornament of her house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> As the lamp shining upon the holy candlestick, so is the beauty of the face in a ripe age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> As golden pillars upon bases of silver, so are the firm feet upon the soles of a steady woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> As everlasting foundations upon a solid rock, so the commandments of God In the heart of a holy woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> At two things my heart is grieved, and the third bringeth anger upon me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> A man of was fainting through poverty: and a man of sense despised:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And he that passeth over from justice to sin, God hath prepared such an one for the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Two sorts of callings have appeared to me hard and dangerous: a merchant is hardly free from negligence: and a huckster shall not be justified from the sins of the lips.</span>
