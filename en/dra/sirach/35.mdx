<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> He that keepeth the law, multiplieth offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> It is a wholesome sacrifice to take heed to the commandments, and to depart from all iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And to depart from injustice, is to offer a propitiatory sacrifice for injustices, and a begging of pardon for sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> He shall return thanks, that offereth fine flour: and he that doth mercy, offereth sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> To depart from iniquity is that which pleaseth the Lord, and to depart from injustice, is an entreaty for sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Thou shalt not appear empty in the sight of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> For all these things are to be done because of the commandment of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The oblation of the just maketh the altar fat, and is an odour of sweetness in the sight of the most High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The sacrifice of the just is acceptable, and the Lord will not forget the memorial thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Give glory to God with a good heart: and diminish not the firstfruits of thy hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> In every gift shew a cheerful countenance, and sanctify thy tithes with joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Give to the most High according to what he hath given to thee, and with a good eye do according to the ability of thy hands:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> For the Lord maketh recompense, and will give thee seven times as much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Do not offer wicked gifts, for such he will not receive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And look not upon an unjust sacrifice, for the Lord is judge, and there is not with him respect of person.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The Lord will not accept any person against a poor man, and he will hear the prayer of him that is wronged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> He will not despise the prayers of the fatherless; nor the widow, when she poureth out her complaint.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Do not the widow’s tears run down the cheek, and her cry against him that causeth them to fall?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> For from the cheek they go up even to heaven, and the Lord that heareth will not be delighted with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> He that adoreth God with joy, shall be accepted, and his prayer shall approach even to the clouds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The prayer of him that humbleth himself, shall pierce the clouds: and till it come nigh he will not be comforted: and he will not depart till the most High behold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And the Lord will not be slack, but will judge for the just, and will do judgment: and the Almighty will not have patience with them, that he may crush their back:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And he will repay vengeance to the Gentiles, till he have taken away the multitude of the proud, and broken the sceptres of the unjust,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Till he have rendered to men according to their deeds: and according to the works of Adam, and according to his presumption,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Till he have judged the cause of his people, and he shall delight the just with his mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> The mercy of God is beautiful in the time of affliction, as a cloud of rain in the time of drought.</span>
