<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The firmament on high is his beauty, the beauty of heaven with its glorious shew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> The sun when he appeareth shewing forth at his rising, an admirable instrument, the work of the most High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> At noon he burneth the earth, and who can abide his burning heat? As one keeping a furnace in the works of heat:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> The sun three times as much, burneth the mountains, breathing out fiery vapours, and shining with his beams, he blindeth the eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Great is the Lord that made him, and at his words he hath hastened his course.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And the moon in all in her season, is for a declaration of times and a sign of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> From the moon is the sign of the festival day, a light that decreaseth in her perfection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The month is called after her name, increasing wonderfully in her perfection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Being an instrument of the armies on high, shining gloriously in the Armament of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> The glory of the stars is the beauty of heaven; the Lord enlighteneth the world on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> By the words of the holy one they shall stand in judgment, and shall never fail in their watches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Look upon the rainbow, and bless him that made it: it is very beautiful in its brightness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> It encompasseth the heaven about with the circle of its glory, the hands of the most High have displayed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> By his commandment he maketh the snow to fall apace, and sendeth forth swiftly the lightnings of his judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Through this are the treasures opened, and the clouds fly out like birds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> By his greatness he hath fixed the clouds, and the hailstones are broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> At his sight shall the mountains be shaken, and at his will the south wind shall blow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The noise of his thunder shall strike the earth, so doth the northern storm, and the whirlwind:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And as the birds lighting upon the earth, he scattereth snow, and the falling thereof, is as the coming down of locusts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The eye admireth at the beauty of the whiteness thereof, and the heart is astonished at the shower thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> He shall pour frost as salt upon the earth: and when it freezeth, it shall become like the tops of thistles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The cold north wind bloweth, and the water is congealed into crystal; upon every gathering together of waters it shall rest, and shall clothe the waters as a breastplate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And it shall devour the mountains, and burn the wilderness, and consume all that is green as with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> A present remedy of all is the speedy coming of a cloud, and a dew that meeteth it, by the heat that cometh, shall overpower it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> At his word the wind is still, and with his thought he appeaseth the deep, and the Lord hath planted islands therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Let them that sail on the sea, tell the dangers thereof: and when we hear with our ears, we shall admire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> There are great and wonderful works: a variety of beasts, and of all living things, and the monstrous creatures of whales.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Through him is established the end of their journey, and by his word all things are regulated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> We shall say much, and yet shall want words: but the sum of our words is, He is all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> What shall we be able to do to glorify him? for the Almighty himself is above all his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> The Lord is terrible, and exceeding great, and his power is admirable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Glorify the Lord as much as ever you can, for he will yet far exceed, and his magnificence is wonderful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Blessing the Lord, exalt him as much as you can: for he is above all praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> When you exalt him put forth all your strength, and be not weary: for you can never go far enough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Who shall see him, and declare him? and who shall magnify him as he is from the beginning?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> There are many things hidden from us that are greater than these: for we have seen but a few of his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> But the Lord hath made all things, and to the godly he hath given wisdom.</span>
