<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Have they made thee ruler? be not lifted up: be among them as one of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Have care of them, and so sit down, and when thou hast acquitted thyself of all thy charge, take thy place:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> That thou mayst rejoice for them, and receive a crown as an ornament of grace, and get the honour of the contribution.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Speak, thou that art elder: for it becometh thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> To speak the first word with care knowledge, and hinder not music.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Where there is no hearing, pour out words, and be not lifted up out season with thy wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> A concert of music in a banquet wine is as a carbuncle set in gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> As a signet of an emerald in a work of gold: so is the melody of music with pleasant and moderate wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Hear in silence, and for thy reverence good grace shall come to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Young man, scarcely speak in thy own cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> If thou be asked twice, let thy answer be short.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> In many things be as if thou wert ignorant, and hear in silence and withal seeking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> In the company of great men bake not upon thee: and when the ancients are present, speak not much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Before a storm goeth lightning: and before shamefacedness goeth favour: and for thy reverence good grace shall come to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And at the time of rising be not slack: but be first to run home to thy house, and there withdraw thyself, and there take thy pastime.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And do what thou hast a mind, but not in sin or proud speech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And for all these things bless the Lord, that made thee, and that replenisheth thee with all his good things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> He that feareth the Lord, will receive his discipline: and they that will seek him early, shall find a blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> He that seeketh the law, shall be filled with it: and he that dealeth deceitfully, shall meet with a stumblingblock therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> They that fear the Lord, shall find just judgment, and shall kindle justice as a light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> A sinful man will flee reproof, and will find an excuse according to his will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> A man of counsel will not neglect understanding, a strange and proud man will not dread fear:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Even after he hath done with fear without counsel, he shall be controlled by the things of his own seeking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> My son, do thou nothing without counsel, and thou shalt not repent when thou hast done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Go not in the way of ruin, and thou shalt not stumble against the stones; trust not thyself to a rugged may, lest thou set a stumblingblock to thy soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And beware of thy own children, and take heed of them of thy household.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> In every work of thine regard thy soul in faith: for this is the keeping of the commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> He that believeth God, taketh heed to the commandments: and he that trusteth in him, shall fare never the worse.</span>
