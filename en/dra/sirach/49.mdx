<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The memory of Josias is like the composition of a sweet smell made by the art of a perfumer:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> His remembrance shall be sweet as honey in every mouth, and as music at a banquet of wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> He was directed by God unto the repentance of the nation, and he took away the abominations of wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And he directed his heart towards the Lord, and in the days of sinners he strengthened godliness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Except David, and Ezechias, and Josias, all committed sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> For the kings of Juda forsook the law of the most High, and despised the fear of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> So they gave their kingdom to others, and their glory to a strange nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> They burnt the chosen city of holiness, and made the streets thereof desolate according to the prediction of Jeremias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> For they treated him evil, who was consecrated a prophet from his mother’s womb, to overthrow, and pluck up, and destroy, and to build again, and renew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> It was Ezechiel that saw the glorious vision, which was shewn him upon the chariot of cherubims.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> For he made mention of the enemies under the figure of rain, and of doing good to them that shewed right ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And may the bones of the twelve prophets spring up out of their place: for they strengthened Jacob, and redeemed themselves by strong faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> How shall we magnify Zorobabel? for he was as a signet on the right hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> In like manner Jesus the son of Josedec? who in their days built the house, and set up a holy temple to the Lord, prepared for everlasting glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And let Nehemias be a long time remembered, who raised up for us our walls that were cast down, and set up the gates and the bars, who rebuilt our houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> No man was born upon earth like Henoch: for he also was taken up from the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Nor as Joseph, who was a man born prince of his brethren, the support of his family, the ruler of his brethren, the stay of the people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And his bones were visited, and after death they prophesied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Seth and Sem obtained glory among men: and above every soul Adam in the beginning.</span>
