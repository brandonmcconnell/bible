<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> A wise judge shall judge his people, and the government of a prudent man shall be steady.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> As the judge of the people is himself, so also are his ministers: and what manner of man the ruler of a city is, such also are they that dwell therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> An unwise king shall be the ruin of his people: and cities shall be inhabited through the prudence of the rulers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> The power of the earth is in the hand of God, and in his time he will raise up a profitable ruler over it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The prosperity of man is in the hand of God, and upon the person of the scribe he shall lay his honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Remember not any injury done thee by thy neighbour, and do thou nothing by deeds of injury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Pride is hateful before God and men: and all iniquity of nations is execrable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> A kingdom is translated from one people to another, because of injustices, and wrongs, and injuries, and divers deceits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> But nothing is more wicked than the covetous man. Why is earth and ashes proud?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> There is not a more wicked thing than to love money: for such a one setteth even his own soul to sale: because while he liveth he hath cast away his bowels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> All power is of short life. A long sickness is troublesome to the physician.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The physician cutteth off it short sickness: so also a king is today, and tomorrow he shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> For when a man shall die, he shall inherit serpents, and beasts, and worms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The beginning of the pride of man, is to fall off from God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Because his heart is departed from him that made him: for pride is the beginning of all sin: be that holdeth it, shall be filled with maledictions, and it shall ruin him in the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Therefore hath the Lord disgraced the assemblies of the wicked, and hath utterly destroyed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> God hath overturned the thrones of proud princes, and hath set up the meek in their stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> God hath made the roots of proud nations to wither, and hath planted the humble of these nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> The Lord hath overthrown the lands of the Gentiles, and hath destroyed them even to the foundation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> He hath made some of them to wither away, and hath destroyed them, and hath made the memory of them to cease from the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> God hath abolished the memory of the proud, and hath preserved the memory of them that are humble in mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Pride was not made for men: nor wrath for the race of women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> That seed of men shall be honoured, which feareth God: but that seed shall be dishonoured, which transgresseth the commandments of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> In the midst of brethren their chief is honourable: so shall they that fear the Lord, be in his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> The fear of God is the glory of the rich, and of the honourable, and of the poor:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Despise not a just man that is poor, and do not magnify a sinful man that is rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> The great man, and the judge, and the mighty is in honour: and there is none greater than he that feareth God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> They that are free shall serve a servant that is wise: and a man that is prudent and well instructed will not murmur when he is reproved; and he that is ignorant, shall not be honoured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Extol not thyself in doing thy work, and linger not in the time of distress:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Better is he that laboureth, and aboundeth in all things, than he that boasteth himself and wanteth bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> My son, keep thy soul in meekness, and give it honour according to its desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Who will justify him that sinneth against his own soul? and who will honour him that dishonoureth his own soul?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> The poor man is glorified by his discipline and fear: and there is a man that is honoured for his wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> But he that is glorified in poverty, how much more in wealth? and he that is glorified in wealth, let him fear poverty.</span>
