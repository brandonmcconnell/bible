<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 28:4; Isaiah 24:13; Hosea 9:10"> Woe is me, for I am become as one that gleaneth in autumn the grapes of the vintage: there is no cluster to eat, my soul desired the firstripe figs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 12:1; Isaiah 57:1; Isaiah 59:7"> The holy man is perished out of the earth, and there is none upright among men: they all lie in wait for blood, every one hunteth his brother to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Proverbs 4:16–17; Amos 5:12"> The evil of their hands they call good: the prince requireth, and the judge is for giving: and the great man hath uttered the desire of his soul, and they have troubled it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 22:5; Ezekiel 2:6; Isaiah 10:3"> He that is best among them, is as a brier: and he that is righteous, as the thorn of the hedge. The day of thy inspection, thy visitation cometh: now shall be their destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:4; Psalms 118:8–9; Matthew 10:16"> Believe not a friend, and trust not in a prince: keep the doors of thy mouth from her that sleepeth in thy bosom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 10:35–36; Luke 12:53; Matthew 10:21"> For the son dishonoureth the father, and the daughter riseth up against her mother, the daughter in law against her mother in law: and a man’s enemies are they of his own household.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25–26; Psalms 37:7; 1 John 5:14–15"> But I will look towards the Lord, I will wait for God my Saviour: my God will hear me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 8:12; Isaiah 9:2; Psalms 27:1"> Rejoice not, thou, my enemy, over me, because I am fallen: I shall arise, when I sit in darkness, the Lord is my light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 37:6; 1 Corinthians 4:5; 1 Samuel 24:15"> I will bear the wrath of the Lord, because I have sinned against him; until he judge my cause and execute judgment for me: he will bring me forth into the light, I shall behold his justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 10:5; Jeremiah 50:33–34; Psalms 42:10"> And my enemy shall behold, and she shall be covered with shame, who saith to me: Where is the Lord thy God? My eyes shall look down upon her: now shall she be trodden under foot as the mire of the streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Amos 9:11–15; Nehemiah 2:8; Nehemiah 2:17"> The day shall come, that thy walls may be built up: in that day shall the law be far removed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 19:23–25; Isaiah 11:16; Hosea 11:11"> In that day they shall come even from Assyria to thee, and to the fortified cities: and from the fortified cities even to the river, and from sea to sea, and from mountain to mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 3:10–11; Jeremiah 25:11; Micah 6:13"> And the land shall be made desolate, because of the inhabitants thereof, and for the fruit of their devices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 23:1–4; Micah 5:4; Psalms 100:3"> Feed thy people with thy rod, the flock of thy inheritance, them that dwell alone in the forest, in the midst of Carmel: they shall feed in Basan and Galaad according to the days of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 78:12–72; Exodus 3:20; Psalms 68:22"> According to the days of thy coming out of the land of Egypt I will shew him wonders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 26:11; Isaiah 52:15; Job 29:9–10"> The nations shall see, and shall be confounded at all their strength: they shall put the hand upon the mouth, their ears shall be deaf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; Psalms 72:9; Psalms 18:45"> They shall lick the dust like serpents, as the creeping things of the earth, they shall be disturbed in their houses: they shall dread the Lord our God, and shall fear thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Isaiah 44:22; Psalms 103:8–9"> Who is a God like to thee, who takest away iniquity, and passest by the sin of the remnant of thy inheritance? he will send his fury in no more, because he delighteth in mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 38:17; Romans 6:14; Isaiah 43:25"> He will turn again, and have mercy on us: he will put away our iniquities: and he will cast all our sins into the bottom of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 28:13–14; Luke 1:72–74; Deuteronomy 7:8"> Thou wilt perform the truth of Jacob, the mercy to Abraham: which thou hast sworn to our fathers from the days of old.</span>
