<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 1:10; Jeremiah 5:4–5; Psalms 82:1–5"> And I said: Hear, O ye princes of Jacob, and ye chiefs of the house of Israel: Is it not your part to know judgment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:27; Proverbs 28:4; Psalms 53:4"> You that hate good, and love evil: that violently pluck off their skins from them, and their flesh from their bones?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 14:4; Ezekiel 11:6–7; Ezekiel 11:3"> Who have eaten the flesh of my people, and have flayed their skin from off them: and have broken, and chopped their bones as for the kettle, and as flesh in the midst of the pot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 1:28; Isaiah 1:15; Ezekiel 8:18"> Then shall they cry to the Lord, and he will not hear them: and he will hide his face from them at that time, as they have behaved wickedly in their devices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:14; Jeremiah 14:14–15; Ezekiel 22:25–29"> Thus saith the Lord concerning the prophets that make my people err: that bite with their teeth, and preach peace: and if a man give not something into their mouth, they prepare war against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 8:20–22; Amos 8:9–10; Isaiah 29:10"> Therefore night shall be to you instead of vision, and darkness to you instead of divination; and the sun shall go down upon the prophets, and the day shall be darkened over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 13:4; Isaiah 44:25; 1 Samuel 28:6"> And they shall be confounded that see visions, and the diviners shall be confounded: and they shall all cover their faces, because there is no answer of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 58:1; 1 Corinthians 2:12–13; 1 Corinthians 2:4"> But yet I am filled with the strength of the spirit of the Lord, with judgment, and power: to declare unto Jacob his wickedness, and to Israel his sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 1:23; Psalms 58:1–2; Proverbs 17:15"> Hear this, ye princes of the house of Jacob, and ye judges of the house of Israel: you that abhor judgment, and pervert all that is right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:9–12; Jeremiah 22:13–17; Matthew 27:25"> You that build up Sion with blood, and Jerusalem with iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:13; Isaiah 1:23; Micah 3:5"> Her princes have judged for bribes, and her priests have taught for hire, and her prophets divined for money: and they leaned upon the Lord, saying: Is not the Lord in the midst of us? no evil shall come upon us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:18; Psalms 79:1; Micah 4:1–2"> Therefore, because of you, Sion shall be ploughed as a field, and Jerusalem shall be as a heap of stones, and the mountain of the temple as the high places of the forests.</span>
