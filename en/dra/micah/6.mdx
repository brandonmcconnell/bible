<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:29; Deuteronomy 32:1; Micah 1:2"> Hear ye what the Lord saith: Arise, contend thou in judgment against the mountains, and let the hills hear thy voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 4:1; Hosea 12:2; Isaiah 1:18"> Let the mountains hear the judgment of the Lord, and the strong foundations of the earth: for the Lord will enter into judgment with his people, and he will plead against Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:5; Isaiah 43:22–23; Psalms 81:13"> O my people, what have I done to thee, or in what have I molested thee? answer thou me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 12:51; Deuteronomy 7:8; Amos 2:10"> For I brought thee up out of the land of Egypt, and delivered thee out of the house of slaves: and I sent before thy face Moses, and Aaron, and Mary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 5:9–10; Numbers 25:1; Judges 5:11"> O my people, remember, I pray thee, what Balach the king of Moab purposed: and what Balaam the son of Beor answered him, from Setim to Galgal, that thou mightest know the justices of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 51:16–17; Romans 10:2–3; Psalms 40:6–8"> What shall I offer to the Lord that is worthy? wherewith shall I kneel before the high God? shall I offer holocausts unto him, and calves of a year old?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 16:3; 1 Samuel 15:22; Psalms 51:16"> May the Lord be appeased with thousands of rams, or with many thousands of fat he goats? shall I give my firstborn for my wickedness, the fruit of my body for the sin of my soul?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:12–13; Proverbs 21:3; Colossians 3:12"> I will shew thee, O man, what is good, and what the Lord requireth of thee: Verily, to do judgment, and to love mercy, and to walk solicitous with thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Hosea 14:9; Isaiah 30:27"> The voice of the Lord crieth to the city, and salvation shall be to them that fear thy name: hear, O ye tribes, and who shall approve it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 3:10; Amos 8:5–6; Jeremiah 5:26–27"> As yet there is a fire in the house of the wicked, the treasures of iniquity, and a scant measure full of wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 12:7; Leviticus 19:36; Proverbs 16:11"> Shall I justify wicked balances, and the deceitful weights of the bag?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 1:23; Jeremiah 9:8; Hosea 7:13"> By which her rich men were filled with iniquity, and the inhabitants thereof have spoken lies, and their tongue was deceitful in their mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 12:23; Deuteronomy 28:21–22; Hosea 13:16"> And I therefore began to strike thee with desolation for thy sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hosea 4:10; Leviticus 26:26; Isaiah 65:13"> Thou shalt eat, but shalt not be filled: and thy humiliation shall be in the midst of thee: and thou shalt take hold, but shalt not save: and those whom thou shalt save, I will give up to the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:13; Amos 5:11; Jeremiah 12:13"> Thou shalt sow, but shalt not reap: thou shalt tread the olives, but shalt not be anointed with the oil: and the new wine, but shalt not drink the wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:51; 1 Kings 16:25–33; Jeremiah 7:24"> For thou hast kept the statutes of Amri, and all the works of the house of Achab: and thou hast walked according to their wills, that I should make thee a desolation, and the inhabitants thereof a hissing, and you shall bear the reproach of my people.</span>
