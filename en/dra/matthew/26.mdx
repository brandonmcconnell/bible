<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; Matthew 19:1"> And it came to pass, when Jesus had ended all these words, he said to his disciples:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 22:1–2; Mark 14:1–2; John 13:1–2"> You know that after two days shall be the pasch, and the son of man shall be delivered up to be crucified:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:47–53; John 18:24; Mark 14:54"> Then were gathered together the chief priests and ancients of the people into the court of the high priest, who was called Caiphas:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 12:14; Psalms 2:2; Acts 13:10"> And they consulted together, that by subtilty they might apprehend Jesus, and put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 27:24; Matthew 21:26; Matthew 14:5"> But they said: Not on the festival day, lest perhaps there should be a tumult among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; Luke 7:37–39; John 12:1–8"> And when Jesus was in Bethania, in the house of Simon the leper,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 12:2–3; Luke 7:46; Luke 7:37–38"> There came to him a woman having an alabaster box of precious ointment, and poured it on his head as he was at table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 12:4–6; Mark 14:4; Exodus 5:17"> And the disciples seeing it, had indignation, saying: To what purpose is this waste?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 12:5–6; Mark 14:5; Joshua 7:20–21"> For this might have been sold for much, and given to the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Titus 3:8; Hebrews 13:21; 1 Timothy 5:10"> And Jesus knowing it, said to them: Why do you trouble this woman? for she hath wrought a good work upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:11; Mark 14:7; John 12:8"> For the poor you have always with you: but me you have not always.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 14:8; John 12:7; Mark 16:1"> For she in pouring this ointment upon my body, hath done it for my burial.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 14:9; Matthew 24:14; Psalms 112:6"> Amen I say to you, wheresoever this gospel shall be preached in the whole world, that also which she hath done, shall be told for a memory of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 22:3–6; Matthew 10:4; Mark 14:10–11"> Then went one of the twelve, who was called Judas Iscariot, to the chief priests,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 11:12–13; Genesis 37:26–28; Exodus 21:32"> And said to them: What will you give me, and I will deliver him unto you? But they appointed him thirty pieces of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 14:11; Luke 22:6"> And from thenceforth he sought opportunity to betray him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 22:7–13; Mark 14:12–16; Exodus 12:18–20"> And on the first day of the Azymes, the disciples came to Jesus, saying: Where wilt thou that we prepare for thee to eat the pasch?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 14:13–16; John 7:6; John 17:1"> But Jesus said: Go ye into the city to a certain man, and say to him: the master saith, My time is near at hand, with thee I make the pasch with my disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 2:5; John 15:14; Matthew 21:6"> And the disciples did as Jesus appointed to them, and they prepared the pasch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 14:17–21; John 13:21; Luke 22:14–16"> But when it was evening, he sat down with his twelve disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 13:21; Hebrews 4:13; John 6:70–71"> And whilst they were eating, he said: Amen I say to you, that one of you is about to betray me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 13:22–25; Luke 22:23; Mark 14:19–20"> And they being very much troubled, began every one to say: Is it I, Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 13:18; Psalms 41:9; John 13:26–28"> But he answering, said: He that dippeth his hand with me in the dish, he shall betray me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 26:22–23; Luke 24:46; Matthew 18:7"> The Son of man indeed goeth, as it is written of him: but woe to that man by whom the Son of man shall be betrayed: it were better for him, if that man had not been born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 26:64; Matthew 27:11; Luke 22:70"> And Judas that betrayed him, answering, said: Is it I, Rabbi? He saith to him: Thou hast said it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:23–29; Luke 22:18–20; 1 Corinthians 10:16–17"> And whilst they were at supper, Jesus took bread, and blessed, and broke: and gave to his disciples, and said: Take ye, and eat. This is my body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 25:6; 1 Corinthians 10:16; Psalms 116:13"> And taking the chalice, he gave thanks, and gave to them, saying: Drink ye all of this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 10:4–14; Hebrews 9:14–22; Leviticus 17:11"> For this is my blood of the new testament, which shall be shed for many unto remission of sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Revelation 7:17; Mark 14:25"> And I say to you, I will not drink from henceforth of this fruit of the vine, until that day when I shall drink it with you new in the kingdom of my Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 21:1; Ephesians 5:19–20; Luke 22:39"> And a hymn being said, they went out unto mount Olivet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; Mark 14:27–28; John 16:32"> Then Jesus said to them: All you shall be scandalized in me this night. For it is written: I will strike the shepherd, and the sheep of the flock shall be dispersed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 28:10; Luke 18:33–34; Matthew 28:16"> But after I shall be risen again, I will go before you into Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 22:33; John 13:36–38; Proverbs 28:25–26"> And Peter answering, said to him: Although all shall be scandalized in thee, I will never be scandalized.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 13:38; Luke 22:34; Matthew 26:75"> Jesus said to him: Amen I say to thee, that in this night before the cock crow, thou wilt deny me thrice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Romans 11:20; John 13:37; Proverbs 29:23"> Peter saith to him: Yea, though I should die with thee, I will not deny thee. And in like manner said all the disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 14:32–42; Matthew 26:42; Luke 22:39–46"> Then Jesus came with them into a country place which is called Gethsemani; and he said to his disciples: Sit you here, till I go yonder and pray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 4:21; Matthew 17:1; Mark 5:37"> And taking with him Peter and the two sons of Zebedee, he began to grow sorrowful and to be sad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 12:27; 1 Peter 3:18; 1 Peter 2:24"> Then he saith to them: My soul is sorrowful even unto death: stay you here, and watch with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 6:38; Philippians 2:8; Luke 22:41–42"> And going a little further, he fell upon his face, praying, and saying: My Father, if it be possible, let this chalice pass from me. Nevertheless not as I will, but as thou wilt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 14:37; Luke 22:45; Matthew 26:43"> And he cometh to his disciples, and findeth them asleep, and he saith to Peter: What? Could you not watch one hour with me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 14:38; Luke 22:46; Ephesians 6:18"> Watch ye, and pray that ye enter not into temptation. The spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh weak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 26:39; Mark 14:39–40; Hebrews 4:15"> Again the second time, he went and prayed, saying: My Father, if this chalice may not pass away, but I must drink it, thy will be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 9:32; Romans 13:1; Proverbs 23:34"> And he cometh again and findeth them sleeping: for their eyes were heavy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:8; Luke 18:1; Matthew 6:7"> And leaving them, he went again: and he prayed the third time, saying the selfsame word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 13:1; John 12:27; Mark 14:41–42"> Then he cometh to his disciples, and saith to them: Sleep ye now and take your rest; behold the hour is at hand, and the Son of man shall be betrayed into the hands of sinners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 9:51; John 14:31; 1 Samuel 17:48"> Rise, let us go: behold he is at hand that will betray me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 1:16; Matthew 26:55; Mark 14:43–50"> As he yet spoke, behold Judas, one of the twelve, came, and with him a great multitude with swords and clubs, sent from the chief priests and the ancients of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 55:20–21; Psalms 28:3; 2 Samuel 3:27"> And he that betrayed him, gave them a sign, saying: Whomsoever I shall kiss, that is he, hold him fast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Matthew 26:25; 1 Thessalonians 5:26; Proverbs 27:6"> And forthwith coming to Jesus, he said: Hail, Rabbi. And he kissed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 20:13; Matthew 22:12; Psalms 41:9"> And Jesus said to him: Friend, whereto art thou come? Then they came up, and laid hands on Jesus, and held him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="John 18:36; Mark 14:47; 2 Corinthians 10:4"> And behold one of them that were with Jesus, stretching forth his hand, drew out his sword: and striking the servant of the high priest, cut off his ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Revelation 13:10; Genesis 9:6; Romans 12:19"> Then Jesus saith to him: Put up again thy sword into its place: for all that take the sword shall perish with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="2 Kings 6:17; Matthew 4:11; Daniel 7:10"> Thinkest thou that I cannot ask my Father, and he will give me presently more than twelve legions of angels?</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; Matthew 26:24; Acts 1:16"> How then shall the scriptures be fulfilled, that so it must be done?</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Mark 12:35; John 8:2; Luke 22:52–53"> In that same hour Jesus said to the multitudes: You are come out as it were to a robber with swords and clubs to apprehend me. I sat daily with you, teaching in the temple, and you laid not hands on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; John 16:32; Mark 14:50–52"> Now all this was done, that the scriptures of the prophets might be fulfilled. Then the disciples all leaving him, fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; Mark 14:53–65; Psalms 56:5–6"> But they holding Jesus led him to Caiphas the high priest, where the scribes and the ancients were assembled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="John 7:32; John 18:15–16; John 18:25"> And Peter followed him afar off, even to the court of the high priest. And going in, he sat with the servants, that he might see the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Acts 6:11–13; Deuteronomy 19:16–21; Mark 14:55–56"> And the chief priests and the whole council sought false witness against Jesus, that they might put him to death:</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:15; Psalms 35:11; Psalms 27:12"> And they found not, whereas many false witnesses had come in. And last of all there came two false witnesses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="John 2:19–21; Matthew 27:40; Mark 15:29"> And they said: This man said, I am able to destroy the temple of God, and after three days to rebuild it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="John 19:9–11; John 18:19–24; Mark 14:60"> And the high priest rising up, said to him: Answerest thou nothing to the things which these witness against thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Leviticus 5:1; Matthew 16:16; Isaiah 53:7"> But Jesus held his peace. And the high priest said to him: I adjure thee by the living God, that thou tell us if thou be the Christ the Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Daniel 7:13; Mark 14:62; Psalms 110:1"> Jesus saith to him: Thou hast said it. Nevertheless I say to you, hereafter you shall see the Son of man sitting on the right hand of the power of God, and coming in the clouds of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:24; John 10:33; Matthew 9:3"> Then the high priests rent his garments, saying: He hath blasphemed; what further need have we of witnesses? Behold, now you have heard the blasphemy:</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="John 19:7; Acts 7:52; Leviticus 24:11–16"> What think you? But they answering, said: He is guilty of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Isaiah 50:6; Isaiah 53:3; Matthew 27:30"> Then did they spit in his face, and buffeted him: and others struck his face with the palms of their hands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Luke 22:63–65; Mark 14:65; 1 Peter 2:4–8"> Saying: Prophesy unto us, O Christ, who is he that struck thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="1 Kings 19:13; Matthew 26:71; Acts 5:37"> But Peter sat without in the court: and there came to him a servant maid, saying: Thou also wast with Jesus the Galilean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:12; Revelation 21:8; Matthew 26:40–43"> But he denied before them all, saying: I know not what thou sayest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Mark 14:68–69; John 18:25–27; Luke 22:58"> And as he went out of the gate, another maid saw him, and she saith to them that were there: This man also was with Jesus of Nazareth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72" data-xref="Matthew 26:74; Isaiah 48:1; Zechariah 8:17"> And again he denied with an oath, I know not the man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v73" data-v="73" data-xref="Judges 12:6; John 18:26–27; Nehemiah 13:24"> And after a little while they came that stood by, and said to Peter: Surely thou also art one of them; for even thy speech doth discover thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v74" data-v="74" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; 1 Corinthians 16:22; Mark 14:71–72"> Then he began to curse and to swear that he knew not the man. And immediately the cock crew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v75" data-v="75" data-xref="Matthew 26:34; Romans 7:18–20; Luke 22:31–34"> And Peter remembered the word of Jesus which he had said: Before the cock crow, thou wilt deny me thrice. And going forth, he wept bitterly.</span>
