<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 3:13; Matthew 15:29; Mark 4:1"> And seeing the multitudes, he went up into a mountain, and when he was set down, his disciples came unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 8:35; Luke 6:20–26; Acts 10:34"> And opening his mouth, he taught them, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 66:2; Proverbs 29:23; Psalms 34:18"> Blessed are the poor in spirit: for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; 2 Corinthians 7:9–10; John 16:20–22"> Blessed are the meek: for they shall possess the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 37:11; Isaiah 29:19; James 3:13"> Blessed are they that mourn: for they shall be comforted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 4:14; Psalms 107:9; John 6:27"> Blessed are they that hunger and thirst after justice: for they shall have their fill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32–1; Matthew 6:14–15; 2 Samuel 22:26"> Blessed are the merciful: for they shall obtain mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 24:4; Psalms 51:10; Hebrews 10:22"> Blessed are the clean of heart: for they shall see God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 12:14; James 3:16–18; Romans 12:18"> Blessed are the peacemakers: for they shall be called children of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 1:12; 1 Peter 3:13–14; 2 Corinthians 4:17"> Blessed are they that suffer persecution for justice’ sake: for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 4:14; Luke 21:17; Mark 13:13"> Blessed are ye when they shall revile you, and persecute you, and speak all that is evil against you, untruly, for my sake:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 6:23; James 1:2; Colossians 3:24"> Be glad and rejoice, for your reward is very great in heaven. For so they persecuted the prophets that were before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Luke 14:34–35; Mark 9:49–50"> You are the salt of the earth. But if the salt lose its savour, wherewith shall it be salted? It is good for nothing any more but to be cast out, and to be trodden on by men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Philippians 2:15; John 8:12; Ephesians 5:8–14"> You are the light of the world. A city seated on a mountain cannot be hid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 11:33; Mark 4:21; Luke 8:16"> Neither do men light a candle and put it under a bushel, but upon a candlestick, that it may shine to all that are in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; Titus 2:7; John 15:8"> So let your light shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father who is in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 10:4; Luke 16:17; Galatians 4:4–5"> Do not think that I am come to destroy the law, or the prophets. I am not come to destroy, but to fulfill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 16:17; Isaiah 40:8; 1 Peter 1:25"> For amen I say unto you, till heaven and earth pass, one jot, or one tittle shall not pass of the law, till all be fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; James 2:10–11; Matthew 28:20"> He therefore that shall break one of these least commandments, and shall so teach men, shall be called the least in the kingdom of heaven. But he that shall do and teach, he shall be called great in the kingdom of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 10:2–3; Philippians 3:9; Romans 9:30–32"> For I tell you, that unless your justice abound more than that of the scribes and Pharisees, you shall not enter into the kingdom of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:17; Exodus 20:13; Numbers 35:30–34"> You have heard that it was said to them of old: Thou shalt not kill. And whosoever shall kill shall be in danger of the judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 John 3:14–15; 1 John 4:20–21; 1 John 2:9"> But I say to you, that whosoever is angry with his brother, shall be in danger of the judgment. And whosoever shall say to his brother, Raca, shall be in danger of the council. And whosoever shall say, Thou Fool, shall be in danger of hell fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 11:25; Leviticus 6:2–6; Matthew 5:24"> If therefore thou offer thy gift at the altar, and there thou remember that thy brother hath any thing against thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="James 5:16; Romans 12:17–18; Matthew 18:15–17"> Leave there thy offering before the altar, and go first to be reconciled to thy brother: and then coming thou shalt offer thy gift.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 12:58–59; Proverbs 25:8; Hebrews 3:13"> Be at agreement with thy adversary betimes, whilst thou art in the way with him: lest perhaps the adversary deliver thee to the judge, and the judge deliver thee to the officer, and thou be cast into prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 18:34; Luke 12:59; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> Amen I say to thee, thou shalt not go out from thence till thou repay the last farthing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:18; Proverbs 6:32; Exodus 20:14"> You have heard that it was said to them of old: Thou shalt not commit adultery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 John 2:16; James 1:14–15; Proverbs 6:25"> But I say to you, that whosoever shall look on a woman to lust after her, hath already committed adultery with her in his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Romans 8:13; Mark 9:43–48; Colossians 3:5"> And if thy right eye scandalize thee, pluck it out and cast it from thee. For it is expedient for thee that one of thy members should perish, rather than that thy whole body be cast into hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Mark 9:43; Matthew 5:29; 1 Peter 2:8"> And if thy right hand scandalize thee, cut it off, and cast it from thee: for it is expedient for thee that one of thy members should perish, rather than that thy whole body be cast into hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:1; Deuteronomy 24:1–4; Matthew 19:7"> And it hath been said, whosoever shall put away his wife, let him give her a bill of divorce.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 7:3; 1 Corinthians 7:10–11; Luke 16:18"> But I say to you, that whosoever shall put away his wife, excepting for the cause of fornication, maketh her to commit adultery: and he that shall marry her that is put away, committeth adultery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:21; Leviticus 19:12; Ecclesiastes 5:4–6"> Again you have heard that it was said to them of old, Thou shalt not forswear thyself: but thou shalt perform thy oaths to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="James 5:12; Isaiah 66:1; Deuteronomy 23:21–23"> But I say to you not to swear at all, neither by heaven, for it is the throne of God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 48:2; Malachi 1:14; 2 Chronicles 6:6"> Nor by the earth, for it is his footstool: nor by Jerusalem, for it is the city of the great king:</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 12:25; Matthew 6:27; Matthew 23:16–21"> Neither shalt thou swear by thy head, because thou canst not make one hair white or black.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="James 5:12; Colossians 4:6; Colossians 3:9"> But let your speech be yea, yea: no, no: and that which is over and above these, is of evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Leviticus 24:19–20; Deuteronomy 19:21; Exodus 21:22–27"> You have heard that it hath been said, An eye for an eye, and a tooth for a tooth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:15; 1 Peter 3:9; Romans 12:17–19"> But I say to you not to resist evil: but if one strike thee on thy right cheek, turn to him also the other:</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 6:29; 1 Corinthians 6:7"> And if a man will contend with thee in judgment, and take away thy coat, let go thy cloak also unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 23:26; Matthew 27:32; Mark 15:21"> And whosoever will force thee one mile, go with him other two,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Hebrews 13:16; Luke 6:30–36"> Give to him that asketh of thee and from him that would borrow of thee turn not away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Leviticus 19:18; Psalms 139:21–22; Galatians 5:13–14"> You have heard that it hath been said, Thou shalt love thy neighbor, and hate thy enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Romans 12:14; 1 Peter 3:9; Luke 6:27–28"> But I say to you, Love your enemies: do good to them that hate you: and pray for them that persecute and calumniate you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Psalms 145:9; Luke 6:35; Acts 14:17"> That you may be the children of your Father who is in heaven, who maketh his sun to rise upon the good, and bad, and raineth upon the just and the unjust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 6:32–35; 1 Peter 2:20–23; Matthew 9:10–11"> For if you love them that love you, what reward shall you have? do not even the publicans this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 5:20; Luke 6:32; 1 Peter 2:20"> And if you salute your brethren only, what do you more? do not also the heathens this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Peter 1:15–16; James 1:4; Leviticus 19:2"> Be you therefore perfect, as also your heavenly Father is perfect.</span>
