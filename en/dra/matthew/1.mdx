<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 22:18; Romans 1:3; Galatians 3:16"> The book of the generation of Jesus Christ, the son of David, the son of Abraham:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 7:8; Hebrews 11:17–18; Genesis 46:8–27"> Abraham begot Isaac. And Isaac begot Jacob. And Jacob begot Judas and his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Genesis 38:29–30; 1 Chronicles 4:1"> And Judas begot Phares and Zara of Thamar. And Phares begot Esron. And Esron begot Aram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:10–12; Numbers 7:12; Numbers 2:3"> And Aram begot Aminadab. And Aminadab begot Naasson. And Naasson begot Salmon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:31; Ruth 1:16–17; James 2:25"> And Salmon begot Booz of Rahab. And Booz begot Obed of Ruth. And Obed begot Jesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 15:5; 1 Samuel 17:12; 2 Samuel 12:24–25"> And Jesse begot David the king. And David the king begot Solomon, of her that had been the wife of Urias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:1–15; 1 Kings 14:31; 1 Kings 11:43–24"> And Solomon begot Roboam. And Roboam begot Abia. And Abia begot Asa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:11; 2 Chronicles 26:1–23; 2 Kings 8:16"> And Asa begot Josaphat. And Josaphat begot Joram. And Joram begot Ozias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 7:1–13; 2 Chronicles 26:21; 2 Chronicles 27:1–32"> And Ozias begot Joatham. And Joatham begot Achaz. And Achaz begot Ezechias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:33–27; 1 Kings 13:2; 1 Chronicles 3:13–15"> And Ezechias begot Manasses. And Manesses begot Amon. And Amon begot Josias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:20; Jeremiah 52:28–30; Daniel 1:2"> And Josias begot Jechonias and his brethren in the transmigration of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:17; Ezra 3:2; Luke 3:27"> And after the transmigration of Babylon, Jechonias begot Salathiel. And Salathiel begot Zorobabel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And Zorobabel begot Abiud. And Abiud begot Eliacim. And Eliacim begot Azor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And Azor begot Sadoc. And Sadoc begot Achim. And Achim begot Eliud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Eliud begot Eleazar. And Eleazar begot Mathan. And Mathan begot Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:17; John 4:25; Luke 3:23"> And Jacob begot Joseph the husband of Mary, of whom was born Jesus, who is called Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 1:11–12; 2 Kings 24:14; Matthew 11:2"> So all the generations, from Abraham to David, are fourteen generations. And from David to the transmigration of Babylon, are fourteen generations: and from the transmigration of Babylon to Christ are fourteen generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 4:4–5; Luke 1:27–38; Job 14:4"> Now the generation of Christ was in this wise. When as his mother Mary was espoused to Joseph, before they came together, she was found with child, of the Holy Ghost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:21–24; Deuteronomy 24:1–4; John 8:4–5"> Whereupon Joseph her husband, being a just man, and not willing publicly to expose her, was minded to put her away privately.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Luke 1:19; Matthew 1:18"> But while he thought on these things, behold the angel of the Lord appeared to him in his sleep, saying: Joseph, son of David, fear not to take unto thee Mary thy wife, for that which is conceived in her, is of the Holy Ghost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 13:23; Acts 4:12; John 1:29"> And she shall bring forth a son: and thou shalt call his name JESUS. For he shall save his people from their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 24:44; Matthew 2:23; 1 Kings 8:15"> Now all this was done that it might be fulfilled which the Lord spoke by the prophet, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Isaiah 9:6–7; Psalms 46:11"> Behold a virgin shall be with child, and bring forth a son, and they shall call his name Emmanuel, which being interpreted is, God with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 15:14; James 2:21–26; John 2:5–8"> And Joseph rising up from sleep, did as the angel of the Lord had commanded him, and took unto him his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 2:21; Exodus 13:2; Matthew 1:21"> And he knew her not till she brought forth her firstborn son: and he called his name JESUS.</span>
