<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 9:1–6; Mark 3:13–15; Mark 6:7–13"> And having called his twelve disciples together, he gave them power over unclean spirits, to cast them out, and to heal all manner of diseases, and all manner of infirmities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 4:21; Acts 1:13; Luke 5:10"> And the names of the twelve apostles are these: The first, Simon who is called Peter, and Andrew his brother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 3:18; Acts 1:13; Mark 2:14"> James the son of Zebedee, and John his brother, Philip and Bartholomew, Thomas and Matthew the publican, and James the son of Alpheus, and Thaddeus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 26:14; Matthew 26:47; John 13:2"> Simon the Cananean, and Judas Iscariot, who also betrayed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 4:9; Acts 22:21–23; 2 Kings 17:24–41"> These twelve Jesus sent: commanding them, saying: Go ye not into the way of the Gentiles, and into the city of the Samaritans enter ye not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:6; Ezekiel 34:6; Isaiah 53:6"> But go ye rather to the lost sheep of the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 28:31; Matthew 3:2; Luke 9:60"> And going, preach, saying: The kingdom of heaven is at hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 3:6; Luke 10:9; Acts 4:30"> Heal the sick, raise the dead, cleanse the lepers, cast out devils: freely have you received, freely give.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 22:35; Luke 9:3–5; Luke 10:4–12"> Do not possess gold, nor silver, nor money in your purses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 10:7–12; 1 Timothy 5:17–18; 1 Corinthians 9:4–14"> Nor scrip for your journey, nor two coats, nor shoes, nor a staff; for the workman is worthy of his meat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 10:7–8; Job 31:32; Judges 19:16–21"> And into whatsoever city or town you shall enter, inquire who in it is worthy, and there abide till you go thence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:6; Luke 10:5–6; Acts 10:36"> And when you come into the house, salute it, saying: Peace be to this house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 10:6; Psalms 35:13; 2 Corinthians 2:16"> And if that house be worthy, your peace shall come upon it; but if it be not worthy, your peace shall return to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 10:10–11; Acts 18:6; Nehemiah 5:13"> And whosoever shall not receive you, nor hear your words: going forth out of that house or city shake off the dust from your feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Peter 2:6; 1 John 4:17; John 15:22–24"> Amen I say to you, it shall be more tolerable for the land of Sodom and Gomorrha in the day of judgment, than for that city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 10:3; Philippians 2:15; 1 Corinthians 14:20"> Behold I send you as sheep in the midst of wolves. Be ye therefore wise as serpents and simple as doves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 13:9; Matthew 23:34; Acts 26:11"> But beware of men. For they will deliver you up in councils, and they will scourge you in their synagogues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 5:25–27; Matthew 8:4; Psalms 2:1–6"> And you shall be brought before governors, and before kings for my sake, for a testimony to them and to the Gentiles:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 4:12; Mark 13:11–13; 2 Timothy 4:17"> But when they shall deliver you up, take no thought how or what to speak: for it shall be given you in that hour what to speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Acts 6:10; Luke 12:12"> For it is not you that speak, but the Spirit of your Father that speaketh in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 21:16–17; Micah 7:5–6; Mark 13:12–13"> The brother also shall deliver up the brother to death, and the father the son: and the children shall rise up against their parents, and shall put them to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 24:13; James 1:12; Revelation 2:10"> And you shall be hated by all men for my name’s sake: but he that shall persevere unto the end, he shall be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 16:28; Acts 8:1; Mark 13:26"> And when they shall persecute you in this city, flee into another. Amen I say to you, you shall not finish all the cities of Israel, till the Son of man come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 15:20; Luke 6:40; John 13:16"> The disciple is not above the master, nor the servant above his lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 11:15; Matthew 12:24; Matthew 9:34"> It is enough for the disciple that he be as his master, and the servant as his lord. If they have called the goodman of the house Beelzebub, how much more them of his household?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 8:17; Mark 4:22; Jeremiah 1:8"> Therefore fear them not. For nothing is covered that shall not be revealed: nor hid, that shall not be known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 5:20; John 16:13; John 16:1"> That which I tell you in the dark, speak ye in the light: and that which you hear in the ear, preach ye upon the housetops.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 12:4–5; Isaiah 8:12–13; Revelation 2:10"> And fear ye not them that kill the body, and are not able to kill the soul: but rather fear him that can destroy both soul and body in hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 12:6–7; Psalms 104:27–30"> Are not two sparrows sold for a farthing? and not one of them shall fall on the ground without your Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 12:7; 1 Samuel 14:45; Acts 27:34"> But the very hairs of your head are all numbered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 12:24; Psalms 8:5; Matthew 6:26"> Fear not therefore: better are you than many sparrows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Revelation 3:5; Romans 10:9–10; Luke 12:8–9"> Every one therefore that shall confess me before men, I will also confess him before my Father who is in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:12; Luke 9:26; Luke 12:9"> But he that shall deny me before men, I will also deny him before my Father who is in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 12:49–53; Acts 14:4; Acts 13:45–50"> Do not think that I came to send peace upon earth: I came not to send peace, but the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Micah 7:5–6; Matthew 10:21; Luke 21:16"> For I came to set a man at variance against his father, and the daughter against her mother, and the daughter in law against her mother in law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Micah 7:6; Jeremiah 12:6; Psalms 41:9"> And as a man’s enemies shall be they of his own household.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 14:26; Matthew 22:37; Philippians 3:7–9"> He that loveth father or mother more than me, is not worthy of me; and he that loveth son or daughter more than me, is not worthy of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 14:27; Mark 8:34; Matthew 16:24"> And he that taketh not up his cross, and followeth me, is not worthy of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 12:25; Mark 8:35–36; Luke 17:33"> He that findeth his life, shall lose it: and he that shall lose his life for me, shall find it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 13:20; Luke 9:48; Luke 10:16"> He that receiveth you, receiveth me: and he that receiveth me, receiveth him that sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="3 John 1:5–8; Hebrews 6:10; 2 Kings 4:16–17"> He that receiveth a prophet in the name of a prophet, shall receive the reward of a prophet: and he that receiveth a just man in the name of a just man, shall receive the reward of a just man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Hebrews 6:10; Matthew 25:40; Mark 9:41–42"> And whosoever shall give to drink to one of these little ones a cup of cold water only in the name of a disciple, amen I say to you, he shall not lose his reward.</span>
