<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 8:4–10; Matthew 13:1–15; Mark 4:1–12"> The same day Jesus going out of the house, sat by the sea side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 5:3; Matthew 4:25; Mark 4:1"> And great multitudes were gathered unto him, so that he went up into a boat and sat: and all the multitude stood on the shore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 4:2–9; Mark 4:13; Psalms 78:2"> And he spoke to them many things in parables, saying: Behold the sower went forth to sow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 13:18–19"> And whilst he soweth some fell by the way side, and the birds of the air came and ate them up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19; Ezekiel 36:26; Zechariah 7:12"> And other some fell upon stony ground, where they had not much earth: and they sprung up immediately, because they had no deepness of earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 13:21; James 1:11–12; Colossians 1:23"> And when the sun was up they were scorched: and because they had not root, they withered away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 4:18–19; Jeremiah 4:3–4; Genesis 3:18"> And others fell among thorns: and the thorns grew up and choked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 13:23; Luke 8:15; Genesis 26:12"> And others fell upon good ground: and they brought forth fruit, some an hundredfold, some sixtyfold, and some thirtyfold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 2:11; Matthew 11:15; Revelation 2:7"> He that hath ears to hear, let him hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 4:33–34; Mark 4:10"> And his disciples came and said to him: Why speakest thou to them in parables?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:9–10; 1 Corinthians 2:14; Matthew 19:11"> Who answered and said to them: Because to you it is given to know the mysteries of the kingdom of heaven: but to them it is not given.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 25:29; Mark 4:24–25; Luke 8:18"> For he that hath, to him shall be given, and he shall abound: but he that hath not, from him shall be taken away that also which he hath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:2; Jeremiah 5:21; Isaiah 42:18–20"> Therefore do I speak to them in parables: because seeing they see not, and hearing they hear not, neither do they understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; Ezekiel 12:2; 2 Corinthians 3:14"> And the prophecy of Isaias is fulfilled in them, who saith: By hearing you shall hear, and shall not understand: and seeing you shall see, and shall not perceive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10; Mark 4:12; Zechariah 7:11"> For the heart of this people is grown gross, and with their ears they have been dull of hearing, and their eyes they have shut: lest at any time they should see with their eyes, and hear with their ears, and understand with their heart, and be converted, and I should heal them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 10:23–24; Ephesians 1:17–18; Matthew 16:17"> But blessed are your eyes, because they see, and your ears, because they hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 8:56; Hebrews 11:13; 1 Peter 1:10–12"> For, amen, I say to you, many prophets and just men have desired to see the things that you see, and have not seen them, and to hear the things that you hear and have not heard them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 13:11–12; Luke 8:11–15; Mark 4:13–20"> Hear you therefore the parable of the sower.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 8:11–15; Matthew 13:38; Hebrews 2:1"> When any one heareth the word of the kingdom, and understandeth it not, there cometh the wicked one, and catcheth away that which was sown in his heart: this is he that received the seed by the way side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31–32; Psalms 106:12–13; John 5:35"> And he that received the seed upon stony ground, is he that heareth the word, and immediately receiveth it with joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 11:6; 2 Peter 1:8–9; Ephesians 3:17"> Yet hath he not root in himself, but is only for a time: and when there ariseth tribulation and persecution because of the word, he is presently scandalized.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:17; Luke 8:14; 1 Timothy 6:9–10"> And he that received the seed among thorns, is he that heareth the word, and the care of this world and the deceitfulness of riches choketh up the word, and he becometh fruitless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 1:11; Matthew 12:33; John 15:16"> But he that received the seed upon good ground, is he that heareth the word, and understandeth, and beareth fruit, and yieldeth the one an hundredfold, and another sixty, and another thirty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 25:1; Matthew 13:47; Matthew 20:1"> Another parable he proposed to them, saying: The kingdom of heaven is likened to a man that sowed good seeds in his field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; 2 Peter 2:1; Acts 20:30–31"> But while men were asleep, his enemy came and oversowed cockle among the wheat and went his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 4:26–29"> And when the blade was sprung up, and had brought forth fruit, then appeared also the cockle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 16:17; 2 Corinthians 6:1; 2 Corinthians 5:18–20"> And the servants of the goodman of the house coming said to him: Sir, didst thou not sow good seed in thy field? whence then hath it cockle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:3–7; 1 Thessalonians 5:14; Luke 9:49–54"> And he said to them: An enemy hath done this. And the servants said to him: Wilt thou that we go and gather it up?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And he said: No, lest perhaps gathering up the cockle, you root up the wheat also together with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 3:12; 1 Timothy 5:24; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> Suffer both to grow until the harvest, and in the time of the harvest I will say to the reapers: Gather up first the cockle, and bind it into bundles to burn, but the wheat gather ye into my barn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 13:18–19; Matthew 17:20; Mark 4:30–32"> Another parable he proposed unto them, saying: The kingdom of heaven is like to a grain of mustard seed, which a man took and sowed in his field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:6; Daniel 4:12; Ezekiel 17:23–24"> Which is the least indeed of all seeds; but when it is grown up, it is greater than all herbs, and becometh a tree, so that the birds of the air come, and dwell in the branches thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Galatians 5:9; 1 Corinthians 5:6–7; Luke 13:21"> Another parable he spoke to them: The kingdom of heaven is like to leaven, which a woman took and hid in three measures of meal, until the whole was leavened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 4:33–34; John 16:25; Matthew 13:13"> All these things Jesus spoke in parables to the multitudes: and without parables he did not speak to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 78:2; Ephesians 3:9; 1 Peter 1:20–21"> That it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the prophet, saying: I will open my mouth in parables, I will utter things hidden from the foundation of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 16:17–20; Matthew 13:11; Matthew 9:28"> Then having sent away the multitudes, he came into the house, and his disciples came to him, saying: Expound to us the parable of the cockle of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:5–7; Matthew 13:41; Luke 10:16"> Who made answer and said to them: He that soweth the good seed, is the Son of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 8:44; Philippians 3:18–19; Acts 13:10"> And the field, is the world. And the good seed are the children of the kingdom. And the cockle, are the children of the wicked one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 13:49; Revelation 14:15–19; Matthew 25:31"> And the enemy that sowed them, is the devil. But the harvest is the end of the world. And the reapers are the angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 13:39"> Even as cockle therefore is gathered up, and burnt with fire: so shall it be at the end of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 24:31; Matthew 18:7; 2 Peter 2:1–2"> The Son of man shall send his angels, and they shall gather out of his kingdom all scandals, and them that work iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 13:50; Luke 16:23–24; Matthew 8:12"> And shall cast them into the furnace of fire: there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Daniel 12:3; Matthew 11:15; Luke 12:32"> Then shall the just shine as the sun, in the kingdom of their Father. He that hath ears to hear, let him hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Proverbs 2:2–5; Colossians 2:3; Philippians 3:7–9"> The kingdom of heaven is like unto a treasure hidden in a field. Which a man having found, hid it, and for joy thereof goeth, and selleth all that he hath, and buyeth that field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Proverbs 3:13–18; Matthew 16:26; Matthew 13:24"> Again the kingdom of heaven is like to a merchant seeking good pearls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ephesians 3:8; Luke 18:28–30; Proverbs 2:4"> Who when he had found one pearl of great price, went his way, and sold all that he had, and bought it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 15:6; 2 Timothy 3:2–5; Matthew 22:9–10"> Again the kingdom of heaven is like to a net cast into the sea, and gathering together of all kind of fishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Matthew 13:30; Matthew 13:40–43; Matthew 3:12"> Which, when it was filled, they drew out, and sitting by the shore, they chose out the good into vessels, but the bad they cast forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Revelation 20:12–15; Matthew 24:31; Matthew 13:39"> So shall it be at the end of the world. The angels shall go out, and shall separate the wicked from among the just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 13:42; Matthew 8:12; Revelation 14:10–11"> And shall cast them into the furnace of fire: there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Matthew 13:19; Matthew 13:11; Mark 4:34"> Have ye understood all these things? They say to him: Yes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="1 John 2:7–8; 2 Timothy 3:16–17; Titus 1:9"> He said unto them: Therefore every scribe instructed in the kingdom of heaven, is like to a man that is a householder, who bringeth forth out of his treasure new things and old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; Mark 4:33–35"> And it came to pass: when Jesus had finished these parables, he passed from thence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; John 7:15–16; Mark 6:1–6"> And coming into his own country, he taught them in their synagogues, so that they wondered and said: How came this man by this wisdom and miracles?</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="John 6:42; Mark 6:3; Luke 3:23"> Is not this the carpenter’s son? Is not his mother called Mary, and his brethren James, and Joseph, and Simon, and Jude:</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> And his sisters, are they not all with us? Whence therefore hath he all these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="John 4:44; Matthew 11:6; Luke 4:24"> And they were scandalized in his regard. But Jesus said to them: A prophet is not without honour, save in his own country, and in his own house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Mark 6:5–6; Romans 11:20; Hebrews 3:12–19"> And he wrought not many miracles there, because of their unbelief.</span>
