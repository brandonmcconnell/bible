<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 15:1; John 18:28; Micah 2:1"> And when morning was come, all the chief priests and ancients of the people took counsel against Jesus, that they might put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 20:19; Acts 3:13; 1 Timothy 6:13"> And they brought him bound, and delivered him to Pontius Pilate the governor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10; Luke 22:47–48; Job 20:15–29"> Then Judas, who betrayed him, seeing that he was condemned, repenting himself, brought back the thirty pieces of silver to the chief priests and ancients,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:30; Titus 1:16; Acts 18:15–17"> Saying: I have sinned in betraying innocent blood. But they said: What is that to us? look thou to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 1:18–19; 2 Samuel 17:23; 1 Kings 16:18"> And casting down the pieces of silver in the temple, he departed: and went and hanged himself with an halter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 6:7–9; Deuteronomy 23:18; Isaiah 61:8"> But the chief priests having taken the pieces of silver, said: It is not lawful to put them into the corbona, because it is the price of blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And after they had consulted together, they bought with them the potter’s field, to be a burying place for strangers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:19; Matthew 28:15; Joshua 4:9"> For this cause the field was called Haceldama, that is, The field of blood, even to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 11:12–13; Matthew 1:22; Leviticus 27:2–7"> Then was fulfilled that which was spoken by Jeremias the prophet, saying: And they took the thirty pieces of silver, the price of him that was prized, whom they prized of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 11:13"> And they gave them unto the potter’s field, as the Lord appointed to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 23:2–3; Mark 15:2–5; Matthew 26:25"> And Jesus stood before the governor, and the governor asked him, saying: Art thou the king of the Jews? Jesus saith to him: Thou sayest it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 26:63; Isaiah 53:7; Matthew 27:14"> And when he was accused by the chief priests and ancients, he answered nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 26:62; John 18:35; Acts 22:24"> Then Pilate saith to him: Dost not thou hear how great testimonies they allege against thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 8:18; Psalms 71:7; 1 Corinthians 4:9"> And he answered him to never a word; so that the governor wondered exceedingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 18:38–40; Luke 23:16; John 19:16"> Now upon the solemn day the governor was accustomed to release to the people one prisoner, whom they would.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 15:7; Acts 3:14; Luke 23:25"> And he had then a notorious prisoner, that was called Barabbas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 19:15; 1 Kings 18:21; Mark 15:9–12"> They therefore being gathered together, Pilate said: Whom will you that I release to you, Barabbas, or Jesus that is called Christ?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 27:4; Acts 5:17; Mark 15:10"> For he knew that for envy they had delivered him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Proverbs 29:1; Matthew 27:24"> And as he was sitting in the place of judgment, his wife sent to him, saying: Have thou nothing to do with that just man; for I have suffered many things this day in a dream because of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 3:14–15; John 19:15–16; John 18:40"> But the chief priests and ancients persuaded the people, that they should ask Barabbas, and make Jesus away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And the governor answering, said to them: Whether will you of the two to be released unto you? But they said, Barabbas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2–3; Isaiah 49:7; Acts 13:38"> Pilate saith to them: What shall I do then with Jesus that is called Christ? They say all: Let him be crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 37:18–19; Acts 7:57; Acts 23:12–15"> The governor said to them: Why, what evil hath he done? But they cried out the more, saying: Let him be crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 27:4; Psalms 26:6; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> And Pilate seeing that he prevailed nothing, but that rather a tumult was made; taking water washed his hands before the people, saying: I am innocent of the blood of this just man; look you to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 2:19; Acts 5:28; Acts 7:52"> And the whole people answering, said: His blood be upon us and our children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 53:5; John 19:1; Mark 15:15"> Then he released to them Barabbas, and having scourged Jesus, delivered him unto them to be crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 18:28; John 18:33; Acts 10:1"> Then the soldiers of the governor taking Jesus into the hall, gathered together unto him the whole band;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 15:17; John 19:2–5; Luke 23:11"> And stripping him, they put a scarlet cloak about him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 53:3; Matthew 20:19; Psalms 69:19–20"> And platting a crown of thorns, they put it upon his head, and a reed in his right hand. And bowing the knee before him, they mocked him, saying: Hail, king of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 50:6; Matthew 26:67; Isaiah 53:7"> And spitting upon him, they took the reed, and struck his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; 1 Kings 21:13; 1 Kings 21:10"> And after they had mocked him, they took off the cloak from him, and put on him his own garments, and led him away to crucify him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 15:21; Matthew 16:24; Luke 23:26"> And going out, they found a man of Cyrene, named Simon: him they forced to take up his cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 19:17–19; Mark 15:22–32; Mark 15:34"> And they came to the place that is called Golgotha, which is the place of Calvary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Mark 15:23; Matthew 27:48"> And they gave him wine to drink mingled with gall. And when he had tasted, he would not drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 22:18; Mark 15:24–32; Luke 23:34"> And after they had crucified him, they divided his garments, casting lots; that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the prophet, saying: They divided my garments among them; and upon my vesture they cast lots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 27:54; Mark 15:44; Mark 15:39"> And they sat and watched him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 23:38; John 19:19–22; Mark 15:26"> And they put over his head his cause written: THIS IS JESUS THE KING OF THE JEWS.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 19:18; Luke 23:39–43; Isaiah 53:12"> Then were crucified with him two thieves: one on the right hand, and one on the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 109:25; Lamentations 1:12; Psalms 69:7–12"> And they that passed by, blasphemed him, wagging their heads,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 26:61; Matthew 4:6; Matthew 4:3"> And saying: Vah, thou that destroyest the temple of God, and in three days dost rebuild it: save thy own self: if thou be the Son of God, come down from the cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Psalms 35:26; Isaiah 28:22; Psalms 22:12–13"> In like manner also the chief priests, with the scribes and ancients, mocking, said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 1:49; Matthew 27:37; Acts 4:14"> He saved others; himself he cannot save. If he be the king of Israel, let him now come down from the cross, and we will believe him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalms 22:8; John 10:36; Isaiah 37:10"> He trusted in God; let him now deliver him if he will have him; for he said: I am the Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 27:38; Mark 15:32; Luke 23:39–43"> And the selfsame thing the thieves also, that were crucified with him, reproached him with.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Amos 8:9; Mark 15:33–34; Luke 23:44–45"> Now from the sixth hour there was darkness over the whole earth, until the ninth hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 22:1; Hebrews 5:7; Isaiah 53:10"> And about the ninth hour Jesus cried with a loud voice, saying: Eli, Eli, lamma sabacthani? that is, My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Mark 15:35–36; Matthew 11:14"> And some that stood there and heard, said: This man calleth Elias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Matthew 27:34; Luke 23:36"> And immediately one of them running took a sponge, and filled it with vinegar; and put it on a reed, and gave him to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> And the others said: Let be, let us see whether Elias will come to deliver him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 19:30; Isaiah 53:9–12; Psalms 22:14–15"> And Jesus again crying with a loud voice, yielded up the ghost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; Mark 15:38; Hebrews 6:19"> And behold the veil of the temple was rent in two from the top even to the bottom, and the earth quaked, and the rocks were rent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Isaiah 26:19; 1 Thessalonians 4:14; 1 Corinthians 15:20"> And the graves were opened: and many bodies of the saints that had slept arose,</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Revelation 11:2; Isaiah 48:2; Revelation 21:2"> And coming out of the tombs after his resurrection, came into the holy city, and appeared to many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 27:36; Mark 15:39; Matthew 27:43"> Now the centurion and they that were with him watching Jesus, having seen the earthquake, and the things that were done, were sore afraid, saying: Indeed this was the Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Luke 8:2–3; Luke 23:48–49; John 19:25–27"> And there were there many women afar off, who had followed Jesus from Galilee, ministering unto him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="John 19:25; Mark 15:47–1; Matthew 28:1"> Among whom was Mary Magdalen, and Mary the mother of James and Joseph, and the mother of the sons of Zebedee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="John 19:38–42; Mark 15:42–47; Luke 23:50–56"> And when it was evening, there came a certain rich man of Arimathea, named Joseph, who also himself was a disciple of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Luke 23:52–53; Mark 15:44–46"> He went to Pilate, and asked the body of Jesus. Then Pilate commanded that the body should be delivered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> And Joseph taking the body, wrapped it up in a clean linen cloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Isaiah 53:9; Matthew 28:2; Matthew 27:66"> And laid it in his own new monument, which he had hewed out in a rock. And he rolled a great stone to the door of the monument, and went his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Matthew 28:1; Matthew 27:56"> And there was there Mary Magdalen, and the other Mary sitting over against the sepulchre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Mark 15:42; John 19:14; John 19:42"> And the next day, which followed the day of preparation, the chief priests and the Pharisees came together to Pilate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Luke 9:22; Matthew 16:21; John 2:19"> Saying: Sir, we have remembered, that that seducer said, while he was yet alive: After three days I will rise again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Matthew 28:13; Matthew 12:45"> Command therefore the sepulchre to be guarded until the third day: lest perhaps his disciples come and steal him away, and say to the people: He is risen from the dead; and the last error shall be worse than the first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Matthew 27:66; Matthew 28:11–15; Proverbs 21:30"> Pilate saith to them: You have a guard; go, guard it as you know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Daniel 6:17; Matthew 27:60; 2 Timothy 2:19"> And they departing, made the sepulchre sure, sealing the stone, and setting guards.</span>
