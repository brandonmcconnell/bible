<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 3:22; Matthew 23:2; Luke 5:21"> Then came to him from Jerusalem scribes and Pharisees, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 11:38; Mark 7:2; Colossians 2:20–23"> Why do thy disciples transgress the tradition of the ancients? For they wash not their hands when they eat bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 2:23; Titus 1:14; Colossians 2:8"> But he answering, said to them: Why do you also transgress the commandment of God for your tradition? For God said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Leviticus 20:9; Exodus 21:17"> Honour thy father and mother: And: He that shall curse father or mother, let him die the death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 7:10–13; Acts 5:29; Matthew 23:16–18"> But you say: Whosoever shall say to father or mother, The gift whatsoever proceedeth from me, shall profit thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:8; Jeremiah 8:8; Mark 7:13"> And he shall not honour his father or his mother: and you have made void the commandment of God for your tradition.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 7:6; Matthew 23:23–29; Acts 28:25–27"> Hypocrites, well hath Isaias prophesied of you, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Ezekiel 33:31; Acts 8:21"> This people honoureth me with their lips: but their heart is far from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Mark 7:7; 1 Timothy 1:4"> And in vain do they worship me, teaching doctrines and commandments of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 7:14–15; Luke 20:45–47; Luke 24:45"> And having called together the multitudes unto him, he said to them: Hear ye and understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 7:15; Matthew 15:18–20; James 3:5–8"> Not that which goeth into the mouth defileth a man: but what cometh out of the mouth, this defileth a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 22:13–14; 1 Corinthians 10:32–33; Galatians 2:5"> Then came his disciples, and said to him: Dost thou know that the Pharisees, when they heard this word, were scandalized?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 15:2; Isaiah 60:21; John 15:6"> But he answering them, said: Every plant which my heavenly Father hath not planted, shall be rooted up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 6:39; Isaiah 56:10; Matthew 23:16–24"> Let them alone: they are blind, and leaders of the blind. And if the blind lead the blind, both will fall into the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 13:36; Mark 4:34; John 16:29"> And Peter answering, said to him: Expound to us this parable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 16:9; Mark 7:18; Mark 9:32"> But he said: Are you also yet without understanding?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 6:45; 1 Corinthians 6:13; Matthew 7:19–20"> Do you not understand, that whatsoever entereth into the mouth, goeth into the belly, and is cast out into the privy?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 15:28; Matthew 12:34; James 3:6–10"> But the things which proceed out of the mouth, come forth from the heart, and those things defile a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 7:21–23; Galatians 5:19–21; Jeremiah 17:9"> For from the heart come forth evil thoughts, murders, adulteries, fornications, thefts, false testimonies, blasphemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 7:3–4; Ephesians 5:3–6; 1 Corinthians 6:18–20"> These are the things that defile a man. But to eat with unwashed hands doth not defile a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 7:24–30; Matthew 11:21–23; Judges 1:31"> And Jesus went from thence, and retired into the coasts of Tyre and Sidon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 9:27; Mark 7:25–26; Matthew 17:15"> And behold a woman of Canaan who came out of those coasts, crying out, said to him: Have mercy on me, O Lord, thou son of David: my daughter is grieviously troubled by the devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 14:15; Mark 10:47–48; Lamentations 3:8"> Who answered her not a word. And his disciples came and besought him, saying: Send her away, for she crieth after us:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:5–6; Romans 15:8; Jeremiah 50:6–7"> And he answering, said: I was not sent but to the sheep that are lost of the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 32:26; Matthew 20:31; Matthew 14:33"> But she came and adored him, saying: Lord, help me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 7:27–28; Matthew 7:6; Philippians 3:2"> Who answering, said: It is not good to take the bread of the children, and to cast it to the dogs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ephesians 3:8; Luke 16:21; Job 40:4–5"> But she said: Yea, Lord; for the whelps also eat of the crumbs that fall from the table of their masters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 9:23–24; Matthew 8:13; Psalms 145:19"> Then Jesus answering, said to her: O woman, great is thy faith: be it done to thee as thou wilt: and her daughter was cured from that hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 4:18; John 6:1; Matthew 5:1"> And when Jesus had passed away from thence, he came nigh the sea of Galilee. And going up into a mountain, he sat there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 6:17–19; Acts 5:15–16; Matthew 14:35–36"> And there came to him great multitudes, having with them the dumb, the blind, the lame, the maimed, and many others: and they cast them down at his feet, and he healed them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Luke 18:43; Luke 17:15–18"> So that the multitudes marvelled seeing the dumb speak, the lame walk, and the blind see: and they glorified the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 27:33; Luke 12:29–30; Matthew 9:36"> And Jesus called together his disciples, and said: I have compassion on the multitudes, because they continue with me now three days, and have not what to eat, and I will not send them away fasting, lest they faint in the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 6:37; Matthew 14:15; Mark 8:4–5"> And the disciples say unto him: Whence then should we have so many loaves in the desert, as to fill so great a multitude?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 16:9–10; Luke 24:41–42; John 21:9–10"> And Jesus said to them: How many loaves have you? But they said: Seven, and a few little fishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 9:14–16; Mark 6:39–40; John 6:10"> And he commanded the multitude to sit down upon the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 22:19; Romans 14:6; John 6:11"> And taking the seven loaves and the fishes, and giving thanks, he brake, and gave to his disciples, and the disciples to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 8:19–21; Matthew 16:9–10; Matthew 15:33"> And they did all eat, and had their fill. And they took up seven baskets full, of what remained of the fragments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And they that did eat, were four thousand men, beside children and women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Mark 8:10; Matthew 14:22"> And having dismissed the multitude, he went up into a boat, and came into the coasts of Magedan.</span>
