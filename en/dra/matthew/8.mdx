<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 8:18; Matthew 4:25–1; Luke 5:15"> And when he was come down from the mountain, great multitudes followed him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 15:25; Luke 17:12–19; Matthew 18:26"> And behold a leper came and adored him, saying: Lord, if thou wilt, thou canst make me clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 5:13; Psalms 33:9; 2 Kings 5:14"> And Jesus stretching forth his hand, touched him, saying: I will, be thou made clean. And forthwith his leprosy was cleansed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 14:2–32; Luke 5:14; Luke 17:14"> And Jesus saith to him: See thou tell no man: but go, shew thyself to the priest, and offer the gift which Moses commanded for a testimony unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 7:1–10; Acts 27:43; Acts 10:1–33"> And when he had entered into Capharnaum, there came to him a centurion, beseeching him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 4:1; Matthew 4:24; Acts 9:33"> And saying, Lord, my servant lieth at home sick of the palsy, and is grieviously tormented.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 9:18–19; Luke 7:6; Mark 5:23–24"> And Jesus saith to him: I will come and heal him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 107:20; Luke 7:6–7; Psalms 33:9"> And the centurion making answer, said: Lord, I am not worthy that thou shouldst enter under my roof: but only say the word, and my servant shall be healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 4:35–36; Titus 2:9; Colossians 3:22"> For I also am a man subject to authority, having under me soldiers; and I say to this, Go, and he goeth, and to another, Come, and he cometh, and to my servant, Do this, and he doeth it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 15:28; Luke 7:9; Mark 6:6"> And Jesus hearing this, marvelled; and said to them that followed him: Amen I say to you, I have not found so great faith in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 13:28–29; Ephesians 3:6; Galatians 3:28–29"> And I say to you that many shall come from the east and the west, and shall sit down with Abraham, and Isaac, and Jacob in the kingdom of heaven:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 25:30; Luke 13:28; Matthew 21:43"> But the children of the kingdom shall be cast out into the exterior darkness: there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 9:29–30; Mark 9:23; John 4:50"> And Jesus said to the centurion: Go, and as thou hast believed, so be it done to thee. And the servant was healed at the same hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 4:38–41; Mark 1:29–34; 1 Corinthians 9:5"> And when Jesus was come into Peter’s house, he saw his wife’s mother lying, and sick of a fever:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 13:21; Luke 4:38–39; Luke 8:54"> And he touched her hand, and the fever left her, and she arose and ministered to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 15:26; Matthew 14:14; Mark 1:32–34"> And when evening was come, they brought to him many that were possessed with devils: and he cast out the spirits with his word: and all that were sick he healed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4; 1 Peter 2:24; Matthew 1:22"> That it might be fulfilled, which was spoken by the prophet Isaias, saying: He took our infirmities, and bore our diseases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 4:42–43; Luke 8:22; Mark 4:35"> And Jesus seeing great multitudes about him, gave orders to pass over the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 22:33–34; John 13:36–38; Luke 14:25–27"> And a certain scribe came and said to him: Master, I will follow thee whithersoever thou shalt go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 84:3; Luke 2:7; Isaiah 53:2–3"> And Jesus saith to him: The foxes have holes, and the birds of the air nests: but the son of man hath not where to lay his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 9:59–62; Leviticus 21:11–12; Numbers 6:6–7"> And another of his disciples said to him: Lord, suffer me first to go and bury my father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 2:13; Luke 15:32; Ephesians 5:14"> But Jesus said to him: Follow me, and let the dead bury their dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 8:22–25; Mark 4:36–41; Matthew 9:1"> And when he entered into the boat, his disciples followed him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 8:23; Psalms 107:23–27; Mark 4:37–38"> And behold a great tempest arose in the sea, so that the boat was covered with waves, but he was asleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jonah 1:6; 2 Chronicles 14:11; Mark 4:38–39"> And they came to him, and awaked him, saying: Lord, save us, we perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10–14; Psalms 65:7; Psalms 107:28–30"> And Jesus saith to them: Why are you fearful, O ye of little faith? Then rising up he commanded the winds, and the sea, and there came a great calm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 7:37; Mark 6:51; Matthew 14:33"> But the men wondered, saying: What manner of man is this, for the winds and the sea obey him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 8:26–39; Mark 5:1–20; Acts 10:38"> And when he was come on the other side of the water, into the country of the Gerasens, there met him two that were possessed with devils, coming out of the sepulchres, exceeding fierce, so that none could pass by that way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 1:24; Luke 4:34; James 2:19"> And behold they cried out, saying: What have we to do with thee, Jesus Son of God? art thou come hither to torment us before the time?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:8; Leviticus 11:7; Mark 5:11"> And there was, not far from them, an herd of many swine feeding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Revelation 20:1–2; Luke 8:30–33; Mark 5:12"> And the devils besought him, saying: If thou cast us out hence, send us into the herd of swine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 5:13; Acts 2:23; Revelation 20:7"> And he said to them: Go. But they going out went into the swine, and behold the whole herd ran violently down a steep place into the sea: and they perished in the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 8:34–36; Mark 5:14–16; Acts 19:15–17"> And they that kept them fled: and coming into the city, told every thing, and concerning them that had been possessed by the devils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 16:39; Luke 8:37–39; Luke 5:8"> And behold the whole city went out to meet Jesus, and when they saw him, they besought him that he would depart from their coasts.</span>
