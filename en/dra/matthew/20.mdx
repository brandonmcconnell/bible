<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 13:47; Matthew 13:24; Song of Solomon 8:11–12"> The kingdom of heaven is like to an householder, who went out early in the morning to hire labourers into his vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:26; Ecclesiastes 12:1; Matthew 22:19"> And having agreed with the labourers for a penny a day, he sent them into his vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:13; Acts 2:15; Matthew 20:6–7"> And going about the third hour, he saw others standing in the market place idle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:11; 1 Peter 4:2–3; Luke 19:7–10"> And he said to them: Go you also into my vineyard, and I will give you what shall be just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:24–26; Acts 10:3; Mark 15:33–34"> And they went their way. And again he went out about the sixth and the ninth hour, and did in like manner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 6:12; John 9:4; Acts 17:21"> But about the eleventh hour he went out and found others standing, and he saith to them: Why stand you here all the day idle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:11–12; Hebrews 6:10; John 9:4"> They say to him: Because no man hath hired us. He saith to them: Go you also into my vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:15; 1 Peter 4:10; Romans 2:6–10"> And when evening was come, the lord of the vineyard saith to his steward: Call the labourers and pay them their hire, beginning from the last even to the first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 20:6–7; Luke 23:40–43; Matthew 20:2"> When therefore they were come, that came about the eleventh hour, they received every man a penny.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> But when the first also came, they thought that they should receive more: and they also received every man a penny.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:16; Luke 19:7; Acts 22:21–22"> And receiving it they murmured against the master of the house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:5–6; 2 Corinthians 11:23–28; Luke 12:55"> Saying: These last have worked but one hour, and thou hast made them equal to us, that have borne the burden of the day and the heats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 26:50; Matthew 22:12; Romans 9:14–15"> But he answering said to one of them: Friend, I do thee no wrong: didst thou not agree with me for a penny?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 17:2; Luke 15:31; Matthew 6:6"> Take what is thine, and go thy way: I will also give to this last even as to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 9:15–24; Deuteronomy 15:9; Jonah 4:1–4"> Or, is it not lawful for me to do what I will? is thy eye evil, because I am good?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 19:30; Mark 10:31; Matthew 22:14"> So shall the last be first, and the first last. For many are called, but few chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 10:32–34; Luke 18:31–34; Matthew 16:13"> And Jesus going up to Jerusalem, took the twelve disciples apart, and said to them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Acts 2:23; Matthew 27:1"> Behold we go up to Jerusalem, and the Son of man shall be betrayed to the chief priests and the scribes, and they shall condemn him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Mark 15:16–20; Matthew 27:27–31"> And shall deliver him to the Gentiles to be mocked, and scourged, and crucified, and the third day he shall rise again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 4:21; Mark 10:35–45; Matthew 8:2"> Then came to him the mother of the sons of Zebedee with her sons, adoring and asking something of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Mark 10:36–37; Esther 5:3"> Who said to her: What wilt thou? She saith to him: Say that these my two sons may sit, the one on thy right hand, and the other on thy left, in thy kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 26:42; Luke 22:42; Matthew 26:39"> And Jesus answering, said: You know not what you ask. Can you drink the chalice that I shall drink? They say to him: We can.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 1:9; Matthew 25:34; Acts 12:2"> He saith to them: My chalice indeed you shall drink; but to sit on my right or left hand, is not mine to give to you, but to them for whom it is prepared by my Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; Philippians 2:3; Mark 10:41"> And the ten hearing it, were moved with indignation against the two brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 22:25–27; Matthew 20:25–28; John 13:12–17"> But Jesus called them to him, and said: You know that the princes of the Gentiles lord it over them; and they that are the greater, exercise power upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 10:43; Mark 9:35; Mark 10:45"> It shall not be so among you: but whosoever will be the greater among you, let him be your minister:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 22:26; Mark 9:33–35; Matthew 18:4"> And he that will be first among you, shall be your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 22:27; Hebrews 9:28; Titus 2:14"> Even as the Son of man is not come to be ministered unto, but to minister, and to give his life a redemption for many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 10:46–52; Luke 18:35–43; Matthew 9:27–31"> And when they went out from Jericho, a great multitude followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Mark 10:46; Isaiah 42:16; Matthew 21:9"> And behold two blind men sitting by the way side, heard that Jesus passed by, and they cried out, saying: O Lord, thou son of David, have mercy on us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 19:13; Colossians 4:2; Matthew 7:7–8"> And the multitude rebuked them that they should hold their peace. But they cried out the more, saying: O Lord, thou son of David, have mercy on us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:37; Philippians 4:6; Matthew 20:21"> And Jesus stood, and called them, and said: What will ye that I do to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 119:18; Ephesians 1:17–19"> They say to him: Lord, that our eyes be opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15–16; Matthew 9:29; Acts 26:18"> And Jesus having compassion on them, touched their eyes. And immediately they saw, and followed him.</span>
