<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 10:40; Matthew 19:1–9; Matthew 7:28"> And it came to pass when Jesus had ended these words, he departed from Galilee, and came into the coasts of Judea, beyond Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 12:15; Mark 6:55–56; Matthew 15:30–31"> And great multitudes followed him: and he healed them there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 10:2; Hebrews 3:9; Matthew 16:1"> And there came to him the Pharisees tempting him, and saying: Is it lawful for a man to put away his wife for every cause?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 2:23; Genesis 1:27; Genesis 5:2"> Who answering, said to them: Have ye not read, that he who made man from the beginning, Made them male and female? And he said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 2:21–24; 1 Corinthians 7:4; 1 Corinthians 7:2"> For this cause shall a man leave father and mother, and shall cleave to his wife, and they two shall be in one flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 13:4; Mark 10:9; 1 Corinthians 7:10–14"> Therefore now they are not two, but one flesh. What therefore God hath joined together, let no man put asunder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:1–4; Matthew 5:31; Mark 10:4"> They say to him: Why then did Moses command to give a bill of divorce, and to put away?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Malachi 2:13–14; Genesis 2:24; Mark 10:5"> He saith to them: Because Moses by reason of the hardness of your heart permitted you to put away your wives: but from the beginning it was not so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 5:32; Luke 16:18; 1 Corinthians 7:39"> And I say to you, that whosoever shall put away his wife, except it be for fornication, and shall marry another, committeth adultery: and he that shall marry her that is put away, committeth adultery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:32–35; Proverbs 19:13–14; Proverbs 21:19"> His disciples say unto him: If the case of a man with his wife be so, it is not expedient to marry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:17; 1 Corinthians 7:35; 1 Corinthians 7:7–9"> Who said to them: All men take not this word, but they to whom it is given.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:32–38; 1 Corinthians 9:5; Isaiah 56:3–4"> For there are eunuchs, who were born so from their mother’s womb: and there are eunuchs, who were made so by men: and there are eunuchs, who have made themselves eunuchs for the kingdom of heaven. He that can take, let him take it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 10:13–16; Luke 18:15–17; Matthew 18:2–5"> Then were little children presented to him, that he should impose hands upon them and pray. And the disciples rebuked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 18:3; Luke 18:16–17; 1 Corinthians 14:20"> But Jesus said to them: Suffer the little children, and forbid them not to come to me: for the kingdom of heaven is for such.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Mark 10:16; 2 Timothy 3:15; 1 Corinthians 7:14"> And when he had imposed hands upon them, he departed from thence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 10:17–30; John 3:15; Jude 1:21"> And behold one came and said to him: Good master, what good shall I do that I may have life everlasting?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Luke 10:26–28; Galatians 3:11–13"> Who said to him: Why asketh thou me concerning good? One is good, God. But if thou wilt enter into life, keep the commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:16–21; Exodus 20:12–17; Mark 10:19"> He said to him: Which? And Jesus said: Thou shalt do no murder, Thou shalt not commit adultery, Thou shalt not steal, Thou shalt not bear false witness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 19:18; Exodus 20:12; Matthew 22:39"> Honour thy father and thy mother: and, Thou shalt love thy neighbor as thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Galatians 3:24; Mark 10:20–21; Philippians 3:6"> The young man saith to him: All these I have kept from my youth, what is yet wanting to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 10:21; Luke 18:22; Luke 12:33"> Jesus saith to him: If thou wilt be perfect, go sell what thou hast, and give to the poor, and thou shalt have treasure in heaven: and come follow me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 6:24; Ezekiel 33:31; Matthew 13:22"> And when the young man had heard this word, he went away sad: for he had great possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Matthew 13:22; Mark 10:23"> Then Jesus said to his disciples: Amen, I say to you, that a rich man shall hardly enter into the kingdom of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 5:44; Luke 18:25; Mark 10:24–25"> And again I say to you: It is easier for a camel to pass through the eye of a needle, than for a rich man to enter into the kingdom of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 10:13; Matthew 24:22; Romans 11:5–7"> And when they had heard this, the disciples wondered very much, saying: Who then can be saved?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:27; Luke 1:37; Job 42:2"> And Jesus beholding, said to them: With men this is impossible: but with God all things are possible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Philippians 3:8; Luke 18:28; Luke 5:11"> Then Peter answering, said to him: Behold we have left all things, and have followed thee: what therefore shall we have?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 22:28–30; 1 Corinthians 6:2–3; Matthew 25:31"> And Jesus said to them: Amen, I say to you, that you, who have followed me, in the regeneration, when the Son of man shall sit on the seat of his majesty, you also shall sit on twelve seats judging the twelve tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 18:29–30; Mark 10:29–30; Philippians 3:8"> And every one that hath left house, or brethren, or sisters, or father, or mother, or wife, or children, or lands for my name’s sake, shall receive an hundredfold, and shall possess life everlasting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 20:16; Luke 13:30; Mark 10:31"> And many that are first, shall be last: and the last shall be first.</span>
