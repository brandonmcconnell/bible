<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 12:35–36; Philippians 2:15–16; Matthew 24:42–51"> Then shall the kingdom of heaven be like to ten virgins, who taking their lamps went out to meet the bridegroom and the bride.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:2; 1 Corinthians 10:1–5; Matthew 13:19–23"> And five of them were foolish, and five wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 3:15–16; 2 Timothy 3:5; Isaiah 48:1–2"> But the five foolish, having taken their lamps, did not take oil with them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 8:9; 1 John 2:27; 1 John 2:20"> But the wise took oil in their vessels with the lamps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:6–8; 2 Peter 3:4–9"> And the bridegroom tarrying, they all slumbered and slept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Matthew 24:31; Matthew 24:44"> And at midnight there was a cry made: Behold the bridegroom cometh, go ye forth to meet him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 12:35; Revelation 3:19–20; 2 Peter 3:14"> Then all those virgins arose and trimmed their lamps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 13:9; Luke 12:35; Hebrews 4:1"> And the foolish said to the wise: Give us of your oil, for our lamps are gone out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 49:7–9; Revelation 3:17–18; Isaiah 55:6–7"> The wise answered, saying: Lest perhaps there be not enough for us and for you, go ye rather to them that sell, and buy for yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 13:25; Matthew 24:44; Luke 12:36–37"> Now whilst they went to buy, the bridegroom came: and they that were ready, went in with him to the marriage, and the door was shut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 7:21–23; Hebrews 12:16–17"> But at last come also the other virgins, saying: Lord, Lord, open to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:3; 2 Timothy 2:19; John 10:27"> But he answering said: Amen I say to you, I know you not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 24:42–44; Revelation 16:15; 1 Thessalonians 5:6"> Watch ye therefore, because you know not the day nor the hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 13:34; Luke 19:12–27; 1 Peter 4:9–11"> For even as a man going into a far country, called his servants, and delivered to them his goods;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:24; Luke 19:13–14; Luke 12:48"> And to one he gave five talents, and to another two, and to another one, to every one according to his proper ability: and immediately he took his journey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="3 John 1:5–8; 1 Timothy 6:17–18; 2 Timothy 2:6"> And he that had received the five talents, went his way, and traded with the same, and gained other five.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 3:9–10; Ephesians 5:16; 1 Peter 4:10"> And in like manner he that had received the two, gained other two.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Peter 1:8; Haggai 1:2–4; Malachi 1:10"> But he that had received the one, going his way digged into the earth, and hid his lord’s money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:12–15; 2 Corinthians 5:10; Romans 14:7–12"> But after a long time the lord of those servants came, and reckoned with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 20:24; Luke 19:16–17; Colossians 1:29"> And he that had received the five talents coming, brought other five talents, saying: Lord, thou didst deliver to me five talents, behold I have gained other five over and above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 25:23; Luke 16:10; John 12:26"> His lord said to him: Well done, good and faithful servant, because thou hast been faithful over a few things, I will place thee over many things: enter thou into the joy of thy lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; 2 Corinthians 8:7–8; 2 Corinthians 8:1–3"> And he also that had received the two talents came and said: Lord, thou deliveredst two talents to me: behold I have gained other two.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Mark 14:8–9; Mark 12:41–44"> His lord said to him: Well done, good and faithful servant: because thou hast been faithful over a few things, I will place thee over many things: enter thou into the joy of thy lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 19:20–22; Luke 15:29; Malachi 3:14–15"> But he that had received the one talent, came and said: Lord, I know that thou art a hard man; thou reapest where thou hast not sown, and gatherest where thou hast not strewed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:6–7; Romans 8:15; Revelation 21:8"> And being afraid I went and hid thy talent in the earth: behold here thou hast that which is thine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 18:32; Job 15:5–6"> And his lord answering, said to him: Wicked and slothful servant, thou knewest that I reap where I sow not, and gather where I have not strewed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:19–20; Romans 3:19; Jude 1:15"> Thou oughtest therefore to have committed my money to the bankers, and at my coming I should have received my own with usury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 19:24; Luke 10:42"> Take ye away therefore the talent from him, and give it to him that hath ten talents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 8:18; Mark 4:25; Matthew 13:12"> For to every one that hath shall be given, and he shall abound: but from him that hath not, that also which he seemeth to have shall be taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Revelation 3:15–16; Matthew 13:50"> And the unprofitable servant cast ye out into the exterior darkness. There shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:7–8; Matthew 19:28"> And when the Son of man shall come in his majesty, and all the angels with him, then shall he sit upon the seat of his majesty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Malachi 3:18; Matthew 13:49; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> And all nations shall be gathered together before him, and he shall separate them one from another, as the shepherd separateth the sheep from the goats:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 100:3; John 10:26–28; John 21:15–17"> And he shall set the sheep on his right hand, but the goats on his left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Peter 1:19–20; James 2:5; Revelation 21:7"> Then shall the king say to them that shall be on his right hand: Come, ye blessed of my Father, possess you the kingdom prepared for you from the foundation of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 3:16–19; James 2:15–16; Deuteronomy 15:7–11"> For I was hungry, and you gave me to eat; I was thirsty, and you gave me to drink; I was a stranger, and you took me in:</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="James 1:27; 2 Timothy 1:16–18; James 5:14–15"> Naked, and you covered me: sick, and you visited me: I was in prison, and you came to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 6:3; 1 Chronicles 29:14; 1 Peter 5:5–6"> Then shall the just answer him, saying: Lord, when did we see thee hungry, and fed thee; thirsty, and gave thee drink?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And when did we see thee a stranger, and took thee in? or naked, and covered thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Or when did we see thee sick or in prison, and came to thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Proverbs 14:31; Matthew 10:42"> And the king answering, shall say to them: Amen I say to you, as long as you did it to one of these my least brethren, you did it to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Jude 1:6–7; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> Then he shall say to them also that shall be on his left hand: Depart from me, you cursed, into everlasting fire which was prepared for the devil and his angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="James 2:15–24; John 5:23; Matthew 12:30"> For I was hungry, and you gave me not to eat: I was thirsty, and you gave me not to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> I was a stranger, and you took me not in: naked, and you covered me not: sick and in prison, and you did not visit me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 7:22; Luke 10:29; Matthew 25:24–27"> Then they also shall answer him, saying: Lord, when did we see thee hungry, or thirsty, or a stranger, or naked, or sick, or in prison, and did not minister to thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Proverbs 21:13; Proverbs 17:5; Proverbs 14:31"> Then he shall answer them, saying: Amen I say to you, as long as you did it not to one of these least, neither did you do it to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; John 3:36; John 5:29"> And these shall go into everlasting punishment: but the just, into life everlasting.</span>
