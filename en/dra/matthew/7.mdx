<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 8:7–9; Galatians 6:1; Luke 6:41–42"> Any why seest thou the mote that is in thy brother’s eye; and seest not the beam that is in thy own eye?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Or how sayest thou to thy brother: Let me cast the mote out of thy eye; and behold a beam is in thy own eye?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 6:42; Psalms 51:9–13; Matthew 23:13–28"> Thou hypocrite, cast out first the beam in thy own eye, and then shalt thou see to cast out the mote out of thy brother’s eye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 9:7–8; Proverbs 23:9; Matthew 10:14–15"> Give not that which is holy to dogs; neither cast ye your pearls before swine, lest perhaps they trample them under their feet, and turning upon you, they tear you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; 1 John 3:22; John 15:7"> Ask, and it shall be given you: seek, and you shall find: knock, and it shall be opened to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 81:10; Psalms 81:16; Luke 23:42–43"> For every one that asketh, receiveth: and he that seeketh, findeth: and to him that knocketh, it shall be opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 11:11–13"> Or what man is there among you, of whom if his son shall ask bread, will he reach him a stone?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Or if he shall ask him a fish, will he reach him a serpent?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 11:11–13; James 1:17; Romans 8:32"> If you then being evil, know how to give good gifts to your children: how much more will your Father who is in heaven, give good things to them that ask him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 6:31; Romans 13:8–10; Galatians 5:13–14"> All things therefore whatsoever you would that men should do to you, do you also to them. For this is the law and the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 13:24–25; Proverbs 16:25; John 14:6"> Enter ye in at the narrow gate: for wide is the gate, and broad is the way that leadeth to destruction, and many there are who go in thereat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 13:23–30; Matthew 22:14; Matthew 16:24–25"> How narrow is the gate, and strait is the way that leadeth to life: and few there are that find it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 4:1; 2 Peter 2:1–3; Matthew 24:11"> Beware of false prophets, who come to you in the clothing of sheep, but inwardly they are ravening wolves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 6:43–45; Matthew 12:33; James 3:12"> By their fruits you shall know them. Do men gather grapes of thorns, or figs of thistles?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 12:33–35; Galatians 5:22–24; Ephesians 5:9"> Even so every good tree bringeth forth good fruit, and the evil tree bringeth forth evil fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 5:17; 1 John 3:9–10"> A good tree cannot bring forth evil fruit, neither can an evil tree bring forth good fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 3:10; Hebrews 6:8; John 15:2–6"> Every tree that bringeth not forth good fruit, shall be cut down, and shall be cast into the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 7:16; Acts 5:38"> Wherefore by their fruits you shall know them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 2:13; Luke 6:46; James 1:22"> Not every one that saith to me, Lord, Lord, shall enter into the kingdom of heaven: but he that doth the will of my Father who is in heaven, he shall enter into the kingdom of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 13:25–27; 1 Corinthians 13:1–2; Malachi 3:17–18"> Many will say to me in that day: Lord, Lord, have not we prophesied in thy name, and cast out devils in thy name, and done many miracles in thy name?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 25:41; Psalms 6:8; Luke 13:25"> And then will I profess unto them, I never knew you: depart from me, you that work iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 6:47–49; John 13:17; 1 Corinthians 3:10–11"> Every one therefore that heareth these my words, and doth them, shall be likened to a wise man that built his house upon a rock,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 1:12; Psalms 125:1–2; 1 Peter 1:7"> And the rain fell, and the floods came, and the winds blew, and they beat upon that house, and it fell not, for it was founded on a rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:9; Luke 6:49; James 2:20"> And every one that heareth these my words, and doth them not, shall be like a foolish man that built his house upon the sand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 13:19–22; 2 Peter 2:20–22; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> And the rain fell, and the floods came, and the winds blew, and they beat upon that house, and it fell, and great was the fall thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 4:32; Mark 1:22; John 7:46"> And it came to pass when Jesus had fully ended these words, the people were in admiration at his doctrine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12–13; Luke 20:8; Deuteronomy 18:18–19"> For he was teaching them as one having power, and not as the scribes and Pharisees.</span>
