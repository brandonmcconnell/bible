<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 66:23; Ezekiel 44:1–2; Exodus 20:9"> Thus saith the Lord God: The gate of the inner court that looketh toward the east, shall be shut the six days, on which work is done; but on the sabbath day it shall be opened, yea and on the day of the new moon it shall be opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:3; Ezekiel 46:8; 1 Chronicles 29:10–12"> And the prince shall enter by the way of the porch of the gate from without, and he shall stand at the threshold of the gate: and the priests shall offer his holocaust, and his peace offerings: and he shall adore upon the threshold of the gate, and shall go out: but the gate shall not be shut till the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 1:10; John 10:9; Hebrews 10:19–22"> And the people of the land shall adore at the door of that gate before the Lord on the sabbaths, and on the new moons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:17; Numbers 28:9–10"> And the holocaust that the prince shall offer to the Lord on the sabbath day, shall be six lambs without blemish, and a ram without blemish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:24; Ezekiel 46:7; Deuteronomy 16:17"> And the sacrifice of an ephi for a ram: but for the lambs what sacrifice his hand shall allow: and a hin of oil for every ephi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:1"> And on the day of the new moon a calf of the herd without blemish: and the six lambs, and the rams shall be without blemish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:5"> And he shall offer in sacrifice an ephi for a calf, an ephi also for a ram: but for the lambs, as his hand shall find: and a hin of oil for every ephi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:2; Ezekiel 44:1–3; Colossians 1:18"> And when the prince is to go in, let him go in by the way of the porch of the gate, and let him go out the same way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 23:14–17; 2 Peter 2:20–21"> But when the people of the land shall go in before the Lord in the solemn feasts, he that goeth in by the north gate to adore, shall go out by the way of the south gate: and he that; goeth in by the way of the south gate, shall go out by the way of the north gate: he shall not return by the way of the gate whereby he came in, but shall go out at that over against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 42:4; 1 Chronicles 29:20; 1 Chronicles 29:22"> And the prince in the midst of them, shall go in when they go in, and go out when they go out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:5; Ezekiel 46:7; Numbers 15:1–41"> And in the fairs, and in the solemnities there shall be the sacrifice of an ephi to a calf, and an ephi to a ram: and to the lambs, the sacrifice shall be as his hand shall find: and a hin of oil to every ephi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:1–2; Ezekiel 45:17; Ezekiel 44:3"> But when the prince shall offer a voluntary holocaust, or voluntary peace offerings to the Lord: the gate that looketh towards the east shall be opened to him, and he shall offer his holocaust, and his peace offerings, as it is wont to be done on the sabbath day: and he shall go out, and the gate shall be shut after he is gone forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 50:4; John 1:29; Exodus 12:5"> And he shall offer every day for a holocaust to the Lord, a lamb of the same year without blemish: he shall offer it always in the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 28:5"> And he shall offer the sacrifice for it morning by morning, the sixth part of ephi: and the third part of a bin of oil be mingled with the fine hour: a to the Lord by ordinance continual and everlasting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 10:1–10; Numbers 28:6; Exodus 29:42"> He shall offer the lamb, and the sacrifice, and the oil morning by morning: an everlasting holocaust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:3; Galatians 4:7; Genesis 25:5–6"> Thus saith the Lord God: If the prince give a gift to any of his sons: the inheritance of it shall go to his children, they shall possess it by inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 25:10; Luke 19:25–26; Galatians 4:30–31"> But if he give a legacy out of his inheritance to one of his servants, it shall be his until the year of release, and it shall return to the prince: but his inheritance shall go to his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:8; 1 Kings 21:19; Ezekiel 22:27"> And the prince shall not take of the people’s inheritance by violence, nor of their possession: but out of his own possession he shall give an inheritance to his sons: that my people be not dispersed every man from his possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:9; Ezekiel 44:4–5; Ezekiel 40:44–46"> And he brought me in by the entry that was at the side of the gate, into the chambers of the sanctuary that were for the priests, which looked toward the north. And there was a place bending to the west.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:13; Ezekiel 44:29; Ezekiel 44:19"> And he said to me: This is the place where the priests shall boil the sin offering, and the trespass offering: where they shall dress the sacrifice, that they may not bring it out into the outward court, and the people be sanctified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And he brought me into the outward court, and he led me about by the four corners of the court: and behold there was a little court in the corner of the court, to every corner of the court there was a little court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> In the four corners of the court were little courts disposed, forty cubits long, and thirty broad, all the four were of one measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And there was a wall round about compassing the four little courts, and there were kitchens built under the rows round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:20; John 21:15–17; 1 Peter 5:2"> And he said to me: This is the house of the kitchens wherein the ministers of the house of the Lord shall boil the victims of the people.</span>
