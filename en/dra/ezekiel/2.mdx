<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 10:11; Ezekiel 3:17; Ezekiel 4:1"> This was the vision of the likeness of the glory of the Lord. And I saw, and I fell upon my face, and I heard the voice of one that spoke. And he said to me: Son of man, stand upon thy feet, and I will speak to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:24; Numbers 11:25–26; Ezekiel 3:12"> And the spirit entered into me after that he spoke to me, and he set me upon my feet: and I heard him speaking to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:25; 1 Samuel 8:7–8; Deuteronomy 9:24"> And saying: Son of man, I send thee to the children of Israel, to a rebellious people, that hath revolted from me, they, and their fathers, have transgressed my covenant even unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:7; Isaiah 48:4; Jeremiah 5:3"> And they to whom I send thee are children of a hard face, and of an obstinate heart: and thou shalt say to them: Thus saith the Lord God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:33; Ezekiel 3:27; Matthew 10:12–15"> If so be they at least will hear, and if so be they will forbear, for they are a provoking house: and they shall know that there hath been a prophet in the midst of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:8; Isaiah 51:12; 1 Peter 3:14"> And thou, O son of man, fear not, neither be thou afraid of their words: for thou art among unbelievers and destroyers, and thou dwellest with scorpions. Fear not their words, neither be thou dismayed at their looks: for they are a provoking house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:28; Ezekiel 3:17; Jeremiah 1:7"> And thou shalt speak my words to them, if perhaps they will hear, and forbear: for they provoke me to anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 10:9; Jeremiah 15:16; Isaiah 50:5"> But thou, O son of man, hear all that I say to thee: and do not thou provoke me, as that house provoketh me: open thy mouth, and eat what I give thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:3; Revelation 5:1–5; Daniel 10:10"> And I looked, and behold, a hand was sent to me, wherein was a book rolled up: and he spread it before me, and it was written within and without: and there were written in it lamentations, and canticles, and woe.</span>
