<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 10:1; Psalms 80:8–16; Isaiah 5:1–7"> Son of man, what shall be made of the wood of the vine, out of all the trees of the woods that are among the trees of the forests?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 14:34–35; Jeremiah 24:8; Mark 9:50"> Shall wood be taken of if, to do any work, or shall a pin be made of it for any vessel to hang thereon?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 15:6; Isaiah 27:11; Psalms 80:16"> Behold it is cast into the fire for fuel: the fire hath consumed both ends thereof, and the midst thereof is reduced to ashes: shall it be useful for any work?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:16"> Even when it was whole it was not fit for work: how much less, when the fire hath devoured and consumed it, shall any work be made of it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 15:2; Jeremiah 7:20; Jeremiah 24:8–10"> Therefore thus saith the Lord God: As the vine tree among the trees of the forests which I have given to the fire to be consumed, so will I deliver up the inhabitants of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:8; Psalms 34:16; Isaiah 24:18"> And I will set my face against them: they shall go out from fire, and fire shall consume them: and you shall know that I am the Lord, when I shall have set my face against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:14; Zephaniah 1:18; Isaiah 6:11"> And I shall have made their land a wilderness, and desolate, because they have been transgressors, saith the Lord God.</span>
