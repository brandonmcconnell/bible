<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:14; Ezekiel 11:24; Ezekiel 11:13"> And the spirit lifted me up, and brought me into the east gate of the house of the Lord, which looketh towards the rising of the sun: and behold in the entry of the gate five and twenty men: and I saw in the midst of them Jezonias the son of Azur, and Pheltias the son of Banaias, princes of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 30:1; Jeremiah 5:5; Psalms 2:1–2"> And he said to me: Son of man, these are the men that study iniquity, and frame a wicked counsel in this city,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:27; Ezekiel 12:22; Ezekiel 11:7–11"> Saying: Were not houses lately built? This city is the caldron, and we the flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:46–47; Ezekiel 21:2; Ezekiel 3:2–15"> Therefore prophesy against them, prophesy, thou son of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:10; Jeremiah 16:17; Revelation 2:23"> And the spirit of the Lord fell upon me, and said to me: Speak: Thus saith the Lord: Thus have you spoken, O house of Israel, for I know the thoughts of your heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:23; Ezekiel 22:27; Ezekiel 22:12"> You have killed a great many in this city, and you have filled the streets thereof with the slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:3–13; Micah 3:2–3; 2 Kings 25:18–22"> Therefore thus saith the Lord God: Your slain, whom you have laid in the midst thereof, they are the flesh, and this is the caldron: and I will bring you forth out of the midst thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 10:24; Isaiah 24:17–18; Job 3:25"> You have feared the sword, and I will bring the sword upon you, saith the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:8; Psalms 106:41; Deuteronomy 28:36"> And I will cast you out of the midst thereof, and I will deliver you into the hand of the enemies, and I will execute judgments upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 14:25; Jeremiah 52:9–10; 2 Kings 25:19–21"> You shall fall by the sword: I will judge you in the borders of Israel, and you shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:3; Ezekiel 11:7–10"> This shall not be as a caldron to you, and you shall not be as flesh in the midst thereof: I will judge you in the borders of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:10; Ezekiel 8:16; Ezekiel 8:14"> And you shall know that I am the Lord: because you have not walked in my commandments, and have not done my judgments, but you have done according to the judgments of the nations that; are round about you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:1; Ezekiel 9:8; Acts 5:5"> And it came to pass, when I prophesied, that Pheltias the son of Banaias died: and I fell down upon my face, and cried with a loud voice: and said: Alas, alas, alas, O Lord God: wilt thou make an end of all the remnant of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:24; Isaiah 65:5; John 16:2"> Son of man, thy brethren, thy brethren, thy kinsmen, and all the house of Israel, all they to whom the inhabitants of Jerusalem have said: Get ye far from the Lord, the land is given in possession to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14; Deuteronomy 30:3–4; Psalms 90:1"> Therefore thus saith the Lord God: Because I have removed them far off among the Gentiles, and because I have scattered them among the countries: I will be to them a little sanctuary in the countries whither they are come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:25; Jeremiah 3:18; Ezekiel 34:13"> Therefore speak to them: Thus saith the Lord God: I will gather you from among the peoples, and assemble you out of the countries wherein you are scattered, and I will give you the land of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:23; Ezekiel 5:11; Ezekiel 42:7–8"> And they shall go in thither, and shall take away all the scandals, and all the abominations thereof from thence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:26–27; Jeremiah 31:33; Psalms 51:10"> And I will give them one heart, and will put a new spirit in their bowels: and I will take away the stony heart out of their flesh, and will give them a heart of flesh:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:11; Psalms 105:45; Jeremiah 30:22"> That they may walk in my commandments, and keep my judgments, and do them: and that they may be my people, and I may be their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:10; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Jeremiah 1:16"> But as for them whose heart walketh after their scandals and abominations, I will lay their way upon their head, saith the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:19; Ezekiel 1:19–20"> And the cherubims lifted up their wings, and the wheels with them: and the glory of the God of Israel was over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zechariah 14:4; Ezekiel 8:4; Ezekiel 10:4"> And the glory of the Lord went up from the midst of the city, and stood over the mount that is on the east side of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:12; Ezekiel 8:3; Ezekiel 11:1"> And the spirit lifted me up, and brought me into Chaldea, to them of the captivity, in vision, by the spirit of God: and the vision which I had seen was taken up from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:4; Ezekiel 3:27; Ezekiel 3:17"> And I spoke to them of the captivity all the words of the Lord, which he had shewn me.</span>
