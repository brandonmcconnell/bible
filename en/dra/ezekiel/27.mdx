<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:1; Jeremiah 9:10; Ezekiel 28:12"> Thou therefore, O son of man, take up a lamentation for Tyre:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:4; Isaiah 23:11; Isaiah 23:2–3"> And say to Tyre that dwelleth at the entry of the sea, being the mart of the people for many islands: Thus saith the Lord God: O Tyre, thou hast said: I am of perfect beauty,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:5"> And situate in the heart of the sea. Thy neighbours, that built thee, have perfected thy beauty:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:9; Isaiah 14:8; Song of Solomon 4:8"> With fir trees of Sanir they have built thee with all sea planks: they have taken cedars from Libanus to make thee masts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 10:4; Isaiah 2:13; Zechariah 11:2"> They have cut thy oars out of the oaks of Basan: and they have made thee benches of Indian ivory and cabins with things brought from the islands of Italy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:9; Exodus 25:4; Genesis 10:4"> Fine broidered linen from Egypt was woven for thy sail, to be spread on thy mast: blue and purple from the islands of Elisa, were made thy covering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 10:18; 1 Kings 9:27; Ezekiel 27:11"> The inhabitants of Sidon, and the Arabians were thy rowers: thy wise men, O Tyre, were thy pilots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 5:18; Joshua 13:5; Psalms 83:7"> The ancients of Gebal, and the wise men thereof furnished mariners for the service of thy various furniture: all the ships of the sea, and their mariners were thy factors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:5; Ezekiel 30:5; Isaiah 66:19"> The Persians, and Lydians, and the Libyans were thy soldiers in thy army: they hung up the buckler and the helmet in thee for thy ornament.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:3–4"> The men of Arad were with thy army upon thy walls round about: the Pygmeans also that were in thy towers, hung up their quivers on thy walls round about: they perfected thy beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 10:4; Isaiah 23:10; Ezekiel 38:13"> The Carthaginians thy merchants supplied thy fairs with a multitude of all kinds of riches, with silver, iron, tin, and lead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 10:2; Isaiah 66:19; Ezekiel 39:1"> Greece, Thubal, and Mosoch, they were thy merchants: they brought to thy people slaves and vessels of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 10:3; Ezekiel 38:6; 1 Chronicles 1:6"> From the house of Thogorma they brought horses, and horsemen, and mules to thy market.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 10:7; Revelation 18:12; 1 Kings 10:22"> The men of Dedan were thy merchants: many islands were the traffic of thy hand, they exchanged for thy price teeth of ivory and ebony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 10:6; Ezekiel 28:13; 2 Samuel 10:6"> The Syrian was thy merchant: by reason of the multitude of thy works, they set forth precious stones, and purple, and broidered works, and fine linen, and silk, and chodchod in thy market.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 11:33; 1 Kings 5:9; Jeremiah 8:22"> Juda and the land of Israel, they were thy merchants with the best corn: they set forth balm, and honey, and oil, and rosin in thy fairs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 7:8; Genesis 15:2; Ezekiel 47:16–18"> The men of Damascus were thy merchants in the multitude of thy works, in the multitude of divers riches, in rich wine, in wool of the best colour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 30:23–24; Psalms 45:8; Song of Solomon 4:13–14"> Dan, and Greece, and Mosel have set forth in thy marts wrought iron: stacte, and calamus were in thy market.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 25:3; Ezekiel 27:15"> The men of Dedan were thy merchants in tapestry for seats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 60:7; Genesis 25:13; 1 Kings 10:15"> Arabia, and all the princes of Cedar, they were the merchants of thy hand: thy merchants came to thee with lambs, and rants, and kids.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 10:7; Isaiah 60:6; Psalms 72:10"> The sellers of Saba, and Reema, they were thy merchants: with all the best spices, and precious stones, and gold, which they set forth in thy market.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 19:12; Isaiah 37:12; Amos 1:5"> Haran, and Chene, and Eden were thy merchants; Saba, Assur, and Chelmad sold to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Kings 2:8"> They were thy merchants in divers manners, with bales of blue cloth, and of embroidered work, and of precious riches, which were wrapped up and bound with cords: they had cedars also in thy merchandise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 2:16; Psalms 48:7; Isaiah 23:14"> The ships of the sea, were thy chief in thy merchandise: and thou wast replenished, and glorified exceedingly in the heart of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 48:7; Ezekiel 26:19; Acts 27:14"> Thy rowers have brought thee into great waters: the south wind hath broken thee in the heart of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 11:4; Ezekiel 27:24; Ezekiel 27:12"> Thy riches, and thy treasures, and thy manifold furniture, thy mariners, and thy pilots, who kept thy goods, and were chief over thy people: thy men of war also, that were in thee, with all thy multitude that is in the midst of thee: shall fall in the heart of the sea in the day of thy ruin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:10; Exodus 15:14; Ezekiel 31:16"> Thy fleets shall be troubled at the sound of the cry of thy pilots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:16; Ezekiel 32:10; Revelation 18:17–24"> And all that handled the oar shall come down from their ships: the mariners, and all the pilots of the sea shall stand upon the land:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:26; 2 Samuel 1:2; Lamentations 2:10"> And they shall mourn over thee with a loud voice, and shall cry bitterly: and they shall cast up dust upon their heads, and shall be sprinkled with ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:18; Isaiah 22:12; Isaiah 15:2"> And they shall shave themselves bald for thee, and shall be girded with haircloth: and they shall weep for thee with bitterness of soul, with most bitter weeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:17; Revelation 18:18; Ezekiel 27:2"> And they shall take up a mournful song for thee, and snail lament thee: What city is like Tyre, which is become silent in the midst of the sea?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Revelation 18:19; Ezekiel 27:3; Ezekiel 28:16"> Which by thy merchandise that went from thee by sea didst fill many people: which by the multitude of thy riches, and of thy people didst enrich the kings of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:26–27; Zechariah 9:3–4; Ezekiel 26:19–21"> Now thou art destroyed by the sea, thy riches are in the bottom of the waters, and all the multitude that was in the midst of thee is fallen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:10; Isaiah 23:6; Ezekiel 26:15–18"> All the inhabitants of the islands are astonished at thee: and all their kings being struck with the storm have changed their countenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:16; Psalms 37:10; Zephaniah 2:15"> The merchants of people have hissed at thee: thou art brought to nothing, and thou shalt never be any more.</span>
