<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:9; Ezekiel 34:14; Ezekiel 20:46"> Son of man, set thy face towards the mountains of Israel, and prophesy against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 26:30; Ezekiel 36:1–4; Jeremiah 3:23"> And say: Ye mountains of Israel, hear the word of the Lord God: Thus Faith the Lord God to the mountains, and to the hills, and to the rocks, and the valleys: Behold, I will bring upon you the sword, and I will destroy your high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:5; Leviticus 26:30; 2 Kings 23:14"> And I will throw down your altars, and your idols shall be broken in pieces: and I will cast down your slain before your idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 23:16; Jeremiah 8:1–2; 2 Kings 23:14"> And I will lay the dead carcasses of the children of Israel before your idols: and I will scatter Sour bones round about your altars,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:14; Isaiah 6:11; Zechariah 13:2"> In all your dwelling places. The cities shall be laid waste, and the high places shall be thrown down, and destroyed, and your altars shall be abolished, and shall be broken in pieces: and your idols shall be no more, and your temples shall be destroyed, and your works shall be defaced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:33; Ezekiel 13:23; Ezekiel 35:15"> And the slain shall fall in the midst of you: and you shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:22; Jeremiah 44:28; Ezekiel 12:16"> And I will leave in you some that shall escape the sword among the nations, when I shall have scattered you, through the countries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:43; Isaiah 7:13; Job 42:6"> And they that are saved of you shall remember me amongst the nations to which they are carried captives: because I have broken their heart that was faithless, and revolted from me: and their eyes that went a fornicating after their idols: and they shall be displeased with themselves because of the evils which they have committed in all their abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:12–14; Daniel 9:12; Jeremiah 44:28"> And they shall know that I the Lord have not spoken in vain that I would do this evil to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:12; Ezekiel 9:4; Ezekiel 21:14–17"> Thus saith the Lord God: Strike with thy hand, and stamp with thy foot, and say: Alas, for all the abominations of the evils of the house of Israel: for they shall fall by the sword, by the famine and by the pestilence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:13; Daniel 9:7; Lamentations 4:22"> He that is far off shall die of the pestilence: and he that is near, shall fall by the sword: and he that remaineth, and is besieged, shall die by the famine: and I will accomplish my indignation upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:28; Hosea 4:13; 1 Kings 14:23"> And you shall know that I am the Lord, when your slain shall be amongst your idols, round about your altars, in every high hill, and on all the tops of mountains, and under every woody tree, and under every thick oak, the place where they burnt sweet smelling frankincense to all their idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 5:25; Ezekiel 20:33–34; Isaiah 9:12"> And I will stretch forth my hand upon them: and I will make the land desolate, and abandoned from the desert of Deblatha in all their dwelling places: and they shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
