<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:2–3; Ezekiel 35:3; Nahum 3:5"> And thou, son of man, prophesy against Cog, and say: Thus saith the Lord God: Behold, I come against thee, O Cog, the chief prince of Mosoch and Thubal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 40:14; Ezekiel 38:15; Isaiah 37:29"> And I will turn thee round, and I will lead thee out, and will make thee go up from the northern parts: and will bring thee upon the mountains of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 76:3; Hosea 1:5; Jeremiah 21:4–5"> And I will break thy bow in thy left hand, and I will cause thy arrows to fall out of thy right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:17–20; Ezekiel 33:27; Ezekiel 38:21"> Thou shalt fall upon the mountains of Israel, thou and all thy bands, and thy nations that are with thee: I have given thee to the wild beasts, to the birds, and to every fowl, and to the beasts of the earth to be devoured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:19; Ezekiel 32:4; Jeremiah 8:2"> Thou shalt fall upon the face of the field: for I have spoken it, saith the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:16; Amos 1:4; Ezekiel 30:8"> And I will send a fire on Magog, and on them that dwell confidently in the islands: and they shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:16; Ezekiel 38:23; Exodus 20:7"> And I will make my holy name known in the midst of my people Israel, and my holy name shall be profaned no more: and the Gentiles shall know that I am the Lord, the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:17; Revelation 16:17; Ezekiel 7:2–10"> Behold it cometh, and it is done, saith the Lord God: this is the day whereof I have spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 46:9; Isaiah 66:24; Malachi 1:5"> And the inhabitants shall go forth of the cities of Israel, and shall set on fire and burn the weapons, the shields, and the spears, the bows and the arrows, and the handstaves and the pikes: and they shall burn them with fire seven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 33:1; Isaiah 14:2; Micah 5:8"> And they shall not bring wood out of the countries, nor cut down out of the forests: for they shall burn the weapons with fire, and shall make a prey of them to whom they had been a prey, and they shall rob those that robbed them, saith the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:18; Numbers 34:11; Ezekiel 38:2"> And it shall come to pass in that day, that I will give Gog a noted place for a sepulchre in Israel: the valley of the passengers on the east of the sea, which shall cause astonishment in them that pass by: and there shall they bury Cog, and all his multitude, and it shall be called the valley of the multitude of Cog.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:23; Ezekiel 39:14; Ezekiel 39:16"> And the house of Israel shall bury them for seven months to cleanse the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:22; Zephaniah 3:19–20; Jeremiah 33:9"> And all the people of the land shall bury him, and it shall be unto them a noted day, wherein I was glorified, saith the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:12; Numbers 19:11–19"> And they shall appoint men to go continually about the land, to bury and to seek out them that were remaining upon the face of the earth, that they may cleanse it: and after seven months they shall begin to seek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 11:44"> And they shall go about passing through the land: and when they shall see the bone of a man, they shall set up a sign by it, till the buriers bury it in the valley of the multitude of Cog.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the name of the city shall be Amona, and they shall cleanse the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:7; Revelation 19:17–18; Isaiah 34:6"> And thou, O son of man, saith the Lord God, say to every fowl, and to all the birds, and to all the beasts of the field: Assemble yourselves, make haste, come together from every side to my victim, which I slay for you, a great victim upon the mountains of Israel: to eat flesh, and drink blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 22:12; Jeremiah 51:40; Revelation 19:17–18"> You shall eat the flesh of the mighty, and you shall drink the blood of the princes of the earth, of rams, and of lambs, and of he goats, and bullocks, and of all that are well fed and fat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And you shall eat the fat till you be full, and shall drink blood till you be drunk of the victim which I shall slay for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 19:18; Haggai 2:22; Psalms 76:5–6"> And you shall be filled at my table with horses, and mighty horsemen, and all the men of war, saith the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:16; Ezekiel 38:23; Isaiah 37:20"> And I will set my glory among the nations: and all nations shall see my judgment that I have executed, and my hand that I have laid upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:7; Ezekiel 28:26; Ezekiel 39:28"> And the house of Israel shall know that I am the Lord their God from that day and forward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:8–9; Isaiah 1:15; Isaiah 59:2"> And the nations shall know that the house of Israel were made captives for their iniquity, because they forsook me, and I hid my face from them: and I delivered them into the hands of their enemies, and they fell all by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:19; Jeremiah 2:17; Jeremiah 4:18"> I have dealt with them according to their uncleanness, and wickedness, and hid my face from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:13; Isaiah 27:12–13; Hosea 1:11"> Therefore, thus saith the Lord God: Now will I bring back the captivity of Jacob, and will have mercy on all the house of Israel: and I will be jealous for my holy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Micah 4:4; 1 Kings 4:25; Ezekiel 16:63"> And they shall bear their confusion, and all the transgressions wherewith they have transgressed against me, when they shall dwell in their land securely fearing no man:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:16; Ezekiel 38:23; Ezekiel 36:23–24"> And I shall have brought them back from among the nations, and shall have gathered them together out of the lands of their enemies, and shall be sanctified in them, in the sight of many nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:22; Amos 9:9; Isaiah 27:12"> And they shall know that I am the Lord their God, because I caused them to be carried away among the nations; and I have gathered them together unto their own land, and have not left any of them there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joel 2:28; 1 John 3:24; Isaiah 32:15"> And I will hide my face no more from them, for I have poured out my spirit upon all the house of Israel, saith the Lord God.</span>
