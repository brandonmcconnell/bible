<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:49; Matthew 13:35; 1 Corinthians 13:12"> Son of man, put forth a riddle, and speak a parable to the house of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 8:1; Jeremiah 48:40; Ezekiel 17:7"> And say: Thus saith the Lord God: A large eagle with great wings, long-limbed, full of feathers, and of variety, came to Libanus, and took away the marrow of the cedar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 43:14; Isaiah 47:15; Revelation 18:11–19"> He cropped off the top of the twigs thereof: and carried it away into the land of Chanaan, and he set it in a city of merchants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 44:4; Deuteronomy 8:7–9; Jeremiah 37:1"> And he took of the seed of the land, and put it in the ground for seed, that it might take a firm root over many waters: he planted it on the surface of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:14; Proverbs 16:18–19"> And it sprung up and grew into a spreading vine of low stature, and the branches thereof looked towards him: and the roots thereof were under him. So it became a vine, and grew into branches, and shot forth sprigs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:4; Ezekiel 17:15; 2 Kings 24:20"> And there was another large eagle, with great wings, and many feathers: and behold this vine, bending as it were her roots towards him, stretched forth her branches to him, that he might water it by the furrows of her plantation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:5–6"> It was planted in a good ground upon many waters, that it might bring forth branches, and bear fruit, that it might become a large vine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:10; Jeremiah 32:5; Isaiah 30:1–7"> Say thou: Thus saith the Lord God: Shall it prosper then? shall he not pull up the roots thereof, and strip off its fruit, and dry up all the branches it hath shot forth, and make it wither: and this without a strong arm, or many people, to pluck it up by the root?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 13:15; John 15:6; Matthew 21:19"> Behold, it is planted: shall it prosper then? shall it not be dried up when the burning wind shall touch it, and shall it not wither in the furrows where it grew?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:19; Ezekiel 17:3; Ezekiel 1:2"> Say to the provoking house: Know you not what these things mean? Tell them: Behold the king of Babylon cometh to Jerusalem: and he shall take away the king and the princes thereof, and carry them with him to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:13; 2 Kings 24:15–17; Ezekiel 17:5"> And he shall take one of the king’s seed, and make a covenant with him, and take an oath of him. Yea, and he shall take away the mighty men of the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:14; Deuteronomy 28:43; 1 Samuel 2:30"> That it may be a low kingdom and not lift itself up, but keep his covenant, and observe it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:3; Jeremiah 38:18; Ezekiel 17:7"> But he hath revolted from him and sent ambassadors to Egypt, that it might give him horses, and much people. And shall he that hath done thus prosper, or be saved? and shall he escape that hath broken the covenant?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Jeremiah 52:11; Ezekiel 17:18–19"> As I live, saith the Lord God: In the place where the king dwelleth that made him king, whose oath he hath made void, and whose covenant he broke, even in the midst of Babylon shall he die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:7; Ezekiel 4:2; Ezekiel 29:6–7"> And not with a great army, nor with much people shall Pharao fight against him: when he shall cast up mounts, and build forts, to cut off many souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:24; 2 Chronicles 30:8; Lamentations 5:6"> For he had despised the oath, breaking his covenant, and behold he hath given his hand: and having done all these things, he shall not escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:11; Jeremiah 5:9; Jeremiah 5:2"> Therefore thus saith the Lord God: As I live, I will lay upon his head the oath he hath despised, and the covenant he hath broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Ezekiel 32:3; Jeremiah 2:35"> And I will spread my net over him, and he shall be taken in my net: and I will bring him into Babylon, and will judge him there for the transgression by which he hath despised me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 25:5; 2 Kings 25:11; Ezekiel 12:14"> And all his fugitives with all his bands shall fall by the sword: and the residue shall be scattered into every wind: and you shall know that I the Lord have spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2; Zechariah 6:12–13; Isaiah 11:1–5"> Thus saith the Lord God: I myself will take of the marrow of the high cedar, and will set it: I will crop off a tender twig from the top of the branches thereof, and I will plant it on a mountain high and eminent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 13:32; Ezekiel 31:6; Isaiah 2:2"> On the high mountains of Israel will I plant it, and it shall shoot forth into branches, and shall bear fruit, and it shall become a great cedar: and all birds shall dwell under it, and every fowl shall make its nest under the shadow of the branches thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Amos 9:11; 1 Corinthians 1:27–28; Ezekiel 24:14"> And all the trees of the country shall know that I the Lord have brought down the high tree, and exalted the low tree: and have dried up the green tree, and have caused the dry tree to flourish. I the Lord have spoken and have done it.</span>
