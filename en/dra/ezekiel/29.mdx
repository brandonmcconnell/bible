<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:17; Ezekiel 26:1; Ezekiel 20:1"> In the tenth year, the tenth month, the eleventh day of the month, the word of the Lord came to me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:2; Jeremiah 44:30; Jeremiah 46:2–16"> Son of man, set thy face against Pharao king of Egypt: and thou shalt prophesy of him, and of all Egypt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:2; Isaiah 27:1; Jeremiah 44:30"> Speak, and say: Thus saith the Lord God: Behold, I come against thee, Pharao king of Egypt, thou great dragon that liest in the midst of thy rivers, and sayest: The river is mine, and I made myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 19:28; Ezekiel 38:4; Amos 4:2"> But I will put a bridle in thy jaws: and I will cause the fish of thy rivers to stick to thy scales: and I will draw thee out of the midst of thy rivers, and all thy fish shall stick to thy scales.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:20; Ezekiel 32:4–6; Jeremiah 8:2"> And I will cast thee forth into the desert, and all the fish of thy river: thou shalt fall upon the face of the earth, thou shalt not be taken up, nor gathered together: I have given thee for meat to the beasts of the earth, and to the fowls of the air.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 18:21; Isaiah 36:6; Exodus 9:14"> And all the inhabitants of Egypt shall know that I am the Lord: because thou hast been a staff of a reed to the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 36:6; Ezekiel 17:15–17; Jeremiah 17:5–6"> When they took hold of thee with the hand thou didst break, and rent all their shoulder: and when they leaned upon thee, thou brokest, and weakenest all their loins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:17; Ezekiel 30:10; Ezekiel 29:19–20"> Therefore thus saith the Lord God: Behold, I will bring the sword upon thee: and cut off man and beast out of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:3; Proverbs 16:18; Ezekiel 29:10–12"> And the land of Egypt shall become a desert, and a wilderness: and they shall know that I am the Lord: because thou hast said: The river is mine, and I made it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:12; Ezekiel 30:6–9; Exodus 14:2"> Therefore, behold I come against thee, and thy rivers: and I will make the land of Egypt utterly desolate, and wasted by the sword, from the tower of Syene, even to the borders of Ethiopia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:13; Jeremiah 43:11–12; Ezekiel 30:10–13"> The foot of man shall not pass through it, neither shall the foot of beasts go through it: nor shall it be inhabited during forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:19; Ezekiel 30:7; Ezekiel 30:23"> And I will make the land of Egypt desolate in the midst of the lands that are desolate, and the cities thereof in the midst of the cities that are destroyed, and they shall be desolate for forty years: and I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations, and will disperse them through the countries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:26; Isaiah 19:22"> For thus saith the Lord God: At the end of forty years I will gather the Egyptians from the people among whom they had been scattered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:14; Isaiah 11:11; Jeremiah 44:1"> And I will bring back the captivity of Egypt, and will place them in the land of Phatures, in the land of their nativity, and they shall be there a low kingdom:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 10:11; Ezekiel 30:13; Ezekiel 17:14"> It shall be the lowest among other kingdoms, and it shall no more be exalted over the nations, and I will diminish them that they shall rule no more over the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 8:13; Isaiah 20:5; Ezekiel 29:6–7"> And they shall be no more a confidence to the house of Israel, teaching iniquity, that they may flee, and follow them: and they shall know that I am the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:1; Ezekiel 1:2; Ezekiel 24:1"> And it came to pass in the seven and twentieth year, in the first month, in the first of the month: that the word of the Lord came to me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:6; Jeremiah 25:9; Ezekiel 26:7–12"> Son of man, Nabuchodonosor king of Babylon hath made his army to undergo hard service against Tyre: every head was made bald, and every shoulder was peeled: and there hath been no reward given him, nor his army for Tyre, for the service that he rendered me against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:24–25; Jeremiah 43:10–13; Ezekiel 32:11"> Therefore thus saith the Lord God: Behold, I will set Nabuchodonosor the king of Babylon in the land of Egypt: and he shall take her multitude, and take the booty thereof for a prey, and rifle the spoils thereof: and it shall be wages for his army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:9; Isaiah 10:6–7; Isaiah 45:1–3"> And for the service that he hath done me against it: I have given him the land of Egypt, because he hath laboured for me, saith the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:10; Luke 21:15; Psalms 132:17"> In that day a horn shall bud forth to the house of Israel, and I will give thee an open mouth in the midst of them: and they shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
