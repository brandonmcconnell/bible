<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:2; Ezekiel 26:17; 2 Kings 24:6"> Moreover take thou up a lamentation for the princes of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 11:3; Isaiah 5:29; Job 4:11"> And say: Why did thy mother the lioness lie down among the lions, and bring up her whelps in the midst of young lions?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:25; 2 Kings 23:31–32; 2 Chronicles 36:1–2"> And she brought out one of her whelps, and he became a lion: and he learned to catch the prey, and to devour men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:4; 2 Kings 23:33–34; 2 Chronicles 36:6"> And the nations heard of him, and took him, but not without receiving wounds: and they brought him in chains into the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:3; 2 Kings 23:34–37"> But she seeing herself weakened, and that her hope was lost, took one of her young lions, and set him up for a lion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:5; 2 Chronicles 36:9; Jeremiah 26:1–24"> And he went up and down among the lions, and became a lion: and he learned to catch the prey, and to devour men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:12; Ezekiel 12:19; Micah 1:2"> He learned to make widows, and to lay waste their cities: and the land became desolate, and the fulness thereof by the noise of his roaring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 4:20; Ezekiel 19:4; Ezekiel 12:13"> And the nations Game together against him on every side out of the provinces, and they spread their net over him, in their wounds he was taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:6; Ezekiel 6:2; Jeremiah 36:30–31"> And they put him into a cage, they brought him in chains to the king of Babylon: and they cast him into prison, that his voice should no more be heard upon the mountains of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 80:8–11; Deuteronomy 8:7; Numbers 24:6–7"> Thy mother is like a vine in thy blood planted by the water: her fruit and her branches have grown out of many waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:3; Psalms 80:15; Daniel 4:11"> And she hath strong rods to make sceptres for them that bear rule, and her stature was exalted among the branches: and she saw her height in the multitude of her branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:10; Hosea 13:15; Isaiah 27:11"> But she was plucked up in wrath, and cast on the ground, and the burning wind dried up her fruit: her strong rods are withered, and dried up: the fire hath devoured her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 2:3; Ezekiel 19:10; Psalms 63:1"> And now she is transplanted into the desert, in a land not passable, and dry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 80:15–16; Ezekiel 19:11; 2 Kings 24:20"> And a fire is gone out from a rod of her branches, which hath devoured her fruit: so that she now hath no strong rod, to be a sceptre of rulers. This is a lamentation, and it shall be for a lamentation.</span>
