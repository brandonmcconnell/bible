<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:20; Ezekiel 40:17; Ezekiel 42:13"> And he brought me forth into the outward court by the way that leadeth to the north, and he brought me into the chamber that was over against the separate building, and over against the house toward the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:13"> In the face of the north door was the length of a hundred cubits, and the breadth of fifty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:15–16; Ezekiel 41:10; Song of Solomon 7:5"> Over against the twenty cubits of the inner court, and over against the pavement of the outward court that was paved with stone, where there was a gallery joined to a triple gallery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:19; Luke 13:24; Matthew 7:14"> And before the chambers was a walk ten cubits broad, looking to the inner parts of a way of one cubit. And their doors were toward the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:7"> Where were the store chambers lower above: because they bore up the galleries, which appeared above out of them from the lower parts, and from the midst of the building.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:6; 1 Kings 6:8"> For they were of three stories, and had not pillars, as the pillars of the courts: therefore did they appear above out of the lower places, and out of the middle places, fifty cubits from the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:12; Ezekiel 42:10"> And the outward wall that went about by the chambers, which were towards the outward court on the forepart of the chambers, was fifty cubits long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:13–14"> For the length of the chambers of the outward court was fifty cubits: and the length before the face of the temple, a hundred cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:19; Ezekiel 44:5"> And there was under these chambers, an entrance from the east, for them that went into them out of the outward court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:1; Ezekiel 42:13; Ezekiel 41:12–15"> In the breadth of the outward wall of the court that was toward the east, over against the separate building, and there were chambers before the building.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:4"> And the way before them was like the chambers which were toward the north: they wore as long as they, and as broad as they: and all the going is to them, and their fashions, and their doors were alike.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> According to the doors of the chambers that were towards the south: there was a door in the head of the way, which way was before the porch, separated towards the east as one entereth in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 10:17; Leviticus 7:6; Ezekiel 40:46"> And he said to me: The chambers of the north, and the chambers of the south, which are before the separate building: they are holy chambers, in which the priests shall eat, that approach to the Lord into the holy of holies: there they shall lay the most holy things, and the offering for sin, and for trespass: for it is a holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:19; Leviticus 8:7; Exodus 29:4–9"> And when the priests shall have entered in, they shall not go out of the holy places into the outward court: but there they shall lay their vestments, wherein they minister, for they are holy: and they shall put on other garments, and so they shall go forth to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:1; Ezekiel 41:15; Ezekiel 40:6–16"> Now when he had made an end of measuring the inner house, he brought me out by the way of the gate that looked toward the east: and he measured it on every side round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:3; Revelation 11:1–2; Zechariah 2:1"> And he measured toward the east with the measuring reed, five hundred reeds with the measuring reed round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And he measured toward the north five hundred reeds with the measuring reed round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And towards the south he measured five hundred reeds with the measuring reed round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And toward the west he measured five hundred reeds, with the measuring reed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:2; Ezekiel 22:26; Ezekiel 40:5"> By the four winds he measured the wall thereof on every side round about, five hundred cubits long and five hundred cubits broad, making a separation between the sanctuary and the place of the people.</span>
