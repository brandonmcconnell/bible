<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:9; 2 Thessalonians 2:4; Isaiah 14:13–14"> Son of man, say to the prince of Tyre: Thus saith the Lord God: Because thy heart is lifted up, and thou hast said: I am God, and I sit in the chair of God in the heart of the sea: whereas thou art a man, and not God: and hast set thy heart as if it were the heart of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 1:20; Daniel 2:22; Daniel 2:27–28"> Behold thou art wiser than Daniel: no secret is hid from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:17–18; Proverbs 23:4–5; Proverbs 18:11"> In thy wisdom and thy understanding thou hast made thyself strong: and hast gotten gold and silver into thy treasures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 62:10; Psalms 52:7; Ezekiel 28:2"> By the greatness of thy wisdom, and by thy traffic thou hast increased thy strength: and thy heart is lifted up with thy strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:2; Exodus 9:17; James 1:11"> Therefore, thus saith the Lord God: Because thy heart is lifted up as the heart of God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:12; Ezekiel 32:12; Ezekiel 30:11"> Therefore behold, I will bring upon thee strangers the strongest of the nations: and they shall draw their swords against the beauty of thy wisdom, and they shall defile thy beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:26–27; Ezekiel 27:34; Proverbs 1:12"> They shall kill thee, and bring thee down: and thou shalt die the death of them that are slain in the heart of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:2; Isaiah 31:3; Psalms 82:7"> Wilt thou yet say before them that slay thee: I am God; whereas thou art a man, and not God, in the hand of them that slay thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:18; Ezekiel 32:19; Philippians 3:3"> Thou shalt die the death of the uncircumcised by the hand of strangers: for I have spoken it, saith the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying: Son of man, take up a lamentation upon the king of Tyre:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:1; Ezekiel 26:17; 2 Corinthians 1:22"> And say to him: Thus saith the Lord God: Thou wast the seal of resemblance, full of wisdom, and perfect in beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:15; Genesis 2:8; Ezekiel 27:16"> Thou wast in the pleasures of the paradise of God: every precious stone teas thy covering: the sardius, the topaz, and the jasper, the chrysolite, and the onyx, and the beryl, the sapphire, and the carbuncle, and the emerald: gold the work of thy beauty: and thy pipes were prepared in the day that thou wast created.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 14:12–15; Exodus 30:26; Ezekiel 28:13"> Thou a cherub stretched out, and protecting, and I set thee in the holy mountain of God, thou hast walked in the midst of the stones of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Ezekiel 28:17–18; Romans 7:9"> Thou wast perfect in thy ways from the day of thy creation, until iniquity was found in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Habakkuk 2:8; Habakkuk 2:17"> By the multitude of thy merchandise, thy inner parts were filled with iniquity, and thou hast sinned: and I cast thee out from the mountain of God, and destroyed thee, O covering cherub, out of the midst of the stones of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:5; Ezekiel 31:10; James 4:6"> And thy heart was lifted up with thy beauty: thou best lost thy wisdom in thy beauty, I have cast thee to the ground: I have set thee before the face of kings, that they might behold thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Malachi 4:3; Revelation 18:8; Amos 1:9–10"> Thou hast defiled thy sanctuaries by the multitude of thy iniquities, and by the iniquity of thy traffic: therefore I will bring forth a fire from the midst of thee, to devour thee, and I will make thee as ashes upon the earth in the sight of all that see thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:21; Revelation 18:21; Revelation 18:15–19"> All that shall see thee among the nations, shall be astonished at thee: thou art brought to nothing, and thou shalt never be any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:2; Genesis 10:15; Jeremiah 25:22"> Son of man, set thy face against Sidon: and thou shalt prophesy of it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:13; Exodus 14:17; Ezekiel 20:41"> And shalt say: Thus saith the Lord God: Behold I come against thee, Sidon, and I will be glorified in the midst of thee: and they shall know that I am the Lord, when I shall execute judgments in her, and shall be sanctified in her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:22; Ezekiel 26:6; Ezekiel 25:11"> And I will send into her pestilence, and blood in her streets: and they shall fall being slain by the sword on all sides in the midst thereof: and they shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 33:55; Joshua 23:13; 2 Corinthians 12:7"> And the house of Israel shall have no more a stumblingblock of bitterness, nor a thorn causing pain on every side round about them, of them that are against them: and they shall know that I am the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:37; Ezekiel 20:41; Ezekiel 36:28"> Thus saith the Lord God: When I shall have gathered together the house of Israel out of the people among whom they are scattered: I will be sanctified in them before the Gentiles: and they shall dwell in their own land, which I gave to my servant Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:24; Ezekiel 28:22; Jeremiah 32:15"> And they shall dwell therein secure, and they shall build houses, and shall plant vineyards, and shall dwell with confidence, when I shall have executed judgments upon all that are their enemies round about: and they shall know that I am the Lord their God.</span>
