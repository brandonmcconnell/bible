<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> They bow themselves to bring forth young, and they cast them, and send forth roarings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Their young are weaned and go to feed: they go forth, and return not to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 11:12; Job 24:5; Job 6:5"> Who hath sent out the wild ass free, and who hath loosed his bonds?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 107:34; Job 24:5; Deuteronomy 29:23"> To whom I have given a house in the wilderness, and his dwellings in the barren land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 5:18; Job 3:18; Exodus 5:13–16"> He scorneth the multitude of the city, he heareth not the cry of the driver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 104:27–28; Psalms 145:15–16; Job 40:15"> He looketh round about the mountains of his pasture, and seeketh for every green thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 23:22; Deuteronomy 33:17; Psalms 92:10"> Shall the rhinoceros be willing to serve thee, or will he stay at thy crib?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 10:10–11; Psalms 129:3; Job 39:7"> Canst thou bind the rhinoceros with thy thong to plough, or will he break the clods of the valleys after thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 1:26; Psalms 144:14; Genesis 42:26"> Wilt thou have confidence in his great strength, and leave thy labours to him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:15; Proverbs 3:16; Amos 2:13"> Wilt thou trust him that he will render thee the seed, and gather it into thy barnfloor?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:7; Zechariah 5:9; 2 Chronicles 9:21"> The wing of the ostrich is like the wings of the heron, and of the hawk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> When she leaveth her eggs on the earth, thou perhaps wilt warm them in the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> She forgetteth that the foot may tread upon them, or that the beasts of the field may break them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Lamentations 4:3; Deuteronomy 28:56–57; 1 Kings 3:26–27"> She is hardened against her young ones, as though they were not hers, she hath laboured in vain, no fear constraining her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 35:11; Job 17:4; Isaiah 57:17"> For God hath deprived her of wisdom, neither hath he given her understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 41:29; Job 39:22; Job 5:22"> When time shall be, she setteth up her wings on high: she scorneth the horse and his rider.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 147:10; Psalms 93:1; Psalms 104:1"> Wilt thou give strength to the horse, or clothe his neck with neighing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:16; Job 41:20–21; Joel 2:5"> Wilt thou lift him up like the locusts? the glory of his nostrils is terror.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:6; Psalms 19:5; 1 Samuel 17:4–10"> He breaketh up the earth with his hoof, he pranceth boldly, he goeth forward to meet armed men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 41:33; Job 39:16; Job 39:18"> He despiseth fear, he turneth not his back to the sword,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 41:26–29"> Above him shall the quiver rattle, the spear and shield shall glitter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Amos 3:6; Luke 24:41; Job 37:20"> Chasing and raging he swalloweth the ground, neither doth he make account when the noise of the trumpet soundeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 70:3; Ezekiel 26:2; Ezekiel 36:2"> When he heareth the trumpet he saith: Ha, ha: he smelleth the battle afar off, the encouraging of the captains, and the shouting of the army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:15; Song of Solomon 2:12; Leviticus 16:11"> Doth the hawk wax feathered by thy wisdom, spreading her wings to the south?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Obadiah 1:4; Jeremiah 49:16; Exodus 19:4"> Will the eagle mount up at thy command, and make her nest in high places?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:4"> She abideth among the rocks, and dwelleth among cragged flints, and stony hills, where there is no access.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 9:26"> From thence she looketh for the prey, and her eyes behold afar off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 24:28; Luke 17:37; Ezekiel 39:17–19"> Her young ones shall suck up blood: and wheresoever the carcass shall be, she is immediately there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And the Lord went on, and said to Job:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Shall he that contendeth with God be so easily silenced? surely he that reproveth God, ought to answer him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Then Job answered the Lord, and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> What can I answer, who hath spoken inconsiderately? I will lay my hand upon my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> One thing I have spoken, which I wish I had not said: and another, to which I will add no more.</span>
