<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Job answered, and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 64:3; Job 18:2; Job 27:2"> How long do you afflict my soul, and break me in pieces with words?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 31:7; Nehemiah 4:12; Job 18:4–21"> Behold, these ten times you confound me, and are not ashamed to oppress me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Proverbs 9:12; Ezekiel 18:4"> For if I have been ignorant, my ignorance shall be with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 35:26; Psalms 38:16; Psalms 55:12"> But you have set yourselves up against me, and reprove me with my reproaches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 18:8–10; Job 27:2; Job 16:11–14"> At least now understand, that God hath not afflicted me with an equal judgment, and compassed me with his scourges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:2–3; Lamentations 3:8; Job 31:35–36"> Behold I cry suffering violence, and no one will hear: I shall cry aloud, and there is none to judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 3:7; Job 3:23; Proverbs 4:19"> He hath hedged in my path round about, and I cannot pass, and in my way he hath set darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:44; Job 29:7–14; Psalms 89:39"> He hath stripped me of my glory, and hath taken the crown from my head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 24:20; Psalms 37:35–36; 2 Corinthians 4:8–9"> He hath destroyed me on every side, and I am lost, and he hath taken away my hope, as from a tree that is plucked up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:24; Job 16:9; Job 33:10"> His wrath is kindled against me, and he hath counted me as his enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 30:12; Job 16:13; Isaiah 51:23"> His troops have come together, and have made themselves a way by me, and have besieged my tabernacle round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 88:8; Psalms 88:18; Psalms 69:8"> He hath put my brethren far from me, and my acquaintance like strangers have departed from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 38:11; Jeremiah 20:10; 2 Samuel 16:23"> My kinsmen have forsaken me, and they that knew me, have forgotten me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 31:31–32; Psalms 123:3"> They that dwelt in my house, and my maidservants have counted me a stranger, and I have been like an alien in their eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 1:15–17; Job 1:19"> I called my servant, and he gave me no answer, I entreated him with my own mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 2:9–10; Job 17:1"> My wife hath abhorred my breath, and I entreated the children of my womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 2:23; Job 30:12; Job 30:1"> Even fools despise me; and when I gone from them, they spoke against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; Psalms 109:4–5; Psalms 55:12–14"> They that were sometime my counsellors, have abhorred me: and he whom I love most is turned against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 102:5; Lamentations 4:8; Job 33:19–22"> The flesh being consumed. My bone hath cleaved to my skin, and nothing but lips are left about my teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 1:11; Psalms 38:2; Job 2:5"> Have pity on me, have pity on me, at least you my friends, because the hand of the Lord hath touched me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 69:26; Job 31:31; Job 16:13–14"> Why do you persecute me as God, and glut yourselves with my flesh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 30:8; Job 31:35; Isaiah 8:1"> Who will grant me that my words may be written? Who will grant me that they may be marked down in a book?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:1; Exodus 28:21; Exodus 32:16"> With an iron pen and in a plate of lead, or else be graven with an instrument in flint stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ephesians 1:7; Isaiah 54:5; Psalms 78:35"> For I know that my Redeemer liveth, and in the last day I shall rise out of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 5:8; 1 John 3:2; Psalms 17:15"> And I shall be clothed again with my skin, and in my flesh I will see my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 73:26; Isaiah 26:19; Numbers 24:17"> Whom I myself shall see, and my eyes shall behold, and not another: this my hope is laid up in my bosom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 19:22; Psalms 69:26; 1 Kings 14:13"> Why then do you say now: Let us persecute him, and let us find occasion of word against him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:14; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Psalms 58:10–11"> Flee then from the face of the sword, for the sword is the revenger of iniquities: and know ye that there is judgment.</span>
