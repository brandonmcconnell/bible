<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 12:4; Psalms 35:15–16; Psalms 69:12"> But now the younger in time scorn me, whose fathers I would not have set with the dogs of my flock:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> The strength of whose hands was to me as nothing, and they were thought unworthy of life itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 24:5; Hebrews 11:38; Job 24:13–16"> Barren with want and hunger, who gnawed in the wilderness, disfigured with calamity and misery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 15:16; 2 Kings 4:38–39; Amos 7:14"> And they ate grass, and barks of trees, and the root of junipers was their food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 4:25; Daniel 4:32–33; Psalms 109:10"> Who snatched up these things out of the valleys, and when they had found any of them, they ran to them with a cry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:1–2; Judges 6:2; Isaiah 2:19"> They dwelt in the desert places of torrents, and in caves of earth, or upon the gravel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 6:5; Job 11:12; Genesis 16:12"> They pleased themselves among these kind of things, and counted it delightful to be under the briers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 16:22; Job 40:4; Isaiah 32:6"> The children of foolish and base men, and not appearing at all upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Lamentations 3:63; Job 17:6; Lamentations 3:14"> Now I am turned into their song, and am become their byword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 26:67; Isaiah 50:6; Numbers 12:14"> They abhor me, and flee far from me, and are not afraid to spit in my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 12:21; Ruth 1:21; Job 12:18"> For he hath opened his quiver, and hath afflicted me, and hath put a bridle into my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 19:12; Isaiah 3:5; Job 19:18"> At the right hand of my rising, my calamities forthwith arose: they have overthrown my feet, and have overwhelmed me with their paths as with waves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 3:12; Zechariah 1:15; Psalms 69:26"> They have destroyed my ways, they have lain in wait against me, and they have prevailed, and there was none to help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:16; Psalms 18:4; Psalms 69:14–15"> They have rushed in upon me, as when a wall is broken, and a gate opened, and have rolled themselves down to my miseries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 13:3; Job 6:4; Isaiah 44:22"> I am brought to nothing: as a wind thou hast taken away my desire: and my prosperity hath passed away like a cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 42:4; Psalms 22:14; Isaiah 53:12"> And now my soul fadeth within myself, and the days of affliction possess me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 7:4; Job 33:19–21; Isaiah 38:13"> In the night my bone is pierced with sorrows: and they that feed upon me, do not sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 7:5; Job 19:20; Job 2:7"> With the multitude of them my garment is consumed, and they have girded me about, as with the collar of my coat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 9:31; Job 42:6; Genesis 18:27"> I am compared to dirt, and am likened to embers and ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 19:7; Psalms 80:4–5; Psalms 22:2"> I cry to thee, and thou hearest me not: I stand up, and thou dost not regard me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 10:14–17; Job 16:9–14; Job 19:6–9"> Thou art changed to be cruel toward me, and in the hardness of thy hand thou art against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 27:21; Psalms 1:4; Hosea 4:19"> Thou hast lifted me up, and set me as it were upon the wind, and thou hast mightily dashed me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:5–7; Hebrews 9:27; Ecclesiastes 9:5"> I know that thou wilt deliver me to death, where a house is appointed for every one that liveth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Judges 5:31; Matthew 27:39–44; Job 19:7"> But yet thou stretchest not forth thy hand to their consumption: and if they shall fall down thou wilt save.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Psalms 35:13–14; Jeremiah 13:17"> I wept heretofore for him that was afflicted, and my soul had compassion on the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:15; Job 3:25–26; Jeremiah 14:19"> I expected good things, and evils are come upon me: I waited for light, and darkness broke out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Lamentations 2:11; Jeremiah 31:20; Jeremiah 4:19"> My inner parts have boiled without any rest, the days of affliction have prevented me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 42:9; Psalms 38:6; Psalms 43:2"> I went mourning without indignation; I rose up, and cried in the crowd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Micah 1:8; Psalms 102:6; Psalms 44:19"> I was the brother of dragons, and companion of ostriches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Lamentations 4:8; Psalms 102:3; Psalms 119:83"> My skin is become black upon me, and my bones are dried up with heat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Lamentations 5:15; Isaiah 21:4; Isaiah 22:12"> My harp is turned to mourning, and my organ into the voice of those that weep.</span>
