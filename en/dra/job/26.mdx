<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Job answered, and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3–4; Job 4:3–4; Isaiah 41:5–7"> Whose helper art thou? is it of him that is weak? and dost thou hold up the arm of him that has no strength?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 71:15–18; Job 32:11–13; Job 33:3"> To whom hast thou given counsel? perhaps to him that hath no wisdom, and thou hast shewn thy very great prudence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 22:23–24; Job 32:18; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> Whom hast thou desired to teach? was it not him that made life?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 88:10; Genesis 6:4; Job 41:1–34"> Behold the giants groan under the waters, and they that dwell with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 139:8; Proverbs 15:11; Hebrews 4:13"> Hell is naked before him, and there is no covering for destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 9:8; Psalms 104:2–5; Isaiah 40:22"> He stretched out the north over the empty space, and hangeth the earth upon nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 30:4; Jeremiah 10:13; Isaiah 5:6"> He bindeth up the waters in his clouds, so that they break not out and fall down together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; Job 22:14; Habakkuk 3:3–5"> He withholdeth the face of his throne, and spreadeth his cloud over it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 8:29; Job 38:8–11; Jeremiah 5:22"> He hath set bounds about the waters, till light and darkness come to an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:8; Psalms 18:7; Job 15:15"> The pillars of heaven tremble, and dread at his beck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 51:15; Jeremiah 31:35; Psalms 89:9–10"> By his power the seas are suddenly gathered together, and his wisdom has struck the proud one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 27:1; Psalms 104:30; Psalms 74:13–14"> His spirit hath adorned the heavens, and his obstetric hand brought forth the winding serpent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:26–29; Psalms 145:3; Romans 11:33"> Lo, these things are said in part of his ways: and seeing we have heard scarce a little drop of his word, who shall be able to behold the thunder of his greatness?</span>
