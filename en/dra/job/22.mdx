<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Eliphaz the Themanite answered, and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 17:10; Galatians 6:7–8; Job 35:6–8"> Can man be compared with God, even though he were of perfect knowledge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 15:8; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Psalms 147:10–11"> What doth it profit God if thou be just? or what dost thou give him if thy way be unspotted?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 14:3; Psalms 143:2; Revelation 3:19"> Shall he reprove thee for fear, and come with thee into judgment:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 40:12; Job 15:5–6; Job 15:31–34"> And not for thy manifold wickedness, and thy infinite iniquities?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 22:26; Ezekiel 18:12; Ezekiel 18:16"> For thou hast taken away the pledge of thy brethren without cause, and stripped the naked of their clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 31:17; Matthew 25:42; Isaiah 58:7"> Thou hast not given water to the weary, thou hast withdrawn bread from the hungry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 9:15; Job 29:7–17; Job 13:8"> In the strength of thy arm thou didst possess the land, and being the most mighty thou holdest it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 24:21; Job 24:3; Ezekiel 22:7"> Thou hast sent widows away empty, and the arms of the fatherless thou hast broken in pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 18:8–10; Job 13:21; Job 6:4"> Therefore art thou surrounded with snares, and sudden fear troubleth thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 69:1–2; Lamentations 3:54; Jonah 2:3"> And didst thou think that thou shouldst not see darkness, and that thou shouldst not be covered with the violence of overflowing waters?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 11:8; Isaiah 66:1; Psalms 8:3–4"> Dost not thou think that God is higher than heaven, and is elevated above the height of the stars?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:12; Psalms 10:11; Ezekiel 9:9"> And thou sayst: What doth God know? and he judgeth as it were through a mist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; Psalms 139:11–12; Psalms 33:14"> The clouds are his covert, and he doth not consider our things, and he walketh about the poles of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 17:26–27; Genesis 6:5; Genesis 6:11–13"> Dost thou desire to keep the path of ages, which wicked men have trodden?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 15:32; Genesis 7:17–24; Psalms 55:23"> Who were taken away before their time, and a flood hath overthrown their foundation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 21:14–15; Romans 1:28; Psalms 4:6"> Who said to God: Depart from us: and looked upon the Almighty as if he could do nothing:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 21:16; Job 12:6; Acts 14:17"> Whereas he had filled their houses with good things: whose way of thinking be far from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 58:10; Psalms 107:42; Psalms 52:6"> The just shall see, and shall rejoice, and the innocent shall laugh them to scorn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 2:6–7; Job 1:16; Job 20:26"> Is not their exaltation cut down, and hath not fire devoured the remnants of them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:9; Isaiah 27:5; Philippians 4:7"> Submit thyself then to him, and be at peace: and thereby thou shalt have the best fruits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; Job 23:12; Deuteronomy 4:1–2"> Receive the law of his mouth, and lay up his words in thy heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 11:13–14; Zechariah 1:3; Job 8:5–6"> If thou wilt return to the Almighty, thou shalt be built up, and shalt put away iniquity far from thy tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 13:12; 1 Kings 9:28; Genesis 10:29"> He shall give for earth flint, and for flint torrents of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 33:6; Psalms 16:5–6; Genesis 15:1"> And the Almighty shall be against thy enemies, and silver shall be heaped together for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; Isaiah 58:14; Job 27:10"> Then shalt thou abound in delights in the Almighty, and shalt lift up thy face to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 58:9; Psalms 116:1; 1 John 5:14–15"> Thou shalt pray to him, and he will hear thee, and thou shalt pay vows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; Psalms 90:17; Psalms 20:4"> Thou shalt decree a thing, and it I shall come to thee, and light shall shine in thy ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="James 4:6; Job 5:19–27; Luke 1:52"> For he that hath been humbled, shall be in glory: and he that shall bow down his eyes, he shall be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 42:7–8; James 5:15–16; Genesis 18:26–32"> The innocent shall be saved, and he shall be saved by the cleanness of his hands.</span>
