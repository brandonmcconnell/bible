<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3; Hebrews 12:12; Proverbs 10:21"> Behold thou hast taught many, and thou hast strengthened the weary hands:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 12:18; Psalms 145:14; Hebrews 12:12"> Thy words have confirmed them that were staggering, and thou hast strengthened the trembling knees:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 19:21; 2 Corinthians 4:16; Job 2:5"> But now the scourge is come upon thee, and thou faintest: it hath touched thee, and thou art troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 3:26; Proverbs 14:26; Job 1:1"> Where is thy fear, thy fortitude, thy patience, and the perfection of thy ways?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 37:25; 2 Peter 2:9; Job 36:7"> Remember, I pray thee, who ever perished being innocent? or when were the just destroyed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 6:7–8; Proverbs 22:8; Hosea 8:7"> On the contrary I have seen those who work iniquity, and sow sorrows, and reap them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 15:30; Isaiah 11:4; Isaiah 30:33"> Perishing by the blast of God, and consumed by the spirit of his wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 58:6; Proverbs 30:14; Psalms 3:7"> The roaring of the lion, and the voice of the lioness, and the teeth of the whelps of lions are broken:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 34:10; Job 8:3–4; Job 1:19"> The tiger hath perished for want of prey, and the young lions are scattered abroad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 26:14; Psalms 62:11; 1 Corinthians 13:12"> Now there was a word spoken to me in private, and my ears by stealth as it were received the veins of its whisper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 33:14–16; Genesis 2:21; Genesis 31:24"> In the horror of a vision by night, when deep sleep is wont to hold men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Job 33:19; Luke 1:12"> Fear seized upon me, and trembling, and all my bones were affrighted:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 1:7; Daniel 5:6; Psalms 104:4"> And when a spirit passed before me, the hair of my flesh stood up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 19:12"> There stood one whose countenance I knew not, an image before my eyes, and I heard the voice as it were of a gentle wind:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 9:2; Job 25:4; Ecclesiastes 7:20"> Shall man be justified in comparison of God, or shall a man be more pure than his maker?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 25:5–6; 2 Peter 2:4; Jude 1:6"> Behold they that serve him are not steadfast, and in his angels he found wickedness:</span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 20:7; Job 14:14; Job 16:22"> From morning till evening they shall be cut down: and because no one understandeth, they shall perish for ever.</span>
