<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Job answered, and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 2:1; Job 34:2; Job 15:11"> Hear, I beseech you, my words, and do penance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 17:2; Job 16:10; Job 16:20"> Suffer me, and I will speak, and after, if you please, laugh at my words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 102:1; Psalms 42:11; Psalms 142:2–3"> Is my debate against man, that I should not have just reason to be troubled?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 29:9; Job 40:4; Judges 18:19"> Hearken to me and be astonished, and lay your finger on your mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:16; Psalms 119:120; Psalms 88:15"> As for me, when I remember, I am afraid, and trembling taketh hold on my flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 73:3–12; Job 12:6; Jeremiah 12:1–3"> Why then do the wicked live, are they advanced, and strengthened with riches?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 17:6; Job 5:3–4; Job 20:10"> Their seed continueth before them, a multitude of kinsmen, and of children’s children in their sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 9:34; Psalms 73:5; Psalms 73:19"> Their houses are secure and peaceable, and the rod of God is not upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 23:26; Luke 16:19; Deuteronomy 7:13–14"> Their cattle have conceived, and failed not: their cow has calved, and is not deprived of her fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 107:41; Psalms 127:3–5"> Their little ones go out like a flock, and their children dance and play.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 6:4–6; Isaiah 5:12; Genesis 4:21"> They take the timbrel, and the harp, and rejoice at the sound of the organ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 36:11; Luke 12:19–20; Psalms 73:4"> They spend their days in wealth, and in a moment they go down to hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:17; Romans 8:7; 2 Thessalonians 2:10–12"> Who have said to God: Depart from us, we desire not the knowledge of thy ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 5:2; Job 34:9; Malachi 1:13–14"> Who is the Almighty, that we should serve him? and what doth it profit us if we pray to him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 1:1; Job 22:18; Genesis 49:6"> Yet because their good things are not in their hand, may the counsel of the wicked be far from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 18:5–6; Proverbs 20:20; Proverbs 13:9"> How often shall the lamp of the wicked be put out, and a deluge come upon them, and he shall distribute the sorrows of his wrath?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 1:4; Psalms 83:13; Job 13:25"> They shall be as chaff before the face of the wind, and as ashes which the whirlwind scattereth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; Ezekiel 18:19–20; Ezekiel 18:2"> God shall lay up the sorrow of the father for his children: and when he shall repay, then shall he know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 14:10; Isaiah 51:17; Psalms 75:8"> His eyes shall see his own destruction, and he shall drink of the wrath of the Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 14:5; Ecclesiastes 2:18–19; Psalms 55:23"> For what is it to him what befalleth his house after him: and if the number of his months be diminished by one half?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 11:34; 1 Corinthians 2:16; Isaiah 40:13–14"> Shall any one teach God knowledge, who judgeth those that are high?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 20:22–23; Luke 12:19–21; Psalms 73:4–5"> One man dieth strong, and hale, rich and happy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 3:8; Psalms 17:10; Job 15:27"> His bowels are full of fat, and his bones are moistened with marrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:16–17; Job 7:11; Proverbs 14:10"> But another dieth in bitterness of soul without any riches:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:2; Isaiah 14:11; Job 20:11"> And yet they shall sleep together in the dust, and worms shall cover them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 5:22; Psalms 119:86; Psalms 59:4"> Surely I know your thoughts, and your unjust judgments against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 8:22; Job 20:7; Habakkuk 2:9–11"> For you say: Where is the house of the prince? and where are the dwelling places of the wicked?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 129:8"> Ask any one of them that go by the way, and you shall perceive that he knoweth these same things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 16:4; 2 Peter 2:9–17; 2 Peter 3:7"> Because the wicked man is reserved to the day of destruction, and he shall be brought to the day of wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Galatians 2:11; Deuteronomy 7:10; Romans 12:19"> Who shall reprove his way to his face? and who shall repay him what he hath done?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 16:22; Psalms 49:14; Ezekiel 32:21–32"> He shall be brought to the graves, and shall watch in the heap of the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 9:27; Job 24:24; Job 30:23"> He hath been acceptable to the gravel of Cocytus, and he shall draw every man after him, and there are innumerable before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 16:2; Job 32:3; Job 13:4"> How then do ye comfort me in vain, whereas your answer is shewn to be repugnant to truth?</span>
