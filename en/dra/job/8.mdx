<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 2:11"> The Baldad the Suhite answered, and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 6:26; Job 15:2; 1 Kings 19:11"> How long wilt thou speak these things, and how long shall the words of thy mouth be like a strong wind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; 2 Chronicles 19:7; Genesis 18:25"> Doth God pervert judgment, or doth the Almighty overthrow that which is just?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 1:5; Job 1:18–19; Job 5:4"> Although thy children have sinned against him, and he hath left them in the hand of their iniquity:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 4:7–10; Hebrews 3:7–8; Job 11:13"> Yet if thou wilt arise early to God, and wilt beseech the Almighty:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 51:9; Isaiah 3:10; Proverbs 15:8"> If thou wilt walk clean and upright, he will presently awake onto thee, and will make the dwelling of thy justice peaceable:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 42:12–13; Matthew 13:31–32; Proverbs 19:20"> Insomuch, that if thy former things were small, thy latter things would be multiplied exceedingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:7; Deuteronomy 4:32; Job 15:18"> For inquire of the former generation, and search diligently into the memory of the fathers:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:15; Psalms 144:4; Job 14:2"> (For we are but of yesterday, and are ignorant that our days upon earth are but a shadow:)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 18:15; Matthew 12:35; Job 12:7–8"> And they shall teach thee: they shall speak to thee, and utter words out of their hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 2:3; Isaiah 19:5–7"> Can the rush be green without moisture? or a sedge-bush grow without water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:6; Psalms 129:6–7; Matthew 13:20"> When it is yet in flower, and is not plucked up with the hand, it withereth before all herbs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 9:17; Job 11:20; Job 13:16"> Even so are the ways of all that forget God, and the hope of the hypocrite shall perish:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 59:5–6"> His folly shall not please him, and his trust shall be like the spider’s web.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 27:18; Luke 6:47–49; Matthew 7:24–27"> He shall lean upon his house, and it shall not stand: he shall prop it up, and it shall not rise:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 80:11; Job 21:7–15; Job 5:3"> He seemeth to have moisture before the sun cometh, and at his rising his blossom shall shoot forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 5:24; Mark 11:20; Jude 1:12"> His roots shall be thick upon a heap of stones, and among the stones he shall abide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 7:10; Psalms 37:36; Job 7:8"> If one swallow him up out of his place, he shall deny him, and shall say: I know thee not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 20:5; Psalms 113:7; 1 Samuel 2:8"> For this is the joy of his way, that others may spring again out of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 4:7; Psalms 37:37; Psalms 37:24"> God will not cast away the simple, nor reach out his hand to the evildoer:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 126:2; Genesis 21:6; Psalms 126:6"> Until thy mouth be filled with laughter, and thy lips with rejoicing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 132:18; Psalms 109:29; Psalms 35:26"> They that hate thee, shall be clothed with confusion: and the dwelling of the wicked shall not stand.</span>
