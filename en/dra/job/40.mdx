<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 30:32; Job 29:9; Job 21:5"> And hast thou an arm like God, and canst thou thunder with a voice like him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 33:14; Job 9:3; Job 9:15"> Clothe thyself with beauty, and set thyself up on high and be glorious, and put on goodly garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 38:1; 2 Peter 3:10–12; Psalms 50:3–4"> Scatter the proud in thy indignation, and behold every arrogant man, and humble him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 38:3; Job 42:4; Job 23:3–4"> Look on all that are proud, and confound them, and crush the wicked in their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:4; Isaiah 28:18; Job 32:2"> Hide them in the dust together, and plunge their faces into the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:13; Job 37:4–5; Isaiah 45:9"> Then I will confess that thy right hand is able to save thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 93:1; Psalms 149:4; Isaiah 59:17"> Behold behemoth whom I made with thee, he eateth grass like an ox.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 4:37; Isaiah 2:11–12; Isaiah 2:17"> His strength is in his loins, and his force in the navel of his belly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 63:3; Malachi 4:3; Psalms 60:12"> He setteth up his tail like a cedar, the sinews of his testicles are wrapped together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 2:10; Esther 7:8; Job 14:13"> His bones are like pipes of brass, his gristle like plates of iron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4–9; Romans 5:6; Psalms 44:6"> He is the beginning of the ways of God, who made him, he will apply his sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 40:20; Psalms 104:14; Genesis 1:24–26"> To him the mountains bring forth grass: there all the beasts of the field shall play.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> He sleepeth under the shadow, in the covert of the reed, and in moist places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 41:23"> The shades cover his shadow, the willows of the brook shall compass him about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 48:4; Job 7:12"> Behold, he will drink up a river, and not wonder: and he trusteth that the Jordan may run into his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 41:33; Job 26:13; Isaiah 27:1"> In his eyes as with a hook he shall take him, and bore through his nostrils with stakes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 104:26; Psalms 104:14; Job 40:15"> Canst thou draw out the leviathan with a hook, or canst thou tie his tongue with a cord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 35:7; Isaiah 19:6–7"> Canst thou put a ring in his nose, or bore through his jaw with a buckle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:5; Leviticus 23:40; Isaiah 15:7"> Will he make many supplications to thee, or speak soft words to thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 13:10; Isaiah 37:25; Isaiah 28:16"> Will he make a covenant with thee, and wilt thou take him to be a servant for ever?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 41:1–2"> Shalt thou play with him as with a bird, or tie him up for thy handmaids?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Shall friends cut him in pieces, shall merchants divide him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Wilt thou fill nets with his skin, and the cabins of fishes with his head?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Lay thy hand upon him: remember the battle, and speak no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Behold his hope shall fail him, and in the sight of all he shall be cast down.</span>
