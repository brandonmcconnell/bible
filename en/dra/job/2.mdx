<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 1:6; Hebrews 1:14; Luke 1:19"> And it came to pass, when on a certain day the sons of God came, and stood before the Lord, and Satan came among them, and stood in his sight,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Job 1:7; Genesis 16:8"> That the Lord said to Satan: Whence comest thou? And he answered and said: I have gone round about the earth, and walked through it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 1:12; Job 1:8; Job 27:5–6"> And the Lord said to Satan: Hast thou considered my servant Job, that there is none like him in the earth, a man simple, and upright, and fearing God, and avoiding evil, and still keeping his innocence? But thou hast moved me against him, that I should afflict him without cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Philippians 3:8–10; Matthew 16:26; Matthew 6:25"> And Satan answered, and said: Skin for skin, and all that a man hath he will give for his life:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 1:11; Leviticus 24:15; Job 19:20–21"> But put forth thy hand, and touch his bone and his flesh, and then thou shalt gee that he will bless thee to thy face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; Luke 22:31–32; Job 1:12"> And the Lord said to Satan: Behold be is in thy hand, but yet save his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:35; Job 30:30; Isaiah 1:6"> So Satan went forth from the presence Of the Lord, and struck Job with a very grievous ulcer, from the sole of the foot even to the top of his head:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 42:6; Ezekiel 27:30; Jonah 3:6"> And he took a potsherd and scraped the corrupt matter, sitting on a dunghill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 2:5; Job 2:3; Malachi 3:14"> And his wife said to him: Dost thou still continue in thy simplicity? bless God and die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 1:12; Lamentations 3:38–41; Job 1:21–22"> And he said to her: Thou hast; spoken like one of the foolish women: if we have received good things at the hand of God, why should we not receive evil? In all these things Job did not sin with his lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Job 42:11; Jeremiah 49:7"> Now when Job’s three friends heard all the evil that had befallen him, they came every one from his own place, Alphas the Themanite, and Baldad the Suhite, and Sophar the Naamathite. For they had made an appointment to come together and visit him, and comfort him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Lamentations 2:10; Ezekiel 27:30; Nehemiah 9:1"> And when they had lifted up their eyes afar off, they knew him not, and crying out they wept, and rending their garments they sprinkled dust upon their heads towards heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 50:10; Nehemiah 1:4; Ezekiel 3:15"> And they sat with him on the ground seven days and seven nights, and no man spoke to him a word: for they saw that his grief was very great.</span>
