<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Job answered, and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 10:1; Psalms 32:4; Job 7:11"> Now also my words are in bitterness, and the hand of my scourge is more grievous than my mourning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 16:21; Jeremiah 14:7; Job 13:3"> Who will grant me that I might know and find him, and come even to his throne?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 13:18; Daniel 9:18–19; Exodus 32:12–13"> I would set judgment before him, and would fill my mouth with complaints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 13:22–23; Job 10:2; 1 Corinthians 4:3–4"> That I might know the words that he would answer me, and understand what he would say to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 13:21; Job 9:33–34; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> I would not that he should contend with me with much strength, nor overwhelm me with the weight of his greatness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 13:3; Romans 8:1; Isaiah 1:18"> Let him propose equity against me, and let my judgment come to victory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 10:1; Job 9:11; Job 35:14"> But if I go to the east, he appeareth not; if to the west, I shall not understand him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:46; Isaiah 8:17"> If to the left hand, what shall I do? I shall not take hold on him: if I turn myself to the right hand, I shall not see him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 1:12; James 1:2–4; 1 Peter 1:7"> But he knoweth my way, and has tried me as gold that passeth through the fire:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 44:18; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Job 17:9"> My foot hath followed his steps, I have kept his way, and have not declined from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:16; Psalms 119:103; Psalms 119:11"> I have not departed from the commandments of his lips, and the words of his mouth I have hid in my bosom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 115:3; Proverbs 19:21; Isaiah 46:10"> For he is alone, and no man can turn away his thought: and whatsoever is soul hath desired, that hath he done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:9; Romans 11:33"> And when he shall have fulfilled his will in me, many other like things are also at hand with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; Psalms 77:3; Job 31:23"> And therefore I am troubled at his presence, and when I consider him I am made pensive with fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 22:14; Job 27:2; Psalms 88:16"> God hath softened my heart, and the Almighty hath troubled me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 19:8; Isaiah 57:1; 2 Kings 22:20"> For I have not perished because of the darkness that hangs over me, neither hath the mist covered my face.</span>
