<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 34:5; Numbers 14:21; 2 Kings 4:27"> As God liveth, who hath taken away my judgment, and the Almighty, who hath brought my soul to bitterness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 2:7; Job 33:4; Job 32:8"> As long as breath remaineth in me, and the spirit of God in my nostrils,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 13:7; 2 Corinthians 11:10; John 8:55"> My lips shall not speak iniquity, neither shall my tongue contrive lying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 2:9; Job 13:15; Job 42:7"> God forbid that I should judge you to be just: till I die I will not depart from my innocence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 2:3; 1 John 3:20–21; Acts 24:16"> My justification, which I have begun to hold, I will not forsake: for my heart doth not reprehend me in all my life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 4:19; 1 Samuel 25:26; 2 Samuel 18:32"> Let my enemy be as the ungodly, and my adversary as the wicked one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; Job 8:13; Job 11:20"> For what is the hope of the hypocrite if through covetousness he take by violence, and God deliver not his soul?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 1:15; Proverbs 1:28; Jeremiah 14:12"> Will God hear his cry, when distress shall come upon him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 22:26–27; Psalms 37:4; Habakkuk 3:18"> Or can he delight himself in the Almighty, and call upon God at all times?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 71:17; Isaiah 8:11; Job 4:3–4"> I will teach you by the hand of God, what the Almighty hath, and I will not conceal it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 21:3; Job 16:3; Job 19:2–3"> Behold you all know it, and why do you speak vain things without cause?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 20:19–29; Job 15:20–35; Isaiah 3:11"> This is the portion of a wicked man with God, and the inheritance of the violent, which they shall receive of the Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:41; Job 20:10; Luke 23:29"> If his sons be multiplied, they shall be for the sword, and his grandsons shall not be filled with bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 78:64; Jeremiah 22:18; 1 Kings 14:10–11"> They that shall remain of him, shall be buried in death, and his widows shall not weep.</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 13:22; Ecclesiastes 2:26; Proverbs 28:8"> He shall prepare indeed, but the just man shall be clothed with it: and the innocent shall divide the silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 1:8; Job 8:14–15; Isaiah 51:8"> He hath built his house as a moth, and as a keeper he hath made a booth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 24:24; Psalms 58:9; Psalms 73:19–20"> The rich man when he shall sleep shall take away nothing with him: he shall open his eyes and find nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 15:21; Job 18:11; Job 21:18"> Poverty like water shall take hold on him, a tempest shall oppress him in the night.</span>
