<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 10:7; Job 33:9; Job 10:2"> So these three men ceased to answer Job, because he seemed just to himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 22:21; Job 40:8; Job 27:2"> And Eliu the son of Barachel the Buzite, of the kindred of Ram, was angry and was moved to indignation: now he was angry against Job, because he said he was just before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 8:6; Job 24:25; Job 22:5–30"> And he was angry with his friends because they had not found a reasonable answer, but only had condemned Job.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 32:11–12; Proverbs 18:13"> So Eliu waited while Job was speaking, because they were his elders that were speaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 32:2; Exodus 32:19"> But when he saw that the three were not able to answer, he was exceedingly angry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 15:10; 1 Timothy 5:1; Titus 2:6"> Then Eliu the son of Barachel the Buzite answered and said: I am younger in days, and you are more ancient; therefore hanging down my head, I was afraid to shew you my opinion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 16:31; Job 12:12; Hebrews 5:12"> For I hoped that greater age would speak, and that a multitude of years would teach wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 38:36; Job 33:4; 2 Timothy 3:16"> But, as I see, there is a spirit in men, and the inspiration of the Almighty giveth understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 11:25; Job 12:20; Ecclesiastes 4:13"> They that are aged are not the wise men, neither do the ancients understand judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:25; 1 Corinthians 7:40"> Therefore I will speak: Hearken to me, I also will shew you my wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 29:21; Job 5:27; Proverbs 28:11"> For I have waited for your words, I have given ear to your wisdom, as long as you were disputing in words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 32:3; 1 Timothy 1:7"> And as long as I thought you said some thing, I considered: but, as I see, there is none of you that can convince Job, and answer his words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23; 1 Corinthians 1:27–29; 1 Corinthians 3:18"> Lest you should say: We have found wisdom, God hath cast him down, not man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> He hath spoken nothing to me, and I will not answer him according to your words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 6:24–25; Matthew 22:46; Job 29:22"> They were afraid, and answered no more, and they left off speaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 1:19; Job 13:5; Amos 5:13"> Therefore because I have waited, and they have not spoken: they stood, and answered no more:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 35:3–4; Job 33:12; Job 32:10"> I also will answer my part, and will shew my knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 39:3; Acts 4:20; 2 Corinthians 5:13–14"> For I am full of matter to speak of, and the spirit of my bowels straiteneth me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 9:17"> Behold, my belly is as new wine which wanteth vent, which bursteth the new vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 8:6–7; Job 21:3; Job 13:13"> I will speak and take breath a little: I will open my lips, and will answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 19:15; Job 13:8; Matthew 22:16"> I will not accept the person of man, and I will not level God with man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 12:2–3; 1 Thessalonians 2:5; Proverbs 29:5"> For I know not how long I shall continue, and whether after a while my Maker may take me away.</span>
