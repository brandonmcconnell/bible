<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 38:2; Job 30:15; Psalms 88:15–16"> For the arrows of the Lord are in me, the rage whereof drinketh up my spirit, and the terrors of the Lord war against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joel 1:18–20; Jeremiah 14:6; Psalms 42:1"> Will the wild ass bray when he hath grass? or will the ox low when he standeth before a full manger?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 34:3; Luke 14:34; Colossians 4:6"> Or can an unsavoury thing be eaten, that is not seasoned with salt? or can a man taste that which when tasted bringeth death?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 3:24; Ezekiel 4:16; Daniel 10:3"> The things which before my soul would not touch, now, through anguish are my meats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 119:81; Job 17:14–16; Job 6:11–13"> Who will grant that my request may come: and that God may give me what I look for?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 19:4; Jonah 4:3; Jonah 4:8"> And that he that hath begun may destroy me, that he may let loose his hand, and cut me off?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 23:12; Leviticus 19:2; Isaiah 57:15"> And that this may be my comfort, that afflicting me with sorrow, he spare not, nor I contradict the words of the Holy One.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 103:14–16; Psalms 102:23; Psalms 39:5"> For what is my strength, that I can hold out? or what is my end that I should keep patience?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 41:24; Job 40:18"> My strength is not the strength of stones, nor is my flesh of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 26:2; 2 Corinthians 1:12; Job 19:28"> Behold there is no help for me in myself, and my familiar friends also are departed from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 17:17; 1 Corinthians 12:26; Galatians 6:2"> He that taketh away mercy from his friend, forsaketh the fear of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 38:11; Jeremiah 15:18; Psalms 41:9"> My brethren have passed by me, as the torrent that passeth swiftly in the valleys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> They that fear the hoary frost, the snow shall fall upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; Job 24:19"> At the time when they shall be scattered they shall perish: and after it groweth hot they shall be melted out of their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The paths of their steps are entangled: they shall walk in vain, and shall perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 21:14; Genesis 25:15; Jeremiah 25:23"> Consider the paths of Thema, the ways of Saba, and wait a little while.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:3–4; Romans 5:5; Romans 9:33"> They are confounded, because I have hoped: they are come also even unto me, and are covered with shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 38:11; 2 Timothy 4:16; Jeremiah 17:5–6"> Now you are come: and now seeing my affliction you are afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 20:33; Job 42:11; 1 Samuel 12:3"> Did I say: Bring to me, and give me of your substance?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 49:15; Psalms 49:7–8; Psalms 107:2"> Or deliver me from the hand of the enemy, and rescue me out of the hand of the mighty?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="James 3:2; Proverbs 9:9; Psalms 39:1–2"> Teach me, and I will hold my peace: and if I have been ignorant in any thing, instruct me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 32:3; Job 13:5; Proverbs 25:11"> Why have you detracted the words of truth, whereas there is none of you that can reprove me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 8:2; Ephesians 4:14; Job 42:3"> You dress up speeches only to rebuke, and you utter words to the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 24:9; Job 24:3; 2 Peter 2:3"> You rush in upon the fatherless, and you endeavour to overthrow your friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 13:4; Job 33:3; Job 27:4"> However finish what you have begun, give ear, and see whether I lie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 17:10; Job 34:5; Job 27:4–6"> Answer, I beseech you, without contention: and speaking that which is just, judge ye.</span>
