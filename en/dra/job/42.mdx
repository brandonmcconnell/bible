<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Job answered the Lord, and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 19:26; Mark 10:27; Genesis 18:14"> I know that thou canst do all things, and no thought is hid from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 131:1; Psalms 139:6; Job 38:2"> Who is this that hideth counsel without knowledge? Therefore I have spoken unwisely, and things that above measure exceeded my knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 40:7; Job 38:3; Genesis 18:30–32"> Hear, and I will speak: I will ask thee, and do thou tell me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 1:17–18; Romans 10:17; John 12:45"> With the hearing of the ear, I have heard thee, but now my eye seeth thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra 9:6; 1 Timothy 1:13–16; James 4:7–10"> Therefore I reprehend myself, and do penance in dust and ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 11:5–6; Job 32:2–3; Job 32:5"> And after the Lord had spoken these words to Job, he said to Eliphaz the Themanite: My wrath is kindled against thee, and against thy two friends, because you have not spoken the thing that is right before my, as my servant Job hath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:1; James 5:16; Psalms 103:10"> Take unto you therefore seven oxen, and seven rams, and go to my servant Job, and offer for yourselves a holocaust: and my servant Job shall pray for you: his face I will accept, that folly be not imputed to you: for you have not spoken right things before me, as my servant Job hath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 11:8; Acts 9:6; Acts 10:33"> So Eliphaz the Themanite, and Baldad the Suhite, and Sophar the Naamathite went, and did as the Lord had spoken to them, and the Lord accepted the face of Job.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:3; Job 8:6–7; Proverbs 22:4"> The Lord also was turned at the penance of Job, when he prayed for his friends. And the Lord gave Job twice as much as he had before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 19:13–14; Proverbs 16:7"> And all his brethren came to him, and all his sisters, and all that knew him before, and they ate bread with him in his house: and bemoaned him, and comforted him upon all the evil that God had brought upon him. And every man gave him one ewe, and one earring of fold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 8:7; Ecclesiastes 7:8; Proverbs 10:22"> And the Lord blessed the latter end of Job more than his beginning. And he had fourteen thousand sheep, and six thousand camels, and a thousand yoke of oxen, and a thousand she asses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 1:2; Psalms 107:41; Psalms 127:3"> And he had seven sons, and three daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And he called the names of one Dies, and the name of the second Cassia, and the name of the third Cornustibil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 27:7; Psalms 144:12; Acts 7:20"> And there were not found in all the earth women so beautiful as the daughters of Job: and their father gave them inheritance among their brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 17:6; Psalms 128:6; Genesis 50:23"> And Job lived after these things, a hundred and forty years, and he saw his children, and his children’s children, unto the fourth generation, and he died an old man, and full of days.</span>
