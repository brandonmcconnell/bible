<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 42:7; Job 2:11; Job 22:1"> And Eliphaz the Themanite, answered, and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 6:26; Job 8:2; Hosea 12:1"> Will a wise man answer as if he were speaking in the wind, and fill his stomach with burning heat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Matthew 12:36–37; Job 13:4–5"> Thou reprovest him by words, who is not equal to thee, and thou speakest that which is not good for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 3:31; Luke 18:1; Amos 6:10"> As much as is in thee, thou hast made void fear, and hast taken away prayers from before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:3–5; James 3:5–8; Luke 6:45"> For thy iniquity hath taught thy mouth, and thou imitatest the tongue of blasphemers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 19:22; Matthew 12:37; Job 9:20"> Thy own mouth shall condemn thee, and not I: and thy own lips shall answer thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 90:2; Job 12:12; Job 38:4–41"> Art thou the first man that was born, or wast thou made before the hills?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 11:34; Jeremiah 23:18; Job 13:5–6"> Hast thou heard God’s counsel, and shall his wisdom be inferior to thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 13:2; 2 Corinthians 11:5; Job 12:3"> What knowest thou that we are ignorant of? what dost thou understand that we know not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 32:6–7; Job 8:8–10; Job 12:12"> There are with us also aged and ancient men, much elder than thy fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:2; 2 Corinthians 1:3–5; 1 Kings 22:24"> Is it a great matter that God should comfort thee? but thy wicked words hinder this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Mark 7:21–22"> Why doth thy heart elevate thee, and why dost thou stare with thy eyes, as if they were thinking great things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 15:25–27; James 3:2–6; Job 9:4"> Why doth thy spirit swell against God, to utter such words out of thy mouth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; Job 14:4; Proverbs 20:9"> What is man that he should be without spot, and he that is born of a woman that he should appear just?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 25:5; Job 4:18; Job 5:1"> Behold among his saints none is unchangeable, and the heavens are not pure in his sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 34:7; Proverbs 19:28; Romans 3:9–19"> How much more is man abominable, and unprofitable, who drinketh iniquity like water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 36:2; Job 34:2; Job 13:5–6"> I will shew thee, hear me: and I will tell thee what I have seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 8:8; Isaiah 38:19; Psalms 71:18"> Wise men confess and hide not their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joel 3:17; Genesis 10:32; Genesis 10:25"> To whom alone the earth was given, and no stranger hath passed among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 27:13; James 5:1–6; Luke 12:19–21"> The wicked man is proud all his days, and the number of the years of his tyranny is uncertain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 18:11; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Leviticus 26:36"> The sound of dread is always in his ears: and when there is peace, he always suspecteth treason.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 20:24–25; Matthew 27:5; Job 9:16"> He believeth not that he may return from darkness to light, looking round about for the sword on every side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 109:10; Psalms 59:15; Job 18:12"> When he moveth himself to seek bread, he knoweth that the day of darkness is ready at his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 24:34; Proverbs 6:11; Job 6:2–4"> Tribulation shall terrify him, and distress shall surround him, as a king that is prepared for the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 5:23; Psalms 52:7; Psalms 73:9"> For he hath stretched out his hand against God, and hath strengthened himself against the Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 18:40; Job 16:12; 2 Chronicles 32:13–17"> He hath run against him with his neck raised up, and is armed with a fat neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 17:10; Jeremiah 5:28; Psalms 73:7"> Fatness hath covered his face, and the fat hangeth down on his sides.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 3:14; Jeremiah 51:37; Micah 7:18"> He hath dwelt in desolate cities, and in desert houses that are reduced into heaps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 27:16–17; Psalms 49:16–17; James 1:11"> He shall not be enriched, neither shall his substance continue, neither shall he push his root in the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 4:9; Job 15:22; Job 18:5–6"> He shall not depart out of darkness: the flame shall dry up his branches, and he shall be taken away by the breath of his own month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 59:4; Galatians 6:3; Psalms 62:10"> He shall not believe, being vainly deceived by error, that he may be redeemed with any price.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:17; Psalms 55:23; Hosea 9:16"> Before his days be full he shall perish: and his hands shall wither away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:39–40; Isaiah 33:9; Revelation 6:13"> He shall be blasted as a vine when its grapes are in the first flower, and as an olive tree that casteth its flower.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 8:13; Job 27:8; Job 8:22"> For the congregation of the hypocrite is barren, and fire shall devour their tabernacles, who love to take bribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 7:14; Hosea 10:13; Isaiah 59:4–5"> He hath conceived sorrow, and hath brought forth iniquity, and his womb prepareth deceits.</span>
