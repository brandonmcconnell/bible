<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 5:7; Ecclesiastes 2:23; Job 25:4"> Man born of a woman, living for a short time, is filled with many miseries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 40:6–8; 1 Peter 1:24; Job 8:9"> Who cometh forth like a flower, and is destroyed, and fleeth as a shadow, and never continueth in the same state.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 144:3; Psalms 143:2; Psalms 8:4"> And dost thou think it meet to open thy eyes upon such an one, and to bring him into judgment with thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 3:6; Job 15:14; Ephesians 2:3"> Who can make him clean that is conceived of unclean seed? is it not thou who only art?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 39:4; Acts 17:26; Hebrews 9:27"> The days of man are short, and the number of his months is with thee: thou hast appointed his bounds which cannot be passed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 7:19; Psalms 39:13; Job 7:1–2"> Depart a little from him, that he may rest, until his wished for day come, as that of the hireling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 27:6; Isaiah 11:1; Job 14:14"> A tree hath hope: if it be cut, it groweth green again, and the boughs thereof sprout.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:36; Isaiah 26:19; John 12:24"> If its root be old in the earth, and its stock be dead in the dust:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 11:17–24; Ezekiel 17:3–10; Ezekiel 17:22–24"> At the scent of water, it shall spring, and bring forth leaves, as when it was first planted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 19:26; Job 7:7–10; Job 14:12"> But man when he shall be dead, and stripped and consumed, I pray you where is he?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 19:5; Jeremiah 15:18; Job 6:15–18"> As if the waters should depart out of the sea, and an emptied river should be dried up:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 10:21–22; Job 3:13; Ecclesiastes 3:19–21"> So man when he is fallen asleep shall not rise again; till the heavens be broken, he shall not awake, nor rise up out of his sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 13:32; Isaiah 57:1–2; Isaiah 26:20–21"> Who will grant me this, that thou mayst protect me in hell, and hide me till thy wrath pass, and appoint me a. time when thou wilt remember me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:51–52; Job 7:1; Acts 26:8"> Shall man that is dead, thinkest thou, live again? all the days in which I am now in warfare, I expect until my change come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 13:22; Psalms 138:8; Job 10:8"> Thou shalt call me, and I will answer thee: to the work of thy hands thou shalt reach out thy right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 10:6; Job 31:4; Psalms 139:1–4"> Thou indeed hast numbered my steps, but spare my sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:34; Hosea 13:12; Job 21:19"> Thou hast sealed up my offences as it were in a bag, but hast cured my iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 6:14; Job 18:4; Jeremiah 4:24"> A mountain falling cometh to nought, and a rock is removed out of its place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 7:6; Job 27:8; Job 19:10"> Waters wear away the stones, and with inundation the ground by little and little is washed away: so in like manner thou shalt destroy man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 14:14; Job 2:12; Ecclesiastes 8:8"> Thou hast strengthened him for a little while, that he may pass away for ever: thou shalt change his face, and shalt send him away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:5; Ecclesiastes 2:18–19; Isaiah 63:16"> Whether his children come to honour or dishonour, he shall not understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 19:26; Job 19:20; Luke 16:23–24"> But yet his flesh, while he shall live, shall have pain, and his soul shall mourn over him.</span>
