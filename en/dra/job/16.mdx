<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Job answered, and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 13:4–5; Psalms 69:26; Job 11:2–3"> I have often heard such things as these: you are all troublesome comforters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 6:26; Job 15:2; Titus 2:8"> Shall windy words have no end? or is it any trouble to thee to speak?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 109:25; Psalms 22:7; Lamentations 2:15"> I also could speak like you: and would God your soul were for my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 27:9; Galatians 6:1; Isaiah 35:3–4"> I would comfort you also with words, and would wag my head over you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 10:1; Psalms 88:15–18; Psalms 77:1–9"> I would strengthen you with my mouth, and would move my lips, as sparing you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 7:3; Job 1:15–19; Psalms 6:6–7"> But what shall I do? If I speak, my pain will not rest: and if I hold my peace, it will not depart from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 10:17; Job 19:20; Ruth 1:21"> But now my sorrow hath oppressed me, and all my limbs are brought to nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 35:16; Job 13:24; Lamentations 2:16"> My wrinkles bear witness against me, and a false speaker riseth up against my face, contradicting me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 35:15; Psalms 22:13; Isaiah 50:6"> He hath gathered together his fury against me, and threatening me he hath gnashed with his teeth upon me: my enemy hath beheld me with terrible eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 27:12; Psalms 7:14; Psalms 31:8"> They have opened their mouths upon me, and reproaching me they have struck me on the cheek, they are filled with my pains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 7:20; Lamentations 3:11–12; Lamentations 3:4"> God hath shut me up with the unjust man, and hath delivered me into the hands of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 20:25; Job 6:4; Lamentations 2:11"> I that was formerly so wealthy, am all on a sudden broken to pieces: he hath taken me by my neck, he hath broken me, and hath set me up to be his mark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 15:8; Lamentations 3:3–5; Joel 2:7"> He hath compassed me round about with his lances, he hath wounded my loins, he hath not spared, and hath poured out my bowels on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 37:34; Psalms 7:5; Psalms 75:5"> He hath torn me with wound upon wound, he hath rushed in upon me like a giant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 14:34; Psalms 102:3–5; Lamentations 1:16"> I have sowed sackcloth upon my skin, and have covered my flesh with ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 15:8; Psalms 66:18–19; Job 27:6–7"> My face is swollen with weeping, and my eyelids are dim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 26:21; Ezekiel 24:7; Genesis 4:11"> These things have I suffered without the iniquity of my hand, when I offered pure prayers to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 1:9; 1 Thessalonians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 11:31"> O earth, cover not thou my blood, neither let my cry find a hiding place in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Psalms 142:2; Job 16:4"> For behold my witness is in heaven, and he that knoweth my conscience is on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 6:10; Isaiah 45:9; Romans 9:20"> My friends are full of words: my eye poureth out tears to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 14:10; Job 14:14; Job 14:5"> And O that a man might so be judged with God, as the son of man is judged with his companion!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> For behold short years pass away and I am walking in a path by which I shall not return.</span>
