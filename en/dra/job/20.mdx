<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 42:9; Job 11:1"> Then Sophar the Naamathite answered, and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 14:29; Psalms 31:22; Job 20:3"> Therefore various thoughts succeed one another in me, and my mind is hurried away to different things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 33:3; Job 20:2; Psalms 78:2–5"> The doctrine with which thou reprovest me, I will hear, and the spirit of my understanding shall answer for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 9:1–3; Job 15:10; Job 32:7"> This I know from the beginning, since man was placed upon the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 37:35–36; Job 18:5–6; Acts 12:22–23"> That the praise of the wicked is short, and the joy of the hypocrite but for a moment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 14:13–14; Obadiah 1:3–4; Genesis 11:4"> If his pride mount up even to heaven, and his head touch the clouds:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 7:10; 1 Kings 14:10; Job 4:20"> In the end he shall be destroyed like a dunghill, and they that has seen him shall say: Where is he?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 90:5; Psalms 73:20; Isaiah 29:7–8"> As a dream that fleeth away he shall not be found, he shall pass as a vision of the night:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 7:8; Job 7:10; Job 8:18"> The eyes that had seen him, shall see him no more, neither shall his place any more behold him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 20:18; Luke 19:8; Job 27:16–17"> His children shall be oppressed with want, and his hands shall render him his sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:26; Psalms 25:7; Job 21:26"> His bones shall be filled with the vices of his youth, and they shall sleep with him in the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 15:16; Psalms 10:7; Ecclesiastes 11:9"> For when evil shall be sweet in his mouth, he will hide it under his tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 9:43–49; Romans 8:13; Matthew 5:29–30"> He will spare it, and not leave it, and will hide it in his throat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 3:13; Psalms 51:8–9; Psalms 32:3–4"> His bread in his belly shall be turned into the gall of asps within him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 23:8; Matthew 27:3–4"> The riches which he hath swallowed; he shall vomit up, and God shall draw them out of his belly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 28:3–6; Romans 3:13; Deuteronomy 32:24"> He shall suck the head of asps, and the viper’s tongue shall kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13–14; Job 29:6; Luke 16:24"> (Let him not see the streams of the river, the brooks of honey and of butter.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 20:10; Job 20:15; Jeremiah 51:44"> He shall be punished for all that he did, and yet shall not be consumed: according to the multitude of his devices so also shall he suffer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 35:9; Proverbs 14:31; James 5:4"> Because he broke in and stripped the poor: he hath violently taken away a house which he did not build.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:13–14; Isaiah 57:20–21"> And yet his belly was not filled: and when he hath the things he coveted, he shall not be able to possess them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:11; Job 15:29; Luke 16:24–25"> There was nothing left of his meat, and therefore nothing shall continue of his goods:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 15:29; Revelation 18:7; 2 Kings 24:2"> When he shall be filled, he shall be straitened, he shall burn, and every sorrow shall fall upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 78:30–31; Numbers 11:33; Psalms 11:6"> May his belly be filled, that God may send forth the wrath of his indignation upon him, and rain down his war upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 24:18; Amos 5:19; 2 Samuel 22:35"> He shall flee from weapons of iron, and shall fall upon a bow of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 16:13; Job 18:11; Deuteronomy 32:41"> The sword is drawn out, and cometh forth from its scabbard, and glittereth in his bitterness: the terrible ones shall go and come upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 21:9; Psalms 120:4; Isaiah 30:33"> All darkness is hid in his secret places: a fire that is not kindled shall devour him, he shall be afflicted when left in his tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 26:21; Luke 12:2–3; Job 16:18"> The heavens shall reveal his iniquity, and the earth shall rise up against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 27:14–19; Revelation 18:17; James 5:1–3"> The offspring of his house shall be exposed, he shall be pulled down in the day of God’s wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 27:13; Job 31:2–3; Psalms 11:5–6"> This is the portion of a wicked man from God, and the inheritance of his doings from the Lord.</span>
