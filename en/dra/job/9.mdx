<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Job answered, and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 4:17; Romans 3:20; Job 25:4"> Indeed I know it is so, and that man cannot be justified compared with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 10:2; Job 40:2; Isaiah 57:15–16"> If he will contend with him, he cannot answer him one for a thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 36:5; Romans 11:33; Ephesians 3:20"> He is wise in heart, and mighty in strength: who hath resisted him, and hath had peace?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; Luke 21:11; Habakkuk 3:10"> Who hath removed mountains, and they whom he overthrew in his wrath, knew it not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Haggai 2:6; Hebrews 12:26; Psalms 75:3"> Who shaketh the earth out of her place, and the pillars thereof tremble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Amos 8:9; Luke 21:25–26"> Who commandeth tile sun and it riseth not: and shutteth up the stars as it were under a seal:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 44:24; Isaiah 40:22; Psalms 104:2–3"> Who alone spreadeth out the heavens, and walketh upon the waves of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 5:8; Genesis 1:16; Job 38:31–41"> Who maketh Arcturus, and Orion, and Hyades, and the inner parts of the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 5:9; Psalms 72:18; Ephesians 3:20"> Who doth things great and incomprehensible, and wonderful, of which there is no number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 23:8–9; Job 35:14; Psalms 77:19"> If he come to me, I shall not see him: if he depart I shall not understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 45:9; Job 11:10; Job 23:13"> If he examine on a sudden, who shall answer him? or who can say: Why dost thou so?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 89:10; Job 26:12; Isaiah 30:7"> God, whose wrath no mall can resist, and under whom they stoop that bear up the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 8:27; Job 9:3; Job 4:19"> What am I then, that I should answer him, and have words with him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 10:15; Job 8:5; Job 10:2"> I, who although I should have any just thing, would not answer, but would make supplication to my judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 29:24; Exodus 6:9; Psalms 66:18–20"> And if he should hear me when I call, I should not believe that he had heard my voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 16:14; Job 2:3; Job 34:6"> For he shall crush me in a whirlwind, and multiply my wounds even without cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 7:19; Lamentations 3:15; Psalms 88:15–18"> He alloweth not my spirit to rest, and he filleth me with bitterness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 9:32–33; Job 9:4; 1 Corinthians 1:25"> If strength be demanded, he is most strong: if equity of judgment, no man dare bear witness for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 15:5–6; Philippians 3:12–15; Psalms 143:2"> If I would justify myself, my own mouth shall condemn me: if I would shew myself innocent, he shall prove me wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 1:1; Psalms 139:23–24; Proverbs 28:26"> Although I should be simple, even this my soul shall be ignorant of, and I shall be weary of my life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:1–3; Luke 13:2–4; Job 10:8"> One thing there is that I have spoken, both the innocent and the wicked he consumeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 24:12; Job 1:13–19; Job 4:7"> If he scourge, let him kill at once, and not laugh at the pains of the innocent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 10:3; Jeremiah 12:1–2; Psalms 73:3–7"> The earth is given into the hand of the wicked, he covereth the face of the judges thereof: and if it be not he, who is it then?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 7:6–7; James 4:14; Psalms 90:9–10"> My days have been swifter than a post: they have fled away and have not seen good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:8; Isaiah 18:2; Job 39:27–30"> They have passed by as ships carrying fruits, as an eagle flying to the prey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 7:13; Jeremiah 8:18; Job 7:11"> If I say: I will not speak so: I change my face, and am tormented with sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; Psalms 88:15–16; Job 21:6"> I feared all my works, knowing that thou didst not spare the offender.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 10:7; Job 21:27; Job 10:14–17"> But if so also I am wicked, why have I laboured in vain?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:22; Psalms 26:6; 1 John 1:8"> If I be washed as it were with snow waters, and my hands shall shine ever so clean:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 64:6; Isaiah 59:6; Job 15:6"> Yet thou shalt plunge me in filth, and my garments shall abhor me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 9:20; Ecclesiastes 6:10; Psalms 143:2"> For I shall not answer a man that is like myself: nor one that may be heard with me equally in judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:25; 1 John 2:1–2; Job 9:19"> There is none that may be able to reprove both, and to put his hand between both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 39:10; Job 13:11; Job 33:7"> Let him take his rod away from me, and let not his fear terrify me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> I will speak, and will not fear him: for I cannot answer while I am in fear.</span>
