<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 20:1"> Then Sophar the Naamathite answered, and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 10:19; James 1:19; Job 18:2"> Shall not he that speaketh much, hear also? or shall a man full of talk be justified?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 17:2; Job 24:25; Titus 2:8"> Shall men hold their peace to thee only? and when thou hast mocked others, shall no man confute thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 10:7; Job 6:10; Job 35:2"> For thou hast said: My word is pure, and I am clean in thy sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 38:1–2; Job 31:35; Job 33:6–18"> And I wish that God would speak with thee, and would open his lips to thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra 9:13; Lamentations 3:22; Daniel 2:28"> That he might shew thee the secrets of wisdom, and that his law is manifold, and thou mightest understand that he exacteth much less of thee, than thy iniquity deserveth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Ecclesiastes 3:11; Psalms 145:3"> Peradventure thou wilt comprehend the steps of God, and wilt find out the Almighty perfectly?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 22:12; Psalms 139:6–8; Isaiah 55:9"> He is higher than heaven, and what wilt thou do? he is deeper than hell, and how wilt thou know?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 139:9–10; Job 28:24–25; Psalms 65:5–8"> The measure of him is longer than the earth, and broader than the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:12–13; Job 12:14; Revelation 3:7"> If he shall overturn all things, or shall press them together, who shall contradict him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 10:14; Hebrews 4:13; Revelation 2:23"> For he knoweth the vanity of men, and when he seeth iniquity, doth he not consider it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 2:20; Ecclesiastes 3:18; James 3:13–17"> A vain man is lifted up into pride, and thinketh himself born free like a wild ass’s colt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 143:6; Psalms 88:9; 1 Samuel 7:3"> Rut thou hast hardened thy heart, and hast spread thy hands to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:23; James 4:8; Job 4:7"> If thou wilt put away from thee the iniquity that is in thy hand, and lot not injustice remain in thy tabernacle:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 22:26; 1 John 2:28; 1 John 3:19–22"> Then mayst thou lift up thy face without spot, and thou shalt be steadfast, and shalt not fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 65:16; Ecclesiastes 5:20; John 16:21"> Thou shalt also forget misery, and remember it only as waters that are passed away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 58:8–10; Psalms 37:6; Proverbs 4:18"> And brightness like that of the noonday, shall arise to thee at evening: and when thou shalt think thyself consumed, thou shalt rise as the day star.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:24–26; Psalms 43:5; Psalms 3:5"> And thou shalt have confidence, hope being set before thee, and being buried thou shalt sleep secure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 45:12; Isaiah 60:14; Job 42:8–9"> Thou shalt rest, and there shall be none to make thee afraid: and many shall entreat thy face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:65; Job 31:16; Job 17:5"> But the eyes of the wicked shall decay, and the way to escape shall fail them, and their hope the abomination of the soul.</span>
