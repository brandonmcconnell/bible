<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 27:1; Job 13:12; Numbers 23:7"> Job also added, taking up his parable, and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 1:10; Job 1:1–5; Jeremiah 31:28"> Who will grant me, that I might be according to the months past, according to the days in which God kept me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 18:28; John 8:12; Job 18:6"> When his lamp shined over my head, and I walked by his light in darkness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 25:14; Proverbs 3:32; Psalms 27:5"> As I was in the days of my youth, when God was secretly in my tabernacle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 128:3; Psalms 127:3–5; Deuteronomy 33:27–29"> When the Almighty was with me: and my servants round about me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 81:16; Job 20:17; Deuteronomy 32:13–14"> When I washed my feet with butter, and the rock poured me out rivers of oil?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 8:16; Ruth 4:1–2; Deuteronomy 16:18"> When I went out to the gate of the city, and in the street they prepared me a chair?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 19:32; Proverbs 16:31; Romans 13:7"> The young men saw me, and hid themselves: and the old men rose up and stood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 21:5; Job 40:4; Proverbs 10:19"> The princes ceased to speak, and laid the finger on their mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 137:6; Ezekiel 3:26"> The rulers held their peace, and their tongue cleaved to their throat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 4:22; Job 31:20; Proverbs 29:2"> The ear that heard me blessed me, and the eye that saw me gave witness to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 72:12; Proverbs 21:13; James 1:27"> Because I had delivered the poor man that cried out; and the fatherless that had no helper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philemon 1:7; Job 22:9; Job 31:19–20"> The blessing of him that was ready to perish came upon me, and I comforted the heart of the widow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; Isaiah 59:17; Psalms 132:9"> I was clad with justice: and I clothed myself with my judgment, as with a robe and a diadem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 10:31; Matthew 11:5; 1 Corinthians 12:12–31"> I was an eye to the blind, and a foot to the lame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 29:7; Psalms 68:5; Job 31:18"> I was the father of the poor: and the cause which I knew not, I searched out most diligently.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 3:7; Proverbs 30:14; 1 Samuel 17:35"> I broke the jaws of the wicked man, and out of his teeth I took away the prey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 30:6–7; Genesis 32:12; Job 42:16–17"> And I said: I shall die in my nest, and as a palm tree shall multiply my days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 1:3; Jeremiah 17:8; Hosea 14:5–7"> My root is opened beside the waters, and dew shall continue in my harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 49:24; Isaiah 40:31; Psalms 18:34"> My glory shall always be renewed, and my bow in my hand shall be repaired.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 32:11–12; Job 29:9–10"> They that heard me, waited for my sentence, and being attentive held their peace at my counsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:2; Song of Solomon 4:11; Matthew 22:46"> To my words they durst add nothing, and my speech dropped upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hosea 6:3; Psalms 72:6; Zechariah 10:1"> They waited for me as for rain, and they opened their mouth as for a latter shower.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 89:15; Psalms 4:6; Psalms 126:1"> If at any time I laughed on them, they believed not, and the light of my countenance fell not on earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:3–4; 1 Thessalonians 3:2–3; Genesis 14:14–17"> If I had a mind to go to them, I sat first, and when I sat as a king, with his army standing about him, yet I was a comforter of them that mourned.</span>
