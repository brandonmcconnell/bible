<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Moreover Eliu spoke these words:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 12:36–37; Job 19:6–7; Job 16:17"> Doth thy thought seem right to thee, that thou shouldst say: I am more just than God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 34:9; Malachi 3:14; Job 9:30–31"> For thou saidst: That which is right doth not please thee: or what will it profit thee if I sin?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; Job 34:8"> Therefore I will answer thy words, and thy friends with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 22:12; Isaiah 55:9; Nahum 1:3"> Look up to heaven and see, and behold the sky, that it is higher than thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:19; Proverbs 8:36; Proverbs 9:12"> If thou sin, what shalt thou hurt him? and if thy iniquities be multiplied, what shalt thou do against him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 22:2–3; Psalms 16:2; Luke 17:10"> And if thou do justly, what shalt thou give him, or what shall he receive of thy hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:30; Job 42:8; Acts 27:24"> Thy wickedness may hurt a man that is like thee: and thy justice may help the son of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 2:23; Job 34:28; Exodus 3:7"> By reason of the multitude of oppressions they shall cry out: and shall wail for the violence of the arm of tyrants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 42:8; Acts 16:25; Psalms 149:5"> And he hath not said: Where is God, who made me, who hath given songs in the night?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 94:12; Job 36:22; Job 32:8"> Who teacheth us more than the beasts of the earth, and instructeth us more than the fowls of the air.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 1:28; John 9:31; Psalms 123:3–4"> There shall they cry, and he will not hear, because of the pride of evil men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 15:29; Isaiah 1:15; James 4:3"> God therefore will not hear in vain, and the Almighty will look into the causes of every one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 9:11; Micah 7:7–9; Psalms 37:5–6"> Yea when thou shalt say: He considereth not: be judged before him, and expect him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 89:32; Job 9:14; Job 13:15"> For he doth not now bring on his fury, neither doth he revenge wickedness exceedingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 38:2; Job 33:8–12; Job 34:35–37"> Therefore Job openeth his mouth in vain, and multiplieth words without knowledge.</span>
