<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> When Job answered, and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 5:21; Job 8:8–10; 1 Corinthians 6:5"> Are you then men alone, and shall wisdom die with you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 13:2–5; Proverbs 26:4; 2 Corinthians 11:21–23"> I also have a heart as well as you: for who is ignorant of these things, which you know?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 30:1; Job 17:6; Psalms 91:15"> He that is mocked by his friends as I, shall call upon God and he will hear him: for the simplicity of the just man is laughed to scorn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 25:8; Proverbs 13:9; Psalms 17:5"> The lamp despised in the thoughts of the rich, is ready for the time appointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 9:24; Psalms 73:11–12; Psalms 17:14"> The tabernacles of robbers abound, and they provoke God boldly; whereas it is he that hath given all into their hands:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:3; Proverbs 6:6; Jeremiah 8:7"> But ask now the beasts, and they shall teach thee: and the birds of the air, and they shall tell thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Speak to the earth, and it shall answer thee: and the fishes of the sea shall tell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 41:20; Jeremiah 27:5–6; Romans 11:36"> Who is ignorant that the hand of the Lord hath made all these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 17:28; Job 33:4; Acts 17:25"> In whose hand is the soul of every living thing, and the spirit of all flesh of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 34:3; Hebrews 5:14; Job 6:30"> Doth not the ear discern words, and the palate of him that eateth, the taste?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 32:7; Job 15:10; Job 8:8"> In the ancient is wisdom, and in length of days prudence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 9:4; Job 36:5; Proverbs 2:6–7"> With him is wisdom and strength, he hath counsel and understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 3:7; Isaiah 22:22; Malachi 1:4"> If he pull down, there is no man that can build up: if he shut up a. man, there is none that can open.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; Amos 5:8; Genesis 6:13"> If he withhold the waters, all things shall be dried up: and if he send them out, they shall overturn the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 13:9; Ezekiel 14:9; 1 Kings 22:22–23"> With him is strength and wisdom: he knoweth both the deceiver, and him that is deceived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 29:14; 1 Corinthians 1:19–20; 2 Samuel 17:23"> He bringeth counsellors to a foolish end, and judges to insensibility.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 2:21; Psalms 116:16; 2 Chronicles 33:11–14"> He looseth the belt of kings, and girdeth their loins with a cord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 45:1; Isaiah 37:36–38; Revelation 17:14"> He leadeth away priests without glory, and overthroweth nobles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 32:9; Job 17:4; Job 12:24"> He changeth the speech of the true speakers, and taketh away the doctrine of the aged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 107:40; Isaiah 23:9; 2 Kings 9:34–37"> He poureth contempt upon princes, and relieveth them that were oppressed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 2:22; 1 Corinthians 4:5; 1 Corinthians 2:10"> He discovereth deep things out of darkness, and bringeth up to light the shadow of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 9:3; Isaiah 26:15; Isaiah 60:22"> He multiplieth nations, and destroyeth them, and restoreth them again after they were overthrown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 107:40; Job 12:20; Hosea 7:11"> He changeth the heart of the princes of the people of the earth, and deceiveth them that they walk in vain where there is no way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 5:14; Psalms 107:27; Isaiah 19:14"> They shall grope as in the dark, and not in the light, and he shall make them stagger like men that are drunk.</span>
