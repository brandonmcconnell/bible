<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 39:4; Job 14:5–6; Job 5:7"> The life of man upon earth is a warfare, and his days are like the days of a hireling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 19:13; Deuteronomy 24:15; James 5:4"> As a servant longeth for the shade, as the hireling looketh for the end of his work;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 6:6; Ecclesiastes 1:14; Psalms 39:5"> So I also have had empty months, and have numbered to myself wearisome nights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:67; Job 7:13–14; Job 17:12"> If I lie down to sleep, I shall say: When shall arise? and again I shall look for the evening, and shall be filled with sorrows even till darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 17:14; Isaiah 14:11; Job 2:7–8"> My flesh is clothed with rottenness and the filth of dust, my skin is withered and drawn together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 9:25; Job 17:15; Job 17:11"> My days have passed more swiftly than the web is cut by the weaver, and are consumed without any hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 4:14; Psalms 78:39; Jeremiah 15:15"> Remember that my life is but wind, and my eyes shall not return to see good things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 20:9; Job 8:18; Psalms 37:36"> Nor shall the sight of man behold me: thy eyes are upon me, and I shall be no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 30:15; Job 10:21; 2 Samuel 14:14"> As a cloud is consumed, and passeth away: so he that shall go down to hell shall not come up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 8:18; Job 20:9; Psalms 103:16"> Nor shall he return my more into his house, neither shall his place know him any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 40:9; 1 Samuel 1:10; Job 10:1"> Wherefore I will not spare my month, I will speak in the affliction of my spirit: I will talk with the bitterness of my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:2–3; Lamentations 3:7; Job 38:6–11"> Am I a sea, or a whale, that thou hast enclosed me in a prison?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 6:6; Psalms 77:4; Job 9:27–28"> If I say: My bed shall comfort me, and I shall be relieved speaking with myself on my couch:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 2:1; Genesis 41:8; Genesis 40:5–7"> Thou wilt frighten me with dreams and terrify me with visions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 27:5; 2 Samuel 17:23"> So that my soul rather chooseth hanging, and my bones death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 19:4; Job 10:1; Job 9:21"> I have done with hope, I shall now live no longer: spare me, for my days are nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 2:6; Psalms 8:4; Psalms 144:3"> What is a man that thou shouldst magnify him? or why dost thou set thy heart upon him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 38:12–13; Jeremiah 9:7; 1 Peter 1:7"> Thou visitest him early in the morning, and thou provest him suddenly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 9:18; Job 14:6; Psalms 13:1–3"> How long wilt thou not spare me, nor suffer me to swallow down my spittle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Lamentations 3:12; Job 7:11–12; Job 3:24"> I have sinned: what shall I do to thee, O keeper of men? why hast thou set me opposite to thee, and I am become burdensome to myself?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; Job 10:14; 1 John 1:9"> Why dost thou not remove my sin, and why dost thou not take away my iniquity? Behold now I shall sleep in the dust: and if thou seek me in the morning, I shall not be.</span>
