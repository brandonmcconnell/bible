<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 18:21–22; Daniel 5:31; Esther 1:1"> It seemed good to Darius, and he appointed over the kingdom a hundred and twenty governors to be over his whole kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Daniel 2:48–49; Daniel 5:29; Esther 7:4"> And three princes over them, of whom Daniel was one: that the governors might give an account to them, and the king might have no trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 5:14; Proverbs 22:29; Genesis 41:38–41"> And Daniel excelled all the princes, and governors: because a greater spirit of God was in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 3:16; Philippians 2:15; 1 Peter 2:12"> And the king thought to set him over all the kingdom: whereupon the princes, and the governors sought to find occasion against Daniel with regard to the king: and they could find no cause, nor suspicion, because he was faithful, and no fault, nor suspicion was found in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 24:13–16; Esther 3:8; Acts 24:20–21"> Then these men said: We shall not find any occasion against this Daniel, unless perhaps concerning the law of his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 2:4; Nehemiah 2:3; Daniel 6:21"> Then the princes, and the governors craftily suggested to the king, and spoke thus unto him: King Darius, live for ever:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 59:3; Psalms 62:4; Daniel 3:27"> All the princes of the kingdom, the magistrates, and governors, the senators, and judges have consulted together, that an imperial decree, and an edict be published: That whosoever shall ask any petition of any god, or man, for thirty days, but of thee, O king, shall be cast into the den of lions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 6:15; Esther 1:19; Daniel 6:12"> Now, therefore, O king, confirm the sentence, and sign the decree: that what is decreed by the Medes and Persians may not be altered, nor any man be allowed to transgress it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 146:3; Psalms 118:9; Psalms 62:9–10"> So king Darius set forth the decree, and established it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Psalms 55:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:17–18"> Now when Daniel knew this, that is to say, that the law was made, he went into his house: and opening the windows in his upper chamber towards Jerusalem, he knelt down three times a day, and adored, and gave thanks before his God, as he had been accustomed to do before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 37:32–33; Psalms 10:9; Daniel 6:6"> Wherefore those men carefully watching him, found Daniel praying and making supplication to his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 6:8; Daniel 3:8–12; Esther 1:19"> And they came and spoke to the king concerning the edict: O king, hast thou not decreed, that every man that should make a request to any of the gods, or men, for thirty days, but to thyself, O king, should be cast into the den of the lions? And the king answered them, saying: The word is true according to the decree of the Medes and Persians, which it is not lawful to violate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 3:12; Acts 5:29; Daniel 2:25"> Then they answered, and said before the king: Daniel, who is of the children of the captivity of Juda, hath not regarded thy law, nor the decree that thou hast made: but three times a day he maketh his prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 6:26; Matthew 27:17–24; 2 Samuel 3:28–29"> Now when the king had heard these words, he was very much grieved, and in behalf of Daniel he set his heart to deliver him and even till sunset he laboured to save him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 6:12; Psalms 94:20–21; Daniel 6:8"> But those men perceiving the king’s design, said to him: Know thou, O king, that the law of the Medes and Persians is, that no decree which the king hath made, may be altered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 37:39–40; Job 5:19; Psalms 91:14–16"> Then the king commanded, and they brought Daniel, and cast him into the den of the lions. And the king said to Daniel: Thy God, whom thou always servest, he will deliver thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Lamentations 3:53; Acts 12:4; Matthew 27:60–66"> And a stone was brought, and laid upon the mouth of the den: which the king sealed with his own ring, and with the ring of his nobles, that nothing should be done against Daniel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Esther 6:1; Daniel 2:1; 2 Samuel 12:16–17"> And the king went away to his house and laid himself down without taking supper, and meat was not set before him, and even sleep departed from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:5; Mark 16:2; Matthew 28:1"> Then the king rising very early in the morning, went in haste to the lions’ den:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:11; Hebrews 7:25; Daniel 6:27"> And coming near to the den, cried with a lamentable voice to Daniel, and said to him: Daniel, servant of the living God, hath thy God, whom thou servest always, been able, thinkest thou, to deliver thee from the lions?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 2:4; Daniel 6:6; Nehemiah 2:3"> And Daniel answering the king, said: O king, live for ever:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:17; Hebrews 11:33; Acts 12:11"> My God hath sent his angel, and hath shut up the mouths of the lions, and they have not hurt me: forasmuch as before him justice hath been found in me: yea and before thee, O king, I have done no offence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:20; Mark 9:23; Daniel 3:27–28"> Then was the king exceeding glad for him, and he commanded that Daniel should be taken out of the den: and Daniel was taken out of the den, and no hurt was found in him, because he believed in his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 38:13; Esther 7:10; Deuteronomy 24:16"> And by the king’s commandment, those men were brought that bad accused Daniel: and they were cast into the lions’ den, they and their children, and their wives: and they did not reach the bottom of the den, before the lions caught them, and broke all their bones in pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 4:1; Ezra 1:1–2; Esther 3:12"> Then king Darius wrote to all people, tribes, and languages, dwelling in the whole earth: PEACE be multiplied unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Daniel 4:34; Luke 1:33; Daniel 3:29"> It is decreed by me, that in all my empire and my kingdom all men dread and fear the God of Daniel. For he is the living and eternal God for ever: and his kingdom shall not be destroyed, and his power shall be for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Daniel 4:2–3; Psalms 18:50; Acts 4:30"> He is the deliverer, and saviour, doing signs and wonders in heaven, and in earth: who hath delivered Daniel out of the lions’ den.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Daniel 1:21; 2 Chronicles 36:22–2; Isaiah 44:28–1"> Now Daniel continued unto the reign of Darius, and the reign of Cyrus the Persian.</span>
