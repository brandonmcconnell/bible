<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 6:28; Daniel 1:21; Daniel 8:26"> In the third year of Cyrus king of the Persians, a word was revealed to Daniel surnamed Baltassar, and a true word, and great strength: and he understood the word: for there is need of understanding in a vision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:4; Daniel 9:24–27; Ezra 9:4–5"> In those days I Daniel mourned the days of three weeks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 6:17; Daniel 11:8; 1 Corinthians 9:27"> I ate no desirable bread, and neither flesh, nor wine entered into my mouth, neither was I anointed with ointment: till the days of three weeks were accomplished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 2:14; Daniel 8:2; Ezekiel 1:3"> And in the four and twentieth day of the first month I was by the great river which is the Tigris.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 1:13–15; Daniel 12:6–7; Ezekiel 9:2"> And I lifted up my eyes, and I saw: and behold a man clothed in linen, and his loins were girded with the finest gold:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 19:12; Revelation 1:13–17; Matthew 17:2"> And his body was like the chrysolite, and his face as the appearance of lightning, and his eyes as a burning lamp: and his arms, and all downward even to the feet, like in appearance to glittering brass: and the voice of his word like the voice of a multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 9:7; Acts 22:9; Genesis 3:10"> And I Daniel alone saw the vision: for the men that were with me saw it not: but an exceeding great terror fell upon them, and they fled away, and hid themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 7:28; Habakkuk 3:16; Daniel 8:27"> And I being left alone saw this great vision: and there remained no strength in me, and the appearance of my countenance was changed in me, and I fainted away, and retained no strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 8:18; Job 4:13; Genesis 15:12"> And I heard the voice of his words: and when I heard, I lay in a consternation, upon my face, and my face was close to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Jeremiah 1:9; Daniel 8:18"> And behold a hand touched me, and lifted me up upon my knees, and upon the joints of my hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 26:16; Ezekiel 2:1; Daniel 8:16–17"> And he said to me: Daniel, thou man of desires, understand the words that I speak to thee, and stand upright: for I am sent now to thee. And when he had said this word to me, I stood trembling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 10:30–31; Daniel 9:20–23; Daniel 10:19"> And he said to me: Fear not, Daniel: for from the first day that thou didst set thy heart to understand, to afflict thyself in the sight of thy God, thy words have been heard: and I am come for thy words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jude 1:9; Daniel 12:1; Daniel 10:20–21"> But the prince of the kingdom of the Persians resisted me one and twenty days: and behold Michael, one of the chief princes, came to help me, and I remained there by the king of the Persians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 8:26; Daniel 2:28; Daniel 12:4"> But I am come to teach thee what things shall befall thy people in the latter days, for as yet the vision is for days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 1:20; Ezekiel 24:27; Daniel 10:9"> And when he was speaking such words to me, I cast down my countenance to the ground, and held my peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 6:7; Daniel 8:15; Jeremiah 1:9"> And behold, as it were the likeness of a son of man touched my lips: then I opened my mouth, and spoke, and said to him that stood before me: O my Lord, at the sight of thee my joints are loosed, and no strength hath remained in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 6:1–5; Exodus 24:10–11; Mark 12:36"> And how can the servant of my lord speak with my lord? for no strength remaineth in me, moreover my breath is stopped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 10:16; Luke 22:43; Daniel 8:18"> Therefore he that looked like a man touched me again, and strengthened me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 35:4; Joshua 1:9; Judges 6:23"> And he said: Fear not, O man of desires, peace be to thee: take courage and be strong. And when he spoke to me, I grew strong: and I said: Speak, O my lord, for thou hast strengthened me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 8:21; Daniel 11:2–4; Daniel 8:5–8"> And he said: Dost thou know wherefore I am come to thee? and now I will return, to fight against the prince of the Persians. When I went forth, there appeared the prince of the Greeks coming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 10:13; Daniel 12:1; Revelation 12:7"> But I will tell thee what is set down in the scripture of truth: and none is my helper in all these things, but Michael your prince.</span>
