<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 45:10; Genesis 46:31; Genesis 46:28"> Then Joseph went in and told Pharao, saying: My father and brethren, their sheep and their herds, and all that they possess, are come out of the land of Chanaan: and behold they stay in the land of Gessen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 7:13; Colossians 1:28; Jude 1:24"> Five men also the last of his brethren, he presented before the king:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 46:33–34; 2 Thessalonians 3:10; Genesis 4:2"> And he asked them: What is your occupation? They answered: We thy servants are shepherds, both we, and our fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 46:34; Genesis 15:13; Deuteronomy 26:5"> We are come to sojourn in thy land, because there is no grass for the flocks of thy servants, the famine being very grievous in the land of Chanaan: and we pray thee to give orders that we thy servants may be in the land of Gessen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The king therefore said to Joseph: Thy father and thy brethren are come to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; Proverbs 22:29; Genesis 47:11"> The land of Egypt is before thee: make them dwell in the best place, and give them the land of Gessen. And if thou knowest that there are industrious men among them, make them rulers over my cattle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 47:10; 2 Samuel 8:10; 1 Samuel 2:20"> After this Joseph brought in his father to the king, and presented him before him: and he blessed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And being asked by him: How many are the days of the years of thy life?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 39:12; Psalms 39:5; Job 14:1"> He answered: The days of my pilgrimage are a hundred and thirty years, few, and evil, and they are not come up to the days of the pilgrimage of my fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 47:7; Psalms 129:8; Hebrews 7:7"> And blessing the king, he went out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 47:6; Exodus 1:11; Exodus 12:37"> But Joseph gave a possession to his father and his brethren in Egypt, in the best place of the land, in Ramesses, as Pharao had commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 45:11; 1 Timothy 5:8; Genesis 47:1"> And he nourished them, and all his father’s house, allowing food to every one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 7:11; 1 Kings 18:5; Lamentations 4:9"> For in the whole world there was want of bread, and a famine had oppressed the land: more especially of Egypt and Chanaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 41:56; Luke 16:10–12; Luke 16:1–2"> Out of which he gathered up all the money for the corn which they bought, and brought it into the king’s treasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 8:8; Psalms 37:3; Matthew 6:11"> And when the buyers wanted money, all Egypt came to Joseph, saying: Give us bread: why should we die in thy presence, having now no money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 4:5; Daniel 6:5–7; 1 Corinthians 10:32"> And he answered them: Bring me your cattle, and for them I will give you food, if you have no money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 2:4; Exodus 9:3; Isaiah 31:1"> And when they had brought them, he gave them food in exchange for their horses, and sheep, and oxen, and asses and he maintained them that year for the exchange of their cattle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 6:26; Jeremiah 38:9"> And they came the second year, and said to him: We will not hide from our lord, how that our money is spent, and our cattle also are gone: neither art thou ignorant that we have nothing now left but our bodies and our lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:2–3; Job 2:4; Lamentations 1:11"> Why therefore shall we die before thy eyes? we will be thine, both we and our lands: buy us to be the king’s servants, and give us seed, lest for want of tillers the land be turned into a wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> So Joseph bought all the land of Egypt, every man selling his possessions, because of the greatness of the famine. And he brought it into Pharao’s hands:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 41:48"> And all its people from one end of the borders of Egypt, even to the other end thereof,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezra 7:24; Nehemiah 13:10; 2 Thessalonians 3:10"> Except the land of the priests, which had been given them by the king: to whom also a certain allowance of food was given out of the public stores, and therefore they were not forced to sell their possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 112:5; Psalms 41:1; Ecclesiastes 11:6"> Then Joseph said to the people: Behold as you see, both you and your lands belong to Pharao: take seed and sow the fields,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 41:34; Psalms 41:1; Psalms 112:5"> That you may have corn. The fifth part you shall give to the king: the other four you shall have for seed, and for food for your families and children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 33:15; Proverbs 11:26–27; Genesis 18:3"> And they answered: Our life is in thy hand: only let my lord look favourably upon us, and we will gladly serve the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 47:22; Ezekiel 7:24"> From that time unto this day, in the whole land of Egypt, the fifth part is paid to the king, and it is become as a law, except the land of the priests, which was free from this covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 46:3; Exodus 1:7; Acts 7:17"> So Israel dwelt in Egypt, that is, in the land of Gessen, and possessed it: and grew, and was multiplied exceedingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 90:10; Genesis 47:8–9; Genesis 37:2"> And he lived in it seventeen years: and all the days of his life came to a hundred and forty-seven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:14; Genesis 24:49; Genesis 24:2"> And when he saw that the day of his death drew nigh, he called his son Joseph, and said to him: If I have found favour in thy sight, put thy hand under my thigh; and thou shalt shew me this kindness and truth, not to bury me in Egypt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 49:29–32; Genesis 23:19; Genesis 25:9"> But I will sleep with my fathers, and thou shalt take me away out of this land, and bury me in the burying place of my ancestors. And Joseph answered him: I will do what thou hast commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 1:47; Hebrews 11:21; Genesis 24:3"> And he said: Swear then to me. And as he was swearing, Israel adored God, turning to the bed’s head.</span>
