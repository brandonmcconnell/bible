<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 7:11; Matthew 8:26–27; Proverbs 8:28"> The fountains also of the deep, and the flood gates of heaven were shut up, and the rain from heaven was restrained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 7:24; Genesis 7:11"> And the waters returned from off the earth going and coming: and they began to be abated after a hundred and fifty days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 19:37; Jeremiah 51:27; Isaiah 37:38"> And the ark rested in the seventh month, the seven and twentieth day of the month, upon the mountains of Armenia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 7:11"> And the waters were going and decreasing until the tenth month: for in the tenth month, the first day of the month, the tops of the mountains appeared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 6:16; Daniel 6:10"> And after that forty days were passed, Noe, opening the window of the ark which he had made, sent forth a raven:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 147:9; 1 Kings 17:6; 1 Kings 17:4"> Which went forth and did not return, till the waters were dried up upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:14; Song of Solomon 1:15; Matthew 10:16"> He sent forth also a dove after him, to see if the waters had now ceased upon the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 11:28; Psalms 116:7; Deuteronomy 28:65"> But she, not finding where her foot might rest, returned to him into the ark: for the waters were upon the whole earth: and he put forth his hand, and caught her, and brought her into the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; Genesis 7:10; Genesis 8:12"> And having waited yet seven other days, he again sent forth the dove out of the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:15; Zechariah 4:12–14; Romans 10:15"> And she came to him in the evening, carrying a bough of an olive tree, with green leaves, in her mouth. Noe therefore understood that the waters were ceased upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 25:9; Isaiah 30:18; Genesis 8:10"> And he stayed yet other seven days: and he sent forth the dove, which returned not any more unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 7:11"> Therefore in the six hundredth and first year, the first month, the first day of the month, the waters were lessened upon the earth, and Noe opening the covering of the ark, looked, and saw that the face of the earth was dried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 7:13–14; Genesis 7:11"> In the second month, the seven and twentieth day of the month, the earth was dried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And God spoke to Noe, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 7:13; Psalms 121:8; Psalms 91:11"> Go out of the ark, thou and thy wife, thy sons, and the wives of thy sons with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 1:22; Genesis 9:1; Jeremiah 31:27–28"> All living things that are with thee of all flesh, as well in fowls as in beasts, and all creeping things that creep upon the earth, bring out with thee, and go ye upon the earth: increase and multiply upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 121:8"> So Noe went out, he and his sons: his wife, and the wives of his sons with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And all living things, and cattle, and creeping things that creep upon the earth, according to their kinds, went out of the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15–16; Romans 12:1; Genesis 22:9"> And Noe built an altar unto the Lord: and taking of all cattle and fowls that were clean, offered holocausts upon the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:15; Ephesians 5:2; Isaiah 54:9–10"> And the Lord smelled a sweet savour, and said: I will no more curse the earth for the sake of man: for the imagination and thought of man’s heart are prone to evil from his youth: therefore I will no more destroy every living soul as I have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 5:7; Psalms 74:16–17; Jeremiah 5:24"> All the days of the earth, seedtime and harvest, cold and heat, summer and winter, night and day, shall not cease.</span>
