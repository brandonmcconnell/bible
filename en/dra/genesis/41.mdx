<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 20:3; Genesis 29:14; Exodus 4:9"> After two years Pharao had a dream. He thought he stood by the river,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 19:6"> Out of which came up seven kine, very beautiful and fat: and they fed in marshy places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 41:20–21; Genesis 41:4"> Other seven also came up out of the river, ill favoured, and leanfleshed: and they fed on the very bank of the river, in green places:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 3:15"> And they devoured them, whose bodies were very beautiful and well conditioned. So Pharao awoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:14"> He slept again, and dreamed another dream: Seven ears of corn came up upon one stalk full and fair:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:10; Hosea 13:15; Ezekiel 19:12"> Then seven other ears sprung up thin and blasted,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 37:5; Genesis 20:3"> And devoured all the beauty of the former. Pharao awaked after his rest:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 4:7; Exodus 7:22; Matthew 2:1"> And when morning was come, being struck with fear, he sent to all the interpreters of Egypt, and to all the wise men: and they being called for, he told them his dream, and there was not any one that could interpret it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 40:14; Genesis 40:23; Genesis 40:1–3"> Then at length the chief butler remembering, said: I confess my sin:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 39:20; Genesis 40:2–3; Genesis 37:36"> The king being angry with his servants, commanded me and the chief baker to be cast into the prison of the captain of the soldiers:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 40:5–8"> Where in one night both of us dreamed a dream foreboding things to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 40:12–19; Genesis 37:36; Genesis 39:1"> There was there a young man a Hebrew, servant to the same captain of the soldiers: to whom we told our dreams,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:3; Genesis 40:20–22; Genesis 40:12"> And we heard what afterwards the event of the thing proved to be so. For I was restored to my office: and he was hanged upon a gibbet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 2:25; Psalms 113:7–8; Psalms 105:19–22"> Forthwith at the king’s command, Joseph was brought out of the prison, and they shaved him, and changing his apparel, brought him in to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 5:16; Daniel 5:12; Psalms 25:14"> And he said to him: I have dreamed dreams, and there is no one that can expound them: Now I have heard that thou art very wise at interpreting them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:5; Genesis 40:8; Daniel 2:47"> Joseph answered: Without me, God shall give Pharao a prosperous answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> So Pharao told what he had dreamed: Methought I stood upon the bank of the river,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:8; Jeremiah 24:1–3; Jeremiah 24:5"> And seven kine came up out of the river exceeding beautiful and full of flesh: and they grazed on green places in a marshy pasture.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And behold, there followed these, other seven kine, so very ill favoured and lean, that I never saw the like in the land of Egypt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And they devoured and consumed the former,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:3; Psalms 37:19; Revelation 10:9–10"> And yet gave no mark of their being full: but were as lean and ill favoured as before. I awoke, and then fell asleep again,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And dreamed a dream: Seven ears of corn grew upon one stalk, full and very fair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 129:6–7; Hosea 13:15; 2 Kings 19:26"> Other seven also thin and blasted, sprung of the stock:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 4:7; Genesis 41:8; Exodus 8:19"> And they devoured the beauty of the former: I told this dream to the conjecturers, and there is no man that can expound it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 2:45; Revelation 4:1; Ephesians 1:17"> Joseph answered: The king’s dream is one: God hath shewn to Pharao what he is about to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 41:47; Genesis 41:2; Genesis 41:53"> The seven beautiful kine, and the seven full ears, are seven years of plenty: and both contain the same meaning of the dream.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 8:1; 2 Samuel 24:19"> And the seven lean and thin kine that came up after them, and the seven thin ears that were blasted with the burning wind, are seven years of famine to come:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 41:25; Genesis 41:16"> Which shall be fulfilled in this order:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 41:26; Genesis 41:46–47; Genesis 41:49"> Behold, there shall come seven years of great plenty in the whole land of Egypt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 47:13; Genesis 41:54; James 5:17"> After which shall follow other seven years of so great scarcity, that all the abundance before shall be forgotten: for the famine shall consume all the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 24:20; 1 Samuel 5:6"> And the greatness of the scarcity shall destroy the greatness of the plenty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Isaiah 46:10–11; Isaiah 14:24–27"> And for that thou didst see the second time a dream pertaining to the same thing: it is a token of the certainty, and that the word of God cometh to pass, and is fulfilled speedily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 41:39; Acts 6:3; Deuteronomy 1:13"> Now therefore let the king provide a wise and industrious man, and make him ruler over the land of Egypt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 22:3; Luke 16:5; Proverbs 6:6–8"> That he may appoint overseers over all the countries: and gather into barns the fifth part of the fruits, during the seven fruitful years,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 45:6–7; Genesis 41:56; Exodus 4:13"> That shall now presently ensue: and let all the corn be laid up under Pharao’s hands and be reserved in the cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 47:13–25"> And let it be in readiness, against the famine of seven years to come, which shall oppress Egypt, and the land shall not be consumed with scarcity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 7:10; 1 Kings 21:2; 2 Samuel 3:36"> The counsel pleased Pharao and all his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Job 32:8; Daniel 5:14; Numbers 27:18"> And he said to them: Can we find such another man, that is full of the spirit of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 41:33; Genesis 41:28; Genesis 41:25"> He said therefore to Joseph: Seeing God hath shewn thee all that thou hast said, can I find one wiser and one like unto thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Proverbs 22:29; Acts 7:10; Psalms 105:21–22"> Thou shalt be over my house, and at the commandment of thy mouth all the people shall obey: only in the kingly throne will I be above thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 28:18; Philippians 2:9–11; Genesis 42:6"> And again Pharao said to Joseph: Behold, I have appointed thee over the whole land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Daniel 5:29; Daniel 5:7; Esther 3:10"> And he took his ring from his own hand, and gave it into his hand: and he put upon him a robe of silk, and put a chain of gold about his neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Acts 7:10; Genesis 45:8; Genesis 45:26"> And he made him go up into his second chariot, the crier proclaiming that all should bow their knee before him, and that they should know he was made governor over the whole land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 105:22; Exodus 11:7"> And the king said to Joseph: I am Pharao; without thy commandment no man shall move hand or foot in all the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Genesis 46:20; Ezekiel 30:17; Exodus 2:16"> And he turned his name, and called him in the Egyptian tongue, The saviour of the world. And he gave him to wife Aseneth the daughter of Putiphare priest of Heliopolis. Then Joseph went out to the land of Egypt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Daniel 1:19; 1 Kings 12:6; 1 Samuel 16:21"> (Now he was thirty years old when he stood before king Pharao) and he went round all the countries of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Genesis 26:12; Psalms 72:16"> And the fruitfulness of the seven years came: and the corn being bound up into sheaves was gathered together into the barns of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Genesis 41:34–36; Genesis 47:21"> And all the abundance of grain was laid up in every city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Psalms 78:27; Judges 6:5; Genesis 22:17"> And there was so great abundance of wheat, that it was equal to the sand of the sea, and the plenty exceeded measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Genesis 46:20; Genesis 48:5; 2 Samuel 8:18"> And before the famine came, Joseph had two sons born: whom Aseneth the daughter of Putiphare priest of Heliopolis bore unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Psalms 30:5; Proverbs 31:7; Deuteronomy 33:17"> And he called the name of the firstborn Manasses, saying: God hath made me to forget all my labours, and my father’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Genesis 49:22; Genesis 50:23; Genesis 17:6"> And he named the second Ephraim, saying: God hath made me to grow in the land of my poverty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Genesis 41:29–31; Psalms 73:20; Luke 16:25"> Now when the seven years of the plenty that had been in Egypt were past:</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Genesis 45:11; Acts 7:11; Psalms 105:16"> The seven years of scarcity, which Joseph had foretold, began to come: and the famine prevailed in the whole world, but there was bread in all the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Matthew 17:5; Matthew 3:17; Jeremiah 14:1–6"> And when there also they began to be famished, the people cried to Pharao for food. And he said to them: Go to Joseph: and do all that he shall say to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Genesis 42:6; Zechariah 5:3; Luke 21:35"> And the famine increased daily in all the land: and Joseph opened all the barns, and sold to the Egyptians: for the famine had oppressed them also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Genesis 41:54; Genesis 42:5; Genesis 41:56"> And all provinces came into Egypt, to buy food, and to seek some relief of their want.</span>
