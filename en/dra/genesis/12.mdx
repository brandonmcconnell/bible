<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 11:8; Nehemiah 9:7; Acts 7:2–6"> And the Lord said to Abram: Go forth out of thy country, and from thy kindred, and out of thy father’s house, and come into the land which I shall shew thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:9; Genesis 18:18; Galatians 3:14"> And I will make of thee a great nation, and I will bless thee, and magnify thy name, and thou shalt be blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 3:8; Genesis 22:18; Genesis 27:29"> I will bless them that bless thee, and curse them that curse thee, and IN THEE shall all the kindred of the earth be blessed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 11:8; Genesis 11:31; Genesis 11:27"> So Abram went out as the Lord had commanded him, and Lot went with him: Abram was seventy-five years old when he went forth from Haran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 14:14; Genesis 11:31; Hebrews 11:8–9"> And he took Sarai his wife, and Lot his brother’s son, and all the substance which they had gathered, and the souls which they had gotten in Haran: and they went out to go into the land of Chanaan. And when they were come into it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 11:9; Deuteronomy 11:30; Genesis 35:4"> Abram passed through the country into the place of Sichem, as far as the noble vale: now the Chanaanite was at that time in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Genesis 18:1; Genesis 13:15"> And the Lord appeared to Abram, and said to him: To thy seed will I give this land. And he built there an altar to the Lord, who had appeared to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:31; Genesis 4:26; Genesis 21:33"> And passing on from thence to a mountain, that was on the east side of Bethel, he there pitched his tent, having Bethel on the west, and Hai on the east; he built there also an altar to the Lord, and called upon his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 13:3; Genesis 24:62; Genesis 13:1"> And Abram went forward, going, and proceeding on to the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 43:1; Genesis 26:1–3; 1 Kings 17:1–18"> And there came a famine in the country; and Abram went down into Egypt, to sojourn there: for the famine was very grievous in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 26:7; Genesis 29:17; Genesis 12:14"> And when he was near to enter into Egypt, he said to Sarai his wife: I know that thou art a beautiful woman:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 20:11; Genesis 26:7; 1 John 1:8–10"> And that when the Egyptians shall see thee, they will say: She is his wife: and they will kill me, and keep thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 26:7; Genesis 20:12–13; Genesis 20:2"> Say, therefore, I pray thee, that thou art my sister: that I may be well used for thee, and that my soul may live for thy sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 6:2; Matthew 5:28; Genesis 39:7"> And when Abram was come into Egypt, the Egyptians saw the woman that she was very beautiful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 20:2; Genesis 41:1; 2 Kings 18:21"> And the princes told Pharao, and praised her before him: and the woman was taken into the house of Pharao.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 20:14; Genesis 13:2; Genesis 24:35"> And they used Abram well for her sake. And he had sheep and oxen, and he asses, and men servants and maid servants, and she asses, and camels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:21; Genesis 20:18; Psalms 105:14–15"> But the Lord scourged Pharao and his house with most grievous stripes for Sarai, Abram’s wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 20:9–10; Genesis 26:9–11; 1 Samuel 14:43"> And Pharao called Abram, and said to him: What is this that thou hast done to me? Why didst thou not tell me that she was thy wife?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> For what cause didst thou say, she was thy sister, that I might take her to my wife? Now, therefore, there is thy wife, take her, and go thy way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 105:14–15; Proverbs 21:1; Exodus 18:27"> And Pharao gave his men orders concerning Abram: and they led him away, and his wife, and all that he had.</span>
