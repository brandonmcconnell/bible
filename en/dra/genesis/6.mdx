<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 1:28"> And after that men began to be multiplied upon the earth, and daughters were born to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 82:6–7; 1 John 2:16; 2 Peter 2:14"> The sons of God seeing the daughters of men, that they were fair, took to themselves wives of all which they chose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:30; Isaiah 63:10; Jeremiah 11:11"> And God said: My spirit shall not remain in man for ever, because he is flesh, and his days shall be a hundred and twenty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 13:33; 2 Samuel 21:15–22; 1 Samuel 17:4"> Now giants were upon the earth in those days. For after the sons of God went in to the daughters of men, and they brought forth children, these are the mighty men of old, men of renown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 15:19; Romans 1:28–31; Mark 7:21–23"> And God seeing that the wickedness of men was great on the earth, and that all the thought of their heart was bent upon evil at all times,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 63:10; 1 Samuel 15:11; Ephesians 4:30"> It repented him that he had made man on the earth. And being touched inwardly with sorrow of heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:3; Psalms 37:20; Jeremiah 12:3–4"> He said: I will destroy man, whom I have created, from the face of the earth, from man even to beasts, from the creeping thing even to the fowls of the air, for it repenteth me that I have made them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 19:19; Hebrews 4:16; Luke 1:30"> But Noe found grace before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 1:6; Hebrews 11:7; 2 Peter 2:5"> These are the generations of Noe: Noe was a just and perfect man in his generations, he walked with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 5:32"> And he begot three sons, Sem, Cham, and Japheth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:17; Hosea 4:1–2; Habakkuk 2:8"> And the earth was corrupted before God, and was filled with iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 53:2–3; Proverbs 15:3; Psalms 14:1–3"> And when God had seen that the earth was corrupted (for all flesh had corrupted its way upon the earth,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 4:1–2; 1 Peter 4:7; Ezekiel 7:2–6"> He said to Noe: The end of all flesh is come before me, the earth is filled with iniquity through them, and I will destroy them with the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 3:20; Luke 17:27; Matthew 24:38"> Make thee an ark of timber planks: thou shalt make little rooms in the ark, and thou shalt pitch it within and without.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:11; Genesis 7:20"> And thus shalt thou make it: The length of the ark shall be three hundred cubits: the breadth of it fifty cubits, and the height of it thirty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 8:6; Genesis 7:16; Ezekiel 41:16"> Thou shalt make a window in the ark, and in a cubit shalt thou finish the top of it: and the door of the ark thou shalt set in the side: with lower, middle chambers, and third stories shalt thou make it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; 2 Peter 2:5; Genesis 7:21–23"> Behold I will bring the waters of a great flood upon the earth, to destroy all flesh, wherein is the breath of life, under heaven. All things that are in the earth shall be consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 11:7; Genesis 7:7; Genesis 17:7"> And I will establish my covenant with thee, and thou shalt enter into the ark, thou and thy sons, and thy wife, and the wives of thy sons with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 7:15–16; Genesis 7:8–9; Genesis 8:17"> And of every living creature of all flesh, thou shalt bring two of a sort into the ark, that they may live with thee: of the male sex, and the female.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 10:11–12; Genesis 2:19; Genesis 7:8–16"> Of fowls according to their kind, and of beasts in their kind, and of every thing that creepeth on the earth according to its kind; two of every sort shall go in with thee, that they may live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 1:29–30; Psalms 145:16; Psalms 136:25"> Thou shalt take unto thee of all food that may be eaten, and thou shalt lay it up with thee: and it shall be food for thee and them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 40:16; John 15:14; Matthew 7:24–27"> And Noe did all things which God commanded him.</span>
