<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 91:11; Psalms 34:7; Hebrews 1:4"> Jacob also went on the journey he had begun: and the angels of God met him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:8; Joshua 5:14; Joshua 21:38"> And when he saw them, he said: These are the camps of God, and he called the name of that place Mahanaim, that is, Camps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 25:30; Joshua 24:4; Deuteronomy 2:5"> And he sent messengers before him to Esau his brother to the land of Seir to the country of Edom:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 3:6; Luke 14:11; Genesis 33:8"> And he commanded them, saying: Thus shall ye speak to my lord Esau: Thus saith thy brother Jacob: I have sojourned with Laban, and have been with him until this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 33:8; Genesis 33:15; Genesis 33:11"> I have oxen, and asses, and sheep, and menservants, and womenservants: and now I send a message to my lord, that I may find favour in thy sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 33:1; Genesis 32:8; Genesis 32:11"> And the messengers returned to Jacob, saying: We came to Esau thy brother, and behold he cometh with speed to meet thee with four hundred men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 35:3; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10; Exodus 14:10"> Then Jacob was greatly afraid; and in his fear divided the people that was with him, and the flocks, and the sheep, and the oxen, and the camels, into two companies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 10:16; Genesis 33:1–3"> Saying: If Esau come to one company and destroy it, the other company that is left shall escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 31:42; Psalms 34:4–6; Genesis 31:3"> And Jacob said: O God of my father Abraham, and God of my father Isaac, O Lord, who saidst to me: Return to thy land and to the place of thy birth, and I will do well for thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 18:35; Genesis 24:27; 2 Samuel 7:18"> I am not worthy of the least of all thy mercies, and of thy truth which thou hast fulfilled to thy servant. With my staff I passed over this Jordan; and now I return with two companies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 59:1–2; Proverbs 18:19; Matthew 6:13"> Deliver me from the hand of my brother Esau, for I am greatly afraid of him: lest perhaps he come, and kill the mother with the children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 28:13–15; Hebrews 6:17; Titus 1:2"> Thou didst say that thou wouldst do well by me, and multiply my seed like the sand of the sea, which cannot be numbered for multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 18:16; Genesis 43:11; Proverbs 19:6"> And when he had slept there that night, he set apart, of the things which he had, presents for his brother Esau.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 1:3; Deuteronomy 8:18; Job 42:12"> Two hundred she goats, twenty he goats, two hundred ewes, and twenty rams,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Thirty milch camels with their colts, forty kine, and twenty bulls, twenty she asses, and ten of their foals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 33:8–9; Genesis 32:20; Proverbs 2:11"> And he sent them by the hands of his servants, every drove by itself, and he said to his servants: Go before me, and let there be a space between drove and drove.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 33:3"> And he commanded the first, saying: If thou meet my brother Esau, and he ask thee: Whose art thou? or whither goest thou? or whose are these before thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 32:4–5"> Thou shalt answer: Thy servant Jacob’s: he hath sent them as a present to my lord Esau: and he cometh after us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> In like manner he commanded the second and the third, and all that followed the droves, saying: Speak ye the same words to Esau, when ye find him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 42:8–9; Proverbs 21:14; Genesis 43:11"> And ye shall add: Thy servant Jacob himself also followeth after us: for he said: I will appease him with the presents that go before, and afterwards I will see him, perhaps he will be gracious to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> So the presents went before him, but himself lodged that night in the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:16; Deuteronomy 2:37; 1 Timothy 5:8"> And rising early he took his two wives, and his two handmaids, with his eleven sons, and passed over the ford of Jaboc.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And when all things were brought over that belonged to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hosea 12:3–5; Ephesians 6:12; Luke 13:24"> He remained alone: and behold a man wrestled with him till morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:7–9; Hosea 12:3–4; Matthew 26:41"> And when he saw that he could not overcome him, he touched the sinew of his thigh, and forthwith it shrank.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hosea 12:4; Psalms 115:12–13; 2 Corinthians 12:8–9"> And he said to him: Let me go, for it is break of day. He answered: I will not let thee go except thou bless me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And he said: What is thy name? He answered: Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 2:17; Genesis 35:10; Isaiah 62:2–4"> But he said: Thy name shall not be called Jacob, but Israel: for if thou hast been strong against God, how much more shalt thou prevail against men?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6; Judges 13:16–18; Job 11:7"> Jacob asked him, Tell me by what name art thou called? He answered: Why dost thou ask my name? And he blessed him in the same place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 24:10–11; Numbers 12:8; Isaiah 6:5"> And Jacob called the name of the place Phanuel, saying: I have seen God face to face, and my soul has been saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:7; Genesis 19:15; Judges 8:8"> And immediately the sun rose upon him, after he was past Phanuel; but he halted on his foot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:5"> Therefore the children of Israel, unto this day, eat not the sinew, that shrank in Jacob’s thigh: because he touched the sinew of his thigh and it shrank.</span>
