<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 37:28; Psalms 105:17; Genesis 37:36"> And Joseph was brought into Egypt, and Putiphar an eunuch of Pharao, chief captain of the army, an Egyptian, bought him of the Ismaelites, by whom he was brought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:14; Psalms 91:15; Jeremiah 15:20"> And the Lord was with him, and he was a prosperous man in all things: and he dwelt in his master’s house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 1:3; Genesis 30:27; Matthew 5:16"> Who knew very well that the Lord was with him, and made all that he did to prosper in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 39:21–22; Proverbs 27:18; Genesis 19:19"> And Joseph found favour in the sight of his master, and ministered to him: and being set over all by him, he governed the house committed to him, and all things that were delivered to him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 30:27; 2 Samuel 6:11–12; Acts 27:24"> And the Lord blessed the house of the Egyptian for Joseph’s sake, and multiplied all his substance, both at home, and in the fields.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:12; Luke 19:17; Proverbs 31:11"> Neither knew he any other thing, but the bread which he ate. And Joseph was of a beautiful countenance, and comely to behold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:11; Ezekiel 16:34; 2 Peter 2:14"> And after many days his mistress cast her eyes on Joseph, and said: Lie with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 23:26–28; Proverbs 22:14; Proverbs 9:13–18"> But he, in no wise consenting to that wicked act, said to her: Behold, my master hath delivered all things to me, and knoweth not what he hath in his own house:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:13; Genesis 20:6; Genesis 42:18"> Neither is there any thing which is not in my power, or that he hath not delivered to me, but thee, who art his wife: how then can I do this wicked thing, and sin against my God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Genesis 39:8; 1 Thessalonians 5:22"> With such words as these day by day, both the woman was importunate with the young man, and he refused the adultery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 5:12; Ephesians 5:3; Malachi 3:5"> Now it happened on a certain day, that Joseph went into the house, and was doing some business without any, man with him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:22; Proverbs 7:13–27; Ecclesiastes 7:26"> And she catching the skirt of his garment, said: Lie with me. But he leaving the garment in her hand, fled, and went out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And when the woman saw the garment in her hands, and herself disregarded,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 10:21; 1 Peter 2:20; Psalms 35:11"> She called to her the men of her house, and said to them: See, he hath brought in a Hebrew, to abuse us: he came in to me, to lie with me: and when I cried out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And he heard my voice, he left the garment that I held, and got him out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 37:32; Jeremiah 4:22; Jeremiah 9:3–5"> For a proof therefore of her fidelity, she kept the garment, and shewed it to her husband when he returned home:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 23:1; 1 Kings 18:17; Psalms 37:14"> And said: The Hebrew servant, whom thou hast brought, came to me to abuse me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And when he heard me cry, he left the garment which I held, and fled out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 4:5–6; Proverbs 18:17; Proverbs 6:34–35"> His master hearing these things, and giving too much credit to his wife’s words, was very angry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 40:15; 2 Timothy 2:9; Psalms 105:18–19"> And cast Joseph into the prison, where the king’s prisoners were kept, and he was there shut up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 3:21; Isaiah 41:10; 1 Peter 3:17"> But the Lord was with Joseph and having mercy upon him gave him favour in the sight of the chief keeper of the prison:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 40:3–4; Genesis 39:4; 1 Samuel 2:30"> Who delivered into his hand all the prisoners that were kept in custody: and whatsoever was done was under him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Genesis 39:2–3; Psalms 1:3"> Neither did he himself know any thing, having committed all things to him: for the Lord was with him, and made all that he did to prosper.</span>
