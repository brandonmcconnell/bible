<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 4:24; Genesis 12:16; Genesis 15:2–3"> Now Sarai the wife of Abram, had brought forth no children; but having a handmaid, an Egyptian, named Agar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 21:4; Genesis 18:10; Genesis 3:17"> She said to her husband: Behold, the Lord hath restrained me from bearing: go in unto my handmaid, it may be I may have children of her at least. And when he agreed to her request,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 16:5; Genesis 12:4–5; 2 Samuel 5:13"> She took Agar the Egyptian her handmaid, ten years after they first dwelt in the land of Chanaan, and gave her to her husband to wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 30:23; 2 Samuel 6:16; 1 Corinthians 13:4–5"> And he went in to her. But she, perceiving that she was with child, despised her mistress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 31:53; Exodus 5:21; Psalms 7:8"> And Sarai said to Abram: Thou dost unjustly with me: I gave my handmaid into thy bosom, and she perceiving herself to be with child, despiseth me. The Lord judge between me and thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:5; Proverbs 14:29; 1 Peter 3:7"> And Abram made answer, and said to her: Behold thy handmaid is in thy own hand, use her as it pleaseth thee. And when Sarai afflicted her, she ran away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 25:18; Exodus 15:22; Genesis 21:17"> And the angel of the Lord having found her, by a fountain of water in the wilderness, which is in the way to Sur in the desert,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 3:9; Genesis 4:10; Ecclesiastes 10:4"> He said to her: Agar, handmaid of Sarai, whence comest thou? and whither goest thou? And she answered: I flee from the face of Sarai, my mistress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5–6; Ecclesiastes 10:4; Titus 2:9"> And the angel of the Lord said to her: Return to thy mistress, and humble thyself under her hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 17:20; Psalms 83:6–7; Genesis 25:12–18"> And again he said: I will multiply thy seed exceedingly, and it shall not be numbered for multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 2:23–24; Exodus 3:7; Isaiah 7:14"> And again: Behold, said he, thou art with child, and thou shalt bring forth a son: and thou shalt call his name Ismael, because the Lord hath heard thy affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 25:18; Genesis 21:20; Job 39:5–8"> He shall be a wild man: his hand will be against all men, and all men’s hands against him: and he shall pitch his tents over against all his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 139:1–12; Genesis 32:30; Proverbs 5:21"> And she called the name of the Lord that spoke unto her: Thou the God who hast seen me. For she said: Verily here have I seen the hinder parts of him that seeth me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 24:62; Genesis 25:11; Genesis 21:31"> Therefore she called that well, The well of him that liveth and seeth me. The same is between Cades and Barad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 25:12; Genesis 21:9–21; Genesis 17:25–26"> And Agar brought forth a son to Abram: who called his name Ismael.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Abram was four score and six years old when Agar brought him forth Ismael.</span>
