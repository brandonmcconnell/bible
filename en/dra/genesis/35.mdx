<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 31:13; Hosea 12:4; Psalms 91:15"> In the meantime God said to Jacob: Arise, and go up to Bethel, and dwell there, and make there an altar to God, who appeared to thee when thou didst flee from Esau thy brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 19:14; Exodus 19:10; Ezekiel 18:31"> And Jacob having called together all his household, said: Cast away the strange gods that are among you, and be cleansed and change your garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Genesis 32:7; Genesis 31:3"> Arise, and let us go up to Bethel, that we may make there an altar to God: who heard me in the day of my affliction, and accompanied me in my journey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 9:6; Deuteronomy 7:25; Exodus 32:20"> So they gave him all the strange gods they had, and the earrings which were in their ears: and he buried them under the turpentine tree, that is behind the city of Sichem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 23:27; 2 Chronicles 17:10; Joshua 5:1"> And when they were departed, the terror of God fell upon all the cities round about, and they durst not pursue after them as they went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 28:19; Genesis 28:22; Genesis 12:8"> And Jacob came to Luza, which is in the land of Chanaan, surnamed Bethel: he and all the people that were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 35:3; Genesis 35:1; Genesis 28:13"> And he built there an altar, and called the name of that place, The house of God: for there God appeared to him when he fled from his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 24:59; 1 Samuel 31:13; Judges 2:5"> At the same time Debora the nurse of Rebecca died, and was buried at the foot of Bethel under an oak: and the name of that place was called, The oak of weeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 35:1; Genesis 18:1; Genesis 26:2"> And God appeared again to Jacob, after he returned from Mesopotamia of Syria, and he blessed him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 17:5; Genesis 32:27–28; Genesis 17:15"> Saying: Thou shalt not be called any more Jacob, but Israel shall be thy name. And he called him Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 17:1; Genesis 9:1; Genesis 22:17"> And said to him: I am God Almighty, increase thou and be multiplied. Nations and peoples of nations shall be from thee, and kings shall come out of thy loins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 12:7; Genesis 28:13; Genesis 26:3–4"> And the land which I gave to Abraham and Isaac, I will give to thee, and to thy seed after thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 17:22; Genesis 18:33; Judges 6:21"> And he departed from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 28:18–19; 1 Samuel 7:12; Exodus 17:15"> But he set up a monument of stone, in the place where God had spoken to him: pouring drink offerings upon it, and pouring oil thereon:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 28:19"> And calling the name of that place Bethel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Genesis 48:7; 2 Kings 5:19"> And going forth from thence, he came in the springtime to the land which leadeth to Ephrata: wherein when Rachel was in travail,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 30:24; 1 Samuel 4:19–21"> By reason of her hard labour she began to be in danger, and the midwife said to her: Fear not, for thou shalt have this son also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 12:20; Acts 7:59; Luke 23:46"> And when her soul was departing for pain, and death was now at hand, she called the name of her son Benoni, that is, The son of my pain: but his father called him Benjamin, that is, The son of the right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Genesis 48:7; Ruth 4:11"> So Rachel died, and was buried in the highway that leadeth to Ephrata, this is Bethlehem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:2; Genesis 35:14; 2 Samuel 18:17–18"> And Jacob erected a pillar over her sepulchre: this is the pillar of Rachel’s monument, to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Micah 4:8; Luke 2:8"> Departing thence, he pitched his tent beyond the Flock tower.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:1; Leviticus 18:8; 2 Samuel 20:3"> And when he dwelt in that country, Ruben went, and slept with Bala, the concubine of his father: which he was not ignorant of. Now the sons of Jacob were twelve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 30:18–20; Genesis 46:8–15; Genesis 29:32–35"> The sons of Lia: Ruben the first born, and Simeon, and Levi, and Juda, and Issachar, and Zabulon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 30:22–24; Genesis 46:19–22; Genesis 35:16–18"> The sons of Rachel: Joseph and Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 46:23–25; Genesis 37:2; Genesis 30:4–8"> The sons of Bala, Rachel’s handmaid: Dan and Nephtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 30:9–13; Genesis 46:16–18; Genesis 25:20"> The sons of Zelpha, Lia’s handmaid: Gad and Aser: these are the sons of Jacob, that were born to him in Mesopotamia of Syria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 13:18; Genesis 23:19; Genesis 18:1"> And he came to Isaac his father in Mambre, the city of Arbee, this is Hebron: wherein Abraham and Isaac sojourned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 25:7; Genesis 50:26; Genesis 47:28"> And the days of Isaac were a hundred and eighty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 15:15; Genesis 49:33; Genesis 49:31"> And being spent with age he died, and was gathered to his people, being old and full of days: and his sons Esau and Jacob buried him.</span>
