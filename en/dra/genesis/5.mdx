<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 4:25; Job 25:4; Psalms 51:5"> And Adam lived a hundred and thirty years, and begot a son to his own image and likeness, and called his name Seth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:1–3; Luke 3:36–38; Genesis 5:26"> And the days of Adam, after he begot Seth, were eight hundred years: and he begot sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 5:8; Genesis 5:11; Genesis 3:19"> And all the time that Adam lived came to nine hundred and thirty years, and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 4:26"> Seth also lived a hundred and five years, and begot Enos.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And Seth lived after he begot Enos, eight hundred and seven years, and begot sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And all the days of Seth were nine hundred and twelve years, and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 3:37; 1 Chronicles 1:2"> And Enos lived ninety years, and begot Cainan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> After whose birth he lived eight hundred and fifteen years, and begot sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And all the days of Enos were nine hundred and five years, and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 3:37"> And Cainan lived seventy years, and begot Malaleel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And Cainan lived after he begot Malaleel, eight hundred and forty years, and begot sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And all the days of Cainan were nine hundred and ten years, and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:2"> And Malaleel lived sixty-five years, and begot Jared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Malaleel lived after he begot Jared, eight hundred and thirty years, and begot sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And all the days of Malaleel were eight hundred and ninety-five years, and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 4:17; 1 Chronicles 1:3; Jude 1:14–15"> And Jared lived a hundred and sixty-two years, and begot Henoch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And Jared lived after he begot Henoch, eight hundred years, and begot sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And all the days of Jared were nine hundred and sixty-two years, and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 3:37"> And Henoch lived sixty-five years, and begot Mathusala.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:12; Genesis 17:1; Genesis 6:9"> And Henoch walked with God: and lived after he begot Mathusala, three hundred years, and begot sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And all the days of Henoch were three hundred and sixty-five years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 11:5–6; 1 John 1:7; Genesis 5:22"> And he walked with God, and was seen no more: because God took him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 4:18"> And Mathusala lived a hundred and eighty-seven years, and begot Lamech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And Mathusala lived after he begot Lamech, seven hundred and eighty-two years, and begot sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And all the days of Mathusala were nine hundred and sixty-nine years, and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Lamech lived a hundred and eighty-two years, and begot a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Peter 2:5; Hebrews 11:7; Matthew 24:37"> And he called his name Noe, saying: This same shall comfort us from the works and labours of our hands on the earth, which the Lord hath cursed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And Lamech lived after he begot Noe, five hundred and ninety-five years, and begot sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And all the days of Lamech came to seven hundred and seventy-seven years, and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 6:10; Genesis 10:21; Genesis 9:18–19"> And Noe, when he was five hundred years old, begot Sem, Cham, and Japheth.</span>
