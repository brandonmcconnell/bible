<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 46:3; Psalms 45:16; Romans 7:4"> Thy navel is like a round bowl never wanting cups. Thy belly is like a heap of wheat, set about with lilies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Song of Solomon 4:5; Song of Solomon 6:6"> Thy two breasts are like two young roes that are twins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Song of Solomon 4:4; Song of Solomon 4:8–9; Psalms 144:12"> Thy neck as a tower of ivory. Thy eyes like the fishpools in Hesebon, which are in the gate of the daughter of the multitude. Thy nose is as the tower of Libanus, that looketh toward Damascus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 35:2; Song of Solomon 4:1; Micah 7:14"> Thy head is like Carmel: and the hairs of thy head as the purple of the king bound in the channels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:15–16; Psalms 45:11; Song of Solomon 4:10"> How beautiful art thou, and how comely, my dearest, in delights!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Song of Solomon 4:5; Song of Solomon 1:13; Ephesians 4:13"> Thy stature is like to a palm tree, and thy breasts to clusters of grapes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14; John 14:21–23; Song of Solomon 2:5"> I said: I will go up into the palm tree, and will take hold of the fruit thereof: and thy breasts shall be as the clusters of the vine: and the odour of thy mouth like apples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Song of Solomon 5:16; Isaiah 62:8–9; Acts 4:31–32"> Thy throat like the best wine, worthy for my beloved to drink, and for his lips and his teeth to ruminate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 45:11; Song of Solomon 2:16; Song of Solomon 6:3"> I to my beloved, and his turning is towards me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Song of Solomon 4:8; Song of Solomon 2:10–13; Song of Solomon 1:4"> Come, my beloved, let us go forth into the field, let us abide in the villages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Song of Solomon 6:11; Ephesians 6:24; Song of Solomon 7:6"> Let us get up early to the vineyards, let us see if the vineyard flourish, if the flowers be ready to bring forth fruits, if the pomegranates flourish: there will I give thee my breasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 30:14; Matthew 13:52; 1 Peter 4:11"> The mandrakes give a smell. In our gates are all fruits: the new and the old, my beloved, I have kept for thee.</span>
