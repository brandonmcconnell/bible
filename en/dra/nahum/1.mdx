<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:13; Jonah 1:2; Zechariah 9:1"> The burden of Ninive. The book of the vision of Nahum the Elcesite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 8:2; Exodus 20:5; Psalms 94:1"> The Lord is a jealous God, and a revenger: the Lord is a revenger, and hath wrath: the Lord taketh vengeance on his adversaries, and he is angry with his enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 147:5; Exodus 34:6–7; Nehemiah 9:17"> The Lord is patient, and great in power, and will not cleanse and acquit the guilty. The Lord’s ways are in a tempest, and a whirlwind, and clouds are the dust of his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 33:9; Psalms 106:9; Psalms 114:5"> He rebuketh the sea, and drieth it up: and bringeth all the rivers to be a desert. Basan languisheth and Carmel: and the dower of Libanus fadeth away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 1:4; 2 Samuel 22:8; Jeremiah 4:24"> The mountains tremble at him, and the hills are made desolate: and the earth hath quaked at his presence, and the world, and all that dwell therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:10; Malachi 3:2; Deuteronomy 32:22–23"> Who can stand before the face of his indignation? and who shall resist in the fierceness of his anger? his indignation is poured out like fire: and the rocks are melted by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 10:27; 2 Timothy 2:19; Lamentations 3:25"> The Lord is good and giveth strength in the day of trouble: and knoweth them that hope in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 28:17; Isaiah 8:22; Ezekiel 13:13"> But with a flood that passeth by, he will make an utter end of the place thereof: and darkness shall pursue his enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 21:11; Proverbs 21:30; Psalms 33:10"> What do ye devise against the Lord? he will make an utter end: there shall not rise a double affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 7:4; Isaiah 5:24; Isaiah 9:18"> For as thorns embrace one another: so while they are feasting and drinking together, they shall be consumed as stubble that is fully dry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nahum 1:9; 2 Chronicles 13:7; 2 Kings 18:13–14"> Out of thee shall come forth one that imagineth evil against the Lord, contriving treachery in his mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 30:19; Joel 2:19; Isaiah 17:14"> Thus saith the Lord: Though they were perfect: and many of them so, yet thus shall they be cut off, and he shall pass: I have afflicted thee, and I will afflict thee no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 10:27; Isaiah 9:4; Jeremiah 2:20"> And now I will break in pieces his rod with which he struck thy back, and I will burst thy bonds asunder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 109:13; Isaiah 46:1–2; Micah 5:13"> And the Lord will give a commandment concerning thee, that no more of thy name shall be sown: I will destroy the graven and molten thing out of the house of thy God, I will make it thy grave, for thou art disgraced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Romans 10:15; Isaiah 40:9–10"> Behold upon the mountains the feet of him that bringeth good tidings, and that preacheth peace: O Juda, keep thy festivals, and pay thy vows: for Belial shall no more pass through thee again, he is utterly cut off.</span>
