<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Now Arphaxad king of the Medes had brought many nations under his dominions, and he built a very strong city, which he called Ecbatana,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Of stones squared and hewed: he made the walls thereof seventy cubits broad, and thirty cubits high, and the towers thereof he made a hundred cubits high. But on the square of them, each side was extended the space of twenty feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And he made the gates thereof according to the height of the towers:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And he gloried as a mighty one in the force of his army and in the glory of his chariots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Now in the twelfth year of his reign, Nabuchodonosor king of the Assyrians, who reigned in Ninive the great city, fought against Arphaxad and overcame him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> In the great plain which is called Ragua, about the Euphrates, and the Tigris, and the Jadason, in the plain of Erioch the king of the Elicians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Then was the kingdom of Nabuchodonosor exalted, and his heart was elevated: and he sent to all that dwelt in Cilicia and Damascus, and Libanus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And to the nations that are in Carmelus, and Cedar, and to the inhabitants of Galilee in the great plain of Asdrelon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And to all that were in Samaria, and beyond the river Jordan even to Jerusalem, and all the land of Jesse till you come to the borders of Ethiopia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> To all these Nabuchodonosor king of the Assyrians, sent messengers:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> But they all with one mind refused, and sent them back empty, and rejected them without honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Then king Nabuchodonosor being angry against all that land, swore by his throne and kingdom that he would revenge himself of all those countries.</span>
