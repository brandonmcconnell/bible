<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Now it came to pass, when Judith a widow had heard these words, who was the daughter of Merari, the son of Idox, the son of Joseph, the son of Ozias, the son of Elai, the son of Jamnor, the son of Gedeon, the son of Raphaim, the son of Achitob, the son of Melehias, the son of Enan, the son of Nathanias, the son of Salathiel, the son of Simeon, the son of Ruben:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And her husband was Manasses, who died in the time of the barley harvest:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> For he was standing over them that bound sheaves in the field; and the heat came upon his head, and he died in Bethulia his own city, and was buried there with his fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And Judith his relict was a widow now three years and six months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And she made herself a private chamber in the upper part of her house, in which she abode shut up with her maids.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And she wore haircloth upon her loins, and fasted all the days of her life, except the sabbaths, and new moons, and the feasts of the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And she was exceedingly beautiful, and her husband left her great riches, and very many servants, and large possessions of herds of oxen, and flocks of sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And she was greatly renowned among all, because she feared the Lord very much, neither was there any one that spoke an ill word of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> When therefore she had heard that Ozias had promised that he would deliver up the city after the fifth day, she sent to the ancients Chabri and Charmi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And they came to her, and she said to them: What is this word, by which Ozias hath consented to give up the city to the Assyrians, if within five days there come no aid to us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And who are you that tempt the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> This is not a word that may draw down mercy, but rather that may stir up wrath, and enkindle indignation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> You have set a time for the mercy of the Lord, and you have appointed him a day, according to your pleasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> But forasmuch as the Lord is patient, let us be penitent for this same thing, and with many tears let us beg his pardon:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> For God will not threaten like man, nor be inflamed to anger like the son of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And therefore let us humble our souls before him, and continuing in an humble spirit, in his service:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Let us ask the Lord with tears, that according to his will so he would shew his mercy to us: that as our heart is troubled by their pride, so also we may glorify in our humility.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> For we have not followed the sins of our fathers, who forsook their God, and worshipped strange gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> For which crime they were given up to their enemies, to the sword, and to pillage, and to confusion: but we know no other God but him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Let us humbly wait for his consolation, and the Lord our God will require our blood of the afflictions of our enemies, and he will humble all the nations that shall rise up against us, and bring them to disgrace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And now, brethren, as you are the ancients among the people of God, and their very soul resteth upon you: comfort their hearts by your speech, that they may be mindful how our fathers were tempted that they might be proved, whether they worshipped their God truly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> They must remember how our father Abraham was tempted, and being proved by many tribulations, was made the friend of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> So Isaac, so Jacob, so Moses, and all that have pleased God, passed through many tribulations, remaining faithful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> But they that did not receive the trials with the fear of the Lord, but uttered their impatience and the reproach of their murmuring against the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Were destroyed by the destroyer, and perished by serpents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> As for us therefore let us not revenge ourselves for these things which we suffer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> But esteeming these very punishments to be less than our sins deserve, let us believe that these scourges of the Lord, with which like servants we are chastised, have happened for our amendment, and not for our destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Ozias and the ancients said to her: All things which thou hast spoken are true, and there is nothing to be reprehended in thy words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Now therefore pray for us, for thou art a holy woman, and one fearing God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And Judith said to them: As you know that what I have been able to say is of God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> So that which I intend to do prove ye if it be of God, and pray that God may strengthen my design.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> You shall stand at the gate this night, and I will go out with my maidservant: and pray ye, that as you have said, in five days the Lord may look down upon his people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> But I desire that you search not into what I am doing, and till I bring you word let nothing else be done but to pray for me to the Lord our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And Ozias the prince of Juda said to her: Go in peace, and the Lord be with thee to take revenge of our enemies. So returning they departed.</span>
