<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 9:3; Ezekiel 27:30–31; 2 Samuel 13:19"> Now when Mardochai had heard these things, he rent his garments, and put on sackcloth, strewing ashes on his head: and he cried with a loud voice in the street in the midst of the city, shewing the anguish of his mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And he came lamenting in this manner even to the gate of the palace: for no one clothed with sackcloth might enter the king’s court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 22:12; Daniel 9:3; Isaiah 58:5"> And in all provinces, towns, and places, to which the king’s cruel edict was come, there was great mourning among the Jews, with fasting, wailing, and weeping, many using sackcloth and ashes for their bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 77:2; Jeremiah 31:15; Esther 1:12"> Then Esther’s maids and her eunuchs went in, and told her. And when she heard it she was in a consternation: and she sent a garment, to clothe him, and to take away the sackcloth: but he would not receive it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:26; Romans 12:15; Esther 1:10"> And she called for Athach the eunuch, whom the king had appointed to attend upon her, and she commanded him to go to Mardochai, and learn of him why he did this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esther 4:3; Esther 7:2; Esther 9:12"> And Athach going out went to Mardochai, who was standing in the street of the city, before the palace gate:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esther 3:2–15"> And Mardochai told him all that had happened, how Aman had promised to pay money into the king’s treasures, to have the Jews destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:4; Job 9:15; 1 Timothy 6:13"> He gave him also a copy of the edict which was hanging up in Susan, that he should shew it to the queen, and admonish her to go in to the king, and to entreat him for her people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Athach went back and told Esther all that Mardochai had said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> She answered him, and bade him say to Mardochai:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esther 5:1–2; Esther 8:4; Daniel 2:9"> All the king’s servants, and all the provinces that are under his dominion, know, that whosoever, whether man or woman, cometh into the king’s inner court, who is not called for, is immediately to be put to death without any delay: except the king shall hold out the golden sceptre to him, in token of clemency, that so he may live. How then can I go in to the king, who for these thirty days now have not been called unto him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And when Mardochai had heard this,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 24:10–12; Philippians 2:30; John 12:25"> He sent word to Esther again, saying: Think not that thou mayst save thy life only, because thou art in the king a house, more than all the Jews:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 45:4–8; 1 Samuel 12:22; Deuteronomy 32:36"> For if thou wilt now hold thy peace, the Jews shall be delivered by some other occasion: and thou, and thy father’s house shall perish. And who knoweth whether thou art not therefore come to the kingdom, that thou mightest be ready in such a time as this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And again Esther sent to Mardochai in these words:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 20:24; 2 Chronicles 20:3; Joel 2:12–17"> Go, and gather together all the Jews whom thou shalt find in Susan, and pray ye for me. Neither eat nor drink for three days and three nights: and I with my handmaids will fast in like manner, and then I will go in to the king, against the law, not being called, and expose myself to death and to danger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> So Mardochai went, and did all that Esther had commanded him.</span>
