<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 27:1–2; Matthew 27:11–14; Luke 22:66"> And the whole multitude of them rising up, led him to Pilate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 17:6–7; John 19:12; Luke 23:14"> And they began to accuse him, saying: We have found this man perverting our nation, and forbidding to give tribute to Caesar, and saying that he is Christ the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 1:49; 1 Timothy 6:13; Luke 22:70"> And Pilate asked him, saying: Art thou the king of the Jews? But he answering, said: Thou sayest it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 18:38; Luke 23:14–15; Luke 23:22"> And Pilate said to the chief priests and to the multitudes: I find no cause in this man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 1:14; John 1:43; John 2:11"> But they were more earnest, saying: He stirreth up the people, teaching throughout all Judea, beginning from Galilee to this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 5:37; Luke 13:1"> But Pilate hearing Galilee, asked if the man were of Galilee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Luke 13:31; Matthew 14:1"> And when he understood that he was of Herod’s jurisdiction, he sent him away to Herod, who was also himself at Jerusalem, in those days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 9:7–9; Matthew 14:1; Mark 6:14"> And Herod, seeing Jesus, was very glad; for he was desirous of a long time to see him, because he had heard many things of him; and he hoped to see some sign wrought by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 27:14; Matthew 7:6; 1 Peter 2:23"> And he questioned him in many words. But he answered him nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 11:53; Luke 23:14–15; Luke 23:5"> And the chief priests and the scribes stood by, earnestly accusing him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 15:16–20; Matthew 27:27–30; Psalms 69:19–20"> And Herod with his army set him at nought, and mocked him, putting on him a white garment, and sent him back to Pilate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 4:27; Revelation 17:13–14; Psalms 83:4–6"> And Herod and Pilate were made friends, that same day; for before they were enemies one to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 15:14; Matthew 27:21–23; John 18:38"> And Pilate, calling together the chief priests, and the magistrates, and the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 6:4; Hebrews 7:26; Luke 23:1–2"> Said to them: You have presented unto me this man, as one that perverteth the people; and behold I, having examined him before you, find no cause in this man, in those things wherein you accuse him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> No, nor Herod neither. For I sent you to him, and behold, nothing worthy of death is done to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:26; Luke 23:22; Mark 15:15"> I will chastise him therefore, and release him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Now of necessity he was to release unto them one upon the feast day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 21:36; Acts 3:14; Acts 22:22"> But the whole multitude together cried out, saying: Away with this man, and release unto us Barabbas:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 3:14; Luke 23:5; Luke 23:2"> Who, for a certain sedition made in the city, and for a murder, was cast into prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 15:15; Matthew 14:8–9; Matthew 27:19"> And Pilate again spoke to them, desiring to release Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 27:22–25; Mark 15:13; John 19:15"> But they cried again, saying: Crucify him, crucify him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 23:14; Luke 23:20; 1 Peter 3:18"> And he said to them the third time: Why, what evil hath this man done? I find no cause of death in him. I will chastise him therefore, and let him go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zechariah 11:8; Psalms 57:4; Psalms 22:12–13"> But they were instant with loud voices, requiring that he might be crucified; and their voices prevailed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 27:26; Exodus 23:2; Proverbs 17:15"> And Pilate gave sentence that it should be as they required.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 3:14; Mark 15:6–7; Mark 15:15"> And he released unto them him who for murder and sedition, had been cast into prison, whom they had desired; but Jesus he delivered up to their will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 27:32–44; Mark 15:21–32; Acts 6:9"> And as they led him away, they laid hold of one Simon of Cyrene, coming from the country; and they laid the cross on him to carry after Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 8:52; Luke 8:2; Mark 15:40"> And there followed him a great multitude of people, and of women, who bewailed and lamented him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Song of Solomon 8:4; Song of Solomon 5:8; Song of Solomon 1:5"> But Jesus turning to them, said: Daughters of Jerusalem, weep not over me; but weep for yourselves, and for your children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 24:19; Luke 21:23–24; Mark 13:17–19"> For behold, the days shall come, wherein they will say: Blessed are the barren, and the wombs that have not borne, and the paps that have not given suck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 6:16; Hosea 10:8; Isaiah 2:19"> Then shall they begin to say to the mountains: Fall upon us; and to the hills: Cover us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17–18; John 15:6; Proverbs 11:31"> For if in the green wood they do these things, what shall be done in the dry?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 15:27; Isaiah 53:12; Matthew 27:38"> And there were also two other malefactors led with him to be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 15:22–23; John 19:17–18; John 18:32"> And when they were come to the place which is called Calvary, they crucified him there; and the robbers, one on the right hand, and the other on the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; Acts 7:60; 1 Peter 3:9"> And Jesus said: Father, forgive them, for they know not what they do. But they, dividing his garments, cast lots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 22:17; Isaiah 42:1; 1 Peter 2:4"> And the people stood beholding, and the rulers with them derided him, saying: He saved others; let him save himself, if he be Christ, the elect of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Matthew 27:48; Matthew 27:34"> And the soldiers also mocked him, coming to him, and offering him vinegar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And saying: If thou be the king of the Jews, save thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Mark 15:26; Matthew 27:37; John 19:19–22"> And there was also a superscription written over him in letters of Greek, and Latin, and Hebrew: THIS IS THE KING OF THE JEWS.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 27:44; Mark 15:32; Luke 23:35"> And one of those robbers who were hanged, blasphemed him, saying: If thou be Christ, save thyself and us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; Luke 12:5; Revelation 15:4"> But the other answering, rebuked him, saying: Neither dost thou fear God, seeing thou art condemned under the same condemnation?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Leviticus 26:40–41; Ezra 9:13; 1 Peter 1:19"> And we indeed justly, for we receive the due reward of our deeds; but this man hath done no evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Romans 10:9–14; Acts 16:31; 1 John 5:11–13"> And he said to Jesus: Lord, remember me when thou shalt come into thy kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Revelation 2:7; Psalms 50:15; 1 Timothy 1:15–16"> And Jesus said to him: Amen I say to thee, this day thou shalt be with me in paradise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 27:45; Mark 15:33–34; Amos 8:9"> And it was almost the sixth hour; and there was darkness over all the earth until the ninth hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; Matthew 27:51; Hebrews 9:3–8"> And the sun was darkened, and the veil of the temple was rent in the midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 31:5; Acts 7:59; John 19:30"> And Jesus crying out with a loud voice, said: Father, into thy hands I commend my spirit. And saying this, he gave up the ghost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 27:54; Mark 15:39; Luke 23:41"> Now the centurion, seeing what was done, glorified God, saying: Indeed this was a just man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 18:13; Acts 2:37; Jeremiah 31:19"> And all the multitude of them that were come together to that sight, and saw the things that were done, returned striking their breasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 8:2; Psalms 38:11; Luke 23:55"> And all his acquaintance, and the women that had followed him from Galilee, stood afar off, beholding these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 19:38–42; Acts 11:24; Acts 10:22"> And behold there was a man named Joseph, who was a counsellor, a good and just man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 2:25; Luke 2:38; Isaiah 8:12"> (The same had not consented to their counsel and doings;) of Arimathea, a city of Judea; who also himself looked for the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 19:38–42"> This man went to Pilate, and begged the body of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Matthew 27:59–60; Isaiah 53:9; Mark 15:46"> And taking him down, he wrapped him in fine linen, and laid him in a sepulchre that was hewed in stone, wherein never yet any man had been laid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 27:62; John 19:31; Mark 15:42"> And it was the day of the Parasceve, and the sabbath drew on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Luke 23:49; Matthew 27:61; Mark 15:47"> And the women that were come with him from Galilee, following after, saw the sepulchre, and how his body was laid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Luke 24:1; Mark 16:1; Deuteronomy 5:14"> And returning, they prepared spices and ointments; and on the sabbath day they rested, according to the commandment.</span>
