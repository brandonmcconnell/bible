<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 18:7; Romans 14:13; 1 Corinthians 11:19"> And he said to his disciples: It is impossible that scandals should not come: but woe to him through whom they come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 9:42; Matthew 18:14; Matthew 18:3–6"> It were better for him, that a millstone were hanged about his neck, and he cast into the sea, than that he should scandalize one of these little ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 19:17; Matthew 18:21; Matthew 18:15–17"> Take heed to yourselves. If thy brother sin against thee, reprove him: and if he do penance, forgive him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 3:12–13; Matthew 18:21–22; Romans 12:20"> And if he sin against thee seven times in a day, and seven times in a day be converted unto thee, saying, I repent; forgive him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 9:24; Hebrews 12:2; Philippians 4:13"> And the apostles said to the Lord: Increase our faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 17:20; Matthew 21:21; Mark 9:23"> And the Lord said: If you had faith like to a grain of mustard seed, you might say to this mulberry tree, Be thou rooted up, and be thou transplanted into the sea: and it would obey you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 12:11; Luke 13:15; Luke 14:5"> But which of you having a servant ploughing, or feeding cattle, will say to him, when he is come from the field: Immediately go, sit down to meat:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 12:37; Genesis 43:16; 2 Samuel 12:20"> And will not rather say to him: Make ready my supper, and gird thyself, and serve me, whilst I eat and drink, and afterwards thou shalt eat and drink?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Doth he thank that servant, for doing the things which he commanded him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:16–17; Job 22:2–3; 1 Peter 5:5–6"> I think not. So you also, when you shall have done all these things that are commanded you, say: We are unprofitable servants; we have done that which we ought to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 9:51–52; John 4:3–4"> And it came to pass, as he was going to Jerusalem, he passed through the midst of Samaria and Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 13:45–46; Luke 5:12; 2 Kings 7:3"> And as he entered into a certain town, there met him ten men that were lepers, who stood afar off;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Matthew 20:30–31; Matthew 9:27"> And lifted up their voice, saying: Jesus, master, have mercy on us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 5:14; Leviticus 13:1–46; 2 Kings 5:14"> Whom when he saw, he said: Go, shew yourselves to the priests. And it came to pass, as they went, they were made clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 30:11–12; Psalms 103:1–4; Psalms 118:18–19"> And one of them, when he saw that he was made clean, went back, with a loud voice glorifying God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 8:5–25; Matthew 10:5; Revelation 19:10"> And he fell on his face before his feet, giving thanks: and this was a Samaritan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 1:21; Psalms 106:13; Genesis 3:9"> And Jesus answering, said, Were not ten made clean? and where are the nine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 50:23; Matthew 15:24–28; Psalms 106:13"> There is no one found to return and give glory to God, but this stranger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 7:50; Luke 8:48; Mark 10:52"> And he said to him: Arise, go thy way; for thy faith hath made thee whole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 18:36; Luke 19:11; Daniel 2:44"> And being asked by the Pharisees, when the kingdom of God should come? he answered them, and said: The kingdom of God cometh not with observation:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 12:28; Romans 14:17; Mark 13:21"> Neither shall they say: Behold here, or behold there. For lo, the kingdom of God is within you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 5:35; Matthew 9:15; John 8:21–24"> And he said to his disciples: The days will come, when you shall desire to see one day of the Son of man; and you shall not see it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 21:8; Matthew 24:23–26; Luke 17:21"> And they will say to you: See here, and see there. Go ye not after, nor follow them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 24:27; Matthew 24:30; Malachi 4:1–2"> For as the lightening that lighteneth from under heaven, shineth unto the parts that are under heaven, so shall the Son of man be in his day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Luke 9:22; Mark 8:31"> But first he must suffer many things, and be rejected by this generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 24:37–39; Luke 17:24; Genesis 7:7–23"> And as it came to pass in the days of Noe, so shall it be also in the days of the Son of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 12:19–20; 1 Thessalonians 5:1–3; Luke 16:19–23"> They did eat and drink, they married wives, and were given in marriage, until the day that Noe entered into the ark: and the flood came and destroyed them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:49–50; Genesis 18:20–21; James 5:1–5"> Likewise as it came to pass, in the days of Lot: they did eat and drink, they bought and sold, they planted and built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Peter 2:6; Zephaniah 2:9; Jude 1:7"> And in the day that Lot went out of Sodom, it rained fire and brimstone from heaven, and destroyed them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:7; Revelation 1:7"> Even thus shall it be in the day when the Son of man shall be revealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 21:21; Mark 13:14–16; Jeremiah 45:5"> In that hour, he that shall be on the housetop, and his goods in the house, let him not go down to take them away: and he that shall be in the field, in like manner, let him not return back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 19:26; Hebrews 10:38–39; 2 Peter 2:18–22"> Remember Lot’s wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 8:35–37; Matthew 10:39; John 12:25"> Whosoever shall seek to save his life, shall lose it: and whosoever shall lose it, shall preserve it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 24:40–41; 2 Peter 2:9; Malachi 3:16–18"> I say to you: in that night there shall be two men in one bed; the one shall be taken, and the other shall be left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 24:41; Exodus 11:5; Judges 16:21"> Two women shall be grinding together: the one shall be taken, and the other shall be left: two men shall be in the field; the one shall be taken, and the other shall be keft.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> They answering, say to him: Where, Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 24:28; Job 39:29–30; Revelation 19:17–18"> Who said to them: Wheresoever the body shall be, thither will the eagles also be gathered together.</span>
