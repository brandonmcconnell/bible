<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:15; Matthew 9:10–13; Luke 5:29–32"> Now the publicans and sinners drew near unto him to hear him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 9:11; Luke 19:7; Luke 7:39"> And the Pharisees and the scribes murmured, saying: This man receiveth sinners, and eateth with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And he spoke to them this parable, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Ezekiel 34:11–12; Matthew 18:12–14"> What man of you that hath an hundred sheep: and if he shall lose one of them, doth he not leave the ninety-nine in the desert, and go after that which was lost, until he find it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Luke 19:9; Isaiah 46:3–4"> And when he hath found it, lay it upon his shoulders, rejoicing:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 15:10; Luke 15:24; 1 Peter 2:25"> And coming home, call together his friends and neighbours, saying to them: Rejoice with me, because I have found my sheep that was lost?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 5:32; Luke 15:10; Luke 15:32"> I say to you, that even so there shall be joy in heaven upon one sinner that doth penance, more than upon ninety-nine just who need not penance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 19:10; Ezekiel 34:12; John 10:16"> Or what woman having ten groats; if she lose one groat, doth not light a candle, and sweep the house, and seek diligently until she find it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 15:6–7"> And when she hath found it, call together her friends and neighbours, saying: Rejoice with me, because I have found the groat which I had lost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 15:7; 2 Corinthians 7:10; Ezekiel 33:11"> So I say to you, there shall be joy before the angels of God upon one sinner doing penance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 21:23–31"> And he said: A certain man had two sons:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:16–17; Mark 12:44; Luke 15:30"> And the younger of them said to his father: Father, give me the portion of substance that falleth to me. And he divided unto them his substance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 29:3; Jeremiah 2:5; Proverbs 21:17"> And not many days after, the younger son, gathering all together, went abroad into a far country: and there wasted his substance, living riotously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:11; Amos 8:9–12; Hosea 2:9–14"> And after he had spent all, there came a mighty famine in that country; and he began to be in want.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Titus 3:3; 2 Timothy 2:25–26; Isaiah 1:5"> And he went and cleaved to one of the citizens of that country. And he sent him into his farm to feed swine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 6:19–21; Lamentations 4:5; Hosea 12:1"> And he would fain have filled his belly with the husks the swine did eat; and no man gave unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:28; Ephesians 2:4–5; Luke 15:18–19"> And returning to himself, he said: How many hired servants in my father’s house abound with bread, and I here perish with hunger?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40; 1 John 1:8–10; Luke 15:21"> I will arise, and will go to my father, and say to him: Father, I have sinned against heaven, and before thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 42:6; Luke 5:8; Psalms 84:10"> I am not worthy to be called thy son: make me as one of thy hired servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 103:10–13; Psalms 86:5; Psalms 86:15"> And rising up he came to his father. And when he was yet a great way off, his father saw him, and was moved with compassion, and running to him fell upon his neck, and kissed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 51:4; Luke 15:18–19; Jeremiah 3:13"> And the son said to him: Father, I have sinned against heaven, and before thee, I am not now worthy to be called thy son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 41:42; Esther 8:2; Revelation 6:11"> And the father said to his servants: Bring forth quickly the first robe, and put it on him, and put a ring on his hand, and shoes on his feet:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 25:6; Matthew 22:2–14; Psalms 63:5"> And bring hither the fatted calf, and kill it, and let us eat and make merry:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 2:5; Ezekiel 34:16; Luke 19:10"> Because this my son was dead, and is come to life again: was lost, and is found. And they began to be merry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 150:4; Jeremiah 31:4; Luke 15:11–12"> Now his elder son was in the field, and when he came and drew nigh to the house, he heard music and dancing:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And he called one of the servants, and asked what these things meant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 9:17; Philemon 1:16; Acts 22:13"> And he said to him: Thy brother is come, and thy father hath killed the fatted calf, because he hath received him safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jonah 4:9; 1 Samuel 18:8; Jonah 4:1–4"> And he was angry, and would not go in. His father therefore coming out began to entreat him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 18:11–12; Zechariah 7:3; Isaiah 65:5"> And he answering, said to his father: Behold, for so many years do I serve thee, and I have never transgressed thy commandment, and yet thou hast never given me a kid to make merry with my friends:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 29:3; Luke 15:12–13; Luke 15:32"> But as soon as this thy son is come, who hath devoured his substance with harlots, thou hast killed for him the fatted calf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 7:27–28; Romans 9:4; Romans 11:1"> But he said to him: Son, thou art always with me, and all I have is thine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ephesians 2:1–10; Luke 15:24; Isaiah 35:10"> But it was fit that we should make merry and be glad, for this thy brother was dead and is come to life again; he was lost, and is found.</span>
