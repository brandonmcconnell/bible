<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 21:23–27; Luke 19:47–48; Acts 6:12"> And it came to pass, that on one of the days, as he was teaching the people in the temple, and preaching the gospel, the chief priests and the scribes, with the ancients, met together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 2:14; Mark 11:28–33; John 2:18"> And spoke to him, saying: Tell us, by what authority dost thou these things? or, Who is he that hath given thee this authority?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 15:2–3; Luke 22:68; Colossians 4:6"> And Jesus answering, said to them: I will also ask you one thing. Answer me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 15:18; Matthew 11:7–19; Matthew 17:11–12"> The baptism of John, was it from heaven, or of men?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 1:34; John 1:15–18; John 1:30"> But they thought within themselves, saying: If we shall say, From heaven: he will say: Why then did you not believe him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 21:26; Matthew 11:9; John 10:41"> But if we say, Of men, the whole people will stone us: for they are persuaded that John was a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 29:14; 2 Peter 3:3; Isaiah 41:28"> And they answered, that they knew not whence it was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 26:4–5; Matthew 21:27; Mark 11:33"> And Jesus said to them: Neither do I tell thee by what authority I do these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 21:33–46; Mark 12:1–12; Isaiah 5:1–7"> And he began to speak to the people this parable: A certain man planted a vineyard, and let it out to husbandmen: and he was abroad for a long time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Nehemiah 9:26; Matthew 21:34–36"> And at the season he sent a servant to the husbandmen, that they should give him of the fruit of the vineyard. Who, beating him, sent him away empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 10:1; Hebrews 11:36–37; 1 Thessalonians 2:2"> And again he sent another servant. But they beat him also, and treating him reproachfully, sent him away empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And again he sent the third: and they wounded him also, and cast him out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 3:17; John 3:16–17; Jeremiah 36:7"> Then the lord of the vineyard said: What shall I do? I will send my beloved son: it may be, when they see him, they will reverence him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 8:17; Hebrews 1:2; Acts 2:23"> Whom when the husbandmen saw, they thought within themselves, saying: This is the heir, let us kill him, that the inheritance may be ours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 13:12; Mark 12:6–9; Matthew 21:37–40"> So casting him out of the vineyard, they killed him. What therefore will the lord of the vineyard do to them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:27; Acts 13:46; Matthew 21:41"> He will come, and will destroy these husbandmen, and will give the vineyard to others. Which they hearing, said to him: God forbid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 118:22; Isaiah 28:16; Acts 4:11"> But he looking on them, said: What is this then that is written, The stone, which the builders rejected, the same is become the head of the corner?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14–15; Zechariah 12:3; Daniel 2:44–45"> Whosoever shall fall upon that stone, shall be bruised: and upon whomsoever it shall fall, it will grind him to powder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 19:47–48; Luke 20:14; Matthew 21:45–46"> And the chief priests and the scribes sought to lay hands on him the same hour: but they feared the people, for they knew that he spoke this parable to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 11:54; Matthew 22:15–22; Matthew 27:2"> And being upon the watch, they sent spies, who should feign themselves just, that they might take hold of him in his words, that they might deliver him up to the authority and power of the governor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4–5; Matthew 26:49–50; Matthew 22:16"> And they asked him, saying: Master, we know that thou speakest and teachest rightly: and thou dost not respect any person, but teachest the way of God in truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:37; Mark 12:14–17; Acts 5:37"> Is it lawful for us to give tribute to Caesar, or no?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 22:18; 1 Corinthians 3:19; Luke 5:22"> But he considering their guile, said to them: Why tempt you me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 18:28; Acts 11:28; Acts 26:32"> Shew me a penny. Whose image and inscription hath it? They answering, said to him, Caesar’s.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 13:6–7; Mark 12:17; 1 Peter 2:13–17"> And he said to them: Render therefore to Caesar the things that are Caesar’s: and to God the things that are God’s.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 20:20; Proverbs 26:4–5; Titus 1:10"> And they could not reprehend his word before the people: and wondering at his answer, they held their peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 4:1–2; 1 Corinthians 15:12; Mark 12:18–27"> And there came to him some of the Sadducees, who deny that there is any resurrection, and they asked him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Genesis 38:8; Genesis 38:11"> Saying: Master, Moses wrote unto us, If any man’s brother die, having a wife, and he leave no children, that his brother should take her to wife, and raise up seed unto his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 20:20; Jeremiah 22:30"> There were therefore seven brethren: and the first took a wife, and died without children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And the next took her to wife, and he also died childless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And the third took her. And in like manner all the seven, and they left no children, and died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:4; Ecclesiastes 9:5; Hebrews 9:27"> Last of all the woman died also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 22:24–28; Mark 12:19–23"> In the resurrection therefore, whose wife of them shall she be? For all the seven had her to wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 16:8; Luke 17:27; Hebrews 13:4"> And Jesus said to them: The children of this world marry, and are given in marriage:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:5; John 5:29; Daniel 12:2–3"> But they that shall be accounted worthy of that world, and of the resurrection from the dead, shall neither be married, nor take wives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; Philippians 3:21; Matthew 22:30"> Neither can they die any more: for they are equal to the angels, and are the children of God, being the children of the resurrection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 3:2–6; Mark 12:26–27; Acts 7:30–32"> Now that the dead rise again, Moses also shewed, at the bush, when he called the Lord, The God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 11:25–26; Colossians 3:3–4; Romans 6:10–11"> For he is not the God of the dead, but of the living: for all live to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 23:9; Mark 12:28–34; Matthew 22:34–40"> And some of the scribes answering, said to him: Master, thou hast said well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 22:46; Mark 12:34; Proverbs 26:5"> And after that they durst not ask him any more questions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 12:35–37; Matthew 1:1; Matthew 22:41–45"> But he said to them: How say they that Christ is the son of David?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Acts 2:34–35; Hebrews 1:13"> And David himself saith in the book of Psalms: The Lord said to my Lord, sit thou on my right hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 19:27; Psalms 110:1; Revelation 19:14–21"> Till I make thy enemies thy footstool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Revelation 22:16; Luke 1:31–35; Matthew 1:23"> David then calleth him Lord: and how is he his son?</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 15:10; Mark 12:38–39; Mark 8:34"> And in the hearing of all the people, he said to his disciples:</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 11:43; Mark 12:38–39; Luke 14:7"> Beware of the scribes, who desire to walk in long robes, and love salutations in the marketplace, and the first chairs in the synagogues, and the chief rooms at feasts:</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 12:40; James 3:1; Titus 1:16"> Who devour the houses of widows, feigning long prayer. These shall receive greater damnation.</span>
