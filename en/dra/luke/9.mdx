<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 14:12; Luke 10:19; Matthew 16:19"> Then calling together the twelve apostles, he gave them power and authority over all devils, and to cure diseases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 10:7–8; Luke 10:9; Matthew 3:2"> And he sent them to preach the kingdom of God, and to heal the sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 22:35; Psalms 37:3; Luke 10:4–12"> And he said to them: Take nothing for your journey; neither staff, nor scrip, nor bread, nor money; neither have two coats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:11; Mark 6:10; Luke 10:5–8"> And whatsoever house you shall enter into, abide there, and depart not from thence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 13:51; Acts 18:6; Mark 6:11"> And whosoever will not receive you, when ye go out of that city, shake off even the dust of your feet, for a testimony against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 4:30; Mark 16:20; Acts 5:15"> And going out, they went about through the towns, preaching the gospel, and healing every where.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 6:14–28; Matthew 14:1–12; Luke 3:1"> Now Herod, the tetrarch, heard of all things that were done by him; and he was in a doubt, because it was said</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:21; Luke 9:19; Mark 6:15"> By some, that John was risen from the dead: but by other some, that Elias had appeared; and by others, that one of the old prophets was risen again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 23:8; Luke 13:31–32"> And Herod said: John I have beheaded; but who is this of whom I hear such things? And he sought to see him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 11:21; Hebrews 13:17; Matthew 14:13–21"> And the apostles, when they were returned, told him all they had done. And taking them, he went aside into a desert place, apart, which belongeth to Bethsaida.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 9:2; Luke 5:31; Hebrews 4:16"> Which when the people knew, they followed him; and he received them, and spoke to them of the kingdom of God, and healed them who had need of healing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 6:1; John 6:5–15; Hosea 13:5"> Now the day began to decline. And the twelve came and said to him: Send away the multitude, that going into the towns and villages round about, they may lodge and get victuals; for we are here in a desert place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 11:24–25; John 6:5–9; 2 Kings 4:42–43"> But he said to them: Give you them to eat. And they said: We have no more than five loaves and two fishes; unless perhaps we should go and buy food for all this multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 8:6; 1 Corinthians 14:40; Mark 6:39–40"> Now there were about five thousand men. And he said to his disciples: Make them sit down by fifties in a company.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And they did so; and made them all sit down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 14:19; 1 Timothy 4:4–5; Matthew 15:36"> And taking the five loaves and the two fishes, he looked up to heaven, and blessed them; and he broke, and distributed to his disciples, to set before the multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 4:44; Matthew 15:37–38; John 6:11–13"> And they did all eat, and were filled. And there were taken up of fragments that remained to them, twelve baskets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 16:13–16; Luke 11:1; Matthew 26:36"> And it came to pass, as he was alone praying, his disciples also were with him: and he asked them, saying: Whom do the people say that I am?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Luke 9:7–8; Matthew 14:2"> But they answered, and said: John the Baptist; but some say Elias; and others say that one of the former prophets is risen again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 16:15–17; John 4:29; John 7:41"> And he said to them: But whom do you say that I am? Simon Peter answering, said: The Christ of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 16:20; Matthew 17:9; Mark 8:30–31"> But he strictly charging them, commanded they should tell this to no man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1–12; Luke 24:7; Luke 18:31–34"> Saying: The Son of man must suffer many things, and be rejected by the ancients and chief priests and scribes, and be killed, and the third day rise again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 10:38–39; Luke 14:26–27; Romans 8:13"> And he said to all: If any man will come after me, let him deny himself, and take up his cross daily, and follow me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 17:33; John 12:25; Acts 20:23–24"> For whosoever will save his life, shall lose it; for he that shall lose his life for my sake, shall save it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 8:36; Matthew 16:26; Psalms 49:6–8"> For what is a man advantaged, if he gain the whole world, and lose himself, and cast away himself?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 10:32–33; 2 Timothy 2:12; Luke 12:8–9"> For he that shall be ashamed of me and of my words, of him the Son of man shall be ashamed, when he shall come in his majesty, and that of his Father, and of the holy angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 16:28; Mark 9:1; John 21:22–23"> But I tell you of a truth: There are some standing here that shall not taste death, till they see the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 17:1–13; Matthew 26:37–39; Mark 14:33–36"> And it came to pass about eight days after these words, that he took Peter, and James, and John, and went up into a mountain to pray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 17:2; Mark 9:2–3; Exodus 34:29–35"> And whilst he prayed, the shape of his countenance was altered, and his raiment became white and glittering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 3:3–6; 2 Corinthians 3:7–11; Luke 1:17"> And behold two men were talking with him. And they were Moses and Elias,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Philippians 3:21; 1 Peter 1:11–12; 1 Peter 5:10"> Appearing in majesty. And they spoke of his decease that he should accomplish in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Daniel 8:18; John 1:14; Daniel 10:9"> But Peter and they that were with him were heavy with sleep. And waking, they saw his glory, and the two men that stood with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 9:5–6; Mark 10:38; Psalms 4:6–7"> And it came to pass, that as they were departing from him, Peter saith to Jesus: Master, it is good for us to be here; and let us make three tabernacles, one for thee, and one for Moses, and one for Elias; not knowing what he said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 40:34–38; Matthew 17:5–7; Judges 13:22"> And as he spoke these things, there came a cloud, and overshadowed them; and they were afraid, when they entered into the cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 3:22; Acts 3:22–23; Matthew 3:17"> And a voice came out of the cloud, saying: This is my beloved Son; hear him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 17:9; Mark 9:9–10; Mark 9:6"> And whilst the voice was uttered, Jesus was found alone. And they held their peace, and told no man in those days any of these things which they had seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 17:14–20; Mark 9:14–29"> And it came to pass the day following, when they came down from the mountain, there met him a great multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Zechariah 12:10; John 4:47; Luke 7:12"> And behold a man among the crowd cried out, saying: Master, I beseech thee, look upon my son, because he is my only one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Mark 9:20; Luke 8:29; Luke 4:35"> And lo, a spirit seizeth him, and he suddenly crieth out, and he throweth him down and teareth him, so that he foameth; and bruising him, he hardly departeth from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 9:1; Acts 19:13–16; Luke 10:17–19"> And I desired thy disciples to cast him out, and they could not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:5; Romans 2:4; John 20:27"> And Jesus answering, said: O faithless and perverse generation, how long shall I be with you, and suffer you? Bring hither thy son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Mark 9:26–27; Luke 7:15; Revelation 12:12"> And as he was coming to him, the devil threw him down, and tore him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 9:43–45; Luke 5:26; Matthew 17:22"> And Jesus rebuked the unclean spirit, and cured the boy, and restored him to his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Luke 9:22; Acts 2:23; Luke 2:19"> And all were astonished at the mighty power of God. But while all wondered at all the things he did, he said to his disciples: Lay you up in your hearts these words, for it shall come to pass, that the Son of man shall be delivered into the hands of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Luke 18:34; Luke 2:50; John 12:16"> But they understood not this word; and it was hid from them, so that they perceived it not. And they were afraid to ask him concerning this word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Matthew 18:1–5; Mark 9:33–37; Matthew 20:20–22"> And there entered a thought into them, which of them should be greater.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 139:23; John 16:30; Luke 5:22"> But Jesus seeing the thoughts of their heart, took a child and set him by him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Peter 5:6; Luke 14:11; Mark 9:37"> And said to them: Whosoever shall receive this child in my name, receiveth me; and whosoever shall receive me, receiveth him that sent me. For he that is the lesser among you all, he is the greater.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Mark 9:38–40; Numbers 11:27–29; Mark 10:13–14"> And John, answering, said: Master, we saw a certain man casting out devils in thy name, and we forbade him, because he followeth not with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 12:30; Luke 11:23; 1 Corinthians 12:3"> And Jesus said to him: Forbid him not; for he that is not against you, is for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Isaiah 50:5–9; Mark 16:19; Luke 13:22"> And it came to pass, when the days of his assumption were accomplishing, that he steadfastly set his face to go to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 10:5; Luke 17:16; Luke 10:1"> And he sent messengers before his face; and going, they entered into a city of the Samaritans, to prepare for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="John 4:9; John 4:40–42; Luke 9:48"> And they received him not, because his face was of one going to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="2 Kings 1:10–14; James 3:14–18; James 1:19–20"> And when his disciples James and John had seen this, they said: Lord, wilt thou that we command fire to come down from heaven, and consume them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; 1 Peter 3:9; Jeremiah 17:9"> And turning, he rebuked them, saying: You know not of what spirit you are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; Romans 12:21; John 3:17"> The Son of man came not to destroy souls, but to save. And they went into another town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Matthew 8:19–22; John 13:37; Luke 9:57–60"> And it came to pass, as they walked in the way, that a certain man said to him: I will follow thee withersoever thou goest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Matthew 8:20; Luke 14:26–33; James 2:5"> Jesus said to him: The foxes have holes, and the birds of the air nests; but the Son of man hath not where to lay his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Matthew 16:24; Matthew 6:33; 1 Kings 19:20"> But he said to another: Follow me. And he said: Lord, suffer me first to go, and to bury my father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:3–4; 1 Timothy 5:6; John 21:15–17"> And Jesus said to him: Let the dead bury their dead: but go thou, and preach the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Luke 14:26; Luke 14:18–20; 1 Kings 19:20"> And another said: I will follow thee, Lord; but let me first take my leave of them that are at my house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Philippians 3:13; Hebrews 10:38; 2 Peter 2:20–22"> Jesus said to him: No man putting his hand to the plough, and looking back, is fit for the kingdom of God.</span>
