<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 3:2; Luke 20:20; Psalms 62:4"> And it came to pass, when Jesus went into the house of one of the chief of the Pharisees, on the sabbath day, to eat bread, that they watched him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And behold, there was a certain man before him that had the dropsy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 12:10; Mark 3:4; Luke 13:14–16"> And Jesus answering, spoke to the lawyers and Pharisees, saying: Is it lawful to heal on the sabbath day?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 22:46; Matthew 21:25–27"> But they held their peace. But he taking him, healed him, and sent him away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 13:15; Exodus 23:4–5; Matthew 12:11–12"> And answering them, he said: Which of you shall have an ass or an ox fall into a pit, and will not immediately draw him out, on the sabbath day?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 20:40; Luke 21:15; Acts 6:10"> And they could not answer him to these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Philippians 2:3; Luke 11:43; Matthew 23:6"> And he spoke a parable also to them that were invited, marking how they chose the first seats at the table, saying to them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 25:6–7"> When thou art invited to a wedding, sit not down in the first place, lest perhaps one more honourable than thou be invited by him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 16:18; Proverbs 11:2; Proverbs 3:35"> And he that invited thee and him, come and say to thee, Give this man place: and then thou begin with shame to take the lowest place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 25:6–7; Proverbs 15:33; 1 Samuel 15:17"> But when thou art invited, go, sit down in the lowest place; that when he who invited thee, cometh, he may say to thee: Friend, go up higher. Then shalt thou have glory before them that sit at table with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 18:14; Matthew 23:12; Proverbs 29:23"> Because every one that exalteth himself, shall be humbled; and he that humbleth himself, shall be exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 5:46; James 2:1–6; Luke 6:32–36"> And he said to him also that had invited him: When thou makest a dinner or a supper, call not thy friends, nor thy brethren, nor thy kinsmen, nor thy neighbours who are rich; lest perhaps they also invite thee again, and a recompense be made to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 14:21; Isaiah 58:10; Proverbs 14:31"> But when thou makest a feast, call the poor, the maimed, the lame, and the blind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Acts 24:15; John 5:29"> And thou shalt be blessed, because they have not wherewith to make thee recompense: for recompense shall be made thee at the resurrection of the just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 19:9; Luke 22:30; Luke 13:29"> When one of them that sat at table with him, had heard these things, he said to him: Blessed is he that shall eat bread in the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 22:2–14; Revelation 22:17; Isaiah 55:1–7"> But he said to him: A certain man made a great supper, and invited many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 11:27–29; Matthew 10:1–4; Matthew 22:3–4"> And he sent his servant at the hour of supper to say to them that were invited, that they should come, for now all things are ready.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Luke 17:26–31; Luke 18:24"> And they began all at once to make excuse. The first said to him: I have bought a farm, and I must needs go out and see it: I pray thee, hold me excused.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And another said: I have bought five yoke of oxen, and I go to try them: I pray thee, hold me excused.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:5; 1 Corinthians 7:33; Luke 14:26–28"> And another said: I have married a wife, and therefore I cannot come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 14:13; Matthew 11:5; Luke 14:24"> And the servant returning, told these things to his lord. Then the master of the house, being angry, said to his servant: Go out quickly into the streets and lanes of the city, and bring in hither the poor, and the feeble, and the blind, and the lame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 14:2; Ephesians 3:8; 1 Timothy 2:5–6"> And the servant said: Lord, it is done as thou hast commanded, and yet there is room.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 22:9–10; Colossians 1:28; Acts 13:47–48"> And the Lord said to the servant: Go out into the highways and hedges, and compel them to come in, that my house may be filled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 21:43; Acts 13:46; John 8:24"> But I say unto you, that none of those men that were invited, shall taste of my supper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 6:24–27; Luke 12:1"> And there went great multitudes with him. And turning, he said to them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; John 12:25; Philippians 3:8"> If any man come to me, and hate not his father, and mother, and wife, and children, and brethren, and sisters, yea and his own life also, he cannot be my disciple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 10:38; Luke 9:23–25; Mark 8:34–37"> And whosoever doth not carry his cross and come after me, cannot be my disciple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 24:27; Matthew 10:22; Joshua 24:19–24"> For which of you having a mind to build a tower, doth not first sit down, and reckon the charges that are necessary, whether he have wherewithal to finish it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Lest, after he hath laid the foundation, and is not able to finish it, all that see it begin to mock him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 6:11; 2 John 1:8; 1 Corinthians 3:11–14"> Saying: This man began to build, and was not able to finish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 20:18; Proverbs 25:8; 2 Kings 18:20–22"> Or what king, about to go to make war against another king, doth not first sit down, and think whether he be able, with ten thousand, to meet him that, with twenty thousand, cometh against him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 12:20; James 4:6–10; Luke 12:58"> Or else, whilst the other is yet afar off, sending an embassy, he desireth conditions of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Philippians 3:7–8; 1 John 2:15–16; Luke 5:28"> So likewise every one of you that doth not renounce all that he possesseth, cannot be my disciple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 5:13; Colossians 4:6; Mark 9:49–50"> Salt is good. But if the salt shall lose its savour, wherewith shall it be seasoned?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 11:15; Luke 9:44; Revelation 2:11"> It is neither profitable for the land nor for the dunghill, but shall be cast out. He that hath ears to hear, let him hear.</span>
