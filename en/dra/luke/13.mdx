<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17–18; Lamentations 2:20; Ezekiel 9:5–7"> And there were present, at that very time, some that told him of the Galileans, whose blood Pilate had mingled with their sacrifices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 9:2; Acts 28:4; Job 22:5–16"> And he answering, said to them: Think you that these Galileans were sinners above all the men of Galilee, because they suffered such things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 3:19; Matthew 3:2; Acts 2:38–40"> No, I say to you: but unless you shall do penance, you shall all likewise perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:15; Job 1:19; John 9:11"> Or those eighteen upon whom the tower fell in Siloe, and slew them: think you, that they also were debtors above all the men that dwelt in Jerusalem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:30; Luke 13:3; Isaiah 28:10–13"> No, I say to you; but except you do penance, you shall all likewise perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 15:16; Isaiah 5:1–4; Galatians 5:22"> He spoke also this parable: A certain man had a fig tree planted in his vineyard, and he came seeking fruit on it, and found none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:19; Luke 3:9; John 15:6"> And he said to the dresser of the vineyard: Behold, for these three years I come seeking fruit on this fig tree, and I find none. Cut it done therefore: why cumbereth it the ground?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Peter 3:9; Joel 2:17; Romans 10:1"> But he answering, said to him: Lord, let it alone this year also, until I dig about it, and dung it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 15:2; 1 Thessalonians 2:15; Hebrews 6:8"> And if happily it bear fruit: but if not, then after that thou shalt cut it down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Luke 4:44; Luke 4:15–16"> And he was teaching in their synagogue on their sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 8:2; Luke 8:27; Luke 13:16"> And behold there was a woman, who had a spirit of infirmity eighteen years: and she was bowed together, neither could she look upwards at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 107:20; Matthew 8:16; Luke 6:8–10"> Whom when Jesus saw, he called her unto him, and said to her: Woman, thou art delivered from thy infirmity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 107:20–22; Mark 16:18; Mark 5:23"> And he laid his hands upon her, and immediately she was made straight, and glorified God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 20:9; Matthew 12:2; John 9:14–16"> And the ruler of the synagogue (being angry that Jesus had healed on the sabbath) answering, said to the multitude: Six days there are wherein you ought to work. In them therefore come, and be healed; and not on the sabbath day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 14:5; John 7:21–24; Acts 13:9–10"> And the Lord answering him, said: Ye hypocrites, doth not every one of you, on the sabbath day, loose his ox or his ass from the manger, and lead them to water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 2:27; 2 Timothy 2:26; Luke 19:9"> And ought not this daughter of Abraham, whom Satan hath bound, lo, these eighteen years, be loosed from this bond on the sabbath day?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 3:16; Psalms 132:18; Isaiah 45:24"> And when he said these things, all his adversaries were ashamed: and all the people rejoiced for all the things that were gloriously done by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 4:30–34; Luke 17:21; Matthew 13:31–32"> He said therefore: To what is the kingdom of God like, and whereunto shall I resemble it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 4:12; Daniel 4:21; Daniel 2:44–45"> It is like to a grain of mustard seed, which a man took and cast into his garden, and it grew and became a great tree, and the birds of the air lodged in the branches thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And again he said: Whereunto shall I esteem the kingdom of God to be like?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 13:33; 1 Corinthians 5:6; 1 Thessalonians 5:23–24"> It is like to leaven, which a woman took and hid in three measures of meal, till the whole was leavened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 9:51; Matthew 9:35; Mark 10:32–34"> And he went through the cities and towns teaching, and making his journey to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 7:14; Matthew 22:14; John 21:21–22"> And a certain man said to him: Lord, are they few that are saved? But he said to them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 7:13–14; 2 Peter 1:10; 1 Corinthians 9:24–27"> Strive to enter by the narrow gate; for many, I say to you, shall seek to enter, and shall not be able.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 25:10–12; Luke 13:27; Matthew 7:21–23"> But when the master of the house shall be gone in, and shall shut the door, you shall begin to stand without, and knock at the door, saying: Lord, open to us. And he answering, shall say to you: I know you not, whence you are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 58:2; Titus 1:16; 2 Timothy 3:5"> Then you shall begin to say: We have eaten and drunk in thy presence, and thou hast taught in our streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 6:8; Matthew 25:41; Matthew 7:22–23"> And he shall say to you: I know you not, whence you are: depart from me, all ye workers of iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 8:11–12; Matthew 22:13; Matthew 25:30"> There shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth, when you shall see Abraham and Isaac and Jacob, and all the prophets, in the kingdom of God, and you yourselves thrust out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 43:6; Isaiah 66:18–20; Isaiah 49:6"> And there shall come from the east and the west, and the north and the south; and shall sit down in the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 19:30; Mark 10:31; Matthew 20:16"> And behold, they are last that shall be first; and they are first that shall be last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 6:9–11; Psalms 11:1–2; Matthew 14:1"> The same day, there came some of the Pharisees, saying to him: Depart, and get thee hence, for Herod hath a mind to kill thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 2:10; Hebrews 5:9; John 17:4–5"> And he said to them: Go and tell that fox, Behold, I cast out devils, and do cures today and tomorrow, and the third day I am consummated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 9:53; Matthew 21:11; Acts 13:27"> Nevertheless I must walk today and tomorrow, and the day following, because it cannot be that a prophet perish, out of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 23:37–39; Deuteronomy 32:11–12; Acts 7:52"> Jerusalem, Jerusalem, that killest the prophets, and stonest them that are sent to thee, how often would I have gathered thy children as the bird doth her brood under her wings, and thou wouldest not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 118:26; Mark 11:9–10; Matthew 21:9"> Behold your house shall be left to you desolate. And I say to you, that you shall not see me till the time come, when you shall say: Blessed is he that cometh in the name of the Lord.</span>
