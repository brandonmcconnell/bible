<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 20:1–2; Mark 16:1–8; Matthew 28:1–8"> And on the first day of the week, very early in the morning, they came to the sepulchre, bringing the spices which they had prepared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 16:3–4; Matthew 27:60–66; Mark 15:46–47"> And they found the stone rolled back from the sepulchre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 24:23; John 20:6–7; Matthew 16:5"> And going in, they found not the body of the Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 16:5; John 20:11–12; Acts 1:10"> And it came to pass, as they were astonished in their mind at this, behold, two men stood by them, in shining apparel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 16:5–6; Matthew 28:3–5; Revelation 1:18"> And as they were afraid, and bowed down their countenance towards the ground, they said unto them: Why seek you the living with the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 17:22–23; Luke 9:22; Mark 8:31"> He is not here, but is risen. Remember how he spoke unto you, when he was in Galilee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 16:21"> Saying: The Son of man must be delivered into the hands of sinful men, and be crucified, and the third day rise again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 12:16; John 14:26; John 2:19–22"> And they remembered his words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 24:22–24; Mark 16:7–8; Matthew 28:7–8"> And going back from the sepulchre, they told all these things to the eleven, and to all the rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 27:56; John 20:11–18; Luke 8:2–3"> And it was Mary Magdalen, and Joanna, and Mary of James, and the other women that were with them, who told these things to the apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 9:16; Mark 16:11; Genesis 19:14"> And these words seemed to them as idle tales; and they did not believe them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 20:3–10"> But Peter rising up, ran to the sepulchre, and stooping down, he saw the linen cloths laid by themselves; and went away wondering in himself at that which was come to pass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 24:18; Mark 16:12–13"> And behold, two of them went, the same day, to a town which was sixty furlongs from Jerusalem, named Emmaus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 6:45; Deuteronomy 6:7; Malachi 3:6"> And they talked together of all these things which had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:20; John 14:18–19; Luke 24:36"> And it came to pass, that while they talked and reasoned with themselves, Jesus himself also drawing near, went with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 20:14; John 21:4; Luke 24:31"> But their eyes were held, that they should not know him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 16:20–22; John 16:6; Ezekiel 9:4–6"> And he said to them: What are these discourses that you hold one with another as you walk, and are sad?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 19:25"> And the one of them, whose name was Cleophas, answering, said to him: Art thou only a stranger to Jerusalem, and hast not known the things that have been done there in these days?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 21:11; Acts 7:22; John 4:19"> To whom he said: What things? And they said: Concerning Jesus of Nazareth, who was a prophet, mighty in work and word before God and all the people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 3:13–15; Acts 4:8–10; Luke 23:13"> And how our chief priests and princes delivered him to be condemned to death, and crucified him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:68; 1 Peter 1:18–19; Luke 2:38"> But we hoped, that it was he that should have redeemed Israel: and now besides all this, today is the third day since these things were done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 16:9–10; Matthew 28:7–8; John 20:1–2"> Yea and certain women also of our company affrighted us, who before it was light, were at the sepulchre,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And not finding his body, came, saying, that they had also seen a vision of angels, who say that he is alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 24:12; John 20:1–10"> And some of our people went to the sepulchre, and found it so as the women had said, but him they found not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 7:18; Mark 9:19; Hebrews 5:11–12"> Then he said to them: O foolish, and slow of heart to believe in all things which the prophets have spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 1:11; Isaiah 53:1–12; Zechariah 13:7"> Ought not Christ to have suffered these things, and so to enter into his glory?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Luke 24:44; Deuteronomy 18:15"> And beginning at Moses and all the prophets, he expounded to them in all the scriptures, the things that were concerning him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 6:48; Genesis 19:2; Genesis 32:26"> And they drew night to the town, whither they were going: and he made as though he would go farther.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Kings 4:8; Luke 14:23; Genesis 19:3"> But they constrained him; saying: Stay with us, because it is towards evening, and the day is now far spent. And he went in with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 14:19; Luke 24:35; Matthew 15:36"> And it came to pass, whilst he was at table with them, he took bread, and blessed, and brake, and gave to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 24:16; John 8:59; Luke 4:30"> And their eyes were opened, and they knew him: and he vanished out of their sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 24:45; Jeremiah 23:29; Hebrews 4:12"> And they said one to the other: Was not our heart burning within us, whilst he spoke in this way, and opened to us the scriptures?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 16:13; John 20:19–26; Acts 1:14"> And rising up, the same hour, they went back to Jerusalem: and they found the eleven gathered together, and those that were staying with them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:5; Mark 16:7; Luke 22:54–62"> Saying: The Lord is risen indeed, and hath appeared to Simon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 16:12–13; Acts 2:42; Luke 24:30–31"> And they told what things were done in the way; and how they knew him in the breaking of the bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:16; Isaiah 57:18; Mark 16:14"> Now whilst they were speaking these things, Jesus stood in the midst of them, and saith to them: Peace be to you; it is I, fear not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:13; Matthew 14:26–27; Job 4:14–16"> But they being troubled and frightened, supposed that they saw a spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:14; Hebrews 4:13; Daniel 4:19"> And he said to them: Why are you troubled, and why do thoughts arise in your hearts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 20:27; 1 John 1:1; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> See my hands and feet, that it is I myself; handle, and see: for a spirit hath not flesh and bones, as you see me to have.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And when he had said this, he shewed them his hands and feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 21:5; Psalms 126:1–2; Genesis 45:26–28"> But while they yet believed not, and wondered for joy, he said: Have you any thing to eat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And they offered him a piece of a broiled fish, and a honeycomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Acts 10:41"> And when he had eaten before them, taking the remains, he gave to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Zechariah 12:10; Luke 24:26–27; Zechariah 9:9"> And he said to them: These are the words which I spoke to you, while I was yet with you, that all things must needs be fulfilled, which are written in the law of Moses, and in the prophets, and in the psalms, concerning me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Acts 16:14; 1 John 5:20; Psalms 119:18"> Then he opened their understanding, that they might understand the scriptures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2–12; Isaiah 50:6; Luke 24:44"> And he said to them: Thus it is written, and thus it behoved Christ to suffer, and to rise again from the dead, the third day:</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 5:31; Isaiah 49:6; Acts 2:38"> And that penance and remission of sins should be preached in his name, unto all nations, beginning at Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Acts 2:32; Acts 13:31"> And you are witnesses of these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Acts 1:4; Joel 2:28–32"> And I send the promise of my Father upon you: but stay you in the city till you be endued with power from on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Acts 1:12; Matthew 21:17; Mark 11:1"> And he led them out as far as Bethania: and lifting up his hands, he blessed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Acts 1:9; Mark 16:19; 2 Kings 2:11"> And it came to pass, whilst he blessed them, he departed from them, and was carried up to heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 28:9; John 16:22; Psalms 30:11"> And they adoring went back into Jerusalem with great joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Mark 16:20; Acts 2:46–47; Acts 5:41–42"> And they were always in the temple, praising and blessing God. Amen.</span>
