<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 13:1–7; 1 Peter 2:13–17; Ecclesiastes 10:4"> Admonish them to be subject to princes and powers, to obey at a word, to be ready to every good work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 4:31; 2 Timothy 2:24–25; Ephesians 4:2"> To speak evil of no man, not to be litigious, but gentle: shewing all mildness towards all men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ephesians 2:1–3; Romans 6:17; Colossians 1:21"> For we ourselves also were some time unwise, incredulous, erring, slaves to divers desires and pleasures, living in malice and envy, hateful, and hating one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4–10; Titus 2:10–11; 1 Timothy 4:10"> But when the goodness and kindness of God our Saviour appeared:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 2:8–9; 2 Timothy 1:9; 1 Corinthians 6:11"> Not by the works of justice, which we have done, but according to his mercy, he saved us, by the laver of regeneration, and renovation of the Holy Ghost;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 2:28; Romans 5:5; Acts 10:45"> Whom he hath poured forth upon us abundantly, through Jesus Christ our Saviour:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:17; Titus 2:11; 1 Corinthians 6:11"> That, being justified by his grace, we may be heirs, according to hope of life everlasting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Titus 3:14; Titus 1:9; Psalms 16:2–3"> It is a faithful saying: and these things I will have thee affirm constantly: that they, who believe in God, may be careful to excel in good works. These things are good and profitable unto men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:14; 2 Timothy 2:23; 1 Timothy 4:7"> But avoid foolish questions, and genealogies, and contentions, and strivings about the law. For they are unprofitable and vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 16:17; 2 John 1:10; Matthew 18:15–17"> A man that is a heretic, after the first and second admonition, avoid:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26; Luke 19:22; Matthew 25:26–28"> Knowing that he, that is such an one, is subverted, and sinneth, being condemned by his own judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:9; 2 Timothy 4:12; 1 Corinthians 16:8–9"> When I shall send to thee Artemas or Tychicus, make haste to come unto me to Nicopolis. For there I have determined to winter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 18:24; Matthew 22:35; 3 John 1:6–8"> Send forward Zenas, the lawyer, and Apollo, with care, that nothing be wanting to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; Titus 3:8; Matthew 7:19"> And let our men also learn to excel in good works for necessary uses: that they be not unfruitful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:5; Ephesians 6:23–24; 2 John 1:1–2"> All that are with me salute thee: salute them that love us in the faith. The grace of God be with you all. Amen.</span>
