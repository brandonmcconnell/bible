<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:24; 2 Timothy 1:8; Philippians 1:13–16"> For this cause, I Paul, the prisoner of Jesus Christ, for you Gentiles;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 1:25–27; 1 Timothy 1:11; 2 Timothy 1:11"> If yet you have heard of the dispensation of the grace of God which is given me towards you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 1:12; Romans 16:25; Ephesians 1:9–11"> How that, according to revelation, the mystery has been made known to me, as I have written above in a few words;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 4:3; 1 Corinthians 4:1; 2 Corinthians 11:6"> As you reading, may understand my knowledge in the mystery of Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 1:10–12; 2 Peter 3:2; Titus 1:1–3"> Which in other generations was not known to the sons of men, as it is now revealed to his holy apostles and prophets in the Spirit:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 3:26–29; 1 John 1:3; 1 John 2:25"> That the Gentiles should be fellow heirs, and of the same body, and co-partners of his promise in Christ Jesus, by the gospel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 1:19; Ephesians 3:20; Ephesians 3:2"> Of which I am made a minister, according to the gift of the grace of God, which is given to me according to the operation of his power:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Colossians 1:27; 1 Corinthians 15:9; Ephesians 2:7"> To me, the least of all the saints, is given this grace, to preach among the Gentiles, the unsearchable riches of Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 1:26; Matthew 13:35; 2 Timothy 1:9"> And to enlighten all men, that they may see what is the dispensation of the mystery which hath been hidden from eternity in God, who created all things:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 1:12; Ephesians 6:12; 1 Corinthians 2:7"> That the manifold wisdom of God may be made known to the principalities and powers in heavenly places through the church,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Ephesians 1:11; Ephesians 1:4"> According to the eternal purpose, which he made, in Christ Jesus our Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 2:18; Romans 5:2; Hebrews 4:14–16"> In whom we have boldness and access with confidence by the faith of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 1:24; 2 Thessalonians 3:13; 2 Corinthians 1:6"> Wherefore I pray you not to faint at my tribulations for you, which is your glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 95:6; Ephesians 1:16–19; Philippians 2:10"> For this cause I bow my knees to the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 1:10; Revelation 5:8–14; Ephesians 1:21"> Of whom all paternity in heaven and earth is named,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 1:11; Philippians 4:13; Isaiah 40:29–31"> That he would grant you, according to the riches of his glory, to be strengthened by his Spirit with might unto the inward man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Colossians 2:7; 1 John 4:16; John 14:23"> That Christ may dwell by faith in your hearts; that being rooted and founded in charity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 103:11–12; Job 11:7–9; Ephesians 1:18–23"> You may be able to comprehend, with all the saints, what is the breadth, and length, and height, and depth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 1:23; Colossians 2:9–10; Colossians 1:10"> To know also the charity of Christ, which surpasseth all knowledge, that you may be filled unto all the fulness of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:8; Romans 4:21; Jeremiah 32:27"> Now to him who is able to do all things more abundantly than we desire or understand, according to the power that worketh in us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jude 1:25; Romans 11:36; 2 Peter 3:18"> To him be glory in the church, and in Christ Jesus unto all generations, world without end. Amen.</span>
