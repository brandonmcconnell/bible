<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 18:4–6; Psalms 120:1; Psalms 34:6"> And Jonas prayed to the Lord his God out of the belly of the fish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 42:7; Lamentations 3:54; Psalms 69:1–2"> And he said: I cried out of my affliction to the Lord, and he heard me: I cried out of the belly of hell, and thou hast heard my voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 31:22; Psalms 5:7; 2 Chronicles 6:38"> And thou hast cast me forth into the deep in the heart of the sea, and a flood hath compassed me: all thy billows, and thy waves have passed over me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 69:1–2; Lamentations 3:54; Psalms 40:2"> And I said: I am cast away out of the sight of thy eyes: but yet I shall see thy holy temple again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 30:3; Psalms 16:10; Isaiah 38:17"> The waters compassed me about even to the soul: the deep hath closed me round about, the sea hath covered my head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 18:6; 2 Chronicles 30:27; Psalms 11:4"> I went down to the lowest parts of the mountains: the bars of the earth have shut me up for ever: and thou wilt bring up my life from corruption, O Lord my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 31:6; 2 Kings 17:15; Jeremiah 2:13"> When my soul was in distress within me, I remembered the Lord: that my prayer may come to thee, unto thy holy temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 3:8; Psalms 50:14; Ecclesiastes 5:4–5"> They that are vain observe vanities, forsake their own mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 8:26–27; Psalms 33:9; Genesis 1:3"> But I with the voice of praise will sacrifice to thee: I will pay whatsoever I have vowed for my salvation to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the Lord spoke to the fish: and it vomited out Jonas upon the dry land.</span>
