<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jonah 1:1; John 21:15–17"> And the word of the Lord came to Jonas the second time, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:7; Jeremiah 1:17; Jonah 3:3"> Arise, and go to Ninive the great city: and preach in it the preaching that I bid thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 21:28–29; Psalms 80:10; Genesis 22:3"> And Jonas arose, and went to Ninive, according to the word of the Lord: now Ninive was a great city of three days’ journey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:22; Jeremiah 18:7–10; 2 Kings 20:1"> And Jonas began to enter into the city one day’s journey: and he cried, and said: Yet forty days, and Ninive shall be destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 11:32; Matthew 12:41; Joel 2:12–17"> And the men of Ninive believed in God: and they proclaimed a fast, and put on sackcloth from the greatest to the least.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 4:6–10; Esther 4:1–4; Daniel 9:3"> And the word came to the king of Ninive; and he rose up out of his throne, and cast away his robe from him, and was clothed with sackcloth, and sat in ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:3; Joel 1:18; Joel 2:15–16"> And he caused it to be proclaimed and published in Ninive from the mouth of the king and of his princes, saying: Let neither men nor beasts, oxen nor sheep, taste any thing: let them not feed, nor drink water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jonah 1:6; Isaiah 55:6–7; Ezekiel 18:21–24"> And let men and beasts be covered with sackcloth, and cry to the Lord with all their strength, and let them turn every one from his evil way, and from the iniquity that is in their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:22; Joel 2:13–14; Psalms 106:45"> Who can tell if God will turn, and forgive: and will turn away from his fierce anger, and we shall not perish?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:8; 1 Kings 21:27–29; Luke 15:20"> And God saw their works, that they were turned from their evil way: and God had mercy with regard to the evil which he had said that he would do to them, and he did it not.</span>
