<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 136:25; Psalms 147:9; Job 38:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">He gave them power to shew his signs, and his wonders in the land of Cham.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 33:4; Ezekiel 37:9; Revelation 21:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their land brought forth frogs, in the inner chambers of their kings.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 1:31; 2 Timothy 4:18; Ephesians 3:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">He spoke, and there came divers sorts of flies and sciniphs in all their coasts.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 63:4; Psalms 146:2; Psalms 145:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">And he destroyed their vineyards and their fig trees: and he broke in pieces the trees of their coasts.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:17–18; Philippians 4:4; Psalms 1:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">He spoke, and the locust came, and the bruchus, of which there was no number.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 103:22–1; Psalms 37:38; Psalms 106:48"> <span className="pl pl-1">And they devoured all the grass in their land, and consumed all the fruit of their ground.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> <span className="pl pl-1">And he slew all the firstborn in their land: the firstfruits of all their labour.</span><br /></span>
