<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 15:26; Isaiah 53:5; Jeremiah 17:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Who coverest the higher rooms thereof with water. Who makest the clouds thy chariot: who walkest upon the wings of the winds.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 56:13; Psalms 71:23; Psalms 103:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Who makest thy angels spirits: and thy ministers a burning fire.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 40:31; Psalms 107:9; 1 Timothy 6:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Who hast founded the earth upon its own bases: it shall not be moved for ever and ever.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Psalms 12:5; Psalms 72:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">The deep like a garment is its clothing: above the mountains shall the waters stand.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 33:13; Psalms 147:19; Psalms 78:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">At thy rebuke they shall flee: at the voice of thy thunder they shall fear.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 86:15; Joel 2:13; Exodus 34:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">The mountains ascend, and the plains descend into the place which thou hast founded for them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 57:16; Psalms 30:5; Micah 7:18–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thou hast set a bound which they shall not pass over; neither shall they return to cover the earth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezra 9:13; Lamentations 3:22; Nehemiah 9:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thou sendest forth springs in the vales: between the midst of the hills the waters shall pass.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 57:10; Luke 1:50; Psalms 36:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">All the beasts of the field shall drink: the wild asses shall expect in their thirst.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; 1 John 1:7; Micah 7:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Over them the birds of the air shall dwell: from the midst of the rocks they shall give forth their voices.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 11:11–12; Hebrews 12:5–11; Luke 15:21–22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thou waterest the hills from thy upper rooms: the earth shall be filled with the fruit of thy works:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:7; Job 10:9; Psalms 78:38–39"> <span className="pl pl-1">Bringing forth grass for cattle, and herb for the service of men. That thou mayst bring bread out of the earth:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 1:24; James 1:10–11; Isaiah 51:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">And that wine may cheer the heart of man. That he may make the face cheerful with oil: and that bread may strengthen man’s heart.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 40:7; Job 20:9; Job 14:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">The trees of the field shall be filled, and the cedars of Libanus which he hath planted:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 100:5; Exodus 20:6; Ephesians 2:4–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">There the sparrows shall make their nests. The highest of them is the house of the heron.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:9; Psalms 132:12; Luke 1:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">The high hills are a refuge for the harts, the rock for the irchins.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 47:2; Philippians 2:9–10; Psalms 9:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">He hath made the moon for seasons: the sun knoweth his going down.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 1:14; Psalms 148:2; Luke 1:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thou hast appointed darkness, and it is night: in it shall all the beasts of the woods go about:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 22:19; Nehemiah 9:6; Luke 2:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">The young lions roaring after their prey, and seeking their meat from God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 49:13; Psalms 145:10; Isaiah 42:10–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">The sun ariseth, and they are gathered together: and they shall lie down in their dens.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Man shall go forth to his work, and to his labour until the evening.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> <span className="pl pl-1">How great are thy works, O Lord? thou hast made all things in wisdom: the earth is filled with thy riches.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> <span className="pl pl-1">So is this great sea, which stretcheth wide its arms: there are creeping things without number: Creatures little and great.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> <span className="pl pl-1">There the ships shall go. This sea dragon which thou hast formed to play therein.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> <span className="pl pl-1">All expect of thee that thou give them food in season.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> <span className="pl pl-1">What thou givest to them they shall gather up: when thou openest thy hand, they shall all be filled with good.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> <span className="pl pl-1">But if thou turnest away thy face, they shall be troubled: thou shalt take away their breath, and they shall fail, and shall return to their dust.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thou shalt send forth thy spirit, and they shall be created: and thou shalt renew the face of the earth.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> <span className="pl pl-1">He looketh upon the earth, and maketh it tremble: he toucheth the mountains, and they smoke.</span><br /></span>
