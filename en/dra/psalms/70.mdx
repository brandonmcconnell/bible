<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 40:13–17; Psalms 71:12; Psalms 143:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">A psalm for David. Of the sons of Jonadab, and the former captives. In thee, O Lord, I have hoped, let me never be put to confusion:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 35:4; Psalms 35:26; John 18:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Deliver me in thy justice, and rescue me. Incline thy ear unto me, and save me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 40:15; Acts 1:18; Ezekiel 26:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Be thou unto me a God, a protector, and a place of strength: that thou mayst make me safe. For thou art my firmament and my refuge.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25; Psalms 40:16; Psalms 35:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Deliver me, O my God, out of the hand of the sinner, and out of the hand of the transgressor of the law and of the unjust.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 40:17; Psalms 141:1; Psalms 69:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">For thou art my patience, O Lord: my hope, O Lord, from my youth;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> <span className="pl pl-1">By thee have I been confirmed from the womb: from my mother’s womb thou art my protector. Of thee shall I continually sing:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> <span className="pl pl-1">I run become unto many as a wonder, but thou art a strong helper.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let my mouth be filled with praise, that I may sing thy glory; thy greatness all the day long.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Cast me not off in the time of old age: when my strength shall fail, do not thou forsake me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> <span className="pl pl-1">For my enemies have spoken against me; and they that watched my soul have consulted together,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Saying: God hath forsaken him: pursue and take him, for there is none to deliver him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> <span className="pl pl-1">O God, be not thou far from me: O my God, make haste to my help.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let them be confounded and come to nothing that detract my soul; let them be covered with confusion and shame that seek my hurt.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I will always hope; and will add to all thy praise.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> <span className="pl pl-1">My mouth shall shew forth thy justice; thy salvation all the day long. Because I have not known learning,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will enter into the powers of the Lord: O Lord, I will be mindful of thy justice alone.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thou hast taught me, O God, from my youth: and till now I will declare thy wonderful works.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> <span className="pl pl-1">And unto old age and grey hairs: O God, forsake me not, Until I shew forth thy arm to all the generation that is to come: Thy power,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> <span className="pl pl-1">And thy justice, O God, even to the highest great things thou hast done: O God, who is like to thee?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> <span className="pl pl-1">How great troubles hast thou shewn me, many and grievous: and turning thou hast brought me to life, and hast brought me back again from the depths of the earth:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thou hast multiplied thy magnificence; and turning to me thou hast comforted me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I will also confess to thee thy truth with the instruments of psaltery: O God, I will sing to thee with the harp, thou holy one of Israel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> <span className="pl pl-1">My lips shall greatly rejoice, when I shall sing to thee; and my soul which thou hast redeemed.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yea and my tongue shall meditate on thy justice all the day; when they shall be confounded and put to shame that seek evils to me.</span></span>
