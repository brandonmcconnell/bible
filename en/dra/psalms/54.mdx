<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 52:9; Psalms 147:1; Psalms 7:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Fear and trembling are come upon me: and darkness hath covered me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 59:10; Psalms 92:11; Psalms 112:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I said: Who will give me wings like a dove, and I will fly and be at rest?</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> <span className="pl pl-1">I waited for him that hath saved me from pusillanimity of spirit, and a storm.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Cast down, O Lord, and divide their tongues; for I have seen iniquity and contradiction in the city.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Day and night shall iniquity surround it upon its walls: and in the midst thereof are labour,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> <span className="pl pl-1">And injustice. And usury and deceit have not departed from its streets.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> <span className="pl pl-1">For if my enemy had reviled me, I would verily have borne with it. And if he that hated me had spoken great things against me, I would perhaps have hidden myself from him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <span className="pl pl-1">But thou a man of one mind, my guide, and my familiar,</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Evening and morning, and at noon I will speak and declare: and he shall hear my voice.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> <span className="pl pl-1">He shall redeem my soul in peace from them that draw near to me: for among many they were with me.</span><br /></span>
