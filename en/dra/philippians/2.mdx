<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 3:12; 1 John 4:12; 1 John 4:7–8"> If there be therefore any consolation in Christ, if any comfort of charity, if any society of the spirit, if any bowels of commiseration:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 12:16; 1 Peter 3:8–9; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> Fulfill ye my joy, that you may be of one mind, having the same charity, being of one accord, agreeing in sentiment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 12:10; Galatians 5:26; Ephesians 5:21"> Let nothing be done through contention, neither by vain glory: but in humility, let each esteem others better than themselves:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:24; Romans 15:1; James 2:8"> Each one not considering the things that are his own, but those that are other men’s.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 John 2:6; Matthew 11:29; 1 Peter 2:21"> For let this mind be in you, which was also in Christ Jesus:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 5:18; 2 Corinthians 4:4; Hebrews 1:3"> Who being in the form of God, thought it not robbery to be equal with God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:9; Matthew 20:28; John 1:14"> But emptied himself, taking the form of a servant, being made in the likeness of men, and in habit found as a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:18; Hebrews 12:2; Romans 5:19"> He humbled himself, becoming obedient unto death, even to the death of the cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 19:16; Matthew 28:18; 1 Peter 3:22"> For which cause God also hath exalted him, and hath given him a name which is above all names:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 45:23–25; Matthew 28:18; Revelation 5:13–14"> That in the name of Jesus every knee should bow, of those that are in heaven, on earth, and under the earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 John 4:15; Romans 14:11; John 13:13"> And that every tongue should confess that the Lord Jesus Christ is in the glory of God the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Peter 1:5–10; Luke 13:23–24; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> Wherefore, my dearly beloved, (as you have always obeyed, not as in my presence only, but much more now in my absence,) with fear and trembling work out your salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 13:21; 2 Corinthians 3:5; 2 Timothy 1:9"> For it is God who worketh in you, both to will and to accomplish, according to his good will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 4:9; 1 Corinthians 10:10; Philippians 2:3"> And do ye all things without murmurings and hesitations;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:14–16; 1 Peter 2:12; Matthew 5:45"> That you may be blameless, and sincere children of God, without reproof, in the midst of a crooked and perverse generation; among whom you shine as lights in the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:26; Galatians 2:2; 1 John 1:1"> Holding forth the word of life to my glory in the day of Christ, because I have not run in vain, nor laboured in vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:6; Romans 15:16; Hebrews 13:15–16"> Yea, and if I be made a victim upon the sacrifice and service of your faith, I rejoice, and congratulate with you all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 1:2–4; Philippians 4:4; Philippians 3:1"> And for the selfsame thing do you also rejoice, and congratulate with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:17; 1 Thessalonians 3:6–8; 1 Thessalonians 3:2"> And I hope in the Lord Jesus to send Timothy unto you shortly, that I also may be of good comfort, when I know the things concerning you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Philippians 2:2; 1 Corinthians 16:10; Philippians 2:22"> For I have no man so of the same mind, who with sincere affection is solicitous for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:24; Matthew 16:24; Philippians 2:4"> For all seek the things that are their own; not the things that are Jesus Christ’s.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:17; 2 Corinthians 2:9; 2 Timothy 1:2"> Now know ye the proof of him, that as a son with the father, so hath he served with me in the gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:3; Philippians 2:19"> Him therefore I hope to send unto you immediately, so soon as I shall see how it will go with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="3 John 1:14; Philemon 1:22; Philippians 1:25–26"> And I trust in the Lord, that I myself also shall come to you shortly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Philippians 4:18; Philemon 1:1–2; Philippians 4:3"> But I have thought it necessary to send to you Epaphroditus, my brother and fellow labourer, and fellow soldier, but your apostle, and he that hath ministered to my wants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Proverbs 12:25; Ephesians 3:13"> For indeed he longed after you all: and was sad, for that you had heard that he was sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 34:19; Philippians 2:30; Psalms 103:3–4"> For indeed he was sick, nigh unto death; but God had mercy on him; and not only on him, but on me also, lest I should have sorrow upon sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 16:22; 2 Timothy 1:4; Philippians 2:26–27"> Therefore I sent him the more speedily: that seeing him again, you may rejoice, and I may be without sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:12; 1 Corinthians 16:18; 1 Timothy 5:17"> Receive him therefore with all joy in the Lord; and treat with honour such as he is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:17; Acts 20:24; Philippians 4:10"> Because for the work of Christ he came to the point of death: delivering his life, that he might fulfill that which on your part was wanting towards my service.</span>
