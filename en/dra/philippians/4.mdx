<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 12:14; 1 Corinthians 1:10; Mark 9:50"> I beg of Evodia, and I beseech Syntyche, to be of one mind in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 3:5; Revelation 13:8; Luke 10:20"> And I entreat thee also, my sincere companion, help those women who have laboured with me in the gospel, with Clement and the rest of my fellow labourers, whose names are in the book of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:16–18; Romans 12:12; James 1:2–4"> Rejoice in the Lord always; again, I say, rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 5:8–9; Matthew 6:25; Titus 3:2"> Let your modesty be known to all men. The Lord is nigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; 1 Peter 5:7; Matthew 6:25–33"> Be nothing solicitous; but in every thing, by prayer and supplication, with thanksgiving, let your petitions be made known to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 14:27; Isaiah 26:3; 2 Thessalonians 3:16"> And the peace of God, which surpasseth all understanding, keep your hearts and minds in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 3:17; Titus 2:7; Romans 12:9–21"> For the rest, brethren, whatsoever things are true, whatsoever modest, whatsoever just, whatsoever holy, whatsoever lovely, whatsoever of good fame, if there be any virtue, if any praise of discipline, think on these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 1:22; Philippians 3:17; 1 Thessalonians 4:1–8"> The things which you have both learned, and received, and heard, and seen in me, these do ye, and the God of peace shall be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:9; Galatians 6:6; 2 Corinthians 7:6–7"> Now I rejoice in the Lord exceedingly, that now at length your thought for me hath flourished again, as you did also think; but you were busied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:6–9; Hebrews 13:5–6; Matthew 6:31–34"> I speak not as it were for want. For I have learned, in whatsoever state I am, to be content therewith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 11:29; 2 Corinthians 12:7–10; 2 Corinthians 11:27"> I know both how to be brought low, and I know how to abound: (everywhere, and in all things I am instructed) both to be full, and to be hungry; both to abound, and to suffer need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Colossians 1:11"> I can do all these things in him who strengtheneth me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Philippians 1:7; Hebrews 10:34"> Nevertheless you have done well in communicating to my tribulation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philippians 1:5; 2 Corinthians 11:8–12; Acts 16:40–5"> And you also know, O Philippians, that in the beginning of the gospel, when I departed from Macedonia, no church communicated with me as concerning giving and receiving, but you only:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:9; Acts 17:1; 1 Thessalonians 2:18"> For unto Thessalonica also you sent once and again for my use.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Titus 3:14; Hebrews 6:10; 2 Corinthians 9:9–13"> Not that I seek the gift, but I seek the fruit that may abound to your account.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Philippians 4:12; 2 Corinthians 9:12"> But I have all, and abound: I am filled, having received from Epaphroditus the things you sent, an odour of sweetness, an acceptable sacrifice, pleasing to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:8–11; Psalms 84:11; 1 Peter 5:10"> And may my God supply all your want, according to his riches in glory in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Psalms 115:1; Revelation 14:7"> Now to God and our Father be glory world without end. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Galatians 1:2; Philemon 1:23–24; Colossians 4:10–14"> Salute ye every saint in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Philippians 1:13; 2 Corinthians 13:13; Romans 16:16"> The brethren who are with me, salute you. All the saints salute you; especially they that are of Caesar’s household.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:14; Romans 16:20; Romans 16:23"> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit. Amen.</span>
