<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 3:3–4; Luke 24:48; Romans 8:17–18"> The ancients therefore that are among you, I beseech, who am myself also an ancient, and a witness of the sufferings of Christ: as also a partaker of that glory which is to be revealed in time to come:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:7; 1 Timothy 3:8; Titus 3:1"> Feed the flock of God which is among you, taking care of it, not by constraint, but willingly, according to God: not for filthy lucre’s sake, but voluntarily:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:9; Philippians 3:17; Ezekiel 34:4"> Neither as lording it over the clergy, but being made a pattern of the flock from the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 1:12; 1 Corinthians 9:25; 1 John 3:2"> And when the prince of pastors shall appear, you shall receive a never fading crown of glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 4:6; Proverbs 3:34; Ephesians 5:21"> In like manner, ye young men, be subject to the ancients. And do you all insinuate humility one to another, for God resisteth the proud, but to the humble he giveth grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 4:10; Luke 14:11; Proverbs 29:23"> Be you humbled therefore under the mighty hand of God, that he may exalt you in the time of visitation:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 55:22; Philippians 4:6; Psalms 37:5"> Casting all your care upon him, for he hath care of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 6:11; James 4:7; Ephesians 4:27"> Be sober and watch: because your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, goeth about seeking whom he may devour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 4:7; Acts 14:22; Ephesians 6:11–13"> Whom resist ye, strong in faith: knowing that the same affliction befalls your brethren who are in the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:17; 2 Thessalonians 3:3; 1 Peter 1:6–7"> But the God of all grace, who hath called us into his eternal glory in Christ Jesus, after you have suffered a little, will himself perfect you, and confirm you, and establish you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 4:11; Romans 11:36; Revelation 1:6"> To him be glory and empire for ever and ever. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 13:22; Acts 11:23; 1 Corinthians 15:1"> By Sylvanus, a faithful brother unto you, as I think, I have written briefly: beseeching and testifying that this is the true grace of God, wherein you stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 12:12; Acts 12:25; Psalms 87:4"> The church that is in Babylon, elected together with you, saluteth you: and so doth my son Mark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 16:16; Ephesians 6:23; Romans 8:1"> Salute one another with a holy kiss. Grace be to all you, who are in Christ Jesus. Amen.</span>
