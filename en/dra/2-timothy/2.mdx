<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Ephesians 6:10; 2 Timothy 1:7"> Thou therefore, my son, be strong in the grace which is in Christ Jesus:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:13–14; 1 Timothy 4:6; 1 Timothy 6:12"> And the things which thou hast heard of me by many witnesses, the same commend to faithful men, who shall be fit to teach others also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2–3; James 1:12; 2 Timothy 4:5"> Labour as a good soldier of Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; Luke 8:14; 2 Peter 2:20"> No man, being a soldier to God, entangleth himself with secular businesses; that he may please him to whom he hath engaged himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:24–27; Revelation 3:11; 2 Timothy 4:7–8"> For he also that striveth for the mastery, is not crowned, except he strive lawfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; 1 Corinthians 9:7–11; 1 Corinthians 3:6–9"> The husbandman, that laboureth, must first partake of the fruits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:5; 1 John 5:20; Luke 24:45"> Understand what I say: for the Lord will give thee in all things understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Romans 2:16; 1 Corinthians 15:11–20"> Be mindful that the Lord Jesus Christ is risen again from the dead, of the seed of David, according to my gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philippians 1:7; Acts 28:31; 2 Timothy 1:8"> Wherein I labour even unto bands, as an evildoer; but the word of God is not bound.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; Colossians 1:24; 2 Timothy 2:3"> Therefore I endure all things for the sake of the elect, that they also may obtain the salvation, which is in Christ Jesus, with heavenly glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 6:8; Colossians 3:3–4; Romans 6:5"> A faithful saying: for if we be dead with him, we shall live also with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 8:17; Matthew 10:33; Revelation 20:4"> If we suffer, we shall also reign with him. If we deny him, he will also deny us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Romans 3:3; 2 Thessalonians 3:3"> If we believe not, he continueth faithful, he can not deny himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 3:9–11; 2 Timothy 2:23; 2 Timothy 4:1"> Of these things put them in mind, charging them before the Lord. Contend not in words, for it is to no profit, but to the subverting of the hearers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; 2 Peter 1:10; 1 Timothy 4:6"> Carefully study to present thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly handling the word of truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Titus 3:9; 1 Timothy 6:20; 2 Timothy 2:14"> But shun profane and vain babblings: for they grow much towards ungodliness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:20; Nahum 3:15; James 5:3"> And their speech spreadeth like a canker: of whom are Hymeneus and Philetus:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:12; 1 Timothy 1:19; Hebrews 3:10"> Who have erred from the truth, saying, that the resurrection is past already, and have subverted the faith of some.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 10:14; 1 Corinthians 8:3; Nahum 1:7"> But the sure foundation of God standeth firm, having this seal: the Lord knoweth who are his; and let every one depart from iniquity who nameth the name of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 9:21–23; 1 Peter 2:5; 1 Timothy 3:15"> But in a great house there are not only vessels of gold and silver, but also of wood and earth: and some indeed unto honour, but some unto dishonour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:17; Ephesians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 7:1"> If any man therefore shall cleanse himself from these, he shall be a vessel unto honour, sanctified and profitable to the Lord, prepared unto every good work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; 1 Peter 3:11; 1 Corinthians 6:18"> But flee thou youthful desires, and pursue justice, faith, charity, and peace, with them that call on the Lord out of a pure heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:14; Titus 3:9; 1 Timothy 4:7"> And avoid foolish and unlearned questions, knowing that they beget strifes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Titus 3:2; James 1:19–20; 1 Timothy 3:2–3"> But the servant of the Lord must not wrangle: but be mild towards all men, apt to teach, patient,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 11:18; Galatians 6:1; 1 Timothy 2:4"> With modesty admonishing them that resist the truth: if peradventure God may give them repentance to know the truth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:7; Isaiah 49:25–26; Psalms 124:7"> And they may recover themselves from the snares of the devil, by whom they are held captive at his will.</span>
