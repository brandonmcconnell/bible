<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 18:15–17; Acts 19:38; 1 Corinthians 14:33"> Dare any of you, having a matter against another, go to be judged before the unjust, and not before the saints?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Daniel 7:22; Revelation 20:4"> Know you not that the saints shall judge this world? And if the world shall be judged by you, are you unworthy to judge the smallest matters?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jude 1:6; Matthew 25:41; 1 Corinthians 6:4"> Know you not that we shall judge angels? how much more things of this world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:12; Acts 6:2–4"> If therefore you have judgments of things pertaining to this world, set them to judge, who are the most despised in the church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:34; 1 Corinthians 4:14; James 3:13–18"> I speak to your shame. Is it so that there is not among you any one wise man, that is able to judge between his brethren?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Philippians 2:14–15; 1 John 2:9–11; 1 John 3:11–15"> But brother goeth to law with brother, and that before unbelievers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 12:17–19; Proverbs 20:22; 1 Peter 2:19–23"> Already indeed there is plainly a fault among you, that you have lawsuits one with another. Why do you not rather take wrong? Why do you not rather suffer yourselves to be defrauded?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:6; Colossians 3:25; Mark 10:19"> But you do wrong and defraud, and that to your brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:9–10; Galatians 5:19–21; Ephesians 5:4–5"> Know you not that the unjust shall not possess the kingdom of God? Do not err: neither fornicators, nor idolaters, nor adulterers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:11; Galatians 5:21; Ezekiel 22:29"> Nor the effeminate, nor liers with mankind, nor thieves, nor covetous, nor drunkards, nor railers, nor extortioners, shall possess the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:30; Colossians 3:5–7; 1 Corinthians 1:2"> And such some of you were; but you are washed, but you are sanctified, but you are justified in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, and the Spirit of our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:23–33; 1 Corinthians 9:27; Hebrews 12:15–16"> All things are lawful to me, but all things are not expedient. All things are lawful to me, but I will not be brought under the power of any.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:19; 1 Corinthians 3:16; Romans 6:12"> Meat for the belly, and the belly for the meats; but God shall destroy both it and them: but the body is not for fornication, but for the Lord, and the Lord for the body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:14; Romans 8:11; Ephesians 1:19–20"> Now God hath both raised up the Lord, and will raise us up also by his power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 5:30; Romans 12:5; Ephesians 4:15–16"> Know you not that your bodies are the members of Christ? Shall I then take the members of Christ, and make them the members of an harlot? God forbid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 2:24; Ephesians 5:31; Mark 10:8"> Or know you not, that he who is joined to a harlot, is made one body? For they shall be, saith he, two in one flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 17:21–23; Galatians 2:20; 1 Corinthians 12:13"> But he who is joined to the Lord, is one spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; 1 Thessalonians 4:3; Colossians 3:5"> Fly fornication. Every sin that a man doth, is without the body; but he that committeth fornication, sinneth against his own body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:16; 2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Peter 2:5"> Or know you not, that your members are the temple of the Holy Ghost, who is in you, whom you have from God; and you are not your own?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:23; Romans 12:1; 1 Peter 2:9"> For you are bought with a great price. Glorify and bear God in your body.</span>
