<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 9:17; 1 Timothy 2:7; 1 Corinthians 9:19"> Am not I free? Am not I an apostle? Have not I seen Christ Jesus our Lord? Are not you my work in the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:12; John 6:27; 2 Corinthians 3:1–3"> And if unto others I be not an apostle, but yet to you I am. For you are the seal of my apostleship in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:16–19; 2 Corinthians 13:3; 2 Corinthians 13:10"> My defence with them that do examine me is this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:6; 2 Thessalonians 3:8–9; Luke 10:7"> Have not we power to eat and to drink?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 1:42; Hebrews 13:4; Matthew 8:14"> Have we not power to carry about a woman, a sister, as well as the rest of the apostles, and the brethren of the Lord, and Cephas?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:7–9; Acts 13:1–2; Acts 4:36"> Or I only and Barnabas, have not we power to do this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 27:18; Deuteronomy 20:6; 2 Timothy 2:3–4"> Who serveth as a soldier at any time, at his own charges? Who planteth a vineyard, and eateth not of the fruit thereof? Who feedeth the flock, and eateth not of the milk of the flock?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 8:20; 1 Thessalonians 4:8; Romans 3:31"> Speak I these things according to man? Or doth not the law also say these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:4; 1 Timothy 5:18; Psalms 104:27"> For it is written in the law of Moses: Thou shalt not muzzle the mouth of the ox that treadeth out the corn. Doth God take care for oxen?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:6; Romans 15:4; Romans 4:23–24"> Or doth he say this indeed for our sakes? For these things are written for our sakes: that he that plougheth, should plough in hope; and he that thrasheth, in hope to receive fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 15:27; Galatians 6:6; 2 Corinthians 11:15"> If we have sown unto you spiritual things, is it a great matter if we reap your carnal things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:15; 1 Corinthians 9:18; 2 Corinthians 11:12"> If others be partakers of this power over you, why not we rather? Nevertheless, we have not used this power: but we bear all things, lest we should give any hindrance to the gospel of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 18:8–20; Numbers 5:9–10; Leviticus 6:26"> Know you not, that they who work in the holy place, eat the things that are of the holy place; and they that serve the altar, partake with the altar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 10:10; 1 Timothy 5:17–18; 1 Corinthians 9:4"> So also the Lord ordained that they who preach the gospel, should live by the gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:9; 2 Thessalonians 3:8; 1 Corinthians 9:12"> But I have used none of these things. Neither have I written these things, that they should be so done unto me: for it is good for me to die, rather than that any man should make my glory void.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 1:14; Jeremiah 20:9; Colossians 4:17"> For if I preach the gospel, it is no glory to me, for a necessity lieth upon me: for woe is unto me if I preach not the gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 2:7; 1 Corinthians 3:8; 1 Chronicles 28:9"> For if I do this thing willingly, I have a reward: but if against my will, a dispensation is committed to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:7–9; 1 Corinthians 8:9; 1 Thessalonians 2:6"> What is my reward then? That preaching the gospel, I may deliver the gospel without charge, that I abuse not my power in the gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 5:13; Matthew 18:15; James 5:19–20"> For whereas I was free as to all, I made myself the servant of all, that I might gain the more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 16:3; Galatians 5:18; Acts 21:20–26"> And I became to the Jews, a Jew, that I might gain the Jews:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 2:12; Romans 2:14; Galatians 3:2"> To them that are under the law, as if I were under the law, (whereas myself was not under the law,) that I might gain them that were under the law. To them that were without the law, as if I were without the law, (whereas I was not without the law of God, but was in the law of Christ,) that I might gain them that were without the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:33; Romans 15:1; 1 Corinthians 9:19"> To the weak I became weak, that I might gain the weak. I became all things to all men, that I might save all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:10; Mark 8:35; 1 Corinthians 9:25–27"> And I do all things for the gospel’s sake: that I may be made partaker thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Philippians 3:14; Hebrews 12:1; 2 Timothy 4:7–8"> Know you not that they that run in the race, all run indeed, but one receiveth the prize? So run that you may obtain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:5; Revelation 2:10; 2 Timothy 4:7–8"> And every one that striveth for the mastery, refraineth himself from all things: and they indeed that they may receive a corruptible crown; but we an incorruptible one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Peter 1:10; Philippians 1:21; 2 Timothy 2:5"> I therefore so run, not as at an uncertainty: I so fight, not as one beating the air:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 8:13; 1 Corinthians 9:25; Colossians 3:5"> But I chastise my body, and bring it into subjection: lest perhaps, when I have preached to others, I myself should become a castaway.</span>
