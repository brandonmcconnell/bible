<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 12:13; 2 Corinthians 8:1–9; 2 Corinthians 9:12–15"> Now concerning the collections that are made for the saints, as I have given order to the churches of Galatia, so do ye also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Malachi 3:9–10; Deuteronomy 15:11–14; Deuteronomy 8:18"> On the first day of the week let every one of you put apart with himself, laying up what it shall well please him; that when I come, the collections be not then to be made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:6; 1 Corinthians 11:34; 1 Corinthians 4:19–21"> And when I shall be with you, whomsoever you shall approve by letters, them will I send to carry your grace to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:4; 2 Corinthians 8:19; Romans 15:25"> And if it be meet that I also go, they shall go with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 19:21; 1 Corinthians 4:19; 2 Corinthians 1:15–17"> Now I will come to you, when I shall have passed through Macedonia. For I shall pass through Macedonia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 15:3; Romans 15:24; Acts 20:38"> And with you perhaps I shall abide, or even spend the winter: that you may bring me on my way whithersoever I shall go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 18:21; 1 Corinthians 4:19; James 4:15"> For I will not see you now by the way, for I trust that I shall abide with you some time, if the Lord permit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 2:1; Exodus 23:16; Acts 18:19"> But I will tarry at Ephesus until Pentecost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:12; Acts 14:27; Colossians 4:3"> For a great door and evident is opened unto me: and many adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; 1 Thessalonians 3:2; Romans 16:21"> Now if Timothy come, see that he be with you without fear, for he worketh the work of the Lord, as I also do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:12; Acts 15:33; Titus 2:15"> Let no man therefore despise him, but conduct ye him on his way in peace: that he may come to me. For I look for him with the brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:12; 1 Corinthians 3:5; Titus 3:4"> And touching our brother Apollo, I give you to understand, that I much entreated him to come unto you with the brethren: and indeed it was not his will at all to come at this time. But he will come when he shall have leisure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Ephesians 6:13–18; Ephesians 6:10"> Watch ye, stand fast in the faith, do manfully, and be strengthened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 13:34–35; 1 Peter 4:8; 1 Timothy 1:5"> Let all your things be done in charity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:16; Romans 16:5; Romans 16:2"> And I beseech you, brethren, you know the house of Stephanas, and of Fortunatus, and of Achaicus, that they are the firstfruits of Achaia, and have dedicated themselves to the ministry of the saints:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 13:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:12; 3 John 1:8"> That you also be subject to such, and to every one that worketh with us, and laboureth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:9; Philippians 2:30; Philemon 1:13"> And I rejoice in the presence of Stephanas, and Fortunatus, and Achaicus, because that which was wanting on your part, they have supplied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philemon 1:7; 2 Corinthians 7:13; 1 Thessalonians 5:12"> For they have refreshed both my spirit and yours. Know them, therefore, that are such.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 18:2; Philemon 1:2; Romans 16:3–5"> The churches of Asia salute you. Aquila and Priscilla salute you much in the Lord, with the church that is in their house, with whom I also lodge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 16:16; 1 Thessalonians 5:26; 1 Peter 5:14"> All the brethren salute you. Salute one another with a holy kiss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Colossians 4:18; Galatians 6:11; 2 Thessalonians 3:17"> The salutation of me Paul, with my own hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 6:24; John 14:21; 2 Corinthians 5:14–15"> If any man love not our Lord Jesus Christ, let him be anathema, maranatha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 16:20; Romans 16:23"> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:16; Revelation 3:19; 2 Corinthians 12:15"> My charity be with you all in Christ Jesus. Amen.</span>
