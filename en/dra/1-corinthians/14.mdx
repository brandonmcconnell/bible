<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:39; 1 Timothy 4:14; 1 Corinthians 12:1"> Follow after charity, be zealous for spiritual gifts; but rather that you may prophesy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 16:17; Acts 19:6; Acts 10:46"> For he that speaketh in a tongue, speaketh not unto men, but unto God: for no man heareth. Yet by the Spirit he speaketh mysteries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:12; 1 Timothy 4:13; Jude 1:20"> But he that prophesieth, speaketh to men unto edification, and exhortation, and comfort.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:22; 1 Corinthians 14:26; Mark 16:17"> He that speaketh in a tongue, edifieth himself: but he that prophesieth, edifieth the church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:28–30; 1 Corinthians 12:10; Mark 16:17"> And I would have you all to speak with tongues, but rather to prophesy. For greater is he that prophesieth, than he that speaketh with tongues: unless perhaps he interpret, that the church may receive edification.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 1:17; Romans 6:17; 2 Timothy 3:16"> But now, brethren, if I come to you speaking with tongues, what shall I profit you, unless I speak to you either in revelation, or in knowledge, or in prophecy, or in doctrine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:8; Luke 7:32; 1 Corinthians 13:1"> Even things without life that give sound, whether pipe or harp, except they give a distinction of sounds, how shall it be known what is piped or harped?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 10:9; Ezekiel 33:3–6; Jeremiah 4:19"> For if the trumpet give an uncertain sound, who shall prepare himself to the battle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:26"> So likewise you, except you utter by the tongue plain speech, how shall it be known what is said? For you shall be speaking into the air.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> There are, for example, so many kinds of tongues in this world; and none is without voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 28:2; Romans 1:14; Colossians 3:11"> If then I know not the power of the voice, I shall be to him to whom I speak a barbarian; and he that speaketh, a barbarian to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:26; 1 Corinthians 14:3–4; 1 Corinthians 14:1"> So you also, forasmuch as you are zealous of spirits, seek to abound unto the edifying of the church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 11:24; 1 Corinthians 12:30; John 14:13–14"> And therefore he that speaketh by a tongue, let him pray that he may interpret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:2; 1 Corinthians 14:15–16; 1 Corinthians 14:19"> For if I pray in a tongue, my spirit prayeth, but my understanding is without fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; John 4:23–24; Ephesians 6:18"> What is it then? I will pray with the spirit, I will pray also with the understanding; I will sing with the spirit, I will sing also with the understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:36; Psalms 106:48; Revelation 5:14"> Else if thou shalt bless with the spirit, how shall he that holdeth the place of the unlearned say, Amen, to thy blessing? because he knoweth not what thou sayest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:4; 1 Corinthians 14:6"> For thou indeed givest thanks well, but the other is not edified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:4–6; 1 Corinthians 4:7"> I thank my God I speak with all your tongues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:21–22; 1 Corinthians 14:4"> But in the church I had rather speak five words with my understanding, that I may instruct others also; than ten thousand words in a tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 2:2; 1 Corinthians 13:11; Romans 16:19"> Brethren, do not become children in sense: but in malice be children, and in sense be perfect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 28:11–12; Jeremiah 5:15; Deuteronomy 28:49"> In the law it is written: In other tongues and other lips I will speak to this people; and neither so will they hear me, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 2:6–12; Mark 16:17; 1 Corinthians 14:1"> Wherefore tongues are for a sign, not to believers, but to unbelievers; but prophecies not to unbelievers, but to believers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 2:13; Acts 26:24; 1 Corinthians 11:18"> If therefore the whole church come together into one place, and all speak with tongues, and there come in unlearned persons or infidels, will they not say that you are mad?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 2:37; 1 Corinthians 2:15; Hebrews 4:12–13"> But if all prophesy, and there come in one that believeth not, or an unlearned person, he is convinced of all, he is judged of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 45:14; Zechariah 8:23; Psalms 72:11"> The secrets of his heart are made manifest; and so, falling down on his face, he will adore God, affirming that God is among you indeed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ephesians 5:19; Romans 14:19; 1 Corinthians 12:7–10"> How is it then, brethren? When you come together, every one of you hath a psalm, hath a doctrine, hath a revelation, hath a tongue, hath an interpretation: let all things be done to edification.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:26"> If any speak with a tongue, let it be by two, or at the most by three, and in course, and let one interpret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> But if there be no interpreter, let him hold his peace in the church, and speak to himself and to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:10; 1 Thessalonians 5:19–21; 1 John 4:1–3"> And let the prophets speak, two or three; and let the rest judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:19–20; Job 33:31–33; Job 32:11"> But if any thing be revealed to another sitting, let the first hold his peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:14; 1 Corinthians 14:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:11"> For you may all prophesy one by one; that all may learn, and all may be exhorted:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 4:19–20; 1 John 4:1; 1 Samuel 10:10–13"> And the spirits of the prophets are subject to the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="James 3:17–18; 1 Corinthians 14:40; 2 Thessalonians 3:16"> For God is not the God of dissension, but of peace: as also I teach in all the churches of the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:11–12; Genesis 3:16; Numbers 30:3–13"> Let women keep silence in the churches: for it is not permitted them to speak, but to be subject, as also the law saith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ephesians 5:12; 1 Peter 3:7; Ephesians 5:25–27"> But if they would learn any thing, let them ask their husbands at home. For it is a shame for a woman to speak in the church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 18:1–17; Zechariah 14:8; Acts 17:15"> Or did the word of God come out from you? Or came it only unto you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 John 4:6; 2 Corinthians 10:7; 2 Peter 3:2"> If any seem to be a prophet, or spiritual, let him know the things that I write to you, that they are the commandments of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3–4; Hosea 4:17; Revelation 22:11–12"> But if any man know not, he shall not be known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:31; 1 Corinthians 14:1; 1 Thessalonians 5:20"> Wherefore, brethren, be zealous to prophesy; and forbid not to speak with tongues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Colossians 2:5; Titus 1:5; 1 Corinthians 14:26–33"> But let all things be done decently, and according to order.</span>
