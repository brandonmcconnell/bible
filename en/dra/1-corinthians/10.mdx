<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 14:29; Nehemiah 9:11–12; Psalms 78:13–14"> For I would not have you ignorant, brethren, that our fathers were all under the cloud, and all passed through the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 3:2–3; 1 Corinthians 1:13–16; Exodus 14:31"> And all in Moses were baptized, in the cloud, and in the sea:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:3; Nehemiah 9:15; Exodus 16:35"> And did all eat the same spiritual food,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 17:6; John 7:37; Isaiah 48:21"> And all drank the same spiritual drink; (and they drank of the spiritual rock that followed them, and the rock was Christ.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 3:17; Jude 1:5; Numbers 14:37"> But with most of them God was not well pleased: for they were overthrown in the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jude 1:7; 1 Corinthians 10:11; Hebrews 4:11"> Now these things were done in a figure of us, that we should not covet evil things as they also coveted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 32:6–8; Psalms 106:19–20; 1 Corinthians 6:9"> Neither become ye idolaters, as some of them, as it is written: The people sat down to eat and drink, and rose up to play.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 25:1–9; 1 Corinthians 6:18; Psalms 106:29"> Neither let us commit fornication, as some of them committed fornication, and there fell in one day three and twenty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 21:5–6; Psalms 78:18; Hebrews 3:8–11"> Neither let us tempt Christ: as some of them tempted, and perished by the serpents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 14:2; Numbers 16:41; Numbers 14:37"> Neither do you murmur: as some of them murmured, and were destroyed by the destroyer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 15:4; Romans 13:11; 1 Corinthians 9:10"> Now all these things happened to them in figure: and they are written for our correction, upon whom the ends of the world are come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:20; 2 Peter 3:17; Proverbs 16:18"> Wherefore he that thinketh himself to stand, let him take heed lest he fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Peter 2:9; 2 Timothy 4:18; 2 Thessalonians 3:3"> Let no temptation take hold on you, but such as is human. And God is faithful, who will not suffer you to be tempted above that which you are able: but will make also with temptation issue, that you may be able to bear it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 21:8; 1 John 5:21; 1 Peter 2:11"> Wherefore, my dearly beloved, fly from the service of idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:1; 1 Thessalonians 5:21; 1 Corinthians 11:13"> I speak as to wise men: judge ye yourselves what I say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 26:26–28; 1 Corinthians 11:23–29; Acts 2:42"> The chalice of benediction, which we bless, is it not the communion of the blood of Christ? And the bread, which we break, is it not the partaking of the body of the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 12:5; Colossians 3:15; 1 Corinthians 12:27"> For we, being many, are one bread, one body, all that partake of one bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philippians 3:3–5; Galatians 6:16; Leviticus 7:6"> Behold Israel according to the flesh: are not they, that eat of the sacrifices, partakers of the altar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:4; 1 Corinthians 3:7; 1 Corinthians 13:2"> What then? Do I say, that what is offered in sacrifice to idols, is any thing? Or, that the idol is any thing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 9:20; Psalms 106:37–39; Deuteronomy 32:16–17"> But the things which the heathens sacrifice, they sacrifice to devils, and not to God. And I would not that you should be made partakers with devils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 6:24; 1 Kings 18:21; 2 Corinthians 6:15–17"> You cannot drink the chalice of the Lord, and the chalice of devils: you cannot be partakers of the table of the Lord, and of the table of devils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Deuteronomy 32:16; Hebrews 10:31"> Do we provoke the Lord to jealousy? Are we stronger than he? All things are lawful for me, but all things are not expedient.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:12; 1 Corinthians 8:9; Ephesians 4:29"> All things are lawful for me, but all things do not edify.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 15:1–2; 1 Corinthians 10:33; Philippians 2:4–5"> Let no man seek his own, but that which is another’s.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:4; 1 Corinthians 8:7; Titus 1:15"> Whatsoever is sold in the shambles, eat; asking no question for conscience’ sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 24:1; Exodus 19:5; Psalms 50:12"> The earth is the Lord’s, and the fulness thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:25; Luke 10:7–8; 1 Corinthians 5:9–11"> If any of them that believe not, invite you, and you will be willing to go; eat of any thing that is set before you, asking no question for conscience’ sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:10–13; 1 Corinthians 8:7; 1 Corinthians 10:26"> But if any man say: This has been sacrificed to idols, do not eat of it for his sake that told it, and for conscience’ sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:19; Romans 14:15–21; 1 Corinthians 8:9–13"> Conscience, I say, not thy own, but the other’s. For why is my liberty judged by another man’s conscience?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Romans 14:6; 1 Timothy 4:3–4"> If I partake with thanksgiving, why am I evil spoken of, for that for which I give thanks?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Colossians 3:17; Colossians 3:23; 1 Peter 4:11"> Therefore, whether you eat or drink, or whatsoever else you do, do all to the glory of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 20:28; 1 Corinthians 10:33; Romans 14:13"> Be without offence to the Jews, and to the Gentiles, and to the church of God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:24; Galatians 1:10; 1 Corinthians 9:19–23"> As I also in all things please all men, not seeking that which is profitable to myself, but to many, that may be saved.</span>
