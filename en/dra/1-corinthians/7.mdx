<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:8; 1 Corinthians 7:26–27; 1 Corinthians 7:37–38"> Now concerning the thing whereof you wrote to me: It is good for a man not to touch a woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 5:28; Ephesians 5:33; 1 Corinthians 7:9"> But for fear of fornication, let every man have his own wife, and let every woman have her own husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 3:7; Exodus 21:10"> Let the husband render the debt to his wife, and the wife also in like manner to the husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 3:3; Matthew 19:9; Mark 10:11–12"> The wife hath not power of her own body, but the husband. And in like manner the husband also hath not power of his own body, but the wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 19:15; Joel 2:16; Zechariah 12:12–14"> Defraud not one another, except, perhaps, by consent, for a time, that you may give yourselves to prayer; and return together again, lest Satan tempt you for your incontinency.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:8; 1 Corinthians 7:25; 1 Corinthians 7:12"> But I speak this by indulgence, not by commandment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 19:11–12; Romans 12:6; 1 Corinthians 9:5"> For I would that all men were even as myself: but every one hath his proper gift from God; one after this manner, and another after that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:32; 1 Corinthians 7:34–35; 1 Corinthians 7:26–27"> But I say to the unmarried, and to the widows: It is good for them if they so continue, even as I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:2; 1 Timothy 5:14; 1 Timothy 5:11"> But if they do not contain themselves, let them marry. For it is better to marry than to be burnt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 5:32; Luke 16:18; Matthew 19:3–9"> But to them that are married, not I but the Lord commandeth, that the wife depart not from her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:1; 1 Corinthians 7:10; Isaiah 50:1"> And if she depart, that she remain unmarried, or be reconciled to her husband. And let not the husband put away his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:17; 1 Corinthians 7:6; 1 Corinthians 7:25"> For to the rest I speak, not the Lord. If any brother hath a wife that believeth not, and she consent to dwell with him, let him not put her away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And if any woman hath a husband that believeth not, and he consent to dwell with her, let her not put away her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 2:15–16; 1 Timothy 4:5; Titus 1:15"> For the unbelieving husband is sanctified by the believing wife; and the unbelieving wife is sanctified by the believing husband: otherwise your children should be unclean; but now they are holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 14:19; 2 Corinthians 13:11; James 3:17–18"> But if the unbeliever depart, let him depart. For a brother or sister is not under servitude in such cases. But God hath called us in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 3:1–2; 1 Corinthians 9:22; Romans 11:14"> For how knowest thou, O wife, whether thou shalt save thy husband? Or how knowest thou, O man, whether thou shalt save thy wife?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:17; Romans 12:3–8; 1 Corinthians 7:24"> But as the Lord hath distributed to every one, as God hath called every one, so let him walk: and so in all churches I teach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 15:5; Acts 15:28; Acts 15:24"> Is any man called, being circumcised? let him not procure uncircumcision. Is any man called in uncircumcision? let him not be circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 6:15; Galatians 5:6; 1 John 2:3–4"> Circumcision is nothing, and uncircumcision is nothing: but the observance of the commandments of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:17; 1 Thessalonians 4:11; 2 Thessalonians 3:12"> Let every man abide in the same calling in which he was called.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 2:18–24; Colossians 3:11; Luke 10:40–41"> Wast thou called, being a bondman? care not for it; but if thou mayest be made free, use it rather.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:16; Philemon 1:16; Colossians 3:22–24"> For he that is called in the Lord, being a bondman, is the freeman of the Lord. Likewise he that is called, being free, is the bondman of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:20; 1 Peter 1:18–19; Titus 2:14"> You are bought with a price; be not made the bondslaves of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:20; Colossians 3:23–24; 1 Corinthians 7:17"> Brethren, let every man, wherein he was called, therein abide with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:6; 1 Corinthians 4:2; 1 Corinthians 7:40"> Now concerning virgins, I have no commandment of the Lord; but I give counsel, as having obtained mercy of the Lord, to be faithful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:1; 1 Corinthians 7:8; 1 Peter 4:17"> I think therefore that this is good for the present necessity, that it is good for a man so to be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:20; 1 Corinthians 7:12–14"> Art thou bound to a wife? seek not to be loosed. Art thou loosed from a wife? seek not a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 13:4; 1 Corinthians 7:32–36; 1 Corinthians 7:26"> But if thou take a wife, thou hast not sinned. And if a virgin marry, she hath not sinned: nevertheless, such shall have tribulation of the flesh. But I spare you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 John 2:17; 1 Corinthians 7:31; Romans 13:11–12"> This therefore I say, brethren; the time is short; it remaineth, that they also who have wives, be as if they had none;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 30:5; Luke 6:25; Luke 6:21"> And they that weep, as though they wept not; and they that rejoice, as if they rejoiced not; and they that buy, as though they possessed not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 John 2:17; 1 Peter 4:7; James 4:14"> And they that use this world, as if they used it not: for the fashion of this world passeth away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 6:25–34; 1 Timothy 5:5; Philippians 4:6"> But I would have you to be without solicitude. He that is without a wife, is solicitous for the things that belong to the Lord, how he may please God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Peter 3:7; Colossians 3:19; Ephesians 5:25–33"> But he that is with a wife, is solicitous for the things of the world, how he may please his wife: and he is divided.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 10:40–42; 1 Corinthians 6:20; Luke 2:36–37"> And the unmarried woman and the virgin thinketh on the things of the Lord, that she may be holy both in body and in spirit. But she that is married thinketh on the things of the world, how she may please her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:33–34; 1 Corinthians 7:36; Luke 8:14"> And this I speak for your profit: not to cast a snare upon you; but for that which is decent, and which may give you power to attend upon the Lord, without impediment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:37; 1 Corinthians 7:9; 1 Samuel 2:33"> But if any man think that he seemeth dishonoured, with regard to his virgin, for that she is above the age, and it must so be: let him do what he will; he sinneth not, if she marry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> For he that hath determined being steadfast in his heart, having no necessity, but having power of his own will; and hath judged this in his heart, to keep his virgin, doth well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Hebrews 13:4; 1 Corinthians 7:1–2; 1 Corinthians 7:8"> Therefore, both he that giveth his virgin in marriage, doth well; and he that giveth her not, doth better.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Romans 7:2–3; 2 Corinthians 6:14–16; 1 Corinthians 7:10"> A woman is bound by the law as long as her husband liveth; but if her husband die, she is at liberty: let her marry to whom she will; only in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:35; 2 Peter 3:15–16; 1 Corinthians 7:8"> But more blessed shall she be, if she so remain, according to my counsel; and I think that I also have the spirit of God.</span>
