<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 18:18; 1 Peter 1:22–23; 1 Timothy 5:9–10"> And I commend to you Phebe, our sister, who is in the ministry of the church, that is in Cenchrae:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 2:29; Philippians 1:27; Matthew 10:40–42"> That you receive her in the Lord as becometh saints; and that you assist her in whatsoever business she shall have need of you. For she also hath assisted many, and myself also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:19; 2 Timothy 4:19; Acts 18:26"> Salute Prisca and Aquila, my helpers in Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Philippians 2:30; 1 John 3:16; 1 Corinthians 16:1"> (Who have for my life laid down their own necks: to whom not I only give thanks, but also all the churches of the Gentiles,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 4:15; Philemon 1:2; 1 Corinthians 16:19"> And the church which is in their house. Salute Epenetus, my beloved: who is the firstfruits of Asia in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 16:12; Matthew 27:55; 1 Timothy 5:10"> Salute Mary, who hath laboured much among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 16:11; Romans 16:21; Philemon 1:23"> Salute Andronicus and Junias, my kinsmen and fellow prisoners: who are of note among the apostles, who also were in Christ before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 4:1; 1 John 3:14; Romans 16:5"> Salute Ampliatus, most beloved to me in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 16:2–3; Romans 16:21"> Salute Urbanus, our helper in Christ Jesus, and Stachys, my beloved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 14:18; 1 Peter 1:7; Deuteronomy 8:2"> Salute Apelles, approved in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 16:21; Romans 16:7"> Salute them that are of Aristobulus’ household. Salute Herodian, my kinsman. Salute them that are of Narcissus’ household, who are in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:12–13; Hebrews 6:10–11; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> Salute Tryphaena and Tryphosa, who labour in the Lord. Salute Persis, the dearly beloved, who hath much laboured in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 15:21; 1 Timothy 5:2; 2 John 1:1"> Salute Rufus, elect in the Lord, and his mother and mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 1:2; Romans 8:29; Hebrews 3:1"> Salute Asyncritus, Phlegon, Hermas, Patrobas, Hermes, and the brethren that are with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 16:2; Romans 1:7; Romans 16:14"> Salute Philologus and Julia, Nereus and his sister, and Olympias; and all the saints that are with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:26; 1 Corinthians 16:20; 2 Corinthians 13:12"> Salute one another with an holy kiss. All the churches of Christ salute you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jude 1:19; 2 Thessalonians 3:6; 1 Timothy 6:3–5"> Now I beseech you, brethren, to mark them who make dissensions and offences contrary to the doctrine which you have learned, and avoid them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 2:4; Matthew 7:15; 1 Timothy 6:5"> For they that are such, serve not Christ our Lord, but their own belly; and by pleasing speeches and good words, seduce the hearts of the innocent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 10:16; 1 Corinthians 14:20; Philippians 2:15"> For your obedience is published in every place. I rejoice therefore in you. But I would have you to be wise in good, and simple in evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:19; 1 John 3:8; Genesis 3:15"> And the God of peace crush Satan under your feet speedily. The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 20:4; Acts 17:5; Acts 13:1"> Timothy, my fellow labourer, saluteth you, and Lucius, and Jason, and Sosipater, my kinsmen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Galatians 6:11; 1 Corinthians 16:21; Romans 16:8"> I Tertius, who wrote this epistle, salute you in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:14; Acts 19:22; 2 Timothy 4:20"> Caius, my host, and the whole church, saluteth you. Erastus, the treasurer of the city, saluteth you, and Quartus, a brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:7; Romans 2:16; Colossians 1:26–27"> Now to him that is able to establish you, according to my gospel, and the preaching of Jesus Christ, according to the revelation of the mystery, which was kept secret from eternity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 1:5; Galatians 3:8; Romans 15:18"> (Which now is made manifest by the scriptures of the prophets, according to the precept of the eternal God, for the obedience of faith,) known among all nations;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:17; Romans 11:36; Revelation 7:10–12"> To God the only wise, through Jesus Christ, to whom be honour and glory for ever and ever. Amen.</span>
