<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 4:27; Isaiah 62:4; 1 Samuel 2:5"> Give praise, O thou barren, that bearest not: sing forth praise, and make a joyful noise, thou that didst not travail with child: for many are the children of the desolate, more than of her that hath a husband, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 33:20; Jeremiah 10:20; Isaiah 49:19–20"> Enlarge the place of thy tent, and stretch out the skins of thy tabernacles, spare not: lengthen thy cords, and strengthen thy stakes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:35–36; Genesis 28:14; Isaiah 43:5–6"> For thou shalt pass on to the right hand, and to the left: and thy seed shall inherit the Gentiles, and shall inhabit the desolate cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; 1 Peter 2:6; Jeremiah 31:19"> Fear not, for thou shalt not be confounded, nor blush: for thou shalt not be put to shame, because thou shalt forget the shame of thy youth, and shalt remember no more the reproach of thy widowhood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 5:25–27; Hosea 2:19–20; 2 Corinthians 11:2–3"> For he that made thee shall rule over thee, the Lord of hosts is his name: and thy Redeemer, the Holy One of Israel, shall be called the God of all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 62:4; Ecclesiastes 9:9; Hosea 2:14–15"> For the Lord hath called thee as woman forsaken and mourning in spirit, and as a wife cast off from her youth, said thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 30:5; 2 Peter 3:8; Micah 4:6"> For a, small moment have I forsaken thee, but with great mercies will I gather thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:3; Isaiah 60:10; Ezekiel 39:23–24"> In a moment of indignation have I hid my face a little while from thee, but with everlasting kindness have I had mercy on thee, said the Lord thy Redeemer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 12:1; Genesis 8:21; Hebrews 6:16–18"> This thing is to me as in the days of Noe, to whom I swore, that I would no more bring in the waters of Noe upon the earth: so have I sworn not to be angry with thee, and not to rebuke thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 89:33–34; Psalms 46:2; Ephesians 2:4–5"> For the mountains shall be moved, and the hills shall tremble; but my mercy shall not depart from thee, and the covenant of my peace shall not be moved: said the Lord that hath mercy on thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4–6; Acts 14:22; Jeremiah 30:17"> O poor little one, tossed with tempest, without all comfort, behold I will lay thy stones in order, and will lay thy foundations with sapphires,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And I will make thy bulwarks of jasper: and thy gates of graven stones, and all thy borders of desirable stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 6:45; Philippians 4:7; Psalms 119:165"> All thy children shall be taught of the Lord: and great shall be the peace of thy children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 3:25–26; Jeremiah 30:10; Zechariah 9:8"> And thou shalt be founded in justice: depart far from oppression, for thou shalt not fear; and from terror, for it shall not come near thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 41:11–16; Psalms 37:12–13; Zechariah 12:9"> Behold, an inhabitant shall come, who was not with me, he that was a stranger to thee before, shall be joined to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 9:16; Proverbs 16:4; Isaiah 46:11"> Behold, I have created the smith that bloweth the coals in the fire, and bringeth forth an instrument for his work, and I have created the killer to destroy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 8:28–39; Isaiah 54:15; John 10:28–30"> No weapon that is formed against thee shall prosper: and every tongue that resisteth thee in judgment, thou shalt condemn. This is the inheritance of the servants of the Lord, and their justice with me, saith the Lord.</span>
