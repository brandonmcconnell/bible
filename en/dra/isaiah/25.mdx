<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 15:2; Psalms 118:28; Numbers 23:19"> O Lord, thou art my God, I will exalt thee, and give glory to thy name: for thou hast done wonderful things, thy designs of old faithful, amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 17:1; Isaiah 13:22; Isaiah 25:12"> For thou hast reduced the city to a heap, the strong city to ruin, the house of strangers, to be no city, and to be no more built up for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 14:9; Isaiah 49:23–26; Psalms 46:10–11"> Therefore shall a strong people praise thee, the city of mighty nations shall fear thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:12; Isaiah 32:2; James 2:5"> Because thou hast been a strength to the poor, a strength to the needy in his distress: a refuge from the whirlwind, a shadow from the heat. For the blast of the mighty is like a whirlwind beating against a wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 20:8–9; Isaiah 49:10; Psalms 105:39"> Thou shalt bring down the tumult of strangers, as heat in thirst: and as with heat under a burning cloud, thou shalt make the branch of the mighty to wither away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 8:11; Psalms 63:5; Zechariah 9:16–17"> And the Lord of hosts shall make unto all people in this mountain, a feast of fat things, a feast of wine, of fat things full of marrow, of wine purified from the lees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 4:18; Isaiah 60:1–3; Matthew 27:51"> And he shall destroy in this mountain the face of the bond with which all people were tied, and the web that he began over all nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; Revelation 7:17; 1 Corinthians 15:54"> He shall cast death down headlong for ever: and the Lord God shall wipe away tears from every face, and the reproach of his people he shall take away from off the whole earth: for the Lord hath spoken it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 27:14; Genesis 49:18; Micah 7:7"> And they shall say in that day: Lo, this is our God, we have waited for him, and he will save us: this is the Lord, we have patiently waited for him, we shall rejoice and be joyfull in his salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 25:6; Isaiah 18:4; Zephaniah 2:9"> For the hand of the Lord shall rest in this mountain: and Moab shall be trodden down under him, as straw is broken in pieces with the wain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 16:14; Isaiah 2:11; Isaiah 16:6"> And he shall stretch forth his hands under him, as he that swimmeth stretcheth forth his hands to swim: and he shall bring down his glory with the dashing of his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 26:5; Revelation 18:21; Isaiah 14:23"> And the bulwarks of thy high walls shall fall, and be brought low, and shall be pulled down to the ground, even to the dust.</span>
