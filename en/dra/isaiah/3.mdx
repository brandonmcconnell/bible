<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 26:26; Ezekiel 14:13; Isaiah 1:24"> For behold the sovereign the Lord of hosts shall take away from Jerusalem, and from Juda the valiant and the strong, the whole strength of bread, and the whole strength of water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 9:14–15; 2 Kings 24:14–16; Lamentations 5:12–14"> The strong man, and the man of war, the judge, and the prophet, and the cunning man, and the ancient.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 8:18; Deuteronomy 1:15; Exodus 18:21"> The captain over fifty, and the honourable in countenance, and the counsellor, and the architect, and the skillful in eloquent speech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:16; 2 Chronicles 33:1; 1 Kings 3:7–9"> And I will give children to be their princes, and the effeminate shall rule over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:3–8; Malachi 3:5; Matthew 27:28–30"> And the people shall rush one upon another, and every man against his neighbour: the child shall make it tumult against the ancient, and the base against the honourable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 4:1; John 6:15; Judges 11:6–8"> For a man shall take hold or his brother, one of the house of his father, saying: Thou hast a garment, be thou our ruler, and let this ruin be under thy hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 5:13; Lamentations 2:13; Ezekiel 34:4"> In that day he shall answer, saying: I am no healer, and in my house there is no bread, nor clothing: make me not ruler of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:22; Ezekiel 9:9; Malachi 3:13–15"> For Jerusalem is ruined, and Juda is fallen: because their tongue, and their devices are against the Lord, to provoke the eyes of his majesty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 13:13; Genesis 18:20–21; 2 Kings 9:30"> The shew of their countenance hath answered them: and they have proclaimed abroad their sin as Sodom, and they have not hid it: woe to their souls, for evils are rendered to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 128:1–2; Hebrews 6:10; Ecclesiastes 8:12"> Say to the just man that it is well, for he shall eat the fruit of his doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:13; James 2:13; Psalms 62:12"> Woe to the wicked unto evil: for the reward of his hands shall be given him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 3:4; Nahum 3:13; 2 Kings 11:1"> As for my people, their oppressors have stripped them, and women have ruled over them. O my people, they that call thee blessed, the same deceive thee, and destroy the way of thy steps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Micah 6:2; Hosea 4:1–2; Proverbs 22:22–23"> The Lord standeth up to judge, and he standeth to judge the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:4; Psalms 143:2; Isaiah 3:2–3"> The Lord will enter into judgment with the ancients of his people, and its princes: for you have devoured the vineyard, and the spoil of the poor is in your house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Micah 3:2–3; Psalms 94:5; Amos 2:6–7"> Why do you consume my people, and grind the faces of the poor? saith the Lord the God of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 32:9–11; Ezekiel 16:49–50; Isaiah 4:4"> And the Lord said: Because the daughters of Sion are haughty, and have walked with stretched out necks, and wanton glances of their eyes, and made a noise as they walked with their feet and moved in a set pace:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:22; Micah 1:11; Revelation 16:2"> The Lord will make bald the crown of the head of the daughters of Sion, and the Lord will discover their hair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 8:21; Judges 8:26"> In that day the Lord will take away the ornaments of shoes, and little moons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:11; Numbers 31:50; Exodus 35:22"> And chains and necklaces, and bracelets, and bonnets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 39:28; Genesis 35:4; Ezekiel 16:12"> And bodkins, and ornaments of the legs, and tablets, and sweet balls, and earrings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 24:47; 1 Peter 3:3–4; Luke 15:22"> And rings, and jewels hanging on the forehead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And changes of apparel, and short cloaks, and fine linen, and crisping pins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 38:8; Ruth 3:15; Ezekiel 16:10"> And looking-glasses, and lawns, and headbands, and fine veils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Lamentations 2:10; Ezekiel 27:31; Isaiah 22:12"> And instead of a sweet smell there shall be stench, and instead of a girdle, a cord, and instead of curled hair, baldness, and instead of a stomacher, haircloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:9; Isaiah 1:20; Amos 9:10"> Thy fairest men also shall fall by the sword, and thy valiant ones in battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Lamentations 2:10; Jeremiah 14:2; Lamentations 1:4"> And her gates shall lament and mourn, and she shall sit desolate on the ground.</span>
