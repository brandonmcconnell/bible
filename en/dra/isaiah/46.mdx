<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:31; Isaiah 51:7; Isaiah 51:1"> Hearken unto me, O house of Jacob, all the remnant of the house of Israel, who are carried by my bowels, are borne up by my womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 71:18; Psalms 48:14; Psalms 92:14"> Even to your old age I am the same, and to your grey hairs I will carry you: I have made you, and I will bear: I will carry and will save.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 40:18; Isaiah 40:25; Psalms 89:6"> To whom have you likened me, and made me equal, and compared me, and made me like?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 17:29; Isaiah 40:19–20; Habakkuk 2:18–20"> You that contribute gold out of the bag, and weigh out silver in the scales: and hire a goldsmith to make a god: and they fall down and worship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 45:20; Jeremiah 10:5; Isaiah 46:1"> They bear him on their shoulders and carry him, and set him in his piece, and he shall stand, and shall not stir out of his place. Yea, when they shall cry also unto him, he shall not hear: he shall not save them from tribulation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 44:18–21; Psalms 115:8; Ezekiel 18:28"> Remember this, and be ashamed: return, ye transgressors, to the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 111:4; Isaiah 42:9; Isaiah 45:21–22"> Remember the former age, for I am God, and there is no God beside, neither is there the like to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Psalms 33:11; Ephesians 1:9–11"> Who shew from the beginning the things that shall be at last, and from ancient times the things that as yet are not done, saying: My counsel shall stand, and all my will shall be done:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Isaiah 14:24–27; Job 23:13"> Who call a bird from the east, and from a far country the man of my own will, and I have spoken, and will bring it to pass: I have created, and I will do it. Hear me, O ye hardhearted, who are far from justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:5; Isaiah 46:3; Psalms 76:5"> I have brought my justice near, it shall not be afar off: and my salvation shall not tarry. I will give salvation in Sion, and my glory in Israel.</span>
