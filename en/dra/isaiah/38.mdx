<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 6:6; Psalms 91:15; 1 Kings 8:30"> And Ezechias turned his face toward the wall, and prayed to the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Psalms 18:20–27; Hebrews 6:10"> And said: I beseech thee, O Lord, remember how I have walked before thee in truth, and with a perfect heart, and have done that which is good in thy sight. And Ezechias wept with great weeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And the word of the Lord came to Isaias, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 34:5–6; 1 John 5:14–15; Revelation 7:17"> Go and say to Ezechias: Thus saith the Lord the God of David thy father: I have heard thy prayer, and I have seen thy tears: behold I will add to thy days fifteen years:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 37:35; Isaiah 31:4–5; Isaiah 12:6"> And I will deliver thee and this city out of the hand of the king of the Assyrians, and I will protect it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 6:37–39; Isaiah 37:30; Isaiah 7:11–14"> And this shall be a sign to thee from the Lord, that the Lord will do this word which he hath spoken:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 20:9–11; Joshua 10:12–14; Matthew 16:1"> Behold I will bring again the shadow of the lines, by which it is now gone down in the sun dial of Achaz with the sun, ten lines backward. And the sun returned ten lines by the degrees by which it was gone down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 18:1; Exodus 15:1–21; Job 5:18"> The writing of Ezechias king of Juda, when he had been sick, and was recovered of his sickness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 102:24; 2 Corinthians 1:9; Psalms 107:18"> I said: In the midst of my days I shall go to the gates of hell: I sought for the residue of my years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 27:13; Psalms 116:8–9; Psalms 31:22"> I said: I shall not see the Lord God in the land of the living. I shall behold man no more, nor the inhabitant of rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 73:14; 2 Corinthians 5:1; Hebrews 1:12"> My generation is at an end, and it is rolled away from me, as a shepherd’s tent. My life is cut off, as by a weaver: whilst I was yet but beginning, he out me off: from morning even to night thou wilt make an end of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 51:8; Daniel 6:24; Psalms 50:22"> I hoped till morning, as a lion so hath he broken all my bones: from morning even to night thou wilt make an end of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 59:11; Psalms 69:3; Psalms 123:1–4"> I will cry like a young swallow, I will meditate like a dove: my eyes are weakened looking upward: Lord, I suffer violence, answer thou for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 21:27; Job 7:11; Job 10:1"> What shall I say, or what shall he answer for me, whereas he himself hath done it? I will recount to thee all my years in the bitterness of my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 12:10–11; 1 Corinthians 11:32; Psalms 119:25"> O Lord, if man’s life be such, and the life of my spirit be in such things as these, thou shalt correct me, and make me to live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Micah 7:18–19; Jonah 2:6"> Behold in peace is my bitterness most bitter: but thou best delivered my soul that it should not perish, thou hast cast all my sins behind thy back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:10; Psalms 30:9; Psalms 6:5"> For hell shall not confess to thee, neither shall death praise thee: nor shall they that go down into the pit, look for thy truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Psalms 119:175; Psalms 78:3–6"> The living, the living, he shall give praise to thee, as I do this day: the father shall make thy truth known to the children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 116:2; Psalms 150:4; Psalms 145:2"> O Lord, save me, and we will sing our psalms all the days of our life in the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 20:7–8; Mark 7:33; John 9:6"> Now Isaias had ordered that they should take a lump of figs, and lay it as it plaster upon the wound, and that he should be healed.</span>
