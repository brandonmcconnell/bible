<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 3:4–8; Amos 1:9–10; Genesis 10:4"> The burden of Tyre. Howl, ye ships of the sea, for the house is destroyed, from whence they were wont to come: from the land of Cethim it is revealed to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 47:5; Isaiah 41:1; Ezekiel 28:2"> Be silent, you that dwell in the island: the merchants of Sidon passing over the sea, have filled thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:18; 1 Chronicles 13:5; Ezekiel 27:33"> The seed of the Nile in many waters, the harvest of the river is her revenue: and she is become the mart of the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 18:23; Genesis 10:15; Jeremiah 47:3–4"> Be thou ashamed, O Sidon: for the sea speaketh, even the strength of the sea, saying: I have not been in labour, nor have I brought forth, nor have I nourished up young men, nor brought up virgins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 2:9–11; Exodus 15:14–16; Revelation 18:17–19"> When it shall be heard in Egypt, they will be sorry when they shall hear of Tyre:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 23:12; Isaiah 21:15; Isaiah 23:10"> Pass over the seas, howl, ye inhabitants of the island.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 22:2; Isaiah 32:13; Joshua 19:29"> Is not this your city, which gloried from of old in her antiquity? her feet shall carry her afar off to sojourn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 10:8; Jeremiah 50:44–45; Deuteronomy 29:24–28"> Who hath taken this counsel against Tyre, that was formerly crowned, whose merchants were princes, and her traders the nobles of the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 4:6; Isaiah 13:11; Job 40:11–12"> The Lord of hosts hath designed it, to pull down the pride of all glory, and bring to disgrace all the glorious ones of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 12:21; Isaiah 23:14; Psalms 18:32"> Pass thy land as a river, O daughter of the sea, thou hast a girdle no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zechariah 9:3–4; Exodus 14:21; Jeremiah 47:7"> He stretched out his hand over the sea, he troubled kingdoms: the Lord hath given a charge against Chanaan, to destroy the strong ones thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 18:22; Ezekiel 26:13–14; Isaiah 23:1–2"> And he said: Thou shalt glory no more, O virgin daughter of Sidon, who art oppressed: arise and sail over to Cethim, there also thou shalt have no, rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:6; Isaiah 10:5; 2 Kings 17:24"> Behold the land of the Chaldeans, there was not such a people, the Assyrian founded it: they have led away the strong ones thereof into captivity, they have destroyed the houses thereof, they have brought it to ruin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 23:1; Isaiah 23:6; Ezekiel 27:25–30"> Howl, O ye ships of the sea, for your strength is laid waste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:22; Jeremiah 25:9–11; Daniel 7:14"> And it shall come to pass in that day that thou, O Tyre, shalt be forgotten, seventy years, according to the days of one king: but after seventy years, there shall be unto Tyre as the song of a harlot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:14; Proverbs 7:10–12"> Take a harp, go about the city, thou harlot that hast been forgotten: sing well, sing many a song, that thou mayst be remembered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Nahum 3:4; Ezekiel 27:6–36; Revelation 19:2"> And it shall come to pass after seventy years, that the Lord will visit Tyre, and will bring her back again to her traffic: and she shall commit fornication again with all the kingdoms of the world upon the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 72:10; Deuteronomy 26:12–14; Philippians 4:17–18"> And her merchandise and her hire shall be sanctified to the Lord: they shall not be kept in store, nor laid up: for her merchandise shall be for them that shall dwell before the Lord, that they may eat unto fulness, and be clothed for a continuance.</span>
