<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:4; Isaiah 51:12; Isaiah 51:3"> Be comforted, be comforted, my people, saith your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 9:12; Isaiah 61:7; Hosea 2:14"> Speak ye to the heart of Jerusalem, and call to her: for her evil is come to an end, her iniquity is forgiven: she hath received of the hand of the Lord double for all her sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Mark 1:2–5; John 1:23"> The voice of one crying in the desert: Prepare ye the way of the Lord, make straight in the wilderness the paths of our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 3:5; Ezekiel 21:26; Luke 18:14"> Every valley shall be exalted, and every mountain and hill shall be made low, and the crooked shall become straight, and the rough ways plain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:14; Joel 2:28; Isaiah 52:10"> And the glory of the Lord shall be revealed, and all flesh together shall see, that the mouth of the Lord hath spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 103:15–16; 1 Peter 1:24–25; James 1:10–11"> The voice of one, saying: Cry. And I said: What shall I cry? All flesh is grass, and all the glory thereof as the flower of the held.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 41:21"> The grass is withered, and the dower is fallen, because the spirit of the Lord hath blown upon it. Indeed the people is grass:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 13:31; 1 Peter 1:25; Matthew 24:35"> The grass is withered, and the flower is fallen: but the word of our Lord endureth for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7–8; Isaiah 12:2; Isaiah 25:9"> Get thee up upon a high mountain, thou that bringest good tidings to Sion: lift up thy voice with strength, thou that bringest good tidings to Jerusalem: lift it up, fear not. Say to the cities of Juda: Behold your God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 22:12; Isaiah 62:11; Revelation 17:14"> Behold the Lord God shall come with strength, and his arm shall rule: Behold his reward is with him and his work is before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 7:17; Micah 5:4; Psalms 23:1–6"> He shall feed his flock like a shepherd: he shall gather together the lambs with his arm, and shall take them up in his bosom, and he himself shall carry them that are with young.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 30:4; Hebrews 1:10–12; Psalms 102:25–26"> Who hath measured the waters in the hollow of his hand, and weighed the heavens with his palm? who hath poised with three fingers the bulk of the earth, and weighed the mountains in scales, and the hills in a balance?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:16; Romans 11:34; Job 21:22"> Who hath forwarded the spirit of the Lord? or who hath been his counsellor, and hath taught him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 21:22; Colossians 2:3; 1 Corinthians 12:4–6"> With whom hath he consulted, and who hath instructed him, and taught him the path of justice, and taught him knowledge, and shewed him the way of understanding?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 40:22; Job 34:14–15; Jeremiah 10:10"> Behold the Gentiles are as a drop of a bucket, and are counted as the smallest grain of a balance: behold the islands are as a little dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Micah 6:6–7; Psalms 40:6; Psalms 50:9–12"> And Libanus shall not be enough to burn, nor the beasts thereof sufficient for a burnt offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 62:9; Daniel 4:34–35; Isaiah 29:7"> All nations are before him as if they had no being at all, and are counted to him as nothing, and vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 46:5; 1 Samuel 2:2; Isaiah 40:25"> To whom then have you likened God? or what image will you make for him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:9; Habakkuk 2:18–19; Isaiah 46:6–7"> Hath the workman cast a graven statue? or hath the goldsmith formed it with gold, or the silversmith with plates of silver?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:3–4; Isaiah 41:7; Isaiah 46:7"> He hath chosen strong wood, and that will not rot: the skillful workman seeketh how he may set up an idol that may not be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 48:13; Psalms 50:6; Acts 14:17"> Do you not know? hath it not been heard? hath it not been told you from the beginning? have you not understood the foundations of the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 8:27; Psalms 104:2; Isaiah 42:5"> It is he that sitteth upon the globe of the earth, and the inhabitants thereof are as locusts: he that stretcheth out the heavens as nothing, and spreadeth them out as a tent to dwell in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 12:21; Psalms 107:40; Isaiah 34:12"> He that bringeth the searchers of secrets to nothing, that hath made the judges of the earth as vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 41:16; Isaiah 17:13; Haggai 1:9"> And surely their stock was neither planted, nor sown, nor rooted in the earth: suddenly he hath blown upon them, and they are withered, and a whirlwind shall take them away as stubble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 40:18; Deuteronomy 4:33; Deuteronomy 4:15–18"> And to whom have ye likened me, or made me equal, saith the Holy One?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 1:16–17; Jeremiah 32:17–19; Isaiah 48:13"> Lift up your eyes on high, and see who hath created these things: who bringeth out their host by number, and calleth them all by their names: by the greatness of his might, and strength, and power, not one of them was missing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 18:7–8; Isaiah 49:4; Isaiah 49:14"> Why sayest thou, O Jacob, and speakest, O Israel: My way is hid from the Lord, and my judgment is passed over from my God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 11:33–34; Psalms 147:5; Isaiah 55:8–9"> Knowest thou not, or hast thou not heard? the Lord is the everlasting God, who hath created the ends of the earth: he shall not faint, nor labour, neither is there any searching out of his wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Philippians 4:13; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> It is he that giveth strength to the weary, and increaseth force and might to them that are not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Amos 2:14; Psalms 34:10; Psalms 33:16"> Youths shall faint, and labour, and young men shall fall by infirmity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25–26; 2 Corinthians 4:16; Psalms 27:13–14"> But they that hope in the Lord shall renew their strength, they shall take wings as eagles, they shall run and not be weary, they shall walk and not faint.</span>
