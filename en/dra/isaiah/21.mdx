<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 14:23; Isaiah 13:1; Jeremiah 51:42"> The burden of the desert of the sea. As whirlwinds come from the south, it cometh from the desert from a terrible land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 33:1; Jeremiah 49:34; Psalms 60:3"> A grievous vision is told me: he that is unfaithful dealeth unfaithfully: and he that is a spoiler, spoileth. Go up, O Elam, besiege, O Mede: I have made all the mourning thereof to cease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 13:8; Psalms 48:6; Isaiah 26:17"> Therefore are my loins filled with pain, anguish hath taken hold of me, as the anguish of a woman in labour: I fell down at the hearing of it, I was troubled at the seeing of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 21:34–36; 1 Samuel 25:36–38; Daniel 5:1"> My heart failed, darkness amazed me: Babylon my beloved is become a wonder to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:11; Jeremiah 51:57; Jeremiah 51:39"> Prepare the table, behold in the watchtower them that eat and drink: arise, ye princes, take up the shield.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:2–7; Ezekiel 3:17; Habakkuk 2:1–2"> For thus hath the Lord said to me: Go, and set a watchman: and whatsoever he shall see, let him tell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 21:9; Isaiah 37:24; Hebrews 2:1"> And he saw a chariot with two horsemen, a rider upon an ass, and a rider upon a camel: and he beheld them diligently with much heed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:1–2; Isaiah 56:10; 1 Peter 5:8"> And a lion cried out: I am upon the watchtower of the Lord, standing continually by day: and I am upon my ward, standing whole nights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 18:2; Revelation 14:8; Jeremiah 51:8"> Behold this man cometh, the rider upon the chariot with two horsemen, and he answered, and said: Babylon is fallen, she is fallen, and all the graven gods thereof are broken unto the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:33; Micah 4:13; 2 Kings 13:7"> O my thrashing and the children of my door, that which I have heard of the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, I have declared unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 25:14; Obadiah 1:1–16; Amos 1:6"> The burden of Duma calleth to me out of Seir: Watchman, what of the eight? watchman, what of the night?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 17:30–32; Acts 2:37–38; Ezekiel 18:30–32"> The watchman said: The morning cometh, also the night: if you seek, seek: return, come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:23–24; 1 Chronicles 1:32; Ezekiel 27:15"> The burden in Arabia. In the forest at evening you shall sleep, in the paths of Dedanim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 25:15; Job 6:19; Romans 12:20"> Meeting the thirsty bring him water, you that inhabit the land of the south, meet with bread him that fleeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 6:19–20; Isaiah 13:14"> For they are fled from before the swords, from the sword that hung over them, from the bent bow, from the face of a grievous battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 120:5; Isaiah 60:7; Isaiah 16:14"> For thus saith the Lord to me: Within a year, according to the years of a hireling, all the glory of Cedar shall be taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zechariah 1:6; Numbers 23:19; Matthew 24:35"> And the residue of the number of strong archers of the children of Cedar shall be diminished: for the Lord the God of Israel hath spoken it.</span>
