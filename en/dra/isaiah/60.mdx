<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ephesians 5:14; Ephesians 5:8; John 8:12"> Arise, be enlightened, O Jerusalem: for thy light is come, and the glory of the Lord is risen upon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 1:13; 1 Peter 2:9; Acts 17:30–31"> For behold darkness shall cover the earth, and a mist the people: but the Lord shall arise upon thee, and his glory shall be seen upon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 13:47; Isaiah 49:23; Revelation 21:24"> And the Gentiles shall walk in thy light, and kings in the brightness of thy rising.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 42:6; Isaiah 49:20–22; Isaiah 49:18"> Lift up thy eyes round about, and see: all these are gathered together, they are come to thee: thy sons shall come from afar, and thy daughters shall rise up at thy side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 61:6; Isaiah 23:18; Isaiah 60:11"> Then shalt thou see, and abound, and thy heart shall wonder and be enlarged, when the multitude of the sea shall be converted to thee, the. strength of the Gentiles shall come to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 2:11; Psalms 72:10; Psalms 72:15"> The multitude of camels shall cover thee, the dromedaries of Madian and Epha: all they from Saba shall come, bringing gold and frankincense: and shewing forth praise to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; Genesis 25:13; Hebrews 13:15–16"> All the flocks of Cedar shall be gathered together unto thee, the rams of Nabaioth shall minister to thee: they shall be offered upon my acceptable altar, and I will glorify the house of my majesty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 7:9; Luke 13:29; Isaiah 60:4"> Who are these, that fly as clouds, and as doves to their windows?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 55:5; Isaiah 2:16; Isaiah 51:5"> For, the islands wait for me, and the ships of the sea in the beginning: that I may bring thy sons from afar: their silver, and their gold with them, to the name of the Lord thy God, and to the Holy One of Israel, because he hath glorified thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 61:5; Isaiah 49:23; Revelation 21:26"> And the children of strangers shall build up thy walls, and their kings shall minister to thee: for in my wrath have I struck thee, and in my reconciliation have I had mercy upon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 21:25–26; Isaiah 60:5; Isaiah 60:18"> And thy gates shall be open continually: they shall not be shut day nor night, that the strength of the Gentiles may be brought to thee, and their kings may be brought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zechariah 14:12–19; Isaiah 54:15; Psalms 2:12"> For the nation and the kingdom that will not serve thee, shall perish: and the Gentiles shall be wasted with desolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:2; Isaiah 35:2; Psalms 132:7"> The glory of Libanus shall come to thee, the Ar tree, and the box tree, and the pine tree together, to beautify the place of my sanctuary: and I will glorify the place of my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 3:9; Isaiah 49:23; Hebrews 12:22"> And the children of them that afflict thee, shall come bowing down to thee, and all that slandered thee shall worship the steps of thy feet, and shall call thee the city of the Lord, the Sion of the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 61:7; Jeremiah 30:17; Isaiah 49:14–23"> Because thou wast forsaken, and hated, and there was none that passed through thee, I will make thee to be an everlasting glory, a joy unto generation and generation:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; Isaiah 66:11–12; Isaiah 66:14"> And thou shalt suck the milk of the Gentiles, and thou shalt be nursed with the breasts of kings: and thou shalt know that I am the Lord thy Saviour, and thy Redeemer, the Mighty One of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 11:40; Isaiah 30:26; Isaiah 1:26"> For brass I will bring gold, and for iron I will bring silver: and for wood brass, and for stones iron: and I will make thy visitation peace, and thy overseers justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 26:1; Isaiah 11:9; Zechariah 9:8"> Iniquity shall no more be heard in thy land, wasting nor destruction in thy borders, and salvation shall possess thy walls, and praise thy gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 21:23; Revelation 22:5; Zechariah 2:5"> Thou shalt no more have the sun for thy light by day, neither shall the brightness of the moon enlighten thee: but the Lord shall be unto thee for an everlasting light, and thy God for thy glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; Psalms 84:11; Isaiah 35:10"> Thy sun shall go down no more, and thy moon shall not decrease: for the Lord shall be unto thee for an everlasting light, and the days of thy mourning shall be ended.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 15:13; Psalms 37:22; Psalms 37:11"> And thy people shall be all just, they shall inherit the land for ever, the branch of my planting, the work of my hand to glorify me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:3; Hebrews 10:36; Luke 18:7"> The least shall become a thousand, and a little one a most strong nation: I the Lord will suddenly do this thing in its time.</span>
