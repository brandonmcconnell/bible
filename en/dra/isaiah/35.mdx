<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:35; Isaiah 51:3; Isaiah 55:12–13"> The land that was desolate and impassable shall be glad, and the wilderness shall rejoice, and shall flourish like the lily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 25:9; Isaiah 60:13; Amos 9:13–15"> It shall bud forth and blossom, and shall rejoice with joy and praise: the glory of Libanus is given to it: the beauty of Carmel, and Saron, they shall see the glory of the Lord, and the beauty of our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 12:12; Job 4:3–4; Luke 22:32"> Strengthen ye the feeble hands, and confirm the weak knees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 5:7–9; Isaiah 41:10–14; Haggai 2:4"> Say to the fainthearted: Take courage, and fear not: behold your God will bring the revenge of recompense: God himself will come and will save you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 42:6–7; Luke 7:20–23; Isaiah 29:18"> Then shall the eyes of the blind be opened, and the ears of the deaf shall be unstopped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 5:8–9; John 7:37–39; Luke 11:14"> Then shall the lame man leap as a hart, and the tongue of the dumb shall be free: for waters are broken out in the desert, and streams in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 26:18; Isaiah 49:10; John 4:14"> And that which was dry land, shall become a pool, and the thirsty land springs of water. In the dens where dragons dwell before, shall rise up the verdure of the reed and the bulrush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 7:13–14; Isaiah 42:16; John 14:6"> And a path and a way shall be there, and it shall be called the holy way: the unclean shall not pass over it, and this shall be unto you a straight way, so that fools shall not err therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 62:12; Hosea 2:18; Leviticus 26:6"> No lion shall be there, nor shall any mischievous beast go up by it, nor be found there: but they shall walk there that shall be delivered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 30:19; Revelation 21:4; Isaiah 25:8"> And the redeemed of the Lord shall return, and shall come into Sion with praise, and everlasting joy shall be upon their heads: they shall obtain joy and gladness, and sorrow and mourning shall flee away.</span>
