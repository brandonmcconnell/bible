<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 22:20; Psalms 12:1; 2 Chronicles 34:28"> The just perisheth, and no man layeth it to heart, and men of mercy are taken away, because there is none that understandeth; for the just man is taken away from before the face of evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; 2 Corinthians 5:1; Luke 2:29"> Let peace come, let him rest in his bed that hath walked in his uprightness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 3:10; Matthew 16:4; Hosea 1:2"> But draw near hither, you sons of the sorceress, the seed of the adulterer, and of the harlot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Peter 2:13; Psalms 35:21; Isaiah 1:4"> Upon whom have you jested? upon whom have you opened your mouth wide, and put out your tongue? are not you wicked children, a false seed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 1:29; 2 Kings 16:3–4; Ezekiel 16:20"> Who seek your comfort in idols under every green tree, sacrificing children in the torrents, under the high rocks?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:9; Jeremiah 7:18; Habakkuk 2:19"> In the parts of the torrent is thy portion, this is thy lot: and thou hast poured out libations to them, thou hast offered sacrifice. Shall I not be angry at these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:16; Ezekiel 23:41; Jeremiah 2:20"> Upon a high and lofty mountain thou hast laid thy bed, and hast gone up thither to offer victims.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:32; Ezekiel 23:2–20; Ezekiel 16:25–28"> And behind the door, and behind the post thou best set up thy remembrance: for thou hast discovered thyself near me, and hast received an adulterer: thou hast enlarged thy bed, and made a covenant with them: thou hast loved their bed with open hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:16; Colossians 2:18; Ezekiel 23:40"> And thou hast adorned thyself for the king with ointment, and hast multiplied thy perfumes. Thou hast sent thy messengers far off, and wast debased even to hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:25; Jeremiah 18:12; Jeremiah 5:3"> Thou hast been wearied in the multitude of thy ways: yet thou saidst not: I will rest: thou hast found life of thy hand, therefore thou hast not asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 51:12–13; Proverbs 29:25; Psalms 50:21"> For whom hast thou been solicitous and afraid, that thou hast lied, and hast not been mindful of me, nor thought on me in thy heart? for I am silent, and as one that seeth not, and thou hast forgotten me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 23:13; Micah 3:2–4; Isaiah 1:11–15"> I will declare thy justice, and thy works shall not profit thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 37:9; Psalms 37:3; Isaiah 56:7"> When thou shalt cry, let thy companies deliver thee, but the wind shall carry them all off, a breeze shall take them away, but he that putteth his trust in me, shall inherit the land, and shall possess my holy mount.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 62:10; Isaiah 35:8; Isaiah 40:3"> And I will say: Make a way: give free passage, turn out of the path, take away the stumblingblocks out of the way of my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 34:18; Isaiah 66:1–2; Psalms 138:6"> For thus saith the High and the Eminent that inhabiteth eternity: and his name is Holy, who dwelleth in the high and holy place, and with a contrite and humble spirit, to revive the spirit of the humble, and to revive the heart of the contrite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Micah 7:18; Jeremiah 10:24; Psalms 103:9–16"> For I will not contend for ever, neither will I be angry unto the end: because the spirit shall go forth from my face, and breathings I will make.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 56:11; Jeremiah 6:13; Colossians 3:5"> For the iniquity of his covetousness I was angry, and I struck him: I hid my face from thee, and was angry: and he went away wandering in his own heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:6; Revelation 7:17; Jeremiah 3:22"> I saw his ways, and I healed him, and brought him back, and restored comforts to him, and to them that mourn for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15; Acts 2:39; Luke 2:14"> I created the fruit of the lips, peace, peace to him that is far off, and to him that is near, said the Lord, and I healed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 3:11; Proverbs 4:16–17; Job 18:5–14"> But the wicked are like the raging sea, which cannot rest, and the waves thereof cast up dirt and mire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 48:22; Isaiah 3:11; 2 Kings 9:22"> There is no peace to the wicked, saith the Lord God.</span>
