<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 10:20; Romans 9:24–26; 1 Peter 2:10"> They have sought me that before asked not for me, they have found me that sought me not. I said: Behold me, behold me, to a nation that did not call upon my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 10:21; Proverbs 1:24; Jeremiah 4:14"> I have spread forth my hands all the day to an unbelieving people, who walk in a way that is not good after their own thoughts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 1:29; Deuteronomy 32:21; Isaiah 66:17"> A people that continually provoke me to anger before my face: that immolate in gardens, and sacrifice upon bricks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 66:17; Leviticus 11:7; Isaiah 66:3"> That dwell in sepulchres, and sleep in the temple of idols: that eat swine’s flesh, and profane broth is in their vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 18:9–12; Luke 7:39; Proverbs 16:5"> That say: Depart from me, come not near me, because thou art unclean: these shall be smoke in my anger, a fire burning all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 50:3; Jeremiah 16:18; Psalms 79:12"> Behold it is written before me: I will not be silent, but I will render and repay into their bosom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:27–28; Isaiah 57:7; Exodus 20:5"> Your iniquities, and the iniquities of your fathers together, saith the Lord, who have sacrificed upon the mountains, and have reproached me upon the hills; and I will measure back their first work in their bosom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 9:27–29; Amos 9:8–9; Romans 11:24–26"> Thus saith the Lord: As if a grain be found in a cluster, and it be said: Destroy it not, because it is a blessing: so will I do for the sake of my servants, that I may not destroy the whole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 9:11–15; Isaiah 27:6; Romans 11:5–7"> And I will bring forth a seed out of Jacob, and out of Juda a possessor of my mountains: and my elect shall inherit it, and my servants shall dwell there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 35:2; Isaiah 33:9; Hosea 2:15"> And the plains shall be turned to folds of hocks, and the valley of Achor into a place for the herds to lie down in, for my people that have sought me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 2:2; Isaiah 1:28; Deuteronomy 32:17"> And you, that have forsaken the Lord, that have forgotten my holy mount, that set a table for fortune, and offer libations upon it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:13; 2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Zechariah 7:11–13"> I will number you in the sword, and you shall all fall by slaughter: because I called and you did not answer: I spoke, and you did not hear: and you did evil in my eyes, and you have chosen the things that displease me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Malachi 3:18; Isaiah 66:14; Isaiah 61:7"> Therefore thus saith the Lord God: Behold my servants shall eat, and you shall be hungry: behold my servants shall drink, and you shall be thirsty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Psalms 66:4; Matthew 22:13"> Behold my servants shall rejoice, and you shall be confounded: behold my servants shall praise for joyfulness of heart, and you shall cry for sorrow of heart, and shall howl for grief of spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 62:2; Romans 9:26; Acts 11:26"> And you shall leave your name for an execration to my elect: and the Lord God shall slay thee, and call his servants by another name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 19:18; Jeremiah 4:2; Psalms 31:5"> In which he that is blessed upon the earth, shall be blessed in God, amen: and he that sweareth in the earth, shall swear by God, amen: because the former distresses are forgotten, and because they are hid from my eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Peter 3:13; Revelation 21:1–5; Isaiah 66:22"> For behold I create new heavens, and a new earth: and the former things shall not be in remembrance, and they shell not come upon the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 98:1–9; Revelation 19:1–6; Isaiah 66:10–14"> But you shall be glad and rejoice for ever in these things, which I create: for behold I create Jerusalem a rejoicing, and the people thereof joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; Revelation 7:17; Isaiah 25:8"> And I will rejoice in Jerusalem, and joy in my people, and the voice of weeping shall no more be heard in her, nor the voice of crying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 5:26; Ecclesiastes 8:12–13; Deuteronomy 4:40"> There shall no more be an infant of days there, nor an old man that shall not fill up his days: for the child shall die a hundred years old, and the sinner being a hundred years old shall be accursed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Amos 9:14; Isaiah 62:8–9; Isaiah 32:18"> And they shall build houses, and inhabit them; and they shall plant vineyards, and eat the fruits of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 92:12–14; Psalms 91:16; Psalms 21:4"> They shall not build, and another inhabit; they shall not plant, and another eat: for as the days of a tree, so shall be the days of my people, and the works of their hands shall be of long continuance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 61:9; Jeremiah 32:38–39; Deuteronomy 28:3–12"> My elect shall not labour in vain, nor bring forth in trouble; for they are the seed of the blessed of the Lord, and their posterity with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 10:12; 1 John 5:14–15; Isaiah 58:9"> And it shall come to pass, that before they call, I will hear; as they are yet speaking, I will hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 11:6–9; Micah 4:3; Acts 9:19–21"> The wolf and the lamb shall feed together; the lion and the ox shall eat straw; and dust shall be the serpent’s food: they shall not hurt nor kill in all my holy mountain, saith the Lord.</span>
