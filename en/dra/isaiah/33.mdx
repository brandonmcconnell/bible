<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Isaiah 24:16; Isaiah 10:12"> Woe to thee that spoilest, shalt not thou thyself also be spoiled? and thou that despisest, shalt not thyself also be despised? when thou shalt have made an end of spoiling, thou shalt be spoiled: when being wearied thou shalt cease to despise, thou shalt be despised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:3–4; Isaiah 59:16; Isaiah 30:18–19"> O Lord, have mercy on us: for we have waited for thee: be thou our arm in the morning, and our salvation in the time of trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 10:13–14; Isaiah 10:32–34; Isaiah 37:11–18"> At the voice of the angel the people fled, and at the lifting up thyself the nations are scattered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 33:23; Joel 2:25; 2 Chronicles 20:25"> And your spoils shall be gathered together as the locusts are gathered, as when the ditches are full of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 2:17; Ephesians 1:20–21; Isaiah 32:15–18"> The Lord is magnified, for he hath dwelt on high: he hath filled Sion with judgment and justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 112:1–3; Proverbs 19:23; 1 Timothy 4:8"> And there shall be faith in thy times: riches of salvation, wisdom and knowledge: the fear of the Lord is his treasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 18:18; 2 Kings 18:37–3; Isaiah 36:22"> Behold they that see shall cry without, the angels of peace shall weep bitterly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 5:6; Isaiah 35:8; Lamentations 1:4"> The ways are made desolate, no one passeth by the road, the covenant is made void, he hath rejected the cities, he hath not regarded the men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 35:2; Nahum 1:4; Isaiah 65:10"> The land hath mourned, and languished: Libanus is confounded and become foul, and Saron is become as a desert: and Basan and Carmel are shaken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 12:5; Psalms 102:13–18; Amos 6:1"> Now will I rise up, saith the Lord: now will I be exalted, now will I lift up myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 7:14; Isaiah 59:4; James 1:15"> You shall conceive heat, you shall bring forth stubble: your breath as fire shall devour you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 27:4; 2 Samuel 23:6–7; Isaiah 10:17"> And the people shall be as ashes after a fire, as a bundle of thorns they shall be burnt with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 48:10; Ephesians 2:11–18; Daniel 3:27–3"> Hear, you that are far off, what I have done, and you that are near know my strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Matthew 25:46; Isaiah 66:24"> The sinners in Sion are afraid, trembling hath seized upon the hypocrites. Which of you can dwell with devouring fire? which of you shall dwell with everlasting burnings?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:37; 1 John 3:7; Ephesians 5:11–13"> He that walketh in justices, and speaketh truth, that casteth away avarice by oppression, and shaketh his hands from all bribes, that stoppeth his ears lest he hear blood, and shutteth his eyes that he may see no evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 12:29–31; Isaiah 32:18; Proverbs 1:33"> He shall dwell on high, the fortifications of rocks shall be his highness: bread is given him, his waters are sure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zechariah 9:17; John 14:21; 2 Corinthians 4:18"> His eyes shall see the king in his beauty, they shall see the land far off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:20; Psalms 31:7–8; Isaiah 38:9–22"> Thy heart shall meditate fear: where is the learned? where is he that pondereth the words of the law? where is the teacher of little ones?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 28:11; Deuteronomy 28:49–50; Jeremiah 5:15"> The shameless people thou shalt not see, the people of profound speech: so that thou canst not understand the eloquence of his tongue, in whom there is no wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 46:5; Isaiah 32:18; Isaiah 54:2"> Look upon Sion the city of our solemnity: thy eyes shall see Jerusalem, a rich habitation, a tabernacle that cannot be removed: neither shall the nails thereof be taken away for ever, neither shall any of the cords thereof be broken:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 46:4–5; Isaiah 48:18; Isaiah 66:12"> Because only there our Lord is magnificent: it place of rivers, very broad and spacious streams: no ship with oars shall pass by it, neither shall the great galley pass through it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 5:31; James 4:12; Zephaniah 3:15–17"> For the Lord is our judge, the Lord is our lawgiver, the Lord is our king: he will save us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 7:8; Psalms 68:12; 1 Corinthians 1:27"> Thy tacklings are loosed, and they shall be of no strength: thy mast shall be in such condition, that thou shalt not be able to spread the flag. Then shall the spoils of much prey be divided: the lame shall take the spoil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:20; 1 John 1:7–9; Revelation 21:4"> Neither shall he that is near, say: I am feeble. The people that dwell therein, shall have their iniquity taken away from them.</span>
