<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Micah 1:3–4; Mark 1:10; Revelation 20:11"> That thou wouldst rend the heavens, and wouldst come down: the mountains would melt away at thy presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 99:1; Jeremiah 33:9; Ezekiel 38:22–23"> They would melt as at the burning of fire, the waters would burn with fire, that thy name might be made known to thy enemies: that the nations might tremble at thy presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 34:10; Psalms 65:5–6; Psalms 66:3"> When thou shalt do wonderful things, we shall not bear them: thou didst come down, and at thy presence the mountains melted away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:9–10; Psalms 31:19; James 5:7"> From the beginning of the world they have not heard, nor perceived with the ears: the eye hath not seen, O God, besides thee, what things thou hast prepared for them that wait for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 3:13–15; Hebrews 4:16; Acts 10:35"> Thou hast met him that rejoiceth, and doth justice: in thy ways they shall remember thee: behold thou art angry, and we have sinned: in them we have been always, and we shall be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 7:18; Romans 7:24; Ephesians 2:1–2"> And we are all become as one unclean, and all our justices as the rag of a menstruous woman: and we have all fallen as a leaf, and our iniquities, like the wind, have taken us away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:30; Hosea 7:14; Isaiah 59:16"> There is none that calleth upon thy name: that riseth up, and taketh hold of thee: thou hast hid thy face from us, and hast crushed us in the hand of our iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; Psalms 119:73; Jeremiah 18:2–6"> And now, O Lord, thou art our father, and we are clay: and thou art our maker, and we all are the works of thy hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Micah 7:18–20; Psalms 74:1–2; Psalms 79:13"> Be not very angry, O Lord, and remember no longer our iniquity: behold, see we are all thy people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 1:7; Luke 21:24; 2 Chronicles 36:19–21"> The city of thy sanctuary is become a desert, Sion is made a desert, Jerusalem is desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 25:9; Psalms 74:5–7; Lamentations 1:7"> The house of our holiness, and of our glory, where our fathers praised thee, is burnt with fire, and all our lovely things are turned into ruins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 42:14; Psalms 74:10–11; Psalms 83:1"> Wilt thou refrain thyself, O Lord, upon these things, wilt thou hold thy peace, and afflict us vehemently?</span>
