<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:2; Psalms 68:26; Isaiah 65:16"> Hear ye these things, O house of Jacob, you that are called by the name of Israel, and are come forth out of the waters of Juda, you who swear by the name of the Lord, and make mention of the God of Israel, but not in truth, nor in justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Romans 2:17; Isaiah 52:1"> For they are called of the holy city, and are established upon the God of Israel: the Lord of hosts is his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 42:9; Joshua 21:45; Isaiah 41:22"> The former things of old I have declared, and they went forth out of my mouth, and I have made them to be heard: I did them suddenly and they came to pass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Exodus 32:9; Zechariah 7:11–12"> For I knew that thou art stubborn, and thy neck is as an iron sinew, and thy forehead as brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 48:3; Luke 1:70; Acts 15:18"> I foretold thee of old, before they came to pass I told thee, lest thou shouldst say: My idols have done these things, and my graven and molten things have commanded them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 4:1; Isaiah 42:9; Romans 16:25–26"> See now all the things which thou hast heard: but have you declared them? I have shewn thee new things from that time, and things are kept which thou knowest not:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> They are created now, and not of old: and before the day, when thou heardest them not, lest thou shouldst say: Behold I knew them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 58:3; Hosea 5:7; Jeremiah 6:10"> Thou hast neither heard, nor known, neither was thy ear opened of old. For I know that transgressing thou wilt transgress, and I have called thee a transgressor from the womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 79:9; Psalms 106:8; Psalms 143:11"> For my name’s sake I will remove my wrath far off: and for my praise I will bridle thee, lest thou shouldst perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 4:12; Proverbs 17:3; Job 23:10"> Behold I have refined thee, but not as silver, I have chosen thee in the furnace of poverty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 42:8; Ezekiel 20:9; Isaiah 37:35"> For my own sake, for my own sake will I do it, that I may not be blasphemed: and I will not give my glory to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 41:4; Revelation 22:13; Revelation 1:8"> Hearken to me, O Jacob, and thou Israel whom I call: I am he, I am the first, and I am the last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 1:10–12; Psalms 102:25; Isaiah 40:26"> My hand also hath founded the earth, and my right hand hath measured the heavens: I shall call them, and they shall stand together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 43:9; Jeremiah 50:21–29; Isaiah 13:17–18"> Assemble yourselves together, all you, and hear: who among them hath declared these things? the Lord hath loved him, he will do his pleasure in Babylon, and his arm shall be on the Chaldeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 1:8; Isaiah 45:1–2; Psalms 45:4"> I, even I have spoken and called him: I have brought him, and his way is made prosperous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 18:20; John 20:21–22; John 3:34"> Come ye near unto me, and hear this: I have not spoken in secret from the beginning: from the time before it was done, I was there, and now the Lord God hath sent me, and his spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 32:8; Psalms 25:12; Job 22:21–22"> Thus saith the Lord thy redeemer, the Holy One of Israel: I am the Lord thy God that teach thee profitable things, that govern thee in the way that thou walkest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:29; Isaiah 66:12; Psalms 119:165"> O that thou hadst hearkened to my commandments: thy peace had been as a river, and thy justice as the waves of the sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 10:22; Genesis 22:17; Romans 9:27"> And thy seed had been as the sand, and the offspring of thy bowels like the gravel thereof: his name should not have perished, nor have been destroyed from before my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:45; Jeremiah 50:8; Isaiah 52:9"> Come forth out of Babylon, flee ye from the Chaldeans, declare it with the voice of joy: make this to be heard, and speak it out even to the ends of the earth. Say: The Lord hath redeemed his servant Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 17:6; Isaiah 43:19–20; Psalms 105:41"> They thirsted not in the desert, when he led them out: he brought forth water out of the rock for them, and he clove the rock, and the waters gushed out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 57:21; Job 15:20–24; Luke 19:42"> There is no peace to the wicked, saith the Lord.</span>
