<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 13:11–14; Psalms 85:9; Matthew 3:2"> Thus saith the Lord: Keep ye judgment, and do justice: for my salvation is near to come, and my justice to be revealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 31:13–16; Isaiah 58:13; Psalms 112:1"> Blessed is the man that doth this, and the son of man that shall lay hold on this: that keepeth the sabbath from profaning it, that keepeth his hands from doing any evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:17; Acts 10:34; Matthew 19:12–30"> And let not the son of the stranger, that adhereth to the Lord, speak, saying: The Lord will divide and separate me from his people. And let not the eunuch say: Behold I am a dry tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 119:111; Luke 10:42; Isaiah 27:5"> For thus saith the Lord to the eunuchs, They that shall keep my sabbaths, and shall choose the things that please me, and shall hold fast my covenant:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 3:12; 1 John 3:1; Revelation 3:5"> I will give to them in my house, and within my walls, a place, and a name better than sons and daughters: I will give them an everlasting name which shall never perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:5; Isaiah 56:2–4; Isaiah 58:13"> And the children of the stranger that adhere to the Lord, to worship him, and to love his name, to be his servants: every one that keepeth the sabbath from profaning it, and that holdeth fast my covenant:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 11:17; Isaiah 2:2–3; Malachi 1:11"> I will bring them into my holy mount, and will make them joyful in my house of prayer: their holocausts, and their victims shall please me upon my altar: for my house shall be called the house of prayer, for all nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:16; Isaiah 54:7; Isaiah 60:3–11"> The Lord God, who gathereth the scattered of Israel, saith: I will still gather unto him his congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:9; Revelation 19:17–18; Isaiah 18:6"> All ye beasts of the field come to devour, all ye beasts of the forest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 29:10; Philippians 3:2; Isaiah 58:1"> His watchmen are all blind, they are all ignorant: dumb dogs not able to bark, seeing vain things, sleeping and loving dreams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2–3; Ezekiel 13:19; Micah 3:11"> And meet impudent dogs, they never had enough: the shepherds themselves knew no understanding: all have turned aside into their own way, every one after his own gain, from the first even to the last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 10:6; Proverbs 27:1; Luke 12:19–20"> Come, let us take wine, and be filled with drunkenness: and it shall be as today, so also tomorrow, and much more.</span>
