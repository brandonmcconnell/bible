<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 12:18–21; Luke 3:22; Isaiah 61:1"> Behold my servant, I will uphold him: my elect, my soul delighteth in him: I have given my spirit upon him, he shall bring forth judgment to the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 12:16–20; 2 Timothy 2:24; Matthew 11:29"> He shall not cry, nor have respect to person, neither shall his voice be heard abroad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Jeremiah 31:25; Psalms 147:3"> The bruised reed he shall not break, and smoking flax he shall not quench: he shall bring forth judgment unto truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2–12; Genesis 49:10; Isaiah 52:13–15"> He shall not be sad, nor troublesome, till he set judgment in the earth: and the islands shall wait for his law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 40:22; Isaiah 45:12; Jeremiah 10:12"> Thus saith the Lord God that created the heavens, and stretched them out: that established the earth, and the things that spring out of it: that giveth breath to the people upon it, and spirit to them that tread thereon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 2:32; John 8:12; Acts 13:47"> I the Lord have called thee in justice, and taken thee by the hand, and preserved thee. And I have given thee for a covenant of the people, for a light of the Gentiles:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 61:1; Ephesians 1:17–18; Isaiah 35:5"> That thou mightest open the eyes of the blind, and bring forth the prisoner out of prison, and them that sit in darkness out of the prison house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 48:11; Isaiah 43:11; Exodus 34:14"> I the Lord, this is my name: I will not give my glory to another, nor my praise to graven things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 43:19; Isaiah 46:9–10; John 13:19"> The things that were first, behold they are come: and new things do I declare: before they spring forth, I will make you head them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 33:3; Psalms 40:3; Romans 15:9–11"> Sing ye to the Lora a new song, his praise is from the ends of the earth: you that go down to the sea, and all that are therein: ye islands, and ye inhabitants of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 32:16; Isaiah 60:7; Isaiah 41:18–19"> Let the desert and the cities thereof be exalted: Cedar shall dwell in houses: ye inhabitants of Petra, give praise, they shall cry from the top of the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 15:9–11; Psalms 117:1–2; Revelation 7:9–12"> They shall give glory to the Lord, and shall declare his praise in the islands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 15:1–3; Psalms 78:65; Isaiah 59:16–19"> The Lord shall go forth as a mighty man, as a man of war shall he stir up zeal: he shall shout and cry: he shall prevail against his enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 83:1–2; Ecclesiastes 8:11–12; 2 Peter 3:15"> I have always held my peace, I have I kept silence, I have been patient, I will speak now as a woman in labour: I will destroy, and swallow up at once.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 50:2; Nahum 1:4–6; Isaiah 44:27"> I will lay waste the mountains and hills, and will make all their grass to wither: and I will turn rivers into islands, and will dry up the standing pools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 30:21; Luke 3:5; Isaiah 60:19–20"> And I will lead the blind into the way which they know not: and in the paths which they were ignorant of I will make them walk: I will make darkness light before them, and crooked things straight: these things have I done to them, and have not forsaken them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 97:7; Isaiah 1:29; Isaiah 44:11"> They are turned back: let them be greatly confounded, that trust in a graven thing, that say to a molten thing: You are our god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 43:8; Proverbs 20:12; Mark 7:34–37"> Hear, ye deaf, and, ye blind, behold that you may see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4; Ezekiel 12:2; Jeremiah 5:21"> Who is blind, but my servant? or deaf, but he to whom I have sent my messengers? Who is blind, but he that is sold? or who is blind, but the servant of the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:2–4; Romans 2:21; Acts 28:22–27"> Thou that seest many things, wilt thou not observe them? thou that hast ears open, wilt thou not hear?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 8:10; Romans 3:31; Isaiah 46:12–13"> And the Lord was willing to sanctify him, and to magnify the law, and exalt it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 102:20; Jeremiah 50:17; Isaiah 51:23"> But this is a people that is robbed and wasted: they are all the snare of young men, and they are hid in the houses of prisons: they are made a prey, and there is none to deliver them: a spoil, and there is none that saith: Restore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 3:22–23; Isaiah 48:18; Deuteronomy 32:29"> Who is there among you that will give ear to this, that will attend and hearken for times to come?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 47:6; Isaiah 10:5–6; Matthew 22:7"> Who hath given Jacob for a spoil, and Israel to robbers? hath not the Lord himself, against whom we have sinned? And they would not walk in his ways, and they have not hearkened to his law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hosea 7:9; Isaiah 57:11; Malachi 2:2"> And he hath poured out upon him the indignation of his fury, and a strong battle, and hath burnt him round about, and he knew not: and set him on fire, and he understood not.</span>
