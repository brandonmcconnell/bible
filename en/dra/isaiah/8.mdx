<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 16:10–11; 2 Kings 18:2; 2 Corinthians 13:1"> And I took unto me faithful witnesses, Urias the priest, and Zacharias the son of Barachias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 1:3–9; Judges 4:4; Isaiah 7:13–14"> And I went to the prophetess, and she conceived, and bore a son. And the Lord said to me: Call his name, Hasten to take away the spoils: Make haste to take away the prey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 9:11; 2 Kings 16:9; Deuteronomy 1:39"> For before the child know to call his father and his mother, the strength of Damascus, and the spoils of Samaria shall be taken away before the king of the Assyrians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 7:10"> And the Lord spoke to me again, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 9:7; Nehemiah 3:15; Isaiah 7:6"> Forasmuch as this people hath cast away the waters of Siloe, that go with silence, and hath rather taken Basin, and the son of Romelia:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 17:12–13; Genesis 6:17; Isaiah 7:17"> Therefore behold the Lord will bring upon them the waters of the river strong and many, the king of the Assyrians, and all his glory: and he shall come up over all his channels, and shall overflow all his banks,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Isaiah 30:28; Matthew 1:23"> And shall pass through Juda, overflowing, and going over shall reach even to the neck. And the stretching out of his wings shall fill the breadth of thy land, O Emmanuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 14:5–6; Proverbs 11:21; Psalms 37:14–15"> Gather yourselves together, O ye people, and be overcome, and give ear, all ye lands afar off: strengthen yourselves, and be overcome, gird yourselves, and be overcome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Lamentations 3:37; Romans 8:31; Job 5:12"> Take counsel together, and it shall be defeated: speak a word, and it shall not be done: because God is with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:14; Proverbs 1:15; Jeremiah 15:19"> For thus saith the Lord to me: As he hath taught me, with a strong arm, that I should not walk in the way of this people, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:14–15; Luke 21:9; Psalms 53:5"> Say ye not: A conspiracy: for all that this people speaketh, is a conspiracy: neither fear ye their fear, nor be afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 12:5; Isaiah 26:3–4; Revelation 15:4"> Sanctify the Lord of hosts himself: and let him be your fear, and let him be your dread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 2:8; Romans 9:32–33; Isaiah 28:16"> And he shall be a sanctification to you. But for a stone or stumbling, and for a rock of offence to the two houses of Israel, for a snare and a ruin to the inhabitants of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 21:44; Luke 20:17–18; Isaiah 28:13"> And very many of them shall stumble and fall, and shall be broken in pieces, and shall be snared, and taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 12:4; Hebrews 3:5; Mark 4:10–11"> Bind up the testimony, seal the law among my disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36–39; Habakkuk 2:3; Lamentations 3:25–26"> And I will wait for the Lord, who hath hid his face from the house of Jacob, and I will look for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 2:13–14; Luke 2:34; Zechariah 8:3"> Behold I and my children, whom the Lord hath given me for a sign, and for a wonder in Israel from the Lord of hosts, who dwelleth in mount Sion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 10:13; Leviticus 20:6; 2 Chronicles 33:6"> And when they shall say to you: Seek of pythons, and of diviners, who mutter in their enchantments: should not the people seek of their God, for the living of the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 1:19; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; Isaiah 30:8–11"> To the law rather, and to the testimony. And if they speak not according to this word, they shall not have the morning light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 9:20; Proverbs 19:3; Lamentations 4:4–5"> And they shall pass by it, they shall fall, and be hungry: and when they shall be hungry, they will be angry, and curse their king, and their God, and look upwards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 5:30; Matthew 22:13; Jeremiah 13:16"> And they shall look to the earth, and behold trouble and darkness, weakness and distress, and a mist following them, and they cannot fly away from their distress.</span>
