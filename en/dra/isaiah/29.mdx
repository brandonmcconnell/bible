<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 4:4–5; 2 Samuel 5:9; Micah 6:6–7"> Woe to Ariel, to Ariel the city which David took: year is added to year: the solemnities are at an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 5:25–30; Isaiah 3:26; Isaiah 10:32"> And I will make a trench about Ariel, and it shall be in sorrow and mourning, and it shall be to me as Ariel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 19:43–44; Ezekiel 21:22; 2 Kings 25:1–4"> And I will make a circle round about thee, and will cast up a rampart against thee, and raise up bulwarks to besiege thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 8:19; Psalms 44:25; Isaiah 3:8"> Thou shalt be brought down, thou shalt speak out of the earth, and thy speech shall be heard out of the ground: and thy voice shall be from the earth like that of the python, and out of the ground thy speech shall mutter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Isaiah 17:13–14; Isaiah 37:36"> And the multitude of them that fan thee, shall be like small dust: and as ashes passing away, the multitude of them that have prevailed against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 13:8; 1 Samuel 2:10; Matthew 24:7"> And it shall be at an instant suddenly. A visitation shall come from the Lord of hosts in thunder, and with earthquake, and with a great noise of whirlwind and tempest, and with the flame of devouring fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 20:8; Micah 4:11–12; Zechariah 12:9"> And the multitude of all nations that have fought against Ariel, shall be as the dream of a vision by night, and all that have fought, and besieged and prevailed against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 44:12; Psalms 73:20; Isaiah 10:7–16"> And as he that is hungry dreameth, and eateth, but when he is awake, his soul is empty: and as he that is thirsty dreameth, and drinketh, and after he is awake, is yet faint with thirst, and his soul is empty: so shall be the multitude of all the Gentiles, that have fought against mount Sion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 51:21–22; Isaiah 19:14; Revelation 17:6"> Be astonished, and wander, waver, and stagger: be drunk, and not with wine: stagger, and not with drunkenness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 11:8; Isaiah 44:18; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–12"> For the Lord hath mingled for you the spirit of a deep sleep, he will shut up your eyes, he will cover your prophets and princes, that see visions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 13:11; Daniel 12:4; Isaiah 8:16"> And the vision of all shall be unto you as the words of a book that is sealed, which when they shall deliver to one that is learned, they shall say: Read this: and he shall answer: I cannot, for it is sealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 7:15–16; Isaiah 29:18; Jeremiah 5:4"> And the book shall be given to one that knoweth no letters, and it shall be said to him: Read: and he shall answer: I know no letters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:2; Ezekiel 33:31–33; Mark 7:2–13"> And the Lord said: Forasmuch as this people draw near me with their mouth, and with their lips glorify me, but their heart is far from me, and they have feared me with the commandment and doctrines of men:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:5; 1 Corinthians 1:19–24; Isaiah 28:21"> Therefore behold I will proceed to cause an admiration in this people, by a great and wonderful miracle: for wisdom shall perish from their wise men, and the understanding of their prudent men shall be hid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:12; Isaiah 47:10; Isaiah 30:1"> Woe to you that are deep of heart, to hide your counsel from the Lord: and their works are in the dark, and they say: Who seeth us, and who knoweth us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 9:19–21; Isaiah 64:8; Isaiah 45:9–11"> This thought of yours is perverse: as if the clay should think against the potter, and the work should say to the maker thereof: Thou madest me not: or the thing framed should say to him that fashioned it: Thou understandest not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 32:15; Matthew 19:30; Hebrews 10:37"> Is it not yet a very little while, and Libanus shall be turned into charmel, and charmel shall be esteemed as a forest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5; Matthew 11:5; Mark 7:37"> And in that day the deaf shall hear the words of the book, and out of darkness and obscurity the eyes of the blind shall see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="James 2:5; Isaiah 61:1; Matthew 11:29"> And the meek shall increase their joy in the Lord, and the poor men shall rejoice in the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Micah 2:1; Isaiah 28:14–22; Luke 16:14"> For he that did prevail hath failed, the scorner is consumed, and they are all cut off that watched for iniquity:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Amos 5:10–12; James 5:6; Acts 3:14"> That made men sin by word, and supplanted him that reproved them in the gate, and declined in vain from the just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 45:17; Isaiah 51:2; Jeremiah 30:10"> Therefore thus saith the Lord to the house of Jacob, he that redeemed Abraham: Jacob shall not now be confounded, neither shall his countenance now be ashamed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; Isaiah 19:25; Isaiah 45:11"> But when he shall see his children, the work of my hands in the midst of him sanctifying my name, and they shall sanctify the Holy One of Jacob, and shall glorify the God of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 15:17–19; Hebrews 5:2; Isaiah 28:7"> And they that erred in spirit, shall know understanding, and they that murmured, shall learn the law.</span>
