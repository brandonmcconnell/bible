<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 1:18; Habakkuk 2:20; Zechariah 2:13"> Let the islands keep silence before me, and the nations take new strength: let them come near, and then speak, let us come near to judgment together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 45:1; 2 Samuel 22:43; Ezra 1:2"> Who hath raised up the just one from the east, hath called him to follow him? he shall give the nations in his sight, and he shall rule over kings: he shall give them as the dust to his sword, as stubble driven by the wind, to his bow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 5:24; Isaiah 57:2"> He shall pursue them, he shall pass in peace, no path shall appear after his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 48:12; Revelation 22:13; Revelation 1:17"> Who hath wrought and done these things, calling the generations from the beginning? I the Lord, I am the first and the last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:15–16; Psalms 65:8; Psalms 66:3"> The islands saw it, and feared, the ends of the earth were astonished, they drew near, and came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 44:12; Isaiah 40:19; Daniel 3:1–7"> Every one shall help his neighbour, and shall say to his brother: Be of good courage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 40:19–20; Daniel 3:1–7; Judges 18:17–18"> The coppersmith striking with the hammer encouraged him that forged at that time, saying: It is ready for soldering: and he strengthened it with nails, that it should not be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 2:23; 2 Chronicles 20:7; Deuteronomy 14:2"> But thou Israel, art my servant, Jacob whom I have chosen, the seed of Abraham my friend:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:22; Psalms 94:14; Deuteronomy 7:6–7"> In whom I have taken thee from the ends of the earth, and from the remote parts thereof have called thee, and said to thee: Thou art my servant, I have chosen thee, and have not cast thee away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Isaiah 43:1–2; Romans 8:31"> Fear not, for I am with thee: turn not aside, for I am thy God: I have strengthened thee, and have helped thee, and the right hand of my just one hath upheld thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 23:22; Isaiah 45:24; Revelation 3:9"> Behold all that fight against thee shall be confounded and ashamed, they shall be as nothing, and the men shall perish that strive against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 37:35–36; Job 20:7–9; Isaiah 17:14"> Thou shalt seek them, and shalt not find the men that resist thee: they shall be as nothing: and as a thing consumed the men that war against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Psalms 73:23; Psalms 63:8"> For I am the Lord thy God, who take thee by the hand, and say to thee: Fear not, I have helped thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 43:14; Psalms 22:6; Isaiah 54:5"> Fear not, thou worm of Jacob, you that are dead of Israel: I have helped thee, saith the Lord: and thy Redeemer the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Micah 4:13; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Zechariah 4:7"> I have made thee as a new thrashing wain, with teeth like a saw: thou shall thrash the mountains, and break them in pieces: and shalt make the hills as chaff.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:2; Isaiah 61:10–11; Isaiah 45:24–25"> Thou shalt fan them, and the wind shall carry them away, and the whirlwind shall scatter them: and thou shalt rejoice in the Lord, in the Holy One of Israel thou shalt be joyful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 55:1; Isaiah 30:19; Isaiah 44:3"> The needy and the poor seek for waters, and there are none: their tongue hath been dry with thirst. I the Lord will hear them, I the God of Israel will not forsake them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 107:35; Isaiah 35:6–7; Isaiah 58:11"> I will open rivers in the high bills, and fountains in the midst of the plains: I will turn the desert into pools of waters, and the impassable land into streams of waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 55:13; Isaiah 37:31–32; Isaiah 35:1"> I will plant in the wilderness the cedar, and the thorn, and the myrtle, and the olive tree: I will set in the desert the fir tree, the elm, and the box tree together:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 12:9; Isaiah 44:23; Isaiah 43:21"> That they may see and know, and consider, and understand together that the hand of the Lord hath done this, and the Holy One of Israel hath created it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 23:3–4; Job 38:3; Micah 6:1–2"> Bring your cause near, saith the Lord: bring hither, if you have any thing to allege, saith the King of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 45:21; Isaiah 48:14; Isaiah 44:7"> Let them come, and tell us all things that are to come: tell us the former things what they were: and we will set our heart upon them, and shall know the latter end of them, and tell us the things that are to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 13:19; Isaiah 42:9; Jeremiah 10:5"> Shew the things that are to come hereafter, and we shall know that ye are gods. Do ye also good or evil, if you can: and let us speak, and see together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 115:8; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Isaiah 41:29"> Behold, you are of nothing, and your work of that which hath no being: he that hath chosen you is an abomination.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 41:2; 2 Samuel 22:43; Isaiah 10:6"> I have raised up one from the north, and he shall come from the rising of the sun: he shall call upon my name, and he shall make princes to be as dirt, and as the potter treading clay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 44:7; Isaiah 41:22; Isaiah 45:21"> Who bath declared from the beginning, that we may know: and from time of old, that we may say: Thou art just. There is none that sheweth, nor that foretelleth, nor that heareth your words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 40:9; Isaiah 52:7; Isaiah 44:28"> The first shall say to Sion: Behold they are here, and to Jerusalem I will give an evangelist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 63:5; Daniel 2:10–11; Isaiah 40:13–14"> And I saw, and there was no one even among them to consult, or who, when I asked, could answer a word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:13; Isaiah 41:24; Jeremiah 10:2–16"> Behold they are all in the wrong, and their works are vain: their idols are wind and vanity.</span>
