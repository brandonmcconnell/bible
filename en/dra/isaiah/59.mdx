<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:17; Isaiah 50:2; Numbers 11:23"> Behold the hand of the Lord is not shortened that it cannot save, neither is his ear heavy that it cannot hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:25; Proverbs 15:29; Micah 3:4"> But your iniquities have divided between you and your God, and your sins have hid his face from you that he should not hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 1:15; Ezekiel 13:8; Jeremiah 2:34"> For your hands are defiled with blood, and your fingers with iniquity: your lips have spoken lies, and your tongue uttereth iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 15:35; James 1:15; Micah 7:2–5"> There is none that calleth upon justice, neither is there any one that judgeth truly: but they trust in a mere nothing, and speak vanities: they have conceived labour, and brought forth iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 3:7; Isaiah 14:29; Proverbs 23:32"> They have broken the eggs of asps, and have woven the webs of spiders: he that shall eat of their eggs, shall die: and that which is brought out, shall be hatched into a basilisk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 57:12; Revelation 3:17–18; Jeremiah 6:7"> Their webs shall not be for clothing, neither shall they cover themselves with their works: their works are unprofitable works, and the work of iniquity is in their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 3:15–17; Proverbs 6:17; Mark 7:21–22"> Their feet run to evil, and make haste to shed innocent blood: their thoughts are unprofitable thoughts: wasting and destruction are in their ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 57:20–21; Romans 3:17; Luke 1:79"> They have not known the way of peace, and there is no judgment in their steps: their paths are become crooked to them, every one that treadeth in them, knoweth no peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 5:30; Job 30:26; Lamentations 5:16–17"> Therefore is judgment far from us, and justice shall not overtake us. We looked for light, and behold darkness: brightness, and we have walked in the dark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:29; Job 5:14; Lamentations 3:6"> We have groped for the wall, and like the blind we have groped as if we had no eyes: we have stumbled at noonday as in darkness, we are in dark places as dead men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 38:14; Ezekiel 7:16; Jeremiah 9:1"> We shall roar all of us like bears, and shall lament as mournful doves. We have looked for judgment, and there is none: for salvation, and it is far from us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezra 9:6; Hosea 5:5; Jeremiah 14:7"> For our iniquities are multiplied before thee, and our sins have testified against us: for our wicked doings are with us, and we have known our iniquities:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 7:21–22; James 3:6; Hebrews 3:12"> In sinning and lying against the Lord: and we have turned away so that we went not after our God, but spoke calumny and transgression: we have conceived, and uttered from the heart, words of falsehood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:4; Ecclesiastes 3:16; Jeremiah 5:31"> And judgment is turned away backward, and justice hath stood far off: because truth bath fallen down in the street, and equity could not come in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Micah 7:2; Jeremiah 7:28; 1 John 3:11–12"> And truth hath been forgotten: and he that departed from evil, lay open to be a prey: and the Lord saw, and it appeared evil in his eyes, because there is no judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:30; Psalms 106:23; Isaiah 64:7"> And he saw that there is not a man: and he stood astonished, because there is none to oppose himself: and his own arm brought salvation to him, and his own justice supported him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 6:17; Ephesians 6:14; 1 Thessalonians 5:8"> He put on justice as a breastplate, and a helmet of salvation upon his head: he put on the garments of vengeance, and was clad with zeal as with a cloak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:10; Isaiah 63:6; Romans 2:6"> As unto revenge, as it were to repay wrath to his adversaries, and a reward to his enemies: he will repay the like to the islands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 7:27; Psalms 113:3; Zechariah 4:6"> And they from the west, shall fear the name of the Lord: and they from the rising of the sun, his glory: when he shall come as a violent stream, which the spirit of the Lord driveth on:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 11:26–27; Titus 2:11–14; Acts 3:19"> And there shall come a, redeemer to Sion, and to them that return from iniquity in Jacob, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Hebrews 10:16; Ezekiel 36:25–27"> This is my covenant with them, saith the Lord: My spirit that is in thee, and my words that I have put in thy mouth, shall not depart out of thy mouth, nor out of the mouth of thy seed, nor out of the mouth of thy seed’s seed, saith the Lord, from henceforth and for ever.</span>
