<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 21:27; Isaiah 51:17; Zechariah 3:4"> Arise, arise, put on thy strength, O Sion, put on the garments of thy glory, O Jerusalem, the city of the Holy One: for henceforth the uncircumcised, and unclean shall no more pass through thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 51:14; Jeremiah 51:50; Luke 4:18"> Shake thyself from the dust, arise, sit up, O Jerusalem: loose the bonds from off thy neck, O captive daughter of Sion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 1:18; Isaiah 45:13; Psalms 44:12"> For thus saith the Lord: You were sold gratis, and you shall be redeemed without money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 46:6; Psalms 69:4; Acts 7:14–15"> For thus saith the Lord God: My people went down into Egypt at the beginning to sojourn there: and the Assyrian hath oppressed them without any cause at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 2:24; Psalms 44:16; Psalms 74:10"> And now what have I here, saith the Lord: for my people is taken away gratis. They that rule over them treat them unjustly, saith the Lord, and my name is continually blasphemed all the day long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 8:10–11; Ezekiel 37:13–14; Ezekiel 20:44"> Therefore my people shall know my name in that day: for I myself that spoke, behold I am here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 10:12–15; Nahum 1:15; Isaiah 61:1–3"> How beautiful upon the mountains are the feet of him that bringeth good tidings, and that preacheth peace: of him that sheweth forth good, that preacheth salvation, that saith to Sion: Thy God shall reign!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 62:6; Ezekiel 33:7; Jeremiah 32:39"> The voice of thy watchmen: they have lifted up their voice, they shall praise together: for they shall see eye to eye when the Lord shall convert Sion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 51:3; Psalms 98:4; Isaiah 48:20"> Rejoice, and give praise together, O ye deserts of Jerusalem: for the Lord hath comforted his people: he hath redeemed Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 3:6; Isaiah 51:9; Psalms 98:1–3"> The Lord hath prepared his holy arm in the sight of all the Gentiles: and all the ends of the earth shall see the salvation of our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:17; Zechariah 2:6–7; Isaiah 48:20"> Depart, depart, go ye out from thence, touch no unclean thing: go out of the midst of her, be ye clean, you that carry the vessels of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 58:8; Deuteronomy 20:4; Exodus 14:19–20"> For you shall not go out in a tumult, neither shall you make haste by flight: For the Lord will go before you, and the God of Israel will gather you together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 2:7–11; Matthew 28:18; John 3:31"> Behold my servant shall understand, he shall be exalted, and extolled, and shall be exceeding high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 50:6; Isaiah 53:2–5; Matthew 26:67"> As many have been astonished at thee, so shall his visage be inglorious among men, and his form among the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 15:20–21; Isaiah 49:7; Isaiah 55:5"> He shall sprinkle many nations, kings shall shut their mouth at him: for they to whom it was not told of him, have seen: and they that heard not, have beheld.</span>
