<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 7:5; Isaiah 28:7; 2 Kings 14:25–27"> Woe to the crown of pride, to the drunkards of Ephraim, and to the fading flower the glory of his joy, who were on the head of the fat valley, staggering with wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 30:30; Nahum 1:8; Ezekiel 13:11"> Behold the Lord is mighty and strong, as a storm of hail: a destroying whirlwind, as the violence of many waters overflowing, and sent forth upon a spacious land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 26:6; Hebrews 10:29; Revelation 11:2"> The crown of pride of the drunkards of Ephraim shall be trodden under feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nahum 3:12; Micah 7:1; Isaiah 28:1"> And the fading flower the glory of his joy, who is on the head of the fat valley, shall be as a hasty fruit before the ripeness of autumn: which when he that seeth it shall behold, as soon as he taketh it in his hand, he will eat it up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 5:4; Isaiah 62:3; Isaiah 60:19"> In that day the Lord of hosts shall be a crown of glory, and a garland of joy to the residue of his people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 18:32–34; John 5:30; Joshua 1:9"> And a spirit of judgment to him that sitteth in judgment, and strength to them that return out of the battle to the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 56:10–12; Luke 21:34; Isaiah 19:14"> But these also have been ignorant through wine, and through drunkenness have erred: the priest and the prophet have been ignorant through drunkenness, they are swallowed up with wine, they have gone astray in drunkenness, they have not known him that seeth, they have been ignorant of judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:26; Proverbs 26:11; Habakkuk 2:15–16"> For all tables were full of vomit and filth, so that there was no more place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 131:2; Jeremiah 6:10; John 12:38"> Whom shall he teach knowledge? and whom shall he make to understand the hearing? them that are weaned from the milk, that are drawn away from the breasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 28:13; 2 Timothy 3:7; Jeremiah 11:7"> For command, command again; command, command again; expect, expect again; expect, expect again: a little there, a little there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:21; Deuteronomy 28:49; Isaiah 33:19"> For with the speech of lips, and with another tongue he will speak to this people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 11:28–29; Isaiah 30:15; Jeremiah 6:16"> To whom he said: This is my rest, refresh the weary, and this is my refreshing: and they would not hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 13:14; Isaiah 6:9–10; 2 Peter 3:16"> And the word of the Lord shall be to them: Command, command again; command, command again: expect, expect again; expect, expect again: a little there, a little there: that they may go, and fall backward, and be broken, and snared, and taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 28:22; Isaiah 29:20; Isaiah 1:10"> Wherefore hear the word of the Lord, ye scornful men, who rule over my people that is in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–8; Ezekiel 13:22; Amos 2:4"> For you have said: We have entered into a league with death, and we have made a covenant with hell. When the overflowing scourge shall pass through, it shall not come upon us: for we have placed our hope in lies, and by falsehood we are protected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 118:22; Acts 4:11–12; 1 Peter 2:6–8"> Therefore thus saith the Lord God: Behold I will lay a stone in the foundations of Sion, a tried stone, a corner stone, a precious stone, founded in the foundation. He that believeth, let him not hasten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 21:13; Amos 7:7–9; Revelation 16:21"> And I will set judgment in weight, and justice in measure: and hail shall overturn the hope of falsehood: and waters shall overflow its protection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 7:7; Isaiah 8:10; Isaiah 28:15"> And Sour league with death shall be abolished, and your covenant with hell shall not stand: when the overflowing scourge shall pass, you shall be trodden down by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:16; Isaiah 36:22; 2 Kings 24:2"> Whensoever it shall pass through, it shall take you away: because in the morning early it shall pass through, in the day and in the night, and vexation alone shall make you understand what you hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 64:6; 1 Corinthians 1:18–31; Romans 9:30–32"> For the bed is straitened, so that one must fall out, and a short covering cannot cover both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 14:11; Joshua 10:12; 2 Samuel 5:20"> For the Lord shall stand up as in the mountain of divisions: he shall be angry as in the valley which is in Gabaon: that he may do his work, his strange work: that he may perform his work, his work is strange to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 10:22–23; Isaiah 24:1–23; Matthew 27:44"> And now do not mock, lest your bonds be tied strait. For I have heard of the Lord the God of hosts a consumption and a cutting short upon all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 1:2; Jeremiah 22:29; Revelation 2:11"> Give ear, and hear my voice, hearken, and hear my speech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:3; Hosea 10:11–12"> Shall the ploughman plough all the day to sow, shall he open and harrow his ground?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:9; Matthew 23:23; Exodus 9:31–32"> Will he not, when he hath made plain the surface thereof, sow gith, and scatter cummin, and put wheat in order, and barley, and millet, and vetches in their bounds?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 31:3–6; Exodus 36:2; Job 39:17"> For he will instruct him in judgment: his God will teach him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 41:15; Isaiah 27:7–8; Jeremiah 46:28"> For gith shall not be thrashed with saws, neither shall the cart wheel turn about upon cummin: but gith shall be beaten out with a rod, and cummin with a staff.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:9–10; Matthew 3:12; John 12:24"> But bread corn shall be broken small: but the thrasher shall not thrash it for ever, neither shall the cart wheel hurt it, nor break it with its teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Jeremiah 32:19; Job 5:9"> This also is come forth from the Lord God of hosts, to make his counsel wonderful, and magnify justice.</span>
