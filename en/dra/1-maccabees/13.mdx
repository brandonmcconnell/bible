<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Now Simon heard that Tryphon was gathering together a very great army, to invade the land of Juda, and to destroy it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And seeing that the people was in dread, and in fear, he went up to Jerusalem, and assembled the people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And exhorted them, saying: You know what great battles I and my brethren, and the house of my father, have fought for the laws, and the sanctuary, and the distresses that we have seen:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> By reason whereof all my brethren have lost their lives for Israel’s sake, and I am left alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And now far be it from me to spare my life in any time of trouble: for I am not better than my brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> I will avenge then my nation and the sanctuary, and our children, and wives: for all the heathens are gathered together to destroy us out of mere malice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And the spirit of the people was enkindled as soon as they heard these words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And they answered with a loud voice, saying: Thou art our leader in the place of Judas, and Jonathan thy brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Fight thou our battles, and we will do whatsoever thou shalt say to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> So gathering together all the men of war, he made haste to finish all the walls of Jerusalem, and he fortified it round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And he sent Jonathan the son of Absalom, and with him a new army into Joppe, and he cast out them that were in it, and himself remained there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And Tryphon removed from Ptolemais with a great army, to invade the land of Juda, and Jonathan was with him in custody.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> But Simon pitched in Addus, over against the plain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And when Tryphon understood that Simon was risen up in the place of his brother Jonathan, and that he meant to join battle with him, he sent messengers to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Saying: We have detained thy brother Jonathan for the money that he owed in the king’s account, by reason of the affairs which he had the management of.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> But now send a hundred talents of silver, and his two sons for hostages, that when he is set at liberty he may not revolt from us, and we will release him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Now Simon knew that he spoke deceitfully to him, nevertheless he ordered the money, and the children to be sent: lest he should bring upon himself a great hatred of the people of Israel, who might have said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Because he sent not the money, and the children, therefore is he lost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> So he sent the children, and the hundred talents: and he lied, and did not let Jonathan go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And after this Tryphon entered within the country, to destroy it: and they went about by the way that leadeth to Ador: and Simon and his army marched to every place whithersoever they went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And they that were in the castle, sent messengers to Tryphon, that he should make haste to come through the desert, and sent them victuals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And Tryphon made ready all his horsemen to come that night: but there fell a very great snow, and he came not into the country of Galaad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And when he approached to Bascama, he slew Jonathan and his sons there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And Tryphon returned, and went into his own country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And Simon sent, and took the bones of Jonathan his brother, and buried them in Modin, in the city of his fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And all Israel bewailed him with great lamentation: and they mourned for him many days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And Simon built over the sepulchre of his father and of his brethren, a building lofty to the sight, of polished stone behind and before:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And he set up seven pyramids one against another for his father and his mother, and his four brethren:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And round about these he set great pillars: and upon the pillars arms for a perpetual memory: and by the arms ships carved, which might be seen by all that sailed on the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> This is the sepulchre that he made in Modin even unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> But Tryphon when he was upon a journey with the young king Antiochus, treacherously slew him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And he reigned in his place, and put on the crown of Asia: and brought great evils upon the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And Simon built up the strong holds of Judea, fortifying them with high towers, and great walls, and gates, and bars: and he stored up victuals in the fortresses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And Simon chose men and sent to king Demetrius, to the end that he should grant an immunity to the land: for all that Tryphon did was to spoil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And king Demetrius in answer to this request, wrote a letter in this manner:</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> King Demetrius to Simon the high priest, and friend of kings, and to the ancients, and to the nation of the Jews, greeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> The golden crown, and the palm, which you sent, we have received: and we are ready to make a firm peace with you, and to write to the king’s chief officers to release you the things that we have released.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> For all that we have decreed in your favour, shall stand in force. The strong holds that you have built, shall be your own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> And as for any oversight or fault committed unto this day, we forgive it, and the crown which you owed: and if any other thing were taxed in Jerusalem, now let it not be taxed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And if any of you be fit to be enrolled among ours, let them be enrolled, and let there be peace between us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> In the year one hundred and seventy the yoke of the Gentiles was taken off from Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And the people of Israel began to write in the instruments, and public records, The first year under Simon the high priest, the great captain and prince of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> In those days Simon besieged Gaza, and camped round about it, and he made engines, and set them to the city, and he struck one tower, and took it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And they that were within the engine leaped into the city: and there was a great uproar in the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> And they that were in the city went up with their wives and children upon the wall, with their garments rent, and they cried with a loud voice, beseeching Simon to grant them peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> And they said: Deal not with us according to our evil deeds, but according to thy mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> And Simon being moved, did not destroy them: but yet he cast them out of the city, and cleansed the houses wherein there had been idols, and then he entered into it with hymns, blessing the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> And having cast out of it all uncleanness, he placed in it men that should observe the law: and he fortified it, and made it his habitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> But they that were in the castle of Jerusalem were hindered from going out and coming into the country, and from buying and selling: and they were straitened with hunger, and many of them perished through famine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> And they cried to Simon form peace, and he granted it to them: and he cast them out from thence, and cleansed the castle from uncleannesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> And they entered into it the three and twentieth day of the second month, in the year one hundred and seventy-one, with thanksgiving, and branches of palm trees, and harps, and cymbals, and psalteries, and hymns, and canticles, because the great enemy was destroyed out of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> And he ordained that these days should be kept every year with gladness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53"> And he fortified the mountain of the temple that was near the castle, and he dwelt there himself, and they that were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54"> And Simon saw that John his son was a valiant man for war: and he made him captain of all the forces: and he dwelt in Gazara.</span>
