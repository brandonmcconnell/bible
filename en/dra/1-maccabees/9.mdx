<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> In the mean time when Demetrius heard that Nicanor and his army were fallen in battle, he sent again Bacchides and Alcimus into Judea; and the right wing of his army with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And they took the road that leadeth to Galgal, and they camped in Masaloth, which is in Arabella: and they made themselves masters of it, and slew many people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> In the first month of the hundred and fifty-second year they brought the army to Jerusalem:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And they arose, and went to Berea with twenty thousand men, and two thousand horsemen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Now Judas had pitched his tents in Laisa, and three thousand chosen men with him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And they saw the multitude of the army that they were many, and they were seized with great fear: and many withdrew themselves out of the camp, and there remained of them no more than eight hundred men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And Judas saw that his army slipped away, and the battle pressed upon him, and his heart was cast down: because he had not time to gather them together, and he was discouraged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Then he said to them that remained: Let us arise, and go against our enemies, if we may be able to fight against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> But they dissuaded him, saying: We shall not be able, but let us save our lives now, and return to our brethren, and then we will fight against them: for we are but few.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then Judas said: God forbid we should do this thing, and flee away from them: but if our time be come, let us die manfully for our brethren, and let us not stain our glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the army removed out of the camp, and they stood over against them: and the horsemen were divided into two troops, and the slingers, and the archers went before the army, and they that were in the front were all men of valour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And Bacchides was in the right wing, and the legion drew near on two sides, and they sounded the trumpets:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And they also were on Judas’ side, even they also cried out, and the earth shook at the noise of the armies: and the battle was fought from morning even unto the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And Judas perceived that the stronger part of the army of Bacchides was on the right side, and all the stout of heart came together with him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the right wing was discomfited by them, and he pursued them even to the mount Azotus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And they that were in the left wing saw that the right wing was discomfited, and they followed after Judas, and them that were with him, at their back:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the battle was hard fought, and there fell many wounded of the one side and of the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And Judas was slain, and the rest fled away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And Jonathan and Simon took Judas their brother, and buried him in the sepulchre of their fathers in the city of Modin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And all the people of Israel bewailed him with great lamentation, and they mourned for him many days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And said: How is the mighty man fallen, that saved the people of Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> But the rest of the words of the wars of Judas, and of the noble acts that he did, and of his greatness, are not written: for they were very many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And it came to pass after the death of Judas, that the wicked began to put forth their heads in all the confines of Israel, and all the workers of iniquity rose up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> In those days there was a very great famine, and they and all their country yielded to Bacchides.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And Bacchides chose the wicked men, and made them lords of the country:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And they sought out, and made diligent search after the friends of Judas, and brought them to Bacchides, and he took vengeance of them, and abused them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And there was a great tribulation in Israel, such as was not since the day, that there was no prophet seen in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And all the friends of Judas came together, and said to Jonathan:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Since thy brother Judas died, there is not a man like him to go forth against our enemies, Bacchides, and them that are the enemies of our nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Now therefore we have chosen thee this day to be our prince, and captain in his stead to fight our battles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> So Jonathan took upon him the government at that time, and rose up in the place of Judas his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And Bacchides had knowledge of it, and sought to kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And Jonathan and Simon his brother, knew it, and all that were with them: and they fled into the desert of Thecua, and they pitched by the water of the lake of Asphar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And Bacchides understood it, and he came himself with all his army over the Jordan on the sabbath day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And Jonathan sent his brother a captain of the people, to desire the Nabutheans his friends, that they would lend them their equipage, which was copious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And the children of Jambri came forth out of Madaba, and took John, and all that he had, and went away with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> After this it was told Jonathan, and Simon his brother, that the children of Jambri made a great marriage, and were bringing the bride out of Madaba, the daughter of one of the great princes of Chanaan, with great pomp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And the remembered the blood of John their brother: and they went up, and hid themselves under the covert of the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> And they lifted up their eyes, and saw: and behold a tumult, and great preparation: and the bridegroom came forth, and his friends, and his brethren to meet them with timbrels, and musical instruments, and many weapons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And they rose up against them from the place where they lay in ambush, and slew them, and there fell many wounded, and the rest fled into the mountains, and they took all their spoils:</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> And the marriage was turned into mourning, and the noise of their musical instruments into lamentation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And they took revenge for the blood of their brother: and they returned to the bank of the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> And Bacchides heard it, and he came on the sabbath day even to the bank of the Jordan with a great power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And Jonathan said to his company: Let us arise, and fight against our enemies: for it is not now as yesterday, and the day before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> And behold the battle is before us, and the water of the Jordan on this side and on that side, and banks, and marshes, and woods: and there is no place for us to turn aside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> Now therefore cry ye to heaven, that ye may be delivered from the hand of your enemies. And they joined battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> And Jonathan stretched forth his hand to strike Bacchides, but he turned away from him backwards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> And Jonathan, and they that were with him leaped into the Jordan, and swam over the Jordan to them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> And there fell of Bacchides’ side that day a thousand man: and they returned to Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> And they built strong cities in Judea, the fortress that was in Jericho, and in Ammaus, and in Bethoron, and in Bethel, and Thamnata, and Phara, and Thopo, with high walls, and gates, and bars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> And he placed garrisons in them, that they might wage war against Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> And he fortified the city of Bethsura, and Gazara, and the castle, and set garrisons in them, and provisions of victuals:</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53"> And he took the sons of the chief men of the country for hostages, and put them in the castle in Jerusalem in custody.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54"> Now in the year one hundred and fifty-three, the second month, Alcimus commanded the walls of the inner court of the sanctuary to be thrown down, and the works of the prophets to be destroyed: and he began to be destroyed: and he began to destroy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55"> At that time Alcimus was struck: and his works were hindered, and his mouth was stopped, and he was taken with a palsy, so that he could no more speak a word, nor give order concerning his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> And Alcimus died at that time in great torment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> And Bacchides saw that Alcimus was dead: and he returned to the king, and the land was quiet for two years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58"> And all the wicked held a council, saying: Behold Jonathan, and they that are with him, dwell at ease, and without fear: now therefore let us bring Bacchides hither, and he shall take them all in one night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> So they went, and gave him counsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60"> And he arose to come with a great army: and he sent secretly letters to his adherents that were in Judea, to seize upon Jonathan, and them that were with him: but they could not, for their design was known to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61"> And he apprehended of the men of the country, that were the principal authors of the mischief, fifty men, and slew them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62"> And Jonathan, and Simon, and they that were with him retired into Bethbessen, which is in the desert: and he repaired the breaches thereof, and they fortified it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63"> And when Bacchides knew it, he gathered together all his multitude: and sent word to them that were of Judea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64"> And he came, and camped above Bethbessen, and fought against it many days, and made engines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65"> But Jonathan left his brother Simon in the city, and went forth into the country: and came with a number of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66"> And struck Odares, and his brethren, and the children of Phaseron in their tents, and he began to slay, and to increase in forces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67"> But Simon and they that were with him, sallied out of the city, and burnt the engines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68"> And they fought against Bacchides, and he was discomfited by them: and they afflicted him exceedingly, for his counsel, and his enterprise was in vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69"> And he was angry with the wicked men that had given him counsel to come into their country, and he slew many of them: and he purposed to return with the rest into their country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70"> And Jonathan had knowledge of it, and he sent ambassadors to him to make peace with him, and to restore to him the prisoners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71"> And he accepted it willingly, and did according to his words, and swore that the would do him no harm all the days of his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72"> And he restored to him the prisoners which he before had taken out of the land of Juda: and he returned and went away into his own country, and he came no more into their borders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v73" data-v="73"> So the sword ceased from Israel: and Jonathan dwelt in Machmas, and Jonathan began there to judge the people, and he destroyed the wicked out of Israel.</span>
