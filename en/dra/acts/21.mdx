<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:17; Luke 5:4; 1 Samuel 20:41–42"> And when it came to pass that, being parted from them, we set sail, we came with a straight course to Coos, and the day following to Rhodes, and from thence to Patara.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jonah 1:3; Acts 11:19; Acts 15:3"> And when we had found a ship sailing over to Phenice, we went aboard, and set forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 21:16; Acts 4:36; Matthew 4:24"> And when we had discovered Cyprus, leaving it on the left hand, we sailed into Syria, and came to Tyre: for there the ship was to unlade her burden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 20:22–23; Acts 21:10–12; Acts 20:6–7"> And finding disciples, we tarried there seven days: who said to Paul through the Spirit, that he should not go up to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 20:36; Acts 9:40; Psalms 95:6"> And the days being expired, departing we went forward, they all bringing us on our way, with their wives and children, till we were out of the city: and we kneeled down on the shore, and we prayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 19:27; John 1:11; 2 Corinthians 2:13"> And when we had bid one another farewell, we took ship; and they returned home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 13:24; 1 Samuel 10:4; Acts 28:12"> But we having finished the voyage by sea, from Tyre came down to Ptolemais: and saluting the brethren, we abode one day with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 6:5; Acts 8:26–40; Ephesians 4:11"> And the next day departing, we came to Caesarea. And entering into the house of Philip the evangelist, who was one of the seven, we abode with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 2:17; Revelation 2:20; Joel 2:28"> And he had four daughters, virgins, who did prophesy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 11:28; Acts 21:4; Acts 20:16"> And as we tarried there for some days, there came from Judea a certain prophet, named Agabus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 21:33; Jeremiah 13:1–11; 1 Samuel 15:27–28"> Who, when he was come to us, took Paul’s girdle: and binding his own feet and hands, he said: Thus saith the Holy Ghost: The man whose girdle this is, the Jews shall bind in this manner in Jerusalem, and shall deliver him into the hands of the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 16:21–23; Acts 20:22; Acts 21:4"> Which when we had heard, both we and they that were of that place, desired him that he would not go up to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 20:24; Philippians 2:17; Philippians 1:20–21"> Then Paul answered, and said: What do you mean weeping and afflicting my heart? For I am ready not only to be bound, but to die also in Jerusalem, for the name of the Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 22:42; Matthew 26:42; Matthew 26:39"> And when we could not persuade him, we ceased, saying: The will of the Lord be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 25:1; Acts 25:6; Acts 25:9"> And after those days, being prepared, we went up to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 16:31; Acts 21:8; Psalms 92:14"> And there went also with us some of the disciples from Caesarea, bringing with them one Mnason a Cyprian, an old disciple, with whom we should lodge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 15:4; Romans 15:7; Hebrews 13:1–2"> And when we were come to Jerusalem, the brethren received us gladly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 1:19; Galatians 2:9; Acts 15:13"> And the day following, Paul went in with us unto James; and all the ancients were assembled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 14:27; Acts 1:17; Romans 15:18–19"> Whom when he had saluted, he related particularly what things God had wrought among the Gentiles by his ministry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 10:2; Galatians 1:14; Acts 22:3"> But they hearing it, glorified God, and said to him: Thou seest, brother, how many thousands there are among the Jews that have believed: and they are all zealous for the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Galatians 6:12–15; 1 Corinthians 9:19–21; Galatians 5:1–6"> Now they have heard of thee that thou teachest those Jews, who are among the Gentiles, to depart from Moses: saying, that they ought not to circumcise their children, nor walk according to the custom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 15:12; Acts 15:22; Acts 19:32"> What is it therefore? the multitude must needs come together: for they will hear that thou art come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 18:18; Numbers 6:2–7"> Do therefore this that we say to thee. We have four men, who have a vow on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 21:26; Acts 24:18; Acts 18:18"> Take these, and sanctify thyself with them: and bestow on them, that they may shave their heads: and all will know that the things which they have heard of thee, are false; but that thou thyself also walkest keeping the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 15:29; Acts 15:19–20"> But as touching the Gentiles that believe, we have written, decreeing that they should only refrain themselves from that which has been offered to idols, and from blood, and from things strangles, and from fornication.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 24:18; Acts 21:24; Numbers 6:13–20"> Then Paul took the men, and the next day being purified with them, entered into the temple, giving notice of the accomplishment of the days of purification, until an oblation should be offered for every one of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 26:21; Acts 24:18; Acts 13:50"> But when the seven days were drawing to an end, those Jews that were of Asia, when they saw him in the temple, stirred up all the people, and laid hands upon him, crying out:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 24:5–6; Acts 21:21; Acts 26:20–21"> Men of Israel, help: This is the man that teacheth all men every where against the people, and the law, and this place; and moreover hath brought in Gentiles into the temple, and hath violated this holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 20:4; 2 Timothy 4:20; Acts 18:19"> (For they had seen Trophimus the Ephesian in the city with him, whom they supposed that Paul had brought into the temple.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 26:21; 2 Corinthians 11:26; Acts 16:19–22"> And the whole city was in an uproar: and the people ran together. And taking Paul, they drew him out of the temple, and immediately the doors were shut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:23–33; Acts 17:5; Acts 26:9–10"> And as they went about to kill him, it was told the tribune of the band, That all Jerusalem was in confusion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 23:27; Isaiah 3:15; Acts 5:40"> Who, forthwith taking with him soldiers and centurions, ran down to them. And when they saw the tribune and the soldiers they left off beating Paul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ephesians 6:20; Acts 21:11; Acts 20:23"> Then the tribune coming near, took him, and commanded him to be bound with two chains: and demanded who he was, and what he had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 19:32; Acts 23:10; Acts 22:24"> And some cried one thing, some another, among the multitude. And when he could not know the certainty for the tumult, he commanded him to be carried into the castle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:2–3; Psalms 55:9; Psalms 58:2"> And when he was come to the stairs, it fell out that he was carried by the soldiers, because of the violence of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 19:15; Acts 22:22; Luke 23:18"> For the multitude of the people followed after, crying: Away with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 21:34; Luke 21:15; Acts 19:30"> And as Paul was about to be brought into the castle, he saith to the tribune: May speak something to thee? Who said: Canst thou speak Greek?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 24:26; Acts 5:36–37; Matthew 5:11"> Art not thou that Egyptian who before these days didst raise a tumult, and didst lead forth into the desert four thousand men that were murderers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 22:3; Acts 9:11; Acts 23:27"> But Paul said to him: I am a Jew of Tarsus in Cilicia, a citizen of no mean city. And I beseech thee, suffer me to speak to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Acts 12:17; Acts 22:2; John 5:2"> And when he had given him leave, Paul standing on the stairs, beckoned with his hand to the people. And a great silence being made, he spoke unto them in the Hebrew tongue, saying:</span>
