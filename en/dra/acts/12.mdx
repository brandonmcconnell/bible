<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 24:9; John 15:20; Matthew 10:17–18"> And at the same time, Herod the king stretched forth his hands, to afflict some of the church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 11:37; Matthew 20:23; Matthew 4:21–22"> And he killed James, the brother of John, with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 23:15; John 12:43; Acts 24:27"> And seeing that it pleased the Jews, he proceeded to take up Peter also. Now it was in the days of the Azymes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Luke 22:33; Luke 21:12"> And when he had apprehended him, he cast him into prison, delivering him to four files of soldiers to be kept, intending, after the pasch, to bring him forth to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:26; 1 Thessalonians 5:17; James 5:16"> Peter therefore was kept in prison. But prayer was made without ceasing by the church unto God for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 21:33; Hebrews 13:6; Philippians 4:6–7"> And when Herod would have brought him forth, the same night Peter was sleeping between two soldiers, bound with two chains: and the keepers before the door kept the prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 5:19; Acts 16:26; Psalms 146:7"> And behold an angel of the Lord stood by him: and a light shined in the room: and he striking Peter on the side, raised him up, saying: Arise quickly. And the chains fell off from his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the angel said to him: Gird thyself, and put on thy sandals. And he did so. And he said to him: Cast thy garment about thee, and follow me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 126:1; Hebrews 11:8; Genesis 45:26"> And going out, he followed him, and he knew not that it was true which was done by the angel: but thought he saw a vision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 5:19; Acts 16:26; Isaiah 45:1–2"> And passing through the first and the second ward, they came to the iron gate that leadeth to the city, which of itself opened to them. And going out, they passed on through one street: and immediately the angel departed from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 6:22; Daniel 3:28; 2 Peter 2:9"> And Peter coming to himself, said: Now I know in very deed, that the Lord hath sent his angel, and hath delivered me out of the hand of Herod, and from all the expectation of the people of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 5:14–15; Acts 12:5; Matthew 18:19–20"> And considering, he came to the house of Mary the mother of John, who was surnamed Mark, where many were gathered together and praying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 12:16; Luke 13:25; John 18:16–17"> And when he knocked at the door of the gate, a damsel came to hearken, whose name was Rhode.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 24:41; Matthew 28:8"> And as soon as she knew Peter’s voice, she opened not the gate for joy, but running in she told that Peter stood before the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:10; Luke 24:11; Mark 16:14"> But they said to her: Thou art mad. But she affirmed that it was so. Then said they: It is his angel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> But Peter continued knocking. And when they had opened, they saw him, and were astonished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 21:40; Acts 13:16; Acts 19:33"> But he beckoning to them with his hand to hold their peace, told how the Lord had brought him out of prison, and he said: Tell these things to James, and to the brethren. And going out, he went into another place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 5:22–25; Acts 16:27; Acts 19:23"> Now when day was come, there was no small stir among the soldiers, what was become of Peter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 16:27; Psalms 37:32–33; John 12:10–11"> And when Herod had sought for him, and found him not; having examined the keepers, he commanded they should be put to death; and going down from Judea to Caesarea, he abode there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezra 3:7; Matthew 11:21–22; Ezekiel 27:17"> And he was angry with the Tyrians and the Sidonians. But they with one accord came to him, and having gained Blastus, who was the king’s chamberlain, they desired peace, because their countries were nourished by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And upon a day appointed, Herod being arrayed in kingly apparel, sat in the judgment seat, and made an oration to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 14:10–13; Revelation 13:4; Jude 1:16"> And the people made acclamation, saying: It is the voice of a god, and not of a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 115:1; Acts 10:25–26; 2 Thessalonians 2:4"> And forthwith an angel of the Lord struck him, because he had not given the honour to God: and being eaten up by worms, he gave up the ghost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 6:7; Acts 19:20; 2 Thessalonians 3:1"> But the word of the Lord increased and multiplied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 12:12; 1 Peter 5:13; Acts 15:37"> And Barnabas and Saul returned from Jerusalem, having fulfilled their ministry, taking with them John, who was surnamed Mark.</span>
