<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 5:6; Acts 15:5; Leviticus 12:3"> And some coming down from Judea, taught the brethren: That except you be circumcised after the manner of Moses, you cannot be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 15:22–23; Acts 15:6–7; Galatians 2:1–2"> And when Paul and Barnabas had no small contest with them, they determined that Paul and Barnabas, and certain others of the other side, should go up to the apostles and priests to Jerusalem about this question.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 14:27; Acts 21:5; 1 Corinthians 16:11"> They therefore being brought on their way by the church, passed through Phenice, and Samaria, relating the conversion of the Gentiles; and they caused great joy to all the brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 14:27; Acts 15:12; Acts 15:3"> And when they were come to Jerusalem, they were received by the church, and by the apostles and ancients, declaring how great things God had done with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 5:1–3; Philippians 3:5–8; Acts 15:1"> But there arose some of the sect of the Pharisees that believed, saying: They must be circumcised, and be commanded to observe the law of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 15:22; Hebrews 13:7; Hebrews 13:17"> And the apostles and ancients assembled to consider of this matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 10:17–18; Galatians 2:7–9; 1 Chronicles 28:4–5"> And when there had been much disputing, Peter, rising up, said to them: Men, brethren, you know, that in former days God made choice among us, that by my mouth the Gentiles should hear the word of the gospel, and believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:24; Acts 10:47; Acts 14:3"> And God, who knoweth the hearts, gave testimony, giving unto them the Holy Ghost, as well as to us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 3:6; Galatians 5:6; Acts 10:28"> And put no difference between us and them, purifying their hearts by faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 5:1; Matthew 23:4; Galatians 4:9"> Now therefore, why tempt you God to put a yoke upon the necks of the disciples, which neither our fathers nor we have been able to bear?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 3:24; Titus 3:4–7; Titus 2:11"> But by the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, we believe to be saved, in like manner as they also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 14:27; Acts 15:4; Acts 21:19"> And all the multitude held their peace; and they heard Barnabas and Paul telling what great signs and wonders God had wrought among the Gentiles by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 12:17; Galatians 2:12; James 1:1"> And after they had held their peace, James answered, saying: Men, brethren, hear me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Peter 1:1; Acts 15:7–9; Luke 2:31–32"> Simon hath related how God first visited to take of the Gentiles a people to his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 15:8–12; Acts 13:47"> And to this agree the words of the prophets, as it is written:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Amos 9:11–12; Psalms 89:35–49; Luke 1:31–33"> After these things I will return, and will rebuild the tabernacle of David, which is fallen down; and the ruins thereof I will rebuild, and I will set it up:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zechariah 2:11; Zechariah 8:20–23; Isaiah 65:1"> That the residue of men may seek after the Lord, and all nations upon whom my name is invoked, saith the Lord, who doth these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 1:4; Ephesians 3:9; Isaiah 46:9–10"> To the Lord was his own work known from the beginning of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 15:28; Galatians 1:7–10; Acts 15:24"> For which cause I judge that they, who from among the Gentiles are converted to God, are not to be disquieted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 15:29; Revelation 2:20; Leviticus 3:17"> But that we write unto them, that they refrain themselves from the pollutions of idols, and from fornication, and from things strangled, and from blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 13:15; Acts 13:27; 2 Corinthians 3:14–15"> For Moses of old time hath in every city them that preach him in the synagogues, where he is read every sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 15:27; Acts 15:40; 1 Thessalonians 1:1"> Then it pleased the apostles and ancients, with the whole church, to choose men of their own company, and to send to Antioch, with Paul and Barnabas, namely, Judas, who was surnamed Barsabas, and Silas, chief men among the brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 23:26; James 1:1; Galatians 1:21"> Writing by their hands: The apostles and ancients, brethren, to the brethren of the Gentiles that are at Antioch, and in Syria and Cilicia, greeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Galatians 5:12; Galatians 5:10; Acts 15:1"> Forasmuch as we have heard, that some going out from us have troubled you with words, subverting your souls; to whom we gave no commandment:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Peter 3:15; Acts 1:14; Acts 15:2"> It hath seemed good to us, being assembled together, to choose out men, and to send them unto you, with our well beloved Barnabas and Paul:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 14:19; Acts 9:23–25; Philippians 2:29–30"> Men that have given their lives for the name of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 15:22; Acts 15:32; 3 John 1:13"> We have sent therefore Judas and Silas, who themselves also will, by word of mouth, tell you the same things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 16:13; Revelation 2:24; Matthew 11:30"> For it hath seemed good to the Holy Ghost and to us, to lay no further burden upon you than these necessary things:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 15:20; Acts 21:25; Revelation 2:14"> That you abstain from things sacrificed to idols, and from blood, and from things strangled, and from fornication; from which things keeping yourselves, you shall do well. Fare ye well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 16:4; Acts 6:2; Acts 23:33"> They therefore being dismissed, went down to Antioch; and gathering together the multitude, delivered the epistle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Galatians 2:4–5; Philippians 3:3; Galatians 5:1"> Which when they had read, they rejoiced for the consolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 11:23; Acts 14:22; Acts 13:1"> But Judas and Silas, being prophets also themselves, with many words comforted the brethren, and confirmed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:11; Acts 16:36; Hebrews 11:31"> And after they had spent some time there, they were let go with peace by the brethren, unto them that had sent them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> But it seemed good unto Silas to remain there; and Judas alone departed to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:2; Colossians 1:28; Acts 13:1"> And Paul and Barnabas continued at Antioch, teaching and preaching, with many others, the word of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 14:6; Acts 13:4; Acts 13:51"> And after some days, Paul said to Barnabas: Let us return and visit our brethren in all the cities wherein we have preached the word of the Lord, to see how they do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 12:12; Philemon 1:24; Acts 13:5"> And Barnabas would have taken with them John also, that was surnamed Mark;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 13:13; Proverbs 25:19; Luke 14:27–34"> But Paul desired that he (as having departed from them out of Pamphylia, and not gone with them to the work) might not be received.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Colossians 4:10; Ecclesiastes 7:20; Acts 4:36"> And there arose a dissension, so that they departed one from another; and Barnabas indeed taking Mark, sailed to Cyprus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Acts 15:22; Acts 14:26; Acts 11:23"> But Paul choosing Silas, departed, being delivered by the brethren to the grace of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Acts 15:23; Galatians 1:21; Acts 15:32"> And he went through Syria and Cilicia, confirming the churches, commanding them to keep the precepts of the apostles and the ancients.</span>
