<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 22:27; Malachi 1:11; Isaiah 32:15"> And the apostles and brethren, who were in Judea, heard that the Gentiles also had received the word of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 10:45; Acts 10:9; Acts 15:1"> And when Peter was come up to Jerusalem, they that were of the circumcision contended with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 10:28; Galatians 2:12; Luke 15:2"> Saying: Why didst thou go in to men uncircumcised, and didst eat with them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 1:3; Acts 14:27; Joshua 22:21–31"> But Peter began and declared to them the matter in order, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 10:9–32; Acts 9:10; Acts 22:17"> I was in the city of Joppe praying, and I saw in an ecstasy of mind a vision, a certain vessel descending, as it were a great sheet let down from heaven by four corners, and it came even unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 4:20; Acts 3:4"> Into which looking, I considered, and saw fourfooted creatures of the earth, and beasts, and creeping things, and fowls of the air:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And I heard also a voice saying to me: Arise, Peter; kill and eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 14:14; Hosea 9:3; 1 Corinthians 7:14"> And I said: Not so, Lord; for nothing common or unclean hath ever entered into my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 15:9; Hebrews 9:13–14; Acts 10:34–35"> And the voice answered again from heaven: What God hath made clean, do not thou call common.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 21:17; Numbers 24:10; 2 Corinthians 12:8"> And this was done three times: and all were taken up again into heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 4:27; Acts 9:10–12; Exodus 4:14"> And behold, immediately there were three men come to the house wherein I was, sent to me from Caesarea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 8:29; Romans 3:22; 2 Thessalonians 2:2"> And the Spirit said to me, that I should go with them, nothing doubting. And these six brethren went with me also: and we entered into the man’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 10:30–32; Acts 10:3–6; Acts 9:43"> And he told us how he had seen an angel in his house, standing, and saying to him: Send to Joppe, and call hither Simon, who is surnamed Peter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 16:15; Acts 10:22; Acts 16:31–34"> Who shall speak to thee words, whereby thou shalt be saved, and all thy house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 19:6; Acts 10:34–46; Acts 2:2–12"> And when I had begun to speak, the Holy Ghost fell upon them, as upon us also in the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 1:5; Mark 1:8; Proverbs 1:23"> And I remembered the word of the Lord, how that he said: John indeed baptized with water, but you shall be baptized with the Holy Ghost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 10:47; Romans 9:20–26; Daniel 4:35"> If then God gave them the same grace, as to us also who believed in the Lord Jesus Christ; who was I, that could withstand God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 10:12–13; 2 Corinthians 7:10; Ezekiel 36:26"> Having heard these things, they held their peace, and glorified God, saying: God then hath also to the Gentiles given repentance unto life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 4:36; Acts 21:2; Acts 15:3"> Now they who had been dispersed by the persecution that arose on occasion of Stephen, went about as far as Phenice and Cyprus and Antioch, speaking the word to none, but to the Jews only.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 27:32; Acts 13:1; 1 Corinthians 1:23–24"> But some of them were men of Cyprus and Cyrene, who, when they were entered into Antioch, spoke also to the Greeks, preaching the Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:66; Acts 2:47; Acts 5:14"> And the hand of the Lord was with them: and a great number believing, were converted to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 9:27; Acts 8:14; Acts 13:1–3"> And the tidings came to the ears of the church that was at Jerusalem, touching these things: and they sent Barnabas as far as Antioch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; Acts 14:22; Acts 13:43"> Who, when he was come, and had seen the grace of God, rejoiced: and he exhorted them all with purpose of heart to continue in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 11:21; Acts 5:14; Acts 9:31"> For he was a good man, and full of the Holy Ghost and of faith. And a great multitude was added to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 9:11; Acts 9:27; Acts 9:30"> And Barnabas went to Tarsus to seek Saul: whom, when he had found, he brought to Antioch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 4:16; Acts 26:28; 1 Peter 4:14"> And they conversed there in the church a whole year; and they taught a great multitude, so that at Antioch the disciples were first named Christians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 13:1; 1 Corinthians 12:28; Ephesians 4:11"> And in these days there came prophets from Jerusalem to Antioch:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 21:10; Acts 18:2; Luke 3:1"> And one of them named Agabus, rising up, signified by the Spirit, that there should be a great famine over the whole world, which came to pass under Claudius.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12–14; Galatians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 8:2–4"> And the disciples, every man according to his ability, purposed to send relief to the brethren who dwelt in Judea:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 14:23; Acts 12:25; Acts 15:4"> Which also they did, sending it to the ancients, by the hands of Barnabas and Saul.</span>
</div>
