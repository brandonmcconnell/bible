<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 15:6; Philippians 2:2; Acts 1:13–15"> And when the days of the Pentecost were accomplished, they were all together in one place:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 4:31; 1 Kings 19:11; Psalms 18:10"> And suddenly there came a sound from heaven, as of a mighty wind coming, and it filled the whole house where they were sitting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 3:11; John 1:32–33; Jeremiah 23:29"> And there appeared to them parted tongues as it were of fire, and it sat upon every one of them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 16:17; Acts 13:52; Acts 4:31"> And they were all filled with the Holy Ghost, and they began to speak with divers tongues, according as the Holy Ghost gave them to speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 2:25; Acts 8:2; Colossians 1:23"> Now there were dwelling at Jerusalem, Jews, devout men, out of every nation under heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 2:3; 1 Corinthians 16:9; Acts 2:2"> And when this was noised abroad, the multitude came together, and were confounded in mind, because that every man heard them speak in his own tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 2:12; Acts 1:11; John 7:52"> And they were all amazed, and wondered, saying: Behold, are not all these, that speak, Galileans?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And how have we heard, every man our own tongue wherein we were born?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 1:1; Acts 18:2; Revelation 1:4"> Parthians, and Medes, and Elamites, and inhabitants of Mesopotamia, Judea, and Cappadocia, Pontus and Asia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 16:6; Zechariah 8:23; Zechariah 8:20"> Phrygia, and Pamphylia, Egypt, and the parts of Libya about Cyrene, and strangers of Rome,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:10; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 78:4"> Jews also, and proselytes, Cretes, and Arabians: we have heard them speak in our own tongues the wonderful works of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 15:26; Acts 17:20; Luke 18:36"> And they were all astonished, and wondered, saying one to another: What meaneth this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:23; Acts 2:15; 1 Samuel 1:14"> But others mocking, said: These men are full of new wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 51:7; Isaiah 51:1; Deuteronomy 27:9"> But Peter standing up with the eleven, lifted up his voice, and spoke to them: Ye men of Judea, and all you that dwell in Jerusalem, be this known to you, and with your ears receive my words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:5–8; 1 Samuel 1:15; Matthew 20:3"> For these are not drunk, as you suppose, seeing it is but the third hour of the day:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joel 2:28–32"> But this is that which was spoken of by the prophet Joel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joel 2:28–32; Isaiah 44:3; Titus 3:4–6"> And it shall come to pass, in the last days, (saith the Lord,) I will pour out of my Spirit upon all flesh: and your sons and your daughters shall prophesy, and your young men shall see visions, and your old men shall dream dreams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; Colossians 3:11; 1 Corinthians 7:21–22"> And upon my servants indeed, and upon my handmaids will I pour out in those days of my spirit, and they shall prophesy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joel 2:30–31; Zephaniah 1:14–18; Malachi 4:1–6"> And I will shew wonders in the heaven above, and signs on the earth beneath: blood and fire, and vapour of smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Amos 8:9; Matthew 24:29"> The sun shall be turned into darkness, and the moon into blood, before the great and manifest day of the Lord come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 10:12–13; Joel 2:32; Psalms 86:5"> And it shall come to pass, that whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord, shall be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 3:2; John 14:10–11; Matthew 12:28"> Ye men of Israel, hear these words: Jesus of Nazareth, a man approved of God among you, by miracles, and wonders, and signs, which God did by him, in the midst of you, as you also know:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 22:22; Acts 4:28; Acts 3:18"> This same being delivered up, by the determinate counsel and foreknowledge of God, you by the hands of wicked men have crucified and slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:14; 1 Peter 1:21; Acts 2:32"> Whom God hath raised up, having loosed the sorrows of hell, as it was impossible that he should be holden by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 16:8–11; Isaiah 41:13; Psalms 62:6"> For David saith concerning him: I foresaw the Lord before my face: because he is at my right hand, that I may not be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 16:9; Psalms 63:5; Psalms 71:23"> For this my heart hath been glad, and any tongue hath rejoiced: moreover my flesh also shall rest in hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 16:10; Acts 2:31; Revelation 1:18"> Because thou wilt not leave my soul in hell, nor suffer thy Holy One to see corruption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 16:11; Psalms 25:4; Psalms 21:6"> Thou hast made known to me the ways of life: thou shalt make me full of joy with thy countenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 13:36; 1 Kings 2:10; Acts 7:8–9"> Ye men, brethren, let me freely speak to you of the patriarch David; that he died, and was buried; and his sepulchre is with us to this present day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 132:11–18; Psalms 89:3–4; Jeremiah 33:14–15"> Whereas therefore he was a prophet, and knew that God hath sworn to him with an oath, that of the fruit of his loins one should sit upon his throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 2:27; Psalms 16:10; Acts 13:35"> Foreseeing this, he spoke of the resurrection of Christ. For neither was he left in hell, neither did his flesh see corruption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 3:15; Acts 1:8; Acts 1:22"> This Jesus hath God raised again, whereof all we are witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 1:4; Acts 5:31; Acts 2:17"> Being exalted therefore by the right hand of God, and having received of the Father the promise of the Holy Ghost, he hath poured forth this which you see and hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Matthew 22:42–45; Hebrews 1:13"> For David ascended not into heaven; but he himself said: The Lord said to my Lord, sit thou on my right hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Revelation 19:19–3; Romans 16:20; Isaiah 60:14"> Until I make thy enemies thy footstool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 5:30–31; Romans 14:8–12; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> Therefore let all the house of Israel know most certainly, that God hath made both Lord and Christ, this same Jesus, whom you have crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 3:10; Acts 22:10; Hebrews 4:12–13"> Now when they had heard these things, they had compunction in their heart, and said to Peter, and to the rest of the apostles: What shall we do, men and brethren?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 22:16; Mark 16:16; Acts 8:12"> But Peter said to them: Do penance, and be baptized every one of you in the name of Jesus Christ, for the remission of your sins: and you shall receive the gift of the Holy Ghost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 44:3; Genesis 17:7–8; Romans 8:30"> For the promise is to you, and to your children, and to all that are far off, whomsoever the Lord our God shall call.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Philippians 2:15; Deuteronomy 32:5; Matthew 17:17"> And with very many other words did he testify and exhort them, saying: Save yourselves from this perverse generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Acts 4:4; Acts 2:47; Acts 16:31–34"> They therefore that received his word, were baptized; and there were added in that day about three thousand souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Hebrews 10:25; Acts 1:14; 1 John 1:3"> And they were persevering in the doctrine of the apostles, and in the communication of the breaking of bread, and in prayers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 7:16; John 14:12; Luke 8:37"> And fear came upon every soul: many wonders also and signs were done by the apostles in Jerusalem, and there was great fear in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Acts 4:32; 1 John 3:16–18; 2 Corinthians 8:14–15"> And all they that believed, were together, and had all things common.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 John 3:17; 1 Timothy 6:18–19; James 2:14–16"> Their possessions and goods they sold, and divided them to all, according as every one had need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Acts 5:42; Acts 20:7; Acts 2:42"> And continuing daily with one accord in the temple, and breaking bread from house to house, they took their meat with gladness and simplicity of heart;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 16:5; Acts 5:13–14; Acts 2:41"> Praising God, and having favour with all the people. And the Lord increased daily together such as should be saved.</span>
