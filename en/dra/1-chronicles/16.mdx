<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:1; Ezra 6:16–18; 2 Chronicles 5:6–7"> So they brought the ark of God, and set it in the midst of the tent, which David had pitched for it: and they offered holocausts, and peace offerings before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 47:10; Genesis 14:19; Leviticus 1:3"> And when David had made an end of offering holocausts, and peace offerings, he blessed the people in the name of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:10; Ezekiel 45:17; 2 Chronicles 35:7–8"> And he divided to all and every one, both men and women, a loaf of bread, and a piece of roasted beef, and flour fried with oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:27–32; Psalms 72:18; Psalms 103:2"> And he appointed Levites to minister before the ark of the Lord, and to remember his works, and to glorify, and praise the Lord God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:39; 2 Chronicles 29:25; 1 Chronicles 15:16–24"> Asaph the chief, and next after him Zacharias: moreover Jahiel, and Semiramoth, and Jehiel, and Mathathias, and Eliab, and Banaias, and Obededom: and Jehiel over the instruments of psaltery, and harps: and Asaph sounded with cymbals:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:12; 2 Chronicles 29:26–28; 2 Chronicles 5:12–13"> But Banaias, and Jaziel the priests, to sound the trumpet continually before the ark of the covenant of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:1; Psalms 18:1; 2 Samuel 23:1–2"> In that day David made Asaph the chief to give praise to the Lord with his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 105:1–15; Isaiah 12:4; 2 Kings 19:19"> Praise ye the Lord, and call upon his name: make known his doings among the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; Psalms 96:1–3; Psalms 98:1–4"> Sing to him, yea, sing praises to him: and relate all his wondrous works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–8; Jeremiah 29:13; Psalms 34:2"> Praise ye his holy name: let the heart I of them rejoice, that seek the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 27:8–9; Zephaniah 2:2–3; Psalms 68:35"> Seek ye the Lord, and his power: seek ye his face evermore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 111:4; Psalms 119:20; Psalms 103:2"> Remember his wonderful works, which he hath done: his signs, and the judgments of his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Exodus 19:5–6; Psalms 135:4"> O ye seed of Israel his servants, ye children of Jacob his chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 48:10–11; Isaiah 26:9; Exodus 15:2"> He is the Lord our God: his judgments are in all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 105:8; Malachi 4:4; Deuteronomy 7:9"> Remember for ever his covenant: the word, which he commanded to a thousand generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 26:3; Genesis 17:2; Genesis 35:11"> The covenant which he made with Abraham: and his oath to Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 17:7–8; Genesis 35:11–12; Joshua 24:11–13"> And he appointed the same to Jacob for a precept: and to Israel for an everlasting covenant:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 13:15; Micah 2:5; Genesis 17:8"> Saying: To thee will I give the land of Chanaan: the lot of your inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; Deuteronomy 7:7; Acts 7:5"> When they were but a small number: very few and sojourners in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 46:6; Genesis 20:1; Genesis 46:3"> And they passed from nation to nation: and from a kingdom to another people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 12:17; Genesis 20:3; Exodus 7:15–18"> He suffered no man to do them wrong: and reproved kings for their sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 20:7; Psalms 105:15; Genesis 49:8–10"> Touch not my anointed: and do no evil to my prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 96:1–13; Psalms 71:15; Isaiah 51:6–8"> Sing ye to the Lord, all the earth: shew forth from day to day his salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2–6; Daniel 4:1–3; Psalms 22:27"> Declare his glory among the Gentiles: his wonders among all people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 89:7; Psalms 76:7; Psalms 66:3–5"> For the Lord is great and exceedingly to be praised: and he is to be feared above all gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 102:25; Leviticus 19:4; Isaiah 42:5"> For all the gods of the nations are idols: but the Lord made the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 16:11; Psalms 96:6; Psalms 28:7–8"> Praise and magnificence are before him: strength and joy in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:10; Philippians 4:13; Psalms 100:1–2"> Bring ye to the Lord, O ye families of the nations: bring ye to the Lord glory and empire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 100:4; Psalms 96:9; Isaiah 6:3"> Give to the Lord glory to his name, bring up sacrifice, and come ye in his sight: and adore the Lord in holy becomingness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 33:9; Psalms 148:5–6; Hebrews 1:3"> Let all the earth be moved at his presence: for he hath founded the world immoveable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 96:10; Revelation 19:6; Psalms 97:1"> Let the heavens rejoice, and the earth be glad: and let them say among the nations: The Lord hath reigned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 148:9–10; Isaiah 44:23; Psalms 98:7–8"> Let the sea roar, and the fulness thereof: let the fields rejoice, and all things that are in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 98:9; Psalms 96:12–13; Revelation 11:17–18"> Then shall the trees of the wood give praise before the Lord: because he is come to judge the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 106:1; 2 Chronicles 7:3; Psalms 107:1"> Give ye glory to the Lord, for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 106:47–48; 1 Peter 2:9; Psalms 14:7"> And say ye: Save us, O God our saviour: and gather us together, and deliver us from the nations, that we may give glory to thy holy name, and may rejoice in singing thy praises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 8:15; Psalms 72:18–19; 1 Kings 8:56"> Blessed be the Lord the God of Israel from eternity to eternity: and let all the people say Amen, and a hymn to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; Ezra 3:4; 1 Chronicles 16:4–6"> So he left there before the ark of the covenant of the Lord, Asaph and his brethren to minister in the presence of the ark continually day by day, and in their courses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:10; 1 Chronicles 13:14; 1 Chronicles 25:3"> And Obededom, with his brethren sixty-eight: and Obededom the son of Idithun, and Hosa he appointed to be porters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 3:4; 1 Chronicles 15:11; 2 Chronicles 1:3–4"> And Sadoc the priest, and his brethren priests, before the tabernacle of the Lord in the high place, which was in Gabaon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 28:3–8; Exodus 29:38–42; 2 Chronicles 31:3"> That they should offer holocausts to the Lord upon the altar of holocausts continually, morning and evening, according to all that is written in the law of the Lord, which he commanded Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Chronicles 5:13; 1 Chronicles 25:1–6; 2 Chronicles 20:21"> And after him Heman, and Idithun, and the rest that were chosen, every one by his name to give praise to the Lord: because his mercy endureth for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 150:3–6; 1 Chronicles 25:6–7; 2 Chronicles 29:25–28"> And Heman and Idithun sounded the trumpet, and played on the cymbals, and all kinds of musical instruments to sing praises to God: and the sons of Idithun he made porters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:19–20; Genesis 18:19; Psalms 101:2"> And all the people returned to their houses: and David to bless also his own house.</span>
