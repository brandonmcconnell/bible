<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 11:1; 2 Samuel 12:26–31; Amos 1:14"> And it came to pass after the course of a year, at the time that kings go out to battle, Joab gathered together an army and the strength of the troops, and wasted the land of the children of Ammon: and went and besieged Rabba. But David stayed at Jerusalem, when Joab smote Rabba, and destroyed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:30–31; 2 Samuel 8:11–12; 1 Chronicles 18:11"> And David took the crown of Melchom from his head, and found in it a talent weight of gold, and most precious stones, and he made himself a diadem of it: he took also the spoils of the city which were very great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 19:2–5; 1 Kings 9:21; Judges 8:6–7"> And the people that were therein he brought out: and made harrows, and sleds, and chariots of iron to go over them, so that they were cut and bruised to pieces: in this manner David dealt with all the cities of the children of Ammon: and he returned with alibis people to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:18–22; 2 Samuel 21:15; Joshua 12:12"> After this there arose a war at Gazer against the Philistines: in which Sabachai the Husathite slew Saphai of the race of Raphaim, and humbled them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:19; 1 Samuel 21:9; 1 Samuel 17:7"> Another battle also was fought against the Philistines, in which Adeodatus the son of Saltus a Bethlehemite slew the brother of Goliath the Gethite, the staff of whose spear was like a weaver’s beam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:20"> There was another battle also in Geth, in which there was a man of great stature, whose fingers and toes were four and twenty, six on each hand and foot: who also was born of the stock of Rapha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:13; 1 Samuel 16:9; Isaiah 37:23"> He reviled Israel: but Jonathan the son of Samaa the brother of David slew him. These were the sons of Rapha in Geth, who fell by the hand of David and his servants.</span>
