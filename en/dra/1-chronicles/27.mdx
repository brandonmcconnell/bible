<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 4:27; 2 Chronicles 26:11–13; Deuteronomy 1:15"> Now the children of Israel according to their number, the heads of families, captains of thousands and of hundreds, and officers, that served the king according to their companies, who came in and went out every month in the year, under every chief were four and twenty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:11; 2 Samuel 23:8"> Over the first company the first month Jesboam, the son of Zabdiel was chief, and under him were four and twenty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 26:20; Numbers 10:14; Genesis 49:8–10"> Of the sons of Phares, the chief of all the captains in the host in the first month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:9; 1 Chronicles 11:12"> The company of the second month was under Dudia, an Ahohite, and after him was another named Macelloth, who commanded a part of the army of four and twenty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 18:17; 1 Kings 4:4–5"> And the captain of the third company for the third month, was Banaias the son of Joiada the priest: and in his division were four and twenty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:20–23; 1 Chronicles 11:22–25; 2 Samuel 22:20–23"> This is that Banaias the most valiant among the thirty, and above the thirty. And Amizabad his son commanded his company.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:26; 2 Samuel 23:24; 2 Samuel 2:18–23"> The fourth, for the fourth month, was Asahel the brother of Joab, and Zabadias his son after him: and in his company were four and twenty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:27; 2 Samuel 23:25; 1 Chronicles 26:29"> The fifth captain for the fifth month, was Samaoth a Jezerite: and his company were four and twenty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:28; 2 Samuel 23:26"> The sixth, for the sixth month, was Hira the son of Acces a Thecuite: and in his company were four and twenty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:27; 2 Samuel 23:26"> The seventh, for the seventh month, was Helles a Phallonite of the sons of Ephraim: and in his company were four and twenty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:18; 1 Chronicles 11:29; Numbers 26:20"> The eighth, for the eighth month, was Sobochai a Husathite of the race of Zarahi: and in his company were four and twenty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:28; 2 Samuel 23:27"> The ninth, for the ninth month, was Abiezer an Anathothite of the sons of Jemini, and in His company were four and twenty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:30; 2 Samuel 23:28"> The tenth, for the tenth month, was Marai, who was a Netophathite of the race of Zarai: and in his company were four and twenty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:31; 2 Samuel 23:30"> The eleventh, for the eleventh month, was Banaias, a Pharathonite of the sons of Ephraim: and in his company were four and twenty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:29; 1 Chronicles 4:13; Judges 3:9"> The twelfth, for the twelfth month, was Holdai a Netophathite, of the race of Gothoniel: and in his company were four and twenty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Now the chiefs over the tribes of Israel were these: over the Rubenites, Eliezer the son of Zechri was ruler: over the Simeonites, Saphatias the son of Maacha:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:30; 1 Chronicles 24:31; 1 Chronicles 12:27–28"> Over the Levites, Hasabias the son of Camuel: over the Aaronites, Sadoc:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:6; 1 Samuel 17:29; 1 Samuel 17:13"> Over Juda, Eliu the brother of David: over Issachar, Amri the son of Michael:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Over the Zabulonites, Jesmaias the son of Adias: over the Nephtalites, Jerimoth the son of Ozriel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Over the sons of Ephraim, Osee the: son of Ozaziu: over the half tribe of Manasses, Joel the son of Phadaia:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:27; 1 Samuel 14:50–51; 2 Samuel 3:37"> And over the half tribe of Manasses: in Galaad, Jaddo the son of Zacharias: and over Benjamin, Jasiel the son of Abner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:1"> And over Dan, Ezrihel the son of Jeroham: these were the princes of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 15:5; 1 Chronicles 21:2–5; Hebrews 11:12"> But David would not number them from twenty years old and under: because the Lord had said that he would multiply Israel like the stars of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:1–17; 2 Samuel 24:1–15"> Joab the son of Sarvia began to number, but he finished not: because upon this there fell wrath upon Israel: and therefore the number of them that were numbered, was not registered in the chronicles of king David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:2; Exodus 1:11; 2 Chronicles 26:10"> And over the king’s treasures was Azmoth the son of Adiel: and over those stores which were in the cities, and is the villages, and in the castles, was Jonathan the son of Ozias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And over the tillage, and the husbandmen, who tilled the ground, was Ezri the son of Chelub:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And over the dressers of the vineyards, was Semeias a Romathite: and over the wine cellars, Zabdias an Aphonite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Kings 10:27; 2 Chronicles 1:15; 1 Kings 4:7"> And over the oliveyards and the fig groves, which were in the plains, was Balanam a Gederite: and over the oil cellars, Joas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:16; Isaiah 65:10"> And over the herds that fed in Saron, was Setrai a Saronite: and over the. oxen in the valleys, Saphat the son of Adli:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 47:6; Job 1:3"> And over the camels, Ubil an Ishmahelite: and over the asses, Jadias a Meronathite:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:10"> And over the sheep Jazia an Agarene. All these were the rulers of the substance of king David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:21; 2 Samuel 13:3; 1 Chronicles 11:11"> And Jonathan David’s uncle, a counsellor, a wise and learned man: he and Jahiel the son of Hachamoni were with the king’s sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:12; 2 Samuel 15:37; 2 Samuel 15:32"> And Achitophel was the king’s counsellor, and Chusai the Arachite, the king’s friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:6; 1 Kings 1:7; 1 Chronicles 27:5"> And after Achitophel was Joiada the son of Banaias, and Abiathar. And the general of the king’s army was Joab.</span>
