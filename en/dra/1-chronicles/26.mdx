<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:21"> The sons of Meselemia: Zacharias the firstborn, Jadihel the second, Zabadias the third, Jathanael the fourth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Elam the fifth, Johanan the sixth, Elioenai the seventh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:38; 1 Chronicles 15:24; 1 Chronicles 15:18"> And the sons of Obededom, Semeias the firstborn, Jozabad the second, Joaha the third, Sachar the fourth, Nathanael the fifth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 128:1"> Ammiel the sixth, Issachar the seventh, Phollathi the eighth: for the Lord had blessed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:28; 1 Timothy 6:12; Judges 6:12"> And to Semei his son were born sons, herds of their families: for they were men of great valour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> The sons then of Semeias were Othni, and Raphael, and Obed, Elizabad, and his brethren most valiant men: and Eliu, and Samachias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:6; 1 Peter 4:11; 1 Corinthians 12:4–11"> All these of the sons of Obededom: they, and their sons, and their brethren most able men for service, sixty-two of Obededom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:14; 1 Chronicles 26:1"> And the sons of Meselemia, and their brethren strong men, were eighteen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:38; 1 Chronicles 5:1–2"> And of Hosa, that is, of the sons of Merari: Semri the chief, (for he had not a firstborn, and therefore his father made him chief.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Helcias the second, Tabelias the third, Zacharias the fourth: all these the sons, and the brethren of Hosa, were thirteen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:8"> Among these were the divisions of the porters, so that the chiefs of the wards, as well as their brethren, always ministered in the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:8; 1 Chronicles 24:31; 1 Chronicles 24:5"> And they cast lots equally, both little and great, by their families for every one of the gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And the lot of the east fell to Selemias. But to his son Zacharias, a very wise and learned man, the north gate fell by lot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:24; 1 Chronicles 26:17"> And to Obededom and his sons that towards the south: in which part of the house was the council of the ancients.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:24; 1 Chronicles 26:10–12; 1 Kings 10:5"> To Sephim, and Hosa towards the west, by the gate which leadeth to the way of the ascent: ward against ward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 9:24"> Now towards the east were six Levites: and towards the north four a day: and towards the south likewise four a day: and where the council was, two and two.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 23:11"> In the cells also of the porters toward the west four in the way: and two at every cell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 16:11"> These are the divisions of the porters of the sons of Core, and of Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:22; 1 Chronicles 28:12–19; 1 Chronicles 22:14–16"> Now Achias was over the treasures of the house of God, and the holy vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:7; 1 Chronicles 6:17; 1 Chronicles 29:8"> The sons of Ledan, the sons of Gersonni: of Ledan were heads of the families, of Ledan, and Gersonni, Jehieli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:8; Nehemiah 10:38; 1 Chronicles 26:20"> The sons of Jehieli: Zathan and Joel, his brethren over the treasures of the house of the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 3:27; 1 Chronicles 23:12; Numbers 3:19"> With the Amramites, and Isaarites, and Hebronites, and Ozielites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:20; 1 Chronicles 23:15–16"> And Subael the son of Gersom, the son of Moses, was chief over the treasures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:17–18; 1 Chronicles 23:15; Exodus 18:4"> His brethren also, Eliezer, whose son Rohobia, and his son Isaias, and his son Joram, and his son Zechri, and his son Selemith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:11; 1 Chronicles 29:2–9; 1 Chronicles 22:14"> Which Selemith and his brethren were over the treasures of the holy things, which king David, and the heads of families, and the captains over thousands and over hundreds, and the captains of the host had dedicated,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 12:14; Joshua 6:19; Nehemiah 10:32–34"> Out of the wars, and the spoils won in battles, which they had consecrated to the building and furniture of the temple of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:9; 1 Samuel 17:55; 2 Samuel 10:9–14"> And all these things that Samuel the seer and Saul the son of Cis, and Abner the son of Ner, and Joab the son of Sarvia had sanctified: and whosoever had sanctified those things, they were under the hand of Selemith and his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:4; Nehemiah 11:16; 2 Chronicles 34:13"> But Chonenias and his sons were over the Isaarites, for the business abroad over Israel to teach them and judge them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:17; 1 Chronicles 23:19; 1 Chronicles 23:12"> And of the Hebronites Hasabias, and his brethren most able men, a thousand seven hundred had the charge over Israel beyond the Jordan westward, in all the works of the Lord, and for the service of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:19; Joshua 21:39; 1 Chronicles 29:27"> And the chief of the Hebronites was Jeria according to their families and kindreds. In the fortieth year of the reign of David they were numbered, and there were found most valiant men in Jazer Galaad,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:11; 1 Chronicles 12:37; 1 Chronicles 23:24"> And his brethren of stronger age, two thousand seven hundred chiefs of families. And king David made them rulers over the Rubenites and the Gadites, and the half tribe of Manasses, for all the service of God, and the king.</span>
