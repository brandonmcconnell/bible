<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:20; 2 Chronicles 34:29–30; 2 Chronicles 29:20"> And David consulted with the captains of thousands, and of hundreds, and with all the commanders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 4:4–20; 1 Samuel 31:1; 1 Chronicles 10:7"> And he said to all the assembly of Israel: If it please you; and if the words which I speak come from the Lord our God, let us send to the rest of our brethren into all the countries of Israel, and to the priests, and the Levites, that dwell in the suburbs of the cities, to gather themselves to us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:1–2; 1 Samuel 23:2; 1 Samuel 14:18"> And let us bring again the ark of our God to us: for we sought it not in the days of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esther 8:5; 2 Samuel 3:36; 1 Samuel 18:20"> And all the multitude answered that it should be so: for the word pleased all the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:21–1; 1 Kings 8:65; 2 Samuel 6:1"> So David assembled all Israel from Sihor of Egypt, even to the entering into Emath, to bring the ark of God from Cariathiarim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 25:22; Joshua 15:9; 2 Kings 19:15"> And David went up with all the men of Israel to the hill of Cariathiarim which is in Juda, to bring thence the ark of the Lord God sitting upon the cherubims, where his name is called upon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; 1 Samuel 6:7; 1 Chronicles 15:2"> And they carried the ark of God upon a new cart, out of the house of Abinadab. And Oza and his brother drove the cart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 150:3–5; Psalms 68:25–27; Amos 6:5"> And David and all Israel played before God with all their might with hymns, and with harps, and with psalteries, and timbrels, and cymbals, and trumpets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:6"> And when they came to the floor of Chidon, Oza put forth his hand, to hold up the ark: for the ox being wanton had made it lean a little on one side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:15; 1 Chronicles 15:13; Numbers 4:15"> And the Lord was angry with Oza, and struck him, because he had touched the ark; and he died there before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:7; 2 Samuel 6:9; Genesis 32:32"> And David was troubled because the Lord had divided Oza: and he called that place the Breach of Oza to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; 1 Kings 8:27; Job 25:5–6"> And he feared God at that time, saying: How can I bring in the ark of God to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:4; 1 Chronicles 15:18; 2 Samuel 4:3"> And therefore he brought it not home to himself, that is, into the city of David, but carried it aside into the house of Obededom the Gethite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:4–5; Proverbs 10:22; Proverbs 3:9–10"> And the ark of God remained in the house of Obededom three months: and the Lord blessed his house, and all that he had.</span>
