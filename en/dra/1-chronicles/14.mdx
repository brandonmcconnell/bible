<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 24:7; Psalms 89:20–37; 2 Samuel 7:16"> And David perceived that the Lord had confirmed him king over Israel, and that his kingdom was exalted over his people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 5:18–19; 2 Samuel 5:13; Ecclesiastes 7:26–29"> And David took other wives in Jerusalem: and he beget sons, and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:5–9; 2 Samuel 5:14; Luke 3:31"> Now these are the names of them that were born to him in Jerusalem: Samua, and Sobad, Nathan, and Solomon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:15; 1 Chronicles 3:6"> Jebahar, and Elisua, and Eliphalet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And Noga, and Napheg, and Japhia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:8; 2 Samuel 5:16"> Elisama. and Baaliada, and Eliphalet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:11; Revelation 11:15–18; Psalms 2:1–6"> And the Philistines hearing that David was anointed king over all Israel, went all up to seek him: and David heard of it, and went out against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:15; 1 Chronicles 14:13; 2 Samuel 23:13"> And the Philistines came and spread themselves in the vale of Raphaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 3:6; 1 Chronicles 14:14; 1 Chronicles 13:3"> And David consulted the Lord, saying: Shall I go up against the Philistines, and wilt thou deliver them into my hand? And the Lord said to him: Go up, and I will deliver them into thy hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 28:21; Exodus 14:28; 2 Samuel 5:20"> And when they were come to Baalpharasim, David defeated them there, and he said: God hath divided my enemies by my hand, as waters are divided: and therefore the name of that place was called Baalpharasim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 32:20; 1 Samuel 5:2–6; 2 Kings 19:18"> And they left there their gods, and David commanded that they should be burnt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 14:9; 1 Kings 20:22; 2 Samuel 5:22–25"> Another time also the Philistines made an irruption, and spread themselves abroad in the valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 9:6–7; 1 Chronicles 14:10; Psalms 27:4"> And David consulted God again, and God said to him: Go not up after them, turn away from them, and come upon them over against the pear trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 13:4; Acts 2:2; Philippians 2:12–13"> And when thou shalt hear the sound of one going in the tops of the pear trees, then shalt thou go out to battle. For God is gone out before thee to strike the army of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 15:14; John 13:17; Genesis 6:22"> And David did as God had commanded him, and defeated the army of the Philistines, slaying them from Gabaon to Gazera.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:25; Joshua 6:27; Exodus 15:14–16"> And the name of David became famous in all countries, and the Lord made all nations fear him.</span>
