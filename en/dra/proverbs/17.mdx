<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 15:17; Psalms 37:16; Proverbs 21:19"> Better is a dry morsel with joy, than a house full of victims with strife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 10:5; Ecclesiastes 4:13; Proverbs 14:35"> A wise servant shall rule over foolish sons, and shall divide the inheritance among the brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 48:10; Jeremiah 17:10; Psalms 26:2"> As silver is tried by fire, and gold in the furnace: so the Lord trieth the hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3–4; 1 John 4:5; Proverbs 28:4"> The evil man obeyeth an unjust tongue: and the deceitful hearkeneth to lying lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 14:31; Job 31:29; 1 John 3:17"> He that despiseth the poor, reproacheth his Maker; and he that rejoiceth at another man’s ruin, shall not be unpunished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 127:3–5; Proverbs 13:22; Proverbs 16:31"> Children’s children are the crown of old men: and the glory of children are their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 29:12; Proverbs 12:19; Psalms 50:16–17"> Eloquent words do not become a fool, nor lying lips a prince.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 23:8; Micah 7:3; 1 Samuel 25:35"> The expectation of him that expecteth, is a most acceptable jewel: whithersoever he turneth himself, he understandeth wisely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 10:12; 1 Peter 4:8; Proverbs 16:28"> He that concealeth a transgression. seeketh friendships: he that repeateth it again, separateth friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 9:8–9; Proverbs 15:5"> A reproof availeth more with a wise man, than a hundred stripes with a fool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 2:46; 2 Samuel 16:5–9; 2 Samuel 18:15"> An evil man always seeketh quarrels: but a cruel angel shall be sent against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 13:8; 2 Samuel 17:8; Proverbs 28:15"> It is better to meet a bear robbed of her whelps, than a fool trusting in his own folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 109:4–13; 1 Peter 3:9; Romans 12:17"> He that rendereth evil for good, evil shall not depart from his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 20:3; Proverbs 25:8; 2 Timothy 2:23–24"> The beginning of quarrels is as when one letteth out water: before he suffereth reproach he forsaketh judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 23:7; Proverbs 18:5; Isaiah 5:23"> He that justifieth the wicked, and he that condemneth the just, both are abominable before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 21:25–26; Proverbs 23:23; Proverbs 18:15"> What doth it avail a fool to have riches, seeing he cannot buy wisdom? He that maketh his house high, seeketh a downfall: and he that refuseth to learn, shall fall into evils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 18:24; John 15:13–14; 1 Samuel 20:17"> He that is a friend loveth at all times: and a brother is proved in distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 11:15; Proverbs 6:1–5; Proverbs 22:26–27"> A foolish man will clap hands, when he is surety for his friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 16:18; Proverbs 18:12; 1 Samuel 25:36–38"> He that studieth discords, loveth quarrels: and he that exalteth his door, seeketh ruin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 3:6–8; Proverbs 10:31; Proverbs 6:12–15"> He that is of a perverse heart, shall not find good: and he that perverteth his tongue, shall fall into evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 19:13; Proverbs 17:25"> A fool is born to his own disgrace: and even his father shall not rejoice in a fool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 15:13; Proverbs 12:25; Proverbs 18:14"> A joyful mind maketh age flourishing: a sorrowful spirit drieth up the bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 17:8; Deuteronomy 16:19; Exodus 23:8"> The wicked man taketh gifts out of the bosom, that he may pervert the paths of judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:14; Ecclesiastes 8:1; Proverbs 14:6"> Wisdom shineth in the face of the wise: the eyes of fools are in the ends of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; 2 Samuel 13:1–22; Proverbs 19:13"> A foolish son is the anger of the father: and the sorrow of the mother that bore him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 17:15; Proverbs 18:5; John 18:22"> It is no good thing to do hurt to the just: nor to strike the prince, who judgeth right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 3:18; James 1:19; Proverbs 10:19"> He that setteth bounds to his words. is knowing and wise: and the man of understanding is of a precious spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 13:5; Proverbs 15:2; Ecclesiastes 10:14"> Even a fool, if he will hold his peace shall be counted wise: and if he close his lips, a man of understanding.</span>
