<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 1:1; 1 Kings 4:32; Micah 1:1"> These are also parables of Solomon, which the men of Ezechias king of Juda copied out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:29; Romans 11:33–34; Job 11:7–8"> It is the glory of God to conceal the word, and the glory of kings to search out the speech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 55:9; Psalms 103:11; 1 Kings 4:29"> The heaven above, and the earth beneath, and the heart of kings is unsearchable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:20–21; Ezekiel 22:18; Proverbs 17:3"> Take away the rust from silver, and there shall come forth a most pure vessel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 16:12; Proverbs 20:8; Psalms 101:7–8"> Take away wickedness from the face of the king, and his throne shall be established with justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 27:2; Proverbs 25:27; Proverbs 16:19"> Appear not glorious before the king, and stand not in the place of great men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 14:7–11; Luke 18:14; 1 Peter 5:5"> For it is better that it should be said to thee: Come up hither; than that thou shouldst be humbled before the prince.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 5:25; Proverbs 17:14; Proverbs 18:6"> The things which thy eyes have seen, utter not hastily in a quarrel: lest afterward thou mayst not be able to make amends, when thou hast dishonoured thy friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 18:5–17; Proverbs 11:13; Proverbs 20:19"> Treat thy cause with thy friend, and discover not the secret to a stranger:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 119:39"> Lest he insult over thee, when he hath heard it, and cease not to upbraid thee. Grace and friendship deliver a man: keep these for thyself, lest thou fall under reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 15:23; Isaiah 50:4; Ecclesiastes 12:10"> To speak a word in due time, is like apples of gold on beds of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 141:5; Proverbs 15:31–32; Proverbs 15:5"> As an earring of gold and a bright pearl, so is he that reproveth the wise, and the obedient ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 13:17; Proverbs 25:25; Philippians 2:25–30"> As the cold of snow in the time of harvest, so is a faithful messenger to him that sent him, for he refresheth his soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 20:6; Luke 14:11; 2 Peter 2:15–19"> As clouds, and wind, when no rain followeth, so is the man that boasteth, and doth not fulfill his promises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; Ecclesiastes 10:4; Proverbs 16:14"> By patience a prince shall be appeased, and a soft tongue shall break hardness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 25:27; Ephesians 5:18; Proverbs 24:13–14"> Thou hast found honey, eat what is sufficient for thee, lest being glutted therewith thou vomit it up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 19:2–3; Judges 19:18–21; Romans 15:24"> Withdraw thy foot from the house of thy neighbour, lest having his fill he hate thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Proverbs 12:18; James 3:6"> A man that beareth false witness against his neighbour, is like a dart and a sword and a sharp arrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 36:6; Ezekiel 29:6–7; Job 6:14–20"> To trust to an unfaithful man in the time of trouble, is like a rotten tooth, and weary foot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Ecclesiastes 3:4; Isaiah 58:7"> And one that looseth his garment in cold weather. As vinegar upon nitre, so is he that singeth songs to a very evil heart. As a moth doth by a garment, and a worm by the wood: so the sadness of a man consumeth the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 12:20–21; Matthew 5:44; Exodus 23:4–5"> If thy enemy be hungry, give him to eat: if he thirst, give him water to drink:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:12; Matthew 10:13; 1 Corinthians 15:18"> For thou shalt heap hot coals upon his head, and the Lord will reward thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 37:22; Psalms 15:3; Romans 1:30"> The north wind driveth away rain, as doth a sad countenance a backbiting tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 21:19; Proverbs 21:9; Proverbs 27:15–16"> It is better to sit in a corner of the housetop, than with a brawling woman, and in a common house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 15:30; Romans 10:15; Psalms 63:1"> As cold water to a thirsty soul, so is good tidings from a far country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:18–19; 1 Samuel 22:14–18; 1 Thessalonians 2:15"> A just man falling down before the wicked, is as a fountain troubled with the foot, and a corrupted spring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 5:44; Proverbs 27:2; Philippians 2:3"> As it is not good for a man to eat much honey, so he that is a searcher of majesty, shall be overwhelmed by glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 16:32; Proverbs 22:24; 2 Chronicles 32:5"> As a city that lieth open and is not compassed with walls, so is a man that cannot refrain his own spirit in speaking.</span>
