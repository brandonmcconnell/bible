<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; 1 Chronicles 22:9; 1 Chronicles 28:5"> The parables of Solomon, the son of David, king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 7:4; Deuteronomy 4:5–6; 2 Timothy 3:15–17"> To know wisdom, and instruction:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 2:1–9; Job 22:22; Proverbs 8:10–11"> To understand the words of prudence: and to receive the instruction of doctrine, justice, and judgment, and equity:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 8:5; Proverbs 8:17; Psalms 119:9"> To give subtilty to little ones, to the young man knowledge and understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 12:1; Proverbs 9:9; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> A wise man shall hear and shall be wiser: and he that understandeth, shall possess governments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 78:2; Mark 4:11; Psalms 49:4"> He shall understand a parable, and the interpretation, the words of the wise, and their mysterious sayings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 28:28; Psalms 111:10–1; Ecclesiastes 12:13"> The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom. Fools despise wisdom and instruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 6:20; Proverbs 30:17; Proverbs 5:1–2"> My son, hear the instruction of thy father, and forsake not the law of thy mother:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 5:29; Proverbs 4:9; Genesis 41:42"> That grace may be added to thy head, and a chain of gold to thy neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; Proverbs 16:29; Psalms 1:1"> My son, if sinners shall entice thee, consent not to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:26; Proverbs 1:18; Proverbs 12:6"> If they shall say: Come with us, let us lie in wait for blood, let us hide snares for the innocent without cause:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 124:3; Psalms 28:1; Romans 3:13"> Let us swallow him up alive like hell, and whole as one that goeth down into the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Luke 12:15; Job 24:2–3"> We shall find all precious substance, we shall fill our houses with spoils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Cast in thy lot with us, let us all have one purse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:101; Psalms 1:1; Proverbs 4:14–15"> My son, walk not thou with them, restrain thy foot from their paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Proverbs 6:18; Proverbs 4:16"> For their feet run to evil, and make haste to shed blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 35:11; Proverbs 7:23; Jeremiah 8:7"> But a net is spread in vain before the eyes of them that have wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 9:16; Esther 7:10; Proverbs 5:22–23"> And they themselves lie in wait for their own blood, and practise deceits against their own souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 15:27; 2 Peter 2:3; James 5:1–4"> So the wage of every covetous man destroy the souls of the possessors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 9:3; Colossians 2:3; Luke 11:49"> Wisdom preacheth abroad, she uttereth her voice in the streets:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 5:20; Proverbs 9:3; Matthew 13:2"> At the head of multitudes she crieth out, in the entrance of the gates of the city she uttereth her words, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 1:29; Psalms 1:1; Proverbs 5:12"> O children, how long will you love childishness, and fools covet those things which are hurtful to themselves, and the unwise hate knowledge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 3:19; Ezekiel 33:11; Ezekiel 18:27–30"> Turn ye at my reproof: behold I will utter my spirit to you, and will shew you my words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 65:12; Romans 10:21; Jeremiah 7:13"> Because I called, and you refused: I stretched out my hand, and there was none that regarded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 81:11; Luke 7:30; Proverbs 1:30"> You have despised all my counsel, and have neglected my reprehensions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 2:4; Proverbs 10:24; Psalms 37:13"> I also will laugh in your destruction, and will mock when that shall come to you which you feared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 3:25–26; Luke 21:34–35; Psalms 58:9"> When sudden calamity shall fall on you, and destruction, as a tempest, shall be at hand: when tribulation and distress shall come upon you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Micah 3:4; James 4:3; Jeremiah 11:11"> Then shall they call upon me, and I will not hear: they shall rise in the morning and shall not find me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 1:22; Job 21:14–15; Proverbs 6:23"> Because they have hated instruction and received not the fear of the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 81:11; Proverbs 1:25; Luke 14:18–20"> Nor consented to my counsel, but despised all my reproof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 14:14; Jeremiah 6:19; Proverbs 22:8"> Therefore they shall eat the fruit of their own way, and shall be filled with their own devices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 8:36; Jeremiah 2:19; John 3:36"> The turning away of little ones shall kill them, and the prosperity of fools shall destroy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 25:12–13; Psalms 112:7–8; Isaiah 26:3"> But he that shall hear me, shall rest without terror, and shall enjoy abundance, without fear of evils.</span>
