<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 21:23; Proverbs 18:21; Proverbs 18:7"> He that keepeth his mouth, keepeth his soul: but he that hath no guard on his speech shall meet with evils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 12:24; Proverbs 12:11; 2 Peter 1:5–11"> The sluggard willeth and willeth not: but the soul of them that work, shall be made fat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 3:9; Ephesians 4:25; Psalms 119:163"> The just shall hate a lying word: but the wicked confoundeth, and shall be confounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 11:3; Proverbs 11:5–6; Psalms 140:11"> Justice keepeth the way of the innocent: but wickedness overthroweth the sinner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 18:11–14; Revelation 3:17; Proverbs 11:24"> One is as it were rich, when he hath nothing: and another is as it were poor, when he hath great riches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; 1 Peter 1:18–19; Psalms 49:6–10"> The ransom of a man’s life are his riches: but he that is poor beareth not reprehension.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 4:18; Proverbs 24:20; Proverbs 20:20"> The light of the just giveth joy: but the lamp of the wicked shall be put out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 19:20; Proverbs 12:15–16; Proverbs 17:14"> Among the proud there are always contentions: but they that do all things with counsel, are ruled by wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 20:21; Proverbs 10:2; James 5:1–5"> Substance got in haste shall be diminished: but that which by little and little is gathered with the hand shall increase.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 16:22; Proverbs 13:19; Psalms 119:81–83"> Hope that is deferred afflicteth the soul: desire when it cometh is a tree of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 15:31; 2 Chronicles 36:16; 2 John 1:8"> Whosoever speaketh ill of any thing, bindeth himself for the time to come: but he that feareth the commandment, shall dwell in peace. Deceitful souls go astray in sins: the just are merciful, and shew mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 18:5; Proverbs 10:11; Proverbs 14:27"> The law of the wise is a fountain of life, that he may decline from the ruin of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:19; Proverbs 4:19; Acts 7:10"> Good instruction shall give grace: in the way of scorners is a deep pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 15:2; Proverbs 12:22–23; Ephesians 5:17"> The prudent mall doth all things with counsel: but he that is a fool, layeth open his folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 25:13; 1 Corinthians 4:2; 2 Corinthians 5:20"> The messenger of the wicked shall fall into mischief: but a faithful ambassador is health.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 15:5; Proverbs 12:1; Proverbs 15:31–32"> Poverty and shame to him that refuseth instruction: but he that yieldeth to reproof, shall be glorified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 7:14–17; Proverbs 13:12; Proverbs 29:27"> The desire that is accomplished, delighteth the soul: fools hate them that flee from evil things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33–34; Proverbs 15:31; Proverbs 9:6"> He that walketh with the wise, shall be wise: a friend of fools shall become like to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 32:10; Isaiah 3:10–11; Romans 2:7–10"> Evil pursueth sinners: and to the just good shall be repaid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:26; Job 27:16–17; Proverbs 28:8"> The good man leaveth heirs, sons, and grandsons: and the substance of the sinner is kept for the just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Proverbs 28:19; Ecclesiastes 5:9"> Much food is in the tillage of fathers: but for others it is gathered with out judgment.</span>
