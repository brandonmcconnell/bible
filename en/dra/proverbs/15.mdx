<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 15:31–32; Proverbs 13:1; Proverbs 13:18"> A fool laugheth at the instruction of his father: but he that regardeth reproofs shall become prudent. <span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 8:21; Proverbs 21:20; Proverbs 10:22"> The house of the just is very much strength: and in the fruits of the wicked is trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 12:34; James 3:6; Ephesians 4:29"> The lips of the wise shall disperse knowledge: the heart of fools shall be unlike.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:11; Proverbs 21:21; Matthew 7:13"> The way of the wicked is an abomination to the Lord: he that followeth justice is beloved by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 12:1; John 3:20; Proverbs 13:1"> Instruction is grievous to him that forsaketh the way of life: he that hateth reproof shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 44:21; Hebrews 4:13; Psalms 139:8"> Hell and destruction are before the Lord: how much more the hearts of the children of men?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 5:10; Job 21:14; Proverbs 9:7–8"> A corrupt man loveth not one that reproveth him: nor will he go to the wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 17:22; John 14:1; Proverbs 18:14"> A glad heart maketh a cheerful countenance: but by grief of mind the spirit is cast down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 9:9; Isaiah 30:10; Proverbs 18:15"> The heart of the wise seeketh instruction: and the mouth of fools feedeth on foolishness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:10; Romans 12:12; Proverbs 15:13"> All the days of the poor are evil: a secure mind is like a continual feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 16:8; Psalms 37:16; Ecclesiastes 5:10–12"> Better is a little with the fear of the Lord, than great treasures without content,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 17:1; Proverbs 21:19; 1 John 4:16"> It is better to be invited to herbs with love, than to a fatted calf with hatred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; Proverbs 26:21; Proverbs 29:22"> A passionate man stirreth up strifes: he that is patient appeaseth those that are stirred up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 35:8; Proverbs 22:5; Isaiah 30:21"> The way of the slothful is as a hedge of thorns; the way of the just is without offence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 29:3; Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 23:15–16"> A wise son maketh a father joyful: but the foolish man despiseth his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 5:15; Proverbs 10:23; Proverbs 14:16"> Folly is joy to the fool: and the wise man maketh straight his steps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 11:14; Proverbs 20:18; Ecclesiastes 8:6"> Designs are brought to nothing where there is no counsel: but where there are many counsellors, they are established.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 50:4; Proverbs 25:11–12; Ephesians 4:29"> A man rejoiceth in the sentence of his mouth: and a word in due time is best.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Colossians 3:1–2; Psalms 16:11; Matthew 7:14"> The path of life is above for the wise, that he may decline from the lowest hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 146:9; Psalms 68:5–6; Proverbs 14:11"> The Lord will destroy the house of the proud: and will strengthen the borders of the widow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 6:16–19; Jeremiah 4:14; Proverbs 15:23"> Evil thoughts are an abomination to the Lord: and pure words most beautiful shall be confirmed by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 23:8; Proverbs 1:19; Jeremiah 17:11"> He that is greedy of gain troubleth his own house: but he that hateth bribes shall live. By mercy and faith sins are purged away: and by the fear of the Lord every one declineth from evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Peter 3:15; Proverbs 29:11; Ecclesiastes 5:2"> The mind of the just studieth obedience: the mouth of the wicked over floweth with evils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 9:31; Psalms 145:18–19; Proverbs 15:8"> The Lord is far from the wicked: and he will hear the prayers of the just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 58:11; Revelation 22:5; Revelation 21:23"> The light of the eyes rejoiceth the soul: a good name maketh the bones fat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 15:5; Proverbs 25:12; John 15:3–4"> The ear that heareth the reproofs of life, shall abide in the midst of the wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24–33; Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 1:7"> He that rejecteth instruction, despiseth his own soul: but he that yieldeth to reproof possesseth understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; Proverbs 18:12; Proverbs 1:7"> The fear of the Lord is the lesson of wisdom: and humility goeth before glory.</span>
