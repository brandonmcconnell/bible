<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 27:12; Proverbs 14:16; Proverbs 29:1"> The prudent man saw the evil, and hid himself: the simple passed on, and suffered loss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; James 4:10; Psalms 34:9–10"> The fruit of humility is the fear of the Lord, riches and glory and life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 15:19; 1 John 5:18; Proverbs 16:17"> Arms and swords are in the way of the perverse: but he that keepeth his own soul departeth far from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 6:4; Deuteronomy 6:7; 2 Timothy 3:15"> It is a proverb: A young man according to his way, even when he is old he will not depart from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 4:1; Proverbs 22:16; Matthew 18:25"> The rich ruleth over the poor: and the borrower is servant to him that lendeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 6:7–8; Job 4:8; Isaiah 30:31"> He that soweth iniquity shall reap evils, and with the rod of his anger he shall be consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Hebrews 6:10; 1 Timothy 6:18–19"> He that is inclined to mercy shall be blessed: for of his bread he hath given to the poor. He that maketh presents shall purchase victory and honour: but he carrieth away the souls of the receivers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 26:20–21; Genesis 21:9–10; 1 Corinthians 5:5–6"> Cast out the scoffer, and contention shall go out with him, and quarrels and reproaches shall cease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 16:13; Matthew 5:8; Psalms 101:6"> He that loveth cleanness of heart, for the grace of his lips shall have the king for his friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:9; 2 Timothy 3:8–9; 2 Thessalonians 2:8"> The eyes of the Lord preserve knowledge: and the words of the unjust are overthrown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 15:19; Proverbs 26:13–16; Numbers 13:32–33"> The slothful man saith: There is a lion without, I shall be slain in the midst of the streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Proverbs 23:27; Proverbs 6:24–29"> The mouth of a strange woman is a deep pit: he whom the Lord is angry with, shall fall into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 13:24; Proverbs 29:15; Proverbs 19:18"> Folly is bound up in the heart of a child, and the rod of correction shall drive it away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 22:22–23; Psalms 12:5; James 2:13"> He that oppresseth the poor, to increase his own riches, shall himself give to one that is richer, and shall be in need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 23:12; Psalms 90:12; Proverbs 3:1"> Incline thy ear, and hear the words of the wise: and apply thy heart to my doctrine:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:16; Proverbs 2:10; Psalms 119:103"> Which shall be beautiful for thee, if thou keep it in thy bowels, and it shall flow in thy lips:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 62:8; Jeremiah 17:7; 1 Peter 1:21"> That thy trust may be in the Lord, wherefore I have also shewn it to thee this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 8:6; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> Behold I have described it to thee three manner of ways, in thoughts and knowledge:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:3–4; 1 Peter 3:15; John 20:31"> That I might shew thee the certainty, and the words of truth, to answer out of these to them that sent thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zechariah 7:10; Malachi 3:5; Exodus 23:6"> Do no violence to the poor, because he is poor: and do not oppress the needy in the gate:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 12:5; Psalms 140:12; Proverbs 23:11"> Because the Lord will judge his cause, and will afflict them that have afflicted his soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 29:22; 2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Proverbs 21:24"> Be not a friend to an angry man, and do not walk with a furious man:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; Proverbs 13:20; Psalms 106:35"> Lest perhaps thou learn his ways, and take scandal to thy soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 11:15; Proverbs 17:18; Proverbs 6:1–5"> Be not with them that fasten down their hands, and that offer themselves sureties for debts:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 20:16; 2 Kings 4:1; Exodus 22:26–27"> For if thou have not wherewith to restore, what cause is there, that he should take the covering from thy bed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:17; Deuteronomy 19:14; Proverbs 23:10"> Pass not beyond the ancient bounds which thy fathers have set.</span>
