<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:7; Psalms 53:5; Isaiah 26:3–4"> The wicked man fleeth, when no man pursueth: but the just, bold as a lion, shall be without dread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 15:28; 1 Kings 16:8–29; 2 Kings 15:8–31"> For the sins of the land many are the princes thereof: and for the wisdom of a man, and the knowledge of those things that are said, the life of the prince shall be prolonged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 18:28–30"> A poor man that oppresseth the poor, is like a violent shower, which bringeth a famine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:32; Ephesians 5:11; Matthew 14:4"> They that forsake the law, praise the wicked man: they that keep it, are incensed against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 92:6; James 1:5; 1 John 2:27"> Evil men think not on judgment: but they that seek after the Lord, take notice of all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 19:1; Proverbs 28:18; Luke 16:19–23"> Better is the poor man walking in his simplicity, than the rich in crooked ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:3–4; Proverbs 29:3; Proverbs 3:1–35"> He that keepeth the law is a wise son: but he that feedeth gluttons, shameth his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 27:16–17; Proverbs 13:22; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> He that heapeth together riches by usury and loan, gathereth them for him that will be bountiful to the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 66:18; 2 Timothy 4:3–4; Zechariah 7:11–13"> He that turneth away his ears from hearing the law, his prayer shall be as abomination.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 26:27; Psalms 37:25–26; Galatians 2:4"> He that deceiveth the just in a wicked way, shall fall in his own destruction: and the upright shall possess his goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 18:17; Ecclesiastes 9:15–17; Proverbs 26:16"> The rich man seemeth to himself wise: but the poor man that is prudent shall search him out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 29:2; Proverbs 11:10; Proverbs 28:28"> In the joy of the just there is great glory: when the wicked reign, men are ruined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 John 1:8–10; Psalms 32:3–5; Job 31:33"> He that hideth his sins, shall not prosper: but he that shall confess, and forsake them, shall obtain mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 23:17; Psalms 2:11; Isaiah 66:2"> Blessed is the man that is always fearful: but he that is hardened in mind, shall fall into evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Matthew 2:16; Proverbs 19:12"> As a roaring lion, and a hungry bear, so is a wicked prince over the poor people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; Jeremiah 22:15–17; Ecclesiastes 4:1"> A prince void of prudence shall oppress many by calumny: but he that hateth covetousness, shall prolong his days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 9:6; 1 Kings 21:19; Exodus 21:14"> A man that doth violence to the blood of a person, if he flee even to the pit, no man will stay him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 10:9; Proverbs 28:6; Revelation 3:3"> He that walketh uprightly, shall be saved: he that is perverse in his ways shall fall at once.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Judges 9:4; Proverbs 27:23–27"> He that tilleth his ground, shall be filled with bread: but he that followeth idleness shall be filled with poverty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 16:10–12; Proverbs 13:11; Revelation 2:10"> A faithful man shall be much praised: but he that maketh haste to be rich, shall not be innocent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 24:23; Proverbs 18:5; Ezekiel 13:19"> He that hath respect to a person in judgment, doth not well: such a man even for a morsel of bread forsaketh the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 23:6; 1 Timothy 6:9; Proverbs 28:20"> A man, that maketh haste to be rich, and envieth others, is ignorant that poverty shall come upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 27:5–6; Matthew 18:15; Psalms 141:5"> He that rebuketh a man, shall afterward find favour with him, more than he that by a flattering tongue deceiveth him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 15:4–6; Proverbs 18:9; Proverbs 19:26"> He that stealeth any thing from his father, or from his mother: and saith, This is no sin, is the partner of a murderer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 11:25; 1 Timothy 6:6; Proverbs 13:10"> He that boasteth, and puffeth up himself, stirreth up quarrels: but he that trusteth in the Lord, shall be healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5; Jeremiah 17:9; Job 28:28"> He that trusteth in his own heart, is a fool: but he that walketh wisely, he shall be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Deuteronomy 15:7; Psalms 41:1–3"> He that giveth to the poor, shall not want: he that despiseth his entreaty, shall suffer indigence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 29:2; Job 24:4; Acts 12:23–24"> When the wicked rise up, men shall hide themselves: when they perish, the lust shall be multiplied.</span>
