<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 6:15; 1 Samuel 2:25; Proverbs 1:24–31"> The man that with a stiff neck despiseth him that reproveth him, shall suddenly be destroyed: and health shall not follow him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 28:12; Proverbs 11:10; Proverbs 28:28"> When just men increase, the people shall rejoice: when the wicked shall bear rule, the people shall mourn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 27:11; Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 15:20"> A man that loveth wisdom, rejoiceth his father: but he that maintaineth bar lots, shall squander away his substance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 29:14; Proverbs 20:8; Daniel 11:20"> A just king setteth up the land: a covetous man shall destroy it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 5:1; Job 17:5; Psalms 5:9"> A man that speaketh to his friend with flattering and dissembling words, spreadeth a net for his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:12; Exodus 15:1; 2 Timothy 2:26"> A snare shall entangle the wicked man when he sinneth: and the just shall praise and rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 29:16; Psalms 41:1; Proverbs 21:13"> The just taketh notice of the cause of the poor: the wicked is void of knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 11:11; Jeremiah 15:1; James 5:15–18"> Corrupt men bring a city to ruin: but wise men turn away wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 26:4; Ecclesiastes 10:13; Matthew 7:6"> If a wise man contend with a fool, whether he be angry or laugh, he shall find no rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 15:18–19; 1 John 3:12–13; Genesis 4:5–8"> Bloodthirsty men hate the upright: but just men seek his soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 19:11; Proverbs 12:16; Micah 7:5"> A fool uttereth all his mind: a wise man deferreth, and keepeth it till afterwards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 20:8; 2 Kings 10:6–7; Psalms 52:2–4"> A prince that gladly heareth lying words, hath all his servants wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 5:45; Proverbs 22:2; Psalms 13:3"> The poor man and the creditor have met one another: the Lord is the enlightener of them both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 16:12; Isaiah 11:4; Psalms 72:2–4"> The king that judgeth the poor in truth, his throne shall be established for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 17:25; Proverbs 29:17"> The rod and reproof give wisdom: but the child that is left to his own will bringeth his mother to shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 37:36; Psalms 91:8; Psalms 58:10"> When the wicked are multiplied, crimes shall be multiplied: but the just shall see their downfall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 29:15; Proverbs 23:13–14"> Instruct thy son, and he shall refresh thee, and shall give delight to thy soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 1:25; Luke 11:28; John 13:17"> When prophecy shall fail, the people shall be scattered abroad: but he that keepeth the law is blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 26:3; Job 19:16; Proverbs 30:22"> A slave will not be corrected by words: because he understandeth what thou sayest, and will not answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 1:19; Proverbs 26:12; Proverbs 14:29"> Hast thou seen a man hasty to speak? folly is rather to be looked for, than his amendment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> He that nourisheth his servant delicately from his childhood, afterwards shall find him stubborn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 15:18; Proverbs 17:19; Proverbs 26:21"> A passionate man provoketh quarrels: and he that is easily stirred up to wrath, shall be more prone to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 23:12; Luke 14:11; Proverbs 11:2"> Humiliation followeth the proud: and glory shall uphold the humble of spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 5:1; Proverbs 1:11–19; Proverbs 8:36"> He that is partaker with a thief, hateth his own soul: he heareth one putting him to his oath, and discovereth not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 118:8; Luke 12:4; Proverbs 18:10"> He that feareth man, shall quickly fall: he that trusteth in the Lord, shall be set on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 19:6; Proverbs 19:21; Psalms 20:9"> Many seek the face of the prince: but the judgment of every one cometh forth from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 John 3:13; John 15:17–19; Psalms 139:21"> The just abhor the wicked man: and the wicked loathe them that are in the right way. The son that keepeth the word, shall be free from destruction.</span>
