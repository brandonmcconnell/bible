<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 1:1; Proverbs 29:15; Proverbs 17:25"> A wise son maketh the father glad: but a foolish son is the sorrow of his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 11:4; Proverbs 12:28; Psalms 49:6–10"> Treasures of wickedness shall profit nothing: but justice shall deliver from death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 37:3; Matthew 6:30–33; Psalms 37:25"> The Lord will not afflict the soul of the just with famine, and he will disappoint the deceitful practices of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 21:5; Proverbs 20:4; Proverbs 19:15"> The slothful hand hath wrought poverty: but the hand of the industrious getteth riches. He that trusteth to lies feedeth the winds: and the same runneth after birds that fly away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 6:8; Proverbs 30:25; Proverbs 17:2"> He that gathered in the harvest is a wise son: but he that snorteth in the summer, is the son of confusion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:2; Proverbs 28:20; Job 29:13"> The blessing of the Lord is upon the head of the just: but iniquity covereth the mouth of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 112:6; Luke 1:48; Mark 14:9"> The memory of the just is with praises: and the name of the wicked shall rot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 7:24–25; Proverbs 12:1; James 3:13"> The wise of heart receiveth precepts: a fool is beaten with lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 28:18; Matthew 10:26; Psalms 26:11–12"> He that walketh sincerely, walketh confidently: but he that perverteth his ways, shall be manifest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; Proverbs 6:13; 3 John 1:10"> He that winketh with the eye shall cause sorrow: and the foolish in lips shall be beaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 13:14; Ephesians 4:29; Proverbs 10:6"> The mouth of the just is a vein of life: and the mouth of the wicked covereth iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 4:8; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7; Proverbs 17:9"> Hatred stirreth up strifes: and charity covereth all sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 26:3; Proverbs 6:32; Isaiah 50:4"> In the lips of the wise is wisdom found: and a rod on the back of him that wanteth sense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 18:7; Proverbs 10:8; Proverbs 13:3"> Wise men lay up knowledge: but the mouth of the fool is next to confusion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 18:11; Proverbs 19:7; Psalms 52:7"> The substance of a rich man is the city of his strength: the fear of the poor is their poverty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 6:23; Isaiah 3:10–11; Hebrews 6:10"> The work of the just is unto life: but the fruit of the wicked, unto sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Peter 1:5–11; Luke 11:28; Proverbs 6:23"> The way of life, to him that observeth correction: but he that forsaketh reproofs goeth astray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 26:24–26; Psalms 55:21; 2 Samuel 3:27"> Lying lips hide hatred: he that uttereth reproach is foolish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="James 1:19; James 3:2; Proverbs 17:27–28"> In the multitude of words there shall not want sin: but he that refraineth his lips is most wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:9; Proverbs 12:18; Genesis 6:5"> The tongue of the just is as choice silver: but the heart of the wicked is nothing worth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hosea 4:6; Proverbs 17:16; Proverbs 1:29"> The lips of the just teach many: but they that are ignorant, shall die in the want of understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 107:38; Genesis 26:12; 1 Samuel 2:7–8"> The blessing of the Lord maketh men rich: neither shall affliction be joined to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 15:21; Proverbs 2:14; Proverbs 14:9"> A fool worketh mischief as it were for sport: but wisdom is prudence to a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 145:19; Isaiah 66:4; Matthew 5:6"> That which the wicked feareth, shall come upon him: to the just their desire shall be given.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 15:5; Psalms 58:9; Matthew 7:24–27"> As a tempest that passeth, so the wicked shall be no more: but the just is as an everlasting foundation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 25:13; Proverbs 26:6; Matthew 25:26"> As vinegar to the teeth, and smoke to the eyes, so is the sluggard to them that sent him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 9:11; Proverbs 3:2; Psalms 91:16"> The fear of the Lord shall prolong days: and the years of the wicked shall be shortened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 8:13; Romans 15:13; Psalms 73:24–26"> The expectation of the just is joy; but the hope of the wicked shall perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 21:15; Romans 2:8–9; Philippians 4:13"> The strength of the upright is the way of the Lord: and fear to them that work evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 37:22; Psalms 125:1; Psalms 37:9–10"> The just shall never be moved: but the wicked shall not dwell on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 37:30; Proverbs 10:13; Proverbs 10:20–21"> The mouth of the just shall bring forth wisdom: the tongue of the perverse shall perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 15:28; Proverbs 12:6; Proverbs 11:11"> The lips of the just consider what is acceptable: and the mouth of the wicked uttereth perverse things.</span>
