<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 1:26; Matthew 7:28–29; Proverbs 23:16"> Hear, for I will speak of great things: and my lips shall be opened to preach right things.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 14:6; James 1:5; John 7:17"> They are right to them that understand, and just to them that find knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 2:4–5; Psalms 119:127; Psalms 119:72"> Receive my instruction, and not money: choose knowledge rather than gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 3:14–15; Proverbs 4:5–7; Proverbs 16:16"> For wisdom is better than all the most precious things: and whatsoever may be desired cannot be compared to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 28:26; Exodus 35:30–4; Romans 11:33"> I wisdom dwell in counsel, and am present in learned thoughts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 97:10; Proverbs 16:6; 1 Samuel 2:3"> The fear of the Lord hateth evil: I hate arrogance, and pride, and every wicked way, and a mouth with a double tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:19; Isaiah 9:6; Isaiah 40:14"> Counsel and equity is mine, prudence is mine, strength is mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 13:1; Daniel 2:21; Matthew 28:18"> By me kings reign, and lawgivers decree just things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> By me princes rule, and the mighty decree justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 14:23; John 14:21; Psalms 91:14"> I love them that love me: and they that in the morning early watch for me, shall find me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:16; Matthew 6:33; Luke 16:11–12"> With me are riches and glory, glorious riches and justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 3:14; Proverbs 10:20; Ecclesiastes 7:12"> For my fruit is better than gold and the precious stone, and my blossoms than choice silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:32; John 10:27–28; Psalms 32:8"> I walk in the way of justice, in the midst of the paths of judgment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 1:4; Proverbs 24:4; Matthew 25:46"> That I may enrich them that love me, and may fill their treasures.</span>

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 90:2; Psalms 102:25–28; Job 38:4–11"> The mountains with their huge bulk had not as yet been established: before the hills I was brought forth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 1:1–31"> He had not yet made the earth, nor the rivers, nor the poles of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 40:22; Job 26:10; Proverbs 3:19"> When he prepared the heavens, I was present: when with a certain law and compass he enclosed the depths:</span>

<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 1:1–3; John 1:18; Isaiah 42:1"> I was with him forming all things: and was delighted every day, playing before him at all times;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 16:3; John 13:1; Psalms 40:6–8"> Playing in the world: and my delights were to be with the children of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 11:28; Psalms 119:1–2; Psalms 1:1–4"> Now therefore, ye children, hear me: Blessed are they that keep my ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 4:1; Psalms 81:11–12; Proverbs 5:1"> Hear instruction and be wise, and refuse it not.</span>

<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 15:32; Proverbs 1:31; 1 Corinthians 16:22"> But he that shall sin against me, shall hurt his own soul. All that hate me love death.</span>
