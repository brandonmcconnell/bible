<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 15:10; Proverbs 9:7–8; Psalms 119:97–100"> He that loveth correction, loveth knowledge: but he that hateth reproof is foolish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 8:35; Acts 11:24; Psalms 112:5"> He that is good, shall draw grace from the Lord: but he that trusteth in his own devices doth wickedly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 10:25; Ephesians 3:17; Proverbs 12:12"> Men shall not be strengthened by wickedness: and the root of the just shall not be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 14:30; Proverbs 31:10–25; Proverbs 14:1"> A diligent woman is a crown to her husband: and she that doth things worthy of confusion, is a rottenness in his bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 139:23; Isaiah 55:7; Psalms 140:1–3"> The thoughts of the just are judgments: and the counsels of the wicked are deceitful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 14:3; Jeremiah 5:26; Proverbs 1:11–19"> The words of the wicked lie in wait for blood: the mouth of the just shall deliver them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:24–27; Proverbs 11:21; Proverbs 10:25"> Turn the wicked, and they shall not be: but the house of the just shall stand firm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:18; 1 Corinthians 4:5; 1 Corinthians 3:10–15"> A man shall be known by his learning: but he that is vain and foolish, shall be exposed to contempt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 14:11; Proverbs 13:7"> Better is the poor man that provideth for himself, than he that is glorious and wanteth bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:4; Genesis 33:13–14; Numbers 22:28–32"> The just regardeth the lives of his beasts: but the bowels of the wicked are cruel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 28:19; Proverbs 14:23; 1 Thessalonians 4:11–12"> He that tilleth his land shall be satisfied with bread: but he that pursueth idleness is very foolish. He that is delighted in passing his time over wine, leaveth a reproach in his strong holds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:7–8; John 15:5; Luke 8:13–15"> The desire of the wicked is the fortification of evil men: but the root of the just shall prosper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 21:23; Proverbs 11:8; Psalms 64:8"> For the sins of the lips ruin draweth nigh to the evil mall: but the just shall escape out of distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 18:20–21; Isaiah 3:10–11; Proverbs 13:2"> By the fruit of his own mouth shall a man be filled with good things, and according to the works of his hands it shall be repaid him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 3:7; Proverbs 26:12; Proverbs 14:16"> The way of a fool is right in his own eyes: but he that is wise hearkeneth unto counsels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 29:11; James 1:19; Proverbs 10:12"> A fool immediately sheweth his anger: but he that dissembleth injuries is wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 14:5; Proverbs 14:25; Proverbs 24:28"> He that speaketh that which he knoweth, sheweth forth justice: but he that lieth, is a deceitful witness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 16:24; Proverbs 15:4; Psalms 57:4"> There is that promiseth, and is pricked as it were with a sword of conscience: but the tongue of the wise is health.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 19:9; Psalms 52:4–5; Job 20:5"> The lip of truth shall be steadfast for ever: but he that is a hasty witness, frameth a lying tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 5:9; 1 Peter 3:8–13; Proverbs 12:12"> Deceit is in the heart of them that think evil things: but joy followeth them that take counsels of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 3:13; Psalms 91:10; Romans 8:28"> Whatsoever shall befall the just man. it shall not make him sad: but the wicked shall be filled with mischief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 5:6; Proverbs 11:20; Revelation 22:15"> Lying lips are an abomination to the Lord: but they that deal faithfully please him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 13:16; Proverbs 11:13; Proverbs 10:19"> A cautious man concealeth knowledge: and the heart of fools publisheth folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 12:27; Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 21:25–26"> The hand of the valiant shall bear rule: but that which is slothful, shall be under tribute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 16:24; Proverbs 15:13; Proverbs 17:22"> Grief in the heart of a man shall bring him low, but with a good word he shall be made glad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 John 2:26; Matthew 5:46–48; Proverbs 17:27"> He that neglecteth a loss for the sake of a friend, is just: but the way of the wicked shall deceive them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 26:15; Proverbs 15:16"> The deceitful man shall not find gain: but the substance of a just man shall be precious gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="3 John 1:11; Romans 5:21; Ezekiel 18:20–24"> In the path of justice is life: but the by-way leadeth to death.</span>
