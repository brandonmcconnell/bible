<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 22:26; Proverbs 17:18; Proverbs 20:16"> My son, if thou be surety for thy friend, thou hast engaged fast thy hand to a stranger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 18:7; Proverbs 12:13"> Thou art ensnared with the words of thy mouth, and caught with thy own words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 4:10; Psalms 31:8; 2 Samuel 24:14"> Do therefore, my son, what I say, and deliver thyself: because thou art fallen into the hand of thy neighbour. Run about, make haste, stir up thy friend:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 132:4; Proverbs 6:10–11; Mark 13:35–36"> Give not sleep to thy eyes, neither let thy eyelids slumber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 91:3; Psalms 124:7; Proverbs 1:17"> Deliver thyself as a doe from the hand, and as a bird from the hand of the fowler.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 20:4; Proverbs 18:9; Proverbs 6:9"> Go to the ant, O sluggard, and consider her ways, and learn wisdom:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 38:39–12; Proverbs 30:27; Job 39:26–30"> Which, although she hath no guide, nor master, nor captain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 10:5; 1 Timothy 6:19; Proverbs 30:25"> Provideth her meat for herself in the summer, and gathereth her food in the harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 24:33–34; Jeremiah 4:14; Ephesians 5:14"> How long wilt thou sleep, O sluggard? when wilt thou rise out of thy sleep?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 24:33–34; Proverbs 6:6; Proverbs 23:33–34"> Thou wilt sleep a little, thou wilt slumber a little, thou wilt fold thy hands a little to sleep:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 24:34"> And want shall come upon thee, as a traveller, and poverty as a man armed. But if thou be diligent, thy harvest shall come as a fountain, and want shall flee far from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:34; 1 Timothy 5:13; Proverbs 16:27"> A man that is an apostate, an unprofitable man, walketh with a perverse mouth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; Proverbs 10:10; Job 15:12"> He winketh with the eyes, presseth with the foot, speaketh with the finger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 16:17; Micah 2:1; Hosea 8:7"> With a wicked heart he deviseth evil, and at all times he soweth discord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:16; Jeremiah 19:11; 1 Thessalonians 5:3"> To such a one his destruction shall presently come, and he shall suddenly be destroyed, and shall no longer have any remedy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 11:5; Proverbs 8:13; Proverbs 11:20"> Six things there are, which the Lord hateth, and the seventh his soul detesteth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 120:2–3; Proverbs 12:22; Psalms 101:5"> Haughty eyes, a lying tongue, hands that shed innocent blood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 6:5; Proverbs 1:16; Romans 3:15"> A heart that deviseth wicked plots, feet that are swift to run into mischief,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 19:5; Proverbs 6:14; James 3:18"> A deceitful witness that uttereth lies, and him that soweth discord among brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1; Deuteronomy 27:16; Proverbs 7:1–4"> My son, beep the commandments of thy father, and forsake not the law of thy mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 3:3; Proverbs 7:3–4; 2 Corinthians 3:3"> Bind them in thy heart continually, and put them about thy neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; Proverbs 3:23–24; Psalms 119:24"> When thou walkest, let them go with thee: when thou sleepest, let them keep thee; and when thou awakest, talk with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 119:105; 2 Peter 1:19; Psalms 19:8"> Because the commandment is a lamp, and the law a light, and reproofs of instruction are the way of life:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 2:16; Proverbs 7:5; Ecclesiastes 7:26"> That they may keep thee from the evil woman, and from the flattering tongue of the stranger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 1:14–15; Matthew 5:28; 2 Kings 9:30"> Let not thy heart covet her beauty, be not caught with her winks:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 15:30; Proverbs 29:3; 1 Samuel 2:36"> For the price of a harlot is scarce one loaf: but the woman catcheth the precious soul of a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 31:9–12; Hosea 7:4–7; James 3:5"> Can a man hide fire in his bosom, and his garments not burn?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Or can he walk upon hot coals, and his feet not be burnt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:1; Malachi 3:5; 2 Samuel 12:9–10"> So he that goeth in to his neighbour’s wife, shall not be clean when he shall touch her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 38:39"> The fault is not so great when a man hath stolen: for he stealeth to fill his hungry soul:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 19:8; Job 20:18; 2 Samuel 12:6"> And if he be taken he shall restore sevenfold, and shall give up all the substance of his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 7:7; Hebrews 13:4; Romans 1:22–24"> But he that is an adulterer, for the folly of his heart shall destroy his own soul:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 5:9–11; Psalms 51:8; Judges 16:19–21"> He gathereth to himself shame and dishonour, and his reproach shall not be blotted out:</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 27:4; Numbers 5:14; Song of Solomon 8:6"> Because the jealousy and rage of the husband will not spare in the day of revenge,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 8:25; Malachi 2:9; Proverbs 7:13"> Nor will he yield to any man’s prayers, nor will he accept for satisfaction ever so many gifts.</span>
