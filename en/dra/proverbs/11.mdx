<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:13–16; Proverbs 16:11; Proverbs 20:23"> A deceitful balance is an abomination before the Lord: and a just weight is his will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 29:23; Proverbs 15:33; Proverbs 16:18–19"> Where pride is, there also shall be reproach: but where humility is, there also is wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 13:6; Proverbs 28:18; Psalms 26:1"> The simplicity of the just shall guide them: and the deceitfulness of the wicked shall destroy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:18; Proverbs 10:2; Genesis 7:1"> Riches shall not profit in the day of revenge: but justice shall deliver from death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 5:22; Psalms 9:15–16; Proverbs 1:31–32"> The justice of the upright shall make his way prosperous: and the wicked man shall fall by his own wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:8; 1 Kings 2:44; Psalms 7:15–16"> The justice of the righteous shall deliver them: and the unjust shall be caught in their own snares.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 10:28; Job 8:13–14; Job 11:20"> When the wicked man is dead, there shall be no hope any more: and the expectation of the solicitous shall perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 6:23–24; Proverbs 21:18; Esther 7:9–10"> The just is delivered out of distress: and the wicked shall be given up for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 2:10–16; Proverbs 4:5–6; 1 John 2:27"> The dissembler with his mouth deceiveth his friend: but the just shall be delivered by knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 28:12; Esther 8:15–16; Psalms 58:10–11"> When it goeth well with the just the city shall rejoice: and when the wicked perish there shall be praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 14:34; Proverbs 29:8; James 3:6"> By the blessing of the just the city shall be exalted: and by the mouth of the wicked it shall be overthrown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 10:19; 1 Peter 2:23; Luke 18:9"> He that despiseth his friend, is mean of heart: but the wise man will hold his peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 20:19; Proverbs 25:9; Leviticus 19:16"> He that walketh deceitfully, revealeth secrets: but he that is faithful, concealeth the thing committed to him by his friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 24:6; Proverbs 15:22; Proverbs 20:18"> Where there is no governor, the people shall fall: but there is safety where there is much counsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 17:18; Proverbs 6:1–5; Proverbs 22:26–27"> He shall be afflicted with evil, that is surety for a stranger: but he that is aware of the snares, shall be secure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 31:30–31; Luke 10:42; Acts 16:14–15"> A gracious woman shall find glory: and the strong shall have riches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; Matthew 25:34–40; James 2:13"> A merciful man doth good to his own soul: but he that is cruel casteth off even his own kindred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 6:8–9; Ecclesiastes 10:8; Hosea 10:12–13"> The wicked maketh an unsteady work: but to him that soweth justice, there is a faithful reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 10:16; Romans 2:8–9; Proverbs 12:28"> Clemency prepareth life: and the pursuing of evil things, death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 119:1; Proverbs 21:29; 1 Chronicles 29:17"> A perverse heart is abominable to the Lord: and his will is in them that walk sincerely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 112:1–2; Acts 2:39; Jeremiah 32:39"> Hand in hand the evil man shall not be innocent: but the seed of the just shall be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 3:3–4; Proverbs 31:30; Nahum 3:4–6"> A golden ring in a swine’s snout, a woman fair and foolish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; Romans 2:8–9; Matthew 5:6"> The desire of the just is all good: the expectation of the wicked is indignation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Luke 6:38; Deuteronomy 15:10"> Some distribute their own goods, and grow richer: others take away what is not their own, and are always in want.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; 2 Corinthians 9:6–9; Isaiah 58:7–11"> The soul which blesseth, shall be made fat: and he that inebriateth, shall be inebriated also himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 29:13; Genesis 42:6; Amos 8:4–6"> He that hideth up corn, shall be cursed among the people: but a blessing upon the head of them that sell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 7:15–16; Proverbs 17:11; Psalms 9:15–16"> Well doth he rise early who seeketh good things; but he that seeketh after evil things shall be oppressed by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:17; Jeremiah 17:8; Psalms 52:7–8"> He that trusteth in his riches shall fall: but the just shall spring up as a green leaf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:16; Proverbs 14:19; Proverbs 15:27"> He that troubleth his own house, shall inherit the winds: and the fool shall serve the wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Daniel 12:3; James 5:20; 1 Corinthians 9:19–23"> The fruit of the just man is a tree of life: and he that gaineth souls, is wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17–18; Jeremiah 25:29; 1 Corinthians 11:30–32"> If the just man receive in the earth, how much more the wicked and the sinner.</span>
