<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 10:13; Psalms 32:9; 1 Corinthians 4:21"> A whip for a horse, and a snaffle for an ass, and a rod for the back of fools.</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 12:16; Matthew 16:1–4; Proverbs 28:11"> Answer a fool according to his folly, lest he imagine himself to be wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 10:26; Proverbs 25:13; Proverbs 13:17"> He that sendeth words by a foolish messenger, is lame of feet and drinketh iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 26:9; Psalms 64:8; Proverbs 17:7"> As a lame man hath fair legs in vain: so a parable is unseemly in the mouth of fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 26:1; Proverbs 30:22; Proverbs 19:10"> As he that casteth a stone into the heap of Mercury: so is he that giveth honour to a fool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 23:35"> As if a thorn should grow in the hand of a drunkard: so is a parable in the mouth of fools.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 2:22; Matthew 12:45; Exodus 8:15"> As a dog that returneth to his vomit, so is the fool that repeateth his folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 29:20; 1 Corinthians 3:18–19; Proverbs 26:5"> Hast thou seen a man wise in his own conceit? there shall be more hope of a fool than of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 22:13; Proverbs 19:15; Proverbs 15:19"> The slothful man saith: There is a lion in the way, and a lioness in the roads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 6:12; Proverbs 12:27; Proverbs 12:24"> As the door turneth upon its hinges, so doth the slothful upon his bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 19:24"> The slothful hideth his hand under his armpit, and it grieveth him to turn it to his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 26:12; 1 Peter 3:15; Proverbs 12:15"> The sluggard is wiser in his own conceit, than seven men that speak sentences.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 20:3; 2 Timothy 2:23–24; Luke 12:14"> As he that taketh a dog by the ears, so is he that passeth by in anger, and meddleth with another man’s quarrel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 25:18; Isaiah 50:11; Proverbs 7:23"> As he is guilty that shooteth arrows, and lances unto death:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 5:4; Proverbs 10:23; Proverbs 15:21"> So is the man that hurteth his friend deceitfully: and when he is taken, saith: I did it in jest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 22:10; James 3:6; Proverbs 16:28"> When the wood faileth, the fire shall go out: and when the talebearer is taken away, contentions shall cease.</span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 18:8; Proverbs 20:19; Ezekiel 22:9"> The words of a talebearer are as it were simple, but they reach to the innermost parts of the belly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 22:47–48; Luke 11:39; Proverbs 10:18"> Swelling lips joined with a corrupt heart, are like an earthen vessel adorned with silver dross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 12:20; Proverbs 10:18; Psalms 41:6"> An enemy is known by his lips, when in his heart he entertaineth deceit.</span>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 28:10; Psalms 9:15; Psalms 7:15–16"> He that diggeth a pit, shall fall into it: and he that rolleth a stone, it shall return to him.</span>
