<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 31:1; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> The words of Gatherer the son of Vomiter. The vision which the man spoke with whom God is, and who being strengthened by God, abiding with him, said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 73:22; 1 Corinthians 3:18; Isaiah 6:5"> I am the most foolish of men, and the wisdom of men is not with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 3:7; Ephesians 3:18–19; Romans 11:33"> I have not learned wisdom, and have not known the science of saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 3:13; Ephesians 4:8–10; Job 26:8"> Who hath ascended up into heaven, and descended? who hath held the wind in his hands? who hath bound up the waters together as in a garment? who hath raised up all the borders of the earth? what is his name, and what is the name of his son, if thou knowest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 18:30; James 3:17; Psalms 12:6"> Every word of God is fire tried: he is a buckler to them that hope in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; Deuteronomy 4:2; Revelation 22:18–19"> Add not any thing to his words, lest thou be reproved, and found a liar:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 10:42; 2 Kings 2:9; Psalms 27:4"> Two things I have asked of thee, deny them not to me before I die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:6–8; Psalms 62:9–10; Psalms 119:37"> Remove far from me vanity, and lying words. Give me neither beggary, nor riches: give me only the necessaries of life:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 13:6; Deuteronomy 31:20; Ezekiel 16:49–50"> Lest perhaps being filled, I should be tempted to deny, and say: Who is the Lord? or being compelled by poverty, I should steal, and forswear the name of my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 14:4; Ecclesiastes 7:21; Deuteronomy 23:15"> Accuse not a servant to his master, lest he curse thee, and thou fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 20:20; Proverbs 30:17; Exodus 21:17"> There is a generation that curseth their father, and doth not bless their mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 18:11; Proverbs 16:2; 2 Timothy 3:5"> A generation that are pure in their own eyes, and yet are not washed from their filthiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 6:17; Isaiah 2:11; Psalms 101:5"> A generation, whose eyes are lofty, and their eyelids lifted up on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Psalms 3:7; Psalms 14:4"> A generation, that for teeth hath swords, and grindeth with their jaw teeth, to devour the needy from off the earth, and the poor from among men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 30:21; Proverbs 30:29; Proverbs 6:16"> The horseleech hath two daughters that say: Bring, bring. There are three things that never are satisfied, and the fourth never saith: It is enough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 27:20; Habakkuk 2:5; Genesis 30:1"> Hell, and the mouth of the womb, and the earth which is not satisfied with water: and the fire never saith: It is enough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 23:22; Deuteronomy 21:18–21; Proverbs 30:11"> The eye that mocketh at his father, and that despiseth the labour of his mother in bearing him, let the ravens of the brooks pick it out, and the young eagles eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 42:3; Psalms 139:6"> Three things are hard to me, and the fourth I am utterly ignorant of.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 40:31; Job 39:27; Exodus 22:16"> The way of an eagle in the air, the way of a serpent upon a rock, the way of a ship in the midst of the sea, and the way of a man in youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 5:6; Proverbs 7:13–23; Numbers 5:11–30"> Such is also the way of an adulterous woman, who eateth, and wipeth her mouth, and saith: I have done no evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> By three things the earth is disturbed, and the fourth it cannot bear:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 19:10; Ecclesiastes 10:7; 1 Samuel 25:10–11"> By a slave when he reigneth: by a fool when he is filled with meat:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 21:19; Proverbs 27:15; Proverbs 29:21"> By an odious woman when she is married: and by a bondwoman when she is heir to her mistress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 12:7"> There are four very little things of the earth, and they are wiser than the wise:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 6:6–8"> The ants, a feeble people, which provide themselves food in the harvest:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 104:18; Leviticus 11:5"> The rabbit, a weak people, which maketh its bed in the rock:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joel 2:25; Joel 1:6–7; Joel 2:7–11"> The locust hath no king, yet they all go out by their bands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> The stellio supporteth itself on hands, and dwelleth in kings’ houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> There are three things, which go well, and the fourth that walketh happily:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 23:24; Judges 14:18"> A lion, the strongest of beasts, who hath no fear of any thing he meeteth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Daniel 3:15–18; Proverbs 20:2; Proverbs 16:14"> A cock girded about the loins: and a ram: and a king, whom none can resist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 21:5; Job 40:4; Proverbs 26:12"> There is that hath appeared a fool after he was lifted up on high: for if he had understood, he would have laid his hand upon his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 17:14; Proverbs 29:22; Proverbs 28:25"> And he that strongly squeezeth the papa to bring out milk, straineth out butter: and he that violently bloweth his nose, bringeth out blood: and he that provoketh wrath bringeth forth strife.</span>
