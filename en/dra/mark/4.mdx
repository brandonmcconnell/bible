<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 5:1–3; Mark 2:13; Luke 8:4–10"> And again he began to teach by the sea side; and a great multitude was gathered together unto him, so that he went up into a ship, and sat in the sea; and all the multitude was upon the land by the sea side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 13:34–35; Mark 3:23; Mark 4:11"> And he taught them many things in parables, and said unto them in his doctrine:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 2:5; Matthew 13:26; Mark 4:26–29"> Hear ye: Behold, the sower went out to sow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 13:19; Mark 4:15; Matthew 13:4"> And whilst he sowed, some fell by the way side, and the birds of the air came and ate it up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:26; Luke 8:6; Luke 8:13"> And other some fell upon stony ground, where it had not much earth; and it shot up immediately, because it had no depth of earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 1:11; Jeremiah 17:5–8; Jonah 4:8"> And when the sun was risen, it was scorched; and because it had no root, it withered away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:3; Luke 8:14; Genesis 3:17–18"> And some fell among thorns; and the thorns grew up, and choked it, and it yielded no fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 15:5; Colossians 1:6; Mark 4:20"> And some fell upon good ground; and brought forth fruit that grew up, and increased and yielded, one thirty, another sixty, and another a hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 11:15; Mark 4:23–24; Revelation 3:22"> And he said: He that hath ears to hear, let him hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 13:10–17; Mark 4:34; Proverbs 13:20"> And when he was alone, the twelve that were with him asked him the parable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 11:25; Luke 8:10; 1 John 5:20"> And he said to them: To you it is given to know the mystery of the kingdom of God: but to them that are without, all things are done in parables:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; Isaiah 44:18; Matthew 13:14–15"> That seeing they may see, and not perceive; and hearing they may hear, and not understand: lest at any time they should be converted, and their sins should be forgiven them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Matthew 13:51–52; Mark 7:17–18"> And he saith to them: Are you ignorant of this parable? and how shall you know all parables?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 8:4; Matthew 13:19; Matthew 13:37"> He that soweth, soweth the word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Matthew 13:19; 2 Corinthians 2:11"> And these are they by the way side, where the word is sown, and as soon as they have heard, immediately Satan cometh and taketh away the word that was sown in their hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 6:20; Luke 8:13; Matthew 13:20–21"> And these likewise are they that are sown on the stony ground: who when they have heard the word, immediately receive it with joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 11:6; 1 Thessalonians 3:3–5; Matthew 12:31"> And they have no root in themselves, but are only for a time: and then when tribulation and persecution ariseth for the word they are presently scandalized.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 8:14; Jeremiah 4:3; Matthew 13:22"> And others there are who are sown among thorns: these are they that hear the word,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; 1 Timothy 6:9–10; 1 Timothy 6:17"> And the cares of the world, and the deceitfulness of riches, and the lusts after other things entering in choke the word, and it is made fruitless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; Luke 8:15; John 15:4–5"> And these are they who are sown upon the good ground, who hear the word, and receive it, and yield fruit, the one thirty, another sixty, and another a hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 5:15; Luke 11:33; Luke 8:16"> And he said to them: Doth a candle come in to be put under a bushel, or under a bed? and not to be set on a candlestick?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 8:17; Ecclesiastes 12:14; Matthew 10:26–27"> For there is nothing hid, which shall not be made manifest: neither was it made secret, but that it may come abroad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 4:9; Matthew 11:15; Revelation 2:29"> If any man have ears to hear, let him hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 2:1; Luke 8:18; Matthew 7:2"> And he said to them: Take heed what you hear. In what measure you shall mete, it shall be measured to you again, and more shall be given to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 13:12; Luke 8:18; Matthew 25:28–29"> For he that hath, to him shall be given: and he that hath not, that also which he hath shall be taken away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 13:24; 1 Corinthians 3:6–9; Luke 8:11"> And he said: So is the kingdom of God, as if a man should cast seed into the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:5; 2 Thessalonians 1:3; 1 Corinthians 15:37–38"> And should sleep, and rise, night and day, and the seed should spring, and grow up whilst he knoweth not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 4:31–32; Hosea 6:3; Philippians 1:9–11"> For the earth of itself bringeth forth fruit, first the blade, then the ear, afterwards the full corn in the ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joel 3:13; Matthew 13:30; Matthew 13:40–43"> And when the fruit is brought forth, immediately he putteth in the sickle, because the harvest is come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 13:31–32; Luke 13:18–21; Lamentations 2:13"> And he said: To what shall we liken the kingdom of God? or to what parable shall we compare it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 19:20; Luke 13:18–19; Daniel 2:44–45"> It is as a grain of mustard seed: which when it is sown in the earth, is less than all the seeds that are in the earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Daniel 4:20–22; Daniel 4:10–14; Ezekiel 31:3–10"> And when it is sown, it groweth up, and becometh greater than all herbs, and shooteth out great branches, so that the birds of the air may dwell under the shadow thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 13:34–35; John 16:12; Hebrews 5:11–14"> And with many such parables, he spoke to them the word, according as they were able to hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 16:25; Mark 4:10; Matthew 15:15–20"> And without parable he did not speak unto them; but apart, he explained all things to his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 8:18; Luke 8:22; Luke 8:25"> And he saith to them that day, when evening was come: Let us pass over to the other side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 3:9; Mark 4:1; Mark 5:2"> And sending away the multitude, they take him even as he was in the ship: and there were other ships with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 8:23–24; Job 1:19; Luke 8:22–23"> And there arose a great storm of wind, and the waves beat into the ship, so that the ship was filled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Peter 5:7; Hebrews 4:15; Isaiah 54:6–8"> And he was in the hinder part of the ship, sleeping upon a pillow; and they awake him, and say to him: Master, doth it not concern thee that we perish?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 107:29; Psalms 89:9; Psalms 65:7"> And rising up, he rebuked the wind, and said to the sea: Peace, be still. And the wind ceased: and there was made a great calm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 8:25; Matthew 8:26; Matthew 14:31"> And he said to them: Why are you fearful? have you not faith yet? And they feared exceedingly: and they said one to another: Who is this (thinkest thou) that both wind and sea obey him?</span>
