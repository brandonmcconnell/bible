<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 15:46–47; Matthew 27:60–66"> And they said one to another: Who shall roll us back the stone from the door of the sepulchre?</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 20:11–12; Luke 24:3–5; John 20:8"> And entering into the sepulchre, they saw a young man sitting on the right side, clothed with a white robe: and they were astonished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 1:17–18; 1 Corinthians 15:3–7; Luke 24:46"> Who saith to them: Be not affrighted; you seek Jesus of Nazareth, who was crucified: he is risen, he is not here, behold the place where they laid him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 14:28; Matthew 26:32; Matthew 28:10"> But go, tell his disciples and Peter that he goeth before you into Galilee; there you shall see him, as he told you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 24:22–24; Luke 24:9–11; Mark 16:5–6"> But they going out, fled from the sepulchre. For a trembling and fear had seized them: and they said nothing to any man; for they were afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 20:14–19; Luke 8:2; John 20:12"> But he rising early the first day of the week, appeared first to Mary Magdalen, out of whom he had cast seven devils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 20:18; Luke 24:17; John 16:6"> She went and told them that had been with him, who were mourning and weeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 24:11; Mark 16:13–14; Mark 9:19"> And they hearing that he was alive, and had been seen by her, did not believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 24:13–32; John 21:14; John 21:1"> And after that he appeared in another shape to two of them walking, as they were going into the country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 20:25; Luke 24:33–35; John 20:8"> And they going told it to the rest: neither did they believe them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:5; Luke 24:36–43; Hebrews 3:7–8"> At length he appeared to the eleven as they were at table: and he upbraided them with their incredulity and hardness of heart, because they did not believe them who had seen him after he was risen again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 28:19; Mark 13:10; Acts 1:8"> And he said to them: Go ye into the whole world, and preach the gospel to every creature.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 11:25–26; John 3:36; Romans 10:9–14"> He that believeth and is baptized, shall be saved: but he that believeth not shall be condemned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 5:16; 1 Corinthians 12:28; Acts 8:7"> And these signs shall follow them that believe: In my name they shall cast out devils: they shall speak with new tongues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 10:19; James 5:14–15; Acts 28:3–6"> They shall take up serpents; and if they shall drink any deadly thing, it shall not hurt them: they shall lay their hands upon the sick, and they shall recover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Peter 3:22; Hebrews 1:3; Hebrews 12:2"> And the Lord Jesus, after he had spoken to them, was taken up into heaven, and sitteth on the right hand of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:4–5; Acts 14:3; Romans 15:19"> But they going forth preached every where: the Lord working withal, and confirming the word with signs that followed.</span>
