<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 15:1–20; Mark 3:22; Luke 5:17"> And there assembled together unto him the Pharisees and some of the scribes, coming from Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 10:28; Matthew 7:3–5; Romans 14:14"> And when they had seen some of his disciples eat bread with common, that is, with unwashed hands, they found fault.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 1:14; Mark 7:13; Colossians 2:8"> For the Pharisees, and all the Jews eat not without often washing their hands, holding the tradition of the ancients:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 9:10; Matthew 23:25; Luke 11:38–39"> And when they come from the market, unless they be washed, they eat not: and many other things there are that have been delivered to them to observe, the washings of cups and of pots, and of brazen vessels, and of beds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:6; Acts 21:21; Matthew 15:2"> And the Pharisees and scribes asked him: Why do not thy disciples walk according to the tradition of the ancients, but they eat bread with common hands?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Titus 1:16; Matthew 15:7–9"> But he answering, said to them: Well did Isaias prophesy of you hypocrites, as it is written: This people honoureth me with their lips, but their heart is far from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Matthew 15:9; James 1:26"> And in vain to they worship me, teaching doctrines and precepts of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 7:3–4; Isaiah 1:12"> For leaving the commandment of God, you hold the tradition of men, the washing of pots and of cups: and many other things you do like to these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:16–17; Galatians 2:21; Isaiah 29:13"> And he said to them: Well do you make void the commandment of God, that you may keep your own tradition.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 21:17; Exodus 20:12; Deuteronomy 5:16"> For Moses said: Honour thy father and thy mother; and He that shall curse father or mother, dying let him die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 23:18; 1 Timothy 5:4–8; Matthew 15:5"> But you say: If a man shall say to his father or mother, Corban, (which is a gift,) whatsoever is from me, shall profit thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And further you suffer him not to do any thing for his father or mother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 7:9; Titus 1:14; Matthew 5:17–20"> Making void the word of God by your own tradition, which you have given forth. And many other such like things you do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 15:10; Isaiah 6:9; Proverbs 8:5"> And calling again the multitude unto him, he said to them: Hear ye me all, and understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 4:23; Leviticus 11:42–47; Titus 1:15"> There is nothing from without a man that entering into him, can defile him. But the things which come from a man, those are they that defile a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> If any man have ears to hear, let him hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 15:15; Matthew 13:36; Mark 9:28"> And when he was come into the house from the multitude, his disciples asked him the parable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 15:16–17; Hebrews 5:11; Isaiah 28:9–10"> And he saith to them: So are you also without knowledge? understand you not that every thing from without, entering into a man cannot defile him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 15:17; 1 Corinthians 6:13; Colossians 2:21–22"> Because it entereth not into his heart, but goeth into the belly, and goeth out into the privy, purging all meats?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 3:6; James 1:14–15; James 4:1"> But he said that the things which come out from a man, they defile a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:9; Romans 8:7–8; James 4:1–3"> For from within out of the heart of men proceed evil thoughts, adulteries, fornications, murders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 20:15; Matthew 6:23; 1 Peter 2:15"> Thefts, covetousness, wickedness, deceit, lasciviousness, an evil eye, blasphemy, pride, foolishness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 7:15; Titus 1:15; 1 Corinthians 3:17"> All these evil things come from within, and defile a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 15:21–28; Matthew 11:21; Ezekiel 28:2"> And rising from thence he went into the coasts of Tyre and Sidon: and entering into a house, he would that no man should know it, and he could not be hid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Mark 9:17–23; Acts 10:25–26"> For a woman as soon as she heard of him, whose daughter had an unclean spirit, came in and fell down at his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Isaiah 49:12; Galatians 3:28"> For the woman was a Gentile, a Syrophenician born. And she besought him that he would cast forth the devil out of her daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 15:23–28; Matthew 7:6; Acts 22:21"> Who said to her: Suffer first the children to be filled: for it is not good to take the bread of the children, and cast it to the dogs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 10:12; Romans 3:29; Acts 11:17–18"> But she answered and said to him: Yea, Lord; for the whelps also eat under the table of the crumbs of the children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 66:2; Matthew 5:3; 1 John 3:8"> And he said to her: For this saying go thy way, the devil is gone out of thy daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 John 3:8; John 4:50–52"> And when she was come into her house, she found the girl lying upon the bed, and that the devil was gone out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 5:20; Matthew 15:29–31; Matthew 4:25"> And again going out of the coasts of Tyre, he came by Sidon to the sea of Galilee, through the midst of the coasts of Decapolis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 11:14; Mark 5:23; Matthew 9:32–33"> And they bring to him one deaf and dumb; and they besought him that he would lay his hand upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 8:23; John 9:6–7; Mark 5:40"> And taking him from the multitude apart, he put his fingers into his ears, and spitting, he touched his tongue:</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 11:41; Mark 5:41; Mark 6:41"> And looking up to heaven, he groaned, and said to him: Ephpheta, which is, Be thou opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5–6; Psalms 33:9; Mark 2:12"> And immediately his ears were opened, and the string of his tongue was loosed, and he spoke right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Mark 8:26; Mark 3:12"> And he charged them that they should tell no man. But the more he charged them, so much the more a great deal did they publish it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 6:51; Mark 5:42; Exodus 4:10–11"> And so much the more did they wonder, saying: He hath done all things well; he hath made both the deaf to hear, and the dumb to speak.</span>
