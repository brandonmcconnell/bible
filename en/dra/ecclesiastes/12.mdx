<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 5:30; 1 Samuel 4:18; 1 Samuel 4:15"> Before the sun, and the light, and the moon, and the stars be darkened, and the clouds return after the rain:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 90:9–10; Psalms 102:23; 2 Samuel 21:15–17"> When the keepers of the house shall tremble, and the strong men shall stagger, and the grinders shall be idle in a small number, and they that look through the holes shall be darkened:</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 4:2–3"> Before the silver cord be broken, and the golden fillet shrink back, and the pitcher be crushed at the fountain, and the wheel be broken upon the cistern,</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:18; John 21:25; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> More than these, my son, require not. Of making many books there is no end: and much study is an affliction of the flesh.</span>
