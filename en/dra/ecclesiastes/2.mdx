<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 12:19; Ecclesiastes 8:15; James 5:5"> I said in my heart: I will go, and abound with delights, and enjoy good things. And I saw that this also was vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 14:13; Ecclesiastes 7:2–6; 1 Peter 4:2–4"> Laughter I counted error: and to mirth I said: Why art thou vainly deceived?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Ecclesiastes 1:17; Proverbs 31:4–5"> I thought in my heart, to withdraw my flesh from wine, that I might turn my mind to wisdom, and might avoid folly, till I might see what was profitable for the children of men: and what they ought to do under the sun, all the days of their life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 7:1–12; 2 Chronicles 8:11; 2 Chronicles 26:10"> I made me great works, I built me houses, and planted vineyards,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 17:27–29; Song of Solomon 4:12–1; Jeremiah 39:4"> I made gardens, and orchards, and set them with trees of all kinds,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:14; Psalms 1:3; Jeremiah 17:8"> And I made me ponds of water, to water therewith the wood of the young trees,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 3:4; Genesis 15:3; 1 Kings 4:23"> I got me menservants, and maidservants, and had a great family: and herds of oxen, and great flocks of sheep, above all that were before me in Jerusalem:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 10:10; 1 Kings 9:28; 2 Samuel 19:35"> I heaped together for myself silver and gold, and the wealth of kings, and provinces: I made me singing men, and singing women, and the delights of the sons of men, cups and vessels to serve to pour out wine:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:25; Ecclesiastes 1:16; 1 Kings 10:23"> And I surpassed in riches all that were before me in Jerusalem: my wisdom also remained with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:9; Ecclesiastes 5:18; Ecclesiastes 3:22"> And whatsoever my eyes desired, I refused them not: and I withheld not my heart from enjoying every pleasure, and delighting itself in the things which I had prepared: and esteemed this my portion, to make use of my own labour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:14; 1 John 2:16–17; Ecclesiastes 1:3"> And when I turned myself to all the works which my hands had wrought, and to the labours wherein I had laboured in vain, I saw in all things vanity, and vexation of mind, and that nothing was lasting under the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:17; Ecclesiastes 7:25; Ecclesiastes 1:9–10"> I passed further to behold wisdom, and errors and folly, (What is man, said I, that he can follow the King his maker?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Ecclesiastes 7:11–12; Matthew 6:23"> And I saw that wisdom excelled folly, as much as light differeth from darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 49:10; Proverbs 17:24; Ecclesiastes 9:11"> The eyes of a wise man are in his head: the fool walketh in darkness: and I learned that they were to die both alike.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 6:8; Ecclesiastes 1:14; Ecclesiastes 1:2"> And I said in my heart: If the death of the fool and mine shall be one, what doth it avail me, that I have applied myself more to the study of wisdom? And speaking with my own mind, I perceived that this also was vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:11; Ecclesiastes 9:5; Psalms 88:12"> For there shall be no remembrance of the wise no more than of the fool for ever, and the times to come shall cover all things together with oblivion: the learned dieth in like manner as the unlearned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:11; Jonah 4:8; Ecclesiastes 4:2"> And therefore I was weary of my life, when I saw that all things under the sun are evil, and all vanity and vexation of spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 39:6; Psalms 49:10; Luke 16:27–28"> Again I hated all my application wherewith I had earnestly laboured under the sun, being like to have an heir after me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="James 3:17; Ecclesiastes 3:22; Ecclesiastes 9:13"> Whom I know not whether he will be a wise man or a fool, and he shall have rule over all my labours with which I have laboured and been solicitous: and is there any thing so vain?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 43:14; Job 17:11–15; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> Wherefore I left off and my heart renounced labouring any more under the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:17–18; 2 Chronicles 31:20–21; 2 Chronicles 34:2"> For when a man laboureth in wisdom, and knowledge, and carefulness, he leaveth what he hath gotten to an idle man: so this also is vanity, and a great evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 127:2; Ecclesiastes 1:3; Philippians 4:6"> For what profit shall a man have of all his labour, and vexation of spirit, with which he bath been tormented under the sun?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 14:1; Job 5:7; Psalms 6:6–7"> All his days axe full of sorrows and miseries, even in the night he doth not rest in mind: and is not this vanity?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:22; Ecclesiastes 8:15; 1 Timothy 6:17"> Is it not better to eat and drink, and to shew his soul good things of his labours? and this is from the hand of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:1–12; 1 Kings 4:21–24"> Who shall so feast and abound with delights as I?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 13:22; Job 27:16–17; Proverbs 28:8"> God hath given to a man that is good in his sight, wisdom, and knowledge, and joy: but to the sinner he hath given vexation, and superfluous care, to heap up and to gather together, and to give it to him that hath pleased God: but this also is vanity, and a fruitless solicitude of the mind.</span>
