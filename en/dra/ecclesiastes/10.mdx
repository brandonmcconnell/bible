<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 5:13–16; Galatians 2:12–14; Nehemiah 13:26"> Dying flies spoil the sweetness of the ointment. Wisdom and glory is more precious than a small and shortlived folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 12:18–20; Ecclesiastes 10:14; Ecclesiastes 10:10"> The heart of a wise man is in his right hand, and the heart of a fool is in his left hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 18:2; Proverbs 13:16; Proverbs 18:6"> Yea, and the fool when he walketh in the way, whereas be himself is a fool, esteemeth all men fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 25:15; Ecclesiastes 8:3; 1 Samuel 25:24–44"> If the spirit of him that hath power, ascend upon thee, leave not thy place: because care will make the greatest sins to cease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:13; Ecclesiastes 4:7; Ecclesiastes 9:3"> There is an evil that I have seen under the sun, as it were by an error proceeding from the face of the prince:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 28:12; Proverbs 29:2; Esther 3:1"> A fool set in high dignity, and the rich sitting beneath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 19:10; Esther 6:8; Proverbs 30:22"> I have seen servants upon horses: and princes walking on the ground as servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 26:27; Psalms 7:15–16; Esther 7:10"> He that diggeth a pit, shall fall into it: and he that breaketh a hedge, a serpent shall bite him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> He that removeth stones, shall be hurt by them: and he that cutteth trees, shall be wounded by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 1:5; Colossians 4:5; Ecclesiastes 10:15"> If the iron be blunt, and be not as before, but be made blunt, with much labour it shall be sharpened: and after industry shall follow wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:17; Psalms 58:4–5; Psalms 64:3"> If a serpent bite in silence, he is nothing better that backbiteth secretly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Luke 4:22; Proverbs 22:11"> The words of the mouth of a wise man are grace: but the lips of a fool shall throw him down headlong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:10–11; Proverbs 29:9; Acts 6:9–11"> The beginning of his words is folly, and the end of his talk is a mischievous error.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 15:2; Ecclesiastes 6:12; Ecclesiastes 5:3"> A fool multiplieth words. A man cannot tell what hath been before him: and what shall be after him, who can tell him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:4–5; Isaiah 57:1; Isaiah 35:8–10"> The labour of fools shall afflict them that know not bow to go to the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 3:12; Isaiah 5:11–12; Isaiah 3:4–5"> Woe to thee, O land, when thy king is a child, and when the princes eat in the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 31:4–5; Proverbs 28:2–3; Isaiah 5:11"> Blessed is the land, whose king is noble, and whose princes eat in due season for refreshment, and not for riotousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 14:1; Proverbs 24:30–34; Proverbs 20:4"> By slothfulness a building shall be brought down, and through the weakness of hands, the house shall drop through.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 104:15; Ecclesiastes 2:1–2; 1 Peter 4:3"> For laughter they make bread, and wine that the living may feast: and all things obey money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 12:2–3; Acts 23:5; Ecclesiastes 7:21–22"> Detract not the king, no not in thy thought; and speak not evil of the rich man in thy private chamber: because even the birds of the air will carry thy voice, and he that hath wings will tell what thou hast said.</span>
