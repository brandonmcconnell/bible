<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Luke 1:74–75; 1 Kings 5:4"> And it came to pass when the king sat in his house, and the Lord had given him rest on every side from all his enemies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:1; 2 Samuel 5:11; Haggai 1:4"> He said to Nathan the prophet: Dost thou see that I dwell in a house of cedar, and the ark of God is lodged within skins?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:7; 1 Kings 8:17–18; 2 Kings 4:27"> And Nathan said to the king: Go, do all that is in thy heart: because the Lord is with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:3; Numbers 12:6; Amos 3:7"> But it came to pass that night, that the word of the Lord came to Nathan, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:3–32; 1 Kings 5:3–4; 1 Kings 8:16–19"> Go, and say to my servant David: Thus saith the Lord: Shalt thou build me a house to dwell in?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 8:16; Joshua 18:1; Exodus 40:18–19"> Whereas I have not dwelt in a house from the day that I brought the children of Israel out of the land of Egypt even to this day: but have walked in a tabernacle, and in a tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:6; 2 Samuel 5:2; Leviticus 26:11–12"> In all the places that I have gone through with all the children of Israel, did ever I speak a word to any one of the tribes of Israel, whom I commanded to feed my people Israel, saying: Why have you not built me a house of cedar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 78:70–71; 2 Samuel 6:21; 2 Samuel 12:7"> And now thus shalt thou speak to my servant David: Thus saith the Lord of hosts: a I took thee out of the pastures from following the sheep to be ruler over my people Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:10; 1 Chronicles 17:8; 1 Samuel 18:14"> And I have been with thee wheresoever thou hast walked, and have slain all thy enemies from before thy face: and I have made thee a great man, like unto the name of the great ones that are on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 9:15; 1 Chronicles 17:9; Jeremiah 24:6"> And I will appoint a place for my people Israel, and I will plant them, and they shall dwell therein, and shall be disturbed no more: neither shall the children of iniquity afflict them any more as they did before,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:27; 2 Samuel 7:1; 1 Chronicles 17:10"> From the day that I appointed judges over my people Israel: and I will give thee rest from all thy enemies. And the Lord foretelleth to thee, that the Lord will make thee a house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 8:20; Acts 2:30; Acts 13:36"> And when thy days shall be fulfilled, and thou shalt sleep with thy fathers, I will raise up thy seed after thee, which shall proceed out of thy bowels, and I will establish his kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 1:31–33; 1 Kings 8:19; 2 Samuel 7:16"> He shall build a house to my name, and I will establish the throne of his kingdom for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 1:5; Revelation 3:19; 1 Chronicles 17:13"> I will be to him a father, and he shall be to me a son: and if he commit any iniquity, I will correct him with the rod of men, and with the stripes of the children of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:23; 1 Samuel 15:28; Psalms 89:28"> But my mercy I will not take away from him, as I took it from Saul, whom I removed from before my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 1:32–33; Psalms 89:36–37; 2 Samuel 7:13"> And thy house shall be faithful, and thy kingdom for ever before thy face, and thy throne shall be firm for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:15; Acts 20:27; 1 Corinthians 15:3"> According to all these words and according to all this vision, so did Nathan speak to David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 8:4; 1 Chronicles 17:16; 1 Samuel 18:18"> And David went in, and sat before the Lord, and said: Who am I, O Lord God, and what is my house, that thou hast brought me thus far?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 55:8–9; 1 Chronicles 17:17; Psalms 36:7"> But yet this hath seemed little in thy sight, O Lord God, unless thou didst also speak of the house of thy servant for a long time to come: for this is the law of Adam, O Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 21:17; 1 Samuel 16:7; Psalms 139:1"> And what can David say more unto thee? for thou knowest thy servant, O Lord God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 115:1; Psalms 138:2; Ephesians 1:9"> For thy word’s sake, and according to thy own heart thou hast done all these great things, so that thou wouldst make it known to thy servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 86:10; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 86:8"> Therefore thou art magnified, O Lord God, because there is none like to thee, neither is there any God besides thee, in all the things that we have heard with our ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:26; Deuteronomy 10:21; Deuteronomy 15:15"> And what nation is there upon earth, as thy people Israel, whom God went to redeem for a people to himself, and to make him a name, and to do for them great and terrible things, upon the earth, before the face of thy people, whom thou redeemedst to thyself out of Egypt, from the nations and their gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:18; 1 Peter 2:10; Genesis 17:7"> For thou hast confirmed to thyself thy people Israel to be an everlasting people: and thou, O Lord God, art become their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 119:49; Ezekiel 36:37; Genesis 32:12"> And now, O Lord God, raise up for ever the word that thou hast spoken, concerning thy servant and concerning his house: and do as thou hast spoken,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:23–24; Psalms 72:18–19; Matthew 6:9"> That thy name may be magnified for ever, and it may be said: The Lord of hosts is God over Israel. And the house of thy servant David shall be established before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:25–26; Psalms 10:17; Ruth 4:4"> Because thou, O Lord of hosts, God of Israel, hast revealed to the ear of thy servant, saying: I will build thee a house: therefore hath thy servant found in his heart to pray this prayer to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 17:17; Exodus 34:6; Numbers 23:19"> And now, O Lord God, thou art God, and thy words shall be true: for thou hast spoken to thy servant these good things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 6:24–26; 1 Chronicles 17:27; Psalms 115:12–15"> And now begin, and bless the house of thy servant, that it may endure for ever before thee: because thou, O Lord God, hast spoken it, and with thy blessing let the house of thy servant be blessed for ever.</span>
