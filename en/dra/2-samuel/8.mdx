<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:47; 2 Samuel 10:16; 2 Samuel 10:19"> David defeated also Adarezer the son of Rohob king of Soba, when he went to extend his dominion over the river Euphrates.</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 11:23–25; Isaiah 7:8; Job 9:13"> And the Syrians of Damascus came to succour Adarezer the king of Soba: and David slew of the Syrians two and twenty thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:14; 2 Samuel 7:9; Psalms 18:34–46"> And David put garrisons in Syria of Damascus: and Syria served David under tribute: and the Lord preserved David in all his enterprises, whithersoever he went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:15–16; 1 Kings 14:26–27; 1 Kings 10:16–17"> And David took the arms of gold, which the servants of Adarezer wore, and brought them to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 18:8; 1 Chronicles 22:16; Ezekiel 47:16"> And out of Bete, and out of Beroth, cities of Adarezer, king David took an exceeding great quantity of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 8:65; 2 Chronicles 8:4; 1 Chronicles 18:9"> And Thou the king of Emath heard that David had defeated all the forces of Adarezer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:10; 1 Chronicles 18:10; Psalms 129:8"> And Thou sent Joram his son to king David, to salute him, and to congratulate with him, and to return him thanks: because he had fought against Adarezer, and had defeated him. For Thou was an enemy to Adarezer, and in his hand were vessels of gold, and vessels of silver, and vessels of brass:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 7:51; 1 Chronicles 29:2; 1 Chronicles 22:14–16"> And king David dedicated them to the Lord, together with the silver and gold that he had dedicated of all the nations, which he had subdued:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:14; 2 Samuel 8:2; 1 Chronicles 18:11"> Of Syria, and of Moab, and of the children of Ammon, and of the Philistines, and of Amalec, and of the spoils of Adarezer the son of Rohob king of Soba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 14:7; 2 Samuel 7:9; 1 Chronicles 18:12"> David also made himself a name, when he returned after taking Syria in the valley of the saltpits, killing eighteen thousand:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 27:29; Genesis 25:23; 2 Samuel 8:6"> And he put guards in Edom, and placed there a garrison: and all Edom was made to serve David: and the Lord preserved David in all enterprises he went about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Amos 5:15; Psalms 89:14; Psalms 72:2"> And David reigned over all Israel: and David did judgment and justice to all his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 4:3; 1 Chronicles 11:6; 2 Samuel 19:13"> And Joab the son of Sarvia was over the army: and Josaphat the son of Ahilud was recorder:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:8; 1 Chronicles 18:16; 1 Chronicles 24:3–4"> And Sadoc the son of Achitob, and Achimelech the son of Abiathar, were the priests: and Saraias was the scribe:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:14; 2 Samuel 20:7; 2 Samuel 20:23"> And Banaias the son of Joiada was over the Cerethi and Phelethi: and the sons of David were the princes.</span>
