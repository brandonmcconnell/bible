<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:1–3; Judges 11:12–28; Judges 10:7–9"> And it came to pass after this, that the king of the children of Ammon died, and Hanon his son reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:1; Deuteronomy 23:3–6; Nehemiah 13:1–3"> And David said: I Will shew kindness to Hanon the son of Daas, as his father shewed kindness to me. So David sent his servants to comfort him for the death of his father. But when the servants of David were come into the land of the children of Ammon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 42:16; Genesis 42:9; 1 Corinthians 13:5"> The princes of the children of Ammon said to Hanon their lord: Thinkest thou that for the honour of thy father, David hath sent comforters to thee, and hath not David rather sent his servants to thee to search, and spy into the city, and overthrow it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4; Jeremiah 41:5; 1 Chronicles 19:3–4"> Wherefore Hanon took the servants of David, and shaved off the one half of their beards, and cut away half of their garments even to the buttocks, and sent them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 19:5; 1 Kings 16:34; Joshua 6:24–26"> When this was told David, he sent to meet them: for the men were sadly put to confusion, and David commanded them, saying: Stay at Jericho, till your beards be grown, and then return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:3; 2 Samuel 8:5; Genesis 34:30"> And the children of Ammon seeing that they had done an injury to David, Bent and hired the Syrians of Rohob, and the Syrians of Soba, twenty thousand footmen, and of the king of Maacha a thousand men, and of Istob twelve thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:8–39; 1 Chronicles 19:8–19"> And when David heard this, he sent Joab and the whole army of warriors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:6; Joshua 19:28; Numbers 13:21"> And the children of Ammon came out, and set their men in array at the entering in of the gate: but the Syrians of Soba, and of Rohob, and of Istob, and of Maacha were by themselves in the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 20:42–43; Joshua 8:21–22"> Then Joab seeing that the battle was prepared against him, both before and behind, chose of all the choice men of Israel, and put them in array against the Syrians:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And the rest of the people he delivered to Abisai his brother, who set them in array against the children of Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 6:2; Nehemiah 4:20; Philippians 1:27–28"> And Joab said: If the Syrians are too strong for me, then thou shalt help me: but if the children of Ammon are too strong for thee, then I will help thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:13; Deuteronomy 31:6; 1 Samuel 3:18"> Be of good courage, and let us fight for our people, and for the city of our God: and the Lord will do what is good in his sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 20:13–21; 1 Kings 20:28–30; 1 Chronicles 19:14–15"> And Joab and the people that were with him, began to fight against the Syrians: and they immediately fled before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And the children of Ammon seeing that the Syrians were fled, they fled also before Abisai, and entered into the city: and Joab returned from the children of Ammon, and came to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 14:2–3; Micah 4:11–12; Isaiah 8:9–10"> Then the Syrians seeing that they had fallen before Israel, gathered themselves together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 19:16; 2 Samuel 8:3–8; 1 Chronicles 18:3"> And Adarezer sent and fetched the Syrians, that were beyond the river, and brought over their army: and Sobach, the captain of the host of Adarezer, was their general.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 19:17"> And when this was told David, he gathered all Israel together, and passed over the Jordan, and came to Helam: and the Syrians set themselves in array against David, and fought against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 19:18; 2 Samuel 8:4; Psalms 46:11"> And the Syrians fled before Israel, and David slew of the Syrians the men of seven hundred chariots, and forty thousand horsemen: and smote Sobach the captain of the army, who presently died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:6; Revelation 18:10; Psalms 48:4–5"> And all the kings that were auxiliaries of Adarezer, seeing themselves overcome by Israel, were afraid and fled away, eight and fifty thousand men before Israel. And they made peace with Israel: and served them, and all the Syrians were afraid to help the children of Ammon any more.</span>
