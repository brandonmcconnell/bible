<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 15:1; Judges 5:1; Isaiah 12:1–6"> And David spoke to the Lord the words of this canticle, in the day that the Lord delivered him out of the hand of all his enemies, and out of the hand of Saul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 18:2–50; Psalms 31:3; Psalms 71:3"> And he said: The Lord is my rock, and my strength, and my saviour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Isaiah 12:2; Proverbs 18:10"> God is my strong one, in him will I trust: my shield, and the horn of my salvation: he lifteth me up, and is my refuge: my saviour, thou wilt deliver me from iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 18:3; Psalms 48:1; Psalms 96:4"> I will call on the Lord who is worthy to be praised: and I shall be saved from my enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jonah 2:3; Psalms 69:14–15; Revelation 17:15"> For the pangs of death have surrounded me: the floods of Belial have made me afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 116:3; Proverbs 13:14; Psalms 140:5"> The cords of hell compassed me: the snares of death prevented me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 18:6; Psalms 116:4; Exodus 3:7"> In my distress I will call upon the Lord, and I will cry to my God: and he will hear my voice out of his temple, and my cry shall come to his ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 26:11; Psalms 97:4; Judges 5:4"> The earth shook and trembled, the foundations of the mountains were moved, and shaken, because he was angry with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Psalms 18:15; Psalms 18:8"> A smoke went up from his nostrils, and a devouring fire out of his mouth: coals were kindled by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 8:12; Psalms 97:2; Psalms 144:5"> He bowed the heavens, and came down: and darkness was under his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 104:3; Psalms 18:10; 1 Samuel 4:4"> And he rode upon the cherubims, and flew: and slid upon the wings of the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; 2 Samuel 22:10; Job 36:29"> He made darkness a covering round about him: dropping waters out of the clouds of the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:9"> By the brightness before him, the coals of fire were kindled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 29:3–9; 1 Samuel 2:10; Job 37:2–5"> The Lord shall thunder from heaven: and the most high shall give forth his voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:23; Joshua 10:10; Habakkuk 3:11"> He shot arrows and scattered them: lightning, and consumed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nahum 1:4; Habakkuk 3:8–10; Psalms 114:3–7"> And the overflowings of the sea appeared, and the foundations of the world were laid open at the rebuke of the Lord, at the blast of the spirit of his wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Psalms 144:7; Psalms 18:16"> He sent from on high, and took me, and drew me out of many waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 3:7; 2 Timothy 4:17; Psalms 56:9"> He delivered me from my most mighty enemy, and from them that hated me: for they were too strong for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 71:20–21; 1 Samuel 23:26–27; Psalms 118:10–13"> He prevented me in the day of my affliction, and the Lord became my stay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 31:8; Psalms 118:5; 2 Samuel 15:26"> And he brought me forth into a large place, he delivered me, because I pleased him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 24:4; 1 Samuel 26:23; Psalms 7:8"> The Lord will reward me according to my justice: and according to the cleanness of my hands he will render to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 8:32; Genesis 18:19; Psalms 128:1"> Because I have kept the ways of the Lord, and have not wickedly departed from my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 119:30; Psalms 119:102; John 15:14"> For all his judgments are in my sight: and his precepts I have not removed from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 6:9; Job 1:1; Genesis 17:1"> And I shall be perfect with him: and shall keep myself from my iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:21; 2 Corinthians 5:10; Romans 2:7–8"> And the Lord will recompense me according to my justice: and according to the cleanness of my hands in the sight of his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; James 2:13"> With the holy one thou wilt be holy: and with the valiant perfect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 5:8; Psalms 18:26; Leviticus 26:23–28"> With the elect thou wilt be elect: and with the perverse thou wilt be perverted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 72:12–13; Isaiah 5:15; James 4:6–7"> And the poor people thou wilt save: and with thy eyes thou wilt humble the haughty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 27:1; John 8:12; Job 29:3"> For thou art my lamp, O Lord: and thou, O Lord, wilt enlighten my darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 18:29; Philippians 4:13; Psalms 118:10–12"> For in thee I will run girded: in my God I will leap over the wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Proverbs 30:5; Psalms 18:30"> God, his way is immaculate, the word of the Lord is tried by fire: he is the shield of all that trust in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:2; Deuteronomy 32:31; Jeremiah 10:6–7"> Who is God but the Lord: and who is strong but our God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ephesians 6:10; Isaiah 41:10; Philippians 4:13"> God who hath girded me with strength, and made my way perfect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13; Isaiah 58:14; Habakkuk 3:19"> Making my feet like the feet of harts, and setting me upon my high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 144:1; Psalms 18:33–34; Ezekiel 39:9–10"> He teacheth my bands to war: and maketh my arms like a bow of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ephesians 6:16; Psalms 115:14; Psalms 84:11"> Thou hast given me the shield of my salvation: and thy mildness hath multiplied me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Proverbs 4:12; Psalms 17:5; 1 Samuel 2:9"> Thou shalt enlarge my steps under me: and my ankles shall not fail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Psalms 21:8–9; 2 Samuel 5:18–25; 2 Samuel 10:14"> I will pursue after my enemies, and crush them: and will not return again till I consume them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Malachi 4:3; Malachi 4:1; Psalms 110:1"> I will consume them and break them in pieces, so that they shall not rise: they shall fall under my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Psalms 44:5; Isaiah 60:14; Colossians 1:11"> Thou hast girded me with strength to battle: thou hast made them that resisted me to bow under me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 23:27; Joshua 10:24; 2 Thessalonians 1:8–9"> My enemies thou hast made to turn their back to me: them that hated me, and I shall destroy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Isaiah 1:15; 1 Samuel 28:6; Micah 3:4"> They shall cry, and there shall be none to save: to the Lord, and he shall not hear them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Micah 7:10; Isaiah 10:6; 2 Kings 13:7"> I shall beat them as small as the dust of the earth: I shall crush them and spread them abroad like the mire of the streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:1–14; Isaiah 55:5; 2 Samuel 3:1"> Thou wilt save me from the contradictions of my people: thou wilt keep me to be the head of the Gentiles: the people which I know not, shall serve me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Psalms 81:15; Psalms 66:3; Isaiah 56:3"> The sons of the stranger will resist me, at the hearing of the ear they will obey me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Micah 7:17; James 1:11; Isaiah 64:6"> The strangers are melted away, and shall be straitened in their distresses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 89:26; 2 Samuel 22:3; Job 19:25"> The Lord liveth, and my God is blessed: and the strong God of my salvation shall be exalted:</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 94:1; Psalms 144:2; 2 Samuel 18:31"> God who giveth me revenge, and bringest down people under me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Psalms 140:1; 2 Samuel 7:8–9; Numbers 24:7"> Who bringest me forth from my enemies, and liftest me up from them that resist me: from the wicked man thou shalt deliver me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Romans 15:9; Psalms 146:1–2; Psalms 145:1–2"> Therefore will I give thanks to thee. O Lord, among the Gentiles, and will sing to thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Psalms 89:29; Psalms 18:50; Psalms 89:20"> Giving great salvation to his king, and shewing mercy to David his anointed, and to his seed for ever.</span>
