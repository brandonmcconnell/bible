<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 6:2; 1 Samuel 1:28; Judges 11:30–31"> Speak to the children of Israel, and thou shalt say to them: The man that shall have made a vow, and promised his soul to God, shall give the price according to estimation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Leviticus 27:25; Numbers 18:16"> If it be a man from twenty years old unto sixty years old, he shall give fifty sides of silver, after the weight of the sanctuary:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 27:9–10; Zechariah 11:12–13; Matthew 26:15"> If a woman, thirty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> But from the fifth year until the twentieth, a man shall give twenty sicles: a woman ten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 3:40–43; Numbers 18:14–16"> From one month until the fifth year. for a male shall be given five sides: for a female three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 90:10"> A man that is sixty years old or upward, shall give fifteen aisles: a woman ten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 14:7; Leviticus 14:21–22; Luke 21:1–4"> If he be poor, and not able to pay tile estimation, he shall stand before tile priest: and as much as he shall value him at, and see him able to pay, so much shall he give.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> But a beast that may be sacrificed to the Lord, if ally one shall vow, shall be holy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 27:15–33; James 1:8"> And cannot be changed, that is to say, neither a better for a worse, nor a worse for a better. And if he shall change it: both that which was changed, and that for which it was changed, shall be consecrated to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:18; Malachi 1:14"> An unclean beast, which cannot be sacrificed to the Lord, if my man shall vow, shall be brought before the priest:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Who judging whether it be good or bad, shall set the price:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 27:19; Leviticus 27:15; Leviticus 22:14"> Which if he that offereth it will give, he shall add above the estimation the fifth part.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 18:14; Leviticus 25:29–31; Leviticus 27:21"> If a man shall vow his house, and sanctify it to the Lord, the priest shall consider it, whether it be good or bad, and it shall be sold according to the price, which he shall appoint.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 27:13"> But if he that vowed, will redeem it, he shall give the fifth part of the estimation over and above, and shall have the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:11–14; Hosea 3:2; Acts 4:34–37"> And if he vow the field of his possession, and consecrate it to the Lord, the price shall be rated according to the measure of the seed. If the ground be sowed with thirty bushels of barley, let it be sold for fifty sides of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> If he vow his field immediately from the year of jubilee that is beginning, as much as it may be worth, at so much it shall be rated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 25:15–16; Leviticus 25:51–52; Leviticus 25:27"> But if some time after, the priest shall reckon the money according to the number of years that remain until the jubilee, and the price shall be abated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 27:13"> And if he that had vowed, will redeem his field, he shall add the fifth part of the money of the estimation, and shall possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And if he will not redeem it, but it be sold to any other man, he that vowed it, may not redeem it any more:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:29; Numbers 18:14; Leviticus 25:31"> For when the day of jubilee cometh, it shall be sanctified to the Lord, and as a possession consecrated, pertaineth to the right of the priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 25:10; Leviticus 25:25"> If a field that was bought, and not of a man’s ancestors’ possession, be sanctified to the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 27:18; Leviticus 27:12"> The priest shall reckon the price according to the number of years: unto the jubilee: and he that had vowed, shall give that to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 25:28; Leviticus 27:20"> But in the jubilee, it shall return to the former owner, who had sold it, and had it in the lot of his possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Numbers 18:16; Numbers 3:47"> All estimation shall be made according to the side of the sanctuary. A sicle hath twenty obols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Exodus 13:12–13; Numbers 18:17"> The firstborn, which belong to the Lord, no man may sanctify and vow: whether it be bullock, or sheep, they are the Lord’s.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 27:11"> And if it be an unclean beast, he that offereth it shall redeem it, according to thy estimation, and shall add the fifth part of the price. If he will not redeem it, it shall be sold to another for how much soever it was estimated by thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 6:17–19; Leviticus 27:21; Exodus 22:20"> Any thing that is devoted to the Lord, whether it be man, or beast, or field, shall not be sold, neither may it be redeemed. Whatsoever is once consecrated shall be holy of holies to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:18–23; Numbers 21:2–3"> And any consecration that is offered by man, shall not be redeemed, but dying shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 23:23; Nehemiah 13:12; Genesis 28:22"> All tithes of the land, whether of corn, or of the fruits of trees, are the Lord’s, and are sanctified to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And if any man will redeem his tithes, he shall add the fifth part of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:13; Ezekiel 20:37; Micah 7:14"> Of all the tithes of oxen, and sheep, and goats, that pass under the shepherd’s rod, every tenth that cometh shall be sanctified to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 27:10"> It shall not be chosen neither good nor bad, neither shall it be changed for another. If any man change it: both that which was changed, and that for which it was changed, shall be sanctified to the Lord, and shall not be redeemed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Leviticus 26:46; Numbers 1:1; Hebrews 12:18–25"> These are the precepts which the Lord commanded Moses for the children of Israel in mount Sinai.</span>
