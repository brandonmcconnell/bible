<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 17:14; Luke 5:12–14; Mark 1:40–44"> This is the rite of a leper, when he is to be cleansed: he shall be brought to the priest:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 13:46; Luke 7:22; Luke 17:15–19"> Who going out of the camp when he shall And that the leprosy is cleansed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 19:6; Leviticus 14:6; Hebrews 9:19"> Shall command him that is to be purified, to offer for himself two living sparrows, which it is lawful to eat, and cedar wood, and scarlet, and hyssop.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 14:50; Hebrews 2:14; 2 Corinthians 5:1"> And he shall command one of the sparrows to be immolated in an earthen vessel over living waters:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 1:5; Zechariah 13:1; Philippians 2:9–11"> But the other that is alive he shall dip, with the cedar wood, and the scarlet and the hyssop, in the blood of the sparrow that is immolated:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 5:14; Ezekiel 36:25; Isaiah 52:15"> Wherewith he shall sprinkle him that is to be cleansed seven times, that he may be rightly purified: and he shall let go the living sparrow, that it may fly into the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 11:25; Numbers 8:7; 1 Peter 3:21"> And when the man hath washed his clothes, he shall shave all the hair of his body, and shall be washed with water: and being purified, he shall enter into the camp, yet so that he tarry without his own tent seven days:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 6:9; Leviticus 14:8; Numbers 8:7"> And on the seventh day he shall shave the hair of his head, and his beard and his eyebrows, and the hair of all his body. And having washed again his clothes, and his body,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 2:1; Matthew 8:4; Luke 5:14"> On the eighth day he shall take two lambs without blemish, and an ewe of a year old without blemish, and three tenths of flour tempered with oil for a sacrifice, and a sextary of oil apart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 5:26–27; Exodus 29:1–4; Numbers 8:21"> And when the priest that purifieth the man, hath presented him, and all these things before the Lord, at the door of the tabernacle of the testimony,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 29:24; Leviticus 6:6–7; Leviticus 8:27–29"> He shall take a. lamb, and offer it for a trespass offering with the sextary of oil: and having offered all before the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 29:11; Leviticus 1:11; Leviticus 4:24"> He shall immolate the lamb, where the victim for sin is wont to be immolated, and the holocaust, that is, in the holy place: for as that which is for sin, so also the victim for a trespass offering pertaineth to the priest: it is holy of holies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 29:20; Leviticus 8:23–24; 2 Corinthians 7:1"> And the priest taking of the blood of the victim that was immolated for trespass, shall put it upon the tip of the right ear of him that is cleansed, and upon the thumb of his right hand and the great toe of his right foot:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 45:7; John 3:34; 1 John 2:20"> And he shall pour of the sextary of oil into his own left. hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 4:17; 1 Corinthians 10:31; Leviticus 4:6"> And shall dip his right finger in it, and sprinkle it before the Lord seven times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 1:2; Ezekiel 36:27; Leviticus 14:14"> And the rest of the oil in his left band, he shall pour upon the tip of the right ear of him that is cleansed, and upon the thumb of his right hand and the great toe of his right foot, and upon the blood that was shed for trespass,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 4:26; 2 Corinthians 1:21–22; Ephesians 1:17–18"> And upon his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 14:12; Leviticus 5:1; Leviticus 12:6–8"> And he shall pray for him before the Lord, and shall offer the sacrifice for sin: then shall he immolate the holocaust,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 14:10; Ephesians 5:2; Leviticus 14:8"> And put it on the altar with the libations thereof, and the man shall be rightly cleansed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 5:7; Leviticus 12:8; Proverbs 17:5"> But if he be poor, and his hand cannot find the things aforesaid: he shall take a lamb for an offering for trespass, that the priest may pray for him, and a tenth part of hour tempered with oil for a sacrifice, and a sextary of oil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:16; Isaiah 59:11; Jeremiah 48:28"> And two turtles or two young pigeons, of which one may be for sin, and the other for a holocaust:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 14:10–11"> And he shall offer them on the eighth day of his purification to the priest, at the door of the tabernacle of the testimony before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 14:12; Leviticus 14:10"> And the priest receiving the lamb for trespass, and the sextary of oil, shall elevate them together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:1; Psalms 40:6; Leviticus 14:14–20"> And the lamb being immolated, he shall put of the blood thereof upon the tip of the right ear of him that is cleansed, and upon the thumb of his right hand, and the great toe of his right foot:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> But he shall pour part of the oil into his own left hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And dipping the finger of his right hand in it, he shall sprinkle it seven times before the Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And he shall touch the tip of the right ear of him that is cleansed, and the thumb of his right hand and the great toe of his right foot, in the place of the blood that was shed for trespass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 14:18; 1 John 2:1–2; Exodus 30:15–16"> And the other part of the oil that is in his left hand, he shall pour upon the head of the purified person, that he may appease the Lord for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 14:22; Romans 8:3; Leviticus 12:8"> And he shall offer a turtle, or young pigeon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 14:18; Leviticus 5:7"> One for trespass, and the other for a holocaust, with their libations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 14:10; Leviticus 14:21; Matthew 11:5"> This is the sacrifice of a leper, that is not able to have all things that appertain to his cleansing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:49; Genesis 17:8; Numbers 32:22"> When you shall be come into the land of Chanaan, which I will give you for a possession, if there be the plague of leprosy in a house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 91:10; Deuteronomy 7:26; Zechariah 5:4"> He whose house it is, shall go and tell the priest, saying: It seemeth to me, that there is the plague of leprosy in my house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Revelation 18:4; Hebrews 12:15; 2 Timothy 2:17–18"> And he shall command, that they carry forth all things out of the house, before he go into it, and see whether it have the leprosy, lest all things become unclean that are in the house. And afterwards he shall go in to view the leprosy of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Leviticus 13:42; Leviticus 13:3; Leviticus 13:19–20"> And if he see in the walls thereof as it were little dints, disfigured with paleness or redness, and lower than all the rest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Leviticus 13:50"> He shall go out of the door of the house, and forthwith shut it up seven days,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Leviticus 13:36; Leviticus 13:27; Leviticus 13:22"> And returning on the seventh day, he shall look upon it. If he find that the leprosy is spread,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Revelation 2:20; Titus 3:10; 1 Corinthians 5:5–6"> He shall command, that the stones wherein the leprosy is, be taken out, and cast without the city into an unclean place:</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 65:4; Revelation 22:15; Matthew 24:51"> And that the house be scraped on the inside round about, and the dust of the scraping be scattered without the city into an unclean place:</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:2; 1 Timothy 5:21–22; 2 Chronicles 17:7–9"> And that other stones be laid in the place of them that were taken away, and the house be plastered with other mortar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:28–30; Jude 1:12; 2 Peter 2:20"> But if, after the stones be taken out, and the dust scraped off, and it be plastered with other earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Leviticus 13:51–52; Zechariah 5:4"> The priest going in perceive that the leprosy is returned, and the walls full of spots, it is a lasting leprosy, and the house is unclean:</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 Kings 10:27; Matthew 24:2; 1 Kings 9:6–9"> And they shall destroy it forthwith, and shall cast the stones and timber thereof, and all the dust without the town into an unclean place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Numbers 19:21–22; Leviticus 22:6; Leviticus 11:28"> He that entereth into the house when it is shut, shall be unclean until evening,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Leviticus 14:8–9; Leviticus 11:25"> And he that sleepeth in it, and eateth any thing, shall wash his clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:11; Job 5:18; Mark 5:29"> But if the priest going in perceive that the leprosy is not spread in the house, after it was plastered again, he shall purify it, it being cured,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Leviticus 14:4"> And for the purification thereof he shall take two sparrows, and cedar wood, and scarlet, and hyssop:</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> And having immolated one sparrow In an earthen vessel over living waters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Psalms 51:7"> He shall take the cedar wood, and the hyssop, and the scarlet, and the living sparrow, and shall dip all in the blood of the sparrow that is immolated, and in the living water, and he shall sprinkle the house seven times:</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> And shall purify it as well with the blood of the sparrow, as with the living water, and with the living sparrow, and with the cedar wood, and the hyssop, and the scarlet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Leviticus 14:20; Leviticus 14:7"> And when he hath let go the sparrow to fly freely away into the field, he shall pray for the house, and it shall be rightly cleansed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Leviticus 15:32; Leviticus 14:32; Leviticus 6:9"> This is the law of every kind of leprosy and stroke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Leviticus 13:47–59"> Of the leprosy of garments and houses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Leviticus 13:2"> Of a scar and of blisters breaking out, of a shining spot, and when the colours are diversely changed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Leviticus 10:10; Ezekiel 44:23; Jeremiah 15:19"> That it may be known when a thing is clean or unclean.</span>
