<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 15:11; Leviticus 11:11; Hebrews 9:10"> Say to the children of Israel: These are the animals which you are to eat of all the living things of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 2:10; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Deuteronomy 6:6–7"> Whatsoever hath the hoof divided, and cheweth the cud among the beasts, you shall eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> But whatsoever cheweth indeed the cud, and hath a hoof, but divideth it not, as the camel, and others, that you shall not eat, but shall reckon it among the unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 104:18; Proverbs 30:26; Matthew 7:26"> The cherogrillus which cheweth the cud, but divideth not the hoof, is unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:7"> The hare also: for that too cheweth the cud, but divideth not the hoof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 66:17; Isaiah 65:4; Isaiah 66:3"> And the swine, which, though it divideth the hoof, cheweth not the cud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; Colossians 2:16; Hebrews 9:10"> The flesh of these you shall not eat, nor shall you touch their carcasses, because they are unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 5:6; James 2:18; Acts 20:21"> These are the things that breed in the waters, and which it is lawful to eat. All that hath fins, and scales, as well in the sea, as in the rivers, and the pools, you shall eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 29:27; Leviticus 7:18; Psalms 139:21–22"> But whatsoever hath not fins and scales, of those things that move and live in the waters, shall be an abomination to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And detestable: their flesh you shall not eat, and their carcasses you shall avoid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> All that have not fins and scales, in the waters, shall be unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 39:27–30; Jeremiah 4:22; Titus 3:3"> Of birds these are they which you must not eat, and which are to be avoided by you: The eagle, and the griffon, and the osprey,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And the kite, and the vulture, according to their kind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 12:24; 1 Kings 17:6; Proverbs 30:17"> And all that is of the raven kind, according to their likeness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 34:11–15; Ephesians 5:7–11; Ephesians 2:2–3"> The ostrich, and the owl, and the larus, and the hawk according to its kind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The screech owl, and the cormorant, and the ibis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And the swan, and the bittern, and the porphyrion,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> The heron, and the charadrion according to its kind, the houp also, and the bat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:10; Philippians 3:18–19; Leviticus 11:23"> Of things that fly, whatsoever goeth upon four feet, shall be abominable to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> But whatsoever walketh upon four feet, but hath the legs behind longer, wherewith it hoppeth upon the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 3:4; Mark 1:6; Exodus 10:4–5"> That you shall eat, as the bruchus in its kind, the attacus, and ophiomachus, and the locust, every one according to their kind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> But of dying things whatsoever hath four feet only, shall be an abomination to you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; Ephesians 2:1–3; 2 Corinthians 6:17"> And whosoever shall touch the carcasses of them, shall be defiled, and shall be unclean until the evening:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 15:5; Leviticus 14:8; Numbers 31:24"> And if it be necessary that he carry any of these things when they are dead, he shall wash his clothes, and shall be unclean until the sun set.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Every beast that hath a hoof, but divideth it not, nor cheweth the cud, shall be unclean: and he that toucheth it, shall be defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 11:23; Leviticus 11:20"> That which walketh upon hands of all animals which go on all four, shall be unclean: he that shall touch their carcasses shall be defiled until evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 11:24–25"> And he that shall carry such carcasses, shall wash his clothes, and shall be unclean until evening: because all these things are unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 11:41–42; 2 Timothy 3:2–5; Psalms 10:3"> These also shall be reckoned among unclean things, of all that move upon the earth, the weasel, and the mouse, and the crocodile, every one according to their kind:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> The shrew, and the chameleon, and the stello, and the lizard, and the mole:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 11:24–25; Leviticus 11:8"> All these are unclean. He that toucheth their carcasses shall be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 15:12; Titus 3:5; Titus 2:14"> And upon what thing soever any of their carcasses shall fall, it shall be defiled, whether it be a vessel of wood, or a garment, or skins or haircloths; or any thing in which work is done, they shall be dipped in water, and shall be unclean until the evening, and so afterwards shall be clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 6:28; Leviticus 15:12; Leviticus 14:45"> But an earthen vessel, into which any of these shall fall, shall be defiled, and therefore is to be broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Proverbs 21:4; Proverbs 21:27"> Any meat which you eat, if water from such a vessel be poured upon it, shall be unclean; and every liquor that is drunk out of any such vessel, shall be unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 15:12; Leviticus 6:28; Leviticus 11:33"> And upon whatsoever thing any of these dead beasts shall fall, it shall be unclean: whether it be oven, or pots with feet, they shall be destroyed, and shall be unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Zechariah 13:1; John 4:14"> But fountains and cisterns, and all gatherings together of waters shall be clean. He that toucheth their carcasses shall be defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:37; 1 Peter 1:23; 1 John 5:18"> If it fall upon seed corn, it shall not defile it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> But if any man pour water upon the seed, and afterwards it be touched by the carcasses, it shall be forthwith defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Leviticus 11:40; Leviticus 11:24; Leviticus 15:7"> If any beast die, of which it is lawful for you to eat, he that toucheth the carcass thereof, shall be unclean until the evening:</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Leviticus 22:8; Ezekiel 44:31; Deuteronomy 14:21"> And he that eateth or carrieth any thing thereof, shall wash his clothes, and shall be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Leviticus 11:29; Leviticus 11:20; Leviticus 11:23"> All that creepeth upon the earth shall be abominable, neither shall it be taken for meat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Titus 1:12; Isaiah 65:25; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> Whatsoever goeth upon the breast on four feet, or hath many feet, or traileth on the earth, you shall not eat, because it is abominable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Leviticus 20:25; Leviticus 11:41–42"> Do not defile your souls, nor touch aught thereof, lest you be unclean,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Exodus 20:2; 1 Peter 1:15–16; Leviticus 19:2"> For I am the Lord your God: be holy because I am holy. Defile not your souls by any creeping thing, that moveth upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44; Exodus 20:2; Exodus 6:7"> For I am the Lord, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, that I might be your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Leviticus 14:54; Leviticus 15:32; Ezekiel 43:12"> You shall be holy, because I am holy. This is the law of beasts and fowls, and of every living creature that moveth in the waters, and creepeth on the earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Leviticus 10:10; Ezekiel 44:23; Romans 14:13–23"> That you may know the differences of the clean, and unclean, and know what you ought to eat, and what to refuse.</span>
