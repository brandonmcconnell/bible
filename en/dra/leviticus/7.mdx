<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 6:17; Leviticus 5:14–7; Leviticus 6:25"> This also is the law of the sacrifice for a trespass, it is most holy:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 1:5; Leviticus 6:25; Leviticus 1:11"> Therefore where the holocaust is immolated, the victim also for a trespass shall be slain: the blood thereof shall be poured round about the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 29:13; Leviticus 3:15–16; Psalms 51:6"> They shall offer thereof the rump and the fat that covereth the entrails:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 3:4"> The two little kidneys, and the fat which is by the flanks, and the caul of the liver with the little kidneys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 1:13; Galatians 5:24; 1 Peter 4:1–2"> And the priest shall burn them upon the altar: it is the burnt sacrifice of the Lord for a trespass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 6:29; Numbers 18:9–10; Leviticus 2:3"> Every male of the priestly race, shall eat this flesh in a holy place, because it is most holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 6:25–26; Leviticus 14:13; 1 Corinthians 9:13"> As the sacrifice for sin is offered, so is also that for a trespass: the same shall be the law of both these sacrifices: it shall belong to the priest that offereth it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 4:11; Exodus 29:14; Romans 13:14"> The priest that offereth the victim of holocaust, shall have the skin thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 18:9; Ezekiel 44:29; Leviticus 2:10"> And every sacrifice of flour that is baked in the oven, and whatsoever is dressed on the gridiron, or in the fryingpan, shall be the priest’s that offereth it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:14; Exodus 16:18"> Whether they be tempered with oil, or dry, all the sons of Aaron shall have one as much as another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 22:18–21; Ezekiel 45:15; Leviticus 3:1–17"> This is the law of the sacrifice of peace offerings that is offered to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 2:4; Numbers 6:15; 1 Peter 2:5"> If the oblation be for thanksgiving, they shall offer leaves without leaven tempered with oil, and unleavened wafers anointed with oil, and fine flour fried, and cakes tempered and mingled with oil:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Amos 4:5; Leviticus 23:17; Matthew 13:33"> Moreover leaves of leavened bread with the sacrifice of thanks, which is offered for peace offerings:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 18:19; Exodus 29:27–28; Numbers 18:24–32"> Of which one shall be offered to the Lord for firstfruits, and shall be the priest’s that shall pour out the blood of the victim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 22:29–30"> And the flesh of it shall be eaten the same day, neither shall any of it remain until the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 19:5–8; Deuteronomy 12:6; Psalms 116:14"> If any man by vow, or of his own accord offer a sacrifice, it shall in like manner be eaten the same day: and if any of it remain until the morrow, it is lawful to eat it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 12:10; Hosea 6:2; 1 Corinthians 15:4"> But whatsoever shall be found on the third day shall be consumed with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 19:7–8; Leviticus 11:41; Numbers 18:27"> If any man eat of the flesh of the victim of peace offerings on the third day, the oblation shall be of no effect, neither shall it profit the offerer: yea rather whatsoever soul shall defile itself with such meat, shall be guilty of transgression.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 11:41; Titus 1:15; Acts 10:15–16"> The flesh that hath touched any unclean thing, shall not be eaten, but shall be burnt with fire: he that is clean shall eat of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 15:2–33; Genesis 17:14; Leviticus 22:3–7"> If any one that is defiled shall eat of the flesh of the sacrifice of peace offerings, which is offered to the Lord, he shall be cut off from his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 12:1–59; Leviticus 11:20; Ezekiel 4:14"> And he that hath touched the uncleanness of man, or of beast, or of any thing that can defile, and shall eat of such kind of flesh, shall be cut off from his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 3:16–17; Leviticus 17:6; 1 Samuel 2:15–17"> Say to the children of Israel: The fat of a sheep, and of an ox, and of a goat you shall not eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 22:31; Leviticus 17:15; Leviticus 22:8"> The fat of a carcass that hath died of itself, and of a beast that was caught by another beast, you shall have for divers uses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> If any man eat the fat that should be offered for the burnt sacrifice of the Lord, he shall perish out of his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 9:4; Leviticus 3:17; Acts 15:20"> Moreover you shall not eat the blood of any creature whatsoever, whether of birds or beasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 10:29; Leviticus 7:20–21; Leviticus 7:25"> Every one that eateth blood, shall perish from among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Colossians 1:20; 1 John 1:7; Leviticus 3:1–17"> Speak to the children of Israel, saying: He that offereth a victim of peace offerings to the Lord, let him offer therewith a sacrifice also, that is, the libations thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 6:20; Leviticus 3:9; Leviticus 3:14"> He shall hold in his hands the fat of the victim, and the breast: and when he hath offered and consecrated both to the Lord, he shall deliver them to the priest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 3:5; Leviticus 3:11; Leviticus 3:16"> Who shall burn the fat upon the altar, but the breast shall be Aaron’s and his sons’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 6:20; Leviticus 7:34; Leviticus 9:21"> The right shoulder also of the victims of peace offerings shall fall to the priest for firstfruits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 26:1–46; Leviticus 6:1–30; Leviticus 7:3"> He among the sons of Aaron, that offereth the blood, and the fat, he shall have the right shoulder also for his portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 18:18–19; Leviticus 10:14–15; Exodus 29:9"> For the breast that is elevated and the shoulder that is separated I have taken of the children of Israel, from off their victims of peace offerings, and have given them to Aaron the priest, and to his sons, by a law for ever, from all the people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 2:20; 1 John 2:27; Numbers 18:7–19"> This is the anointing of Aaron and his sons, in the ceremonies of the Lord, in the day when Moses offered them, that they might do the office of priesthood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Leviticus 8:12; Leviticus 8:30; Exodus 40:13–15"> And the things that the Lord commanded to be given them by the children of Israel, by a perpetual observance in their generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 29:1; Leviticus 1:1–6; Leviticus 6:9–18"> This is the law of holocaust, and of the sacrifice for sin, and for trespass, and for consecration, and the victims of peace offerings:</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Leviticus 1:1–2"> Which the Lord appointed to Moses in mount Sinai, when he commanded the children of Israel, that they should offer their oblations to the Lord in the desert of Sinai.</span>
