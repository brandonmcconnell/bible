<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 1:1; Galatians 4:24–25; Exodus 19:1"> And the Lord spoke to Moses in mount Sinai, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:21; Leviticus 26:34–35; Exodus 23:10"> Speak to the children of Israel, and thou shalt say to them: When you shall have entered into the land which I will give you, observe the rest of the sabbath to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 23:10"> Six years thou shalt sow thy field and six years thou shalt prune thy vineyard, and shalt gather the fruits thereof:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:21; Leviticus 25:20–23; Exodus 23:10–11"> But in the seventh year there shall be a sabbath to the land, of the resting of the Lord: thou shalt not sow thy field, nor prune thy vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 19:29; Isaiah 37:30"> What the ground shall bring forth of itself, thou shalt not reap: neither shalt thou gather the grapes of the firstfruits as a vintage: for it is a year of rest to the land:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 2:44; Exodus 23:11; Acts 4:34–35"> But they shall be unto you for meat, to thee and to thy manservant, to thy maidservant and thy hireling, and to the strangers that sojourn with thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> All things that grow shall be meat to thy beasts and to thy cattle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 23:15; Genesis 2:2"> Thou shalt also number to thee seven weeks of years, that is to say, seven times seven, which together make forty-nine years:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 23:24; Leviticus 23:27; 1 Thessalonians 1:8"> And thou shalt sound the trumpet in the seventh month, the tenth day of the month, in the time of the expiation in all your land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:8; Isaiah 63:4; 1 Peter 2:16"> And thou shalt sanctify the fiftieth year, and shalt proclaim remission to all the inhabitants of thy land: for it is the year of jubilee. Every man shall return to his possession, and every one shall go back to his former family:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 27:17; Leviticus 25:4–5"> Because it is the jubilee and the fiftieth year. You shall not sow, nor reap the things that grow in the field of their own accord, neither shall you gather the firstfruits of the vines,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 25:6–7"> Because of the sanctification of the jubilee: but as they grow you shall presently eat them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 25:10; Leviticus 27:17–24; Numbers 36:4"> In the year of the jubilee all shall return to their possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 19:13; Leviticus 25:17; 1 Samuel 12:3–4"> When thou shalt sell any thing to thy neighbour, or shalt buy of him; grieve not thy brother: but thou shalt buy of him according to the number of years from the jubilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 27:18–23; Philippians 4:5"> And he shall sell to thee according to the computation of the fruits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 25:27; Leviticus 25:51"> The more years remain after the jubilee, the more shall the price increase: and the less time is counted, so much the less shall the purchase cost. For he shall sell to thee the time of the fruits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 19:14; Leviticus 19:32; Leviticus 25:43"> Do not afflict your countrymen, but let every one fear his God: because I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:6; Deuteronomy 12:10; Proverbs 1:33"> Do my precepts, and keep my judgments, and fulfill them: that you may dwell in the land without any fear,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joel 2:26; Psalms 85:12; Leviticus 26:5"> And the ground may yield you its fruits, of which you may eat your fill, fearing no mall’s invasion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 25:4; Luke 12:29; Numbers 11:4"> But if you say: What shall we eat the seventh year, if we sow not, nor gather our fruits?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:8; Proverbs 10:22; 2 Corinthians 9:10"> I will give you my blessing the sixth year, and it shall yield the fruits of three years:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Kings 19:29; Leviticus 26:10; Isaiah 37:30"> And the eighth year you shall sow, and shall eat of the old fruits, until the ninth year: till new grow up, you shall eat the old store.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Psalms 39:12; Ezekiel 48:14"> The land also shall not be sold for ever: because it is mine, and you are strangers and sojourners with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 1:7; Leviticus 25:51–53; Leviticus 25:27"> For which cause all the country of your possession shall be under the condition of redemption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ruth 2:20; Jeremiah 32:7–8; Ruth 3:12"> If thy brother being impoverished sell his little possession, and his kinsman will, he may redeem what he had sold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 5:7"> But if he have no kinsman, and he himself can find the price to redeem it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> The value of the fruits shall be counted from that time when he sold it: and the overplus he shall restore to the buyer, and so shall receive his possession again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 35:9–10; Leviticus 25:10; 1 Peter 1:4–5"> But if his hands find not the means to repay the price, the buyer shall have what he bought, until the year of the jubilee. For in that year all that is sold shall return to the owner, and to the ancient possessor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> He that selleth a house within the walls of a city, shall have the liberty to redeem it, until one year be expired:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> If he redeem it not, and the whole year be fully out, the buyer shall possess it, and his posterity for ever, and it cannot be redeemed, not even in the jubilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 49:7–8"> But if the house be in a village, that hath no walls, it shall be sold according to the same law as the fields: if it be not redeemed before, in the jubilee it shall return to the owner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Joshua 21:1–45; Numbers 35:1–8"> The houses of Levites, which are in cities, may always be redeemed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 18:20–24; Deuteronomy 18:1–2"> If they be not redeemed, in the jubilee they shall all return to the owners, because the houses of the cities of the Levites are for their possessions among the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Leviticus 25:23; Acts 4:36–37; Numbers 35:2–5"> But let not their suburbs be sold, because it is a perpetual possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:7–8; Acts 11:29; Leviticus 25:25"> If thy brother be impoverished, and weak of hand, and thou receive him as a stranger and sojourner, and he live with thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 22:25; Deuteronomy 23:19–20; Ezekiel 18:8"> Take not usury of him nor more than thou gavest: fear thy God, that thy brother may live with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Thou shalt not give him thy money upon usury, nor exact of him any increase of fruits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Leviticus 11:45; Numbers 15:41; Exodus 20:2"> I am the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, that I might give you the land of Chanaan, and might be your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 21:2; 1 Kings 9:22; 2 Kings 4:1"> If thy brother constrained by poverty, sell himself to thee, thou shalt not oppress him with the service of bondservants:</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Exodus 21:2–3"> But he shall be as a hireling, and a sojourner: he shall work with thee until the year of the jubilee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Leviticus 25:28; Exodus 21:3; John 8:32"> And afterwards he shall go out with his children, and shall return to his kindred and to the possession of his fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Leviticus 25:55; Romans 6:22; 1 Corinthians 7:21–23"> For they are my servants, and I brought them out of the land of Egypt: let them not be sold as bondmen:</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Colossians 4:1; Leviticus 25:46; Exodus 1:13–14"> Afflict him not by might, but fear thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Exodus 12:44; Psalms 2:8–9; Isaiah 14:1–2"> Let your bondmen, and your bondwomen, be of the nations that are round about you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Isaiah 56:3–6"> And of the strangers that sojourn among you, or that were born of them in your land, these you shall have for servants:</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 14:2; Leviticus 25:43"> And by right of inheritance shall leave them to your posterity, and shall possess them for ever. But oppress not your brethren the children of Israel by might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="James 2:5; Leviticus 25:26; 1 Samuel 2:7–8"> If the hand of a stranger or a sojourner grow strong among you, and thy brother being impoverished sell himself to him, or to any of his race:</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:5; Hebrews 2:11–13; Leviticus 25:35"> After the sale he may be redeemed. He that will of his brethren shall redeem him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Leviticus 25:26"> Either his uncle, or his uncle’s son, or his kinsman, by blood, or by affinity. But if he himself be able also, he shall redeem himself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Isaiah 16:14; Isaiah 21:16; Leviticus 25:53"> Counting only the years from the time of his selling unto the year of the jubilee: and counting the money that he was sold for, according to the number of the years and the reckoning of a hired servant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> If there be many years that remain until the jubilee, according to them shall he also repay the price.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> If few, he shall make the reckoning with him according to the number of the years, and shall repay to the buyer of what remaineth of the years,</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Leviticus 25:46; Leviticus 25:43"> His wages being allowed for which he served before: he shall not afflict him violently in thy sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Exodus 21:2–3; Leviticus 25:40–41; Isaiah 49:9"> And if by these means he cannot be redeemed, in the year of the jubilee he shall go out with his children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Leviticus 25:42; Isaiah 43:3; Exodus 13:3"> For the children of Israel are my servants, whom I brought forth out of the land of Egypt.</span>
