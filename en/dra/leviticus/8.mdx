<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 28:2–4; Exodus 39:41; Hebrews 7:27"> Take Aaron with his sons, their vestments, and the oil of unction, a calf for sin, two rams, a basket with unleavened bread,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:13; Acts 2:1; Nehemiah 8:1"> And thou shalt gather together all the congregation to the door of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 39:26; Exodus 39:7; Exodus 39:5"> And Moses did as the Lord had commanded. And all the multitude being gathered together before the door of the tabernacle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 29:4–37"> He said: This is the word that the Lord hath commanded to be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:11; Ephesians 5:26; Exodus 29:4"> And immediately he offered Aaron and his sons: and when he had washed them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Isaiah 61:10; Galatians 3:27"> He vested the high priest with the strait linen garment, girding him with the girdle, and putting on him the violet tunick, and over it he put the ephod,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 2:63; 1 Thessalonians 5:8; Isaiah 59:17"> And binding it with the girdle, he fitted it to the rational, on which was Doctrine and Truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philippians 2:9–11; Zechariah 6:11–14; Exodus 28:36–38"> He put also the mitre upon his head: and upon the mitre over the forehead, he put the plate of gold, consecrated with sanctification, as the Lord had commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 40:9–11; Leviticus 8:2; Exodus 30:23–29"> He took also the oil of unction, with which he anointed the tabernacle, with all the furniture thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Titus 3:6; Exodus 30:29; Exodus 16:19"> And when he had sanctified and sprinkled the altar seven times, he anointed it, and all the vessels thereof, and the laver with the foot thereof, he sanctified with the oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 30:30; Psalms 133:2; Exodus 29:7"> And he poured it upon Aaron’s head, and he anointed and consecrated him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 29:8–9; Revelation 5:10; Revelation 1:6"> And after he had offered his sons, he vested them with linen tunicks, and girded them with girdles, and put mitres on them as the Lord had commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:19; Psalms 66:15; 1 Peter 3:18"> He offered also the calf for sin: and when Aaron and his sons had put their hands upon the head thereof,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 4:7; Hebrews 9:18–23; Ezekiel 43:19–27"> He immolated it: and took the blood, and dipping his finger in it, he touched the horns of the altar round about. Which being expiated, and sanctified, he poured the rest of the blood at the bottom thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 29:13; Leviticus 3:3–5; Leviticus 4:8–9"> But the fat that was upon the entrails, and the caul of the liver, and the two little kidneys, with their fat, he burnt upon the altar:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 4:11–12; Exodus 29:14; Leviticus 6:30"> And the calf with the skin, and the flesh and the dung, he burnt without the camp, as the Lord had commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 8:2; Leviticus 1:4–13; Exodus 29:15–18"> He offered also a ram for a holocaust: and when Aaron and his sons had put their hands upon its head,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> He immolated it, and poured the blood thereof round about upon the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 1:8"> And cutting the ram into pieces, the head thereof, and the joints, and the fat he burnt in the fire,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Exodus 29:18; Leviticus 1:17"> Having first washed the entrails, and the feet, and the whole ram together he burnt upon the altar, because it was a holocaust of most sweet odour to the Lord, as he had commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 8:2; Leviticus 8:29; Exodus 29:19–31"> He offered also the second ram, in the consecration of priests: and Aaron, and his sons put their hands upon the head thereof:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 29:20; Leviticus 14:14; Romans 12:1"> And when Moses had immolated it, he took of the blood thereof, and touched the tip of Aaron’s right ear, and the thumb of his right hand, and in like manner also the great toe of his right foot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 9:18–22"> He offered also the sons of Aaron: and when with the blood of the ram that was immolated, he had touched the tip of the right ear of every one of them, and the thumbs of their right hands, and the great toes of their right feet, the rest he poured on the altar round about:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 3:9; Leviticus 3:3–5; Isaiah 53:10"> But the fat, and the rump, and all the fat that covereth the entrails, and the caul of the liver, and the two kidneys with their fat, and with the right shoulder, he separated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 29:23; 1 Timothy 2:5; Acts 5:12"> And taking out of the basket; of unleavened bread, which was before the Lord, a loaf without leaven, and a cake tempered with oil and a wafer, he put them upon the fat, and the right shoulder,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:21; Hebrews 9:14; Exodus 29:24–37"> Delivering all to Aaron, and to his sons: wile having lifted them up before the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 29:25; Psalms 22:13–14; Hebrews 10:14–22"> He took them again from their hands, and burnt them upon the altar of holocaust, because it was the oblation of consecration, for a sweet odour of sacrifice to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:31; 1 Peter 4:11; Leviticus 7:30–34"> And he took of the ram of consecration, the breast for his portion, elevating it before the Lord, as the Lord had commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 29:21; Numbers 3:3; Exodus 30:30"> And taking the ointment, and the blood that was upon the altar, he sprinkled Aaron, and his vestments, and his sons, and their vestments with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 29:31–32; Galatians 2:20; 1 Samuel 2:13–17"> And when he had sanctified them in their vestments, he commanded them, saying: Boil the flesh before the door of the tabernacle, and there eat it. Eat ye also the loaves of consecration, that are laid in the basket, as the Lord commanded me, saying: Aaron and his sons shall eat them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 29:34; Exodus 12:10; Hebrews 3:13–14"> And whatsoever shall be left of the flesh and the leaves, shall be consumed with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 29:35; Numbers 19:12; Ezekiel 43:25–27"> And you shall not go out of the door of the tabernacle for seven days, until the day wherein the time of your consecration shall be expired. For in seven days the consecration is finished:</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hebrews 7:27; Hebrews 7:16; Hebrews 10:11–12"> As at this present it hath been done, that the rite of the sacrifice might be accomplished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 3:7; Deuteronomy 11:1; 1 Kings 2:3"> Day and night shall you remain in the tabernacle observing the watches of the Lord, lest you die: for so it hath been commanded me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; Deuteronomy 4:2; Exodus 40:16"> And Aaron and his sons did all things which the Lord spoke by the hand of Moses.</span>
