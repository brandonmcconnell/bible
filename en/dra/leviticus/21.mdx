<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:25; Leviticus 21:11; Leviticus 10:6–7"> The Lord said also to Moses: Speak to the priests the sons of Aaron, and thou shalt say to them: Let not a priest incur an uncleanness at the death of his citizens:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:13; Leviticus 18:6"> But only for his kin, such as are near in blood, that is to say, for his father and for his mother, and for his son, and for his daughter, for his brother also,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And for a maiden sister, who hath had no husband:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:16–17"> But not even for the prince of his people shall he do any thing that may make him unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:1; Ezekiel 44:20; Leviticus 19:27–28"> Neither shall they shave their head, nor their beard, nor make incisions in their flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 3:11; Leviticus 18:21; 1 Peter 2:9"> They shall be holy to their God, and shall not profane his name: for they offer the burnt offering of the Lord, and the bread of their God, and therefore they shall be holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 21:13–14; Ezekiel 44:22; Leviticus 21:8"> They shall not take to wife a harlot or a vile prostitute, nor one that has been put away from her husband: because they are consecrated to their God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 20:7–8; Leviticus 19:2; Leviticus 11:44–45"> And offer the leaves of proposition. Let them therefore be holy, because I also am holy, the Lord, who sanctify them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 38:24; Leviticus 19:29; Ezekiel 9:6"> If the daughter of a priest be taken in whoredom, and dishonour the name of her father, she shall be burnt with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 10:6–7; Leviticus 16:32; Matthew 26:65"> The high priest, that is to say, the priest, is the greatest among his brethren. upon whose head the oil of unction hath been poured, and whose hands have been consecrated for the priesthood, and who hath been vested with the holy vestments, shall not uncover his head, he shall not rend his garments:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 19:14; Leviticus 21:1–2; Numbers 6:7"> Nor shall he go in at all to any dead person: not even for his father, or his mother, shall he be defiled:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 10:7; Exodus 29:6–7; Leviticus 8:9–12"> Neither shall he go out of the holy places, lest he defile the sanctuary of the Lord, because the oil of the holy unction of his God is upon him. I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 21:7; 2 Corinthians 11:2; Revelation 14:4"> He shall take a virgin unto his wife:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 21:7; Ezekiel 44:22"> But a widow or one that is divorced, or defiled, or a harlot, he shall not take, but a maid of his own people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 11:16; 1 Corinthians 7:14; Nehemiah 13:23–29"> He shall not mingle the stock of his kindred with the common people of his nation: for I am the Lord who sanctify him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 65:4; Numbers 16:5; Leviticus 10:3"> Say to Aaron: Whosoever of thy seed throughout their families, hath a blemish, he shall not offer bread to his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2–3; Leviticus 22:19–25; Titus 1:7"> Neither shall he approach to minister to him: If he be blind, if he be lame, if he have a little, or a great, or a crooked nose,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> If his foot, or if his hand be broken,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:1; Isaiah 56:3"> If he be crookbacked, or blear eyed, or have a pearl in his eye, or a continual scab, or a dry scurf in his body, or a rupture:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 21:6; Leviticus 21:8; Leviticus 21:17"> Whosoever of the seed of Aaron the priest hath a blemish, he shall not approach to offer sacrifices to the Lord, nor bread to his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3; Numbers 18:19; Leviticus 24:8–9"> He shall eat nevertheless of the loaves, that are offered in the sanctuary,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 21:12; Ezekiel 44:9–14; Exodus 30:6–8"> Yet so that he enter not within the veil, nor approach to the altar, because he hath a blemish, and he must not defile my sanctuary. I am the Lord who sanctify them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:18; Malachi 2:1–7; 2 Timothy 2:2"> Moses therefore spoke to Aaron, and to his sons and to all Israel, all the things that had been commanded him.</span>
