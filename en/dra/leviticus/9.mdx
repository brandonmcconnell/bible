<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 28:1; Ezekiel 43:26–27; Leviticus 15:14"> And when the eighth day was come, Moses called Aaron and his sons, and the ancients of Israel, and said to Aaron:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 4:3; Hebrews 10:10–14; Leviticus 8:14"> Take of the herd a calf for sin, and a ram for a holocaust, both without blemish, and offer them before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezra 6:17; Revelation 5:9; Titus 2:14"> And to the children of Israel thou shalt say: Take ye a he goat for sin, and a calf, and a lamb, both of a year old, and without blemish for a holocaust,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 29:43; Ezekiel 43:2; Leviticus 9:23"> Also a bullock and a ram for peace offerings: and immolate them before the Lord, offering for the sacrifice of every one of them flour tempered with oil; for today the Lord will appear to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 5:2–3; Exodus 19:17; Nehemiah 8:1"> They brought therefore all things that Moses had commanded before the door of the tabernacle: where when all the multitude stood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 9:23; Ezekiel 43:2; Exodus 24:16"> Moses said: This is the word, which the Lord hath commanded: do it, and his glory will appear to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 5:3; Hebrews 5:1; Leviticus 4:3"> And he said to Aaron: Approach to the altar, and offer sacrifice for thy sin: offer the holocaust, and pray for thyself and for the people: and when thou hast slain the people’s victim, pray for them, as the Lord hath commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 4:1–12; Leviticus 1:4–5; Leviticus 4:29"> And forthwith Aaron, approaching to the altar, immolated the calf for his sin:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 4:6–7; Leviticus 8:15; Leviticus 4:25"> And his sons brought him the blood of it: and he dipped his finger therein, and touched the horns of the altar, and poured the rest at the foot thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 8:16–17; Proverbs 23:26; Leviticus 3:9–11"> And the fat, and the little kidneys, and the caul of the liver, which are for sin, he burnt upon the altar, as the Lord had commanded Moses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 8:17; Leviticus 4:11–12; Leviticus 16:27–28"> But the flesh and skins thereof he burnt with fire without the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Ephesians 5:25–27; Leviticus 1:1–17"> He immolated also the victim of holocaust: and his sons brought him the blood thereof, which he poured round about on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the victim being cut into pieces, they brought to him the head and all the members, all which he burnt with fire upon the altar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 8:21"> Having first washed the entrails and the feet with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 4:27–31; Hebrews 5:3; Leviticus 9:3"> Then offering for the sin of the people, he slew the he goat: and expiating the altar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 9:12–14; Leviticus 8:18–21; Hebrews 10:1–22"> He offered the holocaust:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 2:1–2; John 6:53; Leviticus 9:1"> Adding in the sacrifice the libations, which are offered withal, and burning them upon the altar, besides the ceremonies of the morning holocaust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 1:20; Romans 5:1; Ephesians 2:14–17"> He immolated also the bullock and the ram, the peace offerings of the people: and his sons brought him the blood, which he poured upon the altar round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 3:16; Leviticus 9:10; Leviticus 3:9"> The fat also of the bullock, and the rump of the ram, and the two little kidneys, with their fat, and the caul of the liver,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 3:14–17; Leviticus 7:29–34"> They put upon the breasts. And after the fat was burnt upon the altar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 7:30–34; Leviticus 7:24; Exodus 29:24"> Aaron separated their breasts, and the right shoulders, elevating them before the Lord, as Moses had commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:5; Luke 24:50; Numbers 6:23–27"> And stretching forth his hands to the people, he blessed them. And so the victims for sin, and the holocausts, and the peace offerings being finished, he came down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 9:6; Numbers 16:19; Luke 1:21–22"> And Moses and Aaron went into the tabernacle of the testimony, and afterwards came forth and blessed the people. And the glory of the Lord appeared to all the multitude:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Judges 6:21; 1 Kings 18:38–39; 1 Chronicles 21:26"> And behold a fire, coming forth from the Lord, devoured the holocaust, and the fat that was upon the altar: which when the multitude saw, they praised the Lord, falling on their faces.</span>
