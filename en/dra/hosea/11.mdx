<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 2:15; Exodus 4:22; Hosea 13:4"> As the morning passeth, so hath the king of Israel Israel was a child, and I loved him: and I called my son out of Egypt.</span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:17; Deuteronomy 1:31; Exodus 15:26"> And I was like a foster father to Ephraim, I carried them in my arms: and they knew not that I healed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 12:32; Leviticus 26:13; 2 Corinthians 5:14"> I will draw them with the cords of Adam, with the bands of love: and I will be to them as one that taketh off the yoke on their jaws: and I put his meat to him that he might eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 7:16; Hosea 8:13; 2 Kings 17:13–14"> He shall not return into the land of Egypt, but the Assyrian shall be his king: because they would not be converted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 13:16; Hosea 10:6; Hosea 10:14"> The sword hath begun in his cities, and it shall consume his chosen men, and shall devour their heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:5; Amos 5:14–15; Hosea 7:16"> And my people shall long for my return: but a yoke shall be put upon them together, which shall not be taken off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:20; Matthew 23:37; Hosea 6:4"> How shall I deal with thee, O Ephraim, shall I protect thee, O Israel? how shall I make thee as Adama, shall I set thee as Seboim? my heart is turned within me, my repentance is stirred up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Isaiah 55:8–9; Micah 7:18–20"> I will not execute the fierceness of my wrath: I will not return to destroy Ephraim: because I am God, and not man: the holy one in the midst of thee, and I will not enter into the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 31:4; Joel 3:16; Amos 1:2"> They shall walk after the Lord, he shall roar as a lion: because he shall roar, and the children of the sea shall fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 11:11; Isaiah 60:8; Zechariah 10:10"> And they shall fly away like a bird out of Egypt, and like a dove out of the land of the Assyrians: and I will place them in their own houses, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 12:7; Revelation 3:21; 2 Chronicles 29:1–32"> Ephraim hath compassed me about with denials, and the house of Israel with deceit: but Juda went down as a witness with God, and is faithful with the saints.</span>
