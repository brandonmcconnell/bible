<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 2:10; Hosea 2:23; Zechariah 13:9"> Say ye to your brethren: You are my people, and to your sister: Thou hast obtained mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 50:1; Ezekiel 16:25; Ezekiel 23:45"> Judge your mother, judge her: because she is not my wife, and I am not her husband. Let her put away her fornications from her face, and her adulteries from between her breasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:22; Isaiah 32:13–14; Ezekiel 19:13"> Lest I strip her naked, and set her as in the day that she was born: and I will make her as a wilderness, and will set her as a land that none can pass through, and will kill her with drought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:14; Hosea 1:6; Ezekiel 8:18"> And I will not have mercy on her children: for they are the children of fornications.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 1:21; Hosea 3:1; Jeremiah 44:17–18"> For their mother hath committed fornication, she that conceived them is covered with shame: for she said: I will go after my lovers, that give me my bread, and my water, my wool, and my flax, my oil, and my drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 19:8; Job 3:23; Lamentations 3:7–9"> Wherefore behold I will hedge up thy way with thorns, and I will stop it up with a wall, and she shall not find her paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:2; Hosea 5:13; Ezekiel 23:4"> And she shall follow after her lovers, and shall not overtake them: and she shall seek them, and shall not find, and she shall say: I will go, and return to my first husband, because it was better with me then, than now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 1:3; Hosea 4:11; Hosea 13:2"> And she did not know that I gave her corn and wine, and oil, and multiplied her silver, and gold, which they have used in the service of Baal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 2:3; Haggai 1:6–11; Malachi 1:4"> Therefore will I return, and take away my corn in its season, and my wine in its season, and I will set at liberty my wool, and my flax, which covered her disgrace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:29; Jeremiah 13:26; Hosea 13:7–8"> And now I will lay open her folly in the eyes of her lovers: and no man shall deliver her out of my hand:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 1:13–14; Amos 5:21; Jeremiah 7:34"> And I will cause all her mirth to cease, her solemnities, her new moons, her sabbaths, and all her festival times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 7:23; Isaiah 5:5; Hosea 2:5"> And I will destroy her vines, and her fig trees, of which she said: These are my rewards, which my lovers have given me: and I will make her as a forest, and the beasts of the field shall devour her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 13:6; Judges 3:7; Hosea 13:1"> And I will visit upon her the days of Baalim, to whom she burnt incense, and decked herself out with her earrings, and with her jewels, and went after her lovers, and forgot me, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:1–2; Revelation 12:6; Ezekiel 20:35–36"> Therefore, behold I will allure her, and will lead her into the wilderness: and I will speak to her heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:2; Ezekiel 28:26; Hosea 11:1"> And I will give her vinedressers out of the same place, and the valley of Achor for an opening of hope: and she shall sing there according to the days of her youth, and according to the days of her coming up out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 54:5; Hosea 2:7; Jeremiah 3:14"> And it shall be in that day, saith the Lord, That she shall call me: My husband, and she shall call me no more Baali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 23:13; Joshua 23:7; Psalms 16:4"> And I will take away the names of Baalim out of her mouth, and she shall no more remember their name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:25; Ezekiel 39:9–10; Psalms 46:9"> And in that day I will make a covenant with them, with the beasts of the field, and with the fowls of the air, and with the creeping things of the earth: and I will destroy the bow, and the sword, and war out of the land: and I will make them sleep secure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:2; Revelation 21:2; Isaiah 54:8–10"> And I will espouse thee to me for ever: and I will espouse thee to me in justice, and judgment, and in mercy, and in commiserations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 John 5:20; John 17:3; Jeremiah 24:7"> And I will espouse thee to me in faith: and thou shalt know that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zechariah 8:12; Isaiah 65:24; Romans 8:32"> And it shall come to pass in that day: I will hear, saith the Lord, I will hear the heavens, and they shall hear the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joel 2:19; Hosea 1:4; Hosea 1:11"> And the earth shall hear the core, and the wine, and the oil, and these shall hear Jezrahel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 9:25–26; 1 Peter 2:9–10; Zechariah 13:9"> And I will sow her unto me in the earth, and I will have mercy on her that was without mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And I will say to that which was not my people: Thou art my people: and they shall say: Thou art my God.</span>
