<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 10:5; Hosea 4:12; Jeremiah 44:17"> Rejoice not, O Israel: rejoice not as the nations do: for thou hast committed fornication against thy God, thou hast loved a reward upon every cornfloor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 2:9; Micah 6:13–16; Joel 1:3–7"> The floor and the winepress shall not feed them, and the wine shall deceive them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:13; Hosea 8:13; Leviticus 25:23"> They shall not dwell in the Lord’s land: Ephraim is returned to Egypt, and hath eaten unclean things among the Assyrians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 8:13; Deuteronomy 26:14; Jeremiah 6:20"> They shall not offer wine to the Lord, neither shall they please him: their sacrifices shall be like the bread of mourners: all that shall eat it shall be defiled: for their bread is life for their soul, it shall not enter into the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 10:3; Jeremiah 5:31; Hosea 2:11"> What will you do in the solemn day, in the day of the feast of the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 10:8; Isaiah 5:6; Isaiah 7:23"> For behold they are gone because of destruction: Egypt shall gather them together, Memphis shall bury them: nettles shall inherit their beloved silver, the bur shall be in their tabernacles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 26:24–25; Micah 7:4; 2 Kings 9:11"> The days of visitation are come, the days of repaying are come: know ye, O Israel, that the prophet was foolish, the spiritual man was mad, for the multitude of thy iniquity, and the multitude of thy madness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hosea 5:1; Ezekiel 3:17; Isaiah 62:6"> The watchman of Ephraim was with my God: the prophet is become a snare of ruin upon all his ways, madness is in the house of his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 10:9; Judges 19:16–21; Hosea 8:13"> They have sinned deeply, as in the days of Gabaa: he will remember their iniquity, and will visit their sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:13; Hosea 4:14; Numbers 25:1–18"> I found Israel like grapes in the desert, I saw their fathers like the firstfruits of the fig tree in the top thereof: but they went in to Beelphegor, and alienated themselves to that confusion, and became abominable, as those things were, which they loved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:18; Hosea 9:14; Hosea 4:7"> As for Ephraim, their glory hath flown away like a bird from the birth, and from the womb, and from the conception.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 7:13; Deuteronomy 32:25; Deuteronomy 31:17"> And though they should bring up their children, I will make them without children among men: yea, and woe to them, when I shall depart from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:1–21; Ezekiel 27:3; Hosea 13:8"> Ephraim, as I saw, was a Tyre founded in beauty: and Ephraim shall bring out his children to the murderer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 23:29; 1 Corinthians 7:26; Mark 13:17"> Give them, O Lord. What wilt thou give them? Give them a womb without children, and dry breasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 4:15; Isaiah 1:23; Amos 5:5"> All their wickedness is in Galgal, for there I hated them: for the wickedness of their devices I will cast them forth out of my house: I will love them no more, all their princes are revolters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 9:11–13; Ezekiel 24:21; Isaiah 5:24"> Ephraim is struck, their root is dried up, they shall yield no fruit. And if they should have issue, I will slay the best beloved fruit of their womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hosea 7:13; Deuteronomy 28:64–65; Acts 3:23"> My God will cast them away, because they hearkened not to him: and they shall be wanderers among the nations.</span>
