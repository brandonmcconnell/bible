<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 11:2; Joshua 3:7; Numbers 13:16"> When Ephraim spoke, a horror seized Israel: and he sinned in Baal and died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 44:17–20; 1 Kings 19:18; Isaiah 46:6"> And now they have sinned more and more: and they have made to themselves a molten thing of their silver as the likeness of idols: the whole is the work of craftsmen: to these that say: Sacrifice men, ye that adore calves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 6:4; Psalms 1:4; Psalms 68:2"> Therefore they shall be as a morning aloud, and as the early dew that passeth away, as the dust that is driven with a whirlwind out of the floor, and as the smoke out of the chimney.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 45:21–22; Hosea 12:9; Isaiah 43:10–13"> But I am the Lord thy God from the land of Egypt: and thou shalt know no God but me, and there is no saviour beside me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:15; Deuteronomy 2:7; Deuteronomy 32:10"> I knew thee in the desert, in the land of the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13–15; Deuteronomy 8:12–14; Nehemiah 9:35"> According to their pastures they were filled, and were made full: and they lifted up their heart, and have forgotten me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 5:14; Jeremiah 5:6; Lamentations 3:10"> And I will be to them as a lioness, as a leopard in the way of the Assyrians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:8; Isaiah 5:29; Psalms 50:22"> I will meet them as a bear that is robbed of her whelps, and I will rend the inner parts of their liver: and I will devour them there as a lion, the beast of the field shall tear them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:17; Jeremiah 2:19; Deuteronomy 33:26"> Destruction is thy own, O Israel: thy help is only in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 8:4; 1 Samuel 8:5–6; Hosea 10:3"> Where is thy king? now especially let him save thee in all thy cities: and thy judges, of whom thou saidst: Give me kings and princes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:19; 1 Samuel 15:22–23; 1 Kings 14:7–16"> I will give thee a king in my wrath, and will take him away in my indignation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:34–35; Job 14:17; Romans 2:5"> The iniquity of Ephraim is bound up, his sin is hidden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 37:3; Isaiah 13:8; Psalms 48:6"> The sorrows of a woman in labour snail come upon him, he is an unwise son: for now he shall not stand in the breach of the children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 25:8; Revelation 21:4; Isaiah 26:19"> I will deliver them out of the hand of death. I will redeem them from death: O death, I will be thy death; O hell, I will be thy bite: comfort is hidden from my eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:12; Ezekiel 17:10; Genesis 41:52"> Because he shall make a separation between brothers: the Lord will bring a burning wind that shall rise from the desert, and it shall dry up his springs, and shall make his fountain desolate, and he shall carry off the treasure of every desirable vessel.</span>
