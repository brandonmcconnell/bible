<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 25:36; Hebrews 10:34; Romans 12:15"> Remember them that are in bands, as if you were bound with them; and them that labour, as being yourselves also in the body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 2:24; Proverbs 5:15–23; Galatians 5:19"> Marriage honourable in all, and the bed undefiled. For fornicators and adulterers God will judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:8; Joshua 1:5; Deuteronomy 31:6"> Let your manners be without covetousness, contented with such things as you have; for he hath said: I will not leave thee, neither will I forsake thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 118:6–9; Isaiah 41:10; Romans 8:31"> So that we may confidently say: The Lord is my helper: I will not fear what man shall do to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:12–13; Hebrews 13:17; Philippians 3:17"> Remember your prelates who have spoken the word of God to you; whose faith follow, considering the end of their conversation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Malachi 3:6; James 1:17; Revelation 1:8"> Jesus Christ, yesterday, and today; and the same for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 4:14; 1 Timothy 6:3–5; Ephesians 5:6"> Be not led away with various and strange doctrines. For it is best that the heart be established with grace, not with meats; which have not profited those that walk in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:7–8; 1 Corinthians 10:20; 1 Corinthians 9:13"> We have an altar, whereof they have no power to eat who serve the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 16:27; Exodus 29:14; Leviticus 9:11"> For the bodies of those beasts, whose blood is brought into the holies by the high priest for sin, are burned without the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 9:12–14; John 19:17–18; Acts 7:58"> Wherefore Jesus also, that he might sanctify the people by his own blood, suffered without the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 11:26; 1 Peter 4:14–16; Acts 5:41"> Let us go forth therefore to him without the camp, bearing his reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Philippians 3:20; Colossians 3:1–3; Hebrews 11:12–16"> For we have not here a lasting city, but we seek one that is to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 107:21–22; Psalms 50:14; Romans 12:1"> By him therefore let us offer the sacrifice of praise always to God, that is to say, the fruit of lips confessing to his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:13; Galatians 6:10; Romans 12:13"> And do not forget to do good, and to impart; for by such sacrifices God’s favour is obtained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:12–13; 1 Timothy 5:17; Hebrews 13:7"> Obey your prelates, and be subject to them. For they watch as being to render an account of your souls; that they may do this with joy, and not with grief. For this is not expedient for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:25; 2 Thessalonians 3:1; Colossians 4:3"> Pray for us. For we trust we have a good conscience, being willing to behave ourselves well in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philemon 1:22; Romans 1:10–12; Romans 15:31–32"> And I beseech you the more to do this, that I may be restored to you the sooner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 10:11; Hebrews 10:22; Psalms 23:1"> And may the God of peace, who brought again from the dead the great pastor of the sheep, our Lord Jesus Christ, in the blood of the everlasting testament,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 2:13; 2 Thessalonians 2:17; Philippians 4:13"> Fit you in all goodness, that you may do his will; doing in you that which is well pleasing in his sight, through Jesus Christ, to whom is glory for ever and ever. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 3:1; 1 Peter 5:12; 2 Corinthians 6:1"> And I beseech you, brethren, that you suffer this word of consolation. For I have written to you in a few words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philemon 1:1; 1 Thessalonians 3:2; 2 Timothy 1:8"> Know ye that our brother Timothy is set at liberty: with whom (if he come shortly) I will see you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 13:7; Hebrews 13:17; Acts 18:2"> Salute all your prelates, and all the saints. The brethren from Italy salute you.</span>
