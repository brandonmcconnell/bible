<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 14:18–20; Psalms 57:2; Hebrews 6:20"> For this Melchisedech was king of Salem, priest of the most high God, who met Abraham returning from the slaughter of the kings, and blessed him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 18:21; Psalms 85:10–11; 1 Samuel 8:17"> To whom also Abraham divided the tithes of all: who first indeed by interpretation, is king of justice: and then also king of Salem, that is, king of peace:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 7:17; Hebrews 7:23–28; Exodus 6:20–27"> Without father, without mother, without genealogy, having neither beginning of days nor end of life, but likened unto the Son of God, continueth a priest for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 14:20; Acts 2:29; Acts 7:8–9"> Now consider how great this man is, to whom also Abraham the patriarch gave tithes out of the principal things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 27:30–33; 2 Chronicles 31:4–6; Numbers 18:21–32"> And indeed they that are of the sons of Levi, who receive the priesthood, have a commandment to take tithes of the people according to the law, that is to say, of their brethren: though they themselves also came out of the loins of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 4:13; Hebrews 7:3–4; Genesis 13:14–17"> But he, whose pedigree is not numbered among them, received tithes of Abraham, and blessed him that had the promises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:27; Genesis 48:15–20; 1 Timothy 3:16"> And without all contradiction, that which is less, is blessed by the better.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 6:20; Hebrews 5:6; Revelation 1:18"> And here indeed, men that die, receive thithes: but there he hath witness, that he liveth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 5:12; Genesis 14:20; Hebrews 7:4"> And (as it may be said) even Levi who received tithes, paid tithes in Abraham:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 35:11; Hebrews 7:5; Genesis 46:26"> For he was yet in the loins of his father, when Melchisedech met him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 8:7; Galatians 2:21; Galatians 4:9"> If then perfection was by the Levitical priesthood, (for under it the people received the law,) what further need was there that another priest should rise according to the order of Melchisedech, and not be called according to the order of Aaron?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Isaiah 66:21; Ezekiel 16:61"> For the priesthood being translated, it is necessary that a translation also be made of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 7:11; Hebrews 7:14; Numbers 17:5"> For he, of whom these things are spoken, is of another tribe, of which no one attended on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Isaiah 11:1; Luke 3:33"> For it is evident that our Lord sprung out of Juda: in which tribe Moses spoke nothing concerning priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 110:4; Hebrews 7:17–21; Hebrews 7:3"> And it is yet far more evident: if according to the similitude of Melchisedech there ariseth another priest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 1:18; Colossians 2:20; Hebrews 7:28"> Who is made not according to the law of a carnal commandment, but according to the power of an indissoluble life:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 110:4; Hebrews 5:6; Hebrews 7:21"> For he testifieth: Thou art a priest for ever, according to the order of Melchisedech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 8:3; Acts 13:39; Galatians 4:9"> There is indeed a setting aside of the former commandment, because of the weakness and unprofitableness thereof:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 13:39; Hebrews 4:16; Hebrews 9:9"> (For the law brought nothing to perfection,) but a bringing in of a better hope, by which we draw nigh to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 18:7; Hebrews 5:6; Psalms 110:4"> And inasmuch as it is not without an oath, (for the others indeed were made priests without an oath;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 110:4; Hebrews 7:17; Hebrews 6:16–18"> But this with an oath, by him that said unto him: The Lord hath sworn, and he will not repent, Thou art a priest for ever.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:25; Genesis 44:32; Hebrews 8:6–12"> By so much is Jesus made a surety of a better testament.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:10–11; 1 Chronicles 6:3–14; Hebrews 7:8"> And the others indeed were made many priests, because by reason of death they were not suffered to continue:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 12:34; Hebrews 7:28; Isaiah 9:6–7"> But this, for that he continueth for ever, hath an everlasting priesthood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 8:34; 1 John 2:1–2; 1 Timothy 2:5"> Whereby he is able also to save for ever them that come to God by him; always living to make intercession for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:21; Hebrews 4:14–15; 1 Peter 2:22"> For it was fitting that we should have such a high priest, holy, innocent, undefiled, separated from sinners, and made higher than the heavens;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 9:28; Hebrews 9:12; Hebrews 9:14"> Who needeth not daily (as the other priests) to offer sacrifices first for his own sins, and then for the people’s: for this he did once, in offering himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 2:10; Hebrews 7:21; Hebrews 1:2"> For the law maketh men priests, who have infirmity: but the word of the oath, which was since the law, the Son who is perfected for evermore.</span>
