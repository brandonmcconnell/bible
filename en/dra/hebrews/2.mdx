<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 8:15; 2 Peter 1:12–13; Proverbs 7:1–2"> Therefore ought we more diligently to observe the things which we have heard, lest perhaps we should let them slip.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 7:53; Galatians 3:19; Hebrews 10:28"> For if the word, spoken by angels, became steadfast, and every transgression and disobedience received a just recompense of reward:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 10:28–29; Hebrews 12:25; Hebrews 5:9"> How shall we escape if we neglect so great salvation? which having begun to be declared by the Lord, was confirmed unto us by them that heard him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 14:3; Ephesians 1:5; Mark 16:20"> God also bearing them witness by signs, and wonders, and divers miracles, and distributions of the Holy Ghost, according to his own will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 11:15; 2 Peter 3:13; Hebrews 6:5"> For God hath not subjected unto angels the world to come, whereof we speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 8:4–8; Psalms 144:3; Job 7:17–18"> But one in a certain place hath testified, saying: What is man, that thou art mindful of him: or the son of man, that thou visitest him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 8:5; Philippians 2:6–7"> Thou hast made him a little lower than the angels: thou hast crowned him with glory and honour, and hast set him over the works of thy hands:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 13:3; 1 Corinthians 15:27; Psalms 8:6"> Thou hast subjected all things under his feet. For in that he hath subjected all things to him, he left nothing not subject to him. But now we see not as yet all things subject to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philippians 2:7–9; Revelation 5:9; 1 John 4:9–10"> But we see Jesus, who was made a little lower than the angels, for the suffering of death, crowned with glory and honour: that, through the grace of God, he might taste death for all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Acts 5:31; Luke 24:46"> For it became him, for whom are all things, and by whom are all things, who had brought many children into glory, to perfect the author of their salvation, by his passion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:10; Hebrews 13:12; Hebrews 10:14"> For both he that sanctifieth, and they who are sanctified, are all of one. For which cause he is not ashamed to call them brethren, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 22:22; Psalms 40:10; John 18:20"> I will declare thy name to my brethren; in the midst of the church will I praise thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 8:17–18; Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 91:2"> And again: I will put my trust in him. And again: Behold I and my children, whom God hath given me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:10; Romans 8:3; Colossians 2:15"> Therefore because the children are partakers of flesh and blood, he also himself in like manner hath been partaker of the same: that, through death, he might destroy him who had the empire of death, that is to say, the devil:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 8:15; 2 Timothy 1:7; Luke 1:74–75"> And might deliver them, who through the fear of death were all their lifetime subject to servitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 3:29; Galatians 3:16; Romans 4:16–25"> For no where doth he take hold of the angels: but of the seed of Abraham he taketh hold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 4:14–2; Philippians 2:7–8; Hebrews 2:14"> Wherefore it behoved him in all things to be made like unto his brethren, that he might become a merciful and faithful priest before God, that he might be a propitiation for the sins of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; Hebrews 4:15–16; Jude 1:24"> For in that, wherein he himself hath suffered and been tempted, he is able to succour them also that are tempted.</span>
