<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 5:12–14; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Hebrews 9:14"> Wherefore leaving the word of the beginning of Christ, let us go on to things more perfect, not laying again the foundation of penance from dead works, and of faith towards God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:13–57; Acts 17:31–32; Romans 6:3–5"> Of the doctrine of baptisms, and imposition of hands, and of the resurrection of the dead, and of eternal judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 18:21; 1 Corinthians 16:7; 1 Corinthians 4:19"> And this will we do, if God permit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26–29; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Matthew 5:13"> For it is impossible for those who were once illuminated, have tasted also the heavenly gift, and were made partakers of the Holy Ghost,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 2:3; Luke 8:13; Matthew 13:20–21"> Have moreover tasted the good word of God, and the powers of the world to come,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Peter 2:21; Hebrews 10:29; Hebrews 6:4"> And are fallen away: to be renewed again to penance, crucifying again to themselves the Son of God, and making him a mockery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 10:12; Isaiah 44:3; Psalms 65:9–13"> For the earth that drinketh in the rain which cometh often upon it, and bringeth forth herbs meet for them by whom it is tilled, receiveth blessing from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 3:17–18; John 15:6; Luke 13:7–9"> But that which bringeth forth thorns and briers, is reprobate, and very near unto a curse, whose end is to be burnt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 10:39; 2 Corinthians 7:10; Hebrews 6:4–6"> But, my dearly beloved, we trust better things of you, and nearer to salvation; though we speak thus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 10:42; Hebrews 13:16; Galatians 6:10"> For God is not unjust, that he should forget your work, and the love which you have shewn in his name, you who have ministered, and do minister to the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 1:5–8; Hebrews 3:6; Romans 5:2–5"> And we desire that every one of you shew forth the same carefulness to the accomplishing of hope unto the end:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 13:7; Hebrews 10:36; 1 John 2:25"> That you become not slothful, but followers of them, who through faith and patience shall inherit the promises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 22:15–18; Luke 1:73; Jeremiah 22:5"> For God making promise to Abraham, because he had no one greater by whom he might swear, swore by himself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Deuteronomy 1:10; Nehemiah 9:23"> Saying: Unless blessing I shall bless thee, and multiplying I shall multiply thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 12:2–3; Romans 4:17–25; Exodus 1:7"> And so patiently enduring he obtained the promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 22:11; Hebrews 6:13; Genesis 31:53"> For men swear by one greater than themselves: and an oath for confirmation is the end of all their controversy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 6:18; Proverbs 19:21; Isaiah 55:11"> Wherein God, meaning more abundantly to shew to the heirs of the promise the immutability of his counsel, interposed an oath:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Titus 1:2; 1 Samuel 15:29"> That by two immutable things, in which it is impossible for God to lie, we may have the strongest comfort, who have fled for refuge to hold fast the hope set before us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 43:5; Romans 5:5–10; Hebrews 9:7"> Which we have as an anchor of the soul, sure and firm, and which entereth in even within the veil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 4:14; Hebrews 3:1; Hebrews 5:6"> Where the forerunner Jesus is entered for us, made a high priest for ever according to the order of Melchisedech.</span>
