<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 8:3; Hebrews 10:11; Hebrews 7:27"> For every high priest taken from among men, is ordained for men in the things that appertain to God, that he may offer up gifts and sacrifices for sins:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; Hebrews 2:18; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> Who can have compassion on them that are ignorant and that err: because he himself also is compassed with infirmity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 9:7; Hebrews 9:7; Leviticus 16:6"> And therefore he ought, as for the people, so also for himself, to offer for sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 28:1; Numbers 16:40; Numbers 16:5"> Neither doth any man take the honour to himself, but he that is called by God, as Aaron was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 2:7; John 8:54; Acts 13:33"> So Christ also did not glorify himself, that he might be made a high priest: but he that said unto him: Thou art my Son, this day have I begotten thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 110:4; Hebrews 7:17; Hebrews 7:21"> As he saith also in another place: Thou art a priest for ever, according to the order of Melchisedech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 14:32–39; Luke 22:41–44; John 17:1"> Who in the days of his flesh, with a strong cry and tears, offering up prayers and supplications to him that was able to save him from death, was heard for his reverence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 2:8; Hebrews 10:5–9; John 4:34"> And whereas indeed he was the Son of God, he learned obedience by the things which he suffered:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 2:10; Hebrews 9:12; Hebrews 9:15"> And being consummated, he became, to all that obey him, the cause of eternal salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 5:5–6; Hebrews 6:20"> Called by God a high priest according to the order of Melchisedech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 13:15; Acts 28:27; 2 Peter 3:16"> Of whom we have much to say, and hard to be intelligibly uttered: because you are become weak to hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 6:1; 1 Corinthians 3:1–3; 1 Peter 2:2"> For whereas for the time you ought to be masters, you have need to be taught again what are the first elements of the words of God: and you are become such as have need of milk, and not of strong meat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:20; 1 Peter 2:2; 2 Timothy 3:16"> For every one that is a partaker of milk, is unskillful in the word of justice: for he is a little child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14–15; 1 Thessalonians 5:21; Ephesians 1:18"> But strong meat is for the perfect; for them who by custom have their senses exercised to the discerning of good and evil.</span>
