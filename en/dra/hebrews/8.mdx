<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 1:3; Colossians 3:1; Revelation 3:21"> Now of the things which we have spoken, this is the sum: We have such an high priest, who is set on the right hand of the throne of majesty in the heavens,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 33:7; 2 Corinthians 5:1; Hebrews 9:23–24"> A minister of the holies, and of the true tabernacle, which the Lord hath pitched, and not man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Hebrews 5:1; Hebrews 9:14"> For every high priest is appointed to offer gifts and sacrifices: wherefore it is necessary that he also should have some thing to offer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 7:11–15; Hebrews 5:1; Numbers 17:12–13"> If then he were on earth, he would not be a priest: seeing that there would be others to offer gifts according to the law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Colossians 2:17; Hebrews 10:1"> Who serve unto the example and shadow of heavenly things. As it was answered to Moses, when he was to finish the tabernacle: See (saith he) that thou make all things according to the pattern which was shewn thee on the mount.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 7:22; Galatians 3:16–21; Hebrews 12:24"> But now he hath obtained a better ministry, by how much also he is a mediator of a better testament, which is established on better promises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 7:11; Galatians 3:21; Hebrews 7:18"> For if that former had been faultless, there should not indeed a place have been sought for a second.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Hebrews 10:16–17; Hebrews 12:24"> For finding fault with them, he saith: Behold, the days shall come, saith the Lord: and I will perfect unto the house of Israel, and unto the house of Juda, a new testament:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:32; Galatians 4:24; Mark 8:23"> Not according to the testament which I made to their fathers, on the day when I took them by the hand to lead them out of the land of Egypt: because they continued not in my testament: and I regarded them not, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:26–28; Jeremiah 31:33; Ezekiel 11:19–20"> For this is the testament which I will make to the house of Israel after those days, saith the Lord: I will give my laws into their mind, and in their heart will I write them: and I will be their God, and they shall be my people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; Isaiah 54:13; John 6:45"> And they shall not teach every man his neighbour and every man his brother, saying, Know the Lord: for all shall know me from the least to the greatest of them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 10:16–17; Isaiah 43:25; Romans 11:27"> Because I will be merciful to their iniquities, and their sins I will remember no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:17; Hebrews 7:18–19; Hebrews 7:11–12"> Now in saying a new, he hath made the former old. And that which decayeth and groweth old, is near its end.</span>
