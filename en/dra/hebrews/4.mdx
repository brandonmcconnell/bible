<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 12:15; Hebrews 4:9; Proverbs 28:14"> Let us fear therefore lest the promise being left of entering into his rest, any of you should be thought to be wanting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:13; Romans 10:16–17; Hebrews 11:6"> For unto us also it hath been declared, in like manner as unto them. But the word of hearing did not profit them, not being mixed with faith of those things they heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 95:11; Matthew 11:28–29; 1 Peter 1:20"> For we, who have believed, shall enter into rest; as he said: As I have sworn in my wrath; If they shall enter into my rest; and this indeed when the works from the foundation of the world were finished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 20:11; Exodus 31:17; Genesis 2:1–2"> For in a certain place he spoke of the seventh day thus: And God rested the seventh day from all his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 95:11; Hebrews 4:3; Hebrews 3:11"> And in this place again: If they shall enter into my rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 21:43; Hebrews 3:18–19; Acts 13:46–47"> Seeing then it remaineth that some are to enter into it, and they, to whom it was first preached, did not enter because of unbelief:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 95:7; Hebrews 3:7–8; Hebrews 3:15"> Again he limiteth a certain day, saying in David, Today, after so long a time, as it is above said: Today if you shall hear his voice, harden not your hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 1:15; Joshua 22:4; Deuteronomy 12:9"> For if Jesus had given them rest, he would never have afterwards spoken of another day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 2:10; Revelation 21:4; Revelation 7:14–17"> There remaineth therefore a day of rest for the people of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Hebrews 4:3–4; 1 Peter 4:1–2"> For he that is entered into his rest, the same also hath rested from his works, as God did from his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 11:28–30; Luke 13:24; Hebrews 3:18–1"> Let us hasten therefore to enter into that rest; lest any man fall into the same example of unbelief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:29; Isaiah 55:11; Ephesians 6:17"> For the word of God is living and effectual, and more piercing than any two edged sword; and reaching unto the division of the soul and the spirit, of the joints also and the marrow, and is a discerner of the thoughts and intents of the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:5; Psalms 33:13–15; Proverbs 15:3"> Neither is there any creature invisible in his sight: but all things are naked and open to his eyes, to whom our speech is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; Hebrews 12:2; Hebrews 9:12"> Having therefore a great high priest that hath passed into the heavens, Jesus the Son of God: let us hold fast our confession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17–18; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Isaiah 53:4–5"> For we have not a high priest, who can not have compassion on our infirmities: but one tempted in all things like as we are, without sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ephesians 3:12; Hebrews 7:25; Hebrews 10:19–23"> Let us go therefore with confidence to the throne of grace: that we may obtain mercy, and find grace in seasonable aid.</span>
