<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 4:8; 2 Chronicles 24:20; 2 Chronicles 15:4"> And he went out to meet Asa, and said to him: Hear ye me, Asa, and all Juda and Benjamin: The Lord is with you, because you have been with him. If you seek him, you shall find: but if you forsake him, he will forsake you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:9; Leviticus 10:11; 2 Chronicles 17:8–9"> And many days shall pass in Israel without the true God, and without a priest a teacher, and without the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 55:6; Deuteronomy 4:29–30; 2 Chronicles 15:15"> And when in their distress they shall return to the Lord the God of Israel, and shall seek him, they shall find him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 5:6; Psalms 121:8; 1 Samuel 13:6"> At that time there shall be no peace to him that goeth out and cometh in, but terrors on every side among all the inhabitants of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 21:22–24; Matthew 24:7; 2 Chronicles 33:11"> For nation shall fight against nation, and city against city, for the Lord will trouble them with all distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; Joshua 1:7; Joshua 1:9"> Do you therefore take courage, and let not your hands he weakened: for there shall be a reward for your work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:12; 2 Chronicles 13:19; 2 Chronicles 4:1"> And when Asa had heard the words, and the prophecy of Azarias the son of Oded the prophet, he took courage, and took away the idols out of all the land of Juda, and out of Benjamin, and out of the cities of mount Ephraim, which he had taken, and he dedicated the altar of the Lord, which was before the porch of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:16; Acts 9:31; Zechariah 8:21–23"> And he gathered together all Juda and Benjamin, and the strangers with them of Ephraim, and Manasses, and Simeon: for many were come over to him out of Israel, seeing that the Lord his God was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esther 8:9"> And when they were come to Jerusalem in the third month, in the fifteenth year of the reign of Asa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:13–15; Numbers 31:28–29; Numbers 31:50"> They sacrificed to the Lord in that day of the spoils, and of the prey, that they had brought, seven hundred oxen, and seven thousand rams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 24:14; 2 Chronicles 23:16; Deuteronomy 10:12"> And he went in to confirm as usual the covenant, that they should seek the Lord the God of their fathers with all their heart, and with all their soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 22:20; Psalms 115:13; 1 Kings 18:40"> And if any one, said he, seek not the Lord the God of Israel, let him die, whether little or great, man or woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:13; Psalms 81:1–4; Nehemiah 10:29"> And they swore to the Lord with a loud voice with joyful shouting, and with sound of trumpet, and sound of comets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:7; 2 Chronicles 15:12; Isaiah 26:8"> All that mere in Juda with a curse: for with all their heart they swore, and with all their will they sought him, and they found him, and the Lord gave them rest round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 15:13–24; 1 Kings 15:10; 2 Kings 23:15"> Moreover Maacha the mother of king Asa he deposed from the royal authority, because she had made in a grove an idol of Priapus: and he entirely destroyed it, and breaking it into pieces, burnt it at the torrent Cedron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 14:4; 2 Kings 12:3; 1 Kings 11:4"> But high places were left in Israel: nevertheless the heart of Asa was perfect all his days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 7:51; 1 Chronicles 26:20–26; 1 Kings 15:14–15"> And the things which his father had vowed, and he himself had vowed, he brought into the house of the Lord, gold and silver, and vessels of divers uses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 15:31; 1 Kings 15:33; 1 Kings 15:16–17"> And there was no war unto the five and thirtieth year of the kingdom of Asa.</span>
