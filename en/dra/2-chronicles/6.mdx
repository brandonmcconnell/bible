<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 18:8–11; Leviticus 16:2; Exodus 20:21"> Then Solomon said: The Lord promised that he would dwell in a cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:4–6; Psalms 135:21; 1 Chronicles 28:6"> But I have built a house to his name, that he might dwell there for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 8:14; 2 Chronicles 29:29; 1 Kings 8:55–61"> And the king turned his face, and blessed all the multitude of Israel (for all the multitude stood attentive) and he said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 72:18–19; Matthew 24:35; Psalms 68:32–35"> Blessed be the Lord the God of Israel, who hath accomplished in deed that which he spoke to David my father, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 20:24; 1 Kings 8:16; 1 Samuel 13:13–14"> From the day that I brought my people out of the land of Egypt, I chose no city among all the tribes of Israel, for a house to be built in it to my name: neither chose I any other man, to be the ruler of my people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:4; Psalms 132:13; 2 Chronicles 12:13"> But I chose Jerusalem, that my name might be there: and I chose David to set him over my people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:1; 1 Kings 5:3; 1 Chronicles 22:7"> And whereas David my father had a mind to build a house to the name of the Lord the God of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; Mark 14:8; 1 Kings 8:18–21"> The Lord said to him: Forasmuch as it was thy will to build a house to my name, thou hast done well indeed in having such a will:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:12–13; 1 Chronicles 17:4; 1 Chronicles 17:11–12"> But thou shalt not build the house, but thy son, who shall come out of thy loins, he shall build a house to my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:4; 1 Chronicles 28:5; 1 Chronicles 17:11"> The Lord therefore hath accomplished his word which he spoke: and I am risen up in the place of David my father, and sit upon the throne of Israel, as the Lord promised: and have built a house to the name of the Lord God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 5:10; 2 Chronicles 5:7; 1 Kings 8:21"> And I have put in it the ark, wherein is the covenant of the Lord, which he made with the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 63:4; Isaiah 50:10; Job 11:13"> And he stood before the altar of the I Lord, in presence of all the multitude of Israel, and stretched forth his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 8:54; Nehemiah 8:4; Psalms 95:6"> For Solomon had made a brazen scaffold, and had set it in the midst of the temple, which was five cubits long, and five cubits broad, and three cubits high: and he stood upon it: then kneeling down in the presence of all the multitude of Israel, and lifting up his hands towards heaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 15:11; Deuteronomy 7:9; Nehemiah 1:5"> He said: O Lord God of Israel, there is no God like thee in heaven nor in earth: who keepest covenant and mercy with thy servants, that walk before thee with all their hearts:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:9–10; 2 Samuel 7:12; 1 Kings 8:24"> Who hast performed to thy servant David my father all that thou hast promised him: and hast accomplished in fact, what thou hast spoken with thy mouth, as also the present time proveth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 2:4; John 15:14–15; 2 Chronicles 7:18"> Now then, O Lord God of Israel, fulfill to thy servant David my father, whatsoever thou hast promised him, saying: There shall not fail thee a man in my sight, to sit upon the throne of Israel: yet so that thy children take heed to their ways, and walk in my law, as thou hast walked before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:5; Isaiah 41:17; 2 Chronicles 6:4"> And now, Lord God of Israel, let thy word be established which thou hast spoken to thy servant David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:6; Isaiah 66:1; Psalms 139:7–10"> Is it credible then that God should dwell with men on the earth? If heaven and the heavens of heavens do not contain thee, how much less this house, which I have built?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 8:28; Luke 18:1–7; John 17:20"> But to this end only it is made, that thou mayest regard the prayer of thy servant and his supplication, O Lord my God: and mayest hear the prayers which thy servant poureth out before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 34:15; Daniel 6:10; Psalms 121:5"> That thou mayest open thy eyes upon this house day and night, upon the place wherein thou hast promised that thy name should be called upon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Micah 7:18; Daniel 9:19; Isaiah 43:25"> And that thou wouldst hear the prayer which thy servant prayeth in it: hearken then to the prayers of thy servant, and of thy people Israel. Whosoever shall pray in this place, hear thou from thy dwelling place, that is, from heaven, and shew mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 22:11; Leviticus 5:1; Numbers 5:19–22"> If any man sin against his neighbour, and come to swear against him, and bind himself with a curse before the altar in this house:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 3:10–11; Proverbs 17:15; Jeremiah 51:56"> Then hear thou from heaven, and do justice to thy servants, so as to requite the wicked by making his wickedness fall upon his own head, and to revenge the just, rewarding him according to his justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 26:17; 2 Kings 17:7–18; Joshua 7:8"> If thy people Israel be overcome by their enemies, (for they will sin against thee,) and being converted shall do penance, and call upon thy name, and pray to thee in this place,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:6–13; Ezra 1:1–6; Genesis 13:15"> Then hear thou from heaven, and forgive the sin of thy people Israel, and bring them back into the land, which thou gavest to them, and their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:23; Deuteronomy 11:17; Leviticus 26:19"> If the heavens be shut up, and there fall no rain by reason of the sins of the people, and they shall pray to thee in this place, and confess to thy name, and be converted from their sins, when thou dost afflict them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Kings 8:35–36; Jeremiah 5:24; Zechariah 10:1"> Then hear thou from heaven, O Lord, and forgive the sins of thy servants and of thy people Israel, and teach them the good way, in which they may walk: and give rain to thy land which thou hast given to thy people to possess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 9:3–11; Joel 2:25; Ruth 1:1"> If a famine arise in the land, or a pestilence or blasting, or mildew, or locusts, or caterpillars: or if their enemies waste the country, and besiege the cities, whatsoever scourge or infirmity shall be upon them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Psalms 32:2–6; 2 Chronicles 6:12–13"> Then if any of thy people Israel, knowing his own scourge and infirmity shall pray, and shall spread forth his hands in this house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:9; 1 Samuel 16:7; Revelation 2:23"> Hear thou from heaven, from thy high dwelling place, and forgive, and render to every one according to his ways, which thou knowest him to have in his heart: (for thou only knowest the hearts of the children of men:)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 9:31; Job 28:28; Psalms 128:1"> That they may fear thee, and walk in thy ways all the days that they live upon the face of the land, which thou hast given to our fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 56:3–7; 1 Kings 8:41–43; Matthew 8:10–11"> If the stranger also, who is not of thy people Israel, come from a far country, for the sake of thy great name, and thy strong hand, and thy stretched out arm, and adore in this place:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:14; 1 Kings 8:16; Revelation 11:15"> Hear thou from heaven thy firm dwelling place, and do all that which that stranger shall call upon thee for: that all the people of the earth may know thy name, and may fear thee, as thy people Israel, and may know, that thy name is invoked upon this house, which I have built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:6–13; 2 Chronicles 18:31; 2 Chronicles 20:4"> If thy people go out to war against their enemies, by the way that thou shalt send them, and adore thee towards the way of this city, which thou hast chosen, and the house which I have built to thy name:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Daniel 9:17–19; Psalms 9:3–4; Jeremiah 5:28"> Then hear thou from heaven their prayers, and their supplications, and revenge them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; James 3:2; 1 John 1:8–10"> And if they sin against thee (for there is no man that sinneth not) and thou be angry with them, and deliver them up to their enemies, and they lead them away captive to a land either afar off, or near at hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:18–20; Daniel 9:5–11; Isaiah 64:6–12"> And if they be converted in their heart in the land to which they were led captive, and do penance, and pray to thee in the land of their captivity, saying: We have sinned, we have done wickedly, we have dealt unjustly:</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:11–13; Daniel 6:10; Daniel 9:3–4"> And return to thee with all their heart, and with all their soul, in the land of their captivity, to which they were led away, and adore thee towards the way of their own land which thou gavest their fathers, and of the city, which thou hast chosen, and the house which I have built to thy name:</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Micah 7:18–20; 2 Chronicles 6:35; Psalms 25:18"> Then hear thou from heaven, that is, from thy firm dwelling place, their prayers, and do judgment, and forgive thy people, although they have sinned:</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:15; Psalms 116:2; Nehemiah 1:6"> For thou art my God: let thy eyes, I beseech thee, be open, and let thy ears be attentive to the prayer, that is made in this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; 1 Chronicles 28:2; Psalms 132:8–10"> Now therefore arise, O Lord God, into thy resting place, thou and the ark of thy strength: let thy priests, O Lord God, put on salvation, and thy saints rejoice in good things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Isaiah 55:3; Psalms 132:1; Isaiah 61:1"> O Lord God, turn not away the face of thy anointed: remember the mercies of David thy servant.</span>
