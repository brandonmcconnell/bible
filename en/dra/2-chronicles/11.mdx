<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 12:21–24; Psalms 33:10; Psalms 33:16"> And Roboam came to Jerusalem, and called together all the house of Juda and of Benjamin, a hundred and fourscore thousand chosen men and warriors, to fight against Israel, and to bring back his kingdom to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:15; 2 Chronicles 12:5–7; 2 Chronicles 8:14"> And the word of the Lord came to Semeias the man of God, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 17:34; Genesis 49:28; Revelation 7:4–8"> Speak to Roboam the son of Solomon the king of Juda, and to all Israel, in Juda and Benjamin:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 50:20; 1 Kings 11:29–38; 2 Chronicles 10:15–16"> Thus saith the Lord: You shall not go up, nor fight against your brethren: let every man return to his own house, for by my will this thing has been done. And when they heard the word of the Lord, they returned, and did not go against Jeroboam,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:2–6; 2 Chronicles 14:6–7; Isaiah 22:8–11"> And Roboam dwelt in Jerusalem, and built walled cities in Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:5; Amos 1:1; 1 Chronicles 4:32"> And he built Bethlehem, and Etam, and Thecue,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 15:35; 1 Samuel 22:1; Joshua 15:58"> And Bethsur, and Socho, and Odollam,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 54:1; Joshua 15:24; 1 Samuel 23:19"> And Geth, and Maresa, and Ziph,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 15:39; Joshua 15:35; Joshua 10:5"> And Aduram, and Lachis, and Azecha,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 13:22; Joshua 15:33; Joshua 14:14"> Saraa also, and Aialon, and Hebron, which are in Juda and Benjamin, well fenced cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:19; Isaiah 22:10–11; 2 Chronicles 11:23"> And when he had enclosed them with walls, he put in them governors and storehouses of provisions, that is, of oil and of wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:22; 2 Chronicles 26:14–15; 2 Chronicles 32:5"> Moreover in every city he made an armoury of shields and spears, and he fortified them with great diligence, and he reigned over Juda, and Benjamin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the priests and Levites, that were in all Israel, came to him out of all their seats,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:9; Numbers 35:2–5; 1 Kings 12:28–33"> Leaving their suburbs, and their possessions, and passing over to Juda, and Jerusalem, because Jeroboam and his sons had cast them off, from executing the priestly office to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 12:28; 1 Kings 12:31; 1 Kings 13:33"> And he made to himself priests for the high places, and for the devils, and for the calves which he had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:9; Acts 11:23; Psalms 84:5–7"> Moreover out of all the tribes of Israel, whosoever gave their heart to seek the Lord the God of Israel, came into Jerusalem to sacrifice their victims before the Lord the God of their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:1; 2 Chronicles 1:1–12; 2 Chronicles 7:17–19"> And they strengthened the kingdom of Juda, and established Roboam the son of Solomon for three years: for they walked in the ways of David and of Solomon, only three years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:6; 1 Chronicles 27:18; 1 Samuel 17:13"> And Roboam took to wife Mahalath, the daughter of Jerimoth the son of David: and Abihail the daughter of Eliab the son of Isai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And they bore him sons Jehus, and Somorias, and Zoom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:2; 2 Chronicles 11:21; 2 Chronicles 12:16"> And after her he married Maacha the daughter of Absalom, who bore him Abia and Ethai, and Ziza, and Salomith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 8:30; Deuteronomy 17:17; 1 Kings 11:3"> And Roboam loved Maacha the daughter of Absalom above all his wives, and concubines: for he had married eighteen wives, and threescore concubines: and he beget eight and twenty sons, and threescore daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:15–17; 1 Chronicles 29:1; 1 Chronicles 5:1–2"> But he put at the head of them Abia the son of Maacha to be the chief ruler over all his brethren: for he meant to make him king,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:3; Genesis 25:6; Luke 16:8"> Because he was wiser and mightier than all his sons, and in all the countries of Juda, and of Benjamin, and in all the walled cities: and he gave them provisions in abundance, and he sought many wives.</span>
