<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 15:24; Ephesians 6:10; Matthew 1:8"> And Josaphat his son reigned in his stead, and grew strong against Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:8; 2 Chronicles 11:5; 2 Chronicles 11:11–12"> And he placed numbers of soldiers in all the fortified cities of Juda. And he put garrisons in the land of Juda, and in the cities of Ephraim, which Asa his father had taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 22:2; Isaiah 41:10; 2 Timothy 4:22"> And the Lord was with Josaphat, because he walked in the first ways of David his father: and trusted not in Baalim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 12:28; Luke 1:6; 1 Thessalonians 2:12"> But in the God of his father, and walk in his commandments, and not according to the sins of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 18:1; Matthew 6:33; Psalms 127:1"> And the Lord established the kingdom in his hand, and all Juda brought presents to Josaphat: and he acquired immense riches, and much glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 14:9; 2 Chronicles 15:17; 2 Chronicles 20:33"> And when his heart had taken courage for the ways of the Lord, he took away also the high places and the groves out of Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:3; Nehemiah 8:7–8; Ecclesiastes 12:9–10"> And in the third year of his reign, he sent of his princes Benhail, and Abdias, and Zacharias, and Nathanael, and Micheas, to teach in the cities of Juda:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 7:1–6; 2 Chronicles 19:8; Malachi 2:7"> And with them the Levites, Semeias, and Nathanias, and Zabadias, and Asael, and Semiramoth, and Jonathan, and Adonias, and Tobias, and Thobadonias Levites, and with them Elisama, and Joram priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 5:46; Romans 3:2; Acts 8:40"> And they taught the people in Juda, having with them the book of the law of the Lord: and they went about all the cities of Juda, and instructed the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:14; Exodus 15:14–16; Proverbs 16:7"> And the fear of the Lord came upon all the kingdoms of the lands that were round about Juda, and they durst not make war against Josaphat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:8; 2 Chronicles 9:14; 2 Samuel 8:2"> The Philistines also brought presents to Josaphat, and tribute in silver, and the Arabians brought him cattle, seven thousand seven hundred rams, and as many he goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:2–6; 2 Chronicles 14:6–7; 2 Chronicles 32:5"> And Josaphat grew, and became exceeding great: and he built in Juda houses like towers, and walled cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:25–31; 2 Chronicles 26:10–15"> And he prepared many works in the cities of Juda: and he had warriors, and valiant men in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:1; Genesis 15:5; Numbers 1:18"> Of whom this is the number of the houses and families of every one: in Juda captains of the army, Ednas the chief, and with him three hundred thousand most valiant men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> After him Johanan the captain, and with him two hundred and eighty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 5:9; Judges 5:2; 1 Chronicles 29:9"> And after him was Amasias the son of Zechri, consecrated to the Lord, and with him were two hundred thousand valiant men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:8; 2 Samuel 1:21–22"> After him was Eliada valiant in battle, and with him two hundred thousand armed with bow and shield.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> After him also was Jozabad, and with him a hundred and eighty thousand ready for war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:2; 2 Chronicles 11:12; 2 Chronicles 11:23"> All these were at the hand of the king, beside others, whom he had put in the walled cities, in all Juda.</span>
