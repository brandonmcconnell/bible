<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:11; 2 Chronicles 33:25; 2 Chronicles 26:1"> Then the people of the land took Joachaz the son of Josias, and made him king instead of his father in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Joachaz was three and twenty years old when he began to reign, and he reigned three months in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 23:33"> And the king of Egypt came to Jerusalem, and deposed him, and condemned the land in a hundred talents of silver, and a talent of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:10–12; 2 Kings 23:34–35; Ezekiel 19:3–4"> And he made Eliakim his brother king in his stead, over Juda and Jerusalem: and he turned his name to Joakim: but he took Joachaz with him, and carried him away into Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 23:36–37; Jeremiah 22:13–19; Jeremiah 26:1"> Joakim was five and twenty years old when he began to reign, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem: and he did evil before the Lord his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:11; 2 Kings 24:5–6; Ezekiel 19:5–9"> Against him came up Nabuchodonosor king of the Chaldeans, and led him bound in chains into Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 24:13; Daniel 5:2–4; Jeremiah 27:16–18"> And he carried also thither the vessels of the Lord, and put them in his temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 24:5–6; Matthew 1:11–12; 1 Chronicles 3:16–17"> But the rest of the acts of Joakim, and his abominations, which he wrought, and the things that were found in him, are contained in the book of the kings of Juda and Israel. And Joachin his son reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 24:8–17"> Joachin was eight years old when he began to reign, and he reigned three months and ten days in Jerusalem, and he did evil in the sight of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:1; 2 Chronicles 36:7; 2 Kings 24:10–17"> And at the return of the year, king Nabuchodonosor sent, and brought him to Babylon, carrying away at the same time the most precious vessels of the house of the Lord: and he made Sedecias his uncle king over Juda and Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 24:18–20; Jeremiah 52:1–3; Jeremiah 27:1"> Sedecias was one and twenty years old when he began to reign: and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:23; Jeremiah 37:2–21; Jeremiah 38:14–28"> And he did evil in the eyes of the Lord his God, and did not reverence the face of Jeremias the prophet speaking to him from the mouth of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:2–3; 2 Chronicles 30:8; Ezekiel 17:11–20"> He also revolted from king Nabuchodonosor, who had made him swear by God: and he hardened his neck and his heart, from returning to the Lord the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:8; Ezekiel 22:6; Micah 3:9–11"> Moreover all the chief of the priests, and the people wickedly transgressed according to all the abominations of the Gentiles: and they defiled the house of the Lord, which he had sanctified to himself in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 35:15; Jeremiah 7:13; Jeremiah 25:3–4"> And the Lord the God of their fathers sent to them, by the hand of his messengers, rising early, and daily admonishing them: because he spared his people and his dwelling place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:12–13; 2 Chronicles 30:10; Luke 22:63–64"> But they mocked the messengers of God, and despised his words, and misused the prophets, until the wrath of the Lord arose against his people, and there was no remedy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 74:20; Ezra 9:7; Ezekiel 9:5–7"> For he brought upon them the king of the Chaldeans, and he slew their young men with the sword in the house of his sanctuary, he had no compassion on young man, or maiden, old man or even him that stooped for age, but he delivered them all into his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:7; 2 Chronicles 36:10; Isaiah 39:6"> And all the vessels of the house of the Lord, great and small, and the treasures of the temple and of the king, and of the princes he carried away to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 79:1; 1 Kings 9:8; Jeremiah 52:13–15"> And the enemies set fire to the house of God, and broke down the wall of Jerusalem, burnt all the towers, and whatsoever was precious they destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:7; Ezra 1:1–11; 2 Chronicles 36:22"> Whosoever escaped the sword, was led into Babylon, and there served the king and his sons till the reign of the king of Persia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:10; Leviticus 26:34–35; Zechariah 1:12"> That the word of the Lord by the mouth of Jeremias might be fulfilled, and the land might keep her sabbaths: for all the days of the desolation she kept a sabbath, till the seventy years were expired.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:12; Ezra 1:1–3; Jeremiah 29:10"> But in the first year of Cyrus king of the Persians, to fulfill the word of the Lord, which he had spoken by the mouth of Jeremias, the Lord stirred up the heart of Cyrus king of the Persians who commanded it to be proclaimed through all his kingdom, and by writing also, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 44:26–28; Ezra 7:13; Romans 8:31"> Thus saith Cyrus king of the Persians: All the kingdoms of the earth hath the Lord the God of heaven given to me, and he hath charged me to build him a house in Jerusalem, which is in Judea: who is there among you of all his people? The Lord his God be with him, and let him go up.</span>
