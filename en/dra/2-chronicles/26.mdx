<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:1; Matthew 1:8–9; 2 Chronicles 33:25"> And all the people of Juda took his son Ozias, who was sixteen years old, and made him king in the room of Amasias his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 16:6; 2 Chronicles 25:28; 2 Chronicles 25:23"> He built Ailath, and restored it to the dominion of Juda, after that the king slept with his fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 15:2–3; Amos 1:1; Hosea 1:1"> Ozias was sixteen years old when he began to reign, and he reigned two and fifty years in Jerusalem: the name of his mother was Jechelia of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:2"> And he did that which was right in the eyes of the Lord, according to all that Amasias his father had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; 2 Chronicles 24:2; 1 Chronicles 22:13"> And he sought the Lord in the days of Zacharias that understood and saw God: and as long as he sought the Lord, he directed him in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 14:29; 1 Samuel 5:6; 1 Samuel 5:1"> Moreover he went forth and fought against the Philistines, and broke down the wall of Geth, and the wall of Jabnia, and the wall of Azotus: and he built towns in Azotus, and among the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:16; 1 Chronicles 5:20; 2 Chronicles 14:11"> And God helped him against the Philistines, and against the Arabians, that dwelt in Gurbaal, and against the Ammonites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:2; 2 Chronicles 17:11; Genesis 19:38"> And the ammonites gave gifts to Ozias: and his name was spread abroad even to the entrance of Egypt for his frequent victories.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:13; 2 Chronicles 25:23; 2 Kings 14:13"> And Ozias built towers in Jerusalem over the gate of the corner, and over the gate of the valley, and the rest, in the same side of the wall, and fortified them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 26:18–21; Isaiah 29:17; 2 Kings 3:4"> And he built towers in the wilderness, and dug many cisterns, for he had much cattle both in the plains, and in the waste of the desert: he had also vineyards and dressers of vines in the mountains, and in Carmel: for he was a man that loved husbandry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 5:2"> And the army of his fighting men, that went out to war, was under the hand of Jehiel the scribe, and Maasias the doctor, and under the hand of Henanias, who was one of the king’s captains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And the whole number of the chiefs by the families of valiant men were two thousand six hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:5; 2 Chronicles 14:8; 2 Chronicles 11:1"> And the whole army under them three hundred and seven thousand five hundred: who were fit for war, and fought for the king against the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 20:16; 1 Samuel 17:49"> And Ozias prepared for them, that is, for the whole army, shields, and spears, and helmets, and coats of mail, and bows, and slings to cast stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 31:4; Matthew 4:24; 2 Chronicles 2:7"> And he made in Jerusalem engines of diverse kinds, which he placed in the towers, and in the corners of the walls, to shoot arrows, and great stones: and his name went forth far abroad, for the Lord helped him, and had strengthened him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:25; 2 Chronicles 25:19; Proverbs 16:18"> But when he was made strong, his heart was lifted up to his destruction, and he neglected the Lord his God: and going into the temple of the Lord, he had a mind to burn incense upon the altar of incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:10; 1 Chronicles 26:6; 1 Chronicles 12:28"> And immediately Azarias the priest going in after him, and with him fourscore priests of the Lord, most valiant men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 30:7–8; Numbers 18:7; Jeremiah 13:18"> Withstood the king and said: It doth not belong to thee, Ozias, to burn incense to the Lord, but to the priests, that is, to the sons of Aaron, who are consecrated for this ministry: go out of the sanctuary, do not despise: for this thing shall not be accounted to thy glory by the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 12:10; 2 Chronicles 25:16; 2 Kings 5:25–27"> And Ozias was angry, and holding in his hand the censer to burn incense, threatened the priests. And presently there rose a leprosy in his forehead before the priests, in the house of the Lord at the altar of incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:22; Esther 6:12; Leviticus 14:34"> And Azarias the high priest, and all the rest of the priests looked upon him, and saw the leprosy in his forehead, and they made haste to thrust him out. Yea himself also being frightened, hasted to go out, because he had quickly felt the stroke of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 13:46; Numbers 12:15; 2 Kings 7:3"> And Ozias the king was a leper unto the day of his death, and he dwelt in a house apart being full of the leprosy, for which he had been cast out of the house of the Lord. And Joatham his son governed the king’s house, and judged the people of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 1:1; Isaiah 6:1; 2 Chronicles 9:29"> But the rest of the acts of Ozias first and last were written by Isaias the son of Amos, the prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:27; 2 Chronicles 21:20; 2 Chronicles 26:18"> And Ozias slept with his fathers, and they buried him in the field of the royal sepulchres, because he was a leper: and Joatham his son reigned in his stead.</span>
