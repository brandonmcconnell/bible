<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:10; Ezekiel 10:14; 1 Corinthians 9:9–10"> And under it there was the likeness of oxen, and certain engravings on the outside of ten cubits compassed the belly of the sea, as it were with two rows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 28:19–20; Revelation 21:14; Mark 16:15"> And the oxen were cast: and the sea itself was set upon the twelve oxen, three of which looked toward the north, and other three toward the west: and other three toward the south, and the other three that remained toward the east, and the sea stood upon them: and the hinder parts of the oxen were inward under the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 7:26"> Now the thickness of it was a handbreadth, and the brim of it was like the brim of a cup, or of a crisped lily: and it held three thousand measures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 7:38; Exodus 30:18–21; 1 Kings 7:40"> He made also ten lavers: and he see five on the right hand, and five on the left, to wash in them all such things as they mere to offer for holocausts: but the sea was for the priests to wash in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–40; 1 Kings 7:49; John 8:12"> And he made ten golden candlesticks, according to the form which they were commanded to be made by: and he set them in the temple, five on the right hand, and five on the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 7:48; Malachi 1:12; Exodus 25:23–30"> Moreover also ten tables: and he set them in the temple, five on the right side, and five on the left. Also a hundred bowls of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 6:36; 2 Kings 21:5; 1 Kings 7:12"> He made also the court of the priests, and a great hall, and doors in the hall, which he covered with brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 7:39"> And he set the sea on the right side over against the east toward the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 7:40–51; 1 Kings 7:14"> And Hiram made caldrons, and fleshhooks, and bowls: and finished all the king’s work in the house of God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 7:41; 2 Chronicles 3:15–17"> That is to say, the two pillars, and the pommels, and the chapiters, and the network, to cover the chapiters over the pommels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 7:20; 1 Kings 7:42; Jeremiah 52:23"> And four hundred pomegranates, and two wreaths of network, so that two rows of pomegranates were joined to each wreath, to cover the pommels, and the chapiters of the pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 7:27–43"> He made also bases, and lavers, which he set upon the bases:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> One sea, and twelve oxen under the sea;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:13; 1 Kings 7:13–14; 2 Chronicles 4:11"> And the caldrons, and fleshhooks, and bowls. All the vessels did Hiram his father make for Solomon in the house of the Lord of the finest brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 7:46"> In the country near the Jordan did the king cast them, in a clay ground between Sochot and Saredatha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 7:47; Jeremiah 52:20; 1 Chronicles 22:14"> And the multitude of vessels was innumerable, so that the weight of the brass was not known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 9:13; Daniel 5:2–3; Exodus 25:23–30"> And Solomon made all the vessels for the house of God, and the golden altar, and the tables, upon which were the leaves of proposition,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:7; Exodus 25:31–37; 1 Kings 6:5"> The candlesticks also of most pure gold with their lamps to give light before the oracle, according to the manner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 6:29; Exodus 37:20; 1 Kings 6:35"> And certain flowers, and lamps, and golden tongs: all were made of the finest gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Kings 12:13; 2 Kings 25:14; Jeremiah 52:18"> The vessels also for the perfumes, and the censers, and the bowls, and the mortars, of pure gold. And he graved the doors of the inner temple, that is, for the holy of holies: and the doors of the temple without were of gold. And thus all the work was finished which Solomon made in the house of the Lord.</span>
