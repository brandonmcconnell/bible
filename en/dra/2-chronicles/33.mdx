<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 21:1–18; 1 Chronicles 3:13; Isaiah 3:12"> Manasses was twelve years old when he began to reign, and he reigned fifty-five years in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:3; Deuteronomy 18:9; 2 Kings 17:11"> And he did evil before the Lord, according to all the abominations of the nations, which the Lord cast out before the children of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:21; Deuteronomy 17:3; 2 Chronicles 31:1"> And he turned, and built again the high places which Ezechias his father had destroyed: and he built altars to Baalim, and made groves, and he adored all the host of heaven, and worshipped them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:16; 1 Kings 8:29; Deuteronomy 12:11"> He built also altars in the house of the Lord, whereof the Lord had said: In Jerusalem shall my name be for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:9; Jeremiah 32:34–35; Ezekiel 8:7–18"> And he built them for all the host of heaven in the two courts of the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 18:21; Leviticus 20:6; 2 Chronicles 28:3"> And he made his sons to pass through the fire in the valley of Benennom: he observed dreams, followed divinations, gave himself up to magic arts, had with him magicians, and enchanters: and he wrought many evils before the Lord, to provoke him to anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:4; 2 Chronicles 7:16; 1 Kings 11:13"> He set also a graven, and a molten statue in the house of God, of which God had said to David, and to Solomon his son: In this house, and in Jerusalem, which I have chosen out of all the tribes of Israel, will I put my name for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:10; Deuteronomy 5:31–1; Leviticus 8:36"> And I will not make the foot of Israel to be removed out of the land which I have delivered to their fathers: yet so if they will take heed to do what I hare commanded them, and all the law, and the ceremonies, and judgments by the hand of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 24:3–4; Proverbs 29:12; 2 Chronicles 33:2"> So Manasses seduced Juda, and the inhabitants of Jerusalem, to do evil beyond all the nations, which the Lord had destroyed before the face of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Jeremiah 44:4–5; Zechariah 1:4"> And the Lord spoke to him, and to his people, and they would not hearken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:36; 2 Chronicles 36:6; Nehemiah 9:32"> Therefore he brought upon them the captains of the army of the king of the Assyrians: and they took Manasses, and carried him bound with chains and fetters to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 10:3; Psalms 50:15; 2 Chronicles 32:26"> And after that he was in distress he prayed to the Lord his God: and did penance exceedingly before the God of his fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:20; Daniel 4:25; Matthew 7:7–8"> And he entreated him, and besought him earnestly: and he heard his prayer, and brought him again to Jerusalem into his kingdom, and Manasses knew that the Lord was God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 27:3; 1 Kings 1:33; Nehemiah 3:3"> After this he built a wall without the city of David, on the west side of Gihon in the valley, from the entering in of the fish gate round about to Ophel, and raised it up to a great height: and he appointed captains of the army in all the fenced cities of Juda:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:3–7; Hosea 14:1–3; 2 Kings 21:7"> And he took away the strange gods, and the idol out of the house of the Lord: the altars also which he had made in the mount of the house of the Lord, and in Jerusalem, and he cast them all out of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:4; 1 Kings 18:30; 2 Chronicles 33:9"> And he repaired the altar of the Lord, and sacrificed upon it victims, and peace offerings, and praise: and he commanded Juda to serve the Lord the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:12; 1 Kings 22:43; 2 Kings 15:4"> Nevertheless the people still sacrificed in the high places to the Lord their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:19; 2 Chronicles 33:12–13; Amos 7:12"> But the rest of the acts of Manasses, and his prayer to his God, and the words of the seers that spoke to him in the name of the Lord the God of Israel, are contained in the words of the kings of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 9:11; 1 John 1:9; Daniel 5:22"> His prayer also, and his being heard, and all his sins, and contempt, and places wherein he built high places, and set up groves, and statues before he did penance, are written in the words of Hozai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 1:10; 2 Chronicles 32:33; 2 Kings 21:18–25"> And Manasses slept with his fathers. and they buried him in his house: and his son Amen reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="James 4:13–15; Luke 12:19–20; 2 Chronicles 33:1"> Amen was two and twenty years old when he began to reign, and he reigned two years in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Kings 21:20; Ezekiel 20:18; 2 Kings 21:1–11"> And he did evil in the sight of the Lord, as Manasses his father had done: and he sacrificed to all the idols which Manasses his father had made, and served them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:12; 2 Chronicles 33:19; Jeremiah 7:26"> And he did not humble himself before the Lord, as Manasses his father had humbled himself, but committed far greater sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 11:22; 2 Chronicles 25:27–28; 2 Chronicles 24:25–26"> And his servants conspired against him, and slew him in his own house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 35:31; 2 Chronicles 34:1; 2 Chronicles 36:1"> But the rest of the multitude of the people slew them that had killed Amen, and made Josias his son king in his stead.</span>
