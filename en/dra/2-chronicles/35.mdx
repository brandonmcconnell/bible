<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 12:6; Numbers 9:3; 2 Kings 23:21–23"> And Josias kept a phase to the Lord in Jerusalem, and it was sacrificed on the fourteenth day of the first month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:2; 1 Chronicles 22:19; 2 Chronicles 29:5–11"> And he set the priests in their offices, and exhorted them to minister in the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:26; Malachi 2:7; Deuteronomy 33:10"> And he spoke to the Levites, by whose instruction all Israel was sanctified to the Lord, saying: Put the ark in the sanctuary of the temple, which Solomon the son of David king of Israel built: for you shall carry it no more: but minister now to the Lord your God, and to his people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 23:1–26; Nehemiah 11:10–20"> And prepare yourselves by your houses, and families according to your courses, as David king of Israel commanded, and Solomon his son hath written.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 134:1; Psalms 135:2; Ezra 6:18"> And serve ye in the sanctuary by the families and companies of Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:5; Hebrews 9:13–14; 2 Chronicles 29:15"> And being sanctified kill the phase, and prepare your brethren, that they may do according to the words which the Lord spoke by the hand of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 8:63; 2 Chronicles 30:24; Isaiah 32:8"> And Josias gave to all the people that were found there in the solemnity of the phase, of lambs and of kids of the flocks, and of other small cattle thirty thousand, and of oxen three thousand, all these were of the king’s substance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:31–33; Jeremiah 29:25–26; 1 Chronicles 24:4–5"> And his princes willingly offered what they had vowed, both to the people and to the priests and the Levites. Moreover Helcias, and Zacharias, and Jahiel rulers of the house of the Lord, gave to the priests to keep the phase two thousand six hundred small cattle, and three hundred oxen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:21–28; Jeremiah 3:10; Isaiah 1:10–15"> And Chonenias, and Semeias and Nathanael, his brethren, and Hasabias, and Jehiel, and Jozabad princes of the Levites, gave to the rest of the Levites to celebrate the phase five thousand small cattle, and five hundred oxen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:16; Ezra 6:18; 2 Chronicles 35:4–5"> And the ministry was prepared, and the priests stood in their office: the Levites also in their companies, according to the king’s commandment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:34; Hebrews 9:21–22; 2 Chronicles 29:22–24"> And the phase was immolated: and the priests sprinkled the blood with their hand, and the Levites flayed the holocausts:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 3:9–11; Leviticus 3:3; Leviticus 3:5"> And they separated them to give them by the houses and families of every one, and to be offered to the Lord, as it is written in the book of Moses, and with the oxen they did in like manner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 12:8–9; 1 Samuel 2:13–15; Deuteronomy 16:7"> And they roasted the phase with fire, according to that which is written in the law: but the victims of peace offerings they boiled in caldrons, and kettles, and pots, and they distributed them speedily among all the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 6:2–4"> And afterwards they made ready for themselves, and for the priests: for the priests were busied in offering of holocausts and the fat until night: wherefore the Levites prepared for themselves, and for the priests the sons of Aaron last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:1–7; 1 Chronicles 26:12–19; 1 Chronicles 9:17–19"> And the singers the sons of Asaph stood in their order, according to the commandment of David, and Asaph, and Heman, and Idithun the prophets of the king: and the porters kept guard at every gate, so as not to depart one moment from their service: and therefore their brethren the Levites prepared meats for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> So all the service of the Lord was duly accomplished that day, both in keeping the phase, and offering holocausts upon the altar of the Lord, according to the commandment of king Josias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 12:15–20; 2 Chronicles 30:21–23; Exodus 23:15"> And the children of Israel that were found there, kept the phase at that time, and the feast of unleavened bread seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:5; 2 Kings 23:21–23; 2 Chronicles 30:26–27"> There was no phase like to this in Israel, from the days of Samuel the prophet: neither did any of all the kings of Israel keep such a phase as Josias kept, with the priests, and the Levites, and all Juda, and Israel that were found, and the inhabitants of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> In the eighteenth year of the reign of Josias was this phase celebrated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 10:9; 2 Kings 23:29–30; Jeremiah 46:2–12"> After that Josias had repaired the temple, Nechao king of Egypt came up to fight in Charcamis by the Euphrates: and Josias went out to meet him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:19; 2 Kings 18:25; Isaiah 36:10"> But he sent messengers to him, saying: What have I to do with thee, O king of Juda? I come not against thee this day, but I fight against another house, to which God hath commanded me to go in haste: forbear to do against God, who is with me, lest he kill thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 5:19; 2 Chronicles 18:29; 1 Kings 22:30"> Josias would not return, but prepared to fight against him, and hearkened not to the words of Nechao from the mouth of God, I but went to fight in the field of Mageddo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 22:34; 2 Kings 9:24; Genesis 49:23"> And there he was wounded by the archers, and he said to his servants: Carry me out of the battle, for I am grievously wounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 12:11; 2 Kings 23:30; Psalms 36:6"> And they removed him from the chariot into another, that followed him after the manner of kings, and they carried him away to Jerusalem, and he died, and was buried in the monument of his fathers, and all Juda and Jerusalem mourned for him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Lamentations 4:20; Jeremiah 22:10; Job 3:8"> Particularly Jeremias: whose lamentations for Josias all the singing men and singing women repeat unto this day, and it became like a law in Israel: Behold it is found written in the Lamentations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:20; 2 Chronicles 32:32"> Now the rest of the acts of Josias and of his mercies, according to what was commanded by the law of the Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:22; 2 Chronicles 25:26; 2 Kings 10:34"> And his works first and last, are written in the book of the kings of Juda and Israel.</span>
