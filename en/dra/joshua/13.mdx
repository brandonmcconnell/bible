<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 14:10; Deuteronomy 31:3; Joshua 24:29"> Josue was old, and far advanced in years, and the Lord said to him: Thou art grown old, and advanced in age, and there is a very large country left, which is not yet divided by lot:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 13:11; 1 Samuel 27:8; Joel 3:4"> To wit, all Galilee, Philistia, and all Gessuri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:23; Judges 3:3; Jeremiah 2:18"> From the troubled river, that watereth Egypt, unto the borders of Accaron northward: the land of Chanaan, which is divided among the lords of the Philistines, the Gazites, the Azotians, the Ascalonites, the Gethites, and the Accronites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 19:30; 1 Samuel 4:1; Judges 1:34–36"> And on the south side are the Hevites, all the land of Chanaan, and Maara of the Sidonians as far as Apheca, and the borders of the Amorrhite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 5:18; Joshua 12:7; Psalms 83:7"> And his confines. The country also of Libanus towards the east from Baalgad under mount Hermon to the entering into Emath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 11:8; Joshua 23:13; Judges 2:21–23"> Of all that dwell in the mountains from Libanus, to the waters of Maserephoth, and all the Sidonians. I am he that will cut them off from before the face of the children of Israel. So let their land come in as a part of the inheritance of Israel, as I have commanded thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 26:53–56; Numbers 33:54; Ezekiel 48:23–29"> And now divide the land in possession to the nine tribes, and to the half tribe of Manasses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 12:6; Deuteronomy 3:12–17; Joshua 22:4"> With whom Ruben and Gad have possessed the land, which Moses the servant of the Lord delivered to them beyond the river Jordan, on the east side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 13:16; Numbers 21:30; Joshua 12:2"> From Aroer, which is upon the bank of the torrent Amen, and in the midst of the valley and all the plains of Medaba, as far as Dibon:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 21:24–26"> And all the cities of Sehon, king of the Amorrhites, who reigned in Hesebon, unto the borders of the children of Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:23; Joshua 12:2–5; Deuteronomy 4:47–48"> And Galaad, and the borders of Gessuri and Machati, and all mount Hermon, and all Basan as far as Salecha,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 12:4; Joshua 14:3–4; Numbers 21:23–35"> All the kingdom of Og in Basan, who reigned in Astaroth and Edrai, he was of the remains of the Raphaims: and Moses overthrew and destroyed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 23:12–13; Joshua 13:11; Numbers 33:55"> And the children of Israel would not destroy Gessuri and Machati: and they have dwelt in the midst of Israel, until this present day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 18:20–24; Joshua 14:3–4; Deuteronomy 18:1–2"> But to the tribe of Levi he gave no possession: but the sacrifices and victims of the Lord God of Israel, are his inheritance, as he spoke to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Moses gave a possession to the children of Ruben according to their kindreds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 13:9; Joshua 12:2; Isaiah 16:7–9"> And their border was from Aroer, which is on the bank of the torrent Arnon, and in the midst of the valley of the same torrent: all the plain, that leadeth to Medaba,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 22:41; Numbers 32:38; Numbers 21:19"> And Hesebon, and all their villages, which are in the plains. Dibon also, and Bamothbaal, and the town of Baalmaon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 21:23; Joshua 21:36–37; 1 Chronicles 6:78–79"> And Jassa, and Cidimoth, and Mephaath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 32:37–38"> And Cariathaim, and Sabama, and Sarathasar in the mountain of the valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 12:3; Deuteronomy 4:46; Ezekiel 25:9"> Bethphogor and Asedoth, Phasga and Bethiesimoth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 31:8; Deuteronomy 3:10; Deuteronomy 2:30–36"> And all the cities of the plain, and all the kingdoms of Sehon king of the Amorrhites, that reigned in Hesebon, whom Moses slew with the princes of Madian: Hevi, and Recem, and Sur and Hur, and Rebe, dukes of Sehon inhabitants of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 31:8; Jude 1:11; 2 Peter 2:15"> Balaam also the son of Beer the soothsayer, the children of Israel slew with the sword among the rest that were slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And the river Jordan was the herder of the children of Ruben. This is the possession of the Rubenites, by their kindreds, of cities and villages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 32:34–36"> And Moses gave to the tribe of Gad and to his children by their kindreds a possession, of which this is the division.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:26; 2 Samuel 11:1; Numbers 32:35"> The border of Jaser, and all the cities of Galaad, and half the land of the children of Ammon: as far as Aroer which is over against Rabba:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:27; 2 Samuel 2:8; Judges 10:17"> And from Hesebon unto Ramoth, Masphe and Betonim: and from Manaim unto the borders of Dabir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 32:36; Numbers 34:11; Genesis 33:17"> And in the valley Betharan and Bethnemra, and Socoth, and Saphon the other part of the kingdom of Sehon king of Hesebon: the limit of this also is the Jordan, as far as the uttermost part of the sea of Cenereth beyond the Jordan on the east side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> This is the possession of the children of Gad by their families, their cities, and villages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> He gave also to the half tribe of Manasses and his children possession according to their kindreds,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 13:26; Deuteronomy 3:13–15; Numbers 32:39–41"> The beginning whereof is this: from Manaim all Basan, and all the kingdoms of Og king of Basan, and all the villages of Jair, which are in Basan, threescore towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 12:4; Numbers 32:39–40"> And half Galaad, and Astaroth, and Edrai, cities of the kingdom of Og in Basan: to the children of Machir, the son of Manasses, to one? half of the children of Machir according to their kindreds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> This possession Moses divided in the plains of Moab, beyond the Jordan, over against Jericho on the east side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 18:7; Joshua 13:14; Deuteronomy 18:1–2"> But to the tribe of Levi he gave no possession: because the Lord the God of Israel himself is their possession, as he spoke to them.</span>
