<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 2:9–11; Numbers 13:29; 1 Kings 10:5"> Now when all the kings of the Amorrhites, who dwelt beyond the Jordan westward, and all the kings of Chanaan, who possessed the places near the great sea, had heard that the Lord had dried up the waters of the Jordan before the children of Israel, till they passed over, their heart failed them, and there remained no spirit in them, fearing the coming in of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:6; Deuteronomy 10:16; Exodus 4:25"> At that time the Lord said to Josue: Make thee knives of stone, and circumcise the second time the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 16:24; Genesis 17:23–27"> He did what the Lord had commanded, and he circumcised the children of Israel in the hill of the foreskins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 26:64–65; 1 Corinthians 10:5; Deuteronomy 2:16"> Now this is the cause of the second circumcision: All the people that came out of Egypt that were males, all the men fit for war, died in the desert, during the time of the long going about in the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:8–9; Hosea 6:6–7; Matthew 12:7"> Now these were all circumcised. But the people that were born in the desert,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:23; Deuteronomy 2:14; Hebrews 3:11"> Luring the forty years of the journey in the wide wilderness, were uncircumcised: till all they were consumed that had not heard the voice of the Lord, and to whom he had sworn before, that he would not shew them the land flowing with milk and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 14:31; Deuteronomy 1:39"> The children of these succeeded in the place of their fathers, and were circumcised by Josue: for they were uncircumcised even as they were born, and no one had circumcised them in the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 34:25"> Now after they were all circumcised, they remained in the same place of the camp, until they were healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 4:19; 1 Samuel 17:26; Genesis 34:14"> And the Lord said to Josue: This day have I taken away from you the reproach of Egypt. And the name of that place was called Galgal, until this present day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 12:6; Numbers 9:1–5; Ezekiel 12:3"> And the children of Israel abode in Galgal, and they kept the phase on the fourteenth day of the month, at evening, in the plains of Jericho:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 23:14; Exodus 13:6–7; Leviticus 23:6"> And they ate on the next day unleavened bread of the corn of the land, and frumenty of the same year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 16:35; Isaiah 65:13–14; Proverbs 13:22"> And the manna ceased after they ate of the corn of the land, neither did the children of Israel use that food any more, but they ate of the corn of the present year of the land of Chanaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 18:2; Numbers 22:23; Numbers 22:31"> And when Josue was in the field of the city of Jericho, he lifted up his eyes, and saw a man standing over against him: holding a drawn sword, and he went to him, and said: Art thou one of ours, or of our adversaries?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 55:4; Genesis 17:3; Revelation 19:10–14"> And he answered: No: but I am prince of the host of the Lord, and now I am come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 3:5; Acts 7:32–33; 2 Peter 1:18"> Josue fell on his face to the ground. And worshipping, add: What saith my lord to his servant?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Loose, saith he, thy shoes from off thy feet: for the place whereon thou standest is holy. And Josue did as was commanded him.</span>
