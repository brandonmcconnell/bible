<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Deuteronomy 7:18; Deuteronomy 1:21"> And the Lord said to Josue: Fear not, nor be thou dismayed: take with thee all the multitude of fighting men, arise and go up to the town of Hai. Behold I have delivered into thy hand the king thereof, and the people, and the city, and the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Joshua 6:21; 2 Chronicles 20:22"> And thou shalt do to the city of Hai, and to the king thereof, as thou hast done to Jericho, and to the king thereof: but the spoils and all the cattle you shall take for a prey to yourselves: lay an ambush for the city behind it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 24:39; Matthew 25:6; 1 Thessalonians 5:2"> And Josue arose, and all the army of the fighting men with him, to go up against Hai: and he sent thirty thousand chosen valiant men in the night,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 20:29; Joshua 8:16; Judges 20:36"> And commanded them, saying: Lay an ambush behind the city: and go not very far from it: and be ye all ready.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 7:5; Matthew 10:16; Judges 20:31–33"> But I and the rest of the multitude which is with me; will approach on the contrary side against the city. And when they shall come out against us, we will flee, and turn our backs, as we did before:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 20:32; Exodus 15:9; Ecclesiastes 8:11"> Till they pursuing us be drawn farther from the city: for they will think that we flee as before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 21:30–31; 2 Kings 5:1; Joshua 8:1"> And whilst we are fleeing, and they pursuing, you shall arise out of the ambush, and shall destroy the city: and the Lord your God will deliver it into our hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:28; Joshua 1:16; Joshua 1:9"> And when you shall have taken it, set it on fire, and you shall do all things so as I have commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 7:2; Genesis 12:8; Ezra 2:28"> And he sent them away, and they went on to the place of the ambush, and abode between Bethel and Hai, on the west side of the city of Hai. But Josue stayed that night in the midst of the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 6:12; Psalms 119:60; Genesis 22:3"> And rising early in the morning, he mustered his soldiers, and went up with the ancients in the front of the army environed with the aid of the fighting men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And when they were come, and were gone up over against the city, they stood on the north side of the city, between which and them there was a valley in the midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 8:2–3"> And he had chosen five thousand men, and set them to lie in ambush between Bethel and Hai, on the west side of the same city:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 8:12; Joshua 8:8"> But all the rest of the army went in battle array on the north side, so that the last of that multitude reached to the west side of the city. So Josue went that night, and stood in the midst of the valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 24:39; Judges 20:34–36; Deuteronomy 1:1"> And when the king of Hai saw this, he made haste in the morning, and went out with all the army of the city, and set it in battle array toward the desert, not knowing that there lay an ambush behind his back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 18:12; Joshua 16:1; Judges 20:36"> But Josue, and all Israel gave back, making as if they were afraid, and fleeing by the way of the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 20:31; Judges 20:36–39; Joshua 8:5–6"> But they shouting together, and encouraging one another, pursued them. And when they were come from the city,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 5:13; Joshua 8:3; Isaiah 19:11–13"> And not one remained in the city of Hai and of Bethel, that did not pursue after Israel, leaving the towns open as they had rushed out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 8:26; Job 15:25; 1 Samuel 17:41"> The Lord said to Josue: Lift up the shield that is in thy hand, towards the city of Hai, for I will deliver it to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And when he had lifted up his shield towards the city, the ambush that lay hid, rose up immediately: and going to the city, took it and set it on fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 76:5; Job 11:20; Genesis 19:28"> And the men of the city, that pursued after Josue, looking back and seeing the smoke of the city rise up to heaven, had no more power to flee this way or that way: especially as they that had counterfeited flight, and were going toward the wilderness, turned back most valiantly against them that pursued.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> So Josue and all Israel seeing that the city was taken, and that the smoke of the city rose up, returned and slew the men of Hai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Joshua 10:28; Joshua 6:21"> And they also that had taken and set the city on fire, issuing out of the city to meet their own men, began to cut off the enemies who were surrounded by them. So that the enemies being cut off on both sides, not one of so great a multitude was saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:8; Joshua 10:17; Revelation 19:20"> And they took the king of the city of Hai alive, and brought him to Josue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 11:10–14; Joshua 10:30–41; Numbers 21:24"> So all being slain that had pursued after Israel in his flight to the wilderness, and tailing by the sword in the same place, the children of Israel returned and laid waste the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:16–18"> And the number of them that fell that day, both of men and women, was twelve thousand persons all of the city of Hai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 17:11–12; Joshua 8:18"> But Josue drew not back his hand, which he had stretched out on high, holding the shield, till all the inhabitants of Hai were slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 8:2; Numbers 31:26; Psalms 50:10"> And the children of Israel divided among them the cattle and the prey of the city, as the Lord had commanded Josue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:16; Jeremiah 49:2; 2 Kings 19:25"> And he burned the city, and made it a heap for ever:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 7:26; 2 Samuel 18:17; Deuteronomy 21:22–23"> And he hung the king thereof on a gibbet until the evening and the going down of the sun. Then Josue commanded, and they took down his carcass from the gibbet: and threw it in the very entrance of the city, heaping upon it a great heap of stones, which remaineth until this present day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:4–6; Exodus 20:24; Genesis 12:7–8"> Then Josue built an altar to the Lord the God of Israel in mount Hebal,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:5–7; Exodus 20:24–25; Joshua 8:34–35"> As Moses the servant of the Lord had commanded the children of Israel, and it is written in the book of the law of Moses: an altar of unhewn stones which iron had not touched: and he offered upon it holocausts to the Lord, and immolated victims of peace offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:2–3; Deuteronomy 27:8"> And he wrote upon stones the Deuteronomy of the law of Moses, which he had ordered before the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:29; Deuteronomy 31:12; Deuteronomy 31:9"> And all the people, and the ancients, and the princes and judges stood on both sides of the ark, before the priests that carried the ark of the covenant of the Lord, both the stranger and he that was born among them, half of them by mount Garizim, and half by mount Hebal, as Moses the servant of the Lord had commanded. And first he blessed the people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:2–3; Nehemiah 13:1; Deuteronomy 31:10–12"> After this he read all the words of the blessing and the cursing and all things that were written in the hook of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:12; Nehemiah 8:2; Jeremiah 26:2"> He left out nothing of those things which Moses had commanded, but he repeated all before all the people of Israel, with the women and children and strangers that dwelt among them.</span>
