<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 34:6; Exodus 3:17; Exodus 23:23"> Now When these things were heard of, all the kings beyond the Jordan, that dwelt in the mountains and in the plains, in the places near the sea, and on the coasts of the great sea, they also that dwell by Libanus, the Hethite and the Amorrhite, the Chanaanite, the Pherezite, and the Hevite, and the Jebusite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 16:14; Joel 3:9–13; Psalms 2:1–2"> Gathered themselves together, to fight against Josue and Israel with one mind, and one resolution.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 10:2; Joshua 9:17; 2 Samuel 21:1–2"> But they that dwelt in Gabaon, hearing all that Josue had done to Jericho and Hai:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 16:8; Matthew 10:16; Mark 2:22"> Cunningly devising took for themselves provisions, laying old sacks upon their asses, and wine bottles rent and sewed up again,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 15:22; Deuteronomy 33:25; Deuteronomy 29:5"> And very old shoes, which for a show of age were clouted with patches, and old garments upon them: the leaves also, which they carried for provisions by the way, were hard, and broken into pieces:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 5:10; 2 Kings 20:14; Joshua 9:9"> And they went to Josue, who then abode in the camp at Galgal, and said to him, and to all Israel with him: We are come from a far country, desiring to make peace with you. And the children of Israel answered them, and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 11:19; Judges 2:2; Exodus 34:12"> Perhaps you dwell in the land which falls to our lot; if so, we can make no league with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:11; 2 Kings 10:5; Joshua 9:11"> But they said to Josue: We are thy servants. Josue said to them: Who are you? and whence came you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:15; Joshua 9:24; Isaiah 55:5"> They answered: From a very far country thy servants are come in the name of the Lord thy God. For we have heard the fame of his power, all the things that he did in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 21:24–35; Deuteronomy 1:4; Joshua 12:4"> And to the two kings of the Amorrhites that were beyond the Jordan, Sehon king of Hesebon, and Og king of Basan, that was in Astaroth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 9:8; Esther 8:17; Joshua 1:11"> And our ancients, and all the inhabitants of our country said to us: Take with you victuals for a long way, and go meet them, and say: We are your servants, make ye a league with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 9:4–5"> Behold, these leaves we took hot, when we set out from our houses to come to you, now they are become dry, and broken in pieces, by being exceeding old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> These bottles of wine when we filled them were new, now they are rent and burst. These garments we have on, and the shoes we have on our feet, by reason of the very long journey are worn out, and almost consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Numbers 27:21; 1 Chronicles 10:13–14"> They took therefore of their victuals, and consulted not the mouth of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:2; Deuteronomy 20:10–11; Joshua 11:19"> And Josue made peace with them, and entering into a league promised that they should not be slain: the princes also of the multitude swore to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 12:19"> Now three days after the league was made, they heard that they dwelt nigh, and they should be among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezra 2:25; Joshua 18:25–28; Joshua 18:14"> And the children of Israel removed the camp, and came into their cities on the third day, the names of which are Gabaon, and Caphira, and Beroth, and Cariathiarim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 15:4; 2 Samuel 21:7; Ecclesiastes 5:6"> And they slew them not, because the princes of the multitude had sworn in the name of the Lord the God of Israel. Then all the common people murmured against the princes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:2; Joshua 9:20; Ecclesiastes 9:2"> And they answered them: We have sworn to them in the name of the Lord the God of Israel, and therefore we may not touch them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Malachi 3:5; Proverbs 20:25; 1 Timothy 1:10"> But this we mill do to them: Let their lives be saved, lest the wrath of the Lord be stirred up against us, if we should be forsworn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:11; Joshua 9:27; Joshua 9:23"> But so let them live, as to serve the whole multitude in hewing wood, and bringing in water. As they were speaking these things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 9:6; Joshua 9:16; Genesis 3:13–14"> Josue called the Gabaonites and said to them: Why would you impose upon us, saying: We dwell far off from you, whereas you are in the midst of us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 9:21; Genesis 9:25–26; Joshua 9:27"> Therefore you shall be under a curse, and your race shall always be hewers of wood, and carriers of water unto the house of my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:15–17; Deuteronomy 7:1–2; Exodus 23:31–33"> They answered: It was told us thy servants, that the Lord thy God had promised his servant Moses to give you all the land, and to destroy all the inhabitants thereof. Therefore we feared exceedingly and provided for our lives. compelled by the dread we had of you and we took this counsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 16:6; Judges 10:15; Jeremiah 26:14"> And now we are in thy hand: deal with us as it seemeth good and right unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> So Josue did as he had said, and delivered them from the hand of the children of Israel, that they should not be slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5"> And he gave orders in that day that they should be in the service of all the people, and of the altar of the Lord, hewing wood and carrying water, until this present time, in the place which the Lord hath chosen.</span>
