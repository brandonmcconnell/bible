<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 7:10; Joshua 13:1–7; Joshua 6:2"> And the Lord spoke to Josue, saying: Speak to the children of Israel and say to them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:2–13; Deuteronomy 4:41–43; Numbers 35:11–24"> Appoint cities of refuge, a of which I spoke to you by the hand of Moses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> That whosoever shall kill a person unawares may flee to them: and may escape the wrath of the kinsman, who is the avenger of blood:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:7; Ruth 4:1–2; Hebrews 6:18"> And when he shall flee to one of these cities: he shall stand before the gate of the city, and shall speak to the ancients of that city, such things as prove him innocent: and so shall they receive him, and give him a place to dwell in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 35:12; Numbers 35:25"> And when the avenger of blood shall pursue him, they shall not deliver him into his hands, because he slew his neighbour unawares, and is not proved to have been his enemy two or three days before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 35:12; Hebrews 9:26; Numbers 35:24–25"> And he shall dwell in that city, till he stand before judgment to give an account of his fact, and till the death of the high priest, who shall be at that time: then shall the manslayer return, and go into his own city and house from whence he fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 21:11; 1 Chronicles 6:76; Luke 1:39"> And they appointed Cedes in Galilee of mount Nephtali, and Sichem in mount Ephraim, and Cariath-Arbe, the same is Hebron in the mountain of Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:78; Joshua 21:36; Joshua 21:27"> And beyond the Jordan to the east of Jericho, they appointed Bosor, which is upon the plain of the wilderness of the tribe of Ruben, and Ramoth in Galaad of the tribe of Cad, and Gaulon in Basan of the tribe of Manasses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 35:15; Joshua 20:4; Joshua 20:6"> These cities were appointed for all the children of Israel, and for the strangers, that dwelt among them: that whosoever had killed a person unawares might flee to them, and not die by the hand of the kinsman, coveting to revenge the blood that was shed, until he should stand before the people to lay open his cause.</span>
