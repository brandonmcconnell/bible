<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> All the cities of the families of Gerson, were thirteen, with their suburbs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:77; Joshua 19:11; Joshua 21:7"> And to the children of Merari, Levites of the inferior degree, by their families were given of the tribe of Zabulon, Jecnam and Cartha,</span>

<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Joshua 20:8; Deuteronomy 4:43; Numbers 21:23"> Of the tribe of Ruben beyond the Jordan over against Jericho, Bosor in the wilderness, one of the cities of refuge, Miser and Jaser and Jethson and Mephaath, four cities with their suburbs.</span>
