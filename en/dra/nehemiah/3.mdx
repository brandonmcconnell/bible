<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:2; Jeremiah 37:21; Nehemiah 12:39"> Phalel, the son of Ozi, over against the bending and the tower, which lieth out from the king’s high house, that is, in the court of the prison: after him Phadaia the son of Pharos.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:1; Nehemiah 11:21; Nehemiah 12:37"> And the Nathinites dwelt in Ophel, as far as over against the water gate toward the east, and the tower that stood out.</span>
