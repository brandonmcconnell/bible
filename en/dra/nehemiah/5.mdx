<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 5:7; Exodus 22:25–27; 1 Corinthians 6:6–8"> Now there was a great cry of the people, and of their wives against their brethren the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Malachi 2:2; Psalms 128:2–4; Psalms 127:3–5"> And there were some that said: Our sons and our daughters are very many: Yet us take up corn for the price of them, and let us eat and live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 25:35–39; Malachi 3:8–11; Genesis 47:15–25"> And there were some that said: Let us mortgage our lands, and our vineyards, and our houses, and let us take corn because of the famine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezra 4:13; Ezra 4:20; Deuteronomy 28:47–48"> And others said: Let us borrow money for the king’s tribute, and let us give up our fields and vineyards:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 4:1; Leviticus 25:39–43; Isaiah 58:7"> And now our flesh is as the flesh of our brethren: and our children as their children. Behold we bring into bondage our sons and our daughters, and some of our daughters are bondwomen already, neither have we wherewith to redeem them, and our fields and our vineyards other men possess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 11:8; Nehemiah 13:25; Ephesians 4:26"> And I was exceedingly angry when I heard their cry according to these words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:19–20; Leviticus 25:36; Exodus 22:25"> And my heart thought with myself: and I rebuked the nobles and magistrates, and said to them: Do you every one exact usury of your brethren? And I gathered together a great assembly against them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 25:47–49; 1 Corinthians 8:11; Matthew 25:29"> And I said to them: We, as you know, have redeemed according to our ability our brethren the Jews, that were sold to the Gentiles: and will you then sell your brethren, for us to redeem them? And they held their peace, and found not what to answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 25:36; 2 Samuel 12:14; Romans 2:24"> And I said to them: The thing you do is not good: why walk you not in the fear of our God, that we be not exposed to the reproaches of the Gentiles our enemies?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:1; Exodus 22:25–27; Ezekiel 18:13"> Both I and my brethren, and my servants, have lent money and corn to many: let us all agree not to call for it again; let us forgive the debt that is owing to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 58:6; Leviticus 6:4–5; Luke 3:8"> Restore ye to them this day their fields, and their vineyards, and their oliveyards, and their houses: and the hundredth part of the money, and of the corn, the wine, and the oil, which you mere wont to exact of them, give it rather for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezra 10:5; Jeremiah 34:8–10; Matthew 19:21–22"> And they said: We will restore, and we will require nothing of them: and we will do as thou sayest. And I called the priests and took an oath of them, to do according to what I had said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 18:6; 1 Chronicles 16:36; Matthew 10:14"> Moreover I shook my lap, and said: So may God shake every man that shall not accomplish this word, out of his house, and out of his labours, thus may he be shaken out, and become empty. And all the multitude said: Amen. And they praised God. And the people did according to what was said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:6; 2 Thessalonians 3:8–9; 1 Corinthians 9:4–15"> And from the day, in which the king commanded me to be governor in the land of Juda, from the twentieth year even to the two and thirtieth year of Artaxerxes the king, for twelve years, I and my brethren did not eat the yearly allowance that was due to the governors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:9; Isaiah 50:10; Proverbs 16:6"> But the former governors that had been before me, were chargeable to the people, and took of them in bread, and wine, and in money every day forty sides: and their officers also oppressed the people. But I did not so for the fear of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; 1 Thessalonians 2:5–6; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> Moreover I built in the work of the wall, and I bought no land, and all my servants were gathered together to the work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 18:19; 1 Peter 4:9–10; 2 Samuel 9:7"> The Jews also and the magistrates to the number of one hundred and fifty men, were at my table, besides them that came to us from among the nations that were round about us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 4:22–23; Psalms 37:21; Psalms 37:26"> And there was prepared for me day by day one ox, and six choice rams, besides fowls, and once in ten days I gave store of divers wines, and many other things: yet I did not require my yearly allowance as governor: for the people were very much impoverished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:31; Psalms 106:4; Nehemiah 13:14"> Remember me, O my God, for good according to all that I have done for this people.</span>
