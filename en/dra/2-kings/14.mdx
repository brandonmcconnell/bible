<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 13:10; 2 Chronicles 25:1–4; 2 Kings 14:15"> In the second year of Joas son of Joachaz, king of Israel, reigned Amasias son of Joas king of Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> He was five and twenty years old when he began to reign: and nine and twenty years he reigned in Jerusalem: the name of his mother was Joadan of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:2–3; Zechariah 1:4–6; 1 Kings 11:4"> And he did that which was right before the Lord, but yet not like David his father. He did according to all things that Joas his father did:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 12:3; 2 Kings 16:4; 2 Kings 15:35"> But this only, that he took not away the high places: for yet the people sacrificed and burnt incense in the high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:3–4; 2 Kings 12:20–21; Numbers 35:33"> And when he had possession of the kingdom, he put his servants to death that had slain the king his father:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:16; Ezekiel 18:20; Ezekiel 18:4"> But the children of the murderers he did not put to death, according to that which is written in the book of the law of Moses, wherein the Lord commanded, saying: The fathers shall not be put to death for the children, neither shall the children be put to death for the fathers: but every man shall die for his own sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:13; 1 Chronicles 18:12; Joshua 15:38"> He slew of Edom in the valley of the Saltpits ten thousand men, and took the rock by war, and called the name thereof Jectehel, unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:17–24; 2 Samuel 2:14–17; 2 Kings 14:11"> Then Amasias sent messengers to Joas son of Joachaz, son of Jehu king of Israel, saying: Come let us see one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 9:8–15; 1 Kings 4:33; 2 Chronicles 33:11"> And Joas king of Israel sent again to Amasias king of Juda, saying: A thistle of Libanus sent to a cedar tree, which is in Libanus, saying: Give thy daughter to my son to wife. And the beasts of the forest, that are in Libanus, passed and trod down the thistle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:16; Deuteronomy 8:14; 2 Chronicles 32:25"> Thou hast beaten and prevailed over Edom, and thy heart hath lifted thee up: be content with the glory, and sit at home: why provokest thou evil, that thou shouldst fall, and Juda with thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 19:38; 2 Chronicles 25:16; Joshua 15:10"> But Amasias did not rest satisfied. So Joas king of Israel went up, and he and Amasias king of Juda saw one another in Bethsames a town in Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:17; 1 Samuel 4:10; 1 Kings 22:36"> And Juda was put to the worst before Israel, and they fled every man to their dwellings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:16; Nehemiah 12:39; Zechariah 14:10"> But Joas king of Israel took Amasias, king of Juda the son of Joas, the son of Ochozias, in Bethsames, and brought him into Jerusalem: and he broke down the wall of Jerusalem, from the gate of Ephraim to the gate of the corner, four hundred cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 14:26; 1 Kings 7:51; 1 Kings 15:18"> And he took all the gold, and silver, and all the vessels, that were found in the house of the Lord, and in the king’s treasures, and hostages, and returned to Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 13:12–13; 1 Kings 14:19–20; 2 Kings 10:34–35"> But the rest of the acts of Joas, which he did, and his valour, wherewith he fought against Amasias king of Juda, are they not written in the book of the words of the days of the kings of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 13:9; 2 Kings 13:13; 1 Kings 1:21"> And Joas slept with his fathers, and was buried in Samaria, with the kings of Israel: and Jeroboam his son reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:25–28; 2 Kings 13:10; 2 Kings 14:23"> And Amasias the son of Joas king of Juda lived, after the death of Joas son of Joachaz king of Israel fifteen years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 11:41; 1 Kings 14:29; 2 Kings 13:8"> And the rest of the acts of Amasias, are they not written in the book of the words of the days of the kings of Juda?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 10:31; 2 Kings 15:25; 2 Kings 12:20–21"> Now they made a conspiracy against him in Jerusalem: and he fled to Lachis. And they sent after him to Lachis, and killed him there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 9:28; 2 Kings 12:21; 2 Chronicles 33:20"> And they brought him away upon horses, and he was buried in Jerusalem with his fathers in the city of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 21:24; Matthew 1:8–9; 2 Kings 15:13"> And all the people of Juda took Azarias, who was sixteen years old, and made him king instead of his father Amasias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Kings 16:6; 1 Kings 9:26; Deuteronomy 2:8"> He built Elath, and restored it to Juda, after that the king slept with his fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hosea 1:1; Amos 7:9–11; Amos 1:1"> In the fifteenth year of Amasias son of Joas king of Juda, reigned Jeroboam the son of Joas king of Israel in Samaria, one and forty years:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Kings 13:6; 2 Kings 13:11; Genesis 38:7"> And he did that which was evil before the Lord. He departed not from all the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nabat, who made Israel to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:17; Matthew 12:39–40; Joshua 19:13"> He restored the borders of Israel from the entrance of Emath, unto the sea of the wilderness, according to the word of the Lord the God of Israel, which he spoke by his servant Jonas the son of Amathi, the prophet, who was of Geth, which is in Opher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:36; Exodus 3:7; 2 Kings 13:4"> For the Lord saw the affliction of Israel that it was exceeding bitter, and that they were consumed even to them that were shut up in prison, and the lowest persons, and that there was no one to help Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 13:23; 2 Kings 13:5; Romans 11:2–12"> And the Lord did not say that he would blot out the name of Israel from under heaven, but he saved them by the hand of Jeroboam the son of Joas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Kings 11:24; 1 Chronicles 18:5–6; 2 Samuel 8:6"> But the rest of the acts of Jeroboam, and all that he did, and his velour, wherewith he fought, and how he restored Damascus, and Emath to Juda in Israel, are they not written in the book of the words of the days of the kings of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Kings 15:8"> And Jeroboam slept with his fathers the kings of Israel, and Zacharias his son reigned in his stead.</span>
