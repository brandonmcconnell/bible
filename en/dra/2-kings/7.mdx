<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 3:5; Exodus 16:12; Ezekiel 37:4"> And Eliseus said: Hear ye the word of the Lord: Thus saith the Lord: Tomorrow about this time a bushel of fine hour shall be sold for a stater, and two bushels of barley for a stater, in the gate of Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 18:12–14; Genesis 7:11; Malachi 3:10"> Then one of the lords, upon whose hand the king leaned, answering the man of God, said: If the Lord should make flood-gates in heaven, can that possibly be which thou sayest? And he said: Thou shalt see it with thy eyes, but shalt not eat thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 13:45–46; Numbers 5:2–4; 2 Kings 7:4"> Now there were four lepers, at the entering in of the gate: and they said one to another: What mean we to stay here till we die?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:18; Luke 15:17–19; 2 Samuel 14:14"> If we will enter into the city, we shall die with the famine: and if we will remain here, we must also die: come, therefore, and let us run over to the camp of the Syrians. If they spare us, we shall live: but if they kill us, we shall but die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:7; Deuteronomy 32:25; 1 Samuel 30:17"> So they arose in the evening, to go to the Syrian camp, And when they were come to the first part of the camp of the Syrians, they found no man there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:24; Isaiah 31:1; 1 Kings 10:29"> For the Lord had made them hear, in the camp of Syria, the noise of chariots, and of horses, and of a very great army, and they said one to another: Behold the king of Israel hath hired against us the kings of the Hethites, and of the Egyptians, and they are come upon us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 28:1; Psalms 48:4–6; Hebrews 6:18"> Wherefore they arose, and fled away in the dark, and left their tents, and their horses and asses in the camp, and fled, desiring to save their lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 13:44; Joshua 7:21; Matthew 25:18"> So when these lepers were come to the beginning of the camp, they went into one tent, and ate and drank: and they took from thence silver, and gold, and raiment, and went, and hid it: and they came again, and went into another tent, and carried from thence in like manner, and hid it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Philippians 2:4; Nahum 1:15"> Then they said one to another: We do not well: for this is a day of good tidings. If we hold our peace, and do not tell it till the morning, we shall be charged with a crime: come, let us go and tell it in the king’s court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 7:11; Mark 13:34–35; Psalms 127:1"> So they came to the gate of the city, and told them, saying: We went to the camp of the Syrians, and we found no man there, but horses, and asses tied, and the tents standing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Then the guards of the gate went, and told it within the king’s palace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 6:25–29; Joshua 8:4"> And he arose in the night and said to his servants: I tell you what the Syrians have done to us: They know that we suffer great famine, and therefore they are gone out of the camp, and lie hid in the fields, saying: When they come out of the city we shall take them alive, and then we may get into the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 7:4; Lamentations 4:9; 2 Kings 5:13"> And one of his servants answered: Let us take the five horses that are remaining in the city (because there are no more in the whole multitude of Israel, for the rest are consumed,) and let us send and see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> They brought therefore two horses, and the king sent into the camp of the Syrians, saying: Go, and see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 22:24; Matthew 24:16–18; Hebrews 12:1"> And they went after them as far as the Jordan: and behold all the way was full of garments, and vessels, which the Syrians had cast away in their fright, and the messengers returned and told the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 7:1; Isaiah 33:4; Isaiah 33:23"> And the people going out pillaged the camp of the Syrians: and a bushel of fine flour was sold for a stater, and two bushels of barley for a stater, according to the word of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 7:2; 2 Kings 6:32; Judges 20:43"> And the king appointed that lord on whose hand he leaned, to stand at the gate: and the people trod upon him in the entrance of the gate; and he died, as the man of God had said, when the king came down to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 18:14; 2 Kings 7:1–2; 2 Kings 6:32"> And it came to pass according to the word of the man of God, which he spoke to the king, when he said: Two bushels of barley shall be for a stater, and a bushel of fine flour for a stater, at this very time tomorrow in the gate of Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 7:2"> When that lord answered the man of God, and said: Although the Lord should make flood-gates in heaven, could this come to pass which thou sayest? And he said to him: Thou shalt see with thy eyes, and shalt not eat thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5–6; 2 Chronicles 20:20; Job 20:23"> And so it fell out to him as it was foretold, and the people trod upon him in the gate, and he died.</span>
