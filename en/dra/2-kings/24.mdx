<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 1:1; Jeremiah 25:1; Jeremiah 25:9"> In his days Nabuchodonosor king of Babylon came up, and Joakim became his servant three years: then again he rebelled against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 35:11; 2 Kings 23:27; 2 Kings 21:12–14"> And the Lord sent against him the rovers of the Chaldees, and the rovers of Syria, and the rovers of Moab, and the rovers of the children of Ammon: and he sent them against Juda, to destroy it, according to the word of the Lord, which he had spoken by his servants the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 18:25; 2 Kings 21:2–11; 2 Kings 23:26–27"> And this came by the word of the Lord against Juda, to remove them from before him for all the sins of Manasses which he did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 21:16; Psalms 106:38; Numbers 35:33"> And for the innocent blood that he shed, filling Jerusalem with innocent blood: and therefore the Lord would not be appeased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:8; Jeremiah 26:1–24; Jeremiah 22:13–17"> But the rest of the acts of Joakim, and all that he did, are they not written in the book of the words of the days of the kings of Juda? And Joakim slept with his fathers:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:18–19; 2 Chronicles 36:6; Jeremiah 36:30"> And Joachin his son reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:5–7; Jeremiah 46:2; Genesis 15:18"> And the king of Egypt came not again any more out of his own country: for the king of Babylon had taken all that had belonged to the king of Egypt, from the river of Egypt, unto the river Euphrates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:9; 1 Chronicles 3:16; Jeremiah 37:1"> Joachin was eighteen years old when he began to reign, a and he reigned three months in Jerusalem: the name of his mother was Nohesta the daughter of Elnathan of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And he did evil before the Lord, according to all that his father had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 25:2; Daniel 1:1–2"> At that time the servants of Nabuchodonosor king of Babylon came up against Jerusalem, and the city was surrounded with their forts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Nabuchodonosor king of Babylon came to the city with his servants to assault it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:1; Jeremiah 52:28; 2 Kings 25:27"> And Joachin king of Juda went out to the king of Babylon, he and his mother, and his servants, and his nobles, and his eunuchs: and the king of Babylon received him in the eighth year of his reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 39:6; 2 Kings 20:17; Jeremiah 20:5"> And he brought out from thence all the treasures of the house of the Lord, and the treasures of the king’s house: and he cut in pieces all the vessels of gold which Solomon king of Israel had made in the temple of the Lord, according to the word of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:28; 2 Kings 25:12; 2 Kings 24:16"> And he carried away all Jerusalem, and all the princes, and all the valiant men of the army, to the number of ten thousand into captivity: and every artificer and smith: and none were left, but the poor sort of the people of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:10; Jeremiah 22:24–28; Esther 2:6"> And he carried away Joachin into Babylon, and the king’s mother, and the king’s wives, and his eunuchs: and the judges of the land he carried into captivity from Jerusalem into Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:28; 2 Kings 24:14; Jeremiah 29:2"> And all the strong men, seven thousand, and the artificers, and the smiths a thousand, all that were valiant men and fit for war: and the king of Babylon led them captives into Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:1; 2 Chronicles 36:4; 2 Kings 23:34"> And he appointed Matthanias his uncle in his stead: and called his name Sedecias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 23:31; Jeremiah 52:1–11; Jeremiah 37:1"> Sedecias was one and twenty years old when he began to reign, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem: the name of his mother was Amital, the daughter of Jeremias of Lobna.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 23:37; Jeremiah 24:8; Ezekiel 21:25"> And he did evil before the Lord, according to all that Joakim had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:13; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11; Jeremiah 38:17–21"> For the Lord was angry against Jerusalem and against Juda, till he cast them out from his face: and Sedecias revolted from the king of Babylon.</span>
