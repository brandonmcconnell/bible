<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 8:30; 1 Kings 13:32; 1 Kings 21:8–14"> And Achab had seventy sons in Samaria: so Jehu wrote letters, and sent to Samaria, to the chief men of the city, and to the ancients, and to them that brought up Achab’s children, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 5:6"> As soon as you receive these letters, ye that have your master’s sons, and chariots, and horses, and fenced cities, and armour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:8–9; 1 Kings 12:20–21; 2 Samuel 2:12–17"> Choose the best, and him that shall please you most of your master’s sons, and set him on his father’s throne, and fight for the house of your master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 9:24; 2 Kings 9:27; Isaiah 27:4"> But they were exceedingly afraid, and said: Behold two kings could not stand before him, and how shall we be able to resist?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 20:32; 1 Kings 20:4; Joshua 9:11"> Therefore the overseers of the house, and the rulers of the city, and the ancients, and the tutors sent to Jehu, saying: We are thy servants, whatsoever thou shalt command us we will do, neither will we make us a king: do thou all that pleaseth thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 2:20–23; Deuteronomy 5:9; Numbers 25:4"> And he wrote letters the second time to them, saying: If you be mine, and will obey me, take the heads of the sons of your master, and come to me to Jezrahel by tomorrow this time. Now the king’s sons, being seventy men, were brought up with the chief men of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 21:21; 2 Kings 11:1; 2 Kings 10:9"> And when the letters came to them, they took the king’s sons, and slew seventy persons, and put their heads in baskets, and sent them to him to Jezrahel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:23; 2 Samuel 11:18–21; Mark 6:28"> And a messenger came, and told him, saying: They have brought the heads of the king’s sons. And he said: Lay ye them in two heaps by the entering in of the gate until the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 9:14–24; Isaiah 5:3; Hosea 1:4"> And when it was light, he went out, and standing said to all the people: You are just: if I conspired against my master, and slew him, who hath slain all these?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 21:29; 1 Kings 21:19; 2 Kings 9:7–10"> See therefore now that there hath not fallen to the ground any of the words of the Lord, which the Lord spoke concerning the house of Achab, and the Lord hath done that which he spoke in the hand of his servant Elias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 18:19; 1 Kings 18:19; 1 Kings 22:6"> So Jehu slew all that were left of the house of Achab in Jezrahel, and all his chief men, and his friends, and his priests, till there were no remains left of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And he arose, and went to Samaria: and when he was come to the shepherds’ cabin in the way,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 8:29; 2 Kings 8:24; 2 Chronicles 21:17"> He met with the brethren of Ochozias king of Juda, and he said to them: Who are you? And they answered: We are the brethren of Ochozias, and are come down to salute the sons of the king, and the sons of the queen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:10; 2 Kings 10:6; 2 Kings 10:10–11"> And he said: Take them alive. And they took them alive, and killed them at the pit by the cabin, two and forty men, and he left not any of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 35:14–19; Ezra 10:19; Ezekiel 17:18"> And when he was departed thence, he found Jonadab the son of Rechab coming to meet him, and he blessed him. And he said to him: Is thy heart right as my heart is with thy heart? And Jonadab said: It is. If it be, said he, give me thy hand. He gave him his hand. And he lifted him up to him into the chariot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 19:10; Matthew 6:5; 2 Kings 10:31"> And he said to him: Come with me, and see my zeal for the Lord. So he made him ride in his chariot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 9:8; 2 Chronicles 22:8; Malachi 4:1"> And brought him into Samaria. And he slew all that were left of Achab in Samaria, to a man, according to the word of the Lord, which he spoke by Elias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 16:31–32; 2 Kings 3:2; Romans 3:8"> And Jehu gathered together all the people, and said to them: Achab worshipped Baal a little, but I will worship him more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 22:6; 1 Kings 18:19; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> Now therefore call to me all the prophets of Baal, and all his servants, and all his priests: let none be wanting, for I have a great sacrifice to offer to Baal: whosoever shall be wanting shall not live. Now Jehu did this craftily, that he might destroy the worshippers of Baal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joel 1:14; Exodus 32:5; 1 Kings 18:19–20"> And he said: Proclaim a festival for Baal. And he called,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 16:32; 2 Kings 11:18; Joel 3:2"> And he sent into all the borders of Israel, and all the servants of Baal came: there was not one left that did not come. And they went into the temple of Baal: and the house of Baal was filled, from one end to the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 28:2; Matthew 22:11–12"> And he said to them that were over the wardrobe: Bring forth garments for all the servants of Baal. And they brought them forth garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 13:30; Matthew 25:32–33; Matthew 13:41"> And Jehu and Jonadab the son of Rechab went to the temple of Baal, and said to the worshippers of Baal: Search, and see that there be not any with you of the servants of the Lord, but that there be the servants of Baal only.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 20:30–42"> And they went in to offer sacrifices and burnt offerings: but Jehu had prepared him fourscore men without, and said to them: If any of the men escape, whom I have brought into your hands, he that letteth him go shall answer life for life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Revelation 16:6–7; Ezekiel 22:21–22; 1 Kings 18:40"> And it came to pass, when the burnt offering was ended, that Jehu commanded his soldiers and captains, saying: Go in, and kill them, let none escape. And the soldiers and captains slew them with the edge of the sword, and cast them out: and they went into the city of the temple of Baal,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 14:23; 2 Kings 3:2; 2 Kings 19:18"> And brought the statue out of Baal’s temple, and burnt it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Daniel 3:29; Ezra 6:11; Daniel 2:5"> And broke it in pieces. They destroyed also the temple of Baal, and made a jakes in its place unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> So Jehu destroyed Baal out of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 12:28–30; 1 Kings 14:16; 1 Kings 13:33–34"> But yet he departed not from the sills of Jeroboam the son of Nabat, who made Israel to sin, nor did he forsake the golden calves that were in Bethel and Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 10:35; 2 Kings 13:1; 2 Kings 13:10"> And the Lord said to Jehu: Because thou hast diligently executed that which was right and pleasing in my eyes, and hast done to the house of Achab according to all that was in my heart: thy children shall sit upon the throne of Israel to the fourth Generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Kings 10:29; Proverbs 4:23; Hebrews 2:1"> But Jehu took no heed to walk in the law of the Lord the God of Israel with all his heart: for he departed not from the sins of Jeroboam, who had made Israel to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Kings 8:12; 1 Kings 19:17; 2 Kings 13:22"> In those days the Lord began to he weary of Israel: and Hazael ravaged them in all the coasts of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Amos 1:3–4; Deuteronomy 2:36; Joshua 13:9–12"> From the Jordan eastward, all the land of Galaad, and Gad, and Ruben, and Manasses, from Aroer, which is upon the torrent Amen, and Galaad, and Basan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 11:41; 1 Kings 14:29; 1 Kings 14:19"> But the rest of the acts of Jehu, and all that he did, and his strength, are they not written in the book of the words of the days of the kings of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 14:20; 1 Kings 14:31; 1 Kings 1:21"> And Jehu slept with his fathers, and they buried him in Samaria: and Joachaz his son reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And the time that Jehu reigned over Israel, in Samaria, was eight and twenty years.</span>
