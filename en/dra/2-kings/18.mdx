<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:27–1; Matthew 1:9–10; 1 Chronicles 3:13"> In the third year of Osee the son of Ela king of Israel, reigned Ezechias the son of Achaz king of Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:1–2"> He was five and twenty years old when he began to reign: and he reigned nine and twenty years in Jerusalem: the name of his mother was Abi the daughter of Zacharias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 20:3; 1 Kings 11:4; 2 Chronicles 31:20–21"> And he did that which was good before the Lord, according to all that David his father had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:1; Numbers 21:8–9; 2 Kings 18:22"> He destroyed the high places, and broke the statues in pieces, and cut down the groves, and broke the brazen serpent, which Moses had made: for till that time the children of Israel burnt incense to it: and he called its name Nohestan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 23:25; 2 Kings 19:10; Matthew 27:43"> He trusted in the Lord the God of Israel: so that after him there was none like him among all the kings of Juda, nor any of them that were before him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 23:8; Deuteronomy 10:20; John 14:15"> And he stuck to the Lord, and departed not from his steps, but kept his commandments, which the Lord commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Genesis 39:2–3; 2 Kings 16:7"> Wherefore the Lord also was with him, and in all things, to which he went forth, he behaved himself wisely. And he rebelled against the king of the Assyrians, and served him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 14:29; 2 Kings 17:9; 2 Chronicles 28:18"> He smote the Philistines as far as Gaza, and all their borders, from the tower of the watchmen to the fenced city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 10:14; 2 Kings 17:3–23; 2 Kings 18:1"> In the fourth year of king Ezechias, which was the seventh year of Osee the son of Ela king of Israel, Salmanasar king of the Assyrians came up to Samaria, and besieged it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 6:7; Amos 9:1–4; Micah 1:6–9"> And took it. For after three years, in the sixth year of Ezechias, that is, in the ninth year of Osee king of Israel, Samaria was taken:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:26; 2 Kings 17:6; Isaiah 37:12"> And the king of the Assyrians carried away Israel into Assyria, and placed them in Hale, and in Habor by the rivers of Gozan in the cities of the Medes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17; 1 Peter 2:8; Micah 3:4"> Because they hearkened not to the voice of the Lord their God, but transgressed his covenant: all that Moses the servant of the Lord commanded, they would not hear nor do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 36:1–22; 2 Chronicles 32:1–23; Isaiah 7:17–25"> In the fourteenth year of king Ezechias, Sennacherib king of the Assyrians came up against the fenced cities of Juda: and took them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 18:7; Luke 14:32; 1 Kings 20:4"> Then Ezechias king of Juda sent messengers to the king of the Assyrians to Lachis, saying: I have offended, depart from me: and all that thou shalt put upon me, I will bear. And the king of the Assyrians put a tax upon Ezechias king of Juda, of three hundred talents of silver, and thirty talents of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 16:8; 1 Kings 15:18–19; 2 Kings 12:18"> And Ezechias gave all the silver that was found in the house of the Lord, and in the king’s treasures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:3; 1 Kings 6:31–35"> At that time Ezechias broke the doors of the temple of the Lord, and the plates of gold which he had fastened on them, and gave them to the king of the Assyrians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 20:1; 2 Kings 20:20; Isaiah 7:3"> And the king of the Assyrians sent Tharthan and Rabsaris, and Rabsaces from Lachis to king Ezechias with a strong army to Jerusalem: and they went up and came to Jerusalem, and they stood by the conduit of the upper pool, which is in the way of the fuller’s field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 19:2; Isaiah 36:3; 2 Samuel 8:16–17"> And they called for the king: and there went out to them Eliacim the son of Helcias who was over the house, and Sobna the scribe, and Joahe the son of Asaph the recorder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 36:4; 2 Kings 19:10; Isaiah 10:8–14"> And Rabsaces said to them: Speak to Ezechias: Thus saith the great king, the king of the Assyrians: What is this confidence, wherein thou trustest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 21:30–31"> Perhaps thou hast taken counsel, to prepare thyself for battle. On whom dost thou trust, that thou darest to rebel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 30:7; Ezekiel 29:6–7; Isaiah 30:2–3"> Dost thou trust in Egypt a staff of a broken reed, upon which if a man lean, it will break and go into his hand, and pierce it? so is Pharao king of Egypt, to all that trust in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:1; 2 Kings 18:4–5; Matthew 27:43"> But if you say to me: We trust in the Lord our God: is it not he, whose high places and altars Ezechias hath taken away: and hath commanded Juda and Jerusalem: You shall worship before this altar in Jerusalem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:42; 1 Kings 20:10; Nehemiah 4:2–5"> Now therefore come over to my master the king of the Assyrians, and I will give you two thousand horses, and see whether you be able to have riders for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 10:8; Deuteronomy 17:16; Isaiah 36:6"> And how can you stand against one lord of the least of my master’s servants? Dost thou trust in Egypt for chariots and for horsemen?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 19:6; Isaiah 10:5–6; 2 Kings 19:22–37"> Is it without the will of the Lord that I am come up to this place to destroy it? The Lord said to me: Go up to this land and destroy it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezra 4:7; Daniel 2:4; Isaiah 36:11–12"> Then Eliacim the son of Helcias, and Sobna, and Joahe said to Rabsaces: We pray thee speak to us thy servants in Syriac: for we understand that tongue: and speak not to us in the Jews’ language, in the hearing of the people that are upon the wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 73:8; Lamentations 4:5; Ezekiel 4:15"> And Rabsaces answered them, saying: Hath my master sent me to thy master and to thee, to speak these words, and not rather to the men that sit upon the wall, that they may eat their own dung, and drink their urine with you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 36:13–18; Isaiah 10:8–13; Ezekiel 29:3"> Then Rabsaces stood, and cried out with a loud voice in the Jews’ language, and said: Hear the words of the great king, the king of the Assyrians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:15; 2 Thessalonians 2:8; 2 Chronicles 32:11"> Thus saith the king: Let not Ezechias deceive you: for he shall not be able to deliver you out of my hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 125:1–2; Luke 23:35; 2 Kings 19:32–34"> Neither let him make you trust in the Lord, saying: The Lord will surely deliver us, and this city shall not be given into the hand of the king of the Assyrians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 4:25; 1 Kings 4:20; Zechariah 3:10"> Do not hearken to Ezechias. For thus saith the king of the Assyrians: Do with me that which is for your advantage, and come out to me: and every man of you shall eat of his own vineyard, and of his own fig tree: and you shall drink water of your own cisterns,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:7–9; Exodus 3:8; Deuteronomy 11:12"> Till I come, and take you away to a land, like to your own land, a fruitful land, and plentiful in wine, a land of bread and vineyards, a land of olives, and oil and honey, and you shall live, and not die. Hearken not to Ezechias, who deceiveth you, saying: The Lord will deliver us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 10:10–11; 2 Kings 19:12–13; 2 Chronicles 32:19"> Have any of the gods of the nations delivered their land from the hand of the king of Assyria?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 10:9; 2 Kings 17:23–33; Jeremiah 49:23"> Where is the god of Emath, and of Arphad? where is the god of Sepharvaim, of Ana, and of Ava? have they delivered Samaria out of my hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Daniel 3:15; Isaiah 10:15; Exodus 5:2"> Who are they among all the gods of the nations, that have delivered their country out of my hand, that the Lord may deliver Jerusalem out of my hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Amos 5:13; Proverbs 9:7; Matthew 7:6"> But the people held their peace, and answered him not a word: for they had received commandment from the king that they should not answer him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Kings 22:11; Genesis 37:29; 2 Kings 5:7"> And Eliacim the son of Helcias, who was over the house, and Sobna the scribe, and Joahe the son of Asaph the recorder, came to Ezechias, with their garments rent, and told him the words of Rabsaces.</span>
