<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And after they had supped, they brought in the young man to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And Tobias remembering the angel’s word, took out of his bag part of the liver, and laid it upon burning coals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Then the angel Raphael took the devil, and bound him in the desert of upper Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Then Tobias exhorted the virgin, and said to her: Sara, arise, and let us pray to God today, and tomorrow, and the next day: because for these three nights we are joined to God: and when the third night is over, we will be in our own wedlock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> For we are the children of saints, and we must not be joined together like heathens that know not God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> So they both arose, and prayed earnestly both together that health might be given them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And Tobias said: Lord God of our father, may the heavens and the earth, and the sea, and the fountains, and the rivers, and all thy creatures that are in them, bless thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Thou madest Adam of the slime of the earth, and gavest him Eve for a helper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And now, Lord, thou knowest, that not for fleshly lust do I take my sister to wife, but only for the love of posterity, in which thy name may be blessed for ever and ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Sara also said: Have mercy on us, O Lord, have mercy on us, and let us grow old both together in health.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And it came to pass about the cockcrowing, Raguel ordered his servants to be called for, and they went with him together to dig a grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> For he said: Lest perhaps it may have happened to him, in like manner as it did to the other seven husbands, that went in unto her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And when they had prepared the pit, Raguel went back to his wife, and said to her:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Send one of thy maids, and let her see if he be dead, that I may bury him before it be day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> So she sent one of her maidservants, who went into the chamber, and found them safe and sound, sleeping both together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And returning she brought the good news: and Raguel and Anna his wife blessed the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And said: We bless thee, O Lord God of Israel, because it hath not happened as we suspected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> For thou hast shewn thy mercy to us, and hast shut out from us the enemy that persecuted us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And thou hast taken pity upon two only children. Make them, O Lord, bless thee more fully: and to offer up to thee a sacrifice of thy praise, and of their health, that all nations may know, that thou alone art God in all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And immediately Raguel commanded his servants, to fill up the pit they had made, before it was day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And he spoke to his wife to make ready a feast, and prepare all kind of provisions that are necessary for such as go a journey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> He caused also two fat kine, and four wethers to be killed, and a banquet to be prepared for all his neighbours, and all his friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And Raguel adjured Tobias, to abide with him two weeks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And of all things which Raguel possessed, he gave one half to Tobias, and made him a writing, that the half that remained should after their decease come also to Tobias.</span>
