<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Tobias answered his father, and said: I will do all things, father, which thou hast commanded me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> But how I shall get this money, I cannot tell; he knoweth me not, and I know not him: what token shall I give him? nor did I ever know the way which leadeth thither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Then his father answered him, and said: I have a note of his hand with me, which when thou shalt shew him, he will presently pay it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> But go now, and seek thee out some faithful man, to go with thee for his hire: that thou mayst receive it, while I yet live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Then Tobias going forth, found a beautiful young man, standing girded, and as it were ready to walk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And not knowing that he was an angel of God, he saluted him, and said: From whence art thou, good young man?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> But he answered: Of the children of Israel. And Tobias said to him: Knowest thou the way that leadeth to the country of the Medes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And he answered: I know it: and I have often walked through all the ways thereof, and I have abode with Gabelus our brother, who dwelleth at Rages a city of the Medes, which is situate in the mount of Ecbatana.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Tobias said to him: Stay for me, I beseech thee, till I tell these same things to my father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then Tobias going in told all these things to his father. Upon which his father being in admiration, desired that he would come in unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> So going in he saluted him, and said: Joy be to thee always.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And Tobias said: What manner of joy shall be to me, who sit in darkness, and see not the light of heaven?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the young man said to him: Be of good courage, thy cure from God is at hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And Tobias said to him: Canst thou conduct my son to Gabelus at Rages, a city of the Medes? and when thou shalt return, I will pay thee thy hire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the angel said to him: I will conduct him thither, and bring him back to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Tobias said to him: I pray thee, tell me, of what family, or what tribe art thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Raphael the angel answered: Dost thou seek the family of him thou hirest, or the hired servant himself to go with thy son?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> But lest I should make thee uneasy, I am Azarias the son of the great Ananias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And Tobias answered: Thou art of a great family. But I pray thee be not angry that I desired to know thy family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And the angel said to him: I will lead thy son safe, and bring him to thee again safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And Tobias answering, said: May you have a good journey, and God be with you in your way, and his angel accompany you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Then all things being ready, that were to be carried in their journey, Tobias bade his father and his mother farewell, and they set out both together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And when they were departed, his mother began to weep, and to say: Thou hast taken the staff of our old age, and sent him away from us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> I wish the money for which thou hast sent him, had never been.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> For poverty was sufficient for us, that we might account it as riches, that we saw our son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And Tobias said to her: Weep not, our son will arrive thither safe, and will return safe to us, and thy eyes shall see him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> For I believe that the good angel of God doth accompany him, and doth order all things well that are done about him, so that he shall return to us with joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> At these words his mother ceased weeping, and held her peace.</span>
