<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 24:31; Daniel 7:2; Jeremiah 49:36"> After these things, I saw four angels standing on the four corners of the earth, holding the four winds of the earth, that they should not blow upon the earth, nor upon the sea, nor on any tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 4:30; 2 Corinthians 1:22; Revelation 7:3–8"> And I saw another angel ascending from the rising of the sun, having the sign of the living God; and he cried with a loud voice to the four angels, to whom it was given to hurt the earth and the sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:4; Revelation 22:4; Revelation 9:4"> Saying: Hurt not the earth, nor the sea, nor the trees, till we sign the servants of our God in their foreheads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 14:1; Revelation 14:3; Matthew 19:28"> And I heard the number of them that were signed, an hundred forty-four thousand were signed, of every tribe of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 1:2–4; Numbers 10:14–27; Numbers 13:4–16"> Of the tribe of Juda, were twelve thousand signed: Of the tribe of Ruben, twelve thousand signed: Of the tribe of Gad, twelve thousand signed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 2:36"> Of the tribe of Aser, twelve thousand signed: Of the tribe of Nephthali, twelve thousand signed: Of the tribe of Manasses, twelve thousand signed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 35:23; Numbers 26:14; Numbers 25:14"> Of the tribe of Simeon, twelve thousand signed: Of the tribe of Levi, twelve thousand signed: Of the tribe of Issachar, twelve thousand signed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Of the tribe of Zabulon, twelve thousand signed: Of the tribe of Joseph, twelve thousand signed: Of the tribe of Benjamin, twelve thousand signed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 5:9; John 12:13; Psalms 117:1–2"> After this I saw a great multitude, which no man could number, of all nations, and tribes, and peoples, and tongues, standing before the throne, and in sight of the Lamb, clothed with white robes, and palms in their hands:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 19:1; Psalms 3:8; Isaiah 45:21"> And they cried with a loud voice, saying: Salvation to our God, who sitteth upon the throne, and to the Lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 5:11–13; Revelation 19:4–6; Revelation 4:10"> And all the angels stood round about the throne, and the ancients, and the four living creatures; and they fell down before the throne upon their faces, and adored God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 5:12–14; Jude 1:25; Psalms 100:4"> Saying: Amen. Benediction, and glory, and wisdom, and thanksgiving, honour, and power, and strength to our God for ever and ever. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 7:9; Revelation 4:10; Revelation 5:5"> And one of the ancients answered, and said to me: These that are clothed in white robes, who are they? and whence came they?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 John 1:7; Revelation 22:14; Hebrews 13:12"> And I said to him: My Lord, thou knowest. And he said to me: These are they who are come out of great tribulation, and have washed their robes, and have made them white in the blood of the Lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 21:3–4; Revelation 22:3; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> Therefore they are before the throne of God, and they serve him day and night in his temple: and he, that sitteth on the throne, shall dwell over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 49:10; Psalms 121:6; Matthew 5:6"> They shall no more hunger nor thirst, neither shall the sun fall on them, nor any heat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 7:37–38; Psalms 36:8–9; John 4:14"> For the Lamb, which is in the midst of the throne, shall rule them, and shall lead them to the fountains of the waters of life, and God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes.</span>
