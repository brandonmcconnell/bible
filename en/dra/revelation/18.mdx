<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:2; Luke 17:24; 2 Thessalonians 2:8"> And after these things, I saw another angel come down from heaven, having great power: and the earth was enlightened with his glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 14:8; Jeremiah 51:8; Jeremiah 51:37"> And he cried out with a strong voice, saying: Babylon the great is fallen, is fallen; and is become the habitation of devils, and the hold of every unclean spirit, and the hold of every unclean and hateful bird:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 14:8; Revelation 17:2; Revelation 18:9"> Because all nations have drunk of the wine of the wrath of her fornication; and the kings of the earth have committed fornication with her; and the merchants of the earth have been made rich by the power of her delicacies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Jeremiah 51:6"> And I heard another voice from heaven, saying: Go out from her, my people; that you be not partakers of her sins, and that you receive not of her plagues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 18:20–21; Ezra 9:6; Revelation 16:19"> For her sins have reached unto heaven, and the Lord hath remembered her iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:29; Psalms 137:8; Jeremiah 51:24"> Render to her as she also hath rendered to you; and double unto her double according to her works: in the cup wherein she hath mingled, mingle ye double unto her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 47:7–9; Zephaniah 2:15; Lamentations 1:1"> As much as she hath glorified herself, and lived in delicacies, so much torment and sorrow give ye to her; because she saith in her heart: I sit a queen, and am no widow; and sorrow I shall not see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:34; Jeremiah 50:31; Revelation 17:16"> Therefore shall her plagues come in one day, death, and mourning, and famine, and she shall be burnt with the fire; because God is strong, who shall judge her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 19:3; Revelation 18:3; Revelation 17:2"> And the kings of the earth, who have committed fornication, and lived in delicacies with her, shall weep, and bewail themselves over her, when they shall see the smoke of her burning:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 18:19; Revelation 18:8; Revelation 18:15–17"> Standing afar off for fear of her torments, saying: Alas! alas! that great city Babylon, that mighty city: for in one hour is thy judgment come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 18:3; Revelation 18:23; Ezekiel 27:27–36"> And the merchants of the earth shall weep, and mourn over her: for no man shall buy their merchandise any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 17:4; Ezekiel 27:5–25; Proverbs 8:10–11"> Merchandise of gold and silver, and precious stones; and of pearls, and fine linen, and purple, and silk, and scarlet, and all thyine wood, and all manner of vessels of ivory, and all manner of vessels of precious stone, and of brass, and of iron, and of marble,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:13; Amos 2:6; 2 Peter 2:3"> And cinnamon, and odours, and ointment, and frankincense, and wine, and oil, and fine flour, and wheat, and beasts, and sheep, and horses, and chariots, and slaves, and souls of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:6; Psalms 106:14; Luke 16:25"> And the fruits of the desire of thy soul are departed from thee, and all fat and goodly things are perished from thee, and they shall find them no more at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 18:3; Acts 19:24–27; Ezekiel 27:31"> The merchants of these things, who were made rich, shall stand afar off from her, for fear of her torments, weeping and mourning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 17:4; Luke 16:19–31; Revelation 18:10–11"> And saying: Alas! alas! that great city, which was clothed with fine linen, and purple, and scarlet, and was gilt with gold, and precious stones, and pearls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:27–36; Revelation 17:16; Isaiah 23:14"> For in one hour are so great riches come to nought; and every shipmaster, and all that sail into the lake, and mariners, and as many as work in the sea, stood afar off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 13:4; Ezekiel 27:30–32; Revelation 18:9–10"> And cried, seeing the place of her burning, saying: What city is like to this great city?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 18:10; Joshua 7:6; Ezekiel 27:30"> And they cast dust upon their heads, and cried, weeping and mourning, saying: Alas! alas! that great city, wherein all were made rich, that had ships at sea, by reason of her prices: for in one hour she is made desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 6:10; Revelation 19:1–3; Luke 18:7–8"> Rejoice over her, thou heaven, and ye holy apostles and prophets; for God hath judged your judgment on her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:63–64; Ezekiel 26:21; Revelation 5:2"> And a mighty angel took up a stone, as it were a great millstone, and cast it into the sea, saying: With such violence as this shall Babylon, that great city, be thrown down, and shall be found no more at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:13; Jeremiah 25:10; Jeremiah 7:34"> And the voice of harpers, and of musicians, and of them that play on the pipe, and on the trumpet, shall no more be heard at all in thee; and no craftsman of any art whatsoever shall be found any more at all in thee; and the sound of the mill shall be heard no more at all in thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 18:3; Isaiah 23:8; Nahum 3:4"> And the light of the lamp shall shine no more at all in thee; and the voice of the bridegroom and the bride shall be heard no more at all in thee: for thy merchants were the great men of the earth, for all nations have been deceived by thy enchantments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 17:6; Revelation 16:6; Jeremiah 51:49"> And in her was found the blood of prophets and of saints, and of all that were slain upon the earth.</span>
