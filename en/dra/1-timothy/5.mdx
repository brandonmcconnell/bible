<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 19:32; 1 Peter 5:5–6; Titus 2:2"> An ancient man rebuke not, but entreat him as a father: young men, as brethren:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:12; Matthew 12:50; Philippians 4:8"> Old women, as mothers: young women, as sisters, in all chastity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:19; Acts 6:1; Deuteronomy 10:18"> Honour widows, that are widows indeed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1–3; John 19:26–27; Matthew 15:4–6"> But if any widow have children, or grandchildren, let her learn first to govern her own house, and to make a return of duty to her parents: for this is acceptable before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:3; Luke 2:37; 1 Corinthians 7:32"> But she that is a widow indeed, and desolate, let her trust in God, and continue in supplications and prayers night and day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 5:5; Revelation 18:7; Luke 15:24"> For she that liveth in pleasures, is dead while she is living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:11; Titus 2:15; 2 Timothy 4:1"> And this give in charge, that they may be blameless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 6:10; 2 Corinthians 12:14; Luke 11:11–13"> But if any man have not care of his own, and especially of those of his house, he hath denied the faith, and is worse than an infidel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2; 1 Timothy 5:11; Luke 2:36–37"> Let a widow be chosen of no less than threescore years of age, who hath been the wife of one husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:18; Titus 3:8; Acts 9:36"> Having testimony for her good works, if she have brought up children, if she have received to harbour, if she have washed the saints’ feet, if she have ministered to them that suffer tribulation, if she have diligently followed every good work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:14; Hosea 13:6; James 5:5"> But the younger widows avoid. For when they have grown wanton in Christ, they will marry:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 1:6; James 3:1; 1 Corinthians 11:34"> Having damnation, because they have made void their first faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 20:19; 2 Thessalonians 3:6–11; Acts 20:30"> And withal being idle they learn to go about from house to house: and are not only idle, but tattlers also, and busybodies, speaking things which they ought not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 2:5; 1 Timothy 6:1; Titus 2:8"> I will therefore that the younger should marry, bear children, be mistresses of families, give no occasion to the adversary to speak evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Peter 2:2; Jude 1:4–5; 2 Peter 2:20–22"> For some are already turned aside after Satan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:3–5; 1 Timothy 5:8"> If any of the faithful have widows, let him minister to them, and let not the church be charged: that there may be sufficient for them that are widows indeed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 13:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:12–13; Galatians 6:6"> Let the priests that rule well, be esteemed worthy of double honour: especially they who labour in the word and doctrine:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:4; 1 Corinthians 9:14; 1 Corinthians 9:7–10"> For the scripture saith: Thou shalt not muzzle the ox that treadeth out the corn: and, The labourer is worthy of his reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 18:16; Deuteronomy 19:15; 2 Corinthians 13:1"> Against a priest receive not an accusation, but under two or three witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:2; Titus 1:13; 1 Timothy 1:20"> Them that sin reprove before all: that the rest also may have fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; 2 Timothy 4:1; 1 Timothy 6:13"> I charge thee before God, and Christ Jesus, and the elect angels, that thou observe these things without prejudice, doing nothing by declining to either side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; 1 Timothy 3:10; 1 Timothy 3:6"> Impose not hands lightly upon any man, neither be partaker of other men’s sins. Keep thyself chaste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 104:15; Proverbs 31:4–7; 1 Timothy 4:4"> Do not still drink water, but use a little wine for thy stomach’s sake, and thy frequent infirmities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:10; Acts 8:18; Galatians 5:19–21"> Some men’s sins are manifest, going before to judgment: and some men they follow after.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 10:9; Luke 11:33; Psalms 37:5–6"> In like manner also good deeds are manifest: and they that are otherwise, cannot be hid.</span>
