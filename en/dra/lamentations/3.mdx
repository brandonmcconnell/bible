<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 88:7; Job 19:21; Psalms 88:15–16"> Aleph. I am the man that see my poverty by the rod of his indignation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 30:26; Isaiah 59:9; Amos 5:18–20"> Aleph. He hath led me, and brought me into darkness, and not into light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:20; Job 31:21; Isaiah 1:25"> Aleph. Only against me he hath turned, and turned again his hand all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 38:13; Psalms 51:8; Psalms 31:9–10"> Beth. My skin and my flesh he hath made old, he hath broken my bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Lamentations 3:19; Jeremiah 23:15; Job 19:8"> Beth. He hath built round about me, and he hath compassed me with gall and labour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 88:5–6; Psalms 143:3; Psalms 143:7"> Beth. He hath set me in dark places as those that are dead for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 19:8; Job 3:23; Jeremiah 40:4"> Ghimel. He hath built against me round about, that I may not get out: he hath made my fetters heavy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 30:20; Psalms 22:2; Job 19:7"> Ghimel. Yea, and when I cry, and entreat, he hath shut out my prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 63:17; Lamentations 3:11; Isaiah 30:28"> Ghimel. He hath shut up my ways with square stones, he hath turned my paths upside down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 10:9; Amos 5:18–20; Hosea 13:7–8"> Daleth. He is become to me as a bear lying in wait: as a lion in secret places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 6:1; Job 16:12–13; Lamentations 1:13"> Daleth. He hath turned aside my paths, and hath broken me in pieces, he hath made me desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 38:2; Job 7:20; Job 6:4"> Daleth. He hath bent his bow, and set me as a mark for his arrows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 6:4; Job 41:28; Deuteronomy 32:23"> He. He hath shot into my reins the daughters of his quiver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:7; Lamentations 3:63; Psalms 22:6–7"> He. I am made a derision to all my people, their song all the day long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:15; Jeremiah 23:15; Ruth 1:20"> He. He hath filled me with bitterness, he hath inebriated me with wormwood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 20:17; Jeremiah 6:26; Psalms 3:7"> Vau. And he hath broken my teeth one by one, he hath fed me with ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 59:11; Zechariah 8:10; Jeremiah 8:15"> Vau. And my soul is removed far off from peace, I have forgotten good things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 17:15; 1 Samuel 27:1; Psalms 116:11"> Vau. And I said: My end and my hope is perished from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Lamentations 3:15; Lamentations 3:5; Psalms 89:50"> Zain. Remember my poverty, and transgression, the wormwood, and the gall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 42:11; Psalms 42:5–6; Job 21:6"> Zain. I will be mindful and remember, and my soul shall languish within me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:3; Psalms 130:7; Psalms 77:7–11"> Zain. These things I shall think over in my heart, therefore will I hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 86:15; Luke 1:50; Nehemiah 9:31"> Heth. The mercies of the Lord that we are not consumed: because his commiserations have not failed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 10:23; Psalms 30:5; Isaiah 33:2"> Heth. They are new every morning, great is thy faithfulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 73:26; Psalms 16:5; Lamentations 3:21"> Heth. The Lord is my portion, said my soul: therefore will I wait for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Micah 7:7–8; Isaiah 40:31; Psalms 130:5–6"> Teth. The Lord is good to them that hope in him, to the soul that seeketh him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Psalms 37:34; Psalms 130:5"> Teth. It is good to wait with silence for the salvation of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:1; Matthew 11:29–30; Psalms 119:71"> Teth. It is good for a man, when he hath borne the yoke from his youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:17; Lamentations 2:10; Psalms 102:7"> Jod. He shall sit solitary, and hold his peace: because he hath taken it up upon himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 40:4; Luke 15:18–19; Romans 3:19"> Jod. He shall put his mouth in the dust, if so be there may be hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 16:10; Isaiah 50:6; Matthew 26:67"> Jod. He shall give his cheek to him that striketh him, he shall be filled with reproaches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 94:14; Psalms 103:8–10; Psalms 77:7"> Caph. For the Lord will not cast off for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 78:38; Psalms 30:5; Psalms 103:11"> Caph. For if he hath cast off, he will also have mercy, according to the multitude of his mercies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:11; Ezekiel 18:32; Hebrews 12:9–10"> Caph. For he hath not willingly afflicted, nor cast off the children of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Zechariah 9:11–12; Isaiah 51:22–23; Psalms 102:20"> Lamed. To crush under his feet all the prisoners of the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Lamed. To turn aside the judgment of a man before the face of the most High,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 59:15; Habakkuk 1:13; Jeremiah 22:3"> Lamed. To destroy a man wrongfully in his judgment, the Lord hath not approved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Psalms 33:9–11; Isaiah 46:10; Proverbs 16:9"> Mem. Who is he that hath commanded a thing to be done, when the Lord commandeth it not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Amos 3:6; Jeremiah 32:42"> Mem. Shall not both evil and good proceed out of the mouth of the Highest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Proverbs 19:3; Micah 7:9; Genesis 4:5–7"> Mem. Why hath a living man murmured, man suffering for his sins?</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:5; Psalms 139:23–24; Joel 2:12–13"> Nun. Let us search our ways, and seek, and return to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Psalms 25:1; Psalms 28:2; Psalms 86:4"> Nun. Let us lift up our hearts with our hands to the Lord in the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 15:18–19; Nehemiah 9:26; 2 Kings 24:4"> Nun. We have done wickedly, and provoked thee to wrath: therefore thou art inexorable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Lamentations 2:17; Lamentations 2:21; Lamentations 3:66"> Samech. Thou hast covered in thy wrath, and hast struck us: thou hast killed and hast not spared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Lamentations 3:8; Zechariah 7:13; Psalms 97:2"> Samech. Thou hast set a cloud before thee, that our prayer may not pass through.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:13; Lamentations 2:15; Lamentations 3:14"> Samech. Thou hast made me as an outcast, and refuse in the midst of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 22:6–8; Lamentations 2:16; Psalms 44:13–14"> Phe. All our enemies have opened their mouths against us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Isaiah 24:17–18; Isaiah 51:19; Jeremiah 48:43–44"> Phe. Prophecy is become to us a fear, and a snare, and destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Lamentations 2:11; Jeremiah 9:18; Jeremiah 9:1"> Phe. My eye hath run down with streams of water, for the destruction of the daughter of my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:17; Psalms 77:2; Lamentations 1:16"> Ain. My eye is afflicted, and hath not been quiet, because there was no rest:</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Isaiah 63:15; Lamentations 5:1; Daniel 9:16–19"> Ain. Till the Lord regarded and looked down from the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:9; Luke 19:41–44; Lamentations 1:18"> Ain. My eye hath wasted my soul because of all the daughters of my city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; Psalms 35:7; Jeremiah 37:15–16"> Sade. My enemies have chased me and caught me like a bird, without cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Daniel 6:17; Jeremiah 38:9; Jeremiah 38:6"> Sade. My life is fallen into the pit, and they have laid a stone over me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Jonah 2:3–5; Ezekiel 37:11; Lamentations 3:18"> Sade. Waters have flowed over my head: I said: I am cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Acts 16:24–28; Psalms 142:3–7; Jonah 2:2–4"> Coph. I have called upon thy name, O Lord, from the lowest pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Isaiah 38:5; Job 34:28; Psalms 6:8–9"> Coph. Thou hast heard my voice: turn not away thy ear from my sighs, and cries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="James 4:8; Isaiah 41:10; Isaiah 58:9"> Coph. Thou drewest near in the day, when I called upon thee, thou saidst: Fear not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Psalms 34:22–1; Jeremiah 51:36; Psalms 103:4"> Res. Thou hast judged, O Lord, the cause of my soul, thou the Redeemer of my life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Psalms 43:1; Psalms 9:4; Genesis 31:42"> Res. Thou hast seen, O Lord, their iniquity against me: judge thou my judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:19–20; Psalms 10:14; Lamentations 3:59"> Res. Thou hast seen all their fury, and all their thoughts against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Lamentations 5:1; Psalms 89:50; Zephaniah 2:8"> Sin. Thou hast heard their reproach, O Lord, all their imaginations against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:3; Psalms 59:7; Psalms 140:3"> Sin. The lips of them that rise up against me: and their devices against me all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Psalms 139:2; Lamentations 3:14; Job 30:9"> Sin. Behold their sitting down, and their rising up, I am their song.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Psalms 28:4; Jeremiah 11:20; 2 Timothy 4:14"> Thau. Thou shalt render them a recompense, O Lord, according to the works of their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10; 1 Corinthians 16:22; Deuteronomy 2:30"> Thau. Thou shalt give them a buckler of heart, thy labour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Psalms 8:3; Lamentations 3:43; Psalms 115:16"> Thau. Thou shalt persecute them in anger, and shalt destroy them from under the heavens, O Lord.</span>
