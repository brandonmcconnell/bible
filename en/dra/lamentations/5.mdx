<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:21; Exodus 22:24; Jeremiah 15:8"> We are become orphans without a father: our mothers are as widows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 3:1; Ezekiel 4:9–17; Deuteronomy 28:48"> We have drunk our water for money: we have bought our wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:36–37; Lamentations 1:14; Jeremiah 28:14"> We were dragged by the necks, we were weary and no rest was given us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 12:1; Hosea 9:3; Hosea 7:11"> We have given our hand to Egypt, and to the Assyrians, that we might be satisfied with bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:29; Jeremiah 16:12; Ezekiel 18:2"> Our fathers have sinned, and are not: and we have borne their iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:15; Zechariah 11:6; Proverbs 30:22"> Servants have ruled over us: there was none to redeem us out of their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:16–17; Jeremiah 40:9–12; Ezekiel 12:18–19"> We fetched our bread at the peril of our lives, because of the sword in the desert.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 13:16; Zechariah 14:2; Deuteronomy 28:30"> They oppressed the women in Sion, and the virgins in the cities of Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Lamentations 4:16; Isaiah 47:6; Lamentations 2:20"> The princes were hanged up by their hand: they did not respect the persons of the ancient.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 16:21; Exodus 11:5; Matthew 23:4"> They abused the young men indecently: and the children fell under the wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:34; Isaiah 24:7–11; Ezekiel 26:13"> The ancients have ceased from the gates: the young men from the choir of the singers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Amos 8:10; Jeremiah 25:10; James 4:9–10"> The joy of our heart is ceased, our dancing is turned into mourning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 89:39; Jeremiah 13:18; Isaiah 3:9–11"> The crown is fallen from our head woe to us, because we have sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 1:5; Job 17:7; Lamentations 2:11"> Therefore is our heart sorrowful, therefore are our eyes become dim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Micah 3:12; Jeremiah 9:11; Psalms 74:2–3"> For mount Sion, because it is destroyed, foxes have walked upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 102:12; Hebrews 1:8–12; Psalms 45:6"> But thou, O Lord, shalt remain for ever, thy throne from generation to generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 44:24; Psalms 13:1; Jeremiah 14:19–21"> Why wilt thou forget us for ever? why wilt thou forsake us for a long time?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 80:3; Psalms 80:7; Psalms 85:4"> Convert us, O Lord, to thee, and we shall be converted: renew our days, as from the beginning.</span>
