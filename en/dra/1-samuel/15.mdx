<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:16; 1 Samuel 12:14; Psalms 2:10–11"> And Samuel said to Saul: The Lord sent me to anoint thee king over his People Israel: now therefore hearken thou unto the voice of the Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:17–19; Exodus 17:8–16; Numbers 24:20"> Thus saith the Lord of hosts: I have reckoned up all that Amalec hath done to Israel: I how he opposed them in the way when they came up out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:16–18; Numbers 24:20; Numbers 31:17"> Now therefore go, and smite Amalec, and utterly destroy all that he hath: spare him not, nor covet any thing that is his: but slay both man and woman, child and suckling, ox and sheep, camel and ass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 15:24; 1 Samuel 11:8; 1 Samuel 13:15"> So Saul commanded the people, and numbered them as lambs: two hundred thousand footmen, and ten thousand of the men of Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And when Saul was come to the city of Amalec, he laid ambushes in the torrent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 1:16; Numbers 10:29–32; Exodus 18:19"> And Saul said to the Cinite: Go, depart and get ye down from Amalec: lest I destroy thee with him. For thou hast shewn kindness to all the children of Israel, when they came up out of Egypt. And the Cinite departed from the midst of Amalec.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 16:7; Genesis 25:18; 1 Samuel 14:48"> And Saul smote Amalec from Hevila, until thou comest to Sur, which is over against Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:1; Esther 3:1; Numbers 24:7"> And he took Agag the king of Amalec alive: but all the common people he slew with the edge of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:15; 1 Samuel 15:19; 1 Samuel 15:3"> And Saul and the people spared Agag and the best of the flocks of sheep and of the herds, and the garments and the rams, and all that was beautiful, and would not destroy them: but every thing that was vile and good for nothing, that they destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And the word of the Lord came to Samuel, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:13; Genesis 6:6; 2 Samuel 24:16"> It repenteth me that I have made Saul king: for he hath forsaken me, and hath not executed my commandments. And Samuel was grieved, and he cried unto the Lord all night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 15:55; 2 Samuel 18:18; 1 Samuel 25:2"> And when Samuel rose early, to go to Saul in the morning, it was told Samuel, that Saul was come to Carmel, and had erected for himself a triumphant arch, and returning had passed on, and gone down to Galgal. And Samuel came to Saul, and Saul was offering a holocaust to the Lord out of the choicest of the spoils which he had brought from Amalec.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 17:2; Ruth 3:10; Proverbs 31:31"> And when Samuel was come to Saul, Saul said to him: Blessed be thou of the Lord, I have fulfilled the word of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 3:13–15; Jeremiah 2:18–19; Jeremiah 2:34–37"> And Samuel said: What meaneth then this bleating of the flocks, which soundeth in my ears, and the lowing of the herds, which I hear?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:21; 1 Samuel 15:9; Exodus 32:22–23"> And Saul said: They have brought them from Amalec: for the people spared the best of the sheep and of the herds that they might be sacrificed to the Lord thy God, but the rest we have slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:27; 1 Kings 22:16; 1 Samuel 12:7"> And Samuel said to Saul: Suffer me, and I will tell thee what the Lord hath said to me this night. And he said to him: Speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:22; 1 Samuel 9:21; 1 Samuel 15:1–3"> And Samuel said: When thou wast a little one in thy own eyes, wast thou not made the head of the tribes of Israel? And the Lord anointed thee to be king over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 10:29; Genesis 13:13; Genesis 15:16"> And the Lord sent thee on the way, and said: Go, and kill the sinners of Amalec, and thou shalt fight against them until thou hast utterly destroyed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:32; 2 Chronicles 33:6; 2 Chronicles 36:12"> Why then didst thou not hearken to the voice of the Lord: but hast turned to the prey, and hast done evil in the eyes of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:13; Matthew 19:20; Romans 10:3"> And Saul said to Samuel: Yea I have hearkened to the voice of the Lord, and have walked in the way by which the Lord sent me, and have brought Agag the king of Amalec, and Amalec I have slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:15; Exodus 32:22–23; Genesis 3:13"> But the people took of the spoils sheep and oxen, as the firstfruits of those things that were slain, to offer sacrifice to the Lord their God in Galgal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 6:6; Psalms 51:16–17; Proverbs 21:3"> And Samuel said: Doth the Lord desire holocausts and victims, and not rather that the voice of the Lord should be obeyed? For obedience is better than sacrifices: and to hearken rather than to offer the fat of rams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 28:16; 1 Samuel 13:14; Leviticus 20:6"> Because it is like the sin of witchcraft, to rebel: and like the crime of idolatry, to refuse to obey. Forasmuch therefore as thou hast rejected the word of the Lord, the Lord hath also rejected thee from being king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:13; Isaiah 51:12–13; Numbers 22:34"> And Saul said to Samuel: I have sinned because I have transgressed the commandment of the Lord, and thy words, fearing the people, and obeying their voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 10:17"> But now bear, I beseech thee, my sin, and return with me, that I may adore the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hosea 4:6; 1 Samuel 13:14; 1 Samuel 16:1"> And Samuel said to Saul: I will not return with thee, because thou hast rejected the word of the Lord, and the Lord hath rejected thee from being king over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Kings 11:30–31"> And Samuel turned about to go away: but he laid hold upon the skirt of his mantle, and it rent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:17–18; Acts 13:22; Daniel 4:32"> And Samuel said to him: The Lord hath rent the kingdom of Israel from thee this day, and hath given it to thy neighbour who is better than thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:14; Numbers 23:19; Titus 1:2"> But the triumpher in Israel will riot spare, and will not be moved to repentance: for he is not a mail that he should repent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 5:44; John 12:43; Isaiah 29:13"> Then he said: I have sinned: yet honour me now before the ancients of my people, and before Israel, and return with me, that I may adore the Lord thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> So Samuel turned again after Saul: and Saul adored the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Revelation 18:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Jeremiah 48:44"> And Samuel said: Bring hitherto me Agag the king of Amalec. And Agag was presented to him very fat, and trembling. And Agag said: Doth bitter death separate in this manner?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Judges 1:7; Genesis 9:6; Matthew 7:2"> And Samuel said: As thy sword hath made women childless, so shall thy mother be childless among women. And Samuel hewed him in pieces before the Lord in Galgal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:4; 1 Samuel 7:17"> And Samuel departed to Ramatha: but Saul went up to his house in Gabaa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:11; 1 Samuel 19:24; 1 Samuel 16:1"> And Samuel saw Saul no more till the day of his death: nevertheless Samuel mourned for Saul, because the Lord repented that he had made him king over Israel.</span>
