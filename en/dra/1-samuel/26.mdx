<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:19; Psalms 54:1–7; Joshua 15:24"> And the men of Ziph came to Saul in Gabaa, saying: Behold David is hid in the hill of Hachila, which is over against the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:2; 1 Samuel 13:2; Psalms 140:4–9"> And Saul arose, and went down to the wilderness of Ziph, having with him three thousand chosen men of Israel, to seek David in the wilderness of Ziph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:19; 1 Samuel 26:1"> And Saul encamped in Gabaa Hachila, which was over against the wilderness in the way: and David abode in the wilderness. And seeing that Saul was come after him into the wilderness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:16; Joshua 2:1"> He sent spies, and learned that he was most certainly come thither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:55; 1 Samuel 17:20; 1 Samuel 14:50–51"> And David arose secretly, and came to the place where Saul was: and when he had beheld the place, wherein Saul slept, and Abner the son of Ner, the captain of his army, and Saul sleeping in a tent, and the rest of the multitude round about him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 7:10–11; 2 Samuel 2:18; Genesis 15:20"> David spoke to Achimelech the Hethite, and Abisai the son of Sarvia the brother of Joab, saying: Who will go down with me to Saul into the camp? And Abisai said: I will go with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2–3"> So David and Abisai came to the people by night, and found Saul lying and sleeping in the tent, and his spear fixed in the ground at his head: and Abner and the people sleeping round about him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:4; 1 Samuel 26:23; Deuteronomy 32:30"> And Abisai said to David: God hath shut up thy enemy this day into thy hands: now then I will run him through with my spear even to the earth at once, and there shall be no need of a second time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:14; Psalms 105:15; 2 Samuel 1:16"> And David said to Abisai: Kill him not: for who shall put forth his hand against the Lord’s anointed, and shall be guiltless?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 47:29; Deuteronomy 31:14; Psalms 37:13"> And David said: As the Lord liveth, unless the Lord shall strike him, or his day shall come to die, or he shall go down to battle and perish:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:6; 1 Samuel 24:12; 2 Samuel 1:14"> The Lord be merciful unto me, that I extend not my hand upon the Lord’s anointed. But now take the spear, which is at his head, and the cup of water, and let us go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 2:21; Genesis 15:12; Isaiah 29:10"> So David took the spear, and the cup of water which was at Saul’s head, and they went away: and no man saw it, or knew it, or awaked, but they were all asleep, for a deep sleep from the Lord was fallen upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:8; Judges 9:7"> And when David was gone over to the other side. and stood on the top of the hill afar off, and a good space was between them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> David cried to the people, and to Abner the son of Ner, saying: Wilt thou not answer, Abner? And Abner answering, said: Who art thou, that criest, and disturbest the king?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And David said to Abner: Art not thou a man? and who is like thee in Israel? why then hast thou not kept thy lord the king? for there came one of the people in to kill the king thy lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 79:11; 2 Samuel 12:5; 1 Samuel 26:9"> This thing is not good, that thou hast done: as the Lord liveth, you are the sons of death, who have not kept your master, the Lord’s anointed. And now where is the king’s spear, and the cup of water, which was at his head?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:16; 1 Samuel 24:8"> And Saul knew David’s voice, and said: Is this thy voice, my son David? And David said: It is my voice, my lord the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:9; 1 Samuel 24:11–14; John 10:32"> And he said: Wherefore doth my lord persecute his servant? What have I done? or what evil is there in my hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:16; 2 Samuel 16:11; 2 Samuel 20:19"> Now therefore hear, I pray thee, my lord the king, the words of thy servant: If the Lord stir thee up against me, let him accept of sacrifice: but if the sons of men, they are cursed in the sight of the Lord, who have cast me out this day, that I should not dwell in the inheritance of the Lord, saying: Go, serve strange gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:14; Matthew 26:55; 1 Samuel 25:29"> And now let not my blood be shed upon the earth before the Lord: for the king of Israel is come out to seek a flea, as the partridge is hunted in the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:24; 1 Samuel 24:17; Exodus 9:27"> And Saul said: I have sinned, return, my son David, for I will no more do thee harm, because my life hath been precious in thy eyes this day: for it appeareth that I have done foolishly, and have been ignorant in very many things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And David answering, said: Behold the king’s spear: let one of the king’s servants come over and fetch it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 62:12; 1 Samuel 24:6–7; Psalms 18:20–26"> And the Lord will reward every one according to his justice, and his faithfulness: for the Lord hath delivered thee this day into my hand, and I would not put forth my hand against the Lord’s anointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; 2 Corinthians 1:9–10; Psalms 54:7"> And as thy life hath been much set by this day in my eyes, so let my life be much set by in the eyes of the Lord, and let him deliver me from all distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 24:9–10; Proverbs 26:25; 1 Samuel 24:19"> Then Saul said to David: Blessed art thou, my son David: and truly doing thou shalt do, and prevailing thou shalt prevail. And David went on his way, and Saul returned to his place.</span>
