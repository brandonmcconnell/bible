<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 12:5; Micah 6:6"> Saul was a child of one year when he began to reign, and he reigned two years over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:26; 1 Samuel 13:5; 1 Samuel 14:31"> And Saul chose him three thousand men of Israel: and two thousand were with Saul in Machmas, and in mount Bethel: and a thousand with Jonathan in Gabaa of Benjamin, and the rest of the people he sent back every man to their dwellings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:5; Judges 3:27; Judges 6:34"> And Jonathan smote the garrison of the Philistines which was in Gabaa. And when the Philistines had heard of it, Saul sounded the trumpet over all the land, saying: Let the Hebrews hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; Exodus 5:21; Zechariah 11:8"> And all Israel heard this report: Saul hath smitten the garrison of the Philistines: and Israel took courage against the Philistines. And the people were called together after Saul to Galgal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:23; Joshua 11:4; Joshua 18:12"> The Philistines also were assembled to fight against Israel, thirty thousand chariots, and six thousand horsemen, and a multitude of people besides, like the sand on the sea shore for number. And going up they camped in Machmas at the east of Bethaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 6:2; Hebrews 11:38; Judges 10:9"> And when the men of Israel saw that they were straitened, (for the people were distressed,) they hid themselves in caves, and in thickets, and in rocks, and in dens, and in pits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:8; Leviticus 26:17; Numbers 32:1–5"> And some of the Hebrews passed over the Jordan into the land of Gad and Galaad. And when Saul was yet in Galgal, all the people that followed him were greatly afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:8"> And he waited seven days according to the appointment of Samuel, I and Samuel came not to Galgal, and the people slipt away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 3:4; Proverbs 21:27; Proverbs 15:8"> Then Saul said: Bring me the holocaust, and the peace offerings. And he offered the holocaust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:13; Ruth 2:4; Psalms 129:8"> And when he had made an end of offering the holocaust, behold Samuel came: and Saul went forth to meet him and salute him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:23; 1 Samuel 13:5; 1 Samuel 13:16"> And Samuel said to him: What hast thou done? Saul answered: Because I saw that the people slipt from me, and thou wast not come according to the days appointed, and the Philistines were gathered together in Machmas,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 66:3; Amos 8:5; 2 Corinthians 9:7"> I said: Now will the Philistines come down upon me to Galgal, and I have not appeased the face of the Lord. Forced by necessity, I offered the holocaust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:22; 2 Chronicles 16:9; 1 Samuel 15:28"> And Samuel said to Saul: Thou hast done foolishly, and hast not kept the commandments of the Lord thy God, which he commanded thee. And if thou hadst not done thus, the Lord would now have established thy kingdom over Israel for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 13:22; 1 Samuel 15:28; Psalms 89:19–37"> But thy kingdom shall not continue. The Lord hath sought him a man according to his own heart: and him hath the Lord commanded to be prince over his people, because thou hast not observed that which the Lord commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:2; 1 Samuel 13:2; 1 Samuel 13:6–7"> And Samuel arose and went up from Galgal to Gabaa of Benjamin. And the rest of the people went up after Saul, to meet the people who fought against them, going from Galgal to Gabaa in the hill of Benjamin. And Saul numbered the people, that were found with him, about six hundred men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:3"> And Saul and Jonathan his son, and the people that were present with them, were in Gabaa of Benjamin: but the Philistines encamped in Machmas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 18:23; 1 Samuel 14:15; Joshua 19:3"> And there went out of the camp of the Philistines three companies to plunder. One company went towards the way of Ephra to the land of Sual;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:34; Joshua 18:13–14; Joshua 16:3"> And another went by the way of Beth-horon, and the third turned to the way of the border, above the valley of Seboim towards the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 24:14; Judges 5:8; Jeremiah 24:1"> Now there was no smith to be found in all the land of Israel, for the Philistines had taken this precaution, lest the Hebrews should make them swords or spears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> So all Israel went down to the Philistines, to sharpen every man his ploughshare, and his spade, and his axe, and his rake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> So that their shares, and their spades, and their forks, and their axes were blunt, even to the goad, which was to be mended.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 5:8; Zechariah 4:6; 1 Samuel 17:50"> And when the day of battle was come, there was neither sword nor spear found in the hand of any of the people that were with Saul and Jonathan, except Saul and Jonathan his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 10:28; 1 Samuel 14:1; 1 Samuel 14:4–5"> And the army of the Philistines went out in order to advance further in Machmas.</span>
