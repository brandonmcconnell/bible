<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:12; 1 Samuel 4:1; Joshua 13:3"> And the Philistines took the ark of God, and carried it from the Stone of help into Azotus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 16:23; Daniel 5:2; 1 Chronicles 10:10"> And the Philistines took the ark of God, and brought it into the temple of Dagon, and set it by Dagon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 19:1; Isaiah 46:1–2; Isaiah 46:7"> And when the Azotians arose early the next day, behold Dagon lay upon his face on the ground before the ark of the Lord: and they took Dagon, and set him again in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 1:7; Isaiah 2:18–19; Jeremiah 10:11"> And the next day again, when they rose in the morning, they found Dagon lying upon his face on the earth before the ark of the Lord: and the head of Dagon, and both the palms of his hands were cut off upon the threshold:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:9; Psalms 115:4–7; Joshua 5:15"> And only the stump of Dagon remained in its place. For this cause neither the priests of Dagon, nor any that go into the temple tread on the threshold of Dagon in Azotus unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:11; Acts 13:11; Exodus 9:3"> And the hand of the Lord was heavy upon the Azotians, and he destroyed them, and afflicted Azotus and the coasts thereof with emerods. And in the villages and fields in the midst of that country, there came forth a multitude of mice, and there was the confusion of a great mortality in the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 12:33; Jeremiah 48:7; 1 Chronicles 15:13"> And the men of Azotus seeing this kind of plague, said: The ark of the God of Israel shall not stay with us: for his hand is heavy upon us, and upon Dagon our god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:11; 1 Samuel 17:4; Zechariah 12:3"> And sending, they gathered together all the lords of the Philistines to them, and said: What shall we do with the ark of the God of Israel? And the Gethrites answered: Let the ark of the God of Israel be carried about. And they carried the ark of the God of Israel about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:6; 1 Samuel 7:13; Deuteronomy 2:15"> And while they were carrying it about, the band of the Lord came upon every city with an exceeding great slaughter: and he smote the men of every city, both small and great, and they had emerods in their secret parts. And the Gethrites consulted together, and made themselves seats of skins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 1:8; Judges 1:18; Joshua 15:45"> Therefore they sent the ark of God into Accaron. And when the ark of God was come into Accaron, the Accaronites cried out, saying: They have brought the ark of the God of Israel to us, to kill us and our people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:6; Jeremiah 48:42–44; Isaiah 13:7–9"> They sent therefore and gathered together all the lords of the Philistines: and they said: Send away the ark of the God of Israel, and let it return into its own place, and not kill us and our people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 12:30; Amos 5:19; 1 Kings 19:17"> For there was the fear of death in every city, and the hand of God was exceeding heavy. The men also that did not die, were afflicted with the emerods: and the cry of every city went up to heaven.</span>
