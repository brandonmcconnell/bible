<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:13; Psalms 78:71; 1 Samuel 9:16"> And Samuel took a little vial of oil and poured it upon his head, and kissed him, and said: Behold, the Lord hath anointed thee to be prince over his inheritance, and thou shalt deliver his people out of the hands of their enemies, that are round about them. And this shall be a sign unto thee, that God hath anointed thee to be prince.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:3–5; Genesis 35:19–20; Joshua 18:28"> When thou shalt depart from me this day, thou shalt find two men by the sepulchre of Rachel in the borders of Benjamin to the south, and they shall say to thee: The asses are found which thou wentest to seek: and thy father thinking no more of the asses is concerned for you, and saith: What shall I do for my son?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 35:3; Genesis 35:1; Genesis 28:22"> And when thou shalt depart from thence, and go farther on, and shalt come to the oak of Thabor, there shall meet thee three men going up to God to Bethel, one carrying three kids, and another three loaves of bread, and another carrying a bottle of wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 18:15"> And they will salute thee, and will give thee two loaves, and thou shalt take them at their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 3:15; 1 Samuel 13:3; 1 Samuel 19:20"> After that thou shalt come to the hill of God, where the garrison of the Philistines is: and when thou shalt be come there into the city, thou shalt meet a company of prophets coming down from the high place, with a psaltery and a timbrel, and a pipe, and a harp before them, and they shall be prophesying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:10; 1 Samuel 19:23–24; Numbers 11:25"> And the spirit of the Lord shall come upon thee, and thou shalt prophesy with them, and shalt be changed into another man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 6:12; Joshua 1:5; Isaiah 7:14"> When therefore these signs shall happen to thee, do whatsoever thy hand shall find, for the Lord is with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:14–15; 1 Samuel 13:4; 1 Samuel 13:8–15"> And thou shalt go down before me to Galgal, (for I will come down to thee,) that thou mayest offer an oblation, and sacrifice victims of peace: seven days shalt thou wait, O till I come to thee, and I will shew thee what thou art to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 38:7–8; 1 Samuel 10:2–6; Judges 7:11"> So when he had turned his back to go from Samuel, God gave unto him another heart, and all these things came to pass that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:5–6; 1 Samuel 19:20–24"> And they came to the foresaid hill, and behold a company of prophets met him: and the spirit of the Lord came upon him, and he prophesied in the midst of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:24; John 7:15; Matthew 13:54–55"> And all that had known him yesterday and the day before, seeing that he was with the prophets, and prophesied, said to each other: What is this that hath happened to the son of Cis? Is Saul also among the prophets?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 54:13; John 6:45; James 1:17"> And one answered another, saying: And who is their father? therefore it became a proverb: Is Saul also among the prophets?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And when he had made an end of prophesying, he came to the high place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:50; 2 Kings 5:25; 1 Samuel 9:3–10"> And Saul’s uncle said to him, and to his servant: Whither went you? They answered: To seek the asses: and not finding them we went to Samuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And his uncle said to him: Tell me what Samuel said to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 29:11; Judges 14:6; 1 Samuel 9:20"> And Saul said to his uncle: He told us that the asses were found. But of the matter of the kingdom of which Samuel had spoken to him, he told him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 20:1; 1 Samuel 7:5–6"> And Samuel called together the people to the Lord in Maspha:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 6:8–9; Nehemiah 9:27–28; Judges 2:1"> And he said to the children of Israel: Thus saith the Lord the God of Israel: I brought up Israel out of Egypt, and delivered you from the hand of the Egyptians, and from the hand of all the kings who afflicted you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:12; 1 Samuel 8:19; Joshua 24:1"> But you this day have rejected your God, who only hath saved you out of all your evils and your tribulations: and you have said: Nay: but set a king over us. Now therefore stand before the Lord by your tribes, and by your families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 7:16–18; Acts 1:24–26; 1 Samuel 14:41"> And Samuel brought to him all the tribes of Israel, and the lot fell on the tribe of Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And he brought the tribe of Benjamin and the kindreds thereof, and the lot fell Upon the kindred of Metri, and it came to Saul the son of Cis. They sought him therefore and he was not found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 27:21; Judges 20:18; 1 Samuel 23:2–4"> And after this they consulted the Lord whether he would come thither. And the Lord answered: Behold he is hidden at home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:2; 1 Samuel 16:7; 1 Samuel 17:4"> And they ran and fetched him thence: and he stood in the midst of the people, and he was higher than any of the people from the shoulders and upward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 1:25; 1 Kings 1:39; 2 Samuel 21:6"> And Samuel said to all the people: Surely you see him whom the Lord hath chosen, that there is none like him among all the people. And all the people cried and said: God save the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:11–18; Deuteronomy 17:14–20; Romans 13:1–7"> And Samuel told the people the law of the kingdom, and wrote it in a book, and laid it up before the Lord: and Samuel sent away all the people, every one to his own house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:4; Joshua 18:28; 1 Samuel 15:34"> Saul also departed to his own house in Gabaa: and there went with him a part of the army, whose hearts God had touched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:13; 2 Chronicles 17:5; 1 Kings 10:25"> But the children of Belial said: Shall this fellow be able to save us? And they despised him, and brought him no presents, but he dissembled as though he heard not.</span>
