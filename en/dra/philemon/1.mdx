<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:1; Ephesians 3:1; Philemon 1:9"> Paul, a prisoner of Christ Jesus, and Timothy, a brother: to Philemon, our beloved and fellow labourer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 2:25; Colossians 4:17; Romans 16:5"> And to Appia, our dearest sister, and to Archippus, our fellow soldier, and to the church which is in thy house:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 1:7; Ephesians 1:2; 2 Corinthians 13:14"> Grace to you and peace from God our Father, and from the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:8; Ephesians 1:16; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> I give thanks to my God, always making a remembrance of thee in my prayers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 1:4; Ephesians 1:15; 1 Corinthians 16:1"> Hearing of thy charity and faith, which thou hast in the Lord Jesus, and towards all the saints:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Philippians 1:9–11; 2 Peter 1:8; Colossians 1:9"> That the communication of thy faith may be made evident in the acknowledgment of every good work, that is in you in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:13; 1 Thessalonians 3:9; 2 Corinthians 7:4"> For I have had great joy and consolation in thy charity, because the bowels of the saints have been refreshed by thee, brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:6; 2 Corinthians 10:8; 2 Corinthians 3:12"> Wherefore though I have much confidence in Christ Jesus, to command thee that which is to the purpose:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:20; Ephesians 4:1; Romans 12:1"> For charity sake I rather beseech, whereas thou art such a one, as Paul an old man, and now a prisoner also of Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 4:9; 1 Corinthians 4:15; 1 Timothy 1:2"> I beseech thee for my son, whom I have begotten in my bands, Onesimus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:11; Job 30:1–2; 1 Peter 2:10"> Who hath been heretofore unprofitable to thee, but now is profitable both to me and thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 6:14–15; Jeremiah 31:20; Mark 11:25"> Whom I have sent back to thee. And do thou receive him as my own bowels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 1:7; Ephesians 3:1; 1 Corinthians 16:17"> Whom I would have retained with me, that in thy stead he might have ministered to me in the bands of the gospel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; 1 Chronicles 29:17; 1 Peter 5:2–3"> But without thy counsel I would do nothing: that thy good deed might not be as it were of necessity, but voluntary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 50:20; Genesis 45:5–8; Isaiah 20:6"> For perhaps he therefore departed for a season from thee, that thou mightest receive him again for ever:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 3:22; Matthew 23:8; 1 Corinthians 7:22"> Not now as a servant, but instead of a servant, a most dear brother, especially to me: but how much more to thee both in the flesh and in the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:23; Matthew 10:40; Philemon 1:12"> If therefore thou count me a partner, receive him as myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4–7"> And if he hath wronged thee in any thing, or is in thy debt, put that to my account.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 6:11; Galatians 5:2; 1 Corinthians 4:15"> I Paul have written it with my own hand: I will repay it: not to say to thee, that thou owest me thy own self also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="3 John 1:4; Philemon 1:7; Philemon 1:12"> Yea, brother. May I enjoy thee in the Lord. Refresh my bowels in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:4; 2 Corinthians 2:3; 2 Corinthians 7:16"> Trusting in thy obedience, I have written to thee: knowing that thou wilt also do more than I say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:11; Philippians 2:24; 2 John 1:12"> But withal prepare me also a lodging. For I hope that through your prayers I shall be given unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Colossians 1:7; Romans 16:7; Colossians 4:12"> There salute thee Epaphras, my fellow prisoner in Christ Jesus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:10–11; Colossians 4:14; Colossians 4:10"> Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, and Luke my fellow labourers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:22; Romans 16:23; Romans 16:20"> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit. Amen.</span>
