<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 7:1; Amos 7:7; Amos 7:4"> These things the Lord shewed to me: and behold a hook to draw down the fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Amos 7:8; Lamentations 4:18; Jeremiah 24:1–3"> And he said: What seest thou, Amos? And I said: A hook to draw down fruit. And the Lord said to me: The end is come upon my people Israel: I will not again pass by them any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 5:23; Amos 6:9–10; Jeremiah 9:21–22"> And the hinges of the temple shall screak in that day, saith the Lord God: many shall die: silence shall be cast in every place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 30:14; Psalms 14:4; Amos 5:11"> Hear this, you that crush the poor, and make the needy of the land to fail,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:15–21; Micah 6:10–11; Hosea 12:7"> Saying: When will the month be over, and we shall sell our wares: and the sabbath, and we shall open the corn: that we may lessen the measure, and increase the sicle, and may convey in deceitful balances,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 2:6; Leviticus 25:39–42; Nehemiah 5:8"> That we may possess the needy for money, and the poor for a pair of shoes, and may sell the refuse of the corn?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 6:8; Hosea 9:9; Hosea 8:13"> The Lord hath sworn against the pride of Jacob: surely I will never forget all their works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 9:5; Hosea 4:3; Jeremiah 46:8"> Shall not the land tremble for this, and every one mourn that dwelleth therein: and rise up altogether as a river, and be cast out, and run down as the river of Egypt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 15:33; Matthew 27:45; Jeremiah 15:9"> And it shall come to pass in that day, saith the Lord God, that the sun shall go down at midday, and I will make the earth dark in the day of light:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:26; Zechariah 12:10; Job 20:23"> And I will turn your feasts into mourning, and all your songs into lamentation: and I will bring up sackcloth upon every back of yours, and baldness upon every head: and I will make it as the mourning of an only son, and the latter end thereof as a bitter day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 74:9; 1 Samuel 3:1; 2 Chronicles 15:3"> Behold the days come, saith the Lord, and I will send forth a famine into the land: not a famine of bread, nor a thirst of water, but of hearing the word of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:7–10; Matthew 24:23–26; Daniel 12:4"> And they shall move from sea to sea, and from the north to the east: they shall go about seeking the word of the Lord, and shall not find it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 2:3; Lamentations 1:18; Lamentations 2:21"> In that day the fair virgins, and the young men shall faint for thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 5:5; 1 Kings 12:28–29; Deuteronomy 9:21"> They that swear by the sin of Samaria, and say: Thy God, O Dan, liveth: and the way of Bersabee liveth: and they shall fall, and shall rise no more.</span>
