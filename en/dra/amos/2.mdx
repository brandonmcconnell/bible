<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:8–9; Ezekiel 25:8–9; Amos 1:3"> Thus saith the Lord: For three crimes of Moab, and for four I will not convert him: because he hath burnt the bones of the king of Edom even to ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:24; Jeremiah 48:41; Amos 1:14"> And I will seed a fire into Moab, and it shall devour the houses of Carioth: and Moab shall die with a noise, with the sound of the trumpet:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 24:17; Jeremiah 48:7; Jeremiah 48:25"> And I will cut off the judge from the midst thereof, and will slay all his princes with him, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:24; Ezekiel 20:16; Ezekiel 20:13"> Thus saith the Lord: For three crimes of Juda, and for four I will not convert him: because he hath cast away the law of the Lord, and hath not kept his commandments: for their idols have caused them to err, after which their fathers have walked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 8:14; Jeremiah 17:27; Jeremiah 21:10"> And I will send a fire into Juda, and it shall devour the houses of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 3:3; Micah 3:2–3; Amos 5:11–12"> Thus saith the Lord: For three crimes of Israel, and for four I will not convert him: because he hath sold the just man for silver, and the poor man for a pair of shoes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 5:12; Isaiah 10:2; Ezekiel 22:11"> They bruise the heads of the poor upon the dust of the earth, and turn aside the way of the humble: and the son and his father have gone to the same young woman, to profane my holy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:12–17; Exodus 22:26–27; Amos 6:6"> And they sat down upon garments laid to pledge by every altar: and drank the wine of the condemned in the house of their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 18:16; Malachi 4:1; Deuteronomy 3:11"> Yet I cast out the Amorrhite before their face: whose height was like the height of cedars, and who was strong as an oak: and I destroyed his fruit from above, and his roots beneath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:7; Exodus 12:51; Amos 3:1"> It is I that brought you up out of the land of Egypt, and I led you forty years through the wilderness, that you might possess the land of the Amorrhite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:25; Numbers 6:2–3; 1 Kings 20:41"> And I raised up of your sons for prophets, and of your young men for Nazarites. Is it not so, O ye children of Israel, saith the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 7:13; Isaiah 30:10; Jeremiah 11:21"> And you will present wine to the Nazarites: and command the prophets, saying: Prophesy not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:14; Isaiah 43:24; Ezekiel 16:43"> Behold, I will screak under you as a wain screaketh that is laden with hay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23; Psalms 33:16; Ecclesiastes 9:11"> And flight shall perish from the swift, and the valiant shall not possess his strength, neither shall the strong save his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 33:16–17; Ezekiel 39:3"> And he that holdeth the bow shall not stand, and the swift of foot shall not escape, neither shall the rider of the horse save his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 14:52; Jeremiah 48:41; 2 Kings 7:8–20"> And the stout of heart among the valiant shall flee away naked in that day, saith the Lord.</span>
