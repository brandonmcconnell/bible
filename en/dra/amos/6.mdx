<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 32:9–11; James 5:5; Luke 12:17–20"> Woe to you that are wealthy in Sion, and to you that have confidence in the mountain of Samaria: ye great men, heads of the people, that go in with state into the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nahum 3:8; Genesis 10:10; 2 Kings 18:34"> Pass ye over to Chalane, and see, and go from thence into Emath the great: and go down into Geth of the Philistines, and to all the best kingdoms of these: if their border be larger than your border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 9:10; Isaiah 56:12; Ezekiel 12:27"> You that are separated unto the evil day: and that approach to the throne of iniquity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 3:12; Luke 16:19; 1 Samuel 25:36–38"> You that sleep upon beds of ivory, and are wanton on your couches: that eat the lambs out of the flock, and the calves out of the midst of the herd;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 5:12; Amos 5:23; Amos 8:3"> You that sing to the sound of the psaltery: they have thought themselves to have instruments of music like David;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 49:22; 1 Corinthians 12:26; Matthew 26:7–9"> That drink wine in bowls, and anoint themselves with the best ointments: and they are not concerned for the affliction of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 7:11; Daniel 5:4–6; Amos 5:5"> Wherefore now they shall go captive at the head of them that go into captivity: and the faction of the luxurious ones shall be taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:14; Amos 4:2; Amos 8:7"> The Lord God hath sworn by his own soul, saith the Lord the God of hosts: I detest the pride of Jacob, and I hate his houses, and I will deliver up the city with the inhabitants thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 5:3; Job 1:2; Psalms 109:13"> And if there remain ten men in one house, they also shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 8:3; 1 Samuel 31:12; Amos 5:13"> And a man’s kinsman shall take him up, and shall burn him, that he may carry the bones out of the house; and he shall say to him that is in the inner rooms of the house: Is there yet any with thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Amos 3:15; Isaiah 55:11; 2 Kings 25:9"> And he shall answer: There is an end. And he shall any to him: Hold thy peace, and mention not the name of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 5:7; Hosea 10:4; Amos 5:11–12"> For behold the Lord hath commanded, and he will strike the greater house with breaches, and the lesser house with clefts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 28:14–15; Luke 12:19–20; 2 Kings 14:25"> Can horses run upon the rocks, or can any one plough with buffles? for you have turned judgment into bitterness, and the fruit of justice into wormwood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 8:65; 2 Kings 14:25; Jeremiah 5:15–17"> You that rejoice in a thing of nought: you that say: Have we not taken unto us horns by our own strength?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> But behold, I will raise up a nation against you, O house of Israel, saith the Lord the God of hosts; and they shall destroy you from the entrance of Emath, even to the torrent of the desert.</span>
