<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 2:10; Jeremiah 8:3; Micah 3:1"> Hear the word that the Lord hath spoken concerning you, O ye children of Israel: concerning the whole family that I brought up out of the land of Egypt, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6; Exodus 19:5–6; Deuteronomy 10:15"> You only have I known of all the families of the earth: therefore will I visit upon you all your iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–16; Genesis 17:1; Genesis 5:22"> Shall two walk together except they be agreed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 11:10; Psalms 104:21; Amos 1:2"> Will a lion roar in the forest, if he have no prey? will the lion’s whelp cry out of his den, if he have taken nothing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:12; Jeremiah 31:28; Daniel 9:14"> Will the bird fall into the snare upon the earth, if there be no fowler? Shall the snare be taken up from the earth, before it hath taken somewhat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Zephaniah 1:16; Isaiah 14:24–27"> Shall the trumpet sound in a city, and the people not be afraid? Shall there be evil in a city, which the Lord hath not done?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:15; Genesis 18:17; Psalms 25:14"> For the Lord God doth nothing without revealing his secret to his servants the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 4:20; Jeremiah 20:9; Amos 3:4"> The lion shall roar, who will not fear? The Lord God hath spoken, who shall not prophesy?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 4:1; Amos 6:1; Amos 1:8"> Publish it in the houses of Azotus, and in the houses of the land of Egypt, and say: Assemble yourselves upon the mountains of Samaria, and behold the many follies in the midst thereof, and them that suffer oppression in the inner rooms thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:22; Zephaniah 1:9; Zechariah 5:3–4"> And they have not known to do the right thing, saith the Lord, storing up iniquity, and robberies in their houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 18:9–11; 2 Kings 17:3–6; 2 Kings 15:19"> Therefore thus saith the Lord God: The land shall be in tribulation, and shall be compassed about: and thy strength shall be taken away from thee, and thy houses shall be spoiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:34–37; Romans 11:4–5; Isaiah 31:4"> Thus saith the Lord: As if a shepherd should get out of the lion’s mouth two legs, or the tip of the ear: so shall the children of Israel be taken out that dwell in Samaria, in a piece of a bed, and in the couch of Damascus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:18–19; 2 Chronicles 24:19; Acts 20:21"> Hear ye, and testify in the house of Jacob, saith the Lord the God of hosts:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 23:15; Hosea 10:5–8; Hosea 10:14–15"> That in the day when I shall begin to visit the transgressions of Israel, I will visit upon him, and upon the altars of Bethel: and the horns of the altars shall be cut off, and shall fall to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 22:39; Jeremiah 36:22; Amos 6:11"> And I will strike the winter house with the summer house: and the houses of ivory shall perish, and many houses shall be destroyed, saith the Lord.</span>
