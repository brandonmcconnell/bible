<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:53; Psalms 139:7–10; Obadiah 1:4"> Though they go down even to hell, thence shall my hand bring them out: and though they climb up to heaven, thence will I bring them down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:16; Jeremiah 23:23–24; Isaiah 27:1"> And though they be hid in the top of Carmel, I will search and take them away from thence: and though they hide themselves from my eyes in the depth of the sea, there will I command the serpent and he shall bite them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:33; Jeremiah 21:10; Jeremiah 44:11"> And if they go into captivity before their enemies, there will I command the sword, and it shall kill them. And I will set my eyes upon them for evil, and not for good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 8:8; Psalms 46:6; Isaiah 64:1"> And the Lord the God of hosts is he who toucheth the earth, and it shall melt: and all that dwell therein shall mourn: and it shall rise up as a river, and shall run down as the river of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 5:8; Amos 4:13; Psalms 104:3"> He that buildeth his ascension in heaven, and hath founded his bundle upon the earth: who calleth the waters of the sea, and poureth them out upon the face of the earth, the Lord is his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 47:4; Deuteronomy 2:23; 2 Kings 16:9"> Are not you as the children of the Ethiopians unto me, O children of Israel, saith the Lord? did not I bring up Israel, out of the land of Egypt: and the Philistines out of Cappadocia, and the Syrians out of Cyrene?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:11; Amos 9:4; Jeremiah 44:27"> Behold the eyes of the Lord God are upon the sinful kingdom, and I will destroy it from the face of the earth: but yet I will not utterly destroy the house of Jacob, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 22:31; Deuteronomy 28:64; Isaiah 30:28"> For behold I will command, and I will sift the house of Israel among all nations, as corn is sifted in a sieve: and there shall not a little stone fall to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 6:3; Isaiah 33:14; Amos 6:1"> All the sinners of my people shall fall by the sword: who say: The evils shall not approach, and shall not come upon us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 15:15–17; Jeremiah 33:14–16; Isaiah 16:5"> In that day I will raise up the tabernacle of David, that is fallen: and I will close up the breaches of the walls thereof, and repair what was fallen: and I will rebuild it as in the days of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 43:7; Isaiah 65:1; Acts 15:17"> That they may possess the remnant of Edom, and all nations, because my name is invoked upon them: saith the Lord that doth these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joel 3:18; John 4:35; Leviticus 26:5"> Behold the days come, saith the Lord, when the ploughman shall overtake the reaper, and the treader of grapes him that soweth seed: and the mountains shall drop sweetness, and every hill shall be tilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 61:4; Jeremiah 30:3; Jeremiah 30:18"> And I will bring back the captivity of my people Israel: and they shall build the abandoned cities, and inhabit them: and they shall plant vineyards, and drink the wine of them: and shall make gardens, and eat the fruits of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:6; Ezekiel 37:25; Isaiah 60:21"> And I will plant them upon their own land: and I will no more pluck them out of their land which I have given them, saith the Lord thy God.</span>
