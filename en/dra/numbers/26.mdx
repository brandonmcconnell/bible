<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 38:25–26; Numbers 1:2–3; Exodus 30:11–16"> Number the whole sum of the children of Israel from twenty years old and upward, by their houses and kindreds, all that are able to go forth to war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 22:1; Numbers 33:48; Numbers 35:1"> Moses therefore and Eleazar the priest, being in the plains of Moab upon the Jordan over against Jericho, spoke to them that were</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 1:1; 1 Chronicles 21:1"> From twenty years old and upward, as the Lord had commanded: and this is the number of them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:3; Genesis 46:8–9; Exodus 6:14"> Ruben the firstborn of Israel. His sons were Henoch, of whom is the family of the Henochites: and Phallu, of whom is the family of the Phalluites:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And Hesron, of whom is the family of the Hesronites: and Charmi, of whom is the family of the Charmites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 46:9; Numbers 26:21; Numbers 2:11"> These are the families of the stock of Ruben: whose number was found to be forty-three thousand seven hundred and thirty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The son of Phallu was Eliab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 1:16; Numbers 16:1–35; Psalms 106:17"> His sons, were Namuel and Dathan and Abiron. These are Dathan and Abiron the princes of the people, that rose against Moses and Aaron in the sedition of Core, when they rebelled against the Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 16:38; 2 Peter 2:6; Numbers 16:31–35"> And the earth opening her mouth swallowed up Core, many others dying, when the fire burned two hundred and fifty men. And there was a great miracle wrought,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 6:24; Psalms 42:1; Numbers 16:33"> That when Core perished, his sons did not perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 4:24; Genesis 46:10; Exodus 6:15"> The sons of Simeon by their kindreds: Namuel, of him is the family of the Namuelites: Jamin, of him is the family of the Jaminites: Jachin, of him is the family of the Jachinites:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 46:10"> Zare, of him is the family of the Zarites: Saul, of him is the family of the Saulites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 1:22–23; Numbers 2:12–13"> These are the families of the stock of Simeon, of which the whole number was twenty-two thousand two hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 46:16; Numbers 2:14"> The sons of Gad by their kindreds: Sephon, of hin; Is the family of the Sephonites: Aggi, of him is the family of the Aggites: Suni, of him is the family of the Sunites:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 46:16"> Ozni, of him is the family of the Oznites: Her, of him is the family of the Herites:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 46:16"> Arod, of him is the family of the Arodites: Ariel, of him is the family of the Arielites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 1:24–25; Numbers 2:14–15"> These are the families of Gad, of which the whole number was forty thousand five hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Genesis 38:1–10; 1 Chronicles 2:3–8"> The sons of Juda, Her and Onan, who both died in the land of Chanaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:24; Genesis 46:12; Luke 3:33"> And the sons of Juda by their kindreds were: Sela, of whom is the family of the Selaites: Phares, of whom is the family of the Pharesites: Zare, of whom is the family of the Zarites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Moreover the sons of Phares were: Hesron, of whom is the family of the Hesronites: and Hamul, of whom is the family of the Hamulites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 1:26–27; Numbers 2:3–4; Genesis 49:8"> These are the families of Juda, of which the whole number was seventy-six thousand five hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 46:13; 1 Chronicles 7:1; Genesis 30:17–18"> The sons of Issachar, by their kindreds: Thola, of whom is the family of the Tholaites: Phua, of whom is the family of the Phuaites:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 46:13"> Jasub, of whom is the family of the Jasubites: Semran, of whom is the family of the Semranites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 2:5–6; Numbers 1:28–29"> These are the kindreds of Issachar, whose number was sixty-four thousand three hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 46:14; Genesis 30:19–20"> The sons of Zabulon by their kindreds: Sared, of whom is the family of the Saredites: Elon, of whom is the family of the Elonites: Jalel, of whom is the family of the Jalelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 1:30–31; Numbers 2:7–8"> These are the kindreds of Zabulon, whose number was sixty thousand five hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 46:20; Genesis 48:5; Genesis 41:51–52"> The sons of Joseph by their kindred, Manasses and Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 17:1; Numbers 36:1; 1 Chronicles 7:14–19"> Of Manasses was born Machir, of whom is the family of the Machirites. Machir beget Galaad, of whom is the family of the Galaadites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 6:11; Judges 6:34; Judges 6:24"> Galaad had sons: Jezer, of whom is the family of the Jezerites: and Helec, of whom is the family of the Helecites:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And Asriel, of whom is the family of the Asrielites: and Sechem, of whom is the family of the Sechemites:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And Semida, of whom is the family of the Semidaites: and Hepher, of whom is the family of the Hepherites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 27:1; Numbers 36:10–12"> And Hepher was the father of Salphaad, who had no sons, but only daughters, whose names are these: Maala, and Noa, and Hegla, and Melcha, and Thersa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 1:34–35; Numbers 2:20–21"> These are the families of Manasses, and the number of them fifty-two thousand seven hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Chronicles 7:20–21"> And the sons of Ephraim by their kindreds were these: Suthala, of whom is the family of the Suthalaites: Becher, of whom is the family of the Becherites: Thehen, of whom is the family of the Thehenites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Now the son of Suthala was Heran, of whom is the family of the Heranites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 1:32–33; Numbers 2:18–19"> These are the kindreds of the sons of Ephraim: whose number was thirty-two thousand five hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Chronicles 8:1; 1 Chronicles 7:6–12; Genesis 46:2"> These are the sons of Joseph by their families. The sons of Benjamin in their kindreds: Bela, of whom is the family of the Belaites: Asbel, of whom is the family of the Asbelites: Ahiram, of whom is the family of the Ahiramites:</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 46:21"> Supham, of whom is the family of the Suphamites: Hupham, of whom is the family of the Huphamites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Chronicles 8:3"> The sons of Bela: Hered, and Noeman. Of Hered, is the family of the Heredites: of Noeman, the family of tile Noemanites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 2:22–23; Genesis 46:21; Numbers 1:36–37"> These are the sons of Benjamin by their kindreds, whose number was forty-five thousand six hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Genesis 46:23"> The sons of Dan by their kindreds: Suham, of whom is the family of the Suhamites: These are the kindreds of Dan by their families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 1:38–39; Numbers 2:25–26"> All were Suhamites, whose number was sixty-four thousand four hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Genesis 46:17; 1 Chronicles 7:30"> The sons of Aser by their kindreds: Jemna, of whom is the family of the Jemnaites: Jessui, of whom is the family of the Jessuites: Brie, of whom is the family of the Brieites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> The sons of Brie: Heber, of whom is the family of the Heberites: and Melchiel, of whom is the family of the Melchielites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Genesis 46:17"> And the name of the daughter of Aser, was Sara.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Numbers 2:27–28; Numbers 1:40–41"> These are the kindreds of the sons of Aser, and their number fifty-three thousand four hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Genesis 46:24; 1 Chronicles 7:13"> The sons of Nephtali by their kindreds: Jesiel, of whom is the family of the Jesielites: Guni, of whom is the family of the Gunites:</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Chronicles 7:13"> Jeser, of whom is the family of the Jeserites: Sellem, of whom is the family of the Sellemites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Numbers 1:42–43; Numbers 2:29–30"> These are the kindreds of the sons of Nephtali by their families: whose number was forty-five thousand four hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 1:46; Numbers 2:32; Exodus 12:37"> This is the sum of the children of Israel, that were reckoned up, six hundred and one thousand seven hundred and thirty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Joshua 11:23; Joshua 14:1; Psalms 105:44"> To these shall the land be divided for their possessions according to the number of names.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Numbers 33:54; Numbers 32:3; Numbers 32:5"> To the greater number thou shalt give a greater portion, and to the fewer a less: to every one, as they have now been reckoned up, shall a possession be delivered:</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Numbers 34:13; Numbers 33:54; Joshua 14:2"> Yet so that by lot the land be divided to the tribe and families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:4; Romans 11:7"> Whatsoever shall fall by lot, that shall be taken by the more, or the fewer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Genesis 46:11; 1 Chronicles 6:1; Exodus 6:16–19"> This also is the number of the sons of Levi by their families: Gerson, of whom is the family of the Gersonites: Caath, of whom is the family of the Caathites: Merari, of whom is the family of the Merarites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Numbers 16:1; Numbers 3:17–21; Exodus 6:20"> These are the families of Levi: The family of Lobni, the family of Hebroni, the family of Moholi, the family of Musi, the family of Core. Now Caath beget Amram:</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Exodus 6:20; Exodus 2:1–2; Leviticus 18:12"> Who had to wife Jochabed the daughter of Levi, who was horn to him in Egypt. She bore to her husband Amram sons, Aaron and Moses, and Mary their sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Numbers 3:2; Numbers 3:8"> Of Aaron were born Nadab and Abiu, and Eleazar and Ithamar:</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Numbers 3:4; Leviticus 10:1–2; 1 Chronicles 24:1–2"> Of whom Nadab and Abiu died, when they had offered the strange fire before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:9; Numbers 35:2–8; Numbers 3:39"> And all that were numbered, were twenty-three thousand males from one month old and upward: for they were not reckoned up among the children of Israel, neither was a possession given to them with the rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Numbers 26:3"> This is the number of the children of Israel, that were enrolled by Moses and Eleazar the priest, in the plains of Moab upon the Jordan, over against Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:14–15; Deuteronomy 4:3–4; 1 Corinthians 10:5"> Among whom there was not one of them that were numbered before by Moses and Aaron in the desert of Sinai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:5–6; Numbers 14:38; Romans 11:22"> For the Lord had foretold that they should die in the wilderness. And none remained of them, but Caleb the son of Jephone, and Josue the son of Nun.</span>
