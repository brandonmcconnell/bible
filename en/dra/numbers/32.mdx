<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> They came to Moses and Eleazar the priest, and the princes of the multitude, and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:34; Isaiah 16:8–9; Numbers 32:34–38"> Ataroth, and Dibon, and Jazer, and Nemra, Hesebon, and Eleale, and Saban, and Nebo, and Beon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 21:34; Deuteronomy 2:24–35; Numbers 21:24"> The land, which the Lord hath conquered in the sight of the children of Israel, is a very fertile soil for the feeding of beasts: and we thy servants have very much cattle:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ruth 2:10; Joshua 7:7; Jeremiah 31:2"> And we pray thee, if we have found favour in thy sight, that thou give it to us thy servants in possession, and make us not pass over the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:5; 2 Samuel 11:11; Philippians 2:4"> And Moses answered them: What, shall your brethren go to fight, and will you sit here?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 13:27–4; Numbers 32:9; Deuteronomy 1:28"> Why do ye overturn the minds of the children of Israel, that they may not dare to pass into the place which the Lord hath given them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:19–25; Numbers 14:2; Numbers 13:2–26"> Was it not thus your fathers did, when I sent from Cadesbarne to view the land?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 13:23–10; Deuteronomy 1:24–28"> And when they were come as far as the valley of the cluster, having viewed all the country, they overturned the hearts of the children of Israel, that they should not enter into the coasts, which the Lord gave them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 14:11; Psalms 95:11; Ezekiel 20:15"> And he swore in his anger, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 14:28–30; Numbers 14:24; Numbers 26:2"> If these men, that came up out of Egypt, from twenty years old and upward, shall see the land, which I promised with an oath to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob: because they would not follow me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:36; Numbers 14:30; Numbers 14:24"> Except Caleb the son of Jephone the Cenezite, and Josue the son of Nun: these have fulfilled my will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:5; Numbers 14:33–35; Hebrews 3:16–19"> And the Lord being angry against Israel, led them about through the desert forty years, until the whole generation, that had done evil in his sight, was consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 11:48; Psalms 78:57; Matthew 23:31–33"> And behold, said he, you are risen up instead of your fathers, the increase and offspring of sinful men, to augment the fury of the Lord against Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:11–12; 2 Chronicles 15:2; 2 Chronicles 7:19–22"> For if you will not follow him, he will leave the people in the wilderness, and you shall be the cause of the destruction of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 33:17; Numbers 34:22"> But they coming near, said: We will make sheepfolds, and stalls for our cattle, and strong cities for our children:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 4:12–13; Numbers 32:29–32; Deuteronomy 3:18–20"> And we ourselves will go armed and ready for battle before the children of Israel, until we bring them in unto their places. Our little ones, and all we have, shall be in walled cities, for fear of the ambushes of the inhabitants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 22:1–5"> We will not return into our houses until the children of Israel possess their inheritance:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 13:8; Joshua 12:1–6; Numbers 34:15"> Neither will we seek any thing beyond the Jordan, because we have already our possession on the east side thereof,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 4:12–13; Deuteronomy 3:18–20; Joshua 1:13–15"> And Moses said to them: If you do what you promise, go on well appointed for war before the Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And let every fighting man pass over the Jordan, until the Lord overthrow his enemies:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 22:9; Joshua 22:4; Deuteronomy 3:20"> And all the land be brought under him, then shall you be blameless before the Lord and before Israel, and you shall obtain the countries that you desire, before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 59:12; Proverbs 13:21; Genesis 44:16"> But if you do not what you say, no man can doubt but you sin against God: and know ye, that your sin shall overtake you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 32:16; Numbers 30:2; Numbers 32:34–42"> Build therefore cities for your children, and folds and stalls for your sheep and beasts, and accomplish what you have promised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 1:13–14"> And the children of Gad and Ruben said to Moses: We are thy servants, we will do what my lord commandeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Joshua 1:14"> We will leave our children, and our wives and sheep and cattle, in the cities of Galaad:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 4:12; Numbers 32:17; Numbers 12:11"> And we thy servants all well appointed will march on to the war, as thou, my lord, speakest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 1:13"> Moses therefore commanded Eleazar the priest, and Josue the son of Nun, and the princes of the families of all the tribes of Israel, and said to them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> If the children of Gad, and the children of Ruben pass with you over the Jordan, all armed for war before the Lord, and the land be made subject to you: give them Galaad in possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 22:19"> But if they will not pass armed with you into the land of Chanaan, let them receive places to dwell in among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And the children of Gad, and the children of Ruben answered: As the Lord hath spoken to his servants, so will we do:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> We will go armed before the Lord into the land of Chanaan, and we confess that we have already received our possession beyond the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 12:6; Deuteronomy 29:8; Deuteronomy 3:12–17"> Moses therefore gave to the children of Cad and of Ruben, and to the half tribe of Manasses the son of Joseph, the kingdom of Sehon king of the Amorrhites, and the kingdom of Og king of Basan, and their land and the cities thereof round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:36; Numbers 32:3; Isaiah 17:2"> And the sons of Cad built Dibon, and Ataroth, and Aroer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 32:1; Numbers 32:3"> And Etroth, and Sophan, and Jazer, and Jegbaa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Numbers 32:3"> And Bethnemra, and Betharan, fenced cities, and folds for their cattle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 21:27; Numbers 32:3; Isaiah 15:4"> But the children of Ruben built Hesebon, and Eleale, and Cariathaim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 46:1; Numbers 32:3; Joshua 23:7"> And Nabo, and Baalmeon (their names being changed) and Sabama: giving names to the cities which they had built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 50:23; Joshua 17:1; Numbers 26:29"> Moreover the children of Machir, the son of Manasses, went into Galaad, and wasted it, cutting off the Amorrhites, the inhabitants thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Joshua 17:1; Joshua 13:29–31; Deuteronomy 3:13–15"> And Moses gave the land of Galaad to Machir the son of Manasses, and he dwelt in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:14; Judges 10:4; Joshua 13:30"> And Jair the son of Manasses went, and took the villages thereof, and he called them Havoth Jair, that is to say, the villages of Jair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 49:11; 2 Samuel 18:18"> Nobe also went, and took Canath with the villages thereof: and he called it by his own name, Nobe.</span>
