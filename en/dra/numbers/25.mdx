<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 22:17; Psalms 106:28–29; Hosea 9:10"> And Israel was initiated to Beelphegor: upon which the Lord being angry,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:17; Deuteronomy 4:3; Deuteronomy 21:23"> Said to Moses: Take all the princes of the people, and hang them up on gibbets against the sun: that my fury may be turned away from Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; 1 Kings 18:40; Deuteronomy 13:9"> And Moses said to the judges of Israel: Let every man kill his neighbours, that have been initiated to Beelphegor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 2:17; Numbers 22:4; Jeremiah 36:23"> And behold one of the children of Israel went in before his brethren to a harlot of Madian, in the sight of Moses, and of all the children of Israel, who were weeping before the door of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 6:25; Psalms 106:30; Joshua 22:30–31"> And when Phinees the son of Eleazar the son of Aaron the priest saw it, he rose up from the midst; of the multitude, and taking a dagger,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 16:46–48; Psalms 106:29–31; 1 Chronicles 21:22"> Went in after the Israelite into the brothel house, and thrust both of them through together, to wit, the man and the woman in the genital parts. And the scourge ceased from the children of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:8; Deuteronomy 4:3–4; Numbers 25:4–5"> And there were slain four and twenty thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And the Lord said to Moses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Psalms 78:58; Deuteronomy 32:16"> Phinees the son of Eleazar the son of Aaron the priest, hath turned away my wrath from the children of Israel: because he was moved with my zeal against them, that I myself might not destroy the children of Israel in my zeal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Malachi 2:4–5; Malachi 3:1; Isaiah 54:10"> Therefore say to him: Behold I give him the peace of my covenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17; Romans 10:2–4; Jeremiah 33:22"> And the covenant of the priesthood for ever shall be both to him and his seed, because he hath been zealous for his God, and hath made atonement for the wickedness of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 1:23; Numbers 26:14; 2 Chronicles 19:7"> And the name of the Israelite, was slain with the woman of Madian, was Zambri the son of Salu, a prince the kindred and tribe of Simeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 31:8; Joshua 13:21; Numbers 25:18"> And the Madianite woman, that was slain with him, was called Cozbi the daughter of Sur, a most noble prince among the Madianites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 31:1–3; Revelation 18:6"> Let the Madianites find you enemies, and slay you them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 2:14; Exodus 32:35; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> Because they also have acted like enemies against you, and have guilefully deceived you by the idol Phogor, and Cozbi their sister, a daughter of a prince of Madian, who was slain in the day the plague for the sacrilege of Phogor.</span>
