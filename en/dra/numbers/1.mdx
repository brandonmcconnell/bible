<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Numbers 26:43; Numbers 2:26"> Sixty-two thousand seven hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Genesis 30:12–13; Genesis 46:27; Genesis 49:20"> Of the sons of Aser, by their generations and families and houses of their kindreds, were reckoned up by the names of every one from twenty years old and upward, all that were able to go forth to war,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 26:47; Numbers 2:28"> Forty-one thousand and five hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Genesis 49:21; Numbers 26:48–50; Genesis 46:24"> Of the sons of Nephtali, by their generations and families and houses of their kindreds, were reckoned up by the names of every one from twenty years old and upward, all that were able to go forth to war,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 26:50; Numbers 2:30"> Fifty-three thousand four hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Numbers 26:64; Numbers 1:2–16"> These era they who were numbered by Moses and Aaron, and the twelve princes of Israel, every one by the houses of their kindreds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> And the whole number of the children of Israel by their houses and families, from twenty years old and upward, that were able to go to war,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Exodus 12:37; Numbers 26:51; Exodus 38:26"> Were six hundred and three thousand five hundred and fifty men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Numbers 2:33; Numbers 3:1–49; Numbers 26:57–62"> But the Levites in the tribes of their families were not numbered with them.</span>

<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Exodus 38:21; Exodus 31:18; Numbers 4:15"> But appoint them over the tabernacle of the testimony, and all the vessels thereof, and whatsoever pertaineth to the ceremonies. <span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 3:38; Numbers 3:10; 1 Samuel 6:19"> When you are to go forward, the Levites shall take down the tabernacle: when you are to camp, they shall set it up. What stranger soever cometh to it, shall be slain.</span>
