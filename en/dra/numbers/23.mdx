<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 23:29; Jude 1:11; Exodus 27:1–8"> And Balaam said to Balac: Build me here seven altars, and prepare as many calves, and the same number of rams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 23:14; Numbers 23:30"> And when he had done according to the word of Balaam, they laid together a calf and a ram upon every altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 23:15; Genesis 8:20; Genesis 22:2"> And Balaam said to Balac: Stand a while by thy burnt offering, until I go, to see if perhaps the Lord will meet me, and whatsoever he shall command, I will speak to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 23:16; Ephesians 2:9; Luke 18:12"> And when he was gone with speed, God met him. And Balaam speaking to him, said: I have erected seven altars, and have laid on everyone a calf and a ram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:18; Jeremiah 1:9; Isaiah 59:21"> And the Lord put the word in his mouth, and said: Return to Balac, and thus shalt thou speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Returning he found Balac standing by his burnt offering, with all the princes of the Moabites:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:4; Numbers 24:3; Numbers 22:5–6"> And taking up his parable, he said: Balac king of the Moabites hath brought me from Aram, from the mountains of the east: Come, said he, and curse Jacob: make haste and detest Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:23; Isaiah 44:25; Numbers 23:20"> How shall I curse him, whom God hath not cursed? By what means should I detest him, whom the Lord detesteth not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:28; Deuteronomy 32:8; Exodus 33:16"> I shall see him from the tops of the rocks, and shall consider him from the hills. This people shall dwell alone, and shall not be reckoned among the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 37:37; Genesis 13:16; Psalms 116:15"> Who can count the dust of Jacob, and know the number of the stock of Israel? Let my soul die the death of the just, and my last end be like to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:2; Numbers 24:10; Numbers 22:17"> And Balac said to Balaam: What is this that thou dost? I sent for thee to curse my enemies: and thou contrariwise blessest them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 22:38; Numbers 23:20; Numbers 23:26"> He answered him: Call I speak any thing else but what the Lord commandeth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 109:17; James 3:9–10; Numbers 22:41"> Balac therefore said: Come with me to another place from whence thou mayest see part of Israel, and canst not see them all: curse them from thence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 46:6; Numbers 23:29; Isaiah 1:10–11"> And when he had brought him to a high place, upon the top of mount Phasga, Balaam built seven altars, and laying on every one a calf and a ram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 23:3; Numbers 22:8"> He said to Balac: Stand here by thy burnt offering while I go to meet him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 23:5; Numbers 24:1; Numbers 22:35"> And when the Lord had met him, and had put the word in his mouth, he said: Return to Balac, and thus shalt thou say to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 23:26; 1 Samuel 3:17; Jeremiah 37:17"> Returning he found him standing by his burnt sacrifice, and the princes of the Moabites with him. And Balac said to him: What hath the Lord spoken?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 3:20"> But he taking up his parable, said: Stand, O Balac, and give ear: hear, thou son of Sephor:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:29; James 1:17; Malachi 3:6"> God is not a man, that he should lie, nor as the son of man, that he should be changed. Hath he said then, and will he not do? hath he spoken, and will he not fulfill?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 10:27–29; Numbers 22:12; Genesis 12:2"> I was brought to bless, the blessing I am not able to hinder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 23:4; Matthew 1:23; Jeremiah 50:20"> There is no idol in Jacob, neither is there an image god to be seen in Israel. The Lord his God is with him, and the sound of the victory of the king in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 24:8; Deuteronomy 33:17; Job 39:9–11"> God hath brought him out of Egypt, whose strength is like to the rhinoceros.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 10:18–19; Joshua 13:22; Galatians 1:23–24"> There is no soothsaying in Jacob, nor divination in Israel. In their times it shall be told to Jacob and to Israel what God hath wrought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 49:27; Genesis 49:9; Numbers 24:17"> Behold the people shall rise up as a lioness, and shall lift itself up as a lion: it shall not lie down till it devour the prey, and drink the blood of the slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 2:1–3"> And Balac said to Balaam: Neither curse, nor bless him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Numbers 22:18; Acts 4:19–20"> And he said: Did I not tell thee, that whatsoever God should command me, that I would do?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 11:29; Proverbs 21:30; Numbers 23:19–20"> And Balac said to him: Come and I will bring thee to another place; if peradventure it please God that thou mayest curse them from thence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 21:20; Psalms 106:28"> And when he had brought him upon the top of mount Phogor, which looketh towards the wilderness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 23:1–2"> Balaam said to him: Build me here seven altars, and prepare as many calves, and the same number of rams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Balac did as Balaam had said: and he laid on every altar, a calf and a ram.</span>
