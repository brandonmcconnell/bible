<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The Lord also said to Moses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 3:11; Genesis 8:21; Numbers 9:2–3"> Command the children of Israel, and thou shalt say to them: Offer ye my oblation and my bread, and burnt sacrifice of most sweet odour, in their due seasons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 6:9; Daniel 11:31; John 1:29"> These are the sacrifices which you shall offer: Two lambs of a year old without blemish every day for the perpetual holocaust:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 9:21; 1 Kings 18:36; Exodus 12:6"> One you shall offer in the morning, and the other in the evening:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 2:1; Exodus 16:36; Exodus 29:38–42"> And the tenth part of an ephi of flour, which shall be tempered with the purest oil, of the measure of the fourth part of a hin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 5:25; Leviticus 6:9; Psalms 50:8"> It is the continual holocaust which you offered in mount Sinai for a most sweet. odour of a sacrifice by fire to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 29:42; Numbers 28:14; Isaiah 57:6"> And for a libation you shall offer of wine the fourth part of a hin for every lamb in the sanctuary of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And you shall offer the other lamb in like manner ill the evening according to all the rites of the morning sacrifice, and of the libations thereof, an oblation of most sweet odour to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 58:13; Psalms 92:1–4; Revelation 1:10"> And on the sabbath day you shall offer two lambs of a year old without blemish, and two tenths of flour tempered with oil in sacrifice, and the libations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:4–5; Numbers 29:22; Numbers 29:31"> Which regularly are poured out every sabbath for the perpetual holocaust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 10:10; Ezekiel 46:6; Ezra 3:5"> And on the first day of the month you shall offer a holocaust to the Lord, two calves of the herd, one ram, and seven lambs of a year old, without blemish,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 15:4–12; Ezekiel 46:5–7; Numbers 29:10"> And three tenths of flour tempered with oil in sacrifice for every calf: and two tenths of flour tempered with oil for every ram:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the tenth of a tenth of flour tempered with oil in sacrifice for every lamb. It is a holocaust of most sweet odour and an offering by fire to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And these shall be the libations of wine that are to be poured out for every victim: Half a hin for every calf, a third for a ram, and a fourth for a lamb. This shall be the holocaust for every month, as they succeed one another in the course of the year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 28:3; Numbers 28:22; Numbers 15:24"> A buck goat also shall be offered to the Lord for a sin offering over and above the perpetual holocaust with its libations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:1–8; Exodus 12:18; Leviticus 23:5–8"> And in the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, shall be the phase of the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 23:6; Exodus 23:15; Exodus 13:6"> And on the fifteenth day the solemn feast: seven days shall they eat unleavened bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 12:16; Leviticus 23:7–8"> And the first day of them shall be venerable and holy: you shall not do any servile work therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:21; Ezekiel 45:21–25; Numbers 29:8"> And you shall offer a burnt sacrifice a holocaust to the Lord, two calves of the herd, one ram, seven lambs of a year old, without blemish:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And for the sacrifices of every one three tenths of flour which shall be tempered with oil to every calf, and two tenths to every ram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And the tenth of a tenth, to every lamb, that is to say, to all the seven lambs:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 8:3"> And one buck goat for sin, to make atonement for you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 28:3; Numbers 28:10"> Besides the morning holocaust which you shall always offer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> So shall you do every day of the seven days for the food of the fire, and for a most sweet odour to the Lord, which shall rise from the holocaust, and from the libations of each.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 23:8; Numbers 28:18; Exodus 13:6"> The seventh day also shall be most solemn and holy unto you: you shall do no servile work therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 23:16; Exodus 34:22; Leviticus 23:10"> The day also of firstfruits, when after the weeks are accomplished, you shall offer new fruits to the Lord, shall be venerable and holy: you shall do no servile work therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 23:18–19; Numbers 28:11; Numbers 28:19"> And you shall offer a holocaust for a most sweet odour to the Lord, two calves of the herd, one ram, and seven lambs of a year old, without blemish:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And in the sacrifices of them three tenths of flour tempered with oil to every calf, two to every ram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> The tenth of a tenth to every lamb, which in all are seven lambs: a buck goat also,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 28:15; Numbers 28:22; 1 Peter 2:24"> Which is slain for expiation: besides the perpetual holocaust and the libations thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 28:19; Numbers 28:3; Malachi 1:13–14"> You shall offer them all without blemish with their libations.</span>
