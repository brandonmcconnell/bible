<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 13:5; Amos 2:11–12; Romans 1:1"> Speak to the children of Israel, and thou shalt say to them: When a man, or woman, shall make a vow to be sanctified, and will consecrate themselves to the Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 1:15; 1 Timothy 5:23; Amos 2:12"> They shall abstain from wine, and from every thing that may make a man drunk. They shall not drink vinegar of wine, or of any other drink, nor any thing that is pressed out of the grape: nor shall they eat grapes either fresh or dried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 6:5; Numbers 6:21; Numbers 6:8–9"> All the days that they are consecrated to the Lord by vow: they shall eat nothing that cometh of the vineyard, from the raisin even to the kernel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:11; Judges 16:17; Judges 13:5"> All the time of his separation no razor shall pass over his head, until the day be fulfilled of his consecration to the Lord. He shall be holy, and shall let the hair of his head grow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 21:11; Leviticus 19:28; Leviticus 21:1–3"> All the time of his consecration he shall not go in to any dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 9:6; Leviticus 21:1–2; Leviticus 21:10–12"> Neither shall he make himself unclean, even for his father, or for his mother, or for his brother, or for his sister, when they die, because the consecration of his God is upon his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:17–18"> All the days of his separation he shall be holy to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 6:18; Acts 18:18; Leviticus 14:9"> But if any man die suddenly before him: the head of his consecration shall be defiled: and he shall shave it forthwith on the same day of his purification, and again on the seventh day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 15:29; Leviticus 1:14; Leviticus 15:14"> And on the eighth day he shall bring two turtles, or two young pigeons to the priest in the entry of the covenant of the testimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 14:30–31; Leviticus 5:8–10"> And the priest shall offer one for sin, and the other for a holocaust, and shall pray for him, for that he hath sinned by the dead: and he shall sanctify his head that day:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 5:6; Matthew 24:13; Leviticus 14:24"> And shall consecrate to the Lord the days of his separation, offering a lamb of one year for sin: yet so that the former days be made void, because his sanctification was profaned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 21:26"> This is the law of consecration. When the days which he had determined by vow shall be expired, he shall bring him to the door of the tabernacle of the covenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 3:6; Leviticus 4:32; Leviticus 4:2–3"> And shall offer his oblation to the Lord: one he lamb of a year old without blemish for a holocaust, and one awe lamb of a year old without blemish for a sin offering, and one ram without blemish for a victim of peace offering,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 2:4; Exodus 29:2; Numbers 15:10"> A basket also of unleavened bread, tempered with oil, and wafers without leaven anointed with oil, and the libations of each:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the priest shall present them before the Lord, and shall offer both the sin offering and the holocaust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> But the ram he shall immolate for a sacrifice of peace offering to the Lord, offering at the same time the basket of unleavened bread, and the libations that are due by custom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 21:24; Numbers 6:9; Acts 18:18"> Then shall the hair of the consecration of the Nazarite, be shaved off before the door of the tabernacle of the covenant: and he shall take his hair, and lay it upon the fire, which is under the sacrifice of the peace offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:15; Leviticus 7:30; Exodus 29:23–28"> And shall take the boiled shoulder of the ram, and one unleavened cake out of the basket, and one unleavened wafer, and he shall deliver them into the hands of the Nazarite, after his head is shaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:7; Exodus 29:27–28; Numbers 5:25"> And receiving them again from him, he shall elevate them in the sight of the Lord: and they being sanctified shall belong to the priest, as the breast, which was commanded to be separated, and the shoulder. After this the Nazarite may drink wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Ezra 2:69; Numbers 5:29"> This is the law of the Nazarite, when he hath vowed his oblation to the Lord in the time of his consecration, besides those things which his hand shall find, according to that which he had vowed in his mind, so shall he do for the fulfilling of his sanctification.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:5; 1 Chronicles 23:13; Genesis 48:20"> Say to Aaron and his sons: Thus shall you bless the children of Israel, and you shall say to them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:23; Psalms 121:4–7; Philippians 4:7"> The Lord bless thee, and keep thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 80:19; Psalms 119:135; Psalms 31:16"> The Lord shew his face to thee, and have mercy on thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 4:6; Psalms 29:11; 2 Thessalonians 3:16"> The Lord turn his countenance to thee, and give thee peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:14; Daniel 9:18–19; Deuteronomy 28:10"> And they shall invoke my name upon the children of Israel, and I will bless them.</span>
