<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 27:13; Numbers 25:14–18; Revelation 6:10"> Revenge first the children of Israel on the Madianites, and so thou shalt be gathered to thy people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 26:25; Numbers 25:11; 2 Kings 10:30"> And Moses forthwith said: Arm of you men to fight, who may take the revenge of the Lord on the Madianites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:8; 1 Samuel 14:6; Judges 7:2"> Let a thousand men be chosen out of every tribe of Israel to be sent to the war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And they gave a thousand of every tribe, that is to say, twelve thousand men well appointed for battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:44; Numbers 10:8–9; 2 Chronicles 13:12–15"> And Moses sent them with Phinees the son of Eleazar the priest, and he delivered to him the holy vessels, and the trumpets to sound.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 21:11; 1 Kings 11:15–16; 1 Samuel 27:9"> And when they had fought against the Madianites and had overcome them, they slew all the men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 13:21–22; Numbers 25:15; Revelation 2:14"> And their kings Evi, and Recem, and Sur, and Hur, and Rebe, five princes of the nation: Balaam also the son of Beer they killed with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; 2 Chronicles 28:8–10; 2 Chronicles 28:5"> And they took their women, and their children captives, and all their cattle, and all their goods: and all their possessions they plundered:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 1:7; 1 Samuel 30:1; Joshua 6:24"> And all their cities, and their villages, and castles, they burned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Joshua 8:2"> And they carried away the booty, and all that they had taken both of men and of beasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 22:1"> And they brought them to Moses, and Eleazar the priest, and to all the multitude of the children of Israel. But the rest of the things for use they carried to the camp on the plains of Moab, beside the Jordan over against Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 5:2; 1 Samuel 30:21; Numbers 31:12"> And Moses and Eleazar the priest and all the princes of the synagogue went forth to meet them without the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 13:19; 1 Samuel 15:13–14; Numbers 31:48"> And Moses being angry with the chief officers of the army, the tribunes, and the centurions that were come from the battle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:3; Jeremiah 48:10; Deuteronomy 2:34"> Said: Why have you saved the women?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 25:1–9; 2 Peter 2:15; Revelation 2:14"> Are not these they, that deceived the children of Israel by the counsel of Balaam, and made you transgress against the Lord by the sin of Phogor, for which also the people was punished?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 21:11–12; Deuteronomy 20:16–18; Deuteronomy 7:2"> Therefore kill all that are of the male sex, even of the children: and put to death the women, that have carnally known men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:10–14; 2 Chronicles 28:8–10; Deuteronomy 20:14"> But the girls, and all the women that are virgins save for yourselves:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 19:11–22; Numbers 5:2; 1 Chronicles 22:8"> And stay without the camp seven days. He that hath killed a man, or touched one that is killed, shall be purified the third day and the seventh day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 19:22; Genesis 35:2; Exodus 19:10"> And of all the spoil, every garment, or vessel, or any thing made for use, of the skins, or hair of goats, or of wood, shall be purified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 30:16"> Eleazar also the priest spoke to the men of the army, that had fought, in this manner: This is the ordinance of the law, which the Lord hath commanded Moses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Gold, and silver, and brass, and iron, and lend, and tin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 19:9; Numbers 19:17; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> And all that may pass through the fire, shall be purified by fire, but whatsoever cannot abide the fire, shall be sanctified with the water of expiation:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 11:25; Leviticus 15:13; Leviticus 14:9"> And you shall wash your garments the seventh day, and being purified, you shall afterwards enter into the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And the Lord said to Moses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Take the sum of the things that were taken both of man and beast, thou and Eleazar the priest and the princes of the multitude:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 22:8; Psalms 68:12; 1 Samuel 30:24–25"> And thou shalt divide the spoil equally, between them that fought and went out to the war, and between the rest of the multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 18:26; Numbers 31:30; Numbers 31:47"> And thou shalt separate a portion to the Lord from them that fought and were in the battle, one soul of five hundred as well of persons as of oxen and asses and sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 29:27; Deuteronomy 12:19; Deuteronomy 12:12"> And thou shalt give it to Eleazar the priest, because they are the firstfruits of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 3:31; Numbers 3:7–8; Numbers 3:25"> Out of the moiety also of the children of Israel thou shalt take the fiftieth head of persons, and of oxen, and asses, and sheep, and of all beasts, and thou shalt give them to the Levites that watch in the charge of the tabernacle of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And Moses and Eleazar did as the Lord had commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And the spoil which the army had taken, was six hundred seventy-five thousand sheep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Seventy-two thousand oxen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Sixty-one thousand asses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And thirty-two thousand persons of the female sex, that had not known men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And one half was given to them that had been in the battle, to wit, three hundred thirty-seven thousand five hundred sheep:</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Out of which, for the portion of the Lord, were reckoned six hundred seventy-five sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And out of the thirty-six thousand oxen, seventy-two oxen:</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Out of the thirty thousand five hundred asses, sixty-one asses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> Out of the sixteen thousand persons, there fell to the portion of the Lord, thirty-two souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 18:8; Numbers 18:19; Hebrews 7:9–12"> And Moses delivered the number of the firstfruits of the Lord to Eleazar the priest, as had been commanded him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Out of the half of the children of Israel, which he had separated for them that had been in the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> But out of the half that fell to the rest of the multitude, that is to say, out of the three hundred thirty-seven thousand five hundred sheep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And out of the thirty-six thousand oxen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> And out of the thirty thousand five hundred asses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> And out of the sixteen thousand persons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Numbers 31:30; Luke 10:1–8; Isaiah 56:10–11"> Moses took the fiftieth head, and gave it to the Levites that watched in the tabernacle of the Lord, as the Lord had commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> And when the commanders of the army, and the tribunes and centurions were come to Moses, they said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 18:9; 1 Samuel 30:18–19; Psalms 72:14"> We thy servants have reckoned up the number of the fighting men, whom we had under our hand, and not so much as one was wanting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Exodus 30:12; Exodus 30:15–16; Psalms 116:12"> Therefore we offer as gifts to the Lord what gold every one of us could find in the booty, in garters and tablets, rings and bracelets, and chains, that thou mayst pray to the Lord for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 7:2–6"> And Moses and Eleazar the priest received all the gold in divers kinds,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> In weight sixteen thousand seven hundred and fifty sicles, from the tribunes and from the centurions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Numbers 31:32"> For that which every one had taken in the booty was his own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Exodus 30:16; Zechariah 6:14; Acts 10:4"> And that which was received they brought into the tabernacle of the testimony, for a memorial of the children of Israel before the Lord.</span>
