<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the people marched from Haseroth, and pitched their tents in the desert of Pharan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:22–25; Numbers 32:8; Numbers 34:18"> And there the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 12:16; Numbers 32:8; Deuteronomy 1:19"> Send men to view the land of Chanaan, which I will give to the children of Israel, one of every tribe, of the rulers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Moses did what the Lord had commanded, sending from the desert of Pharan, principal men, whose names are these:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Of the tribe of Ruben, Sammua the son of Zechur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 34:19; Numbers 14:6; Numbers 13:30"> Of the tribe of Simeon, Saphat the son of Hurl.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Of the tribe of Juda, Caleb the son of Jephone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 24:13; Numbers 11:28; Numbers 13:16"> Of the tribe of Issachar, Igal the son of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Of the tribe of Ephraim, Osee the son of Nun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Of the tribe of Benjamin, Phalti the son of Raphu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Of the tribe of Zabulon, Geddiel the son of Sodi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Of the tribe of Joseph, of the sceptre of Manasses, Gaddi the son of Susi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Of the tribe of Dan, Ammiel the son of Gemalli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Of the tribe of Aser, Sthur the son of Michael.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Of the tribe of Nephtali, Nahabi the son of Vapsi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 13:8; Exodus 17:9; Numbers 14:6"> Of the tribe of Gad, Guel the son of Machi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 1:9; Genesis 12:9; Judges 1:19"> These are the names of the men, whom Moses sent to view the land: and he called Osee the son of Nun, Josue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:14; Exodus 3:8"> And Moses sent them to view the land of Chanaan, and said to them: Go you up by the south side. And when you shall come to the mountains,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> View the land, of what sort it is: and the people that are the inhabitants thereof, whether they be strong or weak: few in number or many:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:23; Deuteronomy 31:6–8; Ezekiel 34:14"> The land itself, whether it be good or bad: what manner of cities, walled or without walls:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 20:1; Numbers 33:36; Numbers 27:14"> The ground, fat or barren, woody or without trees. Be of good courage, and bring us of the fruits of the land. Now it was the time when the first ripe grapes are fit to be eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 78:12; Psalms 78:43; Isaiah 19:11"> And when they were gone up, they viewed the land from the desert of Sin, unto Rohob as you enter into Emath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 13:24; Numbers 32:9; Deuteronomy 1:24–25"> And they went up at the south side, and came to Hebron, where were Achiman and Sisai and Tholmai the sons of Enac. For Hebron was built seven years before Tanis the city of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And going forward as far as the torrent of the cluster of grapes, they cut off a branch with its cluster of grapes, which two men carried upon a lever. They took also of the pomegranates and of the figs of that place:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 34:28; Exodus 24:18; Numbers 14:33–34"> Which was called Nehelescol, that is to say, the torrent of the cluster of grapes, because from thence the children of Israel had carried a cluster of grapes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 32:8; Numbers 20:1; Deuteronomy 1:19"> And they that went to spy out the land returned after forty days, having gone round all the country,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Exodus 3:17; Exodus 13:5"> And came to Moses and Aaron and to all the assembly of the children of Israel to the desert of Pharan, which is in Cades. And speaking to them and to all the multitude, they shewed them the fruits of the land:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:1–2; Deuteronomy 1:28; Numbers 13:33"> And they related and said: We came into the land to which thou sentest us, which in very deed floweth with milk and honey as may be known by these fruits:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 14:43; Judges 6:3; Genesis 15:19–21"> But it hath very strong inhabitants, and the cities are great and walled. We saw there the race of Enac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 14:24; Romans 8:31; Philippians 4:13"> Amalec dwelleth in the south, the Hethite and the Jebusite and the Amorrhite in the mountains: but the Chanaanite abideth by the sea and near the streams of the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; Joshua 14:8; Hebrews 3:19"> In the mean time Caleb, to still the murmuring of the people that rose against Moses, said: Let us go up and possess the land, for we shall be able to conquer it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Amos 2:9; Numbers 14:36–37; Ezekiel 36:13–14"> But the others, that had been with him, said: No, we are not able to go up to this people, because they are stronger than we.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; Isaiah 40:22; Genesis 6:4"> And they spoke ill of the land, which they had viewed, before the children of Israel, saying: The land which we have viewed, devoureth its inhabitants: the people, that we beheld, are of a tall stature.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> There we saw certain monsters of the sons of Enac, of the giant kind: in comparison of whom, we seemed like locusts.</span>
