<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 23:10; Numbers 15:18; Leviticus 25:2"> Speak to the children of Israel and thou shalt say to them: When you shall be come into the land of your habitation, which I will give you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 29:18; Genesis 8:21; Philippians 4:18"> And shall make an offering to the Lord, for a holocaust, or a victim, paying your vows, or voluntarily offering gifts, or in your solemnities burning a sweet savour unto the Lord, of oxen or of sheep:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 29:40; Leviticus 23:13; Leviticus 6:14"> Whosoever immolateth the victim, shall offer a sacrifice of fine flour, the tenth part of an ephi, tempered with the fourth part of a hin of oil:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 28:7; Numbers 28:14; Judges 9:13"> And he shall give the same measure of wine to pour out in libations for the holocaust or for the victim. For every lamb,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 28:12–14; Numbers 15:4"> And for every ram there shall be a sacrifice of hour of two tenths, which shall be tempered with the third part of a hin of oil:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And he shall offer the third part of the same measure of wine for the libation, for a sweet savour to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 3:1; Leviticus 7:11–18; Leviticus 1:3"> But when thou offerest a holocaust or sacrifice of oxen, to fulfill thy vow or for victims of peace offerings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 28:14; Numbers 28:12; Leviticus 6:14"> Thou shalt give for every ox three tenths of flour tempered with half a hin of oil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 15:5; Numbers 6:15"> And wine for libations of the same measure, for an offering of most sweet savour to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Thus shalt thou do:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> For every ox and ram and lamb and kid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Both they that are born in the land, and the strangers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Shall offer sacrifices after the same rite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 9:14; Numbers 15:29; Exodus 12:49"> There shall be all one law and judgment both for you and for them who are strangers in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Speak to the children of Israel, and thou shalt say to them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 15:2; Deuteronomy 26:1–15"> When you are come into the land which I will give you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 5:11–12"> And shall eat of the bread of that country, you shall separate firstfruits to the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 2:14; Exodus 34:26; Deuteronomy 26:2–10"> Of the things you eat. As you separate firstfruits of your barnfloors:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 29:28; Numbers 18:26"> So also shall you give firstfruits of your dough to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 4:2; Leviticus 4:27; Leviticus 5:15–17"> And if through ignorance you omit any of these things, which the Lord hath spoken to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And by him hath commanded you, from the day that he began to command and thenceforward,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 4:23; Leviticus 4:13–21; Leviticus 5:15"> And the multitude have forgotten to do it: they shall offer a calf out of the herd, a holocaust for a most sweet savour to the Lord, and the sacrifice and libations thereof, as the ceremonies require, and a buck goat for sin:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 4:20; Romans 3:25; Hebrews 2:17"> And the priest shall pray for all the multitude of the children of Israel: and it shall be forgiven them, because they sinned ignorantly, offering notwithstanding a burnt offering to the Lord for themselves and for their sin and their ignorance:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 15:24"> And it shall be forgiven all the people of the children of Israel: and the strangers that sojourn among them: because it is the fault of all the people through ignorance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 4:27–28; Acts 17:30; Acts 3:17"> But if one soul shall sin ignorantly, he shall offer a she goat of a year old for his sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 4:35"> And the priest shall pray for him, because he sinned ignorantly before the Lord: and he shall obtain his pardon, and it shall be forgiven him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 15:15; Leviticus 16:29; Leviticus 17:15"> The same law shall be for all that sin by ignorance, whether they be natives or strangers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26; Psalms 19:13; Deuteronomy 1:43"> But the soul that committeth any thing through pride, whether he be born in the land or a stranger (because he hath been rebellious against the Lord) shall be cut off from among his people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:9; Proverbs 13:13; Ezekiel 18:20"> For he hath contemned the word the Lord, and made void his precept: therefore shall he be destroyed, and shall bear his iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 35:2–3; Exodus 31:14–15; Exodus 20:8–10"> And it came to pass, when the children of Israel were in the wilderness, and had found a man gathering sticks on the sabbath day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 8:3–20"> That they brought him to Moses and Aaron and the whole multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Leviticus 24:12; Numbers 9:8"> And they put him into prison, not knowing what they should do with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 7:58; Exodus 31:14–15; 1 Kings 21:13"> And the Lord said to Moses: Let that man die, let all the multitude stone him without the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Joshua 7:25"> And when they had brought him out, they stoned him, and he died as the Lord had commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> The Lord also said to Moses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 23:5; Deuteronomy 22:12; Matthew 9:20"> Speak to the children of Israel, and thou shalt tell them I to make to themselves fringes in the corners of their garments, putting in them ribands of blue:</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 106:39; Proverbs 28:26; Psalms 73:27"> That when they shall see them, they may remember all the commandments of the Lord, and not follow their own thoughts and eyes going astray after divers things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Romans 12:1; 1 Thessalonians 4:7; Leviticus 11:44–45"> But rather being mindful of the precepts of the Lord, may do them and be holy to their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9–10; Ezekiel 36:25–27; Hebrews 11:16"> I am the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, that I might be your God.</span>
