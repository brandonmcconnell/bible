<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Psalms 105:11; Deuteronomy 1:7–8"> Command the children of Israel, and then shalt say to them: When you are entered into the land of Chanaan, and it shall be fallen into your possession by lot, it shall be bounded by these limits:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 14:3; Ezekiel 47:13; Joshua 3:16"> The south side shall begin from the wilderness of Sin, which is by Edom: and shall have the most salt sea for its furthest limits eastward:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 32:8; Joshua 15:3–4; Numbers 13:26"> Which limits shall go round on the south side by the ascent of the Scorpion and so into Senna, and reach toward the south as far as Cadesbarne, from whence the frontiers shall go out to the town called Adar, and shall reach as far as Asemona.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 15:4; Genesis 15:18; 1 Kings 8:65"> And the limits shall fetch a compass from Asemona to the torrent of Egypt, and shall end in the shore of the great sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 1:4; Ezekiel 47:20; Ezekiel 47:15"> And the west side shall begin from the great sea, and the same shall be the end thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 33:37; Numbers 34:6; Numbers 34:3"> But toward the north side the borders shall begin from the great sea, reaching to the most high mountain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 13:21; 2 Kings 14:25; Joshua 13:5–6"> From which they shall come to Emath, as far as the borders of Sedada:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:17"> And the limits shall go as far as Zephrona, and the village of Enan. These shall be the borders on the north side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> From thence they shall mark out the bounds towards the east side from the village of Enan unto Sephama.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 23:33; Deuteronomy 3:17; Joshua 11:2"> And from Sephama the bounds shall go down to Rebla over against the fountain of Daphnis: from thence they shall come eastward to the sea of Cenereth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 34:3; Genesis 13:10; Genesis 19:24–26"> And shall reach as far as the Jordan, and at the last shall be closed in by the most salt sea. This shall be your land with its borders round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 34:1; Joshua 14:1–5"> And Moses commanded the children of Israel, saying: This shall be the land which you shall possess by lot, and which the Lord hath commanded to be given to the nine tribes, and to the half tribe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 32:33; Numbers 32:23; Joshua 14:2–3"> For the tribe of the children of Ruben by their families, and the tribe of the children of Gad according to the number of their kindreds, and half of the tribe of Manasses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 32:32"> That is, two tribes and a half, have received their portion beyond the Jordan over against Jericho at the east side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the Lord said to Moses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 14:1; Joshua 19:51; Numbers 13:8"> These are the names of the men, that shall divide the land unto you: Eleazar the priest, and Josue the son of Nun,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 1:4–16"> And one prince of every tribe,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 26:65; Numbers 13:30; Numbers 14:24"> Whose names are these: Of the tribe of Juda, Caleb the son of Jephone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 49:5"> Of the tribe of Simeon, Samuel the son of Ammiud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 49:27; Psalms 68:27"> Of the tribe of Benjamin, Elidad the son of Chaselon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Of the tribe of the children of Dan, Bocci the son of Jogli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Of the children of Joseph of the tribe of Manasses, Hanniel the son of Ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Of the tribe of Ephraim, Camuel the son of Sephtan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Of the tribe of Zabulon, Elisaphan the son of Pharnach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Of the tribe of Issachar, Phaltiel the prince, the son of Ozan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Of the tribe of Aser, Ahiud the son of Salomi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Of the tribe of Nephtali: Phedael the son of Ammiud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 19:51; Numbers 34:18"> These are they whom the Lord hath commanded to divide the land of Chanaan to the children of Israel.</span>
