<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 28:38; Hebrews 13:17; 1 Peter 2:24"> And the Lord said to Aaron: Thou, and thy sons, and thy father’s house with thee shall bear the iniquity of the sanctuary: and thou and thy sons with thee shall bear the sins of your priesthood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 29:34; Numbers 18:4; Ezekiel 44:15"> And take with thee thy brethren also of the tribe of Levi, and the sceptre of thy father, and let them be ready in hand, and minister to thee: but thou and thy sons shall minister in the tabernacle of the testimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; Numbers 16:40; Numbers 4:19–20"> And the Levites shall watch to do thy commands, and about all the works of the tabernacle: only they shall not come nigh the vessels of the sanctuary nor the altar, lest both they die, and you also perish with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:19; Numbers 1:51; Numbers 3:10"> But let them be with thee, and watch in the charge of the tabernacle, and in all the ceremonies thereof. A stranger shall not join himself with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 27:21; Leviticus 24:3; Numbers 16:46"> Watch ye in the charge of the sanctuary, and in the ministry of the altar: lest indignation rise upon the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 3:9; Numbers 3:12; Numbers 3:45"> I have given you your brethren the Levites from among the children of Israel, and have delivered them for a gift to the Lord, to serve in the ministries of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 15:15–16; John 3:27; Numbers 3:10"> But thou and thy sons look ye to the priesthood: and all things that pertain to the service of the altar, and that are within the veil, shall be executed by the priests. If any stranger shall approach, he shall be slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 6:16; Leviticus 7:6; Exodus 29:29"> And the Lord said to Aaron: Behold I have given thee the charge of my firstfruits. All things that are sanctified by the children of Israel, I have delivered to thee and to thy sons for the priestly office, by everlasting ordinances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 7:7; Leviticus 14:13; Leviticus 6:25–26"> These therefore shalt thou take of the things that are sanctified, and are offered to the Lord. Every offering, and sacrifice, and whatsoever is rendered to me for sin and for trespass, and becometh holy of holies, shall be for thee and thy sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 7:6; Leviticus 6:29; Leviticus 6:16"> Thou shalt eat it in the sanctuary: the males only shall eat thereof, because it is a consecrated thing to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:3; Exodus 29:27–28; Leviticus 10:14"> But the firstfruits, which the children of Israel shall vow and offer, I have given to thee, and to thy sons, and to thy daughters, by a perpetual law. He that is clean in thy house, shall eat them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:4; Exodus 23:19; Exodus 34:26"> All the best of the oil, and of the wine, and of the corn, whatsoever firstfruits they offer to the Lord, I have given them to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 22:29; Exodus 23:19; Micah 7:1"> All the firstripe of the fruits, that the ground bringeth forth, and which are brought to the Lord, shall be for thy use: he that is clean in thy house, shall eat them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 27:28; Ezekiel 44:29"> Every thing that the children of Israel shall give by vow, shall be thine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 34:20; Exodus 13:2; Exodus 13:12–13"> Whatsoever is firstborn of all flesh, which they offer to the Lord, whether it be of men, or of beasts, shall belong to thee: only for the firstborn of man thou shalt take a price, and every beast that is unclean thou shalt cause to be redeemed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Numbers 3:47; Leviticus 27:2–7"> And the redemption of it shall be after one month, for five sicles of silver, by the weight of the sanctuary. A sicle hath twenty obols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 29:16; Deuteronomy 15:19–22; Leviticus 3:2–5"> But the firstling of a cow and of a sheep and of a goat thou shalt not cause to be redeemed, because they are sanctified to the Lord. Their blood only thou shalt pour upon the altar, and their fat thou shalt burn for a most sweet odour to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 7:31–34; Exodus 29:26–28"> But the flesh shall fall to thy use, as the consecrated breast, and the right shoulder shall be thine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:5; Leviticus 2:13; Numbers 18:11"> All the firstfruits of the sanctuary which the children of Israel offer to the Lord, I have given to thee and to thy sons and daughters, by a perpetual ordinance. It is a covenant of salt for ever before the Lord, to thee and to thy sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:28; Deuteronomy 10:9; Joshua 13:33"> And the Lord said to Aaron: You shall possess nothing in their land, neither shall you have a portion among them: I am thy portion and inheritance in the midst of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:44; Deuteronomy 14:22–29; Nehemiah 10:37"> And I have given to the sons of Levi all the tithes of Israel for a possession for the ministry wherewith they serve me in the tabernacle of the covenant:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 22:9; Numbers 1:51–52; Numbers 3:38"> That the children of Israel may not approach any more to the tabernacle, nor commit deadly sin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 3:7; Numbers 18:20; Numbers 18:1"> But only the sons of Levi may serve me in the tabernacle, and bear the sins of the people. It shall be an everlasting ordinance in your generations. They shall not possess any other thing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 18:26; Malachi 3:8–10"> But be content with the oblation or tithes, which I have separated for their uses and necessities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:38; Numbers 18:21"> Command the Levites, and declare unto them: When you shall receive of the children of Israel the tithes, which I have given you, offer the firstfruits of them to the Lord, that is to say, the tenth part of the tenth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 18:30; Deuteronomy 15:14; 2 Kings 6:27"> That it may be reckoned to you as an oblation of firstfruits, as well of the barnfloors as of the winepresses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 6:20–10; Genesis 14:18"> And of all the things of which you receive tithes, offer the firstfruits to the Lord, and give them to Aaron the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> All the things that you shall offer of the tithes, and shall separate for the gifts of the Lord, shall be the best and choicest things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 3:9–10; Genesis 43:11; Matthew 6:33"> And thou shalt say to them: If you offer all the goodly and the better things of the tithes, it shall be reckoned to you as if you had given the firstfruits of the barnfloor and the winepress:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:10–14; 1 Timothy 5:17–18; Luke 10:7"> And you shall eat them in all your places, both you and your families: because it is your reward for the ministry, wherewith you serve in the tabernacle of the testimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 19:8; Leviticus 22:2; Malachi 1:7"> And you shall not sin in this point, by reserving the choicest and fat things to yourselves, lest you profane the oblations of the children of Israel, and die.</span>
