## The people in Nineveh repented after Jonah warned them

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

That city was so big that a person had to walk for three days to go everywhere in it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="person had to walk for three days to go everywhere in it OR it took three days to walk through it OR it took three days for a person to visit/see it all">a</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="person had to walk for three days to go everywhere in it The walls surrounding Nineveh extended five kilometers around, so a person could walk around the city in a few hours. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 11:32; Matthew 12:41; Joel 2:12–17"> The people in Nineveh believed God's message. They all decided that everyone should stop eating food, and they must dress themselves with old/rough clothes, to show that they were sorry for having sinned. So, everyone, including important people and unimportant people, did so.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-stop" id="fnref-stop" data-fn="eating food OR fast OR abstain from food">stop</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 4:6–10; Esther 4:1–4; Daniel 9:3"> Nineveh's king heard the news about Jonah and what the people were doing. So, he took off his royal robes, and he also put on old/rough clothes. He left his palace, and sat down on cold ashes, to humble himself and show that he also was sorry for having sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:3; Joel 1:18; Joel 2:15–16"> Then he sent messengers to proclaim to everyone in Nineveh:<br />"My advisors and I have decreed that no one may eat or drink anything. Do not even allow your animals to eat or drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jonah 1:6; Isaiah 55:6–7; Ezekiel 18:21–24"> Instead, every person must put on old/rough cloth. Put old/rough cloth on your animals, too. Then everyone must pray fervently to God. And everyone must stop doing evil actions/things, and stop acting violently toward others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:22; Joel 2:13–14; Psalms 106:45"> Perhaps, if everyone does so, then God may change his mind and be merciful to us. Maybe God will stop being very angry with us, with the result that we will not die."</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:8; 1 Kings 21:27–29; Luke 15:20"> When they all did that, God saw what they were doing, and he saw that they had stopped doing evil things. So, he pitied them, and he did not destroy them as he had threatened to do.</span>
</div>
