## Half-Acrostic: Yahweh Takes Vengeance against His Enemies

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 8:2; Exodus 20:5; Psalm 94:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh is a jealous God and avenging;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Yahweh is avenging and ⸤full of wrath⸥.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “a lord of wrath”">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh takes vengeance against his enemies;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he rages against his adversaries.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 147:5; Exodus 34:6–7; Nehemiah 9:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh is slow to anger but great in power;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he will certainly not allow the guilty to go unpunished.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">⸤He marches⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “His path”">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in storm wind and in gale;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">storm clouds are the dust of his feet.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 33:9; Psalm 106:9; Psalm 114:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">He rebukes the sea and he dries up the rivers;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he makes all the rivers run dry.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Bashan and Carmel wither;</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="MT reads “languishes,” but the versions reflect “withers,” which also restores the acrostic">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the blossom of Lebanon languishes.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:10; Malachi 3:2; Deuteronomy 32:22–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">His indignation—who can stand before it?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Who can endure ⸤his fierce anger⸥?</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “the heat of his anger”">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His wrath is poured out like fire;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">rocks are shattered before</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “by”">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">him.</span></span>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Romans 10:15; Isaiah 40:9–10"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Nahum 1:15–2:13 in the English Bible is 2:1–14 in the Hebrew Bible">n</a></sup><span className="pl pl-1">Look!
