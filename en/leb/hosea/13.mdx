<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 25:8; Revelation 21:4; Isaiah 26:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Should I redeem them from ⸤the power⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “the hand”">h</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">of Sheol?</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="“Sheol” is a Hebrew term for the place where the dead reside, i.e., the underworld">i</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Should I deliver them from death?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Where are your plagues, O Death?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Where is your destruction, O Sheol?</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="“Sheol” is a Hebrew term for the place where the dead reside, i.e., the underworld">j</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Compassion is hidden from my eyes.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:12; Ezekiel 17:10; Genesis 41:52"> <span className="pl pl-2">Although he may flourish among reeds,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Hebrew “brothers”">k</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the east wind will come, a wind of Yahweh</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">rising from the desert;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">his fountain will dry up,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">his spring will be parched.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It will plunder his treasury,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “its treasury”">l</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">every object of desire.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 8:12; 2 Kings 15:16; Isaiah 13:16"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Hosea 13:16–14:9 in the English Bible is 14:1–10 in the Hebrew Bible">m</a></sup><span className="pl pl-1">Samaria will be guilty,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because she has rebelled against her God;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they will fall by the sword,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">their children will be dashed to pieces,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their pregnant women will be ripped open.</span></span>
