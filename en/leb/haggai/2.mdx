## A Promise to Rebuild

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Haggai 1:1; Haggai 2:20; Haggai 2:10"> In the seventh month on the twenty-first day, the word of Yahweh ⸤came through⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “came through the hand of”">a</a></sup>Haggai the prophet, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Haggai 1:1; Nehemiah 8:9; Ezra 2:63"> “Speak now to Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and to Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and to the remnant of the people, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezra 3:12; Haggai 2:9; Ezekiel 7:20"> ‘Who among you is left that saw this house in its former glory? And how do you see it now? Does it seem like nothing ⸤to you⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “in your eyes”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:20; Joshua 1:9; Zechariah 8:9"> ‘But now take courage,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “be strong”">c</a></sup>Zerubbabel,’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">d</a></sup>Yahweh. ‘Take courage,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “Be strong”">e</a></sup>Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and take courage,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “be strong”">f</a></sup>all the people of the land,’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">g</a></sup>Yahweh. ‘Do the work, because I am with you,’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">h</a></sup>Yahweh of hosts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 34:10; Exodus 29:45–46; Isaiah 41:13"> ‘according to ⸤the promise⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “the word”">i</a></sup>that ⸤I covenanted⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “I cut”">j</a></sup>with you when you came out of Egypt. My spirit ⸤is with you⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “is standing in your midst”">k</a></sup>do not be afraid.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 12:26–28; Isaiah 10:25; Mark 13:24–26"> For thus says Yahweh of hosts: ‘Once again, in a little while, I will shake the heavens and the earth and the sea and dry land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; 1 Kings 8:11; John 7:37–39"> I will shake all the nations so that the treasure of all the nations will come, and I will fill this house<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="That is, the temple">l</a></sup>with glory,’ says Yahweh of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 50:10–12; Psalm 24:1; Isaiah 60:17"> ‘The silver is mine and the gold is mine,’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">m</a></sup>Yahweh of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 85:8–9; 2 Corinthians 3:9–10; Colossians 1:19–21"> ‘The latter glory of this house will be greater than the former,’ says Yahweh of hosts, ‘and in this place I will give peace’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">n</a></sup>Yahweh of hosts.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Haggai 2:20; Haggai 1:1; Haggai 1:15–1"> On the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, in the second year of Darius, the word of Yahweh came to the prophet Haggai, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 10:10–11; Malachi 2:7; Deuteronomy 33:10"> “Thus says Yahweh of hosts: ‘Ask now the priests for a ruling:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “a torah,” or “a law”">o</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 5:2–3; Numbers 19:11–22; Leviticus 22:4–6"> Then Haggai said, “If one who is unclean from contact with a corpse touches any of these, will it become unclean?” The priests answered, “Yes, it will be become unclean.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 28:9; Titus 1:15; Isaiah 1:11–15"> And Haggai answered and said, “ ‘So it is with this people, and with this nation before me,’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">q</a></sup>Yahweh, ‘and so it is with every kind of work of their hands; and what they offer there is unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Haggai 1:5; Ezra 3:10; Ezra 4:24"> But now, please ⸤consider⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “set your heart”">r</a></sup>from this day forward, before one stone was placed on another in the temple of Yahweh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Haggai 1:6; Malachi 2:2; Proverbs 3:9–10"> ⸤from that time when⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “from their being,” or “from the time they were then”">s</a></sup>one came to a heap of twenty measures, there were only ten, and when one came to the wine vat to draw out fifty measures,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Hebrew “measure”">t</a></sup>there were only twenty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:22; Haggai 1:11; Haggai 1:9"> I struck you with blight, and with plant mildew, and hail, all the work of your hands. But ⸤you did not come back to me⸥,’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “there were none of you to me”">u</a></sup>⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">v</a></sup>Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 8:9; Haggai 2:15; Ezra 5:1–2"> ‘⸤Please consider⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “Please set your heart”">w</a></sup>from this day forward, from the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, from the day that the foundation of Yahweh’s temple was laid,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Or “placed,” or “set”">x</a></sup>⸤consider⸥:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “set your heart”">y</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Malachi 3:10; Habakkuk 3:17–18; Proverbs 3:9–10"> Is there still seed in the store chamber? Do the vine, the fig tree, the pomegranate, and the olive tree still produce nothing? From this day forward I will bless you.’ ”</span>
</div>

## The Coming Kingdom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And the word of Yahweh came to Haggai a second time on the twenty-fourth day of the month saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zechariah 4:6–10; Haggai 2:6–7; Haggai 1:1"> “Say to Zerubbabel, governor of Judah: I am going to shake the heavens and the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Micah 5:10; Judges 7:22; Zephaniah 3:8"> and I will overthrow the thrones of kingdoms and destroy the military strength of the kingdoms of the nations. I will overthrow chariots and their drivers; horses and their riders will fall, every one by the sword of another!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 43:10; Isaiah 42:1; Song of Solomon 8:6"> ‘On that day,’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">z</a></sup>Yahweh of hosts, ‘I will take you, Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, my servant,’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">aa</a></sup>Yahweh, ‘and I will make you a signet ring, for I have chosen you,’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">ab</a></sup>Yahweh of hosts.”</span>
</div>
