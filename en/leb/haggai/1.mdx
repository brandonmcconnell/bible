## The Temple Lies in Ruins

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezra 2:2; Ezra 3:8; Ezra 3:2"> In the second year of King Darius, in the sixth month, on the first day, the word of Yahweh came ⸤through⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “through the hand of”">a</a></sup>Haggai the prophet to Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and to Joshua the son of Jehozadak, the high priest, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezra 4:23–2; Nehemiah 4:10; Ecclesiastes 11:4"> “Thus says Yahweh of hosts: ‘This people says, “⸤The time has not come to rebuild the temple of Yahweh⸥.’ ”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “The time is not coming, the time for the house of Yahweh to be built”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezra 5:1; Zechariah 1:1"> And the word of Yahweh came ⸤through⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “through the hand of”">c</a></sup>Haggai the prophet, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:2; Psalm 132:3–5; Philippians 2:21"> “Is it a time for you yourselves to dwell in your houses that have been paneled while this house<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="That is, the temple">d</a></sup>is desolate?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40; 2 Corinthians 13:5; Haggai 1:7"> And so then, thus says Yahweh of hosts: ‘⸤Consider your ways⸥!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “Set your heart on your ways”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Haggai 1:9; Deuteronomy 28:38–40; Hosea 4:10"> You have sown much but have harvested little. You have eaten without being satisfied; ⸤you have drunk without being satiated⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “You have drunk, without becoming drunk”">f</a></sup>you have worn clothes without being warm; the one who earns wages puts it in ⸤a pouch with holes⸥.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “a pierced pouch”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 119:59–60; Haggai 1:5; Philippians 3:1"> Thus says Yahweh of hosts: ‘⸤Consider your ways⸥!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “Set your heart on your ways”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 132:13–14; 2 Chronicles 7:16; Haggai 2:7"> Go up the mountain and bring wood and build the house<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="That is, the temple">i</a></sup>so that I may be pleased with it and honored,’ says Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Haggai 1:4; Haggai 2:16–17; Haggai 1:6"> ‘You have looked for much, and look! It came to little; and when you brought it home, I blew it away. Why?’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of ”">j</a></sup>Yahweh of hosts. ‘Because my house is desolate and you are running each to your own house!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 8:35; Leviticus 26:19; Deuteronomy 28:23–24"> Therefore, because of you the heavens have withheld the dew and the earth has withheld its produce.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Haggai 2:17; Deuteronomy 28:22; 2 Kings 8:1"> I have called for a drought on the land and the hills,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or mountains">k</a></sup>on the grain, the new wine, the olive oil, on what the soil produces, on human beings and wild animals,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Hebrew “animal”">l</a></sup>and on ⸤all their labor⸥.’ ”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “all the labor of the hands”">m</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 50:10; Ezra 5:2; Haggai 1:14"> Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and all the remnant of the people gave heed to the voice of Yahweh their God and to the words of Haggai the prophet, as Yahweh their God had sent him, ⸤and the people feared Yahweh⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “and the people feared from before Yahweh”">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Matthew 28:20; Malachi 3:1"> And Haggai the messenger of Yahweh spoke to the people with the message of Yahweh, saying, “ ‘I am with you’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">o</a></sup>Yahweh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:22; Hebrews 13:21; Haggai 2:21"> And Yahweh stirred up the spirit of Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and the spirit of Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and the spirit of all the remnant of the people. And they came and did the work on the house of Yahweh of hosts, their God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Haggai 1:1; Haggai 2:20; Haggai 2:1"> on the twenty-fourth day of the sixth month in the second year of King Darius.</span>
</div>
