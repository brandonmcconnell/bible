## Jacob and His Offspring Go to Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 28:13; Genesis 26:22–25; Genesis 28:10"> So Israel journeyed with all that he had, and he came to Beersheba and offered sacrifices to the God of his father, Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 15:1; Job 33:14–15; Job 4:13"> And God spoke to Israel in visions of the night and said, “Jacob, Jacob.” And he said, “Here I am.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 12:2; Deuteronomy 26:5; Isaiah 43:1–2"> Then he said, “I am the God of your father. Do not be afraid to go down to Egypt, for I will make you a great nation there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Genesis 28:15; Genesis 48:21"> I myself will go down with you to Egypt, and I myself will also bring you up. And Joseph will place his hand over your eyes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 45:21; Genesis 45:19; Genesis 45:27"> So Jacob arose from Beersheba. And the sons of Israel carried their father Jacob, and their little ones and their wives in the wagons Pharaoh had sent to transport him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 24:4; Isaiah 52:4; Acts 7:15"> And they took their livestock and their possessions that they had acquired in the land of Canaan. And they came to Egypt, Jacob and all his offspring with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> his sons and his sons’ sons with him, his daughters and his daughters’ daughters with him, into Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 1:1–5; Deuteronomy 33:6; 1 Chronicles 2:1–55"> Now these are the names of the sons of Israel, who came into Egypt, Jacob and his sons. Reuben, the firstborn of Jacob</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> and the sons of Reuben: Enoch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 6:15; Genesis 29:33; Genesis 28:1"> The sons of Simeon: Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jakin, Zohar, and Shaul, the son of a Canaanite woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 29:34; 1 Chronicles 6:16; Numbers 8:1–26"> The sons of Levi: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 4:21; Genesis 38:10; Genesis 29:35"> The sons of Judah: Er, Onan, Shelah, Perez, and Zerah (but Er and Onan died in the land of Canaan). And the sons of Perez were Hezron and Hamul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:32; Genesis 35:23; Genesis 30:14–18"> The sons of Issachar: Tolah, Puvah, Iob, and Shimron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:18–19; Numbers 26:26–27; Numbers 1:30–31"> The sons of Zebulun: Sered, Elon, and Jahleel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 29:32–35; Genesis 30:17–21; Genesis 34:1–31"> These are the sons of Leah that she bore to Jacob in Paddan-Aram, and Dinah his daughter. His sons and daughters were thirty-three persons in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 26:15–17; Genesis 30:11; Numbers 1:24–25"> The sons of Gad: Ziphion, Haggi, Shuni, Ezbon, Eri, Arodi, and Areli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 49:20; Genesis 30:13; Numbers 1:13"> The sons of Asher: Imnah, Ishvah, Ishvi, and Beriah, and their sister Serah. And the sons of Beriah: Heber and Malkiel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 29:24; Genesis 35:26; Genesis 30:9–13"> There are the sons of Zilpah, whom Laban gave to Leah his daughter, and she bore these to Jacob—sixteen persons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 44:27; Genesis 29:18; Genesis 35:24"> The sons of Rachel, Jacob’s wife: Joseph and Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 41:50–52; Genesis 41:45; Deuteronomy 33:13–17"> And Ephraim and Manasseh, whom Asenath, daughter of Potiphera, priest of On bore to him, were born to Joseph in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 7:6–12; 1 Chronicles 8:1–7; Numbers 26:38–40"> The sons of Benjamin: Bela, Beker, Ashbel, Gera, Naaman, Ehi, Rosh, Muppim, Huppim, and Ard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> These are the sons of Rachel who were born to Jacob—fourteen persons in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 1:38–39; Genesis 49:16–17; Numbers 1:12"> The sons of Dan: Hushim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 7:13; Numbers 1:42–43; Genesis 35:25"> The sons of Naphtali: Jahzeel, Guni, Jezer, and Shillem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 29:29; Genesis 35:25; Genesis 30:3–8"> These are the sons of Bilhah whom Laban gave to Rachel his daughter, and she bore these to Jacob—seven persons in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 1:5; Genesis 35:11; Judges 8:30"> All the persons belonging to Jacob who came to Egypt ⸤who were his descendants⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “those going out of his loins”">a</a></sup>not including the wives of the sons of Jacob were sixty-six persons in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:22; Acts 7:14; Exodus 1:5"> And the sons of Joseph who were born to him in Egypt were two persons. All the persons of the house of Jacob who came to Egypt were seventy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 45:10; Genesis 44:16–34; Genesis 49:8"> He<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="That is, Jacob">b</a></sup>had sent Judah ahead of him to Joseph to appear before him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, Jacob">c</a></sup>in Goshen. And they came to the land of Goshen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 15:20; Genesis 45:14–15; Genesis 33:4"> Then Joseph harnessed his chariot and went up to meet Israel his father in Goshen. He presented himself to him and fell upon his neck and wept upon his neck a long time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 2:29–30; Genesis 45:28"> Then Israel said to Joseph, “Now let me die since I have seen your face, for you are still alive.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 47:1–3; Hebrews 2:11; Acts 18:3"> Then Joseph said to his brothers and to his father’s household, “I will go up and report to Pharaoh, and I will say to him, ‘My brothers and my father’s household who were in the land of Canaan have come to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 47:3; 1 Kings 9:27; 1 Kings 18:5–6"> And the men are shepherds, for they are men of livestock, and they have brought their flocks and their cattle and all that they have.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 46:32; Genesis 47:2–4; Jonah 1:8"> And it shall be that when Pharaoh calls you he will say, ‘What is your occupation?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 8:26; Genesis 43:32; Genesis 46:32"> Then you must say, ‘Your servants are men of livestock from our childhood until now, both we and also our ancestors,’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “fathers”">d</a></sup>so that you may dwell in the land of Goshen, for every shepherd is a detestable thing to Egyptians.”</span>
</div>
