## Isaac and Rebekah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 47:29; Genesis 39:4–6; Genesis 15:2"> And Abraham said to his servant, the oldest of his house, who had charge of all he had, “Please put your hand under my thigh</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 28:8"> that I may make you swear by Yahweh, the God of heaven and the God of earth, that you will not take a wife for my son from the daughters of the Canaanites in whose midst I am dwelling,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 28:2; Genesis 22:20–23; Genesis 12:7"> but that you will go to my land and to my family, and take a wife for my son, for Isaac.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:2; Exodus 9:2; Exodus 20:7"> And the servant said to him, “Perhaps the woman will not be willing ⸤to follow⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “to go after”">b</a></sup>me to this land—must I then return your son to the land from whence you came?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 5:1; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Hebrews 10:39"> Abraham said to him, “⸤You must take care⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “let it be careful to you”">c</a></sup>that you do not return my son there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 33:2; Genesis 13:15; Hebrews 1:14"> Yahweh, the God of heaven who took me from the house of my father and from the land of my family, and who spoke to me and swore to me, saying, ‘to your offspring I will give this land,’ he will send his angel before you, and you shall take a wife for my son from there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 2:17–20; Numbers 30:5; Numbers 30:8"> And if the woman is not willing ⸤to follow⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “to go after”">d</a></sup>you, then you shall be released from this oath of mine—only you must not return my son there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 24:2"> Then the servant put his hand under the thigh of Abraham his master, and he swore to him concerning this matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 11:31; Deuteronomy 23:4; Genesis 27:43"> And the servant took ten camels from his master’s camels, and he went with all kinds of his master’s good things in his hand. And he arose and went to Aram-Naharaim, to the city of Nahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:11; Exodus 2:16; John 4:7"> And he made the camels kneel outside the city at the well of water, at the time of evening, toward the time the women went out to draw water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 24:27; Exodus 3:6; Genesis 26:24"> And he said, “O Yahweh, God of my master Abraham, please grant me success today and show loyal love to my master Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 24:43; Psalm 37:5; Exodus 2:16"> Behold, I am standing by the spring of water, and the daughters of the men of the city are going out to draw water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 6:37; Judges 6:17; Genesis 15:8"> And let it be that the girl to whom I shall say, ‘Please, offer your jar that I may drink’ and who says, ‘Drink—and I will also water your camels,’ she is the one you have chosen for your servant, for Isaac. By her I will know that you have shown loyal love to my master.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 11:29; Genesis 24:45; Isaiah 65:24"> And it happened that before he had finished speaking, behold, Rebekah—who was born to Bethuel, son of Milcah, the wife of Nahor, the brother of Abraham—came out, and her jar was on her shoulder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 26:7; Numbers 31:17–18; Genesis 39:6"> Now the girl was very pleasing in appearance. She was a virgin; no man had known her. And she went down to the spring, filled her jar, and came up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 4:7; Isaiah 21:14; Genesis 26:1–35"> And the servant ran to meet her. And he said, “Please, let me drink a little of the water from your jar.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 31:26; 1 Peter 3:8; Genesis 24:14"> And she said, “Drink, my lord.” And she quickly lowered her jar in her hand and gave him a drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 24:14; 1 Peter 4:9; Genesis 24:45–46"> When<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “And”">e</a></sup>she finished giving him a drink she said, “I will also draw water for your camels until they finish drinking.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And she quickly emptied her jar into the trough and ran again to the well to draw water. And she drew water for all his camels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 107:43; Genesis 24:12; 2 Samuel 7:18–20"> And the man was gazing at her silently to know if Yahweh had made his journey successful or not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:11–12; Exodus 32:2–3; Genesis 23:15–16"> And it happened that as the camels finished drinking the man took a gold ring of a half shekel in weight and two bracelets for her arms, ten shekels in weight,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and said, “Please tell me, whose daughter are you? Is there a place at the house of your father for us to spend the night?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 24:15; Genesis 22:23; Genesis 22:20"> And she said to him, “I am the daughter of Bethuel, son of Milcah, whom she bore to Nahor.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 32:8; 1 Peter 4:9; Judges 19:19–21"> Then she said to him, “We have both straw and fodder in abundance, as well as a place to spend the night.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 24:48; Exodus 4:31; Genesis 24:52"> And the man knelt down and worshiped Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 24:48; Genesis 24:12; Psalm 98:3"> And he said, “Blessed be Yahweh, God of my master Abraham, who has not withheld<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “abandoned”">f</a></sup>his loyal love and his faithfulness from my master. I was on the way and Yahweh led me to the house of my master’s brother.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 24:67; Genesis 24:55; Genesis 31:33"> Then the girl ran and reported these things to the household of her mother.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 29:5; Genesis 29:13; Genesis 24:55"> Now Rebekah had a brother, and his name was Laban. And Laban ran out to the man toward the spring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And when he saw the ring and the bracelets on the arms of his sister and heard the words of Rebekah his sister, who said, “Thus the man spoke to me,” he went to the man. And behold, he was standing with the camels at the spring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ruth 3:10; Genesis 26:29; Psalm 115:15"> And he said, “Come, O blessed one of Yahweh. Why do you stand outside? Now I have prepared the house and a place for the camels.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Judges 19:21; Genesis 43:24; Genesis 18:4"> And the man came to the house and unloaded the camels. And he gave straw and fodder to the camels, and water to wash his feet and the feet of the men who were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalm 132:3–5; 1 Timothy 6:2; Ephesians 6:5–8"> ⸤And food was placed before him⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “And it was placed before him”">g</a></sup>to eat. And he said, “I will not eat until ⸤I have told my errand⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “I have spoken my word”">h</a></sup>And he said, “Speak.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And he said, “I am the servant of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 24:1; Genesis 13:2; Proverbs 10:22"> Now Yahweh has blessed my master exceedingly, and he has become great. He has given to him sheep and cattle, silver and gold, male slaves and female slaves, and camels and donkeys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 25:5; Genesis 21:10; Genesis 21:1–7"> And Sarah, the wife of my master, has borne a son to my master after her old age. And he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="That is, Abraham">i</a></sup>has given to him all that he has.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Genesis 27:46; Ezra 9:1–3; Genesis 24:2–9"> And my master made me swear, saying, ‘Do not take a wife for my son from the daughters of the Canaanites in whose land I am living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 24:4; Genesis 31:19; Genesis 12:1"> But you shall go to the house of my father, and to my family, and you shall take a wife for my son.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 24:5"> And I said to my master, ‘Perhaps the woman will not ⸤follow⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “go after”">j</a></sup>me.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Exodus 23:20; Psalm 16:8; Genesis 5:24"> And he said to me, ‘Yahweh, before whom I have walked, shall send his angel with you and will make your journey successful. And you shall take a wife for my son from my family, and from the house of my father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Genesis 24:8; Deuteronomy 29:12"> Then you shall be released from my oath, when you come to my family. And if they will not give a woman to you, then you will be released from my oath.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:11; Genesis 24:12–14; Ezra 8:21"> Then today I came to the spring, and I said, ‘O Yahweh, God of my master Abraham, ⸤if you would please make my journey successful⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “if there is you making successful my journey”">k</a></sup>upon which I am going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Genesis 24:13–14"> Behold, I am standing by the spring of water. Let it be that the young woman who comes out to draw water and to whom I say, “Please give me a little water to drink from your jar,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Genesis 24:14; 1 Peter 3:8; Genesis 2:22"> let her say to me, “Drink; I will also draw water for your camels,” she is the woman whom Yahweh has appointed for the son of my master.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 7:7; Acts 12:12–17; Genesis 24:15–20"> I had not yet finished speaking to myself<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “to my heart”">l</a></sup>when, behold, Rebekah was coming out with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “and”">m</a></sup>her jar on her shoulder. And she went down to the spring and drew water. And I said to her, ‘Please give me a drink.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Genesis 24:18–19"> And she hastened and let down her jar ⸤from her shoulder⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “from upon her”">n</a></sup>and said, ‘Drink, and I will give a drink to your camels also.’ Then I drank and she gave a drink to the camels also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalm 45:13–14; Genesis 24:22–24; Isaiah 62:3–5"> Then I asked her and said, ‘Whose daughter are you?’ And she said, ‘The daughter of Bethuel, son of Nahor, whom Milcah bore to him.’ And I put the ring on her nose and the bracelets on her arms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Genesis 24:26–27; Psalm 48:14"> And I knelt down and worshiped Yahweh, and I praised Yahweh, the God of my master Abraham, who led me on the right way, to take the daughter of the brother of my master for his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Genesis 47:29; Joshua 2:14; Proverbs 3:3"> So now, ⸤if you are going to deal loyally and truly⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “if there is you doing loyal love and faithfulness”">o</a></sup>with my master, tell me. And if not, tell me, so that I may turn to the right or to the left.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="An idiom for “so that I might know what to do”">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Psalm 118:23; Genesis 31:24; Genesis 31:29"> Then Laban and Bethuel answered, and they said, “The matter has gone out from Yahweh; we are not able to speak bad or good to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Genesis 20:15; Genesis 24:15; 2 Samuel 16:10"> Here is Rebekah before you. Take her and go; let her be a wife for the son of your master as Yahweh has spoken.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Genesis 24:26; Genesis 24:48; Psalm 116:1–2"> And it happened that when the servant of Abraham heard their words he bowed down to the ground to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Genesis 24:22; Genesis 24:10; Exodus 12:35"> And the servant brought out silver jewelry and gold jewelry, and garments, and he gave them to Rebekah. And he gave precious gifts to her brother and to her mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Genesis 24:56; Genesis 24:59; Genesis 28:5–6"> And he and the men who were with him ate and drank, and they spent the night. And they got up in the morning, and he said, “Let me go to my master.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Genesis 4:3; Leviticus 25:29; Judges 14:8"> And her brother and her mother said, “Let the girl remain with us ten days or so; after that she may go.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Proverbs 25:25; Joshua 1:8; Isaiah 48:15"> And he said to them, “Do not delay me. Now, Yahweh has made my journey successful. Let me go. I must go to my master.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> And they said, “Let us call the girl and ask ⸤her opinion⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “her mouth”">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Luke 1:38; Psalm 45:10–11"> And they called Rebekah and said to her, “Will you go with this man?” And she said, “I will go.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Genesis 35:8; Numbers 11:12; Genesis 24:50"> So they sent away Rebekah their sister, and her nurse, and the servant of Abraham and his men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Genesis 17:16; Ruth 4:11–12"> And they blessed Rebekah and said to her, “You are our sister; may you become countless thousands; and may your offspring take possession of the gate of his enemies.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Psalm 45:10; Genesis 2:24; Esther 8:14"> And Rebekah and her maidservants arose, and they mounted the camels and ⸤followed⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “went after”">r</a></sup>the man. And the servant took Rebekah and left.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Genesis 25:11; Genesis 16:14; Genesis 12:9"> Now Isaac was coming from the direction of Beer-Lahai-Roi. And he was living in the land of the Negev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Psalm 119:15; Psalm 1:2; Psalm 104:34"> And Isaac went out to meditate in the field ⸤early in the evening⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “at the turning of evening”">s</a></sup>and he lifted up his eyes and saw—behold, camels were coming.</span>
