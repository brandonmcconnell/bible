## The Dreams of Joseph

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Genesis 23:4; Genesis 36:7"> And Jacob settled in the land of the sojourning of his father, in the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 35:25–26; Genesis 35:22; 1 Samuel 2:22–24"> These are the generations<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “family records”">a</a></sup>of Jacob. Joseph, being seventeen years old, was shepherding the flock with his brothers. Now he was a helper with the sons of Bilhah and the sons of Zilpah, the wives of his father. And Joseph brought a bad report of them to his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 3:35; Genesis 37:32; 2 Samuel 13:18"> Now Israel loved Joseph more than all his sons, for he was a son of his old age. And he made a robe with long sleeves<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “of many colors”">b</a></sup>for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 27:41; 1 John 3:12; 1 John 4:20"> When his brothers saw that their father loved him more than all his brothers, they hated him and were not able to speak peaceably to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 28:12; Genesis 42:9; John 17:14"> And Joseph dreamed a dream, and he told it to his brothers. And ⸤they hated him even more⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “they added still to hate him”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 44:18; Judges 9:7"> And he said to them, “Listen now to this dream that I dreamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 44:14; Genesis 42:6; Genesis 43:26"> Now behold, we were binding sheaves in the midst of the field and, behold, my sheaf stood up and it remained standing. Then behold, your sheaves gathered around and bowed down to my sheaf.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:27; Acts 7:35; Hebrews 10:29"> Then his brothers said to him, “Will you really rule over us?” And ⸤they hated him even more⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “they added still to hate him”">d</a></sup>on account of his dream and because of his words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 7:9–14; Genesis 37:7; Daniel 8:10"> Then he dreamed yet another dream and told it to his brothers. And he said, “Behold, I dreamed a dream again, and behold, the sun and the moon and eleven stars were bowing down to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 27:29; Philippians 2:10–11; Isaiah 60:14"> And he told it to his father and to his brothers. And his father rebuked him and said to him, “What is this dream that you have dreamed? Will I and your mother and your brothers indeed come to bow down to the ground to you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 7:9; Luke 2:19; Luke 2:51"> And his brothers were jealous of him, but his father kept the matter in mind.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Joseph Sold Into Slavery by his Brothers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 37:1; Genesis 33:18; Genesis 34:25–31"> Now his brothers went to pasture the flock of their father in Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1–3; 1 Samuel 3:8; 1 Samuel 3:16"> And Israel said to Joseph, “Are not your brothers pasturing in Shechem? Come, let me send you to them.” And he said, “Here I am.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 35:27; Luke 19:42; Psalm 125:5"> Then he said to him, “Go now, see ⸤if it goes well for your brothers and for the flock⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “see the peace of your brothers and the peace of the flock”">e</a></sup>then return word to me.” And he sent him from the valley of Hebron, and he arrived at Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 4:27; Judges 4:22; Genesis 21:14"> And a man found him, and behold, he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="That is, Joseph">f</a></sup>was wandering about in a field. And the man asked him, “What do you seek?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:10; Song of Solomon 1:7"> And he said, “I am seeking my brothers. Tell me, please, where they are pasturing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 6:13"> And the man said, “They have moved on from here, for I heard them saying, ‘Let us go to Dothan.’ ” Then Joseph went after his brothers and found them in Dothan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 23:12; Psalm 37:12; Psalm 37:32"> And they saw him from a distance. And before he drew near to them, they conspired against him to kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 28:12; Genesis 37:11; Genesis 37:5"> And each said to his brothers, “Look, this master of dreams is coming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Titus 3:3; Proverbs 27:4; Matthew 2:2–16"> Now then, come, let us kill him and throw him in one of the pits. Then we will say a wild animal devoured him. Then we will see what his dreams become.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 42:22; Genesis 35:22; Joshua 10:28"> And Reuben heard it and delivered him from their hand and said, “We must not take his life.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 22:12; Deuteronomy 13:9; Genesis 42:22"> And Reuben said to them, “You must not shed blood. Throw him into this pit that is in the desert, but do not lay a hand on him”—so that he might rescue him from their hand to return him to his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 37:3; Matthew 27:28; Genesis 42:21"> And it happened that as Joseph came to his brothers they stripped Joseph of his robe, the robe with long sleeves,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “of many colors”">g</a></sup>that was upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:6; Psalm 130:1–2; Zechariah 9:11"> And they took him and threw him into the pit (the pit was empty; there was no water in it).</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 37:28; Genesis 43:11; Jeremiah 8:22"> Then they sat down to eat some food. And they lifted up their eyes and looked, and behold, a caravan of Ishmaelites was coming from Gilead. And their camels were carrying aromatic gum and balm and spices ⸤on the way⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “going to bring down”">h</a></sup>to Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 37:20; Genesis 4:10; Genesis 25:32"> Then Judah said to his brothers, “What profit is there if we kill our brother and conceal his blood?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; Genesis 42:21; 1 Samuel 18:17"> Come, let us sell him to the Ishmaelites, but our hand shall not be against him, for he is our brother, our own flesh.” And his brothers agreed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 7:9; Psalm 105:17; Genesis 45:4–5"> Then Midianite traders passed by. And they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="That is, the brothers">i</a></sup>drew Joseph up and brought him up from the pit, and they sold Joseph to the Ishmaelites for twenty pieces of silver. And they brought Joseph to Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 1:20; Genesis 37:34; Genesis 44:13"> Then Reuben returned to the pit and, behold, Joseph was not in the pit. And he tore his clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 42:13; Jeremiah 31:15; Genesis 42:32"> And he returned to his brothers and said, “The boy ⸤is gone⸥!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “is not”">j</a></sup>Now I, ⸤what can I do⸥?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “where am I going?”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 37:23; Proverbs 28:13; Genesis 37:3"> Then they took the robe of Joseph and slaughtered a goat, and dipped the robe in the blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 15:30; Genesis 37:3; Genesis 44:20–23"> Then they sent the robe with long sleeves<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “of many colors”">l</a></sup>and they brought it to their father and said, “We found this; please examine it. Is it the robe of your son or not?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 37:20; Genesis 44:28; John 13:7"> And he recognized it and said, “The robe of my son! A wild animal has devoured him! Joseph is surely torn to pieces!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 37:29; 2 Samuel 3:31; Psalm 69:11"> And Jacob tore his clothes and put sackcloth on his loins and mourned for his son many days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 42:38; Job 2:11; 2 Samuel 12:17"> And all his sons and daughters tried to console him, but he refused to be consoled. And he said, “No, I shall go down to my son, to Sheol, mourning.” And his father wept for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 40:4; Genesis 37:28; 2 Kings 25:8"> And the Midianites sold him in Egypt to Potiphar, a court official of Pharaoh, a commander of the imperial guard.</span>
</div>
