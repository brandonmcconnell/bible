## Jacob Flees to Haran

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 6:3; Judges 6:33; Hosea 12:12"> And Jacob ⸤continued his journey⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “lifted up his feet”">a</a></sup>and went to the land of the Easterners.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “children of the east”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 4:14; Isaiah 49:10; John 4:6"> And he looked, and behold, there was a well in the field, and behold, there were three flocks of sheep lying beside it, for out of that well the flocks were watered. And the stone on the mouth of the well was large.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And when all the flocks were gathered there, they rolled away the stone from the mouth of the well. And they watered the sheep and returned the stone upon the mouth of the well to its place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 28:10; Genesis 27:43; Acts 7:2"> And Jacob said to them, “My brothers, where are you from?” And they said, “We are from Haran.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 24:24; Genesis 24:29; Genesis 31:53"> And he said to them, “Do you know Laban, son of Nahor?” And they said, “We know him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:9; Genesis 37:14; Exodus 18:7"> And he said to them, “⸤Is he well⸥?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “Is it well for him?”">c</a></sup>And they said, “He is well. Now look, Rachel his daughter is coming with the sheep.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 5:16; Galatians 6:9–10"> And he said, “Look, it is still ⸤broad daylight⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “high day”">d</a></sup>it is not the time for the livestock to be gathered. Give water to the sheep and go, pasture them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 16:3; Luke 24:2; Genesis 34:14"> And they said, “We are not able, until all the flocks are gathered. Then the stone is rolled away from the mouth of the well, and we water the sheep.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 2:21; Song of Solomon 1:7–8; Exodus 2:15–16"> While he was speaking with them, Rachel came with the sheep which belonged to her father, for she was pasturing them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 2:17"> And it happened that, when Jacob saw Rachel, the daughter of Laban, his mother’s brother, and the sheep of Laban, his mother’s brother, Jacob drew near and rolled away the stone from the mouth of the well and watered the sheep of Laban, his mother’s brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 33:4; Genesis 45:14–15; Genesis 45:2"> And Jacob kissed Rachel, and lifted up his voice and wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 24:28; Genesis 13:8; Genesis 14:14–16"> And Jacob told Rachel that he was the relative of her father, and that he was the son of Rebekah. And she ran and told her father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 24:29; Acts 20:37; Romans 16:16"> And it happened that when Laban heard the message about Jacob, the son of his sister, he ran to meet him. And he embraced him and kissed him, and brought him to his house. And he told Laban all these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:12–13; Genesis 2:23; Judges 9:2"> And Laban said to him, “Surely you are my flesh and my bone!” And he stayed with him a month.</span>

## Jacob’s Marriages

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 30:28; Genesis 31:7"> Then Laban said to Jacob, “Just because you are my brother should you work for me for nothing? Tell me what your wage should be.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ruth 4:11; Genesis 49:31; Genesis 31:4"> Now Laban had two daughters. The name of the older was Leah, and the name of the younger was Rachel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 12:11; Genesis 46:19–22; Genesis 24:16"> Now the eyes of Leah were dull, but Rachel was beautiful in form and appearance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hosea 12:12; Exodus 22:16–17; Genesis 34:12"> And Jacob loved Rachel and said, “I will serve you seven years for Rachel your younger daughter.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 6:11; Isaiah 6:5; Psalm 12:2"> Then Laban said, “Better that I give her to you than I give her to another man. Stay with me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:7; Ephesians 5:2; Hosea 12:12"> And Jacob worked for Rachel seven years, but they were as a few days in his eyes because he loved her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 15:1; Genesis 29:20; Genesis 4:1"> And Jacob said to Laban, “Give me my wife, that I may go in to her, for ⸤my time⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “my days”">e</a></sup>is completed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 19:9; Ruth 4:10–13; Judges 14:10–18"> So Laban gathered all the men of the place and prepared a feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Micah 7:5; Genesis 38:14–15; Genesis 24:65"> And it happened that in the evening he took Leah his daughter and brought her to him, and he went in to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 16:1; Genesis 30:9–12; Genesis 46:18"> And Laban gave Zilpah his female servant to her, to Leah his daughter as a female servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Proverbs 11:31; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> And it happened that in the morning, behold, it was Leah! And he said to Laban, “What is this you have done to me? Did I not serve with you for Rachel? Now why did you deceive me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Then Laban said, “⸤It is not the custom⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “it is not thus done”">f</a></sup>in our country to give the younger before the firstborn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 18:18; Judges 14:12; Genesis 2:2–3"> Complete the week of this one,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Leah; the wedding feast would last one week">g</a></sup>then I will also give you the other, ⸤on the condition that you will work for me⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “with respect to the work that you will work with me yet”">h</a></sup>another seven years.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Jacob did so. So he completed the week of this one,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="That is, Leah">i</a></sup>then he gave Rachel his daughter to him as a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 35:22; Genesis 29:24; Genesis 30:3–8"> And Laban gave Bilhah his female servant to Rachel his daughter as a female servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 31:41; Genesis 29:20; Genesis 31:15"> Then he also went in to Rachel, and he loved Rachel more than Leah. And he served with him yet another seven years.</span>

## Jacob’s Children

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:21; Matthew 10:37; Deuteronomy 21:15"> When Yahweh saw that Leah was unloved he opened her womb, but Rachel was barren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:7; Exodus 4:31; Exodus 3:7"> Then Leah conceived and gave birth to a son, and she called his name Reuben, for she said, “Because Yahweh has noticed my misery, that I am unloved. Now my husband will love me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 34:25; Genesis 49:5–6; Genesis 34:30"> And she conceived again and gave birth to a son. And she said, “It is because Yahweh has heard that I am unloved that he gave me this son also.” And she called his name Simeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 32:26–29; Genesis 49:5–7; Genesis 46:11"> And she conceived again and gave birth to a son. Then she said, “Now this time my husband will be joined to me, for I have borne him three sons.” Therefore, she called his name Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 1:2; 1 Chronicles 5:2; Genesis 49:8–12"> And she conceived again and gave birth to a son. And she said, “This time I will praise Yahweh.” Therefore she called his name Judah. And she ceased bearing children.</span>
