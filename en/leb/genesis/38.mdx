## Judah and Tamar

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:1; 2 Kings 4:8; Proverbs 13:20"> And it happened that at that time Judah went down from his brothers and pitched his tent near a certain Adullamite, whose name was Hirah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:3; Genesis 24:3; Genesis 6:2"> And Judah saw the daughter of a certain Canaanite there whose name was Shua. And he took her and went in to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Numbers 26:19"> And she conceived and bore a son, and he called his name Er.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Numbers 26:19"> And she conceived again and bore a son, and he called his name Onan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 26:20; Genesis 38:26; Genesis 46:12"> And once again she bore a son, and she called his name Shelah. And he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, Judah">a</a></sup>was in Chezib when she bore him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 24:3; Genesis 21:21; Matthew 1:3"> And Judah took a wife for Er his firstborn, and her name was Tamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:3; Genesis 46:12; Numbers 26:19"> And Er, the firstborn of Judah, was evil in the eyes of Yahweh, and Yahweh killed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Matthew 22:23–27; Ruth 4:5–11"> Then Judah said to Onan, “Go in to the wife of your brother and perform the duty of a brother-in-law to her, and raise up offspring for your brother.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Titus 3:3; Deuteronomy 25:6; James 3:16"> But Onan knew that the offspring would not be for him, so whenever he went in to the wife of his brother he would waste it on the ground<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Meaning he would spill his semen on the ground">b</a></sup>so as not to give offspring to his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Numbers 11:1; 1 Chronicles 21:7"> And what he did was evil in the sight of Yahweh, so he killed him also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 22:13; Ruth 1:11–13"> Then Judah said to Tamar, his daughter-in-law, “Stay a widow in your father’s house until Shelah my son grows up,” for ⸤he feared he would also die⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “he thought lest he also will die”">c</a></sup>like his brother. So Tamar went and stayed in the house of her father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 15:10; 2 Samuel 13:39; Judges 14:1"> ⸤And in the course of time⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “And the days increased”">d</a></sup>the daughter of Shua, the wife of Judah, died. When Judah was consoled he went up to his sheepshearers, he and his friend Hirah the Adullamite, to Timnah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And it was told to Tamar, saying, “Look, your father-in-law is going up to Timnah to shear his sheep.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 38:26; Genesis 38:11–13; Jeremiah 3:2"> So she removed the clothes of her widowhood and covered herself with the veil and disguised herself. And she sat at the entrance to Eynayim, which is on the way to Timnah, for she saw that Shelah was grown but she had not been given to him as a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Judah saw her and reckoned her to be a prostitute, for she had covered her face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:10; Matthew 26:15; 2 Samuel 13:11"> And he turned aside to her at the roadside and said, “Please come, let me come in to you,” for he did not know that she was his daughter-in-law. And she said, “What will you give to me that you may come in to me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:33; Genesis 38:20; Proverbs 20:16"> And he said, “I will send a kid from the goats of the flock.” And she said, “Only if you give a pledge until you send it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 38:25–26; Hosea 4:11; Jeremiah 22:24"> And he said, “What is the pledge that I must give to you?” And she said, “your seal, your cord, and your staff that is in your hand.” And he gave them to her and went in to her. And she conceived by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 38:14; 2 Samuel 14:5; 2 Samuel 14:2"> And she arose and left, and she removed her veil from herself and put on the garments of her widowhood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 20:9; Leviticus 19:17; 2 Samuel 13:3"> And Judah sent the kid from the goats by the hand of his friend the Adullamite to take back the pledge from the hand of the woman, but he could not find her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> So he asked the men of her place, saying, “Where is that cult prostitute that was at Eynayim by the roadside?” And they said, “There is no cult prostitute here.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Then he returned to Judah and said, “I could not find her. Morever, the men of the place said, ‘There is no cult prostitute here.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ephesians 5:12; Proverbs 6:33; Revelation 16:15"> And Judah said, “Let her take them for herself, lest we be ⸤laughed at⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “as contempt”">e</a></sup>Behold, I sent this kid, but you could not find her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 21:9; Ezekiel 23:19; Judges 19:2"> And ⸤about three months later⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “it happened about three months”">f</a></sup>it was told to Judah, “Tamar your daughter-in-law has played the whore, and now, behold, she has conceived by prostitution.” And Judah said, “Bring her out and let her be burned.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 37:32; Genesis 38:18; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> She was brought out, but she sent to her father-in-law saying, “By the man to whom these belong I have conceived.” And she said, “Now discern<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “examine”">g</a></sup>to whom these belong: the seal and cord and the staff.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:17; John 8:9; Titus 2:11–12"> Then Judah recognized them and said, “She is more righteous than I, since I did not give her to my son Shelah.” And he did not know<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="“Know” is a euphemism for “have sexual relations with”">h</a></sup>her again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 25:24"> And it happened that at the time she gave birth that, behold, twins were in her womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And it happened that at her labor one child put out a hand. And the midwife took it and tied a crimson thread on his hand saying, “This one came out first.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:4; Matthew 1:3; Genesis 46:12"> Then his hand drew back and, behold, his brother came out, and she said, “What a breach you have made for yourself!” And she called his name Perez.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:6; 1 Chronicles 2:4; Matthew 1:3"> And afterward his brother who had the crimson thread on his hand came out. And his name was called Zerah.</span>
