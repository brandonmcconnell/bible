## Sarah’s Death and Burial

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 17:17"> And ⸤Sarah lived⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “the lives of Sarah were”">a</a></sup>one hundred and twenty-seven years; these were the years of the life of Sarah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 23:19; Genesis 13:18; 1 Chronicles 6:57"> And Sarah died in Kiriath Arba; that is Hebron, in the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 10:15; Genesis 23:7; 1 Samuel 26:6"> And Abraham went to mourn for Sarah and to weep for her. And Abraham rose up from his dead, and he spoke to the Hittites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “sons of Heth”">b</a></sup>and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:15; Hebrews 11:9; Genesis 17:8"> “I am a stranger and an alien among you; give to me ⸤my own burial site⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “ownership of a grave”">c</a></sup>among you so that I may bury my dead from before me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the Hittites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “sons of Heth”">d</a></sup>answered Abraham and said to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 24:35; Genesis 14:14; Exodus 32:22"> “Hear us, my lord, you are a mighty prince<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “prince of God”">e</a></sup>in our midst. Bury your dead in the choicest of our burial sites. None of us ⸤will withhold his burial site⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “will withhold from himself his burial site">f</a></sup>from you for burying your dead.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 12:14; Genesis 19:1; Proverbs 18:24"> And Abraham rose up and bowed to the people of the land, to the Hittites.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “sons of Heth”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 25:9; 1 John 2:1–2; 1 Kings 2:17"> And he spoke with them, saying, “⸤If you are willing⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “if there is with your inner selves / souls”">h</a></sup>that I bury my dead from before me, hear me and intercede for me with Ephron the son of Zohar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 13:8; Romans 12:17"> that he may sell<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “give”">i</a></sup>to me the cave of Machpelah which ⸤belongs to him⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “which is to him”">j</a></sup>which is at the end of his field. At full value let him sell<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “give”">k</a></sup>it to me in your midst as ⸤a burial site⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “ownership of a grave”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 34:20; Genesis 34:24; Genesis 23:18"> Now Ephron was sitting among the Hittites.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “sons of Heth”">m</a></sup>And Ephron the Hittite answered Abraham in the hearing of the Hittites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “sons of Heth”">n</a></sup>with respect to all who were entering the gate of his city, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:24; Isaiah 32:8; Deuteronomy 19:15"> “No, my lord, hear me. I give you the field and the cave which is in it, I also give it to you in the sight of the children of my people I give it to you. Bury your dead.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 23:7; Genesis 18:2; Genesis 19:1"> And Abraham bowed before the people of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 13:8; 2 Samuel 24:24; Genesis 14:22–23"> And he spoke to Ephron in the hearing of the people of the land, saying, “⸤If only you will hear me⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “Only if you perhaps hear me”">o</a></sup>—I give the price of the field. Take it from me that I may bury my dead there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And Ephron answered Abraham, saying to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:12; Exodus 30:13; Exodus 30:15"> “My lord, hear me. A piece of land worth four hundred shekels of silver—what is that between me and you? Bury your dead.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 11:12; Jeremiah 32:9; Exodus 30:13"> Then Abraham listened to Ephron, and Abraham weighed for Ephron the silver that he had named<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “spoken”">p</a></sup>in the hearing of the Hittites:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “sons of Heth”">q</a></sup>four hundred shekels of silver ⸤at the merchants’ current rate⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “passing to the merchant”">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 25:9; Genesis 50:13; Genesis 23:20"> So the field of Ephron which was in the Machpelah, which was near Mamre—the field and the cave which was in it, with all the trees that were in the field, which were within all its surrounding boundaries—⸤passed⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “went up”">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ruth 4:1; Jeremiah 32:12; Genesis 34:20"> to Abraham as a property in the presence of the Hittites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Or “sons of Heth”">t</a></sup>with respect to all who were entering the gate of his city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 50:13; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Ecclesiastes 12:5"> And thus afterward Abraham buried Sarah his wife in the cave of the field of Machpelah near Mamre; that is Hebron, in the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:10–14; Ruth 4:7–10; Genesis 49:31–32"> And the field and the cave which was in it ⸤passed⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “went up”">u</a></sup>to Abraham as ⸤a burial site⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “ownership of a grave”">v</a></sup>from the Hittites.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or “sons of Heth”">w</a></sup></span>
</div>
