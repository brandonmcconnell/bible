## Abraham and Abimelech

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 26:1; Genesis 26:6; Genesis 18:1"> And Abraham journeyed from there to the land of the Negev, and he settled between Kadesh and Shur. And he dwelled as an alien in Gerar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 12:15; Genesis 26:7; Genesis 12:11–13"> And Abraham said about Sarah his wife, “She is my sister.” And Abimelech king of Gerar sent and took Sarah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 20:7; Matthew 27:19; Psalm 105:14"> And God came to Abimelech in a dream at night. And he said to him, “Look, you are a dead man on account of the woman you have taken. For she is ⸤a married woman⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “married of a husband” or “a woman owned by a husband”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 18:23–25; 1 Chronicles 21:17; 2 Samuel 4:11"> Now Abimelech had not approached her, so he said, “my Lord, will you even kill a righteous people?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 7:8; 1 Kings 9:4; Daniel 6:22"> Did not he himself say to me, ‘She is my sister’? And she herself said, ‘He is my brother.’ With integrity of my heart and with cleanness of my hands I did this.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:34; Proverbs 21:1; 1 Samuel 25:26"> Then God said to him in the dream, “Yes,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “Also”">b</a></sup>I know that in the integrity of your heart you did this, and I also ⸤kept you from sinning⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “kept back from sinning”">c</a></sup>against me. Therefore, I did not allow you to touch her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 42:8; 1 Samuel 7:5; 2 Samuel 24:17"> So now, return the wife of the man, for he is a prophet, so that he will pray for you and you will live. And ⸤if you do not return her⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “If there is not for you a returning”">d</a></sup>know that you will certainly die, and all that are yours.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> So Abimelech rose early in the morning. And he called all his servants and ⸤told them all these things⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “said all these things in their ears”">e</a></sup>and the men were very afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 12:18; Genesis 26:10; Genesis 39:9"> And Abimelech called for Abraham and said to him, “What have you done to us? And how have I sinned against you that you brought upon me and upon my kingdom a great sin? You have done things to me that should not be done.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Abimelech said to Abraham, “⸤What were you thinking⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “What have you seen?”">f</a></sup>that you did this thing?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 26:7; Genesis 12:12; Job 28:28"> And Abraham said, “Because I thought, surely there is no fear of God in this place; they will kill me on account of the matter of my wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 11:29; Genesis 12:13; 1 Thessalonians 5:22"> ⸤Besides⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “And also moreover”">g</a></sup>she is my sister, the daughter of my father, but not the daughter of my mother. And she became my wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 12:1; Genesis 12:9; Acts 7:3–5"> And it happened that as God caused me to wander from the house of my father I said to her, ‘This is your loyal kindness that you must do for me at every place where we come: say concerning me, “He is my brother.” ’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 12:16; Genesis 20:2; Genesis 20:7"> And Abimelech took sheep and cattle and male slaves and female slaves, and he gave them to Abraham. And he returned Sarah his wife to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 34:10; Genesis 13:9; Genesis 47:6"> And Abimelech said, “Here is my land before you; settle ⸤where it pleases you⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “in the good in your eyes”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 27:5; Genesis 20:5; Genesis 24:65"> And to Sarah he said, “Look, I have given a thousand pieces of silver to your brother. It shall be ⸤an exoneration⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “a covering of the eyes”">i</a></sup>⸤You are vindicated before all who are with you⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “to all who are with you and with all you are vindicated”">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 5:16; Matthew 21:22; Job 42:9–10"> And Abraham prayed to God, and God healed Abimelech and his wife and his female servants so that they could bear children again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 12:17; Genesis 30:2; Genesis 16:2"> For Yahweh had completely closed up all the wombs of the house of Abimelech because of the matter of Sarah, the wife of Abraham.</span>
</div>
