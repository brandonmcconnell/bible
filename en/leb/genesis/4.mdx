## Cain and Abel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 3:12; Genesis 3:15; Genesis 4:25"> Now Adam knew<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “had intercourse with”">a</a></sup>Eve his wife, and she conceived and bore Cain. And she said, “I have given birth to a man with the help of Yahweh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 3:15; 1 John 3:12; Luke 11:51"> ⸤Then she bore⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “And she added to bear”">b</a></sup>his brother Abel. And Abel became a keeper of sheep, and Cain became a tiller of the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 18:12; Leviticus 2:1–11; 1 Kings 17:7"> And ⸤in the course of time⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “it came to pass at the end of days”">c</a></sup>Cain brought an offering from the fruit of the ground to Yahweh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 11:4; Proverbs 3:9; Exodus 13:12"> and Abel also brought an offering from ⸤the choicest firstlings of his flock⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “from the firstlings of his flock and from their fat”">d</a></sup>And Yahweh looked with favor to Abel and to his offering,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:4; Job 5:2; Luke 15:28–30"> but to Cain and to his offering he did not look with favor. And Cain became very angry, and his face fell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 1:18; Job 5:2; Luke 15:31–32"> And Yahweh said to Cain, “Why are you angry, and why is your face fallen?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:15; Romans 2:6–10; Isaiah 3:10–11"> If you do well ⸤will I not accept you⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “a lifting up”; this is an abbreviation of the Hebrew idiom “to lift up the face,” which means “to accept or regard with favor”">e</a></sup>But if you do not do well, sin is crouching at the door. And its desire is for you, but you must rule over it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 23:35; 1 John 3:12–15; Jude 1:11"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “And”">f</a></sup>Cain said to his brother Abel, “Let us go out into the field.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="This phrase is not present in the Hebrew; it is supplied in other versions Samaritan, Septuagint, Syriac, Vulgate">g</a></sup>And when they were in the field, Cain rose up against his brother Abel and killed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; John 8:44; Psalm 9:12"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “And”">h</a></sup>Yahweh said to Cain, “Where is Abel your brother?” And he said, “I do not know; am I my brother’s keeper?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 12:24; Psalm 72:14; Hebrews 11:4"> And he said, “What have you done? The voice of your brother’s blood is crying out to me from the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Job 16:18; Genesis 3:14"> So now you are cursed from the ground, which has opened its mouth to receive the blood of your brother from your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 9:17; Leviticus 26:36; Leviticus 26:20"> When you till the ground ⸤it shall no longer yield its strength to you⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “it shall not add to give its strength to you”">i</a></sup>You shall be a wanderer and a fugitive on the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 15:22; Revelation 16:9; Revelation 16:11"> And Cain said to Yahweh, “My punishment is greater than I can bear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 35:19; Psalm 143:7; Job 15:20–24"> Look, you have driven me out today from the face of the ground, and from your face I must hide. I will be a wanderer and a fugitive on the earth, and it will happen that whoever finds me will kill me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:4; Psalm 79:12; Genesis 4:24"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “And”">j</a></sup>Yahweh said to him, “Therefore, whoever kills Cain will be avenged sevenfold.” Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “And”">k</a></sup>Yahweh put a sign on Cain so that whoever found him would not kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:39; Job 2:7; Genesis 4:14"> And Cain went out from the presence of Yahweh, and he settled in the land of Nod, east of Eden.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 49:11; Ecclesiastes 2:4–11; Daniel 4:30"> And Cain knew<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “had intercourse with”">l</a></sup>his wife, and she conceived and gave birth to Enoch. And when he built a city ⸤he named the city after his son, Enoch⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “he called the name of the city as the name of his son, Enoch”">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 36:2; Genesis 5:21"> And to Enoch was born Irad, and Irad fathered Mehujael, and Mehujael fathered Methushael, and Methushael fathered Lamech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 2:24; Matthew 19:8; Genesis 2:18"> And Lamech took to himself two wives.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “women”">n</a></sup>The name of the first was Adah, and the name of the second was Zillah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 4:2; Jeremiah 35:9–10; John 8:44"> And Adah gave birth to Jabal; he was the father of those who live in tents and those who have livestock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 21:12; Isaiah 5:12; Genesis 31:27"> And the name of his brother was Jubal; he was the father of all who play stringed instruments and wind instruments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:25; Deuteronomy 8:9; 2 Chronicles 2:7"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “And”">o</a></sup>Zillah also gave birth to Tubal-Cain who forged all kinds of tools of bronze and iron. And the sister of Tubal-Cain was Naamah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 23:18; Exodus 20:13; Leviticus 19:18"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “And”">p</a></sup>Lamech said to his wives,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Adah and Zillah, listen to my voice;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">O wives of Lamech, hear my words.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I have killed a man for wounding me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Even a young man for injuring me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 18:22; Genesis 4:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">If Cain is avenged sevenfold,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Then Lamech will be avenged seventy and seven times.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 4:8; Genesis 4:10–11; 1 Chronicles 1:1"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “And”">q</a></sup>Adam knew<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “had intercourse with”">r</a></sup>his wife again, and she gave birth to a son. And she called his name Seth, for she said “God has appointed to me another child in the place of Abel, because Cain killed him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalm 116:17; 1 Kings 18:24; Joel 2:32"> And as for Seth, he also fathered a son, and he called his name Enosh. At that time he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “humankind,” if the 3ms form is considered collective">s</a></sup>began to call on the name of Yahweh.</span>
</div>
