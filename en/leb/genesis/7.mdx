## The Flood

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 5:32; Genesis 8:13"> Noah ⸤was six hundred years old⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “was a son of six hundred years”">g</a></sup>when<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “and”">h</a></sup>the flood waters came upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 17:27; Genesis 6:18; Matthew 24:38"> And Noah and his sons and his wife, and the wives of his sons with him, went into the ark because of the waters of the flood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Of clean animals, and of animals which are not clean, and of the birds, and everything that creeps upon the ground,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 7:16; Genesis 2:19; Jeremiah 8:7"> ⸤two of each⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “two, two”">i</a></sup>went to Noah, into the ark, male and female, as God had commanded Noah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 6:17; Genesis 7:4; Luke 17:27"> And it happened that after seven days the waters of the flood came over the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 8:2; Malachi 3:10; Proverbs 8:28–29"> In the six hundredth year of the life of Noah, in the second month, on the seventeenth day of the month—on that day all the springs of the great deep were split open, and the windows of heaven were opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; Matthew 4:2; Genesis 7:17"> And the rain came upon the earth forty days and forty nights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 6:18; Genesis 7:1; Genesis 6:10"> On this same day, Noah, Shem, Ham, and Japheth, the sons of Noah, and the wife of Noah and the three wives of his sons with them, went into the ark,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 7:8–9; Genesis 7:2–3"> they and all the living creatures according to their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “its”">j</a></sup>kind, and all the domesticated animals according to their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “its”">k</a></sup>kind, and all the creatures that creep upon the earth according to their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “its”">l</a></sup>kind, all the birds according to their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “its”">m</a></sup>kind, every winged creature.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 6:19–20; Isaiah 11:6"> And they came to Noah to the ark, ⸤two of each⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “two, two”">n</a></sup>from every living thing in which was the breath of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 25:10; Luke 13:25; Psalm 91:1–10"> And those that came, male and female, of every living thing, came as God had commanded him. And Yahweh shut the door behind him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; Genesis 7:12"> And the flood came forty days and forty nights upon the earth. And the waters increased, and lifted the ark, and it rose up from the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 14:28; Psalm 69:15; Job 22:16"> And the waters prevailed and increased greatly upon the earth. And the ark went upon the surface<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Hebrew “face”">o</a></sup>of the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalm 104:6–9; Psalm 46:2–3; 2 Peter 3:6"> And the waters prevailed ⸤overwhelmingly⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “very very”">p</a></sup>upon the earth, and they covered all the high mountains which were under the entire heaven.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “the sky”">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:23; Psalm 104:6"> ⸤The waters swelled fifteen cubits above the mountains, covering them⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “Fifteen cubits above the waters swelled, and they covered the mountains”">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 6:13; Genesis 7:4; Genesis 6:17"> And every living thing that moved on the earth perished—the birds, and the domesticated animals, and the wild animals, and everything that swarmed on the earth, and all humankind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 2:7; Genesis 6:17"> Everything in whose nostrils was ⸤the breath of life⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “a breath of spirit of life”">s</a></sup>among all that was on dry land, died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Peter 2:5; 1 Peter 3:20; Hebrews 11:7"> And he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="In context, God">t</a></sup>blotted out every living thing upon the surface of the ground, from humankind, to animals, to creeping things, and to the birds of heaven;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “the sky”">u</a></sup>they were blotted out from the earth. Only Noah and those who were with him in the ark remained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 8:3–4"> And the waters prevailed over the earth one hundred and fifty days.</span>
