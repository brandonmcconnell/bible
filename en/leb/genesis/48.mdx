## Jacob Blesses Ephraim and Manasseh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 41:50–52; Genesis 46:20; Psalm 128:6"> And it happened that after these things, it was said to Joseph, “Behold, your father is ill.” And he took his two sons with him, Ephraim and Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 6:10; 1 Samuel 23:16; Deuteronomy 3:28"> And it was told to Jacob, “Behold, your son Joseph has come to you.” Then Israel strengthened himself and he sat up in the bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 35:9–12; Exodus 6:3; Genesis 28:3"> Then Jacob said to Joseph, “El-Shaddai<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Often translated “God Almighty”">a</a></sup>appeared to me in Luz, in the land of Canaan, and blessed me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Genesis 35:11; Genesis 26:4"> and said to me, ‘Behold, I will make you fruitful and make you numerous, and will make you a company of nations. And I will give this land to your offspring after you as an everlasting possession.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 41:50–52; Joshua 14:4; Genesis 46:20"> And now, your two sons who were born to you in the land of Egypt before my coming to you in Egypt, are mine. Ephraim and Manasseh shall be mine as Reuben and Simeon are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 14:4"> And your children whom you father after them shall be yours. By the name of their brothers they shall be called, with respect to their inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 35:16–19; Matthew 2:18; Genesis 35:9"> As for me, when I came to Paddan-Aram Rachel died ⸤to my sorrow⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “to me”">b</a></sup>in the land of Canaan on the way when there was still some distance to go to Ephrath. And I buried her there on the way to Ephrath (that is, Bethlehem).”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> When Israel saw the sons of Joseph he said, “Who are these?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 27:4; Genesis 33:5; Hebrews 11:21"> Then Joseph said to his father, “They are my sons whom God has given me here.” And he said, “Please bring them to me that I may bless them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 27:1; Genesis 27:27; 1 Samuel 3:2"> Now the eyes of Israel were dim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “heavy” (i.e., his eyelids were heavy; his eyes were closed)">c</a></sup>on account of old age; he was not able to see. So he brought them near to him, and he kissed them and embraced them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 3:20; Genesis 45:26; Genesis 42:36"> And Israel said to Joseph, “I did not expect to see your face and behold, God has also shown me your offspring.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 42:6; Leviticus 19:32; Genesis 19:1"> Then Joseph removed them from his knees and bowed down with his face to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And Joseph took the two of them, Ephraim at his right to the left of Israel, and Manasseh at his left to the right of Israel. And he brought them near to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 41:51; Psalm 118:16; Matthew 19:15"> And Israel stretched out his right hand and put it on the head of Ephraim (now he was the younger), and his left hand on the head of Manasseh, crossing his hands, for Manasseh was the firstborn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 17:1; Colossians 2:6; Isaiah 33:16"> And he blessed Joseph and said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The God before whom my fathers, Abraham and Isaac, walked,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">The God who shepherded me ⸤all my life⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “since my duration”">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">unto this day,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 34:22; Genesis 49:22; Genesis 48:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">The angel who redeemed me from all evil,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">may he bless the boys.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And through them let my name be perpetuated,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “called”">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the name of my fathers, Abraham and Isaac.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And let them multiply into many in the midst of the earth.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 48:14; Romans 9:7–8; 1 Chronicles 21:7"> When Joseph saw that his father put his right hand on the head of Ephraim, he was displeased. And he took hold of his father’s hand to remove it from the head of Ephraim over to the head of Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 19:18; Genesis 29:26; Genesis 43:33"> And Joseph said to his father, “Not so, my father; because this one is the firstborn. Put your right hand upon his head.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:17; Numbers 1:33–35; Genesis 25:28"> But his father refused and said, “I know, my son; I know. He also shall become a people, and he also shall be great, but his younger brother shall be greater than him, and his offspring shall become a multitude of nations.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 24:60; Ruth 4:11–12; Genesis 28:3"> So he blessed them that day, saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Through you Israel shall pronounce blessing, saying,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘May God make you like Ephraim and like Manasseh.’ ”</span><br />So he put Ephraim before Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 50:24; Genesis 46:4; Genesis 28:15"> And Israel said to Joseph, “Behold, I am about to die, but God will be with you and will bring you back to the land of your ancestors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “fathers”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 4:5; Joshua 24:32; 1 Chronicles 5:2"> And I have given to you one slope of land rather than your brothers, which I took from the hand of the Amorites by my sword and with my bow.”</span>
</div>
