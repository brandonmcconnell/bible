## Abram Meets Melchizedek

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:18; Proverbs 19:4; Hebrews 7:1"> After his return from defeating Kedorlaomer and the kings who were with him, the king of Sodom went out to meet him at the Valley of Shaveh (that is, the Valley of the King).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 6:20–3; Psalm 110:4; Hebrews 5:6"> And Melchizedek, the king of Salem, brought out bread and wine. (He was the priest of God Most High).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 7:6–7; Genesis 14:22; Psalm 24:1"> And he blessed him and said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Blessed be Abram by God Most High,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Maker of heaven and earth.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And the king of Sodom said to Abram, “Give me the people, but the possessions take for yourself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 12:7; Daniel 4:34; Deuteronomy 32:40"> And Abram said to the king of Sodom, “I have raised my hand to Yahweh, God Most High, Maker of heaven and earth,</span>
