## The Birth of Isaac

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 12:6; Genesis 18:10; Genesis 18:14"> And Yahweh visited Sarah as he had said. And Yahweh did to Sarah as he had promised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 4:22; Hebrews 11:11; Genesis 18:10"> And she conceived, and Sarah bore to Abraham a son in his old age at the appointed time that God had told him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 17:19; Romans 9:7; Genesis 21:6"> And Abraham called the name of his son who was born to him, whom Sarah bore to him, Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 7:8; Deuteronomy 12:32; John 7:22–23"> And Abraham circumcised Isaac his son when he was eight days old, as God had commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 17:17; Romans 4:19; Genesis 17:1"> And Abraham was one hundred years old when Isaac his son was born to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 126:2; Isaiah 54:1; Genesis 17:17"> And Sarah said, “God has made laughter for me; all who hear will laugh for me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 18:11–12; Isaiah 66:8; 2 Thessalonians 1:10"> And she said, “Who would announce to Abraham that Sarah would nurse children? Yet I have borne a son to Abraham in his old age.”</span>

## Hagar and Ishmael

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:22; Hosea 1:8; Genesis 40:20"> And the child grew and was weaned. And Abraham made a great feast on the day Isaac was weaned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 4:29; Genesis 16:15; Galatians 4:22"> And Sarah saw the son of Hagar the Egyptian, whom she had borne Abraham, mocking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 8:35; 1 John 2:19; Galatians 4:22–31"> Then she said to Abraham, “Drive out this slave woman and her son, for the son of this slave woman will not be heir with my son, with Isaac.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 12:11; Genesis 17:18; Matthew 10:37"> And the matter ⸤displeased Abraham very much⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “was very bad in the eyes of Abraham”">a</a></sup>on account of his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 11:18; Romans 9:7–8; Genesis 17:19"> Then God said to Abraham, “⸤Do not be displeased⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “Do not let it be bad”">b</a></sup>on account of the boy and on account of the slave woman. ⸤Listen to everything that Sarah said to you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “Everything Sarah said to you, listen with respect to her voice”">c</a></sup>for through Isaac your offspring will be named.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 16:10; Genesis 17:20; Genesis 21:18"> And I will also make the son of the slave woman into a nation, for he is your offspring.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:10; Psalm 119:60; Genesis 16:7"> Then Abraham rose up early in the morning and took bread and a skin of water and gave it to Hagar, putting it on her shoulder. And he sent her away with the child, and she went, wandering about in the wilderness, in Beersheba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 3:9; Psalm 63:1; Jeremiah 14:3"> And when the water was finished from the skin, she put the child under one of the bushes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 49:15; Genesis 29:11; 1 Samuel 24:16"> And she went and ⸤she sat a good distance away⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “she sat with respect to her, opposite, far away, as the shooting of a bow”">d</a></sup>for she said, “Let me not see the child’s death.” So she sat away from him and lifted up her voice and wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 16:11; Psalm 50:15; Exodus 3:7"> And God heard the cry<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “voice”">e</a></sup>of the boy and the angel of God called to Hagar from the heavens and said to her, “⸤What is the matter⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “What to you”">f</a></sup>Hagar? Do not be afraid, for God has heard the cry<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “voice”">g</a></sup>of the boy ⸤from where he is⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “in which he is there”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 21:13; Genesis 16:10; Genesis 17:20"> Get up, take up the boy and take him with your hand, for I will make him a great nation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 22:31; 2 Kings 6:17–20; Isaiah 35:5–6"> And God opened her eyes, and she saw a well of water. And she went and filled the skin with water and gave a drink to the boy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 28:15; Genesis 39:21; Genesis 16:12"> And God was with the boy, and he grew and lived in the wilderness. And he became ⸤an expert with a bow⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “an archer with the bow”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 24:3–4; Numbers 12:16"> And he lived in the wilderness of Paran. And his mother took a wife for him from the land of Egypt.</span>

## The Covenant Between Abraham and Abimelech

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 3:7; Genesis 26:26; Genesis 26:28"> And it happened that at that time, Abimelech and Phicol, the commander of his army, said to Abraham, “God is with you, in all that you do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 2:12; Genesis 20:14; Hebrews 6:16"> So now, swear to me here by God that you will not deal with me falsely, or with my descendants, or my posterity. According to the kindness that I have done to you, you shall pledge to do with me and with the land where you have dwelled as an alien.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 6:16; Genesis 14:13; Romans 12:18"> And Abraham said, “I swear.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 26:15–22; Genesis 29:8; Proverbs 25:9"> Then Abraham complained to Abimelech on account of the well of water that servants of Abimelech had seized.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 13:7; 2 Kings 5:20–24"> And Abimelech said, “I do not know who did this thing, neither did you tell me, nor have I heard of it except for today.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 14:22–23; Isaiah 32:8; Romans 1:31"> And Abraham took sheep and cattle and gave them to Abimelech. And the two of them ⸤made⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “cut”">j</a></sup>a covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Then Abraham set off seven ewe-lambs of the flock by themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:14; Genesis 33:8; Exodus 12:26"> And Abimelech said to Abraham, “What is the meaning of these seven ewe-lambs that you have set off by themselves?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 31:52; Joshua 24:27; Joshua 22:27–28"> And he said, “You shall take the seven ewe-lambs from my hand ⸤as proof on my behalf⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “for the sake that it shall be a witness for me”">k</a></sup>that I dug this well.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 26:33; Genesis 21:14; 1 Kings 4:25"> Therefore that place is called Beersheba, because there the two of them swore an oath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 13:17; Genesis 21:27; Judges 13:1"> And they ⸤made⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “cut”">l</a></sup>a covenant at Beersheba. And Abimelech, and Phicol his army commander stood and returned to the land of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:17; Isaiah 40:28; Psalm 90:2"> And he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="That is, Abraham">m</a></sup>planted a tamarisk tree in Beersheba, and there he called on the name of Yahweh, ⸤the everlasting God⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “El-Olam”">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:15; 1 Peter 2:11; Hebrews 11:9"> And Abraham dwelled as an alien in the land of the Philistines many days.</span>
</div>
