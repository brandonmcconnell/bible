## The Rape of Dinah and the Massacre at Shechem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 30:21; Jeremiah 2:36; Genesis 30:13"> Now Dinah the daughter of Leah, whom she had borne to Jacob, went out to see the daughters of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 5:28; Job 31:1; Job 31:9"> And Shechem, the son of Hamor the Hivite, the prince of the land, saw her. And he took her and lay with her and raped her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 2:14; Isaiah 40:2; 2 Chronicles 30:22"> And his soul clung to Dinah, the daughter of Jacob, and he loved the girl and spoke ⸤tenderly⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to the heart”">a</a></sup>to the girl.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 14:2; 2 Samuel 13:13; Genesis 21:21"> So Shechem said to Hamor his father, saying, “Get this girl for me as a wife.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:22; Leviticus 10:3; 1 Samuel 10:27"> And Jacob heard that Dinah his daughter had been defiled, but his sons were with his flocks in the field. And Jacob kept silent until they came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And Hamor, father of Shechem, went out to Jacob to speak with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 7:15; Judges 20:6; Deuteronomy 22:21"> And the sons of Jacob came in from the field when they heard it. And the men were distressed and very angry because he had done a disgraceful thing in Israel by having sexual relations with the daughter of Jacob—⸤something that⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “and thus”">b</a></sup>should not be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 11:2; Psalm 119:20; Psalm 84:2"> And Hamor spoke with them saying, “Shechem my son ⸤is in love with⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “his soul longs for”">c</a></sup>your daughter. Please give her to him for a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:3; Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 19:14"> Make marriages with us. Give us your daughters and take our daughters for yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 20:15; Genesis 47:27; Genesis 42:34"> You shall dwell with us and the land shall be before you; settle and trade in it, and acquire property in it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 33:15; Genesis 18:3"> Then Shechem said to her father and to her brothers, “Let me find favor in your eyes, and whatever you say to me I will do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 22:16–17; Deuteronomy 22:28–29; 1 Samuel 18:25–27"> ⸤Make the bride price and gift as high as you like⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “Increase upon me very bride price and gift”">d</a></sup>I will give what you say to me. But give me the girl as a wife.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 25:27–34; Isaiah 59:13; Job 13:4"> Then the sons of Jacob answered Shechem and his father Hamor speaking deceitfully, because he had defiled Dinah their sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 23:1–39; 2 Samuel 15:7; Genesis 17:11"> And they said to them, “We cannot do this thing, to give our sister to a man who is uncircumcised, for that is a disgrace for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 4:12"> Only on this condition will we give consent to you; if you will become like us—every male among you to be circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Then we will give our daughters to you, and we will take for ourselves your daughters, and we will live with you and become one family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> But if you will not listen to us, to be circumcised, then we will take our daughters and we will go.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And their words were good in the eyes of Hamor and in the eyes of Shechem, the son of Hamor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 17:12; Acts 13:50; 1 Samuel 22:14"> And the young man did not delay to do the thing, for he wanted the daughter of Jacob. Now he was the most honored of his father’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ruth 4:1; Proverbs 31:23; Amos 5:12"> Then Hamor and his son Shechem came to the gate of their city, and they spoke to the men of their city, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> “These men are at peace with us. Let them dwell in the land and let them trade in it. Now, behold, the land is ⸤broad enough for them⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “broad on sides before them”">e</a></sup>Let us take their daughters as wives, and let us give our daughters to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Only on this condition will they give consent to us, to live with us and to become one family—when every male among us is circumcised as they are circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 1:12–13; John 6:26–27; John 2:16"> Will not their livestock and their property and all their animals be ours? Only let us give consent to them so they will live among us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 23:10; 1 Corinthians 7:19; Isaiah 1:10–16"> And all those who went out of the gate of his city listened to Hamor and Shechem. Every male was circumcised, all those who went out of the gate of his city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 49:5–7; Genesis 29:33–34; Numbers 31:7"> And it happened that on the third day, while they were in pain, two of the sons of Jacob, Simeon and Levi, the brothers of Dinah, each took his sword and came against the unsuspecting city and killed all the males.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:26; Deuteronomy 32:42; Isaiah 31:8"> They killed Hamor and his son Shechem with the edge of the sword, and they took Dinah from the house of Shechem and went out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 7:13; Joshua 7:21; Exodus 2:14"> The other sons of Jacob came upon the slain and plundered the city, because they had defiled their sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 1:15–16; Deuteronomy 8:17–18; Job 20:5"> They took their flocks and their cattle and their donkeys, and whatever was in the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> They captured and plundered all that was in the houses—all their wealth, their little ones, and their women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalm 105:12; Joshua 7:25; Exodus 5:21"> Then Jacob said to Simeon and Levi, “You have brought trouble on me, making me stink among the inhabitants of the land, among the Canaanites and the Perizzites! I am few in number! If they gather against me and attack me, I will be destroyed—I and my household!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 6:34; Genesis 34:13; Genesis 49:7"> But they said, “Shall he treat our sister like a prostitute?”</span>
</div>
</div>
