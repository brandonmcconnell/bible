## Joseph Interprets Dreams in Prison

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 40:13; Genesis 39:20–23; Esther 6:1"> And it happened that after these things the cupbearer of the king of Egypt and his baker did wrong against their lord, against the king of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 16:14; Proverbs 19:12; Psalm 76:10"> And Pharaoh was angry with his two officials, with the chief cupbearer and chief baker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 39:20; Genesis 39:23"> And he put them in custody in the house of the chief of the guard, into the prison where Joseph was confined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 39:1; Psalm 37:5; Genesis 37:36"> And the chief of the guard appointed Joseph to be with them, and he attended them. And they were in custody ⸤many days⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “days”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 41:11; Genesis 40:8; Genesis 37:5–10"> And the two of them, the cupbearer and the baker of the king of Egypt, who were confined in the prison, dreamed a dream, each his own dream, with its own interpretation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 41:8; Daniel 2:1–3; Genesis 40:8"> When Joseph came to them in the morning he looked at them, and behold, they were troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:2; 1 Samuel 1:8; Luke 24:17"> And he asked the court officials of Pharaoh that were with him in the custody of his master’s house, “Why are your faces sad today?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 2:47; Genesis 41:15–16; Daniel 2:28"> And they said to him, “We each dreamed a dream, but there is no one to interpret it.” And Joseph said to them, “Do not interpretations belong to God? Please tell them to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 7:13–15; Genesis 37:5–10; Daniel 4:8"> Then the chief cupbearer told his dream to Joseph, and he said to him, “In my dream, now behold, there was a vine before me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> and on the vine were three branches. And as it budded, its blossoms came up, and its clusters of grapes grew ripe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:11–1; 1 Kings 10:5; 2 Chronicles 9:4"> And the cup of Pharaoh was in my hand, and I took the grapes and squeezed them into the cup of Pharaoh. Then I placed the cup into the hand of Pharaoh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 41:12; Judges 7:14; Genesis 41:25–26"> Then Joseph said to him, “This is its interpretation: The three branches, they are three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 3:3; Jeremiah 52:31; 2 Kings 25:27"> In three days Pharaoh will lift up your head and will restore you to your office. And you shall put the cup of Pharaoh into his hand as was formerly the custom, when you were his cupbearer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 2:12; Luke 23:42; 1 Samuel 20:14–15"> But remember me when it goes well with you, and please may you show kindness with respect to me, and mention me to Pharaoh, and bring me out of this house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:17–18; Genesis 39:20; 1 Timothy 1:10"> For I was surely kidnapped from the land of the Hebrews, and here also I have done nothing that they should put me in this pit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 40:1–2"> And when the chief baker saw that the interpretation was good he said to Joseph, “I also dreamed. In my dream, now behold, there were three baskets of bread upon my head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 49:20; 1 Chronicles 12:20"> And in the upper basket were all sorts of baked foods for Pharaoh, but the birds were eating them out of the basket upon my head.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 40:12; Genesis 41:26; 1 Corinthians 11:24"> Then Joseph answered and said, “This is its interpretation: The three baskets, they are three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 40:22; Acts 20:27; 2 Samuel 21:6"> In three days Pharaoh will lift your head from you and hang you on a pole,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “tree”">b</a></sup>and the birds will eat your flesh from you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 40:19; Genesis 40:13; Matthew 14:6"> And it happened that on the third day, which was Pharaoh’s birthday, he made a feast for all his servants. And he lifted up the head of the chief cupbearer and the head of the chief baker in the midst of his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 40:13; Nehemiah 2:1"> And he restored the chief cupbearer to his cupbearing position. And he placed the cup in the hand of Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 40:19; Genesis 41:16; Daniel 2:30"> But the chief baker he hanged as Joseph had interpreted to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalm 31:12; Job 19:14; Psalm 105:19"> But the chief cupbearer did not remember Joseph, but forgot him.</span>
