## God Tests Abraham

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 11:17; James 2:21; Deuteronomy 8:2"> And it happened that after these things, God tested Abraham. And he said to him, “Abraham!” And he said, “Here I am.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 3:16; Romans 8:32; Hebrews 11:17"> And he said, “Take your son, your only child, Isaac, whom you love, and go to the land of Moriah, and offer him there as a burnt offering on one of the mountains where<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “that”">a</a></sup>I will tell you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 119:60; Matthew 10:37; Luke 14:26"> And Abraham rose up early in the morning and saddled his donkey. And he took two of his servants with him, and Isaac his son. And he chopped wood for a burnt offering. And he got up and went to the place which God had told him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 13:32; Matthew 17:23; Hosea 6:2"> On the third day Abraham lifted up his eyes, and he saw the place at a distance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:19; Hebrews 12:1"> And Abraham said to his servants, “You stay here with the donkey, and I and the boy will go up there. We will worship, then we will return to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 19:17; 1 Peter 2:24; Isaiah 53:6"> And Abraham took the wood of the burnt offering and placed it on Isaac his son. And he took the fire in his hand and the knife, and the two of them went together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:15; Matthew 26:39; Genesis 8:20"> And Isaac said to Abraham his father, “My father!” And he said, “Here I am, my son.” And he said, “Here is the fire and the wood, but where is the lamb for a burnt offering?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:36; John 1:29; Revelation 5:12"> And Abraham said, “⸤God will provide⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “God will see to it” or “God will see for himself”">b</a></sup>the lamb for a burnt offering, my son.” And the two of them went together.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 2:21; Hebrews 11:17–19; Philippians 2:7–8"> And they came to the place that God had told him. And Abraham built an altar there and arranged the wood. Then he bound Isaac his son and placed him on the altar atop the wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 2:21–23; Hebrews 11:17–19; Isaiah 53:6–12"> And Abraham stretched out his hand and took the knife to slaughter his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 21:17; Exodus 3:4; Genesis 16:9–10"> And the angel of Yahweh called to him from heaven and said, “Abraham! Abraham!” And he said, “Here I am.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 2:21–22; James 2:18; 1 Samuel 15:22"> And he said, “Do not stretch out your hand against the boy; do not do anything to him. For now I know that you are ⸤one who fears⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “a fearer”">c</a></sup>God, since you have not withheld your son, your only child, from me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; Psalm 40:6–8; 2 Corinthians 1:9–10"> And Abraham lifted up his eyes and looked. And behold, a ram was caught in the thicket by his horns. And Abraham went and took the ram, and offered it as a burnt offering in place of his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 22:13; Exodus 17:15; Genesis 22:8"> And Abraham called the name of that place “Yahweh ⸤will provide⸥,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “will see”">d</a></sup>⸤for which reason⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “which”">e</a></sup>it is said today, “on the mountain of Yahweh ⸤it shall be provided⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “it/he shall be seen”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the angel of Yahweh called to Abraham a second time from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 6:13–14; Luke 1:73; Psalm 105:9"> And he said, “I swear by myself, declares Yahweh, that because you have done this thing and have not withheld your son, your only child,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 15:5; Genesis 24:60; Jeremiah 33:22"> that I will certainly bless you and greatly multiply your offspring as the stars of heaven, and as the sand that is by the shore of the sea. And your offspring will take possession of the gate of his enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 3:25; Genesis 12:3; Galatians 3:16"> All the nations of the earth will be blessed through your offspring, because you have listened to my voice.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 21:31; Judges 20:1; Joshua 15:28"> And Abraham returned to his servants, and they got up and went together to Beersheba. And Abraham lived in Beersheba.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 11:29; Genesis 24:24; Proverbs 25:25"> And it happened that after these things, it was told to Abraham, “Look, Milcah has also borne children to your brother Nahor:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 1:1; Job 32:2; Psalm 60:1–12"> Uz his firstborn and Buz his brother, and Kemuel the father of Aram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> and Kesed, Hazo, Pildash, Jidlaph, and Bethuel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 24:15; Genesis 28:5; Genesis 24:51"> (Now, Bethuel fathered Rebekah). These eight Milcah bore to Nahor, the brother of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 16:3; Genesis 25:6; Proverbs 15:25"> And his concubine, whose name was Reumah, also bore Tebah, Gaham, Tahash, and Maacah.</span>
</div>
</div>
