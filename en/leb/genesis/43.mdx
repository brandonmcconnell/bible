## Joseph’s Brothers Return to Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 12:10; Acts 7:11–13; Lamentations 5:10"> Now the famine in the land was severe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:6–8; Proverbs 16:18; Proverbs 15:16"> And it happened that as they finished eating the grain which they had brought from Egypt their father said to them, “Return and buy a little food for us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 44:23; Genesis 43:5; 2 Samuel 3:13"> Then Judah said to him, “The man solemnly admonished us, saying, ‘You shall not see my face unless your brother is with you.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> ⸤If you will send⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “if there is a sending”">a</a></sup>our brother with us, we will go down and buy food for you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Genesis 44:26; Genesis 42:38"> but ⸤if you will not send⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “if there is no sending”">b</a></sup>him, we will not go down, for the man said to us, ‘You shall not see my face unless your brother is with you.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Then Israel said, “Why did you bring trouble to me by telling the man you still had a brother?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 43:3; Genesis 42:13; Genesis 43:27"> And they said, “The man asked explicitly about us and about our family, saying, ‘Is your father still alive? Do you have a brother?’ And we answered him according to these words. How could we know that he would say, ‘Bring down your brother’?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 42:2; Deuteronomy 33:6; Genesis 45:19"> Then Judah said to his father Israel, “Send the boy with me, and let us arise and go, so that we will live and not die—you, we, and our children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 42:37; Philemon 1:18–19; Hebrews 7:22"> I myself will be surety for him. You may seek him from my hand. If I do not bring him back to you and present him before you, then I will stand guilty before you forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 19:16"> Surely if we had not hesitated by this time we would have returned twice.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 37:25; Jeremiah 8:22; Ezekiel 27:17"> Then their father Israel said to them, “If it must be so then do this. Take some of the best products of the land in your bags and take them down to the man as a gift—a little balm and honey, aromatic gum and myrrh, and pistachios and almonds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 42:25; Genesis 42:35; 1 Thessalonians 4:6"> And take double the money in your hands. Take back the money that was returned in the mouth of your sacks. Perhaps it was a mistake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 42:38"> And take your brother. Now arise and return to the man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esther 4:16; Genesis 17:1; Acts 21:14"> And may El-Shaddai<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Often translated “God Almighty”">c</a></sup>grant you compassion before the man that he may release your other brother to you and Benjamin. As for me, if I am bereaved, I am bereaved.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> So the men took this gift, and they took double money in their hands, and Benjamin, and they rose up and went down to Egypt and stood before Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 44:1; Genesis 43:19; Genesis 24:2–10"> When Joseph saw Benjamin with them he said to the one who was over his household, “Bring the men into the house and slaughter and prepare an animal, for the men shall eat with me at noon.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the man did as Joseph had said, and the man brought the men into the house of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 42:35; Romans 7:8; Genesis 42:21"> And the men were afraid when they were brought into the house of Joseph. And they said “We were brought here on account of the money that was returned to our sacks the first time, that he might attack us and fall upon us to take us as slaves with our donkeys.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> So they approached the man who was over Joseph’s house and spoke to him at the doorway of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 42:3; Genesis 42:10; Genesis 42:27"> And they said, “Please, my lord, we surely came down once before to buy food,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 43:12; Genesis 42:27–35; Romans 13:8"> but when we came to the place of lodging and we opened our sacks, then behold, each one’s money was in the mouth of his sack—our money in its full weight—so we have returned with it in our hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Now, other money we have brought down in our hand to buy food. We do not know who put our money in our sacks.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 20:26; Genesis 42:24; John 14:27"> And he said, “Peace to you; do not be afraid. Your God and the God of your father must have given you a treasure in your sacks; your money came to me.” And he brought Simeon out to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 18:4; Genesis 24:32; Luke 7:44"> Then the man brought the men into Joseph’s house and he gave them water and washed their feet, and gave fodder to their donkeys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 43:11; Genesis 43:16"> Then they laid out<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “prepared”">d</a></sup>the gift until Joseph came at noon, for they had heard that they were to eat food there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Philippians 2:10–11; Genesis 27:29; Genesis 42:6"> And when Joseph came into the house they brought the gift that was in their hand into the house to him, and they bowed down before him to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 42:13; 1 Samuel 25:5; 1 Samuel 17:22"> And ⸤he greeted them⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “he asked peace for them”">e</a></sup>and said, “Is your father well, the old man of whom you spoke? Is he still alive?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 37:7; Genesis 43:26; 2 Samuel 14:4"> And they said, “Your servant our father is well; he is still alive.” And they knelt and bowed down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 42:13; Numbers 6:25; Genesis 30:22–24"> Then he lifted up his eyes and saw Benjamin his brother, the son of his mother, and said, “Is this your youngest brother of whom you told me?” And he continued,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “said”">f</a></sup>“God be gracious to you, my son.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 42:24; Jeremiah 31:20; 1 Kings 3:26"> Then Joseph ⸤hurried away⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “made haste”">g</a></sup>⸤being overcome with emotion⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “his compassion boiled up”">h</a></sup>toward his brother, and sought for a place to cry. Then he went into a room and wept there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 45:1; 1 Peter 3:10; Isaiah 42:14"> Then he washed his face and went out, now controlling himself, and said, “Serve the food.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 46:34; Exodus 8:26; Genesis 43:16"> And they served him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="That is, Joseph">i</a></sup>by himself, and them by themselves, and the Egyptians who were eating with him by themselves, for Egyptians ⸤could not dine⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “were not able to eat food”">j</a></sup>with Hebrews, because that was a detestable thing to Egyptians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And they were seated before him from the firstborn according to his birthright to the youngest according to his youth. And the men ⸤looked at one another⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “each to his companion”">k</a></sup>amazed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 45:22; 2 Samuel 11:8; 1 Samuel 1:5"> And portions were served to them from ⸤his table⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “his presence”">l</a></sup>and the portion of Benjamin was five times greater than the portion of any of them. And they drank and became drunk with him.</span>
</div>
