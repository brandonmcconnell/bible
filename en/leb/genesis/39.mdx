## Joseph in Potiphar’s House

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 37:28; Psalm 105:17; Genesis 37:36"> Now Joseph had been brought down to Egypt, and Potiphar, a court official of Pharaoh, commander of the guard, an Egyptian, bought him from the hand of the Ishmaelites who had brought him down there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:14; Psalm 91:15; Jeremiah 15:20"> And Yahweh was with Joseph, and he became a successful man. And he was in the house of his master, the Egyptian.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 1:3; Genesis 30:27; Matthew 5:16"> And his master observed that Yahweh was with him, and everything that was in his hand to do Yahweh made successful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 39:21–22; Proverbs 27:18; Genesis 19:19"> And Joseph found favor in his eyes and he served him. Then he appointed him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, Joseph">a</a></sup>over his house and all that he owned he put into his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 30:27; 2 Samuel 6:11–12; Acts 27:24"> And it happened that from the time he appointed him over his house and over all that he had, Yahweh blessed the house of the Egyptian on account of Joseph. And the blessing of Yahweh was upon all that he had in the house and in the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:12; Luke 19:17; Proverbs 31:11"> And he left all that he had in the hand of Joseph, and ⸤he did not worry about anything⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “he did not know with him anything”">b</a></sup>except the food that he ate. Now Joseph was ⸤well built and handsome⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “beautiful of form and beautiful of appearance”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:11; Ezekiel 16:34; 2 Peter 2:14"> And it happened that after these things his master’s wife cast her eyes on Joseph, and she said, “Lie with me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 23:26–28; Proverbs 22:14; Proverbs 9:13–18"> But he refused and said to his master’s wife, “Look, my master ⸤does not worry about⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “does not know with me”">d</a></sup>what is in the house, and everything he owns he has put in my hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:13; Genesis 20:6; Genesis 42:18"> He has no greater authority in this house than me, and he has not withheld anything from me except you, since you are his wife. Now how could I do this great wickedness and sin against God?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Genesis 39:8; 1 Thessalonians 5:22"> And it happened that as she spoke to Joseph ⸤day after day⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “day, day”">e</a></sup>he did not heed her to lie beside her or to be with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 5:12; Ephesians 5:3; Malachi 3:5"> ⸤But one particular day⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “and it happened as this day”">f</a></sup>he came into the house to do his work and none of the men of the house were there in the house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:22; Proverbs 7:13–27; Ecclesiastes 7:26"> she seized him by his garment and said, “Lie with me!” And he left his garment in her hand and fled, and he went outside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And it happened that when she saw that he left his garment in her hand and fled outside,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 10:21; 1 Peter 2:20; Psalm 35:11"> she called to the men of her house and said to them, “Look! He<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="That is, Potiphar">g</a></sup>brought a Hebrew man to us to mock us! He came to me to lie with me, and I cried out with a loud voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And when he heard me, that I raised my voice and called out, he left his garment beside me and fled, and he went outside.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 37:32; Jeremiah 4:22; Jeremiah 9:3–5"> Then she put his garment beside her until his master came to his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 23:1; 1 Kings 18:17; Psalm 37:14"> Then she spoke to him according to these words, saying, “The Hebrew slave that you brought to us came to me to make fun of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And it happened that as I raised my voice and called out, he left his garment beside me and fled outside.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 4:5–6; Proverbs 18:17; Proverbs 6:34–35"> And when his master heard the words of his wife that she spoke to him, “⸤This is what your servant did to me⸥,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “according to these words your servant did to me”">h</a></sup>⸤he became very angry⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “his nostrils became hot”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 40:15; 2 Timothy 2:9; Psalm 105:18–19"> And Joseph’s master took him and put him into prison, the place that the king’s prisoners were confined. And he was there in prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 3:21; Isaiah 41:10; 1 Peter 3:17"> And Yahweh was with Joseph, and showed loyal love to him, and gave him favor in the eyes of the chief of the prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 40:3–4; Genesis 39:4; 1 Samuel 2:30"> And the chief of the prison put all the prisoners that were in the prison into the hand of Joseph. And everything that was done there, he was the one who did it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Genesis 39:2–3; Psalm 1:3"> The chief of the prison ⸤did not worry about⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “did not pay heed to”">j</a></sup>anything in his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="That is, Joseph’s">k</a></sup>hand, since Yahweh was with him. And whatever he did Yahweh made it successful.</span>
