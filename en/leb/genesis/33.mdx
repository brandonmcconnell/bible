## Jacob Meets Esau and Settles at Shechem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 32:6–7; Genesis 27:41–42; Genesis 32:16"> And Jacob lifted up his eyes and looked. And behold, Esau was coming and four hundred men were with him. And he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, Jacob">a</a></sup>divided the children among Leah and among Rachel, and among the two of his female servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 29:30; Genesis 30:22–24; Genesis 37:3"> And he put the female slaves and their children first, then Leah and her children next, then Rachel with Joseph last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 42:6; Luke 14:11; Genesis 18:2"> And he himself passed on before them and bowed down to the ground seven times until he came to his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 45:14–15; Psalm 34:4; Luke 15:20"> But Esau ran to meet him, and embraced him, and fell upon his neck and kissed him, and they wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 127:3; Isaiah 8:18; Genesis 48:9"> Then Esau lifted up his eyes and saw the women and the children and said, “Who are these with you?” And he said, “The children whom God has graciously given your servant.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Then the female servants drew near, they and their children, and they bowed down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Then Leah and her children drew near and bowed down, and afterward Joseph and Rachel drew near and they bowed down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 32:5; Genesis 39:5; Genesis 32:13–20"> And he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="That is, Esau">b</a></sup>said, “⸤What do you mean by⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “What to you?”">c</a></sup>all this company that I have met?” Then he said, “To find favor in the eyes of my lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 27:39; Philemon 1:7; Genesis 27:41"> Then Esau said, “⸤I have enough⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “There is to me abundance”">d</a></sup>my brother; ⸤keep what you have⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “let what is to you be to you”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 33:26; Revelation 22:4; Genesis 32:30"> And Jacob said, “No, please, if I have found favor in your eyes, you must take my gift from my hand, for then I have seen your face which is like seeing the face of God, and you have received me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:27; Philippians 4:18; Genesis 30:43"> Please take my gift which has been brought to you, for God has dealt graciously with me, and because ⸤I have enough⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “There is to me abundance”">f</a></sup>And he urged him, so he took it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Then he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="That is, Esau">g</a></sup>said, “Let us journey and go on, and I will go ahead of you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; Ezekiel 34:15–16; Ezekiel 34:23–25"> But he said to him, “My lord knows that the children are frail, and the flocks and the cattle which are nursing are a concern to me. Now if they drove them hard for a day all the flocks would die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 32:3; Isaiah 40:11; Romans 15:1"> Let my lord pass on before his servant and I will move along slowly at the pace<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “foot”">h</a></sup>of the livestock that are ahead of me, and at the pace<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “foot”">i</a></sup>of the children until I come to my lord in Seir.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ruth 2:13; Genesis 47:25; Genesis 34:11"> And Esau said, “Let me leave some of my people with you.” But he said, “⸤What need is there⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “What is this?”">j</a></sup>Let me find favor in the eyes of my lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> So Esau turned that day on his way to Seir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 60:6; Joshua 13:27; Judges 8:5"> But Jacob traveled on to Succoth, and he built for himself a house, and he made shelters for his livestock. Therefore he called the name of the place Succoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 9:1; Acts 7:16; Joshua 24:1"> And Jacob came safely to the city of Shechem which is in the land of Canaan, ⸤on his way⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “at his coming”">k</a></sup>from Paddan-Aram. And he camped before the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 7:16; John 4:5; Joshua 24:32"> And he bought a piece of land where he pitched his tent for one hundred pieces of money<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Hebrew kesitah">l</a></sup>from the hand of the sons of Hamor, father of Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 35:7; Genesis 21:33; Genesis 32:28"> And there he erected an altar and called it “El Elohe Israel.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="That is, “El the God of Israel”">m</a></sup></span>
</div>
