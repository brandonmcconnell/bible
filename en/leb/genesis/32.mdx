## Jacob Fears Esau

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 91:11; Psalm 34:7; Hebrews 1:4"> And Jacob went on his way, and angels of God met him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:8; Joshua 5:14; Joshua 21:38"> And when he saw them, Jacob said, “This is the camp of God!” And he called the name of that place Mahanaim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 25:30; Joshua 24:4; Deuteronomy 2:5"> Then Jacob sent messengers before him to Esau his brother, to the land of Seir, the territory of Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 3:6; Luke 14:11; Genesis 33:8"> And he instructed them, saying, “Thus you must say to my lord, to Esau, ‘Thus says your servant Jacob, I have dwelled as an alien with Laban, and I have remained there until now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 33:8; Genesis 33:15; Genesis 33:11"> And I have acquired cattle, male donkeys, flocks, and male and female slaves, and I have sent to tell my lord, to find favor in your eyes.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 33:1; Genesis 32:8; Genesis 32:11"> And the messengers returned to Jacob and said, “We came to your brother, to Esau, and he is coming to meet you, and four hundred men are with him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 35:3; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10; Exodus 14:10"> Then Jacob was very frightened and distressed. So he divided the people, flocks, cattle, and camels that were with him into two companies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 10:16; Genesis 33:1–3"> And he thought, “If Esau comes to one company and destroys it, the remaining company will be able to escape.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 31:42; Psalm 34:4–6; Genesis 31:3"> Then Jacob said, “O God of my father Abraham, and God of my father Isaac, O Yahweh, who said to me, ‘Return to your land and to your family, and I will deal well with you.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 18:35; Genesis 24:27; 2 Samuel 7:18"> ⸤I am not worthy⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “I am small”">a</a></sup>of all the loyal love and all the faithfulness that you have shown<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “done”">b</a></sup>your servant, for with only my staff I crossed this Jordan, and now I have become two camps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 59:1–2; Proverbs 18:19; Matthew 6:13"> Please rescue me from the hand of my brother, from the hand of Esau, for I fear him, lest he come and attack mother and children alike.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 28:13–15; Hebrews 6:17; Titus 1:2"> Now you yourself said, ‘I will surely deal well with you and make your offspring as the sand of the sea that cannot be counted for abundance.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 18:16; Genesis 43:11; Proverbs 19:6"> And he lodged there that night. Then he took ⸤from what he had with him⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “from that which was going from his hand”">c</a></sup>a gift for Esau his brother:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 1:3; Deuteronomy 8:18; Job 42:12"> two hundred female goats, twenty male goats, two hundred ewes, twenty rams,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> thirty milk camels with their young, forty cows, ten bulls, twenty female donkeys, and ten male donkeys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 33:8–9; Genesis 32:20; Proverbs 2:11"> And he put them under the hand of his servants, ⸤herd by herd⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “every herd by its own”">d</a></sup>and said to his servants, “Cross on ahead before me, and put some distance ⸤between herds⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “between a herd and between a herd”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 33:3"> And he instructed the foremost, saying, “When Esau my brother comes upon you and asks you, saying, ‘Whose are you and where are you going? To whom do these animals belong ahead of you?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 32:4–5"> Then you must say, ‘To your servant, to Jacob. It is a gift sent to my lord, to Esau. Now behold, he is also coming after us.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And he also instructed the second servant and the third, and everyone else who was behind the herds, saying, “You must speak to Esau according to this word when you find him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 42:8–9; Proverbs 21:14; Genesis 43:11"> And moreover, you shall say, ‘Look, your servant Jacob is behind us.’ ” For he thought, “⸤Let me appease him⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “let me cover his face”">f</a></sup>with the gift going before me, and afterward I will see his face. Perhaps he will ⸤show me favor⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “lift up my face”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> So the gift passed on before him, but he himself spent that night in the camp.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jacob Wrestles with God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:16; Deuteronomy 2:37; 1 Timothy 5:8"> That night he arose and took his two wives, his two female servants, and his eleven children and crossed the ford of the Jabbok.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And he took them and sent them across the stream. Then he sent across all his possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hosea 12:3–5; Ephesians 6:12; Luke 13:24"> And Jacob remained alone, and a man wrestled with him until the breaking of the dawn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:7–9; Hosea 12:3–4; Matthew 26:41"> And when he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="That is, the man">h</a></sup>saw that he could not prevail against him, he struck his hip socket, so that Jacob’s hip socket was sprained as he wrestled with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hosea 12:4; Psalm 115:12–13; 2 Corinthians 12:8–9"> Then he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="That is, the man">i</a></sup>said, “Let me go, for dawn is breaking.” But he answered, “I will not let you go unless you bless me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Then he said to him, “What is your name?” And he said, “Jacob.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 2:17; Genesis 35:10; Isaiah 62:2–4"> And he said, “Your name shall no longer be called Jacob, but Israel,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="“Israel” means “he struggles with God,” or “God struggles”">j</a></sup>for you have struggled with God and with men and have prevailed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6; Judges 13:16–18; Job 11:7"> Then Jacob asked and said, “Please tell me your name.” And he said, “Why do you ask this—for my name?” And he blessed him there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 24:10–11; Numbers 12:8; Isaiah 6:5"> Then Jacob called the name of the place Peniel which means “I have seen God face to face and my life was spared.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:7; Genesis 19:15; Judges 8:8"> Then the sun rose upon him as he passed Penuel, and he was limping because of his hip.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:5"> Therefore the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">k</a></sup>do not eat the sinew of the sciatic nerve that is upon the socket of the hip unto this day, because he struck the socket of the thigh of Jacob at the sinew of the sciatic nerve.</span>
</div>
</div>
