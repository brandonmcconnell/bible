## The Death and Burial of Jacob

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 7:4; Esther 8:11; 1 Kings 18:31"> All these are the twelve tribes of Israel, and this is what their father said to them when he blessed them, each according to their blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 47:30; Genesis 50:13; Genesis 15:15"> Then he instructed them and said to them, “I am about to be gathered to my people. Bury me among my ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “fathers”">j</a></sup>in the cave that is in the field of Ephron the Hittite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 23:20; Genesis 23:16–18; Genesis 23:8–9"> in the cave that is in the field of Machpelah that is before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “east of”">k</a></sup>Mamre in the land of Canaan, which Abraham bought with the field from Ephron the Hittite as a burial site.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 25:9; Genesis 35:29; Genesis 50:13"> There they buried Abraham and Sarah his wife. There they buried Isaac and Rebekah his wife. And there I buried Leah—</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 23:17–20"> the purchase of the field and the cave which was in it from the Hittites.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 49:29; Genesis 25:8; Genesis 35:29"> When Jacob finished instructing his sons he drew his feet up to the bed. Then he took his last breath and was gathered to his people.</span>
