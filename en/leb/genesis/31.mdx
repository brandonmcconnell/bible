## Jacob Flees from Laban

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:4; Psalm 49:16–17; Job 31:31"> Now he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, Jacob">a</a></sup>heard the words of the sons of Laban, saying, “Jacob has taken all that our father has,” and “From that which was our father’s he has gained all this wealth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Daniel 3:19; 1 Samuel 18:9–11; Genesis 4:5"> Then Jacob saw the face of Laban and, behold, ⸤it was not like it had been in the past⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “there was not with him like yesterday or the day before”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 28:15; Genesis 32:9; Hebrews 13:5"> And Yahweh said to Jacob, “Return to the land of your ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “fathers”">c</a></sup>and to your family, and I will be with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> So Jacob sent and called Rachel and Leah to the field, to his flocks,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 31:2–3; Genesis 31:53; Genesis 48:15"> and he said to them, “Look, I see the face of your father, that ⸤it is not like it has been toward me in the past⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “it is not to me like yesterday or the day before”">d</a></sup>But the God of my father is with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 30:29; Ephesians 6:5–8; Titus 2:9–10"> Now you yourselves know that I have served your father with all my strength,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 37:28; Genesis 31:29; Numbers 14:22"> and your father has cheated me and changed my wages ten times, but God has not allowed him to harm me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 30:32"> If thus he said, ‘Speckled shall be your wage,’ then all the flock bore speckled. And if he said, ‘Streaked shall be your wage,’ then all the flock bore streaked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 31:1; Genesis 31:16; Proverbs 13:22"> God has taken away your father’s livestock and given them to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:1; Genesis 30:39; Genesis 20:6"> Now it happened that at the time of the mating of the flock I lifted up my eyes and saw in a dream, and behold, the rams mounting the flock were streaked, speckled, and dappled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 58:9; Genesis 31:13; 1 Samuel 3:6"> Then the angel of God said to me in the dream, ‘Jacob,’ and I said, ‘Here I am.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 3:7; Deuteronomy 24:15; Genesis 31:42"> And he said, ‘Lift up your eyes and see—all the rams mounting the flock are streaked, speckled, and dappled, for I have seen all that Laban is doing to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 31:3; Genesis 32:9; Genesis 28:12–22"> I am the God of Bethel where you anointed a stone pillar, where you made a vow to me. Now get up, go out from this land and return to the land of your birth.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ruth 4:11; Genesis 2:24; Genesis 29:24"> Then Rachel and Leah answered and said to him, “Is there yet a portion for us, and an inheritance in the house of our father?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 29:15–20; Genesis 31:41; Genesis 29:27–30"> Are we not regarded as foreigners by him, because he has sold us and completely consumed our money?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 30:35–1; Genesis 31:9; Psalm 45:10"> For all the wealth that God has taken away from our father, it belongs to us and to our sons. So now, all that God has said to you, do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:17; Genesis 24:61; Genesis 24:10"> Then Jacob got up and put his children and his wives on the camels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 27:1–2; Genesis 27:41; Genesis 35:27–29"> And he drove all his livestock and his possessions that he had acquired, the livestock of his possession that he had acquired in Paddan-Aram, in order to go to Isaac his father, to the land of Canaan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:13; Genesis 31:30; Hosea 3:4"> Now Laban had gone to shear his sheep, and Rachel stole the idols<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew teraphim">e</a></sup>that belonged to her father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And Jacob ⸤tricked⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “stole the heart of”">f</a></sup>Laban the Aramean by not telling him that he intended to flee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 12:17; Genesis 37:25; Genesis 46:28"> Then he fled with all that he had, and arose and crossed the Euphrates<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “the river”">g</a></sup>and set his face toward the hill country of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 5:12–13; Genesis 30:36; Exodus 14:5–31"> And on the third day it was told to Laban that Jacob had fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 13:8; Genesis 24:27; Exodus 2:11"> Then he took his kinsmen with him and pursued after him, a seven-day journey, and he caught up with him in the hill country of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 24:50; Genesis 31:29; Genesis 20:3"> And God came to Laban the Aramean in a dream at night and said to him, “⸤Take care⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “watch to yourself”">h</a></sup>that you not speak with Jacob, whether good or evil.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 12:8; Genesis 33:18; Hebrews 11:9"> And Laban overtook Jacob. Now Jacob had pitched his tent in the hill country, and Laban and his kinsmen pitched their tents in the hill country of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:2; Genesis 12:18; Genesis 20:9–10"> Then Laban said to Jacob, “What have you done that you ⸤tricked me⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “stole my heart”">i</a></sup>and have carried off my daughters like captives of the sword?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 15:20; Proverbs 26:23–26; Genesis 31:3–5"> Why did you hide your intention to flee and ⸤trick me⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “steal my heart”">j</a></sup>and did not tell me so that I would have sent you away with joy and song and tambourine and lyre?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 31:55; Ruth 1:14; Acts 20:37"> And why did you not give me opportunity to kiss my grandsons<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “sons”">k</a></sup>and my daughters goodbye? Now you have behaved foolishly by doing this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 31:53; Genesis 31:42; Genesis 31:24"> ⸤It is in my power⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “there is power in my hand”">l</a></sup>to do harm to you, but the God of your father spoke to me last night saying, ‘⸤Take care⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “watch to yourself”">m</a></sup>from speaking with Jacob, whether good or evil.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:11; Genesis 31:19; Judges 18:24"> Now, you have surely gone because you desperately longed for the house of your father, but why did you steal my gods?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 29:25; Genesis 31:26–27; Genesis 20:11"> Then Jacob answered and said to Laban, “Because I was afraid, for I thought, ‘Lest you take your daughters from me by force.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:3–5; Genesis 31:30; Genesis 31:19"> But with whomever you find your gods, he shall not live. In the presence of your kinsmen now identify what is with me that is yours and take it.” Now Jacob did not know that Rachel had stolen them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 24:67; Genesis 24:28"> Then Laban went into Jacob’s tent and Leah’s tent and the tent of the two female servants and did not find his gods. And he came out of Leah’s tent and went into Rachel’s tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 31:19; Genesis 31:17"> Now Rachel had taken the idols and put them in the saddle bag of the camel and sat on them. And Jacob searched the whole tent thoroughly but did not find them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 19:32; Exodus 20:12; 1 Peter 2:18"> And she said to her father, “Let there not be anger in the eyes of my lord, for I am not able to rise before you, for the way of women is with me. And he searched carefully and did not find the idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ephesians 4:26; Genesis 49:7; Numbers 16:15"> Then Jacob became angry and quarreled with Laban. Jacob answered and said to Laban, “What is my offense? What is my sin that you pursued after me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; 1 Corinthians 6:4–5; 1 Samuel 12:3–4"> For you have searched all my possessions and what did you find among all the possessions of my household? Set it before my kinsmen and your kinsmen that they may decide between the two of us!</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 30:27; Deuteronomy 28:4; Ezekiel 34:2–4"> These twenty years I was with you; your ewes and your female goats did not miscarry, and the rams of your flocks I did not eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 22:31; Exodus 22:10–13; Leviticus 22:8"> I brought no mangled carcass to you—I bore its loss. From my hand you sought it, whether stolen by day or stolen by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 2:8; Exodus 2:19–22; 1 Peter 5:2–4"> There I was, during the day the heat consumed me, and the cold by night, and my sleep fled from my eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Genesis 31:7; 1 Corinthians 15:10; 2 Corinthians 11:26"> These twenty years I have been in your house. I served you fourteen years for your two daughters and six years for your flock, and you have changed my wages ten times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalm 31:7; Genesis 31:53; Isaiah 8:13"> If the God of my father, the God of Abraham and the Fear of Isaac had not been with me, indeed now you would have sent me away empty-handed. God saw my misery and the labor of my hands and rebuked you last night.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> Then Laban answered and said to Jacob, “The daughters are my daughters and the grandsons<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “sons”">n</a></sup>are my grandsons,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “sons”">o</a></sup>and the flocks are my flocks, and all that you see, it is mine. Now, what can I do for these my daughters today, or for their children whom they have borne?</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Genesis 31:52; Joshua 24:25–27; Joshua 22:27"> So now, come, let us ⸤make⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “cut”">p</a></sup>a covenant, you and I, and let it be a witness between me and you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Genesis 28:18–22"> And Jacob took a stone and set it up as a stone pillar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:5; Genesis 31:23; Genesis 31:54"> And Jacob said to his kinsmen, “Gather stones.” And they took stones and made a pile of stones, and they ate there by the pile of stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Hebrews 12:1"> And Laban called it Jegar Sahadutha,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Aramaic for “the heap of witness”">q</a></sup>but Jacob called it Galeed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Hebrew for “the heap of witness”">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Joshua 24:27; Joshua 13:8–9; Genesis 31:23"> Then Laban said, “This pile of stones is a witness between me and you today.” Therefore its name is called Galeed,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Hebrew for “the heap of witness”">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Judges 11:29; Judges 10:17; 1 Samuel 7:5–6"> and Mizpah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Hebrew for “watchpost”">t</a></sup>because he said, “Yahweh watch between me and you when ⸤we are out of sight of each other⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “each from his neighbor is concealed”">u</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:5; Jeremiah 29:23; Micah 1:2"> If you mistreat my daughters, and if you take wives besides my daughters, when there is no man with us, see—God is a witness between me and you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> And Laban said to Jacob, “See, this pile of stones, and see the pillar that I have set up between me and you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Genesis 31:48; Genesis 31:44–45"> This pile of stones is a witness, and the pillar is a witness, that I will not pass beyond this pile of stones to you, and that you will not pass beyond this pile of stones and this pillar to me intending harm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Genesis 31:42; Genesis 16:5; Joshua 24:2"> May the God of Abraham and the God of Nahor, the God of their father<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="That is, Terah">v</a></sup>judge between us.” Then Jacob swore by the Fear of his father Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Exodus 18:12; Genesis 37:25; Genesis 26:30"> And Jacob sacrificed a sacrifice on the hill, and he called his kinsmen to eat the meal.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or “food, bread”">w</a></sup>And they ate the meal<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Or “food, bread”">x</a></sup>and spent the night on the hill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Genesis 31:28; Deuteronomy 23:5; Genesis 18:33"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Genesis 31:55–32:32 in the English Bible is 32:1–33 in the Hebrew Bible">y</a></sup>And Laban arose early in the morning and kissed his grandsons<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “sons”">z</a></sup>and his daughters, and blessed them. Then Laban departed and returned to his homeland.</span>
