## Adam’s Descendants to Noah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ephesians 4:24; Colossians 3:10; Genesis 1:26–27"> This is the record of the generations<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “family records”">a</a></sup>of Adam. When<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “On the day”">b</a></sup>God created Adam,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “humankind”">c</a></sup>he made him in the likeness of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 19:4; Mark 10:6; Genesis 2:23"> Male and female he created them. And he blessed them. And he called their name “Humankind” when<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “on the day”">d</a></sup>they were created.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 4:25; Job 25:4; Psalm 51:5"> And when Adam had lived one hundred and thirty years, he fathered a child in his likeness, according to his image. And he called his name Seth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:1–3; Luke 3:36–38; Genesis 5:26"> And the days of Adam after he fathered Seth were eight hundred years. And he fathered sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 5:8; Genesis 5:11; Genesis 3:19"> And all the days of Adam which he lived were nine hundred and thirty years, and he died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 4:26"> When<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “On the day”">e</a></sup>Seth had lived one hundred and five years, he fathered Enosh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And after Seth had fathered Enosh he lived eight hundred and seven years, and fathered sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And all the days of Seth were nine hundred and twelve years, and he died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 3:37; 1 Chronicles 1:2"> When<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “On the day”">f</a></sup>Enosh lived ninety years, he fathered Kenan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And after Enosh fathered Kenan he lived eight hundred and fifteen years, and fathered sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And all the days of Enosh were nine hundred and five years, and he died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 3:37"> When<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “On the day”">g</a></sup>Kenan had lived seventy years, he fathered Mahalalel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And after Kenan had fathered Mahalalel, he lived eight hundred and forty years, and fathered sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And all the days of Kenan were nine hundred and ten years, and he died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:2"> When<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “On the day”">h</a></sup>Mahalalel had lived sixty-five years, he fathered Jared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And after Mahalalel had fathered Jared, he lived eight hundred and thirty years, and fathered sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And all the days of Mahalalel were eight hundred and ninety-five years, and he died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 4:17; 1 Chronicles 1:3; Jude 1:14–15"> When<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “On the day”">i</a></sup>Jared had lived one hundred and sixty-two years, he fathered Enoch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And after Jared had fathered Enoch, he lived eight hundred years, and fathered sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And all the days of Jared were nine hundred and sixty-two years, and he died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 3:37"> When<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “On the day”">j</a></sup>Enoch had lived sixty-five years, he fathered Methuselah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:12; Genesis 17:1; Genesis 6:9"> And Enoch walked with God after he fathered Methuselah three hundred years, and fathered sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And all the days of Enoch were three hundred and sixty-five years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 11:5–6; 1 John 1:7; Genesis 5:22"> And Enoch walked with God, and he was no more, for God took him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 4:18"> When<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “On the day”">k</a></sup>Methuselah had lived one hundred and eighty-seven years, he fathered Lamech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And after Methuselah had fathered Lamech, he lived seven hundred and eighty-two years, and fathered sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And all the days of Methuselah were nine hundred and sixty-nine years, and he died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> When<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “On the day”">l</a></sup>Lamech had lived one hundred and eighty-two years, he fathered a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Peter 2:5; Hebrews 11:7; Matthew 24:37"> And he called his name Noah, saying, “This one ⸤shall relieve us⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “shall comfort us”">m</a></sup>from our work, and from the hard labor of our hands, from the ground which Yahweh had cursed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And after Lamech had fathered Noah he lived five hundred and ninety-five years, and he fathered sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> All the days of Lamech were seven hundred and seventy-seven years, and he died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 6:10; Genesis 10:21; Genesis 9:18–19"> When<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “On the day”">n</a></sup>Noah ⸤was five hundred years old⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “was a son of five hundred years”">o</a></sup>Noah fathered Shem, Ham, and Japheth.</span>
</div>
