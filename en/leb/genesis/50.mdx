## Jacob’s Funeral and Joseph’s Remaining Time in Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 46:4; Deuteronomy 6:7–8; Acts 8:2"> Then Joseph fell on the face of his father and wept upon him and kissed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 50:26; 2 Chronicles 16:14; John 19:39–40"> And Joseph instructed his servants the physicians to embalm his father. So the physicians embalmed Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 20:29; Deuteronomy 34:8; Genesis 50:10"> Forty days ⸤were required for it⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “were fulfilled for it”">a</a></sup>for thus are the days ⸤required for⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “fulfilled”">b</a></sup>embalming. And the Egyptians wept for him seventy days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 18:3; Esther 4:2"> When the days of his weeping had passed, Joseph spoke to the household of Pharaoh, saying, “If I have found favor in your eyes, please speak in the hearing of Pharaoh, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:14; Isaiah 22:16; Matthew 27:60"> ‘My father made me swear, saying, “Behold, I am about to die. In the tomb that I have hewed out for myself in the land of Canaan—there you must bury me.” So then, please let me go up and let me bury my father; then I will return.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 48:21"> Then Pharaoh said, “Go up and bury your father as he made you swear.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 14:16"> So Joseph went up to bury his father. And all the servants of Pharaoh, the elders of his household, and all the elders of the land of Egypt, went up with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 32:24–27; Exodus 10:26; Exodus 10:8–9"> with all the household of Joseph, his brothers, and the household of his father. They left only their little children and their flocks and their herds in the land of Goshen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 14:28; 2 Kings 18:24; Song of Solomon 1:9"> And there also went up with him chariots and horsemen. The company was very great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 2:13; Acts 8:2; 2 Samuel 1:17"> When they came to the threshing floor of Atad, which was beyond the Jordan, they lamented there with a very great and sorrowful wailing. And he made a mourning ceremony for his father seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 10:15–19; Genesis 24:6; Deuteronomy 11:30"> And when the Canaanites, the inhabitants of the land, saw the mourning ceremony at the threshing floor of Atad they said, “This is a severe mourning for the Egyptians.” Therefore its name was called Abel-Mizraim, which is beyond the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 7:16; Genesis 49:29–32; Ephesians 6:1"> Thus his sons did to him just as he had instructed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 49:29–31; Genesis 23:16–18; Acts 7:16"> And his sons carried him to the land of Canaan and buried him in the cave of the field of Machpelah, which field Abraham had bought as a burial site from Ephron the Hittite before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “east of”">c</a></sup>Mamre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And after burying his father, Joseph returned to Egypt, he and his brothers and all who had gone up with him to bury his father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 28:1; Genesis 42:21–22; Psalm 53:5"> And when the brothers of Joseph saw that their father was dead, they said, “It may be that Joseph will hold a grudge against us and pay us back dearly for all the evil that we did to him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 29:25"> So they sent word to Joseph saying, “Your father commanded us before his death, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Matthew 6:14–15; Genesis 45:8"> “Thus you must say to Joseph, ‘O, please now forgive the transgression of your brothers and their sin, for they did evil to you.’ So now, please forgive the transgression of the servants of the God of your father.” And Joseph wept when they spoke to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 37:7–11; Genesis 42:6; Genesis 45:3"> Then his brothers went also and fell before him and said, “Behold, we are your servants.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 12:19; Genesis 45:5; Genesis 30:2"> Then Joseph said to them, “Do not be afraid, for am I in the place of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 8:28; Psalm 119:71; Genesis 45:5–8"> As for you, you planned evil against me, but God planned it for good, in order to do this—to keep many people alive—as it is today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 47:12; 1 Peter 3:9; Genesis 45:10–11"> So then, do not be afraid. I myself will provide for you and your little ones. And he consoled them and ⸤spoke kindly⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “spoke to their heart”">d</a></sup>to them.</span>

## The Death of Joseph

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> So Joseph remained in Egypt, he and the house of his father. And Joseph lived one hundred and ten years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 32:39; Genesis 30:3; Job 42:16"> And Joseph saw Ephraim’s children to the third generation. Moreover, the children of Makir, son of Manasseh, were born on the knees of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 3:16–17; Genesis 35:12; Genesis 26:3"> And Joseph said to his brothers, “I am about to die, but God will certainly visit you and bring you up from this land to the land that he swore to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 13:19; Joshua 24:32; Genesis 47:29–31"> Then Joseph made the sons of Israel swear an oath, saying, “God will surely visit you, and you shall bring up my bones from here.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 50:2–3; Genesis 47:9; Joshua 24:29"> So Joseph died, being one hundred and ten years old. They embalmed him and he was placed in a coffin in Egypt.</span>
</div>
