## Isaac and Abimelech

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 12:10; Genesis 25:11; Genesis 21:22–32"> And there was a famine in the land, besides the former famine which was in the days of Abraham. And Isaac went to Abimelech, king of the Philistines, to Gerar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 12:7; Psalm 37:3; Genesis 17:1"> And Yahweh appeared to him and said, “Do not go down to Egypt; dwell in the land which I will show to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 28:15; Psalm 105:9; Genesis 17:8"> Dwell as an alien in this land, and I will be with you, and will bless you, for I will give all these lands to you and to your descendants, and I will establish the oath that I swore to Abraham you father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 3:25; Genesis 15:5; Genesis 22:17–18"> And I will multiply your descendants like the stars of heaven, and I will give to your descendants all these lands. And all nations of the earth will be blessed through your offspring,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 7:24; Genesis 22:18; Genesis 22:16"> because Abraham listened to my voice and kept my charge: my commandments, my statutes, and my laws.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 20:1"> So Isaac settled in Gerar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 29:25; Genesis 12:13; Genesis 20:2"> When the men of the place asked concerning his wife, he said, “She is my sister,” for he was afraid to say, “my wife,” thinking<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “lest”">a</a></sup>“the men of the place will kill me on account of Rebekah, for ⸤she was beautiful⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “good of appearance”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 62:5; Proverbs 5:18–19; Proverbs 7:6"> And it happened that, ⸤when he had been there a long time⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “when the days there were long to him”">c</a></sup>Abimelech the king of the Philistines looked through the window, and saw—behold—Isaac was fondling Rebekah his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Abimelech called Isaac<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “to Isaac”">d</a></sup>and said, “Surely she is your wife. Now why did you say ‘She is my sister’?” And Isaac said to him, “Because I thought I would die on account of her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 20:9–10; Genesis 12:18–19"> And Abimelech said, “What is this you have done to us? One of the people might easily have slept with your wife! Then you would have brought guilt upon us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 105:15; Genesis 20:6; Proverbs 6:29"> Then Abimelech instructed all the people, saying, “The one who touches this man or his wife shall certainly die.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 4:8; Genesis 24:1; Genesis 26:3"> And Isaac sowed in that land and reaped in that same year a hundredfold, and Yahweh blessed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 112:3; Proverbs 10:22; Genesis 24:35"> And the man ⸤became wealthier and wealthier⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “became great and went, going and became great”">e</a></sup>until he was exceedingly wealthy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 37:11; Genesis 13:2; Proverbs 27:4"> And he possessed sheep and cattle and many servants, so that the Philistines envied him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 21:30; Genesis 21:25"> And the Philistines stopped up all the wells that the servants of his father had dug in the days of Abraham his father. They filled them with earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 1:9"> And Abimelech said to Isaac, “Go away from us, for you have become much too powerful for us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> So Isaac departed from there and camped in the valley of Gerar, and settled there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 13:2; Hosea 2:17; Numbers 32:38"> And Isaac dug again the wells of water which they had dug in the days of his father Abraham, which the Philistines had stopped up after the death of Abraham. And he gave<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “called”">f</a></sup>to them ⸤the same names⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “names as names”">g</a></sup>which his father had given<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “called”">h</a></sup>them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 4:10–11; John 7:38; Song of Solomon 4:15"> And when the servants of Isaac dug in the valley, they found a well of fresh water there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 21:25"> Then the herdsmen of Gerar quarreled with the herdsmen of Isaac, saying, “The water is ours.” And he called the name of the well Esek, because they contended with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezra 4:6"> And they dug another well, and they quarreled over it also. And he called its name Sitnah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 17:6; Exodus 1:7; Psalm 118:5"> Then he moved from there and dug another well, and they did not quarrel over it. And he called its name Rehoboth, and said, “Now Yahweh has made room for us, and we shall be fruitful in the land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 20:1; Genesis 21:31; Genesis 46:1"> And from there he went up to Beersheba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 15:1; Exodus 3:6; Isaiah 43:1–2"> And Yahweh appeared to him that night and said, “I am the God of your father Abraham. Do not be afraid, for I am with you, and I will bless you and make your descendants numerous for the sake of my servant Abraham.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 13:18; Psalm 116:17; Genesis 12:7–8"> And he built an altar there and called on the name of Yahweh. And he pitched his tent there, and the servants of Isaac dug a well there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 21:22–32; Genesis 20:3"> Then Abimelech went to him from Gerar with Ahuzzath his friend and Phicol his army commander.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 26:16; Judges 11:7; Acts 7:14"> And Isaac said to them, “Why have you come to me? You hate me and sent me away from you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 21:22–23; Isaiah 45:14; Isaiah 61:9"> And they said, “We see clearly that Yahweh has been with you, so we thought<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “said”">i</a></sup>let there be an oath between us—between us and you—and let us ⸤make⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “cut”">j</a></sup>a covenant with you</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 24:31; Genesis 21:22; Genesis 22:17"> that you may not do us harm just as we have not touched you, but have only done good to you and sent you away in peace. You are now blessed by Yahweh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 19:3; Romans 12:18; Hebrews 12:14"> So he made a meal for them, and they ate and drank.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:16–17; Hebrews 6:16; Genesis 25:33"> And they arose early in the morning and each one swore to the other,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “to his brother”">k</a></sup>and Isaac sent them away. And they left him in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 2:4–5"> And it happened that on that same day the servants of Isaac came and told him about the well that they had dug. And they said, “We have found water!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 21:31; Genesis 26:28"> And he called it Sheba. Therefore the name of the city is Beersheba unto this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 36:2; Genesis 36:13; Exodus 34:16"> And when Esau was forty years old he took as wife Judith, daughter of Beeri the Hittite, and Basemath, daughter of Elon the Hittite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 27:46–2; Genesis 28:8; Genesis 6:2"> And ⸤they made life bitter⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “they caused bitterness of spirit”">l</a></sup>for Isaac and Rebekah.</span>
</div>
