## The Death and Descendants of Abraham

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:32–33; Genesis 23:1–2; Genesis 28:1"> Now Abraham again took a wife, and her name was Keturah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:32–33; Numbers 25:17–18; Job 2:11"> And she bore to him Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak, and Shuah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:13; Jeremiah 49:8; Ezekiel 27:20"> And Jokshan fathered Sheba and Dedan. And the sons of Dedan were Asshurim and Letushim and Leummim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 60:6"> And the sons of Midian were Ephah, Epher, Hanoch, Abidah, and Eldaah. All of these were the children of Keturah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 24:36; Hebrews 1:2; Galatians 3:29"> And Abraham gave all he had to Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 21:14; Judges 6:3; Acts 14:17"> But to the sons of Abraham’s concubines Abraham gave gifts. And while he was still living he sent them away eastward, away from his son Isaac, to the land of the east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 12:4"> Now these are the days of the years of ⸤the life of Abraham⸥:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “the years of the life of Abraham which he lived”">a</a></sup>one hundred and seventy-five years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 35:28–29; Genesis 15:15; 1 Chronicles 29:28"> And Abraham passed away and died in a good old age, old and full of years. And he was gathered to his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 50:13; Genesis 35:29; Genesis 49:29–30"> And Isaac and Ishmael his sons buried him in the cave of Machpelah, in the field of Ephron, son of Zohar the Hittite, that was east of Mamre,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 23:16; Genesis 49:31"> the field that Abraham had bought from the Hittites.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “sons of Heth”">b</a></sup>There Abraham was buried and Sarah his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Genesis 16:14; Genesis 24:62"> And it happened that after the death of Abraham God blessed Isaac his son, and Isaac settled at Beer-Lahai-Roi.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 83:6; Genesis 17:20; Genesis 21:13"> Now these are the generations<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “family records”">c</a></sup>of Ishmael, the son of Abraham, that Hagar the Egyptian, the maidservant of Sarah, bore to Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:29–31; Isaiah 60:7; Song of Solomon 1:5"> And these are the names of the sons of Ishmael, by their names according to their family records. The firstborn of Ishmael was Nebaioth, then Kedar, Adbeel, Mibsam,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 21:11; Isaiah 21:16"> Mishma, Dumah, Massa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:19; Job 2:11"> Hadad, Tema, Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 17:20; Genesis 17:23"> These are the sons of Ishmael, and these are their names by their villages and by their encampments—12 leaders according to their tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 15:15; Genesis 25:7–8"> Now these are the years of the life of Ishmael: 137 years. And he passed away and died, and was gathered to his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 16:12; Genesis 20:1; 2 Kings 23:29"> They settled from Havilah to Shur, which was opposite<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “upon the face of”">d</a></sup>Egypt, going toward Asshur, opposite;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “upon the face of”">e</a></sup>he ⸤settled⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “fell”">f</a></sup>opposite<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “upon the face of”">g</a></sup>all his brothers.</span>

## Jacob and Esau

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 1:2; Luke 3:34; Acts 7:8"> Now these are the generations<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “family records”">h</a></sup>of Isaac, the son of Abraham. Abraham fathered Isaac,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 24:29; Genesis 24:67; Genesis 22:23"> And Isaac was ⸤forty years old⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “a son of forty years”">i</a></sup>when he took Rebekah, the daughter of Bethuel the Aramean of Paddan-Aram, the sister of Laban the Aramean, as his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:13; Isaiah 58:9; Ezra 8:23"> And Isaac prayed to Yahweh on behalf of his wife, for she was barren. And Yahweh responded to his prayer, and Rebekah his wife conceived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:9; 1 Samuel 28:6; 1 Samuel 30:8"> And the children in her womb jostled each other, and she said, “⸤If it is going to be like this, why be pregnant⸥?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “if so, why this I?”">j</a></sup>And she went to inquire of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 9:10–13; Genesis 27:40; Genesis 27:29"> And Yahweh said to her, “Two nations are in your womb, and two peoples ⸤from birth⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “from your bowels”">k</a></sup>shall be divided. And ⸤one people shall be stronger than the other⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “people than people shall be stronger”">l</a></sup>And the elder shall serve the younger.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And when her days to give birth were completed,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “full”">m</a></sup>then—behold—twins were in her womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 27:11; Genesis 27:16; Genesis 27:23"> And the first came out red, all ⸤his body⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “of him”">n</a></sup>was like a hairy coat, so they called his name Esau.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 27:36; Hosea 12:3; Genesis 38:28–30"> And afterward his brother came out, and his hand grasped the heel of Esau, so his name was called Jacob. And Isaac ⸤was sixty years old⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “a son of sixty years”">o</a></sup>at their birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 11:9; Genesis 21:20; Genesis 10:9"> And the boys grew up. And Esau was a skilled<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “knowing” (knowledgeable)">p</a></sup>hunter, a man of the field, but Jacob was a peaceful man, living in tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 27:19; Genesis 27:31; Genesis 27:4"> And Isaac loved Esau because ⸤he could eat of his game⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “game in his mouth”">q</a></sup>but Rebekah loved Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 13:25; Isaiah 40:30–31; 1 Samuel 14:28"> Once<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “and”">r</a></sup>Jacob cooked a thick stew, and Esau came in from the field, and he was exhausted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 36:1; Exodus 15:15; Genesis 36:9"> And Esau said to Jacob, “Give me ⸤some of that red stuff⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “some of the red, this red”">s</a></sup>to gulp down, for I am exhausted!” (Therefore his name was called Edom).</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Then Jacob said, “Sell me your birthright ⸤first⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “as the day”">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Malachi 3:14; Job 21:15; Job 22:17"> And Esau said, “Look, I am going to die; now what is this birthright to me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 12:16; Genesis 27:36; Hebrews 6:16"> Then Jacob said, “Swear to me ⸤first⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “as the day”">u</a></sup>And he swore to him, and sold his birthright to Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Philippians 3:18–19; Hebrews 12:16–17; 1 Corinthians 15:32"> Then Jacob gave Esau bread, and thick lentil stew, and he ate and drank. Then he got up and went away. So Esau despised his birthright.</span>
</div>
