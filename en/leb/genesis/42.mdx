## Joseph’s Brothers Go to Egypt for Food

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 7:12; Joshua 7:10; 2 Kings 8:3–4"> When Jacob realized that there was grain in Egypt, Jacob said to his sons, “Why do you look at one another?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 43:8; Genesis 45:9; Genesis 43:2"> Then he said, “Look, I have heard that there is grain in Egypt. Go down there and buy grain for us there that we may live and not die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 42:5; Genesis 42:13"> And the ten brothers of Joseph went down to buy grain from Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 42:38; Genesis 43:29; Genesis 11:4"> But Jacob did not send Benjamin, the brother of Joseph, for ⸤he feared harm would come to him⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “he thought, lest harm encounter him”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 41:57; Acts 7:11; Genesis 12:10"> Then the sons of Israel went to buy grain amid those other people who went as well, for there was famine in the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 3:9; Genesis 37:7–10; Genesis 41:40–41"> Now Joseph was the governor over the land. He was the one who sold food to all the people of the land. And the brothers of Joseph came and bowed down to him with their faces to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 42:30; Genesis 42:14–17; Matthew 15:23–26"> And Joseph saw his brothers and recognized them, but he pretended to be a stranger to them. And he spoke with them harshly and said to them, “From where have you come?” And they said, “From the land of Canaan to buy food.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 37:2; John 20:14; Luke 24:16"> And Joseph recognized his brothers, but they did not recognize him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 37:5–9; Exodus 32:35; Joshua 2:1"> And Joseph remembered the dreams which he had dreamed concerning them, and he said to them, “You are spies! You have come to see the nakedness of the land!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 37:8; 1 Samuel 26:17; Genesis 27:37"> And they said to him, “No, my lord, but your servants have come to buy food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 42:19; 2 Corinthians 6:4; John 7:18"> We all are sons of one man. We are honest men. We, your servants, are not spies.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Then he said to them, “No, but you have come to see the nakedness of the land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 37:30; Genesis 42:32; Genesis 44:20"> Then they said, “We, your servants, are twelve brothers, the sons of one man in the land of Canaan, but behold, the youngest is with our father today, and one is no more.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 15:21–28; Job 13:24; Genesis 42:9–11"> But Joseph said to them, “It is what I said to you—you are spies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:55; Jeremiah 5:7; Genesis 42:16"> By this you shall be tested. By the life of Pharaoh you will not go out from here unless your youngest brother comes here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 42:11"> Send one of you, and let him bring your brother, but you will be kept in prison so that your words might be tested to see if there is truth with you. And if not, by the life of Pharaoh surely you are spies.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 40:4; Hebrews 12:10; Genesis 40:7"> Then he gathered them into the prison for three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 25:43; Nehemiah 5:15; Luke 18:2"> On the third day Joseph said to them, “Do this and you will live; I fear God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 45:23; Isaiah 42:7; Genesis 40:3"> If you are honest, let one of your brothers be kept in prison ⸤where you are now being kept⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “in the house of your custody”">b</a></sup>but the rest of you go, carry grain for the famine for your households.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 44:23; Genesis 43:5; Genesis 42:34"> You must bring your youngest brother to me, and then your words will be confirmed and you will not die.” And they did so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hosea 5:15; Mark 9:43–45; Genesis 37:23–28"> Then each said to his brother, “Surely we are guilty on account of our brother when we saw the anguish of his soul when he pleaded for mercy to us and we would not listen. Therefore this trouble has come to us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:22; Psalm 9:12; 1 Kings 2:32"> Then Reuben answered them, saying, “Did I not say to you, do not sin against the boy? But you did not listen, and now, behold, his blood has been sought.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:20; John 16:13–14"> Now they did not know that Joseph understood, for the interpreter was between them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 43:30; Jude 1:22–23; Hebrews 4:15"> And he turned away from them and wept. Then he returned to them and spoke to them, and took Simeon from them and tied him up in front of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Romans 12:17–21; Genesis 45:21"> Then Joseph gave orders to fill their bags with grain and to return their money to each sack, and to give them provisions for the journey. Thus he did for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Then they loaded their grain upon their donkeys and went away from there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 4:24; Luke 2:7; Luke 10:34"> And one of them later opened his sack to give fodder to his donkey at the lodging place and saw his money—behold, it was in the mouth of his sack.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Lamentations 3:37; Genesis 42:36"> And he said to his brothers, “My money was returned and moreover, behold, it is in my sack!” Then ⸤their hearts failed them⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “their heart went out”">c</a></sup>and each of them trembled and said, “What is this God has done to us?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And when they came to Jacob their father in the land of Canaan they told him everything that had happened to them, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 42:7"> “The man, the lord of the land, spoke harshly to us and treated us as if we were spying out the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 42:11"> But we said to him, ‘We are honest; we are not spies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> We are twelve brothers, the sons of our father. One is no more and the youngest is with our father now in the land of Canaan.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 42:19–20; Genesis 42:15"> Then the man, the lord of the land, said to us, ‘By this I will know that you are honest. Leave one brother with me, and take food for the famine in your households and go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 34:10; Genesis 34:21; 1 Kings 10:15"> And bring your youngest brother to me. Then I will know that you are not spies but you are honest. And I will give your brother back to you, and you will trade in the land.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 43:21; Genesis 43:12; Genesis 43:15"> And it happened that when they emptied their sacks, behold, each one’s pouch of money was in his sack. And when they and their father saw the pouches of their money, they were greatly distressed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 43:14; Romans 8:31; Isaiah 27:9"> And Jacob their father said to them, “You have bereaved me—Joseph is no more and Simeon is no more, and Benjamin you would take! All of this is against me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Genesis 46:9; Micah 6:7; Genesis 43:9"> Then Reuben said to his father, “You may kill my two sons if I do not bring him back to you. Put him in my hand and I myself will return him to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 37:35; Genesis 42:4; Genesis 37:33"> But he said, “My son shall not go down with you, for his brother is dead and he alone remains. If harm meets him on the journey that you would take, you would bring down my gray head in sorrow to Sheol.”</span>
</div>
