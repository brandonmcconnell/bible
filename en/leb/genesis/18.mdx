## Yahweh Appears to Abraham as a Man

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 14:13; Genesis 48:3; Genesis 13:18"> And Yahweh appeared to him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Abraham">a</a></sup>by the oaks of Mamre. And he was sitting in the doorway of the tent at the heat of the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 13:2; Genesis 18:22; Genesis 19:1"> And he lifted up his eyes and saw, and behold, three men were standing near<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “by”; the context requires some distance for Abraham to run to them">b</a></sup>him. And he saw them and ran from the doorway of the tent to meet them. And he bowed down to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 32:5"> And he said, “My lord, if I have found favor in your eyes do not pass by your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 24:32; Genesis 19:2; Genesis 43:24"> Let a little water be brought and wash your feet, and rest under the tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 104:15; Judges 13:15; Judges 6:18"> And let me bring a piece of bread, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “and”">c</a></sup>refresh ⸤yourselves⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “your heart”">d</a></sup>Afterward you can pass on, ⸤once⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “for therefore”">e</a></sup>you have passed by with your servant.” Then they said, “Do so as you have said.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 13:33; Hebrews 13:2; Acts 16:15"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “And”">f</a></sup>Abraham hastened into the tent to Sarah, and he said, “Quickly—make three seahs of fine flour for kneading and make bread cakes!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Malachi 1:14; Luke 15:27; Luke 15:30"> And Abraham ran to the cattle and took a ⸤calf⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “a son of cattle”">g</a></sup>tender and good, and gave it to the servant, and he made haste to prepare it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 19:3; Judges 13:15; Revelation 3:20"> Then he took curds and milk, and the calf which he prepared, and set it before them. And he was standing by them under the tree while<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “and”">h</a></sup>they ate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Titus 2:5; Genesis 24:67; Genesis 4:9"> And they said to him, “Where is Sarah your wife?” And he said, “Here, in the tent.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 21:2; Genesis 17:21; Romans 9:8–9"> And he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Yahweh">i</a></sup>said, “I will certainly return to you ⸤in the spring⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “at the time of life”">j</a></sup>and look, Sarah your wife will have a son.” Now Sarah was listening at the doorway of the tent, and which was behind him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 17:17; Hebrews 11:11–12; Luke 1:36"> Now Abraham and Sarah were old, ⸤advanced in age⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “going in the days”">k</a></sup>⸤the way of women⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “the road according to women”">l</a></sup>had ceased to be for Sarah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:6; Genesis 17:17; Luke 1:18–20"> So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “And”">m</a></sup>Sarah laughed to herself saying, “After I am worn out and my husband is old, shall this pleasure be to me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 2:25"> Then Yahweh said to Abraham, “What is this that Sarah laughed, saying, ‘Is it indeed true that I will bear a child, now that I have grown old?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 1:37; Matthew 19:26; Mark 10:27"> Is anything too difficult for Yahweh? At the appointed time I will return to you ⸤in the spring⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “at the time of life”">n</a></sup>and Sarah shall have a son.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 1:8; Proverbs 28:13; Ephesians 4:23"> But Sarah denied it, saying, “I did not laugh,” because she was afraid. He<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Yahweh">o</a></sup>said, “No, but you did laugh.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 15:3; Acts 21:5; Acts 20:38"> Then the men set out from there, and they looked down upon Sodom. And Abraham went with them ⸤to send them on their way⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “to send them away”">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Amos 3:7; Psalm 25:14; John 15:15"> Then Yahweh said, “Shall I conceal from Abraham what I am going to do?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 3:8; Genesis 26:4; Galatians 3:14"> Abraham will surely become a great and strong nation, and all the nations of the earth will be blessed on account of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:6–7; Deuteronomy 4:9–10; 1 Timothy 3:4–5"> For I have chosen<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “have known”">q</a></sup>him, that he will command his children and his household after him that they will keep the way of Yahweh, to do righteousness and justice, so that Yahweh may bring upon Abraham that which he said to him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 19:13; Ezekiel 16:49–50; Isaiah 3:9"> Then Yahweh said, “Because the outcry of Sodom and Gomorrah is great and because their sin is very ⸤serious⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “heavy”">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Genesis 11:5; Zephaniah 1:12"> I will go down and I will see. Have they done altogether according to its cry of distress which has come to me? If not, I will know.”</span>
</div>

## Abraham Intercedes for Sodom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 19:1; Acts 7:55; Ezekiel 22:30"> And the men turned from there and went toward Sodom. And Abraham was still standing before Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 16:22; 2 Samuel 24:17; Genesis 20:4"> And Abraham drew near to Yahweh and said, “Will you also sweep away the righteous with the wicked?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:1; Isaiah 1:9; Matthew 7:13–14"> If perhaps there are fifty righteous in the midst of the city, will you also sweep them away and not forgive the place on account of the fifty righteous in her midst?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 8:3; Isaiah 3:10–11; Job 8:20"> Far be it from you to do such a thing as this, to kill the righteous with the wicked, that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “and”">s</a></sup>the righteous would be as the wicked! Far be it from you! Will not the Judge of all the earth do justice?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:1; Matthew 24:22; Isaiah 65:8"> And Yahweh said, “If I find fifty righteous in Sodom, in the midst of the city, then I will forgive the whole place for their sake.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Isaiah 6:5; 1 Corinthians 15:47–48"> Then Abraham answered and said, “Look, please, I was bold to speak to my Lord, but I am dust and ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 18:29; 1 Kings 20:32–33; Genesis 18:26"> Perhaps the fifty righteous are lacking five—will you destroy the whole city on account of the five?” And he answered, “I will not destroy it if I find forty-five there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; Hebrews 4:16"> And ⸤once again he spoke⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “And he added again to speak”">t</a></sup>to him and said, “What if<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “Perhaps”">u</a></sup>forty are found there?” And he answered, “I will not do it on account of the forty.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalm 89:7; Psalm 9:12; Esther 4:11–16"> And he said, “Please, let not my Lord be angry, and I will speak. What if<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “Perhaps”">v</a></sup>thirty be found there?” And he answered, “I will not do it if I find thirty there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 11:8; Genesis 18:27; Luke 18:1"> And he said, “Please, now, I was bold to speak to my Lord. What if<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or “Perhaps”">w</a></sup>twenty be found there?” And he answered, “I will not destroy it for the sake of the twenty.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="James 5:15–17; Judges 6:39; Matthew 7:7"> And he said, “Please, let not my Lord be angry, and I will speak only once more. What if<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Or “Perhaps”">x</a></sup>ten are found there?” And he answered, “I will not destroy it for the sake of the ten.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 18:22; Genesis 32:26; Genesis 31:55"> Then Yahweh left,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Or “went”">y</a></sup>as he finished speaking to Abraham, and Abraham returned to his place.</span>
</div>
