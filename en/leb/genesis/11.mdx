## The Tower of Babel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 2:6; Zephaniah 3:9; Isaiah 19:18"> Now<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “And”">a</a></sup>the whole earth had one language and the same words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 10:10; Daniel 1:2; Genesis 14:1"> And as people migrated<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “set out”">b</a></sup>from the east they found a plain in the land of Shinar and settled there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 14:10; Exodus 1:14; Exodus 2:3"> And they said ⸤to each other⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “each to his companion”">c</a></sup>“Come, let us make bricks and ⸤burn them thoroughly⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “burn to burning”">d</a></sup>And they had brick for stone and they had tar for mortar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; John 5:44; Luke 1:51"> And they said, “Come, let us build ourselves a city and a tower whose top reaches to the heavens. And let us make a name for ourselves, lest we be scattered over the face of the whole earth.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 18:21; Psalm 33:13–14; John 3:13"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “And”">e</a></sup>Yahweh came down to see the city and the tower that ⸤humankind⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “sons of Adam” or “sons of humankind”">f</a></sup>was building.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 2:1–4; Genesis 3:22; Genesis 11:1"> And Yahweh said, “Behold, ⸤they are one people with one language⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “one people and one language to all of them”">g</a></sup>and ⸤this is only the beginning of what they will do⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “and this they begin to do”">h</a></sup>So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “And”">i</a></sup>now nothing that they intend to do will be impossible for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 33:10; Psalm 55:9; Job 5:12–13"> Come, let us go down and confuse their language there, so that they will not understand ⸤each other’s language⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “each the language of his companion”">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 1:51; Deuteronomy 32:8; Genesis 10:25"> So Yahweh scattered them from there over the face of the whole earth, and they stopped building the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 10:10; Genesis 10:5; Genesis 10:20"> Therefore its name was called Babel, for there Yahweh confused the language of the whole earth, and there Yahweh scattered them over the face of the whole earth.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Descendants of Shem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:17–27; Genesis 10:21–22; Luke 3:34–36"> These are the generations<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “family records”">k</a></sup>of Shem. When Shem ⸤was one hundred years old⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “the son of one hundred years”">l</a></sup>he fathered Arphaxad, two years after the flood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 9:7; Psalm 144:12; Genesis 1:28"> And Shem lived five hundred years after he fathered Arphaxad, and he fathered other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 3:36"> When Arphaxad had lived thirty-five years, he fathered Shelah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And Arphaxad lived four hundred and three years after he fathered Shelah, and he fathered other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> When Shelah had lived thirty years, he fathered Eber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Shelah lived four hundred and three years after he fathered Eber, and he fathered other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 10:21; 1 Chronicles 1:19; Numbers 24:24"> When Eber had lived thirty-four years, he fathered Peleg.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Eber lived four hundred and thirty years after he fathered Peleg, and he fathered other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 3:35"> When Peleg had lived thirty years, he fathered Reu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And Peleg lived two hundred and nine years after he fathered Reu, and he fathered other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 3:35"> When Reu had lived thirty-two years, he fathered Serug.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And Reu lived two hundred and seven years after he fathered Serug, and he fathered other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 24:2"> When Serug had lived thirty years, he fathered Nahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And Serug lived two hundred years after he fathered Nahor, and he fathered other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 3:34; Joshua 24:2"> When Nahor had lived twenty-nine years, he fathered Terah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And Nahor lived one hundred and nineteen years after he fathered Terah, and he fathered other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Joshua 24:2; 1 Chronicles 1:26–27; Genesis 22:20–24"> When Terah had lived seventy years, he fathered Abram, Nahor, and Haran.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Descendants of Terah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 12:4; Genesis 14:12; Genesis 11:31"> Now<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “And”">m</a></sup>these are the generations<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “family records”">n</a></sup>of Terah. Terah fathered Abram, Nahor, and Haran, and Haran fathered Lot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 15:7; Nehemiah 9:7; Acts 7:2–4"> And Haran died in the presence of Terah his father in the land of his birth, in Ur of the Chaldeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 17:15; Genesis 22:20; Genesis 24:15"> And Abram and Nahor took wives for themselves. The name of the wife of Abram was Sarai, and the name of the wife of Nahor was Milcah, the daughter of Haran, the father of Milcah and Iscah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 29:31; Genesis 21:1–2; Luke 1:7"> And Sarai was barren; she had no child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:7; Acts 7:2–4; Genesis 24:10"> And Terah took Abram his son, and Lot, the son of Haran, ⸤his grandson⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “the son of his son”">o</a></sup>and Sarai his daughter-in-law, the wife of Abram his son, and went out with them from Ur of the Chaldeans to go to the land of Canaan. And they went to Haran, and they settled there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And the days of Terah were two hundred and five years, and Terah died in Haran.</span>
</div>
</div>
