## Joseph Reveals His Identity

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 18:15; Acts 10:41; Genesis 43:30–31"> Then Joseph was not able to control himself before all who were standing by him. And he cried out, “Make every man go out from me!” So no one stood with him when Joseph made himself known to his brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 46:29; Acts 20:37; Numbers 14:1"> And ⸤he wept loudly⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “he gave his voice with weeping”">a</a></sup>so that the Egyptians heard it and the household of Pharaoh heard it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 7:13; Acts 9:5; Zechariah 12:10"> Then Joseph said to his brothers, “I am Joseph! Is my father still alive?” And his brothers were unable to answer him, for they were dismayed at his presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 37:28; Matthew 14:27; Genesis 50:18"> So Joseph said to his brothers, “Come near to me, please.” And they drew near. And he said, “I am Joseph, your brother, whom you sold into Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 50:20; Acts 7:9–15; Genesis 45:7–8"> So now, do not be distressed and do not be angry ⸤with yourselves⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “in your eyes”">b</a></sup>that you sold me here, for God sent me as deliverance before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 47:23; Genesis 41:54; Genesis 41:56"> For these two years the famine has been in the midst of the land, but there will be five more years where there is no plowing or harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 44:4; Acts 7:35; Psalm 18:50"> And God sent me before you all to preserve for you a remnant in the land and to keep alive among you many survivors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 15:16; Romans 9:16; John 19:11"> So now, you yourselves did not send me here, but God put me here as father to Pharaoh and as master of all his household, and a ruler over all the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 45:19–20; Genesis 45:13"> Hurry, and go up to my father and say to him, ‘Thus says your son Joseph, God has made me lord of all Egypt. Come down to me and do not delay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 9:26; Exodus 8:22; Genesis 46:34–6"> You shall settle in the land of Goshen so that you will be near me, you and your children and your grandchildren, and your flocks and your herds and all that you have.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 47:12; Genesis 47:6; Matthew 15:5–6"> And I will provide for you there, because there are still five years of famine—lest you and your household and all that you have become destitute.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 42:23; Luke 24:39; John 20:27"> Now behold, your eyes see, and the eyes of my brother Benjamin see, that ⸤it is I⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “it is my mouth”">c</a></sup>who am speaking to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 7:14; Revelation 21:23; 1 Peter 1:10–12"> And you must tell my father of all my honor in Egypt and all that you have seen. Now hurry and bring my father here.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 33:4; Genesis 29:11; Genesis 46:29"> Then he fell upon the neck of his brother Benjamin and wept, and Benjamin wept upon his neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 15:20; 1 Samuel 10:1; Psalm 77:4"> And he kissed all his brothers and wept upon them. And afterward his brothers spoke with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 41:33; Esther 1:21; Genesis 20:15"> Then the report was heard in the house of Pharaoh, saying, “Joseph’s brothers have come.” And it pleased Pharaoh and his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 42:25–26; Genesis 44:1–2"> Then Pharaoh said to Joseph, “Say to your brothers: ‘Do this—load your donkeys and go back to the land of Canaan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 27:28; Numbers 18:12; Genesis 47:6"> and take your father and your households and come to me, and I will give you the best of the land of Egypt, and you shall eat the fat of the land.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 46:5; Genesis 45:27; Genesis 31:17–18"> And you Joseph, are commanded to say this: ‘Do this! Take wagons from the land of Egypt for your little ones and your wives, and bring your father and come!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 1:19; Luke 17:31; Deuteronomy 7:16"> ⸤Do not worry⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “let not your eyes be troubled”">d</a></sup>about your possessions, for the best of all the land of Egypt is yours.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 45:19; Ezekiel 23:24; Lamentations 1:18"> And the sons of Israel did so. And Joseph gave them wagons at the word of Pharaoh, and gave them provisions for the journey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 43:34; 2 Kings 5:5; Judges 14:12"> To each and to all of them he gave sets of clothing, but to Benjamin he gave three hundred pieces of silver and five sets of clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 24:10; Exodus 16:3; Genesis 43:11"> And to his father he sent ⸤as follows⸥:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “according to this”">e</a></sup>ten donkeys carrying the best of Egypt, and ten donkeys carrying grain and food and provisions for his father for the journey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:13; Psalm 133:1–3; Genesis 42:21–22"> Then he sent his brothers away, and when they departed he said to them, “Do not be agitated on the journey.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> So they went up from Egypt and came to the land of Canaan to Jacob their father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 44:28; Genesis 37:35; Luke 24:34"> And they spoke to him, saying, “Joseph is still alive, and he is ruler over all the land of Egypt.” And his heart ⸤went numb⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “became cold”">f</a></sup>because he did not believe him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalm 85:6; Isaiah 57:15; Genesis 45:19"> Then they told him all the words of Joseph that he had spoken to them. And when he saw the wagons that Joseph had sent to carry him, then the spirit of Jacob their father revived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 2:28–30; John 16:21–22; Genesis 46:30"> And Israel said, “It is enough. Joseph my son is still alive. I will go and see him before I die.”</span>
