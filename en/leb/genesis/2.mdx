## The Generations of Heaven and Earth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 5:1; Revelation 1:8; Genesis 1:4"> These are the generations<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “family records”">f</a></sup>of heaven<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “the sky”">g</a></sup>and earth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “the earth”">h</a></sup>when they were created, in the day that Yahweh God made earth and heaven<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “sky”">i</a></sup>—</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 3:23; Hebrews 6:7; Job 5:10"> ⸤before any plant of the field was⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “and every plant of the field not yet was”">j</a></sup>on earth, and before ⸤any plant of the field⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “and every plant of the field not yet”">k</a></sup>had sprung up, because Yahweh God had not caused it to rain upon the earth, and there was no human being to cultivate the ground,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> but a stream would rise from the earth and water the whole face of the ground—</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:7; Job 33:4; 1 Corinthians 15:45"> when<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “and”">l</a></sup>Yahweh God formed the man<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="“The man” indicates the noun is singular and occurs with the definite article">m</a></sup>of dust from the ground, and he blew into his nostrils the breath of life, and the man became a living creature.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13; Genesis 3:24; Genesis 13:10"> And Yahweh God planted a garden in Eden in the east, and there he put the man<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="“The man” indicates the noun is singular and occurs with the definite article">n</a></sup>whom he had formed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 3:22; Revelation 2:7; Revelation 22:14"> And Yahweh God caused to grow every tree that was pleasing to the sight and good for food. And the tree of life was in the midst of the garden, ⸤along with⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “and”">o</a></sup>the tree of the knowledge of good and evil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; Psalm 46:4"> Now<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “And”">p</a></sup>a river flowed out from Eden that watered the garden, and from there it diverged and became four branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 25:18; Genesis 10:29; 1 Samuel 15:7"> The name of the first is the Pishon. It went around all the land of Havilah, where there is gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 28:20; Numbers 11:7; Ezekiel 28:13"> (The gold of that land is good; bdellium and onyx stones are there.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 10:6; Isaiah 11:11"> And the name of the second is Gihon. It went around all the land of Cush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 10:4; Genesis 15:18; Deuteronomy 1:7"> And the name of the third is Tigris. It flows east of Assyria. And the fourth river is the Euphrates.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 128:2; Genesis 2:8; Ephesians 4:28"> And Yahweh God took the man<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="“The man” indicates the noun is singular and occurs with the definite article">q</a></sup>and set him in the garden of Eden to cultivate it and to keep it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:22; 1 Timothy 4:4; 1 Timothy 6:17"> And Yahweh God commanded the man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="“The man” indicates the noun is singular and occurs with the definite article">r</a></sup>saying, “From every tree of the garden ⸤you may freely eat⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “eating you may eat”">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 6:23; James 1:15; Genesis 3:19"> but from the tree of the knowledge of good and evil you shall not eat, for in the day ⸤that you eat⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “of your eating”">t</a></sup>from it ⸤you shall surely die⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “dying you shall die”">u</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:9–12; 1 Corinthians 11:7–12; Proverbs 18:22"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “And”">v</a></sup>Yahweh God said, “it is not good that the man<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="“The man” indicates the noun is singular and occurs with the definite article">w</a></sup>is alone. I will make for him a helper ⸤as his counterpart⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “as his opposite”">x</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalm 8:4–8; Genesis 1:28; Genesis 9:2"> And out of the ground Yahweh God formed every beast of the field and every bird of the sky,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Or “the heavens”">y</a></sup>and he brought each to the man<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="“The man” indicates the noun is singular and occurs with the definite article">z</a></sup>to see what he would call it. And whatever the man<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="“The man” indicates the noun is singular and occurs with the definite article">aa</a></sup>called that living creature was its name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 2:18"> And the man<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="“The man” indicates the noun is singular and occurs with the definite article">ab</a></sup>⸤gave names⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “called names”">ac</a></sup>to every domesticated animal and to the birds of heaven<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Or “the sky”">ad</a></sup>and to all the wild animals.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “animals of the earth/land”">ae</a></sup>But for the man there was not found a helper ⸤as his counterpart⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “as his opposite”">af</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 15:12; Job 4:13; 1 Samuel 26:12"> And Yahweh God caused a deep sleep to fall upon the man.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="“The man” indicates the noun is singular and occurs with the definite article">ag</a></sup>While<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Or “And”">ah</a></sup>he slept, he took one of his ribs, and closed up ⸤the flesh where it had been⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Literally “the flesh in the place of it”">ai</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:8–9; 1 Timothy 2:13; Hebrews 13:4"> And Yahweh God fashioned the rib which he had taken from the man<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="“The man” indicates the noun is singular and occurs with the definite article">aj</a></sup>into a woman and brought her to the man.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="“The man” indicates the noun is singular and occurs with the definite article">ak</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ephesians 5:28–30; 1 Corinthians 11:8–9; 2 Samuel 5:1"> And the man<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="“The man” indicates the noun is singular and occurs with the definite article">al</a></sup>said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“⸤She is now⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Literally “this one the time”">am</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">bone from my bones</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and flesh from my flesh;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">⸤she⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Literally “this one ”">an</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">shall be called ‘Woman,’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for ⸤she was taken⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Literally “this one was taken”">ao</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from man.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 19:3–9; Ephesians 5:28–31; Mark 10:6–12"> Therefore a man shall leave his father and his mother and shall cling to his wife, and they shall be as one flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 3:7; Genesis 3:10–11; Romans 10:11"> And the man<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="“The man” indicates the noun is singular and occurs with the definite article">ap</a></sup>and his wife, both of them, were naked, and they were not ashamed.</span>
