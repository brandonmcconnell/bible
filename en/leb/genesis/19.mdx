## The Rescue of Lot from Sodom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 18:22; Genesis 18:1–5; Hebrews 13:2"> And the two angels came to Sodom in the evening. And Lot was sitting in the gateway of Sodom. Then Lot saw them and stood up to meet them. And he bowed down with his face to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 18:4; Luke 24:28–29; Hebrews 13:2"> And he said, “Behold, my lords, please turn aside into the house of your servant and spend the night and wash your feet. Then you can rise early and go on your way.” And they said, “No, but we will spend the night in the square.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 18:6–8; Judges 6:19; 1 Samuel 28:24"> But ⸤he urged them strongly⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “he pressed upon them very”">a</a></sup>and they turned aside with him and came into his house. And he made a meal for them and baked unleavened bread, and they ate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 13:13; Proverbs 4:16; Exodus 23:2"> Before they laid down, the men of the city, the men of Sodom, both young and old, all the people ⸤to the last man⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “from the end”">b</a></sup>surrounded the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 19:22; Isaiah 3:9; Jude 1:7"> And they called to Lot and said to him, “Where are the men who came to you tonight? Bring them out to us so that we may know<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew idiom for sexual intercourse (cp. Gen 4:1 )">c</a></sup>them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 19:23"> But Lot went out to them at the entrance, and he shut the door behind him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–11; Judges 19:23; Leviticus 20:13"> And he said, “No, my brothers, please do not do such a wrong thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 19:24; Genesis 19:31–38; Judges 9:15"> Behold, I have two daughters who have not known &#42; for protection.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 2:14; 2 Peter 2:7–8; Genesis 13:12"> But they said, “Step aside!” Then they said, “⸤This fellow⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “the one”">d</a></sup>came to dwell as a foreigner and he acts as a judge! Now we shall do worse to you than them!” And they pressed very hard against the man, against Lot, and they drew near to break the door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then the men reached out with their hands and brought Lot in to them, into the house, and they shut the door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 6:18; Acts 13:11; Deuteronomy 28:28–29"> And the men who were at the entrance of the house they struck with blindness, both small and great, and they were unable to find the entrance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 7:1; 2 Peter 2:7; Revelation 18:4"> Then the men said to Lot, “Who is here with you? Bring out from the place your sons-in-law, and your sons and your daughters, and all who are with you in the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 18:20; Matthew 13:49–50; Jude 1:7"> For we are about to destroy this place, because their cry has become great before Yahweh. Yahweh sent us to destroy it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 16:21; Jeremiah 51:6; Numbers 16:45"> Then Lot went out and spoke to his sons-in-law who were taking<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="That is, marrying">e</a></sup>his daughters and said, “Get up! Go out from this place, because Yahweh is going to destroy the city!” But ⸤it seemed like a joke⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “it was as one making fun”">f</a></sup>in the eyes of his sons-in-law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 16:24–27; Revelation 18:4; Genesis 19:22"> And as the dawn came up the angels urged Lot saying, “Get up, take your wife and your two daughters ⸤who are staying with you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “who are found”">g</a></sup>lest you be destroyed with the punishment of the city.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 9:15–16; Titus 3:5; John 6:44"> But when he lingered, the men seized him by his hand and his wife’s hand, and his two daughters by hand, on account of the mercy of Yahweh upon him. And they brought him out and set him outside of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 24:16–18; Luke 9:62; Philippians 3:13–14"> And after bringing them outside one said, “Flee for your life; do not look behind you, and do not stand anywhere in the plain. Flee to the mountains lest you be destroyed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 13:6–8; Acts 10:14; Genesis 32:26"> And Lot said to them, “No, please, my lords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 27:1; Psalm 103:1–22; 1 Timothy 1:14–16"> Behold, your servant has found favor in your eyes and ⸤you have shown me great kindness⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “your kindness which you have done to me you have made great”">h</a></sup>in saving my life. But I cannot flee to the mountains, lest the disaster overtake me and I die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 119:175; Proverbs 3:5–7; Genesis 19:30"> Behold, this city is near enough to flee there, and it is a little one. Please, let me flee there. Is it not a little one? Then my life shall be saved.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 145:19; Psalm 34:15; Psalm 102:17"> And he said to him, “Behold, ⸤I will grant this favor as well⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “I am lifting up your face also concerning this thing”">i</a></sup>that I will not overthrow the city of which you have spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 13:10; Genesis 14:2; Genesis 32:25–28"> Escape there quickly, for I cannot do this thing until you get there.” Therefore, the name of the city was called Zoar.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Destruction of Sodom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> After the sun ⸤had risen⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “had gone out”">j</a></sup>upon the earth and Lot had entered Zoar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:23; Isaiah 13:19; Amos 4:11"> Yahweh rained down from heaven upon Sodom and Gomorrah brimstone and fire from Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalm 107:34; Genesis 14:3; Genesis 13:10"> And he overthrew those cities and the whole plain, and the inhabitants of the cities and the vegetation of the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 17:31–32; Genesis 19:17; Proverbs 14:14"> But his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="That is, Lot’s">k</a></sup>wife looked ⸤back⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “behind him”">l</a></sup>and she became a pillar of salt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:1; Psalm 5:3; Hebrews 2:1"> And Abraham arose early in the morning and went to the place where he had stood before Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 18:9; Revelation 9:2; Revelation 21:8"> And he looked down upon the surface of Sodom and Gomorrah, and upon the whole surface of the land, the plain. And he saw that,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “and”">m</a></sup>behold, the smoke of the land went up like the smoke of a smelting furnace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 8:1; Deuteronomy 9:5; Genesis 18:23–33"> So it was, when God destroyed the cities of the plain that God remembered Abraham and sent Lot out from the midst of the overthrow, when he overthrew the cities in which Lot lived.</span>
</div>

## Lot and His Daughters

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 19:19; Isaiah 15:5; James 1:8"> And Lot went out from Zoar and settled in the hill country<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “mountain”">n</a></sup>with his two daughters, for he was afraid to stay in Zoar. So he lived in a cave, he and his two daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5; Isaiah 4:1; Genesis 16:2"> And the firstborn daughter said to the younger one, “Our father is old, and there is no man in the land to come in to us according to the manner of all the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 18:6–7; Genesis 11:3; Genesis 9:21"> Come, let us give our father wine to drink and let us lie with him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Idiom for “have sexual intercourse with him”">o</a></sup>that ⸤we may secure descendants through our father⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “that we might preserve offspring from our father”">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:15–16; Proverbs 23:29–35; Proverbs 20:1"> And they gave their father wine to drink that night, and the firstborn went and lay with her father, but he did not know when she lay down or when she got up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:12; Jeremiah 6:15; Isaiah 3:9"> And it happened that, the next day the firstborn said to the younger one, “Look, I laid with my father last night. Let us give him wine to drink also tonight, then go and lie with him that ⸤we may secure descendants through our father⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “that we might preserve offspring from our father”">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 24:16; Psalm 8:4; 1 Peter 4:7"> And they gave their father wine to drink again that night, and the younger got up and lay with him, but he did not know when she lay down or when she got up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:15; Judges 1:7; Matthew 7:2"> And the two daughters of Lot became pregnant by their father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:9; 2 Kings 3:1–27; Numbers 21:29"> The firstborn gave birth to a son, and she called his name Moab. He is the father of Moab until this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:19; Judges 10:6–40; Psalm 83:4–8"> And the younger, she also gave birth to a son, and she called his name Ben-Ammi. He is the father of the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">r</a></sup>until this day.</span>
</div>
