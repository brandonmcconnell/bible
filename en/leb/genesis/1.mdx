## The Creation

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 1:1–3; Hebrews 11:3; Isaiah 45:18"> In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:23; Psalm 104:30; Isaiah 45:18"> Now<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “And”">a</a></sup>the earth was formless and empty, and darkness was over the face of the deep. And the Spirit of God was hovering over the surface of the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:6; John 1:5; Isaiah 45:7"> And God said, “Let there be light!” And there was light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:13; Genesis 1:18; Ecclesiastes 11:7"> And God saw the light, that it was good, and God caused there to be a separation between the light and between the darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 74:16; Isaiah 45:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:5"> And God called the light Day, and the darkness he called Night. And there was evening and there was morning, the first day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:15; Psalm 33:6; Job 37:18"> And God said, “Let there be a vaulted dome<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “expanse”">b</a></sup>in the midst of the waters, and ⸤let it cause a separation between the waters⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “Let there be a separation made between waters to waters”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 8:28–29; Psalm 148:4; Matthew 8:27"> So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “And”">d</a></sup>God made the vaulted dome,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “expanse”">e</a></sup>and he caused a separation between the waters which were under the vaulted dome<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “expanse”">f</a></sup>and between the waters which were over the vaulted dome.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “expanse”">g</a></sup>And it was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 1:5; Genesis 1:19; Genesis 1:10"> And God called the vaulted dome<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “expanse”">h</a></sup>“heaven.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “sky”">i</a></sup>And there was evening, and there was morning, a second day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Peter 3:5; Jonah 1:9; Jeremiah 5:22"> And God said, “Let the waters under heaven<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “the sky”">j</a></sup>be gathered to one place, and let the dry ground appear.” And it was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Psalm 104:31; Genesis 1:4"> And God called the dry ground “earth,” and he called the collection of the waters “seas.” And God saw that it was good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 104:14–17; Hebrews 6:7; Psalm 147:8"> And God said, “Let the earth produce green plants that will bear seed—fruit trees bearing fruit ⸤in which there is seed⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “which its seed is in it”">k</a></sup>—according to its<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “their”">l</a></sup>kind, on the earth.” And it was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 4:28; Isaiah 55:10–11; Matthew 13:24–26"> And the earth brought forth green plants bearing seed according to its<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “their”">m</a></sup>kind, and trees bearing fruit ⸤in which there was seed⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “which its seed is in it”">n</a></sup>according to its<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “their”">o</a></sup>kind. And God saw that it was good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And there was evening and there was morning, a third day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 104:19–20; Deuteronomy 4:19; Jeremiah 31:35"> And God said, “Let there be lights<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “light sources”">p</a></sup>in the vaulted dome<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “expanse”">q</a></sup>of heaven<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “the sky”">r</a></sup>⸤to separate day from night⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “to make a separation between the day and between the night”">s</a></sup>and let them be as signs and for appointed times, and for days and years,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and they shall be as lights<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Or “light sources”">t</a></sup>in the vaulted dome<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “expanse”">u</a></sup>of heaven<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “the sky”">v</a></sup>to give light on the earth.” And it was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 136:7–9; Deuteronomy 4:19; Psalm 8:3"> And God made two lights,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or “light sources”">w</a></sup>the greater light<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Or “light source”">x</a></sup>to rule<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “as the authority of”">y</a></sup>the day and the smaller light<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “light source”">z</a></sup>to rule<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “as the authority of”">aa</a></sup>the night, and the stars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 8:3; Psalm 8:1; Job 38:12"> And God placed them in the vaulted dome<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Or “expanse”">ab</a></sup>of heaven<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Or “the sky”">ac</a></sup>to give light on the earth</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:35; Psalm 19:6"> and to rule over the day and over the night, and to ⸤separate light from darkness⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “make a separation between the light and between the darkness”">ad</a></sup>And God saw that it was good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And there was evening and there was morning, a fourth day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 2:19; Psalm 104:24–25; 1 Kings 4:33"> And God said, “Let the waters swarm with swarms of living creatures, and let birds fly over the earth across the face of the vaulted dome<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Or “expanse”">ae</a></sup>of heaven.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Or “the sky”">af</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 104:24–26; Genesis 1:25; Genesis 8:19"> So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Or “And”">ag</a></sup>God created the great sea creatures and every living creature that moves, with which the waters swarm, according to their kind, and every bird with wings according to its<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Or “their”">ah</a></sup>kind. And God saw that it was good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 40:15; Genesis 8:17; Psalm 107:38"> And God blessed them, saying, “Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the waters in the seas, and let the birds multiply on the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And there was evening, and there was morning, a fifth day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalm 50:9–10; Genesis 6:20; Genesis 8:19"> And God said, “Let the earth bring forth living creatures according to their kind: cattle and moving things, and wild animals<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Literally “animals of the earth/land”">ai</a></sup>according to their kind.” And it was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:5; Job 12:8–10; Genesis 2:19–20"> So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Or “And”">aj</a></sup>God made wild animals<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Literally “animals of the earth/land”">ak</a></sup>according to their kind and the cattle according to their kind, and every creeping thing of the earth according to its<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Or “their”">al</a></sup>kind. And God saw that it was good.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 3:10; Ephesians 4:24; Psalm 8:4–8"> And God said, “Let us make humankind in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Or “as”">am</a></sup>our image and according to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Or “as”">an</a></sup>our likeness, and let them rule over the fish of the sea, and over the birds of heaven,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Or “the sky”">ao</a></sup>and over the cattle, and over all the earth, and over every moving thing that moves upon the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 19:4; Ephesians 2:10; Genesis 5:1–2"> So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Or “And”">ap</a></sup>God created humankind in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Or “as”">aq</a></sup>his image, in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Or “as”">ar</a></sup>the likeness of God he created him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Or “it” collective: humankind">as</a></sup>male and female he created them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 9:1; Genesis 9:7; Leviticus 26:9"> And God blessed them, and God said to them, “Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the earth and subdue it, and rule over the fish of the sea and the birds of heaven,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-at" id="fnref-at" data-fn="Or “the sky”">at</a></sup>and over every animal that moves upon the earth.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 9:3; Psalm 104:14–15; Psalm 145:15–16"> And God said, “Look—I am giving to you every plant that bears seed which is on the face of the whole earth, and every kind of tree ⸤that bears fruit⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-au" id="fnref-au" data-fn="Literally “which in it fruit of a tree bears seed”">au</a></sup>They shall be yours as food.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalm 104:14; Psalm 145:15–16; Psalm 147:9"> And to every kind of animal of the earth and to every bird of heaven,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-av" id="fnref-av" data-fn="Or “the sky”">av</a></sup>and to everything that moves upon the earth in which there is life I am giving every green plant as food.” And it was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:4; Psalm 104:24; Psalm 19:1–2"> And God saw everything that he had made and, behold, it was very good. And there was evening, and there was morning, a sixth day.</span>
</div>
</div>
