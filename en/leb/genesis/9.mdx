## God’s Covenant with Noah and Humankind

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 9:7; Genesis 1:28; Genesis 8:17"> And God blessed Noah and his sons, and said to them, “Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 8:4–8; Genesis 1:28; James 3:7"> And fear of you and dread of you shall be upon every animal of the earth, and on every bird of heaven,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “the sky”">a</a></sup>and on everything that moves upon the ground, and on all the fish of the sea. Into your hand they shall be given.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 14:14; Colossians 2:21–22; 1 Corinthians 10:31"> Every moving thing that lives shall be for you as food. As I gave the green plants to you, I have now given you everything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:23; Deuteronomy 15:23; Deuteronomy 12:16"> Only you shall not eat ⸤raw flesh with blood in it⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “flesh with its life, its blood”">b</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 24:17; Matthew 26:52; Revelation 13:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">“As for the one shedding the blood of humankind,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">by humankind his blood shall be shed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for God made humankind in his own image.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 1:28; Genesis 9:1; Genesis 9:19"> “And you, be fruitful and multiply, swarm on the earth and multiply in it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And God said to Noah and to his sons with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 6:18; Isaiah 54:9–10; Romans 1:3"> “As for me, behold, I am establishing my covenant with you and with your seed after you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 8:1; Genesis 9:15–16; Psalm 36:5–6"> and with every living creature that is with you—the birds, the animals, and every animal of the earth with you, from all that came out of the ark to all the animals of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 54:9; Genesis 8:21–22; Genesis 7:21–23"> I am establishing my covenant with you, that never again will all flesh be cut off by the waters of a flood, nor will there ever be a flood that destroys the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 17:11; Matthew 26:26–28; Exodus 12:13"> And God said, “This is the sign of the covenant that I am making between me and you, and between every living creature that is with you for future generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:28; Revelation 4:3; Revelation 10:1"> My bow I have set in the clouds, and it shall be for a sign of the covenant between me and between the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And when I make clouds appear over the earth the bow shall be seen in the clouds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:9; Ezekiel 16:60; Isaiah 54:8–10"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “And”">f</a></sup>I will remember my covenant that is between me and you, and between every living creature, with all flesh. And the waters of a flood will never again ⸤cause the destruction⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “be to destroy”">g</a></sup>of all flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 54:8–10; Jeremiah 32:40; 2 Samuel 23:5"> The bow shall be in the clouds, and I will see it, so as to remember the everlasting covenant between God and between every living creature, with all flesh that is upon the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And God said to Noah, “This is the sign of the covenant which I am establishing between me and all flesh that is upon the earth.</span>
</div>

## Noah’s Descendants

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 10:1; Genesis 10:6; Genesis 9:25–27"> Now<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “And”">h</a></sup>the sons of Noah who came out of the ark were Shem, Ham, and Japheth. (Ham was the father of Canaan.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:4–28; Genesis 8:17; Genesis 5:32"> These three were the sons of Noah, and from these ⸤the whole earth was populated⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “all the earth branched out”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:6; Proverbs 10:11; Isaiah 28:24–26"> And Noah began to be a man of the ground, and he planted a vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Habakkuk 2:15–16; Proverbs 23:31–32"> And he drank some of the wine and became drunk, and he exposed himself in the midst of his tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 30:17; Galatians 6:1; Matthew 18:15"> And Ham, the father of Canaan, saw the nakedness of his father, and he told his two brothers outside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 6:1; Exodus 20:12; 1 Timothy 5:17"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “And”">j</a></sup>Shem and Japheth took a garment, and the two of them put it on their shoulders and, walking backward, they covered the nakedness of their father. And their faces were turned backward, so that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “and”">k</a></sup>they did not see the nakedness of their father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “And”">l</a></sup>Noah awoke from his drunkenness,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “his wine”">m</a></sup>and he knew what his youngest son had done to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 9:23; Deuteronomy 27:16; Judges 1:28–30"> And he said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Cursed be Canaan,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a slave of slaves he shall be to his brothers.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalm 144:15; Genesis 12:1–3; Luke 3:23–36"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “And”">n</a></sup>he said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Blessed be Yahweh, the God of Shem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and let Canaan be a slave to them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 11:12; Romans 15:12; Hebrews 11:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">May God make space for Japheth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and let him dwell in the tents of Shem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and let Canaan be a slave for him.”</span></span>
</div>
