## Jacob Steals Esau’s Blessing

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:2; Genesis 48:10; Genesis 25:23–25"> And it happened that when Isaac was old and ⸤his eyesight was weak⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “his eyes were weak from seeing”">a</a></sup>he called Esau his older son and said to him, “My son.” And he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="That is, Esau">b</a></sup>said to him, “Here I am.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 4:14; Ecclesiastes 9:10; Proverbs 27:1"> And he said, “Look, I am old; I do not know the day of my death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 25:27–28; Genesis 10:9; 1 Corinthians 6:12"> So now, take your weapons, your quiver and your bow, and go out to the field and hunt food for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 11:20; Genesis 48:9; Genesis 49:28"> Then make for me tasty food like I love, and bring it to me. And I will eat it so that I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “my soul”">c</a></sup>can bless you before I die.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 25:28"> Rebekah said to Jacob her son, “Look, I heard your father speaking to Esau your brother saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 6:26; 1 Samuel 24:19; Deuteronomy 33:1"> ‘Bring wild game to me and prepare tasty food so I can eat it and bless you before Yahweh before my death.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1; Genesis 27:13; Acts 5:29"> So now, my son, listen to my voice, to what I command you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 13:15; 1 Samuel 16:20"> Go to the flock and take two good young goats from it for me, and I will prepare them as tasty food for your father, just as he likes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then you must take it to your father and he will eat it so that he may bless you before his death.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 25:25"> Then Jacob said to his mother, “Behold, Esau my brother is a hairy man, but I am a smooth man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:18; Genesis 25:27; Malachi 1:14"> Perhaps my father will feel me and I will be in his eyes as a mocker, and he will bring upon me a curse and not a blessing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; 1 Samuel 25:24; 2 Samuel 14:9"> Then his mother said to him, “Your curse be upon me, my son, only listen to my voice—go and get them for me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 27:9; Proverbs 23:2–3; Genesis 25:28"> So he went and took them, and brought them to his mother, and his mother prepared tasty food as his father liked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 27:27; Luke 20:46; Luke 15:22"> Then Rebekah took some of her older son Esau’s best garments that were with her in the house, and she put them on Jacob her younger son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And she put the skins of the young goats over his hands and over the smooth part of his neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And she put the tasty food and the bread that she had made into the hand of Jacob, her son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And he went to his father and said, “My father.” And he said, “Here I am. Who are you, my son?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 28:15; Matthew 26:70–74; Zechariah 13:3–4"> And Jacob said to his father, “I am Esau, your firstborn. I have done as you told me. Please get up, sit up and eat from my wild game so that you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “your soul”">d</a></sup>may bless me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:7; Job 13:7; Genesis 24:12"> Then Isaac said to his son, “⸤How⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “what is this?”">e</a></sup>did you find it so quickly, my son?” And he said, “Because Yahweh your God ⸤caused me to find it⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “made it to happen before me”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="James 4:8; Isaiah 57:19; Genesis 27:12"> Then Isaac said to Jacob, “Please, come near and let me feel you, my son. ⸤Are you really⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “are you this one?”">g</a></sup>my son Esau or not?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And Jacob drew near to Isaac his father. And he felt him and said, “The voice is the voice of Jacob, but the hands are the hands of Esau.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 27:16; Hebrews 11:20; Romans 9:11–12"> And he did not recognize him because his hands were hairy like the hands of Esau his brother. And he blessed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Colossians 3:9; Ephesians 4:25; Proverbs 12:22"> And he said, “⸤Are you really⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “are you this one?”">h</a></sup>my son Esau?” And he said, “I am.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 27:4"> Then he said, “Bring it near to me that I may eat from the game of my son, so that I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “my soul”">i</a></sup>may bless you.” And he brought it to him, and he ate. And he brought wine to him, and he drank.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Then his father Isaac said to him, “Come near and kiss me, my son.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 6:7; Psalm 65:10; Hebrews 11:20"> And he drew near and kissed him. And he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="That is, Isaac">j</a></sup>smelled the smell of his garments, and he blessed him and said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Look, the smell of my son is like the smell of a field that Yahweh has blessed!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:28; Deuteronomy 33:13; Psalm 133:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">May God give you of the dew of heaven</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and of the fatness of the earth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and abundance of grain and new wine.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Numbers 24:9; Isaiah 49:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let peoples serve you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and nations bow down to you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Be lord of your brothers,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and may the sons of your mother bow down to you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Cursed be those cursing you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and blessed be those blessing you.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And as soon as Isaac had finished blessing Jacob, ⸤immediately after⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “it was only”">k</a></sup>Jacob had gone out from the presence of Isaac his father, Esau his brother came back from his hunting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 27:4"> He too prepared tasty food and brought it to his father. And he said to his father, “Let my father arise and eat from the wild game of his son, that you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “your soul”">l</a></sup>may bless me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 27:18"> And Isaac his father said to him, “Who are you?” And he said, “I am your son, your firstborn, Esau.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 28:3–4; Romans 11:29; Ephesians 1:3"> Then Isaac ⸤trembled violently⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “trembled a great trembling unto exceeding”">m</a></sup>Then he said, “Who then was he that hunted wild game and brought it to me, and I ate it all before you came, and I blessed him? Moreover, he will be blessed!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hebrews 12:17; Proverbs 1:31; 1 Samuel 30:4"> When Esau heard the words of his father he cried out with a great and exceedingly bitter cry of distress. And he said to his father, “Bless me as well, my father!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:6; Job 13:7; Malachi 2:10"> And he said, “Your brother came in deceit and took your blessing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 25:26; John 1:47; Genesis 32:28"> Then he said, “⸤Isn’t that why he is named Jacob⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “that his name is called Jacob?”">n</a></sup>He has deceived me these two times. He took my birthright and, look, now he has taken my blessing!” Then he said, “Have you not reserved a blessing for me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:14; Genesis 27:28–29; Genesis 25:23"> Then Isaac answered and said to Esau, “Behold, I have made him lord over you and I have given him all his brothers as servants, and with grain and wine I have sustained him. Now what can I do for you, my son?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Hebrews 12:17; Genesis 27:34; Isaiah 65:14"> And Esau said to his father, “Have you only one blessing, my father? Bless me also, my father!” And Esau lifted up his voice and wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 27:28; Hebrews 11:20; Joshua 24:4"> Then Isaac his father answered and said to him,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Your home shall be from</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “away from”">o</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the fatness of the land,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and from the dew of heaven above.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Kings 8:20–22; Genesis 25:23; 2 Chronicles 25:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">But by your sword you shall live,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you shall serve your brother.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But it shall be that when free yourself</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you shall tear off his yoke from your neck.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 John 3:12–15; Genesis 37:4; Genesis 50:3–4"> Then Esau held a grudge against Jacob on account of the blessing with which his father had blessed him. And Esau said in his heart,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="That is, “to himself”">p</a></sup>“The days of mourning for my father are coming, then I will kill Jacob my brother.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Genesis 37:18–20; 1 Samuel 30:5; Genesis 42:21–22"> But the words of Esau her older son were told to Rebekah. And she sent and called for her younger son Jacob. And she said to him, “Look, Esau your brother is consoling himself concerning you, intending to kill you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Genesis 27:8; Genesis 11:31; Genesis 27:13"> Now then, my son, listen to my voice; arise and flee to Haran to Laban my brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Genesis 31:38; Genesis 31:41"> Stay with him a few days until the wrath of your brother has turned—</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Genesis 4:8–16; Genesis 9:5–6"> until the anger of your brother turns from you and he has forgotten what you have done to him. Then I will send and bring you from there. Why should I lose the two of you in one day?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Genesis 24:3; Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 28:8"> Then Rebekah said to Isaac, “I loathe my life because of the Hittite women.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “daughters of the Hittites”">q</a></sup>If Jacob takes a wife from Hittite women<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “daughters of the Hittites”">r</a></sup>like these, from the ⸤native women⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “daughters of the land”">s</a></sup>⸤what am I living for⸥?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “What is life to me?”">t</a></sup></span>
</div>
