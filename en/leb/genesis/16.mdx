## Sarai and Hagar

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 4:24; Genesis 12:16; Genesis 15:2–3"> Now<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “And”">a</a></sup>Sarai, the wife of Abram, had borne him no children. And she had a female Egyptian servant, and her name was Hagar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 21:4; Genesis 18:10; Genesis 3:17"> And Sarai said to Abram, “Look, please, Yahweh has prevented me from bearing children. Please go in to my servant; perhaps ⸤I will have children by her⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “I will be built from her”">b</a></sup>And Abram listened to the voice of Sarai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 16:5; Genesis 12:4–5; 2 Samuel 5:13"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “And”">c</a></sup>Sarai, the wife of Abram, took Hagar, her Egyptian servant, after Abram had lived ten years in the land of Canaan, and gave her to Abram her husband as his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 30:23; 2 Samuel 6:16; 1 Corinthians 13:4–5"> And he went in to Hagar, and she conceived. And when she<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="That is, Hagar">d</a></sup>saw that she had conceived, then her mistress grew small in her eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 31:53; Exodus 5:21; Psalm 7:8"> And Sarai said to Abram, “may my harm be upon you. ⸤I had my servant sleep with you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “I put my servant in your lap”">e</a></sup>and when she saw that she had conceived, ⸤she no longer respected me⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “I grew small in her eyes”">f</a></sup>May Yahweh judge between me and you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:5; Proverbs 14:29; 1 Peter 3:7"> And Abram said to Sarai, “Look, your servant is ⸤under your authority⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “in your hand”">g</a></sup>Do to her that which is good in your eyes.” And Sarai mistreated her, and she<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="That is, Hagar">h</a></sup>fled from her presence.</span>

## Hagar and the Angel of Yahweh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 25:18; Exodus 15:22; Genesis 21:17"> And the angel of Yahweh found her at a spring of water in the wilderness, at the spring by the road of Shur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 3:9; Genesis 4:10; Ecclesiastes 10:4"> And he said to Hagar, the servant of Sarai, “⸤From where⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “where from this”">i</a></sup>have you come, and where are you going?” And she said, “I am fleeing from the presence of Sarai my mistress.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5–6; Ecclesiastes 10:4; Titus 2:9"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “And”">j</a></sup>the angel of Yahweh said to her, “Return to your mistress and submit yourself under ⸤her authority⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “her hand”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 17:20; Psalm 83:6–7; Genesis 25:12–18"> And the angel of Yahweh said to her, “⸤I will greatly multiply⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “multiplying I will multiply”">l</a></sup>your offspring, so that they cannot be counted for their abundance.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 2:23–24; Exodus 3:7; Isaiah 7:14"> And the angel of Yahweh said to her:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Behold, you are pregnant</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and shall have a son.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And you shall call his name Ishmael,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for Yahweh has listened to your suffering.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 25:18; Genesis 21:20; Job 39:5–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">And he shall be a wild donkey of a man,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">his hand will be against everyone,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the hand of everyone will be against him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he will live ⸤in hostility with all his brothers⸥.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “against the face of all his brothers”">m</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 139:1–12; Genesis 32:30; Proverbs 5:21"> So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “And”">n</a></sup>she called the name of Yahweh who spoke to her, “You are El-Roi,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="“God of seeing”">o</a></sup>for she said, “Here I have seen after he who sees me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 24:62; Genesis 25:11; Genesis 21:31"> Therefore the well was called Beer-Lahai-Roi; behold, it is between Kadesh and Bered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 25:12; Genesis 21:9–21; Genesis 17:25–26"> And Hagar had a child for Abram, a son. And Abram called the name of his son whom Hagar bore to him, Ishmael.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Abram was eighty-six years old when Hagar bore Ishmael to Abram.</span>
</div>
