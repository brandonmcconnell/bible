<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 11:8; Nehemiah 9:7; Acts 7:2–6"> And Yahweh said to Abram, “Go out from your land and from your relatives, and from the house of your father, to the land that I will show you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:9; Genesis 18:18; Galatians 3:14"> And I will make you a great nation, and I will bless you, and I will make your name great. And you will be a blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 3:8; Genesis 22:18; Genesis 27:29"> And I will bless those who bless you, and those who curse you I will curse. And all families of the earth will be blessed in you.”</span>

## Abram’s Journey

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 11:8; Genesis 11:31; Genesis 11:27"> And Abram went out as Yahweh had told him, and Lot went with him. Now Abram ⸤was seventy-five years old⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “the son of five years and seventy years”">a</a></sup>when he went out from Haran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 14:14; Genesis 11:31; Hebrews 11:8–9"> And Abram took Sarai his wife, and Lot ⸤his nephew⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “the son of his brother”">b</a></sup>and all their possessions that they had gathered, and all the persons that they had acquired in Haran, and they went out to go to the land of Canaan. And they went to the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 11:9; Deuteronomy 11:30; Genesis 35:4"> And Abram traveled through the land up to the place of Shechem, to the Oak of Moreh. Now the Canaanites were in the land at that time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Genesis 18:1; Genesis 13:15"> And Yahweh appeared to Abram and said, “To your offspring I will give this land.” And he built an altar there to Yahweh, who had appeared to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:31; Genesis 4:26; Genesis 21:33"> And he moved on from there to the hill country, east of Bethel. And he pitched his tent at Bethel on the west, and at Ai on the east. And he built an altar there to Yahweh. And he called on the name of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 13:3; Genesis 24:62; Genesis 13:1"> ⸤And Abram kept moving on⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “And Abram moved on, going and moving on”">c</a></sup>toward the Negev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 43:1; Genesis 26:1–3; 1 Kings 17:1–18"> And there was a famine in the land. And Abram went down to Egypt to dwell as an alien there, for the famine was severe in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 26:7; Genesis 29:17; Genesis 12:14"> And it happened that as he drew near to enter into Egypt, he said to Sarai his wife, “Look now, I know that you are a woman beautiful of appearance,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 20:11; Genesis 26:7; 1 John 1:8–10"> and it shall happen that, if the Egyptians see you, then they will say, ‘This is his wife,’ then they will kill me but let you live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 26:7; Genesis 20:12–13; Genesis 20:2"> Please say you are my sister so that it will go well for me on your account. ⸤Then I will live⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “And my soul will live”">d</a></sup>on account of you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 6:2; Matthew 5:28; Genesis 39:7"> And it happened that as Abram came into Egypt, the Egyptians saw the woman, that she was very beautiful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 20:2; Genesis 41:1; 2 Kings 18:21"> And the officials of Pharaoh saw her, and they praised her beauty to Pharaoh. And the woman was taken to the house of Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 20:14; Genesis 13:2; Genesis 24:35"> And he dealt well with Abram on account of her, and he had sheep, cattle, male donkeys, male servants, female servants, female donkeys, and camels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:21; Genesis 20:18; Psalm 105:14–15"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “And”">e</a></sup>Yahweh afflicted Pharaoh and his household with severe plagues on account of the matter of Sarai the wife of Abram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 20:9–10; Genesis 26:9–11; 1 Samuel 14:43"> Then Pharaoh called for Abram and said, “What is this you have done to me? Why did you not tell me that she was your wife?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Why did you say ‘She is my sister,’ so that I took her to myself as a wife? Now then, here is your wife. Take her and go.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 105:14–15; Proverbs 21:1; Exodus 18:27"> And Pharaoh commanded his men concerning him, and then sent him and his wife and all that was with him away.</span>
