## Jacob Settles in Goshen

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 45:10; Genesis 46:31; Genesis 46:28"> So Joseph went and reported to Pharaoh. And he said, “My father and my brothers, with their flocks and their herds, and all that they have, have come from the land of Canaan. Now they are here in the land of Goshen.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 7:13; Colossians 1:28; Jude 1:24"> And from among his brothers he took five men and presented them before Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 46:33–34; 2 Thessalonians 3:10; Genesis 4:2"> And Pharaoh said to his brothers, “What is your occupation?” And they said to Pharaoh, “Your servants are keepers of sheep, both we and also our ancestors.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “fathers”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 46:34; Genesis 15:13; Deuteronomy 26:5"> And they said to Pharaoh, “We have come to sojourn in the land, for there is no pasture for your servant’s flocks, for the famine is severe in the land of Canaan. So now, please let your servants dwell in the land of Goshen.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Then Pharaoh said to Joseph, “Your father and your brothers have come to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; Proverbs 22:29; Genesis 47:11"> The land of Egypt is before you. Settle your father and your brothers in the best of the land. Let them live in the land of Goshen, and if you know there are among them men of ability, then appoint them overseers of my own livestock.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 47:10; 2 Samuel 8:10; 1 Samuel 2:20"> Then Joseph brought his father Jacob and presented him before Pharaoh. And Jacob blessed Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Then Pharaoh said to Jacob, “⸤How old are you⸥?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “How many are the days of the years of your life?”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 39:12; Psalm 39:5; Job 14:1"> And Jacob said to Pharaoh, “The days of the years of my sojourning are one hundred and thirty years. Few and hard have been the days of the years of my life, and they have not reached the days of the years of the lives of my ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “fathers”">c</a></sup>in the days of their sojourning.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 47:7; Psalm 129:8; Hebrews 7:7"> And Jacob blessed Pharaoh, and he went out from the presence of Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 47:6; Exodus 1:11; Exodus 12:37"> And Joseph settled his father and his brothers, and he gave them property in the land of Egypt in the best part of the land, in the land of Rameses, as Pharaoh had instructed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 45:11; 1 Timothy 5:8; Genesis 47:1"> And Joseph provided his father and his brothers and all the household of his father with food, according to the number of their children.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Famine in Egypt Continues

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 7:11; 1 Kings 18:5; Lamentations 4:9"> Now there was no food in all the land, for the famine was very severe. And the land of Egypt languished, with the land of Canaan, on account of the famine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 41:56; Luke 16:10–12; Luke 16:1–2"> And Joseph collected all the money found in the land of Egypt and in the land of Canaan in exchange for the grain that they were buying. And Joseph brought the money into the house of Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 8:8; Psalm 37:3; Matthew 6:11"> And when the money was spent in the land of Egypt and from the land of Canaan, all of Egypt came to Joseph, saying, “Give us food! Why should we die before you? For the money is used up.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 4:5; Daniel 6:5–7; 1 Corinthians 10:32"> And Joseph said, “Give your livestock and I will give you food in exchange for your livestock if your money is used up.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 2:4; Exodus 9:3; Isaiah 31:1"> So they brought their herds to Joseph, and Joseph gave food to them in exchange for horses, their flocks, and their cattle and donkeys. And he provided them with food in exchange for all their livestock that year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 6:26; Jeremiah 38:9"> When that year ended, they came to him in the following year and said to him, “We cannot hide from my lord that our money and livestock belong to my lord. Nothing remains before my lord except our bodies and our land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:2–3; Job 2:4; Lamentations 1:11"> Why should we die in front of you, both we and our land? Buy us and our land in exchange for food, then we and our land will be servants to Pharaoh. Then give us seed and we shall live and not die, and the land will not become desolate.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> So Joseph bought all the land of Egypt for Pharaoh, for each Egyptian sold his field, for the famine was severe upon them. And the land became Pharaoh’s.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 41:48"> As for the people, he transferred them to the cities, from one end of the territory of Egypt to the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezra 7:24; Nehemiah 13:10; 2 Thessalonians 3:10"> Only the land of the priests he did not buy, for there was an allotment for the priests from Pharaoh, and they ⸤lived on⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “ate”">d</a></sup>the allotment that Pharaoh gave to them. Therefore they did not sell their land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalm 112:5; Psalm 41:1; Ecclesiastes 11:6"> And Joseph said to the people, “Look, I have bought you and your land this day for Pharaoh. Here is seed for you so you can sow the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 41:34; Psalm 41:1; Psalm 112:5"> And it shall happen that at the harvest, you must give a fifth to Pharaoh and four-fifths shall be yours, as seed for the field and for your food and for those who are in your households, and as food for your little ones.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 33:15; Proverbs 11:26–27; Genesis 18:3"> And they said, “You have saved our lives. If we have found favor in the eyes of my lord, we will be servants to Pharaoh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 47:22; Ezekiel 7:24"> So Joseph made it a statute unto this day concerning the land of Egypt: one fifth to Pharaoh. Only the land of the priests alone did not belong to Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 46:3; Exodus 1:7; Acts 7:17"> So Israel settled in the land of Egypt, in the land of Goshen. And they acquired possessions in it and were fruitful and multiplied greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalm 90:10; Genesis 47:8–9; Genesis 37:2"> And Jacob lived in the land of Egypt seventeen years. And the days of Jacob, the years of his life, were one hundred and forty-seven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:14; Genesis 24:49; Genesis 24:2"> When ⸤the time of Israel’s death drew near⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “the days of Israel drew near to die”">e</a></sup>he called to his son, to Joseph. And he said to him, “If I have found favor in your eyes, please put your hand under my thigh, that you might vow to deal kindly<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “loyal love”">f</a></sup>and faithfully with me. Please do not bury me in Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 49:29–32; Genesis 23:19; Genesis 25:9"> but let me lie with my ancestors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “fathers”">g</a></sup>Carry me out of Egypt and bury me in their burial site.” And he said, “I will do according to your word.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 1:47; Hebrews 11:21; Genesis 24:3"> Then he said, “Swear to me.” And he swore to him. Then Israel bowed himself on the head of the bed.</span>
