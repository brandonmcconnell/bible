## The Flood Subsides

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 30:22; Genesis 19:29; Psalm 136:23"> And God remembered Noah and all the wild animals, and all the domesticated animals that were with him in the ark. And God caused a wind to blow<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “go”">a</a></sup>over the earth, and the waters subsided.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 7:11; Matthew 8:26–27; Proverbs 8:28"> And the fountains of the deep and the windows of the heavens<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “the sky”">b</a></sup>were closed, and the rain from the heavens<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “the sky”">c</a></sup>was restrained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 7:24; Genesis 7:11"> And the waters receded from the earth ⸤gradually⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “going and returning”">d</a></sup>and the waters abated at the end of one hundred and fifty days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 19:37; Jeremiah 51:27; Isaiah 37:38"> And the ark came to rest in the seventh month, on the seventeenth day of the month, on the mountains of Ararat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 7:11"> And the waters ⸤continued to recede⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “going and receding”">e</a></sup>to the tenth month; in the tenth month, on the first of the month, the tops of the mountains appeared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 6:16; Daniel 6:10"> And it happened that at the end of forty days Noah opened the window of the ark that he had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 147:9; 1 Kings 17:6; 1 Kings 17:4"> And he sent out a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “the”">f</a></sup>raven;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “crow”">g</a></sup>⸤it went to and fro⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “it went out, going out and returning”">h</a></sup>until the waters were dried up from upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:14; Song of Solomon 1:15; Matthew 10:16"> And ⸤he sent out a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “the”">i</a></sup>dove⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “and he sent out a dove from him”">j</a></sup>to see whether the waters had subsided from upon the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 11:28; Psalm 116:7; Deuteronomy 28:65"> But the dove did not find a resting place for the sole of her foot, and she returned to him into the ark, for the waters were still on the face of the earth. And he stretched out his hand and took her, and brought her to himself into the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; Genesis 7:10; Genesis 8:12"> And he waited another seven days, and ⸤again he sent out⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “he added to send”">k</a></sup>the dove from the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:15; Zechariah 4:12–14; Romans 10:15"> And the dove came to him ⸤in the evening⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “at the time of the evening”">l</a></sup>and behold, a freshly-picked olive tree leaf was in her mouth. And Noah knew that the waters had subsided from upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 25:9; Isaiah 30:18; Genesis 8:10"> And he waited ⸤seven more days⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “again another seven days”">m</a></sup>and he sent out the dove. But it did not return again to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 7:11"> And it happened that, in the six hundred and first year, in the first month, on the first day of the month, the waters dried up from upon the earth. And Noah removed the covering of the ark and looked. And behold, the face of the ground was dried up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 7:13–14; Genesis 7:11"> And in the second month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, the earth was dry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And God spoke to Noah, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 7:13; Psalm 121:8; Psalm 91:11"> “Go out from the ark, you and your wife, and your sons, and your sons’ wives with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 1:22; Genesis 9:1; Jeremiah 31:27–28"> Bring out with you all the living things which are with you, from all the living creatures—birds, and animals, and everything that creeps on the earth, and let them swarm on the earth and be fruitful and multiply on the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 121:8"> So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “And”">n</a></sup>Noah went out, with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “and”">o</a></sup>his sons and his wife, and the wives of his sons with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Every animal, every creeping thing, and every bird, and everything that moves upon the earth, according to its families, went out from the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15–16; Romans 12:1; Genesis 22:9"> And Noah built an altar to Yahweh, and he took from all the clean animals and from all the clean birds, and offered burnt offerings on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:15; Ephesians 5:2; Isaiah 54:9–10"> And Yahweh smelled the soothing fragrance, and Yahweh said ⸤to himself⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “to his heart”">p</a></sup>“⸤Never again will I curse⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “I will not add to curse again”">q</a></sup>the ground for the sake of humankind, because the inclination of the heart of humankind is evil from his youth. ⸤Nor will I ever again destroy⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “And I will not add again to destroy”">r</a></sup>all life as I have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 5:7; Psalm 74:16–17; Jeremiah 5:24"> ⸤As long as the earth endures⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “While all the days of the earth”">s</a></sup>seed and harvest, cold and heat, summer and winter, day and night will not cease.</span>
