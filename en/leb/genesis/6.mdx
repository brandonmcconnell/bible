## Prelude to the Flood

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 1:28"> And it happened that, when humankind began to multiply on the face of the ground, daughters were born to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 82:6–7; 1 John 2:16; 2 Peter 2:14"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “And”">a</a></sup>the sons of God saw the daughters of humankind, that they were beautiful. And they took for themselves wives from all that they chose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:30; Isaiah 63:10; Jeremiah 11:11"> And Yahweh said, “My Spirit shall not abide with humankind forever in that he is also flesh. And his days shall be one hundred and twenty years.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 13:33; 2 Samuel 21:15–22; 1 Samuel 17:4"> The Nephilim were upon the earth in those days, and also afterward, when the sons of God went into the daughters of humankind, and they bore children to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 15:19; Romans 1:28–31; Mark 7:21–23"> And Yahweh saw that the evil of humankind was great upon the earth, and every inclination of the thoughts of his heart was always<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “every day”">b</a></sup>only evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 63:10; 1 Samuel 15:11; Ephesians 4:30"> And Yahweh regretted that he had made humankind on the earth, and ⸤he was grieved in his heart⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “he was grieved to his heart”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:3; Psalm 37:20; Jeremiah 12:3–4"> And Yahweh said, “I will destroy humankind whom I created from upon the face of the earth, from humankind, to animals, to creeping things, and to the birds of heaven,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “the sky”">d</a></sup>for I regret that I have made them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 19:19; Hebrews 4:16; Luke 1:30"> But Noah found favor in the eyes of Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 1:6; Hebrews 11:7; 2 Peter 2:5"> These are the generations<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “family records”">e</a></sup>of Noah. Noah was a righteous man, without defect in his generations. Noah walked with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 5:32"> And Noah fathered three sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:17; Hosea 4:1–2; Habakkuk 2:8"> And the earth was corrupted before God, and the earth was filled with violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 53:2–3; Proverbs 15:3; Psalm 14:1–3"> And God saw the earth, and behold, it was corrupt, for all flesh had corrupted its way upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 4:1–2; 1 Peter 4:7; Ezekiel 7:2–6"> And God said to Noah, “The end of all flesh has come before me, for the earth was filled with violence because of them. Now, look, I am going to destroy them along with the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 3:20; Luke 17:27; Matthew 24:38"> Make for yourself an ark of cypress wood; you must make the ark with rooms, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “and”">f</a></sup>you must cover it with pitch, inside and outside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:11; Genesis 7:20"> And this is how you must make it: the length of the ark, three hundred cubits; its width fifty cubits; its height, thirty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 8:6; Genesis 7:16; Ezekiel 41:16"> You must make a roof for the ark, and ⸤finish it to a cubit above⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “to one cubit you must finish it from above”">g</a></sup>And as for the door of the ark, you must put it in its side. You must make it with a lower, second, and a third deck.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; 2 Peter 2:5; Genesis 7:21–23"> And I, behold, I am about to bring the flood waters over the earth to destroy all flesh in which is the breath of life from under the heaven;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “the sky”">h</a></sup>everything that is on the earth shall perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 11:7; Genesis 7:7; Genesis 17:7"> And I will establish my covenant with you, and you must go into the ark—you, and your sons, and your wife, and the wives of your sons with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 7:15–16; Genesis 7:8–9; Genesis 8:17"> And of every living thing, from all flesh, you must bring two from every kind into the ark to keep them alive with you; they shall be male and female.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 10:11–12; Genesis 2:19; Genesis 7:8–16"> From the birds according to their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “its”">i</a></sup>kind, and from the animals according to their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “its”">j</a></sup>kind, from every creeping thing on the ground according to its<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “their”">k</a></sup>kind—two from every kind shall come to you to keep them alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 1:29–30; Psalm 145:16; Psalm 136:25"> And as for you, take for yourself from every kind of food that is eaten. And you must gather it to yourself. And it shall be for you and for them for food.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 40:16; John 15:14; Matthew 7:24–27"> And Noah did according to all that God commanded him; thus he did.</span>
</div>
</div>
