## Abram and Circumcision, the Sign of the Covenant

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Ephesians 3:20; Luke 1:6"> When<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “And it happened that”">a</a></sup>Abram was ninety-nine years old Yahweh appeared to Abram. And he said to him, “I am El-Shaddai;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Often translated, “God Almighty”">b</a></sup>walk before me and be blameless</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 17:4–6; Genesis 12:2; Psalm 105:8–11"> so that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “and”">c</a></sup>I may make my covenant between me and you, and may multiply you ⸤exceedingly⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “with very very”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 13:20; Genesis 17:17; Numbers 16:45"> Then Abram fell upon his face and God spoke with him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 35:11; Galatians 3:28–29; Genesis 12:2"> “As for me, behold, my covenant shall be with you, and you shall be the father of a multitude of nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 1:21–23; Nehemiah 9:7; Genesis 17:15"> Your name shall no longer be called Abram, but your name shall be Abraham, for I will make you the father of a multitude of nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 17:4; Matthew 1:6–17; Genesis 35:11"> And I will make you ⸤exceedingly⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “with very very”">e</a></sup>fruitful. I will make you a nation, and kings shall go out from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 9:7–9; Acts 2:39; Genesis 26:24"> And I will establish my covenant between me and you, and between your offspring after you, throughout their generations as an everlasting covenant to be as God for you and to your offspring after you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 26:12; Psalm 105:11; Exodus 6:7"> And I will give to you and to your offspring after you ⸤the land in which you are living as an alien⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “a land of your sojournings”">f</a></sup>all the land of Canaan, as an everlasting property. And I will be to them as God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 56:4–5; Psalm 25:10; Psalm 103:18"> And God said to Abraham, “Now as for you, you must keep my covenant, you and your offspring after you, throughout their generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 2:11–12; Romans 4:9–11; Acts 7:8"> This is my covenant which you shall keep, between me and you, and also with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “between”">g</a></sup>your offspring after you: Every male among you shall be circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 4:11; Acts 7:8; Deuteronomy 10:16"> And you shall circumcise the flesh of your foreskin, and it shall be a sign of the covenant between me and you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 12:3; Luke 2:21; Exodus 12:48–49"> And ⸤at eight days of age⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “a son of eight days”">h</a></sup>you shall yourselves circumcise every male belonging to your generations and ⸤the servant born in your house and the one bought from any foreigner⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “those born of house and acquisition of money from every son of a foreigner”">i</a></sup>who is not from your offspring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 12:44; Genesis 37:36; Nehemiah 5:8"> ⸤You must certainly circumcise⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “circumcising you will circumcise”">j</a></sup>⸤the servant born in your house and the one bought from any foreigner⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “those born of house and acquisition of money from every son of a foreigner”">k</a></sup>And my covenant shall be with your flesh as an everlasting covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 4:24–26; Numbers 15:30–31; Exodus 12:19"> And as for any uncircumcised male who has not circumcised the flesh of his foreskin, that person shall be cut off from his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “her”; the reference is still to a male; the pronoun is feminine because “person” (nephesh ) is grammatically feminine">l</a></sup>people. He has broken my covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:25; Genesis 32:28; Genesis 17:5"> And God said to Abraham, “as for Sarai your wife, you shall not call her name Sarai, for Sarah shall be her name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 35:11; Genesis 17:6; Isaiah 49:23"> And I will bless her; moreover, I give to you from her a son. And I will bless her, and ⸤she shall give rise to nations⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “she shall become to nations”">m</a></sup>Kings of peoples shall come<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “be”">n</a></sup>from her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 11:16; Genesis 21:6; Genesis 18:12"> And Abraham fell upon his face and laughed. And he said in his heart, “⸤Can a child be born to a man a hundred years old⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “to a man one hundred years old can he be born?”">o</a></sup>or ⸤can Sarah bear a child at ninety⸥?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “can ninety-year-old Sarah bear a child?”">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 2:39; Psalm 41:12; Psalm 4:6"> And Abraham said to God, “Oh that Ishmael might live before you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 1:13–20; Genesis 21:2–3; 2 Kings 4:16–17"> And God said, “No, but Sarah your wife shall bear a son for you, and you shall call his name Isaac. And I will establish my covenant with him as an everlasting covenant to his offspring after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 21:18; Genesis 21:13; Genesis 25:12–18"> And as for Ishmael, I have heard you. Behold, I will bless him and I will make him fruitful, and I will multiply him ⸤exceedingly⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “with very very”">q</a></sup>He shall father twelve princes, and I will make him a great nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 21:2–3; Genesis 26:2–5; Galatians 3:29"> But my covenant I will establish with Isaac, whom Sarah shall bear to you at this appointed time next year.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 18:33; Genesis 35:9–15; Judges 6:21"> When<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “And”">r</a></sup>he finished speaking with him, God went up from Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalm 119:60; Genesis 18:19; Genesis 17:10–14"> And Abraham took Ishmael his son and all who were born of his house, and all those acquired by his money, every male among the men of Abraham’s house, and he circumcised the flesh of their foreskin on the same day that God spoke with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 4:11; Genesis 17:17; Romans 4:19–20"> Abraham was ninety-nine years old when he circumcised the flesh of his foreskin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And Ishmael his son was thirteen years old when he circumcised the flesh of his foreskin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalm 119:60; Genesis 22:3–4; Genesis 12:4"> Abraham and his son Ishmael were circumcised on the same day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 18:19"> And all the men of his house, ⸤those born in the house, and those acquired by money from a foreigner⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “born of house and acquisition of money from the son of a foreigner”">s</a></sup>were circumcised with him.</span>
</div>
