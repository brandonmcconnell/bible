## Yahweh’s Covenant with Abram

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Psalm 119:114; Psalm 84:11"> After these things the word of Yahweh came<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “was”">a</a></sup>to Abram in a vision, saying: “Do not be afraid, Abram; I am your shield, and your reward shall be very great.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 25:21; Acts 7:5; Genesis 12:1–3"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “And”">b</a></sup>Abram said, “O Yahweh, my Lord, what will you give me? ⸤I continue to be⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “I am going”">c</a></sup>childless, and ⸤my heir⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “the son of the inheritance of my house”">d</a></sup>is Eliezer of Damascus.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 14:14; Proverbs 29:21; Ecclesiastes 2:7"> And Abram said, “Look, you have not given me a descendant, and here, ⸤a member of my household⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “a son of my house”">e</a></sup>is ⸤my heir⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “inheriting me”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 17:16; Galatians 4:28; 2 Samuel 7:12"> And behold, the word of Yahweh came to him saying, “This person will not ⸤be your heir⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “inherit you”">g</a></sup>but ⸤your own son will be your heir⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “he who goes out from your body, he will inherit you”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:12; Romans 4:18; Deuteronomy 1:10"> And he brought him outside and said, “Look toward the heavens and count the stars if you are able to count them.” And he said to him, “So shall your offspring be.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 2:23; Galatians 3:6–14; Romans 4:9"> And he believed in Yahweh, and he reckoned it to him as righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 12:1; Acts 7:2–4; Romans 4:13"> And he said to him, “I am Yahweh, who brought you out from Ur of the Chaldeans to give this land to you, to possess it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 1:18; Luke 1:34; Isaiah 7:11"> And he said, “O Yahweh God, how shall I know that I will possess it?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 2:24; Leviticus 14:30; Leviticus 14:22"> And he said to him, “Take for me a three-year-old heifer, and a three-year-old female goat, and a three-year-old ram, and a turtledove and a young pigeon.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:18–19; Leviticus 1:17; Genesis 15:17"> And he took for him all these and cut them in pieces down the middle. And he put each piece opposite ⸤the other⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “its companion”">i</a></sup>but the birds he did not cut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 13:4; Psalm 119:13; Ezekiel 17:3"> And the birds of prey came down on the carcasses, but Abram drove them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 2:21; Job 33:15; Daniel 10:8–9"> And it happened, as the sun ⸤went down⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “to go”">j</a></sup>then a deep sleep fell upon Abram and, behold, a great terrifying darkness fell upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 7:6–7; Exodus 1:11; Exodus 12:40–41"> And he said to Abram, “⸤You must surely know⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “knowing you must know”">k</a></sup>that your descendants shall be as aliens in a land ⸤not their own⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “not to them”">l</a></sup>And they shall serve them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Some nation hostile to Abram’s descendants (Egypt in later history)">m</a></sup>and they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Some nation hostile to Abram’s descendants (Egypt in later history)">n</a></sup>shall oppress them four hundred years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:9–11; Exodus 6:5–6; Exodus 12:32–38"> And also the nation that they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="That is, Abram’s descendants">o</a></sup>serve I will judge. Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “And”">p</a></sup>afterward they shall go out with great possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 5:26; Genesis 25:7–9; 2 Chronicles 34:28"> And as for you, you shall go to your ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “fathers”">q</a></sup>in peace; you shall be buried in a good old age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 21:26; Daniel 8:23; Exodus 12:40"> And the fourth generation shall return here, for the guilt of the Amorites ⸤is not yet complete⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “is not complete up to now”">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:18–19; Judges 6:21; Jeremiah 11:4"> And after the sun had gone down and it was dusk, behold, a smoking firepot and a flaming torch passed between those half pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 1:3–4; Genesis 12:7; Genesis 24:7"> On that day Yahweh ⸤made⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “cut”">s</a></sup>a covenant with Abram saying, “To your offspring I will give this land, from the river of Egypt to the great river, the Euphrates river,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 24:21–22"> the land of the Kenites, the Kenizzites, the Kadmonites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 17:5; Genesis 14:5"> the Hittites, the Perizzites, the Rephaim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 10:15–19; Exodus 23:23–28; Deuteronomy 7:1"> the Amorites, the Canaanites, the Girgashites, and the Jebusites.”</span>
