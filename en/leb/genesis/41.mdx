<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 19:6"> And behold, seven cows, ⸤well built and fat⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “beautiful of appearance and healthy of flesh”">b</a></sup>were coming up from the Nile, and they grazed among the reeds.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 40:5–8"> And we dreamed a dream one night, I and he, ⸤each with a dream that had a meaning⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “each according to his dream we dreamed”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 40:12–19; Genesis 37:36; Genesis 39:1"> And there with us was a young man, a Hebrew servant of the chief of the guard, and we told him the dream, and he interpreted our dreams for us, each according to his dream he interpreted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:3; Genesis 40:20–22; Genesis 40:12"> And it happened just as he interpreted to us, so it was. He<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="That is, Pharaoh">j</a></sup>restored me to my office, and him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="That is, the chief baker">k</a></sup>he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="That is, Pharaoh">l</a></sup>hanged.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 2:25; Psalm 113:7–8; Psalm 105:19–22"> Then Pharaoh sent and called for Joseph, and they brought him quickly from the prison. And he shaved and changed his clothing, and came to Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 5:16; Daniel 5:12; Psalm 25:14"> Then Pharaoh said to Joseph, “I dreamed a dream, but there is none to interpret it. Now, I have heard concerning you that when you hear a dream you can interpret it.”</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Pharaoh said to Joseph, “Now in my dream, behold, I was standing on the bank of the Nile,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:8; Jeremiah 24:1–3; Jeremiah 24:5"> and behold, seven cows, ⸤well built and fat⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “beautiful of appearance and healthy of flesh”">n</a></sup>were coming up from the Nile, and they grazed among the reeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And behold, seven other cows came up after them from the Nile, very ⸤ugly and gaunt⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “Poor of appearance and thin of flesh”">o</a></sup>—never have I seen any as them in all the land of Egypt for ugliness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And the thin and ugly cows ate the former seven healthy cows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:3; Psalm 37:19; Revelation 10:9–10"> But when they went into their bellies<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “inner parts”">p</a></sup>it could not be known that they went into their bellies,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “inner parts”">q</a></sup>for their appearance was as ugly as at the beginning. Then I awoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Then I saw in my dream and behold, seven ears of grain were coming out of one stalk, full and good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalm 129:6–7; Hosea 13:15; 2 Kings 19:26"> And behold, seven withered ears of grain, thin and scorched by the east wind, sprouted up after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 4:7; Genesis 41:8; Exodus 8:19"> And the thin ears of grain swallowed up the seven good ears of grain. <span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 2:45; Revelation 4:1; Ephesians 1:17"> Then Joseph said to Pharaoh, “The dreams of Pharaoh are one. God has revealed to Pharaoh what he is about to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 41:47; Genesis 41:2; Genesis 41:53"> The seven good cows, they are seven years, and the seven good ears of grain, they are seven years. The dreams are one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 8:1; 2 Samuel 24:19"> And the seven thin and ugly cows coming up after them, they are seven years, and the seven empty ears of grain, scorched by the east wind, they are also seven years of famine.</span>

<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 41:26; Genesis 41:46–47; Genesis 41:49"> Behold, seven years of great abundance are coming throughout the whole land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 47:13; Genesis 41:54; James 5:17"> Then seven years of famine will arise after them, and all the abundance in the land of Egypt will be forgotten. The famine will consume the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 24:20; 1 Samuel 5:6"> Abundance in the land will not be known because of the famine ⸤that follows⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “that thus afterwards”">s</a></sup>for it will be very heavy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Isaiah 46:10–11; Isaiah 14:24–27"> Now concerning the repetition of the dream twice to Pharaoh, it is because the matter is established by God, and God will do it quickly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 41:39; Acts 6:3; Deuteronomy 1:13"> Now then, let Pharaoh select a man who is discerning and wise, and let him set him over the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 22:3; Luke 16:5; Proverbs 6:6–8"> Let Pharaoh do this, and let him appoint supervisors over the land, and let him take one-fifth from the land of Egypt in the seven years of abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 45:6–7; Genesis 41:56; Exodus 4:13"> Then let them gather all the food of these coming good years and let them pile up grain under the hand of Pharaoh for food in the cities, and let them keep it.</span>

## Joseph Rises to Power

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 7:10; 1 Kings 21:2; 2 Samuel 3:36"> And the plan<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Or “word”">t</a></sup>was good in the eyes of Pharaoh and in the eyes of all his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Job 32:8; Daniel 5:14; Numbers 27:18"> Then Pharaoh said to his servants, “Can we find a man like this in whom is the spirit of God?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 41:33; Genesis 41:28; Genesis 41:25"> Then Pharaoh said to Joseph, “Since God has made all of this known to you there is no one as discerning and wise as you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Proverbs 22:29; Acts 7:10; Psalm 105:21–22"> You shall be over my house, and to your word<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “mouth”">u</a></sup>all my people shall submit. Only with respect to the throne will I be greater than you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 28:18; Philippians 2:9–11; Genesis 42:6"> Then Pharaoh said to Joseph, “See, I have set you over all the land of Egypt.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Daniel 5:29; Daniel 5:7; Esther 3:10"> Then Pharaoh removed his signet ring from his finger and put it on the finger of Joseph. And he clothed him with garments of fine linen, and he put a chain of gold around his neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Acts 7:10; Genesis 45:8; Genesis 45:26"> And he had him ride in his second chariot. And they cried out before him, “Kneel!” And Pharaoh set him over all the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalm 105:22; Exodus 11:7"> Then Pharaoh said to Joseph, “I am Pharaoh, but without your consent no one will lift his hand or his foot in all the land of Egypt.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Genesis 46:20; Ezekiel 30:17; Exodus 2:16"> And Pharaoh called the name of Joseph Zaphenath-paneah and gave him Asenath, the daughter of Potiphera, priest of On, as a wife. And Joseph went out over the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Daniel 1:19; 1 Kings 12:6; 1 Samuel 16:21"> Now Joseph ⸤was thirty years old⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “a son of thirty years”">v</a></sup>when he stood before Pharaoh, the king of Egypt. And Joseph went out from the presence of Pharaoh and traveled through the whole land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Genesis 26:12; Psalm 72:16"> And the land produced a plenty in the seven years of abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Genesis 41:34–36; Genesis 47:21"> And he gathered all the food of the seven years which occurred in the land of Egypt. And he stored the food in the cities. The food of the field that surrounded each city he stored in its midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Psalm 78:27; Judges 6:5; Genesis 22:17"> And Joseph piled up grain like the sand of the sea in great abundance until he stopped counting it, for ⸤it could not be counted⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “there was no number”">w</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Genesis 46:20; Genesis 48:5; 2 Samuel 8:18"> Before the years of famine came, Asenath, daughter of Potiphera priest of On, bore two sons to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Psalm 30:5; Proverbs 31:7; Deuteronomy 33:17"> And Joseph called the name of the firstborn Manasseh, for he said, “God has caused me to forget all my hardship and all my father’s house.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Genesis 49:22; Genesis 50:23; Genesis 17:6"> And the name of the second he called Ephraim, for he said, “God has made me fruitful in the land of my misfortune.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Genesis 41:29–31; Psalm 73:20; Luke 16:25"> And the seven years of abundance which were in the land of Egypt came to an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Genesis 45:11; Acts 7:11; Psalm 105:16"> And the seven years of famine began to come as Joseph had said. And there was famine in all of the countries, but in the land of Egypt there was food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Matthew 17:5; Matthew 3:17; Jeremiah 14:1–6"> And when all the land of Egypt was hungry the people cried out to Pharaoh for food. And Pharaoh said to all the land of Egypt, “Go to Joseph; what he says to you, you must do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Genesis 42:6; Zechariah 5:3; Luke 21:35"> And the famine was over the whole land, and Joseph opened all the storehouses<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Hebrew “that which was in them”">x</a></sup>and sold food to the Egyptians. And the famine was severe in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Genesis 41:54; Genesis 42:5; Genesis 41:56"> And every land came to Egypt to Joseph to buy grain, for the famine was severe in every land.</span>
