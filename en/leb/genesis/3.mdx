## The Fall

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:3; 2 Corinthians 11:14; Revelation 12:9"> Now the serpent was more crafty than any other ⸤wild animal⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “animal of the field”">a</a></sup>which Yahweh God had made. He said to the woman, “Did God indeed say, ‘You shall not eat from any tree in the garden’?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 58:4"> The woman said to the serpent, “From the fruit of the trees of the garden we may eat,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 2:16–17; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Exodus 19:12–13"> but from the tree that is in the midst of the garden, God said, ‘You shall not eat from it, nor shall you touch it, lest you die’.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 8:44; 2 Corinthians 11:3; 1 Timothy 2:14"> But the serpent said to the woman, “You shall not surely die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:2; 2 Thessalonians 2:4; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15"> For God knows that on the day you both eat from it, then your eyes will be opened and you both shall be like gods,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The plural is in the context of v. 22 “one of us” and the plural suffix pronouns ⸤“you all ”⸥ throughout the verse">b</a></sup>knowing good and evil.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 John 2:16; James 1:14–15; 1 Timothy 2:14"> When<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “And”">c</a></sup>the woman saw that the tree was good for food and that it was a delight to the eyes, and the tree was desirable to make one wise, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “and”">d</a></sup>she took from its fruit and she ate. And she gave it also to her husband with her, and he ate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 59:6; Genesis 2:25; Genesis 3:5"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “And”">e</a></sup>the eyes of both of them were opened, and they knew that they were naked. And they sewed together fig leaves and they made for themselves coverings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:24; Proverbs 15:3; Hebrews 4:13"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “And”">f</a></sup>they heard the sound of Yahweh God walking in the garden ⸤at the windy time of day⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “at the wind of the day”">g</a></sup>And the man<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="“The man” indicates the noun is singular and occurs with the definite article">h</a></sup>and his wife hid themselves from the presence of Yahweh God among the trees of the garden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 4:9; Genesis 16:8; Genesis 18:20–21"> And Yahweh God called to the man<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="“The man” indicates the noun is singular and occurs with the definite article">i</a></sup>and said to him, “Where are you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 2:25; Psalm 119:120; Isaiah 47:3"> And he replied,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “And he said”; “replied” distinguishes Adam as the speaker">j</a></sup>“I heard the sound of you in the garden, and I was afraid because I am naked, so I hid myself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 3:20; Genesis 4:10; Psalm 50:21"> Then he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="That is, Yahweh God">k</a></sup>asked,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “said”">l</a></sup>“Who told you that you were naked? Have you eaten from ⸤the tree from which I forbade you to eat⸥?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “the tree which I commanded to not eat from it”">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; James 1:13–15; Job 31:33"> And the man<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="“The man” indicates the noun is singular and occurs with the definite article">n</a></sup>replied,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “said”">o</a></sup>“The woman whom you gave to be with me—she gave to me from the tree and I ate.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:3; 1 Timothy 2:14; Genesis 4:10–12"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “And”">p</a></sup>Yahweh God said to the woman, “What is this you have done?” And the woman said, “The serpent deceived me, and I ate.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Micah 7:17; Isaiah 65:25; Psalm 72:9"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “And”">q</a></sup>Yahweh God said to the serpent,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Because you have done this,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you will be cursed</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">more than any domesticated animal</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and more than any wild animal.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “animal of the earth/land”">r</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">On your belly you shall go</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and dust you shall eat</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">all the days of your life.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 16:20; 1 John 3:8; Revelation 12:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I will put hostility</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">between you and between the woman,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and between your offspring</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “seed”">s</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and between her offspring;</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “seed”">t</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he will strike you on the head,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you will strike him on the heel.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Titus 2:5; Colossians 3:18; 1 Corinthians 11:3"> To the woman he said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will greatly increase</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">⸤your pain in childbearing⸥;</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “your pain and your childbearing”">u</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in pain you shall bear children.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And to your husband shall be your desire.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And he shall rule over you.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 8:20–22; Genesis 5:29; Isaiah 24:5–6"> And to Adam<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="The noun lacks the definite article and is taken as a proper noun in this context">v</a></sup>he said, “Because you listened to the voice of your wife and you ate from the tree ⸤from which I forbade you to eat⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “from the tree which I commanded saying not to eat from it”">w</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the ground shall be cursed on your account.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">In pain you shall eat from it</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">all the days of your life.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 13:7; Job 5:5; Isaiah 7:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">And thorns and thistles shall sprout for you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you shall eat the plants of the field.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:7; Job 34:15; Psalm 104:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">By the sweat of your brow</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “your face”">x</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you shall eat bread,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">until your return to the ground.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">For from it you were taken;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for you are dust,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and to dust you shall return.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 2:23; Matthew 1:23; Genesis 5:29"> And the man<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="“The man” indicates the noun is singular and occurs with the definite article">y</a></sup>⸤named⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “called the name”">z</a></sup>his wife Eve, because she was the mother of all life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; 2 Corinthians 5:21; 2 Corinthians 5:2–3"> And Yahweh God made for Adam<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="The noun lacks the definite article and is taken as a proper noun in this context">aa</a></sup>and for his wife garments of skin, and he clothed them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 2:7; Revelation 22:14; Proverbs 3:18"> And Yahweh God said, “Look—the man has become as one of us, to know good and evil. ⸤What if⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “And now lest”">ab</a></sup>he stretches out his hand and takes also from the tree of life and eats, and lives forever?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 4:2; Genesis 3:19; Genesis 4:12"> And Yahweh God sent him out from the garden of Eden, to till the ground from which he was taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 1:7; Psalm 104:4; Exodus 25:18–22"> So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Or “And”">ac</a></sup>he drove the man out, and placed cherubim east of the garden of Eden, and ⸤a flaming, turning sword⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “a flame of the sword which was turning”">ad</a></sup>to guard the way to the tree of life.</span>
</div>
