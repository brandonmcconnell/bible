## The Parting of Abram and Lot

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 12:9–20; Joshua 10:40; 2 Samuel 24:7"> Then Abram went up from Egypt, he and his wife and all that was with him. And Lot went with him to the Negev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 24:35; Psalm 112:1–3; Genesis 26:12–13"> Now Abram was very wealthy in livestock, in silver, and in gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 12:8–9; Genesis 12:6"> And he went according to his journey from the Negev, then to Bethel, to the place where his tent was at the beginning, between Bethel and Ai,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:12; Genesis 12:7–8; Isaiah 58:9"> to the place where he had made an altar at the beginning. And Abram called on the name of Yahweh there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:29; Genesis 25:27; Genesis 4:20"> And Lot, who went with Abram, also had herds and tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 36:6–7; Ecclesiastes 5:10–11; 1 Timothy 6:9"> And the land could not ⸤support them⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “lift them up”">a</a></sup>so as to live together, because their possessions were so many that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “and”">b</a></sup>they were not able to live together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 26:20; Genesis 12:6; James 3:16"> And there was a quarrel between the herdsmen of the livestock of Abram and the herdsmen of the livestock of Lot. Now at that time the Canaanites and the Perizzites were living in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:6–7; Acts 7:26; Psalm 133:1"> Then Abram said to Lot, “Please, let there not be quarreling between me and you, and between my shepherds and your shepherds, for we men are brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 20:15; Genesis 34:10; 1 Peter 3:8–12"> Is not the whole land before you? Separate yourself from me. If you want what is on the left, then I will go right; if you want what is on the right, I will go left.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:3; Isaiah 51:3; Genesis 14:8"> And Lot lifted up his eyes and saw the whole plain of the Jordan, that all of it was well-watered land—this was before Yahweh destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah—like the garden of Yahweh, like the land of Egypt ⸤in the direction of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “your going”">c</a></sup>Zoar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 13:9; 1 Peter 2:17; Hebrews 10:25"> So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “And”">d</a></sup>Lot chose for himself all the plain of the Jordan. And Lot journeyed from the east, and so they separated ⸤from each other⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “each from his brother”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 19:29; Genesis 14:12; Genesis 19:1"> Abram settled in the land of Canaan, and Lot settled in the cities of the plain. And he pitched his tent toward Sodom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Peter 2:10; 2 Peter 2:6–8; Jude 1:7"> Now the men of Sodom ⸤were extremely wicked sinners against Yahweh⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “were wicked and sinners against Yahweh very”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 49:18; Deuteronomy 3:27; Genesis 28:14"> And Yahweh said to Abram after Lot had separated from him, “Now, lift up your eyes and look from the place where you are to the north, and to the south, and to the east and to the west,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:7; Genesis 35:12; Genesis 15:18"> for all the land which you see I will give to you, and to your descendants, forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 28:14; Numbers 23:10; Genesis 32:12"> I will make your descendants like the dust of the earth which, if anyone were able to count the dust of the earth, your descendants would be so counted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 13:15; Numbers 13:17–24"> Arise, go through the length of the land and through its breadth, for I will give it to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 18:1; Genesis 8:20; Genesis 35:27"> So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “And”">g</a></sup>Abram pitched his tent, and he came and settled at the oaks of Mamre, which were at Hebron. And there he built an altar to Yahweh.</span>
