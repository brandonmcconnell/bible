## Christ, the Mystery of God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:29; Philippians 1:30; Revelation 3:14–22"> For I want you to know how great a struggle I have on behalf of you, and those in Laodicea, and all those who have not seen my face ⸤in person⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “in the flesh”">a</a></sup>,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 1:27; Colossians 4:8; Ephesians 6:22"> so that their hearts may be encouraged, united in love and into all the wealth of the full assurance of insight into the knowledge of the mystery of God, Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:24; 1 Corinthians 1:30; Romans 11:33"> in whom all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge are hidden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 4:1; Ephesians 4:14; Romans 16:18–19"> I say this in order that no one will deceive you with persuasive speech,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:3–4; 1 Thessalonians 2:17; 1 Corinthians 16:13"> for even if I am absent in the flesh, yet I am with you in spirit, rejoicing and seeing your good order and the steadfastness of your faith in Christ.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Made Alive in Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; 1 John 2:6; 1 Thessalonians 4:1"> Therefore as you have received Christ Jesus the Lord, live in him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 3:17; Jeremiah 17:8; Ephesians 2:20–22"> firmly rooted and built up in him and established in the faith, just as you were taught, abounding with thankfulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:20; Ephesians 5:6; Matthew 7:15"> Beware lest ⸤anyone take you captive⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “anyone be the one who takes captive you”">b</a></sup>through philosophy and empty deceit, according to ⸤human tradition⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “the tradition of men”">c</a></sup>, according to the elemental spirits of the world and not according to Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 1:19; John 10:30; John 1:14"> because in him all the fullness of deity dwells bodily,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 3:19; Colossians 1:16–18; Ephesians 1:20–23"> and you are filled in him, who is the head over every ruler and authority,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 2:29; Philippians 3:3; Romans 6:6"> in whom also you were circumcised with a circumcision not made by hands, by the removal of the body of the flesh, by the circumcision of Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 6:3–5; 1 Peter 3:21; Galatians 3:27"> having been buried with him in baptism, in which also you were raised together with him through faith in the working of God, who raised him from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 2:5–6; Romans 8:11; Ephesians 2:1"> And ⸤although you were dead⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “and you being dead”">d</a></sup>in the trespasses and the uncircumcision of your flesh, he made you alive together with him, having forgiven us all our trespasses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 44:22; Ephesians 2:14–16; Isaiah 43:25"> having destroyed the certificate of indebtedness in ordinances against us, which was hostile to us, and removed it out of the way by nailing it to the cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 2:14; Ephesians 4:8; Matthew 12:29"> When he had disarmed the rulers and the authorities, he made a display of them in public, triumphing over them by it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “in him”">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Do Not Be Judged by Human Religious Rules

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 14:5–6; Romans 14:10; Galatians 4:10"> Therefore do not let anyone judge you with reference to eating or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “and”">f</a></sup>drinking or participation in a feast or a new moon or a Sabbath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 10:1; Hebrews 8:5; John 1:17"> which are a shadow of what is to come, but the reality is Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; 1 John 4:1–2; Colossians 2:23"> Let no one condemn you, taking pleasure in humility and the worship of angels, going into detail about the things which he has seen, inflated without cause by his fleshly mind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 4:15–16; John 15:4–6; 2 Peter 3:18"> and not holding fast to the head, from whom the whole body, supported and held together by the ligaments and sinews, grows with the growth of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; Colossians 2:16; Galatians 4:3"> If you have died with Christ to the elemental spirits of the world, why do you submit to them as if living in the world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:3; Genesis 3:3; Isaiah 52:11"> “Do not handle, do not taste, do not touch,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Titus 1:14; 1 Corinthians 6:13"> which things are all meant for destruction by consuming according to human commandments and teachings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:8; Colossians 2:18; Colossians 2:8"> which things ⸤although they have⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “having”">g</a></sup>, to be sure, an appearance of wisdom in self-made religion and humility and unsparing treatment of the body, ⸤do not have any value⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “are not with any value”">h</a></sup>against the indulgence of the flesh.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
