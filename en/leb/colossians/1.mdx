## Thanksgiving for the Colossian Believers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:2; Colossians 1:9; 2 Timothy 1:3"> We give thanks always to God the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ when we pray for you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 1:15; 2 Thessalonians 1:3; 1 Thessalonians 1:3"> since we heard about your faith in Christ Jesus and the love that you have for all the saints,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 1:3–4; Ephesians 1:13; 2 Timothy 4:8"> because of the hope reserved for you in heaven, which you have heard about beforehand in the word of truth, the gospel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 15:16; Philippians 1:11; Colossians 1:23"> that has come to you, just as also in all the world it is bearing fruit and increasing, just as also among you from the day you heard about and understood the grace of God in truth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 4:12; Philemon 1:23; Colossians 4:7"> just as you learned it from Epaphras, our dear fellow slave who is a faithful minister of Christ on our behalf,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “on your behalf”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 15:30; 2 Timothy 1:7; Galatians 5:22"> who also made clear to us your love in the Spirit.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Prayer for Maturity Based on Christ’s Preeminence

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 3:17; Philippians 1:9–11; James 1:5"> Because of this also we, from the day we heard about it, did not cease praying for you, and asking that you may be filled with the knowledge of his will in all wisdom and spiritual insight,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 4:1; Hebrews 13:21; 2 Peter 1:2–3"> so that you may live in a manner worthy of the Lord, ⸤to please him in all respects⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “to all pleasing”">b</a></sup>, bearing fruit in every good deed and increasing in the knowledge of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 3:16; Philippians 4:13; Ephesians 4:2"> enabled with all power, according to his glorious might, for all steadfastness and patience with joy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 20:32; Ephesians 1:11; Ephesians 1:18"> giving thanks to the Father who has qualified you for a share of the inheritance of the saints in light,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Acts 26:18; 1 Peter 2:9"> who has rescued us from the domain of darkness and transferred us to the kingdom of the Son ⸤he loves⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “of his love”">c</a></sup>,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 1:7; Romans 3:24–25; 1 Peter 1:19–20"> in whom we have the redemption, the forgiveness of sins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 1:3; John 14:9; John 1:1"> who is the image of the invisible God, the firstborn over all creation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 11:36; John 1:3; 1 Corinthians 8:6"> because all things in the heavens and on the earth were created by him, things visible and things invisible, whether thrones or dominions or rulers or powers, all things were created through him and for him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 1:3; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Acts 17:28"> and he himself is before all things, and in him all things are held together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 1:22–23; Revelation 1:5; Ephesians 4:15–16"> and he himself is the head of the body, the church, who is the beginning, the firstborn from the dead, so that he himself may become first in everything,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 2:9; John 1:16; Ephesians 1:23"> because he was well pleased for all the fullness to dwell in him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:18–21; Ephesians 1:10; 1 John 4:9–10"> and through him to reconcile all things to himself, by making peace through the blood of his cross, through him, whether things on earth or things in heaven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 2:12; Titus 3:3–7; Ephesians 2:1–3"> And ⸤although you were formerly alienated⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “and you formerly being alienated”">d</a></sup>and enemies in attitude, because of your evil deeds,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 5:27; Jude 1:24; Ephesians 1:4"> but now you have been reconciled<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “he has reconciled you ”">e</a></sup>by ⸤his physical body⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “the body of his flesh”">f</a></sup>through death, to present you holy and blameless and above reproach before him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 3:14; Hebrews 3:6; Matthew 24:13–14"> if indeed you remain in the faith, established and steadfast and not shifted away from the hope of the gospel that you heard, which was proclaimed in all creation under heaven, of which I, Paul, became a minister.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul’s Suffering and Stewardship

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 5:3; 2 Timothy 1:8; 2 Corinthians 1:5–8"> Now I rejoice in my sufferings on behalf of you, and I fill up in my flesh what is lacking of the afflictions of Christ, on behalf of his body which is the church,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ephesians 3:2; Colossians 1:23; Galatians 2:7–8"> of which I became a minister, according to God’s stewardship which was given to me for you, to complete the word of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 8:10; Romans 16:25–26; Ephesians 3:3–10"> the mystery which has been hidden from the ages and from the generations, but has now been revealed to his saints,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 14:20; Romans 8:10; Ephesians 3:16–17"> to whom God wanted to make known what is the glorious wealth of this mystery among the Gentiles, which is Christ in you, the hope of glory,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ephesians 5:27; Jeremiah 3:15; Ephesians 4:11–13"> whom we proclaim, by admonishing every person<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “man,” used here in a generic sense to refer to persons of either gender">g</a></sup>and teaching every person<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “man,” used here in a generic sense to refer to persons of either gender">h</a></sup>with all wisdom, in order that we may present every person<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “man,” used here in a generic sense to refer to persons of either gender">i</a></sup>mature in Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:10; Ephesians 1:19; 1 Corinthians 9:25–27"> for which purpose also I labor, striving according to his working which is at work powerfully in me.</span>
</div>
</div>
