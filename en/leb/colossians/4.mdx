## Further Advice for the Christian Life

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:17–18; Philippians 4:6; Ephesians 6:18"> Be devoted to prayer, keeping alert in it with thanksgiving,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ephesians 6:19–20; Acts 14:27; 2 Thessalonians 3:1–2"> praying at the same time for us also, that God may open for us a door of the message, to speak the mystery of Christ, for which also ⸤I am a prisoner⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “I have been bound”">a</a></sup>,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:1–4; Ephesians 6:20; Colossians 4:6"> so that I may reveal<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “may make it clear”">b</a></sup>it, as it is necessary for me to speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 5:15–17; 1 Thessalonians 4:12; James 3:13"> Live with wisdom toward those outside, making the most of the time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; 1 Peter 3:15; Ecclesiastes 10:12"> Let your speech always be with grace, seasoned with salt, so that you may know how it is necessary for you to answer each one.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Exhortations Regarding Fellow Workers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 6:21–23; Acts 20:4; 2 Timothy 4:12"> Tychicus, my dear brother and faithful servant and fellow slave in the Lord, will make known to you all ⸤my circumstances⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “the things according to me”">c</a></sup>,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 6:22; Colossians 2:2; Philippians 2:28"> whom I have sent to you for this very reason, in order that you may know ⸤our circumstances⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “the things concerning us”">d</a></sup>and he may encourage your hearts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philemon 1:10–19; Colossians 4:7"> together with Onesimus, my faithful and dear brother, who is one of you. They will make known to you all ⸤the circumstances⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “the things”">e</a></sup>here.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philemon 1:24; 2 Timothy 4:11; Acts 19:29"> Aristarchus, my fellow prisoner, greets you, and Mark, the cousin of Barnabas (about whom you received instructions—if he should come to you, welcome him),</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 11:2; 2 Corinthians 6:1; Acts 10:45"> and Jesus who is called Justus. These are the only ones who are fellow workers for the kingdom of God from the circumcision, who have been a comfort to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 15:30; Colossians 1:9; Colossians 1:7"> Epaphras, who is one of you, greets you, a slave of Christ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “of Christ Jesus”">f</a></sup>always struggling on behalf of you in his prayers, that you may stand mature and fully assured<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “and filled”">g</a></sup>in all the will of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 2:1; Romans 10:2; Revelation 1:11"> For I testify to him that ⸤he is working hard⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “he has much labor”">h</a></sup>on behalf of you and those in Laodicea and those in Hierapolis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Philemon 1:24; 2 Timothy 4:10–11"> Luke the physician, our dear friend, greets you, as does Demas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 16:5; Philemon 1:2; 1 Corinthians 16:9"> Greet the brothers in Laodicea, and Nympha and the church in her<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Some manuscripts, including the majority of Byzantine minuscules, read the masculine form of the pronoun here (“his”), understanding the form of the name “Nympha” earlier in the verse to be masculine">i</a></sup>house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:27; 2 Thessalonians 3:14"> And whenever this letter is read among you, ⸤see to it⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “cause”">j</a></sup>that it is read also among the Laodicean church, and that you also read the letter from Laodicea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Philemon 1:2; Acts 1:17; Ezekiel 44:23–24"> And tell Archippus, “Direct your attention to the ministry that you received in the Lord, in order that you may complete it.”</span>
</div>
</div>
