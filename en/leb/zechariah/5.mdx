<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:14; Jeremiah 1:11–14; Amos 7:8"> And he asked me, “What are you seeing?” And I said, “I am seeing a flying scroll twenty cubits long and ten cubits wide.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="That is, thirty feet by fifteen feet">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 20:15; Isaiah 43:28; Isaiah 24:6"> And he said to me, “This is the curse going out over the surface of the whole earth. <span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:9–11; Proverbs 3:33; Malachi 3:5"> ‘I have sent it out,’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">d</a></sup>Yahweh of hosts, ‘and it will go into the house of the thief and into the house of the one swearing ⸤falsely by my name⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “by my name to lie”">e</a></sup>and it will spend the night in that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “his”">f</a></sup>house and will destroy it, with its timber and its stone.’ ”</span>

## Zechariah’s Seventh Vision

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 1:9; Zechariah 1:14; Zechariah 2:3"> And the angel who was speaking to me went out, and he said to me, “⸤Please look up⸥!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “Lift, please, your eyes!”">g</a></sup>See what this is going out.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 8:5; Ezekiel 44:10–11"> And I asked, “What is it?” And he said, “This is a basket<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “an ephah,” a measure of grain">h</a></sup>going out. And he said, “This is their iniquity<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “eye”; other ancient versions (LXX, Syriac) read “iniquity”">i</a></sup>throughout all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:1–49; Revelation 17:1–18; Jeremiah 3:1–2"> And look, the lead cover was lifted and a woman was sitting inside the basket.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “ephah,” a measure of grain">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 15:16; Amos 9:1–4; Proverbs 5:22"> And he said, “This is Wickedness!” And he thrust her back down into the basket,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “ephah,” a measure of grain">k</a></sup>and threw the lead cover on top of it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="That is, on top of the “basket” or “ephah”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:49; Hosea 8:1; Daniel 9:26–27"> And I ⸤looked up⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “lifted up my eyes”">m</a></sup>and saw, and look!—two women coming forward, and the wind was in their wings, and they had wings like those of a stork, and they lifted up the basket<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “ephah,” a measure of grain">n</a></sup>between the earth and the sky.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “heaven”">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And I asked the angel who was talking to me, “Where are they taking the basket?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “ephah,” a measure of grain">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 11:2; Genesis 10:10; Daniel 1:2"> And he said to me, “To build for it a house<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “temple”">q</a></sup>in the land of Shinar,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="That is, Babylon (Gen 11:2 )">r</a></sup>and when it is put in place, it will be placed there on its site.”</span>
</div>
