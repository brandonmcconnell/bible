## The Two Shepherds

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 11:7; Matthew 23:37; Romans 15:8"> Thus says Yahweh my God: “Shepherd the flock<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The Hebrew term refers collectively to both sheep and goats (small livestock animals)">b</a></sup>doomed to slaughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:7; Hosea 12:8; 1 Timothy 6:5–10"> The ones buying them kill them and go unpunished, and the ones selling them say, ‘Blessed be Yahweh, for I have become rich.’ Their own shepherds have no compassion for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:14; Matthew 22:7; Isaiah 9:19–21"> For I will no longer have compassion on the inhabitants of the land,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">c</a></sup>Yahweh. “Look, I am going to cause humankind to fall, each into the hand of his neighbor, and into the hand of his king; and they will devastate the land, and I will not deliver anyone from their hand.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 11:14; Zechariah 11:4; Isaiah 11:4"> And I shepherded the flock<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The Hebrew term refers collectively to both sheep and goats (small livestock animals)">d</a></sup>doomed to slaughter, even<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew “therefore”">e</a></sup>the afflicted<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="LXX reads “merchants of the flock ” which is reflected in some English translations">f</a></sup>of the flock.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="The Hebrew term refers collectively to both sheep and goats (small livestock animals)">g</a></sup>I took two staffs, one I called Kindness, and the other I called Unity, and I shepherded the flock.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="The Hebrew term refers collectively to both sheep and goats (small livestock animals)">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 49:7; Matthew 23:34–36; John 15:18"> And I got rid of three shepherds in one month, for ⸤I grew impatient⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “my soul became short”">i</a></sup>with them, and ⸤they also became tired of me⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “also their soul detested me”">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:11; Matthew 13:10–11; Matthew 21:19"> So I said, “I will not shepherd you! The one dying will die, and the one to be destroyed will be destroyed. And the ones remaining, let them devour ⸤the flesh of each other⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “each the flesh of her neighbor”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 11:7; Psalm 89:39; Hebrews 8:8–13"> And I took my staff Kindness and broke it, to break my covenant that I ⸤had made⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “had cut”">l</a></sup>with all the peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:12; Luke 2:38; Deuteronomy 31:21"> And it was broken on that day. Then the afflicted<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="LXX reads “merchants (of the flock)” which is reflected in some English translations">m</a></sup>of the flock,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="The Hebrew term refers collectively to both sheep and goats (small livestock animals)">n</a></sup>the ones who were watching me, knew that it was the word of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 26:15; Exodus 21:32; Luke 22:3–6"> And I said to them, “If ⸤it seems right to you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “it is good in your eyes”">o</a></sup>give me my wages, but if not, ⸤keep them⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “refrain”">p</a></sup>And they weighed out my wages, thirty silver shekels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 27:3–10; Acts 1:18–19; Isaiah 53:2–3"> And Yahweh said to me, “Throw it to the potter,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Syriac reads “treasury,” followed by NAB, NRSV, TEV">q</a></sup>⸤this noble price⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “the price of honor”">r</a></sup>at which I was valued by them!” So I took the thirty silver shekels and I threw them to the potter<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Syriac reads “treasury,” followed by NAB, NRSV, TEV">s</a></sup>in the house of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 9:21; Isaiah 11:13; Acts 23:7–10"> Then I broke my second staff Unity to break the family ties between Judah and Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10–12; Luke 11:40; Jeremiah 2:26–27"> And Yahweh said to me, “Take again the implements<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Hebrew “implement”">t</a></sup>of a foolish shepherd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2–6; Jeremiah 23:2; Matthew 23:2–4"> For look, I am raising up a shepherd in the land who will not attend to the ones that are perishing; he will not seek the young man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Syriac and other ancient versions read “the wandering” or “the scattered,” as do several English translations">u</a></sup>he will not heal the ones that are crushed and he will not sustain the healthy ones; he will devour the flesh of the ⸤fattened ones⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “the stall-fed”">v</a></sup>and tear apart even their hoofs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:1; Ezekiel 13:3; 1 Corinthians 8:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Woe, my worthless shepherd</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who deserts the flock!</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="The Hebrew term refers collectively to both sheep and goats (small livestock animals)">w</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">May a sword fall on his arm</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and on ⸤his right eye⸥!</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “the eye of his right”">x</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">May his arm wither completely</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and ⸤his right eye⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “the eye of his right”">y</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">be utterly blinded!”</span></span>
</div>
