## Zechariah’s Apocalyptic Vision

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 13:9; Isaiah 2:12; Isaiah 13:6"> Look! A day is coming for Yahweh, when your plunder will be divided in your midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 21:20–24; Isaiah 13:16; Romans 9:27–29"> I will gather all the nations against Jerusalem to battle, and the city will be captured, and they will loot the houses, and the women will be raped; half of the city will go into exile, but the remainder of the people will not be cut off from the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 12:9; Zephaniah 3:19; Zechariah 2:8–9"> Then Yahweh will go forth and fight against those nations, like ⸤when he fights⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “the day of his fighting”">a</a></sup>on a day of battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 1:3–4; Ezekiel 11:23; Acts 1:11–12"> On that day his feet will stand on ⸤the Mount of Olives⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “the mountain of the olive trees”">b</a></sup>which faces Jerusalem on the east; and ⸤the Mount of Olives⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “the mountain of the olive trees”">c</a></sup>will be split ⸤in half⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “from its half”">d</a></sup>from east to west, by a very great valley; and half of the mountain will withdraw toward the north, and the other half toward the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jude 1:14–15; Amos 1:1; Matthew 25:31"> And you will flee by the valley of my mountains, because the valley of the mountains will reach to Azal, and you will flee like you fled from the earthquake in the days of King Uzziah of Judah. And Yahweh my God will come, and all the holy ones with him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew “you”; LXX has “him”">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 97:10–11; Psalm 112:4; John 12:46"> ⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “And it will happen”">f</a></sup>on that day there will not be light,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Some suggest reading “flame” (= “heat”) here">g</a></sup>the precious things will congeal.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “the noble ones will congeal”; the Hebrew is difficult here">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 22:5; 1 Thessalonians 5:2; Daniel 12:4"> There shall be continuous day—it is known to Yahweh—not day and not night; and at evening time there will be light.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 22:1–2; John 7:38; John 4:14"> ⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “And it will happen”">i</a></sup>on that day, living waters will flow out from Jerusalem, half of them to the eastern sea,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="That is, the Dead Sea">j</a></sup>and the other half to the western sea;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="That is, the Mediterranean Sea">k</a></sup>it will happen both in the summer and in the winter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 4:5–6; Revelation 11:15; Daniel 7:27"> And Yahweh will be king over all the earth; on that day Yahweh will be one and his name one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 12:6; Joshua 15:32; 1 Kings 15:22"> All the land will be transformed into a desert plateau<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “will be changed like the Arabah”">l</a></sup>from Geba to Rimmon south of Jerusalem. But it<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="That is, Jerusalem">m</a></sup>will rise up high and it will stay<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “remain”">n</a></sup>in its place from the Gate of Benjamin to the place of the former gate, up to the Corner Gate and from the Tower of Hananel to the king’s winepresses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 22:3; Jeremiah 23:5–6; Amos 9:15"> And they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="That is, the people">o</a></sup>will dwell in it, and ⸤there will be no more ban imposed⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “and there will not be a thing devoted to destruction” (that is, put under the ban of utter destruction)">p</a></sup>and Jerusalem will dwell in security.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:17–20; Isaiah 66:15–16; Micah 4:11–13"> And this will be the plague with which Yahweh will strike all the peoples that fight against Jerusalem: their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Hebrew “his”">q</a></sup>flesh will rot while they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Hebrew “he”">r</a></sup>are standing on their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Hebrew “his”">s</a></sup>feet; their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Hebrew “his”">t</a></sup>eyes will rot in their sockets, and their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Hebrew “his”">u</a></sup>tongues<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Hebrew “tongue”">v</a></sup>will rot in their mouths.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Hebrew “mouth”">w</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Zechariah 12:4; Revelation 17:12–17; Judges 7:22"> ⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “And it will happen”">x</a></sup>on that day a great panic from Yahweh will fall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Hebrew “will be”">y</a></sup>on them, and ⸤they will seize one another’s hand⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “and a man will seize the hand of his neighbor”">z</a></sup>and the hand of the one will be raised against the hand of ⸤another⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “his neighbor”">aa</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 23:18; Zechariah 12:2; 2 Chronicles 20:25–27"> And Judah also will fight at<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Or “against” (so NAB, CEV)">ab</a></sup>Jerusalem. And the wealth of all the surrounding nations will be collected—gold and silver, and garments ⸤in great abundance⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “as far as the very multitude”">ac</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 14:12"> ⸤Like this plague⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “And so”">ad</a></sup>will be the plague on the horse, the mule, the camel, and the donkey, and every kind of animal in those camps.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 14:17–19; Isaiah 66:23; Isaiah 66:18–21"> ⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “And it will happen”">ae</a></sup>every survivor from all those nations coming against Jerusalem will go up year after year<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Or “from year to year”; or “yearly”">af</a></sup>to worship the king, Yahweh of hosts, and celebrate the Feast of Booths.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="That is, Succoth (or Tabernacles)">ag</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Amos 4:7–8; Isaiah 60:12; Jeremiah 14:4"> ⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “And it will happen”">ah</a></sup>whoever of the clans of the earth does not go up to Jerusalem to worship the king, Yahweh of hosts, it will not rain on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 14:15; Deuteronomy 11:10–11; Zechariah 14:12"> And if the clan of Egypt will not go up and come in, on them will be that plague Yahweh inflicts on the nations that do not go up to celebrate the Feast of Booths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 3:19"> This will be the punishment of Egypt and the punishment of all the nations that do not go up to celebrate the Feast of Booths.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:16–17; Exodus 39:30; Leviticus 8:9"> On that day there will be inscribed on the bells of the horses, “Holy to Yahweh.” And the cooking pots in the house<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Or “temple”">ai</a></sup>of Yahweh will be holy like the sacrificial basins<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="That is, the ceremonial bowls used by the priests for sprinkling blood">aj</a></sup>before the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:9; Romans 14:6–7; 1 Corinthians 10:31"> And every cooking pot in Jerusalem and in Judah will be holy to Yahweh of hosts, and all those who sacrifice will come and will take from them, and will cook in them, and there will be no longer a ⸤trader⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Literally “Canaanite” (Canaanites, especially Phoenicians, were merchants and traders)">ak</a></sup>in the house<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Or “temple”">al</a></sup>of Yahweh of hosts on that day.</span>
</div>
