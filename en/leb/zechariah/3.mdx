## Zechariah’s Fourth Vision

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Luke 22:31; Psalm 109:6"> And he showed me Joshua the high priest standing ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">a</a></sup>the angel of Yahweh; and Satan<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew “the accuser,” or “the adversary”">b</a></sup>was standing on his right to accuse him.</span>

Is this not a stick snatched from the fire?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 64:6; Revelation 19:8; Ezra 9:15"> And Joshua was clothed in filthy garments and was standing ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">e</a></sup>the angel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 15:22; Isaiah 61:10; Ezekiel 36:25"> And he answered and said to the ones standing ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">f</a></sup>him, saying, “Remove the filthy garments from him.” And he said to him, “See, I have taken away your guilt<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="The Hebrew term refers to guilt caused by sin (HALOT 800 s.v. 2 )">g</a></sup>from you, and will clothe you with rich garments.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 6:11; Exodus 29:6; Job 29:14"> And I said, “Let them put a clean headband<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “turban”">h</a></sup>on his head.” And they put a clean headband on his head, and they clothed him with garments. And the angel of Yahweh was standing by.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 28:13–17; Genesis 22:15–16; Zechariah 3:1"> And the angel of Yahweh assured Joshua, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Genesis 26:5; 2 Timothy 4:1–2"> “Thus says Yahweh of hosts: ‘If you will walk in my ways, and if you will keep my requirements, then you will judge<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “govern”">i</a></sup>my house, and you will also guard my courtyards, and I will give to you passageways<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “right of access”">j</a></sup>among these that are standing here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5; Isaiah 11:1; Zechariah 6:12"> Listen, please, O Joshua the high priest, you and your companions<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Possibly his fellow priests">k</a></sup>that are sitting ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">l</a></sup>you. For the men are a sign that,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Hebrew “for”">m</a></sup>look, I am going to bring my servant the Branch.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Hebrew “Sprout” or “Shoot” (“Branch” is a traditional rendering in English)">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 4:10; Jeremiah 50:20; Isaiah 28:16"> For consider, the stone that ⸤I set before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “I gave to the face of, ”or “I gave in the presence of”">o</a></sup>Joshua, on one stone are seven eyes.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “facets”">p</a></sup>Look, ⸤I am going to engrave an inscription on it⸥,’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “I am going to engrave its engraving”">q</a></sup>⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">r</a></sup>Yahweh of hosts, ‘and I will remove the guilt of that land in a single day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 4:25; Micah 4:4; Zechariah 2:11"> On that day,’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">s</a></sup>Yahweh of hosts, ‘you will invite ⸤one another⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “a man to his friend”">t</a></sup>under the vine and under the fig tree.’ ”</span>
</div>
