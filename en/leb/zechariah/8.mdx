## Yahweh’s Promise to Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of Yahweh of hosts came, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joel 2:18; Zechariah 1:14–16; Isaiah 42:13–14"> “Thus says Yahweh of hosts: ‘⸤I am exceedingly jealous for Zion⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “I am jealous for Zion with great jealousy”">a</a></sup>I am jealous for it with great wrath.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 1:16; Zechariah 2:10–11; John 1:14"> Thus says Yahweh: ‘I have returned to Zion, and I will dwell in the midst of Jerusalem; and Jerusalem will be called the faithful city,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “the city of truth”">b</a></sup>and the mountain of Yahweh of hosts will be called the holy mountain.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 65:20–22; Job 5:26; Lamentations 5:11–15"> Thus says Yahweh of hosts: ‘Old men and old women shall again sit in the public squares of Jerusalem, each with staff in hand<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “a man his staff in his hand”">c</a></sup>⸤because of great age⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “because of the multitude of days”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:13; Matthew 11:16–17; Jeremiah 30:19–20"> And the public squares of the city will be filled with boys and girls playing in its public squares.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:27; Romans 4:20–21; Numbers 11:22–23"> Thus says Yahweh of hosts: ‘Even if it seems impossible<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “wonderful”">e</a></sup>⸤to the remnant⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “in the eyes of the remnant”">f</a></sup>of this people in those days, should it also seem impossible<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “wonderful”">g</a></sup>⸤to me⸥?’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “in my eyes”">h</a></sup>⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">i</a></sup>Yahweh of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:8; Romans 11:25–27; Malachi 1:11"> Thus says Yahweh of hosts: ‘Look! I am going to save my people from the land of the east, and from the land of the west,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:28; Ezekiel 11:20; Jeremiah 31:33"> and I will bring them and they will dwell in the midst of Jerusalem. They will be my people and I will be their God in faithfulness<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “truth”">j</a></sup>and in righteousness.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 35:4; 1 Chronicles 22:13; Ezra 5:1–2"> “Thus says Yahweh of hosts: ‘Let your hands be strong, you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Hebrew “the ones ”">k</a></sup>who are hearing in these days these words from the mouth of the prophets who were present on the day ⸤the foundation was laid for the rebuilding of the temple, the house of Yahweh of hosts⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “the temple, the house of Yahweh of hosts, was founded to be built”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Haggai 1:6–11; Haggai 2:16–18; Amos 3:6"> For before those days there was no wage for people or for animals, and there was no safety from the enemy for those who went out or those who came in, and I sent each person against ⸤one another⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “his neighbor”">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 12:1; Haggai 2:19; Zechariah 8:8–9"> But now ⸤I will not deal with the remnant of this people as in the former days⸥,’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “not like the former days I will be to the remnant”">n</a></sup>⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">o</a></sup>Yahweh of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 26:4–5; Deuteronomy 33:13; Genesis 27:28"> ‘For there will be a sowing of peace; the vine will give its fruit, and the soil will give its produce, and the heavens will give their dew. And I will cause the remnant of this people to inherit all these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:20; Zechariah 8:9; Psalm 72:17"> ⸤And then⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “and it will happen”">p</a></sup>just as you were a curse among the nations, O house of Judah and house of Israel, so will I save you, and you will be a blessing. You must not be afraid; let your hands be strong!’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:28; Ezekiel 24:14; Jeremiah 4:28"> For thus says Yahweh of hosts: “Just as I planned to bring disaster<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Hebrew “evil”">q</a></sup>on you when your ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “fathers”">r</a></sup>provoked me to anger,” says Yahweh of hosts, “and I did not regret it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 8:13; Micah 7:18–20; Luke 12:32"> ⸤so again I have planned⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “So I did again, I planned”">s</a></sup>in these days to do good to Jerusalem and the house of Judah. Do not be afraid!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ephesians 4:25; Zechariah 7:9; Psalm 15:2"> These are the things that you must do: speak truth, each of you, with his neighbor; practice trustworthy judgment and peace in your gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 6:16–19; Micah 2:1–3; Jeremiah 44:4"> Do not devise evil in your hearts against your neighbor, and do not love ⸤a false oath⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “an oath of deception”">t</a></sup>because all these are things I hate,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">u</a></sup>Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And the word of Yahweh of hosts came to me, saying</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 8:16; Jeremiah 39:2; Zechariah 7:5"> “Thus says Yahweh of hosts: ‘The fast of the fourth month, the fifth month, the seventh month, and the tenth month will be for the house of Judah jubilation and joy and merry festivals; therefore love truth and peace.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 2:2–3; Micah 4:1–2; Zechariah 2:11"> Thus says Yahweh of hosts: ‘It will happen again that nations and the inhabitants of many cities will come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zechariah 7:2; Hosea 6:3; Psalm 146:1–2"> And the inhabitants of one city will go to another city, saying, “Let us go immediately to entreat ⸤the favor of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “the face of”">v</a></sup>Yahweh, to seek Yahweh of hosts—I also will go!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zechariah 8:21; Isaiah 60:3–22; Revelation 21:24"> And many peoples and powerful<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or “mighty”">w</a></sup>nations will come to seek Yahweh of hosts in Jerusalem, and to entreat ⸤the favor of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “the face of”">x</a></sup>Yahweh.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 55:5; 1 Corinthians 14:25; Isaiah 60:3"> Thus says Yahweh of hosts: ‘In those days ten men from ⸤all the nations of every language⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “all the languages of the nations”">y</a></sup>will take hold of the hem of a Judean man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Many versions translate “a Jew,” but “Judean” highlights Zechariah’s focus on provincial geography">z</a></sup>saying, “Let us go with you, for we have heard that God is with you!” ’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>
