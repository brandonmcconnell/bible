## Yahweh Gives Rain

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="James 5:16–18; Matthew 7:7–8; Isaiah 44:3"> Ask rain from Yahweh in the season of the spring rain—<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh, who makes storm clouds,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he gives showers of rain to them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to everyone vegetation in the field.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; Jeremiah 27:9; Ezekiel 34:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because the household gods</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew teraphim">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">speak deceit,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and those who practice divination see a lie,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the dreamers of vanity speak in vain.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Therefore the people wander like sheep;</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “flocks”; the Hebrew term refers collectively to both sheep and goats (small livestock animals)">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they are afflicted because there is no shepherd.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2; Ezekiel 34:7–10; Jeremiah 23:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">⸤My anger burns⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “My nose becomes hot”">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">against the shepherds,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will punish the leaders,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “male goats”">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because Yahweh of hosts watches over his flock,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “herd”">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the house of Judah;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">and he will make them like his majestic horse in war.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 9:10; Zechariah 9:8; Numbers 24:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">From them</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “him” (or “it”), referring to the flock in v. 3">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the cornerstone will go out,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from them</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew “him” (or “it”), referring to the flock in v. 3">g</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the tent peg,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">from them</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “him” (or “it”), referring to the flock in v. 3">h</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the battle bow,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from them</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “him” (or “it”), referring to the flock in v. 3">i</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">every ruler,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">all together.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Haggai 2:22; Zechariah 9:13; Psalm 20:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">And they will be like warriors,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">trampling in the mud of the streets in the battle.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will fight, because Yahweh is with them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will put to shame the riders on horses.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 13:9; Zechariah 10:12; Jeremiah 3:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I will strengthen the house of Judah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the house of Joseph I will save.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And I will bring them back, because I have compassion on them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will be as if I had not rejected them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for I am Yahweh their God, and I will answer them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 9:15; Zechariah 9:17; 1 Samuel 2:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">And the people of Ephraim will be like a warrior,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their heart will be glad as with wine.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And their children will see and rejoice;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">their heart will shout in exultation in Yahweh.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 5:26; Isaiah 7:18; Jeremiah 33:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will whistle to them and gather them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for I have redeemed them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will become numerous as they have been numerous before.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:9; Hosea 2:23; Esther 8:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Though I sow</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “scatter”">j</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">them among the nations,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the distant places they will remember me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will stay alive with their children, and they will return.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 11:11; Micah 7:14; Zechariah 8:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I will bring them back from the land of Egypt,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and from Assyria I will gather them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">To the land of Gilead and Lebanon I will bring them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">until no room will be found for them there.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:13; Micah 5:5–6; Isaiah 43:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">And he</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="LXX reads “they,” referring to the Israelites (so NIV, NRSV, NASB)">k</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will pass through the sea of distress,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he will strike the waves of the sea,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all the depths of the Nile will dry up.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The pride of Assyria will be brought low,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the scepter of Egypt will depart.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Isaiah 2:5; Zechariah 10:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will make them strong in Yahweh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and in his name they will walk,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">⸤declares⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">l</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-3">Yahweh.</span></span>
