## Zechariah’s Fifth Vision

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:26; Daniel 8:18; Zechariah 1:9"> And the angel who was talking with me returned, and he wakened me as one who is wakened from his sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 4:5; Revelation 1:12; Matthew 5:14–16"> And he said to me, “What do you see?” And I said, “I see, and look, ⸤a lampstand all of gold⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “a lampstand of gold in its totality”">a</a></sup>and a bowl was on its top, and its seven lamps on it, and seven lips on each of the lamps that are on its top.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 11:4; Zechariah 4:14; Romans 11:17"> And there are two olive trees by it, one on the right of the bowl and the other on its left.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 6:4; Zechariah 1:9; Zechariah 1:19"> And I answered and said to the angel who was talking to me, “What are these, my lord?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 4:13; Zechariah 1:9; Daniel 2:30"> And the angel who was talking with me answered and said to me, “Do you not know what these are?” And I said, “No, my lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:7–8; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; 1 Corinthians 2:4–5"> And he answered and said to me, “This is the word of Yahweh to Zerubbabel, saying, ‘Not by strength and not by power, but only by my Spirit,’ says Yahweh of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:25; Zechariah 4:9; Micah 4:1"> ‘Who are you O great mountain? ⸤Before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “To the face of”">b</a></sup>Zerubbabel you will become level ground, and he will bring out the top stone amid the shouts of “Grace, grace to it!” ’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezra 6:14–15; Zechariah 6:12–13; Zechariah 6:15"> “The hands of Zerubbabel have founded this house, and his hands will finish it. And you will know that Yahweh of hosts has sent me to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 8:7; 1 Corinthians 1:28–29; 2 Chronicles 16:9"> For whoever has despised the day of small things will rejoice and will see the ⸤plumb line⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “the tin stone”; this could refer to a plumb line or a plummet">c</a></sup>in the hand of Zerubbabel.”<br />These seven are the eyes of Yahweh which are ranging ⸤throughout the whole earth⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “in all the earth”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zechariah 4:3; Revelation 11:4"> And I answered and said to him, “What are these two olive trees on the right of the lampstand and on its left?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 11:4; Matthew 20:23; Haggai 1:1"> And I replied a second time and asked him, “What are these two twigs of olive trees<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “two clusters of olive branches”; literally “two ears of grain,” perhaps based on similarity of shape">e</a></sup>on the right of the lampstand and on its left?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Zechariah 4:5; Hebrews 5:11–12"> And he replied to me, “Do you not know what these are?” And I said, “No, my lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 11:4; Isaiah 61:1–3; Daniel 9:24–26"> And he said, “These are the two ⸤anointed ones⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “sons of the olive oil”">f</a></sup>standing by the Lord of the whole earth.”</span>
