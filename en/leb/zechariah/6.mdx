## Zechariah’s Eighth Vision

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zechariah 1:18–19; Zechariah 6:5; Daniel 8:22"> ⸤I looked up again⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “I did again, I lifted up my eyes”">a</a></sup>and I saw, and look!—four chariots coming out from between two mountains, and the mountains were mountains of bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 1:8; Revelation 6:2–6; Zechariah 6:6"> With the first chariot there were red horses, and with the second chariot there were black horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 6:2; Revelation 6:8; Zechariah 1:8"> And with the third chariot there were white horses, and with the fourth chariot there were strong<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Other ancient versions read “grey” here">b</a></sup>dappled horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 1:9; Zechariah 5:10; Zechariah 1:19–21"> And I answered and said to the angel that was talking to me, “What are these, my lord?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 1:14; Hebrews 1:7; Psalm 104:3–4"> And the angel answered and said to me, “These are the four winds<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “spirits” (The same Hebrew word can be translated “wind” or “spirit” depending on the context)">c</a></sup>of the heavens going out after presenting themselves before the Lord of all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:48; Jeremiah 4:6; Jeremiah 6:1"> ⸤The one with⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “Her with which”">d</a></sup>the black horses is going out to the north country, and the white horses go after them,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Some interpret the word “after” to indicate a westward direction">e</a></sup>while the dappled ones go to the south country.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:9; Zechariah 1:10; Job 1:6–7"> When the strong horses went out, they were anxious to go to patrol the earth. And he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="That is, “Lord of all the earth”">f</a></sup>said, “Go, patrol the earth.” And they patrolled the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:13; Zechariah 1:15; Isaiah 18:3–4"> And he cried out to me and said to me, “See those going out to the north country have set my spirit at rest in the north country.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Branch is Crowned

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 8:1; Zechariah 7:1; Zechariah 1:1"> And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezra 7:14–16; Ezra 8:26–30; Isaiah 66:20"> “Collect<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="The imperative “Collect” has no object; many translations supply “silver and gold” or “an offering”">g</a></sup>from the exiles—from Heldai, Tobijah, and from Jedaiah, all of whom came from Babylon—and go on that day, go to the house of Josiah son of Zephaniah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zechariah 3:1; Psalm 21:3; Song of Solomon 3:11"> Take the silver and gold and make a crown, and set it on the head of Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zechariah 3:8; Isaiah 4:2; Jeremiah 33:15"> And say to him, ‘Thus says Yahweh of hosts: “Look, here is a man whose name is Branch,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “Sprout” or “Shoot” (“Branch” is a traditional rendering in English)">h</a></sup>and from his place he will sprout, and he will build the temple of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 110:4; Isaiah 49:5–6; Hebrews 3:1"> And he will build the temple of Yahweh, and he will bear majesty and will sit and rule on his throne. And he shall be a priest on his throne, and the counsel of peace will be between the two of them.” ’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="That is, between king and priest">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 26:13; Exodus 12:14; Mark 14:9"> And the crown will be for Helem,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="“Helem” is probably another name for Heldai (v. 10 )">j</a></sup>for Tobijah, for Jedaiah, and for Hen<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="“Hen” is frequently understood as a reference to Josiah (v. 10 )">k</a></sup>son of Zephaniah, as a memorial in the temple of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 60:10; Zechariah 3:7; Isaiah 58:10–14"> And those who are far off will come and build the temple of Yahweh, and you will know that Yahweh of hosts has sent me to you. And it will happen if you will listen diligently to the voice of Yahweh your God.”</span>
</div>
