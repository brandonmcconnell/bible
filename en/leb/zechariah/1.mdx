## Zechariah’s First Vision

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 1:1"> On the twenty-fourth day of the eleventh month, the month of Shebat, in the second year of Darius, the word of Yahweh came to Zechariah the prophet, son of Berekiah, son of Iddo, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 6:4; Isaiah 55:13; Zechariah 13:7"> ⸤I had a vision in the night⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “I saw the night,” which means “I had a vision”">j</a></sup>and look, a man riding on a red horse. And he was standing between the myrtle shrubs that were in the ravine, and behind him were red, reddish-brown, and white horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 4:4–5; Zechariah 5:5; Zechariah 2:3"> And I asked, “What are these, my lord?” And the angel who was talking with me said, “I will show you what these are.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 1:11; Zechariah 6:5–8; Hebrews 1:14"> And the man standing between the myrtle shrubs answered and said, “These are those whom Yahweh has sent to patrol<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “to go about,” or “to walk through”">k</a></sup>the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zechariah 1:10; Zechariah 1:15; Psalm 103:20–21"> And they answered the angel of Yahweh who was standing between the myrtle shrubs, and they said, “We have patrolled<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “have gone about,” or “have walked through”">l</a></sup>the earth, and look, all the earth ⸤is dwelling in peace⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “is dwelling and is at rest”">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zechariah 7:5; Jeremiah 29:10; Psalm 102:13"> The angel of Yahweh answered and said, “O Yahweh of hosts, ⸤how long⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “until when”">n</a></sup>will you have no compassion on Jerusalem and the cities of Judah, with which you showed fury these seventy years?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Hebrew “year”">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:10; Isaiah 40:1–2; Jeremiah 30:10–22"> With gracious<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “kind”; literally “good”">p</a></sup>and comforting words Yahweh answered the angel who was talking with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joel 2:18; Isaiah 40:1; Zechariah 1:17"> And the angel who was talking with me said to me, “Proclaim, saying, ‘Thus says Yahweh of hosts: “⸤I am very jealous⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “I am jealous with a great jealousy”">q</a></sup>for Jerusalem and for Zion!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:12–17; Jeremiah 51:24; Isaiah 54:8"> And ⸤I am extremely angry⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “With a great anger I am angry”">r</a></sup>with the nations that are at ease, for while I was a little angry, they ⸤furthered disaster⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “helped disaster”">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 8:3; Ezra 6:14–15; Zechariah 2:10–11"> Therefore, thus says Yahweh: “I have returned to Jerusalem with compassion. My temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Or “house”">t</a></sup>will be built in it,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">u</a></sup>Yahweh of hosts, “and a measuring line will be stretched out over Jerusalem.” ’</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 51:3; Zechariah 2:12; Zephaniah 3:15–17"> Proclaim again, saying, ‘Thus says Yahweh of hosts: “My cities shall again overflow from prosperity, and Yahweh will comfort Zion again, and he will choose Jerusalem again.” ’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Zechariah’s Second Vision

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 5:13; 2 Kings 24:1–20; Zechariah 5:9"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Zechariah 1:18–2:17 in the English Bible is 2:1–13 in the Hebrew Bible">v</a></sup>And I ⸤looked up⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “lifted up my eyes”">w</a></sup>and I saw, and look, there were four horns!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 1:21; Zechariah 8:14; Zechariah 2:2"> And I said to the angel who was talking with me, “What are these?” And he said to me, “These are the horns that have scattered Judah, Israel, and Jerusalem.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Zechariah 10:3–5; Zechariah 12:2–6; Zechariah 9:12–16"> Then Yahweh showed me four skilled craftsmen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 75:10; Zechariah 1:19; Psalm 75:4–5"> and I asked, “What are these coming to do?” And he answered, saying, “These are the horns that scattered Judah, ⸤so that no man⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “according to the mouth of a man”">x</a></sup>lifted up his head; but these have come to frighten them, to strike down the horns of the nations that lifted a horn against the land of Judah to scatter it.”</span>
