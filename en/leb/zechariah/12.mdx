## The Day of Yahweh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 42:5; Jeremiah 51:15; Isaiah 57:16"> An oracle. The word of Yahweh concerning Israel. “⸤Thus declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “Declaration of”">a</a></sup>Yahweh, who stretches out the heavens, founds the earth, and forms the spirit of humankind in its midst:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “forms the spirit of a man within him”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 14:14; Psalm 75:8; Isaiah 51:22–23"> ‘Look, I am going to make Jerusalem a cup of reeling for all the surrounding nations; it will also be against Judah in the siege against Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 21:44; Zechariah 14:2–4; Daniel 2:34–35"> ⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “And it will happen”">c</a></sup>on that day I will make Jerusalem a stone weight<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “a heavy stone”">d</a></sup>for all the peoples. All those lifting it up will grievously hurt themselves, and all the nations of the earth will assemble against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:28; Zechariah 12:3; Zechariah 12:6"> On that day,’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">e</a></sup>Yahweh, ‘I will strike every horse with confusion, and the one riding it with madness; but over the house of Judah ⸤I will keep my eyes open⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “I will open my eyes,” that is, “I will be responsive” or “I will pay attention”">f</a></sup>and I will strike every horse of the peoples with blindness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 10:12; Joel 3:16; Psalm 18:32"> And the clans of Judah will say ⸤to themselves⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “in their heart”">g</a></sup>“The inhabitants of Jerusalem are a strength to us,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “me”">h</a></sup>through Yahweh of hosts, their God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Obadiah 1:18; Micah 5:5–8; Zechariah 2:4"> “ ‘On that day I will make the clans of Judah like a pan of fire among sticks of wood, and like a torch of fire among stalks<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “stalk”">i</a></sup>of grain, and they will devour to the right and to the left all the surrounding peoples, and Jerusalem will be inhabited again in its place, in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:18; Romans 3:27; Luke 1:51–53"> And Yahweh will save the tents of Judah first, so that the glory of the house of David and the glory of the inhabitants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Hebrew “inhabitant”">j</a></sup>of Jerusalem may not be exalted over Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Micah 5:2–4; Zechariah 9:8; Joel 3:16–17"> On that day Yahweh will put a shield around the inhabitants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Hebrew “inhabitant”">k</a></sup>of Jerusalem, and the one who stumbles among them on that day will be like David, and the house of David will be like God, like the angel of Yahweh, ⸤before them⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “to the face of them”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 54:17; Zechariah 14:2–3; Haggai 2:22"> ⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “And it will happen”">m</a></sup>on that day I will seek to destroy all the nations coming against Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; Ezekiel 39:29; Joel 2:28–29"> “ ‘I will pour on the house of David and on the inhabitants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Hebrew “inhabitant”">n</a></sup>of Jerusalem a spirit of grace and supplication, and they will look to me whom they pierced, and they shall mourn over him, as one wails over an only child, and they will grieve bitterly over him as one grieves bitterly over a firstborn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:24; 2 Kings 23:29"> On that day the wailing will be great in Jerusalem, like the wailing for Hadad Rimmon in the plain of Megiddo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zechariah 7:3; Luke 3:31; 2 Samuel 5:14"> And the land shall mourn, ⸤each clan by itself⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “clans, clans alone”">o</a></sup>the clan of the house of David by itself, and their wives by themselves; the clan of the house of Nathan by itself, and their wives by themselves;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:7; 2 Samuel 16:5; 1 Chronicles 23:10"> the clan of the house of Levi by itself, and their wives by themselves; the clan of the Shimeites by itself, and their wives by themselves;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 9:12"> and all those clans remaining, ⸤each clan by itself⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “clans, clans alone”">p</a></sup>and their wives by themselves.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>
