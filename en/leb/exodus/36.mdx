## Making the Tabernacle

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 4:17; Hebrews 5:4; Exodus 31:6"> And Moses called Bezalel and Oholiab and everyone skilled of heart, in whose heart Yahweh had put skill, all whose heart lifted him to come near to the work in order to do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:29; Exodus 35:27; Exodus 35:5–21"> And they took from Moses all the contributions<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew “contribution”">a</a></sup>that the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">b</a></sup>had brought for the work of the service for the sanctuary in order to do it, and they still brought to him voluntary offerings ⸤every morning⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “in the morning in the morning”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:10; Luke 12:42; Matthew 24:45"> And all the skilled workers who were doing all the work for the sanctuary came, ⸤each⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “man man”">d</a></sup>from his work that they were doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:6–10; 2 Corinthians 8:2–3; 2 Chronicles 24:14"> And they said to Moses, saying, “The people are ⸤bringing more⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “making much to bring”">e</a></sup>than enough for the service of the work that Yahweh has commanded ⸤to be done⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “to do it”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And Moses commanded, and they ⸤proclaimed the message⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “caused a voice to cross through”">g</a></sup>in the camp, saying, “Let no man or woman again make anything for the sanctuary contribution.” And so the people were restrained from bringing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:10"> And the material was enough for doing all the work, and it was left over.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 6:23; Exodus 26:1–37; Ezekiel 1:5–28"> And all who were skilled of heart among the doers of the work made the tabernacle with ten curtains of finely twisted linen and blue and purple and crimson yarns, with cherubim; he made them, the work of a skilled craftsman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The length of the one curtain was twenty-eight cubits, and the width was four cubits for the one curtain; one measurement was for all the curtains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:27; Exodus 26:3; Psalm 122:3"> And he joined five of the curtains ⸤one to another⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “one to one”">h</a></sup>and five curtains he joined ⸤one to another⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “one to one”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 26:4"> And he made loops of blue on the edge of the one curtain, at the end in the set; so he did on the edge of the end curtain in the second set.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 26:5; Exodus 26:10"> He made fifty loops on the one curtain, and he made fifty loops on the end of the curtain that was in the second set; the loops were opposite<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “interlinked” or “matching”">j</a></sup>⸤one to another⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “one to one”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4–5; Ephesians 2:20–22; 1 Corinthians 12:20"> And he made fifty gold clasps and joined the curtains ⸤one to another⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “one to one”">l</a></sup>with the clasps, so that the tabernacle was one.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 26:7–13"> And he made curtains of goat hair for a tent over the tabernacle; he made them eleven curtains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The length of the one curtain was thirty cubits, and the width was four cubits for the one curtain; one measure was for the eleven curtains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And he joined five curtains together and six curtains together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And he made fifty loops on the edge of the end curtain in the set, and he made fifty loops on the edge of the curtain in the second set.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And he made fifty bronze clasps for joining the tent to become one.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 26:14"> And he made a covering for the tent of red-dyed ram skin and a covering of fine leather to go above.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 25:5; Numbers 25:1; Exodus 40:18–19"> And he made the frames<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “boards” or “planks”">m</a></sup>for the tabernacle of acacia wood as uprights.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “standing”">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The length of the frame<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “board” or “plank”">o</a></sup>was ten cubits, and the width of the one frame<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “board” or “plank”">p</a></sup>was one and a half cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> He made two ⸤pegs⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “hands”">q</a></sup>for the one frame<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “board” or “plank”">r</a></sup>for joining ⸤one to another⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “one to one”">s</a></sup>and likewise for all the frames<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Or “boards” or “planks”">t</a></sup>of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And he made the frames<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “boards” or “planks”">u</a></sup>for the tabernacle with twenty frames<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “boards” or “planks”">v</a></sup>for the south<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or “southward southward”">w</a></sup>side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And he made forty silver bases under the twenty frames,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Or “boards” or “planks”">x</a></sup>with two bases under the one frame<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Or “board” or “plank”">y</a></sup>for its two ⸤pegs⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “hands”">z</a></sup>and two bases under the ⸤next⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “one”">aa</a></sup>frame<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Or “board” or “plank”">ab</a></sup>for its two ⸤pegs⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “hands”">ac</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And for the second side of the tabernacle, the north side, he made twenty frames<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Or “boards” or “planks”">ad</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and their forty silver bases, with two bases under the one frame<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Or “board” or “plank”">ae</a></sup>and two bases under the ⸤next⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “one”">af</a></sup>frame.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Or “board” or “plank”">ag</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 26:22; Exodus 26:27"> And for the rear of the tabernacle ⸤on the west⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Or “westward,” literally “seaward,” toward the Mediterranean Sea">ah</a></sup>he made six frames.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Or “boards” or “planks”">ai</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And he made two frames<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Or “boards” or “planks”">aj</a></sup>for the tabernacle corners at the rear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 4:32; Ephesians 3:18–19; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> And they were double at the bottom, and they were completely together on its top to the one ring; he did likewise for the two of them, for the two corners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 26:25"> And there were eight frames<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Or “boards” or “planks”">ak</a></sup>and their sixteen silver bases, two bases, two bases under the one frame.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Or “board” or “plank”">al</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 26:26–29; Exodus 25:28; Exodus 30:5"> And he made five bars of acacia wood for the frames<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Or “boards” or “planks”">am</a></sup>on the one side of the tabernacle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 26:26"> and five bars for the frames<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Or “boards” or “planks”">an</a></sup>on the second side of the tabernacle, and five bars for the frames<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Or “boards” or “planks”">ao</a></sup>at the rear ⸤on the west⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Or “westward,” literally “seaward,” toward the Mediterranean Sea">ap</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And he made the middle bar to run in the midst of the frames<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Or “boards” or “planks”">aq</a></sup>from end to end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And he overlaid the frames<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Or “boards” or “planks”">ar</a></sup>with gold, and he made their rings of gold as ⸤holders⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Literally “houses”">as</a></sup>for the bars, and he overlaid the bars with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; Hebrews 10:20; Exodus 40:21"> And he made the curtain of blue and purple and crimson yarns and finely twisted linen, the work of a craftsman; he made it with cherubim.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 26:36–37; Exodus 40:28"> And he made for the entrance of the tent a screen of blue and purple and crimson yarns and finely twisted linen, the work of an embroiderer,</span>
