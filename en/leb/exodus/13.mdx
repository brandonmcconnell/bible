## Unleavened Bread and Dedication of Firstborn to Commemorate the Rescue from Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 2:23; Numbers 3:13; Deuteronomy 15:19"> “Consecrate to me every firstborn, the first offspring of every womb among the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>among humans<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew “human”">b</a></sup>and among domestic animals;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “animal”">c</a></sup>⸤it belongs to me⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “to/for me it”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:18; Deuteronomy 5:15; Exodus 20:2"> And Moses said to the people, “Remember this day when you went out from Egypt, from a house of slaves, because with strength of hand Yahweh brought you out from here, and food with yeast will not be eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 34:18; Exodus 23:15; Exodus 12:2"> Today you are going out in the month of Abib.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Joshua 24:11; Exodus 12:25–26"> And when Yahweh brings you to the land of the Canaanites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew “Canaanite”">e</a></sup>and the Hittites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “Hittite”">f</a></sup>and the Amorites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew “Amorite”">g</a></sup>and the Hivites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “Hivite”">h</a></sup>and the Jebusites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “Jebusite”">i</a></sup>—which he swore to your ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “fathers”">j</a></sup>to give to you, a land flowing with milk and honey—you will perform this service in this month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 12:15–20; Exodus 34:18; Leviticus 23:8"> Seven days you will eat unleavened bread, and on the seventh day will be a feast for Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 12:19; Matthew 16:6"> Unleavened bread will be eaten the seven days; food with yeast will not be seen for you; and yeast will not be seen for you in all your territory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 13:14; Exodus 12:26–27; Psalm 44:1"> And you shall tell your son on that day, saying, ‘This is because of what Yahweh did for me when I came out from Egypt.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:8; Deuteronomy 11:18–19; Numbers 15:39"> And it will be as a sign on your hand and as a memorial between your eyes so that the law of Yahweh will be in your mouth, that with a strong hand Yahweh brought you out from Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 12:14; Exodus 12:24–25; Exodus 23:15"> And you will keep this statute at its appointed time ⸤from year to year⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “from days to days”">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 13:5"> “And when Yahweh brings you to the land of the Canaanite, as he swore to you and to your ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “fathers”">l</a></sup>and he gives it to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 18:15; Leviticus 27:26; Exodus 34:19"> you will hand over every first offspring of a womb to Yahweh, and every first offspring dropped by a domestic animal that will belong to you, the males will be for Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 34:20; Numbers 18:15–17; Revelation 14:4"> And every first offspring of a donkey you will redeem with small livestock, and if you will not redeem it, then you will break its neck, and every firstborn human among your sons you will redeem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 13:3; Joshua 4:6; Psalm 145:4"> And when your son asks you ⸤in the future⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “tomorrow”">m</a></sup>saying, ‘What is this?’ you will say to him, ‘With strength of hand Yahweh brought us out from Egypt, from a house of slaves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 12:29"> And when Pharaoh was stubborn to release us, Yahweh killed every firstborn in the land of Egypt, from firstborn human to firstborn domestic animal. Therefore I am sacrificing to Yahweh every first offspring of a womb, the males, and every firstborn of my sons I redeem.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 13:9; Deuteronomy 11:18; Deuteronomy 6:7–9"> And it will be as a sign on your hand and as symbolic ornaments between your eyes that with strength of hand Yahweh brought us out from Egypt.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Summary of Travel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 14:11–12; Nehemiah 9:17; Numbers 14:1–4"> And when Pharaoh released the people, God did not lead them the way of the land of the Philistines, though it was nearer, because God said, “Lest the people change their mind when they see war and return to Egypt.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 14:2; Deuteronomy 32:10; Numbers 33:6–8"> So God led the people around by the way of the desert to the ⸤Red Sea⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “sea of reed”">n</a></sup>and the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">o</a></sup>went up in battle array from the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 50:24–25; Joshua 24:32; Acts 7:16"> And Moses took the bones of Joseph with him because Joseph<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Hebrew “he”">p</a></sup>had made the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">q</a></sup>solemnly swear an oath, saying, “God will surely attend to you, and you will take up my bones from here with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 33:5–6; Exodus 12:37"> And they set out from Succoth, and they encamped at Etham on the edge of the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:1–2; Nehemiah 9:12; Deuteronomy 1:33"> And Yahweh was going before them by day in a column of cloud to lead them on the way and by night in a column of fire to give light to them to go by day and night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalm 121:5–8; Revelation 10:1"> The column of cloud by day and the column of fire by night did not depart from before the people.</span>
</div>
</div>
