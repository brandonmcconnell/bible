## Making Garments for Priests

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 31:10; Exodus 35:23; Exodus 35:19"> And from the blue and the purple and the crimson yarns they made woven garments for serving in the sanctuary, and they made the holy garments that were for Aaron, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 28:6–12; Leviticus 8:7; Exodus 25:7"> And he made the ephod of gold, blue, and purple and crimson yarns, and finely twisted linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 36:8; Exodus 26:1"> And he hammered out the leaves of gold, and he cut off cords to weave in the midst of the blue and in the midst of the purple and in the midst of the crimson and in the midst of the linen—the work of a skilled craftsman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> They made joined shoulder pieces for it; it was joined on its two edges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 1:13; Matthew 28:20; Isaiah 11:5"> And the waistband of his ephod, which ⸤was of one piece with it⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “on it from it”">a</a></sup>was of like work, gold, blue, and purple and crimson yarns, and finely twisted linen, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 28:9; Ezekiel 28:13; Job 28:16"> And they made onyx stones mounted in gold filigree settings, engraved with seal engravings according to the names of the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 4:7; Exodus 28:12; Exodus 28:29"> And he set them on the ephod’s shoulder pieces as stones of remembrance for the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">c</a></sup>as Yahweh had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Leviticus 8:8–9; Isaiah 59:17"> And he made the breast piece, the work of a skilled craftsman, like the work of the ephod, of gold, blue, and purple and crimson yarns, and finely twisted linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> It was squared; they made the breast piece doubled; its length was a span, and its width was a span when doubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 28:21; Exodus 28:16–17; Revelation 21:19–21"> And they filled it with four rows of stone; a row of carnelian, topaz, and emerald was the first row;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 28:18; Ezekiel 28:13"> and the second row was a malachite, a sapphire, and a moonstone;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and the third row was a jacinth, an agate, and an amethyst;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and the fourth row was a turquoise, an onyx, and a jasper. They were set with gold filigree settings in their mountings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 21:12"> And the stones were according to the names of the ⸤Israelites⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">d</a></sup>they were twelve according to their names, with seal engravings, each according to its name for the twelve tribes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 10:28; John 17:12; 1 Peter 1:5"> And they made on the breast piece braided chains, a work of pure gold ornamental cord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 25:12"> And they made two gold filigree settings and two gold rings, and they put the two rings on the two edges of the breast piece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And they put the two gold ornamental cords on the two rings on the edges of the breast piece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 28:14; Song of Solomon 1:10"> And they put the two ends of the two ornamental cords on the two filigree settings, and they put them on the shoulder pieces of the ephod at the front of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And they made two gold rings, and they placed them on the two edges of the breast piece, on its lip that is on ⸤the other side⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “beyond”">e</a></sup>of the ephod, ⸤to the inside⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “houseward,” “to house,” meaning “inward”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 26:3"> And they made two gold rings and put them on the ephod’s two shoulder pieces below, at its front near its seam above the waistband of the ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 16:24; 1 Corinthians 1:25; 1 Corinthians 1:27"> And they tied the breast piece by its rings to the rings of the ephod with a blue cord so that the breast piece would be on the waistband of the ephod and not come loose from the ephod, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 28:31–35"> And he made the robe of the ephod, weaver’s work, totally of blue yarn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And the opening of the robe in the middle of it was like the opening of a sturdy garment, with an edge for its opening all around so that it would not be torn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 28:33; Galatians 5:22"> And they made on the hem of the robe pomegranates of finely twisted blue and purple and crimson.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 28:33–34; Song of Solomon 4:13; Psalm 89:15"> And they made pure gold bells and put the bells in the midst of the pomegranates on the hem of the robe all around in the midst of the pomegranates,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 28:34; Song of Solomon 4:13; Song of Solomon 6:7"> a bell and a pomegranate, a bell and a pomegranate on the hem of the robe all around for serving, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:18; 1 Peter 1:13; Isaiah 61:10"> And they made the tunics of fine linen, a weaver’s work, for Aaron and for his sons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Ezekiel 44:18; Exodus 28:39"> and the turban of fine linen and the headdresses of the headbands of fine linen and undergarments of the linen cloth, finely twisted,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 28:39"> and the sash of finely twisted linen and blue and purple and crimson yarns, the work of an embroiderer, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; Hebrews 7:26; Hebrews 1:3"> And they made the rosette<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “medallion”">g</a></sup>of the holy diadem of pure gold, and they wrote on it with the writing of seal engravings: “A holy object for Yahweh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And they put a blue cord on it to put it above on the turban, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Preparations Complete

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 39:42–43; Matthew 28:20; Exodus 25:40"> And all the work of the tabernacle of the tent of assembly was finished, and the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">h</a></sup>had done according to all that Yahweh had commanded Moses; so they did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 35:11–19; Exodus 31:7–11; Exodus 36:1–38"> And they brought the tabernacle to Moses, the tent and all its equipment, its hooks, its frames,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “boards” or “planks”">i</a></sup>its bars, and its pillars and its bases;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> and the covering of the red-dyed ram skins and the covering of fine leather and the curtain of the screen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Hebrews 9:5; Exodus 25:17; Hebrews 9:8"> the ark of the testimony and its poles and the atonement cover;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 25:30; 1 Kings 7:48"> the table, all its equipment, and the bread of the presence;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Philippians 2:15; Exodus 27:21; Matthew 5:14–16"> the pure gold lampstand, its lamps—the lamps of the row—and all its equipment and the oil of the light;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 30:7; Exodus 37:29; Exodus 30:3"> the gold altar, the anointing oil, the fragrant incense, and the screen of the entrance of the tent;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 8:64; Exodus 38:30"> the bronze altar, and the bronze grating that is for it, its poles, and all its equipment, the basin and its stand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Exodus 27:9–16"> the hangings of the courtyard, its pillars, and its bases; and the screen for the courtyard gate, its tent cords and its ⸤pegs⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “hands”">j</a></sup>and all the equipment of the service of the tabernacle for the tent of assembly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 28:2; Exodus 31:10; Exodus 39:1"> the woven garments for serving in the sanctuary—the holy garments for Aaron the priest and the garments for his sons to serve as priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Exodus 35:10; Exodus 23:21–22; 2 Timothy 2:15"> According to all that Yahweh had commanded Moses, so the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">k</a></sup>did all the work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 6:23–27; Leviticus 9:22–23; 2 Samuel 6:18"> And Moses saw all the work, and, indeed, they had done it as Yahweh had commanded; so they did, and Moses blessed them.</span>
</div>
