## Regulations Regarding Hebrew Slaves

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:14; Deuteronomy 6:20; Exodus 24:3–4"> “And these are the regulations that you will set before them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:12–15; Exodus 12:44; Deuteronomy 15:18"> ‘If you buy a Hebrew slave, he will serve six years, and in the seventh he will go out as free for nothing.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “for no payment”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:12–14"> If he comes in single, he will go out single. If he is the husband of a wife, his wife will go out with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> If his master gives him a wife and she bears for him sons or daughters, the wife and her children will belong to her master, and the slave will go out single.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:14–15; Deuteronomy 15:16–17; Isaiah 26:13"> But if the slave explicitly says, “I love my master, my wife, and my children; I will not go out free,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:17; Leviticus 25:40; Deuteronomy 19:17–18"> his master will present him to God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “present him to the judges”">b</a></sup>and bring him to the door or to the doorpost, and his master will pierce his ear with an awl, and he will serve him forever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:5; Exodus 21:2–3"> “ ‘And if a man sells his daughter as a slave woman, she will not go out as male slaves go out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 28:8; Deuteronomy 20:7; Malachi 2:11–15"> If ⸤she does not please her master⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “bad in the eyes of her master”">c</a></sup>who selected her, he will allow her to be redeemed; he has no authority to sell her to foreign people, since he has dealt treacherously with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And if he selects her for his son, he shall do for her according to the regulations for daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:1–6"> If he takes for himself another, he will not reduce her food, her clothing, or her right of cohabitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And if he does not do for her these three, she shall go out for nothing; there will not be silver<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “money”">d</a></sup>paid for her.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Regulations Regarding Murder, Manslaughter, and Various Injuries

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 9:6; Matthew 26:52; Leviticus 24:17"> “ ‘⸤Whoever strikes someone⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “A striker of a man”">e</a></sup>and he dies will surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 20:2–9; 1 Samuel 24:18; Deuteronomy 4:41–43"> But if he did not lie in wait ⸤and it was an accident⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “and God let him happen to his hand”">f</a></sup>I will appoint for you a place to which he may flee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 2:28–34; Hebrews 10:26; Numbers 15:30–31"> But if a man schemes against his neighbor to kill him by treachery,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “with cunning” or “craftily”">g</a></sup>you will take him from my altar to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:18–21; Proverbs 30:17; 1 Timothy 1:9"> And ⸤whoever strikes⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “a striker of”">h</a></sup>his father or his mother will surely be put to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:7; 1 Timothy 1:10; Exodus 22:4"> “ ‘And ⸤whoever kidnaps someone⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “a stealer of a man”">i</a></sup>and sells him, or he is found in his possession,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “in his hand”">j</a></sup>he will surely be put to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 20:20; Mark 7:10–11; Deuteronomy 27:16"> “ ‘And one who curses his father or his mother will surely be put to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 21:20; Deuteronomy 25:11; 2 Samuel 14:6"> “ ‘And if men quarrel and a man strikes his neighbor with a stone or with a fist and he does not die, but ⸤he is confined to bed⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “he falls to bed”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:29; Zechariah 8:4"> if he stands and walks about in the outside on his staff, the striker will be unpunished; he will only pay for his inactivity<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “his sitting”">l</a></sup>⸤toward his full recovery⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “and he indeed will recover”">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 29:19; Deuteronomy 19:21; Numbers 35:19"> And if a man strikes his male slave or his female slave with the rod and he dies under his hand, he will surely be avenged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 25:44–46"> Yet if he survives a day or two days, he will not be avenged, because he is his money.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “property”; literally “his silver”">n</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:18–19; Exodus 21:30; Deuteronomy 16:18"> “ ‘And if men fight and they injure a pregnant woman, and her children go out and there is not serious injury, he will surely be fined as the woman’s husband demands concerning him ⸤and as the judges determine⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “and he will give according to judgments” or “judges” or “arbitrators”">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:21; Numbers 35:31; Leviticus 24:19"> And if there is serious injury, you will give life in place of life,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:21; Matthew 5:38–40; Matthew 7:2"> eye in place of eye, tooth in place of tooth, hand in place of hand, foot in place of foot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> burn in place of burn, wound in place of wound, bruise in place of bruise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 21:20; Job 31:13–15; Psalm 72:12–14"> “ ‘And if a man strikes the eye of his male slave or the eye of his female slave and destroys it, he shall release him as free in place of his eye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And if he causes the tooth of his male slave or the tooth of his female slave to fall out, he will release him as free in place of his tooth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 21:32; Leviticus 20:15–16; Genesis 9:5–6"> “ ‘And if an ox gores a man or a woman and he dies, the ox will surely be stoned, and its meat will not be eaten, and the owner of the ox is innocent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:1–9"> But if it was a goring ox ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “yesterday three days ago”">p</a></sup>and its owner was warned and did not restrain it and it kills a man or a woman, the ox will be stoned, and the owner also will be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 21:22; Exodus 30:12; Numbers 35:31–33"> If a ransom is set on him, he will pay the redemption money for his life according to all that is set on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> If it gores a son or it gores a daughter, according to this regulation it shall be done to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 26:15; Zechariah 11:12–13; Matthew 27:3–9"> If the ox gores a male slave or a female slave, he will give thirty shekels of silver to his master, and the ox will be stoned.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 28:10; Jeremiah 18:20; Ecclesiastes 10:8"> “ ‘If a man opens a pit or if a man digs a pit and he does not cover it and an ox or a donkey falls into it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 22:6; Exodus 21:29–30; Exodus 22:14"> the owner of the pit will pay restitution; he will pay silver to its owner, but the dead animal will be for him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “will belong to him”">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And if a man’s ox injures the ox of his neighbor and it dies, they will sell the living ox and divide the ⸤money⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “its silver”">r</a></sup>and they will also divide the dead one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Or if it was known that it was a goring ox ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “yesterday three days ago”">s</a></sup>and its owner did not restrain it, he will surely make restitution, an ox in place of the ox, and the dead one will be for him.</span>
</div>
</div>
