## Regulations Regarding Work and Festivals

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 25:3–4"> “ ‘And six years you will sow your land and gather its yield.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 26:34–35; Leviticus 25:2–7; Leviticus 25:11–12"> But the seventh you will let it rest and leave it fallow, and the poor of your people will eat, and their remainder the animals<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Hebrew “animal”">j</a></sup>of the field will eat. You will do likewise for your vineyard and for your olive trees.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 13:14; Exodus 34:21; Exodus 35:3"> “ ‘Six days you will do your work, but on the seventh day you will stop so that your ox and your donkey will rest and the son of your slave woman and the alien will be refreshed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 23:7; Zechariah 13:2; 1 Timothy 4:16"> “ ‘And you will be attentive to all that I have said to you, and you will not ⸤profess⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “cause to remember,” “bring to remembrance,” or “mention” in prayer or praise">k</a></sup>the name of other gods; it will not be heard in your mouth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 34:22–24; Leviticus 23:34"> “ ‘Three times in the year you will hold a festival for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 22:7; Exodus 34:20; Numbers 28:16–25"> You will keep the Feast of Unleavened Bread; for seven days you will eat unleavened bread, as I commanded you at the appointed time, the month of Abib, because in it you came out from Egypt, and ⸤no one will⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “they will not”">l</a></sup>appear before me empty-handed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 34:22; Exodus 22:29; Leviticus 23:9–21"> And you will keep the Feast of Harvest, with the firstfruits of your work, what you sow in the field. And you will keep the Feast of Harvest Gathering when the year goes out, when you gather your work from the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 34:23; Exodus 23:14"> Three times in the year all your men will appear before the Lord Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 34:25; Leviticus 2:11; Deuteronomy 16:4"> “ ‘You will not sacrifice the blood of my sacrifice together with food with yeast, and you will not leave the fat of my feast overnight until morning.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 34:26; Deuteronomy 14:21; Deuteronomy 26:10"> “ ‘The best of the firstfruits of your land you will bring to the house of Yahweh your God.<br />“ ‘You will not boil a young goat<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “kid”">m</a></sup>in its mother’s milk.</span>
</div>

## Reasons for Loyal Obedience

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 78:56; Psalm 78:40; Hebrews 12:25"> Be attentive to him and listen to his voice; do not rebel against him, because he will not forgive your transgression, for my name is in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Deuteronomy 30:7; Zechariah 2:8"> But if you listen attentively to his voice and do all that I say, I will be an enemy to your enemies and a foe to your foes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 23:20; Isaiah 5:13; Genesis 15:19–21"> When my angel goes before you and brings you to the Amorites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Hebrew “Amorite”">n</a></sup>and the Hittites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Hebrew “Hittite”">o</a></sup>and the Perizzites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Hebrew “Perizzite”">p</a></sup>and the Canaanites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Hebrew “Canaanite”">q</a></sup>and the Hivites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Hebrew “Hivite”">r</a></sup>and the Jebusites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Hebrew “Jebusite”">s</a></sup>I will wipe them out.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:15; Exodus 15:26; Joshua 22:5"> And you will serve Yahweh your God, and he will bless your bread and your water, and I will remove sickness from among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:14; Job 5:26; Deuteronomy 28:4"> There will be no one suffering miscarriage or infertile in your land. I will make full the number of your days.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Or “give you a full life span”">t</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:25; Genesis 35:5; Deuteronomy 11:25"> “ ‘I will release my terror before you, and I will throw into confusion all the people against whom you come, and I will ⸤make all your enemies turn their back to you⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “give all your enemies to you back”">u</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:20; Joshua 24:11–12"> And I will send the hornet before you, and it will drive out the Hivites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Hebrew “Hivite”">v</a></sup>the Canaanites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Hebrew “Canaanite”">w</a></sup>and the Hittites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Hebrew “Hittite”">x</a></sup>from before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:22; Joshua 17:12–13; Judges 3:1–4"> I will not drive them out from before you in one year, lest the land become a desolation and ⸤the wild animals⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “the animal of the field”">y</a></sup>multiply against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Little by little I will drive them out from before you until you are fruitful and take possession of the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; Deuteronomy 11:24; Joshua 21:44"> “ ‘And I will set your boundary from the ⸤Red Sea⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “sea of reed”">z</a></sup>and up to the sea of the Philistines and from the desert up to the river,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Or “the Euphrates”">aa</a></sup>because I will give the inhabitants of the land into your hand, and you will drive them out from before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Exodus 34:12; Exodus 34:15"> You will not make a covenant with them and with their gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalm 106:36; Deuteronomy 7:16; Exodus 34:12"> They will not live in your land, lest they cause you to sin against me when you serve their gods, for it will be a snare to you.’ ”</span>
</div>
