## Instructions for Making the Bronze Altar

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 38:1–7; Exodus 20:24–26; Exodus 40:10"> “And you will make the altar of acacia wood, five cubits long and five cubits wide; the altar will be square, and its height will be three cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 118:27; Exodus 29:12; Leviticus 4:7"> And you will make its horns on its four corners; its horns will be ⸤of one piece with it⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “from it”">a</a></sup>and you will overlay it with bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 7:45; Numbers 4:14; 1 Chronicles 28:17"> And you will make its pots for removing its fat-soaked ashes and its shovels and its sprinkling bowls and its forks<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1 Samuel 2:13 mentions a fork with “three teeth” used by priests">b</a></sup>and its fire pans; you will make all its equipment with bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 35:16; Exodus 25:12; Exodus 38:4–5"> And you will make for it a grating, a work of bronze network, and you will make on the network four bronze rings on its four ends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 38:4"> And you will put it under the ledge of the altar, below, and the network will be up to the middle of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 35:13–15; Numbers 4:44; Exodus 30:4"> And you will make poles for the altar, poles of acacia wood, and you will overlay them with bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 25:28; Exodus 30:4; Numbers 4:13–14"> And the poles will be put into the rings, and the poles will be on the two sides of the altar when carrying it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Exodus 25:9; Hebrews 8:5"> You will make it hollow with boards. As it was shown you on the mountain, so they will do.</span>

## Instructions for Making the Courtyard

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 38:9–20; Ezekiel 40:32; Ezekiel 42:3"> “You will make the courtyard of the tabernacle; for the ⸤south⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “south southward”">c</a></sup>side will be hangings for the courtyard of finely twisted linen, one hundred cubits long for the one side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 26:19–21; Jeremiah 52:21; Exodus 36:38"> And its twenty pillars and their twenty bases will be bronze; the hooks of the pillars and their bands will be silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And likewise for the north side along the length will be hangings one hundred cubits long; and its twenty pillars and their bases will be bronze; the hooks of the pillars and their bands will be silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And the width of the courtyard for the west<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “sea”">d</a></sup>side will be hangings of fifty cubits, their ten pillars and their ten bases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the width of the courtyard for the east side, toward sunrise, will be fifty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:9; Exodus 26:36"> And hangings for the shoulder<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “side,” referring to the span on one side of the courtyard’s entry">e</a></sup>will be fifteen cubits with their three pillars and their three bases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And fifteen cubits of hangings will be for the second shoulder<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “side,” referring to the span on one side of the courtyard’s entry">f</a></sup>with their three pillars and their three bases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 26:36; Judges 5:30; Exodus 28:39"> And for the gate of the courtyard there will be a screen of twenty cubits of blue and purple and crimson yarns and finely twisted linen, the work of an embroiderer; with their four pillars and their four bases.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> “All the pillars of the courtyard all around will be banded with silver, and their hooks will be silver, and their bases will be bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The length of the courtyard will be one hundred cubits and the width fifty cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “fifty with fifty.” Some suggest that the word for cubits has been accidentally omitted and the number fifty repeated in the Hebrew text. Others suggest that repetition of the number is original and indicates that the two ends of the courtyard are the same size">g</a></sup>and the height five cubits, of finely twisted linen, with their bronze bases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 35:18; Isaiah 22:23–25; Numbers 4:32"> Bronze will be for all the equipment of the tabernacle in all its service and all its ⸤pegs⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “hands”">h</a></sup>and all the ⸤pegs⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “hands”">i</a></sup>of the courtyard.</span>
</div>
