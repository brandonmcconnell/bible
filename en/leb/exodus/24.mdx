## Confirming the Covenant

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 11:16; Exodus 6:23; Leviticus 10:1–2"> And to Moses he said, “Go up to Yahweh—you and Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, and seventy from the elders of Israel—and you will worship at a distance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 24:15; Exodus 24:13; Hebrews 10:21–22"> And Moses ⸤alone⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “by himself”; literally “to the solitude of him”">a</a></sup>will come near to Yahweh, and they will not come near, and the people will not go up with him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 19:8; Exodus 24:7; Joshua 24:22"> And Moses came, and he told the people all the words of Yahweh and all the regulations. And all the people answered with one voice, and they said, “All the words that Yahweh has spoken we will do.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 28:18; Deuteronomy 31:9; Joshua 4:8–9"> And Moses wrote all the words of Yahweh, and he rose early in the morning, and he built an altar at the base of the mountain and set up twelve memorial stones for the twelve tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 18:12; Leviticus 3:1–17; Exodus 19:22"> And he sent young men from the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">b</a></sup>and they offered burnt offerings, and they sacrificed sacrifices as fellowship offerings to Yahweh using bulls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 1:19; Exodus 24:8; Hebrews 9:18"> And Moses took half of the blood, and he put it in bowls, and half of the blood he sprinkled on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:11–13; 1 Thessalonians 5:27; Exodus 24:3–4"> And he took the scroll of the covenant and read it in the hearing of the people, and they said, “All that Yahweh has spoken we will do, and we will listen.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “we will attentively do” or “we will obediently do”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:4–5; Exodus 24:6; Ezekiel 36:25"> And Moses took the blood and sprinkled it on the people, and he said, “Look, the blood of the covenant that Yahweh has made with you in accordance with all these words.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 24:1"> And Moses and Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, and seventy from the elders of Israel went up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 1:18; Ezekiel 10:1; Genesis 32:30"> And they saw the God of Israel, and what was under his feet was like sapphire tile work and like the very heavens for clearness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 15:23–24; Exodus 18:12; Genesis 31:54"> And toward the leaders of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">d</a></sup>he did not stretch out his hand, and they beheld God, and they ate, and they drank.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Start of Forty Days and Nights on Mount Sinai

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:22; Exodus 31:18; Exodus 32:15–16"> And Yahweh said to Moses, “Come up to me on the mountain, and be there, and I will give you the tablets of stone and the law and the commandments that I have written to instruct them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 33:11; Numbers 11:28; Exodus 3:1"> And Moses got up, and Joshua, his assistant, and Moses went up to the mountain of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 17:10; Exodus 17:12; Genesis 22:5"> And to the elders he said, “Wait for us here until we return to you. And look, Aaron and Hur are with you. Whoever ⸤has a dispute⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “is owner of words”">e</a></sup>will bring it to you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 19:9; Matthew 17:5; Exodus 19:16"> And Moses went up to the mountain, and the cloud covered the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 16:10; Numbers 16:42; Ezekiel 1:28"> And the glory of Yahweh settled on Mount Sinai, and the cloud covered it for six days, and he called to Moses on the seventh day from the midst of the cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Deuteronomy 4:24; Exodus 3:2"> And the appearance of the glory of Yahweh was like a consuming fire on the top of the mountain to the eyes of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “in the sight of”">f</a></sup>the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:9; Exodus 34:28; Deuteronomy 10:10"> And Moses went into the midst of the cloud, and he went up the mountain, and Moses was on the mountain forty days and forty nights.</span>
</div>
