## Plague Eight: Locusts

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 4:21; Exodus 7:13–14; Exodus 3:20"> And Yahweh said to Moses, “Go to Pharaoh, for I have made his heart ⸤insensitive⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “heavy”">a</a></sup>and the heart of his servants in order to put these signs of mine in his midst,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joel 1:3; Deuteronomy 4:9; Deuteronomy 6:20–22"> so that you will tell in the ears of your child and ⸤your grandchild⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “the son of your son”">b</a></sup>that I dealt harshly with the Egyptians and so that you will tell about my signs that I have done among them, and so you will know that I am Yahweh.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 5:6; 1 Kings 21:29; James 4:10"> And Moses and Aaron came to Pharaoh, and they said to him, “Thus says Yahweh, the God of the Hebrews, ‘Until when will you refuse to submit before me? Release my people so that they may serve me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 9:3; Joel 2:25; Exodus 11:4–5"> But if you are refusing to release my people, look, I am about to bring locusts into your territory tomorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joel 1:4; Exodus 9:32; Joel 2:25"> And they will cover the surface of the land, and no one will be able to see the land, and they will eat the remainder of what is left—what is left over for you from the hail—and they will eat every sprouting tree belonging to you from the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 8:21; Exodus 8:3; Exodus 11:6"> And your houses will be full, and the houses of all your servants and the houses of all Egypt, something that your fathers and ⸤your grandfathers⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “the fathers of your fathers”">c</a></sup>never saw from the day they were on the earth until this day.’ ” And he turned and went out from Pharaoh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:21; Exodus 23:33; Joshua 23:13"> And the servants of Pharaoh said to him, “Until when will this be a snare for us? Release the men so that they may serve Yahweh their God. Do you not yet know that Egypt is destroyed?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 10:24; Exodus 12:31; Exodus 8:8"> And Moses and Aaron were brought back to Pharaoh, and he said to them, “Serve Yahweh your God. ⸤Who are the ones going⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “who and who the goers?”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 5:1; 1 Corinthians 5:7–8; Deuteronomy 31:12–13"> And Moses said, “With our young and with our old we will go; with our sons and with our daughters, with our sheep and goats and with our cattle we will go because it is the feast of Yahweh for us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 13:21; 2 Chronicles 32:15; Exodus 12:30–31"> And he said to them, “Let Yahweh be thus with you as soon as I release you and your dependents.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “little children” or “little ones”">e</a></sup>See that evil is before your faces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 10:28; Psalm 119:69; Exodus 5:4"> ⸤No indeed⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “not so”">f</a></sup>just the men<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="This word for “men” indicates that Pharaoh means only the able-bodied men can go">g</a></sup>go and serve Yahweh, since this is what you are seeking.” And he drove them out from the presence of Pharaoh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 7:19; Exodus 10:4–5"> And Yahweh said to Moses, “Stretch out your hand over the land of Egypt with the locusts so that they may come up over the land of Egypt, and let them eat all the vegetation of the land, all that the hail left behind.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jonah 1:4; Psalm 78:26; Psalm 148:8"> And Moses stretched out his staff over the land of Egypt, and Yahweh drove an east wind into the land all that day and all night. The morning came, and the east wind had brought the locusts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 78:46; Joel 1:2–4; Psalm 105:34–35"> And the locusts went up over all the land of Egypt, and they settled in all the territory of Egypt, very ⸤severe⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “heavy”">h</a></sup>Before it there were not locusts like them, nor will there be after it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 10:5; Psalm 78:46; Joel 1:6–7"> And they covered the surface of all the land, and the land was dark with them, and they ate all the vegetation of the land and all the fruit of the trees that the hail had left, and no green was left in the trees nor in the vegetation of the field in all the land of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 9:27; 2 Samuel 19:20; 1 Samuel 15:30"> And Pharaoh hurried to call Moses and Aaron, and he said, “I have sinned against Yahweh your God and against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 8:8; Exodus 9:28; 1 Kings 13:6"> And now forgive my sin surely this time, and pray to Yahweh your God so that he may only remove from me this death.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 6:28; Exodus 8:28–30; Exodus 8:9"> And he went out from Pharaoh, and he prayed to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joel 2:20; Exodus 13:18; Exodus 15:4"> And Yahweh turned a very strong ⸤west wind⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “sea wind,” a wind from the Mediterranean Sea to the west">i</a></sup>and lifted up the locusts and thrust them into the ⸤Red Sea⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “sea of reed”">j</a></sup>and not one locust remained in all the territory of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 4:21; Exodus 11:10; Deuteronomy 2:30"> And Yahweh hardened Pharaoh’s heart, and he did not release the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Plague Nine: Darkness

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 16:10–11; Jude 1:6; Luke 23:44"> And Yahweh said to Moses, “Stretch out your hand toward the heavens so that there may be darkness over the land of Egypt and so that a person can feel darkness.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalm 105:28; Revelation 16:10; Exodus 20:21"> And Moses stretched out his hand toward the heavens, and there was darkness of night in all the land of Egypt for three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 8:22; Isaiah 42:16; 1 Peter 2:9"> No one could see his brother, and ⸤because of it no one could move from where they were⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “a man did not rise from under it”">l</a></sup>for three days, but there was light for the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">m</a></sup>in their dwellings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 10:8–10; Exodus 8:28; Genesis 34:23"> And Pharaoh called Moses and said, “Go, serve Yahweh. Only your sheep and goats and your cattle must be left behind. Your dependents<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “little children” or “little ones”; the term here includes women and children and men too old or ill to fight">n</a></sup>may also go with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 9:22; Leviticus 16:9; Exodus 29:1–46"> And Moses said, “Even if you yourself put into our hand sacrifices and burnt offerings and we offer them to Yahweh our God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 23:18; Acts 2:44–45; Proverbs 3:9"> our livestock must also go with us. Not a hoof can be left because we must take from them to serve Yahweh our God. And we will not know with what we are to serve Yahweh until we come there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 10:20; Exodus 4:21; Exodus 14:8"> And Yahweh hardened Pharaoh’s heart, and he was not willing to release them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:16; 2 Chronicles 16:10; Amos 7:13"> And Pharaoh said to him, “Go from me. ⸤Be careful⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “watch for you”">o</a></sup>not to see my face again, because on the day of your seeing my face you will die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 11:27; Exodus 12:30–31; Exodus 11:4–8"> And Moses said, “⸤That is right⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “right you said”">p</a></sup>⸤I will not again see your face⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “I will not add again seeing of your face”">q</a></sup></span>
</div>
