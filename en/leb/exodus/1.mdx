## Israel and Oppression in Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:1–2; Genesis 46:8–26; Genesis 35:23–26"> And these are the names of the sons of Israel who came to Egypt; with Jacob, they each came with his ⸤family⸥:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “house”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 35:22"> Reuben, Simeon, Levi, and Judah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 28:20; Genesis 35:23"> Issachar, Zebulun, and Benjamin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Dan and Naphtali, Gad and Asher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:22; Genesis 46:26–27; Judges 8:30"> And ⸤all those who descended from Jacob⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “every soul/individual/life, the ones going out from the loins of Jacob”">b</a></sup>were seventy individuals,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “souls,” “lives”">c</a></sup>and Joseph was in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 50:26; Genesis 50:24; Acts 7:14–16"> And Joseph died and all of his brothers and all of that generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 46:3; Genesis 1:28; Deuteronomy 26:5"> And the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">d</a></sup>were fruitful and multiplied and were many and were very, very numerous, and the land was filled with them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 7:18; Ecclesiastes 2:18–19; Ecclesiastes 9:15"> And a new king rose over Egypt who did not know Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 105:24–25; Ecclesiastes 4:4; James 3:14–16"> And he said to his people, “Look, the people of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">e</a></sup>are greater and more numerous than us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 7:19; Psalm 83:3–4; Proverbs 1:11"> Come, we must deal shrewdly with them, lest they become many, and when war happens, they also will join our enemies and will fight against us and go up from the land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 15:13; Genesis 47:11; Exodus 3:7"> And they appointed commanders of forced labor over them in order to oppress them with their ⸤forced labor⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “burdens” or “burdensome labor”">f</a></sup>and they built storage cities for Pharaoh—Pithom and Rameses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 105:24; Exodus 1:9; Romans 8:28"> And as he oppressed them, so they became many, and so they spread out, and the Egyptians were afraid because of the presence of the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:20"> And the Egyptians ruthlessly compelled the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">h</a></sup>to work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 7:19; Numbers 20:15; Exodus 2:23"> And they made their lives bitter with hard work with mortar and with bricks and with all sorts of work in the field—with all their work in which they ruthlessly enslaved them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the king of Egypt said to the Hebrew midwives—of whom the name of the one was Shiphrah and the name of the second was Puah—</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 1:22; Matthew 21:38; Revelation 12:4"> and he said, “When you help the Hebrews give birth, you will look upon the pairs of testicles; if he is a son, you will put him to death, and if she is a daughter, she will live.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Exodus 1:21; Matthew 10:28"> But the midwives feared God, and they did not do as the king of Egypt had said to them. They let the boys live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:4; 2 Samuel 13:28"> And the king of Egypt summoned the midwives, and he said to them, “Why have you done this thing and let the boys live?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 2:4–24; 2 Samuel 17:19–20; 1 Samuel 21:2"> And the midwives said to Pharaoh, “Because the Hebrew women are not like the Egyptian women, because they are vigorous; before the midwife comes to them, they have given birth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 3:10; Ecclesiastes 8:12; Proverbs 11:18"> And God did the midwives good, and the Israelite people became many and were very numerous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 11:38; 1 Samuel 2:35; Psalm 37:3"> ⸤And so⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “and he/it was”">i</a></sup>because the midwives feared God, he gave them ⸤families⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “houses”">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 7:19; Proverbs 1:16; Exodus 1:16"> And Pharaoh commanded all his people, saying, “Every son who is born you will throw into the Nile, and every daughter you will let live.”</span>
</div>
