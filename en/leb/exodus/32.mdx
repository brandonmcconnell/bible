## The Golden Calf

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 7:40; Deuteronomy 9:9; Exodus 24:18"> And the people saw that Moses delayed to come down from the mountain, and the people gathered opposite Aaron, and they said to him, “Come,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “arise” or “get up”">a</a></sup>make for us gods who will go before us, because this Moses, the man who brought us up from the land of Egypt, we do not know what has become of him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 8:24–27; Exodus 12:35–36; Ezekiel 16:17"> And Aaron said to them, “Take off the rings of gold that are on the ears of your wives, your sons, and your daughters, and bring it to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 46:6; Isaiah 40:19–20; Jeremiah 10:9"> And all the people took off the rings of gold that were on their ears and brought it to Aaron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:18; Deuteronomy 9:16; 1 Kings 12:28"> And he took from their hand, and he shaped it with a tool, and he made it a cast-image bull calf, and they said, “These are your gods, Israel, who brought you up from the land of Egypt.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 10:20; Leviticus 23:2; Leviticus 23:37"> And Aaron saw, and he built an altar before it, and Aaron called, and he said, “A feast for Yahweh tomorrow.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:7; Numbers 25:2; Acts 7:41–42"> And they started early the next day, and they offered burnt offerings, and they presented fellowship offerings, and the people sat to eat and drink, and they rose up to revel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 32:11; Hosea 9:9; Judges 2:19"> And Yahweh spoke to Moses, “Go, go down because your people behave corruptly, whom you brought up from the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 12:28; Exodus 20:23; Exodus 20:3–4"> They have turned aside quickly from the way that I commanded them; they have made for themselves a cast-image bull calf, and they bowed to it, and they sacrificed to it, and they said, ‘These are your gods, Israel, who brought you up from the land of Egypt.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Deuteronomy 9:13; Isaiah 48:4"> And Yahweh said to Moses, “I have seen this people, and, indeed, they are a stiff-necked people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:14; Numbers 14:12; Deuteronomy 9:19"> And now leave me alone so that ⸤my anger may blaze⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “my nose may become hot”">b</a></sup>against them, and let me destroy them, and I will make you into a great nation.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:18–20; Deuteronomy 9:26–29; Psalm 106:23"> And Moses ⸤implored Yahweh⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “implored the face of Yahweh”">c</a></sup>his God, and he said, “Why, Yahweh, should ⸤your anger blaze⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “your nose become hot”">d</a></sup>against your people whom you brought up from the land of Egypt with great power and with a strong hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 14:13–16; Deuteronomy 9:28; Joshua 7:9"> Why should the Egyptians ⸤say⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “say, saying”">e</a></sup>, ‘With evil intent he brought them out to kill them in the mountains and wipe them from the face of the earth’?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “ground”">f</a></sup>Turn from ⸤your fierce anger⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “the heat of your nose”">g</a></sup>and relent concerning the disaster for your people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 6:13; Genesis 12:7; Genesis 22:16"> Remember Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, your servants, to whom you swore by yourself, and you told them, ‘I will multiply your offspring like the stars of the heavens, and all this land that I promised I will give to your offspring, and they will inherit it forever.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jonah 3:10; Psalm 106:45; Jeremiah 26:13"> And Yahweh relented concerning the disaster that he had ⸤threatened⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “spoken”">h</a></sup>to do to his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:15; Exodus 40:20; Deuteronomy 5:22"> And Moses turned and went down from the mountain, and the two tablets of the testimony were in his hand, tablets written on their two sides; ⸤on the front and on the back⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “from this and from this”">i</a></sup>they were written.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 31:18; Hebrews 8:10; Deuteronomy 10:1"> And the tablets, they were the work of God; and the writing, it was the writing of God engraved on the tablets.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 24:13; Exodus 17:9; Joshua 6:20"> And Joshua heard the sound of the people in their shouting, and he said to Moses, “A sound of war is in the camp.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 15:1–18; Daniel 5:4; Daniel 5:23"> But he said, “There is not a sound of shouting of victory, and there is not a sound of shouting of defeat. I hear a sound of singing.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:16–17; Jeremiah 31:32; Lamentations 5:15"> ⸤And⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “and it was”">j</a></sup>as he came near to the camp, he saw the bull calf and dancing, and ⸤Moses became angry⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “the nose of Moses became hot”">k</a></sup>and he threw the tablets from his hand, and he broke them under the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:21; 2 Kings 23:6; 2 Kings 23:15"> And he took the bull calf that they had made, and he burned it with the fire, and he crushed it until it became fine, and he scattered it on the surface of the water, and he made the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">l</a></sup>drink.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 20:9; Joshua 7:19–26; 1 Kings 21:22"> And Moses said to Aaron, “What did this people do to you that you brought on them such a great sin?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:24; Exodus 15:24; Exodus 16:20"> And Aaron said, “⸤Let not my lord become angry⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “let not the nose of my lord become hot”">m</a></sup>You yourself know the people, that ⸤they are intent on evil⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “they in evil”">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 32:1–4; Exodus 32:8"> And they said to me, ‘Make for us gods who will go before us, because this Moses, the man who brought us up from the land of Egypt, we do not know what has become of him.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 32:4; Luke 10:29; Romans 3:10"> And I said to them, ‘Whoever has gold, take it off.’ And they gave it to me, and I threw it in the fire, and out came this bull calf.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 47:3; Micah 1:11; Revelation 16:15"> And Moses saw the people, that they were running wild<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “out of control,” “running loose”">o</a></sup>because Aaron had allowed them to run wild,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “to be out of control,” “to run loose”">p</a></sup>for a laughingstock among ⸤their enemies⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “those arising against them”">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 9:32; Matthew 12:30; 2 Samuel 20:11"> And Moses stood at the entrance of the camp, and he said, “Whoever is for Yahweh, to me.” And all the sons of Levi were gathered to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 25:5; Luke 14:26; Deuteronomy 33:8–9"> And he said to them, “Thus says Yahweh, the God of Israel, ‘Put each his sword on his side. Go ⸤back and forth⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “through and come back”">r</a></sup>from gate to gate in the camp, and kill, each his brother and each his friend and each his close relative.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:9; Malachi 2:4–6; 1 Corinthians 10:8"> And the sons of Levi did according to the word of Moses, and from the people on that day about three thousand persons fell.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “three thousands of man”">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 25:11–13; Matthew 10:37; Zechariah 13:3"> And Moses said, “⸤You are ordained⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “your hands are filled”">t</a></sup>today for Yahweh, because each has been against his son and against his brother and so bringing on you today a blessing.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:20; Numbers 25:13; 2 Kings 17:21"> ⸤And⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “and it was”">u</a></sup>the next day Moses said to the people, “You have sinned a great sin. And now I will go up to Yahweh. Perhaps I can make atonement for your sin.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 20:23; Daniel 9:11; Daniel 9:8"> And Moses returned to Yahweh, and he said, “Alas, this people has sinned a great sin and made for themselves gods of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalm 69:28; Romans 9:3; Revelation 21:27"> And now if you will forgive their sin—and if not, please blot me from your scroll that you have written.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:20; Ezekiel 18:4; Revelation 20:12"> And Yahweh said to Moses, “Whoever has sinned against me I will blot him from my scroll.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 23:20; Deuteronomy 32:35; Matthew 23:35"> And now go, lead the people to where I spoke to you. Look, my angel will go before you, and on the day when I punish I will punish them for their sin.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 7:41; Exodus 32:25; 2 Samuel 12:9–10"> And Yahweh afflicted the people because they had made the bull calf that Aaron had made.</span>
</div>
