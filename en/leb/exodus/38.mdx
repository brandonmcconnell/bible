## Making the Bronze Altar and the Basin

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 27:1–8; Ezekiel 43:13–17; Exodus 40:6"> And he made the burnt-offering altar of acacia wood; its length was five cubits, and its width was five cubits—it was square—and its height was three cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 27:2; Job 6:12"> And he made its horns on its four corners; its horns were ⸤of one piece with it⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “from it”">a</a></sup>and he overlaid it with bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 27:3; 1 Samuel 2:13"> And he made all the equipment of the altar—the pots and the shovels and the sprinkling bowls and the forks<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1 Samuel 2:13 mentions a fork with “three teeth” used by priests">b</a></sup>and the fire pans—all its equipment he made with bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And he made for the altar a grating, a work of bronze network under its ledge, below, up to its middle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 27:4"> And he cast four rings on the four ends of the bronze grating as ⸤holders⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “houses”">c</a></sup>for the poles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:3; Exodus 25:6"> And he made the poles of acacia wood, and he overlaid them with bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Corinthians 2:2; 1 Corinthians 1:24"> And he put the poles into the rings on the sides of the altar to carry it with them. He made it hollow with boards.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:22; Luke 2:37; Exodus 40:7"> And he made the basin of bronze and its stand of bronze from the mirrors of the serving women who served at the entrance of the tent of assembly.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Making the Courtyard

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 27:9–19; Psalm 89:7; 1 Kings 6:36"> And he made the courtyard; for the south<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “southward southward”">d</a></sup>side were the hangings of the court of finely twisted linen, one hundred cubits,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> with their twenty pillars and their twenty bases of bronze and with the hooks of the pillars and their bands of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And for the north side the hangings were one hundred cubits with their twenty pillars and their twenty bases of bronze and with the hooks of the pillars and their bands of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And for the ⸤west⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “sea”">e</a></sup>side fifty cubits of hangings with their ten pillars and their ten bases and with the hooks of the pillars and their bands of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And for the eastward side, toward sunrise, fifty cubits;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:14"> fifteen cubits of hangings were to the shoulder,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “side,” referring to the span on one side of the courtyard’s entry">f</a></sup>with their three pillars and their three bases,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and for the second shoulder<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “side,” referring to the span on one side of the courtyard’s entry">g</a></sup>⸤on each side⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “from this and from this”">h</a></sup>of the gate of the courtyard were fifteen cubits of hangings, with their three pillars and their three bases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> All the hangings of the courtyard all around were finely twisted linen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> and the bases for the pillars were bronze, the hooks of the pillars and their bands were silver, and the overlay of their tops was silver, and all the pillars of the courtyard were banded with silver.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:14"> And the screen of the gate of the courtyard was the work of an embroiderer, with blue and purple and crimson yarns and finely twisted linen; it was twenty cubits long and five cubits ⸤high⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “height in width”">i</a></sup>like the hangings of the courtyard,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and with their four pillars and their four bases of bronze, with their silver hooks and with their tops and their bands of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:9; Exodus 27:19; Ephesians 2:21–22"> And all the ⸤pegs⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “hands”">j</a></sup>for the tabernacle and for the courtyard all around were bronze.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Amounts of Gold, Silver, and Bronze Used

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 7:44; Numbers 9:15; 2 Chronicles 24:6"> These are the records of the tabernacle, the tabernacle of the testimony, which were recorded at the ⸤command of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “mouth of”">k</a></sup>Moses, the work of the Levites, in the hand of Ithamar the son of Aaron the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 35:30–3; Exodus 31:1–5; Psalm 119:6"> And Bezalel the son of Uri the son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah, did all that Yahweh commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 35:34; Exodus 31:6"> And with him was Oholiab the son of Ahisamach of the tribe of Dan, a skilled craftsman and a designer and an embroiderer with the blue and with the purple and with the crimson yarns and with the linen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 18:16; Numbers 3:47; Leviticus 27:25"> And all the gold used for the work, in the work of the sanctuary, it was the gold of the wave offering—twenty-nine talents and seven hundred and thirty shekels, according to the sanctuary shekel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 30:12"> And the silver recorded from the community was a hundred talents and a thousand seven hundred and seventy-five shekels, according to the sanctuary shekel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 1:46; Exodus 30:13; Exodus 12:37"> It was a bekah for the individual, the half shekel according to the sanctuary shekel, for ⸤everyone who was counted⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “all of the going over to the being counted”">l</a></sup>from ⸤twenty years old⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “a son of twenty years”">m</a></sup>and above, for six hundred and three thousand five hundred and fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 26:19; Exodus 26:25; Exodus 26:21"> And it was one hundred talents of the silver to cast the bases of the sanctuary and the bases of the curtain—one hundred bases for one hundred talents of silver, a talent for each base.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 27:17"> And from the thousand seven hundred and seventy-five shekels he made hooks for the pillars, and he overlaid their tops, and he made bands for them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And the bronze of the wave offering was seventy talents and two thousand four hundred shekels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 26:37; Exodus 27:17; Exodus 27:10"> And he made with it the bases of the entrance of the tent of assembly and the bronze altar and the bronze grating that belonged to it and all the equipment of the altar</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> and the bases of the courtyard all around and the bases of the gate of the courtyard and all the ⸤pegs⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “hands”">n</a></sup>of the tabernacle and all the ⸤pegs⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “hands”">o</a></sup>of the courtyard all around.</span>
</div>
</div>
