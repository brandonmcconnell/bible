## Regulations Regarding Theft, Borrowing, and Accidental Damage or Loss

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 19:8; 2 Samuel 12:6; Proverbs 6:31"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Exodus 22:1–31 in the English Bible is 21:37–22:30 in the Hebrew Bible">a</a></sup>“ ‘If a man steals an ox or small livestock and slaughters it or sells it, he will make restitution with five cattle in place of the ox and with four sheep or goats in place of the small livestock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 24:43; Matthew 6:19–20; Numbers 35:27"> “ ‘If a thief is found in the act of breaking in and he is struck and he dies, there is not bloodguilt for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 21:2; Isaiah 50:1; Matthew 18:25"> If the sun has risen over him, there is bloodguilt for him. He will make full restitution. If ⸤he does not have enough⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “there is not to him”">b</a></sup>he will be sold for his theft.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 22:7; Exodus 22:1; Exodus 22:9"> If indeed the stolen item is found ⸤in his possession⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “in his hand”">c</a></sup>alive, from ox to donkey to small livestock, he will make double restitution.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 22:3; Exodus 22:12; Job 20:18"> “ ‘If a man grazes his livestock in a field or a vineyard and he releases his livestock and it grazes in the field of another, he will make restitution from the best of his field and the best of his vineyard.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 22:9; Exodus 21:33–34; 2 Samuel 14:30–31"> “ ‘If a fire is started and finds thorn bushes and a stack of sheaves or the standing grain or the field is consumed, the one who started the fire will surely make restitution.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 22:4; Proverbs 6:30–31; Jeremiah 2:26"> “ ‘If a man gives to his neighbor money or objects to watch over and it is stolen from the house of the man, if the thief is found, he will make double restitution.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 22:28; Deuteronomy 16:18; Exodus 21:6"> If the thief is not found, the owner of the house will be brought ⸤to the sanctuary⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “to God” or perhaps “to the judges”">d</a></sup>to learn whether or not he reached out his hand to his neighbor’s possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:1; Exodus 22:4; Numbers 5:6–7"> Concerning every account of transgression—concerning an ox, concerning a donkey, concerning small livestock, concerning clothing, concerning all lost property—where someone says, “This belongs to me,” the matter of the two of them will come to God;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “the judges”">e</a></sup>whomever God declares guilty will make double restitution to his neighbor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 12:48; 2 Timothy 1:12; Luke 16:11"> “ ‘If a man gives to his neighbor a donkey or an ox or small livestock or any beast to watch over and it dies or is injured or is captured when there is no one who sees,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 6:16; Leviticus 5:1; 1 Kings 2:42–43"> the oath of Yahweh will be between the two of them concerning whether or not he has reached out his hand to his neighbor’s possession, and its owner will accept this, and he will not make restitution.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 31:39; Exodus 22:7"> But if indeed it was stolen from him, he will make restitution to its owner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Nahum 2:12; Micah 5:8; Ezekiel 4:14"> If indeed it was torn to pieces, he will bring it as evidence—the mangled carcass; he will not make restitution.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:19–20; Exodus 21:34; Deuteronomy 15:2"> “ ‘If a man borrows from his neighbor and it is injured or dies while its owner is not with it, he will make restitution.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 8:10"> If its owner was with it, he will not make restitution; if it was hired, it came with its hiring fee.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Regulations Regarding Various Offences

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:28–29; Genesis 34:2–4"> “ ‘If a man seduces a virgin who is not engaged and he lies with her, he surely will give her bride price ⸤to have her as his wife⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “for him for a woman”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 34:12; 1 Samuel 18:25; Deuteronomy 22:29"> If her father absolutely refuses to give her to him, he will weigh out money according to the bride price for the virgin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 20:27; Deuteronomy 18:10–11; 1 Samuel 28:3"> “ ‘You will not let a witch live.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 18:23; Deuteronomy 27:21; Leviticus 20:15–16"> “ ‘Anyone lying with an animal will surely be put to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:2–5; Numbers 25:7–8; Deuteronomy 18:20"> “ ‘Whoever sacrifices to the gods—not to Yahweh, to him alone—will be destroyed.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Regulations Regarding Foreigners and the Poor

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 23:9; Deuteronomy 10:19; Leviticus 19:33"> “ ‘You will not mistreat an alien, and you will not oppress him, because you were aliens in the land of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:17; Isaiah 1:17; Isaiah 1:23"> “ ‘You will not afflict any widow or orphan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalm 18:6; Psalm 10:17–18; Luke 18:7"> If you indeed afflict him, yes, if he cries out at all to me, I will certainly hear his cry of distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalm 109:9; Jeremiah 15:8; Psalm 76:7"> And ⸤I will become angry⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “my nose will burn”">g</a></sup>and I will kill you with the sword, and your wives will be widows and your children orphans.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalm 15:5; Leviticus 25:35–37; Deuteronomy 23:19–20"> “ ‘If you lend money to my people, to the needy with you, you will not be to him as a creditor; you will not ⸤charge him interest⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “put on him interest”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 20:16; Amos 2:8; Deuteronomy 24:6"> If indeed you require the cloak of your neighbor as a pledge, you will return it to him at sundown,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 34:6; 2 Chronicles 30:9; Psalm 136:10–11"> because it is his only garment; it is his cloak for his skin. In what will he sleep? ⸤And⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “And it will be”">i</a></sup>when he cries out to me, I will hear, because I am gracious.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Regulations Regarding Tribute and Holiness

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 23:5; Ecclesiastes 10:20; Exodus 22:8–9"> “ ‘You will not curse God,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “a judge”">j</a></sup>and you will not curse a leader among your people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 23:16; Exodus 13:2; James 1:18"> “ ‘You will not delay the fullness of your harvest and the juice from your press; you will give me the firstborn of your sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 22:27; Deuteronomy 15:19; Exodus 13:12"> You will do likewise for your ox and for your sheep and goats; seven days it will be with its mother; on the eighth, you will give it to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:14; Leviticus 19:2; Deuteronomy 14:21"> And you will be men of holiness for me; and you will not eat meat from a carcass mangled in the field; you will throw it to the dog.</span>
</div>
</div>
