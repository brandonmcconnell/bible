## Yahweh Rescues Israel at the Red Sea

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 12:1; Exodus 13:1"> And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 33:7–8; Jeremiah 44:1; Exodus 13:17–18"> “Speak to the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>so that they turn and encamp before Pi-hahiroth, between Migdol ⸤and the sea⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “and between the sea”">b</a></sup>before Baal Zephon, which is opposite it, you will camp by the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 4:28; 1 Samuel 23:23; Psalm 71:11"> And Pharaoh will say of the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">c</a></sup>‘They are wandering around in the land. The desert has closed in on them.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 9:22–23; Exodus 7:5; Romans 9:17"> And I will harden the heart of Pharaoh, and he will chase after them, and I will be glorified through Pharaoh and through all his army, and the Egyptians will know that I am Yahweh.” And they did so.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 105:25; Exodus 12:33; Luke 11:24–26"> And it was told to the king of Egypt that the people fled, and the heart of Pharaoh was changed and that of his servants toward the people, and they said, “What is this we have done, that we have released Israel from serving us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And he harnessed his chariot and took with him his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 15:4; Judges 4:3; Psalm 68:17"> And he took six hundred select chariots and all the chariots of Egypt and officers over all of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 13:17; Numbers 33:3; Deuteronomy 26:8"> And Yahweh hardened the heart of Pharaoh the king of Egypt, and he chased after the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">d</a></sup>(Now the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">e</a></sup>were going out ⸤boldly⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “with a raised/high hand”">f</a></sup>)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 15:9; Joshua 24:6; Exodus 14:2"> And the Egyptians chased after them, and they overtook them encamped at the sea—all the horses of the chariots of Pharaoh and his charioteers and his army—at Pi-hahiroth before Baal Zephon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 24:7; Psalm 34:17; Nehemiah 9:9"> And Pharaoh approached, and the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">g</a></sup>lifted their eyes, and there were the Egyptians traveling after them! And they were very afraid, and the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">h</a></sup>cried out to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 106:7–8; Numbers 14:1–4; Exodus 15:23–24"> And they said to Moses, “Because there are no graves in Egypt? Is that why you have taken us to die in the desert? What is this you have done to us by bringing us out from Egypt!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jonah 4:3; Exodus 6:9; Jonah 4:8"> Isn’t this the word we spoke to you in Egypt, saying, ‘Leave us alone so that we can serve Egypt!’ because serving Egypt is better for us than our dying in the desert.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:17; 2 Chronicles 20:15; Exodus 14:30"> And Moses said to the people, “You must not be afraid. Stand still and see the salvation of Yahweh, which he will accomplish for you today, because the Egyptians whom you see today you will see never again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:22; Deuteronomy 20:4; 2 Chronicles 20:17"> Yahweh will fight for you, and you must be quiet.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 7:10; Ezra 10:4–5; Nehemiah 9:9"> And Yahweh said to Moses, “Why do you cry out to me? Speak to the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">i</a></sup>so that they set out.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “journey,” “move on,” “travel”">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 7:19; Exodus 4:20; Exodus 4:2"> And you, lift up your staff and stretch out your hand over the sea and divide it so that the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">k</a></sup>can go in the middle of the sea on the dry land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 14:8; Exodus 14:4; Ezekiel 34:11"> And as for me, look, I am about to harden the heart of the Egyptians so that they come after them, and I will display my glory through Pharaoh and through all of his army, through his chariots and through his charioteers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 7:5; Exodus 14:4; Exodus 7:17"> And the Egyptians will know that I am Yahweh when I display my glory through Pharaoh, through his chariots, and through his charioteers.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 63:9; Exodus 32:34; Exodus 23:20–21"> And the angel of God who was going before the camp of Israel set out and went behind them. And the column of cloud set out ahead of them, and it stood still behind them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14; 2 Corinthians 2:15–16; Proverbs 4:18–19"> so that it came between the camp of Egypt and the camp of Israel. And ⸤it was a dark cloud⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “the cloud was and the darkness”">l</a></sup>but it gave light to the night,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “it illumined the night”">m</a></sup>so that ⸤neither approached the other⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “this did not come near to this”">n</a></sup>all night.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 63:12; Exodus 15:8; Psalm 66:6"> And Moses stretched out his hand over the sea, and Yahweh moved the sea<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “caused the sea to go,” “drove the sea”">o</a></sup>with a strong east wind all night, and he made the sea become dry ground, and the waters were divided.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 11:29; Psalm 66:6; Exodus 15:19"> And the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">p</a></sup>entered the middle of the sea on the dry land. The waters were a wall for them on their right and on their left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 15:19; Exodus 14:17; Exodus 15:9"> And the Egyptians gave chase<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “pursued,” “chased”">q</a></sup>and entered after them—all the horses of Pharaoh, his chariots, and his charioteers—into the middle of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 13:21; Psalm 77:16–19; Exodus 14:25"> And during the morning watch, Yahweh looked down to the Egyptian camp from in the column of fire and cloud, and he threw the Egyptian camp into a panic.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 14:14; Deuteronomy 3:22; Job 20:24"> And he removed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “let turn aside”">r</a></sup>the wheels of their chariots so that they drove them with difficulty, and the Egyptians said, “We must flee away<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “let us flee,” “let me run away”; the verb is singular, but it is plural in context">s</a></sup>from Israel because Yahweh is fighting for them against Egypt.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 14:16; Exodus 8:5; James 2:13"> And Yahweh said to Moses, “Stretch out your hand over the sea, and let the waters return over the Egyptians, over their chariots, and over their charioteers.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 4:18; Psalm 78:53; Hebrews 11:29"> And Moses stretched out his hand over the sea, and the sea returned ⸤at daybreak⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “at the turning of morning”">t</a></sup>to its normal level, and the Egyptians were fleeing ⸤because of it⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “to/for/from the meeting of it”">u</a></sup>and Yahweh swept the Egyptians into the middle of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalm 78:53; Psalm 106:9–11; Habakkuk 3:13"> And the waters returned and covered the chariots and the charioteers—all the army of Pharaoh coming after them into the sea. Not ⸤even⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “up to,” “as far as”">v</a></sup>one survived among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 14:22; Psalm 77:19–20; Isaiah 43:2"> But the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">w</a></sup>walked on the dry land in the middle of the sea. The waters were a wall for them on their right and on their left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalm 106:8; Psalm 106:10; Psalm 92:9–11"> And Yahweh saved Israel on that day from the hand<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Or “power”">x</a></sup>of Egypt, and Israel saw the Egyptians dead on the shore of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 2:11; John 11:45; John 2:23–25"> And Israel saw the great hand<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Or “power”">y</a></sup>that Yahweh displayed against Egypt, and the people feared Yahweh, and they believed in Yahweh and in Moses his servant.</span>
</div>
