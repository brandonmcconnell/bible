## Instructions for Making the Tabernacle

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 36:8–19; Exodus 26:36; Exodus 26:31"> “And the tabernacle you will make with ten curtains; you will make them of finely twisted linen and blue and purple and crimson yarns, with cherubim, the work of a skilled craftsman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:2; Numbers 4:25; 1 Chronicles 17:1"> The length of the one curtain will be twenty-eight cubits, and the width will be four cubits for the one curtain; one measure will be for all the curtains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 36:10; Colossians 2:2; Ephesians 2:21–22"> Five curtains will be joined ⸤to one another⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “a woman to her sister”">a</a></sup>and five curtains joined ⸤to one another⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “a woman to her sister”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 36:11–12; Exodus 36:17; Exodus 26:10–11"> And you will make loops of blue on the edge of the one curtain, at the end in the set; and you will do so on the edge of the end curtain in the second set.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> You will make fifty loops on the one curtain, and you will make fifty loops on the end of the curtain that is in the second set; the loops are to be opposite<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “interlinked” or “matching”">c</a></sup>⸤to one another⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “a woman to her sister”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 36:13; Exodus 26:11; Exodus 26:33"> And you will make fifty gold clasps and join the curtains ⸤to one another⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “a woman to her sister”">e</a></sup>with the clasps, so that the tabernacle will be one.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 26:14; Psalm 45:13; Exodus 26:9"> “And you will make curtains of goat hair for a tent over the tabernacle; you will make them eleven curtains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 26:13; Exodus 26:2"> The length of the one curtain will be thirty cubits, and the width will be four cubits for the one curtain; one measure will be for the eleven curtains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And you will join five curtains together and six curtains together, and you will fold double the sixth curtain at the front of the tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And you will make fifty loops on the edge of the one curtain at the end of the first set and fifty loops on the edge of the curtain in the second set.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 26:3; Exodus 26:6"> And you will make fifty bronze clasps, and you will put the clasps in the loops and join the tent, so that it will be one.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> “And the surplus in the curtains of the tent will be an overhang; the surplus half curtain will hang over the back of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 26:2; Exodus 26:8"> And a cubit ⸤from one side⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “from this”">f</a></sup>and a cubit ⸤from the other side⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “from this”">g</a></sup>in the surplus in the length of the curtains of the tent will be hung over the sides of the tabernacle ⸤equally⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “from this and from this”">h</a></sup>to cover it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 36:19; Exodus 25:5; Exodus 35:23"> “And you will make a covering for the tent of red-dyed ram skins and a covering of fine leather to go above.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 36:20–33; Numbers 4:31–32; Exodus 40:17–18"> “And you will make the frames<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “boards” or “planks”">i</a></sup>for the tabernacle of acacia wood as uprights.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “standing”">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The length of the frame<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “board” or “plank”">k</a></sup>will be ten cubits, and the width of the one frame<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “board” or “plank”">l</a></sup>will be one and a half cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 36:22; Exodus 26:19; Exodus 36:24"> You will make two ⸤pegs⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “hands”">m</a></sup>for the one frame<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “board” or “plank”">n</a></sup>for joining ⸤each to another⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “a woman to her sister”">o</a></sup>and likewise for all the frames<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “boards” or “planks”">p</a></sup>of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And you will make the frames<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “boards” or “planks”">q</a></sup>for the tabernacle with twenty frames<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “boards” or “planks”">r</a></sup>for the ⸤south⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “south southward”">s</a></sup>side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 38:27; Exodus 38:30–31; Exodus 26:37"> And you will make forty silver bases under the twenty frames,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Or “boards” or “planks”">t</a></sup>with two bases under the one frame<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “board” or “plank”">u</a></sup>for its two ⸤pegs⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “hands”">v</a></sup>and two bases under the ⸤next⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “one”">w</a></sup>frame<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Or “board” or “plank”">x</a></sup>for its two ⸤pegs⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “hands”">y</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And for the second side of the tabernacle, the north side, there will be twenty frames<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “boards” or “planks”">z</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and their forty silver bases, with two bases under the one frame<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Or “board” or “plank”">aa</a></sup>and two bases under the ⸤next⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “one”">ab</a></sup>frame.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Or “board” or “plank”">ac</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> “And for the rear of the tabernacle ⸤on the west⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Or “westward,” literally “seaward,” toward the Mediterranean Sea">ad</a></sup>you will make six frames.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Or “boards” or “planks”">ae</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And you will make two frames<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Or “boards” or “planks”">af</a></sup>for the tabernacle corners at the rear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 36:29–30; Psalm 133:1–3; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> They will be double at the bottom, and they will be completely together on its top to the one ring; it will be likewise for the two of them; they will be for the two corners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And there will be eight frames<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Or “boards” or “planks”">ag</a></sup>and their silver bases, sixteen bases, with two bases under the one frame<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Or “board” or “plank”">ah</a></sup>and two bases under the ⸤next⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Literally “one”">ai</a></sup>frame.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Or “board” or “plank”">aj</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 15:1; Numbers 4:31; Galatians 6:1–2"> “You will make five bars of acacia wood for the frames<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Or “boards” or “planks”">ak</a></sup>on the one side of the tabernacle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> and five bars for the frames<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Or “boards” or “planks”">al</a></sup>on the second side of the tabernacle, and five bars for the frames<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Or “boards” or “planks”">am</a></sup>on the side of the tabernacle at the rear ⸤on the west⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Or “westward,” literally “seaward,” toward the Mediterranean Sea">an</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And the bar in the middle, in the midst of the frames<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Or “boards” or “planks”">ao</a></sup>will run from end to end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 25:11–12"> And you will overlay the frames<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Or “boards” or “planks”">ap</a></sup>with gold, and you will make their rings of gold as ⸤holders⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Literally “houses”">aq</a></sup>for the bars, and you will overlay the bars with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Hebrews 8:5; Exodus 25:9"> And you will erect the tabernacle according to its plan, which you have been shown on the mountain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; 2 Chronicles 3:14; Exodus 36:35"> “And you will make a curtain of blue and purple and crimson yarns and finely twisted linen, the work of a skilled craftsman; he will make it with cherubim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 26:37; Exodus 36:38; Esther 1:6"> And you will put it on four acacia pillars overlaid with gold with their gold hooks on four silver bases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 9:2–3; Leviticus 16:2; Exodus 40:21"> And you will put the curtain under the clasps, and you will bring the ark of the testimony there inside the curtain, and the curtain will separate for you between the holy and the ⸤most holy place⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Literally “holy place of the holiness”">ar</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 40:20; Exodus 25:21; Hebrews 9:5"> And you will put the atonement cover on the ark of the testimony in the ⸤most holy place⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Literally “holy place of the holiness”">as</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Exodus 40:24; Exodus 40:22; Hebrews 9:2"> And you will place the table outside the curtain and the lampstand opposite the table on the south side of the tabernacle, and you will put the table on the north side.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 36:37; Numbers 9:15; Exodus 26:31"> “And you will make for the entrance of the tent a screen of blue and purple and crimson yarns and finely twisted linen, the work of an embroiderer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 36:38"> And you will make for the screen five acacia pillars, and you will overlay them with gold with their gold hooks, and you will cast for them five bronze bases.</span>
</div>
