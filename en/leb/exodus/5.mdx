## Pharaoh Rejects Yahweh’s Authority and Makes Israel’s Troubles Worse

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 10:9; Acts 4:29; Matthew 10:28"> And afterward, Moses and Aaron went, and they said to Pharaoh, “Thus says Yahweh the God of Israel, ‘Release my people so that they may hold a festival for me in the desert.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 3:19; Job 21:15; 2 Kings 18:35"> And Pharaoh said, “Who is Yahweh that I should listen to his voice to release Israel? I do not know Yahweh, and also I will not release Israel.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 3:18; Zechariah 14:16–19; Ezekiel 6:11"> And they said, “The God of the Hebrews has met with us. Please let us go on a three-day journey into the desert, and let us sacrifice to Yahweh our God, lest he strike us with plague or with sword.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 1:11; Acts 24:5; Amos 7:10"> And the king of Egypt said, “Why, Moses and Aaron, do you take<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “let loose,” “let run wild” (compare 32:25)">a</a></sup>the people from their work? Go to your ⸤forced labor⸥!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “burdens” or “burdensome labor”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 1:7–11; Proverbs 14:28"> And Pharaoh said, “Look, the people of the land are now many, and you want to stop them from their ⸤forced labor⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “burdens” or “burdensome labor”">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 5:10; Exodus 5:19; Deuteronomy 16:18"> And on that day Pharaoh commanded the slave drivers over the people and his foremen, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 19:19; Genesis 24:25"> “You must no longer give straw to the people to make the bricks like ⸤before⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “yesterday three days ago”">d</a></sup>Let them go and gather straw for themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 106:41"> But the quota of the bricks that they were making ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “yesterday three days ago”">e</a></sup>⸤you must require of them⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “you will put on them”">f</a></sup>You must not reduce from it, because they are lazy. Therefore they are crying out, saying, ‘Let us go and sacrifice to our God.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 18:20; Jeremiah 43:2; Job 16:3"> Let the work be heavier on the men so that they will do it and not pay attention to words of deception.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 1:11; Exodus 3:7; Proverbs 29:12"> And the slave drivers of the people and their foremen went out, and they spoke to the people, saying, “Thus says Pharaoh, ‘I am not giving you straw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 5:13–14"> You go, get straw for yourselves from whatever you find because not a thing is being reduced from your work.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 15:7; Isaiah 5:24; Isaiah 47:14"> And the people spread out in all the land of Egypt to gather stubble for the straw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the slave drivers were insisting, saying, “Finish your work ⸤for each day⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “a thing of a day”">g</a></sup>on its day, as ⸤when there was straw⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “in the being of the straw”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 10:24"> And the foremen of the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">i</a></sup>whom Pharaoh’s slave drivers had appointed over them, were beaten by men who were saying, “Why have you not completed your portion of brickmaking ⸤as before, both yesterday and today⸥?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “as yesterday three days ago also yesterday also the day”">j</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## The Foremen Complain to Pharaoh and Moses, and Moses Complains to Yahweh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the foremen of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">k</a></sup>came and cried out to Pharaoh, saying, “Why do you treat your servants like this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Straw is not being given to your servants, but they are saying to us, ‘Make bricks!’ and, look, your servants are being beaten, but it is the fault of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="The expression is difficult. The word translated “fault” or “sin” has pointing for a second-person feminine singular subject (“you sinned”), which does not go well with either “Pharaoh” or “your people” as a subject; but the noun translated “sin of” has the same consonants, leading to the possibility that the word should be understood as a noun">l</a></sup>your people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 26:8; John 6:27; Exodus 5:8"> And he said, “You are lazy, lazy! Therefore you are saying, ‘Let us go; let us sacrifice to Yahweh.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:18; Daniel 2:9–13"> And now go, work, but straw will not be given to you, and you must give the full quota of bricks.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:8; Ecclesiastes 4:1; Deuteronomy 32:36"> And the foremen of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">m</a></sup>saw they were in trouble ⸤with the saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “saying”">n</a></sup>“You will not reduce from your bricks ⸤for each day⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “a thing of a day”">o</a></sup>on its day.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And they met Moses and Aaron, who were waiting to meet them when they were going out from Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; 1 Samuel 13:4; 1 Chronicles 19:6"> And they said to them, “May Yahweh look upon you and judge because you have caused our fragrance to stink in the eyes of Pharaoh and in the eyes of his servants so as to put a sword into their hand to kill us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:7; 1 Kings 19:10; Numbers 11:14–15"> And Moses returned to Yahweh and said, “Lord, why have you brought trouble to this people? Why ever did you send me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:21; Hebrews 10:36–37; Isaiah 28:16"> And from the time I came to Pharaoh to speak in your name, he has brought trouble to this people, and you have certainly not delivered your people.”</span>
</div>
