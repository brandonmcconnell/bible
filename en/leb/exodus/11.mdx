## Announcement of the Tenth Plague: Death of the Firstborn

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 15:14; Leviticus 26:21; Exodus 3:20"> And Yahweh said to Moses, “Still one plague I will bring upon Pharaoh and upon Egypt; afterward he will release you from here. At the moment of his releasing, he will certainly drive you completely out from here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 3:22; Exodus 12:35–36; Haggai 2:8"> Speak in the ears of the people, and let them ask, a man from his neighbor and a woman from her neighbor, for objects of silver and objects of gold.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 3:21; Exodus 12:36; Revelation 3:9"> And Yahweh gave the people favor in the eyes of Egypt. Also the man Moses was very great in the land of Egypt, in the eyes of the servants of Pharaoh and in the eyes of the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 12:29; Psalm 60:10; Job 34:20"> And Moses said, “Thus says Yahweh, ‘About the middle of the night I will go out through the midst of Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 78:51; Exodus 12:12; Psalm 105:36"> and every firstborn in the land of Egypt will die, from the firstborn of Pharaoh who sits on his throne to the firstborn of the slave woman who is behind the pair of millstones and every firstborn animal.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The Hebrew word often refers to domestic animals">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 12:30; Amos 5:17; Zephaniah 1:10"> And there will be a great cry of distress in all the land of Egypt, the like of which has not been nor will be again.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “which like it has not been and like it will not be again”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 10:21; Exodus 10:23; Exodus 8:22"> But against all the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">c</a></sup>from a man to an animal, a dog will not even ⸤bark⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “move his tongue” or “sharpen his tongue”">d</a></sup>so that you will know that Yahweh makes a distinction between Egypt and Israel.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 12:31–33; Ezekiel 3:14; Daniel 3:19"> And all of these your servants will come down to me and bow to me, saying, ‘Go out, you and all the people who are at your feet.’ And afterward I will go out.” And he went out from Pharaoh ⸤in great anger⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “with heat of nose”">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 7:3–4; Exodus 3:19; Exodus 10:1"> And Yahweh said to Moses, “Pharaoh will not listen to you, ⸤so that my wonders may multiply⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “for the sake of the multiplying of my wonders”">f</a></sup>in the land of Egypt.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 4:21; Exodus 10:20; Exodus 10:27"> And Moses and Aaron did all these wonders before Pharaoh, and Yahweh hardened Pharaoh’s heart, and he did not release the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">g</a></sup>from his land.</span>
</div>
