## Instructions for Consecrating Aaron and His Sons

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 4:3; Malachi 1:13–14; Exodus 12:5"> “And this is the thing that you will do for them to consecrate them to serve as a priest for me: Take ⸤one young bull⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “a son of cattle”">a</a></sup>and two rams without defect</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 6:15; Leviticus 2:15; Leviticus 7:12"> and unleavened bread and unleavened, ring-shaped bread cakes mixed with oil, and wafers of unleavened breads smeared with oil. You will make them with finely milled wheat flour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 6:17; Leviticus 8:26; Leviticus 8:2"> and you will put them on one basket, and you will bring them on the basket and bring the bull and the two rams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 40:12; Hebrews 10:22; Exodus 26:36"> And you will bring Aaron and his sons to the entrance of the tent of assembly, and you will wash them with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 28:2–8; Leviticus 8:7–8"> And you will take the garments and clothe Aaron with the tunic and the robe of the ephod, and you will fasten to him the ephod and the breast piece with the waistband of the ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 8:9; Exodus 28:36–39"> And you will set the turban on his head, and you will put the holy diadem on the turban.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 35:25; Leviticus 21:10; John 3:34"> And you will take the anointing oil and pour it on his head and anoint him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 8:13; Exodus 28:40"> And you will bring his sons and clothe them with tunics.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 18:7; Leviticus 8:22–28; Deuteronomy 18:5"> And you will gird Aaron and his sons with sashes and wrap headdresses on them. And priesthood will be theirs as a lasting rule, and ⸤you will ordain Aaron and his sons.⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “you will fill the hand of Aaron and the hands of his sons”">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 1:4; Leviticus 8:14; Exodus 29:15"> “And you will bring the bull before the tent of assembly, and Aaron and his sons will lay their hands on the head of the bull.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 9:8; Leviticus 9:12; Exodus 29:4"> And you will slaughter the bull before Yahweh at the entrance of the tent of assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 27:2; Leviticus 9:9; Leviticus 8:15"> And you will take some of the blood of the bull and with your finger put it on the horns of the altar, and you will pour out all the blood at the base of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 3:3–4; 1 Samuel 2:16; Leviticus 9:10"> And you will take and turn into smoke on the altar all the fat covering the inner parts and the lobe on the liver and the two kidneys and the fat that is on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 4:11–12; Leviticus 4:21; Exodus 30:10"> And the flesh of the bull and its skin and its offal you will burn with fire outside the camp; it is a sin offering.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 29:19; Exodus 29:3; Leviticus 8:18–21"> “And you will take the one ram, and Aaron and his sons will lay their hands on the head of the ram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 29:11–12"> And you will slaughter the ram and take its blood and sprinkle it on the altar all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:14; Leviticus 1:13; Leviticus 1:9"> And you will cut the ram into pieces and wash its inner parts, and you will put its legs with its pieces and with its head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 8:21; Philippians 4:18; Genesis 22:7"> And you will turn into smoke on the altar all of the ram; it is a burnt offering for Yahweh; it is a smell of appeasement, an offering by fire for Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 29:3; Leviticus 8:22–29"> “And you will take the second ram, and Aaron and his sons will lay their hands on the head of the ram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 50:5; Hebrews 10:22; Leviticus 14:16"> And you will slaughter the ram and take some of its blood and put it on Aaron’s right earlobe and on the right earlobe of his sons and on the thumb of their right hand and on ⸤the big toe⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “the thumb of”">c</a></sup>of their right foot, and you will sprinkle the blood at the base of the altar all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 9:22; Exodus 29:7; Exodus 29:1"> And you will take some of the blood that is on the altar and some of the anointing oil, and you will spatter it on Aaron and on his garments and on his sons and on his sons’ garments with him, and he will be sacred, and his garments and his sons and his sons’ garments with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 29:13; Leviticus 9:21; Leviticus 9:19"> And you will take from the ram the fat and the fat tail and the fat covering the inner parts and the lobe of the liver and the two kidneys and the fat that is on them and the right thigh, because it is the ram of ordination.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 29:2–3; Leviticus 8:26"> “And one loaf of bread and one ring-shaped bread cake of oiled bread and one wafer from the basket of unleavened bread that is before Yahweh—</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 7:30; Leviticus 8:27; Leviticus 9:21"> you will put them all on the palms of Aaron and on the palms of his sons, and you will wave them as a wave offering before Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 8:28; Exodus 29:41; Leviticus 3:16"> And you will take them from their hand and turn them to smoke on the altar beside the burnt offering as a fragrance of appeasement before Yahweh; it is an offering made by fire before Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 8:29; Leviticus 7:31; Leviticus 7:34"> “And you will take the breast section from the ram of ordination that is for Aaron, and you will wave it as a wave offering before Yahweh. It will be your portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 10:15; Numbers 18:11; Deuteronomy 18:3"> And you will consecrate the wave offering breast section and the thigh of the contribution that was waved and that was presented<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “lifted up”">d</a></sup>from the ram of the ordination that is for Aaron and for his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 15:19–20; Leviticus 7:11–38; Leviticus 10:14–15"> And it will be for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “belong to”">e</a></sup>Aaron and for his sons as a lasting rule from the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">f</a></sup>because it is a contribution, and it will be a contribution from the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">g</a></sup>from their sacrifices of fellowship, their contribution to Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 18:8; Exodus 28:3–4; Exodus 30:30"> “And the holy garments that are for Aaron will be for his sons after him in which to anoint them and ⸤to ordain them⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “to fill in them their hands”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 20:28; Leviticus 13:5; Acts 20:6–7"> Seven days the priest who replaces him from among his sons will wear them, who comes to the tent of assembly to serve in the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 8:31; 1 Samuel 2:13; Ezekiel 46:20–24"> And you will take the ram of ordination and boil its meat in a holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 12:4; Exodus 29:23; Exodus 29:2–3"> And Aaron and his sons will eat the meat of the ram and the bread that is in the basket at the entrance of the tent of assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 18:4; Leviticus 10:13–18; Psalm 22:26"> And they will eat them—the things by which atonement was made for them to ordain them to consecrate them—and a stranger will not eat them because they are holy objects.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 12:10; Leviticus 8:32; Exodus 29:22"> If any remains until morning from the ordination meat or from the bread, you will burn the remainder in fire; it will not be eaten, because it is a holy object.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Exodus 29:30; Leviticus 14:8–11; Leviticus 8:4–36"> And you will do so for Aaron and for his sons, according to all that I have commanded you; seven days you will ordain them.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Instructions for Regular Offerings at the Tabernacle

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Hebrews 10:11; Hebrews 9:22–23; Exodus 30:28–29"> “And you will offer a bull for a sin offering every day for the atonement; and you will offer a sin offering on the altar<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “you will purify the altar”">i</a></sup>when you make atonement for it, and you will anoint it to consecrate it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 40:10; Matthew 23:19; Exodus 30:28–29"> Seven days you will make atonement for the altar, and you will consecrate it, and the altar will be ⸤a most holy thing⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “a holiness of holinesses” or “a holy thing among holy things ,” a thing of utmost holiness">j</a></sup>⸤Anyone who⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “anything that”; literally “all touching”">k</a></sup>touches the altar will be holy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:4; 1 Chronicles 16:40; Numbers 28:3–8"> “And this is what you shall offer on the altar: Two ⸤one-year-old⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “sons of a year”">l</a></sup>male lambs ⸤every day⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “for the day,” or per day">m</a></sup>continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:13–15; 2 Kings 16:15; Psalm 5:3"> The first lamb you will offer in the morning, and the second lamb you will offer ⸤at twilight⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “between the evenings”">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Philippians 2:17; Ezekiel 46:14; Joel 2:14"> And a tenth of finely milled flour mixed with a fourth of a hin of beaten oil, and a fourth of a hin of wine as a libation with the first lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:13–15; 1 Kings 18:29; Psalm 141:2"> And the second lamb you will offer ⸤at twilight⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “between the evenings”">o</a></sup>you will offer a grain offering and its libation like that of the morning for a fragrance of appeasement, an offering made by fire for Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Exodus 25:22"> It will be a burnt offering of continuity throughout your generations at the entrance of the tent of assembly before Yahweh, where I will meet with you to speak to you there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="1 Kings 8:11; 2 Chronicles 7:1–3; Malachi 3:1"> “And I will meet with the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">p</a></sup>there, and it will be consecrated by my glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Leviticus 21:15; Leviticus 22:9; Leviticus 22:16"> And I will consecrate the tent of assembly and the altar, and Aaron and his sons I will consecrate to serve as priests for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Leviticus 26:12; 2 Corinthians 6:16; Exodus 25:8"> And I will dwell in the midst of the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">q</a></sup>and I will be their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Exodus 20:2; Jeremiah 31:33; Ezekiel 20:5"> And they will know that I am Yahweh, their God, who brought them out from the land of Egypt in order to dwell in their midst. I am Yahweh their God.</span>
</div>
