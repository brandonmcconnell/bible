## Command to Resume Travel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 12:7; Exodus 32:13; Exodus 32:7"> And Yahweh spoke to Moses, “Go, go up from here, you and the people whom you have brought up from the land of Egypt, to the land that I swore to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, saying, ‘I will give it to your offspring.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 32:34; Joshua 24:11; Exodus 23:27–31"> And I will send an angel before you, and I will drive out the Canaanites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew “Canaanite”">a</a></sup>the Amorites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew “Amorite”">b</a></sup>and the Hittites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “Hittite”">c</a></sup>and the Perizzites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew “Perizzite”">d</a></sup>the Hivites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew “Hivite”">e</a></sup>and the Jebusites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “Jebusite”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 32:9–10; Exodus 3:8; Acts 7:51"> Go to a land flowing with milk and honey, but I will not go up among you, because you are a stiff-necked people, lest I destroy you on the way.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 14:39; Numbers 14:1; Ezekiel 26:16"> And the people heard this troubling<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “bad,” “disastrous,” “calamitous”">g</a></sup>word, and they mourned, and they each did not put their ornaments on themselves.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 16:21; Numbers 16:45–46; Exodus 33:3–4"> And Yahweh said to Moses, “Say to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">h</a></sup>‘You are a stiff-necked people; if one moment I were to go up among you, I would destroy you. And now take down your ornaments from on you, and I will ⸤decide⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “know”">i</a></sup>what I will do to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 33:4; Exodus 32:3; Jeremiah 2:19"> And the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">j</a></sup>stripped themselves of their ornaments from Mount Horeb onward.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Tent outside the Camp

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 55:6–7; Exodus 29:42–43; Proverbs 15:29"> And Moses took the tent and pitched it outside the camp far from the camp, and he called it the tent of assembly, ⸤and⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “and it would be”">k</a></sup>all seeking Yahweh would go out to the tent of assembly, which was outside the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 16:27"> ⸤And⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “and it would be”">l</a></sup>at the going out of Moses to the tent, all the people would rise and stand, each at the opening of his tent, and gaze after Moses until his entering the tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 99:7; Exodus 31:18; Exodus 13:21–22"> ⸤And⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “and it would be”">m</a></sup>at the entering of Moses into the tent the column of cloud would descend and stand at the opening of the tent, and he would speak with Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 8:22; 1 Kings 8:14; Exodus 4:31"> And all the people would see the column of cloud standing at the opening of the tent, and all the people would rise and bow in worship, each at the opening of his tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 12:8; Deuteronomy 34:10; Exodus 24:13"> And Yahweh would speak to Moses face to face, as a man speaks to his neighbor. And he would return to the camp, and his assistant Joshua the son of Nun, a young man, did not leave the middle of the tent.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Presence and Glory

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 33:17; Jeremiah 1:5; John 10:14–15"> And Moses said to Yahweh, “See, you are saying to me, ‘Take this people up.’ But you have not let me know whom you will send with me, and you yourself have said, ‘I know you by name, and you also have found favor in my eyes.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 119:33; Psalm 25:4; Psalm 27:11"> And now if I have found favor in your eyes, make known to me, please, your way, and so I may know you so that I can find favor in your eyes. And see that this nation is your people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 21:44; Matthew 11:28; Joshua 1:5"> And he said, “My presence will go, and I will give you rest.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 34:9; Psalm 4:6; Exodus 33:3"> And he said to him, “If your presence is not going, do not bring us up from here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:7; Numbers 14:14; 1 Kings 8:53"> And by what will it be known then that I have found favor in your eyes, I and your people? Is it not by your going with us? And so we will be distinguished, I and your people, from all the people who are on the face of the ground.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 65:24; 1 John 5:14–15; Exodus 33:12"> And Yahweh said to Moses, “Also I will do this thing that you have spoken, because you have found favor in my eyes and I have known you by name.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 21:23; Exodus 33:20; Titus 2:13"> And he said, “Please show me your glory.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 9:15–18; Ephesians 1:6–8; Jeremiah 31:14"> And he said, “I myself will cause all my goodness to pass over before you, and I will proclaim the name of Yahweh before you, and I will be gracious to whom I will be gracious, and I will show compassion to whom I will show compassion.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 32:30; John 1:18; 1 Timothy 6:16"> But he said, “You are not able to see my face, because a human will not see me and live.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:31; Zechariah 3:7; Joshua 20:4"> And Yahweh said, “There is a place with me, and you will stand on the rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalm 91:1; Isaiah 2:21; Psalm 91:4"> ⸤And⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “and it will be”">n</a></sup>when my glory passes over, I will put you in the rock’s crevice, and I will cover you with my hand until I pass over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 33:20; John 1:18; 1 Timothy 6:16"> And I will remove my hand, and you will see my back, but my face will not be visible.”</span>
</div>
</div>
