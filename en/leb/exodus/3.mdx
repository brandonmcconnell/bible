## Yahweh’s Plan to Rescue the Israelites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 19:8; Exodus 17:6; Psalm 78:70–72"> And Moses was a shepherd with the flock<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The Hebrew noun translated “flock” in this verse is used collectively for sheep or goats or a mix of the two.">a</a></sup>of Jethro, his father-in-law, the priest of Midian, and he led the flock to the west of the desert, and he came to the mountain of God, to Horeb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 7:30–35; Isaiah 43:2; Mark 12:26"> And the angel of Yahweh appeared to him in a flame of fire from the midst of a bush, and he looked, and there was the bush burning with fire, but the bush was not being consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 7:31; Psalm 107:8; Job 37:14"> And Moses said, “Let me turn aside and see this great sight. Why does the bush not burn up?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:16; 1 Samuel 3:6; Acts 9:4"> And Yahweh saw that he turned aside to see, and God called to him from the midst of the bush, and he said, “Moses, Moses.” And he said, “Here I am.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 5:15; Acts 7:33; Exodus 19:12"> And he said, “You must not come near to here. Take off your sandals from on your feet, because the place on which you are standing, it is holy ground.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 20:37; Matthew 22:32; Exodus 4:5"> And he said, “I am the God of your father, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob.” And Moses hid his face because he was afraid of looking at God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 145:19; Psalm 106:44; Exodus 2:23–25"> And Yahweh said, “Surely I have seen the misery of my people who are in Egypt, and I have heard their cry of distress because of their oppressors, for I know their sufferings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 50:24; Genesis 15:18–21; Exodus 3:17"> And I have come down to deliver them from the hand of the Egyptians and to bring them up from this land to a good and wide land, to a land flowing with milk and honey, to the place of the Canaanites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew “Canaanite”">b</a></sup>and the Hittites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “Hittite”">c</a></sup>and the Amorites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew “Amorite”">d</a></sup>and the Perizzites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew “Perizzite”">e</a></sup>and the Hivites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “Hivite”">f</a></sup>and the Jebusites.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew “Jebusite”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 22:22–23; Exodus 2:23; Exodus 1:13–14"> And now, look, the cry of distress of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">h</a></sup>has come to me, and also I see the oppression with which the Egyptians are oppressing them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 6:4; 1 Samuel 12:6; Psalm 105:26"> And now come, and I will send you to Pharaoh, and you must bring my people, the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">i</a></sup>out from Egypt.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:18; 2 Corinthians 3:5; Exodus 6:12"> But Moses said to God, “Who am I that I should go to Pharaoh and that I should bring the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">j</a></sup>out from Egypt?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Romans 8:31; Isaiah 41:10"> And he said, “Because I am with you, and this will be the sign for you that I myself have sent you: When you bring the people out from Egypt, you will serve God on this mountain.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 30:4; Isaiah 9:6; Jeremiah 23:6"> But Moses said to God, “Look, if I go to the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">k</a></sup>and I say to them, ‘The God of your ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “fathers”">l</a></sup>has sent me to you,’ and they say to me, ‘What is his name?’ then what shall I say to them?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 8:58; Hebrews 13:8; Revelation 1:8"> And God said to Moses, “I am that I am.” And he said, “So you must say to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">m</a></sup>‘I am sent me to you.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 17:7–8; Hebrews 13:8; Malachi 3:6"> And God said again to Moses, “So you must say to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">n</a></sup>‘Yahweh, the God of your ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “fathers”">o</a></sup>the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob, has sent me to you. This is my name forever, and this is my remembrance from generation to generation.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 8:4; Exodus 4:31; Exodus 4:29"> Go and gather the elders of Israel and say to them, ‘Yahweh, the God of your ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “fathers”">p</a></sup>appeared to me, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, saying, “I have carefully attended to you and what has been done to you in Egypt.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 15:13–21; Genesis 50:24; Genesis 46:4"> And I said, “I will bring you up from the misery of Egypt to the land of the Canaanites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Hebrew “Canaanite”">q</a></sup>and the Hittites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Hebrew “Hittite”">r</a></sup>and the Amorites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Hebrew “Amorite”">s</a></sup>and the Perizzites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Hebrew “Perizzite”">t</a></sup>and the Hivites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Hebrew “Hivite”">u</a></sup>and the Jebusites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Hebrew “Jebusite”">v</a></sup>to a land flowing with milk and honey.” ’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 9:1; Exodus 7:16; Exodus 10:24–26"> And they will listen to your voice, and you will go, you and the elders of Israel, to the king of Egypt, and you will say to him, ‘Yahweh, the God of the Hebrews has met with us, and now let us please go on a journey of three days into the desert, and let us sacrifice to Yahweh our God.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 5:2; Exodus 6:1; Exodus 7:1–14"> But I myself know that the king of Egypt will not allow you to go ⸤unless compelled by a strong hand⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “and not with a strong hand”">w</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 7:36; Nehemiah 9:10; Exodus 9:15"> And I will stretch out my hand, and I will strike Egypt with all of my wonders that I will do in its midst, and ⸤afterward⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “after so” or “after I do so”">x</a></sup>he will release you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 12:36; Psalm 105:37; Exodus 11:3"> And I will give this people favor in the eyes of the Egyptians, ⸤and then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “and he/it will be”">y</a></sup>when you go, you will not go empty-handed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:10; Exodus 11:2; Exodus 12:35–36"> And a woman will ask from her neighbor and from the woman dwelling as an alien in her house for objects of silver and objects of gold and garments, and you will put them on your sons and on your daughters; and you will plunder Egypt.”</span>
</div>
