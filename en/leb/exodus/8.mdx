<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 78:45; Exodus 9:2; Exodus 7:14"> And if you are refusing to release, look, I am going to plague all of your territory with frogs.</span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 19:11; Daniel 4:37; Acts 12:22–23"> And the frogs will go up against you and against your people and against all of your servants.’ ”</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 46:9; Psalm 86:8; 1 Chronicles 17:20"> And he said, “Tomorrow.” And he said, “Let it be according to your word so that you will know that there is no one like Yahweh our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 8:3; Exodus 8:9"> And the frogs will depart from you and from your house and from your servants. They will be left only in the Nile.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 5:16–18; Exodus 8:30; Exodus 10:18"> And Moses and Aaron went out from Pharaoh, and Moses cried out to Yahweh over the matter of the frogs that he had brought on Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:10–12"> And Yahweh did according to the word of Moses, and the frogs died from the houses, from the courtyards, and from the fields.</span>

## Plague Four: Flies

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 7:15; Exodus 9:13; Exodus 8:1"> And Yahweh said to Moses, “Start early in the morning and stand before Pharaoh. Look, he is going out to the water, and you must say to him, ‘Thus says Yahweh, “Release my people so that they may serve me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 78:45; Isaiah 7:18; Psalm 105:31"> But if you are not about to release my people, look, I am about to send out flies among you and among your servants and among your people and in your houses; and the houses of Egypt will fill up with the flies and also the ground that they are on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 9:4; Malachi 3:18; Exodus 10:23"> But on that day I will distinguish<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “deal differently with”">g</a></sup>the land of Goshen, where my people are staying, by there not being flies there, so that you will know that I am Yahweh in the midst of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And I will put a distinction between my people and your people; this sign will be tomorrow.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalm 105:31; Psalm 78:45; Exodus 8:21"> And Yahweh did so, and a ⸤severe⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “heavy”">h</a></sup>swarm of flies came to the house of Pharaoh and the house of his servants and in all the land of Egypt; the land was ruined because of the flies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 8:8; Exodus 9:27; Exodus 10:16"> And Pharaoh called Moses and Aaron, and he said, “Go, sacrifice to your God in the land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 43:32; Genesis 46:34; Deuteronomy 7:25–26"> And Moses said, “To do so is not right, because we will sacrifice to Yahweh our God a thing detestable to the Egyptians. Look, if we sacrifice before their eyes the thing detestable to the Egyptians, will they not stone us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 3:18; Exodus 3:12; Exodus 34:11"> We will go a journey of three days into the desert, and we will sacrifice to Yahweh our God according to what he says to us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 8:8; Exodus 9:28; 1 Kings 13:6"> And Pharaoh said, “I myself will release you, and you will sacrifice to Yahweh your God in the desert. Only surely you must not go far. Pray for me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 8:15; Jeremiah 42:20–21; Exodus 8:8"> And Moses said, “Look, I am going out from you, and I will pray to Yahweh so that the flies depart from Pharaoh, from his servants, and from his people tomorrow. Only let not Pharaoh again deceive us by not releasing the people to sacrifice to Yahweh.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 8:12; Exodus 9:33; James 5:16"> And Moses went out from Pharaoh and prayed to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And Yahweh did according to the word of Moses and removed the flies from Pharaoh, from his servants, and from his people; not one was left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 8:15; Exodus 4:21; Romans 2:5"> And Pharaoh made his heart ⸤insensitive⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “heavy”">i</a></sup>also this time, and he did not release the people.</span>
</div>
