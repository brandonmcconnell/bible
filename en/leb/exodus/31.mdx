## Provision of Skilled People

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 36:1; Exodus 35:30; John 3:27"> “See, I have called by name<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="In other words, “specifically chosen”">a</a></sup>Bezalel the son of Uri the son of Hur, from the tribe of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:31; Isaiah 28:26; 1 Kings 7:14"> And I have filled him with the Spirit of God, with wisdom and with skill<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “understanding,” “discernment,” “cleverness”">b</a></sup>and with knowledge and with every kind of craftsmanship,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 26:1; Exodus 25:32–35; 2 Chronicles 2:13–14"> to devise designs, to work with gold and with silver and with bronze,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 28:9–21"> and in stonecutting for setting and in cutting wood, for doing every kind of craftsmanship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 35:10; Exodus 35:34–1; Exodus 28:3"> And, look, I have given with him Oholiab the son of Ahisamach, from the tribe of Dan, and I have put skill in the heart of all the skilled of heart, and they will make all that I have commanded you—</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 36:8–9; Exodus 25:10–22; Exodus 26:1–37"> the tent of assembly and the ark of the testimony and the atonement cover that is on it and all the equipment of the tent,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 37:10–28; Exodus 30:1–10; Exodus 25:23–40"> and the table and all its equipment, and the pure gold lampstand and all its equipment, and the incense altar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 38:1–8; Exodus 27:1–8; Exodus 40:11"> and the altar of burnt offering and all its equipment, and the basin and its stand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 4:5–14; Exodus 28:1–43; Exodus 39:1–43"> and the garments of woven material, and ⸤the garments of the sanctuary⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “the garments of the holiness” or “the holy garments”">c</a></sup>for Aaron the priest and the garments of his sons to serve as priests,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; Exodus 30:23–38"> and the anointing oil and the fragrant incense for the sanctuary. According to all that I have commanded you, they will make it.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Provision of Rest from Work

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And Yahweh spoke to Moses and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:12; Ezekiel 20:20; Leviticus 19:30"> “And you, speak to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">d</a></sup>saying, ‘Surely you must keep my Sabbaths, because it is a sign between me and you throughout your generations, in order to know that I am Yahweh, who consecrates you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 35:2–3; Numbers 15:32–36; Ezekiel 20:21"> And you must keep the Sabbath, because it is holy for you; defilers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “profaners,” those who treat it as common">e</a></sup>of it will surely be put to death, because anyone who does work on it—that person will be cut off from among his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 2:2; Exodus 16:23; Leviticus 23:3"> On six days work can be done, and on the seventh ⸤is a Sabbath of complete rest⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “a Sabbath of ‘Sabbathation.’ ” “Sabbathation” is not a real word, but devised as an attempt to convey the sounds of the related nouns in the Hebrew phrase">f</a></sup>⸤a holy day⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “holiness”">g</a></sup>for Yahweh; anyone doing work on the Sabbath day will surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:5; Genesis 9:13; Genesis 17:11"> The ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">h</a></sup>will pay attention to the Sabbath in order to fulfill the Sabbath<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “keep the Sabbath to do/observe the Sabbath”">i</a></sup>throughout their generations as a lasting covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 1:31; Exodus 31:13; Genesis 2:2–3"> It is a sign between me and the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">j</a></sup>forever, because in six days Yahweh made the heavens and the earth, and on the seventh he ceased<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “rested”">k</a></sup>and recovered.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “breathed freely,” “got his breath”">l</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:13; Exodus 32:15–16; Deuteronomy 5:22"> And when he finished speaking with him on Mount Sinai, he gave to Moses the two tablets of the testimony, stone tablets, written with the finger of God.</span>
</div>
</div>
