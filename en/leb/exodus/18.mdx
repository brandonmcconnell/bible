## Jethro’s Visit to Moses at the Mountain of God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 2:16; Psalm 77:14–15; Exodus 3:1"> And Jethro, the priest of Midian, the father-in-law of Moses, heard all that God had done for Moses and for Israel, his people, that Yahweh had brought Israel out from Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 2:21; Exodus 4:25–26"> And Jethro, the father-in-law of Moses, took Zipporah the wife of Moses after her sending away,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 2:22; Acts 7:29; Psalm 39:12"> and her two sons—the one whose name was Gershom, for he had said, “I have been an alien in a foreign land,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 13:6; 2 Timothy 4:17; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> and the one whose name was Eliezer, for “the God of my father was my help, and he delivered me from the sword of Pharaoh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; Exodus 3:12; Exodus 24:16–17"> And Jethro, the father-in-law of Moses, came and his sons and his wife to Moses, to the desert where he was camping there at the mountain of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And he said to Moses, “I, your father-in-law Jethro, am coming to you and your wife and her two sons with her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 29:13; Genesis 14:17; 2 Samuel 11:7"> And Moses went out to meet his father-in-law, and he bowed, and he kissed him, and ⸤they each asked about the other’s welfare⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “they asked a man to his neighbor to welfare”">a</a></sup>and they came into the tent.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 145:4–12; Nehemiah 9:9–15; Psalm 107:2"> And Moses told his father-in-law all that Yahweh had done to Pharaoh and to Egypt on account of Israel, all the hardship that had found them on the way, and how Yahweh delivered them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:26; Romans 12:15; Isaiah 66:10"> And Jethro rejoiced over all the good that Yahweh had done for Israel when he delivered them from the hand of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 14:20; 2 Samuel 18:28; Luke 1:68"> And Jethro said, “Blessed be Yahweh, who has delivered you from the hand of Egypt and from the hand of Pharaoh—who has delivered the people from under the hand of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 95:3; Exodus 15:11; 2 Chronicles 2:5"> Now I know that Yahweh is greater than all the gods, ⸤even in the matter where they the Egyptians dealt arrogantly against the Israelites⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “because in the thing that they dealt arrogantly over them”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 31:54; Deuteronomy 12:7; Exodus 24:11"> And Jethro, the father-in-law of Moses, took a burnt offering and sacrifices for God, and Aaron and all the elders of Israel came to eat bread with the father-in-law of Moses before God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 16:5; Job 29:7; Judges 5:10"> ⸤And⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “And it was”">c</a></sup>the next day, Moses sat to judge the people, and the people stood before Moses from the morning until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And the father-in-law of Moses saw all that he was doing for the people, and he said, “What is this thing that you are doing for the people? Why are you sitting alone and all the people are standing by you from morning until evening?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 15:34; Numbers 27:5; Numbers 9:8"> And Moses said to his father-in-law, “Because the people come to me to seek God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 24:14; 1 Corinthians 6:1; 2 Samuel 15:3"> When ⸤they have an issue⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “a thing is to them”">d</a></sup>it comes to me, and I judge between a man and his neighbor, and I make known God’s rule and his instructions.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the father-in-law of Moses said to him, “The thing that you are doing is not good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 11:14–17; Acts 6:1–4; 2 Corinthians 12:15"> Surely you will wear out, both you and this people who are with you, because the thing is too ⸤difficult⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “heavy”">e</a></sup>for you. You are not able to do it alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 27:5; Deuteronomy 5:5; Exodus 20:19"> Now listen to my voice; I will advise you, and may God be with you. You be for the people before God, and you bring the issues to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:1; Psalm 143:8; Deuteronomy 1:18"> And you warn them of the rules and the instructions, and you make known to them the way in which they must walk and the work that they must do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 6:3; Deuteronomy 1:13–17; 2 Chronicles 19:5–10"> And you will select from all the people men of ability, fearers of God, trustworthy men, haters of dishonest gain,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “unjust profit”">f</a></sup>and you will appoint such men over them as commanders of thousands, commanders of hundreds, commanders of fifties, and commanders of tens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 11:17; Numbers 27:2; Numbers 15:33"> And let them judge the people all the time, ⸤and⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “and it will be”">g</a></sup>every major issue they will bring to you, and every minor issue they will judge themselves. And so lighten it for yourself, and they will bear it with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:6–7; Exodus 18:18; 1 Samuel 8:22"> If you will do this thing and God will command you, then you will be able to endure, and also each of the people will go to his home<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “his place”">h</a></sup>in peace.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezra 10:2; Exodus 18:19; Exodus 18:2–5"> And Moses listened to the voice of his father-in-law, and he did all that he had said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:15; Exodus 18:21; Acts 6:5"> And Moses chose men of ability from all Israel, and he appointed them as heads over the people, as commanders of thousands, commanders of hundreds, commanders of fifties, and commanders of tens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 18:22; Exodus 18:14–15; Deuteronomy 17:8"> And they judged the people all the time; the difficult issues<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “issue”">i</a></sup>they would bring to Moses, and every minor issue they would judge themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 10:29–30; Judges 19:9; Genesis 31:55"> And Moses let his father-in-law go, and he went to his land.</span>
</div>
</div>
