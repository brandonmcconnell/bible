## Instructions for Making the Incense Altar

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 8:3; Exodus 37:25–28; Exodus 40:5"> “And you will make an altar for burning incense; you will make it of acacia wood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 27:2"> a cubit its length and a cubit its width—it will be square—and two cubits its height, its horns ⸤of one piece with it⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “from it”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 25:11; Exodus 25:24"> And you will overlay it with pure gold, its top and its sides all around and its horns, and you will make for it a gold molding all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 25:27; Exodus 26:29; Exodus 27:7"> And you will make two gold rings for it; under its molding ⸤on two opposite sides⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “on two of its sides you will make on two of its sides”">b</a></sup>you will make them as ⸤holders⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “houses”">c</a></sup>for poles to carry it with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 25:13; Exodus 25:27"> You will make the poles of acacia wood, and you will overlay them with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 25:21–22; Exodus 30:36; Leviticus 16:13"> And you will put it before the curtain that is upon the ark of the testimony, before the atonement cover, which is on the testimony, there where I will meet with you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:28; 1 Chronicles 23:13; Exodus 27:20–21"> “And on it Aaron will turn fragrant incense into smoke; ⸤each morning⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “in the morning in the morning”">d</a></sup>when he tends the lamps, he will turn it into smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; 1 Thessalonians 5:17; Hebrews 7:25"> And when Aaron sets up the lamps ⸤at twilight⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “between the two evenings”">e</a></sup>he will turn it into smoke—incense of continuity—before Yahweh throughout your generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1"> You will not offer on it strange incense or a burnt offering or a grain offering, and you will not pour a libation on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 16:18; Hebrews 9:22–23; Leviticus 16:29–30"> And Aaron will make atonement on its horns one time in the year from the blood of the sin offering of the atonement; one time in the year he will make atonement on it throughout your generations; it is a most holy thing for Yahweh.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Instructions for Numbering the People

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 31:50; 1 Chronicles 27:24; Exodus 38:25–26"> “⸤When you take a census of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “when you lift up the heads of”">f</a></sup>the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">g</a></sup>to count them, they will each give the ransom of his life for Yahweh when counting them, and a plague will not be among them when counting them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 27:25; Numbers 3:47; Ezekiel 45:12"> This they will give, ⸤everyone who is counted⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “all of the going over to the being counted”">h</a></sup>the half shekel, according to the sanctuary shekel, which is twenty gerahs per shekel. The half shekel is a contribution for Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 1:20; Numbers 1:18; Numbers 1:3"> ⸤Everyone who is counted⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “all of the going over to the being counted”">i</a></sup>from ⸤twenty years old⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “a son of twenty years”">j</a></sup>and above will give the contribution of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 31:50; Leviticus 17:11; Exodus 30:12"> The rich will not give more, and the poor will not give less than the half shekel to give the contribution of Yahweh to make atonement for their lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:32–33; Numbers 16:40; Exodus 38:25–31"> And you will take the atonement money from the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">k</a></sup>and give it to the service of the tent of assembly, and it will be as a memorial for the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">l</a></sup>before Yahweh to make atonement for your lives.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Instructions for Making the Basin

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 40:7; Exodus 38:8; 1 Kings 7:23"> “And you will make a basin of bronze and its bronze stand for washing, and you will put it between the tent of assembly and the altar, and you will put water there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; Exodus 40:31–32; Psalm 26:6"> And Aaron and his sons will wash their hands and their feet with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1–3; Hebrews 12:28–29; 1 Chronicles 13:10"> When they come to the tent of assembly, they will wash with water so that they do not die, or when they approach the altar to serve by turning to smoke an offering made by fire to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 28:43; Exodus 27:21"> And they will wash their hands and their feet so that they do not die, and it will be a lasting rule for them—to him and to his offspring throughout their generations.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Instructions for Making Anointing Oil and Incense

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:19; Song of Solomon 4:14; Ezekiel 27:22"> “And take for yourself top quality balsam oils, five hundred shekels of flowing myrrh, half as much—two hundred and fifty shekels of fragrant cinnamon, and two hundred and fifty shekels of fragrant reed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalm 45:8; Exodus 29:40; Numbers 3:47"> and five hundred shekels of cassia, according to the sanctuary shekel, and a hin of olive oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; 1 Chronicles 9:30; Exodus 40:9"> And you will make it into holy anointing oil, a spice blend of a fragrant ointment the work of a perfumer; it will be holy anointing oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 7:1; 1 John 2:20; Isaiah 61:1"> And you will anoint with it the tent of assembly and the ark of the testimony,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> and the table and all its equipment and the lampstand and its equipment and the incense altar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and the altar of burnt offering and all its equipment and the basin and its stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 23:19; Exodus 29:37; Leviticus 6:18"> And you will consecrate them, and they will be most holy things; ⸤anyone who⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “anything that”; literally “all touching”">m</a></sup>touches them will be holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 8:12; Exodus 28:3; Leviticus 8:30"> And you will anoint Aaron and his sons, and you will consecrate them to serve as priests for me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; Leviticus 21:10; Psalm 89:20"> “And you will speak to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">n</a></sup>saying, ‘This will be my holy anointing oil throughout your generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 30:25; Exodus 30:37–38; Leviticus 21:10"> It will not be poured on human flesh, and with its measurements<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “recipe” or “formula”">o</a></sup>you will not make any like it; it is holy; it will be holy to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 30:38; Genesis 17:14; Exodus 12:15"> Anyone who compounds perfume like it and who puts it on a stranger will be cut off from his people.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; Exodus 25:6; Exodus 30:23"> And Yahweh said to Moses, “Take for yourself fragrant perfumes—stacte resin and onycha and galbanum—fragrant perfumes and pure frankincense, ⸤an equal part of each⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “part by part it will be”">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 2:13; Exodus 30:25; John 12:3"> and make it into a compound of incense, the work of a perfumer, salted, pure, holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 25:22; Exodus 30:6; Exodus 16:34"> And you will grind part of it to powder, and you will put part of it before the testimony in the tent of assembly where I will meet with you; it will be a most holy thing to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3; Exodus 29:37; Exodus 30:32–33"> And the incense that you will make with its measurements<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “recipe” or “formula”">q</a></sup>you will not make for yourselves; it will be holy to you for Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 30:33"> Anyone who makes any like it to smell it will be cut off from his people.”</span>
</div>
</div>
