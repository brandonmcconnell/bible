## Instruction to Collect Materials

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Nehemiah 11:2; Ezra 2:68"> “Speak to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>and let them bring to me a contribution. You will receive my contribution from every man whose heart prompts him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:9; Job 28:2"> And this is the contribution that you will receive from them—gold and silver and bronze,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 19:8; Genesis 41:42; Ezekiel 16:10"> blue, purple, and crimson yarns, and fine linen and goat hair,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 26:37–1; Exodus 26:14–15; Exodus 36:20"> and red-dyed ram skins, and fine leather,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The particular kind of leather is uncertain">b</a></sup>and acacia wood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 27:20; Exodus 25:37; Exodus 40:24–25"> oil for the lamp, balsam oils for the anointing oil and for the fragrant incense,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Exodus 28:6–30"> onyx stones and stones for mountings on the ephod and the breast piece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Kings 6:13; Revelation 21:3"> And make a sanctuary for me, and I will dwell in the midst of them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Hebrews 8:5; Acts 7:44"> according to all that I show you—the pattern of the tabernacle and the pattern of all its equipment—and so you will do.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Instructions for Making the Ark of the Covenant

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 9:4; Exodus 37:1–3; Deuteronomy 10:1–3"> “And they will make an ark of acacia wood, two and a half cubits its length and a cubit and a half its width and a cubit and a half its height.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 25:24; Exodus 30:3; 2 Chronicles 3:4"> And you will overlay it with pure gold, inside and outside you will overlay it, and you will make on it a gold molding all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 27:7; Exodus 25:26; Exodus 25:15"> And you will cast for it four gold rings, and you will put them on its four feet, with two rings on its one side and two rings on its second side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:15; Numbers 4:11; Exodus 27:6"> And you will make poles of acacia wood, and you will overlay them with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And you will put the poles into the rings on the sides of the ark to carry the ark with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 8:8; 2 Chronicles 5:9"> In the rings of the ark will be the poles; they will not be removed from it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “they will not turn aside from it”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:26; 1 Kings 8:9; Hebrews 9:4"> And you will put into the ark the testimony that I will give to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 9:5; Exodus 37:6; Hebrews 4:16"> “And you will make an atonement cover of pure gold, two and a half cubits its length and a cubit and a half its width.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:20; Hebrews 9:5; 1 Kings 8:6–7"> And you will make two cherubim of gold; you will make them of hammered work at the two ends of the atonement cover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And make one cherub ⸤at one end⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “from end from this”">d</a></sup>and one cherub ⸤at the other end⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “from end from this”">e</a></sup>of the atonement cover; you will make the cherubim on its two ends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:18; 1 Kings 8:7; Hebrews 9:5"> And the cherubim will be with outspread wings above, covering with their wings over the atonement cover ⸤and facing each other⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “and their faces a man to his brother”">f</a></sup>the faces of the cherubim will be toward the atonement cover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 26:34; Romans 10:4; Exodus 25:16–17"> And you will put the atonement cover above onto the ark, and into the ark you will put the testimony that I will give you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 29:42–43; Numbers 7:89; Isaiah 37:16"> And I will meet you there, and I will speak with you from over the atonement cover, from between the two cherubim that are to be on the ark of the testimony—all that I will command you to the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Instructions for Making a Table and a Lampstand

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 9:2; Exodus 37:10–16; 1 Kings 7:48"> “And you will make a table of acacia wood, two cubits its length and a cubit its width and a cubit and a half its height.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 25:11; 1 Kings 6:20–22"> And you will overlay it with pure gold, and you will make for it a gold molding all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 37:2; Exodus 30:3"> And you will make for it a handbreadth rim all around, and you will make a gold molding for its rim all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And you will make four gold rings for it, and you will put the rings on the four corners where its four ⸤legs⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “feet”">h</a></sup>are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 25:14; Exodus 25:28"> The rings will be near the rim as ⸤holders⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “houses”">i</a></sup>for poles to carry the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 25:14; Acts 9:15; Numbers 10:17"> And you will make the poles of acacia wood and overlay them with gold, and the table will be carried with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 4:7; Exodus 37:16; Revelation 3:20"> And you will make its plates and its ladles<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “bowls”">j</a></sup>and its pitchers and its bowls with which libations will be poured; of pure gold you will make them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 24:5–9; Matthew 12:4; Exodus 39:36"> And you will put on the table the bread of presence to be before me continually.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 7:49; Zechariah 4:2; Exodus 37:17–24"> “And you will make a lampstand of pure gold; the lampstand will be made of hammered work—its base and its branch,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “reed”">k</a></sup>its cups, its buds, and its blossoms will be from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And six branches will be going out from its sides, three branches of the lampstand from its one side and three branches of the lampstand from its second side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 17:4–8; Jeremiah 1:11–12; Zechariah 4:3"> Three almond-flower cups will be on the one branch with a bud and a blossom, and three almond-flower cups will be on the one branch with a bud and a blossom—likewise for the six branches going out from the lampstand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And on the lampstand will be four almond-flower cups, with its buds and its blossoms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And a bud will be under the two branches that come from it, and a bud under the two branches from it, and a bud under the two branches from it, likewise for the six branches coming out from the lampstand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 25:18; 1 Kings 10:16–17; 2 Chronicles 9:15"> Their buds and their branches will be from it, all of it one piece of pure gold hammered work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 8:2; Revelation 1:20–1; Leviticus 24:2–4"> And you will make its seven lamps, and its lamps will be set up, and it will give light ⸤in the space in front of it⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “on beyond its face”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Kings 12:13; Jeremiah 52:18; 2 Kings 25:14"> And its snuffers and its fire pans will be pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> It will be made from a talent of pure gold, with all these pieces of equipment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Hebrews 8:5; Exodus 26:30; Acts 7:44"> And see and make all according to their pattern, which you were shown in the mountain.</span>
</div>
</div>
