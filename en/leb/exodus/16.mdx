## Yahweh Provides Food in the Desert

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 33:10–12; Exodus 15:27; Exodus 17:1"> And they set out from Elim, and all the community of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>came to the desert of Sin, which is between Elim ⸤and Sinai⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “and between Sinai”">b</a></sup>in the fifteenth day of the second month of their going out from the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:10; Exodus 15:24; Psalm 106:25"> And all the community of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">c</a></sup>grumbled against Moses and against Aaron in the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 11:4–5; Numbers 20:3–5; Exodus 17:3"> And the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">d</a></sup>said to them, “⸤If only we had died⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “who will give our dying”">e</a></sup>by the hand of Yahweh in the land of Egypt, when we sat by the pots<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “pot”">f</a></sup>of meat, when we ate bread ⸤until we were full⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “to contentment” or “to satisfaction”">g</a></sup>because you have brought us out to this desert to kill all of this assembly with hunger.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:3; Psalm 78:24–25; Deuteronomy 8:2"> And Yahweh said to Moses, “Look, I am going to rain down for you bread from the heavens, and the people will go out and gather enough for the day on its day; in that way I will test them: Will they go according to my law or not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 16:22–23; Exodus 35:2–3; Leviticus 25:21–22"> And then on the sixth day, they will prepare what they bring, and it will be twice over what they will gather every other day.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 6:7; Numbers 16:28; Exodus 16:12–13"> And Moses and Aaron said to all the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">h</a></sup>“In the evening, you will know that Yahweh has brought you out from the land of Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 40:5; Numbers 16:11; John 11:40"> and in the morning, you will see the glory of Yahweh, ⸤for he hears⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “at/in his hearing”">i</a></sup>your grumblings against Yahweh, and what are we that you grumble against us?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 13:2; 1 Samuel 8:7; 1 Thessalonians 4:8"> And Moses said, “When in the evening Yahweh gives you meat to eat and bread in the morning ⸤to fill up on⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “to be satisfied”">j</a></sup>⸤for he hears⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “at/in his hearing”">k</a></sup>your grumblings that you grumble against him—and what are we? Your grumblings are not against us but against Yahweh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 16:16; Exodus 16:2; Exodus 16:8"> And Moses said to Aaron, “Say to all the community of the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">l</a></sup>‘Come near before Yahweh because he has heard your grumblings.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 16:7; Numbers 16:19; Numbers 16:42"> And at the moment of Aaron’s speaking to all the community of the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">m</a></sup>they turned to the desert, ⸤and just then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “look” or “behold”">n</a></sup>the glory of Yahweh appeared in the cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joel 3:17; Ezekiel 39:22; Exodus 6:7"> “I have heard the grumblings of the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">o</a></sup>Speak to them, saying, ‘⸤At twilight⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “between the evenings”">p</a></sup>you will eat meat, and in the morning you will be full with bread, and you will know that I am Yahweh your God.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 11:9; Psalm 105:40; Psalm 78:27–28"> And so it was, in the evening, the quail came up and covered the camp, and in the morning, a layer of dew was all around the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 11:7–9; Psalm 105:40; Psalm 78:24"> And the layer of dew came up, ⸤and there⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “look” or “behold”">q</a></sup>on the face of the desert was a fine granular substance, fine like frost on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 16:4; John 6:58; Exodus 16:31"> And the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">r</a></sup>saw, and they said ⸤to each other⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “a man to his brother”">s</a></sup>“What is this?” because they did not know what it was. And Moses said to them, “That is the bread that Yahweh has given to you as food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 16:36; Exodus 16:18; Exodus 16:32–33"> This is the word that Yahweh commanded, ‘Gather from it, ⸤each according to what he can eat⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “a man for the mouth of his eating”">t</a></sup>an omer per person<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “skull” or “head”">u</a></sup>according to the number of you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “the number of your selves”">v</a></sup>You each shall take enough for whoever is in his tent.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">w</a></sup>did so, and they gathered, some more and some less.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:14–15"> And when they measured with the omer, the one gathering more had no surplus, and the one gathering less had no lack; they gathered ⸤each according to what he could eat⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “a man for the mouth of his eating”">x</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 12:10; Exodus 23:18; Matthew 6:34"> And Moses said to them, “Let no one leave any of it until morning.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 10:14; Numbers 16:15; Ephesians 4:26"> But they did not listen to Moses. Some people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “men”">y</a></sup>left some of it until morning, and it bred worms and stank. And Moses was angry with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 6:6–11; Ecclesiastes 12:1; 2 Corinthians 6:2"> And they gathered it morning by morning, ⸤each according to what he could eat⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “a man according to the mouth of his eating”">z</a></sup>and it melted when the sun was hot.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “and the sun was hot, and he/it melted”">aa</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 16:5; Leviticus 25:22; Exodus 16:16"> And when it was the sixth day, they gathered twice as much bread, two omers for one person, and all the leaders of the community came and told Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 23:3; Exodus 31:15; Exodus 20:8–11"> And he said to them, “This is what Yahweh has said. Tomorrow is a rest period, a holy Sabbath for Yahweh. Bake what you want to bake, and boil what you want to boil. Put aside all the surplus for yourselves for safekeeping until the morning.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 16:20; Exodus 16:33"> And they put it aside until the morning, as Moses had commanded, and it did not make a stench, and not a maggot was in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 16:23; Exodus 16:29; Nehemiah 9:14"> And Moses said, “Eat it today, because today is a Sabbath for Yahweh. Today you will not find it in the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:13; Exodus 20:9–11; Ezekiel 46:1"> Six days you will gather it, but on the seventh day, the Sabbath, it will not be present on it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 20:4"> And on the seventh day some of the people went out to gather, and they did not find any.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalm 78:10; Psalm 106:13; Numbers 14:11"> And Yahweh said to Moses, “How long do you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Plural">ab</a></sup>refuse to keep my commands and my laws?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 31:13; Isaiah 58:13–14; Ezekiel 20:12"> See, because Yahweh has given to you the Sabbath, therefore he is giving to you on the sixth day bread for two days. Stay, ⸤each in his location⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “a man under him/it”">ac</a></sup>let no one go from his place on the seventh day.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:12–14; Leviticus 23:3; Hebrews 4:9"> And the people rested on the seventh day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:3; Numbers 11:6–9; Exodus 16:15"> And the house of Israel called its name “manna.” And it was like coriander seed, white, and its taste was like a wafer with honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalm 105:5; Luke 22:19; Psalm 111:4–5"> And Moses said, “This is the word that Yahweh has commanded. ‘A full omer of it is for safekeeping for your generations so that they will see the bread that I fed you in the desert when I brought you from the land of Egypt.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 9:4"> And Moses said to Aaron, “Take one jar and put there a full omer of manna. Leave it before Yahweh for safekeeping for your generations.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 27:21; Exodus 25:21; Exodus 25:16"> As Yahweh had commanded Moses, so Aaron left it before the testimony for safekeeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:20–21; Joshua 5:12; Deuteronomy 8:2–3"> And the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">ad</a></sup>ate the manna forty years, until their coming to an inhabited land; they ate the manna until their coming to the border of the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 16:16; Exodus 16:32–33"> (And an omer is a tenth of an ephah.)</span>
</div>
