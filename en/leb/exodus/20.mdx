<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:7; Matthew 4:10; Psalm 81:9"> “There shall be for you no other gods before me.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:8; Leviticus 26:1; Deuteronomy 4:15–19"> “You shall not make for yourself a divine image with any form that is in the heavens above or that is in the earth below or that is in the water below the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:9; Numbers 14:18; Deuteronomy 4:24"> You will not bow down to them, and you will not serve them, because I am Yahweh your God, a jealous God, punishing the guilt of the parents on the children on the third and on the fourth generations<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Generations” is understood from comparison with parallel verses (compare Exod 34:6–7 ; Deut 7:9 )">a</a></sup>of those hating me,</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:11; Leviticus 19:12; James 5:12"> “You shall not ⸤misuse the name of Yahweh your God⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “take up/bear/carry the name of Yahweh your God for what is worthless/false/empty”">c</a></sup>because Yahweh will not leave unpunished anyone who ⸤misuses his name⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “bears his name for what is false”">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 26:2; Leviticus 19:3; Exodus 31:13–16"> “Remember the day of the Sabbath, to consecrate it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 13:14; Exodus 34:21; Exodus 23:12"> Six days you will work, and you will do all your work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:14–15; Exodus 34:21; Numbers 15:32–36"> But the seventh day is a Sabbath for Yahweh your God; you will not do any work—you or your son or your daughter, your male slave or your female slave, or your animal, or your alien who is in your gates—</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 1:1–2; Genesis 2:2–3; Exodus 31:17"> because in six days Yahweh made the heavens and the earth, the sea and all that is in them, and on the seventh day he rested. Therefore Yahweh blessed the seventh day and consecrated it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1–3; Colossians 3:20; Deuteronomy 5:16"> “Honor your father and your mother, so that your days can be long on the land that Yahweh your God is giving you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Deuteronomy 5:17; Genesis 9:5–6"> “You shall not murder.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:4; Deuteronomy 5:18; Romans 7:2–3"> “You shall not commit adultery.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Ephesians 4:28; Leviticus 19:11"> “You shall not steal.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 19:5; Matthew 19:18; Psalm 15:3"> “You shall not testify against your neighbor with a false witness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Luke 12:15; Hebrews 13:5"> “You shall not covet the house of your neighbor; you will not covet the wife of your neighbor or his male servant or his female servant or his ox or his donkey or anything that is your neighbor’s.”</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:16; Acts 7:38; Galatians 3:19"> And they said to Moses, “You speak with us, and we will listen, but let not God speak with us, lest we die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:3; Proverbs 3:7; 1 Samuel 12:20"> And Moses said to the people, “Do not be afraid. God has come to test you so that his fear will be before you so that you do not sin.”</span>

## Instructions for Building Altars

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:13; Deuteronomy 4:36; Hebrews 12:25–26"> And Yahweh said to Moses, “Thus you will say to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">e</a></sup>‘You yourselves have seen that I have spoken to you from the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:39; Zephaniah 1:5; 2 Kings 17:33"> You will not make alongside me gods of silver, and gods of gold you will not make for yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5; 2 Chronicles 6:6; Psalm 132:13–14"> An altar of earth you will make for me, and you will sacrifice on it your burnt offerings and your fellowship offerings, your sheep and your cattle. In every place where I cause my name to be remembered,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “proclaimed”">f</a></sup>I will come to you, and I will bless you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 8:31; Deuteronomy 27:5–6"> And if you make an altar of stones for me, you will not build them as hewn stone, because if you use your chisel on it, you have defiled it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 1:16; Ecclesiastes 5:1; Leviticus 10:3"> You will not go up with steps onto my altar, that your nakedness not be exposed on it.’</span>
