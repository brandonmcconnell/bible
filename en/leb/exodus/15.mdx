## Song of Victory at the Sea

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 15:3; Exodus 15:21; Psalm 106:12"> Then Moses and the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>sang this song to Yahweh, ⸤and they said⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “and they said, saying”">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Let me sing to Yahweh because he is highly exalted;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the horse and its rider he hurled into the sea.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2; Psalm 118:14; Psalm 18:46"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yah</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="a shortened form of “Yahweh”">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">is my strength and song, and he has become my salvation;</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “he was for me for salvation”">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">this is my God, and I will praise him—the God of my father—and I will exalt him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 24:8; Exodus 14:14; Revelation 19:11–21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh is a man of war; Yahweh is his name.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 14:13–28; Exodus 14:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">The chariots of Pharaoh and his army he cast into the sea,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and his choice adjutants were sunk in the ⸤Red Sea⸥.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “sea of reed”">e</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 118:15–16; Isaiah 51:9; Revelation 2:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh, your right hand is glorious in power;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Yahweh, your right hand destroyed the enemy.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 5:24; Isaiah 47:14; Malachi 4:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">And in the greatness of your majesty you overthrew those standing up to you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you released your fierce anger, and it consumed them like stubble.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 4:9; Psalm 78:13; Exodus 14:21–22"> <span className="pl pl-1">And by the breath of your nostrils waters were piled up;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">waves stood like a heap;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">deep waters in the middle of the sea congealed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 5:30; Exodus 14:5; Isaiah 53:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">The enemy said, ‘I will pursue, I will overtake, I will divide plunder,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">my desire will be full of them, I will draw my sword, my hand will destroy them.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Deuteronomy 11:4; Exodus 15:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">You blew with your breath; the sea covered them;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they dropped like lead in the mighty waters.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 8:23; Psalm 89:5–8; 1 Samuel 2:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Who is like you among the gods, Yahweh?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Who is like you—glorious in holiness, awesome in praiseworthy actions, doing wonders?</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “wonder”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 15:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">You stretched out your right hand;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the earth swallowed them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 77:20; 1 Peter 1:5; Psalm 77:14–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">In your loyal love you led the people whom you redeemed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in your strength you guided them to the abode of your holiness.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “your holy dwelling”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:25; Joshua 2:9–10; Psalm 48:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Peoples heard; they trembled;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">anguish seized the inhabitants of Philistia.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 5:1; Joshua 2:11; Joshua 2:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then the chiefs of Edom were horrified; great distress seized the leaders of Moab;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">all of the inhabitants of Canaan melted away.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:25; Psalm 74:2; Joshua 2:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Terror and dread fell on them;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">at the greatness of your arm they became silent like the stone,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">until your people passed by, Yahweh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">until the people whom you bought passed by.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 44:2; Psalm 80:8; Psalm 78:54–55"> <span className="pl pl-1">You brought them and planted them on the mountain of your inheritance,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a place you made for yourself to inhabit, Yahweh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a sanctuary, Lord, that your hands established.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 10:16; Psalm 29:10; Psalm 146:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh will reign as king forever and ever.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 14:28–29; Exodus 14:22–23; Hebrews 11:29"> When the horses<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “horse”">h</a></sup>of Pharaoh came into the sea with his chariots<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “chariot”">i</a></sup>and with his charioteers, Yahweh brought back upon them the waters of the sea, and the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">j</a></sup>traveled on dry ground through the middle of the sea.</span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 15:1; Revelation 15:3; Revelation 7:10–12"> And Miriam answered,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “answered to them”">l</a></sup>“Sing to Yahweh because he is highly exalted; the horse and its rider he hurled into the sea.”</span>
</div>

## Yahweh Provides Water at Marah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 16:7; 1 Samuel 15:7; Genesis 25:18"> And Moses caused Israel to set out from the ⸤Red Sea⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “sea of reed”">m</a></sup>and they went out into the desert of Shur, and they traveled three days in the desert, and they did not find water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ruth 1:20; Numbers 33:8"> And they came to Marah, and they were not able to drink water from Marah because it was bitter. Therefore ⸤it was named⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “its name was called”; the Hebrew word for “bitter” is pronounced “marah”">n</a></sup>Marah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 16:2; Exodus 14:11; Numbers 20:2–5"> And the people grumbled against Moses, saying, “What shall we drink?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:2; Psalm 66:10; Judges 3:1"> And he cried out to Yahweh, and Yahweh showed him a piece of wood, and he threw it into the water, and the water became sweet. There he made a rule and regulation for them, and there he tested them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalm 103:3; Deuteronomy 7:15; Exodus 23:25"> And he said, “If you carefully listen to the voice of Yahweh your God and you do what is right in his eyes and give heed to his commands and you keep all his rules, then I will not bring about on you any of the diseases<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Hebrew “disease”">o</a></sup>that I brought about on Egypt, because I am Yahweh your healer.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Revelation 22:2; Revelation 7:17; Numbers 33:9"> And they came to Elim, and twelve springs of water and seventy palm trees were there, and they encamped there at the water.</span>
</div>
