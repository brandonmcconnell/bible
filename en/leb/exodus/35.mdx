## Preparation of Materials and People for Building

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 34:32; Exodus 31:1–11; Romans 2:13"> And Moses assembled all the community of the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>and he said to them, “These are the words that Yahweh has commanded for us to do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 23:3; Luke 13:14–15; Exodus 20:9–10"> On six days work can be done, and on the seventh there will be for you a holy day,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “holiness”">b</a></sup>a ⸤Sabbath of complete rest⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “a Sabbath of ‘Sabbathation.’ ” “Sabbathation” is not a real word, but it is devised as an attempt to convey the sounds of the related nouns in the Hebrew phrase">c</a></sup>for Yahweh; anyone doing work on it will be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 16:23; Numbers 15:32–36; Exodus 12:16"> You will not kindle a fire in any of your dwellings on the day of the Sabbath.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 25:1–2"> And Moses said to all the community of the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">d</a></sup>saying, “This is the word that Yahweh has commanded, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Psalm 110:3; Exodus 25:2–7"> ‘Take from among you a contribution for Yahweh, anyone willing of heart, let him bring Yahweh’s contribution—gold and silver and bronze,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 26:7–14; Exodus 26:31; Exodus 28:15"> and blue and purple and crimson yarns, and fine linen and goat hair,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and red-dyed ram skins, and fine leather,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The particular kind of leather is uncertain">e</a></sup>and acacia wood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 27:20; Exodus 25:1–40; Exodus 30:23"> and oil for the lamp, balsam oils for the anointing oil and for the fragrant incense,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 39:6–14; Exodus 28:9; Exodus 25:5"> onyx stones and stones for mountings on the ephod and the breast piece.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 31:1–6; Exodus 36:1–4"> “And let all the skilled of heart among you come and make all that Yahweh has commanded:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 26:1–37; Exodus 36:8–34; Exodus 31:7–9"> The tabernacle, its tent, and its covering; its clasps and its frames;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “boards” or “planks”">f</a></sup>its bars, its pillars, and its bases;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 25:10–22; Exodus 26:31–33; Exodus 37:1–9"> the ark and its poles; the atonement cover and the curtain of the screen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 25:23–30; Leviticus 24:5–6; Exodus 37:10–16"> the table and its poles and all its equipment; and the bread of the presence;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–39; Matthew 5:14–15; Exodus 37:17–24"> and lampstand of the light and its equipment and its lamps and the oil for the light;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 30:1–10; Exodus 30:22–38; Exodus 37:25–28"> and the altar of incense and its poles; and the anointing oil and the fragrant incense and the entrance curtain for the entrance of the tabernacle;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 27:1–8; Exodus 30:18–21; Exodus 38:1–8"> the altar of the burnt offering and the bronze grating that is for it, its poles and all its equipment; the basin and its stand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:2; Exodus 38:9–20; Exodus 27:9–19"> the hangings of the courtyard, its pillars, and its bases, and the screen for the courtyard gate;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 27:19"> the ⸤pegs⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “hands”">g</a></sup>of the tabernacle and the ⸤pegs⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “hands”">h</a></sup>of the courtyard and their cords;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 31:10; Numbers 4:5–15; Exodus 28:1–43"> the woven garments for serving in the sanctuary—the holy garments for Aaron the priest and the garments of his sons to serve as priests.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And all the community of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">i</a></sup>went out from before Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 25:2; Exodus 35:26; 1 Chronicles 29:14"> And they came—every man whose heart lifted him and every man whose spirit impelled him—they brought Yahweh’s contribution for the work of the tent of assembly and for all its service and for the holy garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:70–72; Matthew 2:11; Ezra 2:68–69"> And they came, the men in addition to the women, all who were willing of heart; they brought brooches and jewelry rings and signet rings and ornaments—every variety of gold object—every man who waved a wave offering of gold for Yahweh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:8; Exodus 25:2–7; Exodus 35:6–10"> and every man with whom was found blue and purple and crimson yarns and fine linen and goat hair and red-dyed ram skins and fine leather brought it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12"> All who were presenting a contribution of silver and bronze brought Yahweh’s contribution, and all with whom was found acacia wood for all the work of service brought it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 16:12; Luke 8:2–3; Proverbs 14:1"> And every woman who was skilled of heart with her hands they spun, and they brought yarn—the blue and the purple, the crimson and the fine linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 35:21; Exodus 36:8; Exodus 35:29"> And all the women whose heart lifted them with skill spun the goat hair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezra 2:68; 1 Chronicles 29:6; Exodus 35:9"> And the leaders brought the onyx stones and stones for mountings for the ephod and for the breast piece</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 30:23–38; Exodus 35:8"> and the balsam oils and the oil for light and for the anointing oil and for the fragrant incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Exodus 35:21–22; Exodus 35:4"> Every man and woman whose heart impelled them to bring for all the work to be done that Yahweh had commanded ⸤by the agency of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “by the hand of”">j</a></sup>Moses—the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">k</a></sup>brought freely to Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 31:1–6; James 1:17; 1 Corinthians 12:11"> And Moses said to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">l</a></sup>“See, Yahweh has called by name<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="In other words, “specifically chosen”">m</a></sup>Bezalel the son of Uri the son of Hur, from the tribe of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 1:17; Isaiah 11:2–5; Isaiah 61:1–3"> And he has filled him with the Spirit of God, with wisdom and with skill<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “understanding,” “discernment,” “cleverness”">n</a></sup>and with knowledge and with every kind of craftsmanship,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> and to devise designs, to work with the gold and with the silver and with the bronze,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> and in stonecutting for setting and in cutting wood, for doing every kind of design craftsmanship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 31:6; 2 Chronicles 2:14; Ezra 7:27"> And he has put it in his heart to teach—he and Oholiab the son of Ahisamach, from the tribe of Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Exodus 35:31; 1 Kings 7:14; Isaiah 28:26"> He has filled them with skill of heart to do every work of a craftsman and a designer and an embroiderer with the blue and with the purple, with the crimson yarns and with the fine linen and a weaver; they are doers of every kind of craftsmanship and devisers of designs.</span>
</div>
</div>
