## The Birth and Early Life of Moses

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 26:59; 1 Chronicles 23:12–14; Exodus 6:16–20"> And a man from the ⸤family⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “house”">a</a></sup>of Levi went, and he took ⸤a descendent of Levi⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “daughter of Levi”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 11:23; Acts 7:20; Psalm 112:5"> And the woman conceived, and she gave birth to a son, and she saw him, that he was a fine baby, and she hid him three months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 1:22; Acts 7:19; Genesis 6:14"> But when she could no longer hide him, she got a papyrus basket for him, and she coated it with tar and with pitch, and she placed the boy in it, and she placed it among the reeds on the bank of the Nile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 26:59; Exodus 15:20; Micah 6:4"> And his sister stood at a distance to know what would be done to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 7:21; Psalm 12:5; Psalm 9:9"> And the daughter of Pharaoh went down to wash at the Nile, while her maidservants were walking alongside the Nile, and she saw the basket in the midst of the reeds, and she sent her slave woman for it and took it</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 8:50; 1 Peter 3:8; Acts 7:21"> and opened it and saw him—the boy—and it was a lad weeping, and she had compassion for him and said, “This must be from the boys of the Hebrews.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Each instance of the implied “she” in verses 5 and 6 is the daughter of Pharaoh">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 2:4; Numbers 26:59; Numbers 12:1"> And his sister said to the daughter of Pharaoh, “Shall I go and call for you a woman from the Hebrews who is nursing so that she will nurse the boy for you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 46:3–4; Psalm 27:10; Exodus 6:20"> And the daughter of Pharaoh said to her, “Go.” And the girl went, and she called the mother of the boy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And the daughter of Pharaoh said, “Take this boy and nurse him for me, and I myself will give you wages, and the woman took the boy, and she nursed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 11:24; Genesis 16:11; 1 Samuel 1:20"> And the boy grew, and she brought him to the daughter of Pharaoh, and he became her son, and she called his name Moses, and she said, “Because I drew him out from the water.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 11:24–26; Acts 7:22–24; Exodus 1:11"> ⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “and he/it was”">d</a></sup>in those days when Moses had grown up, he went out to his brothers, and he saw their ⸤forced labor⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “burdens” or “burdensome labor”">e</a></sup>and he saw an Egyptian man striking a Hebrew man, one of his brothers.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “a Hebrew man from his brothers”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 7:24–26"> And he turned here and there, and he saw no one,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “and he saw that there was not a man”">g</a></sup>and he struck the Egyptian, and he hid him in the sand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 7:26–28; 1 Corinthians 6:7–8"> And he went out on the second day, and there were two Hebrew men fighting, and he said to the guilty one, “Why do you strike your neighbor?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 12:14; Matthew 21:23; Genesis 19:9"> And he said, “Who ⸤appointed you as a commander⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “put you for a man, a commander” or “placed you for a man, a commander”">h</a></sup>and a judge over us? Are you intending to kill me like you killed the Egyptian?” And Moses was afraid, and he said, “Surely the matter has become known.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 7:29; Hebrews 11:27; Genesis 24:11"> And Pharaoh heard this matter, and he sought to kill Moses, and Moses fled from Pharaoh, and he lived in the land of Midian, and he lived at ⸤a certain well⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “the well”">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 24:11; Exodus 3:1; 1 Samuel 9:11"> Now the priest of Midian had seven daughters, and they came and drew water and filled the troughs to water their father’s flock.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="The Hebrew noun translated “flock” is used here and in verses 17 and 19 collectively for sheep or goats or a mix of the two">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 29:10; Genesis 21:25; Genesis 26:15–22"> And the shepherds came and drove them away, but Moses stood up and came to their rescue and watered their flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; Numbers 10:29; Exodus 4:18"> And they came to Reuel, their father, and he said, “⸤Why have you come so quickly⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “why did you hasten coming”">k</a></sup>today?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 50:11; Genesis 29:10"> And they said, “An Egyptian man delivered us from the hand of the shepherds, and he even drew water for us and watered the flock.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 31:54; Genesis 43:25; Hebrews 13:2"> And he said to his daughters, “Where is he? ⸤Why then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “why this”">l</a></sup>have you left the man? ⸤Call him so that he can eat some food⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “call to or “summon/invite” him and he will eat bread”">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 31:38–40; Hebrews 13:5; Hebrews 11:25"> And Moses agreed to stay with the man, and he gave Zipporah his daughter to Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 11:13–14; Acts 7:29; Exodus 22:21"> And she bore a son, and he called his name Gershom because he said, “I am an alien in a foreign land.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="The word translated “alien” sounds like the first syllable in the name Gershom. The second syllable sounds like the word for “there”">n</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 18:20–21; James 5:4; Exodus 3:7–9"> ⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “and he/it was”">o</a></sup>during those many days, the king of Egypt died, and the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">p</a></sup>groaned because of the work, and they cried out, and their cry for help because of the work went up to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 26:3; Psalm 105:42; Exodus 6:5"> And God heard their groaning, and God remembered his covenant with Abraham, with Isaac, and with Jacob,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 4:31; Psalm 55:22; Exodus 3:7–8"> and God saw the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">q</a></sup>and God took notice.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “and God knew”">r</a></sup></span>
</div>
