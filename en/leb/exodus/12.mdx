## Instructions for the Feast of Passover

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Yahweh said to Moses and to Aaron in the land of Egypt, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 13:4; Deuteronomy 16:1; Exodus 34:18"> “This month will be the beginning of months; it will be for you the first of the months of the year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 1:2; 1 Corinthians 5:7; John 1:29"> Speak to all the community of Israel, saying, ‘On the tenth of this month, they will each take for themselves ⸤a lamb for the family⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “a lamb/kid for the house of fathers.” The word translated “lamb” refers to a small livestock animal and could be either a young sheep or a young goat">a</a></sup>a lamb for the household.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And if the household is too small for a lamb, he and the neighbor nearest to his house will take one according to the number of persons;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “the number of selves”">b</a></sup>you will count out portions of the lamb ⸤according to how much each one can eat⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “a man for the mouth of his eating”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 1:18–19; Hebrews 9:13–14; Malachi 1:14"> The lamb for you must be a male, without defect, in its first year; you will take it from the sheep or from the goats.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 23:5; 2 Chronicles 30:15–18; Numbers 28:16"> “⸤You will keep it⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “It will be for you for observation”">d</a></sup>until the fourteenth day of this month, and all the assembly of the community of Israel will slaughter it ⸤at twilight⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “between the evenings”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 11:28; Exodus 12:22–23; Hebrews 9:22"> And they will take some of the blood and put it on the two doorposts and on the lintel on the houses in which they eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 34:25; Deuteronomy 16:7; Exodus 13:3"> And they will eat the meat on this night; they will eat it fire-roasted and with unleavened bread on ⸤bitter herbs⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “bitter things ”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 12:8; Deuteronomy 16:7; Lamentations 1:13"> You must not eat any of it raw or boiled, boiled in the water, but rather roasted with fire, its head with its legs and with its inner parts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 23:18; Exodus 34:25; Exodus 29:34"> And you must not leave any of it until morning; anything left from it until morning you must burn in the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 12:27; Ephesians 6:15; 1 Corinthians 5:7"> And this is how you will eat it—with your waists fastened, your sandals on your feet, and your staff in your hand, and you will eat it in haste. It is Yahweh’s Passover.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 33:4; Exodus 12:23; Psalm 82:6"> “And I will go through the land of Egypt during this night, and I will strike all of the firstborn in the land of Egypt, from human to animal, and I will do punishments among all of the gods of Egypt. I am Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 11:28; Exodus 12:23; 1 John 1:7"> And the blood will be a sign for you on the houses where you are, and I will see the blood, and I will pass over you, and there will not be a destructive plague among you when I strike the land of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 23:21; Exodus 12:24; Exodus 13:9–10"> “And this day will become a memorial for you, and you will celebrate it as a religious feast for Yahweh throughout your generations; you will celebrate it as a lasting statute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 17:14; Deuteronomy 16:3; Exodus 23:15"> You will eat unleavened bread for seven days. Surely on the first day you shall remove yeast from your houses, because anyone who eats food with yeast from the first day until the seventh day—that person will be cut off from Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 23:7–8; Numbers 28:18; Exodus 16:23"> It will be for you on the first day a holy assembly<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “summons,” “convocation”">g</a></sup>and on the seventh day a holy assembly;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “summons,” “convocation”">h</a></sup>no work will be done on them; only what is eaten by every person, it alone will be prepared for you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 7:5; Exodus 13:3; Exodus 12:41"> “And you will keep the Feast of Unleavened Bread, because on this very day I brought out your divisions from the land of Egypt, and you will keep this day for your generations as a lasting statute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 23:5–8; Exodus 12:15; Exodus 12:1–2"> On the first day, on the fourteenth day of the month, in the evening, you will eat unleavened bread until the evening of the twenty-first day of the month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 12:15; Exodus 23:15; Deuteronomy 16:3"> For seven days yeast must not be found in your houses, because ⸤anyone eating food with yeast⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “anyone eating yeasted food, that person”">i</a></sup>will be cut off from the community of Israel—whether an alien or a native of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> You will eat no food with yeast; in all of your dwellings you will eat unleavened bread.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 12:3; Luke 22:7–13; 2 Chronicles 35:5–6"> And Moses called all the elders of Israel, and he said to them, “Select and take for yourselves sheep for your clans and slaughter the Passover sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 11:28; Numbers 19:18; Psalm 51:7"> And take a bunch of hyssop and dip it into the blood that is in the basin and apply some of the blood that is in the basin to the lintel and the two doorposts. And you will not go out, anyone from the doorway of his house, until morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 7:3; Revelation 9:4; Hebrews 11:28"> And Yahweh will go through to strike Egypt, and he will see the blood on the lintel and on the two doorposts, and Yahweh will pass over the doorway and will not allow the destroyer to come to your houses to strike you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 12:14; Genesis 17:8–10"> “And you will keep this event as a rule for you and for your children forever.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “until eternity”">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 3:17; Exodus 3:8; Deuteronomy 12:8–9"> ⸤And⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “and it will be”">k</a></sup>when you come into the land that Yahweh will give to you, as he said, you will keep this ⸤religious custom⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “service” or “work”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 13:14–15; Deuteronomy 32:7; Psalm 145:4"> ⸤And⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “and it will be”">m</a></sup>when your children say to you, ‘What is this ⸤religious custom⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “service” or “work”">n</a></sup>for you?’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “What does this service mean to you?”">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 4:31; Exodus 12:11; 1 Corinthians 5:7"> you will say, ‘It is a Passover sacrifice for Yahweh, who passed over the houses of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">p</a></sup>in Egypt when he struck Egypt; and he delivered our houses.’ ” And the people knelt down and they worshiped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 11:28"> And the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">q</a></sup>went, and they did as Yahweh had commanded Moses and Aaron; so they did.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Death of Firstborn and Deliverance from Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalm 78:51; Exodus 4:23; Psalm 105:36"> ⸤And⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “and it was”">r</a></sup>in the middle of the night, Yahweh struck all of the firstborn in the land of Egypt, from the firstborn of Pharaoh sitting on his throne to the firstborn of the captive who was in the prison house and every firstborn of an animal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 11:6; Amos 5:17; Proverbs 21:13"> And Pharaoh got up at night, he and all his servants and all Egypt, and a great cry of distress was in Egypt because there was not a house where there was no one dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 6:1; Exodus 10:9; Exodus 3:19–20"> And he called Moses and Aaron at night, and he said, “Get up, go out from the midst of my people, both you as well as the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">s</a></sup>and go, serve Yahweh, as you have said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 10:26; Genesis 27:34; Genesis 27:38"> Take both your sheep and goats as well as your cattle, and go, and bless also me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalm 105:38; Exodus 11:1; Genesis 20:3"> And the Egyptians urged the people in order to hurry their release<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Or “to hasten to release them”">t</a></sup>from the land, because they said, “All of us will die!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “We all are dead!”">u</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 8:3"> And the people lifted up their dough before it had yeast; their kneading troughs were wrapped up in their cloaks on their shoulder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Exodus 3:21–22; Exodus 11:2–3; Genesis 15:14"> And the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">v</a></sup>did according to the word of Moses, and they asked from the Egyptians for objects of silver and objects of gold and for clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 15:14; Acts 7:10; Exodus 3:21–22"> And Yahweh gave the people favor in the eyes of the Egyptians, and they granted their requests, and they plundered the Egyptians.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 1:46; Exodus 38:26; Numbers 33:3"> And the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">w</a></sup>set out from Rameses to Succoth; the men were about six hundred thousand on foot,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Or “footmen,” “infantrymen.” The word probably has military connotations, as when it is used elsewhere in the numbering of soldiers (compare 2 Sam 10:6 ; 1 Kgs 20:29 ; 2 Kgs 13:7 )">x</a></sup>besides dependents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 11:4; Zechariah 8:23"> And also a ⸤mixed multitude⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “a large mixture” (compare Jer 25:20 ; Ezek 30:5 )">y</a></sup>went up with them and sheep and goats and cattle, very numerous livestock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 11:1; Exodus 6:1; Exodus 12:31–33"> And they baked the dough that they had brought out from Egypt as cakes, unleavened bread, because it had no yeast when they were driven out from Egypt, and they were not able to delay, and also they had not made provisions for themselves.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Genesis 15:13; Acts 7:6; Acts 13:17"> And the period of dwelling of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">z</a></sup>that they dwelled in Egypt was four hundred and thirty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:3; John 7:8; Acts 1:7"> And at the end of four hundred and thirty years, on this exact day, all of Yahweh’s divisions went out from the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:1–6; Exodus 12:14; Exodus 13:10"> It is a night of vigils<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Or “observances”">aa</a></sup>belonging to Yahweh for bringing them out from the land of Egypt; it is this night belonging to Yahweh with vigils for all of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">ab</a></sup>throughout their generations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Exodus 12:48; Numbers 9:14; Exodus 12:11"> And Yahweh said to Moses and Aaron, “This is the statute of the Passover: No foreigner may eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Genesis 17:12–13; Genesis 17:23"> But any slave of a man, an acquisition by money, and you have circumcised him, then he may eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Leviticus 22:10; Ephesians 2:12"> A temporary resident and a hired worker may not eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Numbers 9:12; John 19:36; John 19:33"> It will be eaten in one house; you will not bring part of the meat out from the house to the outside; and you will not break a bone of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Exodus 12:6; Numbers 9:13; Exodus 12:3"> All of the community of Israel will prepare it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Numbers 9:14; Colossians 3:11; Galatians 3:28"> And when an alien dwells with you and he wants to prepare the Passover for Yahweh, every male belonging to him must be circumcised, and then he may come near to prepare it, and he will be as the native of the land, but any uncircumcised man may not eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Numbers 15:15–16; Numbers 15:29; Leviticus 24:22"> One law will be for the native and for the alien who is dwelling in your midst.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 7:24–25; Exodus 6:26; Exodus 7:4"> And all the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">ac</a></sup>did as Yahweh had commanded Moses and Aaron; so they did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Exodus 12:41; Exodus 6:26"> And it was on exactly this day Yahweh brought the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">ad</a></sup>out from the land of Egypt by their divisions.</span>
</div>
