## Preparation for Receiving the Covenant at Mount Sinai

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 16:1; Exodus 12:6; Leviticus 23:16–18"> In the third month after the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>went out from the land of Egypt, on this day they came to the Sinai desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; Exodus 17:1; Exodus 3:12"> They set out from Rephidim, and they came to the desert of Sinai, and they camped in the desert, and Israel camped there in front of the mountain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 3:4; Acts 7:38; Exodus 20:21"> And Moses went up to God, and Yahweh called to him from the mountain, saying, “Thus you will say to the house of Jacob and you will tell the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:11–12; Isaiah 63:9; Isaiah 40:31"> ‘You yourselves have seen what I did to Egypt and how I bore you on eagles’ wings and I brought you to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:2; Deuteronomy 7:6; Deuteronomy 26:18"> And now if you will carefully listen to my voice and keep my covenant, you will be a treasured possession for me out of all the peoples, ⸤for all the earth is mine⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “because all of the earth is for me”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; 1 Peter 2:9; 1 Peter 2:5"> but you, you will belong to me as a kingdom of priests and a holy nation.’ These are the words that you will speak to the ⸤Israelites⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 4:29–30; Exodus 3:16; 1 Corinthians 15:1"> And Moses came and called the elders of the people, and he placed before them all these words that Yahweh had commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 24:7; Exodus 24:3; Joshua 24:24"> And all the people together answered and said, “All that Yahweh has spoken we will do.” And Moses brought back the words of the people to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:36; Exodus 19:16; Exodus 24:15–16"> And Yahweh said to Moses, “Look, I am going to come to you in ⸤a thick cloud⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “the cloud of the cloud”">e</a></sup>in order that the people will hear when I speak with you and will also trust in you forever.” And Moses told the words of the people to Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; Genesis 35:2; Leviticus 11:44–45"> And Yahweh said to Moses, “Go to the people and consecrate them today and tomorrow. They must wash their clothes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 19:16; Exodus 34:5; Deuteronomy 33:2"> and they must be prepared for the third day, because on the third day, Yahweh will go down on Mount Sinai before the eyes of all the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 12:20–21; Joshua 3:4; Exodus 19:21"> And you must set limits for the people all around, saying, ‘Guard yourselves ⸤against⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “watch to/for you”">f</a></sup>going up to the mountain and touching its edge. Anyone touching the mountain will certainly be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:16; Hebrews 12:20; Exodus 19:16"> Not a hand will touch it, because he will certainly be stoned or certainly be shot; whether an animal or a man, he will not live.’ At the blowing of the ram’s horn they may go up to the mountain.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 19:10"> And Moses went down from the mountain to the people, and he consecrated the people, and they washed their clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:5; 1 Samuel 21:4–5; Exodus 19:11"> And he said to the people, “Be ready ⸤for the third day⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “for third of days”">g</a></sup>Do not go near to a woman.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 12:18–19; Psalm 18:11–14; Hebrews 12:21"> ⸤And⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “and it was”">h</a></sup>on the third day, when it was morning, there was thunder and lightning, and a heavy cloud over the mountain and a very loud ram’s horn sound, and all the people who were in the camp trembled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:10; Deuteronomy 5:5"> And Moses brought the people out from the camp to meet God, and they took their stand at the foot of the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 5:5; Exodus 3:2; Psalm 144:5"> And Mount Sinai was all wrapped in smoke because Yahweh went down on it in the fire, and its smoke went up like the smoke of a smelting furnace, and the whole mountain trembled greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalm 81:7; Exodus 19:16; Nehemiah 9:13"> And the sound of the ram’s horn became ⸤louder and louder⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “was going and strong very” (compare 2 Sam 3:1 )">i</a></sup>and Moses would speak, and God would answer him with a voice.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:13; Exodus 34:2; Exodus 19:11"> And Yahweh went down on Mount Sinai, to the top of the mountain, and Yahweh called Moses to the top of the mountain, and Moses went up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:19; Exodus 3:5; Hebrews 12:28–29"> And Yahweh said to Moses, “Go down, warn the people, lest they break through to Yahweh to see and many from them fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 24:5; Acts 5:10; 1 Chronicles 13:9–11"> And even the priests who come near Yahweh must consecrate themselves, lest Yahweh break out against them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 19:12; Joshua 3:4–5"> And Moses said to Yahweh, “The people are not able to go up to Mount Sinai, because you yourself warned us, saying, ‘Set limits around the mountain and consecrate it.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 4:16; Hebrews 12:18–25; Hebrews 12:29"> And Yahweh said to him, “Go, go down, and come up, you and Aaron with you and the priests, but the people must not break through to go up to Yahweh, lest he break out against them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And Moses went down to the people, and he told them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “and he said to them”">j</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
