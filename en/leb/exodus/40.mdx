## Completion of the Tabernacle

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 26:30; Exodus 13:4; Numbers 7:1"> “On the first day of the month, you will set up the tabernacle of the tent of assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:12; Numbers 4:5; Revelation 11:19"> And you will put there the ark of the testimony, and you will cover over the ark with the curtain.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “veil the ark with the curtain”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 24:8; Exodus 40:22–25; Exodus 37:10–24"> And you will bring the table, and you will arrange its setting, and you will bring the lampstand, and you will set up its lamps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; Hebrews 9:24; 1 John 2:1–2"> And you will put the gold altar for incense before the ark of the testimony, and you will set up the entrance screen for the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 40:29; 1 John 4:9–10; Hebrews 13:10"> And you will put the altar of the burnt offering before the entrance of the tabernacle of the tent of assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 13:1; 1 John 1:7; Psalm 26:6"> And you will put the basin between the tent of assembly and the altar, and you will put water ⸤in it⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “there”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 40:33; Ephesians 4:11–12; Matthew 16:18"> And you will set up the courtyard all around, and you will put up the screen of the gate of the courtyard.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 8:10; Numbers 7:1; Matthew 3:16"> “And you will take the anointing oil, and you will anoint the tabernacle and all that is in it, and you will consecrate all of its equipment,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “vessels” or “utensils” or “contents”">c</a></sup>and it will be holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 29:36–37; Luke 1:35; Isaiah 61:1"> And you will anoint the altar of the burnt offering and all of its equipment,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “vessels” or “utensils” or “contents”">d</a></sup>and you will consecrate the altar, and the altar will be a most holy thing.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “holiness of holinesses,” that is, “a holy thing among holy things”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And you will anoint the basin and its stand, and you will consecrate it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 8:1–13; Leviticus 9:1–24; Exodus 29:1–35"> And you will bring Aaron and his sons to the entrance of the tent of assembly, and you will wash them with the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 28:41; 1 John 2:20; 1 John 2:27"> And you will clothe Aaron with the holy garments, and you will anoint him, and you will consecrate him, and he will serve as a priest for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; Romans 8:30; 1 Corinthians 1:30"> And you will bring his sons, and you will clothe them with tunics.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 25:13; Hebrews 5:1–14; Hebrews 8:1–10"> And you will anoint them as you anointed their father, and they will serve as priests for me. And their anointing will be for them to be a lasting priesthood<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “a priesthood of eternity”">f</a></sup>throughout their generations.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:1; Exodus 40:17–32; Deuteronomy 12:32"> And Moses did according to all that Yahweh had commanded him; so he did.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 7:1; Exodus 40:1–2; Numbers 9:1"> ⸤In⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “and it was in”">g</a></sup>the first month of the second year, on the first of the month, the tabernacle was set up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:27–28; 1 Peter 1:5; Leviticus 26:11"> And Moses raised the tabernacle, and he placed its bases, and he set up its frames,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “boards” or “planks”">h</a></sup>and he placed its bars, and he raised its pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 26:1–14; Exodus 36:8–19"> And he spread the tent over the tabernacle; he placed the covering of the tent over it, above it, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 16:34; Deuteronomy 10:5; Hebrews 10:19–21"> And he took and he put the testimony into the ark, and he placed the poles on the ark, and he put the atonement cover on the ark, above it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 26:33; Exodus 40:3; Exodus 35:12"> And he brought the ark into the tabernacle, and he set up the curtain of the screening, and he shielded the ark of the testimony, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 26:35; John 6:53–57; Ephesians 3:8"> And he put the table in the tent of assembly on the north side of the tabernacle outside the curtain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 25:30; Exodus 40:4; Hebrews 9:2"> And he arranged on it an arrangement of bread before Yahweh, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 26:35; Revelation 2:5; John 1:1"> And he placed the lampstand in the tent of assembly opposite the table on the south side of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 40:4; Exodus 25:37; Revelation 4:5"> And he set up the lamps before Yahweh, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 40:5; Hebrews 7:25; John 11:42"> And he placed the gold altar in the tent of assembly before the curtain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 30:7"> And he turned fragrant incense into smoke on it, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 40:5; Hebrews 10:19–20; Ephesians 2:18"> And he set up the entrance screen for the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 40:6; Hebrews 13:5–6; Hebrews 13:10"> And the altar of burnt offering he placed at the entrance of the tabernacle of the tent of assembly, and he offered on it the burnt offering and the grain offering, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 40:7; Exodus 38:8; Ezekiel 36:25"> And he placed the basin between the tent of assembly and the altar, and he put there water for washing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 30:19–20; Psalm 26:6; John 13:10"> And Moses and Aaron and his sons washed their hands and their feet from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalm 73:19; Exodus 30:19–20; Exodus 40:19"> At their going into the tent of assembly and at their approaching the altar, they washed, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 40:8; Exodus 27:9–16; John 17:4"> And he set up the courtyard all around the tabernacle and the altar, and he put up the screen of the gate of the courtyard, and Moses completed the work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Revelation 15:8; Numbers 9:15–23; 2 Chronicles 7:2"> And the cloud covered the tent of assembly, and the glory of Yahweh filled the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 8:11; Leviticus 16:2; 2 Chronicles 7:2"> And Moses was unable to go into the tent of assembly because the cloud settled on it and the glory of Yahweh filled the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:19; Numbers 9:17; Exodus 13:21–22"> And when the cloud was lifted from the tabernacle, the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">i</a></sup>set out on all their journeys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 9:19–22; Psalm 31:15"> But if the cloud was not lifted, they did not set out until the day of its being lifted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Psalm 78:14; Numbers 9:15; Exodus 13:21"> For the cloud of Yahweh was on the tabernacle by day, and fire was on it by night before the eyes of all the house of Israel throughout all their journeys.</span>
</div>
