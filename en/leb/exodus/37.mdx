<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 25:10–20; Exodus 26:33; Numbers 10:33–36"> And Bezalel made the ark of acacia wood, two and a half cubits its length and a cubit and a half its width and a cubit and a half its height.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 30:3"> And he overlaid it with pure gold inside and outside, and he made for it a gold molding all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And he cast for it four gold rings on its four feet; and two rings were on its one side, and two rings were on its second side.</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:2; Psalm 104:4; Psalm 80:1"> And he made two cherubim of gold; he made them of hammered work at the two ends of the atonement cover.</span>

## Making the Table and the Lampstand

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 1:14; Ezekiel 40:39–42; Malachi 1:12"> And he made the table of acacia wood, two cubits its length and a cubit its width and a cubit and a half its height.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And he overlaid it with pure gold, and he made for it a gold molding all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And he made for it a handbreadth rim all around, and he made a gold molding for its rim all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And he cast for it four gold rings, and he put the rings on the four corners where its four ⸤legs⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “feet”">d</a></sup>were.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The rings were near the rim as ⸤holders⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “houses”">e</a></sup>for the poles to carry the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And he made the poles of acacia wood, and he overlaid them with gold to carry the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 25:29; 1 Kings 7:50; 2 Kings 12:13"> And he made the vessels that were on the table—its plates and its ladles,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “bowls”">f</a></sup>and its bowls and its pitchers with which libations were poured—of pure gold.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–39; Hebrews 9:2; 2 Chronicles 13:11"> And he made the lampstand of pure gold; he made the lampstand of hammered work—its base and its branch, its cups, its buds, and its blossoms were ⸤all part of it⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “from it”">g</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Three almond-flower cups were on the one branch with a bud and a blossom, and three almond-flower cups were on the one branch with a bud and a blossom—likewise for the six branches going out from the lampstand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:5; Jeremiah 1:11; Exodus 25:33"> And on the lampstand were four almond-flower cups, with its<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Refers to the lampstand">h</a></sup>buds and its blossoms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 25:35"> And a bud was under the two branches that came from it, and a bud under the two branches from it, and a bud under the two branches from it, likewise for the six branches coming out from the lampstand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:5; Exodus 25:31; Psalm 51:17"> Their buds and their branches were from it, all of it one piece of pure gold, hammered work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 1:12; Exodus 25:37; Revelation 1:20–1"> And he made its seven lamps and its snuffers and its fire pans of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> He made it from a talent of pure gold and all its pieces of equipment.</span>
