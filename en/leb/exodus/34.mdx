## Yahweh’s Description of Himself

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 32:19; Exodus 31:18; Psalm 119:89"> And Yahweh said to Moses, “Cut for yourself two stone tablets like the first ones, and I will write on the tablets the words that were on the first tablets, which you broke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 19:20; Deuteronomy 9:25; Exodus 19:11"> And be ready for the morning, and go up in the morning to Mount Sinai and present yourself to me there on the top of the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 19:12–13; Exodus 19:21; 1 Timothy 2:5"> And no one will go up with you, and neither let anyone be seen on all the mountain, nor let the sheep and goats and the cattle graze ⸤opposite⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to the front of”">a</a></sup>that mountain.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And Moses cut two stone tablets like the first ones, and he started early in the morning, and he went up to Mount Sinai, as Yahweh had commanded him, and he took in his hand the two stone tablets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 33:19; Numbers 11:25; Luke 9:34–35"> And Yahweh descended in the cloud, and he stood with him there, and he proclaimed the name of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 86:15; Joel 2:13; Psalm 103:8–13"> And Yahweh passed over before him, and he proclaimed, “Yahweh, Yahweh, God, who is compassionate and gracious, ⸤slow to anger⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “long of nose”">b</a></sup>and abounding with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “rich in” or “great of”">c</a></sup>loyal love and faithfulness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 20:5–6; Psalm 103:3; Deuteronomy 5:9–10"> keeping loyal love to the thousands, forgiving iniquity and transgression and sin, and he does not leave utterly unpunished, punishing the guilt of fathers on sons and on sons of sons on third and fourth generations.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 4:31; 2 Chronicles 20:18; Genesis 17:3"> And Moses hurried and knelt down to the earth and worshiped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 33:12; Deuteronomy 32:9; Psalm 94:14"> And he said, “Please, if I have found favor in your eyes, Lord, let my Lord, please, go among us—indeed it is a stiff-necked people—and forgive our iniquity and our sin and ⸤take us as your possession⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “take possession of us,” “make us your inheritance”">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Covenant Stipulations

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:21; Psalm 77:14; Isaiah 64:3"> And he said, “Look, I am about to make a covenant. In front of all your people I will do wonders that have not been created on all the earth and among all the nations, and all the people among whom you are will see Yahweh’s work, because what I am about to do with you will be awesome.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 33:2; Exodus 3:17; Exodus 3:8"> “Keep for yourself what I myself have commanded you today. Look, I am about to drive from before you the Amorites and the Canaanites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew “Canaanite”">e</a></sup>and the Hittites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “Hittite”">f</a></sup>and the Perizzites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew “Perizzite”">g</a></sup>and the Hivites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “Hivite”">h</a></sup>and the Jebusites.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “Jebusite”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 23:32–33; Joshua 23:12–13; Deuteronomy 7:2"> Be careful for yourself, lest you make a covenant with the inhabitants of the land to which you are going, lest it be a snare among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 18:4; Exodus 23:24; Deuteronomy 7:25–26"> Rather, you will tear down their altars, and you will break their stone pillars, and you will cut off their Asherah poles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:24; Deuteronomy 32:21; 1 Corinthians 10:22"> For you will not bow in worship to another god, for ‘Yahweh Is Jealous’ is his name, he is a jealous God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 2:17; Psalm 106:28; Numbers 25:2"> lest you make a covenant with the inhabitants of the land, and they prostitute themselves after their gods, and they sacrifice to their gods, and they invite you, and you eat their sacrifice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:3–4; 1 Kings 11:2–4; Ezra 9:2"> and you take from their daughters for your sons, and their daughters prostitute themselves after their gods, and they cause your sons to prostitute themselves after their gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 19:4; Exodus 32:8; Isaiah 46:6–7"> You will not make gods of cast metal for yourself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 13:4; Leviticus 23:6; Exodus 13:6–7"> “You will keep the Feast of Unleavened Bread. Seven days you will eat unleavened bread, which I commanded you, at the appointed time of the month of Abib, for in the month of Abib you came out from Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Exodus 22:29; Exodus 13:12"> Every first offspring of a womb is for me—all of your male livestock, the first offspring of cattle and small livestock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 13:13; Exodus 23:15; Deuteronomy 16:16"> But the first offspring of a donkey you will redeem with small livestock, and if you will not redeem it, you will break its neck. Every firstborn of your sons you will redeem, and you will not appear before me empty-handed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 23:12; Deuteronomy 21:4; Luke 13:14"> Six days you will work, and on the seventh day you will rest; in the time of plowing and in the time of harvest you will rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 23:16; Deuteronomy 16:10–15; John 7:2"> And ⸤you yourself⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “for yourself” or “as for you, you will observe”">j</a></sup>will observe the Feast of Weeks—the firstfruits of the wheat harvest—and the Feast of Harvest Gathering at the turn of the year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 23:14; Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 23:17"> Three times in the year all your males will appear before the Lord, Yahweh, the God of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalm 78:55; Exodus 33:2; Proverbs 16:7"> because I will evict nations before you, and I will enlarge your territory, and no one will covet your land when you go up to appear before Yahweh your God three times in the year.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 23:18; Exodus 12:10; 1 Corinthians 5:7–8"> “You will not slaughter the blood of my sacrifice on food with yeast, and the sacrifice of the Feast of the Passover will not stay overnight to the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 23:19; Deuteronomy 14:21; Deuteronomy 26:2"> The beginning of the firstfruits of your land you will bring to the house of Yahweh your God. You will not boil a young goat<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “kid”">k</a></sup>in its mother’s milk.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 24:4; Deuteronomy 31:9; Deuteronomy 4:13"> And Yahweh said to Moses, “Write for yourself these words, because ⸤according to⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “on the mouth of”">l</a></sup>these words I have made a covenant with you and with Israel.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Shining Face of Moses

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 34:1; Exodus 24:18; Deuteronomy 9:18"> And he was there with Yahweh forty days and forty nights. He ate no food and drank no water. And he wrote on the tablets the words of the covenant, the ten words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 17:2; Luke 9:29; Exodus 32:15"> ⸤And⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “And it was”">m</a></sup>when Moses came down from Mount Sinai, the two tablets of the testimony were in the hand of Moses at his coming down from the mountain; and Moses did not know that the skin of his face shone because of his speaking with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Mark 9:3; Luke 5:8; Numbers 12:8"> And Aaron and all the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">n</a></sup>saw Moses, and, ⸤to their amazement⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “look” or “behold”">o</a></sup>the skin of his face shone, and they were afraid of coming near to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 3:15; Exodus 24:1–3; Genesis 45:3"> And Moses called to them, and Aaron and all the leaders of the community returned to him, and Moses spoke to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 22:14; Exodus 24:3; Matthew 28:20"> And afterward all the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">p</a></sup>came near, and he commanded them all that Yahweh had spoken with him on Mount Sinai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4–6; 2 Corinthians 3:13–18; Romans 10:4"> And Moses finished speaking with them, and he put a veil on his face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:16; Hebrews 10:19–22; Hebrews 4:16"> And when Moses came before Yahweh to speak with him, he would remove the veil until he went out, and he would go out and would speak to the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">q</a></sup>what he had been commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Exodus 34:29–30; John 5:35; Daniel 12:3"> And the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">r</a></sup>would see the face of Moses, that the skin of the face of Moses shone, and Moses would put back the veil on his face until his coming to speak with him.</span>
</div>
