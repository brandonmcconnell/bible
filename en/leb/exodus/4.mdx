## Yahweh Provides Signs and Help for Speaking

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 3:18; Exodus 2:14; Jeremiah 1:6"> And Moses answered, and he said, “And if they do not believe me and they do not listen to my voice, but they say, ‘Yahweh did not appear to you?’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 4:17; Exodus 4:20; Isaiah 11:4"> And Yahweh said to him, “What is this in your hand?” And he said, “A staff.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 7:10–15; Amos 5:19; Exodus 4:17"> And he said, “Throw it onto the ground.” And he threw it onto the ground, and it became a snake, and Moses fled from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 28:3–6; Genesis 22:1–2; Psalm 91:13"> And Yahweh said to Moses, “Reach out your hand and grasp it by its tail”—” (And he reached out his hand and grabbed it, and it became a staff in his palm.)—</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 19:9; Genesis 48:3; Exodus 3:15"> “so that they may believe that Yahweh, the God of their ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “fathers”">a</a></sup>appeared to you, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 5:27; Numbers 12:10"> And Yahweh said to him again, “Put your hand into the fold of your garment.” And he put his hand into the fold of his garment, and he took it out, and, ⸤to his surprise⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “look” or “behold”">b</a></sup>his hand was afflicted with a skin disease, like snow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 5:14; Deuteronomy 32:39; Matthew 8:3"> And he said, “Return your hand to the fold of your garment.” And he returned his hand to the fold of his garment, and he took it out from the fold of his garment, and, ⸤to his surprise⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “look” or “behold”">c</a></sup>it was restored like the rest of his body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 28:10; Deuteronomy 32:39; Exodus 4:30–31"> “⸤And⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “and he/it will be”">d</a></sup>if they do not believe you and they will not listen to the voice of the former sign, then they will believe the voice of the latter sign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 7:19–25; Exodus 1:22; Matthew 7:2"> ⸤And⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “and he/it will be”">e</a></sup>if they also do not believe the second of these signs and they will not listen to your voice, then you must take water from the Nile and pour it onto the dry ground, and the water that you take from the Nile will become blood on the dry ground.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:6; Exodus 6:12; Acts 7:22"> And Moses said to Yahweh, “Please, Lord, I am not a man of words, ⸤neither recently nor in the past nor since your speaking⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “also from yesterday also from the day before also from then your speaking”">f</a></sup>to your servant, because I am heavy of mouth and of tongue.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Amos 3:6; Psalm 146:8; Ezekiel 3:26–27"> And Yahweh said to him, “Who gave a mouth to humankind, or who makes mute or deaf or sighted or blind? Is it not I, Yahweh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:9; Luke 12:11–12; Matthew 10:19–20"> So then go, and I myself will be with your mouth, and I will teach you what you must speak.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jonah 1:3; Exodus 4:1; Matthew 13:41"> And he said, “Please, Lord, do send ⸤anyone else whom you wish to send⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “by the hand of someone you will send”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 14:13–15; Exodus 4:27; 1 Chronicles 21:7"> ⸤And Yahweh was angry with⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “and the nose of Yahweh was hot against”">h</a></sup>Moses and said, “Is there not Aaron your brother the Levite? I know that he certainly can speak, and also there he is coming out to meet you, and when he sees you, he will rejoice in his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 51:16; Deuteronomy 18:18; Luke 21:15"> And you will speak to him, and you will put words in his mouth, and I myself will be with your mouth and with his mouth, and I will teach you what you must do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 7:1–2; Psalm 82:6; John 10:34–35"> And he will speak for you to the people, ⸤and then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “and he/it will be”">i</a></sup>he will be to you as a mouth, and you will be to him as a god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 4:2; 1 Corinthians 1:27; Exodus 7:9–20"> And you must take this staff in your hand, with which you will do the signs.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Moses Leaves Midian and Returns to Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; 1 Samuel 1:17; 1 Timothy 6:1"> And Moses went, and he returned to Jethro his father-in-law, and he said to him, “Please let me go, and let me return to my brothers who are in Egypt, and let me see whether they are yet alive. And Jethro said to Moses, “Go in peace.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 2:20; Exodus 2:23; Exodus 2:15"> And Yahweh said to Moses in Midian, “Go, return to Egypt because all the men have died who were seeking your life.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 17:9; Numbers 20:8–9; Exodus 18:3–4"> And Moses took his wife and his sons and had them ride on the donkey, and he returned to the land of Egypt, and Moses took the staff of God in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 9:18; Isaiah 63:17; Exodus 14:8"> And Yahweh said to Moses, “When you go to return to Egypt, see all of the wonders that I have put in your hand, and do them before Pharaoh, and I myself will harden his heart, and he will not release the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 11:1; Romans 9:4; Jeremiah 31:9"> And you must say to Pharaoh, ‘Thus says Yahweh, “Israel is my son, my firstborn.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 11:5; Exodus 12:29; Psalm 135:8"> And I said to you, “Release my son and let him serve me,” but you refused to release him. Look, I am about to kill your son, your firstborn.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 17:14; Numbers 22:22–23; Hosea 13:8"> ⸤And⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “and he/it was”">j</a></sup>on the way, at the place of overnight lodging, Yahweh encountered him and sought to kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 5:2–3; Genesis 17:14; 2 Samuel 16:7"> But Zipporah took a flint knife, and she cut off the foreskin of her son, and she touched his feet, and she said, “Yes, you are a bridegroom of blood to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And he left him alone. At that time she said, “A bridegroom of blood,” because of the circumcision.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Hebrew “circumcisions”">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; Exodus 4:14–16; 1 Kings 19:8"> And Yahweh said to Aaron, “Go to the desert to meet Moses.” And he went and encountered him at the mountain of God and kissed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 4:8–9; Exodus 4:15–16; Matthew 21:29"> And Moses told Aaron all the words of Yahweh, who had sent him—and all the signs that he had commanded him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 3:16; Exodus 24:11; Exodus 24:1"> And Moses and Aaron went, and they gathered all of the elders of the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 4:16"> And Aaron spoke all the words that Yahweh had spoken to Moses, and he did the signs before the eyes of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 3:18; Exodus 2:25; Psalm 106:12–13"> And the people believed when they heard that Yahweh had attended to the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">m</a></sup>and that he had seen their misery, and they knelt down and they worshiped.</span>
</div>
</div>
