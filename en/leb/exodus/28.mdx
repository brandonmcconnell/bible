## Instructions for Making Garments for Priests

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 18:7; Hebrews 5:1–5; Numbers 17:2–9"> “And bring near to you Aaron, your brother, and his sons with him from the midst of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>to serve as priests for me—Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar, the sons of Aaron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 8:30; Exodus 31:10; Revelation 19:8"> And you will make holy garments<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “garments of holiness”">b</a></sup>for Aaron, your brother, for glory and for splendor.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “beauty”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ephesians 1:17; 1 Corinthians 12:7–11; Isaiah 11:2"> And you will speak to all the skilled of heart, whom I have given ⸤a gift of skill⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “a spirit of wisdom”">d</a></sup>and they will make the garments of Aaron to consecrate him for his serving as my priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 6:14; Revelation 9:17; Isaiah 59:17"> And these are the garments that they will make: A breast piece and an ephod and a robe and a tunic of specially woven fabric,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Precisely what kind of fabric this was is debated">e</a></sup>a turban and a sash. And they will make holy garments<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “garments of holiness”">f</a></sup>for Aaron your brother, and for his sons to serve as priests for me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 25:3–4; Exodus 39:2–3"> “And they will take the gold and the blue and the purple and the crimson yarns and the fine linen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 39:2–7; Exodus 26:1"> and they will make the ephod of gold, blue and purple, and crimson yarns, and finely twisted linen, the work of a skilled craftsman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 39:4"> It will have two joining shoulder pieces at its two edges, so that it can be fastened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 28:27–28; Leviticus 8:7; Exodus 29:5"> And the waistband of his ephod, which is on it, will be of like work to it—gold, blue, and purple and crimson yarns and finely twisted linen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13; 2 Chronicles 2:7; Isaiah 49:16"> “And you will take two onyx stones and engrave on them the names of the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 1:1–4; Genesis 43:33"> with six of their names on the one stone and the remaining six on the second, according to their genealogies.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="In other words, “according to their birth order”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:19; Revelation 7:2; Jeremiah 22:24"> As the work of a skilled stone craftsman, with seal engravings you will engrave on the two stones the names of the ⸤Israelites⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">i</a></sup>you will make them mounted in gold filigree settings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 28:29; Joshua 4:7; Exodus 39:6–7"> And you will set the two stones on the ephod’s shoulder pieces as stones of remembrance for the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">j</a></sup>and Aaron will bear their names before Yahweh on his two shoulder pieces for remembrance.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> “And you will make gold filigree settings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 39:15; Exodus 39:17–18; 1 Kings 7:17"> And you will make two braided chains of pure gold ornamental cord work, and you will put the chains of the ornamental cords on the filigree settings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 28:30; Exodus 26:1; Exodus 28:4"> And you will make a breast piece of judgment, a work of a skilled craftsman; you will make it like the work of the ephod; you will make it of gold, blue and purple and crimson yarns, and finely twisted linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> It will be squared, doubled, a span its length and a span its width.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13; Exodus 39:10–21; Proverbs 8:11"> And you will fill it with stone mounting, four rows of stone, a row of carnelian, topaz, and emerald is the first row;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:16; Job 28:6; Song of Solomon 5:14"> and the second row is a malachite, a sapphire, and a moonstone;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 54:12; Exodus 39:12"> and the third row is a jacinth, an agate, and an amethyst;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:16; Revelation 4:3; Daniel 10:6"> and the fourth row is a turquoise and an onyx and a jasper. Their settings will be woven with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 21:12; Revelation 7:4–8; 1 Kings 18:31"> The stones will be according to the names of the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">k</a></sup>twelve according to their names, with seal engravings, each according to its name they will be for the twelve tribes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> “And you will make on the breast piece braided chains, a work of pure gold ornamental cord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 25:11–15"> And you will make on the breast piece two gold rings, and you will put the two rings on the two edges of the breast piece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And you will put the two gold ornamental cords on the two rings on the edges of the breast piece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 39:15; Exodus 39:4; Exodus 28:14"> And you will put the two ends of the two ornamental cords on the two filigree settings, and you will put them on the shoulder pieces of the ephod at the front of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And you will make two gold rings, and you will place them on the two ends of the breast piece, on its edge that is ⸤on the other side⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “beyond”">l</a></sup>of the ephod, ⸤to the inside⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “houseward,” “to house,” meaning “inward”">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And you will make two rings and put them on the two shoulder pieces of the ephod below at its front near its seam above the waistband of the ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 15:38; Exodus 28:31; Exodus 39:30–31"> And they will tie the breast piece by its rings to the rings of the ephod with a blue cord to be on the waistband of the ephod, and the breast piece will not come loose from the ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 28:12; Exodus 28:30; Jeremiah 30:21"> And Aaron will bear the names of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">n</a></sup>in the breast piece of judgment on his heart, when he comes to the sanctuary, for a remembrance before Yahweh continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:65; Ezra 2:63; Numbers 27:21"> And you will put the Urim and the Thummim on the breast piece of judgment, and they will be on the heart of Aaron when he comes before Yahweh, and Aaron will bear the judgment of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">o</a></sup>on his heart before Yahweh continually.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 8:7; Exodus 28:4; Exodus 39:22–26"> “And you will make the robe of the ephod totally of blue yarn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:16; John 19:23–24; Exodus 39:28"> And the opening for his head will be in the middle of it; its opening will have an edge all around, the work of a weaver; it will be like the opening of a sturdy garment for it, so that it will not be torn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 39:24–26; Zechariah 14:20; 1 Kings 7:18"> And you will make on its hem pomegranates of blue and purple and crimson yarns on its hem all around and bells of gold in the midst of them all around,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Song of Solomon 8:2; John 15:4–8; Colossians 1:10"> a gold bell and a pomegranate, a gold bell and a pomegranate, on the hem of the robe all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Hebrews 9:12; Leviticus 16:2"> And it will be on Aaron for serving, and its sound will be heard at his coming into the sanctuary before Yahweh and at his going out, so that he will not die.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Zechariah 14:20; Leviticus 8:9; Exodus 39:30"> “And you will make a pure gold rosette,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “medallion”">p</a></sup>and you will engrave on it with seal engravings: “A holy object for Yahweh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 29:6; Exodus 28:4; Zechariah 3:5"> And you will place it on a blue cord, and it will be on the turban, at the front of the turban it will be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Numbers 18:1; Leviticus 10:17"> And it will be on the forehead of Aaron, and Aaron will bear the guilt of the holy objects that the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">q</a></sup>will consecrate for all their holy gifts, and it will be on his forehead continually for acceptance for them before Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalm 45:14"> “And you will weave the tunic of fine linen, and you will make a turban of fine linen, and you will make a sash, the work of an embroiderer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Exodus 39:41; Exodus 28:4; Exodus 28:2"> And for the sons of Aaron you will make tunics, and you will make for them sashes and headdresses; you will make them for glory and for splendor.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “beauty”">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 29:7; Leviticus 8:1–36; Exodus 40:15"> And you will clothe them—Aaron, your brother, and his sons with him—and you will anoint them and ⸤ordain them⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “fill their hand”">s</a></sup>and consecrate them, and they will serve as priests for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:18; Leviticus 6:10; Leviticus 16:4"> And make for them undergarments of linen to cover naked flesh; they will be from loins to thigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Exodus 27:21; Exodus 20:26; Leviticus 5:17"> And they will be on Aaron and on his sons when they come to the tent of assembly or when they approach the altar to serve in the sanctuary, so that they will not bear guilt and die. It is a lasting statute for him and for his offspring after him.</span>
</div>
</div>
