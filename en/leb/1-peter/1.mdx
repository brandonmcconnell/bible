## Greeting

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="James 1:1; Acts 2:5–11; Hebrews 11:13"> Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ, to the chosen<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “elect”">a</a></sup>who are residing temporarily in the dispersion in Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:13; Ephesians 1:4–5; Hebrews 12:24"> according to the foreknowledge of God the Father, by the sanctification of the Spirit, for obedience and for sprinkling with the blood of Jesus Christ. May grace and peace be multiplied to you.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A New Birth to a Living Hope

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 1:23; 1 John 5:4; Romans 15:13"> Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who according to his great mercy has caused us to be born again to a living hope through the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 21:27; Hebrews 9:15; 1 Peter 5:4"> into an inheritance imperishable and undefiled and unfading, reserved in heaven for you</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 1:6; Ephesians 2:8; 2 Thessalonians 2:13–14"> who are being protected by the power of God through faith for a salvation ready to be revealed in the last time,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 1:2; 1 Peter 5:10; Romans 12:12"> in which you rejoice greatly, although now for a short time, if necessary,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “if it is necessary”">b</a></sup>you are distressed by various trials,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 23:10; Isaiah 48:10; 1 Peter 4:12"> so that the genuineness of your faith, more valuable than gold that is passing away, but is tested by fire, may be found to result in praise and glory and honor at the revelation of Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 20:29; 2 Corinthians 5:7; Romans 15:13"> whom, although you have not seen, you love; in whom now you believe, although you do not see him, and you rejoice greatly with joy inexpressible and full of glory,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 6:22; James 1:21; Hebrews 11:13"> obtaining the goal of your faith, the salvation of your souls.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 13:17; Luke 10:24; Luke 24:25–27"> Concerning this<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “which”">c</a></sup>salvation, the prophets who prophesied about the grace meant for you sought and made careful inquiry,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 52:13–14; 2 Peter 1:21; Luke 24:25–27"> investigating for what person or which time the Spirit of Christ in them was indicating when he testified beforehand to the sufferings with reference to Christ and the glories after these things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 15:26; 1 Corinthians 2:10; Ephesians 3:10"> to whom it was revealed that they were serving not themselves but you with reference to the same things which now have been announced to you through those who proclaimed the gospel to you by the Holy Spirit sent from heaven, ⸤things into which⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “into which things ”">d</a></sup>angels desire to look.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Be Holy in All Your Conduct

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Luke 12:35; 1 Peter 4:7"> Therefore, ⸤when you have prepared your minds for action⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “having girded up the loins of your mind”">e</a></sup>by being self-controlled, put your hope completely in the grace that will be brought to you at the revelation of Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 12:2; Titus 3:3–5; 1 Peter 4:2–3"> As obedient children, do not be conformed to the former desires you used to conform to in your ignorance,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:48; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Hebrews 12:14"> but as the one who called you is holy, you yourselves be holy in all your conduct,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44; Leviticus 19:2; Leviticus 20:7"> for it is written, “You will be holy, because I am holy.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="A quotation from Lev 19:2">f</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Some manuscripts explicitly state “I am holy”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 10:34–35; 2 Chronicles 19:7; Matthew 6:9"> And if you call on him as Father who judges impartially according to each one’s work, conduct yourselves with fear during the time of your temporary residence,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:20; Titus 2:14; Psalm 49:7–8"> because you know that you were redeemed from your futile way of life inherited from your ancestors not with perishable things like silver or gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 1:7; 1 Peter 2:22–24; John 1:29"> but with the precious blood of Christ, like that of an unblemished and spotless lamb</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 1:4; 2 Timothy 1:9–10; Hebrews 9:26"> who was foreknown before the foundation of the world, but has been revealed in these last times for you</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Hebrews 2:9; John 12:44"> who through him are believing in God, who raised him from the dead and gave him glory, so that your faith and hope are in God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:5; Hebrews 13:1; Romans 12:9–10"> Having purified your souls by your obedience to the truth for sincere brotherly love, love one another fervently from the heart,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “from a pure heart”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Peter 1:3; Hebrews 4:12; John 1:13"> because you have been born again, not from perishable seed but imperishable, through the living and enduring word of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 40:6–8; Psalm 103:15; James 4:14"> For<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“all flesh is like grass,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all its glory like the flower of the grass.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The grass withers and the flower falls off,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 40:8; Matthew 5:18; Psalm 119:89"> <span className="pl pl-2">but the word of the Lord endures ⸤forever⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “for the age”">i</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 40:6 , 8">j</a></sup><br />And this is the word that has been proclaimed to you.</span>
</div>
</div>
