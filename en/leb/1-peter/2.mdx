## Chosen as Living Stones

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ephesians 4:31; James 1:21; James 4:11"> Therefore, ridding yourselves of all malice and all deceit and hypocrisy and envy and all slander,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 5:12–13; Ephesians 4:15; 2 Peter 3:18"> like newborn infants long for the unadulterated spiritual milk, so that by it you may grow up to salvation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 34:8; Hebrews 6:5–6; Psalm 63:5"> if you have tasted that the Lord is kind,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 34:8">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 118:22–23; Acts 4:11–12; 1 Peter 2:7"> to whom you are drawing near, a living stone rejected by men but chosen and precious in the sight of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 2:20–22; 1 Peter 2:9; 1 Corinthians 3:9"> And you yourselves, as living stones, are being built up as a spiritual house for a holy priesthood, to offer up spiritual sacrifices acceptable to God through Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 28:16; Romans 9:32–33; 1 Peter 2:4"> For it stands in scripture,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Behold, I am laying in Zion a stone, a chosen and precious cornerstone,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the one who believes in him will never be put to shame.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 28:16">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 118:22–23; Mark 12:10–11; Acts 4:11–12"> Therefore the honor is for you who believe, but for those who refuse to believe,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The stone that the builders rejected,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">this one has become ⸤the cornerstone⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “into the head of the corner”">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">,”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14; 1 Corinthians 1:23; Romans 9:32–33"> and<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“A stone of stumbling and a rock of offense,”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 8:14">d</a></sup><br />who stumble because they disobey the word to which also they were consigned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 19:5–6; Deuteronomy 7:6; Revelation 1:6"> But you are a chosen race,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “people,” or “generation,” or “nation”">e</a></sup>a royal priesthood, a holy nation, a people for God’s possession, so that you may proclaim the virtues of the one who called you out of darkness into his marvelous light,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 2:23; Hebrews 4:16; Hosea 1:9–10"> who once were not a people, but now are the people of God, the ones who were not shown mercy, but now are shown mercy.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="This verse contains quotations from Hos 1:6 , 9 ; 2:23">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; Galatians 5:24; 1 Peter 4:2"> Dear friends, I urge you as foreigners and temporary residents to abstain from fleshly desires which wage war against your soul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:16; Matthew 5:16; Titus 2:7–8"> maintaining your good conduct among the Gentiles, so that in the things in which they slander you as evildoers, by seeing your good deeds they may glorify God on the day of visitation.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Submission to Established Authority

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Titus 3:1; Romans 13:1–7; 1 Timothy 2:1–2"> Subject yourselves to every human authority for the sake of the Lord, whether to a king<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “to the emperor”">g</a></sup>as having supreme authority,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 13:3–4"> or to governors as those sent out by him for the punishment of those who do evil and the praise of those who do good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:17; Titus 2:8; Matthew 7:26"> For the will of God is as follows: by doing good to silence the ignorance of foolish people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 5:13; Romans 6:22; Romans 6:18"> Live as free persons, and not using your freedom as a covering for evil, but as slaves of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 24:21; Romans 12:10; Romans 13:7"> Honor all people, love the community of believers, fear God, honor the king.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “the emperor”">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Titus 2:9–10; Ephesians 6:5–7; Colossians 3:22–25"> Domestic slaves, be subject to your masters with all respect, not only to those who are good and gentle, but also to those who are unjust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 5:10–12; 1 Peter 3:14–17; 1 Peter 2:20"> For this finds favor, if because of consciousness of God someone endures sorrows while suffering unjustly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 2:19; 1 Peter 3:17; Matthew 5:10–12"> For what credit is it if, when you sin and are beaten for it, you endure? But if you endure when you do good and suffer for it, this finds favor with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 2:6; Matthew 16:24; John 13:15"> For to this you were called, because Christ also suffered for you, leaving you an example, so that you should follow in his footsteps,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:9; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Hebrews 4:15"> who did not commit sin, nor was deceit found in his mouth,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="This verse contains quotations from Isa 53:9">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; 1 Peter 4:19; Hebrews 12:3"> who when he was reviled, did not revile in return; when suffering, he did not threaten, but entrusted himself to the one who judges justly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4–6; Matthew 8:17; Romans 6:11"> who himself bore our sins in his body on the tree, so that we may die to sins and live to righteousness, by whose wounds you were healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 53:6; Ezekiel 34:6; Luke 15:4–6"> For you were going astray like sheep, but you have turned back now to the shepherd and guardian of your souls.</span>
</div>
</div>
