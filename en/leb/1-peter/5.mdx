## Leadership and Humility

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 3:3–4; Luke 24:48; Romans 8:17–18"> Therefore I, your fellow elder and a witness of the sufferings of Christ, and also a sharer of the glory that is going to be revealed, exhort the elders among you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:7; 1 Timothy 3:8; Titus 3:1"> shepherd the flock of God among you, exercising oversight not by compulsion but willingly, in accordance with God, and not greedily but eagerly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:9; Philippians 3:17; Ezekiel 34:4"> and not as lording it over ⸤those under your care⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “the portion”">a</a></sup>, but being examples for the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 1:12; 1 Corinthians 9:25; 1 John 3:2"> And when the chief Shepherd appears, you will receive the unfading crown of glory.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 4:6; Proverbs 3:34; Ephesians 5:21"> In the same way, younger men, be subject to the elders, and all of you clothe yourselves with humility toward one another, because God opposes the proud, but gives grace to the humble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 4:10; Luke 14:11; Proverbs 29:23"> Humble yourselves therefore under the mighty hand of God, so that he may exalt you at the right time,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 55:22; Philippians 4:6; Psalm 37:5"> casting all your cares on him, because ⸤he cares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “it is a care to him”">b</a></sup>for you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 6:11; James 4:7; Ephesians 4:27"> Be sober; be on the alert. Your adversary the devil walks around like a roaring lion, looking for someone to devour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 4:7; Acts 14:22; Ephesians 6:11–13"> ⸤Resist him⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “to whom resist”">c</a></sup>, steadfast in your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “the”; the Greek article is used here as a possessive pronoun">d</a></sup>faith, because you know the same kinds of sufferings are being accomplished by your community of believers in the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:17; 2 Thessalonians 3:3; 1 Peter 1:6–7"> And the God of all grace, who called you to his eternal glory in Christ,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “in Christ Jesus”">e</a></sup>after you have suffered for a short time, will himself restore, confirm, strengthen, and establish you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 4:11; Romans 11:36; Revelation 1:6"> To him be the power ⸤forever and ever⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “to the ages of the ages”">f</a></sup>.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “forever” (literally “to the ages”)">g</a></sup>Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Conclusion and Final Greetings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 13:22; Acts 11:23; 1 Corinthians 15:1"> Through Silvanus, the faithful brother (as I consider him), I have written to you ⸤briefly⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “by a few”">h</a></sup>to encourage you and to attest that this is the true grace of God. Stand firm in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 12:12; Acts 12:25; Psalm 87:4"> She who is in Babylon, chosen the same as you, greets you, and so does my son Mark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 16:16; Ephesians 6:23; Romans 8:1"> Greet one another with a loving kiss. Peace to all of you who are in Christ.</span>
</div>
</div>
