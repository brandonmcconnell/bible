## Wives and Husbands

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 3:18; Ephesians 5:33; Ephesians 5:22–24"> In the same way, wives, be subject to your own husbands, so that even if some are disobedient to the word, they may be won over without a word by the conduct of their wives,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; 1 Peter 1:15; Philippians 1:27"> when they see your respectful, pure conduct.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:9–10; Romans 12:2; Isaiah 3:18–24"> Let your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “of whom,” referring to the wives">a</a></sup>adornment not be the external kind, braiding hair and putting on gold jewelry or putting on fine clothing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 3:12; 1 Samuel 16:7; Romans 2:29"> but the hidden person of the heart, with the imperishable quality of a gentle and quiet spirit, which is highly valuable in the sight of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Titus 2:3–4; 1 Timothy 5:5; Luke 2:37"> For in the same way formerly the holy women also, who hoped in God, used to adorn themselves by being subject to their own husbands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 18:12; Romans 9:7–9; Isaiah 57:11"> like Sarah obeyed Abraham, calling him lord, whose children you have become when you do good and are not frightened with respect to any terror.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 3:19; Genesis 2:23–24; Matthew 19:3–9"> Husbands, in the same way live with your wives ⸤knowledgeably⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “according to knowledge”">b</a></sup>, as with the weaker female vessel, showing them honor as fellow heirs also of the grace of life, so that your prayers will not be hindered.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Called to Inherit a Blessing

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 12:10; Ephesians 4:31–2; 1 Peter 1:22"> And finally, all of you be harmonious, sympathetic, showing mutual affection, compassionate, humble,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 20:22; Romans 12:17; Romans 12:14"> not repaying evil for evil or insult for insult, but on the other hand blessing others, because for this reason you were called, so that you could inherit a blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 34:12–16; James 1:26; 1 Peter 2:1"> For<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The one who wants to love life</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and see good days</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">must keep his tongue from evil</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and his lips must not speak deceit.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 5:9; Psalm 34:14; Hebrews 12:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">And he must turn away from evil and do good;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he must seek peace and pursue it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 34:15–16; John 9:31; Proverbs 15:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the eyes of the Lord are on the righteous,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and his ears are open to their prayer.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But the face of the Lord is against those who do evil.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Verses 10–12 are a quotation from Ps 34:12–16">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Suffer for Doing What is Good

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Romans 8:28; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> And who is the one who will harm you if you are a zealous adherent for what is good?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 10:28; 1 Peter 2:19–20; Matthew 5:10–12"> But even if you might suffer for the sake of righteousness, you are blessed. And do not be afraid of their intimidation or be disturbed,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The second half of this verse contains a quotation from Isa 8:12">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; 2 Timothy 2:25–26; Luke 21:14–15"> but set Christ apart as Lord in your hearts, always ready to make a defense to anyone who asks you for an accounting concerning the hope that is in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; Hebrews 13:18; 1 Peter 2:15"> But do so with courtesy and respect, having a good conscience, so that in the things in which you are slandered, the ones who malign your good conduct in Christ may be put to shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 3:14; 1 Peter 4:19; Matthew 26:39"> For it is better to suffer for doing good, ⸤if God wills it⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “if the will of God wills”">e</a></sup>, than for doing evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 9:28; Hebrews 9:26; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">For Christ also suffered once for sins,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the just for the unjust,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in order that he could bring you to God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">being put to death in the flesh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but made alive in the spirit,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Peter 4:6; Isaiah 61:1; Isaiah 42:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">in which also he went and proclaimed to the spirits in prison,</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 11:7; 2 Peter 2:5; Genesis 6:3"> who were formerly disobedient, when the patience of God waited in the days of Noah, while an ark was being constructed, in which a few—that is, eight souls—were rescued through water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 16:16; Ezekiel 36:25–26; Acts 2:38"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “which”">f</a></sup>also, corresponding to this, baptism now saves you, not the removal of dirt from the flesh, but an appeal to God for a good conscience through the resurrection of Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; Hebrews 12:2; Hebrews 10:12"> who is at the right hand of God, having gone into heaven, with angels and authorities and powers having been subjected to him.</span>
</div>
</div>
