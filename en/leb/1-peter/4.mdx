## The Time for Behaving Like the Gentiles is Past

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 5:24; 1 Peter 3:18; Colossians 3:3–5"> Therefore, because Christ suffered in the flesh, you also equip yourselves with the same way of thinking, because the one who has suffered in the flesh has ceased from sin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 4:22–24; 1 Peter 1:14; Titus 3:3–8"> in order to live the remaining time in the flesh no longer for human desires, but for the will of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:11; Ephesians 5:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:5"> For the time that has passed was sufficient ⸤to do what the Gentiles desire to do⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to do the intention of the Gentiles”">a</a></sup>, having lived in licentiousness, evil desires, drunkenness, carousing, drinking parties, and wanton idolatries,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 3:16; 1 Peter 2:12; Jude 1:10"> with respect to which they are surprised when you do not run with them into the same flood of dissipation, and so they revile you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 5:9; Acts 10:42; 2 Timothy 4:1"> They<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “who”">b</a></sup>will give an account to the one who is ready to judge the living and the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 8:2; 1 Peter 3:19; Romans 8:9–11"> Because for this reason also the gospel was preached to those who are dead, so that they were judged ⸤by human standards⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “according to men”">c</a></sup>in the flesh, but they may live in the spirit ⸤by God’s standards⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “according to God”">d</a></sup>.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Serving to the Glory of God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 5:8–9; 1 Peter 1:13; 1 Peter 5:8"> Now the end of all things draws near. Therefore be self-controlled and sober-minded for your prayers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 10:12; Colossians 3:14; Proverbs 17:9"> Above all, keep<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “having”">e</a></sup>your love for one another constant, because love covers a large number of sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 13:2; Romans 12:13; Philippians 2:14"> Be hospitable to one another without complaining.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:6–8; 1 Corinthians 12:4–11; 1 Corinthians 4:1–2"> Just as each one has received a gift, use it for serving one another, as good stewards of the varied grace of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:31; Ephesians 6:10; 1 Peter 4:10"> If anyone speaks, let it be as the oracles of God; if anyone serves, let it be as by the strength that God provides, so that in all things God will be glorified through Jesus Christ, to whom is the glory and the power ⸤forever and ever⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “for the ages of the ages”">f</a></sup>. Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Sharing the Sufferings of Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:12; 1 Peter 1:6–7; 1 Peter 5:9"> Dear friends, do not be surprised at the fiery ordeal among you, when it takes place to test you, as if something strange were happening to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 8:17; James 1:2–3; Romans 5:3"> But to the degree that you share in the sufferings of Christ, rejoice, so that also at the revelation of his glory you may rejoice and be glad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 3:14; Luke 6:22; Matthew 5:11"> If you are reviled on account of the name of Christ, you are blessed, because the Spirit of glory and of God rests on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:11; 1 Thessalonians 4:11; 1 Timothy 5:13"> By all means do not let anyone of you suffer as a murderer or a thief or an evildoer or as a meddler.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 3:17–18; Philippians 1:29; 1 Peter 4:19"> But if someone suffers as a Christian, he must not be ashamed, but must glorify God with this name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:29; 2 Thessalonians 1:8; Romans 2:9"> For it is the time for the judgment to begin out from the household of God. But if it begins out from us first, what will be the outcome for those who are disobedient to the gospel of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 11:31; Ezekiel 18:24; Luke 23:31"> And if the righteous are saved with difficulty, ⸤what will become of the ungodly and the sinner⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “the ungodly and sinner where will he appear”">g</a></sup>?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="This verse contains a quotation from Prov 11:31">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalm 37:5; 2 Timothy 1:12; 1 Peter 3:17"> So then also those who suffer according to the will of God must entrust their souls to a faithful Creator in doing good.</span>
</div>
