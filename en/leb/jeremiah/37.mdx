## The Babylonians Withdraw to Meet a Threat from Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:24; 2 Kings 24:17; 2 Kings 24:12"> And king Zedekiah, the son of Josiah, whom Nebuchadnezzar, the king of Babylon, had made king, reigned as king in the land of Judah in place of Coniah, the son of Jehoiakim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 24:19–20; Proverbs 29:12; 2 Chronicles 36:12–16"> But he and his servants and the people of the land did not listen to the words of Yahweh that he spoke by the hand of Jeremiah the prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:1–2; Jeremiah 29:25; Jeremiah 42:20"> And king Zedekiah sent Jehucal the son of Shelemiah, and Zephaniah the son of Maaseiah, the priest, to Jeremiah the prophet, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to say”">a</a></sup>“Please pray for us to Yahweh our God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:15; Jeremiah 32:2–3"> Now Jeremiah was coming and going out in the midst of the people and they had not put him in the house of imprisonment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:15; Jeremiah 37:11; Jeremiah 34:21"> And the army of Pharaoh had come out from Egypt, and the Chaldeans, who were laying siege to Jerusalem, heard their report and they withdrew from Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And the word of Yahweh came to Jeremiah the prophet, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “to say”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Lamentations 4:17; 2 Kings 22:18; Ezekiel 17:17"> “Thus says Yahweh, the God of Israel, ‘This is what you shall say to the king of Judah, who sent you to me to inquire of me, “Look, the army of Pharaoh, which set out to help you, is going to return to his land Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:2–8; Jeremiah 38:23; Jeremiah 34:21–22"> And the Chaldeans will return, and they will fight against this city, and they will capture it, and they will burn it with fire.” ’</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Obadiah 1:3; Jeremiah 29:8; Galatians 6:3"> Thus says Yahweh: ‘You must not deceive yourselves, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “to say”">c</a></sup>“Surely the Chaldeans will go from us,” for they will not go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:45; Isaiah 30:17; Joel 2:11"> For even if you struck the whole army of the Chaldeans who are fighting against you, and only men pierced through remained among them, each one in his tent, they would rise up and they would burn this city with fire.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jeremiah is Arrested and Put in Prison

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:5"> ⸤And then⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “And it will happen”">d</a></sup>at the withdrawing of the army of the Chaldeans from Jerusalem ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “from the face of”">e</a></sup>the army of Pharaoh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:1; Nehemiah 6:11; 1 Kings 19:3"> Jeremiah set out from Jerusalem to go to the land of Benjamin to receive a portion from there in the midst of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Zechariah 14:10; Jeremiah 38:7; Luke 23:2"> ⸤And when⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “And it was”">f</a></sup>he was at the Gate of Benjamin, there was ⸤a sentry on duty⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “an owner of supervision”">g</a></sup>whose name was Irijah, the son of Shelemiah, the son of Hananiah, and he seized Jeremiah the prophet, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “to say”">h</a></sup>“You are deserting to the Chaldeans!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 52:1–2; Jeremiah 40:4–6; Psalm 27:12"> And Jeremiah said, “That is a lie! I am not deserting to the Chaldeans.” But he would not listen to him. So Irijah seized Jeremiah and brought him to the officials.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:26; Jeremiah 20:1–3; Jeremiah 26:16"> And the officials were angry at Jeremiah and they struck him. And they put him in ⸤prison⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “the house of the fetters”">i</a></sup>in the house of Jonathan the secretary, for they had converted it to the ⸤prison⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “house of the imprisonment”">j</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:6; Jeremiah 38:10–13; Lamentations 3:53"> So Jeremiah came to the ⸤dungeon⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “house of the cistern”">k</a></sup>, even to the vaulted cells, and Jeremiah stayed there many days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:7; Jeremiah 24:8; 1 Kings 14:1–4"> Then King Zedekiah sent and fetched him. And the king questioned him in secrecy in his house, and he said, “Is there a word from Yahweh?” And Jeremiah said, “There is.” And he said, “You will be given into the hand of the king of Babylon.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 25:8; John 10:32; Daniel 6:22"> Then Jeremiah said to King Zedekiah, “What did I do wrong to you, or to your servants, or to this people, that you have put me in ⸤prison⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “the house of the imprisonment”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Jeremiah 28:10–17; Jeremiah 6:14"> And where are your prophets who prophesied to you, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “to say”">m</a></sup>‘The king of Babylon will not come against you and against this land’?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:7; Jeremiah 38:6–9; Acts 28:18–19"> Now then, my lord the king, please let my plea fall ⸤before you⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “to the face of you”">n</a></sup>You must not send me back to the house of Jonathan the secretary, so that I will not die there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:13; Jeremiah 38:28; Jeremiah 38:9"> So King Zedekiah commanded, and they handed Jeremiah over in the courtyard of the guard, and they gave to him a round loaf of bread from the street of the bakers ⸤every day⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “for the day”">o</a></sup>until the finishing of all the bread from the city. And Jeremiah stayed in the courtyard of the guard.</span>
</div>
</div>
