## Jeremiah Buys a Field

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:1–2; 2 Kings 25:1–2; Jeremiah 25:1"> The word that came to Jeremiah from Yahweh in the tenth year of Zedekiah the king of Judah, that was the eighteenth year of Nebuchadnezzar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:21; Nehemiah 3:25; Jeremiah 33:1"> Now at that time the army of the king of Babylon was laying siege to Jerusalem and Jeremiah the prophet was confined in the courtyard of the guard that was in the palace of the king of Judah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:2–3; Jeremiah 21:4–7; Jeremiah 26:8–9"> where Zedekiah, the king of Judah, had confined him, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to say”">a</a></sup>“Why are you prophesying, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “to say”">b</a></sup>‘Thus says Yahweh, “Look, I am going to give this city into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he will capture it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:4–7; 2 Kings 25:4–7; Jeremiah 37:17"> and Zedekiah, the king of Judah, will not escape from the hand of the Chaldeans, but surely he will be given into the hand of the king of Babylon, and ⸤he will speak face to face with him⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “his mouth shall speak with his mouth”">c</a></sup>and ⸤he will see him eye to eye⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “his eyes shall see his eyes”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:7; Ezekiel 17:9–10; Ezekiel 17:15"> and to Babylon he will bring Zedekiah, and there he will be until my attending to him,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">e</a></sup>Yahweh. “If you fight against the Chaldeans, you will not be successful” ’?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And Jeremiah said, “The word of Yahweh came to me, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “to say”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:1; Jeremiah 11:21; Leviticus 25:25"> ‘Look, Hanamel, the son of Shallum, your uncle, is going to come to you, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “to say”">g</a></sup>“Buy for yourself my field that is at Anathoth, for ⸤you have⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “to you is”">h</a></sup>the right of redemption to buy it.” ’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Here the direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:7; Jeremiah 32:2; Jeremiah 33:1"> Then Hanamel, the son of my uncle, came to me, to the courtyard of the guard ⸤according to⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “as”">j</a></sup>the word of Yahweh, and he said to me, ‘Please buy my field that is at Anathoth, that is in the land of Benjamin, for to you is the claim of possession, and to you the redemption; buy it<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Here the direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">k</a></sup>for yourself.’ Then I knew that this was the word of Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 11:12–13; Esther 3:9; 1 Kings 20:39"> And I bought the field from Hanamel, the son of my uncle, that was at Anathoth. And I weighed out to him the money, seventeen silver shekels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:44; Jeremiah 32:12; Jeremiah 32:25"> And I signed on the letter<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “scroll”">l</a></sup>and sealed it,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Here the direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">m</a></sup>and I called witnesses as witness, and I weighed out the money on a set of scales.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 2:27; 1 Corinthians 11:16; Acts 26:3"> Then I took the deed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “scroll”">n</a></sup>of the purchase, the sealed copy containing the commandments<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Hebrew “commandment”">o</a></sup>and the rules, together with the one that was open.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:16; Jeremiah 51:59; Jeremiah 36:4–5"> And I gave the deed of the purchase to Baruch the son of Neriah, the son of Mahseiah, ⸤in the presence of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “before the eyes of”">p</a></sup>Hanamel, the son of my uncle, and ⸤in the presence of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “before the eyes of”">q</a></sup>the witnesses who signed the deed of the purchase, ⸤in the presence of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “before the eyes of”">r</a></sup>the Judeans<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Hebrew “Judean”">s</a></sup>who were sitting in the courtyard of the guard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And I commanded Baruch ⸤in their presence⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “before their eyes”">t</a></sup>⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “to say”">u</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> ‘Thus says Yahweh of hosts, the God of Israel, “Take these deeds,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “scrolls”">v</a></sup>this deed of the purchase, the sealed one, and this opened deed, and you must put them in an ⸤earthenware jar⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “jar of earthenware”">w</a></sup>so that they may be kept preserved many days.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 3:10; Amos 9:14–15; Jeremiah 30:18"> For thus says Yahweh of hosts, the God of Israel: “Houses and fields and vineyards will again be bought in this land.” ’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:1; Genesis 32:9–12; Philippians 4:6–7"> And I prayed to Yahweh—after giving my deed of the purchase to Baruch the son of Neriah—⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “to say”">x</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 1:37; Jeremiah 32:27; Genesis 18:14"> ‘Ah Lord Yahweh! Look, you made the heavens and the earth by your great power and by your outstretched arm. ⸤Nothing⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “Not all a thing”">y</a></sup>is too difficult for you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:16; Deuteronomy 5:9–10; Exodus 34:7"> showing loyal love to the thousands, and repaying the guilt of ⸤parents⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “fathers”">z</a></sup>into the laps<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Hebrew “lap”">aa</a></sup>of their children after them. Powerful, great, mighty, his name is Yahweh of hosts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:10; Matthew 16:27; Psalm 62:12"> great in counsel and great in deed, whose eyes are opened to all the ways of the children of humankind, to give to each one according to his ways and according to the fruit of his deeds,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:10; 2 Samuel 7:23; Exodus 10:2"> who accomplished signs and wonders in the land of Egypt, up to this day, and in Israel, and among humankind, and you have made for yourself a name, as it is this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 6:6; Deuteronomy 26:8; Psalm 105:43"> And you brought out your people Israel from the land of Egypt with signs, and with wonders, and with a strong hand, and with an arm stretched out, and with great terror.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Exodus 13:5; Jeremiah 11:5"> And you gave to them this land, which you swore to their ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Or “fathers”">ab</a></sup>to give to them, a land flowing with milk and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezra 9:7; Psalm 78:54–55; Daniel 9:10–14"> And they entered and took possession of it, but they did not listen to your voice, and they did not follow your law. They did nothing of all that you commanded to them to do, and you caused to happen to them all this disaster.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:36; Deuteronomy 4:26; Joshua 23:15–16"> Look, the siege ramps have come up to the city to capture it, and the city has been given into the hand of the Chaldeans who are fighting against it, because of the sword, the famine, and the plague, and what you spoke happened, and look, you are seeing it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Here the direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">ac</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:24; Psalm 97:2; Psalm 77:19"> Yet you have said to me, Lord Yahweh, “Buy for yourself the field with the money, and call witnesses as witness,” though the city has been given into the hand of the Chaldeans.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And the word of Yahweh came to Jeremiah, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “to say”">ad</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 19:26; Jeremiah 32:17; Isaiah 64:8"> “Look, I am Yahweh, the God of all flesh; is ⸤anything⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “all thing”">ae</a></sup>too difficult for me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:3; Jeremiah 32:24; Jeremiah 19:7–12"> ⸤Therefore⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “To thus”">af</a></sup>thus says Yahweh: “Look, I am going to give this city into the hand of the Chaldeans, and into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar, the king of Babylon, and he will capture it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:10; Jeremiah 19:13; Jeremiah 52:13"> And the Chaldeans, who are fighting against this city, will come and set this city on fire, and they will burn it, and the houses where they have made smoke offerings on their roofs to Baal, and where they have devoted libations to other gods, in order to provoke me to anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:22–26; Deuteronomy 9:7–12; Jeremiah 25:7"> For the people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Or “sons/children”">ag</a></sup>of Israel and the people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Or “sons/children”">ah</a></sup>of Judah were doing only evil in my eyes from their youth, for the people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Or “sons/children”">ai</a></sup>of Israel were only provoking me to anger by the work of their hands,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">aj</a></sup>Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Kings 23:27; Jeremiah 27:10; 2 Kings 21:4–7"> “For this city has been for me a cause of my anger and of my wrath from the day that they built it even until this day, so I will remove it from my ⸤sight⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Literally “face”">ak</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Daniel 9:8; Jeremiah 2:26; Isaiah 1:4–6"> because of all the evil of the people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Or “sons/children”">al</a></sup>of Israel and the people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Or “sons/children”">am</a></sup>of Judah that they did to provoke me to anger—they, their kings, their officials, their priests, and their prophets, and the men<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Hebrew “man”">an</a></sup>of Judah, and the inhabitants of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:13; Jeremiah 2:27; Ezekiel 8:16"> And they have turned to me their backs<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Hebrew “back”">ao</a></sup>and not their faces, though ⸤I have taught them over and over again⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Literally “I have taught them doing early and teaching”">ap</a></sup>but they were not listening to accept discipline.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:30; Ezekiel 8:5–16; 2 Kings 21:4–7"> And they set up their abominations in the house that is called by my name, to defile it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:31; 2 Chronicles 33:6; Leviticus 18:21"> And they built the high places of Baal that are in the Valley of Ben Hinnom to present as offerings their sons and their daughters to Molech, which I had not commanded them, and it had not come to my ⸤mind⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Literally “heart”">aq</a></sup>to do this detestable thing in order to cause Judah to sin.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:24; Jeremiah 32:3; Jeremiah 32:28"> “So now ⸤therefore⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Literally “to thus”">ar</a></sup>thus says Yahweh, the God of Israel, concerning this city, of which you are saying, ‘It will be given into the hand of the king of Babylon by the sword, and by the famine, and by the plague’:</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:3; Jeremiah 23:6; Ezekiel 34:12–14"> Look, I am going to gather them from all the lands to which I driven them in my anger, and in my fury, and in great wrath, and I will bring them back to this place, and I will cause them to dwell ⸤in safety⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Literally “with confidence”">as</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:7; Ezekiel 11:19–20; Hebrews 8:10"> And they will be for me a people, and I will be for them God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19–20; Acts 4:32; John 17:21"> And I will give to them one heart and one way, to revere me ⸤forever⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-at" id="fnref-at" data-fn="Literally “all the days”">at</a></sup>for good to them, and to their children after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–33; Ezekiel 36:26; Jeremiah 24:7"> And I will ⸤make⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-au" id="fnref-au" data-fn="Literally “cut”">au</a></sup>with them ⸤an everlasting covenant⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-av" id="fnref-av" data-fn="Literally “a covenant of eternity”">av</a></sup>that I will not turn away from them, my doing good to them, and my reverence I will put in their hearts<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aw" id="fnref-aw" data-fn="Hebrew “heart”">aw</a></sup>so that they will not turn aside from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:6; Zephaniah 3:17; Isaiah 62:5"> And I will rejoice over them to do good to them, and I will plant them in this land in faithfulness with all my heart, and with all my inner self.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ax" id="fnref-ax" data-fn="Or “soul”">ax</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:28; Joshua 23:14–15; Jeremiah 33:10–11"> “For thus says Yahweh, ‘Just as I have brought to this people all this great disaster, so I will bring upon them all the good that I promise to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:11–14; Jeremiah 32:15; Jeremiah 32:36"> And the fields<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ay" id="fnref-ay" data-fn="Hebrew “field”">ay</a></sup>will be bought in this land of which you are saying, “It is a desolation, ⸤without humankind or animals⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-az" id="fnref-az" data-fn="Literally “from there is not humankind and animal”">az</a></sup>It has been given into the hand of the Chaldeans.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:26; Jeremiah 33:26; Jeremiah 33:11"> They will buy fields with money, and they will sign the deeds,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ba" id="fnref-ba" data-fn="Hebrew “scroll”">ba</a></sup>and they will seal them,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bb" id="fnref-bb" data-fn="Here the direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">bb</a></sup>and they will call witnesses as witness in the land of Benjamin, and in the surroundings of Jerusalem, and in the cities of Judah, and in the cities of the hill country, and in the cities of the Shephelah, and in the cities of the Negev, for I will restore their fortunes,’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bc" id="fnref-bc" data-fn="Hebrew “fortune”">bc</a></sup>⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bd" id="fnref-bd" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">bd</a></sup>Yahweh.”</span>
</div>
