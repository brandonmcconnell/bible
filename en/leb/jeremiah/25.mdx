## Seventy Years of Servitude

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:1; 2 Kings 24:1–2; Daniel 1:1"> The word that came to Jeremiah concerning all the people of Judah in the fourth year of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah, the king of Judah, that was the first year of Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:11; Jeremiah 19:14–15; Mark 7:14–15"> which Jeremiah the prophet spoke to all the people of Judah and to all the inhabitants of Jerusalem, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to say”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:7; Jeremiah 26:5; Jeremiah 1:2"> “From the thirteenth year of Josiah, the son of Amon, the king of Judah, even up to this day, these twenty-three years<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew “year”">b</a></sup>the word of Yahweh has come to me, and ⸤I have spoken to you over and over again⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “I have spoken to you doing early and speaking”">c</a></sup>but you have not listened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:3; Jeremiah 26:5; Jeremiah 13:10–11"> And though Yahweh has sent all his servants the prophets to you ⸤over and over again⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “do early and sending”">d</a></sup>you have not listened, and you have not inclined your ear to hear,</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:19; Jeremiah 7:6; Jeremiah 35:15"> and you must not go after other gods to serve them, and to bow in worship to them, and you must not provoke me to anger with the work of your hands, then I will not do something bad to you.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 21:15; Proverbs 8:36; Deuteronomy 32:21"> ‘Yet you have not listened to me,’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">g</a></sup>Yahweh, ‘so that you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “they”">h</a></sup>have provoked me to anger with the work of your hands for your own harm.’</span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:10; Jeremiah 1:5; Daniel 5:31"> And I will bring upon that land all my words that I have spoken against it, everything that is written in this scroll which Jeremiah prophesied against all the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:6; Jeremiah 27:7; Jeremiah 50:9"> For many nations and great kings will let them work, even them, and I will repay them according to their deeds, and according to the work of their hands.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 75:8; Isaiah 51:17; Revelation 14:10"> For thus said Yahweh, the God of Israel, to me, “Take this cup of the wine of wrath from my hand, and you must give it to all the nations to whom I am sending you to drink.</span>
