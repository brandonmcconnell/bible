## The Fall of Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 24:18; Joshua 10:29; 2 Chronicles 36:11"> Zedekiah was ⸤twenty-one years old⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “a son of twenty-one year”">a</a></sup>at his beginning to reign, and he reigned eleven years<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew “year”">b</a></sup>in Jerusalem. And the name of his mother was Hamutal, the daughter of Jeremiah of Libnah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 14:22; 2 Kings 24:19–20; Ezekiel 17:16–20"> And he did evil in the eyes of Yahweh like all that Jehoiakim had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 3:4–5; 2 Chronicles 36:13; Isaiah 3:1"> For because of the ⸤anger⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “nose”">c</a></sup>of Yahweh this happened in Jerusalem and Judah until his casting them from his ⸤presence⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “face”">d</a></sup>And Zedekiah rebelled against the king of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:1; Zechariah 8:19; Ezekiel 24:1–2"> ⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “And it was”">e</a></sup>in the ninth year of his reign, in the tenth month, on the tenth day of the month, Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon came against Jerusalem, he and all his army. And they laid siege to it, and built siege works<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “siege work”">f</a></sup>against it all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> So the city came ⸤under siege⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “into the siege”">g</a></sup>until the eleventh year of King Zedekiah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:2; Isaiah 3:1; 2 Kings 25:3"> In the fourth month, on the ninth day of the month, the famine in the city became severe and there was no food for the people of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:4–7; Jeremiah 51:32; 2 Kings 25:4"> Then the city was breached, and all ⸤the soldiers⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “the men of the battle”">h</a></sup>fled and went out from the city by night by the way of the gate between the two walls that are at the garden of the king, though the Chaldeans were all around the city. And they went in the direction of the Jordan Valley.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “Arabah”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:21; Jeremiah 32:4; Jeremiah 38:23"> But the army of the Chaldeans pursued after the king and they overtook Zedekiah in the plains of Jericho, and all his army was scattered from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:5; Numbers 13:21; 2 Kings 25:6"> Then they captured the king and brought him up to the king of Babylon at Riblah in the land of Hamath, and ⸤he passed sentence on him⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “he spoke to him judgments”">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:30; 2 Kings 25:7; Genesis 21:16"> And the king of Babylon slaughtered the sons of Zedekiah before his eyes, and he also slaughtered all the officials of Judah at Riblah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Jeremiah 34:3–5"> Then he made blind the eyes of Zedekiah, and they tied him up with bronze fetters, and the king of Babylon brought him to Babylon. And he put him in ⸤prison⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “the house of the watch”">k</a></sup>until the day of his death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 25:8; Jeremiah 39:9; Jeremiah 52:29"> Now in the fifth month, on the tenth day of the month, which was the nineteenth year of King Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon, Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Hebrew “guards”">l</a></sup>who stood ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">m</a></sup>the king of Babylon, entered into Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 79:1; Micah 3:12; 2 Chronicles 36:19"> And he burned the ⸤temple⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “house”">n</a></sup>of Yahweh, and the palace<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “house”">o</a></sup>of the king, and all the houses of Jerusalem, even every great house he burned with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:3; 2 Kings 25:10"> And all the army of the Chaldeans who were with the captain of the guard<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Hebrew “guards”">p</a></sup>broke down all the walls of Jerusalem all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:1–2; Zechariah 14:2"> And Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Hebrew “guards”">q</a></sup>deported some of the poor of the people, and the rest of the people who were left in the city, and the deserters who deserted to the king of Babylon, along with the rest of the craftsmen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 25:12; Jeremiah 39:9–10; Jeremiah 40:5–7"> But Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Hebrew “guards”">r</a></sup>left some of the poor of the land to serve as vinedressers and farmers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:19–22; Jeremiah 52:21–23; 2 Chronicles 4:12–15"> And the Chaldeans broke the pillars of bronze that were in the ⸤temple⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “house”">s</a></sup>of Yahweh, and the kettle stands and the sea of bronze that were in the ⸤temple⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “house”">t</a></sup>of Yahweh, and they carried all their bronze to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 27:3; Numbers 4:14; 1 Kings 7:45"> And they took with them the pots, and the shovels, and the snuffers, and the sprinkling bowls, and the pans, and all the vessels of bronze which were used in temple service.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 7:49–50; 2 Chronicles 4:6–22; Leviticus 26:12"> And the captain of the guard<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Hebrew “guards”">u</a></sup>took the bowls, and the firepans, and the sprinkling bowls, and the pots, and the lampstands, and the pans, and the libation bowls, ⸤those made of solid gold⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “which gold gold”">v</a></sup>and ⸤those made of solid silver⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “which silver silver”">w</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 7:47; 2 Kings 25:16; 2 Chronicles 4:18"> The two pillars, the one sea, and the twelve bronze oxen that were under the kettle stands which King Solomon had made for the ⸤temple⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “house”">x</a></sup>of Yahweh—there was not a weight for the bronze of all these vessels!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 25:17; 1 Kings 7:15–21; 2 Chronicles 3:15–17"> Now the pillars, the height of one pillar was eighteen cubits,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Hebrew “cubit”">y</a></sup>and a thread of twelve cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Hebrew “cubit”">z</a></sup>surrounded it, and its thickness was four fingers, hollowed out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 28:25; 2 Kings 25:17; 2 Chronicles 3:15"> And a capital upon it was bronze and the height of the one capital was five cubits, and latticework and pomegranates were on the capital on all sides, all of bronze. And like these was the second pillar with pomegranates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 7:20"> And there were ninety-six pomegranates ⸤on the sides⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “breath”">aa</a></sup>all the pomegranates on the latticework on all sides were a hundred.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Kings 25:18; Jeremiah 29:25; Jeremiah 35:4"> Then the captain of the guard<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Hebrew “guards”">ab</a></sup>took Seraiah ⸤the chief priest⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “the priest of the head”">ac</a></sup>and Zephaniah ⸤the second priest⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “the priest of the second”">ad</a></sup>and three keepers of the threshold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 25:19; Esther 1:14; Matthew 18:10"> And from the city he took one high official who was chief officer over ⸤the soldiers⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “the men of the battle”">ae</a></sup>and seven men of ⸤the king’s advisors⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “those who see the face of the king”">af</a></sup>who were found in the city, and the secretary of the commander of the army who levied for military service the people of the land, and sixty men<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Hebrew “man”">ag</a></sup>of the people of the land who were found in the midst of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Then Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Hebrew “guards”">ah</a></sup>took them and brought them to the king of Babylon at Riblah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:28; Isaiah 32:13–14; Isaiah 6:11–12"> And the king of Babylon struck them down and killed them at Riblah in the land of Hamath. So Judah left from its land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Kings 24:12–16; 2 Kings 24:2–3; Daniel 1:1–3"> This is the number of the people whom Nebuchadnezzar deported: in the seventh year, three thousand twenty-three Judeans;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:12; Genesis 12:5; 2 Kings 25:11"> in the eighteenth year of Nebuchadnezzar, eight hundred and thirty-two persons<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Hebrew “person”">ai</a></sup>from Jerusalem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:15; Jeremiah 6:9"> in the twenty-third year of Nebuchadnezzar, Nebuzaradan, the captain of the guard,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Hebrew “guards”">aj</a></sup>deported seven hundred and forty-five Judean persons;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Hebrew “person”">ak</a></sup>there were four thousand six hundred persons<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Hebrew “person”">al</a></sup>in all.</span>
</div>

## An Allowance for Jehoiachin

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Kings 25:27–30; Psalm 3:3; Genesis 40:13"> ⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Literally “and it was”">am</a></sup>in the thirty-seventh year of the exile of Jehoiachin, the king of Judah, in the twelfth month, on the twenty-fifth day of the month, Evil-merodach, the king of Babylon, in the first year of his reign, lifted up the head of Jehoiachin, the king of Judah, and brought him out from ⸤prison⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Literally “the house of the imprisonment”">an</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 12:25; Daniel 5:18–19; Daniel 2:37"> Then he spoke with him kindly and gave his seat above the seats<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Hebrew “seat”">ao</a></sup>of the kings who were with him in Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:7; 1 Kings 2:7; Genesis 41:14"> So he changed the garments of his imprisonment and he ate food ⸤before him⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Literally “to the face of him”">ap</a></sup>continually all the days of his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:10; Luke 11:3; Matthew 6:11"> And his allowance, a continual allowance was given to him by the king of Babylon ⸤on a daily basis⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Literally “a matter of a day in its day”">aq</a></sup>all the days of his life up to the day of his death.</span>
