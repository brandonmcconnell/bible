## Jeremiah Decides to Remain in the Land

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:15; Acts 12:6–7; Ephesians 6:20"> The word that came to Jeremiah from Yahweh after Nebuzaradan, the captain of the guard,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew “guards”">a</a></sup>had let him go from Ramah, ⸤where he had been taken⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “at his taking him">b</a></sup>bound in chains in the midst of all the exiles<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “exile”">c</a></sup>of Jerusalem and Judah who were being deported to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:24–28; Jeremiah 22:8–9; Lamentations 2:15–17"> And the captain of the guard<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew “guards”">d</a></sup>took Jeremiah and said to him, “Yahweh your God threatened this disaster to this place,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 2:5; Jeremiah 50:7; Daniel 9:11–12"> and now he has brought it about,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Here the direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">e</a></sup>and Yahweh has done ⸤just as⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “as that”">f</a></sup>he threatened. Because you sinned against Yahweh and did not listen to his voice, so this thing has happened to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:11–12; Genesis 20:15; Genesis 13:9"> And so then look, I have released you ⸤today⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “the day”">g</a></sup>from the chains that were on your hands.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “hand”">h</a></sup>If it is good in your eyes to come with me to Babylon, then come, and ⸤I will take care of you⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “I will place my eye on you”">i</a></sup>But if it is bad in your eyes to come with me to Babylon, then refrain. Look, the whole land is ⸤before you⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “to the face of you”">j</a></sup>To wherever it is good and right in your eyes to go, then go there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:14; 2 Kings 8:7–9; Jeremiah 41:2"> While he still had not turned back, Nebuzaradan added, “Return to Gedaliah the son of Ahikam, the son of Shaphan, whom the king of Babylon has appointed in an official position over the towns of Judah, and stay with him in the midst of the people. Or to wherever it is right in your eyes to go, then go there.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Here the direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">k</a></sup>Then the captain of the guard<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Hebrew “guards”">l</a></sup>gave him an allowance of provisions and a present, and let him go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:14; Judges 20:1; Judges 21:1"> So Jeremiah went to Gedaliah the son of Ahikam, at Mizpah and stayed with him in the midst of the people who were left in the land.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Assassination of Gedaliah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:10; 2 Kings 25:4; Jeremiah 39:4"> When all the commanders of the armies who were in the open country and their men heard that the king of Babylon had appointed Gedaliah the son of Ahikam in an official position in the land, and that ⸤he had put him in charge of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “he had appointed to him”">m</a></sup>men, and women, and little children, and of the poor of the land, of all those who had not been deported to Babylon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:1; Deuteronomy 3:14; Jeremiah 42:8"> then they went to Gedaliah at Mizpah—Ishmael the son of Nethaniah, and Johanan, and Jehonathan the son of Kareah, and Seraiah the son of Tanhumeth, and the sons of Ephai the Netophathite, and Jezaniah the son of the Maacathite, they and their men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:11; 2 Kings 25:24; 1 Samuel 20:16–17"> And Gedaliah the son of Ahikam, the son of Shaphan, swore to them and to their men, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “to say”">n</a></sup>“You must not be afraid of serving the Chaldeans. Stay in the land and serve the king of Babylon, and it will go well with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:12; Deuteronomy 1:38; Isaiah 16:9"> ⸤As for me⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “And I”">o</a></sup>look, I am staying at Mizpah to represent you ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">p</a></sup>the Chaldeans who come to us. But you, gather wine and summer fruit and oil, and put them in your vessels, and live in your towns that you have seized.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 16:4; Genesis 36:8; Jeremiah 24:9"> And also all the Judeans who were in Moab, and among the ⸤Ammonites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “sons of Ammon”">q</a></sup>and in Edom, and who were in all the lands, when they heard that the king of Babylon had given a remnant to Judah and that he had appointed Gedaliah the son of Ahikam, the son of Shaphan, in an official position over them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:5"> then all the Judeans returned from all the places to which they were scattered. And they came to the land of Judah, to Gedaliah at Mizpah, and they gathered wine and summer fruit that yielded ⸤in great abundance⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “very numerous”">r</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:1; Jeremiah 40:8"> And Johanan the son of Kareah and all the commanders of the armies who were in the open country came to Gedaliah at Mizpah</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 41:10; 2 Samuel 10:1–6; Jeremiah 25:21"> and said to him, “⸤Are you at all aware⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “know you know”">s</a></sup>that Baalis, the king of the ⸤Ammonites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “sons of Ammon”">t</a></sup>has sent Ishmael the son of Nethaniah, to kill you?” But Gedaliah the son of Ahikam would not believe them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:8; 2 Samuel 21:17; Jeremiah 42:2"> Then Johanan the son of Kareah said to Gedaliah in secrecy at Mizpah, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “to say”">u</a></sup>“Please let me go and kill Ishmael the son of Nethaniah, and ⸤nobody⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “not a man”">v</a></sup>will know. Why should he kill you, so that all of Judah who are gathered to you will be scattered, and the remnant of Judah will perish?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 10:16–17; Romans 3:8; Jeremiah 41:2"> But Gedaliah the son of Ahikam said to Johanan the son of Kareah, “You must not do this thing, for you are telling a lie about Ishmael.”</span>
</div>
</div>
