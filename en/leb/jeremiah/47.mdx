## A Message Concerning the Philistines

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:20; Genesis 10:19; 1 Kings 4:24"> The word of Yahweh that came to Jeremiah the prophet concerning the Philistines ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “at not yet”">a</a></sup>Pharaoh attacked Gaza.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:20; Jeremiah 8:16; Isaiah 8:7–8"> Thus says Yahweh:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Look, waters are rising from the north,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will become as a raging torrent,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they will overflow the land,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and that which fills it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the city and those who live in it.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And the people will cry out,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and every inhabitant of the land will wail,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:16; Judges 5:22; Nahum 3:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">from the sound of the stamping of the hoofs of his stallions,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from the clattering of his chariots,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew “chariot”">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the noise of their wheels.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Fathers do not turn to their children</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because of the slackness of their hands.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 9:7; Deuteronomy 2:23; Isaiah 20:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because of the day that is coming</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to destroy all the Philistines,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to cut off from Tyre and from Sidon</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">every survivor who could help,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for Yahweh is destroying the Philistines,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the remnant of the coastland of Caphtor.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:37; Jeremiah 25:20; Jeremiah 41:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Baldness has come upon Gaza,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Ashkelon is destroyed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">O remnant of their valley,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">⸤how long⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “until when”">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will you cut yourself with a blade?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:3–5; Jeremiah 12:12; John 18:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Ah, sword of Yahweh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">⸤how long⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “until when”">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will you not be quiet?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Gather to your sheath,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">keep quiet and stand still.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:17; Micah 6:9; Isaiah 10:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">How can you be quiet</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when Yahweh has given an order to it?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Against Ashkelon and against the shore of the sea,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">there he has appointed it.”</span></span>
