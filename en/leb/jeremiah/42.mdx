## The Remnant Asks Jeremiah to Pray to Yahweh for Direction

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 41:11; Jeremiah 40:13; Jeremiah 6:13"> Then all the commanders of the armies, and Johanan the son of Kareah, and Jezaniah the son of Hoshaiah, and all the people from the smallest to the greatest, approached</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:7; Leviticus 26:22; Acts 8:24"> and said to Jeremiah the prophet, “May our plea please fall ⸤before you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to the face of you”">a</a></sup>and pray for us to Yahweh your God, for all this remnant, for we are left but a few of many, as your eyes are seeing us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 86:11; Proverbs 3:6; Psalm 25:4–5"> And let Yahweh your God inform us the way in which we should go and the thing that we should do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:23; 1 Samuel 3:17–18; Exodus 8:29"> And Jeremiah the prophet said to them, “I have heard. Look, I am going to pray to Yahweh your God according to your words, ⸤and then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “and it will happen”">b</a></sup>all the words<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “word”">c</a></sup>that Yahweh answers you I will tell you; I will not withhold from you a word.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 31:50; Judges 11:10; Malachi 3:5"> Then they said to Jeremiah, “May Yahweh be against us as ⸤a true and faithful witness⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “a witness of faithfulness and trustworthy”">d</a></sup>if we do not do according to all the words<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew “word”">e</a></sup>that Yahweh sends you for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:23; Deuteronomy 5:29; Romans 8:7"> Whether good or bad, we will listen to the voice of Yahweh our God, to whom we are sending you, so that it may go well with us when we listen to the voice of Yahweh our God.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Remnant Rejects the Word of Yahweh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 27:14; Isaiah 28:16; Habakkuk 2:3"> ⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “And it was”">f</a></sup>at the end of ten days the word of Yahweh came to Jeremiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:1; Jeremiah 41:11–16; Jeremiah 43:2–5"> And he summoned Johanan the son of Kareah, and all the commanders of the armies who were with him, and all the people from the smallest to the greatest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 19:4; Jeremiah 42:2; 2 Kings 19:6"> and said to them, “Thus says Yahweh, the God of Israel, to whom you sent me to cause your plea to fall ⸤before him⸥:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “to the face of him”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:28; Ezekiel 36:36; Jeremiah 24:6"> ‘If only you will stay in this land, then I will build you and I will not tear you down, and I will plant you and I will not pluck you up, for I relent of the disaster that I have brought to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Jeremiah 1:19; Jeremiah 1:8"> You must not be afraid ⸤of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “from the face of”">h</a></sup>the king of Babylon whom you are ⸤afraid of⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “in fear from the presence of him”">i</a></sup>You must not be afraid of him,’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">j</a></sup>Yahweh, ‘for I am with you to save you and to deliver you from his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:11; Psalm 106:45–46; Proverbs 16:7"> And I will show you compassion, and he will have compassion on you and will restore you to your soil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:16; Exodus 5:2; Jeremiah 42:10"> But if you are saying, “We will not stay in this land,” so as to not listen to the voice of Yahweh your God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 41:17; Jeremiah 4:19; Isaiah 31:1"> saying, “No, for we will go to the land of Egypt where we will not see war, and we will not hear the sound of a horn, and we will not be hungry for bread, and there we will stay,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:16; Jeremiah 44:12–14; Jeremiah 42:17"> then ⸤therefore⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “to thus”">k</a></sup>hear the word of Yahweh, O remnant of Judah. Thus says Yahweh of hosts, the God of Israel: “⸤If you are determined⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “If you indeed set your face”">l</a></sup>to go to Egypt, and you go to dwell as aliens there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:27; Ezekiel 11:8; Amos 9:1–4"> then it will be that the sword that you are in fear of will overtake you there in the land of Egypt, and the famine that you are anxious of will pursue after you there into Egypt, and there you will die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:22; Jeremiah 44:28; Jeremiah 44:13–14"> So all the people who ⸤are determined⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “have set their faces”">m</a></sup>to go to Egypt to dwell as aliens there will die by the sword, by the famine, and by the plague, and there will not be for them an escapee or a survivor from the disaster that I am bringing upon them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:20; Jeremiah 18:16; Jeremiah 29:18"> For thus says Yahweh of hosts, the God of Israel: “As my anger and my wrath were poured out on the inhabitants of Jerusalem, so my wrath will pour out on you at your going to Egypt, and you will become a curse, and a horror, and a curse formula, and a disgrace, and you will no longer see this place.” ’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:16; Isaiah 30:1–7; Nehemiah 9:26"> Yahweh has spoken to you, O remnant of Judah, ‘You must not go to Egypt.’ You must certainly know that I have warned you ⸤today⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “the day”">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:3–4; Ezekiel 33:31; Matthew 22:15–18"> that ⸤you have made a fatal mistake⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “you have caused them to err for your lives”">o</a></sup>for you sent me to Yahweh your God, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “to say”">p</a></sup>‘Pray for us to Yahweh our God, and all that Yahweh our God says, so tell us and we will do it.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Here the direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:7; Zechariah 7:11–12; Acts 20:26–27"> So I have told you ⸤today⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “the day”">r</a></sup>and you have not listened to the voice of Yahweh your God, or of ⸤anything⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “all”">s</a></sup>that he sent me for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:17; Ezekiel 6:11; Hosea 9:6"> Now then, certainly you must know that by the sword, by the famine, and by the plague you will die in the place where you desire to go, to dwell as aliens there.”</span>
</div>
