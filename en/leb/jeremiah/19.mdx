## The Valley of the Slaughter

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:11–12; Isaiah 30:14; Lamentations 4:2"> Thus said Yahweh, “Go and buy a potter’s earthenware jar, and take some of the elders of the people, and some of the leaders of the priests,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 15:8; 2 Kings 23:10; Jeremiah 7:31–32"> and go out to the Valley of Ben Hinnom, which is at the entrance of the Gate of the Potsherd, and proclaim there the words that I speak to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:11; Jeremiah 17:20; Isaiah 28:19"> And you shall say, ‘Hear the word of Yahweh, O kings of Judah and inhabitants of Jerusalem. Thus says Yahweh of hosts, the God of Israel: “Look, I am about to bring disaster upon this place so that ⸤everyone who hears it⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “all hears it”">a</a></sup>his ears will ring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:34; Deuteronomy 28:20; 2 Kings 21:16"> Because they have forsaken me, and they have defaced this place, and they have made smoke offerings in it to other gods whom they have not known, they, nor their ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “fathers”">b</a></sup>nor the kings of Judah, and they have filled up this place with the blood of the innocent,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:35; Deuteronomy 12:31; Leviticus 18:21"> and they have built the high places of Baal, to burn their children in the fire, burnt offerings to Baal, which I commanded not, and I ordered not, and it did not come to my ⸤mind⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “heart”">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 15:8; Isaiah 30:33; Jeremiah 19:2"> “⸤Therefore⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “To thus”">d</a></sup>look, days are about to come,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">e</a></sup>Yahweh, “when this place will no longer be called Topheth or the Valley of Ben Hinnom, ⸤but⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “surely if”">f</a></sup>the Valley of the Slaughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 26:17; Jeremiah 16:4; Psalm 33:10–11"> And I will lay waste the plans<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew “plan”">g</a></sup>of Judah and Jerusalem in this place, and I will bring them to ruin by the sword ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">h</a></sup>their enemies, and by the hand of those who seek their life, and I will give their dead bodies<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “dead body”">i</a></sup>as food to the birds<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Hebrew “bird”">j</a></sup>of heaven and to the animals<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Hebrew “animal”">k</a></sup>of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:16; Jeremiah 49:13; 1 Kings 9:8"> And I will make this city a horror, and an object of hissing, everyone who passes by it will be appalled, and will hiss because of all its wounds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 9:20; Leviticus 26:29; Lamentations 4:10"> And I will cause them to eat the flesh of their sons, and the flesh of their daughters, and each one will eat the flesh of his neighbor in the siege and in the distress which their enemies and those who seek their life inflict on them.” ’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:63–64; Jeremiah 48:12; Jeremiah 19:1"> “Then you shall break the jar before the eyes of the men who go with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 2:9; Isaiah 30:14; Lamentations 4:2"> And you shall say to them, ‘Thus says Yahweh of hosts: “So I will break this people and this city as one breaks the vessel of the potter, so that it is not able to be repaired again. And in Topheth they will bury ⸤until there is no room to bury⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “from there is no place to bury”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:13; Jeremiah 11:5"> Thus will I do to this place,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">m</a></sup>Yahweh, “and to its inhabitants, to make this city like Topheth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:29; 2 Kings 23:12; Zephaniah 1:5"> And the houses of Jerusalem and the houses of the kings of Judah will be unclean like the place of Topheth, all the houses where they made smoke offerings upon their roofs to all the host of heaven, and where they poured out libations to other gods.” ’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:2; 2 Chronicles 20:5; Jeremiah 19:2–3"> Then Jeremiah came from Topheth, where Yahweh had sent him to prophesy, and he stood in the courtyard of the house of Yahweh and said to all the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:26; Jeremiah 17:23; Nehemiah 9:29"> “Thus says Yahweh of hosts, the God of Israel, ‘Look, I am about to bring to this city and upon all its towns all the disaster that I have pronounced against it, because they have hardened their neck to not hear my words.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>
