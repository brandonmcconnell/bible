## The Linen Loincloth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:11; Hebrews 1:1; Jeremiah 27:2"> Thus Yahweh said to me: “Go and buy for yourself a loincloth of linen and put it on your loins, but you must not ⸤place⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “bring”">a</a></sup>it in water.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:8; John 15:14; Proverbs 3:5"> So I bought the loincloth according to the word of Yahweh and I put it<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Here the direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">b</a></sup>on my loins.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:8"> Now the word of Yahweh came to me a second time, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “to say”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 4:10; Psalm 137:1; Jeremiah 51:63–64"> “Take the loincloth that you bought, that is on your loins, and stand up, go to the Euphrates and hide it there in the cleft of the rock.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 40:16; Exodus 39:42–43; 2 Timothy 2:3"> So I went and hid it by the Euphrates just as Yahweh had commanded me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> ⸤And then⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “And it happened”">d</a></sup>⸤after a long time⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “from the end of many days”">e</a></sup>then Yahweh said to me, “Stand up, go to the Euphrates and take from there the loincloth that I commanded you to hide there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 3:12; Isaiah 64:6; Jeremiah 13:10"> Then I went to the Euphrates and I dug, and I took the loincloth from the place where I had hidden it, and look, the loincloth was ruined; it was not good for anything.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> ⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “And it happened”">f</a></sup>the word of Yahweh came to me, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “to say”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 26:19; Jeremiah 13:15–17; Zephaniah 3:11"> “Thus says Yahweh: ‘So I will ruin the pride of Judah and the great pride of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:12; Jeremiah 9:14; 2 Chronicles 36:15–16"> This evil people, who refuse to listen to my words, who go<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “walk”">h</a></sup>in the stubbornness of their hearts,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “heart”">i</a></sup>and have gone after other gods, to serve them, and to bow in worship to them, let them be like this loincloth which is not good for anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 19:5–6; Jeremiah 33:9; Psalm 81:11"> For as the loincloth clings to the loins of a person, so I caused to cling to me all the house of Israel and all the house of Judah,’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">j</a></sup>Yahweh, ‘to be for me a people, and a name, and a praise, and a glory, but they would not listen.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:19"> “Therefore, you shall say to them this word, ‘Thus says Yahweh, the God of Israel: “Every jar must be filled with wine.” ’ And they will say to you, ‘Do we not certainly know that every jar should be filled with wine?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 63:6; Psalm 75:8; Psalm 60:3"> Then you shall say to them, ‘Thus says Yahweh: “Look, I am about to fill all the inhabitants of this land, and the kings who sit on David’s throne, and the priests, and the prophets, and all the inhabitants of Jerusalem with drunkenness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:20; Psalm 2:9; Jeremiah 19:9–11"> And I will smash them, each one against his brother, and the fathers and the sons together,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">k</a></sup>Yahweh. “I will not have compassion, and I will not take pity, and I will not show compassion from destroying them.” ’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 28:14–22; Revelation 2:29; Acts 4:19–20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Listen, and pay attention,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you must not be haughty, for Yahweh has spoken.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:9; Isaiah 8:22; Isaiah 5:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">Give glory to Yahweh your God ⸤before⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “at not yet”">l</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he brings darkness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and ⸤before⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “at not yet”">m</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">your feet stumble on the mountains at twilight.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And you will hope for light, but he makes it as gloom,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he makes it</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Here the direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">n</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as thick darkness.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Malachi 2:2; Jeremiah 9:1; Jeremiah 14:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">But if you will not listen to it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">my inner self</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “soul”">o</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will weep in secret places,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because of ⸤the presence of⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “the face of”">p</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">your pride.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And my eyes</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Hebrew “eye”">q</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will weep bitterly,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will melt in tears,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because the flock of Yahweh has been taken captive.</span></span>
</div>
