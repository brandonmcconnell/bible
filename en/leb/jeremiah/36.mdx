## Jehoiakim Burns the Scroll of Jeremiah’s Prophecies

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:1; 2 Chronicles 36:5; Jeremiah 45:1"> ⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “And it was”">a</a></sup>in the fourth year of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah, the king of Judah, this word came to Jeremiah from Yahweh, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “to say”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:2; Jeremiah 25:3; Jeremiah 1:10"> “Take for yourself ⸤a scroll⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “a scroll of a scroll”">c</a></sup>and you must write on it all the words that I have spoken to you against Israel, and against Judah, and against all the nations, from the day that I spoke to you, from the days of Josiah, and until this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:8; Acts 3:19; Jeremiah 26:3"> Perhaps when the house of Judah hears all the disasters<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew “disaster”">d</a></sup>that I am planning to do to them, then they may turn back each one from his evil way, and I will forgive their guilt and their sin.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:12; Jeremiah 36:32; Ezekiel 2:9"> Then Jeremiah called Baruch the son of Neriah, and Baruch wrote from the mouth of Jeremiah all the words of Yahweh that he had spoken to him on ⸤a scroll⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “a scroll of a scroll”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:2; Jeremiah 33:1; 2 Corinthians 11:23"> And Jeremiah instructed Baruch, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “to say”">f</a></sup>“I am held back, I am not able to enter the temple of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:8–9; Jeremiah 26:2; Jeremiah 22:2"> So you must go and you shall read aloud from the scroll that you have written from my mouth the words of Yahweh in the hearing of the people in the temple of Yahweh on a day of fast, and also you shall read aloud in the hearing of all those of Judah who came from their towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:3; Jeremiah 21:5; 2 Chronicles 33:12–13"> Perhaps their plea will fall ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">g</a></sup>Yahweh and each one will turn away from his evil way, for great is the anger and wrath that Yahweh pronounced against this people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:17; 1 Corinthians 16:10; Luke 4:16–30"> And Baruch the son of Neriah did all that Jeremiah the prophet instructed him, to read aloud from the scroll the words of Yahweh in the temple of Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:3; Jonah 3:5; Esther 4:16"> ⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “And it was”">h</a></sup>in the fifth year of Jehoiakim, the son of Josiah, the king of Judah, in the ninth month, all the people in Jerusalem and all the people who came from the towns of Judah to Jerusalem proclaimed a fast ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">i</a></sup>Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:11; Jeremiah 26:10; Jeremiah 52:25"> Then Baruch read aloud from the scroll the words of Jeremiah in the temple of Yahweh, in the chamber of Gemariah, the son of Shaphan, the secretary, in the upper courtyard at the entrance of the New Gate of the temple of Yahweh in the hearing of all the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:10; 2 Chronicles 34:20; 2 Kings 25:22"> When Micaiah, the son of Gemariah, the son of Shaphan, heard all the words of Yahweh from the scroll,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:22; 2 Kings 22:12; Jeremiah 36:25"> he went down to the house of the king, to the chamber of the secretary, and look, all the officials were sitting there: Elishama the secretary, and Delaiah, the son of Shemaiah, and Elnathan, the son of Achbor, and Gemariah, the son of Shaphan, and Zedekiah, the son of Hananiah, and all the other officials.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 22:10; 2 Chronicles 34:24; 2 Chronicles 34:16–18"> And Micaiah told them all the words that he had heard at the reading aloud of Baruch from the scroll in the hearing of the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:2; Jeremiah 41:18; Jeremiah 41:1–2"> Then all the officials sent Jehudi, the son of Nethaniah, the son of Shelemiah, the son of Cushi, to Baruch, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “to say”">j</a></sup>“The scroll that you read aloud from in the hearing of the people, take it in your hand and come.” And Baruch the son of Neriah took the scroll in his hand and he came to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And they said to him, “Sit please and read it aloud in our hearing.” So Baruch read aloud in their hearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:18; Jeremiah 36:24; Amos 7:10–11"> ⸤And then⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “And it was”">k</a></sup>the moment of their hearing all the words, ⸤they turned to one another in alarm⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “they approached in trepidation each one to his neighbor”">l</a></sup>and they said to Baruch, “We must certainly report all these words to the king!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 9:15; John 9:10–11; John 9:26–27"> Then they asked Baruch, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “to say”">m</a></sup>“Tell us please, how did you write all these words, from his mouth?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:4; Jeremiah 36:2; Proverbs 26:4–5"> And Baruch said to them, “From his mouth. He dictated to me all these words and I was writing on the scroll with the ink.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 17:3; 1 Kings 18:10; Jeremiah 26:20–24"> Then the officials said to Baruch, “Go, hide yourself, you and Jeremiah, and let not a man know where you are.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:12; Jeremiah 36:21"> And they went to the king, to the courtyard, and they ⸤put⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “gave”">n</a></sup>the scroll for safe-keeping in the chamber of Elishama the secretary, and they reported all the words in the hearing of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 22:10; 2 Chronicles 34:18; Ezekiel 2:4–5"> Then the king sent Jehudi to take the scroll, and he took it from the chamber of Elishama the secretary, and Jehudi read it aloud in the hearing of the king, and in the hearing of all the officials who stood next to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Amos 3:15; Jeremiah 22:14–16; Jeremiah 36:9"> Now the king was sitting in the quarters of the winter in the ninth month, and a fire-pot was burning ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">o</a></sup>him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 22:8; Proverbs 21:30; 1 Kings 22:27"> ⸤And then⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “and it was”">p</a></sup>as Jehudi read three or four columns, he would cut it up in pieces with the knife of the scribe, and he would throw it<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Here the direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">q</a></sup>into the fire that was in the fire-pot until the whole of the scroll was consumed in the fire that was in the fire-pot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:16; Psalm 36:1; Jonah 3:6"> And the king and any of his servants who heard all these words were not startled, and they did not tear their garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 37:22; Jeremiah 36:12; Acts 5:34–39"> And even when Elnathan, and Delaiah, and Gemariah urged the king not to burn the scroll, he would not listen to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 17:3; 1 Kings 19:1–3; 1 Kings 19:14"> And the king commanded Jerahmeel, the son of the king, and Seraiah, the son of Azriel, and Shelemiah, the son of Abdeel, to arrest Baruch the secretary and Jeremiah the prophet, but Yahweh hid them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:4"> And the word of Yahweh came to Jeremiah after the king burned the scroll and the words that Baruch wrote from the mouth of Jeremiah, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “to say”">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Zechariah 1:5–6; Job 23:13; Jeremiah 44:28"> “⸤Take again⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “Turn back take”">s</a></sup>for yourself another scroll and write on it all the former words that were in the first scroll which Jehoiakim, the king of Judah, has burned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 30:10; Jeremiah 26:9; Deuteronomy 29:19"> And concerning Jehoiakim, the king of Judah, you shall say, ‘Thus says Yahweh, “You have burned this scroll, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “to say”">t</a></sup>‘Why have you written in it, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “to say”">u</a></sup>“The king of Babylon will certainly come and he will destroy this land, and he will cause to disappear from it humankind and animals”?’ ”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Hebrew “animal”">v</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:30; 2 Kings 24:12–15; Genesis 31:40"> ⸤Therefore⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “To thus”">w</a></sup>thus says Yahweh concerning Jehoiakim, the king of Judah, “There will not be for him one who sits on the throne of David. And his dead body will be thrown out to the heat in the day and to the frost in the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 29:1; Jeremiah 19:15; Jeremiah 35:17"> And I will punish him, and his offspring, and his servants for their guilt, and I will bring on them, and on the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and on the people of Judah all the disaster with which I have threatened them, but they would not listen.” ’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:4; Jeremiah 36:18; Exodus 4:15–16"> Then Jeremiah took another scroll and gave it to Baruch the son of Neriah, the secretary, and he wrote on it from the mouth of Jeremiah all the words of the scroll that Jehoiakim, the king of Judah, had burned in the fire, and furthermore was added to them many words like these.</span>
</div>
