## A Message for Zedekiah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:15; Daniel 2:37–38; 2 Kings 25:1–9"> The word that came to Jeremiah from Yahweh, when Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon, and all his army, with all the kingdoms of the earth under the dominion of his hand, and all the peoples were fighting against Jerusalem and against all its cities, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to say”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:10; Jeremiah 34:22; Jeremiah 37:8–10"> “Thus says Yahweh, the God of Israel: ‘Go and say to Zedekiah the king of Judah, now you must say to him, “Thus says Yahweh: ‘Look, I am going to give this city into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he will burn it with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:4; Jeremiah 21:7; 2 Kings 25:4–7"> And you will not escape from his hand, but surely you will be captured, and into his hand you will be given, and ⸤you will see the king of Babylon eye to eye⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “your eyes shall see with the eyes of the king of Babylon”">b</a></sup>and ⸤you will speak face to face with him⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “his mouth shall speak with your mouth”">c</a></sup>and to Babylon you will go.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> However, hear the word of Yahweh, O Zedekiah, the king of Judah. Thus says Yahweh concerning you: ‘You will not die by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:18; 2 Chronicles 16:14; 2 Kings 22:20"> In peace you will die, and as there was burning for your ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “fathers”">d</a></sup>the former kings who were ⸤before you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “to the face of you”">e</a></sup>so they will burn for you, and they will lament for you, “Alas, lord!” For the word I have spoken,’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">f</a></sup>Yahweh.” ’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:16–24; 1 Samuel 3:18; 1 Kings 21:19"> Then Jeremiah the prophet spoke to Zedekiah the king of Judah all these words in Jerusalem</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 10:3; Jeremiah 4:5; 2 Kings 18:13–14"> when the army of the king of Babylon was fighting against Jerusalem and against all the cities of Judah that were left over—Lachish and Azekah, for these remained among the cities of Judah, the cities of fortification.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Zedekiah’s Covenant Concerning Slaves

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 25:10; 2 Kings 23:2–3; 2 Kings 11:17"> The word that came to Jeremiah from Yahweh after king Zedekiah ⸤made⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “cut”">g</a></sup>a covenant with all the people who were in Jerusalem to proclaim release to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:12; Leviticus 25:39–46; Exodus 3:18"> to let go each one his male slave and each one his female slave, the Hebrew and the free Hebrew, so that no one among the Judeans<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “Judean”">h</a></sup>should enslave his fellow countryman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:16; Jeremiah 26:10; Jeremiah 38:4"> And all the officials and all the people obeyed, who had entered into the covenant to let go each one his male slave and each one his female slave, not enslaving them again, and they obeyed and they let them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Here the direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">i</a></sup>go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 2:20–22; Proverbs 26:11; Matthew 12:43–45"> But ⸤afterward⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “after thus”">j</a></sup>they turned back and they brought back the male slaves and the female slaves whom they had let go free, and they subdued them as male slaves and female slaves.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And the word of Yahweh came to Jeremiah from Yahweh, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “to say”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 6:8; Joshua 24:17; Deuteronomy 5:27"> “Thus says Yahweh, the God of Israel: ‘I ⸤made⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “cut”">l</a></sup>a covenant with your ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “fathers”">m</a></sup>on the day of my bringing them out from the land of Egypt, from the house of slaves, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “to say”">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:12; 1 Kings 9:22; 2 Kings 17:13–14"> “At the end of seven years you must let go each one his fellow countryman, the Hebrew who has been sold to you and who has served you six years, and you must let him go free from you.” But your ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “fathers”">o</a></sup>did not listen to me, and they did not incline their ears.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Hebrew “ear”">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:8; 2 Kings 23:3; Nehemiah 10:29"> And you turned back ⸤recently⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “the day”">q</a></sup>and you did right in my eyes, to proclaim release each one to his neighbor, and you ⸤made⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “cut”">r</a></sup>a covenant ⸤before me⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “to the face of me”">s</a></sup>in the house that is called ⸤by⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “on it”">t</a></sup>my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 19:12; Exodus 20:7; Jeremiah 34:11"> But you turned back and you profaned my name when you brought back each one his male slave and each one his female slave, whom you had let go free according to their desire, and you subdued them to be to you as male slaves and as female slaves.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Deuteronomy 28:25; Galatians 6:7"> “⸤Therefore⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “To thus”">u</a></sup>thus says Yahweh, ‘You have not listened to me to proclaim release each one to his fellow countryman and each one to his neighbor. Look, I am going to proclaim to you a release,’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">v</a></sup>Yahweh, ‘to the sword, to the plague, and to the famine, and I will make you a terror to all the kingdoms of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 15:10; Genesis 15:17–18; Hosea 6:7"> And I will make the men who transgressed my covenant, who have not kept the words of the covenant that they ⸤made⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “cut”">w</a></sup>⸤before me⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “to the face of me”">x</a></sup>like the calf which they cut in two and they passed between its parts—</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:10; Zephaniah 3:3–4; Daniel 9:6"> the officials of Judah, and the officials of Jerusalem, the eunuchs, and the priests, and all the people of the land who passed between the parts of the calf—</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:33; Jeremiah 16:4; Jeremiah 19:7"> and I will give them into the hand of their enemies, and into the hand of the seekers of their lives, and their dead bodies<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Hebrew “dead body”">y</a></sup>will become as food for the birds<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Hebrew “bird”">z</a></sup>of the heavens<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Or “sky”">aa</a></sup>and for the animals<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Hebrew “animal”">ab</a></sup>of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:24–27; Jeremiah 39:6; Jeremiah 52:10"> And Zedekiah the king of Judah and his officials I will give into the hand of their enemies, and into the hand of the seekers of their lives, and into the hand of the army of the king of Babylon, who retreated from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:11; Jeremiah 39:8; Jeremiah 39:1–2"> Look, I am going to command,’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">ac</a></sup>Yahweh, ‘and I will bring them back to this city, and they will fight against it, and they will capture it, and they will burn it with fire, and the towns of Judah I will make a desolation ⸤without⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “from there is not”">ad</a></sup>an inhabitant.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>
