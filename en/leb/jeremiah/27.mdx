## Jeremiah Uses Fetters and Yokes to Illustrate His Message of Submission

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:3; Jeremiah 28:1; Jeremiah 27:12"> In the beginning of the reign of Jehoiakim, the son of Josiah, the king of Judah, this word came to Jeremiah from Yahweh, ⸤saying⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to say”">a</a></sup>—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Amos 7:1; Amos 7:4; Jeremiah 27:12"> thus said Yahweh to me—“Make for yourself fetters and yokes and put them on your neck,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 1:9–3; Ezekiel 25:1–17; Ezekiel 17:15–21"> and send them to the king of Edom, and to the king of Moab, and to the king of the ⸤Ammonites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons of Ammon”">b</a></sup>and to the king of Tyre, and to the king of Sidon in the hand of the envoys who have come to Jerusalem, to Zedekiah, the king of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:19; Jeremiah 10:10; Jeremiah 25:27"> And you must command them for their masters, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “to say”">c</a></sup>‘Thus says Yahweh of hosts, the God of Israel, this you shall say to your masters:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:17; Jeremiah 51:15; Psalm 115:15–16"> “I have made the earth with humankind and animals<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew “animal”">d</a></sup>that are on the face of the earth by my great power and by my outstretched arm, and I give it to whomever is right in my eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 28:14; Jeremiah 25:9; Daniel 2:37–38"> And now I myself have given all these lands into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon, my servant, and also the animals<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew “animal”">e</a></sup>of the field I have given to him to serve him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:31; Revelation 13:5–10; Daniel 5:25–31"> And all the nations will serve him, and his son, and ⸤his grandson⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “the son of his son”">f</a></sup>until the coming of the time of ⸤his own⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “also his”">g</a></sup>land. Then many nations and great kings will let him work.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:21; Ezekiel 17:19–21; Jeremiah 38:17–19"> “But it will be that the nation or kingdom that will not serve him, Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon, and that will not put his neck under the yoke of the king of Babylon, I will punish that nation with the sword, and with the famine, and with the plague,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">h</a></sup>Yahweh, “until I have destroyed it with my hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:14; Malachi 3:5; Jeremiah 29:8"> And you, you must not listen to your prophets, and to your diviners, and to your dreamers, and to your interpreters of signs, and to your sorcerers who are ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “to say”">i</a></sup>‘You will not serve the king of Babylon.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:31; Jeremiah 28:16; Jeremiah 23:25"> For they are prophesying to you a lie, so that you will be removed from your land, and I will drive you away, and you will perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:8; Jeremiah 27:2; Jeremiah 38:2"> But the nation that will bring its neck under the yoke of the king of Babylon and will serve him, yet will I leave it on its land,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">j</a></sup>Yahweh, “and they will till it, and they will live in it.” ’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 28:1; Jeremiah 38:17; 2 Chronicles 36:11–13"> And I spoke words like these to Zedekiah, the king of Judah, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “to say”">k</a></sup>“Bring your necks under the yoke of the king of Babylon, and serve him and his people, and live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:31; Jeremiah 27:8; Proverbs 8:36"> Why should you die—you and your people—by the sword, by the famine, and by the plague, as Yahweh has spoken concerning the nation that will not serve the king of Babylon?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:14; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15; Jeremiah 29:8–9"> And you must not listen to the words of the prophets who are speaking to you, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “to say”">l</a></sup>‘You must not serve the king of Babylon,’ for they are prophesying a lie to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:10; 2 Chronicles 25:16; Jeremiah 6:13–15"> For I have not sent them,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">m</a></sup>Yahweh, “but they are prophesying in my name ⸤falsely⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “to the lie”">n</a></sup>so that I will drive them away. And you will perish—you and the prophets who are prophesying to you.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Vessels of the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 24:13; Jeremiah 28:3; Daniel 1:2"> Then I spoke to the priests and to all this people, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “to say”">o</a></sup>“Thus says Yahweh, ‘You must not listen to the words of your prophets who are prophesying to you, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “to say”">p</a></sup>“Look, the vessels of the house of Yahweh are about to be quickly brought back from Babylon”, for they are prophesying a lie to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:17; Jeremiah 38:23; Jeremiah 7:34"> And you must not listen to them; serve the king of Babylon and live. Why should this city become a site of ruins?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:8; Jeremiah 18:20; 1 Samuel 12:19"> But if they are prophets, and if there is with them the word of Yahweh, let them please plead with Yahweh of hosts, that the vessels that are left over in the ⸤temple⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “house”">q</a></sup>of Yahweh, and the house of the king of Judah, and in Jerusalem, must not go to Babylon.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 25:13; Jeremiah 52:17–23; 2 Kings 25:17"> For thus says Yahweh of hosts concerning the pillars, and concerning the sea, and concerning the stands, and concerning the rest of the vessels that are left in this city,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:1; 2 Chronicles 36:10; 2 Kings 24:14–16"> which Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon, did not take when he deported Jeconiah, the son of Jehoiakim, the king of Judah, from Jerusalem to Babylon, ⸤along with⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “and”">r</a></sup>all the nobles of Judah and Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> For thus says Yahweh of hosts, the God of Israel, concerning the vessels that are left in the house of Yahweh, and in the house of the king of Judah, and in Jerusalem:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:10; Ezra 7:19; Jeremiah 32:5"> ‘They will be brought to Babylon, and there they will stay until the day of my attending to them,’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">s</a></sup>Yahweh. ‘Then I will bring them up and restore them to this place.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>
