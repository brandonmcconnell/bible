## The Fortunes of Judah and Israel Will Be Restored

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:28; Jeremiah 37:21; Jeremiah 32:2–3"> And the word of Yahweh came to Jeremiah a second time while he was still held back in the courtyard of the guard, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to say”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 6:3; Exodus 15:3; Isaiah 37:26"> “Thus says Yahweh who made the earth,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Here the direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">b</a></sup>Yahweh who formed it to establish it, Yahweh is his name:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:12; Isaiah 65:24; Ephesians 3:20"> ‘Call to me, and I will answer you, and I will tell you great things and inaccessible things that you have not known.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:24; Habakkuk 1:10; Ezekiel 4:2"> For thus says Yahweh, the God of Israel, concerning the houses of this city and concerning the houses of the kings of Judah, that were torn down to make a defense against the siege ramps and against the sword:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 8:17; Jeremiah 21:10; Jeremiah 21:4–7"> ‘They are coming to fight against the Chaldeans, and to fill them with the corpses of the people whom I will strike in my anger and in my wrath, for whom I have hidden my face from this city because of all their wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 10:10; Isaiah 30:26; Titus 3:5–6"> Look, I am going to bring healing and health to it, and I will heal them, and I will reveal to them abundance of peace and reliability.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:26; Jeremiah 30:3; Amos 9:14–15"> And I will restore the fortunes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “fortune”">c</a></sup>of Judah and the fortunes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew “fortune”">d</a></sup>of Israel, and I will rebuild them as in the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Zechariah 13:1; Micah 7:18–19; Psalm 51:2"> And I will cleanse them from all their guilt that they sinned against me, and I will forgive all their iniquities that they sinned against me, and that they rebelled against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 126:2–3; Isaiah 60:5; Zephaniah 3:17–20"> And it will be to me a name of jubilation, a praise, and a glory before all the nations of the earth, who will hear all the good that I will do for them, and they will fear,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “tremble”">e</a></sup>and they will tremble because of all the good, and because of all the prosperity that I will provide for it.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:43; Jeremiah 32:36; Ezekiel 37:11"> “Thus says Yahweh: ‘Again will be heard in this place, of which you are saying, “It is a waste, ⸤without⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “from there is not”">f</a></sup>people and ⸤without⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “from there is not”">g</a></sup>animals,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “animal”">h</a></sup>in the towns of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem that are desolate, ⸤without⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “from there is not”">i</a></sup>people and ⸤without⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “from there is not”">j</a></sup>inhabitants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Hebrew “inhabitant”">k</a></sup>and ⸤without⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “from there is not”">l</a></sup>animals,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Hebrew “animal”">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:34; Psalm 106:1; Psalm 107:22"> the voice of jubilation, and the voice of joy, the voice of the bridegroom, and the voice of the bride, the voices<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Hebrew “voice”">n</a></sup>of those who say, “Praise Yahweh of hosts, for Yahweh is good, for his loyal love is ⸤forever⸥,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “to eternity”">o</a></sup>the voices of those who bring thank offerings<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Hebrew “thank offering”">p</a></sup>to the house of Yahweh, for I will restore the fortunes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Hebrew “fortune”">q</a></sup>of the land as in the beginning,’ says Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 65:10; Ezekiel 34:12–15; Jeremiah 36:29"> Thus says Yahweh of hosts: ‘Again there will be in this waste place, ⸤without⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “from there is not”">r</a></sup>people and animals,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Hebrew “animal”">s</a></sup>and in all its towns, pasture for shepherds allowing their flock to lie down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 27:32; Luke 15:4; Jeremiah 17:26"> In the towns of the hill country, in the towns of the Shephelah, and in the towns of the Negev, and in the land of Benjamin, and in the surroundings of Jerusalem, and in the towns of Judah, flocks will again pass under the hands of the counter,’ says Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Haggai 2:6–9; Jeremiah 23:5; 1 Chronicles 17:13–14"> ‘Look, days are coming,’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">t</a></sup>Yahweh, ‘and I will fulfill the good promise<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “word”">u</a></sup>that I promised to the house of Israel and to the house of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 11:1–5; Jeremiah 23:5–6; Isaiah 4:2"> In those days and in that time I will make a branch of righteousness sprout for David, and he will execute justice and righteousness in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:6; 1 Corinthians 1:30; Isaiah 45:17"> In those days Judah will be saved, and Jerusalem will dwell ⸤safely⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “with confidence”">v</a></sup>and this is what they shall call it: “Yahweh is our righteousness.” ’</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 2:4; Psalm 89:29–37; Luke 1:32–33"> For thus says Yahweh: ‘A man who sits on the throne of the house of Israel will not be cut off for David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; Revelation 1:6; Deuteronomy 18:1"> And for the priests, a man from the Levites who offers burnt offerings<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Hebrew “burnt offering”">w</a></sup>and who burns grain offerings<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Hebrew “grain offering”">x</a></sup>and who brings sacrifices<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Hebrew “sacrifice”">y</a></sup>will not be cut off ⸤before me⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “to the face of me”">z</a></sup>⸤forever⸥.’ ”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “all the days”">aa</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And the word of Yahweh came to Jeremiah, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “to say”">ab</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 8:22; Psalm 89:37; Isaiah 54:9–10"> “Thus says Yahweh: ‘If you could break my covenant with the day, and my covenant with the night, so that day and night would not come at their time,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 89:34; 2 Chronicles 7:18; 2 Samuel 23:5"> then my covenant could be broken with David my servant, from ⸤having⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “being for him”">ac</a></sup>a son who could rule on his throne, and with the Levites, the priests, my ministers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 15:5; Genesis 22:17; Hebrews 11:12"> As the host of heaven cannot be counted, and the sand of the sea cannot be measured, so I will make numerous the offspring of David my servant and the Levites who minister to me.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And the word of Yahweh came to Jeremiah, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “to say”">ad</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:2–4; Psalm 83:4; Psalm 44:13–14"> “Have you not seen how these people speak, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “to say”">ae</a></sup>‘The two clans<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Or “families”">af</a></sup>whom Yahweh chose, he has also rejected them’? Thus they spurn my people from being a nation ⸤before them⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “to the face of them”">ag</a></sup>any longer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalm 74:16–17; Jeremiah 31:35–36; Psalm 104:19"> Thus says Yahweh: ‘If my covenant with day and with night, the regulations of heaven and earth, I had not established,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:37; Isaiah 14:1; Hosea 2:23"> then the offspring of Jacob and David my servant I would reject, from choosing rulers from his offspring over the offspring of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob; for I will restore their fortunes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Hebrew “fortune”">ah</a></sup>and I will have compassion on them.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>
