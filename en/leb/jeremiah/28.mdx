## Hananiah, the False Prophet

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:1; Joshua 9:3; Isaiah 9:15"> And it was in that same year, at the beginning of the reign of Zedekiah, the king of Judah, in the fourth year, in the fifth month, that Hananiah, the son of Azzur, the prophet who was from Gibeon, said to me in the house of Yahweh before the eyes of the priests and all the people, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to say”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Ezekiel 13:5–16; Jeremiah 27:2–12"> “Thus says Yahweh of hosts, the God of Israel, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “to say”">b</a></sup>‘I have broken the yoke of the king of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 24:13; 2 Chronicles 36:10; Daniel 1:2"> ⸤Within two years⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “In still two years days”">c</a></sup>I will bring back to this place all the vessels of the house of Yahweh which Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon took away from this place and brought to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:24; 2 Kings 25:27–30; Jeremiah 28:2"> And Jeconiah the son of Jehoiakim, the king of Judah, and all the exiles<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew “exile”">d</a></sup>from Judah who went to Babylon, I will bring back to this place,’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">e</a></sup>Yahweh, ‘For I will break the yoke of the king of Babylon.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 28:1; Jeremiah 26:2; Jeremiah 19:14"> Then Jeremiah the prophet said to Hananiah the prophet before the eyes of the priests and before the eyes of all the people who were standing in the house of Yahweh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 41:13; 1 Kings 1:36; Jeremiah 11:5"> and Jeremiah the prophet said, “Amen! May Yahweh do so; may Yahweh fulfill your words that you have prophesied, to bring back the vessels of the house of Yahweh and all the exiles<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “exile”">f</a></sup>from Babylon to this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 22:28"> Nevertheless listen please to this word that I am speaking in your ⸤hearing⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “ears”">g</a></sup>and in the ⸤hearing⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “ears”">h</a></sup>of all the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:26–27; 1 Kings 17:1; 1 Kings 22:8"> The prophets who were ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">i</a></sup>me and ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">j</a></sup>you from ancient times prophesied against many countries and against great kingdoms of war, and of disaster, and of plague.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:22; Jeremiah 6:14; Jeremiah 4:10"> The prophet who prophesies peace, at the coming of the word of the prophet, will become known as the prophet that Yahweh has ⸤truly⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “in truth”">k</a></sup>sent.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:2; Jeremiah 28:4; Malachi 3:13"> Then Hananiah the prophet took the yoke from the neck of Jeremiah the prophet and broke it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 14:7; 2 Chronicles 18:22–23; Jeremiah 27:2–12"> Then Hananiah said before the eyes of all the people, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “to say”">l</a></sup>“Thus says Yahweh, ‘This is how I will break the yoke of Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon, ⸤within two years⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “in still two years days”">m</a></sup>from the neck of all the nations.’ ” And Jeremiah the prophet went on his way.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:2; Daniel 9:2; 2 Kings 20:4"> And the word of Yahweh came to Jeremiah after Hananiah the prophet broke the yoke from the neck of Jeremiah the prophet, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “to say”">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 149:8; Jeremiah 27:15; Lamentations 2:14"> “Go, and you must say to Hananiah, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “to say”">o</a></sup>‘Thus says Yahweh, “You have broken yoke bars of wood, but you have made in place of them yoke bars of iron.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:48; Jeremiah 27:6–7; Daniel 2:38"> For thus says Yahweh of hosts, the God of Israel, “I have put a yoke of iron on the neck of all these nations, to serve Nebuchadnezzar, the king of Babylon, and they will serve him, and I have even given the animals<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Hebrew “animal”">p</a></sup>of the open field to him.” ’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:14–15; Jeremiah 27:15; Jeremiah 23:21"> Then Jeremiah the prophet said to Hananiah the prophet, “Please listen, Hananiah, Yahweh has not sent you, and you have made this people trust in a lie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:32; Genesis 7:4; Exodus 32:12"> ⸤Therefore⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “To thus”">q</a></sup>thus says Yahweh, ‘Look, I am going to send you from the face of the earth. This year you will die because you have spoken rebellion against Yahweh.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zechariah 1:6; Isaiah 44:25–26"> And Hananiah the prophet died in that same year in the seventh month.</span>
</div>
</div>
