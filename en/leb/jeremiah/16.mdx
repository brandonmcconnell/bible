## Jeremiah is Commanded Not to Marry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:2; Jeremiah 2:1; Jeremiah 1:4"> And the word of Yahweh came to me, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to say”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:26–27; Luke 23:29; Genesis 19:14"> “You shall not take for yourself a wife, there shall not be for you sons and daughters in this place.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:9; Jeremiah 16:5; Jeremiah 6:21"> For thus says Yahweh concerning the sons and concerning the daughters born in this place, and concerning their mothers who gave birth to them, and concerning their fathers who fathered them in this land:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:33; Psalm 83:10; Jeremiah 9:22"> “They will die by diseases, they will not be mourned, and they will not be buried. They will be as dung on the face of the earth. And they will perish by the sword and they will perish by the famine. And their dead bodies<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew “dead body”">b</a></sup>will become food for the birds<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “bird”">c</a></sup>of the air, and for the wild animals<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew “wild animal”">d</a></sup>of the earth.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:16–23; Isaiah 27:11; Jeremiah 15:1–4"> For thus says Yahweh, “You must not enter the house of the funeral meal, and you must not go to lament, and you must not show sympathy for them, for I have taken away my peace from this people,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">e</a></sup>Yahweh, “the loyal love and the compassion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:1; Jeremiah 41:5; Jeremiah 47:5"> Both the great and small will die in this land. They will not be buried, and they will not mourn for them, and no one will cut himself with a blade, and no one will shave himself for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 9:4; Ezekiel 24:17; Deuteronomy 26:14"> And they will not break bread for the mourning ceremony for them, to comfort him because of the dead. And they will not give drink to them, the cup of desolation, for their fathers,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “father”">f</a></sup>or for their mothers.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew “mother”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:17; Ecclesiastes 7:2–4; Isaiah 22:12–14"> And you shall not enter the house of the banquet, to sit with them, to eat and drink.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:10; Jeremiah 7:34; Ezekiel 26:13"> For thus says Yahweh of hosts, the God of Israel, “Look, I am about to cause to disappear from this place, before your eyes and in your days, the voice of jubilation, and the voice of joy, and the voice of the bridegroom, and the voice of the bride.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:19; 1 Kings 9:8–9; Deuteronomy 29:24–25"> ⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “And it will happen”">h</a></sup>when you tell this people all these words and they ask you, ‘⸤Why⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “On what”">i</a></sup>has Yahweh pronounced against us all this great evil, and what is our iniquity, and what is our sin that we have sinned against Yahweh our God?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 106:35–41; Deuteronomy 29:25–26; Jeremiah 22:9"> Then you shall say to them, ‘⸤Because⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “On that”">j</a></sup>your ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “fathers”">k</a></sup>have forsaken me,’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">l</a></sup>Yahweh, ‘and they have gone after other gods, and they have served them, and they have bowed down in worship to them, and they have forsaken me, and they have not kept my laws.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:10; Jeremiah 7:26; Ecclesiastes 9:3"> And ⸤you have behaved badly⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “you have done evil to do”">m</a></sup>more than your ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “fathers”">n</a></sup>and look, you are going each one after the stubbornness of his evil heart, without listening to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:36; Deuteronomy 4:26–28; Jeremiah 15:14"> So I will throw you from this land to the land that you have not known, you or your ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “fathers”">o</a></sup>and there you will serve other gods day and night, for I will not show you favor.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Nation’s Return to the Land

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:7–8; Deuteronomy 15:15; Exodus 20:2"> ⸤Therefore⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “To thus”">p</a></sup>look, days are coming,’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">q</a></sup>Yahweh, ‘and it will no longer be said, “⸤As Yahweh lives⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “The life of Yahweh”">r</a></sup>who led up the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">s</a></sup>from the land of Egypt,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:18; Jeremiah 24:6; Jeremiah 32:37"> ⸤but only⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “but if”">t</a></sup>“⸤As Yahweh lives⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “The life of Yahweh”">u</a></sup>who led up the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">v</a></sup>from the land of the north, and from all the lands where he had driven them,” for I will bring them back to their ground that I gave to their ancestors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or “fathers”">w</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:14–15; Amos 4:2; Micah 7:2"> Look, I am sending for many fishermen,’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">x</a></sup>Yahweh, ‘and they will fish them out, and ⸤afterward⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “after thus”">y</a></sup>I will send for many hunters, and they will hunt them from every mountain, and from every hill, and from the clefts of the cliffs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 15:3; Jeremiah 23:24; Proverbs 5:21"> For my eyes are on all their ways, they are not hidden ⸤from before me⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “from to the face of me”">z</a></sup>and their iniquity is not concealed from before my eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 35:33–34; Isaiah 40:2; Revelation 18:6"> And ⸤I will first doubly repay⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “I will reward first second”">aa</a></sup>their iniquity and their sin because of their profaning my land with the dead bodies<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Hebrew “dead body”">ab</a></sup>of their abhorrences, and with their abominations they have filled up my inheritance.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 25:4; Jeremiah 17:17; Nahum 1:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">O Yahweh, my strength, and my stronghold,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and my refuge in the day of distress,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to you the nations will come from the ends of the earth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will say, “Our ancestors</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Or “fathers”">ac</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">have inherited only lies,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Hebrew “lie”">ad</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">vanity, and there is no profit in them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 37:19; Psalm 115:4–8; Galatians 4:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Can a human make for himself gods?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Yet they are not gods!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 9:16; Psalm 83:18; Jeremiah 33:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">“⸤Therefore⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “To thus”">ae</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">look, I am about to let them know,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">this time I am going to let them know</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">my ⸤power⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “hand”">af</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and my might,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will know that my name is Yahweh.”</span></span>
</div>
