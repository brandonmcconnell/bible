## Two Baskets of Figs

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:2; 2 Chronicles 36:10; 2 Kings 24:12–16"> Yahweh showed me, and look, there were two baskets of figs placed ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">a</a></sup>the temple of Yahweh—after Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon had deported Jeconiah the son of Jehoiakim, the king of Judah, with the officials of Judah, and the craftsmen, and the smiths,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew “smith”">b</a></sup>from Jerusalem and had brought them to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 5:7; Isaiah 5:4; Micah 7:1"> The one basket had very good figs, like ⸤early figs⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “the figs of the early ripened fruit”">c</a></sup>and the other basket had very bad figs that could not be eaten because of their bad quality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 8:2; Zechariah 5:5–11; Matthew 25:32–33"> And Yahweh asked me, “What are you seeing, Jeremiah?” And I said, “Figs—the good figs, very good, and the bad figs, very bad, that cannot be eaten because of their bad quality.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Then the word of Yahweh came to me, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “to say”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 13:9; Nahum 1:7; Galatians 4:9"> “Thus says Yahweh, the God of Israel, ‘Like these good figs, so I will regard as good the exiles<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew “exile”">e</a></sup>of Judah whom I have sent away from this place to the land of the Chaldeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:10; Jeremiah 29:10; Jeremiah 33:7"> For I will set my eyes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “eye”">f</a></sup>on them for good, and I will bring them back to this land. And I will build them and not annihilate them, and I will plant them and not uproot them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 8:10; Jeremiah 30:22; Ezekiel 36:24–28"> And I will give to them a heart to know me, that I am Yahweh, and ⸤they will be my people⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “they shall be for me as a people”">g</a></sup>and ⸤I will be their God⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “I will be for them as God”">h</a></sup>for they will return to me with the whole of their heart.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:1; Jeremiah 39:2–9; Jeremiah 24:2"> But like the bad figs that cannot be eaten because of their bad quality—for thus says Yahweh—so I will treat Zedekiah the king of Judah, and his officials, and the remnant of Jerusalem who remain in this land, and those who live in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:4; Jeremiah 29:18; Deuteronomy 28:37"> And I will make them as a terror, an evil to all the kingdoms of the earth, as a disgrace and a proverb, as a taunt and a curse, in all the places where I will drive them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 51:19; Ezekiel 5:12–17; Jeremiah 16:4"> And I will send among them the sword, the famine, and the plague, until they perish from the land that I gave to them and their ancestors.’ ”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “fathers”">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
