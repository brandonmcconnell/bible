## Jeremiah Escapes Death

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:4–5; Jeremiah 27:1; 2 Kings 23:34–36"> At the beginning of the reign of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah, the king of Judah, this word came from Yahweh, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to say”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:2; Acts 20:27; Matthew 28:20"> “Thus says Yahweh: ‘Stand in the courtyard of the house of Yahweh, and you must speak to all the cities of Judah that come to bow in worship in the house of Yahweh all the words that I command you to speak to them. You must not omit a word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:3; Isaiah 1:16–19; Jeremiah 18:7–10"> Perhaps they will listen and turn back each from his evil way, and I will relent of the disaster that I am planning to do to them because of their evil deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 9:6; Jeremiah 44:10; Isaiah 1:20"> And you shall say to them, “Thus says Yahweh: ‘If you will not listen to me, to walk in my law that I have set<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “given”">b</a></sup>⸤before you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “to the face of you”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:7; 2 Kings 9:7; Jeremiah 7:13"> to listen to the words of my servants the prophets whom I have sent to you, ⸤over and over again⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “and doing early and sending”">d</a></sup>though you have not listened,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:9; 2 Kings 22:19; Jeremiah 25:18"> then I will make this house like Shiloh, and this city I will make a curse for all the nations of the earth.’ ” ’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:31; Micah 3:11; Acts 5:17"> And the priests and the prophets and all the people heard Jeremiah speaking these words in the temple of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 4:13–14; Acts 7:52; Jeremiah 18:18"> ⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “And it happened”">e</a></sup>as Jeremiah finished speaking all that Yahweh had commanded him to speak to all the people, then the priests and the prophets and all the people laid hold of him, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “to say”">f</a></sup>“You will die!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 4:17–19; Acts 19:24–32; Micah 2:6"> Why have you prophesied in the name of Yahweh, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “to say”">g</a></sup>‘This house will be like Shiloh, and this city will be in ruins, ⸤without⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “from there is not”">h</a></sup>inhabitant’?” And all the people gathered around Jeremiah in the temple of Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:10; Jeremiah 38:4–6; Jeremiah 37:14–16"> When the officials of Judah heard these things, they came up from the house of the king to the temple of Yahweh, and they sat in the entrance of the New Gate of Yahweh’s temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:4; Matthew 26:66; Deuteronomy 18:20"> Then the priests and the prophets said to the officials and to all the people, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “to say”">i</a></sup>“⸤This man deserves the death sentence⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “A sentence of death is to this man”">j</a></sup>because he has prophesied against this city as that which you have heard with your ears.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Jeremiah 26:15; Acts 4:19"> Then Jeremiah said to all the officials and to all the people, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “to say”">k</a></sup>“Yahweh sent me to prophesy against this house and against this city all the words that you have heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jonah 3:9; Jonah 4:2; Isaiah 1:19"> Now ⸤therefore⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “so then”">l</a></sup>amend your ways and your deeds, and obey the voice of Yahweh your God, and Yahweh will relent of the disaster that he has spoken over you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 9:25; 2 Samuel 15:26; Jeremiah 38:5"> But as for me, look, I am in your hand, do to me what is good and right in your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 35:33; Proverbs 6:17; Matthew 26:25"> Only you must certainly know that if you put me to death, you will bring on yourselves innocent blood, and on this city and on its inhabitants, for ⸤truly⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “in truth”">m</a></sup>Yahweh sent me to you to speak all these words in your ears.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 5:34–39; Acts 23:29; Acts 25:25"> Then the officials and all the people said to the priests and to the prophets, “⸤This man does not deserve⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “Not to this man”">n</a></sup>a sentence of death, for in the name of Yahweh our God, he has spoken to us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 5:34; Micah 1:1"> Then men from the elders of the land arose and said to all the assembly of the people, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “to say”">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Micah 1:1; Nehemiah 4:2; Zechariah 8:3"> “Micah the Morashtite was prophesying in the days of Hezekiah, the king of Judah, and he said to all the people of Judah, ⸤saying⸥:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “to say”">p</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Thus says Yahweh of hosts,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Zion will be plowed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and Jerusalem will become a heap of ruins,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the mountain of the ⸤temple⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “house”">q</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">as high places of wood.” ’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 32:14; 2 Samuel 24:16; Isaiah 37:15–20"> Did Hezekiah, the king of Judah, and all Judah actually put him to death? Was he not in fear of Yahweh? And he entreated the face of Yahweh, and Yahweh relented of the disaster that he had spoken against them. But we are about to do great disaster to ourselves.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:2; Joshua 9:17; Joshua 15:60"> Indeed, there also was a man prophesying in the name of Yahweh, Uriah, the son of Shemaiah, from Kiriath-Jearim, and he prophesied against this city and against this land like all the words of Jeremiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 10:23; Matthew 14:5; Jeremiah 36:26"> And when King Jehoiakim, and all his warriors, and all the officials heard his words, then the king sought to put him to death. But Uriah heard, and he was afraid, and he fled and went to Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:12; 2 Kings 22:12; 2 Kings 22:14"> Then King Jehoiakim sent men to Egypt. Elnathan, the son of Achbor, and men with him went to Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:30; Revelation 11:7; Ezekiel 19:6"> And they brought out Uriah from Egypt and they brought him to King Jehoiakim, and he struck him down with the sword, and he threw his dead body into the burial sites of the sons of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:5–7; Jeremiah 39:14; Jeremiah 1:18–19"> However, the hand of Ahikam the son of Shaphan was with Jeremiah, so that he was not given into the hand of the people to put him to death.</span>
</div>
