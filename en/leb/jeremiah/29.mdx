## Jeremiah’s Letter to the Exiles in Babylon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esther 9:20; Acts 15:23; Hebrews 13:22"> And these are the words of the letter that Jeremiah the prophet sent from Jerusalem to the remainder of the exiles,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew “exile”">a</a></sup>and to the priests, and to the prophets, and to all the people whom Nebuchadnezzar had deported from Jerusalem to Babylon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:24–28; Jeremiah 27:20; 2 Chronicles 36:9–10"> after the going out of Jeconiah the king, and the queen mother, and the court officials, the leaders of Judah and Jerusalem, and the artisans,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew “artisan”">b</a></sup>and the smiths<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “smith”">c</a></sup>from Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:11; 2 Chronicles 34:20; Jeremiah 39:14"> by the hand of Elasah, the son of Shaphan, and Gemariah, the son of Hilkiah, whom Zedekiah, the king of Judah, sent to Nebuchadnezzar, the king of Babylon, to Babylon, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “to say”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:5; Isaiah 10:5–6; Isaiah 45:7"> “Thus says Yahweh of hosts, the God of Israel, to all the exiles<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew “exile”">e</a></sup>whom I have deported from Jerusalem to Babylon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:28; Jeremiah 29:10; Ezekiel 28:26"> ‘Build houses and live in them,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Here the direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">f</a></sup>and plant gardens and eat their fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 24:3–4; Genesis 24:51; Genesis 28:1–4"> Take wives and father sons and daughters, and take for your sons wives, and give your daughters to men that they may bear sons and daughters, and multiply there, and you must not be few.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:1–2; Ezra 6:10; 1 Peter 2:13–17"> And seek the prosperity of the city where I have deported you, and pray on behalf of it to Yahweh, for in its prosperity you will have prosperity.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 5:6; Jeremiah 5:31; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11"> For thus says Yahweh of hosts, the God of Israel, ‘Do not let your prophets who are in your midst, and your diviners, deceive you, and you must not listen to your dreams that you are causing them to dream.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:15; Jeremiah 29:31; Jeremiah 29:23"> For they are prophesying ⸤falsely⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “in a lie”">g</a></sup>to you in my name; I have not sent them,’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">h</a></sup>Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 9:2; Zechariah 7:5; Jeremiah 24:6–7"> For thus says Yahweh, ‘⸤As soon as the time has passed⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “When to the mouth of being full”">i</a></sup>seventy years<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Hebrew “year”">j</a></sup>for Babylon, I will attend to you, and I will fulfill my good word to you, to bring you back to this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 55:8–12; Psalm 33:11; Micah 4:12"> For I know the plans that I am planning concerning you,’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">k</a></sup>Yahweh, ‘plans for prosperity and not for harm, to give to you a future and a hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–8; Jeremiah 33:3; Psalm 50:15"> Then when you call me, and you come and pray to me, then I will listen to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:29–31; Psalm 119:2; Matthew 7:7"> When you search for me, then you will find me, if you seek me with all your heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:1–38; Jeremiah 30:3; 1 Chronicles 28:9"> And I will let myself be found by you,’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">l</a></sup>Yahweh, ‘and I will restore your fortunes,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Hebrew “fortune”">m</a></sup>and I will gather you from all the nations and from all the places to which I have driven you,’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">n</a></sup>Yahweh, ‘and I will bring you back to the place from which I deported you.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:8–9; Jeremiah 28:1–17; Ezekiel 1:3"> Because you have said, ‘Yahweh has raised up prophets for us in Babylon’—</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:2–3; Jeremiah 38:17–23; Ezekiel 17:12–21"> for thus says Yahweh concerning the king who sits on the throne of David and concerning all the people who live in this city, your fellow kinsmen who did not go with you into the exile—</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:8–10; Jeremiah 29:18; Jeremiah 24:1–3"> thus says Yahweh of hosts, ‘Look, I am going to send among them the sword, the famine, and the plague, and I will make them like rotten figs that cannot be eaten because of their bad quality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:8; Jeremiah 15:4; Jeremiah 42:18"> And I will pursue them with the sword, with the famine, and with the plague, and I will make them a terror to all the kingdoms of the earth, a curse, and a horror, and an object of hissing, and a disgrace among all the nations to which I have driven them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:19; Jeremiah 26:5; Jeremiah 7:24–26"> because they did not listen to my words,’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">o</a></sup>Yahweh, ‘when I sent to them my servants the prophets, ⸤sending over and over again⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “doing early and sending”">p</a></sup>and they would not listen,’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">q</a></sup>Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:5; Micah 4:10; Ezekiel 3:15"> And you, hear the word of Yahweh, all you exiles<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Hebrew “exile”">r</a></sup>whom I sent away from Jerusalem to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:14–15; Lamentations 2:14; Jeremiah 29:8–9"> Thus says Yahweh of hosts, the God of Israel, concerning Ahab, the son of Kolaiah, and concerning Zedekiah, the son of Maaseiah, who are prophesying to you in my name a lie, ‘Look, I am going to give them into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon, and he will strike them before your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 65:15; Daniel 3:6; Daniel 3:21"> And a curse will be taken up because of them by all the exiles<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Hebrew “exile”">s</a></sup>of Judah who are in Babylon, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “to say”">t</a></sup>“May Yahweh make you like Zedekiah and like Ahab, whom the king of Babylon roasted in the fire,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:14; Hebrews 4:13; Malachi 3:5"> because they have done a disgraceful thing in Israel, and they have committed adultery with the wives of their neighbors, and they have spoken words<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Hebrew “word”">u</a></sup>in my name, lies<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Hebrew “lie”">v</a></sup>that I have not commanded them, and I am he who knows, and I am a witness,’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">w</a></sup>Yahweh.”</span>
</div>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:3; Jeremiah 29:29; Jeremiah 52:24"> “Thus says Yahweh of hosts, the God of Israel, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “to say”">y</a></sup>‘Because you yourself sent a letter<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Hebrew “letters”">z</a></sup>in your name to all the people who are in Jerusalem, and to Zephaniah, the son of Maaseiah, the priest, and to all the priests, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “to say”">aa</a></sup></span>
