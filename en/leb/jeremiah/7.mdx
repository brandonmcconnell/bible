## Jeremiah’s Proclamation at the Gate of the House of Yahweh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The word that came to Jeremiah from Yahweh, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to say”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:2; Revelation 3:22; Amos 7:16"> “Stand in the gate of the house of Yahweh and proclaim there this word, and say, ‘Hear the word of Yahweh, all of Judah, those who enter through these gates to bow in worship to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:11; Jeremiah 26:13; Isaiah 1:16–19"> Thus says Yahweh of hosts, the God of Israel, “Make your ways and your deeds good and let me dwell with you in this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Matthew 3:9–10; Zephaniah 3:11"> Do not trust in ⸤the deceitful words⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “the words of the deception”">b</a></sup>⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “to say”">c</a></sup>‘The temple of Yahweh, the temple of Yahweh, they are the temple of Yahweh.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 6:12–13; Isaiah 1:19; Jeremiah 7:3"> For if you truly make your ways and your deeds good, if you truly do justice between a man and his neighbor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 22:21–24; Zechariah 7:9–12; Jeremiah 13:10"> you do not oppress the alien, the orphan, and the widow, you do not shed innocent blood in this place, and you do not go after other gods ⸤to your harm⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “to harm to you”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Jeremiah 25:5; Jeremiah 3:18"> then I will let you dwell in this place, in the land that I gave to your ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “fathers”">e</a></sup>⸤forever and ever⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “from eternity and until eternity”">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 28:15; Ezekiel 13:6–16; Jeremiah 23:14–16"> Look, you are relying for yourselves on ⸤deceitful words⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “the words of the deception”">g</a></sup>without benefiting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:6; Exodus 20:3; James 4:1–4"> Will you steal? Will you murder? And will you commit adultery? And will you swear ⸤falsely⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “to the lie”">h</a></sup>And will you make a smoke offering to the Baal? And will you go after other gods whom you have not known?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:34; Ezekiel 23:37; Ezekiel 23:39"> And then you come and you stand ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">i</a></sup>me in this house, which is called ⸤by my name⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “my name over it”">j</a></sup>and you say, ‘we are safe ⸤to go on doing⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “for the sake of the doing of”">k</a></sup>all of these detestable things.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:45–46; Isaiah 56:7; Matthew 21:13"> Has this house, which is called ⸤by my name⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “my name over it”">l</a></sup>become a cave of robbers in your eyes? Look, I, even I, have seen it,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Here the direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">m</a></sup>⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">n</a></sup>Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; Jeremiah 26:6; 1 Samuel 4:10–11"> “For go now to my place that was in Shiloh, where I caused my name to dwell in the beginning, and see what I did to it ⸤because of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “from face”">o</a></sup>the wickedness of my people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 65:12; 2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Jeremiah 7:25"> And now, because of your doing all these things,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">p</a></sup>Yahweh, “and ⸤I have spoken to you over and over again⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “I spoke to you doing early and speaking”">q</a></sup>and you have not listened, and I called you, and you have not answered,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 78:60; 2 Kings 25:9; Jeremiah 7:4"> therefore I will do to the house that is called ⸤by my name⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “my name over it”">r</a></sup>in which you are trusting, and to the place that I gave to you and to your ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “fathers”">s</a></sup>⸤what⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “as that”">t</a></sup>I did to Shiloh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:1; 2 Kings 17:23; Jeremiah 52:3"> And I will cast you ⸤out of my sight⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “from at my face”">u</a></sup>just as I cast out all of your blood relatives,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “brothers”">v</a></sup>all of the offspring of Ephraim.” ’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:14; Jeremiah 14:11–12; Jeremiah 15:1"> “And you, you must not pray for this people, and you must not lift up for them a cry of entreaty or a prayer, and you must not plead with me, for I will not hear you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:6–18; Ezekiel 14:23; Jeremiah 6:27"> Do you not see what they are doing in the towns of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:13; Jeremiah 44:17–19; 1 Corinthians 10:22"> The children are gathering wood, and the fathers are kindling the fire, and the women are kneading dough to make sacrificial cakes for the queen of heaven, and they pour out libations to other gods for the sake of provoking me to anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:11; Jeremiah 2:19; Daniel 9:7–8"> Are they provoking me to anger? ” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">w</a></sup>Yahweh, “Is it not themselves they hurt, for the sake of the shame of their faces?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:18; Lamentations 2:3–5; Isaiah 42:25"> ⸤Therefore⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “To thus”">x</a></sup>thus says the Lord Yahweh, “Look, my anger and my wrath will be poured out on this place, on the humankind, and on the animal, and on the tree of the field, and on the fruit of the ground, and it will not be extinguished.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hosea 8:13; Jeremiah 6:20; Amos 5:21–23"> Thus says Yahweh of hosts, the God of Israel, “Add your burnt offerings to your sacrifices, and eat the flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 6:6; 1 Samuel 15:22; Psalm 51:16–17"> For I did not speak with your ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Or “fathers”">y</a></sup>nor did I command them in the day of bringing them out from the land of Egypt, concerning the matter of burnt offering and sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 15:26; Deuteronomy 5:33; Exodus 19:5–6"> But I only commanded them this word, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “to say”">z</a></sup>‘Obey my voice, and I will be to you God, and you will be to me people, and you must walk in all of the way that I command you, so that it goes well with you.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalm 81:11–12; Jeremiah 8:5; Jeremiah 7:26"> Yet they did not obey, and they did not incline their ear, but they walked in their own plans, in the stubbornness of their evil heart, and ⸤they became worse instead of better⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “they were to backward and not to face”">aa</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:15; Jeremiah 25:4; Nehemiah 9:26"> From the day that your ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Or “fathers”">ab</a></sup>came out from the land of Egypt until this day I have sent to you all my servants the prophets, day after day, ⸤sending again and again⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “doing early and sending”">ac</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:12; 2 Chronicles 30:8; Jeremiah 7:24"> Yet they have not listened to me and they have not inclined their ear, but they have hardened their neck, they did more evil than their ancestors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Or “fathers”">ad</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:2; Jeremiah 1:7; Isaiah 65:12"> So you shall speak to them all these words, but they will not listen to you, and you shall call to them, but they will not answer you.</span>
</div>

## The Valley of the Slaughter

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 59:14–15; Proverbs 1:7; Isaiah 1:4–5"> “And you shall say to them, ‘This is the nation that has not obeyed the voice of Yahweh their God, and they have not accepted discipline. The truth has perished, and it is cut off from their mouths.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Hebrew “mouth”">ae</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 1:20; Micah 1:16; Jeremiah 6:30"> Shear your hair and throw it<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Here the direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">af</a></sup>away, and lift up a lament on the barren heights, for Yahweh has rejected and forsaken the generation of his wrath.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:34; Ezekiel 7:20; Daniel 9:27"> For the people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Or “children”">ag</a></sup>of Judah have done evil in my eyes,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">ah</a></sup>Yahweh, “They have set their abominations in the house that is called ⸤by my name⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Literally “my name over it”">ai</a></sup>to defile it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Kings 23:10; Jeremiah 32:35; Leviticus 18:21"> And they built the high place of Topheth, which is in the Valley of Ben Hinnom, to burn their sons and their daughters in the fire, which I have not commanded, and it did not come into my mind.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Or “heart”">aj</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:11; Jeremiah 19:6; 2 Kings 23:10"> Therefore look, days are about to come,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">ak</a></sup>Yahweh, “and it will no longer be called Topheth or the Valley of Ben Hinnom, but the Valley of the Slaughter, and they will bury in Topheth ⸤until there is no more place⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Literally “from there is no place”">al</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:9; Deuteronomy 28:26; Revelation 19:17–18"> And the dead bodies<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Hebrew “body”">am</a></sup>of this people will be as food for the birds<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Hebrew “bird”">an</a></sup>of the heavens,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Or “sky”">ao</a></sup>and for the animals<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Hebrew “animal”">ap</a></sup>of the earth, without anyone to scare them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Here the direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">aq</a></sup>away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Revelation 18:23; Hosea 2:11; Ezekiel 26:13"> And I will cause to disappear from the towns of Judah and from the streets of Jerusalem the sound of jubilation, and the sound of joy, the voice of the bridegroom, and the voice of the bride, for the land will become a site of ruins.</span>
</div>
