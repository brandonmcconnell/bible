## The Massacre of the Mourners

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Psalm 55:23; Ezekiel 22:27"> ⸤And then⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “And it was”">o</a></sup>the moment of their coming to the middle of the city, then Ishmael the son of Nethaniah, ⸤along with⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “and”">p</a></sup>the men who were with him, slaughtered<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="The Hebrew verb is singular">q</a></sup>them and threw them to the middle of the pit.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “cistern”">r</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 6:2; 1 Kings 15:17–22; 1 Samuel 13:6"> Now the pit<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Or “cistern”">t</a></sup>into which Ishmael threw all the corpses of the men whom he had killed ⸤along with⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “by the hand of”">u</a></sup>Gedaliah was the same one that King Asa had made because of Baasha the king of Israel, who was against him. </div>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> ⸤And then⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “And it was”">y</a></sup>the moment that all the people who were with Ishmael saw Johanan the son of Kareah and all the commanders of the armies who were with him, they were glad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> So all the people whom Ishmael had taken captive from Mizpah turned around and returned and went to Johanan the son of Kareah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 21:30; Proverbs 28:17; 1 Kings 20:20"> But Ishmael the son of Nethaniah escaped with eight men ⸤from⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “from the face of”">z</a></sup>Johanan, and they went to the ⸤Ammonites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “sons of Ammon”">aa</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:4–7; Jeremiah 42:8; Jeremiah 41:10"> Then Johanan the son of Kareah and all the commanders of the armies who were with him took from Mizpah all the rest of the people whom he had recovered from Ishmael the son of Nethaniah, after he had killed Gedaliah the son of Ahikam, strong men, ⸤soldiers⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “the men of the war”">ab</a></sup>and women, and little children, and eunuchs whom he brought back from Gibeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:37–38; Jeremiah 42:14; Jeremiah 43:7"> And they set out and stayed at the lodging place of Chimham that is near Bethlehem, ⸤intending⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “going”">ac</a></sup>to go to Egypt</span>
