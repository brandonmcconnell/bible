## A Message for Baruch

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:1; Jeremiah 36:4; Jeremiah 32:12"> The word that Jeremiah the prophet spoke to Baruch, the son of Neriah, at his writing these words on a scroll from the mouth of Jeremiah, in the fourth year of Jehoiakim, the son of Josiah, the king of Judah, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to say”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 2:18; Isaiah 63:9; 2 Corinthians 7:6"> “Thus says Yahweh, the God of Israel, to you, O Baruch:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:16; 2 Corinthians 4:1; Galatians 6:9"> ‘You said, “Woe please to me, for Yahweh has added sorrow to my pain. I am weary in my groaning and I do not find rest.” ’</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:7–10; Jeremiah 1:10; Jeremiah 31:28"> Thus you will say to him: ‘Thus says Yahweh, “Look, what I have built I am about to tear down, and what I have planted I am about to pluck up, it is all the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:18; Matthew 6:25–32; Jeremiah 21:9"> And you, do you seek great things for yourself? Do not seek great things,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Here the direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">b</a></sup>for look, I am about to bring disaster upon all flesh,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">c</a></sup>Yahweh, “but to you I will give your life as booty in all the places where you may go.” ’ ”</span>
</div>
