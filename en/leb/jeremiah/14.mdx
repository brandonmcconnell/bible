## Lying Prophets

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:16; Jeremiah 11:14; Exodus 32:32–34"> So Yahweh said to me, “You must not pray for this people, for their happiness.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “good”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:11; Ezekiel 8:18; Proverbs 1:28"> Although they fast, I am not listening to their cry of entreaty. And although they present burnt offering and grain offering, I am not favorable to them. But by the sword, and by the famine, and by the plague, I am about to destroy them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:14; Jeremiah 4:10; 2 Peter 2:1"> Then I said, “Ah, Lord Yahweh, look, the prophets are saying to them, ‘You will not see the sword, and famine will not be yours, but reliable peace I will give to you in this place.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:6–7; Ezekiel 12:24; Jeremiah 29:8–9"> And Yahweh said to me, “The prophets are prophesying lies<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “lie”">h</a></sup>in my name. I have not sent them, and I have not commanded them, and I have not spoken to them. They are prophesying to you a lying vision, and a divination, worthless, and the deceitfulness of their ⸤minds⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “heart”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:12–13; Ezekiel 14:10; Jeremiah 23:14–15"> ⸤Therefore⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “To thus”">j</a></sup>thus says Yahweh concerning the prophets who prophecy in my name though I have not sent them, yet they are saying, “Sword and famine will not be in this land.” “By the sword and by the famine those prophets will perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:33; Proverbs 1:31; Jeremiah 15:2–3"> And the people to whom they are prophesying will be cast out into the streets of Jerusalem ⸤because of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “from the face of”">k</a></sup>the famine and the sword. And there will be no one who buries them, their wives, or their sons, or their daughters. And I will pour out on them their wickedness.</span>
</div>
