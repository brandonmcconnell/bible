## The Broken Covenant

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The word that came to Jeremiah from Yahweh, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to say”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:6; Exodus 19:5; 2 Chronicles 29:10"> “Hear the words of this covenant, and speak to the ⸤people⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “man”">b</a></sup>of Judah and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:26; Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Deuteronomy 29:19–20"> and say to them, ‘Thus says Yahweh, the God of Israel: “The man is cursed who does not obey the words of this covenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:12; Jeremiah 7:22–23; Deuteronomy 4:20"> which I commanded your ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “fathers”">c</a></sup>in the day of my bringing them from the land of Egypt, from the furnace of iron, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “to say”">d</a></sup>‘Listen to my voice and do according to all that I command you, ⸤so will you be my people, and I will be your God⸥.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “and you shall be to me as people and I will be to you as God”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:12–13; Psalm 105:9–11; Jeremiah 28:6"> in order to keep the oath that I swore to your ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “fathers”">f</a></sup>to give them a land flowing with milk and honey, as it is this day.” ’ ” Then I answered and said, “⸤Let it be so⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “Amen”">g</a></sup>O Yahweh.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 1:22; Romans 2:13; Jeremiah 7:2"> And Yahweh said to me, “Proclaim all these words in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “to say”">h</a></sup>‘Hear the words of this covenant and do them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:15; Ephesians 4:17; 1 Samuel 8:9"> for I solemnly admonished your ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “fathers”">i</a></sup>in the day of my leading them out from the land of Egypt and up to this day, ⸤admonishing persistently⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “do early and admonishing”">j</a></sup>⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “to say”">k</a></sup>“Obey my voice.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:24; Jeremiah 7:26; Jeremiah 35:15"> But they did not obey and they did not incline their ear. Instead each one walked in the stubbornness of his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Hebrew “their”">l</a></sup>evil heart. Therefore, I brought on them all the words of this covenant which I commanded them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Here the direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">m</a></sup>to do, and they did not do it.’ ”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Here the direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">n</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 6:9; Matthew 26:3–4; Micah 3:11"> And Yahweh said to me, “A conspiracy is found among the ⸤people⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “man”">o</a></sup>of Judah and among the inhabitants of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:16; Ezekiel 16:59; Psalm 78:8–10"> They have turned back to the iniquities of their former ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “fathers”">p</a></sup>who refused to obey my words, and they have gone after other gods, to serve them. The house of Israel and the house of Judah have broken my covenant that I ⸤made⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “cut”">q</a></sup>with their ancestors.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “fathers”">r</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:18; Proverbs 1:28; Isaiah 1:15"> ⸤Therefore⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “To thus”">s</a></sup>thus says Yahweh, “I am about to bring on them disaster from which they will not be able to escape. Though they cry out to me, yet I will not listen to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Deuteronomy 32:37; 2 Chronicles 28:22"> Then the cities of Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem will go and cry out to the gods to whom they are making smoke offerings, but they will certainly not save them in the time of their trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; 2 Kings 23:13; Isaiah 2:8"> For the number of your towns are your gods, O Judah, and the number of the streets of Jerusalem are the altars you have set up to the shameful things, to make smoke offerings to Baal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:16; Jeremiah 11:11; 1 John 5:16"> Therefore you must not pray for this people, and you must not lift up for them a cry of entreaty, or a prayer, for I will not listen in the time of their calling to me, in the time of their trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 2:14; Titus 1:15; Jeremiah 3:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">⸤What rights has my beloved in my house⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “What for my beloved in my house”">t</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when she has done many wicked things?</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Hebrew “wicked thing”">u</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Can the flesh of holiness pass over you?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">For ⸤you engage in your wickedness⸥,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “your wickedness”">v</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">then you exult.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 11:17–24; Isaiah 27:11; Psalm 52:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh called your name, ‘A leafy olive tree, ⸤beautiful with fine fruit⸥.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “beautiful of fruit of appearance”">w</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">With the sound of a great storm he will set fire to it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and its branches will be good for nothing.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 5:2; Jeremiah 2:21; Jeremiah 12:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">And Yahweh of hosts, the one who planted you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">has spoken evil against you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because of the evil of the house of Israel and the house of Judah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that they have done to themselves,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to provoke me to anger by making smoke offerings to Baal.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## A Warning for the People of Anathoth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:11–12; 2 Kings 6:9–10; Ezekiel 8:6–18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Moreover, Yahweh let it be made known to me, and I knew.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Then you showed me their deeds.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7–8; Jeremiah 20:10; Psalm 83:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I was like a gentle ram-lamb that is brought to slaughter,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I did not know that they planned plans against me, saying,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Let us destroy the tree with its fruit,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and let us cut him off from the land of the living,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so that his name will no longer be remembered.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:12; Jeremiah 17:10; 1 Samuel 16:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">But O Yahweh of hosts, who judges in righteousness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who tests the ⸤inmost being⸥,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “kidney”">x</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the ⸤mind⸥,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “heart”">y</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">let me see your retribution upon them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for to you I have revealed my legal case.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:10; Amos 2:12; Luke 13:33–34"> ⸤Therefore⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “To thus”">z</a></sup>thus says Yahweh concerning the people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Or “men”">aa</a></sup>of Anathoth who seek your life, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “to say”">ab</a></sup>“You shall not prophesy in the name of Yahweh ⸤or⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “and not”">ac</a></sup>you will die by our hand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:21; 2 Chronicles 36:17; Jeremiah 9:21"> ⸤Therefore⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “To thus”">ad</a></sup>thus says Yahweh of hosts: “Look, I am about to punish them. The young men will die by the sword. Their sons and their daughters will die by the famine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:12; Micah 7:4; Hosea 9:7"> And a remnant will not be left for them, for I will bring disaster to the people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Or “men”">ae</a></sup>of Anathoth, the year of their punishment.”</span>
</div>
</div>
