## The Faithfulness of the Rechabites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 23:35; Jeremiah 1:3; Jeremiah 25:1"> The word that came to Jeremiah from Yahweh in the days of Jehoiakim, the son of Josiah, the king of Judah, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to say”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:55; 1 Chronicles 9:26; 1 Kings 6:5–6"> “Go to the house of the Rechabites, and speak with them, and bring them to the house of Yahweh into one of the chambers, and give them wine to drink.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> So I took Jaazaniah, the son of Jeremiah, the son of Habazziniah, and his brothers, and all his sons, and the whole of the house of the Rechabites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:1; 2 Kings 12:9; 2 Chronicles 8:14"> and I brought them to the house of Yahweh, into the chamber of the sons of Hanan, the son of Igdaliah, the man of God, which was beside the chamber of the officials, which was above the chamber of Maaseiah, the son of Shallum, the keeper of the threshold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:7; Amos 2:12; 2 Corinthians 2:9"> Then I ⸤set⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “gave”">b</a></sup>⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">c</a></sup>the house of the Rechabites bowls full of wine and cups, and I said to them, “Drink wine.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 1:15; 1 Chronicles 2:55; 2 Kings 10:15"> But they answered, “We will not drink wine, for Jonadab, the son of Rechab, our ancestor,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “father”">d</a></sup>commanded us, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “to say”">e</a></sup>‘You shall not drink wine, you or your children, ⸤forever⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “until eternity”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 35:10; Genesis 25:27; Nehemiah 8:14–16"> And you shall not build a house, and you shall not sow seed, and you shall not plant a vineyard, and it shall not be for you, but in tents you shall live all your days, so that you may live many days on the surface of the land where you are dwelling as aliens.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Colossians 3:20; Proverbs 4:10; Proverbs 1:8–9"> And we obeyed the voice of Jonadab, the son of Rechab, our ancestor,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “father”">g</a></sup>in all that he commanded us, not to drink wine all our days, ourselves, our wives, our sons, or our daughters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:6; Jeremiah 35:7; Psalm 37:16"> and not to build houses for our living, and there is not vineyard, or field, or seed for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> But we have lived in tents, and we have obeyed, and we have done all that Jehonadab, our ancestor,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “father”">h</a></sup>commanded us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:14; Jeremiah 4:5–7; Daniel 1:1–2"> ⸤But then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “And it was”">i</a></sup>at the coming up against the land of Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon, then we said, ‘Come and let us go to Jerusalem ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="“from the face of”">j</a></sup>the army of the Chaldeans, and ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="“from the face of”">k</a></sup>the army of the Arameans.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Hebrew “Aramean”">l</a></sup>That is why we are living in Jerusalem.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And the word of Yahweh came to Jeremiah, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “to say”">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:33; Jeremiah 5:3; Jeremiah 6:8–10"> “Thus says Yahweh of hosts, the God of Israel: ‘Go and say to the people of Judah and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem, “Can you not learn a lesson to listen to my words?” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">n</a></sup>Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:3–4; Jeremiah 7:24–26; Proverbs 1:20–33"> “The words of Jonadab, the son of Rechab, that he commanded his descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “children”">o</a></sup>to not drink, have been carried out, and they have not drunk until this day, for they have obeyed the command of their ancestor.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “father”">p</a></sup>But ⸤I have spoken to you over and over again⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “I have spoken to you doing early and speaking”">q</a></sup>and you have not listened to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:11; Ezekiel 18:30–32; Jeremiah 25:5–6"> And I have sent to you all my servants the prophets, ⸤I have sent them over and over again⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “doing early and sending”">r</a></sup>⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “to say”">s</a></sup>‘Turn back please each one from his evil way, and amend your deeds, and you must not go after other gods to serve them, ⸤so that you may live⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “and live”">t</a></sup>on the land that I gave to you and to your ancestors.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “fathers”">u</a></sup>But you did not incline your ear and you did not listen to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Malachi 1:6; Jeremiah 35:14; Luke 15:11–13"> For the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “children”">v</a></sup>of Jonadab, the son of Rechab, have carried out the command of their ancestor<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or “father”">w</a></sup>that he commanded them, but this people did not listen to me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 66:4; Isaiah 65:12; Romans 10:21"> ⸤Therefore⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “To thus”">x</a></sup>thus says Yahweh, the God of hosts, the God of Israel, “Look, I am going to bring to Judah and to all the inhabitants of Jerusalem every disaster that I have spoken against them, because I have spoken to them and they have not listened, and I have called to them and they have not answered.” ’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:16; Exodus 20:12; Ephesians 6:1–3"> But to the house of the Rechabites Jeremiah said, “Thus says Yahweh of hosts, the God of Israel, ‘Because you have listened to the command of Jonadab your ancestor,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Or “father”">y</a></sup>and you have kept all his commands, and you have done all that he commanded you,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:19; Luke 21:36; Jeremiah 33:17–18"> ⸤therefore⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “to thus”">z</a></sup>thus says Yahweh of hosts, the God of Israel: ‘A man will not be cut off for Jonadab, the son of Rechab, to stand ⸤before me⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “to the face of me”">aa</a></sup>⸤always⸥.’ ”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “all the days”">ab</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
