## A Message for Zedekiah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:25; Jeremiah 37:3; Jeremiah 52:24"> The word that came to Jeremiah from Yahweh when King Zedekiah sent to him Pashhur, the son of Malchiah, and Zephaniah, the son of Maaseiah, the priest, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to say”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:3; Jeremiah 42:4–6; Jeremiah 37:7"> “Please inquire of Yahweh on behalf of us, for Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon is fighting against us. Perhaps Yahweh will do with us according to all his miraculous acts, so that he may go up from against us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Then Jeremiah said to them, “Thus you shall say to Zedekiah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:5; Jeremiah 37:8–10; Isaiah 5:5"> ‘Thus says Yahweh, the God of Israel: “Look, I am about to turn back the weapons of war that are in your hands,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew “hand”">b</a></sup>with which you are fighting against them, the king of Babylon and the Chaldeans who are laying siege to you from outside the wall, and I will gather them into the center of this city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 63:10; Exodus 6:6; Isaiah 5:25"> And I myself will fight against you with outstretched hand, and with strong arm, and in ⸤anger⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “nose”">c</a></sup>and in fury, and in great wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:3; Ezekiel 14:13; Jeremiah 12:3–4"> And I will strike the inhabitants of this city, both humankind and animal; by a great plague they will die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:17; 2 Kings 25:5–7; Habakkuk 1:6–10"> And ⸤afterward⸥,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “after thus”">d</a></sup>⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">e</a></sup>Yahweh, “I will give Zedekiah, the king of Judah, and his servants, and the people, and those who remain in this city from the plague, from the sword, and from the famine, into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar, the king of Babylon, and into the hand of their enemies, and into the hand of those who seek their lives, and he will strike them with the ⸤edge⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “mouth”">f</a></sup>of the sword. He will not take pity on them, and he will not have compassion, nor will he show compassion.” ’</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Way of Life and the Way of Death

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:19; Deuteronomy 30:15; Deuteronomy 11:26"> “And to this people you shall say, ‘Thus says Yahweh, “Look, I am setting ⸤before you⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “to the face of you”">g</a></sup>the way of life and the way of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:2; Jeremiah 45:5; Jeremiah 39:18"> He who stays in this city will die by the sword, or by the famine, or by the plague. And he who goes out and goes over to the Chaldeans who are laying siege to you will live, and his life will be to him as booty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:11; Amos 9:4; Jeremiah 44:27"> For I have set my face against this city for evil and not for good,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">h</a></sup>Yahweh. It will be given into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he will burn it with fire.” ’</span>
</div>
</div>
