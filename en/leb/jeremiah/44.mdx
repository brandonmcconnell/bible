## A Message of Rebuke for the Rebellious Remnant

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:14; Isaiah 11:11; Exodus 14:2"> The word that came to Jeremiah concerning all the Judeans who were living in the land of Egypt, who were living at Migdol, and Tahpanhes, and Memphis, and in the land of Pathros, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to say”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 6:11; Jeremiah 9:11; Jeremiah 34:22"> “Thus says Yahweh of hosts, the God of Israel: ‘You yourselves have seen all the disaster that I have brought on Jerusalem, and on all the towns of Judah. And look, they are a site of ruins this day, and there is not in them an inhabitant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:26; Deuteronomy 13:6; Deuteronomy 32:17"> ⸤because of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “from the face of”">b</a></sup>their wickedness that they committed to provoke me to anger by going to make smoke offerings, serving other gods whom they had not known, neither they, you, or your ancestors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “fathers”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:5; Jeremiah 7:25; Jeremiah 7:13"> And I sent to you all my servants the prophets, ⸤sending over and over again⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “doing early and sending”">d</a></sup>⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “to say”">e</a></sup>“Please, you must not do this detestable thing that I hate.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:10; Jeremiah 19:13; 2 Chronicles 36:16"> But they did not listen and they did not incline their ears<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “ear”">f</a></sup>to turn back from their wickedness, to not make smoke offerings to other gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:20; Jeremiah 21:5; Isaiah 51:17"> So my wrath and my anger were poured out<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="The Hebrew verb is singular">g</a></sup>and burned<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="The Hebrew verb is singular">h</a></sup>in the towns of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem, and they became as a site of ruins, as a desolation, as they are this day.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:10; Jeremiah 51:22; Ezekiel 33:11"> So then, thus says Yahweh, the God of hosts, the God of Israel, ‘Why are you doing great harm to ⸤yourselves⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “your lives”">i</a></sup>to cut off for yourselves man and woman, child and suckling, from the midst of Judah, to not leave over for yourselves a remnant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:18; Jeremiah 25:6–7; Jeremiah 44:3"> provoking me to anger with the works of your hands, to make smoke offerings to other gods in the land of Egypt where you have come to dwell as aliens, so as to cut off yourselves, and so that you are becoming as a curse, and as a disgrace among all the nations of the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:17–18; Joshua 22:17–20; Jeremiah 44:15–19"> Have you forgotten the wicked things of your ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “fathers”">j</a></sup>and the wicked things of the kings of Judah, and the wicked things of their wives, and your wicked things, and the wicked things of your wives, which they committed in the land of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 28:14; 1 Peter 5:6; Revelation 15:4"> They have not shown contrition up to this day, and they have not shown reverence, and they have not walked in my law and in my statutes that I set ⸤before you⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “to the face of you”">k</a></sup>and ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">l</a></sup>your ancestors.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “fathers”">m</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:10; Leviticus 26:17; Leviticus 20:5–6"> ⸤Therefore⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “To thus”">n</a></sup>thus says Yahweh of hosts, the God of Israel: ‘Look, ⸤I am determined to bring disaster on you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “I am setting my face against you for disaster”">o</a></sup>even to exterminate all Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:15–18; Jeremiah 42:22; Isaiah 65:15"> And I will take away the remnant of Judah ⸤who are determined⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “who set their faces”">p</a></sup>to come to the land of Egypt to dwell as aliens there, and everyone will perish. They will fall in the land of Egypt by the sword; they will perish by the famine, from the smallest to the greatest. By the sword and by the famine they will die and become as a curse, as a horror, and as a curse, and as a disgrace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:22; Jeremiah 43:11; Jeremiah 44:27–28"> So I will punish those who live in the land of Egypt just as I have punished Jerusalem, with the sword, with the famine, and with the plague.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 9:27; Jeremiah 22:26–27; Jeremiah 42:17"> And there will not be a survivor or an escapee of the remnant of Judah, those who have come to dwell as an alien there in the land of Egypt, to return to the land of Judah where they are ⸤longing⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “lifting up their soul”">q</a></sup>to return to dwell there, for they will not turn back, ⸤but only⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “but if”">r</a></sup>some survivors.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 11:21; Jeremiah 5:1–5; Genesis 19:4"> Then all the men who knew that their wives were making smoke offerings to other gods, and all the women who stood by, a great assembly, and all the people who lived in the land of Egypt in Pathros, answered Jeremiah, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “to say”">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 21:14–15; Luke 19:27; Jeremiah 16:15–17"> “Concerning the word that you have spoken to us in the name of Yahweh, we are not going to listen to you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:18; Nehemiah 9:34; Jeremiah 44:21"> But certainly we will do ⸤everything⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “all the word”">t</a></sup>that went out from our mouths,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Hebrew “mouth”">u</a></sup>to make smoke offerings to the queen of heaven and to pour out to her libations just as we did, we, and our ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “fathers”">v</a></sup>our kings, and our officials, in the towns of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem, for then ⸤we had enough to eat⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “we satisfied ourselves with food”">w</a></sup>and we were well-off, and we did not see disaster.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Malachi 3:13–15; Numbers 11:5–6; Job 21:14–15"> But ⸤since⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “from”">x</a></sup>we stopped making smoke offerings to the queen of heaven and pouring out libations to her, we have lacked everything, and by the sword and by the famine we have perished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:18; Jeremiah 44:15; 1 Kings 21:25"> And indeed, when we were making smoke offerings to the queen of heaven and ⸤pouring out⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “to pour out”">y</a></sup>to her libations, was it not ⸤with the consent of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “from apart from”">z</a></sup>our husbands that we made for her sacrificial cakes ⸤marked with her image⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “to the copying of her”">aa</a></sup>and we poured out to her libations?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Then Jeremiah said to all the people, to the men and to the women, and to all the people who answered him a word, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “to say”">ab</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:17; Jeremiah 44:9; Jeremiah 11:13"> “The offering that you made, smoke offerings in the towns of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem, you, and your ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Or “fathers”">ac</a></sup>your kings, and your officials, and the people of the land, did not Yahweh remember them, and did it not come to his ⸤mind⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “heart”">ad</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:18; Isaiah 7:13; Malachi 2:17"> And Yahweh was no longer able to bear<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Or “endure”">ae</a></sup>it, ⸤because of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “from the face of”">af</a></sup>the evil of your deeds, ⸤because of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “from the face of”">ag</a></sup>the detestable things that you committed. Thus your land became as a site of ruins, and as a horror, and as a curse, ⸤without⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “from there is not”">ah</a></sup>inhabitants, as it is this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 9:9; Daniel 9:11–12; Nehemiah 13:18"> ⸤Because of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Literally “From the face of”">ai</a></sup>the fact that you made smoke offerings, and that you sinned against Yahweh, and you did not listen to the voice of Yahweh, and you have not walked in his law, and in his statute, and in his legal provisions, ⸤therefore⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Literally “unto thus”">aj</a></sup>this disaster has happened to you, as it is this day.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:7; Jeremiah 44:15–16; Jeremiah 42:15"> Then Jeremiah said to all the people and to all the women, “Hear the word of Yahweh, all Judah who are in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 14:9; Acts 23:12–15; Isaiah 28:15"> Thus says Yahweh of hosts, the God of Israel, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Literally “to say”">ak</a></sup>‘You and your wives have promised with your mouths<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Hebrew “mouth”">al</a></sup>and with your hands<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Hebrew “hand”">am</a></sup>you have fulfilled, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Literally “to say”">an</a></sup>“We certainly will carry out our vows that we vowed, to make smoke offerings to the queen of the heavens,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Hebrew “heaven”">ao</a></sup>and to pour out to her libations.” You must certainly keep your vows and you must certainly carry out your vows.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalm 50:16; Ezekiel 20:39; Hebrews 6:13"> ⸤Therefore⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Literally “To thus”">ap</a></sup>hear the word of Yahweh, all Judah, those who live in the land of Egypt: ‘Look, I hereby swear by my great name,’ says Yahweh, ‘surely my name will no longer be called in the mouth of any man of Judah, saying, “⸤as the Lord Yahweh lives⸥,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Literally “the life of Lord Yahweh”">aq</a></sup>in all the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:28; 2 Kings 21:14; Jeremiah 1:10"> Look, I am going to watch over them for harm and not for good, and all people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Or “men”">ar</a></sup>of Judah who are in the land of Egypt will perish by the sword and by the famine until their perishing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:14; Isaiah 27:12–13; Isaiah 10:19"> And the survivors of the sword will return from the land of Egypt to the land of Judah ⸤few in number⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Literally “males of number”">as</a></sup>And all the remnant of Judah who have come to the land of Egypt to dwell as aliens there will know whose word will endure, from me or from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Matthew 24:15–16; Jeremiah 44:30"> And this will be to you the sign,’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-at" id="fnref-at" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">at</a></sup>Yahweh, ‘that I am going to punish you in this place, so that you may know that surely my words will endure against you for evil.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 25:4–7; Jeremiah 43:9–13; Jeremiah 34:21"> Thus says Yahweh: ‘Look, I am going to give Pharaoh Hophra, the king of Egypt, into the hand of his enemies, and into the hand of those who seek his life, just as I gave Zedekiah the king of Judah into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon, his enemy and the one who sought his life.’ ”</span>
</div>
