## The Fall of Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:1–2; Jeremiah 52:4–7; 2 Kings 25:1–12"> In the ninth year of Zedekiah, the king of Judah, in the tenth month, Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon and all his army came against Jerusalem and laid siege to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:6–7; Micah 2:12–13; 2 Kings 25:3–4"> In the eleventh year of Zedekiah, in the fourth month, on the ninth day of the month, the city was taken by assault.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:4; Jeremiah 38:17; Jeremiah 39:13"> And all the officials of the king of Babylon came and sat in the Middle Gate: Nergal-sharezer, Samgar-nebo, Sarsechim the chief officer, Nergal-sharezer the high official, with all the rest of the officials of the king of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 2:14; 2 Chronicles 32:5; 2 Kings 25:4–7"> ⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “And it was”">a</a></sup>⸤when⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “as that”">b</a></sup>Zedekiah the king of Judah saw them, and all ⸤the soldiers⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “the men of the war”">c</a></sup>with him, they fled and went out at night from the city by the way of the garden of the king through the gate between the walls. And they went out toward the Jordan Valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 23:33; Joshua 5:10; Jeremiah 52:26–27"> But the army of the Chaldeans pursued after them and overtook Zedekiah in the plains of Jericho. And they took him and brought him up to Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon at Riblah in the land of Hamath. And he pronounced ⸤sentence⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “judgments”">d</a></sup>on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:10; Jeremiah 34:19–21; Jeremiah 24:8–10"> And the king of Babylon slaughtered the sons of Zedekiah at Riblah before his eyes. The king of Babylon also slaughtered all the nobles of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; 2 Kings 25:7; Jeremiah 52:11"> Then he blinded the eyes of Zedekiah and tied him up with bronze fetters to bring him to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:3; Jeremiah 38:18; Jeremiah 21:10"> And the Chaldeans burned the palace<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “house”">e</a></sup>of the king and the houses<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “house”">f</a></sup>of the people with fire and broke down the walls of Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:1; Genesis 37:36; Jeremiah 10:18"> Then the rest of the people who were left in the city, and those deserting who had deserted to him, and the rest of the people who remained, Nebuzaradan, the captain of the guard,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew “guards”">g</a></sup>deported to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 25:12; Jeremiah 40:7; Jeremiah 52:16"> And some of the poor people, ⸤who had nothing⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “who there was not for them something”">h</a></sup>Nebuzaradan, the captain of the guard,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “guards”">i</a></sup>left in the land of Judah. And he gave them vineyards and fields on that day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 24:23; Jeremiah 15:21; Job 5:19"> And Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon commanded concerning Jeremiah ⸤through⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “in the hand of”">j</a></sup>Nebuzaradan, the captain of the guard,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Hebrew “guards”">k</a></sup>⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “to say”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Jeremiah 40:4; Proverbs 21:1"> “Take him and set your eyes on him. And you must not do something bad to him, ⸤but only⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “but if”">m</a></sup>that which he speaks to you, so do with him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:3; Jeremiah 39:9"> So Nebuzaradan, the captain of the guard,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Hebrew “guards”">n</a></sup>sent word, ⸤along with⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “and”">o</a></sup>Nebushazban the chief officer, and Nergal-sharezer the high official, and all the chief officers of the king of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:28; 2 Kings 22:12; Jeremiah 26:24"> And they sent and took Jeremiah from the courtyard of the guard and gave him to Gedaliah the son of Ahikam, the son of Shaphan, to take him to the house, so he stayed in the midst of the people.</span>
</div>

## Ebed-melech is Rewarded for His Faith

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:9; Jeremiah 32:1–2; Jeremiah 36:1–5"> And the word of Yahweh came to Jeremiah at his confinement in the courtyard of the guard, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “to say”">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 9:12; Zechariah 1:6; Joshua 23:14–15"> “Go and say to Ebed-melech the Cushite, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “to say”">q</a></sup>‘Thus says Yahweh of hosts, the God of Israel: “Look, I am about to bring my words to pass against this city for evil and not for good. And they will be ⸤before you⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “to the face of you”">r</a></sup>on that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 41:1–2; Psalm 91:14–15; Psalm 50:15"> But I will rescue you on that day,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">s</a></sup>Yahweh, “and you will not be given into the hand of the men ⸤of whom you are frightened⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “who you are frightened from the face of them”">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:2; Psalm 34:22; Jeremiah 21:9"> For surely I will save you, and you will not fall by the sword. But your life will be for you as booty because you have trusted in me,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">u</a></sup>Yahweh.’ ”</span>
</div>
