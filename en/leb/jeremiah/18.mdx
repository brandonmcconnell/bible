## The Potter and the Clay

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The word that came to Jeremiah from Yahweh, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to say”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 1:1; Acts 9:6; Jeremiah 19:1–2"> “Stand up and go down to the house of the potter, and there I will let you hear my words.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 15:14; Jonah 1:3; Acts 26:19"> So I went down to the house of the potter and look, there he was doing work at the potter’s wheels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 9:20–23; Jeremiah 18:6; Isaiah 45:9"> And the vessel that he was making with the clay was spoiled in the hand of the potter, so ⸤he made again⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “he turned back and he made it”">b</a></sup>another vessel, as it was right in the eyes of the potter to make.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the word of Yahweh came to me, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “to say”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; Romans 9:20–21; Jeremiah 18:4"> “Like this potter, am I not able to do to you, O house of Israel?” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">d</a></sup>Yahweh. “Look, like the clay in the hand of the potter, so are you in my hand, O house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:10; Jeremiah 12:14–17; Jeremiah 45:4"> One moment I speak concerning a nation or concerning a kingdom, to uproot, and to tear down, and to destroy it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Here the direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:21; Jeremiah 26:13; Jonah 3:9–10"> But if that nation turns back from its evil that I have threatened against it, then I will relent concerning the disaster that I planned to do to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:28; Amos 9:11–15; Jeremiah 1:10"> And the next moment I speak concerning a nation or concerning a kingdom, to build, and to plant it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Here the direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:18; 1 Samuel 2:30; Ezekiel 18:24"> But if it does evil in my sight, to not listen to my voice, then I will relent concerning the good that I said I would do to it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 17:13; Isaiah 55:6–7; Jeremiah 35:15"> “So now then, say, please, to the ⸤people⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “man”">g</a></sup>of Judah and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “to say”">h</a></sup>‘Thus says Yahweh, “Look, I am preparing evil against you, and I am planning a plan against you. Please turn back, each one from his evil way, and walk rightly in your ways and your deeds.” ’</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:25; Jeremiah 7:24; Deuteronomy 29:19"> But they will say, ‘It is hopeless, for we will go after our own plans, and each one of us will act according to the stubbornness of his evil heart.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:30; Hosea 6:10; Jeremiah 23:14"> “Therefore thus says Yahweh,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Please ask among the nations, “Who has heard ⸤the like of this⸥?”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “like these”">i</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">The virgin of Israel has done something very horrible.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 6:68"> <span className="pl pl-1">Does the snow of Lebanon leave from the crags</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Hebrew “crag”">j</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">of Sirion?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Or are the cold waters flowing from distant mountains dried up?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:16; Jeremiah 2:32; Hosea 11:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">But my people have forgotten me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They make smoke offerings to the idols,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Hebrew “idol”">k</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they caused them to stumble in their ways, the ancient ways,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to go into ⸤bypaths⸥,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “paths of way”">l</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">not one that is built up.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 22:7; Jeremiah 25:9; Jeremiah 19:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">To make their land a horror,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">an object of whistling for eternity.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">All who pass by it will be appalled,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he will shake his head.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:24; Jeremiah 2:27; Job 27:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Like the wind from the east</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will scatter them ⸤before⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">m</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the enemy.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will show them my back and not my face</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the day of their disaster.’ ”</span></span>
</div>

## Jeremiah’s Imprecatory Prayer

<div className="bp poetry">
