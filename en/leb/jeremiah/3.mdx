## Yahweh Gives Israel a Letter of Divorce

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 17:7–17; Ezekiel 16:31; Jeremiah 7:24"> Then Yahweh said to me in the days of Josiah, the king, “Have you seen what apostate<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “unfaithful”">i</a></sup>Israel has done? <span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:46; Hosea 6:1–4; Hosea 14:1"> And I thought, ‘After her doing all these things to me she will return,’ but she did not return.
