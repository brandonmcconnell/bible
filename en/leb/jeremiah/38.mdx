## Jeremiah is Thrown into the Pit of Malchiah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:9; Ezra 2:3; Acts 4:6–10"> Now Shephatiah the son of Mattan, and Gedaliah the son of Pashhur, and Jehucal the son of Shelemiah, and Pashhur the son of Malchiah heard the words that Jeremiah was saying to all the people, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to say”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:18; Jeremiah 34:17; Jeremiah 45:5"> “Thus says Yahweh, ‘The one who stays in this city will die by the sword, by the famine, and by the plague. But the one who goes out to the Chaldeans will live. And his life will be for him as booty, and he will live.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:10; Jeremiah 32:3–5"> Thus says Yahweh, ‘Surely this city will be given into the hand of the army of the king of Babylon, and he will capture it.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:11; Acts 16:20; Nehemiah 6:9"> Then the officials said to the king, “Please, this man must be killed, ⸤because⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “unto thus”">b</a></sup>he is making slack the hands of ⸤the soldiers⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “the men of the war”">c</a></sup>who are left in this city, and the hands of all the people, by speaking to them words like these, for this man is not seeking for welfare<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “peace”">d</a></sup>to this people, ⸤but only⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “but if”">e</a></sup>for harm.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 19:12–16; 2 Samuel 3:39; 2 Samuel 19:22"> And Zedekiah the king said, “Look, he is in your hand, for the king is not able to do a thing against you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:21; Jeremiah 38:22; Jeremiah 37:16"> So they took Jeremiah and threw him into the pit<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “cistern”">f</a></sup>of Malchiah, the son of the king, which was in the courtyard of the guard. And they let Jeremiah down by ropes. Now in the pit<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “cistern”">g</a></sup>there was no water,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “waters”">h</a></sup>⸤but only⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “but if”">i</a></sup>mud, and Jeremiah sank in the mud.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:19; Amos 5:10; Jeremiah 29:2"> When Ebed-melech the Cushite, ⸤a eunuch⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “a man of a eunuch”">j</a></sup>who was in the house of the king, heard that they had put Jeremiah into the pit<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “cistern”">k</a></sup>—now the king was sitting at the Gate of Benjamin—</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Ebed-melech went out from the house of the king and spoke to the king, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “to say”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:21–6; Esther 7:4–6; Proverbs 31:8–9"> “My lord the king, these men have done evil in all that they have done to Jeremiah the prophet, in that they have thrown him into the pit,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “cistern”">m</a></sup>and he will die ⸤there⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “in it”">n</a></sup>⸤because of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “from the face of”">o</a></sup>starvation, for there is no longer any bread in the city.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 21:1; Esther 8:7; Esther 5:2"> Then the king commanded Ebed-melech the Cushite, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “to say”">p</a></sup>“Take ⸤with you⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “in your hand”">q</a></sup>these thirty men and pull Jeremiah the prophet up from the pit<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “cistern”">r</a></sup>⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “at not yet”">s</a></sup>he dies.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> So Ebed-melech took the men ⸤with him⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “in his hand”">t</a></sup>and went to the palace of the king, to a place beneath the storehouse, and he took from there ⸤rags⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “the rags of rags”">u</a></sup>and ⸤worn-out clothes⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “rags of ragged clothes”">v</a></sup>And he let them down into the pit<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or “cistern”">w</a></sup>by ropes to Jeremiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 12:10; Ephesians 4:32; Romans 12:15"> Then Ebed-melech the Cushite said to Jeremiah, “Please put ⸤the rags⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “rags of the rags”">x</a></sup>and ⸤worn-out clothes⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “ragged clothes”">y</a></sup>under the joints of your arms under the ropes,” and Jeremiah did so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:21; Acts 28:16; Acts 23:35"> And they pulled Jeremiah by the ropes and brought him up from the pit.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “cistern”">z</a></sup>And Jeremiah stayed in the courtyard of the guard.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:17; 1 Kings 22:16; Jeremiah 21:1–2"> And Zedekiah the king sent and made someone bring Jeremiah the prophet to him, to the third entrance that was at the ⸤temple⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “house”">aa</a></sup>of Yahweh. And the king said to Jeremiah, “I am asking you ⸤something⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “a thing”">ab</a></sup>you must not hide ⸤anything⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “a thing”">ac</a></sup>from me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 22:67–68"> And Jeremiah said to Zedekiah, “If I tell you, will you not surely kill me? ⸤Besides⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “And”">ad</a></sup>if I advise you, you will not listen to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:17; Jeremiah 38:1–6; Isaiah 57:16"> So Zedekiah the king swore to Jeremiah in secret, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “to say”">ae</a></sup>“⸤As Yahweh lives⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “Life of Yahweh”">af</a></sup>who has made for us this life, I will not kill you and I will not give you into the hand of these men who are seeking your life.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:12; Jeremiah 27:17; Jeremiah 38:2"> Then Jeremiah said to Zedekiah, “Thus says Yahweh, the God of hosts, the God of Israel, ‘If only you will go surrender to the officials of the king of Babylon, then ⸤you⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “your life” or “your soul”">ag</a></sup>will live, and this city will not be burned with fire, and you will live, you and your house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:3; Jeremiah 24:8–10; 2 Kings 25:4–10"> But if you do not go surrender to the officials of the king of Babylon, then this city will be given into the hand of the Chaldeans, and they will burn it with fire, and you will not escape from their hand.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 12:42; Jeremiah 38:22; John 19:12–13"> And Zedekiah the king said to Jeremiah, “I am afraid of the Judeans who have deserted to the Chaldeans, lest they give me into their hand and they abuse me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 4:27; Isaiah 55:3; Jeremiah 26:13"> And Jeremiah said, “They will not give you over. Please obey the voice of Yahweh with regard to what I am saying to you, and it will go well with you, and ⸤you⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “your life” or “your soul”">ah</a></sup>will live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:7; Ezekiel 2:4–5; Jeremiah 26:15"> But if you are refusing to surrender, this is the thing that Yahweh has shown me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:6; Lamentations 1:2; Lamentations 5:11"> Now look, all the women who remain in the house of the king of Judah are being led out to the officials of the king of Babylon. And look, they are saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘⸤Your trusted friends⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Literally “The men of your peace”">ai</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">have misled you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they have prevailed against you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Your feet are stuck in the mud,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so they turned backward.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 41:10; Jeremiah 39:6; Jeremiah 38:18"> And all your wives and your children will be led out to the Chaldeans, and you will not escape from their hand, but by the hand of the king of Babylon you will be seized, and this city will burn with fire.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And Zedekiah said to Jeremiah, “No man must know about these words so that you will not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:27; Jeremiah 38:4–6"> And if the officials hear that I have spoken with you, and they come to you and say to you, ‘Please tell us what you said to the king. You must not conceal it from us so that we will not kill you. And what did the king say to you?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:20; Jeremiah 37:15; Esther 4:8"> Then you shall say to them, ‘I was presenting my plea ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">aj</a></sup>the king, to not cause me to return to the house of Jonathan to die there.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:15–16; 2 Kings 6:19; Acts 23:6"> And all the officials came to Jeremiah and questioned him, and he informed them like all these words that the king commanded. So they fell silent, for the ⸤conversation⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Literally “word”">ak</a></sup>was not heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:21; Jeremiah 39:14; Psalm 23:4"> And Jeremiah stayed in the courtyard of the guard until the day that Jerusalem was captured. And it happened that Jerusalem was captured.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Jeremiah 38:28b in the Hebrew Bible is 39:1a in the English Bible">al</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
