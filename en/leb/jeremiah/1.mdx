## Yahweh Appoints Jeremiah as a Prophet to the Nations

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:60; Jeremiah 11:21; 2 Chronicles 36:21"> The words of Jeremiah, the son of Hilkiah, among the priests who were in Anathoth, in the land of Benjamin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 13:20; Jonah 1:1; Jeremiah 1:11"> to whom the word of Yahweh came in the days of Josiah the son of Amon, the king of Judah, in the thirteenth year of his reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:2; 2 Chronicles 36:5–8; 2 Kings 23:34"> It came also in the days of Jehoiakim, the son of Josiah, the king of Judah, until the end of the eleventh year of Zedekiah, the son of Josiah, the king of Judah, until the exile of Jerusalem in the fifth month.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:16; Ezekiel 1:3; Jeremiah 1:2"> And the word of Yahweh came to me, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to say”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 139:16; Isaiah 49:1; Galatians 1:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Before I formed you in the womb I knew you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and before you came out from the womb I consecrated you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I appointed you as a prophet to the nations.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 6:12; Exodus 4:10–16; 1 Kings 3:7–9"> Then I said, “Ah, Lord Yahweh! Look, I do not know how to speak, for I am a youth.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “boy”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 16:15–16; Ezekiel 3:27; Numbers 22:20"> But Yahweh said to me, “You must not say, ‘I am a youth,’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “boy”">c</a></sup>for to ⸤whomever⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “all of whom”">d</a></sup>I send you, you will go, and ⸤whatever⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “all of that”">e</a></sup>I command you, you will speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Joshua 1:9; Isaiah 51:7"> ⸤Do not be afraid of them⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “you must not be afraid from the faces of them”">f</a></sup>for I am with you to deliver you,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">g</a></sup>Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Exodus 4:11–12; Luke 12:12"> Then Yahweh stretched out his hand and he touched my mouth, and Yahweh said to me,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Look, I have put my words in your mouth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:28; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Isaiah 44:26–28"> <span className="pl pl-1">See, I appoint you this day over the nations</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and over the kingdoms,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to pluck up and to pull down,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and to destroy and to tear down,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to build and to plant.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Yahweh Confirms Jeremiah’s Call

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Amos 7:8; Amos 8:2; Zechariah 5:2"> And the word of Yahweh came to me, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “to say”">h</a></sup>“What are you seeing, Jeremiah?” And I said, “I am seeing a branch of an almond tree.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:28; Ezekiel 12:25; Ezekiel 12:22–23"> Then Yahweh said to me, “You did well to see, for I am watching over my word to perform it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:3; Ezekiel 11:7; Zechariah 4:2"> And the word of Yahweh came to me a second time, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “to say”">i</a></sup>“What are you seeing?” And I said, “I am seeing a ⸤boiling⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “blown upon”">j</a></sup>pot, and its face is from the face of the north.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 41:25; Jeremiah 4:6; Jeremiah 10:22"> Then Yahweh said to me, “From the north disaster will be unleashed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “opened”">k</a></sup>on all the inhabitants of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:10; Jeremiah 25:9; Jeremiah 9:11"> For, look, I am calling to all the clans<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “families”">l</a></sup>of the kingdoms of the north,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">m</a></sup>Yahweh, “and they will come and set each one his throne at the entrance of the gates of Jerusalem, and against all its surrounding walls, and against all the cities of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:9; Jeremiah 19:4; Acts 7:41"> And I will pass<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “pronounce”">n</a></sup>my judgments on them because of all their wickedness in that they have forsaken me, and they made sacrifices to other gods, and they bowed in worship to the works of their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:7–8; 1 Kings 18:46; 1 Peter 1:13"> But you, you must gird your loins, and stand, and speak to them all that I command you. ⸤Do not be afraid of them⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “You must not be filled with terror from the faces of them”">o</a></sup>or I will shatter you ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “to the faces of”">p</a></sup>them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 50:7; Ezekiel 3:8–9; Jeremiah 15:20"> Now look, I have made you ⸤today⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “the day”">q</a></sup>as a fortified city and as an iron pillar and a bronze wall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Hebrew “walls”">r</a></sup>against all the land, against the kings of Judah, against its princes, against its priests, and against the people of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Jeremiah 1:8; Jeremiah 20:11"> And they will fight against you but they will not prevail against you, for I am with you,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">s</a></sup>Yahweh, “to deliver you.”</span>
</div>
</div>
