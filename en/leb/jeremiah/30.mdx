## Israel and Judah Will Be Delivered from Their Distress

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:15; Jeremiah 1:1–2"> The word that came to Jeremiah from Yahweh, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to say”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:32; Isaiah 30:8; Habakkuk 2:2–3"> “Thus says Yahweh, the God of Israel, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “to say”">b</a></sup>‘Write for yourself all the words that I have spoken to you in a scroll.</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:10–11; Amos 8:10; Isaiah 5:30"> “For thus says Yahweh:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘We have heard a sound of trembling,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">terror, and there is no peace.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 12:1; Zechariah 14:1–2; Malachi 4:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Alas! <span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:24; Jeremiah 1:19; Isaiah 8:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I am with you,’ ⸤declares⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">j</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh, ‘to save you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">For I will make a complete destruction</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">of all the nations to which I scattered you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but you I will not make a complete destruction.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And I will chastise you to the measure,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will not leave you entirely unpunished.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:18; Jeremiah 30:15; Jeremiah 14:17"> For thus says Yahweh,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Your injury is incurable,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">your wound is overcome by sickness.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:39; Jeremiah 2:3; Jeremiah 50:33–34"> <span className="pl pl-1">And their children will be as of old,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their community will be established ⸤before me⸥.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “to the face of me”">n</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And I will punish all their oppressors.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 16:5; Ezekiel 34:23–24; Acts 5:31"> <span className="pl pl-2">And their noble will be from them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and their ruler will come out from their midst.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And I will bring him near and he will approach me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For who is he that would pledge his heart to approach me?’ ⸤declares⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">o</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:19–20; Zechariah 9:14; Jeremiah 25:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">Look, the storm of Yahweh!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Wrath has gone forth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a whirling tempest.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">It will whirl upon the head of the wicked.</span></span>
