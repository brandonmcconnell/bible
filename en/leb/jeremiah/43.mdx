## The Throne of Nebuchadnezzar Will be Set Up at Tahpanhes

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:2–12; Jeremiah 19:1–15; Jeremiah 51:63–64"> “Take in your hands<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “hand”">h</a></sup>large stones and bury them in the mortar in the clay floor that is at the entrance of the palace<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “house”">i</a></sup>of Pharaoh in Tahpanhes before the eyes of the people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “men”">j</a></sup>of the Judeans,</span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 19:18"> And he will break the stone pillars of Heliopolis, which is in the land of Egypt.
