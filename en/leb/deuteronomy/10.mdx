<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 34:1–2; Exodus 25:10–15; Deuteronomy 10:3–4"> “At that time Yahweh said to me, ‘Carve for yourself two tablets of stone just as the former ones, and come up the mountain to me, and you shall make for yourself an ark of wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 8:9; Deuteronomy 10:5; Deuteronomy 4:13"> And I will write on the tablets the words that were on the former tablets, which you smashed, and you must put them in the ark.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 34:4; Exodus 37:1–9; Exodus 25:10"> And so I made an ark ⸤of acacia wood⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “of wood of acacia trees”">a</a></sup>and I carved two tablets of stone like the former ones, and I went up the mountain with the two tablets in my hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:10; Exodus 34:28; Hebrews 12:18–19"> And he wrote upon the tablets ⸤according to the first writing⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “as the writing the first”">b</a></sup>the ten words that Yahweh spoke to you on the mountain from the midst of the fire on the day of the assembly, and Yahweh gave them to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 34:29; Exodus 40:20; Deuteronomy 10:2"> And I turned, and I came down from the mountain, and I put the tablets in the ark that I had made, and they are there, just as Yahweh commanded me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 33:38; Numbers 20:23–28; Numbers 33:30–33"> “And the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">c</a></sup>journeyed from the wells of Bene-Yaqan to Moserah; there Aaron died and was buried, and Eleazar, his son, served as a priest in place of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 33:32–34"> From there they journeyed to Gudgodah, and from Gudgodah to Jotbathah, a land flowing with streams of water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:5; Deuteronomy 18:5; Numbers 6:23–26"> At that time Yahweh set apart the tribe of Levi to carry the ark of the covenant of Yahweh to stand ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “the faces of”">d</a></sup>Yahweh, to serve him and to bless the people in his name until this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:28; Numbers 18:20–24; Deuteronomy 18:1–2"> Therefore ⸤there was not⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “it/he was not”">e</a></sup>for Levi an allotment or an inheritance along with his brothers; rather Yahweh is his inheritance just as Yahweh your God ⸤promised⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “spoke”">f</a></sup>to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:18–19; Deuteronomy 9:25; Exodus 34:28"> And I stayed on the mountain just as during the former forty days<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew “day”">g</a></sup>and forty nights,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “night”">h</a></sup>and Yahweh listened to me also on that occasion;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “occurrence” or “that time”">i</a></sup>Yahweh was not willing to destroy you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 33:1; Exodus 32:34"> And Yahweh said to me, ‘⸤Come, continue⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “Arise, go”">j</a></sup>your journey ⸤before the people⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “in the faces of the people”">k</a></sup>so that you may go and take possession of the land that I swore to their ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “fathers”">l</a></sup>to give to them.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 6:8; Deuteronomy 6:5; Luke 10:27"> And now, Israel, what is Yahweh your God asking<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “requiring”">m</a></sup>from you, except<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “but only”">n</a></sup>to revere Yahweh your God, to go<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “to walk”">o</a></sup>in all his ways and to love him and to serve Yahweh your God with all your heart and with all your soul,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “inner self”">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:39; Deuteronomy 6:24; James 1:25"> to keep the commandments of Yahweh and his statutes that I am commanding you today ⸤for your own good⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “for good to you”">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 19:5; Psalm 24:1; Psalm 115:16"> Look! For to Yahweh your God ⸤belong⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “is”">r</a></sup>heaven and the ⸤highest heavens⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “heavens of the heavens”">s</a></sup>the earth and all that is in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:37; Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Romans 9:13–23"> ⸤Yet⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “Only”">t</a></sup>to your ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “fathers”">u</a></sup>Yahweh was very attached, so as to love them, and so he chose their offspring after them, namely you, from all the peoples, as it is ⸤today⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “the day the this”">v</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:4; Deuteronomy 30:6; Leviticus 26:41"> So you shall circumcise the foreskin of your heart, and ⸤do not be stubborn⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “your neck you shall not make stiff any longer”">w</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 10:34; Romans 2:11; Daniel 2:47"> For Yahweh your God, he is God of the gods and Lord of the lords, the great and mighty God, the awesome one who ⸤is not partial⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “does not lift up faces”">x</a></sup>and he does not take bribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 146:9; Psalm 68:5; Jeremiah 49:11"> And he executes justice for the orphan and widow, and he is one who loves the alien, to give to them food and clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 22:21; Leviticus 19:33–34; Luke 17:18"> And you shall love the alien, for you were aliens in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:13; Deuteronomy 13:4; Luke 4:8"> Yahweh your God, you shall revere him, you shall serve him, and to him you shall cling, and by his name you shall swear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:14; Psalm 106:21–22; Exodus 15:2"> He is your praise, and he is your God, who has done with you these great and awesome things that your eyes have seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 46:27; Exodus 1:5; Genesis 15:5"> With only seventy persons your ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Or “fathers”">y</a></sup>went down to Egypt, but now Yahweh your God has made you as the stars of the heaven ⸤with respect to multitude⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “as far as number”">z</a></sup></span>
</div>
