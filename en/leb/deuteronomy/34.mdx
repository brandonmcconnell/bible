## The Death of Moses and the Commissioning of Joshua

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:2; Deuteronomy 32:49; Joshua 19:47"> Then Moses went up from the desert plateau<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “plains/lowlands”">a</a></sup>of Moab to Mount Nebo, to the top of Pisgah, ⸤which is opposite⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “which is on the face of”">b</a></sup>Jericho, and Yahweh showed him all of the land, Gilead all the way up to Dan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:24; Numbers 34:6; Exodus 23:31"> and all of Naphtali and the land of Ephraim and Manasseh and all of the land of Judah, up to the western sea,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, the Mediterranean Sea">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:15; Judges 1:16; Judges 3:13"> and the Negev and the plain of the valley of Jericho, the city of palms, on up to Zoar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:52; Genesis 12:7; Genesis 28:13"> And Yahweh said to him, “This is the land that I swore to Abraham to Isaac and to Jacob, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “to say”">d</a></sup>‘To your offspring I will give it.’ I have let you see it with your eyes, but you shall not cross ⸤into it⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “to there”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:50; Joshua 1:1–2; Hebrews 3:3–6"> Then Moses, the servant of Yahweh, died there in the land of Moab ⸤according to the command of Yahweh⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “at the mouth of Yahweh”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jude 1:9; Deuteronomy 3:29"> And he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="That is, Yahweh">g</a></sup>buried him in the valley in the land of Moab opposite Beth Peor. But until this day no one knows his burial site.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:2; Joshua 14:10–11; Genesis 27:1"> ⸤Now Moses was a hundred and twenty years old⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “and Moses was a son of a hundred and twenty year”">h</a></sup>⸤when he died⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “at/in his dying”">i</a></sup>⸤his sight was not impaired and his vigor had not abated⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “not faded his eyes and not had fled away his vitality”">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 50:3; Numbers 20:29; Genesis 50:10"> And the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">k</a></sup>wept concerning Moses thirty days; finally the days of weeping and mourning for Moses were completed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 11:2; Daniel 6:3; Acts 6:6"> Now<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “And”">l</a></sup>Joshua the son of Nun was full of the spirit of wisdom because Moses had placed his hands on him, and the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">m</a></sup>listened to him, and they did as Yahweh had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 33:11; Numbers 12:6–8; Deuteronomy 18:15–18"> And not again<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “since then”">n</a></sup>has a prophet arisen in Israel like Moses, whom Yahweh knew<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Hebrew “knew him”">o</a></sup>face to face,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:19; Deuteronomy 4:34; Psalm 105:26–38"> as far as all the signs and the wonders Yahweh sent him to do in the land of Egypt, against<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Hebrew “to”">p</a></sup>Pharaoh and all of his servants and against<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Hebrew “to”">q</a></sup>all of his land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and as far as all of ⸤the mighty deeds⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “the hand the strong”">r</a></sup>and as far as ⸤the great awesome wonders⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “the terrifying the great actions ”">s</a></sup>Moses did before the eyes of all Israel.</span>
</div>
