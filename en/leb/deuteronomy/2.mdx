<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 36:8; Joshua 24:4; Jeremiah 27:5"> Do not get involved in battle<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “provoke”">g</a></sup>with them, for I will not give you any of their land, not even ⸤a foot’s breadth⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “a sole’s foot of space”">h</a></sup>of it; since I have given Mount Seir as a possession for Esau.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 7:12; Numbers 20:19; Romans 12:17"> You shall buy food from them so that you may eat; and also you shall purchase water from them with money so that you may drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 22:35; Nehemiah 9:21; Deuteronomy 8:2–4"> The fact of the matter is, Yahweh your God has blessed you in ⸤all the work you have done⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “the work of your hand”">i</a></sup>he knows ⸤your travels⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “your journeying”">j</a></sup>with respect to this great wilderness; forty years Yahweh your God has been with you; you have not lacked a thing.” ’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 9:26; Judges 11:18; 2 Kings 16:6"> And so we passed by our brothers, the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “sons”">k</a></sup>of Esau, who live in Seir, past the road of the Arabah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “Jordan Valley” since the Arabah is an extension of it">l</a></sup>from Elath and Ezion Geber, and we turned and traveled along the route of the desert<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “wilderness”">m</a></sup>of Moab.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 14:5; Deuteronomy 2:11; Numbers 13:22"> (The Emim previously lived in it, a people large,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “great” in the sense of influential">p</a></sup>numerous, and tall, like the Anakites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; Numbers 13:28; Genesis 14:5"> They were reckoned also as Rephaim as the Anakites were; but the Moabites called them Emim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:22; Genesis 14:6; Deuteronomy 2:32–11"> The Horites previously lived in Seir, but the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “sons”">q</a></sup>of Esau dispossessed them and destroyed them ⸤from among themselves⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “from their presence”">r</a></sup>as Israel did with respect to the land of their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “its/his”">s</a></sup>possession that Yahweh gave to them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 21:12; Numbers 13:23"> So now arise and cross over the wadi<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="A valley that is dry most of the year, but contains a stream during the rainy season">t</a></sup>of Zered yourselves; and so we crossed the wadi<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="A valley that is dry most of the year, but contains a stream during the rainy season">u</a></sup>of Zered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 26:64–65; Numbers 14:28–35; Deuteronomy 1:34–35"> Now the ⸤length of time⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “days”">v</a></sup>that we had traveled from Kadesh Barnea until the time when we crossed the wadi<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="A valley that is dry most of the year, but contains a stream during the rainy season">w</a></sup>of Zered was thirty-eight years, until the perishing of all of that generation; that is, the men of war from the midst of the camp as Yahweh had sworn to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 106:26; Isaiah 66:14; Psalm 32:4"> The hand of Yahweh was against them to root them out from the midst of the camp until they perished completely.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Yahweh spoke to me, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “to say”">aa</a></sup></span>
