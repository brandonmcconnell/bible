<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 21:5; Romans 9:8; Galatians 3:26"> “You are children<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “sons”">a</a></sup>of Yahweh your God; therefore you must not gash<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “cut”">b</a></sup>yourself, and ⸤you must not make your forehead bald⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “you shall not make/place baldness between your eyes”">c</a></sup>for the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6; Leviticus 20:26; Exodus 19:5–6"> For you are a people holy to Yahweh your God, and you Yahweh has chosen to be a treasured possession from among all of the peoples that are on the surface<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “face”">d</a></sup>of the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:14; Romans 14:14; Leviticus 20:25"> You shall not eat any detestable thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 11:2–45; Acts 10:14; 1 Kings 4:23"> These are the animals you may eat: ox, ⸤sheep, goats⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “small livestock of the sheep and a small livestock of the goats”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> deer, gazelle, roebuck, wild goat, ibex, antelope, and mountain sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 18:1; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Psalm 1:1–2"> And any animal having a split hoof<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “divides the hoof”">f</a></sup>and so ⸤a dividing of the hoof into two parts⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “a dividing cleft creating two hoofs”">g</a></sup>⸤and that chews the cud⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “and that brings up the cud”">h</a></sup>among the animals<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “animal”">i</a></sup>—that animal you may eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:22–23; Titus 1:16; 2 Timothy 3:5"> Only these you may not eat from ⸤those chewing the cud⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “bringing up the cud”">j</a></sup>and from ⸤those having a division of the hoof⸥:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “those having a division of the hoof divided”">k</a></sup>the camel and the hare and the coney, because ⸤they chew the cud⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “they bringing up of the cud”">l</a></sup>but they do not divide the hoof; they are therefore unclean for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 11:26–27; Isaiah 66:3; 2 Peter 2:22"> And also the pig ⸤because it has a division of the hoof⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “because a division of the hoof”">m</a></sup>⸤but does not chew the cud⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “but not a chewing cud”">n</a></sup>it is unclean for you; from their meat you shall not eat, and you shall not touch their ⸤carcasses⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “dead body”">o</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 11:9–12"> “This is what you shall eat from all that is in the water: ⸤everything⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “all of that”">p</a></sup>⸤that has fins and scales⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “for it is fins and scales,” showing possession of these features">q</a></sup>you may eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> But ⸤anything that does not have⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “all of that there is not for it,” showing lack of possession of these features">r</a></sup>fins and scales, you may not eat, for it is unclean for you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> “All of the birds that are clean you may eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 11:13–19"> Now these are the ones you shall not eat ⸤any of them⸥:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “from them”">s</a></sup>the eagle and the vulture and the short-toed eagle,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="This list of birds is difficult to translate since the terms are not definitely known: e.g., some translations render the last bird as a “buzzard” (NASV); other translations give different names for all three: griffon vulture, black vulture, bearded vulture (NEB)">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and the red kite<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Various options are available: large bird, kite, red kite, glede, buzzard">u</a></sup>and the black kite or ⸤any kind of falcon⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “or the falcon according to its kind”; other options for falcon: bird, falcon, kite (others as carrion-bird), vulture, crow or raven, buzzard">v</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and any kind of crow<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or others translate as “raven”">w</a></sup>according to its kind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 30:29"> and the ⸤ostrich⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “daughter of the ostrich”; others “desert owl”">x</a></sup>and the short-eared owl and the seagull<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Or “long-eared owl”">y</a></sup>and the hawk according to its kind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> the little owl and the great owl and the barn owl,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “white owl”">z</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> and the desert owl<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Or “large bird” or “horned-owl”">aa</a></sup>and the carrion vulture<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Or “large bird”">ab</a></sup>and the cormorant,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Or “large bird,” or “fisher-owl”">ac</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and the stork and the heron according to its kind and the hoopoe and the bat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philippians 3:19; Leviticus 11:20–23"> And also all of ⸤the winged insects⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “the swarmers of the flyers ” or “all the swarms of things that fly”">ad</a></sup>they are unclean for you; you shall not eat them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> You may eat any clean bird.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 23:19; Exodus 34:26; Leviticus 22:8"> “You shall not eat any carcass;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Or “corpse”">ae</a></sup>you may give it to the alien who is in your ⸤towns⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “gates”">af</a></sup>and he may eat it, or you may sell it to a foreigner, for you are a holy people for Yahweh your God; you may not boil a kid in its mother’s milk.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:37; Deuteronomy 12:17; Deuteronomy 26:12–15"> “Certainly you must give a tithe of all the yield of your seed, ⸤which comes forth from your field year after year⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “the going forth of the field year by year”">ag</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5–7; Deuteronomy 4:10; Deuteronomy 12:17–18"> And you shall eat ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “before the faces of”">ah</a></sup>Yahweh your God in the place that he will choose to make to dwell his name there the tithe of your grain, your wine and your olive oil and the firstling of your herd and your flock, so that you may learn to revere Yahweh your God ⸤always⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Literally “all of the days”">ai</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:21; Deuteronomy 12:5; Deuteronomy 11:24"> But if ⸤the distance is too great for you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Literally “it is great from you the journey that”">aj</a></sup>so that you are not able to transport<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Or “carry”">ak</a></sup>it, because the place that Yahweh your God will choose to set his name there, it is too far from you, when Yahweh your God will bless you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> then in that case ⸤you may exchange for money⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Literally “you may give it in for the money/silver”">al</a></sup>and you shall take<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Or “bind”">am</a></sup>the money to your hand and go to the place that Yahweh your God will choose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:7; Deuteronomy 12:7; Matthew 21:12"> You may spend the money for anything ⸤that you desire⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Literally “that your soul/inner self desires”">an</a></sup>for oxen or for sheep or for wine or for strong drink or for anything ⸤that you desire⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Literally “that your soul/inner self desires”">ao</a></sup>and you shall eat it there ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">ap</a></sup>Yahweh your God, and you shall rejoice, you and your household.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 18:20; Deuteronomy 14:29; Deuteronomy 12:12"> And as to the Levite who is in your ⸤towns⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Literally “gates”">aq</a></sup>you shall not neglect him, because there is not a plot of ground for him and an inheritance along with you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:22; Amos 4:4; Deuteronomy 26:12–15"> “At the end of three years you shall bring out all of the tithe of your yield for that year, and you shall store it in your ⸤towns⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Literally “gates”">ar</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:10; Deuteronomy 14:27; Proverbs 19:17"> And so the Levite may come, because there is no plot of ground for him or an inheritance with you, and the alien also may come and the orphan and the widow that are in your ⸤towns⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Literally “gates”">as</a></sup>and ⸤they may eat their fill⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-at" id="fnref-at" data-fn="Literally “they may eat and they may be satisfied”">at</a></sup>so that Yahweh your God may bless you in all of the work of your hand that you undertake.”</span>
</div>
