<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:7–8; Deuteronomy 31:8; Psalm 20:7"> “If you go out to war against your enemies and you see a horse and a chariot, ⸤an army⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “a people”">a</a></sup>larger than you, you shall not be afraid because of them; for Yahweh your God is with you, the one who brought you from the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 31:6; 2 Chronicles 13:12; Numbers 10:8–9"> ⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “And it will happen”">b</a></sup>when you approach the battle, then the priest shall come near and speak to the troops.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 13:6; Psalm 3:6; Revelation 2:10"> And he shall say to them, ‘Hear, Israel, you are near ⸤today⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “the day”">c</a></sup>to the battle against your enemies; ⸤do not lose heart⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “do not be faint/tender-hearted”">d</a></sup>you shall not be afraid, and you shall not panic, and you shall not be terrified ⸤because of them⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “from their faces”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 14:14; Deuteronomy 3:22; Deuteronomy 1:30"> for Yahweh your God is going with you to fight for you against your enemies to help you.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:27; Numbers 31:14; Deuteronomy 16:18"> And the officials shall speak to the troops, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “to say”">f</a></sup>‘Who is the man who has built a new house and has not dedicated it? Let him go and return to this house, so that he does not die in battle and ⸤another man⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “a man other”">g</a></sup>dedicates it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 19:23–25; Deuteronomy 28:1–30; Jeremiah 31:5"> And who is the man that has planted a vineyard and has not enjoyed it? Let him go and let him return to his house, so that he does not die in battle and ⸤another man⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “a man other”">h</a></sup>enjoys it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:5; Deuteronomy 28:30; Luke 14:18–20"> And who is the man who got engaged to a woman and has not married her? Let him go and let him return to his house, so that he does not die in battle and ⸤another man⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “a man other”">i</a></sup>marries her.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 7:3; Deuteronomy 1:28; Revelation 21:8"> And the officials shall continue to speak to the troops, and they shall say, ‘⸤What man⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “Who is the man”">j</a></sup>is afraid ⸤and disheartened⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “and soft/weak of the heart”">k</a></sup>Let him go, and let him return to his house, and let him not cause the heart of his brothers to melt<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="The verb has a causal meaning here">l</a></sup>like his.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And ⸤when the officials have finished speaking⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “it will happen as to finish the officials to speak”">m</a></sup>to the army troops, then they shall appoint commanders of divisions at the head of the troops.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:14; Joshua 11:19–20; Joshua 16:10"> ⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “And it will happen”">o</a></sup>if ⸤they accept your terms of peace⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “if peace they reply to you”">p</a></sup>and ⸤they surrender to you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “they open to you”">q</a></sup>⸤and then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “and it will happen”">r</a></sup>all the people ⸤inhabiting it⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “being found in it”">s</a></sup>shall be forced labor for you, and they shall serve you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> But if they ⸤do not accept your terms of peace⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “not they make peace with you”">t</a></sup>and they want to make war with you, then you shall lay siege against it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="That is, the city">u</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 2:6–12; 1 Kings 11:15–16; Psalm 110:1"> And Yahweh your God will give it into your hand, and you shall kill all its males with the ⸤edge⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “mouth”">v</a></sup>of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 8:2; Joshua 22:8; Numbers 31:9"> Only the women and the little children and the domestic animals<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Hebrew “animal”">w</a></sup>and all that shall be in the city, all of its spoil you may loot for yourselves, and you may enjoy the spoil of your enemies that Yahweh your God has given to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Thus you shall do to all the far<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Or “distant”">x</a></sup>cities from you, which are not from the cities of these nations located ⸤nearby⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “here”">y</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 34:5–6; Jeremiah 50:35–40; Deuteronomy 7:1–2"> Rather, you shall utterly destroy them, the Hittites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Hebrew “Hittite”">aa</a></sup>and the Amorites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Hebrew “Amorite”">ab</a></sup>the Canaanites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Hebrew “Canaanite”">ac</a></sup>and the Perizzites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Hebrew “Perizzite”">ad</a></sup>the Hivites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Hebrew “Hivite”">ae</a></sup>and the Jebusites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Hebrew “Jebusite”">af</a></sup>just as Yahweh your God has commanded you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:30–31; Exodus 23:33; Ephesians 5:11"> so that they may not teach you to do like all their detestable things that they do for their gods and thereby you sin against Yahweh your God.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 3:10; John 15:2–8; Deuteronomy 26:6"> “If<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Or “when”">ag</a></sup>you besiege a town for many days to make war against it in order to seize it, you shall not destroy its trees by wielding an ax against them,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Hebrew “it”">ah</a></sup>for you may eat from them,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Hebrew “it”">ai</a></sup>and so you must not cut them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Hebrew “it”">aj</a></sup>down. Are the trees of the field humans that they should come in siege ⸤against you⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Literally “from your face”">ak</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:15; Jeremiah 33:4; Ecclesiastes 9:14"> Only<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Or “But”">al</a></sup>the trees<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Hebrew “tree”">am</a></sup>that you know ⸤are not fruit trees⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Literally “not a tree of food”">an</a></sup>you may destroy and you may cut down, and you may build siege works against that city that is making war with you ⸤until it falls⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Literally “it to fall”">ao</a></sup></span>
