## Covenant Renewal, Oaths, Restoration, Charges to the Nation

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:2–3; Hebrews 8:9; Exodus 24:2–8"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Deuteronomy 29:1–29 in the English Bible is 28.69–29:28 in the Hebrew Bible">a</a></sup>These are the words of the covenant that Yahweh commanded Moses to make with the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">b</a></sup>in the land of Moab ⸤besides⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “from to alone”">c</a></sup>the covenant that he made with them at Horeb.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 19:4; Exodus 8:12; Joshua 24:5–6"> And Moses summoned all of Israel and said to them, “You saw all that Yahweh did before your eyes in the land of Egypt and to Pharaoh and to all his servants and to all his land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:32–35; Nehemiah 9:9–11; Deuteronomy 7:18–19"> that is, the great trials that your eyes saw, and those great signs and wonders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 4:18; Isaiah 6:9–10; Proverbs 20:12"> But Yahweh has not given to you a heart to understand, or eyes to see, or ears to hear, even ⸤to this day⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “until the day the this”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:4; Nehemiah 9:21; Deuteronomy 8:2"> And I have led you forty years<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew “year”">e</a></sup>in the desert; your clothes have not worn out ⸤on you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “from on you”">f</a></sup>and your sandal has not worn out ⸤on your foot⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “from on your foot”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:3; Ephesians 5:18; 1 Corinthians 9:25"> You have not eaten bread, and you have not drunk wine and strong drink, so that you may know that I am Yahweh your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:24–17; Numbers 32:33–42; Psalm 135:10–12"> And when you came to this place then Sihon the king of Heshbon, and Og the king of Bashan, came out to meet you for battle, and we defeated them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:12–13; Numbers 32:33"> And we took their land and gave it as an inheritance to the Reubenites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “Reubenite”">h</a></sup>and to the Gadites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “Gadite”">i</a></sup>and to the half-tribe of Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 2:3; Deuteronomy 4:6; Luke 11:28"> And ⸤you must diligently observe the words of this covenant⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “you must keep the words of the covenant the this and you must do them”">j</a></sup>so that you may succeed in all that you do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joel 2:16–17; Revelation 20:12; Deuteronomy 31:12–13"> “You are standing ⸤today⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “the day”">k</a></sup>all of you, ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">l</a></sup>Yahweh your God, your leaders, your tribes, your elders, and your officials, all the men of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 12:38; Joshua 9:21–27; Exodus 12:48–49"> your little children, your women and your aliens<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Hebrew “alien”">m</a></sup>who are in the midst of your camp, from the choppers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Hebrew “chopper”">n</a></sup>of your wood to the drawers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Hebrew “drawer”">o</a></sup>of your water,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:12–15; 2 Kings 11:17; Deuteronomy 5:2–3"> ⸤in order for you to enter into the covenant of Yahweh your God⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “for you to go over into the covenant of Yahweh your God”">p</a></sup>and into<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “with”">q</a></sup>his oath that Yahweh your God is ⸤making with you⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “cutting with you”">r</a></sup>⸤today⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “the day”">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 6:7; Genesis 17:7; Deuteronomy 28:9"> in order to establish you ⸤today⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “the day”">t</a></sup>⸤to himself⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “for him”">u</a></sup>as a people and so that he may be for you as God, just as he ⸤promised⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “spoke”">v</a></sup>to you and ⸤just as⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “according to that which”">w</a></sup>he swore to your ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Or “your fathers”">x</a></sup>to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Hebrews 8:7–12"> “Now I am not ⸤making this covenant⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “cutting this covenant”">y</a></sup>and this oath ⸤with you alone⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “with you to alone you”">z</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 2:39; 1 Corinthians 7:14; Jeremiah 50:5"> But with ⸤whoever is standing here⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “with who he is here with us standing”">aa</a></sup>with us ⸤today⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “the day”">ab</a></sup>⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">ac</a></sup>Yahweh our God, and with ⸤whoever is not standing here⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “with who he is not standing here with us”">ad</a></sup>with us ⸤today⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “the day”">ae</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:19; Deuteronomy 2:9; Deuteronomy 2:4"> For you know how we lived in the land of Egypt and how we traveled through the midst of the nations that you traveled<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Or “journeyed”">af</a></sup>through.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:36"> And you have seen their detestable things and their idols of wood and stone, silver, and gold that were among them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 12:15; Deuteronomy 11:16–17; Deuteronomy 13:1–15"> so that ⸤there is not⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “lest there be/develop”">ag</a></sup>among you a man or a woman or a clan or a tribe ⸤whose heart⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “his heart”">ah</a></sup>turns ⸤today⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Literally “the day”">ai</a></sup>from being with Yahweh our God to go to serve the gods of these nations, so that there is not among you a root sprouting poison and wormwood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:24; Numbers 15:30; Numbers 15:39"> And then when he hears the words of this oath, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Or “and”">aj</a></sup>⸤he will assure himself⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Literally “he will bless himself”; HALOT 160 suggests “to consider oneself fortunate”">ak</a></sup>in his heart, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Literally “to say”">al</a></sup>‘⸤Safety shall be mine even though I go in the stubbornness of my heart⸥,’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Literally “Peace shall happen/be for me, although/even if in the stubbornness of my heart I go”">am</a></sup>thereby destroying the well-watered land along with the parched.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Some translators prefer to include the last clause as a part of the words of the wicked man (NASV vs. NEB)">an</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 25:41; Joshua 7:1–26; Ezekiel 13:9"> And Yahweh will single him out for calamity out of all the tribes of Israel, according to all the curses of the covenant written in the scroll of this law.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:8"> “And the next generation, that is, your children who will rise up after you, and the foreigner who will come from a distant land, when<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Hebrew “and”">ao</a></sup>they will see the plagues of that land and its diseases that Yahweh has inflicted upon it, will say,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:9; Jeremiah 17:6; Isaiah 34:9"> ‘All its land is brimstone and salt left by fire, ⸤none of its land will be sown⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Literally “all of its land will not be sown”">ap</a></sup>and it will not make plants sprout out and it will not grow any vegetation; it is as the destruction of Sodom and Gomorrah, Adman and Zeboiim, which Yahweh overturned in his anger and in his wrath.’</span>

What caused the fierceness of this great anger?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 8:9; Isaiah 47:6; Jeremiah 50:7"> And they will say,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Or “answer/respond”">as</a></sup>‘It is because they abandoned the covenant of Yahweh, the God of their ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-at" id="fnref-at" data-fn="Or “fathers”">at</a></sup>which he ⸤made⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-au" id="fnref-au" data-fn="Literally “cut”">au</a></sup>with them ⸤when he brought them out⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-av" id="fnref-av" data-fn="Literally “at/in his to bring them out from the land of Egypt”">av</a></sup>from the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:64; Judges 2:12–13; Judges 5:8"> And they went and served other gods and bowed down to them, gods whom they did not know them and he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aw" id="fnref-aw" data-fn="That is, Yahweh">aw</a></sup>had not allotted to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Daniel 9:11–14; Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Deuteronomy 27:15–26"> So ⸤the anger of Yahweh was kindled⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ax" id="fnref-ax" data-fn="Literally “became hot the nose of Yahweh”">ax</a></sup>against that land to bring upon it all the curses written in this scroll,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:20; 1 Kings 14:15; Psalm 52:5"> and Yahweh uprooted them from their land in anger and in wrath and in great fury, and he cast them into another land, ⸤just as it is today⸥.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ay" id="fnref-ay" data-fn="Literally “as the day the this”">ay</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Daniel 2:22; Acts 1:7; Romans 11:33–34"> “The hidden things ⸤belong to Yahweh⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-az" id="fnref-az" data-fn="Literally “are for Yahweh”">az</a></sup>our God, but the revealed things ⸤belong to us⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ba" id="fnref-ba" data-fn="Literally “are for us”">ba</a></sup>to know and to our children ⸤forever⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bb" id="fnref-bb" data-fn="Literally “until eternity”">bb</a></sup>in order to do all the words of this law.”</span>
