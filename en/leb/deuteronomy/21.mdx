<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 26:21; Psalm 5:6; Proverbs 28:17"> “If someone slain is found in the land that Yahweh your God is giving to you to take possession of it and is lying in the field, and it is not known who ⸤killed him⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “struck/smote him”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 13:3–4; Deuteronomy 16:18–19"> then your elders and your judges shall go out and shall measure the distance to the cities that are around the slain one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 19:2; Matthew 11:28–30; Jeremiah 31:18"> ⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “And it will happen”">b</a></sup>the nearest city to the slain one, the elders of that city shall take a heifer of the herd that has not been worked with in the field, that has not pulled a yoke,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 2:21–24; 1 Peter 3:18"> and the elders of that city shall bring the heifer down to a ⸤wadi that flows with water all year⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “an ever-flowing wadi”">c</a></sup>and that has not been plowed and has not been sown; then ⸤there they shall break the neck of the heifer in the wadi⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “they shall break there the neck with respect to the heifer in the ever-flowing wadi”; the Hebrew verb carries the meaning “to break the neck of”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:13; Malachi 2:7; Deuteronomy 10:8"> Then the priests, the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “sons”">e</a></sup>of Levi, shall come near, for Yahweh your God has chosen them to bless in the name of Yahweh, and every legal dispute and every case of assault will be ⸤subject to their ruling⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “on their mouth”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 73:13; Psalm 26:6; Psalm 51:7"> And all of the elders of that city nearest to the slain person shall wash their hands over the heifer with the broken neck in the wadi.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="A valley that is dry most of the year, but contains a stream during the rainy season">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 21:21–23; Job 21:31–34; 2 Samuel 16:8"> And they shall declare, and they shall say, ‘Our hands did not shed this blood, and our eyes did not see what was done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jonah 1:14; 2 Kings 24:4; Ezekiel 23:24–25"> Forgive your people, Israel, whom you redeemed, Yahweh, and do not ⸤allow⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “place/put”">h</a></sup>the guilt of innocent blood in the midst of your people Israel, and let them be forgiven with regard to blood.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:12–13; 2 Kings 10:30–31; Deuteronomy 13:18"> And so you shall purge the innocent blood from your midst, because you must do the right thing in the eyes of Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:10–16; Joshua 21:44"> “When you go out for battle against your enemies, and Yahweh your God gives them into your hand, and you lead the captives<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “his captive” but singular pronoun refers to the many captives taken with plural sense">i</a></sup>away,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 31:10; Genesis 34:3; Genesis 12:14–15"> and you see among the captives<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Hebrew “captive”">j</a></sup>a woman beautiful in appearance, and you become attached to her and you want to take her as a wife,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 14:9; Numbers 6:9; Ephesians 4:22"> then you shall bring her into your household, and she shall shave her head, and she shall trim her nails.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 45:10–11; Luke 14:26–27"> And she shall remove the clothing of her captivity from her, and she shall remain in your house, and she shall mourn her father and her mother ⸤a full month⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “a month of days”">k</a></sup>and after this ⸤you may have sex with her⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “you may go into her”">l</a></sup>and you may marry her, and she may ⸤become your wife⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “become for you as wife”">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:29; Genesis 34:2; Deuteronomy 22:19"> And then if you do not take delight in her, then you shall let her go ⸤to do whatever she wants⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “according to her desire/soul”">n</a></sup>but you shall not treat her as a slave, since you have dishonored<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “humbled”">o</a></sup>her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 29:33; Genesis 29:20; Genesis 29:18"> “If a man has two wives, and the one is loved and the other one is disliked and the one loved and the one that is disliked have borne for him sons, if it happens that the firstborn son ⸤belongs to the one that is disliked⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “is to the wife who is hated”">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:10; 1 Chronicles 5:2; 2 Chronicles 21:3"> nevertheless ⸤it will be the case that⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “it will happen”">q</a></sup>on the day of bestowing his inheritance upon his sons, he will not be allowed to treat as the firstborn son the son of the beloved wife ⸤in preference to⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “over the faces of”">r</a></sup>the son of the disliked wife, who is the firstborn son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 49:3; Genesis 25:31–34; Psalm 105:36"> But he shall acknowledge the firstborn son of the disliked wife ⸤by giving⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “to give”">s</a></sup>him a double portion of ⸤all that he has⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “all that is found for him”">t</a></sup>for he is the firstfruit of his vigor;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “the beginning of his strength”">u</a></sup>to him is the legal claim of the birthright.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “the just claim of the firstborn”">v</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 19:3; Exodus 20:12; Amos 4:11–12"> “⸤If a man has a stubborn and rebellious son⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “If shall be for a man, a son stubborn and rebellious”">w</a></sup>who ⸤does not listen to⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “and there is no listening/obedience”">x</a></sup>the voice of his father and to the voice of his mother, and they discipline him, and he does not obey<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Or “listen to”">y</a></sup>them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 13:3; Deuteronomy 21:2; Deuteronomy 16:18"> then his father and his mother shall take hold of him, and they shall bring him out to the elders of his city and to the gate of his ⸤town⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “place”">z</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 23:29–35; Proverbs 20:1; Proverbs 23:19–21"> and they shall say to the elders of his city, ‘This our son is stubborn and rebellious; ⸤he does not obey us⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “there is no listening to our voice”">aa</a></sup>and he is a glutton and a drunkard.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:19–20; Deuteronomy 13:10–11; Leviticus 24:16"> Then all the men of his city shall stone him with stones and let him die; and so you shall purge the evil from your midst, and all of Israel will hear, and they will fear.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 19:31–38; Acts 26:31; Luke 23:33"> “And ⸤if a man commits a sin punishable by death⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “when shall be against a man a sin of judgment of death”">ab</a></sup>and so he is put to death and you hang him on a tree,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 3:13; John 19:31; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> his dead body shall not hang on the tree, but certainly you shall bury him on that day, for cursed by God is one that is being hung; so you shall not defile your land<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Or “ground”">ac</a></sup>that Yahweh your God is giving to you as an inheritance.”</span>
</div>
