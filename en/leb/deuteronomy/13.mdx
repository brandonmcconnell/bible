<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:25–28; 1 John 4:1; Jeremiah 29:8"> “If a prophet stands up in your midst or a dreamer of dreams<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew “dream”">a</a></sup>and he gives to you a sign or wonder,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:6; Matthew 7:22–23; Revelation 13:13–14"> and the sign or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew “and”">b</a></sup>the wonder comes about that he spoke<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “promised/declared”">c</a></sup>to you, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “to say”">d</a></sup>‘Let us go after other gods (those whom you have not known), and let us serve them,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:2; 1 Corinthians 11:19; Psalm 66:10"> you must not listen to the words of that prophet or to that dreamer, for Yahweh your God ⸤is testing you to know whether you love⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “a testing of Yahweh your God concerning you in order to know whether there is you loving”">e</a></sup>Yahweh your God with all of your heart and with all of your inner self.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “soul”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; Deuteronomy 10:20; 1 Corinthians 6:17"> You shall go after Yahweh your God, and him you shall revere, and his commandment you shall keep, and to his voice you shall listen, and him you shall serve, and to him you shall hold fast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:13; Deuteronomy 18:20; Jeremiah 14:15"> But that prophet or the dreamer of that dream shall be executed, for he spoke falsely about Yahweh your God, the one bringing you out from the land of Egypt and the one redeeming you from the house of slavery, in order to seduce you from the way that Yahweh your God commanded you to go in it; so in this way you shall purge the evil from your midst.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:2–7; 2 Peter 2:1; Ephesians 4:14"> “If your brother, the son of your mother or your son or your daughter ⸤or your wife whom you embrace⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “or the wife of your embrace”">g</a></sup>or ⸤your intimate friend⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “your friend who is like your soul/inner self”">h</a></sup>in secrecy ⸤says⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “to say”">i</a></sup>‘Let us go and let us serve other gods!’ gods that you and your ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “fathers”">j</a></sup>have not known,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> from among the gods of the people who are around you, ⸤those near you or those far from you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “the near ones to you or the distant ones from you”">k</a></sup>from one end of the earth and up to the other end of the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:13; Proverbs 1:10; Deuteronomy 7:16"> you must not give in to him, and you shall not listen to him, and your eye shall not take pity on him, and you shall not have compassion, and you shall not cover up<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “conceal” him">l</a></sup>for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 7:58; Luke 14:26; Matthew 10:37"> But you shall certainly kill him; your hand shall be first against him to kill him and next the hand of all of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 7:25; Leviticus 24:14–16; Exodus 20:2"> And you shall stone him with stones and let him die, for he tried to seduce you from Yahweh your God, the one bringing you from the land of Egypt, from the house of slavery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:20; Deuteronomy 17:13; 1 Timothy 5:20"> And all of Israel shall hear, and they shall fear, and ⸤they shall not continue to act⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “they shall not add to do”">m</a></sup>according to this evil thing in your midst.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 20:1–17; Joshua 22:11–34"> “If you hear in one of your towns which Yahweh your God is giving to you to live in, someone ⸤saying that⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “to say”">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 John 2:19; Deuteronomy 13:2; 2 Corinthians 6:15"> ⸤worthless men⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “sons of Belial”">o</a></sup>have gone out from your midst and have seduced the inhabitants of their town, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “to say”">p</a></sup>‘Let us go and serve other gods!’ whom you have not known,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:4; Isaiah 11:3–4; John 7:24"> then you shall inquire and examine and interrogate thoroughly, and, look! It is true; the thing has actually been done, this detestable thing in your midst,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 22:20; Leviticus 27:28; Deuteronomy 7:2"> then you shall certainly strike down the inhabitants of that town with the ⸤edge⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “mouth”">q</a></sup>of the sword; you shall destroy it and everything in it, its domestic animals with the ⸤edge⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “mouth”">r</a></sup>of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:2; Joshua 8:28; Isaiah 25:2"> And then you shall gather all of its booty into the middle of its public square, and you shall burn the town and all of its war-booty totally for Yahweh your God, and it shall be ⸤a pile of rubble forever⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “a tell/ruin forever”">s</a></sup>it shall not be built again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 26:4; Deuteronomy 7:26; Joshua 7:26"> And let not something cling to your hand from the things devoted to destruction, so that Yahweh may turn back ⸤from his burning anger⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “from the burning of his nose”">t</a></sup>and he may show compassion to you and he may continue to show compassion and so multiply you ⸤just as he swore⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “as that he swore”">u</a></sup>to your ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “fathers”">v</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:28; Matthew 7:21; Deuteronomy 12:25"> if you listen to the voice of Yahweh your God, to keep all of his commandments that I am commanding to you ⸤today⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “the day”">w</a></sup>so as to do the right thing in the eyes of Yahweh your God.”</span>
</div>
