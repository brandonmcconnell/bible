<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 7:42; James 2:13; Luke 6:34–38"> And this is the manner of the remission of debt: every ⸤creditor⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “owner of the loan of his hand”">a</a></sup>shall remit his claim that he holds against his neighbor, and he shall not exact payment from his brother because there<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew “it”">b</a></sup>a remission of debt has been proclaimed unto<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “for”">c</a></sup>Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:20; Matthew 17:25–26; 1 Corinthians 6:6–7"> With respect to the foreigner you may exact payment, but ⸤you must remit⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “your hand shall remit”">d</a></sup>what shall be owed to you with respect to your brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 58:10–11; Proverbs 28:27; Proverbs 11:24–25"> Nevertheless, there<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew “it”">e</a></sup>shall not be among you a poor person, because Yahweh will certainly bless you in the land that Yahweh your God is giving to you as an inheritance, to take possession of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:1–15; Isaiah 1:19–20; Joshua 1:7"> If only you listen well to the voice of Yahweh your God ⸤by observing diligently⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “to observe so as to do”">f</a></sup>all of these commandments<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew “commandment”">g</a></sup>that I am commanding you ⸤today⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “the day”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:12–13; Luke 6:35; Nehemiah 9:27"> When Yahweh your God has blessed you, just as he ⸤promised⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “spoke”">i</a></sup>to you, then you will lend to many nations, but you will not borrow from them, and you will rule over many nations, but they will not rule over you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 3:16–17; Deuteronomy 15:9; Proverbs 21:13"> If there is a poor person among you from among one of your brothers in one of your ⸤towns⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “gates”">j</a></sup>that Yahweh your God is giving to you, you shall not harden your heart, and you shall not shut your hand toward ⸤your brother who is poor⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “from among your brothers, the poor one”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 6:34; Matthew 5:42"> But you shall certainly open your hand for him, and ⸤you shall willingly lend⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “lending you shall lend”">l</a></sup>to him enough to meet his need, ⸤whatever it is⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “whatever is lacking for him”">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:15; Matthew 20:15; Jeremiah 17:10"> ⸤Take care⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “Watch for yourself”">n</a></sup>so that there<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Hebrew “it”">o</a></sup>will not be ⸤a thought of wickedness⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “a thing in your heart wickedness”">p</a></sup>in your heart, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “to say”">q</a></sup>‘The seventh year, the year of the remission of debt is near,’ ⸤and you view your needy neighbor with hostility⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “is bad your eye against your brother who is needy”">r</a></sup>and so you do not give to him, and he might cry out against you to Yahweh, and ⸤you would incur guilt against yourself⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “it will be against you a sin”">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 22:9; Hebrews 13:16; Isaiah 58:10"> By all means you must give to him, and ⸤you must not be discontented⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “and not shall be bad/evil your heart at/when”">t</a></sup>at your giving to him, because on account of this very thing, Yahweh your God will bless you in all your work and ⸤in all that you undertake⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “in all of the sending/putting forth of your hand”">u</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 14:7; Matthew 26:11; John 12:8"> For the poor<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “the needy person”">v</a></sup>will not cease to be ⸤among you⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “from the midst of “">w</a></sup>in the land; therefore I am commanding you, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “to say”">x</a></sup>‘You shall willingly open your hand to your brother, to your needy and to your poor that are in your land.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:14; Leviticus 25:39–41; Exodus 21:2–6"> If your relative<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Or “brother”">y</a></sup>who is a Hebrew man or a Hebrew woman is sold to you, and he or she has served you six years, then in the seventh year you shall send that person out ⸤free⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “free from with you”">z</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Malachi 3:5; Jeremiah 22:13; Proverbs 3:27–28"> And when you send him out free from you, you shall not send him away empty-handed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:2; Proverbs 10:22; Psalm 68:10"> You shall generously supply him from among your flocks and from your threshing floor and from your press; according to that with which Yahweh your God has blessed you, you shall give to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:12; Matthew 18:32–33; Deuteronomy 5:14–15"> And remember that you were a slave in the land of Egypt, and Yahweh your God redeemed you; therefore I am commanding you thus ⸤today⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “the day”">aa</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 21:5–6; Psalm 40:8; Psalm 40:6"> And then if it will happen that he says to you, ‘⸤I do not want to go out⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “I will not go out”">ab</a></sup>from you,’ because he loves you and your family, because it is good for him to be with you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:22; Leviticus 25:39–42"> then you shall take an awl, and you shall thrust it through his earlobe and into the door, and he shall be to you ⸤a slave forever⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “a slave of eternity”">ac</a></sup>and you shall also do likewise for your slave woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 17:7–8; Isaiah 16:14; Isaiah 21:16"> It shall not be hard in your eyes ⸤when you send him forth free⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “in/at you to send him forth free from being with you”">ad</a></sup>because for six years he has served you worth twice the wage of a hired worker; and Yahweh your God will bless you ⸤in whatever you will do⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “in all of that you will do”">ae</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Exodus 13:12; Leviticus 27:26"> “Every firstling male that is born of your herd and of your flock you shall consecrate to Yahweh your God; you shall not do work with the firstling of your ox, and you shall not shear the firstling of your flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:17; Deuteronomy 14:23; Deuteronomy 12:5–7"> Rather ⸤before Yahweh⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “in the face of Yahweh”">af</a></sup>your God you shall eat it year by year at the place Yahweh will choose, you and your household.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:1; Malachi 1:7–8; Leviticus 22:19–25"> But if there is a physical defect in it, such as lameness or blindness, any serious defect, you shall not sacrifice it to Yahweh your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:21–22; Deuteronomy 12:15–16"> In your ⸤towns⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “gates”">ag</a></sup>you shall eat it, the unclean and the clean together may eat it, just as they eat the gazelle and as they eat the deer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:16; Leviticus 7:26; Deuteronomy 12:23"> But you shall not eat its blood; you shall pour it on the ground like water.”</span>
