## Ceremonies

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 15:14; Luke 11:28; James 2:10"> Then Moses and the elders of Israel charged the people, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to say”">a</a></sup>“Keep all of the commandment that I am commanding you ⸤today⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “the day”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 8:31; Ezekiel 36:26; Ezekiel 11:19"> And then on the day that you cross the Jordan to the land that Yahweh your God is giving to you, then you shall set up ⸤for yourselves⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “for you”">c</a></sup>large stones, and you shall paint<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “coat/cover them”">d</a></sup>them with lime,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 8:32; Deuteronomy 26:9; 2 Corinthians 3:2–3"> and you shall write on them all the words of this law at your crossing, so that you may come into the land that Yahweh your God is giving to you, a land flowing with milk and honey, as Yahweh, the God of your ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “fathers”">e</a></sup>⸤promised⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “spoke”">f</a></sup>to you.</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 20:25; Exodus 24:4; Joshua 8:30–31"> And you shall build an altar there for Yahweh your God, an altar of stone, but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “and”">j</a></sup>⸤you shall not use an iron tool to shape the stones⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “and you shall not wave to and fro over them an iron tool”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Leviticus 1:1–17"> You must build the altar of your God with unhewn stones, and you shall sacrifice on it burnt offerings to Yahweh your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 3:1–17; Isaiah 12:3; Psalm 100:1–2"> And you shall sacrifice fellowship offerings, and you shall eat them there, and you shall rejoice ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">l</a></sup>Yahweh your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:2; John 16:25; 2 Corinthians 3:12"> You shall write on the stone all of the words of this law very clearly.”</span>

## Blessings and Curses

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Moses charged the people on that day, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “to say”">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 9:7; Genesis 30:24; Genesis 35:18"> “These tribes shall stand on Mount Gerizim to bless the people ⸤when you cross⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “at/in crossing your”">q</a></sup>the Jordan: Simeon and Levi and Judah and Issachar and Joseph and Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:4; Genesis 29:32; Genesis 30:20"> And these shall stand on Mount Ebal for delivering the curse: Reuben, Gad and Asher and Zebulun, Dan and Naphtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:11; Deuteronomy 33:9–10; Joshua 8:33"> And ⸤the Levites shall declare⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “they shall answer the Levites”">r</a></sup>and they shall say to each man of Israel with a loud voice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 34:17; Exodus 20:4; Deuteronomy 5:8"> ‘Cursed be the man that makes a divine image or a cast image, which is a detestable thing for Yahweh, the work of the hand of a skilled craftsman, and then sets it in a hiding place.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “a secret place”">s</a></sup>And ⸤all the people shall respond⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “and they shall answer all of the people”">t</a></sup>‘Amen.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 19:3; Exodus 21:17; Deuteronomy 21:18–21"> ‘Cursed be the one who dishonors his father or his mother.’ And all of the people shall say, ‘Amen.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:14; Proverbs 22:28; Proverbs 23:10–11"> ‘Cursed be the one who moves the boundary marker of his neighbor.’ And all the people shall say, ‘Amen.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 19:14; Matthew 15:14; Proverbs 28:10"> ‘Cursed be the one who misleads a blind person on the road.’ And all the people shall say, ‘Amen.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:18; Deuteronomy 24:17; Proverbs 17:23"> ‘Cursed be the one who deprives the alien, the orphan, and the widow of justice.’ And all the people shall say, ‘Amen.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:30; Leviticus 18:8; 1 Corinthians 5:1"> ‘Cursed be the one who lies with the wife of his father, because ⸤he has dishonored his father’s bed⸥.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “because he uncovered the hem of his father”">u</a></sup>And all the people shall say, ‘Amen.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 18:23; Exodus 22:19; Leviticus 20:15"> ‘Cursed be the one who lies with any kind of animal.’ And all the people shall say, ‘Amen.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17; Leviticus 18:9; Ezekiel 22:11"> ‘Cursed be the one who lies with his sister, the daughter of his father or the daughter of his mother.’ And all the people shall say, ‘Amen.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 20:14; Leviticus 18:17"> ‘Cursed be the one who lies with his mother-in-law.’ And all the people shall say, ‘Amen.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 35:31; Leviticus 24:17; 2 Samuel 11:15–17"> ‘Cursed be the one who strikes down his neighbor in secret.’ And all the people shall say, ‘Amen.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:17; Exodus 23:7–8; Psalm 15:5"> ‘Cursed be the one who takes a bribe ⸤to murder an innocent person⸥.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “to strike down an individual person in blood”">v</a></sup>And all the people shall say, ‘Amen.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Romans 10:5"> ‘Cursed be the one ⸤who does not keep⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “who not keeps”">w</a></sup>the words of this law, to observe them.’ And all the people shall say, ‘Amen.’ ”</span>
</div>
