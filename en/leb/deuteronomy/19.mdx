<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:29; Deuteronomy 6:10; Deuteronomy 7:1–2"> “When Yahweh your God has ⸤exterminated⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “cut off”">a</a></sup>the nations concerning whom Yahweh your God is giving to you their land, and you have dispossessed them, and you have settled in their towns and in their houses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 21:13; Joshua 20:2–7; Deuteronomy 4:41–43"> you shall set apart three cities for you in the midst of your land that Yahweh your God is giving to you to take possession of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 57:14; Hebrews 12:13; Isaiah 35:8"> You shall prepare the roads<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew “road”">b</a></sup>for yourselves, and you shall divide the regions of your land into thirds that Yahweh your God gives you as a possession, so that ⸤it will be available for any manslayer to flee there⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “it shall be to flee there all/anyone killing”">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 35:15–24; Deuteronomy 4:42; Isaiah 30:33"> “Now this is the case of the manslayer who may flee there and live there who has killed his neighbor ⸤unintentionally⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “with no knowledge”">d</a></sup>and he did not hate him ⸤previously⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “from yesterday and the day before”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 35:25; Proverbs 27:12; 2 Kings 6:5–7"> ⸤For example⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “And as”">f</a></sup>when somebody goes with his neighbor into the forest to cut wood, and the iron head slips from the handle of the tool and strikes his neighbor and he dies, then he may flee to one of these cities, and so he may live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 35:12; 2 Samuel 14:7; Deuteronomy 21:22"> He does this lest the avenger of blood might pursue after the killer, because ⸤he is hot with anger⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “is hot his heart”">g</a></sup>and he overtakes him, because it is a long distance to the city of refuge, and so ⸤he kills him⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “he strikes him down as to his life/soul”">h</a></sup>but ⸤he did not deserve a death sentence⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “for him there was not a judgment of death”">i</a></sup>because he was not hating him ⸤before⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “from yesterday and the day before”">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Therefore I am commanding you, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “to say”">k</a></sup>‘You shall set apart three cities.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:20; Exodus 34:24; Exodus 23:31"> Then if Yahweh your God enlarges your territory just as he swore to your ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “fathers”">l</a></sup>and gives<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Hebrew “will give”">m</a></sup>to you all the land that he ⸤promised⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “spoke”">n</a></sup>to give to your ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “fathers”">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 20:7–8; Deuteronomy 11:22–25; Deuteronomy 12:32"> then ⸤if you diligently observe this entire commandment⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “if you observe all of the commandment the this to do it”">p</a></sup>that I am commanding you ⸤today⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “the day”">q</a></sup>by loving Yahweh your God and by going<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “walking”">r</a></sup>in his ways ⸤at all times⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “all of the days”">s</a></sup>then you shall add three more cities for yourselves to these three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Proverbs 6:17; Matthew 27:4–5"> Do this so that innocent blood will not be shed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Hebrew “is not shed”">t</a></sup>in the midst of your land that Yahweh your God is giving to you as an inheritance and thereby bloodguilt would be on you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Hebrew “shall be on you,” but conditional sense of imperfect is clear">u</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 35:16–21; Deuteronomy 27:24; Genesis 9:6"> But if someone hates<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Hebrew “is hating”">v</a></sup>his neighbor and lies in wait for him and rises up<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or “gets up”">w</a></sup>against him ⸤and murders him⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “and strikes him mortally with regard to his life and he dies”">x</a></sup>and the murderer flees to one of these cities,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 2:28–34; 1 Kings 2:5–6"> then the elders of his city shall send and take him from there, and they shall give him into the hand of the avenger of blood, and he shall be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 2:31; Deuteronomy 21:9; Deuteronomy 7:16"> Your eye shall not take pity on him, and you shall purge the guilt of innocent blood from Israel, ⸤so that good will be directed toward you⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “and good shall be for you” or “it shall be good for you”">y</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:17; Proverbs 22:28; Proverbs 23:10"> “You shall not move the boundary marker of your neighbor that ⸤former generations⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “the first settlers/ancestors”">z</a></sup>set up on your property in the land that Yahweh your God is giving to you to take possession of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 35:30; Deuteronomy 17:6; 2 Corinthians 13:1"> ⸤The testimony of a single witness may not be used to convict⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “Not shall get up only a single witness against a man”">aa</a></sup>with respect to any crime and for any wrongdoing in any offense that a person<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Hebrew “he”">ab</a></sup>committed; on the ⸤evidence⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “mouth”">ac</a></sup>of two witnesses or on the ⸤evidence⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “mouth”">ad</a></sup>of three witnesses ⸤a charge shall be sustained⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “shall be established a case/charge”">ae</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 27:12; Psalm 35:11; Exodus 23:1–7"> If ⸤a malicious witness⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “a witness of violence”">af</a></sup>gets up ⸤to accuse⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “against”">ag</a></sup>anyone to testify against him falsely,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:9; Deuteronomy 21:5; Malachi 2:7"> then the two men ⸤to whom the legal dispute pertains⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “who for them are the legal dispute”">ah</a></sup>shall stand ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">ai</a></sup>Yahweh, ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">aj</a></sup>the priests and the judges who are in office in those days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:14; Deuteronomy 17:4; 2 Chronicles 19:6–7"> Then judges shall make a thorough inquiry, and if it turns out<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Or “in fact”">ak</a></sup>that the witness is a false witness and he testified falsely against his brother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 19:5; Proverbs 19:9; Daniel 6:24"> then you shall do to him as he meant<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Or “planned”">al</a></sup>to do to his brother, and so you shall purge the evil from your midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:21; Deuteronomy 17:13; Deuteronomy 13:11"> ⸤And the rest⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Literally “and those remaining”">am</a></sup>shall hear and shall fear, and ⸤they shall not continue to do such a thing again⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Literally “and they shall not do again to do again”">an</a></sup>as this evil thing in your midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 5:38–39; Exodus 21:23–25; Deuteronomy 19:13"> ⸤You must show no pity⸥:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Literally “And not take pity your eye”">ao</a></sup>life for life, eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot.”</span>
</div>
