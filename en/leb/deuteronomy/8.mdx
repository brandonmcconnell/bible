<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:1; Deuteronomy 5:32–3; 1 Thessalonians 4:1–2"> “All of the commandments<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “every commandment”">a</a></sup>that I am commanding you today you must diligently observe, so that you may live and multiply, and you may go and take possession of the land that Yahweh swore to your ancestors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “your fathers”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 1:3; 1 Peter 1:7; Deuteronomy 8:16"> And you shall remember all of the way that Yahweh your God led you during these forty years in the desert in order to humble you and to test you to know what is in your heart, whether you would diligently keep his commandments or not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 4:4; Luke 4:4; Hebrews 13:5–6"> And he humbled you and let you go hungry, and then he fed you with that which you did not know nor did your ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “fathers”">c</a></sup>know, in order to make you know that not by bread alone but by all that goes out<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “by all of the going out of”">d</a></sup>of the mouth of Yahweh humankind shall live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:21; Deuteronomy 29:5; Matthew 26:25–30"> Your clothing did not wear out ⸤on you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “from on you”">e</a></sup>and your feet did not swell during these forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 3:12; 2 Samuel 7:14"> And you should know with your heart that as a man disciplines his son, so Yahweh your God is disciplining you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “has disciplined you”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:31; Deuteronomy 5:33; 1 Samuel 12:24"> So you must keep the commandments of Yahweh your God by walking in his ways and by fearing him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:9–12; Exodus 3:8; Ezekiel 20:6"> For Yahweh your God is bringing you to a good land with streams of water, springs and underground water, welling up in the valleys<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew “valley”">g</a></sup>and in the hills,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “hill”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 6:13; Psalm 147:14; Deuteronomy 32:14"> to a land of wheat and barley and vines<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “vine”">i</a></sup>and fig trees<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Hebrew “fig tree”">j</a></sup>and pomegranate trees,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Hebrew “pomegranate tree”">k</a></sup>a land of olive trees,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Hebrew “olive tree”">l</a></sup>olive oil and honey;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:14; Deuteronomy 33:25; Joshua 22:8"> to a land where you may eat food in it ⸤without scarcity⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “not in scarcity”">m</a></sup>you will not find anything lacking in it, a land where its stones are iron and from its mountains you can mine copper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 103:2; Romans 14:6; Proverbs 3:9"> And you will eat, and ⸤you will be satisfied⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “you will eat your fill”">n</a></sup>and you will bless Yahweh your God because of the good land that he has given to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 106:21; Hosea 2:8–9; Proverbs 1:32"> “Take care for yourself so that you not forget Yahweh your God by not keeping his commandments and his regulations and his statutes that I am commanding you ⸤today⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “the day”">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 30:9; Deuteronomy 28:47; Deuteronomy 32:15"> lest when you have eaten and you are satisfied and you have built good houses and you live in them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 1:3; Genesis 13:1–5; Luke 12:13–21"> and your herds and your flocks have multiplied, and ⸤you have accumulated silver and gold⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “and silver and gold has multiplied for you”">p</a></sup>and all that ⸤you have⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “is for you”">q</a></sup>has multiplied,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 106:21; 1 Corinthians 4:7–8; Deuteronomy 8:11"> then your heart ⸤becomes proud⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “raises up”">r</a></sup>and you forget Yahweh your God, ⸤the one who brought you out⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “the one bringing you out”">s</a></sup>from the land of Egypt, from the house of slavery,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 21:6; Numbers 20:11; Jeremiah 2:6"> the one leading you in the great and terrible desert infested with ⸤dangerous⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “burning”">t</a></sup>snakes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Hebrew “snake”">u</a></sup>and scorpions<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Hebrew “scorpion”">v</a></sup>and parched ground, where there is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or “was”">w</a></sup>no water, and the one bringing out water for you from flint rock,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:3; James 1:12; Exodus 16:15"> the one ⸤feeding you⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “causing you to eat”">x</a></sup>manna in the desert, food that your ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Or “fathers”">y</a></sup>did not know, in order to humble you and in order to test you so that he could do good to you ⸤in the future⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “in the end/later”">z</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:4; 1 Corinthians 4:7; Daniel 4:30"> And you may think<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Or “say”">aa</a></sup>in your heart, ‘My strength and the might of my hand ⸤acquired this wealth for⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “gave this wealth to”">ab</a></sup>me.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 10:22; Hosea 2:8; Deuteronomy 7:8"> But you must remember Yahweh your God, for he is the one giving you strength to acquire wealth in order to confirm his covenant that he swore to your ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Or “fathers”">ac</a></sup>⸤as it is today⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “as the day the this”">ad</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:26; 1 Samuel 12:25; Zephaniah 1:18"> And it will happen that if you indeed forget Yahweh your God and you go after other gods and you serve them and you bow down before them, I warn you today that you will surely perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 9:11–12; 2 Chronicles 36:16–17"> As with the nations that Yahweh is destroying ⸤before you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “from your faces”">ae</a></sup>so you shall perish because you would not obey the voice of Yahweh your God.</span>
</div>
