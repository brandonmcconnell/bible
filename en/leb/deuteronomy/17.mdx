<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 2:20; Hosea 8:1; Joshua 7:11"> If there is found in one of your ⸤towns⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “gates”">c</a></sup>that Yahweh your God is giving to you a man or a woman that does evil in the eyes of Yahweh your God to transgress his covenant</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:31; Deuteronomy 4:19; Job 31:26–27"> and by going and serving other gods and so he bows down to them and to the sun or to the moon or to any of the host of heaven ⸤which I have forbidden⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “which not I have commanded”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 7:51; Deuteronomy 13:12–14; Deuteronomy 19:18"> and it is reported to you or you hear about it and you enquire about it thoroughly and, indeed,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “look!”">e</a></sup>the trustworthiness of the deed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “the thing”">f</a></sup>has been established, it ⸤has occurred⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “has been done”">g</a></sup>this detestable thing, in Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 24:14; Joshua 7:25; Leviticus 24:16"> then you shall bring out that man or that woman who has<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “have”">h</a></sup>done this evil thing to your gates; that is, the man or the woman, and you shall stone them with stones ⸤to death⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “and so they die”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 35:30; Hebrews 10:28; 1 Timothy 5:19"> ⸤On the evidence of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “By the mouth of”">j</a></sup>two or three witnesses ⸤the person shall be put to death⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “he/she shall be put to death, the dead person”">k</a></sup>The person<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Hebrew “he/she”">l</a></sup>shall not be put to death by the mouth of one witness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:9; 1 Corinthians 5:13; Deuteronomy 17:12"> The hand of the witnesses shall be first against the person<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Hebrew “him”">m</a></sup>to kill the person,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Hebrew “him”">n</a></sup>and afterward the hands<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Hebrew “hand”">o</a></sup>of all the people, and so you shall purge the evil from your midst.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5; Haggai 2:11; Deuteronomy 1:17"> “⸤If a matter is too difficult for you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “If is difficult/wonderful from you a thing for judgment”">p</a></sup>for example disputes between blood and blood,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “between one homicide and another”">q</a></sup>between legal claim and legal claim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “one kind of lawsuit and another”">r</a></sup>and between assault and assault<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “one kind of abuse and another”">s</a></sup>and between matters of discernment in your ⸤towns⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “gates”">t</a></sup>then you shall get up and you shall go to the place that Yahweh your God will choose;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:24; Haggai 2:11; Malachi 2:7"> then you shall go to the priests and the Levites and to the judge who will be in office in those days, and you shall enquire, and they shall announce to you ⸤the verdict⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “the word of the decision”">u</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 22:2–3"> “And ⸤you shall carry out exactly the decision⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “and you shall do according to the mouth of the word”">v</a></sup>that they announced to you from that place that Yahweh will choose, and ⸤you shall diligently observe⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “and you shall observe and do”">w</a></sup>according to all that they instruct you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 1:7; Deuteronomy 5:32; Romans 13:1–6"> And so according to ⸤the instruction of the law⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “mouth of the law”">x</a></sup>that they teach you and according to the decisions that they say to you, you shall do; you shall not turn from the word that they tell you to the right or to the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 15:30; Deuteronomy 13:5; Deuteronomy 18:5"> And the man who treats with contempt<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Or “acts presumptuously”">y</a></sup>so as not to listen to the priest who is standing to minister on behalf of Yahweh your God or to the judge, that man shall die; so you shall purge the evil from Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:11; Deuteronomy 19:20; Numbers 15:30–31"> And all the people will hear and will be afraid, and they will not behave presumptuously again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:19–20; 1 Samuel 12:19; Joshua 1:13"> “When you have come to that land that Yahweh your God is giving to you and you have taken possession of it and you have settled in it, and you say, ‘I will set over me a king like all the nations that are around me,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:21; 1 Chronicles 22:10; 1 Chronicles 12:23"> indeed, you may set a king over you whom Yahweh your God will choose, from the midst of your countrymen<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “brothers”">z</a></sup>you must set a king over you; you are not allowed to appoint over you a man, a foreigner, who is not your countryman.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Or “brother”">aa</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 31:1–3; Ezekiel 17:15; Deuteronomy 28:68"> Except, he may ⸤not make numerous⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “not multiply”">ab</a></sup>for himself horses, and he may not allow the people to to go to Egypt ⸤in order to increase horses⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “in order to make numerous horse”">ac</a></sup>for Yahweh has said to you that ⸤you may never return⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “not you may do again to return”">ad</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:26; Matthew 19:5; Proverbs 30:8–9"> And he must not ⸤acquire many⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Or “make numerous”">ae</a></sup>wives for himself, so that his heart would turn aside; and ⸤he must not accumulate silver and gold for himself excessively⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “gold and silver not he must make numerous for him very”">af</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:9; 2 Kings 22:8; 2 Chronicles 34:15"> “⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “And it shall happen”">ag</a></sup>⸤when he is sitting⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “as/when his sitting”">ah</a></sup>on the throne of his kingdom, then he shall write for himself a copy of this law on a scroll ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">ai</a></sup>the Levitical priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 1:8; Psalm 119:97–100; Psalm 1:2"> And it shall be with him, and he shall read it<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Hebrew “in it”">aj</a></sup>all the days of his life, so that he may learn to revere Yahweh your God by ⸤diligently observing⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Literally “by keeping ... to do them”">ak</a></sup>all the word of this law and these rules,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:32; 1 Kings 15:5; Psalm 131:1–2"> so as not to exalt his heart above his countrymen<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Or “brothers”">al</a></sup>and not to turn aside from the commandment to the right or to the left, so that ⸤he may reign long over his kingdom⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Literally “he may make long his days over his kingdom”">am</a></sup>he and his children in the midst of Israel.”</span>
