</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 2:2; Zechariah 13:2; Numbers 33:52"> And you shall break down their altars, and you shall smash their stone pillars, and their Asherah poles you must burn with fire, and the images of their gods you shall hew down, and you shall blot out their names from that place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 20:23; Deuteronomy 20:18; Deuteronomy 16:21–22"> You shall not worship Yahweh your God like this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:11; Deuteronomy 26:2; 2 Chronicles 7:12"> ⸤But only⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “But if”">d</a></sup>to the place that Yahweh your God will choose from all of your tribes to place his name there as his dwelling shall you seek, and there you shall go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:19–20; Malachi 3:10; Leviticus 27:32–33"> And you shall bring there your burnt offerings and your sacrifices and your tithes and ⸤your donations⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “the contributions of your hand”">e</a></sup>and your votive gifts and your freewill offerings and the firstling of your herd and your flock.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 21:25; Judges 17:6; Proverbs 21:2"> “You must not do just as we are doing here ⸤today⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “the day”">h</a></sup>⸤each according to all that is right in his eyes⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “each man all of the right in his eyes”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 8:56; Psalm 95:11; Deuteronomy 25:19"> For you have not come up to now to the resting place and to the inheritance that Yahweh your God is giving to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:11; Ezekiel 34:28; Deuteronomy 3:27"> But you will cross the Jordan, and you will settle in the land that Yahweh your God is giving you as an inheritance, and he will give rest to you from all your enemies from all around, and you will live securely,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5; Deuteronomy 15:20; Deuteronomy 17:8"> ⸤and then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “and it will happen”">j</a></sup>at the place that Yahweh your God will choose, to let his name dwell there, there you shall bring all the things I am commanding you, your burnt offerings and your sacrifices, your tithes and ⸤your donations⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “the contribution of your hand”">k</a></sup>and all of the choice things, your votive gifts that you vow to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:7; Deuteronomy 10:9; Deuteronomy 26:12"> And you shall rejoice ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “the face of”">l</a></sup>Yahweh your God, you and your sons and your daughters and your slaves and your slave women and the Levite who is in your ⸤towns⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “gates”">m</a></sup>because there is not for him a plot of ground and an inheritance with you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 12:28–32; 1 Kings 15:34; 2 Chronicles 15:17"> “Take care for yourself so that you do not offer your burnt offerings at just any place that you happen to see,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:11; 2 Corinthians 5:19; Deuteronomy 12:5"> ⸤but only⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “but only if”">n</a></sup>at the place that Yahweh will choose among one of your tribes; there you shall offer your burnt offerings, and there you shall do all the things that I am commanding you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 9:4; Deuteronomy 15:23; Deuteronomy 12:23–24"> Only the blood you must not eat, but on the ground you must pour it like water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:14; Deuteronomy 12:6; Deuteronomy 14:22–29"> You are not allowed to eat in your ⸤towns⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “gates”">q</a></sup>the tithe of your grain and your wine and your olive oil and the firstborn of your herd and your flock and all of your votive gifts that you vowed and your freewill offering and ⸤your donations⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “the contribution of your hand”">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:7; Deuteronomy 14:23; Deuteronomy 12:5"> But only ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “before the face of”">s</a></sup>Yahweh your God you shall eat it, at the place that Yahweh your God will choose,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Hebrew “choose it”">t</a></sup>you and your son and your daughter and your slave and your slave woman and the Levite who is in your ⸤towns⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “gate”">u</a></sup>and you must rejoice ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “before the face of”">v</a></sup>your God ⸤in all your undertakings⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “in all of the sending of your hand”">w</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:4–21; Nehemiah 10:34–39; Deuteronomy 14:27–29"> ⸤Take care⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “Watch for yourself”">x</a></sup>so that you do not neglect the Levite all of your days on your land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:8; Deuteronomy 11:24; Exodus 34:24"> “When Yahweh your God enlarges your territory just as he has ⸤promised⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “spoken”">y</a></sup>to you, and you say ‘I want to eat some meat,’ ⸤because you want it⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “because your soul/inner self desires it”">z</a></sup>⸤whenever you desire⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “at/in all the wanting of your soul/inner self”">aa</a></sup>you may eat meat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5; 1 Kings 14:21; Deuteronomy 16:11"> If the place that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Or “where”">ab</a></sup>Yahweh your God will choose to put his name there is too far from you, and you slaughter any of your herd and any of your flock that Yahweh has given to you just as I have commanded you, then you may eat whenever you desire in your ⸤towns⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “gates”">ac</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 1:13; Leviticus 17:11; Leviticus 1:9"> And you shall offer your burnt offerings, the flesh and the blood on the altar of Yahweh your God, and the blood of your sacrifices you shall pour out on the altar of Yahweh your God, but the meat you may eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; John 15:14; John 15:10"> ⸤Be careful to obey⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “Watch carefully and listen”">ag</a></sup>all these things that I am commanding you, so that ⸤it will go well⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “he/it is good”">ah</a></sup>for you and for your children after you ⸤forever⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Literally “until ever”">ai</a></sup>because then you will be doing what is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Hebrew “the”">aj</a></sup>good and right<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Hebrew “the right”">ak</a></sup>in the eyes of Yahweh your God.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 23:4; Deuteronomy 19:1; Deuteronomy 9:3"> “When Yahweh your God has cut off the nations whom you are ⸤about to go to⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Literally “about to go to there”">al</a></sup>to dispossess them ⸤before you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Literally “from the face of you”">am</a></sup>and you have dispossessed them, and you live in their land,</span>
