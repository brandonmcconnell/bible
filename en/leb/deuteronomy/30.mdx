<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:19–20; Isaiah 63:17–18; Deuteronomy 4:26"> I declare to you ⸤today⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Literally “the day”">ai</a></sup>that you will certainly perish; ⸤you will not extend your time⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Literally “you will not make long days”">aj</a></sup>on the land that you are crossing the Jordan to go there to take possession of it.</span>

So choose life, so that you may live, you and your offspring,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Or “seed”">am</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 11:25–26; Deuteronomy 10:20; John 17:3"> by loving Yahweh your God by listening to his voice and by clinging to him, for he is your life and the length of your days in order for you to live on the land that Yahweh swore to your ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Or “fathers”">an</a></sup>to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, to give to them.”</span>
