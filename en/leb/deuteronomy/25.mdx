</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 22:24; Luke 20:28; Mark 12:19"> “When brothers dwell together and one of them dies and has no son, the wife of the deceased shall not become the wife of a ⸤man of another family⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Others: “strange man,” “man outside the family” (NEB)">j</a></sup>her brother-in-law ⸤shall have sex with her⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “shall go to her”">k</a></sup>and he shall take her ⸤to himself⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “to/for him”">l</a></sup>as a wife, and he shall perform his duty as a brother-in-law with respect to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:19; Psalm 9:5; Psalm 109:13"> And then the firstborn that she bears ⸤shall represent his dead brother⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “he shall stand upon the name of his brother the deceased”">m</a></sup>so that his name is not blotted out from Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ruth 4:5–6; Ruth 4:1–2"> But if the man does not want to take his sister-in-law, then his sister-in-law shall go up to the gate, to the elders, and she shall say, ‘My brother-in-law refused ⸤to perpetuate his brother’s name⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “to cause a name to stand for his brother”">n</a></sup>in Israel, for he is not willing ⸤to marry me⸥.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “to consummate the marriage with the widow of a his brother”">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ruth 4:6"> Then the elders of his town shall summon him and speak to him, and if he persists and says, ‘⸤I do not desire to⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “I do not delight in”">p</a></sup>marry her’</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ruth 4:7–8; Numbers 12:14; Isaiah 50:6"> then his sister-in-law shall go near him before the eyes of the elders, and she shall pull off his sandal from his foot, and she shall spit in his face, and she shall ⸤declare⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “respond/answer”">q</a></sup>and she shall say, ‘This is how it is done to the man who does not build the house of his brother.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And his ⸤family⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “name”">r</a></sup>shall be called in Israel, ‘The house where the sandal was pulled off.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “the house of the man whose sandal was pulled off”">s</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:13; Deuteronomy 19:21; Deuteronomy 7:2"> then you shall cut off her hand; your eye shall not take pity.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 11:1; Proverbs 16:11; Leviticus 19:35–37"> “There shall not be ⸤for your use⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “to you”">t</a></sup>in your bag ⸤two kinds of stone weights, a large one and a small one⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “stone and stone large and small”">u</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> There shall not be in your house ⸤for your use⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “for you”">v</a></sup>⸤two kinds of measures⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “ephah and ephah large and small”">w</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Deuteronomy 4:40; Ephesians 6:3"> Rather a full and honest weight shall be ⸤for your use⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “for you”">x</a></sup>there shall be for you a full and honest ⸤measure⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “an ephah”">y</a></sup>so that your days on the land that Yahweh your God is giving to you may be long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 11:1; Revelation 21:27; Deuteronomy 22:5"> For detestable to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “for”">z</a></sup>Yahweh your God is everyone who is doing such things,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Or “are all who are doing”">aa</a></sup>everyone who is acting<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Or “all who are acting”">ab</a></sup>dishonestly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 17:8–16; Numbers 25:17–18; Numbers 24:20"> “Remember what Amalek did to you on the journey when<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Or “at/in”">ac</a></sup>you went out from Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 3:18; Psalm 36:1; Proverbs 16:6"> that he met you on the journey and attacked you, all those lagging behind you and when you were weary and worn out, and he did not fear God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 17:14; 1 Samuel 14:48; Exodus 17:16"> ⸤And when⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “And it will happen when”">ad</a></sup>Yahweh your God gives rest to you from all your enemies from around about you in the land that Yahweh your God is giving to you as an inheritance to take possession of it, you shall blot out the remembrance of Amalek from under the heavens; you shall not forget!”</span>
</div>
