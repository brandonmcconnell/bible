## Judah’s Impending Judgment

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:2; 2 Chronicles 34:1–33; 2 Peter 1:19"> The word of Yahweh that came to Zephaniah the son of Cushi, the son of Gedaliah, the son of Amariah, the son of Hezekiah, in the days of Josiah son of Amon, king of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 7:13; 2 Kings 22:16–17; Ezekiel 33:27–29"> <span className="pl pl-1">“I will surely destroy everything</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from the face of the earth”—a declaration of Yahweh.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 4:3; Ezekiel 7:19; Jeremiah 12:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">“I will destroy humanity and beast;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will destroy the birds of the sky</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the fish of the sea,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the stumbling blocks</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A reference to idols">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with the wicked.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And I will cut off humankind</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from the face of the earth”—a declaration of Yahweh.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 10:5; Micah 5:13; 2 Kings 23:4–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">“And I will stretch out my hand against Judah</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and against all the inhabitants of Jerusalem.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And I will cut off from this place</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the remnant of Baal,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the name of idolatrous priests with the priests,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The last phrase, “with the priests,” may be a later explanatory gloss">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:13; 1 Kings 11:33; 2 Kings 17:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">and those who bow down on the rooftops</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to the host of heaven,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and those who bow down, swearing to Yahweh</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but also swearing by Milcom,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “their king”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 1:4; Jeremiah 2:13; Jeremiah 15:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">and those who turned back from ⸤following⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “after”">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and who did not seek Yahweh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and did not inquire of him.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:20; Zechariah 2:13; Zephaniah 1:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Be silent before the Lord Yahweh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for the day of Yahweh draws near,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for Yahweh has provided a sacrifice</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and has consecrated his guests.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 24:21; Isaiah 3:18–24; Jeremiah 22:24–30"> <span className="pl pl-1">And it shall be that on the day of the sacrifice of Yahweh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will punish the officials</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the sons of the king</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and those who dress in foreign clothing.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:5; Amos 3:10; 1 Samuel 2:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">And on that day I will punish</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">all who leap over the threshold,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who fill up the house of their master</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with violence and deceit.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:14; Amos 8:3; 2 Kings 22:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">“And there shall be on that day”—a declaration of Yahweh—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“a ⸤loud outcry⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “voice of a cry of distress”">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from the Fish Gate,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and a wailing from the Second District,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and a loud</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “great”">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">crashing from the hills.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="James 5:1; Jeremiah 25:34; Hosea 12:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">The inhabitants of the Mortar shall wail,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for all the traders have perished;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">all who trade with silver have been cut off.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 6:1; Jeremiah 48:11; Ezekiel 8:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">And it shall be that at that time</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will search Jerusalem with lamps,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will punish the men</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">⸤whose senses are dulled from drinking⸥,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “who are thickened with their dregs”">g</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who say in their hearts,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘Yahweh will not do good, nor will he do evil.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Amos 5:11; Micah 6:15; Deuteronomy 28:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then their wealth shall be as plunder,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their homes as desolation.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And they shall build their houses and not inhabit them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they shall plant vineyards and not drink their wine.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Ezekiel 30:3; Zephaniah 1:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">The great day of Yahweh draws near;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it is near and coming very swiftly.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The sound of the day of Yahweh is bitterness;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">there, a warrior cries out!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joel 2:2; Isaiah 22:5; Revelation 6:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">That day will be a day of wrath,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a day of trouble and distress,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a day of disaster and ruination,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a day of darkness and deep gloom,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a day of clouds</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “cloud”">h</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and thick clouds,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “cloud”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 8:1; Amos 3:6; Isaiah 32:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">a day of trumpet and trumpet blast</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">against the fortified cities</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and against the high corner towers.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 3:17; Psalm 83:10; Jeremiah 2:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I will bring distress to all humankind,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they shall walk about like the blind,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for they sinned against Yahweh;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">their blood shall be poured out like dust</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their entrails like dung.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:8; Ezekiel 7:19; Proverbs 11:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Moreover, their silver and their gold</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will not be able to save them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">on the day of the wrath of Yahweh.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And in the fire of his zeal,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the whole land shall be consumed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for a terrifying end he shall make</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for all the inhabitants of the land.</span></span>
