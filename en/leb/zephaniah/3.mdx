## Judgment of Jerusalem and the Nations

<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 9:9; Jeremiah 36:3; Micah 2:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have said, ‘Surely she will fear me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">she will accept discipline.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then her dwelling place will not be cut down,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">nor all that I have brought upon her.’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Surely they rise early;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they make all their deeds corrupt.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 54:4; Joel 2:26–27; Isaiah 11:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">On that day you shall not be humiliated</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">on account of all your deeds</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">by which you have rebelled against me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For then I shall remove from your midst</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">those exulting in your pride,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and you shall no longer be haughty</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in ⸤my holy mountain⸥.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “the mountain of my holiness”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 14:32; Nahum 1:7; Zechariah 13:8–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I will leave in your midst</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a people afflicted and poor,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they shall take refuge</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the name of Yahweh.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 14:5; Zephaniah 2:7; Micah 4:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">The remnant of Israel</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">shall not do wickedness;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they shall not speak deception,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">nor shall a deceitful tongue be found in their mouth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For they shall graze and lie down,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and none shall frighten them.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 12:6; Zechariah 2:10–11; Micah 4:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Shout for joy, O daughter of Zion!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Cry aloud, O Israel!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Rejoice and be jubilant with all your heart,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">O daughter of Jerusalem!</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 12:12; Isaiah 35:3–4; Isaiah 41:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">On that day it shall be said to Jerusalem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“Fear not, O Zion;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">your hands shall not hang limp.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 149:4; Psalm 147:11; Jeremiah 32:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh your God is in your midst;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a mighty warrior who saves.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He shall rejoice over you with joy;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he renews</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The translation follows the LXX text; the traditional Hebrew text reads “he will silence you in his love”">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you in his love;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he will exult over you with singing.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 42:2–4; Ezekiel 36:24; Lamentations 2:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will gather those of you grieving on account of the feast;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they were raising against her a reproach.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 49:25–26; Isaiah 60:14; Jeremiah 30:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Behold, at that time I will deal with all your oppressors;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will save the lame and gather the outcast.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will change</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “make”">g</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">them from shame</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to glory and ⸤renown⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “a name”">h</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">throughout the whole world.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:14; Malachi 3:12; Isaiah 56:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">At that time I will bring you in;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">at the time of my gathering you together.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For I will make you ⸤renowned⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “a name”">i</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and praised</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">among all the nations of the earth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">when I restore your fortunes before your eyes,”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">says Yahweh.</span></span>
