## Judgment of Judah’s Enemies

<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 17:13; Hosea 13:3; Nahum 1:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Before the birth of the decree,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the day flies away like chaff;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">before the fierce anger of Yahweh overtakes you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">before the day of the anger of Yahweh overtakes you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 5:14–15; Isaiah 26:20–21; Psalm 76:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Seek Yahweh, all you afflicted of the land</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who have fulfilled his law;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">seek righteousness;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">seek humility.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Perhaps you will be concealed</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">on the day of the anger of Yahweh.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:16; Jeremiah 47:7; Joshua 13:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Woe to you, inhabitants of the region of the sea,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">people of the Kerethites!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The word of Yahweh is against you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">O Canaan, land of the Philistines.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will destroy you ⸤until there is no inhabitant⸥.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “from there is not an inhabitant”">a</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 17:2; Ezekiel 25:5; Isaiah 5:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">And you, O region of the sea, shall become</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">pastures and meadows for shepherds</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and sheep pens for the flocks.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 4:31; Ezekiel 39:25; Zephaniah 3:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">And it shall become a region</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for the remnant of the house of Judah;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">upon them they shall graze,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and in the houses of Ashkelon</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they shall lie down in the evening.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For Yahweh their God shall be mindful of them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he will restore their fortunes.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 1:13; Psalm 83:4–7; Jeremiah 48:27–29"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have heard the reproaches of Moab</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the scorning of the ⸤Ammonites⸥,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with which they have taunted my nation</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and made boasts against their territory.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 15:1–9; Isaiah 11:14; Deuteronomy 29:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Therefore, as I live”—a declaration of Yahweh of hosts,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the God of Israel—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Moab shall be as Sodom,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the ⸤Ammonites⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like Gomorrah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a place of weeds and salt pits</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and a desolation forever.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The remnant of my people shall plunder them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the remainder of my nation shall possess them.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 16:6; Jeremiah 48:29; Zephaniah 2:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">This shall be for them in place of their pride</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because they mocked and boasted</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">against the people of Yahweh of hosts.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Micah 4:1–3; Zechariah 13:2; Psalm 138:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh will be awesome against them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for he will destroy all the gods of the earth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and all the lands</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “islands”">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">of the nations;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">each in its place will bow down to him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:4–9; Isaiah 43:3; Jeremiah 51:20–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">You also, O Cushites,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they shall be killed by my sword.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Nahum 3:7; Nahum 1:1; Isaiah 10:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">And he will stretch out his hand against the north,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he will destroy Assyria</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and will make Nineveh a desolation,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a dry wasteland like the desert.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 13:19–22; Isaiah 34:11–17; Amos 9:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Herds shall lie down in her midst,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">every wild animal of a nation;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">even the desert owl and the screech owl</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">shall lodge on her capitals.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">A voice shall hoot in the window;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">rubble</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The traditional Hebrew text has “waste,” while LXX has “a raven,” reflecting a different word with one consonant changed">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">on the threshold;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for the cedar is laid bare.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:2; Isaiah 22:2; Isaiah 47:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">This is the city of rejoicing</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that lived securely;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the one saying in her heart,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“⸤I am, and there is none besides me⸥.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “I am and none me”">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">How she has become a desolation,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a lair for wild animals!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Everyone who passes by her hisses and shakes his fist.</span></span>
