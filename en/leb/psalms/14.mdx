## The Folly of the Godless and God’s Final Triumph

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">For the music director. Of David.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ps 14 The Hebrew Bible counts the superscription as the first verse of the psalm">a</a></sup></div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 10:4; Psalm 53:1; Romans 3:10–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">The fool says in his heart, “There is no God.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They are corrupt.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They do abominable deeds.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">There is none who does good.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 3:11; Psalm 33:13–14; Hebrews 11:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh looks down from heaven upon the children of humankind</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to see whether there is one who has insight,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">one who cares about God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 3:10–12; Isaiah 53:6; Isaiah 64:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">All have gone astray;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they are altogether corrupt.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">There is not one who does good;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">there is not even one.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 79:6; Jeremiah 10:25; Psalm 82:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">All who do evil—do they not know,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they who eat my people as though they were eating bread?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They do not call on Yahweh.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 112:2; Psalm 73:15; Psalm 24:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">There ⸤they are very fearful⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “they feared a fear”">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because God is with the generation of the righteous.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 9:9; Psalm 42:10; Psalm 4:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">You would put to shame the plan of the poor,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “but”">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh is his refuge.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 53:6; Psalm 85:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">⸤Oh that from Zion⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “Who will give that from Zion”">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">would come salvation for Israel!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">When Yahweh returns the fortunes of his people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Jacob will rejoice; Israel will be happy.</span></span>
