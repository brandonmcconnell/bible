<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">A song. A psalm of the sons of Korah.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 28:1; 2 Corinthians 1:9; Psalm 143:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am reckoned with those descending to the pit.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I am like a man</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The Hebrew suggests a strong, vigorous man">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">without strength,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “without help”">b</a></sup><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 6:5; Isaiah 26:19; Psalm 30:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do you work wonders</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “wonder”">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for the dead?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Or do the departed spirits</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew rephaim , sometimes translated “shades”; the weakened state of those who have died">g</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">rise up to praise you? Selah</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 5:3; Psalm 30:2; Mark 1:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">But as for me, I cry for help to you, O Yahweh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and in the morning my prayer comes before you.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 6:4; Luke 22:44; Job 7:11–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am afflicted and perishing from my youth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I bear your terrors. I am distraught.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Hebrew meaning uncertain">k</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 90:11; Isaiah 53:4–6; Romans 8:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your burning anger has passed over me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">your sudden fears have destroyed me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 22:16; Psalm 116:3; Psalm 69:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">They surround me like water all the day;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they circle about me altogether.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 88:8; Psalm 38:11; Job 19:12–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have removed loved one and friend far from me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">my acquaintances far from my darkness.</span></span>
