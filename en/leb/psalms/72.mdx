<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 7:9; Psalm 92:12–14; Isaiah 30:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">May there be an abundance of grain in the land</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">even on the top of the mountains.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">May his crop</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “May his descendants”">i</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">sway like the trees of Lebanon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and may those from the city blossom like the grass of the earth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:23; Psalm 89:36"> <span className="pl pl-1">May his name endure forever.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">May his name increase ⸤as long as the sun shines⸥,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “in the presence of the sun”">j</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and let them be blessed in him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let all nations call him blessed.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 41:13; Psalm 106:48; 1 Chronicles 29:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Blessed be Yahweh God, the God of Israel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who alone does wonderful things.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 14:21; Matthew 6:10; Psalm 41:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">And blessed be his glorious name forever,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and may the whole earth be filled with his glory.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Amen and Amen.</span><br /></span>
