## Praise to Yahweh for His Salvation and Judgment

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">A psalm.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ps 98 The Hebrew Bible counts the superscription as the first verse of the psalm">a</a></sup></div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 15:6; Isaiah 52:10; Psalm 33:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Sing to</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “sing concerning”">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh a new song, for he has done wonders.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His right hand and his holy arm have secured his victory.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 52:10; Isaiah 62:2; Psalm 22:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh has made known his salvation;</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “victory”">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to the eyes of the nations he has revealed his righteousness.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 22:27; Acts 13:47; Deuteronomy 4:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">He has remembered his loyal love and his faithfulness</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to the house of Israel.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">All the ends of the earth have seen</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the salvation</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “victory”">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">of our God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 44:23; Psalm 100:1; Psalm 66:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Shout joyfully to Yahweh, all the earth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Be cheerful</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “Burst forth”">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and sing for joy and sing praises.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 51:3; Revelation 5:8; Psalm 33:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Sing praises to Yahweh with lyre,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with lyre and melodious sound.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 81:2–4; Revelation 19:16; 2 Chronicles 15:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">With trumpets and sound of horn,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">shout joyfully before the king, Yahweh.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 49:13; Psalm 24:1; Psalm 96:11–1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let the sea with</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “and”">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">its fullness roar,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the world and those who live in it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 55:12; Psalm 89:12; Psalm 65:12–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let the rivers clap their hands.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let the hills sing joyfully together</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 96:13; Psalm 96:10; Acts 17:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">before Yahweh, for he is coming</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to judge the earth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will judge the world with righteousness</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the peoples with equity.</span></span>
