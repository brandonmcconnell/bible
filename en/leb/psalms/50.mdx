## An Oracle Concerning Sacrifices

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">A psalm of Asaph.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ps 50 The Hebrew Bible counts the superscription as the first verse of the psalm">a</a></sup></div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 113:3; Joshua 22:22; Matthew 25:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Supreme God, God, Yahweh, has spoken</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and summoned the earth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">from the rising of the sun</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to its setting.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 48:2; Psalm 80:1; Revelation 21:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">From Zion, the perfection of beauty,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God shines forth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 7:10; Numbers 16:35; Leviticus 10:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Our God comes and he is not silent.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Before him fire devours,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and around him it is very tempestuous.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Isaiah 1:2; Deuteronomy 31:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">He summons the heavens above</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the earth that he might judge his people:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 26:28; Exodus 24:3–8; Hebrews 13:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Gather to me my loyal ones,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">those who have made a covenant with me by sacrifice.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 97:6; Psalm 75:7; Psalm 89:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">And the heavens declare his righteousness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because God himself is judge. Selah</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 20:2; Psalm 81:8; Jeremiah 2:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Hear, O my people, and I will speak,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">O Israel, and I will testify against you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I am God, your God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 1:11–31; Hebrews 10:4–10; Hosea 6:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is not concerning a lack of your sacrifices that I rebuke you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and your burnt offerings are before me continually.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Micah 6:6–8; Hebrews 10:4–6; Psalm 69:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will not take from your house a bull</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">or from your stalls a he-goat,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:5–6; Psalm 8:6–8; Psalm 104:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">because every animal of the forest is mine,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the cattle on a thousand hills.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 1:20–22; Matthew 6:26; Luke 12:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">I know every bird of the mountains,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and every moving creature in the field is mine.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 19:5; Deuteronomy 10:14; Psalm 24:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">If I were hungry I would not tell you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because the world and its fullness are mine.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do I eat the flesh of bulls</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">or drink the blood of goats?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15; Psalm 56:12; Deuteronomy 23:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Offer to God a thank offering</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and pay your vows to the Most High.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 107:28; Psalm 91:15; Psalm 34:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">And call me in the day of trouble;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will deliver you, and you will glorify me.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Psalm 78:36–38; Romans 2:17–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">But to the wicked God says,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“What right have you to recite my statutes</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and ⸤mention my covenant with your mouth⸥,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “take my covenant on your mouth”">b</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:26; Proverbs 12:1; 2 Timothy 4:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">while you yourself hate discipline,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and cast my words behind you?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 1:32; 1 Timothy 5:22; Hebrews 13:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">When you see a thief, then you are pleased with him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and your association is with adulterers.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalm 10:7; Psalm 52:2–4; Psalm 55:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">You give your mouth free rein for evil,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and you harness your tongue to deceit.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 10:21; Proverbs 10:18; Leviticus 19:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">You sit and speak against your brother;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you slander your mother’s son.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 2:4–5; Proverbs 29:1; Isaiah 57:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">These things you have done, and I have been silent;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You imagined that I was just like you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will rebuke you and present an argument before your eyes.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 8:13; Psalm 9:17; Hosea 4:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Now consider this, you who forget God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">lest I tear you apart, and there will be none to deliver.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalm 91:16; James 3:13; Psalm 24:4–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">He who sacrifices a thank offering honors me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he who orders his way;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will show him the salvation of God.”</span><br /></span>
