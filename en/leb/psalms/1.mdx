## The Ways of the Righteous and the Wicked

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 26:4–5; Proverbs 4:14–15; Proverbs 13:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Blessed is the man who does not walk in the advice of the wicked;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">nor does he stand in the way of sinners;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">nor does he sit in the assembly of mockers.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 1:8; Psalm 119:92; Psalm 119:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Instead, in the law of Yahweh is his delight,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and on his law he meditates day and night.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:8; Ezekiel 47:12; Psalm 92:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">And so, he is like a tree planted by streams of water</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that gives its fruit in its season;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">its leaf also does not wither.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Therefore all that he does prospers.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 21:18; Matthew 3:12; Psalm 35:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Not so the wicked.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Instead, they are like the chaff that the wind scatters.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 5:5; Matthew 25:46; Malachi 3:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore the wicked will not stand in the judgment,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">nor sinners in the congregation of the righteous;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:19; John 10:14; Proverbs 15:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">for Yahweh knows the way of the righteous,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Plural">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the way of the wicked will perish.</span></span>
