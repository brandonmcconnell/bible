## A Prayer for Rescue from Enemies

<div className="bt">

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 142:3; Psalm 77:3; Psalm 102:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">And so my spirit grows faint within me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">my heart ⸤within⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “in the midst of”">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">me is desolate.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 77:5–6; Psalm 77:10–12; Micah 6:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">I remember the days of long ago;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I meditate on all your doings.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “doing”">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I muse on the labor of your hands.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 63:1; Job 11:13; Psalm 84:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">I stretch out my hands to you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">my soul longs for you like a dry land. Selah</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 69:17; Psalm 28:1; Psalm 27:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Quickly answer me, O Yahweh;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">my spirit fails.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Do not hide your face from me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">or I will become</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">like those descending to the pit.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 25:4–5; Proverbs 3:5–6; Isaiah 48:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Cause me to hear your loyal love in the morning,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for I trust you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Cause me to know the way that I should go,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for I lift up my soul to you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 18:10; Psalm 61:3–4; Psalm 59:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Deliver me from my enemies, O Yahweh.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I take refuge in you.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="This reading for the difficult Hebrew “I cover to you” is suggested by the LXX">d</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 25:4–5; John 14:26; Hebrews 13:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Teach me to do your will, for you are my God;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">your Spirit is good.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Lead me onto level ground.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 31:1; Ephesians 2:4–5; Psalm 71:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">For your name’s sake, O Yahweh, preserve my life;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in your righteousness bring me</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew “my soul”">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">out of trouble.</span><br /></span>
