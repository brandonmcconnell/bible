## Social and Spiritual Corruption

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 22:12; Amos 6:1; Amos 5:11"> Hear this word, you cows of Bashan who live on the mountain of Samaria, who oppress the powerless, who crush the poor, who say to their husbands, “Bring something so that we may drink!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:16; Psalm 89:35; Habakkuk 1:15–16"> My Lord Yahweh has sworn by his holiness that, “Behold, the days are coming upon you ⸤when they⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “and he”">a</a></sup>will take you away with hooks, even the last of you with fishing hooks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:5; Ezekiel 12:12; Matthew 16:26"> And you shall leave through breaches in the wall, each one in front of her. You will be dragged off toward Harmon,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The meaning of this Hebrew word is uncertain">b</a></sup>⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">c</a></sup>Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:39; Hosea 4:15; Numbers 28:3–4"> “Come to Bethel and transgress; to Gilgal and ⸤multiply transgression⸥!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “increase to transgress”">d</a></sup>Bring your sacrifices in the morning and your tithes on the ⸤third day⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “for three of days”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 22:18–21; Psalm 81:12; Hosea 9:10"> And bring a thank offering of unleavened bread, and proclaim freewill offerings, pronounce them, for so you love to do, O ⸤people⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “children”">f</a></sup>of Israel,” is the declaration of my Lord Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Haggai 2:17; Jeremiah 5:3; Isaiah 9:13"> “And I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Emphatic use of personal pronoun">g</a></sup>in turn gave you cleanness of teeth in all your cities and lack of bread in all your places. Yet you did not return to me,” is the declaration of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 9:26; Jeremiah 3:3; 2 Chronicles 7:13–14"> “And I also withheld the rain from you when there were still three months to the harvest. And I would send rain on one city and send no rain on another city. One tract of land will be rained on, and the tract of land ⸤on which it does not rain⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “which does not rain down on it”">h</a></sup>will dry up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 4:6; 1 Kings 18:5; Haggai 1:6"> So two or three cities wandered to one city to drink water and they were not satisfied, yet you did not return to me,” is the declaration of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:22; Haggai 2:17; Joel 1:4"> “I struck you with blight and with mildew. Frequently the cutting locust devoured your gardens and your vineyards and your fig trees and your olive trees, yet you did not return to me,” is the declaration of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:60; Leviticus 26:25; Joel 2:20"> “I sent among you a plague in the manner of Egypt; I killed your young men with the sword, along with the captivity of your horses. And I made the stench of your camps go up into your nostrils, yet you did not return to me,” is the declaration of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 13:19; Zechariah 3:2; Genesis 19:24–25"> “I overthrew some of you as God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah, and you were as a stick snatched from the fire, and yet you did not return to me,” is the declaration of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2–4; James 4:1–10; Amos 9:1–4"> “Therefore, thus I will do to you, O Israel. Because I will do this to you, O Israel, prepare to meet your God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Amos 5:8; Micah 1:3; Amos 9:6"> Yes, here is the one who forms the mountains, and the one who creates the wind, and the one who reveals to humans what his thoughts are, and the one who treads on the heights of the earth; Yahweh, the God of hosts, is his name!”</span>
