## A Lament and Call to Repentance

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:1; Jeremiah 9:10; Jeremiah 7:29"> Hear this word that I am going to intone over you as a lament, O house of Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:17; Lamentations 2:13; Jeremiah 2:27"> Virgin Israel has fallen and will not rise again. She is deserted on her land; there is no one to raise her up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 10:22; Ezekiel 12:16; Isaiah 6:13"> For thus says my Lord Yahweh, “The city that marches out with a thousand will only have a hundred left. And the one that marches out with a hundred will only have ten left for the house of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; Zephaniah 2:3; Jeremiah 29:12–13"> For thus says Yahweh to the house of Israel, “Seek me so that you may live.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 4:15; Amos 4:4; Amos 8:14"> But do not seek Bethel or go to Gilgal or cross over to Beersheba, for Gilgal will certainly go into exile and Bethel will come to disaster.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:11; Isaiah 55:6; Ezekiel 20:47–48"> Seek Yahweh so that you may live, so that he will not break out like a fire against the house of Joseph! And it will devour, ⸤with none to quench⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “and there is not one to quench”">a</a></sup>it for Bethel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 6:12; Amos 5:11–12; Zephaniah 1:6"> Ah, you who turn justice into wormwood and hurl righteousness to the ground!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 4:13; Job 9:9; Amos 9:6"> The one who made the Pleiades and Orion and who turns deep darkness into the morning and darkens day into night, the one who calls for the waters of the sea and pours them out on the surface of the earth, Yahweh is his name!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 13:17; Jeremiah 37:10; Micah 5:11"> The one who makes destruction flash upon the fierce, ⸤so that⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “and”">b</a></sup>destruction comes upon the fortification.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 29:21; 1 Kings 22:8; John 8:45–47"> They hate the one who reproves in the gate and they abhor the one who speaks honestly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:30; Micah 6:15; Zephaniah 1:13"> Therefore, because you trample on the poor and you take a grain tax from them, you built houses of dressed stone, but you will not live in them. You built vineyards of delightfulness, but you will not drink their wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 2:6–7; Amos 5:10; Isaiah 1:23"> For I know your transgressions are many and your sins are numerous, you foes of the righteous, those who take a bribe, and ⸤those who⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “they”">c</a></sup>push aside the poor ones in the gate!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 4:4; 2 Timothy 3:1; Ecclesiastes 3:7"> Therefore, whoever has insight will keep silent in that time, for it is a time of evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Matthew 6:33; Micah 6:8"> Seek good and not bad in order that you may live, and so Yahweh, the God of hosts, will be with you, as you have said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 12:9; 3 John 1:11; Psalm 97:10"> Hate evil and love good and establish justice in the gate; perhaps Yahweh, the God of hosts, will be gracious to the remnant of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joel 1:11; Jeremiah 9:17–20; Jeremiah 9:10"> Therefore, thus says Yahweh, the God of hosts, my Lord, “In all of the public squares there will be wailing, and in all of the streets they will say, ‘Alas, alas, alas!’ They shall call the farmers to mourning and to wailing, to those who are skilled in lamentation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 12:12; Jeremiah 48:33; Isaiah 16:10"> And in all of the vineyards there will be wailing, because I will pass in your midst,” says Yahweh.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Day of Yahweh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joel 1:15; Joel 2:1–2; Joel 2:31"> Alas, those who desire the day of Yahweh, why is this for you the day of Yahweh? It will be darkness and not light!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 24:17–18; Jeremiah 15:2–3; Acts 28:4"> It will be as if a man fled from a lion and a bear met him; or he went into the house and leaned his hand against the wall and a snake bit him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Ezekiel 34:12; Zephaniah 1:15"> Is not the day of Yahweh darkness and not light, and pitch dark with no brightness in it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 1:11–16; Proverbs 28:9; Leviticus 26:31"> “I hate, I despise your festivals, and I take no delight in your assemblies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Micah 6:6–7; Amos 4:4–5; Isaiah 66:3"> Yes, even if you offer me your burnt offerings and grain offerings, I will not accept them, and I will not look at the fellowship offerings of your fattened animals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Amos 6:5; Amos 8:10; Amos 8:3"> Remove from me the noise of your songs, and I do not want to hear the melody of your harps!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Micah 6:8; Jeremiah 22:3; Hosea 6:6"> But let justice roll on like the water, and righteousness like an ever-flowing stream.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 7:42–43; Ezekiel 20:8; Nehemiah 9:18"> Did you bring to me sacrifices and offerings those forty years in the desert, O house of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 23:12–13; 1 Kings 11:33; Leviticus 20:2–5"> And you will take up Sikkuth, your king, and Kaiwan, your images, the star of your gods which you made for yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 7:43; 2 Kings 17:6; Amos 4:13"> And I will deport you beyond Damascus,” says Yahweh—the God of hosts is his name.</span>
</div>
