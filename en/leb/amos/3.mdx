## Israel’s Guilt and Punishment

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 2:10; Jeremiah 8:3; Micah 3:1"> Hear this word that Yahweh has spoken against you, O people of Israel, against the whole clan that I brought up from the land of Egypt:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew “Egypt, saying”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6; Exodus 19:5–6; Deuteronomy 10:15"> “You only have I ⸤chosen⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “known”">b</a></sup>of all the clans of the earth. Therefore I will punish you for all your iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–16; Genesis 17:1; Genesis 5:22"> Do two walk together unless they have met?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 11:10; Psalm 104:21; Amos 1:2"> Does a lion roar in the forest ⸤when he has no prey⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “and there is no prey to him”">c</a></sup>Does a fierce, young lion cry out from his den unless he has caught something?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:12; Jeremiah 31:28; Daniel 9:14"> Does a bird fall into a trapping net on the earth when there is no snare for it? Does a trapping net spring up from the ground unless it has certainly caught something?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Zephaniah 1:16; Isaiah 14:24–27"> Or is a horn blown in a city and people are not afraid? Or does a disaster occur in the city and Yahweh has not done it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:15; Genesis 18:17; Psalm 25:14"> Surely my Lord does not do anything unless he has revealed his secret to his servants the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 4:20; Jeremiah 20:9; Amos 3:4"> A lion has roared! Who is not afraid? My Lord Yahweh has spoken, who will not prophesy?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 4:1; Amos 6:1; Amos 1:8"> Proclaim to the citadel fortresses in Ashdod and the citadel fortresses in the land of Egypt and say: “Gather on the mountains of Samaria and see the great panic in her midst and the oppression in her midst!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:22; Zephaniah 1:9; Zechariah 5:3–4"> “They do not know how to do right,” ⸤declares Yahweh⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “the declaration of Yahweh”">d</a></sup>“those who store up violence and destruction in their citadel fortresses.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 18:9–11; 2 Kings 17:3–6; 2 Kings 15:19"> Therefore, thus says my Lord Yahweh, “A foe shall surround the land and bring down your strongholds, and your citadel fortresses shall be plundered.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:34–37; Romans 11:4–5; Isaiah 31:4"> Thus says Yahweh, “Just as the shepherd rescues two legs or a piece of an ear from the mouth of the lion, so shall the ⸤people⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “children”">e</a></sup>of Israel who dwell in Samaria, with the corner of a couch and the damask of a bed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:18–19; 2 Chronicles 24:19; Acts 20:21"> “Listen and testify against the house of Jacob,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">f</a></sup>my Lord Yahweh, the God of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 23:15; Hosea 10:5–8; Hosea 10:14–15"> “For on the day I punish Israel for ⸤its transgression⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “the transgression of Israel upon it”">g</a></sup>I will also punish the altars of Bethel and the horns of the altar will be cut off and fall to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 22:39; Jeremiah 36:22; Amos 6:11"> I will ruin the winter house as well as the summer house, and the houses of ivory will perish and the great houses shall come to an end,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">h</a></sup>Yahweh.</span>
</div>
