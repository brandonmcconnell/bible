## Judgment on Israel’s Neighbors

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joel 3:16; Jeremiah 25:30; Amos 9:3"> And he said, “Yahweh roars from Zion and he utters his voice from Jerusalem; the pastures of the shepherds wither and the top of Carmel dries up.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 8:12; Amos 1:9; Isaiah 8:4"> Thus says Yahweh, “For three transgressions of Damascus and for four I will not revoke the punishment,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “it”">b</a></sup>because they threshed Gilead with threshing sledges of iron!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:27; 2 Kings 6:24; 2 Kings 13:25"> So I will send fire against Hazael’s house and it will consume the citadel fortresses of Ben-Hadad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 16:9; Amos 9:7; Jeremiah 51:30"> I will break the gate bars of Damascus and I will cut off the inhabitants from the Valley of Aven and the one who takes hold of the scepter of Beth Eden, and the people of Aram will go into exile to Kir,” says Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 1:11; 2 Chronicles 28:18; Obadiah 1:11"> Thus says Yahweh: “For three transgressions of Gaza and for four I will not revoke the punishment,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “it”">c</a></sup>because they exiled a whole community, handing them over to Edom!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 18:8; Jeremiah 47:1; Zephaniah 2:4"> So I will send a fire against the wall of Gaza and it will devour its citadel fortresses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:16; Isaiah 14:29–31; Jeremiah 47:4–5"> And I will cut off the inhabitants from Ashdod and the one who takes hold of the scepter of Ashkelon. I will also turn my hand against Ekron, and the remnant of the Philistines will perish,” says the Lord Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 9:11–14; Isaiah 23:1–18; Joel 3:4–8"> Thus says Yahweh: “For three transgressions of Tyre and for four I will not revoke the punishment,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “it”">d</a></sup>because they delivered up a whole community to Edom and they did not remember the covenant of brotherhood!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 9:4; Amos 1:7; Amos 1:4"> So I will send a fire against the wall of Tyre and it will devour its citadel fortresses.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:12–14; 2 Chronicles 28:17; Malachi 1:4"> Thus says Yahweh: “For three transgressions of Edom and for four I will not revoke the punishment,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “it”">e</a></sup>because he pursued his brother with the sword! He stifled his compassion and his anger tore them apart ⸤continually⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “forever”">f</a></sup>and he kept his rage forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:7; Obadiah 1:9–10; Jeremiah 49:20"> So I will send a fire against Teman and it will devour the citadel fortresses of Bozrah.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 13:16; Ezekiel 25:2–7; Jeremiah 49:1–6"> Thus says Yahweh: “For three transgressions of Ammon and for four I will not revoke the punishment,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “it”">g</a></sup>because they ripped open the pregnant women of Gilead in order to enlarge their territory!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:2; Amos 2:2; Deuteronomy 3:11"> So I will kindle a fire against the wall of Rabbah and it will devour its citadel fortresses with a war cry on the day of battle, with a storm on the day of the violent tempest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:3"> And their king will go into exile, he and his commanders together,” says Yahweh.</span>
</div>
